Well, I've recently acquired a second PC and subsequently a second account. I've been planning on starting up an MP and I'd also like to take advantage of my second PC/account by playing two characters at once. Now, I know most people would recommend a Soldier/Enforcer and Doctor combo, as that would definitely be the most efficient, but I've already played a Soldier to 92 and would rather try something different. What I was considering was an MP and Engineer, trimmed Engi pet plus the MP heal/attack/mez pet and the MP's DDs and stat boosts etc. Sounds like a very fun combo with great "solo" potential. I'd like to hear some more suggestions before I decide, so if anyone has any fun/effective combos or just comments on my idea, please let me know. A Fixer would definitely be nice for the run buffs...hmm...so many options, it's so hard to choose .
