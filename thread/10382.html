Sasquash's ideas
Some of these sugestions are ramblings. Some are deeply serious. I leave it to you to decide which is which:

1. Remove "Swim" skill. Merge it into running.

2. Remove, or make "Teaching" skill usefull. Making "Teaching" self-only is not usefull.

3. Make "Riposte" work. Or remove it.

4. Remove "Adventuring". A seperate skill just for using backpacks is useless. Make special backpacks depend on one of the six basic skills according to whatever special boost a backpack gives.

5. Combine all three vehicle skills (air, ground & water) into one skill; "Vehicle". Give different vehicles different armour (independent and not stackable with the players armour class), speed, and other gifts and curses. Some high equip time doesn't hurt.
Ad weapons to vehicles; Air-air, air-ground, ground-air, ground-ground. Outpost vehicles. Command vehicles. Support vehicles.
Make it possible to transport others with your vehicle. Make the vehicle upgradable with components. Could even build a whole nano line around buffing vehicles...
Basicaly make vehicles more like vehicles, and not just some character in a vehicle form.
Make vehicle speed independent of run speed.

6. Some weapons have a lot (!!) of skill requirements to use. Like the nifty "Tripler". Try to cut down on the number of skills needed or make those weapons worth the investment.

*Make a tripler with some of the style of the Galahad sword. And make it part of a duo of triplers that do cold and fire damage. And make all the blades of the Tripler have equal lenght. Make them dungeon drops. One chilly boss and one hot boss.* (He, he. Shameless)

7. The "Nano Resist" skill is acting realy weird. It is my lowest skill on my lvl 65 adventurer, despite the amount (not too much) of IP i've used to increase it. It is even lower than all the skills I've never increased. Explain why, and/or fix it.

8. Different backpacks are a simple solution to the inventory sorting problem. But not very practical. Give us a bank with different compartments. Like different pages of an Excel sheet. Allow the player to rename the compartments on the client side. This way the server doesn't have to store any "labels".
Some sorting systems could be added too; Quality, name, type (armour, implant, trade item...), and so on. All this is controled client side. The server only has to know what is stored in each compartment, just like it now knows what is inside each bag.

9. Make backpack inventory an extension of the normal inventory of the character.
A small button can toggle open/closed backpack. The backpack inventory can show up at the bottom of the common inventory, with some kind of visible seperation (scroll down).Would make the backpack a nice place to store all the stuff a character always lugs around, like med-kits, nano-chargers and ammo. Or even make ncu-belts able to store these basic things ("Diablo" style). Could remove the need to wait for these basic things to respawn in the resurrection booth.

10. Split the chat and the "event" messages. Chat messages are often lost in the garble of "Monster hit you for 999, Your damage shield hit Monster for 6, You hit Monster for 7, Monsters damage shield hit you for 9999999....". Some buttons around the area where you actually do the typing/chatting that glow and tell you which "room" you are posting into could help. Or just move the "Friends" tab down to the type area.

11. AOMD has a good idea. Use it.

12. Ad a "mission size" slider to the mission options. Let the one-hour-a-day player play. Adjust reward (a huge mission could give you a complete set of armour!) and possible token reward accordingly.

13. Remove empty boxes in missions.

14. Make all storage windows resizable.

15. "The grey zone";
Allow all characters on one AO account access to the same bank.
Let all characters on one AO account share all the tokens.
Or improve the way tokens are rewarded. There are already "title levels" in the game. Tokens could be tied up to the title level. Title two = 2 tokens as possible reward, title three = 3 tokens...

16. Make Tank armour and Battlesuits usefull, and not just novelties.
Give the Battlesuit some huge armour class and a strenght bonus. Maybe even ad a small damage modification.
Allow enforcers and martial artists (and insane soldiers and similar) to use these suits to dish out some extra damage. Keep the downsides (slow, nano-cost, equip time...). The Battlesuits could also have "upgrades" and a seperate weaponsystem from the weapon of the character using them. Make each "Battlesuit weapon" depend on skills to use similar to other weapons. An enforcer in a Battlesuit should prefer to mount melee weapons upon their Battlesuit.
Tank armour should have a higher armour rating than a coat! Ad some nice damage reflection bonus to up some of the downsides, and you have the soldier and enforcer armour of choice. Let the different tank armour types have different up and down sides. A heavy tank armour of the same quality of a light one should have a higher armour rating, but also higher/more penalties.

17. Nanoclusters could be used to upgrade just about anything! (Vehicles, Battlesuits, Tank armour, weapons, armour...)

Adventurer complaints;
18. Change some of the adventurer skills;
- Make "Vehicles" blue or dark blue. We are not drivers!
- Ease up on "Brawl" (light blue +4, or even +5!!)
- Dimach could use a little cheapening.
- Ease up on Bow skills. Bows look good on an adventurer.
- Give "Eagle Eye" to the agent. Don't force those poor sneaks to change profession everytime they want to boost their rifle skill...
- Remove the non flying reet nano. No need for feathers if I can't use them to fly. Make the low level "speed-morph" be a Silvertail or something...They look pretty fast. And cute.
But give me flight as soon as possible. (Lvl 50-ish at least)
- Ad a damage modifier nano line. Let adventurers change the type of damage a weapon does on the fly. The quality 200 of this nano would ofcourse be a disease damage enabler.
Usefull for us cursed 1hEdg and pistol users (I hate damage shields on others!). And a nice boost in a team. Probably too nice, eh?
- A "Tame" line of nanos could be within the specs of the adventurer profession. Let this nanoline have a jump of quality 20-25 for each new tame nano. A quality 50 tame nano can tame a critter (non humanoid shaped) up to level 50. Make the tame program take forever to run, so the critter we are trying to tame gets plenty of time to kill us. Ten times over.
And make it dependent on Time&Space and PsyMod.
The whole tame nano thing would offend MP's. This makes it even more perfect!
(I'm not too serious about this "tame" thing, but it sounds like fun)

19. Let beaurocrats tame humanoid monsters (one monster in addition to the beaurocrats helper).

20. Let engineers take control of robotic monsters (one monster in addition to the engineer pet).

21. Give doctors their little nurse (a white orb similar to the black torture droid in SW?)

22. Player made shoulder, ring and braser items. Customize your needs. (Tiny boosts, +1, +2, +3...)

23. Team-missions. Rewards and tokens for everyone.

Final. IP reset! One time, or every title level, or every level, or whatever. It just has to be done. Every character will be ruffed up at one time or another by game changes.

...It is realy weird how this game can go from frustrating, to amazing, back to frustrating again in such fast turns. Usually connected to a patch...

Oh, and I'm looking for a job. Hire me!
(I am hostile to authorities, have an imagination, like to draw, fight for good, and am a general nice guy)
