A place to go to "work"
There really needs to be one place in the larger cities where folks who are "working" (selling buffs and various other services) can go while they're in "working for money" mode. This would help cut down on a lot of the "buff me please" spam/harrasment that goes on in the larger gathering areas, as well as saving a lot of legwork for those of us looking for people offering their services.

This place could be it's own building, or a simple "shed" like the one where the mission terminal booths are located in front of the Fair Trade in Tir. Some might say that the trade areas in the shops were originally meant for this purpose, but as you know, you can't broadcast across the well trafficed shopping channels from there, which is why I think the shed idea is a better one. It would be stupid simple to implement, and would be a nice convience.
