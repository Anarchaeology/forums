I've got my degree in IP allocation and now I'm graduating. Bye all!
I came back for the free re-trial in December and had a great time for about three weeks. I made several new characters from several classes and had much fun. The game has improved tremendously in the past few months.

However... IP allocation and repetitive missions killed me... again.

There's really no other viable way to start a new character than to run missions... even if you have friends who will give you millions of credits, those token boards are just to precious (...my precious) to go without! Sixty missions will nab you an extra 150 HP on top of implants... that'll save your ass in a pinch up to level 40 or so... and that's as many as I have the patience to get before missions become impossibly huge later on...

IP allocation is the Beatch Goddess of all RPG systems... just spent a whole weekend leveling up that "perfect" character... only to discover that you're not ever going to use skill X again? All those point could have been going into evades or nanos? Can't face the job of leveling a gimped character when you KNOW what could have fixed it? Heck, all you have to do is start over again... and again... and again...

Make a character... do missions to level 20... discover IP misallocation... get tired of using same old weapons and nanos all over again anyway... start over...

I really like this game... I will give it another shot later this year if I'm not into something else by then... so NO you can't have my stuff!

Good hunting all! Omni-Tek is your friend!
