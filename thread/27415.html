Friends list commands
Just thought we'd inform you about a few chat commands in AO that not everybody know about, as they weren't included in the manual (not even the online one).

The commands are handy for adding and removing friends, and getting rid of the long lists of people on the "unknown" list (below the "?"). We've seen some people using these - e.g. making scripts for adding everybody in their organisation to the list, while others don't know about them.

The commands are:

!cc addbuddy <name> (adding <name> to the list)
!cc rembuddy <name> (removing <name> from the list)
!cc rembuddy ? (emptying the "unknown" list)
