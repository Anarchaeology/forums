The listing of what the topics would look like, up at the top of this thread, left out both of the Tech forums. I'm assuming this is an oversight, and not part of the plan. Right?

Love, love, love the idea of player polls. And I'm just OK with merging the in progress and envisioned stuff into one forum; I was never sure which one to post suggestions to.

Do the suggestions we make in In Progress and Envisioned even get shown to the developers? If they do, I'm guessing that somebody, probably Cosmik, brings them up in a weekly or monthly or whatever meeting. Could we get the developer feedback on the suggestions, from the minutes, posted to the Suggestions forum from time to time? It'd feel less like shouting into a barrel.

Natelie's "Why?" forum suggestion sounds sweet, and she's right, it would probably cut trollage down by at least 30%. But to work, it'd take a committment from Gaute Godager and/or the developers to be willing to explain what they were thinking when they made various decisions, and a similar committment from Cosmik to track down those answers and post 'em. It would be really, really wonderful.

See, now, when you run into something that sucks about your profession, most people assume it's there because Funcom hates them. Me, I've been around enough to know that they're probably there for a reason. It may not be a good reason. It may not be working the way the developers intended it to. Or contrariwise, it may be that none of the players have figured out how the developers intended it to be used. But absent any feedback from the development team when their decisions are questioned, a solid half of the people seem to want to assume the worst.

Hence, all the threads entitled "Why does Funcom hate fixers/agents/adventurers/nanotechs?" I assume that Funcom doesn't hate those professions. I assume that Funcom thought, when they designed them, they'd be perfectly playable. (For 3 of those 4, I think I've got the "why" figured out. The design of the NTs baffles me; I still can't figure out how Funcom intended people to play them.)

And if we had a forum where we could get answers back from the development team, where they were willing to explain why they thought those designs would be playable, I'm pretty sure there'd be a lot less whining. There might even be a lot fewer cancelled accounts.

I realize that the suggestions I've made make more work for the Community Team. To address that, I suggest that the stupid antique IRC server be shut down, and its hardware scavenged to make Broken Shores run faster, and freeing up Dai-Galean's time to work on something useful.
