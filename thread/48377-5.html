My tuppence worth:

I don't believe a nerf is the answer. Unless they can accomplish fundamental changes to the ransack/deprive line effective only in PvP then taking away this ability would cripple thousands of players who have learned to play the game within the given mechanics. (mind you, other classes have had to do major adjusting because of whiney-ass PvP based nerfs, NT's & agents anyone?)

I play an NT on RK1 & RK2. I will not, under any circumstances, engage a trader in PvP unless I am caught short and have no choice. I agree with the prevailing opinion here that certain classes, traders among them, are totally dominating high and low end PvP in this game. Doctors are just as bad, stacked DoT's are a killer, self heals that still heal for 100% when everyone's damage is halved, an initiative debuff that renders almost every player practically useless. Doc's own.

Fought a level 55 SUPER twink Fixer on RK2 today. The guy was wearing Grid Armor, I kid you not. He had HoT's running on him, self-buffed allegedly, that soaked up over 2k of damage and seen me hit the deck in 15 seconds without making a dent on his health bar. He hit me with 5 crits in a row courtesy of an old 5% LLTS and I presume also an MA crit buff. Where did he get the ip to have mega health, maxed dark blue nanoskills to wear GA and run top-end HoT's, raise the tradeskills to equip the scope as well as wielding a bloody nice SMG that lay the smack down on me quick as you like? No my char ain't crap, he has good evades and was on 40% agg. Just an example.

The problem isn't with these classes owning everyone else, it's with the people that are getting owned having no real way to negate or minimise that class advantages that make them so good at killing other players.

The only nerf I'd argue for is have trader drains NOT affect nano skills as well as weapon skills. Crippling players across the board in both combat dependent character skills is taking the mickey just a little bit. An NT could live with a lower attack rating but we can't live with a couple of nano's runningin our NCU's that also turn is into 50% effective casters, our main ability.

In the meantime, fix the classes that are everyone's gimp. Look at MA's and Fixers compared to where they where a few months ago? Get your collective fingers out Funcom, there's work to be done on critical game mechanics. You can keep Booster Packs etc till the classes are as balanced as they can realistically be
