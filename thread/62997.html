Craftsmen should be able to PREVENT quality level increase
Ok, tonight after having three implants jump to a higher quality level than the intended recipient could actually use I have to complain about the fact that I, as a master craftsman, have insufficient control over the quality improvement of my product.

Things are fine when it doesn't matter if the finished product quality jumps, but there are some times -- particularly when the recipient is in a title cap period -- where a quality too high ends up being a major annoyance, representing lost time and often non-trivial spending of money that ends up feeling lost (even if the item _can_ eventually be used).

This happens even when I take every possible precaution to keep it from happening. For example, on the chest implant I just programmed, not only did I de-equip my 1000 token board, made sure I had no nanoprogramming buffs running, and didn't have any other intelligence increases, I even debuffed my intelligence by 12. Programmed shining and bright first (after needing to strategically increase my nanoprogramming to within a few points above the skill required, then reversing that when the cluster was in). Managed to keep the implant from jumping. Then faded went in, with the mininum possible nanoprogramming skill I could have without an IPR, and wham, QL jumped by 2.

An ear implant I had jumped by _six full quality levels_ even though I had taken every precaution against jumping.

This doesn't just affect nanoprogramming, it affects other skills that result in equipment players can use. More than once have I seen an MCS end up being too hard for the fixer who wanted it to use it.

PLEASE GIVE US A WAY TO CONTROL QUALITY INCREASE.

Thank you for your kind words.

I think that very often, simple is beautiful.
So I try to keep it simple, but still with enough room for improvements.



As I said, retuning an item can be as simple as Shift + Rightclicking the appropiate tool on it and have enough skill to do it.

1 Tool to adjust downwards.
1 Tool to adjust upwards.

Adjusting 1 QL downwards is easy enough, you just replace it with 1 QL lower and run it trough the calculation algorithm for stat requriements and stuffs.

When you try to do a QL going upwards, it will calculate the implant WL and stuffs and see if it's possible that it could be 1 QL higher - If it is, it makes a new item qith 1 higher QL and removes the old.
And if it cannot, it makes a Message to the Chat box "You have already rached the best adjustments that this Quality (itemname here) can support."



All the calculating algorithms are already in place.
I mean, if you were a programmer, you could almost see all the "IF" and "THEN" I use in my examples are almost calling for someone to implement them.
