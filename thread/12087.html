5 (6) suggestions for additions that should be easy to implement
These suggestions do NOT include nerfs, things with exploitation potential or complains about PvP imbalances. AND they should all be fairly easy to implement.

Now ain’t that something?

1) Make an easy way of transferring items between chars. For instance make the bottom 3 slots of the bank box available to all chars on the same account (and make it a no-nodrop-zone to prevent token farming abuse). People will argue that it will be twinker’s heaven and of course they’re right. But if people have the resources to twink their younger chars, they’ll also have friend to help them out anyway.
My claim is that 99% of the times a feature like this will be used, would be when your soldier finds that TimeSpace nanocluster that just fits in your MP’s head implant, or your shotgun doc happens to find that wonderful beam for your enforcer. Instead of having to waste your friends’ time or rely on strangers with the risk of being scammed, just put it in the Common Slots and log in with the right char…

2) Increase the size of the shortcut bar. Run out of numbers? Well, if the height is tripled, there’d be room for #, ctrl-# and alt-# for the first, second and third row respectively. No harm done, better control for everyone. It could even be made optional for those that like fewer options…

3) In a find-and-return-mission, also list/mark the location of the terminal where the item must be returned to. If that feature can be exploited or is unfair to anyone, tell me how…

4) Make a way to delete old nano programs you won’t ever use again. Somehow I guess I won’t be missing Lesser Anger Manifestation much…

5) Include nanos in the scripts. Please? It could be extremely useful AND it would be great for role playing. If you want to make it easy for us, make a script editor (should be VERY simple compared to all the other aspects of a game like this). Alternatively add a command (let’s say /execute) where you can cast the nano (provided, of course, you have the skills and the actual nano). Example:
/say Servant! Come to my aid!
/execute “lesser anger manifestation”.
/delay 2000
/pet “anger manifestation” rename Servant
/say Ahhh! There you are. Now go kill something…
/pet Servant hunt

…6) And a pet class only suggestion: a /pet command to target your pet. Something like /pet “name” target. Good for animas, heals etc.

Was I lying? Doesn’t this sound like more fun for us all, fair to everyone AND very, very easy to implement?

Comments more than welcome...
