I kited the Trash King Lackey's solo at 95 (QL130 mobs with quite a bit of HP and an AoE nuke) using snare and shoot tactics. I don't think I ever went below 50% hp (on some of them - I don't think they even hit me once) Goes something like this...

1) Find a flat spot of ground to pull to so that the mob won't warp.
2) Pull mob (AoE bullets are good to pull with as they have range - so long as no other mobs are around!)
3) Snare mob and back up.
4) Shoot mob while keep moving backwards a little bit each shot on gun recharge
5) Mob breaks snare - resnare and run forwards! You'll get out of range much faster than going backwards or trying to turn.
6) If Nanopool gets less than 50%... backup, sit and use battle nano kit.
7) If HP gets less than 50% ... root, sit and use a couple of battle HP kits while your HoT tops up.
8) goto step 4 until mob==dead.

Took about 10 minutes per bot and I only got 35k xp for each one which I was a little dissapointed with but still it was a load of fun.

Snares are very powerful and allow us to solo a mob many professions wouldn't stand a chance against - you just have to pick a nice flat spot so you don't encounter warping too much.

Rick: Best HoTs you can swing, both long and short (keep them running!), good runbuff, best snare you can manage.

Find a dark red melee mob. Root it. Burst it. Snare it when the root breaks and start kiting. If you're ever hurting, root it and back off and let your HoTs plug the damage.

Getting a HE buff from an NT will make your life *much* easier.

Good mobs to try to kite around 60 - give the Anuns in Mort a shot. They're hard, but it definitely feels good when you finish.

You won't make as much XP doing this as missioning, but its a lot more fun.
