Red Tape/Omni-Admin is expanding!
Red Tape is expanding our operations! We have many openings in entry to mid level positions within our organization. That means we are now actively recruiting talented and dedicated individuals like yourself that are interested in bettering Omni-Tek.

Red Tape is an Omni-Admin affiliated organization that coordinates with other Omni departments and organizations into working as a cohesive whole. The old notions of warfare have completely changed since death isn't fatal on Rubi-Ka. Brute strength alone will not forge a lasting solution. A lasting solution can be achieved through diplomacy and moderation. We work with those that have the foresight to realize it.

We have a distinguished history on Rubi-Ka. We have been an active part of the Rubi-Ka community with our BureauCons(Bureaucrat Conventions). We have hosted 9 and look foward to hosting many more. Our organization are one of the founding organizations of OTSEC(Omni-Tek SEcurity Council) which played a part to help bring the Dust Brigade menace under control. The reformed OTSEC is now acting vanguard for the company. A multitiude of different organizations and talents working dilligently to help secure a bright prosperous future for the company and its employees.

If you wish to join an organization that is active in Rubi-Ka's politics and want to help make a difference in the world around you sign up with us today! There will be an interview and we do require that you have no outstanding criminal backgrounds or vendettas from/against other organizations. We offer our employees an environment to help them flourish and grow.

Requirements:

  • Must be Omni employed or plan to become Omni employed before we can allow you to join. (Our company registry computer simply will not allow non-Omnis to join.)
  • Need to have basic travelling experience on Rubi-Ka equal to or greater than level 50. (Most of our members are level 50+, you can still join if you really want.)
  • No outstanding warrants or "bounties" from Omni-Tek and other organizations.
  • Must be able to act in a mature manner.


If you are interested in joining our organization we have a need in the following areas with the preferred profession for each department listed.

Acqusition and Supplies - (Engineer, Fixer, Meta-Physicists, Trader)
Human Resources - Any profession
Intelligence - (Agent, Fixer)
Public Relations - (Bureaucrat), Any profession
Search and Rescue - (Adventurer, Doctor, Engineer)
Security - (Soldier, Enforcer, Martial Artist, Nano-Technicians)

If you are accepted you will be put on a trial period of two weeks to see if you fit in with us. For recruitment information or schedule an interview please send a /tell to Kithrak, /tell Beria or /tell Deuscloud our generals or reply to this thread.

[OOC] We are a guild of casual to serious/experienced roleplayers. The guild was founded on the concept of mutual assistance. There are bi-weekly guild meetings and all members are expected to be able to at least attend every other one. We need a webpage. [/OOC]
