The Plot Thickens.. like some kind of thickening thing...
Well, The HitchHiker's Guide to the Galaxy.

I was watching this great show last night for the first time since I was a tiny child with my sisters, right, and I remembered Marvin the depressed robot real well, so I was happily surprised to find Marvin at 10-3 outpost in Rubi Ka but could not understand why he was there..

then it all came crashing together in a puff of logic!

On Gramathea, the mythical planet oif planet builders, there awoke a 5 million year old Gramathean named Slarmibartfast, and he created Norway, hey he even won an awrd for it, and this is mentioned quite mightily in the HItchHiker's Guide to the Galaxy, episode 4, or was it 5, anyways, it was mentioned quite mightily.

Then I recalled with much abandon that Anarchy Online was created in Norway, and suddenly it all made nonsense.

If anyone has anything else to add here, we might be able to submit this post the Encyclopedia Galactica for review and possible allocation.



Or at the very least, you should be able to buy a copy of HitchHiker's Guide to the Galaxy Compendium in the Trade shops.
