Well, actually I never heard of any declaration of war either. There was a period of Amnesty during which the two sides agreed to allow people to switch loyalties without much difficulty. Of course that went by the wayside.

But, there's no war going on. Everything is still going along with respect to the Tir Accord. Clan has control of Clan territory, Omni has control of Omni territory. Stay in your territory or you can get pseudo-legally killed by the other side.

Maybe someday there will be war. Probably when the factions figure out how to actually keep control of an outpost they manage to secure. For now, it's just a lot of tension and posturing.
