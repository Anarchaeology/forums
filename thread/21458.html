My Team Mission Vision
I love team misions as I'm sure everyone else does but there has always been one major problem that often wipes out teams. The whole mission is always easily completed (with a good sized team of course) but much of the time when we get to the boss level there is always 4 mobs (give or take) and the boss which often aggros and seriously hurts the first person to enter causing them to have to return to the level below, heal, and thus stranding the rest of the team with a similar fate. My "vision" is that instead of the boss button leading to the main room, where they all gang up on you instantly, it lead to something of a "prep room". There everyone in the team can buff, set up a strategy or do whatever they need to and then go on all AT ONCE allowing them to at least have some sort of a chance against whatever may wait in the next room. Also, another thought to prevent one of the boss's minions from attacking them while they prepare is always have the door to the main room locked, but only a lock that anyone could pick easily. That's my idea and I hope you agree. I'd also like to hear some feedback on what everyone else thinks about this.
