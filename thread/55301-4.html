Oh how much more I would LOVE PvP if Team PvP was implemented (in that you can gain titles while teamed). Then people wouldn't always want to be solo to get kill points.

And if you're in a team, you should get a small number of points for a kill your team makes, regardless if you've done the majority of the damage to the killed player or not. Perhaps the person who did the majority of the damage can get more PvP points than the rest of the team, but the rest of the team shouldn't be left out.

That would also make Doctors more desirable (since, hell, we're trained to watch your healthbars, dammit! ), and we might actually get PvP titles above Apprentice!

My biggest complaint with the current PvP system however is people zoning. It's bad enough that 19 out of 20 people won't duel me (and I won't turn into a ganker). But it's even worse that out of the 1 of 20 people who duel me, 75% of the time they run away and zone if I'm winning. I swear I'll be forever stuck at Freshman because my damage over time is so low by comparison

So yes, 1) Team PvP implemented, and 2) no points for killing same-faction.

Azzazzi's post had many good ideas, though I don't like the idea of losing PvP points if you go a week without a PvP fight, or whatever time frame he was suggesting. If you attain a title, it shouldn't randomly disappear because you take a break. Or maybe it should. But then I can see that exploited too by running into 2ho, targetting someone of the opposing faction, pressing Q and zoning once a week to keep your title.
