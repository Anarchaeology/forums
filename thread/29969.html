Just a few weeks before the announcement that the expansion "Shadowlands" would be released, the "envisioned" section of this forum was removed....

Will there be no new content (not items, actual game play content) added before the expansion is released?

Will we, above lvl80, have to continue doing nothing but missions in BS for the next six and a half months until the expansion is released (also there is PvP, but that isn't for everyone)?

Releasing a half done product.....
