Ok well heres a start for anyone interested, not alot to go on but it's something anyhow.

Some descriptions with tiny bits of info in them taken from db descs:

DeCranum was a Rubi-Ka native, famous for his many arena victories, who experimented with combining Kevlar and Energized armor types, hoping to eliminate weaknesses and highlight strengths of the individual materials. The few prototypes that he managed to produce are highly efficient.

Nano Crystal - Created in memory of the famous Arena fighter Mongo, this formula taunts all enemies in the area onto the Enforcer. It also temporarily increases the maximum health of the Enforcer by 1,789 points.

Rupert Kirch experimented with notum threads in various types of armor. To be able to use them in kevlar armor, he had to remove 50 of the 640 layers that normal kevlar consists of. However, the notum threads benefit the user with a weak type of expansive energy shield, so the protection is generally the same. In addition the threads add to your performance in a few, selected areas.

The Flak Armor was mainly designed to reduce damage from projectiles. To improve its usefulness, some protection from melee weapons and radiation has been added. It has a number of large heavy metal studs. Usually Flak armor is heavily padded, but for some time Jane Flowers experimented with exchanging the padding with a type of porous kevlar material she had invented. It did indeed improve the performance of the armor, but regular production of the new material proved being to expensive. Wearing it for an extended period requires some strength and stamina, as well as some agility because of the stiffened padding.

The Flowers Tech Armor has its name from Jane Flowers. Jane Flowers was a Rubi-Ka native soldier who preferred the Tech Armor; her only issue with it was the heat. Together with an Engineer friend, she developed a new sensor that would keep her cool. This sensor has now been developed further, and is integrated in the new version. It will not just keep you cool; it also helps reducing damage from fire-based attacks.

Ravens Energized Armor has its name from Venice Raven. Venice Raven is a famous Doctor and one of the first to test the prototype of this armor. She worked as a field medic during a clean-up operation on the planet best known as Crime. It was supposedly one of the largest military operations of its time. As the only field medic in the first wave of attackers to survive the entire conflict, Raven gave her suit of armor much of the credit. Today most people have heard of Venice Raven and her armor, but few know how and where she gained her fame... This Energized Armor gives enhanced protection against energy and also some protection against cold.





oh and the funniest db item I could find was... If you look closely at the icon you'll see why.

Soltoyz Intimate Helper

The intimate helper is an advanced toy that 'lifts your spirits' by modifying your metabolism and stimulating the production of white blood-cells. A single occurence where a citizen of Galway died from over-production of those cells was the only incentive Omni-Tek needed to prohibit the toy on Rubi-Ka.
