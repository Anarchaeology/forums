Even a trader vs. MP fight is a bit skewed towards the trader. In my sporadic pvp experiences...long ago...what usually happens in a mass battle with a trader in it...is that the trader and MP end up trading debuffs.

Traders, like Docs, have an instantly casting debuff where MP's have a 6-9 second execution time with nano shutdown depending on nano init. Anything less than a nano shutdown on a trader is to invite a lesser plunder and doesn't stop their roots.

Trader plunders have ...around twice the range of Nano Shutdown with a range extender applied to both classes.

There are also a few cosmetic game mechanics that work against MP's. Namely, we are very con****uous with our floating balls. Vice versa, its sometimes a bit tricky for me to pick out a trader toon out of a crowd just by looking at it. Shotguns are very common weapons and many people have similar armor setups. I actually have to swing my laggy curser and select him to see if the name is someone I recognize.

So, being 1) easy to spot combined with 2) having a long casting time, combined with 3) my short range usually meant I am the one who is debuffed first.

A trader would have to be a complete idiot not to notice the character with the floating balls, running up to him within short range. I usually open with mind quake and depending on if he already has plunders running in his ncu, it might force him to switch hotkey bars. Then when I start casting Nano SD, he then has 5-6 seconds to root my pets and cast his debuff which is possible if he keeps his head about him.

I've made traders run before, but quite frankly I've never killed one. Pets get rooted and they land their debuff on me during my execution of nano shutdown. We end up debuffing each other. But since my debuff doesnt lower their weapon skills, they still shoot and crit.

Not that debuffing an MP does too much. We have very few abilities to debuff after all, but if you degrade my nuke and weapon skill, I wont be hurting you too much. If the trader managed to root my pets before I debuffed him, I run. If he hasn't, he might run instead.

I had a fight with a trader once that was just like this. I start casting NSD, he does his plunder and screws me over. Then, I finish execution and I screw him over. We both ran away. I think we traded debuffs once more, before other players became a factor in it all. Naturally, it took me longer to recover from his debuff than I recovered from his....3 minutes versus 1 minute.

Traders/Docs/MAs/Fixers are great PvP characters in all lvls.

Debuff the hell out of someone (you won't have enough NCU to get the benefit of the debuff but who cares ), they will not be able to hit through the shield and reflect....... its hillarious.

Fixers are also a problem if i can't get a debuff to stick.
When i fight a doc its all about who hits who with a debuff first.
A few well played adventurers out there whoop me everytime.

Before the heal nerf it was easy to spam heal a 1k team heal and with the low recharge, extend the battle quite a bit if needed. Now, when I start to get below 50% i have to make a decision to either delay the inevitible by healing or hope to crit a bunch and take them down before i die.

PvP low lvl is all about outside buffs while PvP at the upper lvls is all about bracers. Go fixer or Trader
