Sitting cross-legged on the floor of her office, a small screwdriver tucked behind an ear, her hair for once kept tight on the back of her neck, Jen casts a quizzical look to the terminal lying in front of her, its opened metal guts revealing the tangled mass of wires and connexion cables inside.

"Sooo... here we go, lil' one!" she whispers with a ferocious smile, grabbing the screwdriver and deftly beginning to unmount the broken, half-melted hard drive, slightly humming to the sound of music coming from the speakers installed on her desk, plugged to another computer. Grid-broadcasts are what she prefers - no interferences, quick and efficient broadcast.

"...got shot down,
o'er Tir one day
and crashed in West Stret Bank,
The clanners came and grabbed him
and dumped him in a cell that stank!..." the Fixer absently goes on humming, before stopping abruptly, suddenly realizing what she's listening to.

"Wha... someone please tell meh they haven't made *that* into a public song? Oooooh, my..." she slowly grins.

And she resumes her work, barely noticing that, after a minute, the melody comes back to her lips.
