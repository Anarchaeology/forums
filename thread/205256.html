11 suggestions that will DRAMATICALLY improve gameplay in AO!!
I love MMORPG's. I started out playing UO about 5 years ago and was addicted. I must say the graphics in AO are really FANTASTIC and the storyline is interesting but the gameplay itself is WEAK and many others agree with me on this one. So far everything in the game has been mindless hack and slash to get XP so you can get to the next level. I joined an org hoping to find the "****e" in AO. Hoping that the spontaneous defending/attacking towers and having pvp matches will bring some more life to the dull hacking and slashing. I have 7 diff characters in 7 diff professions, so I have a pretty good feel of the game. I thought I'd give a few suggestions I haven't seen up so far and am a little curious as to why they haven't been implemented yet. I am aware that these are way too many changes to take place at once and things people might not be used to, It would be GREAT to run a seperate server with these different rules. These new changes would create more of an emotional attachment to the game itself, allow more freedom, better gameplay, directly affect the world of Rubi-Ka, have the ability to become more well known, gain Notoriety or Respect throughout wich is what people really want instead of following a set story with too many rules. Please by all means comment on ALL of my suggestiong to tell me what you think it's advantages/disadvantages are.




Here is the list, and you can find out more on each suggestion in the post below(Since I ran out of space):

#1 Doll Icon
#2 Labeled Items
#3 Traveling
#4 PvP
#5 Bounty
#6 Death
#7 Credits
#8 City Control
#9 Places
#10 Faces & Clothes
#11 Owning Physical Space




This is supposed to be a game about a huge war fighting for a desperate cause! where is the struggle? Where is the chaos and pain? I don't feel it in this game everything is too hunky dory, too nice. This game feels like a futuristic Everquest and it gets borring. A person's only goal is to reach level 200+. What do you do then? You can't really associate with lower level individuals in this game, they are too low for a team, and the gameplay revolves around being in a team. All you can do is walk around with a high title, fancy armor and have people ask you for buffs and money. I got about a total of 5 million my first few weeks of playing AO, people have way to much money and are so glad to give it away making lower level people able to get anything they want right away thus making the game a bore. Yes It's nice to get some money but I got a total of 5 million and there was really nothing to work for after that.

My point being in this long post is that obviously I really like AO, FC has a great game going here graphics wise, the story line is interesting but in fact the gameplay itself is what is truly weak. I can see Rubi-Ka as a much more addicting, spontaneous, adventurous and exciting place to be in than its current hack and slash everquestique state. The key is to give players MORE freedom to physically and emotionally affect this virtual world, MORE freedom to gain Notoriety or Respect and to know it rests in your hands(or fingers). After all that is what an MMORPG is about and why we pay the $14.95 per month, if not you might as well stick to X-Box or something.

continued.....
#1 Doll Icon

- When Shift+Clicking a person there should be a doll icon of the persons body so you can see in detail what they are wearing from head to toe, UO style. If the person is wearing a robe, the gloves & boots should still be viewable, you can also switch to X-ray vision to view the implants.


#2 Labeled Items

- At a certain lvl/amount of XP your name should appear next to armor/implants or anything you are able to make. (IE "Carbon Sleeves crafted by poisonx") This of course would work best with idea #1 and has the ability to make people well known for certain things. It would be good to have a skill where you can make clothes crafted by yourself too.


#3 Traveling

- Traveling through the Grid and whompas is ok as back up but it kind of sucks if that is the only means of transportation. Every player should have the ability at one point to teleport to some exact location. It could be done by standing in a specific place and recording the location in some sort of memory chip. You would name the memory chip whatever you want and then you would cast a teleport nano. When you cast it you then would have to click on the memory chip(location) you would like to go to. It definatly beats having to terminate yourself deep in a temple or dungeon because you would die on the way back anyway or it's just too far. I mean come on there isn't always a fixer around. I don't see anything wrong with this idea and if anyone does I can't see the bad outweighing the good. Why do we have to fight through 100 grays to finally get to something that is green or yellow, why if we are not omni must we travel extra far to get to the temple if we die? Things like that just make no sense. Of course teleporting wouldn't be instantaneous because you don't want to make it that easy to flea from combat, the nano should take about 5-7 seconds to complete and you should be able to teleport whenever and whereever you want.


#4 PvP

- I know this is a very touchy subject but honestly the UO PvP rules were the best ever in any MMORPG. Hear me out on this one. It goes a little something like this... When you highlight a person with your mouse their whole body will turn into a shade of one of these colors:

Blue=innocent player
Gray=someone who just recently comitted a crime (When commiting a crime there will be a loud announcement within the vicinity letting everyone know the purpetrator & they have about 5 real minutes to turn back to Blue, being open to attack by anyone in the meantime)
Green someone in your org
Orange= someone in a warring org
Red= serial killer

The Rules:

This is how it works, basically anywhere outside a city is grounds to be attacked. If a blue attacks another blue the attacker turns gray making them open to attack from anyone else. If the attacker successfully kills the innocent "blue", they get 1 murder count, and after 5 murder counts the person turns "red". It would take many in game hours for 1 murder count to dissapear. No Reds or Grays are allowed in any City they will be killed on site. Reds would have their own bank in a remote location. Reds are outcasts and being a red would be a hard life to live, anyone may attack you at any moment, even other reds, and would not get a murder count for doing so. Reds either learn to survive together or kill each other off. If you exceed a certain amount of murders and die as a red you would lose a few levels and IP when you ressurect (about 10 levels average +5-10 levels for each murder count after that) This only happens once and will not happen again as long as you don't get anymore murder counts.

Healing/buffing a red, gray or orange:

Healing or buffing anyone except a fellow green or blue is a crime and will make you turn gray. Of course there are accidents wich is why before doing so there should be a notification saying "This action will flag you as a criminal are you sure you want to proceed" Click Yes or No. Same goes in team situations. If one of your team members turns gray throughout an adventure and you are a doctor casting a team heal for instance, the doctor may heal the entire team but recieves a message about the gray person: "Gray'sNameGoesHere is flagged, the following action will cause you to go gray do you wish to proceed?" Click Yes or No. These messages may also be adjusted under the options. You can choose to never or always heal a gray/red/orange team member without getting the message or choose "notify me when a team member is gray/red/orange"


#5 Bounty

- Somewhere in the city there should be a board or computer/terminal of some sort with a list, or maybe you can just use the helpbot to announce a list of all reds. The highest ranked person on the list is the one who has killed the most innocent people and their Bounty Reward would be the highest(in credits). Only a blue is able to recieve a reward for the bounty. If it was done in a team the reward is split. You would claim your reward by "looting the persons head from the corpse"(It could be a head, heart, ear whatever FC decides but something physical that you must bring to the Bounty Terminal)


#6 Death

- Another touchy subject, Death in AO is a mere neusance at most. It's just annoying to have to travel back to where you were, and go through 100 grays to continue from where you left off. #3 comes in handy here too. Losing XP when you die is fine but It's just like "oh well" thats about it. Death should be a bit more feared the same way it is in real life. This is how death should work in AO, hear me out on this:

When a person dies, at a certain level one of the following should happen: Either all of their belongings in their inventory and what they are wearing (besides implants/no drop items/memory chips described in #3) remain on their corpse OR a few completely random items in your inventory and inside backpacks remain on the corpse. You can retrieve your belongings one of the following ways:

a) Wait until your corpse rots and dissapears and your items will appear in the reclaim booth (might as well wait a little longer since res effects take a while to rise)

b) Quickly use one of the memory chips (described in #3) nearest to your corpse and attempt to loot your own left over belongings

c) Ask a fellow org member (if they were near you at death) to loot your remains for you.

Only fellow org members may loot Blue remains without "flagging (turning Gray)". Any other Blue who loots anything from a corpse will immediatly turn Gray and there will a loud announcement in the vicinity letting everybody know the purpetrator and they will be open to attack by anyone. All people may look at the remains without penalty but the act of "looting the corpse" is what "flags" you or turns you Gray.

The Arena:

The function of the Arena should still remain the same a "Free For All" type of place where you can fight without fear of losing any items, credits and no XP either. There should be a seperate arena away from any city where reds can pvp each other without fear of losing anything this would also be a good area for them to wait out their murder counts(wich will take a long time) eventhough they may also be up for attack by anyone else in the arena even other head hunting Blues (bounty killers).


#7 Credits

- The Bank should be a place to store your credits(money) safely. That's what banks are supposed to do in real life anyway right? When shopping you need not have credits physically on you to purchase an item. But when you accumulate credits while in a mission/dungeon/temple they should go on your person not directly into the bank. If a person dies credits should be a physical icon in the persons body that may only be returned one of the ways described in #6 or may be among one of the things able to be looted. Credits should be in groups of max of 5-10k on a corpse maybe more depending on how much they have before storing any in the bank. Lets say a person has about 20k on them while doing a mission and dies, there would be 4 groups of physical credits on the persons body totaling 5k each. This just makes it more difficult for someone to loot all of the credits and then try to escape without being killed.


#8 City Control

- I heard that Newland was once neutral and now it is part of the omnis. I'm not too sure how or why it changed but it sounds like a great idea if orgs themselves decide WHAT side owns a city. Changing the side of the city would depend completely upon how many neutral, clan, or omni towers around that area have been knocked down and wich side has the overwhelming amount of towers. Of course 3 main cities should never change like Rome Tir and Borrealis because these are home to each side. I think this is a great idea not exactly sure if this is how it woks now, but it should.


#9 Places

- Theres an endless amount of beautiful land in AO lots of cool clubs and other nice looking places that are all sooo.......EMPTY!! I've heard a lot of people complain about this issue. I do agree there needs to be clubs in more populated areas. What is the point of having the tons and tons of empty space on a server that no one uses?? There has to be more initiative to go to these places. You call some of these places cities when they look more like ghost towns to me. The point of a MMORPG is to be able to interact with other real people and to physically and emotionally be able to change the world around you. If I wanted to walk around aimlessly in a game by myself I wouldn't be paying $14.95 a month to do that. Maybe FC is making AO a little too big or not putting things in the right locations. Most people just cluster around the plain gray walls of borrealis near the shops our outside a temple or dungeon looking for a team. What is the point of all the emotes and all the different clothes when they hardly get used?


#10 Faces & Clothes

- I really love all the variety we have in choosing a face to a character we are trying to portray(One of the best things in AO), It would be great there could be some sort of plastic surgery terminal or maybe a skill that a doctor can have. You can only do plastic surgery once or twice in a characters life and it will be expensive or rare to do so, just how it is in real life(unless you are Michael Jackson lol). You can change your face/height/weight just once or twice and you may only use the same face in your breed you cannot change breeds by this.

Dye:

It would be cool to be able to dye your clothes different colors. theres no need to buy 3 pairs of the same thing and some items don't come in the color you want, this adds for greater customization.
It would also be cool if solitus/atrox/opifex breeds could dye their hair different colors throughout the game. In character selection you could chose a face and then choose your own hair style you want that fits with that breed instead of selecting a premade face. This adds a whole new level of uniqueness among each person and personal attachment to your character(for instance maybe I want my female solitus to have a pink mohawk, maybe I want my opifex with orange lips to have spikes on her head?) not sure if the game mechanics will allow this but it is a good idea.


#11 Owning Physical Space

- Having an apartment automatically given to you when you start is nice and all. But who actually uses these for anything more than storage? Having a physical space on the lad of Rubi-Ka that others can see or visit anytime will have a huge IMPACT on gameplay. One could put up their own tent, large house/mansion, or tall narrow tower made of steel or cement for instance. These places could be used for countless purposes:

a) a safe place to log out, store items, a physical place where anyone can visit anytime

b) a physical place the org can visit gather together, have meetings, parties, initiate new members

c) a type of player run mall where the owner and others can place vendors and people can sell their unique goods, a different way of making money for people who make their own armor/implants/clothes or anything else

These physical places can be placed anywhere outside a city on flat terrain. The owner is able to kick or ban people from visiting the place. Places like this are a mark of Success or power of an org, individual or group of people. I am sure this idea is not new to AO as many other MMORPG's have this but i want to know WHY can't this be possible in AO?? It's 1000 times better than the current automatically given apartment system now.

Leaving a copse when dying would once again be something to exploit. Some of us freeze for a while after dying, due to large ping or not top of the line hardware, so having a 30s to 1min time period where you should act quickly to save your things would not be fair for all. A lot of things in this game demand hours of camping, we do not need to lose our items that we have worked for.

This is a bit harsh example but think of ToTW, camping for GTA or Keepers Vitality for ages, you finally loot it off the corpse of you enemy only to see that some idiot pulls a train on you. So basically lootable corpse would serve only those Daddy! Get me a fiber connection and an uber hardware, pleeeeeese!  type of people. It would cause an adrenaline rush all right, of anger, right before loggin in to my account to press the big cancel button there...

#7 Credits
As someone all ready pointed out, the credits in this game are not pieces of gold they are digital credits. Paid by your employer (clans, omni, ICC?) so having piles of gold being left behind goes against the basic idea of the game, this is after all a futuristic Sci-Fi game, not romanticized Middle Ages situated Fantasy

#8 City Control / #11 Owning Physical Space
As seen in recent events, bringing PvP to cities is not a great idea, the zones are too big and the amount of people that want to take part in these events are basically the downfall of these things, servers cant handle the amount. #10 Faces & Clothes

Organizations and teaming with friends are the things that make AO worth playing for me.
