I must say the most pressing matter thus far is guild housing, in my opinion. Many of the larger guilds have taken up residence in/outside static locations, such as bars and whatnot (my own guild, Division 9, is headquartered in Galway Castle), but I don't believe any want to be there. Here are my ideas for guild housing...

-There is simply a place in ent (as there was, or maybe even is now) or Tir or Borealis/Newland with a sign that says "GUILD HOUSING" over it... going inside it would bring you to your specific guild's place, like an apartment. Maybe even a seperate "guild housing access card" to get in. They would have a save term, a reclaim, and a bank. That's all you'd really need. The layout for the basic model could be something absurdly similar to the current highrise backyard. A guild receives this housing when it has maintained 10 members for a period of 7 days (this will help a lot server-side) Following this there are 2 other models...

The first upgrade model would basically contain the same thing as the basic one, but also an indoor room accessible to only a guild-leader defined group of people, be it 2nd level or 3rd level or whatever. In order to open the door you'd have to "swipe" your housing access card in the door by using it like a lock pick .Inside is a special team-mission generator that has a special setting - what type of reward you can get, whether it be weapons, armor, nanos, implants, or misc (clusters, shields, NCU, Wen-Wens, etc.), as well as a slightly boosted xp reward (say +50%) An officer actually being helpful in the process of acquiring a mission?!? Egad! This model would cost the guild 10 million collective credits to start off with, as well as an additional 1 million per week rent. Should you have insufficient funds in the guild bank to pay the rent, you simply get downgraded to the lower level housing.

The second upgraded version would be the same as the first, rooms-wise, save a few differences. There would be a small 0% zone that is fenced off similar to the arenas, where a guild could carry out a competition free of the threat of gankers. A guild drop-box, similar to the one described in the first post in the thread, where you could dump stuff and guild members with a guild-leader defined access level could retrieve any items they needed. There would also be a grid entry point (not exit) inside. It drops you off in the standard area in front of Omni-1 Trade district grid point. To get to your guild house from the grid... simply go to the "Emergency exit" area on the first floor out towards the west. Maybe you could change the sign to say "Guild housing" but for those without a guild it would still be the standard emergency exit. Whatever. This would call for an initial payment of 50 million credits, a weekly rent of 5 million credits, and a minimum guild member count of 100.

Now just leaving this to the winds... if FC so desired to make the most powerful guilds happy (I know I am included in this group, but don't hate me for it, it IS a good idea), perhaps the top 1 or 2 or 3 of all sides (even neutrals) could be given a custom-made house, with a special door and a special sign outside with the name of the guild. The house would be included in the next patch. This is, of course, completely up to FC.

That's not a bad idea at all. I particularly like the "swipe card" feature and the idea of fixed layouts, as it ties in with some thoughts I've been having on housing in general.

I've been thinking of giving up my apartment (even now that it's got a ceiling fan ) and moving out to the mutant camps on the outskirts of Borealis. For a start, those shacks are more salubrious than my three-room hovel. As well as having furniture, they're accessible to other people, so you can use them as a place to do business. However, they're not a secure place to store things, you can't decorate them and they're not exactly private areas. We really need something between the two extremes of the inaccessible private apartment and the unsecured public place.

Many people (probably most of us at some time or other) have suggested making apartments accessible to others, but there are a few problems with the idea which usually aren't addressed. The big one is how to get someone into a friend's apartment which shares the same front door as their own appt. This is likely to be fairly common, as I think the majority of people probably have a place in Backyard 1 of their particular city, and there are only four doors in there to choose from.

The problem is that you don't need to use your keycard to make it work, you just need to carry it. However, what if the keycard was just a container for the character's keys? To enter an apartment you have a key for, open the keycard as if it was a backpack, select the appropriate key and use it on the door as if it was a lockpick. This way, it doesn't matter how many of your friends' apartments are behind the same door, you can control which one you access. Make the keycard and individual keys no-drop items, and make sure you can only duplicate/trade your own apartment key.


Some apartment commands, to control access:

/apartment changelocks [all] [name] - renders duplicated keycards useless, either for all keyholders except the owner or for a specified keyholder or list of keyholders.

/apartment access [open] [athome] [locked] - controls how other keyholders are allowed to access your place. [open] allows access when you're not there, [athome] allows access when you're in, but not if you're out and [locked] prevents access to any but the owner.

/apartment evict [all] [name] - boots troublesome individuals out of your appt. and zones them to the backyard.

/apartment move - resets your apartment, allowing you to select another one as if you'd lost your key and /petitioned. Might save a few ARK's some time, too. Only usable from outside, and for preference requiring some sort of confirmation before it takes effect.


I'd prefer to see individuals able to hold multiple apartments. Under the current system, this would be tricky to do. However, if individuals were able to buy additional apartments of differing levels of quality (ie. nicer layouts) from vendors, and add them to a keycard, this would solve the problem nicely. Under this system, you wouldn't need the "/apartment move" command, as you could just buy another place and delete the existing one.

Speaking of layouts, we know funcom can do them. Personally, I'd love to be able to buy an apartments with some of the layouts I've seen in missions. It would solve the furnishing problem (it's all built-in) while allowing you to personalise the place with decorative items.


One last thing, a small item Funcom could add to vastly improve even the basic apartment - sleeping pads. I saw these in a mission in Athen Shire, three bedroll-type things lying on the ground. They didn't affect pet/mob pathing, and it'd be a lot more satisfying than logging out sitting on a bare floor every time...

Comments?
