Guess what? We're not that gimped after all....
I was at Ace camp with friends last night, and one friend had a home-written application to log and compare total damage. It was me, 3 MA's, an NT, and a doc(great healing btw, doc *huggles*). With damage being tallied over several fights and then compared, yes, I was behind the MA's, but shockingly close nonetheless. Especially considering that I'm a "gimped" 1hE user. My equipment is ql200 Byom/Rider combo with a ql250 scope. I had normal self-buffs on, and MOP/DF/PNH/doc HP as outside buffs. I kept Mongo Crush active for the HP buff, and chain-Raged for the additional inits(and runspeed when I needed it LOL).

While it's true I didn't pull aggro hardly at all, the point is that I wasn't HUGELY out-damaged by the MA's.......and they're all very good ones too. Actually, after about 15 fights, I think I ended up as #2 behind my uber wife Corgan. If I was a 1hB user with higher AR, I might very well have out-damaged them all. Or, if I was able to use the Adv-only SOL's........*drool*

Take this info for what it's worth.......information from one particular night from one particular group. Maybe I attacked faster and inflated my damage that way, I have no way of knowing. Maybe it was inflated when I smacked around a Quartus add while the team took down a Primus. But those were the results, flawed or not. Flame away.

edit: I'm lvl193, and the MA's were lvl190-200
