Feedback from FC about Mez Pet.
This is a temporary rooted thread.

The Mez Pet issue was originally stated as follows:

quote:
The Mez Pet has never worked as intended since its introduction a year ago and has been killing both its owner and team members with the phantom/lag damage bug for the last 5 months. When will the mez pet be fixed and made useful in both PvM and PvP?
Feedback to this issue is:

quote:
Meta-Physicists
The mezz pet effect is a tricky issue. We've looked (and tested internally) several solutions, and none are really good, or are simply hacks which we really are not comfortable with.

The current mezz pet uses a proc to place the pacification nano on the target. However, because of the way this system works, this can mean that the nano is placed on the target even if the mezz pet's attack misses. Now if this nano was changed in such a way (such as a monster-only stun, for example) that it actually worked all the time, there would be little reason for MPs to use the higher level mezz pets because the proc could still go off even with low level versions always missing their targets.

We need to investigate a new AI script that has the mezz pet actually cast a mezz nano on its target. If this is found to work, then the attack ratings of the mezz pet will mean something, and mean that MPs need to use a mezz pet of the appropriate level, as the low level ones simply won't be able to land the nano on high level monster because of their lower nano skills. This requires resources from other departments.

The designers are very much aware of the issue though, and very much hoping for time to fix it soon.
So, there is acknowledgement that the Mez is not working at all because the Mez requires new AI. The "mez" effect is not an actual program that the pet casts. Because of that, low or high level Mez Pets do not have their effects checked by nano resistance or q-lvl of the monster it attacks.

If a fix is delayed, its not due to game mechanics reasons or concerns that the MP's mez pet shouldn't be fixed because the class is already "uber"...its a manpower issue. No specific timetable is given of course, but its on the table as a stated priority. The MP's are incomplete without the fix. We'll have to wait.
