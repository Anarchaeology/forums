First, I would expect you didn't talk to a GM, but an ARK. Few seem to make any notice of this difference, but it's important.

Arks are just another player, with very limited training, and they (unfortunately) have a tendency to 'answer' a question with their opinion, without making it clear it's JUST an opinion/guess on their part, not official.

Next, there's a well known (to those who've been around long enough) bug where a name of a since deleted character is NOT removed from the name database, and thus, becomes unusable for a new character, even if there is no character of that name shown. Ghost names are real.

I rather doubt that 'grid' is prohibited as part of a name, and the 'auto' censor (which is in your client, not at the server end, by the way) only checks a few very specific words of the 'seven words you can't use on TV' sort, and some racial slurs.

Any other rejection of a name is because it's been added to the server name list, either by a live character, a 'ghost name' from a character now gone, or manually added by GM's to prevent folks from using it again when they rename someone with an objectionable name. (These unusable names don't show in the accessable name database, either)

If A GM/ARK objects to your name, or someone petitions it, the ARK/GM will come change it. Or, if it's nasty enough, maybe just delete your character without warning. <grin>
