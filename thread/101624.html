OOC - Where are we going?
--OOC--

So, where are we going?

Let me paint a picture here...Rubi-Ka is now under the control of a far reaching and fair democracy. Omni players and clan players are all teaming together, non-agression is the word of the day, and nary a shot is fired in anger... Any rogue Org not in the democracy passes by the towers and bases of the participating members, less they incur the wrath of the collective superOrg...

Meanwhile, FunCom has realised that with this structure in place, there is no need for any more story content, because all they could do is Leet uprisings, or santa hats if they want to keep their peace loving customers happy.

Where is the fun in that?

How about a little Chaos? How about a little Anarchy?

Im not saying that this community of Roleplayers needs to draw up sides and never look back. You are all pals now (as evidenced by Templus joining a pseudo clan org) no need to change that, but at least play a role that is something other than yourself...How much Roleplay skill does it take to play a toon that votes on election day, fights for their country and alies only when attacked, slaves away at a hamster wheel of a job, trying desperately to get a promotion, only to cut lose at the club on the weekend? It seems to me that you are trying real hard to make AO mirror real life.

Why not once a month we have an RP'ers ball, where all RP folks of all factions come and party together and plot events for the next few weeks...Then go back to fighting eachother the rest of the month with a renewed sense of story and wonder.

I think we are losing sight of what the challenge is here...We need to step outside of ourselves and become a person dwelling on Rubi-Ka...Omni hating clans for stealing land and profits with terrorist tactics...Clans using guerilla tactics to overthrow the oppressive corporation and guarantee the freedom and safety of their families.

Where is the fun in roleplaying peace? That would turn this game into a leveling treadmill, or a simple chatroom, or a lapdance at Reets...

/me whoopie...we are having big fun now, I tell you.

There are a few very talented people who make story lines independant of the conflict that add to the flavor of the gaming experience, like Dabblez, Demenzia, and Ahgenda. But their stories are the color that fills in the lines...By bluring or eliminating the lines, the game loses its individuality, and becomes EverQuest (ugh).

I could be way off base here. I could be alone in this minority. Im ready to resign myself to that, and fade away into annonimity...But I hope I wont have to...

Lets FIGHT! Lets BATTLE! LETS PLAY!

OOC myself...
I have three words for you.

Notum Wars Fatigue.

To expand... most people in general, RP and non-RP, are tired of fighting alot, and would like some time to go back to "normal" non-NW play, level up, get some new gear, spend time with their friends, and so forth.

Since picking fights with other orgs is a good way to ensure your own bases will start coming under attack, alot of orgs are avoiding starting attacks for less than concrete reasons. Especially considering that base defense is disruptive and frequently annoying, alot of us would rather not spend the bulk of our in-game time doing it. There are a lot of NW mechanics issues, that in the end, combine to make NW quite frustrating on a regular basis.

Finally, alot of "RP orgs" seem to avoid attacking each others' bases. I'm fine with that. NW can breed bad feelings, and I don't want to see that happening within the RP community.

That said... I also have four more words for you.

Meeting And Politics Fatigue.

A lot of people are sick of spending hours in meetings, spinning their wheels while getting nowhere. A lot of people are tired of the same old political arguments, that as Nevver pointed out, seem to be getting argued just for the sake of argument. I increasingly feel that these get-togethers are a waste of my time, time that I could be spending on goals I can actually achieve.

I think we've just come to a juncture where people are sick of fighting and sick of arguing. Who knows, if I'm lucky, at least a few people will realize that it's their own egotistical obstructionism that's holding up the works for a lot of others, and graciously stand aside in the name of progress.

Chaos and Anarchy are sometimes fun, and sometimes not... but always are tiring. And those of us who have been neck-deep in it since before NW was released, are tired. We're taking a breather. Or at least, I am.

Arguing that the Sentinels are evil is not a lot of fun since the Sentinels are not actually players unless there is some actual org playing that side (maybe there is and I just don't know about it - I'm speculating).
