Ideas for enhancing roleplaying in AO
More sub-faction clothing so we can identify ourselves with a particular group.

More emotes, and possibly allow them to be tied to certain spoken text phrases. For instance starting a sentence with the word No would cause the /nono emote to instantly be run.

Finish the incomplete bars and lounges in the game. Add more music to them.

Bring back player driven official storyline. Allow people to get their names on the AO website for participating well in events again. Why doesn't the news notice that the clans are winning the notum wars right now?

More storyline NPCs.

More story advancements... why haven't most of the clans gotten fed up with the Sentinels yet? Why haven't the neutrals gotten fed up with the mercs yet?

More in-game explanations of strange objects and places. Why the enigma trees?

Faction identification on NPCs similar to players. "Knights Clan", "Omni Trans", etc (maybe in a different color so we don't confuse them for players).

More story content for the "present anarchy" section of the website. More Clans, more Omni departments, Neutral factions, etc.

If you've got more ideas, please post them.
