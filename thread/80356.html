When people talk about zone's being 25% or 5% there talking about the suppresion gas in the area. the supresion gas is what allows you to be able to fight other players. 100% no fighting
75% is usually in areas with monster u can fight(sometimes called PvM) but u cannot fight other players. a 25% suppression gas zone lets you fight other players (refered to as PvP areas) but i think they have to be the oppisite side unless you fight in an arena . 5% zones means you can fight anyone even if your both clan.

Your grace period is a timed count down that sarts as you enter a zone where the suppression gas is 25% or 5% its usually a one minute count down so incase you accidently wondered in a PvP zone you can still have time to run back out before i or anyone else could attack you.
