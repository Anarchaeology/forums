Uh-oh! It sounds like some adventurers need a quick, down-and-dirty pet class! Here goes:

First, /pet help lists every pet command.

Second, there's a new pet status window that can be opened from the shift-t menu.

Now, I'm not sure what these new towers do, exactly, but I assume that they buff us, correct? Or do they heal?

If they're supposed to heal, making them work should be as simple as targetting yourself and typing /pet heal.

To make them stop doing whatever it is they do, you can use /pet behind.

To see how much HP/NP it has, as well as its NCU, time remaining, and target, type /pet report.

When you're sick of it and ready to move on, type /pet terminate.
