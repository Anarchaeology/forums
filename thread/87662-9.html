I am the guy who wants a new line of nano resist nanos, and want no changes to any profession.

Julia, IMO, you tend to examine things in a vacuum. Yes, DOTs and debuffs cost nano. Yes, you "only" have 8000 nano, but you also have HE/CC and an upgrade. The point is that though "you can't afford to run around and debuff everyone" you CAN take 10-15 people out of the battle with a combination of well-timed DOTs and debuffs, while at the same time keeping yourself healthy. Or, you can stick to debuffs and totally cripple an entire attacking/defending force in a matter of seconds.

Tower PvP is all about critical mass in att/def and for one profession to be able to tilt the scale to this extent is why tower battles are not nearly as fun as they could be. No other class can do this to the extent Doctors can, but Traders and NTs can do a it to some extent also. This is why I think a new line of nano resist nanos is in order. The anti-trader drain nano given to docs was a step in the right direction but only a small baby step. Similar nanos should be introduced for nanoresist generally (yes I am aware of nano repulsor; that's not enough)

It is irrelevant that one of your debuff lines is not working. It is irrelevant that you can be debuffed down to a lesser debuff by an MP or trader; what you are left with is still unbalanced.
