Heh...not really fair, Bionitrous. If a unique instance of the Camelot or Smuggler's Den dungeons spawn for every team wanting to take them on, they'd never be able to finish them in an hour at their intended levels

Funcom needs to acknowledge that the single biggest factor that causes camping is something that is the only source for an item. So long as it's the only source for that item, it will end up getting camped. Camped by people that feel they need to use that item. Camped by people that feel they need to collect an item that comes from a unique source.

Changing the spawn rate for the mob won't prevent camping. Making the item drop randomly on the mob won't prevent camping. Creating a unique instance of the area containing the mob reduces the area's lifetime down to a single successful visit (rough translation: a waste of a worldbuilder's time). Acknowledge these facts and fix all 3 in a single solution. Eliminate the 'single source' mentality. And then you'll have a successful static dungeon.
