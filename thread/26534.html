Low Light Targeting Scopes
This is just about the only thing I can think of to propose a solution to the problem of the scopes. Now I do realize that having and selling them is up to the item holder, however I just saw a ql 188 scope being sold for 500 million by Infamousduck. I'm not complaining about the price, I could care less if someone asked 300 trillion for a ql 1 lock pick... however since you have removed these from the game the people that own them have been preying on society like vultures. Nothing against you people that do own them, but taking advantage of a screw up like that is wrong and you all know it. I still remember when they were available in the stores regularly. Why they were removed is beyond me... I haven't the desire to search the forums and find a reason why they were removed.

Personally, if Funcom decides to remove an item from the game it should be removed completely.. such as the armor that was available a while ago that was taken out since it had no stat requirements. I know this is not the case with these items since they do have requirements that match the ql, however.. if the armor had super high stats and great AC's and was readily available it would be cheap. When an item is removed from the game the price jumps to almost unreal for even a QL 40 item (perfect example of supply and demand). If you're going to remove an item, remove it. If you're going to leave an item in the game after its been taken out of regular circulation, make the items NODROP so people can't continue to sell them and prey on the demand created by what you did. And we would still be able to use them if they wanted so no harm done. Otherwise put the scopes back in the game temporarily so everyone can buy one and the prices will drop.

I haven't had one since I deleted mine along time ago, nor do I ever wish to have one again. If you wish to flame me about this, which I know will happen, I could really care less. My opinion has been voiced, and that is all that I care about.

Sincerely,
