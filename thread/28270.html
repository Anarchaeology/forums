Clan Attack in Clon****
If you have read the Clan News then you are aware that we went in and spent a few hours destoying the Notum cannons on Sunday. I would like to pint out a few highlights from the battle that didn't make the news.

1) About 100+ People showed up for the attack. We met in West Athens to save and brake into teams.

2) Many Omni showed up. Some of whom helped us destroy the guards and Umpire Oppressor cannons. Some who stood by and watched, helplessly, as the clans ran unchecked through Omni's most important asset on Rubi-Ka.

3) I'd like to thank BlackCloak, Twilight Dynasty, and RK-Mafia for helping me and my guild, Synergy Factor, plan and implement this attack. I have been planning this for 3 weeks.

4) We announced the attack in all clan and neutral cities an hour before we moved out. On hearing of the attack many guilds came with all thier forces. I would like to the Are Wave, Mercury Dragon, Blood Brain Barrier, and Rising Sun for showing up and helping out. If I missed anyone who came, I thank you too.

5) This was a player run event. My cohorts and I petitioned numerouse times asking for anyone from FC to show up, amongst other things. I emailed FC two weeks prior to the attack, got a response telling me to petition and talk to a GM. Dreamsong, an Events ARK finally showed up AFTER the attack. We had retreated to the grid and where making sure all clanners go away safely when I finally got a tell from Dreamsong, the Events ARK. I talked to her for a little while. She gave me the link to the news submition area at AO's site (like I didn't already have it) and said I shoult tell her about the next player run event we stage so she or another Evetns ARK could show up (like that'll happen). Also she asked which ARKs/GMs I talked to in my petition and who had responded to my previouse emails about this event. Aparently I was 1) supposed to get way more support than I did and 2) They were suposed to put me in touch with an Events ARK. ANyway, I didn't give her any names, if they have internal politics/weak links/deficiancies it's not really my problem.

6) We killed a number of Umpire Opressor cannons, all the guards, and all Mobs in the Notum Cannon area. We held the area for 2 hours. Even lowbies got in and hung out.

7) We had people at reclaim constantly buffing those who were ressing.

8) There was an after raid party at the Reet Retreat. Cosmik was there in all his glory, putting the rest of the dancers to shame.

It was a really fun day. Omni's and Clanners showed up in all their glory to totally over run a large installation. Everyone had fun.

We are going to be staging further player events. Some will be announced in the Events Calander, some won't. Obviously suprise attacks like this one won't be announced. But when you get the call to battle, go report for duty and recieve you orders.

Also, I am currently looking for active presidents of Nuetral guilds to talk with. I also need to talk to the presidents of guilds who are Omni alligned, but destroy Omni from within. Send me a tell ingame. Don't respond here, I don't want any relationship or affilition to be made public.

/EDIT : I think I came donw on FC to hard in point 5. I would like to state that I WOULD have gotten more input/help from Event ARKs if I had goen through the correct channels. I _think_ I'm supposed to petition and let the petition people that I am staging an event and that I need to talk to Event ARK instead of regular ARK.

President Ankokujin, Synergy Factor
