OET, Original Electronicum, K
The OET pistols are very rarely available in stores, and are somewhat rarely available as mission rewards.

I saw an Original Electronicum pistol offered as a mission reward, but I didn't go get one. At the time, I was mission-booth camping for a pair of Kaen-Archibald Kolt pistols for my adventurer. Nice damage, nice clip size, very low multi-ranged requirement, and +1% XP per pistol (nice!). On top of that, unlike the Sentinel ETE pistols I used to love so much, the QL (relative to skill requirements) is low enough that I can "sneak" high enough difficulty missions to get them when I need them! Woo hoo!
