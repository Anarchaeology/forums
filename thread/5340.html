Welcome to my Town, Guild houses on the right, dead enemies on the left...
Hi, im prety sure many of you have had similar ideas and some of the ideas im putting here are already "envisioned" but i want to write down the bigger picture as i see it, sorry if there is already similar posts.... so on with it.

CONCEPT: A player run Town...3 to be more exact 1 for Clan, one For neutrals and one for Omni. (we will talk about the Clan city but other would work in similar ways)

LOCATION: The Location would be a 0% zone... Mayhaem. the cities for each faction would be in appropriate zones of the map for each side (close to clan teritory) The mayhaem zone would extend further away from the town itself.

TRANSPORTATION: There would be a Whompa and Grid access to the town centre but only Clan members and neutral can use this. There will be a Grid access for Omni within walking distance from the town but not too close, the grid exit would be 75% and will be large enough for when you step into 0% zone, you will be far enough from the gird point so you cant run and zone. omnis can gather there and will have to move onto the mayheam zone and walk/travel to the clan town to attack it, what this accomplishes is:
a) there wont be any "camping" at the whompa, so omni forces can organise themselves
b) there wont be any zoning technics as is seen with shops and whopans in 2ho atm, once a fight starts there is no safe place, its either kill or be killed or if you can, excape but you better run fast or get on that yalmaha and climd, there's no zoning gona save you.

THE TOWN: The town will be made of smal square buildings, this bluidings are spaced evenly and each building is a Block, at first the lote is mostly empty, the building will be very small, as you upgrade your building and buy more buildings the Block may be completly filled by the buildings, at first buildings are all empty (no owner) and look the same. to own a building you will have to pay a price when buying it, AND a monthly "rent", only guilds can own buildings, only the Guildmaster and the next emidiate guild rank can make the porchase, the ownership reverts always to the guild itself, so no matter who in the guild buys the building the ownership is of no one player but the guild. This accoplishes a simple thing, not everyone will own a building, not everyone can upkeep a building... wich will realy favor large organisations and keep the overcrowding down a bit. Only one Block per guild.

BUYING/OWNING BUILDING: To buy a building the Guildmaster enters an empty Building and is prompetd witha list of available building types he can buy, if its the first building then the list will be only composed of one choice: Guild House.
There will be the name of the building the buying price and the RL monthly rent price:
Guild House - x cretis to buy - n credits monthly upkeep
There will be several types of building and each has a pre-requesite to own.
Not going in too much detail it could be something like this

*all building have a price/upkeep cost; all buildings can be upgraded to better versions)
all buildings will be "placed" inside same block, this palce will be automatic as the location for each building upgrade is predefined, there must be some tought to leave enough growing space for future additions to this system as new building types are implemented in-game

Guild House: must be the first building; guild must have at least 25 members( this is the guild heart, will have a hall, a meeting room, an office (for guildmaster and high council) and will have a guild bank etc etc.)

Guild Shop: Must have guild house (guild members that are authorised can place they'r items for sell here.

Guild Appartments: Must have guild house, must have at least 40 members (will be an appartment complex for guild members only)

Guild house upgrade I: must have shop and guild appartments (Will make the guild house bigger and with more facilities)

Guild Barracks: must have guild house upgrade I ( will have a small arena for duels, no outsiders interference will generate one npc guard of level 100) this guard will be named "<NameOfGuild Patroler" or something like that
this guards would have a 1hour spawn rate when killed, they are meant to patrol when there are not many players online to defend the town, but must not spawn as fast as to interfeer too much when a full scale invasion occours, they will help but when dead will take some time to come back so the fight is concentrated between players from opposing sides.

there can be lots of othere buildings and upgrades, like bars and stuff, or upgrade to make spawn more npc guards and higher lvl ones etc. this is just a draft.

The buldings could be either dinamicaly implemented right then and there or there could be a 24 hour periode wait for the GMs to implement em after the guildmaster buys the new building.

DEFENCES: The first and obvious defence will be the players themselves, another will be the npc guards spwaned if the required building is bought. Others may include cannons spwaned by the appropriate building that will shoot anything airborn... to stop those pesky ppl that like to hoover above in their yalmahas away from gun range.

The zone could be 25% instead so there wouldn't be so many accidental attacks, but i think 0% serves better the roleplaying aspect, just be carfull you hit enter before typing anything with your attack key in it ;P

theres so much more that could be added, tell me your ideas and aditions to this Concept, Funcom Devs feel free to comments and add
