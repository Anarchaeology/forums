Faction Formulas (finding mob's and how much you'll gain)
The purpose of these are to find out how much faction you will gain if you kill a mob. In order to do this you must kill the mob once.


1.) Write down your current faction - this will be *Initial Faction* -- and the number of people in your team -- this will be *# of members in team*


2.) Kill the mob, write down your gained faction - this will be *Gained Faction*


3.) Use this equation to find the Mob's Faction:

10 x *Gained Faction* x *# of members in team* + *Initial Faction* = *Mob Faction*

(ten times faction gained times number of teammates plus inital faction equals mobs faction)


4.) Now that you know the Mob's Faction, use this formula to find out exactly how much faction you would gain if you killed him again

(( *Mob Faction* - *Your Current Faction* ) / 10 ) / *# of members in team* = *Faction you will gain*

(Mob's faction minus your current faction divided by 10 divided by number of people in your team equals the amount of faction you will gain)
