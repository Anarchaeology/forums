Back in AO for a week now...
When I ended my AO acount in January this year I was not planning to come back to Rubi-ka.
Things change and a week ago I opend a 7 day trail acount to see what happend to this beautyfull world.
After 2 day's I opend my old acount again and this world is still amazing but what every game needs and make people stay are friends and most of my friends I had before play alts or are way higher then I am now .
But some old friends still play and even after a year nothing changed they are still friends.
It made me decide to change side.
All "my girls" where true Omni's and to change that was a big decission.
So now I switched from Omni trade to Tir Fair Trade and with a very nice guild and my old friends I enjoy my time in AO again.
Even leveling up is a still a dull thing to do but flying towards an upcoming sun above Rubi-ka compesate that a lot.
My favourite place is still castle Galway and when the server comes back up I go take a visit inside.
If I'm right it's a clan side and the guards won't attack me now.

Some simple changes like zoning without falling down to the ground makes it easy to exsplore and I never took the time to exsplore the Lands in clan teretory so lots to do for me now.

This world is truely amazing , happy to be back!
