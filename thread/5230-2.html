I want to throw some ideas around about it... this already assumes that it is fixer only...

the black market should be NPC based (I think someone already suggested that). it should be roaming NPCs that change position often so that you have to stay close to the grapevine to find out where 'he' is now.
there could even be a password that you have to /tell to the npc to activate him and the password could also change week to week.
to non fixers the NPC would act differently or sell different things.
and at all times there is a chance that after you have 'aquired' something from the source it would not be as it appeared to be in the description. perception ability could alter the chances of being duped. or the more your frequent the source the happier he becomes with you and will give you different items or different discounts.

because it is a blackmarket then it is illegal, and there should be a penalty for anyone caught.. (any ideas on a penalty?)

because of the NPC way of doing it then you could often get GM's pretending to be the source in order to catch you out. that way we would have to stay close to our network and run checks on names or listen for rumours of undercover 'cops'.

it could be part of agents missions to pose as sources and catch us out?
and so there should be an ingame way of running name checks... that way it brings crats into the game as they could falsify records in order to catch people out.
