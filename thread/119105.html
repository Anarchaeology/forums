Crats and Charms
Ok, methinks some of you crats complain too much about charms and the 'dreaded psychic check.'

141 *Atrox* crat here, been soloing PW dynacamps (anuns and the sandworm mostly, tho I have done the mantises from time to time when I'm feelin frisky) - self buffed - since 130. Those are level 170-200 bosses, and give a whole lotta xp at that level to the soloer (600k is normal for the saltworm one, 500k fer anuns), not ta mention tons of nice boss loot

And yet, on this very board I see nanomage crats complaining that their charms aren't very powerful.... Which leads me to wonder, why would someone who implants for droids complain when their charm is sub-par? Because that's the only explanation I can come up with for a nanomage (or solitus, or opifex) crat not being able to throw the same charm as my dumb atrox self can. And considering that I uploaded Insidious Beguilement (711 SI/PM/BM) self-buffed at 126, ya'll should be able to as well. Now, if you chose to implant for droid rather than charm, that's yer own dumb fault, but not any indication of a problem with the psychic requirement on the charms.

What super-uber rare items am I using to get my nanoskills so high? Nuttin, honey. Yes, I got into most of my ql 200 imps at 126 (eye had to wait till 127 for more sense, head is still a measly 167 or sompin), but beyond the imps the only other stat boosting items I had to use ta hit 711 was my jesses. My org doesn't have any towers up, so no bonuses there either. Somewhere between 127 and breed-capped int (at 400!) I was able to do away with the jesses for good. (For the curious, imps are shining PM head & faded PM ear to make up for the low ql on the head, bright and faded SI and BMs)

Yes, my droid is horribly gimped (I can just barely cast the top minion with mochies, my self-buffed one is quite green), and in ql 190+ missions I'm little more than a baton dispenser and mezzer. But who wants to do missions when you can go out into the great outdoors, charm up a nice desert anchorite or pit lizard, and lay waste?

Read, digest, understand: The power of a charm nano does not lie in what you can charm using it, but in what you can kill with what you can charm.

For example, droids are barely a distraction to dynacamp bosses (due to shield ripper), whether it's a feeble worker-droid or a fire-breathing bodyguard. The boss's own bodyguards, on the other hand, are a whole different story. (90% of the time, some guards take shield ripper damage and some are too much lower level than the boss to be effective.) Granted, if the boss happens to have that accursed pink aura and hence 15 bazillion hps, it's going to be a long fight, but still doable with patience (tho I suppose a 1-hour battle against such a boss, for no correlative increase in xp gained, isn't everyone's cup of tea..)

Anyways, I just wanted to point out while those psychic checks might be annoying at times, they are far, far from being the harbingers of gimpiness that some crats would want you to believe. If you wanna implant droid, go for it, you'll be more useful in missions. But don't come back afterwards crying that you can't charm anything worthwhile, it's like implanting for SMG and then bitching that yer rifle skill blows.

-Gronkle, 141 atrox omni 'crat, RK-2

Good points, Charles, and allow me to expound on my implants and my IP expenditures:

Psymod: ql 167 shining + ql 200 faded (to make up for the shortfall)
SI & BM: ql 200 bright & faded both
MC: ql 200 bright
TS: ql 200 faded

You'll notice that since I'm not using the faded genius in ear I don't fall short in PM like you would expect due to the low ql head. Yes, my MC and TS are low, but that's ok by me.
As to 'gimping' everything else: Funny, I've never felt gimped. No, I didn't really start working on a weapon until after 100, (just got pistol capped at 141), and my evades are in the 450 range with being double implanted (ql 200 shining & bright). Complit, psychology, nano c inits, all nanoskills (but mm) and treatment have been maxxed or near maxxed since lvl 1. From the titlecap at 83 I started working on getting my stats buffable to 404 for the ql 200 implants - hence my ability to get them in at 126-127.

Yes, the treatment req on ql 200s is high, but using a full omni-med suit, the +80 doc buff, and a shining head treatment (think I used a ql 160sh) goes a long way towards making up the difference. Throw in a quartz and whatever biomech helm and cloak you can equip, along with mebbe sense, int, and agi buffs and you're there.

Now, as to feeling useless to team: You are 100% correct that I'm not much use in missions beyond crowd control and charming the occasionnal slayer. Is that the whole game? I think not. It is, most certainly, the whole game for most players post-100, but there's a whole world full of things to see and kill beyond BS. Being able to solo PW dynacamps not only brought me from 130-141 faster than I would have been able to do with mission teams (between lft, waiting for others, afks, buffs, deaths, and the like), but it got me a whole lot more creds (22 mil+ in 2 days from selling bossloot to vending machines) and loot (almost a full set of ql 200 So8 atm, along with all my high end boss-loot nanos and various sundry trinkets).

Let me say again, charming '150sh level mobs' (actually up to 166sh with drain psychic, 167 if I whack 'em with the briefcase) does not mean I can only kill level 150sh mobs. Desert eremites/cenobites (lowly 140-150s) take borgs handily, and a 'measly' 156 pit lizard absolutely destroys most anything under 200. Since I've been killing lvl 180-200 *bosses* with my weak 150-160 charmies, I don't see the rest of TL4 titlecap being much of a problem.

Add in that, since I can solo these camps, I can form a team to speed up the process - a team that could not even attempt said dynacamp without me - and you might understand why I feel *far* from useless in groups. Mission groups, sometimes, groups in general, never. Just gotta know yer strengths and play to them. When I get to the next long charm (which will happen somewhere between 150-160, unless I manage to get towers up), I'll be able to do even higher level dynas with the same ease I've done these, so I'm not seeing an end to my solo or group life anywhere in the near future.

As a final note, I did not intend to say that going droid-based is wrong. Droid-based 'crats are most certainly more useful to mission teams, and BS missions surely are what 90% of the post-100 player base does. I even considered going that route myself for my ql 200 imps. What is wrong, however, is for a crat that made the conscious decision to sacrifice charms in favor of droids to come back and complain about how weak or broken the charm system is - it would be exactly the same as if I (as a charm-based crat) started complaining that droids are woefully underpowered and useless. You all would just tell me I'm set up wrong - which is exactly what I'm telling you regarding charms and the droid-based crat.

If you wanna speed it up break a mez and short charm (I dislike throwing a short charm at the beginning now, recharge is too long compared to mez). If you pull the boss aggro, snare it and run around a bit till it switches back to pets. If boss kills the pet(s), just snare and go break the mez on yer next charmie ;p
