Suggestion: Loans
Some of low-mid lvl chars are starving for cach, and high-level don't know what to do with it. I suggest that high level player could issue long-term loans for low-mid level player. For example lvl 20 can get a 100k and repay back 500k to the level 50+ The term of payment could be flexible - like 10 % of cash income until lvl 30, 30% until lvl 40 and 50% after that. The same lvl 50 could loan 4 mil (for Yalm ) and pay back 10 mil to lvl 100. The lower lender level the more risk, the more premium. Paper form for loan could be produced by Bureaucrats (paperwork tradeskill )
And of cause loans could be traded - here is a bond market
