Farewell to Rubi-ka Party - Wed Sept 4
It is with a heavy heart and something apparently in an eye causing it to tear up, I am announcing a Farewell to Rubi-ka Party for a close friend of the Catt family. My wife Liss and I, as well as the entire Relax! Inc. Guild are hosting the party for Tuxx, who has to leave the Rubi-ka Universe. RL issues (as always) intrude and it saddens her as much as a lot of us to see her go. You can read her message about it at the Neon Zebra site, it's front page, headline news there. Click on the link in my sig below to get the site. I won't go into the details, you can read for yourself. This post is just to let you know that on Wednesday, Sept 4, 10pm EST we are hosting a Party for Tuxx to let her know how much we appreciate her and will miss her on Rubi-ka. It will be at the Relax! Bar in Omni Forest. I'll be DJ'ing, and we'll all have a good time while wishing our good friend the best of luck in the future.

What: Farewell Party for Tuxx, the Neon Zebra
When: Wednesday Sept. 4, 10pm est
Where: Relax! Bar - Omni Forest. Grid to Omni-Ent, head out east gate. Go south past 2 groups of buildings. We are in the 3rd group of buildings with a neon sign outside that says Relax!
