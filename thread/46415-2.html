hmmmmm......
OK. FC meant for the shop terms to be a money sink (obviously doh!!) but it all went a bit wrong.

Shop prices are ludicrous.....the prices aren't really a problem for higher levels...but low, low levels (and I mean 1-30) have a hard time. Why take 12k off a low level (bankrupting them) for a gun??

To a level 40 12k is nothing....but to a lvl 5 it's a fortune.

We need to be draining money from the high levels. At the moment it just stays in the system as you pointed out. Even if it's given to alts it isn't really gone....afterall we all know that you get a better price as a buyer from other players...so this cash is just moving around.

Other MMORPGs in the pipeline have a few systems their including (as far as I know) from the start....FC have the same ideas but have yet to implement them. Here are my ideas and comments on some proposed ideas by others.

---

Guild Housing.
I think there would be a huge thumbs up from the players for this one. Size and location would affect the price, so a small mid level guild could still have a guild HQ, it would just be a small or part of a large building in an unattractive (unfrequented) part of the town.

Guild Uniform.
This would have to be controlled so we don't have fools taking advantage and putting lewd or offensive graphics in the game. A guild could, for an ongoing cost (to cover manufacture), put a uniform terminal in their Guild HQ and this terminal supplyed clothing that was UNIQUE (so a player could only have one uniform). If the guild stops paying for the terminal the uniform is taken from it's players ***ongoing Money Sink***. Just a thought...probably difficult for FC to implement but you did say ideas.

Buff Terminals.
Don't like the idea. FC have one thing right by pushing player interaction, why the hell do people play this game (with the ongoing costs) if they don't want to deal with other players???

State Taxation.
Nope....big black mark for this one. You say we get an income....who the hell is paying this income?? OK Omni Tek are a corp and therefore it's feasible (in a RP sense), but the clans and neutrals??

Advertising.
Like this one...Like it a lot.

Player Home(Apartment).
Yes. We've got millions and millions of creds and incredible gear....and we live in a 2 room apartment with no furniture....err continuity error I feel. We don't even have to make new buildings in the cities....allow us to upgrade our apartments, fill them with furniture and items only available through a shop term (the really flash stuff costing a bomb of course).

Almost every 'paper and pen' RPG I've played over the years (and there's been a lot) have included a system for a players homes....so why not AO??

Plus the bigger the apartment, the bigger the 'bills' we pay for public utilities (electricity, water, etc in present terms) another ***ongoing Money Sink***.

Land Control.
They say it's going to be a money sink. Since there's very little info available of the system they intend to use at the moment, I can't really comment on how effective this will be.

Night Clubs.
Add a few more and put an admission fee on the door. You want to go in...you have to pay. Make some clubs 'exclusive'....you have to pay a lot AND be a certain level to get in....would add an element of achievment when you can finally get into 'THE' club to be seen in.

What if a club (only one or two planet wide) could be 'rented' for a night? When you rent it you get a mission key, the guy/gal on the door dupes the key to allow admission....and make the dupes unique so guests can't just dupe their own and hand out.

City Improvement.
What if a player or guild could contribute towards a public fund, and when a certain level of cash was reached in the fund, the city received an upgrade? Be it grid access, a whompa, mission terminals, bar (which further upgrades could turn into a nightclub), shop, more guards/NPC vendors, etc.....any thoughts??

I admit the major starting cities don't really need it....but what about Borealis, Athens (maybe fix up those wrecked buildings?), even an Outpost. If players could contribute and upgrade an outpost to a small town, then to a medium town and keep going upto a small city say, do you think they would do it?? Afterall it has the added benefit that we're changing the game world...we make a difference.

Mercenaries.
Not really that enthusiastic about the idea. Afterall MOBs aren't as effective as players...if a merc guild could work within the game mechanics of AO that would be something to get excited about.

More Social Clothing.
More Miir stuff. Simple and straight forward...we all want to look good in those exclusive clubs don't we?? lol Oh yeah and put some Male stuff in that will (finally!!) look better on us than the females lol .

Advanced Treatment suits.
We all know how to squeeze the last drop out of treatment to get implants in....so these wouldn't really unbalance the system. Make em expensive and give them level requirments (I hate those as much as everyone else) so they can't be abused by players handing them down to low level alts.

Vehicles.
More and varied. As pointed out in an earlier post, why bother getting a high ql Yalm?? Doesn't make that much of a difference....but if they looked different + were much faster (outside cities)....the higher the ql the better they looked. This would encourage respending, buying the next yalm and then the next to look better and fly faster than others. It would be a status symbol that you could publicly show off everywhere you flew .

Can't really come up with anything else at the moment...bit late here.

Edit :- Corrected some spelling and reading my own post I see a few problems with some of the ideas (gameplay and mechanics stuff) but the basic idea is there.

about economics:
i admitedly did not read after the first post.
there is a glaring flaw in the thread-starter's economic theory. it may have already been pointed out, but as i didn't want to forget my counter point, i am just stating it now.

so...if someone else already said this, then sorry in advance
__________________________________________________ ____

the wealth of a nation (or in this case, dimension) is not determined by how much $$$$ there is floating around in circulation. it is determined by the purchasing power of that nation, or more simply, total assets.

from an economics standpoint, rubi ka follows none of the rules of basic economics, most namely...

rule #1: scarcity. there needs to be a limited supply of resources. in anarchy online, you can create wealth and items out of nothing by rolling a mission. since this money is freshly printed just for you as you complete ur mission, there is no way that "inflation" in the true sense of the word could happen.
in the true sense of the word, "inflation" is not measured by how much money there is in circulation, it is measured by how much you can buy with a given amount of money, relative to some prior (base) year. so, as long as prices stay the same (which they do for the most part) the value of money won't change, and thus, inflation will not occur.
you might say "but on the shopping channel, prices are going up~!". well, that's an example of an auction. in auctions, there are no price guidelines...goods and services are sold to the highest bidder.

...and that's really all that matters. money has no value, since anybody can "create" it. furthermore, you can roll for nearly any item, so a barter system isn't really applicable (due to lack of scarcity).

so, in summary, there is NO economy on rubi ka, due to it's infinite resources. what exists is simply a market; a market for rare goods and services, but not an economy.

if you really wanted an economy, funcom would have to put a cap on how much $$$$ is in circulation. also, items would have to be limited as well. finally, as the amount of wealth changes, funcom would have to constantly change the prices of goods in all the shops.
this is arguably alot more work than it's worth...and besides, aren't we playing ao to get away from the real world???

What about actual in-game production chains ?
I know. I'm an optimist, but think about it.
There are factories in several zones, there are mining facilities and cultivated forests.
And there are shops in the cities.
There should be a constant flow of goods floating between all these facilities, and the flow should affect prices through supply and demand.
Items particularly in demand might be profitable to transport by players from one facility to another.
Missions might tie in with the economy and you could get
"There is a shortage of weapon x in city x. Go to production facility y and transport it to distribution facility z, city x"
If you are doing a mission you obvisouly dont pay for the goods or get the profit of selling them. Nothing should stop you from looking at supply and demand yourself...and take advantage of it.
The only multiplayer game I have played so far that came even close to having a system like this is Jumpgate, and even there it was rudimentary at best. But it was fun to load up a large transport ship with highly prized goods, get a few guildmembers to fly as wingmen and haul it to the destination for a nice profit.
Not sure if this will ever make it into AO...but I can dream can't I ?
