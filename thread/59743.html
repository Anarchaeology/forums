Too late to make much of a coherent answer, but off the top of my head I would just like to say this:

Yes, there is a lack in High level content, no arguing there. Period.

HOWEVER, that doesn't mean there is none. What you choose to do after you have leveled sufficiently enough to be a "power" in the game (whereever you feel that limit or level to be) is up to you. If you do nothing but mission for more XP, and at the same QL missions, of course it's going to get boring. I myself do it waay to much too, but that is a choice I've made, because to me, 200 is the primary goal. Then I can go about my business. Now, I don't expect all to agree with me, since a LOT have voiced opinions counter to this, but I still maintain that this game holds high level content to keep you busy for a little more than a wee while. There is still MOB's out there yet to have their cherry popped. I for one would love to go and rough it out with them, but I would need ... oh say somewhere in the vicinity of 99-149 fellow fighters to feel comfortable doing so.
You're going back to EQ you say? Well, then I take it you are well familiar with that type of gaming. You NEED to get together a massive amount of people for some, heck a lot, of the cooler drops... er monsters I mean
