Disillusioned
Greetings all ... I've been here since game launch and after more than a few months of play I figured it's time to evaluate what I've been staying for. I've worked this game over from top to bottom and it seems to be running out of features to further challenge me. I have a few alts that have kept my interest by re-living my experiences at lower levels. The game has a lot of problems, they have been mentioned time and time again. Although a few of these go away they are easily replaced by new ones courtesy of Funcom and Players.

A question to you all who care to post. What keeps you entertained in game? Try to stay away from mentioning all the problems and issues this game has because it has tons and they have all been recycled on the boards. As for me the following 2 things have kept me enjoying my in game time:

1.) Collecting items
2.) Guild -- very very very small guild like 5 or 7 of us =)
3.) Other Players -- The Good, The Bad, and The Ugly. They all offer me various types of amusement.

Take care and Safe Journey
