Take a full group. Stuff in there isn't exactly tightly packed, but it is social...and very much so.

Additionally, some of those, ahh, "things" have about two tons of hps apiece, despite what they may actually con to you. You'll need a full group to take them down in reasonable time.

My nanomage MA dropped by the other day to look around and gather a few credits from the copious armor drops. She's now lvl60ish (more or less) and did find a few green mobs (well short of Neleb). Deep green, and I can't even begin to imagine what this thing had for hps. Easily 50k. EASILY. This toon dishes out viscious damage for her level and even with 600 to 900 pt crits right and left, this MP pet-like critter was still at about 60% health when I finally ran out of nano, fell behind on the charger curve, and was about 40% health myself. Nasty.

Take a full group. Neat place, but take lots of friends.
