Letting friends into your apartment
On the official FAQ it states rgarding letting others into your apartment:

"We are discussing ways to make the apartment more fun and inviting to players. This includes the option of letting other players enter your apartment. At the moment there is no way for someone else to enter your apartment, but we are looking into it."

The last sentence is not entirely true. Currently you can let friends into your apartment by doing the following:

1) Have them enter your team
2) Enter your apartment while still teamed
3) once inside, tell you friend to enter
4) After a little bit (sometimes it takes a bit of running into the door) your friend will enter the apartment

If your friend wishes to be in the apartment alone for some reason, then do the process above, and then leave your apartment while your friend remains inside.

Mind you I haven't read all of the threads on all of the forums, so if someone already posted this, I appologize.
