Um, there is no time when you can have events and not say that someone is missing them.

0100 GMT (1700 PST). Well, it is good timing for Americans.
0300 GMT (1900 PST). Same.
0500 GMT (2100 PST). Same.
0700 GMT (2300 PST). Boarderline for everyone - early in Europe, late in America.
0900 GMT (0100 PST). Bad.
1100 GMT (0300 PST). Same.
1300 GMT (0500 PST). Same.
1500 GMT (0700 PSP). Same.
1700 GMT (0900 PST). Better for Europeans, bad for Americans.
1900 GMT (1100 PST). Same.
2100 GMT (1300 PST). Same.
2300 GMT (1500 PST). Best time for both - late night in Europe, just after most people get off work in America - but it is cutting it close.

There is NO good time to host an event. A good chunk of the people will be at work, school, or sleeping at every interval.

If you work off of the work concept, most people in the US get off work around 5 or 6. That is around 11 or 12 GMT... somewhat late to expect most of your European crowd to be on. The evening in GMT is work day in America.

Before blindly flaming FC for having horrible timing, think about their scheduling options. My guild is mostly Norwegian with assorted foreign-types, like myself, so I am very familiar with GMt scheduling. I have to miss half a class (albeit, and unimportant one) to attend guild meetings at 11 am on Fridays, when the European GMT+1 crowd is free.

So weekends are your best alternative - or late Fridays. But FC has to pay more people to work then.
