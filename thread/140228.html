Scientists! Researchers! Engineers!
Rubi-Ka Universal Robots (RUR) are looking for partners (organisations or just individuals) to help research and develop Token Board Technology for neutrals. We'd like to invite scientists and technical people from all factions to work with us on in what is surely the most certainly the most ambitious engineering project since the Rubi-Ka Subway!

If you'd like to take part, either as individual or an organisation, please sign up below. And feel free to contact me or any RUR employee if you have any questions.

Thank you very much for you attention. I look forward meeting you.

Regards,

Dabblez
Director of RUR

/ooc
This, of course, is just a roleplaying initiative. Only 'the powers that be' could actually introduce Neutral token boards; we sure can't. But in a mmorpg where most activities involve going out killing things and generally tends to favour brutal killing machines over the quaintly named 'support classes', we are hoping to turn the tables a bit. Our aim with this project is to offer an opportunity the rest of us to shine. A chance for engineers to actually play engineers and for nano-technitians to be technical about... stuff. This is our time, a time for all of us who play techies, geeks and nerds to come out of our dusty, old laboratories and claim our rightful place in the suns!

Still not convinced? Here are a few more reasons to sign up:

* Meet interesting roleplayers from all factions! You don't really think were going to run this project without throwing in a party (erm make that.. 'important scientific conference') or two?

* Make yourself a target (in character, of course) for Clan or OT extremists like Chimera! There is nothing quite like that feeling of being wanted... dead or alive.

* Are you something of a sci-fi buff? Why not write up something about how, given what we know about AO's technology, you imagine token boards could actually work?

* Incorporate the Token Board Technology (TBT) project into your character's background. Be a part of Rubi-Ka history!

* Even if you don't roleplay or don't have time to get too deeply involved, signing up is a way to show your support!

* Any profession welcome, yes even that atrox enforcer (we will need to test the prototype on someone eventually, no?)

* Increase your post count!

So, what are you waiting for? Sign up now!

Asterik heads to the Startup Equipment kiosk. He purchases a couple Merit Award Boards and heads back to the War Academy where he had some of his most enlightening realizations along his path from simple miner to Neutral Metaphysicist.

His old mentor from his Clan days is still present offering words of wisdom regarding how to treat life as War in all aspects. They exchange pleasantries and ol' Ast has to fend off the urgings to 'join the cause' that have always been a part of their conversations since the now-Metaphysicist left the Clans in a haze of disillusionment. A few cups of strong tea later and Asterik assumes the lotus position in his always available private room.

'Is science enough?' he thinks. He knows his friends amongst the non-Clan and non-Omni community deserve something to put around their neck to help them along their path from birth to insurance revokation. He's determined to help improve their quality of life. But, is Science enough.

No stranger to the baser sciences, he fiddles with both the boards and the tokens he has had gathering dust for so long.

Nothing.

Time to reach out, he thinks. Placing the blue and placing the yellow and placing the boards, he arranges the items in a pleasing pattern on the rug in front of him. Strangely beautiful in their combination of simple geometry and base colors.

It's time to turn inward ...
