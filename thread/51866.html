Nano Shutdown isn't really the issue. Probably more due to the fact that pets cannot be relied upon in pvp in a game where 4 of the 5 rooting classes are popular with PvP. And Funcom keeps adding crowd control abilities to other classes that have PvP implications. Adv's can now mez the heal pet. MA's can now fear the combat pet.

As Nothinman already pointed out in his MP experiences in pvp, and as I already pointed out also, pets are usually taken out of commission 95% of the time in a mass battle. When that happens, an MP is basically just a wanna-be soldier with debuffs. If we wanted to play a wanna-be soldier with debuffs, we would have been Traders...and would have been better at it to boot.

Funcom over did it with the crowd control. The collective presence of all the classes with crowd control abilities keeps pet classes out of political zones and thats the basic "free market" reason why pet classes aren't popular in pvp.

NT's like to complain about how they are solely relying on nano skills and complain how the debuffing classes are overpowering. There are only two massively debuffing classes they really need to fear: MP's and Traders. One of those isn't even popular in PvP.

In contrast, the pet classes have to worry about 5 rooting classes, 4 mezzing classes, 3 fearing classes, and 2 charming classes (occasionally even Traders like to amuse themselves by sending our pets right back on us)...in addition to the very same 2 debuffing classes the NT complains about being vulnerable too.

So, even though an MP has Nano Shutdown, its usefulness is limited by its short range, high resist rate and very long casting times; compared to the long range and insta-cast crowd control abilities of other classes. So, the exposure that the MP class has to others is quite frankly overwhelming. Hey, I tried pvping, but I got really tired of having to summon my combat and heal pet 25 times or more in the space of an hour. When this is the character of each and every one of your battles, it gets really old, really fast.

If you want to level up an MP and try PvP and actually find it fun, more power to you.

Basic issue is that we can't rely on our pets. It would've been different if Funcom limited all crowd control ability to only 1 or 2, at most 3 classes....but they didn't. They passed it out like candy.

/shrug

I have absolutely no doubt in my mind that the best pet class in PvP is the Bureaucrat, notably for their awesome crowd control abilities that are second to none and very respectable nukes and weapon skills.

MP's would be good but lets face it...most of you guys are in the process of trying to get the Trader debuffs nerfed and will probably succeed. And the NT Professionals are of the opinion that all debuffs: Trader and MP should be nerfed down to 30 seconds in duration...so an MP nerf to our debuff skills down the road is something I expect...in fact Funcom has already told the NT community they will receive a shield from the effects of debuffs (read the NT reports for this). So, it has already begun...

So MP's who want to PvP should indeed be nervous.
