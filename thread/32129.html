tie guard presence to zone percentage?
Perhaps the best way to handle how the zone percentages is calculated is to base that number on a quantification of military might that is present in the zone... That's what the number is *really* correlated with anyway, right?

For example, a 100% zone would have substantially more military power in it than would a 25% zone. Not to mention fixed items that can kill players -- laser cannons, etc.

So, it *could* be theoretically possible for the clanners to drop OT to 0%, but it would require the participation of a *large* majority of members to pull off, because Omni is so strong at home.

As far as zones changing hands... if the clanners could hold a zone for a few days and endure the reinforcements that arrive (which means the zone is 0% of a period of time)... then it could change hands and begin as a lower percentage zone (10% or so)... Over time, a zone's strength could increase if the guards and troops are left alone, so 2HO could become 100%.

I think the same thresholds should apply (0, 25, 50, 100) but the zone "status" should be dynamic. This would allow a more realistic depiction of a reality within the game.
