Suggestions to make Soldiers less boring
Soldiers are a pretty straight forward profession, but soldiers are lacking depth, thus leading to boredom at later levels. Other professions can use soldier style weapons very close within our own damage potential while retaining their own strengths. That gives soldiers nothing unique. Soldiers seem like a part of a team, but teams can get along fine without a soldier, however soldiers aren't at their peak without a team. Ever wonder why lots of soldiers stop levling around 160-170? There is no need to spent time for leveling so that the attack skill can be raised for 25 more points, and at level 160 you are using nearly all usefull weapons and nanos.

Various people made lots of creative ideas on how to make soldiers less boring. This summary should also help that these great posts don't get lost over time. These ideas have been suggested by other peoples many times before, without getting response from Funcom - looks like we have them to repeat over and over...

I don't want to make soldiers uber gods, just less boring. I would be very happy if just a few ideas (also for more balancing all mentioned modifier can be decreased or increased) make it into game - but hey, Funcom has now a list of suggestions to choose from...


IMPROVEMENTS
  • Reflection Nanos
    Total Mirror Shield
    The mirror shield line should *always* count as a better nano than the reflect shield or be additional to reflect shield. TMS should have a visual effect (bubble), reflect nanos (damage, debuff, roots) and taunt so the mob attack the soldier with TMS up in Team. In PvP TMS is not powerfull anymore, PvP-fights (50% damage + Heals) last much longer then the 1:20 of MkX. Typically fights go for 4-7 min. Either expand the time of TMS, remove the nanoshutdown (and lock only one skill), or reflect more damage.
    Reflection Shield
    Make ReactiveReflectField self only and introduce a lower Team version.
    TTMS
    How about a total team mirror shield?

  • Weapons
    More assault riffles suited for soldiers. Its boring if everybody uses a novaflow.
    a) custom made assault riffles or miniguns with soldier profession requirements.
    b) better versions of Nova-like weapons (Example: HeavyWeapon requirements and give Soldiers a self only big HeavyWeapon buff)
    c) (hand)grenades with various effects (smoke, flash, gas) working like specials
    d) shoulder mounted mini cannons

  • Reload Button
    I would like to click a button to reload the weapon currently in use. Dragging ammo over the gun in the open wear window is not very handy. An ammo status bar would be nice too.

  • Burst Damage
    Simple: 3 bullets should do more damage then 1 bullet. Increase burst minimum damage. A crit burst hit should be more than a normal crit.

  • Armor
    camoflage suits
    In different versions, each one adds different skills. The suit is normal drop and can be upgraded with a special tradeskill item to reflect one damagetype (snow-cold, marine-chemical, urban-energy, desert-fire, jungle-melee, ?-nano, night-poison, forrest-projectile, ?-radiation).
    Barret
    A barret in 3 colors (clan/neutral/omni). Clan version gives attack bonus against omni mobs/player, omni get bonus against clan and neutral gets a lower bonus against clan and omni. You should get the barret after solving a interresting quest (no camping or uber mob killing).

  • Vehicles
    Give us Tanks, Dune Buggys, Motorcycles, Jeeps, Bombers, Mechs, X-Wing Fighters, ...

  • Damage
    At level 140 I still use the +10 Damage Amplifier, and that is a joke. Give us higher damage modifiers and self only ranged crit buffs! Delete HelepolisOfTheBesieger, that +22 damage nano for 658 MatterMeta - nobody puts IP into maxing MM only for this.

  • AMS
    The highest AMS buffs should us enable to touch a GA4 fixer, and not only hit with specials. I think you need more then 2k attack rating for this.
    Under The Gun: (ql50) +25 AMS
    Automatic Targeting: (ql106) +50 AMS
    Intelligent Homing Bullet: (ql150) +150 AMS, self only
    Furious Destroyer: (ql200) +500 AMS, self only

  • Masteries
    +25, +50, +90, +140 for following skills (the highest with +140 should be self only):
    Assault Rifle, Rifle, Ranged Energy, Fling, Burst, Full Auto, Heavy Weapons, Grenade
    These masteries could go to other professions (but a soldier should be trained in all weapons, or?): Pistol & MultiRanged -> Adventurer, MG/SMG -> Fixer, Shotgun -> Trader, Aimed Shot -> Agent, Bow & BowSpecial -> MartialArtist

  • Aggro
    Better aggro devices (or nanos, but you can't use nanos during TMS...). When I need aggro most, running TMS in a team, the mob stops hitting me and favors another member of the team - and I can't really do anything against it.

  • AC
    The AC nano line needs something unique the make people want it. Right now it boost AC less then buffs from other professions, you get AC from your egnineer friend, not the soldier in team. Perhaps add Aggro Bonus?

  • HP
    The HP nano line needs a redo too, add an first aid boost.
    First Aid is the only way a soldier can heal himself during a long hard fight (forget OneMoreHitHealing). Something that reduces the time between first aid usages and boost the first aid skill would help a lot. Or increase that heal delta from 1-3 to something like 100-500.

  • OneMoreHitHealing
    That nano is funny - it's one of those nano's that soldier either love or hate. But its something different, unique and against boredom! To bad its nearly useless at higher levels, how about a TwoMoreHitsHealing?


UNIQUES
  • Reload, Recharge And Swap Times
    Drill Instruction : -10% reload & weapon swap time
    Boot Camp Training: -20% reload & swap, -10% fling & burst recharge time
    Reload-O-Matic : -30% reload & swap, -20% fling & burst & fullauto recharge time
    Shoot Dat Fasta : -60% reload & swap, -50% fling & burst & fullauto recharge time, self only

  • Stimpacks
    Like the marines in starcraft, boost for a limited time and wasted later. Is instant cast and locks something for a short time before it can be used again. Effectively this would heal you, give some time limited bonus and puts a dot on you.
    Stimpack: +2000 health, +200 init, +200 evades, +200 AMS, +100 Damage, +100 DOT (example of various qls)
    and some simple stims like "burst of speed", but adding init, attackrating, hp, a Hot, evades ect.

  • Movement
    Jeep: Vehicle Ground +25 / +50 / +75
    Navy: Vehicle Water +25 / +50 / +75
    Airforce: Vehicle Air +25 / +50 / +75
    Scuba: Swimming +100
    Paratroop: Reduces Fall Damge by 75%
    The Long March: Gives all team members temporary run speed of the soldier

  • Airstrike
    Soldier radios in assualt aircraft to lay down a brutal line of surpression cluster bombs. Works only outside, locks something for 30mins, cast time 2min, damage everything in 50m radius including team and soldier himself.
    Cluster Air Strike Mk1: 2k explosive + 2k melee damage
    Napalm Air Strike Mk2: 3k explosive + 3k fire damage
    Atomic Air Strike Mk3: 5k explosive + 5k radiation damage

  • Soldier To Soldier
    Nano: Gunner - Range +10 and Damage +100 to Heavy Assault Rifles (Steiner, Westinghouse, etc.) Requires Ranged Energy, Heavy Weps, and Someone running 'Loader Nano' nearby.
    Nano: Loader - Increase Ammo usage 10x. Requires Ranged Energy and Heavy Weps.
    Item: Light, med, or heavy mortar tube. Requires Ranged Energy, Heavy Weps, and Weapon Smithing. Requires someone running "Loader" and someone else running "Spotter" nano nearby. Delivers a long ranged area damage effect similiar to NT but using energy damage.
    Nano: Loader - see above
    Nano: Spotter - Requires Ranged Energy, Heavy Weps, and Perception.

  • Kamikazi Training
    If the soldier dies while this nano is running the nano activates a high yeild compact nano bomb within the soldiers NCU. This bomb explodes dealing 1,000-8,000(based on QL) points of energy damage to to all enemies within 10 meters. The blinding flash and report of the explosion has a very high chance of stunning or blinding the targets as well. This nano is locked from use for 2 minutes apon casting. Duration: 10-90 seconds depending on the QL of the nano.

  • Throw Yourself On The Grenade
    You "Throw Yourself On The Grenade" and draw all agro to yourself. That would surely kill you, but your team would have a better chance of survival.
    Mk1: +200 Team Heal, +200 Team Nano, Soldier -800pts health
    Mk2: +500 Team Heal, +500 Team Nano, Soldier -2000pts health
    Mk3: +1000 Team Heal, +1000 Team Nano, Soldier -4000pts health
    edit: All team damage is directed to YOU for 30-60-90 seconds. This saves everyone and gives the solider a reason to get enough nano to cast his TMS right after this one.

  • Miscellaneous
    K9: Calm all dog mobs
    Martial Law: heals targetted NPC (only guards?) to full health.
    Drum and Bugle Mk1: Plays a drum sound
    Drum and Bugle Mk2: Plays "Taps" on Bugle
    Drum and Bugle Mk3: Plays "Amazing Grace on the Bagpipe"
