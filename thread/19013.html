I learned by fluke that if you right click and hold on a mission icon it will pop up a menu that allows you to upload the mission location into your maps.

Once this is done, check out your big map (press 'P') and the 'M' flag will be where the mission is.

Once you are in the area the mission entrance is, the compass at the bottom of your screen will also show a yellow bar in the direction the entrance is.

If you take a mission and the 'M' flag dissapears (sometimes this happends) just open up your mission menu and do the right click thing again.

Hope this helps.
