First, I agree that sometimes you don't get what you expect in single player missions. Even with the same settings and same ql mission, it could be impossibly hard, pretty easy, or actually what you want, i.e. challenging but not overwhelming. This needs to be fixed.

Second if you're going to token farm, its easy enough to do by selecting missions around 50% or less. I've never had a Mob tougher than light orange at 50%. That some take a fair while to clear out, is IMO not a big deal. Missions are designed to give you EP, not quick and dirty tokens. For me, tiny missions are a pain.

In terms of the loot, there's a couple of points. First, in higher level misions nano rechargers and med kits are nice to get because you can't buy over ql 125 in shops. Even at lower levels, I like getting them because they're actually pretty expensive and getting them in missions can save a fair bit of cash

Second, IMO a lot of marginal loot makes sense. Most of what you get ends up as machine food anyway, so it doesn't really make a difference whether it takes 5 skills or 2. What would be nice, though, is if you got at least one very nice item out of every singly player mission. Team missions have the boss loot and so are pretty well guaranteed some decent stuff. By contrast, a single player mission is a crap shoot, and you can often walk away with almost nothing. That's a problem.

Last, I've never (withing the last 9 months or so anyway), at any level, had a problem getting missions within a 5 minute run if I wanted them. You should know, though, that different cities tend to give missions in different places. Because of that, some cities also give missions with significantly higher rewards. Also, you should know that you can load the mission location into your large map directly from the mission terminal. This allows you to see exactly where it is,and choose your mission accordingly.
