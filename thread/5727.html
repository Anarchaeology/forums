*****Does Council of Truth support terrorism?*****
*****Does Council of Truth support terrorism?*****

This was the issue when a large group of people from several faction yesterday visited the CoT HQ in Tir to ask them to publicly express their sympathy with the fallen OT employees during the bombing of Omni-1

The 2 protest demonstrations was arranged by Arila from The Nordic Conspiracy and Chartan of the Omni-Emune guilds during an OT meeting to discuss the terrorist attack on Omni-1. Other prominent people at that meeting was Kryos TNC, Jamespond TNC, Mitara Div9, Spinzer OE, and Baxie Enigma. Later the SRS guild also agreed to this demand.

Mitara from Division9 reported from the first demonstration of up to 40-50 people had arrived at Tir to demonstrate and make CoT aware of their demand to publicly disannounce support to the Dust to Dust faction. The first demonstration went on fairly peacefully with only a few finer comments as We offered you an olivebranch and you turn us down?

The second meeting the day after (30/12) had the same objectives, to demand CoT taking a distance to the terrorist organisation Dust to Dust. CoT is a hard faction to get a hold of so the demonstration evolved into violence when Levrivers of the Enigma guild started to attack Clan guards in Tir.

To avoid excalating this, Arila invited clan representatives to a talk. The issues were

1) Bombing by Dust to Dust  CoT take a distance in public
2) Illegal implant research by clanners (which never really came up)

Prominent attendances were Arila TNC, Chartan OE, Zordin and Fouloleron from Beggars guild, Kryos, Zphinx, Dansk, Eilee from TNC, Oshovah, Sunraven and Ibeu2 from Ragnarok Incorporated, Baxie and Chimaen from Enigma, Mitara from Div9, Thewanderer and Tricia50 from 3305 Local, Boxxoroxx and Pomka from Spirits of Russians (AA), Agtmekun and later Rederon from Knights of Sacrifice. Other latecomers were from Agents of Fortune and Division13. Not to mention a couple of strippers, it was Baboons after all

Pomka kept order and announced order of speakers. Boxxoroxx was first and mentioned some unprovoked attacks in 2h0 by both sides. Ibeu2 wanted both sides to work together. Tricia50 was honest and said he did not really want the peace, just to be free. Jamespond expressed his opinion in War is coming, like it or not. Chartan agreed with Arila in saying We want CoT to publicly announce they take a distance to Dust to Dust. Rederon later said I have lost my faith in CoT, a serious thing to say being a clanner.

There was general agreement that the possibility was for a third party to manipulate with the delicate ceasefire between CoT and OT and that this matter would have to be looked into further.

Still the last word was said by Arila TNC We demand CoT to publicly disnounce any support to Dust to Dust or the ceasefire period for over

Aftermath:
Today a group of OT started to attack the guards in Tir as promised by several of the guilds leaders. A participate told that more then 20 Tir clan guards was killed.
