Give our Xp back
In a new patch the new players get special treatment by not having their xp taken away after dying until lvl 20. I think this is a very nice thing to do for new players but i think other players already above lvl 20 should have their stolen xp refunded to them.
I work in and ER and the time i get to play is very minimal and although i try to play as often as possible it is usually not for very long, so alot of times i wont have time to do a mission. I cant tell you how many times ive spent more than two days trying to level just once and died only to start a majority over; I do save often of course, but it takes me so long to get just a few xp relatively speaking that if i die and loose the xp it takes me forever to get them back.
Now, the rules are the rules but since they changed i feel that the time i spent doing something over one night because i lost the xp from the last night is wasted! This i remind you is time that i pay for. So if the time is wasted then my money is wasted. I feel that the xp that was lost to me and to other players before lvl 20 prior to this new rule should be given back to us to make it fair.
