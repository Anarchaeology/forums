Well this is of course very DAoCish but it's fine with me.

Questions :
- How would a city like Meetmedere be captured ?
- What bonus will we have when captured (if any) ?
- How will be know the status of the city ?
- Could this be applied to other territories other than cities ?

Ideas :
- Make 3 or 4 cities capturable, some outposts, maybe some patch of land like a wood with good spawning points or a bridge ...
- Obviously flags or 3D holo-display would show the tag of the ruling faction and guards would spawn with the adequate armor.
- Some bonus that could be done : guard protection of course, a slightly better HP / nano regen rate, 5% xp bonus, 5% damage bonus, 5% AC bonus ... not everything of course but some of these or maybe cumulative bonus over time :

city is captured : guard are changed to the ruling faction
city has been hold 1 hr : better HP / nano regen rate
city has been hold 5 hr : 5% xp bonus
city has been hold 10 hr : 5% AC bonus
city has been hold 1 day : 5% damage bonus
etc ...

- A city / land would be captured if all the guards are killed, or a certain building is filled with ppl from 1 side only, or a given item is looted (mayor coat or helmet for example, something very obvious that wouldn't leave the city perimeter and that would be lootable on any body), or if all the 10 flags are clicked to change them ...

Something to consider : what we do with insurance terminals ?

Put them in the controlable zone and we could have ugly terminal camping going around. It would be alaso a huge advantage to be able to respawn immediatly in the city your are defending...
Don't put them here and ppl that would hold a city for many hrs and get xp could see their efforts washed away when the city will fall.

Mmmmh... this is difficult.

Maybe if you saved in the city, got killed and the city has changed it's side, you should respawn to the previous save point you had.



Well, just a few ideas ...

-I don't think the Funcom programmers should worry too much about sending them to a previous booth you reclaimed at. instead have smaller "lookout towers" that are constantly protected and uncaptureable to send eliminate players to. I see two problems with this:

--To defend and outpost and constantly spawn in it until its been caputered is a terrible annoyance to me and the people trying to take it. Any idea how hard it'd be to fight an infinite army until you capture the place?

--Another problem is that these lookout towers are maybe too much of a good thing. Maybe there should only be an insurance terminal off to the side with no real enforcement. but this then too brings up camping as an issue. Maybe the pros outway the cons on this. I'd go with the lookout tower.


2. Advantages: In the spoils of war, how would you reward these people, these heros of the day. I'd put them in charge, not much else. They'd sit there and defend it like any other soldier.

-Maybe they can collect a percent of all transactions in the place, depending on rank. This'd make a really big deal with defending the people who come here to trade. The safer it is, the more transactions and more money going around.

-Honorary equipment for the standing guard! Armor that can only be equiped by the appointed defenders of the town. No stat requirements, you just have to be there and enlisted. The armor stats too would be the best armor in the game at QL (your lvl+25 or 50) this way you can't be a lvl 25 with a set armor value of a lvl 100 type person, and it all depends on you already giving you a huge advantage (especially the +50 if that's decided).
