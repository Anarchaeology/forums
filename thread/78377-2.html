Buurn,

both of your statements are right of course - it would be nice to have. But you're missing the point here a little. Part of this process is to find a way of implementing /pet warp that FC will see as unchallenging and balanced. By doing so we hope to get it implemented at all....

Let's remember - FC has known about the calls for a /pet warp for well over 8 months. Pet Pathing has been FCs #1 issue for 4 months... and yet we have no /pet warp in the game. Part of the reason for this is that there are perceived balancing issues with a straight /pet warp.

One of these issues is that hiding a pet and suddenly warping it to yourself is seen as being unbalancing. In FC's opinion, pets are supposed to be visible and not hideable - this is why FC has consistently refused calls to make pets hide with their owners using 'conceal'. There have been official FC statements to this effect. FC will not at this time implement a system that would allow pet owners to hide their pets and suddenly reveal them in battle.

If we want to get /pet warp to fix missioning, getting them up hills, dungeon play etc etc then we have to find a way of implementing it that will not allow us to hide our pets despite them being next to us.

It's not as if we're losing anything! We can't do this now.

Same goes for the hatelist issue. Yes! It would be really beneficial to MPs in some situations to warp them without wiping the hatelist... but precisely these situations are the ones that FC will be wanting to avoid.

Let's try to get a basic /pet warp working so that the 90% of pet owners' time that is spent XPing can be productive again and then perhaps worry about the 0.05% of the time when being able to warp a pet in wait-mode or with its hatelist in tact might be useful....
