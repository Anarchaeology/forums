Poems by Hattie (expurgated version)
"Time" by Hattie Callagher

Ovid christened time the devourer of things,
The greatest innovator, said Bacon,
It is given, and taken, and beaten and shaken,
It is wasted by peasants and kings

Hidden from watchful, prying eyes
The clock in gluttony does dine
On flesh and on wine, on souls, the divine
Half-tellings, half-truths, half-lies.

Divided by day, as one through the night
Manipulated, freakish and burning
Writhing, yearning, touching, learning
Was it Innovation?
Was it Devouring?
In the end ..... was it right?




"For Nevver" by Hattie McCallagher

Rumbling thunder shakes the room,
Disturbing the Land of Nod's hold,
Tossing and turning, wakeful returning,
Grumpy, restless and cold.

Uncovered, naked, shivering in bed,
I search for the source of unrest,
The best of all lovers, the thief of the covers,
Administers loves largest test.

For there you lie, sweet as can be,
Content, happy, adoring,
So unaware of the burden I bear,
To put up with your GOD DAMN SNORING!!!!

Sexless, hulking, giant of Cour
Come read, come watch, come tell
How words do becon, with quiet alure
The creative spirits that swell

For the artist's true heart
Lies not in the verse
But the primal desirings inside
While no words are written
there lies not a curse
In the giant the talents abide
