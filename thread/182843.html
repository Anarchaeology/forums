Desktop Wallpapers for the Professional!
Well over the last few weeks I have been playing around with Photoshop and putting some ideas together to make Desktop Wallpapers for each of the AO professions. This isn't professional by any means, this is just a hobby of mine, but I hope that you all will enjoy these as much as I did making them.

To date I have 2 of them done but have some more ideas and over the next few weeks/months I hopefully should get 4 or 5 more done. Rest assured eventually I'll get to all the professions.

BTW, each of the people portrayed are all real characters in AO so you might recognise some as you play.

These are all public domain so you guys can use these however you like. I only ask a few things:

1. All rights and copyrights are held by Funcom. PLEASE make sure the AO Insignia and the Anarchy Online name remain as is unaltered.

2. Each of the people I ask to pose for me have put in their own creative input and I want to give them credit for doing so. PLEASE leave the name under the quotes unaltered.

3. If anyone asks you where you got yours, please send em here I am not overly concerned about becoming famous or anything but I would like to get credit for the work I have done. Please no claiming you made these

All Wallpapers are in 1280 x 1024 as this is the resolution I play in. As long as your resolution is proportional to this you should have no problem with graphics or text being distorted. Just make sure the dimensions are directly proprtional.

To put these on your Desktop simply open the links, right click on the pictures, and left click on "Set as Background"
