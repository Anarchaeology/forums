Cries of injustice ... yeah right.
A young solitus male walks up onto the stage and looks around, then sighs before he starts talking.

"It has been interesting to sit back and listen to the cries about injustice that have been increasing lately and then exploding today.

Let us take a look into the recent past. CoT either disbands or goes deep underground, not very surprising when some of their member and/or employees helped the Dust Brigade, a terrorist group that as I understand it, even most clanners hate.

This leads to Tir being abandonded by clan guard, and ICC moves in to help the clanners to keep law and order in the vacum after their own leaders and council desert them.

It doesn't even take a day before the clanners start shouting, furios to see a few OT Employees walk in their streets, then as the cries of injustice raise, the Simon Silverstone brings his extreme gang into Tir. It doesn't take long before the cries of injustice fill the air again.

These liberators of Tir are killing neutral Rubi-Ka citizens, ICC watches it from the side line, after all, they put their tail between their legs and ran home to their HQ when they saw the Sentinals approach the city. And they haven't lifted a finger against those barbarians.

And then now, some neutral citizens have obviously had enough of their friends being killed by the Sentinals in Tir, and have hired a group of experienced soldiers to keep clanners out of their only true city."

Moradus raises his hand to stop any outbursts.

"Please wait until I'm done. Yes I said that Newland is the only true Neutral city, and even though Borealis and Last Ditch is currently patrolled by the ICC "Peacekeeper", they are still located on OT land, as they have always been. If you don't agree, then show me the proof of those two towns ever being given to the neutrals, I can assure you, you won't find it.

Now the employees of Warr hunt down any clanner they see in Newland, not strange when you consider how the Sentinals do the same with neutral Rubi-Ka citizens that enter Tir.

I must congratulate those that did hire them, you have my sympathy, and you had the forsight obviously to ask the Warr's employees to stay long enough away from the Whom-Pah's to not interfer overly much with clanners just passing through.

Now there might be shouts of outcry about this, but wait, please wait. I went up to Newland myself today to see what was going on, and yes there where clanners exiting the Whom-Pah's that were killed, but why, isnt' the guards placed far enough away from that not to happen, wasnt' that what I just said?

Yes it was, and the reason for these clanners, that might just have been just passing through was killed, was that other clanners had attacked the guards, and then when they found that they could not outmatch Warr, they ran for the safety of Borealis or ICC, knowing full well that Warr, under contract would not pursue them there.

Of course, angered by these "brave" clanners, that got away, Warr being an atrox, did as I saw myself take the anger out on other those clanners that was just passsing through.

So who is to blame here? Is it Warr?? Is it the Sentinals?? Is it the former CoT?? The Dustbrigage?? or is it Omni-Tek??"

The young solitus smiles

"Now use your head, if you blaim us of Omni-Tek for this, then you have certainly been brainwashed. Oh, and by the way, I guess I didn't introduce myself at the start, the name is Ken Strimple. Have a nice day"

The young solitus leavs the stage and sits down, an amused look on his face.
