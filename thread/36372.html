This Solution promotes playing AO is it is to be played on a WAR setting.

Soldiers should kill Soldiers and leave Civiliens out of it.

Harry,who is without a Title, will gain PvP points upon killing a Titled OR Un-Titled Person, thus becoming Titled himself.

After Harry is titled he will LOOSE PvP points upon killing an innocent un-titled person.

However if an Un-Titled person attacks Harry FIRST, Harry MUST defend himself, right? In this case, he will infact GAIN PvP points IF he manages to kill the Non-Titled agressor.

If the un-titled person kills Harry, Harry will loose some PvP-Points and the Un-Titled person will get a gain in PvP Points.

If a Titled person attacks Harry, then the normal PvP Rulez apply.

Only through this way, will ''PvP'' be kept pure, and not turned it into ''Griefing''.
