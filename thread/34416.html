A bad idea
I cannot in good conscience support any form of amnesty at this time. An amnesty was already attempted, and was subsequently overthrown due to excessive abuse for terrorism purposes.

Entertainment venues make prime targets for terrorism. The liklihood of having a wide diversity of Omni-Tek security staff of all ranks and duties together in one place is very high - particularly during a party.

The issue is involved primarily with precedent. Making a rule that allows Gridstream Productions to perform in Omni-1 sets a precedent for other entertainment groups performing in the area - to do otherwise would be anticompetitive. And that in turn leads to possible abuse - maybe not by GP, but by someone.

If Gridstream Productions are so keen to run such performances, a better solution would be to distribute their material via a recognised Omni-Tek entertainment consortium (eg: Vixxen Entertainment) and make sure all material is run past Omni-Reform for screening and content control before public broadcast.

Omni-Tek is your friend.

Amnesty
I can fully understand the concerns of Omni Tek employees who work and live in Omni Entertainment. I can fully understand their desire to protect themselves and their friends. I find it admirable that such desires are expressed here on this topic of Amnesty, it shows that Omni Tek employees truly do care about one another.

I feel that I must openly express here not only what GridStream Productions stands for, but what this Amnesty would mean to GridStream Productions, and other Clan entertainment organizations that exist in Rubi ka. From the beginning GSP has been about providing the best in live entertainment to all of Rubi ka and striving for peace for all. We are not prejudice against Omni Tek employees or those individuals who remain Neutral. In fact, GridStream Productions stands strongly against terrorist actions such as the attacks on Omni Outposts by certain Clan Organizations. If it were in our power to eliminate such elements from our world, we would do so.

I will not quote the propaganda and rumors that have arrisen from past GSP events, but I will say that never once has a member of GridStream Productions opened fire on an employee of Omni Tek or Omni Pol. In the past, when we have come to Omni Entertainment, it has been for the sake of offering entertainment to Omni Tek employees and no other reason.

I read in this petition a mention of the fear of terrorists infiltrating one of the Clan based entertainment guilds, and I can understand this fear completely. However, I can also state that the application process for joining GSP is extensively long, detailed, and thorough... and at this time, the Board of GSP does not forsee hiring any other members for the organization.

What this Amnesty would mean for GSP is protection for the DJs, who are already willing to risk their own lives to enter Omni Entertainment and offer their services to the club managers. It would mean an agreement between the respectable leaders of Omni Pol, the employees of Omni Tek and the Clan entertainment organization that we, the entertainment orgs, are willing to agree to rules and regulations laid out in an Amnesty contract for the betterment of all involved. Let's face it... after a hard day at work, wouldn't you rather listen to good music while drinking a beer?

In the end it is up to Omni Tek employees, Omni Pol itself, and the members of the Omni Entertainment community. I personally place myself at hand for any meeting these three divisions of Omni might wish to have, and I ask most humbly that this Amnesty be supported.

Thank you.
