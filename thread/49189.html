Dear Clan Members,

Recently my daddy purchased me a new yalm. After buzzing the pristine towers of Rome and Doing some shopping at Omni-Trade I thought I'd go slumming and see what the people on the other side of the tracks were like. I have one bit of advice to the clans of Rubi-ka.

"buy some soap"

I realize that maybe you don't benefit from the types of education that the members of Omni take for granted. So I am only offering this advice from the bottom of my heart. Even in my shiny new Yalm, flying high above your dirty little cities (are they called cities when they're that small or do you prefer towns?) the scent of thousands of unwashed clanners permeated my plush leather upholstry. It took me days to finaly wash the stench out! I'm sure that you are all very embarrased by all this, and it may be a sore subject, but a nice washing would do you all a world of good. Who knows you may feel so mutch better once you get the crust out from your private areas that you'll wise up and join Omni!
