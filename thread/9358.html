Communication increase and lag decrease!
Please make this one sticky as it is something that can change the future of AO, and is as much of intrest for Funcom as us players.

Here is a idea for communication improvement between clients and server.

By giving graphics, like characters, a code and letting the servers keep track with a grid system and codes for each action the characters and npc's make, the communication between servers and clients can be more effective and lag almost elimated in the process, even for the slowest users (those with old modems, e.a.) We understand of course that this might require a remake of the structure of Anarchy Online. It may in turn further increase the speed in which the servers are capable of calculating and responding to the actions of the players.

This may of course demand more of the clients hardware when it comes to graphic renderings (models, effects, shadows) especially for computers with older graphic cards. But as we all know the hardware issues of this game with heavy demands is not really an issue with the advances in computer technology today.

Another improvement would be of course fixing of the constant memory leak, as this kind of bug can slow down even the fastest of computers. One idea that we are toying with is, what if AO had its own texture swap-file? Say, 256 megabyte.

Bump and respond with your comments and own suggestions.

This is just a draft, a starting point.

Good night!
