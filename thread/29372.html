Eliminate all spam from the game
This is an honest and straightforeward suggestion. I'm not flaming Funcom. I think that if they took a hard look at what in the game is spam the overall feel of the game would be improved markedly.

My definition of Spam is anything put forward by the game for me to look at that will completely waste the time i spent looking. I don't have any intention of wasting my time. I want to squeeze this game for entertainment, not diddle around looking at spam 1/2 the time.


What i consider to be spam:

1: Messages I cannot disable
Do i care if jimmy got a critical hit? Not really, i can see the mob's health just fine. So far i think FunCom has done well on this score - there is very little spam you can't disable. They've also done a good job of keeping the disable buttons seperate so you aren't forced to disable important messages in order to eliminate the useless ones. However, the new spam of appearing/disappearing is irritating.

2: Items no one wants
I don't know about the AO world, but in the real world products don't last very long if they have no redeaming features. In fact crap products have a life span of <advertising campaign length> - <word of mouth>. In the real world a lousy pistol might still have a few redeaming features. It may look great, or it may be cheap. It might just be that it won't break grandma's wrist when it's fired. I won't get into too much reality here as the system isn't designed to be realistic. However the fact remains that 90% of items have no redeaming features - not even the cost of the item. Hence they qualify as spam. Any shop that truly relied on sales would go broke with all the crap items selling in them. The intent seemed to be to 'add flavor to the game' but that lasts only the first week of play. After that it's just time-wasting spam.

3: Mission Dialogue
Excuse me funcom. Hahaha.. My name is <Bleep - edited>. Wow! You've come a long way. Been packing back the bronto burgers i see! Have you gained weight? Well anyways let me talk to you about missions. Can't we just treat mission terms as agents who sort through the crap for us? I have an idea! Why not make the reward/location appear and then let us click on the mission to get the dialogue rather than the reverse. It makes more sense to have the mission terminal act as an agent that sorts missions for you. Selecting a mission for more-in depth examination would give you all the flavor you could handle and show you all the dialogue. In a real world the mission terms would go out of business in no time flat, as someone would come out with a competitive terminal that worked more efficiently and indexed the available missions for you. Clicksaver does some of this, but i think Funcom should do this themselves. Currently mission terms are just spam-terms and we need a third party app to stop our time from pouring down the drain.

4: Ludicrous mission rewards
Stop offering me laughable missions. I won't do them. No one is going to kill 100 Veteran soldiers for a reward of a map / tatoo / pair of sunglasses!!! Thats ridiculous! Currently the reward system is only wasting my time. It's silly. If you want to give us an opportunity to find a newbie item even at high level then why not allow us to roll low Ql missions? Is that so wrong? I don't get why i have to pay some newb a few credits so he can get something I with all my power can't get without doing a mission 100 times as hard. That's messed up.

Funcom: Your mission is to start making sure that rewards fit the mission. If you can do this and all your countless hours of coding work result in an appropriate reward system I will give you a hero cookie. Oops! Cookie not good enough? How about a toothpick? No? Maybe a thimble? An apple? An old beaten shoe? a cigarette butt? A lid from a jar? An old coffee cup? A used kleenex? A handful of pocket lint? What is it you want? OH! You want subscription money! Well why didn't you say so?!? Maybe if you offer me a valid reward i'll offer you one then. hehe - i hope you get the point.



I'm not complaining, as i'm just going to eventually move on when the frustration exceeds the fascination for the game. Also that's not a threat - it's just a normal feature of life. When a game irritates you more than entertains you you'll drop it like a bad habit. Spam is one of the things i see creating just that sort of irritation for the players. The thought that alot of work has gone into creating things to waste our time and keep us from really entertaining ourselves in the AO world makes me wonder what the game plan was in the first place.

I would just like to point out: DON'T WORRY! It's a great world! The human interaction element keeps us coming back for more. We don't need things to slow down our experiences in the game. There is so much to experience in this great game it shouldn't be a worry. Just eliminate the spam and let us get to it.
