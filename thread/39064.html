Worst...mission location...ever...
Okay...today I got into a team, whose names will remain censored. (This is on my level 17 alt)

One person couldn't find the way to a mission in AEGAN so there were needless complications.

But soon the Mission Triathalon begun. First, our 5 contestants would have to start the trek across a desert. This would be almost a three mile trek, and it was filled with nasty auto-aggro rollerrats. But soon I was the first to hit the second part of this challenge....the river.

There was an extremely wide river, full of pirahnas and plenty of auto-aggro wolf things. I made a first attempt at swimming, but was attacked by several pirahna and decided to run along the right coast to find a place where there were no pirahnas. 5 minutes later I was accross, and had to attempt the third challenge: jumping up a near vertical riverbank.

Soon I made it, and had to go accross the Forest Full Of Very Nasty Red Enemies Who Like To Auto-Aggro You. With my Fixer speed, however, I made it accross, and made first place inside the mission, with another teammate following a close second.

However, 2 teammates kept dying, over and over again. We kept hearing reports of 'AAHHHH VULTURE' 'RUN ROVER RUN' or 'OMG ITS A HUGE STORM' 'HELP IM BEING ATTACKED BY ENGI BOTS' 'ITS TOO DARK TO SEE' 'IM NOT GONNA MAKE IT GO ON WITHOUT ME!'

At this point, we are waiting for the last 2. We have lost 2 players, and are hoping they will make it, after dying about 6 times each...

I'll update this when they get here.
