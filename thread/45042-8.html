Okay, I STILL haven't read pages 4-7, (I will, I will,) but I wanted to point something out to the last? post.

Putting a 20% chance timer every 20 minutes approximates what you seem to be calling for.

20 minutes after a mob is killed there is a 1 in 5 chance of it respawning. Not real good odds. Twenty minutes later, (assuming the 1D10 roll came up anything 1 through 8), another 20% chance. This continues until FINALLY the law of averages kicks in and the thing spawns. This means, if I'm not completely mistaken, (and I'll admit I forgot MOST of any statistics I ever took, zzzzzz, was awake for), that the thing should spawn ABOUT once every 100 minutes, but sometimes 20-40 minutes early or late. RARELY, it might even spawn 20 minutes after being killed, and EVEN MORE RARE, it might not spawn at all for days, (more likely hours. Even the most jynxed pulls a "win" occasionally, and even the most lucky has to loose sometime.)

For a decent simulation of the timetable, and what it means to camping. Dig out one of those 10, or 20 sided dice you've been neglecting since MMORPG's became your new drug of choice over TT RPG. 1 in 5 means a roll of 9 or 0 on a ten sider is a "success", and any roll 17-20 is a success on a 20 sider. Take a piece of ruled paper and mark it off like so. 00, which gets an x by it, 0:20, 0:40. 1:00. 1:20, 1:40. 2:00. ...etc. Then roll those dice and but a X or an O by the appropiate number to determine on THAT roll whether or not something popped. (I'm assuming it gets taken down so quickly the timer's sinc isn't effected. It shouldn't be, but that just means sometimes you might wander onto this thing spawning at 12:05, instead of 12:00.)

Note that in all, you only get one in 5 spawns, and overall they should be spaced ABOUT 100 minutes apart, but the camping party wouldn't know if it was going to have to wait 20 minutes to kill something again, or 40 minutes, or an hour, or an hour, 20 minutes...


Basically it should mean the professional ranchers get bored with wondering if the thing was going to spawn this 20 minutes or not, and wandering off. Or getting inattentive and forgetting to re-log into the alt they have stationed and camped there to watch the mob, every 20 minutes, (and potentially loosing the kill to a wandering opportunist, and thus loosing a sense of the timing schedule to check on it further.)
