Notum Wars: A proposed change for griefing
We have all seen it, done it, hated it etc.

Clan/Omni attacks towers, defenders beat back the attackers and the attackers now wait in blissful 75% for the next 5 hours till the towers go 75% again. They do not attack but only gas camp the area and at times will initiate another attack 2-3 hours later forcing defenders BACK to the towers from whatever they were doing.

This is griefing this is not an attack, if it was an attack it would be planned out and done continously till towers went 75% or they had destroyed the base.


What I propose is that FC change how gas and attacks operate.

1) Shields MUST be disabled to initiate an attack on towers. This will make it IMPOSSIBLE for anyone to initiate a false attack.

2) Once shields are dropped the attackers MUST destroy at least 1 tower in the next 30 minutes to keep the gas at 25%.

3) For every hour after that if at least 1 tower is not destroyed then gas will revert back to 75% and will not return to 25% for both the 18 hour cycle and the remainder of what would have been 25%.


What these changes do is prevent gas camping. When defenders arrive they need only keep the attackers from destroying a tower, since any serious attack that has any hope of succedding should have no problem killing 1 tower an hour and if they are not able to do that then they certaintly have no hope of destroying the whole base or even making a dent in it.

This will also require them to start their attack early if they ever hope to take a base and will have bigger penalties for them if they fail as the base will be safe for a much longer length of time.

I am tired of the griefing that is done in NW and am tired of sitting around at a CT for 6+ hours while not a single shot is fired. I believe such changes as these would help to solve some of these issues.
