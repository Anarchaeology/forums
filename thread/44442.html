FunCom's Contradictions
Being as I am too tired to calmly put this thread to good use I am going to type off the top of my head.


FunCom's policy on bug reimbursements is completely asinine . Usually I would not care if these things happened to me alone but tonight four other players and I lost all experience and our loot when our team mission boss 'poofed'.

Now after waiting for the better part of an hour to get a response the quick one liner appeared. We all know of it:

GM - "Unfortunately there isn't anything we can do about mission bosses that vanish, we aren't able to respawn a mission or change it in any way."

Myself- "Ok, be careful about your next response... Is it either FunCom policy not to, or are you not capable of doing it? I.E. lack the tools.

GM - "Both."

Well seeing as how Khatrina --- got his/her shoulderpads back by a GM I cannot see how the excuse that they lack the tools to do these things is commonplace. Obviously they have the tools and are capable of doing these things. The question is; why is FunCom crippling their GM's by denying them the one thing that would make us players happy?
Why after a couple hours in a team mission we cannot get our dead boss back to loot. I could care less about the experience. We all know though that the loot is what matters.

I guess this is a rant/plea to FunCom to actually tell us what the heck is their policy. I am TIRED of getting half ass answers and half truths. Where's the love FunCom. It certainly is not stockpiled and heading out the door. More like a finished bowel movement that got flushed down the toilet. I mean its one thing to do this to me on occasion but to subject new players to this and old alike is just plain stupid. Maybe you should review your policies and give us loyal gamers a break.

Hell just my 2cr. of ranting.

I am now on a mission to post here every instance of FunCom doing reimbursements of items, cash, or experience. Just to show that the system is screwed. If you other posters out there have links of these instances please post replies here so that I may accumulate all this into one massive post.
