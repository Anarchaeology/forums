Espada
Seems to work okay under limted experimentation
I have done four missions post patch this morning. I did one mission at 1/4 easy difficulty two at normal difficulty and one at 3/4 difficulty. I am a level 41 agent with 202 base concealment skill. Added to this I run shadow crown, concealment expertise and either a ql30 or ql 49 cloaking device when in pure sneak mode. For the most part, unless I opened a chest within Los or a locked door around a green or yellow mob I was not noticed. Orange and Red mobs especially red mobs tended to agro when in LOS. Orange mobs tended to Agro occassionally. Next level I plan on using Ruse of Taren, I speculate the effect on Red Mobs will be the same. I for one would like to know.

I was able to accomplish a full sneak mission at 3/4 difficulty. With chests and locked doors breaking concealment and this fix, Funcom has made the game playable and challenging for agents. I am not a fanboy, and I have been critical of the concealment nerf- I think at least based on my limited experiments Funcom did a nice job balancing the skill with the exploits. It took them some time, and they screwed concealment big time in the process but the current changes are for the better it seems. Granted the game has other problems which need to be addressed but this is a step in the right direction. WTG Funcom. Back to testing......

ALSO.....I have tested a Gripo ql20,35 and 45. The gun at least for me, is working fine. The ql 45 Gripo hits for an average damage of 81 and criticals in the mid 500s.
