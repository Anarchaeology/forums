In my time I've done TONS of foot exploring (yes, even trecked across SAV on foot). It's more of a personal preference than anything else.

The main reason why I'd like to see more difficult travel is because it creates communities. I remember in EQ, although the game totally sucked, the communities that popped up in far-off areas were cool. You'd run into the same people often, make friendships, hunt at local areas, etc. And then exploring the world felt a lot more special than it does in AO... it was a lot scarier. The scariness wears off really, really fast in AO.

The ability to hop into a plane and be totally invulnerable takes away a lot from the game experience IMHO. Soon as you get a yalm, the world is suddenly not that scary, and you can get anywhere with zero risk.

MMORPGs should all be about risk/reward. If you want to travel to far off places, that's cool, but invulnerability makes the place you're going to not worth going to.

But do I wish that my everyday means of transportation should be inferior or demised? No! Why? Because I dont want to use forever getting between one city to another, and as you all know, you dont go into citys for the sole purpose of hunting. Missions perhaps (at low levels), but also missioning will take you far out of the cities at later levels. I soley use whompas and grids for shopping and collecting missions. I do not spend much time in cities. The fact of the matter is that the main part of the game takes place in the countryside and the wilderness, to where you cant grid or whompa (I dont do missions in BS because of lag).

Back to my main point, I see this thread as a symtom of boredom, and the ones that shout the loudest about implenting subways etc. are victims of rutine, ie.: Pickup mission in city, grid/whompa to location near mission, fly to mission, do mission, get back same way as you came, pickup new mission, whompa/grid to location... etc. That is really what is eating me.
