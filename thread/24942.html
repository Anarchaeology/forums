Team Missions a.k.a. Boss Problems
Problem:

In team missions it is really easy to take high level missions because of damage output of groups. The problem with this is that boss mobs generally crit non stop. At level 49 with 150ish evades I get critted non stop against boss mobs.

Second problem is that fights are usually over so quickly that DOTs, HOTs, Bots, and Pets are USELESS!

Funcom has tried to overcompensate these problems by adding a high aggro range and several minions in the boss areas, which causes another problem of lag on zoning into boss areas / up and down elevators and getting swarmed by mobs.

The solution I propose would be to
-increase the HP on mobs in team missions (I am talking double or thereabouts, not this insane hp on bosses and dungeon mobs crap)

-give mobs random hate at the beginning of a fight, so different people get hit at the beginning of a fight instead of the same predictable people. (this would increase the need for aggro building up and controlling)

-elevators should cause people to spawn NEXT to elevators, not on top of it, and in random positions around the elevator like a clock, to stop the insane texture/clip plane lag on a whole team entering an area simultaneously (this will also help cause less deaths in bail out situations when an Atrox is blocking the button)

-allow mobs reasonable AoE attacks

-Mix up mobs a bit, seeing 3 Rookie Enforcers in the main room and having all of the bosses minions the exact same is crap. A mixture of professions in these rooms would allow for more colorful fights and strategy. IE the need to take out the healers first, etc. proceeding on down the threat list.

The main reason for the upped HP is to lengthen fights long enough to make pet classes have a reason to bring their pets along. May perhaps even give me a reason to buy my next set of DOTS as a doc. Random aggro allows for team HOT's to have a bit more use, and gives calmers and tanks reason to control taunt even after the pull. Elevators are just a huge problem with team missions, why couldnt a freakin staircase be implemented? I've noticed RPGs have a real problem with a damn Z axis.

These changes should come without an increase to xp. XP is gained fast enough as it is currently leaving people equipped poorly for their level, with OE changes, people need to be equipped as best as possible for their level.

Anyways, those are my 2 credits on this matter.

49 Doc - Tired of stupid deaths in team missions that could be prevented with better coding.
