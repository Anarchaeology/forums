interesting...
Ulltima was reading the latest GridNews when a new memorandum dropped in her in-box. With a tired sigh, she skimmed the title... then quickly closed all other open feeds.

"What in the Hell..." She read the message once... twice. "A peace conference? Here? On Rubi-Ka?" She scoffed at the notion of a quickly attained peace settlement... as desirable as it would be - war is bad for business - it would only hurt Omni-Tek and benefit other parties.

"Ambitious... futile, but very ambitious." Ulltima paused; more memorandums regarding the peace conference had arrived. She smiled at the efficiency of her Gridbot and quickly skimmed those messages as well, taking in the demands of Omni-InternOps and other parties.

To Ulltima, it seemed to be a simple matter: How much would Omni-Tek Rubi-Ka be willing to give to gain a cessation of hostilities? After all, that was what diplomacy was about... giving, taking, and hoping you took more than you gave in the long run. Unfortunately, the nuances of diplomacy had been lost with the open mining wars and the recent incidents in Jobe. Both sides wanted the planet, its rich notum supplies, and the promises of new technology and power from the Shadowlands... an area her organization was only beginning to explore.

"I'll have to see what these people have to say... it should be interesting, if nothing else." With that, Lorena "Ulltima" Siwinksi sent a private message to her immediate superior, expressing interest in this conference. As an afterthought, she made a Mental-Note(TM) to buy a nice outfit for the conference...
