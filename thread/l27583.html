Re: Roles of ARKs (and what powers do they have)
1. What are ARK and GM hours?

We have 24/7 GM and ARK in game support. Advisors choose the hours they wish to participate.

2. What CAN ARKs do with regards to whatever powers their proclaimed nano-enhancements give them? Can they teleport players out, can they teleport only players in their queue or ANY one? Do they have stealth capabilities? Can they teleport themselves to any locality? Can they hold items in their inventory? Can they attack/defend etc.

I cannot go into details about all of the abilities of Advisors or GMs. We do have special enhancements that allow us to perform our customer service roles. Some of the more obvious ones are the ability to summon, teleport, etc. However, these are only used for CS, and we do not adventure with our CS characters.

3. Why are ARKs given a lvl 50+ status, as opposed to a level-less 'God-mode' where they cannot be killed, nor can they interact with the environment (ie npcs or monsters or players)?

The levels given to Advisors and GMs are honorary. These are not our play characters, and as I mentioned above, we do not adventure with them. The character is designed as a tool to provide customer service in game.

4. Are ARKs allowed to play in game using their ARK characters? In other words, can they increase their levels, kill monsters etc?

No. There is a very clear distinction between ARK/GM characters and play characters.

5. Are ARKs allowed to have clan/guild associations? Can they join them?

They are certainly welcome to join a clan with their play character. However, when they are playing, they gain no special treatment. This is something we emphasize in ARK. Even the perception of favoritism will create a lot of problems.

6. Are they paid for their 'voluntary' services? Will this fall foul of the recent fiasco over volunteers in onling gaming?

I could write a book about this subject, but I will save that for some other time. The Advisors of Rubi-Ka is a program unlike any other. Sure there are similarities with other programs, but ARK was founded with certain ideals and principles that are very important. We value caring and appreciation of the Advisors over "covering our asses" and we do our best to show that every day. These people are not disposable slaves who are cannon fodder for the flames and arrows of the irate customers. They are people who care so much about the AO community that they are willing to devote their free time to make it special. I am honored and privileged to have the opportunity to work with such an amazing group of people.

Finally, I'm not out to rub the ARKs the wrong way. I *know* how hard it can be to be an ARK, and how frustrating it can be when as an ARK, you can get attacked from all sides - Funcom, GMs, and players, and how difficult that when you want to help a player but cannot do so or are unable to. I can only wish all ARKs good luck and hope that you folks do a good job but spend time enjoying the game too lol

Thanks. We do appreciate the support and hope to meet and exceed your expectations.

Roles of ARKs (and what powers do they have)
Been following the thread on ARKs and the recent 'furore' over lvl 50+ ARKs that are around. From past experience as a UO SRC and counsellor I know that most of the 'hatred' towards counselors in UO are due to misinformation and more importantly the LACK of information on what exactly do we do, can we do, are capable of etc.

Perhaps someone from funcom or the ARK team could help illuminate this issue? I know that while I was in AC, there were great unhappiness over the fact that normal players with guild alignments become 'counselor' equivalents and are even encouraged to level up their characters, which brings on the question of biasness, partisanship etc. This is again happening here I see.

Some pertinent questions:-

1. What are ARK and GM hours?
2. What CAN ARKs do with regards to whatever powers their proclaimed nano-enhancements give them? Can they teleport players out, can they teleport only players in their queue or ANY one? Do they have stealth capabilities? Can they teleport themselves to any locality? Can they hold items in their inventory? Can they attack/defend etc.
3. Why are ARKs given a lvl 50+ status, as opposed to a level-less 'God-mode' where they cannot be killed, nor can they interact with the environment (ie npcs or monsters or players)?
4. Are ARKs allowed to play in game using their ARK characters? In other words, can they increase their levels, kill monsters etc?
5. Are ARKs allowed to have clan/guild associations? Can they join them?
6. Are they paid for their 'voluntary' services? Will this fall foul of the recent fiasco over volunteers in onling gaming?

Other readers feel free to add to this list.

In any online customer support community like counsellors or ARKs there are bound to be some bad seed that got in despite the 'vigorous' vetting and interview process; that cannot be avoided. You'll also get disgruntled ARKs eventually who would do something crazy. The point is if these ARKs are hidden under a veil of secrecy as to their powers/abilities/alignments, then normal players will increasingly question their motives and you'll get accusations flying around eventually. I think Funcom or the ARK/GM team should do something to disspell this or at least enlighten the public.

To give an example... since ARKs are the first line of defence for problem solving in game, they are also potentially privy to bug/exploit reporting by players. I know this has happened in my tenure as counsellor/SRC and I don't see that NOT happening here. Which then beckons the question - if ARKs are aligned to clans, what's to stop them from spreading out information about exploits to their clansmen/women? In UO, while counselors can maintain their own chars, players will have to associate their counselor chars to normal chars before they can make the link. This doesn't happen in AC which is why some players would complain that certain guilds are getting privileged information etc.

We really need transparency of information here. Not just an interview which keeps things vague lol

Finally, I'm not out to rub the ARKs the wrong way. I *know* how hard it can be to be an ARK, and how frustrating it can be when as an ARK, you can get attacked from all sides - Funcom, GMs, and players, and how difficult that when you want to help a player but cannot do so or are unable to. I can only wish all ARKs good luck and hope that you folks do a good job but spend time enjoying the game too lol

ARK = Advisor of Rubi-Ka.

The ARKs are basically online customer support run by volunteer players if I'm not mistaken. They are the ones who handle the queue of /petitions from players, and if they cannot resolve it, presumably they will hand it on to higher ups who can deal with it properly.

There are apparently FOUR levels to this:-
Apprentice Advisors who are undergoing on the job training
Advisors - The normal grunts lol
Guardians - presumably these are a level up the hierarchy structure, and would comprise of volunteers who will handle the more touchy situations (eg disputes, angry players). Presumably as well, these guardians will have some powers that the ARKs do not have, and hopefully the support-events team will help enlighten us on the differences.
Genetic Manipulators (or GMs or Game Masters) - These would be employed by Funcom (again presumably if they hold the same structures as other MMORPGs), and have final say on matters and are the omnipotent beings in the game. The GMs in UO had the power to unleash massive hordes of monsters against players if they choose to, usually in response to players whining about lack of monsters lol. Hopefully the GMs in here won't be so mean... :P
