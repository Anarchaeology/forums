In UO you could play a character for 3 years and not get bored. If you looked at your character 3 years after he got his first 'GM' you probably wouldn't recognize him.

In AO, your character is the same from 60 to 200. You do the same things from 20-200.

Your only option is to start a new character.

That's part of the problem.

The other part is their insistence that PvP is the only thing that matters.

Not having player-run shops. Not having tradeskills that work the UO tradeskills work.

Believe it or not Fishing, Treasure Hunting, Baking, Poisoning, Woodworking. All these things kept people playing and interacting. A GM Carpenter could play the game for 5 years and set up a shop and get player interaction continuously.

Killing other people gets old. Real old. Escaping to a virtual world to set aside the stress of RL never gets old. Killing other people may be a 'part' of it. If it becomes the 'goal' of logging on to the game, then the game is just that. Just a game. People get bored of games and look for new ones. People stay in a world they have invested in.

FunCom has either not figured this out or has discarded the notion.

Look how easy it is for people to simply leave. I would be heartbroken to see the Catskills shard be shut down. There's a chunk of my free time in there and relationships, organizations, structures, and memories that are imprinted in the 1's and 0's of that server's database.

I'll never forget the time our whole guild got drunk in the guild house and we started *hicupping* from the drink and as we lurched over we all decided we looked like native americans doing a war dance. 2 hours later 'hiya, hiya, hiya, hi' we were done parading around Fire Island having scared away the evil spirits.
