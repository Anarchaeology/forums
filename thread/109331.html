The Circle Continues
Hi everybody, I just wanted to share a little thing I made today

When I was 5, my father was killed
for helping the clan he was instantly grilled

When I was 10, my mother escaped
from this burning world, and left us behind

When I was 16, my brother turned omni
to sell out his people or personal gain

When I was 20, I got into the clan
to be a soldier and make payback head-on

When I was 30, I started to count
the homes i destroyed, the people I killed

Now I am 40, and look back at it all
was it really worth it? Killing them all?
