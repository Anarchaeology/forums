Market forum changes
To hopefully speed up the forums a bit, I've made some changes on the market forums. The changes are not fully done, so the hopeful speed-up won't come right away, but we're working on it.

The changes:
- Threads older than 3 months (no replies in that time) are removed. This to reduce the number of posts the database's search index has to cover.
- No posts in the market forums count against your total posts. This is to prevent people's post count to jump up and down each time we remove old threads.
- The forum rules are now an announcement, not a sticky thread. This is to prevent is from being moved by accident with other old threads.

The threads are currently only moved to a closed and hidden forum, but we hope to be able to archive them elsewhere, and thus remove them from the forum database. This will hopefully speed the forums up a bit, and together with some other tweaks we hope to have fast running forums for you again soon.
