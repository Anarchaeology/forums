Trust me, I'm a scientist
When I write an in-character post, it is usually takes the form of a memo, an open letter or maybe an article is some fictional publication. Either way I just have I can easily and logically identify which character is the author by having them sign the letter at the bottom.

If I am writing a story, even if it involves, one of my characters, for me that is still an ooc post (assuming its written in thrid person and not in first person as in a diary), after all its the author that is doing the writing, not the character. In fact I would say that between fiction and post are about roleplaying in AO, most of what I write here is ooc. And as such I don't mind at all that the story is associated with my usual forum name.

As for many people, my forum name happens to be the same as my oldest or at least most established character's. While I can see how this might generate a little bit of confusion regarding ic/ooc, it has the advantage that you can contrieve to meet in game the characters of players with whom you get along on the forum. Of course the same can be done by listing your characters in your sig file.
