I am PISSED!
I despise Omni-Tek. Not because it has more money than God and almost rivals Her in supreme power. No, I despise Omni-Tek for its callous treatment of all living beings. People are not people to Omni-Tek; they are, as described in Omni-Tek's own corporate documents, Fungible Worker Units ("FWU's"). Expendable liabilities, like those poor miners trapped those many years ago. Absolute power has clearly corrupted Omni-Tek absolutely. So therefore I joined my fellow citizens in the fight against Omni-Tek.

But why are we fighting this war? Is it to obliterate Omni-Tek everywhere? Of course not. No one believes it is possible to wipe out a corporation with more than a billion people with a few fractured pockets of resistance fighters. And besides, would we even want to? No one can deny the good that mega-corporations provide. Without Omni-Tek, there wouldnt be the advances that weve all enjoyed in medicine, technology, entertainment, and virtually every other facet of life. And it goes without saying that without Omni-Tek, we would not be able to enjoy life without death, as we do here on Rubi-Ka.

Instead of death and destruction, what I am fighting for is the most basic of rights, the right to life. I am not and will never succumb to being a slave for the Corporation, as many of our forefathers were forced to do, living solely as Omni-Tek sees fit. I want the freedom to live my life as I see fit, without fear of the Corporation.

So why am I pissed?

As I continually fight for the rights of everyone on Rubi-Ka, it goes without saying that I see myself and my fellow Clan members as clearly fighting on the side of good. Recent developments, however, have made me question that. The papers are covered with news of the links between the CoT and the Dust Brigade. In case you dont know, the Dust Brigade are scum. They are worse than Omni-Tek. The Dust Brigades history of terrorism, like the cowardly destruction of an Omni-Tek apartment complex, proves that it gives even less consideration to life than Omni-Tek does. Omni-Tek has the power to wipe us all off the face of this planet with the push of a button. It would never do that however, if for no other reason than the devastating effect it would have on its image. Now imagine those weapons in the hands of the Dust Brigade. Would they hesitate to use them? I think not. Pure and simple, they are terrorists whereas I am a freedom fighter.

Now it appears that Radiman is cozying up with this scum. This is unacceptable and NOT what I signed up for. Although I hope others agree with me, yesterday I stood outside of the Councils building and protested until I was blue in the face without any support from my fellow citizens. I was calling for Radiman to step down and for the Council to end its heinous association with the Dust Brigade, but not one single person joined in this protest. Instead, everyone just ran by on their missions for our leadership, without even questioning whether that leadership deserves to be there in the first place. The only person who stopped to see what I was so upset about told me that there was no one on Rubi-Ka named Radiman. This coming from a fellow clanner!!

I say to all of you, pull your head out of your rear and pay attention to whats going on! We are NOT terrorists! If the Council continues on this path, I fear that we will lose the popular support that we have enjoyed on Rubi-Ka. This loss could ultimately prove to be the death of the Clans.

My dear Danjaq,

There are a few points I would like to take up with you...

"Fungible Worker Units"... on what document did you see this? Did you have to dig through the library archives to find that description? I would be most interested to view a document, from *this* century, with that description on it, actively applied to today's inhabitants of Rubi-Ka. I would also like to know who the author was of said document.


"...what I am fighting for is the most basic of rights, the right to life. I am not and will never succumb to being a slave for the Corporation, as many of our forefathers were forced to do.."

My dear fellow, you, as so many of the citizens of Tir, are living in the past. Truly. I work for Omni-Tek, and I made that decision of my own free will. I am most definitely not a slave. Of the many friends I have made in the company, I have yet to meet anyone who would consider themselves a slave. Unless, perhaps, a slave to fashion? Ah, but I shouldn't make light of such a serious issue.

As far as I'm aware, there haven't been any slaves on Rubi-Ka for... well... more years than I can even fathom at the moment. My travels have taken me to several of the mining communities on this planet, and I have yet to meet anyone in any of those towns, who would apply the phrase 'slave' to themselves. The people who I see, residents of these towns, are all ordinary, average people, who get up, kiss their wives, husbands, children, and go to work. They come home, have their dinner, do their shopping, fold their laundry. They live, laugh, love, just like the many thousands of OT employees living in the City.

Frankly, I don't appreciate to be labelled an oppressor, or held responsible for events that happened several hundred years ago.

In my opinion, clanners like to use phrases such as 'fighting for liberty', 'being a slave', to promote an agenda. I have no doubts that there are many people, misguided, who truly believe that they are fighting for a cause.

I think, though, that these days, the cause they're fighting for isn't truly what it seems. If it were to come to an out-and-out war between Omni-Tek and the Clan settlements, who do you think would stand the most to gain out of this? Not Omni-Tek, who would have to invest manpower, money, time in this war. And not the Clanners, who would do likewise. Whoever wins, would still have less than they started out with.

It seems to me that the party or parties who would stand to gain would be other members of the ICC, those corporations who would like to get their hands on leasing rights, and trade concessions. To say nothing of the criminal organizations, who always flourish when they have two sides to play against one another...

You are right to question the relationship of the Council of Truth to the activities of the Dust Brigade. But perhaps it might also be an excellent idea to look a little more closely into the Dust Brigade's connections to the ICC.

And perhaps you might ask yourself, what are these 'rights' you're fighting for?
