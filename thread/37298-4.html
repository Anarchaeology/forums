I definitely think that the problem here is Shadowlands buffs, not our existing game mechanics or the skill/implant/buff system we're all used to now. The level limits on nano formulas just doesn't make sense - in fact it especially doesn't make sense - when you look at the concept of "uber buffs" from the expansion.

Think about any given profession. If this profession is a nano caster, in order to use their new nanos with extreme skill requirements, they will need the uber-buffing of Shadowlands items and nanos. If they are a fighter profession, they will need uber-buff nanos and items to keep their new weapons with extreme skill requires from being OE'd. Am I correct so far?

Now, the inclusion of these super nanos and super items, that are so good that super-buffing is required to use them, implies that there will be super things to do with them. Ie, super-monsters to fight. And at least presumably, the super-monsters will be worth fighting, and there will be enough of these super-monsters spawning often enough that it's worth it for people who aren't a member of an uber-guild to buy the expansion. Furthermore, the new nanos and new weapons and armors will have to be so much better than what exists now, that it's definitely worth going through the rigamarole of buffing into them, right? If the new super-monsters can be fought and killed with our existing nanos and weapons, after all, there's no need to use the new super-nanos and super-weapons, and thus no need for super buffs. Furthermore these new weapons and nanos will have level requirements on them in addition to super-high buff-required skill requirements, from what I understand.

So... why not just get rid of skill reqs on everything and replace them solely with level reqs, and forget the idea of nano-skills entirely? Why put us through the trouble of buffing into something even after we've met the level requirement? Buffing isn't much fun, looking for someone else's buffs isn't much fun... why make us do it when you already are making us meet a minimum level to ensure "balance"?

It follows from all this, that the uber-buff-required new stuff (nanos, items, monsters) will make even the top-line stuff of the existing game totally obsolete. It has to, or else the vast majority of the player base just won't bother with it. After all, why should I (a doctor) buff into Shadowlands-uber-heal-#7 if I can self-cast Complete Healing and Complete Healing will do the job of healing my tank sufficiently well? What are we going to be facing that Complete Healing won't be enough? Why use the new stuff if the old stuff works and the new stuff requires way more effort to use?

The conclusions I am starting to draw are the following:

1. Either the new stuff is going to be so incredible our eyes will burst, or the stuff we have now is going to be nerfed, hard. I think it will probably be some of both.

2. People who don't buy the expansion might as well quit now, because either way #1 goes, they will not be capable of playing the game up to the designer's par anymore.

3. Remember that other game, EverCamp? Where are the new nanos and items going to be coming from? I'm gonna take a wild guess and say it'll mostly be from the new super-monsters. Kinda like our profession-balancing items are dropping off our current super-monsters, mission bosses.

4. People who do want to buy the expansion might as well quit now anyway. Engineers can already attest to the fact that running around looking for buffs to get their robots out every day stinks like a septic tank. MPs can attest to the fact that being spammed with tells "mochs plz" every time they set foot in public stinks like a compost pile. Now imagine most everyone looking for buffs and everyone having buffs someone wants? Is that fun?

5. PVP, already something of a joke, goes completely to the dogs. Either you buy the expansion and EverCamp for Shadowlands uber-stuff, then go through the routine of buffing up to use it, or you get easily demolished by someone who has.

Please, someone - anyone - from Funcom, please tell me I am wrong here, tell me that there's something I don't know or got wrong, and tell us what that is and how it makes things different.

Greetings,

What exactly is off-balance and "uneven" in the game, that requires CASTER level restrictions on a nano to be uploaded/cast? Understanding the base "cause" which has resulted in the "effect" (level restriction on casters uploading/casting a nano) would help tremendously in understanding Funcom's logic and reasoning.

I'm going to take a stab in the dark here and guess at what might be Funcom's reasoning:
Funcom wants to prevent (lower-level) over-equipping/"twinking".

If this IS the case, wouldn't a restriction--not on the CASTER of the nano--but on the RECIPIENT of that nano (buff) make far more sense?

In this scenario, it wouldn't prevent someone from being able to upload/cast a nano, if they meet the skill requirements, but, it does ensure that someone lower than the *appropriate level range* of that particular nano can't receive that buff. Logically, the nanos with recipient-level-restrictions would pertain specifically to skill/stat/ability buffs--the nanos which are the "cause" of the OE/twinking "effect". In this scenario, it would still allow for flexibility in which breed one chooses to play, and how one chooses to allocate their IP. The skill system currently in place would not be compromised. This scenario would be much less contradictory and much less defeating to the current skill system already in existance.


To reiterate:

- Level restriction is RECIPIENT only, NOT caster.
- A character of level lower than the appropriate level range of that nano can not receive that buff.
- The nanos with recipient-level-restrictions would pertain specifically to skill/stat/ability buffs.
