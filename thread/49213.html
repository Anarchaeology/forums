Should the CoT hand over it's own?
This could end up being a pretty nasty situation. As we've already seen, Omni obviously wants control of these people, since a secure Grid feed was refused. However, the Council cannot flat out deny Omni's request, or Omni-Pol will come into Tir and take who they're after.

The Council needs to make an effort to meet Omni-Pol's request, else the resistance that will result when Omni-Pol comes into Tir will not be completely justified.

My solution is to require Omni-Pol to open it's investigation to the Council of Truth as well as allow CoT delegates to accompany the wanted individuals to Omni HQ in order to monitor their treatment and questioning. There is no reason Omni should deny this request, as it would certainly avert an obvious confrontation.

All Omni grand-standing aside, I think the Tir Accord is with the CoT on this one. Omni would do best to stick to the rules as Mr. Ross has suggested.

quote:
The Omni-Tek police department (Omni-Pol) will be allowed to run routine investigations inside the Council-allocated districts in the interest of national security.

No Council or affiliated personnel within the boundaries of Council-allocated districts may be available for Omni-Pol questioning without the prior consent of the Council.
This basically means that Omni-Pol doesn't have a leg to stand on. Unless Omni-Pol can convince the CoT that it's a good idea to hand these people over, the CoT has no reason to, and in fact, should not. It would set a very bad precident, as was said in today's release. Omni-Pol is more than welcome to come over and poke around in Tir with the CoT's approval and monitoring, but hands-off all citizens unless we tell you otherwise.

It was actually a very gracious offer made by the CoT to allow Omni-Pol to interview the people in question via Grid feed. They had no reason to do it, and it shows that the CoT went that extra step in the name of cooperation. Now lets see Omni make a similar effort at compromise.
