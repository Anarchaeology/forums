FC needs to add more standard voice commands
I know many people have been putting out their own voice packs, and more power to them for doing it, however it's really, really hard to get everyone that has NW to d/l the voice packs so we're all uniform. Funcom needs to either record more of their own or, maybe better in my opinion, start a contest to get people to record their own voice packs and send them in to be judged and later patched in. I believe everyone agrees that 6 dinky lil voice commands don't help much at all. We need some like:

Follow me
Attack my target
Calm the adds
Cease fire
Attack the north/south/east/west side
Defend the N/S/E/W side
Command Acknowledged (Affirmative)
Command Declined (Negative)
What did you say? (Repeat)
Shazbot

And I'm sure several others that I can't think of right now but I know some of you people out there can. We need a much larger standard set of voices than what we have now. If an individual guild or person wants to release their own pack, then sure let them do it, but those of us who don't feel like downloading a new pack of user made voices every week in order to use them with the other 200 people on the planet (if we can find them) need an alternative. I'm in favor of a contest because I think I can do a killer opifex military and nano distinguished, but not much else I suppose you could send in up to 4 different race/gender/type combos (i.e. solitus/male/cool, opifex/male/cool, solitus/male/simple, atrox/simple) in order to make sure people only send in their best. What's the worst that could happen?
