Bravo Enerbias and Bonefish, er Boss... Wowzers!

Very good points, and summary about RP issues we see in Rubi-Ka these days. I couldn't possibly word it any better, Enerbias and Bonefish have done that for me, but I can voice my agreement with them.

We have to remember folks, IC, even if it can reflect a bit of RL personality is in no way intended to hurt OOC. I can't stress that one enough. Intention means a lot sometimes, and please, please remember, that no respectable RPer intends to annoy, hurt, or insult you as a player.
I may not roleplay a nasty bad guy, but I do sense on occasion that some could take my character a bit personal. Thankfully, it doesn't happen too often.. Just remember, if you're out there to RP, be prepared for anything, and be prepared to take it as you would IC and keep it there.. letting something like that get to you OOC isn't necessary, warranted, or worth it.

To those that keep up RP of a "nasty", my hat's off to you.. I know it's not easy. Also, I can tell you right now that I've got a lot of respect for a few IC enemies(*cough* Lille *cough* Val *cough* etc) of mine, because they are both mature and respectful of the concept of Roleplaying.

Now get out there and roleplay!
