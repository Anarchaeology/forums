How To Kill a trader (By a trader)
This is in response to all the Nerf Trader threads out there.
Im Airty, a level 47 Apprentice Trader. The fact of the madder is that i dont die, unless i am fighting an unusually good trader or every now and then a fixer or a MA with very high evades. The reason that i die is because i do not run.

At level 47 i am able to equip a 6 slot NCU belt, a level 120 Vector or ithica self buffed, and armor is not really an issue for any class.
With my 225 NCU (i could have more but i have a nano init chip in If i do not wish to i really dont ever have to die. With an Essence, HE, Offensive steam roller up, My drains are instant, i can root you for ever, and i have enough HP to take on 3 peopel at the same time. (I also like to have masteries running)

Now if i dont want to die, there is a 99% chance that i wont, and that is not because of skill, but because of how much traders just own in general. With Masteries up, i can drain most people weapons to 25% or 0% depending on how OE they are. Now against a fixer i may not ever be able to crit, and with a shottie that makes me usless, but the fixer wont be doing that much dammage to me with his gun at 25%. when i see my HP slowly depleating, i could root forever, instantly at full agg, and slowly make an exit.

A martial Artist with high evades is the only sure fire way to kill a trader at my level, and even then they can NOT use a claw. If you are inturupting my drains, Resisting my roots or casting Gernade incompatence on yourself to break them, i am worthless.
Most MA's that use claws continue to wield them even when i debuff them to 25%. TAKE THEM OFF! If you are puncing me you will do a TON more dammage then if you are still using your claw.
A non Melee class has little to no chance of killing me, except if they have maxed their evades. at level 47 my attack rating goes up to 550 when fully drained, and that is higher than anyone in my level range can get their evades without implants or Grid armor.

So here's the deal - want to kill a trader - dont fight unless he is self buffed. Have good evades, and carry around a little weapon you can wield at 100% when fully drained, because it will deal a lot more dammage then your current weapon at 50% or less. I have lost to an ENF that put on a krys knife (level200) and slaughtered me.

Melee- Save your special attacks for when i start to drain, because if i dont get my drains off my own gun is only 50%, and i ownt do anything to you. If i dont get drains off fast enough the fight is nearly over.

Ranged- Just have a smaller gun, thats all you can do.

SO all in all at levels 74 and down traders are not invincable when self buffed, but i realize that if i dont want to die, i dont really need to. NOT BRAGGING because its not me thats doing it, its the fact that i am a trader. I will continue the post later, please respond.
