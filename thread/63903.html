I think he meant like an alliance channel.
Yes you can make one now, but as soon as the leader logs out the channel is closed.

Example:

The Mockers places the winning bid for being the official Clan Police Department for one term. (Maybe one RL month).
To setup communications with law enforcement departments in Neut and Omni areas a special Law Dispatch channel is created.
Anyone sanctioned to be a law enforcer is added to the channel as soon as they log in.

Another example:

CoA needs to coordinate the war effort and creates a CoA channel.
Everyone who is a member of a CoA guild and has higher ranking then member is added.
