Again, I fail to see the an exploit potential big enough to warrant the drastic measure. Read below for my take on mobiles.

1) Increase range of plants and other static mobs (now there's a contradiction in terms)

2) The problem did not exist for mobiles. No matter how far away they were hit from they always aggroed back. Granted, their pathing is horrible and they could occasionally take forever finding you (even in the middle of a featureless desert), but crappy pathing code isn't an exploit.

3) Add higher range enhancer ability to weapons. They need some option of adding range (at the cost of damage/specials) to be able to combat high range nano classes. The new tradeitems giving higher range should of course be loot only and as rare as nullity sphere. Maybe after a few hundred soldiers mail you complaining they can't find high range extenders yall will see the folly of the non-availability of the "NT fix" nanos
