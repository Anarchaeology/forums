No, you are confusing 2 stats. The Positive Agg/Def trimmer changes the robots "Agg/Def" bar, the same way you have an Agg/Def bar. The q100 Agg/Def trimmer increases the bots speed, but lowers it's evades.

The Mudslinger-Defamation 101-Character Assasin line along with the higher level +ar pet buffs add "agressivness." It's supposedly supposed to cause that much more aggro on a mob when hit by the bot. They DO work. On a raid to high level mobs, since I am usually the only bot user, my bot usually flickers aggro from the tank. It'actually sort of funny.
