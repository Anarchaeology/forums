The Nanomage Liberation Front Recruiting
Hello all you Nanomages and Mighty Atrox! The Nanomage Liberation Front is here to help you, and we need your help to save Rubi-Ka. For more information tune into our gridstream at http://www.zenpushergasoline.com/nlf .

OOC: we are a zealot militaristic organization bent on ending all mining on Rubi-Ka and the betterment on Nanomage kind. We are also interested in helping our much abused Atrox brothers. We are a roleplaying org first, but we also PvP, team, help eachother get items and at camps and all the other fun things to do on Rubi-Ka. Internally we have a family atmosphere so if you are interested you can contact Aazamon, Ramiyana, Lydadoc, Zargin, or Queen Fleshreaper online. Or post here. Please before asking to join we ask that you read our site, manifesto, history, and story so you know what we are truly about. Thank you very much.
