Used item stores
In the 'Envisioned' section, you ask for ideas on how to implement a method that items can be sold. My suggestion is a Used Item store, similar to a general store. Let's say that I find an Engineer nanocrystal on a mission. I go to a machine in the used general store in a city. I click on that machine bringing up a window, I move the crystal from my inventory to the machine's window and enter a price. That item then appears, alphabetically, in the Engineer nanocrystal machine in that same used item store with the price that I requested. Another player finds it and buys it just as he would from a regular general store machine. I then get a message saying that it was sold and the credits are added to my total (perhaps after the machine takes a cut off the top?).

Perhaps if there are already at least, say, 10 of those nanocrystals in the machines already then it won't let me sell it there and I have to wait or find another used store to sell it. Maybe if it unsold after 15 days, the item is given back to me (prevents items sitting with ridiculous prices for too long).

Just an idea off the top of my head. Might be a coding nightmare, I don't know. Certainly would be great if it could become a reality.
