Suggestion for Added Content
I left AO about 9 months ago and came back about 2 weeks ago. I like the changes and I think things are beginning to head in the right direction. However, the biggest concern of mine has not as of yet been addressed.

I think the largest problem is with the storyline/content of AO. Its lacking. It somehow feels hollow. I feel like I can keep doing missions indefinetly and will at some point reach the end. What end? What am I doing anyway? I would like to know I have some purpose perhaps and that I should be focusing on something imparticular.

Here is my suggestion. Its probably already been suggested but I'll spit it out anyway.

I would like to see profession specific quests that mark, reward and limit levels. So a fixer for example, must visit his profession boss (for lack of a better fantasy term) before he can do any more missions once the he has reached level 5. He will speak with his boss and get rewarded for his level ( a token or a nano perhaps? ). He will also receive a tough mission. A mission that he cannot do by him/her self. He must create a group. It requires that everyone in the group gets the exp from the boss mob or the quest is failed and must be started over. It requires teamwork and to make friends/talk with others before you can do any more missions. Every 5 (or 10 or some parabolic curve) levels you must do a quest from your Boss. The higher the level, the more complex the quest will get. Maybe you have to travel for some specific drops, return them to a person in a city, then meet someone in a different city, have a tradeskill person make you something, return to your boss and THEN receive the mission. The rewards for the mission should be profession specific. From lvl 75 and up missions could involve some form of assassination of players from opposing sides (other than neutrals).

You could do a lot to this to make it more indepth and coloful. This would be a huge step to adding much needed content and ensuring memebers must help each other on occation as well as ask for help from those they normaly wouldnt. It forces people to expand their game knowlege, visit places they normally wouldnt go and the quests would explain parts of the storyline and various subplots.

Even if this is blown off, I hope some other changes are coming to increase the plot of the game. I dont want to have to give it up again. Good work so far. The changes to date are very nice. I agree with another post: Give up on the mechanics. Dedicate just one person to balance (most players would love to help with unbiased input if you but ask) and put ALL the rest on content. Story story story. Make me go do dangerous things so I can fullfill some difficult part of the storyline.
