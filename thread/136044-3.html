I was upset when I woke up this morning to find this thread muddled with the same flames we see everywhere else :/

Let's keep the focus here on MPs and leave the other classes to their own boards, if we could.

We're all upset over the past two years and that's perfectly understandable, but we're going to have to demonstrate the self control to keep our focus true and not start commenting on other classes or dooming our event to failure. Too much hindsight and not enough foresight. Or maybe it's the other way around.

Cz's responses on this thread give me alot of hope that we are and will be listened to, and that this event will be taken seriously.

I also wanted to clarify my point about our problems.

Last night myself, Kurushimi (engineer), peircingevil (NT) and Forgottenone (MA) had a conest in the backyard to see who could kill the guides in the backyard the fastest.

Kurushimi and I both killed our guides at the same time in all three of our tests. I beat piercingevil by something ike a half second (I'd call this a tie actually) and beat out Forgottenone by a slightly larger (but still tiny) margin.

Kurushimi was using martial arts with a 15% scope and a slayerdroid.

Forgottenone was using fists and a QL149 Torture Tool

Piercingevil was using nukes and a pistol

I was using nukes, Enmity and Manex.

If you look closely, you'll see what's wrong with this picture. Engineers have a +300 MA buff in the form of slayerdroid transferance and green tradeskills to equip scopes. By using Martial arts with a scope, she fits her profession's profile quite well and uses the tools of her trade to do the job.

Forgottenone uses his fists and an MA only weapon to get the job done. Both tools of his trade.

Peircingevil has light blue pistol skill, so her profile does indeed suggest that the Pain of Patricia she wields fits her profile well. Pevil has a low attack rating with her pistol, as she implants for MC instead of pistol and her towers are geared towards nanoskills instead of weapons. The pistol is almost an afterthought instead of her main focus and probably not needed in some situations.

Now me....I use a

non-standard implant layout that gimps my debuffing skills (tools of my trade) and instead focuses entirely on SMG and ranged init skill instead of nano skills

a weapon that requires me to max two dark dark blue weapons skills and a dark blue init skill

a ql179 pet instead of a ql200 pet

4 high level towers, two of which are there solely to boost my weapon attack rating and one of which fills in the hole left from using a bright SMG cluster instead of a bright TS or MC cluster in the right hand, and a faded SMG in the chest instead of a faded SI

Uberly rare and hard to acquire equipment to boost my attack rating a little bit more

All this and I only tie with classes that only used their own profession's tools. Kurushimi did not have to wreck her nanoskills or use a weapon that did not fit her profile. Piercingevil was able to focus mainly on her nanoskills instead of having to worry about her attack rating with her gun and Forgottenone used his toolset without any deviation whatsoever.

Now imagine if I had solely used items from the MP toolset and the MP profile to do the job and arranged my implants to boost nanoskills instead of weapons skills.

I'd be wielding either a pillow or creation weapons and have a slow hitting demon, bugged mezz pet.

How differently would that contest have gone if I was using only my profession's tools?

Granted, my debuffing skills would be insanely good......but what good are debuffing skills in pvm? What benefit would I bring my team?

Sure, mobs will indeed have true nanoskills in shadowlands, or so we hear. So then are we to follow around our team until we come across a mob that requires a debuff?

This is a damage/healing based game at its very core. A hack n slash. If you aren't healing or mezzing, you better be hacking and slashing and hold your own against the others, or you're not doing your part.

So we've become a redundancy and a burden if we use our own tools and only our own tools to aid our team. If mochams are the only thing we can bring to the table to help our team, then please take them back and give us something worthwhile.

Someone had mentioned the crat and that's a good point. The main difference between the MP and the crat is that the crat has things he can bring to the table that benefits everyone, regardless of profession or level. Don't kid me here. Crat speeches are loved by everyone. XP buffs are loved by everyone. Charms, roots, snares, calms, AOE calms, roots and snares. Even your pet is useful because of your long term AMS buffs plus the addition of your speech which is also casted on the pet. Crat is the ultimate support class with a purpose and a desire in every team. Your profession profile also suggests that pistol (even smg) works well for your profession. So using only your profession's tools and rich toolset, you are highly desired and highly useful. you also do insane damage when using your toolset to the fullest (charms if possible). you have your problems, but your problems do not affect in you EVERY situation, like our problems affect us in every situation.

I can see traders point of view in some situations. The uncle bazzit shotgun however is the start of a nice fix. Rare, but when coupled with something like a home defender and an incredibly high attack rating, not too bad at all. Barter armor is also easy to come by and recently got a boost. There's signs that your issues are slowly being thought out and addressed and you're able to use the tools of your profession well. Your calms and roots and AOE roots will benefit your team in most situations and your wrangles benefit everyone in every situation. You're also faced with the same nano resist issues that we are and you have a few horribly broken tools, but in the whole you're desired and needed for all situations. I see lots of traders lately using manexes and rifles which is a bad sign (that your profile is not entirely viable), but you can and do make up for it with increased AR.

In addition, in some ways you do our own job much better than we do. We boost the nanoskills of another person by up to 140 points, each boosted nanoskill requiring 50-52 ncu each, whereas the trader boosts all nanoskills and all weapons skills at the same time up to 132, can cast this effect on all teammates simultaneously and has an ncu footprint of about half that of two mochams.

I don't mean to downplay the above classes, but I wanted to make the point of their differences from our problems.

We do have buffs that lower level players and a couple higher level people need to boost their own toolset up, but like someone said, if there was a mocham machine by the mission terminals, would we been needed at all? Can the trader not fill in?

Just things to think about to clarify our situation.

Every class has issues, we just happen to have alot of issues. Deep down everyone playing this game knows that, but everyone wants their profession to be next in line for review. That's understandable and forgiveable, but for all those that play other professions posting here, we're only asking you to put aside your own needs and desires and support us in just getting a few answers, some time, some caring. We don't want the moon, we'd settle for moonpies. The purpose of this event is not to immediately get fixed, but rather just to talk and feel as if we have interaction and start on the long road to improved communication about our class, which I'm sure will eventually lead to fixes.

Everyone, put down your guns and let this thread remain peaceful and not sullied with angry words and insults and your own needs/desires and picture the following imagery in your head.

A hungry middle classed child is walking down the street and is upset that his mother will not give him an ice cream cone or McDonald's. On the way home while walking with mum, they pass by a starving homeless child in tears because his stomach hurts so bad from malnourishment and all he wants is a scrap of bread so he can settle his tummy and sleep.

The MP profession is like that starving child right now. You're all hungy and that's understandable, but if communication and love is our food, then we haven't eaten in years. Please think of that before you mention your own class's desires on this thread.
