[nostalgia]Early on when I started playing this game, the world seemed endless..

I had visions of preparing for the voyage, saving up money for a car, and making the trek to that other city, then returning to my apartment in Tir with all the things I had found far away.[/nostalgia]
It's true that right now, without a Yalm, there are very few places that I can't get to thanks to the whompa system and the grid. For me, that makes the places that are off the beaten track all the more interesting - or it would if it weren't for the fact that one place is pretty much like another, apart from the scenery. The same goods are available in the same shops, you get the same missions with the same interiors etc.

Maybe it'd be different if the three whompa networks weren't all connected, or if there was no grid. Then it'd be something special to be a Fixer, MP or Adventurer since you'd have access to places that others couldn't easily reach. There would be a logical reason to differentiate the economies of different zones, and therefore a good reason to make the difficult trek from one territory to another.

Of course, the current refusal of vendors to sell to those outside their own faction acts to discourage, rather than encourage travel. My home base is my home base because I can restock my supplies there, and I can't if I relocate to somewhere else.

Without some compelling reason to travel outside your own whompa net, nerfing travel would probably result in three different Rubi-Ka's, Clan, Omni and Neutral, with little or no traffic between them. Missions in other zones do not count as "compelling" since you have the option to ignore them...
