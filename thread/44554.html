Good question
This is for clans only, not single character. A player is of course allowed to affiliate ("join" in a RP manner) one of the large clans, like the Knights, but we do not currently have the resources to deal with individual characters' memberships.

Affiliation with the listed clans is like being a sub-clan of e.g. The Knights. For the structures it will be as supporters of e.g. The Council of Truth. (I.e. you're not getting a seat in CoT for affiliating with them. )

Note that being a sub-clan of The Knights basically puts you in support of the CoT anyway, since those are affiliated already.
