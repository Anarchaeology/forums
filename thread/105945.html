The Ninja of A.I.R!!
Last night while performing a noble task for a man, who wouldn’t like to have his name mentioned here, I entered a house in Home East.

While preparing for the battle ahead, we agreed the looting was for all, and anything good, we divide among us, on the lottery principle, everyone agreed to that, and then my mighty team started to perform the noble task of removing the OT occupation troops stationed there.

The first part was tough, but trough hard work and a bit of good luck we cleared the first rooms.

Then a MA of A.I.R started to pull the OT troops to the door for us to kill them, shortly after he started to pull, we started getting reports that indicated that he was looking in all chests while he ran by them.

Oh well as team leader I told him that looting chests was considered ok, as long as he shared his loot with his team. I got the response "OK" so I thought nothing of if, until the message contained the words "Notum" and "ql 200" in the same line. Naturally we where very excited, since this could mean a large boost for our team, so started a roll. Everyone had a fair chance, and Jpegg, an Agent of Excalibur won the roll, now the MA didn’t understand the simplest language on his comm., and he kept the fragment for himself.

This was not the way we wanted it to go :-( But after some messaging i got the answer that
"He could open the chests so he should get all the loot".

After this the team broke up, and I had to return to my employer and tell him the mission was a failure.

Just a warning to you all, even the nicest player may turn out to be more aligned with OT that you think.

Stoffer “Team Leader”
149 Clan Doc

(OOC) Last night I was on a mission with http://www.anarchy-online.com/charac.../name/kreathor of the clan http://www.anarchy-online.com/org/stats/d/1/name/315393. While we where buffing we decided to leave team loot at all, and anything good would be rolled apon(namely Fragments and dog tags). But kreathor decided that since he was pulling mobs to the entrance and he could open chests, he should get all the content of said chests.
Naturally we asked him to concentrate on the pulling, and that we would share whatever was in the chest when the floor was clear. He just kept doing it, and the final straw was when he looted a ql 200 Notum fragment, and didn’t "understand" what I wrote to him in English, because he was French. So I tried Babel Fish as translator from my English to something as close to French as I could manage, still no reply while asking him to give the fragment to Jpegg.
Later I just got “I can open the chests, so I should get everything that’s in it” line, and I did something I haven’t done in a long time ”./ignore kreathor”. Just a warning for the rest of you guys out there.

You know what I do in such cases?

Might seem a bit drastic, but what always works imho is, when you are team leader, let him pull, midfight, the rest of the team stops attacking. Let the character in question draw the aggro (preferrably when there are adds around) and then just kick him out of team in midfight.

The sight of him going down, shouting for help can be quite amusing and ahhh... in fact rewarding. (*chuckles*)

He will keep his uber loot, but will at least not reap the XP from a mish that he carried out on the backs of the other team mates.

I know it is harsh, some might say even fiendish, but if someone chooses to ignore basic rules of working in a team, my opinion is that he should be shown that he might as well and go try to do it ALL by himself.
