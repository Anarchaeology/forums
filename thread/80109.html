What reason is there to fight...

Well I guess the main reason is to have FUN!!
Geez I never understand why people think they need some reward or something to actually do something in MMO's. Maybe it stems from the leveling mentality we all get - work on building your character for hours a day so that you have a nice finished product in the end. So we are all looking for that end reward of having a great character. But everyone seems to forget about haveing FUN. Grinding it out is not all there is to AO.

There is so many things you can do if you dont constantly focus on level, level, level. Go explore a bit, hang out in the reet retreat and try and learn some new dance moves (believe me i was a skeptic till i tried it, it really turned out to be a heck of alot of fun), and now you can go do team based pvp.

This is the funest thing of all. Absolutely fantastic. Sad that they had to add something new to the game for people to discover what was available the entire time this game has been out. I bought AO envisioning the battles that are finnally hapening now. Unfortunitly, untill now whenever I mentioned anything about team based or an entire guild vs. another guild the only reply I got was "why man we dont get any titiles for team pvp"

Well it's simple. IT'S FUN!! Can't speak for anyone else but that is why I play games: to have fun. I need not gain some title on my char that only states how much I can twink it. Big deal anyone can do that. My char is better the yours blah blah blah. Wow let me bow down to your magnificent talent.

Now team based adds a whole new element. STRATAGY. Use roots to hold the melee of your doc, kill the nukers first (they die fast and do alot of damage if you dont kill them fast)... Anyway you get the point. It's not about how twinked your char is now, it's about how well you play it.

Sorry this has gotten a little off topic. I hope I gave you a couple good reasons to enjoy the game you pay for much more fully. Take advantage of all it has to offer. Everyone will hit lvl 200 eventually, but not everyone remembers to enjoy the ride along the way.
