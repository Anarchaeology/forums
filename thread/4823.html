The Shovahs standpoint about the bombing
In my dreams I saw this, I knew this would happen. I knew about this event.
I told no one for no one would ever listen, but hear this.

I know what I know, for what I know is a great many things.
If you hope to know what I know, then succumb to the nothingness so you cannot know nothing. For when you know nothing you truly know everything.

If I say that I know nothing about nothing do I then mean that I know anything about nothing?

I knew about this event but no one would listen. I speak in riddles and metaphores for a reason. The truth is that I have spoken about this event, alas I spoke in metaphores and in tounge. All perceive me as a crazy man, not a farsighted one and I can live with this for I have no other choice. For I am proud of who I am and what I am.

People think that we, the shovahs, bring destruction and decay with us. Why do the civilisation of man think like this, I know, for they are afraid of the greater good and many are afraid of what they do not know. But all know in their hearts and soul what the greater good is. One thing is for certain, I feel no regret for what has happened, only that of the corruption of myself. But I was cleansed by the ritual of release, but still many, many hunt me, they wish to see me dead.
Still many, many follow the cause of the greater good, spreading the word and tutoring possible shovahs. For all among you walk the path of enlightenment, it is although hard to perceive this path when many walk the path of paradox, or the broadest road.

All those who died in the accident I feel no pity for, they have all joined and are one as they are many. They have joined with their brethren in the plane of soul and life. This is the way it should be, so do not mourn for them, they are in a better plane. I saw what had to be done, but did not act against this act.

It is all for the greater good and we can all affect the outcome, in our hearts we are all shovahs and we all have a great destiny. Awaken this destiny and affect, for we are shovahs and we know what must be done!

It is of course sad that entitys have to leave this plane, but in the end there will be no loss because all death in this plane counts as one step further into the greater good.

Oshovah
The farsighted
Voice of the planet
Undying flame of Jehna
Shovah of the first circle
Master of the alpha plane

Shovahs
I advice everyone to listen to the words of Oshovah, he is a man of wisdom.
I myself is an Apprentice of Oshovah, though I am not a Shovah yet, but hopefully the time for that will come soon.

I am not the only one who believe RubiKa has a life of its own, or rather a consciousness. We, the people of RubiKa, must learn to listen to the world around us, we must learn to see the signs, we must be one.

I was "Awakened", if you can call it so, on my very first night on RubiKa. I had a dream, not like any other dream, although it took me a long time, and a lot of help, to fully understand the blessing of that dream I will tell you of soon. It opened my mind, I saw new things, it was a beginning, a beginning on a long road, though I haven't walked fare yet I sense great things...

The dream:
"I was lying in the grass, there was a silence, unlike my apartment in Omni trade, I was very relaxed as the wind grew stronger. The moons started to move faster, suddenly it was day and the suns where rasing across the sky, and then the night came again, faster this time, it all went faster, faster, and faster still.
I was still calm, I knew nothing could hurt me, the wind grew even more until a storm was upon me.
the trees around me bent in the storm, until there roots snapped and they flew up as the wing grew even stronger, yet I was untouched. Soon the grass followed the trees, and the thin layers of earth.
There I was, in a red wasteland, the wasteland that IS RubiKa.
As the wind calmed down I felt something taking hold of me, dragging me in to red wasteland, down towards the coil of the planet. I saw things on my way down, things I will not utter here and now, if ever.
In the burning coil of the red planet, I felt overwhelmed, I saw faces, I felt feelings, feelings of thousands of individuals, yet they where one, they where lost, cod not find home, if they even knew they had one, I know, I was there.
Then, I remember only one thing... pain..."


If you have had similar experiences, please contact Oshovah, we have to unite.
If you only knew the feeling of surrendering to nothingness...
