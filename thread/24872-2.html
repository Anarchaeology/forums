Wow a constructive soldier thread for once

Without reading much of the others top ten I got some of my own.

1) Soldiers should be able to wield an weapon without meeting all the requirements. Why? Reson: OE patch, when a soldier is at level 200 he got maybe 1100 to AR and 800 to LR. Let's say that is buffed and fully implanted.

The only thing that makes a good soldier, is a soldier that can do the nova / flashpoint change. You face a trader, deprive / plunder (advanced) whoa you have -415 debuff running. throw in general inexperiences and you got -435. Even if you have 1200 to ar and 900 to lr that's 33% on ar and almost 50% on lr. This could be one of the soldier specific things, since we don't have any other uniqueness with us unless for 1:20 minutes max.

2) Combat buffs, we have TMS I - X other than that we have total focus, which is a attack rating buff and debuffs our evades. What I'd like is something that makes the soldiers furious and angry in combat and causing a havoc. Like increasing all weaponskills for just 30 seconds, giving us hp and something to endure combat, not something to be shot at and just as easily rooted with. You stand there like a baffoon looking at those who laugh at you from a distance. The only reason why I have nanopool over 1k is because I want to use MK X, there's no other reason why I want to use nano besides buffing my weapon skills to raise my attackrating.

3) More weapons, ammo which does different damage, energy weapon ammo, radiation weapon ammo, even random weapon damage ammo. So that people don't get a 30% deflect and use energy bracers and have a 1 hour 10 minute long energy weapon ammo mk.

4) More guns, heaps of them. I want to have an arsenal that is as big as the nuke arsenal that NTs have. And I don't want guns with 1-xxx give me a gun with 100-300 that would make me happy. As a level 171 soldier it is NOT funny to hit a mob for 1 point of damage with a ql 200 nova (which I did today).

5) Make soldiers able to get their nanoresist up, I mean seriously NTs have better evade buff then us, give us a big nanoresist buff.

6) Don't shut down all the nanoskills when mk is up. Why do we even have aggro nanos? When we put up mk when the mob is on us, chances are that it changes it target. When it does, our taunts doesn't work... Lock mc & ts and we can't chain cast mk.

7) Smg, shotgun, ar, lr, fling, burst and full auto lines should be added. Srdeni had an idea earlier about this. We should be getting +25, 50, 90 and 140, kinda like mp nano lines. They should be exactly the same but it helps a soldier equip AND do far more damage with weapons than any other profs wielding one.

8) Make the best weapons be built, where you can add components and it changes. For instance you have a base weapon. Requires 800 to ar, has 20m range, 1.5 second attack and 20 clip.

To the weapon base you add a scope, which gives it 30m range. That will make it require 850 to AR. Then you add a possibillity to burst, and add requirement of 500. You want the gun to be faster, then requirements of both goes up, now it requires 875 to ar and 550 to burst. Add fling and you need 400 to fling and the rest goes up 25.

Let's say you want it do do chem damage, you add a component and make it slower. You want even more range, then the damage goes down. You want more damage, the speed drops. You want faster weapon again, the requirements goes up. You want more clip, well add the clip and you get a slight increase of skill. This is just an example of how it can be done. There are many ways to how this can change, but just an idea to get it started.

Or let us add and modify existing weapons or have a weapon that we can 'level' too. (latter is hard to explain, but if you played planescape torment and had D'akkon in your group, he had weapon like that which 'grew' with him).

9) New armor that is soldier only, shouldn't be uber or anything, but it should add small amounts to weapon skills. It should also add something to ncu and a small amount to hp. So it will be an alternative for soldiers to wear instead of MK II armor (corona).

10) Grid nano. It's useful, I want it.


That was my top 10, err actually I didn't have 10, but 9 & 10 were made up just to get 10.

Just a thought tho. Make a general grid nano. If everyone can use a grid nano that takes 2 minutes, why not ? Why do docs & traders got it? Why do NTs have warp time and space rome, newland etc.. ? I'd say make it equal, it doesn't give any benefits, but it may actually make people travel more. So make everyone able to grid home. Personal and team grid.

I am not a soldier, but...
I am not a soldier, but now is the time when the un-gimpy fixers need to start giving you guys a hand to get as fixed as we were. Without further addo I give a top 10 things I'd like to see you soldiers doing.

1. Combat armor - Soldier only. Backslot exoskeleton armor armor which fits over an air conditioned, tactical computational suit. Individual air conditioned pieces provide bonuses to nanoskills, weapons skils, and whathave you, similar to the love suit for a fixer. Exoskeleton allows for limited 'jumpjet' style flight, where for a minute, a soldier can fly (must land in order to avoid fall damage), allowing for greater speed. Each 'jump' shuts down flight capabilites for 5 minutes. While in flight, a soldier cannot fight, but it allows them to cover greater distances quicker.

2. Give Soldiers 'Sides' Map upgrade even without the map, just use a black screen (replacing 'no map' text) with little apropriate colored dots for mobs and players. The soldiers job is to be in control of the battlefield.. that means collecting logsitcal information from the surrounding area.

3. Combination Weapons - Soldier only. I want to see the soldier with the heavy assault rifle/grenade launcher combination. Yes, that means Grenades need to be fixed. Grenade launcher range should be near max, ar range should be apropriate shorter. I want to see grenades devastate a group of people, not just one of them. But rather than switch between two weapons, just change the weapon functionality. This also somewhat adresses the Mk/Weapon switching issue.

4. Hardcore Combat analysis tool - HUD2, level upgradable. Using this item does not increase the attack rating of the soldier, but decreases the defensive capabilities of the oponent targeted, effectively 'painting' the target for other teammates, and making the soldier the guy everyone wants to /assist.

5. A Berret. Agents get the Balacava... Soldiers get the Berret. Once again, combat bonuses, nano bonuses, etc.

6. (its for everybody yes, but) A reload button.

7. New ammo types for soldiers, armor piercing, flechette, explosive. Armor piercing ignores a portion of AC based on the QL of the ammo (like fixer ammo). Flechette does low damage, but is less effected by DMS modifiers, think in terms of GA fixers - low armor, high DMS, acceptable, but not uber damage is still output by the soldier. Explosive amunitions would do an initial damage, and then a nano effect fire damage against the target if they hit successfully. Note: I'd give agents hollowpoint - not soldiers.

8. New line of nanos revolving around outdoor combat - namely artilery barrages. No idea how to do this.

I do not have a 9 or 10.
