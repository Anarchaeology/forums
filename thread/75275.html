So, when most people don't get NW on Friday?
Well, in my earlier post I said that none of the stores in my town (Lexington, KY) had heard of or stocked AO:Notum Wars. I called again, thinking that with the holiday rush, it might be hard to ship stuff (see AO News & Updates). Needless to say, none of them had it, knew of it, or had it in their computers.

Well, we've discussed this before, so I'll stop beating the horse (he's already dead). My point today is:

What happens when most people don't get Notum Wars tomorrow?

This would be an incredibly unfair advantage to certain mega-guilds, or guilds lucky enough to get their hand's on a few copies. They will have extra days or even weeks before the general population of AO even gets to install the game, destroying the prinicple of the game. If a few guilds occupy all of the territory and fortify it, they will have an unfair advantage which will be unlikely to match.

How will Funcom account for this? We know it's not FC's fault that shipping is fubar, bur should the players be punished for that?

A single guild can have only one controller of each level range, which means one guild can control at most 5 areas, all of differing level ranges.

Bearing in mind that PvP level range rules apply to attacking towers, or them attacking you, a guild must have a good number of members at each level range if they expect to be able to defend all 5 areas.

Also note that it may take a while for guilds to actually be able to place towers....

Tower placement requires Complit and Psychology. (Psych? Why? To make you spend IP in an otherwise useless skill, natch!)

It may take some levelling before those high level guildies have the IP to raise psych, if they haven't.

Not a BAD req, I think it's 400 Psych for a level 200 tower, but it will take some redoing of points.
