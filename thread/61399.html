The dungeon is in the zone called "The Longest Road".

Basically, grid to Athen, go west, into Athen shire and look for the long road on your map

Follow that road until you see a piece of the road missing (a pothole, fairly large). This means you should turn south and follow the path in the intersection going south. This will take you into an Omni-tek outpost. There is a large cavelike entrance in the west(?) part of the outpost with a white sign that says "Foreman's".

Once you enter, you will be met by some truly stunning architecture. High ceilings, pillars, banners. And lots of low level mobs called "Base defenders" wearing omni-pol and using Sunbursts.

The dungeon is designed for a team of 60-80ish level I belive, and I could solo my way down there with ease as a level 100 MP (just breaking out of my title cap so I could've done it at level 90ish as well).

Mobs get progressively harder, and while the base defenders would hit me for 40-60 dmg (I only have 2500 ac), the suppressors you will meet further down hit for 130-150 and the bodyguards for 250-300.

As a non-healing class you may encounter some trouble, but Mps, calmers or healers should have no trouble going down there alone.

Once you enter a big circleformed room with a stairwell in the middle and 4(?) bodyguards on the ground level, you are close to the "Lab director". This is a crat boss who drops the infamous crat forms, the bow tie form, and the IMI Desert reet 1000 pistol. He was pretty easy to solo and conned almost full green to me at level 100.

It got a bit hairy at one point because my heal pet had a LOS bug and I was being wailed on by 4 bodyguards, but once the Lab Director went down the bodyguards fell one after one.

The bodyguards drop the Gamma Ejector SMGs, which are good for fixers, the assault tank armour which is good for Enforcers (enf only, lets you have a 2000dmg absorb shield with a long recharge I think). If you are after the Doc combat tank, they drop off of Neutralizers, who are doctor mobs with MA and some sort of stick about halfway down to the Lab director. The "personal safes", that contain an instruction disc and a trade skill item, drop off of security guards, who are atrox mobs with warblades.

If you bypass the round rooms and keep going east I think it is, along the corridor, you will soon reach a U shaped corridor room with security guards that leads down to the TIM room.

TIM, that drops the engineer STM scope and the +180 ranged init scope is a huge slayerdroid mob (he looks very very cool) and cons limeish to me at level 100. I tried to solo him for the hell of it, and got him to 80% before he had killed both my pets and started hitting on me.

All in all, Foreman's (or "Biomare" or "TLR") is a very nice dungeon, and is something you almost have to experience
