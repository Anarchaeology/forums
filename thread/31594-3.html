New Grid: Reason to team with Fixers
Interesting thread, tho the subject has wavered a *tad*
IMHO, the 14.4 (Fixer Love) patch does indeed have many good point for making Fixers more attractive to both solo play, and to have in teams:

- New Fixer Grid to improve transportation, making 'remote' areas more accessible (hey, might even reduce lag in BS) The more adventurous players will appreciate this :-)

- Fixer NCU buffs to help team share buffs, maybe also freeing belt slots for CPU OPtimizers, Recompilers and Range Increasers

- Speed buffs: Damage penalty removed, also introduced team versions. Essential for outdoor hunting and for pullers.

- Frightened Rollerats aggro from 14.4 - Fixers can now be useful in disarming the chest and dispose of the damned rat in a ... um... appropriate way.

- Better SMGs are coming (not necessarily in 14.4) , so all you 'damage dealers' better watch out
