Screenshots for the best
Hi all, I'm French and please apologize for my poor language.

I just got a little idea with screenshoots. We know all, Adventuring Skill isn't useful for all. 21 Adv' Little Backpack are enough for everybody and fall damage ... But in roleplay, Adventurers are the best geographical technicians. I think we could have new items called 'Cameras' with differents QL using Adventuring skill.
F12 key could work only if you are equipped with a camera in slot Hud. So, with different QL, we could have different quality images like filters of Photoshop (but easy coding). Low QL could significate missing photography (there would not be any scrnxxx.png). High QL could take nice screenshoots with perfect colors.
Taking screenshots could cost 1 to 10 credits, does works or not.

Let me inagine discuss between two people of RK :
- Hey Marco, take a look to this amazing monster !
- Yep ! Could make a screenshoots but my camera isn't good at all
- I have a good one, I'll do )
