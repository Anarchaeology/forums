The "best" solution to BS/20k Mission Problem
As well all know, Funcom will probably not impliment Outdoor Hunting for high-levels. And personally, I understand their reasoning. Hunting usually leads to greifing, kill-stealing, camp-camping, etc. [what really needs to be done] And since funcom is far too lazy to put in a *lot* of hunting spots, or a few *very large ones* that everyone can *share from* where it's not a *25% zone*, then maybe outdoor hunting would be viable.. [/what really needs to be done]

But that won't happen. So, I believe, that we need to do something about the endless BS/20k mission boredom. The best way to add fun is risk. It's boring when there is no risk. I am not saying make the current mobs harder, but give us a REASON to do missions filled with aces/real means. I have been fighting the same mobs (Vets/Masters) since they were blood red at level 110 or so. Still blood-red at level 150.. but then it started getting a bit orange at 160.. lighter-orange for many at 170.. at 180 I saw my first yellow/greens.. and now at 191 I see the ocasional -----|o---- mob.. but most are green/yellow. The same mobs I fought 81 levels ago I am fighting now. How increadiably fun.

So, what I think needs to be done.

Due to the fact that Aces/Real means HP/ACs/Damage scales HORRIBLY, you need to equalize that with an insane XP scale. For example, I occasionally blitz ql 240ish missions for Animas to sell to stores (Cause I was tired of boring missions.. just thought I'd do something and make a little cash on the side... not that you need it.) Mobs there occasionally hit me for over 800 damage, through 40%-45% reflect. A mob like that would probably give around 90k XP for me in a team. Insane, isn't it? It's like trying to kill tarasque, except you get insanely low xp for it.

So, you should either

A) Increase the XP on RMs/Aces drastically, increasing as the difficulty increases. (i.e, a low Real Mean or Ace you see in a regular ql 195 or so mission gives 100k in a team with peeps your level, and in a ql 220 mission, you have ql 220 Real Means / Aces that give over 500k xp a kill. That seems like a lot, but that would take a lot of time to do, and is high risk.) This would give people incentive to do high-level missions. High Xp, High Risk. Makes it so the team must use stratagy and once again stick together.. hell, maybe even having a healing class would be required!

B) Increase the Xp Reward on ql 200+ missions drastically. Therefore this gives another incentive without changing much of the game mechanics of Xp rewards on mobs, etc. A ql 200 mission, drawn by a level 200, will give about 1.7 million xp. A ql 220 mission should give around 5-10 mill. (get rid of the stupid 5% cap) The problem with this method is that death isn't much of an issue, so long as you can get the xp reward. (and also encourages mission jumping.. leaving if the mission is anything but very small)

So, I believe that A is the best (TEMPORARY, funcom, MAKE OUTDOOR HUNTING W ORK) solution.

Just my 2,000 credits (inflation bites)
