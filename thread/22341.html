Fixers and the detaunt item
There's been a bit of backlash towards the news that Agents are getting an item that helps detaunt a MOB off them. The backlash stems from a comment Trond made a while back in which he said an item like that would be great for the Fixers but at that time we weren't able to give anyone that kind of thing. Then, lo and behold, the Agents get one such item.

Now I want to let you all know that Fixers are also getting an item which helps detaunt MOBs, although it will work in a different way to that of the Agent item. This has been on the Fixer slate for some time now, but due to the Agent patch coming first, the Agent item was announced first. I didn't expect the reactions to it I got actually, so am now coming here to say you Fixers too will get a "detaunter".
