Items. missions&shopping
First to the shops. I think that the limited list of items sold in shops, should always be availible, at set ql's. Just like the Implants and

nanokits are. Nice and easy. Basic shops got 5,10,15 and 20. This way buying what you need is not such a bore. This applies to all the things sold

in shops. Tradeitems as well.

The smaller npc shops could have more diversity, and maybe some items not found in the regular shops.

Next point.
Missions should pay better, and mission rewards should be removed. Special items should only be found in mission chests or as loot. This of course

requires that chest&monster loot ql is upped so useful items can be found. The problem now is that the only way to get a decent item is through

spending ages rolling on the mission terminals. Or trading with other players, which is good by the way. Everyone still spend far to much time on

the mission terminals. It's boring. It degrades the game's funfactor.

This change would eliminate the boring mission rolling. People would instead team up and do them.
It would make basic item shopping a breeze.
The special items, not found in shops would be special.
-->A lot more trading between players, since ex. an nt can no longer bore him/herself to death at the mission terminals getting the nano armor.

FUNCOM, please listen. These are easy and good changes, that will benefit all.
