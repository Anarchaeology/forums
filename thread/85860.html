Like shooting Bonefish in a barrel
The comm crackled harshly in the breezing gust of Tir.

"Yeah, well i've got the money, you've got the anonimity and surprise, that's why your the perfect choice..."

Zaduda's brow knotted in thought, hardly believing why an omni would be hiring him for such a thing...

"But Mr.R.."

"DONT say my name, numbskull...."

The large trox growled to his comm at the derogatory comment.

"I wont do anyfin if yer call me more names."

"It's simple, rule #1 dont say my name, rule #2 do your job on time, when the insurance has been hacked, and he's there to be permakilled. #3 Dont muck it up and you'll be a millionaire in the new year."

The money was worth it, even if it did seem like an insane job.
But a job was a job was a job.......or was it?

"So your gonna set this bone guy up, and all i have to do is be at a certain place at a certain time and do the rest?"

"Exactly, like shootin fish in a barrel." answered the static voice.

"K, im in. Although you better make sure im incognito when this all goes down. If i get caught fer this, im takin you down with me!"

A chuckle echoed back through the comm. as a tall solitus walked past the Bronto stand. The laugh itself attracted the attention of the passer by. Zaduda decided it best to turn inwards, facing the stand and covering his arm.

"Agreed, you can take me down as far as you want if this all goes pear shaped. But i promise you it wont. Now i gotta go, to ensure this conversation 'never took place'. I'll contact you again sooner the time."

The comm fizzed as the link disconnected. Zaduda pulled off his katara shades and glanced around the cityscape of the brownish yellow city.

"What have i got myself into?"

Raquel frowned quietly.

The Director'd been gone for a week now, and she, frankly, missed him. Work had be a bit slow of late, also, and she sometimes wondered if she shouldn't go on holidays too, once in a while.

"Oh well, work'll pick up again so fiercely that I'll start wishing it hadn't.", she thought to herself as she chuckled. Work was Raquel's life though, and secretly she yearned for the bustle. Sigh. He was due back in about four days, she believed. Maybe this time his packetmail box wouldn't be flooded.

She chuckled again before noticing her comm was lit up like a stim junkie. Curiously she tapped the light on and the display popped up. The agent's eyes scanned over the message and her face grew grim. This was not good.

Her brow furrowed in concern, annoyance, and curiosity. Always curiosity. It plagued her, and she welcomed it. Raquel nodded slightly to herself and shut off the softly glowing display, leaning back against the booth. She'd almost forgot her tea on the table; something she seemed habitual in doing.

"...watch ya Omni 'friends', lady.. that's all I'll offer..."

What now?... the agent mused inside her head.
