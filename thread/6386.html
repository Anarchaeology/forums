Mission Rewards - Nope..
As an NT, the only mission rewards that I really want are nano formulas. They cost a FORTUNE in the shops, and dont even bother making them (except rare or un-buyable ones).

However, the only mission reward nanos I am ever offered (and thats a lot) are for other classes. I'm assuming this is intentional, and can only assume there was a problem with it. Why oh why would I solo a mission for a useless nano?

The only sucessfull way of doing it seems to be pairing off and helping each other get nanos - i.e pair with an enf, I get a mission with an enf nano, and they get a mission with a nt nano.

But they're SOLO missions....

FUNCOM - whats the thinking?
