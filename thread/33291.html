The Joint was Jumping III (Former Bouncer Chosen to Manage Rompa Bar)
It was all arranged, her new position with Omni-Tek. All right, so she was a disaster as a receptionist with one of Omni-Meds Prime-Med clinics. Its not like anyone died because of her. Nonetheless, the clinic director wanted her out and so she was gone. Ehrynn was lucky that the Rubi-Ka News Service had spotted her freelance writing and offered her a probationary staff writer position. So far so good, besides she had at least a years supply of those mood elevating Schedule III nanos. It wasnt hard to pack her things and move on from the clinic once she those were tucked away.

The managing editor decided that she could expand her writing by keeping up the column: The Joint was Jumping. When things were slow she could work on a new travel series, the Savvy Traveler or something equally inane like Rubi-Ka on 10,000 Credits a Day. She tried not to roll her eyes when the titles were suggested.

Of course, her position wangled her an interview with the new Omni-Tek manger of the Rompa bar, Mr. Eskew. Now thats getting results, she thought. Ehrynn checked her makeup, electronic journal, and walked briskly over to Mr. Eskews booth. He was sharing a drink with Poweflux and Ragingfire.

Ehrynn smiled and introduced herself; he was one of the most imposing Atrox she had ever seen. Swallowing hard she got right to work, "Mr. Eskew ("Throll" to his friends) how do you feel about your new assignment as the new Rompa manager? She realized he was not happy with the question. He grinned, sighed a little, and eventually said he was not thrilled with the idea. Too much of an old warrior to feel at home managing a club, he claimed. Mr. Eskew went on to explain that he was the interim manager during this transition period, while Omni-Tek completed its search for a proper manager. Ehrynn thought he was being too humble but nodded and went on.

Mr. Eskew, do you have any plans for Vixen Entertainment? He smiled, it was infectious, and revealed that he had subcontract the daily operation of the club to Aktrez and her group. Moreover, he found her staff efficient and professional. The club and Omni-Tek, he noted, was well served by Vixen Entertainment. Mr. Eskew was also quick to point out that he believed that subcontracting was open, in his view, to any loyal Omni-Tek employee group.

Confident that she had enough for the moment, Ehrynn thanked Mr. Eskew for his time and candor. As she was about to move away, he smiled again and said, You know Ms. Tedders, I started here as bouncer. Its odd to find myself back at Rompa, especially as the manger. Kinda like things have come full circle. Know what I mean?

Ehrynn had no clue what he meant, but she nodded, smiled, and said: Oh yes, like its deja vu all over again! The Atrox blinked and then sighed, I guess you could say its something like that Ms. Tedders.

(Next installment, Everyone comes to Rompa and the General Danced at Dawn.

News Flash, GridStream and Nano-Lust plan Baboon's Bash
After the interview with Throll, Ehrynn was able to get a few minutes with Ragingfire of Nano-Lust. He was still needling Powerflux about the mystery woman who had failed to show for a rendezvous she had arranged with Power; a little over two hours had gone by since the agreed upon time.

Powering up her journal, Ehrynn asked if he would be willing to answer a few questions, on the record, about Nano-Lust's plans for the future. Ragingfire shifted slightly so he could give Ehrynn his full attention. "Well, you really should interview Doncarnage but I'll give you my take on things." Ehrynn nodded.

"First, Nano-Lust has been subcontracted to run Baboon's and we've recently brought some new staff on board to meet that commitment. Second, our goal is to be the number one entertainment organization on Rubi-Ka." He winked at Ehrynn, "The boss is just a little ambitious but we've got the people to pull it off."

Ehrynn smiled, "Good for you, I'll make sure and set up an interview with the Don as soon as possible." Then, a big grin appeared on Ragingfire's face when Powerflux mentioned that he was surprised the Atrox was willing to speak to a woman without first asking her to take her clothes off. They punched each other on the arm when Ragingfire asked Powerflux how he defined being stood up, "was it two or three hours?"

When Ehrynn finally got his attention back, Ragingfire added, "Immediate plans for Nano-Lust includes a GridStream Production blowout at Baboons this Wednesday. You should come it will be quite an event." He winked and went back to trading barbs and punches with Powerflux.
