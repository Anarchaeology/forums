I find it humorous that people are just now starting to complain about the Agent Class.

What is it that draws most people to this class? I know for me it was the description of what this class was supposed to be. I think Agent I think KGB, CIA, British Secret Service, Israeli Special Forces, Marine Corps Scout/Snipers, undercover police officer, etc. (James Bond, being the most well know Agent archetype; Jean Reno as the professional; Tom Cruise as Ethan Hunt in MI, etc.). The AO agent is nothing like any of these.

Granted an Agent should by no means be an Uber-do-everything class, but lets get real. An agent should be proficient in small arms and small bladed (e.g. pistols, knives, and sniper rifle). Why is only the rifle a Semi-Green skill?

The Concealment skill is his bread and butter, why would he need it for yellow and greens? The whole point is that an Agent should be able to sneak past orange and reds (within an acceptable level range 5 lvl would seem appropriate).

Now I hate to b!tc# at anyone but please FC you promise this exciting class and give us a now feeble façade of what it should be. The Adventurer is closer to what an agent should be than the Agent you have given us.

I am not asking for a super class but I am asking you to listen to someone who has worked a job close to what a agent should be (for all you who must know plain clothes officer). Every day my life depended on being able to fit in with groups that had they known who I was, would have killed me outright or lost the collar and pretty much ruin my ability to work as such. Now in a game where sure I get continually revived this loses a bite of the bite, but it is a serious role-play issue.

My concept for my agent is paranoid loner assassins who will take on missions that he feels are inline with his view of what is moral (the mission system works great for this). However this puts me at an extreme disadvantage when I can not sneak in to my marks lair and put a bullet in the back of their head because I cant even sneak past the greens that are there to find the mark.

Now the measure of an assassin is the amount of distance between himself and the mark. The easiest and most novice of means is the rifle. It allows a target to snipe at some one from a great distance away (again AO fails to realize this). Now in RL I can put a good grouping of rounds in to a man sized targets head at 500 yards with nothing but a stock Colt M-16A2 rifle. Any Marine sniper with a .50 cal and a scope can easily do the same at twice the distance. Why call it an aimed shot if it does not have a chance of killing a target outright or at least doing a critical hit every time?

Please FunCom you have already ruined the Agent class please dont kill it by taking away its one saving grace.

As I am not one to complain without suggesting a solution here you go:

1. Exp Bonus for groups of 2 or less, penalty for 5 or more (yes this sucks if you to group but by definition then you should be an adventurer or soldier)
2. Concealment works up to 5 levels higher.
3. More semi-green skills Pistol & 1h edge would be great.
4. Allow for specialization, allow the agent to pick his weapon of choice. (e.g. first melee/ranged skill advanced beyond 100 becomes Green all others become light blue)
5. Reduce the amount of IP gained by the Agent 10% (I personally dont agree with this as I already stated a penalty in 1 above, but this is for all the whiners from other classes).

So that being said my point  Bump

Lazy Programmers and Ineffective Communication
See my rant in this thread:

http://aoforums.funcom.com/showthre...=&postid=241636

I'll try to be a little more reasoned here. But, no promises as there's little reason behind the 'fix' they put out or how it was thrust upon us.

My problem with this idiotic move is not that I've lost the ability to sneak through a mission on the hardest difficulty setting unmolested. It has to do with LAZINESS on the part of the designers/programmers and lack of commitment to original thinking. The fact that the communication from the game developers sucks so bad it's not even funny is just icing on the 'why am I wasting my time with this game' cake.

IMO, the Agent should be able to use his intended main non-combat ability to sneak through any mission that yields monsters that are equal to his character level or maybe a few above with very good buffs. The resulting rewards from these missions should be items at his quality level. In short: A level X Agent should be able to sneak past a level X monster (YELLOW, not simply green which is below his level) and get a level X item. That is called playing a profession 'in-character'.

Let me take a second to illustrate what an Agent playing in-character like this strives for. Most of us have seen the first Mission Impossible movie with Tom Cruise. Well he's got that one sequence where he sneaks around all kinds of safeguards and drops himself into the 'mission-cheeze' room and swoops out with his objective. Totally undetected and successful. Tons of suspense and use of a myriad of 'AGENT' skills. What real 'agent' wants to turn into a fricken' bird? No offense to Adventurers is intended. I have one myself and enjoy it somewhat for its own semi-unique merits.

I don't think that FunCom had any problem with this image of the man in the black ski-mask using all his skills to the utmost in order to succeed. If they honestly feel that every damn profession needs to kill, kill, kill, kill, to advance anywhere in the game then they are short-sighted and won't be seeing me around for long. Don't need the boredom. The problem comes from those stupid profession-altering nanos that have resulted in an unexpected interaction. Just look at the Paladin and his auras in Diablo 2 and you'll see the kind of problems that 'profession stacking' can cause in the area of balance. I will agree wholeheartedly with the need to address this issue if the Agent in Leet form had a 100% chance to complete any mission on hardest difficulty (after first getting to the mission area. Mort, Meetmedere anyone??) without stirring monsters many times his level. Something clever obviously would need to be done to address this issue. If Nerfing comes to mind then the developers better walk like they are on thin ice when it comes to addressing the issue and communicating their intentions to the playerbase.

What we get is a total knee-jerk reaction to this is which makes the skill completely, 100%, totally useless. Why? Because the programmers are TOO LAZY to address the real issue which is an Agent in Leet form benefitting from both his inherent well-balanced abilities and the inherent well-balanced abilities of an Adventurer. TOO LAZY to fix the real imbalance of class interaction. Instead, as many have stated, they introduce a situation where, myself included, most will simply abandon the character who has been rendered useless in his two main skills. Aimed Shot and Sneaking are what defined the agent.

Instead of reducing the concealment bonus for Leet form to +25 when used by an Agent, they took the easy way out. Instead of making failed lockpick attempts or aggressive actions reveal the Agent, they took the easy way out. Instead of revealing a player when they spoke, they took the easy way out. Instead of putting a 30 second delay on an Agent trying to get back into Stealth Mode after doing something that would reveal them, they took the easy way out. Instead of ELIMINATING THE FALSE PROFESSION NANOS, they took the easy way out (they could have just got rid of the Adventurer False Profession). No real 'agent' wants those stupid formulas. Did you know I thought that wearing soft-soled shoes would reduce my chances of being detected? There are different sound files for the different footwear. Would have made sense, no? Must have been TOO HARD for the developers to program something in that made sense. All I sense is LAZY, LAZY, LAZY. I'm a programmer by trade and that's my god's honest impression of how they addressed the Agent in Leet form: LAZY.

My other issue is with communication from the development team. All people who play Agent characters get is a condescending one line 'screw you' note on the update page. There is no communication in the forums about whether the playerbase thinks the Agent in Leet form is unbalanced or whether sneaking an entire mission with monsters at your level is acceptable. There is no talk of impending nerf to uselessness. There is no discussion of alternative methods of addressing this perceived imbalance. Just one line that reeks of 'love it or lump it'. Want to hear the proper way that a responsible development team would have handled this:

"The designers have determined that an Agent with Maxed Conceal while in Leet form can succeed in missions without entering combat at a level much higher than we intended. To address this issue we have temporarily drasticly reduced the effectiveness of this skill to prevent rampant exploiting. The programmers responsible are weighing several options to address this. These options will be presented to the players before finalizing our approach to fixing this unwanted interaction. Once we have a final solution, your Agents will once again be able to return to their stealthy ways. Until then be patient and understand that we want fairness in all aspects of this game. Why not take this time to explore another profession while we work towards this solution. You might like it. "

That's called communication and professionalism. That earns respect. The actions taken today reduce my respect for the people responsible immeasurably. It leaves me with a sour feel for the game and reduced enthusiam for even playing.

Good job, guys!
