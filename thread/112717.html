Ya that was a blast, but very true about disorganization - there were way too many people talking and emoting to keep up.

Awesome idea, Boglwe If people want to speak, they should submit in advance, particularly the topic that they want to speak about. There's no sense having dozens of people speaking about the exact same topic (unless they're presenting different sides of the same thing; i.e., no redundancy).

Also, if someone speaks, don't only point out a problem - offer solutions to that problem. Also, speakers should be prepared to take questions afterwards, but as constructively as possible.

Some things that I think need to be addressed:

(1) Enemies. OTSEC vs. the CoA was an awesome RP time (for those that RP in the main storyline). We need more of that kind of thing, dammit!

(2) In-fighting. Tarryk & Metalynx addressed it on the last episode of Talk Deviant, and they were bang on - there is WAY too much disharmony in OT. Granted, there will always be inter-departmental rivalries and "1-upmanship", but there is a TON of chaos among OTers. (much props to Kithrak for trying to remedy this recently).

(3) MASS RP stories. Having participated in an awesome one (run by the now-departed Tisantous) and having co-coordinated one, these are a BLAST. The problem Uwen, Viray, Raveleet and I had was controlling it once it got running . People who are interested in GMing/running these should be encouraged to do so.

(4) OOC Flames. The touchiest topic of all. Most of these are (whether rightly or wrongly) directed at OP, and I think everyone agrees these have to stop. People mess up; say things they shouldn't have; do things they shouldn't do. But, sometimes it's better to forgive and especially forget.

These are just my thoughts on what should be discussed/resolved. Anyone with any thoughts, go nuts
