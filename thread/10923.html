Plead to change the roots
Recently NPC mobs on missions seem to use roots very often.
I've heard this was changed to prevent blitzing. That's fine.

But when doing missions the normal way killing all mobs u get rooted very often. What happens is that the mob roots you, you kill it in 10 seconds and after that spend 1min 50 sek waiting for the root to drop.

Ofcourse some profs can get rid of the roots using means that are considered exploits by funcom but pets for example cant get rid of the roots.

My suggestion is that you change the roots so that they drop when the mob that has casted them on you dies.
This way roots still prevent blitzing.

I really dont really see any point in roots staying after mobs die but annoyance.

I hope to get official FC response considering this matter.
