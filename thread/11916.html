RP Central Closes its Doors
Okay, I know some of you here have signed up to RP Central and don't check that often.. so I've taken the liberty to post this letter here in case you check one day and see something different. To everyone else, just ignore this thread.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Eulogy for the Beloved
Tue, February 19, 29476 09:04 RST

Dear Friends,

Some say that it may have been too grandiose idea. Others might say that it wasn't ambitious enough. Regardless of which side of the fence you may sit on, the fact is that the Roleplaying Central, which has been home to myself and so many others since last year has been languishing alone.

Tormented by the reopening of the official funcom boards and taunted by the ever repeated promise of expanded official "character profiles", this site has attempted to find its own personality. However, the personality it seems to have chosen is that of a ghost town. Despite the fact that we regularly get new members, few people post on the boards, and even fewer still submit event news.

As such, it is apparent that this community no longer needs this site. We prefer not to think of it as merely a death of a site. It's just that like all things in life, we have to move on. On that note, we bid farewell to our home for the past several months. I do sincerely hope that you all keep in touch and remain true to roleplaying. Thank you all for spending the past few months with us.



----------------------------------------------------------------------------
In terms of administrative stuff, this site will remain to serve as an auxiliary bulletin board for the South Rubi-Ka Society. All users accounts that are not affiliated with SRS will be deleted in one or two weeks' time. This is to allow you to backup your data in the character registry and image folders.

When your account is deleted, so will your contact information. As I promised from the beginning, in no way will your information be used for reasons other than this site. All new account requests waiting in the membership queue will be deleted immediately and not processed.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------



Sincerely,
Tappert Solminski
& Slotine Zembower
AO Roleplaying Central

Arion, I am flattered you hold the South Rubi-Ka Society in such regard, but when it comes down to it, we are neither big nor mighty. I'd like to think pulled off one or two good events in the past, but it's always a struggle. And for each project that turned out okay, there were many which simply crashed and burned out of inexperience, lack of interest or lack of resources. So please don't cast us as some sort of "l33t rp guild" when in reality we are just barely coping, learning as we go along pretty much like anyone else.

As for Tappert's RP Central, it was created to replace the official AO message board, back in those dark days FunCom had shut them down, and he really deserves a lot of credit for that. But from the moment FunCom decided to reopen it's message boards, keeping a reasonably level of traffic at RP Central's was always going to be an uphill struggle. Simply put there is little that can be posted at RP Central that cannot be posted on the Funcom message board, with the difference that the official messge board reaches a lot more readers, including those new to AO.
