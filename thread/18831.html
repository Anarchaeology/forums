More MA modifications
Hi all. Just a few other things I'd like to see in the MA profession... some I've posted before, others that I just thought of now.

If MAs are supposedly against technology, seeking "an alternative in a more spiritual life to balance the eternal strife for progress that dominates the planet", then how come I can only get anywhere by pumping my guy up with the best implants I can get my hands on? Wouldn't it make more sense if there was some benefit for MAs who choose not to wear implants, since the profession is supposed to be wary of technology?

I've also thought that there could be some "meditate" function for MAs that they could use between fights. Going into meditation mode could drastically increase HP and nano recharge time. Prehaps the increase in recharge speed could be dependent on if the MA is wearing implants/how many implants the MA is using.

MA special attacks don't really make sense, in the sense that they are NoDrop and therefore aren't possible to share amongst fellow MAs. The idea I have about this is to keep the special attacks as NoDrop items, but tie them into the tutoring skills that the MA is green in. I would like to be able to "tutor" other MAs to use the special attacks that I've collected. The apprentice MA could use the same skill, at a reduced QL (say, 75%) but only until they die and are reclaimed, at which point they would "forget" or lose the learned special attack.

A final suggestion is to increase the parry/riposte ability. Many forms of martial arts (Judo springs to mind) focus on using the attacker's energy and turning it back on them, flipping them or hitting a particularly vulnerable part of their body, stunning them.

I'm not asking FC to make the MA prof super-powerful, but there's got to be some marked differences between an MA and any other unarmed character. I know we got some lovin' a few patches ago, but while this helps me out when soloing missions, it makes it tough to do effective damage without aggroing the mob off the tank.
