Given the right tools, right weapons and using all that is used in their favor, the damage if every profession would rank somewhere like this (you probably wont like my list but having played most classes, I think you will be very surprised). This is based off SELF BUFFS. This is damage alone, not ability to tank.

1. MP
2. Crat (yes, Crat)
3. NT (yes NT)
4. Martial Artist
5. Enforcer
6. Engineer
7. Doctor (yes, Doctor)
8. Soldier
9. Trader
10. Adventurer
11. Agent
12. Fixer

Here is the reasoning. The basis of my list is damage over time. There happens to be this thing called TIME in this game that a lot of people seem to forget. Most people think that the only damage worth counting is anything that as "Critical Hit!!!" at the end of it. Not true. Keep in mind, I put these in my order, but the damage difference for most of these are MINIMAL.

1. MP: The MP is simply the best damage dealing class just slightly over a Crat. Some people are amazed that I ever put a crat as a damage dealing profession, but i'll explain that. Given using an MPs full Abilities, and MP can dish out amazing damage with their Pets (some of the best damage alone from pets) Sickly fast and high damage nukes (outdo NT nukes) and when weapon skills are raised, you have a very high output of damage. Most MPs use SMGs for the quick damage and low cost, along with Burst.

2. Crat: Huh? How did a Crat get to the top of the list? Well, I would have even put crat as the number one damage dealer but I had to take some points away because charm isnt exactly viable for a large portion of the game. Crats are an amazing profession. I started playing one thinking I would be a gimp of all gimps. But not in the least. My bot (q200 Director Grade Bodyguard) Rivals the Slayerdroids that I play with frequently (trimmed, my bot attacks skightly slower then the Slayer but does the same damage per hit, higher crit. Bot damage seemed to change when MA damage did). Crats have Nukes, while not as good as NT nukes, VERY close (My NT and Crat nuke difference is 100 points). And given that you pump weapons skills, you could be dishing out very high damage from your weapons alone. Crats choost Pistols and Martial Arts as their main forms of damage.

3. Nano-Tech: I know I used to complain damage wasnt high enough, but in fact damage for NTs is VERY high. Most NTs dont use their own potential and think that their nukes should pull their weight. Hell no. No profession should ever neglect weapons skills. I'm sorry, but if there is an Option for more damage, take it. NTs have very high damage nukes even if they dont think so. Nukes alone wouldnt put NTs high on my list, the fact that they have light Pistol Skills does. An NT can cast a high damage nuke and shoot like mad during his recharge. Given that nukes are high damage and reletively have medium recharge, around 4 seconds, with 2 pistols you can dish out a very large amount of damage just in between nukes. In my case, I actually do more damage with my guns. :P

4. Martial Artist: Yes, they are very high up there with the recent changes. The one and only reason I put MAs up here is because of 1 weapon. A Zenith Tiachi. The MA is the Melee class that has 3 attack bars. 1 for Martial Arts, and 2 for each Tiachi he weilds. I never thought those weapons were good and it shows in groups when the Martial Artis is always getting out damaged, but he isnt using his potential. The Specials just dont cut it. So I see a high level Martial Artist with Zenith Tiachis. Using his self Buffed crits and Fire fists, I saw the screen spam by 400-600 damage from his 3 weapons along with the frequent 2300 damage crits from his fists and crits from his Tiachis. You may think i'm crazy, but i'de rather have 3 400 damage hits in 1 second then 1 3k crit in 5 seconds.

5. Enforcer: The Enforcer is just a sick profession to begin with. They are very much GEARED toward damage. Their weapons are very high damage and they have very good weapon buffs. Now, most Enforcers would say they ar "ok" but I happen to believe they are one of the best, even better then Soldiers. Why? I happen to think that a lot of Enforcers using slow weapons should try and speed them up a bit. Enforcers have Flurry of Blows which increases their output by an Insane amount over time. They have very good weapons such as Native Alloy staves and Support Beams. And they have things like the Rage and Challenger lines which increase their damage output. I dont see Enforcers use Challenger that much, I sort of wish they did. It holds back Mongo I know. A little added bonus is the damage shields.

6. Engineer: These guys are the wanna-be "screw a tank, i'll make one" guys we know and love. It's no doubt that Slayers do a lot of damage, but through an Engineers Career they do a lot of damage. The fact that they have no nuke shurts them a lot in the damage dept. But given the nice Pistol skill and buffs, these buys can be firing like a Wanna-Be Soldier while they have a Droid tank for them doing damage that would Rival a Soldier alone.

7. Doctor: I know what you are saying "Lucid, you are nuts to put a Doctor over a Soldier!!" Well, deal with it. The only reason Doctors are above Soldiers is simply they have very light Martial Arts skills. With the Increases in Martial Arts, Doctors who specialzed will kick but. And they have open to them Martial Arts special attacks such as Blessed with Thunder or Shen. Combine that power of MA with the Doctor DOTS which happen to be insane amongst themselves, they have more damage pumping from them alone then any Soldier could do with his Nova Flow. I'm sorry, but damage coming from Fists, Specials and 2 stackable Dots is too much to pass up. Theres been a new breed of Tank-Docs i've been playing with. Nobody wants them because they dont heal, but they do damage let me tell you.

8. Soldier: These guys rank low. I hate to say it. Nova Flows are literelly the only viable Assault Rifles and Pistols that a Soldier would want (i.e. the IMI Telius) are just a bit too rare for my taste. This leaves the Soldier with 1 form of attack, His Assault Rifle. He gets some reflected damages and good defense shields, but with the single form of attack, I dont see the numbers adding up here. I am constantly pulling Aggro from Soldiers. The way I see it, the single form of damage is what hurts them. Their Alpha Strike helps them, but since they nerfed equip times and such, it isnt much of a help. High damage all at once, and then very little the rest of the time.

9. Trader: NERF THE TRADER! Yeah right. A Traders power is in another professions buff. Simple as that. Shotguns otherwise are mediocre weapons which could not even compare to the Assalt Rifle classes with their Specials. Since shotguns only use the Shotgun skill, and are usually VERY slow, the Crit was the added bonus to make up for the lack of the Special. Fling and Burst and even Full Auto will slowly gain on your damage over time, and without a Crit buffs, Traders are wanna-be Soldiers with high attack rating and occasional crit to make up for their lack in special attacks.

10. Adventurer: These poor guys. The BBI Faithful has given them an Edge and they can now compete damage wise, but they still lack secondary damamage and damage buffs. Their morphs are very good but dont compare to the Enforcers. This jack of all trades professions sacrifices it all for the Ability to heal well. They have the best damage shields but it doesnt really help much in the End. I wouldnt say this profession is LOW damage, I would say it's pretty close to all the rest. However, there just isnt enough there to push them over the edge.

11. Agent: Simple reason here. Rifles SUCK. They suck worse then anything. Agents only shine when they have a very rare Aimed Shot, which is only once per fight. That one 10k Aimed Shot does not make up for the every other times when the Agent is reloading due to small clip size or pee-shooting due to small weapon damage and lack of the ability to dual weild. Since you cant cast Agent dots in FP, they dont count much.

12. Fixer: Last, sorry guys. Given the Stats of a Mausser Chem, you would be doing sick damage if you pumped your inits. But Sadly, the reqs on Chems are so high that you will always be weilding one much lower then you should be. And given that Mausser Chems are the only really viable choice for Fixers, it's hard to show where your damage is coming from. Burst recharges quickly, but you dont even have an Agents ability to OE or to FP and use some other persons nano buffs.

All things being equal, this is how I see it. Now you add an Unbalanced Crit buff like Funcom did, and you get what we have today. People with horrible high crit weapons relying on that crit so much that they scoff at other forms of damage. Professions like the Trader doing more damage then anybody in the team and whining when his UVC runs out because he cant help the team out. I have no sympathy at the changes in Crits. They skewed everything that would balance this game.

One thing about trying to balance classes in an RPG is that you will never be able to achieve balance in both PvP and PvM. If you balance for one, you will make some too strong or too weak in the other. Personally I would like to see Funcom balance for PvM and let PvP (for the handfull that actually concentrate on it) be whatever it turns out to be.

Since Im coming from a PvM point of view, here is how I think the balance should be damage wise.

1. NT - hands down these should be the top damage dealer. They should be able to outdamage everyone with their nukes alone. The trade off is they have no hp and crumble like paper when attacked.

2. Agent - This should be situational. If they get a lot of crits or a nice aimed shot off they should do as much or almost as much damage as an NT.

3. MA - Funcom says MAs are supposed to be the masters of melee damage over time. And that they should be. They should do more damage than any other class below on this list without crits, and should be up there with the NT when they crit a lot. MAs should be the ultimate melee fighter. Higher evades, higher damage and lower hp than enforcers/adventurers.

4. Enforcer - The tank. They are designed to take a beating, and their damage should be the base line. If they do 100% damage, then MAs should do 125% of their damage, and Agents should be able to do 130% situationally. Without an AS or crits an Agent should do 95% of their damage.

5. Soldiers - Their damage should do slightly less than an enforcers. They should do 95% as much consistently, offset by the fact that they have more HP naturally (unbuffed), and should have better taunting nanos than an enforcer. An enforcer should be able to outtaunt a soldier with damage and their taunt tools, but with all things equal a soldier should be able pull mobs off the enforcer by their nanos over the enfs damage.

5 (tie) Adventurers - These guys should be on par with soldiers damage wise, whether they are melee or ranged. This is offset by their versatility (they should be the most versatile class in the game). They should get something from every class only slightly weaker. Jack of all trades, master of none.

6. Engineers - imo no pet class should be outdamage pure combat classes. Engineers dont get nukes and only have their pet and their weapon skills to do their damage. Trade skills suck right now, but this is my "how things should be" list, so tradeskills should allow the engi to make some awesome items. They should make the best weapons in the game. They should be able to take any item and make it better.

7. Fixers - yes this is where I think fixers should be damage wise. They should be the thief class. Their strengths should be on that line. They should get something similar to aimed shot that allows them to hit someone hard from behind or concealed, but it should be a slow refresh. They should also be versatile as hell. They should be able to conceal and move about freely outdoors, with a tougher time indoors but still should be able to sneak a good bit. So they should get a big opener that requires stealth like the agent, but a more fixer-like attack. Their damage should be fair but they should be bloody hard to hit. They should be the only one with better evades than the MA.

8. MP - With a near full team with them at any time, they shouldnt be doing as much damage. Their pets should do less than engis, but more than crats. They have a doc, crat and enforcer with them at all times in their pets, so they should have a tougher time dishing out the damage. They should be able to solo like madmen tho. They have their buffs for groups, and they should be the best soloers.

9. Crats - They should be better mezzers. Their damage shouldnt be what makes em strong. They should be able to put most mobs on ice very easily, but their damage should be average.

10. Trader - Masters of debuffing, but average damage.

11. Docs - best healer, worst damage dealer. Best hp buffs, damage on the bottom of the scale. Nobody should want a doc in their team for damage.

Now, my list is how I think things should be if every class went with their strengths, with the best race for their class. An Atrox MP with a beam should be doing more damage than a nanomage MP with pillows. A doc using MA should be able to move up the list. The trade off is they become decent damage dealers but should suffer in their heals/buffs. Any class can become a better damage dealer by specializing away from type. Certain skills should be better damage. Shotguns should be average damage. MA or 1HE or 2HB should be awesome damage. The thing is, no non MA should have higher MA skills and thus should do less damage at it. No one should be able to use melee weapons better than an enforcer. No one should be able to do more ranged weapon damage than a soldier.

Anyways, this has been a fun thread. A bright light amidst the whining

If the team is having to throttle back a large amout, it's usually because the tank isn't doing good enough damage. If the team is in the fortunate position of being able to keep their damage output at an optimum for their class/level, then the tank is doing a good job.
