Bot/pet images
I'd like to see bots and pets have more "types". Not to see damage or movement or anything changed, but a lvl 5 MP has a floating puffer fish for an attack pet.........a lvl 85 MP, well, has a floating puffer fish for an attack pet. Now demons are awesome, but can't we see more models for pets as the levels get higher? Both the Crat and MP pets only have two types. The MPs have the floating fish and demons, the crats have the little droids and ninjas. Engies have 4 different looks to their bots and I know that the engies I team with are always excited to get that warmachine for the first time, even though the damage step isn't really a factor when compared to the top guardbot (hope I'm getting the names right, I've never played an engie, or any pet class for that matter).

Anyway here are my ideas. With an MP, around ql45 have MP attack pets look like ghouls or some hunched over nasty looking thing. At ql 115ish change them to some verly large dog-like creature (similar to the two animals at the end of the original Ghostbusters movie). And then, of course the demons.

Crats, I don't have as many suggestions for these, but I'd like to see the ninja image moved down to the lower-mid ql100's range and have the top ql ones look like mechanical Atrox's with a black suit on, ear piece and shades (kind of a secret service image).


Anyway, just some suggestions.
