Monster Debuffs & Roots
This doesn't really happen at lowre levels, but once my NT passed level 40 I started noticing it.

When a monster casts a debuff or root on me, it's squared to last 2+ mins, especially in the case of the Trader (Deprive) Skills series. Now I understand the importance of having this in the game and such, but after I kill the monster, I get HIGHLY annoyed having to sit here for 2 mins, or more, while the root or debuff wears off. I'm not going to press on with a -60 to my Matter Creation - it's just not gonna happen.

Can we do something about the duration of these? Maybe making them wear off if there is no combat engaged in 10 seconds or if no damage is taken (I don't want to nullify DoT). It's just obnoxious, and I hate having my time wasted.
