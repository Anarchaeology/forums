Grid Armor and NS Line Quests?
Hey all, I was just wondering how you all felt about the current situation about the GA and NS lines... right now they are very hard to get a hold of, and these nanos imo define the class... Here is an idea that will help out all of the *other* people out there, the ones that dont sit and farm it just to make credits...

Make NS and GA lines into quests from NPC's, put a level and skill req on these quests, also make them for their profession only *Agent allowed in FP*, make it so that doing these quests will take aprox as long as the fixer grid quest, but a little longer.
basically I can see an NPC out in the middle of aegean talking to fixers (or possibly by the fixer shop term?), offering them GA quests, starting at level 50 for GA I, 80 for GA II, 120 for GA III, and 160 for GA IV. The Grid Armor crystals will be given a no drop and unique flag, and can only be done once. I think this would be a nice way for fixers to get GA rather then paying hundreds of millions of creds to greedy engys/MP's/NT's and all the others that take their time to farm the disks... Not saying that it isnt a good way to make money, I just think that it shouldnt be right to exploit fixers into giving you their money.

I can also see an NT NPC out somewhere in a high tech place offering quests to NT's and agents in FP, for NS and NS MK II, 80 for NS and 160 for NS II... All of the nano crystals gotten this way will be no drop and unique also, and this quest can only be done once, just like the GA line.

These are just 2 of the main lines that define classes... there are many other classes that get a very hard to get nano that defines their usefulness... I think that NPC's should be added for each of these lines, with semi-difficult quests to complete them thus letting the classes have an equal chance as one another, not just to see who has more money gets the GA/NS/etc. =)

Plz post feedback on this and tell me what you think.
