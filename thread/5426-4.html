Additional ideas, not that FC is even paying attention to this thread anymore
I still believe the 'exploiting' of stationary mobs is what caused the range nerf. As many in this thread have shown and enumerated, mobs could and would attack them or chase them from well outside 40m distance. Perception range? Aggro range? I don't buy that answer. If mobs were responding to attacks from outside 40m, then they could do it.

Miir, it wasn't an 'exploit'. It was very poor design. Stationary mobs are by definition targets for ranged attacks. Ever hear the old phrase "like shooting fish in a barrel?" FC should have thought out how XP would be rewarded for stationary mobs and fixed THAT, rather than just capping every players range to an absurd and totally unrealistic value of 40m.

NT's and Agents suffer most from this. Agents possibly more than NT's, since once the mob reaches us, we don't have a whole lot we can do except keep shooting and hope. NT's can still nuke on their first shot, and continue to nuke. Agents only get ONE aimed shot. And it rarely crits (for me anyway.) Fix range nerf.

Or at least give Agents a crit multiplier on that singular aimed shot we get to start the combat. Which only works if we are undetected by the mob. And we are sneaking. That would help the Agents overcome not having the sniping range they were designed around.

NT's can calm. Agents can't.

Bring back proper range for AO. Ask anyone who has USED a rifle. 40m cap? Where are you, in a fog bank? No, I see. You've got your heads buried in the sand. No wonder you can't see.
