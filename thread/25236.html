Org. tax, WHY!!!!!!!!!!
Guilds/orgs are the only real means of social interaction within this "RPG". I see a tax on orgs another reason to make people solo. Guild Houses are still non existant and I see no reason to "charge" people who wish to be in an org. Funcom preaches teamwork , however they impliment things to discourage team play. Most of the terrific hunting areas are no more, last summer it was the most exciting thing to do, groups are always forming and it made things terrific. I for one think the changes to supression as well as elimination of hunting areas, changes to zones and now this addition of org taxes will make teams a thing of the past. The player should decide who and how much he gives, not the game. Choices have been severly limited as it is, and now we have another burdon to bear. With the upcoming expansion pack, they will again force current players to upgrade, or be totally outmatched by whats included, so it appears the almighty dollar is all they are concerned with. I have held on in the hopes of improvement and interaction, however events are rare, and the players are now given even less control over their options than before. I guess it's time to consider other gaming options once my current subscription is over, I had high hopes for AO.

Celtic..

Any decent org either doesnt have the /org tax turned on, or, if it does, the level is set to some amount that is very easily recoverable by just a little hunting/missioning.

The tax is there in anticipation of future improvements to the organizations, like the org HQs and the org "bonuses"...each of those (plus various other things I'd imagine as well) will require an "upkeep" cost that is taken out of the org's bank account automatically by the system.

If you're interested in joining an organization, do a little homework..ask questions of it's current members, especially it's high ranking officers...especially ask if the tax is being used and if so, at what amount.

A member is always free to manually donate credits as well, and I agree that such manual donations should somehow override the /org tax, however they dont (too complicated to code I'd imagine).

Dont go condemning the tax just yet...Yes, it seems like a pain now, but down the road, when the creds are actually getting used, you may actually be greatful for it...
