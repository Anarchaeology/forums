Ninja Looting (as defined by Verant):

"Ninjalooting is defined as taking special equipment from a fallen creature
without the express permission of those who killed the creature if they are in
attendance.

"The purpose of this rule is to stop those who stand near creatures that carry
special items and attempt loot that equipment prior to it being looted by the
rightful owner."


Ninja Looting (as it is used in AO):

Ninja looting as most commonly used in AO can mean:

Taking loot from chests or the boss without offering to share it with your teammates.

Sneaking off during a mission to go loot chests without your teammates knowledge.

Looting every single corpse in a team mission before it even hits the ground!

Also I've seen Ninja Looting used to mean "Over Looting" and "Uber Looting": see this example taken from Ninja Looting 101 http://deczege.hypermart.net/editorials/ninja.html:

"NINJA LOOTING

Pay attention, Grasshopper! Your training in the Dark Side of looting will not be complete until you're already looting a corpse before it touches the ground! Pay attention well, and you may yet earn your black belt in the dark teachings of NINJA LOOTING!! Simply put, ninja looting is looting a corpse before it even knows it's dead! A ninja looter is someone who loots fast and loots for him/herself. I am a Black Belt at Ninja Looting when I have to be. It's both sad and funny to have to ninja loot. I look at it as a two-party system: the rich and the slow. You need to have good timing and quick reflexes to be a Master Ninja Looter."

My Fixer alt was a part of that team, and it was my action that caused the rat to spawn (from the first chest in the mission no less). I owned up at the time and I'll say it again here : I opened the chest before you started fighting. I was first in the empty room but you ran straight past me into the next room and attacked the mob there. The resultant rat aggroed me immediately and I responded in kind, soloing it (in fact I was comfortably beating it down until it attacked you in the other room). And you are probably right, your team heals would have caused it to about-face and aggro you instead.

I am sorry you almost got killed, no one wants an add in the middle of battle, whichever you consider the add to be. However, the thing that bugged me (and indeed the other fixers present, although profession is no excuse here) was your attitude. You asked that no one was to even touch a chest from that point on. Except that you didn't. ASK that is. It's difficult to say you even told us. Insisted, yes. Dictated, yes. Demanded - definitely. No one got a chance to offer an opinion. Just because you are a Doctor (and a good one I hasten to add) it doesn't give you the right to demand how the mission is done. At the ql we were doing, 2 x unlocked chests = 1 mob's xp. Not a bad return for two seconds work. Whether you like it or not, chests are a valuable part of a mission and should not be ignored. You may say they contain nothing of value, but the xp alone is worth the time. I mean just how often does a rat spawn? 1 in 5? 1 in 10? And how often do they actually KILL someone? Rarely in my book, unless you are completely inept. Obviously chests should never in any circumstances be opened during a fight, but to take the attitude you did was out of order. I can understand you being irritated at coming close to dying, and for that I apologise. The other fixer was wrong in what he said too, it only served to antagonise you.

As for the 'Do the chests afterwards' argument, that never holds water for me. Teams split or grid out the moment the mission is over. No one is ever interested in going back; especially if there is another mission to be done.

My solution is this : Assign ONE person to do all the chests for the whole mission. You know who to blame if things go pear shaped. Just like Boss loot, he lists everything. Chest looting...
A couple of points.....Not touching chests in a mission is a silly mistake, too much valuable loot in there, and the XP from lockpicking and rollerats is too good to waste.

However, everyone in the mission running off and dragging adds to the main team isn't workable, so each team needs a system. In a pickup group, bring up the point early. If everyone is capable of opening locked chests then it is workable to let that person do it, otherwise I think letting one person do it is best, and insist they list loot.

Same thing goes for looting corpses, loot alpha is best. I've seen 180+ missions where the same dork looted every body on the ground.

The funny thing is in high level missions where the rats are welcome as extra XP, you hardly have anyone opening chests. Most missions have 1 or 2 rats in them at least, so by not opening chests you are cheating yourself out of 30K of XP or more.

But, the thing that never happens is people going back and looting after a mission is complete. I have never seen that done.
