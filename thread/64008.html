*Spywolf slowy walks up this steep hill looking for his target his trainer has given him. He looks for a Clan Dotcor with the name of Jennifer Ramagano. He lowers himself to the ground on his stomach. He raise the scope of his rifle to his eye as he zooms in on the target. His cross-hairs come in connect with the targets head. He slowly loads two shells into his rifle as the click of the bullet in place can be heard. He closes his eyes for a second collecting his thought. Right as he does this his mind flashes back to the moment he came home from training to find his dad dead.*

----------------------------Flash Back------------------------------------------
*Spywolf walks through the door and see his dad a Bureacrat for Omni-Tek dead on the ground with his head bashed in. He runs over to his dad crying. Right at this moment his mom walks into the room with a it seems like a Clan Enforcer. He looks up at his mom.*

Spywolf:Mother what has happen to Father? Did these clan enforcer beside you kill Father? What is that smirk on your face about...

*His Mother replies with a smirk on her face.*

His Mother: Alex you see I couldn't stand to life two lives anymore...It became too much I was cheating on your father with this Clan enforcer. I made a simply shoice and that is to Clan Side. Alex you see what Omni is doing to us is wrong. So Alex what is your choice?

*He looks at his mother with a angry and hatred in his eyes. He speaks in bitter and hateful voice.*

Spywolf:Listen here I don't want a damn thing to do with you any more. You better hope to god that I don't get a chance to find you again or you will meet my cross-hairs. I will not think twice about pulling this Trgger. So I suggect you get the hell out of here before worse happen to you then what happen to my father.

*His mother looks at Alex with a tear in her eye. She looks scared and sad.*

His Mother:But...Alex...You...Don't...Understand...

*The Enforcer looks at Alex then back to his mother.*

Enforcer:Come on Jennifer we better be leaving before Omni Pol shows up.

*Right as His Mother and Clan Enforcer leave Alex real quick loads one single bullet into his gun. He points it to back f The Enforcer head and pulls the trigger. all that can be heard is the bullet flying through air.
