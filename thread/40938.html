Vox Populis response to Radimans Return
How does a Man who abandons his post in a time of crisis expect to lead people that no longer respect his authority.....

If Radiman expects to lead then drastic changes must be made.
For all the talk of a free society the Clans are at best disorganized factions ruled over by an ineffective oligrachy led by Radiman and stacked with yes men that rubber stamp whatever stalemated probosal he comes up with.

For our society to work and for the future of our people the leaders of the clans must be heard. For your Council to become a true force for the clans you must give those the clans people have chosen to represent them a way to bring the needs of the people to your attention.

For us to become a true free society a Congress of the leaders should be formed. With a say in what the Council can and cannot do...

Failing this your council will continue to be what it is today
a falsely created government claiming to upphold the ideals of freedom while having no authority outside the halls of your council room.
