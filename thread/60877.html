I don't have a top-end computer either (800 Mhz Athlon). 768k down / 512k up DSL. Voodoo3 3000 16mb. I never LD in BS. I fully zone in less than 3 seconds, no matter where I'm zoning in/out. I'm not bragging, I'm just saying that *maybe* it's not 100% FC's fault. I turned down my visual settings long ago, made a huge difference.

Fire up AO and hit the Settings tab then Advanced Visual Settings

Texture Quality: Medium
Texture Color Quality: 16
View world distance: 75
Char view distance: 50

Neither "Continous level..." or "Compressed textures..." are checked.

Ground Quality: 150
Ground Full Quality Radius: 10

In game, I don't have awesome sunrays and client wildlife. Basically, turn off as much eye candy as you can. Sacrifice something to gain a better overall experience. Might be worth a try.
