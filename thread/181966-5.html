It's all in the buffs, and that towers are too dumb to stop shooting you even if it will kill them on your shield. And that doesn't take any skill, it doesn't take a well built character outside of NCU, as I said in my original post.

You don't need to have any skills of your own, your character doesn't need any special power, all you need to be able to do is get buffs and move around

But it only works for attackers, because attackers win by killing towers and low level towers - immobile, weak hps, weak damage, stupid - are easily killed by reflects.

Edit: oh and PS - even though I think that this situation is unfair and totally biased in favor of the attacker, to me the question is less "is it fair" and more "is it fun?"

Is it fun to watch helplessly as your base is demolished by a character that is nothing more than a vehicle for level 200 buffs?
To put it on a bumper sticker: buffs for attacker = win for attacker. Same buffs for defender = almost nothing.
