Well, don't permit buying guild houses - renting them is a much greater potential money sink
So what guild house enhancements would people be willing to pay for? Here's a start
  • Bank
  • Backyard with QL1 mobs (for deprives and leet charming)
  • dueling arena
  • permanent chests to hold guild equipment - preferably with icons like backpacks have
  • bartender (for style and a place to sell loot)
  • a guild message board - one section only allows posts from officers, another section allows posts from any guild member
  • ability to change the background music occasionally

Some of these are obviously more useful than others, so prices would have to vary accordingly. Then you get into other options that are just for fun or to show off how much money you have
  • swimming pool
  • sauna
  • deck overlooking the backyard
  • links to members' apartments so each can have their own personalized space within the guild house
  • yalmaha detailing shop - allows choice of different yalm skins
  • deck overlooking the actual city streets so you can watch the "little people" scurry about and chat about how they resemble ants

Not sure if I'd be willing to pay for things like Save/Reclaim terminals or a personal basic/advanced/superior general store. But those could be offered as options, too. Maybe allow a choice of multiple backyard options with scenic extras like a waterfall or snow capped mountain being extra.
