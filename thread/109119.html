Alone
I performed this at the Groovey Poetry night in The Cup. Once again, I claim no real talent but just felt like sharing with the esteemed people here.


My friend, why are you alone?

There is no need you know.

Alone, one, isolated in a crowd.

I know the pain of one.

Take what is offered you my friend.

A hand, a caring word and kind hello are there for you.

Don't be alone my friend.

I have known the pain of one, my friend.

Walk beside us my friend, and never be lonely again.
