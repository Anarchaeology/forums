Greetings Once Again,

Once more, I would like to address a few points. To begin, I don't think that anyone here, regardless of profession, etc, would deny the fact that -everyone- needs, and will eventually get attention.

The problem however, comes because of the way our society itself is setup. In a world of instant gratification, the concept of patience and having to wait on something is a foreign thing. Everyone will eventually get their turn. Only FC can determine what that is -- regardless of what anyone here wants, says, whines about, etc.

So don't be so quick to state the obvious.

Now, back to you, Oberon.

1) Judging a profession's worthiness or ability, by gauging how many new players take up that profession, is extremely short sighted. Assuming too, that said profession is all powerful, because a majority of new players has taken it up, is also a rather narrow minded view.

The Martial Artist profession is what I liken to the "EverQuest Warrior". Anyone who has played that game will know what I mean. In short, it's an easy class to pick up, learn, and advance with. For many, yes, it is a training class.

That, I feel, is what accounts for the majority of the new players having MA's.

As regards everyone making MA alts? Well that's called jumping on the bandwagon. It has always and will always happen. Go ahead and try to count how many new fixers you see, once 14.4 comes out. It's a fad, just like anything else.

2) As regards you trying to 'save us from obvious letdown once FC decides to nerf MAs'. Well, let me be the first to thank you for your most gracious attempt. However, I feel that I speak for many of us in saying; please, focus on your own profession. We're better off without your 'help'.

Funcom has fixed many problems with the martial artist class. There still remain issues, but we've been given a great boon in our fixes lately. The -only- reason I would see any dreaded nerfs coming, is from narrow minded people like yourself, who's self serving interests guide them to whine about profession XYZ on half the threads they come across.

Try to see the whole picture, for what it is, then we'll talk.

3) As regards MA's and bows. Firstly, to address your earlier comment, yes, bows -should- be on physical initiative, not ranged initiative. There is a vast difference in the effective use of a bow and a gun, and it does take some degree of physical skill and prowess to use a bow. As for MA's getting 50% crits on bow shots? I doubt those numbers, honestly. Secondly, agents get something akin to that as well. Thirdly, it's a small percentage whatever the real numbers are, and really doesn't deserve the overblown light you've thrown it in.

But that's just my opinion :-)

4) Finally, regarding you hearing about all of these groups looking for martial artists. Wow, I sure would have liked to have been around for that. My own experience, and that, I'm sure of many others, is that before our fixes, martial artists were wanted ONLY because of the crit buff lines. Funcom has restored the balance there, and seated it where it belongs.

Now groups want martial artists for the damage dealing capacity, something we truly haven't experienced much before these fixes.

In closing; yes, everyone does bring their issues to the forefront, and want them addressed. It remains squarely with the Dev Team to determine who and what gets attention at any given time. Do not assume, nor presume that simply because we have been given fixes, that we will not continue to bring problems to FC's attention. I highly doubt that you, or many others would.

It remains with them, to prioritize and dole out the fixes. You have issue, take it up with them -- we're not the ones making the changes.
