Why are Map Coordinates Backwards ?
Anyone who has ever done map reading knows that coordinates are always given in Latitude then Longitude. For example, a location described as 500X3800 means Lat. 500 X Long. 3800. It's a convention which is taught universally, as far as I am aware. You want to find map coordinates, firstly you plot the latitide, then you look for the longitude.
Its been done like this since before Christopher Columbus. so why is it that FC had decided that their maps should be the other way around.
Funcom Co-ordinates are Logitude first, then Latitude. This means that, if you know anything at all about conventional navigation in real life, you actually have to force yourself to think backwards to find anywhere in the game.
I dont think this is a RP thing. I think it's more probable that whoever wrote the AO map system never saw a real map in his life.
