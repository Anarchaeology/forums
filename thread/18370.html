If you notice..
If you notice..

I've never once said that we were gimped and unable to group. The hazzards of grouping are just more dangerous for us because we tend to taunt more than the average tank.

It really depends on the person. If the person has a good head on their shoulder and knows what he or she is doing, then grouping is easy.

Being an NT is completely about timing. You have to know how much damage you are going to take and know when to precisely calm to remove aggro.

I am a group leader and a master solo artist. I solo 180+ missions (fighting) with no problems at all. But, I had to figure out my own scenario on how to do this. When I group I go full bore on the enemy. I nuke as fast as I can nuke, brawl, da taunter (yes I taunt on purpose) and push push push. When I hit 3/4 damage I hit nullity and continue to nuke and taunt. I am a savior to the group because the fight is finished usually before nullity wears off. (nullity helps a lot). If I've used nullity and the mob isn't dead, I know precisely when its going to wear off and i calm before its finished. Sit, nano, health, stand nuke to finish. I haven't died in a group mission in 23 levels. I'm 143 now.

Solo, the only tactics that I use that help me concern three types of mobs: MP mobs, Trader mobs, and the (damage shielded) adventurer mobs. The damage shielded mobs usually hit for 91 points of damage and this shielding damage goes through nullity so I have to be careful.

I always pull these mobs back to a room that isn't adjacent to any other mobs so no other aggro. I immediately calm them and sit and get health back and layer up. Then I start with a big nuke like annihilating hadron string which hits for up to 3700 damage for me. When a mob is calmed when you first initially hit they attempt to hit you but then they stop and walk around. After I initially nuke, I time it so I brawl and taunt at the same time it hits so it acts as one attack. Then I immediately try to recast and during the second cast, they are walking around so I get two nukes for every volley. I then calm and sit, nano and health up and start the nuke again. Notice this is just for those types. This keeps them from casting on me and depriving/shutting down etc.

For the rest of the mobs I rely on nullity and fast nukes and timing.

Hope this helps.

At 143 I have 4389 health and 146 when I get to wear my full MKII armor I will have approximately 5100 health. I have my 1k token board and full health imps in. Anyone can do this if they set their mind to work. The 1k token board was by far the hardest achievement because I did it legitimately unlike some ash exploiters. I would definitely recommend for those of you to work on your token boards. If you need some help on this and are Omni I can tell you where you can find short missions in abundance to get them faster.
