Unlock skill after death.
I'v only died a few times, but it's still a hassel.

Whenever you use an ability that locks a skill, and then die, that skill remains locked for as long as the ability sais.

I'm getting rather irritated by this. When I die, I always use my dimach befor I do (last attempt). I'v made it out like 90% of the time, but when I do die, my dimach stays locked. And it stays locked for a loooong time. So does my MA attacks and such when I use like Angel of Night befor I parish. Perhaps we can make it so the locked skill(s) get unlocked after death? I'd like it. However, I could imagine some one making a hair-brain scheme that would exploit that function somehow.

It's not that important, anyways. Just one of those nice things to have.

IMO
If you die, I feel as though it should reset all of your abilities that were used prior to your death. I see that if you lose all buffs/debuffs as well as possible experience AND the temporary loss of some skill/stat points, you have received enough of a penalty for death. If you have to wait for a considerable time for your ability/attacks to reset after death, it is more of a punishment due to the longer wait to fight. This goes for those players that depend on the specials in combat. If you fight without them, it is not much a difference but those who do suffer longer then others. A bit of an unbalance in my eyes.
