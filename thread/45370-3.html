What does that tell us? Nothing.

Anyone can tank around level 150. It's because the players get progressively stronger while the mobs around them stay the same (ie. doing all QL 190 missions past level 120ish).

That is also why people percieve agents as getting slightly uber past 150.

The reason is because 10k aimedshots start becoming very common. Is that 100-200 points of aimedshot difference between the level 100-140 agents making THAT much an impact?

Nope. The aimedshot skill means nothing. The real reason 10k aimedshots become so common is that the agents attack rating has increased substancially while the mobs level has remained about the same (190ish). This, combined with various high level crit buffs, give the agent a very great crit chance.

Since aimedshot is truely based off your crit chance and the gun you use, this leads to "uber" aimedshots.

Had these groups started taking on missions that challenge them you would find the agent only hitting for max gun damage with his aimedshot, and never being able to tank.

This is also exactly why an NT can pick up an X-3 and start throwing out the 10k aimedshots with mark of pearl and TTS+UA.

In fairness, most classes (except for soldier) will not be able to compare to the agents damage unless they have agent buffs. Having said that, they are always sure to take my buffs when I get in a group - then shoving me into FP crat so I can't use my crit buffs. Effectively making me a gimped crat doing on par damage with everyone else around me.

To put it simply: The agent is only a better rifle user than other classes when he is alone. That does the agent no good.
