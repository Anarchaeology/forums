Doesn't Anybody Want to be a Famous Reporter?
I know that there are storyline events going on. I've been on the fringes of three of them in the last week:

  • The Omni-Pol occupation in force of Stret West Bank. (I was one of the first on the scene, got slaughtered before I fully rezzed, never got far enough in to see what was going on),
  • The brief appearance of the Omni Grid Monitoring Program icons in the grid. (I shot some screenshots, then bailed when the "shooting" started - I wasn't backed up.)
  • The attempted assassination of DJ Tarryk by a member of the Dust Brigade during Wednesday night's live show from Reet's. (I was listening on the radio. I stayed away because the damage this guy was reported in guild chat as doing would have one-shot killed me before I cleared the door).


Now imagine my surprise that nobody, and I mean nobody, seems to have written news accounts of these activities and submitted them to the Voice of Freedom! I know that there were people there. Surely someone could have written up a more detailed account, and would like to be famous for fifteen minutes?
