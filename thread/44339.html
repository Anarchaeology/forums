Well, here are some possibilities...

1. The dev's just discovered another major exploit and are trying to fix it (said exploit being something like players figuring out how to get pet pathing to work as it should, not as intended).

2. We were told the next patch would be 14.6 but with no details. Then a few days ago we're told the next patch is really only 14.5 and will be some sort of outdoor content (again, no details). In reality the patch tomorrow is only 14.45 in which all the buildings in Entertainment turn cyan and all the buildings in Athen turn pink and we find out that Radiman and Ross are really one person with multiple personality disorder. Oh and shops will be broken in a whole new and exciting way (such as dispensing nothing but aerosolized artificial dairy products).

3. Funcom finally learned the following:

3a. Posting patch notes prior to releasing the patch gives the player base expectations that are unreasonable due to the fact that many things in the patch notes never make it in game or major mechanics changes that were never announced *do* make it in.

3b. Putting up the patch early on the Test server gives the player base false notions that its input is valued and that bugs and major issues found prior to going Live will be addressed.
3c. When players realize that announced changes don't happen, unannounced changes do happen and that all the hard work of the *paying* testers (i.e. customers) is being ignored, they get a little miffed and have a tendency to rant and rave, call people names, make threats and otherwise become intolerable.

Assuming #3 is the case then I wouldn't expect to see preliminary patch notes ever again, nor would I expect to see any further updating on the Test server. The insurmountable silence for the past couple of months regarding the next patch (like what and when) definitely lends credence to this theory. Then again, I could be completely off base here. I doubt it though.
