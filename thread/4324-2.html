Love the Social "tab" idea
I love the idea of having a "Social" clothing tab.

(I can equip all of my own armor without help, I just hate equipping & unequipping all the time because it is such a pain. Having an Engineer character with Multiple bags to hold tradeskill stuff doesn't give me much room in my inventory to put my armor, when I want to put on social clothing. And the bank, forget it. I have it full. Just waiting to find that ore or something I can do with all the instruction discs & high QL parts which I have that I can't use right now.)

Full tank armor being worn under a bikini., combat boots hiding under high heels. (something like this is probably the original reason that armor could not be worn "under" clothing). Also, one would not expect someone wearing normal clothing to have AC of say 200. It just isn't reasonable, unless you are using programs, but who can tell, unless you see someone with glowing sparkles outlining their body.

I think that it would be great to have a social clothing tab.
I also agree that there needs to be some stipulations, although these are just some ideas...

1-Some social clothes, plain street clothes, dresses, bikini, swim trunks, should not allow you to keep the AC bonus of the armor you are wearing in your armor tab. It just doesn't seem practical.

2-Other clothing, such as coats, backpacks, sunglasses (unless you are wearing a helmet) should not interfere with your AC bonus from the armor you are wearing, or if they do, (i.e. coats getting in the way, backpacks unbalancing you, sunglasses obstructing vision) should give only a slight decrease, not completely wipe out the AC bonus of the Armor you wear.

3a-I never understood why you can't wear a backpack with a coat/cloak. That makes no sense. I can wear a backpack with a real life trenchcoat. Why can't I do it in game. I really think they should fix this.

3b-I do understand why you can't wear a backpack with TANK armor. Did you see that stuff, It protrudes near your neck, making it very difficult to be able to wear that backpack. This actually makes sense, although I'm sure it doesn't help the people wearing it who have to carry all their belongings. Oh well.

4-Someone (forgot who) mentioned that it is kind of boring seeing everyone looking similar in the city. While the amnesty is on, I think it's great that people are walking around in their party clothes, but I don't think that everyone should unequip their armor when in cities. In reality, if I were there, in a city which is supposed to be at war with some other "faction", I would probably walk around with some armor on. I might take off my helmet, and I probably would not be let into the bar with a weapon equipped, but I would be walking around with some protection. I think it would be odd if there were no people wearing social clothing in this supposedly war-torn world of Rubi-ka. Then it would not be 'Anarchy Online'. Take that Cantina bar in Star Wars, there are people in there with armor & weapons of all sorts?
While I really like the idea of social clothing, I don't think that it would hurt to be able to wear combinations of Armor & social clothing. What if you
5-It is so hard to distinguish one person from the next. Funcom has done a wonderful job creating so many different face models, and with many different accessories, such as armor and clothing, you would think people would find it easy to customize their character to create individuality. In reality, with the large number of players in this online world, there is not enough options to go around. Let us distinguish ourselves more through use of social armor. Or make more armor types that look different.

We need something here people.
