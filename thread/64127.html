Mongo is alot of things, depending on the situation.

If I team with a crat, mongo is a teamkiller (if there are calmed adds)

If I team with an NT, it's a HP-boost that I really don't need, since only every third mob will concider hitting me. And when they do, they only hit me max three times.

If I team with other professions than mentioned above, it's a taunt that actually CAN draw aggro of most people.

When the team goes for the boss-room, it's a temp HP-buff for when the rest of the team is down, and the boss start hitting me because the NT is down healing. (unless it's a warping boss.)
