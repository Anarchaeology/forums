In Beta, Gaute was careful to poll us on how high the ratio of sites:guilds is. If there are more sites than there are guilds, everyone will have one and fighting won't be important. If there are too few, the fighting will be too intense and constant. I believe his solution was a ratio based on the number of guilds.

Also, keep in mind that several areas on each server were disabled because of the delayed release.
