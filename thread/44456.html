Thoughts on a PvP dedicated server, and 100% damage.
In the community corner there was a thread asking how many would potentially want a PvP server. A majority of the people who posted liked it, and a good amount suggested that it be 100% damage. I think the idea of a fully pvp server is really cool, but there's more thinking that needs to be done.

Aspects of 100% damage:
-Class balancing doesn't exist. The biggest gun wins. You can be a nano tech with a flashpoint or a soldier with a flashpoint, you'll still wipe the crap out of anyone with a good full auto.
-NO tactics whatsoever. 1 hit kills will be all over the place, and you'll think you were playing quake with the FPS-ish style of combat. MMORPGs, i feel, are the same as every other game, but magnify the potential and ideas a game is really about, expanding the base roots of all games.
-The only real advantage i can find in 100% damage is that it's funny. Quick fighting, big battles, just like how it used to be, i can't say i didn't have a laugh or two when my mauser could kill an agent pre-burst nerf in MMD. It gets old though.

Anyone care to comment?

Now picture this: EVERY zone is either 25% or 0%. There would be tons of action, tons of fighting, pvp for once in a long time would be the highlight of the game. There's another game to AO though. Incoming players? It isn't very encouraging when you buy AO, get to level 2, and right when you walk outside the door someone within like level is waiting for you...or even travelling as a noob to close-by city missions, theres always a chance you'll see someone within your level waitin to cap ya. 'its war,' i know, but everyone needs to level up regardless. And if you die? What if all the reclaims are camped, and you get killed over and over again? Even if you put a 'radius' people couldnt attack within not only would that defeat the purpose of a 'pvp server' but there would be line camping just like the reason a lot of these people supporting such a server hate right now. I don't see being opposed to this makes me a 'carebear' although it's common sense a person shouldnt be forced to shut down their computer or /quit and wait to log on at a better time when there's less people around...how would they control this?

Making all the zones pvp'able interferes with player advancement. I think it would be cool to have a major expansion of the limited amount of zones people pvp in, but a fully pvp server i think is taking it too far.

Any thoughts? (Note i said 'thoughts' not flames.)
