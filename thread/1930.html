Team PvP Titles = Beneficial to Game
Right now, solo PvP is dying. But almost no one wants to team up for PvP because then you don't get ANY benefits. At least solo you get a title so you have an idea of your relative prowess.

Since there are no concrete benefits to PvP (other than exciting fun), I don't see why it would hurt to allow team PvP to add to titles, also. (And like xp in team hunting, the whole team should share in the kill reward for PvP title points...otherwise 4 or 5 members of the team will get zip while they help the one bruiser get all the PvP points.) Sure, it would indirectly encourage team ambushes, but those happen already...the group of players works together but just aren't in an actual team. At least with team PvP benefits you would be encouraged to go round up your OWN posse and go get some payback.

Teaming up for fun is what a MMORPG is all about.

Somebody remind FunCom of this.
