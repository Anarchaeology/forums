I'd say it doesn't even necessarily take a save area too close... In other words if it were something like Ace Camp was. My opinion is simple there are two choices:

Level in missions or level in the outdoors

Right now here are the perks of each

Missions:
nice boss loot with limitted minions
Extra loot (crates full of tons of extra loots)
mobs room-by-room which limits adds, while maxing mob per area
best choice for xp (I currently get 1.25mil xp bonus as a reward)
token (everyone is now getting their 1k token board with ease)

Outdoors:
Great scenery


OK.. see a problem here?????? I do... why would anyone go outdoors? There in lies the problem, the game is designed to keep everyone in missions.

My suggestion is simple... give considerably more xp per mob outdoors... i.e. just a simple code (if in playfield xxx, 1.5, 1.75, 2.0 times the normal xp or whatever). Missions have their own playfields, so they would not be subject to the new xp multipliers. There needs to be a reason for people to be outdoors enjoying the great graphics of this game.

Missions are so boring now... and crate and boss loots cause too many teams to break up (i.e. ninja looters)... I can remember hunting the SW mines in lush and the NW mines and I'd be in teams for 4-5 hours in a row... now it's on average about 1.25 missions.

Saves terminals aren't evena big issue in the lvl 150+ game... I haven't saved in quite a few levels at times that I am only xping...


Read my sig, as it says my thoughts completely... There needs to be rewards for both and currently there are only rewards for one. I think anywhere that boosts xp per time, will quickly gain players hunting the areas. Then the world would be better split into people doing missions for tokens and loot (at a slight cost to xp) and people hunting outdoors for max xp (at a slight cost to loot and tokens)
