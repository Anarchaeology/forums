Engys, give them a root breaker line for their pets already. They are a joke in PVP.

Fixers, make their guns crit as well as shotguns. Give them some unique stuff that no other class has that can be useful in combat. One example could be a DECOY. Fixer casts a decoy nano, it makes a replica of him that mirriors his actions. If the decoy is fired upon it seems to take damage just like the fixer so the attacker can't tell if the decoy is the fixer or not from 1 shot. The decoy should mirror all of the fixers actions so the attacked cannot tell which is the fixer and which is the decoy (thou the decoy will do no damage). Anyway, give them 2 or 3 distraction nanos like these, smoke screens, things that make them more elusive. You can figure it out, that's why you get paid the big big bucks.

Advents. Yes please, help them already.

Lot more to say no more time to say it.
