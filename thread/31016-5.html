About the gun, No it doenst have flingshot. There are many ways to look at this. It could be an IP saver at lower levels. The rated normal damage on this gun is higher than that of a nova. The gun has some stat modifiers (Minimal effect) and the downside is that the crit modifier is 50% of that on the NovaFlow IV. The gun also has a clip size that is 2.5 times as large as the Nova. The burst recycle is less than that of the nova, however it lacks fling.


There is nothing to say that a soldier gun has to have fling shot, alot of soldiers used the HGB before because it had good crits, even though it had no specials. Just because the gun we have now (nova) does have fling, who is to say that the new gun has to have it.

I dont expect funcom to come out and say "look a soldier only gun that is better than anything else in the game, all yours go have fun and kill everyone with it."

I am also not saying that this gun is the answer to everything, in fact I cant say anything about it from more than a paper and pencil stanpoint because I haven't used one yet. We wont know untill the gun is actualy put in game.

I can say from the start that I think the gun will not be viable in PvP because of the longer than average equip time. However I do think, just looking at the numbers that it has the potential to be a ok PvM gun. If you take a look at the other soldier only gun in the game, you will find that there is only one, the IMI Tellus TT, a pistol which for the most part has been widly unused.


Soldier are at a state where we have some serious issues that need to be adressed, however I don't think that this fact should be used to judge the quality of this gun. It is better than if 14.4 came with no soldier items, we could have been totaly ignored, becasue we still are an average class.

I also agree that it would be nice to get some feedback regarding the new buffs, I think it could give some idea of what is comming up.

If you dont like my viewpoints, fine by me... but don't get hostile about it, and please, please try to stick to the issues.

My thoughts on guns that have a minimum damage of 1...

Consider for a moment the posibilities when shooting a gun. You can either miss totaly, hit in a less wounding location, or land a shot in a area that inflicts a decent amount of damage, or plop one square in the forhead....


All of these options do exist on assault rifles in game, You can miss ( and this happens often enough), you can get low damage (the equivalent of having a bullet graze your arm), you can get an ok shot (shot in arm), or you can get a critical hit.

When you shoot, there are many possibilities, if you want to compare this to some guns that have higher minimum damage you need to consider the above points, as well as the way the gun would work, a shotgun for example would have higher minimum damage, because the projectile has better odds of doing more damage upon any hit bassed upon size/type.

I would also like to add that some other weapons such as the shotgun have thier own problems, which shouldn't be factored in in determining the acuracy of another weapon, for example the range on a shotgun.
