Psychic checks are currently too restrictive.
At the 4th Bureaucrat Convention one of the main topics disscussed was how crats have one hand tied behind their backs with the overly restrictive psychic checks on our charms and fears.

There are "gaps" in the charms where the old charm's psychic check makes it obsolete but they are unskilled enough to use the next charm nano. The same happens with fears.

There were three very good ideas presented at the BureauCon that are a very reasonable solution.

First one was to simply give us a more powerful psychic debuff. The existing -12 general debuff still dosen't quite fill the gap in. An unmake or dominate psychic debuff could be made available to crats to help ease their transition into the next higher charm.
(Keep in mind, stable/unstable charms are not in a sense the same nanoline. A crat should be allowed to stick with only one line ie, stable and not be forced to use the unstable line. Try it sometime, its really "too" unstable.)

The second idea that came up to fix this was to have a charm nano that can only be cast while in a team. This charm would have a higher psychic check thus, allowing the bureaucrat to retain usefulness in a team environment.

The third idea was to make the psychology skill which is currently still not useful with its paltry bonus to the charm attack skill auctually increase the psychic check. For example, the psychology skill currently adds 14-15% to the attack roll of a charm. What if the psychology skill also increases the psychic check/limit by say 5%? That would give us bureaucrats a very good incentive to increase our psychology and would make the existing charm nanos far more useful and realize their full potential.

There were many other good ideas on what things our profession really can use like a way of seeing how much time we have left on our charmed pets. If typeing /pet report will display your droid's health and time left, having our charmed pet also show up with its time remaining would help crats have an easier time keeping tabs on things.

Requests for 'Crats
Having now hit level 53 and spending about a month with my 'Crat, I feel like I can contribute to this thread. First, unlike some professions I do not feel like 'Crats are "Gimped" or imbalanced, but needs some final tweaks to be complete.

1) Psychology as a tool to reduce the insane Psychic checks.
Make it more along the lines of every 5 points of Psychology
increases the Psychic Limit of Charms and Scares by 1pt.
Hence having 200 Psychology will increase the Psychic limit
of all your charms and scares by 40 points. [You get the idea].
This means we can charm and scare during tough team
oriented hunting and missions and helps in those higher
levels where charm nanos become further apart. This should
be a priority.

2) For the most part my "Mez" line works ok, I would like to
see it's effect occur sooner and not let them keep getting
those last few hits in. This might be a lag or bug, but it's
annoying and diminishes it's usefulness to some extent in
group hunting. Also it would be nice to remove the Mez'd
target from my pet [or the team pets] "hate list", this is
a nasty little issue that drives most of us 'Crats crazy.

3) Introduce some AoE Mez's earlier, right now there is only
one uber high one, seems like an ommission?? This would
greatly help us in groups.

4) The "Cut Red Tape" Nanos should need to be more aggressive
with the Init Buffs and make "lite" versions available at
the lower levels (like with leadership, or Mez's). Seems
silly there is only one.

5) "Red Tape" debuffs should debuff Init's more aggressively
and should be made available at lower levels. Again it seems
like this should have been a whole line of Nanos and most
of them have been ommitted? An AoE of this and #4 would
be nice.

6) Remove the Aura "chat" Spam from the "Leadership" line
of Nanos.

7) Introduce some tradeskills that use Psychology, perhaps
along the lines of insurance? Buffs? Something we can
earn some credits doing.

8) Better [higher level] pistol mastery buff.

You know what, I really cannot think of much else. Really only item 1 is a serious issue that needs to be addressed ASAP. Items from 2 on are useful [if not important] requests to help us be more effective especially in a team, hunting tough targets (and face it most if not all players in AO do this - daily).
