Happy Birthday, Anarchy Online
Greetings!

Well, I remember when Anarchy Online was born. I was stumbling along in my early level 20s with my first character in Everquest. Through much trial and error, and many hours playing Everquest, then Dark Age of Camelot, I finally "discovered" Anarchy Online at the beginning of this year.

For all intensive purposes, I adore this game.

Yes, there are things with this game which I find frustrating--and my frustration is born from the fact I really like this game and I don't want those issues marring the experience to a level which detrimentally off-balances and negates the "fun factor".

As far as people's discontent with this game is concerned, you can look at the forums for ANY on-line game out there, and you will find the same unhappy voicings as you find here. I don't know of any other type of product (MMORPGs) which elicits such inflammatory and reactionary communication from the consumer, consistently.

The Beast Which Is The MMORPG is an unruly one, indeed. Hopefully, over time, the MMORPG Wildebeast can be tamed, controlled...and predicted a bit better by their respective development companies. The MMORPG territory is still vastly uncharted and untamed.

Anarchy Online has experienced intense growing pains, and for all it's gone through, it's still an intelligent, bright child, capable of tremendous, wonderous things. The lesson here is to raise that child as an individual; allow it to be open, expressive and creative; and not bow to peer pressure or "what the other MMORPG kids" are doing.

*grins* Ahh, and we're just stepping into the "Terrible Twos", eh? This will be interesting, indeed!

A very happy birthday to you, Anarchy Online.

Best wishes.
