PVP, PVE, and Overequipping
AO will never have a 'fair' or 'balanced' PVP system as long as the PVE system is based on mobs with many thousands of hitpoints and AC stats so high we have problems with the 'min damage' bug.

The PVP damage cap is an implicit admission of this fact: Player stats are so weak compared to mob stats that weapons which are merely adequate against mobs are devastating against other players.

Of course, the damage cap had its unintended consequences - bringing healing and debuffing classes to the forefront of PVP. It's not a fix so much as a hack - it papers over the fundamental problem: PVP and PVE are entirely different animals.

In isolation, the proposed overequip "fix" will have unintended consequences as well - in particular in the PVE area and for the Trader profession

Maybe more creative minds than mine, with intimate knowledge of both PVP and PVE play, can come up with a solution whereby PVP and PVE can be integrated. But myself, I think they should be seperated. PVP areas should be rolled back to specific contested battlefields, or suppression gas should be lowered to allow battles as part of the storyline, but otherwise, most areas should not be PVP enabled.

The system is fundamentally flawed as it stands; while it has its place in the game, it should be tightly restricted and carefully controlled.
