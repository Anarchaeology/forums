While I don't want to see the introduction of a melee-in-range root (mainly because it will req something nobody uses - 'FC balance') I do agree that roots / snares are overpowered.

  • Even on full def with max resist, roots / snares land all the time. I have never resisted a root in PvP with any toon. The total shut-down of melee capability that roots cause are similar to a 100% nano skill drain or a ranged skill drain.
  • The casting speed on roots, even at full def, means that chain-rooting is a simple prospect.
  • Even though roots can be broken by attacking, they are not always - sometime you get 2-3 hits before it breaks, and before you can close the distance, a new root hits you.
  • Root-breaking stims are not effective enough in terms of the duration of and roots / snares. With a 2 second delay between stim uses, and, in some cases, stims reducing the root by mere seconds, you may as well not bother.
  • The issue with melee weapons is a moot point. The fact that recent melee weapons are overpowered affects only those capable of using them. The EQB / PR problems are seperate - those weapons need balancing, but that is no reason to leave PvP unblanaced. In addition, all MA users, and MAs specifically, are totally useless in PvP because of the root problem. The MA attack is not overpowered. Add to this the parry issue (not so big in PvP, but vs toons with max parry...) which ranged users do not suffer from. Ever had a mob / toon 'dodge' that 10k AS?
  • While roots / snares [i[do[/i] need to be balanced for all three aspects of their use, we have already seen PvP specific adjustments to combat mechanics.
  • The AS issue is also seperate to this problem - see above.

I would suggest that roots / snares recieve a PvP only adjustment - something in the order of 1/2 (1/4?) duration, or a more fragile breaking point (i.e. every hit breaks them, or longer cast / recharge (not a good idea), and a *big* increase in the effectiveness of stims (perhaps in PvP only, or maybe in both PvP and PvM).

These (not all together!!) would help rooters retain the edge they deserve in PvP, not mess up PvM, and allow the introduction of additional PvP strategies - beyond the 'I'm gonna win so I'm gonna root-n-shoot'.
