Bur careful, don't make the mistake of thinking that you get more money for combined patterns

Once you combine 2 pieces... they are worth 0 in shops.

So you make your choice: Either get a pattern assembled then find a team to pop the relevant pocket boss, whose loot is either much more valuable then the single patterns or impossible to get in other way....or sell the patterns for quick cash.

Personally, I make 2 of each complete pattern and store it. THEN I start selling pieces of that pattern. I got 3 backpacks of full patterns, novicum and unpolluted crystals in my bank Now I must start getting teams to pop pocket bosses! Hehehehe
