Chest loot - What went wrong?
As far as I remember, the last patch included fixing of the boring chest loot (newland/mort maps, runspeed buffs, grenade ammo, etc). Right after the patch was released, it really was like that. I found nice armour, blister packs and weapons in many chests.

But at some point all that ceased to happen. 90% of all chests I find with my three chars (28, 35, 71) are empty. What's in the rest? Runspeed buffs, useless maps, ammo and tutoring devices. I know I would not be able to use more than 5% of what I find in chests, but nevertheless, the way it works now, it really adds to the boredom of single missions.

So the question I've asked myself many times in AO: How can these changes happen, when there has been no new patch applying them?
