Garamond,

The South Rubi-Ka Society is an OT org dedicated to roleplaying. In character the Society functions as "gentleman's club" ( we even have our own butler now). The purpose of the Society is to organise cultural, charity or educational events for the citizens of Rome and Omni-1, as well as enhance its members social life.

In ooc terms, the purpose of the Society is to offer an escape from the levelling treadmill and focus on the life of our characters when they are "off duty" (i.e. not out hunting or on missions). Currently we are running an table top style rpg adventure for our members based on nano-formula drugs in Omni-Sewers and a Society sponsored drug rehab centre Omni-Sewers which is under threat by some local thugs (of course, there is more to it, but that would be telling). Other events have included an after-dinner talk on the theory behind metaphysics, a string of events surrounding professional Leet Fighting (Rubi-Ka's answer to dog fighting) or simple day trip to visit to the ICC HQ in Andromeda. Basically any excuse to interact in character or make Rubi-Ka feel more alive.

There are no level requirements to join the society. All you need is to be Omni-Tek on Rk1 and have in interest in roleplaying. Ideally we aim to recruit people who are interested in creating events as well; in pen and paper terms ideally our members are as much Gms and they are players, but that is not a requirement.
It is also worth noting that at present most of our members are European or play during European peak times.

If this sounds like something you might be interested in visit our website http://rubika.no-ip.org/srs/ or give Marog, Nephilous, Tappert, Heksa or Savoy a /tell in game. You can also reach me at the following email address gsoylent@hotmail.com
