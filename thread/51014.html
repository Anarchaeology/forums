pvp points system suggestion
i was just thinking.
pvp as a high titler is getting rather boring and is nowhere near as lively as when i was a freshman , rookie etc.
there are very few novice or above omni for me to kill atm and get pvp points of.
i understand that u cant get any points of someone that is more than 1 title below or more precise within 100 pvp points of u. but working towards the next title for me and getting pvp points was what pvp in ao is about.
what i would like is that anyone not within 100 pvp points of u gives some credit when u kill them. for example killing someone within your "skill" range atm gives something like 16 points a kill (+/- some depending on point difference). why not give kills that dont lie within your skill range something like 1 or 2 points?
imo any kill (*if your a real life killer or live in some fantasy world and slay demons or whatever) u gain some experience and knowledge from. practise makes perfect they say in this setup at least i could make my way to experienced very slowly. but in the current setup i doubt i will ever get there unless i get free kills or title farm etc
what u think? give your opinion pls
