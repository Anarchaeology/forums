Hmm...
A big part of the 18H spawn time (spawn times period for that matter) is about 9 months ago the game got away from Funcom.

A Funcom official wrote (I believe on their main newspage) that they expected only a handful of lvl 200's by the end of the 4 year story arc.

At that time, there were already several 200's and people in the upper 100's.

People were able to OE more than Funcom anticipated due to a very creative player base, and a few exploits didn't help the situation either.

Bottom line is, Funcom didn't have any high level content in place. Either they thought they had time to program it and add it to the game, or they thought PvP would be the high level content.

Either way, in a desperation move they opted to copy EverCamp to add some high level content to the game as fast as possible to prevent a further exodus of high level players.

High spawn times are a lack of time, or maybe even creativity, to program something a bit more complicated with more detail that provides gamers with activities (no, watching TV for hours or just chit-chatting waiting for something to pop again should not be considered an in-game activity) to improve their characters and be a part of the story.

Since the game is what it is at this point, how about every team that enters the dungeon and fights Tara gets a team mission icon (just like any other team mission) and the team with loot rights would automatically have each team member get 1 item that would drop. Make the items NODROP UNIQUE, and have code in place to guarantee you get an alternate item from the drop list if you already have what it wanted to give you.

Also, decrease the spawn time. There is no way making the spawn time high makes the game more fun. Again it just makes it easier to program. Instead of providing real in-game activities to finally reach the objective, you are willing to take that off of their hands by camping (again camping = no game activity = not fun).

I am surprised some people actually think the long spawn makes the game more fun. I can only attribute that to the TV-Surfer mindset. You want to watch TV so you surf around and finally settle on whatever is there. The fact that EverCamp was successful doesn't make camping fun, it just means that is what people settled for at the time.

Forget the reasons why people think camping is justified, how many people here actually enjoy camping and all that is associated with it? I don't. Camping means I am not playing the game at that time. I know people in EQ who have had people page them when they were through camping so they could get out of bed and camp for the item.

This is a game. A game should be fun and the game should be designed to revolve around lives not have people revolve their lives around a game. That is what camping does.

As soon as a game that handles high level content and rare phat loot without camping is released, people will look back and laugh that they actually camped much less thought it was fun to do so.
