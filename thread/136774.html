Boost-suggestion for ALL the creation-weapons.
Been looking over the stats on the creation-weapons and here is a suggestion on how to improve them. It must be easier to improve them if u have something to start from, so here goes:


The Crotalus (QL 45)
Change the damage to 20-140(30) and lower the multi-melee-req to 220.

Viper Staff (QL 63)
Change recharge to 1.5 sec.

Vital Buckler (QL 68)
Just remove its ability to attack so it wont slow down the main attackweapon in the other hand.

Notum Defender (QL 68)
Remove its attack-ability so it wont slow down the main attackweapon in the other hand and increase the Nanoresist-bonus to 52.

Solar Guard (QL 82)
I dont have much to say on this shield as long as it can be dualwielded with something else. However, since this is a shield and is not ment for attacking with, remove the attack-ability on it so it wont slow down the Main attack-weapon held in the other hand.

Coplan's Hand Taipan (QL 88)
Should have its damage increased to 37-230-(74) and have its multi-melee-req decreased to 400.

Living Shield of Evernan (QL 109)
Just remove the attack-ability so it wont slow down the main attackweapon in the other hand. Other than that, its ok.

Asp of Semol (QL 116)
Make this dualwield-able and change the 12 sec recharge to 3 sec recharge because there is absolutely no reason why that weapon should have 12 sec recharge whatsoever. I would normally say that this weapon should have even lower recharge but I noticed that this staff-line has a 27% chance of adding a poison-DoT.

Wave Breaker (QL 125)
Remove its attack-ability so it wont slow down the main attackweapon in the other hand and increase the Nanoresist-bonus to 91.

Bitis Striker (QL 133)
This weapon should clearly have its damage increased to 42-320(84) and the multi-melee-req decreased to 600.

Belthior's Flame Ward (QL 144)
This is a higher QL version of the Solar Guard so same changes should apply to this.

Wixel's Notum Python (QL 157)
Change the recharge to 2 sec. Allso make this dualwield-able.

Mochams Guard (QL 180)
Remove its attack-ability so it wont slow down the main attackweapon in the other hand and increase the Nanoresist-bonus to 137.

Gold Acantophis (QL 187)
This should have its damage increased to 60-460(180).

Azure Cobra of Orma (QL 198)
This weapon is a higher QL version of the Asp of Semol and should have 3 sec recharge too. Allso make this dualwield-able.

Shield of Asmodian (QL 199)
Remove its ability to attack so it wont slow down the main attackweapon in the other hand.


Well there u have it. My point of view anyway. MP's should feel that they want to use these weapons, in various combinations, whatever they feel is most usefull within the skills they have.

Yup,

The creations wouldn't need much in the way of tweaking to sort them out.

Just for info: the shields don't hamper the weapons as much as you might think. They have 8%TS/2%1HB attack skills and so only add up to 10% of a weapon. In reality, if you're hitting at 1/1 with the weapon in your right hand, you'll only lose 1/9 hits from the weapon by wearing the shield. It could possibly be reduced still further of course - perhaps by making it 0%TS/0%1HB... or maybe 0.1%.

I'd also say that you'd need a consistent recharge speed for the 2HB Staves throughout the line. You have the low QL one with 1.5, the mid QL one with 3, another one with 2 and then the high QL one with 3 again. It's worth keeping in mind that if you set it to 4 seconds, the MP will still achieve 1/1 speed with it when he maxes melee init. 3.5 seconds might be a good overall recharge speed. The lower ones would hit slowly still and the high level ones would have a good speed.

As you point out though, if the speed on the 2HB ones is improved, then it might be a good idea to reduce the chance of landing the DoT a little.
