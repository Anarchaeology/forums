Hard to say the exact level, but when you have your ql 200 weapon, your ql 200 implants and ql 200 armor you don't have to rely much more on credits.

130ish perhaps?

But by 80 single player missions starts to slowly pay off more cash, especially if you clean them.

You basically have two options:

1) Clean out missions and sell to machines.

2) Blitz for stuff people want. At low levels this is concrete cushions. (Not creviced concrete cushions, but concrete cushions.) By 50 you can blitz for omni-pol armor. Do so, when you have a complete set all the same ql sell it for 1-3 million. Rinse and repeat.
