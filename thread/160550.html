NEWS-FLASH! Omni-Employee Chadwick "Arakiba" Vein Found Dead

The Omni Inquisitor - IS Division
Report By: Jacelyn "Lem" Waldram
March 29477 RST

Exclusive images of the remains of Omni-Tek Employee Chadwick "Arakiba" Vein was obtained by the Omni Inquisitor early this morning. The images clearly show that he is indeed dead, and speculation of his whereabouts can now be put at rest. Next to the body, another Omni Inquisitor exclusive, was a message to all reading:

"Arakiba is dead. Those who seek Demoneyes to do him harm will all die."

Who is this Demoneyes I ask? Why is there a ten million credit bounty on his head? Does Deputy Director Zhen even have a clue on what is going on? Should she be replaced? When will we know more? Are we being told the full truth, and if not, why?

More details coming in the next release of the Inquisitor.
