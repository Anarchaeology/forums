quote:
Originally posted by TanisAS
Marketing downside... I dont see one.
Well, the marketing downside is this:

Having boxes on shelves in stores will help Funcom get more customers. So they want to have boxes in stores. To keep boxes in stores, you have to have strong sales in stores. If the booster is available for download, then current customers probably won't go to a store to buy it; they'll just charge the $20 and wait for it to download.

If the current customers don't go to stores to buy it, then the in-store sales will not be as strong. If stores have boxes of the booster pack going unsold, they won't continue to stock it.

So, while I'm pretty sure the booster will eventually be available for download, I bet it won't be until after most of us have gone to a store to buy it.
