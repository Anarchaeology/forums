READ THIS
Okay .. this post is 5 pages deep and no one will read it but here goes.

Basic game mechanics put soldiers at a huge disadvantage against most if not all other proffesions at higher levels (pipe down level 15 soldiers) in PvP.

1. Damage. A soldiers only friend in the cold world of Rubi Ka is his assault rifle. However All but one (and the Div 9 isn't that great at PvP) good weapon for PvP is atainable by all classes.

That translates into Soldier+Other-proff for all other proffessions Vs. Soldier.

2. Reflect. A soldiers best defense is TMS. Which can protect him against a lot of dmg for a short period of time. However if done properly all proffesions can gain a reflect level close to if not higher than TMS on a permanent level. The Soldier has the added inability to use nanos for a long period after he uses TMS.

That translates into Solder+Other-proff. for all other proffesions Vs. Soldier-nano skills after a short period.

3. Dmg Nerf. Soldiers who where way tweaked used to be able to instantly kill thier opponents with a quick alpha strike. So FC nerfed damage. However healing still has it's full effect. Debuffs still have thier full effect. The trick (not that there is much trick to this) to killing a soldier is debuff his init or root him back off and heal.

That translates into Solder+Other-proff Vs. 1/2 of a Soldier.


So what you end up with is a proffesion where most of the games PvP mechanics work against it. Any proff that can heal will own a Soldier. Any player with a soldiers gun and some reflect bracers and a friend to cast RRF on them are 80% of a soldier and have thier own proff skills on top of that.

End of story. Not to knock the Soldiers on the top 10 PvP list ... but a title dosen't mean PvP power. It means playing the game well.

Where a soldier should accel (armed combat with others) they are the weakest of proffesions. I wanted to get into PvP ... but as I close in on title 6 I am seeing that I can't be effective as a soldier. They are basicaly broken. Who knows .. maybe beyond repair.
