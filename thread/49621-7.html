Lohem comments don't suck, he just made a correct observation of a problem in this game. And there is a problem, and in the middle of this problem happens to be a profession called traders.

Its only the traders that seem to have a problem acknowledging this issue that currently creates such an imbalance to the game.

In my opinion there are two issues that currently create huge amounts of imbalance in this game:

1. Long duration debuffs in PvP are too powerful.
2. 100% effective heals when all damage is halved.

Anyone with a half of a brain could see that there is a problem. And some less self-centered traders and docs also have said that there is indeed a problem.

We have 12 professions in this game. And from those 12 professions only 3-4 are viable in PvP at the moment. While a huge advantage in every area at the moment belongs to traders, traders are the profession that is on the top of the food chain atm, they aren't even slightly above some other professions, but way ahead.

It is not players fault, that when damage was halfed and OE got implemented, FC did not consider huge implances this would create to some professions with a very limited or no ability to debuff nor heal. While some professions enjoy enormous ability in one of those things or even both.

If you guys have ever played DAoC, you should know what belonging to the top of the food chain one day and being banished to most useless profession the other day means. I know, we all should avoid this from happening. However, if this game is ever going to have decent and somewhat balanced PvP system, FC needs to address issues with both heals and overpowering debuffs...now that they have implement half damage and OE fixes.

This should affect docs, traders and MP...and would create balance at the end. Sure you would have to join the same "Whining-club" with rest of the gimp professions, but at least we would have same thing in common...none of us isn't uber any longer. We would all be the grey mass with some slightly better ability in combat then others, and some better support role in combat then others and some better trade skills in the game then others. Not one or two professions that is godly in every single aspect of the game.
