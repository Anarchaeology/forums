OOC: A Public Plea to Funcom - Re: Baboon's Night Club
This message is entirely OOC. I'll be sending it out in as many forms as possible to try to make contact with someone at Funcom...so hopefully at least someone--anyone--at the FC offices might read it through. If any regular roleplayers on AO have any updates or editorials to add to this within the next couple days, please do so. I'd like to see it shipped off (by as many mediums as possible) by this coming Tuesday, Dec 17th.

I don't expect a response, but I haven't tried yet either, and that is a much bigger mistake than just letting it be.

Dear Funcom,

Merry Christmas!

My name is Lan "Tarryk" Kozar in the world of Rubi-Ka on the RK1 server. I am writing to you as a loose representative of the well-established community of roleplayers on the world of Rubi-Ka. While I may not speak for all of them, I do speak of things that have been troubling the night life on Rubi-Ka since the game's release date, and I've had a lot of confirmation to the fact that there are many in agreement on this issue.

It concerns one of the social would-be hot-spots on the planet of Rubi Ka: Baboon's Night Club in Omni Entertainment. This is far and away the most beautiful club you have created on the world, and it is regarded by every social roleplayer on the planet as a sort of forbidden zone of what COULD be.

I don't know if you've ever had a developer literally take a stroll through the night club. I can garauntee you certainly haven't done so on a busy night recently. Why? Because there are no busy nights recently.

At first glance, the average developer and/or gamer would perceive this to be the results of a loss of roleplayers on the world. I don't know how I can prove it to be otherwise to you beyond saying "look around a bit more", but this is so untrue it's practically funny. The reason that the greatest night club on the planet of rubi-ka is deserted is because the issue of lag inside the club is so horrible that even the most powerful systems with the fastest connections run into crippling chop inside the club--both graphically related AND server-side lag related (especially when it's crowded).

This has been attributed by different players to many factors, including but not limited to the massive number of textures concentrated in certain areas, and the ill advised placement of doors and room seperators. Whatever the issues, they are altogether responsible for the constant demise of Baboons as a social outlet for the people of Rubi-Ka.

Right now I am begging. Pleading. Even whining. Please donate at least a small resource to the analysis and restructuring of Baboon's Night Club. Even if it is one developer's mind for not more than 1 hour a week, please at least have someone look at the issue of the severe lag in Baboon's and determine what might be a few good steps to take in a future patch to alleviate it.

I know that RolePlay concerns are a low priority for you, and I know that you are much more concerned with the thousands of whining flames you get constantly concerning "harder" gameplay features.

We just ask that you give a little (just a smidgen) more "developer's recognition" to the very underrated yet comparitively HUGE society of die-hard roleplayers that your game has become a home to. We may seem like a small bunch of elitists to other players (even to you, perhaps), but I assure you we are keeping a closer eye on the evolution of Life On Rubi-Ka than the average player could possibly imagine. And the Baboon's Lag that has existed now for 2 years has always been a highly voiced and major concern for all of us.

We don't ask for crippling or unbalancing changes to the game. We don't ask for nerfs or twinks. We just ask for a place to call home...minus the crippling lag/chop.

Thanks for your time!

Sincerely,

Lan Tarryk Kozar
