Looking for RolePlaying, Notum War, RK1, Clan
Greetings,

I've been gone for a long time and returned to AO to find the clan I created had died out. Even more sadly the Gathering of Souls event I created had died out.

RolePlaying:
I don't see the point in playing a MMORPG if you don't try to live a bit like the world is real aka roleplay your char. I'm a relaxed roleplayer and jump in and out of char depending on the situation. I just don't feel there is a reson to force rp on the unwashed masses (When was the last time you took a shower in AO, well there you go, you are unwashed.)

Notum War:
What I've seen of NW so far looks promising and fun, so I'm definately looking for a clan fighting in the tower wars.

What I hope to bring to the clan:
Active and energitic (is that a word, well sounds cool anyway) personality. If your org need someone to organize raids, host RP events, help old/new -bies.

If all that matters to you is whatever I use a size 200 shoe or 10, if honor is only an issue when you can use it to degrade others and my acceptance into the guild depends on some complicated mathematical formula, then you definately never want me in the clan.

I don't want to lead a clan, I want to be a good little puppie and bark at the clan leaders enemies and get padded on the head in thanks.

Active is a two edges sword, it can be used to help the clan or disrupt it, if I end up having more ambitions than the clan does, then we will end up pushing and pulling and leaving no one happy.
