Let's list the best weapons available, not reliant upon OE or crit buffs!
I love weapons, and usually build my characters around using a particular kind. Not going to include unique drops, just 1-200 regular weapons of any kind. Which do you think are best overall for PvM?

These choices were based on Antiguardians stats, and take into account 1. Maximum damage 2. weapon speed 3. IP cost 4. special attacks 5. popularity, and many other factors, including personal experience and research done by others.

Enforcer: Sledgehammer or Beam
Adventurer: Dual rider executioners, dual BBI's, or Schuyler bow
Trader: Single S.A. tranquil home defender, or Vektor
Fixer: Mausser Chemical Streamer
Agent: Disaffiliation Sniper, Stigma Rifle, etc.
Soldier: Nova Flow/flashpoint/Supernova
Engineer: Brilliant OT 12 (new for 14.2) http://auno.org/aodb.php?t=ag&cmd=view&id=160183
NT: True Katana or longmoon
MP: True Katana, longmoon or Schuyler bow
MA: Martial Arts skill
Bureaucrat: Martial arts skill
Doctor: Martial arts skill

I think it would be a very cool thing to have alot more viable weapons for each class. I've listed mostly obvious ones...some not so obvious. Please add any that people should know about!
