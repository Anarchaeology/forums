The Static Mission
Ok...I got my Dodga pads prior to the second and third missions being available. At the time, not many people on RK2 missioned for them. So, as a result, I had a pretty easy time getting all the little robot parts.

Over the weekend, I helped my buddy out with mission number 1, for the first set of pads. He already had 7 out of the 8 parts. So, I thought getting the 1 last part would be easy.

WRONG.

We killed well over 700 robots and didn't find the part we needed. Every bot spawn was camped HEAVILY. Out of the 700+ bots we killed, only about 15 of them actually dropped the UNIQUE items.

This mission was not fun at all. It was boring and tedious, a complete waste of time, and pointless. And, since the last item never dropped....it is endless!

So, thanks for the nice content.
