Name Rubi-Ka's Suns Contest
Name Rubi-Ka's Suns Contest

Rubi-Ka's Suns are currently named ZMJ-A and ZMJ-B. I know we can do a lot better than that. So after hearing about the Name Rubi-Ka's Moons Contest the ICC's Galactic Cartography Board has asked for a follow up contest to name our suns.

To enter, all you have to do is place an entry for each sun's name(there are two suns, one yellow and one blue) in this thread. Only one entry for each sun per person. The names must be appropriate to say publicly and the suns can't be named after yourself. However names of *other* people that have done great things for Rubi-Ka are acceptable.

The judging panel will select the best names and submit them to the ICC's Galactic Cartography Board where they will (hopefully) be made the official names of the suns.

The winning entry for each sun's name will receive 10 million credits. That's right! You can win 20 million if both of your entries are selected. The total may even go up if I receive more donations.

This contest is open to ALL of Rubi-Ka's citizens regardless of affiliation. So hurry up and submit your ideas. Remember to post them in this thread.


We have a winner!! Please congratulate Votary on his winning entries of Animus and Amicus
