Babtism by Fire
Hey all
Well first off I'd like to say that my first time at Tara was a blast.
Before Tara I experianced my first taste of PVP or was it Mass PvP at 2hO, Omni was outnumbered till the word got around. My first mistake was to grid into 2ho, the first thing I saw was "lol" and 12 seconds later I was dead. I was in a team with Wildwal, Vergil, Rebecca and Cantik.

Well it wasnt going too well for us since the 6 of us( I forgot the other team mates name sorry) seemed to be the only Omni there, or at least it felt like we were. Later on more Omni arrived and we were able to contain the clanners in the bunkers, picking off those we could from time to time.
I died a total of 2 times I think... Didnt kill anyone.

Then came Tara later on nice place, lots of ppl and well lots of lag. I just want to thank Meila and Nerfmeee(sp) for tips to reduce lag. Now this was fun. Sure I died 3 times, but it was a lot of fun.

Anyways I just want to say thanks to the people mentioned above for helping me out and showing me that there's more than just leveling in AO.

Thanks!

Anu.
