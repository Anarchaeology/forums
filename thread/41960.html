Curtlee, planning and promoting an event is not a small task and sometimes they do not go as expected or as well as hoped. If you really want to do this, you need to promote it better than saying, "Well, I'm going to have this event, but if it sucks, I'm going to stop doing them." That is not going to get people to your event.

Aktrez and I both know that a big, exciting promotion that you keep pushing is what gets people to events. Just look at our signatures - lots of color and flash that you can't miss! Okay, maybe it's a little much, but both of us usually manage to have successful events. This wasn't always the case though. When we were in Belle Rouge together, our events weren't as big and elaborate as they are now; we were still figuring out what worked and what didn't which is something that we're still doing, but getting better at.

If you're going to have successful events, it's going to be because you put in the effort to come up with something interesting and then promoted the heck out of it. Sure, you have some obstacles ahead of you that most people don't (I'll spare you a speech about that), but when Aktrez and I started out, nobody had even heard of us - at least you have name recognition. Either way, you're going to have to establish yourself as someone who does fun events; after you do a few of them, people will remember your last one and tell their friends about the next event.

If Aktrez is willing to help you out (even though the timing is bad for her), I am too. I'm a little too married to be the date girl, but maybe I can help in some other way. Let me know.
