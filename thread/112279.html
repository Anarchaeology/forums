Uniting the clans
It's about time we clanners stop valuing our differences for a sec
and try to focus on what we have in common and work together instead!

Since before we lost the CoT, clanners have more or less ruled themselves.
We banded up in various alliances with the ones who we felt we could cooperate with, and it worked ..
It still does. However, whenever anything actually had an impact on clan politics,
clanners would divide more and more and split up into more and more alliances ..
And we've been valuing it as well as it encompasses some of the freedom we clanners fight for.


Why?

This has got to come to an end! The lease of Omni-Tek will expire in not too long,
and those of you who partly know what we're doing only need to throw a glance at the map
to tell that we're winning. What happens when we do? It is our belief that if the clans
as a whole are not ruled by one single governing body, we will plunge into a civil war!
The reasons for are plenty and the reasons not to are few.


The Idea

Thus, as a small step to unite the clans, I want to invite the clan leaders to a meeting.
This meeting is not an attempt to rule any of you, it is not a new alliance, but it is a chance
to come and perhaps get a bit oriented in the clan political landscape.
The idea is that if different spokespeople from the various alliances and clans show up
and present us their cause, maybe some will find others likeminded and can join the
alliances that already exist f.ex.
Also, various clans can keep an eye on where other clans are situated politically.

This is meant as a step to strengthen the various voices of the clans as much as the clans as a whole.

I won't post the where, but I was thinking Saturday the 29th 'o March for the first meeting.
Of course, if there will be future meetings, we might want to move it to another weekday.
So, be sure to check on the leaders channel for rumours.

Summed Up :

What? : A .. Clans Unity Meeting?
When? : Saturday the 29th! .. 22GMT!
Where? : Oops, won't tell here.

Keep an eye on the Leader's Chan.

While I am not a leader of an org I strongly support this movement and hope for some amount of success in it. A stronger unity is needed.

Some points:

If a central form of clan government is formed the elections must be held fairly. Allowing all members of an org to vote could easily overpower the votes by number of said members. ( a 20 person org vs. a 100 person org is not a fair vote ).

Distribution of land for mines. This I see as a very important measure to consider. If we have central leadership there must be a way to divide mineable lands evenly to prevent feuds within the clans over said lands.

Unified defense plan. Every org of the central governing body must agree to and adhere to a plan of mutual defense. Anyone outside of said agreement would be seen as a hostile, be it clan, omni, or neut.

Note that the following rant has nothing to do with the Clan Union Meeting.
Also note that this post is not written on behalf of the Clan Imperialist Army.

I think the question of a centralized goverment is an important one.
If it was up to me, I'd make it something like this :

Clans who don't rule themselves now are ruled in various alliances.
For a goverment to be effective, it needs support from these alliances.
Those deciding what political issues are important could be comprised
of leaders elected from these various alliances. These leaders can debate issues amongst themselves
and then present them to the various clan leaders at a meeting like the Clan Union Meeting f.ex.
On this meeting, the "ordinary" clan leaders would vote and decide, much like parlamentarism.
While giving most power to the alliances (as they would be the ones deciding what the issues are),
it would still allow room for the un-allied clans to have a say in the decisions that are being made.

Also, this would strengthen the alliances with both power and members (probably),
thus also promoting general clan cooperation.


The idea would need some detailing, but I think it's democratic,
sensible and doable enough to gain some support.
