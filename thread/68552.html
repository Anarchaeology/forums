Mercenaries, and also Neutral ideas
I was thinking that maybe the game could have NPC mercenaries in it. Mercenaries would work as pets, and be usable by all professions. They could also be used in addition to other pets, so for instance an MP could have both an attack pet and a mercenary. The way the merc would work could be this: you pay a fee, either credits or some special item(s) and you can order the merc around for a period of time. Or, the merc would drain credits at a certain rate. If the merc died, you would lose it.

I was also thinking, that perhaps Neutrals could be given special status as the only faction in which players could be hired. How would this be different from teaming, or someone else helping you? There are lots of ways to solve this problem. One could be that, when a person hired a neutral (player) merc, they would not get lower exp normally resulting from teaming, and the merc would get a large cash bonus. Another way I can think of to make hiring out desirable is to put a special flag on either the merc or the hiring person, so that the aggro focused on them is either increased a lot or lessened a lot.

How else do you think Neutrals could be given some content? What do you think of the idea of NPC mercs or neutral player mercs?

What do you all think of these ideas? Please tell me what you think of them, or how they might be changed.
