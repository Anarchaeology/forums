nano resist really doesnt mean too much to an adv, because it is a really dark blue skill. so even if it is maxed, and fully implanted, it will still be lower than the nano skill of the opponent which means you're gonna get hit with that nano.

so, advents will get rooted and advents will get deprived/plundered because we're simply going to hit the ceiling on that skill at a much lower rating.

i haven't pvp'd in a while...i'm currently seeking to upgrade my armor/implants/weapons/nanos to check it out...

i don't think a pistol wielder, whether it's an advent, soldier, engie, or whoever is going to be good in pvp. especially against a GA fixer. why? no real special attack...fling is worthless.

so...after our fling shot...we basically just have to sit back rooted and heal ourselves while we get bursted/rooted to death. eventually our nano pool will run out, and we will die.

i think that the damage shields will do significant damage, but the HoTs that fixers have are really nice and will nullify a lot of the damage the fixer takes from the damage shield.

but, honestly people, i don't think it's an advent v fixer issue here...it's a pistol v any profession issue.

so, what's the alternative for the advent? 1he and all melee will result in a pretty quick death when fighting a rooting class. that leaves the old stand byes that all pvp'rs use....X3, Ithicas, maybe even the Manex. i think that's the problem...there are absolutely no real pvp pistols other than the custom desert reets, and those outlive their pvp usefulness at about level 70ish.

there needs to be a real pistol that has pistol/fling/burst damage. the regular reets do really poor damage...and that's our best alternative for a pistol/fling/burst weapon at high levels.

just my opinion...fixers and ga does not need to be nerfed...just give advents the pistols we've been begging for. make the pain of patricia accessible (if it's even in the game at all). if it does drop off that patricia mob at the primus camp, you would need to have about 50 title level 5-6 players with a well thought out and coordinated attack in order to actually get the gun....(if it's even in the game at all).

or make a strict pistol/burst pistol for advents to hold as offhand weapons. i never understood why the vultures were never pistol/shotgun/fling/burst instead of what they are pistol/shotgun/fling. the added burst would have offset the lower attack rating of the gun because of the 66% pistol/33% shotgun.

i know i've rambled and gone off topic a bit...but to recap:

1. an advent may be able to kill a ga fixer...but most likely a pistol advent won't.

2. ga fixer is powerful, but not unbeatable if you have the right weapon (my guess is a manex is probably the best weapon to beat a fixer with because of the high burst damage and rapid burst rate).

3. only traders are unbeatable.

4. give a pistol/burst option to allow pistoleers a better chance at pvp.
