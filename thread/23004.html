It might not be as tough as we all think. Two very doable ideas come to mind:

First, if they have unique item tags for all objects including bags, then they can easilly add a feature to the client to add a custom name to any item that is moused over. This information would be stored client-side as it holds no real interest for the server or other players.

Example, hold your mouse over a bag and type '/itemname "Eye Implants". Now a string gets stored in your characters Prefs folder with a reference to that item's unique id. Anytime you mouse over that item you see "Eye Implants" instead of "Large Backpack".

Secondly, assuming that the client knows what is in each bag. The rollover mouse tooltip could simply be the name of the item in the first slot in the bag. You could have six bags holding weapons. But, if you mouse over them you might see "Vektor Dragon Shotgun", "Deluxe Disafilliation Sniper Rifle", "Loose Slank Chop", etc.

Surely you'd know which bag to open to get Shotties, Sniper Rifles, or Edged Weapons.

Either of these implementations would probably satisfy us for some time.

Edit:

And Tappert is right. The item system in AO is so completely inflexible and rigid that you will probably NEVER see such things as Maker's Marks on tradeskill items, variable weapon enhancement (just look at the kludge job they did with the Summers SMG's, or random rare loot. Adding the ability to have a maker's mark on every weapon that can be made would balloon their database like a shut-in's ass after a case of Jolt and a carton of Bon Bon's.

But, move the work clientside for non-exploitable conveniences such as this and it's doable.
