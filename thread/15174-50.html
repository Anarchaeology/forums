my opinion
Hello,

I believe Funcom is going in the right direction with this upcoming patch. However, I hope they will reconsider the planned total shutdown of pets when skills are not met anymore (maybe lower their abilities, but I suspect this is not a simple task since damage output will not be the only thing to change for the pet... there will be HP, AC, evades etc...).

I've seen people say that lowbies should not be giving their opinion on this topic. I would like to say I do not think so. It is at the lower levels that overequiping is the most noticeable. And it is those people who are on the receiving end of the overequiped beams or hammers or whatever. They are the ones who can suffer most from this problem, while high level characters can afford to equip "legally" quite high QL armor or weapons.
I, too, have been a low level player, and I had my own vision of the game. I have seen someone in this thread say we should check if we could get a refund because FC is about to turn the game into something different than what we wanted at launch. I must say that it is the opposite for me: they are turning it into what I was expecting in the first place, back when I registered for the game (for the people wondering... Date of registration 2001-06-29 22:11:55 UTC). I was coming from EverCamp, and one of the features that interested me in AO was the skill system: it gave more control on the evolution of the character over time. I chose Solitus/Doctor/Clan. And the first time a trader NPC landed a skill debuff on me, he scared me like hell, and I'm not even sure I survived the fight. I've hated trader NPCs since then, BUT I was very happy, thinking that in AO debuffs actually worked (the shaman in EQ has loads of useless debuffs). This is what I was expecting for a game based on a skill system...
Back to the OE problem... Some people asked to look at the bigger picture, this is how I view it: the skill system is an important part of the backbone for this game. Skills have to be equally important for every profession in the game. Have that system work well, and then you can safely start banlancing the professions.
The solution proposed in 14.2 regulates damage dealing for weapons users in a way that ressembles the one for nano users: you want to do more damage? get a better weapon/nanocrystal *AND* raise your skills (through buffs, IPs, implants...); you got your skills debuffed and cannot get them back high enough? switch to a NF/weapon you can actually use with your current skills, that means use a lower QL NF/weapon. The only difference with weapons is that it will not be necessary to switch them, since their QL will be lowered through the DamageBonus, DamageMin and DamageMax values.
Note: globally, weapon skills for weapon users go up at the same rate as nano skills for nano-users (i.e. they are on the same scale), so to be fair, a debuff of X skill points should be as bothersome for weapon users as it is for nano-users => that is why I am not in favor of the solution proposed by Bionitrous in another thread: give some high QL debuff nanos that will remove a very big amount of weapon skill points -- besides, isn't the attack rating compared to the evade skill of the target to determine if the opponent is hit (please correct me if I am wrong)? If so, too big skill debuffs will be too powerful...
I find the solution proposed in 14.2 logical, but then again, I am of those who think that skills are important.
However I think they should work on this a little more: several people have put the finger on potential problems and I will not repeat them all here... just think more about pet classes; 20% seems too big a step, can't a smoother curve for lowering damage be used?; how are special attacks dealt with?; maybe re-evaluate trader debuffs; certainly re-evaluate MOBs stats after the patch; definitely re-evaluate professions...

I do not think OE'ing (i.e. uploading/equiping a NF/weapon/armor which will eventually have requirements that will not be met) is cheating, but I believe that damage has to be more closely related to skills for weapon users. These were my thoughts on the skill system in AO and the upcoming 14.2 patch. Please do not see my supporting of this change as an evidence that I envy those who claim to have superior IQ, as it is not the case.

PS: I believe this patch will give FC more control on every profession's damage output, thus making the balancing easier.
