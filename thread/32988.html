It's used to update any team members that weren't in the vicinity when you got the mission.

Your team no longer has to be in the same playfield for you to get a team mission. But if they're not, they don't get the mission info/key. When you're near them, hit the 'update team members' function, and it copies the mission and key to them.

Allows one person to get the next mission while everyone else is shopping or otherwise not in the area. Or the fixer can grid back and get another one while everyone else waits in the playfield, then update them when he gets back with it.
