There's no nano cost increase on the medic tank armor. The description says it's because it's very light and it's modified to increase the flow of nanobots while equipped.

About the sealed orders: they only drop once in a while as stated before, and there are several types of orders.

QL65 clan suit
QL65 OT suit
QL80 clan suit
QL80 OT suit
QL80ish bow-tie

There may be more, but these are the different sealed orders I've seen/heard about.

Also, apparantly the Bodyguard NPCs drop another type of tank armor called Assault Tank Armor (or similar) with a 75% nano cost increase, high ACs and a 2000 point shield that can be cast once in a while. It's enforcer only. I haven't found this myself so I don't have the exact stats available.
