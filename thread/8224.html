PvP / PK Titles
There is a lot of talk lately about how unbalanced titles are. People complains about some things:

Pkilling omnis in EFP. They do not like to be killed while they are earning xp by people 70 levels higher than them...well, this really sounds PK, they do not expect a attack against them and are not ready for it.

In the other side, PvP'ing people in 25% zones. 2ho, 4holes, newland... people use those places for PvP. People are ready for match, have their better guns and their better buffs.

So my proposal is: make different titles for 0% and 25% kills.

I mean, if you kill someone in a 0% zone, it must be countered as a player killing. If you kill someone in a 25% zone, it must be a PvP kill.
