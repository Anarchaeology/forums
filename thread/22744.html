The professions - A balance chart/scoring system
Ok, first off I'll give some credit for this list and system to DaveDread and another person whos name I missed, that inspired me with their posts. The basic system was layed out it just wasn't set up and put to numbers with a list of all classes. Here is how I think we can figure out what needs to be done to attain balance.

First off we all know some classes are lacking while others go above and beyond. So I when I put these class lists into number form and scored them, i was actually surprised that the numbers
show pretty much what is going on exactally with a couple little surprises.

First off there are 4 categories that you can rank a profession in:

a) Damage Dealing (Nukes, Weapons, Crit buffs, AMS buffs, etc)
b) Damage Prevention (Roots, Snares, Calms, Speed, Evades, and other such things)
c) Damage Taking (Hit Points, HP buffs, AC, AC buffs, Absorb Shields, Damage Shields, etc)
d) Damage Repair (Heals, Team Heals, HoTs)


Now while I'm quite aware I'm not listing every nano line for every class, I intentionally left out the ones with no effect on the list at all, or ones that are so insignifigant they shouldn't
be counted (like a +8-20 dmg per hit buff).

So here we go. Each category is scored out of 2.5 for a total class score of 10. Note that a

BALANCED class will show a score of 5.

NOTE: For pet classes I include the damage capability of their pets in their damage dealing category.

I also excluded the possibility of every class using tweaked out shotties as that isn't a class issue except with classes who are meant to use them, and is a personal choice to raise a dark blue weapon skill.


Agent (4 - Needs something added, not sure if the 14.2 changes are enough)
~~~~~
(2) Damage Dealing - Ranged, AMS buffs, Rifles preferred (avg-high dmg)
(2) Damage Prevention - Roots, Snares, and diverse False Prof buffs
(0) Damage Taking - None
(0) Damage Repair - None


Crat (5 - BALANCED)
~~~~
(2.5) Damage Dealing - Ranged, Crit Buff, Bodyguard Pet, Strong DD Nanos
(2.5) Damage Prevention - Roots, Snares, Calms and Charms
(0) Damage Taking - None
(0) Damage Repair - None


Enforcer (5 - BALANCED)
~~~~~~~
(2.5) Damage Dealing - AMS buff, Challenger, Init Buffs, Rage, High melee dmg
(0) Damage Prevention - None
(2.5) Damage Taking - Hit Points, HP buffs, Mongo Line, Absorb Shields, Damage Shields
(0) Damage Repair - None (note: Mongo not counted as a HoT)

Engineer (3 - Lowest score, needs some SERIOUS work)
~~~~~~
(2) Damage Dealing - Ranged, Robot Pet
(0) Damage Prevention - None
(1) Damage Taking - AC buffs, Damage shields
(0) Damage Repair - None

Fixer (4 - Needs some work, look to the fixer forum to figure out what)
~~~~
(1) Damage Dealing - Ranged, MG/SMG Medium-low damage output
(2.5) Damage Prevention - Grid Armor**, Roots, Snares, Evades, RUn Buffs
(0) Damage Taking - None
(.5) Damage Repair - Weak HoT line

Doctor (5 - BALANCED [After they make heals 50% in PVP that is])
~~~~
(.5) Damage Dealing - Ranged, DoTs
(1) Damage Prevention - Init Debuffs
(1) Damage Taking - Hit Point Buffs
(2.5) Damage Repair - Superior Heals, Teamheals and HoTs

Martial Artist (5 - BALANCED [actually i had them at 5.5 but adjusted])
~~~~~~~~
(2) Damage Dealing - Melee, Crit Buffs, AMD Buffs, Damage type buffs
(1) Damage Prevention - Run Speed, Evades
(.5) Damage Taking - AC Buffs
(1.5) Damage Repair - Healing, Team Heals

Meta Physisist (6 - I refuse to suggest nerfs, just pointing out the facts)
~~~~~~~~~
(2.5) Damage Dealing - Ranged/Melee, Attack Pet, Nano Buffs, DD Nanos
(2) Damage Prevention - Evade Buffs, Mez Pet, Stuns, Nano Debuffs
(0) Damage Taking - None
(1.5) Damage Repair - Heal pet

Soldier (5 - BALANCED)
~~~~
(2.5) Damage Dealing - Ranged, High Dmg, AMS Buffs, Init Buffs
(0) Damage Prevention - None
(2.5) Damage Taking - TMS, AC buffs, Damage Shields
(0) Damage Repair - None

Trader (7 - Surprised?)
~~~~
(2.5) Damage Dealing - Ranged, Shotgun, Very High Dmg, Ransacks->Huge AMS
(2.5) Damage Prevention - Huge Debuffs, Calms, Roots, Snares
(0) Damage Taking - None
(2) Damage Repair - Superior Team Heals

Adventurer (5 - Sorry guys but you're supposed to be jacks of all trades and you are)
~~~~~~~
(1.5) Damage Dealing - Personal Choice, Decent with everyting but not amazing
(1) Damage Prevention - Run Buffs, Pet Morphs
(1) Damage Taking - AC Buffs, Damage Shields
(1.5) Damage Repair - Heals

Nano-Technician (3.5 - For damage over time worse DD than metas, halved nano pool for pvp)
~~~~~~~~~~
(2) Damage Dealing - Ranged, Strong DD Nanos
(1.5) Damage Prevention - Roots, Calms, Stuns, Layered prot
(0) Damage Taking - Nulity Sphere**
(0) Damage Repair - None

** - Nulity Sphere and Grid Armor are not being counted in either case due to EXTREME rarity, when only a few people have these nanos, it is not relevant to balance.


So, an overview?

I'll throw in a suggestion thou, just to complicate things a bit. 8P
Most of these categories can be transferred to other professions and/or be benificial to a team. So, how about a 5th category: Team Benifits?

"Team Damage Dealing"
While it's hard to transfer pure damage, a few professions can improve other professions damage. Crit buffs, AMS buffs, Inits. MA's, Agents and Soldiers should rank pretty high here along with the Crat.

(As an footnote, MA's will be nerfed in this area with 14.2, ranking them lower here, but relatively higher in category a: Damage Dealing.)

"Team Damage Prevention"
Evades are almost always buffed up, and few teams goes into combat without calming/mezzing capability. Crats, Traders, NT's and perhaps MA's would rank pretty well here. Unfortunantly for the Fixers, not many people want a runspeed buff for teaming purposes.

"Team Damage Taking"
This is probably the most widely used category in teams.
Enforcers rank high here with their Essence, Docs with HP buffs. Solders/Advents have their reflect shields as well. Strangely, people tend to forget the Engineers in this category, they have quite massive AC buffs, but I rarely meet engies with a team AC running. Also, if one can get a pet to tank, that should rank quite good. This might be an area where Engies could be improved.

"Team Damage Repair"
What's a team without a doc? Docs, Traders, Adventurers and to a lesser degree Enforcers, MA's, Fixers and Soldiers here. It's either or.


Also, two professions have a very special rating here, MP's and Traders. Both are of immense value to a team since they can make the Doc heal better / MA do more damage / Enforcer get a larger HP buff / NT nuke harder etc.

We also have the "special cases", like Engie, NT, Doc and Fixer teleporting cababilties and the Crats speech line of nanos.

This is where I feel the Fixer is broken. They have no benefits to a team that cannot be given by another profession. Teleporting is rather common, runbuffs not needed much and also given to a lesser degree by Adventurers and MA's, who bring higher damage dealing/repair capabilities to the team.

Whaambulance, Kudos for sorting out my vague ramblings into this coherent piece. It sure makes a lot more sense. 8)
