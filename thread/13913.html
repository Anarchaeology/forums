As to vehicle upgrades... i see something like this...

Engineers being able to fine tune the engine output for better speed or rebuild vehicle for more sitting space (more sitting space = less speed) Lets face it, a carrier should be able to accomodate a whole team at least...

How about buying new engines for your vehicles? Perhaps as much as 5 different engines with different output. Engineers would have to install the engines, and the best engines would require high engineering skills to install. Higher speed would require more skill to use. So, while engine upgrade 1 would be similar to todays ql 30 requirements for a yalmaha, engine upgrade 5 would perhaps be similar to the requirements of a ql 180 today.

Since weapons on civilian vehicles are severely restricted, Fixers could install illegal weapons on vehicles. Installing wepons would severely affect the speed because engine power would be redirected to weapons and because of worse aerodynamics.

Hey, you could even be caught using an unlicenced weapon on board a vehicle. Guards could fire upon you if havent got high enough concealment skill. There could be say 5 different weapons upgrades to buy (restricted to fixer shops or better "the black market"), and each would have a higher concealment requirement to use without being detected. Bigger guns should be more difficult to conceal. The bigger the gun the more engine output would be required. The biggest gun would perhaps require the biggest engine to be efficient. The biggest guns would require more engine output than the total power output of a MK1 engine, and cac therefore not be fitted. An MK3 engine might have the power output to support am MK5 weapon, but it would be SLOOOOOOOW.

This would be FUN!
