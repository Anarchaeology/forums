I agree that we need new pets and that was actually my point Dellagon. I think a nanomage should always be able to reach the skill level required to cast a nano before another race. I would go so far as to propose something like an innate bonus to nano skills based on the level of the character. At level 1 its +1 to all nano skills, at level 200 its +20 to all nano skills. SOmething like that scaled to whatever range seems appropiate without being way too overpowered. At mid level(100ish) a nanomage breed would basically have an innate +10 to all nano skills. This would help to distinguish them but not overpower them. Also a nanomage should have an innnately greater nano recharge delta as an Atrox should have an innately greater health regain delta. These types of changes make a race unique and give them distinguishing abilities associated with the race. Although I feel for a nanomages hp's I don't believe giving them more hp's would be appropiate, that's not a nanomage's strength.

I would love to see more pets and a demon transference. I think pets in general are not scaled properly in comparison to weapons. The pet pathing issues just makes it worse. Not only do demons have a slower attack rate, they don't "dual wield" and the worst thing IMO is they have undoubtably the lowest attack rating of any pet. An MP is suppose to be reliant on their pet, as evidenced by our complete and utter lack of any weapon affinity ( ie. all of our weapon skills are DARK blue). However, our pets even self buffed are the worst of all the pet classes. Our top end pet, should have the attack rating of a level 200 tank wielding a QL200 weapon. Or at least a level 189 character wielding a QL189 weapon. As it stands now cacodaemon feels like a level 100ish tank using a ql 120 weapon =P.

Another annoying thing about pets is their susceptibility to being rooted. I don't mind my pet being rooted but what I do mind is that it is effectively a perma root on them! If our pet is rooted/snared and we are attacked this attack should also affect the pet. So if we are damaged our pet is also considered to be taking damage so they can be unrooted by the attack. This is very consistent with how root/snare and damage is applied to melee classes. This allows people to root our pet in PvP but attacking us would be the same as attacking the pet and as a result the root is broken on the pet if we are damaged. And more importantly to me anyways is that in PvM our pets are no longer rooted for the entire duration of the root, where it seems just after rooting the pet the mob loses aggro on the rooted pet...
