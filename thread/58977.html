aaronb
Supa Leet
Max nano resist and implant it (regardless of color), fight at full defense, have your debuffed heals available on an alternate speedbar. If the fight draws out at all, take advantage of your run buffs and try to draw the trader into friendly territory (guards or what not). Don't fight a trader that already has divest/plunder running if at all possible.

Beating a trader is a matter of speed. You need to get the trader worried about his health and not worried about draining you. If you see the trader start healing (you'll see the damage part of the heal), switch to full offense to get the AMS advantage and try to finish him off.

These are generic tips. I've fought very few adventurers and I haven't been killed by one yet, so I can't really refer you to anyone.
