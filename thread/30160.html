Kudos to the greenies!!!! Great RP!
Just wanted to make a short note sending kudos out to those arks or GMs who are playing some of the green named characters in the clubs around rubi-ka. Over the last couple of weeks i have noticed some great roleplay going on from them, and too many times folks dont post unless they are mad about something.

Ive noticed a few green named chars doing some hardcore rp , Dave "Diamondcut" and this Shades guy were doing some good criminal rp in Reets the other day when i passed through, and i listened in for a bit, was quite interesting from the little i got to hear. Plus there is a Chab Xzique, who just seems to be going insane, talking about death etc, dont know his gig, but hey, it seems like he is really in character with it. I also saw an agent named "Garrisen" in baboons doing some good stuff too. I have also heard of a few others but cant think of the names offhand.

I see these guys more and more around the game world and its nice to see that there are some characters out there promoting roleplay, and doing a good job at it. And its also cool to see reoccuring characters that dont just show up one time, then are gone.

Now if we could just get some more main story characters in there (like the ones in the manual etc and animated series) it would be awesome, but im happy to see this at least.

Good job ark and Gm's! WTG!!!!
