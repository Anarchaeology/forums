I think Lush Fields is the best place to go if you're that lvl... from ~lvl20-60 or so you'll have no problems finding a spot there with endless spawns. Go a bit SW from the outpost at lvl20-40 and then head up to NW of outpost.

Rhino Cockpit was as I recall good at that level for a team, might be a bit tough for only 2 though. There are one or two oasis about in the desert, so you should be able to find something. Failing that, grid over to Harry's and visit some of the popular spots in Lush, just beware of omni guards...

Other possible spots might include near West Athens or Rising Sun.

At that sort of level, you will start to have to learn to avoid nastier critters and mobs that aggro or are social. Hunting is at its best in this game from 30-50, so you may need to level a bit more yet..

EDIT: Rhino Cockpit has a save point btw, and usually has folk looking to join teams...
