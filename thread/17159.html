Init bugged - Proof
Here is a copy of the e-mail i sent nizno the test server gm to give the devs about the init problems fists have. hopefully we can get some response/action on this now:


Through a series of events today on the test server i believe i have undeniable proof as to the bugginess of phys init with fists. More specifically, i should say that phys init is not bugged, it does not work period. In fact i have discovered that fist speed is solely based on MELEE INIT rather than phys as it should be. This is how i came upon this revelation :

I had found a ql 142 extreme low-light scope that i intended to equip for the +11 crit chance. Once i received the maestro buffs to fit on the scope, i proceeded to test my crit rate. After a few tries, i began to notice that i was hitting slower with my fists now, eventhough my aggro bar was at full aggresive. This certainly couldn't have been the case, since i had 924 phys init before i equipped the scope which deducts 565 from phys, melee, and ranged init, leaving me with 359 phys init still remaining. Sure enough though, i was punching at about the rate i usually see from setting my aggro bar to 1/2. This made me think back to the guesses among the MA community about fist speed being based on melee init rather than phys. It would certainly make sense in this scenario, with my melee init now at around -300 (i didnt record the actual number). So being in the unique position of having tons of extra IP still at lvl 190 i decided to max my melee init skill, which put it at 726 total, or 161 with the scope equipped. Sure enough, when i tested now with the scope penalty i was punching at full speed once again at full aggressive. I then took off the scope and thought to myself "if the 600 init = -1/-2 sec theory of init usage is true, then with 726 melee init i should be able to set my aggro bar to 1/2 and see no slow down of fists, which i always expected with 600+ phys init but never saw." And what happened? With 1/2 aggressive i saw no difference between the speed of that setting and full aggressive.

All of these facts together surely must convince FC that the init assigned to govern fist speed is erroneous. To me it seems fairly easy to fix - simply switch the melee to phys. This should make right in practice was was intended in design.
