Lets say for the sake of argument for a moment that the ability to sneak through a mission for cash wasn't the issue. I know everyone thinks thats it. But if Funcom is at all truthful in their statements that credits are "not valuable" at higher levels, I'm willing to give a slight benefit of the doubt. Ok then what was the exploit then.

As someone else posted the possiblity that at higher levels if you set the slider to max exp, then at say level 40 or so you are able to get 20k EXPERIENCE through sneaking through a hard mission, maybe that is the exploit that they are trying to fix. Ok take that assumption and work through the possible solutions that have been identified and implemented todate for what are considered EXPERIENCE exploits.

Killing fish in water.. no exp if they can't fight back
Killing guards that never fought back.. no exp for killing guards.
Killing melee creatures from a "perched" position.. no exp for a creature that can't reach you.

Ok now apply the same reasoning to the mission issue and wouldn't it make a whole lot more sense to reduce the EXPERIENCE reward for those missions that have the possiblity to be solved with high level concealment, rather than reducing the effectiveness of a primary class skill?

If concealment was left alone, then there would be no significant EXPERIENCE to be gained by sneaking, thus the only benefit of sneaking would be the credits which we already have been told is not really important. Adventurers, Agents, and Fixers have a method of making some cash to support the ever increasing cost of armor, weapons, etc while making the EXPERIENCE gathering issues more in line with what is expected.

Just seems like a more reasonable approach to the solution than what they deemed appropriate. I would prefer this type of approach to possible exploit solutions, then any type of character related reduction in skills, abilities etc.
