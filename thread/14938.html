Here's why.
I thought that post would speak for itself, but seems like some explanation is in order.

It get's old. Todays high level "hunting" is this: hanging around in Home doing "/s lev 1xx <profession>" untill you find a team. Then go to the right door and do the same mission with the same mobs over and over again till you reach level 200 or cancel out of boredom. No new scenery in the missions, only human mobs, and the same routine every time. Never going to any other zones, never travel outside. All this because for some reason the only city we are allowed to do missions in are in Home (why we even are allowed to do that is beyond me, the way I see it getting to the mission should always be part of the challenge at high level).

If these team missions are as good as FC tells us they will be, a team won't have to go from team mission to team mission, because it will take quite a while just to finish one. Then it shouldn't be a problem if the mission is half a zone away from the nearest grid terminal or wompah. I find doing missions in Home WAY to "easy", and incredibly boring. But it's the only place to level at 100+, simply because it's the easiest way. Why go to Southern Foul Plains to do a mission when you can do a mission in Home, where it's easy to get to and you can save outside the door. Most players will always take the easy way out, and thus it's up to FC to make the game more challenging.



I'll throw in a suggestion while I'm at it:

Unless they plan on making some hunting grounds that's actually worth using they should deal with things the way they are now. When team missions is implemented they should make a special zone where they place a few team mission booths. One zone in the biggest clan city, and one in the biggest omni city. Neutrals don't get their own but can use both sides, simply because if there where a neutral team mission booth both sides would end up using that one and it would cause more lag.
Now, why am I suggesting this. Well, simply to make it easier to do the boring part of the mission, that is finding a team. By just making one clan team mission booth, every clan person who's looking for to do team missions will go there, and thus it should be easy to put together a team if you don't know anyone you team with at a regular basis. Players need a gathering point, somewhere to hook up with potential team mates. At the moment this is Home for higher level players.
Also, by making it a zone it allowes for FC to very easily make sure the lag is at a minimum. If it's a hit and alot of people start hanging out there, they can deal with a lag problem much easier then in a big zone like Broken Shores.
And last, it would prevent the constant LD and sync problems that people doing missions in Home experience at a daily basis. Or at least it would limit the problems to the team seeking/mission finding prosess, and make the actuall getting to and doing the missions less laggy. And that would be a step in the right direction.
