Introduction:

Rubi-Ka is not a friendly place as many know.
Especially with all the Clan trouble, the Omni stronghold of the planet
and now the ICC poking their heads in..
But sometimes even the smallest things that may seem insignificant to most, matters a lot to a few.
Like what just recently happen...


Prologue:

Maul, a lovely gal, just revcently became Miss Omni and almost become Miss Rubi-Ka as well.
Life was good, no worries, no trouble with bills.
And with the ICC in Tir she was actually able to check out the clubs here now, even cooler...

I mean, who doesn't love to go clubbing, right?

It was getting quite dark, and in the distance she could see small signs of a storm coming. "Hope it's not a big one!"
Pulling her clothes tighter she moved away from the Happy Rebel door and headed towards Enjoy it While it lasts club.
"It better be open, I'd hate to be out here in the storm..."

It was a pretty busy day, people rushing all over.
Maybe some skirmish battle was going on somewhere again,
she didn't care. She was tired of all the constant fighting going on
all over Rubi-Ka.

Suddenly headed straight at her was a huge pack of wolfs,
barely able to throw herself out of the way before they sprinted right thru the air where she had stood 2 seconds earlier.

"Hey watch it will'ya you damn advents" she shouted at them.

"Sorry lady, we're in a hurry. Tarasque has been spotted again at Camelot" One of the adventurers yelled back as they headed for the grid terminal.

Maul sighs, "Wish they'd leave poor Tara alone..."

What Maul didn't notice was she had managed to get lost in the Tir backstreets due to this distraction.
Tir is large but not too large, but the streets are narrow and oddly
layed out.

From one of the rooftops a pair of narrow eyes studied her every move, calculating the strength and weaknesses of it's prey.
A small grin appeared as the shadow leaped into the dark alley.

"Damn! Where the heck is the alley back out to the main street!" Miss Maul said to herself.
Suddenly she heared a sound and turned around, but nobody was there...
Not noticing she was walking backward towards a door,
she bumped into it, causing it to swing open.
And she fell tumbling backwards into the room.

Sprawled out on the floor she look up and and twist her head backwards to get a look around in the room.
She wished then she hadn't, and got herself up and out the door
faster than she tought possible,
because what she had seen was not something she really would want to see any more of at all...

With the haunting image of two atroxes 'getting it on' she rushed
thru the narrow alleys,
and just as she rounded a corner. bam.
Everything turns white.
"wha. what? Dammit, rez shock? What the hecks going on, I didn't hear anything shooting at all!" she tought...
Great, damn clan scums, some punk is prolly looting my body right now. "That's odd, rez shock never takes this long" she tought.
Then suddenly the white starts to fade, but rather than seeing a reclaim terminal infront of her she sees nothing. nothing but growing darkness. "Noo, nooo. dammit. I insured dammit. No! Great maker noooooooooooo!" her mind screams right before
the nothingness draw her in...


Kat pulled her Kryst knife slowly out of her prey's spine,
the body dropping into a pile on the tiled pavement.
Blood coloring the cracks dark red.
She carefully whipes off the knife, then rumage thru the pockets
of the dead body.
"Ah! Lookie here, a rich one. These credits will come in handy. And nice expensive stuff in the backpack as well!" She said rather loudly!

She was carefull to cover any traces she had left.
Kicking the dead body she mumbled "That oughta teach ya to trust insurance!" As she grinned and leapt up unto some trashcans then vanished among the rooftops.

Somewhere down the alley two atroxes came walking,
hand in hand. "Holy nano!" one screamed as he saw the dead body on the ground. "I'll go get one of the ICC guards, just hope they can help, is she still alive?" the other atrox yelled as he headed towards the mainstreet.
