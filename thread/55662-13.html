I very much oppose this change on principle. You (funcom) stated/implied that LLTS would not be changed/removed, and now you are reversing that decision. While I understand that in MMOGs its a living world, and that as a situation changes, RARELY previous statements must be contradicted, for the good of the game. I dont believe this is one of those situations, though, and once again, you risk alienating your longest (thus best in terms of cash flow) term customers.

LLTS by their nature, have negatives associated with the benefits they provide. Negative Inits have a pretty significant effect on attack speed and agg/def settings as it is. This will become even more apparent, if the changes to Inits discussed in other forums are implemented. Namely that buffs/implants/item mods/trickledown affect recharge in addition to attack speed. Before you seriously consider nerfing LLTS, please implement the bug fix for inits, as it will have significant impact on the viability of LLTS vs. VEs.

All of that said, if a nerf is coming, at least provide value for value. We all know that players, especially high levels, place a very large value on crits. There are many reasons for this, in PVM and PVP, but if you are going to remove a portion of the critical chance from LLTS, at a bare minimum give us other things that we value in this item. What do people 125 or 150+ value? All Off/Def is good. Max HP is good (though this doesnt follow the theme of an LLTS). Stat mods are ok. Distinguishing visuals are ok (may not be possible with LLTS). Plus all damage types is ok (has to be significant, +10 dmg is pointless at this level). Evades are good. Reflect is good. Mods to special attacks are ok for some, good for others. Exp bonus is very nice for some, useless for others. When it comes down to it, you cant keep everyone happy. But, if you at least put some reasonable benefits on the LLTS to make it more valuable than a similar % VE, then folks will probably deal with it and move on.

Would I trade a:
15% ELLTS with ~neg800 mods

for

8% ELLTS with ~neg600 mods
+120 all Off
+120 all Def
+5 to all stats

Id probably be ok with a change like that. Would everyone? No, but at its better than just getting 300m creds and no scope, heh. Adding to All Off would help to offset the loss of damage from crits. Adding All Def would help me survive a bit more, to inflict more damage. A few points in stats trickles down all over the place, and is marginally helpful all around.

Basically it comes down to thisgive us value for value. Just remember, people who use high QL LLTS value crits VERY highly, or we would have just blitzed a ql240-250 VE, and traded our scopes for some other nice item, heh.
