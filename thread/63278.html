Tarasque is UNACCEPTABLE (revisited)
Ok, so maybe Funcom fixed the health level of Tarasque to be reasonable, so that it doesn't take 2 hours to kill the dragon. Now there are other issues...

Imagine this scenario: A group of players from one Faction get into a playfield where Tarasque is supposed to be spawning in a number of hours. Small teams are formed and engineers begin to warp in players from the other side of Rubi-Ka. Soon the playfield is filled capacity, and then overfilled for good measure. There is also the issue of filling up a playfield entirely through player walk-ins.

Either way it achieves the exact same thing... It doesn't allow other players access to the playfield and forces them into other playfields that may not spawn for hours or may have been killed hours ago.

The playfield is intended to be a mass PvP field and filling with same-side players defeats it's purpose.

I would make the following suggestions:

a) Disable warping into the playfield. It's simple but it doesn't solve the entire problem.

b) Limit players per Faction in addition to players total, with nuetrals not counting against Faction limits. This will allow mass PvP in the zone to function closer to what was intended. Clan and Omni players will be limited to around 30 players each in each playfield.

c) Playfields cleared of LinkDead or Logged out players automaticly. This is to combat the exploit of using /quit to avoid dying, and also to keep an accurate Faction-Limit count.

d) Forced respawn of all mobs in an empty play field, with reduced chances of rare loot.

This should make PvP within the Camelot dungeon zone(s) more fair and entertaining. No longer will Camelot PvP consist of filling a playfield and going AFK until the loot is raffled off.

It is clear that Funcom cannot leave fairness in the hands it's players.
