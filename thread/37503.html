A tragic end, to a tragic tale.

Say what they will, be it "Oh god not this again", or "Someone get these stupid clanners out of here", or some similar dribble... the presence of the Sinister Anarchists in the Omin-tek lands upset the standard day-to-day perfectionism and facism that the place is full of (the Omni-Pol and Omni-AF clones, for example), and added a degree of opposition unlike the normal clan-omni rivalry.... something similar, and yet totally different.

We all know how it ended... with his first ban.

It started to crank up once more after he got his account back and his character wiped, but after getting up to level 100 in about 10 days (...of which I can vouch for the legitimacy of... "Hey! Let's go level!" was something that I heard every time I logged on. -.-' ), and then he got banned AGAIN for telling people how he got banned the first time. Intelligent move on FC's part, ehh?

Well, he rebounded from that and tried yet again... making the Cult of Yxxil (known as the "Followers of Yxxil", precisely), and working with various event actors towards resurrecting the Demon.

...Then, FC stepped in again, and thrust their balls into his face. They came up with their OWN little cultists, the "Shovites" (which is actually a direct rip-off of Oshovah, who was around when Yxxil was first summoned many months ago), and talked completely backwards from what he did. Now who do you think the sheep-like populace is going to believe, as far as story... something that a normal player says, or something that one of these green-named "event actors" say? You have to admit that they had some serious nuts to screw with this one. That was totally unlejit.

Not only did they do that, but they "turned" Dave "Diamondcut" Duster into some sort of messiah, or "being of light", or something.. which incited so much b*tching that they canned the ENTIRE storyline, and ordered the Event Actors to call off everything that had anything to do with it. Dave's excuse? "Oh, I was drunk..." ....Right.
