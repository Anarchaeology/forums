* NCU hacker interface
- easy to build at lower levels (use a lockpick on a nanoprogramming interface tool -- you can buy both at a standars tools booth)
- HUD2 slot
- leveling item -- rightclick to upgrade to your own level
- adds much needed nanoskills, this item is a must.

* Dissasembly clinic
- guide for building it at anarchy arcanum
- goes in UTIL2 slot
- adds up to 20 treatment

Still use this for installing implants, but it's main function is for stripping implants (removing clusters). I don't wear it anymore, tho (see further down)

* Pick-a-finger
- available from the wrecked shop
- right finger slot (wearable with GA)
- adds 16 to b&e and trap disarm
* ICC Internops coat
- available from the wrecked shop
- back slot
- adds 8 to b&e

With these two items I can achieve enough b&e to clean 200 implants without swapping any implants. Until I got enough B&E to manage with only these two, getting "crowbar subtlety" from an engineer would also let me go into "cleaner" mode without swapping implants.

Two more items which help B&E (don't need these now, but used them earlier):
* Sugarfree rum - pirate
- available from the trader shops
- easy to wield/dualwield
- adds points to B&E
* Manex STR 77 Neverland (ql147 only)
- blitzable
- good if you already put points in Assault Rifle
- adds 22 to trapdisarm and 26 to B&E

* Ebony figurine
- available as loot from mercenary camp in EFP (or buy/get from another player)
- goes in UTIL2 slot (instead of the dissasembly clinic)

* Personal Lockpick
- combine hologram camera with ID extractor, then combine the result with a regular lockpick
- NODROP
- this is vital for blitzing, as the loss of your lockpick (drop on ground, stick in wall) will ruin the run

* Droid Control Device
- follow the link Argulace posted
- This item would perhaps be more "usable" to a non-GA fixer than to a GA-fixer, as you cannot wear it with grid armor. It adds very good bonuses to the PM and SI nanoskills.
- never found a use for this myself, unfortunately, up until recently. The psychology boost is great for placing towers.

No links, but...

First Aid kit: Because you just let fly with a burst from your Manex, and the doc hasn't noticed that the mob now wants to kill you instead of the Enforcer in your team. Given that we all like to bite off more than we can safely chew in team missions, and dual HoTs aren't usually fast enough in this case...

Feel free to ignore if you have Grid Armor.

Cluster bullets: available at the Fixer store. Guaranteed to anger even the most placid Traders, NTs, and Bureaucrats. Usually good at angering mobs, too. It's more insulting to be hit for 77 chemical damage by a Fixer than it is to have a Library of Foul Language used on you.

Avangeline Terrigena Web Cloak, Intelligent Kevlar Shoulderpad: Every bit of Ranged Initiative helps. The web cloak also helps you show off the CDS armor you spent hours camping Cyborgs to get.

Feel free to ignore if you have Grid Armor.

Advanced Hacker Tool: Available nearly everywhere, because Rubi-kan society is one of thieves and murderers. Use it to hack your Mausser, open sealed weapons containers, envelopes, and other packages. Sometimes, you'll even get a tip when someone asks you to use one on some item!
