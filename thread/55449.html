Leveling...
Although I wouldnt want to get in your way of choosing a role playing guild, as I think its a great idea. You can level, albiet, slowly, buy picking locks and 'pick up' object based missions. This is best done by an agent. You can blitz missions, best done by a fixer. You can mez missions, again picking locks along the way for experience. All in all, if you want to go fast through your levels, you need to kill things. Not knowing what your RP background is for your character, I cant tell you the best way of going about this, but the above are non killing options.

Dont get me wrong, I enjoy role playing. But I also enjoy, getting the next level so I can get the next gun/armor/nano/thingy majig. More power to you if you can manage to pull off the non combat leveling scheme...
