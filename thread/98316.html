OOC: The story is out there, it just needs to be found.
Ok ok, I have been as hissed off as the next person over the lack of pretty much everything other than pvp in this game in the last...urmm, well...along time.

Along time back, I posted a thread about an NPC from Omni-Trade who gave out story info on the build up to Shadowlands...and later on she got sacked (yeah she was really depressed but it was kind amusing to see FC even sack the NPC's as she sulked around)....anyhow, shes back, as are many more scattered all around the planet and giving bits of the story/s out there. And heres some of what's been discovered so far...

Firstly theres the biggy, Ross being booted from power and Eva Pourais (theres a few NPCs with info on this)...she seems to have taken power by taking control of the department heads and putting in the most ruthless there is...some even believe these might actually be clones but dunno on that one. Ross is still around but it seems he's only a puppet wheeled out to wave to the crowds when needed and is expected to have an "accident" pretty soon.

Next, and also with Omni is during all this power changes it seems to have really split up OT and Omni-Pol and Omni-AF are now at war with each other to see who can take power. It seems to be (not totally sure as lets face it, NPCs are iffy at the best) that Pol is backing Eva and AF is after power for themselves.

On the subject of Ross there also a supposed hidden room in the grid where theres an NPC with pics of Ross naked and stuff on Ross and Radiman, not sure if this is a dev joke but there is some kind of npc inside the fixer grid I've heard about (cant get too unfortunatly) who unfortuantly doesn't yet talk...shame, could be a good laugh or even better info.

On the Clans there is of course Silverstone, who to be honest other than generally hating the ICC is more into towers than anything else (kind of a boring guy to be honest). Fisk is just a neut hater....then again am surprised he knows what one is considering he/it/shes a trox and is probably even more easily confused when he/she/it has to decide which toilets to use :P

Theres also all that Warr and placement of Omni sided supposed neutral NPCs who the majority of neuts never wanted to ever see kicking around in Newlands...not too sure how this is going but with Eva probably behind it, its gotta be touble.

There is also a thing about a ring of traders, and some big trading empire....I currently have little info on this other than Lord Galahad before his name change was a huge player on it, and is some kind of trade genius...one of the NPC's involved in this tells of another NPC but still looking for them (doesnt seem to be around).

As for the NPC who started all this? well she's back again, back in a job, and back giving some news about Shadowlands, although not quite as much as she used too...either way worth a looksee

Well, thats kinda the most of the officially type story things ingame at the moment if you look for them. The big one, and the one I've been going crazy trying to dig up info on and so far failing dismally is Radiman and the Pilgrims...can but keep on searching though
And good luck to anyone else who wants to go search for the AO story/s as well they are there, just they need to be found and if you find anything on Radiman, would be great if you let me know
