Department leader found alive and rescued
Press Release from Mayhem
From the desk of general Morphex

In a successful search and rescue operation today, the president of Mayhem, Dociceman, was found alive and rescued.

The operation started at 18:00 CEST, and hundreds of Omni-Tek employees participated. Some in the field searching for Dociceman, others in Omni 1 putting together the first aid kit for him.

After an hours search, Dociceman was found by the guild The Blackhand. He was badly hurt just like expected, but they quickly brought him a new chest implant to replace his old damaged one, and the emergency treatment kit he needed. He was soon at normal health, and gridded back to Omni 1.

In a short ceremony, Nosson accepted the reward of 10 millions on behalf of his guild. Mayhem also gave out special thanks to Wildwal, Muencha of Spydervenom of The Blackhand who especially contributed.

Todays event really showed how Omni-Tek employees stand up for each other despite of internal conflicts. A big thank you goes out to all the persons and guilds that participated in saving Dociceman.
