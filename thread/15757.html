Envisioned for a high level game
Just some thoughts.

I envision fighting different mobs at lvl 150-200 than i do at level 100.
Real Means and Aces don't plug into Risk vs Reward as they are 4X the risk for 1.25 times the reward. No one i know intentionally hunts aces. Instead you see lvl 150+ hunting exactly the same thing as level 100's, just hunting them faster. Killing 2 Vets shouldn't be 4 times as fast as a Real mean and 1.7 times the xp. That's just ridiculous.

I envision finding an alternative to missions that doesn't get nerfed.
Medusas weren't so popular because they were easy - but rather because they where the only alternative to BS missions. They were seen as too easy though and nerfed - thus Meds is now a wasteland.
FC take note pls: If players have only one alternative to boring BS missions, there is going to be 100's flooding to that area - it doesn't mean that that area is easy - just exciting. The cycle of monitoring busy high level areas and nerfing them on general principle is not based on informed decisions.
How can the Nerf at Medusas be justified? There is no Risk vs Reward there - just endless death.

Anyway, when we say there is no high-level game, what we the players mean is that there are no alternatives to doing the same thing we did at level 80+. You cant throw a mob into the game 6 times as tuff and assign it 1.5 times the xp. That isn't really an option now is it? IMHO is we kill something 6X as difficult, we should be getting at least 3 times the xp, maybe more. FYI even making Real Means worth 2X the xp of a vet isn't good enough.

Yes it is nice to see more static content. But in my experience static content isn't exactly an XP area is it? Nobody used the Steps of Madness as a hunting area - the mobs are about 8 times as difficult as comparible mobs for their level. Lets start putting some new hunting grounds into the game pls. Or maybe just un-nerf the old hunting grounds..
