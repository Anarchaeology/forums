The silver lining of farming
Okay, some assumptions here, but there is a silver lining to this farming situation:

More points entering the PVP pool.

Now, as the virtual capitalists that we are, how do we capitalize on these new points?

Well, we have to gank/grief. And hope to get opportunities. That's what the free market is all about.

I'm basing this forecast on the fact that 1) in the past it has been hard to visualize a power base for the higher pvp ranks, and 2) the farmers will want to show off their title characters from time to time.

Now, we can either work to reduce the points in the system or we can work to reap what the farmers are sowing.

All hail the reapers!
