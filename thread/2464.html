Right now there are very few ways to balance weapons out without making them identical. The key is to make more weapons with various types of damage or abilities. For instance - if the low damage stun glove actually stunned your opponent for a few seconds - it might actually be worth something! Or if the glue gun actually GLUED your opponent down, it might actually be worth something! Or, if the staves/staffs increased your nano skills, it might actually be worh something! OR, if you could make ultra fast weapons (like .25 attack / recharge that actually worked) - even though they might do low damage - it might actually be worth something!

As it is now, you want the highest damage with weapons around a 2.50 attack and recharge time at most. Beams being the exception. The weapons have to have high max damage - because otherwise at higher levels, they will only do minimum damage to mobs. As heavy weapons - the flashpoint and steiner rifles are worthless because their damage is too small for their recharge time in PvM. It's supposed to be a heavy armor damaging weapon - but a enforcer beam which recharges and hits faster does far more damage. The nova flow which has low attack and recharge rates is also a far better weapon, the damage over time it does is more then significantly higher. If these 'heavy weapons' are to work - their stats also need to be significantly raised. I can understand pvp concerns - but that really can be dealt with at a later time - these weapons need stats and/or abilities that justify their name/intended purpose.
