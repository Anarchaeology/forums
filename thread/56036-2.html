Supa Leet
Even though I agree with you completely (AO combat is MISERABLE at best), it wasn't supposed to be the focus of this game. The point of a MMORPG (at least in its current inceptions) is player interaction. They want as many players as possible, and don't want tried-and-true FPers who've played everything from Quake on absolutely OWNING the regulars. By making combat relatively equal across gaming-skill levels, it gives even the n00bs and those not particularly interested in combat a chance to compete.

Don't get me wrong, I would LOVE to see FC stick the Quake engine in this game - I would probably double my gameplay time and be a lot less anxious for SWG to come out.

But what about looking at it from a network efficiency perspective? The sheer volume of packets that need to be sent and received in AO already lags it to death, and if you top that off with all of the positional and physics data required to be sent to and from client and server, as is the case with FPSs, you would cut down on the number of people able to simultaneously participate. TFC and Half-life (and, more recently, Jedi Outcast) can only handle 32 people max per dedicated T1 server; anything above that results in terrible lag. If you've played TFC as a sniper in one of those 32-man Well or 2Fort brawls, you know what I'm talking about.

I think that, unfortunately, this aspect of the game will have to accepted as eternal. If there's an AO2, though, I'm sure engine redesign will be top-3 on their list of improvements.

Optimization is the answer
To answer your question about why frame rates suck on your more than capable computer, but are uber in other games?

Opimization. UT and Quake have hired the best, the brightest, and the most expensive designers to pack the most, the quickest into the least amount of space.

Some of the optimizations that are done include a massively reduced colour palette, compressed textures handled by video cards, and reducing the number of actual objects. We have a whole suitcase of tricks that can be done.

In any game there is a trade off that has to be done. AO needs to include as many people as possible to keep it playing longest. UT doesn't really need to do that because once you have bought the box, they have made all the money they are going to make. So, they can afford to require you to have uber computers to make them work. AO cant afford to take advantage of the latest and greatest because of the number of people out there with old computers who are still playing.

Another answer is network. turn on FPS and run though a city. You will notice your fps die whenever you get to a large population, and bounce back when you get alone. Then try switching areas. FPS is partially dependant on the amount of information flowing to your machine. Under the present network topology, I believe the only solution would be for FC to put fast proxie servers in major population centers. Anyone got got some cash to spare? can we test it in my city first?

FPS also suxxor because the playing community is demanding content rather than FPS. That is a major point for me in this post.

I believe the drive for content is misplaced. This is just a game, and people grow bored of all games. What happens though is that you have worked on a character for months, or years, and the boredom sets in. So, the average player starts looking for more things to do. What they should be saying is, "hey, there's a world out there, why dont I go out on a date, watch a movie, spend some time with friends instead of playing AO all the time." Then you could return to AO and want to play again. I have seen too many people who whip through the levels, and then say they are bored and the game suxxor. Its all a matter of balance. Its not that the game suxxor, its that people are obsessive and demanding too much from it. AO is not a reality replacement, just a nice excape.


Balance of real world, and game play. Balance of engine / FPS /graphics improvement VS content. So next time you light the match to flame, or make a suggestion, I would request that you consider asking for a bug fix rather than content. Or ask that you can jump between platforms, have crates you can smash open instead of lockpick, or real dodging damage.
