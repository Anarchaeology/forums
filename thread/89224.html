LFG and LAG
Lag is going to be a problem wherever FC put mission terminals and is the current place 'in spot' to get a team together. The problem is not with terminal placement, but with the whole mechanics of how people go about getting a team.
I would suggest having lots of Team Terminals where you could register yourself as LFG and could view a list of others currently LFG and be able to invite people into teams that have registered on the terminal by clicking on their names on the LFG list. This would completely remove the problem of having to be in the same location to be invited into a team, would save all that /s LFG chat spam, and would also stop you having to TAB through all the people in the area to find that person LFG to invite them.
Also this would show to the person being invited what the lvls and profs are of the people inviting them to team with.
Whenever you accept a team, or logged out (or LD'd) you could be automatically removed from the LFG list.
