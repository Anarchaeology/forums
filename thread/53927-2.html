To add a little insight to this discussion:

I went the vektor/HD route for most of my trader career, never touched an ith of a Krutt, even back in the UVC days. Then when KIWDs came out, I tried them, they were decent but not as good as an HD. Then i tried the light suppressor with a 10% scope firing at close to 1/1, which was also decent (conflicting imps were trouble though). Then I got my hands on a 13% scope and tried the ith, and sweet jesus I will never switch to anything else. I mean I easily outdamage anyone but a tl6 trader in team missions, no contest, unless I go on a freak no-crit streak. With MoP running (which I always grab at tir grid before I head out), I crit probably 2/5 shots, with fling and AS thrown in for fun. You truly realize the power of an ith when you open with 17k+ dmg in 3 shots.
The bottom line is simply that shotguns give the greatest dmg of any line of guns if a trader uses them with the right tools, and for most, that's all that matters. You can play around with other guns, but nothing works better. Does that make them the most fun? In a sense, yes, because the only thing that even approaches fun in missions (at least past 120-130) is watching the dmg you do on mobs. Does that make them right for eveyone? Of course not. I'd like to see more variety out there too, but frankly I'd rather see traders get their nanos fixed and the profession re-evaluated before we're thrown a few new shotguns as tripe.
