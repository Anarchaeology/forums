My attempt to bring holiday cheer to the Clanners
I decided that since this is the time of year for joy and stuff, that I should attempt to bring some happiness to the the miserable Clanners. So, I visited my local general store to buy gifts for them and stocked up on Omni applications to give out (more on that in a moment).

I arrived safely in Old Athen in my Omni-Elf suit since the guards were drunk and passed out (big surprise there!) and first met up with an Atrox named Demonwraith. We got off to a bad start since he was not interested in holiday cheer and failed to recognize his superior. Since he didn't seem interested in what I had brought for gifts, I granted his wish as best as I could (it IS the season for giving!) and gave him Titan.

After Demonwraith scampered off to do whatever it is that Atrox do (I imagine it included pie), the other fellow in the picture, Migeira, struck up a conversation with me. I tried to offer him one of my presents, but to my dismay, the applications are nodrop! Aaargh! My plan was foiled! Well, Migeira thought this was quite funny and, after I looked forlorned, offered his condolences and we had a nice conversation about Clanners, Omni-Tek, notum, and how I would make a great Clanner (ha ha!).

So, while my mission to give the gift of Omni-Tek was a bust, I did meet one Clanner who I did not feel needed to be killed.

Happy Holidays!
