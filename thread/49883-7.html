For reference: I define fast as <1.5s, mid as 1.5s-3s, and slow as > 3s.

Let's go over this.

Any weapon with fast recharge is going to cap recharge so quickly it hurts. Attack will be the limiting factor on 1/1. The only weapons affected here are ones where people are used to scoping the hell out of them because they could ignore the debuff on recharge.

A mid/mid weapon isn't a good candidate for scoping, but can be capped in high levels. (CHS, native staffs, Rider executioner, Rider Warblade) Again, attack is the guiding factor for 1/1 operation. Unaffected, except maybe making it a bit better for lower levels.

A slow/mid weapon again has attack as the limiting factor (support beam, Supernova*hah*,). Isn't hurt by this, but not helped much either.

A mid/slow weapon. Here's where the benefit is most felt. Weapons like the True Katana, Sledgehammer suddenly become a lot more viable when the recharge is fully affected. Taking a True Katana to 1/1 at full agg would still require something like 1800 init, but getting it down to 1/4 is pretty doable.

A slow/slow weapon... heh. Well, these are pretty rare. Manex Catastrophe, maybe. Helped a tiny bit, but not much.

A slow/REALLY slow weapon like the Queen Blade sees some bonuses here. Makes recharge reduction easier.

As to Shub's Raid pistols, they're still pretty good. You need a modified RI of about 200 to cap at full agg, or a modified RI of 500-600ish at full def. Try using a VE instead of a LLTS if you want to sit at low aggdef. PvP, due to high(er than most mobs) player AC, crits are still the way to go. PvM though this can lead to overall better damage over time unscoped or scoped only so that you can keep 1/1.

Hmm, lets check a couple of weapons. Versus 2500AC (average mobs), 900 ini unscoped, all scope choices available with -50ini per 1% crit, aggdef 87.5% giving values listed, and 0% giving +1.75/+1.75, 100% -0.25/0.25. (Not tested personally aggdef yet, but that is one of the value sets I've read.) AR 900. +50 dam buffs.

Queen blade: 1500ish avg dam, 180ish DPS (all these DPS values are noncrit, nonreload DPS) unscoped full agg and 120ish full def, 3250ish crit. Crit is about double avg dam hit, so each crit% gives same % over time damage increase. -800 ini from 15% scope drops DPS (without crits included) fulldef to 90ish and fullagg to 120ish. Note how the scope increase doesn't get anywhere near to being enough to pay for the -ini _AND_ the skills required to use that scope. It actually hurts to raise those skills to equip the scope. Sort of like multi-wield with some weapon choices.

X-3: 570ish avg dam, 280ish DPS unscoped full agg and 150ish unscoped fulldef. Crit bit under 3k, compared to the bit under 600ish avg dam normal hit. About 5 times as high crits as normal avg hits. So about +4% per each crit% over time damage. Scoped to 100ini 70ish DPS fulldef, 150ish fullagg. 70ish +60%, not enough. 150+60%, not enough. Again, scoping high would hurt. Scoping down to 690ini at fullagg would keep you at 1/1, that's about 4% crit enhancer. Full def, use TIM.

My fav, Gluegun : 370ish avg dam, DPS 180ish full agg, 150ish full def. Crit about 1700ish, so 3.5ish% bonus per crit% to DOT. Lol, -800 ini full def 60ish DPS, full agg 130ish. On full agg, there's actually a bonus from the scope, but who would use a gluegun if going fullagg? :P

Ithaca: 200ish avg dam, DPS 100ish full agg 90ish full def. Crit about 2800ish. +13% DOT per crit%. -800 fulldef DPS 35ish (*smirk*) and same 100ish full agg. A whopping +195% from 15% scope, giving a 300ish full agg DPS. Still gonna see scoped ithacaists around, just that they'll be on full agg.

PvP with higher ACs will skew figures to promote the high hitters: crit% scoped or very slow weapons will be comparably better. Also note that these computations are only spesific to the circumstances taken for the basis of the comparison. Do note the lack of outside crit buffs or "natural" crit chance, which will both diminish the value of scoping (which for most of these weapons won't exist after ini fix).
