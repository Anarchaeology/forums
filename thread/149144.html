DBS (Daluz Banking Service) will open up again under the same name but the the service has been change a bit.

I think some people have had the wrong opinon what DBS is really about..

I dident start DBS just to give out cash... I started it because I wanted to help out new players with small donations so they could buy those nanos they needed, imps or weapons. BUT that was just a smal part of what DBS was really about, for the most part it was all about answering questions, helping with implant setups, what profession they could choose or where they could find certain places or items, these was just some of the questions anyone could get help with.

I feel these questions are so much easier to explain in-game and to understand so I am back where ever you like it or not!


SO WELCOME TO RUBI KA!!!

**Daluz Banking Service (DBS) **OPEN**

*Donations L1-20 (2k per Level, so a L10 would get 20k)
