This is just incorrect thinking Nal, and a large mistake the game designers are currently making.

High level content needs to be the top priority, and for several reasons.

1) It is precisely at high lvls that you need additional challenges, since you are no longer acquiring new functionalities (new nanos for example), and you have long since mastered the basic challenges -- i.e. missioning, teaming, hunting etc.

2) Because of the guild system, you want to insure high lvl stability, and not have continual exodus of the most experienced members of the community. Also, there is something to be said for rewarding the commitment and investment of time that these players have made.

3) Most importantly, however, is that putting content at the top does serve the mass of people in the mid lvls, since it motivates them to level so that they can get access to the interesting stuff available later. This is the model that has served EQ so well -- note that they primarily add high lvl, challenging content to EQ in the form of raids. This is not just good for the high lvl people, it is good for everyone, since it provides goals for everyone to eventually strive for.

4) Similarly, if everyone knows that there will eventually be nothing to do, it kind of kills off motivation.

5) It smooths the process of adding stuff to the game if you add stuff that very few people can do at first. Otherwise, you get the steps of madness phenomenon, where the people killing neleb are often lvl 100+

The team mission system that has been discussed, with unique mobs perhaps, should help both high and low to mid lvls. But as far as other content goes, the priority has to be on high lvl stuff first and foremost.
