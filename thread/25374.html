thoughts on 3rd party software
This regards the message that is posted on the patching program about 3rd party software. There are very few programs out there (YET) that "interfere with, hack into, or decipher any transmissions to, or from, the servers running Anarchy." There are only three 3rd party software programs I have used that relate to AO, and two of them do not alter or even read a single AO file.

One is Nano Nanny. Beautiful beautiful program, it is. It is a program that calculates everything you need to know about installing implants, how much NP skill is required to assemble them, the requirements to wear them, and how much skill they boost. It never uses any files regarding the implants already installed on your character (you have to select what types of nanoclusters you use and the quality level manually).

The second program I have used is AMacr0. It's a very simple program that just streamlines the scripting process. You just click on one of them and it types it in for you. Again, it has nothing to do with any AO files, and runs completely independently.

The only program I can think of that reads AO files is Clicksaver (which I only used for about a day). When you go to a mission booth, it just summarizes the mission that are offered to you, showing the cash reward and also the item reward. I found it to be pretty worthless.

Just a thought...
