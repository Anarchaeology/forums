Firstly, I think what Silverstone did was a wonderful thing for the clans. He stood up and showed resolve where everyone else failed and got rid of the ICC, thereby returning control of the clan capital to the clans and out of the au****es of the corporate community.

But, what we object to is that he has ordered his guards to slaughter neutrals without question. Neutrals are innocents to this conflict, caught in the middle of the battlefield with Omni-Tek at one end and the clans at the other. They were trapped like caged animals, doing their best to survive and yet remain impartial.

How does a caged animal react? In the case of the neutrals vis-a-vis the Sentinels, they lash out at those who are persecuting them. Elite Operations was the claw of this animal's paw, and hired mercenaries provided by Loren Warr that kill clanners on sight.

And why did the neutrals do this? Because they were being caged, put on display and beaten. Partly by the eruption of the Notum Wars, partly by being denied the right to travel to one of Rubi-Ka's most illustrious cities, and partly by the actions of one militant clan who turned a valiant action into an all-out war against all non-clans.

What's next? Will Silverstone now declare that all moderate clans are persona non grata in Tir? Will clans such as Unity of the Rose, Whisper's Edge, and Valour Eternal be KoS by Sentinels' guards? It seems a definite possibility.

That is why we are leery towards Mr. Silverstone and the Sentinels.
