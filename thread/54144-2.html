Oh my god, you all are too sweet. I just saw this thread and it is very touching :_) You all are too kind.

No i have not quit yet. I have just not been on a lot recently. I've had a real speed up of responsibilites work wise as well as an improved love life thats taking up a good portion of my free hours. Real life seems to have come and grabbed me a bit.

I did start work on a new map for the new patch that updates the zone supression, fixes some errors that have been pointed out to me by players and adds some other handy features.

I probably will not be adding all of the new boss camps to the map (at least not immediately) for a few reasons (after some debate with players and friends):

1) Its too much of a spoiler
2) It may cause camping problems
3) It will clutter up the map quite a bit

If the locations of these mobs becomes widley known or they get added to other maps, i may just go ahead and add them anyway (once cat gets out of the bag) but for now I'd rather let players enjoy a sense of discovery and newness about searching out these spots. My map is really meant to be a guide for getting around but I don't want to wreck "discovery" for people too much.

Some of the best ones are still very unknown and fairly private. So placing them on my map would really wreck it for some groups that took the time to seek them out. I did put the andromeda camps on the map as a test and they did get pretty well camped out (i wont be removing those since they are already there).

In other news the programmer that helped me initally make the map files into the proper format has quit the game. So I am currently without anyone with the technical know how to make the graphic files into the end usable format. When I get the new version all done I'll see if he can do it for me still, if not I may need to call on someone handy with things like that to help me out here on the forums.

Thank you all for your kind words. It's nice to be missed. I miss many of you as well. :_)

-Dovve
