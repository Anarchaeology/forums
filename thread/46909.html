Booster Pack =(
With the release of SWG and AC2 on the horizon, the pricing of the booster pack in my opinion is far too high.

I have already decided that as soon as either AC2 or SWG launches then AO will be doomed to the dusty shelf until I need a new coaster for my coffee cup.

I have no intentions what so ever of paying for a PvP fest, but obviously those who are high level and feel the need to control land, then this might be a good offer.

To me, this is basically double charging for the SL expansion and I am therefore not going to join the masses who will buy both. If a booster is $19.95, then what price is SL going to be. For the same price I am sure that I could have either AC2 or SWG and a free month. I have had enough of Funcom.

This post is only to voice my opinions and to say what I have decided to do. It in no way is meant to make others follow suit and therefore should be taken as such.

When December comes, I am out of here (unless either of my Beta apps get taken up beforehand (for SWG and AC2.. not SL))
