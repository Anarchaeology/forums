Claim your prizes at Rubika Run II
Ancarim Iron Legion proudly presents:

The Rubi-Ka Run II

Ancarim Iron Legion wishes all clanners and neutrals welcome to participate
in the event.
Not so long ago AIL held the first Rubi-Ka Run, which was a success. This
time we hope for even more participants, and have made a brand new route.
People of all levels are welcome, but please note that geographical
knowledge of Rubi-Ka is necessary to complete the race.

The Run itself starts on August 17th at 12.00 GMT outside the Tir West Gate.
Please be there at least 30 minutes ahead due to information hand-out. If
you arrive after the Run has started, you can not enter the competition.

Rules for the Run:
At each post you will get a riddle. Solve this and you will get the answer
to where the next post is. You will also get a keyword at each post. At the
finishline you must give the postguard the commmon denominator for the
keywords. You can only claim your prize if you are one of two at the goal
and give us the right word.

No vehicles, no grid, no fixer grid and no other warping/beaconing buffs are
allowed. Run buffs will be supplied.
Whompas are allowed.

There are two kinds of prizes; one for high-level people and one for
low-level people:

- Complete set of QL 200 implants, including all the free help you need to
combine them with clusters and all buffs you need to get them in place.

- 5.2 millions credits (which means you can get a yalm, or just some extra
cash )

The Ancarim Iron Legion welcomes you to join us on a fun, adventurous and
exploring trip around Rubi-Ka. Good luck all! See you all outside Tir West
Gate, Saturday the 17th of August at 12.00 GMT.

Regards,
The Ancarim Iron Legion,
Event Coordinators,
Cybcer and Lillemjau
