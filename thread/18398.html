To answer the title FC's definition of melee is as follows:

Melee - 1. A person to stupid to pick a shot gun/nuke/uber crit range weapon class.
2. Cannon fodder for anyone with a ranged attack, pet or root.
3. A running joke in Oslow. 4. Someone that will never see neophyte or higher pvp title.




To answer the post, Drkrawn is right that's desync. No melee doesn't hit harder just faster. Anyone with a ranged weapon or nuke out damages us.
