Bump for weapon holsters.
--Duel pistol holders on your hip.

--A rifle sling around your back.

--Sheathing your 2hE weapon on a scarbard attached to your back.

--A scarbard for your sword.

--Holstering your small 1HE knifes inside a two scabards attached to your forearms.

Hell, you can even mix and match. You can use up to two at a time. Having both pistols and a large gun on your back.

They could go in the spots that normally are used for grafts and reflect bracers.
The back holstered weapons go in the back slot.

Immersion factor=triple. We hate it that everyone runs around in armor with their guns in hand, even in cities.

This would likely take a long time to code, do the artwork, and figure out how each weapon will be put on... but I garentee the players will love you for it. Perhaps you could put it in the expansion.
