MAs can still win by ganking. It's not very nice, but go to Meetmedere and you'll see a bunch of people just standing around in the 25% zone. They're usually cocky pet classes with their red pets. Just be careful of any gankers around that may gank you while you're ganking someone else. Don't gank Doctors, it's useless; they'll just heal right back up.

I don't take PvP too seriously. I don't care about titles. I PvP when I get bored and am hungry. I'll find someone standing in the 25% zone. Turn on my Flurry of Blows. Run up to him as fast as I can. Hit him with Shen, Dimach, Brawl, and continue to punch until I get rooted. If he's not dead already, I'll just chain heal and use my QL 125 First Aid kits hoping that the DOT from Shen kills him. Otherwise I just die, and I'll make myself something to eat while my chars rezzing.
