Nanomage info.
Mirm flicks on her comm. Looks through the current grid news files. Some about the suit being stolen. Some about how horrible Omni-Tek is to make the Nano mage dependant on Notum. Mirm turns on a record function and records some information she thinks would be of intrest to prove the slander agaist Omni-Tek wrong.

"Attention. I may be slightly off on the biomechanics and genetic templates used. But from what I understand from reading old Omni-Tek reports, is that the nano mage's having notum infused directly into their very DNA is what makes the Nano Mage breed possible. Without the infusing they would be just another Solitus, if a incredibly weak Solitus with a high IQ, but they would be no better at controlling notum as anyone else. In fact I would be sure that if the Omni-Tek officals where displeased about the failure of the nano mage breed there wouldn't be around any to complain. Not that Omni-Tek would kill them but more along the lines of why make a flawed product? But if it was intended that way it's possible when they where made Omni-Tek figured that the Nano Mage would be happy with it's abilities and home, an oversight true but hindsight is 20/20. Now if Omni-Tek didn't approve of the nano mages genetic template wouldn't the clans be wondering right now what ever happened to those first experimental nano mages as they would be surely be dead and hardly anyone would know the word Nano mage... And about the suit. It's horribly sad it was stolen. But these things happen."

Mirm smiles into the pickup.

"But if I had any reason to believe I knew the suspects involved I would be sure to return them... In peices and hardly enough left to be human true but alive for long enough to give the information I am sure so many people would like to ask them. Have a nice day and please do try to stay in one peice."

Mirm smiles again and turns off the off the comms recording function and hits send.
