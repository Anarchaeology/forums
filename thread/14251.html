Add to the next patch
Things to add to the next patch.

An item you wear that show you want peace and let you travel to all location on rubi-ka without the guards attacking you. Only Org can buy this item and it stop you from attack anyone and make you only defend yourself like the Neutrals. This will let OT, Clan and Neutral beable to work together for peace and other Org still get attacked if they want war.

Have one whompa that transport members to the org building (once org have a building) or Org grid. Anyone can use the whompa but the computer look at the Org name of the player and transport him to their building. The building can be place anywhere in rubi-ka and all non-members have to walk to the Org building.

Have Org pay for guard for their building and the guard will attack anyone that enter the building that is not a member of the Org unless they are on a team with a member. The more guard you want the more you pay for out of the Org bank.

Org suppy room where Member of the org can share equip with other in the Org. But if another Org kill the guards they can take half or all that is in the suppy room.

Have any alarm (if paid for) to tell all members of the Org that their guards are under attack in their building. So that they could help support the guards.
