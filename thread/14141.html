Suggestion - Guild Facilities
I think a VERY appropriate addition to this game would be that of facilities for guilds to use. Instead of all of this "Where in the heck are you guys?" answered by "We're at 2053x572 beside the pink flamingo with 12 legs" each guild (marked by size, contributions, or purchase?) could have an individual facility where gatherings could take place.

What do you guys think? I realize that someone could just dupe their apt key, but 1) that would take away the personal space, and 2) I doubt that a guild with more than 3 or 4 Atrox chars is going to FIT inside 1 apartment.

I'm not saying that this is imperative to the playability of the game or anything, I just think that it would add to the atmosphere for all those concerned ... Guilds could hold social events, recruiting functions, etc etc ...

Anyhow, think it over and reply with your comments ...

Thanks Guys,
