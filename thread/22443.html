"Social" mode?
I don't know if this has been mentioned earlier, it's hard to search for something specific in the maremagnum of posts... anyway, there it goes:

It'd be nice having a "mode toggle" between social and combat/exploration/whatever mode.

Let me explain. As the game is, you find people inside cities wandering around in full armor and carring their guns everywhere they go. I think it adds to the atmosphere having all the social elements in the game (like clothing) but right now, it's a pain to remove all the armor and guns and equip them only to "feel cool". It would be a lot better having another pane in the inventory window like the weaps/clothes/imps (for example) for your "social" mode, where you put clothes and food/drink/etc if you want and have a mode switch toggle to automatically change the outfit without fussing with the inventory. I think that would make the cities "friendlier", they'd look more like places where people live and less like military encampments.
