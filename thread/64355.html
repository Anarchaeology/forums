So I tried to make a PvP character...
Awile back, I started an Enforcer (Lilredhot) with the idea in mind to make a PvP orientated character.

Well, I tried. And decided:

It's just not worth it. Everyone I fight has a bigger essence than I can run anyway, and running around for untold hours getting buffs from everyone I can just isn't my idea of fun.

So I did some IPR on Lilredhot and give her a Longmoon.

As Glarawyn, I've PvPed now and then. Mostly I've gotten killed, but I've won a few fights too. I was more interested in becoming a L200 Neutral adventurer really. Then, 2 days ago, I did IPR with Glarawyn and gave her 2 SOL Fire Executioners. And woah do they beat out a Nova Flow. So maybe I'll be able to get into the buff hunting required to PvP, with a character I never really intended to do any serious PvP with (until I got to L200 anyway...)

And I'm sure I'll make another attempt at a PvP character. But then again, it's me. So I think something like a Nanomage Doc is in order.
