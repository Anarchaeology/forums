**READ THIS - Petition for ability to switch dimensions.
How many of us have friends on another dimension, but dont want to make a brand new character? But we can't do anything with them without making brand new characters and not even being able to give them money or items from a main character!
I suggest a "portal" that would log you off the current server, temporarily copy your character's data over to the other server, and let you stay there untill you wished to return, or you log off.
This would be a great addition to the game, but I realize it may not be possible or practical. If this is the case, what about just letting ARKs permanently move characters to the other dimension?
If you agree that this is a good idea, please post your support here, and TELL YOUR FRIENDS AND GUILDS TO READ THIS! We need support and numbers if you want something like this to happen!
Thank you,

Not to flame, but... well, no.

Economic destruction is my main problem. I would support a permanent move IF and ONLY IF credits could not be moved with. Items aren't a problem, because an item worth 250mil on RK-1 is only worth 100-150mil on RK-2, thereby preventing an economic distortion.

But if the 250 mil came over to RK-2 as 250 mil, there would be a problem. Even worse if that 250 mil went to RK-3.

So, if credits are zeroed when you move dimensions, it's okay, but I think it'd need to be a permanent thing as well.

Move, lose your cash, and you stay where you end up.
