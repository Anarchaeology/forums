missioned out
The boredom of repetitive missions is getting to me. I hover my mouse cursor over the AO icon and no longer feel up to playing anymore.

Have done ok 148 crat, 138 trader, 85 engi, 84 doc and 60 fixer. Promises that were made or implied when the game first came out have gone horribly wrong.

It seems that Home missions and camping NPC's is what this game is all about now and I look foward to neither and PvP has never worked properly.

12 months after release and the game still has so many problems that should have been solved in beta. Bugged health bars, pet pathing, useless nanos, trade skills, and the biggest nerf stick in the industry even a year after release.

I'm going to give this game a break. I'll check back every 3-6 months to see if there is anything new. Who knows in a few years from now the game might be ready for release.

Good luck everyone and happy hunting.
