Maybe shoulder or HUD items with scale modifiers (not necessarily useless, but you get the idea) or something.

Rewards that affect useful stats would not benefit people who fight mass PvP a lot (*edit: because they would expect to die a good amount), and would benefit the wrong people (i.e. level 1's who like to kill people for fun easily =D, title-exploiters who may be hard to catch because they only go up to rookie or so).

There are already benefits for people who take place in base-building/defending -- tower/org bonuses.
