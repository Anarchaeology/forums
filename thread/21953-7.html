The more i think about it, and the more i read, the more i reach a conclusion. My conclusion is this:

Funcom did not spend enough time when actually designing the game mechanics and the classes. The mechanics are, put simply, poor, it seems that no real thought was put into the creation of such things as:

AC, Surely it should be a percentage reduction, not a set number

Skill - The whole skill system is poorly designed IMO. Needing skills to equip weapons and armour, then adding buffs. Surely it is obvious that by combining buffs, people would be able to equip weapons and armour high above their level. Obviously not, this problem is only just being dealt with, IMO poorly. (Don't get me started about OE!)

Combat - Nothing necessarily bad about it, but nothing particulary good either. Usually consists of press Q, get up and make yourself a cup of tea. Teaming is slightly different, although there still isn't enough variety. Taunts and detaunt items are being added now, but these should have been in the launch, and there should be more! Hitting burst ever minute does not make the battle more fun!

Missions - These had potential, when i first read what FC were planning, when the game was in development, i got excited. Little did i know that it consisted of the same setting and layout over and over again.

If you want some pointers, i suggest you look at DAOC. I played that for a while, but couldn't afford to keep it up. It is in no way perfect! But it does have some very good ideas when it comes to quests and combat especially. While other parts of DAOC are sub-par.

CLASS BALANCE

To put it bluntly there wasn't any. Classes should have been balanced on launch. Sure the odd tweak and addition here and there is good, but revamping a whole class with new programs, items and weapons should have been done on lanch surely?

And the fact that some still haven't been "fixed" (i prefer the term, created) annoys me further. I am a fixer. And not proud, as we are unfinished. But not only that, but the fact that time and time again we get told by FC that theres some big fixes instore for us! Instead, they dribble out little fixes:

Patch XX.XX launch day:

"Oh dammit, we said we'd have a fix for fixers in this patch didn't we?"

"You're right, any ideas? We only have an hour"

"There are some ideas on that message board thing, but i can't be bothered to read them. I know, lets give them some nano to do with the grid, since they love that stuff! And their nano skills are blue, so it'll be something to spend their bucket loads of IP on"

"Yeah, thats a great idea. We can let them summon ammo! But that would be imbalancing, so lets make the ammo type completely random! And since fixers have loads of IP, they'll be pleased if we make it require a new nano skill."

"Sounds good, shall we test it?"

"Nah, just bung it in."

"We're good!"

Sure, exagerated like hell, but this is what i feel FC do when patches come up. They obviously can't be bothered to give us anything worthwhile. This new grid thing, FC will probably feel it is unfair, so they'll make it accessible by everybody.

Blackmarket was mentioned ages ago, we get some poxy broken shop. And we have heard nothing since. Our major problems aren't being tackled, they are just adding random rubbish for the sake of it.

First it was the fixers holy grail, now it is nothing more than a piece of clothing that makes you go blue. Grid Armour. Way to go Funcom!

What many people (non-fixers, FC) don't realise, is that these are only a snippet of the fixers problems. I honestly don't think FC know even half our problems, they choose to ignore them, they don't care.

Not many of you board guys knew me, but i was there, i was a fixer through and through. Now that time has passed, i have given up believing Funcom, and their lies. Its pessimistic, but hey?

See you around...
