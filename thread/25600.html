Why I'm better than you.
I'm not much into the bulletin boards, though i do check the pvp info every now and then and still havent been able to find funcom's official description of the supposed trader heal nerf. Anyway, like most other threads, this is a simple whine in my frustration of how other people choose to play the game, so dont take it too seriously.

That said, i have to say that the general pvp attitude is pretty pathetic and depressing. this isnt an omni thing, or a clan thing, its a title hugging, dont-die-at-any-cost thing. I'll start with an explanation of my own point of view. As a 150+ trader i like to pvp. I do well, and it gives me a chance to stretch out my character's abilities. Unfortunately i spend an inordinate amount of time looking for people to fight. People laugh if i ask for a duel in the arena, and really, can i blame them? So, I run around like a crazed transient stalking the shops of mmd and hanging off the roof in 2h0, with the occasional aerial blitz of the omni arena thrown in for kicks. The hours I play aren't the most popular too, so whereas there'd normally be a slim chance of running into someone worth fighting, for me, often its hours of pure boredom interspersed with rooting a passing yalmer in the hopes that he has some high lvl friends that'll come back and kick my ass. So basically, despite the hours i spend looking for fights, i dont actually do much pvping.

Consequently, my frustration with pvp often has other sources than just dying, though that gets on my nerves too, especially when its due to my own mistakes. What i'm trying to get at is that i'd rather fight more, and lose some than just kill passing yalmers, i just dont get a kick out of it and im afraid im putting bout half the omni population on /ignore. So when im in mmd, and angelicnova is running around (omni at the time) i like the idea of getting a chance to kill a neophyte and stick around. Granted she's killed me more than any other omni but that was lvls ago and i think i honestly have a chance now. Evidently, so does she - i get one drain in, root the pets - and its off to mort. Now she wasnt at deaths door here, in fact i was at 2/3 health bout 2/3 nano and she was showing full health to me (though i can never really tell how much hp anyone has in a fight, sometimes they just fall over). I tried to root while sprinting after her but couldnt manage to chase her down. Later syrah jumps me while standing by the grid, I get one drain in on her, she shoots me 4 times with luckily only one being a crit. She stay and fight it out? switch to her alpha gun and put up a lower TMS? Keep blasting me in the hopes of 3 consecutive crits putting my little opi ass in a grave? Uh, Noooo, follows angels footsteps straight to mort - I had barely touched her yet.

I'll address this right away - YES i run away from fights too. Running when You're 100% sure you cant kill someone is one criterion. Another is to turn tail and bolt if you're not 100% sure of certain victory. I spend too much time looking for fights to scamper away to the nearest zone at the first sign of someone who might kill me. If i come across a soldier in 2h0 thats standing on the bunkers, i wont even bother to shoot you. Whats the point? with TMS and 19 different places to zone - Sitting by Mikal and alphaing the random passersby is certainly an effective strategy, in fact im surprised i see soldiers pvping anywhere other than 2h0 at all, its just too easy there.

I've come to the conclusion that neo is sort of like a gold medal in a 100m sprint - congratulations, you know how to run well And if you get a kick out of that thing hanging over your head, then put those ips into runspeed and spend the rest of the game killing grays, GL to you.

After the two incidents i just mentioned in mmd, i went to tir arena and died probably 40 times, dropping from apprentice to rookie. I can only imagine how many more deaths it'll take to get back to no title but i'll keep at it until i lose fresh. I want no part of the title system, i think it makes people with questionable motives (come on we get a kick out of killing other people) end up acting like biiatches. I like the idea of no one benefiting from my death almost as much as i like the idea of me dropping some neo down to prentice in one fell swoop. Ok maybe im getting a little ahead of myself with that last one but you get the point- the title does nothing for me, the GM's wont remove it, they wont make it significant, or even relevant.

So, make a fuss, ask them to remove your title, ask them to "make pvp meaningful." Stand and fight if there's a chance you might yet win, even against unfair odds or against someone who's orange to you - ive had a couple great fights decided by who got the last crit. Of those fights, i think ive lost more than i won but i'm still glad i didnt just root and run - you learn the most by dying any way. Remember its easier to get new buffs than it is to find a good fight.


Well enough rambling, go send an email to support@anarchy-online.com (just me or do they give all the arks a macro with that one?) Remove yourself from the pointless absurdity of the pvp system. Oh, and offer me some hints on how to most quickly lose my title, do i need to have finished rezzing? be over half health? whats the best way to do this.... Go out and pick a fight, and remember its ok to loose

PS bb noob, so hope the spaces help
