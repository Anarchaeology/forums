The most hateful thing in AO...
The situation: The mob is down to less than a sliver of life. You raise your sword/aim your gun/point your nano-enhanced finger, ready to do them in and...

Tradermob xxxx forces your NCU to run Deprive Skills (Hella-big)
Agentmob xxxx forces your NCU to run 12-minute Snare
Somemob xxxx forces your NCU to run 6-minute Root
Biologymob xxxx forces your NCU to run Unmake: Essential Skill
Metamob xxxx forces your NCU to run Cut Your Damage Output by 85%
Somemob xxxx sticks a big old honking DoT on you

Oh, great. Now I SUCK until this spell wears off. Checking the NCU shows that's going to be, oh, four minutes. Four, minutes. I guess I'll... ummmmm... go make a sandwich, then come back and refresh the buffs that have worn off in that time, then get on with playing.

Come on. This is the single most annoying thing in the game at the moment (outside of chaincasting enemies, that's been beat to death). Funcom has made huge, HUGE strides in reducing the boredom-death-killer of certain "other" MMOGs, namely, downtime. It's not any fun to sit and wait for a bar to refill so you can fight another globzork.

(That's a made-up, "fantasy" monster)

Treatment kits and nano rechargers were an inspired idea. MOST battles in AO require at most 15 or 20 seconds to recuperate from. A real major ass-whipping might take a minute or two. Contrast this to the endless ass-sitting present in a "game" like EQ, a "game" where mind-numbing downtime is so prevalent they programmed a game INTO THE GAME so you'd have something to do during the hours and hours of sitting idly doing nothing.

But long-term, crippling, hostile nanos like the ones mentioned above can bring the hated downtime back. If you're doing a mission/hunting someplace where every mob is a challenge, you are NOT going to forge onwards while under the influence of a dangerous debuff. Instead, you will do what is most hated in MMOGs, SIT.

Funcom should go ahead and have mob spells dissipate on mob death. Having them persist for their full length adds nothing but frustration to the game, something AO shouldn't (but does) have. Either that, or make EVERY enemy debuff cancel when you cast "sharp object inexperience" on yourself.

Make it so! Comments, discussion, pointless, idiotic flames?
