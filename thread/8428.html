You can get a car at around 75,000 credits. I equipped my enforcer at level 30, could have done so earlier.

The point I want to stress is this: Make sure you look at the run speed increase. Kodiaks, regardless of ql are much faster than Midi's. Midi's, I think, make you less visible to aggros in the field.

I remember, when I traded my ql 50 Kodiak for a 240,000 credit ql 75 Midi and found myself going much slower. Back in those days the display did not tell you what the run speed would be. That sucked!
