Morphs, Home, MotD
Morphs: Absolutely agree with the people who say it's a huge pain in the neck. Right now, the graphics system puts your PoV just slightly forward and down from the geometric center of the top of the character. On bipeds, that makes sense. For the dogs and cats, it makes no sense at all. It would make more sense to calculate PoV as the front of the figure, just below the top ... but as I'm writing this, I realize what the problem with that is going to be, namely that bipeds keep waving their arms out in front of them, making their extended hands the "front." The only work around I can think of would be to calculate the eye position once, when the shape changes or the character zones. (If you're sitting down when you morph back, it'll miscalculate because of your crossed legs. Which means your PoV will be out in front of you until you zone. As rare and non-crippling as that would be, it would beat the status quo.)

City of Home: Everything Jayde said. I read an interview on one of the AO fan sites where a world designer said that what he loved about working on AO was that he had over 7,000 textures available for world design. (Not counting who knows how many thousands of textures for clothes, faces, weapons, and critters.) The Broken Shores region almost certainly uses every texture in the game. It either needs to be broken up into zones, the way Tir/TC/GTC is, or somebody needs to be put on a severe texture diet.

MotD: Agree. The Mockers just installed one using a chat bot, but that's a plug-ugly hack. This is something that every active guild needs.
