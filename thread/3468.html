Heh, here we go again...

There's a general arc of the story that lasts 4 years. This, most likely, will not change.

Certain aspects of the story can and will change due to players actions. The recent accused assassins that stood on trial were in fact players that attempted to disrupt an in-game event.

The arc of the story wasn't changed, but the immediate situation has been altered and now different events will occur to bring it back on track.

I'll bring up an analogy with Babylon 5. Babylon 5 had a 5 year story arc. *B5 Spoilers if nobody's seen it.* It was supposedly supposed to end with Captain Sinclair becoming Valen at the end of season 5. (Rumored.)

After the first season, Michael O'hare, who played Captain Sinclair, decided he wanted to move on. So they replaced him with Sheridan, played by Bruce Boxleitner. He affected the immediate part of the story by leaving and having a new Captain come in, but the story arc remained the same. They just bumped up his becoming Valen into the middle of season 4. The Shadows still attacked, the Vorlons still were Vorlons, and the story remained the same, just with a different character.

That's kind of how I see AO. The story arc is there. The endgame will be reached in 4 years. We can affect the immediate part of the story, like the assassination attempt, but there are many people much bigger than any of us that are really pulling the strings. (Ross, Radiman, etc.) We will return to the "regularly scheduled" part of the arc very soon, if we have not already returned to it.
