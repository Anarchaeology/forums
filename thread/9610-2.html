Weather
Oh-oh, since I am "in RL" a part time weather forecaster, this could be a dangerous thing for me to comment on this thread.

1) Please do NOT make it darker at night. I have my brightness
pretty high on my monitor and I still bump into things. Yes,
I do have night vision and that helps, I just do not see the
need for total darkness given it seems the planet with 2
suns is dark more of the time than light. How
about some moonlight??

2) Storms doing damage - I like that but it needs to be done
with some care. Scorch players too often and it will only
anger them, can you imagine the whining of a player
that gets killed by lightning that losses a level because of
it? It's not going to be a pretty sight for the ARK's and
for the forums.

Lightning should do small amounts of damage, but never
more than some fixed percentage of the players health.
Take the 40% PvP special cap for instance. This way
lightning is *dangerous* [especially if your huntning]
but it alone will not kill you. This serves the purpose of
both giving it purpose and fear without going overboard.
(Yes, there will be whining anyways but it will be vast
minority).

Wind - slows your runspeed down Yes, I like that. If
your running against it - should increase your runspeed
significant if running with it. This brings up a question, Cosmik -
we know there is "wind" in general, but does the game
engine take it far enough to "know" the wind's direction or
is it strictly sound effect?

Straight line wind damage [most thunderstorms produce it],
causes a small DoT and maybe even "pushes" you in the
direction of the wind. (Only during the worst of storms).
The Dot would be a nuicanse, nothing that can kill as such.

Tornado - Heh, probably would kill everyone with lag, but
would'nt that be an incredible sight? Picks you up [and the
Mobs] into the air, some random height then drops you,
you take fall damage [and might be carried away for
1000 meters or so from the spot you got picked up in]. You
can run from it and keep away from it's AoE. Getting caught
like this might well kill you, but since you can see it and
move, its a fair trade off and gives you a real reason to
fear some of the bigger storms.

Dust Storms should have a somewhat stronger Dot, again
should not really be enough to kill, just a nuicance or hazzard
to be aware of, definately should further reduce visibility
than it does now [though I've seen it get pretty low before].

Edit - [Added] Someone mentioned having some special NPC's
or Mobs come out when it's dark, I like that!

How about making certain kinds of dangerous NPC's or
Mobs spawn during the bad storms? I.E. during a certain
kind of storm there is a 15% chance that 3 damaged A-500s
will spawn in Lush Fields and aggro people? Or some
Mob that does energy damage, or Dusters using the storm
as an Tacticle Ops cover, so they come in with a drop ship
and cause trouble?

Av.
