14.2 Bugs/Issues
1. From the launcher - "All servers up!!
Unfortunately, we have discovered a bug as we where about to open the servers again. You will notice that some of the cloaks have wrong description; they will visually either have QL 1 or 200 stats in your inventory. This is a pure visual bug, and will only apply for some cloaks that are obtained as pick up items in missions. Your character will not be affected by this in any way, the cloaks will give the correct modifiers and they will not be effected by the over-equipping rules. This is a bug we found early in the patch process, but unfortunately it have come back to haunt us."

One thing to note, and a quick fix if you wan't to know the stats on your cloak - if you drop the cloak in your chat, the correct requirements will be shown.

2. The autopatcher does not work the first time - this is a known issue and is solved by clicking OK and trying the autopatcher again.

3. Bugged/stuck bags in reclaim terminals prevent you from scanning - This issue with items stuck in reclaim terminals will be able to be handled by the Support team. If you have an item stuck in the reclaim terminal give Support a /petition in game and they will fix it for you. You'll be able to use the insurance terminals.

4. You are taxed a small amount of credits each time you zone, if part of an organisation - We have programmers looking into this right now, and I will keep you informed of updates.

5. Dogda (Alvin/Dogda mission) not taking your items if you got the mission before the patch - to fix this, delete your Alvin/Dogda mission from your mission view, and get a new mission from Dogda. Then proceed to hand in the items you already had (no need t get new ones), and you will be able to move onto the next step.

6. Players on Dimension 1 will lose experience, gain rez shock, and their items will appear in reclaim terminals if they die at any level. It should work this way -

*No loss of XP from death until level 20

*No Resurrection Shock until level 15

*No loss of items until level 10

We are working on a fix for this. This is only Rubi-Ka 2. Rubi-Ka 1 and Die Neue Welt (German server) is not affected.

7. Some players are having problems patching; either crashing or getting corrupt files. We are looking into this.
