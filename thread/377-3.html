you miss the point cub
hey cub, i hear what you are saying but this is the point...

take a QL70 Rider and QL90 pistol...they do about the same damage. However, because the Rider is only QL70 the multi-melee, Heavy Weapons, and all other requirements are going to be lower than the pistols QL90 pistol/burst/fling requirements. However, the REAL killer is the multi-ranged requirements.

So, in order for a pistol wielder to compete with a sword wielder damage wise, the pistol wielder needs to have that weapon that is about 20 QLs higher in both hands.

As it is now...that's just plain impossible. Even if you are level 50 with a full set of level 70 multi-ranged implants, you still couldn't carry QL 85+ pistols (that's assuming you can get the burst/fling up to where you can actually aquip it) because of the multi-ranged requirements.

Because the swords are inherently a more powerful weapon, while it would be really nice to hold two really powerful swords, it isn't absolutely necessary in order to compete...or survive for that matter...

With pistols it is.

that was my only point. while i can see how some would find it unfair to give a multi-ranged buff but not a multi-melee buff...it really isn't unfair because of the inherent weakness of pistols and the necessity to "over equip" pistols not just in the main hand, but the off hand as well.
