Funcom, please read
Before I start, please be aware that this thread may be rather long, but I would suggest reading it anyway.

I started playing AO a couple of months ago and firstly I would like to thank Funcom for producing a game which generally speaking is very good and reasonably well balanced.

This is the second MM online game that I've played and is so much better than the other I won't even begin to go there.

So far i have played around in this game, trying to find a character class that I like, that is playable (solo) all the way to 200 lvl.

I now have an MA at lvl 30 and a fixer and NT to lvl 40, I've also tried other classes like the soldier and have a RL friends that have played just about all the other classes out there.

At the moment I'm sticking with the NT, some may ask why, but I like playing this class.

I'm not trying to tell anyone how to suck eggs, I'm just trying to be constructive, so if anyone disagrees with what I'm saying, then that it is only my opinion, it isn't gospel! I'm just trying to make a point.

The observations that i have made so far in this game are as follows:-

All the character classes are hard to get to high levels, some may be difficult at lower levels, so at middle, some at high levels, but they are all difficult at some points!

The only way to advance in the game (regardless of your class) is to kill things, so in realities sake, the only difference in the classes is how you kill things!

Some classes have a harder time than others, because they got indirectly nerfed by Funcom trying to fix something else.

All the classes out there are reliant on their nano skills past lvl 30, if they don't raise the nano's they will quickly find the game becomes unplayable!

Every class has to make choices about which skills they want to raise and which they can't afford to!


So how to fix the problems for everyone?????? All the classes out there????

Well to try and re-arrange the skills I would imagine is very hard, not impossible, but very hard! Funcom, you have the ideal opportunity to make the skills costs, more realistic, by combining the changes that need to be made, with IP reset, everyone will have more than enough IP reset points to reset a skill that was then costing less to raise, and thereby get the excess IP points back.

For example change the adventurers multi ranged and melee to green. The character can then reset the skill and gain back the IP points spent, that would otherwise be gone forever!!

Secondly, when you change a character class, change things that are specific to that character class. By that I mean change the nano's, make class specific equipment available, instead of making a change, that whilst fixing a bug or exploit that people are using also nerf's far more people than were using the exploit.

For example over-equipping.

I understand that you want to stop people using their high level characters, to get their new/low level characters into weapons that are vastly higher ql than than the character, and power leveling all the way to lvl90-100. But STOP!

Think about the impact that this change is going to have on virtually every character out there! Whether you realise it or not you are going to seriously nerf for both PvM and PvP ,most of the proffesions!

But the one class that it will make very little difference to is the one that is already the most powerful, at most things, and that is the trader!

You may think that i'm exagerating, but lets examine the case for a moment!

Lvl 150 char walks into a mission first room, gets mobbed by 3 dirty orange/red trader class NPC's.
One of them ransacks and deprives you! instantly, you are now robbed of 300 weapon and nano skill points!

Your skill to start with, was only 800 (if your lucky), you are now 40% overequipped, so what happens?

Your weapon now attacks 40% slower, and does 40% less damage, this is on top of the damage reduction that you will be taking from having your skill reduced!

So in reality, you do maybe half the damage in 1.5 times the amount of time! That means your are effectively doing 1/3rd damage!

This is ridiculous!!!!! You will be having enough difficulty trying to take one of the npc's out, without the overequipping, now you have one choice. RUN! and hope to heck the buggers don't root you, if they do, you die!

So what are we all supposed to do??? avoid any trader class character or NPC????

Most character classes have enough difficulty leveling past 30 now as it is! Lets not make it impossible!

Another example.

By massively increasing the multi ranged requirements for pistols, yes you have stopped NT's and MP's from using them, but in the process have completely screwed the adventurer.

Please Funcom!!!!!!!

When you make changes, make them to the things that are totally specific to the character class.

E.G, unnerf the adventurer by changing their nano's and giving the availablity of class specific weapons that do more damage.
That way, it won't make any difference to any other character class!

Nerf the trader by making their ransacks and deprives less effective! Why are these two skills stackable??????

Make the enforcer's and fixers nano's require lower level skills and lower nano costs. What is the point in having nano skills for fixers, that require nano skills in the 800 bracket, the preffered breed for a fixer is opifex and all of their nano's are dark blue!!! without some very serious implants or buffs, even at lvl 200, it will be nearly, if not totally impossible for them to use them!

What i'm saying is make the game more interesting and better balanced by tinkering with class specific things, not wholesale changes that will cause more problems than they fix!

Hope you Listening
