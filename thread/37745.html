Erm...First to answer the questions jabbawocke asked:

P.P.E. stands for Programmed Particle Emitter. It's a step in the process for creating a nanocrystal or MRR.

M.R.R. stands for Mass Relocating Robot. It's part component and part final product. They're components for creating Mass Relocating Robot (Improve Slashing/Clubbing/Stabbing Weapons). They're also the final product as the final product indicates. Last patch Mass Relocating Robots (Shape Hard/Soft Armor) were added, which allows the making of tradeskill armor.

As for Repulse, have you checked out the prices on the component Mass Relocating Robots needed to finish creating the Mass Relocating Robot (Shape Hard/Soft Armor)?
