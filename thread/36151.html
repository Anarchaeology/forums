Trader Shops can be found in several cities, Borealis coming to mind right offhand for one. They are trader only, having to be at least a lvl 25 trader I think to access. The BBI is an adventurer only pistol once having been sold in these trader shops,they can no longer be found there. Don't know if they will ever return to the shops but I think you can still get them in missions as boss loot and as rewards for solo's.

Man, if they had at least fixed the bug with the BBI faithfuls, maybe it wouldn't be that bad that they're harder to find. For those of you who didn't know, the BBI Faithfuls, despite being pistol weapons, rely on one's melee init skill, rather than the ranged init as one would expect. This has the delightful effect of making it possible for enemies to parry bullets! It had been promised it would be fixed in 14.4, but that patch came and went without an observable change to the BBI Faithful. Too bad.....nice gun.
