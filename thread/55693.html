Undo boss nano immunity!
It seems to me that after the last patch the bosses are totally immune to a large number of nanos. I know my NT's calms have absolutely no effect (not "resisted", they are simply immune). Lots on players have reported the same.

Many professions rely on non-damge nanos in combat (calms, drains, roots, whatever). The boss already has a huge Nano Resist, it does not need absolute immunity.

The latest patch added outside camps, and then made them impossible for many professions. Unless the intention was to require a tank + doc and have the rest of the team twiddle their thumbs, please consider revising that change. The bosses are already hard, this immunity is simply stupid.

If this was done to prevent some specific exploit, fix that specific exploit, don't ruin the whole concept for nano professions.
