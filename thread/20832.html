New Classes Your Ideas
I notice with each expansion pack EQ releases they add new Classes. While AO has more classes than EQ anyway I thought it might be fun to try and come up with a few more heres my ideas.


NecroTech.

The necroTech is able to reanimate the dead, bring them back to life as a controlled puppet. They are basically a nano cass able to create there own pets from the fallen in the same sort of way crats and controll the alive. They are also given other speical nanos, which are able to leech life from the living, sucking life out of the victums giving it to the caster. These come in two forms DOT leech and Nuke Leech.

The NecroTechs could be outlawed by Omni Tek

Mutants

The mutant is unable to use any equipment but makes up with this by its own mutant attacks and armour. Each level the mutant is given mutant skill points which he/she is able to spend on there mutant skills. The skills define an attack, such as Rad attack, chem attack, Claw attack etc, but also in defence skills such as Skin, Scales, plates etc which add to a mutants AC.

As these points increase the Mutant is able to get new skills, there nano powers increase or they get speical attacks in the same form as MA's.

Over time the Mutant is able to transform based on there skills a mutant with say rad attack will look diferent from a chem or claw attack, the type of armour used will also effect the look.




Ok Well thats my two see what you can come up with. Who knows maybe FC might even take up one or two ideas.

Entertainer
I'd like to see a purely social profession. Lets call it Entertainer, minimal combat abilities, absolutely not designed for the rough and tumble world the other professions excell at, has just barely enough fighting ability to squeak by in a group so he can level up.

This profession would have a ton of summoning nanos that bring temporary items perfect for social interaction, it would be absolutely important to this profession to be able to give the items away.

Some ideas for for summoned items include food & drink(perfect for those people who enjoy playing waiters/waitresses), unique clothing/glasses & headgear (imagine acting as sponsor for a dance competition), unique held items (clip boards, brief cases, party blowers), unique furniture items (party lamps, bean bag chairs, dj stand, moving colored spotlights). All items temporary of course and when I say unique I do mean items not currently available in stores and no regurgitated graphics please funcom, higher an art department, we know you had one at one time.

Other nano type formulas would be unique (same rules apply fc) visual effect nanos that would be great for parties, maybe the ability to shape change people into some highly amusing forms (party hat wearing leets or something else equally silly, no combat bonuses please)
