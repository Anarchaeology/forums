Mission 'fix', did more bad than good.
2 night ago i set off for my night of missions to get my lvl 28 enforcer some cash. Went to tir grid to the mission terminals there, got myself 3 missions all around meetdemere all paying 10-13k each. These were set at just over the mid way on difficulty, full money, full bad.

I went out, did them, got myself lotsa cash and xp in 2 hours.

Today, i got out looking for my normal mission, see the 'fix' (and i use the term loosely) about missions.

I go to my normal tir terminal and look up my normal missions, but now, somin has changed, they are all next to or in tir, and they all pay 1-2k. Now on my sort of mission doing 3 or 4 at a time mostly full of dark oranges to get me good xp i spend about 2.5k a mission on my aid kits and labs.

The way the missions are now is wrong, it should be put back as soon as possible. I know people were moaning about mission being in stret, that was becasue yu were etting them in the wrong place.

Lemme give yu a quick run thru.

Tir - Mission around meet.
Newland - Missions around Aegan
Athen - Missions around stret.
Avalon - Mission north of athen/east of borealis
Borealis - stret and borealis forests.

If yu dont want to walk far, go to the right city and choose missions for the town yu know well. I go to tir because the mission terminals are right next to the grid and then its a quick grid to meet and off to my missions, done and done.

Now the further away i could get from tir was when i put it on full easy (everything way grey) full bad, full money and full hidden. and even that only payed out 4k.

The missions were not broken beforehand, they were better, only some ppl moaned because they couldnt figure out the simplicity of choosing the right missions in the right area for them.

We must put the missions back, all this has done is made it harder for anyone under lvl 50 to make major cash easily. I know it isnt supposed to be easy but its gone from fairly easy to amazingly hard.

Now if i go to tir i still get the same missions, still as hard with all the dark oranges in them, still the same amount of them, still the same amjount of aids, just they take 3 minutes less to get too and pay much less.

I know at lvl 28 i cannot talk for everyone but i knew of many other lvl 100+'ers who did this the exact same way i did to customise where there missions are.

I am normally not against Funcom, compared to the amount of stuff they have fixed to the last year and a half i spent at UO and the stuff they STILL have wrong this game is doing very well, and the little glitches everyone keeps whining about are easily avoidable. But this is a major mistake and will make reaching the allready stupidly high shop prices even harder than it allready is.
