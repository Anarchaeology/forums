Fists are kindof dual wield by default. Since you aren't actually weilding anything it's not exactly dual wield and does not require a dual wield skill (other than Martial Arts). You can't hit bare handed while holding a weapon with your off-hand or vice versa. Martial Artist make up for not having weapons with their special attacks which can only be performed while unarmed.
The number of attacks you make per round is decided by skills just as with any other weapon.
