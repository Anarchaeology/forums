Well
Just to reiterate a point. There should be a mechanism to *allow* for these types of 'dream' missions that would be optional. In otherwords, if you wanted a vanilla mission you could just go get one. If you had a group together, or a bunch of friends, and wanted to tackle a 'deam mission', then you would indicate (perhaps via a checklist as described very early in the thread) what professions were in your team. Then each profession check would generate types of puzzles that could be encountered. Mission difficulty could set how many total puzzles were encountered, whereas, professions selected would set what types of puzzles were encounters.

Remember though, above all things, AO was originally touted as having a mission system that would create missions based on the skills of the characters involved.

IMHO, missions should not be little hunting grounds (little being a relative term). Hunting is hunting, specifically outdoors. Missions though should be *missions* with obstacles to overcome, and definitive timelimits to beat and require certain classes to overcome those obstacles.

At this point, though, I've said many a time that these types of missions should be optional and distinctly tailored to the needs of the group generating them.
