Nano programs
When it comes to breeds like Nano Tech (and other casters), there need to be made some changes. Either you have to implement some over-equipping feature like Gaute suggested, that will lower other profesions damage if they do not have the necessarry skills to use their weapon (i.e. twinking alot to equip a beam etc), or you should make Nano program requirements for upload. Meaning any NT can go get a Mochams Gift to upload their attack, and then be allowed to use it after the Mat Crea drops below the requirements. That is exactly how it works for other profesions, why are we being 'punished'?

Tbh, I would prefer to see over-equipping gone instead of us getting the upload feature, but one of them has to come, to make this game more fair.
