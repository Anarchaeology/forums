Add more epic encounters
Hi, I just thought I would share something that I really enjoyed in other MMORPG's; Epic Encounters.

I think it would be really cool if there were more dungeons added, and really cool special looking mobs to kill, with really unique items that drop from them.

Games like Everquest (though somewhat primitive compared to now) were really strong because of this (in my opinion, of course).

I really loved those encounters where you needed a few groups and organization in order hunt a certain zone, or kill a certain boss mob.

Anyways, I know you have a few of these already, but dungeons would be great with new environments. I think this is a good idea because it breaks the monotony (sp?) of doing missions over and over and over again.

The big problem with uber encounters is that you need to make it worth the while to acheive the goal. Just having bragging rights is not often enough, since the time investment to gather a large enough force and the traveling there, the setting up for the kill ( clearing all mobs buffing , bringing back the dead ... )

Now usually what they will do is put on this mob uber type rewards ( read, weapons/armor/gizmos)... now since these items are much better than the normal kinds, people will flock to these encounters no matter how tedious they are.

Just to get their hands on these uber items.

After a while as these items get known for how much they improve the abilities of your character, the problems start. You feel you absolutely need those items to compete, people will try and steal your kill to get those items ect...

Already you see some of this behavior with boss loot in dungeons like SoM , TLR ... Camping is also an issue with uber loot in known locations on uber bosses.

Is there a solution to this tho? There probably is, just need to be thought of. As it is people have demanded in the past to have dungeons and boss loot and Funcom supplied us with some. I just personally feel it was a step in the wrong direction.

Personally i would have felt it was more in line to have zones made and dedicated to PvP and conquest of territory. Have NPC mobs that will defent their bunkers and have them drop the special loot instead.

The loot would have been a good reason to PvP aside from the PvP tittles. This zone could've had massive underground tunnels enabling you to infiltrate the other faction's compound, bunkers set in strategic places, trenches and all sorts of combat situations to promote the storyline.

And from there it would have been easy as well to make events where important figures of the AO storyline could make appearances and by incapacitating or killing them introduce those special items that we could all be in awe off.

With the trend of introducing uber loots on boss mobs, it also diminishes the supposed importance of tradeskills for AO. Originally it was advertised that post level 125 your sole ressource to acquire armor and weapons would be the tradeskill classes as a primary source of gear. Mission loot was also a possibility but was to be considered more troublesome as just going to see your local weaponsmith and ordering your new weapon made.

But tradeskills have been plague with non-combining recipes, and the difficulty of finding the right QL of components and the need to go from shop to shop to find them, made tradeskills undesireable at best. Also a lot of tradeskills are still missing from the game namely the crat paperskills and armor making.

But tradeskills are for another discussion entirely =)
