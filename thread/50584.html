Innately useless maps
Well, it's a touch hard for me to fathom why everything else in the world can be solved with technology, but something as simple as marking a map (say, which room the boss button is in) is undoable. Any moo-ran in RL can go buy an atlas, with shops, roads, areas, all written down if they so choose, for like 12 bucks. So if you can do things in AO you can't do in RL, because it's so "futuristic", why are these fundamental things so lacking?

/me gets out her Sharpie.
/me marks an "X" on the Grand Canyon.
