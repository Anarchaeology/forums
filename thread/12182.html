The Mafia....?
[A lighted cigar dangled from his mouth, smoke flying up into the yellow glow of the dim lights. Below him was a party, like almost everynight at Baboons. He dropped the burning ashes into the electric ashtray, instantly clensing itself after the ashes were dropped in. He raised his eyes from the hood of his, looking at one of the fake trees among many in the club. Shaking his head, he sent a glare down onto the people..he looked at the person in front of him]

The person said, "Don Reynaldo..."
He just nodded, with a slight grin to let him know he was listening.

The person continued, "We have been on this planet for quite some time now, and now I think the time has come...the time to begin an empire. Through history people have seen this friendly power, giving to the poor, taking for the rich. We can be HEROS to be people, Don Reynaldo. We must start this empire..."

Reynaldo said, "My friend, indeed its probably time to start. But who would accept it in this time, we are so far away from Earth and are ancestors, the people who slaved over this land to create something. Who would accept the idea..."

(OOC: I will continue this if you want. I myself want to start this. Every rp i have ever played in I have been part of the Mafia. My character is Reynaldo Griffel. Who wants a mafia..who would actually join..)
