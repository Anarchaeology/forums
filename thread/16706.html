Improving game play, 14.2 and OE
Before I start i want to be very clear that i love the game and in general think that it is a well designed, well thought out game!

Having read all the available info on the changes proposed for 14.2 and over-equipping/IP reset in particular, in general i think it is a good idea, however I would like to make the following points.

A lot of people myself included, have commented about character classes being nerfed, in some way or other, so that either their nano's are horrendously expensive(IP wise) to pump into, or that they have to put so many points into so many different skills, just use a weapon. That everything else is so compromised by the lack of IP points available.

IP reset points are a good idea, however I would make the following point. All the very high lvl character's out there should not have any problem with IP points! Some of you may ask why? the reason is, that prior to one the patches released a few months ago, if you died, you could lose lvl's! however you didn't lose the IP's that you had gained. so you then re leveled and gained another lot of IP for gaining the same lvl!

So in theory at least, and for those that exploited the bug, you have characters out there with 10's if not 100's of thousands of IP points more than they in theory should have, so are they worried about IP points? proabably not, would

So patches keep getting issued which unnerf the character class?
Now I know that Funcom are trying to keep the game balanced for both PvP and PvM modes, however In my opinion, the fundamental issues within the game need to be addressed.

What I mean by fundamental issues, are what are the differences between classes?
The only real difference that I can see, is how anyone kills a monster to gain experience.

So as an enforcer you beat it to death, as an Agent you shoot it to death, as an NT you nano it to death, as a doctor you poison it to death and as a MP or engie, you have your pet kill it!

Therefore I would ask the question, how can this be a role-playing game? when everyone has to do the same thing, just a different way!

Take a look at the character classes, what is a Engineer? do they build anything other than their robot that is used for killing things?
short answer, is that unless their skills happen to allow them to no!

Every character class is supposed to be the best at something. So why is it that some character classes have loads of mastery nano's for lots of different skills, but some have 1 or none, with some having so many, that are completely out of character for that class that it is ridiculous!

Take the NT, they are supposed to be the kings of nano programming and casting offensive nano's!
So why is it that the MP is the one with the masteries in all nano skills and the NT has none?

Why does a trader, who is supposed to trade! have all the masteries in all the trade skills, as well as comp lit and shotgun?

A trader doesn't build things, he is a middle man who makes his cred from buying and selling!

Why does a fixer, not have any masteries apart from stealth? They are supposed to be the kings of getting unusual things, masters of the grid and the masters of SMG's. To my mind the fixers nano's should not be all dark blue, they should be the ones with the comp lit mastery and have an SMG mastery as well.

It should be the engineer with some of the trade skills masteries, not the trader! Most trade skills have nothing to do with how effective you are as a trader, but how effective you are as an engineer!

I know that a lot of trader players will say that I'm wrong and their class is not to powerful, but come on people, be honest with yourselves it is.

A trader can shoot better with a shotgun than anyone! they can have a better comp lit than anyone, they can buff or debuff massively, they can build things better than any other class, and if the OE rules are brought in, the class that is going to benefit most is the trader. Everyone else will have to worry, is my weapon/armour/pet to high? The trader says i'm not worried, I'll just ransack the next thing to come along!

So the only people that will be able to still put the stupidly high ql gear on, guess who???? The Trader. Please Funcom, can we have the trader a little less powerful??? not more!!!!!

If Funcom, really want to balance the game out a bit, move some of the nano programs from one class to another! And make the skills more appropriate. I would make the following suggestions, all of which, would make the game more balanced across the classes and make it better to role play!

Move the nano programming and matter creation masteries to the NT!

Move the building trade skill masteries to the engineer!

Move the comp lit mastery to the fixer and add an SMG mastery!

Give the trader a negotiation mastery nano, so that they can deal with the non computer trade post's better.

Make some of the fixers nano's light blue.

Give the Enforcer some edged masteries! and make their nano's less costly in skills required and NCU cost.

Make some of the assault rifles and swords not require so many skills to use!

Give the soldier masteries, in all ranged weapons! that way we might see some people using things other than assault rifles!

Make the OE rules only apply to friendly buff's, but not apply to being ransacked and deprived!

Dramatically increase the experience gained from building things! that way you can have genuine traders and engineers!

Remove the light blue skills that are completely inappropriate to that class, and make some of the more appropriate skills light blue

Some of the other classes are nerfed, so that they are very difficult to play, but the trader, certainly at high ish lvl is to powerful, because they are to versatile! They are to good at too many things, they aren't jack of all trades master of none.

They are jack of all trades, master of most!

So please funcom, i love the game, but can we make some of the skills and nano's available more appropriate to the classes!

I would like to be able to play the game and enjoy it, playing as any class, but at the moment i can't!, well at least not much above lvl30. Some are just to much like damm hard work!
