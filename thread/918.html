Fix the low income on missions with a Distance Slider!
I have posted a thread like this one in the future changes section and I am now posting one here in the hopes of raising a lot of support for the idea of adding distance sliders to mission terminals, and hopefully catching the eye of funcom. I know that the idea was presented to them a while ago and they apparently 'loved' the idea but we have yet to see them do anything about it.

Distance sliders will solve the problem that many of us are having right now with missions not giving out enough money because they are not as far away as they used to be. I know that my fixer gets on average about half of what he used to.

I also have a concern right now that has not come up yet in the game but that I'm sure will be discussed in the near future. My fixer was looking into buying a car, but now that missions are closer I won't need it; i can just grid to my mission locations or within one zone of the location almost all the time. Distance sliders would nullify this by allowing us to set the slider to a farther location thus making the investment in a vehicle a worthwhile one. As it stands, a car would be a waste of money for me.

Please post your comments on distance sliders, and any points I have raised, or just post your comments. Hopefully we can get funcom to listen to this.
