Level 80 melee adv, so I'll stay avoid comments on pistol problems (though I think there are 1 or 2 people with complaints about them )

1) Morphs - The biggest problem I have with these is that they have limited usefulness at higher levels... Reet form was great at lvls 20-40, but as many have said, by the time we can cast Flurry of Feathers, most of us have spent millions on a Yalmaha. I don't think it's a big stretch to make this form include flight, as MP's can cast Quantum Wings at roughly the same level. Playful Cub I've never used, but I actually really like Grinning Hunter. It has a nice damage increase, but would be much more effective with larger crit boost and an HP boost (A large sabretooth would have a thick hide!). The problem I'm running into now though, is that there is no useful morph until the insanely high Calia's forms. Would be great to have a more evenly stepped selection, or additional morphs (something for the water??) and have something to look forward to. No need to address the doorway issue, as you've stated it's a bug and you're fixing it.

2) Heals - I think these work fine. We're no doc, but I have no prob being a secondary healer or subbing for a doc when necessary.

3) Damage Shields - Another nano line that is good at lower levels and useless >50. The IP investment in Matter Creation is huge for a shield that only absorbs a few points of damage (and in rarely seen damage types.. ie cold, fire, etc.) and deals back a few points. At my level I'm dealing back 30+pts. per hit, which over the course of a fight =~300 total hps. Insignificant when the mob has 4k+ hps, not to mention that I won't be able to scale up the damage quick enough to make any difference at future levels. I can deal with the small reflection if you can implement the GREAT idea put forward about the shield recharging.. make it once per mob, or once every 2 mins. Not sure why it wasn't always this way.

4) Run buffs - I'll repeat many other comments: Did you forget about us >lvl 50? Why does the run buff just stop at +100? Add later versions pls...

5) AC buffs - Like run buffs, these seem to just stop at later levels. Also the IP cost seems to scale very quickly for mild AC boosts. I would love to see either reduced cost or additional stepping.

6) Gen buffs - Pls give us melee/pistol buffs, incl. dual wield buffs! I'm sure since every other buff req Psy Mod, this will require another IP investment, but would be well worth it! Not only do we REALLY need these, but would give us something other classes actually want. It would be nice to not be looked on as the poor stepchild of Rubi-Ka.

I've really enjoyed playing my adv up to this level, and all in all I think you guys have done a great job improving AO to where it is. We're just all looking for you to focus some attention on us and give us additional things to look forward to like you've done with all of the other classes.

Thanks...

I am, sadly, not a veteran like most of the posters here, but I actually have been playing since August, so I'm not necessarily a "newbie"; I'm a student, and that automatically forces me to be a casual gamer. My first and still main character is an adventurer. I dual-wield 1-handed-edged weapons; I am on level 42, and I, strangely enough, have a PROTECTOR executioner in one hand, and a right slice in the other (and even though I may regret it later, I enjoy being a tad bit unique).
I haven't yet experienced all of the problems that I am sure that adventurers have at higher levels, but I still have a few suggestions:

-Our Green Skills. The darndest things are green for the adventurer. I think we would all appreciate it if things that were a little more useful to our class were made green, it would help ease the ip crunch that we are always in. For instance, every single one of our vehicle skills are green, yet the only useful vehicle skill for an adventurer is the vehicle air skill (at least that is what I have found after consulting my own experiences and those of others that I have read about), and you only need around 90-100 points in that to be able to use a lower mid-30 quality yalmaha. I frankly would rather have brawl light blue than any of those skills green.

-Nanos. We definitely need better nanos, new lines, etc (and I won't go into specific examples of why because I think that that has already been covered quite adequately). I realize that we aren't supposed to really like technology, and that nanos are a form of technology, but nanos are absolutely necessary for us to use because we need them to supplement our jack-of-all-trades-level fighting skills --I don't believe that I have ever been able to get through a mission of 50% difficulty without using my healing nanos. And that is why we needs more effective ones (not necessarily healing, though...healing is the most useful out of all of them). If the problem is that it would disrupt our "I don't like technology" reputations, than why don't you create another skill that creates effects (like morphs, shooting lightning bolts out of our fingertips, making plants grow, or whatever) that are akin to nanotechnology, but that aren't actually technology. Not "magic" necessarily, but "spirit" or "bestiality" or SOMETHING. Maybe it could be attached to the adventuring skill, for we all know how useful that skill is.

-More Morphs. Morphs, while not useless, are not always worth the time and ip spent trying to get them. I played Anarchy Online for sixth months almost exclusively with one character to be able to transform into a giant dog with annoying night vision that can't fit through doors (I realize that the door problem is supposedley going to be fixed, but it really is ridiculous.) Eh? And the next nano in line I won't be able to use until I am about 20 levels or so higher, I believe; and it is simply another reet form that can fly, which is neat and all, but quite frankly, the adventurer morphs are the one thing in the game that break the dull monotony of gaining levels for me, and it would be nice to have more of them. I realize that if you take into account that the morphs have "other" and "team" forms, they actually are spaced decently (and even generously), but if we are such loners, than why should these count for so very much? Even if you were to add morphs that we could not cast on other people (like leet form currently), I would greatly appreciate it, for it is the only thing that makes us special in any way. In every other way, we are easily and gladly replaced on the team scene by another character class. And I don't believe that it is too difficult to find inspiration for discerning what these new morphs should be, for there are numberable forms of wildlife on Rubi-Ka to choose from.

Better-Quality Morphs. I don't find morphs to be useless, but some morphs need to remain useful longer instead of just turning into tricks to make people crack up. There's got to be more uses to being a tiny little leet than just making newbies gape.

-Oneness with Nature. I don't really see the "oneness with nature." The only thing that supports that is the morphs, which need some work. Can't we do something profitable, like making special potions from plants or somthing? I realize that that is a rather lame idea, but if we are so "one with nature" than why am I so skilled at driving a man-made flying car? We need some special "one with nature" type skills that make us either desirable team members or better solo characters, or both even.

-Concealment. In our class description, it says that we, as scouts and loners, often make use of "cunning" as opposed to just direct combat. Exactly when and how do any of us ever do that? We really should have some way to disappear or get away, some sort of thing that corresponds to our affiliation with nature. Even if it's just a morph that turns us into a golden hamster that can hastily burrow into the ground and out of harm's way, I would be satisfied. Or a skill that makes us real scouts. You can see a giant bird from a mile away. A morph that is actually small, or even not an animal. Being able to turn into a cactus that can move so that if a creature approaches I can stand still and they won't be able to tell that I am acutally not a cactus (of course, there would have to be some restrictions like it cannot be executed if you are being attacked, etc. and you cannot attack in this form, etc.).

Anyways, I think I've rambled long enough. There are no doubt several things that need to be done with the adventurer class, and giving that so many adjustments are being made to everybody else, I don't think it's too much to ask to have at least some of these things attented to.
