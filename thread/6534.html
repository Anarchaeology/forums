omni or clan? That is kinda vital information... (or maybe even neutral?)

Hunting in teams are superior to hunting solo. Killing is faster, and there's the social aspect of it as well.

BUT, here's my advice (assuming you're omni, as I cannot say anything about clan territory...)

50'ies and early 60'ies Lush Fields provide good hunting grounds.
NW of Lush outpost (grid to Harry's and follow road left. scan at outpost, then travel NW.) There's a cave which you can escape into, and several things to kill .... among them is shades, bio's eye's claw's and lifebleeders. Lifebleeders (popularely called LB's) go aggro on the closest thing it sees, so beware of them. They also give good loot btw.

If that camp is a little too hard, there's another straight south of that one (almost in the end of the zone), which has almost the same mosnsters (except LB's) but are easier to kill (lower lvl'ed)

When you "grow out" of lush, I suggest 20k. (wom-pah from trade). Straight north of the village, there's an eye-camp. they are easy to kill, but you WILL get adds unless beeing very carefull. When pulling from that camp, I usually get 6-7 adds, which is fine if you have a good tank/team/healer.

There are more places in 20k, but if you want to go solo up to 70, I don't think I should mention them, since my experience is that a good, solid team is required on most places in 20k. prefferrably doc/advent/MP or some similar healing capabilities.
