The whole 20 thing is minimum damage rearing it's ugly head.

Damage cannot (barring bugs) drop below min. This means that people who grossly overequip their armor will, for the most part, only be hit by min dmg from mobs.

Like the other guy said, try playing with 0AC then say AC does nothing.
