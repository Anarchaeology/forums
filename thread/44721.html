I have been waiting for the 14.6 patch for a while now. CZ posted on AO's 'Next Patch' forum that (s)he would try to have preliminary patch notes by the end of last week. Now there is news of a 14.5 patch. Is this 14.5 patch to come before the 14.6 patch? emp...i sure hope you get an answer. but, odds are you won't. it sounds like you have the same type of disappointment in funcom that i do. lately, i've been thinking how ironic it is that the same people who came up with the great concept of making AO, the online role playing game, are the same people that didn't have enough vision to implement it correctly - or enough sense to have decent customer support.

lessons learned, i've come to realize that asking funcom questions that should be answered can't be answered by them, or more likely won't be answered by them because either they honestly don't know, or just don't care. either way, it goes to the root of the problem. if they don't know, shame on them for not having the vision to know which direction this game is going. if they don't care, then shame on them for not knowing how to support the player-base...the people who have been loyal to the game despite its creator's repeated blunders.

hopefully, this new secret patch 14.5 and the ultimate patch which has been in progress for more than a month 14.6 will fix some stupid problems that have persisted in this game, and finally bring some balance to the professions, and stability to the game. hopefully, funcom will have created a streamlined policy for how to reimburse players in a quick and accurate fashion. hopefully, funcom will finally implement the content that was advertised on the side of the box...and have an active ingame storyline on all its servers. but it probably won't.

sadly, i think you won't get an answer...and leave. i'd put 1000 credits that you won't get one.

good luck, and may swg, shadowbane, and eventually the matrix learn from the mistakes of anarchy.
