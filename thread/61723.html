The Descendants are Recruiting. OT Guild.
The Descendants is an OT Guild on RK-2. It is also a group of long time friends who play or have played in other mmorpg's as well.

We are looking for fun loving people to join with us in our primary mission: to have fun and to help one another out.

You are not required to buy Notum Wars to join our guild. However, our guild is planning to set up some towers for those who do. We can use your help.

We accept recruits at all levels. We also have players in the GMT as well as US time zones.

To learn more about us, our history and philosophy, and other games we play in, please visit our web site at: The Descendants.

If you are interested in joining, best way is to post a request on our website and say what your time zone is and typical playing time. This way we can have an appropriate officer contact you.

Or, you can /tell one of our officers in game:
Gamic GMT
Varfleet or Greylend EST
Grokker PST
Juraeval Late PST
