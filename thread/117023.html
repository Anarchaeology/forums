Opening more forums?
I want to open more forums.
I want to do it for two main reasons:
1. There's too much off-topic posting in some of the current forums.
2. A significant portion of the community wants more forums on the official bulletin board.

So why don't I?
The two main reasons:
1. I don't have moderators enough. More forums require more moderation resources. And being a moderator is depressing when all you do is moderate, and all you get from the community is flak, so I have a hard time recruiting more of them from ARK.
2. The Funcom bashing, unconstructive posting and total disrespect (or simple ignorance) of the social guidelines. I don't see the point in rewarding that kind of behaviour with more forums (and thus more topics) to spread this to.

What can be done?
To take each of the points above:
1. Player moderators. They would have to respect, follow and enforce the social guidelines at all times, and would also have influence on them. 2-4 moderators per forum maybe, in addition to the ARKs and myself having access anywhere. Some system of application and approval would be needed.
2. Since I can't give more answers and feedback at the time, and I can't/won't force others to, I don't see how I can satisfy the demands for everybody. That means either people have to start just acting nicer, or we'll have to run a tougher line on moderation of the "Refrain from sending statements that could be perceived as offensive or hurtful against anybody..." part of the social guidelines. I guess the former would follow the latter after a few weeks.

What's your opinion on this then?
First of all, you're talking to me now. Just Cz. Not the rest of Funcom. Asking for more resources to Community is not a part of this, nor is demanding the dev team reduce work on Shadowlands and patches and rather stay more updated on the forums, as I'm not the one deciding that. Both are always being considered though.

So, for now, for this thread, keep it to what you, the ARKs and I can do together. Yes, together. That's the word.

Player moderators
Good or bad idea? My thought is that they will be more known beforehand, having a name and a reputation (and hopefully respect). For an ARK to build the same would take months.

2-4 moderators a good number for a forum? This is mainly to be sure somebody is around every so often to see what's going on. It would depend on the forum of course.

Should moderators be active posters in the forum themselves? I think so, as it means they will have a genuine interest in checking the forum often, and keeping it in line.

I'll add a discussion of its own later on this if needed.

Whining, bashing and bitching
Don't believe I consider all negative comments whining now. I don't. However, I do believe it's fully possible to post in a constructive manner at all times. And making things personal is never needed.

But how do we get rid of this? And that "we" includes you guys too. This is you, ARKs and me now, remember. Nobody else.

Is tough moderation and suspending those who get out of hand the only way? If so, we can do that. If it helps the rest of the users of this bulletin board, no problem at all. Note that with more moderators comes more interpretations of the rules, so we might need some clarifications on some rules.

Taking the soft way of talking people into calming down would of course be preferable, but that takes more time, which I personally don't have right now.

Any suggestions?

Last note
I'll aim for running this discussion for a week or so. Please don't expect me to post every day, but I will read every single word written here. And keep it both on-topic and nice. No need for any unpleasantries here.

So, your thoughts, ideas and other comments here please. It's the future of the bulletin board we're talking about.

If I had to nominate which forums should be added/reintroduced...

Suggestions - one of the most significant causes of off-topic posts.

World of Rubi-Ka or something like it as a more 'social' but non-rp forum, somewhere to discuss the game and the world in a general way.

OK, that out of the way...

Player Moderators... something that should at least be tried. I'd reckon about 3 per forum should be sufficient, and if 1 was an ARK then that would help to. You'd need to have some form of quasi-formal agreement with those players (or, maybe for legal reasons, something formal), and a single moderator's forum might be useful, somewhere issues of moderation and the rules could be discussed (obviously, moderator access only). Profession forums should maybe include the professionals in their moderators (if they aren't already).

Should the moderators of a given forum be known? If rules of conduct regarding abuse toward these moderators were strictly enforced then this is probably something worthwhile. But, moderators should have additional accounts just for moderation purposes. That way the moderator can be known and contactable, but still seperate from their normal forum persona (and possibly from their in-game character(s) too).

Flaming and such like will never be eliminated, and provided it doesn't get too out of hand, again, isn't a major issue. Its a matter of scale and degree. Overtly personal attacks are another matter mind you.

We all can get a bit too involved in something on occasion, attach too much persoanal baggage to it, and get upset as a result. I always try to be reasonable, though I've stepped over my personal boundaries once or twice over the time I've been posting here. I think I've always appologised if I've felt I was out of order though.

If the rules are as clear as they can be, the moderators have a forum to discuss them, and where necessary, suitable action is taken, then that is as good as we can expect. Being able to edit posts (and give reasons), close out of hand threads (again, with reasons), and if needs be raise a poster's conduct up to an ARK or Cz himself should be sufficient.
