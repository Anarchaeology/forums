From lvl 50-100 (some players say 50-150), MOST professions have a hard time. Yes, this is why overequipping happens. Early in AO's life mobs weren't powerful enough. People were lvl'ing too quickly, thus making the life of AO short. FC made a mistake and made these mid to high lvl mobs insanely powerful. Hence, everyone said damnit, I need better equipment, blah blah, etc. Hello overequipping!

In early lvl's (early meaning 1-30 or so) red mobs can be killed solo if you've got on the right equipment and use good strategy.

Should we be able to kill yellow and higher mobs solo at all?

Hell no. I see yellow that means the mob is just like me in power (or should be). It means I'm gonna have a hard time taking it down, but if I survive, the rewards will be better then a green mob. I see orange, that means the chance of my survival is slim. I see red and I don't go near it unless I've got a person or people backing me up. (I never really understood how any solo mission should have anything above a green inside for FC's system of mob difficulty, but oh well) That's the way FC intended it, but because of a maybe too early release of the game or lack of proper testing, we have to deal with unbalances.

Can you solo all the time? Not the way things are now. FC stated that you can play AO solo, and I think if things were balanced, this could be true. Me, personally, I haven't soloed since lvl 30 with my first character, an MA. And with my soldier, heh, I didn't solo a day of her life, but I'm having more fun playing with a team. If things were more interesting, maybe AO would be worth it solo. IMO they aren't. Its the people that make this game fun, and playable. If I was still trying to play solo to this day, well, I'd slap myself and hit the cancel button because its just too frustrating.

Mobs with insane AC, HP, and initiatives: these are horrible. Granted not all are like this, and I'm strictly talking about human mobs here. If I see a mob with 'Seasoned' or 'Hardened" in their title, I run far far away. Lasersnipers are a pain too, as well as those big enforcers. They are way overpowered. This too high AC crap has been in the game long enough. FIX IT. Don't give a mob high AC, lots of HP, AND damn fast initiatives. That's just plain silly. Balance it. Give a big atrox mob high HP, give a scrawny MA mob high initiatives, give a low HP NT mob some good AC. This would make sense.

Damage shields and healing mobs: I entered a mission and I swear I'd stepped into the deepest pit of hell. 3 hardened class mobs ALL with damage shields, ALL healers. I wanted to cry. Even with a doc healing me we had to zone in and out at least 5 times to kill ONE. Okay, here's the deal with these damage shield things and my idea on how to lessen the penalty for us players when we come up against one. (this is all just an example)

You have a lvl 50 mob, using a ql 50 damage shield that does 22 damage on you every time you hit it. How many lvl 50 players are walking around using ql 50 damage shield nanos? Not many I guarantee it. Don't nerf damage shields, that would mean nerfing a profession and advents have enough issues. Instead drop the ql nanos that mobs have. Don't know how many ql's exactly, but less then what they are now. The only professions that are probably using nanos equal or better to their level are casters. (MP's, NT's, docs, etc). Those are the skills they're good at. But combat classes, hell no they aren't using nanos of the same ql as their lvl. So when you give mobs good skills all around, yes they will be uber, yes they will kick my ass, yes they will become monotonous to fight and what are you left with FC? A lot of frustrated customers. Please please please consider a change in this.

Monster mobs: Certain monsters are gonna be harder then the next. Ones to watch out for (and I honestly don't know if they're overpowered by design or if they need to be touched up along with certain human mobs, but they are mean as hell even when green): Those kitty cats, puppies, and worms on steriods. You've seen em, Black Death, Lesser, Greater (whatever) Grimwolves, Lambest Mambas (the list goes on). Stay away. A green wolf took out me and my soldier friend. (we are lvl's 103 and 105). Sorry, that's a little crazy. I can understand some mobs being strong/faster/more protected then others, but these guys have it all!

Despite these issues, I still have fun, but like I said, I'm a social player and never go off on my own. I think with some time, this game will be awesome, but its got a very long way to go yet. I know people are frustrated, and I know you've been told to be patient, so if you aren't happy here, or don't wanna wait for something to happen, then take a few months off. FC will respond the fastest when their income/player base decreases. Sad, but true.

Anyway, this post became a LOT longer then I intended, so I'm cutting it off here.

-Dezzie, 105 MA
-Aikera, 89 soldier
