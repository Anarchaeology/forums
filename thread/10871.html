Tradeskill "Combat"
Here's an idea I've been tossing around:

What about treating tradeskills as an actual combat sequence?

Example: I want to make a Treatment Center. First I buy all the neccessary equipment, then I take it to a secure location.

I then initiate "tradeskill combat" with a certain key item, let's say the nanojar. It starts with full "HP", and my HP is parsed from say my Nano Pool. We both begin "attacking" each other.

The cup's attack is actually the degredation of nano material within the jar as I am attempting to create the treatment center and keep the environment contaminant-free. It's damage is done directly to my nano, explainable as my usage of those nano's to redeem the environment of the jar.

My attack is actually the construction of the kit, by using a specific attack form (based on Pharma Tech), I repeatedly "strike" at the jar. Incrementally (let's say each time I win the combat), I install one more item neccessary for the creation of the treatment lab. If my nano pool reaches zero before the assembly is completed, then whatever materials have been used thus far dissolve into a worthless mass of contaminated material, and I get no xp.

BUT, if I actually manage to complete all the rounds of "combat" and finish the assembly, booyah! Tons of XP and a finished treatment center ready to sell.

Thoughts?

Have a Trade vendor give out a list of items he needs made, and a stack of parts (nodrop) in a small no drop pouch, you get xp as you build and a bonus xp when you turn in the order, the order will come with a pouch (assign a ID number like a mission key) that all the compleated items go in, and you give the bag back to the vendor(all items made are nodrop). BUT the risk involved is adding a little chance to fail. Tie the failur to a skill like Psycology for mechanical stuff, and treatment for med supplies. If you fail you loose a random part or part you were trying to install at that time, thus making you go out and purchase or in some way replace the missing item(s).

The compleated items can be in a QL range of the persons skills and XP determind based on the QL of the item. that way if a LvL100 guy came along and didn't raise his trade skills he would only get a order that he could compleate based only on his trade skill LvL, and only the xp based on the item, and not his LvL.
The main thing for this is that if a person would go the route as a Pure Trade class he would have to spend more IP in the trade skills, leaving him much too weak to be a combat vetaren.

I see nothing wrong with a weak trades person, in many cases people would have a separate trade charater if there was a alternative to gaining IP without fighting.
