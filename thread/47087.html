Hello all

Well, I _do_ wish to help FunCom who seems to be running out of resources rapidly; I'm not of the mindframe "don't buy the pack and show them, um, something" - I understand that running and improving game is expensive.

However, regardless of the price, the booster pack's _contents_ seem rather unattractive for me atm... I may be missing something, and if so, please let me know what it is .

first of all, I hear "better graphics". Well truth be told, with 1.1GHz Athlon and Original ATI Radeon 32MB, I'm not running full resolution already, and of course things slow down after a full team starts chain-casting nanos. second, I hear "land control". Now granted, I haven't been able to surmise much detail from the press release, but I'm wondering, as a true-neutral, why should I care? What's in there for me? Is it something that I should be excited about, or for that matter, that I can use at all? :-|
