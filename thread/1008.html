I have some serious concerns with the 15 second invulnerability period.

The invulnerability should be broken when the person moves. Otherwise, you'll have people with fast computers zoning in and out to keep themselves invulnerable and running around looking for a target. When they find the perfect target they'll attack, and the other player won't be able to attack back until they do.

As pointed out earlier, a person zoning into an area shouldn't have a huge advantage over those already in the area.

The solution to this is to have invulnerability break as soon as the person moves.
