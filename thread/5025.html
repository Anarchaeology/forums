New tradeskill item discussions
I know it's a bit futile to ask this, but let's try to keep this thread civil? :) I know this is a touchy subject, and I know that some weapon kits still don't work and all that other stuff. I don't want to talk about that stuff at the moment. (I also know that fixers are weak and agents can't sneak and all that other stuff, BTW.)

Ok, now with that out of the way.

There are many new interesting things in the new tradeskill shops, and I want to try to solve some of the mysteries of what they do.

The ones I am mostly interested in right now are:

Omni Multi-Form
Digital Camera
Identity Extractor (think that's what it's called)

I have found that if you use the id device on the camera, it will give you an item called "Personal ID" or something like that. (5xp, no particular skills needed unless it was complit or nanoprogramming). This is a nodrop item, which in the description says that is keyed with your personal data, and can be used as a key or as a digital signature. Interesting!

So I start looking around for something to sign my name on with my digital signature, and lo and behold there are Clan, Neutral, and Omni Multi-Forms in one of the shops. The description on those says they can be used as a reciept, contract, etc. Cool! So I buy one of those and try to sign it, no joy. Can't figure out what to do with it and it's driving me crazy. :)

Saw the new armor stuff in there too, making armor could be cool.

Anyone had any experiences with the new tradeskill stuff? Post it here let's talk about it.
