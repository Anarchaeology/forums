Both are good
The Vektor ND does great damage & lands great crits, & the Krutt 219 does fair damage & lands absolutely-through-the-roof-ludicrous crits.

Also, don't overlook the S.A. Home Defender (strong, even if you only use one) & the Windchaser OT SM30 (a little faster, & still hits hard). These are both just as good, & require shotgun skill only.

Whichever you choose, make sure you're using a low-light targeting scope. It will slow you down a great deal, but they're worth it anyway (even after they were nerfed). They require the weapon & mechanical eng. trade skills... which Traders can buff for you... but I wouldn't recommend having this done. Buy the generic +20 trade skill buffs, spend some IP, & do it yourself. Otherwise, you'll have that awful bright green lighting in effect all the time .

Look for the lowest-ql scopes that offer the best possible critical increase. Currently, my Trader uses a ql 49 scope that increases crits by +4 (the ql 48 scope only adds +3 to crit increase).

-Templar Red
