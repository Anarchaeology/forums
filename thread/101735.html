I went through that stage for a while where I regretted choosing Solitus.

But listen, we all have our strenghts and weaknesses. I find it's more important to know what your weaknesses are, aknowledge them and then concentrate on your strenghts in an attempt to make the best you can out of what you got.

I can't cast CH yet and I don't use a weapon (not until lvl 150 that is). However what I can do is HEAL! Whether each of us has more nano or more hp, has more special items for our breed, or an easier time geting into armor, the one thing that we all have in common is our ability to heal.

To me playstyle determines more of how good you are as a doc than what breed specific items you have or how big your nano pool is. In her own right a Lvl 20 Doc could outshine a Lvl 200 Doc given the right circumstances.

Just do things according to how you like to play. Remember there was some reason you choose Solitus over Nanomage during character creation. Just try to remember what that reason was and go with it. For me, I chose Solitus for looks. So what I like to sometimes do is go and get all dressed up and stand around in Omni Trade or Omni Ent. flirting with and buffing people. Not to say a nanomage couldn't do the same thing but I like to think I look better at it

My point is this game is similar to life in the respect that there always going to be choices you regret making and things you regret doing. And there's always going to be someone one step ahead of you. Just find your place and slide in there.

But this game has one BIG advantage over RL. You can always start over as a different breed. If you really don't like Soli, just re-roll a Nanomage. Sure it takes time but if you did it once you can do it again.

But in my opinon we're all Doc's regardless of breed, Lvl, equipment, etc.

I'm proud to be a Solitus, but more importantly, I'm proud to be a Doctor!

And that about sums it up, really... the jones vest is good, even great if you have a +18 or better one, for your nano skills.

But it's not the "top line" chest armor for casters anymore, really - azure is. And there's very viable other chest options at high levels, such as spiritual rites, omni-AF/sentinels, or at any level in the form of metallic mantis, and CAS.

The price of wearing a jones vest and shades of lucubration, though, is that you pass up on 1500-2500 points of AC and potentially between 250-500 or more additional hitpoints. You could also be passing up on -25% nano cost reduction (GPH and azure BP) and a bunch of additional NCU.

By the time you make it to the real high level game, you'll find that you can cast all the nanos you really need as an atrox, let alone as a solitus. You won't miss it if you can't cast Sentient Nano Gorger or the 60-second grid warp.
