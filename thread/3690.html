please re-think PVM a bit
The difficulty curve of mobs according to level needs to shallow out above 100. You could Log scale it which would make a TON of sense, or you could just shallow it out or you could custom design the mobs the way characters are designed with a set amount of IPs.

WHAT I WOULD REALLY LIKE, but Im sure its too much work for you guys, would be for you to rethink it all. Make mobs just like us, give them all the HPs and all the specials. Give them the same PVP limitations, 40% caps on specials, but no 50% off on either us or them. You could put the 40% cap on us too but maybe that's not a good idea considering the next point.

Now make a LOT MORE of them in each mission or in the great outdoors, and make them cooperate INTELLIGENTLY. If a mob becomes aware of my presence in a mission and I am fighting another mob let him join in. Teach them to do the tasks they do best. Make them call for friends and make them defend objectives. Make them move like we do instead of popping all over mission zones at 1000+ run speeds. Lower the exp they drop since they will drop faster.

What you will have then is a far more enjoyable game. Your first fix of making them have 4x hps or so made combat HARDER, but not INTERESTING. In fact it made it FAR LESS interesting. Giving them equal quality items was a nice GESTURE but not really RELEVENT to the issue of game balance. My challenge to you is to make combat INTERESTING. Make it dynamic by writing some GOOD AI for mobs, so they can think like us, make more of them, and make them equal to us in stats. Examples would be an Agent NPC vs a MA, make the agent root and back out of melee range. Been playing HALO a lot recently and MAN that is some of the best AI I have ever seen. Give those guys at Bungie a holler, I bet they are really nice. I know it's a very different type of game but I bet you could get a few good ideas from it.

Also just lose the really old and really buggy mission environments, you know the ones that mobs get stuck on everything, and they can't un-stick themselves? I mean its nice and all to run into an object on a wall for a time out to heal up, or use a boulder or stalagmite in the middle of a room to stick 2 mobs too while you kill one, but hey it's like stealing candy from retarded children. Sure they have more HPs. All that means is it takes me longer to kill them in these buggy environments. It's not more CHALLENGEING, it's boring. Also fix people running through walls, dying and falling thru walls and similar problems. They aren't game stoppers but they make the game look cheap and sloppy. make them interact with walls, slide down them when they die, and path the mobs through the rooms so they don't have to teleport through walls.

Also when is the story going to start? Last time I checked stories are dependent on CONFLICT. Conflict is that thingie that moves the plot along. Peace isn't really much of a start to a story. It seems to me more like a resolution to conflict. Kind of more like an ending. Hey didn't one of your DEV doods go to NYU film school or something? Did he ditch his screenplay classes? ACT 1, introduction of characters and conflict, STAKES RISE. We have been in act 1 since the release date, and the conflict has decreased. You guys need to spend some serious time on making this game a LOT MORE DYNAMIC. I remember reading all this propaganda about how we should brace ourselves for change come OCT 31, since stories are about change and here comes the story. Talk about an anti-climax. The only thing that has changed is a few bars have popped up a few funny dungeons, some new fashion in Miiir, and guards are so polite now.

Hope you don't read this as harsh criticism, but you guys have a VERY long way to go.
