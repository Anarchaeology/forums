QL 300 weapons/items/bots
I'm nowheres near being able to wield/use anything Ql 200 or near, as I just started playing AO like 3 weeks ago, but I've been reading these forums when I'm at school. I've noticed a general trend of there being *nothing* that can't be equipped after level 120 or so.

Once someone hits 100-120, they've got all the Ql 200 things they need equipped, due to wrangles and buffs, etc. By level 150, they've got everything self equipped that they need. There needs to be something out there that helps high level characters, and gives them something to work towards.

What funcom should do, IMHO, is make the max Ql of items 300 or so, so that a level 200 can weild something a level 150 can't. (obviously, the overequpping changes need to be implemented for this to work). I'm not sure what the skill caps are, but Funcom could raise the skill caps at later title levels in order to allow people to wield these.

This would also require some changes to classes like NT's and Engineers that aren't so dependent on QL items- sure, a super buffed engineer can make the slayers at level 83, but you should be able to fiddle with nukes and pets to balance them with the higher QL weapons.

Note that I'm not near level 100, so I don't really know what its like there, this is just based on what I read in these forums- so don't flame me for any inaccuracys :P
