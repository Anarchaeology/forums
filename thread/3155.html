Davyn, great idea - allow me to take it a little further.

Fightclubs. Truly excellent idea - small one on one arena (boxing ring) with the seating leveled steplike higher. This would be a place where something actually happened, and would give people a reason to come.

Gambling Halls. I think the FC designers could come up with some really cool stuff here, like leet racing, grilla monster fighting and the sorts. Just have someone in charge handle betting and you should be all set.

What would be even more involving, would be to give control of these places to the factions themselves. Say a faction that wants to run a club applies (danceclub, fightclub, gambling etc) for one at FC, and is then given control of it. This would mean they are responsible for things running smoothly, taking bets, arranging happenings and so on. There could even be some sort of gain to be had here if a factions runs a good club. Maybe getting a few creds from every "item" sold at the bar, or maybe a fee from every fight and so on (maybe even a covercharge to get in at happenings). This would encourage a faction to run their club well, handling all the promotions and making sure people had a good time - the more people that thougth a particular club was fun, the more people will come and leave their money.

This would also have factions more rooted in the game, and would give them an equal opportunity to make a name for themselves. And even more important, be a damn fun new addition to the game =)
