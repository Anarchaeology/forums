I agree that AO felt much more alive when there were the player created news and the storyline. Now, unless you are into pvp tower battles (which a lot of us aren't), the only difference between being Omni and being Clan seems to be which cities you can go to without getting guard aggro. Go to Temple of Three Winds and look at the average team--I was there from level 25 - 64 and almost all of my teams were mixed teams. No one cares if they team up with their mortal enemies or not--most don't even notice. Please, FC, make AO vibrant again. There are plenty of player events going on now and I'm sure there is no shortage of people who want to participate. Recently there's been Stret Spring Fair, a crat confrence, a doc confrence (starst in 50 min!), a wedding, a protest march, a beauty contest, a name the suns and name the moons contest, etc. All of this demands a news forum for reporting. There are a lot of MMOGs coming out and if AO wants to keep it's player base, it needs things like social events and a compelling story to keep people interested. If all most players see is camping and ad nauseum mission running, then what's their motivation to stay? They can get that type of repetitive play anywhere.

There have been some fantastic suggestions in this forum regarding how FC can bring this important element of the game back with minimal overhead so there really is no reason not to have it. Imagine if we had an Omni Professional and a Clan Professional and they were each tasked with overseeing things like the VOF. They could become editors-in-chief, taking submissions from the player community, editing them, and submitting them to FC who only has to read it once (taking only a few minutes of someones time) before they publish it. Furthermore, ARKs should be encouraged to and rewarded when they put on player events. I remember when I first started playing seeing screenshots of player events where there were maybe a hundred players fighting off some gigantic slug-like unique mob that was attacking somewhere. That was something I always really wanted to participate in (and something like that would go a long way to keeping many burned out gamers happy and their accounts active).

Heck, I'll volunteer my services as a former writer/editor for a college paper to help with this. Imagine, once a week a new edition of the 'newspaper' comes out full of news from last weeks events, tower battles, etc. You could even put in player make classifieds if you wanted. I'm sure there are some creative people here who would donate their services to make ads for things like Miiir Fashion and even faux coupons to Bronto Burger. Profile a different city each week in a Travel section. Have an advice collum where people write in with questions and get answers. And FC cold even have editorial cartoons drawn by real people and a screenshot of the week contest.
