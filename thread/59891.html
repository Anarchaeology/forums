Scouting Report - When oh When will my WenWen Win?... Oh Today!
OK, well here is my compilation of stuff that seems to be in the next patch. Feel free to post addition/comments. Oh, and please don't flame the req's / abcence of items, not that they aren't way off in a few areas, but there are allready a miilion threads about that. You can flame me if you want though

OK... lots of new changes in 14.6

First There is a new character creation screen, this is very coo,l, but hard to explain, and what is the engie doing to his bot?... Bah you will see.

Second, they have added a new static dungeon. This is accessed by the subways in the citites. It is for low level players, so maybe tifir can go

Your org name will now appear under our name when we walk about rubi-ka

Pet Class Mobs now can make pets when you fight them (tip go after the mob, not his pet, casue he will just make a new one. and the pet will deactivate after you kill the mob, as long as you don't attack it)

No more ninja looting, now when you loot, a msg appears saying who looted what in team chat.


New Health bar that gives you a better idea how much HP your opponet has.

Wen Wen's appear to be fixed now. Woot!

Enforcers finally get their Edged and Pircing buffs

Soldiers finally get their Full Auto buffs (but they are very hard to do.)


NT's get a +150 NT Buff

Atrox's will get more HP per a point in body dev then other breeds

Nano Mages will get more Nano points per point in Nano Pool then other breeds.

Stims's seem to work now. Never used one, now maybe I will

Fixer's have a new line of heals

Nanopool Buff for Doc's (+240)

Hered a rumor of a crat XP save buff (30 % I think)

Well that's what I've found so far. Here is a link to all the new items... looks like some new static quests in there.
