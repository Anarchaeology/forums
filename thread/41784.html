Thread About an Off Topic Thread
So,Ayvalen started a thread that basically was asking, why is there no patch information, when will we be seeing it, and what is taking so long. After a few days of no reply we all started getting a little off topic. Then we got a LOT off topic. Eventually CZ came along and offered this response

We have replied now, and the thread is going completely off-topic. Closing it.

If you want to continue any political discussion or such, please find another bulletin board.

What exactly was his reply you may wonder? Well, on that thread the reply was simply to tell us he replied. On the official community thread the reply was, no estimate yet.

Um, way to spoon out the information CZ. Maybe next time you want to wait a little longer before saying nothing, so that we have enough time to prepare for the shock. Honestly, why is it so difficult to pull information out of these guys? All we want to know is what is going on over there? That's never been answered. We all have our little theories on what it is, but we don't know because we're not being told.

My question is, how is it possible for the players to spend a year complaining about the lack of communication and still not have and meaningful change? There are lots of things people don't like about the game but the universal ones have always been game stability and a complete lack of communication on Funcom's part. The developers continually address stability (and I think they've done a great job), but the communication is still terrible.

Listen, all of us that had gotten off topic were in the Next patch forum. We would have gladly discussed the next patch if we had one to talk about. But since you don't feel the need to giver us something to talk about we'll discuss other things. We've been begging for information on it for more then a month. It isn't our fault you havn't given it to us.

Edit:
The least you could have done would have been to keep the thing open and move it to another forum. Then we could have continued.
