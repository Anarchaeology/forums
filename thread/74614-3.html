My 0.02
I'm a level 162 MA on RK2, and member of The Dojo, over 400+ strong, all MAs, and by far the best guild on RK2. I knew that MAs weren't the best PvPers in the world, so I took the ideal 'Leave it for the people who can do it." Worked fine for a long time.

Then the Notum Wars came.

Guild towers are a lifesaver, I'm not afraid to admit it. Got me to self-cast Kali and (almost) UVC by the time my nanos capped. Great fun.

Then all 3 of my guilds towers were torched in a 2-day span. All I was was cannon fodder.

And so were my guildies. You'd think that 10 high level MAs could take down 8 green others. I visited the reclaim so many times that battle it wasn't funny.

Shelving yourself is one thing, completely devastating an entire community is another. After a while, people started targeting our towers because they KNEW that The Dojo is MA-only.

That's also why I've seen so many people leave my guild; all because they couldn't stand being meatshields for Div-9 Bursts, or being shot full of aimed-shot swiss-cheese holes. Some joined guilds where there were other people to defend them, or in the case of those who couldn't stand it anymore, they simply up and left AO altogether (RIP Tubarolean). It's not fun to be faced with 'deal with it' when you've grown attached to your character. I know a lot of MA-main people who couldn't bear to start over from scratch. 40+ days of game time is not worth because you've been gimped, and as many others have stated, your other char might just be as well nerfed later.

I myself almost WANT to leave AO, it's so painful to play my MA now. If not for the friends I've made, I'd be free.

No heals, can deal with it. Damage shields can eat you alive, no problem. Nothing to look forward to, at least I can travel along.

When you forced us to PvP with Notum Wars, then stole away the last thing that made us viable, then you've obliterated an entire class.

Thanks FunCom.
