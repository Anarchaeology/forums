1. Puller tries to pull a MOB
2. MOB is pulled but team gets an add.
3. Add attacks someone whos using any form of deflect nano/bracer.
4. Trader, Crat or NT tries to calm the add.
5. Add gets calmed but hits deflect user at the same time.
6. Deflected dmg breaks calm
7. Loop from line 4.

Most of all higher lvl ppl Ive encountered know about this and therefore most of them dont use deflect when in team with a "calmer", but since this is supposed to be a newbie guide my opinion is that everyone should know about this from the beginning. =)

The other thing I tend to do alot in high level missions with adds is to taunt the add as soon as I see it coming in. That way I'll have at least 2 on me at the start of the fight. If my deflection shield hits a calmed add that was on me, it's not too much of a problem as I can TMS up.

As long as I keep as many mobs away from weaker team members, I'm happy and they're alive.

Also, one thing that we tried the other night was doing a L193 mission without a doc. 2 soldier's, an enforcer and an MA. We found that we could actually deal with most mobs be breaking down into two sub groups, with each soldier taking a mob each, and the enf and ma supporting them. It worked fairly well for a while, but considering that they were at least 40+ levels higher than us, we did suffer on occasion.

If you don't have a calm/mezz/root class in your team, work out 2 sub teams and try it that way. You'll find that it works pretty well.
