Runbuffs and bots don't mix.

I'm lucky, in that I have an understanding group of guildmantes to team with, but I've still encountered all the problems mentioned above. Engineers are fragile, the bot is your fighting power, forget pistols, shotguns, martial arts or anything else you might have short of slayer transference. If an orange mob aggros you and the bot's lost somewhere, you're dead. It's touch and go with yellows.

Solo, you learn how to move through rooms - slowly. You learn when to use /pet hunt and when to use /pet attack, and how best to position the bot so it doesn't get stuck in the doorway trying to aggro a mob in the next room. In teams, things are much more difficult, since even the most understanding team won't have a full appreciation of the rituals you have to go through to get a successful attack from your droid.

Lastly, there's the damage issue. Solo, your bot's enormous capacity for punishment means you can successfully carve your way through some fairly tough missions, as long as you don't mind doing it very slowly. In teams, the bot just doesn't do enough damage to be worthwhile. Mobs aggro players in preference to bots most of the time, which means that the bot doesn't even make a good tank for much of the time. Aggro trimmers work, but they work best on green mobs you don't really need a tank for anyway. Oranges are usually fairly resistant to their effects, which means that the bot makes a great tank right up until the point where you really need one, then it all goes a bit wrong.

The wierd thing is that although all of these issues drive me away from my engineer character time and again, I always end up coming back.
