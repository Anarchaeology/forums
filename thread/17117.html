First number is West-East, higher the number the more east.

Second is North-South, higher the number the more north it is.

Easy way to do stuff is have a dry erase board or grid matrix in front of you. Start with 0 and put 4000 along it. Plot your position and your desired postion on the coordinate plane. Then you can easily get your bearings.
