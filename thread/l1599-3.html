Anyone out there have any info or opinions on the relative proportion of different zone types? It seems there is a lot of demand out there for complete free for all areas, with total war going on, take your life in your hands but take no prisoners, knock down drag 'em out, fighting, and if you can't stand the heat, stay out of the kitchen. Ok, will 5% of the zones be that way, or 50%?
One of my concerns about people arguing against lvl restrictions is that while people who don't want to engage in pvp should have plenty of alternative areas to go to, but most importantly, people who *do want* to engage in pvp, but are only lvl 30, should be able to go somewhere to fight other players without having the constant worry of being attacked by characters 5 times their level. Sure, real wars don't respect such niceties, but let's face it, real people on the front lines of battle don't have experience levels, and pistols that do 4 times the damage as the other guy's pistol.
Coming from UO, I was turned off to PvP for a long time, because I was a craft character, and my miner was always getting attacked for his ingots. That's not pvp, that's war lords coming in and attacking villagers. It wasn't until I found a few friends and we went out with our adventuring characters on test servers where I found some of the joys of combat against evenly matched opponents. I'd like to do more of that in AO, but I really don't think it's fair to expect people who only play on weekends to wait 2 years until their chars are lvl 175 to go to a pvp zone so they can survive long enough to see whether they're any good fighting someone of their same level or not. Within a couple months, *some* people will be powerlevel gods, ruling the roost in any area where there are no level resrictions. Level restrictions should not mean restrictions that actually reduce the amount of pvp going on, it should encourage it.
If they keep level restrictions to political zones, and there are enough of those, then it sounds like most people will be able to PvP, whatever their level, as long as they're willing to just fight players from the other side. Unless we add a lvl restricted mayhem zone tho, two rebel buddies will never know which of them is the better fighter until they're lvl 180? How many different zone rules will it take to get to the center of a tootsie roll pop?
