Council of Testers
Over-equipping : A newbiew view
Having only recently started playing, I've been reading the forums and have
seen that this is a havily debated topic. So, needless to say I've decided
to give my 2ps worth.

Ideally, the game would have worked like DiabloII which disables any item you
no longer meet the requirements for and gives visual feedback of this. This
would discourage over-equipping as nanos would be of little use, and debuffs
could render half your equipment unusable.

However, the game doesn't work like this. I don't know if this was intentional
or not, but there we are.

(As an aside, if this wasn't intentional, then we have a bug which is being
exploited and should be fixed - though this would be problematic politically).

So, given the way the game does work, what can be done to reduce the effects
of the worst offenders without punishing 'joe average' who's only a bit
over-equipped?

The suggestion of reducing effectiveness for items you don't meet the requirements
for is probably best, allowing a bit of leeway. This could be done in stages with
clear communication so everyone was aware of what was being done. And being in
stages gives people time to adjust more easily. Start with a lot of leeway before
the decreases begin, then reduce this in the next patch, and again in the patch
after that, until we reach zero leeway.

Note that this should compare requirements and NOT levels, this allows fair and
honest use of better gear.

This reduces the effects of over-equipping, makes debuffs more effective, and,
most importantly, gives everyone time to adjust to the new regime.

And thats my view - over to you again!
