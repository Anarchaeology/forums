Some small, desired weapon changes.
1) Make the AC bonuses on item shields (not the summoned kind; don't have any experience with those) mean something. A QL 30 or so shield or buckler will probably only give you 10 AC in something. They're worthless for both their intended purpose, and as a damage dealer.

2) Add a new modifier to weapons: nano-interrupt %. I almost never manage to interrupt a nanoprogram. There's even an NCU add-on(-in?) componant that drastically reduces the chance for interrupts, but it's worthless, because how often do you manage to pull an interrupt off anyway? There would be two ways to handle this: 1) add a small percentage to every weapon, based on QL; this makes sense, because whether you whack someone with a chair or shoot them point-blank with a pistol, it would certainly be distracting. =p or 2) add significant bonuses to particular things; I'd like to see a bonus for this added to Aimed Shot, Burst and Full-Auto, and some melee weapons.

3) A line of martial arts-themed gloves (and possibly also boots?). The only thing resembling this is the stun glove, which you can't find until QL 60 and uses 1hBlunt, which kind of makes it unattractive to Martial Artists. The idea behind these are simple gloves of the normal armor kind, like plasteel or cyber or whathaveyou. They can only be equipped if you aren't wielding a weapon, in the same manner that you can't use MA special attacks when wielding a weapon. The gloves add N amount of base damage; normal would be melee, and perhaps other gloves could do other types of damage, like radiation or fire or such. The no-weapon workaround solves programming/balance problems so that a melee weapon character doesn't just gain some extra damage points for no good reason. It's also important to note that these don't -have- to be restricted to Martial Artists, if there was some public outcry of other professions wanting to use these.

I know there were others, but I can't think of them now. =p

edit:
mwaha! I remembered one of the others.

4) Damage shield penetration items/modifier on weapons. Damage shields -kill- MAs and fast, low-damage melee weapon users. The damage shield reflects -every- time a hit is made, which is simply murder when you rely on a lot of fast, low-damage hits to take out an enemy. This could either be a utility-slot item, like Flurry of Blows, which works for a limited amount of time and then locks up, or as a modifier on some weapons; the weapons have some special coating or design or nanoformula running in them that allows extra penetration capability.
