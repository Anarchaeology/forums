Make higher quality implants and nano clusters more available
Funcom, you should make available higher quality blank implants [ql125+] to the implant terminals and make the higher quality nano clusters [ql150+] appear more often in the terminals.

Yes, I do know that I can get higher ql blank implants from missions either by pulling a mission for them or having one drop off a mob. Heh, I've got bags full of ql150-200 implants in my bank, just cause I save em in case a guild mate needs one. Anyway...

Back to the point. It would be more convenient to purchase them from a terminal. Give us a superior shop that would actually live up to it's name - SUPERIOR. Have it cater to the higher level players, give us implants terminals that offer implants ql 125-200.

Next on the list, the nano cluster terminals. I don't like flying across the whole planet searching the "superior" shops for days on end for nano clusters to complete my implant sets. Also, I've yet to meet another player who likes to shop for nano clusters. No where on these forums have a seen a thread "Thanks FC for making my nano cluster shopping more timeconsuming."

Why do you think this is Funcom? If there were a way to make a all of the ranges of clusters available in a shop, I'd be all for it. Or better yet like this: in the beginner shop, have the nano cluster terminals have all the available clusters from ql1-ql50ish, the advanced stores stock them in ql50-125, and the "superior" shops stock them in ql125+

Make an interface where we could enter in a QL number and then it would make all of the possible clusters for each of the terminals (shining, bright, and faded). Players could have more time to play the game and spend less time on making implants or trying to go on a cluster goose chase.

I'm not gonna preach that your company sucks or whatever, I'm just making a suggestion. Of all the businesses that I've dealt with in my life, i'm only 24 y/o btw, the best ones always took care of their customers. They always looked at their suggestion box and fixed things that customers saw as wrong or not working quite right. This made them a better business. So please, stop dumping out your suggestion box everytime it gets full. Because one day, the only people that are going to be left on Rubi-Ka will be the NPC's. Maybe then you will read your 'suggestion box.'
