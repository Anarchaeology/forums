The thing is that this is not just a Shotgun Vs. SMG problem. It's a Shotgun Vs. Pistol, Bow, Assault Rifle (a few exceptions here), Rife (again a few exceptions here too), and SMG.

And fundamentally, this is all caused by speed and huge crit modifier. Especially the HUGE crit modifier seems to be a particular Shotgun and all things Shotgun trait.

Here are a few more examples of *good* weapons

Premium Ultra-Light Missile Pistol Raid 9-X is pretty viable high level weapon too, guess what? It has a very large crit modifier (850) and uses Shotgun as one of the skills too.

O.E.T. Co. Type 77a Lovemaker is another very good weapon... guess what? (581 crit modifier) Shotgun req again...

Premium Electrical Pacifier also has some high level users (601 crit modifier). There is the shotgun req again...

All these weapons have very good to extremely good crit modifiers. This trait (high crit) of shotgun is what's causing this dependency on shotguns at high levels. This is also the reason that crit buffs are the only deciding factor at high level.

Should it be fixed by nerfing shotgun? I certainly hope not... That will only make everyone's live harder. The again, it also doesn't make sense that we Fixers are only used to 250 (MPS) and 350 (MCS) as the crit modifiers when anyone can put on a shotgun based weapon and have 500 to 1000 as crit modifier.

Ok, I gave this some thought. Not that it really matter because I doubt things will change.

Bow: Low ROF, high dmg, moderate crit, moderate range. Options like aimed shot and bow special. You'd think this would be a stealthy weapon but theres no such thing in AO. So, I would make the strength of this weapon as being able to pick and choose your damage types easily. IE selecting different types of arrows to do different dmg.

Pistols: moderate ROF, Low dmg, low crit moderate range. Options like fling. Its a pistol for cristsakes. You can make exceptions and either up the dmg or the crit to make it better, but your best bet is that they can be dueled. I mean most of the prof's that are supposed to be using these anyway, aren't doing their main dmg with weapons.

Rifles: Low ROF, Moderate dmg, high crit, long range. Options like aimed shot. Its a rifle, the sniper's weapon. If you start getting into high ROF then you're not a rifle you're an assualt rifle.

SMG: High ROF, low dmg, low crit moderate range. Options like burst and FA. Like pistols, low dmg and crit, but with more options and faster firing. I'd say about half should be able to duel weilded.

Assualt rifle: Moderate ROF, Moderate DMG, Moderate crit, moderate to high range. Options like burst and FA. Its an assult rifle, does everything well but doesn't excel and one thing. Plus it has damaging options.

Shotgun: Low ROF, Moderate to high dmg, Moderate crit, moderate to short range. Ahhh the infamous AO shotgun. I can tell people are looking at the low ROF and saying, "What about the Bennely?" That would be an ASSAULT SHOTGUN (shotgun assualt rifle combo), its the exception, not the norm. This example is also very different from how it is in AO.

I outlined this as a guide of maybe how things should be and I used real life as sort of an example. However, as a disclaimer I really didn't put an incredible amount of thought into balancing these completely among themsleves and among the professions that should naturally use these types of weapons. Hopefully this will start a dicussion on this topic.
