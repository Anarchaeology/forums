Might as well post a reply, since I have managed to get the item that you need.

You need a Blood-Stained Dog Tag from a legchopper clone. You can't solo these guys, they are tough. Not sure if legchopper himself drops one of them, but if he does that's a bonus.

Legchopper clones has 5-6 different spawns, but I don't know the loc and the one I know about is withheld until those I know have gotten their mission done.

Get the Dog Tag and trade it with the captain and you are done.

He despawns after that, but he's only gone for 5-10 minutes.

Enjoy people
