A story to be told
We are..
We are all innocent
We are all innocent
We are..
We are..

She awoke with a start realizing that she had fallen asleep again. She thought to herself that she would never get this project done if she kept falling asleep. lights the word no sooner came from her mouth than the lights in her apartment blinked on with full radiance. Squinting lights to one quarter she said aloud, when the lights had dimmed she opened her eyes to find herself at her drafting board. She asked herself when was the last time she had actually slept in her bed and not at her drafting board. She realized that it had been weeks almost a month since she had seen her bed let alone seek to sleep in it. She rose slowly wondering what had startled her awake from her sleep that, for once didnt have that horrible nightmare in it. She slowly rose from the board covered with technical drawings and specifications that had been her bed for so long. Rising out of the chair unsteadily while reaching for her now cold cup of coffee drinking it down not noticing the pungent taste from the now cold beverage. Then she heard it again the almost imperceptible knock at the door that had startled her awake. She told the intruder to hold on that she would be there in a minute to let them in. A voice from the other side of the door replied that it had a package for her and the package required a signature for delivery. Grumbling to herself she moved slowly to the door looking about the mess that was her workshop and home. Eyeing stacks of technical journals scattered throughout the place in piles some neatly stacked other just thrown down as if to read them later.

She reached the door and peered through the peephole seeing only a man in a brown jumpsuit it seems that even this company was still going strong. Opening the door she saw the man was holding a medium sized package in his hands with a clipboard on top. He maneuvered the package to where he could hand her the clipboard she took it without saying a word and signed her name Katelin Boothby. She handed the clipboard back, he took it with a smile looking at it and handed her the package with a nod telling her to have a nice day. She closed the door behind her and locked the bolt, what she needed now was a shower and a hot cup of coffee to start her day. She glided over to the counter and placed the package on it never looking at it or where it came from. Her only thought was a hot cup of coffee and a shower. She went through her daily routine of putting the coffee on to brew then headed to the shower. The hot shower had felt good to her. She was starting to feel like she hadnt had a shower for over a week. Then to think about it when was the last time she had had a shower. After she got out of the shower she went through her normal routine of brushing her hair bringing it back into a ponytail like she had done for ever. With only one thought on her mind a fresh cup of hot coffee.

The man walked quickly he had more deliverys to make and was not going to be late; he had never been late before on a delivery and didnt plan to start being late now. When he made it back to the delivery van he noticed a man approach him from the corner of his eye. The stranger stopped him and asked him if he had a package on his van that was destined for a Katelin Boothby, he went on to say that he had sent the package and was trying to intercept it before it got delivered. The office had told him that it was out on a van somewhere but that they didnt know where so he thought that he would wait here to get it before it was delivered. The deliveryman informed him that he had just delivered it to her and he was sorry about it and that he had more deliveries to make. The stranger looked crestfallen and turned thanking the deliveryman for his time. The deliveryman got into is his van and started to drive away thinking to himself what a peculiar fellow that man was. Nobody seemed to notice the stranger staring up at the high-rise for some reason. Though they did notice the heated discussion he did have with his wrist. But they ignored it; many things were best left ignored.

She hurriedly put on her favorite oriental style dress. It was a black dress with a gold dragon arching on the bottom as if to attack and gold trim up the collar. She had seen it in one of the shops and decided that she couldnt live without it. One thought though still consumed her mind and one whiff of its aroma remaindered her she had just started to brew a pot of coffee before she took a shower. She slipped into a pair of nylons and into her favorite pair of sandals and glided towards the kitchen. As she poured herself a cup of steaming hot coffee she started to look for her cigarettes, she finally spotted them on the coffee table buried under a stack of papers she was looking at last night. She then headed to balcony she hated smoking inside her apartment. Opening up the sliding door let a blast of cool air as she stepped out onto the balcony to look around. The clouds loomed overhead like a dark grey shadow threatening to unleash their torrent of rain. Fall was in full swing on Caledonia III and it would only get colder she thought to herself as she lit her cigarette. As she sat down to drink her cup of coffee and smoke, her mind kept wondering back to the package that she had received that morning. She just couldnt figure out what was in it or who could have sent it. Laying her cigarette in the ashtray she went in to retrieve the package.

A faint beeping from a large stack of papers alerted her that someone was trying to get a hold her via her wristcom, ignoring it she moved towards the package on the kitchen counter. Walking back out to the balcony she sat back down and began to examine the package. It looked like a plain cardboard package with no return address. How odd she thought there was no way to know who sent it. Taking a long drag on her cigarette she carefully started to open the package first by putting a fingernail under the tape to cut it, then by running her finger the whole length of the edge fully cutting the tape in two. She pulled the side flap back and put her hand inside finding a small rectangular object that she gently pulled form the box. In her hand she held a small rectangular block about 4in. by 5 in. and 2 in. deep. Examining it she noticed a small latch which she promptly opened, to find a small wind up music machine. She had heard about music boxes from her mother, she had even seen them in the museum but she had never seen one this close. A gust of wind reminded her that she was outside, gathering her coffee cup and remembering her now burned out cigarette she headed inside.

As soon as she stepped in the door she heard the beeping of her wristcom again and wondered what it was that was so damn important for her to be bothered on her day off from the lab. Laying the music box on the coffee table she immediately began digging through papers until she came upon her wristcom unit. Putting it on and punching the answer button she found she was looking at her assistants face on the small view panel. Fredrick Hereda was a Tall handsome man that not very many women could resist. A man with a smile that could make a woman melt, or so she had heard the young interns say. Though to her he was and will always just be her research assistant. He was much like her taking, top honors from the Caledonia University College of Engineering. They said a genius in the making, someone who would shake the foundations of engineering science. She always wondered why he wanted to be her assistant with his honors he could have had his own research lab to himself, but yet he chose to just be a research assistant. He looked quite agitated though for a Tuesday morning. She let out sigh and asked him why he was bothering her on her day off. The voice form the other side went on to say that there had been an accident at the lab and the lab was in a shambles. She asked him what had happened. He told her that he thought it had been the new drive system prototype that overheated when they tested it but was not sure; he suggested she come down to the lab and see for herself. She sighed and said that she would be there in an hour she had a few things to take care of here that she wanted to take care of first. Fredrick nodded and said that he would send everyone home for the day and wait for her. Pushing the end button she got up and started to tidy up her apartment, after all it better be tidy the holidays were coming and she still had to throw the lab party like she does every year. Grabbing her purse off the kitchen counter she started for the door. As she grabbed her coat from the coat closest she decided that she would take the music box with her, maybe she can puzzle it out at the lab while she was there, since she expected to be there almost all day while they looked for what has caused the accident. Though she hoped she might be able to slip away and get some shopping done today. Reaching the door she turned the lights down to almost nothing but with a word. Walking out into the brisk December air she turned and placed her eye near the retinal scanner and with an audible click the closed door locked.

Walking to the penthouse elevator she couldnt help but wonder what it was that went wrong. A burnt out heat sensor, a corrupted thermal coupler? Or did the anti matter injector nozzle freeze up, maybe it was a combination of things. The elevator doors opened to admit her and she lightly pressed the button for the garage. As the elevator started its moderate descent to the parking garage she decided that she would think nothing more until she was able to inspect the damage for herself. The elevator doors opened to the parking garage on the 40th floor fingering her wristcom she heard her yalmaha start up and begin to warm up. She couldnt help but wonder how today had started out as a strange day, with a mysterious package arriving for her and then this. Oh well it can only get better she thought as she climbed into her yalm and headed out the garage towards the lab. She failed to notice a loan figure watching her. After her yalm had cleared the garage he raised his wrist up to his mouth and spoke softly and headed towards the elevator.

The trip to the lab was uneventful and boring as it was always. She flew once around the lab and found to her dismay that there had been a hole blown out the building and the roof partially collapsed where the drive technologies lab had once been. When she had landed Fredrick was there to greet her with a report in his hand, that was fast she murmured as she flipped through the pages looking at the hastily put together report of what he had found so far. They started to walk towards the drives lab as if was known to everyone. there was one thing that I didnt mention on the com link.. Fredrick said aloud, she stopped walking waiting for him to finish there was one person injured in the blast. It was Dr. Yakima I called the ambulance as soon as it all happened and the hospital just called before you arrived saying that he was alright. She breathed a sigh of relief that there hadnt been more to it than just one injury. We were lucky to only have one injury Fredrick I hope we dont have anymore accidents like this one again, or the next time someone might not walk away from it. Considering the nature of the work we do here, the whole building could have gone up along with it. They both walked in silence towards the building as she finished reading his hastily put together report.

The man walked slowly to the set of elevators keeping a watch out to make sure he hadnt been spotted or seen by anyone who thought he shouldnt be here. He chuckled to himself if they thought that then they would be right. The elevator didnt open for him he had expected it since the pressure plate on the floor was set to her weight. Pulling a slender cord form his wristcom he plugged it into the data port on the elevator and began to hack the controls. After several unsuccessful tries he finally made it muttering under his breath about how easy this was supposed to be easy so far my ass he muttered to himself as the doors finally opened to admit him. He punched the key for the penthouse and waited as the elevator ascended to the penthouse. He thought to himself all he was here to do was retrieve an object and nothing else. For some reason his employer wanted nothing else touched, strange very strange indeed he thought. Though there seemed to be something strange about this employer he said that he wanted a package back that he had sent to this lady, he wondered why not just get it back himself, go up to her and ask for it back. Why higher someone of his talents to get it back unless he didnt want her to know who sent it. Oh well it will be an easy job, easy money in the bank. The elevator doors opened to the penthouse hallway, the man quickly walked the distance to the door keeping an eye out for cameras and other surveillance equipment. To his luck there was none, maybe this would be an easy assignment after all. His employer had given him a small envelope and told him that he would know when to use it. Opening the envelope he slid out what looked like a retinal ID card. Not just any kind of card though only the one time use types that the laser beam destroyed as it was read. A shame he thought, what he could do with one that wasnt disposable. Putting the card up to the reader activated the scanner and the man watched as the card slowly disappeared in front of him as it was read. A beep from the reader signaled it was done and an audible click from the door signaled that he could enter.

The man slowly entered into the main room of the apartment. The lights were dim he figured around 10% radiance enough for what he needed to get done. Slowly he crept through the apartment first checking the living room then the bedroom and dinning room for the package. He was about to give up when he remembered the balcony. He crept to the balcony and sure enough it was there. Sliding the door open he grabbed the package, sliding the door closed behind him he headed for the door to leave, thinking to himself this was an easy job for a man of his talents. Grabbing the second retinal card from the envelope he quickly closed and locked the door behind him and headed for the elevator. He took the elevator to the air garage on the 40th floor then switched elevator to go down to the main floor, you can never be too careful he thought to himself. Walking out the front lobby he waved to the guard and wished him a merry Christmas. Walking out the door he headed west along the sidewalk in the freezing drizzle that had began to fall, to where he was to meet his employer. Walking at a brisk pace it only took him 20 minutes to reach the spot, with him doubling back to make sure he wasnt being followed. The limo was waiting like he had been told; waiting no longer he strode up to the limo as the window rolled down he handed the package through the window as a gloved hand proceeded to give him a thick envelope, he turned around to walk away as he opened the envelope he reached in and dragged out nothing but paper worthless paper. Enraged he turned around just as the first shot hit him square in the chest, followed by another shot near the first. A look of complete horror and rage contorted his face at the realization that he had been double crossed. He stood there for a minute not saying a word or moving till he fell to the ground face first, his face still contorted in a sick mask of rage and terror. His only thought as he watched his life blood flow from his chest mixing with the water that fell from the sky, was that this shouldnt be happening to him, this shouldnt be happening this was supposed to be an easy job. Darkness slowly overcame him as more of his blood flowed freely into the street. A voice inside the limo murmured so much for good help these days as the empty box was tossed out the window landing near the now cooling corpse. Driver we are done here lets go. With that the limo slowly pulled away from the corpse, rolling down the alley and finally pulling out into the street and picking up speed as if it had never been there.

They had wasted no time when they got inside the lab with the investigation; still it had taken the rest of the morning and the better part of the afternoon. From what she could tell it had been a malfunction in one of the plasma conduit thermo couplers. But she couldnt tell for sure till she spoke with everyone, and had more time to go over the wreckage. Till then she would order the lab sealed and a guard posted till tomorrow when she could get some more sleep and attack it from a different angle. She told Fredrick to contact everyone and have them meet here tomorrow for a debriefing on what had happened today. Till then he was to enjoy the rest of the day. Sitting down at her desk she made the necessary calls to have a few guards posted at the lab and waited for them to show up. During the hour it took them to arrive she decided to go over the report once more to see if she might have missed something. A knock at the door startled her from her reading as sergeant Mallory walked into the room to report that his security team had arrived. She stood up and told him that she didnt want anyone near the lab and that under no circumstances was anyone to touch anything in the lab not even someone from his team. The sergeant nodded and went about issuing orders to his men. After he had finished he turned back and asked if that was all. She nodded and told him that she was headed home and if that there was any disturbance to call her right away. The sergeant nodded and told her that he would. As she opened the door to the outside of the lab she noticed it have been raining. Touching a button on her wristcom she could hear her yalm start. As it moved to the overhang near the building she remembered that she hadnt been shopping yet for her food for the annual lab party. She climbed in and headed towards the nearest grocery store to pick up the supplies she would need. Mallory watched as the yalm slowly moved out of view, when it was finally out of view he turned and went back inside locking the lab door behind him.

The drive home from the grocery store was uneventful as always it was. Taking the groceries out of her yalm she headed towards the elevator. On the way up the elevator she remembered the tiny music box in her purse, with al the excitement she had forgotten about it. Finally she would find a little bit more about it. Putting her eye next to the scanner activated it with its usual hmm and then the resounding beep followed by a click from the door to signal it had unlocked. Carrying her groceries inside and murmuring for the light to go to one quarter radiance, she then closed and locked the door behind her. Putting her coat in the coat closet and dropping her purse on the coffee table she started another pot of coffee to brew while she put her groceries away. Once her groceries had been put away she poured herself a cup of coffee and grabbed her purse and made her way to the couch. Setting her coffee down she proceeded to pull the small music box out of her purse. Sitting on the couch and laying her purse on the coffee table she proceeded to examine the music box more closely. It seemed to be made of a dark reddish color wood one would almost say it is cherry, or a red maple but cherry seemed more appropriate. Opening it she saw that is was lined with burgundy velvet. There was a ballerina on the inside of the box in a white raised material. She guess it was Opal for it seemed to glimmer when she turned it in the light. Turning it around she noticed a small key, she figured this was to wind it up and preceded to wind the key till it wouldnt wind anymore. The song that proceeded to come from the music box was a haunting melody that she was sure she had heard before though she couldnt place it. After hearing the first half of the song it finally came to her, it was a song about innocence lost and how everyone is innocent at one time, and how that innocence is lost forever. And then her world fell apart.
