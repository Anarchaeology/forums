Hate to say this but...
... you CAN get to missions without Yalms... you simply need to:

A) Take a group... preferably one with a speed buff
B) Max your own run speed (even if it is dark blue) and get run-speed implants. Once you get to a 300ish run speed, you'll be able to outrun a LOT of mobs.
C) Be careful. I ran a level 6 from Athens to Omni on foot (that was hard). Once you have a map upgrade and have the map of the hunting area, avoiding mobs is not hard.
D) Accept the fact that you can and will die sometimes if you try a mission in a bad area. I bit it once on a mission to Southern Fouls Hills when I was in my 60s because I ran the wrong way when I got a mob chasing me.
E) Avoid taking missions to areas that are difficult to run through. As an Omni, you can get missions to Clon**** all the way into your 80s, no sweat. There is really noplace in Clon**** you can't get to on foot if you're paying attention.

If you do these things (even some of these things), a Yalm is not necessary. I lived without mine for almost 70 levels without much incident. They are very nice and they are "game changing", but I feel they are entering the world at the right pace.

No
It's not a matter of not being able to get to missions safely. At this point, I have a pretty good idea where the "bad neighborhoods" are, and I don't mind being wrong once in a while.

It's a matter of not being able to get to missions in time to complete them, before I have to do something else in real life.

Fixer, using Grid Runner, just short of 500 run speed. Even ignoring all missions that are several maps away, it's not once in a hundred that I get a mission that isn't on the far side of the playfield from where I am or where any grid terminal or whompa is. And even at something like 480 run speed, it takes me probably ten to fifteen minutes to get from the mission terminal to the mission. Fortunately, with Grid Phreak, it takes less than three minutes to get back (10 seconds to run the buffs, 90 seconds to run GP, 10 or so seconds to zone into the grid, a few seconds to cross it, 15 or 20 seconds to zone into Tir ... it all adds up).

If I get one of those brown cave missions the size of Omni Trade, it's likely to take me an hour and a half to finish the mission. Onto this, tack what, half an hour of travel time? And all of that spent watching trees, grass, and grey mobs scroll by, occasionally hitting the "z" or "c" key to go around an obstacle?

That's not fun. That's commuting. It's boring. Especially on foot.

Sure, I could power-level. Run fifteen to thirty minutes to the nearest spawn-camp near my level, hope to prime to get into a group, then spend the same ninety minutes going up a level or two. /camp, next time I'm in, hope to find another group, level up a time or two more ... until I need better armor, or a better weapon, and find that I'm screwed. Why? No rewards, no money. So now I'm stuck doing missions again ... with gimped armor and and a gimped weapon.

That's not fun. That's aggravation.

I've got a level 55 martial artist. I looted a QL32 Yal, not during the Great Used Vehicle Sale, but before it, when it wasn't common, but still not terribly rare. I'd say that back then, 1 in 4 characters managed it between level 25 and level 50. With that character, I can fly anywhere on planet in 5 minutes, which makes missions a lot more feasible. It doesn't guarantee I can survive there. If I have to zone out to heal, I need to be able to do it on foot, and red aggro mobs can still kill and eat me. It doesn't make it totally safe. It just makes it possible without having to commit an entire day to it. If I weren't bored beyond tears by playing a martial artist, I'd stick with that character just for the Yalmaha.

Life's little ironies: I've also got a level 40ish MP who looted a Yal, during the Great Used Vehicle Sale. But I'm bored and aggravated with playing an MP, too. So it means that the only two characters I have with Yals, by bad luck, happen to be on two professions that I found deeply un-fun to play. *sigh*

Suggestion: Split the Difference
So obviously we need something between a Kodaik and a Yal. And I can understand why Funcom doesn't want everybody to have a Yal. From 45 meters up, you don't see much of any of the landscape, and they worked hard on that landscape. Heck, from 45 meters up, the playfields look awful; they weren't meant to be seen from that high up. And you get to bypass nearly all the risk. They want that to be something you worked hard to get.

(Or cheated hard, and got away with it. But I digress.)

So fine, let's add another vehicle to the game: something that's:

  • commonly available from QL30 up,
  • for around a fifth of the price of a Yalmaha (which is still a lot of money), and
  • with Vehicle Air requirement of around 80 at QL30 to maybe 400 at QL200, and
  • that moves at a speed about three quarters of what the Yal does, and
  • can't fly higher than 10 meters above ground.


That puts it solidly in range of mobs and PvPers with ranged weapons (including rooters!) and some of the larger melee mobs, and keeps you down where you see the scenery. But it lets you get to the mission in more-or-less straight lines, and at an acceptable speed. And it still leaves the Yalmaha as the ultimate luxury, and clearly superior, and worth the money.

Cosmik, or CZ, would you please run this idea past the developers, and at least let me know what they said?
