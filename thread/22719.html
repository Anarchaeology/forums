Well, I have a major complaint with the general idea that the higher in level you get the longer missions should take. Most people only have a fixed amount of time that they can play per session and that amount does not go up as you level.

The other problem is that missions just get boring after a while. There is no way that a player is going to have fun doing the exact same thing for 3+ hours.

Missions should take no longer that the amount of time that the average player is willing to spend per playing session. In fact it should be even less than that because even at that limit you will still have missions that are too long for many players.
