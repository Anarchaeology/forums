14.5.0 delayed - 'official' discussion
Seeing seven threads about the patch being delayed, I thought I'd might as well make an 'official' thread for it.

Let me start the thread with our Director of Operations' post on AO Basher about the delay:

quote:
Just wanted to take a moment to clarify why the patch was postponed with such short notice. While testing 14.5, a few server crashes were detected (2) over the weekend, these were researched, a fix developed on Monday and implemented internally Tuesday morning, and patched to Test Live Tuesday afternoon. This patch, addressed the server crash but as we discovered, Wednesday morning this patch had an adverse effect to certain attacks. The problem was quickly identified and a fix implemented Wednesday morning and delivered to internal QA an hour before the scheduled time to start patching 14.5 to the live servers. Essentially not leaving enough time to complete internal QA let alone allowing it to be tested externally before bringing it to the Live servers. For this reason, we opted to delay 14.5. Now there are a few other factors involved here as to why we decided to wait until the following Wednesday to bring 14.5 to the production machines vs. attempting to distribute the patch Thursday or Friday. Without getting into specifics, from lessons learned in the past we do not patch unless the developers will be available post patch to address any emergency issues that arise. So Friday is out, and Thursday wasn't good either because we are in the process of moving the Oslo offices this weekend.

l0k3y, sorry to hear that you've opted to cancel your accounts over this delay. We are just as distressed over this as you are... I am an avid gamer myself. I've played Meridian59, UO, EQ, AC, AO, DAoC (briefly), and am currently helping to beta test a few others. I am aware of the intervals that other MMORPGs release their patches and what their patches tend to contain. Just a side note here, yes there is room for improvement on AO patch deliverable rates: we tend to be a little overzealous in what we want to deliver. Which results in the patches growing beyond what we first envisioned. Ideally we strive for a 4 to 6 week release cycle. Additionally there are many factors that the general public is not aware of that have effected our patch cycles lately. Lastly, please take a moment to compare the content that is delivered from all MMORPG patch cycles when making an assumption about anyone cycle in particular.

This delay was not something that we desired any more that the rest of you, and we appreciate those who have expressed their understanding and also those who have voiced their frustrations. Both positive and negative feedback is welcomed so long as it remains constructive. We are gamers just like you, we appreciate a pat on the back from time to time, and expect to have or posteriors lit, occasionally, as well if we drop the ball.

Respectfully,

Adam "De`Valos" Young
Director of Operations
Lead Database Admin/Engineer
Funcom Oslo AS
