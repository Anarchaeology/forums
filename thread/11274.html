Realism
I think that the game is realistic enough. In fact, I don't know of any game that is as realistic. The problem I see is in two areas: restrictions in areas that affect roleplaying, and a world that the players have no power to affect.

By restrictions, let me give you some examples: limit to items in apartment, no tables/chairs for apartments, restrictions on wearing social clothes, restrictions on how you can initiate combat with another person (for roleplaying purposes), I love emotes, but there should be more, there are just not enough objects that can be used for roleplaying purposes that actually do anything!

In terms of a static world, I would like to: capture an outpost and have it mean something, plant a tree, rent shops or merchants, affect political structure or control, create new objects that people use for combat or roleplay, be able to initiate roleplaying scenarios that have people interacting with the world.

Oh, and more than anything, I would love for a mission to actually have a purpose other than just give me an item and a little cash. These missions have no real purpose, lets just face it. Even if they just kept statistics about how many missions were completed in what areas of the world and dynamically changed what was happening in those areas because of it.

Also, I think that there should be some sort of penalty for not completing a mission.

Probably not what you were looking for ...

Personally, I think realism is a hook for roleplaying, but it's also got a very thin line between "just right" and "overdone".

In AO, I like how they've made realistic actions that have no actual bearing on gameplay, such as eating and drinking. This makes such actions casual, and also helps you label yourself as "In-Character and RolePlaying" when you perform them in public. Just like dancing. All the other emotes, for that matter, add just the touch of realism that is needed to bring someone that much closer to their own character's persona.

Then there are the realisms that simply shouldn't be, like making it neccessary to eat. Thank goodness this is not so, because it would be nothing more than a pain in the neck. Activities that we do every day in real life in order to sustain ourselves are not activities that I want to have to simulate when I'm playing a video game, ESPECIALLY an mmorpg. I'd like the option of emulating these actions for social purposes, nothing more.

As far as fighting and combat, I lapse back into the "there is such a thing as too realistic". The neccessity for combat is something that even Funcom has admitted to not being a part of a perfect game. They've stated that while they would like to implement features through which a character may advance him or herself without fighting, doing so would run a severe risk of causing a LOT of imbalances in the game (moreso than there already are).

In any case, I don't mind having to fight. In real life, we go to our day job. In AO, we kill stuff. Wouldn't have it any other way.
