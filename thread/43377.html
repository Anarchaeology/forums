OpCentre(RK1) is Recruiting
Faction: Clan

Government: Republic

Description:
Perhaps the longest standing clan intelligence agency, OpCentre is dedicated to assisting the Council of Truth with it's intelligence requirements.

Objective:
OpCentre's objective is to assist the Council of Truth with intelligence operations against Omni-Tek and any other power that is yet to emerge that threatens the basic human rights of the Clans.

History:
OpCentre was conceived in June 29,474AD when offworld interest in Rubi Ka and Omni-Tek's operation there reached a peak. An office was established in February 29,475AD and after much preparatory work was deemed operational with a core of Staff in December of that same year.



"The Horde"

- It's quieter in the back of the transport and Grey addresses the team: "On behalf of myself, OpCentre and our sponsors, welcome to Rubi-Ka ravenous horde!" This is greeted by the specops warcry. Grey grins. Just like old days. "Those of you that don't know me - don't worry. I don't have executive powers over you, I'm just the party mixer. We have all operated, indeed still operate under the motto "de oppresso libre" and people, you will find a lot of that here. Your skills are desperately needed. The locals are all fired up but by and large they lack the expertise and discipline to conduct a successful guerilla campaign. We will change that, like we've always done before....." Another cry.

- The Grey Man



OpCentre is recruiting primarily agents, but is also looking for other professions to perform specialist roles(R+D, LRP, Deep Recon, Fire Team Members, etc.) If you are lvl 50+ and interested, please contact Greyman, Apathetik or Mardelis.
