I am not familiar with the functions of Auctionbot, but I understand it serves a purpose.

Omnibot, and it's older sister Shopbot serve a very useful purpose above and beyond running auctions. These bots have the ability to notify you, by /tell, of items that you ask it to look for. That means that instead of trying to sift through all the junk on the shopping channels for what you really want. You can let Shopbot and Omnibot do it for you. Since you are notified directly of a match, you can then turn off the shopping channel, or go do something outside the cities, without missing that important offer when it happens.

I find a lot of Shopbot's advertising to be in bad taste, but it serves its purpose. These are tools the programmer created to be used, and the more people using them, the better (to a point). Most of Rubi-Ka wouldn't have even known it was there, if it didn't advertise itself.
