And what is the rationale behind this? Ross and Radiman decide that they're going to have a one-day war every 7 days? How about one day a week (let's say Friday) you guys all log on to a Counterstrike server?

It's been said enough times. People don't want PvP for no reason. Having a million more chances to get ganked simply because 'it's Friday' does not sound appealing.

On the other hand if the Dust Brigade managed to sabatoge the suppression gas generators and it took some time to get them back online, I'm sure a lot of memorable encounters might ensue.

The more I see people on both pro-PvP sides of the argument, the more I realize that the two sides can NEVER come to a consensus.

The one side sees the world as populated with a bunch of X's and O's and if yer X and see an O you shoot it. Anything that prevents the quantity of O's that you are allowed to shoot at is a detraction to your enjoyment of the game.

The other side says if you give me reason to do you harm and I'm assured some sort of positive result out of assaulting you I will do so. Preventing me from using force to protect my ideals is lessening my enjoyment of the game.

Both encourage the presence of PvP. Neither side's argument above is necessarily colored with judgement of the systems' 'fairness' or 'balance'. The major difference is that one portion just wants to run around with their gun and shoot everything that is not an 'X'. This is the reason for playing the game for them. To shoot non-X's. The other side sees PvP as an integral part of a true-to-life SciFi game. They see when battle is required, when peace is required, and tend to take a more realistic approach to picking who they target to do harm to.

I can't honestly say I did more than chuckle at the suggestion that Fridays become Gankday on Rubi'Ka 'simply because'.
