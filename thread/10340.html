Dimension Switching ideas?
K so we know now that dimension switching can be done. Why they havn't implemented it remains to be seen, but lets not point any fingers quite yet.

So here are some of our ideas for impelementation. A command is made to switch dimensions (lets call it /command switch). But something has to be done to prevent people from switching to see an event on the other dimension. So, you add a delay. Player types /command switch, 8 hours later (or more 24 maybe) he's notified his passage to RK1 has been prepared, pack your bags cause it's a bumpy ride.

Can anyone think of other potential issues? Over-population and the like?
