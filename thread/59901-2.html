quote:
Not to flame the developers at all but I sometimes wonder why they wasted the time making certain weapons that are so obviously completely inferior and useless compared to other weapons.
Not reading it as a flame either The explanation so many of those weapons are of little use - is that they were mostly made during beta and the release of AO.

There are many reasons they are of little use now, the game has changed much since then, and some of those weapons were simply not good designs. Weapons in AO are quite complex things, those were our first weapons and we did some mistakes.

So the question is probably not really why we made them, but why we don't remove them... The two main reasons we haven't done the latter is that they are still among the "sell items" that monsters drop. Also it would take us some time to decide which ones are truly "worthless" and which ones are not (some of them give buffs to abilities and skills, that make them useful even if they don't do much damage).

Hope that gives you some answers
