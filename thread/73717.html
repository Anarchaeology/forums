The Onyx
Landing on Rubi-Ka. Two Omni-Tek workers step off the ramp.
Sommer "Shenubo" Alsaqri and Deanne "Catyra" Fecat.

"Wow, This is Rubi-Ka." Shenubo says looking at all the sights to see. "we really have are work cut out for us cat."

Catyra looks up at the welcome gate. Her eyes calm and quite. "Yes, Indeed. But we have much work to do. And we must do it soon." Catyra strides to the Omni booth to check in with Shenubo following quickly behind.

"Yea, I better get started on finding poeple to work for us. There are not meny here we can trust. And we should start working on finding out more about this world." Shenubo stops for a moment to look out the large glass windows. "I wounder if there will be others here who will join us. and help us reach are goals." Looking back Shenubo see's that Catyra has reached the check-in booth for Omni-Tek. "You better hurry Ma'am. You don't want to be late." Catyra says with a smirk and proceds down the ramp.

Sheubo looks out the window again. "All I want to do is see this world and all it has to offer." Turns to the Omni desk and heads down the ramp to her new life.
