Changes I would Like to see
Heya gang, 1st time poster long time reader ROFL!

Well i have been playin the game for a short time and so here is a list of things i would like to see changed in the game 8-). None of these changes should apply to PvP ONLY PvE so this is definitly not a call for Nerf this class or Nerf that class.

1) Mobs with Guns: Here there are 2 things i would like to see happend. First off could we please get mobs reloading guns. LOL if i have to do it so should they. Also I have noticed that while i play with a gun 30% of the time atleast i will hit for minimum dmg wether the mob has armor or not. (although this becomes more frequent as mobs start wearin armor) However a monster using a gun seems to consistantly hit me for his AVERAGE dmg. This is not to say that his dmg doesnt change but rather the dmg range for a mob using a gun is significantly better than a players. For instance at level 10 My agent has a Gripo that does 2-26. Fighting a monster that is yellow/green to me the monster will hit me for 10-18 during the entire fight but my dmg drops as low as 2-3 dmg per hit. I would like to see monsters that have the same chance to hit their lower end dmg that i have. This is not to say i want to be Uber but i want to see mobs hitting me for 2-3 dmg as often as i hit them for that dmg.

2) Conceal: Ok heres a biggie for me. My main is actualyl a MA at level 49 i can clear a group of 3 MOB's sitting in the first room of most missions 1 at a time. This is because i get in close enough to aggro the monster but do not attack, i run back into the entrance room, kill the single mob, then go back and get another. However at this point my level 10 Agent is probably gonna have to stop killing things in missions because while at this point i can probably sneak past the first room, if i take a shot all 3 will gang up on me. What i would suggest is this..... If you are concealed and use aimed shot or sneak attack then all other mobs in the area should ignore you. This once again only applies to PvP and should only be truly effective in missions.

Well thats it gang... for now. Like i said no screams for Nerfs and no screams to make these chars Uber. Simply some changes to level the playfield in missions and mob hunts.
