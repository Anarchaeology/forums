Re: Few basic questions
1. No idea.. Can do this with my x-3 using doctor, too. Can't imagine agents are too happy about this, but feel free to ask them.

2. Whichever is highest ql is the the one you get. No they don't stack. Get a MA to give you the crit buff and run the AMS/DMS speech instead, if you're in a group together. Be aware it breaks agent's sneak every 20 seconds and they'll sometimes complain.

3. I think it's worth it. Especially at lower lvls. After you've got a ton of init it's still nice because you can go defensive and still instantly cast.

4. I'd only go charm if you plan on soloing a ton if you're under lvl 130. Reasons why:
  • People notice a gimpy green or grey bot and think you're a weak teammate.
  • You probably still won't be able to charm anything in your missions, so you've weakened yourself with no benifits.
  • You can get a ninja sooner with mocham's and a wrangle to impress your friends.
Switching after that is very viable as you'll be able to charm the occasional mob in missions your team is on.
