Gailyn and I were in the arena last night, testing NR.

He was on full defensive, I was on full aggressive. He is an NT, high level, fairly high nanoresist (around 750, IIRC). The attack skill for the debuff I was testing most heavily (Luck's Fickle Fate) was around 620. LFF has a 130% resist rating.

I cast it around 15 times on him, then he debuffed my skills with the -80 Frost nano, and I cast another 15 times or so.

In the end, with the 620 attack rating vs. his 750 (1000 effective) nanoresist, I landed a little more than 1/3 (6/15) LFFs on him.

After debuff, with a 540 attack rating, I landed a little less than 1/3 (4/15) on him.

So, for a 2:3 ratio of attack to NR, I was getting a 1/3 hit ratio.

I'd say NR works just about exactly as well as it's supposed to; given a 1:1 ratio of attack skills to NR, you should probably see about a 50% resistance rate. The problem is that very few people actually have a high enough NR to see a significant amount of resists against the attack rating of the people they fight.

All numbers subject to the flaws of my memory after a very poor night's sleep.
