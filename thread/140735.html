TAG and Voice of Freedom
I am not sure if I am speaking as a single voice, a minority, or a majority…

However, I am aware of an area, no, more of a communication vehicle that has disappeared from the Rubi-ka landscape. And based on the disappearance of this tool a very loyal group of residents have not only suffered because of this change in policy, it has also lent to dwindling numbers of this devoted group of citizens.

What I speak of is the ability the inhabitants once had to disseminate news via the Voice of Freedom and Tag online periodicals.

I am not quite sure if those that chose this path in policy quite understood the rippling effect that it would have on this community, so I will make a few points about the benefits that this simple area enhanced in the past, and how it sets Rubi-Ka apart from the rest.


((OOC))

For those of us that looked at the leveling treadmill as something we could find in any number of MMOGs, the one thing that set AO apart from any other gaming world was the "nod" the developer’s gave to the RP community.

This is evidenced in many ways that is solely unique to the Rubi-Ka environment.

This would include…


The largest selection of social clothing on any gaming world

Clubs/Bars that are populated by folks having a “club experience”

Apartments that could be decorated

FunCom sponsored Events that would have unique gifts (Christmas, Halloween) that lent to the fun factor and nothing more

Recognition of player run events by the Arks/FunCom as an integral part of the community

And lastly, the reason for this post, a communications vehicle that allowed the players to tout their events and accomplishments worldwide


There are many more...

These unique enhancements allowed for an environment that would spawn ideas, and the imaginations of those that called Rubi-Ka their home.

Now it is understood that the periodicals lent a level of overhead to the production staff at FunCom, however, instead of seeing this highly visible, multi-purposed tool go by the wayside, I would like to find a way to reinstate this imperative tool, while not affecting FunCom's business side.


A few proposals…

Make it part of the Ark program…
You have Arks who own the sole responsibility of talking to new players (surveys), how much of a burden would it be to have an Ark in charge of the communication function?

Have a “Professional” do oversight…
Have one “Professional” area that isn’t necessarily for a Profession, but more slanted towards communication. One from Clan, and One from Omni-Tek, who take responsibility as the editor in chief for their respective periodicals.


These are two ideas that come to mind first, however, based on the creative minds that we have both in the population, and at FunCom, we could find other means to an amiable end.

You see, AO is unique from any other world in one more perspective, and this might not be obvious without some thought. The whole community lives mostly on one server. Where other gaming environments can designate an RP server, or a PvP server; Anarchy Online must have a level of coexistence for all of these player types. In removing the periodicals you have greatly affected your RP community, an area that I have stressed earlier is perhaps your most loyal group of players. These are your AO advocates that realize what makes AO different from any other gaming experience, and spread the word through out.

Again, I would urge you to work with this community in reinstating the policy of giving your player community a voice, and look at the creative alternative means in which we can do this as a team.


Thank you,
Jolynn “Munkie” Creehan

PR is Dead...Bringing meaning to the Game suffers
I am fortunate to be involved in Public Relations for my guild. I was also honored to have 5 or 6 articles written by me appear in VoF. (Published under various names.) Having a planet-wide 'news' outlet was very exciting. It hurt me, and maybe all of R-K, when it was discontinued.

My desire to 'role-play' has diminished with the demise of the news. It was fun to suspend belief, and pretend that one could really make a difference on R-K. I used to use the words 'The pen is mightier than the sword' as part of my signature. I no longer use those words...and I rarely think about the 'grand-scale' of the politics and life on R-K. It is sad.

I make no threats or promises, but the lack of a forum for player led movements and political trends makes AO much less interesting to me. The end of my playing days drew signinficantly nearer with the demise of player-driven news events. I am much less valuable as a PR player than I was when news mattered. Now, I am just another gimpy player.
