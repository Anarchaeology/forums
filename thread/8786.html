Give us *progress*. Make this game worth playing.
Simple:

Extend the levels out and make it possible to progress in a single playing session.

In other words:

Extend the level cap to 1000.
Make each character 5 times his current level.
Multiply the QL level of every item in the game by 5.
Let us level 5 times as quickly.
Give us 1/5th the IP per level.

This would not change the balance of how we play one bit, but it would allow us to, at the least, "progress" though the game and achieve reasonable goals.

I am currently lvl 100. I have lvled and/or re-rolled each profession to 70+. This game is getting sickeningly boring regardless.

After lvl 75 or so, unless you are a pet class teaming with 5 other pet classes, leveling (and "progressing") takes far too long. Let's face it, this game is boring once the leveling slows down.

I like to achieve something for my play-time, and spending a week or more to level has gotten to be too much.

You loyal player, for the moment,

Provisioner.
