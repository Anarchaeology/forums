Actually Yaz, we have not had any NanoFormula changes since the game went retail except that the cut the AMS modifier in half on the challenger line. They have also messed with the Rage line a bit, but it was certainly not an improvement.

While other professions have grown and evolved and for the most part improved. Enforcers have remained the same. Obviously FC originally intend for Enforcers to wield blunt weapons and more obviously they want us to wield 1h blunt. (ever notice the one and only guild advantage buff?) I think of a thug as a street fighter. A person that uses any weapon they can find to fight with. The Beam is the premer weapon for an enforcer, but Solies can't use it. The Alloy/Pipe combo is also a premeir combo for enforcers and sadly, it is probably the most effective weapon combo that Solies have. Byom/Rider combos are also good, but with the absence of edge buffs, blunt is just better. My only issue with the assumption that Enforcers are meant to be blunt weapon wielders is the EDGE OF TARASQUE. Please notice that this is an enforcer only weapon. The other thing that casts a shadow of doubt is SILKEN LEGCHOPPER GLOVES. These are Atrox only. While that does not mean an enforcer has to use them. Most Adv are Solitus & an Atrox Soldier or MA would not even consider the type of weapon these gloves would help with. Clearly FC is thinking of Enforcers as more than just a "guy with a stick".

I don't think that any of us feel gimped, but I certainly think that FC has NOT evolved the Enforcer as they have the other professions.
