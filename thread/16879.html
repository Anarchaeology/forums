Puddle, you have to either fight mobs whose bodies can be converted to blood plasma after they die (apparently some players can make lots of money this way by fighting lower level mobs which can be killed quickly), or you have to blitz missions.

Blitzing, running the mission for the mission objective without bothering to kill anything. Blitz missions for items you can sell to other players, or for battlesuits which you can sell to the stores. See how high a level mission you can manage to blitz, you want to do the highest level ones possible.
