Steps of madness
Did my first dungeon raid tonight with my deparment at Steps of Madness. Me and a friend wanted neleb's cloak and all of us low levels wanted to see the dungeon..
We outdamaged another team to get the robe the first time, let them have the next spawn, and took the one after that.. then the whole room filled with demons and slayerdroids as crowds of high level people moved in.. they were taking three robes, so it's a good thing we were done cause we could in no way outdamage these guys .. did not unmake Neleb so he did his shield thing and for a long while was hardly taking any damage..
Reason is i did not know which unmake it takes and do not even have all unmake nanos but i would really like to have that piece of info..

The dungeon is quite nicely designed.. there are oddly shaped rooms with dinosaurish skeletons and some other stuff, though nothing really compared to screenshots i've seen of camelot and such..

It was quite easy from the combat point of view cause we had 3 high lvl (80+) leading the raid and us 5 or 6 in the 50ies and below ..
The mobs do have incredible hitpoints, but i think a well organized group of lvl 50 could do the whole dungeon, as i am lvl 54 and with good armor (1800ish + MP shield +buffs) none of the mobs were hitting real hard. And i mean a group with a good tank, a doc, crowd control i guess and/or pullers, and an MP or whatever to unmake/debuff neleb.
Which is what i think funcom intended to say when they said it's a dungeon for lvl 30-50.. what does everyone expect, that you can solo the dungeon at lvl 50? or do it at the intended lvls without a good healer? In fact i read of a lvl 60ish bureaucrat who soloed it, and got killed by neleb.. a full team of lvl50s, i think, can do a lot better.
The one thing i think is quite wrong is the fact that mobs are so low level compared to how tough they actually are.. did not get any exp myself as i was grouped with people too high lvl but i imagine it's quite measly, even neleb was just yellow to me.. and a yellow normally goes down to my pet in little time.. not neleb..
So you make mobs with extra hp? ok, no problem, but raise the xp they give accordingly, and the color they appear to players too..
