i made a post in this forum within the past 2 weeks about the same thing. personally, i think as makes perfect sense as it is. frankly, the way it works in pvp makes less sense. as is a sniping shot, think rp. you sneak up and have time to pinpoint a vulnerable spot on a stationary target.

my idea was simply that as should do a LOT more damge, if only for agents, thus making it worth the wait. if a sufficiently overbuffed agent (using nanos and implants) could take 50% or more of the mob's hp every time w/ as, then people might be willing to wait for it. to keep this from adding to other profs that can use as in addition to fa and burst (making them too powerful), it would need to be something like an agent-specific nano line, big as damage boost of some sort.... this would also give some variety in team missions, so it's not always the tank that leads.

this, to me, is an essential problem. the tank always wants to attack first so he can get a head start on holding agro, if the agent w/ half his hp attacks first, then he has to try to get it back... as would have to do a lot of damage to compensate for that.

but, then, i wouldn't argue if fc would just make it so that you can hit as any time its available and the mob is attacking someone else...
