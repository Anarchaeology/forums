Protagonists?! Pfffft! What fun is that?

If you're looking for some free-form RP, just keep in mind who the RP orgs are and watch the little tags floating over people's heads. Now, this isn't always fool-proof; in some contexts, RPers are completely OOC (like waiting for a buff at BB in Omni-1 Ent), while in others (like a bash at Reets), they're 100% IC.

The best way to meet people is to network. You meet one RPer, get to know them, they introduce you to more, etc. These forums are perhaps the most centralized location where EVERYONE in the RP community meets, and also the best place for introductions. An easy way for people to get to know you (which I've noticed quite a few RPers do), is to create a literary backstory for your character, and post it. Let everyone read it/contribute their thoughts, and get known.

And if you are dying for some RP, but are having a hard time jumping in, just send one of the RPers a message - we are ALWAYS happy to welcome new members

OOC:

And let me add one thing for you, Roleplay a good person. Someone who's happy and nice and all smiles and tolerant of everyone else. Because Villains in this game get castrated from the get go. With the Gas thing, you cant back up anything you say with force. People are free to 'spit' in your face as they see fit.

Playing a villain in AO, not msg boards, but actually in game, you'll find yourself having very little fun. Yes, a few people will roleplay ignoring the gas...or even you 'doing things' like sabbotage, viral infections, etc etc. But most people wont. Mastermind villains can have 'some' fun by manipulating events. But if you want to just play the straight forward 'bad' guy your not going to have fun. People have no concept how to roleplay to a villain character.

You'll get /tells about people complaining about how you've 'hurt' them irl. Others will come up to you and do stupid things, and because theres gas, you can't do anything about it. Oh, and dont bother trying to actually roleplay the villain and attempt to create elaborate meticulous plans because most people will simply do what they want anyway.

End result, dont play the bad guy. People will tell you they want villains, but none of them will actually Roleplay it to that extent. Play the happy cheery person, or the moody brooding person who lost his one time love and now seeks to be devoid of emotions. Or hey, play the 'spit' in the face of authority and do what you want type. Or play the nice person who is overly emotional. All those others will gladly roleplay to and make long posts even on the msg boards about. But play a villain, not on the msg boards, but in game, and you'll find it doesn't work.

Have fun.

I personally enjoy playing a variety of roles throughout. I like playing my little bubbly one (and trust me, that can be played very evil too, as some people already know), but I do enjoy playing the bad guy as well. I think it all depends on what makes you happy.

Cute and bubbly comes naturally to me, cause it's very close to my RL personality, so I find it a fun break to be someone completely my opposite. Even though some of my chars may be butts IC, I know that's the way they must be played in order for them to be effective. Some of the great "bad guys" are listed above, and it's wonderful to see the people out there doing it. Playing the good guys is easy. Playing the bad guys can be tough, cause it's not all about blind hatred. You have to be calculating and devious. You have to give off that air that will MAKE people fear you.

Granted, I don't play my villians as much as my good guys (mainly since I have WAY too many obligations otherwise) but I like the idea of being able to pull dirty tricks, and stab others in the back at times. (Yes, I'm breaking your cutesy image of me. Tough!)
