Figures of Twilight (RP org)
Greetings fellow clans men and and women, I am Elaynia, founder of the Clan organization known as the Figures of Twilight (FoT). For those who do not know us, we are a mercenary guild dedicated only to ourselves and the pursuit of money. Many may find this detestable, but we all know life on Rubi Ka is harsh, and some are forced to go our way. It is to those of you who follow our way of thinking that I speak to today. We are looking for new clan members to bolster our ranks due to coming developments. We do not discriminate against class or race choices, we just prefer that you have the desire to work to better yourself and the organization. If you have questions contact me in game (/tell Elaynia) or post back here. We look forward to seeing some new members soon, but until then..

We are those who live in shadow...We are the Figures of Twilight
