Clan Leaders Call for Action. (FNN News Story)
Clan Leaders call for Action.

All is not well within the territories of the Rebel Clans. Many Leaders seek a peaceful solution to the ongoing conflict with Omni-Tek. However, in the midst of the controversy surrounding the Council of Truth, and the looming threat of an Omni-Pol invasion of Tir, many prominent Clan Leaders are calling for more action, and less diplomacy.

A statement was forwarded to the FNN Offices this evening from the Offices of The Dragons. Apparently the Dragons Monarch, Master, 10th Dan Chauncey "Jasonx" Boehme has a very pointed opinion on the current state of the Clans.

"We, The Dragons feel that The Clans aren't doing their job to suppress omni-tek's ongoing offensive upon us. The news may dictate this is happening... but I see it on a daily basis... we all see it. Yet we continue to lend support Omni-Tek Employees whenever it will suit our own personal needs. This cannot continue.
Therefore, The Dragons will treat Clanners that do wish to associate
themselves with Omni-Tek Employee will be treated accordingly as traitous Clanners."

Also in an interview earlier in the week, Lance "Mellithin" Bourgoin, President of the Black Dragons had this to say:


"I really would like to spread the word about the other clan Leaders and their non-militaristic ways. They have never taken action, and its kinda getting bad. We cannot win without fighting. But its not really about winning, its about freedom. We need to fight to protect our freedom. To tell you the truth, I think the other leaders have alot of Omni friends, and dont want to hurt them."

Roselee "Bayala" Depaolo, President of the Dust Brigade, said this When asked about the threat by Omni-Tek to enter Tir to conduct an investigation, and also regarding the accused inaction of the Clan Leaders:

"The Leaders need to take immediate action against the looming threat of an Omni-tek presence in Tir. If we give them any type of leeway they will walk all over us. We need to step up and take them down, but not by a straightforward conflict. That will accomplish nothing. Only covert actions will reign in this day and age."

Well there you have it.

The Call for Action has been made. Will the Clan's unite and fight? Only time will tell...
