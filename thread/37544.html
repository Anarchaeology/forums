[ooc] We need Roleplaying "cast"
We have fully ready events (including pre-made scripts for dialogue with some room for improv roleplaying).

The following are 1-Part Events:

"Missing Assistant"

What is needed:

1 Male Clan Doctor lvl 50 or higher.
1 Male/Female Clan Doctor lvl 15-20.


"Raw Deal

What is needed:

1 Clan Trader lvl 30.
3-5 Clan Enforcers lvl 50+.


"Explosive Offer"

What is needed:

1 Clan Fixer lvl 50+.

"Blood Feud"

2 Clanners lvl 50+ (must be almost same level)

"Death Dealer"

What is needed:

1 Clan Doctor lvl 20+
1 Clan Trader lvl 50+
1 Clanner (Any profession, any level)

"Warrant"

What is needed:

1 Clan Soldier lvl 50+

Unnatural Selection

What is needed:

1 Neutral Doctor lvl 20+

The Following are 2-Part Events:

Part 1 "Revenge"

What is needed:

1 Clan Fixer lvl 50+

Part 2 "Ground Zero"

What is needed:

1 Omni Agent lvl 50+
1 Clan or Omni Fixer (able to team grid or beacon warp)


I'm writing 22 more 1-Part events, another four 2-Part events, I'm working on re-writing a 3-Part event due to the fact the main character/player left AO (but will be writing another two 3-Part events afterwards).
I'll be working on a massive multi-clan storyline event which MAY be focused on the neutrals (instead of the classic omni/neutral conflict), I'm not sure yet. I will ONLY include roleplaying clans/individuals in these events, so if you are in a roleplaying clan or are just a roleplayer please POST in this thread so I can add you to a list of people I can contact ahead of time to see if you are available to be part of an event or assist an event.
