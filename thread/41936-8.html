Few thoughts. Bear in mind: I don't, and never have, played an NT. I play a fixer, and one of these days I'll probably start playing my li'l crat again. Or not. I do, however, team with an excellent NT (Savedsoul gets props from me!) on a regular basis, so I have at least a little knowledge.

Things that NTs can waste in PvP: GA fixers. Yep. Dem nukes cut right through. Keep that in mind, next time you're out in MMD and some Mk4 freak is owning left and right.

As to improving NTs: I'd like to see you guys tweaked some, yeah. I do think that NTs should follow the AD&D mage model of initially weak, but can rock at the high end. So, some ideas:

New nukes will probably help. If you had something like ILW with a a/r of more like 12/6, but similar damage, it'd rock, eh? Your new nukes are going to have level requirements (they are, you know it, I know it, so live with it) so the MC req should probably stay right around where ILW's is, but 12/6 at the high-end when you can cut 6 seconds off attack via init wouldn't be too shabby, if you're nuking for 4-8k with it.

A stacking reduction line, self-only, that would bring the total reduction down to something like 40-50 percent, maybe even more. That'd solve the problems with NT's lasting through a long nuking match, and keep it unique to NTs (preventing MPs and crats from getting buffed and being able to last just as long). (This refers to Crunchom/Izzy's Recompiler etc., nanos that reduce cost to cast)

And finally, and this may not be loved by you guys, but struck me as an interesting concept. Prolly useless in PvM, but great for PvP. A Nano Leech/Corruption line. Basically a DoT that attacks their nano pool. I dunno if that sounds interesting to you, so let me know.

But yeah, I'm looking forward to seeing if the NTs get 14.4 Fixer love or 14.4 adventurer love.
