Your level and the location of the mission also seems to play a large part. Countryside missions seem to be caves more often, and caves seem more likely to have monsters. Full Chaos seems to have a better chance of turning up robots or monsters too.

However, I don't believe it's possible to always assure yourself a "monster" mission at all levels. For instance, it's obvious that there is a "robot pocket" of missions at where you get waste collectors and janitors. There's also a pocket of mission levels where you get gladiator and soldier bots. But those bot missions seem to be confined to a small spread of levels. Humans appear at all mission QLs, but it seems that monsters and bots run in small streaks.

I, personally, avoid monster/bot missions on purpose, because monsters/bots tend to hit a lot harder for their level. I have noticed that monsters in the newbie backyards tend to drop rings and bracers more often, but I'm not sure about mission monsters, because I avoid them.

If I seriously wanted to look for monsters, I would go Full Chaos and look for outdoor dungeons in areas known for cave missions (like Clon**** or Broken Shores). If I tried a couple of missions at my desired level and didn't see any monsters... I would lower the QL of the mission a step or two and try again.
