Would you have a look at this please?
I know these ideas might already have been posted earlier, but they can't be stressed enough.
I would really like to see these ideas implemented or fixed soon.

1) Exclude team members and their pets/bots from TAB selection

It is outright silly that this is still an issue. If you are teaming with a neutral or even - god forbid - an Omni (coming from a clanner's mouth, mark you), we shouldn't have to TAB through our own teammates when we want to target hostile mobs or players. Teammates aren't hostile. Mostly =)
Fix it please.

2) Userdefined waypoints

The ability to store your current world location in a list, with a title and link with uploadable coordinates would really help out a lot.
Imagine being able to store this great hunt spot for the level you're currently at, with a nice title "Tir County Hunt Spot #1 - Lvl 40-50" or something like that. And maybe have a popup window in which you can edit and view your waypoints in.

3) Better sorting system for containers and inventory

Items get scattered all around in your inventory, backpacks, bank and so on. This is really annoying. What about being able to rightclick inside your container/inventory, and choose "Sort by name | title | type | QL" and so on?

These are ideas I am really missing in this game, and I think it would really make a difference to a lot of people.
I hope dev. team will have a look at them.

Thanks, and keep up the good work!
