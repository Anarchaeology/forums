Well, as of right now here's the deal with weapons. At least what I know. There may be more issues I don't.

As Hexxie stated, if brawl/dimach are listed as requirements for the weapon, you can use them.

While wielding a weapon, you will attack with the weapon, and during the recharge time you will attack with your punches. (Currently I use a weapon with 7+ seconds of recharge time so I can get my punches in.)

If you debuff, buff, level, or zone while wielding a weapon, your punches will not work at all. ie: you will be shown as punching but they will not actually hit your target. Fixes for this are to keep an eye on your buffs. Don't let them run out. Remove your weapon(s), rebuff, wield weapon(s). Just learned from Hexxie that when you level, you can remove weapons, debuff, rebuff, rewield weapons, and you should be fine.

While wielding a weapon, you can not use MA special attacks.

Weapons look cooler then no weapons.

There you go. My probably limited weapon knowledge as I've only got a level 27 MA. However, by using weapons, he solos dark reds. So as of right now, the evidence seems to point towards using weapons as a good thing. Unfortunately I don't have the experience of being a high level MA who wields weapons. Perhaps they'll add their own insight.

On a side note: When I first started using a weapon, buffing while wielding weapons didn't negate my punches. I hit level 25 and all of a sudden it did. Wierd.
