Read this before replying


To everyone posting here, instead of flaming FC or saying 'OMG these problems are so obvious,' please reply with specific examples that illustrate the problems and/or link to threads that cover them. It is more important for FC to see the problems and fix them then it is for us to be right. Let's make Znore glad s/he posted here. This will encourage FC to become even more involved with us in the forums, not less. Use this as a chance to speak to a real life, bona-fide developer.


Thank you for posting, Znore! I wrote up my wishlist as spoof patch notes because I figured they would get more attention and have a better chance for getting ingame then if I just whined or spammed the suggestion forum. I think it's great that you're reading this and posting here (risking getting flamed)--no other game company that I know of would do that.

In addition to your specific points, I want to address one large concern that many forum-goers have complained about: unnecessary downtime. Many people have complained that AO is becoming more and more like Everquest. With all the quests necessary just to get into new areas of SL, with all the forced downtime caused by things like coils (NO one likes being forced to sit and do nothing), and with the up to ridiculously long transit times, the amount of downtime in AO has greatly increased. Many people came to AO as an antidode to all the sitting and camping of EQ, and now we find the same tedious elements being added to the game we love, so we are rather uspet. For instance, why must we remain sitting to recharge nanos? No one likes sitting so I don't see why coils can't just be NoTs that continue to work even if you stand back up and continue fighting/healing. Many docs have complained about how hard it is to get into advanced playfields and how they need help and need to run around for hours. This is not fun and is turning many players off (especaially us docs, since w need help). Also, I understand the no vehicles rule for SL (makes sense to me), but why disable fixer run buffs, grid nanos, and especially beacon warping? If my doc dies (Which is highly likely), I will have to run all the way back to my team by myself, but since I'm a heals only doc, that is nearly impossible. My team would have to run all the way back and get me--this is not fun. I enjoy seeing the SL world (and it is a beautiful world ), but I've hear of others who took 45 minutes to return to their team! If these nanos were allowed, they would vastly decrease down-time and would give added teaming value to specific professions.
