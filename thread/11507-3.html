Roots are fine.
Roots are fine. And it's perfectly fine with me if most professions can't break them during a fight. (I say *most* because it may be logical for some professions to be able to do that.)

Just give us a way to break them after the fight with an NPC is over. I'm not suggesting that ALL NPC-cast nanos should stop after the NPC dies. That would be nice - it's annoying to have to endure all the other hostile nanos after a fight is over. But it's not as if that really kills the game play.

Getting hit harder in my next fight because someone cast Projectile Magnet on me in the previous fight makes the game play more challenging/annoying. But the only challenge to enduring a long-lasting root after a fight is over is the challenge not to gain weight. It takes just about 2 minutes to get yourself another bowl of Nacho Cheese Chips while your glowing blue character just stands there twiddling his/her thumbs.
