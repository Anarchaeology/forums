You don't need a special set of aimed shot implants to get enough aimed shot skill to equip a high level rifle. Just the +20 general buff, and the +15 from sniper's bliss, and the aimed shot bonus from even a low level Low Light Scope, will be enough.

And, Drgena, you need to overequip if you want to be successful.

As to being able to sneak missions... yeah, concealment is totally screwed. It >is< possible to sneak missions, sort of. Concealment lasts for 30 seconds or a minute (or maybe 10 seconds, or maybe 1 second).

Basically, sneaking missions and blitzing missions (running around at high speed looking for the item, trying to find it as quickly as possible before you die) are fairly similar. Sneaking is mostly just a way of helping you to blitz a bit better.

Read up in other threads about how to blitz missions, ask others how this is done, this is really the secret to making money. As an agent, you can get false profession fixer run buffs, you'll need these to blitz missions. Once you've figured out how to blitz missions, you can use concealment to help you blitz a bit better.
