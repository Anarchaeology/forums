no brainer suggestions 101
Make the black background of the map translucent when in missions, and fade out the surround as the inventory and other windows when not in use.

When I zone out of a mission, I already know what parts of the map I've visited, so cut out the nonsense with having to revisit the entire map again - make what we've already seen visible.

Ability to add waypoints to both radar and world map, to mark out places of interest to player - be it missions, or whatever else. Also the ability to share this information with other players, and/or team mates.

Friends list - right click to bring up current information instead of having to see them to get latest status.

Enable the ability to make team from friends list, similarly, the ability to form guilds in same manner.

Reclaim booth - one click stop, instead of this individual item collection... sheesh!

View items in bags in the bank... Again, sheesh!

Ability to build custom weapons. The trade shops are seriously squandered (and in some cases, just plain broken) and has the potential to be put to some seriously funky use. In fact, tradeskills are woefully underutilised all round, so a massive overhaul would be a welcome addition.

And weapon recipies just don't cut it here, I want the option to able to build weapons using the parts I want, not chancing my luck with a broken recipe to build a weapon that isn't appropriate to my profession.

Hacking items... nanocrystals, weapons and other trade items. More useful items to build.

NPC's. include phrase 'do you know any rumors' to the list of chats. Make some NPC's need bribes to increase the chance of getting decent information, or not. Over tip them, and repeated visits, then make the NPC react differently - remember players who visit regularly and begin to fleece them if they over tip or continually hassle the NPC.

Add NPC barflys in clubs and similar. Works same as above.

Consider introducing the idea of an NPC mafia/blackmarket. Missions given out as already handled. Benefits include unique items to be bought, but introduce extra elements to the missions - shorter time windows, various penalties for failing missions from the mundane (non payment) to the extreme (wanted, hunted down, unable to enter certain areas or cities).

Helpbot - /tell value <ql> <item> to gain an apx value of item based on past 24 hours of item being sold in both shop and on shopping channels. I'd suggest auctionbot, but that seems to be down more often that up...

Introduce more vehicular traffic. Some nice static vehicles being squandered in the game, put them to use.

Outside 20K that tracked land train vehicle, for instance. Introduce logical routes for them to use, such as trade or equipment runs.

On the landing pad area of Omni Entertainment, there is a craft which looks vaguely similar to Slave-1. Use this model as patrol craft within the game.

Get the taxi from Tir working around Rubi Ka's main cities

Use the shuttle and big silver blob ships hovering above Harrys' used as supply ships. Again, introduce flight plans which are logical.

The hydrovehicle model - make this a flying craft, if for no other reasons than it looks cool and breaks the monotony of Yalms.

Sort out the irritating 'nose intrusion' when in first person mode in a Yalm.

Include an altometer when in a Yalm

Introduce a program for use in Yalms/vehicles 'evasive action' for use when entering 25% zones if in a mission. Similarly, add a 'diplomatic immunity' item for those on foot and who don't want to be hijacked and drawn into pvp involuntarily. It would be a non negotiable (unique, always on) item, which stays active while in these zones, so neither parties can initiate combat. Lasts until mission is completed and player is clear of the pvp zone.

That'll do, I've lost the creative edge...
