Attn Omni Tek Landlubbers on Atlantean
Ahoy!

Have you ever wanted to pillage the Clans?

Do you want to do the above with STYLE?

Then sign up for Ye Olde Scurvy Mates the rowdiest band of Pirates to ever set the skies over Tir on fire! We are looking for more dedicated freebooters, buccaneers, privateers, and pirates to proudly carry the Letter of Marque of Omni Tek. Reading and writing optional, hygeine required, large storehouses of rum a +. Seek out Rumguzzler, Shadowfruit, Apocalyptic, or any of our crew swaggerin under the banner of Ye Olde Scurvy Mates if ye wish to hoist the Jolly Roger with us!

YARRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR

/OOC
We are a Pirate themed OT Org. Its silly, its fun, but the possibilities of merging the romanticized notions of Pirates with the post-modern future of cyberpunk are ENDLESS. Chrome peglegs. Reets on your shoulder. Lots of drinking. Besides, ever heard of software piracy? We are a strange subculture bred by the jaded notions and cutthroat policies of Omni Tek. CyberPirate? PiratePunk? You be the judge.

Think of us as a bunch of "733+" with pirate hats who butcher pirate jargon beyond all recognition. Style is our first priority, fun is a close second, and hardcore dedication to bringing the war and the good fight to the clans is our third. And imagine the uproar we will cause when RK news reports our raids...

All in due time. Give it a thought.
