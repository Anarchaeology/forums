We don't need a /list command: Privacy and Lag.
I would like to hear one good reason why we need a /list command and how it would be of any real use. I have had this conversation with many people in game, and not one has been able to give me a good reason.

I have some good reasons why we should *not* have a /list command. The biggest being lag. Large numbers of players asking the server to send them a list of all clients in a zone *will* create more lag.

Another reason is it is an invasion of privacy. Especially if you are a well known Trader or MP. You're out there trying to do some missions or hunt with your team and you have people bugging you for buffs because they know you are in the same area as them.

/list sounds mostly bad
Number of players in zone is fine.

Number of enemy players in zone is stupid. Go find out for yourself how dangerous the zone is.

Specific names is unthinkable for obvious reasons. If you want to see if a friend is on, look at your buddy list or send him a /tell.

The spy loophole has been noted above. If you are clan and want to see if a specific enemy is in a zone, then just Beacon Warp an Omni-Spy in and do a /list.

'/list profession' serves no useful purpose other than to make the game less enjoyable for those who will be hounded for buffs. Do the legwork yourself and find a player and actually talk to them if you want a buff.

I can't see a single reason I would want or need any information that would come out of /list other than the number of players in a zone (to find out how laggy it might be.)

Every other use leads to grief or the reduction of PvP maneuvering tactics.

Better options:

- If you are a Tradesperson or Buff provider, then they should add bulletin boards where you can 'advertise' that you are in a particular zone (Tir or Trade, for example). Players can then go to the bulletin board and see who is in-town that has the skills they need. Those not interested in being pestered can opt not to put their 'open-for-business' sign up while in town.

- Implement the LFG features that DAoC has. They can start with doing it simply through the chat client. '/lfg on' will flag you as looking for a group. '/lfg enforcer 80-90' will return information on all enforcers lvls 80-90 looking for a group in your current zone. 'lfg any 80-90' will return information on all lvl 80-90 players looking for a group in your current zone. They could do the same with 'tlm' (team looking for member). '/tlm doctor 80-90' would then provide feedback to all doctors in that range when they turn their 'lfg' flag on that there are team that might want them.

- Make /list show the number of players in a zone or simply provide the '% full' information that is currently available via the XML page. Players will soon enough learn what percent full a zone can be before becoming unstable.

- Allow '/list friends' to show the location of players in your guild or allied guilds. Allow '/list' to show the location of players in your group. If you are in a guild and want to hide your location from other members then maybe you should rethink your membership.

Somebody needs to tell us what positive purpose /list would have.
