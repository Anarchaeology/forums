Enforcer/Fixer Team Quest Spoiler! (lol..)
The following quest spoiler is fictional. If one day FC does consider having this sort of quest, that's great! If not, oh well :P

So... Everyone's interested in where I bought my wigs? I wasn't going to tell you guys since my life would be endangered, but I just can't stand having 3 tells every second!!! So here it is!!!

First of all, you'll have to finish a team quest, requiring a Fixer present in the team. You may obtain this quest from the Godfather of RK Mafia--Don Corleone, who usually stays in his headquarter in Smuggler's Den. The only way to enter his secret HQ is to use the Data Receptacle (created by Fixer's Hack Grid nano) on the elevator.

His room is located in the very north of the HQ. It's better if you brought along a mezzer to mezz those Gangsters and Thugs...

When you enter the room, you'll need to answer correctly in the conversation IN TIME! If you talk too slow or said the wrong thing, all the mobs in the HQ will become aggro toward you and your team mate, and unmezzable!!!

Here's the conversation:

Don: Hey, who the hell are you? How did you get in here?

Enforcer: My name's [your name here], and this is my pal [Fixer's name]. We come here to join the Family.

Fixer: We heard about this place from one of my friend, and we really would like to join in.

Don: Hmm... Who told you about our family? Tell me his name.

(If you are RK2 and answer Phione, I will be on their hit list for 720 Hours, and it'd also make you attackable by me anywhere, even in 100% zone).

Enforcer: We're not gonna tell ya! He's our friend! We'll never betray our friendship!

Fixer: Yeah!

Don: Heh, you got a point, kid. I was going to smash your skull, but you got a point.

Don: So... You 2 want to join the Family? Here's the thing. The skinny one (or blue boy if the fixer is wearing a GA) with a machine gun had proven himself useful by getting you in, now's your turn big fella! (That's if you're a Atrox, it'd be "boy" for male char and "lil girl" for female char)

Enforcer: Bring it on!

(You'll then need to leave the team and solo his Bodyguard, Punch)

(If you got beaten, you'll need to start over, but the fixer will be able to continue the quest on his own)

(If you beat Punch, it'll continue like this)

Don: Now that's more like it! The Last time Punch got beaten, it was 5 months ago by that Phione girl.

(Again, if you say anything that tells him you know me, you'll get yourselves and me in trouble).

Don: Ok, here's what I need you to do... This will be sort of like your Entry Exam to the Family... Are you up to it?

Enforcer: Yes boss.

Fixer: You may count on us boss.

Don: That's the Spirit!

Don: You'll need to locate the Trash King. Use one of those grid hacking thing on it. If all goes well, you should be able to retrieve the "stuff" our business partner hidden in there. Bring it back, and I'll allow you to become part of the Family. SO DON'T SCREW UP!!! Now get going!!!

You will then receive a Team Mission, the keeper will be the Enforcer.

You will be able to leave the HQ without being attacked by the mobs in there.
