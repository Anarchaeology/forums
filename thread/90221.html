The Abbadon Chassis is NOT uber.
Everywhere I look I see people refering to the new "Uber" soldier armor . I'd just like to point out a few things here.

A normal 160+ soldier will gain 28 or less attack rating using this armor.
A normal 160+ soldier will gain less then 2k AC's using this armor.
A normal 160+ soldier will gain 30-55 nano resist using this armor.
A normal 160+ soldier will gain... well may actually lose some +damage or gain up to +18.

For that a normal 160+ soldier has
less reflect.
less hp's
less nano points
no yalm (ouch ouch... this'l be what keeps people from using it)
no skill bonuses (FA, AR, ect)
less xp bonus
no evades bonus's
ect


It's nice, I'd wear it if I could get it simply cause it's new and there's so very little unique soldier stuff I'd feel guilty not doing so.
But please, no more referals to the "uber" soldier armor that might not even be obtainable .
