ooc: questions about Notum
Recently I have seen alot of people mention Notum depletion in Roleplaying and I was wondering about the origin of Notum. First of all, I read somewhere that notum is not depleted, but mysteriously recreates itself perpetually (had something to do with "notum rifts" I think). Because of this, a notum mine never runs out, but there are limited notum "patches" on the planet. I'm not sure if this was on the web site, or from talkting to the NPC in charge of Clon****.

Anyone else read this?

Secondly, since Notum is central to the AO storyline, is there any reading material anywhere about what exactly it is, and what it does?

This kind of info could make some RP alot easier when discussing Notum and its mining/trade.

edit: Just went back and talked to Odys Mady in Clonyke about Notum. Apparently it spontaneously regenerates inside the rock all over the planet in notum rifts. They are called rifts because it is like the rock is torn apart from the inside and filled with notum. Its not a gradual process, but rather one minute there is no notum after its mined, the next its all back.

He also mentioned something about ghosts and spirits down in the notum mines.

OOC: No one really knows the true source of Notum, but we do know a few facts.

1) Notum is a power source.

According to the timeline "Notum is incorporated into nanotechnology, and, for the first time, nano-bots are now able to operate outside of the human body for an extended period of time".

This is probably because the body gives off waste heat which can power a nanobot and the body is constantly getting rid of dead cells which could be used as raw materials to make more nanobots(though maybe what we are made of isn't the best material to make nanobots out of). Nanobots outside the body would need to find an alternative source of energy. Sunlight is an alternative but that may not be strong enough for every use of nanobots and it wouldn't work in the shade, during overcast weather, at night, or indoors. If Notum is a solid form of easily accessible, large amounts of energy(which doesn't exist in our universe) then it would solve both problems.

You could build nanobots out of this substance and then you wouldn't have to worry about adding a battery to each nanobot. They would just consume eachother as they needed power. This would also explain why nanoformulas only last so long and why we don't carry raw materials for nanobots around with us. Just Notum rechargers.


2) Notum doesn't appear anywhere else but on Rubi-Ka.

The "rift" theory fits in perfectly with this. If this is a substance that occurs naturally in another universe where the laws of physics allow it to, then having it appear on Rubi-Ka(the only place with these "rifts") makes sense.


I think it would be great if FC could write up a little guide with all the facts that a typical Rubi-Ka citizen would know about Notum. It does seem a little awkward playing a Nobel-Prize winning Engineer that can't even say a few simple facts about Notum.
