Tip for those: sidle up to the door and use /pet hunt. Once the fight starts and the mob is half dead, move into the room, select the mob and use /pet attack, which prevents the bot charging off to find something else to fight once he's killed the current mob.

It doesn't always work according to plan, since there are occasions where the bot will attack a mob in a different room from the one you intended, but it does mean that you don't have to do the rush-in-tab-and-rush-out dance every single room.
