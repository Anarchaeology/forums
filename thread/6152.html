BiggerDave's Wish List
Things that I'd like to see in the long run, not anytime soon...

* Revamped apartments
* Apartment blocks/residences outside of cities (small villages, etc.)
* Player created info screens on themselves (e.g. brief descriptions)
* Audio chat through a phone system (i.e. you could 'ring someone' by dialing their mobile and talk to them through a microphone)
* Offensive vehicles (yes I know about the tank but there should be more, and they shouldn't need such high stats)
* Defensive gun turrets in outposts and cities
* The ability to crouch/lay down to avoid being seen and for greater accuracy with guns
* Vehicles that can carry more than one person
* FC Putting PvP back to how it used to be!
* And the Official BiggerDave's NUMBER ONE thing he'd like to see in the game:

A REAL aimed shot mode like in a first person shooter! That would be uber-hard to do but my god would it kick some EQ arse!

So come on FC, just one of my wish list ideas...just one?

Please?
