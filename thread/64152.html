Pulling The Trigger
She crouched down quickly, the leather pants creaking underneath the thin-plated armor. Once her knee touched the ground, she placed a small device next to her right foot, and touched the top of it. It lit up with activity as she stood and ran full speed away from it.

"Explosive activated in zone three!" shouted PFC Williams from his console at tactical.

The captain had been nearly asleep in his chair during the four-hour yellow alert. He was on his feet in half a heartbeat.

"Red alert, battle stations!" he barked.

The red alert sounded, the station came to life with bustling privates and ensigns, all scrambling to get to their posts.

Williams was staring at his console in awe. "Captain...I have a final reading on that explosive..."

"Report!"

"I think you should see this..."

The Captain walked up and looked over Williams' shoulder at his readout.

EXPLOSIVE: K6 NANO-CARBINE HIGH EXPLOSIVE. POSSIBLE LIFE-DISRUPTING DUE TO GRID-VECTOR FIELD DISTURBANCE. EQUIVALENT TO 50 KILOTONS CONVENTIONAL.

His face went white. "That's impossible."

"Incoming transmission, Captain!" shouted a tech at comm station.

"On speakers."

The speakers crackled to life with a distorted female voice.

"I tried to warn you, Captain. I tried to warn everyone..."

Another crackle of static.

"...but people just don't listen anymore..."


...do you know who YOUR trigger is?
