Ability to change profession?
Well after you reach 200 and there is not much to do in this game. It would be nice, if FC would offer one time chance to change a profession to your character.

With this you would have to strip naked, just like with IP reset. And would also lose all profession specific nanos, but not common nanos. Plus you would lose 25% of your experience and according levels.

What do you think?

It would be great for those that feel that some other profession has more to offer them then their current choice as they learn the game dynamics

It woudl also be great for those people who feel that their profession has either been nerfed or lacked attention of FC for too long. Or they simply want to be back on the top of the food chain again.

Pre-release, it was an idea of theirs to make a system where you can change profession. It was ditched.

Post-release, pre-IP realloc system, tons of ideas were tossed for IP realloc. Anyone who has played MU*s with reincarnation systems is familiar with the "re-create from scratch except with most of exp intact" idea. Which was suggested, atleast by me and probably by others too, during the time IP realloc was heavily discussed. When Funcom was very much anti-IP realloc...

So, they may have already considered it and thought it was either unwanted (from their perspective: no "2nd time round" chars, less time spent playing... this is of course false, as anyone who has played reinc system MU*s knows :P nothing says "34th time round" more than a reinc system, HUGE bonus for replayability), or too "hard" to code but maybe someday implemented(Tm).

Well, needless to say I think it would be tons of fun and add tons of playtime for countless of players (bored of 200lvl x prof? do a complete memory restructuring, lets play 150-200 lvls as y prof instead now). Not to even talk about the money sink, as suddenly you need to purchase/get every new profession nano you need.
