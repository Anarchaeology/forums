Keep an eye out for this person...
Last night, while having a quiet evening at the Rompa Bar, Don and I noticed an odd fellow at another table. This "man" (there may be a more apt word, but I'm not sure what it would be), who goes by the handle "Demoneyes", seemed to be predicting doom for Omnitech, particularly the city of Omni-1, and have access to some kind of visions of the future.

When Don confronted him, tempers quickly flared, and I started to leave. Demoneyes followed me, however. I don't really remember too much of what happened after that... One second I was looking at him, the next, he had his teeth clamped down on my neck! I don't know why he was able to do this with the suppression gas in Rompas...

Needless to say, most of what I remember after that involves a lot of blood, and Don dragging me to a safehouse while he tried to figure out what had happened (thanks again, Don ^_^).

All I can say is, keep an eye on this one... I don't know what to make of his warnings, but his culinary sensibilities definitely leave something to be desired...
