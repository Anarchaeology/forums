Freedom, the Clans and dream of Unity
In the name of God, most Gracious, most Merciful.

The Clans have lost their way. In fact, the Clans have lost their way long time ago - I would say about the time Radiman have left and thereby created a power vacuum in the Clan community. Whereas, while CoT ruled, the Clans concerned themselves with the struggle against Omni-Tek oppression and furthering the well-being of all the clansmen, after the collapse of CoT, the Clans became concerned with only one thing - power. CoA, EG, RMC and the plethora of other variously named alliances have been competing between each other for the right and the possibility to establish a Clan government and thus, gain power. True enough, some of the participants of those alliances have aspired to form a democratic form of government, while others merely wanted a government, a unified structure of governance for the Clans, irrespective of how democratic or undemocratic such government will be.
In essence, the Clans stopped caring about the ideals and goals our predecessors have set their sights on. And, with the fulfilment of the Notum Wars prophecy, the general state of affairs on Rubi-Ka has degenerated into open warfare. The Clans are concerned not only with political power now, but with the acquisition of the Notum mining rights and territories. Thus, even though it saddens me greatly to admit it, the Clans are now more like Omni-Tek than they have ever been before.
In light of this and the fact that the most ardent supporter of the peace movement (Fixerben) has laid down his sword, so to speak, I suggest that a profound change of attitude and direction is required.
Notum Wars is a reality that we have to face and live with. Any attempts to restrict Tower warfare will eventually prove fruitless until such an alliance can be established that will encompass the majority of the Clans  one cannot give the power and then forbid its use. I respectfully submit that even a possibility of such an alliance in a short-term and even in medium-term is a hopeless dream. To unite a sufficiently large number of the Clans for such an alliance to be effective there has to be a general desire among guild leaders to work towards peace among them and to forego the previous experiences that now set the guild leaders apart from each other. Thus, I venture, such an alliance in not a political reality nor it can be such if the cessation of Tower Wars is one of its purposes.
I therefore propose the following.
The quest for power should be replaced with the quest for unity among the Clans. Instead of concentrating on the issues that divide us, the Clans should focus on the things that unite us  our common heritage, our despise of the Corporation and our contempt for thugs in Tir and NLC. It is a fallacy to think of our forefathers as the raw models for democratic values. They were united by the burning hatred of Omni-Tek, by their desire to be free and live as they wished on the planet they called home. However, beyond those common goals, the rebellion incorporated both the extremists and the moderates, all having differing views as to what to do next. This is clear from the present situation on Rubi-Ka  different Clan guilds have differing agendas because the primary purpose that unified them, the freedom from Omni-Tek, is gone. We should stop looking at ourselves as victims under the yoke of the Corporation. Those days are gone for good  we have our own land, we have our own might and we have our own freedom. It is unlikely Omni-Tek will want to start another major war  war is costly and Omni-Tek hungers for profit. They already have more Notum than they will manage to extract before their lease will run out, so it is unlikely that Omni-Tek will be so stupid as to anger ICC and start another major war with the Clans just to gain a few more Notum territories. What is needed now is to re-establish the Clan government that will be able to address the issues we have with the other parties. Thus, we do not need a government that will govern the Clans, for the Clans do not want to be governed, what we need is a unified voice to represent us in talks with Omni-Tek and Neutrals.

The Omni-Tek and Neutral Notum mining territories are to be declared terra nullius by the Clans and thus are open to conquest. Since this is a reality of our everyday lives we might as well accept it for what it is rather than struggle against the tidal wave. It is essential that inter-guild fighting is brought to a minimum. To facilitate this, the Clans need to be encouraged to attack terra nullius rather than each other.

All hopes of peace with Omni-Tek or Neutrals are a dream until such time that the Clans can put forward a unified voice. This is clear from the history of our world. An effective leadership brings with it an ability to negotiate effectively both with Omni and Neutrals and thus the ability to establish peace on our planet. I propose that a general assembly of guild leaders is held in the near future to establish a common ground and a foundation for formation of a unified voice for the Clans.

I welcome the ideas of all those interested in the unity of the Clans.
Long Live the Clans!
