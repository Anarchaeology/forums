Display the coords in the chat line
It's very boring to rewrite the F9 coord in the chat to give a position.

Why not simply make a hotkey, exactly like the Shift-R hotkey ?
It will rewrite the X and Y coord, and the area:

Example:
My GPS position is 1204x1357 at Greater Tir County


---------------------------

An other idea in this forum.
Invent a new map upgrade that constantly displays our x,y position in the corner of the screen.

Then, with the new hotkey, we can copy this info in the chat.

---------------------------

A third idea. (Looks like a mission info window idea)
A snapshot map device. When used, it will contain a Snapshot of the map where we stand. Then, in drag and drop this device in the chat window, we can see in purple:

Here is my GPS INFORMATION COPY.

When a teammate click on the purple text, he will see in his automap the blink spot of the recorded position. Or the image of his leader automap snapshot. (if he had got the map).

In the chat area, a message diplays:

<Name of the Owner of the Snapshot> is in <X>, <Y> at <Zone>.

-------------------------------

Many fun ideas.
