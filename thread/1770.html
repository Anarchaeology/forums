You don't find the pieces, you build them.

The parts you need to find are robot junk, and silver rings.
You then have to buy some nano wire.

Use a screwdriver on the junk to get a nano sensor. Use a piece of nano wire on it.

Then create silver wire from a silver ring, and combine that with another nano wire. Then use the product on the nano sensor.

This creates a part of the braclet. You can't do more than this at the moment.
