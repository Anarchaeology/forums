Funcom - Please, 2hE Buffs for enforcers
GIVE US HEADSPLITTER/BRUTAL NINJA AND YOU WONT BE SORRY!

I'll regurgitate something that has probably been said a thousand times and needs to be said again. By only giving enforcers a buff for one of their three possible weapon styles you are DISCOURAGING DIVERSITY. 90% of the enforcers are running around with hammers and beams and the 10% who are brave enough to stick with edged weapons are constantly asked dumb questions like "you know a beam would be better, right?" Even with the sub-par weapons that are available for Edged players, you can at least give them a headcracker style buff for their gimped swords, and with the OE patch coming, with them not being able to self buff their weapon skill by 90 points they are going to be even more gimped which just damn well isn't fair. Punishing originality and individual thinking is wrong, I know you aren't doing it on purpose, but please think of this in the next few patches, you could easily copy the code for Headcracker and Brutal Thug, alter skill->2hb to be skill->2he and change the name and description of the nano and bam you helped make enforcers more diverse, make many players happy and guess what, NO ONE would be mad, Blunt wielders feel for their edged brothers, and no other prof is gonna even notice let alone think it's a twink, they all know that even with buffs edged still wont hurt them close to as much as blunt.

Thanks for taking the time to read, if you do.
