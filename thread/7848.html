Re-program AI pathing...
I think Pathing is insanely crude for such an advanced game...

How is it so difficult?

If the pet has been following the master for x amount of time, and still isn't within LOS, warp it. If the pet has been in the same sector for x amount of time, and has been in "following" status the entire time, and still no LOS, warp it. You shouldn't base the AI Pathing to specific nodes throughout maps, it should be done dynamically by sector, or brush, find the bounding box of sector/brush, then find the center, move bot to center, move to next sector/brush. If a brush is concave, have a way to find the center of corridors within a single brush, without using nodes (most likely based on the 4 extreme vertices of the extruded area). Do Not path based on the bot's current position and the distance to master and next doorway, Path according to sector/brush.

And stop the friggin MOBs from warping through walls! yeesh...
