You have two separate issues bro, one more serious than the other.

The black screen you get when windows boots up is common with detonator drivers.

Boot into windows XP safe mode, go into the display control panel, click the settings tab, click "advanced", click "adapter", click "properties", click "driver", click "uninstall", hit ok. It will ask you to reboot your system, go ahead and hit "no".

Now do a search for NV* (the '*' is a wildcard character) and delete all the NV files that it finds in the windows\inf, windows\system, windows\system32 and windows\help file. Also delete the file called nwiz. Delete the entire "nvidia" folder in the C: drive and make absolutely sure that any "NV" file in c:\windows\inf folder (or if you upgraded from nt or win2000, the folder will be winnt\inf.

Now when you reboot your system, force it to install the driver for "PCI VGA card". It'll have you reboot and you'll be treated with a nice 16 color screen. But that's what we want. Now install the detonator drivers, reboot and the black screen should go away (Im working from memory here, so don't shoot me if I missed a step).

The black screen is caused when you install new detonator drivers and it either fails halfway through the install, or doesn't completely remove the old drivers. It's all too common with geforce cards.

Now the other problem...the graphics artifacts. That could be a couple things. Bad RAM on the video card, or it could be overclocked too high, could be overheating (even non overclocked systems can overheat), poorly seated video card (remove the video card, clean the copper contacts with a lint free cloth moistened with 15% isopropyl alcohol and let dry), fan failure on the video card, overclocked front side bus, or even a power supply that's starting to fail.

So alot of possibilities there. But the first thing you want to worry about is getting rid of the broken drivers you have. I wish I could see it for myself to make sure that the steps I listed were exactly what you needed :/

Good luck man!
