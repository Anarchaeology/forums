Freelance Reporter - ARK Fashion Frenzy
The desires of battle weary and lost souls spread across Rubi-Ka, calling for help without politics or propaganda. In answer to this silent need, the Advisors of Rubi-Ka emerge. Our vigilant reporter relays this moment as it unfolds

**Grid uplink connecting Password accepted Welcome to the Rubi-Ka Grid

I'm Lacey, a freelance reporter for the citizens of Rubi-Ka. I left my previous investigations of enigma and medusa mutants in the territories to find any truth to the rumors of an ICC organization in Newland. So far I have discovered a humanitarian group called Advisors of Rubi-Ka (ARK) wanting to build offices in all the major cities. I have travelled from the territories to my source in Newland, following my investigations into any links between ARK and the ICC.

When I arrived in the neutral city I was looking forward to a hot shower and soft bed, but as luck would have it - I was just in time for another ARK visit. Advisor Apotheos was asking for people to go on a "dangerous but highly paid errand". He was looking for a group of people to seek out Viiir, the sister of famous fashion designer Miiir. ARK wanted to hire her to design their uniforms. Unfortunately Viiir is difficult to approach, as she is known to be temperamental about strangers and has many wild pets to guard her privacy.

Advisor Apotheos had a gift of good will and message to pass onto Viiir for any willing adventures that could follow instructions to seek her out. To ensure success, I was informed that groups from Newland, Tir and Omni were sent to find the wayward seamstress. The first team to successfully approach Viiir would receive a substantial reward and the other teams some creds for their trouble.

Now with potential office sites and the designer Viiir commissioned for new uniforms, it appears ARK are preparing to make their full presence felt in Rubi-Ka. I was able to confirm ARK have been assisting trapped citizens and advising on all aspects of Rubi-Ka life. Hopefully an interview with the Advisory Council of ARK will give us details about any connection to ICC.
