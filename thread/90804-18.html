The only time when lower QL has higher value is when the benefit of the item is the same but the lower QL is easier to equip. Thus a QL1 Second Hand Old English Trading Company pistol is worth more than a QL13 because the QL1 can be dual-wielded by almost anyone for +10 Intelligence, but the QL13 can be used by fewer people for only the same Intelligence buff.

For Concrete Cushions, QL12 should still get pretty close to what you could get for QL10.
