Rifles
At lower levels with low ranged init you might be better off with a
Caterwaul (http://auno.org/ao/db.php?cmd=view&id=160191&t=ag) due to the lower recharge. This gun is also available as mission rewards. As you can see the non-crit damage is almost as good as the X-3. However once you get beyond 300 RI (base, without implants or scope) the X-3 rules.

The RI base thing is because recharge is apparently only affected by IP spent in RI, not implants and scopes. See the MA-boards for info from Chronita on this.
