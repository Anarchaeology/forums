Would like to say thanx to Storm for making this day one of the best for a lot of people in our guild and myself too in AO so far.

What started as an ordinary trip to Camelot to get the dragon ended out in big scale war through the caves under Castle of Camelot. First Storm tried to kill Tarasque, but The Council sneaked in behind and ruined that thing.
