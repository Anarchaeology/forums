How Rubi Ka dies( An Omni Testimonial )
I was born in Rome. It was 11.0 and the world was well and we ate of fruitbats and orangatangs. oops, wrong story..

anyways, From Rome, I dove headlong into Galway Shire, hunting bulls with friends.

from there we ventured to higher grounds in Glaway County hunting hammer breeders, and soon the elite team of manteze hunting, bring your swimsuits!! Watch out for them rock gardens, eeeeeiiiiiyyyayaa!!

ok from Galway County we'd head to Lush and go mine hunting, if we felt really brave we'd venture to the high cave in Lush and hunt there.. Spot a lifebleeder and it was RUN AWAY !!! RUN AWAY !!! ( radiation damage was the scourge of hell! )

From there we'd move to 20k and bothackers, something people coin as Hotbackers nowadays.

Havent hunted there in 100 levels :P

From 20k we sought out Newland, Broken Shores.. and the outlaying communities

This all leads me to a revealing pattern and a relavent conundrum.

The pattern in which Rubi Ka dies.

Funcom does nothing to make locations interesting so the staying power wains quickly and with clockwork precision.

GalwayShire was hopping in 11.0. Its been legally dead for a year untilt they intriduced the new subway.

Rome was hopping in 11.0 and well, it's deceased.

Galway County .. dead. Broken Shores missions took all the people away from the fun of this county.

Lush Resort... dead swimming pooll entertaintment value, etc never realized.

20k... dead. not enough high end content

Newland. my guess is will be next to die

Broken Shores... has always been dead due to lag exept for people LFG,but they do that now in Newland.

So what do I forsee as the next center of attention and hangout for Rubi Ka? I predict that everyone will eventually leave Newland just like they did all the other places and start hanging out in Broken Shores, come NW, since everyone will be wanting to towercontrol the area.

Another center could very well be Borealis if some high end content was put out in the wilderness out by the waterfalls or the sattelite?

Another center could very well be MORT, bring back the pumpkins of war !! RAWR!!!

If there were more out in PW i think youd see that area really hopping since a lot of high levellers really like going after cyborgs these days and the Dust Brigade.

Perhaps something could be done with EFP since alot of people love the new primus camp.

What I would like to see is some content brought back to Galway County, and 20k

I too remember those days...
I had left AO back in April but the pull was too great and I came back the beginning of November. I was shocked at the complete lack of people EVERYWHERE.
Not just in Galway or Lush, but in Ent, and Trade, in the bars, and everyplace else in between.
I remember when there would be 50-60 people easy hanging out at the BB near the whompasin Ent. Now, I consider it full if there are 10.
Everyone I knew has left this game. I've made some new friends, but my friends window went from a list of 30+ down to only 7. It's hard being in the Trade district and seeing maybe two people in the shops at any one time...
In the time I was gone, I tried: Meridian59. AstoniaIII, Project Entropa, Earth & Beyond, Neocron, Redmoon, Asheron's Call, Asheron's Call2 (beta), and a whole bunch of other korean-comic-book-based MMORPG's, but nothing snagged me the way this one does.
It's a shame to see it dying by degrees like it is. One can only hope that Notum Wars and Shadowlands will bring in some sorely needed bodies into this game.
I DO NOT want to go to SWG, but at the rate things are going, I may have to.
This game has so much more potential than anything I've ever played, and I've been around a while. I just hope there comes a point where everyone, AO player and developer alike, can reach that ephiphany that is so badly needed to turn this game around...
