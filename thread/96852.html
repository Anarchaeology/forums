NANOMAGE RADIO Has Returned!
Long ago, the NANOMAGE people secured a broadcast tower, that allowed them to transmit their own signals to the NANOMAGE people of Rubi ka, devoid of all propoganda by those powers in control, uncensored and uncut. An epicenter which radiated truth and and gave the NANOMAGE their own news, music, and opinions. Sadly, the tower was destroyed by our enemies, who would see the truth buried to protect their wallets and their interests.
The NANOMAGE LIBERATION FRONT is proud to present the return of NANOMAGE RADIO, 24 hours a day, seven days a week, with a playlist that includes all NANOMAGE music, commercials, opinions, and even the occasional live NANOMAGE concerts by our own musical genious, General RAMIYANA. Tune in brothers and sister, and share in the joy of your own brothers and sisters music! www.live365.com/stations/fleshreaper
Finally a voice for our people! TILL ALL ARE FREE!

Queen Fleshreaper,
