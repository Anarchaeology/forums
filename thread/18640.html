Reload Bug
Ya, I've done quite a bit of testing with this w/ small clip guns, as far as I can tell the problem is with client/server synch about how many bullets are really in the clip. Let me explain a bit, and a pain in the butt work around until they fix it:

This is assuming you have a "normal" attack rating for your level, i.e. you know your not going to actually miss what you're shooting at. Say your clip 5 at start of fight. You start firing, you get 5 hits (count in your head to work around bug). Then as soon as fifth shot hits, break off combat and manually reload. Otherwise, the problem is, the ghost shots your client fires during the auto reload timer are being subtracted from your client's clip total, so by the time you're done reloading, your clip probably is down to 1 or 2 shots left, causing 1-2 hits, then reload message again. With a 1 clip gun, if you let auto reload handle it, you will be long waiting for another hit

So in short, keep track of your clip in your head, break off when it should be empty and manual reload, and you will always be hitting, its a major pain, and not even possible essentially if you're being hit by a mob, but I found I'd rather do ANYTHING to get more shots in than being bugged out to hell waiting to hit the mob for 5 minutes. In PVP or solo PVM using these type guns, just root, run out of range and manual reload, (manual reload is much quicker than auto as well).

Anyhow, GL, hope this helps someone.
