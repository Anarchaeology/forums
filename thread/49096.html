Same Side PvP vs. Same side ganking... Rules of same side combat
OK for those that have no common sense, A duel is 2 sides agreeing to fight each other. This can be 1 on 1 or handycapped accordingly.

(Fake convo. to show a Duel._

Silverfinger a level 10 Enforcer
Horridflesh a level 10 Doc

Silverfinger ask Horridflesh to a duel. They agree on terms of the duel. THe terms of duel are Self buff only, and no others can help in the duel. Both players agree to terminate to show they are self buffed.

after they rez, both get buffed and duel.

This is the proper way for 2 clanners to duel.

(terminteing is optional)

Now to show the improper way.

(the same 2 people are at Tir arena.)

Silverfinger walks into the PvP Area. Instead of asking for a duel, Horridflesh Jumps Silver with out asking for a duel. Horrid has essance of titan, and a fixer hot.

Now this is one way to gank. If horrid losses, he will generally whine about it not being fair, even when he started this.

Some other ways to properly duel someone of your same faction.

Always remember that it is ganking if you jump into a duel with 2 members of the same faction.

If someone is in a fair duel with an Omni. please wait till the person of the same side wins/losses, before triing to jump in and kill the Omni.

Never Just assume someone wants to duel you. This is just bad judgement.

Never whine when you loose a fair duel. You can be a good looser.

be helpful when you beat someone in the clans. If your do the better everyone will be in this war against Omni.

The only time to ever gank someone of the same side as you, is when they are ganking another of the clanners, or they are on your KOS.
