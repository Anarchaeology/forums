UVC+Flashpoint, or River6.. MAs...
No /duel command

Special attacks poorly thought and at max having the same effect as the others(ie dimach will take off the same amount of damage that Fullauto can)
etc,etc,etc

No.

--Reflect bracers + Reflect buff are too overpowering. Bracers should be ignored when player damage is given. That keeps them fine for PvM.

--Heals. No logical reason why they should not be capped at 40% and heal 50%. It's just not right when a doctor can survive a beating from 4 others even level by chain casting complete heal.

--Zone hugging crap. Make a zone totally 25% or don't waste the time.

-- Arena's need a dueling system. Maybe a machine you can click on and recieve keys for, give these keys to whoever you want to duel with in the arena. You won't be allowed out of the arena until someone logs, dies, or delete's their key (thereby giving up and losing PvP points).

--PvP needs a purpose other than title ganking in the arena. Give us areas to take over and do it now.
