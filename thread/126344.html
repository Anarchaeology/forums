that was the best post I have ever read in two years of playing AO!

It's definitely all about the friends and it makes me happy to have friends like you guys!

Loot, levels, XP, money gets in the way of the social aspect of this game alot. Ive seen folks do some cruel things to each other for the sake of a trinket, but then on the flipside I've seen folks do some really cool things as well.

But the end result that we should never forget is that this game is about the people. The loot, the levels, nothing would matter at all if we had nobody to share it with.

When we get a new piece of loot or figure out a new way to improve our chars, the first thing we do is share the info with friends. not braggins, just sharing our happiness. When someone shows me their new dragonchest or azure or what not, Im always touched that they thought about me enough to send me a tell and share the good news. Thats the kind of thing that keeps me playing AO. On the same token, I love to share my own knowledge with others.

I'm a busy guy and I miss more tells than I actually see or can respond to, but when I see a nice post like this, it makes me stop and reflect on how much I care for my fellow AOers. Over the years we've become like a tight knit family and I wouldnt have it any other way.

Luv you guys
