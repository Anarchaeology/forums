An Alarming Announcment
From the desk of..... Emma "Pyormanche" Peele, General, The Union


Citizens of Rubika.. beware that the airways are not safe.. any and all transmissions may be stifled at anytime. This is an assault upon our freedom of speech and we believe aimed directly at not only Nanomage radio, but to the nanomage people themselves.

Thursday evening, during the "On Fire with Pyromanche" show, it happened.. the radio transmission was cut off and could not be restarted. At this same time, sitting on top of the Weapons Dealer on the North end of Borealis, were members of the NLF including Aazamon (NLF Prime Minister), Dr Ramiyana (NLF General) and a few other members of their organization as well as Pyromanche, when the signal went down, those members if the NLF and their leaders found themsleves at reclaim with no idea what had happened.

WHO.. who could possibly be responsible for such a horrible and devious act? WHY would anyone feel the need to harm these people? WHAT do they accomplish by cutting it off during so popular a show?

They have not put an end to Nanomage Radio yet, of this I can assure you!! We will be back!!

I am having the members of my office being an investigation, anyone with information please contact us.
