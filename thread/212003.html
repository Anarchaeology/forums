A Letter to the ICC Board of Directors
To the Board of Directors, ICC:

I am filing this proposal on behalf of the Omni-Tek Corporation, Rubi-Ka.

As you know, there has been an ongoing conflict between Omni-Tek and the Clans on Rubi-Ka. To date, Omni-Tek has endured lost lives of it's personnel and their family members as well as lost profits due to these Clans interfering with Omni-Tek notum production. This interference has been in the form of attacks on Omni-Tek notum mining facilities, private businesses and on individual employees.

The Clans were once a group who considered themselves to be some sort of freedom fighters but have turned into a conglomerate of smaller business entities which all compete with Omni-Tek for notum production and it's sales. During this time, the Clans have been able to become quite rich since Omni-Tek has been forced to comply with ICC regulations that demand it to do the following:
[list=A][*]Buy notum from the Clans at a cost higher than what it costs Omni-Tek to mine notum itself[*]Provide shipping facilities for a competitor[*]Open insurance technology to non employees at no additional cost[*]Open grid technology to non employees at no cost[/list=A]These are just the major points directly associated to our cost of doing business that stem from current ICC regulations on Rubi Ka. Add on to this that we must maintain a militia in order to protect our facilities from the very entity that we are subsidizing and you can see how Omni-Tek's expenses are quickly adding up. In essence, you are telling Omni-Tek to help a direct competitor who sabotages Omni-Tek's business at every opportunity.

The Clans have grown rich and fat and it is time to cut the umbilical cord. What I propose is the following:
[list=A][*]Omni-Tek no longer must purchase notum mined by other entities but may do so if the opportunity favors Omni-Tek[*]Omni-Tek no longer must provide shipping facilities to anyone other than itself for it's own notum but may do so at it's discretion[*]Impose an additional surcharge on reclaim insurance for anyone who is not an employee of Omni-Tek[*]Either allow Omni-Tek to charge a "toll" on grid access or close it altogether to non employees[/list=A]If Omni-Tek is forced to share it's lease with another business entity at no cost to that entity, then surely that entity should carry it's own weight and pave it's own way or start sharing in the cost of the lease. The Clans have been subsidized for long enough and they do not want to do business with Omni-Tek in the first place, expressing it every chance they get in the form of attacks on Omni-Tek itself. How long is Omni-Tek to put up with this?

Thank you for your time.

Janella McCallagher
Employee of Omni-Tek, Rubi Ka

Yes the regulation states that the Clans are to sell all mined notum to Omni-Tek at the current market value. So for that Omni-Tek is paying the Clans. Then once the Notum is shipped off the planet, Omni-Tek sells it at that same value, and they make their money right back. So depending on the market value, Omni-Tek could lose, gain, or break even in Credits. So Omni-Tek is not paying the Clans for anything really since they still make their own profit on their Notum that they export.

The clans are also no longer smuggling Notum offworld to the SBC. That was centuries ago we were doing that, and that was only to fund the original revolt. Since the Clans freed themselves from the mines Omni-Tek has controlled all offworld exportation of Notum.
