Have you read Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy? If so YOU MUST READ!!!!
Okies, first off....I havn't read the book... I found this out purely by accident and with the help of my friend Zionai. Here's what happened:

Was on my way to a mission when I saw some weird ruined looking city. I went over and saw that it was full of deep deep reds but still quite pretties =0). Then I saw this robot standing outside of a factory....it had a name:

Marvin

Thinking it was some kind of weird quest thingy I hadn't heard of I said hello, he replied with "Hello, I am very depressed" I asked him why and he replied that he was depressed because :

A. His diodes hurt
B. He had a brain as big as a planet

It confused me, so I asked him how I could help and I got a bunch of replies about how he was very depressed and all he was getting from me was babytalk =0T.

So I asked some people, and it seems lil Marvin is a character from the book The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. I thought this was great! So great I went out and bought the book last night from Border's.

So yeahs, here's a link to some pics I took of Marvin:

http://www.geocities.com/mistress_vex/marvin.html

and here are the coords for all you explorers =0):

Outpost 10-3
1253.9, 2363.7
Near Stret East Bank

Okies!!! Just wanted to share this finding with everyone =0).

The Rest of the Story of Marvin and Rubi-Ka
Actually, there is more to this story that I just ran across. In a small town in Athen Shire (1542,321), there is an NPC named Brutus Leonidis. If you ask him to tell you a story, he'll respond with:


A couple of years ago, I came upon an old story which had been handed down from generation to generation about a restaurant at the end of the universe and an inter-galactic hitchhiker. I decided to see if it was possible to pull some of the persons from that time forward to our time, and hear the true story!
I studied this tale for some time and combined it with what I know of nanotechnology. In the end I managed to perform an experiment which I hoped would pull forward one of the protagonistsof the story itself to our time.
A portal opened up, and out came a strange robot, who called himself Marvin.
I tried to get the story out of him, but all he did was complain and moan. Eventually I gave up. I think he moved out east somewhere.

Asking him to tell me about Marvin got:

Brutus Leonidis: Oh. He moved east. To a place called Southern Artery Valley, I think.

Asking him to tell another story got:

Once when I was on my way to Tir, I came across an old woman by the side of the road.
She had a real long nose, and it was stuck in an old tree.
She told me that she would grant me three wishes if I helped her.
So I put her out of her misery, cut her nose off and sold it at the nearest shop. Made a good profit on that run!


Those guys at FC really had too much fun with this ;)
