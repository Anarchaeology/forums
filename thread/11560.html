Mind Quake vs. NT Nukes.
I know Garzu started a fuss about Mind Quake (best MP Nuke) doing more damage then NT nukes... well his numbers are pretty skewed. He is forgetting a few things really.

Mind Quake: 736-1515 energy damage. Taunt
818 Matter Creation; 743 Psycho Mod
4.19a 1.82r

On paper, this may look wonderful. But first, lets remember Psymod is light blue for MPs. More importantly though, this nuke does Energy damage. From my experiences with Energy damage nukes, they like to hit for min damage most of the time. I try never to use Energy damage nukes because I will rarely if ever hit for higher then min damages.

In Garzus example, he didn't use the best equiv nuke. It would be:

Volcanic Eruption: 649-1402 fire damage. Area of Effect
836 Matter Creation
3.72a 1.54r

Wait? AOE? Yes. The MP nuke while decent would be considered by most NTs to be a sub-optimal nuke because of the damage type and therefor the low min damage. NTs have a MUCH better nuke in Volcanic Eruption and it's damage output cannot be compared to Mind Quake simply because it is Area of Effect. If Mind Quake was an NT nuke, more people would be using Stinging Missle Swarm over it (1,497-1,762) simply because the min damage of this nuke is the same damage as the top end of Mind Quake, plus there is no Taunt Mod on it so you can use it more often without getting aggrod.

Things aren't always how they seem. We don't want to be jumping to conclusions before we know exactly what is going on.

Don't get me wrong here.

MPs should not have such a great nuke considering everything else they have. I think Mind Quake comes "too close for comfort" when compared to NT nukes. I wouldn't go as far as saying they are "better" but I would say that they come pretty damn close as far as usefulness. In that respect, it is completely unfair.
