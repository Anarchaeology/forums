Mission rewards dissappeared!
Last night in a ql 170 Broken Shores mission I had an unpleasant experience. It was a "target this guy" mission, and I made the mistake of not checking my inv before I rushed down to do it. So we all rushed down there, I id him and whammo, all my rewards are in overflow. Not having the time to sort my inv, I just dropped on the ground the right amount of stuff and clicked all the stuff in overflow. It dissappeared in the overflow window as I clicked them, but did _NOT_ appear in my inv! So now I have nothing in overflow, empty slots in my inv, and all the stuff I dropped to get the free space lying on the ground.

This sucked big time, my only consolation being that the nano I desperately wanted was given as a mission reward to someone else, and they were nice enough to give it to me.
