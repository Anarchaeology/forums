Expire,

The robe is No Drop. What you are hearing about with high levels giving the robe to their alts is most likely them going in grouped with the low level and an engineer who can do a beacon warp. The High Levels go to Steps of Madness and fight their way through to Neleb. The Engineer beacon warps the low level in to loot neleb after he is dead. As to 3 level 50's going into the steps to get a robe.....possible but not likely. MOB's in there have an incredible number of hitpoints for their level. And Neleb has that incredible shield he casts, takes quite sometime to take him down usually. Not sure if Neleb is camped on RK2, as I am on RK1. And I think the respwan rate on Neleb is around 30 minutes, but I can't be sure about that, as it has been a while since I was last in there.

Neleb's respawn time is 30 minutes, not 20. You might be able to do it with three level 50-somethings with a lot of persistence. You should know that the monsters in there have about 1 million hps each. Neleb himself does not hit that hard, but he puts up this crazy shield and then you can only do like a max of 50 damage to him or something even if you normally hit for 2,000, so it takes forever to kill him. Last time I went in to kill him it took over 10 minutes of 4 people whacking on him to take him down. What you should do is take your MP and keep hitting him with Unmake:Spacetime so he can't put the shield up.

Aside from the robe, there are some other nice items in there for people of your level. Three different kinds of gloves that add to blunt weapon skills, edged weapon skills, and nano skills. My doc is still wearing a pair of the "Loving Hands". Also there is a crit-enhancing head implant thing, and I saw a ton of IQ rings on the monsters I killed in there. Even if you don't make it all the way to Neleb it might be worth a trip there. Maybe round up 2 or 3 more people around your level to be safe.
