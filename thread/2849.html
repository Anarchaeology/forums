My thoughts on the Beacon Warp
I see many engies are very busy defending this new nano of theirs at every chance they get. Their main point seems to be that the flavor text don't mention the grid. But of course it doesn't mention the grid. Simply because if it did, FC could not defend the fact that fixers don't have it. But is it not a transport nano, and is not the grid a transport system that "exists everywhere"? The flavor text might as well have read; "the caster becomes a grid exit himself, and the target is transformed into a stream of information transported at light speed across the grid, to materialise again next to the caster". That explanation is just as good as the original, and even use an existing transport system instead of having to implement new concepts to explain a single new nano.

Which brings up a whole new question. Why can't these "micro-sats" be used to transport people under other conditions, when any engineer can do it with a little experience in a few nanoskills? And why can't this "satellite feed" be used to determine peoples excact location without nessesarily having to warp them around? I mean, the engie should then also be able to determine the coordinate and excact location of anyone he wants to, as must be able to do it in order to gri.. ehh beacon warp someone.

It seems to me like many engies are touchy when it comes to anything that even remotely looks like it has to do with their new nano.. maybe because they know they shouldn't have it but don't want to give ut up because they like it too much.

All right, people.
As much as I'd love to agree with you all about how the engineer shouldn't have this nano, I just can't. Not because I have a bias for engineers, but because there's a genuine reason *why* they should have this nano.

As stupid as it may sound, the engineer actually transports his bot parts through time and space to contruct the gladbot, slayerdroid, whatever. It's sort of like they contact the great junk shop in the sky and are sent a whole bunch of nano-compressed stuff that magically forms into the shell of a bot.

In the same way, the engineer can tap their space/time abilities and transport a person as if they were "downloading" their bot.

I'm not making this up. I sort of put two and two together regarding beacon warp, because I knew about the whole downloading the bot thing, and it didn't seem so far-fetched to me that engineers should be able to use this same method on people.

I swear I'm not making up the part about the junk shop in the sky -- one of the moderators posted something about it a while back. I swear! But the beacon warp part is my own theory.
