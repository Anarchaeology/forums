Possible wrangler solution for patch
I dont know if this is the solution to all of your problems, dearest traders but I feel that a "nerfing" of overequipping is in order. Both NTs and MAs are professions that are very weak at the moment because they cant overequip. Also the difference between casual gamers and those in big guilds with higher level friends that can supply them with uber equipment and buffs, is too big in my opinion. When a person can wield a weapon 3x the QL of his own level something has to be done.

Now with the possible "75% of skill to do full damage"-rule coming up here is my solution for the problem:

The problem is that Traders will have a whole line of nanos that are useless, so lets change the line a bit. Let the team-wrangles get a meaning. Why dont the teamwrangles buff all the skills they do but not debuff the trader as much. Also let the buff last for 30 mins or so. This way, overequipping people will want to team with a trader to get the full benefit of their gun.

The deprives must never ever have their time increased as it will kill NTs completely. Im sure you all agree on this.

P.S. The fact that you are buying wrangle nanocrystals for millions of creds is because it has up to now been worth it. Also you are buying QL150+ nanos at lvl 75. Please. Of course they will cost a lot for you. Its the same for NTs, only the prices arent that high because they dont make money on their nanos.

its totally meaningless whether the over-equipping or the uber-mobs came first - the important thing is the state of the game at the moment; the power disparity between mobs and players is such that characters who would otherwise be perfectly happy to not overequip r in fact forced to do so to remain viable. im sure there r many players who waste hour upon hour attempting to blitz insanely difficult missions that would rather use their game time in a way that less resembles work. providing a game that is actually playable with equipment of near-level is the single most important factor in stopping the general overequipping trend.

regarding ur proposed solution, there r of course mixed feelings in the trader community about the team wranglers, but i for one think they're utterly useless -- its just not a whole lot of fun to play when u can cast nothing of ur level and have ur attack multiplier nerfed -- thats really the objection to being deprived in pvp after all, isnt it? it isnt attractive to me to be debuffed 100% of the time -- smart team players would insist that their trader do this constantly in order to have the 'full benefit' of their weapons in exchange for the trader having even less. take a poll as to how many traders use the team wrangler for anything other than a substitute for an individual wrangler -- i think u'll find it instructive. extending the duration as u suggest destroys the parity with deprives when clearly the deprives should not also be extended. if u simply remove the debuff aspect of the team wrangler, it really isnt trading anymore, and u've destroyed the design concept in some ad hoc solution.

'over-equipping' is built into the design of the trader -- its why why we have double, stacking lines of nanos and why our skillset is low compared to other professions; yes, we can massively buff ourselves, which is why we dont have a green shotgun skill, or any green nano skills for that matter, and why there is no 'shotgun mastery.' all four of our greens r on the tradeskill panel, and no im not counting the green tutoring that everyone has and nobody put ips into that simply didnt know any better. the change that is proposed will have a huge impact on traders - please dont whitewash that. skill wrangler is not simply another nano line in our arsenal.. for good or ill it has become the primary definition of the trader.

the self-only change to the comp lit buffs hurt, but clearly was intended to stop massive over-equipping at the source of the problem, and it fit the design of the buffs, which were intended to lower prices at shop terms. there is no design intention of the skill wrangler other than raising skills.. so this is nothing other than a huge change in design and a rendering useless of our 'main thing.' some weak upgrade to the team wranglers is just simply not a solution.

u stated "When a person can wield a weapon 3x the QL of his own level something has to be done." okay - so who's doing this? traders can ratchet up through divests and plunders such that we certainly can over-equip and over-nano.. so dont let more than one member of a deprive line run - if we cant replace Plunder Skills with Plunder Skills Advanced, we cant ratchet. we also wont buy the ql150+ crystals at lvl75. if ur talking about other classes, its surely the ones with huge buffs of their own.. u could simply prevent these from stacking with wrangler. sure -- rifle mastery is largely useless because it wont stack with the agent buffs.. but it prevents the probem u describe here.

i flat out disagree that pet classes cant overequip - but if ur concerned about the lack of buff potential for the nano classes, u might consider adding int/psy buffs which r con****uously missing from the game while str/sta buffs flow freely. i strongly agree with the statement above that u should consider adding to the game rather than subtracting from it, but if i'm to propose a solution it would be to decide on a list of nanos that will no longer stack with skill wrangler. i apologize for not offering a better solution, but i simply dont know what would 'solve' removing a professions reason for being in the game.
