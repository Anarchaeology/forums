Role play value of hand downs?
I grew up in the unterraformed areas of rubi ka. My parents, and the parents of my friends were low level explorers, who would find a vein of notum, mine it, sell it to corrupt officials, and then get moved along if the vein proved profitable. Its not much of a life, but its freedom, and heaven to some.

One day in one of the crackdown on such behavior, the thugs came in and killed all the adults. They felt that the children were not a threat of mining, so they left us, telling us that the orphanage would be along in a while. The orphanage people never came.

For a year, the children of our outpost, whom we came to call sisters and brothers, lived off the supplies left. We buried our parents, tried our best to stay alive. None of us had ever seen the city before, so we didn't know to go there. One day a wild prospector came across our outpost, hoping to get some parts for his failing kodiak. He did not expect the outpost to be filled with 12 kids.

He took us to the orphange, little feral children were we by this point. We refused to be separated, refused to take classes at first. Over time the people at the orphanages softened us a bit, built our trust. and we took up arms to correct the wrongs done to our parents.

It is natural for me in my family to want to pass on the best of equipment to my sisters and brothers. It would not make sence for me not to want to pass things on. We prefer to live together, and to share everything, its what living in the wild taught us to do. If the powers that be would turn a kind eye on how my family wants to live, we would thank them. As it stands, with the difficulty of doing it, and the general scorn passed on the practice, we feel that our family is being prohibited against that which seems natural.
