You won't lose your title level.

You only lose your "side tokens".

My soldier recently switched from clan to omni. She kept her 120 token board but lost her 220 clan tokens. Your character's token count is checked when zoning or equipping a merit board.

So you will still have your board, you just can't use it.


When you use an application from, it will spawn an empty merit board, of the appropriate side, in your inventory.
