some suggestions from a new player
Hi,

I've only been playing this game for 3 weeks, but I have a few ideas for some improvements I'd like to see (some of which I expect have been covered before)

Nanos
I play an Agent and organising nanos is a big problem. I would like to be able to delete nanos I longer want (or even better remove them for resale). I would love more options for arranging them. It would be nice to add extra pages and rename them. It would also be nice if the nanos stayed on the pages I did put them on (a bug I assume). I'd like to organise my nanos by class and utility, not by the color of the crystal. I'd also like to be able to name the pages with my own descriptions. I wouldn't mind if it made the nano screen larger (indeed resizing it or moving it to another part of the screen would be great).

Map
I think the smaller map should have more detail - often you don't know that there is a wall or other obstacle in your way.
I would like the ability to mark a co-ordinate on the large map like missions do - and ideally with a marker on the compass as well. Even if it was a map upgrade or separate device needed to do this.

Selling
The method of selling things seems very hit and miss - and even if you want to buy something, the channels become just spam. I suspect this is why the sales forums are so busy - but they are equally so busy that finding something on them becomes hit and miss. Your sales/buys end up pages down in just a few hours.
My solution would be to add another terminal type to the game, the small traders terminal. This would work in two ways. Firstly like a bank - you put in there all the items you have for sale. Secondly people can browse items for sale - but with search controls like a mission terminal so you can select (or not) ql range, item type, profession, slot, etc. You then get to browse all the items that match. You can click on the link of the item. To conclude the transaction either then contact the seller or have an automatic price reservation system, but with the machine deducting a percentage.
Maybe an advanced version of the terminal could handle an automatic auction - with bids and reserves, but a higher fee.

Play items
There has to be a better way to reload a weapon.
When I log in I'd rather not still be sitting.

Clothing
why do back items (cloaks) look like dresses (on my character) and not cloaks?

Saved options
I would like for window positions to be saved. I always play with my inventory open and would prefer that the game saved it's status when I exit. Similarly I select the channels and groups I want listed - but the filter should to be activated each time I log in.
