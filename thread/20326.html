Re: Nano Disks?

Originally posted by Bullfrog
How do you use the disks found in missions? I have a ton of them but can't figure out how to use them.
You can build NFs out of them, but it's a complex and very expensive process. You need expensive tools (Jensen personal ore extractor, isotope separator, and neutron displacer) that, even at only QL40, cost about 1 million together and goes way up from there, and some expendables. You also need carbonrich rock to make into a blank program crystal (easy to find though). Various trade skills needed for this too, nanoprogramming, complit, mech eng, quantum FT, etc.

The best bet for you if you don't have the skills and tools and don't want to or can't invest, is to find someone who can and negotiate a price with them to make your NF.

Don't bother with making instruction disks into NFs if you can buy that NF in a store. Only bother with the ones that you can't buy in a store.
