Soul of RubiKa
Well met young ones, I am Oshovah, the farseer.

I have a story to tell, and it is truly an important one...
It is about the planet...

RubiKa has a soul, she is my mother, he is my father. I was produced by it.
Therefore we are many.

But I am not important. It is RubiKa that is important. She has been corrupted by Omni and Clan. Nobody is innocent.
Even I have been corrupted...

The planet cries for help, and produced me, a bit of her essence.
Therefore I am the voice of the planet. I know her destiny.
I know what must be done.

All who wishes too meet with me, contact me on my comlink.

I am Oshovah, the farseer.
