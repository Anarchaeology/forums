Star Gazers..... (Idea for developers)
While I think the social moves on AO are fabulous and at times quite comical, there is one social that I think would be out of this world cool.

Would it not be a wonderful thing to be able to find a quiet spot and lay back to star gaze?

I know many people that would...

The game developers have done such an astounding job of bringing beauty not only to the play fields but to the skys of our playground, it would be such a thrill to be able to lay back and star gaze while chatting with a friend or org mates.

I do love the new social move of "Lounge" but why not take it a step further and make one where we can lay flat on our backs?

Granted I know it might be a concern that many people would abuse this social by finding a way to turn it into toon porn, (which some of the social moves already have been tried and tested with success that could be translated as such (including the new lounge social as well as crawl) but there are ways around that.

What about a hand behind the head and knees up position, or two hands behind the head with knees up, or flat with ankles crossed?

I would love to be able to star gaze... why not take advantage of the beautiful eye candy FC has given us?

Imagine a side by side pair of friends, or head to head or even atop a favorite platteau takeing some time to rap under the stars... Imagine two sweethearts being able to share the stars too...

Maybe I am a bit of a romantic.. and star gazing might not appeal to the masses, or would it?

I also would love a hug social but I did hear somewhere that it was being considered or in development... wooohooo! I hope there is two kinds actually... one of those warm and fuzzy type of hugs for girls or guys that are just huggy types, and one for those men that are more into the manly types of guy hugs hehe.

High fives would rock too!

Any thoughts on this?
