Resist
Som,

The nano resist factor is multiplied with the targets nano resist skill, then attack skill (MC) and resist skill (Nano resist) are compared, and the target has a chance of resisting the spell all together. the higher attack skill compared to resist, the beter your chances of a hit of coarse

as such, a 50% res spell will compare MC to half the targets resist , so will be very hard to resist (you will notice they usualy cost more nano for same damage as well), spells with like 120% resist (like calms for example) give the target a 20% boost to resist in the calculations, so is quite easy to resist (PS. attack skill for calm is 50% of SI skill + 50% of PM skill)
