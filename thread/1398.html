No range is the price you have to pay for good damage (in theory) and heals.

I'm a nanotech, and if you get in range of me, I'll be toast, so why can't I fry you when I'm not in range?

If you want range, go with agent, soldier, nanotech, or any other profession that specializes in range.

If you don't want to be rooted, raise your conceal (it actually helps you...it prevents you from showing up on someone's Tab-list), raise your nanoresist (it actually helps you...it is possible to counter a root), increase your runspeed (so when the root does break (i.e. when you're hit), you can run in the direction the shot came from and get a fair distance before the next root is cast (usually ~3-4 seconds under good lag for the caster).

In short, don't just sit there, waiting for the next root. Either do something or don't complain. Run away if you think they are too far, or run towards, if you think you can make it. Or you could run to the side, just for kicks. You could even call in a few friends.
