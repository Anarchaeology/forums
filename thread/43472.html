Funcommunism strikes again: Don't make this a "team-mission-oriented" game.
All in favor....say "Aye"!

A team-mission-orientation has several disadvantages. As you may already know, people are rebelling against this altercation by signing up with team members only to "solo" team missions to get the good stuff.

Well Funcom, you're discluding about 50% of the people who play this game and 50% of the time that those who usually team with people take to do things on their own.

I'm representing people like me. I love to team with others. It's a lot more fun. And when I do a solo mission, the loot is not very good. There's no incentive. So I sign up with "volunteers" and solo team missions which, I'm sure, you're thinking about nerfing. Solo missions have already been nerfed and might as well not exist. But if you're not thinking of fixing them and making soloing a better place than it is, well, think again.

It would be a grave mistake.

First of all, I only get on for a couple of hours at a time sometimes during which I like to do a mission or two. I'm sure I speak for more people than myself here. We have to solo because we have real lives that we have to take time from to play this game.

So, taking the time out of real-life to do some soloing is common. So it would be nice to get on and do a solo mission with incentive behind it. But you've made that impossible without soloing a team mission as I stated above.

Teaming in the few hours of play that I have just ends up being a waste of time to someone like me because getting a team together takes time, and lots of it. Between the 15 minutes to hour and half it takes to find a team or get one together (and no one likes to be in charge of a team...takes a lot of work and energy), also people go Link Dead, laggs, miscommunications, finding a good mission and area, and the list goes on. Well, there goes my 2 hours and I've done nothing. By the time the team gets started I have to leave.

Secondly, people don't do missions simply for the xp. I mean, that's the only reason why I would do a solo mission because the loot sucks...to speak frankly. But I want loot and experience. That's what the incentive is. And by xp I mean each kill, not the mission reward xp. That sucks too. There I go again speaking frankly.

Thirdly, the xp per kill for soloing a team mission is awesome! You don't have to change that! =) Maybe it's a well-kept secret. But it's hard to take away some good that you've already given us. That is, the ability to solo team missions. However, when you team the xp drops in half but at least you can do higher missions as a team to compensate. Nevertheless, there's something going on here that isn't quite right. I can get 5k per kill for soloing a team mission as a lvl 60 fixer, but 2-3k in a team for the same quality mission? Yes the kills come faster, but that's quite a difference.

Here's some suggestions:

1. Give different types of good loot for both types of missions: team or solo

2. Give better xp for solo missions per kill.

3. Create better outdoor hunting for teams or solo and better loot. Perhaps outdoor hunting areas like you had before...

To touch on that last one...what the heck happened to outdoor hunting? It was great to explore the great outdoors. To get a team together and travel to some outdoor hunting area together. If there's anything that you did to counteract "Team Play" it's nerfing the respawns for outdoor hunting. I mean, gimme a break...that's how I used to team all the time.

How about this? Create outdoor mission with mobs that only players in the team with the mission can fight? That way, the team can get the coords and meet there. The mission might be small...maybe 5-7 mobs or so with a boss that appears only after the 5 -7 are killed? Mobs can be marked by a special symbol on their name to signify they are part of that mission. Just throwing out ideas.

I'm sure you "creative" Funcom people can come up with some ideas about this. But, in closing, there needs to be a "balance" between: A. Team Missions B. Solo Missions C. Outdoor Hunting

If any of these suffer...in this case, Solo Missions and Outdoor Hunting are terribly lacking...leaving a large number of players to be forced to play a certain way which they do not prefer. Eventually this leads to unhappy players. In my opinion, creating a team-mission-oriented game as you most certainly are, will be your own undoing.

Just trying to help as usual...regards.
