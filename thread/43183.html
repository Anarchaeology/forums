An Old RP Memory from the days of Scarney, SA, Real Pol and Iotani Rani.
There was a gathering
within Omni-1 Entertainment District. It was a
gathering of many very different people, both faction
and belief. All aligned under one glorious cause had
these unique souls gathered, lining the Entertainment
District community platform with a colorful array of
nano programs both harmful and helpful.

Citizens passed the growing crowd and it was obvious
that fear was in the air as the group was identified
as none other than the renegade Omni-Tek detachment
The Black Alliance -- Surely this gathering was not
of glorious cause, or was it?

Shade Methlon Peak, President of the Sinister
Anarchists, a branch of The Black Alliance bent on
the assimilation of Omni-Tek and destruction of
several specific detachments, stepped forward from the
crowd of hasty-to-fight soldiers and prepared to
speak.

His words echoed through the city streets, speaking of
the harm that Omni-Tek brought upon all people and of
how The Black Alliance were not the citizens
enemies, but their friends. The Black Alliance would
save Omni-Tek, they would heal the sick and free the
persecuted, lead Omni-Tek into a new age of glory upon
Rubi-Ka.

The crowds were startled and soon enough the OTSEC
Administration had gathered in the square along with
several Omni-Pol operatives. Though these executives
and Omni-Tek officials had gathered, Methlon was not
by any means discouraged. Soon there was a call and
from the alleyways marched single file the Clans.

Lead by Commander Redruum Bacarella, the well known
Opifex commander of Clan Anarchist Syndicate, a member
of the UF assembly of Clans, they marched to the
square and opened attack upon the Omni-Pol.

Mr. Peak commended Commander Redruum on the agreement
that had been made that day, to fight Omni-Tek as one.
The Black Alliance fought along side the Clan
Anarchist Syndicate with the cries of war sounding out
around them.

The assault of the two raged on and eventually it was
shifted towards Omni-HQ via Grid, beginning the real
plan that had been devised.

Omni-Pol Elite guards were called upon and they fought
back the two forces, slaughtering all those in there
way, including three young Omni-Tek employees and four
neutral visitors to the city.

No apology was issued, The Black Alliance forces
were pushed back long with the Clans, while on escape
saving two of the fallen employees who had been
needlessly trampled.

It was at this time that information had persisted and
it was discovered that Omni-Pol had captured Commander
Redruum. His location is said to be confidential and
release is not clear.

Is it really clear what The Black Alliance truly
wishes for Omni-Tek?
