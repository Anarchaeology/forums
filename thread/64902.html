Obviously not Gamers.
Man, if you guys were true gamers...not just peeps who played AO, but played a variety of games in general, you'd realize that Anarchy Online (as well as most other MMORPGs) have the best Price/Play ratio of any other game out there.

Let's take a regular RPG game...standard price for a new game is $45 (average between 40 and 50). You get roughly 80 hours of gameplay from a decent RPG TODAY, this is playing the game through and through until you get absolutely every single ounce of enjoyment out of it. That'll come out to about $0.56 per hour of gameplay.

Let's look at your average FPS game...standard action, shouldn't take more than about 12 hours to beat (if you're a half-decent player), add in an extra 20 hours worth of Multiplay (until you realize that you're the only one in a world full of newbs that can play this game decently), that's roughly 32 hours of playtime, for $45. That comes out to $1.46 per hour of gameply.

Alright...now you've got Anarchy Online. You pay $20 up front, then $12 a month, each month after the first. Let's say you play an average of 1 hour per day each month (that's very very very casual) Now, let's say you play for 3 months. After 3 months, you've played for 90 hours (30 days to a month), and have payed 20+12+12 = $44. You're paying $0.48 per hour of gameplay. Now, let's look at a more realistic AO player...let's say an average of 3 hours per day...after 3 months, you've played 270 hours, and still paid only $44. You're only paying $0.16 per hour of gameplay!!! Heck, throw in another 20 bucks for the booster, and you're still in the nickels and dimes range.

Why complain about pricing? The only way I can see someone complain about pricing on any of these games is if the price suddenly shot up to about $20 or $30 a month, and the game cost regular price to buy. But considering you're basically playing this game for peanuts, why be so whiney about it? Geez, seriously, you're the type of people who just ruin moral for companies like FunCom and Verant who try to make these games. Don't let the AO community turn into a rehash of the EQ-Whiners.

This also extends to people who constantly complain about bugs, bug fixes, "Love for XXX class", "If I don't get this I'm gone", blah blah blah, whine whine whine, boo hoo hoo. It's perfectly OK to point out a bug, a need or a want, but to make these sad posts about how badly you're getting nerfed and need love and all that BS, when you don't even realize that A) you're playing this game for peanuts, and B) this is an incredibly massive piece of software engineering with all of its complexities, ideosyncracies, nuances and hidden problems. Yes, in most software, most bugs are so deeply hidden that they are excruciatingly painful to go in and debug...hence, what to you may seem like "only 1 or 2 lines of code", because you obviously know nothing about software engineering, is actually much much more complex than that.

Now, considering the low price for the high enjoyment we get out of this game, and the fact that maybe 5% of the people who post on these boards actually have a clue about any sort of programming, can we please stop with all the whining? It's just sickening and disheartening...you think the FC developers actually WANT to improve anything when all you got are a bunch of whiney little punks asking for hand-outs?

Just play the game...
