Yixxil, Firestorm Clan, and White Trash Part 2
Sight was by this time at the Grid entrance in Newlands, he thought it best to assess the situation first, so he changed into Leet form, got his shields up and running, and jacked in. He easily found the Clon**** exit, and stopped a moment, wandering why he hadnt heard from Savoy. He opened up a channel, there was no reply. Damn it!.. Better not dally to long here goes nothin. He thought as he jacked out into the World.

As soon as he zipped out there was gun fire going on all around him. He looked around and got his Hud to ID the attackers Firestorm Clanners? he thought to himself, never having heard of them before. He decided to scuttle along a bit further to get a better viewpoint. As he ran around the corner he saw two of the enemy, who suddenly turned to attack. Sightsee couldnt help but let out a High pitched EEEPP!, as they started to shoot him away

Later that night, when Sight had recovered, he decided to get some gossip from the Underground and headed to Baboons. He still felt a bit sore, and had discovered the news, Savoy was luckily insured and so was OK now. Skybert had joined in the fray in Clon**** and good ol Rusty (His Girder weapon) had taken one out before they had heard (On the SRS channel) a Yay hay Rusty!.. Oh O! from the Big atrox that is Skybert. He had perished too shortly after wards.

Sight entered Baboons and donned his Omni Poll clothing (which he had acquired, through dubious means). He thought to make Diamondcut Dave laugh a little, but as he walked towards where he was sat, he noticed Thyme there, and a couple of Mockers too (Smockers?). The air had about it the same feeling he had encountered with the group of people who were talking of this Yixxil. After Sight greeted the stoic looking Diamondcut (with no response from him), he looked dizzy and he nearly fell off the chair-suddenly he just simply vanished with a pop Sight shrugged his shoulders believing it to be some kind of nano program, ( he had seen some other use it to disappear). He did wonder why Diamondcut didnt seem to go through the motions of casting it, until he noticed Trelleme staring at him, at his uniform, he seemed to get a better grip on his Rifle.
Hey Congratulations Sightsee said cheerfully to the two Mockers.
Congratulations for what? Said Trelleme, with sarcasm in his deep voice.
Well you and Diamond have joined forces right? Sight was worried that the information Savoy had seen was incorrect, therefore making Sight look like an idiot once again.
Yea thats right, we Mockers are running security here. Sight didnt believe him. He knew that there was a time not so long ago when Diamonds relationship with The Mockers was very strained to say the least. But he decided not to pursue the matter. Any words that come from any of their mouths he considered to be untrustworthy anyway.

Sight sighed as he left Baboons, he was feeling exhausted mentally speaking. All these thoughts about Lizor and now Lanaea, Yixxil, Firestorm Clanners, White Trash, Mockers and an imminent Extraction job rushed around his head. He decided to get his mind off things and headed to Reets for a few drinks.

When he got there he discovered there was another party going on, he decided to check it out, the dance and Leetpiss would help him to forget the recent stress that he had gained in the last few days. He headed to the rumble room to hear cheers of Cogratz Valaik! and Woot to the married couple! Valaik, the Mockers guy he had some contact with seemed a bit more trust worthy than his colleagues. Sightsee congratulated the newly wed couple, and started to dance and drink some.

Valaiks presence didnt help him to forget his concerns of White Trash, with the help of getting tipsy on the booze he decided to make his concerns know to him. The fact that it was probably the worst time to ask didnt cross Sights mind.
Hey Val, can we talk? Sight asked, his wife Des replied.
No work today OK?
Hey it aint work just gossip hehe. Laughed Sight nervously as the realisation of his picking the wrong time sunk in.
Alright but make it quick! Valaiks voice was commanding and to the point. Ill be right back baby Valaik kissed his wifes hand gently. OK Move to the corridor! he ordered Sight, who obliged and followed him.
I hear you have Baboons now? Sight was trying to catch Valaik out, to get a feel for what was REALLY their agenda. True Val?
Im head of security for the Mockers there, yes.
So whats Diamonds deal now Has he been ousted? Sightsee faked ignorance.
Nope, he still owns the place, Thyme just manages the place, and The Mockers are the security for Baboons. Ill give you a full rundown tommorow if you want? But I need to get back to the party now. Valaik turn and started to walk away.
Fair enough But if I catch you dealing White Trash Im gonna be all over you like a rash Got it? Sight could be firm and imposing when he wanted to, but all Valaik said as he entered the rumble room was I dont deal stims.
Yea right Sight muttered as he followed Valaik back to the party. A few minutes later Sight heard Soloadventurer shout across the music, So where is this big scary demon Lord? To which DJ Tarryk shouted We fed him bagels and he went away!
