The root of what you're complaining about is the fact that no one uses all of the cool empty space out there. The rest are just simple aesthetic preferences you might have from your years of playing whatever other game. It wouldn't have gone too far for anyone who has ever touched UO for even so much as a week to not realize which game you were taking about 90% of your ideas from whether you mentioned the name or not. At least you came clean with that.

As far as the lack of people in all of that empty space and the "ghost towns" out there, the reason for that is because our travel system is TOO easy. Not the other way around. Why is no one out there? They don't have to be. They get a mission, they go in mission, they complete misison, rinse and repeat. Missions are easily accessible at any level from a single grid jump.

So I'm not sure where you're coming from on putting in even MORE insanely simple ways to travel. I think we have plenty.

Most of your suggestions, most notably the part about running to your corpse and having to loot it, I'm not even going to touch with a 10-foot pole. That means more time-sink, and more inane gameplay that I could better spend picking lint from my belly-button or some other equally entertaining activity.

As to social activity on the game, there isn't enough for some, more than plenty for others. In fact, this game, were there more players has more social potential than any other game I've ever played. More clothes with different looks, more emotes, and a bountiful harvest of clubs and other places that go from sleazy to posh, and back. There is even a PvP club for the powergamer/closet roleplayer, mud-wrestling club (kinda) for the more kinky among us, club with a glass floor, a starbucksesque coffee shop, WE GOT AN ATROX IN A WEDDING DRESS. If you've never seen these things, or wonder why they aren't used, think about it next time you get ready to right click on the grid terminal or walk through that whompa before suggesting YET ANOTHER simplistic way to travel.

I'm always one for new ideas, and disdain anything to clone from another game. I play AO for it's originality, and amazing creative team. Most other games have taken from AO, FC doesn't need to return the favor.

Want to find some social draws? Head into Reet's, listen to some Gridstream Productions, look at the immense number of fansites out there and let some of them guide you.

In the meantime, you've registered on Nov 2003, you've had 7 different characters, you haven't logged on for a week, you're saying that whompas are bulky to use (no one uses them btw unless it's for a single hop, maybe 2). To me this says you haven't played the game long enough to really form an opinion. You've played around 3 weeks total, and divided your attention to get all of these characters an average of maybe 15 levels each. Play a prof to level 100 and you understand them somewhat and can form opinions regarding them. Before that, you haven't really *played* a profession.

Tips:
Level a character up a bit and go explore until you die, lots. It's great fun, and you'll find plenty of really neat places and hunting grounds.
Don't pay attention to most of the forum whining, they detract from the fun of the game. Yes, the game is still fun no matter what the fatalists say.
Bored? Go to Stret West Bank, hit up Reet's Retreat, and tune your real player in to listen to Gridstream's webcast. www.gridstream.com
