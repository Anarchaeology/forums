Nice Guide, there is another static Dungeon in "Utopolis Unreedemed Village." There is a bridge that goes from land that is guarded by 3 of those unredeemeds, if you run across bridge you will see a door. Go in that and it is FILLED with tons of unredeemed mobs. If you go to the next room and go up the spiral stairs there is a choice of three more rooms you can go to. If you go down the big straight path instead of the other two which are kinda curved. You will end up in a place where there is alot of "shadow" mobs, that drop loads of Shade armor. Even deeper in that you can find other Static Dungeons altogether. If you have any characters on RK2 Sillirrion I would be happy to show you around there if you like. Once you enter the area where the "shadow" mobs are at they will agro no matter what your level, and there is quite alot of them in there. The next dungeon I spoke about has some pretty big mobs in it. I havent been able to make it too far in there because they hit like 500 damage each and alot of agro.
