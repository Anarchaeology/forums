Sounds like...
Often when you are linkdead, you are linkdead for several seconds or even minutes before the client figures it out, decides it can't get through the to server, and quits the game back to the launcher with that 'You have been disconnected' message. Basically during this time, your client and the server are out of synch. This is why you'll be moving, see yourself moving, then go linkdead and when you come back, you're back where you started from. Your client wasn't sending the movement info successfully to the server, and the server is where your position is stored.

This sounds like what happened to you, only in between desynching and disconnecting you died. It probably disconnected you and 'faded' your character out of the game during the 'white light' part of the death sequence where you haven't actually zoned back to reclaim yet. That's why you logged back in at the place you died, even though you really had died and were in rez shock.
