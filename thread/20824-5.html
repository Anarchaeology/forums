From my own experiences, the only outdoor hunting spots worth the bother are human mob camps.

Monsters hit harder, generally have poor loot, and generally give less xp. So if you're fighting some monster spawn, just know that you'd be getting better xp from similar level human spawns.

I've done very little outdoor hunting myself, since I honestly don't believe there's anyplace outdoors that's as efficient as team missions. However, there have been a few places I felt were close.

Ace camp in eastern foul plains. It's 25% suppression, but I never saw pvp there. There is also a house you can run to when in trouble. It is basically a large compound filled with veterans, aces, really means. Vets assist vets, aces assist aces, etc. Fairly easy to handle adds, the difficulty of which is one of the biggest weaknesses of outdoor hunting. I suppose you could xp here till 200 if you really hate team missions so much. 90+

Bothackers in 20k. Again, more human mobs. Strongly recommend a calmer of some sort as the compound is very small and thus very easy to get many adds. 50-60+

SAV. While I can see that some people liked it...I felt it was horrible xp. (Was around level 110 when I tried it.) Like most outdoor hunting camps, adds were the big problem. Coupled with monster mobs (less xp, more damage per hit), absolutely crummy loot, and being in a zone that most 100ish people do not have a map for...it doesn't even come close to being a 'good' hunting spot to me.

The sad truth is that missions allow you to kill 1-2 mobs at a time, allows you to never have to kill monster mobs, allows you to loot more credits, gives you chests to sell stuff from, and gives you a boss mob that gives you good loot. There isn't a hunting spot on the entire world of Rubi Ka that comes close to that.

Mort and Andromeda
Dunno about lvl, but I explored these areas a little with a lvl 60. Outside of the ICC base, you'll find Red Widows, Vulture Squibblers, and Woodwolves, which were all red to me. Further out, I found Hammerheads (Orange), Bronto Mamas (dark yellow), Pixies ("Burly", don't remember color), Minimammoths (yellow, I think), Offal Larvae (green), and others, pretty much in abundance.

Take the whompa to Hope (mort) and go west out of the gate. There's a crater just out of eyeshot of the town, where there were more Red Widows, as well as a breed of snake (don't remember name, also red).

That's to say, I don't know what level all this would be good for, though solo hunting around Andromeda would probably be feasible at 60+ (maybe high 50s).
