great money-maker for clan newbies...
i know it's hard to believe for people who are twinked or in guilds, but a lot of us have trouble getting enough money, especially at lower levels. i just ran across this myself trying to save up for ql50 implants at lvl 33. i also needed to buy a bunch of nf's and upgrade my armor, so i started running a few missions. i found that if you go to avalon and get ql29 missions from the terminals there, you can grab lots of old athens missions. what i did was use aomd to look for missions with special items in old athens and grabbed them 3 at a time. look for pillows, beams, mission-only nf's like enfraam's or animas, med suits, stuff you can sell in the shopping channel. the missions themselves pay around 11k each and are pretty small. of course, since i was lvl 33, the mobs weren't scary, either.

anyway, just wanted to pass this along to others getting started and having a hard time getting money in the early levels.

Probably not too viable anymore, but I made millions before I was level 20 by offering to sit on the shopping channel and sell stuff for high level mission runners. It took a while to get started (the obvious trust issues had to be circumvented :)), but before long I had a huge client base. I asked for 20% of the proceeds of each sale, which was a great deal of money with no risk and little effort. I actually had a fairly large amount of competition, so there were many lowbies who were making money this way back then.

Alas, auctionbot took my best sellers away (and I eventually got bored with it), so I ended up going out of business. It is probably still viable though, there were quite a few who preferred my personal service.
