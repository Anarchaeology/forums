Neutral Token Board - Disaster in the Desert
[ Note the following story is a hook for a roleplaying event related to teh Neutral Token Board storyline . See below for details of the next event]

In a remote Newland desert location, dressed in full radiation suit Dabblez regards the disaster site. All around her she see twisted metal, sand fused into glass and a mountain of black, black ash.

"We've got five minutes, boss," says Noholdsbarre. "After that even the rad suit isn't going to help us."

"What happened here, Noholds?"

"Not entirely sure yet. At 4 AM exactly we lost all signals from the central nanobot infusion generator plant, so I had some of the boys go take a look. This is what they reported." The atrox gestures with his large arms at the surrounding wreckage.

"Any idea what caused this?"

"Not yet. The explosion was thermo-nuclear in nature."

"The Nanobot generation plant for the neutral token board was not nuclear." protests Dabblez.

"Exactly." nods the atrox.

"A bomb then?"

"Best theory I can come up with so far."

"But, who would have done something like this? I know some people were against the project, but to use a nuke? Besides, this location was secret. No one outside the project new about it."

Noholds shrugs. "It gets worse."

"Worse than this?" mocks Dabblez.

Noholds looks away. "The generator was loaded with notum-charged nanobots ready to be transmitted to test token boards at the time of the explosion. Just a routine test. The sudden release of all that notum we think might have interfered with the operators insurance reclaim processing."

"Are you saying that the victims of the blast are dead-dead?"

"We are still searching. Maybe they just reclaimed somewhere unexpected and have not been able to get in touch yet. But with each passing hour the chances of finding any survivors gets slimmer."

"How many?"

"There were sixteen men, women and atrox manning the generator plant at the time."

Beneath the rad suits helmet, all colour drains from Dabblez face. "I think I am going to be sick."

"Come boss, nothing more to do here and our five minutes are almost up. I'll have a robotic decontamination unit sent out here to clean the place up so we can conduct a proper investigation."

"Sixteen people..." whispers Dabblez.

"I leave, and this is what happens..." Lt. Craddock was sitting at his desk in Omni-Pol HQ, reviewing the latest reports at a data terminal. He probably would have passed by the report concerning a large explosion in Newland Desert (that wasn't Omni-Pol juresdiction, why should it concern him?), but something about the coordinates seemed familiar. Then it hit him; that was the location of one of R.U.R.'s Neutral Token Board facilities. His first thought was of concern for his ex-coworkers, followed by a sense of frustration that Dabblez hadn't said anything to him. Did she not trust him? Did she think that Omni-Pol would sit by while Omni-Tek citizens were blown up? He scrolled through the report, but didn't find any important information. He checked the patrol schedule, and noticed an opening on Sunday. Good, he thought, the men could use a trip to the desert anyways. A few taps on the keyboard, and a patrol was set up to investigate the scene of the explosion. Satisfied, he shut off the terminal, and set out for Rompa Bar...

Report from on site invesitgation of the nanobot notum induction plant disaster

First of all, many thanks to The Honored Maidens and Omni Mining for providing support in numbers, Ariekel and Kattybrie for the Clans, Yarko and Boltgun for the Neutrals. As there was opposition to the Neutral Token Board project from all three sides of the conflict, it is good and healthy that we should have investigators from all sides looking into it.

In a nutshell, we travelled from Stolt's outpost to the disaster site. There the technical teams got to work taking readings, soil and other samples for proper forensic examination. In the middle of this, a band of cyborg worker drones ( old, 'Buzzsaws' models) attacked us. The borgs were destroyed except for one we kept alive for questioning.

The borg, being a low ranking model, had very little useful information. All it could tell us was that it team had been sent here by 'Cyborg Command Hive V512' to clean up the area and remove any remaining traces of borg presence.

This suggests the borgs were behind the bombing. It gives us no indication as to why or if the borgs might have been working for another party, as several people suggested. It would also be extremely useful if we could map the cyborg name 'Hive V512' to an specific cyborg base.

I am sure more information will come out from the forensic examination of the samples taken.
