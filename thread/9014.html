Ummm... Yeah... Right...

Originally, according to an official post here, the patch was supposed to go live on 1/23. However, the patch wasn't even put on Test until a few days before that. Not nearly enough time to find problems (of which there were, and are quite a few). So the 23rd comes and no patch and no word from FC about it. At all. Since I play on Test I asked a GM there. The answer I got was "sometime next week". Several days ago there was another official post that the patch was going to go live the first half of this week, by 1/30 at the latest. That's tomorrow and still no official word if the servers are planned to come down and Cosmik, in particular has been completely silent (on any issue) for the last 3 days. So I'm willing to wager a lot of credits that the patch *will not* be tomorrow.

Don't get me wrong. I have no problem with the patch being delayed if there are problems still to resolve, but the fact that they won't even tell us that's the case is disturbing, to say the least. In any event, don't hold your breath for a patch any time soon.
