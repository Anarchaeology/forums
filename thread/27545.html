What does roleplaying mean in AO?
This online gaming is new to me. I hear people talk about "roleplaying", but it seems to mean different things to different people.

Options:

1) Roleplaying is being an Omni or Clan.

2) Roleplaying is acting the part of your profession to provide specialized services in the game.

3) Roleplaying is playing dressup, shaking your booty in a disco, drinking fake wine as you dine on fake cheese to watch the fake suns set, or decorating your apartment.

4) Speaking and acting in character (I, Atrox,need eat. No bathe, kill leet.)

5) All of above

6) Other

What do you mean when you say you are "roleplaying"?
