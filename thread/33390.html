Gridstream Productions Books Dates at Baboons Nightclub
Omni Entertainment, Rubi Ka (June 24, 29476) - Devils Advocate and Gridstream Productions are proud to announce that Gridstream will be providing DJs to Baboons Nightclub located in Omni Entertainment.

"We are very excited to have Tarryk, Boco and Shadowrunrr booked to perform live at Baboons!" exclaimed Devils Advocate's president Thyme. "To kick it all off, we're introducing a new drink that is being provided exclusively to Baboons. I don't want to give away what it is, but I think our customers are really going to like it! We will also have a new staff on hand that is being managed by Nano Lust. They're a top notch, professional outfit!"

All bookings have been arranged by Gridstream Productions' Wyldthing. Dates and times for the Baboons shows are as follows:

Tuesday, June 26th: DJ Shadowrunrr
Monday, July 1st: DJ Tarryk
Friday, July 12th: DJ Shadowrunrr
Thursday, July 18th: DJ Boco

Tarryk's shows all start at 8pm (EST)
Shadowrunrr's shows all start at 10pm (EST)
Boco's shows all start at 8pm (EST)

Baboons Nightclub is easily accessible via the Grid in the Entertainment District.

Been a while since I viewed the boards.

I haven't been contacted about playing at Baboon's just yet, but I think that is doable, just not this week.

This week we have something a bit special for Saturday. We plan on making the Neutral Bar our main stomping grounds, and use the Reet Retreat on our Saturdays as scheduled. I think this week we'd like to open up with a blast, so we will be moving our show to the Neutral Bar for Saturday instead of the Retreat.

I've been caning some of those Icey tunes this week so you can expect to have em on Saturday since this will be our biggest set ever. (Last weekend was almost 60 records, this will be even better.)

There will most likely be some advertisement on RK2 as well, just I obviously can't be both places, but you can definately listen to the music.

I'm streaming my mixes in two qualities now: one approximately 64k/44khz and another 32k/22khz. That means almost CD quality for the first one for you Cable/DSL/ISDN listeners and just under FM radio quality for 56k listeners.

I hope everyone enjoys the new stuff, it should sound more than twice as good as it had about a week ago.

On a final note, I've written a small program to do online playlists and voting. There are instructions given out at the parties but it is only a matter of visiting the site, entering in your character name and rating the tunes on a 1-10 scale. Since I have had so many requests for what is/has played, this can be used as an easy way to see what you liked in the mix to check out later by saving the playlist and searching for your 10s.
