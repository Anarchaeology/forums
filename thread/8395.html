Some profs needs a nerf, some needs boost, healing, debuffs and crits is overpowered!
We all know the balance between profs is broken, a few profs got a godmode, and these needs a nerf, other profs is broken and needs a fix, this is my list:

Ill start with the overpowered profs (those that needs a nerf):

Trader: Traders is currently one of the most powerful prof in the game, because of their debuffs, healing and good shotgun skill. Even docs cant stand a chance against a trader, but it depends on who debuffs who first, if doc debuffs with slow first, he got time to stack a few dots before trader can debuff. Their debuffs is to powerful, makes opponent useless, docs cant heal, NTs cant nuke, enfs cant rage, soldiers cant MK etc, and the list goes on, all this while the trader crits you for 1k+ dmg and drains your health and adds to his own.

Doctor: Competing with Trader on who is the most powerful prof in the game, between trader and doc it is who debuffs who first that wins the fight. Healing is overpowered, no profession in the game can solo a doc (except trader and MP), this is becuase docs heals much faster than any other prof can do dmg. The slow debuff is to powerful, it makes opponent unable to do good dmg over time, a -1450 init debuff makes any lvl 200 char slower than a lvl 1 char at max agg, you get to fire a bullet/execute a nuke every 10 secs with a fast gun/nuke! all this while you got stacked dots running in your NCUs and the doc nukes and fires his gun at you. You simply dont stand a chance.

Meta Physicist MP is in the same boat as docs and traders, between these 3 profs it is who debuffs the other first that wins. Nano shutdown is the most powerful debuff in the game, if this debuff successfully hits opponent (which it does 99% of the time, NR is bugged, it has to be) no profession in the game can take down an MP. This is ofcourse becuase they got a healing pet, which has unlimited nano pool for 30 min, and because of the 50% dmg and 100% heal, the pets heals faster than the dmg inflicted. + they got their attack pet hitting you, while you get shot/nuked at the same time, awesome combo with free heals and attacks, + their own nukes and guns, overpowered. I laugh everytime i see MP say they are "just a very well balanced profession" Haha, compared to who? Docs and traders yes, but MPs are uber compared to all others.


None of the above is combat profs, but rule pvp and pvm, it should not be like taht, they should be at the bottom of this list.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Then we have the "balanced" professions (those that needs minor adjustments):

Enforcer This is a good profession, they are not invincible, but can be hard to kill if played right. They got awesome health and good "heals" (which of course needs to be 50%, overall, not only enf) and do great dmg if they got beam/hammer, which crits for alot (crits needs nerf too, overall nerf, not only enf) a fairly well balanced profession

Soldier Soldier is not as good as Enforcer, and needs some adjustments, but their MK shield is very powerful, maybe to powerful, MK X lasts for 1. min 20 sec. and can be racast 40 secs after it wears off, so every 2 min they are close to invulnerable for 1.33 min. But, MK shield is the only thing soldier have, without it they would be very gimped, both dmg wise and defense, but something has to change.

Agent I can only feel sorry for this profession =(, they have really felt the nerf hammer, and now they will probably loose their MP pets (but this is needed, agents should not have MP pets, this is a very profession specific skill for MPs, and should belong to MPs only). They also felt the range nerf badly and dmg nerf cap. AS is now gimped, i really dont like instakills, but it should do more reliable dmg. Yes sometimes agents hits for 10k as dmg in PvM, but this is not often, maybe once every 50 AS shot. Conceal is broken, but will get fixed (FC is very slow on this, Agents are leaving the game, needs to be fixed quickly!) FC has said taht conceal is going to work in PvP too, that would be cool, looking forward to this. Otherwise their fp is very good, many agents go fp soldier to use MK shields, and they can pretty much self buff almost everything. A fairly well balanced profession, at least when things get fixed.

Engineer This is a good balanced profession, they do not rock in PvP, but can do ok against nonrooting professions, especially if they got a good shotgun or any other hard hitting weapon with specals. PvM they do very well, solo they can take down reds easy, even aces can be soloed by enfs with good bot healing nanos and a high ql offensive trimmer to keep the aggro on bot. This is first and foremost a trade skill profession, that is where their skills shine, fix Kamikaze bots and Engineers should do very well.

Bureaucrat The crat is the "unknown" but i really think this profession is very good. They got charms, and can charm up to 2 mobs. Imagine a crat with 2 Real Mean Enforcers and their Bodyguard pet + nukes and weapons, they should do very well in PvP. They also got very good speech nanos (spams alot, needs to change) and their xp buff makes them very wanted in teams. They also got AE roots and AE calms, making them even more wanted in teams. Their nukes is very good too, not as good as NTs, but their top nuke is very close. Crats is a very good profession, at least what i have seen of them, only time will tell if they need a nerf or boost, nerf is more likely if FC do not decide to give the below professions a boost.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Then we have the gimped professions that needs a boost:

Nano-Technician This is the least gimped profession of the gimped, but dont get me wrong, they are gimped. Range nerf hit NT really bad, very much because of their low defenses. They are now forced to stay within range of other ranged weapon users, making roots very ineffective against them, except when they need to run (problem is of course roots are easily broken with exploits, but we cannot balance the game out of exploits so this should not be taken into the consideration). NTs also get outdamaged by weapon professions (beause crits is to powerful, needs nerf), this makes the whole point of being an NT pointless, why sacrifice defenses when you dont get anything back for it. It is a bit pathetic when a weapon prof can fire 3 bullets for every nuke they execute, and if one hit is a crit (which is very usual, at least with crit buff), weapons outdamage them easily. NTs is mostly wanted for HE buffs and calms, but Crats got calms too, so if there were more crats in Rubi-Ka, the crat would be chosen over an NT without doubt. NTs needs a boost.

Martial Artist MA is only wanted for their heals and crit buff, they do not do any dmg to speak of, unless they pump up hammer or shotgun. But MAs are gimped, and most professions own them in PvP. MA needs a boost.

Adventurer Only wanted in teams for heals, the only good damage dealing Advs i have teamed with is those with Shotguns. Adventurers needs a boos and get their polymorph nanos fixed and some other stuff to make them worthwhile to play. Currently they are just a bad doctor, they got worse heals with same weapon, what is the point of being an adv when they heal worse and do same damage as docs?

Fixer Maybe the most gimped prof in the game, to bad they all run around with gimped maussers, only a few has chosen other weapons, and only nice buff they have is run buff, it is very good in PvP, but they need more stuff to make them a valid profession. Fixers needs a boost.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Then we have gone through all the professions, and some needs a boost, some needs a nerf, there is no way FC will balance the professions to equal the current state of docs/traders/MPs. Thats why we will have to nerf some, and obviously doc, trader and MP will have to feel the nerf stick very soon.

Here is a list of what needs a nerf:

Healing Healing needs to be nerfed to 50% and capped at 40% of max health, this should be obvious because damage is nerfed the same way.

Criticals Crits is currently overpowered in the game, all who has teamed with MAs for hunting knows the mobs goes down 10x as fast with a crit buff. Crit buff needs to be self only, that would be a good step towards balance.

Shotguns Shotguns do more damage than assault rifles, and work against duck, which is a great advantage, assault rifles have already been nerfed, now shotguns is up for a nerf, 50% of rubika is using shotguns, in a month at least 70% will use them, something is wrong when we see advs, MAs and soldiers running around with shotguns. Shotguns need a nerf, to even out the gap between them and ARs, and duck skill should be removed from the game, evade and dodge is more than enough.

Debuffs Debuffs is as it is now to powerful, roots breaks when dmg is done to the rooted, same should happen with all other debuffs, nano shutdown should bread when damage is done to the debuffed, the same with doc slow buff and trader skill debuffs.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

These changes are needed to balance the professions, and is needed fast, this is the only way to balance the game, as it is now, the game is broken, i wish FC would make these changes next patch.
