Both ideas have problems from a technical standpoint, I'm afraid. Currently, AO keeps a library of shapes and textures on your hard disk. When the server tells the client that it needs a particular texture, your machine can load it from this local cache, which is a lot faster than having to download it from the servers.

To have a custom texture for a guild cloak would mean one of two things:
  • Textures for guild cloaks have to be downloaded from the server, which takes longer and adds to the chop.
  • Textures for guild cloaks are released as part of a patch update, meaning delays of a month or more before your uniform is ready.
The same problems apply to guild logo items that sit in HUD or utility slots, of course.
