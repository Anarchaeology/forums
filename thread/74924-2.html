Oh, by the way, I have great news for enforcers. Because enforcers are in such desperate need for love, Funcom has decided to take away the following class abilities for the following classes:

Adventurers: They lose their heals and Morphs

Agents: They lose False Profession and 100%/30% crit.

Engineers: Well, there wasn't much to take away, but they took away their pets and reflect shields.

Doctors: All their heal costs are doubled, and Complete Heal now requires 1000 BM/MM.

Soldiers: Their TMS and Reflect shields were taken away. (Well, Reflect / TMS still work, but it requires Target: Enforcer.)

Martial Arts: Crit buffs and heals taken away

Nano-Techs: All nukes are taken away except Ice Flechette. NS has a 20 second recharge time.

MetaPhys: Mochams/Infuses/Masteries/Teachings have been removed* and their pets have been taken away.

Crats: Their pets have been removed, Mez/Charms removed.

Fixer: Grid armor removed, and all of their HoTs/Runbuffs were given to enforcers.

Traders: Drains were removed from their nanoline, along with their healing line.

*Because it was nice for enforcers to have Mochams at times, all the enforcer nanos now require 1 in all nanoskills.


It looks like Funcom is finally taking a stand on the poor performance of enforcers. But, will this be enough? Maybe this along with the juncture of the ideas listed above will make enforcers a decent class.
