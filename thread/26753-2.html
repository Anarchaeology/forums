Hi.. just a few things I need to make perfectly clear..

1 These people I lovingly call "Dreams" ..are doing this out of the kindness of their hearts.. therefore I personally feel responcible for their saftey and well being.. if there is any Dream that feels he or she needs a Chaperone for the date.. I will provide one at the request of the Dream.

2 There is absolutely no group bidding..what I mean by that is..
if there is a group of guys pooling their money together to win a bid.. then decide which of you get the date.. under no circumstances will a Dream be required to go on a date with more then one person. For their own saftey.. and its a social date anyway so I just wanted to put that rule in there.. JUST in Case..

3 To keep this a FUN EVENT ..and in the spirit of FAIRNESS
there will be a limit.. ONE Dream per Dreamer. I don't want some filthy rich person thinking they can waltz up and bid on a handful of guys or girls and walk away with more then one date. One Date per person. I know its a charity event.. but honestly for me.. everyone having fun and a rocking party is more important then the possiblilty of the guild bank overflowing.


4 This I mentioned before.. it is a SILENT Bid.. any Bids SHOUTED OUT will be IGNORED.. I have an auctioneer for the event to handle bid tells.. please don't flood him (DJ shadowrunrr) or send in bogus bids..give him time to keep track of the bids so he can announce the HIGHEST BID ..so the bidding can either close or continue.. PLEASE THis Is Important.. for the bidding to actually happen..


In addition to the Dreams that will be present the day of the event.. I have some dreams that are still willing to help out ..but just can not be availible to be here for the event itself..
So Im proposing an absentee bid.. if the absent Dream is bidded upon and the Dreamer gets the absentee bid.. you and the Dream will recive an email from either myself or a member of the Healing Hands as a reciept of sort.. that way you will know yes you are still entitled to this date and the Dream knows she acutally has a date to plan with you.

Oh yeah..seeing how this is a charitable event and nothing more then the promise of a social date, a chance to know someone ect ect..there will be no refunds given if your date does not go as planned.. so either you should know what you think a Dream Date..NOTE THE WORD Dream Date ..Not EROTIC DREAM..WET DREAM ect ect.. but a nice date would be..perhaps a Dream already has a plan..but maybe not.. please don't think cause you have donated to the cause that things are going to just fall into place.. communication is key.

Please just lets all try to have fun with this.. since at the moment its impossible for me to do so..
