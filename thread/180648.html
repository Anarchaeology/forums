ENCRYPTION CODE: 987.089.65

From: Marcai (personal terminal)

To: Grid-net (all)

Subject: Time to form some peace

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------


Hello citizens of Rubi-ka (clan, Omni-Tek, or Neutral),


All of Rubi-ka has seen war, even the farthest reaches of our great planet. I personally have seen so many battles it is hard to count. I am tired of this war, I am part of Omni-Pol, I want to terminate criminal activity, not clanners. That is why today I decree that we all have a meeting to try to begin the road to peace. Let Omni-tek and the clans try to work out a peace agreement for the time being while the clans try to re-establish the council of truth and reform as a whole.

This meeting in no way involves Omni-Pol at all, it is my personal intent to try to bring a temporary peace settlement. I even call Radiman himself to attend the meeting to try to bring a temprary calm to the storm.

Please my fellow Omni-Tek emplyees, we must try to form a peace, it is far more profitable than a civil war.

Please clans, come to the meeting to form a peace settlement that you want so desperatly.


I will hold the meeting at Reets retreat. The suppression gas will be at 100% at the meeting to avoid any "accidents." The time of the meeting is at 7:00 PM EST.

Thank you,

Marcai

PS: Post below if you wish to attend.

END TRANSMISSION

I am hoping to get alot of support from all sides.

Interesting initiative, unfortunately 7PM EST is going to be too late for me. I can send my best wishes and perhaps a few thoughts. There are warmongers and there are moderates on both sides; hawks and doves if you will. You can get the Clan and Omni doves around the negotiating table, that is still not going to have any impact on the hawks. Worse still, what we have seen in the past is that the actions of the hawks will eventually drive a wedge between the doves of different sides for, when it comes right down to it, most people place loyalty to their side before loyalty to their own ideals of peace.

So the challenge really comes down to this;
Can the doves find a means to keep in check the hawks of their own side?
Can the doves of both sides come to such a level of bonding and understanding that the hawks of either side can no longer tear them apart?

If you can solve these age old problems you deserve a statue in every town on Rubi-Ka.
