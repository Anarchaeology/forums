A large battlefield
This may have been suggested before but i write it as a reminder.

I have been thinking about the large battlefield in the end of the intro movie. why don't make some large PvP areas that can be used as battlefields, where teams from
omni-tek and clans can meet and fight each other until there is only 1 side left alive.


For some time ago i came a cross a large fight in Daoc (Dark ages of Camalot), and it was some of the most spectacular fight i have ever seen in a game. The two sides lineup on the ridges of a smal valley, ready to kill each other. When they meet on the bottom of the valley the magicians starts trowing spells on the enemy, and the entire battlefield lights up.


To have something like this in AO would be a great boost too the game.
