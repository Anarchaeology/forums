Illuvatar has the right of it -- I also calm ql 200+ missions w/o much difficulty. If you want to end the auto-attack, just go max defense, that is fixed again. Use a rune, like adv. layered prot, to absorb the initial hits, and make sure your evades and armor are decent, and only multiple aces/RMs are all that tough.

However, there are some weird issues with calm.

1) Mobs will attack 1-3 times even after a report that the nano is succesful. I doubt this is an intentional feature. I suspect it has something to do with client/server communication. I.e. The attacks are being passed to the client at the same time as the successful calm is being sent up to the server. This is a guess based on the fact that more lag seems to increase the problem. What you see in a mission is rarely accurate -- e.g. mob location is frequently misreported, causing all the targeting problems. Running during recharge helps solve the problem.

Anyone out there w/a T3?

The worst thing you can do:
recalm. this starts the process all over again lol. Take your hits and like it, calmng missions is cheesy anyway...

rooting first is also an excellent tactic, especially outdoors where mob location is a bit more accurately reported.

2) a worse problem is that calms can be reported as successful, but then drop off, or never actually take despite the success report and the graphic. Sorting case 1) from case 2) is the big problem of calming missions.
Basically i use a 4 hit rule. After 4 hits, I recalm.

3) MY main problem with calm is different though. Since i mostly group, occasionally mobs will reaggro other players even though they are calmed. This happens most often when the mob walks away after the calm. Doc. team nanos seem to generate this occasionally, which is really a drag. Have others noticed the same thing?

4) Puppy dog effect: mobs will occasionally trail around after you after being calmed. If this was reliable, it would make a great pulling tool, but it is inconsistent. Again, rooting first solves.

Final note: I think calming missions is an awful game feature, to tell you the truth. Same with blitzing. It provides some mild tense fun, but mostly it is too rewarding and way too easy. Yesterday I pulled almost an entire set (-one sleeve) of ql 200 nano armor from chests. I guess it is something to do besides AE grey mobs looking for nullity, or waiting for a group. I only just started doing it in the last 2 lvls (i got impatient for ql 200 set of blank implants), but i think it ultimately robs the game of a lot of excitement by maikng the best gear in the game too readily available.

Of course, having something else to do would, as usual, solve the problem.
