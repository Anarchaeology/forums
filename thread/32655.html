I already do this from time to time. The trouble with just handing out starter packs is that if the player is truly new, they may be so far back on the learning curve that they won't know what to do with any of it, much less be able to self-equip half of the stuff.

As for myself, I tend to wander around the backyards in Omni Ent handing out buffs and lowbie nanos, usually a backpack full of profession-specific ones.
