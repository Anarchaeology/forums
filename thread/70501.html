The original poster was well aware that Shopbot was made by a player. His point was that Funcom needs to do something similar. That is, make a selling and auction tool that is fully integrated into the game.

As such, his post is in exactly in the right spot: suggestions for new features.

I fully agree. The selling and other bots made by players, while better than nothing, are ultimately hacks, because they can only operate through text. You can't store or retrieve items without direct personal interaction; there are no terminals (ie. a fully functional GUI) in the world to browse, order, select, and bid on stuff, etc; no full catalog of items currently under auction. Etc. Etc.

Such features are basic to a workable economy. The currrent selling channels are absurdly primitive, and promote spamming, and standing around for hours trying to buy and sell stuff, that may or may not be of interest to people, or available. Shopbot was a step in the right direction, but by no means a complete feature. What we need is something well thought out and complete. This is Funcom's job.
