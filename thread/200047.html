Sneak peek: December issue of SoS~Mag is coming!
From: ~Society of Salvation~ ::Entertainment Department::
To: The Citizens of Rubi-Ka and Jobe ::The Buzz::
Re: SoS~Mag ::Upcoming December Issue::
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Hey all you beautiful Rubi-Ka people!

The ~Society of Salvation~ is proud to announce yet another release of our SoS~Mag this December! We've had such success with previous issues, this one could be even better!

Our new Entertainment Department General, Gwendolyn "Ghiggles" Ghirl, and her SoS~Mag staff are storming up some awesome ideas. A few of the features we're working on for this issue are:

  • Short Stories
  • NEW! Personal diary entries from ~SoS~ Members
  • News from Rubi-Ka and the Shadowlands
  • NEW! Jokes and Humorous pictures
  • NEW! Advice Column
  • Our well-known "~Pinups~" section
  • NEW! Personal Notes Page!
  • Advertisements


Our new "Personal Notes Page" will be for ANY readers and interested parties to submit messages, quotes, or just anything to SoS~Mag. For a few credits (we're not sure how much just yet, but all proceeds go toward the funding of future SoS~Mag publishing and ~SoS~ parties!) we'll include any text you like in this section, as long as it's not hostile or offensive. Great for announcements, marriage proposals, love notes, getting your organization name seen and heard, or even just to say 'Hello!'

This issue will also be titled "Holidays in the Shadowlands" and will include numerous pictures of the different scenery in Nacense, Elysium, Scheol, and anywhere else we can get to with our cameras! Reports of Shadowlands excursions, stories of adventures, feel free to submit some to us!

To ask questions, submit stories, advertisements, or to send letters for our Advice Column and notes to our Personal Notes Page, just email the ~SoS~ at sosmagazine@hotmail.com . Be sure to properly identify your subject so we can sort it faster!

Thanks to everyone for making SoS~Mag such a hit!



The ~Society of Salvation~ Entertainment Department

~ Gwendolyn "Ghiggles" Ghirl- General and Editor-in-Chief

~Damon "Korejar" Thompson- Part-time Editor

~Juliet "Lunya" Lochlan- Part-time Creative Staff & Writing

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The ~Society of Salvation~ is an Omni-Tek organization committed to providing Rubi-Ka with the upbeat, fun, and diplomatic environment we believe this planet needs. The content of SoS~Mag and other ~SoS~ productions may not always reflect the views and values of all members, but we try to make sure a majority is represented in all decisions and that our ~Mag, parties, and our members are friendly to everyone, regardless of race, gender, or political affiliation.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

~ RESPECT ~ TOLERANCE ~ FUN ~

Wanna put an Ad in the SoS~Mag? Here's the price:
Looking for somewhere to advertise your org, an event, party, or service? The SoS~Mag has just the space for you! The ~Mag will be seen by countless eyes accross Rubi-Ka, and for a small fee those eyes can see your message!

Here are the prices for Ad space in the upcoming December issue of SoS~Mag (in Credits):

  • 1/4 sheet advertisement 25k
  • 1/2 sheet advertisement 50k
  • Full sheet advertisemtent 100k


You should provide all the information you want included in the ad, as well as any pictures you wish to be published. Sorry, pictures can not be returned. Also specify any text you wish to appear in or under the photo. In order to follow Omni-Tek regulations, the ~Society of Salvation~ cannot accept recruitment ads from Clan or Neutral organizations.

If you'd like to advertise a party or event, make sure you send us the date, time, location, and contact information (including which dimension).

Please contact Gwendolyn "Ghiggles" Ghirl, Entertainment General; or send an email to sosmagazine@hotmail.com to purchase adspace.

Our deadline is fast approaching, so grab your ad and send it in to the ~SoS~ today!
