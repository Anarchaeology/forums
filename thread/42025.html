Stare at the suns : Fiction pt 1 and 2
Well,. following the lead of many I have started to write my backstory. Just a few things to mention first.

1. I am by no means a writer and I expect there will be people who read it and go....um...yup..she's not a writer

2. I expect that my knowledge of rubi-ka history is still a little weak and it is quite possible I am completly wrong in my way of interpreting how things are done. This was just what I came up with given the information I had, and filling in some blanks a long the way

3. The story is ongoing with part 3 actually being role played in the game. Part 2 will be posted soon and if anyone would like to play a role in the story, please let me know, and I'll try to include you...in the backstory and in the present story. everyone is welcome, but not everyone will be included.

4. I will edit this as I learn what information needs to be fixed in order for it to make sense in the play world.

I hope you enjoy...feel free to post comments below
