Oci: In the launcher there should be an option labelled "Register Account" on the bottom right area. In the window that opens when you click that, go to "register notum wars" link in the top right of that window. Put in the Key labelled "Notum Wars" from your CD jacket in that box. NOT the key labelled "Anarchy Online CD Key." If you put in one key and nothing works, put in the other. If neither does anything, then you've got a problem and should contact support@anarchy-online.com.
