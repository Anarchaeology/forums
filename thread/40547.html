There was a full IP reset option during 14.2 which is no longer available, but you can reset individual skills using IP reset points. You get a certain number every title level, which stack with any you have saved up. At lvl 84 you should have 5 reset points, which allows you to completely reset 5 skills and recover the IP from them.

Note that there are horror stories of people using the reset and simply losing the points for one reason and another. Personally, I've used it twice on two different characters and had no problems, whether resetting one skill or multiple skills.

In order to do a reset, your character must have all implants removed, be wearing no armour and equipped with no weapons or other gear. Then just click the RS button in your skills view, click on a skill and it'll be reset for you.
