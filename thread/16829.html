Sounds to me like you have some serious syncing problems going on between you and the server.

1- Mobs hitting after you gain xp/are told you may loot: Everyone is getting this right now, it'll get fixed in time. Usually they only hit once, 24 times seems a bit excessive. You probably actually took that damage during the fight, and the client was made aware of this damage only after the fight (due to bad connection/slower computer).

2- Again, this could be lag. MP casts a nuke, client thinks that the mob took X damage and displays accordingly. In reality, however, the mob only took Y damage (X > Y), and when the server alerts the client to this fact, the client updates accordingly. What you see is the mob gaining health. It's all 'lag'.

3- Instant nukes can be blamed on the same thing as above. You don't see the mob casting during battle because your client doesn't know that the mob is casting, as the server has not told it yet. The client only finds out that a nuke was being cast when the nuke goes off. You see this as instant damage. Lag again.

4- Lag again. The pet is merrily beating the snot out of some mob, and the server is registering all the hits, but it is neglecting to tell the client that the pet doing damage.

5- This one is weird, you cast a nano, the chat window says "Nano executed successfully" but the nano never takes effect. This is almost definately lag, as most times if you wait a few seconds, the nano will actually go off.

6- I have no idea what's wrong with the macros on the toolbars vanishing when you log/get kicked. I get this sometimes, and its very annoying, I agree. My best guess would be something odd happening to your config files, where all your macros are stored, but again, I have no real idea. My solution? I wrote a simple script that makes all of my macros for me in one shot, giving me a 3 second delay after making each one so that I can place it on the toolbar . Not the correct solution, I know, but it works.

All this week, I've been fuming, thinking that all this crap that's going on is something Funcom could have, and should have, fixed. Hell, it's still hard not to think that... but let me point something out to you, see if it helps.

I didn't have much trouble with the symptoms you are having at all after the patch, up until this week actually... then it started, where I hit 6 times straight instantly at the beginning of the fight, the mob stays standing and hitting me while it tells me the mob's already dead, where I hit a heal spell (playing on my girlfriend's doc trying to level her) and I have to sit there, and sometimes it'll finally come off after about 4 or 5 seconds, sometimes not at all, and I end up smacking the key and getting the whole "Executing blah program, nanoformula aborted, executing blah program, nanoformula aborted" loop...

And when I get kicked out of the game, it doesn't save any of the new macros I've done... I think it does that when you log out normally, honestly, not when you're kicked...

But here's the point to all this. I did a ping to www.anarchy-online.com and to the 2 dimension servers, and was getting about 60% packet loss. I thought "Great, Funcom's servers and network isn't able to hold us. Just great."

So I did a traceroute. In case you're not familiar with this all (and please excuse me if you are, just trying to help out) I did

Start->Run
type 'Command' hit enter
type 'tracert www.anarchy-online.com > trace1.txt'

Wait. A while. That'll output the path your packets are taking to Funcom, and it'll tell you with little *'s where the timeouts are occurring. Check it for the 2 dimension servers, it should show up the same (did for me).

Lo' and behold, on the exodus network, some backbone provider apparently, I'm losing just about ALL my information. Now, I don't know if Exodus is some internal network to Funcom, but given that my packets are doing about 10 hops on there, and about 7 of them are losing at least half the packets that get sent, I think that the network issue we're all facing is something external to Funcom, but something they need to know about.

This has been happening to me every night from about 8 p.m. EST to 10 p.m. EST, same symptoms, same routers screwing up. I keep logging the packet routes and drops, and I'll be emailing them to Funcom, but you may want to go ahead and try to 'ping -n 50 www.anarchy-online.com > ping1.txt' and replace 'www.anarchy-online.com' with the two dimension servers (at work so I can't look up their address in the AO directory) and see if you're running into the same issues I have been...

I hope some of this helps. It's not a solution, but if enough of us spot a problem in the same area, maybe that'll be enough to get things done...

Sorry for the confusion, by 'lag' I mean general communication slowness, not just network latency. I believe (could be wrong) that a lot of the 'lag' we all experience is not due to our respective ISP's or internet access speeds, but to the server/network on the Funcom side. www.antiguardians.com tells me that there are over 39,000 characters on RK-1 alone, over 52,000 characters across all three dimensions. If we assume that everyone has one alternate character (not entirely true, I know, but bear with me), that means that there are roughly 19,500 actual people that have characters on RK-1, though the number is probably more like 15,000 due to many characters per account. At any given time, I think it might be safe to assume that between 5000-8000 people are logged in and playing on RK-1 alone. I'm not a big networking guru, but it seems to me that 5000 connections at once, each sending/requesting information fairly often, could possibly take it's toll on a server, even a big multi-processor unit like I would assume Funcom has for RK-1 (perhaps even each zone?).

My point is that, with that kind of load, there's bound to be some communication difficulties. The server can't reply to every single information request instantly. This is what I define 'lag' as. It is to this lag that I attribute the delayed nano casting (including where you cast a nano, the nano bar reaches full, and the nano doesnt go off for 2 seconds after the nano bar reaches full), the instant nuking, etc. I'm not totally sure about the "I get hit after the mob should be dead", as that fun little bug just appeared a couple patches ago, 13.9 I think. Right about the same time as the instant, unwarned, no-damage-done death. The character just sort of keels over dead for no reason, they don't ever see the damage that kills them. Those bugs might be something weird happening with the latest iteration of the client or the server or both, but again, I'm not sure.

My traceroute spends about 8-10 hops running around the Exodus network before it finally reaches its destination, but I'm not showing any packet loss, just horribly large pings (250-600 on a full 10 Mb DS3 connection). Of course, it's only 9:30 am US Mtn time, so I'm not sure how much traffic the servers are dealing with right now, as I have no way to know how many people play from different parts of the world.

Pretty technical, huh? Disclaimer time: I am NOT a networking expert, just a college student in Computer Science. Everything above is just a theory. I don't want a bunch of flames from big time network admins telling me I'm an idiot. I know I'm probably wrong in everything above, but I'm just advancing my theories for the contemplation of the community at large. If I'm wrong, please correct me . Oh, and if anyone knows how to find out the real statistics on the number of players, number of accounts, number of people logged in, etc, post a link so that everyone can see how crappy my assumed numbers are .
