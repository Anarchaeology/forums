Ghostbusters!
Anyone remember Ghostbusters? Apart from being a cracking good film, it was in my view one of the few comedy roleplaying games that really worked. It worked because, despite the gags and jokes, it could still feature proper stories and proper characters.

I found myself thinking about how the Ghostbusters concept would translate to Rubi-Ka, a world where technology and the supernatural seem to crossover rather a lot, where thousands of people die and come back to life each day and where one's subconscious feelings can be given physical substance. I imagined myself in a team of Ghostbusters off on a mission to capture the mysterious Phantom of Baboons or on the trail of the Omni Forest Sasquatch. It seemed a match made in heaven.

So here is the proposal. I plan to run a mini-Ghosbuster campaign on Anarchy Online. What I am looking for a set of dedicated roleplayers who may be interested in taking part.

The key facts:

* We will be using the old West End Games Ghostbusters rpg for action resolution and character creation. It is a very rule-light system and should work well in the online environment. If you have never played Ghosbusters before, don't worry it is very simple. If you have never done any pen & paper roleplaying then some of the concepts might be a little tricky.

* I aim to run this campaign once a week for as long as the campaign remains fun (7-8 weeks, who can tell?). Ideally I'd like to arrange this for Thursdays at 19:00 GMT but this is negotiable.

* The Ghosbusters will not be organised in a guild. As such characters already in a guild but looking for fresh roleplaying opportunities are welcome.

* There will be no level requirements, though it would be good if the players can at least move around Rubi-Ka without living in fear of the odd rollerrat.

* It will be an Omni based campaign. Characters will need to be Omni or at least Neutral and on Rubi-Ka 1.

I appreciate we often get post on this forum regarding new, mad rolpelaying schemes, many of which fail before even getting off the ground, and maybe this is just another one of those. In all fairness, I have been running events and roleplaying adventures on Anarchy Online and have a half-decent track record for seeing projects I start to their conclusion.

Anyway, if you are interested or have any questions fee free to contact me in game (Savoy), by email at gsoylent@hotmail.com or just reply to this thread.

Well Sambika, sounds like the prefect opportunity to create that OT alt you've been dreaming about :-)

But seriously, this does serve to highlight the question, how do the player characters fit into the Ghostbuster campaign?

Simply put, and in in-character terms, being a Ghostbusters is a job. You do the hours, you get paid at the end of the week. You might have an interest in the supernatural, you might not, at the end of the day its a living.

Ghostbusters Inc, in as far as this campiagn is concerned, runs a chain of franchaises in a number worlds. They have recently opened a branch on Rubi-Ka and are hiring staff on a part-time basis. Those hired are given some basic training ("don't cross the streams, crossing the streams is bad"). issued the Ghosbusters uniform and the all important proton pack.

The "part-time" basis means that being a Ghostbuster is not necessarily your character's only occupation on Rubi-Ka which is handy for players who intend to use an already active character for this campaign.

When using an active, roleplaying character for the Ghostbusters campaign it important to consider if and how this 'job' fts with their background. It's highly unlikely a general of the Mercury Dragons would moonlight as a Ghostbuster. On the other hand, a tailor from Rome who has recently lost his buisness might sign up as a way to make means meet (hint, hint :-) ).

Of course if your character has not really be roleplayed, or is an alt created just for this campaign, this isn't a issue.

Anyway, still looking for players. So any roleplayers out there looking for something a little... okay a lot... different, do get in touch.
