Curiousity: Miir
Excuse both my cynicism and my curiousity, but I'm wondering if any of the female characters I see running around in Miirs are played by female players?

It just doesn't seem to me like something that 99% of the women I've known would want their characters to be seen in outside the bedroom.

I know, we're not supposed to care what gender the player is. Heck, I've got characters of both genders myself. But when I'm playing a character of another gender, I try to stay in-character.

And even in the 293rd century on a frontier world, even with the "women "in question behing heavily armed, the sheer number of women in pigtails, with Barbie's figure, wearing nothing more than straps that would require nanotech to offer any support, that I see running around in public is straining my Willing Suspension of Disbelief muscles.
