Apartment Thieves
Hi Everyone,

This is just an alert.

Apparently there has been a rash of "Apartment Thieves" in Rubi-Ka. It seems that any time you try to leave over 10 items in your apartment these thieves come in and take that 11th item.

It is now recommended that you only place 10 items or less in your apartment in an effort to curb the criminal activity. This includes your decorative items.


Or, outside of RP terms.

Apartments are only able to hold 10 items at the current time. Please do not put more than 10 items in your apartment or they will be lost.

We are currently working on ridding Rubi-Ka of this criminal element. Or, we are working on a fix.
