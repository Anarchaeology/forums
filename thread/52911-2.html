Sol Disaster lamps - This spear like light extends quickly to act as a marker and general area lamp. It appears on all friendly scanners to attract emergency personelle to the person in distress. The nanobots that power this device consume it for power, it only lasts 20 minutes.

Sol Teddy Bears - Designed to protect orphans, these childrens toys have basic scanners, and raise sence and perception by 2. They are designed for children to try and make due until the rescue craft arrive. Enhanced versions have some shielding potential.

Sol Eternal Fuel Cell - A useful source of power for low level survival equipment. Although every precaution has been made against its use in combat situations, some hackers are very tricky. (this is a tradeskill item)

Bundle of cheep wires - Comes with many connectors to all battery types. (this is a tradeskill item)

Sol Energy Amplifier (this combines the Sol eternal Fuel cell and bundle of wires to add +5 damage to energy weapons)

You talk about range. Witness the range extenders for nano programs and the range increasing nanos the NTs got. Well, casting was nerfed to 40m like all other fighting so they are useless! The NTs best tactic used to be range. NTs have dark blue HP and likewise evades. Rooting only works on melee mobs (and can be broken with the smallest general debuff) so what have you got left? Range. Except you don't have that anymore.

I was testing this in a BY just the other day and even when wading in leets that were close together I could only hit max 3 at the time. AE nukes are only useful for when you are getting swarmed by several melee mobs.

Hit for 1400 Crit!
Brawl for 1600
Hit for 300
<mob is STILL not dead>
Hit for xxx
and mob dies.

See? This mob was grey, below level 70 and still had more than 3000HP! And as they get closer to level 100 their HP and AC rise dramatically. This, incidentally, was why MAs used to have such a hard time around level 70. They kept hitting for min dmg due to the high AC of the mobs, and HP on mobs rose sharply too.

Now I'm not saying soldiers don't need some love, far from it. But NTs need it more. They are supposed to be the masters of nano and yet the others beat them hands down in almost every field.
- NP buff? MPs and traders got it, NTs dont.
- Nanoskill buffs? MP got that, NTs got... general buffs.
- Nukes? MPs, crats and traders got that too.
- Nanocost reduction nanos? MPs got it too.
- Calms? Crats do it better (and AE), traders got it and adventurers got some last time.

Just about the only thing the NTs got alone is humidity extractor. Which has huge gaps in the line.

Yeah the damage itself on a nuke isn't bad which is what other people notice usually in a team. What they often don't notice is that NTs usually only get one nuke off before the mob is down. Next time you team with one try to look at how much damage you did put together on a mob and compare it to the one or two nukes the NT managed to get off. The most damaging nukes takes 10-20 seconds to cast and no team want to stand around that long for every kill and THEN try to draw aggro off the NT.

Actually the better the team the worse NT come out damage wise. My MA did a ql 130 team mission when he was about lvl 90 with 3 other MAs and an NT. Often we 4 MAs had the mobs down before the NT had finished casting. He calmed adds very nicely, but after he had to leave we did the rest of the mission with little or no loss in kill speed.

So please, let the NTs get some love.
