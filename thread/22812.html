Why is shopping so hard?
This has come up on a lot of other forums so I figured I would start a thread here on it.

In real life if I need to buy something within an hour I can usually can find what I am looking for and purchase it. Why then in a world set in the future is it nearly impossible for a person to find what they need? It isn't fun spending 6 hours running from store to store looking for guns, armor, clusters and etc. of the quality and specification you need. It's ridiculous that no matter what I need it's like I am trying to purchase a Playstaion 2 on the day of it's release. (Since we are all gamers I figure we can all relate to that analogy.) There has to be a better way to handle stocking stores. The shopping channels help but you need to be lucky to catch what you need when you need it. I am not even talking about high level items or loot only items. Simple things like finding a Mausser in your quality range or a matched set of armor. The only things that are easy to shop for are nanos and implants because the stores stock set qualities and/or have a full range of all of the items you can buy. Please do something to make this easier I easily waste 5-7 hours a week just running around shopping when I could be having fun playing the game.

I would be willing to accept specialized vendors. For example in there are 8 of each type of shop (basic, advanced, superior) in OMNI Trade, if each one of those specialized in one or two types of armor/weapons and was stocked with a great variety of qualities of those weapons. Even that would be easier because then you would know if I am looking for sub machine guns I go to this shop and I have a good chance to get something in the range I need once you get past the initial learning curve of know what shop has what. Another option would be to let you reroll the the shop inventory like spinning missions. This isn't so unrealistic it would be like searching the web or ebay for a purchase. Neither of these are perfect solutions but I think they are better than what we have.
