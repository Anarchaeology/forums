25-30 hours of work for me gets charged at between 10 and 25 dollars an hour, depending on how much/which of my gear gets used. 10 is a base rate for me doing minimal amounts of work, using only my desktop. If I need to do anything location-wise with my laptop, or use any of my other specialized gear (video and audio stuff, mostly), then the rate goes up.

You didn't have a contract, you did the work for a friend of the family, I'd say go with $250-$300 for the final product. If they offer more, great! But don't take less than $10 an hour - it's skilled creative labor, and skilled creative labor should never be worth less than that.

edit: These all assume you live in a midrange area of the US. If you live in NYC, scale up, if you live in a cheap place to live, scale down. My numbers are based on a midrange midwestern town of ~100,000 people.
