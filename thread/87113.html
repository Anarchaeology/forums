Single soldier seeks...
With all these weddings going on recently (congratulations to my very good friend Letah on her forthcoming marriage with Octo btw) it's no wonder that the nights in one's apartment can seem a bit lonely and grey.

Therefor I set out on my quest to fill my days (or at least the part of my days I dont spend blasting clanners) with the warmth and wonderfulness that is a female companion.
Sadly, during my intensive combat tour of duty I have become slightly rusty in the art of charming and have lost track of where to meet new people.

So I thought to myself, why not put a message on the gridfeed. So this is to all of you single women out there, be it solitus or opifex. (sorry, I have a thing against kissing women with circuits sticking out of the skin and eyes that glow in the dark...no offense meant)

Single well trained soldier seeking woman for dating and whatever might follow.

Likes politics, guns, spending time in scenic places away from civilisation, killing clanners, cuddling and good discussions.

If I've caught your interest, do leave a message or send one to my personal communication device and I'll get back to you as fast as duties will permit.

Looking forward to hearing from you

-Gestava Drake
