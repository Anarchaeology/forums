as stated often enough the engy is no pvp profession...

you are completely relianted on your bot and it can be rooted easily. or charmed by crats. or you can be debuffed, so you cant control it anymore.
an engies only chance to stand against any other profession in pvp is to get a decent weapon (or maybe go the martial arts way), so you can do some damage by yourself. but even then you will just be a VERY gimped soldier, with low attack rating, no mirror shield, low evades (not to mention the engy skills you had to abandon just to be able to raise all these dark blue skills).
so with your ac shields and maxed body dev you will MAYBE live long enough to run away. you will NEVER stand a chance in a 1 on 1 fight against equal, or slightly lower lvls of any other profession. of course you could kill greys...if you stay clear of crats, mps, traders and docs .

if you are really interested in pvp, roll another char.
