The engineer blind aura is an interresting idea, but it wont work. Heres why....

The photon disruptor lines break immediately if a mob is hit with anything. Even 1 point of dmg, or as I like to call it; If the mob scratches his nose, it will break.

So having said that, as stated above you would need a large group of ppl to do this. Since you can use the aura on the main boss (since ppl are fighting him) the object is to aura on the smaller bosses. However, since you can bet your life that everyone and their mother at this level uses whatever reflect they can get, especially against higglvl mobs, the aura is useless the second the mob makes a successful hit. All these mobs have high AR and I expect that they will hit more often than the 20 sec refresh on the aura, thus making the aura virtually useless. Lets say the mob still hits every time with just less dmg.... Aura is totally useless in such a scenario.

So, engineers wont make much difference in such a battle, apart from the 62 less dmg they provide with sympathetic AC. If Funcom would come to their senses and make the auras abit more resistant to dmg, it would be a nice tactic.


Ofcourse, if you where really good, and had 20 engineers in perfect sync that refreshed the aura every second it would be another matter entirely. But where on RK would you go to find 20 engineers that have the dark blue skill to actually run the best photon?
