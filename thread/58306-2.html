Well, I formed two characters today, (yesterday?) and here's my report.

I LIKED the playzone based character creation. It was different. I'll admit the new system is probably more intuitive for new players, and if you could navigate it like you could the old one, I'd prefer it.

Paging through the menus to create a character though... I want to walk between the statues and look at them, not click on a tab and be whisked to face the one it goes to. I want to step up to the race/gender displays looking like a bad experiment, (or at least a shaved solitus in a medical gown,) and look them over from all directions.

The recruiting station. Great. Too bad you don't have functional merchants there, but I guess it'd be pointless since no one has any credits at that point.

The question/answer system of the NPC's is nice. No ambiguity about what questions will work and which ones will get a "huh?" response.

I couldn't pick a fight, which is okay, (I'd get creamed, but I wanted to know if I could,) but I did manage to warp through the glass. Nice view.
