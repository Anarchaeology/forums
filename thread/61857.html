Suggested Features and Functionality
Fuctionality :

1.) Sizable Windows
(scrolling each mission description is a pain)


2.) Mission Selection Improvments (away with the randomness)

why not let the player choose the item he/she wants from a list of the available items at the available quality levels, and why not let the player choose from the list of available locations) Ive noticed that there -is- a -set- list of items available at a paticular time/difficulty/player level/ so why not just skip the randomness?

on average, i spend 15+ minutes finding a couple missions that i want. somtimes even more. this is not fun gameplay. and i dont think there are many people who take missions that they dont want.. so this 15+ minutes of mission trolling is currently a nessicary part of gameplay that i think we could do without.


3.) -Real- Bullets. Why do bullets fly thru walls or hills and not impact them instead?. Why does every shot fired hit or miss based only on a characters accuracy/dodge skill?.

Can we make bullets more physical and less -animation-? It'd add a TON to PvP. Let player hide behind buildings or flee from combat?. (afterall, a running target should be more difficult to hit then a stationary one, if bullets fired at the anticapated location of a moving target instead of just -always- hitting) PvP would become alot more interesting.


4.) Implant -Sets-

Give us a device that lets us insert a set of implants into it, and then we would be able to equip/de-equip various sets all at once.

Why not?. its handy to have implants to suit various situations, but who could possibly manage multiple sets of implants?



---- Some Agent related suggestions ----


.) Agents/Stealth and NPC Line-Of-Sight

If a player is sneaking -behind- a monster (ie. if the monsters back is turned), the agent should be -considerably- more difficult to detect compared to if the monster is looking right at the Agent.

This would make sneaking alot more interactive. (not to mention fun/challenging). An Agent, with patience, for example, could potentially sneak a much harder mission, if they timed it right.

Improved damage from a backstab style attack would also be nifty.

4.) Agents and Bows (silent attacking)

If sneaking, an Agent might have a couple free attacks, while using a bow (if they attacked from a relativly obscured location) The Agent might not be zero'd in on until the second or third shot, since bows are supposed to be quite silent.

The Agent could potentially fire an arrow, sneak away, and repeat. (perhaps with an increased difficulty check to the Agents concealment each time) Not sure if this would unbalance anything, but it would be rather James Bond secret agent-ish.
