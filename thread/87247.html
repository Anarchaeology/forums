Let me explain you how the system work.
You get points depending on the damage you did to a player. Points are not shared between teamates, but they are shared with the damage dealer(who could be you teamates). Lets say for example that two Freshmans gank another Fresman who got 1500 HP and kill him. Player1 did 1000 damage and Player2 did 500 damage to the Freshman. Well, Player1 will get 66% of the PvP xp points while Player2 will get 33%. Teaming doesnt matter in this. Now, lets say that a Freshman and an Apprentice kill another Freshman with 1500HP. The frshmn do 1000dmg and the App do 500dmg. The Freshman will gain 66% of the pvp points while the App will get 0%, this mean that the victim will only lose 66% of the points instead of 100% if he didnt get ganked by an App. The rewards are shared between the attackers but some wont get points, kinda like killing a grey mob. When you receive the message 'Target didn't lose enough to grant any reward' or You can loot theses remains' this only mean that you did the most damage to the victim and you have the right to loot, it doesnt mean that you didnt receive any points like alot of people may think. You alway get points if you do damage to someone in your PvP title range. Even if the damage is low, you will get a few points and the target will only lose a few points from you. I hope this cleared some preoblems. The only reason to not team with people is to benefit from the grace period. If a teamate is fighting and he's in the same area as you. People will be able to attack you during grace period, even if you see the countdown. Sometime you also dont get the 'loot right' when your teamed but I think its a bug.
