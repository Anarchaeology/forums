Attack Problem
Since they took out what was called "Q" tapping they have made it hard for ppl who weild slow attacking weapons.

If you are fighting mutiple enemies and want to switch a target and tap "Q" to stop attacking you cannot tap "Q" again for sometime because you get a message "Waiting for previous attack to finish" or something along that line. In that time who ever attacks you, you will auto attack them. Making it almost impossible to switch targets with ease.

They need to allow you to engage a new target even if your weapon has not cycled or allow a way to turn OFF Auto Attack because not everytime someone attacks me do I want to attack them back. I may be trying to target a new enemy while another enemy is attacking me making me continue to attack the enemy i DO NOT want to attack.

Hopefully they will lookinto this.
