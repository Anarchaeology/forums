The eternal question of "what is best"

I'll go with the standard answer... there is no best.

All classes have strengths and weaknesses.

I have played most classes, but most of them not very far.

My main character is a NT. NT's are good for teaming but not the best choice for solo hunting.

My personal favorite so far for solo hunting is MP.
However, pet classes have difficulties with pet pathing indoors, so there's a tradeoff there too.

My best advice is to try them all out.
