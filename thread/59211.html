Bring back the 25% and 0% zones...
I know this will not happen since the gaslevels were changed in preparation for the stupid booster pack and I suppose for the dyna camps as well but I would still like to see the return of all the previous 25% and 0% gas zones.

There was always this little tingle when you passed through a pvp zone, as if you were never quite sure if you would make it or not. Now it's just boring flying through Mort and all the other zones that used to be 25%.

Mayhem zones are the only places where we as a faction can practice and develop our team pvp tactics, have wargames and other possibilities for more RP-like activities.

FC is seems to be gearing the main game to accomodate the booster, which is not good news for people who do not plan on buying it or are simply not interested in fighting over the towers.
There are no longer hardly any places for two factions on opposing sides to have a faction war and absolutely no place where they have a reasonable chance of being able to fight without being jumped by others.

So, please prove me wrong FC and let us roam through 25% and 0% gas areas and zones once again.

Disclaimer: I have not had the time to check out the entire map but to my knowledge the only areas still 25% are 2ho, Meetmedere and possibly 4Holes? I know there are still 0% gas rooms and bars dotted around but they are not really useful anyway. If anyone knows any more areas than the ones I listed fell free to add them here, that would be much appreciated.

I see things both ways on this. The main problem I have with wide areas for PvP is that the current PvP level bracketing is very unfair.

PvP can be fun for the loser in a more or less 'even fight' as well as the winner. But PvP is not fun for the loser if someone twice their level or more kills them in three shots. We tend to lose sight of this as we increase in level, since as it stands, no one is that much higher and no one is capable of killing us in just three shots.

So, rebracket the levels in which PvP fighting is allowed past level 75 and I would support a few of the old mayhem zones coming back.
