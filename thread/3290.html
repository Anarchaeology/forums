By now, I'm sure you have heard of the arrest of Portman by two Omni-Tek organizations.

Who do you think led protests and battled against Portman? Not these two groups that will be getting credit.

Omni-Tek Assault Corps did. On multiple occasions. Two protests in Tir as well as a direct assault on Portman, which resulted with the loss of Red Freedom. Despite full-frontal attacks and diplomacy, nothing occured.

In fact, these two groups in question have never taken action against Portman in the past, and they have both shown to be traitors to Omni-Tek.

How you ask?

The Black Alliance
Threatened all Omni-Tek employees with death if they interfered in their eradication of clans from the planet. Ah, a death threat you your "comrades" that goes against Ross' orders!

Dark Carnival
Arrested and found guilty of killing an ally of Phillip Ross, harassing other Omni-Tek employees, mocking the judicial system of Omni-Tek and the punishment, and threatening to kill Radiman again (I have images following the trial of their leader saying: "I am planning to kill Radiman again") as well as horrendous amounts of threats aimed at fellow Omni-Tek employees. The most recent atrocity was asissting exiled Omni-Tek employee Aberic in breaking a court order and converting back to Omni.

Why would two groups that have refused to lift a finger except to threaten to kill other Omni-Tek employees or disobey orders end up capturing Portman?

Here is the simple explanation:

The Black Alliance guilds and dark carnival are terrorists who have been working alongside Portman.


Do not be shocked, just look at the facts.

Both groups have:
1. Threatened to kill Omni-Tek employees.
2. Threatened to kill all Clans.
3. Willfully disobeyed direct orders from Ross.
4. Have insulted and threatened Ross.
5. Have killed Radiman and promise to do again (even after verdict)
6. Have done nothing to deal with the Portman threat.
7. Have done nothing to follow Omni-Tek orders of promoting amnesty.

Do those seven points describe organizations that work for the better good of the company that they are employed by?

I think not.


When this idea was first brought to my attention I almost dismissed it outright, but after investigating the evidence I felt sick to my stomach. It is true

1. Omni-Tek has promised to be vigilant when dealing with "rogue" Omni organizations after the guilty ruling of traitors truwind and Aberic.

2. Peace has been desired by the majority of Omni-Tek organizations and many clan organizations.

3. Those that ignore the amnesty (on both sides) have begun to be known as "terrorists" and lost any respect they might have had outside of their own dwindling ranks.

4. Without the image of being a hero, these organizations were nothing more than violent criminals lacking the proper medication to co-exist in a civilized society.


What position is it harder to get? Power among disorganized rebels, or power among a massive company such as Omni-Tek? No doubt, power among the hypercorporation known as Omni-Tek!

This was why Portman sacrificed herself; to protect the cover of her allies within Omni-Tek. To protect the cover of TBA members and dark Carnival!

During the trial for the now convicted criminals mentioned above, the name "Unicorn Company" was mentioned. First time to be heard by the majority in attendance.

Truwind claimed both she, aberic and her guild are employed by Unicorn Company, and that they were who ordered the death of Radiman.

Why would this other company do this, you ask?

It's because Unicorn company is a competitor of Omni-Tek. Has been for centuries. Omni-Tek's creation of nanotechnologies has hindered the sales performance of their product. Weapons.

How else do you bolster profits quickly, but by making them a necessary tool for existence!

With peace, Unicorn Company loses even more of the market share and eventual backruptcy.

With war, Unicorn Company can gain ground on Omni-Tek. With Omni-Tek preoccupied with the war, Unicorn Company can smuggle in goods to supply both sides of the war!

With Radiman dead (no doubt, Unicorn company with it's insider connections with the Black Alliance and dark carnival had corrupted Radimans stored genetic configurations) war would be inevitable.


Now is the time for Omni-Tek to act. The traitors are here, waiting for praise but must be met with the fist of God. Strike down on those that are bringing violence and turmoil to our planet and to our company.


Bring peace to Omni-Tek.
Bring peace to Rubi-Ka.

Bring justice to these traitors.
