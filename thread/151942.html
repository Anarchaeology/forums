WAY more than that
Man I'm glad you started this thread, this will give me a small opportunity to rant a little...

After all is said and done, and all the complaining that goes on about AO - I have to say this has been by far my favorite MMORPG. I've quit a few times to try other games, but always come back.

I have to say though, the one thing I fear may damage my interest in AO are the relentless nights of searching for a group. Thats it.

Generally, 50% of any given block of play time I've had is spent just <i>looking</i> for groups. The other evening I spent 4 hours at Omni Ent BB searching for a group. It was absolutely horrible. Ok one might say "well why didn't you just go solo or farm tokens or something". I suppose that is a valid argument, but still....

I think what we desperate LFGers need is an advanced LFG system. not simply serverwide LFG channels, but an interface where you can tap in and automatically feed in what lvl you are, race, class, what you are interested in doing etc. Even a section for your team to specify what lvl range and what classes you are seeking to fill your group. Something that would look like this, yet more glorified:

Glumy | Omni | Trader | 108 | Team Missions
Yoda | Omni | Doctor | 132 | Team Missions
TheDude | Clan | Fixer | 115 | Dyna Camps or Team Missions
MrHoofaa | Clan | Soldier | 32 | Team Missions

And you could set filters, etc. even block unfriendly names to this list. Teams would have a similar interface that would show them people LFG that match their criteria for their team.

Now THIS would make life AWESOME for us drooling LFGers....
