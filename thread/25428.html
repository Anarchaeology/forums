Tokens of Gratitudes - improving social interactions
Well, we can get tokens for missons, so why not introduce player - revarded tokens ? For example your are getting token for each 2 hours of playing time, or per level. You can't use it yourself, but can give it to someone, for help in mission, buffing, guiding etc., and he could put it into his "social" board. To evoid exploiting this token wouldn't impove combat stat, but only social standing - title floating around head, availability of special missions, guild status, part in storyline (heh, if there will be any), special quests etc. Such a token also would be a great reward for player-made mission.
Also that way high-level players will have more reasons to help newbie...

1. Because all big guilds would choose 1 member to give the tokens to every day so everyone would get tons of them

Each member have 1 token per 1-2 RL days. That way in a big guild he would wait a year to get his 300 tokens. No one would agree on such a setup. From the other hand if they all agree to give their token to the leader he defenitly deserve them. That is the hole point of the tokens.

2. people would trade tokens

If someone want buy token - so what ? He is helping to newbie by his money. That is like a charity dinner or a fund raising for newbies. To make it more interesting token could only be given - not traded. That way if your are not trusting person and he is not trusting you you will not trade. If your are trusting each other - that mean he deserve token
