I work in NY Harbor, I drive a workboat that cleans up oil spills. On Sept 11, along with most of the other tugs and workboats in the Harbor, we were sent to Manhattan to help evacuate the city and bring in supplies and rescue workers. We were there for 4 days.

It was the most horrifying thing I have ever experienced. Myself and my crew were never the same afterwards. I will never forget the look in the eyes of the rescue workers working at the site......utter despair. And the smell, I will never forget the smell.

To those who were there, or at the Pentagon, or lost loved ones in the attack, my heart goes out to you.

To those people in this community that I have met, I wish to thank you. The members of AO that I have met have, for the most part, been great. You really have made the past year that much better.

God Bless
