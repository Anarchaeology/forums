-Make dual skill weapons give more than 100% bonus from skills.

-Heavy weapons buff

-Fix dual weild. Everyone wins to some degree. 2 guns and you meet the dual reqs should be twice as fast, period.

-Better burst. Popular desire I can see.

-More minimum damage. A ql200 BFG shouldn't have a single digit minimum. Hell, a ql200 anything shouldn't have a single digit minimum. At least 100-200 minimum base damage for the high end BFGs.
