Breaking root on yourself...
This is beginning to become VERY annoying.

I am doing a large mission. I charge into a room and see Agent/Crat/Fixer - or some other class with a rooting nano. Ok, the poor sap roots me once or twice during a battle, but roots usually breaks when I take damage. However, these classes seem to make a point of slapping a root on me the last second before keeling over. I try to thwart it as much as possible, usually not using Brawl near end of fight, and mashing it as soon as the root is broken, hoping to finish them off before they cast again. (I am MA, lvl 90 right now)

But very very often - almost 70 % of the time - I end up with a fresh root on me. Well, at lower levels, when roots lasted 20-30 seconds, it was OK. But twiddling thumbs for 2 whole minutes, waiting for Greater Hold Victim to wear off is a tad too much. I once timed a mission with stopwatch. Total time to do mission: 1h,40m. Total time spent waiting for roots to expire: 1hour. Yes, more than 50% mission time spent just standing in one place. With grid, wompas, tratment kits, nano rechargers, reclaim terminal system etc, Funcom did a fine job of reducing the boring downtime. But they sure flopped this one.

Does anyone know if there is any way to cancel a root on yourself? Provided you are not in combat? If no, how about implementing something?
