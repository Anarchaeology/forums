I suggest adding some new mission types
Right now, there's the "Find Item", "Find and Return Item", "Find Person", "Find Person and Assassinate", and "Repair". I want to suggest 2 more kinds of missions (yeah, I thought of this while bored at work)...

1) Escort Mission. What you do is escort an NPC to some sophisticated equipment as he's the only guy who can fix it. He'll meet you at the first room of the mission, and become your "pet". He won't follow any commands, but will defend himself if attacked. Once you find the room he's supposed to go to and he enters it, the mission is a success. However, the mission is failed if 1) the time runs out and 2) if he dies along the way.

2) Rescue Mission. You must find the room where the NPC is being held hostage. If you enter the room where the NPC is at or if you attack any of the mobs in that room the NPC will become your "pet", and the mobs will subsequently attack him. (if you attack the NPC while outside the room where he's at the mission will be a failure). The mission will be complete when that NPC enters the the door you entered the mission area from.
