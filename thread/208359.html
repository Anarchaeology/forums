They are letting me talk, BOGLWE
It has been 6 months and 7 days since I, Boglwe, has seen the face of a fellow clan member or a neut friend! There are several people involved with my daily activities here in what I have come to believe is some building inside ROME. There are only two rooms and one hallway I have seen since my arrival here 6 months ago blind-folded. I can hear street movement if things are very quite and there is a lot of activity outside. I am assuming all this, I have no windows!!! Daily activities include what Omni officials and the 4 guards (2 for each shift, EGADS they work them hard!!!) call the lunchion and thats about it! the rest of my time is by myself in what look likes half of an ordinary apartment with no emenities. THe lunchion consists of food and questions. The food has only been good twice and I think those were gifts of an OMNI friend. The rest of the time the food is just enough to keep me alive but dizzy and malnourished and skinnier then ever. After the meal are usually the questions. They have asked me about everything from Clan weaponry to Clan Guild leaders. The leaders they ask me about are Vixentrox, Wolfe, and Szentasha. I allways keep my mouth shut and this usually angers them but not much anymore.

In the begining when I would give no information it was a lot worse. I was tortured. One of the things they used to do to me was hold me down with my back to the ground and then the two guards would hold my arms and my feet square to the ground while the officer open my mouth with the butt or the barrel of his rifle. They would then pour water onto my face and let it drip into my mouth until i was almost dead from drowning. They would then turn me over and let me drain and then repeat the process over and over again, Sometimes they would not even ask me questions. They would also do things to me I cannot mention here.

This transmission was released VIA an OMNI friend. She told me she would let me on a comp unit for a few minutes. She seems to have a heart. I remember this women from way back when I was just a pup and new nothing about anything except dancing at reets and baboons and was more or less a sex addict. My rise to leadership of two seperate guilds (Angels of Light & Angels of Harmony) within Clan and my relations and love for the the Neuts seem to catch the attention of OMNI officials. My old husband Rueter1215 is no where to be found I guess. Lost contact with him many months ago, Although he did help support those two guilds via large payments to me wich I in turn put them in the guild bank. My dear friends Dooley and Katpaws have made contact with me twice in the last 6 months via my OMNI friend here on the inside.

I am asking for help!! I know some names of people whoi might be able to facilitate and attack to get me out. TheDeacon (The scoudrel that he is) is one such fellow. And maybe even Metalynx or Doompie. Jynne or Yoshima. Vixentrox. Quanten is another. DrTiny is one more who may help. I dont know the first thing about any of this or where I am. I do have an Onmi friend, as mentioned before, helping with these things. I believe her to be a spy. But she tells me nothing and has never even spoken to me, just delivered messages or Items like this here comp unit.

I am feeling much older now, and I am wearing out. I dont think I have much time left. I have held up for this long but. I am tired. Dont worry about me talking to them though, It won't happen. I believe if I talk it would not make a difference on my fate anyways. The only hope I have for my life (which is less important to me then Clan-Neut relations and the happiness of my fellow clanners) is that about 5 months ago an OMNI officer shouted at one of the guards (Kogan, i think his name is) to not finish me off, as in beat me to death, because, he said "If you kill her there will be HELL to pay from the OMNI peace keepers" (whoever they are). SO there is hope. I will give my life if I have to to salvage relations. But if MY rescue cuases the DEATH of one Clan, NEUT or OMNI soldier or civilian. THen please dont do it. MY LIFE FOR PEACE!!! I love you all.


~~~~~~END TRANSMISSION~~~~~~~
