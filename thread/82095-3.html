Witness Account of Demoneyes' Activity
December 18, 29476

First-hand witness account of an encounter with Demoneyes
Racheal "Blackpetal" Schuemann

Last night, while having a quiet evening at the Rompa Bar, Don and I noticed an odd fellow at another table. This "man" (there may be a more apt word, but I'm not sure what it would be), who goes by the handle "Demoneyes", seemed to be predicting doom for Omni-Tech, particularly the city of Omni-1, and have access to some kind of visions of the future.

When Don confronted him, tempers quickly flared, and I started to leave. Demoneyes followed me, however. I don't really remember too much of what happened after that... One second I was looking at him, the next, he had his teeth clamped down on my neck! I don't know why he was able to do this with the suppression gas in Rompas...

Needless to say, most of what I remember after that involves a lot of blood, and Don dragging me to a safehouse while he tried to figure out what had happened.

All I can say is, keep an eye on this one... I don't know what to make of his warnings, but his culinary sensibilities definitely leave something to be desired...

The strangest part is that the wound is healed now... even with Don's first aid and some nanoprograms, I was expecting an ugly scar, but when I took the bandages off this morning to look, there's no sign that I was ever bitten... Whatever nanotechnology he has in his saliva is very peculiar...

Still, I have to say, there's more going on with this guy than meets the eye...

Sssss...the hissing...the light..., the prophecy that had nagged at his mind since the sun finally rose during his quiet realizations on the wounded transport. "...your ends drawss closser to your doors..." It was the only one of Mr. Clyburn's warnings that had yet to reveal itself.

The procession from the central spaceport of Omni-Prime to the Admin district's Omni-Pol station had been slow, solemn. He was partly numb, partly lost, and mostly in mourning.

"Malcom..." Her voice. Always melodic, yet always so mischievous. He couldn't even count the number of nights that voice - that melody - had calmed him in moments of severe frustration.

"...I was thinking..." She was always thinking; embued with a sharp mind that he had always admired and respected. A mind that could see his every weakness and drive his every strength.

"And you're certain of this, Citizen?" The officer asked, rousing him from his thoughts. Alamexis simply nodded, unblinking, and followed the officer outside.

As they continued walking, much slower than the officer would have liked, memories flashed continuously across his mind's eye. The night they had first met, when she had saved his life by pulling him away from a trapped door that he mistakenly thought he had disarmed; the many nights they had engaged in epic battles of chess, at which their relative record was tied, a stalemate that would never be broken; and the night of their graduation, the first and only night they had...

The Omni-Pol officer gasped, and immediately radioed for backup. The Admin district was rife with murders, and the officer was reasonably experienced enough to recognize blood when he saw it. The two of them stood in silence, in the Great Hall of the Omni-Admin central library, and, written on the floor in blood - in her blood - were three latin words:

"quid pro quo". Something for something.

Alamexis remained frozen in place, still trapped within his mind. I brought her into this... He had repeated this torturously over and over again. He had avoided telling her about Raquel, even when Raquel stood in front of him; knowing she could hear the anguish in his voice, and despite constant questioning by her, be resolved himself to keep her as far removed from the shadows as possible; and, in a fleeting moment of weakness, when the door to his mind seemed closed, he had reached out for her, and this resolve had capitulated.

As the large oak doors were slowly opened by the contingent of Omni-Pol officers, Alamexis struggled to withhold his tears. Slumped at a desk in the library - his desk - was the "ends" to his capitulated resolve.

As Alamexis turned away from the body - Ashley's body - he silently prayed that her soul still lingered about, and whispered, ever so softly, ad infinitum, your death will never be in vain...

The city was burning. Oily black tendrils of smoke, and thick, ugly licks of fire dotted the landscape for as far as the eye could see. Omni-1. Home to millions, the pride of the Omnitech Corporation, and the seat of power for Omni's holdings on Rubi-Ka was burning. A sound that chilled the heart and soul of any human being who heard it. Omni-1 was dying, engulfed in a hellfire borne of an unholy agent too horrifying to consider.

Blackpetal awoke, drenched in sweat, with a scream still trying to wrench itself free of her throat. For a moment, she scarcely dared breathe; her eyes searched around, trying to place her surroundings. Her whole body shook with fear and adrenaline. Home. Her apartment in Rome. Drawing a sheet around herself, she went to the window. Outside, the beautiful night sky hovered, suspended over the elegant neo gothic towers of the city she loved so much. There was no fire. Blackpetal sighed. She let her focus shift to her own reflection in the window. For a moment, she thought the saw a red glow reflect in her eyes, then it was gone. She blinked and rubbed her eyes. As the sheet slid off her bare shoulder, she stared in the reflection at the spot where she was bitten a few nights earlier. The wound was gone; no trace of it remained, and yet, its presence lingered. Blackpetal stared at her shoulder, still glistening with sweat. She felt as if something alive was crawling just under her skin. What was it? Post-hypnotic suggestion? Some nano-virus? Or maybe Im just losing my mind, she finally said. She tried to force a laugh, but all the came out was a dry cough.

Blackpetal felt her legs go weak under her and she collapsed, sprawling onto the floor.
