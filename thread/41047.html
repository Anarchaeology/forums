If she was the leader of the group when formed and I chose the mission, who would have to click the mission mob to complete it, and what would happen if the groups split, like she left our group and yet we never got it completed, could she complete it even though I got the mission and she or her boyfriend killed the mob?
Who the team leader is, doesn't matter when it comes time to complete a mission. Whoever chose the mission is the missionholder and the only person that can complete the mission. If you were the missionholder, and the team split up before the mission was complete, YOU are the only one that has the ability to finish the mission for the token. You need to be in a team to get the mission assignment, but you can be solo to complete it.

Hope that clears things up a bit for you.
