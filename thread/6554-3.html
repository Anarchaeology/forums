Pet pathing can be fixed... I don't know how much drastic of a change it will be in scripting, but it's not impossible... so why create a /pet summon command, that could be potentially exploited, when they could do this?:

I've noticed, as some others on the AO boards, that a pet, as well as npc's, end up stuck, because of what I believe to be impassable areas. Of course they aren't impassable, whenever an NPC or pet attacks for some reason everything seems to be fine, and if you yourself come accross these areas, you can just jump over them or think intelligent enough to weave through them. These objects also affect nano and attack range, as i'm sure most have seen when trying to shoot a MOB 3 feet in front of you from the other side of a table, it tells you you're out of range. What this appears to me is that the game interprets them as walls, so if you're behind one, then you can't do anything, unless you can jump over it. Why can't they just make these "walls" appear as ground terrain like a hill? Where a character can freely automatically hop over, when in the game mechanics the character is just going over elevated terrain. Or, they can adjust it to whatever happens when a MOB attacks (ever notice how a MOB attacking you in the water room with the two ledges just seems to hover right to where you are?). That and better pathing (remember someone talking about track routes) will solve it I beleive.
