Heya


I think new players are going to be disadvanted more after 14.2 then they are currently. AO's learning curve is a little on the steep side at first. So natrually an new player will not know all the details of the game in planning his/her char. After 14.2 in order to compete with the long time players, a new player will need to learn how to design implant sets, what buffs she/he needs to get them in, find someone to give them the buffs, find someone to build them the implants,Figure out what buffs they can then self cast and buy them, then maximize the weapons choice based on the lame 20% rule. I for one think that many high level players represent a pretty positive contribution to newbies that in no way not interferes with their learning curve. I benefited from a high level player converting and healing when I started and I for one am glad there are players to do that.

As for recruiting, I personally think a newbie should avoid joining an organization before learning enough about the game to decide what that newbie wants from a group. That being said, I think a newbie is better off being recruited by a high level player who can offer expertise and help than being recruited by another newbie who may only know enough to be dangerous.

quote:
The over equipping patch is one step in the right direction for Funcom. Too bad most of the other steps seem to be backwards
The overequipping patch is in my opinion just wrong-headed. I am afraid that the patch will actually reward twinking more, not less. The lower your level, the greater the impact the OE limitation imposes. So what will allow a newbie to over equip beyond 20%? Simple answer: use implants. Someone wanting to twink a new character still can use implants in combination with ability buffs to insert implants that are NOT subject to the 20% overequip limitation. The result will be that a massive gap will continue to exist between genuine newbies and new characters twinked by a higher level players. Moreover, a massive gap will still exist between twinked and untwinked characters at any given level at least until you hit the level where you can no longer over equip anything higher than 20%. Even then, there are going to be players with legacy items like stepped up implants or low light targeting scopes who will always have an advantage over players who did not have them before FC nerfed them. All that will have happened is to mess up play balance that is already less than ideal. What is bottom line? The players who clamored for OE limitations will still find the situation basically unchanged.

Personally I do not see why anyone would care so much about the OE limitation. I think it just comes from envy of players who are more uber because they make the most aggressive use of the game system. Every player has to decide what he or she wants from the game. A massively overequipped player is just going to get bored if that player cannot find challenging stuff to do (unless they are one of those griefers or player killers whom, I have to confess, I just do not understand). Anyone who massively overequips really is just creating a risk that they will interfere with their own enjoyment of the game. I think that should be their decision.

I happen to like twinked players. I like to group with them. It just means being able to do higher ql missions. They can give you good suggestions. They usually have lots of money and items so that they usually are not loot pigs. I love to group with them. I wish I could group with more of them.
