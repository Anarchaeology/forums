Lopwrath - The Awakening (Long Story)
<<EPISODE 1>>
(Please do not carry this storyline on, but please DO post your comments)


November 02 - 29129 (A long time ago)
Notum Mine near Omni-2 (Rome)

"Mining. Bah... it just gets so boring. We mine all the time. I'm so tired. And hungry...". Jergot looks up from his workdesk, situated near the muttering miner.

"Give it a rest Iotrop. I'm sick of your constant moaning. You're here to work now get on with it!" Jergot shouts, only just managing to keep writing at the same time.

"Sorry Sir." Iotrop keeps quiet and gets back to his mining. "I just am really hungry. I could murder a roasted Reet right now."

"Bloody atroxes. Give it a rest already, 2 hours and you can have your break. I have a ton of paperwork to get through - stop interupting me." Jergot scratches his head, trying to finish calculating the advanced problems on his paper.

"Sir... I have been thinking. Now, I know that I ain't the sharpest tool in the shed, but my mining spot has some strange things to it. I been noticing through the last few days that when I hit the rock it isnt making the same sound as it used to." Iotrop does his best to sound intelligent, but cant help itching his behind whilst talking. Jergot stands up, his face blood red.

"Iotrop, I have just about had it with your messing around. You want to see how to mine? This is how you mine!"
Jergot, the somewhat smaller nano-technician, picks up the atroxes pickaxe and starts hitting the rock.
"You see? Its not hard! Stupid atrox!" Jergot pants, but carries on hitting. "Look! Its easy!" As Jergot feels he has proven his point, he realises he has reached a layer of rock. Very dense rock.
"Thats not normal rocks there Sir." Iotrop says, whilst still scratching
"Nonsense. Its just a little denser." Jergot swings with all his might. Nothing happens. "Hmm. Very strange. Hang on, I can do this." Jergot swings again. Nothing. "Grr! I don't have time for this! I have important paperwork to do!" With all his rage, Jergot swings mightily at the rock. And smashes through. Yes, thats right, smashes through. As behind this dense layer of rock, there was a small open area. A strange little cave in the mountain. It beared no resemblance to the rest of Rubi-Ka, and contained purple fungi like things, growing everywhere.

"What on Earth..." Jergot dropped the pickaxe in shock. "Iotrop, get in there now, see if there are any other miners in there. Perhaps this is one of our tunnels."

"But I thought we were in the deepesdt tunnel?" Iotrop mumbled, starting to sound a little scared.

"Just... Get in there now! Don't argue with me or I will reduce your rations for two weeks!" Jergot shouted. Iotrop noticed a faint shake in Jergots voice as he shouted.

"Yes Sir."

Iotrop slowly made his way through the small hole in the wall that Jergot made into the cave beyond.
Jergot waited.
And waited.
And waited.

After 2 hours, Jergot was getting worried. It wasnt like Iotrop to miss his lunch break. And besides, even though he never showed it, Jergot acctaully liked that big lovable oaf a little.
Jergot had to go in. That atrox was carrying some expensive equipment which couldn't be lost. So he made his way into the small cave.
Purple moss was everywhere. The cave smelt musty, Jergot could hardly breathe. Everywhere he stepped the moss seemed to squeal in pain as he stepped on it. As he moved further away from the miners tunnel it started to get dark, so he equipped his light. And made his way deeper into the cave.

It was a long walk, and Jergot was starting to think about going back. But he had to go on... Iotrop was a friend, even if he never showed it.

It was getting scarier. Deep roars echoed throughout the cave, too deep to be the sounds of explosions of the other miners. Jergot knew he shouldn't have come. He started to turn around to go back, when he heard a moan. A very slight, gentle moan. Iotrops moan.

"Iotrop! Where are you?"

Jergots torch scanned the area. He started to panic. Iotrop was hurt. He had to find him!

"Uhhh....."

Iotrop moaned again. Jergot found him, lying face down in the corner of a small room type area in the cave. He couldn't have been lying there long, yet the purple moss was already starting to spread onto him.

Jergot sprinted over to Iotrop.

"Iotrop! Are you ok? I have been worried sick!" Jergot started trying to roll Iotrop face up, which was no easy task.
"Iotrop! Answer me! Come on we have to-"
As Jergot finally managed to roll the massive atrox onto his back he screamed.
Liquid Notum was pouring through Iotrops mouth, eyes and nose. His eyes and tounge were lying next to him, ripped out. Jergot took off his glove and desperatly tried to wipe some of the incredibly hot liquid notum off of Iotrops face, revealing a horribly disfigured head. The notum had mutated and burnt his face. He hardly looked like an atrox at all.

"Oh my god. Iotrop! What happened? Whats going on? Can you hear me?" Jergot desperatly searched the atrox for a pulse. It was no good. Iotrop was dead.

"No..." Jergot could only whisper. He had a lump in his throat, he felt as if he was choking. A tear slowly rolled down his cheek, falling into the liquid notum, immediatly evaporating. You weren't allowed to get emotionally attached to each other in this line of work. But Iotrop was a friend.

The roar came again. Very loud this time. Jergot couldn't handle it. He had to get out. He stood up, getting ready to make a run out of the cave, when something grabbed him from behind. Jergot desperatly tried to shake his way out of his enemies grip, but it was no use. He blacked out.

"Uhhh...." Jergot slowly opened his eyes. He was hoping to be welcomed by a warm hospital bed, with Omni employees attending to his every need. But no, he was still in that cave. He couldn't move. He could hardly see, and his vision was worsening. In the distance, deeper in the cave, he saw a figure. No figure he had ever seen before. The figure walked straight past Jergot heading towards the mining tunnel. As the figure walked past, it muttered one thing.

"Lopwrath..."
