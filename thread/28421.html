OT Roleplaying MA only org. needs...
...some founding members.

The org is called Theochiu Triads. Loosely based on the northern chinese triads. The Theochiu Triads started as a domestic organization between 4 villages: Mei Hsien, Chin Hei, Yao Ping, and Chao Yang. Present day, the Theochiu Triads are considered the wealthiest and most powerfull chinese gang, with their largest numbers in Hong Kong and Bangkok, Thailand.

What I hope to create with this Org. is a sense of a mofia style family with eastern customs and traditions. The goal is not to go out and pvp as much as possible or level until we drop. While leveling is important always, I feel significance should be placed on social events and gatherings as well. I am hoping to find other creative and interested people to help me mold this idea and org. into more.

Only MA's will be accepted and roleplaying is STRONGLY encouraged.

Feel free to contact Yonoko in game.
