Token And Award Board Faq
Since there wasn't one out there, I thought I'd start one and people could punt it to the top.

When you start a new character one of your starting items will be an award board. This board tracks your progress through the promotional structure of your faction. When you are a new player, do NOT right-click on this board. If you do it will disappear. You can shift-left click on it, though, to see its description. Also, if you are tired of it taking up room in your inventory, you can wear it around your neck.

To advance in your organization you need to collect tokens. Tokens are little round circles that are part of item rewards in missions. Some missions have them and some do not, so when selecting missions look for the ones that offer tokens as well. I've seen all kinds of missions offer tokens. Remember that generating new missions is relatively cheap, so if you want a token mission and don't see one, just change a slider and generate a new set of missions.

When you get a token, right-click on the token. The token will disappear and you will get a message telling you how many tokens you have collected. Note that this message may be off by one; I've seen it report 0 on the first token collected, but rest assured your token is still counted and the actual total is 1. There's no need to save up tokens; go ahead and right-click on them when you get them.

Your first reward comes when you've collected 10 tokens. After you've collected 10 tokens, only THEN right click on your award board. The award board will be "upgraded" to your first faction reward. This new board, when worn around the neck, will give you bonus hit points. The next award comes at 30 tokens total (20 more after your first reward). You may now safely right-click on this new board and it won't disappear; instead it will tell you how many tokens you've collected and will also put a cool little visual display around your character's head of their current status.

IF you right-click on your board before you get 10 tokens and it disappears, you need to get a new board. To do this you need to find your organization vendor; the one for Omni-Tek is in OmniTrade. You can go there and buy a low-level OT Application. Right-click on it and it will turn into a new award board for you. Clans and Neutrals should have something similar.

PS - The newbie right-click bug is supposed to be fixed in a patch Real Soon Now. Remember after you collect your first 10 tokens it's safe to right-click on it.
