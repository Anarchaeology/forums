Nice idea. You could have hidden valleys, small towns, lost temples etc. As long as you have to zone to get in, the area can be generated afresh for each mission key holder, as with underground missions.

A nice little addition would be to make a public version of the zone that could be accessed without a key. Wild, outdoor places could be hunting areas or even just beauty spots. Town environments could have save terminals, a few shops etc. Drop in a few interesting NPC's - they don't have to do anything much in terms of loot, quests etc, just have a few anecdotes or snippets of info when you open the chat interface.

Define these "outdoor" areas as indoor on the server to prevent people using Yalms etc. and I think there's no functional difference between this and the missions we already have.
