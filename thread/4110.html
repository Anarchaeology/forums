IP - reallocation is coming !
Patch note:

Dear customers. The next patch will be addressing one of the most debated issues in AO, namely IP reallocation. Based on the feedback we have recieved from you, our dear and respected players, we have come up with the following change to IP investment:

When levelling you will be allowed to reallocate the same amount of IP you get when you level. I.e.:
lvl 2 - lvl 14: You will gain 4 000 IP and you can reallocate 4 000 IP
lvl 15 - lvl 49: you gain 10 k IP and you can reallocate 10 k IP
etc
etc

Now, for the high end players (thx for still being with us) we have created a rare, special item, that will allow you to reallocate a limited amount of IP when rightclicking on that item. There are a few restrictions:

Level req' : you must be lvl190+
Skill req' : none, obviously
This item will disappear after use, it is a NODROP (non-tradeable), and it will be ql200 only, i.e. not found in any shops. There will also be a time cap on this feature so that even if you do have two of these items you can only use it once every 48hrs playtime.
By using the command /played you will also be notified the time left for when you can use the item next time.

We hope this new feature will mean that we can make new fun and exiting items in the future (or change existing items if found necessary) without you feeling you have spent all your presious RL time in AO on a character no longer worth playing.

Have a very merry x-mas and happy hunting.


Now that would have been a merry x-mas eh ?
