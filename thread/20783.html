whatever happened to outdoor hunting?
i was reading a similar thread and it got me to thinking..

I've played since beta, and my first char, a NT (Binaural) started out of course, hunting. I killed minibulls, hammer breaders, the cage, manteze, and stuff in galway, then i went to 2h0 to kill mutants for a long time. I also went to 4holes to hunt, and various other locations around the world exploring. this was genuinly the funnest time in AO, getting xp outdoors in teams and exploring and killing new monsters all the time.

After i stopped playing my NT, i made my soldier (nothinman) he was a pvp twink and i never levled him very much, just got him to apprentice then retired him after pvp patch (come back to this char later)

then i made my meta (Giventofly) I hunted with this char, also in galway, minibulls etc. then moved onto lush as galway became more deserted, after lush i did 20k. the usual, eye camp, Vb's, shades, bothackers etc.. at this point came the famous medusa patch. I was about lvl 100 when the medusas started to be hunted alot. This was REAL fun, i could head out to efp, get a team and hunt outdoors with my pets for pretty good exp. I realise that this area became super camped and needed a change, but my point is that hunting medusas in EFP and in DAV was REALLY fun, because i was outside.

After medusas were nerfed, and probably before this too, the entire mission for exp thing started. Broken shores was packed with hundreds of people doing missions because medusas were nerfed, ace camp was overcrowded.. and well.. there are really no other viable exp spots in the game.

so i went along and did missions form lvl 130-150 then i got burned out of them.. specially with a meta and pet pathing, so i played my soldier again. and yes.. i hunted for awhile GREAT fun, until i had to start missioning again.. ive been doing missions from 90-140 now and im sick of it, yet i still do them to keep leveling.

I have to ask, why is there such a massive detailed world with so much wildlife yet no one can hunt outside in it? wouldnt it be easier to just remove most of the world and put mission entrances in a smaller area? I would love to hunt, and many of you will tell me, "well no ones stopping you!!" well my response is that.. for me. a good hunting spot is one where:

1. the exp is half decent
2. the mobs are hard to kill, and thought has to be taken in how to hun them (pull, or kill and calm)
3. has to be 75% zone.. no one likes griefers.
4. most importantly, there has to be enough people around to just go there, get a team and kill some stuff. like the eye camp. you can stand there and /s lfg! and you can get a team (well could.. now its newland)

I miss hunting outdoors, fighting new and different mobs... using a demon or a slayer outdoors is a joy. the graphics are much nicer.. and it's overall alot more fun.

To finish off I'd like to list a few outdoor hunting spots i know of and if any of you can add some that would be cool. maybe i can go out there with some guildmates sometime.

-medusas in EFP - aggro male toons, hit very hard, exp comparable to vets in missions.

-medusas in DAV - aggro male toons, hit very hard, a few different types as well that are harder. many more adds then EFP, EXP can be good if strong team, long flight tho.

-ACE camp- great palce for lower lvl teams, OR one higher level team, exp lower then a good mission, but really good if there is only one team there and no griefers.

-borgs in mort- only hunted there once being omni, exp was great, dangerous wihtout a good calmer, beware griefers.

-EFP in general- lotsa red mobs that give 25-60k exp, adds can be bad exp overall not that great but fun and chance of good loot from mobs. (flowers, mantis, dooms,biles, etc)

-CAV- enigma forest- trees, twigs etc.. had a good time here ages ago, have not been here in months.. upside, exp is good, really neat area to hunt in, lotsa of stuff to kill, and not camped. downsides, trees hit for cold damage and are social aggro now i think. have to go through 2ho to get there fast (or 4 holes ferry) and can be very deadly with adds.

Just compare them
Just compare missions to outdoor hunting areas and you'll see why missions own outdoors hands down.

1) Items
-Missions have chests with rings and nanos, npcs that drop guns/armor/tokens.
-Outdoors have monster parts, SOME npc camps drop guns/armor.
2) Experience
-Missions have monsters on demand, conveniently spread out for single or double pulls at most. In the exact xp range you want.
-Outdoors have large xp ranges. Often times find fresh fixers in same area as Fearplanters. Monsters can spawn and pop in the middle of nowhere and right in front of you. Multiple adds can be common. Sometimes you don't even see them cuz of lag...
3) Travel
-Missions located in towns or 10 seconds from towns.
-Outdoor hunting areas often require a long flight, 2-3 zones over
4) Safety
-Missions have scan terminals just outside. Always safe suppresion. Added bonus of pathing exploiting.
-Outdoor hunting areas are often 1 minute to 5 minutes away from a scanner. Some are in 25% or less suppresion areas.
5) Tokens
-Missions give you tokens
-Outdoor areas do not.
6) Finding people
-People hang around town to sell stuff and get group at same time
-Outdoor areas are empty of people, and almost no one will go with you. People won't even do a mission outside eye camp or homes, why on earth would they hunt outside ???

As you can see, there are virtually no advantages to outdoor hunting. Missions should NOT have every advantage... Right now, missions are BETTER for xp, BETTER for loot, BETTER for tokens, BETTER for safety, and EASIER to get groups.

Funcom, why did you bother making 5 million zones that no one is ever in when we could have simply had 3 towns (Tir, OmniTrade, Newland), and done all missions in Backyards by the newbie zones. There would be virtually NO difference to how the 100+ game is played (with the exception of dungeon camping...)

You need to give outdoor zones a bigger advantage. I've already proposed you SIGNIFICANTLY increase the experience you get from outdoor monsters. On the order of +100% to +200% depending on the distance from a scan terminal and the suppresion in the zone. (i.e. CVA would have +200% xp bonus, Varmint Woods maybe 100%) They should also drop random tokens (maybe 1% chance), or items that you can TURN into side-aligned NPCs for tokens (so both sides can get). Afterall, you get tokens for being brave right... well, kicking ass out of some Nokken in a 25% suppresion zone is definitely more brave then beating up some vat grown clanner that is conveniently living under an omni outpost.

The second thing you need to do is make a WORLD-WIDE LFG channel. This is the year 32,000 right, we do have that sort of technology right???
