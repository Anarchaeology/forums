Please keep in mind that Omni-Tek is a MASSIVE company, and Rubi-Ka is just one of its MANY interests. Some people confuse Phil Ross with the head of Omni-Tek, while hes just one of probably 1000's of high ranking OT individuals.

OT's crime against humanity was using a large portion of Northern America as a giant testing ground for all sorts of ill gotten ideas, then as time went by, it turnt into a scrap yard of sorts.
