A pox! A pox on vicious vixens!
Before I begin I find it proper to introduce myself. I am Stosh Monco, called "Vanguarde" by some, a seller of goods and provider of services. For some time I have been away from cities, preferring to peddle my wares in the solitude of smaller settlements. Nearly a fortnight previous I returned to "civilization", and the behavior of this generation is downright appalling!

Earlier this eve I was in a certain establishment known as "Reets Retreat" in an old haunt of mine, Last Ditch. Perhaps some of you have heard of it? It does seem rather successful... Sorry, enough ballyhoo, cutting down to brass rivets and making short of a short story, I was assaulted! I, a gentleman trader and explorer, attacked by two black-robed, hooded little tarts! No respect for their elders! None whatsoever!

These pernicous prowlers drew me into a side room, with quite ghastly decor I must say, and proceded to carve my carcass like a bronto hide! The sheer nerve! They pretended to listen to my gallant tales of dashing derring-do, only to knife me in the cranial cavity with hardly a "how do you do". Be on the lookout for them...2 women, black cloaks, light build. Probably Opifex breed.

This generation needs a sound thrashing and to learn some proper respect. Now let's hunt these women down and give them what for.
