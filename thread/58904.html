Need an online computer auction system.
The chatter in zones like tir is annoyign to say the least and it forces people to hang around lookign for that one in 100 that wants what they have to sell or has what they want to buy.

The auction channel needs to be suplemented with an auction computer system. You should be able to put your items in a terminal and set auction parameters such as those on ebay. A floor price, a time limit, an instant purchase price and so on.

As a customer you could browse items by category and Quality level. Perhaps even by skills. For example, I could choose ranged weapons, then bows then QL 45.

The results would open the economy up to more enhancements and make AO compeditive with upcomming MMORPGS that will have these systems installed. The whole task is a small bit of server and client coding that would vastly improve the game.
