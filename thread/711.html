Wailing Wastes Zone
I've been hunting in the Wailing Wastes zone (on Rubi-Ka 1) for a few days now, and something is just wrong with that zone and/or server. Its impossible to follow a team member because they "ghost" every few seconds, even if they are just a few feet away. Also monsters disappear and reappear just as badly, making it tougher to hunt for fear of a social aggro. Also, the lag kills are insanely horrible. You'll be hitting an enemy thats is just standing there, then all of the sudden you're dead. I've scrolled up many times to see what kind of damage I've been getting after a lag death, and everytime the damage never shows up.

This also goes for healing teammates. One sec they are full health, next they are dead.

Here's an example: I go to attack a MOB with my group. I notice he gets a critical on me and is hitting harder than I would prefer, and I can only cast one more heal on myself with my limited nano pool, so I run like a bat out of hell and tell my teammates to do the same. Next thing I know, he and my teammates are ghosting behind me leaving trails of themselves. After a while, I stop, sit down and heal myself. I go back and regroup fully healed. I spot another MOB and go up to him to attack, I hit my attack key, but nothing happens. I jump, try to use a special. Nothing. The only thing that seems to help is if I get attacked then I can attack back normally.

Please fix this zone, reboot the server, adjust some settings, please. There aren't any other zones that do this that I know of.
