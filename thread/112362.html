Feedback on MA professional question
EDIT: Question was the possibility of upgrading the MA fist weapons over the MA skill 1000 cap, possibly adding other bonuses at certain skill platues.

Quoting Cz here:

quote:
Martial Artists
Shadowlands, grasshoppers. Shadowlands.

Ok, not so cryptic: For Shadowlands we will upgrade the MA attack templates. Below a skill of 1000 Martial Artists may receive a new template giving them more damage. However, this is not finalized yet and we may decide to not change the templates below 1000 skill. Other professions will continue to use the current template, and everybody will receive new templates above 1000 in skill. Exactly how strong each will be won't be divulged yet, but Martial Artists will be the strongest.

Oh, and even though the upgrade comes with the release of Shadowlands, it's automatically applied to everybody, so you won't have to buy SL to get this upgrade. There's that other snack there, of course...


So, there we have it. Patience then, ehe.

You already figured it out, didn't you?

MA skill translates to (skill/2) on the MA attack 'item'. E.g if you have 250 in MA skill, you'll use the MA attack template at QL 125. I'm sure any of the MA Professionals can give you a full description.

When talking about adding new templates, it's basically making new templates above the QL 500 one, so having more than 1000 in skill gives you a better basic weapon in addition to the modifiers for high skill.
