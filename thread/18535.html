Idea for quests
I've played about every MMORPG out there (dear gods, I have NO life. I'd weep, but my eyes are too dry from stareing at the damn monitor for 19 hours a day).

Each game has it's strong points and downsides:

AC: Upsides
=========
good social system (guilds offer diffinate and obvious rewards to both the high and low end players)

Tons of quests

Monthly updates for content and general improvements

Good content amount for all but the highest level players

Quest system designed to reduce camping

Huge playing area

Continuing story

Downsides:
========
Badly aged and out of date graphics

Lack of diversity in player types (you really only have straight melee, and full on mage)

Lack of high end game

Lack of newbies, and out of balance ecomony



EQ: Upsides:
=========
Large player base

Lots of quests

Very large playing area

Wide array of player types

Downsides:
=========

Way, way... way too much camping

No real story

Little to no soloing ability

Ecomony extreamly out of balance

Extreamly hard on newbies

Death penalty too harsh


DAoC: Upsides:
===========

Innovative quest system

encouragment of team play

realm verses realm with real impact on the game world (well, in theory. they haven't actually got that working yet)

Interesting diversity of player types

Very stable

Downsides:
=========

Very small playing area

Lack of things to do

Gets boreing very quickly

Serious imbalance in PvP

Lack of content

Very little in the way of story


AO: Upsides
=========

Interesting storyline

Huge playing area

Good diversity of player types (although, some like Fixer and Advent are badly in need of fixing)

Lots of soloing ability

Unique world setting (thank the gods! No more damn forests and elves and goblins!)

Incentive to team, and join guilds

wide variety of skills available to all classes

Downsides:
========

Bugs. Oh dear gods the bugs!

Class imbalances

Forceing PvP on everyone (even if you have no interest in PvP, you still end up getting ganked by jerks in pvp zones you have to go through)

Laggy gameplay (it makes team missions and hunting very difficult and annoying)

Massive lack of content

The NPC quest system is FUBAR'd

Lack of communication to players on important issues (I know Cosmik and CZ try to keep us informed, but often important issues get no response for weeks, or even months)



I play AO because I can't face another "Greetings to thee your traveler, go out and kill thee some rats" game right now. I love the sci-fi setting and content of AO. I'd like it more if it worked.

I'd also like it if there was more to do than missions, team hunting, and getting in line for the 3 massivly overcamped static dungeons.

I'd like suggest a way to combine several features from other MMORPG's, to improve the gameplay content of AO. A timer would work like this: AFter picking up a unique item from a mob in a static dungeon (ie: nebs robe, shade of lub, class special items), that player CANNOT pick that item up again (even if that item is no longer in their inventory) for a set amount of time. The more rare the item, the longer the timer (so, a month timer on the shades for example).

1) Quest timers. they work like a charm in AC. It would drastically cut down on dungeon camping. and prevent the Ebay farming of certain items (shades of lub, vests, etc).

2) Stop making the NPC quest gives dissapear! it just annoys everyone. Quest timers will help here too. No one should have to sit there for 2 hours (if they're lucky) just to hand in an item.

3)Make static dungeon's like solo missions. IE: if you go in a static dungeon, it spawns a new instance of that dungeon for you (and your team), instead of everyone on the planet going into the same dungeon. There are so few dungeons, and so many players, it's camp city.

4) MORE DUNGEONS/QUESTS

5) Make unique missions pop up from time to time at the terminals. Something that offers a high challenge, but with greater rewards for those of us who enjoy solo play.

6) A greater range of dungeons/quests. Make some for very low level players, and some for very high level ones.


Just my thoughts.

So, after talking to yet another player that wants to quit due to the whole extreme boredom factor involved with -mission - hunt- lvl - repeat... I think that some serious dirversity is in order.

How about some profession specific missions?

Say I'm an agent - I need to sneak into clan territory (as an Omni) and go into a building and steal documents. Token and xp there - then take the plans to Omni HQ - token and xp. Stealth and survival would be a factor - no xp for NPC's killed en route and aggroing a mob would set off an alarm for nearby mobs. (obviously the xp at reward would have to be high enough to make it worth it.) Having it set up this way means that it would be desireable to actually use stealth or guile to get around... killing everything you see would be dangerous, time consuming and just cost ammo...

Or as an NT, I have to find elements to create a specific nano. An instruction disk here, a crystal matrix here, etc. and perhaps that nano would be the only thing that would allow the possibility of defeating a specific monster.

Soldiers find the parts to assemble a quest related weapon. ( again, the only way to kill mobX)

Engineers could find all the necessary parts for an Uber-bot.

These things should be unique and not combine with standard parts to make items.

The missions should be easy enough to implement , the standard mission booths already know what profession you are (as evidenced by the trader-only booths) and the team booths could require each member to get a specific part.

This would, in theory, give some meaning to putting skill points into specific attributes... right now the only thing my nano programming skills are worth is if I want to make implants for other players, most times I don't throw points at it because I always need more to hit the next QL and I don't change mine that often... make it worth my while to blow points on it - not something I would use once a month.

These quests should forward your experience - not just your wallet and make it worthwhile to do them, instead of running out to the fields and shooting mobs...
