Press Release: Federated Clans of Rubi-Ka
February 28.29477 RST

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Schuemann and Associates Will Consult on Clan Constitution

After several weeks of discussion between Racheal "Blackpetal" Schuemann, senior partner of Schuemann and Associates legal firm, and Christopher "Uwen" Macgreggor, Schuemann and Associates has agreed to serve as legal consultant on a planned democratic constitution for current clan-controlled territory.

"Rubi-Ka is at an important crossroads right now," said Ms. Schuemann. "If all her citizens are to enjoy a measure of prosperity, safety, and freedom, I can think of no more important step than the creation of a responsive and stable government overseeing Omni-Tek's northern neighbors."

Asked about her own role in the creation of the legal document, especially as an Omni-Tek employee, Ms. Schuemann responded, "My law firm is serving on a consultation basis only. I'm not interested in nation-building. I must say, on a personal level, that I am also doing this to honor the memory of the late Malcom "Alamexis" Age, who was brutally slain by extremists as a result of his efforts to bring a peaceful resolution to the struggle between Omni-Tek and the Clans. Such a sacrifice cannot be allowed to pass without notice."

Ms. Schuemann added, "Some will call me a traitor, but I think of myself as a patriot. History, I suppose, will be the final judge."

The legal firm of Schuemann and Associates is based in Rome.

Racheal Schuemann is kept on a non-exclusive retainer by Devil's Advocate, but Schuemann and Associates is an independent entity. Schuemann and Associates is not affiliated with Devil's Advocate.
