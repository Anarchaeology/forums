We got lousy hp and have crappy alpha strikes, we're just a strange profession to fight against, because we're doing so many different things at one time. Just concentrate on the mp. dont touch his pets.

Do this: Take your current skillset and minus -220 from it. now figure out which mk shield you can use with -220 on all nanos. then get that ready for when you can use mk shields again.
