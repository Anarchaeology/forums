Positions open for loyal Omni-Tek women and atrox
Heya! I'm Tamara Esselte Alsaqri, Madame Canvasser for The Honored Maidens.

Esselte smiles.

The Honored Maidens are a group of woman and atrox based on Rubi-Ka Prime (AKA RK1) who are loyal to Omni-Tek and Philip Ross. By loyal, I mean loyal. Mr Ross says "Don't attack clanners," we don't attack clanners. Mr. Ross says "Protect Radiman," we protect Mr. Radiman. No matter what our personal opinion on the matter is.

Obviously, maturity is a must. There are some... no, many... who have recently been giving into their baser instincts.

Esselte frowns slightly.

We're not among them, I can assure you.

Esselte brightens.

Anyway. I'd better summarise. I know that atrox in particular like things put simply and clearly, so here goes.

Honor.
Loyalty.
Maturity.

If you've got all three of those, and you're a woman or atrox working for Omni-Tek, you're welcome among our ranks. Oh, I think I might still have a form or two with me. I think there was one in here somewhere...

Esselte rummages around in a medium-sized backpack for a minute.

Yeah, here it is. Don't worry about the creases, it'll still work. Hmm... I'd better go get some more of these, actually. Listen, if you're interested, just fill it out and send it to thm_recruit@discombobulate.net. It'll take some time to go through the bureaucratic machine, but in less than a week we'll be able to tell you if you made the cut.

Thanks for listening.

Esselte smiles.
