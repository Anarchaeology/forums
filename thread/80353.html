Re: A few questions for the experienced..
1. Well you can start pvping at any level, some classes excel at lower levels, some are worse, some get better after 100.

As for learning the pvp system, its not just how to pvp, you need to know the game mechanics, what happens if he does this, or I do that. You need to know what the person you are fighting can do, and what best to counter that if at all possible. There is ALOT to learn I couldnt just tell you here.

Best bet is to ask for a friendly duel, or get a friend to help you.
What proffession are you?


2. Well a trader 'can' be a good pvp char from start to end in solo fights. But as I said classes vary performacne in levels. An enforcer would probably be the BEST all round fighter vs any proffession. You have a slight hp advantage to most people, you can be almost immune to roots eventually, or break them better than anyone can. You have a ever increasing volly of weapons to chose from, you can deal the damage and take it. Very good in pvm and pvp.


3. Well, yes it is up to the player to keep the character the best it can be. Implants, armor, weapons, special items dont hurt either. But if your fighting a player who doesnt know how to pvp with his prof you could probably win. For example, I saw a Enforcer fight someone, never used mongo, rage or challenger ever and died.
But it can be a paper/rock/scissors game, some classes can outwrite OWN another, such as NT>GA fixers.
If you put two people exactly the same in experience, same proffesion, then it would be the one with the most luck Crits and best damage can win the fight.


Not much help here but, you really need to take part in pvp to learn it, even if it involves dying over and over. Maybe you could ask to fight to 50% health, or test with your friends.
Know your enemy is the best tool, alot of classes have weaknesses.
