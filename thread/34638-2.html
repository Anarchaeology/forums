Originally posted by Vydas

Actually I think it is. Ninja looters can get a team killed. Arguing usually will not. I usually ask that the team loot be changed to leader only for the boss room and the loot splitting tends not to be very painful unless you stumble into a team of ego maniacs lacking a semblence of conscience. Even if there are arguments, the resulting distribution will be better. Right now, those who are the worst team players typically reap the biggest rewards. Loot is a team asset not one for the person with the highest B&E or the greatest willingness to shirk team responsibility to make a few credits. It would be nice to see a solid tank, a great doc, an awesome nuker or an expert mezzer get rewards commensurate with their contributions for a change.

Even if the results are occassionally messy, team members would LEARN something from the process of dividing loot. Right now the team loot system teaches you only one lesson--be a pig or forget loot. An open loot system will teach something different. If you contribute, you get rewarded. If you hold out on others, you get ostracized. No system will be perfect but I would sooner see merit--rather than greed--be an occasional factor in loot distribution.
