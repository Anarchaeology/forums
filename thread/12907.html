Conf Need your Support (meeting)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
During the last meeting of the members of Live Free or Die it was
decided, with input from the leader of Independent Rubikans and
a member of GVRK, that we would set up a meeting of leaders of all
organizations interested in undertaking a joint project that is
in the interests of all citizens of Rubi-Ka. The project is to find
and apprehend the members of Dust Brigade known as the Masked Commandos.
To this end we invite all leaders, Omni, Clan and Neutral, to a
meeting in Reet Retreat, location Last Ditch, On March 2 at 4pm GMT earth standard time.
All leaders attending the meeting should bring 2 body guards, one
to stand with them and the other to help stand watch outside the
meeting, as we have no intention of being caught off guard by the Terrorist DB again.
Remember, we need to support each other in this endeavour. United
we can achieve more than we ever will on our own.
Anyone wanting more information, or just to confirm your attendance,
can contact the following people:

Royr, Leader LFD.
Abrii
Silenna, Leader Independent Rubikans.

My Org don't have time to talk with every clan, OT, or Neut Org. If you have contact to any Org please invite them to this conf.

I know that most of you don't think that we cant work together but what harm is it to try. I not ask to trust each other to the point where you can turn your back but we do need to work together in finding DB.

I ask each Leader that show to bring to guards, and think about having a team on the ready to support us if DB attack the conf. Leader can also bring one person with them inside the meeting room.

Solo members are welcome but need to be known by one of the Org members. If no one knows the solo member then he or she is still welcome but will be watched by one or two guards.

I will not stand for inhouse fighting at the conf. So please keep your view on Clan, OT, Neut to yourself. This conf is about DB and only DB and their removal from this world.
