A Tribute to lost life.
Tomorrow morning 1 year ago is the anniversary of a horrible thing that happened to those persons in NYC, both members of public service and individuals who were in the Twin Towers buildings. People all over the world are in the process of creating memorials to this horrible occurance. Total Aggression Gaming will assume a period of 24 hours, in game. During this period we will not initiate any combat in 25% zones in honor of those who lost family, friends or relatives on Sept 11th 2001.

This period begins as of 12:00PM EST (NY Time).

TAG/MoK is not asking anyone else to do this, however we would be happy to join with others who would do so of their own free will.

Regards,
