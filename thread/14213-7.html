I can answer this:

"Make the Fixer Area Burst damage be a weapon damage and not a nano damage, i normaly have other hit by other adn other hit by nano turned off for spam control, so i cannot see what damage im doing with my area burst "

I assume you think it does nano damage because you can sometimes get the "target resisted" message (and the fact that "other hit by nano" turns it off). This feedback doesn't actually have anything to do with the type of damage dealt, but the WAY it's dealt (not like a normal shot from a bullet).

So: The attack does deal projectile damage, but the feedback when the target dodges is wrong. It should have been something like "Target dodged your attack" - and it should be displayed the way other special attacks are displayed.

It will be fixed. I can't tell when, though.
