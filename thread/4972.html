How about bringing back the Original FOV from Beta?
How about letting us change the FOV?

AO simply looked better in Beta, and it's 100% due to the FOV.

To get the game to look anything like it did in Beta4 I have to play in 1600x900. The vertical stretching in this resolution closely resembles the visual perspective AO had before you widened the FOV just before release.

Problem is the game runs crapy in 1600x900. There are tons of glitches and areas where I normally get 13-20 fps with 1600x1200 or 1280x1024, I am getting 2fps (that's a pretty drastic reduction in performance wouldn't ya say?)

So PLEASE, give me the option of choosing the original Field of View.
