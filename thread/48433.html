Whats in a name?
Well, what IS in a name? Quite a lot in my humble opinion. Consider:

Your name is often THE first thing people get to base an opinion on. First opinions are, in my experience, incredibly hard to change if you make a bad one, and therefore a fairly important aspect of any chars development. Its whats put up in lights if you ever manage to convince a TAG Reporter that they ARE in fact interested in sordid revalations about your "dubious" experiences with Leets at College. Its what people define you by, what they know you as, hell just look at the whole Superslag affair for evidence of how important names are.

The point to all this? Well it just makes me wonder why people can be so damn lazy when choosing names! I've counted at least FIVE variations on the name "Behemoth" in the last month alone. Wheres all the imagaination gone?
