Someone please correct me if I've forgotten...

PM and SI factor into many, if not most, of our commonly used nanoprograms.

I give them my top priority as nanoskills.

Whether you group often or not, HoTs are important. If you prefer mission groups, Speed nanos aren't as important (it's hard to corner when you're going fast), other than for the boost to evades.

I think the usefulness of the higher end of the evac nanos is questionable, unless you intend to spend a lot of IP in nano init.

It was said long ago that the better part of the snare and root lines are useless, too. I don't agree, especially now that their effectiveness has increased.

So...PM and SI, TS and BioMet of tertiary importance (for me), and whatever else I need to keep my HoTs as current as I can. No MC. I gave up on GA long ago.

I also cut it as close as I can to get into new nanos, which is a pain, because buffs tend to run out at the most inopportune moments.

Edit: Yeah, sacrifice evades (but not too much) for nanoskills at the very low levels. Who do you think you're dodging, anyway? Once you hit 50, though, get with evades. I wish I did.
