Hey there everyone,

While I myself agree with the majority of people here that the "streamlining" of the Official Bulletin Boards is not really that beneficial to the community, I can see why Funcom have decided to make this decision. I raised my concerns about these actions potentially splitting the Anarchy Online community into several groups when this development was first mentioned to me, but as I see it, the decision has been made and we're going to have to make do the best we can with the resulting situation.

I am glad however that the changes will ease the pressure on Cz a little, even if I though that hiring more Community staff would be a better way to progress. At the end of the day that is merely my opinion.

Anyway, onto the discussions here about potential forums. I'm not going to take an aggressive stance and shameless plug the AO-Basher forums because the last thing I want to do is force players to use a system their not comfortable with. The most important thing is that people keep talking somewhere because as soon as that stops, the life-blood of this game is drained away. For all the problems that Anarchy Online has had and are having, I have no doubt that the game's community is easily the best of any MMORPG.

However, the AO-Basher forums are there for those who want to use them. I can also categorically state that any details you enter will NEVER be used in any commercial way, they'll just sit in the AO-Basher SQL database doing nothing unless readers want them to. Also the site has absolutely no advertising at all. We have been offered advertising deals in the past, but I absolutely hate them and as we are a one off and not part of a network, I luckily get to make all these decisions myself.

Next an apology. As mentioned in threads here, I know the boards are very underused at the moment, primarily because the Official Bulletin Boards are so good.We will be happy to create new forums for people who want an exact replacement of those being removed from here. We also know that the site's content is very old, but we are working to change this and we need the average players help. If you have guides, screenshots, maps, even your own editorials, don't hesitate to send them in as I have a policy of posting every bit of news I receive (within reason).

The AO-Basher team is only constructed of three people at the moment, and so you can imagine that we don't have the power to get as much done as some of the fansites with larger staff numbers, but to battle this I would love to have more editorials and articles from players. See it as working as a freelance reporter. If you have something to say and want it read by lots of people then come to us and we'll do our best to get it up for you.

Anyway, that's enough waffle for now. I just wanted to explain AO-Basher's current status to those who are looking into finding a "new home". Good Luck
