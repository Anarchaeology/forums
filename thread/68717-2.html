Opinions on the video...

First off, it's a scum-sucking bit of rigged clan propaganda. This should be borne in mind from the outset. 6 on 5 and none of the clanners die?


Ahem. I digress.....observations are indexed by time in the movie.

0:12. Just WHAT is that bow wielding miscreant wearing on her head? Can't recall seeing that design before - can anyone identify it?

0:20: And the Omni "leader" is out of uniform (only wearing low-tech armour as well?). Ye gods, what's this corporation coming to?

0:28: Background shows a walled city, my guess is Tir.

0:29: Background ships seem to be dynamic - the one directly behind the towers is moving right to left - though slowly.

0:58 AE nuke (to judge by sound effects) centered on the tower, apparently cast by same.



Conclusions:

Nothing we didn't really know before. Dynamic scenery, such as the traansport ships will add to immersion, provided it doesn't cripple us with lag.

Video images are ingame footage, but some things have been altered and touched up. Makes it hard to tell what details are ingame and what are edits.

Only 5v6? I'm sure you've got footage of bigger fights....be interesting to see how it panned out.

Fight didn't really show any of the tactical depth that AO PvP can have. None of the Omnis used their mirror shields, for example.

Overall? Good, but not I don't think we were the target audience for this one. This looks more like something intended for the non-AO players of this world.

(PS: For a good example of a trailer, the old System Shock 2 trailer is hard to beat.). Not sure if it can be gotten any more though...
