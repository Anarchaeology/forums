Rules to Roleplaying (Becoming Apparent)
OOC: Its becoming more and more apparent we need a set of rules to which we can follow for roleplaying. What could of been resolved tonight in about 1 line of text took around an hour and a half. If you were there, you know. If you wernt, there is no need to explain. If you dont want to RP, or never want to RP at all, you need to let people know. IF your OOC let people know so they dont take the time and effort to roleplay when in fact its useless. Establish your status as far as IC and OOC go.

My rule
I use two very simple rules for this kind of thing:

1) I never, ever involve someone in roleplay unless they make an active attempt to get involved. I will merrily roleplay any conversation with a person (even when talking to a non-RPer) but I assume people are non-roleplayers until proven otherwise.

2) If I am to engage in RP, I will send the person an OOC /tell at my earliest convenience explaining to them that I am indeed roleplaying. If the response is a '???' or a 'what is that?' or similar, then I cease and desist any 'involved' RP with that person.

But that's just me. I know many purists out there who refuse to do anything OOC in any way when online. However, I'll say to those people: there are non-RPers playing AO and they have as much right to be there as you do. Not everyone is there for the same reasons as you.

I think it's pretty obvious that it's a lot harder to have good IC interaction without some OOC communication.

Using temporary chat groups can be great for this in situations where many people are involved. Invite everyone who is RPing with you into a temp channel, and use that as your own "private OOC" while all whispers, tells, shouts and vicinity are IC. Someone will have to be the designated "keeper of the temp channel" and there should be a backup for them, too in case of crashes/linkdeaths/having to go due to emergencies/etc.

These two people could be further designated as the "scene GMs" whose role is to mediate/resovle disputes, keep track of who's in and who's out of the scene, and basically be referees.

If you come late to a scene, you can ask who the scene GM is, send a tell, and get brought in to the OOC channel. Everyone already in there will be able to see you say Hi, so they'll know you're RPing with them, and you'll be able to see them talk, so you'll know who you're RPing with.

Thoughts?
