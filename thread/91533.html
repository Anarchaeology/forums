I'd be genuinely interested if you could come up with a way of generating content that didn't seem jaded or automated.

The verisimilitude or variety of the simulation isn't the issue here.

The problem is that you may have read the mission description the first time after the 5th it simply becomes the "find item" mission.

Sure if you went round every mission thinking "Hey I need to dump this poison into the food hopper because somebody hacked into the mission terminal and gave me this really important task to do", but the chances are you won't even read it.

So perhaps hand scripted quests are the way forward. Trouble is they take a lot of work to generate and once solved and the solutions disseminated they're really no different from missions. (Dodga/Alvin)

My point is that whatever you come up with sooner or later seem formulaic. "Oh this is the get 3 people to open the doors from different directions mission". "Or this is the mission where the fixers have to hack the mainframe."

To take this to an extreme imagine FC came up with a seemless automated world generator. Something that could generate endless scenery and intelligent complex, deep interconnected subtle plots and continously changing quests. (and somebody will one day).
Sooner or later you'd start feeling, "damn more computer generated scenery and interconnected plots", "what's the point it's just random numbers, good looking random numbers, but random number nonetheless."

So what does make a difference?. I'll tell you what! people. Real people.

In Quake or Medal of Honour you play it through once but the chances are you'll never play it through again. You will however go online and play the same rotation of team missions over and over again until your fingers bleed. Would we do the same against bots?

The single overarching thing that keeps us in this game is the other people. Funcom know this too I suspect which is where notum wars came from. But is NW enough, have they perhaps sanitised it too much, made it too fair? Do people really care that much about their 3 plots of land. Or the treadmill of aquiring new 3 plots of land. Maybe if they freed the system up a bit and made it so Storm or Legion could own half the land on the planet it would be different, who knows?

Whatever the answers, the future lies in anything that focuses on the human element. Rip away all the smoke and mirrors and the AO is a chat client with an element of competition thrown in.
