Poisened President - change to command of the IR
The dust filled winds blew other the hills and across the main square of Borealis as the suns slowly moved the way up the sky, marking the beginning of another day. Marcos "Yarko" Orender, stepped onto the square from the Newlands whompa. Almost instantly a quick blue flash ran over up the street, and what seemed like right through him. A quick pain was felt in Yarko's chest as he made his first step from the Whompa, shaking off that unusual feeling of having your body ripped apart and accelerated across the planet and then hastily put back together in just seconds. And also the odd feeling of the very hasilty passing fixer.

He paused a moment and rubbed his chest. Thinking for a moment how strange it was, the sharp pain in his chest as he made the entry from Newlands, and he might just drop around and see one of the Healing Hands later for a check up after the meeting if he had time...whats the worst that could happen? he has insurance after all.

And so he was at another meeting for the Independant Rubikans. The organisation that he has now lead for the last 8 months....thinking "was it really that long" to himself for a moment he prepared his notes for the evening, coughed, and felt dizzy. Hmmm, I wonder what the DW have been putting on the fire again in the factory, seems Borealis is spinning

The meeting was running as normal...promotions, news, chat, arguments, the odd sacrificial slaying of the noob. And throughout the colour of the President continued to change for the worse. With those before him enquiring if he was really feeling fit for the meeting. Sluring his speaking from time to time. He began to make more and more mistakes till he stumbled forwards and collapsed to the floor. The people around him rushed forward to try and catch him, but it was too late as he hit the floor hard. Feeling for a pulse the on hand medical staff looked for any sign of life....yet non came.

Immediatly search partys were dispatched. He must have insured somewhere. He will be around? After combing Rubi-ka news slowly came in that someone resembling Yarko had been sighted in one of the backyards. Curiously the search partys went to these areas around Borealis to see if any sign of the fallen President could be found.

Amongst the trees and hills of the Borealis backyards was a lost man. Dizzy, and in unfamiliar lands. He seemed to be saying he had only just arrived off the Morning Star, and he was busy like most other new citizens chasing down and hunting the helperbots and evil rollerrats in these areas. The man stopped as the group in the search party approached. Whatever could all these people want in an area like this, and why where they looking so strangely at him. And who was this Mr President they called him? they all seemed to be deluded or have they mistaken him for someone else? They all seemed nice enough, for a group of obviously deluded loons, but he went along with them for the time being as they weren't causing him any harm. And the nice lady agent seemed to be offering to help him fix the cut on his head caused by the last rollerrat....so they can't be too bad

Mademoiselle "Oliewabie" Pompadour changed into her medical equipment and went closer to where Yarko was standing. Although she is an agent, she was all that was available for the moment to try and save the Presidents memory, and see what could be done. Taking a swift gulp from her whiskey bottle she slowly began to operate on the President. And with the assistance of Lothori who had been busy cleaning the Presidents memory seemed to piece him back together....but only time will tell.

Yarko slowly got back to his feet as nanobots slowly knitted his head back together. He was still dizzy, and lost. But there was faint memorys from the past of names and faces he knew. He couldnt as yet piece them together. But it was a start. And who was this Mocham lady he has so many memories have?

Major Prabel slowly escourts his former President to the Newlands whompa. The nanoites flowing over his grey armour.

With the incapacitation of the leader, Major Nyadach Prabel handed over his Ambassidorial keys to the other Advisors, and picked up the keys to the Presidential desk...and with it the access codes to the IRs arsenal, and librarys. Leaning back into the presidents chair he paused and thought a moment....It had been some time since he had this seat, and isn't it strange how the future seems awefully much like the past. Speaking aloud he wondered and asked himself,
"Do things ever actually change? or is it just faces."

Smiling he relaxed back into the seat just as an emergancy call comes in. It seems as though one of those under his command have attacked a tower....sighing as he hit his comm....
