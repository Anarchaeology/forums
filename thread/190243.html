Just have to add a small shout-out for Fixers here. I think Fixers tie with Martial Artists for one of the best professions to start with. In terms of nanos: Long and short HoTs ensure that you have effectively many more HPs than you actually do, runbuffs increase your evades and make you go reallyreally fast, NCU buffs are just nice, and, of course, you have the grid teleportation lines. Weapon-wise, SMGs tend to be cheap and most of them are pretty easy to get your hands on. (The exception to both these rules is the Manex Catastrophe, a boss-only drop which can run you a couple million at high QLs. It's really good, though, especially with a TIM scope.) They do great damage and burst is a great skill. Perk-wise, we get Acrobat, and once you finally get Limber, you'll wonder how you survive without it.

And then there's money-making. Evade, runbuffs, and evac means that you can blitz the highest level missions you can pull with a good degree of success, especially easily-blitzable missions like Find Person or Repair, and you can get home before any other equivalent class has even made it to the mission. Fixer Grid even allows you to get even closer than 99.9% of the rest of the world.

But, don't get me wrong, there are downsides too. Six dark blue nanoskills mean you'll constantly have problems uploading nanos that are your level -- and if, by some miracle of fate and chance, you get Grid Armor, you'll have trouble equipping nanos 5-10 QLs below your level. Though our SMGs are ridiculously nice, and Burst+a hit+Cluster Shells = tons of alpha = instant aggro, we aren't really tanks and we don't have the evades for getting wailed on. In the first 5 seconds of a battle, you'll probably steal aggro from more or less anyone if your SMG is over-equipped, but when they take it back again, both you and your doctor will be very glad.

Finally, looting chests. Now, I love looting chests. I levelled my B&E skill just so I could do this. But everyone else will hate you for it. Either you'll get fantastically good loot that they can't really ask you for, because it's yours by the right that you opened the chest, or you'll get some sort of hideous trap -- God forbid a roller rat -- that seems benign enough until you realize your entire team is engaged in battle somewhere else. And then you add a roller rat to them, and people get confused, and can start dying.

Well, that's my take on it, at least.
