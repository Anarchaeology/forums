Proposal for an "events forum" to make up for slow/nearly nonexistant news updates.
We all know that there are an awful lot of small events that happen in Rk1 and Rk2, but since the huge majority of them fade into the shadows of nothingness once it's over never to be heard from again for the majority of us nothing actually happens unless you or a friend personally stumbled upon it...

If there was a new forum added that had each event done given a new thread that gave the time/date it happened along with a brief overview of what went on then we as players could see that things happen and keep up with the news that funcom couldn't possibly devote a staff to writing articles on each and every event.
