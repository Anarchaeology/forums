Some new ideas that can boost AO
1. Each weapon should contain in its description a list of all players killed by this weapon on PVP. While it sound weird at first, think about the value and interest in specific weapons that killed AO's most popular players.

2. Have each dungeon act like a team-mission, where for every 6 or so guys you get a spawned boss. This will eliminate camping and inject many more game-time hours for many players. So what if everyone will have that uber coat that the dragon drops? FC gets the money at the end of the month and we get more funnage. Players won't cancel their subscription at the end of that month if FC will provide many more of those dungeons.

3. Fix boss drops NOW, not in the next patch. Have bosses drop 6 items, all unique. Again, people are dropping from AO like flies, why not try the method that says "If I know I can get 200 unique items, I will play until I have 200 unique items" ?

4. Give us some new zones to hunt in. If it requires too many programmers from your precious NWN project, give us medusas back, at EFP and DAV. Make those medusas 100kxp each and eliminate the stupid uber dmg you put on them. Make EFP 25% also. Right now you got more problems caused by boredom-quitters than problems caused by players getting to 200 lvl and quitting.

5. Add grid points to EFP, SFH, PW and any other zone without a grid point. Add mobs (not neccessarily new mobs, so again, you won't have to cut some programmers and gfx guys from NWN) in 200 lvl to those zones and you get more funnage for your buck.

6. Add those trade-skills that enables us to improve weapons we got by 10-20 QLs. Have only fixers able to do those changes, so they feel somewhat more needed.

7. Fix the stupid soldier line. Take out MK for all I care, I've heard enough of "You soldiers don't need to be fixed since you got MK". It's stupid and if you're rooted, useless too. If you can't find anything to fix MK with, give us some healing nano or root breaking nano that we can use even when in nano-inop (nano nerf after casting mk).

8. Fix Docs so they can remove roots from players on your own side. The nano that was supposed to do it got nerfed by FC and now it's considered as a hostile nano which means that a doc on your side can't clear roots on you...

9. Let us know exactly how many PVP points we have and how many we need till next level. You will get tons of new PVPers that way and many more players will stay in the game because of such a tiny detail.

10. Give us a button to reload our weapons automatically. Enough with the stupid drag and drop.

11. Give us a button to make our DEF/AGG bar the opposite of its current state. Meaning, that if I'm at 100% AGG and get attacked by 10 guys, we can hit that button, move to 100% DEF and run or whatever.

12. Start replying to people on those forums. If you don't have enough manpower to do that, hire more guys. Use some of the money we pay to show us that you care.

13. ARKs are now simply a filter - they filter out problems that they can't solve from problems that they absolutley can't solve. If they are so useless, hire some guys to make their job more efficiently. I don't care to wait 2-3 hours if I know that the guy I'm facing isn't a 60 lvl enforcer that plays 2 weeks but someone who can actually help me. So far, after playing since day one, I didn't get even one ARK generated solution. Only GMs.

14. WARNING - WORK AHEAD - SKIP IF YOU'RE AN FC EMPLOYEE - Fix the DB so you can tell whether or not that guy who says he lost all of his equippment due to a bug, really lost his stuff or is he goofin' around. Because just between us, this is the real reason you don't compensate people on their losses. This hard-headed policy will cost you alot of players. In this case, it's better to get skimmed a few times than to not get skimmed at all.

All of the above will INSTANTLY generate many more players for AO. Guild will suddenly see members they haven't seen for weeks.

And before you tell us it's all being planned for ShadowLands:
WE WILL NOT BUY SHADOWLANDS IF YOU DON'T FIX THOSE PROBLEMS NOW!!
We simply can't trust a company that tells us that the expansion will be so-and-so while the game we currently play is dying on us and not living to what the same company promised us months ago.
