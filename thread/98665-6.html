Various Clans have been attacking Neutral towers, and the guards in neutral towns for a very long time now.

Attack those clans that support them. Unless we are saying that the Sentinels are more powerfull than the rest of the clans combined. If this is the case, we may as well start bowing down before our new lords and masters now.
