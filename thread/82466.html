Um, well there were no inhabitants of Rubi-ka before Omni-tek (ok that's not really true... there were plants and some indiginous life) but no settlers. Omni, an at the time struggling hyper-corporation, put in a bid to lease planet rubi-ka and, after failed ICC attempts to survey the world, provided their own survey results to ICC. No other corporations challenged the lease, and OT was granted the planet. It was only after this (officially) that OT discovered notum, and its amazing potential, and began mining and offworld shipments. Everything else is history. It's not OT that's taking over the planet in a hostile fashion, it's the clans that have broken away from their parent company, Omni-Tek who by the way loves all of its employees, and are currently responsible for most of the agression taking place on Rubi-ka.

So, all in all, other than it being the far future and sci-fi, I don't really see the parallel's to Star Trek.
