Organization bonuses: A look at it from both sides
I see many clanners recently demanding that they get their bonuses, because they claim Omni-Tek has theirs.

Fine. Fair enough.

Omni-Tek does have bonuses. Clans should have an equal (but different) bonus. Let's see.... +5 to one stat? How about Pharma Tech, maybe +10 to tutoring?

Come on people, Omni-Tek gets a 2-5% discount (can't remember which, but it's miniscule) and can wear decent armors. Is that really a problem? Omni only Armors sure aren't even in the top three types of armor, so why do you care? Is 2-5% more cash important to you? It isn't to me.

Any stat bonus is an incredible bonus when compared to this crummy "perk" Omni-Tek employees get.

What should be done is a tiny bonus given to each clan organization (a few choices) and then all organizations can use the customizing stat boosting bonuses. That way clans still have a slight edge, but the interesting customization this organization perk feature adds will not be limited to just a few organizations. That way, clans get a small stat boost but Omni-Tek isn't stuck with a weak perk. I mean, 2-5% discount? Armors no one cept tanks and wierdos use? Ha!

Look at the balance and you'll see that a +20 boost to a stat is many times more useful than a tiny discount and Omni-Tek armors. I'd happily trade a 10% discount for a stat booster like these clans want.

Anyway... I already know most clanners just cheat and use alts to buy stuff from Omni shops for em. So what's up with that? Clan will work around their limited "perks", but Omni can't do the same.

Each side should have equal (in terms of value) perks, and a tiny discount and mediocre armor use is not anywhere near the value of stat bonuses.

At level 200, who will be better off? The guy with 3% more cash or the guy with +20 more in a few skills?

Gee...

Hopefully Funcom won't limit the idea of customized organizations, otherwise they'll just waste what could have been a fairly interesting idea.


(don't tell me "be clan then", cause that isn't a valid response. Explain how stat bonuses could be equal in "fairness" to a tiny discount and use of armor 1/2 the people in the game wouldn't use)
