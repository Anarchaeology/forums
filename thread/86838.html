I personally am burned out on missions. On the rare occasions that I get a team, it's like pulling teeth to keep me motivated. We really need more fun ways of gaining experience and levels in this game, yknow?

Outdoor camps to me are still a viable option. The bosses out there give *alot* more xp than mission bosses. My MA could solo about a level 210-217 snake boss and solo that would net me like 8-900,000 xp. In a team, it's something like 5-600k, but not too bad, considering the boss would spawn about every 10-15 minutes, drop all the regular boss loot and also some goodies you can't find in indoor missions. But more importantly, it's alot of fun. I would have a hoot spending all day filling up bags with outdoor nanos and surprising a friend with all his new nanos. I actually got so many of them that nobody I knew needed them anymore so I sold a bunch and made a bit of cash.

It can get to be lonely work though, so having a team out there would make things 200% better. If ever you're inclined to check it out, there's a snake camp in CAV right on the east border (almost directly east of a fixer grid exit too). Level 200 minion snakes. Each would go down solo in 6-9 seconds with my mas, depending on how many crits I got and each would give me 45-56,ooo xp per kill on a one minute spawn. That's alot of xp.

In a team though, you might want a bit more of a challenge. Directly north there's another snake camp with lvl 210ish boa rexes and two bosses, one level 135 and one level (eep) 175. It;s not to difficult to avoid the bosses if need be though. The snake AI is pretty predictable.

Well, hope that helps someone out that's looking to avoid the bore of missions Send me a tell if you ever have a slot open. MPs make pretty decent teammate outdoors!

My 2 cents
Here's my 2 cents for what it's worth:

At low levels solo-ing is a viable option. It's much easier to level and you can keep all the loot. Low level chars need money and they still get good XP.

I've also noticed there are certain "spots" where people gather when looking for groups to team. I used to hang out at SW mines to hunt when I was lvl 40-70, 20k for lvl 70-110, and Newland City for lvl 120+. I'm not sure how these "spots" came to be but someone returning to the game might want to know.

As AO evolved the incentives to hunt/team changed. When I first started there were no team missions so more people hunted outdoors. When the mission terminals were activated, suddenly hot hunting spots like Oasis were empty. More recently AO created additional outdoor mob bosses with unique loot so some of those are popular again.

I have a lvl 143 fixer. If anyone can suggest an outdoor spot I can solo I would love to hear about it. Sounds fun.

-Blaik
