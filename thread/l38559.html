Not an exploit...
I dont really see this as an exploit as such.

Given the promises that you would not need to rely on combat in the game to get anywhere I don't think anyone would agree that the only way to complete a mission should be to kill everything there, unless that was the goal of the mission. Especially as they nefred Concealment so stealth isn't an option anymore.
There are some missions (Item Identification ones) that is says in the description that you are supposed to kill everyone though as you say you can just charge through, pick up the item and charge out again, usually with 4 or 5 mobs on tail. Perhaps the should fix that.

However, this is the ONLY way my Doctor can do any missions these days unless he just does easy missions, and even then he requires a big HP buff and a regeneration nano on.
It is possible to get yourself into a very sticky situation if you've got 4 or 5 mobs on you and then someone roots you, and I've died a few times because of this. Like DoubleClick says, you dont want to try this on hard level.

Still, this makes up for the fact that these days it takes me about 30mins to run to the area where the mission actually is, assuming I can even find out how to get there (Mantis Dungeon? Like thats on one of those informative maps that they've managed to develop 30,000 years into the future)
