Factoring in weapon damage
==========================

When I factor in weapon damage, the "best" nano to use changes. Note that these calculations are based upon the weapon I use and my skills and inits against mobs with 3000 armor. My weapon averages 269.36 damage / second with a weapon cycle time of 2.35 seconds. The weapon cycle time (attack + recharge) is important as your actual damage per second for your weapon follows the following formula: AvgWeaponDmg / (WeaponCycle + (WeaponCycle * ActualNanoAttackTime / NanoRechargeTime))

Note in the above forumla that ActualNanoAttackTime is adjusted for nano init and in many cases 0 (thus the formula simply becomes AvgWeaponDmg / WeaponCycle). However, in those cases where ActualNanoAttackTime is > 0, you're decreasing your overall weapon's damage per second. For example, if I'm using Resonance Blast with 5s off the NanoAttackTime my weapon's damage / second goes down to 103.6. I don't factor specials into this as they take no time and impact all nanos equally, and thus don't factor into a decision on which nano to use.

Obviously the above means that the "best" nano results will somewhat vary based upon weapon damage and cycle time, and thus you may want to redo calculations based on the weapon(s) you use.

My spreadsheet was slightly different. I calculated the damage for max, average and low.

When calculating the weapon damage you have to keep in mind recharge of nano and the "cast time", You will also get more damage with certain weapons. The cast time is also increased, which will reduce the effectiveness of your weapon. I calculated with both Ithaca and v3/reet. You will LOSE a lot damage over time from your weapons when using these nanos.

I also included the nanos cost in this sheet, which means that it won't take that long when you run out of nano and have to wait for 15 seconds (and 30 secs) to get more nano. Also considering that you need outside buffs to use them and simple MC inexpertise is enough in many occasions to make these nanos useless.

The real question I was trying to answer weather the damage from nanos was enough to compensate for lost damage. And it was able to do that when looking at the low damage. When with max or average damage, these new nanos lose out quite a bit.

After that you have to add specials from weapons to your damage output, which against increase it quite a bit. Albeit this is still viable with both options.

My conclusion was that the nanos are not enough to compensate for shots lost from your weapon in PvP. Only at low damage you are able to perform slightly better.
