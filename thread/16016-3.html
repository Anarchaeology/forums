Implanting trade-offs
To address and issue raised and a question posed by a couple of posters --- yes, it is possible to improve your charming ability at the expense of other opportunities.

As someone posted in this thread already, you can implant heavily for the 3 charm-related skills. The trade-off indeed is that you can't implant as well for Matter Creation or Time & Space. In my case I go heavy on implants for crowd control/charming, with the bit of slack left over I go a bit heavier on MatCreat for slightly earlier nukes. T&S get somethin but it's not much and at best I manage an orange pet. I have always found that to be generally adequate for the way I play and it does mean I am better at charming generally as I'm progressively further along in the charm I'm using over someone who has implated for big pet summons (fair though if that suits them I say).

That Int allows for better Int/Psy armor, better Int-based implants, better CompLit, MapNav, Psychology, Treatment and Engineering skills.
