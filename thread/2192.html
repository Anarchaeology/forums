Map feature
Wouldn't it be nice if you could make marks on the map? Right click on the location you want to remember, enter a name and it appears as a little flag on your map. When you move your mouse over it, it displays the name you entered and the coordinates.

Would make it a lot easier to find places where you want to go, but don't remember where they were...

I don't mean that as an upgrade, but rather as a GUI feature that everyone has access to.

just an idea...
