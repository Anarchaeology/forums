My Suggestion to Improve "Talk Group" Channel Selection
Like most people in the game, I alternate mainly between talking to players in the Guild Chat and Area Chat in-game channels.
To change between these two most commonly used chats I have to scroll through all the "LFG" and the "Shopping" Channels. This is a total pain in the ass, because mouse movement is so dependent on game lag.
Please put the "Guild Chat" and "Area Chat" NEXT to each other in the Friends box, so that it becomes much easier to change quickly between the two.
Hope this gets through.

I don't know howto get to the guild chat, but you can get to the area chat using the command:
/ch v

You can also change to any form of chat (besides vicinity) by clicking on the [<channel name>] part of the message.

For example, you'd see something like this in the game:
[team] JoBob_Briggs: HELP!!! I NEED THE DOC TO HEAL ME!!

and you could click on the [team] part to switch to team chat. I image it would work that way for guild chatting, as well. I know it works for shopping and indivitual messages.

'/ch t' also switches you to team chat.
