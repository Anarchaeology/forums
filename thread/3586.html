The Eye of the Nexus
Hail and Greetings all those interested in roleplaying in the world of Rubi-Ka, RK1!!!!

I, Central Eye Madren, bring greetings from the eye of the storm.

The Eye of the Nexus is a guild build on the foundation that a better Rubi-Ka can be. It has multiple sects within its core, all with the same goals in mind. What are those goals you wonder?

Priority 1: FUN and playing in a team!
Priority 2: Roleplay!
Priority 3: Helping each other!
Priority 4: Story Arch Involvement!

We are small now, but are bound to grow! we have great members, and seek to become bigger, but throughout remain close. If you wish to know more, and become a Nexan, then follow me into the Eye of the Nexus, it is there that your answers will be found! But remember we only want those that share our views of fun, roleplaying and teamwork!

Check nexus.bomb.net for more!

And always feel free to send me an email at aomadren@yahoo.com.

Feel free to contact Cyblade7, Valex, Breogan, Inquisitar, Macsen or Chainsaw if you wish to know more, and more importantly would like to join us . Take care residents of rubi-ka, and no matter what path you walk, I hope we can call each other friend, when we meet, even if it is at opposite sides of a bloody battlefield! .

Central Eye Madren
