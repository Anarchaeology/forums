Well, we docs are hard to kill by the non debuff classes; nearly impossible. One thing though; we do bad bad bad dmg too so we're good gods

Against traders, most MP's and some NT's we die a quick death; they disable our healing ability. The massive dmg dealers can sometimes give us a hard time too; esp ma and enf. Next patch enf will do even more dmg, so start to fear em

Against classes like soldiers, engineers, crats, most nts, we sincerly "own" most of the time our HoT can keep up with their dmg. I'll give ya a short list for 190+ pvp:

We "own" at lvl 190+ against:
- Soldiers (dont do enough dmg, they cant heal, TMS isnt enough)
- Engineers (like no dmg, no heals, no special tricks)
- Crats (Just like engineer with a worse pet)

We do ok (as win a lot) against:
- Fixers (chaincast EP, if it sticks dot and you win after a while)
- NT (init debuff, use mochies to counter the crown of frost)
- Agent (as long as you counter their debuffs in trader and their alphas with 100% crit)

We need to carefully fight but should win:
- Enf (they do a lot dmg so you must watch out, but they cant beat a smart doc)
- Ma (they do massive dmg and can heal a *lot* esp with HE/CC like we can, their alphas can bring us down quickly)
- Adv (just like the ma's they can heal and do a lot dmg. Advs can CH as well to make up for the less dmg ma's do)

-->
below this the hard part -->
We have *big* trouble to win against:
- MP (dominates, shutdowns, argh! Try get a lot NR, go full def and hope..)

We can't win from:
- Trader (makes your nanoskills and weaponskills halved, no more nice heals no more doing dmg, no running because of roots)



Do we "own"? Good docs win from most classes yes, if they are in perfect duelling situations. The problem of the doc is that the "ownage" we do means we're shooting at someone for 3+ minutes. We cannot root so everyone can run away if they wish.

Do you feel you won if the other can easely walk away? That's for you to decide, but it for sure doesnt make us "uber".

In mass pvp situations we don't do *any* dmg at all vs the others, you'll see you'll never get or rarely get the kill. We do heal a lot though! IMO AO is made for mass pvp, not some silly duels, but again, thats only my opinion.
