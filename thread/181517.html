Planet Wide Strike continues to hamper the transfer of vital supplies!
The view atop the Borealis Port facility may be majestic but it is also quiet and lonely. Too quiet, as this is normally a spot of important activity for the reception of the life preserving necessities of the largest of the Free Neutral Cities on Rubi-Ka. The Port regularly registers more than 50 transports a day coming in and out of the facilities bearing foodstuffs, clothing, supplies and other sundries which permits the city to survive and thrive on the harsh terraformed borderlands of Central Rubi-Ka. The reason why no transports are flying today or for the last three days rests on the shoulders of one of the few independent contractors unions on the planet. Most citizens of Rubi-Ka are unaware that in order to insure equanimity of inter-faction commerce, ICC allowed for the establishment of a local branch of the Teamsters And Truckers Union (TATU), which has successfully integrated itself on more than 300 planets to date. The Union contract is negotiated on a yearly basis and has been a rubber stamp affair taking place at the ICC headquarters involving delegates from the Free Neutral Cities, Major Clans, and Omni-Tek. This year however it appears that talks began to breakdown sometime at the end of last month and have slowly degenerated since.

Earlier this week the Union announced a boycott after two days of heated debate mostly centering on Tir representatives insisting that their percentage paid to the union be reduced by more than 7% due to the drop in economic activity, since the takeover by the Sentinels Clan, Tir has seen a dramatic decrease in its trade revenue. No official from Tir was able or willing to speak to me regarding their stance on this issue but I did receive a strong warning(at a distance mind you) from Commander Wickham The Butcher of the South Gate as many Neutrals call him and official Sentinel Ground Commander of Tir, to be very careful in my presentation of the facts. The few locals whom I was able to speak with, off the record, expressed dismay and concern over the events and are hoping for a speedy resolution.

Omni-Tek and Neutral City representatives replied that no provisions for a ratio based payment plan were in place and that the fees paid to the union have traditionally been divided equally with local maintenance costs absorbed by individual factions thereby providing for a total distribution of the costs of commerce based on fairness and volume. The local council in Borealis, seen coming out of the Union Guild Hall yesterday led by Councilman Yuri Svatspopov, who assured me that he had all faith that the negotiators would soon have the issue resolved. But, he added quickly, it should be noted for the record that when Tir was the economic powerhouse of the North and it was the Neutral cities who were paying a larger share per volume, there was never any mention of this kind of radical contract negotiation with the Union.

Sentinel presence notwithstanding all non-Clan parties at the table agreed that the format should remain unchanged. This led to accusations of Omni/Neutral collusion by Clan representatives, mostly aimed a Newland City and began to form the spearhead of a call for a reworking of the existing contract. Omni-Tek has taken these accusations very seriously. I was able to get a brief interview with Justine Claireveaux, the council for Omni-Trans, and this is what she had to say.

The statements made by the Sentinels and some of the other Clan members at the meeting are not only specious and slanderous, but they have become typical of the mindless rhetoric issuing from Tirs new caretakers. There is not now nor has there ever been any evidence showing illegal ties between the Omni-Tek corporation and the ruling body in Newland or any other Neutral enclave for that matter. This is clearly a stunt to try and prop up a failing economy in Tir and to shunt the majority of commercial costs onto the shoulders of more capable and intelligent partners. If Tir cannot stand on its own then it should consider taking out loans or better yet, Omni-Tek would be willing to begin leveraging outlying and unused real estate or mining contracts in the vicinity of Tir as a help.

Just minutes after the meeting ended the Planetside Shop Steward of TATU, Marci Comstock, along with her legal council left the meeting and ordered all union employees to stop working. She was unavailable for an interview but the Steward of Local 555 in Omni Trade, one Federico Shenkel, was willing to give us a few minutes.

The Union has been a great friend to Rubi-Ka ever since we arrived and we work hard everyday. If the main people cant agree to our standard contract then the work wont get done. Plain and simple. When I asked about why the union would go planet wide when the issue seemed to rest with the Clans, his reply was short and to the point. We negotiate with the factions here as a whole and so we strike against everyone the same. Let these guys get their acts together and stop playing the Union like a fool.

The doors of the ICC offices in Andromeda were closed and when we attempted to speak with someone there we were brusquely ushered away. It is worthy of mention that presently it appears that ICC headquarters is experiencing some sort of spatial disparity as movement there is hampered severely. Word on the street is that there is some sort of technical glitch in the environmental control stabilizers and parts cannot be transported from the Morning Star facility during the strike. Later while preparing to leave I was met by Carlos Castandeda of the ICC diplomatic corps.

He informed me that although ICC was not making any official statements regarding the strike or related matters he did wish me to know that progress was being made on the matter and he hoped I would not turn the event into a media circus. I can tell you with complete confidence that I believe we will see resolution on this issue within a few days.

As anyone walking the streets of Tir can attest no transports have been seen there or any major Clan city in days. Newland, Borealis and Harrys are no exceptions and even mighty Omni Trade is not immune as images from their Landing Grids show empty platforms. So, with commerce hampered to such an extent why is it that this is the first most people have heard of the situation? You can thank your local governments and ICC for that. The cities governments have been able to ration and scramble effectively so far to meet the needs of their immediate communities. How long that will last is anyones guess and the shortages are already being felt in the outposts which they serve but cannot supply. The ICC for its part has kept this affair quiet with requests to the various News agencies to treat it carefully due to the explosive nature of the situation and the potential for panic. But the time has come for the people to hear what is going on and so as the crisis mounts we of good conscience must speak. As Shakespeare said so many thousands of years ago, The truth will out. Keep your eyes here for more updates on this as they are made available.
