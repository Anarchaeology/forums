Reply to Dionysus
quote:
Originally posted by Dionysus
Sorry, I don't like PvP myself (I play an Adventurer), but Zeroshift is right. PvP zones are only a very small part of the world, and if you choose to travel through them, you have to accept the risk that this brings with it.

Yalmahas are NOT "get out of PvP free" tickets, and they shouldn't be... [snip]
First off, it's not realistic to just avoid PvP areas altogether. At some levels, you really need to be in certain hunting grounds or mission areas and there's just no good way to get there without crossing a PvP area. Also, sometimes you're doing a mission to get a certain reward, and you can't get to that particular mission without crossing a PvP area.

I don't object to being attacked in a vehicle. I agree there should be a risk of that. I also think one of FC's few appropriate nerfs was to make it impossible to equip a vehicle when you're already in a fight, so you can't use it to run away from a fight in progress. If I'm in a vehicle and you're fast enough to keep up with me and hit hard enough bring me down before I get away, so be it.

What I am saying is that I don't think it's appropriate, from either a common sense standpoint or a balanced game mechanics standpoint, for you to be able to root a vehicle. You can't fight back when you're in a vehicle, and it's a long wait to get out of it.

Further, I want to point out that the spirit of PvP is about testing your skills against another player, with roughly-equal level characters. FC understands that--it's why there are level limits to whom you can PvP, and it's also a big part of the reason for the upcoming OE patch.

The jerks camping the mission paths are not interested in a fair fight. They want to bully people who are lower level, less equipped, and/or unprepared for a fight so they can wear a title they don't really deserve.

You can come up with all the shoddy justifications you want for this behavior, it doesn't change the core fact. There is no risk to the attacker in destroying a person in a vehicle. Making it extremely difficult to do so by nerfing the ability to root a vehicle would be a step in the right direction.

Perhaps an even better step would be to not provide any reward for PKing a person while they have a vehicle equipped. This would largely eliminate the problem at its source.
