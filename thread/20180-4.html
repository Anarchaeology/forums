This is an interesing discussion, and one in which you really wish you could sit and talk with Funcom directly about it. I come at this discussion with my main not being either a doc or a trader, but an adventurer at level 149. I've played a doc up to level 43, and have never rolled a trader. From my standpoint, having a doc in a team is always a joy-as that means I can drop my aggro bar down to 50% and not have to be the main healer in the group. It is always nice when your health is below 1500, and you are worried that you may be critted any moment now by a mob and see those 1000, 1200 heals coming your way. So, having a doc on a team is always a good thing from my standpoint.

But, watching a high level trader do their thing is also rather impressive. Watching them divest, seeing the green cloud go up, followed by the heals all the while tearing up the mob is pretty cool. I like having traders on my team as well, and I can't say I'd like to see them nerfed.

But, if a team needs a healer, a doc beats a trader any day IMO. But, having a trader along is always useful, just like having a MA or an ADV along in a team, providing back up heals. At high levels it is tough sometimes to find a doc, and while the standard MA/ADV/Trader is along things are managable, usually the MA, trader and ADV would be more than happy to have a doc around to handle the healing.

Now, from an adventurer standpoint, the sad thing isn't DOC vs trader, but as coming from a profession that is "the second best healer" we find that from a group standpoint, having a trader is better, both from a damage dealing aspect and as a healer. I don't think trader heals should be downgraded in any way, as nerfing isn't the answer, but I think docs should have a higher level team heal as should adventurers, so at the higher levels heals are at least comparable to what traders can do at level 120.

My 10 cents worth....

quote:
About the nano, every nanomage has the same amount of nano, its not class-based.
That is incorrect. A Solitus Soldier, with exactly the same amount of 'Nano Pool' skill as a Solitus Doctor, and of the same level, will have far more hitpoints, and far less nano than the Doctor. The amount of nano and hitpoints you get per point of Body Dev and Nano Pool is dependent on Breed. The amount of nano and hitpoints you get per level (it goes up even if you don't increase the associated skills) is dependent on Profession. Falcon made a good table of the base values for breeds and professions


I can't mimic the code he used to post the table, but it's near the bottom of this thread.

Profession actually has as great an impact, if not more so, than Breed
