My favorite equipment for 1 on 1 duels as a GA fixer? The "exit" button.

Ok, I couldn't resist.


The following is all based on high level PvP while wearing GAIV:

Soldier: God help you if they're running HoTs or HE. If so, you are most likely not going to win. However, if they are not, you have a chance. You MUST use the shoot, root and scoot tactic to beat soldiers anymore. Personally I preferred to go into battle against a soldier using a Manex and on nearly full DEF. Absorb their first specials as best you can, then go more AGG. They key is, you must do as much damage as you can against them before their fast burst recycle knocks you out. Do damage until you're very low on health, root them, back off and let your HoTs work. High QL stims and kits are necessary to help you as well. Once you're back up, go back, burst, geta few shots in, root, etc. etc. It helps if you have an AS gun in your bag to assist, but it can be done with the Manex (which incidentally willalso do some damage through their TMS, which is important). Be prepared to fight long and hard to beat them, but you will slowly use up their Nano and once they can no longer use TMS, you will win.

Trader: Remember the exit button? LoL. Ya, you can expect to lose against traders in GA most of the time. However, you CAN win against them sometimes if you get lucky. You must also catch them before they use that crap ass "predrain" technique. If you can do that, the key is a fast, full out assault on them using the Manex. Some folks say that they like to go defensive so that they resist drains. Ok, sure, but you'lldo a lot less damage and you'll give the trader time ot land those drains and plunders...and they WILL eventually land on you. SO go in fast and hard at full AGG and blast them with the Manex. Sometimes you get lucky and they freak out about the damage they are taking and panic. Press them hard and just keep blasting. If you get low on health and still have enough nano skills to root them once you get drained....do so and back off to heal up, then go in charging again. Even OE'd a manex burst will still do great damage.

Doc: I don't know what to tell you here. I've never been able to beat a like level doc. Between their init debuffs and their DoTs, if you stay around, you are gonna regret it. Again, if you try to go defensive, you will resist dots and debuffs for a bit, but they WILL eventually land. You will also do measely damage while they heal up and watch you wither away under the DoTs and most likely some nice Caterwaul/X3/Manex specials.

MA: Wow...poor MA. Ok, beating an MA is pretty hard (snicker). First, take off all your armor. Then remove all your buffs. Then root them, shoot them, root them, shoot them. For variety you can snare them, shoot them, snare them, shoot them. Seriously, unless the MA is using a bow or ranged weapon, they're about the only class we're guaranteed to kill. If they're using a ranged weapon, you should still be able to beat them, but it will be a bit closer of a fight. Use whatever weapon you like.

Adventurer: Melee advent? See MA above. Ranged advent? Well, they can be pretty nasty if done right. Go full AGG to keep the damage high and counter their healing capabilities. Wear them down till their nano is gone and close in for the kill.

NT: You're going to die. Sorry, no kidding this time. You're toast. Grid out, come back to the boards, and tell FC they blow. Works for me.

'Crat: Rule of One accompanied by a Caterwaul or x-3 and they can mean BIG trouble for you. You're not goingto resist their nuke much, so go full AGG and try to smack the tar out of them, rooting and healing as you go if possible.


Enforcer: High level enforcers are the bane of many classes existances anymore. They are going to hurt you and they are very very very hard to beat, but again you have a chance. Use the Manex to get through their damage shields. Do NOT try to root or snare a high level Enforcer. You'll waste nano as they'll just break it damn near immediately. Go defensive and run like a little girl, turn and burst them.....run like a girl again.....turn and shoot....run.....burst.....run....etc. If you have another gun to swap for specials, USE IT. You need their nano to be gone before you have a chance. Once their nano is gone, they might as well be an MA, but that doesn't happen often.

Agent: I do not have any experience with Agents since the change in special recharge. I have a feeling they are hard as hell now with AS recharging as fast as I've heard it does. Previously, I'd go defensive to try to reduce AS damage, then go at them with an MCS. I beat them 75% ofthe time before the change. I would think that would have to drop to 30 or 40% now though. I dunno. ADDITION - if the agent goes FP trader, don't be afraid. Yes, they'll still most likely drain you, but it will be harder than fora regular trader. Remember, while they're castingthose damn drains, they're not shooting. Use that to your advantage by raising AGG a bit and dishing out more damage.


Engineer: Again, I have no experience against them one on one since the changes with their bot buffing, but I would think a fixer should still be able to beat them. Treat the bot as an enforcer that MIGHT be ableto be rooted or slowed and focus your attentions on the engineer for the kill. Keep running from the bot as needed and use the MCS to burst the Engineer every time it recycles as you keep running from the bot ifyou can't root it.


MP: Use an MCS and go defensive to keep from getting NSD on you very easily. Area slow the MP and his horde IMMEDIATELY. If they get NSD off on you before you slow the demon, you're gonna regret it. Once they're hopefully slowed (and if you can root the demon even better) KILL THE HEAL PET FIRST. With AS on those rifles most use recycling so fast, its hard to keep your HP up, so just back off and heal if needed. The root or slow should still stick on the demon. Once the heal pet is gone, the MP should fall quickly. Just keep the demon at bay, kill the healer and all is well.


So there you have it IMHO. In all of these situations, there are fundamentals that recurr throughout.

1. Weaopns with fast special recharges are your doom. Extra caution is needed if you are even goingto have the slightest chance against them. (Div9, Caterwaul, Hellfury, MCS)

2. Avoid damage pets by attempting to subdue them and attack their masters.

3. Anyone that can debuff you, you must go after quickly since even in defensive, you can count on the debuffs eventually landing. (Other than MP as I described before)

4. Root and back off to heal whenever needed and possible. Every other class for the most part is going to do more damage than you. Key in on your ability to heal.

5. NEVER try to press a bad situation. If it looks like you only need a shot or two to win, but a shot or two will kill you, count on dying. A fixer needs to play by His or Her rules to win. Wait till the odds are in your favor. Caution is the better part of valor.
