Assembly is recruiting
From: Assembly recruitment office
To: Public channels (all)
Subject: Joining Assembly


Greetings free clansmen and -women

Do you spend hours and hours in a workshop or a medical lab? Are you all about muscles and protection? How about regulating, bureaucratic and diplomatic work? Are you skilled in nano-technology, meta-physicism or coding gridstreams and network connections? Do you frolic in the wilderness, or perhaps more in the shadows and covert operations?

Assembly is looking for more members to cover positions in these categories. You must be able to commit to the following:
  • Services and skills available for client contracting
  • Regularly available
  • Call-to-arms against hostile troops and actions
In return, Assembly provides the following:
  • Free healthcare
  • Paid vacation
  • Free tradeskill services
  • Bonus payments
  • Equipment depot (full members)

Assembly is open for all clan members above security level 20. Presently, there is a special need for the following:

1. Doctor - our medical staff needs more practicians. Both battletrained and laboratory M.D. are needed.
2. Bureaucrat - more specifically, we need a person well conversed in laws and financial contract legislation. Courtroom experience is a plus.
3. Trader - our economy office is very interested in signing up a trader. You must be willing to spend much time working for the organization.
4. Agent - Special operations. Details presented only in private conversations.

More information is available on our public gridfeed and also on our public gridforums.

If you think you have what it takes, please don't hesitate to fill out the application form on the public gridfeed, or contact either Tussa, Lurai or Lillemjau for a talk.


[Assembly is a clan roleplay guild on Atlantean (RK-1). People are expected to stay in-character both in guildchannel and in vicinity. If you can't live without towers, raids and uniques, then we are not the people you're looking for.]
