It certainly would be a good idea to have a /target command of some sort which could be put on a macro which automatically targetted your pets.

Pet professions could also use a Summon command, or nano, which brought their pets to them, to make up for the crappy pet pathing. I would suggest /Pet Summon. It could, theoretically, not work while in combat, to prevent any possible exploits. Not sure if this last part is necessary or not.

Of course, pet professions are now probably far back on the list of professions which need work, so don't hold your breath waiting for these improvements.
