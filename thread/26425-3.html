More answer
Blackmane was able to supply me with more answers to your question.. Hence as follows.



TMS - I commented on why TMS is overridden by other deflection nanos in the other
reply. There is no built-in deaggro in the TMS either. There is no such thing as 'nano'
damage - a nano nuke inflicting fire damage is treated the same as a gun inflicting fire
damage.

LLTS - the optical effect is not going to be made switchable. In fact, it will likely be made
into something that cannot be turned off using the current system. The new LLTS do not
have a visual effect on them - use these new versions if you don't want the night vision
effect.

Soldier nanos:
Soldiers will never get a critical buff. No one else will get a critical buff.
Most likely the soldier will get some other weapon skill buffs along the lines mentioned.
The taunt nanos are going to be looked into in terms of their effectiveness.
AC - in terms of AC buffing, soldiers are the third/fourth rank. You are not meant to be as
good as engineers, and won't be made to be as good as engineers. The soldier AC buffs
are the buffs the soldier can fall back on if they don't get an engineer AC buff.
Phalanx has been changed in 14.4
