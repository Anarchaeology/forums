Turn the Page
I've been roleplaying in AO for a year an a half and an avid reader of the RP Forum. It's always been an important source of information and, ultimately, a fun place to hang out. Over the last couple of months though it's all gone horribly wrong. There been so much bitterness and bad blood on these forums of late, I am ashamed of being part of this community.

I have no interest in going into the specifics of various incidents. I suspect there is nothing really new about them. People are people, personality clashes and other disagreements will occur in the roleplaying community as anywhere else.

What is new is the current fashion of airing these private squabbles in public. It's been aptly compared, I forget by whom, to an episode of the Jerry Springer show and it just makes us all look like morons, as if roleplayer did not have enough credibility problems as it is.

I have no solutions to offer, nor do I speak from any position of authority to demand anything, but I would like to encourage people to deal with their private issues privately. Truth be known I don't really think most of us are really all that interested in the gory details.
