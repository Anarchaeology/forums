New Scoop On Bombs In Tir!
January 21, 29476

Clanner Claims to Disarm Tir Bomb!
Reporter: Myles Anam Pippin


Fair Trade/Woompah Complex, Tir
(Note: This article is late news, yet it contains pertinent information)

I was about to step into the woompah in Newland to make may way through to Stret West Bank when I heard cries that there were bombs in Tir! All the clan channels were buzzing with the excitement of such rumour.

I made a change of plan and decided to head into the Tir portal. I encountered congested traffic as many were racing to and from Tir. Needless to say the woompahs were at full load. After getting to Tir and running to the Council of Truth building, where it was said the bombs were, I heard Edgerunner claim across the channels that he had disarmed the bomb! Quickly I shot out a personal comm and arranged to meet him back at Fair Trade. The following is what I recorded in that quick interview, with his Coalition leader, Karrd, standing near. You can see all the excitement with all of the background conversations as well:
