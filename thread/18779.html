The New 20k
Hello Everyone... My names Japella... As some of may remember, Newland a long time ago, looked alot different then it does today... It was at one time almost impossible to get thru the west gate in to the city due to the Bank, Reclaim, and the Insureance Terminals being located to close together there... Everyone tends to mass around these items and it coses insane lagg... so The Newland City Council made planns and moved the Reclaim & Bank Terminals away from the West Gate up near the Newland Prision...making everyones visit to Newland a happy lagg free visit...

I work for Bateman & Fletcher, LLC... And i am a consultant... We have been asked by the Free Council of 20k to save their City... I meet with the Staff of the Mayors office and with a few other city officals... they told me of alot of issues they would like to 'fix' with their City... Most important to them was that they too have the same problem that Newland had... all the cities hot spots like the Bank, Missions Terminals, and there Wham-pah were all to close together cosing alot of unhappy visitors to thier City... this is not only affecting everyone who comes into the city but it affects the citys tourism also (some people refuse to even go there due to lag)... along with that their second main concern was for the well being of their citizens who live there... 20k went to the top of the Most polluted Cities list when Tir's Waterways were cleaned up... 20k has a huge Toxic dump 'In' the city thats been the cos of several diseases & infections over the years... the council needs this dump cleaned up ASAP...

After meeting with some of my people i came up with these planns on future changes for 20k... i went before the 20k Council with these plans today... take a look and post yer thoughts...

Ever hear the expression 'two birds with one stone'?... First step in my plann is to clean up the nasty pool of Toxic waste in the South East corner of the city and relocate the whampah there...
http://home.columbus.rr.com/loveugly...20kWhampah.jpg

Second is to move the bank and possibly the mission Terminals to the North East part of the City...
http://home.columbus.rr.com/loveugly...New20kBank.jpg

Its now up to the 20k council to make the next move...
