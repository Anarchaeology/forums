Just a thought?
I feel that we are all under the misconception that we are in control...or even have a say.

Omni, clan or neutral, the category does not matter, we are only individuals, and as the saying goes 'A person is smart, but people are stupid'.

If the clans take a course that you don't agree with, what can you do about it?

This isn't being critical but a genuine enquiry.

Is it easy to change the way a large number of people think when the momentum of action has already taken hold??

I was amazed that the amnesty lasted as long as it did.

I am even more startled that a single action/conflict (by either side) has not raced out of control.

For instance, I see the guards of Newland shot down outside the gates everyday. I do not know whether to feel anger or sorrow at this.

Anger, that both Omni and clan members feel that Newland is just another hunting ground.

Sorrow, that we who live in 'no mans land' seem to have no value in the eyes of our fellow Rubikans.

Beyond my personal feelings, how easy would it be for either side to view these actions as a possible move towards an attack on them?

Omni Tek could see it as a preclude to the clans strengthing their position by taking Newland, it does have a significant whompa junction.

The clans could view it as Omni preparing to take Newland for use as a staging area for attacks into their cities, once again the whompas (Tir and Athens).

The Good, the Bad and the Ugly.
Fellow Citizens:

I'm affiliated with the clans perhaps more out of fate than free-choice. My parents left me in the care of a clan martial artist. When I came of age, knowing only martial arts, I ended up doing mercenary work for the Council of Truth, believing I was on the side of goodness. These days I'm not always so sure I'm fighting on the side of the good against evil, rather than, on the side of the ugly that would destroy the good with the evil.

Burrowowl is right to point out:

"Omni-tek terraformed Rubi-ka. In a way, our homes are all made by Omni-tek, whether those homes are apartments in Omni-1, a shack outside of Last Ditch, or in a backyard in Tir. Rubi-ka is habitable due to an investment of effort and capital and technology by Omni-tek."

Yet, if we build a house of our of our own sweat and blood, do we not have some ownership right to the house? And, what about the plot of rubika that the house sits on? Is it still Omni's to take, whenever it may serve their bottom line?

I respect Omni-Tek's right to mine and manufacture on Rubi-Ka and, appreciate their terraforming and, technology and products. But, I'm convinced there will be no lasting piece as long as we are ruled by corporations who exist to profit, rather than, to establish a Rule of Law, to justly settle questions of ownership and rights.

So, I remain committed to the clans insofar as they resist government by corporations (Omni-Tek or otherwise). I fear, however, that in our zeal, we'll try to destroy Omni-Tek altogether and, the vacuum left may well be filled with a worse despotism then we can imagine.

I also respect the neutrals for having the courage to live according to their convictions. I suspect it will ultimately be the neutrals that bring a lasting peace to Rubi-Ka.

-Zarquan
President of the League of Liberty
(whose membership was 2 at last count).
