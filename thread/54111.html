Dearest Funcom
I browse these forums and i feel so bad for you all. I have yet to read a single column here stating what a great job you all did on AO. It's a wonderful game, if it wasn't we wouldn't bother playing it. I for one am excited about the booster pack. And would like to thank you for offering a major addition to the game at such a nominal price. I'm sure It'll add new and interesting elements to the game-play and you won't here me ranting and raving on this forum about minute details that may go against my individual desire for the game. I realize that I am one player, and cannot speak for everyone here, but rather than utilize this space to endlessly whine and moan about how unfair you are for actually wanting to charge me for months of your hard labor, I'd like to thank you.

I play up to three hours a night.
I love the game.

Thank you.

Conquistador Coffee would like to offer all members of your staff a complimentary cup of our fine coffee.
