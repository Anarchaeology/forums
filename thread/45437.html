As an Adventurer, not only will you have a green swimming skill, but your parrot forms will be able to buff your swimming skill!

Yes, you could have a Swimming skill of over 2000

The following nanos buff swimming:-

Swim Proficiency - +10, requires PM/SI 31, General nano

Swim Expertise - +20, requires PM/SI 61, General nano

Sparrow Flight - +190, requires BM 133, MM/SI 112, Adv nano

Flurry of Feathers - +400, requires BM 440, MM/SI 399, Adv nano

Calia's Form: Parrot - +1300, requires BM 715, MM/SI 644, Adv nano
