Bronto Hunting Tournament
A Team Bronto Hunting Tournament open to CLANNERS ONLY, will be held just outside of aegean and wartorn valley. In order to join the tournament, you must A: Be at least lvl 15 and your level can be no greater than 25. B: You must have a team of at least three people, C: supply a 200 credit per PERSON entry fee, and D: be at the Athen whompas (443.9 x 294.7 Old Athen) on september 28, 2002, from 4:00 EST to 4:30 EST. Sign-Up will close at 4:30 EST, and you will be led to the hunting grounds of the tournament. A recruitment center for the Clan Intelligence Agency will be at the grounds. the event ends at 5:15 EST, which will be followed by the award ceremonies, which will end at 5:30 EST. Prizes will be awarded to the top three teams. Prizes are:

First Place: 15,000 credits

Second Place: 10,000 credits

Third Place: 1,500 credits per team member

Remember, your team can keep any loot they find on the brontos.
If you level up to a level over 25, you will be kept in the tournament. If someone dies, they are disqualified from the tournament, and cannot help their teammates for the rest of the tournament, though they will still get a prize if their team finishes in the top three. All entrants must use the designated insurance terminal before the tournament starts. Only one team of a maximum of 6 people can join the tournament. If any two or more teams are found combining their points, or joining eachother during the event, they will be disqualified. Any teams that are not present at the start of the event will be disqualified. A tell will be sent to each team leader 5 minutes before the start of the event.
The event will be held in Rubi-Ka 2.
This event brought to you by:

The CIA: Clan Intellegence Agency
