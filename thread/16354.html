Sea of Change - RP - Rubi-Ka 2 Looking for Dedicated Members
A circle has formed, an enlightened group driven by change. Change in one's self, change in society, change in politics. The cloaks have awakened, walking the shadowy streets of Rubi-Ka, seeking those who are like minded, who want rebellion. The system has broken us, therefore we must fight back, we must break new ground, forge a new path.

The many who came before us built foundations for us to further build upon, they cleared paths through the mundain lives of others, which provides us with a base to start with. We will build on ancient priciples, we will bring them to Rubi-Ka, we will change the repeditive nature of the world, we will clear our own paths. We must go further, to new heights to make a difference on the planet, and make our ideas the foundation for others.


[We are looking for dedicated roleplayers to help us bring roleplaying to the forefront of Rubi-Ka life, to change the view and make it prominent. We want active members who will be available for events when possible, and who have a desire to make a difference with their roleplaying.

We are not looking for powergamers, overequippers, or people who want to get free items, so please if this is your style of play, pass us by.

If you are interested in learning more, please contact "Sepentura" on Rubi-Ka 2, or email serrephim@hotmail.com ]
