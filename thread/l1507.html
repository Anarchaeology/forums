bump for consideration

There has been a virtual eruption over this issue. The poll has more replies than any other poll to date. It also ranks 6th amongst thread views. This shows that this topic is of huge concern to everyone pro or con. I can see the point of both sides, but as bcbdla has shown/told us, the most effective way to make our voices heard is to offer up some direction as to how WE feel this should be handled. In that effort I am trying to revise the poll. Here's what I've come up with so far. Any revisions/input are welcome and hopefully we can come to an agreement on what our final poll should look like. Funcom needs our input to design what we, the fans, want in this naming system.

Poll: How should the naming system be handled?
1. Make First and Last random, choose middle.
2. Make First and Middle up to choice, assign last.
3. Make First and Last up to choice, no middle.
4. Make First and Last random, choose middle. Option to change later on First and Last names.
5. Make First and Last random, choose middle. Options to delete or change entire name at some point.
6. Only have First and Last, no middle.
7. Only have a nickname, Middle.

Remember, we're looking for solutions. How should we guide Funcom toward what we want? Post your opinions/remarks please.
