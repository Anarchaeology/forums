My AO dream (no.. actually it's a nightmare... a long one)
I'm writing this rather long post to describe a vivid nightmare I just had last night. I hope you at least find it entertaining and maybe you'll share the nightmares that you've had.



Well, I went out on a mission near the city of Tir. I met up with 5 clanners (they understood that while I'm an Omni-Tek Employee, I'm only against the clan called Sentinel)

We went into a cave as our mission area thinking it was going to be the same as usual. Then I started to realize that the stairway we were descending into was kinda steep... so steep that some of us took falling damage while running...

Well, we finally got down the stairway and admired our surroundings... lots of wires, pipes, and stuff on the walls. The path ahead of us curved to the left. At the end of the path was another stairwell, this time not so steep. We went up the stairwell, which spiralled up for some distance until we found ourselves in a large hall. Several rooms later we found a giant dining room... The room was empty, save for the throne at the very end.

Sitting in the throne was a regal looking old guy in a red robe, with white fur around the collar. As we gazed he got up and sneered...

"Hello! I am the Robe King, and welcome to your doom!!!"

He then waved his hand and sneered...

"Your supplies are now contaminated. You can no longer resist the embrace of death!"

Our agent was the first to attack him. She let loose with her rifle, striking him dead on.... but I saw the message in my window...

"Celfina has taken 333 points of projectile damage"

"What the heck? She just took her own damage!"

After a few shots by our party we retreated; we were taking damage from our own nanos, weapons and damage shields. Quickly our 'crat and MP summoned pets to deal with the Robe King; we knew he would come after us soon. Sure enough he appeared, and the crat and MP sent their pets after him.

Soon we lost our 'crat and MP; the King waved his hand, and their pets turned against them, murdering them. Our remaining party ran out several rooms ahead of him, trying to find the stairwell we came out of. We took a rest, and our Agent used her medkit to recover her hitpoints... only to die of poison damage! Owch!

Our fixer had to rest just a little bit to regain enough nano to activate his grid program, but the Robe King caught up to us. The Enforcer and MA tried to fight him off, but they were causing themselves damage by hitting him. Finally our Fixer gridded us out of there, but a little too late for the melee types; they died just as we gridded out.

You'd think it was over now, but it isn't. All the exits were marked "The Robe King"... 'cept for the emergency exit. The Fixer suggested that we wait in the grid and that we should be safe. However, the Robe King showed up in the grid and started attacking him. We both ran for the emergency exit. I managed to make it, but the Fixer didn't; he was killed just as he touched it.

I found myself back in the throne room of the Robe King, but this time there were other people in here. Many of them were wondering how they got in here from their mission areas, others were trying to figure out how the Cell Scanners and Apartments transported them here. Just then the Robe King showed up and the slaughter began.

The GMs sent out a system message saying that the RUBI KA 1 server has been compromised by a virus; anyone slain by the Robe King ends up being permanently deleted. Those who were not in the Robe King's room were advised to logout immedietaly.

People tried to logout, others tried to fight the Robe King only to meet death. The ones who logged out ended up going LD; even past the 30 seconds the characters didn't logout, they just stood there as the Robe King killed them.

I ran to the back of the crowd to get away from the King, watching as more people appeared; they were all LD. Apparently anyone anywhere on Rubi Ka who logged out was transported here....
An Ark tried to intervene, but was killed as well by the Robe King.

Soon I was the only one left, and I was attacked. I realized that with my death no one would be left on Rubi Ka 1 other than those who had not logged in. All the work I put in this character was about to go to waste...

Then, right before that final hit took my HP down to 0... I woke up.
