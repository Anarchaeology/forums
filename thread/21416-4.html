Phear Leet
If they're going to do a full IP reset, they should let people do it two or three times, as per the original 90 point plan.

Why? So people don't screw up their characters permanently. the points (within a short timeframe) would give people a chance to make mistakes and correct those mistakes, and that's important whenever introducing new changes like this.

Also, yes, it's VERY important that IP be kept tabulated correctly. Really, there's no excuse for Funcom not having an IP checksum that runs on characters to see if they have the right IP for their level.

AS to the . . "oh nooos cookay cutter" bit. .

Allowing IP reallocation, even casual IP reallocation, will encourage experimentation and new templates. If mistakes cannot be corrected, no one will ever experiment -- the risk is too high, they'll stick to the pre tested cookie cutters. With the ability to undo mistakes, the risk of experimentation won't be so high.

They should let people experiment as much as they want during the few weeks of the IP reset. Frankly, 90 points might be too few. What's the danger? People might have too much fun?
