Upgrading implants
Here is a thought I had last night while trying to get parts for my ql200 implants together.

I have worn ql125 implants since lvl 100. I am lvl 132 now. The reason? Quite simply put.


I HATE BUILDING THEM!!!!!!!!

I dont really know anyone who enjoys building implants. Its VERY tedious. You need to keep track of 50 gazllion parts. make sure cluster A goes with Implant B ( and how would a low end noob know this stuff? there is NO documentation regarding implants )


SO, my idea you ask?

Let us UPGRADE the implants we ALREADY HAVE IN. In order to do so, you would have to de-equip all your imps. give them to say, a high lvl nano programmer ( as not to remove the need for them in the game ) and let him make you ql50's into ql70's, or 90's. And yes, let them go OVER the 125 mark. If you have the np, then upgrade them to 200's. There are of course some flaws to this... building QL1 imps and then having them upgraded into ql200. You would have to put some kind of cap on them. ( these implants are to low to upgrade to selected lvl ) or then something like, you can only upgrade a pair of imps once or maybe twice. It would save the low lvls ALOT of money. And it would save HOURS of time and aggrevation. Simply put, I will wear ql125's until I can put in ALL my ql200's. because I HATE DESPISE HATE AND DESPISE, building implants. My lvl 60 MP still has ql20 implants in. Lame eh? Not because I cant afford them, or find the parts. I just HATE doing it. Most people I know feel this way. I know what I outlined isnt perfect, and needs some refinement, but its at least an explorable idea.
