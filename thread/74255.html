question about crit%chances (Utter Concentration)
Utter Concentration
quote:
modify self chemical damage modifier80
modify self cold damage modifier80
modify self critical chance200
modify self energy damage modifier80
modify self fire damage modifier80
modify self melee damage modifier80
modify self poison damage modifier80
modify self projectile damage modifier80
modify self radiation damage modifier80
critical chance200? How..why...what for? With 100%chance of crit, you should theoretically crit every hit. 100% is everything. 100% of a glass of water IS the whole glass of water. 200% of a glass of water is two glasses of water.

Why would anyone need 200% critchance when 100% makes you garanteed crits? Unless there are things out there that reduce critchances. Is there? Is def-modifier calculated in here? Does this means that an agent running a 100% crit nano might experience not critting against......GAfixers?
