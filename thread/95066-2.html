(OOC)

The biggest problem is probably the fact that things have cooled-down on Rubi-Ka to the point where there is no REASON for a conflict any more.

Clans basically got what they were originally fighting for (freedom and mining rights), so why exactly is there a reason to fight any more? OT can't make agressions on the Clans officially, because the ICC has "stabilized" the situation. And Clanners really have no particular reason for NEEDING to attack OT any more. (Not to say folks don't just attack each other for the heck of it and because of habit. lol)

Neutrals don't want to cause a rukus, so nothing from them either...

Basically, the "conflict" between OT and Clan is dead right now. There's no point to it.

Now, there is "conflict" from Notum Wars, but that's very distinct from OT vs. Clan. And it's certainly not "deep" enough to warrant much RP based on.

The Clans have it almost as cushy as OT now, which really inbalances the ideal "good guy vs. bad guy" story, simply because OT is not being the bad guys--partially because they're not allowed to, and partially because they don't want to.

Not to mention, the whole idea of warring in a world where hardly anyone ever dies from fighting is rather silly.

Simply put, the "conflict" has no teeth right now. And FC trying to drum it up with this whole Neutral vs. Clan thing is horribly misplaced and silly. FC should be giving the Clans and OT more reasons to hate each other, not making it so generally peaceful Neutrals are a primary target due to boredom. lol

-Jayde

My problem is:

a.) I'm not a 'role player', however I definately 'play' my characters. They're all different.

b.) I'm a real nice guy, imho.

c.) I can string a few words together to explain my thoughts.

Stuck between a rock and a hard place.

Look at it this way. Me, being Mr. Niceguy gets saddled with a bunch of great people in an organization role playing that we are mercenaries. So, now the game is obviously getting stale and fighting my basest instincts I have to fully and thoroughly support hiring these thugs to add some ****e to the game. Then I gotta back it up in the forums to ease my conscience.

I find myself in these forums on occasion without ever having roleplayed in the game. Well, there was this red headed Solitus in Reet's one time ....

Anyway, so we got basically our whole organization playing the 'extremist Neutrals' when it's not in our nature. It really makes it difficult when most of yer org's Advisors are adults and sometimes parents are are genuinly a bunch of great people. Then to get accosted repeatedly in-game both by FunCom and other players forcing you to act in-character with your character but out-of-character with your character is unsettling.

Point being, I think as touched on above, most people play characters that are at least somewhat tangentially connected to their own makeup. We have bad guys to talk about. But, they often really are bad guys.

This game was oh so much more even before the Story began in October of 2001. We had bad guys to talk about. The baser elements of society hadn't moved into the game. We all discussed the good guys and bad guys of the story in this forum. It was refreshing. Similar to how we are surmising whether or not The Immortal One is Roman Dragov or Vanya.
