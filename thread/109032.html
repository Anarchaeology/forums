Say hello to VIKING KITTIES!!!

Are you looking for a good time? Are you artistic? Can you stand your ground and deal out a tongue lashing the likes of which none will wantingly oppose. Then you might just want to take a look at this org. We are no nonesense cut to the chase type of people here my friend. You wanna get the job done early, have yourself a good time and at the end of the day know somebody worthwhile has your back? You came to the right place. Every member shall be evaluated for true viking toughness whether it be fighting of monsters, killing that platoon of clan scumbags (yes we are omni don't like it pishoff) and making ourselves happy all over rubi-ka. None shall stand in the wake of Viking Kitties!!!
Tender hearts need not apply here, for we are bloodthirsty, we shall ravage your women, drink your drink, and foul your soil till nobody stands in our path to Valhalla. You have pirate blood you might stand a chance here. Prior experience as a bar wench or rowdy rum guzzling rapscallion is welcome. If you find yourself knee deep in a charming beautiful garden full of pretty flowers that smell good you are not our sort. Thirst for blood and Triumph over those that would oppose our objectives. No shades of grey, no little white lies. Our approach is your with us or against us, and those that oppose us will fell our wrath.

((note... wrath may be rp banter opposed to ripping your head of in a 5% zone, unless you want to fight me which by all means bring it )) 50% roleplaying, 30% getting your levels and spending time on your own terms, 20% Anything you please, sitting around loafing, chatting, whatever. These are taken in the order that most suits you and applied as such. The rolepayer will feel more at home in this org, the artist will have a means to express themselve to others ((including a website that will be updated often)). And it will be expected and enforced that members of the org include outside orginazations in there daily activities. For more information see below.

Here is the bottom line...

Led by myself Lord Bongo "Ognom" Barnaby the foul mouthed braggard.

Rules to join the crazy viking kitties are as follow...

1. Some experience with rp (will train)
2. Artistic talent of some sort (contribute to the viking kitties websight with poems, artwork, music, screenshots... All material remains yours and will be removed at request)
3. Hatred of the clans (For rp purposes, they smell quite badly actually, if you happen upon a clan wench/soldier you may do as you wish but be warned you will be judged for your interaction with said stinky.))
4. Not an alt or alt that is played more than a few hours a week
5. Willingness to adventure the lands of rubi-ka, by foot or by vehicle, study of nature, understanding of the planet, and a thirst for knowledge of the planet. ((This is a very important part))
6. FRIENDLY ((If your not friendly and can't deal with people don't bother applying ingame, vikings can be ruthless at times))
7. Not playing for the sole reason of uber loot ((This pertains more to high lvl people, but constantly asking when someone will buy/make/has something in org chat will not be tolerated. This org is not for personal gain or to get you "uber loot"))
8. Like to party like a viking! ((Like to party like a viking.))
9. Likes cats ((Since the flash animation that started this idea involves cats dressed as vikings wielding weapons it's a given))
10. Some sort of melee weapon any ql that looks like an axe sword or.... you get the point here, were vikings, for rp purposes you need some sort of melee weapon-hammers are a good choice here, lower the ql the better if you don't use 2hb. ((These will be provided if you can't find any, which would be wierd if you couldn't))

I OGNOM and my first Knight GINOVA can be contacted ingame for recruitment. There may be a small test of your "Vikingness" to assure quality viking likeness

Positions are still open for Knight and vassels at this time, as it is a fuedal system we have. Rank will not be important in this org, it is for recruitment purposes only.
