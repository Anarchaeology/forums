The Eagle, Warrior, and Bear - All in One.
I feel no shame for who I am -- no guilt nor doubt for what I am.
I am who I am, and my mind is my own. Not one can take this from me.
My free thought -- my spirit so free.
Like an Eagle I fly so high -- into the sky,
soaring among the clouds so free.
I am a prestine warrior who's heart is true to his ideals.
So magnanimously defined am I.
You may come at me with words or fight and I may fall.
But know thee this that I shall stand again like a righteous
indignant being. And like the ferocious Bear, I shall
roar loud in defiance.

My heart is fragile; my soul like glass; and my mind like a dust covered book, ages old and never read. All thoughts of arrogance aside -- all jest put away. And before thee I come as a mere mortal man, unwashed and weary, and my weaknesses put before thee.

- Depel Kull
