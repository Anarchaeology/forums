What kind of powers do GM's or ARK's have?
I had a recent bad expierence at the SW mines the other day leveling up an ALT. I was in a good team hunting right out in front of the mine entrance. We were clearly the only ones hunting that spawn point for several minutes. This didn't last too long as we eventually got the same lowlifes every body does when they get a good group going, kill stealing from us. I politely asked them to stop taking our kills, and was replied to with nothing but slurs and profanity. Typical 13 year old behaivor. I sent a petition about this after 10 min of this continual behaivor. I informed them of this all Leetatrox said was 'F**ing C**t'. Then she bragged that she knows LongShot and talks to him everyday, and that petitioning would do nothing but hurt us. Well, low and behold, 5 minutes later, our team witnessed something I never thought I would see in AO. I have hunted in SW Mines in Lush with 6 characters now, and never seen what I am about to describe next. The most add we ever got that entire day were 3-4 shades and 2-3 tac's. About 5 min after she bragged that she knew longshot, we pulled 2 more shades. All of a sudded, at least 6 shades spawned from no where, along with 4 tacs. I was the puller so I know we didnt get the tacs. I almost lost our whole team. We pulled together and took them all out, barely missing a trip to reclaim. We were all very surprised as no one has ever seen this before. My second point is this, about 5 min later, the 2 or 3 players that were griefing us they pulled 4 tacs beside us, we were still recovering and re-buffing so we just watched them. They were counting on us fighting them so they were clearly overwhelmed. Well, surprise surprise, after Leetatrox fell, they brought the other member of their team down to about 1/16th health, when all of a sudden, the 3 mobs that were attacking him dissapeared into thin air. This was not zone lag and he didnt warp anywhere like it does with lag. The mobs clearly vanished out of thin air as do ARKS or GMs when teleporting. What is going on here? This is clearly another example of a rogue GM or ARK that is showing favoritism with certain players. Has anyone else expeirenced this?
