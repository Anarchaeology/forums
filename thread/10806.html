Roleplaying Content #2: Specific areas for RP purposes.
From a RP point of view AO offers so much, we have a vast beautiful world filled with outposts, camps, crashed spaceships and lots more.
I would love if we could have some sort of access to some of these locations for RP-ing purposes.

An example; One of our Officers get a dispatch from Mr.Ross office that a spaceship containing very important documents have crashed in Andromeda. The Officer asks for a volunteer group from within the Faction to drop everything in order to retrieve these documents asap. No certain coords are given for the crash site, they will have to search for it. When they find eventually find the ship they are supposed to enter and retrieve the documents and return them to an office building in OmniHQ.
All of this is possible to do right now to a certain extent with exception that we can't really enter the wreckage of the ship and we probably can't enter the office building in OmniHQ.
What if we had that possibility? It would open up the game for smaller RP events run within factions or smaller groups of people. FC could create and maintain a pool of locations "for hire", so to speak for these purposes. Shipwrecks, offices, caves, apartments and prisons.

Imagine that we had the possibility to create a scenario where we have to rescue one of our own from a jail in Athens, Tir or some godforsaken outpost somewhere. FC could supply us with one of their specifically created jail locations, insert the person being imprisoned into a cell and spawn x amount of mobs for us to fight through as we come to the rescue.
The possibilities would be endless.

Players supply their own scenarios and schedules and has to apply for the use of a location through the proper channels. At the proper time FC could assist by teleporting the person or object to where they are supposed to be and spawn the mobs. Missions are already created from a pool, I imagine anyway... A similar system might be used to decide the difficulty of the scenario.

I know that there are a lot of persons in AO who really yearns for smaller events which can be run by themselves, but still seem like it is part of the storyline even if it's not.
By doing this FC would supply us with the means to really immerse ourselves even more into AO.
