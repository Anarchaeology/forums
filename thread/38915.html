Jynne
Supa Leet
HP is based entirely on Body Dev and level.

Body Dev is 100% dependent on Stamina, so raising Stamina will add points to Body Dev. The color of your Body Dev skill, which determines how much per level you can raise it (like normal skills) and how much it costs to raise, is entirely profession-based. A Solitus Enforcer has the same colored Body Dev as an Atrox Enforcer. They can both raise it the same number of points per level.

Breed factors into the HP equation in three ways - 1) the stamina cap, 2) starting stamina and the IP cost of raising it, and 3) a breed-based modifier to HP-per-Body-Dev-per-level.

1) Because Stamina is a "base stat" no matter what color it is, it can only be raised by 3 per level. An Atrox only has higher maxed Stamina than other breeds at a given level, until around level 150/160, because he starts with it higher. But Atrox Stamina will max at a higher point, towards the end of the level ladder, than other breeds. Solitus base stats stop at 480... reachable I think at around level 160. I believe Atrox stops at around 510-520 for Stamina. This works out to "not much" extra Body Dev, though. Maybe 15 to 25ish extra Body Dev points over the Solitus, as a guesstimate.

2) Atrox spend less IP than anyone to raise their stamina. It's more practical for them to keep it maxed their entire lives, though really, anyone who wants to spend the IP on this can do so. It's just a "better choice" for an Atrox than for any other breed. This particular Atrox stamina advantage really disappears at the high levels when everyone can max all their base stats.

3) Nanomages have a penalty to hitpoints per Body Dev per level. I'm not sure if Atrox have a bonus, though it would be intuitive that they do... I believe that they don't. I have heard that an Atrox at level 200 will have less than 300 hitpoints more than a Solitus of the same profession and level. A Nanomage, on the other hand, will have about 800 hps less than a Solitus.

Thus, the effect of breed on raw HP is actually pretty minimal, at any level, unless you are a Nanomage (in which case it's a bad effect)... correct me if I'm wrong?
