The Sentinels
Ever since the hated Sentinels invaded and occupied our beloved Tir, we as clanners have caught flak for not doing everything in our power to oust the Warlord Silverstone and his Band of Thugs. The recent news has only increased the problem and also hurt the clan argument that thugs and criminals with guns shouldn't run a city and/or close it off to whomever they wish.

We have also gotten angry that they are here and sometime gotten angry at ourselves for letting them take it and then letting them stay. I am certain that if OmniTek took over Tir, we would do something about it. Now, you may say the Sentinels are not as bad as OmniTek, as bad as the Sentinels are. Well, perhaps you are right, but look at what the Sentinels have done to us. They have given us a bad name. They rule over our capitol and still do, seemingly unopposed which leds many non-clanners to believe we condone or, worse, support them. They have made themselves the loudest spokesmen for the clans and since they have a radical, militaristic, warmongering agenda, that certainly doesn't give those of us who still think peace is a possible solution any credibility.

The neutrals look to us to fix the problem. Our children look to us to fix the problem. Moderate Omni employees look to us to solve the problem. There are those, however, in OmniTek that think that we cannot solve this problem. They believe they must solve it for us and invade and take control of Tir. How exactly is that better? We trade a Clan dictator for an Omni one? Hmmm, I don't know about you all, but my father taught me never to trade down. Therefore, we must fix the problem.

And, if we are all so opposed to the Sentinels, why haven't we done anything? I know that there are some people who have tried attacking Sentinels but even though they win, the rapidity of the Sentinels reclaim process is absurd and within 15 seconds of being killed, the thug is back and fighting. The forces are always too small.

So, if they come back so fast, what are we to do? Well, I know that attacking in small numbers is certainly not the answer. And perhaps attacking the petty thugs themselves is not the answer. The only solution to removing the thugs from our capitol is amassing as many clanners as we can and cutting off the head of the Sentinels. We need to kill Simon Silverstone and we need to make sure he stays dead. Silverstone is too charismatic, too hate filled, and too power hungry to be allowed to live. If he did come back, even if we stripped him of his power, his charm and ambition would always find him a way to gain power. This, of course, will not be easy. But tell me who ever told you the life of a clanner was easy? We live this clanner life, whether by choice or circumstance, and we had best do the best we can with what life we have. We might all die horribly, but at the very least, we'll show the world we are dead serious about getting the Sentinels out of Tir.

Tell me what you think. You know this must be done and we might as well do this sooner, rather than later.

My fellow clanners, I implore you to listen. Silverstone and the Sentinels are not only our problem now. Destroying him permanently will disorganise the Sentinels and give us enough time to strike their other leaders, such as the infamous, ignorant Fisk. Once we have cut off all the major heads and burned the wounds in the form of destroying their insurance, then the heads will never come back.

Once again, this problem has moved from a military occupation of Tir to a possible military strike against our friends, the neutrals. And do not try to tell me that the actions of a few wealthy, misguided people that hire thugs to infest their city justify any action by Simon Silverstone. Silverstone invading Newland would be to the neutrals as OmniTek invading Tir would be to us. We must fight stop dictatorship and authoritarianism on all side, not just in OmniTek. We have internal problems that need to be dealt with before they become external problems, which, it seems, has a possibility of happening very soon.
