New Forums allow for easier ignoring of player questions
Each time the forums are changed, one of the reasons given for this is to allow Funcom staff to more easily respond to players' issues.

In the last 10 days, Funcom staff have posted a whopping 11 messages answering player questions. They are all mirrored into the Official Answers thread:
http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...?s&threadid=27

Having Funcom staff post 1 message per day may seem like a lot at first glance, but...

Well, actually, it doesn't seem like a lot. It seems like a pathetically small number of responses to player questions and issues.

For months now, we've been asking why we can't have some answers to our questions and concerns from someone at Funcom. For months, we keep being told that "one of these days now, we're actually going to start answering your questions, just wait, it'll be great!"

What's so difficult about having an employee, or a VOLUNTEER (as I keep suggesting!), read through all the threads and actually answer >all< of the player questions?
