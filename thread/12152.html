Funcom: Newcomer ideas (r u nubi?)
I know those early levels go quick, but I recently rerolled a character and forgot how dark the newbie backyard is. For players learning the game and dealing with the learning curve, this backyard area is too dark!

Yeah Yeah, I know, get the Night Vision goggles. A new player off the street learning the game is not going to be able to buy them for the 249cr, and you can't even use them right away (level 5) because of the cap on skills to use it.

So why should level 1-5 be in darkness unless you do the mission over and over?

Even at level 2, you can exit the city, only to find darkness. AO is a dark game as it is, and without the glasses, you can't even target mobs in the Backyard.

I recommend lowering the price and requirement on the Goggles and Lockpick. I had a mission at level 1 that I couldn't do because the door was locked! How am I going to get 200+ credits at level 1?

If you want to be newbie friendly, don't leave new players in the dark, the manual is bad enough as it is.
