200 nano init is -1 sec off recharge. I tested this numerous times.

The attack value on the nano description is unmodified value, which gets modified by your init and your agg-def value. The basepoint is at 50% agg-def value. There are only 3 positions of the agg-def bar that have different effects on the attack speed (only for NanoC Init, other inits take full advantage of the slider positions). By moving your slider from 100% to 99% it will reduce the attack speed by 1 second, if you move it from 100% to 0% it will reduce it by 2 seconds.

So to cast your 2.7/1.5 nano instantly (0/1.5) at full aggressive, you will need 340 NanoC Init.

The formula is simple and it works for every character I played so far.

Just to add with 550 Init I have one 1 character I insta cast 3.5/x nanos, so that 540 is way off.
