Its time for some DNA manipulation of the Nano Breed
Quote from AO manual Breeds:

Nano (Male/Female)
This is the most xenomorphic breed on Rubi-Ka, uniquely specialized to control nanobots - tiny robots that reside in the host body and in the air, and that contribute to the well-being and quality of life of all Rubi-Ka citizens - and to create and run various programs. The Nanos are surrounded by a continuous energy field, controlled and networked by their NCU - their nanobot control units - which manifests itself as a glowing halo around members of other breeds, should they stand close to the Nano. Because everything happens on the molecular level, the use of nano technology will appear to the untrained eye as magic, something that has been known to cause superstition and mistrust in the past.


Nano breed is also the only breed that can never leave Rubi-Ka, we are addicted to the Notum in the air, without it we die.

At lvl 200 the Solitus got the same sized nano pool as Nano Breed, and very close at lower lvls too.

Choosing Nano breed for the NT will only gimp you. At lvl 200 we also get 1k+ less health and the enormous 7 more to nano skills compared to Solitus.

quote:

So, what needs to be done? We certainly need some stuff to make us a valid breed, and since more health is very little likely, we should get something witht the word "nano" in it, or some Int req thing, which would fit the description of the Breed.

DNA manipulation suggestions:

1. Since the Nano breed is so "uniquely specialized to control nanobots" I think some Nano Resist bonus for our breed would fit very well. Some additional NR each title level would be nice, maybe 50 for each title lvl on average. so at lvl 190+ we get +300 NR compared to the other breeds.

2. NCU bonus. Cosmik has already shown he is positive to this. An average of 10 extra NCU for each title level would be nice. At lvl 190+ we will have an additional 60 NCU on top of what we already got.

3. Nano skill bonus. Not the Int bonus included in this one. Something like +5 to all nano skills every title lvl, at lvl 190+ that means +30 to all nano skills.

When I say "on average" I dont mean that is the exact number we should get every title lvl, but for example +10 at lvl 1, +20 at 2, +30 at 3, +50 at 4, + 80 at 5 and +110 at 6.


Any suggestions?

A differing opinion
I have a differing opinion about this. First, if you make the nano breed so special that it is given too much priority for certain professions then there will never be diversity.

There are two things that need to be mentioned here. First, nano breeds are not the only breeds that are shafted. Nanos suffer from the lack of health and Atrox suffer from the lack of nano. However, an Atrox's health is barely separated from Solitus as a nanomages nano is barely separated from a solitus.

So here is my take on a good solution. First, for nanomages - increase their overall nano pool 25 percent more, give them a bonus to nano resistance, and allow them a self regenerative process that effectively gives all nanomages X amount of nano over time infinitely. On the same flipside, for atroxes give them an overall health increase of 25 percent more, give them an AC factor for all damage types due to strong constitution and allow them a self health regenerative process that effectively gives them X amount of health over time infinitely.

As for Opifex - no fixes should be done - they are balanced and they have the lowest total ability costs than any race. Solitus should not be changed either.

This still allows for people to diversify and choose professions not based off of the OBVIOUS choice but rather what they feel good at roleplaying. As an atrox NT, I sacrificed the nano for the health to work as a combat NT that aggros and takes the damage somewhat. i didn't realize until too late that Solitus have almost the same health as I do.

Nanomages are not the problem. The basis for what is a solitus is the problem and each race does not have a defining attitude or built in personality curve.
