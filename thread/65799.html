My suggestion for this is to have HUD health bubbles, like the current selection, for all your pets. These bubbles would be selectable, and doing so would make them your current target. This way you can see at a glance what their health is, and also quickly select them without the absurd cntl-tab mechanism that scans the universe.

Additionally, these pet health bubbles would have an indication of the time remaining, so you can keep track of it. If you're a crat and have a charmed pet, the bubble would indicate time remaining on the charm.

An enhancement to these bubble would be to right click on them to bring up a pane on your pet that contains relevant information, like current heal/attack target, name selection (w/ rename ability), currently running nanos, etc. etc.
