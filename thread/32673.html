Sometime after level 120 many players find they earn more credits than they spend on maintaining their characters.

This tendency grows stronger as you level higher up...

And since there are no money sinks to take any sizeable amounts of money out of the game, the money pool keeps growing, while the amount of things to buy does not.

Which is why, eventually money starts being so abundant at high levels that items are traded for prices that seem ludicrous to most players...but the fact is that higher levels have a serious inflation problem.

I have never had more than 5-8 million myself, at level 124, but that is because I spend a lot on funding my alts

As they are starting to be able to fund themselves, I too will probably end up paying silly amounts for rare items, as there is not much else to spend it on.

Buying real estate and hiring guards to protect it might soak up some cash.

Emiko, a fellow adv here, lvl 168. First of all, you are at the level where you should start thinking about joining a guild. Usually in a guld there are high levels who will help you out equipment wise-just look around and see who you are playing with, most guilds are approachable.

As to making money....I made all mine the old fashioned way, just soloing missions, killing all the mobs, looting the chests, and selling most to the store. Normally in a 50% mission you'll get 2 or 3 pieces of armor in a chest that are 10 levels higher-you need to do lots of missions of course, but eventually you will build a full set. Adv have good run speed so you can also run missions over your level to get valuable items, and you can also try sneaking missions if you raise conceal.

The big payoff in those missions is when you find the bracers and melee and physics rings....those can be sold for quite a sum.
