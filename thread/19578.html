14.2 OE Nef: Not just for the lowbies..
Alright, here we go...

The most common misconception about this nerf is that it doesn't effect people over the level 110 or 120. This is a complete lie. At level 165 (about the level dark blue shotty maxes for the 159 title caps) you cannot self equip a QL 199 ithaca... the most common gun in the game. This is with all QL 200 implants.

Much less at level 130 when the level 125 title caps come into play for the dark blue skill. With the implant stacking -NERF-, not a fix, it was never a bug, it is close to impossible for a class with 4 per level treatment to get QL 200 implants in... And that is needed if you want to get a QL 199 ithaca on when shotgun maxes out for the title caps with a wrangler, etc... And for a lot of classes, they can't constantly have the three implants in. Usually they have to have it switched out for something. Usually either the bright or faded, which means it will be outside of the 20% grace period..

I think the greatest part about the 14.2 OE patch is that it affects "current state" as well.

What I mean by this:
In past, fixes/nerfs/balancing issues or fixes of exploits haven't affected players who already used them.
In particular and as examples, there are still lvl 25 players with 392 NCU's (I understand it was a tutoring glitch), and there are still players with QL 200 implants at levels where they are currently unaccessible because of skill caps (I understand it was a treatement glitch).

The issue with those balancing fixes was, they actually created imbalance in a way! - the old players and the new players were drastically differently equipped.

etc, there are different examples...

However, this patch will from what I understand apply "accross the board" in every possible way. Everybody will be affected the same (within professions/types of course). Somebody who OEd a (say) hammer yesterday will have the same penalty as a person who tries OE-ing it today.

So in that sense the patch is fair, and I can't say I have a problem with it. I think it's a positive move forward to bringing this game toward what it is meant to be .
