Freelance Investigator - City Survey's for New Offices
As the suns of Rubi-Ka rise and set a rumor unfolds for our freelance reporter. Revealing pieces of a puzzle, only to raise more questions then answers.

**Grid uplink connecting Password accepted Welcome to the Rubi-Ka Grid 

I'm Lacey, a freelance reporter for the citizens of Rubi-Ka. I had been informed recently about an organization with possible ICC links forming in Newland. From the territories, I decided to take the easier road to Tir. I would whompa to Newland from the Clan City. This turned out to be a good decision regarding my investigations. I arrived at Tir West Gate, just in time to hear Advisor Gemax looking for "potential construction site for an office building". He was paying a finder's fee for coordinates any one cared to offer. He did stipulate any site suggested near sewers or doorways of other buildings would not be accepted.

Upon answering my questions, I discovered Gemax and his black robed companions were representatives of an organization called ARK, simply looking for office space in the cities to help the citizens of Rubi-Ka.

Gemax would fade into thin air to see anyone with a potential construction site. Garret, Raiven, Tanhouser, Scarecrow & Threax all participated and received their finder's fee with the promise of an additional reward should their coordinates be used for the ARK office in Tir.

It came to my attention that Tir was not the only city to have these mysterious visitors. Newland and Omni-Tek also had ARK representatives looking for suitable office construction sites.

Freelance writer Jatney Jones from Newland had this to say;

quote:
Report from Jatney Jones in Newland

Newland, an established and normally quiet town sanctuary for those not wanting to involve themselves in the ongoing struggle between Omni and The Clans, was visited today by a mysterious group calling themselves Advisors.

Late in the afternoon, Asmodeus, one of these Advisors, shouted across the city that his group was paying well for surveyors to locate a spot within Newland for their new offices. Though most seemed reluctant at this new addition to their city, a few willing folks searched out and found a few possible locations of these new offices. Asmodeus was handing these helpful citizens 500 Cred each for their surveys and promised a hefty 10k Cred to the person that selected the spot they would eventually build their offices upon.

Asmodeus was not alone. Spotted around the city, standing quietly, were several other figures that were clearly not Newland Citizens. While mysterious, these Advisors, given the attitude of Asmodeus, seemed generally to have nothing but good intentions about their new offices. The coming days should be interesting to say the least, as we wait for the Advisors to return and begin building their offices.

Jatney Jones
Freelance

Well known Omni-Tek reporter Hadon Gadez covered what happened in Omni-Entertainment;

quote:
Report from Ha'don Gadez in Omni-1

A strange occurrence happened to this reporter today. A pretty young lady who called herself Advisor Adia showed up in Omni-1 Entertainment, wearing a mystical black coat and asking for help. She was surveying for a potential site for the new headquarters of an organization called "Advisors of Rubi-Ka." Intrigued I started to interrogate this young female to find out what this was all about. She was elusive but hinted that this was a humanitarian organization dedicated to the people of Rubi-Ka. We are here to help everyone. Omni employee and Rebel alike. Said Adia. Daring words in these times

Several people responded to her request for help and were rewarded handsomely for their assistance. One of the helpers was heard to say, It seems like a good idea. People helping people. I'm all for it! According to Advisor Adia, several good sites were located and final selection and building will progress within a month.

Further investigations by this reporter reveals, this organization seems to have been set in motion by the ICC but not many details are available as of yet. Also noted was that these Advisors seemed to wield a lot of power, keeping my usual sources unusually quiet.

This is Hadon Gadez, signing off.

The Advisors of Rubi-Ka have some high tech nano formulae to be able to fade in and out of view, I believe they have the technology to help the citizens of Rubi-Ka. At last I found some truth to the rumor I was told in the territories. My next stop will be Newland, with many questions for this ARK organization in mind, I hope my source will lead to more answers.
