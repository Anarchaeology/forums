Nanoprogramming/implant Design Available Again!
Now that I'm lvl 200, I find myself with a good deal more free time than before (read: Bored out of my goddamned skull), so I am once more extending my nanoprogramming and implant design services to you!

I can build any implant up to QL220 refined, with the exception of jobe implants.

For jobe imps that require psychology (i.e. defensive mastery), I can assemble non refined faded and bright and may also be able to do shining as well.

If you're not sure what you need, I can design a custom nano nanny template based upon your breed, profession, weapons choices and current level.

Need a set of QL200 imps? Not a problem! I can provide the imps AND the assembly and take pretty much all of the legwork off your hands.

Normal Implant orders will be delivered within a 24 hour time period unless unusual circumstances occur (i.e. tower attacks, sickness).

up to 1350 or so nanoprogramming is available and is charged on a case per case rate. Please send me an PM via these boards, not ingame, and let me know what you need.

I tend to get ALOT of ingame tells, so it's hard for me to effectively communicate sometimes and I don't want to come across as being short or distant.

Things to include in the pm:

Breed
Level
agility (max this out before you send me the email)
Stamina (max this out before you send me the email)
Weapons choice (link or the name and QL of your weapon)
Treatment level (MAX THIS OUT)

Special circumstance: If you are a fixer, please include whether or not you use GA, and also include your level of matter creation.


With every custom order, you will also receive a copy of your implant template for future use, and instructions on stepping into the highest QL implants possible.

Why should you go with me? As a veteran player, I have well over a year of experience designing implants, twinking, etc.

I don't just build you the implants, I build you the best implants possible for your breed, profession and level. I know my junk

All prices include temporary "stepping" implants and if need be, you can borrow stats boosting items such concrete cushions, quartz rifles and omni met suits/biomech armor.

So remember people, Thedeacon puts the "imp" back into "gimp"!!

P.S. - er, I only do implants. No weapons building, CAS armor building, Dragon armor building, etc

P.S.S - I NO LONGER DO CLUSTER RUNNING! For the nominal amount that people are willing to pay for this, it's just not worth it. Cluster running as we all know can take hours and to prevent confusion, I have to cluster shop for one order at a time. I got like 6 orders for cluster running and it ended up taking days to complete the task

I'll give ya a shopping list instead hehe.
