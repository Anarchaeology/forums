"I'd like you to convert This image into real life, Here are the plans." John said calmy. "Woah man, Aren't you that dude form that Cool Infomercial?" Said the Saleman. The Ammunition was placed in a resistant tank that was mounted on The back of the 'Bio-Chemical Troopers' It could hold over 1,000 Gallons of this subtance. John G. Marched his troops To the Gates of Omni-Headquaters. "Welcome to Omni-Headuaters, What Can we do for you?" Asked an operator. "We've come to End your Filth of a corporation once and for all!" Exclaimed John. "One second sir, Opening the gates. mumbled the operator. John Cringed wuth joy as he marched all of his 7,000 Troopers into The Headquaters. "READY? FIRE!"
