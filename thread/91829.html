Veteran Rewards For AO
Hi I was just thinking of what I think is a pretty good idea, funcom should impliment a sort of request system for vet players that havent been able to do things like kill that big dragon, kill tim the robot, or other such things

for example every year a paying player will get one reward request they can ask a gm to spawn so that so and so player will get a chance with her/his team to kill and have a fun experience and have that chance at something that only a very small percentage of players are able to do

not only does it take away from x player being bitter that they're guild just isnt big or strong enough to out dmg a much stronger guild but causes a fun and pleasant experience that one day they will be able to do a rare spawn

I think it's a win win situation since it awards vet players for being a loyal paying customer to funcom and funcom gets extra money from players that want that chance to do an encounter that they never thought they would be able to do
