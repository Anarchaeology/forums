Professionals Questions: Vote Results #4
The top priority issues this time are:
    1) Roots in PvP being too powerful, and not all can get rid of them.

    2) Healing range in PvP - level 150 Doctors being better than level 200s.

    3) Crowd control heavily limited in PvE due to bosses and monster immunities / high resist.

    4) Multi-wield update - is the gain supposed to be only 30%, and if so, why?

    5) Outdoor bosses being very dangerous, yet they are necessary to take down to get new nanos

    6) Trader nanos look-over - charm rooting, and nano drains and health funnels not paying off.

    7) Black and white nature of Psychic checks on charms create level gaps in charm and fear effect

... with mez pet as Number 8 on the list but unfortunately a few points short of being 'top priority' in the vote. Judging by this it should definitely make it next time.
