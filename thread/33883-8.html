What i miss in the game
The character development is excellent.
The world/environment is beautiful.
The mission generation is fun.
The content is ... content? What content? The PvP is practically non-existent and Notum Wars won't make a huge difference

1. GUILDS
- official cooperation between guilds. Alliances. This should be greatly beneficial in Notum Wars. As it is now, there is no reason why one guild should help another. In NW this situation will not be changed. Build Alliances, and in NW an alliance should be able to have a tower.

2. PvP
- what pvp? This game (will) succeeds in making some classes completely useless should there be a pvp occasion once every 500 years. Once again, I don't see NW changing this situation. There is no need for strategy, for being cunning, for trying to outsmart your enemies. No real scouting, no real luring out the opposition. Only example I can give of PvP being really developed pretty well is Dark Age of Camelot. It is not perfected, but they are well on the way to it. Currently in AO there is motivation for and no benefit from PvP. NW will change this slightly but not enough by far. See 1. GUILDS on this for Alliances. Also build in extra benefits. Like ranks, increasing your standing with Clan or Omni, and an increased standing leading for example to an increased percentage on looting treasures, or increasing certain skills or abilities a little without needing IP.

3. CHAT CHANNELS
A bit disorganised. You have to switch around a lot. Too much clicking =P

For me the two main points are the working of guilds (it doesnt do ANYTHING at the moment) and the pvp-content.

NPC Dialog

All NPCs should have dialog like the following

If the NPC is a quest NPC it should immediately start talking to you when you double click on it and the quest dialog should be at the top

Next you should have a Directions dialog option which gives directions to static dungeons, whompas, or other points of interest in the game

Next you should have Rumors now rumors should give you rumors of quests, dungeons, or events in the world.

Quest Dialog (only quest NPCs)
Directions
Rumors (this helps you find quest NPCs and take part in worldwide events)
Help (this should give the commands found in the /help command)
Shopping (Only if vendor)

Billboards and News

There should be big billboards with news around the world or there should be news terminals around the world in which you can get roleplaying based news that points to quests and events players can do. The more roleplaying based stuff the better even if most players dont roleplay.

Crowd Control System

I think that initially you should'nt put this system into operation and you should just see what happens if nothing is done. If things start crashing like you expect and THEN you put the system in place I think players would be more accepting of it because it will then be obvious that something really needs to be done. Also the removal of people from the zone should be as random as possible with NO balancing constraints. I really dont like any artificial constraints on competition. Chaos should reign and EVERYONE in the zone should have the same chance of getting the boot out of the zone if lag gets bad. Be fair.
