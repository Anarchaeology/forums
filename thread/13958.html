Fix this prob now or your game will be more of a mess then now
Every thing in this game is directly or indirectly related to the lv of a char or a mod. And of course the lv system works on ip and skill points. Its a good system all for the fact that at this point its set up partly in the way it was ment.

The orginal set up idea was to have you lv you get ip, you put it in what skills you have and you try out equipment for that skill. You find out what things you like and not like and narrow what you spend your ip in. Great base set, you can waste ip and its no big deal bc its designed for ip wasting. The advantages of this system is you have the characters lv slowly and have a lv range of equip you can use for your lv (this is where the term over equip came from). Mods stay the same difficalutly during the game unless you dont narrow what you spend your ip on.
Now you say well how would I not run out of ip? increase the amount you get per lv?
Not even; ever lv you should be able to equip items 5 or so lv above your lv. Any ip you put in a skill about what you need to get this item will only make your skills more effect (this would play with failing % of not make a trade skill item, failing to cast a nano program, etc).
How can cap equipment when mods are so powerful?
This is an easyone. Mods are bugged in the sense of a bug. Their growth in power is WAY to high, it should be a fast if not a slight bit slower or faster, (you could only tell if you had a lv 10 then had a lv 100 then mods would take longer/shorter time to kill and take away more/less hp when vsin them).
What would this do to PvP?
It would allow PvP to be not so cut and dry, at the mount you have 2 lvs on someone you pretty much can win if you both put your ip in the most powerful way possable. Its not fun to know if your gonna win or loss before you even battle. This would change it so a player 2 lvs higher then your isnt much more powerful then you.
How will this effect equipment?
Equipment will be like it is now but will be good, yes I said it good.
You will have about the same lv equiped bc you dont need alot high lv on to kill mods so in PvP ppl do overall less dam then they did at the same lv the way it is set up now. (PPL were doing way to much dam bc they had such high lv of equipment on). When you get close to lv 200 you wont be like oh crap im just gonna get less powerful bc I already have 200 lv equipment on. Items you find in missions will be of use. Getting equipment wont cost so much money so you wont have to blitz so many missions (or complet them) to get new equipment, youll have more time to hunt, PvP or RP. (4 mill wont be so hard to get for a yam bc your spending SO much less for equipment by the time you can begin to use one )
PPL will have more money
Yes this is good bc ppl who mainly do missions will have alot of xtra cash that they can buy RP things without feeling like they spent so much money. (It's like the econ when things are cheap ppl have more money bc they are spending less on what they need so they have more money for fun things )
Now wont there still be a prob with wrangles being used for equipeding?
Not if you know how to fix it. Wrangles (and frequent customer which they should have fixed in this way insted of nerf) would add to the total for susces of hitting an ememy and not to equiping. They would be stacked differently in the code. You would have prewrangle number which could be used to equip and after wrangle that would figure combat success.
What about nano's you havent talked much about them.
Well since mods are less powerful and over players are over equiping you dont need as powerful nanos like with weapons and armor. The requirements for professions like soldier, fixer, enf wouldnt feel such a hit when spending ip on nano's.
Wait wait how could they not feel the ip hit, when I lv enough to get my next nano lv i feel it hit me hard, I lose ip that I could have used else were.
Well thats bc nano's need more lvs. For exampe the fixer dam buff line is +5, +10, +13, +15. Well change that so in the same amount of lvs you have a +1,+2,+3,+4,+5,+6,+7 etc. This lets the player realise if they like the nano line more then the others and deciede which ones to stop invest ip in (narrowing of skills used as you gain lvs).

If you think about this this is the way it was intended to be, back of box there is a skill bar open on a char, if you notice their ablits arent maxed out bc it has no point to be maxed out bc it will not further what armor that character can use. The talk of ip tree before realise saying as you lv you will need less ip then the lv before bc you narrow what you will use. (though maxing out lets you become better then the avg joe in a skill but you wont use it much bc you will feel the ip pinch if you do). Over equiped wouldnt be a term in anarchy if there wasnt a desired lv range for equipment you use.

WAIT WAIT WAIT. If you do this, set the ip system the way it was you will just patch your way back to the way it is now...
No bc you will fix it the rite way. Mods were made more powerful bc they gave out xp to fast. FC didnt want to "nerf" xp givin to solve this problem so they made mods more powerful which which made so many more problems then if they would have just lowed the xp amount. If that was done in the first place mods would be less powerful and you could solo mods that are higher lv then you are.

Whats gonna stop groups from just hunting these mods at high lvs?
Xp cap; you will not gain bonus xp from mods that are higher lv then you. (you will have to balance the amount of bonus vs the quickness you can get xp but have the quickness be a little bit more (benifit from being in a team ) Another improvement to this would be to have a max number of xp you can get from a kill and the only way to increase this is to solo so you can get the bonus. So this way there is no benift from killin a mod 10 lv's higher then you then a mod that is only 5 higher then you.

You need to get on track FC and get the orginal idea for the ip system up bc its just making more of a mess bc you arent goint to the root of the problem. But ppl need to understand if this happens the game will be alot different now, sure you want have your weapon that is 30 lvs higher then you on you will insted have a weapon that is around 5 but you will be over all more powerful.
