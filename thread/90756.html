the friends window breaks down into 3 grops right now: buddies online, buddies offline, people that sent you a tell.

you can right click on one of the people that sent you a tell to promote them to buddies. you can also use the command /cc addbuddy <charName>.

to remove people from your friends list right click on them and you should get an option to remove them. the command /cc addbuddy <charName> removes people from your buddy list.

i've not come across a method to demote buddies to mere people that sent you a tell, maybe somebody else has the answer for how to do it if there is one.

it -would- be nice to get a command such as: /cc addacquaintance <charName> . this addbuddy can be used to 'promote' people in your friends window and addacquantance could be used to 'demote' them ( if the command existed ).
