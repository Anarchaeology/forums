The main problem with PvP balance is the fact that its solo stressed in a MMORPG. Ever notice how everything else in this game is geared towards teams? With PvP there are NO benefits for teaming to fight the other side. A war cannot be fought when the battle is meant for only two people. Maybe the booster pack will remedy this, maybe it won't, only time will tell. I seriously hope it does so we can have some of the full fledged wars and maybe actually influence the now nonexistant story.

I want to be able to send out agents and flyers to spy on clan outposts and report back to us what is coming our way. Line up the fighters in the battle front and keep those nukers and healers in the back. Then when the clanners come to us we'll have ourselves a real fight.

This arena dueling and 2HO camping is BS.

And how about creating a new suppression gas? 50% - You can fight any faction here, but no points will be gained or removed for kills. This way there could be an area where people could go to hone their PvP skills and tactics without worrying about losing their titles to gankers. As combat-oriented as this game is there should be a place to go for basic PvP training without worrying about some random jerk 100 levels higher then you swooping down and killing you just for the hell of it. Yes, most people are polite enough to ask before shooting you in the arena, but there's always that one jerk just waiting to prey on some lowbie.
