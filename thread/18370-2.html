"Enforcer, you will never mongo unless I tell you too. I will simply say MONGO and that's your queue. Mongo twice if the mob doesn't pull to you. Trader do not use deprives/ransacks on the mob until the mob is at half."

The reason for this is I solely control where the aggro goes. I am controlling the fight.

"Before we fight everyone remove buffs completely. Okay MA buff "UVC etc" on everyone. Adventurer buff AC on everyone. Enforcer buff essence on everyone. If anyone else wants buffs after those two, say so and state what you need. After that you can buff only what you need personally."

I usually give humidity at this point to MA, Adventurer, Enforcer, Trader.

"Okay I'll be pulling singles always unless I know I want to pull multiples. If I pull multiples, MA is primary assist. Everyone assist off him and I'll mez adds when I feel its necessary."

Sometimes I'll ae nuke adds while using nullity and still have time to mez/calm the two I want to sit idle and then fight the primary one if its not already dead. I am a master puller and no how to pull singles even in break-ins and never pull an add unless I want too. Anyone can do this it just takes practice.

If a real mean mob shows up I'll generally keep the aggro on me and fight unless I see resists - depending on the mob 50/50 chance it happens. If I see an Ace - I mock piss it off, keep the aggro on me and then calm it. I relayer and heal and nano up and call mongo because the MA or soldier just took the aggro away from the enforcer. Very seldom does the trader take the aggro and only usually when the dumbskull did what I told him not to do and that is deprive/ransack to early (big taunt). Otherwise, I'll stand up and piss off the ace by casting calm, brawl to break calm initially, da taunter and mock nukes that do no damage. Hey I just stole the aggro back.
