I don't blame you for what you did. Hell, I had to do it several times myself. But asking you to tank TK for THEM, like you owed them something. And then sending his guildies after you. Yknow, that could actually be considered harrassment (having his guild send you nasty tells).

Yeah, I agree that it's funcom's fault for making him so camped, but I think we've kicked them enough times in the last year and a half that kicking them again just isn't going to help. Long time ago, back when there were no uber items in AO, we begged them to put uber items into the game and cursed them out for not being more like EQ in that regard. Funcom is new to the mmorpg market and to be honest is a bit of a tiny company to embark on such a huge venture. So the camping is sort of a hastily assembled solution to a much bigger problem, which is that of content.

We tell them we want it, and we want it now, they respond by throwing in a unique mob that drops some cool item. I have high hopes that the booster pack really is going to bring some cool things to the table and in the meantime some of the new quests that we're all bumping our heads on is a huge step in the right direction. We have dynamically spawning quest mobs (give letter A to npc B and npc C spawns), MOBs that may SEEM invincible but have some 'trick' to beating them, etc. This is what a quest should be all about. It eliminates the camping aspect by making the mob spawn only for that quest instead of 'popping' at a predetermined time and it really makes us put our heads together and think about the solution to the problem (the trick to killing 'the one' is clever). Maybe NPC A or B even has a clue on how to defeat npc C?

But yes, sloppy quests such as the trash king must go, but if Funcom changes that quest in any way, we're going to lynch them on these forum boards. It's obvious that Funcom is making a huge effort to right the mistakes they made early on, and if we as a community could show a little more patience before we say "it's all Funcom's fault" and maybe offer some more realistic suggestions, then we might arrive at a solution that would be acceptable to both us and them.

But I totally agree with you on what you did. As cruel as it sounds, it's not necessarily the early bird that gets the worm in this game. It's the biggest, fattest, meanest bird. It's not the best way, but you have to do what you have to do. Maybe the 200 mp should consider using a more damaging pet than the demon and nuking a little bit more in between weapon recharges. That's how I've always gotten loot rights at TK

Remember, while FC's implementation of this quest may be horrid, those players should maybe act a bit more mature when they don't gain loot rights. It's not Funcom that made them whining, accusing babies, it's bad rearing. When I was trying to get TK's rack for my little MA (it took me over 20 days. I had 1 hour left on the mission when I got the part), I'd show up there and see a team of lvl 180+ camping him. Never would I demand them to get the part for me or blame them for wanting the part too. We're all in the same boat here and I can't fault them for doing the exact same thing I'd do in their place. Instead, I'd wish them well, offer them buffs to make it easier on them and then I'd leave to try again.

Ask yourself this. Were the situation in reverse, would they have given the loot to YOU? No, of course not. They would have done the exact same thing that you did. Outdamage them and then take loot for themselves. This is no different than real life. Haves and have nots. If you have not, you better figure out a way to have
