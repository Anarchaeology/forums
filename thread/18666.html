Haven't noticed this myself
I've found that in general I have a better chance at a token
with higher difficulty missions, especially in cases where I
don't in fact kill all the mobs. Killing just 20% of the mobs
on a 50% mission pretty much guarantees not getting
a token, IMO, but killing 20% in a 75% mission gives
me a fair chance, or so it seems, especially if the
mission is fairly large.

On the other hand I've never NOT gotten a token with
a 50%+ mission with all mobs killed so long as there was
at least 5 of them. It sounds to me like you are experiencing
some new and wonderful bug with the mission system.

However, I should say I've only done 3 missions since
the downtime on Thursday, and got tokens for 2. The
one I didn't get was a "snake" type mission with only
one mob, and the two I succeeded with were smallish
levels with around 10-15 mobs at around 60% difficulty;
in all three cases I cleared the levels and found the
objective.
