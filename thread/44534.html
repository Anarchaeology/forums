Beam - Seems underrated to the actual damage it can do when
you use the higher Rage/challenger/fobs/scopes

SSC/Mantis Scissors - Overral good damage in both pvp and pvm.
bit of a hassle to get on since multi melee reqs are 880, but keep a 200 ambi-eye in the back, and it's fixed this compo requires a *lot* of IP, so don't do it unless you can.

*note* you loose Dimach with this combo

1-90 - Hammer/beam

150-180 - Beam
180+ - 1he dual SSC with either Scissors or something else unless you want to stick with the beam... it really rocks

All my ideas have changed since I wrote this thread. I've gained more PvP experience and Ip's to toss into the various wep skills for more testing (I don't dare to think about the fact that I've maxed 1hb,2hb,1he,brawl,dimach,heavy weps,fast,sneak and multi)

At the time I wrote this, I'd just swapped to Byom/scissor and had *honestly* not enough tested to even have a saying that others should take as a fact

However, PvM-wise theyr'e not all that far out.
This is my new "idea" on weapons I would choose if I'd reroll another enforcer.

1-10 Hammer
10-TL5 skillcap 1hb combo
TL5cap-200 Beam, Scissor/Byom.

Some may say I'm neglecting the nanoskills at low level due to the heavy ip-dump into secondary skills, but... is it really all that much?. keep them at a minimum till you have the spare IP's to max them. In my experience sitting at the arena-edge in Tir seing enfs wielding beams getting beaten to death by other enfs with dual combos without even getting close to winning all the way up to lvl 160ish makes me realize that without a Proper FoB and reflect (which we're waving bye bye to) there's really no use for a beam untill higher levels... sure it's awsome to bring back mobs with a huge dent in the HP bar due to the first brawl/hit, but all in all, your'e pretty much weak alone if you can't do damage over time. Therefore... save that beam for later, bro. Me and a lot of the other enfs changed away from beam disregarding the advice we got from the few enfs that'd skyrocketed away from us and actually had the level to use it to its optimal. I regret the day I IPR'ed 2hb .. should've gotten 3 levels and saved it
