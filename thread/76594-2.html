The whole idea of a hard cap is totally redundant, its just not needed.

Currently because of the hard cap a lvl 149 character can ONLY PvP characters of lvl 149 and below!!!! And a lvl 150 character can ONLY PvP characters of lvl 150 and higher.

Which means when a lvl 149 and a lvl 150 square up to each other the only thing they can attack each other with is harsh language. With all due respect to those who think capping is a good idea this is the reality of capping, it is stupid that a lvl 149 cannot attack a lvl 150 and it is also stupid that a lvl 150 cannot attack a lvl 149.

If you look at any other lvl character such as say a lvl 100 or a lvl 170 they can attack players of a higher lvl and players of a lower lvl. They have a PvP spread this spread is similar to (but not quite as wide as) their ability to team. If all players had a PvP spread similar to their ability team then this problem would be gone. What is more a lvl 200 would still only be able to attack down to a lvl of 150, so the cap in effect still exists but it is not hard. It would mean a lvl 150 character wasnt limited to being ONLY able to PvP players of a higher lvl than him, and also would stop the lvl 149 characters being the top of the food chain in sub lvl150 PvP.
