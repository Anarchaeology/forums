Quests, events and 'unique' items
I dont have time to go through ALL post so this might have been suggested allrdy

I think there should be more events and happenings to stir up the everlasting xp hunting. Make the Supression-gas crash in trade one day, 0% gas wo letting anyone know and after 10-15min make a broadcast and get it back on after a while. Make Tarasque leave his lair and roam for a while etc. etc.

More quests would be fun, gives you something else to do

The thing about unique items are that they aren't that unique. Its only unique for you in the sense that you can only have/wear 1.
Make some one-of-a-kind items!
Make them untradeable and undroppable. I dont mean weapons that do uberdamage or armors with 10k AC although enough to make a diffrence (iow not 10kAC extra and not 4points either). Diffrent texture, higher min dmg, dmg in cold instead of chem, a fancy bonus in some skill, maybe an extra attack, or really low reqs so you cant be OE'd. A Tank armor or coat that looks diffrent and adds to evades maybe, things like that. The thing is that they could be variations of existing stuff or brand new things but the main thing is that they should only be locked to the first person that picks it up and not prof/breed specific.

Make them drop at random from random mobs, maybe at events or quests. That gives us variety and uncampable goodstuff.
