The change of everything on Rubi-Ka...or just paranioa?
"Thoughts unfold, events occur, the lines of infinite possibilities branch out before us like so many roads. Realisation dawns in an awakening of an idea, belief or concept that literally stuns a person to their very centre

Some call this paranoiathe over su****ion of any and every occurrence. To me this is something more. A glimpse of the futurea peak along just one of the many paths of possibilities that we may be walking.

I had a dream. In this dream a great man was killed. A man with the power to unite all on Rubi-Ka. This man was assassinated on his way to a peace talk with a so called opposing faction. In this dream, those that ruled the planet quickly spread propaganda after this atrocity. They said this great man was in fact still alivethat in fact a decoy had been killed.

In my dreama dark future was bred from this lie. In truth a great leader had been killed. And nowin his place stood a puppet. A fake placed in a position of power. In my dream Omni-Tek and Clan stood alignedtwo great men side by side. But one was no longer who he appeared. For during an event of great magnitude and the Trials and Arguments that followedno one noticed that the real Henry Radiman had indeed been killedand replaced by a puppet attached to someones malicious strings.

In this dreamthe future spread forward like a dark terrible tide, polluting and corrupting the very essence of Rubi-Kaand from there the known Universe.

Possibilities exist. We are blinded by the obvious. Take heed for all is not always as it seems.

I pray that these words and dream are the mad rantings of the paranoidI pray that I do not bring prophecy"


-- Halana
