Social Armour fix for RP'ers
I have posted the following in the Suggestions forum, and believe it raises important questions for Role Players. Check it out and bump if you agree and maybe....hehe....just maybe we'll get somewhere!

Post:

Ok, how many times have you been dissuaded from putting on some comfy robes or something to pop down the bar in because it will take too much time and buffs to get your armour back on??

This is, as I see it, a fundamental flaw in the clothing structure for characters. I believe you should be able to don social armour, i.e. clothing that looks nice but doesn't necessarily do anything, without any need to totally re-equip your combat-grade armour afterwards.

The reason? Many will groan and moan when I say RP, but does not Funcom and ARK want to promote events, promote community, promote organisational structures? Whether it be as a guild uniform, or as a character-specific rp aid (e.g. dark robes and leathers for a sneaky agent), there are many people out there who are frustrated at the fact they cannot wear what they like when not fighting, because of the consequences of kitting up afterwards.

This is unnacceptable in its current form, and I believe rectifying this would encourage a lot more rp, a greater sense of community, and afford a lot more fun for those in the game who do enjoy a bit of rp now and again. Solutions? Have an extra slot for a set of social armour like the current Back Slot, or even better, simply a new tab window entitled clothing - stuff you can wear 'over' your armour providing you are not in a fight.

Nerf Social Armor
Mercury Dragons are required to wear uniforms on official gatherings and casual clothes at other times. Of course, it's a pain to get into the armor again, but we're able to handle it. Of course I'm never going to win PvP that way, but if faced with the decision of either cutting down roleplay or being without a PvP title, I'll stick with PR and let others grab the laurels.

I would appreciate if we had the possibility to switch between clothes and armor, but I don't think this will happen.

It's basically a game design flaw from my point of view. The multitude of QLs of the same armor is far from reality - after all, there might be a used rifle, a new rifle and a calibrated sniper rifle of the same sort, but anything else is just so that all the powergamers get something to camp ... er ... do. I would appreciate it if FC lowered reqs on some types of armor and imposed more level / title level restrictions. That would be a nerf for twinkers while not harming the others. Of course this would mean that you can't solo TK any more at lvl 120 with one hand tied behind your back, but then again, who enjoys this more than once in his life?

On the other hand (playing the advocatus diaboli) FC should start imposing skill reqs on social armor: " Your Chemistry is required to be at least 223 to wear this Robe." Sounds weird? Why do I need Psychology to wear DeCranum armor? Do I need to talk it into letting me don it? Does it have a mental disorder? Even Tutoring would be more appropriate.
