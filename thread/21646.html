How is this for flawed logic:

At level 50ish my fixer is wearing 75ish Flowers and Senpai. This aror is almost self equippable... The character also has Grid Armor MK1. A quality 60 nano that requires him to find Mocham's buffs to use.

Not a big deal, right?

What happens after that 45 minute buff expires? He's **WAY** overequipped and his cool armor loses 80-100% effectiveness based on his stats.

Yeah, that's fair.

BULL****!

The only way this nerf would at all be fair is if they changed Grid armor from 'social armor' to something that fits into one of the HUD slots. I can almost promise you wouldn't hear a fixer complain about that. Grid Armor would be something of a bonus and certainly not the crutch that it will become after this nerf.
