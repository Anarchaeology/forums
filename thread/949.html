So..there is Portman...I've never seen him..probably 99% of all players could care less what he does..and how he raided the Op. Lack of change after events...
/ooc

I think a major flaw that needs to be addressed is that the events I have seen so far -- three of them -- are simply transitory in nature, and do not seem to really impact the actual game as a whole.

For example, good events would have some actual impact on a permanent level in a particular zone. Maybe one zone switches from Clan to Omni guards for example. Economic supply and demand in shops are impacted by some event in another zone. Insurance terminals "break" in one area, or even switch to a different side. Unusual mobs begin to roam an area as they are "released" someplace by perhaps Omni scientists for example.

There are so many ways to program "permanent" changes in the story as they occur, and allowing the players to feel like they have some influence in what happens goes a long way in maintaining and adding to your customer base.
