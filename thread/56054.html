Yet another 'love' thy advent post
To start off, let me state one thing right up front: The only class I play is an adventurer. I have no alts, no other accounts, nothing but my advent. I have tried most of the other classes, (skippin Doc and fixer) up to ~100, and they just were not as fun as playing Neuromncr... she has attitude that the others just seemed to lack

That said, on to the next point. I am not asking FC to drop plans for all other professions. In fact, please give NTs, Soldiers, engineers, and enforcers their much needed 'love'.(and any others that I left out... those are the ones I hear the most about)

As to advents, I am not saying that we are 'gimped', unplayable, or anything of the sort. We got out love, but it was 'back alley' type love: quick, ugly, and I really didn't feel satisfied when it was done. I love playing my toon, but there seems to be a few problems I see...

The first being pistols: The reason given for the sky high multi-range skill is that other classes with pets or DD nanos should not be able to weild 2 high QL pistols or something along those lines. So you introduced Advent Only pistols... great... the only problem is that they too suffer from high multi-ranged requirements. Well, that and the one pistol that I see starting a good trend is using the wrong inits (BBI Faithful), and the other (MTI Grey) is no where to be found.

AC buffs: Nice, a bit high on the NCU, but nice. Oh wait... they stop at +373.... and I remember casting that level 110 or so... nothing past that? So 90 levels to go, and no new AC buffs... continue the line... 'nuff said.

Damage shields: Pretty cool... be nice, though, if our line of Cyclic shields that were even stated in patch notes to be in the game, WERE in the game!!!

Morphs: Heck, we turn into stuff... no problems with these... well, besides the broken animations, and the fact that the buff wears off and we can't recast it till the server decides to unmorph us, which mostly happens in the middle of fights


Weapon Buffs: Use them all the time. Nice to have. Not top of the line, not bottom of the line. Right where we should be. But, since they are self-only, how about having the multi weild do a bit more?


Ya know, I could go on all day long about what I wish that us adventurers had, didn't have, and what we need. I guess that the point of this post is this: We are not broken, gimped, or really hurting, FC, but why don't you finish what you started for us, give us the nanos and items that you slated for us, and move on to the next profession that needs fixing, just don't move on and leave us half done.
