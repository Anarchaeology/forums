IE the Advent/Agent (or whatever) who starts pulling in the first room while everyone is still buffing. THat's fine with me, I calm them all while veryone buffs and Mr. Gogogo gets whacked by one of them. THe problem with that person is that he's generally not the tank. THis is confusing cuase most peeps assist the Enf or assigned tank. ANyway, when I am teamed with Mr. Gogogo I make sure people know that he's going to do the pulling if he is good at it, but if he sux I either let him get his own dumb@ss whacked or I yell at him till he concedes to let the real tank do his job.

Anyway, I would sugget calming every add 2-3 times and letting everyone know your policy.

I have a few macros:
1) It is my policy to calm adds 3 times. If you keep whacking my add then I'm not going to chain calm it.
2) %t is tanking. If you are not /assisting %t then don't expect your add to get calmed more the twice.
3) I am calming --[ %t ]-- hit ESC and quit hitting it!
