Consider using a system. I always go left at every turn and work my way around. Generally this will end you back up at the beginning when you are finished. It also has the nice side efffect of everyone on the team knowing exactly where they are going next in case they lag out, go afk for a minute or just were not paying attention.

If you decide to leave a mission before you are done, then turn around and make all rights to get out. You are guarenteed to only go through rooms you have already cleared and are safe for passage, even it you go LD and lose your map.

I also suggest getting your map nav skill to 130(implants help just for loading them, then switch back to your regular implants) and getting the machines, monster and people map upgrades, that will keep you from going in rooms that have mobs you want to avoid as well as showing you all the doorways.

Anyways, that is good single mission advice. For team missions just try to remember where the ports are on each floor, thats the simplest thing. They are usually in one of the 4 corners of the map.
