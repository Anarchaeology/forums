Donzilla - Even though you were flaming me ill answer your questions anyways.

Q. Why do I get critted every hit?
A. Because your evades are too low, raise them.

Q. Why can't my pet take on multiple Aggro's?
A. Because crat pets are weak and can't take on a equal level mob. They should NEVER take on multiple mobs. Use mez to calm adds and kill them one at a time. My record so far with single target mez is 6 dark oranges/reds at once.

Q. Why can't my pet hold aggro on a mob?
A. Are you using Trimmers? Use positive aggressive trimmers to increase hit attack speed, use offensive trimmers to increase his damage. Offensive trimmers work on a % of pets total damage. Use the Trade Skill aggro enhancing stims. Don't start chain casting your DD immediately when the fight begins. I haven't even decided on a weapon yet, but big crit guns like vektors are actually not as desirerable as you would think because its a massive taunt. There is also the line of pet taunt nanos (not available in stores). At your level you should be using the ql44ish one called "MudSlinger".

Q. Why can't I mez something in a 75% mission at lvl 75?
A. What ql implants are you using? You should be in ql90-100s at the least. Use drain psychic before you try to charm.
