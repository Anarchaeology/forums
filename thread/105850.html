Funcom is letting cancelled members come back
quote:
Former Players Invited to Trial of The Notum Wars
03 March, 2003
Editor

We have today unlocked cancelled Anarchy Online accounts for a 30 day free trial of the The Notum Wars. The reason for this is to allow former players to try out the booster pack with their old characters.

From the press release:

"Were extremely proud of the product and the unique gameplay it adds to the game," Funcom CEO Trond Aas explains, "but we feel our regular free trial is more a trial of Anarchy Online, not The Notum Wars. Land control and organisation warfare can take a little time to get into, so we wanted to let former subscribers log on their characters and get straight into the action without having to level up a new character first."

E-mails have been sent out to all former players who are eligible to take advantage of the offer. Some people might have changed their contact information since they had an active Anarchy Online account. More information can be found here.
