Radiman, Terror to all ... friend to none.
Sources say that First Order's security team is scheduled to meet this Saturday to discuss whether the war on the Dust Brigade should include helping to defeat anti-Omni party's that are currently residing within our very cities.

Opponents of direct counterinsurgency within Omni argue that it could raise human rights concerns and lead to a quagmire reminiscent of a civil war.

Executive ranks of First Order say they already have sped up the delivery of fresh mining supplies to ensure our production is not halted, and have posted guards outside several key mines to see our product leaves Rubika unhindered.

An unidentified source has told us Henry Radiman has a new fancy, and is willing to go to almost any length to get it. He wants control of the mines that produce the finest Notum in the universe. Recent tactics, appalling even by the clan's violent standerds, suggest that Radiman has resorted to bombings, bribes, murder and even funding the Dust Brigade to gain his objective.

Residents say that Henry Radiman maintains armed death-squads in Tir that are "equipped better than the clan military" and that he has omni commanders on his payroll. A boy of 13 years, now in custody by First Order said Radimans men sent him and two other children, 8 and 9 years old, to an Omni notum mine to work as informants. "Radiman's patrolmen offered me 500,000 credits to find out where Omni's main vein of notum is located." the terrified youth said. "They said if I didn't accept the job, they would hang me by my throat and use me as a bull's-eye."

Early last week according to private sources, a plane flew over the mining zone dangling two filled plastic bags. When it dropped them from 1,200 feet, witness said shrieks could be heard from one of the bags. Inside one bag where two of the informants, their clothing in the second.

The escalating warefare had extended into 4holes earlier last month, when clan forces working for Henry Radiman repeatedly shot and killed innocent unarmed employee's of Omni. Omni must band together to eliminate this common threat, and it appears to be stemming from one man.

Henry Radiman.
