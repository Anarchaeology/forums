I'm trying not to get ticked off about the whole situation, but the posts by Cz and April don't make it any easier

I'm not knowledgeable in any way with software distribution, retail space on shelves or any other part of the mechanism for getting, but i have been involved in selling product and dealing with the fallout when things don't go right.

Obviously something hasn't gone right with the launch of NW, and at the end of the day, the ultimate point of blame ends up at FC's door. NW went live on the servers and many of the people who wanted to get it couldn't, and many will not be able to for days or weeks, myself included.

What i'd like to see from FC is an explanation of the following:

1) What went wrong
Not just there was a problem with the distribution network, but a concrete reason. It might seen like useless information but it would damn well make me feel like part of the loop.

2) What they are going to do about it
All i've read at the moment is there's a problem, it's out of our hands. That doesn't tell me anything, it almost as bad as just saying nothing.

3) How they are going to prevent it from happening again
If they ever do release shadowlands, whats to stop all this happening again? I missed out on the initial launch of AO (lucky me!) but i shudder to think of having to go through all this again.

Which of the following options sounds better to you?

Customer: I'd like to buy product x please
Supplier: You can't we don't have it
Customer: What? But i've seen this CGI video and it looks great and other people have it, and i'm going to miss out on the action.
Supplier: something went wrong, come back in a week, maybe a month.
Customer: Thats terrible, what are you going to do about it?
Supplier: nothing, out of our hands

Customer: I'd like to buy product x please
Supplier: you can't we don't have it because of 'x'
Customer: That sucks, when can i get it?
Supplier: We're doing 'Y' to get it out faster and reduce any problems that might be caused by you not having it. Next time we going to do it 'Z' way to avoid all these problems.

It's the lack of this sort of information that makes me hacked off.

There seem to be plenty of theories about what went wrong, what they can do about it and what they should do next time, but all i can see in reply is what didn't go wrong, what they can't do about it and why it's not their fault.

As for enjoying the game without the booster pack, I went on a guild raid on a tower last night, which stalled because no one had a shield disabler, which apparently you can only buy if you have NW installed (don't know if this is true or not) In the end we knocked out three or four missile towers (due to a bug i assume) but the general feeling of the guild was what's the point? The clanners in control didn't even turn up to give us some pvp. No xp, no loot, no real point in destroying it, except to knock a few million creds off the guild until they put it back up again.

I have NW on back order from DR, being in the UK i can only see it coming out on the 6th of December, which is a lot better than some. I don't see FC doing anything to speed up delivery times, or reducing the problems caused by it.

If downloading is not a viable option due to requiring on shelf visibility, how does that work with product ordered from DR? why not take every preorder placed, bill for the game and issue CD Keys? Then ship out all the fancy box and voice add-ons when available? This is all product that won't be on the shelf, will still count as sold from DR's standpoint. If this is not possible because people will preorder now instead of buying in the shops, then why not pick a date and only issue keys for people who ordered before then. I only ordered a week or so ago, so i'm not expecting to get mine anytime soon, but some people have ordered months in advance.

Or what about removing some of the unclaimed land areas? I don't think it's right to shut off the expansion features now, but if no-one has claimed there, then it shouldn't be a problem. There is bound to be an advantage if you can access more features of the game before other people. Keeping more areas back would allow for more room for people to fight over when they finally get NW, instead of being confronted with a full map.

AO is the best MORPG i have played so far, and you'll have to do a lot worse to make me go back to EQ, but i like to know whats going on. In the absence of a copy of NW, information is about the only thing you can offer me, and i'm not seeing too much of the real thing here, just diversionary PR.
