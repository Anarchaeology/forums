quote:
Originally posted by Mr Bean
Simple Docs don't have unlimited nano poll. Soilders can simply cast MK shield sit there and hope the doc runs outta nano before the Soilder runs out of MK

Actually, they can get unlimited nanopool if they wanted to. Get Boundless / Personal Notum Extracter, Izgimmer's Obfuscated Recompiler (28% nanocost decreaser), plus have on a 15% CPU Upgrade and Gaily Painted Hood (15% nanocost decreaser), and have 627 first aid for Nanokits.

58% nanocost decreaser means that CH only needs 588 nano.

Personal Notum Harvester gives 235 Nano a tick, every 15 seconds.

So, in a 60 second time period, adding 1 nanokit and a half (+627 nanopool, or 940 for 60 seconds) 940 + 940 (from notum extracter) = 1880.

That means that the doctor can cast Complete Heal a little over 3 times every minute. Saying that they are properly buffed, the average doctor will have about 8,000 HPs. Therefore, that gives them around 24,000 HPs every minute, or 48,000 non-PvP damage.

Take into consideration that they probably have at least 50% reflect shields, that's nearly 100,000 damage per minute that they can absorb for as long as Humidity and Iz's Nanocost Decreaser hold out.

Not that it matters. Not complaining, just bored and felt like doing some math hehe.
~Chris

PS: They also have a 5k nano base, so that's another 8-9 complete heals to start off with
