A few things (6 to be exact)
1st, no drops. Why are there so many no drop items out there? Will it harm the economy too much? I finally decided to explore a dungeon and started with Steps of madness. I found a few nodrop items, ones that are of no use to me. So now I delete them manually or they clutter inventory. Anyone else see a prob with this? Basically, what I'm getting at is, most if not all nodrop items should become at least sellable to NPCs, giving a reason to hold on to them should they be useless to your character. Another idea would be to eliminate the nodrop attribute all together. Or, another way to allow the trading of no drops, I suggest a special procedure. In every general store there would be a NODROP Transfer Terminal or something to that effect. For a fee (based on the QL of the item and the levels of the seller and buyer), you'll be able to trade a nodrop item so that it doesn't go to waste. I'm sure there's a logical reason why they must be nodrop, but I'd at least like to hear it. If not, I wanna log on one day and be able to sell/buy them.

2nd, neutrals, they don't have anything for the neck, do they? The only things for the neck I've seen are the token boards. If there are neck items, then disregard this post (I obviously haven't encounterred any yet). But, if neutrals don't have anything for their necks, I suggest giving them token boards, too, or implementing a few neck items for most levels to compensate for the lack thereof.

3rd, a 4th "Wear" tab, Miscellanneous (sp?): Here, you would place various things such as skin chips, augmented items (i.e. Capsule of Thin Blood), etc., and social armor (to eliminate the need to remove everything you wear. And for sanity's sake, you shouldn't be able to enter PvP combat with social armor on. Being killed by a man in a bikini wouldn't seem right).

4th, Meta Physysist suggestion: MPs are a fine class and have basically everything they need, however, they are restricted. Mainly, the area MPs need help in is with their pets. I understand that having 3 pets is probably more than enough, but three pets that die faster than the MP isn't enough. The MP class needs, 1) Pet AC and/or health buffs, and/or 2) Nanos to make the enemies aggro the attacker pet, and/or 3) More and stronger combat nanos, and/or 4) Faster healing from the healing pet or a "team" heal for the healing pet, healing the MP and his/her pets only. Aside from these, the MPs need one more thing: A working mezz pet. Of course, with working mezz pets, we might be perfectly fine without any of the other 4 suggestions being implemented. but whatever the case may be our mezz pets need to be fixed.

5, another neutral idea to help them travel safer, make Tir, Athen, Omni-Ent, and Omni-Trade 100% suppression gas. That or implement one of these ideas: OT/Clan mobs cannot attack neutrals inside a city zone, thus bringing the guards onto them or make a buyable passport to allow safe travel for the neutrals (prices differ for each city you wish to enter).

6, yet another neutral suggestion, this is for missions. Please allow us to target neutral sided enemies in the missions through use of Tab rather than shift-tab. Also, make it so that Tab no longer targets team members should they be clan or OT. Also, please make it so you either encounter neutrals and OT or neutrals and clan in missions picked by a neutral. it doesn't seem right being attacked by a mob that includes a Clannie and Tekkie.

Meh, there's my ideas, so how do you all like them? =P

As a level 162 mp I'm going to disagree with you a little.

While it would be nice to have the things you suggested, I think for balancing purposes that the strengths of our pets are quite fine the way it is now. In fact, if we were to lose the third mez pet, I'd still think that on paper we're quite strong.

That said, the reason why we aren't is because so much of our power lies in those pets. And pet pathing as we all know is something Funcom can't seem to figure a work around for.

If Funcom could implement a working pet pathing system, I feel that we mps would be very strong. Fix the pet pathing. Allow pets to hide when their owners hide. Those are the only two things I feel we mps 'need'. Anything else would be great, but not 'needed'.
