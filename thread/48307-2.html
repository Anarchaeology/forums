woot!
i just started farming for my 1k board at lvl 106 and i found this thread to be MOST informative. thanks to everyone who contributed info to this.

being clan, i was reluctant to go the clon**** route that was suggested so i came up with my own route which i think is easier and potentially much faster for clanners. go to wine and pull aegean missions. the locations you want are the ones in northeast aegean. NOT rising sun. both coordinates should be over 1000. after you pull the missions, wompa to varmint woods and then just yalm west. it's easier to just yalm over from varmint woods because you only zone twice total(wine>vw>aegean) as opposed to 5 times(wine>vw>ww>oa>wv>aegean). one of the mission locations i take is right on the zone border(2700,2700) and the other 2 are close by. like clon****, the missions are ALWAYS blue cave or subway. i don't think the subways are that bad and it's quicker to just do it than waste a trip. after you do the missions, fly back to vw to sell the crap you got, save, and zone back to wine for more missions. after getting into a routine, i've been averaging about 25 tokens an hour doing this and should get 1k in a few days.

i hope this helps others in the same situation who want that 1k board before they hit lvl 120.

There is another option to those who are getting close to their 1k board.

Kill patrollers in Smug's base in WW with some willing friends. Many of them drop 1 token and the spawn rate is fairly fast.

I've done this for several people and their tokens rolled in fairly fast. And you didn't have to camp mission terminals and fly back and forth between missions.

Probably not the best idea if you wish to solo for all your tokens though.

Also, that's for Omni. If you're Clan, then the bases in Avalon could be similar...but they're a bit smaller then the Clan base in WW so may not be as effective.
