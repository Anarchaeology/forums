Pvp is still messed up bigtime
If you read my rant in the Pvp section, you will see why I say this. For this forum I will just state that people who grid in cars are totally helpless to grid campers. The grace period is way too short and I have been rooted during the grace period.

You cannot de-equip your car once attacked so you cannot even fight back. First aid will not work and you cannot execute nanos to heal yourself.

Point: Pvp types can attack any level player in a car without fear of attack. Cars are deathtraps.

Solution: If you are unwitting drawn into Pvp, in your can you have 2 options. Run or Detonate. Detonate would allow you to unleash huge damage on the attacker, attacker only, and regardless of his distance from you. You would die, so would he. Everyone else would get to enjoy the big fireball!

This would help with the grid camping issue which has never been fixed and allow those who are just trying to run some missions in the deathtraps called cars a little safety.
