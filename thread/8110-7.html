Quick word on team missions: Please, please, please do not make them take any more time than current missions do. Those times that I can only play for 2 hours, I want to be able to find a team and complete a team mission with them. Putting together a team takes about 45min (sometimes longer, sometimes shorter) on RK2. Add some travel time (etc.), and I've only got 1 hour left. I'd like to be able to complete even QL200 team missions in 1 hour with a proper team. [Obviously less people / overwhelmed team = more time than that.]

Keeping the current size / # of mobs of solo missions might accomplish this; I'd suggest that you test for this however.

On the flip side, solo missions should be made *much* smaller, as they're geared towards solo use. Heck, make it impossible to dupe the solo mission key if you want. But having to spend 3 hours to fight your way through a QL80 mission solo is ridiculous and not fun.

I am not suggesting that there shouldn't be activities that take a very long time to complete. However, I personally view this more under the static dungeon / mission area, then day to day missions.
