I am not 100% certain but I have a feeling that the attack rating is based on the actual skill for a specific weapon multiplied by a constant. The speech will add 100 to this attack rating. The attack rating is viewed on your Stats menu. I don't think you can see your Def rating which would be based on your evades multiplied by a constant.

It is important to note that the higher your attack rating is the better your weapon will work. So, you want to max your weapon skills at all time. Most weapons are dependent on several skills to handle (e.g. some require Heavy Weapons and 1HE) and this isn't neccessarily evenly divided between the two skills. See the information on your weapon to find out how each skill affect the attack skill.
