Theres what the player knows, and what the character knows. I think some of you let one slip too much into the other.

We all know as players that Omni-Tek and the Unreedemed are the evil bad guys and the Clans and the Reedemed are the good guys. This has been hammered in to us time and time again by Funcom. Theres no mystery here, no higher understanding for you, as a player, to grasp. Every piece of out of character literature we get from the developers reminds us repeatedly whos bad and good. They have never, at any point, made any attempt to confuse or trick us in an out-of-character context. Read the books. Read the pamphlets that come with the game. Read the timelines. Its all there.

Heck, I remember back when AO was in beta, there was a section on the forums that flat out asked players who were considering having OmniTek characters questions like do you mind playing on the evil side? and does it bother you knowing your side may not win in the end? and do you like having access to the best equipment?, etc etc.. wrapping it all up with Then OmniTek might be for you. They were totally defined as being the bad, evil organization, and a same set of questions defined the Clans as being the heroic, good guys.

Now, in the game its a different story. People act how they want to act and play how they want to play. Youll meet the nicest Omni characters, and youll most de****able Clan characters (and visa versa). Funcom cant control that. But they can define how the rest of the universe is around you, and you have to realize that (despite your actions) we have two entities: The Clans and Omni-Tek. Again, the Clans have been dubbed the "good guys" and Omni the "bad guys" by the developers who create the story we play in. You can play a character whos a nice person that happens to work for OmniTek, but when it comes down to it you're a PC furthering the goals of an evil organization.

How many NPC Clan members has your PC gunned down to get to where he or she is today? Who are these NPC's that get terminated by the hundreds of thousands? We have to assume they fit in the organization roles defined by the developers (Clan = Good, Omni = Bad). Your character walks into a mission and wastes 50 Clan NPC's who may be the nicest, kindest folk on the planet (because they are defined as "the good guys") who are defending this installation you're infiltrating, and you want to walk away later pretending you're still a saint just doing his job for a mega-corporation? Doesn't work that way.

As for the Unreedemed, it's not just how they intend to use Notum which makes them evil (that just makes them foolish as we know it will eventually lead to the destruction of mankind again). What makes them evil is their behavior and actions up to this point, which speak loud and clearly as to their evil alignment. Look at the map that came in your Shadowlands box. Flip it over and read the history of the Redeemed and the Unreedemed. Its all right there.. theres really not much of a mystery as to where these two sides are coming from. And this is out-of-character information provided to us by Funcom.

From it, we can gather that the Unredeemed are:

Greedy
Power-hungry
Megalomaniacs
Sinister
Selfish
Dark
Scheming
Controlling
Dominating
Manipulators
Murderers

Paint it with whatever brush you want, but I think most of us will call that evil. And I doubt any of you would want someone with those qualities hanging around your kid sister.

As for Ergo, he is an NPC character within a story giving his storyline point of view of events and other NPCs. Thats all he is. And thats fine for your character to believe, but his babblings hold no water outside of the game when players are discussing the nature of the game from an external perspective.

I should note that I have Omni characters who are aligning with the Unreedemed, and Clan characters who are doing the opposite. So there is no bias behind my statements. I simply choose not to kid myself about which sides are good and evil as a player (although theres nothing wrong with a character being blind or ignorant to the events going on around him or her).

But I get a little surprised whenever another player seems confused or shocked about which side Funcom envisioned as good and evil (whether its Redeemed/Unreedemed or Clan/Omni). They make no attempt outside of the game to hide which is which.
