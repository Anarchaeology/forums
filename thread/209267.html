A Prelude to Destiny
A hulking form in the dead of space.

As Raijou looked out the port-side viewport in the infiltration ship, a sleek,
stealthy star ship created for surprise attacks on other ships, the hulking form caught
the light of a nearby star. As the light spread over the other ship, the damage became
all to visible. A large section of the residential sector was now a small amount of debris
, lazily floating away from the ship into the blackness that surrounded all. The infiltrator
was here to stop the ones responsible. The space-based terrorists were becoming more
common as news of the newest frontier planet, called Rubi-Ka, first reached the the
SigmaAlteron ,the ship which acted as the HQ for the SATEC. SATEC (Space Anti-Terrorist
Enforcment Corps) was an elite freelance mercenary group, made up mostly of warriors
who were born and bred for space combat. Hand-to-hand experts so that they did not rupture
the hulls of the ships with weapons fire, their technology and news were far from up to date.

"You guys seeing this info?" asked Olto Sorine ,the pilot of the Sigma, over the comm system.
Raijou looked at a nearby viewscreen as text about this 'Rubi-Ka' began to scroll quickly across
the screen.
"Do you see how old this data is?" ,he growled into his comm unit, "We need to see this stuff
while its still fresh, Olto." He ended his reply and switched the comm to inter-team only.
The voice of Torien Bleu, the represetative of their employer, was just beginning to speak.
"The only intel we have is that this terrorist group is 'different' than others in their class." ,he
said with a slight hesitation. "How different?", asked Tim Burlt, the second of four SATEC
members present for this mission. "We received some garbled transmission from the
commander of the captured ship saying that the intruders had some sort of 'enhanced armor
and weapons', whatever that means" Bleu calmly replied. "It means," spoke up Yemmly Hennerson,
"that we have an unknown factor. That always means more trouble for us." Brig, the final member
of the team, who had remained silent until now commented, "We better get paid for that extra
trouble, too. Trouble ain't free y'know." The ship had just finished quietly attaching itself to the docking
bay, and opening the sealed hatches. "Here we go, everyone make sure you pair off before we split up."
Raijou spoke as he released his safety restraints, and moved toward the hatch. "Tim, you and Bleu
are with me. We'll go left and work our way to the bridge." Everyone nodded and began to make
their way onto the ship. "I don't know, it seems kinda weird ,right?" Tim asked as he looked at the
lifesign reader he carried in his hand, "I don't see any lifesigns, and the only things mechanical here
are the normal ship systems." Brig spoke over the comms, "Maybe that armor they were talkin' bout
was some stealth crap." Tim looked back as Bleu slowly came to a stop, just outside what looked to
be a storage bay. "Whats the hold-up?" he asked Bleu as he stepped toward him. A glimmer as the
dull overhead light flashed across the blade Bleu thrust deeply into Tim's throat was the last thing he
would ever see. Bleu pulled a small device from a pocket of his protective suit just as Raijou was turning
to see why they had stopped. "I know that everyone can here me, so listen. I'm sorry to have decieved
you all, but I'm actually part of a group hired to assasinate you." ,he said as a sick grin spread across
his face. "You were a little to good at stopping the terrorists, the larger groups took out a hit on your
entire group." Raijou looked at the device Bleu held firmly in his grip, "I suppose that thing is a detenator
,huh?" he asked through gritted teeth, already knowing the answer. "Right you are my friend, if I let go,
this entire ship is turned to scrap!" he spoke as he pulled a small comm device from another pocket.
"White Ghost to Fracture, do you read me?" he spoke into the device, "Come on I know your out there."
Raijou smiled and said "Your really that stupid aren't you? They sent you on a suicide mission and you
didn't even know it." "Oh, I already knew that, this is about something else entirely." As he finished his
sentance ,the ship rumbled from a shockwave hitting the hull. Over his comm relay Raijou recieved a
recorded measage from the Sigma, "Sorry sir, they surprised us, we're already dead if your hearing this,
you did a good job, saved me and everyone else more times than I remember. There was nothing you
could do. Don't just regret this, it was a fun trip all these years. Powers surging now, bye..." the
transmission became static ,then ended. "We heard it Rai" Brig spoke with a peacful voice. "I don't
want Bleu to kill us." he spoke again a moment later. "So if we're gonna die, kill him before he kills us."
Before Bleu could retort Raijou lashed out with his sword and freed his torso from his legs. "It was good
working with all of you..." an explosion ripped through the ship and after a flash of pain, there was
blackness ,and cold.

"It looks like someone survived." A voice, distorted and distant, spoke. "He's in bad shape, we'll have to
freeze him." another voice spoke, "Where do we send him, he has no contacts on record, and he was
born in space, so no hometown either." Another voice, a woman this time, asked what the record should
show, and was answered by the second man. "Just say deceased, 20:03, July 15, 29435." he paused,
then added, "I know some of the people over at OmniTek are looking for colonists, I'll take him to them."
He looked at the name on the cryostasis unit and added, "'Raijou' should get a chance to start a new life.
I'm the one that told them about this mission, so I feel somewhat responsible." Then the cryo-tank was
sealed and Raijou drifted back into a dreamless sleap.

October 2, 29477, 01:42, a man stepped out onto a new planet. This place was new, fresh. It would be
years before the residual effects of extended cryo-sleep, and his past injuries would be recovered. He
would have to start from scratch, recondition his body, and mind to wht it once was. He squinted as
the sunlight hit his eyes after so long. "Guess this world is home now, for good or bad." With that statment
and the few items and credits that were given to him, he strode out into a city named Newland, to start his
new life.
