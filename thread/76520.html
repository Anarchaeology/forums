MA's make a difference in Notum Wars?
I had no intentions of buying Booster but since my guild base was really in a pinch for tower coverage i decided to do by far the most challenging quest FC has ever implemented........hunting for the damn booster.After 6 failed attempts to locate one i got lucky and found a store that had 2 copies left,only a short 40 min drive to upstate NY too(I live in NJ).

Anyways.......shop checked out towers dropped and grace is up,RED went out excited and lookin for trouble.Lemme just say that in AO I AM an MA.I have a trader alt that i swore NEVER to PVP with since that would make me a hypocrite(everyone knows im not a big fan of the debuffs),so everything i did in NW was as MA.First stop:Legion's base in East DAV...we knew we couldnt take it with the handful of peeps we went with,our only intention was to keep Legion busy and outa Storms base war.Still the PvP was pretty constant,we managed to blow up at least 1 of their guardian towers and got a solar turret to 50% before we had to fall back outa their base,and to my surprise i actually stayed alive the whole time(well i died once,running like a sissy baby init debuffed half way across the friggin zone with 3 peeps on me....I bet they were lost as hell after they killed me tho)
.Ive heard all the horror stories about us...so i havent bothered to PvP since 14.6....I was damned surprised that i was havin as much fun as i was,tryin out those goofy"make u wiggle"first aid kits and the virus purge items.

After our group got sent home =\,we took out some abandoned base in north DAV and then headed to the big war in east BF.OP4,Diversity,RED,and Apoc showed up at the biggest part of it.Kind of a shortage of high lv omni's there tho.As bad of a hit we took in PvP,there was NO shortage of MA's there.From start to finish it was a blast.I did it all fought blinded,snared,ran a little(actually swam a little,thx Kirousima =p )remember getting 2 kills too.Had a good 1 on 1 with Nyleve...actually got im down to 20% and chased to turrets where i got blasted by them =),and fully soloed a heavy missle turret ql150 to divert damage from people right next to it attackin the controll tower.

Basically a "not as gimped as he thought"MA did his part,made a difference and had more fun than he thought possible in AO.Don get me wrong we still suk bad in PvP overall(especially in duels),but not so much that we still cant have a blast with these raids.Still need more crit chance tho to actually make people worry when we attack.If Fc coult make PvP critchance 75% instead of 50% we would be perfect.Just about all the other classes use non crit weapons now anyways and wont really get the benefit that MA's need right now

Hell,only reason i even posted this was cause i was the biggest FC critic after 14.6,was reeeeeaaal close to quittin,and wound up doin a total 180 after the great time i had in the past 24 hrs.

Well, I didnt have time to read that speech, so im just going to answer your basic question, do MA's make a difference...

YES

Get a good bow and you are set, thats really all there is too it. I recommend the Pow bow for Alpha'ing AS and unequiping it (0.1 sec equip time) and going brawl and dimach. Has fast AS recharge so you can do that often.

Tonight, my guild raided a Clan base, I was doing somewhere around 400-700 damage on the Towers with my QL180 Pow Bow, then the clanners started pouring in, and my first AS was on an NT:

1.5k Aimshot

Unequip

600 brawl

1.1k Dimach

And with the help of some other team mates he was dead even when he threw NS up.

The key to NW fighting is speed (both in the assault group and your own physical speed) range, and numbers.

Enfs are the only ones who can survive in NW in melee combat, and they usually die first. MA's CANNOT go all melee if they wish to be a threat or anything but an annoyance.

GO BOW
