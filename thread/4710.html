Need some new mission types!
Well, I just wanted to say I always feel like a hero when I get sent out to kill someone for a nice (insert The Price Is Right's MC voice here) A New pair of sunglasses! Or have a chance to save the clans from a terrible new threat by finding the new prototype water balloon, but I'm ready to take it to a new level. Can we get some new mission objectives please? Let me suggest a few:

1. Mugging: Find so and so at such and such a place and mug him. Mugging wouldn't involve killing him necessarily, you just need to beat him around enough to get him to surrender with a gesture. Then he gives you his money and you have MUGGED him.

2. Raping: So and so is getting uppty. go teach him/her a lesson. Raping again could involve gestures and we could get some pretty stimulating visuals here.

3. Vandalize: well we can see how this can work. You could be given a special can of spray paint that would work with 1 charge and spray something clever. You need to find the statue of Buffy Summers or something and spray paint it.

4. Kill an endangered species: make several unique looking mobs, Mission description: go to such and such a place, there has been a sighting of what is believed to be a family of the last of the Apudainto bears, as you know their pelts are very valuable, please speed there at once and kill them and take their pelts back to the MT for a reward, some nice fur boots.

Anyway just wanted to say that the story and the missions really give me a sense of purpose and direction, and with these few refinements I think you could really take the "sense of heroism" in playing the game to a new level.
