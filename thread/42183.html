Harassment
I have seen far too many times where someone is minding their own business, talking with a friend, and another player comes along and begins to harass them. Yes... harass them. Whether it be with spammy vicinity talk, or standing on someone and doing /emotes, it is all a form of harassment.

Just the other night I witnessed a new form of harassment, one that I personally will not tolerate in the game. Luckily for the player doing the harassing, it was not me the 'conversation' was aimed at, instead it was a friend of mine.

It happens that a friend of mine in game, a male character, enjoys dressing in social clothing of both sexes. Yes I know, there are lots of males and atroxes out there who run around in thongs and tattoos, and I say more power to them... but in the case of my friend I have now witnessed not once, not twice, but three times that people have begun to harass him for the way he dresses... not just the manner of the clothing, but lewd comments about his sexuality and sexual acts. Unfortunately my friend is not a native to the English language, and thus when speaking comes across a bit less intelligent then the person really is... which adds to the harassment my friend receives.

I am not going to name names here, you know who you are and you know what you have done. The point of this post is to all those people out there who hear this harassment. Don't ignore it, don't look the other way. Stand up for a fellow human being and /petition about it! Let the GMs and ARKs know that this is happening in their game! If enough of us take a stand against harassment of any kind, perhaps Funcom will enpower the ARKS and GMs to actually -DO- something about it.

It's no fun to play a game and be harassed... it has to stop.

STAND UP FOR YOUR RIGHTS!
