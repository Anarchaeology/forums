Basically a critical hit is the biggest damage dealing you can do with your weapon or fist, those extra large supersized hits, Its when you see

Hit leet for: 13,23,23,23,14,13,450,23,23,23,14,13,23,23,23,14, 450.

The 450's are a critical hit. Each time an attack hits it has a certain chance of landing a critical hit, lets us say that in all normal circumstances the chance is 10%.

Now MA's as far as I know have 2 crit buffs. The first one is called Subconsious Guidance(SubG) and the second Vunerability Seeker (VS)

Now lets say my MA casts SubG on you, you know have a 18% chance of getting a critical hit. With VS the chance is 23% I believe. I hope this helps and if any of this information is incorrect please correct me.
