Council of Truth Open Panel Discussion in West Athen!
Greetings once again fellow Rubikans!

My latest investigative report will bring you the details on a Council of Truth Open Panel Discussion that took place wednesday night.

The gathering was called by Commander &quot;Caid&quot; Nottoris , Advisor of First Light Clan , Clan Leader Jenae &quot;Tussa&quot; Godfray from Assembly Clan , Clan Leader Anthony &quot;Cogs&quot; Mcduff from Cerberus Clan and Clan Leader Cathern &quot;Vixentrox&quot; Flowers from Whisper`s Edge Clan and it took place in an abandoned fort in the West Athen area.

Fellow clan leaders and their officers were invited to participate.

I thank Miss Jenae "Tussa" Godfray and Mr. Giga "Bizzarro" Vehementi who provided me recorded logs of this meeting.
