walking out of the bathroom of his flat, Cedric sees the flashing light on one of his terminals, set up to display a alarm at any number of keywords and events in the gridfeeds.

Walking away he crosses to the fridge, wrapping the towel around his hips before reaching into it for a can of smudge cola, popping the tab and taking a deep drink as he walks over to the grid-terminal and sits down, fingers flying over the keys.

"Sumokan, I see you have now gone out to publically take responsibility for your and your guild's actions in hiring these mercenaries.
Well, I guess that is good, though I wonder why you did not do so before.

To defend neutrals against the Sentinels, I would have said to hire the mercenaries to go after the Sentinels themselves. The old saying of ' The best defence is a...' and so on."

Fingerst tap for a moment against the metaplast of the portable terminal beside the one he is using, while the other hand reaches for the can of drink, another gulp, before the typing resumes at a fast pace.

"Ofcourse, not being a military man myself, the tactics of it might be beyond me..

The response this would bring from the clans against all neutrals must have been obvious though?

However, Personally I do not mind the mercenaries that much, though there are a few people's company I might miss, I can always see them in other places, as I never went to newland much myself. Though, I wonder, how much extra would it cost for them to keep Omni's out of Newland as well? Would you talk to Loren to find out?

As to guild reactions. I do ofcourse not speak for my own guild, Desert Winds, for an offical guild response towards this, you would have to contact Naxilite. However as Relations between us has always been favourable, I expect them to remain so more or less.

We need no curse, we already live in interesting times.."

A soft chuckles comes from him and he shakes his head with amusement as he hits the 'send' button. A moment later he hears a sleepy mutter from the direction of his bedroom, and a sleepy voice calling out "Cedric...get back in here..."

"Damn girl, you trying to kill me by exhaustion?"

A soft chuckles is heard as the door closes behind him.
