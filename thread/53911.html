Order of Corporate Templars Disbands
One of Rubi-Ka's oldest and largest guilds disbanded only one short week ago. The Order of Corporate Templars was formed back in the golden age of the settlement of rubi-ka, outside of the city of Rome a group of friends determined to make a difference on this new world, made their intentions substance when they formed the Order of Corporate Templars. It did not take long for those of like mind to find them, growing the organization to one of the largest on the planet.
Even through the mass exodus of people from Rubi-Ka during the days of Reclaim failure and unstable enviroments, the Order continued, serving Omni-Tek as was demanded.
Then with the Terraforming back under control, and the resurgance of population, the Order of Corporate Templars found their ranks swelling, till they were the largest guild on the planet.
But as things change on Rubi-Ka, the Templars found themselves alone. So in a quiet meeting in a small club just outside of Omni 1, the leaders of the Templars made their decision. It was time to call and end, after years of service to Omni-Tek, the fathering of several off shoot guilds, it was time for the Rubi-Ka Sect to go their own ways.
