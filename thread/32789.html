Okay,

This has been a burr in my kiester since about level 25 or so to me.

(I know, that's a bit early to be running up to WW, but you are talking to the person that used to run up to PW in his boxers at level 15 when he got bored, screaming "EAT ME! I'M TASTY" when he got aggroed by anything nasty.)

I LOVE maps. I love to see where I'm going, and where I've been. I like to know how far I am from the zone border at any given time. Not having a map pisses me off. Not being able to GET a map makes me furious.

Right now, two things particularly piss me off

1) No map of Wailing Wastes exists outside of Clan Startup. This is frankly stupid. I mean C'MON, people! You can't tell me that Joe Adventurer for Rand McNally hasn't come to Rubi-ka, mapped the entire planet, and is selling the maps clear across the universe.

2) The map for Galway County (Inluded in OT startup) costs 600 Map Nav for anyone else. 600! What's so important or special about Galway that it takes 600 Map Nav? I mean, by the time you get it, you can walk through Galway naked and bare handed and smeg anything that blinks at you funny in one hit (well, except Best In Brass).

Here is my opinion on maps in general.

1) There should be various QL of maps, with various levels of detail and Map Nav costs. The earliest should have BASIC layout (eg, land and water at the most) and as you get higher QL maps that cost more Map Nav, you get more detail. (Add forest here, add roads towns, etc. At the highest QL, maybe add markers of the names of cities towns, important sites, and maybe have a clickable on/off grid showing every 100 or 250 coords.

2) You should be able to make maps as a Tradeskill Item. You get a item such as a mapping tool, equip it in your utilities and as you walk/run/crawl/flap/fly through an area, it maps the surrounding area. Once you've crawled around an entire zone, you take that tool, combine it with a "data emitter", use trade skill implements to create a "empty map crystal" use the crystal on the data emitter and BINGO! A homemade map. THis is especially good with the QL idea above.

This would be particularly good, and a potential source of profit for, adventurers. Gives them a reason to go out and Explore Rubi-ka, which really should be the core of the adventurers.
