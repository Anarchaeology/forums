I have to say, I am looking forward to this expansion. Perks, specializations, a new city? They all sound fun to me, and I want to try them.

As someone who recently came to this game from a competitor, I have to say that it is really fun, and special. I played a lot of Asheron's Call, especially after the expansion came out for it (I had to have a house...). The transitions weren't always smooth for expansion players and non-expansion owners, but some planning usually helped in that respect. Of course people were jealous because they couldn't get new lands or buy houses, but if they really wanted them, they bought the expansion. There was still plenty of gameplay without them. I doubt Funcom is going to do any different in that regard.

A game like this survives by being visible. It is not an accident that Everquest has so many players, for a game that isn't all that great (in my opinion, anyway). It is constantly putting new boxes on store shelves, where consumers see it when buying new things. From a marketing perspective, not having a new box on the shelves every so often is suicide, and Funcom has to be smart enough to understand that.

An expansion pack is for new content, not bug fixes. It doesn't sound like just a little bit of new content, either. Until we can live in a more perfect, less money hungry world, it isn't cheap to develop new content. Since Funcom is apparently committed to adding new content with their regular patches, as well as making this expansion, I see this as a good deal. Unless folks would like to see the expansion pack goodies just doled out to them over the next couple of years, as time allows.

Anyway, just a few thoughts....
