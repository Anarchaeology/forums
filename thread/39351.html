Dangers Dune :

Start from Athen ---> East gate, you're now in Warthorn Valley ---> Go North, following the road to Aegan.

Danger Dune is at Aegan, just after zoning, it's right on your left.

Just Beware the Desert Eremite, it's aggro and it's tough. You don't stand a chance at your level so if he aggroes, just run over

There are scooplets here to kill for dodga not bad drop
