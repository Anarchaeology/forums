Of course it's possible. The Clans left a sour taste in the mouths of many a Neutral. Even those who don't condone the presence of their 'guests' in NLC have heard of the repeated aggressions against them or their neighbors.

I as a Neutral have always worked hard to seperate the conflict between the Clans and Omni-Tek in my mind. It was and IS their fight. Not mine. The current situation stems from actions completely seperate from friction between the warring factions. This is about being pissed on and disrespected by high profile Clan organizations. Disillusionment is a key theme in my character's makeup.
