A friend is missing...
Ah, another busy evening at Rompa... she made her way slowly through the masses of people. Everybody laughing, drinking, dancing, having a great time. She smiled. A girl bumped into her, her wine sloshing over the rim of her glass, just missing Kiyoko's jacket. "Oops, sorry". Kiyoko just laughed and kept walking.

She spotted Sejanus sitting by himself at booth. "Alex", she greeted him, "how are you?" "Fine, as always", he said. He had such a cheerful disposition, she really liked that about him. She slid into a seat nearby, giving a sigh of relief as she did. She was only three months along. Six to go. Ugh, that might as well be six years. She shrugged away the thought, smiling at her friend. He opened his mouth to say something, but just then one of the waitresses came up, asked if they needed anything. Kiyoko ordered a cup of coffee. "I'm fine", Sejanus indicated his cup, still full and steaming.

The waitress wandered away, and Alex leaned forward again. "Have you spoken to Jaxxa lately?" She frowned, trying to recall the last time she'd seen her. "Not in awhile... when Edwin and I got back from our honeymoon, she was away on assignment. Hmm... I'd say it's been about a month since the last time I talked to her. Why?"

He looked worried. "It's been about as long since I've spoken to her, as well. Things have been so busy, with the CoT falling to pieces, and the Tir Accord collapsing... I've just gotten caught up on paperwork, press releases, all that stuff". Kiyoko nodded, and he continued. "But I finally had some free time, tried to contact her, no answer. Not even voice mail".

She smiled reassuringly. Im sure shes all right. Shes probably just really busy, she said, with more conviction than she actually felt. In fact, she had some misgivings. Jaxxa was always busy, but never too busy to call her friends. She made a mental note to swing by Jaxxas apartment later. I think Ill stop by her place after I leave here, said Sejanus, echoing her thoughts. Just to make sure.

And off we go...
The two friends continued to chat, sipping their coffee and catching each other up on the events in their lives. Catching up to degree anyway, as Sejanus was always indirect and vague on the details of his personal and professional life. "Best to keep a low profile and not draw attention to your actions" he had recommended to an old Fixer acquaintence. He grinned lightly over his cup of coffee, Kiyoko's irrepressible and persistent curiosity being visibly frustrated by his vauge comments about a "complicated" personal life or late impromptu meetings in HQ. The fact that Kiyoko hadn't heard from Jaxxa in weeks either floated to the center of his attention as they talked. Four weeks was a long time to be on mission, especially for someone still "young" in the profession of an Omni agent like Jaxxa. He allowed himself a moment of pride at how well she had been working out since her return to her Company family, rising quickly through the ranks. He would occasionally mention her latest award or rise in rank in passing to his superior if he had heard from her the day before and it was always greeted with small grin from the man behind the desk. The current situation up north had kept them all busy, trying to keep bees in the hive without getting too badly stung by the bees. The fact that he hadn't heard from her in a while had been lost in all the confusion and activity. There had been no word of trouble from any of his sources or the operatives that he had been loaned. The hackers that had been plaguing them had seemingly "run to ground." A loose end he wasn't particularly happy about.

His comm-link buzzed, bringing him back to the moment. "Sorry Kiyoko, let me get this a second." She was currently being surrounded by some attention starved talking Leets seeking snacks, shiny objects and petting...not necessarily in that order...and indicated it was ok.

It was his old hunting comrad Phetia, comm-calling for a minute between mission to impart a tiny story from her latest adventure. Another beep sounded on his comm...then another.... "Good grief, it never fails. Answer one call and then EVERYONE tries to call you at once." Finishing the call with Phe he switched over to check the messages he missed. He smiled at the silky voice and suggestive nature of the first message, lightening his mood greatly. Then, calling up the second message, he was brought back to solid ground. He looked at his watch, sighing at the lateness of the hour and being greatful that he'd been getting his caffeine fix.

"Sorry Kiyoko, looks like it's back to work for me. Looks like I'm going to miss seeing Edwin. Give him my best regards, we'll have to try to all get together sometime. I'll swing by Jaxxa's and let you know what I findout. Please let me know if you hear anything."

Standing up to leave he handed one of the more presistent Leets that had gathered a shiney OmniExecutive pen and chuckled as the small creature squeeked "PHAT L00t!" and ran to hide it's new found treasure behind one of the bar's couches. Saying his goodbyes in the bar he headed off to Omni-HQ, taking the long way, though the neighborhood in Rome where Jaxxa lived. Making his way through the highrise to her apartment door he paid close attention to every detail of the path he took looking for anything even slightly unusual. The elevator...the walkway...the plants in the back yard...the ever vigilant Omni Guide...the two level 1 Nano-Technicians standing on the table taking turns mooning and grabbing their crotch at each other...nothing out of the ordinary or su****ious. Pressing the door chime he waited...and waited. He pressed the chime a few more times without response. Calling up security records on his comm-link it indicated that the door to her apartment hadn't been activated in almost a month, around the time he and Kiyoko had last heard from her.

"Hmmmmm..." he said aloud to himself, Weighing whether he should have OmniPol open the door. At that moment his superior buzzed him again, wondering if someone had moved Omni-Ent (and thus the bar where he had been sitting) to the other side of Rubi-Ka without telling him. Sejanus chuckled lightly "Uh, no sir. Sorry, I got a little side tracked. I'm on my way."

Sejanus reluctanly left the highrise and headed to HQ. He made a mental note to talk to his aid Tataglia at the first free moment about getting her door lock discreatly "popped". Depending on what he found he would start checking with her recent known contacts. "Hmmmm...she had talked about joining SPARTA with alot of enthusiasm," he thought to himself. "I wonder if Silvestrini has her doing something hush-hush...better give him a call before we go knocking over tables and digging in people's garbage. Need to thank him for Miss Deminzia's help earlier in any case."

About a month ago...
Gridgrinder had been driving for about an hour, getting gradually closer to the small blinking spot on his datapad. He happened to glance to his left, when he quickly looked back and tried to get the car to move faster. It was a spider. One of those real nasty, six foot long, bloody toothed spider. As he sped on, the creature didn't notice him, and slowly shuffled in the opposite direction. Grindgrinder pressed on, almost reaching the 2 hour mark. Taking another look at his datapad, he realized he should be right on top of it, and quickly realized what he was seeing as the huge boulder he was staring at slightly flickered.
"Hologram PD," he mumbled to himself. he hopped out of his car, and reaching into his pocket, pulled out a small silver sphere. He tapped a small button on it, and threw it towards the rock. After a flash of light and the sound of electrical short out, the boulder dissipated and a small, somewhat shabby lab appeared.
"Nice place,"-he thought as he went to work on the door with his lockpick.

Gridgrinder snuck quietly through the building, but soon he realized no one was there, and began to explore the place more thoroughly. looking around, it seemed like a basic lab, if not a bit messy. papers and forms were strewn about, tools were still lying on the operating table. No glasses. Whoever was in here was definitely trying to leave somewhat hurriedly, but he couldn't put his finger on why. The next instant he heard someone coming.

"Hello there, fellow Fixer," a tall, slim Solitus said to him with a grin. "I figured you'd be here." Gridgrinder was desperately trying to put his mind back in focus after this empty lab had suddenly procured a person exspecting to see him.
"Who-"
"Well, Mr. Gruesbeck, it's a pleasure to finally meet my target in person." Gridgrinder slowy figured it out.
"You-"
"Yes. I was hired by a member of Omni-Admin seeking to purge his datafiles of a very conniving hacker. And you're him."
"I was going to say," started Gridgrinder again,
"that you're pretty damn rude."
"Ha. It really will excellent when that is used to show your general disrespect for authority. But, to try you, I suppose we'll have to arrest you first. Have fun at Omni-pol prison, and be sure to send me a GridCard," the solitus said with a sickening smile, and pressed a small device, sending a signal to Omni-Pol. In a few seconds, a small task force had come in through the door, weapons all pointed at Grid.
"Put your hands in the air scum!" a rather tall Atrox weilding a rather nasty Nova Flow shouted. Gridgrinder felt this somewhat conflicted with his plan, and slipped one of the nano bot pills he had constructed earlier between his fingers. His hands slowly rose above his head, and when they were fully outstretched, he spread his fingers, and the 3" pill fell almost silently to the ground. Then, Gridgrinder began executing a nanoprogram as everything went black.

Gridgrinder smiled to himself as he ran from the building, the taskforce right behind him as their blindness nanoprogram wore off. He kept speeding along, slowing down periodically to let the task force catch up. they cleary hadn't traveled without a Yalm in a long while. He reached the spot he was thinking of, a cliff at the edge of a large lake. pulling out his comlink and deleting it of all 'important' data, he waited for the Omnis to catch up. He wanted somebody to see this. They were within firing distance, which was definitely close enough for the Fixer. He kept running, and, looking as if he stumbled, promptly fell off the cliff.

As the task force made it to the base of the cliff, they searched near where he jumped, though they knew no one could survive the fall. At the edge of the water, they found a cracked, beaten up comm-link which displayed "Lazaro 'Gridgrinder' Gruesbeck" on the screen, and slowly began to crackle and silently shorted out.

Gridgrinder was traveling through the Grid, on his way to the entrance to the City of Home, one his more prefered areas to stay in. He wanted to be somewhere nice when he did this. Reaching the node, re uploaded himself back to reality, and quickly walked back over to the terminal, re-encoding himself into the Rift. The Fixer situated himself near Omni-HQ, where he'd get the best accesibility to incoming datastreams. He opened his personal file, and began what would quite possibly the last hacking job 'Gridgrinder' would ever do.


Well, now that *that's* out of the way, let's get back to the present..

One letter. It was all he needed, one letter and a new last name, and Lazaro 'Gridgrinderr' Pillot had gone undectected by anyone worth noting for nearly a month. 'Lay low, don't attract attention', was the plan he'd been sticking to that whole time. He cursed to himself again as he remembered that time after dying soon after his new identity's creation, where a small coding error caused him to lose all of his ranks, credits, and items. He was getting on well enough though. He liked having the 'inside information' that made starting out in the company so much more simple. As he walked through the streets of Rome, he accessed the homepage of his still favorite news source, GridNet. Not seeing anything of real value, he decided to do his first hacking work in a while. Taking a more 'detailed' look in to GridNet's archives, he noticed a story that was apparently never put on the news reel, but was definitely an interesting read. He saw the title, 'Returning Child of Omni-tek not seen for two weeks, rumors begin to speculate.'
Going further, he checked Jaxxa's file, grinning as he tried to remembr the last time he'd done it. His grin quickly subsided, as he saw that she hadn't accessed her credit account for nearly a month. If he knew her as well as he thought it did, something was definitely wrong. checking further, he saw that she hadn't sent any messages to anyone in that time either, nor had her appartment door or anything inside been accessed for awhile. Her mission requests were three pages long. Something was definitly up. He looked up her apartment number, and was soon on his way towards her highrise.

Stiffling another yawn, Jen raised her head, slightly stirring while looking at the ceiling above. Rompa's was deserted at this hour of the morning, but the call of the coffee had been stronger than the desire to stay at home - and Rompa's coffee was a damn good one. The booth where she was sitting, at the rear of the room, was only half-lit by the lava lamp on the table in front of her, and the faint light of the term display on her knees. Yes, a quiet place, but also a good one in case she'd have to meet people or conduct other unexpected kinds of business, not counting the fact that she didn't like to use her own flat when she had to remotely monitor the Grid.

To tell the truth, the last weeks had been quite a hasle. Running here and there and conducting all these extra tasks had finally left her exhausted - living on cigarettes and coffee for almost two weeks was never a good thing, even when sustained by nano-bots - and once again, she had had to find a good Engineer to help her with her Yalm, after she had been caught off-guard by hidden Patrollers near West Athen's gates while on her road to see the one they used to call 'the Foreman'. She still had the feeling that her head was full of algorithms, of formulas, and of this awful mindset she had to conform to to push Shermann's coding template to its best efficiency. Maybe that was the reason why she was here at the moment. Maybe that was why Thompon's call had almost been a relief, the night before, bringing her attention on matters that had seemingly settled down.

The Jaxxa woman. Some obscure records found within the Grid had shown that she had taken a shuttle, almost three weeks ago, and simply left Rubi-Ka; records however could easily be modified or easily implemented... and what would a 'Ka native, without known living family, do off-world all of a sudden? Unless she had been able to lay hands on hidden information that could have caught her attention... it was more likely that Danica Craddock had disappeared, and the fact that she had been in contact with the boss contributed to enforce the feeling; you didn't disappear like that when applying for a job. Jen cursed herself for a second about this; due to more important work matters, she hadn't been able to really investigate the case at that time, and it may have been too late now to really do something. But the Grid didn't forget information; maybe searching would be long, yet sooner or later she'd bump into something more or less related, and follow this new thread.

The Fixer took another sip of coffee - what had Raq called it? Her 'poison'? The Agent may be right, after all... - and turned her attention back on her portable term. The last news, and also, news that were a little older. News from the last three or four weeks. News from various daily Gridfeeds, including the Clanner ones, which also holded their lot of useful information, once cleared of their usual propaganda and call to arms. Ah, yes, the crumble of the Council of truth, the disbanding of the Clanners' 'government', the odd events around Spiva and Solari, the Dusters once again involved making her grind her teeth and swear that one day, one day, some people would be sorry for simply having been born... that had been a mess.

"Hear, hear", she muttered for herself. "The ICC's gonna beat the crap outta this rock o' terraformed sand if nuthin's been done. Dammit, where's this report I found the other day?..." Jen slightly frowned, her muttering fading off until she was silent again, her thoughts drifiting further and further. Sejanus had briefly mentioned the two hackers again, and it had been hard not to chuckle at this, but now she was browsing through more secured files, she didn't feel like laughing at all.

She suddenly realized she hadn't slept the last night at all - in fact, she wasn't even able to say when was the last time she had managed to really sleep. Yet once again, the thought was troubled, forced to drift by another fact. Someone was dead, someone whose name she hadn't forgotten, though the boy... the boy had been damn discrete in the last weeks. The Fixer frowned again, and silently cursed between her teeth. There was a chance that it wasn't related... but given the date, the last date he had been seen alive... and the date when the death report had been released... all of this compared to Jaxxa's vanishing from the planet... now that was too convenient for her to accept without at least a few questions.

"Can it be..."

Another set of informations displayed on the term. Information easy to find, easy to tap into, for her at least; and if Sejanus found it, well, screw him - she was meant to ivnestigate, and she'd do it accordin to her own methods. Jaxxa's file. Credit account. What she had bought. When. Last time she had taken on an assignment. Last time she had completed one.

How much time the files had been accessed. By whom they had been accessed. Bank. Various organisms, such as the OT-division allowed to send the assignments locations and data. A few other people. Calls from Sejanus, from Kiyoko Grebel (true, she was Edwin's wife now...). Something elose, someone that had left a faint trace, but no call, no message... nothing else than simply... checking. This method... Ah, this method wasn't unknown to her.

"I can't believe it. He can't be!" Jen firmly muttered, then realized she was talking alone again, and looked around. No one was around, and she let another sigh.

"Fixers don't die. You know this..."

She slightly smiled, and nodded. Someone like him couldn't die that easily. She'd have to look at this closer. If it was related... if he had anything to do with Jaxxa's disappearance... then it'd be time to use more... coercitive means. She was becoming quite talented at this, though her methods weren't as direct as the boss' ones.

"You want me to do a scan search in the Rift? It's the best place to do it..; and maybe Gridman has seen something, too."

"Yeah, please. Do so. I wanna find him again. I guess... I guess it's time we talk to each other. Really talk, I mean."

Jen smiled for herself again, letting her fingers rest for a few seconds against the gun at her waist, hidden under her coat. A nie gun, she had to admit. Light. Fast. Deadly. She somewhat regretted her old Kerans, but the Ejector was definitely a weapon of choice now.

She wished she wouldn't have to use it. But she knew that if needed, then she wouldn't hesitate a second.

The last time she had met him... when had it been? One month ago? Two? Before Jaxxa's disappearance, for sure. Yes... surely that was the same evening she had met the young Agent in the Grid. The evening they had all spent in Rompa's. Ahh, Rompa's. Definitely one of the best places for this kind of... meetings.

Jen was now realizing that even after so much time, even now he was hiding like he did, she was still so keen on making him feel awkward.

Lazaro "Gridgrinderr" Pillot. The similarity of both callsigns was already in itself a wonder, but there was no mistake possible. This face... these long dark hair... this attitude the small, slim Opifex had been displaying... the voice, even!... Bumping into him like this in Rompa's, just the evening before, almost exactly like the first time, had been a wonderful luck, as well as another opportunity to test him. She had to admit that maybe - maybe - she had had to calm her nerves on someone, or something, after this odd conversation she had interecepted, after the discussion with the Engineer who had sat at Raquel's booth a few minutes before she joined them with the Boss... after the crap going on with Lupoh again, the way he had had to look at her, different than... this damn Clanner had dared threatening her, after all! In the middle of Rompa's, surrounded by other SPARTAns, and all he could have come up with was to threaten her. As if she could care more. "Laugh it up now Demenzia, while you can"... She had already received so many threats from Clanners, after all, one of the most recent ones having been Rezillo's, that she really couldn't care less tonight.

What odds had even made her offer 'Grindgrinderr' a coffee... Jen wouldn't know, but what she'd never forget was the shocked look on his face, when she had casually walked by him, befoe she waved her hand with an innocent "Y'know, it's weird, but ya really remind me of someone, boy. Really...".

Oh, he had acted the innocent-looking boy very well, but their conversation, from the beginning to the end, had been held on two levels, which Rompa's customers had fortunately been unaware of. he couldn't be trying to persuade her that he wasn't Gruesbeck - he couldn't, because he had recognized her, and knew, from the looks they had exchanged for a brief second, that she had spotted his face even in the middle of the bar's crowd, and walked to him on purpose.

Oh, maybe she could have been deceived. But Heiji hadn't. And when the both of them would agree on something, the chances of it being wrong were dropping at an alarming rate.

"Matching DNA scans, Dee. No doubt.... no doubt." And she had silently nodded, for he was right. Heiji was able to make so many things within the Grid, and to find so many interesting information, even when she didn't ask him. And he was behaving more kindly with her, also, since the few last weeks, though, oddly, she couldn't really tell why.

"It's good to know yer not this person, in fact, y'see... Cause if ya were...I guess I'd really need ta have a word with ya. A long word, y'see. A very long one", she had whispered to him, as they were enjoying their cups of coffee. An odd conversation, full of understatements and hidden threats; she was enjoying it even far more than her drink, and this also because she could see, in his eyes, on his face, that himself wasn't.The Fixer smiled to herself when this new Lazaro tried to change subject, and asked her about her guns. Exactly the right thing to ask - or the wrong one, depending on the point of view.

"Ah. These. Yeah, yer right. Gamma Ejectors. Ya know why I love these guns? They're fast. Light. Precise. Powerful. A single shot, and it can burn a whole face until no one can recognize it. A simple burst... an' there's nuthing left of ya. Of course, dat's if yer lucky. If yer not, ya can... take a few more hours to die from the wounds. See?"

Gruesbeck - or rather, 'Pillot', now - was too talented, and too aware of his own talent, to give up just because of a few threats. She had to convince him that she knew - that whatever he did, she'd knew too, again and again, until he couldn't escape anymore. And to convince him that she was a threat, a real one, made even more dangerous by the fact that she hadn't stroke at him when she could had done so... seemingly waiting, waiting until...

"Y'know. I'm after someone. Someone who's talented enuff not to have been caught... yet. An' I intend on catchin' 'im someday. Whatever I need ta do it."

"Darn it. It's time to leave", the Fixer muttered, half for herself, knowing that she still had a certain amount of things to check. No. And arresting him herself... well, as the boss was regularly forced to repeat, over and over, SPARTA wasn't a police or military force.
