When the trader charms were first added, this was heavily debated on the forums. (Debated = Funcom was flamed for it. ;)) I believe the final answer is that Funcom wants to add additional functionality to the trader charms at some point in the future, but that it's not going to be easy so it's not going to happen anytime soon. Several possiblities were discussed, including having the player actually swap bodies with the mob, letting him or her control the charmed mob using regular player controls, while leaving his own body behind to stand motionless until the nano wore off or his charmed body got killed. That sounds pretty cool! They also mentioned needing to fix the bad effect not wearing off for the duration, but again that was coming at a later date.

The upside is that the swap psyche line is still one of the best 11th hour survival nanos in the game as long as you keep them up to date. Considering the fact that you can /pet terminate the charmed mob... Just make sure you fight stupid stuff that has low psychic. :)
