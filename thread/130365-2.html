Phear Leet
Gestava shut off the computer screen and looked out of his window.
It was pouring down today. Rome was barely visible through the massive amounts of water coming down.

Sparta was gone. He had just read it in his weekly summarised Omni-tek news report.
Gestava shook his head. In itself it wasnt a particularly dramatic event. Sparta was not a big department. And even though Garret was definately one of the most accomplished leaders and politician he'd met, Omni-tek RK would find someone else to replace him as well.

No. What concerned Gestava was that this was far from an isolated occurance. Omni-tek was losing experienced personell. Fast.
Much faster than was acceptable. Much faster than they could be replaced.

Not that he blamed them for being disillusioned.
While Omni-tek as a whole was undoubtedly a strong an incomprihensibly strong entity, the Rubi-Ka department was increasingly looking like a hollow shell.

Even his own commitment had started to wane as of late. He had no plans of leaving anytime soon, but he couldnt help but be dissapointed in the lack of activity from Omni-tek leadership.

No...he would stay...for a while. Give Omni-tek a chance to show that they were more than a hollow shell. And if it turned out that they in fact were...well...there was always work for a mercenary...he had heard Sol Banking was hiring a lot lately...

As day turned into night Gestava sat watching the city of Rome. Watched as the lights came on turning the structures into towers of light.
Indeed...he wouldnt like to leave if he could help it...
