OOC:

Well, it is not entirely true that WE is mostly made up of anti-level whores. I moved along at a rather fast pace during the last few weeks and am now 133. I am constantly looking to team up with some of the RPers, and did so on occasion.

Back to the original question of this thread:

I have noticed something interesting while moving up from level 78 till here during the last few weeks. As I continued to be on the looking for teams to go hunting, I built a wider base of friends and buddies with whom I would team on a regular basis. Not that I would actually sending them pm's asking wether they want to team up. It is more like there are only so many players in my level range and we all seemed to move on at oughly about the same pace. So it happens very often that I accept an invitation into a random team only to notice that I have teamed with some of the members before.
And I have noticed that even with guys from non-RP guilds there is usually a big "hello" and "how have ya beend doing" before actually kicking off for the mission proper... and the longer I play the more people I get familiar with. So, even though the pace is generally frantic, levelling becomes more and more fun since you know your teammates already, know their style of hunting, actics etc. These teams work like a well greased machine, disciplined and very efficient. With that I usually find that we start taking time to relax a bit since no one hardly ever dies...
Agreed, this only works if you are a bit of a level-whore since it takes time to build up a base of acquaintances... but as I said, power-levelling can be fun this way.

On une utterly hilarious occasion, clan space was devoid of anyone who would start a team within my level range... so I decided to fly over to NLC and hover above the plaza in my yalm, so that the mercs wouldn't shoot me. I shouted that I was looking for a team and promptly got an invitation. On our way to the mish I found out to my great dismay, that I ended up with five omnis... the Doc was even from Omni-Pol!
I pointed out that I was Clan and asked wether they minded at all teaming with me. They didn't...
Once in the mish (which was a hard one), we basically spent the rest of the time shouting and laughing because we would almost always target each other instead of the mobs when hitting the tab key, creating a huge confusion....

Back to OOC teaming:
