Wen-Wen auto-fire
I find it really dumb to have to right click on my wen-wen every 15 seconds during a fight.

Let's face it : the Wen-wen is a weapon, so if you are in a fight, should it really stay over your shoulder and do nothing ?

Moreover, wen-wen have uber-crappy damage (My dual-wield guns each make at least 12 times more damage EACH) so in the end, looking after the Energy Weapon Skill Available message during a fight isn't worth bothering, and that makes the Wen-wen useless. Heck most of the time I don't even use it, for whole missions !

If wen-wen would fire automatically during a fight, they would still be utlra-crap, but at least that would be a little extra damage at no cost. THAT would be helpfull.

My level 57 "junior" wen-wen is equivalent to a 10-40 weapon with 1 shot magazine and 15 second recharge time. Not the kind of thing you'd count on to settle a fight.
