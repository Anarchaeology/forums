---> Great Day for RK3!!!!!!! <---
Hi all.

Today has been a really great day for Rubi-Ka 3 (German server)..

After one try with severals deaths we killed --> The General's Right Hand <-- in the second try!

Only one person died this time... She got aggroed after a burst, FA kombo and died faster than any doc could heal... Rest in peace Dragonladie :P

Main Tank was a lvl 200 Enforcer called Midnight, who was holding Aggro really well and had several Docs (Lazarus, Queen, Tenchi, Allenstein, Tesla and Sharalatan) healing her. Both from omni and clan. The engineers were doing a great job too, using the blind aura to help getting less damage....

Others were just shooting and doing damage... We had around 40 ppl helping... As I said from both sides of the conflict (since we hardly have 50 ppl above lvl 150)

To name all of them would probably be too much for this post, but I will try to name most of them as I cannot remember them all at the point....

I am not quite sure, but considering that on rk1 GRH was killed with crit bug (as far as I am informed), we are the first on rk3 to kill him in a "normal" way.
