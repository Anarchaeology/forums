3 on 3 in WTF.
Well last night an offer was made from some clanners - come to WTF dungeon and fight. 3-man teams, no outside buffing other than what your team can do.

So we took up the gaunlet, assembled the fighters and headed out to WTF dungeon.

The teams were:

Clan: Cyphienne , Jaxin and Jadoo

Omni: Isoldei , Gladtohearit and Littletza .


Alot of disgussing went back and forth regarding tactics. When everything was set, we moved into the dungeon...

And a long, hardfought battle followed. All the lag, LOS problems, player cunning, tactics, intensity, panic and the will to fight made this about the most fun I've had in a long while.

Thank you for the great idea Jadoo Perhaps this would be the starting point of a tournament?







Oh yeah, Omnis won
