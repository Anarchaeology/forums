Pet Pathing Solution + More
This may have been mentioned in the past. If it has, I apologize for the spam. This could help pet pathing and mission enjoyment for everyone.

I've read countless posts about how bad pet pathing is. I've fooled around with Engi/MP classes and discovered it can be horrible in missions.

When I stop and think about it, almost all complaints are directed at mission pathing...that i've seen anyway. But why should anything require 'fixing'? Why not just adjust a few things about the way missions are made? Instead of stressing over fixing pathing, make it not an issue.

I'll try to make this as short and to the point as possible.


Outline:

1. Open Area Missions
(a. Underground hangars
(b. Spacious caves
(c. Secluded gardens

2. Single Room/Area Missions
(a. Line 'em up, Shoot 'em down
(b. Ambush
(c. King of the Hill

3. Tradeskill/Mission reward items
(a. Short-range teleporter upgrades
(b. A.I. upgrades
(c. Pet usable devices/tools



-There are many variations to the ideas I have, but I narrowed it down to 3 in each category fore the sake of time-



1. Open Area Missions - Each mission shouldn't have to be in cramped quarters. If there was more space to move without worry of desks, walls, and doors, a pet's life of servitude would be much more simple.

(a. Underground hangars - The x-files comes to mind here. Large military type areas located underground with a high ceiling. This doesn't necessarily have to be one huge room. There can be partial dividers. For example: weapon container stacks, crates, building materials. Something that divides the space of the room, but leaves it far from being closed-in and cramped.

(b. Spacious caves - Yes! Larger underground caverns! The mission caves we have now are far to squished for pet users and the clostrophobic. They don't need to be extremely massive. The key here is not how many doors and walls you can have, but how you use the few that are there. Larger walls that divide more space and that are broken apart by larger doorways. Perhaps we don't even need walls, but chasms or large pools of stagnant water.

(c. Secluded gardens - All these inside missions make nature jealous. How about a nice above-ground mission? Maybe a nice garden with various plants and flowers. Bushes and small trees or hedges can serve as space dividers. Note that they are just dividing space, not closing in on us for hugs. It would do us good to have several more yards of breathing room at all sides.


2. Single Room/Area Missions - If we started and ended our mission without even leaving the room, there isn't much chance of our pets getting confused over how to walk through a door. This would be a blast for teams as well.

(a. Line 'em up, Shoot 'em down - What if we didn't just take a mission to go kill a bunch of mobs? What if they took a mission to come kill us? There is a new 'hot-spot' that the other side wants so they send troops to take it while you defend it. Once you get to your mission area/room you must kill waves of attackers. They can come in small amounts at once or a virtual horde, depending on your mission terminal settings.

(b. Ambush - You obtain secret info that a group of the opposition will be traveling through a certain area. You get there ahead of time and ambush them as they travel through. The variations on this are enormous.

(c. King of the Hill - How long can you last? It's all about Ego. This sort of mission would test your endurance. You issue a challenge of sorts to the opposition. Once you reach the mission area there will be a marked-off area that you must stay in. You must stay alive and not leave this designated area or you lose. If you stay alive and in the area, the opposition will eventually give up and go home to cry.


3. Tradeskill/Mission reward items - Sure, we have trimmers and the like, but are they really enough? There is so much unused potential in this area.

(a. Short-range teleporter upgrades - Why should our pets be bound to our way of movement? With this upgrade your pet would have the ability to teleport short distances. What does this mean? If you are looking at a mob in the next room and instruct your pet to attack, it doesn't simply run there...but *poofs* and teleports to the mob's location. This could work for calling your pet to your side as well. Now, this obviously has great potential for abuse. Limits would be needed, such as.... 1) A delay on the teleport time that is equal to the amount of time it would take to run there. 2) Your pet would teleport to the location when the command was given. It would not follow a target indefinately while in limbo. 3) It would be subject to Line of Sight when attacking, but could travel through walls if it was teleporting to your side (if you aren't in a fight).

(b. A.I. upgrades - Makes sense right? If you want your pet to be smarter then you upgrade its thinking. There are many ideas here, but I will just list a few. 1) Your pet would be able to reasonably decide what it could or could not kill. Example: Your pet would not charge a room with 3 red mobs, but would charge a room of 3 green mobs if you so chose. You could pre-decide these factors and input them into your pets cpu or brain. 2) Pets would be able to make mid-battle choices without help. Example: If you were fighting a mob and another mob attacked you (the pet owner), your pet would switch targets until it had the attention of the Add. You could adjust the settings on this so it would only switch if you dropped below a certain % of life or somesuch. You get the idea.

(c. Pet usable devices/tools - This could really add some flavor. It wouldn't be that special to just give your pet weapons that people use. If there were items that allowed your pets to perform 'moves' or 'special attacks' that no one else could do, then that Would be special. These wouldn't be more powerful than what players can do already. Here are a few examples. 1) Short Circuit: a special move that mechanical pets could perform that would 'shock' all enemies within the listed radius, damage + taunt maybe...or even a small debuff. 2) Lamentations: a special move for the MP attack pet. The target would be stunned by furious wailing and would sustain damage + taunt. This would have a good chance of breaking attacks and nano formulas being used/cast. The reuse time on these would be subject to balancing of course. There are many more ideas that could be created, no doubt.



Again let me say, there is so much more that could be listed under these categories. There is so much potential in this game, if it can only be implimented. I love sitting around and thinking up this stuff. Although I'm sure some of this is an influence of everyone else's input on other posts.

If this stuff was already posted by someone, sorry for claiming your idea. I wouldn't do it on purpose.

I would also appreciate any feedback, official or otherwise.
