The return
Silently he sat in his shuttle glansing out into space. He had been off planet for some time now. His head was full with thoughts, what's been going on down there and how has the conflict escalated? He was interupted from his thoughts when the pilot called out in the speaker "1 more hour until landing in Omni-HQ, please fasten your seatbelts, the ride might be a bit jumpy"
Ugluk grabbed his seatbelt and fasten them, while he waited he wiped some of the dust off his elite helmet.
He had been at a secret training camp too polish his combat skills a bit further, so he was ready to come back to Rubi-Ka.
He prayed that Ross had finally understod that Clan was a direct threat, and given the official word off war, then again Ross hadn't done much right so he had no real high expectations.
He sat there in the gloomy cabinett, stroking his gun with his right hand
"We have had alot of fun, you and me, he mumbled to himself, we have alot of work to do when we get back, you and I.
"We will be arriving shortly, hope you have had a nice trip Mr. Howryla" the speaker called out.
Once again Nehcron Howryla, aka Ugluk, was awoken from his thoughts. He took his elite helmet and shoved it over his head.
You can't be safe anywhere, was his motto, that's why he always was fully protected and armed at most occations.
He felt the bump of the carrier craft landing on the surface of Rubi-Ka and he unstrapped his seatbelts. He stood up and walked to the door which opened before him. Ugluk jumped down from the door too the solid iron plateform on which the ship had landed.
He glared around him and saw what he searched, his breathren, The Blackhand.
From beneath his helmet Ugluk grinned wickedly and walked to his family.
He was back.
