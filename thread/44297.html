Important information.
Last night, I found a highly encrypted disc. Curious as I was, I looked up one of my sources to have the encryption broken so that I might view the information contained within. What I found was breathtaking. What I found, was a disc containing important research.

While I have yet to view all the information on the disc as it contains many years of notes and research. Here are some bits.

"Last week we had a breakthrough. It seems that Notum, the material wich we use for several things, including nano technology, has certain connections to the strange mutations that have been occuring for quite some time now.

It seems that a body exposed to large ammounts of Notum, over time will change. This affects both plants, animals and humans, as well as some other materials. The ways they will change are not predictable. Experiments on leets shows that while on some, it might cause an increase of strenght, or make them stronger in mind, however, this is not very common. Most of the time, they just mutate, for the worst. While not as much as the mutants ravaging the wilds of rubi-ka, still there is sure signs that notum can affect us greatly."

"Four days ago, I was going to visit Jobe, to see if anyone else had found out anything about notum mutation. However, I was hindered by a guard, who told me I could not leave. I was to stay here under strict surveilance until told otherwise and continue my work. I was also given a note signed by one of the Omni-med officials. 'You will continue your research, and will seek to find a way to eliminate the side effects of the mutation, and how we can best use this to our advantage.'"

"I must escape this place, it is driving me insane. I have been stuck here doing research for several weeks now with little or no progress. They keep sending me human test subjects that I experiment on, to my disgust."

"Thats it! I must destroy all my research so that they stop these horrible experiments. Perhaps it wont stop them at all, but instead make them more eager to keep going, but I got to try even if it so means that I will be killed in the process. But first, I need to find a way to deliver this disc to Jobe, where it will be revealed to the public just how dangerous notum is, if used in the wrong way."

"I managed to bribe a guard to take the disc out. One of my old friends will then meet with him after he stops working for the day. So this is the last of my notes. I hope all this information will come to use for the good of rubi-ka."

Omni-tek must not get ahold of the information contained on this disc. I deliver this to you in hope that you will know what to do with it.

/Shad "Joramon" Galanga
