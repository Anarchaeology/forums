Director Moscardelli's Statement on Notum Prices
Greetings Citizens of Rubi-Ka,

Due to the increased public concern and the end of Omni-Teks Notum Promotion, it is now time for me to speak on this matter. I will address a few points and highlight a few things about the recent events.

But I will begin with the conclusion of the notum promotion. I am very pleased of the outcome. Over the past week, fellow delegates and I began a galaxy-wide sale of notum. The reasons of this sale are many. One, being the most important, was the demands from clients. Ever since the ICC has agreed to open notum mining to private organizations, notum supply as a whole has increased. But we have kept the prices at the same. Clients began to notice the increased amount of notum, and due to the supply and demand business rule, they demanded cheaper prices. Thus in order to fulfill the wishes of our clients and to see if we could get a larger demand; we have agreed to do a sale on notum. We thought this would be a benefactor to everyone, and after this week this was proven true. Since we began the promotion all we seen was a success. New clients lined up, waiting eagerly to sign their contracts. Now we have a new demand to fit to increased amount of notum.

But over the week we have noticed specific statements from individuals about this. We have noticed that several Rebel Clan organizations have halted their shipments of notum in order to increase the price of notum. But this hardly gave anyone a panic in the halls of Omni-Mining and Omni-Admin. Several clanners even said we could not lower prices. This made me laugh slightly. Why can we not change prices of our own products? Notum is an Omni-Tek business, it always has been. Thus we control the prices. Of course we must have a reasonable price, but we still control it. We have every right to do a sale on our products. This is not oppression in anyway. We are looking out for our clients best interests. This is why we lowered the price.

Some stated that this is not legal since the clans own half of the notum supply. This statement created a good joke in the halls of Omni-Mining. This is far from the truth. The Rebels own private notum towers. The notum towers were created to mine small amounts of notum in small areas that could not be mined easily by traditions methods. The total amount of notum coming from these private mining towers is only 25%. The remaining 75% still comes from the mines of Omni-Mining. The statement of clans owning half of the notum towers is true. Thus the clans only give 12.5% of the overall notum supply. Might I also add that Omni-Mining refines that 12.5% and Omni-Trans ships that 12.5%. This means that Omni-Tek still holds majority rule and saying in all ways related to the product of notum.

All in all the promotion increased clients and helped establish a more reasonable price on notum. As many have stated already, the halting from a few clan organizations has hurt not only their little economy but also their reputation. The few clients that clans actually had are happily moving to new contracts with Omni-Tek. As for the overall economy from notum exportation, it was hardly affected, if at all, from the few who halted their agreement. There is no need to worry about the state of the economy. The economy is now growing once again, to an even better state, to help all.

The success this promotion has generated, has even amazed people on Omni-Prime. We may plan another in a future date.

But for now this is all I have to say on this matter. I thank you all for your time.
