Healers bewarned!
All you mps out there...How many times have you been deep into a fight...getting slashed and shot so a bloodly pulp. while your healing pet just sits there smiling and saying I love you I love you!. I have a lowly lvl 31 mp. I have Salvious. What I dont' get is i tell him to heal me, he starts healing...give him a few minutes and he will stop whether I am full or not. And forget about making him heal when I really need it. All the pet will do is tell you that it can't do that it cant do that...What I did find is this....mps walk during the mission. I think it has something to do with the walls inside the missions. I know the going is slow but in the deep when you need that extra 124 hp heal every couple of seconds it's worth it. And if you pet ever stops healing...just tell it to guard you...and keep telling it that for about 10 times...then tell it to heal. like 75%of the time this works.

One last thing. Make your macros seperate for each pet you have...the healing pets don't like being told to attack and the attacking pets don't like being told to heal.

/macro heal /pet Alexis heal
/macro attack /pet Alex attack

simple as that. keep the pets to themselves.
This also could be part of the problem with pets getting confusing orders and decide to do nothing over nothing what they are supposed to do.
