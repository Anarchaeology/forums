No need to explain to me. I use a hammer on my Enforcer as well.

My ql 95 hammer hits for 200, 600,30, 900 etc. It may be irregular, but it'll out damage most soldiers I group with. I would rather have a hammer than a flashpoint that can F/A a few thousand damage every 5 minutes.

Add to that my Brawl, and I'm quite happy, every few fights it'll do 500+ damage in one hit. Dimatch I could do without. It's only useful to save my ass in a close fight.

If you find yourself not criting as much as you would like, try raising your skills to the hammer's requirements. That'll raise your attack rating quite a bit. Mine is at 500 with Challenger on, ~350-400 without (depending on implants and how old my nanos are) Squeezing into a ql200 hammer with trader buffs (well, in 10 or 20 levels) would reduce my crits consiterably due to the decrease in attack rating. In some cases you might even do LESS damage with a higher QL weapon due to the decrease in crits.
