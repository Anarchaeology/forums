Curious Fixer Roleplay Realization
After hearing about "blitzing".. which was a term I had never heard until coming back recently ... I think we used to just call in..... put your sliders to the right, left, right, left, right, left... and run real fast and just grab the item...

(course this was when you could see the mission token in the reward screen.. hehe)

Anyhow, Fixer's RP is to be the guy who can "get stuff", and seeing that Fixers are natural blitzers, this seems a really fun roleplay (obviously, since the Mockers make a living at it..hehe)

I just wonder if Funcom realized that when they gave fixer the set of abilities that they do, and setup missions the way they are....

Anyhow, kinda cool..

Kind of a pointless post, but it was a little personal epiphany I had LOL
