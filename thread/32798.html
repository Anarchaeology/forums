Most memorable moment on Rubi Ka
I just thought this would be an interesting way for people to share some stories of their best or worst times in life (and death ) on Rubi Ka.

Ill start things off



The first moment that springs to mind is my first encounter with an Eremite. The confusion as i got incoming hostile messages on my comms and then seeing the dot race towards me at lightning speed on my HUD. I still couldnt see it. I had no idea the cheeky thing was a burrower.

Seconds later it roared up from beneath the surface and towered above me. At that moment there is no panic. Its just complete and utter awe. I didnt try to run or even flinch .. i just stood staring in amazement as it hovered for a moment before striking the killing blows.

That was a long, long time ago, and as my friends know ive had kinda an obsession with them ever since.... Which leads me onto my second most memorable moment....

This was the first time i discovered the Eremite Shrine/Altar in Deep Artery Valley. Ill be honest... I burst into tears when i found it. I have no idea what came over me, but the surge of emotions was just too much. Very strange moment. Kinda my best and worst moment rolled into one...


Well, there are mine. I hope this inspires some people to get creative with their memories

My most memorable experience was the day a friend of mine took me into SAV to meet Marvin, the paranoid android of the ancient Earth saga, the Hitchiker's Guid to the Galaxy. We were so young, we did not have yalms, and there sure as hell was no whom-pah to Outpost 13 in thoses days. Heck, few people even knew Marvin was on Rubi-Ka.

Just getting to SAV was a great adventure. And when we got there, there he was, still complaining about his diodes and life in general. He'd been a hero of mine since I'd written an essay on Marvin back in school for my Earth Lit. course. I was thrilled to bits.

The worse day was when I learned that there were people intent on killing Marvin just to "see what he drops". I realised then what kind of barbarians I was sharing the planet with; and not just among the clans.
