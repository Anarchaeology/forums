Hunting Club on Rubi-Ka
Hello everyone,

As an agent, I see my fair share of missions undertaken for the betterment of Omni-Tek. However, once and a while, wouldn't it be nice to simply gather together with a group and go hunting in the wild? I feel the need to use some of my Omni vacation time coming up, and I'm considering putting together the "Omni Hunting Club", or some such private club. Now by hunting I don't mean going to some random camp and killing things as they appear for countless hours. I mean going out as a group(s), and travelling around the vast beautiful landscape looking here and there for small herds of things to drop in on and hunt, once the area has been picked clean, then we pick up and fly off somewhere else searching for more things to hunt.

I've gone on many a mission out in the middle of no where and seen wounderful miles of land that is untouched and ripe for hunting in this manner. So if there is anyone else interested in being part of such a club (not an org, this should in no way conflict with those), then contact me at 'ext1@sbcglobal.net', and if there is enough interest I will set to work to get this together. Also, if your intersted in helping put this together let me know that as well. I'm open to all ideas and suggestions! Catch me on the platform alot in Entertainment and say hi, tell me what you think!

Keep away from the recliams! - Bethas

OOC: well I'm certainly glad to see positive feedback on this! As to the lvl question, the varied range of people that would be interested would take some work to get going. So initially what I can say is this : The Hunting Club can consist of really any levels that are interested. The caviat to this would be that there is people within your grouping range that are available in the club. Thats not to say that if your stuck in a 'dead' range with no one to group with your out, it just means you'll have to work at getting into a range with people or hope that others around your level join soon! I myself am lvl 110, and I see the range is broad so far of interest. What I will do is sit down this weekend and put a simple webpage up for the Club to help generate some hype and give people something to look at for considering. Along with that I will also start a mailing list for members, now my vision would be that the list wouldn't be a big 20 emails a day list with people conversing, but simply a mailing list that I would control to send out info on like upcoming events (hunting parties...) and the like. so fear not the spam I myself am not in an org of any sort, so what I may do is start an org with only a few members (enough to start one) and basically just have it under myself if need be so the org can be listed on the AO official site and I can put membership info and members there. Like I said, this is NOT another org, it should be an exclusive members only club geared around the sport of hunting and exploration, something differant from the mission treadmill once and a while to take in some of AO's vast and lovely landscape that gets so neglected.

Stay tuned to this thread for website info and the like. It looks like this is something that could take off
