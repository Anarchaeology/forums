Island of Jobe...
What happend to it??
A while ago a very nice screenshot was presented to the community, but since then it has all been quiet... not a word from FC and not a word from the community it self. My wonders are if the squealing and complaining from the community has made FC stray away from the path of developing the story and the world of Rubi Ka, to the advantage of making patches, buf fixes, more than they should and perhaps what is needed.

Prioritys seem to change for FC, change to what the ones in the community who yells the loudest would like it to be, like a little boy in a shop, wanting ice cream, and is given one by his mom, so she will not be embarrased. So it seems FC does as well, they give the minority of the community their ice cream, and continue to do so, instead of deliver the product promised. Not that I am miscontent, if I was would I still play and enjoy the game? I don't think so.. but it could be so much more.

I feel that the community as a whole, should stop whining and let FC work on the game as they intended, perhaps we will get the events we so badly miss, perhaps we will get the promised story line, perhaps things will happen.. IF we stop giving them new things to do all the time, things that are not of out most importance for the game. Things as cheats, exploits, game crashes should of course be a priority, but all the fixes for the different professions.. Just because you feel things are not the way you would like them to be with the profession you have choosen, doesn't mean it is a thing that needs a immidiate reponse and a fix. Sure a lot of people will be mad at me for this, saying I have no clue, and of course fixing your profession is important. Well to you it is, but stop beeing selfish and see the whole of the game? What do we, as players, paying customers enjoy the most, a new patch to make us more this or that, or a evenly divided stream of events and new and exciting things to appear on the planet and the game world?

I just ask this and you all see what you deem the most important. I know where I stand.

Bionitrous
Supa Leet
More zones = greater server stability

Put it in simple terms. If there was an almost identical zone to Broken Shores that had the same features as the current one, we'd have a more stable Broken Shores. Half the people would go to the alternative.

If Jobe was an exciting place to visit then we'd have people leaving 20K and Home to go see it. The net result is a more stable 20K and Home.

No additionally instability is added by having the unopened zones turned on. Add in Plains of Salt, Sparta, Jobe, The Spur, The Wreck, etc., and you move people across more servers. The zoning server is not taxed any more than it is now unless the improved planet draws more customers.

All it costs FunCom is money. Money to pay for the additional servers and money to complete the artwork and populate the zones. As a business FunCom needs to evaluate whether the improved stability of Broken Shores is worth the money they spend on implementing the city of Sparta. If they put it in an expansion pack, they get money that way. If the improved world experience is enough to draw gamers from other games or prevent them from going to Star Wars:Galaxies, that's a plus as well.
