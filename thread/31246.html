Single player mission bosses
I would like to see a boss in SP missions because most of the time I would say the majority of players cannot get into team missions consistantly enough to get the rewards. Not to mention its hardly worth sending a team thru an entire team mission that is so low that the mobs hardly give any decent XP, but they can't take a higher level mission or the boss will be practically immortal, and his room full of 5 uber red death mobs to back him up.

I'm seeing the shopping channel already slowly reverting back to release when the entire economy consisted of Freedom Arms 3927's and sunglasses.

Add to that I really want a web cloak, but I can't get into team missions very often, and even though I can solo a 50% team mission, the items that drop were useful to me ~15 levels ago.

Bosses in single player missions should be less difficult (equal to the level of the mission generated maybe), with no companions to help them out. They should always drop a weapon or a piece of armor and have a ~25% chance of dropping one of the special boss loot items.

The only problem I forsee with this is boss farming in PF's like Clon**** and 4 Holes where a lot of the missions are very, very small.

Discuss.
