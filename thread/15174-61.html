I might as well add in my thoughts to this looong thread. Oh and I tried but I only managed to read up till page 30, so I would ask your tolerance if I get in to things that have already been resolved.


First things first. I run a L63 Engineer, my pet, an Advanced Gladiator Bot, cons as orange to me (I think he counts as QL70 or so, middle of the + side of the scale). I use QL78 implants. With the +20 Expertise Nanoprograms running the only skill or ability I have lower than my equipment is Treatment and that's just because the clinics give that lovely +100. Every half hour I take two minutes out and renew my full line of buffs. I accept QL80 missions. Yes, I have to be careful, but I only die when I am unattentive.

If the above means you consider what I have to say valueless, then that's your loss.


Pet Loss:
I've seen it commented several times that the pet user is hurt more because they lose 100% of their "weapon". While that is true, it happens AFTER the fight. During the fight the pet is still 100% effective. Sure some Mob may ransack, deprive or unmake my skills, but he will still die BEFORE my robot stops working.

And after the fight, big deal. So I sit down for a few minutes till the debuff goes away. Gives me a chance to make a coffee.

Combine the above with the suggestions from other threads that hostile nanos terminate with the death of the caster and I would never be troubled by this patch.


PvP:
Of course group PvP is a different matter. Sure the pet will continue to fight his current target, but this time it won't necessarily be the same person as the debuffer. And just for an added degree of difficulty, I'm neutral, so you get to cast the debuff before I get to target you.

This is not a (real) complaint though, I don't PvP anyway, never intend to, never intended to. My sole function in PvP is as a moving (actually a "running away making shrieking noises") target.


Implants & Belts:
I haven't yet seen a good reason for them to be excluded from the OE checks. Okay, not checking the Treatment skill for implants, I can understand and agree with that. But check everything else.



Have a feeling there was something else I wanted to comment on, but I can't remember it and I'm tired. So I'll finish here.
