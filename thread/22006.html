Things that seriously need to be changed and some needed suggestions
Things that seriously need to be changed or looked at... im sure at least some people will agree.



1) Tabbing of your team mates and their pets.

This is annoying and.. well... annoying as hell. Its bad enough not being able to tab OT mobs (im omni) that I have to tab though upto 20 people/pet (yes i have been in a 5 mp team with their 3 pets each before) just to get to the first mob is insane.

./me hears the cries of "Why don't you use assist?"

Well as a soldier, 7 out of 10 times i am the tank and expected to attack/pull mobs first. thus i have no one to assist. I also believe in helping my team as much as possible, Waiting those extra 2-4 seconds while whoever im assisting could mean the difference of 6k damage to the mobs (assuming 2 crits with my 1/1 attack speed) and possibly the life or death of a team mate.

It can't be that hard to fix programming wise. Do a check.. if the target belongs to my team then move to the next target.




2) FC's Neglect of neutral players

I am not neutral currently but i did spend 120 levels of heartache trying to be. Neutrals have nothing going for them. They can't shop or save in OT/Clan areas even though OT/Clan can do that in their areas. They don't get anything special. No real ability to sell anything on channels.(they have them but with 5% of the pop being neutral they literally get 1/10th of the possible exposier that everyone else does. No token boards. Only recently did the ability to have PVP titles but only the small PVP community cares about that. They can't even defend themselves in 25% areas since they can't attack until they are attacked first, which in turn means that the nice places at the higher levels like camelot are pretty much off limits. I had a good neutral friend have their entire team slaughtered yesterday because of ONE low level trader. The trader picked them off one by one and no one could help because they themselves wern't under attack.

Being neutral is hard work and should be rewarding at least a bit. And since they only make up 5% of RK's population they don't get much support. Usually its just Omni/Clan people who have really no idea of waht being neutral is like. Give them something. Its logical that neutral people should be able to shop/save in places they arn't concidered enemies. Let them save anywhere. Since they are not part of the conflict People should not be able to attack them unless they are attacked by the neutral person, not the other way around. This game is suppose to be a war. Im not sure what planet RK is but usually you don't attack possible allies.



3) SOME boss monsters.

Boss monsters with +200000 hp are doable, boss monsters with a 60% chance to crit are a pain in the arse but doable with a pet. But some of these boss monsters are essentially impossible to do and should be looked at... I recently had an experience which this is based on.

Heres what happened:
First of all I am not exactly a weakling. Im a 140 soldier wielding a lvl 200 beamer with 900 attack rating. (Ie. upto 1k normal shots and 3.4k crits against Vets with a 1/1 attack speed)

In short I was in a mission with friends and we steamrolled through it until we got the the boss. This boss was insane and had almost impossible AC. I mean i was minimum hitting for 5. Yes just FIVE. and critting for a whopping 90. With 2 soldiers, 2 agents, a doc /w shotgun and a NT on him he was healing so fast that after 10min of attacking jsut him we had taken him down to ~95% health remaining. Sounds great doesn't it? At first i thought it was a one of them Bosses with the 90% reflect shields for a certain kind of damage but we had 4 different kinds of damage going (poison, fire, energ and projectile) and everyone has the same results. I know team missions are suppose to be tough, but thats a little rediculus IMHO.

(I have screenshots btw. if anyone wants to donate space i will gladly post them)



4) Saving before a boss in team mission.

Now before you piss on me for saying that, hear this. I AM NOT AGAINST IT. I encourage saving before a boss and am perfectly ok with anyone who does.

What i do find a problem is the downtime this causes. Let say my team has jsut spent 3 hours in the mission and has gained 12mil exp during the process. rightfully everyone should save. Now i have to wait 10 min for people to run out, 10 more for them to save and 10+ more for them to find their way. Thats 30+ min i have to wait to go kill a boss which we might not even be able to kill if you've read my last post. 30+ min is just my guess, and is entirely reasonable. It may take shorter or longer (if you've had mort OP cave team missions you'll know what i mean. those are HUGE and its not uncommon to have ones that last 8 hours) but i concider 30+ min of downtime a reasonable average

True you can get someone to warp you out, where your mission jsut happens to be directly beside a save point and have an ENG beacon warp you in. But this just isn't the normal case. Luckly you'll have someone to warp you out.

What im saying is you should implement a way of saving before a boss. Im suggesting a small machine that will only save the exp of whoever uses it. Make it so that the mission has to be 95% or more completed and only people who have the icon are able to use it (to avoid exploits or something). Place it with the boss button and everyone will be happy. No more needless downtime. I doubt people would care that if they died they'd be sent back to whereever it they really saved last. If thats too hard to do (or if the developers are too lazy) Implement those save pills that can be found in the item database. Make them unique so you can only have one and spawn 6 around the boss button so people can pick them up Or give the fixers a hack insurance terminal line. You've already given them the grid and have though about giving crats access to their bank. This would make fixers actually valuable in teams.

Your team mission are great fun, which lasts until the boss when you get 30+ min of downtime because everyone is saving

I AM REPEATING AGAIN THAT I AM NOT AGAINST THE ACT OF SAVING. I ENCOURAGE IT. I JUST THINK THAT THERE SHOULD BE A BETTER, NON-TIMECONSUMING WAY TO DO IT.



5) Ninja looting / General greadiness

No one likes a ninja looter ( they often get a team killed and don't help out) Make chests count with the /team loot alpha. Or make chests different colours so that only one person can open a specific colour. You know how many chests spawn in a mission, how many people are there for the mission, make it so people can only open a certain amount of chests ( #chests / #people). Just do something, anything!




6) /team loot random

Why not have a team loot random. This would be a nice little change. No more people killing a leet every 6th monster to screw you over, no more *****ing about the loot settings or people who hog loot.

No one in a team kills a small number of monsters. Most kill hundreds and even thousands, A random loot setting wouldn't give any advantage to anyone. Flip a coin 100k time and your gona get an even 50/50 split.

This will probably become more liked when you implement the new special items and unqiues found on boss monsters in 14.2, Everyone will be rushing for the corpse if its set on all. People will be trying to kill the boss on their loot turn for alpha and although it would be nice., ther are an ever increase number of people that i know i can't trust with leader loot settings. ( I even had a team last night where the leader would have it on alpha all the way there. Then right before the boss died would spam lots of messages in attempt to hide the fact he just set the loot to leader before the boss fell. Some people i've seen are even stupid enough to take all the good loot on a boss corpse then claim it was all junk. Upon looting the corpse next you see bunches of holes.

Im not saying everyone is like that, most people are good and honest and list/share loot with those who need it. so a /team loot random wouldn't effect anyone like that, just the greasy S.O.B.s who woudl rather the team die if they got their loot. I myself couldn't care less about loot since i have everything i need, but when new items come out in 14.2 i would like a cut so to say.





I think thats it. Took me an hour to write that... have RL things to do. May add more. Try to limit reponces to contructive thought and ideas. Flame me if you wish, but remember it only takes away from the thread and I won't care/respond anyway.
