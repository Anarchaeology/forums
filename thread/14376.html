You've never hung out in Omni Trade or St. George's Square, then. :-p

Newland City is great because of its size, simplicity, etc. It is a Grid and Whom-pa hub, has all stores close together, etc. I use it because it is a great and fast way to do things. Well, they did decrease the lag some time ago (I don't remember how long) when they moved the insurance and bank terminals to that hill above the subway.

That must have been one of the happiest moments of my AO life, because they used to be to the right of the main gate (that zones to the lake), and the mission terminals used to be on the other side of the building they are currently by, so everything was right next to each other. All of the terminals, plus the main gate. The lag was absolutely horrendous.

But I agree with Vrach. As bad as the lag can be at times, the city seems a bit too small to split in two, and it would really just be nice if they put a mission terminal or two next to where the apartments are.

Of course, because there is only one set of mission booths, that area effectively becomes the town square and it's easier to "advertise" (looking for a team, buying, selling, buffing, etc.) there.

I suggest that if you are really having lag problems (such as if your computer is disconnecting, etc.), go hang around in Borealis. It's fairly small as well, has the same shops (if not more), and is much less crowded than Newland City is. Just a suggestion. Plus, it now has a whom-pa to Stret West Bank, which is a nice zone.
