Healthbars aren't nearly as bad as they used to be. However, if you're in a situation where you expect that you're not seeing all the damage your team is taking, here's a very common healer trick: run your fingers over all the function keys from F2 to F6. That selects every one of your team mates one at a time. Ever since launch, selecting someone has caused the game engine to update your info on that char. So, after you do that, their healthbars in your team window will be updated.
