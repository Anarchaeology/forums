Elerion
When I first saw the patch message today, I thought it was completely justified. Concealment has been completely overpowered for me. As a level 30 agent I can get about 450 concealment, which would only be obtainable closer to level 90 without buffs. With my cloaking device, I can also run while sneaking. This made me able to do team missions at maximum difficulty, without much risk. That was clearly overpowered. But then I started hearing of friends with the same concealment getting spotted by greys. I went in and tried it, and it was really that bad. With the concealment equivalent of a level 90 I would be spotted by high grey mobs within 10-20 seconds every time. Green mobs would use anywhere from 0-10 seconds, and yellow mobs I could only cloak by 1 in 3 times. This means that an unbuffed level 90 character can not sneak by a level 30 NPC reliably? And he will even be spotted by level 20 NPC within 20 seconds? This is completely fubar'ed. I am not usually one to complain about nerfs, and I frown at those that whine when overpowered things get fixed. But this time Funcom has gone too far. The concealment nerf brings down at least two other skills with it, namely Aimed Shot and Sneak Attack. This breaks Agents down to a very low-powered class, and hurts a few other classes as well.

I spent some time thinking about this issue while mowing the lawn. I came to the conclusion that the problem is not concealment itself, but two other factors:
1. Pronouncement of Greatness (Leet Form)
This spell is simply overpowered. +200 concealment at level 25 with no real drawback is just insane. It is a really fun spell (*snicker*description*snicker*) but they just went overboard on this one. It should be +50 concealment at most.
2. Cloaking Devices
These enable anyone with a skill of 150+ in concealment to run while sneaking, with an EXTREMELY small hp and nano drain. They also buff your concealment some more. Since you spend less time passing the enemies, they get fewer perception checks, and their chance of spotting you decreases dramatically. You also spend half the time through a mission, compared to what you would do sneaking.

I feel these are the only real problems. Maybe Shadow Crown should be nerfed to 50 points or so. But think about it agents. Without Leet form, Cloaking devices and Shadow Crown, there is no way you could do anything harder than 75% missions without spending a lot of time trying&dying. And 75% missions would not be unreasonable for the masters of sneaking. I have soldier friends that solo 75% missions all the time.

I realise I didnt list Shadow's Crown as a major problem, yet said that without it, you couldnt do what you do. This is because the majority of agents are now in their sneaking prime. With Shadow's Crown and Concealment Expertise only, a 22 agent can almost double his concealment skill. This is quite impressive, but in another 20 levels, those spells won't do that much anymore. And I have not seen any mid-high level versions of Shadow's Crown yet. Compare it to title levels or EQ pets. At some levels, EQ pets were way overpowered. This was when they were brand new. The few levels before mages and necros could use their next pet, their current pet was no way near as strong (this was most evident in the low levels). Shadow's Crown will not be as impressive when most agents are 50+.

I honestly think the concealment matter would be solved best by removing Cloaking Devices (or making them A LOT harder to use! I am talking only for 100+ characters) and Pronouncement of Greatness (or make it +30 or so), and reverting concealment back to it's previous state. This would stop agents and adventurers from doing anything harder than 75% missions, and at high levels, probably nothing more than 50%. As it is now, they have destroyed the agent class. Three of our green skills just became 99% useless (concealment, aimed shot, sneak attack).

That is pretty much all we have. Sure, you can say we have False Profession nanos, but these are currently not useful for anything but buffing. Another class' nano cast will cost 2.5 times the "normal" cost, if you have the nano. This makes you unable to be a viable replacement for a doctor or a crat (assuming crats will one day have the EQ-enchanter-like role of crowd control, some have told me that they will). That leaves us with suboptimal weapons (especially now that aimed shot is out), low hp, useless agent nanos, and the ability to use almost any buff in the game, although several levels later than the "real" class and at an EXTREME cost (and with dark blue T&S, we cant use the best nano kits to make up for it).

So Funcom, please realise the error you have done. Either un-nerf concealment to some middle ground (making it useless for non-buffed characters like fixers, but balanced for agents and adventurers) or remove the things I suggested. Because at the moment, even I feel tempted to give up and start a fixer or soldier. And I am not one that usually complains about nerfs.
