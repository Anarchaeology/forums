multi-tokens
the whole multiple token from one actual token in your inventory thing has been in since they started rewarding mission tokens based on level. you can verify this by doing the following:

1.) get a mission.
2.) complete the mission and get a token.
3.) right click on your board in your wear window.
4.) notice how many tokens it says you have collected. store this in variable x.
5.) right click on the token in your inventory screen. notice how many tokens it now says are collected. store this in variable y.
6.) perform the following calculation to determine how many tokens you actually recieved. z = y - x. take note of the result stored in z.

by following these simple steps you can confirm the multiple token theory youself using a rigorous scientific method.
