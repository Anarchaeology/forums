ARK Expo Reaction Thread!
The ARK Expo on Atlantean & Rimor is now over! I'll keep this thread sticky for you all to link your screenshots, tell stories, or whatever about the ARK Expo 29477! {August 15th & 16th of 2003}

I'll update later with info and fun stuffs {if possible.}

Recap: What happened? Who was out there?
- Cz was out there.
- GM Lucien was out on both days (ARK Manager), shout out to him for giving a thumbs up on ARK Expo.
- ARKs from every department: bug hunters, events, CR, etc. were out talking to people.
- Loads upon loads of people to fill area servers on both Dims.
- Morphed ARKs as: pumpking heads, yuttos, brain dog, and more.
- Lighting effects.
- Prizes galore! Jobe T-Shirts, Teddy Bears, and more.
- LIVE Music By Gridstream: We even had ARK AgtOrange DJing live with Tarryk.

Let us know what you thought of it all! We want to hear from you!

Wow!!!
What an event!!! I was there, I witnessed it all
I decided to head to the party early and I'm glad I did because the lag started to build up pretty fast.

Ever stayed online with 3.5 million ping? I did and that's a really weird feeling. LOADS of peeps showed up at Reets. I spent most of my time scouring for all the ARK's and taking their picture.

I think 100 ARK's showed up in total and I got pics of about 60 of them (I think) Do I get the award for snapping the most ARK's?

Highlight of the party (if you COULD party that is, lag was really bad) was this giant UBAH female that showed up called Buffies.
Now most players know about the Buffy legend, there was even supposed to be a shrine to her near SG at Tir, but to see THE actual Buffy (God of Leets) show up in person was amazing!!!

She was really tall, had the scariest looking head and wore what I can only estimate as being Shadowlands armour.
Got some screenshots of her too

Also at the party was some people from Gridstream Productions, unfortunately I could not hear anything but I'm sure the music was great.

Afterwards I headed off to the ICC for the expo and Buffies promoted a few ARK's in a short ceremony. After that the ARK's split up into sections.

The BEST thing at the expo was the 2 GIANT leet ARK's
One of them was really playful and cuddly and the other was a bit shy but still very cuddly

Overall I think it was MASSIVE, I never seen anything like it before and I don't think we ever will again.
