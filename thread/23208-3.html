AOMD no longer works at all. That is because it sniffs packets before they get to AO ,using WinPcap. The reason why that won't work anymore is because FC has compressed the packet stream, which has the same effect as encrypting it (although the intention was to allow for faster network communication). ClickSaver hooks into an AO dll and gets mission data after it has been decompressed.
