Sorry to provide incomplete information - I'm not at home and therefore I don't have my stored screenshots. But here's what I recall.

The Yuttos Tools vendor in Adonis (by Finding Serenity and the Milliner) sells two items I haven't seen anywhere else. Both of them state that if you combine them with a Spirit Tech Crafting Tool, you'll get a shell. I bought both items, and sat down to work on them.

One of the items required EE and FQP, and the tool turned it into one of those Adventurer-only spirit tech machine shells. QL 65 or 75, sorry I can't recall which. I didn't have enough time to work on the second item.

I know, this is incomplete, but I hope it's enough for someone else to finish.
