Where the roleplayers are...
In case anyone missed this...

Entrak has kindly set up a chat bot to help bring the roleplaying community of AO closer. The conversation on the bot is (mostly) ooc. It is there organise and advertise as well as just help roleplayers from all factions and both dimensions to get to know each other better. Think of it as The Buzz in chat form.

The bot has been around now just under a month and has developed a pretty loyal following. So, assumming you don't already have bots coming out of your ears, why not join in?

To join it, simply send the following tells on:

- Rimor: /tell Roleplay !join
- Atlantean: /tell Roleplaying !join

If you want, auto invite to the bot can be arranged too.
