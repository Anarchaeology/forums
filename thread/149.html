To whom it might concern.
I've had it!

My patience just ran out. I've tried being patient with you guys, but I just can't take it anymore. This game gives me more frustration then it gives me enjoyment, and now I'm simply fed up.

--------------------

The missions

They're boring, have been broken since day 1, and implemented badly. OK.. I could live with that. I would instead team up with others, socialice and have fun. If I wanted to get some tokens or needed credits to improve my armor, weapon, or buy some other items I needed, I would simply run a few missions and I'd be up to date again, no problem.

Then they removed the tokens from missionrunning.. it was a hit, but I could understand it. And I guessed I could always do missions the old fashion way if I wanted tokens in the future. Then I reached level 60 something, and where no longer able to do missions the old fashion way even with up to date equipment, being a fixer and all. Then I figured they would probably implement team missions some day where all members would get a token, so they would still be avaliable.

Then they removed xp from mission running, one I didn't really understand at my level because the xp I got from missions was nothing worth talking about. And unless this really was a problem with high level players I think they should have left it in, because mission running, at least for me, has it's risks. And I think I actually deserve a level after running 100-200 missions should I choose to do so.

Then they removed the mission running alltogether. That's where I draw the line folks. If there is no real way for my fixer to earn credits I don't really see the point of playing anymore. I can't belive you did something like that, and without asking the players for their opinions, without even mentioning it in the patch info. That's one you're not getting away with. At least not in my book.

--------------------

The professions:

Why do you make the game so combat oriented when many classes are unable to solo at higher levels? Missions are now only avalible to the combat classes (and maybe the truly over-twinked), which I find really unfair. And what are taking so long fixing the fixer? It's not like you don't have enough ideas too choose from. Which brings me to my next point..

--------------------

The support:

I for one don't get the feeling of being heard. I don't think many other players feel like they're being heard either. Seems to me you just run your own show, and don't care much for creative player input and ideas you didn't think of first. Maybe harsh accusations, but it's the way I see it, doesn't have to be true as long as I, as a player, percive it that way.

--------------------
