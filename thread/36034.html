a commentary
i have to admit that i never expected this to explode like it did...

mugginns, like i told you before (after apologizing for your framerate drop, mind you), i do it because it makes people smile. you were the only one who has complained to me about it, but i explained to you that i was having fun (incidentally i really do not appreciate being compared to jeffery dahmer simply because `he did what he thought was fun and look where he ended up'), and also that it's worth it for me to endure criticism and inappropriate comparisons to serial killers(!) if it brightens even one person's day. the simple fact is that 99% of the people that see this `artwork' are glad to see something light-hearted and fun while en route to yet another mission in broken shores on the endless leveling treadmill.

the primary reason i started drawing things is because it amuses me, and amuses others. evidently not everyone is amused. alas, all it takes is one wet blanket to petition about me and as such, i have ceased to draw pictures in high-traffic, graphics-intensive areas. if i may be so presumptuous, may i suggest that the rest of the community also cease to draw pictures in areas like trade, tir, newland, etc? we don't want to ruin muggins' or anybody else's staggering framerates in these laggy areas. i personally will continue to draw pictures in shops around rubi-ka and other low-traffic areas until it stops amusing me. i am not willing to stop having fun simply because certain people don't like what i or other individuals are doing. i'm perfectly willing to compromise, however. hence moving my `hobby' to shops instead of public areas. if you don't wish to be burdened by other peoples' creativity, i suggest you petition again and complain.

as for making player-dropped items (specifically fixer summons and mission keys) disappear after 10 minutes, i hate to break it to you, but doing so won't change anything. i and many others have been perfectly happy drawing pictures with furniture and plants in the past, and these items decay in well under 10 minutes. i spent a good two hours recently decorating the grid with a friend of mine. if this becomes an issue with anyone, i suggest you petition and complain. hell, eventually due to complaints, funcom may stop allowing items to be dropped in general, in which case i and many others will draw pictures with our own corpses by /terminating repeatedly. when that stops working, we will make creative use of roots to position mobs in aesthetically pleasing configurations before we kill them. when that stops working, we will simply arrange large groups of characters into random patterns that are funny the first time but ruin framerate. oh wait that's tir/trade/ent/newland already...
perhaps we should have funcom make players themselves last 10 minutes or less. that way everyone will get nice framerates in crowded areas.

ffs.

in any case, we will continue to spend our time on rubi-ka the way we enjoy it. some of us enjoy dropping items in meaningful patterns. some of us enjoy complaining about people who enjoy their time in different ways. (that said, can we please keep our creativity on the non-offensive side? i understand that it may be difficult to avoid offending *someone*... but let's keep everything in good taste at the very least.)

by the way, welcome to the fixer community muggins. i hope your ride on the bandwagon continues to be enjoyable.
