QL 51 Pillow with Important Stripes. QL 52 would probably be fine.

I say this because at 51, the Bio Met/Mat Met/Mat Crea bonuses given by the pillows jumps to 15 apiece. A QL 51 Pillow and a QL 99 Pillow both give the same bonuses, but the QL 99 has much higher requirements than the QL 51 pillow with no real added benefit.

That'd be my recommendation. I can't recall what I paid for for my QL 51 Pillow, but it was at least 150k and it was probably more than that.

If you're looking for more challenging missions, try Nano Armor and Biomech Armor once you're able to pull QL 75 missions. Biomech helmets and cloaks are especially valuable since they add in a little bit of a treatment bonus and can be worn with the Omni-Med clothing during implant switching.

Hope that helps.
