It's not so much of a "no place to fight" it's more of a "no place worth fighting FOR". PvP will stay in the state it is in now until some of the following are added.

1. Forts that trade alignment based on the killing of the base commander.
2. Special assignments in 25% gas that can be completed by either alignment but only one side gets the reward and one person gets an additional reward.
3. Action in 25% gas zones for all levels. Exp and credit events like bank tellers carring 1000-100,000 credits and being guarded by 4-5 guards. Find the enemies banker and get some money but watch out for those that guard the funds of their government.
4. All exp gained in 25% gas gets a 5-20% bonus varying by the hour.
5. Air based "forts". Raids on Air convoys (really just stationary ships but with large spaces and at least 2 levels). Would require Yamaha's or an engineer with a Yamaha for a team.

Until something like the things I mentioned above are added there really is no reason to expand PvP because there really is no supported reason to do it unless you roleplay. Until then you'll need to deal with the fact that PvP is about dueling, ganking, and retreat (aka zoning).
