Famin, there is a big difference. You seem to act as if the Clans were original inhabitants of a peaceful planet who were conquered by a foreign invader. If that were the case, you could argue that your had prior rights over Rubi-Ka and that ICC laws do not apply to you. That is not the case. The Clans are descendants of ICC colonists living on a planet terraformed by an ICC member, Omni-Tek, and regulated by an ICC lease. By any civilised standard, you do fall under ICC law. Hiding you head in the sand is not going to change that.

That doesn't mean you can't enjoy your freedom. The Clans won the right to rule themselves centuries ago. In Tir and Athen you have your own guards, set your own taxes, run your shcools as you choose. If you want to make polygamy legal in Tir I am sure you could. However when it comes to international law, when it comes to rights over territory the ICC has a fair claim to, when it comes to trading with other planets and corporations in the ICC then naturally you are subject to ICC law.

But the Clans are also protected by ICC law. The ICC is waht prevented OT from using orbital bombing and weapons of mass destruction during the worse moments moments of the conflict, the ICC is what has forced OT to share it insurance system, share the grid and ensure the continuing operation of the terraforming machinery. If you as so keen to abbandon the ICC, are you ready to give all these benefits up too?

As for you example, I think a more accurate picture of the situation would be as follows: imagine how would you feel if you called a plumber into your house, asked him to repair a leak and plumber turned around and said, "I like this place, I'll make this my home from now on, get out of here or I'll beat you up."
