Raising dark blue skills is a necessary evil at times, but not one I can afford at this point. It's not really a big deal.

As for uniqueness and specialization, I wouldn't mind that, really, but as the game moves forward, they're slowly choking out the ability to solo by increasing difficulty, adding this and that, making it very hard to solo past a certain level, etc.

I wouldn't care if it had been the way of things since day one. But I've become used to self-sufficience, and feel it is a "freedom" you don't often see in other games of this genre. Of course, soloing won't be as rewarding as group play, but that's okay.

I'd just love to have the option of being able to complete a mission in less than an hour, BY MYSELF, without having to depend on Fixer Joe to get this chest open for me, or dragging along Doc Bob to heal me, or taking Enforcer Bruno for some tank back-up. Right now, I can solo, but the reports from higher level folks aren't at all encouraging when it comes to solo play.

If they're pushing team-play, and this lockpicking thing is just one way to encourage it, that's fine. But I've been around since beta4, and I've noticed the ability to solo become increasingly more difficult as the game progresses. Kind of annoying to those who enjoy this much-advertised feature of AO.

Funcom has said in the past that they will entirely change the way people play the game, and we won't necessarily like it, but we'll get used to it.

Argh, I'm rambling. Anyway, is my point clear? Soon we won't be able to accomplish anything without a full-fledged, well-rounded team, and while some may think this is a good thing, I can't quite agree. Soloing is good because you don't have to spend the time it takes to get a group together, get everybody organized, etc. And that's not always feasible when you only have an hour or two in which to play the game.
