You ARE completely wrong as far as PvP is concerned in SW: G. If you don't declare yourself an overt operator for the Empire or the Rebels (later the Hutt Faction) you CANNOT be attacked. You can still fight for your side against NPC's from the other side, however.

Battlefields will spawn and the call will go out to your faction to help defend. You will enter the battlefield with your compatriots and fight the enemy. Only it will be NPC's. Whether you win or lose (and whether players on the other side win or lose) can affect the political control of a sector.

Also Tradeskillers will have input into keeping control of a region through various means not involving combat.

That does not mean you can't engage in direct PvP. The same battlefields as mentioned above will spawn and the same call will go out. Only this time you will fight actual people on the other side. The outcome of the battle will determine political control of a region.

Now, those who do not declare themselves 'overt' can still enter these direct PvP battles and fight for their side. They will be open to attack while in the battlefield and for a short time thereafter. However, they will go back to covert status eventually and be safe from ganking forever.

Finally, Neutrals will be able to join in either of the two types of battlefields by declaring a temporary alliance. They can help their buddies for a fee or just join in the mayhem for a session.

The mission system is implemented in SW: G. The penalty for failing a mission is that a bounty is taken out on your head. You put up your own cash equal to the value of goods to be delivered or item to be retrieved or whatever as a 'bond'. Now, if you fail in your mission, your bond is forfeit. A bounty hunter can choose to hunt you down and collect this amount of money. You don't lose experience or gear. You just have an incentive to complete your mission and be selective about what you agree or don't agree to do for a mission giver.

As far as the mechanics themselves, they have been likened to Magic: The Gathering. You have a set of skills and weapons you can use. Combat is a series of moves and counter moves. The 'smart' player will make the right moves. The 'n00b' will learn something and maybe go out and study a new skill or change tactics next time. It's not all about gear and loot.

PvP in SW: G is pretty damn well thought out.
