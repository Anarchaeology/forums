Answer to Dodga Card II Problem.
Before the full Dodga quest was implemented this is what happened. You would get all the parts from the bots, make the 'brain' and give it to Dodga. In return he would give you Dodga's Card I. After a short wait you then gave Dodga's Card I to Dodga and in return recieved the first Shoulderpads and Dodga's Card II. That's as far as that quest went.

Then the patch came. Now instead of giving him Dodga's Card II and continuing on to Mission 2 of the Quest you recieved Mission 3. You needed to give him Dodga's Card I to get Mission 2 which, if you recall, you already gave him for the Shoulderpads and Dodga's Card II.

I don't know how much simpler I can put it.
