The First Order of Crimson Warriors seeks new members!
The First Order of Crimson Warriors is seeking new clan members of any profession. We are a guild based upon three principles -

1 - Honor in battle comes before individual gain
2 - Character makes ordinary men and women heroes
3 - Strength of heart can overcome any adversary

These principles are what we live and die by...

If you are looking for a guild and believe in these principles as we do, I would love to meet with you. We welcome all professions and levels, especially new characters. This guild shares everything we own with each other because brother/sisterhood is the only way that we can ever attain freedom.

We would be honored to have you fight and learn along side us. If you are new, we will help you grow and develop your character.

Please send me a tell if you have any questions or would like info on joining
