Announcing the RK2 Guild Fair
Hello fellow citizens of Rubi-Ka,

I would like to take this opportunity to invite all of you, Clan, Neutral and Omni alike, to attend the Guild Fair. The purpose of this is to allow citizens looking for a guild to talk with current members about the guild's ideals, policies and other information, so they can decide which guild they would like to join.

Representatives from guilds from Clan, Neutral, and Omni will be attending, so all are welcome.

This event will be held on June 29th (Saturday) at 20:00GMT at Reet Retreat in Stret West Bank.

(DIRECTIONS: From Newland City, take the whompa to Borealis. From Borealis City, take the whompa to Stret West Bank. Once you are in Stret, turn left and go through the first archway that you can see and you'll get to a grey path. Turn left again and follow the path up the hill. On the side of a very tall building you will see a sign that says "Reet Retreat." The door on the left hand side of it, at 1205,2795.)

If your guild is interested, please contact me if you haven't already.

Hope to see you there!

- Ikia, Souls of Eternity President
