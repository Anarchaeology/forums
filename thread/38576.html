I believe the mission icon/time limit will reset each time you finish a bot. If you can't find Trash King (first to be tagged) in 6 days.. something's wrong :P.

You will hear the "Mission Completed" music twice with each bot. Once for simply targeting the bot (mission icon will change), second for picking up/right-clicking the Transmission Bug on the bot (mission icon will change back to a "red eye"). You must have a "red eye" icon before moving to the next bot.

Bots are to be tagged/bugged in order:
Trash King, Trash King Lackey, Electro Unique, Nuts & Bolts, Greasy Joints, Live Metal, Best in Brass, Metalomania and finally Galvano.

The third quest will present you with a problem being level 41. It requires killing all these bots for a unique Communication Processing Unit ID=TKL. (TKL meaning Trash King Lackey). Each bot contains a piece that goes into a Complex Communication Processing Unit in the same order as above. This drops from the Trash King himself.
