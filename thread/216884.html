Arul Saba Bracer Construction Service

Ok well i guess its time for me to advertise my arul saba bracer service

I'm not the first to make them and i'm sure i won't be the last but i'd like to think that i'm charging very reasonable prices for my service.

I consider myself to be very approachable and friendly so if you have any questions regarding anything to do with the bracer construction which isn't explained below feel free to contact me.

I look forward to hearing from you all

Votomol

======================================

I'll only really concentrate on building 3 and 4 gem bracers, i am happy to build the 2 and 1 gem kind as well but most engie's should be able to make them, so it may well be a lot easier to find someone else to build them for you instead of me

For a 3 gem bracer you will need:
1 x 3 slot bracer blueprint (available from arul saba in the IPS building in Jobe Market)
1 x wrist balancer (left or right) this will make the bracer to be only equipable on either the left or right wrist.(available from arul saba in the IPS building in Jobe Market)
4 x QL150+ small silver ingots (these can be created by reclaiming many of the rings found as mission loot, i'll be happy to turn them into ingots if you can't do this yourself)
4 x personal furnace (available from any trade department of a general store)
6 x QL150+ nano wire (available from any trade department of a general store)
3 x QL150+ robot junk (drop from hellhounds in missions a lot, or from robot mobs all over RK)
1 x Arbiter Gem
1 x Monarch Gem (Must be of the same type as the arbiter gem)
1 x Emperor Gem (Must be of the same type as the arbiter/monarch gem)

I will charge 2 million credits per 3 gem bracer, these however are not that hard to make and i'm sure a lot of other engineers may make them for you cheaper, so feel free to shop around, and don't be afraid to negotiate with me

For a 4 gem bracer you will need:
1 x 4 slot bracer blueprint (available from arul saba in the IPS building in Jobe Market)
1 x wrist balancer (left or right) this will make the bracer to be only equipable on either the left or right wrist.(available from arul saba in the IPS building in Jobe Market)
5 x QL150+ small silver ingots (these can be created by reclaiming many of the rings found as mission loot, i'll be happy to turn them into ingots if you can't do this yourself)
5 x personal furnace (available from any trade department of a general store)
8 x QL150+ nano wire (available from any trade department of a general store)
4 x QL150+ robot junk (drop from hellhounds in missions a lot, or from robot mobs all over RK)
1 x Arbiter Gem
1 x Monarch Gem (Must be of the same type as the arbiter gem)
1 x Emperor Gem (Must be of the same type as the arbiter/monarch gem)
1 x Stellar Gem (Must be of the same type as the arbiter/monarch/emperor gem)

For each 4 gem bracer i will charge 20 million credits
I will charge 20 million each for the first 4 bracers and 10 mil for every bracer after that

I'm also interested in crackerjack sleeves, Repair Coordination Assistants or any ql180+ control unit symbiants so will be more than willing to accept those instead of payment in most cases
