Docs do NOT have problems healing. Docs do NOT have problems finding teams. Their problems lie, from what I'm told, mostly in soloing, where this nano would do ****-all. So don't pretend this is something docs need; it would be a nice bonus.

Fixers don't need GA. NTs... well, maybe some day they won't need Nullity, but right now its important to give them survivability. They are, in fact, 'lollypops'.

And stop whining about fixer HoTs. Do enforcers complain that docs have HP buffs?

So, some ideas for 'lollypop' nanos for the classes that don't have them.

Adventurer: "One With Nature": outdoors, mobs do not aggro the adventurer's team so long as they are lower in level than the nanos effect level (i.e. OWN 1 will keep level 1-60 from aggroing, OWN 2 will keep level 1-120, OWN 3 will keep level 1-180, OWN 4 will keep level 1-240 from aggroing). Additionally, normally social mobs (same restriction) will not become social with each other when the advent has this running, even if a mob they are social with is attacked.

Agent: "One Shot, One Kill": the next Aimed Shot is enhanced with nanobots. These nanobots make CERTAIN that the next AS will hit for the maximum multiplier possible given that agents skills (i.e. if the agent can hit a 10k, they hit a 10k, if they can hit a 5k, they get a 5k, etc.) NO COOLDOWN NEEDED.

Bureaucrat: Iron Will, Steel Will, Titan Will (any of those names already taken? I forget). Target's Psychic is reduced by a factor related to the Bureaucrats Psychology and the quality of the nano (mk1, 2, 3) At the high end of the scale, a reduction in Psychic of ~150ish should be achievable. I.E.

Mk1: Psychic reduced by 5% of Psychology.
Mk2: Psychic reduced by 10% of Psychology.
Mk3: Psychic reduced by 15% of Psychology.

This could be implemented, even if some of the other charm-enhancing measures make it in-game, as this would still serve the purpose of making a crat with this a better charmer than one without. Concurrent with this, bosses will have to be made properly uncharmable, rather than just giving them very high Psychic (I think a high-level crat tested this by tagging along on a lowbie team mission and charming the boss, if I'm wrong, well, no extra work needed!)

Doctor: Rez reduction nano: Mk1, 45 second rez, Mk2, 30 second rez, Mk3, 15 second rez, Mk4, 10 second rez.

Enforcer: Mungo's Edge. Not much is known about Mongo's sibling Mungo. Some claim he was the runt of the family, and forever tried to make up for what he lacked in size by experimenting with nanotechnology designed to compensate for his weak muscles. This seems to have worked, because pirated copies of Mungo's Edge programs still exist in the Grid, somewhere...
Reduces the enforcers Melee Init, but massively increases the damage done on each swing, doubling it. Mk1: MI is set to 0, +150 AR. Mk2: MI is set to 150, +300 AR. Mk3: MI is set to 300, +450AR.

Engineer: Have TBW. Make TBW work properly, then we can get them something else, because engies need it. Make their 'lollypop' weapon-modifications to mount to their bots, maybe?

Fixers: Have GA.

Martial Artist: The Zen of NCU. MA's NCU is 'frozen'; no new nanos can be run within it, hostile or friendly. Duration is: Mk1, 30s, Mk2, 60s, Mk3, 90s. Cooldown of 120s. The MA can still run nanoprograms; however, they won't affect the MA (not sure how heals work, they may still work... or not. I think normal heals would work, but team heals and healpets wouldn't work, based on current game mechanics.) Yes, this nano is pretty much only useful in PvP, but that's the MAs current big problem, so...

Meta-physicists: Spirit Possession. MP calls a more powerful spirit into the shell of their attack pet, increasing the pets attack rating significantly. Mk 1, +85 AR, Mk2 +175 AR, Mk3 +255 AR, Mk4 +350 AR (these are done based on my vague memory that MP demons have an AR around 600-650, and I wanted them to increase to around 950-1000)

NTs: Have Nullity.

Soldiers: Air Becomes Lead. A short duration nano that *massively* increases the soldiers RI. RI bonus on the order of +2400 RI (i.e. a soldier could, with some effort and a TIM scope, get a IM-50 down to 1/1). Mk1 lasts 8 seconds, Mk2 16 seconds, Mk3 24 seconds. All would add approximately
Cooldown of 2 minutes, 8 seconds (just enough time for TMS to recycle after the nano is over... ). Instant cast time, 1 second recharge.

Traders: x Percent Interest Rate. The negative effects of traders 'trade' nanos are reduced by x%. Comes in 15, 30, 45, 60 versions. I.E. if a wrangle would normally reduce the traders skills by 100, if he's got 45% Interest Rate running, he only loses 55 points, while his team retains full benefit. (Finally gives traders NO excuse not to keep up that team wrangle. )


These are just ideas, so comment, please, but don't flame. I fully admit to not having seen all effects/exploitabilities of these nanos... but they're there for idea more than for actual numbers.
