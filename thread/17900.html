Easy...its not profession or level based either
Stupid teamates.

Most people save before going into pvp areas...

But a stupid teamate can take away 12 million xp when you're only 2 kills from level. Its not a one time thing either. And they don't have titles on them that say 'stay away from me' like many hardcore pvpers do.

-Chronic afkers

-Chronic smokers who can't smoke indoors (see above)
-Still living at home with mom and dad, who pull them off the computer every 5 minutes for some chore they didn't do, or to make them go to bed in the middle of a mission

-Calm-busters, even when you explain it to them 50 times ('oh I forgot to de-Q it' or 'but it was still attacking me!')

-Puller-wannabes (always ignoring the puller and getting their own mobs, people who love to rub up against doors, or follow the puller into a room when he is trying to passively pull only 1 mob. BTW don't press your nose to a locked door while someone else is unlocking it, stand back)

-Chronic LD'ers...even though its not entirely their fault, after the fifth LD that gets someone ELSE killed in an hour, you lose your sympathy

-Leeches/Ninjalooters/Beggars

-Braindead pet people...yes pathing is awful, yes pets are buggy, but you should still have /pet behind macroed and should NOT have /pet hunt macroed anywhere near your team skillbars. You should also understand aggro, sending pets near doors/in multimob rooms is guarenteed adds...let someone pull one into the room THEN you can put your uberpet on it to tank.

Know your role...traders are fun because we can often fill many roles, healer, de-facto tank (often after crit buffs damage, crowd control, buffer...we're pretty good at it too.

THATS who I'm afraid of when I log!

NOT the Novice or Apprentice standing next to me...in fact they tend to be very good in teams, since someone who understands the nuances of pvp probably understands the concept of teaming quite well. They also tend to be dedicated players, and spend a lot of time understanding the game. It doesn't hurt that they are usually very well equipped too I rarely see 'not enough ncu' messages when I try to buff them, for example.
