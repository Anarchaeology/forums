Unused Skills into IP
Just an Idea. As u lvl up raise abilities you start to gain all kinds of skill points in areas you dont need points in. Seems there is never enough IP why not make it where u can decrease unused skill IP and get a % of it back into your IP bank. I think this is in place because some skills will influence others...e.g. increasing psychic will also cause your nano pool to increase. It's a realism aspect, I believe; logical associations.

If reroll is the real desire here, maybe simply give characters the option to reset their skills at one point in their career, say halfway through, level 100. Or, let them do it more often, but with significant penalties; you can reset skills, but you lose so many thousands of IP points you currently have, to re-allocate upon reset. Just some rough ideas.
