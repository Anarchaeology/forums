About the German dimension
Just a few clarifications, for those I haven't spoken with in IRC and through e-mail already:

The servers for the German dimension will be located in the US, at the same place as the two current dimensions, not in Europe.

The dimension will be aimed at, but not limited to, German speaking players. Anybody will be able to log in on the dimension, as long as they have an open Anarchy Online account.

German players will not be forced to play on the German dimension. They are free to choose any dimension among the three.

There are no plans on allowing character transfers to the German dimension. That will have to wait for when we implement dimension travel for all dimensions.

If you have any other questions, post them here, and I'll see what I can dig up.

Why I love this Die Neue Welt
*no lag
* friendly players
*chance to learn a little German from immersion
*easy to find team

I find German players are mostly able to speak enough English to put up with the fact that I cannot speak German. In fact, most have been very friendly and helpful to me, since they already had the experience of playing on RK-1 or 2 before where the primary language there was not native to them and they understand the difficulties.

There are already many people there level 100 and up, so don't think of this as a newbie server. If anything I would say fewer new players and more experienced ones. Not that new players are bad. Just the proportion is low enough so people have time to help them out. Begging just never happens as far as my limited understanding of German can detect. People won't bug you. Nobody spams the ooc or shopping channels with macroed messages.

For German players: Do not worry, most of us non-Germans try to be polite enough not to spam your ooc and shopping channels with messages in other languages. We understand this is a server for you, and are thankful for the nice way you treat us as visitors/foreign residents.

For non-German players: Don't dismiss this dimension. I have characters on all of them, and Die Neue Welt has already become my favorite. Try it, you might like it. Lag just isn't an issue at all for me anymore, even in big cities like Omni-Ent. You don't have to be lonely though, there are enough players to team and interact with and since everyone is trying to level up in this new place, people are very likely to help each other. Please do remember this is a German dimension and if you don't speak German, don't expect a German player to magically know your language necessarily. Most do know English and in many cases several other languages, but of course some will not. Be polite. If you speak even a little German, try to use it if you can. If you must speak in your own language like me, it is a nice gesture to apologize for the necessity.

For anyone who speaks at least one language other than German, please send a tell. I am trying to form an Omni affiliated guild to assist speakers of other languages. German players are *very* welcome to join. I hope in time to have branches for Clan and Neutral so nobody needs to feel lost.

For me, Die Neue Welt = Peace and quiet, and good connection.
