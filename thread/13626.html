The elite auctioneer- check it out.
The elite auctioneer is used to sell items greater than ql 125. The shopping channel, as is, is a very inefficient way to obtain specific and many times, general items that you need.

This can be a new NPC or a machine.

Activate machine

"Hello Hobbes, will you be buying or selling"

Screen pops up, I click, "selling"

A screen pops up listing a items for sale, I scroll to the bottom of the list to an empty slot. I click the object over the chat box (like the shopping channel) and my item appears on the screen in text form.

The machine will know who I am, but I will be unknown to other players browsing this board. Prevents you being spammed by bidders saying "please sell it to me, sob, sob".

This board will have several columns: current bid, sell now, quit, timer.

I originally had a minimum bid column, but I found that this would lead to abuse of the system.

The timer will last for 30 min., 1 hour, whatever seems to work. The key is to prevent the bidders from having to wait forever and from the list becoming impossibly long.

The sellor will see the current bid but the bidder will be unknown. Those bidding will not be able to see what others have bid. This will avoid people raising each other 1 credit and if you really want something you will give a good bid to get it, knowing you may be outbid.

As the bids come up, they will be sent to the sellor as a private msg. If he likes the bid he can accept at any time, regardless of the timer. He must go to a machine to do this, however. So be aware, your bid may be accepted immediately and you will not have time to cancel.

When bidding, the money you bid will be withdrawn from your account so you can't bid money you don't have or subsequently spend. You can cancel your bid by hitting "quit". The Sellor can also withdraw his item with the same button.

At the end of the time limit, the computer will send you a tell saying "time is up, the final bid is "X", do you accept or reject. This can be done remotely as the buyer doesn't want to wait forever for you to find a machine.

The system of buyers will be annonymous as well, so the sellor can't send lies to the bidders about what others players have offered. Even the final sale should be annonymous.


This would be great for buying and selling upper level stuff and will let supply and demand determine the market price. I am psyched about this as a solo player with no upper level help.

Please bump and or help me refine this because I'm sure I've not figured it all out

I like the suggestions made here.

The machine concept is good, but I feel it may lead to people inflating their prices, hoping someone will eventually break down and pay it. Seeing posted prices tends to cause inflation. Also, with no incentive to move items, the machines will become cumbersomely filled with lots of crap.

The unknown bidding will encourage players to determine for themselves what something is worth and go for it. Also the timer will help genrate turnover and all those crappy items will get flushed.

Bionitrous,

Good pick up, my system did not cover players who logoff. Hopefully the timer will be reasonable, like 30-45 minutes. So, if you post an item an log off, you will automatically accept the final bid and you will receive a message of your sale when you log on.

Now you might say, why can't I just withdraw my item, if I have to get offline. You can, but then you would subject to the time delay, before posting it again. Players bidding on items will have their bids cancelled when they logoff.

Why doesn't logging off, just cancel the sale? I thought about that, then I realized this was an opportunity to buck the system. I you don't like your bid with a minute left, you could just logout and then login and repost it. I am trying to anticipate our shady characters.

I am currently leaning towards a one time only auction. It would really put press on buying and selling, rather than posting, waiting, posting. I say this because most people selling items will never need them, so they are losing nothing but a little time and some space in their banks. I like forcing the issue. If you do not sell, or decline the final bid you can still sell it to machines or on the old shopping channel.

Also, lets say you post during slow times and no one bids at all, you may repost without penalty.

Anymore helpful comments? This is a lot more fun than *****ing about the token changes.
