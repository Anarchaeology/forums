Leetas
There are a lot of good players out there that should be able improve their characters by equipping better items who don't particularly want to join guilds who focus almost entirely on raiding mercs.

The only option that is left up to these people is to join public raid bots. It really is as simple as that. Perhaps the 24/7 thing on Smug and Fred may be going a bit far - everything in moderation, as they say. However, I think that Smug/Fred is camped more than usual because those two uniques and the Mercs drop some of the best equipment in the game. Because access to mercs through raid bots during hours most people are online isn't viable, most people (the bots) flock to Smug/Fred because AF is at least better than most anything but Azure.

Now I realize that to many professions AF Tank > HNT and raid guilds, of course, want access to that and should be able to have access to that without too much hassle. Because people, after all, have spent a lot of time and energy building guilds that are fully equipped to take out all Mercs within 1 hour at all hours of the day and that kind of level of organization and energy shouldn't be dismissed.

But, of course, the same could be said for those who've had a hand at creating and building raid bots.

The problem I see is that Smug is dominated by raidbots and Mercs are dominated by raid guilds and a few clan organizations and recently these raid bots have started to do Mercs and so these guilds are afraid that what happened with Smub will happen to Mercs. Understandable.

I don't see why it's utterly and completely impossible for people to able to compromise. People seem to be hell bent on spending more energy whining, complaining, and making threats rather than focusing some of that on trying reach a solution to the problem. The solution can't be to remove raidbots entirely because that prevents, as I stated earlier in this post, good players' access to equipment that would benefit them and very well should given the time and energy they put into the game. (Of course the comment about scuttle leveled players is entirely valid too and these people are really obvious wherever they go and should be removed until they know more about game mechanics.)

During weekends, if both bots agree to do smug/fred only twice during the day and once during the evening, wouldn't that allow anyone who wanted to do Smug the chance at phat lewt during the days they are most able to play? And if not weekends, two other days. (Acom doesn't even do these uniques during the weekends so that'd leave only AORH and it's usually not functional during U.S evenings anyway.)

And as far as I can tell, Mercs are divvied up to those who were there first to spawn them. So, for instance, if Mercs are being killed and a small group (6 or so for spawning) wait until the Mercs are dead and declare their rights to the next spawn, this entitles them to this spawn if it is known that more will be on the way once the Mercs are actually spawned. So, if it just so happens that raid bot people have done this during "busy" hours say, 2 times a week, would it be unjust to those who have access to them -most- of the time? AORH would have their 2 chances during the day (again PST/EST), naturally as they are mostly european and Acom would have theirs time during the evenings (PST/EST). I know first hand that Acom doesn't do Mercs hardly -at all- during weekends so this wouldn't crimp any other guilds style. And if AORH chose to do a raid during the day on a weekend wouldn't affect anyone too greatly either.

I think this or some other variation could curb a lot of the negative feelings towards raidbots while at the same time allowing people like myself to get good equipment but still remain in a guild that doesn't focus entirely on raids.
