Death of a legend
This is kind of a long story so bear with me.

This is an account of a few events that have cause me to leave my guild(TC) and hit the cancel button for the last time. Its all started yesterday when TC killed Mr T and the coat dropped. We raffled it and lo and behold I won, much to my surprise. I was so happy. I just got one the most sought after items in the game. Its all mine.

Fast forward to this morning:

I get a QL200 Brillian OT 12 I want to try out. I IPR 2 skills and im good to go. But while putting all my stuff back on my beautiful Heavily Padded Overcoat goes POOF! I zone, check all bags, inv, body, relog, but nothing. As you can imagine I am pretty upset. So I petition at a bit before 10AM EST. 38 petitions infront of me. Settle in for a long wait. So for about 4-5 hours I sit and chat a run around a bit and sit. Finally i get to 1 petition infront of me, yipee. /petition status you have no petition in the que. OMFG
Ok how do you think I felt now?

So now I do something I probably shouldnt have done but needed to get results so I petitioned I was stuck knowing they would come running. And they did. First Thyrin who has helped me many times before. I explain that I wasnt stuck but had waited over 4 hours and my petition diappears. Mind you through this whole ordeal noever did I actaully get to tell any of the 3 arks that came why I originally petitioned. So now another ark comes, cant remember her name. And a third. The third was Kathrin and was the highest of the 3.

Afetr some discussion I am told that I abused the petition and I am getting a warning that I didnt follow the rules. I explain that I had followed the rules and had gotten lost in those rules. Then she tells me that my petition was answered while I was offline. This is odd due to the fact that i never logged off after I petitioned and another thing, if my petition was answered, why would I get a status report on it? Hmm. Then she asks me if Im calling her a liar. Which obviously one of us is, and if you they check the logs they will clearly see that I never logged off. So then they give me the usual have a nice day crap and bam they are gone. Leaving me sitting there wondering what the hell just happened.

So log onto IRC and plead with Cosmik in private to help me. Not that I actually thought I would get any. Basically he tells me that GMs are handling tons of petitions and that sometimes they get lost in the pile. Oh yeah one side note, petitions fall off the line after 4 hours. So they wanted me to petition again. So I do, 58 infront of you. Hmm, more than 38 will be longer than 4 hours. My petition will fall off again. So when exactly do I get my petition answered?

I leave you with that. I am not looking for sympathy but I am just telling a small story in the big scope of how FC treats their customers. It took me way too long to actually get to this point.

TC I had alot of fun and will miss all of you. Thanks for eveything.
FTC it was a blast while it lasted, some of my closest friend and Im sorry I abandoned you.
ECPCS thanks for introducing me to the life of the guild member, gave me a better understanding of the game and people in it.


To all the clan I've killed before, was fun while it lasted.

P.S. I might have left some small details out but you get the gist of the experience I had.
