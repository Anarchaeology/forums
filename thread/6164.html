The problem is what the server is doing and what the client predicts it's doing, not to mention the comunication of those two. When you see a mob 50m away from you or behind a wall it's not 100% that it is really there. AO Client predicts too much and doesn't check with the server if the prediction is true b4 making it real.

There are tons of examples. For instance if you have a mob running to you from a distance, calm it, the calm will be succesful, then watch it slide all the way to you. The mob will be close (client prediction) to you and calmed. but when you break the calm by shooting at it, it will warp back to the position it was originally calmed. Same thing happens with the "stuck" bots at the doors. If you pet is stuck and you issue a follow/attack commands to it....the client thinks it was succesful and you see yer pet gettin free off the obsticle, but in fact it is still stuck on the server and it will warp back to the position where it was, once it gets to the point for client to really check what's going on on the server.

Things really do get amplified with server-client latency.
