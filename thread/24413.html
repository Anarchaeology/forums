Current mechanics discourage teaming
Funcom and Gaute have said several times that they want to encourage teaming. Just recently they decided to not subject NCUs to oe reqs because they felt that would discourage teaming. Fair enough, but recent changes seem to go against that grain.

I had a very tough mission the other day. Tough, but I could do it on my own with just a little luck. Then a friend logged on and offered to help, so I left the mission, teamed with him and went back in. The result was not only was the XP cut by ca. 40% but now the aggro was so bad the mission wasn't doable.

Before I could walk up to a door until it opened, target the mob on the other side and send in the pets. After I was in a team, if a door opened (whether anyone was near it or not) then the mobs would come pouring out. Since I do crowd control that was handleable a few times, but inevitably there was the consecutive fumble/counters and everything was dead.

Repeat: When I was alone this mission could be soloed for excellent XP. Once I was in a team, the mission was suicide for mediocre XP. I have heard about and experienced this scenario several times since you made a change in mob aggro. Please tone down mob aggro vs. teams (at least in solo missions!) and decrease the XP penalty for teams. If at all possible, make it scale so that the current XP for 6 person teams taking on BIG reds is about the same but that a two person team doesn't make both parties wish they were soloing.

Those aren't bugs, and are not likely to be fixed.

For one if you do a solo mission with more than one person then you get team aggro. That is there to lower the desire to do easier solo missions as a team instead of doing team missions.

As soons as you team with another you will lower the Xp that you will recieve. If you add a third to the team it will lower even further. This is simply in relation to the ease of which more players can kill a mob. Now if you make a full team if six the XP will jump back up to close to the solo value. That was designed to promote teaming by making it more valuable to fill a team.

Solo missions are supposed to be for one person.
