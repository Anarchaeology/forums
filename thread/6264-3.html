I'm kind of surpised no one mentioned this:

There is an NPC robot called "Best in Brass" that will spawn in Galway Shire and attack the guards outside the Rome Blue east gates. Best in Brass is a nasty high-level bot with AOE snare/DD attacks in addition to his normal attacks, which hit the guards for 300-850 per attack.

He will fight the guards and kill them, but they respawn fast enough that after several sets of respawns and about 15-30 minutes (depending on luck) Best in Brass will finally be killed by the guards.

UNLIKE NORMAL GUARD KILLS the body of Best in Brass actually stays on the ground. After a few minutes the loot block lifts and anyone can loot the corpse. The corpse always has an item to my knowledge, and it is NOT always the same. I have personally seen him drop two of the items on the list for this quest. The one I got was #8 on the list, and the one another player looted and showed me was #7 on the list.

I have also heard from other players in an OOC channel discussion that one person had looted #4 and another person had looted #6 on the list.

I think it's entirely possible that Best in Brass drops every item on the list, though my discussions with others indicate that #8 is his common drop. Unfortunately this battle with him doesn't happen often enough to make it worth camping his death rather than going after the -lets. I have no idea what his spawn rate is, but I have seen his fight to the death with the guards several times now and one person watching once said they thought he spawned once every several hours.
