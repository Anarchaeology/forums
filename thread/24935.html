More Attractions in Omni-Entertainment?
Ok, Baboons and the Rompa Bar are fun to hang out in for a while- nobody has a problem with Reet Retreat either- but eventually they get to be the same old thing.

Theres tons of empty space in Omni-Entertainment. So why not open up some new attractions? I mean, you can always forfeit a mission entrance or two.

Suggestions:

Open up that communications tower on the black hill thing in Entertainment-East to people without yalms. It can be an observation tower! Plus, you should add a bit more floorspace, and maybe a bartender/party mixer so people can even throw sky-parties up there. I mean, wouldnt it be awesome with that view and the staggering height?

Add a classy café-type place. The café could sell coffee, tea, desserts, and various other treats.

How about an expensive restaurant? You could even have NPC waiters and waitresses take orders and place food on the tables. Or, better yet, you could have a mix of NPCs and actual players. I imagine itd be a little like Rompas, but with higher capacity and a bit more...expensive-looking.

A swimming pool, indoor or outdoor

I like the idea of a theater where players or ARKs could organize plays, either with scripts or just improvisation. Plus, players could do stand-up comedy, or interview people.


If any of the rest of you have ideas, post them! I want to hear what you all think of this

-Maulie (As people tend to call me)
