AO starting New Server????
This is the mail I got today (it was in German, so I'll translate loosely):

quote:
Ladies and Gentlemen,

as you might have noticed there is no German Server for AO.
This will change in the near future - hopefully to please all German speaking players.
Funcom and Gamigo will probably start in April with the Betatest of a German Server for AO. (http://ao.gamigo.de)

This Server will have German gamemasters and - of course - German Support (the game itself will stay English at first). Gamigo has already some experience in the MMORPG field with their game "Die Vierte Offenbarung" and has a well-trained support team.

We'd be pleased if you could post some news about this to your site.
*snip*
A third server? Is this needed? Why doesn't FC inform us about this? Will ppl be able to move their chars? Questions, questions...
The reason why I'm concerned? Well I'm Guild-Leader of one of the biggest German-speaking Omni Guilds on RK1. Will this tear us apart? What will happen....
I, myself, shall *not* move, that's for sure, not even if I can take my chars. For one I've made by far too many friends among the non-German players on R-K1 and I would dearly miss my friends, for two I prefer playing in a multi-national environment.
So what's the deal? Gamigo's website is less than informative on this...
Any official information????
