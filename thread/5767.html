Campaign 1 Operation 1 - Troll Under The Bridge.
A strategic force of employees from Omni-Tek will be making a few messages over the next few weeks.
These messages will be directed at the clans.
We will strike at different advantage points around the terraformed regions of Rubi-Ka, places of key clan infrastructure.

These attacks will be of high visibility, with low complication or organization.
- To show how weak the clans really are.

I will say that I am not acting on any part of Philip Ross orders.
I will say this though. Philip Ross... You best watch out... Mutiny is within the ranks of Omni-Tek.
If you wish to keep your role on this planet you best listen up to your advisors for once.
The clans ARE taking advantage of the Amnesty.
They are building up the forces with experience and generating fear among our own inexperienced troops.
When war does finally break the seal then do not be surprised if we are still throwing leet dolls across the front line.
You do not need to play a game of chess with them. Infact it is so simple - you only just really need to be playing checkers.

I call all over generals in other detachments of Omni-Tek to go forth and do the same as I.

Enough of this Amnesty. Enough of the clans. War is already here.
And I am not having anymore of my workers attacked by clan forces. This planet was terraformed by us, and thus is ours.
Long live Omni-Tek, and it's goal of ever expanding our galatic fronts.

But I send a warning to the employees of ICC and the offices of ARK:
You have no jurisdiction nor interest in what I am about to do. You are only observers to this planet.
And believe me you do not want to see the burning trail I can leave behind.

And to what ever length happens to me... well let me tell my forces this...
No matter in any form I will continue to lead my forces in spirit.
I will take full responsibility on my forces actions, as they are following orders.
