/OOC Is there going to be any story for Rubi-Ka2?
Hi,

I live on Rubi-Ka 2, and despite its ups and downs, I truly love the place. My unfortunate problem is that there isn't any form of a story there. From time to time there are small mini-events, but they happen about once every three months, and they do not have anything to do with the ongoing plot. I enjoy the mini-events, and do not wish for them to stop, but I would like some of the major groundbreaking events to be included on Rubi-Ka 2. We may not have as big of a population on Rubi-Ka 2, but we still deserve a chance to be a part of things. There are many good people there who would love to be a part of a story event, any type. I'm not trying to whine or anything, I just wanted to bring to everyones attention that Rubi-Ka 2 should be supported. It wouldn't be too hard to hold one of those big attacks, or peace talks, or an ICC meeting on Rubi-Ka 2. I would like to invite the ICC to come to our dimension, to see the people, to really see what it is like. Do not underestimate us! Our numbers mean NOTHING!

We will do anything to be noticed as an important part of the story, so please whoever has taken the time to read this show your support for Rubi-Ka 2!
