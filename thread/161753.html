First steps on Rome
I walked down from the shuttle looking around. I felt a bit confused. What had I done when I accepted the job they had offered me...? There's no turning back now I'm at Rome already.

Friendly stewart showed me my appartement: a small hole if I've seen one. Well, everything starts from the bottom. Repeating that mantra in my mind I commenced first physical training in holo-deck. What for? That's the question I asked myself countless of times. Well that part had been answered on my contract, being wartime and all...

Tall buildings of Rome, pillar-like statues, friendly people... yes it was as I dreamed it would be. Now I just have to get to know people around here and inspect the situation with the clans. Are they as big menace as the OT officials told? That remains to be seen...

(( Hello to you Byrokki ))

From the glorious structure of a Rome administrative office building, Gabriel studied his old base of operations and memories came to him once again of his initial training within the organisation that was the Phoenix Foundation. His mentor, Kharema, had shown him how to survive to an extent of what little experience he possessed, in what way to save precious knowledge collected throughout the course of extensive missions sent from Omni-Tek's operators.

Those times were good. Gabriel was now changed with time as is always the case.

Attention was casually focused on a transfer shuttle that had approached his eye level, squinting even through his sunglasses at the fuel burn trails. The craft steadied itself for a moment, turned on it axis and pushed on up and through the atmosphere of Rubi-Ka.

"Correct location and time..."

Passing through the gates leading out into the fields surrounding Rome, Byrokki seemed pleased to have returned from, what Gabriel would guess, the subways that ran beneath the city. New pasts were being written, possible futures approaching for another individual yet to discover his potential. Lowering the scope of his rifle away from Byrokki, Gabriel turned to his objective whom had just stepped out from high rise he'd been watching.

"...welcome to Rubi-Ka."
