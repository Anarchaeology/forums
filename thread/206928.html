Light Shift

It all lay before him within his arms, between his fingers. The lights that glowed warmly into his vision caused a frown to crease across his forehead, muscles hardening with the tension he caused to ripple throughout his body. Drawing his fingers to touch across both hands, he settled them across his lap whilst pursing his lips and sliding a released finger across the patterns that were etched under his skin. Irritating images flowed into his minds eye as the tattoos seemed to burn out from his flesh.

No angels this evening. Not for him. Others were now forming around his body, his dream that he hated to admit existed. Forcing a bitterness into a constructive version of destruction, a controlled layer of chaos gripping his gut and wrenching it back and forth, inside and out. Teeth tore at him in lipless speeches, they wanted their side to be heard but he would drown them out with the sound of his own blood. No more causes to be blindly followed without question.

The General knew this. Perhaps he would respect just what lay within, something that had been felt for... no. It had never been there. Something had. Had what? What point could be found in the end of this crushing hallway, what shifted the angle in which he approached it?

All occuring again, over again. Raising his head up to the chilled air he screamed out amongst the vapour... and silence entered after the violent echo.

Contact was made from those who operated with him. One had already been lost to emotion, possibly. Dericad. Things do change, can be changed. He felt blood warmer than ever now... always a good sign. Zero had also been keeping himself out of sight, away from oozing eyes but the move was necessary to set solid his involvement. There was one whose mouth was set to open up pages to a book not meant to be read, so had to be closed. Contract sent and word settled with a lethal signature, Zero will be sent. The burn sets in. It is that which shall cover the path not meant to be followed.

Gabriel shifted his feet in the water he lay within, supplied by a mountain stream, brought himself out of the water and dressed. The gears worked well. Dark Angel itself will be made manifest this night. The beauty of confusion and uncertainty to Gabriel caused a vacant smile to creep across his skin through his lips.
