A short history of powerbalance
Hi!

I am Wolfe. Maybe you have heard about me or read something I wrote. I noticed there has been much talk about something I created together with a few friends a long time ago. Before you proceed with reading this please beware that this is not an attempt at creating yet another whinefest or other unpleasant debate regarding peoples failing intellect, age, gender, religion or whatever thing you will attempt to bring in to turn anything me and some of my friends say into an unpleasant reading experience. If you want to voice negative stuff do it with style.

(Too many people just try to use foul language to kill the arguments of their enemies. With these boards beeing heavily moderated it wouldnt be good practice to make it a habit out of using foul language to make people shut up or such tactics might be used in return and we see an escalated conflict with a result no one will be happy about).

I am a leader of a guild called Storm. I and a group of friends made this guild a long time ago and we have had good fun making it and playing this game together. Storm has existed since the first day it was possible to make organsations ingame (or possibly the second day or so, its too long ago to remember exactly). When Storm was a young guild there were many other guilds around on both Clan and Omni side that were fairly equally "strong" (ooc: as in having noticeable numbers of players playing many hours per day together). This balance of strength lasted for a fairly long time.

The old records where my organisation keeps track of allies and enemies has many entries describing the strong and potentially strong enemy guilds in now what we can call ancient times. I personally knew about a noticeable number of guilds that were on the brink of becoming guilds that would one day be able to rival the strength of my own organisation.

This knowledge led Storm to follow a path that had an agenda where we took upon ourselfs to help with creation and survival of allied organisations (basically other clan guilds). We know that the big enemy is Omni-Tek, so all other clan guilds were and are our allies. We have been using a number of "secret" tricks to help establish other clan guilds than just our own. However it is difficult to make a strong guild and despite our efforts to avoid making it more difficult for other guilds to gain strength many potentially strong allies failed to gain a noteworthy status until todays date. I am however happy to notice that a good number of other guilds now have managed to get established well enough to be a noteworthy force in the battle against omni-tek.

One day the records of potential enemies got rewritten, this was over a year ago now. The large number of potentially strong enemy guilds got reduced to a smaller number, namely the number 1. I am not well informed about the details regarding this move from our enemies, however I can describe the result of their actions from my own perspective.

Each of the potentially strong omni guilds were based on a core of players who were the driving force behind those guilds struggle for power. Each of these guilds would given time be able to establish something I usually call a "culture" where their respective organisations would build a home for the players on the Omni-Tek side of the conflict. The day when my records show a noticeable change was when we noticed how all these potentially strong omni guilds were removed from the list and replaced with just one guild, namely The Council.

Somehow one of the omni guilds decided to recruit almost all the core members of all the other potentially strong omni guild. This now one and only noticeable Omni-Tek organisation had to replace all the other guilds and they were more or less standing alone against the still functional alliance of clan guilds where I myself was just leading one guild among many. This one guild fought bravely but it seems they made some bad choices and since they already had removed all other guilds from our records of potential enemies it became very easy to battle and defeat Omni-Tek. Until the creation of The Council we clan guilds had to either focus our attention on one enemy among many, or we had to spread our efforts thin to try work against many guilds at the same time. Now however the path to victory was simple: Defeat The Council.

A few months after the creation of The Council we could make a new entry in the records of potentially strong enemies stating "NONE". The Council as enemy was eliminated from the powerbalance.

It has taken Omni-Tek a very long time to try and recover from the residues of what The Council accomplished. Meanwhile the clan guilds have been establishing themselfs and preparing for the first omni guild to get an entry in the records of noteworthy enemies. From my point of view I can understand that the Omni-Tek guilds are having a very rough time gaining victories over Clan in this war we fight. If The Council never would have existed then I myself believe that at least a number of the guilds that existed prior to The Council would have risen up to fight the battle for Omni-Tek still today.

The Council defeated Omni-Tek for us, the clan guilds got the upper hand in the battle the day they were created and the omnis have never been able to recover from such a devastating blow.

Will Omni-Tek ever recover? Maybe, but it will require sacrifices very few are willing to make. Pride is hard to overcome especially when you are forced by an enemy you can run from.


If you have any questions regarding this brief history and theory go ahead and ask. I might not be able to give satisfying answers, but maybe we all can learn something new about the community we are part of together.


Regards,

Someone here asked what kinds of sacrifices the Omnis would have to do to be able to use the full strength of their forces. At least this is how I see the question since Omnis really dont have any influence over the clans abilities or intentions.

I myself dont know any solid answer to this question. However I have made a few observations regarding massive numbers of players in this and other similar games.

The one most disturbing an problematic fight for those few who really seek unity and power thru cooperation is to conquer the unreliable nature of other players. As Baffo said The Council pretty much disbanded itself due to things that happened without much outside interferance. When you have a guild that relies heavily upon certain individuals and these people break the law and gets executed your organisation loses momentum and it is fairly easily happened that those who relied upon the now executed (OOC - consider executed people to be banned due to one reason or another) members will decide to give up and stop building on the organisation they had together.

I have been wrestling this problem myself as the athorities executed a number of my own officers I had a large number of their friends decide to give up on the game. Having this incredible loss of what we had been building together came close to making me give up myself. I can imagine many other guild leaders actually giving up for real rather than starting over with new people.

Similar problems can arise where one guild over time makes a few misstakes so large portions of the guilds members decide to leave the guild and create a new one under new leadership. The common scenario here is that the new leadership after a number of weeks notice they got more work than they asked for, go into hiding and many players get a dissfunctional guild with inexperienced leadership and unestablished procedures... and so on and so forth.

The sacrifice I speak of that I know has to be made is the one where all the people who possibly could be your ally sets their differencies aside forget old animosities and grudges to achieve a common goal. Can you cooperate with a guild whose guild leader took loot from you by strongarming you into agreeing on a crappy deal? Can you cooperate with a guild that "ninjaed" the land you battled for to get ownership of yourself? Can you coperate with a guild that is based on members who rather left your own guild to make a new guild when you think they should have stayed to work on the guild you had together... The sacrifices can be many, and I myself make sure to avoid involving personal feelings, grudges and possible animosities whenever fighting for the common cause of the clans.

Omni battle leaders also seem to be making strategical and tactical errors when planning their battles against clanners. I wont try to tel you the secrets of those as that would compromise my own guilds security.
