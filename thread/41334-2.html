Man, I had some guy in my guild begging for Albrecht Tank Armor. He was like a 140+ Trader. So, I bust my ass and manage to get him a 197 and then find out he's wearing full Carbonum underneath it. Sheesh!

But, seriously. =) I always take time to drop buffs on anyone who's asking for credits. Having a high level MP, I'm sure that dropping 'Teachings of MC/TS' on an 18 Engineer is worth a fair amounts of credits to the hard working noobs out there.

Other occasions, if I get begged while dumping loot after a mission, I'll find something that would sell for the approximate level of credits they beg for and give it to them to sell. Half the time they're annoyed that they actually have to go to a shop and sell it.

Finally, I do always help Neutral faction mates with credits, advice, items they can use for the future. Usually it's the advice that comes first and then the rest follows if there's any sincerity in the conversation. I try to avoid financing Twinks as I know how utterly easy that is considering I got 6 characters with Yalms and full nano crystal sets waiting in their banks. Used to be easy to slip in a question like 'have you seen the big pyramid, yet?' and get them to fumble and say 'oh, yeah the one in BS' to blow their covers.

When it comes to beggars we all have to realize that while we often do them no favors to finance them for 30 levels, it's also possible that by getting a little guy 'over the hump' we may be preventing a good guy from leaving the game due to frustration. Then again, if we let 'em leave, we may be doing them a favor in the long run.

Bio,

In how many instances do you get someone saying "I'm having trouble understanding..." or "This game is tough..."?

Almost every time (estimating 95/100 times), the beggar says "Can I have creds?" or "I'm a n00b...Got creds?" or "/s Need 20k for nano crystal!".

Understand that if anyone is truly ignorant to the game, it's pretty easy to tell.

If the new player (or even the twink) would like access to an org's resources, they can join an org. If not, they are more than welcome to learn the lessons of life the "hard way" (remember Bio, I soloed to roughly 145 - took a year, but at least I know where "Wailing Wastes" is, unlike a certain ARK I know).

...and saying "damn, I've done 30 missions and no Albrecht Tank Armor. If you find some, let me know" isn't begging.

Folks join orgs for a combination of 2 reasons:

1) Sharing of the social experience (Community)
2) Sharing of the game resources (Commonwealth)

Honestly, I cannot think of any reason to join an org other than these 2 reasons (anywhere on the spectrum, of course...You may have someone who is 100% community and 0% Commonwealth, or 50/50). Can you think of other reasons?

Anyway, I agree that we should help people within our community (org-mates, faction-mates, etc), but blindly providing anything to an "unknown" seems silly. These resources can be used to enhance our own community.
