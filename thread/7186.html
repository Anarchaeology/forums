Suggestion for nerfing shotguns
Before you all flame me, I wield a shotgun myself. But seeing the huge cries for "nerf shotguns" I thought I'd put out an idea and see what the community thinks.

1) reduce the range on all shotguns (except urban snipers) by 5-6m. This would bring the Home Defender down to the range of a Gamma Beamer, a Krutt below the range of a Nova, and a Vektor very close to the range of a nova.

2) add a Ranged Energy requirement to the Vektor. This is very much in line with the description on the Vektor.

3) (optional) add a flingshot requirement to the Home Defender. This could be either good or bad.

This would still allow shotguns to help a soloer or hunter significantly, but reduce their effectiveness in pvp. Thoughts?
