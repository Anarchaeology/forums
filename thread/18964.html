Bars
Rompa Bar-Omni Ent NW of the Whompas in North Entertainment district

Baboon's-Omni Ent NW of the Whompas in North Entertainment district (just north of Rompa)

Reet's Retreat-Stret West bank, SW of the Whompa, the biggest the building there (just follow the metal road)

Happy Rebel Inn-Tir, near the grid exit, and there's another bar in Tir on the other side

The Cup-Athens, next to the grid exit (on the right)

Neuters 'R Us-Newland, in the back near the apartments and outside eating place


The Rompa Bar is very active especially in the evenings with nice waiter service by Lunar Entertainment Co. (Don't forget to tip!)

Baboon's isn't too active anymore but people still go there and it has events

Reet's Retreat is fairly active with a lot of dancing in the evening's with DJ Tarryk

Happy Rebel Inn-Since the amnesty is over I can't really say how active this is anymore but it had people in the evenings on some days

That other bar in Tir- I wouldn't know, the guards would kill me before I get there

The Cup-A couple people in the evenings or I just have bad timing

Neuters 'R Us-Do people know if this even exists because it isn't used whenever i go there
