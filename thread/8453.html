Player Events Site
It is good to see the number of player events these days both clan and omni even on RK2 now. It is a far cry from when I started and the only roleplaying to be found was the people who used to meet up in baboons and run parties.

But the attention the parties get on the web news sites is still low, but only to be expected as we have several AO news items a day. People do post here on the boards about events, but I think people forget about them once the post is a few days old.

Now i currently look after the baboons nightclub site where i post news about events i do coming up. I also post news on events which have taken place with screen shots. I also started profiling the party goers and had a few new features I am planing to add.

At the moment it is just restricted to the events run in baboons or by the group of people whom first started out there. I have been thinking about expanding this for some time to include all parties on both servers to try and increase the quality of events and the number of people attending them.

Before I do this I wanted some feedback on the idea, and if the guilds and people hosting parties at the moment would support the site. I will not be able to attend all parties so would rely on yourself to cover the party for the site.
