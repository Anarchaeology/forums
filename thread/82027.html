Disguise Kit for Agents
I think agents need a way to temporarily change their names to facilitate any "real" chance at assassinations, let them fill the role of silent, unknown assassin. The way things are now an agent can attack you, maybe kill you, but you'll always know who did it. They can change their faces, but not their names so what good is it. Agents can switch sides to spy or get close to someone, but not if their name is known previously.

Have an item that will change an agents name to a random name for a period of time depending on the ql.
