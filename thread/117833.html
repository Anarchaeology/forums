EVENT: Rubikan Olympics - coming soon to a planet near you!
((OOC/ Greetings, let me just point out first that final dates and times have yet to be finalised, but we are hoping for 2 –3 weeks time for this. The timing for events will be spread so people from all corners of the world will have a chance to partake in something (if they wish), and we hope that as many as possible will be up for a laugh and take part.))


Welcome to the Rubikan Olympics!
Its that time again to put on the running shorts and dust off your Leet mallet and come out to the Olympics. The events as always are designed for all people, no matter of faction standing.


Day 1 (Monday):
Opening Parade
The Borealis Speedway (only 18-months after the first, but yes a sequel)

Day 2 (Tuesday):
100m Fixers Dash
Yalmaha Tunnel Racing - down the passages of Dead Maze.

Day 3 (Wednesday):
Archery
Speed Cross-country Boating boat racing from the Bronto Burger stand in Holes in Wall to Borealis and on via the Whompa through Newlands and first one into the Lake wins.

Day 4 (Thursday):
Rubikan Pitch and Putt Golf Masters – played in the usual Rubikan fashion of mallets and Leets…find out just how far you can propel that Leet with your mallet, and hopefully in the hole! Also involves best golfing attire award.
‘trox Masterminds contest to be held at Baboons nightclub, in Omni-Entertainment.

Day 5 (Friday):
Clay MP Shooting – How many MPs can you shoot down in 30secs?
Marathon

Day 6 (Saturday):
Football ((or those of the American disposition, soccer)) to be played on the recently built PW football stadium (formally part of the RK World Cup).
Swimming

Day 7 (Sunday):
Relay Race
Closing Ceremony + alcohol at the Reets Retreat (Last Ditch)

Rubikan Pitch and Putt Golf Masters
Played in the usual Rubikan fashion of mallets and Leets…
Everyone, of any level or profession is equiped with a sledgehammer and teamed with a leet. The mission if they choose to accept it to propel the leet, using the forementioned hammer as far as possible into the hole.

Awards for "best" dressed in golfing attair will also be given at this event

((OOC/ more a RP event))

Hole 1:
Tee is at the Tir Pavillion with the hole at the grid terminal.

Hole 2:
Tee is the bridge between the two sides of Rome, and the target/hole is the centre island pillar - a bit of a water hole.

Hole 3:
Tee off from the Broken Shores grid exit and hole is into the Pryamid.

Hole 4:
Tee off from Newlands grid exit and hole is a small outpost area.

Hole 5:
Tee off from outdoors reclaim terminal and hole is a mission hole (co-ords given at event).

Hole 6:
Tee off from a mission hole and hole is the Newlands grid terminal.

Hole 7:
Tee is at the Borealis grid terminal with the hole at the Subway.

Hole 8:
Roof of Borealis subway into the Last Ditch Whompa.

Hole 9:
Last Ditch Whompa into Reets Retreat.
