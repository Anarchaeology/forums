Inner Circle - Our 250 tower
I've been nice to most Clanners around Rimor... hell I help out on buffs, I give a cred if they ask and even tank on NPC hunts if they are in sore need of one...

But basicly what happened today is not something that happened over night...

Dear clanners:

Have you ever tried fighting on a loosing side since the game started? We have, and we've seen the reactions it's had on omnis... Several quitting/going Clan doesn't help the situation or the imbalance that has grown over the past year. I can't blame the Omnis that turn clan, but I *will* give a slap on those who give omni hell for trying to rise up.. Clanners have, as far as I know treachury more than anyone.. Tarasque problems, Guild issues etc etc... lots of greed... We've tried to stear clear of this after we learned our lesson in Camelot.

The leaving of clanners to turn omni is a *huge* sacrifice and it includes loosing their tokens, friends and so on...

I've been omni since the start, fought several clanners at a time, lost many, won a few and I'm starting to find it difficult to keep faith... so does most omni. It just hasn't payed off to stay omni, so why the hell do it?

Today Inner Circle Merged with Last Hope and got a few more members on the side as well. This is to try one last time to change the turn of the tide... Doesn't anyone get how frustrating it can be for omni to fight you guys? Our best attempts have been struck back. hell we show up with *all* we have and it's still not enough..

Noone in their right mind can say omni/clan is balanced now..

There are many Answers as to how to balance this... we could use a year or two putting up posters or trying to convince clanners to go omni. but how much would that do?

We simply play our role now.. We are omni tek, and our goal is to fight clanners. As mentioned, treachury might've been the only way we can get this base... well what does that tell you? We need balance... This, with the merge to a new strong omni guild is the start of a new hope for omni...

My apologies goes out to those clanners whom I know.. Jdude and lots of other OC'ers, I hope you won't hate me for this as you know what we've been through as omnis the past time. People as these are the few I really can apologize to.

I've recieved tells that qualify as Harrasment by angry clanners, but I understand some feelings have been stirred up.. I just want to say, in the end... try to be omni for some time before you pass that judgement.. ever tried to command apathy to battle or to raise the spirit amongst someone who haven't eaten for days?

hell, go try it out some... I don't apologize for trying. And I'm tired of excusing.

so since I've probably dropped my rep down the drain, I have one last statement on behalf of omni.. no matter if we keep this base or not...

it reads:

"Up yours"


Deng - Omni
