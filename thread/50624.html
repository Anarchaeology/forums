As an MA I'd really not bother with tradeskills other than maybe a bit in PharmaTech and the obligatory lots in Computer Literacy. And nano crystals need about 4 skills to make... not worth the expenditure of IP.

To be honest only trader and engineer can hope to make good use of most trade skills (Pharma Tech and Nano Programming being the exceptions).

At high levels, you'll have spare IP, then you might want to consider it as a sideline.
