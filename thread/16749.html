Wow!
WOW! This game has come a long way. I've been alot of researching and reading since AO's release and I am actually surprised it is good as Vederman in PC Gamer boasts. I shyed away from any further MMOG after getting burned by WWIIOL release (its still a good game though).

I downloaded it yesterday afternoon, installed it, played it and went through a barrage of video card problems. At first I was like "what the f*ck!", but I got through it and fixed it. Once I got beat the memory dump problem I had a great time.

Created an human Omni-Tek solider, he's now lvl 3 after 2 hours of play. This game is great looking first of all. Secondly, the sci-fi theme is great. Very refreshing from EQ.

Now I have only played 2 hours, but I plan on logging in plenty of hours this coming weekend. I'm glad to have joined you guys and looking forward to very many fun filled nights of adventuring on Rubi-Ka
