Old AO "Features"
OK, so I'm bored at work, but I was thinking about all those old bugs that used to make playing this game so "fun". So I thought I would start this little thread to let people who weren't playing when they where in game know about that (hmmm, sure there should be a comma or something in that sentance somewhere )

please add any other bugs I missed, only rule is they have to have been fixed.

1) Newland Grid. Lots of people talk about this, and say they want it back. The reason for this is it used to be about half a mile up in the air, so anyone coming out of it would fall to their deaths, and I mean death, as fall damage wasn't capped at that point (did anyone survive it? I never saw anyone walk away, but I could be wrong). Used to be fun watching newbies falling from the sky

2) "Take it easy". Brought in to stop people continually pressing the q key in fights, the game was supposed to give you the message "take it easy" if you did. However what it often did was wait till you got agroed by some big meany, then every time you tried to fight back, would tell you to "take it easy", thereby giving you a free trip to the reclaim.

3) Various types of mission not working. Like the fix radar or put sack of poison in mutants food machine ones. The Machine never spawned in the mission.

please add any other actualy features of AO that have now gone for good too, only one I can actually think of off the top of my head is when the weight limit actually worked, and you couldnt carry 40000 rounds of Ammo out with you

hugs
