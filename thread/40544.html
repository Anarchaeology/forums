2 new soldier nanos
Here are 2 ideas I had for new soldier nanos. I'm hoping to at least partially address problems I hear from soldiers: boredom, tanking and PvP.

Ok, the first idea is to give soldiers something to do in team combat other than wait for specials to recycle and to help you hold aggro. It would be a small reflection buff you could cast on others during a fight. It should have a short cast time, take a small amount of NCU, last no more than 30 seconds and only reflect 5 or 10% of the damage taken (not stacking with or overriding any other reflect nanos). The damage reflected, though, would be credited to the caster as in SoldierJoes reflect shield caused nn damage to EvilMonster. This would be similar to how HoTs and HE are credited to the caster.

So how does this help the soldier? Well, first you have to pay enough attention to know whos being hit. Then you have to select your teammate and fire off the nano. This gives you a reason to not watch television during combat. Second, it adds to the aggro you generate, helping you to get the aggro back  thus making you a slightly better tank.

Second idea is for those soldiers who arent happy with the current balance of damage vs healing in PvP. This would be a line of nanos built around the concept of aggravating an existing injury. The idea is to lock in a certain amount of damage for the duration of the nano  thus limiting how much benefit the target can receive from heals. It would be something like this (numbers are just total guesses):
QL50 Aggravate Wound
NCU 100
Nanocost 100
Duration 120s
Attack Time 2s
Recharge Time 6s
Range 20m
Requirements Bio Met: 350
Mat Met: 350
Visual profession: soldier
Targets current <= targets max health - 600
Effects modify target max health = max health - 300

In this example, if you have the target down at least 600 health, you could lock in 300 points of that damage for the next 2 min. This is done by reducing the targets max health by 300 for the duration of the nano. Other nanos in this line would probably keep the restriction of locking in only half the damage inflicted, but would allow larger numbers.

Well, hopefully these ideas don't stink. Again, the specific numbers listed are just guesses.

Not bad, I like the second idea, but here is my take on it:

Instead just turn it into a regular old debuff, forget all the 'lock ind damage' crap... Have multiple levels of it that give a negative bonus to max health of the target for a short period. Examples:

1) Jagged Wound
-150 Health for 3 Minutes

2) Deep Wound
-300 Health for 3 Minutes

3) Mortal Wound
-600 Health for 3 Minutes

4) Headshot
-1200 Health for 3 Minutes

Perhaps these would all have a lockdown time like Sureshot and other such nanos, thus only usable every say 5 minutes. This way you get to fire one off at the start of your duel and have it last long enough so that it will end one way or another. At the very least these nanos would help counteract the outside (or even self cast for some professions) health buffs that the target will likely have in PvP combat. As for PvM, throwing a -1200 health bonus on a mob ever 5 minutes in your mission isnt too bad either, it's an extra hit or burst or whatever
