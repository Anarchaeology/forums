Re: Traders and Team Heals
Cosmik, please could you pass on the following comments to your nano designer.

The Trader in AO is not *even* a tradeskill character as despite our tradeskill buffs there is almost nothing worthwhile doing with them. As the game is currently implemented Tradeskills should be disregarded in relation to our abilities. Also please remember that AC drains and debuffs have no noticable effect on combat and nobody has the space for our AC buffs. Wrangles cannot effectively be used during combat (they have too little effect, they use too much NCU, they only last 3 minutes and we cannot cast them continuously. Our charms do not work and our drain nano line is so ineffective its pointless. The reason I'm adding all this is that in a real team the trader only has two functions - heal and calm. Without effective healing you leave us only calms. This may not be how the trader line of nanos is envisaged as working but ask any trader and you'll get the same answer we heal and calm.

Whilst I would like to believe you I find it stretches the bounds of credibility to say that a class who's only startup nanos are team healing and health drains is not intended to be a competent team healer. Despite the PvP damage change health payments have always worked in the same way, the 50% change is neither here nor there - we were still able to function as a good healer. I find it extremely unlikely such a wild difference in abilities was simply an oversight. To my knowledge, we have a greater number of heals and healing nanos than any other class other than Doctors.

Now, on to single target heals (in our case health haggles). I'm sure you are aware that very few doctors and almost no traders use single target healing except after a fight. There is one very good reason for this - team health bars are bugged and extremely laggy and give a totally untrustable view of a players actual health. When considering what capability our team heals should have please remember that we will not be able to use single target heals in the vast majority of circumstances. This is unfortunate but in the real world its a fact of life.

OK on to specifics. Most traders would agree with you with the concept of our healing having a downside attached - it's fundamental to the profession. However where we differ is the concept of 15 seconds and 1k+ damage being in anyway usable. Firstly 15 seconds is an age in a fight. Its more than enough time for team mates to die.

As simply as I can state it a 15 second lockdown period would make the nano useless for anything other than a one off emergency heal during a fight. However, having to take the full DoT (in excess of 1000hp for the higher ones) is a huge sting.

The enormous delay between healing (twice that of an MA and 3 times that of an Advent) means we wont be able to use our healing to save either ourselves or our teammates - 15 seconds is more than enough time for a squishy profession to die. MAs are not even considered a secondary healing class, their team heal is a nice little bonus they throw in when it might help but just too slow to do much else. At 15 seconds our heals will be twice as useless - the extra amount we heal for means little, its the frequency that counts for everything. By all means halve the value but please dont take away the frequency.

Now, assume you do give us a 15 second delay and full DoT damage over that 15 seconds. If I decide to use the nano its a fairly good bet that things are going badly for the team. If my trader is 1000pts down on HP and receives a shift in aggro due to a crit I'm in deep deep trouble. Further, since many doctors try to use their more effective single heals there is a goood chance I wont receive any outside healing during the DoT period.

So not only do I have a 15 second recharge, before using the heal again but there is a very good chance I will have to make up the health loss myself. Either:

a) I have to stop and use heal packs (two with a total time of 30 seconds for full health recovery for the heal I use, or
b) I have to cast multiple health drains (many seconds and much nano)

Either of these two options will delay the time before I next feel safe and able to heal (espcially with healing drawing aggro I will be inclined to be fairly careful).

In summary, in the real (imaginary) AO world the proposed change effectively cuts our healing down by a factor of more than 4 and makes it very risky to use. I doubt many traders will bother investing many points into BioMet for a nano solely useful as an emergency heal and one which adds more risk to the trader in an already risky fight.

[b]Other solutions...[b]

Firstly it's not necessary to have a damage amount that is greater than the healing, any significant damage taken makes health payment vastly inferior to any other heal in the game, having a damage level higher than the actual heal is not required to give us a significant penalty.

The following are more workable ideas from our standpoint and still keep within the scope of your design concept (option a is by far the most sensible).

a. Make the team heal *team* only (i.e. not the Trader) and have a single DoT worth say 40% of the heal value. Keep everything else the same. This allows the Trader to heal like mad for a few seconds but casting more than a few times is very likely to kill them or make us so vunerable to any change in Aggro we have to stop and heal (either with a heal kit or drain) every couple of casts.

b. Make the DoT component a single hit for say 60%-70% of the heal value but keep everything else the same. This keeps the heal very effective for team members but means the Trader gets hardly any net healing from it hence cannot tank and heal and cannot risk taking aggro while healing.

c. Make DoTs of the same heal Stack. At the moment casting two identical DHPs gets you a single DoT. Casting two different DHPs gets you two DoTs. If each cast caused an additional an DoT to be added the Trader would soon be unable to continue casting and would probably die since heal kits would not be able to keep pace with 5-10 simultaneous DoTs. Again, this keeps the nano useful for backup healing but makes it a very risky and double edged sword that would be pretty much impossible to use as a primary healer.

TO elaborate on that point:

I think traders are supposed to be specialists of a kind -- specialist in a method, though, not specialists in an end. Our means are specific -- drains, funnells, logistical stuff -- but in our ends, the class is generalist -- we do a little bit of everything. We shift stuff around.

And in a group situation, what needs to be shifted around most is health. In a group, you need to be able to heal damage, prevent damage, or deal damage, or you aren't helping.

So it seems to me that, in our end effect, we should be about on par with Adventurers, the other generalist class -- able to fill in in different roles as needed, including the role of healer. We should, however, have to work a lot harder at it than an adventurer does -- sending out our own health, funnelling health from enemies to replenish it, etc. -- since that's our chosen operating method, our field of expertise.

I think a lot of people who choose traders do so because they're a deliberately hard to play, challenging class, more complex than most others. That's a good thing, but there's nothing wrong with there being some corresponding advantages to the class for players who successfully navigate those additional challenges. There's nothing necessarily wrong with traders out-healing adventurers, if traders have other "trade offs" that make doing so more challenging & riskier than it is for the adventurer.
