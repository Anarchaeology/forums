Fighting a fixer is 150% dependant on your setup. If you are setup against fixers, MKIV or not, you will most likely win as an MA unless the fight is held in a location which has a high ammount of open space to run around.

MA's are the hardest hitting profession in this game, hands down.

Depending on buff's, there are times I would rather fight a level 200 NT over a level 190MA simply because they can get their attack ratings and crit% up so high.

Either way its a challenge. As far as Cele and my duel, I'm not sure what happened there and why it was so ownage. I had RRFE, had no crit buff and was running my usual self buffs.

For some reason, nearly everything I did worked out well for me there and after I had hit him a couple good times, I unloaded the alpha and it all hit. It's not very often you get a 2400 Full Auto in this game anymore, but I did that night.

Usually MA's give me a great deal of trouble and I have to run to regain life with the HoT's because they crit SO much. MA's hardly ever hit me for average 4-500 damage, its either a miss or a crit 9/10 of the time.

The AS thing can work out well for them also. Johi and I had a 15 or so minute scuffle in Tir last week which I purposely zoned from in the end to get back at him for something. In any case, the battle seemed as if it would go on forever but as soon as my RRFE died, he basicly just flat out owned me in about 12 seconds with AS/Brawl/Diamach. I was dead meat had i not zoned.

Fixers fighting MA's depends on a lot of things. The main thing being outside buffs because self buffed, no fixer of equal level to a good well balanced MA will win. Again, not unless the playfield is in favor of the fixer (places to move around in).

With outside buffs, its a very different ballgame because the fixer can now spend less time rooting and more time shooting which takes up that valuable healing nano from the MA. These fights (outside buffed) can go on forever sometimes and others it just ends after a 20-30 minute fight where both parties just decide its worthless.

As far as Doc's go, depends on the doc. If they use what they should be using based on their skillset and which ranged weapons they should be specialized in, they can be nasty to a fixer.

Traders are a null point. They just kill us. Self or outside buffed, matters not.

The list goes on, this discussion has been done over and over.
