Maybe even some of the people from FC could talk about ideas or plans they have to help players in future.

The next part was the main event, a roleplaying event in which everyone can take part in you will be given a goal, but in order to do this goal you will have to speak to a number of players. The key thing is that you will have to be your own role for this to work. The point is really not to have to solve the goal but it will force or give a reason for people to talk in role so we all know more about the player in AO.

I do know what the goal is, I do have a great event in mind but if I post it here that will spoil it for everyone so I will keep it to myself.


The other idea I had is to create a website which lists peoples roleplaying profiles. So we can learn more about the player, his background, his standing on different events, keep a diary and news on the players. My own is at

http://baboonspeople.tripod.com

To also do the profiles for each of the guilds, a dir giving a description of the guild, what they stand for basic structure and of course links to websites and how to join.

But these are big projects and will require a number of people to work on them also is there enough reason for them ?

Inviting RP Guilds & RPing
Greetings fellow bretheren of our Divine world! I am the Vicar of Chronicles for The Enligtened of Rubi-Ka. And I have great interest in what you have to say.

OOC: Ok could not resist. But the thread drew my attention. I typically read a lot but little grabs my interest enough for me to raise my voice.

I have been mulling over a post to get all RP guilds to list their guild and POC for joint-interactive RP storytelling; I have been hesitant due to the amount of effort this could snowball into. This thread is actually a good starting point as it seems there are interested parties out there in doing the same.

On EQ, over two years ago, I did organize and run RP storylines that involved multiple guilds and some independent RPers; this has given me experience and new ideas for improving the process of running player based storys without any GM assistance - EQ NEVER gave any assistance to anything we did.

I had also started an initiative to create a council of RP guilds that was based on a storyline and RP purpose. But unfortunalty I got pulled away by RL... damn RL! So this never got to full steam but had a lot of interest.

The council of RP guilds was actually based on a storyline where 9 artifacts had been forged in the age of legends and had long since been lost. These guilds now formed a council to aid each other to find them (and in some cases prevent them from finding them)... the nice thing was that the council was composed of guilds that were diametrically opposed in a number of ways.

This concept was extrapolated from the Heartstone concept. The Heartstone was a concept formed by someone but was a storyline used to bring together the RP'ers and RP guilds both.

Essentially the council was not for political purposes... though that was to be part of the RP process... but to bring together the RP'ers on the server and give them a purpose/story to focus upon.

Now what if we extended such a concept to AO. We create a storyline or very broad concept that is a tanget, parallel, or seperate from the AO storyline. Something that both RP guilds and individual RP'ers can get involved in.

Heh! The disclaimer is that this concept would have to be something that would not impact, change, nor affect the ongoing storyline but something that could be run in parallel and perhaps be impacted by the ongoing FC story. (Thats for the flamers out there who want to trounce me for "Dont be so stupid, you cant change the storyline!")

Well, just an idea! Let me know what you think and if anyone is interested we can meet in game or some chat room and go further.

But regardless, I think this thread is a great idea as while my guild is an RP guild, and while I am aware of a few RP guilds... I am very much in the dark as to who is and isnt into RPing. Extend this to individuals and it grows even worse... my personal belief and feeling is that you do not have to belong to an RP guild or even a guild at all to RP but to RP requires some knowledge of who is out there.

heh... I am rambling now!

I'm more than interested...
so count the Rubi-Ka Reform Party, and Breogan, my character, in. But I have something to say....

I love the idea of gathering a list of roleplaying oriented guilds... but I think we roleplay the best at the smallest. Let me explain.

Gathering 20 or 30 representatives of various guild into an event is always a pain, the events are either social parties, or degenerate into a caotic mess that doesnt go anywhere.

BUT, knowing there are X number of organizations out of there interested in roleplaying, listed along their members, interest, points of views, etc may alow me to approach them and set particular mini-events between us, maybe leading to better mega-events.

For example, before forming the RK-RP, I was a member of clan Eye of the Nexus. Members used to post their char bios, and some of them, for various reasons, started having an evil Omni-Tek commander, Major Richards, in their backgrounds... wouldnt it be wonderful to use this list of contact to approach an Omni Guild and ask them to play with us, making one of them Major Richards new alter-ego, sharing a common story and working as gamemasters with them to give the concerned players something to roleplay? Droping hints, having them search for clues, finding their nemesis is now know as X in Omni-Department Y, infiltrating somebody there, etc...

Another example: one of the best roleplaying sessions I had in AO was one night that a misterious Omni Agent approached one of my clanmates, wanting an interview with our leadership. What followed was a tense meeting in one of the houses of Tir where she tried to get us involved in a kind of shadow power war inside her own guild, giving us early warning of some Omni attack, pointing to some other clansmates as Omni spies, and trying as hard as she could to figure out names and roles of the leaders of our clan, while we debated between us and tried to confound her and not reveal our position... all in all, a very exciting night and I didnt kill anything! :-)

So, I'm all up for a kind of RolePlaying Directory of Rubi-Ka, but advocate for smaller events and getting to know each other and get each other as involved as we can in the other's life before planning mega-events.

My Sentiments Exactly!
Breogan, you summarized my sentiments exactly. When I spoke of the events I had helped organize and run in that OTHER MMPORPG our RP events had started with a small internal guild event.

We then expanded by doing an event with another guild... initially small. I am actually doing the same right now. There is an omni guild I am working with behind the scenes to craft and run a story for both of our guilds. Starting small in the beginning is important, New Rome was not built in a day!

You do have to start off with building a repoirte with the other guild; specifically the individual in the other guild who is responsible for organizing events/player run story lines. In fact, one misconception I have seen enacted are players expecting player run events to take off simply via Roleplaying. While sometimes that works, it typically results in chaos with a number of vocal, disgruntled guild mates who view player run events with skepticism... LOL... yes I am speaking from personal experience also.

Back to subject. For player run events to occur between guilds, you do need to organize OOC... this has been a bit of a frustration here in AO as some of the guilds I have contacted interacted via IC and would not flip over to OOC. To organize a storyline you really need to plot, plan, and implement OOC... very much like Game Mastering a tabletop RPG.

Anyway, I feel your comments struck to the heart of the matter and I am in full agreement with you. Contact me in game (DarqOmen) or send me an email at DarqOmen@hotmail.com if you would like to talk further. Sounds like we have the same viewpoints.

I think we are really getting somewhere now. I am in absolute agreement with Breogan
and Darqomen here. There is nothing more OOC than talking about roleplaying, but on the other hand you have to make that initial OOC investment to get the roleplaying properly organised.

Having spoken with Kirrana and discussed this with my guild, here is what I would like to suggest.

1. RP Conference to be held at Reets Retreat (Rubi-Ka 1) on Sunday 10th of Feb at 20:00 GMT. The Neutral location will allow OT and clan alike to participate. (the date is negotiable - but I would not want to lose the momentum).

2. The Conference proper will take form of a series of short presentations from the roleplaying guilds and other interested parties. It will cover who they are, what sorts of things they done and what they hope to do in the future. This will be totally OOC and designed to give the various Rp guilds a chance to learn about each other and maybe help some unguilded roleplayers find a good home. We will have to create a signup form on this message board for those wishing to give presentations. The event itself should be held in the main hall at Reets (the one with the glass floor).

3. Additionally we will invite volunteers to run seminars, workshops or yoga lessons if they like to be held after the conference presentations in the various side rooms at Reets. Perhaps the Oshohva or the Friendleet Foundation might want to give a talk about their special interests, or maybe Darqomen might like to chat about his roleplaying council idea. We will have to have set up a room booking system (again a thread on the message board most likely).

4. Participants not involved in any of the workshops can either dance the night away at Reets or return to their favourite hunting grounds.

5. If this format works, it is probably the kind of thing we can do every 2-3 months, as the landscape of AO players and guild does change rather rapidly.

If we are all in agreement over this format, we can move to promoting this event, making sure as many people as possible get to hear of it.
