In order to get better loot to drop from team missions, you have to have more then one person doing them. You will get crap loot, if you solo team missions and better loot depending on number of team members doing that mission.

In order to get ql 200 Kirch for example you have to do ql 220-230 team missions. The problem is that not many people bother doing them.

And loot from bosses have always been pretty horrible even post 14.5.

You get Albrecht Heavy Tanks every 1/20 missions or you might get lucky and get 3 dropping in a row.

You get X-3 every 1/15 missions. Same with other good weapons.

The rest of the loot has always been crap from bosses. Unless you are a specific profession looking for So8 etc.

The best way to gear up has always been single missions...blizzing or mezzing. Apart from rare loot that does not drop in missions anyway.
