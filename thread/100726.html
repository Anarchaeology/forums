Operation Adustum - Cleansing SWB
ENVIRONMENTAL REPORT, 7th SEPT, LAST YEAR
DESERT WINDS

7th Sept, a small team of members of the Desert Winds travelled to the Stret West Bank area to determine for themselves the effects of the recent climate change on the populations of the variations species there. A second aim of the trip was to cull members of the more populus species. Stret West Bank has a diverse ecosystem but as a recently terraformed area is still quite fragile, any rapid change in climate could have quite drastic effects there.

The Desert Winds team saw that the population levels of several species lower down the food chain, such as leets, reets and chirops were within expected parameters. However, species of animals further up the food chain such as scorpoids, sasteprowler alpha males and females, nightcrawlers and above all rollerrats had more members than would be expected. Such an imbalance could well destabilise the ecosystem there and regular culls of these species are recomended. The presence of the roller rats is quite worrying, these species can breed fast and is very aggresive. An area of Stret West Bank was identified to contain a high proportion of the fertile matriarchs and queens and is likely a breeding area for the roller rats. A future trip by the Desert Winds is planned to reduce the numbers of these animals in this area before they spread.

Eldridge "Naxilite" McCovery, President of the Desert Winds, was impressed by the dedication and professionalism his guild members showed on the mission. He also stated his concern at the apparent imbalance in the ecosystem in Stret West Bank and Omni-Teks policy of 'turning a blind eye' to such problems. "This just goes to show Omni-Tek care only for money and notum. They dont seem to have any interest in establishing Rubi-Ka as a self sustaining colony. The Desert Winds will continue to campaign for an independent self sufficient Rubi-Ka."

SUPPLEMENTAL
This was a previously published report by the Desert Winds group. We investigated Stret West Bank some time after the Weather Control System was changed. Our findings showed the region was especially susceptible to changes in the Rubi-Kan environment. Since then the region settled down somewhat as it adapted to the changes in environmental conditions.

Before the turn of the year the ICC opened up mining all over the terraformed region of Rubi-Ka. By its very nature mining has a dramatic impact on the local and global ecology. Last week a second team of Desert Winds members headed to Stret West Bank to determine the effects of mining on that region. DESERT WINDS REPORT ON STRET WEST BANK, Feb 9th THIS YEAR
Stret West Bank is situation on the largest body of fresh water in the terraformed zone, The River Stret. I dont even have to stress the importance of this to you all, I am sure you have all read my report on hydration strategies in terraformed zones.

Our previous work, see appended file, reported on the diverse range of species within Stret West Bank, their locales and behaviour. Our first observations suggest that the wildlife there no longer shuns mankind and are in fact far more aggresive than normal. Calming nano programs were less than effective on enraged roller rat queens for example. A number of creatures seemed to have altered in colouration, tentative studies on biopsies taken from creatures indicate elevated levels of various toxins and dioxins within each sample. These were not present in our original trip to the area and therefore must be as a result of the mining. It is no surprise to see Stret West Bank has a relatviely large number of mines/per unit area than most other areas in the terraformed zone.

As noted animal behavior has radically altered. For example the roller rat 'castle', once an over thriving roller rat breeding colony is now empty. The roller rats are now spread further over Stret West Bank than before. This aggresive speices is a natural predator and being so spread will impact every food chain in the region. I am sure I dont have to emphasize how detrimental that will be. Similar observations were recorded for every species there. Interestingly, no creatures were observed within the boundries of any mining facility, no doubt they are avoiding contaminated areas. The plant life is not so lucky, a number of mutated plants engaged us in ranged combat.

To conclude, SWB is a crucial area and the mining there is having many diverse effects on the local ecology. I recommend this be rectified before it is too late.

DESERT WINDS/NANOMAGE LIBERATION FRONT JOINT OPERATION
OPERATION ADUSTUM

A joint group of Desert Winds and Nanomage Libeartion Front members proceeded to Stret West Bank with the aim of reducing the mining there as much as possible. The small force of seven troops advanced on the Omni-Tek AF base (~1289,1359). This facility borders several areas of scientific interest and is close to a fresh water river that feeds into the Stret. The group attacked and took down several polluting turrets and guardians from the site before moving onto the second target. The Symbiosis {SC} (~822, 1409) mine. This extensive facility was totally destroyed by the joint strike force, the land cleansed and left to return to a natural state.

The two forces of troops handled themselves well and have struck a vital blow for the well being of Rubi-Ka. All polluters and rapers of the planet should beware, no more will Rubi-Ka take this abuse lying down.
Major E. McCovery, leader of the Desert Winds.

In a brief statement, Matriarch of the Nanomage Liberation Front was less than apologetic, to say the least, "This was a victory for both the NANOMAGE people and Rubi ka herself, but be forewarned, todays assaults were only the beginning. All of Rubi ka must be known that the line has been drawn, and if it is crossed, they will have Desert Winds and The NANOMAGE Liberation Front to deal with on the other side. Till All Are Free! "
Queen Fleshrepaer, leader of the Nanomage Libeartion Front

Further operations are planned
