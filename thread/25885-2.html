FC has never played Asheron's call, I'm sure of it. In fact I bet they don't even know what it is.

AO's quest system is a tired old relic of the EQ days. Ultra hard mobs, day long respawns, and a 10% chance of dropping anything, are not fun. What they are is flawed attempts at keeping these rare items from getting very common -- Wrong approach.


The quest system in AC was thus:


{1} Unique items in AC respawned anywhere from 10 seconds to 1 minute. If it was a unique mob, it would spawn every 5-10 minutes.

This ensured that everyone in the group got what they came for, no waiting, no fighting, no camping. They were then out of there and the next group could enjoy the dungeon by themselfs.


{2} All unique items had a personal quest timer on them to stop players from farming them, or picking up more than one. Depending on the item, weither or not it was droppable, and it's value on the market: The personal quest timer could last anywhere from 1 day, to 4 months.

This simple system ensures that no one can repeatedly camp unique stuff and ebay it, or stock up for their guild.


{3} Last, but most importantly; Quest items were never the best you could get. They were nice, and definately worth you time, but the ultra rare uber loot dropped randomly off critters.

Thus, there was no need to limit these quest items because they were not the absolute best, they were just unique.
