theory of profession specific armour
Profession specific armor has often been made to give some of the weaker professions a bit of a boost.

I like the idea of professional restrictions, for an RP reason. The energy forces that the MP's can control are different than the ones the Enfo's use. The Engies have better bot control, so their armour should be tailored to their strength.

If it is left open, it makes for more cookie cutter characters - right now you can tell a twink from a new character at level 25 because the twinks are all in carbon armour, while the noobs are running in nail and flak peices.

I like the idea that the Über armour is profession specific, for the same reasons, the considerations going into making the armour, were probably done by a designer of a specific profession, who designed it for personal use. It is also a status symbol.

The way I see the situation:
crappy drop armour - from hunting, mission lewt, drops often (mostly useless, like nail or flak)
standard armour - drops from bosses, or requires tradeskill process, nice if you can get it. Such as padded love, or CAS.
Über armour - GA, nelebs, alvin/dodga sholderpads, Ceremonies, line of armour , quest, expensive tradeskill, or special drop.

I think the more time you put into it, the better it should get. The professional restrictions increases the ease for less popular professions to get their armour. This would work because there would be a tonne of enfo's fighting over who gets the Über tank armour, but the spawn for the Über Engie armour would usually be available for the engie who think's he's got the right stuff.

I do support though, that there be considerations to make sure that each profession does not become a cookie cutter machine for characters. There are just too many exarc / stigian enforcers out there, and MCS / GA fixxrs. The only way to get them to be less cookie cutter is to introduce simular equipment, with other considerations, so that people could chose to go either way, such as Exarc robe, or some other simular robe, or tank.
