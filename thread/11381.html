New Suggestion - Solid objects
I hope it's new anyway...

Make players, cars, boats, planes, robots, monsters etc.. SOLID

Yes, that's right; <solid>... So it is possible to block someone. Ok fine, maybe around common areas, keep people 'ethereal' like around whompas and shops.

I say this because i just came from a game of Wolf 3D and me and my buddies stopped an allied soldier from escaping by blocking the exits. The fact that he couldn't go through us won us that little skirmish.
Think about the implications for crowd control etc when Ross or Radiman give speeches. Bodyguards would have a purpose.

It's true that there are many pitfalls to making people solid. Possibly you could have arks etc turn people 'ethereal' or if you stand in one spot too long you go ethereal.

What do you people think?
