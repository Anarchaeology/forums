1. Heals are 100% effective when damage itself is halved. 2. Trader drains should be less effective in PvP, the duration of the penalty should be less for the target (around 20-30 seconds) and carry a time penalty after every succesful drain in PvP, or the penalty effect should simply be halved for both Divest and Plunder in PvP. There are also other thought in this forum about this issue, please read them for further information.

3. Nano Shutdown, should carry a time penalty and should be shorter in duration, around 20-30 seconds (with 1 minute penalty when successfully used)

4. Init Debuffs, same as nano shutdown.

If the following changes would get implemented at any given time, most professions would became viable again instead of just 3-4.
