Guild insignia
My suggestion is that once every patch, there is an optional choice for people to download a little image created by the leader of every guild which wishes to participate, an image of a sort of logo for the guild, to be placed over some of the items that a player in a guild is wearing. Such items could be weapons, clothes, vehicles or armour. The images would have to be very small, like 32x32 pixels, and also limited to a few colours and shades. This would be useful for multi-guild events, quickly finding people in your a guild, and would help promote your guild, make you look differnet etc. etc. I just think it'd be cool. Probably won't happen but you never know
