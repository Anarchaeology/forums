Meant it has nothing to do with exactly pistols. If there was a high-dam low attack speed pistol, you would do JUST fine.

It has everything to do with way AC reduces damage. Which gimps fast hitting low damage weapons (most common ones being pistols and dual-wielding non-rider melee weapons).

Say, a shotgun shoots for 1-500. And your pistol for 1-200, but you are wielding two pistols and they shoot half as fast as the shotgun. So you'd think you would do more damage after all than the shotgun, since you are getting 1-200+1-200 + 1-200 in the same time period. Except:

Random(200) - MonsterACReduction(150) = damage of 1-50, where you will hit minimum 75% of the time. So mostly you are hitting for nada, zip zilch nothing just spriddling some pepper on the monster...

Whereas one-timer 1-500 minus that 150, you will hit 1-350 with only about third of the time hitting for minimum... That's actually being generous with the values, usually it is just straight to minimum damage for fast/lowdam weapon users
