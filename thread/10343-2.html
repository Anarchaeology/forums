I play an engineer. While I disagree with the first post in many aspects I just want to add something to the debate.

Slayerdroids

Yes, it is ridiculous that a level 100 engineer can bring a Slayerdroid into battle. The suggested changes that you have to be in a certain % range in skills is a good one IMO. But, take the level 100 engineer into PvP. If you are his/hers opponent and attack the slayerdroid instead of the fragile engineer, you are plain dumb. Just go after the engineer who has very low HP and evades, and you should be able to kill him/her very fast. If you have the ability to root, heh you have nothing to fear. Root the pet which has about 0 nano resist, and kill the engie.

I know there was a high level soldier who fought a slayerdroid in the arena once. It was just an experiment, so he did not attack the engineer at all. Even though the engineer healed the pet etc, the soldier could still beat the slayerdroid. Yes, he killed a slayerdroid but he had MKX of course.

When it comes to PvM, the slayerdroids are really nice. But, Aces can easily kill a slayerdroid (especially enforcer types) if the engineer doesn't have nice heals. A lower level engineer does not have a very good pet heal usually, so it is unlikely that a level 112 engineer can solo a lvl 200 mission unless it is one of those really small cave missions.

I can usually do about 65% difficulty missions when I bring a slayerdroid with me. But I very often die because the mobs will aggro me instead of the pet, and I am never safe inside a mission. Also it takes a lot of time to complete even a 50% difficulty mission. The missions are huge, and even though engineer pets make good tanks, they aren't a damage dealer like a soldier (and they shouldn't be either). I only do harder missions when there is an item I really need, so when I die, I don't mind that much. I just let my soldier or fixer friends run the mission instead if I can't complete it. Professions that can blitz/sneak missions make a LOT more money than an engineer will since they can complete 10 missions in the time an engineer has completed one.

I never expected to be any good in PvP and I never will since I use pistols. Maybe when I get to level 170 or so I will use a shotgun and have maxed evades and then try some PvP, but it is useless for me now. Engineers are fine in PvM, but it's not like we can do a /pet hunt then go watch tv for an hour and then come back and see that I have gained a level.
