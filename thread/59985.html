things to help get the story moving
ok here is a suggestion...i think that there should be some be some sort of "secret" trigger that unlocks a sequence to the story. this my come in a form of a quest or by talking to the right ppl or doing certain stuff. i'm taking an example from eq..wher uber guilds woke the sleeper(a basically unkillable dragon) by killing four guardians and somehow woke it from its slumber. even though i dont really want fc to do this...i thought the basic concept was interesting, that certain events may be triggered.
example:
found some wandering npc outside of tir in a remote area. talk to him and he will tell a story of the past. you think nothing of it, but then you come across something in the story he mentioned.....interacting may somehow unleash an event which can the be announced to all clanners in the area that something has happned. everyone investigates which can thus be lead to some sort of event.

i know this may be a little far fetched..but it really gets players involved. implementing it would probably be too dificult as fc doesnt seem to want to do events and seeing how a lot of things dont work already.
