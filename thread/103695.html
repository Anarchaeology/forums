THE BLUE MOON (PART 2)

Time passed slowly and rarely was there a break from the drudgery. Oh, there was the occasional holiday where the mine essentially shut down. Sometimes on these holidays us prisoners would be exempted from work, we received new clothes and maybe some extra food. For the most part however, one day was the same as another. Occasionally there were arena bouts that featured some of the tougher prisoners. The prize was often one of the young females (or males depending on the winners preference) in the camp, extra food and clothes, and time off from mining. It offered some excitement but us most of young ones were terrified of being chosen as a prize. Ill touch more on the arenas later in my story.

As I was saying, time passed slowly. Viks was turning into quite the attractive young woman. Evidently the mine foreman also noticed her blossoming as well. On her 15th birthday her presence was requested for an evening at the mine foremans mansion. Apparently she was to receive a birthday present from the foreman. Our mother protested but Viks said not to worry and off she went with the guard. Mother was livid. I tried to comfort her and said that maybe there really was a nice birthday present for her at the mansion. It was not unheard of for the females to receive some nice things from the foreman. It wasnt long after Viks was taken to the mansion that a pair of guards came to our tent. They ordered mother to collect her few things and come with them. We both protested until the guards pointed their guns at us and threatened to kill us both if she didnt move her whore ass quickly. With that threat she quickly bundled her things up, then gave me a hug and a kiss. As she left the tent she told me she loved me and for me to keep Viks safe. I began to cry as they walked her to a transport. Neither of us ever saw her again. We both hope against hope that she is alive somewhere.

After some time Viks came back. She saw me there alone, crying. The guard pushed her into the tent and chuckled, Heres your birthday present slut! Viks screamed, Wheres my Mother! The guard just laughed and walked away. I held my sister and explained what happened. Viks told me everything that happened. She was raped by the foreman and then humiliated by the guards. I suspected as much. The rumors I had heard in whispered conversations were true. We both knew know what our mother endured for the sake of a few extra bits of food and cloth. I wondered when my turn would comeif it would come.

The foreman apparently liked my sister a great deal since he called for her on a regular basis. Although I was horrified, she seemed to have grown accustomed to it. She said, If you just do what he wants in the fashion he wants its over quickly. I was disgusted by the thought but I must admit that her visits provided far more food and luxuries for us then mothers visits ever provided. My sisters clothes were the best in the camp and she was allowed to take frequent hot showers in the foremans mansion. She was given scented oils and perfumes, which she used liberally on me. I guess once one gets accustomed to being clean, the scent of unwashed humanity becomes repugnant.
I noticed my sisters personality changing but I said nothing. She was becoming a bit of a snob and haughty towards the other camp females. I guess she forgot that she was still nothing more than a prisoner, although a favored one.

All things happen for a reason I suppose. Because of my sister being a favorite of the foreman, both of us benefited. Our normal duties were reduced and we had more time to ourselves. We were also able to wander around the camp much more freely than was possible otherwise. I found myself a new father-figure during this time. I wont mention his name because he might still live in the mines. Anyway, he is the one who sparked my interest in rifles and the skills of those who follow the agent profession. Father was in the mines because he had been caught manufacturing DNA weapons. DNA weapons as everyone knows, can circumvent normal insurance encoding and visit perma-death on those unfortunate enough to be targeted. Father and the things he taught me will become important later on in my story.

Viks and I continued to grow more distant as the months went by. Sometimes I wonder if the pair of us was some lab experiment the foreman dreamed up. Forcing us to act as sisters then doing things to drive us apart for his humor. Whatever the case my sister was becoming more and more of a bitch, flaunting what she had. Then one evening, the guards stomped up to our tent and shouted. Cathern Flowers, congratulations! Its your 15th birthday. The foreman has something special for you. My blood felt like it turned to ice in my veins and my stomach knotted up in fear. My sister whispered to me, Whatever you do, dont fight him. I was very scared but I put on a brave face because I knew the guards would just torment me if they seen weakness. I hugged my sister and off I went.

I wont get into all details of what happened. Suffice to say that my introduction to womanhood was both extremely brutal and painful. I couldnt bring myself to follow my sisters advice and I fought back. It nearly cost me my life. No matter how much I struggled I couldnt hope to fend of the foreman and his guard cronies. They took what they wanted in every way they wanted leaving me bleeding, torn, ripped, and bruised, with broken ribs and a dislocated shoulder to boot. I must have lost consciousness at one point because I awoke having my injuries tended by the mine doctor. I use the term loosely because the doctor for the prisoners only had the most primitive medicines and treatments to give us. I went out again and when I came to I found myself in my tent again.

I must have been out the whole day because when I awoke it was early evening. I peered out my tent and there was the blue moon. I heard my sister talking to me but it wasnt making sense, like she was speaking a foreign language or something. After a few minutes I became aware of pain. My whole body hurt and I felt so dirty. I wanted nothing more than to go wash myself in the unheated showers we had but I couldnt make my body function properly. I cant remember if I said anything, but I must have made it clear to Viks what I wanted. She helped me to the showers were I proceeded to clean myself, as if the stink of the foreman and his guards were still on me. I was still bleeding a bit from my private places and it was hard to breath. The pain was like hot coals. I must of went unconscious one more time because I remember the soap my sister had earned tumbling from my hands and the floor rising up to meet me.

The days that followed are blurry in my mind. It seems like I just couldnt understand what people were saying to me. I barely spoke and when I did I was later told most of it was gibberish. Slowly things came into focus and I knew HATE and RAGE! HATE became my friend, RAGE my motivator. I hated what had been done to me by the foreman and his bitch of a child. Hatred for my surroundings. Rage against being told what I could and could not do. Hated my existence. It was then that I begin to plot once again. This time, I would follow through with my plans. This time my hate would give me the courage to do what I must. I was no longer a child being bullied by another. I would make the foreman pay and then I would make Omni pay for what had happened. My hate and my rage became unquenchable fires hidden inside me.
