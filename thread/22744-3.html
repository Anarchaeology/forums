Looking at the nano lines and what they do is a start, but there's more to a class than what nanos it casts.

The problem with this sort of tabulation is that it always reflects the biases of the tabulator.

The best way to guage class balance? Look at what classes are the most played. Look at what classes are the least played. If a class has an average number of players, it's probably average. If it has waay more players than anyone else, it's probably more powerful or more fun than the other classes. If one class is being played significantly less than other classes, it's probably got some problems.

That's the best way to guage balance. Look at whether people are playing the danged classes or not. Anything else is supposition and hypothesis. Looking at whether the classes are getting played, and how much, is empirical data.
