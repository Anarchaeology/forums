One good thing about the Matter Creation nanos is that none of them have to get by a resist roll and they all can be cast out of combat. Skills like Sense Improvement that are used for Snares should be raise as high as possible to avoid resists, while Matter Creation only needs to be raise high enough to meet the nano requirements.

Also, because all the nanos can be cast before combat, a Fixer could switch implants and get MP buffs, summon the weapon/armor/ammo (ok had to laugh, those are still useless), summon the Grid Armor, then re-equip to go hunt.

When I first heard about the Matter Creation line of nanos, I had decided not to use them because I didn’t want to dilute my IP investment. After I realized my previous two points though, I’ve decided to give it a try. In addition remember that Fixers have green treatment (good for implants) and Computer Lit. (good for buffs).

The implants and buffs we get are going to be even more important as we get higher levels and could explain our high nano skill cost. With green Comp Lit., Fixers should be at the top when it comes to NCU space. That means that while an Atrox might have less nano skill cost, they won’t be able to use the Mocham's Gift MP buffs that add 140 points to you nano skill. They also won’t be able to install the high level implants that will increase our skill even more.

There might be a method to Funcom’s madness regarding Fixers. It still does suck that we have to jump through hoops to cast our nanos, when other just have to invest the IP. If you find a hole in my logic, please tell me before I totally gimp my beloved character.
