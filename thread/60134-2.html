I guess I would think that a mix (camps and wanderers both) would be good. Now, you know how I think, and normally, more wanderers would not be high on my list, because it can interfere with lower level character travels. But, the new travel facilities (both whompa and, in the case of fixers, the grid) make travel much easier anyway.

Back in December, I went to Andromeda a few times without the benefit of a yalm. I died a few times, too, on my way to those missions, to wandering mobs.

Recently, I took a lower level character to those same locations, also without a yalm (I may have a lot of characters, doesn't mean I have a lot of twinks). I'm going to say I made a dozen trips over the last month. Only once did I even see a mob (almost died, but was able to outrun it to get to my mission door, although without the zone map, finding the door was a challenge in and of itself ).

Anyway, it should be worthwhile to have more wandering spawns. Zones like Andromeda, where you can just run around and easily find stuff, not wait for minutes in between mobs.

Also, if save terminals were more abundant in closer places, I would think that would be attractive, also. Since the risk of death is higher, the idea of investing much travel time to return to a group isn't a good one.

Finally, I know that there are people who can't get their friends/guildies to go with them without a major sell job. I really think it's worth pursuing with them what their reluctance is. It could be that it's just faster/easier to run missions, or there may be more to it. There could be some kernels of wisdom in their responses, though.

Outdoor XPs should be MUCH higher than they are currently.

Based on the xp/hour scale, nobody will really hunt outdoors because
a) the big xp/uber loot mobs are laughably hard to kill as far as xp is concerned
b) ease (lack thereof) of travel to PW, EFP etc for most profs
c) human tendency toward laziness (duh)

currently, only team missions offer what players need: fast XP, loot/$ and tokens, easy accessibility, low risk
outdoor hunting? minimal xp, little to no loot/$, 0 tokens, high risk, distance

I don't propose BS/team mission be nerfed in any way. Nerfing is a cop out.

I suggest raising 150-200% outdoor hunting xp at higher lvls, 125-15-% lower levels. 10% isn't gonna do it. If I can defeat a Sandworm at lvl 100 I should get more than 25,000 xp. 27,500 isn't worth the risk. Since i'm not likely to get a token, big $ or good loot hunting outdoors, the XP should be significantly high enough to make up for it.

I think the only thing that's going to really increase the popularity of outdoors hunting is altering the outdoor mob drop tables. Useful items, tradeskill components, quest components, all not available from missions. The tradeskill component factor would be a big winner if there was some better way to market them to other players.

Currently (according to some sources) bronto hide above QL60 is only available as mission loot. And all QL chest slot pieces are mission only. There are probably other examples of this. Reverse this. Make component gathering an outdoor sport again.

Btw, I understand 14.6 will have lowlevel static dungeons. Wonderfull idea. EQ'ers will recall Blackburrow and Crushbone. Social soloing. Work with people you aren't even teamed with. What better advertisement for us poor docs than dropping a few heals as we pass by? Or being able to yell for help when we get in over our heads.
