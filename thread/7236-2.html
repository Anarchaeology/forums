War with who....
Smash, you have some points, but you, and others here, keep making the same mistake over and over and over.

There is no Omni-Tek
There is no Clans
There is no Neutrals

There are individuals, each one of us, that work for some goals, some private, some shared.

Omni-Tek killed and slaved us? No. People in Omni-Tek decided to use the resources of that company as a way to fullfill their agendas for wealth and power, withour respect for human rights.

Clans killed the people in Omni-1? I dont know. But if there are clanners or whole Clans behind the event, they choosed to get revenge for past wrongs on innocents, or are just bloodthirsty murderes looking for an excuse to kill and not a cause.

Meanwhile there are people in both sides that see that all this is madness, and the only possible outcome is a ruined Rubi-Ka.

I'm a die-hard clanner; I will never accept a Rubi-Ka under the goverment of a private corporation where its people dont count as anything but numbers. I want a democratic and free Rubi-Ka, and I think that OmniTek has a place in it. They are here for the notum, lets them have it; as long as they stick to business and dont interfere with the will of all inhabitants of RUbi-Ka, the company can stay. That includes freedom to their employees, to vote for civil authorities, civil projects, etc.
I know there are people in Omni that would be happy under this kind of agreement.

So yes, I fear war is in the horizon, and yes, I'm not putting away my armor (and cant put away my weapons as I follow the way of the empty hand), but I participated in the Peace March, and made a little speech there. Cause if we, the rational, the moderate, the HUMANE, can avoid the escalation of hostilities, we will have a war and we will win it. A war against those in both sided that see us only as cannon fooder for their goals.

I'm calling for all interested organizations to declare officially that their will NEVER initiate any hostile action against the other side, even if their "leaders" (Ross and Radiman) call for it. This pact would allow you the right of self defense in case of attack, but you should never, ever attack, even in retaliation.

If we all stick to this, the ones that desire only our destruction will fail. We will deprive them of their favorite tools: hiding in our mist and getting us involved in the wars they start.

Lets declare war to warmongers and win it with peace.
