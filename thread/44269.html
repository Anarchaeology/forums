There are people who think they are semi-gods b/c they have high level characters etc. The fact is that this is a social computer game and you won't get real-life benefits from mastering it, its purely for fun and enjoyment...to pass time in pleasure. Well, if you were in a team doing QL26 missions at level 15, they were almost *definitely* alts, just badly played ones.

The vast majority of new players have to equip themselves from mission rewards. Because you basically level every mission at that level, buy the time you win a piece of armor, you've outgrown it.

The upshot is that, by level 15, most new players are *barely* into armor and weapons of their level. Unless they're smart enough to discover and exploit flaws in the game like 100k QL1 concrete cushions.

I think it's safe to say that no new level 15, cheesy gear and all, is going to survive a lvl26 mission.
