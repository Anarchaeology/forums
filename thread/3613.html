Some thoughts about the Adventurer
I'm not going to compare power levels here. These are suggestions to make the Adventurer more of an Adventurer. Every class should be useful and cool to some extent. The Adventurer should be the natural guide and knowitall in the outdoors.

Team nanos:
Lodestone: A simple nano that focuses the team compasses on the Adventurer. "Get that scattered team easily back together"

Guide: A nano line that makes the outdoor critters not aggro you/team. Take those noobs in your guild for a sightseeing in DAV. (Its a long walk, so better get an engineer to tansport you there). Team version should of course be an aura type of nano. This would make the Adventurer the natural guide. It will of course not calm things if combat has started. It just makes it very unlikely that something will aggro on you if not provoked.
Adventuring skill could play an effect here.(maybe like the envisioned psychology for crat charms.)

Camping: An Adventurer should be able to get a bonfire started. This could be an item equipped like a vehicle based on Adventuring skill, or a nano. Item is my favorite. While the "camp" is equipped(character sitting by the fire) monsters will not attack anything within a perimeter. There could be other effects as well. HP regen or whatever. This item is mostly for RP'ing. After hours of hunting, huddeling up around the bonfire for a chat cant hurt.
