He didn't know which offered him more strength. He felt compelled then to rub his eyes, which began to dry out when he forgot to blink, as he was still doing... His vision had only become united as one solid point of view about a minute ago, as someone he barely recognized as a former friend who was discovered to be a Clanner, threatened to chase him out of Reet as he had driven her from Rompa.

Of course, she had also been a prostitute, but he didn't condemn her for that when he though she was Omni, and that wasn't why he condemned her now. He didn't condemn her at all really, save her her wisdom for siding with the Clans and for her choice of profession. Aside of that he simply held her to the Tir accord, aside of previous friendship. Something she didn't take very well...

She had parted after he replied to her threats, and hissed something about him being even more infuriating alive than he had been dead. He doubted even Barlau caught that part... but his hearing was painfully acute right then, as were all his senses. Even so he didn't catch what Barlau said to whatever-her-name-was as she left, but whatever it was caused the woman to become vastly more indignant than she already was, but hastened her departure all the same.

He focused again on Deckrd as he tried to tune out the drumming of his own heart.

Deckrd had come in and greeted Storm nuetrally enough, but from the moment he'd seen Barlau he'd become increasingly more agitated, and cryptic...

Stormrazer struggled to understand what he was getting at. Something about Deckrd had always seemed off, such was the reason he'd had Sargeant Yengchoe send the Omni-Pol elite armor he'd purchased from Deckrd to a lab to be tested for any manipulations.

Still, Deckrd's cryptic remarks didn't refer to armor, but he acted very much as he had something up his sleeve. Something he was darkly proud of.

Again, he sighed mentally. "I can feel this going bad... He's acting stranger. I hate being so weak... Doing this here was a bad idea..."

He would have kicked himself for not looking for a more secure location more, but he couldn't think of anything more accessible, and normally Deckrd and the lot haunted Rompa, not Reet... He wasn't certain that if he'd gone Rompa instead, other enemies would have been in wait there.

That, and he didn't need to kick himself as uncomfortable as his present state was. Even if he had felt capable of such a thing.

Deckrd continued to rant with no point in sight. Often hinting that he and Barlau were in a relationship, all but asking outright.

Often he corrected him, saying they were merely friends...

It was honest, of course, and he'd seen little evidence that they were anything but so far. But then, he'd missed her blushing entirely...

Stormrazer neglected to say that he hoped for anything more with Barlau. But then he wasn't quite admitting that to himself yet.

Besides which, Deckrd seemed to be angling for some sort of confirmation.

Stormrazer blinked and tried to leap to his feet as Deckrd paused to put on a clear, but just noticeable air filter over his mouth and then suddenly withdrew a Chemical Mausser. He only succeeded in leaning forward slightly...

"Seems he's given up on drawing out information... How the hell did he get a suppression gas filter..."

Stormrazer waited for a moment of silence, and quickly spoke into his comm to ask if she was still upstairs. She replied instantly that she was. He asked her to stay there for a minute... and shut off the comm before she could reply. If Deckrd noticed his communications, he showed no signs of caring.

She had done enough, more than enough... And the very thought of her being endangered by this old, malevolent aquaintence, made him ill, then slowly and increasingly angry...

And then she walked into the room, and he was instantly reminded what mortal fear was like.

Soleet
She stood for a split second, just staring, unable to move. So this is why he was so strange on the comm. Deckrd hadn't seen her yet, but Storm had. Deck was looking down at his mausser, one hand holding it trained on Stormrazer, the other hand fiddling with something on the handle. So he wasn't looking when Stormrazer turned his head and mouthed at her, Get out of here, go!

She shook her head at him, and began to slowly approach. Even as her brain was telling her, in a clear, logical voice, "it isn't too late, run!", she was walking towards the two men. Carefully, not wanting to startle Deckrd, she cleared her throat slightly, then said, "What is this?"

Just as she drew even with Stormrazer, Deckrd swung the gun, held it against her forehead. She took a deep breath, shut her eyes for a moment. She felt Storm make some slight movement, and quickly opened her eyes. "Deckrd... you don't want to do this." Go easy , she told herself, Don't let him see how scared you are. You can talk him down...

He cackled with glee. "Storm's about to lose a sweetheart. Just like I did". He rambled on, but she didn't hear anymore of what he said, suddenly realizing something, horrified...

Yes, insurance was a common thing on Rubi-Ka. But she didn't have any, she didn't think, anyway. When she'd arrived on Rubi-ka, she'd gone through a routine medical exam. They'd done a number of tests and things, making certain she wasn't carrying any foreign germs that might endanger the population. Did that exam include an insurance scan? She certainly hoped so. She'd never actually purchased insurance. How could she have been so dense?

She realized, suddenly, that Stormrazer was looking at her oddly. I'm all right , she thought, hoping he could read it in her eyes. She turned her gaze back to Deckrd. "I'm sorry to hear about your sweetheart. But you are mistaken. Storm and I are merely friends. That's all."

She winced as he started screaming. "You're going to die! You're both going to die! You'll go first - Storm can watch his girlfriend die!" Stormrazer made some small movement, and Deckrd quickly aimed the weapon at his head. Stormrazer kept both hands at his side, and his eyes locked on Deckrd's.

Kiyoko kept talking, slowly, in a low, calm voice. "Deck, I don't know what happened... but this isn't your solution. And you're mistaken. Storm and I are friends. Nothing else. Let's just discuss this calmly". Deckrd seemed to relax a bit, his eyes still trained on Storm's, and without thinking, her hand crept up toward's Deck's, towards that gun.

But he saw the movement, and immediately swung the gun back toward her. She held her hands up in a surrender position, then slowly lowered them to her sides. "I'm sorry", she said, "it was purely instinctive". She glanced over at Stormrazer. He was looking pale and shaken. "Please", she said, "can't we sit down?"

This seem to take their captor off-guard, and after a moment, he waved his gun, motioning them toward the sofa. She sat down next to Stormrazer, but not too close, hoping to reinforce to Deckrd that really, truly, they were just friends.

Deckrd paced back and forth in front of them, waving his gun, railing at Stormrazer. Something about his sweetheart, who had vanished, something about Omni-Pol. He seemed to be accusing Stormrazer of something, but he was incoherent. She glanced over at Storm. He appeared to be just as mystified as she.

Then abruptly, he switched gears, asking Stormrazer how he'd liked that armor Deckrd had sold him. Storm replied that the armor was in the custody of the AF lab, being tested for any signs of sabotage. Deckrd gave him a peculiar grin at this.

Truly, the man was frightening. For the next several hours, it was the same routine - he was screaming one moment, then speaking almost in a whisper, in a voice that was tender, caressing, as he looked at Kiyoko. It made the little hairs on the back of her neck rise. But she forced herself to appear relaxed, occasionally speaking in a low, calm voice, hoping to bring him back to sanity. At some point he pulled out a different gun. She didn't know much about firearms. But she was willing to bet that it would hurt every bit as bad at point-blank range as the other weapon...

Stormrazer finally asked him, his voice low and serious, why he was doing this. It wasn't the first time he had asked... but finally the question seemed to have penetrated. Deckrd howled, and lunged for Barlau. Wrapping his fingers in her long hair, he hauled her to her feet, his gun pressed against her temple. She couldn't help a cry of pain, as he dragged her over to the other fireplace. "Come over here", he commanded Stormrazer. Storm obeyed, moving slowly, carefully. "I'll tell you why I'm doing this", Deckrd grinned.

It was hard to remember to breathe, with the cold steel against her skin. She could feel the pulse beating rapidly, shallowly in her temple. She tried to listen to his words. Deckrd's girlfriend had been some sort of clanner spy. And Deckrd believed that Stormrazer was the one who had gathered the evidence and turned her over to Omni-Pol. She gave Stormrazer a look, asking with her eyes, is this true? The look he gave in return said, No, I didn't do it . She believed him.

Deckrd seemed to catch this unspoken exchange, and pressed the gun harder to her temple. She saw his finger move. He squeezed the trigger.

"Click", he whispered, "Click".

In one fast movement, he let go of her, shoved her away, and had thrown aside the empty gun, pulling his mausser back out, and pointing it at her. Her legs buckled, and she sat down on the floor.
