A Dovve's Map Upgrade
Ive held back on sharing this map with anyone else other than my guild and a few friends, because other mappers who've untaken similar projects have been met with some hostility.

Producing an in-game map which contains information about dyna camps and some unique mobs is a contentious issue really - I dont want to take away the pleasure of finding these things out for yourself, nor do I want to take away from the people who have made the effort to find these things for themselves.

However, Im of the opinion that since most of the information contained in my map is readily available at fansites and within this forum all im doing is moving that information to a more convinient location and anything that encourages folk to get around the famtastic landscapes on offer on Rubi-KA as much as I have in the making of this map is a good thing.

Enough waffle anyway heres what ive changed:

-Revised Some Text and the Colour scheme to show more of the terrain from the original game map
-Revised Grid Requirements list to include Borealis and Old Athen
-Ammended Grid points in Trade and Tir and added Old Athen and Borealis

-Added Dyna-Camp markers and approximate boss level
-Added Unique Encounters markers
-Added Legend For Unique Encounters

The Dyna-Camps contained in the map represent the event that took place within my guild over the few days following patch 14.5 and the hard work undertaken in that event to find all these locations.

For the Unique Encounters section I used Darxy's excellent thread and all the contributions therein as a reference, in addition to the knowledge gained in one and a half years of playing AO.

Last word must be a mention for Dovve, I wouldnt have even attempted this had she not done all the hard work first.
