1) The races are currently pretty balanced, and I'm quite happy with them. With the possible exception of ability caps on the races i think the system works pretty well. I agree with Funcom that a moderate bonus/penalty to HP and NP is the way to go. The Sol. is the base to which you either add or subtract about 1/3 (not quite though because of stat differences) based on your race. If you want to play the stong race that isn't so good at casting you choose Atrox. If you want to play the smarter race that isn't so good at fighting you choose Nanomage. Otherwise you want a Opifex or Sol. Simple, makes sense. I like it.

2) Neither race is set up as the end all be all for a paticular class. This is a good thing. I don't like games where whenever i see an Imp i know he's a wizard and whenever i see a dwarf i know he's a blacksmith. Some people seem to want the Nanomage to have some kind of awesome advantage in casting and nothing else will do. This is the wrong way to go. Once you do that you start getting cookie cutter templates for every profession to the point where people wonder if you even have a brain in your head for not following the template.

3) Extra Nano skills for the NM is not a good solution. That is unbalancing and is a step in the wrong direction, headed right torwards cookie cutter templaes. Atrox don't get a special bonus to their fighting skills, just the minor stat bonus that NM gets too.

4) Nanomages have been penalized since the beginning in HP. This is nothing new. Why all of sudden the big problem?

5) More is always better. Even with Nanopool. You never know when a bunch of stuff is going to aggro and a NT or a Doc is going to need more pool. I run out sometimes. Not all the time, but it does happen.

6) NM don't really need more HP, just a team member that knows how aggro management is supposed to work. Remember, its primarily a team game we play. And for soloing, the typical NM professions can use the extra NP to counteract the disadvantage of the HP. NT's, Traders, and Crats can calm. Docs can heal themselves, and MP's have heal pets (yeah, that one has nothing to do with the Nanopool.)
