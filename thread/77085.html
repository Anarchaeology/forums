Notum Wars - gameplay issues
Seems to be quite a bit of discussion here on a variety of gameplay issues realting to NW, PvP and towers.

In order to make player's concerns about these more noticable, I suggest people post issues relating to the design and gameplay of NW as replies to this thread.

Please post only issues relating to the design and gameplay, not issues relating to bugs (yes, lag issues would count under gameplay since they impact on that).

And please just post the issue, described as clearly as possible, with examples where relevant. Do *not* post suggested solutions, that would just obscure the issues.

Maybe we can then get a discussion going on individual issues in their own threads...
