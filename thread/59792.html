14.6 Grenades
It looks like the next patch will have new grenades. They are buildable and appear to be an area affect weapon. There's also a small taunting effect that sounds kinda scary but will be more than worthwhile if it makes a nice explosion.

http://v020u46eff.maximumasp.com/AOD...sp?AOID=164781


http://v020u46eff.maximumasp.com/AOD...sp?AOID=165117

They appear to be low damage and low resist like the fixer cluster bullets. The first one does 120 damage(blah) and locks your skills for 10 seconds and the second one does 200-1000 damage and locks your skill for 20 seconds. By comparison, the cluster bullets lock your skill for 20 seconds and do 100-800 damage. I'm guessing by the skill reqs (900 grenade) that you'd need extreme prejudice and implants to use the ql 200 ones before TL6.

All in all OT 12 and Arwen M-404(the inits are fixed btw) users will now have a much needed special. Anyone else find this interesting? Oh yeah, this seems to be mainly an eng thing (even though I saw a trader the other day with an OT 12) since soldiers didn't get their grenade buff yet.

My question to the wonderful people on the test server are:
Do you have to equip them before you throw them?
Are the parts to build them available in shops?
