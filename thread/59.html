What's the status of tank armor?
Is the change in game mechanics to tank armor in 13.0 intentional, and if it is, is it here to stay?

For those who are not famillar with the issue:

In 13.0 the tank armor mechanics changed such that twice the nano is required to cast any NF while tank is worn. Previously, if you had 800 nano and the NF required 600 nano, you could cast the NF, but be left with zero nano. Now, 1200 nano would be required.

This is a serious problem for soldiers and enforcers, 99% of whom wear tank armor as soon as possible. For instance, Mirror Shield Mk 5, a QL86 NF, requires 867 nano to cast. Wearing tank armor, it would require 1734 nano. For an atrox (2 points per nanopool click), this is nigh impossible, until near the end of the character's career. Mk X, which requires 1451 nano, will never be castable wearing tank.

Imagine the surprise of many tank-wearing classes who logged on to find that they can no longer cast any of their high nano NFs!

The ramnifications:

Practically, this means that soldiers and enforcers will switch to Decus coats, which provide the same AC with no penalties of any sort. I suppose they could not wear tank, or wear nano cloaks too. However, I am pretty sure that the intention of the game designers is not to have tank armor be unused. After all, the graphics are very cool.

Could we please have an official response one way or the other?

Actually, Tank Armor always had a +100% nano cost modifier on it.

"Previously, if you had 800 nano and the NF required 600 nano, you could cast the NF, but be left with zero nano. Now, 1200 nano would be required."

Yes, that it true. But it was really a bug. Due to this bug you could still cast formula with less than the needed nano. This same bug also applied to False Profession as well.

Funcom has simply fixed that bug that let you cast without the proper nano as well as updated the description of Tank Armor to correctly indicate the nano cost modifier.

All I can suggest is that you take off your tank armor prior to buffing yourself.
