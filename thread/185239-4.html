Np

The imps spawn from fleas right when you enter Inferno. So, in-order to get the boots, you need the ring for inferno first. Once you have the ring, make sure you take along healers. Better yet, if you can ask someone who has the boots already to help you kill the imp(s).

The fleas are a one hit wonder. An imp will respawn after you kill a flea. Becareful though, it has fast regen and the 2k dots will toll away at the group. Drop rates is not 100% so you will have to hope for a lucky kill (I recommend you get the team level up before you go in for a better chance of it dropping).
