Blitzing
Blitzing: Entering a mission, with the sole purpose to get the item. You won't stop to kill any mobs, but just run, run, run. By doing this, you aquire a fair share of money/loot.

It is advised to have a lot of speed and/or health before trying this, at least if you're running a mission some levels above you.

Obviously, the only reason to blitz a mission is if it's too hard for you to fight your way through. So this is usually done with missions that have the difficulty slider pressed toward the right. Also, the missions of the "find item" variety seem to work best. Definitely avoid the assassinate missions for blitzing.

When I blitz, I try to find a mission with a reward that I want - a piece of armor, nano or maybe an implant I could use. Also, a high credit reward is a big plus. The most important part, though, is to get a mission in an area that has a high likelihood of producing a small mission. It's soooo much easier to blitz a 30 room mission than a 100 room mission. Home is a very popular spot at high levels. Clondyke seems to work well at lower levels.

When you blitz, make sure you have every defensive/speed buff going that you can. Also, remember to set your Agg/Def slider to the far left - it reduces damage taken and stops you from auto-attacking. Burst of speed stims are very useful for blitzing.

Then do as Vilbo said, run your little legs off. In some missions, you can start out by avoiding the mobs in the first few rooms. This lets you have a look around before you have to start running. Try to determine which part of the mission goes the farthest from the main room - that's usually where the item is hiding. This is easiest if you can see the doors on your mini-map.

Note that it's fairly common to die while blitzing - even if you're successful. Once you pick the item up, they can still hit you on your way out. Also note that it's ok if you have to run out without having found the item - just wait a few minutes until the entry room is empty and try again.

Low level blitzing
Just a tip . . . I have found that my low level characters usually suffer from a lack of decent armor and therefore get hurt a lot.

So just for fun, I started blitzing every one of them at level 4--even to 100% blitzes, depedning on the profession. I found I could, in a short and momentarily thrilling span of time, get a full set of cast off armor and a few extra credits toward a decent newbie weapon or my first nanos.

I now try to take missions for armor only--quest the armor, buy the guns and nanos, and mix and match as needed for implants. I'll take three missions at a time in the same place, complete one or two until I level, then blitz the rest for the armor I wanted. That way my armor deosn't lag behind my level so much.

I tend to solo a lot. This way I get a change--some patient killing, some hair-raising blitzing.

How to blitz
Here are the few basic rules/ways of doing this. ONLY take Find Item missions, or repair/fixit missions.

1.) If you are not a fixer, get a run buff from one.


2.) If you are not a soldier, get a reflect buff from one.

3.) If you are not an enforcer, get an Ess buff from one.

4.) If you are not a doctor get an HP buff from one.

5.) Buy some speed stims if you can afford it.

6.) try and take NPC/building dungeons of possible. The ones that are in the caves out in the wilderness are usually alot more un-organized and more maze like. harder to find your way around.


The item, 90% of the time is in the NW corner of the map. Run there as fast as you can ( damn fast with a fixer run buff+stim )


Make sure to keep your lockpick handy for any locked doors. ( i keep my inventory window open with the lockpick right there easily accesable )

Please note, this is how I do it, and may not be for everyone, works great for me though. I never die. But then, im a doc. I run it with HoT/big heals going. ( though, rarely do I even have to heal myself. I have a 1500 buffed run speed. )
