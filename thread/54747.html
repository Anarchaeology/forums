thanks for making me a online game
About a week ago it seemed as if the world was overrun with adventerous types. Yes barely a week ago a hue and cry was heard across the land to rise up and oppose these so called new masters of the rubi ca countryside, omni, neutral and clan alike all rouse to the occasion, the mighty guilds leading the way. Such a call to arms Ive not seen in almost a year on this planet. Men and Woman ive not seen for months rose up in arms to once again make there names known and feared. A glorious morning that was, no sooner had the sun risen then of we marched, no mob on the planet was to tough no foe to large a army of adventurers marched out of the cities and laid waste to these upstart noobs. The battles where glorious long and will long be remembered. but in the bars that night the whispers started and the names where repeated, like all the others I was amazed to hear the names of the fallen. The roll call of heros that died that day was amazing, many people found death that had not seen in years it seemed, no one was invulnerable no armor strong enough no nano good enough.

within 24 hours of that glorious dawn I saw it in the eyes of my fellow warriors, defeat and gloom, a cancerous malange of boredom and doubt. The silent quetion "Is Rubi Ka doomed", oft heard mentions was a hope of a better Galaxy among the Stars, with Wars that could be won.

Tis a quite world now many now gone, defeated for the last time enough is enough they said, tickets for off world are selling like wildfire. Me well I know the grass is not always greener on the other side so for now I hunt alone mostly, its to dangerous in a group I must be careful quiet and very descreet. The politicians wont admit it and the papers wont print it but the truth is we have lost the countryside. There are new masters in this world and no one person or even the mightest guilds can long stand up to there wrath.

alas there is one bright spot in this darkness. the new master of Rubi Ka use some very fancy technology unknow to most on this planet. So I hunt them, they own the land but I own the shadows, the dark spot the alley's and the backways. Everyone makes mistakes and when they do I am there to take them down. The bright spot, well once in awhile not often mind you I recover a baubble from the charred bodies. This new technology is very very nice and Ive used some myself to greatly increase my chances against this new menace and what I dont use I sell for a pretty penny in the towns.

Oh problems yes I have a few. I did have a mishap the other day I was flying to low and a few fellows called masked cammondoes blew me out of the sky with 2 shots I was rather impressed, 4k and 3k with 2 hits. Oh well that car was due for a tuneup anyways.

sometimes it seems nice out here but then I realize how lonely it is, have ever tried to talk to one of these mobs, geez they got big guns but not to much upstairs.

oh well maybe the gods will change the course that is upon us and once again the brave souls will rise up, one can only hope. but for now if you look close in the shadows you will see a body or two, look above the left eyebrow if you see a little "t" you will know Ive been there.

I dont dream of a galaxy far far away but Im a little lonely and maybe the light is faiding on my career?
