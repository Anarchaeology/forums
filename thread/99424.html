The Future Is Here
The Clans are dead. Oh yeah, of course there is a whole load of people prowling around whose Affiliation is stated as Clan, but those who have risen from the ashes of exploitation and slavery, those who have fought Omni-Tek to gain freedom, the Clans are gone. All that remains are groups of people arguing with each other over ancillary issues. You still occasionally hear about the need for unity among the Clans, need for a common voice, but this is all talk. There no longer is a single person who commands the trust and support of the majority of the clan population. The person Radiman was, in as much as he was incompetent and impotent in his duties, he was a single voice for the Clans. Most of the political actors present among the Clans carry with them a history baggage that is almost guaranteed to produce strong voices of opposition whatever that person(s) may decide to undertake for the benefit of the Clans. The Clans themselves have stopped caring for the most part  we live in a different world than those who came before us: the War does not stop us from travelling all around this planet nor does it impede our progress in this world; we no longer need to fight for our freedom  we have Sentinels providing protection and the fact that those thugs murder innocent people bothers only a few. And most of us are concerned about the political situation only as far as our own territories are involved, otherwise it becomes someone elses problem. A society that lacks cohesion and where the majority of the population does not care as to what happens around them is a dead society. The Clans are in need of rebirth, the Clans are in need of guidance and leadership.
Who will provide it?
