Omni Guild recruiting level 75-125
The Descendants are now recruiting players level 75-125. We will take quality players of any level, but this is the range where most of our members are at present time.

We are an Omni guild looking for new friends and like minded folks that would like to join us. We are not a RP guild nor are we a PVP guild, we are basically a bunch of mature down to earth normal folks that enjoy gaming, having friends in game with which to do things and succeeding at the things we do.

Feel free to check out our web site: The Descendants<http://www.the-descendants.net/>

If this sounds like the type of organization that might interest you please drop by our message board here: The Descendants<http://pub36.ezboard.com/bthedescendants> and leave us a message telling us a bit about yourself.
