At lvl 100, the mki starts to feel a bit snug. How much is mkii auctioning for again? How many claws have you killed today?

At lvl 1??, mkii's AC (750 across the board) begins wearing out. GA mkiii anyone?

By lvl 1??, the mkii is tiny, and even mkiii is to small.

So... two investment challenges here... once going down the GA path, a fixer is locked into spending ip on matt creat. After putting on the first blue suit, they're automatically needing the next.
