Ponder.
quote:
Originally posted by EvilGlitch

This talk of Clan getting skill advantages is utter crap. EVERYONE in their right mine wil instantly fill out a clan application form, the game will really be fun then.
How is this, as you say, "utter crap"? "The game will really be fun"? Was that sarcasm, or what?

My view of the Clans - and by association, Clan characters - is that, yes, everyone -should- be in a clan. You're not a clan member because you hate Omni-Tek; you're a clan member because you're not an OT employee, -and- are part of a seperate organisation working against the OT agenda, either peacefully or not. If you're not part of a clan, you're just a neutral out to get Omni. Mind you, that's my view, and not necessarily anyone else's, so don't bug me about it. ;p

I do think that every clan character -should- be in a Clan and, yes, everyone in their right mind -would- want the skill bonuses. Why not? It enhances the RP aspect. Imagine:

The Bashers are a group of Atrox (Disadvantage: Atrox only) living out in the Wastelands. They trade with the Rhinomen (Advantage: Rhinomen Ally), and hunt the various wildlife for food and sport. They're slow learners (Disadvantage: 5% XP penalty) and not terribly bright (Disadvantage: -20 Intelligence) on the whole, but use the bones and other bludgeoning implements that they find to great effectiveness (Advantage: 1h Blunt and Advantage: 2h Blunt).

Now, obviously a couple of those things might not be used: the "Ally" I envisioned as being accepted by an established group of social NPCs; if they're normally aggressive, they ignore you, if they're normally passive, they'll come to your aid. Likewise, the stat and experience penalties might not make it in.

But I think the point stands that advantages and disadvantages (don't leave off that second one) would be a wonderful tool to set your clan and character apart from everyone else's. You can back up your claim of "We beat people up with blunt things the best" by an actual skill improvement.

And to head it off at the pass: while I think every clan member should be in an established clan by about level 10 or so, not everyone is going to be, or will want to be, so maybe you'll just have a background clan of family members or such. Again, my feelings on the subject, and not really relevant to the discussion. Forgive me my wandering. ;)

You also seem to insinuate that people playing neutrals and Omni would immediately switch sides for these advantages. Why? Roleplaying - even in minor form, even if it's just the idea you have for your character - does come in to play, if you'll pardon the strained pun. I'll never play a Clan, because I just can't sympathise with them, and their cities are so damned -dirty-. Hard to respect people like that. ;)

Also, remember that Omni detachments (our version of clans) will soon(ish?) be able to join the established OT departments, like OmniMed and OmniPol. I think we can safely assume that there will be -some- bonus for that. Maybe more equipment, which we'll talk about later down, maybe more money, or something non-skill related.

Let's look at your third point (I skipped the first; don't see anything -terribly- wrong with it):

quote:
By the way, if you ever shift+clicked on your Stamina ability you would see that Implants are suppose to lower your stamina, but they do not. Things change. Besides I am always hearing "Omni get equipment, Clan get skills" but why have I never heard this from anyone or anything even remotely official?
On the Stamina/implant issue, I hope to see this in at some point. It'd certainly help limit the massive overequipping that goes on. I don't think its been ruled out; maybe we can get an FC comment on that.

"Besides, I am always hearing "Omni get equipment, Clan get skills" but why have I never heard this from anyone or anything even remotely official?"

I'm sort of curious where you're coming from here. Breaking it down:

"Omni get equipment,"

Well.. yeah. Forest, Desert, Omni-Pol armours are all Omni-only. They're pretty good, not to mention they look spiffy. I think this is one of those things that will get expanded as time goes by.

"Clan get skills"

The original plan had been that Clanners would get bonus IP per level; this has -always- been planned from the get-go, as far as I can recall. I can't point you to a line on the website that says this, because the setup has changed and some of the pages that used to be around a few months ago have been moved, replaced, or deleted. But it's not some backroom rumour.

I think the push for Advantages and Disadvantages for the clans is a revamping of this idea. I'd have to check the Envisioned list; and speaking of that..

"..but why have I never heard this from anyone or anything even remotely official?"

Its been on the website. Exhibit A, from the Envisioned list:

Omni-Tek Departments (such as Omni-Pol)! Omni-Tek will be opening their ranks to their citizens - Omni Guilds will be able to join story-based official departments such as Omni-Pol.

and Exhibit B:

Clan special abilities  Clan organisations will be able to choose certain skills in which all members of the organisation receive a buff.

So.. I guess you're just not looking in the right place.

Summing up, the different sides (except probably neutral ;) are all going to have different advantages and disadvantages. I know people are screaming and railing that FC needs to shape up by the beginning of February or they'll cancel their six-month accounts, and maybe they're justified, and maybe they're not. I know that I'm a patient individual, and few AO as a large, pretty MUD, and don't get too worked up about it. I'm content to wait and see what happens.

Your mileage may vary, of course.
