Helpbot: Steps of Madness is located in the southeast part of the world, in Omni Forest at 800x2800. Leave Omni Ent by the east gate to reach the dungeon. Recommended for teams of lvl 35-45.

To [helpbot]: whereis cyborg barracks
Helpbot: Cyborg Barracks is located in the northeast part of the world, in Greater Tir County at 3230x2340. Recommended for teams of lvl 70-90.

To [helpbot]: whereis Smugglers Den
Helpbot: Smuggler's Den is located in the southeast corner of the world, in Southern Foul Hills, at 1755x872.

To [helpbot]: whereis Camelot Castle
Helpbot: Camelot Castle is located in the northwest part of the world, in the center of the city of Avalon and can be reached by whompa from Old Athens. Recommended for teams of lvl 100-130.
