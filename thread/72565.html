A personal shop for a trader
Being a trader I have one suggestion that make my and other traders life a bit (a lot) easier. To sell an item (let's say a nano that I have just made myself) I would have to post it on one of trading channels (never worked for me, may be the thing I was trying to sell were crap ) or ask people around whether they want to buy it or not. It feels very nice in the beginning and it is very annoying as soon it becomes a rutine. I would like to have my personal shop where I would be able to sell the thing that I created, found, traded etc. To make it fun I am ready to pay a rent, a high one if it is necessary. What do you think?
I guess other profesions would like to have shops for themselves too, it would be cool as well.
