The Guardians Seeking New Members
Hello, we are a clan guild working on Rimor (RK-2) for the benefit of others and the defense of the clans. We currently hold the notum mining location just North of the New Land Desert Whompa and hope to hold this location in the future.

We seek recruits for our guild of any level and experience level. We spend considerable energy helping new players in AO as our guild's primary mission is to help others.

If you would be interested in learning more about us and helping us defend this mining location please send a tell in game to Sedition, Bagaboanta, or Dezmathio. You can also post here with your ingame contact information and we will contact you. If you want to just try out a guild you can join and leave at any time.

Thank you for your time and thanks to all the guilds that stood with us on 12/3/02, we appreciate all of your efforts and look forward to helping you in turn.
