Re: < 5th BureauCon > Planning and feedback
quote:
Originally posted by Charles
2) Future issues [no topics scheduled]
Having some priorities to propose ideas for FC would maybe be helpful.

Our skills are right now divided in several categories that could make our profession utterly flexible, both able to solo and indispensable for grouping :

PET skills
Pet : Feeble tank, walking DoT. Some buffs and heals.
Charm : Stable and unstable line, flexible nanos used for tanking and crowd control. Hindered at heigher level by Psy checks and the lack of adequate buffing.

DAMAGES
DD : Good and reliable, second only to NT.
Stuns : Too high level for me to get a grasp here.
Pistol : Some buffing for useless weapons, granted that some of them are ok for their Int bonus.

CROWD control
Mezz : Crowd control solo or group oriented with high level AoE
Root : idem, with early AoE.
Snares : OK for outdoor adventuring and some hardcore mission blitz. Nothing special to say.
Fear : Crowd control not much used since damage seems to break fear like for mezz.

GROUP nanos
Speeches : Still under evaluation, crit buff are very nice, a lot of spamming right now that should be fixed soon.
XP buff : Nice buffs that were increased recently.
XP save : The promised last second "I'mdyingbutnotloosingXps" feature.

PvP
In all the previous categories some of our nanos are disabled.
We are thus a bad NT able to root / nuke with a marginal droid DoT.




In all these categories what are the ones we should focus on ?

I'd say that right now the droid part of the PET category is fine, when we all know that these Psy checks have to be tweaked for the charm nano lines. Getting some feedback on the end of a charm is also required.
Still some pathing errands subsist and a pet teleport nano would help a lot.

In the DMGES category we are also fine. Pistols are not a positive nor negative issue : we don't need weapon and if we want to PvP with an extra source of damage, shotguns are the choice.

CROWD control is fine too. I am not sure if roots need a bit more tweaking or not but it's unlikely imho. We can have a look at the Fear nano line, since it seems that none bother with it ...
Having a way to toggle off automatic attack when aggroed without moving AGG-DEF slider would be nice though.

GROUP oriented nanos -speeches- are a bit more difficult to evaluate : they haven't been live for long and their effects are not spectacular. We need more feedback on the crit buff at least.

Basically I have the feeling that Charm is the only issue pending in PvM. That and maybe the shift that Crats feel at 90-100 when the are not that much a pet profession but a group crowd control profession. Having some extra useful buff that would enhance pet casting and mainly charms would be quite a nice move.

PVP : Enhancing the way Mezzes are working on players would be a first step.
These Mezzes should have a stun effect on opponents or at least a major chance of interrupting nano casting / attack / specials, even if the stun is lasting only 1-2 sec.
Alternatively they could have an attack init debuffing effect. Slowing down attacks is maybe more balanced than stunning ...

I have a very strong feeling that we should be able to charm players too, even with really failure probability. I know that FC is very biased about this, but I also know that it's working very smoothly in EQ.

Anyway, I'd be glad to see fear in the eyes of ppl fighting Crats in PvP. Let's just keep in mind that we are doing a very good job against MP and Engies, unlike most other professions.

Sadly, once again I will be at work while all this goes on (I dont get home until 7pm eastern on a Saturday). But I will toss in some suggestions...

In going to disagree with Gummizluv, and support the proposal
put forth by game designer Gaute last week. Gaute proposed thats roots and snares be changed to become far more useful in group hunting especially. Here is what he proposed to sum up.

>To make roots become somewhat of long range combat debuffs. >(This change will not affect people or pets in PvP! Rooting is >powerful enough as it is in PvP!) It only means rooting a >monster will reduce its ability to hit you in normal PvM. This >should make roots more effective, and the rooting professions >more wanted.
>The higher level the root, the more effective the Initiative Debuff.

I strongly support this and 'Crats might wish to discuss it further at the convention. This of course benefits any "root" profession not just us.

But I will point out that means we should VERY strongly push for the "Red Tape" to be beefed up significantly since we will likely see low level roots outdo the debuff init values on our very high level nano which would make "Red Tape" almost pointless. I also think that some lower level "Red Tape" debuffs should be made available [enabling crats to init debuff enemies would further enhance us in groups [and solo] and further distinguish us from other classes - also will help us help out pets without needing any pet changes. So in other words there might be "Minor Red Tape" at level 25, "Lesser Red Tape" at level 50, etc etc. These nanos debuff both runspeed and init's.

Gaute talking about upping the antee on Mobs and huntning, with more "Boss" spawns, aggro etc.. I believe a good line of init debuffs will be critical to keeping us in the game for crowd control capability.

Gaute also proposes making snares [runspeed debuffs] much tougher to break.

I personally would like to see a lower level AoE Mezz, say around level 50-60? Next one in the low 100s?

Here is another thing that has had 'Crats talking.

>To split auto-return fire from a high Agg/Def setting. Right now, >if you are attacked, you will auto-return fire when your Aggdef >setting is above 0. This is a problem for all crowd-controllers, as >they will hit the opponent just after making them sleep, and >thus breaking the nano. The auto-return fire will be an option >setting to control as you wish. You will then have to hit "attack".

Again, I applaud this, but some of you might disagree. Certainly worth discussing and possibly showing support on?

We've beaten the Psychic check horse to death? I think we have fairly solid unity on how to alleviate this problem by using our green Psychology skill as an offset and/or provide a fast/strong Psychic debuff. I'd prefer the former over the later but again is it worth debating again?? Maybe draw up a petition?

Nanos are costing me a fortune, anyone like to see 'Crats [and Docs] get some kind of better nano regenerator (something closer to, but not quite as good as humidity extractor?]

Tradeskills: Coffee, Insurance??

Alot of people are moaning about hunting in the far away regions [where no nearby insurance terminals and even by yal takes awhile to get there].

Insurance policy - a paper tradeskill where you buy a policy, cash it in for a 1 time insta save. Unique - so you can only have 1 at a time. You will respawn whereever you last had saved.

Diplomatic Immunity - a paper tradeskill - When this is used, it executes a 1 NCU nano that lasts 1 hour, and makes you immune to PvP. You cannot be attacked and you cannot attack. The nano acts somewhat as a hostile nano, so you cannot use it to "gank" someone, you can go into 25% or 0% zones and not PvP at all for that period of time. This means a group could go hunting with no fear of being attacked by the enemy. [Taunted maybe, but not attacked]. This could also solve alot of RP problems that occur when certain people show up to gank a speaker or an event centered person... *cough*.

-ADDED- I should also say this includes guards. Someone else originally posted this idea as a way to travel to an enemy city without being ganked by the guards, guess my idea takes it 1 step further.

Coffee?? Maybe we can figure out what the deal is with that. Small buffs perhaps?

Faction Advantage/Disadvantage - Gives guilds certain unique characteristics, would be nice to hear more about whats planned here, anyone have suggestions or express what they would like to see?

Droids - really only thing I'd like to see is some kind of "Droid Mastery" buff that say adds an extra +25 to time/space, matter creation to help keep our droid reasonably strong without needing to pester MP's for masteries. Might endear Engineers to us to help them keep their pets up for the same reason.

Can we be more of a help in Role Playing? What tradeskills, buffs or powers could we get that would help us be true diplomats and business people of Rubi-Ka?
