Mine would be:

Deaddreamer's Apathy
Item: Misc.
QL: 1
Description: An illegal substance that gives the user a feeling of detachment from their surroundings, darkening everything around them except for a few feet in front of them. Wings of Gossamyr
Item: Social Armor
Location: Back
QL: 1
Description: Conceptualized by programmer Madeline Fonataro, this piece was developed to accentuate the female form, and still give it a modern look. Originally woven with thin fiber-plasteel tubing with reactive gasses and nanobots, it creates a shimmering effect through the feathery patterned lace, backed by a silvery undercoat.

Somnatanna Deck
Item: Misc.
Location: Deck
QL: 50
Deck Slots: 4 (1,3,5,6 open)
Visual Profession: Nanotechnician
Description: After tinkering with the generic brand decks, Madaline Fontanaro of Omni-Tek altered the assembler code to a more efficient system of interchange. The end result was a more adaptable, plug and play system that allowed for devices to be connected in the middle of the deck instead of on the end, as the 6-slot decks would accomodate.
