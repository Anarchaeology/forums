Forget the dots, they are useless until post 125 and even then you have to focus on mat crea rather then t&s (team hp buff). Don't touch the debuffs at your level as well, enfs won't have the tools to get aggro back from you.

Leveling is tough as a doc mid level, hang in there tho make sure you are doing stuff you know you can handle, better to team with a lower team knowing you'll survive. Also, it's better to save 2 times during a mission then to die losing all, if your teammates get upset, tell them why you save, if they want speed, they should play Q3. It will get better.

Try to hook up with a decent enforcer and do a few missions together, he can do the damage while you manage his health. This got me through a lot of hard times, doesn't go fast, but at least it keeps going and it's a lot easier to control.

Take it from a doc that has been one more times times then I can count. We are a good class, need some touches here and there, but in general we are not bad at all (especially post 125)
