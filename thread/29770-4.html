In order to really get the full benefit from using MA weapons, you have to make sure the weapon's and your fist's speeds are as close to 1/1 as possible. Parry sticks and wall-blades are much slower than fists; you usually have to have 600+ inits to make them sync up with your fists. Most MA's stick with claws (or just fists) because of that fact.

I've experimented with all three types of MA weapons (there is a 4th now - the Bang-Bang glove - but I haven't made an effort to try them out yet), and I found that claws are best at low- to mid-level fighting despite their low damage ratings and lack of buffs. Once I get up to 150 or so (and have IP to spare), I'll probably throw on a wall-blade and go to town.
