The mobs round the op in lush fields drop instruction disks. QL depends on which area you hunt:

NW Pipe: ql 10-20

SW Pipe: ql 15-25 (more if ur lucky)

SW Mine ql 20-30 (I think)

NW Cave area: ql 50+ and Ive got 79 there once.

They're all mutants of some form, but if you see a Lifebleeder at NW Cave - run because your life depends on it. It generally takes 1-2 of the groups that hunt there to kill one. I've died loads cos they agg NTs.

Also - the mutants drop implants (at same ql), monster parts (also same ql) and disks - so it can take time to find any good ones.
