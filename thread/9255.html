New Fixer Abilities and Teaming
Although I have played all the profs, I have spent most time with my fixer and the class is as interesting and innovative as any, albeit somewhat less "powerful" than most.

The reason for the post and why it comes into soloing/teaming is to do with use of the grid.

Everyone loves and uses the grid (when they can) and it's an innovation that our hosts deserve credit for. Recently my fixer was blessed with a plane, which allowed me to cross into areas, which I had never dreamed of being able to visit. I discovered new cities, brain dog encampments, huge waterfalls and a number of unique denizens, which I'm sure few have seen.

I also have seen all over RubiKa unusual equipment which looks like a cross between an insurance terminal and a grid access.

My idea would be to spread out the grid access more widely into difficult areas, limit it to higher complits, but make the grid locations "fixer only". The fixer would also need a team version of beacon warp to get the whole team there.

The effect of this would be to make the fixer more useful in reaching distant locations - even some locations take some while by plane - encourage groups to hunt more, rather than continuous mission in BS, and give easier access to higher lvl opposition, for the 100+ players, of which I am not one (Lvl94 currently).
