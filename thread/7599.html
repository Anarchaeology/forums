Guild Issue
I've recently created a guild with my lvl 40 Enforcer, and one of the thing that I find most annoying is having to give my members their wages seperately. I believe that a guild bank account would be a great way of keeping check of the guild funds, and of distributing wages. For example, the guild could automatically pay its members from the guild account, so that the leader, or whoever deals with wages, doesn't have to go through each member seperately and give them their money that way. Also, my guild has a 'quartermaster' (that being me ) it would be good if/when the guild houses are set up to perhaps have an 'armoury' room, where weapons, armour, etc are stored for guild members who want them.

Anyway, that's my bit, see ya ppl!
