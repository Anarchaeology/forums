Don't remember coords, but:
- Newland: Neuters'r'us. Find bronto burger and walk inside to bar from nearby door. Very small, but pretty clean.
- Reet Retreat: Go from the Newland to the Borealis, and from Borealis to Stret West. Retreat is huge building, middle of the town.
- Dancing Atrox: Just outside Omni-E, behind wall.
- Mort's bar (don't remember it name now... hmm... something like "beer and booze"...). Worst bar in Rubi-Ka (=bring your own beers). Neutral Outpost in Mort. Avoid barfights and bar's toilet.
