Feedback Requested
Ok for starters I must say that I am no developer for Funcom but I have read on a few forums that people would like to see a page where they could post their opinions of other in game characters as both a warning to others and a compliment to those that are truely great.

My idea is this: A site that would work must like a searchable forum, where one could put the name of the person they wish to look up and get a list of player feedback about that person be it good or bad. Furthermore those that wish to provide information about a character would be able to input the players in game name and post information. Before I begin this undertaking however I would like to get feedback, criticisms, or ideas of any kind that you all have about this.

I have said it over and over again. ALL kind of blacklisting is BAD.

Someone killed you in PVP? Name them in the list as a bad person.
Someone kicked you from a team as you were behaving badly and calling everyone else idiots? Name them all as bad people.
Someone does not have time to buff you? Name them as a bad person.

Unless you have a definite way of confirming every single entry, don't bother. People generally remember good people and don't bother to go out of their way to make sure everyone else knows they're good, but when they have one bad experience with someone they have never seen before they might very well want to do something about it if they are given an easy way to do so.
