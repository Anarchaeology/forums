When will the other 99% of weapons in this game become viable?
I am sure lots of people are sick and tired of seeing everyone dress exactly the same and use exactly same weapons. This is Anarchy Online not Attack of the Clones (online) dang it.

Seriously, it's just some simple logic

more viable weapons = more variety

more variety = more styles.

more styles = more interesting gameplay & easier to RP.

more interesting gameplay & easier to RP. = harder to get bored

harder to get bored = prolonged game life

prolonged game life = more $$$ for Funcom.

more $$$ for Funcom = they could actually save themselves from the crap they pulled off last June now.

you would think after all this novaflow, beam and shotgun craps Funcom would've learnt something and start dealing with 99% of other useless weapons and actually make the game more fun. The way I see it, 14.2 is a good chance for Funcom to redeem itself and starts doing something about it.


Modify existing craps so they might actually be useful

There are some advantages in this, such as saving time to write descriptions and making new arts for the new weapons, does not increase server load, immediately improvment of "variety" in game etc.

Add new weapons

New stuffs are good as long as...

-They are actually worth using.
Please no more moon edge, burning copper katana, and a ****load of crappy pistol that does 1-100 damage at ql 200. They add NOTHING to the game since nobody will never, ever use them.

-They are available by normal means
This is the most important part, variety means people have CHOICE to use a weapon that suit his/her style. Something so hard to get that it only a handful of people will ever see them does NOT exactly give people a choice, thus does zippo in making the game more fun.

Modify the game engine so fast low damage weapon will have a chance to compete with slow hard hitting weapons

This is hard and franky there is a zero chance Funcom will ever pull this off.


On the side note when are you going to fix MA attacks anyway?

And how about bow special attacks? Bugs aside, it seems someone in Funcom thinks doing 800 damage every 12 minutes (ql 200 btw) is "balanced", giving you such high hope for the future of this game...


Remember:

camping is BORING.

everybody and their mon wear the same armor is BORING.

everybody and their dad use the same weapon is BORING.

everybody and their brother put IPs into same set of skills is BORING.

Another view we could have is that in a war, each side has its own equipment and that battle personnel usually dress alike.

For armor, I beleive there should be restrictions by profession to at least differ professions that way.

For guns, I beleive a Funcom employee should try each gun, same ql, against the same mob and they would see that only a few guns are viable for survival. As a soldier, I got alot of guns ql 200 that use approx. the same skills. I must a tried at least 10 similar Premium guns against the same mob, conclusion:


1-Without Crit buff = Nova flow
2-With crit buff = Gamma beamer
3-Flashpoint = Takes out big chinks, but swith time once specials are passed makes un-useful in PVM

I won t even mention the other guns.Those are the 3 most popular soldier guns.

The big problem is the range of DMG our guns produce is so unpredictable from MOB to MOB, that the same mob that you got down in 5 hits in one battle, can take you down the next time around even thought you may have 40 hits in! (I experienced 20 dmg hits against grey mobs one hit, and a 3.4 k crit the next, I seen a ratio of 90% crits on one mobs and 10% crit on another. I seriously can t understand why I would have 7 crits in a row on the first enforcer and none on the second even though they are same level mobs)

It simple, it comes down to DOT(Dammage over time) since there is no run or hide strategy wihout zoning. Now, we got methods to slow down DOT and so do mobs, but those methods also remain unpredictable.

So bottom line is, we need more viable equipment, I remember starting to play wanting to be a dual pistol wielding soldier, boy where those dreams shattered quickly!
