Mission Problems.
I dunno what's goin' on, but I'v been getting some really odd missions....

First off, around half the missions I get, I find locked doors. Usually, they arn't a consern. I'm able to find other ways around 'em. Or I don't need to go into the area in order to finish the mission. I just shrug and leave the monsters to do their thing. But i'v been getting missions where the very first door is locked! I can't get into the main room of the mission, at all. These happen once every ten missions or so, if even. Still, they're a hassle when they're there.

Second, I recently just did a mission, where the whole map was only about 13-15 rooms totall. One monster. Had to get a Philip Ross painting. Took about 5 minutes to explore the whole place. It was odd, though. I could only find one monster, and I made triple sure I didn't miss any doors. When I picked up the painting, I didn't get my token, either. I figured there had to be more monsters, and that means more rooms. As you'v probably guessed, I still found no other rooms then the ones I'v been in already. Am I missing something here?

These mission are starting to act up the more levels I gain. Perhaps they're just getting more difficult because of that, and not any bugs or anything. Still, I could use some re-assurance it's not the games fault. Anyone else getting these?

Every once in a while I get those 'one mob missions.' You won't get a token for these. I guess the game thinks you didn't work hard enough or something to earn a token. I seriously doubt those missions are supposed to exist. I've done hundreds of missions, and those type you described didn't appear until after patch 13.0 I believe. I get them every 30 missions or so. They're always 'white cave' type missions, and the 2 zones I noticed them in were Milky Way and Clon**** (Clon**** y coord less then 1000). Don't remember the coords for MW. Somewhere something got bugged. I submitted a report on them, but heard nothing.

As for those missions with the first door being locked, dunno what to tell you there. All missions have locked doors somewhere. My advice to you would be to raise your B and E or bring an agent or fixer friend with you on missions to pick the locks. The door locks are generally weaker then chest locks, so you wouldn't have to raise your B and E very much to get past them.
