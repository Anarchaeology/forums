Improved aggro management
When teaming, we lack one important tactic; formations. I.e. we can not put big tanks in front of the weaker professions to suck up the damage. Sure, we can physically place them in front but mobs will totally ignore this and run straight through the meat shield and start whacking the doc if it feels like it. To compensate for this, I suggest implementation of Taunt Modifier (TM).

Taunt modifer affects how much a character's damage output and healing actually annoys the mob. For example: an enforcer and an agent are engaging a mob. The enforcer has a +10% TM and the agent has -10% TM. Assume both of them deals out 1000 points of damage to the mob. The enforcer will taunt the mob as if he had done 1100 points of damage, while the agent only taunts for 900 points. This will make the enforcer hold aggro, simulating that he is physically blocking the mob from reaching the weak agent.

Some suggestions on what could affect TM:
  • Certain nanos: Mongo, essence, challenger, replace useless soldier taunts with short duration TM nano
  • Profession: enfs get an innate +TM, doc -TM
  • Breed: atrox get +TM, nanomage -TM
  • Special attacks: Full auto, brawl; +TM, sneak attack, aimed shot; -TM
  • Weapons: Torturing tool comes to mind as a good taunter
