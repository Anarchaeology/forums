Pre pre patch notes :)
Patch 14.6 should be released later this month, pending Quality Assurance approval. The following are just the few things we think are major changes:

Fixes

The Atrox have more hitpoints and Nano-Mages have a larger nano pool.

Content Additions

A new dungeon for lower-level players.

New nanos for several classes.

All new character creation system!

An arrival hall following character creation is much like a futuristic airport with lots of helpful information about Rubi-Ka.

Booster pack shops...you can't buy anything yet, but you'll see what will be available.
