Problems with Tradeskills
I'll keep it short and simple: It should be cheaper to construct weapons and items via Tradeskills than to buy them in the shop. I don't know about you, but I was trying to construct a QL37 Nano Crystal (Shielding Barrier or something), assembled it half-way, got down to needing a specific part, and my mouth hit the floor when I found out that the last piece I needed costed 350000 credits. 350k?! The crystal in the store was only 21k! I might as well as save 329k and BUY the actual crystal! Also, Tradeskills should be expanded a bit, ergo:

We should be able to construct certain 'desirable' items, ergo Rings of *insert damage type here*, though it should be NODROP to prevent a major flock to the Tradeskill professions.

Rings that actually DO things. I like constructing jewelry for cash and all, but it's kind of mind-numbing after a while, since while it turns a nice profit, and earns me some XP, I can't make anything useful for my character (Engineer).

So, to sum it up: Let us build some USEFUL weapons, and lessen the cost of Tradeskill items so that it's cheaper to build them than buy them. Simple and easy. =)
