Ack... I can't support Borrace's MC option at all... Sorry Borrace.

Always triple-implant your primary weapon skill! Always, always!

1) Help you crit more
2) Help you miss less
3) Help you do more damage
4) Help you equip better pistols

If 42 isn't much on Pistol...or equipment...then it's even less useful for damage shields. What's a couple more points of shield damage when 42 more attack rating will raise your average damage on a PoP by 30+ points a hit?

Anyway, to the other options...

The Shining Map Nav can be swapped with Rifle.. That's kinda up to folks. It's not hard to equip a TIM scope with a shade of IP anyway. (Or a buff)

Sense in the head will raise your Pistol skill, and IMO that's a little more useful than having Perception. Considering I can get 950+ Perception right now with just the Ear implanted and all buffed up. (Note that I've spent 0 IP in Perception) There isn't really a particular reason for the faded cluster that I can think of...

Revelator, that's not a bad idea. Trap Disarm is mostly just there for the heck of it... No huge reason for it--although we will need about 300 Trap Disarm to get rid of Mines in the 14.8 missions.

-Jayde
