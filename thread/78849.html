Re: AORC.exe
quote:
Originally posted by Emiko_MoK
This is normal for logging into AO ingame chat, but how doI know my passwords are not sent to some malicious unknown destination?
I guess you really don't. I do not transfer the usernames or passwords in ANY way out of AORC. This is meant to be a nice third party chat program for Anarchy Online, and in no way would I ruin the application's or my own reputation by adding things like that.

But then again, you can't just take someone's word for it. As others have suggested, you can set up a firewall to only allow AORC to connect to the specific hosts and ports for the AO dimensions (you can get those hosts and ports in the latest version of AORC).

Basically it comes down to trust. This really isn't asking you to trust anymore than CS, Nano Nanny, or any other EXE that you willingly download and run, and could either help you with your clusters or erase your entire harddrive.

As a side note, I also develop professionally for a living IRL, so this also plays into ethics. Again, you don't know me from any other online scum, but putting back-doors, virii, trojans, etc in my applications is something I would NEVER do.

Anyways, if you do build up the trust to try out AORC, I very much hope you like it
