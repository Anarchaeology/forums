Alternative "Mission" Tokens
Hey all i've got a not so crazy idea. What if the Funcom were to implement tokens similar to mission tokens but were Hunting Tokens? I feel that there is honor and heroism in clearing the wasteland of evil and monsters don't you?

I for one HATE missions. I hate them with a passion. The endless well lit rooms crawling with the same mobs and empty treasure chests and the repetition kills me. Am I the only one that finds missions redundant? Mission tokens and the Token Boards that come with them are a really great thing to have.

I feel that if you were to get a token for simply hunting for a while this would add another level to the game. You could use the same token board you use for mission tokens and they would be the same except in name.

What do you think? Please leave some ideas I'd love to hear some feedback on this such as what you would need to do to attain these tokens.
