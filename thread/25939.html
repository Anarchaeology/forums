Don't keep us in the dark
I admit first and foremost I write this after I just die because I entered a room thinking I would be able to Mez the Mob in there but for some reason it didn't agro on me. I knew immediately oh-on and sure enough 10 seconds later everything catches up as I get a flurry of messages catching up to me and am sent to the reclaim losing 2/3 of the experience towards my next level and not a chance to execute even one nano or know what had happened to me. I would write this off as a fluke except that it has happened so many time in the last week that it is becoming unbearable. My only request is that if you read this and have experienced anything like this since the New German server opened then bump this, it needs attention and we need Funcom to respond to their own PR.

With the release of the new German server (RK-3) many people have begun to experience problems of lag, hesitancy or delays in commands being responded to by game systems, and total drop outs. After extensive use of ping, pingtrace, and a few routing latency tools it has been determined by a host of individuals that the "new German server" is actually being hosted from the same site as RK-1 and RK-2. The actual routing for it seems to be primarily using RK-2 routing subsystems in my tests, this is once again an example of FunCom convienently twisting a Press release to say what they want to. By New Websters dictionary of the English Language :

resources n. - pl. funds: available property, natural sources, means or capabilities of any kind; the ability to deal with any problem or situation.

By this definition Bandwidth and routing capabilities is a resource which they are pulling away from the current existing players and customers. On the other hand they have been refering to resources as manpower which although part of the definition is not the whole of it. So for everyone who has been experiencing issues this isn't your imagination that difference of 20% or greater in ping and those lost packets with your nanos and shots you've been firing are real. And once again it's Funcom overstepping themselves, taking advantage of their customer base and not putting out the whole truth.

Once again bump it if you have experienced it.
