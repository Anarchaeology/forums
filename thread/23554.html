feature articles direct to your comms
IC

Some of you may know I came here from sol to report the story of the clans to would be supporters and potential colonists. You may even know that I decided to stay and join the clans. You might have noticed that I haven't been writing as much lately as I spend more time getting to understand Rubi-ka.

On the other hand, you may not want to spend your time on Rubi-ka constantly checking external feeds... so you might not even have heard of Meranews. It's more features than news really, and I should point out that I have never ever claimed to be an unbiased news reporter. I write features, opinionated features at that. But - should you want to hear my interviews of Caid Nottoris or of General Tredge, my analysis of the clanners habit of protesting or protecting the weak - they can now be read directly to your comm set via the newsbot meranews - why not try it!

I had never thought of this possibility, and am indebted to clan leader Akurei for making this possible... I hope that some of you listening out there may enjoy them.

Mera - signing off

OOC

/tell meranews list for articles list
/tell meranews art# 0 to start an article (it then prompts you to move to art# 2 etc)
It can be a bit slow but it does work
Snippets aren't available yet - just the full interviews - for snippets still use the grid feed www.anarchy-meranews.org.uk
