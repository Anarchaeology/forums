Implant and cluster names
Currently the clusters you can insert into implants have names that relate to the skill they add points to. An example is clusters for boosting the Bow skill, which is named "NanoCluster of Manual Marksmanship".

This is confusing to many players, and creates unnecessary work on figuring out which cluster boosts what skill. Thus it's been suggested we simply change all clusters names to reflect the exact skill. In the example above, the cluster would change name to "NanoCluster of Bow" or "NanoCluster of Bow skill", or something similar.

Good idea? Bad idea? Let us know.
