Okay, is it me or does shadowlands sound like a really stupid upgrade to Anarchy online?

When AO was released it was one server and all citizens were in that one world. But the program was still so shoddy that it lagged to heck and so we were all split up. Of course after a few months the coding was fixed and most of the lag issues were solved, but it was not aplicable to unite rubi-ka 1 and 2. And now that people have left our planet it is quite quiet. And the chances of uniting both rubi 1 and 2 are slim to nill due to tech issues.
