Question: Why don't Shades have Token Boards?

1) Shade is a born native to Shadowlands. Keeper is not. This combined with their affinity for 'magical' tattoos, and very very limited use of other armor and other Rubi-ka creations is one reasoning that Token Boards don't fit with the character's story.

Funcom on Shade origin from The Shade
quote:
The Shade comes from the lowest pits in the Shadowlands, where they were constructed by the first explorers so they could specialize in getting around in the darker, deadlier places of the realm.


Funcom on Keeper origin
quote:
...they [Keepers] are seen as the front-line fighters that have been directly recruited by the Redeemed and Unredeemed to fight in the Shadowlands.


2) Even though the token boards are described as special natural articles (Clan) or merit badges/ribbons, there is a deeper nanotech component that makes them able to augment your health, computer literacy, experience gaining potential and so forth. This is incompatible with non-natives of Rubi-ka. (The Shade)

3) Since the Shade was native-born to Shadowlands and starts as Neutral an argument goes that Omni and Clan do not recognize them in the same way for 'working' for the Clan or Omni-tek cause.

Out of character (OOC) argument: It's not fair Shades miss out on boards for extra health, nano programming, comp lit, experience % etc...

Shades do have it rough with Computer Literacy (expensive skill, no spirits, no special nanos to raise it).

Q: Is this harmful to the Shade character that we can't get into grid as early?

A: Grid is not a Shadowlands construction, so probably not.

Q: Is this harmful to the Shade character that we lack health and Nano from boards?

A1: Spirits do a good job offsetting HP (I demonstrated that in another thread), and some of their armor and tattoos have both Nano and Health bonues.

A2: It is by design that they have low hp.
quote:
The Shade will be a tough character to solo due to his limited hit points.


A3: If any of you want to run tests in which you buff up a shade with decent spirits and armor/tattoos and they show that the belt and ncu you get into is insufficient with had data, then I would definitely encourage you to present that report here for the designers to consider.
