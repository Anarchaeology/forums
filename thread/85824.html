Notum mining and the environment
What is the true impact of large scale notum mining on the environment? I don't know either, but its something that worries me greatly. My organization is very concerned about environmental issues, but we also mine notum. A conflict? Perhaps. But we are taking steps to address this problem.

Unity of the Rose will be begining a study on the environmental impact of notum mining on the environment. This will be a long term study, and we do not plan to rush to conclusions before we have collected lots of data. We are seeking Clan/Omni/Neutral partners to participate in the study .. especially Omni. You must fulfill the following requirements:
- Must have a notum mine to use in the study. Geographical location is important, thus our need to have Omni participation.
- Mines used in the study should be well defended. Loss of a mine will be detremental to our study.
- Must have some level of commitment to this environmental study. Withdrawing from the study after it has begun will be detremental to our study.

If you can assist in the design, manufacture, or maintenence of our environmental monitoring equipment, that would also help.

Contact Szentasha or Syntasha in game or send an email to szentasha@darksphere.com.
