Parry will continue to annoy you throughout your career. But, against green mobs you shouldn't be seeing them very much...

The way misses and parries work, is that they check your attack rating against your the targets evades. So, if you are wielding Sol F's, get your 1HE as high as you can. Upgrade your implants, cast the best buffs you can and be sure that you max 1HE every level, the higher the better.
