Speech/Aura spam
In their current form, Crat Speech and Engie Aura nanoprograms 'pulse' every 20 seconds.

While this sounds good in theory, in reality their implementation leaves a lot to be desired. Not only are they very spammy, they create extra screen and chat lag.

Every 20 seconds you get the following 3 chat messages.

Nanoprogram executed successfully.
Executing program XXXXX.
XXXXX will terminate in 10 seconds.

I have a number of suggestions to make these programs a lot less annoying to all players.

1) Set the duration of the programs to 31 seconds and keep the pulse at 20 seconds. That way, you will cut out 1/3 of the messages the caster will see and 1/2 of the messages other players will see.

2) Set the duration to 71 seconds and the pulse to 60 seconds. Even less spam and lag for all.

3) have an option to turn off speech/aura messages.


Please add any other suggestions you might have regarding these nanos.
