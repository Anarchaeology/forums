Thanks for the booster
Thanks for the booster, Funcom. It's something we've long waited for (land Control). It will help us feel like we have an impact on Rubi-Ka! Not everyone (like some of the flaming posts below) hate the idea of a booster. It makes sense if there are large enough programming changes to require very large downloads to create a booster. I'm glad you are not having us wait til the expansion to add the ability to control land.

If anyone is so up in arms about paying for the booster, the best part is YOU DON'T HAVE TO. It will not be required to play the game. It will just add more options for those of us that do. From what I gather in the News article.

I hope you keep making the game better with each patch, booster, and expansion. I have talked to many people in game that are very much excited about the booster. So I am beginning to think that people only post on these message boards to complain. So I wanted to post and thank you for adding content that we have asked for.
