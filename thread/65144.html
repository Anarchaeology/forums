Clicksaver Integration
My two cents on this is that Funcom ought to buy Clicksaver from the guy/gal that made it and integrate it into the mission selection screen. Odds are, from what I've read about Funcom's position on things, and I could be wrong, is that they aren't too thrilled about probably 50% or more of the population of Rubi Ka using Clicksaver. Like I said, I could be wrong. The fact is, the mission terminal ought to be revamped so that it integrates the basics of Clicksaver so that you don't have to shift click a mission to see the reward, and you don't have to pull up a third party program to easily see what you're getting or shop for an item(s).
