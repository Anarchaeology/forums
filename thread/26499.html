Thx Funcom for a great event!
First of all, i would like to thank Funcom and the Ark´s arranging this. A great deal of the population of RK2 needed this. And feel that it was something exciting going on, too bad that not much people could participate.


This is a report of what took place in an abandoned base in the Perpetual wastelands desert.
Event started about 21:00 CET (around that)

I´m just back from the front.
And i will try report what happend to all of you that missed it.

It started of in Tir grid, where an ark where looking for recruits, he choosed around 20 ppl. Everyone where then "shipped" to a base in Perpetual wastelands. After an initial briefing at the base, we were orderd to secure the area, look for a possible omni´s in the area. When the area was conciderd secured, we waited.

There were som quiet moments, just hearing the wind blow until we got our first contact with the enemy. We backed up against the main gate, and had cower fire from some MK1 cannons.
There were hoards of Vet´s and some Ace´s storming the north gate. We took some heavy casualties, loosing many great men and wommen.

But we held them off.

After the fights were wearing off, and the omni lines were falterinng, we decleared this a great victory to the clan.

This happines was shortlived.
Omni were bringing in massive reinforcements, we got surprise attacked by some high lvl omni´s. And PvP combat started. Team PvP. I dont know when i last had som much fun. The fightings were short and we finaly drove them off.

LONG LIVE THE CLANS

Back to reality.

This is AO at it´s best!
The ARK roleplayed magnificaly, and so did the clanners that were there.

They balanced this event up so great, first there were some "usual" mob killing (lotsa killing). And after that, it came real player Omnis, that started attacking us.

While this all is this taking place, the ark gives us regular reports what´s happening around, reports like "Reinforcemts have been sent for, and are inbound" This gives a great RP feeling.

I realy hope that the bad days are over, and we can see more events aranged in a way like this.


Thx goes to the great team i were in aswell.

Cosmosis
and me

(yes we were 7 :P)

Thoriak.....Signing off.!!!
