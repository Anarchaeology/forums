The problem with the 50% heals is that advents and ma's will get screwed.

Perhaps there could be a max % of health that could be healed at one time...like there is a max amount of damage that can be inflicted at one time (ie. no 1 shot kills anymore). that way, a doctor will still be able to heal a lot...there will just be a cap, and the mid-heal/mid-damage classes (like ma's and advents) will not be affected at much, or at all.

Simply putting a 50% limit on healing really takes advents out of any chance to pvp (if we had any to begin with). It is a poorly thought out attempt at a solution that only creates more problems - the very reason that it will probably be implemented, it's a funcom "solution."
