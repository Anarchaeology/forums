Using a gun and a melee weapon...simultaneously.
I've just discovered that I can dualwield a gun (pistol) with a one-hand blunt or blade.
And during my discovery a couple of questions came to mind;

1. Are we actually supposed to be able to do this?

Pistols and one hand melee weapons suddenly show the dual requirements for the "opposite" type of weapon. A 1hEdg blade now has a statment with "multi ranged = 0" in it's wear requirements.

2. Brawl and Dimach doesn't work in these combinations. Is this on purpose?

Fling shot, aimed shot, burst, fast attack and sneak attack does work however. You get the effects of both the melee and pistol special attacks. (I didn't find a gun with full auto for my experiment, but I presume it works too). Only the "Body" attacks doesn't take effect. They are executed (brawl & dimach show up above my character, and I get the brawl recycle), but they have no effect. No damage. Don't know if this is the case of Parry too...

Oh, and my experiment was ofcourse done as an adventurer. One gun and one sword.
