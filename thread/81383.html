1. Lagg
2. Lagg with Loss of people in your group even though they could be standing right next to you and screaming for a heal
3. Lagg with mob beating on you but you just stand there knowing that when the server finally DOES catch up you will be dead.

Here is what I expect from a release like Notum Wars .. In context FIRST DO NO HARM. When you seek to add thousands upon thousands of items to the server that need to be monitored and upgraded and defended or destroyed you have added those items at the expense of lag. Why lag ? More items to update to your client not replacement of items addition to items. I dont care if all the notum in the universe if sucked out of that planet and taken away as long as half my team that im responsible for healing doesnt disapear in the next minute and die right next to me while screaming for a heal.

Its not my connection or system .. Im on a T-1 , with a very beefy pc.

The worst item of all is that now to maintain server up your just going to pop people all over the planet to reduce the server load at any given point. I dont care what you say this smacks of heavy handed treatment that Asherons Call used to keep their precious no zone world running. Sorry that didnt work either.

Suggestions:because its not fair to complain with out trying to help acheive a workable solution.

1. Let Only the guild that puts up the tower be able to see it with the exception of guilds of the same level of clan origin.
So if you really want to spend your well earned credits for a big tower go right ahead.

2. Start Immediately to build additional servers for just missions that handle only missions and have enough memory and power to run them efficiently.
