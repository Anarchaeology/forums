Alright, found an engie who could actually build the Aban bracelet last night.

Requirements from Aban (middle level):

1) blue print for wrist thing
2) mold (left or right) for wrist thing

Requirements that can/or MUST be met in RK:
3) silver (must be RK silver till FC fixes SL ore 'unmeltability' snafu), preferably ql 100 or higher, 2 units
4) personal furnace to melt the silver, 2, can be bought for a pittance in either Jobe (Aban) or RK shops,
5) robot parts (preferably 150 or better), 2 of them (must be RK, far as I know)
6) spool of nano wires, 2, can be bought for pittance in either RK or Aban/jobe, preferably ql 100 (this the ql determinant?)
7) the Arbiter Gem itself.

Each gem yields certain mods that enhance certain abilities that are associated with profs but not limited to them, (Silent Killer gives a poison mod intended for Agents, one would presume, but can be worn by anyone)... some gems yield health/nano boosts, etc.

Neither I nor the engie I was working with could determine WHAT was the QL determining component. Her prior bracelets were ql 60, one's she made with me were ql 100, I was using 200ish robot parts and silver, the Arbiter gems display no apparent ql.
