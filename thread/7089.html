k I designed a sort of algorithm for a type of 'interactive' dungeon.
you most definitely cannot read my writing, especially in crappy low quality jpeg, but here:
http://clang33k.tripod.com/dungeon1.jpg
just in case you need a visual

ok, the main premise is, the entire thing goes along by the players actions - in order to advance, the player must use his psychology skill to convince someone to do something, or whatever. Also, according to what skills the player uses to get to the objective, that's what prizes he is awarded with - with the example I used, there are 3 or 4 possibilities - the person finds the dungeon, and he has enough psychology skill to trick the feller into opening the door. bam, he's on the track towards getting psychology skill things. He doesn't have any psychology skill, but the guy says 'you better not try to hack my computer' so he knows about computers ( a fixer) and hacks it. boom he's on the track to getting smg's, grid equipment, whatever. The player is a dumb atrox, he blows up the door, boom he's on the track to getting atrox-related equipment. You get my drift? you see what i'm sayin? capish?
