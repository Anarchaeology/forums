Need Something more Potent
Mr. Bean,

A non aggression pact is not needed, there is already one in place, as you point out there is "The Tir Accord". This clearly spells out who has authority where and what the limits are to who can do what. In his most recent statement dated today, Chairman Ross urged Omni-Tek to fully obey the Tir Accord, ensuring that Tir will not be attacked by Omni. Most of us will obey that, a few radicals will not [and at this point I do not even need to name names]. It is unfortunate that Omni and the CoT are both unwilling to punish violators of the accord, but we can work around that.

So the real question or proposal should be, what can we do beyond what the Tir Accord specifies. Neither of our respective authorities [Omni-Tek, Council of Truth] have forebade ties between each other or independant faction agreements.

One such agreement previously forged was the "Athens Accords". Damn shame it fell apart, but still that was more of a "peaceful negotiation" deal, not so much what is needed to meet a threat to planetwide security.

Effectively what we need is a full blown "Mutual Defense Pact", this is the step that the Athens Accord technically did not take, though many of the sponsoring guilds on both sides did as gestures of good faith.

Some of the things needed to make this work? The most
important part is the sharing of important intelligence information.

1) Report Duster Troop activity to all participating faction officers
2) Routine air patrols of all zones
3) Occasional spot inspections of bases and camps suspected
of harboring duster activity.
4) Border patrols to monitor the "Outzone" regions
5) Formation of a "Rapid Response Unit" of joint clan/omni/neutral
troops to neutralize active duster threats when reported
on neutral ground. [Since city fighting would be very
difficult due to overly aggressive and noisy guards].
6) Political Arm to influence the administration of Omni and
CoT to allow us to directly patrol the OutZone, whether they
think there is anything there or not. Omni admits it cannot
visually see whats going on out there and can only say
their few facilities out there are secure. This is where the
Brigade hid before, why not now? Not to mention that
Sol Bank was caught red handed minining Notum out there!
*They* must have had their own facilities, and who knows
who else?? Also in the spirit of mutual defense we need to
reduce the aggression of the guards so they do not attack
on sight. Once that is accomplished then it would be
expected that participating guilds will come to whatever
city is in trouble no matter what the alignment.

When 1 city is attacked, all cities and zones should go on to a hightened alert state since many times they stike several targets at once. So it is critical to make sure all participating guilds are aware of an in progress attack to prepare the defense of their own locations.

An effort needs to be made to track Duster Dropships. In the last few attacks, Dropships were seen and identified. They need to be followed if at all possible, this may reveal their power centers and give us a hint to whether or not they are in the Outzone, in a sub-orbital position or both.

Periodic meetings need to be held by the participants to share information, strategy and data needed to supplement or enhance our defensive postures and eventually form a proactive offensive front against the Brigade and any other force threatening us.

It should also be acknowledged that there exist subversives in both sides and there will likely be clashes between those groups. While it is certainly possible that these radical groups may have direct or indirect ties to the Dust Brigade, in the interest of progress these individuals whom represent a small minority must be disregarded in the interest of the greater good. Let them fight amoungst themselves, this should not sway us from the mission of protecting the planet, we cant change human nature overnight.
