Hello there Axyrn!
Perhaps a differnt approach would make you change your mind.
Here is what I do (short version):

I never set the mezz pet to guard me from the start of a team hunt... I only set it on guard as soon as it is needed - when someone else is taking rapid damage. First I press the "mob debuff" macro button enough to get the aggro on me, then I press my "mezzpet /guard" macrobutton and press the "amina pet buff" button a couple of times and that is usually more than enough to make the mob stop kicking my m8's hiney - and mine included. As soon as the mob has selected the the tank again, I press my "mess pet /behind" macrobutton and everything is nice and dandy again. So you see, the third pet has its ups! Actually I think you have to re-evaluate the way you think of its concept. The way I use it, I dont demand that it should freeze the mob for a long period of time. I only demand it to wipe the hate list so I can make sure that my pet gets on top of it. When it stops doing that, I know I have to get a better mezzpet.

So my advice to you is that you dont put your pet on /guard right from the very start of a fight. Use it wisely and in a manner that makes you in controll of the situation. If that means that you wont use it at all, then that is an individual decision and perhaps a wise one at that too.

Oh... and I found that "/messpet attack" doesnt work. It only does its thing when you put it on /guard. You know when its done its thing by the fancy lights around the mob and you can also see what it is doing by "/pet report".

Sincerely,

A Quick Test
Here's what I did last night ...

I went into a 50% mission and killed the group in the first room. Then, I went looking for green mobs. When I found one, I terminated my pets.

I stepped into the room and was quickly aggroed by the mob. I counted to five, and ran out of the room. Of course, it followed me. I nuked it and hit it, nuke, hit ... eventually it goes down.

Now, I cast my (ql17?) Greater Distracting Sphere (at 22 last night, she was still green) and set her to /guard. I find another green mob, and do the same thing as before.

This time, though, the mob doesn't follow me out of the room. It was 30 seconds before the mob was free to follow or attack.

I tested that a few times, and had similar results (10 secs. was more usual, but it went as high as 30). Then I tested the results if I break the mez. As I expected, as soon as I ATTEMPT to nuke or thwack the mezzed mob (it doesn't matter if I hit, I just have to swing) the mob is free to follow and attack.

Obviously, this doesn't work as well when ACTUALLY TRYING TO ACCOMPLISH SOMETHING. My attack pet quickly breaks the mez, and I can't figure out a way to get the mezzer to efficiently switch targets. But she helps out in the first room -- she can hold one of the multi-mobs at bay for most of my battle with another.
