people like missions, in particular, team missions
why
1. relativly safe
2. good xp
3. good (boss) loot
4. easy to get to
5. you can chose the ql with a slider instead of looking for another camp

disadvantages are

1. little variance, same mission templates for 200 lvls
2. boss loot is unpredictable

the outdoor/indoor delema can be compared to a set of scales, and right now the mission side vastly outweighs the outdoor side

by adding dyna camps they have added the boss loot to the outdoor scene, but you cant give away things for free. so you add bosses to get the loot of off and a few protector mobs to aggro you and your group, etc.
