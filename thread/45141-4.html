When people first left the backyards to do city missions - everyone jammed up outside the same mission door trying to get in. Tons of people bunched up in a running motion, jumping on top of each other trying to zone into the mission - but not able to.

People getting stuck in the basic shops for hours unable to zone out.

When a fixer snared his or her own char it increased runspeed.

When outdoor hunting groups needed to use tactics. Hugging the zone line. Trying not to get adds. When the NT's ran the mob in a circle while the rest of the group blasted away. When running the mob in a circle was standard practice.

When a team on the way to an outside hunting spot was surprised by a massive mob respawn - then being chased by a gigantic pack of mobs - the team running away in utter panic.

The team trying to regroup and find each other after the above.

When people broadcasted that an event was going on, and everyone rushed to the zone only to be discoed repeatedly, or the zone crashed.

The pvp scene at the bridge near Holes in the Wall before there were twinks and exploits.

Having one of the first Mids, and people in your team were envious of your means of transportation.

Teams getting completely lost in outdoor hunting zones.

When as a clanner I was decked out in omni armor (clan could wear omni armor and clothes), and other clanners complained that it totally did not make any RP sense.

When it took you hours to find and buy the implants that you needed because the shop terminals were so chaotic.
