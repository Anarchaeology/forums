Thoughts on new soldier weapon:

1. The current weapon used by the vast majority of soldiers as thier primary weapon is the nova flow. If I were designing a new weapon id look at the nova flow and see what improvements I can make ( as well as lok at the feedback from the community that is intended to use the newly designed weapons)

a. Nova flow does fling as well as burst. If you read the forums youll see that most soldiers agree that fling dmg is better than burst. The new weapon has burst only as the only special. This in not an improvement. Its going the other direction.

b. Nova switches out fairly fast. The "proposed" new weapon has a 8 sec equip time which means this weapon is not suited for switching regardles whether its pvp or pvm. As is stands today with only 1 special, soldiers will want to switch it out but thats not viable. Again, this doesnt improve naything, actually makes it allot worse.

c. The dmg is only slightly better than nova with a crit thats quite a bit less. The min dmg is still 1. its amazing that after you get to the point you can master assault rifle. RE, etc and equip a ql200 weapon . your still capable of doing a dmg of 1.

d. We have been begging for a weapon that is 100% based on a single skill(assault rifle for example) for attack rating. If you feel the need to leave ranged energy as a requirement, then fine but why the hell cant it based on 1 skill. As a thought you could make the ratio 67/33 go up based on ql. ql100 67/33..ql150 75/25 ql200 100% assault rifle so that the soldier would have something to look forward to. This didnt improve anything.

e. Range is 28m vs 20 m for nova flow. Got this one right, this is an improvement.

Overall as it stands now, the division9 soldier weapon (as proposed) is nothing more than a fixxer weapon that uses assault rifle instead of smg/mg as a base skill and throws in ranged energy as another requirement for good measure. I can only speak for myself but no way in hell do I stop using Nova flow for this weapon based on what is proposed. People can argue wait til its introduced and try it but i have yet to see FC improve any weapon after its introduced in game, only modify in the other direction.

I am an engineer and have been designing buildings for over 15 years. In every design we start the most important thing I do is to sit with my client and get input on what he "needs". Without that i can design something that i know will work, meet code, etc. but if the end result doent meet the client's/customers needs, then i failed. We take time out here to provide FC with feedback on things we know could be better but somehow the feedback doesnt seem to get in the hands of those that design. "No one is as deaf as he who will not listen"
