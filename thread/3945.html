wipe...
funcom should give everyone a free three months, institute a system that works, and start over.

this would be fair.

however...this would also drive away about 90 percent of the player base including myself.

funcom should have had the first three "non-beta" months free with the expectation that the servers would be wiped and started over in conjuction with the story line. then all of these balance issues and other player issues would have been spotted and resolved prior to entering the story. but, as we all know, this wasn't done.

i've found that funcom lacks foresight. it seems to be a company that is pretty good in determining what is wrong and trying to fix it after a large number of people are affected by it...but it isn't good and figuring out what is going to be a problem prior to it being implemented and heading off problems at the pass.

as a result we have balance issues, and mission issues, and equipment issues, and people who have 1000 tokens and ql200 implants due to obvious exploits. we have a large group of people who take advantage of "over equiping" and another large group who seem to be unable to do so. we have entire series of armors and weapons that are mysteriously pulled from the game, and skills that have been changed from blue to green without reimbursing the IPs spent for the now green skills.

you wanted anarchy online...that's what we've got.

we seem to have a conglomeration of software developers and game writers that have a lovely idea...but dont' take the time to think things through prior to throwing something together.

now...i will admit, that the latest patch was very nice. not that many bugs, and certainly nothing drastic. but no matter what funcom does, it is going to be impossible to change the great errors that have already been made without alienating most of the population of rubi-ka.

all of the ideas on making drastic changes may bring balance to the game...but at what cost?

patch 12.6 was supposed to make it so that over equiping would no longer be possible. however, there was such a backlash to that idea that funcom dropped it.

wiping ips or de-equiping everyone would do the same exact thing.

this reminds me of a MUD i used to play called alexmud.

when i first played it there were about 100 players a night...
the implementors tried to balance the game so they "fixed" the professions...and half the player base left.
the implementors tried to balance the game so they "fixed" equipment...and half of the remaining players left.
the implementors tried to make game more stable so they "fixed" the code...and now there are about 7 'players.'

sure...balance is good, but at what cost?

any of these drastic suggestions would do a tremendous amount to bring balance to the game...but i feel that most people would be too frustrated at the thought that all of their hard work has gone down the drain...and they would leave. i probably would.
