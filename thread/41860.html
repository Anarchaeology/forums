Biography of Laticia "Nelida" Kerans-Shone
Advisor of Clan CAS
CAS Ambassador to the Neutrals

I was born Laticia "Nelida" Shone daughter of Stephaq and Delia two of Athen's preeminent traders. I was born in West Athens actually, since both my parents preferred a less hectic life style, but spent much of my time with my parents while they conducted business in Athens, Tir, and Borealis. My mother was Solitus and my father Opifex. That in and of itself raised many eyebrows in their business community but their accomplishments provided our family with a quiet respect.

I have one older sibling; a sister named Zuleica. When I was born Zuleica was already 25 and away from home...not following the footsteps of our parents as a Trader but in the underworld as a Fixer. I saw little of my sister until one very traumatic day when I was eight. That was the day Omni Pol raided our housing complex in West Athen. To this day I do not know what, or who, they were looking for but I begin to suspect it was not my parents but...my sister. My parents must have known and, in an attempt to protect her contact information, the Omni police robots were ordered to kill them. My parents were not even killed by human hands...only by the cold and unfeeling plasteel of a lifeless robot. The Omni intruders left me crying over the dead bodies of my parents. I only found out much later that it was their way of using me as bait for my sister.

None of our neighbors had dared to enter our apartment for fear of the ever-watchful OmniPol. For two days I ate from our food dispenser and stared at the dead bodies of my parents waiting for their resurrection and return. It never came. The insidious OmniPol had hacked the reclaim terminal and blocked the transmission of the data that would have reformed their bodies. To this day I do not know if they still exist in some digital form frozen at that time, or if the data itself was destroyed. One thing that stays with me all these years is finding those that would create and peddle reclaim hacking tools for their own selfish profit. I have some ideas as to the identity of such people and I will find them and deal with them for such an insidious and inhumane act...I consider them as guilty as those that would use such devices.

My sister did come two days later. She, in her strange Fixer way, appeared one early morning and before I could say a word she drugged me and stole away into the early dawn light. For the next nine years Zuleica cared for me in a remote desert outpost in what is today known as the Wailing Wastes. At times she would disappear for many days, and when I was older even weeks at a time. She never spoke of her trips nor did she take me with her. We rarely visited other cities and only infrequently did I ever see West Athen. But my sister had learned more than just being a thief and assassin, she had learned all there was to being a Trader as well. This she taught me. She taught me to follow, not in her footsteps, but in those of our parents whose only crime had been to cover for their first-born child. She taught me how to acquire, equip and use the very best weapons to be found on Rubi?Ka. But more importantly she showed me how to avoid coming under Omni scrutiny...something she apparently had failed to do herself.

One dry hot day at our outpost Zuleica said goodbye to me. Reminiscent of the morning she stole me from my dead parents in West Athen, she drugged me with her words; "Nel", as she handed me a package and envelope, "take this package to Tir. Deliver it as instructed in the envelope." She then hesitated and did something I'd never seen her do...she kissed me on the cheek. As my mouth dropped open she said softly; "and Nel, don't return here. I won't be here and you no longer have a home here." With tears in my eyes I watched as she ran a grid-encoding program and vanished from my sight and from my life. I have not seen her since although infrequent odd rumors find there way to my ears...that she is still alive and dodging the Omni Pol.

When I arrived in Tir I proceeded to follow the delivery instructions. To make a long story short I became the apprentice of a very stern but kind man whose name I shall not mention. He gave me a new identity and taught me Trader ways much more advanced than I'd ever known were possible. But even he, one day, said a short but sincere goodbye. As I sat and gazed at his face... a face that in all prior times had been hidden to me...he too vanished from my life.

I remember many carefree years hunting in the northern wilderness of Rubi'Ka with friends such as Javaschick and Drtiny. Some of these friendships became deep and lasting ones that I still cherish today. I know that these friends will last through any hardships...friendships that can always be mutually counted on in good times and in bad.

At the still tender age of 30 I was recruited into the guild CAS by a kind woman named Sweetkryss. I quickly found CAS to be my new family. They cared for me, taught me and eventually promoted me through the ranks to the position of Advisor. Today I share my life with them...each and every one I consider Family, Commander Redruum is like a father to me and fellow Advisor Vixentrox stood by me at my wedding. One day I discovered a new member of CAS, a young MA named Baiyue, was indeed my half-sister. Here within my adopted family I had found my blood family!

In renewing my friendship with Drtiny, then leader of Neutral guild IR, I became impassioned with the rights and abuses of the Neutral community. I forged friendships with many of the inhabitants of Borealis and members of IR. Realizing my growing connection to the Neutral community and my desire to help them find justice when confronted with the crimes of my fellow Clansmen against them, CAS assigned me the position of Ambassador to the Neutral Community. During this time I met a young MP named Orgela and quickly became her friend and her adopted older sister. We continue a fast friendship, although she has no concept of good money management and continually drives my bankers to frustration and dismay.

At that same time I met the woman that would soon become my wife, Katelin "Cyani" Kerans. Our hearts and souls fell in love instantly, though our minds took just a bit longer to catch up. Cyani and I were married at Tir Falls in front of friends and family, Clan, Omni and Neutral...all momentarily suspended in peace and respect. I am proud to now be called Laticia "Nelida" Kerans-Shone. Cyani has given me a perspective on our lives here on Rubi?Ka that has changed my thinking. She has helped me form my pacifist views. For this enlightenment, and her freely given True Love, I dedicate my life to her.

Today I share the wonders of Rubi'Ka with my wife, family and friends and look for opportunities to find peaceful ways to solve our differences while still supporting the goal of my Clan; Freedom.
