First time I read about Blacksnow was a few months back. Somebody had posted about the company and their exploiting/money making tactics, and gave a link to the AO Exchange site. But all that aside, I was shocked and appalled that these people were selling 1000 tokens, simple as that, for however many US dollars. I was used to seeing credits, but tokens? That really got my blood boiling. It was obvious they had hacked the client and done God knows what else.

I'd never buy credits and items for this or any other game. In fact, I don't even think I'd sell my characters/account. I've been playing since release and before (beta), and though I may be somewhat casual in my approach to the game (just hit 100 with one of my characters, heh!), I still have put a lot of time into AO - socializing, helping others, and learning and exploring in this complex world.

Blacksnow kind of takes the time I spent and throws it in my face, saying "Now if you had the money, you could have gotten ALL your characters to level 100+ with all the money and extras you'd ever need in a quarter of the time! You're a busy individual and not everybody can spend hours playing AO."

But that's just not my style. And I don't take it personally or get really mad at these companies like Blacksnow OR their customers. I figure plenty of people have more money than brains and don't care at all if they're missing the point of building up their character and enjoying a meaningful game experience. Ah well.

Thing that gets me about BS is that they basically lied, cheated and stole and yet they have the nerve to go to court about it? They want their accounts back. And they won't exploit anymore? Suuuure. I believe that.

They're mad because they were making money and now they're not. And I really doubt they're going to build up characters and sell legit credits and such. Takes too much time to be profitable, even if you have 800 monkey slaves powerlevelling for you.

My significant other came up with a good analogy. It's like a felon who gets his gun and license taken away and then asks for his gun back so he can go rob banks. Nobody wants people like that playing/cheating/ruining a game that's supposed to be fun.

They're a bunch of fools and if this ever goes to court, it'll probably be thrown out. At any rate, I think BS is trying to get Funcom for defamation or some such nonsense. Bah!
