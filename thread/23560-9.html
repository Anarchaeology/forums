Also, FC should really think about other morphs for us. I really like the "elemental morph" ideas; I love the idea of turning myself into a living creature of flame and burning my opponents into little piles of ash. Or turning into a lumbering giant made of rock that pounds mobs into the dirt. /me drools

However... these new morphs should be balanced. We're already supah-uber with our pit lizard morph (even if we have to be in the top tier of levels to even consider using it); there's no need to tilt the balance of the game upside-down and have everyone screaming nerf. (I hate nerfs....)

One more thing while I have it on my mind - using weapons while morphed. I heard somebody talking about morphing into a wolf/sabretooth while armed with pistols (or some such stuff). WTF is that? These morphs should boost our MA skills and allow us to fight unarmed (claw, bite, rake, headbutt, etc.). You don't see mobs whipping out Rider Execs to kill us...

All in all, I like what I see... except for that annoying little "self-only" tag on the buffs and damage shields. So far, all we really have to offer teams is our vehicles and morphs (plus heals if there's no Doc around). We need more.
