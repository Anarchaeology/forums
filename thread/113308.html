/me hopes Ragnar is back with Anarchy Online. He's one of the people on the the team I truly respected for both his creativity and vision. I can't say that for certain other senior members of the design team. It was a real sad day when I read in his journal that a.) there would be no sequal to Prophet and b.) he was off the AO team.

I really miss his posts in these forums and his successor has done little to inspire confidence that the Storyline will move forward either in-game or out. I forget who his successor's name is (isn't that a sign that he's not as effective as the 'original'). He contacted some people I know and was asking like 'what do you think we should do next?'

Well, duh. Aren't you supposed to be the creative one? How the hell can you entertain us, if we're telling you the Story?

/me passes by and talks to the two NPC's in Omni-Trade and wonders why more people aren't freaked out by the apparent coupe attempt by Eva Pouiros (or however you spell her last name). /me is saddened even more that this whole plotline with Ms. Eva has been stagnating for coming up on a year.

I guess that was part of Ragnar's story and the new guy either don't know about it or just doesn't care.
