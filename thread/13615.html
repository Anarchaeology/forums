Bottom Line:
All skills are capped at 600 base. That's it. 600 base.

Now, that MatCre Skill may go up to 750 with high int, but it's still 600 base. You may get your CompLit with a sweet token board, buffs, and high int to 1100, but you're still capped at 600 base.

It does not matter if your initial skill cost is 5 (green), 20 (DkBlue), or 50000(funky rainbow colored) - you will be capped at 600 base skill. So, green skills allow you to get to the cap faster with less overall IP invested, but in the long run all skills develop to the same max (again, 600 base).

So most skills will allow for around modified 1000+ or so (600 base, +150ish from maxed stats, 200ish from implants, buffs, etc).

If anyone wishes to challenge this, please feel free, but provide OFFICIAL information (no guesses or assumptions based upon what your friend thinks - I've played since Beta, you?). This information comes from the Prima Strategy Guide, btw (well, paraphrased).
