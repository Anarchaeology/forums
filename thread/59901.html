Trader report - the followup (what happened really?)
As promissed I would take a look at the original trader-report and post a followup.

So here it is.

Original questions in bold.
Funcom's answer in itallic.
My comment in red.
________

Bugs
- Profession specific bugs


Guard Convoy lasts 10seconds
This is a known bug and is being looked at. I can't give you an estimate of when it will be fixed, but the developers do know about it.

Problem is fixed.



Charms totally broken since 14.2
We are reevaluating this entire line of nanos. They will be fixed in a future patch. Remember though that Trader charms are never supposed to work as well as the Bureaucrat charms.

Well, they work, but they are as useless as they were the day they were added to the game. They need to be reevaluated which never happened.


All AC-drains and buffs has wrong numbers in the description on the crystals. When they are uploaded they are correct
This was fixed in the 14.4 patch.
Indeed it was.


Some of the trader-nanos upload wrong one (Sorry havn't been able to get the exact name as I already have all the nanos uploaded, but I think it was Lossy Delayed Health Payment and Lossy Health Funnel. At least they had same firstname.)

We are aware that four nanos are effected by this. The Nano Crystals Lossy DHP, Lossy Health Freeloader, Lossy Health Plunder, and Lossy Health Funnel, when uploaded become the nano formulas Average DHP, Average Health Freeloader, etc... This is not a severe issue, but will eventually be resolved in a future patch.

Havn't checked, but I doubt it was fixed.



- Bugs that affect all professions (or a number of professions)


Mobs still hit for the same and cast same nanos when they are debuffed by a trader.

It was never our intention that skill drains would have an effect on the MOBs. These nanos are meant to counter the effects of wrangeling your team. We may look at the possibility of having the drains effect MOBs in the future.

Sounds a bit strange to me really, why even have the debuff on them if they weren't intended to debuff people? And how come NPCs have that effect on you?

Mobs has major nanopools, so a nanopool drain has no effect whatsoever on them.

Trader nanopool drains are not meant to shutdown MOBs from being able to cast nanos. They are meant to help the Trader recover from the large amounts of nanopool their nano formulas require. We are not looking into lowering the nanopools of MOBs.

Fair enough


Balance
- Profession specific balance issues


Health Funnels/plunders are badly scaled. Great until lvl 10, horrible after that. These are the ones we are going to rely on to heal ourselves.
We can't rely on a heal that gets frequently fumbled and resisted.

We feel that Trader health funnels are propperly scaled at this time, but may look at this in the future. These nanos are meant to only recover small amounts of HP, and we do not want them to become high powered nukes or a way to counter the DHP DOT. We may look at lowering their recharge time and nano pool cost in the future.

Nothing happened to these, and they do scale badly. Or actually - they are just generally bad. If the recharge and nanocost were lowered they would probably have a use

Nanopool drains way too slow and way too little. They cost 1/3 of what they will ever give you.
And it takes more than 1 minute for trader to gain the drained nano.
A way to get these balanced is to scale them to work on the same principle as health plunders, i.e.: for a small amount of nano (and a posible credit expense), a trader can plunder a targets nano in one single pull. Something like "Forced Bankruptcy" would changed to something like: (nano-description stripped)

Interesting idea. I have passed this along to the developers who will be looking at it. I can't promise anything other than they will look at it.

I can't tell if the devs looked into this, but nothing changed on those and they are still too slow ticking.

AC are more or less not needed. If you can debuff a mob with -2500AC its great.
But all the others have close to no effect with the way AC works. Same story with our AC buffs. Too many NCU for the little benefit they have. To be able to cast the highest AC buff without leaving yourself on 0AC you need to stack the two highest AC drains, which are needing 110NCU roughly on the trader, only for most of
the team to cancel it because they don't have NCU-space for such a buff.

We are aware the AC drains only effect the damage of a crit against a MOB. This is a known bug that we are looking into. We will be evaluating the NCU cost of some of the Trader nanos, and may be reducing them slightly. I cannot give you any promises though.

Nothing was changed on the AC buffs, they are just as useless as they always were. Too many NCU.

Team Heals in 14.4. They are a big issue and I have done some testing now on test. The lock totally removes the idea in using them. With the dot being 15sec and full damage it is already hurting a lot when you cast it and fast. On my lowbie on test I was team healing about 14hp and taking 25damage in 15sec. Believe it or not but 1 heal wont make any change. If people are about to die, that one heal wont help one bit, other than making sure that the trader will die as the first one. Remove the lock, then traders can be the emergency healers. They still get hurt a lot, but at least they are able to keep a team alive for a short while. With the lock they can't.

We are aware that this change has hit the Trader community hard, and that a lot of people are very upset about it. We are monitoring the situation and deciding what to do. We will not be removing the lock for the DOT. If you need to heal while the lock is in place, you can always cast a different heal.

Turned into a religious discussion, so Im not gonna go into that. Wonder if they are still monitoring the issue.

The trader damage buffs are only luring new traders into spending IPs on matter meta just to realise when they learn more about the game that these are totally useless and wants to reset it. The nano line should be moved to another skill to help the new traders.

We have no plans to move the skills required by any nano formulas. As I mentioned above we are looking into the bugged Trader damage nano Guard Convoy, but have no plans to change any of these nanos.

Well, seems like Cz could see the joke in Relentless Slayer. So it is being reported again. The whole nano-line is a joke in its current state.



- Balance issues that affect all profession (or a number of professions)


There are very little or no difference on casting a ql1 root or a ql200 root. Only the duration is different. It should be easier to resist a ql1 root than a ql200 root. Same thing goes with mezz. The only thing that defines the effectiveness of the root/mezz is the attack-skill of your nanos, then you can use whatever ql of the nanos you want
with same success. Why should e.g. trader or NT use the highest roots when the only difference is that they root for 5minutes other than 1 minute and costs twice the amount of nano to cast? It could maybe be fixed so a certain root
can handle a certain amout of damage before broken or something like that. The higher ql the more damage. I don't know if this is the right way, but some way to make diversity between low ql root/mezz and high ql would be much appriciated.

I did some of my own tests and found that higher level roots do work better than lower roots. Can you tell me which roots you are using on which MOBs where are you seeing this?

Been doing some testing a short while ago. Higher ql roots stick better on players now (against debuffs). And the higher a root the higher the NCU it take on target (not actually taking NCU, but their nanos check against this number) the higher rage the enforcer need. However the highest rage do break all our roots. Even our highest one.

AC has too little effect on higher levels. Engineer's ac shields are close to useless as they are just a NCU-drain and pretty much the same with trader's ac buffs. When AC is getting beyond 3500 AC you can't really see any difference anymore.

ACs are currently working as intended. AC buffs should only be needed to supliment an ACs at lower levels (before you have 3500ACs), or at higher levels to go against special MOBs.

I don't know how engies like that their nanos are only meant at lower levels when they are in fact their main buff line. :P Its a jaded area, I like a lot of AC - but the AC buffs in the game takes up a lot of NCU. Someone should probably take a look at how the balance is AC vs. NCU-cost.

Tradeskills require too high skills. Noone can do ql200 tradeskills without shuffling tradeskillimplants and using
trader's buffs.
And even some of the instruction discs are not able to be made with the current requirements. It would be nice if e.g. a trader could actually do usefull tradeskills without changing implants for each step in the process. This would players could actually avoid being forced into being mules unless they have a doctor around all the time. A lvl 100 tradeskiller should be able to make tradeskill objects for people around that lvl with the buffs he/she has without shuffling implants.
If he/she wants to make higher QL stuff he/she has the option to do the implant-changing. As it is now a lvl 100 trader with maxed tradeskills can make items for a lvl 60, and a lvl 200 trader with maxed skills can make things for a lvl 170.
And thats even with the trader-tradeskillbuffs. A few tradeskills are already balanced like that (Pharmacy and Weapon Smithing).

We are adding in new Trade Skills all the time, as you can see in the last patch when we added Armor making . We also have a lot of trade skill fixes planned for the 14.6 patch. We have no plans to reduce the amount of skills needed for making certain trade skills.

I don't think this is much of an issue anymore as there are added several items adding to tradeskills now. And that pharmacy is easier on skills seems to be countered by the cost of doing them buying the components.

Too many of the weapons made by tradeskills are either broken or horrible designed/balanced. The weapons available through receptables seems to be a lot better. Is this the future way of making weapons through tradeskills or are we going to see
the older ones repaired and beefed up?

All of the weapon recipes will be fixed in the 14.6 patch which I posted about here.

I tried making a mausser and a few other things on test in 14.6 and in fact they worked.

I didn't get that link to work, so I'm not quite sure what was said, and by who, but "all weapon recipes" was not fixed in 14.6. Some might have been, but there are still broken ones out there.
