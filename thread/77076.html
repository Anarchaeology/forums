Whisper's Edge always recruiting
Whisper's Edge is always interested in gaining new members who are interested in RP or storyline activities. WE is a RK1 guild.

Faction: Clan

Government: Department

Description:
Semi-roleplay guild that is very involved in the storyline aspect of the game. Whisper's Edge will use diplomacy, politics, and military force to achieve our goals. Visit us at www.whispers-edge.com

Objective:
To cripple Omni-Tek through subversion, diplomacy, and strikes against military and industrial targets

History:
Whisper's Edge was founded to intelligently fight against Omni-tek with both weapons and words; aid Neutrals in maintaining their independence; and helping all non-criminal Clan citizens survive and thrive on Rubi-ka.
