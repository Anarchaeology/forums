Something funny - FunCom Responsible?
I don't know what sparked this on to a post, but I'm stuck at work and very bored and there doesn't seem to be any threads I feel like adding to so I'll post a story that happen to a group of mine awhile back that I think a GM might be responsible for.


Well, I'm sure you all remember when they nerfed the medusas, and we were all forced underground to doing missions from level 80-200. One night me and a group of my close friends decided that with the sheer mass of the world map in this game, there must be hundreds of hunting spots that haven't even been discivered yet, so we decided to do some scouting. We assembled a group of around level 120-150 and headed to milky way. Our original intent was to see what kind of xp the cyborgs at the tree hut area in milky way gave, and although the scenery was great, and no other people, they were dissappointing, only around 20-30k per kill. So we moved on and did some flying, passed the shipwrecks because of obvious reasons and checked out some neighbouring zones. Before we packed up and headed to Central Artery for the next leg of the scouting we decided to drop on foot and kill some red snakes. After playing with them for awhile we began to work our way around a cliff and found a couple scattered cyborgs which gave much better xp than the ones at the waterfall. This perked our attention and we were having a great time just exploring and killing stuff on the way.

Then it all went terribly wrong.

We were in the middle of a large plain with no monsters anywhere near us, we hadn't even come reomtely close to dying at all in the quest yet, and had a team of 5. The adventurer in the group changed us all into cats for fun while we were waiting for one of the members to use the bathroom and all of a sudden the adventurer (lvl 150ish) just fell over dead from full health, no monsters anywhere. We assumed he terminated for some reason and shouted at him on roger wilco, he was just puzzled saying he fell over with no terminate and no damage messages. Not 5 seconds later our soldier dropped almost simultaneously as our doc, dead to the ground. While we were yelling and being confused, me and the other enforcer suddenly bit the bucket too with no source of dmg or aggro anywhere near us, in fact we hadn't seen a monster in awhile at this point.

We all respawned in Omni Ent confused as hell and trying to figure out what happened. The best we could guess either a SEVERE bug hit us or we waltzed into some unfinished zone that's border instakilled us. Another theory was that a GM was watching us and decided that a new outdoor hunting area wasn't what they wanted us using at that time, much like how they manipulate monster spawns so they can manipulate the average levels of characters. While I doubt there is a conspiracy, I found this incredibly weird and my opinion is it was some unfinished zone border. I dont have the coords as we weren't expecint it to happen, but it was a ways northeast of the cyborg waterfall in milky way on an open plain with a lot of snaked at the entrance.

Just thought I'd post this to kill some time. I'm off in 30 mins, yay

But yes it is a very true story.
