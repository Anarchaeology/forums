14.6 has one good thing for me :P
A long time ago, I bought a g4 nvida video card. While it was great with many of the games on my computer, it tended to pretty much suck with AO. It had an annoying habit of 'lagging' while attempting to initially render a zone. In fact, the performance it showed during the initial rendering of a zone was worse then the generic 16mb card that came with this computer. :P

I don't know if it was something you did, or something I did, but that initial render lag is absolutely gone. Grid in to BS, bam. It's all rendered. Fly over enigma forest, bam, no more render lag. I suffer virtually no render lag anymore.

Like I said, I don't know if this had anything to do with 14.6, but if it did, kudos to you. :P If it didn't, then damn. Whatever fixed that rediculous render lag deserves some rice krispy treats and a hug.
