Boredom - Brought From Soldier Forum
Well im now officially completly bored of the Soldier class and i only made it to lvl 55. So i got thinking about the one thing we lack.."Individuality"

One thing ive always heard people say would be cool is having a method of transport for a team...being that this is the soldier forum you can probably guess where im headed

We have three transport classes so i will keep it simple

Air: Gives soldiers a team "Dropship"

Ground: Anyone fancy a tour round lush hills in my shiny new "APC"

Water: How bought some kind of Amphibious craft..or even a hovercraft type that could be used on both ground and water.

So as to prevent other classes saying these make soldiers too uber the catch would be they can only be used when you are in a team therefor they are of benifit to ALL classes not just the soldier. And what would be the point in having one..well thats easy...All team members get an AC Bonus plus the benifet of a sppeed increase while in the transport.

The only thing that i would demand is that it would be a SOLDIER ONLY transport.

Hell you could even expand on this and traders could have cargo ships which allow extra inventory space.

Engineers can spawn a form of bot which can be used as a veichle.

I will now apoligise for all the spelling errors/ Incostincies and bits ive probably left out.

Just writing these thought shave got me excitied about the possibilities so i would love to hear others opinions.
