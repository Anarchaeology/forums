Issue solved for those that were concerned.

I cross-post the result : the Yalm code is checking your Vehicle Air skill when you zone, without consideration for any modificators (implants, armor parts, nanos). Being in full Dillon's atm, I had a VA skill a few points above what it would be 'naked'.

It does infact take implants, armor and items/nanos into the consideration as long as the items modifies the skill in question DIRECT. (not thru abilities)

I.e if you had one of Cz nanos "Escape from the Forum Trolls", that adds +900 Vehicle Air, you would not be in trouble.

The problem is when implants, armor and items/nanos modifies the main abilities that dribble down into Vehicle Air. These points that come directly from ability modifiers (dribble down), will not be taken into account in the "zone-calculation". This is not fixed in 14.6.
