Wives and AO, the great mystery.
This is ment to be taken lightly (:

Ok so I was reading another post and the person stated that they recently started playing AO again and in (quite to the dismay of my wife).

It made me think of my situation. My wife feels the same way. Even if I only play for an hour i still feel guilty. My wife has been known to say things like "your thinking of AO right now aren't you" when i'm day dreaming and what not. Unfortunately she is correct most of the time. AO is definatly addictive. But i left and came back after 3 months of not playing. I have come to realize that AO is an incredible game, with great depth to it. Its something that i enjoy playing and therefore will continue to play.
So, dealing with it. After being gone for 3 months i have been able to come back and work AO into my life easier then before, and deal with my wifes feelings of abandonment lol (:
Something I found that helps, is if you think about whats going on at home first before you play. Does she have plans? Does she look at you with that look of, i'm waiting to spend time with you? What i have found is that if i think about how much time i can play for before i start playing i won't get into too much trouble, so...
Before starting to play AO i recommend figuring out how much time you have to play. Say you note that she seems needy. Well then try to only play for an hour, i know this is hard but its easy if you know ahead of time. Avoid doing ql90+missions with 200+ guys in them, thats a sure way of geting on your wifes bad side...seeing as they can take freaking 4hours to do lol If you know you have 4 hours then go for it. And unfortunately one of the best ways to spend time with AO, is to stay up late past your wifes bed time. Luckly my wife goes to bed like at 9pm usually so i don't have to stay up too late, usually till 12.
So for me, the key is knowing how much time i can spend on AO before i start. Twinking a charcter or shopping or things like that i can stop anytime if she is calling. But if i'm in a team, i tmakes things difficult.
