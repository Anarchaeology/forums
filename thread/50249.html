Rheena's Story
Janea Rheena Grusebeck came from the neaighboring planet, Deltbuz 5, a small hovering world. After graduating from Voltect
college with a major in computer and information systems, she
left her world to seek work . She took the first shuttle to Rubika.
She heard that were many jobs available there and decided to
take apply around. She took the city bus through Rome Green. The bus driver made an abrupt stop. He waited for the pack of
animals to cross the road. Across the intersection , a sign read
''Blubbag crossing!". ''Anyone want to get off here they can because these Blubbags are going to take their time , '' he said.
So Rheena existed the bus and took a walk around a few blocks.
''Should there be a animal catcher of some sort ,'' she thought.
