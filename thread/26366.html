An honestly good event!
NEW BLOODSPORT GRIPS TIR

A mysterious trader named Murdercity called would be fighters to take bar in a series of cage fights in a Tir Nightclub.

A crowd soon assembled in a Tir Nightclub, and the first of the nights bouts was announced

A Clan Fixer named Trilk took up his offer and was lead inside to the caged pool where the bout was to take place

Once Inside Murdercity took bets from the assembled crowd and introduced the other challenger an Omni Adventurer named Etorphine , the bout started with a flash of stage lights and on a ready, set, go Tir had itself a new bloodsport!

In bout one Trilk despatched her foe to a reclaim in Newland.

Murdercity was good to his word and paid out on his bets, Neriak fumed quietly having foolishly bet on the Omni!

Giving her a chance to win back her loss - Murdercity called for more challengers, with no Omni brave enough to challenge, The President of the De Opresso Libre clan Drsepsis and one of his own Martial Artists called Fanatik voluntered for a bout.

The bout soon raged between these two martial art practising friends, but Drsepsis superior healing powers won out and he was decalred the winner, much to Neriaks relief as she had placed 20k credits on him!

Murdercity let it be known that he would be hosting these bouts regularly and all interested parties should contact him immediatly, whether these bloodsports have been authorised by the CoT has not been established!
