The Devil came to Rubi-Ka(Epic poem by Engelen!)
The Devil came to Rubi-Ka

By Engelen



He came from the east, riding with the pest
Leaving a trail of terror and death
With a sharpened scythe, he came to harvest
until mankind laid gasping for their last breath

The sea turned to blood and the forests died
Men fell dead and he took their soul
Nobody could stop him, they died as they tried
The Devil came to Rubi-Ka with only one goal

The darkness fell over the once lush land
his throne of terror raised from a pile of bone
He sat down and grinned, victory at hand
For he was sure that mankind was gone.

The darkness grew thicker, it covered the ground
The Devil grew stronger as he feasted on men
All around was silence, there wasnt a sound
As the Devil started consuming the world again

The Towns were empty, the fields were dead
The Devil got tired of the work he had done
"There`s not a soul left" the Devil said
He sat down on his throne, fell asleep and then he was gone.

But all of a sudden, In the Devil`s deepest sleep
A couple of brave souls came riding with the grid
They cried in horror and pointed at the Devil`s Keep
And decided to kill him for what he did.

The Bureaucrat gathered the remains of man
He commanded the men til he was out of breath
It was he who made the masterplan
That would end the Devil`s reign of death

The Engineer made the tools so they could strike
He analyzed the situation with a great mind
He equipped every man with shield and spike
And told men to use every tool they could find

The Agent was sent out as the spy
He sneaked behind the Devils defense
He marked the devil, he was going to die
And went back to the team to prepare the offense

The Preperation was complete and the men were ready
The Soldier went up ahead and led the Armed Force
With their weapons loaded, and their aim steady
They had to go, they had no remorse

The Soldier formed a line infront of the Devils gate
The Enforcers behind with their giant hammers of doom
It was now or never, or it would be to late
Mankind would strike at gloom.

ATTACK! Shouted the Bureaucrat and the war had begun
The Enforcers smashed the gate and mowed their way
The Soldiers blasting the devils minions with their loaded gun
And the Fixers filled the fleeing demons with a bulletspray

With a Inner Strength not seen before
The Keepers glowed with a godly light
The Martial Artists killed the giants guarding the door
They both fought for all that was right

"By the hands of my Demon shall the Demons Die"
Said the Meta Physicist and sent his pet
He tore them apart, he made them bleed and cry
The sight was something nobody would forget

The demons screeched with a eerie sound
As the Nano Technicians burnt them with mighty fire
And as they fell down to the scorched ground
They saw The Shades were waiting with great desire

The devil woke up, but he was rooted in his throne
The traders stole his powers and weakened his might
His minions fled, soon he was alone
And victory was soon ahead in the darkest of night.

The devil was outnumbered against the army of men
He roared towards the sky and broke the root
The doctors pointed and shouted "He is trying to run!"
And the lone Agent calmly answered "I`ll stop him with my gun"

The Agent pulled the trigger and whispered "goodbye"
One single bullet, right in his head
Time stopped as his roar filled the sky
The Devil was dead

With a giant cheer mankind had won
They put down their weapons, they lowered their shields
Evil was defeated, the Devil was gone
They dropped their armor and danced on the Lush fields

The Adventurer planted the trees and cured the land
She helped the animals, she worked and strived
She spent her life, more than any man
It was due to her that Rubi-Ka survived

Men and women, hear my warning:
We never know if he is coming back
But If you ever see the sky turn dark one cold morning
Make sure you are prepared to attack!
