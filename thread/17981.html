< 6th IC BureauCon> @4/6/02 22:00GMT Reet's Retreat, Fireplace Room, RK1
Greetings fellow Bureaucrats of Rubi-Ka!

As you may all know recent relations between Omni-Tek and Clans have been better. It is my hopes that a chance for us Bureaucrats to meet one another in a non-hostile environment would help ease the tensions. There are no strings attatched, no hidden agenda and, no fighting.

There will be security present provided by Red Tape. If you feel that your safety is still at risk we will allow up to one bodyguard per guest. I repeat, the purpose of this is purely a social event to help ease tensions betwen the sides and to give bureaucrats from both sides to meet one another. We have more in common most would like to admit.

Dress code will be semi-formal. Dress accordingly. The location and time will be as follows.

Event: 6th BureauCon

Location: Reet's Retreat, Fireplace Room

Date: 4/6/02

Time: 22:00 GMT

Dimension: Rubi-Ka 1

Notes: Dress code is semi formal. Each guest is allowed to bring a maximum of 1 bodyguard. (Droids do not count as a bodyguard.) Security will be provided by Red Tape.


[OOC Note] Please leave the Omni and Clan name calling back at home. We really aren't expecting trouble so just have fun and it'd be a great chance to see what politics on the other side is like.
[/OOC Note]
