Recruitment of The Shadow Guard
The Shadow Guard will be recruiting this Saturday the 14th starting at 2000 GMT until 2400GMT. Soldiers, Adventurers and Enforcers only, all levels are welcome, recruitment will take place in Rome near the whompah's. Any not able to make this timeframe but interested send tells to Coppicemanus or Crowdog.
Information on the Guild and any questions will be gone over at the recruitment. I hope to see many of you there, thank you.

General info on our Guild, Main idea being a organization where members can hunt, mission and coduct Guild events together. For that goal members are asigned units with other members of a teamable range. We also offer a Guild armory and other support functions. Unlike some Guilds this is not just a chat channel, we ask of all members to interact within the org and with other members. We also support role-playing amongst our ranks.
