Allow me please.

  • Grid Armor requires 3 dark blue skills to cast
  • The requirements of Grid Armor are higher than the requirements of similar QL nanos of other professions.
  • The effects of Grid Armor are very level dependant. Grid Armor doesnt have any AC at all, so if an opponent can hit you, the Fixer is in for a very quick death. So if you use a low level Grid Armor against a high level opponent, kiss you a$$ goodbye.
  • Team only fight high level opponents


When you add all that up what you get is that by the time a Fixer can cast Grid Armor naturally it is already out of date. Fixers get around this problem by getting nano skill buffs to allow them to cast the higher level Grid Armors sooner.

So what is the problem you ask? If a fixer can cast it, then why cant he wear it? Well he can, but the problem is that Grid Armor only lasts 2 hours. If you dont have an MP around to buff you, your Armor will disappear and you wont be able to cast another. Fixers have been getting around that problem by storing Grid Armors in the bank (which suspends the 2 hour time limit) and removing them as needed.

With the new requirement checks that wont be possible anymore. Unless a Fixer is teamed with an MP, after 2 hours they will be without armor. Now the Fixer could try to put on normal armor at this point, but unless he can get some more buffs, he wont be able to overequip it like everyone else. So now the Fixer will actually be penalized for using Grid Armor. When you factor in the amount of IP required to cast GA and the difficulty in obtaining the Nano, it is just not worth it anymore. The end result is that the closest thing the Fixer has to an advantage in the game will be removed with the next patch.

All of this is being done to a class that is generally regarded as one of the worst, if not the worst, implemented professions in the game. A class that was scheduled to be fixed last November, but still has a hard time finding a group because they bring very little to a team. Many Fixers see their months of work on their characters being flushed down the toilet and are just a little bit upset by it.
