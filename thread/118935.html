Jypsie's career with Omni-Tek after going through Omni-Reform, and coming out with a new outlook on life , was working as Wilderness Control as part of Omni-Trans. Basicly, it was her job to keep down the population, and keep off the roads, of dangerous beasts in the Omni-Forest, Lush Fields, and Clon**** regions. I got this idea when my first character always died pulling missions to Clon**** by sabertooths. She became an expert of wildlife, half scholarly training, half "Crocodile Dundee" style applied experience. After she joined the Division, she was assigned as a combat medic to support Niner squads, eventually moving on up in the RSGE department as she contiuned her study of mutant wildlife.

That's just one spin on the Omni-Tek Advent. The professions is pretty versitle and allows for folks from really any kind of background.
