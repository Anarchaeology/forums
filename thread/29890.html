Sight the 'Dream Date': The story continues...
Sightsee accompanied by Savoy, approached the small lake where the Healing hands 'Dream Date' auction was to take place. They could only see a few people, but was assured that more would be on their way by Luxurea. Sight and Savoy sat down.
"So any more of our organisation showing up Boss?" He asked.
"Don't know? Maybe later?" Savoy was looking OK, and he suspected that she was here, to make sure he would get a few bids. 'As if I need it, heh', he thought. Something was niggling, lurking at the back of his mind. Tarryk, Rythum, Boco and Shaddowrunrr showed up, and like a passing shadow his memory came flooding back. He opened his eyes wide. Savoy noticed but waited for his response.
"Oh no God damn Leetpiss!" He tried not to shout too loud.
"What is it Sight?"
"A week or so ago I kinda told Tarryk that I'd get more cash then him" Sight shook his head and looked over to where Tarryk was sat. Savoy laughed, at him whilst he slapped himself repeatedly on the fore head. After a short time he plucked up the courage to talk to one of Rubi-Ka's prominent Dj's.
"Errr Hi there Tarryk!"
"Whas up?" He replied. 'Could he have forgotten the bet?' 'Naa it was aired that night for all to hear' Sight pondered.
"Err I was only pullin your leg about getting more cash than you dude hehe", luckily Tarryk's attention was taken elsewhere, so there was no reply.

As the event started and people were being auctioned off, his mind wondered on a journey about a girl, Saalz's friend Lizor. He couldn't understand why her sharp tongue and dislike of him intrigued? He felt as though it was a cover, that maybe she felt some chemistry there? But he probably had ruined any chances after continually putting Saalz down. The truth was quite simple, Sight had never felt so alone as he did now, and had been for Months, but he kept up appearances-he had to, for what really else was there to him? What worried him the most was that he was missing Lizor, but didn't want to face up to that, merely putting it off as just a 'thing'.

"Sold to Fillina for 4 Million creds!" Shouted the auctioneer, Shadowrunrr.
'Fellina?' the name was familiar, and as soon as that thought sprung up in his mind, he realised that Dark Desires were present Lizor too maybe?

As a few more of the men got auctioned off for not much credits, Sight began to feel very nervous and tried to recite what he would do and say when it came to his turn to be auctioned off. He knew he wasn't well known here on Rubi Ka, and that he had lost the ability to talk about things of no import, to socialise. Part of the reason he knew was simply the work load that he had taken on recently, partly because of that girl with fiery red hair and piercing green eyes that is Lizor. The night was drawing on, and Savoy grew very weary and tired from her week off planet with the Navy.
"Boss, its OK you better turn in for the night. Thanks for coming though ". Sight was getting more nervous, and really wanted Savoy to stick around and 'save his skin', should it be needed, but she did look awfully tired. She insisted on staying and did so for a little while before having to turn in for the night. Now he was really going crazy, thoughts of backing out on this auction sprang to mind. He was hoping that Tarryk was a modest man, and not make too much of a big deal about it, should he win, which was looking increasingly likely.

Just when he wasn't prepared, it was his turn to strut and impress. A bid of 300,000 creds was made as an opening bid. But Sight had already forgotten what he was gonna say or do, but it was looking hopeful, until the auctioneer implored with the spectators.
"C'mon! gotta have some more bids!" The damage was already done, and he floundered, almost tripped whilst dancing in time to some horrendous music, (Vanilla Ice). Someone shouted "Yea Ice definitely suits him!"
"Sold! For 300,000 to Lizor!" Announced Shadowrunrr. 'Just one frickin bid!?' thought Sight before the name cut through his mind like a migraine. Lizor was no where to be seen. He heard her voice, distant almost like a whisper floating along the wind. He realised it came from below him and saw her, through nano tech, she had decreased her size to barely 6 inches. That was the last straw of the moment, there standing by that lake. No doubt Lizor had saved him from complete embarrassment, and that was all, why else would she wish to appear nearly invisible to all that were present? All he could do was to follow her as she walked to where her friends and colleagues were seated.
"Err thanks Liz." Was all he could think of saying, to which she replied.
"When and if I want that date, I'll call you later about it" Stern and quiet were her voice (she was still minute in size). He didn't know what to say, but he knew what he was thinking, 'Yea she just saved my skin coz of her buddy Saalz'.
"I have heard disturbing news about you Omni people interfering in the Grid I'm going to Roompa's" She stated, and before she could continue Sight interrupted.
"So you're brushing me off?"
"If I was brushing you off I wouldn't have told you where I was going." She entered her Yalm and shot off into the sky, Sight opened up a channel to her.
"OK guess I'm on my way."

Sight entered Roompa's, he walked with his usual style, but he was in no mood for anything but liquor, it had been another bad day. Now somewhere in here she waited, probably to wind him up, or to shout pro Saalz slogans at his face. He found her and some others of the Dark Desires organisation partying in very skimpy but sexy clothing. Lizor wore a dress that clung closely to her body, and as red as her fiery hair, he could smell what reminded him of some place called 'home' coming from her. 'A contradiction in terms to her mouth when she opened it', he thought to himself.
"OK Liz baby, want something to eat or drink? Luxurea gave us 'Dreams' a whole dinner, to share with our owners?" He smiled, but got none in response.
"No we need to talk about business." She said, Sight mentioned news that Saalz had left the Unity of the Rose.
"I told you he just joined them for that Duck girl, obviously didn't pan out. If you're his friend how come you didn't hear about it?" Sight mused.
"I have seen even less of him then I have you! Shouldn't you be consoling your Brother?" That word cut through his heart, but he managed not to show it to her, the mirror shades twitching was the only sign of it.
" Brother?" He asked. "Liz, we just grew up together OK!" Liz's blood pressure began to rise, Sight braced himself for yet another barrage, why did he even bother with her?
"Lets not go there Sight!" She exclaimed. There was a pause. Sight sighed and scratched his head, wondering if he should start drinking the champagne, or the left overs of the night before's leetpiss.
"OK lets take a seat and talk business." He tried to keep up his stoic expression, and hoped Lizor didn't see the tiredness in his eyes as he removed his shades.

She required him to obtain Omni Nanao tech, so it seemed she really did have no interest in him; well that's what he thought to himself. As she was Clan it would be difficult to obtain it, Sight being Omni would have no problem (after all she did pay 300,000 creds for this service). As for the date that may or may not happen, he was sure it wouldn't. He kept the goodbye simple and quick and left Roompa's, somewhat unwilling to think about anything except getting drunk. He morphed into a Pareet and flew to Baboons to drown out his thoughts with alcohol.

He was in the main dance floor after already downing a bottle of Champagne that Electronix04 had given him at the event. He was feeling the now familiar rush to his head, and he began to smile, Elektricka's tracks were thumping out loud techno. There were quite a few people here, some kind of party by Indoctri-Nation. Sight started to execute a nano that created a shield of flashing blue around the target, and kept on and on doing it to the thanks of some of the 'Babooners' there. He drank a bit more and nearly choked, as he saw Lizor walk passed him with her hair in a ponytail. He followed her, but it wasn't Lizor. Turned out to be someone else, who looked very similar to her? He realised that she might be worried that he was stalking her, and so sat in the back room sofa.

She was about to walk away, when she stopped and turned to look at him, Sight raised a hand in greeting. She smiled warmly and accepted an invitation to sit beside him. They both drank together and Sight found himself getting increasingly drunk. He kept seeing Lizor's face in place of hers looking at him.
"So what do you do?" She asked.
"Well I am an Advisor of the South Rubi-ka Society, we are a charitable organisation"
"How sweet" She beamed a smile to him again.
"But my other jobs, well I do extractions, and other stuff that I really can't talk about hehe." He liked this lady, and found out that she was interested in forming her own organisation, that her name was Lanaea. "Hey maybe when you do you could provide my organisation with services?" He asked whilst eyeing her drunkenly up and down.
"What kind of services do you want?"
"Extractions baby BIG ones!" He smiled at her broadly, she responded by smiling and biting her lip seductively.

He had tried to control himself during the rest of the night, Lizor cropped up in his mind but Lanaea would suddenly kiss him passionately every time. The chemistry was too strong! They'd gone to her apartment, and he tried thinking of ways to get away without disappointing her, but boy was she a HOT lady!

She went to the bedroom to change, and when Sight saw her beautiful figure, he could only think of one thing he wanted.
"OK Errr I-I just wanna get one thing straight baby, you ain't one of those girls looking for anything serious are ya?" It was an awkward question to ask, but he didn't really know what was going through his mind anyway, not since the event.
"Trust me, I'm not." Lanaea's eyes were full of lust, and he couldn't resist.

He left early the next morning, feeling light headed and relieved (in more ways than one). But as he got back to his apartment, Lizor crept up like the slowly rising tide, occasionally Lanaea would splash down upon it. He felt the familiar vacuum within him come back and reached for the scotch whiskey from the fridge.
