Hide and Seek
A blue light washed over her face, the glow of the terminal was the only light in the room. She all but hunched over the small keypad as she typed away.

....processing request.
....
....
....no record on file.

Chewing her lip, her mind raced quickly... she didn't have long to act, the security measures of the computer system she was invading, ahem, researching, were quite tight, and this little hole she dug through was fast closing. Raquel still covers her tracks pretty well, I see. Raquel had been almost as the young girl remembered, and just as sneaky, too. It'd been years, but time had not changed her subject much it seemed. Time never really affected either of them, only people did... A sudden realisation dawned on her, wrenching her from the brief daydream, and she decided to take another path in the system.

....request lookup.
_Malcom Alamexis Age_
....processing.
....lookup successful.
....
Malcom Alamexis Age
Omni-Tek Employee
Former Employment: The Law Society of Rubi-Ka
Current Residency: N/A
DECEASED
....

Damn, she thought. It was worth a try, and who knows, many defected to clan with a bang, but would the system be that truthful with her? There was always a chance that cover ups lay even this deep. Bah. She pulled up her wrist and decided to look over a few notes before continuing, this death confirmation bought her at least a moment or two: an end unexpectedly tied up. With the press of a button or two, a holo-display popped up and a vid clip began to play. She studied it a while, then skipped to the next. Twelve of these clips were stored on her wrist-comm, all taken at opportune times by a hidden source in the areas. They came with a small price, but they were worth it, at least to her.

Pressing another key, she "zoomed in" and rotated the holo-display, turning it slightly. A theme had crept up, in the majority of these vids, her subject was being kept company by the same face. She zoomed in on the new subject and adjusted it to get a good "mug shot". Finally, she tapped in a command sequence, the holo-display glowed a bit as it scanned the section of the clip, then shut down. Her wrist-comm continued to glow with activity, and she put her attention back to the terminal. Soon, her comm unit would alert her to the results, but until then, she'd work on securing that tiny hole she ripped for herself in this particular system.

Chewing her lip again, the young woman attempted to re-sequence her entry, and try to buy a bit of time. It wasn't looking very good, however, as the system began to shut off her access slowly, still. One by one, libraries of information were going back to LOCKED status and her access was dying. In a few moments, she'd be back to citizen access status and barely able to get a weather report, if that. Her reasoning for opening such a deep tear was her uncertainty at this point. She wasn't necessarily a detective, and the info she had to go on was thin, but aquiring information was simple.. if she knew what she was looking for.

Her wrist-comm beeped softly and she quickly opened the display. Staring back at her was the face of a man, the one scanned only a moment before from the vid-clip. His basic, public information was listed with the mug shot along with standard access numbers for various purposes. Her hands floated back to the terminal with renewed interest.

....request lookup.
_Brendan Braedon Demesa_
....processing.
....lookup successful.
....
Brendan Braedon Demesa
Clan Citizen
Employment: President of Valor Eternal*
Residency: Unknown
....

....this channel has been closed.
....connection terminated.

Omni-Tek Protects. Have a Nice Day!

After tugging lightly at her boots, the young opifex sat up with smile. She straightened out her tight-fitting shirt then ran her fingers through her hair: a black bob with a stylish, shocking peach hue to it. Uncertain, she pulled a comb from the drawer and sunk it into her hair, determined to style it perfectly.

"Ugh", she groaned, obviously not having much success. All the training and assignments she'd been doing for Omni-Tek lately seemed to have drained her, not only mentally, but it seemed to have even made her looks want to go on vacation. She gave up, set down the comb, and spritzed her hair a few times. Oh well, nothing was perfect huh; not even in the land of everlasting life. With a sigh, she lounged back against the chair and lazily pressed a key on her desk terminal. A new screen appeared, replacing the mirror with an information window, listing the current time, some kind of "To-Do" list, as well as a map in the corner. She bit her lip, studying the terminal display, then finally shut it off.

Drumming her fingers across the desk, she sank into thought, wondering if her choice was a good one. If she approached say, Raquel's co-workeres, she'd be given away, suspected, perhaps investigated. Unwanted attention was the last thing she needed right now, which was partially why she had had the films taken for her, instead of spying on her subject herself. If what she had heard was true, caution and patience was necessary. So far, it hadn't been a problem... but it wasn't exactly easy.

Yes, this seemed to be the best course of action: approach what seemed to be a good friend of Raquel's at a very popular party. Less trouble would come of it, especially if she worked it right. Botching this now would be horrible, and possibly throw months of planning out the window. Tonight would be a most interesting evening indeed.

The opifex reached into the drawer once more and pulled out a few objects, some sleek and device-like, and slipped them in her small pants pockets. She stood up, stretched a bit and smoothed out her shirt once more before heading to the apartment hallway. Once in the "foyer", she stopped at a small compartment in the wall and entered in a keycode on the keypad nearby. The compartment opened up with a quiet "swoosh", and she reached in to pull out a small packet. The door to the alcove shut back and formed into the wall almost seemlessly again. Gently, she opened the packet and glanced inside. All seemed in order as she slipped the packet in a side pocket on her pants. After a few taps on her wrist-comm, she was out the door of the apartment and on a different kind of mission.

"She seems... worried, about something.. and I have a feeling she's going to tell me," she furrowed her brow slightly, "she seems annoyed to be down South in Omni-Lands."

"Oh? Annoyed how?", Braedon queried, still guarding her hand in his, keeping her anchored to the discussion.

"She was always neutral, like my family.. is.. She went down there to find me, I'm sure of it, and order isn't really her thing. Corporations? Bah."

Braedon agreed with Raquel and stated that she didn't seem very "corporate" to him only one week ago when he had first met Vashti. The agent chuckled.

"No, no she's not... she may play the ruse if she has to, but otherwise she's wherever she wants to be at the time. However, she said it was 'a long story', their deaths, so there's a lot more to come I know.", she rambled on... She was too late...

"So about this danger, you mean there's nothing else you know yet?"

She shook her head at him. "I haven't contacted her yet. My choices are strange... trust her, and contact her, or look into it myself." Braedon's face turned to one of slight confusion at this statement, and the young woman clarified. "We were so close, but it seems as though enemies are everywhere these days."

"Do you feel as though you might not be able to trust her?", he understood now, and she merely smiled.

"I find it hard to trust anyone sometimes, but maybe I just don't want to let myself trust her... perhaps it's corporate conditioning."

"Didn't they condition you to not trust a Clanner with this sort of thing, too?", he smirked slyly. Raquel narrowed her eyes at him, ashamed she led herself into such a corner. She let out a "harumph" and grinned.

"Of course, I will bite her bait. Curiosity dictates it, whether or not I trust her. I have no choice."

"So you will contact her then?", he asked.

"Yes."

"And you'll tell me what you find out, yes?", he smiled again.

"Mm hmmm..."

"One thing...," he looked thoughtfully at her, "Vashti said whatever this was about would make you very unhappy... and I'm only warning you because you're my friend, and you might need to steel yourself for what she has to say."

"It certainly isn't shaping up to be pleasant."

"Well, whatever it is we'll deal with it.", he stated matter of factly.

How strange it was for this clanner to be such a... help to her. He should curse her name, and she his, for being what they were... but instead, they seemed to concentrate on who they were. Perhaps that wasn't such a bad thing.
