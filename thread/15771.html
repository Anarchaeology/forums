Looking for loyal RK2 Omni Employees
Division 3: Omni Reclamation (On RK2) is recruiting for all professions, but especially interested in looking for the following as we are weak on numbers in these professions (although we do have at least 2-3 of each profession and a total of 60 people):

Currently looking for docs of any level to fill out Medical Services within the division.

We are also looking for traders and fixers of any level to fill out Trade Services.

Finally, we are looking for NTs of any level to fill out several different departments including Special Forces and Research & Development.

All other professions are welcome to apply and you will be allowed to petition for what department you wish to enter. All department descriptions, as well as rules and regs can be found at the website under Division 3.

If interested, go to http://www.projectrevenant.com and fill an application out in the forums, under the Academy forum. Academy Class Bravo is scheduled to begin training on Sat., Mar. 16. After that, we will be accepting applications for Academy Class Charlie.
