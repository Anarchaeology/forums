Omni-Mining Contract with Elite Operations
Greetings!!

I have the pleasure of announcing Omni-Minings newest contract with our Neutral friends Elite Operations. Omni-Mining will be conducting a study into the feasibility of constructing a new building, or converting an existing building, into a public arena for Borealis. Elite Operations wishes to provide new recreational activities in Borealis and simply add something more to their community.

Asterik of Elite Operations came to Omni-Mining, proposing a project of converting an existing building into a public arena. Ideally the arena will be indoors; therefore conversion is a feasible option. The goals of the study are simple. First Omni-Mining will determine if the project can be done at all. Second a cost projection will be provided if the project is viable.

Omni-Mining has the corporate resources needed to conduct a study like this one. With our knowledge of notum and excavation Omni-Mining can provide the services at a minimal cost.

A press conference will be held with Elite Operations and Omni-Mining. The location, date, and time will be announced. Omni-Mining endeavors to further relations in the Neutral community. Questions about this project should be referred to Asterik of Elite Operations, Swiftmind the Director of Omni-Mining or myself.

Thank you for your time!
