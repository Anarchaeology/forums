Another suggestion for apartments
First of all, I would much prefer to see the changes that the rest of you have mentioned than what I am going to suggest. However, I think it might be easier to implement and it should require relatively little resources on the servers, so it may have a better change of being done sooner. I still hope the other changes are done, but this could be a temporary fix. In the worst case, this would be better than no change at all.

The rooms that are used for missions could be used for apartments. Each player could still get an empty apartment, but if they could also take that key to a shop and trade it in for an apartment made of mission rooms. Different rooms would have different prices and the player would be able to choose how they were connected to each other and where the entrance would be. Any of the doors that can now be mission entrances could be picked as the apartment entrance. If the player preferred to have a hideout in a cave, or a guild headquarters with offices and meeting rooms, they could pick those rooms, too.

This should be fairly easy to implement because it would be almost the same as a mission, only it would not disappear after a few hours. I expect the hardest part would be creating the interface to let the player set up the rooms. The rooms already have furniture in them and since it is part of the model for the room, it would not use as much resources on the server as individual pieces of furniture would. Even if the 10 item limit remains, 10 items used to personalize an already furnished apartment would look better than 10 items set around an otherwise empty room.
