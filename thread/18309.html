Two VERY serious MA concepts--
Ok gang! Now this is my first post, mainly because this is the first thing I've had to say that is important. And I would like the readers of this post to know that I have put a whole lot of thought into my concepts. I am not one of those players that simply want to uber up their profession. No, I'm seeking a much different grail. My main purpose is to seek interesting and, dare I say "Fun", game play values that fit snuggly into general feel of a profession, without puking on game balance in the process. So Funcom, if you even bother to read this post, please take it seriously. Because I know I do.


-Without futher delay I present the first of my MA concepts.

MA Nano - Lily Stride

With mind and body united as one, and his soul purged of any thought, the Martial Artist will summon forth his true power of self-- And walk on water as a lily floats in a pond.

[Ok now I thought about this one real hard. And I came to the conclusion that - If left as is - It would be a bit uber in lets say, PvP. Now comes time to add that game balance that so many other peoples concepts lack. How about this-- To me this sounds like a multi ql nano, with each higher ql in the set adding a longer duration. As to were this would start, would say around 135 Psymod and SenImp would be about the skill reqs for the first nano in the set. And it would last for around 2 minutes. And then the next nano in the set would be around 215 skill, and the duration would increase to 4 minutes 30 seconds. And from there u simply keep on going. The relatively short durations on this nano, and the possible high NCU and/or high NP cost of this line would be more than enough to prevent someone from, lets say, casting it to escape PvP combat by running across a river. To sum up, it would be very much similar to that Fixer line of speed buffs in that you could cast them on team members for a nice shortcut to that team mission way out in Newland. I think you get what I'm stabbing at here, so Im going to move onto concept #2.]


MA Nano - Feather Leap

Shaking free the shackles of gravity, if only for a moment, allows the Martial Artist to propel himself upwards- before drifting softly back to the earth once more.

[Fairly impressive eh? I would certainly think so. Lets break this down. In its simplest form, this nano would grant the MA the awe inspiring ability gracefully leap, almost float, in an arching fashion to his final destination. Now, were not just talking big jumps here. Oh no, this is an entirely different beast. This would be more like antigravity moon jumping than anything else. And in my opinion, if implemented carefully, would be quite beautiful to see. And to restate, were talking very high, and very far leaps- this would probably require quality animations to go with it, to get that low gravity effect. Ok here comes the balance. This to me sounds like a one time nano, much like the MPs QW nano. And would more than likely be nothing more than a method of transportation. It would not be cast able on others just like QW for the MP. It would have a similar duration and the user could not cast it while in combat of course. Ok now Funcom, if youre reading this, personally I would make the requirements for this nano the same, or very close to that of MA mastery. My reason for this is that MA mastery plays such a huge roll in the life of a MA. When you first get the nano, which for most is in your early 50s, you gain so much when it comes to damage. Because it gives you +60 to MA skill, and when youre a MA, youre skill IS your damage. We cant be wrangled to equip a bad ass pair of metal hands. When youre MA, skill is life. And my concept nano would fill the gap in that span of a MAs life. Because at least to me, I would like Martial Artists to be a little more than the boxers of Rubi-Ka.


So thats about it. Think it over. Flame me, curse me, whatever. I love the opinions of others when it comes to my concepts.

Ok, now time to handel feather leap!
Ok guyz, Feather Leap is very hard to explain with words. The real dynamics that i can picture in my head- It's almost impossible. But i'll try to give you the best mental image I can muster.

Imagine standing in front of Tir FT, looking twards the tower. Then, at the press of a key, you spring from the ground in a sort of majestic slow motion. And then taking off like a shot, much faster than any Yalm or Kodiak. And landing firmly upon the top of the tower.

If you have ever watched superman, then you know what i'm talking about here.

But enough about how it would look, let me move on to how it would be used.

A nano like this could be used for anything ranging from the obvious transportation of the MA, to something more tactical like escaping a very tuff mob when you've bit of more than u can chew. But ingame statistics would dictate the uses, such as NCU cost, NP cost, or the ability to cast it while in a fight. So in essence, it would be up to FC to decide in the end.

Now as a MA myself, I am looking forward to the select damage nanos. But i know that those wont be all that amazing. But if FC can make a MP fly, a Fixer capible of tranfering himself from anywere into the safty of the grid, and a Meat shield able to buff his HPs out the wing-wong. I do not see why either of my concepts would be "uber". In fact i feel as tho they would a MUCH needed fun factor to the stale "punch-punch" profession that is MA.

And to be honest- All a MA seems to do is punch things and give eveyone who passes by subcon. I know it, you know it, and FC knows it. Our healing abilities are 3rd rate, our NRG giving nanos are pointless, and on top of that, they have made it almost impossible to get a move that uses the full extent of your skill.

Some simple things that FC needs to do could be:

-Make shops fully stocked with special moves, up into very high QLs

-Make is so players can trade and sell spec. moves, but they wont drop out of your inventory on death.

-And of course patch to 14.0 already, geeze =)

But I don't wanna complain, I know FC tries hard to please players, but they need to realize that MA should be a lot more that "Punch-Punch-Subcon-Punch"

Also, I would not mind seeing a little variation in the damage animation. We're not boxers, we're Martial Artist.
