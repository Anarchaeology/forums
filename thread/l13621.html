Hi Everyone,

Actually, this would be more my department.

Omni-Pol is a department within the Anarchy Online universe. This makes it an integral part of the storyline. Unfortunately, this also means you cannot own or "be" Omni-Pol.

Omni Police Officers Association is an organization created by fans outside of Omni-Pol. Creating something similar, kind of like Omni Police Benevolent Association would be acceptable as well.

There will be events and storyline issues related to Omni-Pol and every character and organization you have heard about in Anarchy-Online.

A basic rule related to any MMORPG. Try to avoid making any organization or character that could be considered directly related to or actually be something already in the game. In other words, do not create a character storyline based on you being a long lost daughter of Philip Ross. And do not create an organization called Council of Truth. These characters and organizations will eventually conflict with the actual storyline and only cause all your hard work to seem false. This, in turn, can cause hard feelings or frustration to the players.

SO! A quick basic answer.

It would be in your best interest to not create any organizations or characters that could be perceived as the actual organizations or characters in the game. It will only cause frustration for you in the end.

I hope this answers your questions regarding this issue. I will be more than happy to help if you need more information.
