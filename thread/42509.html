Get Real
Of course the next MMORPG will work better and have less bugs than AO. You can't play AO for ten minutes without placing your character at risk not due to your play decisions, not due to the dangerous mobs, but due to the technical inadequacies of the programming of the game. How can there be willing suspension of disbelief when I continuously have my nosed rubbed in the fact that the server is playing one game and the client is playing another and once in a while, if I'm lucky, the two decide to to give me a brief glimpse of that game? How can I feel like I'm in a brave new fantastic world when the success of my avatars in that world depends more on how I learn to work around the realities of funky programming and client/server lag than what actions I take in the game world?

When AO was released it barely worked at all. When everyone was going to leave after the first month trial period FunCom published a false claim that the fixes would be patched in by the following week.

Everybody renewed and what did we get? A 10kb patch full of nothing and FunCom's patented company mantra was born..."We're sorry. We're working on it. Send more money."

This was the first of many such inflated claims from FunCom (and that doesn't include all the BS features they list on the box that you won't find in game).

AO has a great big beautiful world, intricate and deep mechanics and almost no content. Heck, there's more content in the tiny world of DAOC than in all the vastness of Rubi Ka. What content there is today was added long after people started paying to play and see the beautiful new world. Many of us are still looking for the game we were promised.

So why do people like me play AO? Because I like scifi and like it or not FunCom has filled the scifi vaccuum. I can't stand FunCom as a corporate entity, but I play because I love MMORPGs and this is the only scifi MMORPG there is. It's not the best, and might be the worst technically, but it's still the only one there is.

Is it fun? Well I suppose that depends on what is fun for each individual. It seems to be a very fun place for griefers, exploiters and borderline sociopaths. It's as good a chat room as any game for the socializers. Me? I'm a builder. For me spending IPs is the most fun. Unfortunately that fun is coming more and more infrequently with each passing level just like in every other MMORPG so far. AO is not a step forward, but at least it's scifi.
