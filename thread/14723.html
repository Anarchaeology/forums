What will happen is that

Your existing token record will be reset to zero as per the application form. Your existing upgraded merit board will remain, it doesn't get deleted.

When you zone, the merit board will be "redded" out since it has a playshift requirement of a certain amount of tokens, which you no longer have, as well as the faction requirement.

Anything else should be considered a bug. In the past this is what happened.
