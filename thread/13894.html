I'm sure they do better, but at high levels they can't be too good. I'm sure now their crits hit for 40% the same as most other classes, but they have no debuffs, no roots and are range dependent, their healing is inferior to traders and doctors, and they aren't going to punch their way through an MK. Giving an MA more damage from here won't really matter in PVP since it's mostly about who crits the most the fastest while using some method to avoid damage (healing, MK, nullity, layers whatever).

You are going to have to do a little fortune telling here with your MA. If crits are nurfed they will do better at high levels, if there is an over-equiping nurf, they'll be about the same but a little better, if damage goes back to 100% they'll be a bit better, in that they will be owned by less classes.

MAs do make great PVP characters up till about level 60. Most of the high level ma's I know, celest, takuan, all have traders now, barbie has a crat.
