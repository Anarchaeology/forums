In old athens there is one solo Whompah near SSW corner of map (if you come to Old athens from Weast athens then just take road and go South until you hit city wall then follow that wall east and keep an eye on sky, because you might se that bit spike antena which all whompah have. If you get to Old athens thru trio whompah then just run south until you hit wall and go a bit west)

That whompah will lead you to Clan outpost in BS (Broken Shores) that outpost are 100% gas. From there you need to find your way to OT outpost which have whompah to OT trade. Just look out for vultures, sandworms and 25% gas areas.

However if you take Grid to BS then you will end in town HOME 100% gas Clan/Neut controlled and home for all lv100chars who do xp mission team hunts.


Those arrows which point up/down will only move you betwin grid livels.


PS: When you in grid take look att its celling you will se a deformated map of Rubi-Ka and some spikes on it. Each such spike are representing a Grid/Whompah point on Rubi-Ka.
I asked GM about it because there is more spikes that todays grid/whompahs. And he said that those points are not in game yet
