Token odds
Assume you kill all mobs in mission, then I would guess
the odds are around as follows:

1 mob down: < 5% chance of token
2 mobs down: < 10% chance of token
3 mobs down: < 50% chance of token
4 mobs down: < 75% chance of token
5+ mobs down: close to 100% chance

If you don't kill all mobs, then the chance appears
to be related partly to the percentage of mobs killed,
and partly to the total amount of xp gained. I.e.
if you kill only a few mobs in a high difficulty mission you
are more likely to get a token than if you kill the
same percentage in a low difficulty mission.

Xp from chests and doors contributes to the chance
for a token, as does the difficulty level of a mission,
so "max hidden" should help a bit, since it seems to
make more locked doors and chests (at least that's
the way it appears).

I'm pretty sure chest and door xp is counted, because
I was surprised to get 3 tokens from 3 calm/blitz missions
on a recent day in which I killed very few mobs in fairly large
levels, but opened a lot of doors and chests. In fact, I
recently got a token by killing no mobs at all on a high
difficulty level which had lots of locked doors and chests.

However, I can only be reasonably confident of a token
in missions in which I kill most or all mobs.
