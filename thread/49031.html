Suggestion for Side/Profession-Specific Items
Hi,

I had a thought a while ago re: people changing sides/agents FP-ing to equip items perhaps never envisaged for their use.

Would it be feasable to make side alignments/profession checks constant or on every time you zone?

This would instantly stop people changing sides to equip a given item, then wait out the time limit only to change back. I'm not sure how much of a deterrant losing ones token board will be ...

If it's not feasable for side items, what about profession-specific items? Agents FP-ing to equip MA suits etc. While I know there are things they can't equip, and that it takes certain levels to be even able to masquarade as certain professions I always found the agents situation to be a unique one.

Anyway, just a couple of ideas. I'm not hardcore about them or anything so feel free to flame or back me up
