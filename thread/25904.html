Content Content Content
As a player who recently re-entered the AO world and played previously from Beta 4 through December of last year I can honestly say that I have seen vast change in the performance side of AO and can give kudos for that to Funcom. Unfortunately they reason I left before and will probably end up leaving again if no changes are in the works is the lack of in game content. By content I mean something more to do in the game than do missions for the purpose of advancement. While it is true one may explore the world of Rubi-Ka there is no incentive to do this, there aren't missions that force the exploration and task people to go on a designated route to find or do something. So instead people stay in their safe areas and get bored with the game try a few other classes and call the game boring and leave. One of the best and simplest ways to eliminate this would be to provide more things for people to do other than missions. This is what I mean by content, things that won't be the cause of frustration to the play base but will instead enhance it and span a variety of levels. Examples of this are rampant on these boards from the thread about dream missions to ones about suggestions for the next patch. Unfortunately there seems to be too little feedback on these topics and certainly no indication being given out by FC about if or when anything that adds more content to the game will be implemented. If I were to choose the top 3 things to be added for content purposes, they would be...

1) Completion of the trade skills - this is a thing indicated during initial release would be a great portion of the game and there are even indications that some progress has been made in this regard be the expansion in the supermarkets (thank you FC), but unfortunately it isn't nearly enough. There should be things that drop from various Mobs that are useful in tradeskills ie... skins from the salamandars for leather armor, twisted metal from mechanical creatures for nail armor, sinous vines from the plants that can be combined with other materials to create bows. These are just a few things off the top of my head and I'm certain with this player base and a bit of feedback people could come up with a submission list to be put into the database for Mob drop items.

2) Missions that involve something more than pre-designated go to this randomly generated location to do x and get a reward. While these are a great idea they do very little to create a long term impersive feel and do nothing to add content to the game (as of this point). To further make my point in this regard it is unbelievable that there is not a single NPC that I've run into that even has made mention to me about any of the dungeons even SoM and it was the first one. Would it be that hard to have NPCs that have a perception range of 10 meters and when you enter that range they say something about an event or happening in the world. It could even be done by having two talking to each other just put them slightly out of the way so they aren't constantly being set off in a high traffic area. The other option would be static missions like the Alvin / Dobga (sp?) one but don't have the break at every patch and keep the NPC present not having he despawn all the time. There are even signficant characters in game that could task people to do things for them like deliver this message to ICC HQ, or go to the overrun outpost at the Mantese village and recover this item for me. things that would require more involvement than the current missions do.

3) Anything that would allow people in game to know what is happening or has been happening. By this I mean a news service of some sort. Suggestions for this would be a channel that is broadcasting the latest in game happenings for those who want to participate. If that would take too much bandwidth then perhaps the same kind of thing but it could only be turned on in your apartment or comes as a message service to your apartment. This would then also allow people to look back at "previous issues" and would also give people a reason to go to their apartments and might in and of itself facilitate more game content and might even be expandable as a way to notify guilds and friends about in game happenings.

All in all there is alot in this game that is good but without things to keep people interested and thriving in the game it will continue to slowly die. This is just my humble input and I'm certain that there are people who will disagree with portions of this but I also think there is a large player base that could grab onto protions of this and do things with it.
