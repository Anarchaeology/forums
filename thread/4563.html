Agents.
Like many other threads about agents, here is another.

I had been thinking about the current world we agents live in as of today. And I'd like to come with a few suggestions that eventually will lead solution for concealment and aimed shot.

As a minor suggestion, I think it would be nice if we had a way of knowing that we were concealed or not. Mayhap a similar effect that the cloaking device produce? If an enemy spots you - your conceal is off also running breaks the conceal.

Now, on to the the suggestions.

¤ Concealment ¤
If we ought go by the 'RPG'-terms of AO, I have the feeling that the concealment works like this.

Agents concealment vs. Enemy perception.

Simple, but I think that this rule is flawed. First of all, there is only one class that has advantage of perception and that is the agent class. But how about you made the rule something more like this?

Agents concealment+agility vs. Enemy perception+sense.

This way you balance the elements of the game toward a real roleplaying game, you have the agility for move silently through the shadows (making no noises and so) and the sense for having the classical feeling that this is something wrong here. Of course, this is quite sketchy and needs some work making it work. But it is a start.

¤ Aimed Shot ¤
Remake it into a standard attack that takes longer time before the attack itself, but if successful it causes more damage.

The rule would be something like this; Agent Perception+Rifle+Aimed Shot vs. Enemy Agility+Armor+Evasive skills.

Yes. It is a bit unfair, but keep in mind that AS is and still will be a attack that solely relies on the critical chance if successful.

¤ Head Shot ¤

This would be something like the Martial Artist Dimach. With this technique you try to aim for the head instead of the rest of the body which would be a very nasty critical hit. Very dependent on Conceal and Rifle.

Rule: Agent Concealment+Rifle+Perception+Aimed Shot+Sense+Agility vs. Enemy Agility+Armor+Evasive skills.

This is a attack that would be disabled in PVP as it serves no purpose there, since the agent must be in concealment and remain in concealment.

I have few other ideas, although they are very very rough and not really anything you can post as they have no real solid grounds.

And I am working on a few other classes as well. Mainly the fixer and the Bureaucrat.
