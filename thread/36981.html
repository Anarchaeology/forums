NPCs that sell items...
I used to go to the NPC near 2HO to get BBIs for my Adventurer, and I just went there w/ my doc to look for more BBIs for a friend in my guild. She didn't have any BBIs at all, and she used to keep at least 6 in stock.

Anyway, that made me wonder what other NPCs are out there and I'd like to start this thread asking for ideas and places to find various NPCs that sell rare or useful items. Not the NPCs near the cities and town but the ones where it takes a while to get there and they carry various items.

So, I can start with this one...

___________________________
OT Specialist Tradesman
Located at 1075 x 1075 in Stret East Bank
Easiest way to get there is to Whompa to 2HO and go SSE out of city. Takes about 5 mins at 300 runspeed.

She sells various items including NCU, Implants, Clothing, Weapons, and much more... One of the weapons is a Schuyler Bow of different QL, about 7 of those.
