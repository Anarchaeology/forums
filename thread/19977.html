Missions making a difference, EUREKA!
Why not make the completion of missions MAKE A DIFFERENCE TO THE WORLD. Lets tie the consequence of completing missions directly to the suppression field in a zone.

I've killed 5 million clan npcs, yet not matter how many more million i kill, my actions have virtually no impact on the world.

Lets say that both clan and omni groups are working missions in an area. The suppression field should be going down, ideally be at 25% when both the clan missions and omni missions succeeding are at the same rate. People will have to fight to get to missions areas, and these zones will become hostile. Now, when one leans toward another... the suppresion rate begins to climb toward a faction. (Like 100 tokens per 1%.) When the suppresion field reaches 75% toward a faction, all the guards/npcs BECOME that alignment.

Example.

Lets say Clondkye is currently 75% omni oriented. A bunch of clanners begin doing missions there, alot more then omni's are currently completing. The suppresion rate starts to move down to "political zone" (NOW THAT MAKES SENSE, CLANS ARE KILLING OMNI NPC IN MISSIONS AND THIS AREA IS BECOMING POLITICAL.) Over the course of 5 days, enough clan missions are completing that Clon**** is becoming a political zone (suppresion hits 25%.)

Two things can happen now. Omni people can realize this and try to retailiate (by doing missions in the political area, WHICH NATURALLY WOULD MAKE SENSE, a POINT to doing missions, PURPOSE!!! STOP THEM CLANNERS FROM TAKING OVER OUR ZONE) If they would do more missions then Clanners were doing, then they would shift the suppresion back to the way it was. (See, isn't this making like perfect sense???) Since the zone is political, they could actually try to STOP those clanners through pvp (camping mission entrances, etc). Wow, this is all fitting together...

The other thing that could happen is that Omni does nothing... in which clanners keep doing the missions (killing more and more omni npcs), and the suppresion would shift to 75% clan. Clan would then control Clon****. All the omni-pol elite guards would switch to clan guards, and the area would become CLAN controlled. Since clan have KILLED all the omni npc's in missions, they simply have cleansed the zone of Omni. It SHOULD be clan now (this is like perfect...)

GM's could tie this to the story line, either by drawing upon it, or creating their own missions (that may have more of a punch to supression then normal missions do.) Omnitek might realize that clanners are trying to subvert Clon****, so GM's could add omni npc's at strategic places to deter furture subversion. This whole DYNAMIC process could take place...People would have purpose, that whatever they did actually changed the world.

So, what do people think? I think implementing something along these lines would liven this game up!

Hey sounds like a great idea to me.

I think in the envisioned list, FC is looking at making zones switch from one side to the other, but they never gave any details on how. (And if there's a thread around here somewhere explaining their ideas then sorry - there's too much to read on this forum)

Anyway, this would be a great way to implement conquering the world.

A couple of comments. What do the Nuetrals do? I don't know if the Neutrals really care if one side "wins" or the other, but we can't just leave them out, can we?

Let me suggest that it would NOT be in the Neutral's interest to allow the suppression field to get to 75% for either side. I'm still a newbie (lvl 17 MA - been playing for a month) and I haven't been around any unsafe zones so I'm a bit ignorant of what Neutrals go through right now. But as a Nuetral I wasn't attacked by any clan guards in the clan cities I entered. Nor Omin Trade which is obviously OT.

To make my point, it should be changed so that Neutrals get attacked by clan or OT guards if the suppression field is 75% for either side. The missions the Neutrals take will then always be against whoever has the most control over the zone. So what I'm saying is there should exist a 50% field suppression level. (I don't think that exists right now)

Also, we have to guard against one side completely taking over the world. If OT took over the whole planet then Clan or Neutral would not be able to take any missions. Even if they could they would be so out of it that they couldn't possibly hope to come back.

I say we either have a few cities that never change control. Or, better yet, we allow every zone to change control, but periodically random events happen and a city (or zone area) revolts and switches sides imediately. FC would have to figure out how to do it so it's not cheesey, but you get the idea.

I would love the possibility that Clan could possible overrun and gain control of Omin, and vise-versa. This would give everyone of all levels something to work towards as a team.
