Proposal to the dev team...
I have an idea I'd like passed along to the Development team which reflects numerous posts I've seen in these forums. Before each patch which is designed to give love to a profession, one member of the development team...not someone else, but one of the ACTUAL members... should create and play a character of that profession for 1 month. Do not use your access to create an idealized member of that profession...just do it the same way we do. I honestly believe that this would help a lot with the choices you have to make in modifying the professions. find out what it's like for a fixer to try and find a group....or an NT when his nanos fail far more than 3% of the time....or a pistol Adventurer who can't do any decent damage....or any class to have to fight and kill a boss mob in a team mission to have a CHANCE to get their new item. Consider this not only a suggestion, but a dare. Come play it with us, on our terms, then make your fixes...I'll bet your view on things not only changes, but that the level of appreciation you get from players on these boards improves too!
