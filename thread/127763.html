The Honored Maidens Honors Mothers Day
Long ago on Old Earth, before the Long Winter, the primitive Homo Sapiens held yearly rituals to recognize the effort involved in raising children before the advent of nanotechnology. The called this day Mothers Day. Every year, a womens offspring would gather to wish her well, and recognize the loving relationship between a woman and her children.

Here on Rubi-Ka, conflict has tore families apart. Rare is the employee who does not know at least one person who was orphaned at a young age by the cowardly acts of a radical Clan. Many have immigrated from the core worlds, leaving family and loved ones behind to come to the untamed frontier. In addition, Rubi-Ka is home to vast numbers of vatborn employees, many of whom have never known the parental love of a proud genetic engineer pleased at the success of their gene line.

On May 11th, The Honored Maidens will stand guard at the Reclaim terminals in Omni-Tek held cities, and provide healing and nanobots to our soldiers who have unfortunately returned to civilization through the sudden shock of death. All we ask in return is that you pause for a moment, and reflect on your family and your reasons for continuing the campaign against the forces of terrorism and all those who would stand against our efforts to improve shareholder value. Let us all hope and pray that ours will be the last generation to know this pain.

Who we are: The Honored Maidens are an organization of female and atrox Omni loyalists. We were formed on 10 July 29475 by Philip Ross to focus the strengths of an all-female group with atrox acting as guards. We continue to support and enforce the governing will of Philip Ross for the betterment of Rubi-Ka and all of Omni-Tek. More information is available about us at http://ao.honoredmaidens.org

(The legal department of The Honored Maidens wishes to inform you by our departments choice to honor Mothers Day in no means signifies the presence of a discriminatory view that the freeborn are more important or superior to the vatborn. THM is a department where the contributions of the vatborn are recognized on an equal basis of merit alone with the contributions of the freeborn.)
