I agree whole-heartedly with Dave.

The problem with over-equipping is that everyone and his grandmother is over-equipping much, much, MUCH more than really should be possible. I'm completely okay with relatively mild OE, but it is no longer an elitist situation where it gives the character an extra edge, it is now a necessary thing to do if you don't appreciate being laughed at all the time by other players (or in cases of higher level players, if you'd like to survive a battle with a yellow).

If over-equipping weren't such a problem, then maybe people wouldn't have the problems that they are currently having with minimum damage. Do you know what happens when everyone that isn't a soldier or an enforcer starts having minimum damage problems? They all start running around with nova flows, steiners, shotties, sledgehammers, etc., and there is a growth in the number of atrox characters in rather non-atrox professions, because they get to use beams, which are very effective in this game. That isn't what I call diversity. Why is everyone using hard-hitting, slow weapons? Because with the AC that the normal experience-giving monster has, it is extremely ineffective to use weapons that hit lighter and faster (this is due to the way a hit is calculated; I believe that AC removes a certain amount off of the attack, and the lighter weapons are much more affected than the harder weapons). The AC is so massively high because Funcom didn't want players getting to level 200 in what, a month or two? Or even three?

OE should give a player an edge. But it shouldn't make him capable of slaughtering a non-OE character 20 or so levels above himself.

If OE was curbed, then maybe PvP would actually become more common in this game. As it is, players won't touch PvP with a ten foot pole unless they are over-equipped to holy h*ll.

I am a fairly casual gamer. I started playing this game a little over six months ago, and my first, main, and only character above level 14 is only on level 60. That's right, 60. I'm an advent on level 60, and because I'm a casual gamer who is only over-equipped as much as I can do by myself, then I fully expect to level slower than someone who is recieving the help of a high-level guild or begging for wrangles or whatever. This is fine. I take about 8 hours, more or less, to gain a level nowadays. And that is relatively all right with me. The characters that take the time to beg for buffs and transfer money from higher level characters or whatever it is that they do to get a weapon 100 levels higher than they are, they deserve to be better than I am. My character right now is at about where Funcom thought characters should be, that is, I can kill a yellow (with some difficulty depending on the type), I can kill oranges if I'm lucky, I usually don't have any problems with greens, and I fly like a bat out of h*ll if I see a red. But I hear stories of players who can take two dark reds at a time; and I'm supposing that this can only be due to massive OE.

With the way that OE is probably going to be dealt with, it will still be possible to do things of that nature...that is, if the buff is still on you, and in that case the person who gave it to you is probably on your team. This would encourage teaming for players who want to be devastatingly uber; you really should have to team if you'd like to effectively wield a weapon that is twice your level. And those characters that have really nice OE buffs - well, they can still cast them on themselves whenever they'd like, it'd take up NCU all of the time (and it really should), but it's not as if they're getting their buffs taken away. The enforcers can still be badass, but perhaps we won't see so many beam-wielding atrox meta-physicists.

On my profession forum, the high-level players are horrified at the thought of fixing over-equipping, because as a high-level advent, it is basically the only way to have a viable character. Well perhaps it wouldn't be such a problem if Funcom wasn't forced to make the monsters godly. And perhaps dual-wielding pistols or fast swords (that's me) will be possible post 100.

I realize that much of this is probably ranting, but I'm tired of grossly-huge OE. I'm tired of seeing everybody with shotguns, giant two-handed blunts, assault rifles, and atrox everything. It is no longer a feature (for it is obvious that it was supposed to be a feature, look at implants), it has practically attained the level of an exploit in some cases. The more you over-equip, the more of an edge you should have, but not so much of one as to make you a god, so much so that the trouble of becoming a god is far smaller than the benefits, and forcing all of the casual gamers to be gods too if they wish to kill something their level (post 100) due to outrageous monsters that have been tailored to the growing god population.

That's ridiculous, and it needs to be regulated; I repeat, not gotten rid of completely; but it needs regulations.
