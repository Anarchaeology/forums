Nano shields and better nano recovery could be the answer
Just suggesting an idea that has probbaly been mentioned before. The more knowledgeable AOers can probably explain why this may or may not be a good idea.

First though an observation:

People can be healed for thousands of hit points but nano can only be regenerated really slowly... kinda strange given that nano/notum is what makes Rubi-ka so unique. If nano could be regenrated much much faster then NTs, MPs, Traders etc might not need to become soldiers at high levels but could instead rely on their nano skills more...

But they might still miss one important thing and this is particulalry relevant given the atrox/nanomage HP/nano change coming:

Some sort of way to trade nano points instead of taking damage. Its a concept used in losts of other games - "mana shields". Similar in effect to the absorbtion nanos but draining nano dynamically and not nessarily a complete block like NS but perhaps a 25-50% block that drains nano. The nanomage / nano classes would then be concerned with efficient management of their nano pool and not just their hit points.

I realise there would be a great danger of doing this wrong and unbalancing things (assumes for a moment that things are balanced now). I think it is worth exploring though.

Better minds than mine could suggest a way of keeping the balance but my desire would be see nanomages and the nano classes freed of the necessity of guns at higher levels - the choice should still be there but there should be a viable alternative.

Let me know what ya think.

Cheers,

Raza
