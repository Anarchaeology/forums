Nano cost comparsion (Or, Why Fixers are Broken)
All,

I have said many times that fixers are the class most in need of help, and most people here agree (http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=23606). I and many others have put forward lots of ideas to help fixers, usually met with harsh silence (http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=21965). IMO, one of the biggest problems with the fixer class is absurdly high nano costs. Now a skill emulator program has been developed that lets us test that theory (http://nanotechnician.homestead.com/files.html).

The following numbers are for a level 25 Solitus with an Intelligence of 80, no other attributes were modified. For each profession I took the essential nano skills of that profession from their base 21 (due to Int) to 100. The following list states how much IP was spent to raise those skills to 100. In a few cases I had to decide how important a skill was to a profession, the numbers in parentheses include a nano skill that I thought might be considered non-essential.

Adventurer: 23290 (+PM 29547)
Agent: 34065
Bureaucrat: 21552
Doctor: 29200
Enforcer: 43450
Engineer: 27112 (+SI 35454)
Fixer: 55962
Martial Artist: 31980 (+MM 38932)
Meta-Physicist: 25028
Nano-Technician: 20856
Soldier: 50401
Trader: 28156 (+MM 33718)

Using all 6: Agent, Doctor, Fixer, MP, NT, Soldier.
Using all 6 in the parentheses: Engineer, MA, Trader.
Using 5: Crat, Enforcer.
Using 5 in the parentheses: Adventurer.

What class has to pay the most for their nanos? The supposedly tech-hating MAs? No. The masters of gun damage Soldiers? No. The 'big,dumb' stereotyped Enforcers? No. The 'use everybody's nano' Agents? No. Plain and simply and obviously: Fixers.

55,962 is just plain absurd. There is no way their number should be higher than soldiers and enforcers, who have much better weapons. At the very least it needs to be brought down to the cost of the other stealth-class: agents.

Scorus
