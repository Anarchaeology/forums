Welding & Prabel Industries are proud to bring forth the third part of the animated team mission. Questions have undoubtly arisen since last time: Will our heroes defeat the Omni-Tek villain? Will the robot learn to obey orders? And will the Fixer finally get his awaited experience? Only one way to find out.

As an addition to the released movie, we bring the news that the series have expanded from four to five parts, to be able to show all the necessary aspects of the deeds in which so many Rubi-Kans participate every day.
