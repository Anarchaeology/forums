Your best bet would be to ask other soldiers what weapons you should use.

As a soldier though you should have no real difficulty using any weapon. Go to your nearest mission assignment center and take a mission with the greatest money reward. Keep using the best looted weapon until you have saved enough creds to buy a good weapon.
