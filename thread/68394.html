The Subway
#1 - Kudos to FC for finally getting off their hide quarters and giving us a dungeon like TLF/Foremans - huge, mega dungeon, with levels of spawns that get tougher as you go in.

#2 - A level one can now go 1-5 in the Backyard (1-10 iof they want to camp snakes). The subway gives a new char a place to hunt with good spawns, and the loot that can keep them in the dungeon - stims.

#3- we took a guild team down from the Obsidian Order, and took out the boss. It was a good fight, we had a few detahs on the first team as we where learning just what was down there in the rooms.

But when we went back down 2nd time, we had no deaths and took out the boss.

My only negative comment on the boss was the lack of an uber item on the drop to make us stay and get more kills of the boss.

My positivie comment on the boss was was the lack of an uber item on the drop to make us stay and get more kills of the boss.


Any spawn that does not need to be camped for an item is a good thing.

That there was no item to hold up and cheer over...well, thats just human nature.

Will we be camping the boss to take all the spawns, no.

Will we encourage members to run low level teams into the subway - a big yes
