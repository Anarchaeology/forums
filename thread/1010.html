I left it sitting right in the middle of difficulty so that I could do it, chose one in Omni forrest and started out. The first monster is a claw he is yellow to me and when I hit the T the circle is one above the line. This should be doable. It was not. I am a level 68 enforcer all my armor is QL 71 or better and I have a lvl 70 2hb weapon at this time. This claw kicked my butt up between my ears. I had no chance what-so-ever.
