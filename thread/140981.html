Bringing New Life to Rubi-Ka
The new entertainment organization, Inferno Entertainment, has started working the Rompa Bar over that last few days. Inferno is an organization dedicated to managing the Rompa Bar, it customers, and its parties! We are working in cooperation with several well-known groups to bring life back to the Omni-Ent nightlife we all used to love and cherish back in the day. We are rapidly growing, hiring dedicated staff members that regularly "work the joint" and pretty soon we'll be able to provide anyone who comes to Rompa with service at any time.
I would like to clear up that although we are an entertainment organization, our main services are Waiting tables, security for the bar, and bartending. We do provide dancers, but it is NOT our main function. Inferno Entertainment is NOT one of these uncouth "stripper orgs". We are a respectable establishment very dedicated to our goal or reviving the nightlife on Rubi-ka!

With these things cleared up, I would like to invite you all to drop by Rompa, enjoy a nice cool drink, and relax after a long day of getting missioned out. We plan on having parties once a month and we will begin the cycle as soon as our organization is well established. Our ranks are growing rapidly, going from 0 to 22 members in a mere two days. If you would like to apply for a job, please contact me on my commlink, or simply leave a message if I am sleeping.

((on an ooc note, Inferno Entertainment was created in response to the vulgar lack of out-of-organization roleplaying and hopes to revive this old habit of us rp-ers ;p. We're working with a bunch of rp orgs, as well as some greenies to try and provide the experience that roleplaying used to be back in the day. We'll be sponsoring events, parties, getting GSP over for some DJin etc etc. We just need support from you all! So drop by Rompa so we have more incentive for more employees! It'll be great! we're having great success so far! DDD))
