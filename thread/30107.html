Vehicles
I've got an idea related to the upgradable vehicles.

Make the vehicles have HP too, so if the player gets attacked while flying, the vehicle takes damage *first*. This way, if the vehicle is damaged it will *crash* and cause damage to the passengers, who must then get out (if they aren't dead).

If the owner dies, the vehicle comes back with the owner on rez (maybe fully intact and *repaired*). Or, maybe a certain skill, or vendor, is needed to repair it so it is usable again if it was damaged/destroyed.

The addition of weapons to the vehicles would allow a new type of PvP and PvM. Perhaps some monsters could be created that are so powerful they *have* to be attacked via vehicles? Plus, players could gank other players from within their vehicles. I think most people would have fun with it.

Clans could actually implement air forces, etc...

Just my two cents.
