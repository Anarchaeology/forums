Looking for a honorable Clan guild
Greetings, I am Joramon. My real name is unimportant. It was not long since I came to Rubi-Ka, and to be honest, I dont know quite how I got here, but I endured.

Then I started thinking. I had seen the injustice and atrocities commited by Omni-tek. I had seen friends being abducted by Omni-Pol agents in the middle of the night, for the sole reason of thinking differently, only to be cell scanned in their HQ, to be killed over and over again. Enough of this, i though, and thus I joined the rebels.

I realised soon, that without an organized force, there was not much that I could do against Omni-tek as a lone person.

Now I am seeking a Honorable Clan Guild that I can join, to better help fight the crimes of Omni-tek. For the freedom of Rubi-ka, for the freedom of the people!
