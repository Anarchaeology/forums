2 things
Someone above in the thread asked why we request a punishment for this... Well, its because i dont think it will feel like roleplaying black market without some dangers...Example: if someone in athen asks me if i can get this or that, and i say "Oh, yeah, gimme a second, ill run to the -shop- in athen".. And thats it. That just gives it a feel of being yet another SHOP.
Also, wihtout the options of structrual buildings and cites (all biuldings possible to enter or buildings in cities with multiple floors, etc), its kinda hard to make the black market a "black market out of the street". YET another reason: You cant steal ANYTHING in this game. In fact, nothing you do (except maybe buy credits from Ebay, which IS illegal!) makes you [i]feel[/] like a criminal.

Now, to the second part: Punishment/dangers involved.
Alot of ideas evolved here. First of all, keep the ideas at a sensible level. Too much fuzz and programming too implement the actual game ideas... Some may just be too much.
But, the punishmen should be there in some way....
Either its a %chance that every time you enter the black market/summons in (however this is done), you get sent to jail.
When i first entered this game, i knew little of the actual features of it. So when running around in Borealis in the first days, i saw the JAIL... and 2 guards... 2 HEAVY guards.. "damn", i thought, "i wonder how i get to jail.. Will killing guards send me to jail?". I stood there for 2-3 minutes, thinking, and waiting to see if someone atually came out of jail... Nooo.. A couple of questions here and there showed there is no jail implemented in game.. Boring, is what i think......I WANNA BREAK OUT OF JAIL!
If jail is not implemented, then how? Running to the end of the world seems stupid to me. A low level fixer might wanna do some trades to, but frankly, do you think he would like to run into Varmint woods or run from Athen to 4holes just to pick up a gun? no.
I like the idea of making it quest-like. I go to a dealer, he assigns me a mission, which is in a building in THAT CITY (make it a nano: summon dealer!), i enter that building and have to look through some rooms..Mobs in there could make it take like too long to get the item, som forget that. Voila, i find the headmaster of illegal goods, and he hands me the item..

Loosing money: no option. Some might have just 1000 credits, other have 5 million. Loosing 10.000 credits make NO difference for the mill dude, but sure, it will break the broke fixer...

The part of being sent to jail gives so much more roleplaying. Add that, and you can add new stuff for crats, soldiers, enforcers, agents. Crats can bribe or pull some strings to get you out faster, soldiers and enforcers can kill the guards to get you out, another fixer can break his way in and help you escape, agents can bribe and do this&that. Even give good xp for getting someone out of jail!

Yummy
Hi all fixers and no fixers.

Being a fixer myself I really enjoyed this tread. There are some debating about who would gain access to the BM and I am all for Fixer ONLY. No FP liners, no traders, just fixers.

As for the location.
I always thought since I read about the Bm that it would be grid accessed or at least a static location. Having it move around and making the zone around it PvP will not work.
Here's why. A player that dont have a yal or grid nano have completed a mission. S/he is heading back to a town to scan, enters a are where the Bm is and wham, smack s/he is killed by some campers that found the area. Bye to all xp gained in that mission. Not a good idea to have a PvP zone around the BM.

Finding the location.
I liked the idea of having a nano telling you where to go, or maybe grid you there, after all it is fixer only. Make this nano accessable to all levels of fixers. The only restriction will be the ql of the items found in the BM. Let a level 5 fixer playshift to a BM that sells ql5-15 items for example. The higher the nano the better ql items

Punishment.
I like the idea of having punishments. As for long prison time, I dont think it is viable, but having the fixer being transported to a prison is a good idea. Make it in the city where s/he last scanned. But have the fixer being stripped of his weapon and armor i like. (I know this will cripple the fixer some and forcing them to buff into the equipment again, but life on the shady side isn't suppose to be easy.) A loss of the item/s bought at the BM and maybe even a way to put felon, first time offender, rookie crook, criminal, hardened criminal or something similar in the info when targeted by others. I mean you get titles for PvP, as you level and for tokens you collect. Implement this as well. Maybe strip the fixer of some tokens, but since not all players out there gather tokens, this might not work. The idea of having fixer do some brawl in the prison to pass time is funny. I tried hand to hand with an agent in the arena. after 5 minutes of pounding eachother we still had loads of HP left.

Items to be sold
Illigal items offcourse. I read about some weapon being illigal further up, but I only know of stigmas. Implement some more illigal guns, armor, stuff. Having loot only items will work. Grid armor must be easier to get hold off. I have my entire guild hunting for grid armor, but we all draw a blank. We all range from level 60-120 and do missions but no grid armor in sight. I also liek the idea of hacker tools that can hack more stuff. What I would like to see is a way to hack crystals, not to remove the proffesion cap, but to lower the reqs for using it (This would also change the stats on the crystal) <--- Might be to hard to code. Also I dont think that all items for sale should be available all the time. Have it done like in the normal stores. with the items being random and then refresh the list every RK day or so. Some items would be harder to find, while some items would always be available. discs, pills, armor, social clothes, replicas of Phillip Ross paintings, bootleg CD's or Holodiscs, Hot stereos, vehicles. Have them done as a disc or pet shell. that way it can be traded.

Pricing.
Some said that illigal items are costly, because they are illigal. Another post said something about his runin with a thief at a gas station. The items this dude had for sale from his trunk was cheaper then if you would be to buy them at the store. Some items might be expencive but not all. stolen items are sold cheaper then if you were to hit the actual store to buy it, the guy selling them is making a profit, either he stole it or bought it cheap. either way, I don't think the black market stuff should be overly expensive.
after all, If I get approached by someone wanting something illiegal they would like to know the cost for this item, having to dish out lots of cash and then find that the buyer isn't interested because you had to make a profit would not benefit the BM implementation at all. I know you can send tells and such, but keep the pricing so it would be lower then in normal sotres for similar items.

I think that is all for me now. I am a Clan fixer and proud of it
