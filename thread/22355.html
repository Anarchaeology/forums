Bounties
I would like to be able to put bountries on peoples heads.
ie when I get shot down exiting the grid, rather than just beeing annoyed I could put a bounty on the person.

At the moment there is no way of making sure if someone has killed them.

This could be done in several ways

1. Create a mission, entering the knowledge of the person, so that whoever has the mission gets a reward when the person dies. (not a particularly good way because people have to collect the mission from you first)

2. Make a time signed trophy for each person killed in pvp. This would allow the person who killed them to have proof of there kill to collect the reward from the person giving the bounty. (problem is collecting the reward afterwards)

3. Make a /bounty command, with the name (or knowledge) of the person and the amount of creds the bounty is for. This would then be automatically transfered once the target has been killed. Once dead the bounty go be removed from the person.
This command would also include a way of checking for a bounty on someone and listing current bounties the the area.
(this is my personal favorite, as the creds would be transfered as soon as the bouty is made.
