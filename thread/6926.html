Thoughts
alright... lets go down the line.

imp/proj: any object that is fired at you. this can include but is not limited to bullets, bolts, darts, knifes, and other projectiles which fly thru the air.

Energy: anything designed to give a nice nasty shock. This type of damage should be very good at interupting nano casting as it's primary effect is to do this. damage can come from melee or ranged weapons. this includes any lightning bolt type fire weapons. all tazer like description weapons. for sake of simplicity the actual contact of the weapon doesnt' do anything near as nasty as its energy discharge. Thus it only really needs this ac.

Melee/ma: hack, slash, bash, chop, punch, brawl, dimach, kick, throw, stone cold stunner, peoples elbow... you get the idea.

cold: Intense negative tempature. I call this the "Ol' man winter" attack class. ranged weapons while uncommon can do this damage type. desert creatures should be vulernable to this type of damage. a snowball would be under this as much as Mr. freezes (see batman) gun. also note that some places may have such a negative tempature it's like sitting in acid (constant damage)

Fire: Burn, melt, scorch. mainly flamers, a hot bullet can be considered this as well. melee weapons would be easier to controll on this as you just figure they're holding an impact heat discharge weapon. I'm still looking for a hair drier ingame.

Chemical: hard to clasify, i call this the acid class. acid, while not hot, does melt objects. if it's not directly causing any other damage i suggest it be placed here. this is definatly the hardest ac to defend against.

Radiation: little particals which cause the damage for you. you won't need to hit well even. just contact with radiation can cause death in many cases. this is easy to put on any weapon of any type but would require energy knowledge skill (melee/ranged) in order to use without harming yourself in the process.

Disease: The one ac nobody knows much about. once your armor has been passed, you're stuck with the full damage the disease does. call this an infection attack or cellular damage. the common cold. the uncomon cold. chicken pox. or for a more lethal version HIV and the blubonic plague. (no offense intended for anyone who's currently under the effetcs of any of these, especially chicken pox)

once these ACTUALLY make it into the game there might be uses for half the weapons which really dont do as much damage because they attack an AC which is much lower.

eventually once they fix the bugs we should see a full weapons repair so that they actually do the damage intended and weapons which have skills that should be attached get fixed as well.

Hope i didnt take too much space, but it seems you were asking about the AC's here.
