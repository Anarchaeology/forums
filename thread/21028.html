THE BIG PICTURE:

Funcom designed a game with 12 different classes. Anarchy Online was born. Like anyone else they have an idea or goal of what they want the game to be. So the only thing left is to develop the game to that goal. (easier said, then done)

So, the 12 classes were created with logical skill sets:

Note: This is my opinion: from playing with the other classes in missions and hunting. So, if i am missing something let me know.

They got 3 out of the 12 classes: balanced right after the beta process.(as a whole)

1) The Enforcer: which is really no big suprise, because of the simplicity of the char.'s framework. (take a beating and keep on ticking)
2) The Trader: which is suprising, because of the complexity of the idea funcom had with creating a char. that could trade: skill pts., heal points, nano pts, trade skill pts, mob control, charm, ........ BASICALLY COULD DO IT ALL but not do it as well as any of the other classes. The idea hear is, we trade everything but the skills we trade are not as good as the other classes specific skill sets. We fill in the gaps in the team.
I believe funcom wanted a character like this for one reason only. SO, TO FEEL IN THE GAPS. example: 1) if u ran across a situation where u didnt have any doc in the group and u needed some healing. puff the trader is now the healer. 2)u ran across a group of mobs that jumped out from nowhere, and u dont have a brat. with u to mezz. buff trader is now assigned to mob control. 3) u find yourself out in the middle of nowhere and a big bad ugly mob runs up and the group doesnt have an enforcer. buff trader takes the role as a tank and power hitter.
3) The engineer: which is no suprise, some as the enforcer simplicity in the char.'s framework. (bot takes a beating and keeps on ticking)

Do u see what im talking about. the trader was designed to do basically do everything but not as good as anyother char. class specific skill lines. It doesnt take a rocket scientist to look at our nano skill line to figure this one out.

We are well-balanced and very needed in any group. The problem is not the trader is to powerful, we problem lies in some of the other classes are not well balanced yet.

#1 A character who has achieved 3/4 of the levels in the game can pretty easily have access to 4/4 of the game's content. This goes for every class in the game.
