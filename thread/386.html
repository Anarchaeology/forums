NTs now Doctor's best friend
In the midst of all the complaining, I have to comment on something positive: the new NT humidity extractors are great! They cut down on nano kit consumption *a lot*. Last week I was hunting near the Temple with a fun group, which included a Doctor. I ran the 51-nano-per-tick Humidity Extractor on our doc, and he absolutely loved it, said it regenerated his nano at the same rate he used it to heal us

So a team hint: get an NT in your group, and have him run a Humidity Extractor on the healer (at least). Very useful - assuming the Doc has NCUs to spare for it.

On the subject of other NT woes: while NTs need not be fantastic combat characters, the latest patch turned us into a bit of a joke (see other threads where people have done the math). I don't mind having a hard time, but making it flat-out impossible to win a PvP fight is a bit too tough :/. Range was our major survival point, now with it gone we are usually toast. Even my Atrox NT has a very low survivability in close quarters combat, and the nanomage "pure" NTs are even worse off. I hope Funcom is reading these boards....
