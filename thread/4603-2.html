IP reset.
Okay here's a heads up for the new players that haven't been with AO since it's launch.

Saying that those that want / need a reset is the result of poor planning is the result of a lack of knowledge on the person saying it's part.

Let's take a look at the way the game has changed ( on the the major ones ) since release. I'm refering specifically to things that made IP spent in what where worthwhile areas into IP black holes. Keep in mind that none of the things implanted after release where things that players where give any warnings about.

1) Pistals. They used to be a very viable weapon the removal of the FA from the game left a lot of people very nearly unplayable. ( there is another reason for this I'll cover in a sperate point )

2) Initiative / evades: They didnt' used to work. On release the evade skills had not yet been implamented, and the Init's didn't work yet either, again this is something that when changed left a lot of people flat footed.

3) Concealment: This skill used to be a viable way to do mission for anyone that sunk in the points to the skill, even allowing playerrs with a decent skill to fight 1 out of mulitple spawn mission room. The skill was heavily nerfed so that it is now only viable for agents in any potential sense of usefullness ( and a lot of agents aren't happy that the masters of stealth aren't nearly as stealthy as you'd think ).

4) Aimed shot: indirectly nerfed because concealment was nerfed and DIRECTLY nerfed because of PvP one shot kills ( which has been addressed with the changes to the PvP rules and as such should be reinstated to it's former operation )

5) Burst: again another nerfed skill, why? Due to PvP again, and again the problem that caused the out cry is resolved with the new pvp system.

6) Multie Melee / ranged skill changed: Both of these had their skill req's upped substaintially since release causeing many players to have to rethink their weapons of choice, MANY decided that having one more ip dump for a minimal damage increase wasn't worth carrying yet another blue skill ( to dark blue ) and changed.

7) NPC hit points: Indirectly this effected a lot of people, while not specific to skills the damage output needed to kill the average npc trippled. Meaning those that had previously been effective but not hyper combat oriented where shafted for spending ip in skills other then combat skills. IE any trade skill at the time.

8) the promsied nerf to the Martial Artist attack Blessed with Thunder: at current the only MA attack that keeps most MA's playing from level's 90 to 180. Due to pvp issues ( or so i summize ) FC wants to completely change that way this attack functions, nerfing it in a major way, the requirements to use the attack include massive points being dumped into the parry skill, which beyond using this attack is of dubious value.

In a nut shell FC has forced AO to become more and more oriented to the combat god characters and less about being a true role playing game. They have changed the rules of the world repeatedly. They have made Ip that WAS wisely spent under the rule of the day completley useless in some cases and much better spent in other areas in other cases.

The players had no say in any of these changes. The IP was spent and gone, and the skill the use to be of use changed without any way of anticipating what FC would decided to nerf next.

An IP reset IS called for at this point.

BUT more then this, a RULES FREEZE IS EVEN MORE IN ORDER! One Ip reset will not address the main problem, a game world where the rules keep changing.
