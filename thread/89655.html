I was later showing the /emotes to my sister with a female avatar (/ballet impressed us, but that's beside the point). When I got to /adjust, I was somewhat dissapointed that it was the same as the male version.

Part of me thinks this is a silly thing to make my first, and possibly only post on these boards. Like cleavage adjustment.
