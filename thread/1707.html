Name and x-dimensions!
It was promised, from the beginning, that cross-dimensional travel was going to be possible (well, the promise was one large dimension - I also remember the "no zoning" promise. When you couldn't make that work out, you changed it to x-dimensional travel).

Now, with the character lookup tool, I see that just about every one of my characters has a duplicate on the other dimension. What's the point? Are there going to be two of us? Will we have stupid names/tags like "JoeShmoe - Rubi-Ka1"?

What's going on here? What happened to x-dimension travel? What happened to UNIQUE character names? I feel there is nothing unique about my characters anymore (something I quite liked about this game)...

Don't go the way of UO/EQ/AC and make changes or promises you just can't handle.

I want unique names back!

Names are supposed to be unique across both dimensions, in order to eventually allow the dimensional portal to work correctly.

However, the comunications system between the two servers has either been disabled, or is not working at peak efficiency. I have found through a bit of testing that "established" characters that have been around a while have their names locked on both servers. That is, since I have had 'Nenechan' on Rubi-Ka 1 and 'Konril' on Rubi-Ka 2 for a while, there can't be a Nenechan on Rubi-Ka 2 or a Konril on Rubi-Ka 1. But, when a new character is created, the other server does not get the message that the callsign is now taken. So there are a lot of young characters that have been created with the same callsign on dimension 1 and 2.

I don't know what Funcom's plans are to correct this. As is, if the dimensional gate opens up, there are going to be some technical name issues. Someone internal to AO development would have to comment on if and when a fix is going to happen in this area. I do know the priority is currently low, so it may take a while.
