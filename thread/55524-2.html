It´s kind of simple: Layer is as good as Mongo as soon as you use up 3 complete Layers Types. If you have to recast it earlier it´s less effective. So Layer Shield (LS) would be most effective if had 8 Mobs attacking you at once each using a diffrent damage type. In that case Layer Shield would kind of "heal" you for awesome 5960 points. Also it´s put on top of your regular life so it´s better than an actual heal. But the less damage types you get (eg. less Layer Types used up) before recasting it, the less effective it becomes.

Usually you get one damage type unless you want to wait to receive some other kind of damage. If you use Rage that adds to the usefulness of LS but you´ve got to make compromises because you either loose effectivness when casting it before Rage runs out or flexibility because you can´t cast it as soon as you want to (eg. First Layer type used up). Under optimal circumstances Rage + Other damage Type that would make it 1490 points of damage "healed".

I´ll use MAs Enlightened Aura of Healing for comparison. Requirements, Nano costs and casting time all are lower on that Nano, it heals for 566-1026 points of damage (796 on average) and it uses no NCU of course. Disadvantage is, it´s less effective as soon as you take more than one damage type. Also it can´t heal life you haven´t yet lost which Layer Shield kind of does. On the other hand there is no wasted points on it because obviously life is used for all kinds of damage. So from the healing point of view the MA heal should do about as good as LS if you use rage (taking the lower cost and speed into consideration). Only their heal uses up no NCU and it can heal only damge already taken.

Another comparison with enforcers own Mongo Crush!
Mongo Crush heals for 2300 points of damage over a period of 20 seconds. It takes up the same amount of NCU as LS does and it costs about 2,6 times the MA heal and 1,8 times the LS Nano to cast. From a pure healing point of view it´s about as effective as the MA heal and the LS with the disadvantage of not giving it back instantanious. It shares the advantage of LS of having it put on top of the actual life.

Conclusion:
+ Mongo and LS have the advantage of adding to the actual life.
+ LS gets more effective the more damage types you take. The effectifness of MA heal and Mongo are reached at about 2 completely used up Layer Types.
- While healing about as effective as the MA heal, both Mongo and LS use up NCU.
- Layers cannot be healed like normal life.
