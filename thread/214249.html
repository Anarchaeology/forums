Old files...and the future
It was late, and the moons of Rubi-Ka had already travelled half the heavens. Cogs was sitting on the patio of his Jobe residence, going through old files...remembering days of the past....contemplating present events, and dreaming of a future not yet known. He pulled files of Rubi-Kan notabilities..and came upon one name that sent shivers up his spine...
Ivan Sergeyich , Chief of Omni Pol
Strengths and weaknesses : Cruel and sadistic, known even to OP as "Ivan the Terrible", known to have tortured and murdered his own stepfather.....

.....Puffing on his cigarette, Cogs shakes his head, and digs further...

In reading this, he remembers another piece of information: Omni-Tek promises criminals a pardon if they sign up. And where are these criminals posted? In the very ranks of the forces that are supposed to keep Rubi-Ka at peace. OP is filled with murderers, drug dealers, and rapists!

Cogs decides to pull up the old Rubi-Ka recruitment brochure, circulated on Earth long ago, but still in effect...

What can Omni-Tek and Rubi-Ka offer you, the prospective colonist?

System-wide pardon for all prior crimes and convictions! (1
Free housinig in a town of your choice! (2
A secure and well-paid position with the Omni-Tek family of companies, perfectly suited to your particular talents and interests! (3
A world where death isnt fatal! (4
Open spaces, untouched land, beautiful scenery and exotic wildlife! (5
A glorious future for you, your family, and your friends! (6

..glancing further down in the brochure, the footnotes...those all too often overlooked footnotes, forgotten and not seen by so many, dazzled by the initial promises..

1) Requires subject to sign a multiple-lifetime tenure contract with Omni-Tek, forfeiting signer's legal rights in perpetuity.
2) Subject to availability. Your first choice may not be available. Omni-Tek has the right to select an alternative domicile for signer and signer's family. Assigned accomodations may change without prior notice. Omni-Tek cannot guarantee signer and signer's family residence in the same location.
3) Omni-Tek will decide precisely where signer's talents and interests are best suited at any particular moment.
4) May not apply to all situations. Omni-Tek cannot be held responsible for actual physical death. Resurrection is subject to certain restriction, all of which may change without prior notice at any time. Signer is personally responsible for his or her immortal soul.
5) The exotic wildlife may be hazardous to your health.
6) "Glorious future" requires that all rules, regulations and restrictions defined by Omni-Tek are followed to the letter at all times. Failure to do so will put signers "glorious future" in dire jeopardy.

Cogs couldnt help smiling softly, ironically...how many had been fooled by this....this colorful pamphlet of lies.....
Browsing on, he came upon the short presentation of Eva Pourais, Head of Omni-Reform. She was responsible for the in OT's eyes very successful kid and teenage campaign "Embrace the Omni in you"......one of the most successful mass-brainwashing devices ever constructed, or as Omni-Tek liked to describe it; One of the best corporate image campaigns ever.

Cogs couldnt help but admit that it had been successful....but he would never, ever acknowledge it or the corporate propaganda..nor could he, knowing the atrocities that had unfolded, the intrigues that had been spun, and kept being spun..the lies the people had been told....he couldnt help feeling helpless at the sheer amount of people that had actually bought it.
As the night passed on, and the moons completed their celestial travel, Cogs grew more and more convinced that the fight had to go on. The Council of Truth had to reemerge.....the Clans had to unite....it was their destiny and written in the stars. The souls of the millions had to be avenged, and there was but one way to do it....
United, as one fist, the Clans had to come together now, in a massive effort for a free, democratic Rubi-Ka!

Long live the Clans and the Council of Truth!
