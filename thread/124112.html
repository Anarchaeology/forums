PVP twink killer
I have never tried player verse player because it seems that at low levels it is all about what kind of buffs you can get. A doc with ess of behemouth, mochams, best fixer hot is invincible in player verse player at lvl 25.

My suggestion is that in certian player verse player zones such as MMD and tir arena there would be debuffing terminals. These terminals would kind of be like insurance terminals except that when you clicked on them they would clear your ncu and put a message into chat in a different color that you could not chat in, to the players in the immediate vicinity. The message could be something like "player luxx has cleared his ncu of all buffs".

This would be nice when looking for duels. You could be certain that your opponent was self buffed and you could get a fair fight. People could refuse to debuff and then you just wouldn't duel them. This suggestion has nothing to do with tower battles only a option for 1 on 1 duels. If this happened I would try PVP but right now it makes no sense to me. I could just go get crazy buffed and slaughter anyone my level.

Failing that, here's an odd idea. I've heard that some enforcers will cast their smallest essence on a non-enforcer opponent before a duel to see if they are running any sort of essence. If the "NCU Error: Better program already running.." message comes up, then the opponent already has an essence and is needless to say not buffed.

Make a few insta-cast nanos that use no NCU and give no bonus to the popular nanolines such as Mochams and Essences. Put them at the bottom of the list of stackable nanos, so that one of the real buffs will overwrite it.

For example, let's say I'm going up against a doctor with a rifle. Since I know docs don't have a rifle buff beyond the +20 expertise that anyone can use, I cast "Check Rifle Buffs" nano. "Check Rifle Buffs" is in what auno.org calls "Strain 194." Therefore if that player has Rifle Mastery, Sniper's Bliss, or Unexpected Attack running, then it'll tell me "NCU Error: Better program already running.." and I'll know that the player is not truly self-buffed.

By the way, Rifle Expertise is in a different nanoline and therefore would not be affected. That's only fair, since a nano that any class has access to should be considered a "self buff".

It's not a perfect solution and would need to have the glitches ironed out. I also don't talk about PvP much so perhaps this is already been suggested, and I still like the debuff terminal idea better. What do you think?
