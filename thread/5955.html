"OFFICIAL CLAN ANARCHIST SYNICATE PRESS RELEASE

WAR DECLARATION

By Shawnta Clancy – SAT 29 Dec 2001

Today is a momentous day in the history of our planet. The time has come when action rather than speech is required. For many long years the suffering of fellow Clan members has gone virtually ignored by the Council of Truth. While the Council of Truth talks, Clansmen die. While they debate with Omni-Tek, our people are killed. While this amnesty is in place, Omni-Tek fortifies their positions.

Diplomacy has been tried. Negotiations have met nothing but deadlock. Peaceful protestors invited to Omni-HQ under the supposed protection of Philip Ross have been massacred. the so-called amnesty.

Commander Redruum, President of Clan Anarchist Syndicate and Odeen, President of Great Vikings of Rubi-ka herby issue a joint declaration of war against the forces of Omni-Tek. From this day forward, all Omni-Tek soldiers, militia, police forces, and facilities are subject to attack. Omni-Tek employees uninterested in the fighting are advised to flee Omni-Tek held areas.

Those dissatisfied with the rule of Omni-tek are invited to join us or become neutrals. Every care will be taken to prevent innocent civilian casualties but it is war and there will be collateral damage. A state of war will continue until such time Omni-Tek decides to leave the rule of the planet to its populace or until Clan Anarchist Syndicate and Great Vikings of Rubi-ka see fit to declare peace. We invite other freedom loving clans to ally with us in this struggle for independence. Omni-Tek
has lit the fuse to this war but it is we who shall prevail. Liberty!
Freedom! Victory!"
