Well traders are resilient little buggers I give you that They are my worst pvp opponents by far, Im a solider at lvl 183 and still have problems with players like abid, ransaak etc. My strategy so far has been to buff myselfe with reactive reflective, and equip my ql200 nova. At the same time I have a ql130 flashpoint ready and a list of lower MK shields on my shortcut bar. I let the trader divest me and get a few shots in, to let him think he have the upperhand, then at 50% HP or so I raise the best MK shield I can. Making them think they almost got you makes them sloppy and sometimes they forget to heal, or run away when you MK. So I try to get them down to 60-70% before I switch to my flashpoint and alpha strike him/her to 0% hopefully, if not I switch back to a lower ql nova.

Now this isnt always sucessfull since they might be superbuffed after divesting those cute little leets or whatever just 10sec before you meet them So a good tip if your deciding your going to flee is to go full-def, end combat, take a speed stim and run like crazy when they shoot the root off you =)
