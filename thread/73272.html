The Grid Ghosts
OOC:

I was looking through my AO sound fx and music awhile back and came across the sound effects that are used in the Grid. One of the aforementioned sounds is called "grid ghost". I kind of wondered at the time, 'What IS a Grid ghost?'

For roleplay purposes I have come up with the following definition of a Grid Ghost, and I think it's most creative.

We all know that if you pay your insurance on Rubi-ka, you don't get perma-killed. This is unless of course, your insurance account is "hacked". Now, keep this principle in mind, as it really is a most interesting one.

The second concept which comes into play, is the item called Grid Armor. Yeah, we all know what it is, Vamp.... Now, if you read the description of Grid Armor (any MK), it tells you that the Multi-spatial Phasing Beacon is what makes it work. Grid Armor isn't really something you wear. It's an item that, for the most part, transports the users body into the Grid. Leaving only a semi-solid avatar of themself.

Now for the final principle, the one which I have created..... What would happen if you were perma-killed while wearing Grid Armor? i.e., the Multi-spacial Phasing Beacon is destryoed, which then destorys/kills the user. Normally you'd end up at reclaim really, but what if your insurance was unpaid or hacked?

Thusly, the formula for a Grid Ghost is as follows:

Grid Armor user with no insurace + Perma-death = A Grid Ghost

What is a Grid Ghost? Well, that would be a person perma-killed while wearing their Grid Armor. When killed, they do not transcend planes of existence, because of the fact that they die in the Grid. And the Grid isn't constructed to support death, if you notice the 100% suppression. As such, when this person is killed, their ethereal soul is inadvertantly trapped inside of the Grid, as a Grid Ghost.

I'm going to write a big rp story based around this concept soon, so that's why I posted it here. So, please Cz, don't Bacon Warp my post to World of RK or anything ;p


Thx for reading, cya soon. (constuctive criticism/support is welcome. Flames aren't)
