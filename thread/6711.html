Firstly, QL 200+ rocks are 'easily' available because there are few items in that item range. When you finally get an item of that level, the chance of it being such a rock is high.

Lower level ones will be more readily available in the future, in their own way. The highest level ones will be more uncommon as more items for the level range are introduced. Simple example of probability.
