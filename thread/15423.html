What happens to heals when crits are removed from PvP?
Yes, I do expect alot of flames

It seems to me that a large majority of the players wants crits removed from PvP, and some say it is not heals that is the problem, but it is crits... But when the crits are removed, how do you expect to kill a healer?

Should we make dmg 100% again? Many will say no, becuase they say it would make the nukers such as NTs to powerful... But, I am not going to argue for that... (not yet at least )

Should we make heals 50%?, Something has to be done at least, cause it will be even more impossible to kill for example docs if we remove crits and let dmg and heals stay 50% and 100%. Not only docs will be totally uber, but also advs and maybe MAs, dont know to much about MA heals, except they got a slow recharge (can the MA hit for dmg while the nano bots are recharging, not counting specials in?)

Then we have Traders, who will get a UBER boost in the OE patch when it comes to PvP... their debuffs will get extremely powerful, and totally disable all professions and their pets and nanos and weapons.... They also have the 2nd best team heals in the game, at my lvl a trader can heal for 1k dmg every 3 sec....

I look forward to some input and the general opinions of the forum posters.

I thinkt he big pvp issue with the OE effects coming up is traders as Garzu said... Think about how the trader is the bandwagon class right now, everyone rolled a trader or at least a ton of people. As it is now, traders debuffs are incredibly huge, and they STACK, you can easily be hit for -300 to weapon or nano skills even on mid levels which is freakin insane. Not to mentiono they can heal themselves, and they have green weapon skill int he most overpowered weapon in the game, which they can overequip to no end and keep their attack rating huge with debuffs. As they stand now.. they are the most dominating force in pvp. Now lets throw in the OE patch:

Cons: Traders can no longer overequip as much, well not that that matters, no one else can either, except the fact traders will still be using ql 150 guns on lvl 80 because if they have their debuffs stacked and running like they always do, their att rating will be so high they wont suffer the negatives the patch will bring about.


Pros:

a) The fact alone that while all other profs will be nerfed in the patch with the exception of those who choose not to use weapons, traders will remain as uber in this aspect as ever, which is an enormous advantage in its own.

b) The ransack and debuffs lines wiell become SO much more powerful in pvp it will be ridiculous. That -300 to your attack rating will make you do 25 dmg on level 100 against the trader who is still critting you for 1300 each hit and healing.
