A battlesuit is a single unit full body armour. You put it on your back like tank, but all other armor is removed. Looks cool, not worth using, though.

I got one battlesuit, sold it for 80k in a store.

Two points:

1) If you want a battlesuit mission you will need AOMD to help you sort through the many hundreds of missions till you find one. Otherwise, only an obscessive/compulsive would actually sit for hours looking for the right mission. Not my cup of tea.

2) I rerolled a character after the token change. He is now level 73 with 402 tokens. No Yalhama, but 5 million credits ( 2 million was a twink, but I wasted alot of it on expensive armor). I'm just trying to tell you not to expect one in a chest.

In all of my mission running (450 from old 1 token/mission system and 400 from new token system) I have found 2 Yalhamas in chests (lvl36, lvl 85) and all were before the token system changed.

But, Yalhamas are well worth 5,000,000, they are the greatest thing in the game.
