Everquest escapee #44351
I too escaped EQ to play AO.

I remember my friend was playing on a special 'gift' one month free trial free version of the game (lucky devil) which a few FC reps were handing out. So I stole his CD and signed up on the 7-day free trial (this was April this year).

I chose Omni because he had chosen Clan, and we thought it would be cool to get to shoot at each other.

My very first impressions were WTF? in the back yard. I was expecting steel and concrete towers of nasty cyberpunk city, and to be presented with a nice pretty forest-and-swamp with a small area with a blue fence put me off. The backyard proper impressed me, though.

So I tried killing some stuff (engineer), until I saw someone else running around with a robot. I asked him about it and found out that I too could use such things!

My first trip into the sewers was a little worrying - I fell in and died, and then spent a bit more time in the backyard.

When I finally found Omni-1 'proper' I was soooo impressed. And then to discover there was a Whomp-pa to get from one side to the other just made me go WOW! This place must be HUUUUUGE!

It was a lot smaller than I thought, but I didn't know at the time.

First trip into the forest scared me. I didn't realise that supression gas existed, so I was constantly expecting a clan raiding party to come over the hills and squish me flat. I spent the whole time going 'OMG OMG OMG' and on an adrenaline high expecting to be shot at any second.

It was actually a REAL buzz!

These days I'm spending a lot of time playing a neutral in 25% zones. And I feel considerably less threatened than I did that time in Omni-1.

Ah, those were the days!
