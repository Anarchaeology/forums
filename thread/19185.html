Chaos Zones
FC you are wasting tons of your content, meaning that there are large parts of Rubi-ka go pretty much unused and unexplored, what I'm talking about is the chaos zones, why is there not more of an effort to make these areas more attractive to players.

These zones need to be developed ASAP, there are a great many high level players that are looking for something to do besides missions in BS, they want to hunt, they want to PVP and want reason to stay in AO, many people love the game but just feel that you left the high level game a little lacking. Additions like smuglers den and avalon castle are good snaks but really we need more.

My suggestions are as follows.

1- Add a central location in all chaos zone, towns and what not(these areas seem almost perfect locations for criminal communities) towns that will have scanners, shops, and banks, you can make a set up similar to home where you can get misisons to the towns as well, make these areas 75% gas with areas that are chaos. This would make it possible to go there without fear of constant attack, or make it 75% with a large arena area, bigger that the ones we have now.

2- make some grid access points to this area make it that you need very high level comp lit to get there(600-700) this would eliminate the problem you have in home of lOObie teams running around all over town.

3- Put something attractive in the area so that teams want to go there, from my exploring I have seen tons of red monsters from the air, beef up some of the mobs in the area so that high level teams will benifit from the risk of going there.

4- Outposts, when the possibility comes along this summer with the ability to capture territory this should be the prime areas of conflict as no one really has a strong claim to the area, having out posts with gaurds that change sides will really make for great fun. It would be great to be Warlord over your own little stretch of rubi-ka.

5- Fixer and trader shops, this would be a great area to expand the idea of fixer only shops, giving fixers the possibility to purchase high level gear(beyond level 125) would make it well worth the risk, I could see teams of people escourting there fixers to this area just so they can get in.

I'm sure there are tons more things that can be very easily applied to these areas, it would be great use of the area. any suggestions would be great, lets try to keep this a suggestion thread and not a flamme thread.

Bump if you agree
