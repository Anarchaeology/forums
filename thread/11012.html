I feel that evades are important since they make criticals on you less often. With good evades you just get hit for the minimum damage provided that your armour is good enough. If you examine the readout on what damage you take you will find out. For instance if you during the fight gets hit for 41dmg all the way then you know things are all right. If it is 41, 82, 41, 82, 123, 82, 123 then your evades are crappy and you get hit for criticals all the time, this chews up your life bar in no time. If you get hit for 41, 44, 43, 48, 41, 50 etc your armor is out of date and you need an upgrade.
