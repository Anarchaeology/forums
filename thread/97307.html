Actually, Ben is pretty much correct on this from my perspective.

We generally have one of two objectives when we assault a base:

1. We are trying to establish a foothold in a region in order to stage assaults on Omni or to provide preferred targets to our "homeland" areas.

2. We are trying to gain a land control area in a specific range and in a specific region in order to turn it over to an organization that needs it. This is where neutral targets come into play.

Neutrals are active in the northern regions, which are closer to areas we control (and consequently, less likely to be deemed as targets). Additionally, as I've stated before with statistics, neutral retaliation really isn't something that we consider a threat. If your bases were all around Omni Entertainment, neutrals would be whining about omni right now instead of clan.
