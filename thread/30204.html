The new armor from boss-Discussion
Kudos to Funcom...I know a lot of us aint happy with boss spawn and the like. I am one of them. But we have our random spawn sort of....we just have to fight our way up to boss and kill him for the random item.

But I have to admit its early days.

What I do like is now the new types of armor....yes they look like heated plasteel, or leather etc....But the bonuses they can confer! Its a random drop. If boss loot tables were just picked up slightly and Funcom expanded this to cover other armor. Like nano flowers elite and the like it would start a new trend in trade within the game. It would give players a option to collect armor other than Mk2. I mean we can buy or do mission terminal solo for our ql 200 Flowers body armor. But if the boss mob in a team mission dropped a version of ql 200 flowers that confered a HP bonus or something plus say some abilities bonus. It would start not to weaken Mk2 but give players another option for items to wear and trade for.

I mean there is no purpose to wearing nano armor now... but say each peice gave a intel/pyshci boost, a nano init boost and a say 3 extra Ncu per piece. Now you could wear that to look different get some bonus from that ...say call it Ingimzzers Nano Armor. mk2 unique armor is still the best and requires 50 plus hours camping a limited mob spawn and the like.

Anyway just I think Funcom intention is going the right way as I have started to look at this patch and some of the rare items in weapons banging round. The new weapons are nice.....you dont need it to get by for your class....but if you got that item your not going to be disappointed.

but presently the boss should at least drop one rare item, armor nano/weapon of a varoius ql everytime we go. Say he drops one ql 200 Saitek glove....and the team scratches its head and ask well who wants that? I know thats pathetic still but hey he dropped something rare it was worth the fight. Currently you fight him and we got a repressor and a ql 163 light beamer off a boss in a ql 197 team mission.
