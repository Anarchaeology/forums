Where is "here"?
"Here" is the exact same mission terminal from which your accepted the mission. Return to that terminal, and take the item on your mouse and right-click it onto the terminal. Wait 10-30 secs, and WALLA, your mission will complete and you will receive the reward (and maybe a token, depending on completion percentage and chance).

Be aware, the right-clicked "find" item will dissappear! You don't get to keep it. If you want it more than you want the actual mission reward or token, then just keep it and delete the mission.
