Mission: Clan's Terminals
Omtron

Athens, Clan Terrority (Omni-Tek Press Corps) -- Reporter Omtron goes in search of Clan Mission Terminals and discovers their flaws.

In Athens it's not uncommon to see a large group of people surrounding mission terminals. The first thing a good reporter would do is see what type of missions are generated for Clanners. After much time was spent trying to access the Clan Mission Database, and failing, it was made clear that this report would require a look over more visable aspects of the Clan Mission Terminals.

The machines seemed dirty and in a state of disrepair. On various parts of the machines, I found crumbs from food and other unidentified substances stuck to the surface. This reporter can only assume that the Clans think they must feed these machines in order for them to function.

After completing my examination of the machine I managed to get an interview with a local...

Omtron: I'm investigating these substandard Mission Terminals.
Slock: They are dented things. But are they substandard?
Omtron: These poor quality mission terminals look like they could break or cause shock to a user, this could be a safety concern.
Slock: Well, safety for who, really?
Omtron: The users of these devices.
Slock: Just wondering at an Omni concerned with our welfare.
Omtron: Omni-Tek cares for everyone. And even Omni-Tek employees should have the right to know the truth about how it is to live in Clan areas.
Slock: I just find it su****ious you come investigating something so small, just after a meeting to restore the Council of Truth happens.

The conversation ended there. It was this reporters decision to avoid any political debates or questionings during this story. I was unable to get anyone else for an interview.

So, let us be thankful that with Omni-Tek's support we know we'll always have state of the art Mission Terminals that are clean and well cared for. I'll be questioning fellow Omni-Tek employees in the future about our own Mission Terminals in one of my, "Take three with Omtron" reports.
