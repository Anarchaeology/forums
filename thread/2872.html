Sure, I'll clarify *grins evilly*

The infamous NT patch. It boosted Humidity Extractors, which was seriously needed. 4 nano-per-tick was total BS. The formerly 4 nano-per-tick is now 21 nano-per-tick. Much better. The 6 is now 51 ... you get the picture. It is very good.

We also got Nullify Sphere, which makes us invulnerable for a (very) short period of time. During which, we are rooted. Still, it would be nice to get off that one last nuke; but alas, so few people have it it may as well not be in the game.

Then we got the Enfraams' line. The health/AC increase is really pitiful. But hey, we're magi, not tanks.

But there lies the crux of the problem. We are not tanks. Yet in "our" patch, we got 1 thing fixed (HE's) and 2 things that basically say "Yes, you are tanks." Not only that, but the stuff meant to fix us became tradeskill-only (but that seems to be the theme, lately, so it's a moot point.)

Then, in the next few patches, we get the lovely PvP change, which halfed damage, yet kept nanocosts the same. Meaning that NT's (and other nuke professions) have half as much ammo capacity in PvP. Many NT's have done the math, and found it a statistical improbablility to kill anything except another nanomage breed. This is because of the impossibility to reload in battle, and the extremely limited ammo capacity. Doing max damage every shot (Even without the 40%hp cap), My NT would find it impossible to kill ME in anything less than 45 seconds. During which time, I have almost completely run out of nano, and I where thwacking my nuking' self with a g-staff, the melee version of myself would win.

We also got hit the hardest (arguably Agents got it as bad) by the 40m Range Cap. It hit everyone, just not everyone equally. I bet the majority of the MA's out there don't even notice the range cap. You see, we have 3 buffs out there that increase the range of all of our nukes beyond 40m. The smallest range nukes we have is 25m. The 3rd highest range-increaser buff does it by 67% ( Superior Nano Command ). With a little help from Mr. Calculator, that gives us a range of 41.75m. The other two higher do by 86% and 104%. Not to mention the deck range increasers with a max increase of 150% (q200). Granted, that an absurd range on our longest range nuke, 35m * 3.54 =123.9m, but at the very least, the last of our range increasing buffs shouldn't be useless on the majority of our nuke. 25m * 2.04 = 51.
Not to mention the fact that the root/nuke tactic (just about the only one we have w/o an absurd range) is outright dangerous with such a short range. If the root doesn't stick (resist/fumble/blackhole) we have no time to recast before that things' ontop of us (killing us, since we're no tanks).

A recent development with calms is that they don't stick half the time for some unknown reason. No feedback, they just keep attacking.

Another recent development (an admitted bug) is auto-attacking on hostile cast. This makes calming (a hostile nano) alot harder (even without the blackholes we keep getting) because, if we get the tiniest bit of lag at the wrong time, we attack..and break the calm.

--------

So, to sum it up, we got a useless nano tweaked into usefullness (humidity), we got a nano that basically isn't in the game (nullify), we got a useless nano (enfraam's), we got shafted in PvP (full nanocost, 40m range), we got shafted in PvM (40m Range).

I'm not seeing too much improvement, here. Granted, other classes have much wrong with them, too; but NT's were supposed to have been fixed, but they wern't. Not even close.

Oh yeah, did I forget to mention that at higher levels (100+), most NT's find it impossible to kill a green without recharging in the middle of a fight (which means getting a successful calm) due to nanopool constraints?
