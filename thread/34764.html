Leaving ---> Dankman
I'd just like to let my friends know I am leaving the game indefinately. I'm sure you have noticed my lack of interest the past few months and I'v finally pulled the plug. I know there will be no content in the game until the expansion is out. They are using us to make money to finance the "expansion" pack, which is the real game, also the competitor of SWG and AC2. At the same time, we are testing their game engine. This has all been quite obvious for a while now, but I'm not going to pay for it anymore. I might return to try out the real game, Shadowlands, that I'v paid for the last 10 months. I'm sure it will have all of those features boasted about on the box I bought. What a genius way to make money, test your product, and get a head start on the competition.

Good luck to my friends, and everyone else, and I will miss you. We had some good times.
