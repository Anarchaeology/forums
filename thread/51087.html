I thought funcom took grafts out of visible mission rewards, because I havent been able to roll any since 14.5.

Probably the most useful grafts are:

Feet of Stone (goes without saying)

Lesser Deflection Shield (Extended): locks TimeSpace for 13 min but gives an hour of 13% reflection...very rare grafts...useful for when soldiers arent around and probably will be *quite*valuable when soldier reflect buffs become self-only. I have 3 of these grafts, and I love having them. This is the very best reflection graft available...not sure where you're getting +21% reflection from unless you're talking about bracers. A 21% reflect is a q96 soldier nano and that level of nano doesnt exist on grafts.

Company Policy: Low level Doc team heal...not all that great, but in PvP is very useful for a non-healing class to have considering 50% dmg in PvP. The q200 version locks BM for 13 min. I have 4 of these.

Healing Touch: Low level MA heal...heals for about 180-360...again not great, but useful in pvp. LOCKS A DIFFERENT SKILL THAN THE COMPANY POLICY GRAFT. So, theoretically, a non-healing class can have 2 heals every 13 minutes. I have 3-4 of these somewhere.

Enhanced Senses: +15 to Sense, not all that great, an agent is usually closeby to buff this on you anyway. I have 1 of these.

Sparrow Flight/Playful Cub: Not useful really, just for fun grafts.

Glowing Retribution: a +99 to Hitpoints buff and adds to minor AC

Advanced Shielding Barrier/Wilderness Protection/Lesser Shielding Barrier/ Lesser Defensive Screen/Lesser Absorption Shield/Lesser Protective Field/Advanced Shielding Barrier - all of these raise AC's, the best one is Advanced Shielding Barrer +343 to all AC's I think, the equivalent of reducing dmg by 34 from mobs and 68 from players...lasts for 1 hour.
