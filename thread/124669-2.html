Day after Blackswords meeting with Naxilite
It was nearly noon, the suns shown brightly over Clon****. Cristin stood in her office, looking out the window. John Rm stood farther back, behind the desk, taking a peak of the view. A grand view it was he thought. It over looked southern Clon****. You could see everything from the old Clon**** mine, the Notum Exportation Base, the Omni Outposts far south and even some of Galway County. A view that can only be seen from Omni-Mine Headquarters in Clon****. What news do you bring me John? Cristin asked while looking out the window. John pulled out the file from a compartment in his belt. It was hard to track him down, but I got it back. John said as he placed it on Cristins desk.

She turned from her window and walked towards her desk. She picked up the file, flipped it over, Hmmmlooks like the right serial number. Cristin then inserted the file into a computer system on her desk. In an instant the word Decrypting appeared on a holographic screen with a clam female voice stating the word. Lets just see what was seen and not seen. Cristin stated. Second or two later a list appeared. Well you were the only one who actually decoded the file John. Coping is another story though.
John looked down and then back up, Something happened while I was getting the original from Naxilite.
Cristin look at John with a puzzled look then returned to a neutral face. He saw the file contents? she questioned.
John nodded slowly, Yesyes he didI was decoding the file to see if it was the right one. He saw the picture. I explained basically what it was. I got him to understand that this must remain quiet for the safety of many.
Cristin thought for a moment, This is not good John; he will just go explain to his followersexplain more then they could have gotten out of attempted decoding.
I think differently Director. I trust that
Trust? You trust himnot just a Borpac terrorist, but one of the leaders of it? He is one of the craziest out of them all. How could you even remotely think of trusting such a man?
He has the motive to not speak. I explain the potential danger if he spoke.
Motives may aid in decision making but does not make the decision, John. I will have Omni-InterOp keep close surveillance on him.
That is not necessary Director, he wont speak.
You base this on what? Trust? Trust of a man who even lies to his own peopleno John, I can not trust a man who has attacked us. I will keep surveillance on him, at all times.
John remained silent for a moment, About the copies.Here is one but I there are two more. He said as he handed the copy to Cristin.
Yes I know. They have been taken care of.
They have? I havent started looking
You were only supposed to get the original and the first copy. I can not send you for everything. I had some friends of mine in the Corporation help out in getting them back. I have received both of them yesterday. My ninja droid over there had a fun time destroying them.
John thought for a moment, How did they get returned?
They were returned to me by Omni-InterOp, friends in the Corporation as I said.
How though?
Ah, they have their ways. Intell, agents, security dronesyou know. The important thing is the copies have been returned and destroyed. Why do you ask so intently?
I followed the trace program of the second copy to the apartment of Rougefire. I turned into a leet and headed into the air ducts. I discovered Rougefire dead. I dont mean regular dead, she was permanently killed.
Cristin showed a surprised look, Permanently killed? Are you sure about this John?
John looked at Cristin with mile long eyes and said "Director I've permanently killed six people...I know what it looks like...and please don't ask who I killed."
Six? Your citizenship background does not mention this.
John sighed, Long ago Director...very long ago but I remember the smells and sights. Did you order her to be killed Director?
Cristin gave John a confused look then stated, I? Order an assignation? What ever made you think of this John? I would never want to take the responsibility or face the guilt of such a drastic, barbaric action. It such an immoral, improper and unsanctioned act that I would never remotely think of doing. I of course did not give the order to have this Rougefire killed.
Then someone did. I'll do something...She didn't need to die...I will find out why she was killed and by whom. I will check here computer and its logs; I will ask aroundthis murderer can not hide from me forever.
John, John, calm down for a moment and think about this. There is no need to run around and search for a murderer. We have more important things to do. But, I can see you are committed to this, and it is worth looking into. If you want you go search her computer system and anything else you can find. But this will not get in the way of your primary job here. You arent going to run off and follow a shadow just because of past guilt. But a time of relief has come. You did well John, as always. I will contact Ollie Svancara and have C104N-B returned to Omni-Research immediately. I will also recall all other sensitive information back to its original location. Such an incident wont happen again.
I hope not Director. This took time away from being with Oleta.
Cristin smiled, Yes it took time away from many. Time that could have been spared.
Well if I am finished here Director, I will go. John then started towards the door.
Very well John, good bye. Cristin said as she watched John walk back towards the door.

Cristin pulled the file out of the computer system and spun around in her chair towards the window. She shook her head slowly, All this stress for a small filea reportjust imagery analysis. But nevertheless it was a small part of a bigger piece, even the smallest is always importantyes important. She spun around again and hit a few buttons on the holographic screen on her desk. Soon enough a man appeared.
Well it seems I have some more work for Omni-InterOp Cristin stated to the man.
We are honored to help you, Director Moscardelli.
Please, keep surveillance on the Desert Winds leader, nicknamed Naxiliteclose surveillance.
Ah yes him, we know a lot about Naxilite. But John got the original file back. Why do we still need to keep a close eye?
Our neutral friend Naxilite has seen some of the file contents while John was checking it. We just need to keep surveillance on him in case he goes and tells another we just need to watch him.
The man nodded, I see, well watching is what we do best. I will have human and or robotic eye on him at all times. Anything else?
Cristin thought for a second, No nothing else, thank you for your continued aid in this. I must thank you again for getting the other two copies back to me.
Of course Director Moscardelli.
Well then that is all for now, good bye.
Good bye Director.

Then Cristin selected another name from her contact list on the screen. Soon enough an older man appeared, gray short hair, dull blue eyes, pale white skin. He smiled when he saw Cristin.
Greetings Dr. Whineyard. I hope you are doing well today.
Indeed I am doing well, Director. I have heard that C104N-B was returned to you today. The sentence brought relief to me. Of the many things I need to worry about, that did not need to be one of them.
Yes I am pleased they have been returned. I still must apologize about them even being taken by Desert Winds.
Oh, my dear, you dont need to worry about it. It wasnt your fault. Just a mistake, mistakes dont always need explanation. Besides no one got to decoded them.
Hmmmwell.., Cristin said distantly then returned to a normal voice, There was yet another mistake. It seems Naxilite saw the file decoded when John was checking it. But Naxilite did see it quickly, so I assume Naxilite doesnt understand what he saw. But the picture is still of worry.

I have not. Though I have sent some to watch over Naxilite in case he says something
Wise, yes. Well Director, when will I be expecting C104N-B IA report to return to Omni-Research?
By the evening it should be there.
Very well, I will be waiting. I am sorry but I must go for now Director Moscardelli, my presence is requested in lab 14-B. I will speak to you later Director. Dr. Whineyards portrait then disappeared.

Cristin then stood and looked out her window once again. A beautiful day she thought.
