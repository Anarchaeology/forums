Another change
I think the problem here goes beyond the outdoor vs. indoor debate. The problems are:

Mobs are dumb
All there is to do is kill things.


These two things must be changed for anarchy to last. There needs to be more to missions then moving from room to room and killing things. Heck, there are free MUDs out there that can accomplish more interesting things. Admittedly, its harder to add content to a modern 3D game then a text based MUD, but this is progress, and AO just doesn't appear to be capable of keeping up. AO needs to make it so there are decisions and thought involved in the missions and not just mind numbing repetition.

Mobs act like idiots. They have no sense of tactics. They behave like robots without intelligence. MOBs should not have to have uber stats to compete with humans. They should work together. They should flee when seriously injured. They should have good pathing, rather than zooming in random directions when trying to attack. They should use their nanos intelligently (no rooting with 5 hp left). Etc.

Ideally I'd like missions to be more than a set of rooms thrown together at random. Instead, it'd be nice to have it set up in a logical fashion. An office building would look and feel like an office building and not a random collection of rooms. Caves would have large caverns and narrow crevices, not a set of random rocky rooms. The Mobs would be logical. Security guards at the front would be strongest, and then the weaker workers would be located inside with a few exceptions.

These are problems both indoors and outdoors. Hopefully funcom will make both equally fun and advantageous. Its really a shame that they made the world look so good but set up the game so your doomed to wonder horrible looking dungeons all the time.

positive suggestion to make missions better
By now we have all figured out there are a limited number of rooms that are put together at random according to a farely recognizable set of rules. Anyone who has made an effort to get good at blitzing knows these rules.

I suggest for team missions making sets of rooms that go together. This is just like the individual rooms that we have now, but they would be larger peices of a couple of rooms together. These rooms would be related to each other in some way. Puzzles, tricks, and items requiring profession specific or number specific items.

For example.
room with a button that has to be held to keep a door open. Someone with a fast run speed would have to hold the button, while everyone else went through, then he would have to get through another way.

another example.
doors that are timed to open and close. it would add a new level of excitement when blitzing to realize that there was the eight door corridor coming and the doors had to be timed right.

another example.
swinging bridges (i hate these)

another example.
doors have to be held open to keep another door open at a long corridor. It might mean sacrificing a pet, it might mean a uber run speed required, it might mean having to do parts of a mission while one person is left holding a door open.

another example.
in mission mini missions. for example mini boss x has the key for the main boss room. Or the generator is going to blow, if you can get the slapon unit to it, you will be fine, otherwise, its lanterns.

another example.
x number of buttons have to be hit simultanously, but when it happens, x number of mobs are released, so the challenge is for hte team to get back into fighting formation before all members are killed individually.

another example.
solo portal, only one member can get into a room to perform a task. This is where profession specific tasks could really come into play. Traders and engies would be in great demand if the team mission stated you would need x in quantum field to finish, halfway through the mission you find the force field keeping you from the boss pin can only be removed by an engie in a solo portal room, and there are uber mobs in it.

another expample.
NPC's in missions. Help, i'm lost, i dont know how to get out, and that monster wants to eat me. If you get me to the door, i will give you this item i wont need anymore because i am safe

another example.
innocent mobs. You walk into a room, there is a mob who shold not be killed or you cant complete the mission. but overzelous n00b wants to kill everything.

another example.
rising water levels, who wants to swim when there is a cyborg in the room.

another example.
uber complex mazes. I hate them, dont do this. xp points for completeing the mission.

These changes would not be too difficult to include, and may be on the drawing board already. If you could impliment them sooner, i would deeply appreciate it.
