The lil' herder in the picture is a Yuttos, with an example of his flock in the background, an Ottous.

They are intelligent beings, with a highly developed form of song. (a visit is a must to any norwegian players, btw )

The yuttos are easiest found when travelling up the Longest road, in the area known as the Neverglades. It's to the south of the road after you pass the intersection with a paved road leading south.
