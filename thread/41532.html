Expansion? Heh, who cares
I don't think its really fair to compare EQ to AO, but brought to you by the warped genius behind the spirit of Hotpie, here we go anyways =). To me, EQ was a fatally flawed system that revolved around the concept of greed, writ large (you camp for stuff cause you cant buy it or make it, or camp stuff that is used to make something nice).

Criminally wrong to sit in a single spot for hours, sometimes days (cleric epic) at a time in the hopes of an item you or your friends might benefit from. Monsters that drop miniscule amounts of cash made it impossible to turn around and give your buddy 200k plat without worrying about bankrupting yourself (hell never saw anyone with more than 20k let alone 200k).

AO is the best game in town, uhhhh DISCLAIMER- At. This. Time.

Do I plan on buying the expansion? No, why would I? So I can sit at mission terminals, line up a mission then zip through a lag infested forest at a comfortable 300 meter altitude? Someone basically said earlier that once the various shiny new toyz of Shadowland wore off on its novelty, everyone would go back to sitting around in Tir/Omni I/Broken Shores all over again. That didnt even raise my eyebrows. Who cares if there's new zones to fly through? Seems like 90% of the player base has never gotten up close enough to listen to a Braindog panting as Enigmas walk by.... or would even care. Shallow player base or shallow game? You can make the call, but I think they are both sides of the same coin.

One of the things that EQ had an advantage on AO was that you could actually feel like you were loyal to your base faction, whether it be the Dwarves of Kaladim, or the Ogres of Oggok, what have you. Here, we have mission tokens and the token boards which leave a very hollow feeling.
The guards don't smile at their loyal children or glower at those who fraternize with the enemy, here. There is no faction system. And in that the game seems pretty limited in scope. I have no connection to the story line. Especially being on RK-2, where there is just a vast wasteland of an anechoic chamber, a vacuum where no story line is present aside from the static missions. The last time I saw an event was where I spelled HOTPIE WAS HERE out in front of Fair Trade in Tir with summoned melee weapons, and attracted a crowd, upon which I heaped disestablishmentarianist propaganda. To which two ARKs promptly arrived and scolded me, then proceeded to destroy my artwork. Ho-hum. Best event I've seen yet, anyways.

In my opinion, EQ's faction system seemed like a good start, but only that- a start. Here in AO we don't even have that much. We have no way of personally undertaking a sensitive mission for Omni Pol, or the Council of Truth. There is no way to feel any kind of a connection to what FunCom has created. Last time we saw anything even vaguely close to that was the Amnesty.

So really. Expansion, shmexpansion, who cares. Don't tell me you actually believe there will be bug fixes or (gasp) real content in it, I'm sure you wouldn't believe it if someone else told you that, anymore than if FunCom told you that the sun was going to rise in the East tomorrow at about the same time as it did yesterday.
