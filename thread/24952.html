To be fair all, agents are gimpy, it's the way funcom designed them to be. Agents were all about aimed shot, that was their method of pvp'ing and they were good at it. Funcom designed them so that that was pretty much the only way they could pvp.

And then people whined that they didn't like being killed without getting a shot off. And funcom halved damage, and capped max damage at 40% of someones max. With these two changes they ripped the guts out of the pvp'ing agents and have done nothing since to help them out. So here we have a proffession that's been designed to kill people in 1 shot, funcom nerfed this ability to hell, and then funcom didn't follow up and replace the ability they took away with anything else.

It's easy to say "raise evades", but agents were never designed to need evades. Hence the dark blue color of the skills. The only agent I've seen successfully pvp'ing was a lvl 160ish one who had a fine old time ganking us lvl 120's. However I have my doubts about how effective he'd be against his own lvl range.
