The time has come for me...a WEDDING!!!
OOC: I dont know if this is right, but I have decided to get married, and I am doing it in a weird way. Now let me explain before you make up your own ideas of what I mean when I say weird.

A while ago I started an Omni Agent. I met this girl named Sonya, and I helped her get started in AO. She stuck around with me, and kinda clung to my arm in a cute girly way. So we have been hanging out and leveling our little characters together, and we have grown to like each other, and more...

As we all know I play many characters. Fixerben, Benjacrat, and now Secretben. Secretben was the Omni Agent. I decided last night to go back to Clan because Omni no0bs are more annoying than Clan no0bs. Now since I have 3 very busy characters, I didnt want to leave her alone when I am off playing my main which is level 166. We decided that she would make 3 characters to get married to my characters so no matter what Character I was on she could be with me.

I was just wondering, how many of you think this is a good idea? It seems logical to me, but when have I ever been accused of being logical. So...for now the plan is for Sonya and Ben to marry, regardless of the character I am playing. She likes "me" not the character.

I would like to announce that Sonya and Ben will be brought together in the sacred union of marriage on Rubi-Ka. We have not set an official date, or decided which characters we will use for the official ceremony. I would prefer to have my Solitus at the ceremony, but we are still discussing that.

So give me your blessings or you curses. Let me know what you think. Thanks everyone...

-Ben
