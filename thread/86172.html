Those are good questions. There was an earlier thread somewhere about where to find the RPers. Many of us that RP to one degree or another are collected in a few guilds. I am on Atlantean so I am only speaking from what I seen there.

A good way to spot a RPer is someone who walks (as opposed to running/flying) around. Walking is almost a sure sign of a RPer because it shows everyone you are not in a rush and you are approachable to talk to. The bars are good places to look for RPers as well. These forums...are a good place to see some names. Look for us on world. If you can't think of a way to start RPing, send one of us an OOC message that you are interested in some RP and I think most of us will be happy to meet you. Once we meet with you we will most likely start RPing our chacraters. Just follow our lead.

A good tool is to develope a background story for your toon. Even a brief background outline is a good place to start. Have a brief background on where your character is from, do you have relatives, how did you come to choose your faction. Everything else can be built on later.

If you have multiple characters...make sure they are RP as different people. My main character has a "step-sister" They trade insults and don't like each other very much. The one is more fun loving and a partier. The other is more serious and stern...but who is rather pleasent once you get to know her.

I'm sure many others can give ya some RPing tips as well. Good luck in your future RP endeavors.

I would just like to add that just posting here is a good step, too! If you hang out in Rompa long enough, someone is bound to recognize your name and say "Hi!" ^_^

Some hints about your backstory, from a role playing standpoint... keep in mind that there is a strong ideological underpinning to the current struggle on the planet (in addition to the usual concerns over control of resources, etc.). Try to figure out where you stand politically. Are you a dove, believing that peace between Omni and the Clans is the only sustainable solution? Are you an party line loyalist, who believes that the Company's position is absolutely right? Are you undecided about, or ignorant of, the politics behind the conflict, and waiting for someone to offer you a reasonable solution?

If you can answer these questions, and other questions that flesh out how you feel about various issues, you'll be able to jump into other people's conversations occasionally, and meet people that way!

-Blackpetal

P.S. On a side note, because there is so much "background chatter," even roleplayers often communicate in team chat or /tells... I like to try to use vicinity say and whisper as much as possible, thought, because it makes it possible for other people (people I might not expect) to react to what I say. Why use a /tell when I can /whisper and enjoy the risk that another nearby player will overhear something? ^_^

What the other posters say is pretty much true. This forum, the bars and the existing roleplaying guilds are the best route in.

There is a lot of roleplaying in AO and it comes in a lot of different flavours. Some of the roleplaying tends to be more social, some is more political and some is just plain strange. You'll find casual roleplayers who aim for a balance between being in-character and still getting those levels and fancy items as well as those for whom roleplaying is the game, levels be damned. And again some will tell you that PvP and "cybering are logical extensions roleplaying, others will view these activities with distrust and distaste. Amongst it all there is a lot of good stuff, which is what makes AO really stand out from the crowd of mmoprgs and some bad stuff too, in terms of disagreements turning into personal fights, big plans which run out of steam before even getting off the ground and guilds who started of with a roleplaying agenda and then settled for the level treadmill with the occasional pool party.

Basically it's all there if you have the patience to shop around until you find something that suits you. Don't get discouraged if the first few experiences don't work out for you and most of all don't let anyone tell I the roleplaying is dead in AO.

Heres what I can say about rp from my point of view. I started out in an rp org pretty much so that helps a great deal-military structures and ranks help alot to build character. Organized raids with a purpose or just any old thing that has a slight bit of diffence on rubi-ka is rp in my mind, whether your raiding steps of madness to free that deranged neleb of his madness or investigating corrupt omni out at foreman mine it's all rp material.

After you get old and crusty and have done all the basic dungeons you can goto camelot and perhaps rp a bit even though it's all pvp and loot there really. Personally I see alot of rp taking place in a castle like that with the round table and old earth connotations.

Basically it comes down to what you feel is what you want out of the game, your not gonna find alot of rp orgs out there and most of it takes place in bars over trivial things, but once in a great while you can get caught up in something juicy if you keep your eyes open, keep your eyes open for plot or storyline to follow.

Rules not to break... Don't automatically dismiss anything anyone says, alot of people have something to say, use your judgement-if a guy is mooning you and grabbing his crotch calling you a dirty clanner or omni pig you might wanna keep on trucking-name calling gets old fast, and most good rp has gone way past trivial things like that. Don't get me wrong calling Tir a stinky hell hole is a good way to get a clanners attention, or Calling omni fascist pigs but expand on that make an argument, bring some facts to the table, it's not all dictated by the GM's so make something up if you have to.

RP is what you make of it, find a cause and try to stick to it. don't condescend yourself over and over. Pick a side, Confrontation is the best rp imho.

Thats all I can add, stick with it though.

Another thing to keep in mind is don't join an organization that is anti or non-supportive of role play. RPers will see your guild tag and basically not notice you becuase of the bad expereinces many of us have had with Non-RPers. Additionally the RPers will see it as a waste of time attempting RP with members of said guilds. Attempting to RP from within one of those orgs could set you up for hostility amongst the non-rpers and leaves the rest of us confused as to where you stand. Additionally, some RP has a tendency to draw in entrire RP guilds. So...mixing a non-RP guild in with RP stories is problematical at best. Just some observations of mine. Take them or leave them.
