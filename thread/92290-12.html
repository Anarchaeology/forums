Hey All,

I'll have to admit to not reading the whole thread through. I just thought I'd drop in to say that I'm pleased that FC is sorting out a manageable workload for the Community Team. Right now covering all of the forums, emails, PMs etc leads to the Community Team not being able to fully cover its advertised role - feeding customer feedback into the design and development process and getting information from the design/development process back to the customers.

We often hear complaints on the forums that FC doesn't enter into a dialogue with its customers enough here in the forums. The hope would be that by refocussing the efforts and workload of the community team, the dialogue will improve. I'd expect to see more of the Professionals Questions being answered as a result of freeing up some more of Cz's time for example. Any overall increase in dialogue and information flow must surely be seen as a positive thing.

From what I can see, the biggest losses will be in the forums where player/player communication is most important. While I'm sure FC could reduce the workload of the community team by simply saying that those forums wouldn't be moderated and covered by the community team, it really isn't viable to have official FC forums that aren't covered by the community team and it would lead to problems down the line.

It seems to me that we can either stretch the work of the community team by spreading it out so thinly that they can only do a mediocre job across all of the information channels - or we can focus the work of the community team so that they do an excellent job on a selected range of channels. I'd rather see them doing an excellent job on a few channels since that's likely to get players more and better representation overall.
