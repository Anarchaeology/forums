Bring Back The Theater!
I would really like to see Funcom bring back the animated movies. They added a hint of realism to the in game experience. Not only that, but it developes the major characters within the story line and helps the average player grasp a basic concept of what is currently going on. The movies also added the essence of excitement to the story/game. To see major characters that ingame we normally dont see, interacting, going about business and creating plot for the story was a nice touch, and then to have those movies depict what actually happens in the game itself was in itself spectacular (Omni Ent Bombing) So I say bring back the movies, they seemingly left off with a cliffhanger and the masses wish to see more. Merge them moreso with how they affect the ingame story. Encore!
