Some Notes on Apartments
My wife and I play AO together and we think the idea of apartments is really cool. Now for the weird thing:

We created OMNI chars at the same time and happened to be picking our apartment right at the same time and at the same location. To our surprise, we found both of us in the same apartment and we can consistently go there separately or together and change the decor and so on. She ended up with two apartment keys, while I only have one. We're guessing there's a duplicate key in a database somewhere or some such, but this is not really a bug report, because...

We think it's damned cool! :) In addition to being able to invite people into the apartment (which would be a great change), it would be really cool to be able to share an apartment without it being a bug. So anyway, here's a list of what we think would be cool changes to how apartments work:

1. Being able to share (roommates)

2. Being able to invite friends

3. Being able to put more than 10 things in there. You can't decorate an apartment with only 10 things. :)

4. Make some real furniture like couches, chairs, tables, beds and such available. If there is any other than the dressers, we haven't seen it. You know... stuff you can sit on.

5. Make furniture solid, so you can put the lava lamp or your favorite cactus on top of the dresser, not just next to it.

6. The upgradeable apartment is a good idea, we think. Some suggestions would be things like a balcony to the outside (imagine the nice view in Athen West), a second story (more of a town house I suppose) or maybe a bank-terminal interface.

We realize that these changes are largely cosmetic and don't necessarily get priority when there are gameplay bugs to fix, but we thought they would make the apartment much more interesting and would add greatly to the role-playing experience. You could invite friends over, eat take-out from bronto burger and talk about how much it sucked getting stuck in the last brown cave dungeon. ;)

Anyway,

my $00.02
