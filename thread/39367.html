One of the main reasons people pull BS missions is because its easy to get to... but in most cases the PRIMARY reason (at least for me) is because you're less likely to get those insanely huge missions.

For instance, Omni-HQ or Pleasant Meadows tend to be HUGE. The last 2 I did in omni-hq garnered about 11 million xp, each. Now, you're probably saying "wow... why are you complaining?" Well at the higher levels, most of the xp is gotten from the reward and keeping a team together for the 3 - 4 hours that such a mission sometimes requires is really hard. Literally, it took 4 mobs to raise the % by 1 point. That's 400 mobs and while it can be fun at first, after the first 2 hours people tend to get tired since you haven't had that break, people stop talking... and it drags on and on and on.

Pleasant meadows is another "OMG its been 1% for the past 8 mobs" type of place." I actually got 4.8 million xp off of one once... and it was a 50% mission at level 125... so you can imagine how big that was... when 90% of the creatures were giving you 8K xp.
