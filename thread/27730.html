Special Attacks for other professions
Here's an idea... we all know that Martial Artists can purchase and use special MA-only attacks. Why not expand the number of attacks and make them usable by all professions? These attacks would work a lot like the MA attacks - they would cost nano to use, would lock a skill on use, and could even give benefits to their users for short periods of time. Here's a couple of examples:

Bladestorm - for 1HE or 2HE users only. Attacks 2-4 times depending upon QL. Higher QL versions could buff attack rating for next normal attack. Locks Fast Attack. Weapon requires FastAttack for use.

Fore! - for Blunt weapon users. Automatic critical hit and stun. Reduces user's attack rating for remainder of combat (regained over time like when rez sick, but takes a lot less time to do so).

Hamstring - for anyone. Normal damage, but reduces target's running speed and initiative. Also increases user's defensive skills slightly.

Again, these are just examples. Feel free to add your own or flame me out of existence if you wish.
