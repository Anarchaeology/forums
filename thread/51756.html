If I recall correctly, when the whole concept of IPR was introduced, there was mention from the devs of a way to get additional IPR points in the future, probably via quests. Haven't heard anything on that subject for a long time though.

I, for one, would be very happy if that type of quest were introduced. Not for my own benefit at this moment (I'm an 85 adv who's very happy with his SOL Fire Execs right now ), but for general variety in the game. This way, a high level player that wants to do something different for a while could reset some of his skills and dump the ip into trade skills, have some fun creating stuff and change back later. Basically, it would enable people to keep their character interesting for a much longer time than they are now.
