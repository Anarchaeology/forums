Like Minded Omni's Sought
Although I am not a new colonist to Rubika I have been away travelling across the galaxy for a considerable amount of time. Needless to say I have missed much, from the current Notum rush to the collapse of the Council of Truth.

In my absence those that I knew have passed on or left Rubkia altogether. I am seeking fellow Omni personel who can fill me in on any interesting or necessary information that I must know to ensure I do not wind up on the buisiness end of a reclaim terminal.

But where are my manners, my name is Somantic and I the study ancient arts and teachings of the Temple of Kaman and I seek an organistation who I can pledge my devotion to.

I follow the teachings of my masters well and adhere to their philosphy of trust and honour. Needless to say in order for me to consider an organisation it would have to follow the basic principals of helping both the cause of the corporation and those in lesser circumstances, in addition to which have strong views about the "carefull application of the use of force".

Although my knowledge is somewhat outdated I believe that I could be as of use to the organisation I join as in reverse.

My feet set down upon the planet of Rubika in but two weeks time. I am awating the transport from ASUS which will once again deliver me to my homeland of Rome.

If you have any knowledge of any such groups or are a member of a suitable band that may be interested in my teachings then please enlighten me.
