Why do fixers pretend they're useless?
So, when i first started playing the game i was doing my very first mission. On that mission i ran into a chest that was locked. i didn't know what to do. So I went into the newbie chat and i asked around. That's when i learned about Lock picks and the breaking and entering skill. Well, i decided not to up my B&E but i got a lock pick anyways. I wandered around fo the next 40 levels opening every chest i saw with no problem. But a funny thing started happening around 50th level. I can't open the chests anymore with a 12 skill!. How insane. Well, i thought to myself, now I'm screwed. How will i eve know what's in this box. That's when it dawned on me, Fixers. Fixers have that skill as green. So i hopped on the looking for team screen and got me a fixer to group with. I rolled around the mission whacking everything i could find, and he opened all my boxes. Without him the mission would never have been such a success.

So you see, he was a 50th level box opener.

And fixers have it rough, Puh-Lease.

If you really want to feel sorry for someone, feel sorry for one of those classes that can't do anything but solo reds. Poor bastards.
