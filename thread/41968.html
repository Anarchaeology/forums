Yer, with multi weapons you need to remember that you must meet every single requirement on both weapons with one exception.

The multi-wield skill of the weapon in your off hand does not have to me met, only the one in your main (right) hand.

So, for example, I use 2x Rider Executioners.

With all the buffs available in use I meet all the requirements for both weapons, however the one with the lowest multi-melee skill requirement is the one that has to go in my Right Hand.
I can equip the higher QL Rider into my main hand, but as soon as I try and place another weapon in oy off hand (even though I meet all the requirement on it) my mail weapon is automatically disarmed.

For that Reason I have to use a QL171 in my main hand and I'm currently using a QL185 in my off.
