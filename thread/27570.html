A new line of useless and easily abused nanos!
Yep yep! Certain profs will soon be getting the Grid Spam line of nanos!

Well you know back in the year 2002 we had all that annoying spam, as in advertisements? Well, we can relive all that with the GRID SPAM NANOS!

Lesser Grid Spam: Anyone currently in the grid has their runspeed reduced a small amount for 30 seconds.

Greater Grid Spam: Anyone currently in the grid has their runspeed reduced a greater-than-small amount for 30 seconds.

Colossal Grid Spam: Anyone currently in the grid has their runspeed reduced a large amount for 45 seconds. As well, their FPS is reduced by large billboards falling from the ceiling.

Onslaught of Grid Spam: Anyone currently in the grid has their runspeed reduced to a 14.4kb modem crawl for 1 minute, as well as their FPS reduced to 3.5 by lasers, music, effects, billboards, and the like.
