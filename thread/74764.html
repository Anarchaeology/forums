Alphastrike wants you!
Alphastrike is recruiting!

We are a long established, mature and friendly organisation currently looking to swell our ranks. We enjoy all aspects of the game and have a core team currently participating in the booster pack, Notum Wars.

Although we have members from all around the globe the majority of activity takes place during European peak hours.

Our organisation is divided into two formal organisations on Atlantean, Alphastrike and Omegastrike, using a chat bot so that both orgs can communicate with each other.

Alphastrike has a level restriction at 75+ for new members, and a maximum of three characters per player. The aim with these restrictions is to keep a roster with active players, and where most members are within "teaming range". Omegastrike accepts new members at all levels (although the focus is on level 74 and lower)

Both AS and OS have a Department organisation structure to ensure a wide range of ranks.

A broad range of players ensures that there is normally someone available to team or offer advice.

For further information, please contact one of the following Officers in game:
Alphastrike
Cephous
Medicae
Skizzz

Omegastrike
Wrekless
Ankat
Jellomold

Theres no test to pass and the only criteria are to participate and have fun, we're waiting for YOUR application - become a Striker today!
