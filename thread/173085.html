Mission Terminals
Much like a slot machine at a casino the mission terminals come up with random missions at every click of the button. However more than once I have seen 3 missions I would love to take because they are all items I would like my character to have. The BAD part is once you pick one mission it closes the window.

It's a small suggestion but one that I feel would improve mission selection greatly. It wastes so much time trying to roll a good mission reward anyway, but when they are all on the same mission screen it kills me known I can only have ONE of them.
