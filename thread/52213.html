Fix Abilities! (Except Sense)
Currently, Abilities are broken. Why you ask? Because they don't title cap like every other ability they breed cap unlike every other ability.

Why you ask?

Because the Ability skills are NOT Title Capped:

1) A Player knows exactly what their abilities are going to be 10, 20, even 50 levels ahead!
2) Since all the abilities that DO title cap are affected by them (Another way that they are different!) they get raised! That cannot be intended!
3) Abilities are unaffected by any other ability in game, No trickle down for them!

What this basically means is that the designers made a mistake when they were initially coding the game. This is obviously not the way it was intended to work, and thus is flawed because I say so.

Take the case of how high you can jump. Agility and Strength are the prime reqs for that. Implants, buffs, debuffs - NONE of these things affect how high you can jump! Does that sound like any other ability in the game? NO... oh..... wait.... yeah it does. But still! Fix jumping! Its different! While you are at it fix the base abilites! They are even more different!

If its different then it must be a bug! Fix everything in the game that doesn't work the way every thing else works! Remove the aggro bar nothing in the game works like that. People who use it are utilizing a degisn flaw that was there from the start and are gaining an unfair advantage on everyone else! Don't you WANT to see the bugs fixed you silly people? Its different, so its a bug, so fix it!

Don't even get me started on Evades and Nano Resist. They are different too... therefore they are bugged, and must be fixed!

Is that clear? I hope it is. Please fix this and fix this soon!
