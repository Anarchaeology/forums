You heal pet heals once every 8-10 seconds.
If it doesn't immediately start healing, it means it has just healed another target.
There is a 5-6 second execution time for the pet heals.


Heal pets can be very stubborn when you try to change their targets when the target is not fully healed. A /follow or /behind command sometimes coereces them to switch.


Experiment with it a bit.
Get to know how they work and you'll get the hang of it.
