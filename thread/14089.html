Customization Ideas.
Bare with me a moment.

In Japan, among teenagers, there is an entire subculture, known as Gals. This group is broken up into smaller groups, such as Ganguro, and Kogaru (Ko-Gals).

These girls hang out in Shibuya and other major shopping areas, wearing the latest magazine fashions, chasing popular boys, keeping up with trends and shopping. (Huge platform boots, miniskirts, anything...)

Ganguro girls tan their skin as darkly as possible, then wear lots of often light eye and lip makeup, bleaching their hair to blonde. They look pretty rediculous to most people, but!

(Further information about Gals can be EASILY sought out on the internet.)

Makeup makes all the difference to these girls.

Makeup makes a lot of difference to a lot of people.

I propose a constantly changing line of fashion in Rubi-Ka. As well as makeup.

I want tanning, skin depth changing options, I want the ability to create a kogaru gal in Rubi-Ka. ;_;

I want platform boots, red leather miniskirts, shopping bags instead of backpacks, the whole works. I want my char to look like a shopping fiend prep rather than a warrior or some random homeless chick.

*end proposal*
