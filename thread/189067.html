The Way of the Ninja-Part 1
I wrote this story the other day when I was rather bored, and had nothing else to do. It is not as good as deacon's or any of the other great writer's on these forums but I believe it is ok.


It was a cold, dark night in Omni - 1 Trade, and there was no one to be seen for miles around. The mission terminals on the east side flashed every minute showing new information about new missions. The mission terminal was in use by a huge atrox wearing shiny, metallic armor. The atrox kept peering over his shoulder, but it was so dark he could barely even see his finger in front of his face. He finally found something that interested him on the mission terminal, so he started to read about the mission information. Out of the darkness behind the atrox, appeared a short, thin, man dressed in a tight black suit. He walked up behind the atrox silently, and killed him before he even heard a sound.

"Hmph, too easy," mumbled the mysterious man,"time for something new, I believe."

He walked up to the mission terminal and started searching for a specific mission.

After searching for a while, he finally found what he was looking for, it was an assassination mission to kill a man named Zaba Gruesgol. The mysterious man had an evil smirk on his dark face.

"The time has come....," he whispered.

After accepting the mission he took off to find the man he had been looking for for 15 years.

As he came out of the grid into Broken Shores, he was placed in the middle of a few people talking about ancient technology. He ran by them, nerves tingling, looking for the building that the man he was looking for would be in.

A priest popped out around a corner and told the mysterious man something about his sins, but he merely pushed the priest away and kept on searching.

He finally came to a buiding that he knew had to be it. He knew this because there was a bright red sign above the door that read "Home of Zaba Gruesgol, Rubi Ka's original, notorious black market leader!"

"Ha! Seems as if no one has enough guts to come get him, well then...."

The mysterious man entered the building without hesitation, only to find three huge atroxes standing in front of him....

This is just the first part of this short story I will prolly start to work on the second part if the people actually halfway like this story. Thanks much for reading! Btw, this is my opinion of what a MA should be like, although this character sounds more like a assassin, but who cares!!
