Line of Sight: Proposed changes
1) Relocate each player's "singularity" (the point from where all LoS calculations are made) to the camera, instead of the soles of their boots.


2) If you can see it and you're in range, you can shoot it.

3) If (2) applies and the target has the range, it can shoot back.


Any practical/theoretical reasons why this can't be done? Just seems it would get rid of some of the more, um, bizaare situations in the game...

FC's current system can't be purely top down - it takes account of even small alterations in the floor's height (one of the most glaring faults in the system, by the way - Steps of Madness is an excellent place to see this in action). All that has to be done is move each model's singularity to a more reasonable location, presumably by adding a "height" variable to each basic model and then scaling as needed.


Azzassimon: I assume you mean that the caster must have LoS to each potential target, not merely the entity the nano is originally aimed at?

If so, I suspect the mechanic of an AE nano is akin to a grenade or an artillery shell - the original nano/grenade/shell is directed at a single target point, where it detonates and send the effects out over the area of effect.


Of course, this would imply that a viable target would have tohave LoS to the point where the AE nano was originally aimed...
