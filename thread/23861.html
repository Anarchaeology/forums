Chemistry idea!
It's a green skill for agents. It's useless. How about we make it useful.

Agents could use it to build poison tipped bullets.

They would work like cluster bullets. You make the ammo and right click to fire it.

Although the damage would be even lower than the already low cluster bullets, the bulets would inject a long lasting DoT that will do much more damage than a cluster bullet if it runs it's course.

The bullet DoT would stack with our current line of DoTs. You are giving the fixer's stackable HoTs, why not give us these bullets as a stackable DoT. It still wouldn't compete with the doc's but it would be nice.

We could really use the extra damage. Even with our upcoming 14.2 nanos we won't be doing enough damage to take down a mob like we use to be able to do with our high level heal pets.
