Gathering of Souls promotion !
I dunno if people know this event, so i make that promotion for the Gathering of Souls.

It is a event organized by the Clanners, rather high level... I maybe forgot some of the actors as i'm just an helper in this event, but those I know who organized it are Miravlix, helped by Unitsi, Bearsoul and many clan leaders.

You may ask What is it It is a Free Buff and Implants Making for all. It takes place every Sunday, starting at 16pm GMT or 16pm EST, depends on the week so keep aware of the Sunday's anouncement, and it takes place on the West Athen road (you can't miss the place !). Everybody can have Masteries MC, T&S and all buffs you wanna, & implants asembled, all for free.

We are really waiting for you there if you need a buffon Sunday, it is the way to do :P

Also, on my own promotion for the GoS, i can make nanocrystals from your disc (up to QL 90 at the moment) for the only cost of the intermediate toolz needed (they are one-time-use tools). It is only for the GoS of course, usually you'll have to paid for the service


I repeat, i'm not at the origin of this event, just wanna promote it as only few people knows it exists...
