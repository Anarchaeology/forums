Bureaucrat petition to be heard by the dev(s)
Request for a chat with dev(s) about crat balance issues

I would like to make an open request for a chance for the devs to talk to the bureaucrat community. The reasons for the request is simple.
  • Make sure the dev(s) understand the issue.

More than once during the professionals weekly Q&A main issues the bureaucrat's question/issue got badly misinterpreted. (See also Example 1 and Example 2.) We want a chance to make sure both the devs and player community are looking at the problem from the same angle.

What may look like a neat 'feature' to a dev may be a future source of constant headaches for the players that will be afflicted with that feature.
  • Let the player community know that the problem is known.

It can't be fixed if the problem isn't acknowledged in the first place. This does not mean that the problem must be fixed. Even a simple 'We know this is an issue but cannot look into it at this time.' would do wonders.
  • Allow the dev(s) a chance to get ideas.

This is my personal theory. Part of the reason that crowd control continues to be nerfed(Yes, it is a nerf not a feature.) and that crowd control continues to be of extremely limited or of absoloutely no use in unique and high level encounters are because the devs don't know how to fix it.

On one hand letting us crowd control everything would make it too easy. Ok, we can live with that. On the other hand, what happens is that we end up with not being able to crowd control anything.

This is partially because that perhaps the dev(s) don't have any simple solution off-hand to fix the issue because it is a very fundamental one of crowd control not working at all. Give us a chance and let the bureaucrat player community work with you(the dev).

I have been working almost non-stop in trying to improve the balance of the bureaucrat profession for one and a half years now with the last nine months as a bureaucrat professional. There have been very little done to address the fundamental issue of crowd control functionality over that time. The bureaucrat community is tired, don't leave us out in the cold.

Respectfully,
Charles "Kithrak" Houston
Bureaucrat Professional
Level 199 Bureaucrat, RK1

P.S. A notification of acceptance or rejection would be appreciated. There is a Bureaucrat Convention scheduled for July 12th. The talk will be on your terms and is not a demand of any sort. If the BureauCon is too public we are open to other suggestions at your convience.
