Ideas to make life easier on Rubi-ka
A few suggestions I'd like to see asap:

1. World map auto centers on the player when first being opened.

2. Make everyone in your team un-tabable, shift tab for team mates even if they aren't on your side (Including their pets)

3. In the status bar at the bottom of the screen, make a small screen that shows your current coords somewhere near the compass.

4. Make team mates HP show up somewhere on the right side of the screen, my quick menu is filled with pet commands and with the nanoprograms menu down I lose about 1/4 of my visibility, unless I use the compacted screen, but then everything looks weird. Kinda hard to tell who needs healing.

5. New script commands, like randomize(1,100), if-then-else and a new variable thing, like %1- would mean the first variable and everything after it instead of having to use %1 %2 %3 %4 a.s.o.

6. Fix inits on bugged weapons. (BBI Faithful for example)

7. Lower "map nav" requirements. No one's willig to raise a blue map nav skill to 700+ anyway.

8. Remove Clan/OT shopping 1-50/50-100/100+ and replace them with one trading channel for each sides, make it automatically parse/display the item ql instead.

9. Make a swatch Beat watch at the status bar too, to allow players to easier make appointments.

End.
