Look in my sig

The breed bonus isnt that big, only 9 nanoskills. Nanomage soldier is good/better then solitus because:
- During lvling 1-160 you'll get to use WAY better shields/TMS
- During lvling 1-140 you'll have more treatment = better ims = better atk skills
- During lvling 1-189 you'll be able to NOT implant nanoskills giving room to imp locations like Shining RE, giving a lot more attack skill then solitus (unless they gimp their nanos of course)
- Nanomage looks cooler
- You get to use Shades of Luc (+8 to skills) and Jones Vest (+25 to nanoskills). Those are rare, only usable by nanomage
- More nanopool - If you can hold out 40s this means another TMS

At lvl 123 I was casting RRFE and using TMS X. Duelling soldiers at that lvl was easy for me, I could outlast everyone. At lvl 154 I could use OS, a nano most atrox never cast, most solitus need 190+ for. I always had superior attack skills due to not needing to implant them. Especially at the real low lvls (1-60) I was a killingmachine; great imps because of my high int and Shades meant great twinkability.

Okay, downsides, because -of course- we have those too:
- Less HP. It's still a lot because soldier adds a lot HP too, I really don't mind if I check what I get for it in return
- Less str/sta/agil. Hard at low lvls, costs loads IP.
- You need Shades+Vest to really get that edge about solitus. These are rare and/or cost a lot.

My advice; dont start one without Jones Vest, it wont give you an edge that much.
