Omni-Pol Bulletin 10-25-29476
13:46 October 25, 29476 RST


OMNI-1 (TOH) -- Omni-Pol has issued an all points alert concerning one Dylan Fukuda, a.k.a. Mctrout, in the wake of an audacious escape from the Maximum Security Psycho-penal Facility on Omni-Prime. The notorious trader skilfully eluded Omni-Pol forces after incapacitating several correctional officers and subsequently commandeering a Sol Banking Systems bulk freighter destined for Tawra. It is believed that Fukuda redirected the craft to Rubi-Ka 2, where he may have ties with the independent mercantile guild known as Conquistador Coffee Inc..

According to Omni-Med files, Fukuda displays many of the symptoms of paranoid schizophrenia, including delusional behaviour, confused thinking and frequent hallucinations. He has also been diagnosed with a battery of phobias including an irrational fear of heights, sex, constipation and being touched. It is further believed that Fukuda is in the possession of illicit nanotechnology, which may have been used in the breakout. He is presumed armed and should be considered highly dangerous.

Formerly a Staff Sergeant with Omni-AF, Fukuda was in the midst of serving a 1,040-year, non-commutable sentence for the murder of thirteen commissioned officers in March 29467. The criminal trial and subsequent court-martial hearing garnered considerable publicity in light of Fukudas exemplary service record and numerous distinctions obtained during tours on Hengeii, Boston Prime and Rubi-Ka. Fukudas defence had unsuccessfully argued that he was acting on direct orders to eliminate conspirators allegedly responsible for the massacre of 1,461 Omni-Tek soldiers in October 29466.
