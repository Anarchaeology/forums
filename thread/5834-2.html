Fist of all Gaia is more than jus an earth entity to say that something like that is dead you have to first understand that you are talking abou a concept. For a concept to be dead it i lost. The concept is not lost and thus the idea that it embodys is not gone. This has nothing to do with the matter at hand however. I belive that NPCs must be role played and used to further a plot. NPC stands for non player character. KEY WORD = character. A character is a member of a plot line that (through interaction) furthers the plot or story to allow for a final theme to be explored.

In this instance I think it is safe to say that the NPCs do not exist in the way Snarf was thinking. He meens that they do not interact as characters being directly invovlved in the story. Insteed they are a part of another story. The movies and the FC planed events that players have no influence over. These are meant to furhter the plot andthe expierience for the players. However in many instances they may as well happen in another world, a sort of Rubi-ka 3 where no players have any power. These are the infalable parts of storys that drive the game. In this sense, in the rubikka 1 they do not exist.

This is not to say that they do not have some influence on the story but rather that they being the only influence on it take away much of what role playing is. The big difference between role playing and story telling is that the GM allows the players to shape and be a part of the whole theme. In this instace is seems that we are living in a world where there is a story being told and we are meerly incorpreal (sp) bystanders who look on and are ocasionally lucky enough to veiw a byproduct of an envent on this rubika 3.
