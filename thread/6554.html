Funcom: Pet /Summon Proposal
Problem: Pet path finding issues commonly found inside missions and static dungeons are causing frustration, destruction of countless pets, and frequently resulting in the loss of the owners life.

Example: Pets sticking to game objects such as doors and timber and inside water or falling through deep caves (due to lag). Without the ability to remove them from the problem causing area it becomes necessary to destroy and recreate the pet. Added frustration comes when you dont notice the pet gone missing (stuck) and have to play the Wheres my pet game while feigning off hoards of monsters.

Solution: Add a  /pet summon  command giving owners the ability to summon all pets to the owners feet. This should not be limited in range (including zones) unless otherwise stated to why it should be. Code is assumed to be already available in game through the countless summon features present such as the engineers team summon and ARK player summon abilities. This should theoretically not pose any major design issues or long development time.

Note (1): Pet path finding should not be looked at as a disadvantage of those professionals in terms of like a Doctor not being able to cast strong nukes. This is a problem, and is not contained to just Anarchy Onlinealmost all games offline and online have pathing problems.

Note (2): The idea of including the ability to summon your pet has been talked and debated for some time, but unfortunately I see a lot of people give examples in regards to making the summon ability into a nano line. I personally strongly disagree with this because pet classes should not be hindered with executing a nano program (while they really should be doing something else) due to pets getting stuck on world objects.

Fact: Path finding issues have been present since the beta of Anarchy Online, and this simple solution can solve a very frustrating problem with ease. We play (and in this case Pay) games to have fun and pets getting stuck interrupts our ability to continue having enjoyment due to the frustration of dealing with path finding. While many professionals require enhancements, putting a summon command in game would more then likely require very low development time but would have a huge impact on three major professions.

Please support this proposal by posting feedback! Any official comment would be MUCH APPRECICATED. I hope that this ability can be put in with the next patch.

~Blacktie~
