To inject some realism into this discussion, agents can't even cast infuses at level 112 on their own, they can't come anywhere near to being able to cast them. They could have a trader wrangle them and a high level MP cast mocham's gifts on them... but this is true for all professions, including MP's. A low level MP could do the same thing to get high level pets.

Agents have a trick to being able to use high level nanos, that of going into False Profession Trader, ransacking and depriving a low level mob just before the false profession runs out, thus giving them a couple hundred extra nanoskill points for 2-3 minutes to cast their own nanos or those of another false profession.

This is really just a parlor trick, it's not realistically useful. It requires 30 minutes under FP trader to pull off, it has to be done in an area with low level mobs like a newbie backyard, which would make doing it and then travelling to some far off mission highly unlikely. And once the pets die, the entire routine must be repeated.

Not to mention the fact that the timing is highly difficult. Many times when trying to ransack and deprive, one fails. The monster resists the nano, or you get the timing wrong, or whatever. And if this happens, FP trader will run out and you'll have to start over.

And if you succeed, with 2 minutes or so after FP trader runs out, you will then have to cast your agent buffs like sniper's bliss, then go into false profession MP, and then cast your MP buffs. Do you know how long it would take to cast all those MP buffs? 3 different mochams, plus odins, and perhaps having to cast the infuses first to allow the casting of the mocham's? And then cast the 2 pets, and then cast the init buff on the attack pet?

Under false profession, with each nano taking 15+ seconds, and with only 2 minutes or so to cast them all in, you're quite likely to run out of time and not be able to get the pets at all. And then you'll have to start all over again with the whole routine.

This is certainly not a big problem, it is a rare occurance that some agent wants to kill a bunch of time messing around to temporarily get a few high level pets.

And, guess what? Funcom's overequipping changes will already solve this problem (if it even is a problem). Under the proposed overequipping changes, pet users will not be able to control their pets anymore if their skills drop below those needed to cast the pets.

So any agent who went through all of the above would find that he had 2 high level pets running around following him which wouldn't attack, heal, or follow any other command.
