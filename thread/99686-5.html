'Elicia, I need your help...'

The frail, shy voice of the girl woke her up from daydreams, and she immediatly answered.

'Why, what's wrong Jenny?'

It wasn't very often she and Jenny spoke, but she always enjoyed it. The young girl was so bursting with life and always so happy and cheerful. She would hate to see that something bad happened to her.

'Well, you see...there's this guy....'

She relaxed a little. If she spoke about a guy, then at least she wasn't in mortal danger or something.

'His name is Benjamin, and he goes under the callsign Fixerben'
'Mhm?'

Jennys voice was shy again, she seemed nervous.

'Well...I really want to meet him...'
'And how can I help you with that, Jenny?'

'I was thinking that perhaps you could make him meet you for some business or something, and I could be there too...kinda...co-incidentally...'

'What? No! I'm sorry, Jenny, but I can't help you there. I don't even know the man myself...how odd do you think it would sound if a perfect stranger asked him to meet her for "some business"...'

'But Elicia...I don't dare talk to him myself...he's so fine and my voice just disappears whenever I'm even remotly in the same district as him. Pleeeeaaaaseee....you gotta help...just this once?'

She sighed. She knew how it felt and she too had been her age once. And after all...she was such a sweet girl, how could she say no?

'Ok, ok...no more begging. I'll contact him for you....silly girl...'

'Yay!'

Jennys voice was bubbling, and they talked about the latest fashion from Miiir, and what Jenny would wear when she was going to get to see this Benjamin up close, and maybe even talk to him, and how fine he was, and how manly his voice was, and how her dad had urged her to take the initiative and how her pets always complained about the guys she'd been out with before and, and, and....there was no stopping that girl...

How on earth did I just say yes to this. Poor man will think I'm some sort of nutcase stalker...but hey, I promised her, so now I better do what I said I would do. Goddess...when will I ever learn...
