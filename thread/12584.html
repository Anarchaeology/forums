Give more exp in pvp zones!
I've seen this mentioned elsewhere, and I think it's a great idea.

Give players more exp for hunting in pvp zones than in 75% zones. This would give players a real incentive to go to 25% zones and hunt, and it would also give them less reason to complain if they got killed by the various griefers who are currently plaguing them.

Mort, which was one of the main clan levelling spots for players 70-120th level, is now mostly empty. What's the point in hunting there when you have to risk someone 50-100 levels higher than you coming and killing your whole group?

But if you could get considerably more exp for hunting there, it would make the zone much more useful and interesting.
