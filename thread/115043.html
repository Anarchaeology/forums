MD and CAS Merge!
Earlier today officers of MD and CAS got together, and unanimously decided that the decline of soldiers in each clan was unacceptable in the current Notum Wars. After 2 hours of heavy discussion an agreement was formed. CAS and MD will merge and starting this weekend will form a new clan, Mercury Dragons - AF (AF=Alus Force).

All CAS and MD officers will keep their rank, some will be promoted to a higher position.

We'll be working on merging Notum Bases as well. The CAS Armor will be renamed MD-CAS 29477 to note the new merger and the year it took place.

A few CAS members are required to go through anger management classes.

We will not incorporate any part of the current CAS site into ours. Former CAS members are not qualified to get free MD email addresses.

That is all for today.
