Fixer suggestion:
I noticed you plan to make chests give more exp...

How about doing two things to make agents and fixers a little more worthwhile in team missions.

1. Making chests harder to open. As someone said... Like someone on the fixer boards said: Why should anyone be able to max a skill and open a chest in a full hard mission? Where is the Fixer and Agent's skill that they can max to kill reds and oranges like a soldier or MP?

2. Don't just make chests give more exp overall.. but make chests give even more exp based on your B&E skill. Also give more exp based on how many tries it too to open, and weither or not a trap went off. This would obviously reward those who max their B&E skill, even those who implant it, as opposed to those who raise it simple enough to get by.
