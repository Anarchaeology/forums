I'd have to say that it has something to do with the leveling process being unbearable and people willing to do anything to avoid actually having to _play_ a character.

It also probably has something to do with the 'flavor of the month' syndrome. Towers in UO sold for thousands of dollars a couple years ago. I don't know if that's still the case.

Having gotten to approximately the same point in both games (25 in DAoC and 55 in AO) in terms of where the 'grind' starts, I can say for sure that I might have a character at lvl200 in the next 3 years on Rubi'Ka. I had difficulty even logging on anymore in the other game. It's all a matter of taste, I guess.

I for one love the potential that _this_ game presents. Every time I run into a bubbling zone wall and it won't let me through, I think 'I can't wait till they open that zone up'. I been thinking about how large the terraformed portion of the planet is. In all reality, it's got to be about as big as Alaska. That's pretty huge and more is on its way.

A game is only as small and boring as you choose to make it. I see level junkies all the time hanging out in Broken Shores because it's the 'only way to level.' You wonder if they've ever been to Perpetual Wastelands. Perhaps if AO were more popular, these people would be the ones paying 3K for a lvl 200 account.

My character levels. He's happy when he does because it opens up more to explore. I suppose lvl200 will come in time. I won't be so bored out of my mind that I'll have to pay for it, however.
