Nokkens can only be found in one small valley in Upper Stret East Bank (USEB for short)

It doesnt really matter which way ya coming from because ya can follow the bank of the Stret North or South until ya find the valley entrance. (You will know it when ya see it. Also a Nokken or two may be relaxing on the shoreline )
