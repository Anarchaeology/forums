Funcom pls read. Character Clones!!!!
We asked for a way to equip social stuff (like clothes) using a toggle or something to switch social clothing on or off so that we dont have to take the time to take all individual items off and rebuff, etc. All of friggin Rubi Ka looks like a darn military outpost. Funcom, you have the power to fix this by implementing a simple solution.. A social clothing tab where we can equip clothing to toggle on or off. The process is too long and tedious to change gear and sometimes involves buffing, etc, so people don't bother. The result is the stupid "I'm dressed for battle 24/7" look.

Right now, Rubi Ka looks like a full time military assault scenario. It gets pretty sickening. Since its way more convenient to not bother with the time and effort to change, people dont. If they let us have a social slot, then you would see cities look like cities instead of warzones 100% of the time.

I'm getting sick of seeing the standard weapon in hand, Senpai coats, tank armor, or armor gear on people 100% of the time. This would do a lot to help to give the game more of a pulse. Its hard to believe that this doesnt get the attention that it deserves because this would make things appear to be a lot less of the same old stuff. It would encourage social activity and its better for the game's health in the long run.

Until this gets done, I'll see you all in your military-outpost-looking-city on Rubi, Military-Outpost, Ka.
