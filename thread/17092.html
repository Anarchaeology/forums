Re: Oki, some of what I had said
quote:
Originally posted by NanoKiss

I agree that the prices shouldn't be SO Low that someone could make stuff and then sell it to the stores for a Profit.
I don't think that should always be true. IMO it has been a major failing of most tradeskill systems. The thing you have to realize is that some people are tradeskill FREAKS. They love that stuff more than anything else. More than fighting and gaining exp or anything.

I think that what they are doing is eliminating the job of the assembly plant. They are eliminating that step of the manufacturing process. In essense they are trading their time (spent finding all the components and trading other skills for having the skill to do the assembly) for the money and exp gained by producing the product.

I have always been a firm believer that if someone actually WANTS to run around stores all day and kill their eyes looking for components then they should be rewarded for it with profit and exp. While I could never do this, as I lack the patience, some people do.

I think that it should be set up like this. For any given item of a given QL, one should be able to purchase the base components for 50% of the retail price. After assembly of said item, it can then be sold to a vendor terminal of the correct type for 75% of the retail price.

This allows a player who has put in the time and effort of finding everything, then using their valuable skill to actually assemble the product, to receive a justified reward. The reward is not so extreme as to make it more desireable than hunting, but at the same time it is perhaps enough to keep some people interested in tradeskills. As it stands, for each category of weapons, there are 2, maybe 3 types that people will use. The rest are never used by anyone EVER. They don't use them because they are inferior. At least this way, these items can be assembled and used to *some* benefit by someone, even if it is just for selling to vendors. In cases where they are making desireable item, then they can still sell it to other players for a fair price if they want to, making more money.

I think that the whole tradeskill system could use an overhaul. It also goes without saying that all the bugs and broken recipes need to be squashed before anyone actually takes it seriously. New items were added before existing recipes were fixed. That's bad business. But at the same time I'm glad there is at least someone inside Funcom who is thinking about tradeskills enough to come up with some new items to add. But the whole system is really a shambles tight now. The broken recipes need to be taken out of the game until they are fixed. Nothing is so annoying as taking the time to find everything only to find that it won't work no matter what you do. Case in point. I wanted to make a Chemical Mausser for my fixer. So I decided to go all out and totally make it from scratch. I got my QL 21-40 Mausser Particle Streamer recipe and went to work. I rounded up every component and set to working. Much to my dismay I never got past the first step. No error message, no lack of skill message. Nothing. Luckily I hadn't invested in the Chemical upgrade parts yet or I would have been even more angry. I spent more money than a Mausser of the same QL could have been purchased from a terminal. That is no way for tradeskills to be setup.

Right now, the only trade that is worthwhile to be skilled in at all for the average character is Pharma for the Blood Plasma. Hopefully someone will take a long look at the tradeskills and make improvements soon. The system could be SO cool and deep, it's almost a crime that it is in such a state right now. Please fix it, we want to make things. Really.
