How to "Fix" the fixer
I recently read about the upcoming ways to "Fix" the Fixer in AO and I have a suggestion. Vehicals. Fixer's are meant to be good pilots. It goes along perfectly with our skill layout and the fixer "speed" we all know and love. Currently the vehicals in Rubi-ka are filling a woefully tiny nitch and serve mainly for transportation. When I first saw the intro movie for AO so many months ago my first reaction was "cool! I can pilot a gunship!" However as time progressed it seems that such things have not come to pass... I believe a good way to strengthen the fixer class would be to give fixer specific vehicals with mounted weapons of some type. Certainly this would require a very high skill to pilot such a craft but thats what were good at right? (all pilot skills are green!) Anyway thats my two cents and its also the reason I have played a fixer since the game was released. In the hope that "Someday" I would be able to pilot one of those nifty flying gunships.... perhaps my dream will become a reality?

A dedicated fixer.

1. I tend to stop reading things pretty quickly, losing interest in whatever was trying to be said.

2. It is NOT our job to come up with unrealistic expectations and blame FC for not fulfilling them. An *intelligent* person would know that you have to present a well balanced and thought out idea in order to convince a company to consider to enact it as a change in their game. Just throwing out vague concepts isn't going to accomplish much. They don't have the time or will to sit there and mull over everything we say, justify it, balance it, etc. They will look at it once, for 30 seconds, think about it being possible or not, and pass it on or dismiss it. Thus, you have to present your concept with enough thought that it makes sense in the game world, is justified by the work put into it, is doable in the current game setup, and is thought out enough to be brought in as soon as possible. The problem with these boards is that they are full of drivel and spam that make no sense, like "We want big fighting robots" and "We want porn socials". Both of those aren't gonna happen; robots because that takes away from the game concept and adds needless complexities to balance and skills, taking them beyond the scope they were intended for, and the porn because this game has a rating in the US that prevents any overt sexual presentation... the same reason drugs aren't popping about freely, as they are in the original description of the game world.

3. I present things that I think are necessary. At this point, I think that it is MUCH more important for FC to take care of the things they need to fix and have on their list than it is to bother with adding nice touches and extra for-fun things. If I do present an idea, it will be one that has a defined purpose, reasoning, rules setting, etc. Balance is somethign that always needs to be worked on, and can't be done by one person. Hence my devil's advocate position. I will point out ever flaw and weak concept that I see, because at some point it will come up. The sooner, the better. If the author has a way to fix it, a problem is solved, and I consider myself thanked. :-p
