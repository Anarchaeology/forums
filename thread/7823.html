Important Economy Suggestion
A friend, on leaving the game, made the following important comment:

"Much that I have enjoyed Anarchy Online it is lacking something fundamental that other online games have.

Most of the MMORPG are that Role playing, and newbies/Lowbies are actually useful to the economy of the game as they produce items and/or gather resources that high level players will pay good money for (more than the merchant will pay). Also craftable items ATM it is cheaper to get a Weapon found/buy it from a shop than it is to assemble it yourself in my belief that is Very Wrong!

Because the Economy in AO has lack of advantage to low players (e.g. low players cannot sell high level players useful items), and the crafting system leaves a lot to be desired. It leaves Role playing to a select few, my feelings are this - The game has become Hmmm if I do this this this and this I can use THIS uber item! and can kill that Supa Red monster in solo Mwhahahahaha. "

The point is that there is little to no incentives for high levels to interact with low levels for anything except friendship, charity, and/or twinking. There are a million reasons why lowbies need and want to interact with high levels but there is nothing tangible that they have to offer to the relationship.

As a level 63 character, I earn more experience and more credits in one mission than a level 10 will earn in 20 missions. They don't get items that I need and you'll rarely, if ever, see a high level ask a low level for nanos or tradeskills.

If there were any single imbalance in the game that needs to be addressed, this is The ONE (TM). [TM = trademark]
