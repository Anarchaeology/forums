MMORPG's , socializing, pvp, etc.. rants..
I first came to these AO message forumns when I was upset within the game about hunting grounds. Typical reasons.. I had reached lvl 100'sh and finding places to hunt are difficult and require a lot of TIME. Flying around in a Yalmaha, excited to have it mind you, for seemingly hours seeking places to hunt.. exploring new lands. The end results usually being a lot of death and finding virtually no one in any of these areas.

Don't get me wrong, zoning into 'medussa territory' near Belial Forest was a sight I won't soon forget, very nice looking area. It reminded me of playing Beta when I would log in and run around exploring, yes RUN around, not fly, for hours. There are many areas that are neat to see.....

Lets not forget WHY I ended up in these areas tho. I explore to find places to hunt, whether it be in a team, or solo.. Whatever I can find for steady exp works for me. When I log in, I want to be productive. So, I figured.. why not come to the message boards and ask around, see what suggestions people might have.

So... I read countless posts about 25% zones, no places to hunt, whiners, griefers, etc etc etc... I learbed that high lvl chaarcters go to Broken Shores. So, for the past 10 lvls or so ..lvl 110 now.. I have done missions in Broken Shores. In my opinion, doing missions over and over and over is one of the most mind-numbing things to do. But hey, like everyone else I wnet to BS and did the missions.... ok maybe not everyone else... like the couple people who post here saying "There are places to hunt, explore." ....... of course they won't share these spots with anyone...... and why would they....

So... enough babbling yet? Bah, no such thing.

I came here to hopefully discuss hunting areas with other players. Instead I am reminded of previous games I have played in the past.. Ultima Online for example.. where the emssage boards are full of 'pvp vs no-pvp' discussion.. talk of Care Bears ..damn I liked that cartoon too....... who want the 'easy life' vs those who earn respect through use of force ..respect? ....

I could go to the forumns of ANY MMORPG and find the SAME tone... *Insert Game Name Here* needs more pvp areas. *Insert Game Name Here* is full of bugs and exploiting. *Insert Game Name Here* has nerfed yet another profession. *Insert Game Name Here* has gotten me sooo fed up. *Insert Game Name Here* has ... you get the point ..I hope .....

People... Why can't message forumns be constructive sometimes? Surely these forumns serve a purpose othe than being her for the sole purpose of rants and flames... Perhaps I can read them sometimes and find helpful and informative threads.

So... Can anyone tell me of some palces to hunt for nice experience. Save points in the area, or in neighboring zones. 75% zones, political zones, mayhem.... Wherever.. Just some kindly given info on palces to hunt for say... my lvl 110 adv, would be much appreciated. I don't mind risk of death if there is opportunity for experience. A save point within 10 mins of the hunting area would be nice tho.

For instance..2200x2200 Belial Forest.. a nice save point within Mayhem... Convenient to the zone containing Medussa territory.. which I tried to hunt in.

Soem things to ponder...

Games are meant to be fun. If you find yourself playing a game and you are not having fun.........

MMORPG's are not fair to everyone. That is not possible. In many ways, they are a reflection of real life. many people are just not good.. Face it...


Ok.. fine.. I am done babbling for now... Oh yeah.. about those hunting areas.. Anyone?
