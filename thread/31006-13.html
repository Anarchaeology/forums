A rant from an adventurer
- not just regarding the patch, but about the game in general.

Okay, I got into the game a few weeks ago. You see, I was an UO addict back in '97-'98. I felt like looking into MMORPGs again. I decided on AO primarily for two reasons:

1. The PC Gamer article. Bugs are fixed? It's beginning to realise its potential? It's what MMORPGs should be? Sweet.

2. The free 1-week trial and no-cost game download. What? They're not forcing me to buy the game at full price and THEN pay subscription? I can just download it and play? Sweet.

So download, install, signup, etc. It went without a hitch.

I got into the game. I selected an Adventurer to play - you know, I had no idea which professions did what, I definitely did not feel like playing a "bureocrat" or a "trader". Heh. I thought the ADV seemed like a nice choice. When I was done with character creation, off we go into Omni Ent Backyard. It was night time on Rubi-Ka. I could hardly see anything, and began looking for some gamma correction options or whatever. Couldn't find anything, so I went ahead and turned up the brightness all the way on my pretty damn expensive monitor - there, that did it. Uhh, now I can see. It's a bit gloomy and all, but let's see where this takes me.

Then the unbelievable happened. A foxy chick showed up, ARK something-or-other. She started giving me directions. She was typing long sentences with perfect spelling and punctuation pretty fast, so I assumed she was a 'bot - but she turned out to be a real person, a volunteer! She was a huge amount of help - she was with me chatting while I was stuck in the game environment (Ent Highrise 1, behind the Surgery Clinic terminal) waiting for the petition to be answered she told me to put through. Well, the petition WAS answered in what seemed like a very long time - but nevertheless, someone CAME.

Now freed from bug prison, and educated in the ways of life in Rubi-Ka (having said goodbye to foxy ARK something-or-other), a dude walks up wearing some expensive-looking armor. He says to me: "Hi Paramp. You new?" I'm like, umm, yeah, what can *I* do for you? He said "Take this kind sir, and provide for yourself." - and gave me 50k. W00t!

So off I went to the training grounds for some exciting leet hunting - feeling VERY VERY good about the game.

Time was passing, and I did not yet make any friends. I did not really mind, I was having fun. After the third or fourth mission though, it started being MUCH less fun. Sure, I was advancing in levels, but I also messed up my character pretty bad. I put IPs into whatever I needed at the moment. I found an assault rifle, so I trained myself in that. I bought an ugly melee weapon, so I trained myself in 2HE. And so it went.

Doing missions after missions, I was starting to feel dizzy, and a little numb. I mean, these stupid missions are ALL the same. Is there more to this game???? You enter the "mission", play some gimp-quake with a bunch of monsters, go to the farthest room, do what you need to do, ding, get on with the next one. I mean, how can one still have fun in this damn game after a week or so? Then salvation came. I hooked up with some people, got into my 1st team, and we went hunting! Not spawn camping, mind you, but hunting! Wow! The game's beautiful, I knew that from my missions that took me to places like Rome. But those sunsets! Those trees, rocks, water! Man, I felt like home again.

Not to mention 14.2 that came out and let me reset all IPs, allowing me to make my adventurer a shotgun-wielding PH34RS0M3 level 20-something. I felt like I was ready to kick some serious ass.

But now, closing in on level 60 or so, I am getting bored with the game again, and it looks like it's for good this time. Reasons?

1. My character is weak. Sure, I got all the implants. Sure, I switched to dual pistols using two IPRs. Adventurers are so damn weak that we can hardly solo missions our level, and we're not a useful addition to any team. Heh, right now, I can get into teams of course because:

2. Rubi-Ka 2 is totally empty. It can take an hour or so to put a decent team together.

3. When you do put a team together, what do you do? You either do team missions or go "hunting" which really means "spawn camping" when you're experienced enough. But these suck!

4. Team missions suck big time because they are BORING and REPETITIVE. They are purely a waste of time. You spend some time getting to the boss killing everything you encounter instantly, you hardly pick up any chest loot because you have two ninjas in the team (the server's empty so you don't have a real choice in teammates), when you get to the boss you fight it hard, some possibly die, the boss gets ninja-looted, and if god forbid it's YOU who gets to the boss 1st people don't believe you that all you found on it was PURE JUNK. On to the next team mission then? Hell no, you don't want to waste another hour doing exactly what you have been doing before: silly running around in a silly computer-generated maze with a silly layout, insta-killing every mob then getting to the boss who finally puts up a tough, sometimes impossible fight. So you go "hunting".

5. Hunting aka Spawn Camping. So you hang around at NW mines or the VB camp, killing everything in sight. Sure, it's fun for the first time, but after that it feels like work. "I'd like to gain a level or two, so I'll hit the camp, get into a team, spend an hour and there, I got 20k IPs. W00t!" I mean, give me a break. It's like doing missions with a pretty backdrop but even worse loot.

6. Static dungeons or quests. I have yet to find a team that's willing to hit Steps of Madness with me. (You know, server's empty blah blah.) But hey, there's this ALVIN guy, it sounds like fun! So I look him up get the quest, and then what? Oh, right, hunt robots. But where? Oh right, Galway Shire. So let's kill those Waste Collectors, since they're gray like the mech dogs but much easier. But hey, they do not yield ANY Alvin loot. Umm, ok, so there's a reason that the 15 people in the area are all killing mech dogs and leaving alone waste collectors? Wow, look at that level 130 dude, attacking a mech dog, running around the playfield to draw 5-6 adds, then proceed to insta-kill each and every one of them. Mechanized Guarddog was hit for 1330 points of melee damage. You received 1 xp. Fun fun fun (not)! So it's time I get me some. When I can actually find a dog to kill, I mean. It takes some time, but you CAN do it, spawn's pretty good. :-) I have now killed maybe a hundred mech dogs, and found four of the neccessary eight Alvin pieces - but I found one twice, and could NOT pick that up, nossir, you can't pick up two and trade, because they are UNIQUE NODROP. So I actually found three that I can use. So let's see, if every 25th mech dog has a random item, how many do I need to kill? 8x25? Nah... There's a good chance that you will find all 8 items if you kill 25+(25*2)+(25*3)+(25*4)+(25*5)+(25*6)+(25*7)+(25*8 ) doggies. That's only 1100! If you're lucky you get them sooner - if you're unlucky, well, you don't get them EVER. Sounds like fun? Damn right it isn't.

Soo... why am I ready to quit?

My character's weak compared to other professions. I'm NOT starting fresh with a MA. I'd like to have fun with the guy I invested so much time into. Oh, but Funcom's fixing adventurers in the next patch? Ok, I'll hang on and see what happens in 14.4 - but I'm not holding my breath. I mean, they've improved the damage shields by creating a new line of nanos which make the old one obsolete and worthless? I mean, they now let us charm leets and not zone with them? (Not like I would EVER want to charm a leet - but if I did, maybe I'd like to take it to places.) I mean, we don't get to fly EVER? I mean, pistols are FINALLY based on Ranged Init instead of Melee? I mean, if I want to get the new nanos I'll have to do team missions AGAIN and AGAIN and AGAIN?

Even if my character was on par with the others, what fun would there be? With RK2 as extinct as it is, PvP is not an option. Team missions are not an option. Static missions, ahem, if they will be like the Alvin quest, well, no thanks, don't waste your (and our) time Funcom. Yeah, so maybe I'll hunt and do static dungeons - but wait, can't I do just that in any Bioware game? Okay, I don't get the massively multiplayer angle there, but some of them ARE multiplayer if I want them to be. Maybe not massively, but you have to have some nerve to call RK2 massively multiplayer... And they have CONTENT! In fact, CONTENT is what they are about.

Obviously a huge amount of work and creative juices went into creating AO. The game world is beautiful, it's diverse, some sceneries are gripping. It has huge potential. But right now, it stinks as a whole.

Willing to stick around for 14.4 and hoping for a miracle.

Paramp, level 56 adventurer, RK2
