The use of the term "twinking" really doesn't relate to AO and has bothered me for some time.

Twinking is a term that started years ago in reference to the act of taking much higher level characters and using them to acquire items for their younger characters that those younger characters would never be able to get for themselves.

Given the fact that equipment is not unique in AO an is only scaled by skill requirements, you cannot "Twink" here. You can, however, equip items well above your level.

What is the difference?

Twinking has always carried with it a conotation of cheating. You use your high level character to allow your low level character to get something that they shouldn't be able to obtain.

Equipping very high level items in AO doesn't have the smallest hint of cheating to it. EVERYONE of any given level can equip the same QL stuff if they spent enough time planning the right implants and getting the right buffs.

When I hear people cry and whine about twinking and "overequipping" in AO, I want to smack them. They too could use high level equipment if they had the intelligence, creativity, patience and desire to do so.

Many people say that they can't get high level buffs because they don't know anyone who has them. Well, how the hell do they think other people got the buffs? MAKE FRIENDS.... ask around. PAY for them.

No matter who your are, what class your are, what level you are....everyone can equip items far above their level. This is not twinking. This is not overequipping.

The ONLY area that is of concern is profession balance. Some professions inherently have the ability to buff themselves while others have NO ability to buff themselves. If people want to complain about this then that is fine. I dislike the fact that I don't have a single solitary equipment buff or ability buff as a fixer (well, ok, I have ONE and one only...minor suppressor, a level 1 nano). However, I can still get buffed if I try hard enough.

So I say, if your smart enough and resourceful enough to equip yourself with the highest level stuff available...kudos to you! Go do it and reap the rewards that come from effective planning.

If you're not, then don't come crying.

Rather than speak in theoretics or generalities, I thought it would also be good to give a very specific example of equipping at the highest QL available at a given level.

Two weeks ago, I was somewhere around low 90's in level. It was time for me to upgrade all my equipment.

How do I start?

I go to the mission booth!

For two weeks, all I did was go to mission booths, set the sliders to 100% bad, 100% money, 90% Difficult.

These settings gave me mission items of QL 142. I didn't select 100% difficult because I couldn't equip the QL 166 armor that 100% setting gave me.

It took a few hours every day to page through missions to find what I needed, but I found the items. As either the item to "find" in the mission, or the reward I found missions for every single piece of Flowers Armor, a Hanshi Primus coat and Implants for every single slot.

Then I ran. I ran like hell. I blitzed those missions, sometimes dying up to 6 times per mission before I found where the item was hidden, but I got them. I also got a hell of alot of credits for completing the missions as well.

There I was then. A full set of armor, a full set of empty implants.
I took the money I made and bought all the clusters I needed. Perfectly planned cluster sets that would raise all the skills and all the abilities I needed as a Fixer.

I then set out to equip all this crap.

I found a doctor for the +80 Treatment buff and Iron Circle.
An enforcer in my guild gave me a +18 Essence buff.
I paid an agent for feline grace.

Using these buffs, I equipped all my new QL 142 implants.

With those implants and those buffs, I put on the armor.

Bingo...done.

Was it alot of work? Perhaps, but it became a sort of quest for me. Was it twinking? Hell no! There was no cheating or exploiting involved in me getting this stuff.

Someone might say...well, you got those buffs! No kidding. Why do you think buffs exist? Is there any reason anyone else couldn't get those same buffs? Is there some magical forcefield surrounding certain people that keep them from getting buffed? No. Anyone can do it if they have the desire.
