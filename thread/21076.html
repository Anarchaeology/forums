Lev Rivers - The Adventurer Action Hero Returns (comedy)
".......So there i was, staring at a bunch of "Dance_Champion 'Cosmik' Dracai" Clones who were about to dance at me provocativly in their thongs until i passed out. It was their way of attack on this freakish world. It was that moment i realized 'Maybe its time to give Rubika another try....'. A war between two vicious opposing factions, huge killer brontos, cyborgs, bald tailors, traitors, backstabbers and womanizers HAD to be better than my offworld adventure so far....

At reaching that decision, i instantly fled from the 7 or 8 Cosmik clones and made a break for the old junkyard that was where all the old broken down ships were kept. After trudging over several old Omni-Tek freighters, i found a serubian fighter jet, still in pretty good nick. After disregarding the "Made in Tiawan" seal on the door, i jimmied it open and got inside. Several minutes later of spading out the dung from a leet family that had made this ships interior its home for months by the looks of it, the ship was seeming more promising.
"K.." i thought "..time to see how this baby works..."
To my surprise, the ships power batteries were still nearly 2/3rds full, probably enough to reach another system.
I pressed the boot up sequence button and the whole console lit up.
"Windows 30,000 OT-Edition" read the screen
"Great" i thought... "Lets see,..." I pulled down the opening menu
"Ship statistics, New Ship Settings, Edit with clipboard, Print Statistics, Import and Export, Start-Up Properties....a-ha!"
I clicked open the new menu
"Hmmm, 'type in G-force'......1.3........"
" 'select planetary type .... Moon Rich Oxy..."
" 'select velocity. type in 'gazillion'.....what?!!!? 'INVALID SETTING'?!!!??!"
I hit the machine.
"Help File. lets see, Set Up routines, Charging the Engine, Charging the Engine's Fizzler, Fizzling the Charger.....oh godDAMN this is annoying!!!"
I quickly used an old trick i'd learnt on good ol Rubika, i took a blaster and shot repeatedly at the console. It soon came around to my way of thinking.....
Off and away, i soon left that awful little cesspit of a planet behind, and with it, all the disturbing imagry of Cosmik in thongs i had endured....


I just bearly made it to the Ross Station, self named after the guy with a bigger ego than ol ladies man Ambrosia. The station itself was exactly what everyone had come to expect from good ol OT. Grubby, delapadated, not the most biggest or well known station so it got the lowest funding possible. At docking my ship with its rich smell of Leet dung wafting out of the airlock, two Pol guards were there to greet me, as is standard for any munchkins that arent granted a pass code.
"Woah! What HAVE you been doin in there? It smells like my ol grandma's dirty washing laundry bin!"
I grimaced a frown at him.
"Yeah, well i'd of *preferred* to see your granny in thongs too....."
"What??!?!" said the guard half shocked and puzzled.
"Oh, nothing...." i replied.


The stations plaza of bars and cantina's had an unusually large amount of Troxes around, much more than usual. Walking into the "Geeet Yeur Hanover Heeer" Bar, i noticed the poster on the wall
"TROX FASHION SHOW - TODAY IN HANOVER BAR"
"Great..." i thought. "Troxes in all sorts of clothes....." I shuddered at a thought ".....dresses too...ugh, i think i'll definitly have to be far away by then!"
I bought my drink of choice, a Party Mixer, whisked, not beaten, and then found the interspace comm terminals.
The computer had an attitude, probably due to the company it had encoutnered thru the course of its life working here in a backspace bar.
"Gimme your name, Fex...."
"Rivers, Lev"
"You call THAT a name? Pah....ahh well. Who'd you wanna talk to, like its worth bothering...."
The computer was surprisingly plesant, more so than most terminals in Tir back on good ol Rubi K of A.
"Erm, let me think......try.... Baxie or Tappert, whichever you can contact first...."
"Processing, like life could *get* any worse...."
After a few seconds of taxing the poor terminals brain power, it finally bleeped and found one.
"Solminski, Tappert : Occupation - Tailor, Residence, Omni-1, Southern Province, Rubika."
"Wow!" i thought and then said "I didnt realize you backwater terminals out here had so much info beamed to you.
"Hell yeah, i have lots of dirt.....urm.....info..... on people.....go on, try me!"
"Um.....okay......gimme something bad about Baxie."
"Processing" this time the terminal chuckled as it did so, seemingly menacingly as if it enjoyed the evil side of life. These old x-5 terminals really were getting old and eccentric...
"Found Baxie - Hmmm, wow, poor sucka! His mom has been covered up by Inter-Ops for him! She was actually a dumb old Atrox!"
My eyes buldged "REALLY? YEW!" i screamed, making nearly everyone in the bar look around. I then frowned in puzzlement "Actually, *how* is that possible, i mean they have no......actually......*dont* answer that! i *dont* wanna know!.......Just get me thru to Tappert."
The terminal grumbled and did its job, finally.
Tapperts old face appeared on the screen.
"Lev! But..... i ... i thought i saw your mangy body drown in Newland lake?....."
I smiled "Yep, and if you go back out there, they say on a clear day with clear water, you can still see my old body floating to the surface with my copy of 'Hot Solitus' magazine in my back pocket. I needed to fake my death, things were getting 'heated' back there."
"Really? I knew you had quit as an assassin and a few old 'friends' were tryina settle old scores, but i didnt know you had that many enemies...."
"Well, it doesnt matter now Tapp." Tappert nodded, i squinted and shielded my eyes immediately..
"AGH! DONT *DO* THAT!!!"
"huh? What?"
"Nod! Your bald head reflected the light and nearly blinded me!!!"
"Oh, sorry...."
"....well, you can make it up by coming and getting me..."
"Ah,okay, will do. Slotine's been busy all week designing her own range of clothes, 'Slotiine Simple'. Its basically string. One strip of string covers all the bits in the right places, it costs nothing coz so little material is used, and because we're calling it designer clothing, we can sell it for thousands of creds at a time! Ha haaaa!"
I frowned in bemusement.
"Tapp?"
Tappert stood down from his ego trip of plans to topple Miir.
"Huh? Oh sorry, yes yes, will do. I'll go and get my keys to 'Old Slow Coach' now and come n get you."
Old Slow Coach was a 60 year old freighter, made by Skoda. It was as reliable as Tapperts hair was to show up at a barbers.

After cutting off the terminal, i clicked my head implant to give me the time in my right eye. 13:42.
I groaned.
"It'll take him 8 hours to get here...... the Trox fashion show will start in 4....."

It was going to be a loooooong day.

But i knew, i was going to be back on good ol Rubika. And what i didnt know, was soon my old friend Dave Duster would offer me the position as manager of Baboons, i'd be holding the biggest party ever on Rubika, and once again, i was back to being the man of adventure......
