Varity, and why this game needs more of it..
Varity, and why this game needs more of it..

Kind of big, sorry, I split it up, please please skim though at least one section, and leave your comments. I feel these are very important, very basic, problems that can be fixed very easily.


I am a vet of MMORPGs. Ive truly found the best MMORPG with Anarchy Online. Every game I play, I look at it, and I see all these stupid things that, I, a simple game player could of designed or did allot better. I cant say that about AOs game play, interfaces, cities, skills systems, weapons, items and monsters. However, the game has little in varity, lots of people quit to go play everqust or even doac because of the huge lack of varity. This was a problem for the popular internet RPG Diablo 2, there seem to be certain items people go for, now, and their whole goal in the game is to get a full set of MK II armor and a QL 200 X-3, Im not dissing ultimate items like dragon flesh armor or blood bats or even Edge of the Tarsique (duno if thats spelled right)

Weapon Varity:

All soldiers use a Nova flow or Division 9 at low level and switch over to a Custom Heavy Suppressor or a Flash Point Nova flow combo, and its relatively easy to switch between the two options (Flash/Nova, CHS) . The reason this is a problem for me is, there is no option for an Soldier (this applies to many other classes, fixers and streamers, enforcers and beams, agents and x-3s) unless they want to gimp them selves. I think there should be a balanced selection of items, which the players can debate over, such as the fast and light with high crit, or the hard and slow with low crit. This just isnt how it works, for agents, the X-3 is the only true option, it hits at a medium speed, has huge critts and huge base damage. (off topic!! I think they should carry out grippos out to ql 200) This is not just a problem for the weapon system but its a problem for visual appeal. Many soldiers like the look of pistols, or a rifle, but are forced into using one of the primary choices or being gimps. Same for enforcers, and enforcer may love the look of a katana or mace, or something other than a beam, but the truth is, a beam or the led pipe/notium staff combo is, and always be better than any other weapon for the enforcer (with the acceptations of non drops and PVM only weapons). I could carry out the examples but Im sure every one here can relate to this problem with their class.

Armor Varity:

This is another big problem; most of the best armor can be blitzed by characters and sold in bundled sets for credits, with the exception of dragon armor and MK II. Starting at level 50+ I think there should be non drop armor that far out weighs blitzed armor, or smithed armor for that matter (the current carbonum fad). And there should be a large varity in looks and mods. Lets keep In mind that this is an RPG and in RPGs you r o l e  p l a y .. remember thats what RPG stands for? I know lots of people forget that, and play these games as a competition to see who can get the best stuff the fastest, and thats just not how there are meant to be played. A desert player should be able to find special non drop armor in the desert that looks similar to nomadic armor, and has a nomad looking cloak to wear it with, wearing tank armor with a nomad helm just doesnt look right. And if youre a desert character, what in the heck are you doing wearing MK II? There needs to be a larger variety of high quality non drop armor a for a larger level range and a larger variety in looks and mods.

Cars and Vehicles.

I think this is an obvious subject think there are to many yalms? And whose idea was it for them to be low as QL 30 in and in shops!! There should be a vehicle over haul, yalms shouldnt be available in shops starting tomorrow, their speed should be fixed, and more, interesting, cars should be available from boss only drops and as quest rewards (not missions.. big.. long.. lots of walking quest..) . I know some people have seen those cars on the pads in Tir, and said, damn I wish I had one of thoes, where are those? And those giant cars you see every where? Where are those? I hate it when a game designer puts items in the back ground and not in the game. The vehicles should be greatly enlarged (If their reduced size is to reduce lag, then have it as an option in the menus or as an optional mod/patch) and six seat carriers should be put in the game for people doing team missions (ever have a team break up before they ever get to the mission?) Funcom, please hear my cry and fix this, this is just plane wrong to have the most advance mode of transportation available to some one at level 25. The only real way to fix it, is like is said, add more flashy, cars and larger cars with interesting options like a radio (plays ambient club music and what not) and able to seat multiple people. But if you fix anything with Vehicles PLEASE make an option so they can be seen at their actual size and not as some messed up looking shoe box buzzing around. ( I reallllllyyyyyyyy want a car people can go into and have parties with music, a dj booth and what not, and make it so the space ship could move with the people in it (not nessacarly a floating plat fourm, but when some one joined youre team they could "zone" into youre space ship which would cut down on the lag) , and i would like these things to be RARE, Like heavily padded over coat rare only no drop)
