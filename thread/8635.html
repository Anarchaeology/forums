Just to give a little more info to those of you wishing to join the ranks of Division 9....

We are on RK 1..we have no plans to expand to RK 2, as that would stretch us kind of thin.

In order to be accepted into our training academy, the character you apply with needs to be a minimum of level 25...if you are under this level, your application will most likely be rejected, however some exceptions are made...and you are always free to reapply once you have reached 25th level as well (if you weren't that high when you first applied that is...heh). If you apply with an alt, but your main is over this limit, that isnt considered...once you are in, however, alts are allowed even under the level 25 cap...

Our recruitment opens quite frequently as we graduate folks out of the Academy and into the main unit...so if our main page states that the academy is full, keep checking (at least every couple of days), odds are you'll be able to submit an application soon.
