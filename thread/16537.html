13.92 patch notes (going live Wednesday March 20)
Hey hey,

Here I am with a bag of patch goodness for your hungry appetites (hey, its lunch here. Im hungry).

The 13.92 patch is as follows:

-Both missions and bravery tokens have been made unique. This means that you may only have one of these in your possession at all times. IMPORTANT NOTE; if you have tokens in your inventory at this time, you need to upload these before you can get a new one.
-Chest loot in missions has been changed. There should now be a larger variety of items dropped.
-Ash Andersen in Smugglers' Den no longer has any assignments to give out. This is due to some players not fulfilling the mission to his liking...

Downtime will be 3 hours. Come join the fun in #anarchyonline (irc.funcom.com). Ice cream for everyone.
