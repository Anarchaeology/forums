Melee characters = screwed by unbreakable root
As a player with both an Agent and a Martial Artist, I can tell you firsthand that if there is no way to break roots in PvP, roots = certain death to melee opponents. Not "advantage". "Certain Death". Is this intentional, FunCom?

At least with the current "system", if my MA gets rooted, he can spend the next few seconds debuffing and try to break out. It doesn't always work, and I don't get to attack while breaking the root. Then I have to try and run down the rooter. If I can't break the root the first few times I try, I usually die. If they root again (rinse repeat) we have détente.

My agent loves his root. And my MA is helpless against it. MAs (and perhaps melee ADVs...can't really speak for them) might as well retire from PvP. Half of the classes in the game have roots.

Assuming there will be no reliable "general" method to break them after this "fix", I offer up a few humble suggestions:

1) Fix nanoresist before implementing such an imbalance. Duh. This may simply mean keeping the current NR calculation method but adjusting root resist %'s. Or it may mean a nanoresist calculation overhaul. As it is now, NR is just an IP black hole with no justifiable reward. With maxxed (dark blue) NanoResist, I resist what...5% of nanos? Yaay. And for pete's sake, have the QL of the hostile nano figure into the resist %...in PvP there is NO POINT to casting anything other than the lowest level root vs your opponent: Just as effective, and saves lots of nano for nuking or chain rooting + shooting.

2) For MAs, why not simply make the controlled destruction line break roots just like the ENF rage line does? It fits ("focused chi"), and has an inherent penalty in casting it (no nano skills for 20+ seconds).

3) Give MAs a line of major NR buffs, self-only. This also fits the class description. "Bane of nanocaster classes", indeed.
