Results of the Crowd Limiting Poll (so far). Funcom we need a response!
The community was asked if Crowd Limiting was a good change or a bad change, and whether or not if should be removed or left in place.

Of the 113 people who voted thus far:

17.7% voted it was a good change.

82.3% voted it was a BAD CHANGE

The link to the voting is in my sig. But this brings a good question into play. Considering through the course of the voting the %s stayed relatively the same, why is Funcom continually making changes that only please 17.7% of the community?

Why do you continue to push a concept that is hated to the point of puking (on my part) by your player base? I would rather swim in raw sewage then play this game with crowd limiting. I have no plans to reactivate my account simply because this BS concept is in game still even though it's despised.

We need a response.
