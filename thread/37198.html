Redruum is very hard to read in its current form, it make my eyes go dizy just looking at the page.

My advice is Light grey text on a dark grey background is a bad choice stick to either white or black.

You need more spaces to break the words up so you can tell one line from the next. You need to make the story look to flow on the page. Break the mass of text up by using paragaphs.

Also the page is quite long dont be scared to spead even one chapter across a few pages this may help to break the text up.

Also you need to read your own story, it lacks the right sort of pace. In places you use commas to much, I know you do it to pause for effect but to many in one line kills the picture your trying to create.

In other places a lack of them causes you to rush where you really could use a pause.

Also you pose to many questions to quicky you need time to work with the idea. Consider the read has to have time to ask himself the question. Often the reader needs an answer to those questions or at least give him something to work with, to get his mind working on what might come next.

lol I am starting to sound like my old english teacher, red if you make the text more readable that would be a good start.
