Thinking about our combat bufflines, at the lower levels we really only have the gun/weapon enhancement lines, which aren't all that good as they're often specific to one damage type. The buffs that affect your attack rating, Extreme Prejudice and Slayerdroid Transference, are only available at the high levels.

Perhaps the simplest solution to the "Help! I've been aggroed and I can't fight!" problem would be to extend both these lines across the entire level range. Allow people to choose which route they wish to take (pistol, MA or grenades) and make the appropriate buffs available. Mind you, a few decent grenade launchers wouldn't hurt either...

I'd had some thoughts along the lines of determining your attack rating partially from tradeskills, so that one line might use 67% MA and 33% Mech Eng doing melee damage, another might use 33% Quantum FT and do Radiation damage, and so on. The upside is that tradeskills are green, and it would provide a genuine reason to max them. The downside is that the MA buff from transference would only give 67% of its benefit. I don't know whether this would end up raising or lowering your final attack rating, and in any case it would potentially mean re-working the existing transference nano.
