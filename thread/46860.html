Area Transitions
I've been playing for several weeks now, and in that time I've found very little to legitimately complain about. I noticed something today, however, that was fairly irritating. I'm exploring a lot more of the map as my character levels and one of the impressive features of this game, to me, was the continuity of the world.

To get to the point, I'm finding these invisible zone walls with no geometric facade to justify my lack of advancement in the forbidden direction. Behind Borealis the hills span on toward Bliss but you can't cross. The bull pasture (or whatever it's called) near Bliss doesn't cross to Athen Shire/Holes in the Wall either. It's just an invisible wall in the middle of the freaking desert. I'm not sure if there's a way to remedy something like that at this stage of the game, but it would certainly be nice.

Maybe it's an Omni-Tek conspiracy. Maybe they seek to defile the gorgeous sunsets and magnificent steppes by cleverly placing transparent walls in hopes that meandering passersby will get bloodied noses from walking into them.

In the mean time I suppose I'll just have to figure out what puzzle pieces are connected and what ones aren't.
