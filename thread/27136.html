if this game had been constructed so that a larger portion of the players did pvp then it would be possibel. See it this way - a person that has 1300 pvp-score (check pvp-score-thread if you are lost here) cant loose anymore points. That means that if someones fight this fellow and wins, points will be created/brought into the game. Its just like a foodchain =) the points can only be created once people that cant lose points lose, since in any other fights points will be transfered not created. (Note that due to the way the formula is constructed there can be "created" and "destroyed" pvp points when people in different multiplier areas fight each other, but this will imo happend very rarely so the only real way to get high titles is by having a large ground of non-title pvpers that can "give life" to a mass of freshmans and so on. Hopefully 14.2 and the oe changes will make more people join pvp. Besides that I hope that everyone reads what cosmik wrote:
