HOGG and EXPLOITING!!
Let me tell you this now, I absolutely HATE cheaters/exploiters. I also hate that there is an XP tag to ANY NPC. I'm sick of /petitioning about an NPC being killed (which I despise - there is no CHALLENGE in it), and being told different lines from the book. One GM told me it was an exploit to kill an NPC. One GM told me it was ok to kill it but not to camp it. One GM told me it was ok to kill and camp it.
This is the one thing (and only thing) that would make me quit AO. I love this game to bits - apart from the fact exploiters/cheaters/lazy people kill these NPC's. It makes me SO damn furious.....

I was running around north-east trade (near the prison) with 2 of my friends, doing some newbie missions to level them up. We were on our newbie chars, (our main chars are MPs - level 121 119 and 106).

We noticed a battle happening (noticing that it was damage done by a HOGG and inside town) and we decided to investigate. We saw ;
http://www.anarchy-online.com/chara...ame/eugenichogg &
http://www.anarchy-online.com/chara...me/dgenratehogg
there killing 'Iziris Agathon'. We noticed that she was an OMNI NPC and we knew HOGG were OMNI. We looked at the level of these chars (above) and noticed Eugenichogg was level 119 and Dgenratehogg was 104. After they had killed 'Iziris Agathon' they DID NOT loot her, but I looked at their info again to write down their level, name, guild - encase, and noticed Dgenratehogg was now level 105 !! He had just gained XP and levelled on it !! So, that was it we were in action. I asked Dgenratehogg how much xp he gained from killing 'Iziris Agathon' and his response was 'You tell me ' and instantly logged out. I guessed it was an exploit - due to his quick departure from the game (and the fact he has HOGG in his name).

I then sent in a petition to get your attention about this matter. I then noticed ;
http://www.anarchy-online.com/chara...name/tronixhogg &
http://www.anarchy-online.com/chara.../name/scornhogg
hovering high above us in their ships watching. We proceeded (while waiting for a GM to come) to do our mission which was next to this occurrence. After 10 minutes in the mission, having completed it, we came outside to notice ALL FOUR of the above killing 'Iziris Agathon' again !!

By this time we had been waiting so long for a GM to come - we really thought they were going to get away with this. That is when we decided to drum up some support for ANTI-HOGG. We shouted out in trade, on the shopping channels and on the OOC, that HOGG were exploiting and gaining XP and the location. It started a big discussion on how HOGG are ALWAYS behind exploits and can only survive if they are cheating !

About 2 minutes after causing the discussions an ARK appeared next to us *grin*. Seems we got their attention! So we proceeded to show the ARK the NPC involved. Unfortunately, although we got the attention of the ARK - HOGG had all logged....... (surprise surprise). I explained this whole situation to the ARK and she said it would get sorted. I have NO doubt that the whole of the HOGG guild new of this exploit. I have noticed people killing the NPC before, but it really didn't occur to me (probably because I was busy in RL at the time). It was atleast a week if not more, ago that I first saw them (but they could have been there earlier) - I made my newbie a week ago and am running around trade all the time - hence the sighting.

NOTE : Dgeneratehogg and Tronixhogg were at the NPC 'Iziris Agathon' AGAIN; Wednesday 20th February 2002 @ 09.35 (F9 is great). Scornhogg joined them around 10.35 the same day. They were being rude and generally HOGGish (I don't care if they are 'in character' that shouldn't be a 'get out clause' to be able to use profanity !! They were asking why i was there and complaining that i was stopping them play the game !!
