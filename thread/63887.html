There is also a concept I call 'trust level' that could be applicable here. Right now everyone is trusted with a certain set of org commands, based on their org rank.

Using trust level essentially would allow for promotion without the title - your rank doesn't change but you gain access to the commands of a higher rank. People with trust level 0 (ie, leader commands) could set trust level for others, with current rank being the default trust level.

This would allow leaders to entrust additional commands and powers to new people without demoting their existing officers should all officer position be full, and it also allows leaders to entrust one or more members with leadership commands.

Target your "victim" and type '/org trustlevel #'
