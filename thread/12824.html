Rival Company. A Neutral Criminal Syndicate (RK1)
If you’re interested in doing something different then consider being a member of Rival Company.

Rival Company is a new neutral roleplaying guild, an underground criminal syndicate based in Newland.

At this point in time we are looking for people of all levels and professions. We wish to benefit from having a wide range of skilled members. By joining Rival company you can gain your most fondest desire whether it’s money, status or simply smashing peoples heads together until they pay us back what they owe.

Gambling, Protection, Robbery, Hijacking, Smuggling, Kidnapping and Loansharking are a few of the current markets we deal in but we are also looking to expand our operations elsewhere.

“Tell us where it is and we will leave you a finger to call Omni Med.”
Rival Company Motto

If you are interested contact Dergon at djc8@ukc.ac.uk or tell in-game.
