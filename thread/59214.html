Body Count/Blood doesnt wash out
Here's a quick question for players. This is the RP thread, and i'm not in character right now. But what i want to know is, if people could, in character, post a thread here. What to post you ask? Well, how does your character feel about ALL the lives he/she has taken. Now i know most things we kill 'come back' to life because of the regeneration technology. But i've had missions to 'permanently' kill people.

So...i'd love to see what goes on in the mind's of some of the characters out there who have permanently taken away someone's life. Blood is very hard to wash off...and i'm sure some of you have spilled enough to fill a river. Anyway, if you feel like it, share what your character thinks about when he closes his eyes at night. Does it bother him? Or not at all?

If you don't wish to post it In character, then you can say OOC what you personally think of the idea of this endless contast state of killing. The whole moral dilema of the matter intrigues me on the matter. Imagine if you were the one 'constantly' killing. Day in day out your covered in blood. How would you feel? Or would you even feel anymore?

Let us know...if you wish.
