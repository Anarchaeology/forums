Christmas Party on RK2 this Sunday
The Velvet organisation, in association with Freedoms Edge, Section 4 and The Fellowship, Sartan Epoch and The Pakk plan to host a Clan Christmas party,

On December 23rd at 20:00 GMT in Reet Retreat.

Clan, Omni and neutrals are all invited to attend, no armour once inside, no weapons..only social clothing, swimsuits and drinks! Expect christmas competitions and maybe some surprises!

If Omni-Tek Employees want to come they are welcome to do so. Special peace-keepers will be there to ensure all citizens of Rubi-Ka can enjoy the celebration of this wonderful event!

Also depending on turnout there will be a special arena tournament, location to be announced on the day. This will involve as before just social clothes and food or drinks, no nano buffs, just good old fun, hit your friends and enemies with pizza and a fudge cake - join in for the big Christmas massive party food fight!

We hope to see everyone there, having fun and playing games with a possible visit from Santa for all the young people...

ARK members warmly invited to attend and to party down with happy people arriving from all over Rubi-KA!
