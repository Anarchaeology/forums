The Crat droids were armed with SMG's once. It didn't work as well as you might think though, because the bot spent too much time running around looking for a good place to shoot from. They actually get into combat faster if they're based on close combat.

Don't ask me why that might be. Pathing code is intractible stuff though, at least in 3D environments where you have to conform to a surface. Ranged mobs suffer from the same problem from time to time. Maybe part of the problem is the way LOS checks work, since currently an avatar's position is represented as a point on the surface it's standing on. LOS checks from avatar to avatar are made between those points (ie. feet to feet), rather that from head to head or head to any point on the target. This is why you can't trace LOS to a target if there's a slight rise in the terrain between you.

A pity, since otherwise it's a nice idea. I'm not sure whether it would help much in PvM, but it'd definitely help in PvP where one of the prime tactics against pet professions is to root the pet then stay out of its range.

kamekazi bots
I've probably missed a few things as I was away for several months, but I'm disappointed that the kamekazi bots were never implemented. In effect this woud have given engies an area nuke, which could have been fun.

As it stands we have very little by way of combat skills. We have no roots, snares, poisons, calms or nukes. We may have transference, but that starts at level 185 - bit of a long time to wait IMO. We also have few weapon skills, so engies are forced to either use the pistol - not the best of damage dealers, or use a single skill weapon - usually a Vektor. Our pets were level-capped, forcing us to spend a lot of money on finding mochams and infuses from a dwindling number of MP's (a seriously unpopular class these days).

Money also remains a major problem for this class. The tailoring of chest items to profession was little compensation. Nobody I know ever makes weapons, least of all from treasure items, but all I ever find in chests these days are weapon parts. Nobody makes them because they don't sell.

We need something to make up for the fact that these days the engineer class has no real PvP strength, has little to make it attractive to team with, and has a boring and unrewarding solo game. I suggest new skills. Useful ones that we could bring to teams as well as individuals. For example, as engineers we could supertune player's weapons, or make a yalmaha fly faster, or reverse warp (i.e eng warps to team). For PvP, being able to unsnare/root our pets, or calm/root/tame/ hostile bots would be a major improvement.
