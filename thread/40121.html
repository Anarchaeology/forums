the Dark workshop of twinking up.

the LLTS in that low percentage (3-4%) will not help you out that much, but will add alot of AS. Which enables you to use the Tsakachumi sniper rifle (adds +20 agility, the ql 40+ one).
With this extra amount of agility you can squeeze into umm better agility implants, and then equip better complit implants.

Weapon of choice for docs this lvl, either shotgun or MA (MA together with a BwT of course, quality 55-60 depending on how high you get your brawl and parry).

And yes Parry shields / Riposte shields is worth it, although maybe not at that level heh. But if you go the brawl / Parry road, you migth aswell use Parry shields. Quality 20 should add about 500 ac.

The only thing you can rely on at the int/psy front is the standard boosts, and the shades of L (if your Nanomage of course heh), so I wouldnt advice you to go that direction (stupid)

Novaflows are umm not worth it low level wise (I had a 12 soldier with a quality 60 novaflow 100%, and still couldnt penetrate defenses, learned later that ranged initiative was seriously megaimportant for novas, O_O)
I really think shotgun or MA (brawl / parry) is the way to go at that low level, maybe its mainstream but... it works, and thats why it is mainstream heh.

If you are really good you could actually be able to equip a 5x slot belt, but then you'd have to twink forever and ever (atleast in my opinion) so instead just equip Carbonum armor, quality 40-50, since those will add about 21-29 ncu depending on where the quality stands (And yes, carbonum is VERY worthwhile at this low level). I am sure you could squeeze into ncu that gives you atleast 100 more.
BUT however, I would advice you to equip a nanoformula recompiler, for your heals, since that will be your main skill so to speak.
Get bracers from Steps of madness, they might not add much, but they help alot.
Either double or triple implant HP! So very very important! if you want nano, just get a HE, never fight selfbuffed, that will get you nowhere in the cruel world of twinking up. And if you ever receive more damage then you make, run as hell since you wont be able to beat the duellist (155 shield... sucks.)

And try to max out First aid, hehe, its useful to say the least. Saved my ass a couple of times. (alot)

hope this information umm proved somewhat useful. I'll add the strenght / stamina part later, and yes a 75% gun will be better then a 100% one. And yes 75% armor will be slightly better then 100% armor (Both cases if the 100% is a couple of qualitys lower duuh)

Darksnake out.

LLTS only useful at low levels for someone using aimed shot. My level 2 agent has a ql 31 llts which adds 2 percent crit, and 60 to aimed shot. Obviously as aimed and rifle both implant shining in the eye, having a scope on allows me to implant rifle over aimed. Unbuffed, with my tidy ql 40 implants, i have rifle and aimed shot over 80.

Going either MA attack or shotgun will need you to max agility, and probably strength, and you should be maxing stam with an eye to implanting stam / agility based imps.

Armour of choice has to be Carbonic, with an ncu modifier of 2 per piece as low as ql 1 The modifiers dont suffer from OE rules, so get the best set you can fit, taking into consideration the ncu modifer - somewhere it changes from + 2 to + 4 i guess - lvl 50? - so if you squeezed into a set with FG, agility boost, IC and anything else you need....With a full set on my lvl 2 has 46 ncu, though i could increase this if i reimplanted comp lit imps.
