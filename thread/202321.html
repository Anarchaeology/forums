SL stuff
Been a daily routine to give tips about SL stuff for enfs daily on rimor, so I thought I'd complile the viable and decent stuff (that I can think of) in a post.

Thanks to Ninj00 for the additional item-lookup

'ere it is

Weapons:

Someone asked for High end PVP-piercing options?
Die Kleine Nadel (the small needle) decent offhand for the pvp interested piercing enf. If you feel like using a 2-handed Piercing wep, the Spear of Forbidden Ceremonies seems like a nasty option, carrying all specials on 100-600.

Naganita / Sword Staff seems to both be good, viable Alternatives to QB/EQB/FBR, being more Flexible when it comes to pvp/pvm. a level 100 enf should be able to pop on a 160ish one with a little work (provided that he's got the faction).

Skull Smasher / Staff of the Tempest Outdamaged by a Plastic pole/Manestyr on the High AC mobs (hecklers ++) but works fairly good in pvp at higher QL. FA recharge about 20s on 1K skill.

Maul of Menestyr - The carrot. Are you running yet? (Ithaci, NE inferno 250 Hecler boss)

Plastic Pole is the obvious 2HB choice for heckler hunting. Gelid/FBR/EQB OD it unless you are able to get a 260+ with the Saba/dmg add works.

Chef Cleaver - Your days of 1he pain at lvl 100-150 is over if you get your hands on this. Very decent wep with good minimum dmg and low FA reqs. PS: has Dropped several times on Dryads North of Adonis Abyss.

Implants:

For those of you that doesn't know this allready, Jobe clusters are sold in Jobe Market straight ahead of the portal from platform. (I must say I am delighted to trade with a trox)
Getting Alldef clusters in at TL4+ is getting more and more standard by the day, so no need to hesitate untill TL6 for that
Skill lock modifier is.. mm... yummy to get that Fast attack/First aid recharge down.

Head Symbiant of the Love Child Very nice trox symbiant. HP in head when you do not need those extra nano-skills, doesn't sound all that bad, does it?

Armor:

Tier , the option for those who doesn't have time/level to do mercs. This armor grows with you by left clicking it (like living cyber does). It costs to get it made tho. And then there's the farming of Embryos, visions, insignias... This item goes from 100-160

http://www.aodb.info/showitem.asp?AOID=220308Tier2[/URL]
Very nice HP add :-) Absolutely worth getting. Goes from level 160-220

Bellum Badonis Armor - new with 15.4 and with it's *very* nice HP add and ACs, it's something you want for your TL7+ enf. This has dropped on minibosses in Inferno (personally seen it drop at NE Incarnator from a 215 boss)

Sanguisugent Armor The chest piece, believe it or not, works like a notum focus. Every 25 sec you can right click it to heal you for a given ammount of HP A full set does a good AR boost + NR + Multimelee (note: need Melee init to use the heal) Drops from Highrises in Scheol, Minx in ely or Penumbra Hollows (Hiisi bosses south in dungeon)

Ancient Container can't really complain about this one. Wearable at level 100+ and has a great add to AC/HP/inits. In order to get one, you need to gather a certain ammounts of insignias and hand over to a cute lil mob in one of the sided dungeons in adonis (not spoiling to much there, am i?)

Enel Gil's Raging Spirit Tattoo / Nar Shere's Temperamental Spirit Tattoo ... just look at it's shape.. how can you resist this. Cute and semi-useful

Conscientious Knight's Armor (link to coat) dropping from the sided castles in Inferno. Nice armor, looks good have nice AC's.

Gil Jha's Discreet Helmet / Man Dal's Gaudy Helmet- good AC's. Equippable at leve 100+ and will follow you for a long time (untill you get tired of the moo-comments). Adds to Blunt weapons. Drops in Adonis Sided dungeons on the Ecclaist. Fairly easy to kill with a team of 150ish people or 2 TL6+ people

Gilthar's Ring of Force / Dalja's Ring of Power . Another twink item for the tanks. Drops off QL 170 Gilthar/Dalja Pocket boss. Patterns farmed in sided camps inside/outside in adonis.

Infantry armor - you usually just see people wearing the helmet here, but some go for the set. Totally it has a few nice stat adds+good AC. A decent piece of armor. Hard to gather full sets at the moment, tho Drops more frequently off named mobs. If you want something around QL 100, Scheol Named bosses in sided areas is probably best

Short Circuited Spiritech Circlet, Meta-Cerebellum Ring, Short Circuited Spiritech Circlet - nifty buff armor items
Smack all of them together and you got a Stabilized Spiritech Circlet.

Other Gadgets:

Ring of Burning Flesh - Drops of the Hecklerbosses in Inferno (low low droprate)

Star of Fortitude - no comments needed you want it! Read the description.

Melee Fighter's Speed Manual or Melee Fighter's Skill Manual (clan only) for the last points you need

Vanya/cama's pearl of the right brain If you need those last precious points for ... say... Gelid/skull Fast attack?

Bracelet of Arul Saba - Are you using gelids? what if you could pop in an extra few points of damage per hit? There are *several* different Arul Saba bracers, all depending on the ammount/quality of gems you insert. You can get a *very* nice dmg/HP add from these and they aren't that hard to get a hold of.

Impudent Bracer - Psychology ring for the clan-enf

The Underground Sided castles usually give a lot of ability buffing rings + "power bugs", mad first aid kits, but they drop so an so often and there's a ton of different rings there

PS: garden rings add treatment, and osmosium/platinum ingots sells for a good buck!
