Omni-Tek To Reclaim Rights To Jobe Portal
quote:
OMNI-TEK TO RECLAIM RIGHTS TO JOBE PORTAL
OMNI-HQ, RUBI-KA (Sept. 1, 29477) - Omni-Tek announced today that it would reclaim its rights to the Jobe Portal. According to ICC commercial law, Omni-Tek has the right to exclusively review the Jobe Association of Metaphysical Exploration (JAME) research that was funded by Omni-Tek.

According to Omni-Tek spokesman, Michael Dunleavy, Omni-Tek funded almost 100% of the research conducted at the Jobe laboratory. As a result we are asserting the right to exclusive access to the portal work for review.

Dunleavy added, Once our research group has had a proper chance to review the material at hand, the portal will be handed back to the Jobe scientific community intact and unharmed to utilize as they see fit. Dunleavy did not indicate whether Omni-Tek will sell its stake in this portal technology to Lord Kaldrani Skallagrim.

However, Dunleavy did indicate that OMNI-TEK will not exclude the implementation of a 'transportation tax' for all non-OMNI members wishing to utilize the Jobe Portal.

When questioned if OMNI-TEK would lay claim on the extensive research facilities that JAME has erected as a foothold within the Shadowlands, Dunleavy answered that OMNI-TEK would not lay claim to these and was merely interested in the technology and it's applications.

Lady Giljabet Skellibjalla, the spokeswoman for JAME only commented that: Negotiations are underway, and we are working hard to solve certain differences regarding investments and the actual ownership of the portal technology. JAME controls and owns the portal and the technology on which it is built. That is clearly stated in the financial agreements, so although Omni-Tek invested in the research and the construction, it has no direct control over it. Our stance is that the portal is, and will be, run by JAME. And JAME will honor its agreements with all its investors who will all benefit from their investment in relation to their funding.
