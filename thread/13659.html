Suggestion for Funcom....
I was thinking about the constant complaints of people that funcom doesn't read the forums (I know they do, but...) or that they don't communicate with us.

I know it is out of the question that funcom replies to each and every thread on the boards so we have to have some other way of getting feedback.....

The thing that came to my mind was that funcom should put a weekly summary of what issues people are concerned about and what is done about them on the regarding boards. Maybe even creating a FAQ ( I know this would be a huge think for newbees )....

I know that about 80% of the threads are repetitions of old issues or asking for changes that would increase imbalance, but there are actually some good points around.

Another good thing would be if it was easy to find the threads on a given board that have a comment by funcom in them....

Just my opinion, but I think Funcom should shine up their communication skills.....
