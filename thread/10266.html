Water vehicles..time they go away!
Considering the fact, that water takes up little volume in Rubi-Ka, the existance of Water Vehicles is pretty null. Here are some suggestions to phase out this useless form of transportation:

1) Eliminate the Water Vehicle stat. Any IPs spent by a player in this statistic is to be refunded into their IP pool.

2) Allow a one-time trade-in of any water vehicle to shops for either cash or a ground vehicle of equal Quality Level.

3) Modify all ground vehicles to allow travel over water. They hover, so this should be easy to implement.

With Mids, Kodaiks, Yalmaha and other Flying and Ground Vehicles about, Water Vehicles have really no place in our inventories. To us, the players, they were simply a good idea that just couldn't work.

Anyone at FunCom care to respond to this? What about you fellow players? I'd be interested to hear what all of you have to say on this matter.
