Rechage speed of the weapon affects them, mostly. There is also much spuculation (and apparent verification by Funcom on a few occasions) that base skill (not buffed or implanted!!) has an effect as well.

Just for the sake of spuculation, here are some forumlas I picked up on the boards once... Can't remember who posted them, otherwise I would credit them.

Aimed Shot = Recharge Delay * 40 - Aimed Shot Skill * 3 / 100
Burst = Recharge Delay * 20 + [Burst Delay] - Burst Skill / 25
Fast Attack = Attack Delay * 15 - Fast Attack Skill / 100
Fling Shot = Attack Delay * 15 - Fling Shot Skill / 100
Full Auto = Recharge Delay * 40 + [Full Auto Delay] - Full Auto Skill / 25
Sneak Attack = 40 - Sneak Attack Skill / 150

Where [Burst Delay] and [Full Auto Delay] are the values in the database by those names for the weapons in question.

These forumlas have only been casually verified by me...so I take no responcibility if they happen to be wrong.
