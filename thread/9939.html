There are lots of places that used to be crawling with noobs (for Clanners, the northwest outpost in Tir is a prime example), that are virtual ghost towns now. Part of it is that they've moved some of the spawn sites, and part of it is lack of noobs.

On the bright side, this means that for many of the sites that remain where they were, you can go hunt them all day long by yourself with virtually no competition or interruptions. It's like a mission without all the headaches.

Also, there *are* a lot more newbies around than there were, say, a month or two ago. There was a time when you could walk through Tir itself (I assume it was the same with Omni) and see nary a soul. Today it's starting to get crowded again. It's just that people are hanging in different spots, and there still aren't quite the numbers of them that there were pre-diaspora.

(Personally, I'm lovin' life now that the population has decreased. The wilderness is actually playable now and the whole game's just a lot more "real" and immersive to me. Of course, I'm a soloer, so I would prefer that...)
