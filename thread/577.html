Undocumented changes...
Okay, I've been reading through the boards (current and archived). Funcom has stated in a number of posts and press releases that they'll always inform us of any changes they are making or getting ready to make.

Now, the major bug issues asside, let's look some of the things that Funcom has changed without any notice whatsoever (before, during or after makind the change). This is not just from the 13.0 patch but a variety of things I recall and in no particular order. Feel free to add others that I missed.

1. Right clicking your token board causes your character to steam or smoke. The more tokens, the more polution you provide to the game. (This was later removed again due to the fact that it increased game lag)

2. Chaning the text colors in the chat window.

3. Removal of locked doors in missions.

4. Reintroduction of locked doors in missions.

5. Mobs don't cast nano's on player (readded several patches later).

6. Chest no longer just explode and hurt you when disarming fails. Now you might get blinded or you may wake up a Frightened Rollerrat.

7. When in the grid you now get spammed with comp lit requirements when using an exit, even if you have comp lit above the requirement.

8. Mission terminals in various city zones changing locations. Sometime by only a few feet, sometime by hundreds of feet (Newland city comes to mind for the former and Entertainment for the latter).

9. Certain pieces of clothing changing to "Social Armor" status which means you can no longer wear them with armor.

Now admittedly, none of these issues are major, or game-stoppers, or in some cases are they even problems. However, when they say they're going to inform us of all changes, they should at least follow through.
