Hmmm.......

I have spent a long time reading the posts from both sides of this argument, and feel I understand both points of view, but still must side with those players that build and buff twinks.

I know for a fact that you are fairly mistaken on how these twinks get buffed. Yes, the first place asked is org chat, however, especially since the advent of SL, the buffs aren't always readily available in a timeframe that would make it possible to use them all. The next stop for all these tower fighting twinks is the local gathering spots. Omni often goes beggar's hill outside NLC, while clan tends to congregate at SG. Now that the novelty of SL has worn off, you can generally find all these buffs you worry about in less than 5 mins.

H&Q wears off fast enough, so I am not even going to address fixer HoT's...

To the issue of reflects...

Best NCU I've been able to pull on a non-trader at lvl 25 would be around 225-250+NCU buff. So make that est 265-290 NCU total at that lvl. This is not enough to hold all the buffs you seem to think that lvl 15's hold, and I"m describing a lvl 25 char. Many of these buffs you see have 50+NCU usage...

Now I must admit that I have not build a 15 twink, but I suspect that with a trader one could acheive similar base NCU, but no fixer buff to increase it, so 225-250 NCU, but only with a trader!

What this means is that to get the mega reflect and AC's there will be some other sacrifices in how they buff, thus providing your opening to attack them. Maybe it will take you dieing once to find out where they made their compromise, but they have made one!

The process of getting that type of NCU is actually fairly easy to do, involves nothing more then acquiring a Guardian Belt and some dark memories if you're ambitious...

So, does this process mean that low-lvl PvP is borked?

NO!

You say your guild can't hold a tower site cuz 1 guy can come rip it down? The key to holding land is organization. One member of your org needs to be in charge of PvP. This person needs to talk with other PvP'ers in other orgs. Again this is a very easy thing to do. The PvP general will have access to all towers channel and simply needs to show up at these battles. You will notice that many of the same people are at every lowbie battle. Put them onto your friend list and organize with their org sometimes. Go on some tower strikes with them, and when you have a decent rapport built, ask them to help you take towers. I never once had trouble with this part of PvP when I helped run the PvP of a small clan org. We captured 1 site and held it until our org disbanded because we chose a good site and cultivated allies with our neighbors...

Our second field was always somewhat problematic because of our org's size: EG, we couldn't build enough towers to defend our second field. Not a broken PvP issue, simply us getting too big for our britches.

To sum up, lowbie PvP is not borked from high lvl buffs. The buffs simply make it more obvious who has and who hasn't worked for a long time on their characters.


PS: the process of twinking doesn't have to be monetarily crippling. The most important parts (belt and memory) can be acquired free of charge. The implant/armor setup as you so admirably pointed out is not as important, but can be put together for around 1.5-2M.

Of course they would change back (unless they are lazy), but it would cost an attack in time, maybe enough to keep the doc alive! If the char made a pshycology check they could resist the taunt and fight on, otherwise, target swap.

This thread type is always fun...

You should disect one of those PVP twinks that you spent so long concentrating on, discover the most important part of it.

Weapon - The ability to do damage is important of course, you have to be able to kill stuff after all. A good weapon is an asset, but it won't keep you alive against 3 or 4 defenders.

Armour - Again helpful, either for improving skill or just preventing damage. But again it won't keep you alive against 3 or 4 defenders.

Implants - Same applies, with additional life, and higher skills you can survive longer and deal out more damage. But once again, it won't keep you alive long against 3 or 4 defenders.

With all of those above you can be very good, and very strong, but you can't just wander in and completely solo an entire base, when out-numbered by defenders.

Then you move onto the final element of your hard working twink.

NCU - On its own it means nothing, an ability to fit programs in that's all.

So we're all done with the twinking... a smashing character that should be a lot of fun. Bored? Perhaps we could go solo a base or something? Lets see, what else would help us out when soloing that base?

Outside buffs - There are several that are very useful for PVP in any form. Every buff obviously provides something, more health (Essence), greater protection (AC buffs and Reflect Shields) not one of them will keep you alive forever against multiple defenders, except one.

Dr Hack 'n Quack - 30 Minutes duration, heals 362 - 370 damage every 10 seconds (180 ticks which equates to 65160 HP worth of healing over 30 minutes).

If healing provided by that single buff is greater than damage over time done by any number of enemies and towers then you are invunerable for 30 minutes. May as well kill those towers.

If healing provided by that buff and any other healing provides an acceptable increase in HP over time to keep the player alive for long enough then you may as well be invunerable for 30 minutes. May as well kill the towers.

If healing is greater than damage then why do you need to fire on the defenders? Just leave them running around being totally ineffective.

Clearly there's no balance in PVP between completely new players and twinked players, and really I don't think there should be - the system should not be completely flat that would be dull.

But there are some things that push that slider much too far to the other side.

The "go get the same buffs" kind of attitude doesn't really work for me. Sure the defenders could well do that, but what would it gain?

Initially lost time looking for buffs while towers drop.

Then a kind of stalemate between the attackers and the defenders is unlikely, more likely is that the attackers will simply focus on those elements that can't get these buffs, aka the towers.

I'm sure someone can produce samples of damage over time for level 20, 40 and 60 characters. Instead of just saying everyone against this is wrong, prove that it doesn't make a discernable difference to the state of play. Prove that the twinked character is the most important aspect not the buffs running on that character.

The answer to this situation will always be a difficult one, nerfs are horrible things, especially PVP nerfs that effect PVM.
