Oh tell me about it... a lot of low "über" traders around giving the profession a bad rumour. Yay... and believe me, traders at my level aren't uber in pvp as people say. We are good, I don't deny that. But not uber.

my experience say:

trader vs. soldier - 30/70 , root the soldier, go defensive and recieve his/her alphastrike and don't fire back. Run out of distance, heal up, go back and drains his skill a bit and wait for his shield to run out. Then you start to root and shoot. And its the only chance you have. 90% of the times the soldier zone back to the shop he came from as he never moves more than 2m from the entrance. ^_^

trader vs. doctor - 50/50 , who debuffs who first is really the issue here. The init-debuffs docs have are as effective as a nanoshutdown. You wont be able to cast anything after getting that on you. So pray for getting your divest in first, but don't count on it as their debuff are instantcast.

trader vs. NT - 60/40 (pillow NT 70/30) , well, actually very hard to say as it depends a lot on how the NT is build. But a pillow-wielding NT will have close to no chance in PvP.

trader vs. MP - 70/30 , our roots and mezz makes their pets complete useless and debuffing them makes them unable to use their nukes and debuffs. (if they debuff you just use a lower divest to debuff them) A good twinked MP might make it to 60/40, but I really havn't faced any.

trader vs. crat - 50/50 , only really one clan crat at my lvl pvps. But snares, alphastrike and pets are making them a good pvp-class.

trader vs. engineer - 80/20 , engineers are simply not good in pvp. Only way for them to survive is to group pvp and have a good alphastrike. To easy to root/mezz the bot.

trader vs. adventurer - 60/40 or 70/30 , traders use better weapons and better heals and can disable the adventurers heals pretty effectively. Melee-using adventurers are not doing very good in pvp against traders.

trader vs. MA - 60/40 or 70/30 , depends if they get close and do a lucky dimach. Well, MAs are not very good against any rooters.

trader vs. fixer - 60/40 if they stick around, 20/80 if they run away or grid evac. Quite hard to kill fixers in pvp unless you get them so heavy divested that they can't use evac. Mausser using fixers aren't doing that great in pvp. Fear the suppressor's specials. Not its normal damage.

trader vs. trader - obviously 50/50

trader vs. enforcer - ?/?
Can't really say as I havn't encountered one.


So yes we are good. But godly - no. Definately not. Doctors are just as good as us. With mocham's and a good weapon a lvl 150+ doc is better than a trader in pvp. Soldiers are far better than traders in pvp by given rules. The mkX shield simply is well lets say powerfull in pvp. I don't care much, makes it fun for a bunch of people to take down the zoning soldier, though I could mention more fun ways to pvp.

More on topic:

Traders in PvP, at both low level and high are uber, and uber for a reason that is not specific to traders - nano resist.

Roots, divests, and plunders all land with resounding success in PvP, even against high-resist opponents such as NTs and Doctors. Our plunder line was really meant to be a gamble, a riskier nano to cast, with it's 190% resist rate - but I've only had it resisted in PvP a grand total of 3 times. Roots and such are supposed to be fair enough so that melee classes have a chance, but it works nothing like that right now.

In a nutshell, roots shouldn't be so 100% end-all, against melee classes and nanoresist should be a viable skill instead of an IP dump. Resist works fine for mobs, plundering can take 1 cast or maybe even 3-4. Divest usually lands except for unlucky shots. Mobs use fake nanoresist for their level though. :\

Nanoresist is the same reason why most rooting classes just own meleers unfairly and also why docs become uber later.

Now aside from resist being broken and letting us land nanos so easy in PvP, there's an issue of how much skill our nanos add. Thing is though - not ONE combat-oriented skill has a growth rate of 5. Period.

Shotgun is aqua/4, and that's our absolute best right there. Nanoinit is aqua/4 too. -ALL- nanoskills are 4 growth as well, which means we have an inherent resistance disadvantage relative to classes with 5 nanoskill growth and higher nanoskill caps - this doesn't mean much now, but it will when NR is fixed (please note this is also a problem for fixers already - attack skill of nano vs resist is mean for their low-cap nanoskills).

... So we have subpar caps EVERYWHERE for our skills. Ranged init, ranged specials, melee skills, etc, all cap lower, grow slower, and cost more than the skills of the combat specialist classes. This difference maginifies over our levels due to title caps, something that's hard to notice early on. Our divests are how we compete in this arena, but they come with their own disadvantages - cast time, nano consumption, rate of failure.

So then there's the issue of overequipping. Yep, at low levels it can be strong. But weapon ql's stop somewhere, and by the point in time where fighter classes can equip their highest ql weapon (120ish?), the bonus is over and we're fair as anybody else.

/rant
All you low level trader PvP twinks that will never level over 74 out of fear - you all are just ganking cowards. Hit level 75 and hit the PvP zones then and you'll realize the feeling of REALLY scoring victory instead of ganking people because nobody over a certain level can stop you. It takes cajones to stop and fight a soldier 45 levels over you, and boy does it feel good to win.
/rant
