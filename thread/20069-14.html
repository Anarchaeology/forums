I thought of another way to spruce up missions, even if it doesn't actually make them better.

Interface rooms. Right now mission areas are somewhat homogenous. If the walls are round in one area, they're round in all areas. Even when you step into a cavern, the doors match up to round areas on both sides. Have a room that is intentionally room on one side and cavern on the other. Try to have one for each distinct pairing of mission environments. Have the generator use these rooms to mix and match mission area styles.

Maybe a bunker that butts up agains the remains of a mine for instance.

As the starter of this thread insinuated, put a mission area IN the grid. Maybe three, one on each level. The whole mission would be under grid rules. No casting of fresh nano's, pyrimid body, etc.

Build some better rules for room ordering. A bathroom can connect to anything, but should there really be a boardroom next to a power plant, next to a supply room? Perhaps if the mission is in a wrecked transport, to use one example, the layout and rooms should better reflect the things original purpose as a vehicle that flew through space. Cockpit in "front", storage down both sides, engine room in the back.

Of course, when it comes right down to it, I'd rather FC focus on expanding missions themselves than revamp the mission area generator in any way.

Making the walls materially transparent from the backside would be nice though.
