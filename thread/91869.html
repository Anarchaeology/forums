Tower attacks
Defending towers is almost a waste of time. When you get there to attack, the attackers have already moved to the 75% gas, causing everyone to stand off and look at each other.

A better solution might be to assign a PvP tag to anyone, regardless of organization, who attacks a tower. At least this way we could defend a tower without wasting hours of time staring at the opposition.
