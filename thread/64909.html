A Winter's Tale
"Gee, this place gets kinda creepy when you’re all by your lonesome." remarks Vallentia as she roams the empty halls of the South Rubi-Ka Society headquarters in Rome. The place had been semi-deserted over the last few weeks, a consequence of the recent smear campaign the Society had been victim of and which had eventually forced many of its more prominent members to resign.

Another consequence of the current crisis was that the leadership had fallen on Val's young shoulders. They'd made her the president South Rubi-Ka Society. Never mind there wasn't much of a South Rubi-Ka Society left to be president of, this is still some achievement for a kid who'd just two years ago was still running in Omni Sewers street gangs.

Though there were advantages to having the place to herself, Val is increasingly finding the silence oppressive. She considers getting a state-of-the art sound system installed with speakers in every room
to play her old The Rollerrats albums all day on. After all it would be such a shame to left her new expense account go to waste.

Val finds herself at one end of a long, imposing corridor. The floor is made of fine Italian marble, or so she was once told. Val doesn't know where Italy is, but she figures it's got to be expensive. On the walls hang portraits of celebrated Rubi-Kan humanitarians, people like Dr Dorchester who'd so valiantly helped bring an end to the Aegean Plague of '72 before dying of the disease himself and Harrison Coppit, known planet-wide for the work he had done to improve the plight of notum miners in the early year. She briefly wonders if one day her portrait will also hang from one of these walls, but it doesn’t seem very likely.

Giving her skateboard a little push, Vals rides gently down the corridor. She notices a disapproving stare coming from Coppit's portrait, stops in her tracks, walks up to the offending portrait and turns it on itself so that the reverse side of the frame is facing outwards. “Next time I get any attitude from you old man, you’re goin’ to to the basement.” The, quite naturally does not reply. Satisfied, she gets back on the skateboard and continues down the hallway.

At the end of the corridor, Val takes a right down another long corridor that will eventually get her to the kitchens. As today was the day Wodehouse, the Society's butler, gives lessons at the Rome Academy for Butler and Valets, she'd have to take care of her dinner herself. "Man, I am getting spoiled." she reflects.

As she gets closer to the kitchen, Val seems to hear voices. The sounds tunr out to be more than just voices, but music of sorts. It’s a strange, off-key sort of music she's never heard the likeness of before. Curious, she gives skateboard an extra push to glides into the kitchen area.

Inside the vast kitchen Val sees Morgan, one of the security guards, making a multilateral, mammoth sandwich. "Hey Gumshoes." she says. "Didn't know you were working late."

"Good evening madam president" he replies with a smile. "I just a few lose threads to wrap up."

Val spies his humongous sandwich. "You got everything in that thing?" she says critically.

"Actually not really. I to be honest, missy, I couldn't find me no bacon."

"Oh, I know where the bacon is. Let me show you." Val rides her board to one of the industrial sized refrigerators and opens it up. She literally has to climb into it to reach her target. "Here it is, right next to the avocado."

"Avocado ehi? I reckon I could use a bit of that too."

"Sure thing."

Morgan gets to work, adding a layer of bacon and avocado to the precarious structure that is his sandwich. "Ah much better. This sandwich just wouldn’t taste right without a slice of bacon."

Val sits down on one of the tall counters and watches him. "What's that music you’re listening too anyway?" she asks.

"Well that would be the blues, madam president."

"The blues huh?" Val spends a few minutes listening to the tune playing. "Do people actually like that stuff?"

"I reckon some do."

"Why?"

Morgan grins. "Well, some say folk listen to the blues cos when you hear of someone else's pain, it sort of washes away your own. Others say people it’s a state of mind. Me, I reckon its just mighty fine music."

"If you say so" she replies, unconvinved.

Morgan bites into his monster sandwich, while Val leaps of the counter and starts to fix herself something to eat too.

"Morgan? I was thinking...." she says as she searches the fridge for something that catches her fancy.

"Mmm?" replies Gumshoes with his mouth full.

"You know all the bad stuff that happened to us, that holo-vid show and all. We're pretty sure House Eris was behind it all, right?"

"There is no doubt in my mind about it. They did their homework, dug up every little bit of dirt on us they could find, dressed it up real nice so that it looked a whole lot worse than it really was and then hit us hard and fast with it.”

"Why would they do that?"

"Hard to tell. Rich folk can be awful peculiar. I guess they figured there was not enough room for two so similar organisations such as the SRS and Ancient House Eris. The more well known the Society got the more they figured we was stealin' their thunder, so to speak. Who'd every thought the world of philanthropy could be so cutthroat, huh?"

"Yeah I guess. What I was thinking is, why can't we pull the same sort of stunt against them?"

Morgan stops chewing and appears to ponder the question for a while. "It's not that simple, missy. Wolf and I did some research on House Eris of course. Didn't find nothin', but I reckon if we pressed a bit harder we'd find a hidden skeleton or two. However the circumstances are different. Ya see, the Society's leadership is ultimately accountable to the SRS's Board of Trustess. It's the trustees that control the purse strings. House Eris didn't really have to prove any of it's allegation's against us. All they had to do is put enough pressure to get the trustees all nervous and jittery.

“By contrast, House Eris ain't accountable to no one. They can be as secretive as they like about their affairs and no one really can tell them what to do.

"Ya also got to consider that all them Eris members all belong to Rubi-Ka's finest families. We are talking about seriously old money and seriously powerful connections. All of which makes 'em a real tough nut to crack."

"How tough is tough? I mean is it a question of money?"

"Well money sure, but what you'd really need is a clever plan. Something real subtle."

Val frowns. "I am not really a subtle sort of girl."

Morgan laughs lightly, "Yeah, me neither."

Val settles for a pack of microwaveable cheece burgers while Gumshoes tackles his sandwich.

"Morgan?"

"Mmm?"

"You remember that con artist guy? The one that sold us the phoney leet painting?"

"Mm-mm"

"What was his name?"

Morgan chews away quickly so that he can answer properly. "Matisse. Fellow was called Matisse."

"You reckon you'd be able to find him for me, and maybe even set up a meeting?"

This startles Morgan. "Now why would you wanna do a fool thing like that, missy? That guy is bad news."

"Answer me Morgan, could you find him?"

"I reckon I could."

"Then do it. I want to speak to him."

Morgan shakes his head. "If your thinkin' what I think your thinkin', you better think again. We don’t want to get involved with a conman, got enough trouble as thinks stand."

"Just do it Morgan."

Gumshoe’s sighs. "All right then. Your the boss."

"Damn straight I am." replies Val.
