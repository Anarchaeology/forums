I too had a really good experience with GM Karmakappa the other day. He was very patient and polite.

I have found that if you treat GMs/ARKs with some respect and good naturedness, that they respond in kind. I have seen players totally be rude to these people and get a cold response. Players need to understand that GM/ARKs are people in RL, yes that is right, they are real people. And if you are rude to them, what do you expect?

I have only ever had one bad experience with a certain GM who will remain nameless, where I felt like he was being condescending and rude, but then...over a month later, he answers a /petiton I had where I was stuck in a mission and he was totally nice, and went out of his way to help me. I figure, well, GMs are people too and maybe this certain GM had a bad day the first time he helped me.

All in all, I have had good-great experiences with GMs, and most but not all Arks. And every time I have been stuck in the 3d architecture, they have responded immediately.

It's very simple, if you want to work out a problem/issue to a solution, then remain calm and factually methodical, and polite. Ranting and raving and rudeness will get you nowhere.
