No question that the loot table generally and the boss loot table in particular is broken. I do not want to take boss loot in a group anymore because the list has so many pathetic items ( Oh yeah I need another adventurer fire shield and another instruction disk for a fixer nano) that people are convinced you are keeping primo items. I just cannot stand having boss loot full of instruction disks and hybrid weapons with 4 or 5 requirements. Is there actually anyone who uses these weapons?

I do see some decent, profession-specific items: padded Love armor, Servants of 8, and organic combat suits. You see some good weapons most readily usable by a particular profession agent rifles, 1he weapons, and plasma projectors. But rarely do you see the items that are highly prized by multiple professions like Albrecht. I have seen 1 Albrecht in a boss loot (assuming that none were pocketed by Ninja slime who never displayed it). And let's face it, you are probably not getting an Albrecht if you are not the one looting. I have also never seen a web cloak except in a team mission chest (again have found only one).

I know that agents and fixers needed love, but MAs? Please! It is bad enough that there are virtually no special items for some professions --bureaucrats, traders and doctors to name a few-- but making items that are usuable by a number of professions virtually non existent is just not helpful. As much as I love my MA, fixer and agent friends I would like the outside chance of finding something useful to me. How about ensuring that a steady flow of decent items is in loot generally and boss loot in particular. It might also help with the utterly insane prices that these items bring.
