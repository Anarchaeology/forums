Opps! ok, here it is!
an·ar·chy:
1. Absence of any form of political authority.
2. Political disorder and confusion.
3. Absence of any cohesive principle, such as a common standard or purpose.

This is more of a question of philosophy, what is anarchy
without laws to rebel against, what is rebellion without
injustuce? Without a group of people who belive in the power of
law, there is no such thing as anarchy. What we have on rubi-ka
is just a bunch of people doing what they want because there is
no oganization telling them what they shouldn't do. People know
they shouldn't attack shop keepers, because they are stronger
than any mob in the wilds, or dungeons. but, doesn't that take
away from something? we have the sentinels who attack any
omni dumb enough to walk into their visual range, and omni-pol
who attack any clanners who wander into their sight. Fine,
dandy, hell, nessecary (uber <insert string of obscenties here>
smacking around newbies is just plain WRONG) but is that all
there is? anarchy is meaningless if there is noone to enforce law,
like in omni-1 you have signs like "No Running!" but is some omni
pol drone gonna shoot at you for it? ... no. for fixers, they say
hacking the grid's data stream is illegal, but are you going to get
re-routed into the hands of a slayerdroid for doing it? ... nope.
Theres a 10 most wanted list for Clan, Neut, And Omni but are
these people going to be treated any different by security npcs?
err... as far as i know, NO! they're going to get shot at just like
the rest of us, but chances are they might kill the npc just for
trying. Shoot at a shop owner, shopowner kills you. shoot at
security npc whos on your side, well, your just going to get killed
once, and then they forget about you. Accept an illegal mission,
its not like omni-pol or the sentinels are going to be waiting
outside (or even INSIDE) for you

Why can't there be a 4th side to all of this? an umbrella
organization that tries to enforce galatic law in Every territory.
That will plant fake missions to lure the people on the most
wanted list to their arrest and imprisionment, so friends and allies
can have some fun trying to secure them an early release, or to
have them try and fail horribly? That will plant fake rares in shop
terminals when a certain person uses it, and if the person buys it,
its going to A: take the person's money, and B: plant a nano bug
on said person! A group that can booby trap grid termnials to
send One person to a special "Grid Cell", untill "They" can collect
said criminal?! Not knowing if your going to get ambushed
walking to that next mission door?
A side full of real people with "interesting" special ablities. I
know that Sometimes a Very Red <whatever> hired killer spawns
in missions, but I want so much more, I want to be stoped on the
street and questioned, I want to be rooted if i try to run away, I
want to have my reckless digitization canceled if I try to escape. I
want to have a group of people that piss off BOTH sides! or hell,
even make special divisions of the clan army and omni-tek, (and
NO i don't want it to be angent only)

I'll be honest, I'm a hardcore fixer, and i love the class, but
what i want is something better than what im getting, a world
that i actually have to keep looking left and right for that group of
people to come rushing from the shadows, i want to be able to
hack the computers that appear in the missions, and find
something that will fetch me even More money and exp. i want
to be able to break into OTHER people's apartments, and
accidentally leave some small piece of evidence that proves i was
there, then get forceably arrested and punished!

come ON now!
not one of you can say you wouldn't like something like this!!
PLEASE someone from funcom!! PLEASE READ!
