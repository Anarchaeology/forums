Gah. I just wrote a huge post about the perils of social elitism, and how our part of the AO community (the RP part) is steadily declining into the biatchy highschcool environment from hell.

There are plenty of "big name" role-players from AO, well known people that some might suggest are at the heart of alot if not all of the well known stories and role-playing that happens both in-game and on this forum, and within our RP community in general, who will be going to role-play on SWG.

Yes, I said going to role-play on SWG.

Yes, I am one.

No, I'm not leaving AO. I'll just be there as well.

And no, I won't "out" any of the others.

I'm not saying this because I'm feeling difficult about anything that anyone's said, because I don't give a fu...rollerratsarse about what's been said, however elements of GSP and DA would be wise to quieten down a little, given several of their well known and respected role-players are amongst those going, along with many of those who are socially friendly to both those orgs.

Because it'll be something new, something different to AO. That's all.
