Speed caps and the AC system, study of mechanics
Alot of threads debate the uselessness of most weapons in AO. Rightfully so, basically because funcom decided to balance combat by limiting player abilities with a set of hard caps, on everything cept Max dmg. (Except for PvP with the 40% cap that only hard hitting weapons and high crits can achieve anyway)

Most weapons in AO are designed to follow a certain dmg/time ratio, most weapons are fairly close to this ratio (as long as you disregard specials, and specials will be debated later)

At high level (120+) its quite easy to make almost all weapons attack at the speed cap (with the exception of those weapons that are slower than 3/3 (as in 3 seconds attack time and 3 seconds recharge time) This in itself makes all weapons faster than 3/3 only rely upon their max dmg and crit bonus, (since any weapon that has high enough min dmg to be considered a valuable parameter always has too low max dmg to do anything except min dmg)

Some debates has circled around the value of having a weapon that is so fast that it dosnt slow down when your fighting at full def. This argument fails since your total dmg/time will still be lower with a fast weapon on full def than it will be with a hard hitting and slow weapon on full def - end of that argument (somewhat connected to the study of specials)

The way AC works enhance this fundamental problem. If only Ac was tweaked for absorbing a percentage of the dmg between the weapon min dmg and the weapon max dmg we would see a slight improvement of the weapons that are fast (the argument of fighting on defensive slider position would grow stronger). but the weapons dealing the highest dmg and still hitting close to the speed cap would still dominate total dmg/time.

One of the fairly recent patches made the melee speed cap a little faster, but it is still a hard cap that is too slow to make the fast weapons useful.

One way to fix the speed cap would be to make inits reduce a percentage of the time it takes for a weapon to swing (or fire) this would basically make all weapons viable, if the fast weapons min dmg is high enough. Some weapons would be generating extreme spam, but their dmg/time would still never become higher than that of a good assault rifle.


Regulating player performance thru hard caps is a bad idea. everyone will soon find the caps and adapt to use them to their best advantage.
