Karaoke night on TNT!
Can you sing? ... or better yet, do you LIKE to sing? Well, TNT wants to hear you!! Tomorrow (Sunday!) at 8pmEST tune into TNT for a very special Karaoke night!!

Contestants will be judged by our listeners and the winner will win cold hard credits! (..and maybe a date of your choice?)

How does it work? Well.. For everyone who's listened before, you all know of CALLERBOT (works on both RK1 and RK2). Anything you /tell callerbot will be seen by both TNT hosts, Threx and myself. When we ask, throw up your number in callerbot with the song you'd like to sing and we'll call you! (When we call you, you'll have to turn your radio down.. If you'd like, you can put the song of your choice on and sing over it for a true Karaoke effect!)

Whether you can sing, can't sing or just want to sing, we want to call!!

To tune in play location http://stream.gridstream.com in winamp/realplayer.

Don't miss Talk Deviant with Tarryk and Metalynx at 6pmEST (Right before TNT!)

Good luck and we hope to hear from you!
