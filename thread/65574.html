The problem is not so much 'who needs help' but more 'how do you define how needs help'

Engineers arent _supposed_ to be good at PvP, and I dont think I've seen any Engineers actually asking for that be be changed. But a Traders is _very_ good at PvP and _very_ good at Tradeskills but _not_ good (in my 27 levels of using one) at PvM (certainly no where near as good as My Engineer - assuming the MOB stays on the bot) so who needs help more? Depends on what part of the game you're interested in.

I read the list of 'who owns soldiers' in an earlier post and half of me came away thinking "This guy isnt going to be happy until he can beat every other prof at least 50% of the time" (and thats what every other combat prof is thinking too). But then the other half of me isnt so sure thats what he means/wants.

Given the large number of professions its going to be impossible to get the rock/paper/scissors effect that people would (probably) be happy with before you take PvM, solo vs team & tradeskills into the equasion.

How much 'tradeskill' or 'PvM' makes up for a lack of 'PvP'? Probably impossible to answer, however since this whole game is based on XP and hence PvM, the game should be _at least_ balanced for PvM. Then you worry about PvP (I know thats not going to go down well with the bored high level guys, but I didnt make the game mechanics up.

Now if PvP got you as much XP as the equivalent PvM, and so did tradeskills then suddenly balance is not really an issue, since support classes can suck as PvP and not worry about it since they will rule at something else (that is suddenly equally valuable). If tradeskills were able to make lots more in game stuff, cheaper and better quality then you defocus PvP and PvM for those classes (and as such they dont worry about it as much). Make the PvP and PvM classes _need_ the tradeskill classes, and thus everyone gains XP at an acceptable rate and no one complains about not being good at one thing or another (since you will either excell at one and suck at the other 2, be reasonable at 2 and not bad at the 3rd or be equally medium at all 3)

Personally I think soloing should get you more XP then teaming but solo should be harder for an eqivalent mission (which I think we'll all agree it is) this would counteract the fact that FC are going more 'team oriented' with the boos loot candy etc. Again if Solo'ers levelled faster I dont think they would mind (as much) as its a tradeoff you make.
