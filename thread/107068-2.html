Read the book Snow Crash by Neal Stephenson. That's exactly what AO is for me.

Literally "living" on Rubi-Ka is not an option for me, and I have no inaccessible wishes to change that. But I live there, nonetheless. Whenever I have the time in RL and nothing pressing to tend to, I allow Tarryk to use that time...because he has many equally pressing things to tend to. That's just the way it is.

In many ways, AO is a parallel reality to our own. I try not to look much deeper than that. I like the balance I draw out between the two, and I love my experiences in both worlds, RL and AO. I wouldn't change a thing.

(save for a decent couch in my apartment...on both worlds...).
