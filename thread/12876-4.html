Well Korant I would have to agree that you were truly Gimped. While there are some profs where soloing a 50% mission may not indicate gimpage (is there a doc in the house?) all of the combat and semi-combat profs should have no problem with them.

What I tire of is the attitude that if I am not as good as all others I am gimped. Most here don't agree with that either.

Now a level 100 Fixer who can't Fgrid? I pity them, Fgrid is not only prof defining it also opens up RK like nothing else. Couple Fgrid with a yalm and you no longer care where the mission is. With Team Fgrid and your team doesn't either. Before that they may be a bit pissed when you pull that mission for Perp Wastes or Deep Artery Valley.

Running fast, quck evacs, snares and roots are all part of the prof, even GA is a part of the prof. Fgrid is one of the defining factors of the prof. I put it right there with Quantum Wings for MP's. One very cool nano that only we have access too unless we decide to share it with others in our team. Also the first quest is one of the most anxious and nerve racking quests in the game, Lord I loved it.

So again, yes I agree, a level 100 Fixer without Fgrid I would consider gimped and would pity him all the fun he has missed. I would probably wait until level 130ish to consider a fixer gimped without Team Fgrid.
