The issue is not so much about having to reload we all expect and accept the need to do that

The issue here is the process involved the equivalent to opening your backpack/going through your pockets to find where you put those extra clips, then taking a couple seconds to check wich hand your weapon's in then you can spend the time it takes to reload your weapon, before you again make sure the gun is in your hand and stuff the empty clip back in your pocket.

What's being asked for is some way to execute this action through one click/keystroke instead of a multitude as it is for all other actions (with perhaps a couple exceptions) in the game. The reload time itself is still implemented through the action, and the mid fight reloads. There's your realism.

I've seen a couple people say here that having to manually reload might be a balance thing. However manually reloading is tedious, boring, monotonous, and buggy, and no gaming company should use tedium and monotony as a game balance check.

And reloading is buggy, often the first couple shots after you auto reload during a fight black hole. With low clip guns sometimes they won't reload at all, so there will be say 2 rounds left in a 5 round gun, but it won't reload. Sometimes during fights the reload's take upward of 15 seconds, reloading needs a serious lookover.

I believe that having one button (R) cause the game to reload your gun would be perfect. It is something all games should have, but strangely only funcom lacks this function for it's guns.

press R
1 second
2 second
3 second
fully reloaded

Is that so horribly unbalancing?
