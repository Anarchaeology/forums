*Reet's Retreat Narrative*

Tarryk runs into Reet's Retreat, and huffs his way up to the glass floor, shouting "I made it! I made it! My line's back up!!"

No-one is here. Tarryk checks his watch. 1:30am. He made it, all right. Several hours late.

Tarryk sits down in the center of the glass floor. He looks around, seeing if anyone's hiding about.

A draft comes from a vent shaft on the ceiling, causing a single fallen party streamer to sweep upwards from the floor. As if in defiance, it lightly slaps against Tarryk's face as the draft carries it away.

He looks down through the glass floor, and sees a janitor bot sweep several fallen streamers and a few official "Missmaul's Fashion Thingy" drink coasters into it's trash receptacle.

His elbows lean onto his knees, and he rests his jaw on his fists, looking dejected.

Nearly a minute of silence passes. Nothing but the howl of the wind shuddering against the eaves outside.

"Stupid grid lines," Tarryk says quietly, in reference to his failed connection for so long.

His eyes drift close.

"Stupid," he repeats, but doesn't quite get to the rest of it.

Tarryk falls asleep where he sits. His body evaporates into the air as auto-stasis kicks in.
