Well, I just bought one for one of my guys who has the Pioneer Backpack (+150 Tutoring, no IP spent for this guy on Tutoring yet). The one I got was out of the Basic Store and as such was really low level.

Target yourself and 'use' the device.

It required 140 Tutoring and gave me +8 to CompLit for a brief period. Effect was gone immediately after zoning. Don't know the duration if you don't zone.

They were supposed to let you transfer skills to other players letting you act as a 'teacher'. Recently, it became self-only (though, rumors are floating that they might have fixed it again so that you can once again Tutor others.)

The skill is basically broken unless you have a Pioneer backpack, have empty implant locations for Tutoring, or unless they re-allow Tutoring others. Having a 'teacher' in a Guild is a good thing as his IP's spent would benefit many people.

At extremely high levels it can be an option for 'pure' tradeskills characters (yeah, everbody has to kill) in that you can get +40 to the applicable tradeskills by only putting points into one skill. Even then, the sacrifice for the points into tutoring would have to come at the expense of another skill or the hassle of swapping out different sets of implants.
