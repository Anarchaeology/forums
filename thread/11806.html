Discuss: 13.8 General bug fixes and changes
  • You will now be able to apply implants when wearing social armour.
  • You will now receive feedback when trying to use Brawl and Dimach before the recharge time has expired
  • A bug with closing lockpicked chests has been fixed.
  • Some mission descriptions were missing locations. This has now been fixed.
  • Your faction will not appear as monster in the character creation any more.
  • You will no longer hear your own footsteps while flying with Quantum Wings.
  • Added new background bitmaps in the launcher. (Added Tuesday 19th) NOTE: This means that if you are using a different skin on your launcher now, it will be overwritten. If you would like to keep it, please make a backup.


Added Tuesday 19th:
  • Fixed a graphic bug that caused models to become distorted when using continuing level of detail.

Just a Game
I keep hearing that AO is just a game.....and while this is true to it's players, it is a JOB to the folks at Funcom.

Sometimes there is no excuse for the way that job is done....previously fixed bugs being reintroduced in a subsequent patch, for instance, show a lack of either the ability or desire to follow established IS standards.

And remember also, that much of what I work 40+ hours a week is my leasure time...and that time is very valuable to me....so regardless of how I spend it, in a game or otherwise, it has a greater value to me than the $11 a month I pay Funcom.

Golf is just a game to the folks to swing the clubs...but for the folks who run the golf course it s a JOB. Whould it be accepted for a golf course to have a number of the cups on the greens missing, or a complete fareway removed from the course, or tall grass on the green or quicksand it the traps??.....Of course not...but it is still just a game...

I can understand many of the problems Funcom has had with startup and the introduction of new bugs...that happens everywhere in software developement....and Funcom cannot realistically be expeted to do a valid parallel test of patches where it would involve 10s of thousands of online testers...so this final testing is ultimately done "live".

But what does boggle me is that after all these months, we still have inventory shuffling problems...and I do believe there was some backsliding with the problem after the last patch series.

I shake my head when Funcom asks me to recommend AO to my friends when they connot demonstrate the competence to fix this basic bug, be it because of a lack of priority or a lack of ability applied to the problem for some reason.

I do hope Funcom does what it takes to allow AO to reach it's pontential...but I do worry that they won't be able to also.

Thanks for your time..
