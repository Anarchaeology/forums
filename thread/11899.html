Weapons - why the game will be ruined if they keep this up
Hi, i am extremely displease at the way FC is taking the weapons in this game... Its completly unbalanced

here is my idea of how weapons should work, note that maximum spetials is related to the weapon class, not all of the weapons in a class will have all the spetials.

Assault riffle
Rate of fire: Medium
Regular damage: Very high
Critical Damage: high
Range: high
Maximun Spetials available: Aimed Shot; Full Auto; Burst; Fling Shot
Description:
The assult rifle is built for heavy duty combat, it has good power and uses large caliber amunition, it maker for a high range and very high regular damage, the strong recoil doesnt favor critical shots but the power of the rounds it fires makes for high critical damge, the rate of fire is medium to allow control.

Rifles
Rate of fire: Medium
Regular damage: medium
Critical Damage: very-High
Range: Very high
Maximun Spetials available: Aimed Shot; Fling Shot
Description:
Rifles are the weapon of choice when long range and accuracy is needed, they have high critical shots but only medium regular damge, the rate of fire is medium

SMG:
Rate of fire: Very High
Regular damage: medium
Critical Damage: Medium
Range: medium
Maximun Spetials available: Full Auto; Burst; Fling Shot
description:
Small machine guns are extremely fast and suited for quick combat, the size of the rounds is not too high caliber allowing for lower recoil and high fire rate, regular damge and criticals are medium, range is medium

Pistols:
Rate of fire: High
Regular damage: medium
Critical Damage: Low
Range: medium
Maximun Spetials available: Burst; Fling Shot
Description:
Mainly used as a light combat weapon or sidearm, the pistol has High rate of fire, the regular hit is medium, but it has lower critical damge for lack of power, range is medium.

Shotgun:
Rate of fire: Slow
Regular damage: very-High
Critical Damage: Low
Range: low
Maximun Spetials available: Fling Shot
Description:
Shotguns are intended as a close quarters weapon only, as the shotgun shells will disperse and loose its power at long range, the caliber is large and makes for a deadly weapon at close combat, range is low but regular damage high, this weapon is not meant for aiming at critical shots, its rather mwant to point in the general direction of the target and hit it hard, critical hits are low, the mechanism to operate the large rounds make it for a slow weapon.

Rules of thumb:
If a weapon requires more skills to use, it must have an advantage over other similar weapons that dont use as many
i.e SMG/AR combo would have higher rate of fire than the AR and higher damage than the SMG; and SMG/Rifle combo could give the option of AS;FS;Burst to the weapon
Everything must have a reason and make sence, there must be consistancy.

As it stands now, Everything with SMG on it is terrible, its weak and its not fast enough for at least have the aggro bar to deff, Everything with Shotgun is too powerfull, Criticals are insane (how can a shotgun be accurate enough to make high criticals?? it is powerfull enough to have very high regular shot) and its way too fast, c'mon you are introducing new SMGs that have fire rate of 4 seconds (3/1) when you have shotguns with higher regular and insanly higher critical firing at 1.5/1.25 whats going on...
Soldier should have powerfull weapons, agents the highst critical rifles, fixers fast SMG to make use of their evades, and the non weapon spetialised classes can choose wich path to take.

Has it stands now, the current weapon configurations is going to ruin the game behond anything you can imagine, eveyrone is turning to shotgun/shotgun hybrids, even Soldiers and Agents... this cannot be, i cannot relate to the weaponschemme it makes no sence. pls take a look at it and fix it good, you have the ip reset in the works, fix it before you do the reset...

Can someone pls agnolege i think its a good discussion you could bring the attention of the devs, maybe hire a weapons expert, a ex-military guy of sorts to give you(FC) some good advice, becasue the weapons in the game are completly ackward for their class. It's not fun like this... thers no sence to it

Thx for your time, hope you enjoy the idea or at least the heads up
