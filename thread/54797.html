Stay silent and loyal
The sharp sound of a pistol being fired surprised her, but not as much as the pain rushing through her leg. Did he actually shoot her? Damn! The pain was getting worse. Having no armor on made her take the full impact, and she couldnt heal it up while her hands were tied on her back. She looked at the blood gushing out from the wound in the leg, and could feel her strength leave her.

"Fielddress that wound to keep her from bleeding to death, but dont heal her"

The voice was oh, so cold...this person had scared her the first time she met him, and now she was terrified. Of course, she would never admit that to him or his goons. Her chin would be held high and she would stare into his eyes and defy his every try to make her crack. If only she just didnt feel so weak...

She underwent question after question, answering in a stubborn tone. No way she would give them what they wanted. They called her and her guild terrorists. Ha! Coming from the mouth of the oppressor...

Then suddenly, one of her guildmates' angry voice met her through one of the womens speaker comunit: "I am all for killing every Omni-Tek employee. They are all the enemy - there are no exceptions" ****! They had hacked their way into the guilds gridfeed, and of course they would present her with the most radical statement. Why couldn't they read it in context? Or at least read the rest? She kept silent as they laughed and told her over and over again how the guild were terrorists. She still kept silent. They asked her to tell them about the generals, the squad commanders, the planning of future "terrorist attacks" as they put it. As if they had any? We are not terrorists!

The wound began to throb and hurt more. She felt weak. And still she wouldnt answer them any questions.

"I wonder if your dear husband knows anything, Elica" one of the women said.

"F*** you!" She felt scared. They obvoiusly knew she was married, but it quickly became clear that they didnt know who to. And by the gods, they wouldnt get to know either.

"How about your friends? Do you care about them? Do you care about their lives?"

She started to cry, letting down her head and hoping they wouldnt notice the tears. And she kept silent.

The pistolhandle came so quickly towards her head she didnt notice the movement. She felt the sharp pain in the back of her neck as it hit her. Dazed and in pain she looked up at him. He smiled coldly, and pistolwhipped her again. And finally she found peace and relief of pain when she lost conscience.

Her body relaxed, while her tremendous feeling of loss and betrayal grew. She started crying heavily, and Garret removed the fingers from the legwound. Instead he moved to stand behind her, pinning her to him, very tightly. She didn't even notice he was so close to her, she was so utterly distraught, and her sorrow was eating her up.

Drowsy, she couldn't focus on anything. Her eyes were swimming and her head was heavy. It felt like the whole body was someone elses. She could see Garrets hand, holding the vial, moving towards her legwound. In slow motion she saw how the little worms looking like centipedes landed on her leg as he poured the contents of the small vial near the wound. With tears flowing down her cheek, she watched as they quickly found the wound and diappeared inside. She whispered "You...have killed me"

He asked her again, and this time she could offer no resistance. He was sitting in front of her now. His voice was warm and affectionate and he talked to her almost in a fatherly way. Every word that escaped her mouth killed her a little bit more, as she told him what he wanted to know. Her soul was empty and both the physical and mental pain was more than she could bear.

But, with some unknown force she mustered up a last reserve of defiance, and didn't give him the final piece of information he wanted. What did it matter? She was dead, and she had let down her friends, the only family she had left. She was nothing anymore. The only thing keeping her alive was the pain, and that would go away soon enough. The worms would eat her from the inside, and soon she would be nothing more than a hollow shell. Death would be a blessing then.

The warm and supportive voice was gone, and he was back to the normal, cold and hateful voice she knew much to well. He ordered someone over the commlink to make an immediate insurance update, complete with her physical condition, and to erase any traces of older insurance scans. He wanted her to suffer, as if she hadn't suffered enough, and now she couldn't even self-terminate. She never saw the fist coming towards her. All she knew was darkness, as he knocked her out cold.

1am: As the director walked back in to his office, the lights came on automatically, a low, dull, almost warm glow. It was well past midnight and Rome was mostly asleep outside, and the offices AI knew it.

Under the silent suns...
As the first rays of sunlight appeared on the horizon behind the trees, bathing the landscape in this golden light she knew so well, she simply looked up, and closed her eyes for a minute. She didn't pay anymore attention to the odd man mumbling about having being disturbed, in the yard between the buildings - what was his name? Zibell, or something similar? She simply stayed there, sitting on the landing platform on the top of the highest building, the platform marked with this large black star, indicating... what kind of remnant was it? An accident, maybe? A Yalmaha crashing, not long enough before for the marks to have already been washed by the wind and rain...

Earlier in the night, she was still in Omni-Ent. In a few minutes, she'd be back to the Grid. Travelling had become so easy for her now, and on a sudden and unexpected thought, she had decided to fly to the long Artery Valley a few hours ago to see if she could collect some carbon rocks, trusting the night to let her see better where the transluscent and slightly glowering rocks would lie. So many places. So much freedom in the infinite space. So many paths to follow.

"They're gonna come fer meh too", Jen quietly muttered, opening her eyes again. "Hei-kun. Hei-kun, tell me. Ya think it was a mistake?"

She gave a weird chuckle, her head filled with the thoughts of the Doctor's good friends coming to fetch them, her and Raquel and Corrine and Malcom and the boss, and certainly some of the others as well if they could lay their hands on them. Curiously enough, she wasn't afraid. She knew how the death process would go, and which states were to be encountered, and how long it'd take. When you had come back from perma-death once, there were some things you learnt to avoid this happening to you again.

Torture, yes. Torture was something she could fear, except that her mind was... too different for her body to really be a bother if she decided so. She was always phasing, anyways; it had been hard in the beginning, but now it was like a second nature again. And for the first time since long months, she had dreamt of the Heiji she had learnt to fear - not the young man she had known when her old memory was still a human being, but the desperate and hateful Fixer that had taken her hand on this cold night in the sewers, on what seemed to be a... century ago now. Heiji... Her second mind. Her dark friend and lover. She was never alone, and this, as long as she'd be alive; and it was something nobody else knew.

"Not a mistake. Maybe a crazy and dangerous thing to do. But not a mistake", Heiji answered with a kind smile. "You're just too reckless sometimes, Jenny."

She didn't answer to this comment. Somewhere, deep in her heart, she knew that it hadn't only been her decision. This was the kind Heiji, the one she loved, the one who loved her; but his old personality hadn't been erased, and sometimes, she had the feeling that her own actions were but a reflection of his anger. He had been reckless as well... hating himself so much, without even knowing it, that staying alive wasn't a priority anymore. maybe that was the reason, the reason for which she never was too regardant about hitting the Reclaim more than it was truly necessary.

Jen lowered her eyes on the disc she was still holding. The scans and other analysis she had done. All had been classified now - classified, and used to harm the woman as well. She knew they'd finally come, for her or for one of the others. Sooner or later. It was only a matter of time, before they figured out that she could be used a well, before they realized that what she knew and what she had already done could be another threat on the long term. She was the one who had found out about Damon, after all.

She simply hoped that when the day would come, she'd be ready to welcome them as they deserved it.
