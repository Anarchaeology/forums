For the record:
It should also be noted at this point that both Whompa and The Grid are the sole property of Omni-Tek.

"Omni-Trans administrates the airspace on and around the planet, and all official hyperspace access points in the system. They are bound by ICC rules for fair use, but may restrict traffic by competing corporations and governments according to predetermined trade regulations. Omni-Trans also controls and operates official Omni-Tek surface traffic  excluding the Grid, which is handled by Omni-Com  including the Whompas and the still uncompleted subway system." - Omni Trans
Omni-Trans (Omni-Tek Transportation and Shipping)

Be thankful that the greater corperation of Omni-Tek has not yet retracted the rights and use of those debated access points currently "controlled" by the Clans, nor has it ever used such as a point of pressure in it's endevour to find a peaceful solution to the Clan's hostile anarchistic activities.

On the other hand, do not think that Omni-Tek will further fund new installations of either facility, nor grant the necessary permissions to make use of any future installations the Clans may wish to build. It would be well within the boundaries of the Tir Agreement and ICC protocul for Omni-Pol to use whatever force necessary in preventing the illegal use and access to either. The ICC has prevented Omni-Tek from protecting it's rightful properties in the past but would NOT be able to intervene in the event of pirated hardware or illegal access to The Grid.

Remember, the Clans are the aggressors in this conflict. Omni-Tek has been more than patient and generous in it's approach, but there shall come a time when the gauntlet will finally come off. I truly hope that the Clans are ready for the war that they are, sadly enough, building themselves up to.

Everyone can fall astray at times, even Omni-Tek realizes and accepts this. There is still time for you, individuals, to rejoin Omni-Tek Corp. The Clans do not even have a unified agenda, half of the Clans intent upon their own dictatorship once their absurd fantasies of overthrowing Omni-Tek are realized. Are these the people you will call your friends, family.. and possibly one day, masters?
