Omni-Pol Officer: Lida "Templus" Pierce
Report: Investigation of Orbital Explosion

Time 11/11/02 05:11:00
Location: Omni-1 HQ

On November 11, the shuttlecraft Ravens Claw was destroyed. The ship lifted off the planet at 04:00 and broke free of the atmosphere shortly after. Since the ship was not under Omni-Tek ownership, records of its passengers are still unknown at this time.

The explosion from the ship was noticeable all across the broad side of the planet. The explosion can be described as a blue and white fireball with an extremely large visible blue shockwave. The fireball burned for relatively twenty minutes in orbit and shone a bright white light down onto the surface of Rubi-ka. The shockwave continued to increase in radius till it finally
faded out. Reports from the Morning Star Space station show that the explosion also sent as massive radiation wave in all directions. The radiation was examined and identified to be radioactive isotopic notum, which is highly instable.
No damage was sustained to the space station. But few reports are coming in about some employees being injured by the flash of white light. Burned retinas or something. Omni-med is doing an inquiry.

Another similar incident happened today. A much smaller, blast, similar to the one in orbit, occurred in the Perpetual Wastelands. Satellites have so far only investigated this explosion. The satellites report that the much smaller and focused blast also consisted of the isotopic notum. Since that area is deserted and nothing but a baron wasteland there will be no further
investigation into it.
