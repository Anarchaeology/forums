Cosmik, others, please read! BALANCING THE GAME! priority one! PROFESSION BALANCE!!
THis message I posted as a reply to one message prteviously, but I decided to expand upon things and put it in here! It is Important!

Profession Balance HAS to be your highest priority Funcom. Don't get so involved in the story that you forget that. Look at UO, they understood the importance of balance. They worked hard on removing all bugs, and making sure that skills worked well. Yes some things needed tweaking, but never as much as on here.

Professions need improvement! NTs, make them the artillery they need to be. Doctors, give them SOME fighting prowess, they will never be a PvP class, but allow them to be able to fight mobs well. MPs, WEAKEN them.. or if not, weaken, because I NEVEr believe anything needs to be taken away, without giving anything back. Nerfing is never a good idea. So if you dont' weaken, fix others to match. Adventurers, help them become a bit more that they are. Anyways, please read my post below for more.

Everyone, please support this, we need to puish the balance of things in the game: Professions, economy, organizations and others... We can only do so if we are constructive however. So no flaming on this post please. It doesn't help all of us.


OK Hi there... I won't lie to you and say I know all, and do all... but you removed our range... fine, you say we can't get that back, at least anytime in the soon future. Thats fine too... but I am spotting a great weakness in the supposed improvements you did to NTs.


ISSUE 1:
NT's are supposed to be the Nukers/Artillery... Am I wrong? That was my initiatial understanding.

FACT: If you calculate things over time, you come to a very basic conclusion, we do NOT do the most damage. A level 50 MA, can do almost 100-120 damage per one attack, and he attacks pretty fast. In 5 seconds, perhaps 4 with AGG at maximu, I can do 300 NO MORE. So, an MA just beat me out on things.

FACT: It CAN be fixed, and pretty easily. Make our Nanos more powerful, reduce casting time or increase reacharge time. truthfully, I believe the best oen would be increasing the power of our nanos, as the other two again make us use nano much faster, therefore again weakening us, and bringing us around in a vicious circle.

ISSUE 2:

The new patch was to introduce many things that would benefit us in the long run, and was supposed to balanace our profession compared to others?

FACT: Things are not balanced as things stand. An MP can solo The temple North of Tir, in Greater Tir. His twin pets, and soon to be THIRD pet are bring them so far beyond being balanced its not even funny anymore.

FACT: The changed you introduced are impossibilities. Finding any of those nanos is near impossible. i luckILY found one yesterday, and I refuse to spend what precious time I have in life, playing a game and SEARCHING for nano formulas. Why not just make our lives easier, and make them available in shops, perhaps for a higher cost. At least in GETTING the credits, I feel like I am working towards something, as I hate the feeling of accomplishing nothing.


ISSUE 3: Just so that you know, I am not only partial to NTs. I feel a LOT for Doctors and poor Adventurers.

FACT: Twin pistols is a very very weak skill. This HAS to be fixed. Adventurers can hardly solo anything these days.

FACT: DOCTORS.. oh poor doctors. Has anyone besides me noticed they are very very rare these days! Cosmik, CZ, you need to move on this... doctors are entirely far far too weak. Yes they can heal themselves which is excellent, and possibly could me on a one on one battle, since for every 300 I hit them for, they heal for 300, but with mobs, they are VERY weak.

Ok, enough from me, but basically there are many simple ways to improve NTs. Make us the artillery we are meant to be. We where supposed to be wizards, make us them. Weaken our health even, I couldn't care less! Implants are there fvor a reason, but make us damagers... we are meant to be so! Teams refuse me entry these days because what value add am I?? NADA. I am a weak NT, byu the time i throw my 10 seconds 400-1000 which most of the time ends up doing minimum damage since its a team attacked monster and its powerful, the fight is over. Please fix us this way. we don't ask for much, just balance the professions.

Priorities thatg you should deal with:

- Fix Critical Bugs: crashes, dangerous exploits not simple ones, etc...
- Balance Professions, lay your groundwork. Don't see every profession individually! see them as a whole, that is very important. Work mostly on balancing things for P vs Mobs as that is more important than PvP for MANY people.
- Fix Non-Critical Bugs
- Improve organizations... At least introduce an organization sort, that will put members in one list, and adds them instantly. Its important for me to know which ones of my clan are online etc...

Anyways, I've deviated a lot, but basically the storyline can WAIT... work hardest on making the game the game it was meant to be, the vision YOU saw.

Anyways, thats all I ask... I hope this message does not fall on unlistening ears.

Before I start, I understand that enemies need some sort of advantage since they can't think as well as the standard Homo sapien, but come on. When I attack with my dual QL83 Sentinel Strike ETE pistols (dear God, multiranged is expensive) which I would consider relatively near my level -- perhaps I am mistaken -- I can rarely do greater than thirty or so damage per hit. Now when taken with the extremely good Attack and Recharge times of the ETE pistols and my Agg/Def at full, it doesn't seem like I'm doing too bad for a doctor. My enemy, however, manages to deal out 130+ damage and attacks roughly the same amount of times that I do with only one pistol.
