The Droid on the Hill
This was written as a tribute to R.U.R's 1-year anniversary.

"The Droid on the Hill", by Walter "Shadehawk" Fernatoro


A long time ago in a far away place,
a sun and a planet revolved through deep space.
The planet was beautiful, lush and green.
A "Second Eden" said the surveying team,
and named it Ara-Rakeen.

And humans came forth, from faraway Earth,
to settle the land, to children give birth.
The colony company sent with them a droid.
For such is the rule, it cannot be void.
"An android must always go first".

And soon the new colony, strong and good,
on a lush plain near a small hill stood.
The area scouted, no danger was found.
The earth was ploughed, seed put in the ground,
The colony's future was sound.

The droid was soon given other tasks.
For although it's face was a human's mask,
the people got tired of his mechanical ways
the tireless searching that went on for days.
They told him to climb up the hill.

"Go up on that hill", the android was told.
"Keep watch from up there, for dangers untold".
The android complied, and went up the hill.
From there he kept watch, motionless, still,
and guarded the town down below.

The people were happy to send him away.
"No need for him here, up there he can stay.
And he will keep watch, whatever may come".
But some things a guard droid can't shield you from,
like sickness and illness and death.

For one thing they missed, the surveying team
who proclaimed Second Eden on Ara-Rakeen.
A centennial flower grew up through the mould,
a rose with magnificent colors in gold.
But with thorns that meant slow, painful death.

The people rejoiced as the flower unfold
a beautiful rose, a sight to behold!
The fragrance was perfume, so light and so sweet.
But the very same fragrance that lay 'round their feet,
was a poisonous fume most extreme.

And soon every human on Ara-Rakeen,
succumbed to the plague, so cruel and mean.
They signalled for help, they called through the void,
But the only one near was the guardian droid,
and he had to burry them all.

For vast is the void and the signal was weak,
like shouting for help from a high mountain peak.
So no help arrived, and soon all where dead.
Every man, every woman, every child in its bed.
Forevermore buried on Ara-Rakeen.

The droid having finished this horrible task,
had no one to turn to, no one to ask.
He climbed ever slowly back up on his hill
and sat himself down, perfectly still,
and guarded the tombs down below.

Slowly the days turned into years.
The droid sitting up there, alone with his fears.
For who is to say, that human emotions
can't somehow be made into complex equations
that runs through an old android's brain.

For eons he sat there, never asleep.
His last order standing, to vigilance keep.
The internal nano-bots keeping him hale,
but forgotten by humans, through time's misty veil.
For none came to Ara-Rakeen.

The long watch he kept through his iron-strong will,
to finally it seemed that time itself stood still.
So with boundless sorrow and a longing so keen,
the last one to die on Ara-Rakeen,
was a lonely old droid on a hill.
