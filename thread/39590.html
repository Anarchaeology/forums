quote:
Originally posted by Chinamon

my friend has dual Xeon processors OMFG!!!! he still cant escape server sides, but the robustness gives him a better chance of no timing out.
Last time I checked, this game wasn't written to take advantage of multiple processors. I'd be interested in seeing what his utilization was across both processors while running AO. Multiple processors in a PC is mostly wasted money if you don't use applications that are written to take advantage of more than 1.

If you really want a machine that can crush the competition, go AMD, put your game data on a Raid 0 array, cache the partition your game sits on to minimize hits to your hard discs since they are MUCH slower than RAM, get enough RAM (fastest RAM you can get) to handle the cache, game, and OS requirements (most likely 1GB+), and make sure get the fastest video card you can afford. You can try to get a massive amount of RAM and disable paging (best results for performance, but is unforgiving if resources run low). If you can't disable paging due to lack of memory, put your page file on a fast raid array on its own volume to maximize performance.

You should tune your video card to the best visual experience to performance ratio (entirely up to you).
