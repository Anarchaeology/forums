Alternative: 1 pet user, 1 or more ranged/melee attacker.

The lackeys of the Trash King (all eight of them) will always /follow the person/pet that attacks them first. Thus, simply sic your pet on the lackey, then stand out of range of the AoE nukes and safely hammer the lackey with ranged attacks.

Of course, you can also go into melee combat with the Lackey, but that enables it to strike back at you.
