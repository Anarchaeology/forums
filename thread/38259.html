Pomy, multiple Camelots have been there since its introduction. FC just 'tweaked' the parameters in almost every patch since.

This however doesnt mean i dont aggree with you on the multiple instanse issue. However FC will have to decrease spawn timer to compensate for the lesser loot.

One thing most people tend to miss, though not FC i suspect, is that while a 18 hour respawn on a 2k max people server may provide enough loot for it to be sparse it does in no way work on a 15-20k subscription server.

I essence an 18 hour spawn corresponds to a 4-7 day spawn time in other lower populated games with respect to loot pr person.

Since we, the players, several times have stated that camping was not our favorite past time i suspect that other issues is holding FC from changing this. Most likely a desire to keep the high lvl's in the game for as long as possible.

This in essence is one of the things that the clan side has been feeling lately. Since most of the high lvl PvP oriented clanners got the T loot back in the early days they stopped coming or quit due to boredom.

What we need is more high level bosses like T, faster spawn time, less loot pr boss. Put one in Omni HQ, one in PW and one in DAV. No need for nice big modelled dungeons with hours of artist work. Just throw them into those already empty zones.
