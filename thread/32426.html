A new and brave neutral org on RK1: Circle G. Your safety is our business.
(Newland, text: Steve Caine)
Official records state Circle G Inc to be an intergalactic firm, offering professionally trained security personell for guarding, escorte, and transport of personell and goods through insecure areas. They are now establishing a branch on Rubi-Ka.

Malcom Tedder, often referred to as "Great Father" amongst the employees, is the fifth generation Tedder who runs Circle G Inc. A corporate firm that appears to recruit only family members for the highest positions.
This is one of the very rare interviews any family member have granted the press.
The occasion was Mr. Tedders visit to Rubi-Ka, regarding the official opening of their new department on this planet. Not less than seven of the hugest Atroxes Ive ever seen, are guarding the room. Something best described as an army, is guarding the quarter around Neuts'r'Us, for the occasion cleared of regular visitors by Mr. Tedders personal assistants.
Mr Tedder himself, a rather tall, but thin man, is leaning backwards in the sofa. He is comfortably drinking of a glass supplied by one of his assistants.

- "This is a small town. And a rather small planet. And most importantly, we have not done any business here yet. We have no enemies here. It represents no risk for me to visit."
Tedder takes a sip of his drink.
- "However, it would be rather unprofessional of me to risk drinking local beverages, dont you think?"
Malcom grins.

Circle G is by no means a large company, compared to its competitors. However their existance have gained some attention. Some parts of the Intergalactic press have renamed the Circle G Inc as "Crime G Inc", and its members as a "lawless mob offering protection only from themselves and their associates, run by a family whos moral standing is quite questionable".
- "Public relation has never been our strength", Tedder admits, with a concerned expression in his face, "And how can we fight these ruthless accusations when most of our customers and assignments, for obvious security reasons, wish to stay anonymous?"

Circle G is a new, neutral company on Rubi-Ka. We are well organized with a professional and experienced staff of managers and board members. Our current status is recruiting employees and train newly hired employees.

Curious? Dont believe the rumors. Dont believe the hype. Please have a look at our website, and get the facts straight from the source.

We are afraid Malcom "GreatFather" Tedder are currently unavailable for interviews to the Rubi-Ka press. For further information or questions please contact our director of the Rubi-Ka department of Circle G, Deanne "Dreammate" Tedder, by sending her a /tell. A reply is guaranteed within 24 hours.
