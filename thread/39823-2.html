I'm not sure I really like any of these suggestions, but then I think I have a completely different attitude toward PvP than most people.

I don't like the idea of title toggling. I tend to think that a title means you're a hardened soldier who's seen it all and willing to face any battle armed with your experience...not a gank-scared dueler that's never had more than one gun pointed their direction at a time. Allowing title toggling just accentuates the need is many players to have the "best" title to make you "uber"...which is just annoying.

I can understand the fear of losing your title in "PvP Events" though. It makes sense to me that points are only gained or lost in "real" combat, meaning, if I take on 4 people and kill them all, I get a good number of points. If those 4 people gang up on me and kill me (teamed or not) they don't get much and I don't lose much. Of course, this would require a major reworking of the current PvP system, but this needs to be done pretty badly anyway. Hopefully, the current system works like MOBs do in that the only person that gets credit for the kill is the person who did more than 50% of the damage to you...otherwise no one gets anything.

Also, if zoning during combat is such an issue, just don't have combat end when you zone. I'd like to see PvP chases span the world and end in the Basic Shop in Old Athen. That'd be kinda cool. Obviously, the combat has to end at some point; maybe when there are two or more zones between people.

I do agree with the side restriction though. It's utterly stupid that you get titles for killing people of your side. I think the whole concept of "dueling" PvP in a war oriented game is absurd. We're supposed to be a war (or nearly at war) yet the only PvP that really ever happens is 1 on 1 lameness and side isn't really an issue. Silly.
