Shhhhhh!
Hmmm, I was just thinking..........yes, about Enhanced Queen Blade

quote:
600 phys init = weapon speed lowered by 1s/2s*

-the "neutral" spot on the aggro bar is 87.5% aggressive.

-for every 1/8 of the aggro bar, the speed is affected by .25/.25. So if you place the bar at full aggresive, you get a -.25/-.25 speed bonus for your weapon.
-from Chronita's Init FAQ in the MA Forums-

HUGE thanks to Chronita for her hard work coming up with the formulas

So, what does this have to do with our new(hopefully) weapon? I'm about to tell you!

I currently have 412Melee init with +107 Physical Dominance running, after the -425 from my ql250VE scope. With damage of 619-1383(66), using a scope with the EQB isn't even an option. So, remove my scope and I'm back up to 837 init. Add in Infernal Rage for another +350, and you get a grand total of 1187 Melee Init. Close enough, we'll call it 1200 for easier conversion with Chronita's formulas.

Recharge inits are currently bugged....only IP/implants are affecting recharge inits atm, buffs don't affect it. So, while the entire 1200init would apply to the attack speed currently, 457 of that is buffs, and won't affect recharge. Call it 450 for round numbers, and 750 init is the total affecting recharge.

The EQB has attack/recharge of 4/7.2

The attack conversion is easy....each 600init is -1, so 1200 is -2
The recharge conversion is a little harder, but not much.
750/600=1.25........1.25*2=2.5 (remember, recharge is affected by 2 instead of 1)

So, with the current bugged inits, there is a reduction of 2/2.5 assuming 1200 init. End result is a weapon speed of 2/4.7. But remember, that's based on an aggro bar set at 87.5%. So remove another .25/.25 for full aggro, and you get 1.75/4.45

Yes, that's still really slow........but this weapon does AWESOME base damage, so it doesn't seem out-of-line to me.

NOW, for the hat trick. When recharge inits are fixed, it will make a HUGE difference. instead of reducing recharge by 2.5 at 87.5%, recharge is now reduced by a full 4 seconds. Without boring you with redundant math, the end result is 1.75/2.95 at 100% aggro.

My point is this: this weapon is being introduced before the recharge inits are fixed, and I'm not sure the devs considered this when they set the damage and speed. Once the fix comes, I think we need to be prepared for cries to "nerf the uber Enforcers!"

I've never used a damage calculator, so I'll leave that up to someone else. Again, the numbers to run are:

1.75/4.45

AFTER FIX
619-1383(66)
1.75/2.95
