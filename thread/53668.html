Ideas for making the new camps/bosses attractive
Though I'd dedicate a thread for listing sugestions that could make the new camps attractive. I'm sure most of you feel the same way as I do, that you looked forward to dyna camps and are very bored with BS missions.

I find the current dyna camps very unbalanced concidering the risk vs reward part.

IMO, you need to tweak the following moments, based on my experience from many hours at the new mercenary camp (aka ace camp or bcd)

1) Increase the xp even more since its outdoors its more risky and you should get a higher reward. You got higher chance of adds. I got no accure numbers to give but maybe 10-20%?

2) Mobs like primus/secondus stuff needs to get their nano resist tuned down. I dont mind their evades but atleast make them root/debuffable. Support profesisons like crats wants to have a role at camps too. But as said quatrus/teritus are no problems.

3) I have yet to manged to pull together a team that can empty the camp so i have no idea what'll happend then. So just a big maybe you need to look into the spawn for the camp(s). Doesn't seem to spawn fast enough if you're only there for the easy front door camps (the ones SW and NW i think).

4) The camp bosses are wayyyyy too hard unless you expect several teams to co op. Hmm lets see. Blitz a mission and get 1.3M xp and a easy boss with no hp and wussy dmg with 'decent' loot or a hard boss who does a AE for 2-3k every 10 secs and does fair dmg (200-1400) and drops 1 Q205 normal armor bit? the camp bosses needs to be tuned down abit if they're gonna keep that insta warping thing who warps you if you're even close to the whole camp. Would sugest 2-3M for killing the boss maybe, else its not gonna be worth it, and tune down him so 1 team can do him.



And about the other bosses. Feel free to keep them as tough as they are right now, i dont mind, i can die as many times as i have to when im not looking for xp. But make it guaranteed to drop _something_ atleast. I dont want to fight 2 hours for -nothing-. The mobs are hard enough anyway.

And, I see the jack clones as a sucesfull experiment, reduces need of camping as the same time its abit rewarding to camp the real jack. However the clones shouldn't have fixed drop, for instance you have one clone that drops the part for the MP quest. Make it random what the clone will drop since the clone are so hard you need several other professions to kill him and most people wont come to help if they can't score at getting something for their quest.

To be updated with your ideas/concerns or when I think out something myself

However I would like to see FC's view on outdoor hunting vs missions. Rewards vs Risk.

Edit: Agree/disagree? I want a debate here so we can show FC what their customers really want

My guild did a boss camp once. 6 of us flew out to an obscure location killed some smaller mobs and then went for the boss. The whole process of killing mobs and boss probably took 2 minutes. Mobs were giving 20-30k and the boss gave 300k (!) xp. Yeah there was no exp reward but it didn't take 15-30 minutes. Yeah the loot was bad but everybody already has 5 sets of albrecht and enough advent nanos to cover the whole planet. Overall we were happy.

Problems I see:

What to do after killing the boss. Should we go out and look for another camp? Should we stay and wait for the boss to respawn?

Sheep mentality. BS missions are pretty straightforward and safe. You shout lfg for a few minutes, join a team and you know what to expect. With outdoor camps you might end up with mobs that're too hard or too easy. You don't know where you're going and there's constant exploring.

So while camps as they are may be just as good, the whole teaming and hunting system is different. I can't really see this catching on because humanity tends to resist change.
