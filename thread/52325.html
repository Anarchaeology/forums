QL does matter
The difference between a QL118 and QL200 is the time that it locks a particular Nano skill out. The default time is 30 mins for all Grafts minus 5 sec per QL. So a QL118 will lock a skill out for 30mins - QL118 x 5 (590 secs or 9 mins roughly) = 21 mins that that skill is locked. A QL200 will lock a skill out for 30mins - QL200 x 5 (1000 secs or 16 mins roughly) = 14 mins. Pretty considerable difference there. I think that perhaps unfortunately you got ripped.
