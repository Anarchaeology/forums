A Guide to Rubi-Kan Flora and Fauna

Omni-Tribunal and Tribunal Langels are pleased to publish, for public perusal, 'A Guide to Rubi-Kan Flora and Fauna' - the most detailed classified Rubi-Kan beastiary reference ever attempted. Given the sheer scope and diversity of wildlife on the planet, this beastiary is by no means presented as a completed reference at this time but rather as an on-going project.

The brain behind this huge undertaking is Gabriella Nynx Rouse, Tribunal Langels' intrepid eco-geographical surveyor and photographer extraordinaire. This is what Nynx has to say of her work:

"From the moment I started here, I longed for the day when I would be able to travel the planet, finding animals and plants that few people ever encounter. Exploration of the planet, and discovery of it's plentiful wildlife, eventually went from a simple hope to an ironclad goal.

Out of this desire for exploration grew the concept of the beastiary. Generally, there have been many resources devoted to explaining tradeskills and profession setups, but there have never been any high-quality resources devoted to the animals and plants of Rubi-Ka. Perhaps this is because of the sheer size of the task, perhaps because, before now, this was not deemed as important as other aspects of life on Rubi-ka. Whatever the reason, little was known about the existence of much of Rubi-Ka's wildlife, even less about their appearances, locations, levels, strengths and abilities.

When I began exploring the world, after receiving my Yalmaha from a mission, I made it my goal to rectify that situation. Thus, I created this beastiary. In it, one will find a detailed classification system, along with pictures, strength information, levels, general locations, and descriptions of an organism?s behavior and appearance. Hopefully, this information will be expanded by the community at large, and together, we can build a dialogue about hunting, exploring, and the world of Rubi-Ka outside of the cities and the conflict."

Omni-Tribunal is a privately owned, privately funded Research and Development Firm. Tribunal Langels is the Exploration and Reconnaisance Specialist Unit of Omni-Tribunal.
