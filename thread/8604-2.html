Alright, Gentlemen.

I've already gone over most of this in my last statement, which can be found in the "Tir Accord Broken by Omni-Tek" thread. So I'll do my best to just recap.

The very freedom of the Clans is a threat to innocents on Rubi-Ka. There are many Clans that do not follow the Council of Truth and operate independently of them. The Council has no power to control or police these groups, for that could be considered an "infringement of their rights" or some such nonsense.

This results in terrorist actions that require Omni-Tek involvement. This is why in Annex 2B: Police and Investigation point one of the Tir Accord we're allowed to move into Clan territory without the permission of the Council.

If the Clans decide to remain defiant to our investigation...total war is imminent.

There are a number of results, all of which are pretty nasty.
  • ICC allows Omni-Tek to wage a full-scale war on the Clans. Most likely, the Clans are wiped out with heavy casualties, both military and civilian, on both sides.
  • Omni-Tek, due to either outside influence or a reserved ICC, is forced off planet. Confidence in ICC falters and the Confederation collapses. Hypercorporations answer to no one but themselves, resulting in increased injustice towards employees and uncontrolled war between rival corporations. Sol-Bank takes control of Rubi-Ka, subjecting the populace to a treatment a hundred fold worse than Omni-Tek ever did.
  • Omni-Tek, for similar reasons, is forced off planet. Rebellions occur in every hypercorporation. The balance of power in effect for tens of thousands of years collapses, and the human race enters a dark age era of anarchy and strife. The Omega very well might come out of hiding.

Now, Rubi-Ka is a harsh planet, and controlling it calls for harsh policies. This is unpopular, but unavoidable. Besides rival corporations desiring war, there are mutants, Cyborgs, Dusters, and God knows what else we have to deal with. It is possible for us to deal with them as a unified front.

When the problems are dealt with, we can talk about improving worker conditions.

Rubi-Kas the most profitable planet in OTs pocket.

President Broegan...one Marcus Tulluis Cicero, a man of ancient Rome...that is, the Rome of Earth, not Rubi-Ka, coined a phrase more true today than ever before.

"Endless money forms the sinews of war."

If we continue down this road, we are both doomed. Defeating Omni-Tek will pave the way for a new hypercorporation, say Sol Banking, as surely as an extermination of the Clan would bring about further resentment and rebellion on this planet...notum is the endless money that Cicero spoke of so many millenia ago.

We need not resign ourself to this dark fate. We have the foundation for peace, let us follow it.
