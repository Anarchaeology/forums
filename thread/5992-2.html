My 2 creds
Love the ideas, teh balconies and gardens are great, but....one problem. Where are they going to put all this? If they do what you say it will look like a college campus with frat houses. First off, to do this they will have to come up with the real space, I.E. Server space. That mainly takes guild houses to under the stove and off teh back burner. Next, explaining it in-game... Omni-Tek owns Rubi-Ka, and we were all employees at one time generally. We were all "put" or "engineered" here. Lavish living quarters are probably near impossible on such a place.
However, the barracks style talked about would be much more down to earth. And, much easier to explain. As an omni-tek faction, you have the barracks to keep the morale of your faction up, as well as hygene and what-not. As clan, much easier. Your faction uses a barracks because it is more defensive and makes for an easy staging area. There would be no way to explain the mansions, this just isn't that kind of world. Anything past the simple pleasures is not in this world as we see.
Now, the training area thing, that was awesome, but I got one up for ya. A team mission terminal in the guild house. A teleportation device to take your team to teh mission (in other words, that's the mission entrance.) You get almost no money for winning, because that goes to OT to pay for your faction (easiest way I saw to explain it anyway, no uber-money making.) Another part, is that there would be 2-5 rewards depending on difficulty, and all the rewards go to the guild bank directly after succeding teh mission. These missions, however, should be a higher difficulty. a new slider could be added for # of teams. I believe many guilds would lunge at this chance, to assemble the whole guild, section groups, tackle missions with more than one group, and arrive back at your bunker. That is basic life on rubi-ka. These missions should have multiple objectives as well, and the harder one would leave some out...kinda like hidden things. Ya know, kinda like this situation.....engineer - fix this, fixer - hack this, agent - sneak and recover this, enforcer - break this, soldier - kill this guy, doc - steal this serum, and so on...
Another problem of guilds is teh dreaded message board, no one checks it cause they are almost always in game....so move it in game. Nothing like checking the boards after a long mission......especially since we bought that bartender vendor. Which leads to part 2, vendors... Each one you get costs so much per month, but that money has to be made through the missions aformentioned. Thus, the money stated on them goes to teh guild pool, and pays for extras.
Ending notes - Apartment size off of guild rank. Each place has a hallway with doors so people can get to their new apartment, after bringing furniture with them. Should be enough doors to accomidate even the largest guild. Next, name above your door. Easy enough, eh? Great suggestions all, but remember, This planet is harsh, so try to keep in perspective that none of us make enough to have mansions in the big scheme of things. But hey, ya never know....maybe they can add furniture as a trade skill as well.
