Yes.
Leveling is THAT important.

While your story is certainly something that should never be spoken publicly about again, it is a proper indication of ONE (1) of TWO (2) styles of gameplay.

(1) You're a roleplayer. The game could suck ass mechanically and you'd never know (or care) - clearly that's the case here. Unfortunately for you and others with similar mindsets, you are in a minority in most MMORPGs. I'm not sure why you're seemingly unaware of...

(2) Gamers. Obviously people who are obsessed with being the best and pursuing power-leveling, constant item upgrades, and twinking fall into this category. These aren't bad people; they just have fun not-doing-table-dances and not-paying-monthly-fees-to-run-around-as-a-cat. For them, the fun is in the math, the thrill of the upgrade, the wet dream of a good wrangle. They make up the majority, which is why you see more of their kind of complaining - they, for all intensive purposes, have leagues more to complain about. Their style of fun is regularly violated and threatened.

To put it in perspective, think if FunCom started removing sunsets, alcohol, and shapeshifting from the game. Your aforementioned story that you so thoroughly enjoyed wouldn't exist at all.

So yes, leveling is that important, if leveling is important to the individual. I, frankly, wish there were more people that existed between the two extremes.

Anyway, now you know.

Some might think I, of all people would never say this: yes, levelling is important in AO. It's a great enabler.. allowing you to explore the far reaches of Rubi-Ka. Some interesting places are placed in rather obscure and dangerous places, which sadly, a level 14 has a rather low chance of survival - even if accompanied by a high level group (adequete speed buffs for instance might take more NCU than you can spare).

But to those nodding your heads now, I should also temper the above with the sad fact that if you're levelling for just the sake of levelling, you'll soon run out of 'uberness' to aspire to. No one is saying it's a wrong way to play, but honestly, I pity you the same way I pity people who want beamers for show.

Me, I'm just happy playing the game that is AO, not the rat-race some people make it out to be. If I had wanted to do pure number crunching, I have a trusty companion.. Microsoft Excel - and that doesn't have a $12 monthly fee.
