A fond farewell from an old friend.
ENTIRELY OCC:

Well folks, I am drawing out my white flag and stepping away from a game that has become a part of me. I have fought the battle for far too long and no longer have the drive to fight it.

I have handed leadership of Vixen Entertainment over to my trusted friend Treylla. I hope that she has the urge and drive to continue to wage this war without me around.

I wanted to say goodbye and a big thank you to all of the friends I have made on Anarchy Online. I will never forget you .. and those who want to, please stay in touch.. I'm only a click away.

For those of you who work at Funcom: I would like to say that I'm greatly dissapointed in how this game has gone from a wonderful roleplaying experience to a sex driven pubecent nightmare. Not everyone mind you, but a good majority of the people that I have seen recently want nothing more than to go into a bar and cyber for credits. This is not hte game I had grown to love and I find it heartbreaking that my cries have been ignored for so long. All that I can think is that those who answered my plea want the game to turn into what it is today. If any of you care enough to find out more details, to possibly prevent losing more of your long time player base, you have my contact information.

I will continue to check in on Treylla via ICQ to be sure everythign is still functioning.. and again, my friends can always contact me.

I will miss you all.. and I hope that one day, we will meet again in a more advanced and mature universe!!

LOVE ALWAYS - Aktrez
