our rules aren't strict rules but are there none-the-less.

1) We have a uniform, which I or Guilt can provide for those that can't afford it. I also provide med suits and fist aid/treatment kits for those lvl1-3(ish). You dont have to wear uniforms in cities if you have over equiped your armour (I have been OEd for the last 30 lvls). But the must be worn at events and meetings.

2) Free trade amongst guild members (if you don't like this, don't advertise amongst guild members)

3) No tax, but donations to the bank is appreciated.

4) Promotions are given regularly
-Unit member received just for parading the correct uniform infront of a general or me.
-Completing quests
-Taking a bullet for the President (thx GENERAL Logiee)
-Being a good Ranger and recruiting good players
-Taking time to help out new players

6) Asking for help with regards to buffs and what-not is fine, but NO BEGGING! I often give small 5000 cash bonuses to lvl1-3 members but, don't expect it.
