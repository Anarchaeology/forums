Some background on traders.

Traders have 0 buffs to help them before combat. Everything a trader has to increase damage output has to be done during a fight and only lasts 3 minutes. Traders have a blue/green shotgun skill (only 4 points per lvl), Dark blue ranged int and blue evades. Since 85% of our nano lines are completely useless, we really only need 2 nano skill lines so we can streamline ourselves for PvP better saving IP wasted on the other nano skill lines.

Before the crit and heal nerf a trader was very hard to bring down.

Now traders can't heal during the fight and we don't crit nearly as much as before (high attack rating helps).
Weapons:
Since shotguns are of 2 types:
A. slow, good damage with 1 or no specials.
or
B. Fast very low damage with a high crit,
Because of these options
traders seem to get wailed on a lot now.

Fast, high damage from multiple specials are what wins pvp now. Before everyone was running around with snakemaster and uvc, now everyone is running around with nova/flashpoint and Alpha'ing people... even traders.
