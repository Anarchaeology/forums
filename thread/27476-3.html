Hmmm... yes Odin's is a very useful buff for an MP. It's the buff that allows MPs to get pretty much all of the most used nanos and pets in use by level 130ish. This is, however, a double edged sword. Many MPs go into semi-retirement shortly after this because there's nothing left for them to get really. So in a sense, it shortens the average lifespan of an MP, which isn't a good thing. Basically, we start going backwards compared to other professions from here on in. Admittedly, we're pretty uber at this level so we're going backwards towards a norm, but still...

We also get some very nice benefits from some of the creation shields/weapons, though these are generally not used much by MPs beyond level 125 or so. For those MPs who decide to continue to higher levels, at around this time they'll need to add damage with a secondary weapons skill to keep up with damage being done by other professions and the weapon of choice will be a ranged weapon which cannot be wielded at the same time as the creations. The only non-creation weapons choice that MPs have that can be wielded with a creation is 1HB, which most MPs will never choose because of low damage, low crits and it being melee.

The benefits of the shields/sticks can be used by high agg instacasting MPs. The creation weapons then help in allowing them to nuke for longer. But in order to keep up with the damage that (for example) NT nukes can do, they really need to be chain instacasting their best nukes which have a very high taunt factor due to nano skill debuffs being built in. (we can cast MQ twice as fast as LDI but get about half the damage mostly). This also removes any defensive shielding that the pets could offer, because the MP will always get the agg in this situation above any pet. I've played this style myself and mostly got agg even above NTs in teams as a result.

Also, as for NTs, to get the instacast MPs need to use hardware in NCU slots which causes them far more problems than it does for NTs as most MPs need huge amounts of buffs to get odins/mochams etc in for their pets (MC/TS/BM) and some for the nukes they'll use too (add PM to the list) and some for the Chant line of pet buff (add SI). If you've ever played an MP you'll know that NCU is always at a premium.

On top of it all, of course, Ankokujin did neglect to mention that the Cobra and Asmodian creations he quoted require 1030 skill in TS, BM and MC.... oh and 680 in the Dark blue 1HB and/or 2HB skill to wield... It's a huge IP investment especially if you go for the 1HB creation stick alongside the creation shield and have to add lots to Multi Melee too...

It's a complex thing to understand how certain routes that seem to be available to MPs aren't really valid for most MPs playstyles. Still, I do think that removing odin's would be good for the MP game overall. It is Odin's that make the gap between infuses and Mochams so short. It is odins that leaves MPs with few useful nanos to chase after level 130 (making the game pointless for many). It is odin's that shortens our lives and many of us would be happy to see it go.

As to whether NTs should get it... that I'm not so sure on. Not really my place to say either. I do think that NTs should have the NP buffs though... and a recharge reduction buff too!
