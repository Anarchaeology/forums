Force melee weapon users to reload!
Those of us who use ranged weapons (80% of us) all know the great joy of having to manually reload our weapons.

A few oddballs have asked for a reload button. They have implied that we'd be better off with such a button, and some have even claimed that Funcom programmers are dumb as a fence post and couldn't figure out how to implement a reload command.

Don't listen to those oddballs. We LOVE reloading manually! There's nothing more fun than opening your inventory screen, opening your wear screen, left clicking on the bullets, right clicking on your weapon, then closing your wear screen, then closing your inventory.

And the best thing is, we get to do this thousands of times during our stay in Rubika!

But it makes us sad that melee users are missing out on the fun. >They< never get to experience this, the most enjoyable aspect of the whole game.

So I'd like to propose that melee users also be forced to reload their weapons. Give melee weapons some sort of "ammo", it doesn't matter what, give their weapons a clip (probably a clip of 5), and a long wait while the sledgehammer or sword or whatever "reloads".

At first they'll complain, but just wait! Once they discover reloading, they'll be thankful for the change, believe me.

Open your inventory. Whee!
Open your wear screen. Fun!
Left click on the ammo. Wow!
Right click the ammo on the weapon. Woohoo!
Close your inventory. Yes!
Close your wear screen. Ahhh!

I predict that the enjoyment of the game will be doubled for melee users once they too are allowed to reload their weapons dozens of times a day.

I mean, other ranged weapons users must find this to be fun too, right? That must be why Funcom won't add a reload button. It can't just be that those at Funcom are too lazy or incompetent to do it, can it? Of course not!

Just a few thoughts here.
Trying to see it from FCs perspective.

Ranged weapon users have several advantages over
melee weapon users. Reloading might be a balancing issue.
(as opposed to just a realism/roleplay idea)

A ranged weapon user can pull more effectively than a melee'r.

A ranged user can usually hit first, and can sometimes knock
a good chunk of health off the mob/opponent before they
get close enough to hit back.

Many ranged weapon classes have roots,
which helps them maintain the range advantage.

Ranged weapons generally have more special attacks,
and those specials often do up to 10K damage at higher levels.
(brawling really cant compete with an alpha strike)


In addition, many weapons appear to have clip size
and reload time factored in to their average damage.
Why do some guns have a clip of 5 and some a clip of 120?

If this is the case, then someone more anal than me might
consider manually reloading during combat to negate the
automatic reload time close to an exploit
remember Q-tapping to avoid recharge time with hammers?

IF this is the intention, FC should really say so
instead of ignoring all the 'reload-key' threads.
