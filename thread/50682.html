I agree and disagree. It is fairly difficult to have any sort of coodination in 2h0 because of how battles work there. Because zoning options are so near by, they make battles almost pointless. The only real battle you get is around the hill and whompa when you first charge in. After that it is just a matter of trying to take advantage of someone zoning out of bunkers, trying to sneak up behind you, or waiting for someone in a yalm far above you to de-yalm. This isn't real combat, and cannot be coordinated. Omni also have a very clear advantage to holding 2h0. It is difficult for clanners to amass a force there when they are not in control. Omni can easily amass a force in 10-3, where we have a save point just 100m from the Whompa.

I have been there a number of times when someone who knew who all the clanners were would call out targets for everyone to attack. This sort of one-at-a-time strategy would work to at least alieviate some of the clan pressure. After we evened out numbers more it was just a matter of charging in and hoping we end up on top. Not the best strategy, but it worked most of the time.

I had been there on a few occasions when someone like Vergil would actually try to coordinate an attack. We actually got a couple people in the grid waiting for signal, and some of us were supposed to ferry in from 4holes and attack from the north. However I was the only person who seemed to understand how to get to SEB from 4Holes by way of ferry. So when we all attacked we couldn't devide their forces enough for our three front attack to do anything worth while.

Part of the problem with coordinating attacks, is that most Omnis don't listen to what anyone has to say. They just want to rush into battle and kill some clanners. These kind of selfish acts don't help our cause at all.

For example, the Omni were holding the box room in GS2 a few weeks ago for a good 40-60 mins waiting for T to spawn. This was the day that Vergil went into Camelot as a Freshman, and left as Apprentice. We killed clanner after clanner after clanner and were amazed we were doing so good with our small numbers. However the clan were packing GS2 full, so when we died we could not replace anyone. Even though we said countless times not to follow clan up the stairs, people did anyway. This allowed the clan to pick us off one by one until we were down to about 15 people. They then stormed the box room at once and easily took the remainder of us out. it was a great 40-60 minute battle for the box room, and we probably would have won if we didn't allow the clan to pick off our numbers so slowly throughout the battle. :/

Omni need to learn to listen to people who appear to be in charge. Those people are usually shouting orders because they've been in that situation a great number of times before, and they know what is going to happen if you don't listen.

Just my thoughts.
