View from a gimp
Was told this thread was open... Dealt a lot with the issue we're talking about the day it happened. Both with players and Funcom.

Let me start with saying.. This is not ENTIRELY about what happened with the 4 banned players... this is about you, Funcom... this is about where you stepped wrong, call it constructive critisism, call it whining... but don't ever call this a lie, because this is how things have been.

A little review of what happened:

In order to get some fuzz out of the way on what happened to the 4 people we're discussing here (Hauruk, Meyra, Shtylez and Cocio), I, on behalf on a decent ammount of omnis asked for a chat with FC to get some questions answered a couple of hours after they got the boot.

I sent a petition where I asked for a Gm and a person from the exploit team to come and clarify things for us (as there was problems sorting out what really was concidered a bug or not).
As I've had a lot to do with the exploit team and a lot of GM's in general I expected *some* seriousness on my request.
However, I was met by a GM alone.
I explained the situation, our opinions, asked my questions and pointed at some contradictions in the rules versus the threads they've written and explained the frustration that was going around on RK2 at the moment.

Thing that upset me about FC's handling of this situation is this:

- After 15 minutes, the GM stepped away from the situation telling me he didn't have anything to do with this (obviously he felt the tenseness in the situation and didn't have a long enough pole to keep it at a distance)
- I was told to gather up the people, send an email to "support@funcom.com". This mail would be given to the GM who would put it in Cz' box who would concider if this message was worthy a post on the boards or not
- I MAILED FC ON THE CORRECT ADRESS AND DIDN'T GET A REPLY!
- When we, after being let down by FC, refusing a direct conversation on chat, irc or any other "Real time" thing, ended up writing down some things on the board, the thread was closed by Cz after 15 minutes.. was the same "type" of discussion as this. Now.. I know FC had gotten a lot of both hopeless shouts ingame and on boards concerning this, but to Censor the thread Grontha made and moving it to somewhere people wouldn't read it is *HARDLY* something that raises the allready fading view many people've had on FC's incompetance on gathering the GM's arks and the rest of the staff around a COMMON policy and treatment standard on players .

Now.. if I hadn't have to leave the next morning for a course, I'd taken this further than the "exlploiting by killing grey mobs in trade" - joke we had that day.

Before I continue, I'd like to say something about Funcom. I saw the birth of FunCom, I've seen the problems that've been in the past (inner loop.. hint hint) I've been there IRL... I've taken a cig outside listening to the peeps working there talking about AO, their coworkers, bosses and the players ingame (respecting the people who told me what they did that they perhaps... shouldn't've... told a dedicated player, I will neither reveil what was said nor whom I talked to, but it was a parade of dissapointments... FC doesn't know their own game, the society or the responsibility they've taken upon them is all I can say).

Now... Funcom deliver a massive product, What can we expect from them? what is the *minimum* we can say is expected from a company with close to 100.000 players.

1 - Clear rules
2 - Respect
3 - Information when something happens
4 - Equal treatment
5 - Professional customer treatment

All of these have been broken from time to time. There's no way to avoid this, as they all are human beings, BUT... what I've experienced with other companies (as I have worked with simmilar things) is that when something happens... you simply *DON'T* push players away... you face them, tell them what happened, get feedback, correct what might've been wrong and improve. This is how you get respect...

When it comes to banning... here's something to chew on:

A teacher of mine and I had a good tone.. I was Quiet.. talked when I should, did my homework etc...
One day me and a bunch of friends, filled with evil, decided to play a little prank on the teacher. We heated up the doorhandle to the classroom "somewhat" before she came.
She came.. burnt her hand badly, and in rage, she was searching for someone to strike down on as an example... I never got a notification.. I told her I was a part of it... The only ones who got a notification were some loudmouthed people in class she didn't fancy. This just to set an example. they were out of school for 1 week for that.

Now... this is pretty much what I see has happened on RK2. 4 cignificant players known on both Omni and clan side have been weighed, measured and found a good bunch to use as an example to everyone else. Now.. before you shout back that "this is an exploit, I don't use it.. they do.. ban them..." think about this:

They are allready level 200. They can do anything they want... if they get another char to 150... so what?.. If anyone should be struck down at, I say pick the ones who never went the hard way through 1 million BS missions, who never ran their own 200 armor at level 112 because they get the higher levels to do it for them or bought it from money they begged from the occational 150+. Strike down on someone who didn't spend a year trying to improve the game. Make an example out of the ones who make this place a troublesome soap-opera (who you decided to make Professionals instead... again.. you don't know your own game).
I've heard.. "hey.. he might be PL'ing a trader... He's going to get a PVP char going..." now using your own imbalanced PVP system as an accusive point in the argument doesn't become you at all.

If you haven't caught my draft yet.. here it is again...
I didn't know that PL'ing was concidered an exploit untill I heard "Deng... they banned Shty, Coc, Hauruk and meyra...".
Now.. I searched some for the information about this exploit... in some time, it popped up on top of the forumchannels again. I was told by another guildleader that a GM Popped by Leader channel (around 5 am, my time) and said that it was oficcially concidered an exploit now...

Well... That's good and all.. but did you concider that this alone *might* reach just a few people.. and the chance that all guildleaders are on at the time able to inform their people of these news, equals the chance of Bush being able to write a good speech himself. And did you concider the non-guilded?

Here's some Ideas:
