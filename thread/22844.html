Lets chat about Battle Royale
Ok gang, I'm a little concerned about a couple things regarding BR, here is why.

1. I'm not so sure this game will be the same after the 14.2 patch.
2. Even if it is the same, how long will it take before all of our pvp friends are finished with IP's and ready to rock.
3. How many of the champions will be different characters after the patch and thus nullify the fact that they won a BR in the first place?
4. What professions will hurt? Who knows? Nobody knows till its done and on live.

The reasons above lead me to the following decision.

Battle Royale MUST conclude in its entirety before 14.2 is released.

This means, we are accelerating the process.

Next week will be 2 BR's, the week after 2 BR's. The next week, we will have the championship for the 20M and your web page on the www.totalaggression.com/ao site.

MoK is developing a contest for teams next. The rules on this team play will be VERY specific and NOT negotiable.

MoK would appreciate some support from the larger guilds to make this team combat worth doing. It is our intent at this time (can change without notice) to offer the winning team a 600M credit grand prize.

We also want to see more clan's come play this new team tournament so the place of holding will change and will NOT be in an Arena. So far, we are thinking of lining MMD with powerful PVPers to guard the teams while they go at it in the 25% zone there.

Your idea's are valuable! Please post input here.

We are also coming up with a form to fill out so that you can enter your team in the Battle Royale - Team Annihilation Tournament.

Regards,
