Asheron's Call
Good post Lohr. I think Funcom could learn a lot by looking at Asheron's Call. Of all the mmorpgs to come out that I have played (everything but UO) I would say that it had the closest thing to a storyline. It also was the closest thing I have seen to a true "world" in any of these games so far.

With each update they gave you a little background on that months story and maybe a little info on one of the monsters found in the land.

Asheron's Call had seasons, In the winter you got snow on the ground and new types of clothing (winter robes, some NPC's in heavier looking clothing) heck you even got new recipes for food products. (fruitcake recipe for Yule). Asheron's Call also had neat quest that told you about the different races of people or the areas in which they were found. There were several libraries full of stories about the ancient events and people. Plus players could write scrolls and books that could be passed to other characters. Even weapons/armour had a place where you could type in a customized message. I found weapons with everything from the coordinates of the dungeons they were found in to poetry to guild recruitment on them.

I have all ways felt saddened that AC's game play just did not click with me for some reason.
