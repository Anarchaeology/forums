Bank Share for Organisations
I would love to have a special bank that only people in the same organisation could share. For example, it could be used as an organisation's armory.

Sample uses:
As a soldier, I could visit my org's bank and reload on some ammo. My org could just stockpile 1000's of rounds of ammo.

A member in our org just leved, so they visit the org bank to see if there is any new armor that they can equip.

For a Omni-Pol type organisation, they collect full sets of Omni-pol armor so the whole org can match. Old armor could be recycled back in the bank so lower level members can use it.

A nice usage for the /org bank. The org tax funds can go toward purchasing items that can be stored which members can share.

I would set member access based on org level. E.g. Directors, Board, Members have add and remove access. Applicant level would have only add access.


What do you guys think?
