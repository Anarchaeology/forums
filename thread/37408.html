Why do you want Level 200?
Getting to Level 200 requires you to spend many hours doing the same repetitive task and without a change of scenery. How long this takes depends on your team, the competence of your team, and the level of the mobs; thus, resulting in powerleveling or faking LinkDeath because your team is full of newbies.

Theres a large part of this community where their typical day in AO is finding a team in BS, getting a mission, and standing around harvesting XP from the mission. Its easy and quick, the most efficient way of getting experience- so naturally thats what people do.

So...
  • Why do you want to get to Level 200?
  • What do you plan on doing when you get there?
  • Do you find missions borring?
  • What keeps your from canceling?

And for those of you that don't have level 200 as your goal...
  • What do you find fun in Anarchy Online?
