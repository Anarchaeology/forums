While I like it in a hypothetical sense, in actuality any MMORPG that did this would have way too many people showing up at events, resulting in the server crashing or the people being too lagged to really participate. The only reason events work in a game with 2000+ players is because 1900 of them dont know about the event or choose not to show up. I have not yet played any MMORPG that can support 200 people in close proximity, much less 2000. Getting notification out to a significant number of players about an event ensures its failure.
