Mission map and Probe droids
There is one feature i for long missed, but was unable to put my finger on. I finally found out what.
As i was running through a mission dungeon, and had to zone out, it annoyed that the map was reset (the small overview map, u know). Since i had been in this dungeon just seconds ahead, why the heck did it turn black again?
Fix this!
As I pondered about this, it struck me that it should be a way of checking areas out, ahead of where u walk... Damn i hate to come running into a new room, and to see that there are -2- mobs, yellow, standing so close to eachother they would be shown apart on maps... I want a droid, a probe droid, maybe in form of a nano (FIXER NANO, this one is PERFECT for fixers!), which i can send ahead and probe around with. Should be real simple to implement this: A controllable probe, what it sees, is shown in a small window in your own screen. That screen also contains controls (left, right, back, forward). Let this droid be a non-aggroed one (would be meaningless if a ql/lvl 1 droid entered the first room only to be aggroed by mobs!).
Another reason why i want this (and why i want this for FIXERS, cos fixers are.... -fixers-..), is to check out and maybe plan ahead which route to take towards mission object.
Being able to use this in wildlife/outside: Send this ahead to where your mission area is, and do some surveillance to see if there are enemies if the area is PvP

Final note: IF FC EVER USES this idea, call this thingie Teknolord Inc Probedroid(TIP)
