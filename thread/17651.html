First of all the benifits of a guild. Well, thers always friendly people to talk to, teams, and help. The drawbacks, well that depends on what kind you join, and what style of play you have. If you are a roleplayer, you won't be happy with a powerlevel guild, and visa versa.

basically many guild who list themselves as roleplaying guild focus on more than the level and mission aspect of the game. A RP guild makes stroies for their characters and plays them out. They are interested in the social parts of the game. Most stay in character throughout all conversation, but not all do however.

Sea of Change, the guild I lead is a roleplaying organization based on Rubi-Ka 2. We differ form other organizations because we are focused on true roleplaying and making a differnce on RK2. All our members speak incharacter in guildchat, as well as to perfect strangers. We want to add more events to the planet and make it more exciting. We pride ourselves on good RP, not levels or items. We are always seeking those who have a like mind. If you are interested in joining us, contact "Sepentura" on RK2, or email serrephim@hotmail.com
