I've been playing AO for 2 months now (wohoo), and I've spent quite a lot of time reading the forums.

A1: Vektors, maybe Krutts. High crits and packs a mean punch.

Funcom has written about and stated that over-equipping (OEing) IS a problem and IS going to be fixed. Only about 1/10th the population of the game are severe enough over-equippers that there 'game' would be seroiusly affected.
