Back in the day: Weren't we gonna be selling vehicles? And furniture!
Back before the day-long rollback and mass exodus of players, there was big talk of allowing players to sell their vehicles to other players... what ever happened to that idea?

It's a pain in the ass to find your first one, and once you do you usually find 10 more that just sit in your bank. Are there any plans of changing this?


Something else that's bugging me, you can only have 10 items in an apartment (not nearly enough to decorate it) and you are limited to pictures, plants, lamps and drawers. We need to bump up the item limit to say.... 30? Then we need more furniture, chairs, couches and beds. You have that furniture in the missions so it wouldn't be very hard to throw a price on it and put it in the furniture machine.
