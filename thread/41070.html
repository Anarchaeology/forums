Medusaes in Foul Plains: Conclusions
Me and 5 other people, all level 150+, spent a couple of hours in FP killing Medusaes, and from the experiance I came to some conclusions about why it is not a good leveling spot/what would make it better.

1) What kind of hunting ground is it supposed to be? A high level or low level hunting ground?

A)If it supposed to be a 150+ hunting ground it has the following problems.

The monsters are too spread out, not concentrated enough. It is simply too hard to pull them fast enough to keep up a steady flow of experiance.

The monsters hit too hard. Proper exp monsters, say....mission mobs, give the same or more exp as medusaes and hit for the same amount, but hit half as often. Meduases double hit for 500-600, mission mobs hit singly for 400-700.

They do not give enough exp per monster given the above two limitations. Mission mobs are both easier, die faster, give better loot, and are easier to get to,. All without the risk of PK death.


If you intended the foul plains medusaes to be a level 150+ hunting spot, you have failed. To remady this situation you need to make them more concentrated, make them spawn faster, make them hit half as often as currently. Or you could just triple the exp given per monster.

Doing these would make the PK-death risk and lack of loot acceptable for the tradeoff in exp leveling rate. At least it would then be compareable to missions.



2) If you intended this to be a level 100-125 hunting ground.

It is suatible as is, except that the medusaes need to hit half as often as currently and the zone where they are found needs to be made 75%.

It is foolish and blind to expect level 100-125 players to hunt in a 25% zone. A single high level player who has saved with nothing to lose could easily cause a complete wipeout. There is no reward worth that risk.


It needs to be made more accessinble.

You can expect level 150+ players to have yalms and be willing to fly out to the middle of nowhere. You can not expect that of a level 100-125 player. Place a whompa and save termial nearby, moveing the omni outpost located SE of the medusaes several thousand meters closer and adding a whmpa to it would solve this problem adquatly.






Currently the medusaes in Foul Plains serve no purpose whatsoever in AO. First you need to decide what you want them to be, then you need to implement the suggestions in one of the above two proposals.

Outdoor hunting
Outdoor hunting in general needs to be more attractive to players. Its pointless the way things are now. I could stand in a far away zone and shout MA LFG! till I'm blue in the face and I'd never find a team.

Outdoor hunting generally consists of non-human mobs. These mobs are more powerful and more unpredictable then mission mobs. There aren't any walls or locked doors to block LOS so the chance of random aggro is very high, especially for all the high level mobs which are pretty much ALL aggressive. The majority of high level hunting is located in meyhem or political zones, saves are far away, travel time is much longer, the loot doesn't even come close to what you find in missions, and the xp generated is actually less when you factor in the XP reward given by missions, as well as the xp generated over time. The risk/reward factor is unbalanced and that is why everyone chooses missions.

Missions are controlled environments with tons of cash and loot just sitting there begging to be plucked. Outdoors is risky and difficult without a good team. Not to mention the loot basically includes monster parts which sell for what, 5 bucks?

Players used to swarm around Rubi-Ka, exploring the different zones and discovering good hunting grounds. I used to go flying around DAV, Mort and EFP and find people all over the place. Now, those zones are empty. If I see a white dot on my map I think, wow, another player is out here, only to find out seconds later its a NPC.

When players hit on a good thing, it was nerfed (ie. medusas). Not long after, FC handed us easy xp on a silver platter within missions. Now the routine is to find a team in Newlag and grab 3 team missions for Broken Connections resulting in faster leveling, faster token generation, and easier access to high ql loot with barely any risk at all. I just wish FC would make up their minds as to how easy they want it to be for us to have it all. (Keep in mind camping for weeks for an item isn't hard either, its just mindnumbingly boring and very frustrating.)
