did this with my guild once. we had everyone line start at same spot. . .here were rules.

1) log off and make a new char and lvl to 3
2) name has to include something u announce then and there so someone can't make or use a prefab alt/twink. I think I used "run" as our word that had to be in name. We had Runninmofo, Runnynose, etc.
3) then they had to run to clothing shop in Rome Blue where a race manager was. The racers would be handed 50k to spend to get some mirr clothing and then head to shop to get a weapon. (note no armor allowed and weapon only for show so they can get creative)
4) then the first one to start line that was lvl 3, had mirr clothing on, had "run" in name, and was holding a weapon won part 1 of race.

Start line the gate in bs in the omni-tek base. . .where u can whompa in.

Finish Line of race was to run NW and reach the Clan whompa also in BS.

Now u might think that's a short race. . .well it took hours and many deaths for someone to finally make it. We had high lvls in guild following in Yalms and snapping pics of them running and hiding and skirting enemies and also pics of them falling, screaming, and dying. Lol one member lvl 130ish got grabbed and killed by an ermite while flying. Its a good route for lowbies cause a lot of aggroing mobs.

Now if u really wanna make it fun. . .have a few people go make clanner alts (or omni if u are clan) because there are many 25% pockets in BS. . .hehe.

Then they get prizes for:
1) first to start line
2) first to finish line
3) best dressed
4) best name

Have fun. . .always nice when people do things outside the normal lvling grind.
