k, i'm rly bored atm so here ya go ^^
What i was thinking, was a system like this

For example, say there were 4 guilds participating.
Guild A, Guild B, Guild C, and Guild D

Guild A has 50 people in guild
Guild B has 40 people in guild
Guild C has 25 people in guild
Guild D has 10 people in guild

Guild A currently has 80 points
Guild B currently has 60 points
Guild C currently has 90 points
Guild D currently has 50 points

On a tara raid, exactly 5 people from each raid came.

Each guild gets + 5 points (1 for each member who attended)

When tara is killed, Adv ring, and dragon chest drops.

Each item that is won by a guild will take a certain number of points away from the guilds points.

Lets say..
Adv ring is worth 6 points
Dragon chest is worth 80 points

When you roll, the item worth is the max points you are allowed to have in the roll, and the number of points that is taken away if you win.

Any guild can roll for the item, but when the item is won, that number of points is taken away. ( no you cant go below zero)

Guild A C and D rolled for dragon chest.

A = 85 points
C = 95 points
D = 55 points
(each guild got 5 points from their 5 people going to the raid)

Guild A won the roll, and 80 points were taken away from them, leaving them with 5.

Guild c rolls for the adv ring with 90 points (-5 points or so from rolling d chest) and wins, leaving them with 84 points. (-6 from winning ring)

At the end of the raid, the points are as follows
Guild A = 5 points
Guild B = 65 points
Guild C = 84 points
Guild D = 50 points

Doing something like this would do a few things

1. Because therse a max allowed points that a guild can have in a roll for 1 item, it wouldnt be impossible for people to win specific stuff like it is now.

2. A few people in the guild could be incharge of handing out the loot, and maybe the guild could set up a sheet of things people want.

3. Because people would only get things they need, less stuff would be up for sale on forums, and tara l00t would be worth more.
