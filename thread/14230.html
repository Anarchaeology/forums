new idea for static missions
I know Funcom has talked about having the game be about more then just killing monsters of xp. Having professions do what they do best for xp points is a great idea. They have started some of it by giving xp for doing implants or picking locks etc....but lets face it you can't lvl off of making implants...its just not feasible. What i think would be really cool is making static missions that would give you xp and or lvls upon completion. My main is a 97 soldier so i'm gonna just go with an idea i had for soldier but you can make up whatever you want for your class. Make a mission for a low lvl soldier similar to Boot camp. You have to go to some training ground to meet your Drill instructor...become proficient in some weapon skill and training...once you pass you get xp or a lvl or a new understanding of something...like the token board you have a training board or something. Get a patch for completing boot camp. then when you get higher lvl you go off for training in a certain weapon maybe or something like that. Get a specialist on the Nova flow, flashpoint or whatever gun...bonus would be a boost to attack rating when using that particular weapon or something. Make them so you can do it only once and that more then one person can do them cause when they come out you will have ppl camping for HOURS or DAYS to finish if they are worth it. you could have something like that for all professions. like a internship or something...just to give us something to do besides killing mobs for xp and money and items. I think it would enhance roleplaying and would in the end be something that would be kinda neat. Any Thoughts?
