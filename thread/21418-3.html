Actually ...
Well, initially I had the same reaction everyone else has had: Booooooo.

However, the more I thought about it, this is what I came to:

Newbies aren't going to know the difference anyway, so even though it's easier, they'll still enjoy it and find it a challenge. The levels, while I thought it was high, gets them through their first title level and quickly to the point where they'll start to see their gimping take effect. With IP resets, they can correct that and keep going, or if they're egregious (like IPs in throwing), they can start over and level back up pretty quick.

For the experience players, all it means is you'll spend a lot less time in the levels that are probably boring to you anyway. A steady diet of muties should get you past 30 in no time. Hopefully, this will foster a lot more experimentation (let's see how my opi NT with zeniths would work ...) and possibly (gasp!) less emphasis at rushing headlong to 200.

Yes, there will probably be a perpetual pool of 30ish newbies, but they should be getting into enough trouble at that level to wise them up pretty quick. The only real drag I see is that all the panhandlers that have infested Ent (at least on RK2) will be 30 and asking for 200k credits instead of 25k at level 2.

Though I am still bitter I wasted time on my alts ...
