Thoughts on Aggro - an idea
My thought on aggro, that I've presented to others goes like this:

a mob aggroes on someone who has built up most "Aggro-points".

As you damage the mob, you build up aggropoints. Some type of damage is more "fit" to steal aggro points from a mob.
Mongo builds up a lot of aggro points if chaincasted.

Now the reason I believe it works like this, is that I did some tests.

------------------------------------------
point by point result of the some recent on aggro that I did:

Testing Damage-aggro in mission with an MA.

- Full def, not attacking enf
- Enf mongocrush 2 times, MA attacks
- after 5K-ish damage, aggro switches to attacking MA
- Mongo with enf, Mob comes back, but returns to the MA after a couple of more hits.
- Mongo, aggro back, MA finishes off the Mob being hit 1 time totally
-----------
Testing Calm-aggro in mission with Trader.

- Full def, non attacking enf
- Mob Calmed by Trader before fight starts
- Enf mongoCrush twice. at first mongo, the Mob attacks Trader, second, attacks enf
- Rest of the fight is pretty much the same as with the MA

note: didn't try with Trader Depriving
-----------
Testing Heal-aggro In mission with doc (and 2 support players for documentation):

- Full def, non attacking enf, non attacking doc
- 2 mongos, Full aggro on enf
- Doc Single heals Enf twice, gets aggro

- new mob, same thing, but this time, Doc Team heals once and gets aggro on this.

- new mob, same thing but only enf and doc in team now. takes 2 team heals to aggro mob now
------------
Testing Mongo-aggro in mission with another enforcer:


- Full def, 2 non attacking enfs
- Enf1 cast Crush, gets aggro (both identically buffed, neither with Dmg shield or reflection, slightly different AC's and evades)
- Enf 2 cast slam. Gets aggro on 4th try
- tested 3 times, all identical results on both enfs

- new test, identical, but Enf2 casts Bash.
- Enf 2 gets aggro after 3 Bash

- 2 Smash as Bash.
---------------

Testing General Debuff aggro in mission

- Full def, 2 non attacking enfs
- Enf1 cast Crush, gets aggro (both identically buffed, neither with Dmg shield or reflection, slightly different AC's and evades)
- Enf2 cast inexperiences. after 5 tries, aggro is stolen
--------------

Now this is *not* neccecarily a definite thing as I believe there might be other things that will depend on aggro, as we know even breed has something to say on different mobs
I'd love for some more input on this.

Btw, a non-aggro mob drops aggro on you if you dont inflict damage on him/her within a certain ammount of time (1minute or so)

Here's a hypothetical theory:

a mob has unlimited aggropoints to build up

Say the mongo line gives the current number of aggro points:

Slam - 50
Bash - 100
Smash - 150
Crush - 200

- Regular damage gives 1 aggropoint per 10 damage inflicted.
- Regular Heals gives 100-200 per cast
- Team heal gives 50-100 per person healed in team that is in attack and that is in range of recieving it.
- Calm lets the mob start with about 100-200 aggropoints on the one who calmed him when the calm is broken.
- Regular debuffs deal out 40-50ish aggropoints.

Now this is just the start of something and is not to be taken as a "truth" or a "what on earth is this... I've mongoed 20 times without getting aggro.. u suxorz".
I started this out because of some discussion on the mongo line and various views on the ammount of aggro gained with this.
I would *love* for some people on RK2 to contact me for further testing on aggro, (or for some RK1'ers to do some dirtywork too )

Lots of things to test since I've had little reply in my petitions to recieve some sort of system on the aggro-system.
