can't buy them? Blitz them
Most people don't use the equipment they find in missions. You need to overequip to survive yada yada yada.

But you should be able to do 100% mission fairly easy as an enf. Go and farm for something pretty at 100%, find a fixer, get a run buff.

Go to the mission site

Cast all you combat nanos, and set your agg/def bar to full def.

Cast whatever mongo you use and get yourself to full hp.

Go in the mission and run your booty off looking for the mission item (btw, assassination missions aren't blitzable ). Grab it, look at the train of nasty mobs following you and start dancing until you get recycled.

Wow, you just got a ql 140ish item and a good handful of cash.

No its not quite this easy, but it can be done. Just keep trying.


Edit : Yah you'll have trouble finding the exact item you want from the mission terminal, it very well might take 10-20min of putting coins into the booth before you find one. We all do it, it sucks, but odds are you'll find one. I've seen plenty (lvl 102). If you don't find one after 20min of farming, go and get a lvl and try again.
