My thoughts exactly... That place is great fun. I tried out one of my newbie twinks in there, and it's VERY cool.

Particular things I liked:

- Spread out so that you don't run into griefing, KSing too much. (Different pathways, areas)
- Great progression (as you level, you can make it further and further down)
- Ambiance (music changes in different areas, MOBs for the area actually make sense, great textures and level design)
- Upgradable armor, etc (For a newb that kind of stuff is GREAT)
- Good reward (you can actually get XP in there, and decent loot)


Echoing the sentiments of Taniya, here's hoping that we see more dungeons like this for us high-levels. (I'm not saying the high-level places aren't fun, there's some great designs there too, but no good reward for hanging around! When was the last time you went to a dungeon for XP?)
