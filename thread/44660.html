An Idea about the Booster Pack, to placate everyone.
Here is what I propose:

So this booster pack supposedly takes a portion of Shadowlands that everyone wants, and instead of making us wait for Shadowlands to get it, allows us to buy this extra booster and get it early.

I feel that since they are taking this route, that the people who buy the booster pack should be able to get Shadowlands for a lesser price when it does become available. Say Shadowlands costs 40 bucks at a store, and the booster pack costs 15 bucks. If you purchase the Booster Pack you should be able to get Shadowlands for 25 bucks.

This would appease all those who are yelling about Funcom ripping us off, and at the same time make all the people yelling for content able to get what they desire. If you don't care for PVP or the new features in the booster pack, you can pay full price in the store for access the Shadowlands.

This would be easily done in 2 ways:

1. Put a coupon in the Booster Pak for so much off the purchase of shadowlands.

or

2. Sell them both in the online store, along with an account upgrade key, and allow people who have upgraded their account to be able to purchase the Shadowlands at a discount from the online store. Also you would have to make sure they can only buy 1 discounted Shadowlands, that way one guy who bought the booster doesn't buy a shadowlands for all his friends.


Just an Idea
