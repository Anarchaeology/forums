Cz, Cozmik...do you realize?
With each patch, AO is getting closer to simply being EQ with a Sci-Fi theme. Check it:

Static dungeons (memories of SolA, LGuk, ToFS come to mind...you do them once and never want to go back)
Static mobs (memories of the AC and his ridiculous spawn time (in excess of 24 hours I think) come to mind)
Static quests (reminds me of the Wizard Staff of the Wheel quest. At least the different environments one had to travel through to get the pieces made it somewhat interesting.)
Camping galore (Jack the Legchopper = EQs Ancient Cyclops...everyone needed to kill the ancient cyclops to get his ring which everyone needed for the JBoot quest and now everyone needs to kill Jack the Legchopper for the profession ring quest.)

This is disappointing to see....a game with such potential turning into an EQ clone. I came to AO in hopes of escaping the mind numbing amounts of camping in EQ, and now it seems like I never left.

I have spent much time climbing the level curve and I don't ever think I will have the patience to reach the top of it, considering both the time it takes and the fact that there's only one viable method of advancement (BS missions).

14.6 is coming soon and I'm hoping that one of two things will happen in 14.6:
1. The bugs that plague a majority of the game are fixed...
2. Something drastic is done to the game (like a dimension-wide nerf on camping) to change the horrid path this game is treading down.

Hopefully 14.6 won't make me to ask the one question I don't want to ask....: Who wants my stuff? :\

After the first time doing the Alvin/Dodga quest I decided to leave quests alone. Standing in line or camping an NPC that spawns every 4 hours is tedium and the slaughter of mech dogs that took place after Alvin/Dodga was "fixed" so they never despawned was stupid.
A logical solution would be to make the NPC's spawn randonmly and for them to move around so they are not so easy to find. The problem with that is that we as players are lazy and would surely whine about how hard it is to find the NPC... Apparently Leg-chopper is a wandering NPC so we will see how that turns out. Trust FC to come up with one NPC to kill for all profession quests, though...

What is more worrying to me is that in their attempts to improve the game they by adding items and new armors etc. it seems like they have no clue as to what would be really useful for different professions/breeds. A lot of times the requirements are totally impossible to attain and if they were the items gives totally neglectable boosts. In their desire to fill AO with "content" the design philosophy is 'the more, the better' instead of 'less is more'. I know for sure that I would appreciate less items, but ones that would be truly useful, useful enough to do a quest for even.
Another thing that they are doing is ignoring problems that a lot of professions have, like our NCU belts. Instead of forcing me into Carbonium armor in order to gain more valuable NCU's, why not simply make us move things like Recompilers from our belt or make 7-8 slot belts. There are lots of problems like this, but we all know that, don't we?

I know for sure that me ever entering Camelot would ruin what love I still have for this game. For starters...it's a castle with a dragon in it, in a sci-fi MMORPG!!! 'Nuff said. Secondly...I hate camping and just the thought of entering a raffle to get my hands on some nice loot boggles my mind.
Unique and rare loot should drop randomly from a range of mobs, as it is now you have to be in a faction (preferably a high level one) to stand a decent chance of getting the truly useful items in the game. Combine this loot with outdoor hunting and we will suddenly have a reason to venture outdoors again. The new boss camps is one of AO's worst ideas so far, their idea of content seems to be more and longer fights against mobs that still are variations on the ones we have been fighting for 50 levels, sad indeed.
Spread the good loot all over Rubi-Ka, give players a reason to venture out in the wild. Instead of more or less forcing us into 4 or 5 places where the good stuff is, spread some out far and wide, make us explore and hunt.
Imagine an Omni news bulletin saying that a unique mob have been spotted somewhere in PW carrying a delivery to the clans, like a Nullity Sphere MII. He has to be stopped and whoever stops him gets to keep the crystal. SCRAMBLE!!!
It doesn't take much...


I was awed when this game first came out, I was blown out of the water and thought I had found my new home away from home. Of course you get jaded after a while but I feel FC should have been able to look after this world better than they have been doing so far.

Another big problem that AO suffers from is the way missions reward us with items. By allowing us to mission for items, the load generated on the server from all the item farmers using clicksaver causes a bunch of the lag. The concept is flawed and the implementation has negative effects on game play.

You could easily solve this by doing two things:
1. Make a majority of the items (up to QL 200) easily available in the shops and remove the item reward from missions and objectives. This would drop the need for clicksaver altogether.

2. Allow us to specify which zone to generate the mission in. By doing so people could get the mission they want the first time around, thus eliminating much of the server load generated by repeated clicking of the "request mission" button.

Lag is bad, treat it as such.
