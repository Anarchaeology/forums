The biggest problem I have with the big nukes is that there is very little reason to use them. They have the highest dam/time ration, but so what? More time = more chance for interrupt. Also, on these nukes, it's more time wasted in the (likly) event of a fumble or resist.

The dmg/nano of all the nukes is really close to each other. Well...All the nukes I have. The lowest was .5 and the highest was 2.9. Most were in the 2 - 2.5 dmg/nano range. I personally think that the longer the nuke takes, the more dmg/nano it should do.

For example, if you've got a 3s nuke that does 2 dmg/nano, your 10s nuke should do at least 5 dmg/nano. That way, the two nukes would have similar nano costs (the long one would cost more), but you would make up for the long cast with more damage.

As it is, it's just about as efficient to chain-cast your 1s nuke as to cast your 10s nuke. You get less dmg/time, unless your 10s nuke gets fumbled, inturrupted, or resisted, then chain-casting is signifigantly more efficient in terms of dmg/time. And even without the fumble, inturrupt, or resist, it's the same efficiency in terms of dmg/nano.
