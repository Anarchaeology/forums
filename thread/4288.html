Creepy's wish list of items!!
Hello boys and girls, I have a list here of stuff I'd like to see added in up coming patches and I want to hear your ideas about them, as well as suggestions of your own (Services first, then items, then game changes) Im not going to repeat the ones that often get attention (furniture, clothes ect) though i do want those too.

<b>SERVICES</b>
1) Paint shop! : Picture this for a money sink.. You take your Yalm to a paint shop, you go in.. Inside is decorated with Kodiaks , Yalms, and other vehicles on lifts and such.. For a decent sum (this is a money sink idea after all) You pay for a "paint job".. Theres 1 vendor for each vehicle type in the shop.. SO you go over to the Yalmaha vendor and buy yourself a nice black paint job with flames up the side.. You shift/right click your Yalm with the paint job kit and bing bam boom.. Jesus built my hot rod!


2) Cel Restructuring Clinic! : You go to this place and get to pick out new hair, face, body styles.. New hairs and faces culd be added over time.. It would cost a bit of cash to do it (100-200k perhaps) but its another money sink idea and would make a lot of people happy.. Racial changes ofcourse would not be available.


<B> ITEMS</B>
1) More weapon altering items: Poison for edged weapons, weight reducers for heavy ones, clip extentions for more types of guns, the possibilities here are endless and WAY underused.

2) New Omni-Pol armors:

First: Omni-Med combat armor (doctor specific) looks like omni-pol but is red/white. Checks INT/STAM. Adds the usual bonuses for omni-med clothing.

Second: Omni-Insurgence armor (Agent Specific) Looks like Omni-Pol but is camoflague colored, checks Sense/Agil. Adds conceal bonus.

Third: Arena Issue armor (all classes) this is the only item that isnt palet colored existing armor. Looks like the armor Cyborgs wear, adds a small bonus to evades and pvp related AC (comparable to omni-pol elite, but looks more RP-Arena guy).


3) Clanner stuff:

One side note : Clanners SHOULD be able to equip omni armor, just not buy it brand new.. SO! To prevent people from selling omni armor to clanners and cleaning up.. Make different palet of the omni-armors, name them "liberated omni-pol ______ armor" and make them look beaten and battered.. Make the armor be dropped by omni NPC's .

1) Clanner Unique armor: Clanners do not have the funding of Omni, but they have some very neat storyline stuff that should allow for "special addition" weapons and armors that are clan only. Example's are below..

"Sentinals Watchman Coat" Looks like the black Tabards worn by the guys up at Avalon.

"Camalot Edition swords" Clan only, add bonuses like other special addition weapons.

"Battle of Athen memorial guns" Clan only, bonuses like other guns.


<B>GAME CHANGES</B>
Missions: Most people do missions to get coats, unshopped nanos and what not to sell to other players. Thats all well and good, I do however have some changes Id like to see..

1) For every merit board increase you gain a key and a mission instantly. The mission takes you to a place that could be very rewarding.. Such as "newly discovered munitions barracade" where you would find lots of chests (2-3 per room) , the NPC's would be NON-AGGRO, and instead sell you very very low cost armor/weapons (instead of the usual greet, it would be "please dont turn me in "your name" here.. Ill cut you a great deal on these weapons") These NPC's would sell items for 50-75% the cost of a store... And if you get REALLY lucky, you will find a car vendor. This mission key CANNOT BE DUPLICATED.

2) Eliminate the clothing rewards by assigning them Quality Levels not exceeding 25.


3) As well as the one time cool mission, how about more incentive to do more missions? make some clothing items wearable ONLY if you have a certain merit board. Example: "Golden Sunglasses" requires that a lvl 3 merit board be equiped in order to wear. ect..




Well thats a few of my BRAINIAC ideas.. What do you think?
