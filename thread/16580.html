No Previous Profession.
First, a few things:

1) I'm posting this here because it might be in the next patch (some indication below), and I'd like to get some information on how it is being done.

2) The ability to change professions has been on the envisioned list for a long time.

3) If you put your cursor over the top panel (where your name, title, profession) are listed on Skills/Abilities Overlay, you get a pop-up message saying: No Previous Profession.

- - - - - - - - - - - -

I think profession changes are a good thing. There should be limitations and heavy restrictions when it is done, to keep game balance and predictability (am I pvping with a fixer or a doctor?).

Other gaming system have long used multi-classed, dual-classed characters. I am not sure that would work well in AO, where each profession seems to have a broad range of skills already (nano programs). In AD&D, for example, the class lines only have a few types of spells and a multi-class is limited to lower level spells and a more difficult time leveling.

In AO, a profession change with the major IP reset will allow people to re-create their characters. I'm NOT suggesting breed changes be allowed.

In future AO, where you only get a few reallocation points with each title, you'll have to plan out your character well, being an enforcer/soldier for a while using certain skills, and being careful to save the points needed to adjust your character over time. This with OE changes, means you'll really have to design your character based on how you play: solo/group.

I'm sure some people will be totally against it. Of course, no one is making you change your profession, just like in real life, sort of. I am sure though that after a year of play, some people feel that they should be able to change professions in the moderns times of advanced medicine, engineering and programming that is Rubi-Ka. After all, we can insure your life with the press of a button.

Anyhow, I just want to get more information and a heads up on this. Thanks, Ecce (a NT who would rather be a Doctor, lvl 93).
