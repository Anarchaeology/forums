Combine "ingame-game" with "pvp-fix"
Many people (including me) have suggested adding some sort of ingame game. Many (including me) think that pvp is borked atm. Not just because of game mechanics but because people dont pvp for fun anywhere anymore.

So how about mixing these 2 topics by adding an ingame game based on pvp?

One idea would be "siege" where the goal is to capture and keep OP's. The more OP's kept by a faction brings the xp-modifier up just like towers do. Each OP have a set bonus to skills just like org contracts except that they affect all players of the OP's faction. If a OP in lush got a 200 HP/nano-modifier then all lvl 200's of the faction that currently hold the OP will get 200 hp/nano and 100 hp/nano at lvl 100 and such.

Other OP's would have nanoskill-modifiers and some would have weaponskill-modifiers.. and so on, u get my drift.

Once an OP is captured by clan then the save-terms turn clan and if Omni take the OP back then it will change back to omni of cource.

Any OP containing guards will make the guards change faction as the OP is taken by the opposite faction obviously.

Exactly how an OP would get "taken" is not something I have thought of just yet but thats for FC to fix. Maby put some flag or something in the center of the OP.

Some might think that this will force PvP on people but I think thats an extreme way of looking at it. As it is today some OP's are clan, some are omni and some are neutral. If this "game" gets put ingame then this wont exactly change. Where the clan, omni and neutral op's are might change from time to time but thats it.

If ure clan then u wont go into an omni OP anyway so u wont be forced to pvp whatsoever. (OP's will have 25% gas but there are guards there so a lonely person wont exactly come get u if ure surrounded by 6 guards)

All neutral OP's can be used by all 3 sides ofcource.

An thoughts? (remember that this is a very rough suggestion, not much details as to how it works but the basic suggestion should be understandable).

Not liking for several reasons......

First, it makes the planet map utterly useless for travel as you could not determine where you could go by looking. I have the Dovve map uploaded and it helps me a great deal.

Second, travel between points might become impossible for some players as they may be too low level to take on guards and just plain Whom-pa to someplace they could travel yesterday. The minute they arrive, "WHAM!", they butchered by oppposing guards, then wake up at reclaim with a, "WTF?!?" dangling from their lips.

Third, they reclaim glitch might reappear at an inopportune time. For those who don't know what that is currently, it's where you save at a terminal, switch factions, and forget to re-save somepace safe for your new faction. What happens is this, you get killed and FOREVER reappear in the old save location to be cut down by guards again and again. Trying to petition while this is happening is hard, because you can't type fast enough to do so before you're killed again (yes, I've actually witnessed this happen....). keep in mind, this is not even supposed to be possible. If outposts changed sides, you could be running missions or questing having saved at a particular location (you might even log for a break or emergency and come back), then you get killed and "whoops!", you keep getting yourself killed at the reclaim terminal because it, not you, switched sides!

Lastly, if it's all about outposts, it only encourages pvp for high level characters. Low level characters couldn't really participate because of the guards. Guard levels should also be increased to make taking an outpost harder, which again, excludes low levels. We want to encourage pvp for ALL levels, not just a few.

These were just the reason I thought of to come out against this in the few minutes it took me to read this post. I'm sure there are more. Sorry, but I think it's a bad idea. Might be great for reality (like this is real....), but very bad for playability.
