Looking for corrupt Omni employees..
Yes, that's right, we're looking for those of you who joined Omni-tek for the paycheck and access to goods, and who are looking to make a buck, by whatever means necessary.

Kicked out of your previous organization for selling shipments of guns to clanners? Can't find an organization that won't whine if you murder defenseless neutrals? Get a kick out of price gouging? If any of the above apply, join the Order of the Lamp!

We're looking for profit-oriented folks who won't turn up their noses at any job, if the payout is high enough. We're particularly interested in fixers and agents, but anyone short on traditional "ethics" is welcome. Level is unimportant, but attitude and a sense of humor is required.

We're a relatively new org, and don't yet have a lot of members, but we're growing quickly and welcome any and all devious types who don't get squeamish about a clandestine meeting in ICC to provide supplies to the opposition.. for a 'reasonable' fee.

OOC note: This is NOT a guild of exploiters or grief players. While our methods are not exactly approved by Omni-Tek, we're not out to make other players' lives hell.. we're just getting things done. By whatever means necessary. Roleplaying is encouraged but not required.

If interested - contact Eyesandy in game or leave a message here. (As I'll be at work until 10:30pm CST & can't get in game but will be reading the boards). Note as this org was created last night, the org info page isn't up yet.. *sigh*

Not a chance on changing the org name now that we've become fairly well known on RK2 (and hated, for the most part). I hear the clanners call us the "lightbulbs" and I'm "Chief Lightbulb". I told my general Mercinax about this and he remarked, "In that case, I'm General Electric."

As for the origin of the name - it has two meanings:

1> Those of you who played Daggerfall will know that the Order of the Lamp was an unimplemented guild that a huge number of Daggerfall fans wanted to join and tried for ages to do so because it was supposed to offer some particularly cool benefits.. many people spent months looking for a way to join this guild when in fact, it did not exist.

2> I chose the name more because it brings to mind (to me) a clandestine meeting in a dark alley at midnight, both dealer and customer carrying dimly lit lanterns - while this is somewhat archaic, it represents an old tradition being carried on..

Anyway, I'm half asleep so please excuse my rambling. At this time we're requiring new members to have a sponsor due to security concerns (related to our participation in the recent events on RK2). Perhaps you could meet some of our lower level members and team with them sometime and get to know them a bit and if they'll vouch for you, you're welcome to join us.

We do quite a bit of RP and participate in the GM run events on RK2, as an example, a recent news article was posted about us on the official site.
