Cut the dead weight
Many of us are looking forward to the character wipe for inactive lvl 1 characters. I dont really understand the reasons why, but I think it is like never active characters are like cobwebs and mold growing in the stats.

I would like to see:

"probationary removal" of inactive characters if they have not logged in in over 2 months, so their name would be greyed on org lists, and not counted in totals

I would like to see this with orgs as well, to clear up the names and numbers of the hundreds of one person orgs out there.

It would also be helpful if the presidents of orgs received special attention, if they did not log in for 6 weeks, they would get demoted, and the most active member of the next highest rank would receive the presidency. I have heard too many horror stories of dead leaders and then gruelling death spiral whereas a auto leadership transfer would have kept friends together.

I would like to see the name erase instituted as an ongoing practice.

I would like to hear of other ways people think that automatic cleanup could be applied or help.
