Better light source!
It's scientific fact (honest! I looked it up! ;) that the Electric Torches are a joke. They provide virtually -no- light; they add maybe, what, a foot? It's pretty bad.

So: add a new, single-direction light. You know those big search towers in Omni areas (I don't play Clan, so can't comment on their setup ;)? Like the little gate areas in Omni-1 Screening? Take those rotating lights off from the top, make a portable version. You could make them a Util or HUD slot, or even Back (big light-thingy you have to mount?).

Higher quality levels could provide more and more light; and if there was some thought that this could be abused (I don't know how, but I count on twelve-year-olds to try and make life miserable for everyone), make them drain nano points, like Cloaking Devices do with health.
