Hrm.. how about this? Do missions for valuable items. Trade or sell them for stuff you need. I have a bag full of nano cloaks, Omni pol elite, a support beam, etc. Am I ever gonna use it? Nope. It is useful? Sure, it helps me get what I want.

I have a friend who looks out for rifles in his missions: he gives them to me. He gets back MP Creation and Anima nanos that I find. Sorry, but it just seems like you want it too easy - ask a terminal for exactly what you want, then go do it. Now you may say, "it's not easy - I have to fight for it!" There's a lot to be said for non-combat ways of playing this game, be it tradeskills, sneaking, buying/selling, camping terminals, living in the arena, or what have you. I'm not saying I neccesarily enjoy camping mission terminals, but it does give me a chance to sit back in town, relax, watch the shopping channel, chat with friends, etc.
