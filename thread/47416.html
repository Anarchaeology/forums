The **** Oddities of AO.....
How come you can form a team of 2 omni, 2 neut, and 2 clan? Aren't omni & clan supposed to be at war with the neuts being hippies wanting peace?

How does an Enforcer have the cheapest Chemistry skill cost in the game? And why don't Enforcers have a bright green skill in 2h Blunt?

Is there something special on top of certain mountains in Rubi-Ka that require the "Climbing/Adventuring" skill?

Why is it that Enforcers have a cheaper Fast Attack skill than MAs do? I see enforcers as big, "I will hit you a few times really hard and you will die" people, whereas MAs would seem to be faster with their hands....hmmmm.

How come the Trader has green skills in both Electrical Engineering and Mechanical Engineering? Are Traders (who I envision to be masters of umm...trading) supposed to be that fluent in engineering?

Why is tutoring green across the board? Is this like the everyone can do tradeskills skill?

Why is B&E most expensive for NTs? Have you ever seen this headline: "Doctor in New Jersey arrested for Breaking & Entering!"??

Why is Bow / Bow Special Attack green for MAs and blue for Advs? Should be the other way around....

Why is it that all classes have a lite blue or better nano pool skill, yet only weapons skills are primarily blue (cept soldiers / advs)?

Why is it that the Galahad "Priscilla" was (according to its description) designed with the NT in mind, yet it reqs the most expensive skill for an NT (most expensive skill in the game as well) in Full Auto?

And what is with MPs having a blue Quantum FT skill where Traders & Engies have a green one? Quantum FT seems out of the realms of trading & engineering.....

Why is Nano Resist lite blue or better for all but 3 profs, yet for Duck, Dodge, and Evade, there are 5 profs each for whom this skill is dark blue???

Why do grenades require Brawl skill to toss? If you have nade skill, you should be able to nade! To this day, I have NEVER seen anyone use a grenade and that sucks. Soldiers should have a green skill in grenade and be able to toss grenades as a special attack if they posess a grenade (be sure not to target yourself when you use the special ).

How come Agents are the only one's with a dark blue "Adventuring" skill? Are these people really that boring in bed?

How can any class be more proficient (cheaper skill) with a gun type weapon than a soldier? Examples: MG/SMG (Fixer), Rifle (Agents / Advs) ??

Why is it that when I have an evade buff on, my own evade buff tells me "Better program running", yet my evade buff gives me 10 more points to evades the buff currently running?????

*sigh*
Traders do not trade physical objects, they trade skills and knowledge. All of their nanolines are based around this idea. I see no problem with traders being highly skilled at tradeskills. Though engies should be better.

quote:

quote:

Why is Bow / Bow Special Attack green for MAs and blue for Advs? Should be the other way around....
Bows are muscle powered weapons, very much in tune with the mystical nature of MAs. Adventurers understand that it's better to let chemical explosives do the job of propelling projectiles.

quote:

Why is it that all classes have a lite blue or better nano pool skill, yet only weapons skills are primarily blue (cept soldiers / advs)?
Fluff explanation: Because everyone on Rubi-Ka is exposed continuously to Notum, providing them with the ability to harness nanobots and not everyone spends all their time practicing with weapons.

Game design: Because Nanos are the primary obvious differentiation between professions, so you should be able to have enough nano pool to use those nano formulae.

quote:

Why is it that the Galahad "Priscilla" was (according to its description) designed with the NT in mind, yet it reqs the most expensive skill for an NT (most expensive skill in the game as well) in Full Auto?
Because the Galahad line of pistols uses Full Auto and the Priscilla is a simple modification of the skill-chips on the pistol.

quote:

quote:

It's easier to harden NCU against hostile nanite intrusion than to get a relatively unathletic person to learn how to anticipate incoming fire.
