Huge Blast in Stret East last night
Last night I was leading a mission to Borealis from 4-Holes. My small team was heading south down the road that follows the bank of the Stret River on the eastern side. We had passed the ferry and we'd just crossed the large ironwork bridge, when we noticed what appeared to be a second sun on the horizon, slightly to our left.

Stopping for a better look, we marvelled as this distant ball of light grew larger and larger. Eventually it was huge, filling the entire sky. The closest approximation I can think of is that of a distant nuclear blast. The ball of light appeared to be a huge blast shockwave expanding out in all directions. The edge covered the horizon and raced across the river toward us. I thought we were all going to be obliterated - it was as if 4-Holes had been hit by a nucelar warhead.

Strangely, this wave of light washed over us with seemingly no effect. We were dazed and a bit nervous, but all seemed well. We carried on with our mission, and I monitored the faction leader channels to see if anyone else had witnessed this strange event.

I'm hoping that some of you might have insight into what this could have been. We have a few screenshots - I will try to add them to this thread as soon as possible.
