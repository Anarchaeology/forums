This is probably never gonna get read, and it may have allready been said but as A Guild Leader These are some of the things I think of as ESSENTIAL to be able to run my guild better.

1) Guild housing - I don't care if we all have to share the same door or whatever but we need this, and we need to have ones that are bigger / smaller and vary in cost / upkeep

Guild housing needs :

1a) The abilty to invite people not in your guild inside (maybe kind of like the way you invite people into your home.)

1b) A meeting hall - a place with chairs centred round a table for meetings (the size may be related to guild size or how much you pay for your housing

1c) A reception

1d) guild shop / vault - This is vital it could either take the form a a vault into which anyone can drop items and guild leader / ranks leader sets can determine the value or amount of items that can be taken at any time, and members would have to replace those items or items to that value in the bank / vault before they can take any more. If it is possible to implement then being able to mark items that have been placed in the guild bank as Guild owned and not be able to be sold / traded

The other option would be a guild shop, and this may be a little harder to implement, basically your guild leader could set a general rate which could be greater or less than the normal shops, when you put items in you are paid creds from the guild bank, when items are resold the money goes back into the bank. Again guild leaders should be able to set up a limit on the amount of Identicle items that can be in the shop at any time, and if the guild bank has no money, then it can't offer you anything if your trying to sell. The guild shop could be used by people from other guilds, provided they have been allowed into your guild house.

I am sick of trying to manage a load of backpacks in my bank and inv to find items for guildies that I have collected or been donated.

1e) At least 1 office (maybe more offices the more you pay)

Offices need to be attached to accesing a terminal that allows you to control guild functions - the leader set up what ranks in the guilds have access to what - it may be he wants his Generals (for example) to have access to the same functions he has. Functions should include granting access to guild vaults / rates on guild shop items. ANYTHING I have said or will say a leader / leaders should be able to decide on should be accessable here, from a special interface window, that is ONLY accessible from there.

1f) A grid terminal

1g) Workshops - places where members can create tradeskill items, maybe they get an area effect buff on tradeskills when they are in the workshop

1h) Traing area A small 0% area where members can practise pvp, maybe a small area like a backyard in which players can kill monsters for exp, but gain no money or loot. The monsters spawning there could depend on the levels of players there.

1i) A club / recreation area - this would greatly be enhanced by the adding of casino's and casino style minigames and maybe some sort of trading card game - which could also give players something to do outside there housing - while waiting for afk team members / while team leader is recuting / while people are derezzing / while people are camping mobs etc

I see a - f as being essential to every guild, g to i should be available to the Guilds paying the highest prices for their housing, and maybe some of the less essnetials (b - f) should also be upgrades.

2) Message of the day - this small feature would be so helpfull in keeping the whole guild informed of recent events.

3) Land control - being dealt with in the booster pack

4) some sort of allinece system to make Alliences more official, maybe include a chat channel for the higher ranks of the associate guilds, and access to each others housing.

5) some sort of Guild War system basically the opposite of an allience, with the option of sending rival guilds treatuies. Even simple things like Your members may not attack our members or the treaty is broken would be cool.

6) Some sort of Guild medals/rewards system - adds no buffs or bonuses to players to prevent exploiting but aloow guild leaders to give medals/rewards to players that other players can see (this may be in the form of general Medals or specialised medals - guild leader could set what they are called and a description of what they are for

Ok thats all I can think of right now, I'm sure I had more ideas but I can't think right now. Ideas 4 and 5 may not be implementable and are definatley less important than all the ideas related to guild housing. Also 1i) has suggestions that may or may not be implementable but could have a postive effect on the game as a whole, little gambling mini games or a trading card game may not be original but could draw people into the clubs (in the case of mini games) and make downtime more enjoyable (in the case of the trading card game)

Using the chat commands as a means to mamnage members, taxes and voting is poor, time consuming and annoying we need the offices and some sort of leaders interface, even if it only handled the commands that we allready have, it would be a great impoovement.

more dances? WOOT!
I'm going to expand upon MissMaul's idea for more dances. I'm sure most of these have been suggested already. However, being an avid dancer in RL, I feel these things should not be overlooked in a game where social interaction is *supposed to be* important.

So, with no further ado, here is my list:

/slowdance (slow dances with selected target; target can refuse if they wish)
/bumpngrind (that really close, sexy dance you can do to just about any song out there; again, target can refuse)
/rave (really fast, wild dancing - multiple versions would be great!)
/waltz (obvious - target can refuse)
/breakdance (obvious)
/snake (crawling along like a snake - old, I know, but I still see people do it)
/drop (crouch and stand up multiple times in rapid succession - can be included in a /rave emote)
/split (do the splits sideways)
/airsplit (jump and do the splits)
/forsplit (forward splits - like James Brown)
/twirl (like twirling batons.. or pipes, or SOL Execs)
/360 (360 degree spin)
/720 (720 degree spin)
/macarena (suggested already)
/elecslide (the all time classic Electric Slide!)
/cartwheel
/forroll (forward roll)
/backroll (backwards roll)
/thriller (for all the MJ fans out there )
/robot (another classic)
/tango (another dance your target can refuse)
/swing (again, target can refuse)
/limbo (I like this idea!)

A few other things I'd like to add:

1) Guild emblems. This really isn't as difficult to implement as one would think. The key to making sure that server load and client lag time is not increased dramatically is to limit the number of possible combinations for emblems. Then you would have to figure out how to show an emblem. Of course, the easiest way to show an emblem would probably be on chest armor, trenchcoat, or a cloak (which reminds me - get open cloaks and trenches in the game, not those "raincoat" crappy ones). Oh, one more thing - make a hefty fee for "registering" a guild emblem. DAoC used a similar system to the one I described, and it worked out OK (only prob was the limit of possible emblems, but the hefty fee to get one made that a moot point).

2) Guild housing. Again, not that difficult. FC already has a system in place for apartments; why not expand it to guilds? Guilds can use funds from their bank to upgrade the guild house to include bank terminals, reclaim/insurance terminals, and even basic shops (for treatment kits, ammo, nano recovery kits, etc.).

2a) EXPAND THE POSSIBILITIES FOR APARTMENT FURNISHINGS!!! Sorry for the caps but this is something apartments seriously lack - amenities.

3) The models for characters do lack a bit in the realism department. I understand that this can't be helped much, but there should be some effort put into making our characters look like they're actually holding that weapon instead of it being pasted onto your open hand. And MA punches - I'm a 1st degree black belt in TaeKwonDo, and I would never punch like AO characters punch. (Except Atroxes; they do better than my Opifex MA does). Two words: CLOSED FIST! Nuff said. I've also noted issues with sunglasses and Opifexes, but that's another issue that's been mentioned already.

4) I've noticed something else - there are is very little ethnic variety in Opi, Nano, and Trox models. You get some of the main ethnic groups in the Solitus breed (Caucasian, African, Asian), but none of them show up in other breeds. There should be different skin tones and similar ethnic features for the other breeds. And what's up with those Nanomage faces?? Can we say "botched facial surgery?" Oh, and FC should think about adding Middle Eastern facial models as well. We wouldn't want to think that an entire ethnic group had been wiped out in the far future, would we?

5) Add a new profession: The Driver. He would be the vehicle-fighting profession, using airplanes, cars, tanks, and boats to fight for his side. This would be a good way to introduce some other sci-fi elements to the game (like anime-style mecha and powered armor, which should have been invented in the 300th Century AD - hell we've got droids, why not mecha?). This leads to my next point:

6) Outdoor mission areas. These missions would be small by neccessity (but could be larger) and would allow some action besides spelunking and urban warfare. Plus, it would allow our new profession to have some fun blowing stuff up with his new combat Yalm.

I've read over MissMaul's list, and I give thumbs up for all of the other ideas she's compiled. I hope some of my ideas will be added to the list soon.

BUMP!

Making of nano-discs...
I just got this fun idea to make trade skills more fun to use :

The ability to make customized items, I saw there was a idea for drinks further up, but I have been thinking of making discs for nanocrystals...

If we were able to make customized discs for nanos, FC would have much less to do with making new nanos for all professions although it would be hard to implement (consider it a challenge FC )

The MMR have no real use at the moment, but if it was changed to a programming interface for making of programdata for discs it would be a very useful item. (put the price accordingly)

The programming should not be easy, and should also be set so that you could only make discs of your lvl or lower, and adding a chance of better stats of a certain QL if you made a disc much lower than your lvl.
Example: a lvl 200 player making a QL50 disc should be better than a lvl 50 making a QL50 disc...
What I mean with that it should not be easy is that it should require you to study the art of making these programs whether it is damage, healing, buffs +++ that you are making, also if a lvl 50 making a QL 50 disc that disc if it had the same use as a existing QL50 disc/nano should have the same stats, or slightly better.

It should also be possible to set a name of the nano for yourself rather than an title based on what it does...
Example: Flapsie's Yutto Morph

The way of compiling this disc would then be to get a hologram of a Yutto and perhaps also a recording of it's sounds to get the voices in, but it should also be possible to make it without the sound.
The QL disc you are making should set what additional stats you could add to the nano for example HP+, damage+, crit+, and so on and you should have a set amount of "points" to increase the amount your "abilities" could increase.
Example: you have 10 "points" at QL 10
1 point give 2 HP
2 point 4 HP and so on
1 point give 2 nanopool
2 point 4 nanopool and so on
10 point give +1 max ncu
+++

It should also be so that if you are a doctor and you make healing nanos they would work better than if someone else made it. And also if you are making the nano Doc only the heal would be better and if a Doc made a Doc only nano for healing it would be even better again.

This way the possibilities in nanos is endless...
You could by doing this also add the possibilities to make party effects and lightshow nanos without actually making them yourself

This system could also be used with all other trade skills making for example armour that added to stats, +++

This I know (or at least can imagine) would be very hard to implement, BUT it would make people travel the world even more (who would not like a Brain Dog Morph ? ) and it would also change the whole trade aspect of the game. It also have the possibility to become an enourmous money sink...
It should not be cheap to make special nanos, and not easy either, it should not be so easy that you could make a QL200 nano in 2 minutes, I would rather think it would take closer to a couple of months of real time to study the process and planning how to make it and what to put into it and so on...

It could also make it possible for other people to get information from out and about using the reference tool and to sell those references to people for use in the making of discs. This would also increase the social interaction between all inhabitants.

But the main thing I come up with this idea was that AO is currently very booring if you don't like to fight all the time... and actually enjoy sitting about making stuff

I really hope this thing will be implemented sometime

One change in 14.6 especially intrigued me: the changes to the monster info screen. The changes in the patch look like a good start, but I was thinking we could go even further: why not just remove Level, Aggressiveness, and HP and NP tools and make the info screen do a check against Perception?

For every one point of Perception, you can see the aggressiveness of a level of beast.
For every two points of Perception, you can see the level of a beast.
For every four points of Perception, you can see the HP and NP of a beast

Thus, at skill level 800, you can see the HP and NP of a level 200 beast, at skill level 400, you can see the level, and at skill level 200 you can see the aggressiveness. Make the more useful info require a higher skill.
