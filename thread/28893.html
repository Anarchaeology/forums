Add an XP bonus to guild leadership
I am suggesting this as taken from the mechanics of Asheron's Call.

How it works:

In AC, your leader gets bonus experience points every time you get experience. Now, you do not get any less, your leader just gets a small bonus.

For example:

Leaderless Player A: Kills an ace and gets 40,000 xp.

Player B: Kills an ace and gets 40,000 xp.

Player B's Leader: Gets 100xp.

Why to implement it?
As most guild leaders know, it is hard to contribute a lot of time to a guild for fun and events AND get levels. I run into this paradox all of the time. I can spend my day helping others, running events, etc. but I gain zip xp. Eventually everyone is higher level and I am stuck. So, I have to power level for weeks and ignore my guild duties. I would much rather "run" the guild than kill monsters for exp; I enjoy the guild stuff more.

I see my proposed solution as an incentive for leaders in guilds to contribute their time to the guild. This is a win-win solution as member's don't get anything less and leader's can help out guilds and get something tangible in return. In the long run, guilds get people dedicated to helping the guilds without worry of nerfing themselves.

AC does the following to prevent abuse:

1. You can only join a leader who is higher or equal level to you.

2. The amount of XP you get is determined once an hour or so, you don't see a message every single time someone gets a kill.

3. The amount of XP bonus your leader gets is based on the amount of time you have been "sworn" to them. This means if you hop around they will get very little for a good period of time.

In the two years I have had an AC account I have never seen the system abused. Its not like you get a ton of XP for being a leader, but at least it is something.
