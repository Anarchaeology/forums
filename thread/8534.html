Yes, But Not In PW
I saw it this afternoon, but not in Perpetual Wastelands. I saw it in Tir County. And I have an advantage over the previous observers -- I saw it from overhead.

I was flying between Tir and Crater Farms at about 30 meters altitude when I saw two huge glowing objects on the far side of Crater Farms from me. That would be south by southwest from the crater. As I got closer, I was able to resolve it into two vertical disks of light, on edge, sunk partway into the terrain. It's hard to judge distances on the ground from inside a Yalmaha, but I'd say they were about the height of the Crater Farms walls. I caught up with them just past the crater, and watched them pass under me. They then passed through the crater, swept across it from one side to the other, and kept going.

I pursued, but it wasn't easy; they were moving at almost exactly my speed, maybe a bit faster, and had a head start. They were headed straight for Tir. I called out a warning on the only comm channel I knew would get that far (Clan OOC) and maintained pursuit.

They got as far as Western Tir Approach, just short of those rock pillars where the road turns, and then quickly evaporated.
