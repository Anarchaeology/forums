No Profession Defining Ability Should Be Ultra Rare
This Post is for Fixers and Nano Techs who are currently having to deal with what funcom feals a just and fair over rare availability of grid armor and nulity sphere nanos.

When Grid Armor first came out i questioned if it would actually be usefull and raise the defensive ability enough to actally dodge shots and not just avoid crits, which is pretty much all that evades do right now anyway. I later found out it did and began to look for it. From two different people on RK2 including one of the fixers who actually have grid armor MK1 i was advised to kill shovelets in clon**** to find it. I have been doing that for two weeks now gained over 1.5 million credits (each monster drops from 500-700 creds) well over 100 instruction discs and not any grid armor discs.


When mez pets came out for MPs in a following patch (and they should have been given to Crats first after all they are more geared towards mind control than a MP is.) MPs everywhere started aquiring mez pets, i was mindblown to find that the third pet was not made another ultra rare like Grid Armor and Nulity.


I can accept unique weapons in dungeon settings not dropping all the time. I can accept the MPs mez pet and all the other love they get in the nano department. But what I find very hard to accept is that Funcom feels that grid armor and nulity sphere are just too good to make every bit as common as nanos available to any other class if they where soooo good and unbalancing then why where they added in the first place? its like waving meat infront of a hungry dog on a chain while the dog choaks itself to death trying to get to the meat.

Right now i think i am one of the many fixers and nano techs choaking to death trying to get at some meat. In the next patch please consider making these nanos every bit as available to us as you have made other additions available to other professions. And in the future when you do hopefully ad a nano line to make fixers more group friendly dont make that another ultra rare stinkup all it does is make people bitter at eachother.
