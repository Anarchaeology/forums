Well...
((OOC post))

There's three ways to find a person with a 'green' name.

1) As you said, just wander around and eventually somewhere, somewhen you'll see one doing something. Reets and Rompa are good starting points, but nothing is guaranteed. This is probably the answer you're looking for.

2) Go and purchase a new AO account. Brand new one. Create a new character and wander around looking entirely lost and confused. Shortly, somone with a green name will come by and welcome you. This is probably not the answer you are looking for.
Note that hanging out in backyards will sometimes allow you to catch sight of a 'greenie' welcoming some other new player.

3) Type /petition Help me I have a problem!
This will, in most cases, cause a person with a green name to come to where you are to assist you. This is probably not the answer you are looking for either, and if you don't actually have any kind of issue needing their assistance this will get you into lots of trouble. So actually, this is a bad idea.

That's the only ways I know of.

((/OOC))
