NEWSFLASH: The boat has left the dock....
....and most of the people on these forums have missed it.

Yeah I said, it needed to be said and now it has. I'm so sick of people complaining about being able to level at a "decent rate" and how FUncom keeps nerfing hunting spots to slow down leveling. Yes, thats exactly what they did...and you know why they did it? Because of this (taken from AO home page):

A four-year story arc, that will tell the story of Anarchy Online both in-game with content and staff-run events, and through animated movie episodes. Players will be able to participate in the story, and ultimately help influence it through their own actions.

A four year story, does everyone have that? Lets repeat for all the real atrox out there, a four year story. Granted, FC hasn't done much in the way of providing an involving story or events, but their intention was to create a game that people would spend the next four years playing. It is my understanding that they may have originally envisioned it taking a full 4 years (or the better part of that time) to level a character to 200. I'm sure that they never thought it would be possible for characters to be hitting level 200 (or anywhere near it) this soon.

So yes, FC is trying to slow down leveling, it shouldn't be a huge surprise, it shouldn't even be that big of a deal. They are trying to create a Roleplaying game world where friendships and ally's are made over a period of time. Does that bother me? Not at all. I cut my teeth in RPGs with AD&D where the Roleplaying was so involving that it took years to max a characters level out. The day that I quit playing AD&D was the day my main character hit level 36.

Why? Because there was nothing left to do.

Will I quit AO when I get a character to level 200? Probably, because there will be nothing less to do.

Am I going to enjoy the time it takes to get there and the friends I make along the way? Yes, thats the only reason I'm playing at all, the social aspect.

Will I stop playing before I get to lvl 200? Probably not, I enjoy challenges and look at FCs various nerfs as the ultimate challenge. If they nerf shottys tommorrow, I'll find something else instead of blasting FC and P&Ming on these boards.

Do people have a right to complain over nerfs? Yeah, I believe they do, however I'd like to see more people acting like adults about this game. And all of the kids playing it, I'd like to see understand a little better what it means to RP.

In this age of instant gratification, people are missing out on alot things in life and missing out on the best parts of this game. Explore Rubi-Ka, make some friends, help some people, fight a few battles, and oh yeah...gain some levels somewhere in there. Leveling is not all there is to AO. The sonner you realize that, the better off you'll be, and if you don't like that...quietly leave and move to something that suits you better. There is no point in coming to these boards and telling everyone how you are quiting and how bad this game sucks. Do you really think there is someone sitting there, reading your post, going "Wow, I'm having a great time playing AO, but this guy isn't. I think I'm gonna quit because this guys sucks at RPG and he's quitting." If you do, then you have more serious problems then how 14.2 is going to keep you from getting 75 levels a week.

Of course thats just my opinon...flame as you will.

If there were anything to do besides leveling, I'd do it. As it stands leveling with friends is enjoyable. You BS, joke around and kill monsters for cool stuff and bragging rights about who's the highest 'level'. I wish to god it weren't so, but it's a fact.

If FunCom reduces the leveling rate to where you only get a level every week or two, then what is the point? Do we all sit around a campfire and poke barbs at each other? Might as well be in an IRC chatgroup for that. I'm not into a game that makes me bang my head against a wall for half a month before getting a cookie. Who honestly thinks that killing monsters over and over is any more physically or mentally challenging than banging your head against the wall?

What fun is it to sit and look at a yellow bar that does not move at all when there is nothing to do in the game of any worth other than to watch the yellow bar move? They refuse to implement self-sufficient tradeskills. Apartments are a joke. Guild functions are crippled. The provisions for role-playing are weak at best. It is impossible to interact with the storyline unless you are part of a 200+ member guild.

So, to make the time more enjoyable while hanging out with guildmates, we level. I don't know these people in RL otherwise, I'd be hanging out with them in a bar or sharing a beach house in the summer. It's AO and you do what FunCom provides the means to do. FunCom has given us the ability to kill monsters and level.

Rather than slow the leveling rate, FunCom would be better served to reassess the 200 lvl limit and they better do this soon. They will lose a LOT more people if they slow leveling to a crawl with changes such as the 14.2 nerf than they will from those who hit lvl200 and quit.

The day I can't get a level in a week and there's nothing else entertaining to do in the game is the day I re-evaluate the money I give FunCom. Twinking an alt might be a viable route. But, eventually, the 8 character slots will fill up with characters who just can't take the headache from banging their heads against the wall in an effort to nudge the yellow bar a millimeter.
