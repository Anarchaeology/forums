The Shovah... the true story
In a recent post, Oshovah (Darkflame on this forum) mentioned the names of the allies of the Shovah guild and "many others who can not be named because of their current political position."

I'd like to intoduce this "other". Our name is Omni Justice Organization. We were the original "Shovah".

We are all the original members of the Shovah guild. The Shovah's downfall was immediate after the attack on Omni HQ. The members felt abandoned after Oshovah and his generals left our guild for various reasons. I, Benna, was left in charge of the remains of the Shovah, and I did serve under Oshovah for a short period of time. Eventually it was obvious that the Shovah was dying, so after a unamimous vote among all the members, the guild went through a complete restructuring.

So how can a guild turn from "proclamied terrorists" to a Omni-based organization that fights for justice? Why the change in political alignment?
I can go about writing our history here, but it's already written at http://www.omnijustice.com under the "OJO Infomation" link.

The purpose of this post is not to discredit Oshovah and his work, but to get this out information out to anyone who cares. I hope it does not sound like I'm blasting Oshovah, and the OJO would like to remain on good terms with him and his allies.
