PvP, UO and AO Roots(How AO could benefit)
Hello there,

Anyone here ever PvP in UO ? The paralyze spell (root obviously could be broken by a trapped bag, which damaged yourself. This was kinda a pain in the ass, because first you have to hunt down the bags...buy 20...then trap them all. This was a fair way to deal with the attack, para, attack, para (newbie killer) combo because the caster would run outta mana (nano obviously eventually.

So basically here's what I'm thinking...A device that varies in, QL from 10-200, call it Nanobot Disruptor or some ****. (Forgive me if I'm off scale with the skills and effect duration) At level 10, Require 40 Mechanical Engineering(or some other skill). The device explodes causes root to break. But it takes 7 seconds and causes 20 dmg. Level 50, require 150 M.E., same effect but in 4 seconds 50 dmg. Lvl 100, req 250 M.E. and 2.5 secs for 90 gmg. Up to QL 200, Instant root break. People will then say, blah blah waste IP, blah blah nerfing roots....I say if you spend your valuable IP on a defensive skill that would actually do something (unlike evades/resist) WTF not?

I probably haven't thought this out thoroughly enough, and I'm also a PvP newb but flame away...cause I'll have more ideas where this came from. Peace
