You'd be surprised what people will do, especially at the mid range levels, and at the request of certain character classes (do this for me and I'll buff you). Yes, it does break down towards the higher end of the character scale (post 160 or so).

The *main* problem with teams doing single missions is the risk/reward equation. A team of 6 can easily do a full red single mission fairly quickly with little risk (at the very high end the initial room again becomes a problem, but thats only one room).

I just think this is a way to make them less attractive to a team to do. But I do agree with several ideas on this board

A)Solo mission should be soloable at 50% (Green). But by Soloable I mean that your still accepting a death risk for taking the mission (say 25% chance of dieing. I know, its really hard to quantify a chance of dieing)

B)Team mission will *need* to take a team, and if fact should likely be on the risk factor of some of the smaller static dungeons now. I think that thier difficulty should be so that a Green is *hard* with a incomplete team of 3 (comparable to a red)

C)Static missions need to be Organization Missions, that take 2-4 teams of people to complete without a death wish. They also need level capped (can only be this tall to ride this ride) to avoid a single high level team from slumming through it.

Overall, tho, teams should be deep hunting into high risk areas, not missioning for xp.
