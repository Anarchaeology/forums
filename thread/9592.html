4 Demands To Make AO Survive
1. Highly increase the amount of monsters in the wilderness
2. Remove PvP titles (possible)
3. Remove Trader nano line called Wrangle
4. Reset all Characters to level 1

If Anarchy Online is gonna survive 4 more years, we can't keep on like we're doing now. I'm not talking about nerfed MA damage or Dual Pillows, I'm talking about the whole system.

I put this in "Next Patch", because it have to get "next patch" priority, or AO will lose as good as all players before summer. Why? Because it's damned BORING! Anyone remember the early days? When we all used to team up and hunt for monsters far out in the wilderness? I've been on for months without that happening to me any more. I only get invited to Cyborg Camping teams because of my Subconcious Guiding Nano.

My point is that because Funcom did the unforgivable (and not yet fixed) mistake ruining creature spawn, there're no longer any teams out hunting. Except Temple outpost in Greater Tir County and Bridge Bully Campers in Varmint Woods.
Speaking of Varmint, I've several times been able to fly for as long as 10 seconds without seeing a single red dot on my map. In ten seconds, my Yalmaha covers a pretty big area, and I got a high Perception.

So where's all the focus now? PvP only. To do anything in PvP, you need weapons and armor twice your level, or you won't stand a chance. How do you get that? Transfer money from high-level chars (which you use only to get money), then get a big heap of implants, and bully/threaten/bribe a doctor/trader/enforcer to buff you into ridiculously strong weapons. Then you grid into MeetMeDere, and ruin the experience for all who hasn't spent that much money on implants 3-4x you own level. Take me, for instance. My first PvP I lost to an Enforcer lower level than me, hitting me for 1k per hit, with an AC on about 2000.
And that was in November, I dare not to think of how it's now.

Yeah, I know it's not my business. Personally I don't care how much you over-equip. But if all players go in that direction, it's gonna be very lonely outside MMD and 2HO. I mean, even if I'm a Solitus, I don't wanna be THAT solitary!
And I DO respect that people has been sitting for years, designing a landscape and a wildlife yet unmatched in the digital world.

AO is gonna continue for at least 4 more years. If not basic fundaments change immediately, this game's gonna lose (as good as) all players very, very soon.

And, of course, when all is fixed, we gonna need a reset in characters, unless we gonna have a thousand level 5 PvP enforcers running around with level 105 hammers.

This is an advice for both players and Funcom, if we wanna play this game. In the end, I wanna make noticed that I've been a fanatical (dunno why) supporter of AO even since the beginning. But now there seems to be no other alternative than quitting and waiting for Star Wars Galaxies....
