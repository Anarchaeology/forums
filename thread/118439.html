Sheathing Weapons (Part II)
Well Glad I can start posting this again.

Anyways, I think it would be cool to have your character sheath or holster weapons. & there would be a command like /sheath to put the weapon away(though still equiped) & type it again & you unsheath it. Or if it's sheathed & you attack, it automatically unsheathes & you fight. After the fight you sheath it again since ya didn't take the command off

Now here's another suggestion: Perhaps make a secondary Inventory so people can equip drinks or other social items without taking off their weapon so they dont have to go get another Wrangle, etc.

What would even make it cooler would be if you could sheath your weapons & have the drinks in hand at the same time.
