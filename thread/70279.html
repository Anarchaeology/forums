In all seriousness, the reason behind the decline of PvP is simple.

The majority of people who wanted to PvP in AO chose to be professions that were at the "top of the heap" and generally out of balance with the others. As such, they played Traders, MAs, Soldiers and Enforcers at the beginning.


Soldiers got turned into running targets with the first round of damage changes in PvP.

MAs got turned into rooted targets with this last change.

Enforcers are perhaps the best balanced class around, but since they don't seem to have an overwhelming advantage against other classes, many of those players only seem to PvP when they've got people with them.

And, of course, no one wants to fight Traders since they're RIDICULOUSLY overpowered in PvP


So, now you have the 4 classes that historically made up the largest body of the PvP population and because they're either gimped, or overpowered, they don't PvP anymore. Hence, the death of PvP.


Funcom has to get a clue and BALANCE THE PROFESSIONS. ROCK, PAPER, SCISSORS PROFESSION DESIGN DOES NOT WORK.
