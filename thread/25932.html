ideas to add to the PvP aspect (add yours please)
Was reading some posts here in the PvP forum and saw a few people had mentioned how they'd like to see PvP in other areas or have something to fight over in DAV or CAV (tarasque type situation was mentioned).

I think that'd be great. Just an idea-

1) I'd like to see a situation like the camps for dodga quest pieces and corona armor in smuggler's den out in the open in some of the central zones (not same pieces of course). That would definitely add some interesting moments.

2) Put a whompa from a clan city to a small clan OP and same for omni in same zone. Put them on opposite sides of the mobs spawning that would be fought over. Also keep them a good distance away from said mob spawn area (500-600 meters).

An addition like this would get instant attention to some of those hardly ever visited areas.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

One other idea, make a new high level unique mob that drops some good loot.

Have him spawn in random locations in say CAV.

Also have him mobile and make it a SHORT spawn time, 20 mins or so.

That would encourage people to actively look for the mob and thus you'd have teams running into each other having small battles all over the zone. I think this relatively small add (just have to model a single new mob and set up the spawn and locations, ect) would add a lot of fun and excitement to pvp and would put all those beautiful zones in the middle of the map to some use.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Reguardless, we need some grid points or whompas and some save points in DAV, CAV, EFP, SFH. I know there are some, but they are very sparse.

Add your ideas please (not about mechanics, just game additions that will make pvp more meaningful/ fun)

Thanks
