The Mjolnir clan, our doors are open :)
We are a relatively laid back clan that just enjoys playing this game. We have an average size player base, with a lot in the lower/middle ranges. Our lvl range is roughly 20-130, but any level is welcome.

We welcome all playstyles and character types. We all just like to have friends that we can team with and help each other out, no mandatory anything.

We have an open vote policy, which means that any issue that deals with the entire clan will be voted on by all members, with no vote carrying any more weight than others. Basically, when it comes to deciding where our clan is headed, the president and newest member have the same weight. This system has worked well for us and for the most part, we've had a really great time.

So, if you're a clanner somewhere between lvl1 and 200, have a respect for the people you play this game with and would like to be involved with a great and helpful org, just click the link in my Sig below and read the "About" section of the guild site and drop us a line in our forum. If you're interested in applying for applicant status in our clan, just post the requested info in the "Applicants" section of the forum and one of our advisors will arrange an in-game meeting with you. thanks for your time.


Thanks,
