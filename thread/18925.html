Crits are bad ;p
Ok, first off, I'm not wearing flame retardant clothes right now, so hold up on the flames ;p.

My idea has to do with crits.

I've seen this mentioned a few times (very few), but when I did I agreed with it. This game is entilerly too crit dependant. I mean traders are pvp gods. You'd think a combat class would hold that title, but that's not how it works.

On most games that have some version of the crit people are excited to get them, the team comments on it. On this game you suck if you don't crit at least once every 3 hits.

Crits are what allow people to take extremely high level missions and mobs. OE is only a part of that problem.

Also, I'd say that at least half of all people who play nano based profs use shotgun. That really bugs me. I mean there are hundreds of weapons, of which at least 20 are very viable for nano based profs to use. But they all use shotties because of the crits.

So basically what I'm saying is that crits need to be dealt with. Not sure how though. Make them more rare, increase the evades vs. crit formulas, or make crit chance come from AC instead of evades.
