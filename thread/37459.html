Dead Lev (an ooc thankyou)
...And so, with blood soaked eyes and a defiant look, Lev fell to the ground in Omni-HQ. Surrounded by Heathen's who only bound themselves to work for a corporation that condoned murder and genocide, then dished out punishment for when it was retaliated in kind.
There was no reclaim, no 2nd chance and no white light beckoning to bring him back to the terminal. It was a different feeling as a permanent death set in, and the life ebbed out of his body. This, was the end.



ooc : I just wanna say a whooping big thankyou to *EVERY*one that was involved tonight, it was huge and fun! I'll still be around, to RP with everyone and have some more interesting conflicts, just not as Lev anymore, who i feel in this game has run his course.

It's been a year now, and from the early days when Rubika's Roleplaying was confined to one club called Baboons, when we rocked the nights away long before the sea of RPing that happens now; to the Enigma days of covert-ops and assassination; the great love of going back to neutrality and fence sitting with Independant Rubikans; and to my eventual final days of causing real trouble as the clanner who kidnapped neutrals, killed and robbed omni's, and tried to wipe out all of your omni asses with bombs It's been a blast! (sorry, ive heard enough of thoes puns tonight) lol

Ive enjoyed RPing Lev with u all and i hope you liked at least ONE of my jokes over the millions i threw at ya! And if not, i hope you liked my movie (below), and if not that, then the first parties we threw on Rubika, or if not that, then the rubber suit.....and if not THAT,....then HEY! GET OUTTA HERE!

I'll be about, you guys will just have to watch out for me....if you see a trox doing too many /italian 's you know i'll be given away

(now all i have to do is ask Cosmik about changing my character profile on the boards here)

Cya all in RP land!
