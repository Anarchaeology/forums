Loretta
Phear Leet
I got off the wagon
AO is a very complex system. Not just because of game mechanics, but also because human players are involved.

Lets take an imaginary Agent character called Carrah. Lets say she is lvl 65 and wields QL 135 lovemaker.

She crits for 1600, and does average damage for about 600 againts leets.

Now she met this Trader, and they went back to training area and he wrangled her (she loves to be wrangled )

So she was able to equip ql 153 stigma rifle (she can't equip ql 153 lovemaker because her shotgun requirements are too low).

She crits for 1300, and does average damage for 500.

The lovemaker has a 5 clip magazine, and it's speed is 2.5/2.5*
The stigma has a 125 clip magazine, and it's speed is 1.0/2.5

That gives her a 40% speed increase over the lovemaker. Thus stigma is a better gun for her.

With the proposed cap, however, the lovemaker becomes a much better choice. In fact so much so, that there is no longer any sense in wielding a pure rifle anymore (one based on rifle/aimed shot)*

In fact, I predict that everyone will start wearing shotguns soon, especially my soldier (why should I put my ip into 6 different skills when I can just up one, shotgun)** I will be capped, and so will everyone else. That is until shotguns are nerfed again. And the screaming for IP re-allocation will start again.

Putting a cap on weapons and armor narrows the choices down immensely. I won't go off on my coat/str/agi rant again, I do hope, however that you get the picture.

These changes might benefit the casting classes for a moment, not because they improve NTs, but because they nerf everyone else. But remember, that the nerf pendulum will swing back.

Funcom, I understand that it is hard to model AO mathematically. But here is an idea.

Scan your database for most commonly used weapons and armor for each class above, say, lvl 50. Then ask yourselves why is it that there are only a few choices being made, when there is so much available out there.

Take those weapons, and armor, see what the average over-equipment factor is.

If it is 25%, then I'd say we are there already. Now please, improve the game for all of us...

-----------------------
*lovemaker depends on rifle/aimed shot/shotgun)

**they are: assault rifle, ranged energy, burst, fling (and for those using switching weapons: heavy weapons, full auto)
