Great idea, works fine for console or even most PC games,
but it doesn't work with MMORPGs

this is because the games change so much, whether it be EQ, UO, DAoC, or AO, all of them are constantly changing, so the players guide is almost completely obsolete by the time it is printed. Look at the guide for AO, other than a couple maps (the map of the city of TIR was especially helpful), and some comments on which areas were good for low level players, most of the info is completely irrelevant now. Professions have been re-vamped since the guide was published, weapons dont work the same, tradeskills are totally different, etc, etc.
It would be great if the companies that make guides for MMORPGs made subscription guides, so that when you pay the $30 for the guide, you get a password to their website, which includes updates for one year, accessible only via password, or something like that. Not that it's the best answer, but it would sure be better than a printed (hard copy) players guide that is outdated 3 months after you get it.
