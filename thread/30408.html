PvP needs another bracket.
I still see folks debating over how "leet" you need to be at 74, how jacked your armor needs to be and what weapons you need to crit the most, etc.

The "74" cap is the brink of exposing yourself to all sorts of PvP issues. At 75 you become everyone's "*****" and those way too powerful for you to handle can strike you down easily. There's no honor amongst PvP'rs in as far as who they will and won't attack, so you generally have some level 80'ish getting gang-banged by 3 or 4 150+ players. All of us know that the lone 80's players is clearly outmatched even with one of these, but the objection to this sort of behavior is silent. Commonly heard is that no one wants to PvP until level 150+ for fear of getting ganked by multi's and some players have told me their stories of being level 75+ and encountering a 170+ enemy that kills so fast they hardly had time to react.

I think Funcom should implement a new bracket for levels 75-129. This new bracket should enable many players to enjoy exploring while retaining the possibility of self defense, and offer new avenues for PvP fun to those that normally would refrain from it due to the advantage given to higher leveled players now (being that they can fight down to level 75 targets). While a 75 most likely will not survive a battle with a 129, there will be more encounters between players of 100 and 129, or 80-110 (for example) since they will not fear being ambushed/jumped/etc by much higher (if not impossible to beat) players.

This bracket would also offer a "training period", if you will, for those that will continue to level on, as they will begin to understand the requirements for tackling harder foes in PvP and learn what to expect when dealing with much more difficult targets as they compare tackling 120'ish players at levels 75-100 (for example).

It does not prevent 130+ players from participating in PvP, but it does mean that they will be forced to hunt more appropriate targets then the "just leveled to 75 - ready for my first spanking" player in which besting that level 75 holds no reward, no honor, only grief and the sense of accomplishment only a perverted or derranged mind can appreciate.

This bracket would also give a more widespread use of the Arena and might lead to a few Doctors I know getting involved in PvP.

I disagree, Hextorr, and here's why:

Along that line of thinking, you create an environment where all players feel they must reach 200 to compete or even enjoy PvP.

Without this step (the bracket) in PvP, level 75's that are simply attempting to mission and have no desire to PvP are forced to have their own freedom, or right if you will, to play and enjoy the game stripped away by a level 150+ looking to get a title or simply kill someone. In essence, you're telling players that @ 75+ they can enjoy the game - as long as they don't go to 25% zones - because it they do, then they place themselves in harm's way. Right? Yes. And the issue is that most of your level 75's won't mind losing to a 129, but will become discouraged (and possibly quit - I see it often enough) and view the game's higher levels as unattainable, or their character is gimped, or that profession sucks, etc. And I've heard those statements over and over...

With the current rules and brackets, people who do not wish to PvP are forced to endure it by the whim of people who do, or that want to cause grief, or that simply enjoy PvP enough they will kill someone who doesn't even stand a remote chance in hell of putting up any form of fight.

Perhaps for you, this is acceptable, but for most people it's not. A level 200 killing a level 75 has no honor - it's a hollow victory. My Enforcer friend on RK1 (lvl 170+) ran up to a level 115 Doctor and sent her to reclaim in but a few seconds. Seconds. He and I talked and, like so many others, he engaged because he was certain of the kill. He knew she would not win - period. This motivated him to attack her and he admits that 'it's nothing - pointless since she's not able to fight back, but since you can get them - do it'.

With the change, more people will feel more comfortable about venturing into PvP zones, entering PvP, and possibly waging large battles.

Also, consider that about 127 you might be capping out, there is already motiviation to continue on and level past 129. That in itself certifies that you will have folks above the new bracket that can still enter the large battles and use large tactics as you mentioned (relying upon higher level allies, etc.). So placing the new bracket will not stop the higher level PvP action - it might make it tougher for all the 150+ that have been preying upon 75's, it might mean that they have to actually *gasp* make and effort - It WILL provide positive motivation for people in the new range to take more chances then they currently are likely to do.

The effect of this discussion in my guild alone has generated hope that many members in these levels cna enjoy "some solid PvP fun without the B.S. of those gankers ganking you." This in itself is enough to prove that the interest in improving PvP is present in the players.

I'm not asking for a PvP switch, and I got the Arena for dueling. I'm suggesting that something that was clearly not viewed in the long term be revised to improve our enjoyment. The trend in all games is too make PvP an enjoyable, low risk, experience.

And if you were on Pacific, chances were it was me or someone I knew ganking you with the X-Bow. Of course, tossing a Vortex out there to play with the "blues" was fun too.
