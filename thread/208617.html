ECONOMICAL & TRADE

·1 Auction Terminals

To me, one of the biggest frustrations is sometimes to trade stuff with other players for money. Even if I have something worth a lot, like a GA disc, its not always so that there are enough players online at the moment.
So, to make trade even more interesting and less frustrating I think there should be Auction Terminals in the game for the trading people in every great city (and maybe at some other random places too).

The player wanting to sell something transfers his/her items into an account on the auction terminal and pays a small auction fee (10xlvl?). Maybe there should be choosable categories to place them under?
The selling player also states a minimum bid and duration of the auction ( 1 - 7 days limit?). I was thinking maybe up to 7 items (full armor set) could be placed into an account at the same time.

Now the buying player can send an offer on any auctioned item/group of items via the terminals. He does this by placing his bid money on the item (Directly drawn from his current amount of money. If he looses the auction he gets them back directly when auction is over.)

Maybe the selling player can at any time walk in and choose one (or none) of the current bids and thus end the auction prematurely?

The buying player who won can collect the items at the terminal next time he uses it. If noone bids, the items could be picked up at the terminal by the selling player within one day after it ends. If he still hasnt done it, they should be moved to his bank.

I was thinking that it would be wise to make this faction related, just like the shopping channels. Or maybe they would be city/area dependant?


·2 Real Banking

Players should be able to store money on their bank accounts. This money should then grow depending on rates.

Imagine having 2 different Banks: The collossal Omni-Bank and the planetwide Bank of Independence.
The Omni-Bank should only be open to Omnis and Neutrals. Of course Neutrals would have to pay a certain large fee for membership in the Omni-Bank while Omnis of course are in by default.
The Bank of Independence is respectively open to anybody individual and requires no membership fee whatsoever.
The Omni-Bank would have higher interest rates, making it the better of the two, not too surprising.

This should allow players to secure certain amounts of money on themselves and not be 100% dependant of organisations for economical growth. After all one of the things that makes AO so brilliant is the many choices, and the great possibilities of solo play. This should enhance this further.

I have no idea how often the players would get money back for the interest rates, and how large these percents should be, since I have no clue as to what would be a good monetary balance ingame.


·3 Loans

Individuals should be able to take loans from the bank. The amount they should be able to borrow should be dependent on their level and the taxation rate they choose.
They should be able to go to the bank and pay back the whole sum of the loan if they have enough cash. But that is kinda self-explanatory.

The only obvious problem I see with this is for the game to check ALL money transactions to the player, not only those in shops but also those in between players, and apply the proper tax on the players income.

Also, maybe rates could grow over time if the players dont pay back soon enough?


·4 Harvesting the land

The upcoming booster pack Towns of Rubi-Ka and the little I know of it gave me another great (?) idea for income.
If the inhabitants of Rubi-Ka can build settlements, shouldnt they also be able to harvets the land? After all, in truth, its not like bronto burgers are made of bronto meat only. There needs to be some salad and tomatoes too. And you cant eat Notum after all (or can you?).
So, if they can build houses, why not gardens or farms? And if so, whynot be able to sell the reosurces from those?

This should be a slow type of income and should be isolated o individual investments and not organisation dependant.
Of course, to harvest the land the player would need to hire some NPC (in stores) as a gardener, farmer to help them out (after all the player have better things to do with his time than sawing seeds when he logs on to the game).

In addition I was thinking that maybe the type of greed or whatever was area dependant. You dont grow salad in Newland Desert, but maybe some type of ****e. And maybe different resources give different income over time?

This got me thinking further: Maybe mutant-carrots are common in omni territory, while hellfire-****e is common in clan territory. Selling hellfire ****e to clanners would thus give lower income, while selling them to omnis would give larger and vice versa. And how do you sell to certain factions/cities? Well, without wanting to turn this into an EVE-wannabe game, I guess you could just buy a certain trade contract with different locations on Rubi-Ka.

Anyway, I still think this idea might help encourage people to colonise Rubi-Ka more, without only RPG/PvP as an argument.


POLITICAL/SOCIAL

·5 Organisation HQ

With an upcoming expansion like Towns of Rubi-Ka it would be pretty neat to make the organisation build a HQ building at their townsquare. One which can be entered. The design of such a place should come in various models if you ask me, maybe Faction dependant, maybe not. But I think it would be neat if you if you could enter a org town and the main building could show which Faction controlled the land.
Now what should you be able to do in such a place? Well, for one it could work as a good place to meet up to discuss raids, so it should have a Lobby of some sort. There should also be a main hall with the usual Bank Terminal. I also think you should have a save and reclaim terminal there. What definitely should be there is a Grid entrance so you could easilly access the grid from your org town (This of course should call for the need to modify the Grid itself with an additional Org HQ grid exit that would take a character to his/her orgs HQ consequently). Maybe some basic shop where you could sell your junk and buy FAs and Treatment kits.


·6 Bulletin Boards

Each city on Rubi-Ka worth its name should have bulletin boards. Here players and organisations could leave messages to each others. Also it would be a good tool for further roleplay and atmosphere. Organisations and DMs alike could write down news and other nice tidbits of information. Maybe there is a need for headhunters when an org wants to get hold of a certain character? Write it down in the bulletin board.


·7 Organisation Banners

A favorite idea of mine, but probably hard to implement. The basic idea is to let organisation hire some of the banner-signs for personal marketing.

Problem is: How do you import custom graphics into AO for this?

My solution is to make an ingame engine for creating banners, where there is a certain amount of fixed graphical components and textfonts that can be used for this.

Still probably quite cumbersome to implement though, and possibly not worth the effort.


·8 Social Armor Revisited

Ever overequipped your character, and then felt like doing some nice RPGing at the local bar? Of course not. When youve overequipped your character it is overequipped and you dont want to go through the process of doing it again, since it took you two hours of game time getting that tank armor on.
As I see it, it would add a lot of incitaments to roleplay if changing to social clothes were a lot easier.
My suggestion would be stolen from Diablo 2. Have 2 sets of armors. One for clothing, and one for armors. Make the player then able to switch between the two with a simple mouseclick or by doing some keyboard combination like Ctrl + C or such. The game should remember that the old equipped armor is on, but not give the armor buffs from them while social armor/clothes is showing, and of course the same for the clothes when changing back to armor.

WORLD RELATED

·9 Warzones

Probably the most insane idea of mine. But one that, just like Shadowlands did, would open up great new possiblities to gameplay.

There is a conflict on Rubi-Ka. But except for players from different factions not able to pass safe through certain territories or cities and PvP battles, there is VERY little signs of a struggle. Sure, you read a lot about it on the official site and in the game news, but you witness very little of the actual conflict ingame.
Sure, the tower battles has opened it up a little, but since omni organisations sometimes attack other omni towers and same thing for clans, that isnt really true either.

So, my solution is: Create some different type of warzones. These could be placed anywhere there would be a good cause for permanent battle or conflict. Maybe in some of those shadowed areas on the Rubi-ka map that hasnt been opened for play yet? Zones of interest for both clan and omni-tek?

Anyway, the basic idea about what a warzone is and how it should function is as follows:

Most Warzones should be considered as a new type of Missions, although you dont get them at mission terminals, but at certain military offices only available to clan and omni. The missions will usually have the form of:
1. Defend/Hold position at point Y for XXX minutes at Warzone Z
2. Take point Y in XXX minutes at Warzone Z
3. Disable/Place bomb at location/iten Y in XXX minutes at Warzone Z
4. Retrieve/Save item/NPC X from Warzone Y
5. Kill X mobs at Warzone Y
6. Kill mob-officer X at Warzone Y


The mission in itself will not start until you enter a warzone area.
These types of mission warzones should be layered for different mob QLs (like a warzone for lvl 1-15, 16-30, and so on)

All warzones can be played single, but at a high price, since the difficulty would be hard. Mobs should spawn and advance in waves.

Also, I have in mind 2 other types of warzones. The PvP one is a definite, as to emphasize the faction struggle. PvP warzones should be more of anarchy style warzones and should have no level limits up (but maybe a min level for entering, like lvl 100.). Mobs should be spawned here too, for both sides and mobs should randomly also attack each other if from different factions).

The other is the kind of warzones that should be able to be spawned at ant location at any given time by a DM. Like an assault on Tir, where the clanners get a warning like 30 minutes ahead of time, and then they have to kill all attacking mobs that spawn to attack outside the city for like 30 minutes (or until they are all dead).

Now why would any player choose to enter Warzones? What are the benefits? Well, for one good XP. However I think the loot would consist most of item drops, just like in Shadowlands, and not in money. Items should be fairly good, but, to give it a little edge: All items dropped at Warzones should be PvP lootable. All loot in warzones should also be lootable by ANYBODY to simulate the chaos of the battlefield, not only by the one sho killed the mob.
Maybe characters having done a certain amount of warzone missions get rewards and titles?
These are of course just vague ideas. I bet someone else can come up with plenty other, maybe better ways of simulating warzones or assaults into the game.


·10 Battle Vehicles and Turrets

As an addition to warzones there should be introduced new vehicles that with great success could be used in the warzones (or maybe at Tower battles?).

Im thinking of slowmoving vehicles with great battle power. Most vehicles in AO today rely mostly on speed and maneuvearbility. I think it needs some tank Vehicles. Or maybe some deployable battle turrets that work almost like a vehicle, except its fixed?


·11 Advanced Missions

I know that the AO community has been, and still is, screaming for more quests like the pads quest and alike. With this idea I dont offer that, but I offer a more dynamic sort of mission I think should be available besides the current type of missions.

These are missions that would require solo play (but I guess could work perfectly with team enhanced versions too) and be randomised somewhat like the current missions at terminals.

The difference is that the advanced missions should require more steps to finish.
Here its not just enter randomised dungeon and do X and leave you need to finish it, but you rather have to go through a small simulated quest.

The mission should consist of a random number of steps (3-6?) to be finished. The objective at each location will vary and be defined in the location previosuly visited. All steps will be randomised, and each new step will be randomised when the one before is finished. When all steps are done you get rewards like usual in mission + tokens or whatever. Maybe they should be a bit higher since a lot of travel will be abound. (Higher for neuts I think, whose XP instead of token should be considerably higher for this type of mission).

For example, a 3 step mission could work like this:

- At the mission terminal you get the mission to decrypt some info at a certain place (justlike a regular mish). That is step 1. You go to the location and find the device to decrypt the info you get. Now, you will get a new mission from the info you decrypted: Find person X at location X and interrogate or assasinate him. (randomised, but in this example we say its like that)

- That is step 2. At this location you will, say, enter a small office randomised from a few types of offices. Sometimes maybe the person is friendly and you will just have to talk to him. Maybe he isnt, and you have to kill his tough bodyguards. In the later example the NPC you need to talk to wont talk unless you kill those. If you should talk to this NPC hes unattackable, but if hes to assasinate just go for it. Anyway, the result will be the same. If you talk to him, you will get a new mission step since its a 3 step mission. If you kill him, you will get a new mish from killing him.
In this case lets say the random mission genreator chooses its a conspiracy you are uncovering, and now you need to find the hard evidence and bring back to your employers. That is step 3.

- You go to step 3s location and uncover the evidence (again, probably a dungeon-like place) in the usual mission way. When finsihed you get your reward.

Now, this was of course just an example of one type of advanced mission. I chose the uncover conspiracy theme. There should of course be many other different types. Other examples could be:

Simple variants:
- Visit X places to talk to X people
- Visit X places to get X items

Complex variants:
- Collect evidence of person Xs existance
- Find person X
- Uncover a secret organisation
- Undercover operation
- Find Person X and rob him

Also, advanced missions should call for a few new environments (generated or fixed mini-dungeons consisting of only very few rooms) except the usual mission dungeons:

Offices:
- Shabby Office
- Omni official Office
- Luxuary office
- Clan official office
- Battle-torn office
- Neutral office

Warehouses:
- Large
- Medium
- Small

Hideouts:
- Thief hideout
- Clan hideout
- Agent hideout

Of course there might be more exciting environments this type of missions could benefit from, but these are the ones I came to think of.
Preferabbly all dungeons in this type of mission should be 1-level, even in team. Team mission should just get the additional boss room maybe?
Anyway, I hope you get the idea or a hint of what I'mthinking here. I hardly think this could be a hard thing to code either. Not that my opinion matters.


·12 Reporter Career

As I see it, there can never be enough profession choices in an MMORPG, or any MMOG for that. The more choices, the more diversity, the more fun. Thats the fundament of dynamic gameplay if you ask me. However a new career should also make a difference; Add something to the game and not just only be a direct variant of another career.
The Reporter is the kind of career I personally would love to see in AO, just because it gives social implications, and also gives more idea of actual civilisation on RK. At least as how I envison it.
So what type of career choice should the reporter be then? My first image is that of a reporter hiding behind some ruined wall on a battlefield, accompanied by two merceneries for guarding his safety, while he takes photographs documenting of the ongoing battle. The second image I get is that of the investigating reporter, sneaking into an office to steal some secret documents to reveal the evil corporations ongoing illegal affairs.
So what makes a good reporter on Rubi-Ka? His ability to know where the scoop is, be where the scoop is, and his talents to actually getting that scoop. To always be ahead. Thus to me, a good reporter:

· Can manipulate people for different purposes
· Is good at getting to places and into places
· Has a good network of friends and contacts
· Is probably quite a good hacker
· Is objective in his research

My idea of simulating this in AO would be something of the following:
A reporters tradeskills should consist primarily of Electrical Engineering, Psychology and Computer Literacy. Make those green skills preferably. Psychology would make the reporter good at bluffing, getting past the mobs. Electrical Engineering and CompLiteracy simulates his hacking abilities.
Vehicles should also be one of the prime skills for a reporter. It would be nice if there was a career that could be considered the best vehicle drivers. Thus I think the reporter career would be a good excuse for such. Maybe there could be made more tradeskill items for such a career, that could tinker and trim the existing vehicles more? Break and Entry and Disarm skills should also be skills hes fairly good at. Lightblue at least. When it comes to weapons I think the reporter should be equally good at all weapons, and thus be able to choose whichever one he wants. The only exception I could think of would be heavy weapons and 2-handed weapons. A reporter needs to move easily on the battlefield.

Reporter Nanolines? I guess this is the tricky part, where it would seem like the career could be just a mix of Agent, Fixer or Bureaucrat. Sure it would seem tempting to make the reporter be able to have such things as fake profession nanos. But I think thats bad idea. However, obviously many buffs would be typical and probably not original. But I dont think thats an issue as long as there are a few nanolines that can be considered unique and that main focus will be on. And that those mimmicking other careers arent better than those original professions.
My ideas for such nanolines consists of just taking a few of my earlier ideas about the reporter further. Hes a good driver? Then let some nanos affect vehicles in a way or another. Give him some vehicle battle nanos for the manta or any new vehicles that FC could release. Let him boost Vehicle speed, or Vehicle skills for the team. Also, maybe a nano many would like would be a masstransport one. Lets say the Reporter casts it before equipping his vehicle, then it affects the whole team for like an hour or two (depending on QL?). If the reporter within that limit of time equips a vehicle, all of his teammates will get a question if they will ride with the reporter. If they say yes, they will get the reporters view but will not be able to steer. The reporter steers. Anyone wanting to jump off the vehicle only need to shut down the nano. When done so they will go back to normal view.
Another nanoline would be one I would call confusion. This could work in many ways. One would be to work like the crats nanos, except the effect is random between calm/charm/fear. Another idea would be to make the confusion nanos debuff the inits of opponents and also calm or fear them for say 1-5 seconds (depending on QL?).
Other than that I think there should be some team buffs, similar to crat motivational speeches, except these ones should be called Morale-line and Demoralise-line, and except off/def skills also affect inits and crit chances. Maybe the reporter also should have some small Cloak-nanoline with personal-only evade buffing, though not as good as a fixer. One nano-line should also buff the reporters ability to bluff, for solo play purpose. Not directly raise psychology, I think the crat should excel at that, but just give a percentage bonus that buffs the chance of bluffing a mob. Maybe the same line could buff disarm/break and entry skills? Calling the line Investigating? All in all I think a reporter should rely heavily on buffing his team and debuffing the enemy in the direct vicinity.

Also, most of the BEST (definitely not all, but obviously dominant) equipment for a reporter should be Neutral only. And have some nano buffs that can only be cast on Neutrals. Why? Well, the best investigating reporters are always the independent ones (who strive more to be objective). No question about that. It would also give the game some credibility and a reason to play Neutral except for roleplaying issues or to just make it more of a challenge. It would also make sense that reporters would like to stay neutral while documenting the ongoing conflict between the Omnis and The Clans. I think all in all, designing reporter items should be easy. There would be a lot of interesting gear a reporter uses. Maybe it could compete with crats in coolest gear if ever realised.

I know theres lot more to come up with for a profession like this, but these are at least my main concepts about it.

BALANCING

·13 Dungeons Revisited


Rubi-Ka needs more dungeons. Period. It doesnt need more dungeons in context of filling up level ranges or offering more überitems. It needs more dungeons to offer more places to adventur or raid. Take Totw as a prime example. Its the first dungeon every newbie this side of the sun hears of. Thus its the place everyone in that certain level range crowds in. The benefit of that is that its easy to team. The loss is that everyone runs around in totw cloaks and scythes and whatnot in that range. Just because they have become the only items worth having in that range. The GoT-belt, Exarch, Tank and the Stygian being the prime examples. Nothing is wrong with that if that is whats intended: That everyone MUST visit Totw unless they want to have a character everyone laughs at.
So, what is needed is equivalent dungeons in the same level ranges to the dungeons already existing, with equivalent powerful item droppings and xp/money per kill. Not the exact same objects, but equivalents. Or with emphasis on other careers.


·14 Some Neutral Advantages

Even though I dont play neutrals, I really think Neutrals should become at least a little more attractive to play. And its something I will always push for.

One idea I had was that Neutrals should get a small quest in their Training grounds/Backyards from an ICC representative whod ask them if they would help ICC out. It would be some straightforward quest like killing some lvl 4-5 miniboss (in the training ground) which you easilly could kill with a small team of lvl 2 peoples, or to find some unique drop from a specific mobtype. The reward would be an ICC-badge (named Friend of ICC or maybe something less cheesy) that just as with the omni and clan boards could be worn at the neck. The badge itself would give neutrals some buffs that would be considered ok for the first 15-20 levels.
The badge could be updated/replaced with a better one if you as a neutral would decide to join ICC. This should be done at the ICC HQ, where there should be another ICC NPC handing out quests to people depending on their level and as long as they are neutral and have the first badge. Make 5-6 quests, and make the reward a better ICC title-badge.
However, these badges should never be as good as the faction boards. But it would give neutrals at least something. And you could also add other good item rewards for these ICC quests.
Likewise I think there should be more simple quests in Jobe for neutrals handed out by Jobe researchers, that could compete with the ICC badges, so that the Neutrals could choose to help the Jobe research team or join the ICC.
This would also add a lot of rpg atmosphere to the game.


·15 Crat Issues

I hear many complains about the crat. Some I agree with, most I dont. However, the crat is nerfed. It is not as good at what it does compared to other careers at the same levels as it should be.
Sure, the crat got better with SL, but only in attractiveness for others to team with. It is still nerfed, even if its a lot better than it used to be.
So how to make the crat better and more enjoyable for the player himself? I have a few suggestions:

· Psychology as a tradeskill right now is pathetic. Sure Jobe implants and all, but before a crat has 1250 in Psychology? Are there like 7 or 8 tradeskill items that use Psychology even? Add a few more good/semi-good tradeskill items that need psychology (and comp lit?) to make the crat more useful to others except in PvM teamplay.
· My example of a new type of tradeskill item would be a Psionic brainwave distorter (or call it Psionic Defense Transmitter.. whatever works..) that could be worn in a HUD or UTILS slot. Make them give the wearer a small buff to all AC and all Defensive skills, and have 5 different models of it at 5 different QLs to be made (which could be controlled by the QL of the main component, whatever that would be named). A suggestion is QL 15, 30, 60, 90, 120. QL 15 would give a +2 bonus to ACs and a +4 bonus to evades and a +1 to agg/def. Every new QL variant would double the stats of the previous ones. Make complit the main requirement.
· A Briefcase of Holding Mark 2 with some nanoskill buffs, maybe from TL 5?
· Team empowered charms suck as they are now. Whats the point in having them team empowered when they only last for about a minute? That makes them useless for teamplay!! Make them last a little longer. Double the time they work now I would suggest. Or make that team member dependant too. 1 minute per team member or so
· Let the normal, non-empowered charms last until the crat zones. After all they are mostly used in soloplay because of their psychic limitations. There is no reason to keep the normal charms as they are now. Give the crat SOME battle advantage of his own.
· Why does the crats Pet time-out after a little more than an hour? This seems highly unmotivated too. Its not like when you summon your assistant like when you summon a demon from the nether realsm or somesuch. It is obedient to you, and bound by you by program, not by some enchantment. Let it stay like engineer pets does. Because its just annoying as it is now and makes little difference if they stay until you log. It's not the crat pets are über-fighting machines anyway.
· Make the enhanced coffee buffs show on the item info. Many seem totally unaware of what it actually does which makes it hard to sell.
· Another psyhology buff nano would be nice. On Ql 100 or so, for +80 in Psychology. Also Authorative Figure should last longer.
· Why is BM the main nano skill for crats, when the charm line is the only useful nanoline using it? Make some other nanoline (Roots? hint hint) use BM also instead of one of their existing skills.
