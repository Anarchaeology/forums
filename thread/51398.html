Just a quick rundown on how evades and crits work.
Opponents attack rating checks versus your evades. If they are equal, the opponent has a 50% chance to hit you. If the opponents attackrating is superiour (exact numbers unknown) he will crit you because of his attackrating. If your evades are superiour he will miss. Now, crit buffs and scopes (22% in your example) means that 22% of the hits landed on you will be crits. No way to decreasing that number is known, so... dont get hit. Lower opponents attackrating so he cant hit you, or raise evades and use the agg/def bar to go defensive. Less hits on you = less crits on you.
