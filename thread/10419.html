Weird biotech/mantis items and mission types
I've gotten a couple of really weird missions (at around level
90 difficulty) to locations which basically consisted of a weird
red organic environment with the antechamber being a large
mouth and tongue. The find-item targets appeared to be
related to pet breeding, and possibly to mantises. I've
now got a weird alien egg containment field and a set of
breeding tubes and am wondering what other interesting
bits of related tech might be scattered around.

Any idea what these things are for? Mantis ravagers can
rip me apart in passing at the moment, so I'm not going
wander around the nastier parts of sfh at the moment
looking for clues.

I'm speculating this is connected to a new static mission
someplace, or at least to some new trade skillable thing
that can be done with all this junk, but possibly it is
related to some story event yet to happen.

Regardless of whether the stuff is actually usable for something
it looks interesting, and the new mission types are really
weird and visually interesting at least, so kudos to the
designers, anyhow....

-Strycker OT NT 90

max money find-item quests. I suspect I had max order
