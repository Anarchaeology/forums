City defence!
How come I can't aid guards when clans come in and openly attack them in the middle of Omni-1? Why can';t I defend my home? I, therefor, have this suggestion: 50% suppression gas, an optional PvP action. If you choose you can lower the suppression gas around you, perhaps through an option or item, to 50%. Using Omni-Tek affiliation as an example, with 50% supression gas you can attack clannies inside of Omni-Tek controlled areas and be attacked by them. However, when entering clan controlled areas, you are autmatically placed into 50% supression gas. However, in 50% gas, you cannot attack someone else who is still under 75% gas. In places of neutral affiliation, however, you cannot drop to 50% gas from a 75% gas zone. Also, out of this, you will be allowed to heal, buff, and aid any OT affiliated NPC. This will allow people to actually HELP rather than stand around and watch the guards be killed in the middle of Trade. Just note this would go for clans, too, I used OT as an example.

Also, for neutrals, they should also be able to switch to 50% gas and attack OT or Clans and aid their NPCs. However, the only way for neutrals to be in 50% gas is if they choose to be. They will always remain in 75% gas in OT or Clan terratories unless they choose 50% gas. However, since some neutrals will take advantage of this, if they attack or are attacked by OT or clan guards (note, guards, not just regular mobs) they are dropped to 50% gas automatically and OT (or clan) are allowed to attack them.

Please consider this idea, Funcom! Cz, Cosmik, whoever brings these suggestions to the developers, please make sure this one is on top of the list. I absolutely hate it when I'm forced to watch some clannie attack and kill guards in the middle of trade or ent yet be unable to do anything about it.

Edit: Forgot a couple things. You obviously cannot drop to 50% in 100% suppression gas. Also, when entering 50% gas, it warns you. "Suppression gas around you is dropping! You will be able to attack the (Omni-Tek corporate scum/rebel Clan scum) and be attacked by them in 15... 14... 13... (etc.)"
