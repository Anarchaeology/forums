A copy and paste of my answer on a question by a new player with a low/mid level doc


1) Agg-def bar
Already said, put that agg-def bar all to the right.
Some people who team say put it up to ~70%, still decent speed but a lot less aggro.
That is until your nano init is so high that you can insta use nano's then you can lower the bar.

2a) Comp lit part 1
Very important. you want this high, high enough to be able to at least use a 5x belt (highest quality you can buy in the store).
350 odd complit allows you to easily equip a 6x belt, only problem is that those cost 1 million to 1.5 million and you need to find the right player to buy it from (mission only item). Don't forget you can use implants to equip that belt.

2b) Comp lit part 2
NCU memory, once you can use that 5x belt you can stick in decent ql memory (ql 125 shop bought [if available] costs around 200k, ouch)

Why do you want this? If you solo you can stick a cpu upgrade (lowers nano costs) and a recompiler (increases nano init) in the last two slots. In a team it allows you to have more friendly buffs (last 2 slots filled with memory, or memory & recompiler).
Don't forget that comp lit is cheap for a doc (around the same cost as doc main nano skills), use it.

3a) Implants
Doctors are the absolute master of implants, light green treatment skill, at 75+ biomech cloak & helmet, add in an omni medsuit, treatment buff, general buff and you can easily get to the 600 treatment (after clinic bonus) required to use ql125 implants. Worst problem is that you can only start equipping them (after using the boost nano for abilities) at lvl 80, that is without other implants to step required abilities.
If you stick to the important doc locations (head, eye, chest, waist, right hand, maybe ear for the int) you can add all required implants by using lower ql implants for the abilities asked in other places (ex. Psyc in chest/waist to get in the eye and ear, then remove them to get the other implants on those places in).
Also think about what ability an implant requires, bright chest bio met ->str, add shining life and it becomes int much more of a core ability (and if you added int to the eye & ear now equipable).

I got most of my ql 125 implants in at levels 70-75 by the smart use of implants to temp up abilities. Might want to save the leg implant for the last & then get a friendly agent to use feline grace to get it in (most clusters in the leg make it require agility).

After you get in the main implants you can start thinking about other stuff, shotgun, ranged init, evades, run speed , first aid, pharma tech ,ac clusters, etc.

3b) Nanonanny

Use this gem of a program, it shows what cluster goes where, if you want to stick multiple clusters in the same place, gives the amount of treatment & amount in the ability needed to wear the implant.

Nanonanny

4a) Ok how to finance this

Since empty implants cost 61k (and a bit) for ql125 ones, shining clusters easily 100k+, 30k or so for brights & around 8k for faded a complete set of 13 ql 125 implants runs in the millions (not to mention a lot of time to hunt for compatible clusters).

The best way to finance this is monster part farming. I don't know what faction you are and I can only give decent info around newland you might want to ask around where lvl 40 to 50 monsters are for your faction. Alternatively you can start to do low level mission farming going for battlesuits.
Even with all the lag I recommend the Newland City mission booth since you can get missions to wailing & varmint woods which if you stick the order/chaos slider to max chaos usually results in a mission with monster what in turn means monsterparts (check mission for keywords like cave and den).

4b) Clicksaver
To prevent you from getting RSI by having to scroll through every single mission to find something of value (in this case battlesuits) someone made this gem of a program that (if you run in windowed mode) shows the info on all 5 mission in one easy overview.

Clicksaver

5) Solo
Get sense imp & psy mod up to the level that you can use exhaust muscles.
Up mattercreation, at your level scythe A and another DoT combined will do reasonable damage. You might want to add a low level direct damage nano to get rid of roots fast.
First aid is your friend. might want to have it at a level that you can use ql70 nano stims. Specialist treatment & first aid expertise add 40 points, leaves 310 (or so), ql 75-80 biomech (8 locations 8 4) adds 32,80+ adds 40.

Just don't forget you can't equip the armour until you are in your hunting zone or mission. The game strips you of proffesion when you zone, then adds the armor, then gives you your proffesion back, resulting in no bonus (since the first aid/treatment bonus is doc only) and a penalty if you remove your armor until you zone again (your a doc so it strips the bonus when you remove the armor resulting in lowered skills)

6) Dying
Lure every single mob in a mission to the entrance room.
