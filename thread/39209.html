Quests
Now first off, I'll go on record and say that I don't know exactly what is entailed in making quests in a game like AO. But after doing Alvin part 1&2 tonight, and after thinking back to some quests I'd do in the old MUDs I use to play back when the www was a telnet address, I'm afraid I'm going to run the risk of sounding foolish by saying pathetic. I can not believe it took much effort to creat the Alvin quest. It sucks. It's boring. It takes hours of killing a gazillion mechdogs, camping TK, blahblahblah. There is absolutely no creativity involved. The text displayed can't even wrap correctly on my chat screen. And yet, by the fact that there is only one quest in the entire game (I do not count the fixer quest or cyber armor quest), am I suppose to believe that implementing quests is a hard thing to do? Or better yet. Am I suppose to believe that missions are an excuse not to create quests?

So here's my suggestion. Round up some of those swedes who worked on the early MUDs and hire them to write you some quests. Then get your dev team to create the items/npcs etc needed. Because after doing Alvin, I'm afraid that whoever created that quest had the creativity of a head of cabbage. Now I realize that you, Funcom, have like three employees. So here's the deal. Take the two employees who worked on designing new skins for chests and put them to work on creating mods for the items/npcs etc that would be used in whatever quests your super swedes come up with. (Hiring them might cost money, but subscriptions are 'soaring' right?) Take the third employee who has shown his genius through his work on the level locked items and nanos, and put him to making coffee so the other two employees and the swedes can work 24/7.

Once the world of Rubi-Ka has a gazillion quests with many interesting personalities to talk to, who say more then 'I do not understand.', you can put all your resources back into nerfing the proffession.
