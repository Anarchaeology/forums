Blood transfusion service is pretty much balanced
Ok- I am now a research Doc (lol) and have been experimenting with making the kits. I think that this trade skill is pretty well balanced at the mo'.

Why ? Because any doc is going to have to invest a *lot* of money initially to get the maximum value out of the monster parts.

From my initial attempts it appears that you can put any ql mob parts into the bio-communitor. I will try to do some more research this weekend when I have time and the cash !!

You then get blood plasma that is near the level of the communitor- hence you run the risk of producing lower standard material than you could have got if you had used a higher ql communitor.

The blood plasma can then be sold (with a price level related to the new ql). Ql 15 blood plasma could be sold for only approx 330 credits at the shop.

To use it further you need to buy similar level equipment to make the appropriate level treatment kit.

In order that you get the maximum money out of the monster parts you need to buy a range of communitors- Once you have done so there is the question of collecting the parts (and Tukon is right, they are not as easy to come by as you may think).

So- the blood transfusion (lol) trade is a way of making money but will not be a get rich quick scheme. There will have to be a lot of investment and a lot of time collecting the parts and processing. Some docs will not want to do this.

Personally I do not mind- it adds to my role.

I also think that the real money to be made will be when the treatment kits and stims are unbroken... things will get really interesting then.
