AS the title says your welcome. Also I forgot to mention that if you set the slider to full money and picked up radar type missions you still get a decent amount of cash PLUS you get full exps. See the slider for the money worsk in that if you set the slider to full money and get a mission that says 80K creds, you get 80K creds for completing the mission. If you set the slider for full exps and get 80K exps you really only get the max exps for that level which is like 20K for missions around level 70.

So the trick is to set the slider to full money and do radar missions. Radar missions always give exps even when set on full cash, but give out about half the cash. So instead of 80K creds you get 40K creds BUT you get 20K exps. So if you got 3 identical missions and could do them in rapid succession you are getting 60K+ exps in a matter of minutes. If you are out in broken shores you can get missions for brokenshores (which is what fiez, sent, morbid, and ryuze do) and run through them very fast. Never stop to loot chest, or kill monsters cause that slows you down. Just get 3 missions, get 60K exps, get 3 more missions, get more exps, rinse/repeat add nauseum.

Also as you get higher level you need more exps to get to the next level but the beauty of missions is the exps scales up with you as you level so getting 1 level at 70 takes just as much time as getting one at level 100. I know cause I was talking with a guide and he went on a mission he made and he got 486K exps for it at level 150. Do you NOT see how he higher level people are making levels really fast and doing it solo?

The hard part is the middle levels where mission exp rewards suck and you need a group with a doc to mass kil reds to go up levels this fast but once you reach the break even point go missions and never look back.

This last patch fixed a bunch of this crap and the thing I like best is that clanners can no longer by the better equipment in omni cities like Fiez does. So since they never stop to loot chests anymore it's going to be a bit tougher for them to stay on top with the best equipment.

Of course that will change because they will all group again when they need equipment and go through a couple missions like normal to get it.

And yes they do sometimes just set the mission to hard and go through it like normal people in a group would cause I did it with them so it's not 100% of the time exploiting for them.

And I'm not even sure running through a mission just for mass exps/creds is really an exploit. Why should one be forced to stop to kill things along the way? This game boasted playibility in that one never needed to kill a thing.

Quiffco
If you really are desperate to change to omni/clan after lvl 11, it's not that hard, just means camping a mission booth for a while. all you need to do is set the difficulty above max (so that the form is usable at your level) and then money to max, and top slider to 'bad', then keep spawning the "find (not retrieve) item" missions, and keep checking the item you have to find (not reward) for a low/mid/high lvl form.

I turned my lvl 21 neutral agent to clan a couple of days ago with this method. I'd seen the low level omni-form come up, but the slider was below middle, so it'd have been too low, but later I got a clan low level, which was usable up to lvl 22 (just above middle difficulty)

So it's not impossible to go clan/omni after lvl 11, it's just not so easy.
