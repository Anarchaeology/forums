Hmm...
What NERF's are you talking about specifically? Your post makes it sound like every single NERF was due to PvP, and that is not true.

The NERF's are about game balance.

As an example, As Yazule mentioned, MK Shield needed to be "fixed".

This wasn't just PvP. People were killing NPC's way beyond their lvl. In fact that fix HELPED PvM, because you no longer see groups of soldiers killing "uber NPC's". Instead they are in groups, groups that you might be a part of and benefit from having a soldier in the group.

Also, if any class can lvl too fast due to game mechanics (the MK Shield fix is a good example), it hurts game balance, and that needs to be fixed.

Also, anything that hurts game balance hurts you further in PvM. If a certain class is known to have a serious advantage or be able to use a bug, unless you are that class, you may have more difficulty getting invited to groups.

People want to feel like they belong and are needed and that they provide some benefit (Role Play).

The following from your post is indirectly incorrect:

<incorrect>
you will never see someone come complaining saying "this enforcer in my team hits way too hard on mobs, please nerf enforcers."
</incorrect>

I have read many posts where someone felt they were in a gimped class because they were in a group and saw someone from another class absolutely owning everything in site, while they were struggling to survive greens. I have also read many posts where that person thought the other class was overpowered in some way.
