Compiled list of practial BM/MM items:
well, i figgured since i was going to blitz the db to find all the items i can that will boost my BM/MM anyway that i might aswell make a list here. please feel free to add any good ones i miss or ones i put in that are not practical due to drop rates etc... (be kind, it was quarter after 3 in the morning when i finished this).

so in no particular order.....

Neleb's Nano-circuit Robe first and foremost... +8 to all nano skills, looks dead sexy. Drops from Steps of Madness in Omni Forest. requires 231 in all nano skills and lvl 36

Adapted Notum Crystal - Polymorphing I-V Boots just BM. team boss/dyna loot. requires Melee energy. Ql 1-200

Adapted Notum Crystal - Transformation I-V Partner to the Polymorphing crystal. also only needs Melee Energy.

Black Cloak Hood and White Cloak Hood ql 175. Drops off the Unredeemed Prophet and Redeemed Prophet in NE and NW Penumbra respectively, about 75% drop rate (thanks to Sheffy for the drop info)

* Ring for the Support Unit shadowland rings for docs, advs, and mps. adds to several good skills. MAD camping/questing to get

Soft Pepper Pistol found in sealed weapon recipticles. sweet spot at ql 51. can only wear 1 at a time (must go in left hand) requires Pistol and Multi Ranged.

Combat Medic's Light Tank Armor found in Foremans. used to be one of the better back slot armors for low lvl docs untill ToTW and the Exarch Robe.

Exterminator Unit Crepuscule gloves, skinchip and sleeves SL int/psy armor. ql's 1-300 are in database, who knows what ql actually drop, or where? full set of ql300 adds quite a bit.

Fancy Stethoscopic Glasses Drops off of Ris Lee in East Foul Plains (merc mob). no reqs other then lvl. (thanks to Sheffy for drop info)

Felix Crystal I-V doc only crystal, needs piercing and melee energy. team boss/dyna loot.

Notum Ring of the Three and the First, second and third Circle of the Inner Sanctum Found in ToTW and IS respectively. NoDrop so you will have to find your own.

Gaily Painted Hood who DOESNT want one of these? arguably one of the most wanted items for any casting class. very rare drop off of Tara in Camalot.

Galahad Inc T70 Tsuyoshi another buffing pistol similar to the Soft Peppers. only avaliable from ql 99-125. Store buy-able up to ql 125, mission rollable. Requires Pistol, burst and FA. (thanks to Cheapthrill for drop info)

Galahad Inc. Ginger Scout BM/MC buffing rifle. if you are going the X3 way you chould get one (thanks again to Cheapthrill for reminding me of this gun)

nano cloaks (note, only MPs can wear the red) basicly the armored version of Nelebs. from ql 100-150 all buff the same amount (8).

Liquid Notum NCU TC-10 Prototype near insance comp lit reqs could put it out of reach of most players (850 for the lowest ql). comes in ql 160-200, goes in slot 5 and 6 of your NCU belt (where your cpu upgrade and range increaser go)

* Pillow with Important Stripes great starting stat buffer, losses use as the required skills increase. Misson rollable.

Loving Hands drops off of some named mob in the Steps of Madness (cant remember wich one. can someone remind me?). only adds 2 to BM/MM but sometime that is enough.

Massive Cannon Turret THE tower for docs. if you are in an org with land you need one of these towers. Comes in ql40-250.

OAF Pants not exactly atainalbe by everyone... but have great AC's and +10 to BM.

Omni-Med Battle Pants see above

Superior Sentinel Armor Pants dito

Pants of Azure Reveries also a very high lvl item... only +8 BM this time, but more mods to other stats.

Boots of Azure Reveries +8 MM.

Ring of Presence +4 to loads of skills at ql 200.

Silver Spider Knuckledusters from ql100-300, SL item. Boosts MM, good amount for a pair of gloves. Needs Melee energy(?) and intel

Temple Tubes a nice head slot item, buffing all nano skills. found in the catacomes underneeth the spirit rooms in the main dungion in SL. (thanks AGAIN to Sheffy for drop info)

Temporal Chalice dont pass lvl 60 without one! drops from the boss in the middle temple at ToTW. while epuipt you will not be albe to do ANY damage with it, so be sure you are dedicated to being a team doc for this item.

The Expensive Kevlar Vest of Professor Jones between 14 to a whoping 20 to all nano skills. nanobreed only

Unhallowed Chalice the bigger, badder IS cousin of the Temporal Chalice. +15 BM/MM

White Sack drops off of Joo. a rather firey little leet outside Steps of Madness. be sure to get one of these when you are getting your Nelebs.

[edit] fixed Prof Jones link [/edit]


Phulakterion's Seems FC is getting the hint that we need some major nano skill buffs with these bad boys... Pensive Spirit Phulakterion buffs BM/MM/MC while Watchful Spirit Phulakterion buffs TS/PM/SI. Drops off named mobs in the catacombs in Sch and Ely. (thanks to Kayl and Sheffy)

These are tradeskill items - Guide here

Hides drop off many mobs in the Shadowlands but the larger hides can be a pain to find - some confirmed locations are:

Haxxor and Roxxor - two named Shadowleets in Lower Scheol - drop medium and large parts regularly (prob 75% drop) QL100-150
Limping Predator - Northern part of Central Elysium - rarer drop (less then 50%) QL50-80
Named Croakers in Lower Scheol - small hides are common, other hides very rare
Shadowleets in Penumbra - the snow versions in Pen drop medium skins every now and again.

The canisters are not an issue (apart from being NoDrop) drop regularly off many mobs in most Shadowlands zones

The baubles are much rarer but Hiathlins seem to be the best source - found some in Lower Scheol and Eastern Elysium.

Some nice bonuses on these items, armor is NoDrop though
