The fact is that Ninja-looting is griefing of the highest order. Griefing is considered to be bad. FC has a double-standard on this issue mainly because it is difficult to control. A similar issue was debated about for over a year before FC took action. That isseue was the ability of team-members to loot things while you were fighting. Now we have a loot message to keep everyone honest.


Seriously, i would be fully in favor of ninja-looting, chest-looting, uncontrolled PvP etc if this game were truly Anarchy. However, the fact is that you can train mobs on a team one minute, ninja-loot the next, and if you never leave a 75% zone you are perfectly safe to do so. Even worse, you can create ninja-looting characters who's sole purpose is to ninja-loot, thereby never paying the consequences of your actions, socially or otherwise. In essence, ninja-looting is too easy. When it is that easy to take the reward from a group that spent 2 hours trying to earn it you undermine the integrity of the game itself. That's the trouble. Honestly, it is things like this that drive me towards other games. No one wants to spend hours playing and come away with a splitting headache and a fealing of helplessness and frustration.

IMO ninja-looting is really only in an adolescent stage of popularity. Once more people realize how easy (and how anonamous and safe) it can be to do this we will have a horde of ninja-looters at every major kill. At that point people will most likely keep trying until they simply abandon the game for one they can compete fairly in. It is things like these that kill multiplayer games.
