New Club for Tir
Hail and greetings to you Cosmik and all Citizens of Rubi Ka!

Allow me to introduce myself. I am Wyldthing, a level 27 Adventurer of the organization Legion of Ashes. Today I am speaking for the 30+ members of this organization who have noticed the same oversight that I have, and asked for me to express the organizations thoughts in a post.

Recently I went in search of a clan city club at which our organization could gather for role play and meetings. I was thinking of something with a dance floor and bartender, tables and chairs. I wanted something that would add to the feeling of a club and the role play, and also that might draw others to role play with us. What I found disturbed me.

In Tir I found two bars, the Happy Rebel, which is rather small and filled with small rooms, and Enjoy It While It Lasts, which is even smaller and has two women standing in a vat of water doing nothing. So next I went to Athens and Avalon, but found that neither of those cities even had a bar. In West Athens I found The Cup, but again it was a small place, more like a bar.

Omni has Baboons, a great club for gathering, role play and even has its own web page. They also have Rompas another large, club like establishment that attracts a great many role players. The Neutrals have Reets Retreat, an amazing club with several separate bars, rooms and even a glass dance floor in it. The Clans have Nothing. It was very upsetting.

This post is to request that a larger, more desirable club be added to the city of Tir, or that the Happy Rebel be expanded to a multi-roomed club like Baboons or Reets Retreat.

I know there are other Clanners out there who have noticed this oversight and who would enjoy a nice, large club in a Clan city for role play. If you are among those people, please support my and my organizations post with your own posted thoughts and comments.

Thank you very much.
~Phoenx / Wyldthing
