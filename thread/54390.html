Announcement (RK2): Gladiator Day!!!
Announcing the first ever (that I heard of) Gladiator Tournament at the Omni Ent Arena!!!!

This will be PvP with a 1000 cred going to each winner of their bracket. It is an all day event and will have 2 sessions

Early Session: 13:00:00 to about 16:00:00 GMT
Thu Oct 17 2002. Hosted by: Foxdine

Late Session: 01:00:00 to about 04:00:00 GMT
Fri Oct 18 2002. Hosted by: Laytonne

If you would like to be one of the gladiators then send me an email. Laytonne. Please provide your Nickname, Level, Title Level, and tell us what session you want to fight in.

THE ONLY RULE: You cannot have any outside buffs from other people. You can self buff only.

We will be sending out the match list a few days before the tournament. If you have questions feel free to email me

I wish everyone good luck!
