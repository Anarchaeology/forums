Easy to implement temporary fix for the high level blues
I've been reading alot lately about the high level blues, and I won't get into what's been discussed time and time again but I think there is a very simple temporary solution to keep people from quitting the game post level 50-80 - new armor!

Look at the following list of top level armors that start at q75:

Doctor: Biomech
Nanotech: Nano
Martial Artist: Senpai Primus
Agent: Obtru
Engineer: Nano
Adventurer: Elite Omni-Pol
Enforcer: Elite Omni-Pol
Meta-Physicist: Nano
Soldier: Elite Omni-Pol
Fixer: Obtru
Bureaucrat: Nano

Look next at the top level armors that start at q40/50:

Doctor: Links
Nanotech: Bau/Waitt Cyber
Martial Artist: Flowers Tech
Agent: Nadir
Engineer: Links
Adventurer: Omni-Pol/Plasteel
Enforcer: Omni-Pol/Plasteel
Meta-Physicist: Bau/Waitt Cyber
Soldier: Elite Omni-Pol/Plasteel
Fixer: Nadir
Bureaucrat: Bau/Waitt Cyber

Herein lies the problem - most people, including first time characters, are able to with q50 implants at 30, or q70 implants at 50, wear these armors 25 levels before their quality. So at level 50 many characters are wearing the q75 armors, and at level 30 many characters are wearing the q50 armors. What does this mean? Around level 50, most characters have the armor they will be wearing for the next 150 levels. The only real incentive to level at this point is some of the higher level weapons but the "loot" most spend time collecting is a full set of the armor of their choice. Post level 50, its just collecting higher quality levels of these armors.

What I would suggest, and this requires no change to gameplay mechanics but simply new content, is new armor types that are obtainable at q90, q120, q150 and q200. At level 80 my martial artist is wearing q120 armor and I know the game would be alot more fun for me if I had a new type of armor I could wear and had 2 more sets to look forward to.

PLEASE FunCom, consider spending the time to get your artists and game mechanics designers together and rollout some new armor types. Currently variations of Obtru, Biomech, Flowers, Primus, Nano and Omni-Pol Elite are seen on 90% of characters level 50 and up for all professions, which also makes appearance of characters based on their profession kind of hard to differentiate. ONE other idea is to add stat bonuses to armor at the quality level frequencies described above, like %XP, + to nanos, and + to stats that the professions using it need.

I am disappointed in the new bureaucrat suit obtainable in the new dungeon, nelebs robe etc. because these items add some minimal stats that are nice to have but kill the armor class available on other back worn (coats, tank) items. I personally think if you get through this new dungeon for instance you should be rewarded with a suit that adds stats just under a senpai coat at equivilent ql PLUS the bonuses - come on guys, this is a static dungeon reward - it needs to be worth the effort. I cleared steps of madness with a group of 3 q60s by the time it was out and the dungeon was still somewhat tough and time consuming, it should really reward the high end of the level range that the dungeon is targeted at, not the low end (like it currently does).

Anyway - what do you think people? Is this too much to ask? Would this make the game more fun in higher levels while Funcom works on gameplay additions like taking over outposts, etc. in the meantime?
