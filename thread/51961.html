Patk9/Morrizon wins Hope Bar 2v2 Tourney
Thanks to those guys that showed up this Saturday for the 2 vs 2 tourney in Hope, and congrats to Patk9 and Morrizon for placing 1st. The next one will be held this coming Saturday at 7 PM EST in CAV, 1200x1000 (Enigma Forest). The Hope Bar proved to be a nice lag-free locale for the tourney, and I hope CAV will too. This time around I'm also thinking about running a calcutta (team auction) where anyone there can bid on the players and win credits. Let me know what you guys think about that.

RESULTS:

Winners: Patk9 / Morrizon
Award: 33 mill



EDIT: (basic info for the 2v2 tourney)

Place: Hope Bar in Mort. Take the whompa from NLC to Newland Desert, then take the whompa from there to Hope.
Time: this Saturday, 7PM EST. (Let me know if some other time/day is preferable)
1 mil entry fee per person.
money added, amount depending on tournout (23 million was added in the last tourney)
No level/profession restrictions
Outside buffs allowed. (Most likely everyone will be buffing each other there. Thumps up for good sportsmanship )
