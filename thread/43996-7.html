Here's a wishlist...
Apparently, those of you who complain about the cost of this booster pack are out to lunch on a nifty little fact: you don't need to buy the thing! You can still partake in the battles over the towers and all, just you can't build them.

This thing is darn near literally the land control Funcom promised so long ago. For those who don't want to buy, they get land control in the form that was promised, for those that want to buy, they get a lot more.

And a note to people with multiple accounts: you'd only really need to buy one booster as the purchase really only entitles you to tower construction, although the second booster pack would happily enhance the sound and graphics (I think there was mention of graphics).


But as for the land control itself...

What this land control scheme does is add Side versus Side combat. This isn't Player versus Player anymore, it's a whole new ballpark! Think DAoC's RvR when you consider SvS, and the epic scale combat that can occur there, although this may be good memories and bad.

SvS changes the general landscape of Rubi-Ka a lot, so what about the PvP though? Arenas are too small and don't properly provide for sporting between the sides, so perhaps the addition of a battle-simulator is called for to provide a fix for the PvP junkies out there. Adding this as well to the booster pack would make it more than one new card game, but two.

Back to the SvS, though. A few things added to this would be great! Things like, but necessarily being:

Player assigned defend missions:
An organisation enlists the aid of players outside it to help defend their towers and controllers. The missions require a player to be in a certain area for a certain amount of time, or until a certain time check-point is reached, or until the situation is considered 'under control' at which point the hiring entitiy reviews the contracts and decides to award the players that tried to help defend adequately. A neutral under such a contract would be given the ability to attack within a certain range of the defense area. Maybe while in the area titled in the contract, players would gain the benefits the controller provides to it's owning guild.

Giving neutrals the chance to become 'pirates' or 'mercenaries':
Neutrals are people both who want to stay out of the conflict and people who have no strong convictions about either side. Of course, convictions to the almighty credit run rampant through nearly everyone and I'm sure neutrals would love the chance to get in on the attacking bit.

A defense tower information network:
Defense towers request aid from all members of a particular side to assist in their defense and tower owners would have the ability to turn this off and on. Players of course, would have the option of viewing this data.

Larger teams or 'care lists':
Give doctors and and other healers the ability to assist players outside of their team without having to click around to find injured combatants.

Recall towers, doctor ressurection:
Allow slain players a chance to get back into the action with relative speed either through a system that teleports them from a station in a city to a selectable destination, or by allowing doctors to return slain players to life and send them to the nearest reassembly pad and reclaim booth on their side. Why add these things? To prolong the length of fights and even things out a bit. If one side has the tactical advantage of being near a city or outpost or town with a grid exit or whompas or insurance, that point will be very difficult to acquire.

And something, just a little thing, I would LOVE to see added, be it patch or booster pack: an NCU counter and an XP meter to go along with the health and nano bars. I always have my stats screen open and it clutters the screen a lot.



That's it, comment away.
