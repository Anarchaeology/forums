My apologies firstly... not in good mood here.

Nearly every nano is self-castable (except for the nasty ones like Soldier Clip Junkie) when you've got all the good equipment. The idea behind providing a baseline for skills like I've done is to give some idea of the basic pros and cons of a breed. Not everyone will have 2 VT Eggs, an RE Statue, Azure/Omni-AF armour, etc, and secondly, not everyone will follow the same/exact path.

If I tried to make a guide for all the high-end kit and how it relates to a soldier, then I would be spending all my time catching up with patches, and not enjoying the game. Even rings provide a contentious issue... do you pick OT rings, XP rings, Fractured Sanity's, NCU rings? Do you go VT eggs (try finding all the bits), or stick with Intel Kevlar for RI? With CAC's on the way, alot of specific information may become irrelevant with a good ol' Wednesday patch. If I try and use concrete paths of analysis too much, then I would be tricking readers into closing their eyes to the fact that they have to do some work themselves, and that they should always be open to change. You only have to look at the changes to the HUD3 slot to see that by providing specifics could be dangerous (TIM's reversed HUD drop-rate, (E)LLTS going AWOL, V-Scopes and the crit-calc alterations).

People like Boci, Nugget, Garv, Zerosignal, Skymarshal, Sjoko.. come to think of it, virtually every high level soldier has had some form of input whether they know it or not, and many of us have spent masses of time pondering over the effects of breed against a soldiers skills.

Finally... I'm not sure whether to take the, quote: "so be cool to see some real guides". The first version of this document is well over a year old, and I've seen very few other professions that have taken as much time as the soldier community has in helping create this guide. The guide exists not only to try and encourage soldiers to get to 200, but primarily to try and provide soldiers with enough basic knowledge to make their own informed decisions, based on day-to-day mechanics.

Ranged Energy falls off Psychic now.. used to be based on Stam, even tho the text said Psy.

The reason I've stuck with things like the Novaflow Mk4 and E-Beamer is because they're fairly easy to stumble across upto QL125 (you don't want to go any higher than QL40 or 50 in E-Beamers anyway, because the damage curve levels out and the NF4 takes over).

By all means feel free to test out other weapons. The guide is exactly that... a guide. As ARK's are taught when they start, it's not about giving new people fish, it's about teaching them to fish.

Oh, and I firmly believe that crits should be treated as a bonus, and not as a standard. Even when crits were calc'ed better than they are now (and (E)LLTS were about), it was still a fairly even call as to whether to head for speed (i.e. TIM hud) or to an (E)LLTS.

Soldiers rely just as much on speed and good defence, as they do on putting out damage.
