Omni repels clan invasion
April 18 29576

As feared, today brought more bloodshed as several clan and rogue Omni forces attempted to invade Omni cities. A large scale assault led by rogue group SA and several high ranking clan factions was aimed at the very heart of Omni-1. For approx. 20 minutes police battled with the invaders and rebels at the very base of Omni Med in Omni HQ. The attackers were eventually repelled by Omni police, but not before several Omni Supreme commanders where killed in the line of duty. It is unkown how many of the rebels and clanners were killed in the battle, but early estimates indicate heavy losses. It apears that the attack was orchestrated by SA and many high ranking clan members who rallied support during the clan day of celebration, which Omni officials feared would cause just such a civil disturbance.

In other news, it apears that the illegal flow of arms into Omni -1 is increasing, and officials and police are concerned that gunrunners and smugglers are helping escalate crime levels within the city. Police have not uncovered any leads as to where these illegal weapons are being manufactured and how they are being distributed.
