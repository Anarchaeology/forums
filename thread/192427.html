Down with the Mercs, Up with the Bots!
Dabblez, Yiis and Crattyjack storm into the RUR's advanced robotics laboratory, knocking over a pile of empty pizza boxes in the process.

"Okay everyone, listen up." announces Dabblez. "Drop everything you are doing, we need a brand new robot design by Wednesday!" The engineers look up and begin to grumble, but Dabblez continues undeterred. "You tell them Yiis."

Yiis, the finance director, clears her throat and takes a step forward. " We are in trouble. The Neutral Token Board project was, from a financial point of view, a disaster. Project Infinite Circle looks very promising, but it is not going to make us money for a very long time yet. Unless we get a big contract soon, the banks are sure to foreclose."

"And this big contract could potentially be here, just outside our noses." says Cratty. "Guys, take a look out of the window guys. What do you see?"

Gingerly pulling the blinds apart, Gluestick peak outs. "Neuter R Us..a Bronto Burger.. oh.. are we going to work for Bronto Burgers?"

"No, not the BB. I mean the huge atrox. Her name is Loren Warr. Her mercenary company has been stationed here for the last nine months, and she might just be our ticket to save all of our jobs."

"We are going to work for the mercs now?" protests Shadehawk.

"Quite the contrary." replies Dabblez. "What we are going to do is replace them. A lot of residents seem to feel the mercs have outlasted their welcome. Doctor Sizzleen, Naska and I attended a NGI, or Newland Guards Inititive, meeting last week about this very issue. But here's where it gets interesting. One of the ideas suggested for replacing the mercs in Newland City was - wait for it - robots!"

"Hey, I said that!" chimes in Sizzleen.

"Indeed, and The Thinker and Kithrak seemed to be of the same opinion. So, if Newland City is to have robotic guards, why not RUR robotic guards?"

Aulus rasies his hand. "Because we've never made security bots before?"

"Yeah. All our bots fools Asimov's Three laws of Robotics." says Noholdsbarre, then quotes them all with almost religious fervour.
"1. A robot may not injure a human being or, through inaction, allow a human being to come to harm.
"2. A robot must obey orders given it by human beings except where such orders would conflict with the First Law.
"3. A robot must protect its own existence as long as such protection does not conflict with the First or Second Law."

"Not to mention the 4th Law: " adds Gluestick. "All robots will eventually turn on their creators and go on a mad,
murderous rampage regardless of the First, Second or Third Law. "

Dabblez glares dangerously at Gluestick. "There is no 4th Law. It is just a myth." She points emphatically to a large sign hanging on the labs wall which reads, "Remember, there is no 4th Law." in bright, red letters.

Gluestick shrugs, unconvinced.

"Look guys, I know it won't be easy, and I know we haven't got much time. But focus on the positive sides. Our QPT robotic brains are ideal for the more subtle aspects of the job. For instance, a one QPT robots would be able to easily understand the complex concepts of someone "acting su****iously". And we've got the local advantage here. There not many robotics manufacturers based in Newland City."

"Erm.. say we succeed." ponders Gluestick out loud. "Won't the Warrs get, you know, kind of pissed off at us?"

A hushed silence ensues whilst the consider the various scientists and engineers consider the implications.

"Let's cross that bridge if and when we get there. We don't know for sure that the Warrs are going to be replaced. If they do we don't know that they will be replaced with robots, let alone RUR robots. I just know that we have an opportunity here and if we more quickly we might just be able to rescue RUR from the banks and the loan sharks. So call your spouses and order your favourite pizzas, 'cos we are pulling an all nighter and by morning we are going to have designed the damn finest guardbot the world has ever seen!"

And all at once lab explodes in the chant "Down with the Mercs! Up with the Bots! Down with the Mercs, Up with the Bots!"

A week later, the employees of RUR gather in the cafeteria to view a sneak preview of the promotional holo-vid for their new guard bot. Crattyjack nervously regards the expectant crowd while Dabblez, on down on her knees fiddles with the faulty holo-projector.

At long last she announces "I think if got it." Getting up on her feet she adds, "Can we kill the lights please?"

As soon as the lights go off, a column of bright, white light shoots out from the projector and up to the ceiling. Almost instantly a whole range of shadow hand puppets of leets, bunnies and snakes appear in the projection area amidst the stifled giggles of the RUR engineers.

"Oh grow up." complains Dabblez. Then to Crattyjack. "You can being when you are ready."

Crattyjack swallows hard and then steps in font of the gathered audience. Cucumber, carefully positioning her hands in the air manages to get a pair of shadow horns to appear over Cratty's head, drawing further snickers from her colleagues.

"Hello dudes and dudettes of RUR." Crattyjack starts. "Thanks for coming. In a minute I'll be showing you the promotional vid for our new guardbot, the one we hope will one day be used in place of Warr's mercs to protect Newland City. Now I understand our new guardbot design hasn't really be finalised yet and that you are having a few minor glitches here and there."

"Minor glitches? Our last guard bot prototype is currently out there, building an ark by Newland Lake and collection two specimens of every animal. You call that a minor glitch?" protest Aulus.

"Erm.. I am sure that is something you boys can resolve in no time." Nervously loosening his tie, he continues. "But of course, building the bot is only half the battle, we need to sell them too. Let Joe Public know about our products and learn to trust the RUR brand name. And as we are facing stiff competition from such notable firms as Circle G, " Cratty pauses for emphasis. "So we have to get in their fast, and dazzle the Newland City Council with the sheer brilliance of our proposal. So, with no further ado, I give you GuardMaster 900 holovid!"

The RUR logo appears on the holo-projectors display area, followed by the sound of a fanfare of trumpets and a deep, masculine voice-over.

"Welcome to the future of law enforcement.
"Part robot....
"Part vacuum cleaner...
"All cop.
"It's the RUR model ST49 GuardMaster robot. "

The image of a plain brick wall appears on the holo-projection. Suddenly, the GuardMaster bursts through the wall. The camera pans to the robots head as it says "Greetings citizens. Stay our of trouble."

The voice-over continues. "Tough as steel, gentle as a cool breeze and always 100% alert, this is one cop you won't find sleeping on the job! "The GuardMaster is unstoppable, incorruptible and indefatigable. Its prime directive is simple: to serve, to protect and to kick butt. "

The image then switches to a laboratory. The GuardMaster sits on an examination table. Two smiling RUR technicians in white lab coats remove the robots chest plate to reveal the content.

"The GuardMaster is powered by a 64 RD QPT processor, new Bacontronics sniffer technology, single elementary
Dr Watson alert system, advanced Holy Batcave, multi-spectrum sensory array, twin rotating Dun-Kin v.1.8.2 do-nut breaks.

"So what are you waiting for? You order your RUR ST49 GuardMaster today."

The GuardMaster adds "You have 20 seconds to comply... "

The hologram fades and Dabblez switches the light back on. "So what do you think?"

The RUR employees respond all at once.

"That looks nothing like the bot we are working on."

"GuardMaster? I thought we were calling it Guard-o-Matic?"

"No, it was going to be called MegaCop!"

"MegaCop is a comic book character. We can't use that name."

"Right, as if the phrase 'The Future of Law Enforcement' isn't a copyright infringement."

Dabblez sighs softly. This was going to be a long meeting.
