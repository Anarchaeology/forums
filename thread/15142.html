Sea of Change Looking for members RK2 RP
Sea of Change is a focused group with very direct goals. We seek to bring the notice our second dimension deserves, to let free voices to be heard through all of Rubi-Ka, and to unify the public in making Rubi-Ka 2 active. We are looking for people who wish to make a difference on Rubi-Ka, people who seek an experience beyond normal day to day hunting parties, or item hording. We are a very different group of free thinking individuals, and we have many personalities within our ranks. Those who seek membership will be interviewed and given the chance to ask any questions they may have.

A movement is forming to take Rubi-Ka 2 back. We lead a force who will give Rubi-Ka 2 back to the people, those who deserve it most.

OOC:
We are looking for active Roleplayers on RK2, who wish to rolaplay all the time. We want to make our mark on the story in any way possible, through events, and our own determination. We want to give Rubi-Ka the notice it deserves in the press and in the game. We won't allow RK2 to be overlooked again.

If you want to be a part of us, please email serrephim@hotmail.com or send an ingame message to "Sepentura"
