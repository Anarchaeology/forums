Overequip fix:

Item QL = True QL * ( Skill/Stat value divided by Requirement)

Bad thing, this adds a lot of extra procedures (computing power needed), as it has to be checked at debuffs, buffs running out, heck in so many different situations, for every piece of equipment...




What the simple line means in a concrete example:

You uber-equip *smirk* a ql 100 rifle with the huge amounts of buffs (+rifle, +riflegeneric, +aimedshot, eagle eye, trader). However, once all those silly buffs run out, you end up with (fictional numbers):

Item QL = True QL (100) * ( 400rifle skill / 600 rifle req) = 66QL efficiency rifle.

So instead of using stats of a QL 100 rifle, the weapon would use the stats of QL 66 rifle of same type. Same goes for armors and implants. Easy, simple, but a lot of extra computing power taken... but would be worth the computing power. And oops, now traders would be really wanted in groups for their wrangles _at combat time_ too. Darn, that will just suck for traders? (A trader wrangling and giving effective +20QL on your uber-equipped weapons... THAT is what wranglers were probably meant for, not buff-once-and-forget type thing.)

And most importantly, it would not make uber-equipping TOTALLY useless. It would make uber-equipping still very valuable _if you play in groups and buff up_. It would also actually make uber-equipping help casual gamers too, muah-aha-hahaa!

How? Well, you'd only need to upgrade your equipment once in a while, since you could go high and uber-equip stuff with buff-o-mania, but your fighting statistics would ultimately give the "QL" of them. So you uber-equip, then you can play casually and ignore your equipment until you exceed the stat/skill requirements on armor/weapons/implants! Instead of what is now, that you uber-equip but you can uber-equip even more uber-equip every level... so you should re-equip often to be totally uber. Uber this and that, uber uber uber... ops I lost it.

On what req to compare it, for weapons it would naturally be the skill the weapon uses, for armor a weighed average (60% dependant on higher stat and 40% dependant on lower stat), and for implants the _base ability_ score (unbuffed value), not the treatment skill of course. You start doing the checks from implants, so their +skills and +stats effect armor/weapon.

Something that gets missed
An overequipped character can barely solo the game on easy mobs at mid to high levels. A same-ql character has an almost-impossible time in missions starting at mid levels, and an underequipped character gets slaughtered.

SO. Nerfing character effectiveness any more will simply frustrate players more, and drive many new ones away. MANY people like to solo, at least some of the time, and this is rapidly being taken out as an option, because the game is seen as being too easy grouped.

Now, most people don't really care what QL gun or QL armor they are wearing. As long as they can beat yellows and slighty-oranges, they will be content (after all, we ARE the heroes, right?). Every class should be able to solo a mission with yellow mobs, right til the end of the game, as long as they have "decent gear". The difficulty should be challenging, but not frustrating.

Partied, people want to be able to take out much harder mobs. And, I don't think there's anything wrong with this -- what's the point of having 5 of your friends with you, if you can't kill things substantially tougher? It's all part of the challenge/risk/reward balance.

Now about overequipping in PvP: At 75, the PvP level cap is removed anyhow, so, honestly, who cares if you're overequipped. Level 75-85 people die like pigs to level 150+ people (all they see is "easy kill" tatooed accross the forehead). It is incredibly rare to find a fair fight -- 99% of all pvp deaths are predetermined, cheap kills. In any case, at level 120+, you start running out of things to overequip anyhow, and it all starts to even out.
