The mistake every game makes
The mistake every game makes eventually is that they cater to those who play 16 hours a day, 7 days a week, 4 weeks a month etc. These people get uber and the gaming company goes "oh, we need to make it harder". They make it harder. The same people who seem to play the game for a living, continue to stay uber. Do these gaming companies realise that nothing they do will actually affect these people? That by catering to those who live the game, they neglect the majority who have jobs/school/etc? From my experience in every major on line game, the answer is no.

When it comes down to it, what exactly is so bad about having great items being used by every player? In a game that existed for player verse player conflict, yes, it is bad. Anyone who thinks Anarchy Online is such a game is lying. I'm not going to tell people what Anarchy is for them, except to say that this is not a player vs player game. Maybe that's what was intended, but the system that's in place to do such a thing is utter rubbish.

Most players, I think, just want to have a good time. Camping anything is boring. It eats up time. This affects those who don't have hours and hours to sit in front of their computer. AO went in the right direction with health kits/insurance terminals/nano chargers. Those brownie points mean nothing if you turn around and institute camping.

So heres the deal. You, Funcom, don't want every player to have those cool items. Your solution: Create a system where 98% of the playerbase will never have the oppurtunity to even attempt to try to obtain the items. The other 2% will repeatedly get the items. This creates a huge market for said items with huge inflationary prices. If the 98% of the player base wants said items, they must now buy them instead of having fun trying to get them themselves.

I won't even go into alternatives to this sytem. There's about 200 previous posts that have already explained them in preschool english.

Not that this matter will probably ever affect me. I just recently started playing AO, and love it for now because of the almost complete lack of down time. If I ever reach a level where I find myself camping spawns, I'm off for Yahoo chess, where I won't have to wait 6 hours to not get a chance to have some fun.
