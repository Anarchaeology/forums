My experiences with my (rediscovered) Ith is that it's not at all that bad in pvp. Just the other night I managed to kill off a lvl 200 trader with it. It feels bad having half crits in the beginning, but with a decent pool on full deff and against a non-shutdown class like MP or trader you should be ok. That I can't heal greys, fine, but the fact that I can't heal a lot of greens at tower battles with me is confusing and very frustrating. AoE heals sound pretty sweet, but need to be balanced to not nullify the AoE nukes that, for instance, Ian Warr or pest throws at ya. For instance, very short range (10 - 15 meters with a 5 meter radius), otherwise 4 docs spamming AoE heals w/ 4 docs spamming CH would seriously take the challenge out of these fights. Imo our dots should tick twice as fast but maybe for only half the duration to make em useful. Also a cost reduction could be nice, either that or both lines should be at 100% or less NR.

There has been a few comments on PvP heal ranges. Wanting to increase the heal range, maybe abolish the limiter completely.

NO!

NO!

NO!

That's what the situation was when NW was launched. They fixed it for good reason.

I once took my (then) lvl 30ish enf to a tower site for a grudge match. The target was about the same. Problem was, there was a 172 doc present. Every few seconds, POOF. All my efforts eradicated. That was not fun at all. Granted it's an extremish example, but it's also exactly why the limiter was put in.

Also: No one else can buff friends outside PvP range in tower battles. There is NO valid reason why docs should be excempt from this rule.

Of course, should the PvP range be increased to match the team range, the heals would have to follow to match. This might be a good idea. But healing outside PvP range? NO! It's WAY too unbalancing. Might as well let those 200 enfs loose on the 120's. (As has been suggested for heal range.) Anyone heard of minced meat? Just as fair. The whole point here is that if you want to heal someone, you have to share the risk. Healing outside PvP range would be completely risk free, since the opposition will have no way of countering your efforts. (No. I wouldn't be able to shout for higher-level assistance to take out those big nasty healers, because I'm in a small guild and am currently among the top ranked people there. Not everyone have a huge guild full of power-players who camp uniques all day. In fact, most don't.)

Personally, I'd want the PvP ranges more restrictive, not less, at high levels to make things more even.

Allright this might seem a bit harsh, but it's intended to be, since some of the previously stated opinions were in sore need of a counterpoint.
