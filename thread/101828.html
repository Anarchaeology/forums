To my love.
To my love:

I remember the smell of that first forever night.
It was apples, cinnamon,
Sugar, and ****e.
The things girls are made of.
Everything nice.

That scent awoke me
It stirred me inside
Things came from my mouth
I could no longer hide

The days slowly pass
as I slip further into darkness
Caught as a blue ghost
Cold in form and heartless

Lay my life into the grave
That I soon may find another
Now I say goodbye
To my one true lover

Fixerben pulls his hood over his head as he turns and disappears into the darkness...
