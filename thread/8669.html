Clan Heinlein meeting Jan 27
Clan Heinlein is having it's first meeting this Sunday, January 27 at 2000 UTC (that's 12 noon PST, 3 pm EST, and 2100 CET). The meeting will be at Enjoy It While It Lasts, the nightclub just east of the Council Building in Tir. Punch and pie.

Attendance is voluntary! This will just be an informal get-together to meet and get to know each other, ask questions, et cetera. I will have details by then about clan ranks and so forth.

Non-members who would like to meet us and ask questions are welcome. Clan Heinlein is open to all breeds, professions and levels.
