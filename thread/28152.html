Is it the game? Or is it the people?
As a "new'er" player to the game (only 2+ months), I am quite disappointed to say, that typically it is not the actual game mechanics itself that really bring this game down from where it should be. But typically the people.

Don't get me wrong, I have died to bugs, died because pets get stuck, died because I cast "calm" and it says it took and it doesn't(5x in a row), died because I have fallen through the floor in a mission etc etc, yada yada... Same as probably all of you have at one point or another.

This patch (IMO) will fix some of the issues within the game. Some of them are NOT going to help some of my toons at all, and will really change how I used to play them. Like the trader heal changes. Not one I am looking forward to. However, there are reasons behind the changes. Do I have to agree with them? No. After all it is their game, I am choosing to play. I don't have to. I can however, supply positive feedback on the boards about what I think. Its hard enough to see positives ideas that people have, when you have to go through all the flames and slams.

But the part I wish they could patch, is some of the people. Yes there are great people out there, but unfortunately not enough of them. My wife I and started together. I remain, she has left. She did not leave because of the game "problems", but rather the

1-Asking a question in OOC/area chat, politely of course, and getting ignored.

2-Helping someone who needed it. Like a heal or help on a monster if they are about to die. And they just walk away.

3-Asking her for a buff, and again, the wonderful ignore.

4-How about helping that person out who needed 3k for that one nano they just had to get, without a thank you.

5-And of course, the "better than thou" people. You know the ones, when you play your MA they ignore you, when you play your trader, they are sending you the tells asking for help

Sadly I am fully expecting to get "flamed". I have a thick skin, so say what you want. However if my wife left, as others have before and will in the future, perhaps think about it. The person is going to leave, might have been an awesome friend/guildmate/grouper etc.

I only hope that one person out there reads this and actually trys to, maybe be more helpful. Or be polite. Or remember that when someone talks they are REAL people, with real feelings behind the keyboards. Whether its in game or on these boards. If you wouldn't say it to their face in RL, then why say it?

Even more sadly, is I let my wife read this. And her response was "People will not change.." I hope she is wrong. I hope people remember that this on line "social" game. A chance to have some fun and meet other people from around the world. To get together and kill the bad guys...

At any rate,
Have a nice day.
