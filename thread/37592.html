Answers to Professional Report
Here are some answers that we got to our report, please keep in mind that this report was written before 14.4 was released and some issues no longer apply.

quote:
Bugs

Grid Armor
When a fixer is critically hit while wearing grid armor, they are hit for more damage than they would take if they were hit while wearing nothing at all. Grid Armor has a bug and when it occurs it will cause the user to take critical damage + about 28%. As a result of our testing, we conclude that Grid Armor reacts poorly against any melee mob or mob which uses melee damage if and when the bug occurs. When this bug occurs, it is not uncommon for the subject to be hit for the same critical damage until they are dead. Additionally, the bug most commonly occurs when fighting a melee mob, although it does occur while fighting projectile and chemical mobs, just not as often. Agro / Def does not effect the bug one way or the other, it is 100% the same full agro or full def.

100% of Special Attacks and Nano based attacks get through Grid Armor defenses and cause 40% damage if said weapon/special attack is capable. There is no variation. If the attack is capable of doing 40%, it does 40% of life. Although Nano Resistance has been adjusted as recently as 14.2, nukes seem to have not been effected. This probably stems from the fact that the NTs casting these nukes will have a very high attack rating, from their high nano skills. Unfortunately, it is near impossible for even a lvl 200 fixer to combat the casting skill of an NT, even with Grid Armor MKIV. This data may be inconclusive due to any unknown variables in the Nano Resistance system, or planned future changes to it.




Grid Armor was given a good overhall in 14.4 and is much more effective now. Also, the problem with Grid Armor causing MOBs to gain an unusal amount of crits should have been fixed in 14.4. With these 2 changes in mind, let me know how Grid Armor now stacks up post 14.4.


quote:
There is also a bug with grid armor regarding what happens when the armor �expires�. If you do not delete grid armor or remove it before it times out on you, you have to zone or relog to put another set on.




I'm glad to say that when I went to talk to PQA about this, they were actually testing it at that exact minute. It's being looked at and fixed


quote:
Cluster Bullets
Cluster bullets sometimes moves the health bar of the target the wrong way, so it looks like it's healing the mob. Da Taunter can also be seen doing this on occasion.




This appears to be a synch error, rather than the cluster bullets actually moving the health bar. It has been reported and will be addressed.


quote:
Snares
Sometimes, when a snare is cast, the target seems to move at the exact same speed. This seems to happen if the target is already moving when the snare is cast.




This is a bug and is currently with PQA for testing.


quote:
Grid Nanos
When the caster has 1001 nano initiative or higher, the fast versions (6 seconds casting time or lower) of the �grid phreak� begin acting strangely. With the self version, the caster will have their Health and Nano reduced to 1, but they will remain in the same place. With the team version, the same thing will happen, but the rest of the team will be transported into the grid.




This has been reported and will be addressed. An odd issue, indeed.


quote:
Balance Issues

Cluster Bullets
Cluster bullets are not usable on hybrid (ie: Submachine/Assault Rifle) guns, and this weakens their usefulness severely. When dual wielding a pure Submachine Gun and a different kind of gun, this attack is unusable. If it will be permanently limited by the use of a gun requiring mg/smg and burst, we respectfully request it be limited to any and all guns requiring mg/smg and burst, including hybrid weapons.




No, cluster bullets will not be able to be used with SMG hybrid weapons. They are intended to boost the attractiveness of pure SMG weapons. I will continue look at what we can do about the problem if you a dual wielding, however.


quote:
The cluster bullets are also not a reliable means of outputting damage. This special attack should be tightened down to hold a much higher maximum and average damage. One subject tested was a level 196 Fixer using a QL 200 Area Burst and a QL 200 Suppressor. Damage ranged from 73 to 800 with 800 damage occurring 3% of the time out of 400 attempts and the average damage being 260.73333.




Cluster bullets are meant to be used as additional damage, and to fill the Fixer void where area effect damage is needed. The damage is not intended to be constant, predictable damage. I would be interested to hear how much of the damage was in the top 75% of the possible damage though - only listing the times top damage was reached misses out on listing 790 damage, for example, which is still great. I'll look into the average damage further with the weapon designer.


quote:
Grid Nanos
Fixer emergency escape nano�s do not perform intended function. Because fixer emergency escape nano�s do not perge hostile programs in NCU, the Fixer is gambling by using this nano.

For example; A Fixer who is taking damage from a nano such as a DoT and is rooted by a mob. This Fixer is near death, has substantial experience unsaved and is in a mission. If they use the emergency escape nano, they are stuck standing up in the grid and taking damage from a damage over time nano. They die anyway.

If this is to be, we suggest adding one simple function to the line. Utilization of single or team versions should immediately and without exception purge 100% of all hostile nano�s from the Fixer�s (and ALL team members) NCU Memory. This makes it worthwhile to sit in the grid and recharge life and nano. It also makes it more reliable as a �savior� nano line, which seems to be its intention in the first place.




For the fast Grid transportation time out of a dangerous situations (the exact situation where evac Grid nanos are intended to be used), the evac Grid nanos are meant to, and will continue to, have the health modifier. In situation that seems hopeless, these nanos are designed as a last resort, and they will get you out of any place faster than any other means - but they do have the disadvantage if a DoT is on the Fixer. This will not change.


quote:
Grid Armor
Fixer Grid Armor does not allow the equipping of items on the wrist or shoulders. Sought after items such as the Virral Egg, Dodga/Alvin Shoulder Pads, and all bracers are completely unusable while wearing grid armor. These items are widely used in both PvM and PvP, and denying them to fixers in grid armor definitely puts us at a disadvantage. Removing the shoulder/wrist restrictions would help to bring them to the same level as other players.




That is because Grid Armor is classed as social armor - so combat armor cannot be worn with it. This is due to the modifiers of Grid Armor - the modifiers gained are very good, and the added modifiers from other items should not be, and are not intended to be, needed. Please, do let me know how Grid Armor fairs with other professions wearing normal armor/items, or even Fixers wearing normal armor. But, as said, it is intended for the Fixer to be able to rely on Grid Armor itself as a means of protection, and not need other defensive equipment.

------

Well folks, that was our report and there are Funcom's responses. Give us feedback to give back to them if you like, or you can ask questions regarding post 14.4 issues. Thanks.

Ok, so nobody advertises they like the level requirements. Lets consider why FC wants them: to prevent lower level characters from giving themselves unfair advantages over people their level in both PvP and PvM (I'd bet PvP moreso). For example, our NCU buff has potential to be majorly unbalancing, if a Level 1 character were allowed to have an extra 250 NCUs. Can you say twinkage?

So, the level req was included to bring some sort of cap to the twinkage. Well, the problem with the req is that FC has chosen the spacing prematurely and as a result people get their nose out of joint. Some people (like me) focused on nano skills and have basically left themselves set until I turn 195. I don't like that I can't cast the nano, but I understand FC's intent on preventing me from twinking royally. As soon as you can get 150 NCU, you can slap 2 mochams and a wrangle on and OE a gun to insane levels, if there were no level req - that can be potentally an extra +291 in two nanoskills. At 493 in skill, 784 in a skill - and hence the largest SMG buff can be put on. this may be really cool sounding, but lets figure how easy it is to get 493. lets say at least 100 of that is implanted, and about 60 points of that are base (based on ability scores only), that means only an investment of 333 needs to be made. I don't know about you, but I had that invested in two nanoskills by about 84. Now a level 84 character could potentally use the top of the line nanos without a level req. While thats relatively cool for the level 84 fixer, its extremely play imbalancing to the other classes, and relatively imbalancing to the other fixers (higher level) as well.

Perhaps a more apropriate basis for nano requirements would be title level. In otherwords the 141 buff goes to TL6, the previous two go to TL5... this would mean the nanoskills are still dispersed sparingly, however there is an advantage for some to invest in nanoskills at a rate which they choose (to use the higher buff as soon as they hit title), or find an MP to cast the second tier of nanos at your title level... A level 150 char using MP buffs to cast a 135 SMG buff, a 200 point NCU buff, or a better team runbuff sounds pretty reasonable to me.

I do see the advantage of spacing out things to Title level 6, but making 'the man' wait to cast his top buffs is just stupid - generally he'll find you a nice set of shoes for your swim trip to the stret.
