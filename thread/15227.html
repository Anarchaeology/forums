Overpowered Drill Inspector mob
I was with a group doing a mission in EFP this weekend and we got a QL152 mission that had these red robot mobs called Drill Inspectors ("inspector" might not be correct). They seemed very overpowered for their xp reward. Other red mobs we could handle fairly well as a group were giving us 20-30k exp each, but these drill mobs took everything we had to kill and only gave us 14k exp each. They seem way overpowered for their experience reward. Has anyone else fought these things? They sort of look like robot versions of the old spiderman villian "Doc Octopus".
