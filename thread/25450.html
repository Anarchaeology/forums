I think one of the ideas of team missions is that it's supposed to be difficult. It wouldn't make very good business sense for Funcom to allow players to reach the highest token board after just a "few weeks" of playing. I've done some team missions and they don't seem too terribly difficult. What did you have the difficulty slider at? If you set it anything higher than the middle, you will have to expect it to be much harder. Yes, the boss mobs have a lot of HP, but hey...they're BOSS's. They shouldn't be a walk in the park to take down.

I would suspect the reason you could only convince 3 out of 200 groups to do a team mission isn't because these missions are too hard. Most likely it was due to the following:

1.) Your team was already hunting and making exp. Why would they want to stop, go to a mission terminal, then make a run to a mission?

2.) Missions involve making a commitment. All team members may not want to commit themselves to a couple of hours doing the mission.

3.) Missions are slower than hunting for exp gain. Many people just play to make as much exp as fast as possible. Missions don't help them do that.
