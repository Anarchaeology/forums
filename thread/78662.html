Garishna, the majority of the atrox population was created by the corporate giant known as Omni-Tek for the purpose of handling heavy duty tasks in the OT mines. A special blend of lab made genetics and the original human genome has created the atrox.
Im not to enlightened on the subject of Atrox origin, but I do know that there are several "home made" atrox's, people that got ahold of one of the machines.

Im pretty sure that to this date, most of the atrox population has been created, not born out of a womb like the other 3 species that populate Rubi-Ka (although Opifex's, Nanotechnicians, and even Solitus' have been created by these "machines", they are capable of breeding and repopulation, while Atrox's are not, at least to my knowledge).

Hope this helps somewhat.
