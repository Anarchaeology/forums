The Origins of White Trash
I thought I might write a little about the origins of the drug of "White Trash" given that it got a mention in the official news and a lot of players seem to have heard about it. I've sort of put this in a reply on the Story forum, but in a way it fits here better.

Simply put "White Trash" is a drug I created out of thin air for an adventure the South Rubi-Ka Society started playing back in April. There was nothing official about it; just a small roleplaying guild doing it's thing. There is a somewhat confused write up of the adventure at this link if anyone is interested.

http://rubika.no-ip.org/srs/srsadvent.htm


Somehow or other (maybe through the adventure write ups posted on this site or through in character converstaions with some of the players involved in this adventure) the name "White Trash" left our little circle and made its way in the big wide world.

In one way is pretty cool seeing our little plot device making the big time. On the other hand it does cause me a little headache. In our above mentioned adventure (of which the final chapter still has to be played out) "White Trash" is a very specific drug created for a very specific purpose. It is only found among the dealers in Omni-Sewers and among it's side effects is rapid, premature ageing. I really can't add more without blowing the story, but it is not just another drug. People have died for just talking about "White Trash".

I suspect this is not how most people who have head about "White Trash" think about it. Still one must be practical about things. I appreciate "White Trash" might just be bigger than the South Rubi-Ka Society as things stand (!) and I am not going to try take back something which has slipped into the public domain. When I do get back to running the final chapter of this adventure, I'll figure out a way to reconcile to two versions of "White Trash" about; reconciling inconsistencies is what p&p GMs do best.

Still I guess this goes to show just how hungry roleplayers are for any details about the setting.
