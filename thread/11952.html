How do YOU Roleplay on Rubi-Ka?
Just a couple questions for the masses. I find that recently, and somewhat often, I'm online when none of my friends are, and I'm just DESPERATE to do some RP.

I have several working methods of approaching strangers with RP situations, and some of them work, some of them don't, it all depends on the timing, really.

The question:

If you're online when none of your usual RP group is on, how do you roleplay? What is your approach? Setting/timing?

If you are in an RP mood, how would you most like to be approached from someone else on the hunt for RPers?

Well, when I approach someone to roleplay I simply walk up to them with a simple Hello, how are you. Before hand I think of something that I want to RP out, like a situation...for example I might tell them of a recent battle I was a part of, or a story about how I came to Rubi-Ka. That usually strikes up a good RP mood.

Well I would like to be approached in any way possible to tell the truth. I love RP, and do it even if no one else is...So anything works for me if I am approached by a stranger.

If the people you appoach do not RP, don't worry about it, continue to speak In Character to them, look at it as a challenge to you ability to stay In Character. With your RPing you can draw nearby people in to it also.

"If you're online when none of your usual RP group is on, how do you roleplay? What is your approach? Setting/timing? "

I don't actually like the clubs too much. They are a little too sleazy for my tastes and can be a bit intimidating when I am on my own. Besides the roleplaying tends to slip a lot into plain ooc socialising, which is fine if that is what you are after.

My favourite spots to just hang out are parks (Rome in particular) and the little open air cafe in Newland. If I hang out in one of the those spots long enough in street clothes and wielding nothing more dangerous than an apple or a drink, it's not uncommon that I end up striking up a conversation with someone there.

I think the real challenge not so much meeting roleplayers but moving beyond the casual conversation and into a more sustained roleplaying environment. Unfortunately roleplayers face a peculiar dilemma; we can't just approach someone saying "Hey we I guess we are both roleplayers, let's roleplay together a bit!" it just doesn't work that way. The conversation eventually dries, then what, go hunting? Why? What if you are radically different level? And a few days after, when you see this person online again, on what pretext do you then contact them again? Is that really what you're character would do or are you just fudging it because roleplayers are few and far between?

It's not simple.

quote:
Originally posted by ODieLag
to tell you the truth, I don't know how to roleplay.
Roleplaying is not something that only a few crazy people do.
That's really simple to roleplay in an environnement as Rubi-ka.. it will takes time to be really in your role tough.


In my opinion, Roleplaying isn't:

- going to a bar and talk to everybody
- wear social clothes
- decide you don't want to team with the opposite faction
- a loss of time you could have spent levelling

Then what is Roleplaying?

Roleplaying in my opinion is to know Who You Are.
.. I mean to know who your character really is, what's his/her history, his/her sensitivity, his/her emotions, fears, his/her objectives, ...

The servers will go down 3 hours today.. If you don't have anything else to do in "Real Life", just go to your character biography page, look at your picture.. and Imagine:

- Who are you?
- Who were your parents
- Do u have brothers or sisters?
(Btw, my Real name is Gloria Barlau, only friends call me Amanite.. I'm looking for more people named Barlau to see if we are linked in any way)

- How old is your char?
- Is your char subjects to somes fears, or particular emotions?
( I hate all that looks like a spider and run when I see one.. I cannot even fight! - I am for peace and will never attack first - )

- Do u have some desires? Some way of life?
(I depend of my friends --> I never solo, I never ask anything, but I'm always happy when someone give me and old piece of armor or and old Ncus.. they know that and they help me.. I never sell anything to people I know. I give all my possessions to my needing friends. I don't have money, but I don't want money as I don't know what to do with it... And lots more!)
(My only real desire is to be known as a politician that brought peace to Rubi-KA.. still a lot of works hehe)

- Why are you on Rubi-Ka?
-Why are you in this particular side of the conflict?
- How much involved are you in this conflict?
( as u can see, I'm not totally reformed by Omni-Tek, even if they control me)
- What are your feelings for the other side?

- Why are you in your particular profession?
- Are you a good engineer/ma/crat/... or an excellent one.. or a bad one? Is that important for you?

- Why have you chosen your race? Is that important to you?
What are your feelings for the other races?
(Nanomage scare me a bit..)

- Do u have any passions?

..... There are really more questions than those, but if you can answer to at least 3 of those.. and decide to stick to your answers in alln you're doing, that's great!
The best thing to do is to write a little history of your char on a paper and read it sometimes..


Then:

If you are a social character whose only desire is to meet people, or if you are an illegal merchant, or only someone who needs to have a drink, you can

- go to a bar and talk to everybody

If your appearance is important to you, if you like the fact that people recognize you in the crowd,

- wear social clothes

If you don't want to be seen, if you're a secret agent, ...
wear the same coat as everyone..

If your brother is currently in Omni-Tek Jail, if your husband has been captured by the rebels, if you really don't like the way the neutrals think:

- decide you don't want to team with the opposite faction

In the other way, if you are for peace, if you want to meet more people, if you want to be a spy,...
Don't hesitate to team and talk with everyone!

If your only objective in your life is to become the most powerful enforcer of Rubi- Ka, if you don't like to talk, if you want to be strong Nt to be able to kill all those spiders on rubi-ka.

-Then maybe your only way of Roleplaying will be to level

If you want to be known by people who are not necessary of your levels, if you want to be recognized, if the important thing for you is having fun in bars, or swimming in pool with friends, or dance contests, or conventions, or contests, or.....

Then Roleplaying will never be

- a loss of time you could have spent levelling

But Levelling will become

- a loss of time you could have spent living with your character..

Amanite
Omni-Tek Bureaucrat

Edited to add this:
This is not a "bible" of roleplaying or anything else.. I'm not a "Power-roleplayer" but I think that 1 hour spent thinking about who your char is can really improve how you will enjoy your life on Rubi-Ka.
It is only a personnal opinion.. and there are certainly roleplayers who will have another opinion of what roleplay is.
Listen to them and make you a personnal opinion too
