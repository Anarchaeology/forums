evades do work
Evades do work, BUT it is only effective against lowbies.

The reason evades sucks is because of this one fact:

If ATK rating equals evades then percent chance to hit is 75%.

Given this and the fact that ATK is a LOT easier to raise than evade (attack being lighter than evades to most professions and easier to implant and wrangle), ATK are never equal to evades for equal level players, but a LOT higher. Thus making evades appear to be broken.

Evades do work, just not very well. If Funcom wants to extend the length of battles then make ATK equal evades = 50% chance to hit and evades will be much more effective.
