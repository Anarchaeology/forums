Items who's time has come:
Here's some inspirational items that should be made:

Notum Regeneration Optumizer: CPU upgrade, ups NanoDelta.

Cellular Rebuilder Optumizer: CPU, or maybe even shoulder armor, that improves HealthDelta.

Robot/Droid Tactical Combattive Evaluation/Upgrade: Tactical CPU Upgrade: improves robot init/damage.

Master Lockpick Set: Requires Break&Entry, but has a MUCH higher chance of unlocking AND disarming traps at the same time.

Improved Android/Manifestation/Droid Self-Repair: Increases pet HealthDelta

Some more Soldier Nanos, any type.

Pet Control: Mind Switch. Switches player control DIRECLTY to his/her pet, while leaving the master unguarded/unprotected/frozen in place. Meanwhile, the player controls the pet.

DEFINATELY more frequent healing nanos for Adventurers. Poor guys get stuck with HUGE gaps between heals.

Armor construction (with unique types of armor).

Hopefully soon, you'll increase max QL of armor/weapons sold in shops. QL120+ equipment is starting to show up more and more frequently.

More class-specific and breed-specific gear.
