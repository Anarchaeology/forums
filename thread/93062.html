Response to Today's news
Reply to News submitted by Benjacrat this morning...

In a press release today from Philip Ross, and the infamous mercenary Loren Warr, they claimed that:

“For months, Clanners have been relentlessly attacking neutral notum mining bases; Omni-Tek does not condone that. That must be crystal clear.”

I have seen more Omni attacking Neutral Bases, and even other Neutrals attacking other Neutrals. This demonstrates to be a point that I have been tring to make for months and months. Since I arrived on this planet I have seen nothing but highly skilled killers running around yelling "Use your new gun on a Clanner today!" The point is that when the ICC deregulated the mining of Notum it blurred factional boundaries greatly.

I am a founding member of an oranization of Inter-factional Non-agression pacts. The only Interfactional organization I have ever seen on this planet. I personally am not in a guild. I gain no profit from this organization. I am in it for the peace it brings to its members. The comfort of knowing that certain people will not attack you. Basically its an organization that will limit your enemies on the field of battle.

Some examples of organizations already in the RMC are as follows:
- The Independant Rubi-Kans (Neutral)
- Izgimmer's Legacy (Omni)
- Whisper's Edge (Clan)

As you can see from this small example the RMC(Rubi-Kan Mining Cooperative) is truly interfactional. If your membershipis accepted into the RMC you will enjoy peace with whichever organizations are current members. There is no requirement to aid other members if they come under attack, but it is always each guilds option to offer aid to one another.

Organizations such as the RMC I see as the future of this planet. A world where 'all' the people of this planet can live in relative peace, with an organization to become a Democratic form of Government. As the RMC expands its membership, it will have more internal activity. One day I hope that the RMC can grow into a large group of Factionally and Culturally diverse peoples to live together, and to have a say in the everyday activities of the RMC through votes. Representatives and such just as a democracy is supposed to be.

My views for the future are quite a ways off if people like Philip Ross, and Simon Silverstone are allowed to keep ruling us. The Barbaric Simon Silverston, and Felonious Philip Ross are not the right kind of people to be ruling us. We should be free to rule ourselves in a Democratic form of Government. We are the ones who fight, and die. We are the ones who break our backs working for them. Should we not be able to live our own lives? Shouldnt we be able to rule ourselves?

Join us today, join us in Peace and Freedom. The future or Rubi-Ka is yours...
