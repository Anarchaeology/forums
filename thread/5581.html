Ideas To Better Anarchy Online's Gameplay and RP Faction.
You have to realize that I'm a hardcore, experienced player with this game. With every patch introduced, Funcom manages to screw up this game little by little. I enjoyed playing this game when it first came out when I was newb. I didnt care about the crashes every few minutes. I didnt care about little bugs and glitches here and there. All I cared about was the game play. AO was a much more entertaining game to play a couple months ago.

Well, since I -love- this game too much to just delete my level 120 character from being sick and tired of all the changes and nerfage, I've introduced a few ideas that can better the game play and style of AO.


Omni-Tek / Clansmen :

I stumbled across this idea and its probably one of the greatest features Anarchy Online can ever consist of.

There's obviously no "real" feature to diverse the Clans and Omni from each other. Both sides on the Forums have made little points, but nothing concrete can be offered to fully support one another. Face it; each side is as bland as the other. Here are my ideas that can drastically change Anarchy Online for the better.

Omni-Tek, being the multi-trillion corporation it is, has the ability to research and mass produce new and innovative weapons/armor at will. My idea is this: Why not introduce more Omni-only equipment? Rather then just Elite, why not MORE and different kinds of armor? Why not Omni-only weapons? Make the armor and weapons more effective then current patch. It would make sense RP-wise, and would add more depth to the game.

With this stated, the Clans should also get a very distinct advantage of their own. Since the Clans consist of nomads grouping together to fight this corporation, they rely on instinct and self-taught methods to live on. Why not enable Clansmen the ability of Trade skills? Have almost every available trade skill Green for IP reasons. The possibilities would be endless for trade skill items. There's already a QL 50 treatment clinic that can heal for 1500 HP and Im sure there are many other very useable items.

With this said, Funcom *MUST* get rid of *ALL* Clan/Omni applications. If applications were left behind, people would exploit the concept of Omni-only armor, and would put all their extra IP into green trade skills. Once a side is chosen on character creation, it must be final and unable to change.


Player versus Player :

I believe the current PvP system is a complete joke that manages to screw up the entire concept of dueling another player. Why are there TWO different fighting styles in this game? Think about it. Damage doing 50% while healing is at a full 100%? Specials doing 40% max health? Would it make much sense for a level 200 Soldier to FA with a QL 200 Flashpoint for 300 damage against a level 75 Fixer? Does it make much sense for Dimach to do as much damage as Brawl, even though it cycles every half hour?

Funcom NEEDS to bring back the old PvP system. I realize that many players do not build up their characters for PvP fights, so why not allow an option to participate at PvP on character creation? Exactly how the EverQuest servers were and UO, having only certain servers PvP able. Only in the case of AO, certain players would be PvP able upon creation. This concept would add much more drama and excitement to the game.

Classes :

It's pretty clear that Anarchy Online's class system is mangled beyond help. Metas getting 3 pets while Engis and Crats get one? Soldiers doing minimum damage when they're regarded as the game's damage dealing class? Traders (yes, TRADERS) buffing in Shotguns that deal incredibly insane crits? MAs not being able to dish out 2k crits like everyone else at level 120+? etc etc etc. Funcom needs to straighten themselves out and ask the PLAYERS on the Forums on how their specific characters SHOULD be created. Almost every single class in this game needs to be addressed. In my opinion, I believe MPs and Enforcers are done, while Crats are coming in with the next couple of patches. Funcom cant "uber" out one class every other month. They NEED to work their asses off to balanced EVERY class at the SAME time.
