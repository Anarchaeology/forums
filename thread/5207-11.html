nightvision
Hello, I've been away from the game for awhile so I don't have much to add right now. A lot of things have changed. But, I noticed from this thread that one old issue still remains: on many systems, the nightvision effect is painful to look at during gameday, and both the low-light scope and a particular morph turn that effect on. Currently, the way it works is if you have more than zero things with nightvision, then it takes effect. A relatively easy way to give the player control of easily turning that effect on and off is by making it only take effect if you have an odd number of things with nightvision. That way, you can always turn nightvision off (while retaining other special effects, like zone wall shimmer) by either de-equipping or equipping nightvision goggles.

Of course, it would be even easier to implement (and easier for gameplay) to just remove nightvision from everything but nightvision goggles.

Copy and paste
You all have to forgive me but Im going to copy and paste (with some minor changes) some posts of mine into this thread as they are my personal top 10 (or whatever number).
Starting with the morphs, continuing with some, in my mind, amusing nanos.

Negative effects on morphs are not always very popular but I myself see the morphs as a way for the Adventurer to reconstruct her body to focus power on certain skills, thereby leaving others behind. The negative effect will also allow room for additional buffs for the profession. Each form should have an application of its own so you kept using it for those special occasions when you need just that particular skill setting. Human form should also be attractive when you need a mixture of skills with no weaknesses nor strength.


Pronouncement of Greatness
The normal leet form with concealment boost. I see no immediate use of this myself but if nothing else the leet roll always makes me smile. I would leave it as it is.
Application: Sneaking missions and maybe sneaking raids in future mass PvP battles (!)

Sparrow Flight (and descendants)
The Reet form that gives speed boost and some evade increase. It should be possible to cast inside and none of the reets should be able to fly (read on please before saying WTF!). You should be able to fight in this form but with a say 20% damage reduction penalty for the first form and increasing as the run speed becomes better.
Application: Fast transportation inside and outside. Stay away from fighting in this form unless absolutely necessary.

Playful Cub (and descendants)
Transforms the caster into a wolf (and I for one wouldnt mind if it was a real wolf and not a monster but that is a side note). This greatly increases the metabolism which makes the adventurer regenerate hp much faster and increases body development greatly but nano pool is decreased with the same amount. Strength and stamina is augmented but you suffer in intelligence and psychic. Your damage output is increased with X % (yes percent, enough with the fixed amounts already) and run speed is increased with a moderate amount.
Application: When your team lacks a puller and a tank but has a competent doctor you go wolf. In solo missions versus traders where healings might not be available or versus enforcers where you want to try to match their HP (which of course is impossible)

Grinning Hunter (and descendants)
The tiger is the most attack oriented form. Your damage output is increased by Y % (where Y is a larger value than the X for the wolf) and strength and agility is increased while your intelligence and psychic is lowered. The melee and ranged initiatives are increased but your nano initiative is lowered greatly. Also your BioMet and MatMet skills are lowered while you gain some points in AC, a higher chance of critical damage (not 1%, that is lame) and a attack rate bonus. Run speed is not affected (this is a heavy creature, not a cheetah). Be sure to let your team know that they cant count on you healing their butts when in this form.
Application: When your team has a tank and a good doc you just want to contribute with as much damage output as possible and let someone else worry about the healing. In solo missions versus low hp mobs or when you want to take down your opponent as fast as possible and hope you will survive with first aids.

Sight from a cloudless sky
Your first flying morph. Transforms the caster into a great eagle which highly augments perception and evades but lowers HP. Fighting and casting is not possible in this form except for the special attack Claw eyes which would have the effect of the nano by the same name (but longer duration) and would have to be performed at melee range. This form should be reachable at around level 75. Maybe there should be no Others version of this nano, self only like MPs QW.
Application: Transportation and scouting. Could also be used when outpost capture is in place (!) to send in the first wave of adventurers to scout the place out and blind the enemies before the heavy troops are sent in.

Unicorn (and descendants)
Same model as Ruby Eyes in Lush Fields. This form would increase your nano pool but lower your body development with an equal amount. Nano init is boosted a great deal but combat initiatives are lowered at the same time. BioMet and MatMet are increased to allow more powerful heals in this form but attack rating suffers a penalty.
Application: When no doctor is available in your team this form optimizes the adventurer for healing. Although fighting is possible it would probably never be a good idea to assume this form when soloing.

Vipers venom (and descendants)
Transforms the caster into a snake and thereby lowering run speed greatly. Damage type is changed to poison and every hit has a chance of poisoning the opponent which besides DoTing him also puts a slow nano program in his NCU (like Blizzard of Red Tape, to name one). Attack rating is increased but evades are lowered.
Application: Maybe PvP, if you got the run speed to compensate the penalty.

Pit Lizard
The heavy air assault form. Outside only. Can fly and attack while flying. Attack range is however lowered to melee range no matter what weapon you use so you have to dive down on your opponent to attack. Nano casting is only possible while on ground (toggling between flying and walking should be possible, needless to say). Damage is increased with Z % (probably lower than the highest tiger form) and the damage type is randomly changing between fire, chemical and disease. Evades are lowered but body development and AC is boosted
Application: Probably group PvP, should territory capturing ever be possible.

Silent Protector
Transforms the caster into a tree or a bush, or better yet, something that can be found in the surroundings. Outside only of course. While morphed, the adventurer is unable to move, attack and cast and cannot herself be attacked, casted upon or even targeted. This morph should have a long casting time (30-40 secs?) and it should not be possible to cast while in a fight.
Application: I can find only one, PvP ambush ( lets go sit under those oddly placed 6 trees)


All models exist so the main development time would be testing and evaluating.

And here are some nanos I would not mind having



Skilled/Adept/Master Swordsman
Boosts the 1he and 2he skills by 30/60/90. First should probably be reachable at level 25-30, next by level 60-70 and the last by level 100ish

Skilled/Adept/Master Marksman
Same as above but augments pistol skill

Natures loving embrace (line of nanos)
A unique Adventurer root. Actual roots emerge from the ground and keep the target glued to the spot. These roots can be targeted and destroyed by dealing it damage and thereby breaking the root. If mobs should be able to understand that they can break the root it must probably be pretty far up on the hate list which will make this nano an excellent way of helping low HP team mates who have managed to get themselves aggrod. The balance issue is how long it should last and how much HP it should have.

Howl from the beast within (line of nanos)
Augments combat initiatives greatly and increases the chance of critical hits. Shut downs nano capabilities for 2 minutes. Self only.
Father to Son, Mother to Daughter  generation 1, 2 and 3
Generations of herbal knowledge has been concentrated into these nanos making the healing capabilities of the target more accurate. What this means is that the minimum healing on other nanos is increased with say 30% for generation 1, 45% for the second and 60% for the last (numbers are taken from thin air, dont pay too much attention to them). So a healing program that has a healing range from 100-200 would have had 130-200 with generation 1 running and so forth. This could up adventurers popularity a tad even in groups with good docs.

Protector of the Glade (unique nano)
Summons a great beast (a bear?) to aid you when youre about to lose all hope of survival. This is not a pet but a creature acting on its own and attacking whatever is attacking the adventurer. Duration of the NF should probably be something like 2-3 minutes and some skill should be locked for 20-30 minutes so you really had to make a judgment call when to use it. Should this unbalance PvP then make it unable to attack players.
Endure the cold winter (unique nano)
Hostile polymorph. Transforms the target into a wooly sheep and thereby lowering all initiatives greatly in conjunction with an X % damage penalty. Every hit the target takes has a chance of breaking the enchantment.

Deceptive friends (line/unique ?)
Hostile polymorph. Transforms the target into a chicken (or whatever model already in game). This does not affect his combat or nano abilities but will put him rather high up in other mobs hate list. This will cause them to attack their former friend (or at least there will be a chance of attack) who in that case will defend himself, thus leaving the adventurer alone. The enchanted target is still hostile to the adventurer and will immediately attack the player again should he be able to kill his opponent(s).

Glorious creek of purifying water (line of nanos)
During centuries legendary adventurers have found far away creeks with water so pure it could purge the body from poison. The essence of this water has later been encoded bla bla. This nano would attempt to purge any DoT from the targets NCU. If the QL of the purifying water nano was higher then the DoT, the chance of success would be very high and the lower the purifier was relative to the DoT, the lower the chance. Point is that even the lowest form of purifier has a chance of purging the highest DoT although very small. The line could start with Muddy creek of purifying water and go up to Glorious.

Behold natures wonder! (line of nanos)
Summons a tiny fairy-like creature that flies up in front of the target. If successful the target is awed be the beauty of the creature and will forget to fight. If unsuccessful the target will kill the creature. This is not a mez pet, it cant be controlled and it has a very short life span. It would be quite high up in the hate list so other mobs could potentially help an awed friend by killing the creature. There is a model for a creature like this already in the game so no need for new graphics.

Leader of the Pack
Works in conjunction with wolf morph (and maybe tiger and dragon?). The group, seeing the great beast fighting by their side, gets a morality or spirit of heart boost, effectively increasing their combat initiatives and the chance of hitting in their weapons upper damage range. The minimum and maximum damage of the weapons would stay the same but the probability of hitting above average is higher then hitting below. All to the group hostile mobs would get demoralized by the sight of the wolf (maybe just human mobs) and suffer from the reverse effect. This could be implemented like the auras or speeches (but only effect the group and the enemies of the group, not everyone that happens to walk by), plainly a Spirit of heart line of nanos for adventurers.


I would also like to add that if you, Funcom, ever find that a particular morphs would be over powered on some professions then change the Other-version of the morph. If that is not possible then remove the Other-version. Quite frankly, I don't see the need of the other and team versions at all, just as there is no Quantum Wings - Other or Total Mirror Shield - Other. Doing what you did to the reet form will hopefully never happen again.
