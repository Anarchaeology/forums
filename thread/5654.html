1: Planetary Air
Breathing in deeply as the other passengers disembarked into the terminal, one of the new arrivals regarded the air as a little stale... and the after taste of a rich nano environment left bitterness on the back of his tongue. All that aside, the air here was better than the refiltered air in the ferrys cabins. Rubi-Ka. After all those days of travelling.

"Going to be here long Mister... "
The custom officer filtered through the information pad that Malachii passed over to her from his travel case, whilst he scratched something off from his coat, the flicked his hair out from his eyes with a pleasant smile.
"Found my name yet Miss?..."
Nodding, with no reaction to Malachii's attempt at disarming the rather serious toned cusoms officer, she placed the pad back into Malachii's case.
"Welcome to Rubi-Ka, Omni-tek wishes you a pleasant stay Mister Gabriel."
Raising another brief smile he bowed his head sharply then straightened up positively aching to use a lavatory. Casting his eyes over the busy bustle of the port he failed to recognise anyone here. Good thing.
In friendly neon lights were the words he was searching for. The way to relief from his long trip on the ferry. Once he tucked his case under his arm he started into a light jog toward the toilets, avoiding others who seemed to be rushing elsewhere in the terminal.

Comforting darkness swept over him with the wash of water he splashed onto his face then opening his eyes he reached for a soft flannel of some kind of fur. Perhaps one of the native leet pelts?
"You little brat!" The shout errupted in a thunderclap behind Malachii followed by a sharp and hard shove in his back pushing him forward. Opening his eyes quickly let too much light, leading to a slow recognition.
"Western! You old git... I thought by now you'd have..."
"Died?... I am not that old you pup!" Bearnorm grinned through his grizzly beard and his eyes smiled with him.
"Death?... Funny... no not dead, developed a sense of subtlety perhaps?" Malachii wiped his face dry then clapped Bearnorm on the shoulder leading him out into the main foyer of the terminal. Destination; a brisk drink and then business.

"So!..." Bearnorm leaned forward with his teeth bared, "We're to take our place here then? I must say it's quite a nice place. The women look incredible here..."
Malachii savoured the decent drink, allowed it to settle around his tongue before swallowing, then blew hair from his eyes.
"Have not noticed yet, but obviously there will be time with no doubts. My homework upon this world tells me there are two main factions here, freedom fighter named the Clans and the glorious empire of the Omni-tek companies."
Bearnorm nodded then swallowed a large amount of Rubi-Ka brew. As it was still evident the associate was still listening, Malachii continued.
"And so here we are..." Malachii gestured with open arms around him, "The wonderful land of Omni-tek. The riches that are passed through here are phenominal! We should base ourselves here now my friend. The rewards are rich indeed."
Malachii paused.
Bearnorm drank.
Malachii wondered if Bearnorm was breathing in beer; then decided to keep on with conversation.
"We set ourselves up here, make ourselves up to be valued assets to the great company of Omni-tek... then make it known that the Arcane Reverie are accepting new members... "
Bearnorm at that moment, choked.
"New?! Mal... these people are Imps! It's all 'Nano's this' and 'Wrangle that'... it's a crazy drug... no one on this planet accepts NOT having implants or Nano magic running in their veins!"
Nodding gently and setting his drink down to entwine his fingers then pressing his thumbs together, he allowed Bearnorm to lose the unhealthy red colour that had collected in his face.
"Old man... people can be cured of blindness. And those who can... are obviously the kind of people we look for to be within our sacred and elusive band."
Bearnorm looked over Malachii's shoulder.
"We have company young 'un. We had best make moves to Rome."
Without questioning, Malachii stood and collected his pack from the table. Bearnorm continued to watch the slowly advancing group of dark clothed officials who had seemed to have been observing the two saboteurs social drinking session.
Bearnorm quietly informed Malachii that the tools of his trade had arrived safetly and that they would be picking them up from Rome shortly. Malachii was itching to lay hands on his rifle and worktools again.

-End
