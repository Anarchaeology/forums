How to buy ALOT of ammo! Easy, better, faster. more! =)
Tired of clicking your mouse for what seems as an eternity? Like to stack up on a ton of ammo before heading out on those huge missions or hunting with a team a long way from civilization?

Worry no more! Until FC decides to improve the shop gui and let you buy as much you want of things in one lump, you can download Click from here!.

This program clicks your left mouse button as many times as you specify, and only after the number of seconds you choose. (So you have enough time to position the mouse over what you want, before you let the program work its magic.)

Comments, bugs and issues can be posted in this thread and I will try to answer them. =)
