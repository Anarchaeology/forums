BREAKING NEWS! ARK being used by ICC to set-up corporate takeover by SOL at Rubi-Ka!

The Omni Inquisitor - IS Division
Report By: Jacelyn "Lem" Waldram
August 18 29477 RST

ARK. When you hear that acronym, what runs through your mind? Helpers. Guardians. Teachers even perhaps? But who are the ARK? How is it they can just 'appear' whenever they want to? What is their real purpose here on the planet of Rubi-Ka?

Recently, I interviewed an Advisor of Operations. I at first wondered what ARK was, the way they can do what said above with their teleporting methods... but as I asked more and more questions the truth became more and more clouded. Using my interface with my com I was able to unravel certain truths about ARK, the ICC, and SOL Banking. An Assistant Director came at the end to try and disrupt our chat. He succeeded for the Advisor shortly left... and he tried to feed my lies but the truth will shine through. But first...

Here's the facts:
1) March 13 29444: ICC and Omni-Tek agree to extend the lease to January 1. 29550, with an option to extend it further if OT complies with all future ICC rulings.
2) March 15 29444: Sol is allowed to buy limited interests on Rubi-Ka starting January 1, 29500.
3) Year of 29455: Sol Banking Corporation is suspected of operating covert notum mining-operations in the Outzone. The corporation denies the accusations. ICC investigates and finds no evidence of wrongdoing.
4) Year of 29465: The ICC ends up fighting both clan and Omni-Tek soldiers on two fronts, and quickly pulls back.
5) July 04 29470: The Council of Truth, Omni-Tek representatives, and ICC observers sign the Tir Accord. A permanent ceasefire is finally in effect.
6) November 23 29475: The Council of Truth acknowledges the amnesty and votes to enter into official peace talks with Omni-Tek.
7) December 21 29475: Omni-Tek denies media rumours of secret cloning facilities, calling the reports "a hoax", and pointing to research that proves it's impossible to clone a person's life-force.
8) August 21 29476: The ICC announces, through Secretary General Isidra Derousse, they will use the emergency powers to deploy additional Peacekeeper troops on Rubi-Ka in order to ‘protect innocent civilians’.
9) October 18 29476: ICC Secretary General, Isidra Derousse, immediately voices her concerns, and plans to lodge a formal investigation into the decision made by Omni-Tek.
10) November 29 29476: The ICC declares an end to Omni-Tek's absolute monopoly of notum mining, opening up for all organisations to drill the precious element. The new rules stipulate, however, that Omni-Tek retains its monopoly on off-world distribution of notum.
11) December 05 29476: In an editorial in the radical publication the Voice of Freedom, the editor looks at ICC’s decision to end Omni-Tek’s monopoly on notum mining, and brands the decision the opening of the fourth Rubi-Kan Civil War. He calls it The Notum Wars.

Now, what does all this mean? It is a giant puzzle, planned so perfectly that no-one saw the truth in it. Yet, the dots have been connected and the truth cannot be more clear. The ICC's role is to make sure there is Galactic Peace and Prosperity. The ICC chose Omni-Tek but has come to realize that for maximum efficiency there needs to be unity in the mining of Notum, and that is not apparent here on Rubi-Ka. They do not believe the Clans and Omni-Tek could have ever worked out their peace agreement, thus, they sought after another to take over. That other? SOL Banking.

When did this happen? The ground-work was set March 15 29444. Two days before, the ICC tried to show they are aligned with Omni-Tek, but this is just a ruse to have others overlook this simple but small decision. By having SOL Banking limited rights, they open the gates for SOL to come through. Eleven years later... there is suspected activity on Rubi-Ka by SOL... but nothing is found... or was there? The truth is simple, SOL Banking was designing the city some have given the name of Jobe. Impossible you say? It's not, and the reason it is not is because of ARK.

ARK was founded by the ICC, yet it has become a bit more independant over time. Yet, it is the ICC that has given the Advisors of Rubi-Ka the ability to teleport, which is the whole key to this puzzle.

"The ICC Genetic Manipulator improve your body for being able to manipulate Notum clouds, so that you can instantaneously Move yourself to any citizen in need on the planet. wherever he/she/it would be."

Yes. You heard it from The Advisor. "It" she said. As in, a structure. The reason nothing was found is that everytime it is about to be found they make it disappear elsewhere. Thus, giving them time to build it. It is said that the Jobe facility is built by rogue scientists. Rogue, as in do not answer to Omni-Tek or any Clanner association. Rogue... as in... SOL Banking. By building this facility, SOL Banking is given time to establish a base on Rubi-Ka. Yet, even the mighty technological behemoth of the ICC could not withstand against a united Clan and Omni-Tek front as shown in 29465, they know this. So what happens? As you may note from the facts I listed, Omni-Tek and the Clans were slowly approaching peace. Then... came 29475. Clones? Yes, that's impossible isn't it?

"Improving a citizen takes loads and loads of notum energy, you certainly already have noticed how all the planet is affected when ARKs are improving members."

Improving? Once infused with the technology, what more is there to improve? Impossible to clone? In theory, to do so would require a... large amount of notum. I tried to pursue why the nano-drain occurs, and I was told that it was not her department and thus could not give me the data. In fact, when asked what the limit is for the planet she could not answer, stating: "Its out of my knowledge i fear."

Out of her knowledge? Yes, even the ARKs themselves are being lied to by the 'higher-ups', as we call them in the business world. They do not know, and are trained not to know.

"We're not teachers as well, we can answer most of all questions (not all unfortunately) but we're not teachers, there is no better teacher than experience. for teaching New arks member we go on work with em directly. Mentoring em."

The Advisors try to answer questions but don't actually help. They... 'set the stage'. Give us our first weapon out the shuttle and put us against each other. The smart ones are the nuetrals, but even they are not smart enough. For as long as the planet is divided, the total power of those who could fight against the ICC is weakened. "I would say that directors answers the ICC comitee directly." The individual ARKs are taught to teach others how to fight and do so, but remain out of it. But why? With such great technology, if the ICC really cared about maintaining global prosperity couldn't they just end the conflict? What you don't realize, however... is they already have set this in motion.

Where is Radiman? Where is Ross?

These two have not been since Secretary General Isidra Derousse came to the fore-front. October 18 29476...Derousse launches a formal investigation. But what is the outcome of that investigation? Simple, the capture (or quite possibly murder, though I do not believe this is true as a body would have been found) of both Radiman and Ross. Impossible? Yep, there's that word once again... and once again must I remind you nothing is impossible because of one thing:

"The ICC Genetic Manipulator improve your body for being able to manipulate Notum clouds, so that you can instantaneously Move yourself to any citizen in need on the planet. wherever he/she/it would be."

This time, the "it" is not important as much as the "he/she" is. Through the usage of high ICC-Loyal ARK personal, both Radiman and Ross were captured. This is why we haven't seen them. This is why we haven't heard from them. But why has neither side said anything? Because of November 29 29476... the end to Omni-Tek monopoly of Notum. This launched the fourth civil war, the Notum Wars, as some call it. Even a Clan Editor saw that this was all caused because of the ICC. How could they do something so blatant to start up warfare once again? While at war, neither side could admit to the other their leader had vanished, for both did not want to seem weak to the other. Thus, no peace resolution is being worked out and none will be. We shall go on killing each other, until Jobe appears.

Jobe. The city of wonders some call it already. Yet, it is all the opposite. It is the city of nightmares. It is the city that will cause the Omni-Tek corporation to crumble, the Clanners to disperse, and SOL to take over. The ICC is not waiting any longer for SOL to get rights to Rubi-Ka (29500) and for the Omni-Tek lease to expire (29550).

I have recorded the interview I had with the Advisor, as proof it did take place. She does not remember it. Upon calling her a bit later, I heard strange noises coming from her com. She would not answer, and she acted as if she never met me. They tried to cover it up, but they cannot cover this up anymore.

Help put a stop to the ARKs, to SOL, to this pointless war. Let us free our leaders from their grasps, and work together to resolve what we started in November 23 29475.
