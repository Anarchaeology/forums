I have one major gripe with people who say that they would rather role-play and not know the mechanics of the game:

The mechanics of the game are arbitrary so what is the point to figuring them out?

What your role-playing boils down to is systematically trying every option until you figure it out. There is no thought or deduction involved. It would be entirely different if you knew A, B and C then you could figure out D. Instead AO has rules that let me be a master at bursting with a gun but not able to full auto at all.

What makes the matter worse is that the underlying rules can change or are bugged. Do we even know if all the mission settings work? Dual wielding was made harder. PvP damage was halved. Surgery clinics were broken and stacked the treatment buff.

The only real way for you to role-play is to know what the developers had in mind in the first place. Then you can role-play based on the intent of the game and not get side tracked. The gamming world simply isnt complete or stable enough to truly role-play. The best that you can do is to role-play within the limits of the world, and knowing those limits is essential.

Finally, the best role-playing ever is real life and I dont see that changing just cause science and mathematics can attach numbers to it.
