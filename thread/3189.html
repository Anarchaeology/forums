Not sure about holiday items, at least in a traditional sense. With so many people over the world (in real life) playing AO, something is bound to offend someone.

Would be better if FUNCOM made up Rubi-ka's own holidays, celebrating some of the culture of Rubi-ka.

Here are some examples:

1--Holidays celebrating some of the people already known to be famous for something...I have seen at least SID & VITO, who are famous for developing certain types of Armor. Maybe they can have a holiday as well.
2--Political days --based on the political structure - Omni-Tek would celebrate the day Phillip Ross came into power, Clans could celebrate the day Henry Radiman came into power (or maybe the clans that don't like Mr. Radiman can use this day as a "reminder, rally" that things must change).
3--Maybe there can be a holiday based on some event that happened in game (some war in the past, etc)
4--Clans can celebrate the anniversary of Omni-2 (presently ATHENS)takeover.
5--Omni can have some type of "Employee Day" or Labor day, to celebrate dedicated service to the parent corporation.

Special Items/or bonuses can be created for these 'holidays'.
example: on EMPLOYEE DAY, all stores in OMNI-TRADE sell things at a reduced rate to employees, or something like that.

Clans can have thier reduced rate on all items sold in ATHENS on their OMNI-2 Takeover Anniversary).

On VITO, or SIDs holiday(s), armor can be upgraded (at a special upgrade scanner booth or something) to 1 QL higher than present armor. (*available only once to each character, only applies to armor worn while stepping into the scanner booth)--of course, if you don't have the stats for it, it will fall into your inventory.

Also - I really like the idea of new faces. I though I had met my twin, but then I realized that that 'special project' that I volunteered for actually created clones of me. Omni-Med must have recognized my superior genetic profile :-)

Seriously, though, we do need new faces-just don't give the women manly faces and muscular chests like EverQuest recently did - It's awful.

If you do add more face models, I wish, although we may not be able to change our breed (which changes our skill learning rate/cost), we should be allowed to buy plastic surgery, or genetic engineering to our face to allow us to alter our appearance (perhaps charge a fee for it), -This is of course, the future.

I wish my female character can have green hair. I want to look like an anime chick. Why are the females (solitus at least) limited to Normal colored hair - don't they have hair dye, or genetic hair color enhancements available 30,000 years in the future?

I wonder if we are ever going to get new breeds, I mean, maybe way into the future, there's gotta be some of those failed experiments that got out, are sentient, and intelligent. It would be interesting to actually play one of them, or a rhinoman maybe.?
Maybe they will introduce a new humanoid breed?

I'll wait and see what develops.
