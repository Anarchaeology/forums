Some Suggestions
I haven't played an Adventurer long enough to comment about all the problems, but I have noticed the trouble with dealing and taking damage. My adventurer just uses a pistol so it takes longer to kill a mob than my nano tech character, who uses a pistol and "nukes". This also leads to taking more damage in a fight.

I only skimmed through the other posts so I apologize if I'm repeating these suggestions...

Let Adventurers have a program or series of programs that gives them added damage to pistols/1H edged that can only be "cast" on themselves. The first would be something a 10th lvl char could get which gives say, +25% to damage. This would continue up to and maybe beyond +100% to damage. Therefore, those low damage pistols/1h weapons would do more.

OR, have a program that allow you to do damage equal to your level while slowing down your Ranged Init speed. A 10th lvl Adventurer would get +10 points of damage with a pistol/1H but be slowed down as if the Aggro bar was moved towards Defensive (w/o the benefits). This would allow Adventurers to do more damage without getting out of hand with the rapid firing that pistols allow.

This next one is more helpful for solo adventuring, which Adventurers should be the best at...and resigned to do since groups don't really care for them.

It's simple...allow a larger range for mobs to remain green. Adventurers would know the inner workings of the wild creatures that inhabit Rubika better than any other profession. They know how to take advantage and make the most of the wilderness. They therefore can get further knowledge (experience) from encounters (grey colored) where as blundering city dwellers wouldn't know how to learn as much from these same encounters. This ability of Adventurers could be directly linked with the adventure skill, making it useful.

Anyways, that's it for now.

top ten
After reading most of the posts it appears that most all the best improvements have been covered fair well, though I'd like to add my voice to the improvements that I would like to see most.

1. Either get rid of the 'agent' buffs in our line of nanos or lighten out our rifle and aimed shot skills. The eagle eye and hawk eye are obvious agent buffs in our nanos, and I dont know about other adventurers, but I find that a little degrading that we are the only class that doesnt appear to have weapons buffs for the weapons that we are suited for. Either give us 'edged weapons' buffs or lighten our rifle skills so that we can use that line of nanos.

2. Augment the morphs, as mentioned several times, allow us to use Cub and Hunter indoors. Drop the extra head of the Cub and thin the Cat out abit to allow us to use them in missions. Speed up the Cat and augment the bonuses a tad. And, ... give us flight sooner. Also a few more morphs would be nice. Maybe a bull (a serious tank), spider (fast attacker), and a vulture (early flier) as examples.

3. Give the adventurer a nano line to help us read maps. Since most adventurers spend more time beating the brush than our non-adventurer counter-parts, we should have a better idea of the lay of the land. And allow us to use the other alignments starter maps after a certain level.

4. A distract or daze line of nano for non-human monsters would be cool. Though not pets for the adventurer please.

Thanks for your time,
