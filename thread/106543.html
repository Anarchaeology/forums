A plea to Omni and Clan alike
Often times, it seems to me that Omni Employees and Clan members alike don't know what it is to be neutral, and I'll attribute that to the fact that most Omni Employees or Clan members were born Omni/Clan, and will die Omni/Clan. Well that's just fine, but that means you need to know what it is to be neutral.

Neutral means we don't have any established govenrment, because neutral alignment is NOT A FACTION! consider every neutral org to be it's own faction. for example, Circle G is an intersteller corporation, just like Omni-Tek. Unlike Omni-Tek, we have but a small presence on Rubi-Ka. Because we aren't here as Omni-Tek contractors, we're not Omni aligned. Because we don't want to cut off all business oppertunities with Omni-Tek both on rubi-Ka and in other corners of the galazy, we can't be clan aligned. OOC: If the AO game mechanics allowed it, we'd should up as Circle G for our faction, not neutral.

That established (or, at least I hope it's established...) let us address how we are treated by Omni and Clan...

Omni and Clan are treating the entire neutral population as a single faction. For example:

quote:
Free Trade Alliance's tower gets attacked by Chimera (an omni department).
Reason: The RP session that the NLF (nanomage liberation front) put up basically declaring war on anyone who has a tower complex up.
They yelled: This is what happens to neutrals as long as the NLF is neutral.
So the actions fo one neutral org has reprecussions to an orginization who exists to facilitate peacful trade between everyone. As far as I know, Free Trade Allience does not support the actions of the NLF.

So Chimera should be interested in destroying NLF's towers, and any org that has aligned with the NLF (Like Elite Operations). If you're going to blindly attack any neutral tower in retaliation for the actions for a neutral org, make sure your message will be heard and attack the correct towers!

Since FTA and the NLF are completely seperate orginizations, what are they going to do? Embargo the NLF? The NLF is a terrorist orginizations people, if not by their own admission then by their actions and behavior. They can call themselves whatever they want, but their actions speak for themselves (The above is my opinion, and should not be considered the official stance of Circle G, Inc.)

And now I go OOC: If chimera did this out of RP, they should consider OT's standing orders conserning neutrals: OT is, in theory anyway, supposed to protect the neutral population of Rubi-Ka. I haven't seen Boss Ross withdrawing that policy yet. So if I'm wrong, point me in the right direction.

As for defence, when you attack a neutral tower expect the defending force to consist of mutiple orgs due to a undocumented, unofficial "you scratch my back I'll scratch yours" type agreement. Since we are under fire from both Clan and Omni forces, and ocasionaly other neutral forces, you basicall force us to band together to defend ourselves and protect out mining interests. (OOC again: and if team side actually worked worth a damn in PvP, the option of having Clan or Omni orgs help in defence wouldn't be uncommon. As the system is now, it's simply not worth the problems it raises)

Well, I hope this rant was helpful to someone out there, if not, at least I feel better.
