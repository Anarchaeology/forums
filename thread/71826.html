Bodyguard skill for the Soldier
When the bodyguard skill is cast on a team member, 50% of the damage done to the 'bodyguarded' should be reflected to the soldier.

The skill should also lock the nano skills of the soldier, but not as long as as tms.


There could also be a team bodyguard skill, which reflects 10% of all damage done to the team to the soldier.


So if there are two soliders in a team, they could cast bodyguard on each other sharing their hps.

I sympathize, however, as a Doc what I see is that when Soldiers are under attack (and aren't using TMS), they need focused healing to keep them alive.
