Pvp server is much needed for many reasons that have been stated in other threads listed on this server. Most of all it will bring more people into the game. How to fix PvP!

#1. First and foremost, fix the level restrictions for PvP. In the early days, sure there may not have been many high level players, but that has certainly changed now.

#2. The reward system needs to be fixed and can only be fixed after the level restrictions are fixed. Currently, you get a "title" after so many kills or what not. Big whoop. They used to tease Freshman in high school, like I wanna be one now. Kills in PvP should result in IP points but only if the victim is higher level than yourself. The resulting IP points should not be enough to raise a skill after a single kill, but enough to make people want to PvP.

#3. Areas to PvP in. In Ent, if you engage into a fight with someone and they leave the arena and you chase them out of the arena, you can fight them anywhere in the zone. In EFP (Eastern Foul Plains), there's areas where the supression gas "flickers" from 75% to 25% and back if you're going in the right direction. MMD's grid pop is 10 feet from a 25% zone, yet you pop in a 75% zone. In SFP (Southern Foul Plains), there's an area called Klatran in which there is an outpost surrounded by a pool of green (radioactive?) liquid. Within the pool, it is 25%. Outside, it is 75%. Huh?? Any zone with any shred of 25% or 0% gas should have no other kind of gas anywhere in the zone. The zones should not have reclaim, save, or bank terminals anywhere within them. There should also be no exits spare the zone lines (in other words, once you pop into a 25% zone, the closest exit is the closest zone line). Nerf the supression gas p00ns.

#4. Profession balancing? Now there's a can of worms not even worth opening.
