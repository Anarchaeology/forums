Stacking MA nano programs
Can someone help clarify the reasons behind the following:

Brawl Expertise (general nano) boosts Brawl by 20.
Dirty Fighter (MA only nano) boosts Brawl by 45.
Therefore Brawl Expertise + Dirty Fighter = 65 Brawl boost

Shouldn't DF override Brawl rather than stack?

This is minor compared to:

Lesser Controlled Rage (MA starter nano) ups MA skill by 12
Martial Artist Expertise (general nano) boosts MA by 20.
Martial Arts Mastery (MA only nano) boosts MA by 60.
ALL of which stack
LCR (12) + MA Ex (20) + MA Mastery (60) = an MA boost of 92!?!

Ok I ~know~ that cannot be the intention, that's just insane.
(Not that I'm complaining, I enjoy the boost, just feels a little dirty is all..)

And one last note, do we have a petition for getting the length of time our MA is locked on the special attacks description?

Thanks in advance.

Oh, MA buffs stacking are indeed intended, most of the buffs in game work like this. Four Fists of Kali overrides MA Mastery, but they both stack with MA Expertise and LCR. Enforcers startup nano "Thugs Delight" stack with "Brutal Thug" and 1HB/2HB expertise, there are plenty of other examples.

So, enjoy.

Regarding the skill lock time for our MA specials, nah FC wants us to read the cute mumbojumbo and not have a clue what they really do. You'll find most of the lock times in the MA forums thou, so have a look there.
