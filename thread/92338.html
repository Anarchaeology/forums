What I've hlearned troughout my limited experience with MMORPG's is that the roleplaying usually starts with you. Coming from Dark Age of Camelot and being a very forwarded person concerning roleplay on a particular server community, the step to AO was quite a pleasant one. It's a techno cyberpunk future, so roleplay around the words powerlevelling. Give them a twist in words and stray away from the word-shortcuts. Call the medic Doc, give your character a little twist of speech.

But also keep in mind that due to that cyberpunk genre this game is on, and the massive playerbase, you ARE going to encounter a lot of "doods" (hate that word). Just grit your teeth, or look at what they are saying, shrug and think that they're trying at least if it sounds reasonable. Because that'll keep you going. Solo.

Guildwise? Yeah, check the links. Whispers' Edge for Clan seems pretty solid, I talked to Tron20 a while ago, and he was very helpfull, no /tells involved. Walked up to him, did a few emotes, drew his attention and took him for a walk, then heard about his guild and everything. Their stance on the Omnitech sides and everything. Pretty good.

I hope this helped somewhat!
