OOC: An idea for FC
This whole threads OOC

Ok, I have had a lot of people whining for months, oh years actually about where the main story is. Yes...FC has it out there but its no longer in our face like it once was but is now hiden in slow moves with patchs (like the Sentinals and Loren Warr in Newlands), and also hiden in discussions with NPCs. Soooo, I had an idea, and if FC is interested in listening, or if anyone quiet likes it then please say here...I've mentioned it with many people in game as an idea, and so far haven't heard anyone think its bad....so here it is...

Have weekly, or even fortnightly scheduled server downtime. Say every Wednesday morning FC takes down the servers, they get the nice time to make adjustments as necessary which seem of late to be coming more often, and the players get a story. A story in that its nothing big, but say one week OT tanks are parked in Wartorn Valley, the next week Clan artillery is put in 20k, the following week theres wreckage of tanks in Wartorn Valley and the tanks have moved back into Aegean. You all get the idea here. All the moves are made via objects. Nothing needing FC to provide time and people to man events in these places. Just objects placed or moved in an area which shouldn't take the world designers more than an hour to do. The players get a small patch and everyone wins. We get a moving story. FC gets more time for maintenance and server tweaking.

Anyhow, what do you guys think?
