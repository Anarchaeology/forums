Good to be back
Hola Folks.

I quit playing sometime last summer because i was just fed up with all the nonsense from Funcom. The game didn't work, it stopped being fun, and the team seemeed very unresponsive.

However, currently there are no new multi-player games to play and my buddies and I were getting fedup with games like Heroes IV that got released without multi-player. That's when we came up with the idea of returning to Anarchy to give it another whack. None of us could find our disks, but fortunately Funcom let's you download the game now, that's great. So, we logged in last night for the first time.

Before we left we all got cranky and gave away everything we collectivly owned (oops), so we made new charcters. I made a brand new fixer (which it looks like that was a mistake, lots of people are griping about fixers right now). So I might re-make into something else tonight. But I'm happy to be back in the game. I think its going to be fun again and despite the fact that Anarchy seems to be required to have some problems it looked good last night.

The interface has changed some and some of the memory leak and lag problems i remember weren't around quite so much. I'm kind of excited to be back.

Question:
Is it just me or are there a lot less people on the game. The cities didn't seem too crowded last night. I was kind of suprised.
