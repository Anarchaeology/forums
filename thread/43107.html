Lol... apparently you people have never even attempted to program anything on this scale.. they are TRYING to fix this stuff... and you people use the term "lag" incorrectly. Lag is related to latency.. the rate at which packets are sent to and from the server. The lag many of you refer to is simply low FPS. How do you fix this? Get a better computer, make the graphics worse, or LOWER YOUR VISUAL DISTANCE SLIDER! In some of our more crowded areas, you can have over 150,000 polygons on the screen at once. To put that into perspective... if you were playing Quake 3 with 64 people, and they all fired rocket launchers simultaneously... you'd have about 80,000 polygons on your screen. If you don't like the low FPS here then go buy Luclin, play EQ, and get low FPS there. Or wait for SWG and get low FPS there.

Inventory bugs... those are infinitely more bearable than compared to launch. Once again, very hard to fix. But at least the item fixes itself when you zone, as compared to being screwed up forever like it was before.

Memory leaks... these are not leaks. The AO engine works by putting all textures you encounter along the way into the swap file. It's intended to do that. Why? I don't know, they say it helps stuff load faster, but for that to work you need like 4 gigs of RAM. Thus if you zone into say 10 different zones without relogging, each with people wearing various things in them.. you might have tens of thousands of textures in your swap file. Like they said, simply lower the max size of the swap file. It will take longer for you to zone in many cases, but you will have to use less virtual memory and AO will close faster. Upgrading your RAM speed may also help - if you're still running on 233Mhz RAM or something... upgrade!
