PigLick
Leetas
My biggest question now is when this change is going to take place. I can only hope that it won't be changed until significant work is done on all the other trader abilities, i.e. this change is part of the "Trader lovin'" patch. If that were to be true, I would not be nearly so concerned about this change. I can accept that traders were never meant to be top-tier healers (though by their own admission, we were meant to be tied for 3rd, out of 14 classes), and that these changes are being made in accord with that.

However, what we *were* meant to be was is useful in groups. As of right now, we are useful in groups. We have good healing, decent damage output, some calming/rooting ability, and a few other minor abilities. Without the healing, however, the damage, calm/root and other minor abilities will not be ebough to make us attractive members of a group. That spot would be much better spent on just about any other class; many have rooting abilites, several do more damage, and at least two (are supposed to) have comparable, if not better, healing.

If, however, our other nano lines/abilities were fixed/adjusted so as to be useful, we may actually have something other to offer to groups other than healing and damage which, in my opinion, are not what I picture the trader doing primarily anyway. As I said, our rooting and calms are adequate, not the best, but not the worst (in fact, only two other profs even have calming abilities, so we're doing pretty well in that department). If our divests and plunders actually acted in detriment to NPCs and had reasonable durations, that right there would be a great step in the right direction. Add onto that team wrangles that actually had a duration long enough to be useful and health funnels that were more on-par with the level they can be cast at, and things are looking up nicely. Lastly, throw on worthwhile nano funnels, and possibly the ability to increase the nano-pool and/or nano-regen rate of the group, and possibly even tweak our charms a bit (unlikely of course, this has been gone over again and again) and we're suddenly looking like pretty attractive group members once again, and fitting nicely into the "trader" role which we're supposed to be playing in the first place.

I'm picturing a situation something like this: get group formed up, and head out. While everyone else buffs up the party, the trader bides his time- he's not much use until the action actually starts. The pulling begins, and the trader uses the first wave to plunder and divest his own skills/AC up as high as possible. Next, he then uses his wranglers to hand out some of those increases to his group- after all, those nano increases are much more valuable in the hands of docs, NTs, engis and MPs than traders, while the weapon skills are advantageous for the soldiers, enforcers, martial artists and agents. Already the group is bolstered by the presence of the trader, and he still has a few tricks up his sleeve. He can use his nano funnels to help keep his nano topped off, so that he can be liberal with his heals and resulting funnels (since his heals damage him, he'll need the funnels to stay alive), and be sure to be able to throw out the roots and calms when necessary. [Another option which I think is pretty neat, depends on tweaked charms. After the trader has wrangled away much of his skill, he's no longer of much use to the group. Therefore, he could use his mind-swapping charms to take over an NPC to help the group for awhile. The trader character itself will be locked, of course, but since his attack and nano skills are fairly low at this point, it's a fair trade-off. When the divests/plunders.wrangles are getting close to wearing off, the trader breaks the charm and prepares to do it all again].

These changes would give the trader a very unique role and set of abilities which fit very well in-line with what they seem to have intended for the profession, and in my opinion would make for a very fun, active character to play. All just a pipe dream I'm sure, but what the heck, a guy can dream, can't he?
