Trick or Treat
Rubi-Ka October 31 29477

Grisser looked out across the view from his apartment in Tir and the big Atrox grumbled to himself slowly, the storm was coming.

"It's happening again", he mumbled, "dang sky getting dark again."

"Really?" Silirrion asked sitting up from the sofa and discarding his can of Plumbo XXX made his way to the window, "Cool, you're right, it's definately happening again"

"Wat ya doing?"

"Getting my stuff together - trick or treating to be done tonight"

"Wot's twick or tweating?"

Silirrion smiled, "an old earth tradition, long lost to many but the most avid historians, not really sure what its basis is, but its a certain day every year when scary stuff happens and we go out and kill stuff"

The Atrox rubbed his head "dat sounds like every oder day round here, wots so special bout this trick ow tweating?"

Silirrion shrugged, "Not sure exactly, the person who told me about it all was quite drunk, but it's like we go around and ask scary things if they want a trick or a treat, and if they don't give us a treat we trick them."

"You mean me smash em up?"

"Well yeah"

The Atrox didn't sound convinced..."and you've done dis ting before?"

"Yeah, last year"

"..and did any of the scary stuff you spoke to just hand over a tweat?"

"Hmmm...not exactly"

"You had to kill em all didn't you?"

Silirrion nodded, "yeah, but we did get lots of cool stuff."

The Atrox shrugged, "Well sounds like any other day to me"

"except it's all dark and scary!" Silirrion said pointing out through the window at the unnaturally darkening skies.

"Tell you wot" The Atrox said finally after a moments thought, "you can ask for dese tweats and I'll do the twicking? Deal?"

"Deal"

"So who dese scary things we looking for?"

"Well," Silirrion explained slowly, "They are kinda ragged looking dudes with pumpkins for heads"

"PUMPKINS? as in pumpkin pies?"

Silirrion nodded

"Why the gank didn't you say so man, lets go, Grisser LOVES pumpkin pie!"
