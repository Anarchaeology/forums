Division 3: Omni Reclamation - recruitment
Division 3: Omni Reclamation is looking for loyal employees of all professions to augment its ranks. Though Division 3 is primarily a military organization, we also have need of non-combat personnel for research, logistical and technical support, and administration. Any employees interested in learning more are invited to contact any of our officers and review the information on Division 3 which can be found at www.projectrevenant.com Or you can simply reply here and we will have someone contact you.(we are in the process of building a new site, so the only areas that are current at the moment are the forums, which you can access after signing up for a forum account.)



Division 3 is part of Project Revenant, a OOC alliance of several organizations of all three factions. Project Revenant was created to foster role-playing for all factions, and to give anyone a chance to be part of a player-run storyline where their actions can make a difference. Having organizations from all sides of the conflict allows our members to have interaction with both friends and enemies in a RP setting. All of the organizations in Project Revenant agree to the same basic rules, and strive to create events and a story line for their members. The events are not scripted, and the actions of the participants determine the course of the story.

We are looking for people who want to role-play their affiliation with their faction, whether you play several hours every day, or just a couple of hours a week. Anyone interested in the Clan side of Project Revenant should visit www.projectrevenant.net.
