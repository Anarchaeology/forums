A good Crat concept
Hello all - I come bearing a perfect concept for the Crat profession.

Int. and Psy. Buffs - These could be used for putting on nano type armor. And to be honest these buffs are lacking in the game. If other people can buff on Str Agily armor and the like - Why cant we do the same with our nano type armor? There is no reason not to, Funcom.

Secondly....

Int. and Psy. D-Buffs - This would be perfect. It might even be put into a combo D-Buff nano that could zap both stats at the same time. This could be used for Charming pets which becomes hard l8r on in life.

Last but not least....

Maybe some sort of MC and TS buff (self only). This would be most helpful - but i would not cry if it were not added into gameplay in the short term.

Also....

Common FC - What the hell is up with the massive gaps in Crat nanos!? Your stuck with the DD nano Stinging reminder for like 20 lvl's. That is just plain half-asked thinking. FILL THE GAPS!
