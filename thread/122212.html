Word filter
Hi everybody,

I've cleaned up the word filter a bit, as it contained a lot of old stuff that I really think we don't need in it - like "alternative" ways to write certain four letter words.

I hope people understand that this is no excuse to use those alternatives. Using mature and proper language is expected by anybody posting on these forums, and name-calling and profanity is prohibited by the social guidelines. Yes, to some it's natural to say "fu.. you" to anything - even with a smile, or say "sh.." in every sentence when they're surprised or upset (actually, both are fairly common in Norway, as those words don't hold much "punch" here). But in my opinion people should understand that others react, and out of respect for others keep it to an absolute minimum, preferably zero. Posting on a forum does give you some time to think about what you say, after all.

So, I wondered, do we really need a word filter? Shouldn't it be enough to tell people they can't talk to others like that, as we do in the social guidelines? Of course, some people never care about the guidelines anyway, and consider anything possible as allowed. But does that warrant a word filter, or should we rather just kick those people out (after a warning or three, of course)?

Just a random thought, which I guess some of you might have an opinion about. Word filter or not?
