Why is a Game That is Team Oriented Making it so Stressful to Form and Manage a Team?
It seems now when you want to get a team you had better be prepared to have all day. I just spent 2 hours getting a team together that disband 2 minuets after getting a mission, I am going to list the problems in order of phases that cause problems.

PHASE ONE: Gathering Members
- This task keeps getting harder and harder as you increase in level, with the seeking team chat slowly becoming more and more worthless there are a few areas (such as 20K) where people go to shout for a team. It's not so hard to gather people except for there is also a need for Phase two.

PHASE TWO: Tank and Healer
- As if just finding members wasn't hard enough now we need specifics. Due to phase four dealing with xp, players now ALWAYS seek a high QL mission because it's too hard to gain XP at your current level mission. So someone strong enough to take blows is required. Naturally this is done by an Enforcer or Soldier and occasionally a Martial Artist. Not only that a Doctor is required, either that or 3 other professions to make up for the doctor's abilities. The Doctor should not be the only profession greatly capable of casting enough heals for the party. Believe me I know the programming is hard for what I'm asking but there should be more professions that can heal as good as the Doctor. Naturally this would require giving the Doctor stronger combat abilities. But I digress. Most (and please note I say most but not all) Doctors are big-headed due to the fact they know teams can't normally function properly without them. How many time have you heard "Team of 5 looking for a doc" it's not right for a team to 'need' to search for one specific profession.

PHASE THREE: People Leaving
- It might be hard for some people to understand but there are real lives outside of Anarchy Online. I, personally have all day, but most don't as they manage school, or a job, or a family, or all of the above and simply cannot wait hours for a team to form. Many don't want to even wait any longer than 30 minuets. I honestly think watching the hair on my arm grow is about as entertaining as trying to form a team. If a member leaves you've got added minuets there, estimated on 10. Another hour+ if you lost your Doctor. There isn't really anything you can do about people leaving the team but it adds to the existing problems.

PHASE FOUR: Getting a Mission
- Why is this such a hard task? Honestly I just don't understand it. First of all in just about every 10 levels or less your xp amount needed to level doubles so you have to get a QL mission just about double your current level. Another issue is unless it is believed that by level 50 everyone should have a yalm making it take another 30 minuets to get to your mission is extremely pointless. So people have to travel to other towns to get a mission and then come back to give it to the rest of the team, just to get it in a decent area. Which wastes MORE TIME. I can understand Funcom wants us to not just stay in the same place all game but running for half an hour though dangerous areas isn't my idea of a good time. Please don't think that we're too stupid to not move to a different area if one is starting to get boring. The distance to travel to a mission location is absurd.

PHASE FIVE: The First Room
- How many times have you had to remake a team because 4+ mobs in an overly high QL mission poured out into the very first room? People don't want to spend all of this time creating a team only to die. They just leave! If we were meant to take on this many mobs at once why do the rest of the rooms contain 1-2 mobs instead of 4+? It doesn't make any sense.

PHASE SIX: Boss Room
- Not only is the Boss room twice as hard as the first room, it also is necessary to finish the mission and get the boss only loot items. I have no problem with this except if you want boss only loot you have to spend all of this time to get a team, you've played about 3+ hours now between forming your team and going through the insanely large mission with the insanely large QL for the insanely low xp. Now if half of your team didn't already ditch because of this time lapse you still have to go through the boss. This "you cannot use this item while in a fight" issue is pointless. Exactly how many teams have you been in where people don't use the lift to go back down and heal? You didn't stop them from healing, Funcom all you did was make them waste another 10 minuets running out of the mission and back in again. Or waiting another half an hour if someone dies because the walk was too long back to the mission. Or just plain quitting because it's too much stress.

Look, these are real issues here that affect everyone. Not one person, not one small group of people, not one profession, not one side, it is affecting everyone. It's making the game have too many additional problems. It's also 'causing more people to go solo or hunt and it's greatly angering the people who solo and hunt because they can't get the items made only boss loot. There has got to be a large number of people out there who can relate to this issue. Please reply with your thoughts and/or suggestions to help destroy these problems affecting the community.

There's a bit of 'moon on a stick' in there, but the basic point is valid. Best method is to belong to a good guild or be on friendly terms with a good guild. Ad-hoc teams will never work as well as a good team that know each other and team regularly.

At higher levels, yalms become pretty important, though having an engineer in the team can get round this.

Having healing is useful for sure, but an MA, an Adv, or an MP/Agent with heal pet on the tank does a reasonable job.

People leaving is always an issue. Best bet is just to quit the mission, then try and get another, though as pointed out, that can be a pain in the neck. If your doing high QL missions, then your after xp, not cash/items in general, so simply deleting isn't a major issue. Some of these high QL missions can be *huge* anyway...

First room can be a problem, but if it goes pear shaped, then if delete/re-roll is an option, just do it. A well disciplined team and a good puller are a must.

Boss rooms are more of a pain, though I've not seen as many bad ones recently. Again, good tactics and discipline are a must. And if your only after the xp, doing the boss is optional...

Teaming can be a pain, but if it is, there are things *you* can do to improve the situation. Don't wait for the'perfect' team, just get some people together. If you can't get a full team, then don't waste time waiting for that one more person, just get a lower QL mission.

The only problems I ever have is actually getting everyone in the team *ready* (and sometimes finding the right mission depending on team members, yalms, engineers etc.)

"Needing" a doctor is a fallacy. The "need" for a doctor is based on the false premise that you "need" or even "should" do level 190 missions at level 110. You "want" to do those missions, but you will not be punished or penalized in any way if you don't. Doing veterans is a pure bonus, serendipity, not required in any sense.

Further... doctor healing ability becomes completely superfluous to a full team doing missions by level 160; a level 160 agent FPing doctor can meet that team's healing needs in a level 190 mission, or a level 150 adventurer can. You "need" a doctor if and only if you are attempting something far beyond your level's intended capabilities. The more you level, the less use you'll have for healing.

If you can't find a doctor within 15 minutes or so, get an adventurer for heals and do an easier mission. Level 150 to 160 at level 110 sounds about right. Or learn how to follow an engineer pet and learn how to keep your own aggro down, and let the robot be the tank and the engineer play doctor for it. Either way, adapting your tactics and getting some exp before you have to leave is better than standing on the steps in Home shouting for a doctor.
