Sakuraz Interview
The interviewer sat down in front of her. Still without an office both were perched in the Rompa Bars toilets upon sinks.

"Do we have to sit here?" the interviewer asked with a slight disgusted look on her face.

"Yes we do, and now you only have 9 questions left" I said giggling at making her interviewer squirm.

"So, am I right in saying you have been promoted to Vixen's Media Director". She got out her plastic pen and started jotting things down on her comm.

Sakuraz's feelings of shyness about this interview quickly washed away, it was now replaced by professionalism. "You would be right, I've had the opportunity to help Mechfix manage a portion of public relations, along with managing our Radio Department."

"I heard that you're stepping on quite a few feet with your radio station." She said head tilted downwards to her comm avoiding eye contact.

"This is the thing I've been trying to explain to so many people. Guess some people find it hard to listen when they think that you're automatically wrong. Lets start from the beginning and hope some enlightened people read this. We created Vixen Radio, as we wanted to enhance our own events and keep The Rompa Bar a nice fun place to be. We are not trying to compete with anybody; yet, I know we have indirectly made ourselves competition. All we want is to provide some extra small entertainment in our area of the world."

"I see, I also heard that Aktrez err.. Your boss has let you take full responsibility for Vixen Radio." She keeps tapping and writing onto her comm with the plastic pen presumably writing up the conversation.

"Yes that is the truth and I welcome it. So if anyone has any problems they should come speak to me and not Aktrez."

"Is there anything else you'd like to say before we finish up?" A light on her comm started blinking red probably meaning its energy was running low.

"Yes I do, I just want to say that I take full responsibility for My DJ's and will do my best to keep Vixen Radio broadcasting to patrons at the bar and other organized events."
