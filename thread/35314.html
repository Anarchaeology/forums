Whatever you do, DON'T JUMP!
So at the end of a 18ish team mission, we're splitting loot. I didn't really want anything, so I was just jumping around to waste time. I get caught in the ceiling and 5 seconds later I'm at a reclaim booth. I somehow got stuck up there and it took 996 damage off of me until I was dead. I was 300k from level 142.

Morale of the story is don't jump in missions, walk equidistant from the walls and stay away from funiture.
