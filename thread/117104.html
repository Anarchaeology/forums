It does seem unlikely that Neutrals have atrox reproduction facilities, and though Clan _might_, my guess would be that any facilities the Clans do have aren't producing as many Atrox as OT's facilities.

So, here's my theory: Atrox are born neutral, and emerge from the breeding facility fully matured, and that the breeding tanks have some sort of tutoring devices or what have you installed. Atrox were intended to work after all, so it seems likely that the growth from fetus to mature Atrox doesn't take more than a 1-5 years, aided by nano tech and whatever else is in those breeding facilities.

And there you have my Atrox theory.
