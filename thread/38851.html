For all those who replied with affirmation, get your friends to read and sign this if they like the idea of unrestricted PvP. If 12 people sign this, chances are, FC people won't even notice it, and really make a fourth carebear server somewhere in Korea... on the other hand if 12 hundred people respond, it might make them think about throwing together some hardware to accommodate the PvPers.

If anyone with some pull in FC organization reads this thread by some pff chance, I'd like to point out that every other big MMPORPG has at least one PvP server, and all of them are a success. I personally know at least five dozen of old UO PvPers who quit UO and AC's Darktide (PvP) server to play DAoC. (I did too, but for some reason DAoC gameplay just bores the hell out of me, so I decided to give AO another try.) DAoC's Mordred server was a HUGE success, in case you haven't heard. I had to wait on average around half hour just to log onto it, because if was full and didn't allow any more people on until the maximum number of users dropped below the allowed cap. This prompted DAoC to set up a second PvP server, "Andred", within the first two weeks of Mordred's release. I really like AO, it has awesome graphics and great concepts, but as it is now, I don't recommend it to any of my like-minded friends due to the lack of fulfilling PvP system... But it would change in an instant had this particular wish come true. And FC would probably gain a considerable revenue increase, since many of the people who quit due to boredom would come back to play the game they used to like a lot, as well as new users would try it out just as they did with DAoC.
