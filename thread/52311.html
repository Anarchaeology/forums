Job Positions at the Rompa Bar
Vixen Entertainment and the Rompa Bar would like to announce that we are currently accepting applications for the following positions:

Security Personell - Responsible for maintaining and enforcing a safe and secure enviornment for the Rompa Patrons, Staff and event guests

Waitstaff - Responsible for serving drinks and food to our loyal Rompa patrons.

Dance Team- The Dance team is responsible for providing clean and fun entertainment on a nightly basis for our Rompa Customers including, but not limited to, nightly floor shows and sync dance numbers. Our dance team will also be performing at events and parties all over Rubi-Ka.

Events Staff/PR team - Responsible for assisting Aktrez with events and party requests that come in. Also responsible for assisting Mechfix with any PR runs or posts that come up.

Journalist - We are looking for 1 or 2 staff journalists to document any events or social happenings in Rompas. Please be prepared to submit a sample of your writing for consideration

We are also in need of a few Computer programers. Please speak to Mechfix about details.

To apply for one of these postions - Please send an e-mail to
VEjobs@yahoo.com with your name and qualifications. Be sure to also include the position you are interested in applying for. The appropriate department manager will contact you with furthur details.

Thank you for your interest in Vixen-Entertainment.
We hope you will be joining us soon!

~*~ Aktrez - Director of Vixen Entertainment ~*~
