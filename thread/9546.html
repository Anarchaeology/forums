Roots can be broken, there is no doubt about that.

When I hit rooted mobs with my nanos, the roots sometimes break. If I hit a rooted mob with my uber-pillow-of-133tn355, then the root may break. As an NT, I keep my smallest nano on hand. If I'm rooted, I target myself and fire off this nano to attempt to break the root.

In my time of playing AO, I have only once seen a root last its duration whilst I've been beating on it.
