Circle G - Always looking for talented people
Circle G's Rubi-Ka division is always on the lookout for neutral citizens who want to join us.

What is Circle G?
We are a neutral organization, specializing in security, intelligence, guarding, escort and retrieval of personnel and goods.

OOC: What is Circle G?
We are a role-playing guild. Our in character actions are not always popular, and sometimes ride the border between legal and illegal, moral and immoral, and opinion's tend to differ depending on which side of the conflict you're on.

What does the G stand for?
That is a company secret. The only way to find out is to be hired.


Are you interested? Do you want to know more? Interested persons should contact Dreammate, Madrugar, or Boinky to start the interview process. If you can't reach our board members, you can contact Glarawyn, Garotta, or Whealer with questions, though you will eventually have to talk to our board members to be hired.

Don't miss out on our next big thing that will bring fame and fortune.

Circle G is an Equal Opportunity Employer, all breeds, sexes, professions, and levels welcome.
