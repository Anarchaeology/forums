NEUTRALS - stand for what you believe in!
Hi, and thanks for reading. If you've got this far than I guess you are a Neut and are considering a guild/clan/organisation/whatever. So who are we I hear you ask. We're the Independent Rubikans, and we publish a local newsfeed out of Borealis which discusses Neutral issues and whats going on around us. The IRN (Independent Rubikan News) is already setting it self up as a popular source for Neutrals to tell the world whats on their mind, and we average over 1000 readers per day *huge grin* (with a record set last week of just under 2600).

The guild is connected to the paper, but not in a heavy way. If you wish to write for us and tell us what you've encountered, seen, heard than please do. If you just wish to meet some new friends, share equipment and skills and work together for the benefit of Neutrals (partically in these testing times with FC possibly going to allow some of our towns to be lost to the factions) then we would love to hear from you.

If you are interested in joining please contact Silenna (our presidente) or one of our Advisors (Nyadach, Artoxon, DrTiny, or Fractaloon) and they can discuss the IR with you. If you want to see us in action (if thats the right word for the way our meetings usual decend into histerics) you can come over see a meeting at 10pm GMT on Sundays in our guild house just outside Borealis (see map and pictures on IRN site).

Hope to see you soon, and maybe as a member of Rubi-Ka's largest Neutral Guild (which is the IR btw).
