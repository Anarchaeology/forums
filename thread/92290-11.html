I can understand the trimming of the official boards if it is for more efficient managing. However, the number of sections being cut is excessive. Why does Funcom want to diffuse a thriving community by pushing it to other fansite forums? /me shakes head

This is the impression Funcom is giving me - your customer who has been around since Beta 4 and who has years of service industry, media analysis and performing arts experience.

* This move does not appear to re-focus support, instead it overwhelmingly reeks of a move that will divide a thriving community and diffuse that same community's voice.

The value of a collective voice outweighs any single voice effort (email, pm, irc, etc). A smart company is one that will harness such a collective voice to its benefit and to the benefit of the playerbase, hence a win-win situation. This move looks a lose-lose situation in the long run.

** This move does not appear to re-focus support for the playerbase, instead it blatantly appears to bolster Funcom's support for 3 specific fansites.

The fansites do their bit for the game, kudos to them. But, for goodness sake, if people don't already flock to them in droves, it does say something about the demerits of the site's drawing power, doesn't it?

Furthermore, I will be like most players around here - I visit the official forums for convenience and to present my voice and to listen to the other voices that make up the collective voice of the community here. I will balk at having to visit 3 other sites with popup-ads, suffering email spam as a consequence of providing an email address, etc ... all just for a game I pay supposedly to get enjoyment. As a customer I want convenience and enjoyment, not added hassle.

*** If Funcom doesn't have time for 1 board, it won't have time for 4.

Please do not pull wool over our eyes. If you do not have the resources, you do not have the resources simple as that ... people can see and understand. By trying to smooth it over with 're-focussing excuses' and weak denials of the consequence ... only insults the intelligence of your customers and lowers respect for your company.

**** Do not flippantly dismiss the disappointment being expressed by the players here. Address their concerns with solid reassurances.

Understand that you are disappointing the community by such a move. A community that has developed over time and one that is about to be broken down. A community that was allowed to build up because you allowed it as such by the very forum sections you provided. People do not like to see stuff they build up being torn down, it's psychological. It's a let-down, pure and simple.

----

I suggest keeping the RP section and make what's left of your role-players happy. Crowd-control is already making a joke of the MMO part of the mmorpg, please don't make a further joke of the RPG part of the mmorpg as well. Your broken storyline was already a major disappointment to many who were drawn to AO by the initial storyline premise in the first place. And with Shadowland coming, AO is looking more fantasy than sci-fi. Players need to find creative ways for continuation and bridging. Creativity fuels interesting interactions ingame - that plus the friendships bonded along the way.

I suggest keeping the Tradeskills section also. The bulk of your playerbase is more concerned with PvM than PvP. Notum Wars may be a booster but has become a PvP aspect of the game that got too much prominence over the core game itself lately. With or without NW, players still want to figure out tradeskill processes and share findings and bugs, all of which are beneficial to the ENTIRE playerbase.

I suggest keeping the patch section - it would be so much easier for you to gather all the post-patch problems and bugs at one spot. And your players will feel more assured seeing problems being reported by others and acknowledged by FC. Be more ruthless with off-topic posts in that section so that it stays relevant ... after a while players will learn to stay on topic for that particular section.
