The l33ts and the n3wbs of PvP in AO
"I have met very few people that are not cowards and will stand their ground in 1 on 1 if they're losing in 2h0"

I have to take a beef with this comment by Highjack in the fixer thread, even though its way off topic now, just for the hell of it. I heard this conception alot in UO too, and I just never understood it. To this day it still boggles my mind that people could call you a coward for accurately evaluating that you aren't likely to win a fight and escaping to save you the discomfort of death. It sounds like common sense to me.

Why would you intentionally throw 5-8 minutes of your time away recouperating from death to keep up some big ego trip about your "honor" thats staked into winning a fight with some guy in a computer game. I just don't get it. I'm not your employee, I'm not obligated to perform the service of "letting you kill me" to amuse you. I owe you no debt. I am playing this game for my enjoyment, why would I waste 8 minutes of my life doing a favor for someone who I don't know.. especially for someone I don't know who's trying to kill my charachter off.

Maybe if I was dueling with a friend to the death I'd be willing to go through with it, but hell.. with all the other smart mouthed punkos running around on the PvP field? Cmon..

Can someone experienced in dealing with this logic please explain to me how it has anything to do with being a "coward", as opposed to making the smart choice in combat? In UO there were tons of unspoken and ridiculous yet somehow "logical" rules of honor in PvP. What seperated the "l33t" from the "n3wbs" (as far as the general PvP populations attitude at least) in my experience was not how effective they were in combat, but whether or not they stuck to the rules of "l33t PvP". For instance, a l33t PvPer never ran from a fight unless they were being attacked by too many assailants, or their opponent was "cheating" and using tactics that l33t people are not explected to be able to beat. A l33t PvPer also was never a melee, always a mage. Melee were simply not l33t because not as much macro manipulation was involved (Though this is actually debatable. A well played melee utilized a large array of macros that the mage didn't use). Anyone playing a melee was automatically a "n3wb", even though in many situations melee was a more effective method of combat. I won fight after fight after fight using fighting tactics that were not "l33t", and had alot of fun doing it. Not just because I was winning, but because the tactics I was using were interesting and fun, and definitely not less complicated than the supposed "l33t" tactics.

Anyway, sorry to bore you all with a long rant about UO, but I hope you saw my parallells. Whenever I see this attitude in a PvPer in AO it really irks me.. makes me want to run my mouth off at him for awhile. I'll do it here instead. And yes, I made that the title in hopes of pulling in readers looking for their name on a list, I'm just a bastard like that >;D
