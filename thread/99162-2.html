The Skill Lock jobeclusters have a marginal effect on specials refresh rates - because they reduce the refresh rate in percentages.

E.g. a Shining QL200 Skill Lock jobecluster would reduce all refresh rates by 5% - for specials recycling in 10 seconds, this would be 9.5 seconds instead.

I suppose these Jobeclusters have more effect on long skill lock times.
