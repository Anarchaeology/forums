Great Read!
I received the book via Fed Ex yesterday after ordering it just 2 days ago. I am already on page 207! I can't put this book down.
I am an avid yet particular reader and I TOO must admit having reservations about the quality of the novel but they were layed to rest rather quickly.

For those of you who are familiar with "The Longest Journey" you would recognize that Tornquist seems to have a knack at weaving a great story while working within the boundries of a PC Game/RPG environment. You don't even have to be a fan of Sci/Fi to enjoy his work in "Prophet Without Honor". It is an extremely fast paced, suspenseful and unpredictable piece of work that keeps you glued to the page way past your bedtime.

While it probably won't win any Literary Guild Awards due to it's genre and the fact that this is Tornquist's first full length novel, I believe that the author has a promising future and I would recommend this book to anyone, AO RPG'er or not.
