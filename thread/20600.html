MatCre
I'm always puzzled when people say shield nanos are not worth it. Fairly often I am solo or in a team with no shield nano profession. Would you play without you Flowers helmet? You're Obtru helmet? DeCranum's? How about your Decus coat?

Well I do, actually. I do not wear the Decus coat for stylistic reasons. Lucky for me, I am rarely the primary tank and I have very high evades. I can tell you, though, that I am frequently asked for my "best shield nano" by the tank. Transcendent Shen is -62 damage per hit for melee and -53 for all other types.

You could argue that it is a drop in the bucket, but that is a bad argument. Stack -62 damage per hit with a pair of ReflectAC rings, a soldier reflect shield and a full set of of QL200 armor (or DeCranum if you prefer) and what do you have? Extremely good protection.

16 hits and you just saved 1K HP with Transcendent Shen. And then of course there is always Form of Tessai. Which is a goofy spell but offers slightly higher AC protection at the expense of agility and run speed. But again, consider that as a precious gift to a Soldier that can spare those two things and will benefit greatly from the AC.

Yes, the cost for MatCre is substantial (at lower levels). We can get by without it because we are masters of evasion (evade, parry, riposte). But as a gift to your team, it is well worth the investment. If you are soloing, you can ignore it. Later on you will probably party, unless you like leveling at 1/2 or 1/3 the rate of other people. At that point, rethink the costs. It is a valuable skill to have.

Oh, just for reference, I didn't train MatCre until level 140. But now I work on it for the above reasons and because I have some IP to spare on "secondary" things.

My suggestions for implants for MA's and IP placement:
First, SI, PM, TS and BM are the only nano skills that you should be focusing on. Make implants for these for SG, VS, LMA, UVC...
Shining Head: SensImp
Bright Chest: BioMet
Faded Ear: PsyMod
Bright Eye: PsyMod
Faded Eye: SpaceTime
Bright Right Hand: SpaceTime
This will keep your Crit buff nano line most even. (keep the skills maxed)
Do ALL Agility Clusters and keep Agility maxed, MA is based 50% on Agility.
Sense does not need to be raised too much in my opinion.
Keep Intelligence up the to keep nano skills high.
Strength and Stamina keep maxed for Brawl (Sta for HP as well)
Body Dev, this is obvious, always keep it maxed on all profs, more hp means you stay alove longer
Nano Pool....up to you, as a MA you will find yourself as a makeshift doc alot (more at higher lvls), if you have 2 Healing MA's in your group, it is usually better than 1 Doc
Keep MA and Brawl Maxed of course....Dimach..if you plan to PvP (or use Shen) or have the spare IP (good luck, IP is hard to come by on MA's)
Parry, only if you plan to use BwT (can't use attacks if you wield a weapon), as the sheilds don't help much and you don't Parry red enemies.
Back to Nano Skills dept...MatCrea (for the AC buffs) and MatMet (for the 2 Strength buffs (+12 and +25) that don't stack with IC or Prodigous Strangth)........crap in my opinion, I plan on reclaming every bit of IP from these 2 skills come IPR.
Physic Init....raise it if you can spare the IP...
Evades....same story (they really only work if you go down from AGG to DEF, which means you'll have to raise PI)
Also, on your implants, do all three Max HP Clusters, a set of 125's adds about 550hp, very helpful.
I don't know what else I forgot but I've been playing around with skillemu and NanoNanny alot lately, waiting for IPR.
