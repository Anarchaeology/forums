The Steps of MAdness were designed so that the "lower level cap" of players able to complete the dungeon were 35-45.

However, that doesn't mean a lvl40 should even remotely consider entering on their own. It's necessary to have at least one - preferrably two or up - balanced team with strong medical support, massive firepower and so forth.
Tactics are necessary. It was supposed to be a challenge, not a walk in the park.
