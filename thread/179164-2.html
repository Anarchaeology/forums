I dont know if I quite like this idea. First the Aura of Revival really does not scale up so its advantageous to us at Lv200. Its basically crap for the amount of perk points that need to be spent there.

Reaver- Dont discount this perk skill, maxxed out it will give you +180 2he which is huuge! especially if were going to be equipping those insane requirement 2he weapons in SL. Plus you get 4 extra attacks in which you will do 3715-6843 extra damage if you use all 4.

Street Samuri- this one is also pretty nice adding 20 to 2he 200 to each parry,riposte and Fast Attack. Plus adding 187 to all damage types. But the specials are kinda lacking. The jury is still out on this one. the key to this perk skill will be the usefulness of Parry/repostle and fast attack to equip the high end SL weapons.

Blessing- the key here is the additional 2770 Hit Points when fully maxed. This is a huge HP addition and should help our tanking ability. The specials are a HoT Lay on Hands and a Reflect AC shield.
