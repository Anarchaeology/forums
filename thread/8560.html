This is needed - Area Chat Dampening
One of the problems that I have with FunCom's non-combat events are the massive number of players who go just to disrupt them by /shouting all sorts of garbage. Would it be possible to have areas that would allow only designated players to send chat to the vicinity? A GM or Ark could then serve as moderator and allow/disallow players to add to the conversation.

For example, take the 'trial' that was held a couple of months ago. If the room would have had this limited chat, then the judge, lawyers, and defendants would have been the only ones allowed to talk in the area, but the audience would be able to hear all that was said (and be able to /tell others). If a witness was needed to testify, he/she would be asked to come forward and the GM/Ark would then run the macro enabling them to talk. If the person got abusive, this could always be rescinded.

In another example, say an ICC contact came to talk to the Clans (on a wild whim :-). He would be at the front of the room and be one of the few people allowed to talk. The specators would be requested to sit. When it would be time for questions, the players would /tell the event moderator that they have a question, then the moderator would ask the player to stand then give the command for them to speak.

Is this possible?
