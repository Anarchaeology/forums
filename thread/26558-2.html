People are goobers.
A lot of amazingly uninsightful things have been said on this thread, and it is the mightiest act of will to not respond to every one of them. Instead, I'll just say that Funcom seems to have dealt with the entire issue in good faith. Anyone who accuses them otherwise may a) be ignoring the fact that they're human or b) has not experienced CS in another MMORPG.

Funcom, Verant, and others, are blazing trails through ground that has never been seen before. There's not even a standard to hold them up to yet, except each other. And there's little precedent for them to follow.

I'm surprised no one has yet mentioned the connection between reimbursing, account trading, and Ebay. This complicates things trememdously for these companies. If Funcom credits have cash value, then duping Funcom credits is just this side of counterfeiting. Same goes for equipment. Reimbursement becomes very tricky when it's more than just game balance on the line.

This also affects transferring accounts. If it were legal to sell accounts, a group of players could band together and actually "produce" characters for sale by powerlevelling and camping the best spots to equip their "products." This would adversely affect everyone trying to actually build their own character as the best spots and loots become less and less available to them.

The more one thinks about all the ramifications, the more problematic it becomes.

Many players attribute Funcom's actions to them having a crowbar wedged into their nether regions. But when you think about it, most of the things MMORPG companies do make sense from an overall fairness standpoint. A no-reimbursement policy makes sense for the good of the majority of players. If you want to complain about losing items to bugs or phantom damage, you are perfectly justified, and there's threads for that. But a more lenient reimbursement policy is not a good answer for the game. The answer _must_ come from the other side, from bug fixes.

/change topic

Funcom seems to do well with communication. Funcom reps regularly read the board I normally haunt, and sometimes post there. No, I'm not telling you which one it is, cause then the d00dz might find us. Verant had to take down the EQ forums because customer feedback turned into a shoutfest where legitimate suggestions and concerns got bumped off page one in minutes by organized groups who whined in unison for their class or issue, building up huge threads full of sound and fury. I hope that doesn't happen here. A noisy thread isn't always shouting what's best for everyone in the game.
