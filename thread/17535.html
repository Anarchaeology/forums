A shout out to a kind Enf.
So there we were, in the foyer to the Camelot Castle. What started out as a simple 3-person scouting mission through the dungeon became what was supposed to be a full team romp, that should have been complete with some decent loot and cool memories. Going through the process of getting the team together took in upwards of an hour (I'm sure most of you know the tribulations associated with getting a team together, especially when it is spontaneous) due mostly to the fact that one member had to travel back from CAV (Central Artery Valley).



ANYHOW. We charge into the front room only to get bum rushed by a couple of omnis (credit where credit is due: Gellana & Jacksr). So we're talkin' about 2 lvl 190+ vs. our team which ranged from 78-121 (no, we weren't considering the dragon, just a friendly romp), you all know what the outcome was, luckily I was the only one who died, the rest of my team bailed out smartly. So I get my stuff outta reclaim and quickly run back into the dungeon to see the rest of my team that decided to stick around and those 2 omni. Having experienced being blindsided in the past, I have gotten over the rage that used to overcome me, and just wanted to chat in a civil manner; so I ask "So tell me, did you two really wait in there for over an hour just to kill what you would consider a nubi?". I got no reply, but a high level clan atrox enforcer happened in there at about the same time. His name (It's name?) was Tortureyou.

Ok, all omni affiliated folks who have been cheering, prepare to be silenced. Clan brethren, grab your popcorn.

I ask Tortureyou if he saw those 2 omni. He acknowledged and I asked him to please bash them with his I-beam when he got a chance. He replied by asking if we wanted them removed, and I let him know that THAT would be ideal. He says he would go take a look, and before I could buff up, he had dispatched Gellana.

Monrakashi: Wow.
Cedari: Jeepers, that was quick.
Tortureyou: Yep, about 3 hits =)
Darkwizard: Awesome
Monrakashi: Ty so very much!

He then proceeded to buff those of us who had the NCU space and actually stood guard at the entrance at the bottom of the stairs! He even scared off another high level Omni who had come along (Nallepuuh). This Atrox was unbelievable! I was very happy to see such a kind act, especially as it followed a very unkind act. Tortureyou, in my opinion, deserves every bit of kindness that the clans can provide, and I plan on letting my guild know that he should be buffed on every sighting.

Hope y'all enjoyed my little story.

-Mon
