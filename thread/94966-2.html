Just a quick question: why not go for a format similar to nanoformulas?

1.) Nanoformulas are color coded by type. Combat nanos are always red, protection nanos always green, medical nanos blue, space nanos purple, and psi nanos yellow. This also breeds a familiarity with what skills a nano is likely to need. Blue USUALLY will need BioMet and MatMet, Yellow PsyMod and SensImp (there are always exceptions, of course).

The new icons that nanoclusters have seem to work in a similar way, but some clusters seem off (if I recall, Nimbleness is one example, but it's been awhile since I looked.) Why not make a simpler schema of color coordination based on skill type? Say, all blue clusters affect abilities, all green clusters affect Protection, all purple clusters affect Nano & Aiding, etc. The point is that when someone sees a cluster they instantly know what skill set it affects (or what implant type it fits into, if you color code by location).

2.) Rename clusters to start with Nano Cluster (like Nano Crystal).

3.) Add a second statement for Faded, Bright, Shining.

4.) Add skill type affected OR location used, your choice (so they all clump together alphabetically)

5.) Add skill affected.

So, a shining nano cluster that affects BioMet would be:

A purple sphere with the name NanoCluster (Faded): Nano & Aiding (BioMet)

What you'd end up with in the shop is a clump of purple clusters that say:

NanoCluster (Faded): Nano & Aiding (BioMet)
NanoCluster (Faded): Nano & Aiding (FirstAid)
NanoCluster (Faded): Nano & Aiding (MatCrea)
NanoCluster (Faded): Nano & Aiding (MatMet)
NanoCluster (Faded): Nano & Aiding (PsyMod)
NanoCluster (Faded): Nano & Aiding (SensImp)
NanoCluster (Faded): Nano & Aiding (Time&Space)
NanoCluster (Faded): Nano & Aiding (Treatment)

Followed by a clump of green clusters that affect Protection and preceded by a clump of white clusters that affect Melee (I may be missing a skill type in there, but you get the idea). Or you could go a different route and color code by location.

What this requires is changing the icons (did it once, not sure how hard it is) and the names. I prefer the skill type/skill option, since we all have readily available a list of skill types and skills simply by opening the window. This way, you'll instantly be able to piece together the name by looking at your skill window.

The only clusters that MAY be a problem here are Jobe clusters, but I don't think they're sold in general stores anyway, and a way around that would be NanoCluster (Faded): Jobe (NanoDelta) for example.
