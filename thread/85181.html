Re: This whole reporting thing
It's unfortunate that someone would have such an obscenely poor sense of humor as to report a guy for making a joke like the Ebay reference in Fenris' signature. However, given the state of intelligence on the internet (which I generally refer to as "a vast wasteland," with due respect to Newton Minnow), I'm not entirely that shocked.

The fact is, people have no sense of humor on the internet, which is due largely to the fact that sarcasm doesn't travel well over a digital medium. Actually, sarcasm travels across such a medium as well as over a telephone, but one has to have a certain skill for detecting it as abject sarcasm rather than truth. Personally, I think the Ebay reference in Fenris' signature is rather clever due to the fact that there is a certain rust-colored ring of truth to it. Ergo, when So-And-So read that he'd have to either buy it for real-world cash or for several million credits, he probably wigged out and blamed the messenger; in this case, Fenris.

You know, sure, it's unfortunate that you can't go out and kill the mythical Phantom Leet and get a GA4 instruction disc. It's too bad that a lot of us will never use Grid Armor at all. And it's even too bad that your alt doesn't have enough credits or intelligence to be able to twink your other Level 1 alt into GA4. It's a horrible, horrible world, I know. But if you're too stupid to realize that the Ebay reference is a bit of a joke, and feel the overwhelming need to be the third person of the day to open up a new thread asking, "Where can I find Grid Armor?!" without consulting this simple link first, then I have no idea how you get through your day without having to have someone remind you to breathe periodically.

I've been looking for proof that Darwin was wrong, and that maybe mankind is indeed going back to the ocean from whence his ancestors came. I do believe that whoever reported Fenris may be the proof I need to refute Darwin and prove my own theory of devolution. Thank you.
