Clan Quizotic (RK2)
We are a dedicated band of RP Clan members. A tight nit group, that groups regularly, often with two or more groups in the same place crushing our opposition. Our members have a wealth of knowledge and expiernce in the world of Rubi-Ka, and help one another complete each others goals and missions. Most of all we are focused on having fun, extreme gaming and killing omni scum!

Our member base consists primarily of 100+ players, and as such dont have that much to offer for lower level players. Only positive people with ingame skill and maturity are welcome.

Hope you are driven to join us!
