Envisioned: Faction
One thing that will definatly make me quit this game for SWG is the lack of Faction, or nasty things that might happen for killing the wrong people.

I do realize the game is Anarchy Online. True Anarchy would mean there is no Supression Gas anywhere, in that case I could see not having some kind of system in place where you would lose respect with your side for killing your own.

On the other hand, we do have supression gas... and in this case there should be severe problems for an Omni that kills Omni for example.


As it stands there is not. SWG has this option, and should AO not have it come release .... I feel you will lose many people.


Including myself.


Big loss, no you can't have my stuff (Friends would get it) .. but the NPC's just don't seem interactive.


For a game far above Everquest in technology .. Anarchy Online sure has a few things to learn from them.


If I kill an Omni Guard, I should have a loss of respect .. or some kind of nasty happening, there should be an internal fear of killing your own.


However you institute that is up to you entirely ... I for one would love to see it happen.
