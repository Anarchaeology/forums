Couldn't agree more
Just adding my concern about the MoBs (especially human ones) in missions now.

One of the greatest concepts in AO is the mission concept. A way to make money, make exp, and the tokens were brilliant. In every respect you really have succeded in making a game that is FUN to play...low down time...ability to solo....good game content.

Then suddenly everything changes. Missions no longer pay out the money one needs to support ones character. MoBs in missions become impossible to fight.

Many are now afraid you are going the way of EQ and others by making the game harder and harder to play (read that less and less fun). Most of us left the other games and came to AO to get away from that frustration.

It has never made sense to me that as a character increases in levels the game makes it more difficult to get ahead. It is as though levelling is penalized. It shouldn't be the oposite either...it should be...balanced.

I know you are trying to do the right thing and sometimes it takes a while to shake the naughty bugs out. Just want you to know, however, that your origianal concepts were RIGHT!!!! Stick with them. Resist going down the road of all those other games everyone is leaving

AO is the kind of game I have been looking for. Please don't make it into a clone of all the others.
