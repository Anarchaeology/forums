Supposed to be?
Errr... I think 'supposed to be' fell out of AO a LONG time ago.

Support class these days are MPs as self-mobile Moch dispensers, and thats it.

Surely Enfs MAs and Sols were 'supposed to be' the un-rivalled Layers Down Of The Unholy Smackdown, and currently Enfs are indeed doing that, but the MAs are lying in foetal positions in the corner of the room whimpering and the Sols have just started suspecting that maybe that long hard winter might, conceivably, come to an end.

Surely Traders and Docs weren't 'supposed to be' walking around the battle field whistling a happy Broadway tune, and killing everything in sight?

If there is two profs that has 'support class' written all over it is Doc and Trader, and yet there they are, happily wiping the blood splatter of their faces, feared and respected by all.

Surely Fixers were never 'supposed to be' tanking uber mobs 80 lvls above them without breaking a sweat? Yet there they are, laying the smackdown on The One with only a HoT running whilst declaring that GA4 is soooo weak, and isn't it time that Fixers got some love too soon?

This was never a nerfing thread at all, and I never called for nerfing anyone (well, not true, I did for the fixers a little bit, but I was trying to flame, and I didn't really mean it! And my flame failed miserably anyways...), the thread was about some of the other profs, maybe, getting some of that good stuff too.

In this case something that would make it easier to get back from the middle of nowhere in an easy and timely manner, and NOT a GA nuke, uber Toothbrush of Doom or 10k AC gear, and would incidentally make it easier for us to hunt and solo in faraway places, and therefore not clutter up the battle field with our unseemly gimped carcasses.

What the non Fixer/Enf/Doc/Trader classes are trying to do here is to find something good outside of the PvP area, only to have the same people who OWN us pop up and say:

'I'm sorry but it's totally out of the question for you to get that, because even though it doesn't actually threaten my personal safety in any way, and is in fact totally irrelevant to combat, be it PvP or PvM, it will make me feel a little bit less special about myself, and therefore you are de facto calling a nerf on my class. So there!"

Nobody wanted to make the fgrid public property, nobody asked for an insta-grid battle field evac opportunity, all we asked for was something that will get us back to civilization in 3 min instead of half an hour.

Was that REALLY too much to ask? Are the blessed profs really so cheap that they can't stand to see other profs get just a little? Even the crumbs from the table?

There is NO 'supposed to be' in this game, and the upside of that is that it is constantly changing. Heck, in 2 patches MPs could be out there in the arena shredding EQB Enfs like Kleenex, or maybe one fine day on the blood streaked grass around a Controller you will hear the cry of 'OMG! An engie is here, run away!'

Not very likely maybe, but I hear tell that Fixers at one time weren't all that uber.

What comes around, goes around...
