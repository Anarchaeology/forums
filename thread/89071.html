You live in the past

Actually there is way more to do at high levels now than at low levels, and with teammissions it is now possible for everyone to reach level 200 in a decent amount of time, even for people who have to work. The XP/level curve is exactly right at this time, everyone slows down leveling the higher they get. But it is not not too slow as it was before team missions where most people just stopped leveling at some point. With the Primus camp, a decent high end outdoor leveling spot was added for the first time as well.

The differences between classes widened and a good amount of high end items/nanos for each class was introduced since the launch and makes it extremely interesting to play more than one profession up to a high level to test out the full potential.

After recovering from the shock of level requirements for nanos and items a while ago, which took me maybe a week, I think it is one of the best things Funcom ever did and helped to make high level gameplay a lot more interesting than before.
