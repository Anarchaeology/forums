Apartment Improvements
Firstly, I think that Apartments are one of the best things about this game. Having a world where you own a little corner that you can decorate as you see fit brings MMORPG's to that whole new level of reality emulation. I know that AO is in constant development, and the Funcom guys probably can't keep up with all the bugs fast enough to fully devote themselves to game improvement. Therefore, I suggest (and reiterate to some degree) the following ideas pursuant to those aforementioned.

- Increase the number of domestic items for apartments:
- More sofas, chairs, tables, toilets, bathtubs, beds, desks, bookshelves, counterspace, sinks, ovens, etc etc etc. More quantity and variety.
- The ability to put more than ten (10) items in the apartment should be the first thing to go if more stuff is implemented.

- The ability to have furniture that holds other items, like the bank does. Perhaps a wardrobe that holds your favorite outfit, or a footlocker that holds loose items, or a fridge full of food.

- The ability to put one item on top of another. I want my lamp to go on a table, or a refrigerator, or something else (within reason of course, I don't think your sofa would fit on the houseplant very well)...

- The ability to move items once placed, for a fine-tuning to the placement of your objects.

- More rooms, to an extent. The apartment I live at in real life has more than one room. Or upgrades to better apartments as a character gains levels, prestige, company kickbacks, etc.

- The ability to change the location of your apartment. I would like to move from Omni I Ent. backyard to the Trade Dist. or somewhere else. Maybe for a fee?

- The ability to hand people a one-use copy of your apt key to have them over for tea, or maybe make a shady deal. Or even a permanenet copy to your friend.

- I like the Guild apartment idea. But rather than apartments, perhaps separated Guild Houses (have I seen signs for these in areas?) to gather at for discussion, trade, or whatever with your organization.

- Interaction with objects. This addresses a whole new issue, I understand, but it sure would be cool to sleep on a bed, or open a fridge, turn on/off lamps, sit correctly on couches, etc.

- Speaking of turning on/off lamps, you should be able to have a light switch to the main lights that are always on in your place.

- Objects in apartment should also be solid, so you can't walk thru them.

I know these are mostly far-off suggestions that would add another element into an already complex game, but I think that it would interest more people, possibly tap an entirely new market (we all know how well "The Sims (tm)" did with its 3 or so expansions...)

-AK
