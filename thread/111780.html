This was posted by "AuntBeast" in the test server forum on a long thread I had started asking for some testing with buffs and a possible fix for high lvl outside buffs on low lvls. I think this sums up what a rp'er goes through trying to play land controll and pvp.


Hi, several of you probably know me from Wolf Brigade.

This game has level restrictions. I got a week off of work and levelled past my regular team mates, so I waited for them. We are a guild of newbies. Almost all of our players are below 100. For those of you playing for years, let me tell you how it goes for a newbie.

I see my husband playing a game that looks cool, I ask about it, download it, install it and start playing. I survive the backyard, progress to subway, still throwing away things that say "irreperable" cause I think they don't work. I love the gameplay, the teaming, the missions, the mobs. I'm beboppin' around having fun. I join an org. Org has tower. Need to get NW. Local stores don't have. Order it. YAY! Tower is under attack. I run my little fanny to the tower since they called for folks around level 20, I'm a 24! Whee! Finally get to check out this whole tower thing. I see the countdown, make sure my duraermanent pets are hip and ready to go. 3...2..1...reclaim
wha? What? How did that guy hit me for 800 damage? He's the same level as me? Huh?

Then twinking is explained to me. By then, I have found out that neutrals are scorned regularly by almost everyone. We get a crappy side xp bonus. But I like being a nuet, I can suck up the side xp. No problem. So I am getting myself as uber as my newbie self can, hey, dun mess with me, my pet is RED to me! Oops! Tower under attack. Leave clinic with 1/2 implants in, no armor on (needed to reip) run to tower, get my fanny kicked again. Bear in mind, I have not attacked a tower yet, just defended.

I have no problems twinkin' Hey, if ya can, go for it. More power to you. Yep, you got the cash, the time, the energy, the buffs, twink your little hearts out.

My question is. How on earth can you get any enjoyment out of killing an untwinked noob? You know and I know that it's like me going to the backyard and killing lvl 1 rollerrats. Slight personal satisfaction, a tad bit of revenge, but it only lasts about 5 seconds. Then I feel dirty, and small.


Our base was wiped out yesterday morning by two lvl 25 trader twinks. Fine, they were "more prepared" than we were. They get the land. What? What was that? They destroyed our base for sport? Oh, that's right CoA already has a type 1 controller, they don't need our land or want it. We spent five hours defending our base. Why? Because the attackers weren't polite enough to tell us they were just flexing their muscles at us and had no intention of CONTROLLING the land. After our CT was destroyed, I sat there for an hour waiting to be able to rebuild. No one came to try to claim the land.

Tower battles are not about land control. Tower battles are cheap, easy ways for twinks to get their pvp titles, flex their muscles and boost their ego. If they are anything else, I haven't seen it.

Oh, and it's a good way to dishearten newbies. It's not that they die. It's not that they die by people "better prepared" It's that they can spend 5 hours trying their hardest against pretty much invincible odds to find out that it was for NOTHING. Nada.
Zilch. The noobs are chum in the water and the twinks are hungry.

At least that big red boss at the end of the mission rewards you. Invariably, newbies walk away feeling dirty and used.

I am always so proud of my newbies when they keep getting killed and keep coming back, not because they think they might win, but because even if that tower means nothing to you, it's our tower, and we are proud of it.

Stop using lowbies as chum.
