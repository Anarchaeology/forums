Serious?
To be honest, most of this idea sounds to me like a player who is tired of there being people 'better' than he is in the game because A> they have more time to play or B> they have been playing the game longer overall.

You cannot seriously believe that a company will make money by finding more hardware, cutting down on their primary servers bandwidth, etc... just so they can offer a separate place to play for people who 'claim' to be hard-core 'elite' players. For one, how to you discriminate who those players are? Oh... they have a level 150 character on RK1.... ? how do we know they are the original player on that account?

There are problems enough getting things fixed that have been wrong with the game for almost the entire time its been open to the public (and I'm talking about bugs, not balance issues). Why in the world would you ask them to take time away from fixing things just so some people who aren't satified with playing with the rest of us can have their own server?

You claim you want better RP? How do you have good quality RP with a lower player base? You don't.

You claim you want only team missions because too many classes are able to get the reward without really doing the mission (ie... sneaking or mezzing a mission). Do you know what happens when you mezz a mission? You get no experience, no token, only a cash bonus and the item reward. Same thing for the agent who sneaks it. So yes, those folks may be able to get rewards that others cannot... but they're also generally professions that rely on a team when they need experience, because they are not good enough at combat to do it alone.

Stop looking for ways to make an 'elite server' and look for ways to make the ones that exist better. The time would be much better spent for all of us.
