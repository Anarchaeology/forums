How does QL apply to Dmg Stats for Guns?
I'm trying to figure out how the QL applies to the stats on a gun. For example, a QL 31 rifle I have has damage stats of "1-80(100)" (don't qoute me on that, I'm not looking at the game right now). My understanding is that the 1-80 is the 'normal' damage range, and the (100) is the 'critical hit' damage. So, if these are cumulative stats, then the maximum possible damage should be 180 (assuming the formula is to add the max normal and crit together). During combat, I've been able to hit for 290+ on occasion, so obviously I don't understand how the stats work. Can someone explain them to me please? Thanks!

Well, theres another factor, which is your attack rating.

Your attack rating is defined by your skills, for instance:

Your rifle has as attack skill 100% Rifle

If your rifle skill is 300, your attack rating is 300


Now, each 33 attack rating gives aproximately a 25% bonus to damage, therefore, if you have 132 attack rating your damage is doubled, in other words:

Instead of doing 1-80 (100) damage ull do 2-160 (200), making for a possible maximum critical of 360

To calculate your damage, i suggest checking out this webpage:
