A Protest!
As a member of the clans and a supporter of New Dawn, I would like to officially protest the actions of the Sentinels in Tir. They are currently attacking and killing, WITHOUT WARNING, anything that steps through the Whompas that is not clan. This includes neutrals.

Your over-aggressive actions are going to give all clanners a bad name.

What is your intentions anyway Mr Silverstone? to protect us from Omni-Tek or to alienate us from the rest of the galaxy??
