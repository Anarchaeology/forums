Complete Patch Notes
I have a slight problem with patch notes. At best, they contain 60% of the changes that were actually made.

I know this has been brought up in the past, but I feel it should be revisited again.

13.2, the small patch, off the top of my head, I can list 2 things that were never mentioned in the notes.

QL of loot in chests.

B&E skill actually needed to open locked chests.


I'm sure there are tons of others I'm missing in even this small patch. In larger patches, the unmentioned changes outnumber the mentioned changes.

All the changes are NOT bad. QL of loot in chests? VERY good, I like it, but it was not mentioned. B&E skill needed to open chests, finally, a use for a before-now defunct skill. However, had it been mentioned, I "might" have siphoned some IP into it before the patch went live, or at least saved some IP to put in it if I needed to.


We NEED to know these changes beforehand to plan and prepare for it.

Please try and get all changes gathered into the notes, good & bad. It's very frustrating to me at least, to not have these mentioned at all.


I'm going to skip over the auto-attack on cast thing, as many threads have suggested it's a bug and not meant to be there, and if that is truly the case, I understand it not being in patch notes. I just hope they fix that one soon. It's killing me.
