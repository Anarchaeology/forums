idea: /follow-me command
Hi all,

I got an idea the other day the while waiting for my team member to finish in the bathroom. I guess you all have had this happen to you several times, and will most likey happen again.

As the machine can go on and on and on, we, on the other hand, are merely humans... we need our little "coffee brakes" and what-not

What was annoying the last time this happened to me, was that we had a long walk to our mission, and she had, what seems to be a forever-taking bathroom brake... I was annoyed that she didnt /follow me before she left.

I really wish I would have a /follow-me command at that time! What do you think? Bad idea?

I know you guys are thinking "that could easily be misused", thats what Funcom did at least, and me! I dont think Funcom read my suggestion through. Because I also mentioned that a /follow-me command would only work in a team (for instance). Then, who would exploit it?

Just my idea! Share thoughts and comments plz
