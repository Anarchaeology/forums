After doing AOR and Bioshield as I could up till level 130, I think I'll be doing a complete turnaround (albeit slowly) now.

AOR looks rather worthless at higher levels. And bioshield, while great, takes up a lot of perk points I'd rather have for something I'd use more of. Don't get me wrong. It's saved my life countless times already, but I see my role as grabbing and maintaining aggro as vanishing for a large number of levels, if not forever. And I just can't justify spending all those perk points just for the few times I'd be 'tanking'.

So it looks like I'll be trying out just maxing Street Samurai and Reaver as I slowly untrain my perks. AOR first, to be used for Reaver, and then Bioshield, to be used for SS. Gives me some time to wean myself from Biorejuvination and cocoon. :/

AoR 10 - Despite what people say , it's a welcome addition to your heals.

Bioshield 10 - Cannot stress over and over how uber this is. Saved me SO many times I can't remember. Use it for soloing stuff in inferno, couldn't do anything without it IMO.

Street Samurai 10 - Damage adds are the way ahead - forget AR you'll be a max MBS cap most of the time. Heck I don't even need to implant 2he to get MBS cap so I use sinew of tarasque instead for the +damage. The finalising move is really nice and the stun immunity often overlooked.

Blessing 10 - More heals and a big chunk of HP buff. Also cures blindness and provides a strong reflect which when stacked with soldier reflects and reflect items+bioshield is heading towards TMS levels.

I really think this is about the best perk setup a keeper can get, will be interesting to see how it works at 220. I'll keep you informed !
