New network code being tested
We will be testing new network code on the Test Server later today. The goal is to reduce network traffic, which will help people on low bandwith connections when they get into areas with lots of traffic who encounter a lot of packet loss. You will not need a new client, since the code for this is already in the 14.1 (aka 14.07) client, we're simply turning it on now.

Naturally, if you are using some third-party tool, you might encounter problems after this change.

Looking good.
We had numerous crashes on the Test server the last days, because the debug code on the servers was detecting a bug in the GM playfield. Whenever an ARK, Developer or GM went there, it would go down pretty much immediately, taking all other playfields on that server down in the process and sending a bug report. The issue is fixed, but if you've had a lot of problems the last 3 days, that's probably why.

As for tools not working, I suppose there's no stopping the makers of it from making new ones anyway :-) I don't actually know whether or not these tools break the EULA (section 7, especially) or any of those weird US laws against reengineering, so I won't comment on them. But in general, we cannot test any of them inhouse (danger of trojans), and we do not want to either (I definitely have a lot to do as it is).

Yes, the data is now compressed. It cuts down the traffic by a factor of 3 to 4, and the extra cost in CPU seems to be negligible - I did some testing under simulated high load here, can't tell a real difference. So that's good.

On the question of what you'll notice on a highspeed line: Not that much, I guess. It will not improve your ping latency, and you should already not have a problem getting the traffic anarchy pushes out. Maybe you'll see a little faster load time when you first get into a crowded playfield, but I wouldn't bet on it, there's too much else going on beside network at that point.

My confirmed issues with this network test
I'll be sending all of this off in an email to Nizno as Liaker asked me to as well as posting it here.

1. Medinos pet. If any of the following conditions are met it stops healing and/or will not change heal targets:

- Its target is a PC and dies.
- Its target is another pet that dies, or is within 180 seconds of self-terminating.
- It falls below 50% hp itself.
- It is within 120 secs of self-terminating.
- Its target is in another room in a mission, even if line-of-sight exists.

Giving Medinos the /pet wait command 3 times in a row works about 25% of the time, but no other comands affect it. The other 75% of the time you simply have to destroy and recreate it.

Medinos also will not follow a PC heal target through a team mission floor-port. The owner has to give it the /pet follow command, port with it and then reassign it to the heal target.


2. MP Spells (maybe other classes as well). About every 4th or 5th casting my nuke heals the target for the hp adjustment rather than damaging it. Same effect occurs with both Mind Pain and Frost Slivers. Additionally, using Douse Anger on any robot-based NPC gains me aggro for the entire fight no matter where in the fight I cast it or what my pets do in the meantime.


3. Sync offset. Mobs dying and giving exp with 10-15% hp left on their bar graphic and pets still attacking it as it lays on the floor. This is opposite of what I've seen on Live lately where your mob shows no hp but continues to fight you for 10-20 seconds.

4. And, as many of you have posted, both AOMD and Click-Saver are currently busted. Using the newest version of WinPCap doesn't help, either. I agree that this adds a new facet of "suckiness" to the AO experience as your challenge should be IN the mission, not GETTING the mission.
