The offical: "What we want changed in 14.2" thread.
-- Grid armor requirements should be toned down so that a fixer of reasonable level with maxed nano skills and modest nano implants can put it on. Grid armor should not be made too easy because it was probably designed for fixers who maxed their nano skills at the cost of evades.

-- The LLS changes are good. Leaving the old LLS in is BAD (I have a QL 120 one, so I am not biased here)
All the old LLS should be transformed into the new ones. There are too many people that exploited to put on a QL 200 LLS.

-- Considering that the LLS was made with agents in mind you should make an agent LLS. This new one would have very high perception requirements, but would make up for it with lower tradeskill requirements. This agent LLS would be a quest item.

-- The IP reset should be MANDATORY. Everyone should be required to use the IP reset at least once. Code in something that denies players the ability to gain further EXP until they use the IP reset. This would be the magic bullet that solves all the problems of AO. Because you have to remove everything you have on to use the IP reset, it solves all the problems of people having uber stuff equiped that they should not (like QL 200 implants, QL 200 LLS, or 400 NCU... Some people have all of those without ever putting a point into comp lit, tradeskills, or treatment.). People can't use the excuse that they don't want to bother requiping their stuff again, because with 14.2 everything you have should be self equiped.

-- The newbie changes are fine, but they should stop at around level 15. By then you have a good grasp on the game. Level 30 is too long.
The issues that truely plauge newbies is the lack of equipment and cash. Make some "newbie gear" that is extermely cheap and sold in the start up stores.
The newbie gear would be a full set of armor, and have a large selection of weapons for aspiring newbies.
This gear would be useable at about level 5-10, and should be affordable to someone of that level. The newbie gear would be extremely good for it's level and cost, kind of like QL 15 gear with the requirements of QL 5 gear. By level 15 or 20 they will have to start buying and finding new equipment.

-- The agent items and nanos were put in to balance the poor profession. These items should not require doing 100's of team missions find them, or paying millions of credits for them. Balance is no good if only 3% of the population gets to expirence it. All the new agent nanos should be possible to find as mission rewards or loot, just like everyone else's nanos that can't be bought in a store. The agent's second line of damage buffs should probably be available in the stores if they are below QL 125 too.

-- Although the agent goodies are nice. They do not balance the profession, and this is not the fix all patch we were promised. Aimedshot, sneaking, concealment bugs, and crappy rifles are still important issues that were not addressed.

-- These changes make us a little more unique, but they do not make us anymore desired in teams, or any more viable as solo hunters. MP pets were our solo viability, and MA crit buffs were our team desirablity (at least when a real MA wasn't available).

-- We want a "/pet warp" command and we don't want to wait any longer. You "fixed" mob pathing by making them warp to the target if they can't see it. If it's good enough for mobs it's damn well good enough for pets. I see absolutely no way a /pet warp command could be abused.

That's all I could think of. Please post your changes to 14.2 if I have left anything out.

I didn't want to go into detail in that post but I elaborate here:

1. LLS was made primarly with agents in mind.

2. Agents and traders will be hurt the most by this change. In fact, with the loss of crit buffs, the LLS is required for them to function.

3. This item would not be agent specific, but it would require very high perception. THe perception requirements would be so high that only the Agent, Trader, Fixer, and Adventurer could viably use this device.

It just so happens that the 3 worst classes happen to be the only ones with green perception! This quest LLS helps balance out all three of them, plus fixing the problem of the trader not getting access to crits in 14.2. Pretty cool eh?
(traders get light blue perception. Two other classes get regular blue, while the rest all get dark blue.)

4. This LLS would be built using tradeskills.

5. The components for this thing could be bought or found, but there is one item that must be gotten in a mission to complete the LLS. The new LLS can be made at all QL's absed on the component QL's. The special quest item has no QL, it works with any QL LLS.

6. The high perception required will have the benifit of lowering the requirements for weapon smithing and electrical engineering by a large ammount. Another large boon.
