fun = challenge
I've been playing AO since the begining; although i missed the pioneer backpack bonus by one week; i've never been bitter about that, infact i really never cared. Actually i never cared about any of the problems much at all. Sometimes i got frustrated when trying to solo; since i always wanted to yield a high token count; but whatever; after all is said and done AO is just a game.

For me when a game is difficult; i tend to come back for more; even when the problem was game mechanics; as in many of the bugs that seem basically fixed. I can only hope that players with super high tweaks can see that power once possessed is no longer obsessed. What i mean by that is: if the playing field is evened out; then the quest for power (which is the goal for all games; including monopoly for crying out loud) will become that much more exciting.

It is unfortunate that we had played this game; having built up stategies around over-equipping for such a long time; since i have to admit was a fun and challenging process in itself. It was almost like a tradeskill. The reason why i have to support this patch however; is because i always wanted a group to benefit from a well balanced team that would include several classes. I dont think i have ever teamed with a trader. I've gotten a few buffs for my doctor, but i always felt that if one does not have the skill to use something; then how can one honstly use it?

The only real complaints i ever had with AO is the fact that not all classes have a valuable OE buff to market themselves for team play inclusion. Also the fact that items can't be upgraded with trade skills. You finally get a look or design that you like for your character; then you have to chuck it all and get new equipment. Since the cost of gear is so high, you must go to the missions. Since loot is ussually junk you must find a mission with the exact item you want; taking hours of game time that could!! and should!! be enjoyed with teams or solo play. Eventually you have to settle for less so you give your atrox; female flowers body armor to go with your vito's and chilled armor mismatch hell. It seems to me that weapon smithing should be a skill used to increase the quality of weapons and armor; while 'engineering' and 'technology' skills should increase the quality of items related to those things; with the exception of items containing charges such as nano packs. Trades would then be much more useful and marketable as an economic base for AO.

Any way i'm getting off the topic. My point is team play and more importantly; player interaction are the hands down best qualities of MMORPG's; and any patch that promotes team interaction and cooperation is a plus in my book.

I recently made a suggestion to Funcom about a new line of nano's for agents. My suggestion was mimic alignment. I can only hope that this is included in the new agent nano lines they are planning but it was pretty recent so i won't count on it.

Basically with the exceptions mentioned above; i think AO is a great game; with a fantastic engine and beautiful environment. It could use a little more wide open pvp zones with no save terms; easily accessed from opposite ends by both factions for the purpose of full scale wars.

I wont be quiting AO any time soon or at all for that matter; no matter how lame a patch they put out. I'm in this for the long haul. Any way they can't improve the game if no one is playing to give them input.
