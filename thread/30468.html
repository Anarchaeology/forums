Sugestion about IEC Flashpoint Laser rifles
This assault rifle seems unbalanced to me, even though the damage is good and it has all those specials I think it fires a little too slow. Now, I'm not suggesting putting the recharge time and attack time to some rediculous low number like 2.50, but I think if the attack time was lowered to a solid 5 or 6 seconds instead of almost 8 it would be a more useful assault rifle.
The only reason I make this suggestion is because I have a friend who's a fixer using a ql 17 smg, while I used a ql 21 Flashpoint. Durring missions while attacking a mob in a 30 second time frame he does 3 times the damage I do (if I use fling and burst I can cut that down to usually him doing only 50-25% more damage than me), I see that as a little unbalanced.
I'm sure there are people out there that disagree with me and would tell me to just go with a nova flow and shut up about it, but I like being able to do all those specials and I feel that if I'm going to spend that much IP to equip a gun it should be a little more balanced.
