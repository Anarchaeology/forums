Social Armor
I quit playing about a month ago, but I am paid up until Feb 6, so I continued to download the updates and read the update files.

I logged on today to see what my 'prize' was, and found a nice backpack, thank you. I also see that you have stopped redisarranging my inventory every time I zone, this is a very good thing, I just couldn't cope with this anymore, it is one of the major reasons that I left.

I am not particularly interested in being 3% taller, but I wanted to try on my new backpack, so I took off my swimsuit to put it on, and found that I could not put my swimsuit back on, it is now classified as social armor! It did not say this in the description when I bought it, and it does not say this now.

AO has so many nice features, one of which is all the nice clothing that you have created. I have several nice dresses that I could never wear, simply because it was too awkward and time consuming to wear them. Having to take off all my things to put on a dress for awhile, and then replace all my things just takes too much time. And when do I ever have 14 free inventory slots to use up when I come in to town? Now my swimsuits go into the dustbin with my dresses. I can't even wear my sunglasses with my swimsuit.

This social armor business is goofy. Clothing adds nothing to your AC, why do you care if I wear it or not? Why do you take a really nice feature and destroy it with a senseless restriction like this? The swimsuits worked just fine before you 'fixed' this. Please do something to make it possible for me to wear all the nice expensive clothing that you sold me.

It is a pain to switch from armor to whatever social clothes you like to wear.

One thing I"d like to see is a forth "wear" slot added called Armor.
You would put the "armor" on in that slot, and your clothes on in what is currently the "clothes" slot. Then just make a command to switch between the two (add in some delay, and make a skill check for the armor again if desired).

I would like to see an Ultima Online style of layering. Each item is on a certain layer for that slot. It would be possible to wear sunglasses and a hood for instance. Either way, something needs to be changed. I'm tired of everyone looking the same.
