Apologies to Elite Operations

I would like to start with saying that Im deeply sorry for any damage I have caused the relationship between Neutrals and Omni-Tek .

The reason, I know its not an excuse since there is none in this case, that I attacked your towers-base in Omni-Forest was this:

About two hours before I started the attack, our (Omni Tek Assassins) towers where attacked by troops from the Clan organization FURY. There forces have repeatedly attacked our towers over the last few months.

We managed to withstand the first wave of the attack, and drove the FURY forces to retreat, and intimidated them not to attack again.

We who participated in the defense where all roused with adrenaline and started searching for Any thing to attack.

Scout reports indicated a Neutral tower base in Omni Forest, just outside our beloved Omni Entertainment.

With the thought of our victory fresh in memory we started to smite plans for another glorious battle.

The adrenaline rouse wore of fast on most people, but not on myself Im sad to say.

When the time arrived I tried to rally to forces once again, but the response was scarce, so I decided to scout myself.

Upon my arrival, there was already an army forming (from another Guild, but they have to answer themselves for there mistake). I quickly joined with there forces and prepared for battle.

The outcome of that battle we all sadly know.

Im sorry for what I have done, and I hope that You Elite Operations will not hold this My mistake against other members of Omni Tek Assassins.
