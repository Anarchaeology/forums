my contribution
Grats - short for Congratulations. Commonly used when someone dings or acheives a major milestone (like getting a Yalm or a rare item).

Slayer - a slayer droid, the most powerful engineer pet available. Very nasty in team missions, very hard to calm/mez.

Calm - see Mez, above. Basically gets a mob to stop attacking.

Mob - short for mobile obstacle. Refers to monsters and NPC's.

WTS/WTB/WTT - Want to Sell/Buy/Trade. Used on shopping channels.

NoDrop - a type of item that cannot be traded. Some people try to sell NoDrop items on the shopping channels - and get laughed at soon after.

RM - Real Mean. The most powerful human mobs usually have this in their names. If you face down one of these and live, solo or in a group, you are definitely uber.

UVC - Universal Vulnerability Compendium. The best MA crit buff.

FFoK - Four Fists of Kali. Another kickass MA nano.

Damage team - A team with very little or no healing/calming support, relying strictly on damage output to survive.

Warp - Refers to NT and Engineer warp progams.

Rubberband lag - when you're running and suddenly end up back where you started moving from. Also called "warp lag".

Chain - when something happens multiple times in a row or very quickly. Usually refers to multiple debuffs/roots being used on you (chain roots, for example).

HE - Humidity Extractor. A line of NT buffs that restores your nano pool like a HoT restores health.

Ess - Essence. A line of Enforcer buffs that improve your health, strength, stamina, and combat skills. Also makes you slightly larger.

Oh, and one more...

Pwned - Leet-speak. An easy victory, usually in PvP or against a boss. If you get pwned, it doesn't mean you're a gimp... it just means you got beat down hard and didn't do much in return.

GS = Group Selector: Certain playfields (such as static dungeons, stores, etc) have a maximum number of players allowed in them at once. When that number is exceeded, a duplicate playfield is created to allow more people. These duplicate playfields are differentiated by group selector numbers. So if a bunch of your guildmates are heading for Smuggler's Den and you see them start saying "GS4 everyone", it means you should make sure you're in group selector 4. You can check what GS you're in by hitting shift-F9.

This should not be confused with:

CL = Crowd Limiting: A feature which will limit the number of players that can be present at a Notum Wars tower battle. Usually somewhere around 35 players per side (attackers/defenders). Anyone over that limit will be (randomly?) teleported to somewhere else in the zone.

CL should not be confused with:

CC = Crowd Control: The ability for certain classes (Crat, NT, others) to "control" enemy creatures, by rooting/slowing/mezzing/scaring them. Generally this is done so that the team can focus on one mob at a time and not have to worry about adds.

I see a lot of people using "CC" when they're really talking about "CL"...

This is a really helpful thread. I've been playing a while, yet there were many terms here I didn't know. Here are a few more additions that I don't think were mentioned (might have just missed some of them):

QL - quality level
SMG - submachine gun or the skill of the same name
K - OK
WB - welcome back, typicaly said to a team member returning after going link dead
YW - you're welcome
leet - elite, meaning a well connected high level player, also the name of an adorable little two-legged mob which anyone who has played the game has no doubt encountered
leet speak - a style of chat in games using intentional misspellings and substituting numbers for letters
aye - yes
d00d - A certain type of player distinguished by a tendency to use curt sentences, type all in caps and generally behave in an obnoxious way (example: "CREDS PLZ!!! WTF?! HELLO?? R U ALL NUBI? GIVE ME SOME CREDS!")
gj - good job
* - means a correction to a previous error when used after a word like this: word*
tweak - improving a character to a high degree of perfection
oop - out of power, a term from other games used by healers to indicate they are out of nano and the team should pause, not heard that much in AO since the nano bar can be refilled quickly with nano rechargers
SWG - Star Wars Galaxies, another MMORPG
DAoC - Dark Ages of Camelot, another MMORPG
EQ - Everquest, another MMORPG
UO - another MMORPG
big d - a dungeon at Sentenels in Mort, the higher level companion to little d
little d - a dungeon at Sentenels in Mort, the lower level companion to big d
MPS - Mausser Particle Streamer, a so-so SMG which can be converted into a better weapon popular with fixers called a Mausser Chemical Streamer
MCS - Mausser Chemical Streamer, a SMG popular with fixers created by converting a Mausser Particle Streamer using tradeskills to chemical damage
borg - cyborg, a popular mob to hunt for xp
sup - a friendly greeting, derived from "what's up?"
plz - please
ppl - people
pk - player killer
sta - the stamina ability
str - the strength ability
sen - the sense ability
agi - the agility ability
psy - the psychic ability
int - the intelligence ability
r u - are you (example: "r u in a team?")
spawn - when a mob first materializes into the game it is called spawning
respawn - after a unique mob is killed it will respawn, or reappear, again after a certain amount of time at it's designated spawn point, respawning also occurs after the mobs have been cleared from a dungeon or outdoor hunting area (example: "We've cleared the area, now we'll have to wait for respawn.")
mocham - a series of meta physicist buffs that raise nano skills
enhanced senses - an agent's buff that raises sense
iron circle - a doctor's buff that raises stamina and strength
ownz - to dominate an opponent, typically used in PvP, (example: "I ownz you!"), also the name of a unique mob near Tir
AH - Athen Hill, the raised area in the middle of the city of Old Athen, a common meeting area
FT - the Fair Trade store, there are many Fair Trades but this almost always refers to the one in the center of Tir, a place where clanners gather to look for teams
alt - alternate, a character which is not the main character you play
avatar - your avatar is the visual representation of your character you see on screen (example: "I have blond hair in real life but my avatar is brunette")
char - character
TK - Trash King, a huge robot mob in Athen Shire that is part of a quest
req - requirement, most items in AO have a requirement of a certain minimum skill level to be equipped (example: "It's an easy gun to equip because of it's low burst req")
thx - thanks
FoL - the MA attack Flower of Life
bot - robot
rawk - rock
kewl - cool
Miiir - a clothing store
nav - skills in the navigation category
B&E - the breaking & entry skill, used for
NF - nano formula
m8 - mate

Carb - Carbonum armor, popular playermade armor type.

GOC - Globe of Clarity. Shoulder item dropped from Tara.

VTE - Virral triumvirate egg. Playermade shoulder item.

CDS - Cyborg death squad armor. Fixer armor that drops from cyborgs.

Imps - Short for implants.

Bp - short for breastplate

TL - Title level. TL 1 = lvl 1-15, TL 2 = 15-50, TL 3 = 50-100, TL 4 = 100-150, TL 5 = 150-190, TL 6 = 190-200. Upon reaching a new title lvl skills you were previously unable to raise due to them being "title capped" will again be raisable.

Some more terminology, and I've updated the master list which can be found here:

http://aoaide.curvedspaces.com/terminology.htm

inits - refers collectively to the initiative skills, namely Melee, Ranged, Physic, and NanoC found in the Speed category
evades - refers collectively to the Evade, Dodge and Duck skills found in the Speed category
parts - this could mean lots of things but often refers to the "su****ious" items which must be gathered from bots and assembled to complete the Dodga/Alvin quest (example: "I was in Athen Shire hunting bots for parts")
pads - shoulderpads, often refers to the pads given as rewards for the popular Dodga/Alvin quest
greeter - a type of ARK who specializes in welcoming new players
registrar - a type of ARK who records character marriages in the game
cya - see ya, slang for "I'll see you later"
pillows - concrete cushions
clr - clear, typically heard in dungeons to indicate all the mobs have been cleared from the dungeon
lag - I won't attempt to define exactly what constitutes "lag" in a technical sense, but when used in game it refers to when the game stalls, stutters, jerks or slows down for whatever reason
phreak - when a fixer warps to the grid, from an old hackers term relating to hacking into the phone system
sol - the solitus breed
opi - the opifex breed
tradeskills - 1. the processes by which players may create items in games, usually by combining various parts together 2. refers collectively to the skills used in the creation of tradeskill items
PGC - Personal Grid Converter, sort of a key, sold in stacks, that allows you to access the grid from your orgs controllers
grid balls - PGCs are sometimes called this because of their icon's appearance of a cluster of interconnected balls
sec - second (example: I'll be there in a sec)
peeps - people (example: "Hi, what are you peeps up to?"
ladder - when trader's "ladder" their drains it means to cast one drain which gives them enough skills to cast a higher drain and so on
ohw - on his/her way (example: "the doc is ohw")
SOL - s**t out of luck
chest - armor breastplates are sometimes called chests
ruins - usually refers to a hunting spot for cyborgs in Mort near Sentinels
ME - the Mechanical Engineering skill
EE - the Electrical Engineering skill
QT - the Quantum Force Field Technology skill
WP - the Weapon Smiting skill
PT - the Pharmacological Technology skill
psych - the Psychology skill, sometimes used to refer to the Psychic ability which can be confusing
chem - the Chemistry skill
ROF - rate of fire, the speed at which a weapon fires
PC - player character
TTYL - talk to you later
AR - attack rating
TotW - Temple of the Winds, a dungeon in GTC
