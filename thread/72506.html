Easy enough. Select the tab you want 'active' at any given point in time. That tab becomes what you wear, and you only get bonuses from that tab (either social or armor tab). The other tab goes inactive; you don't have to re-equip things on that tab, but neither would you be able to benefit from them while in social mode.

This fixes the problem with GA and normal armor being worn together, and the quasi-exploit of a full medsuit and a biomech suit at the same time. However, it would make it easier to put a medsuit in your social tab, implant up w/ ability implants, put on your armor in the armor tab, swap back to social and swap in normal implants.

This, of course, could be easily fixed by making medsuits non-social armor, so they couldn't be put in the social tab.
