My 10th credit...
Maelice was, and is, the greatest RP player in this game you are every likely to meet. During my time as Director of Black Valley, Maelice was ALWAYS there for questions, we set up numerous social gatherings, parties and events. How many people remeber our pool party? If you ever went to a party on Rk2, odds are, you went to a Maelice thrown party. This guy was in character when he crawled our of bed in the morning, only to stumble over to his pc and blearily connect to AO. Not that I'm actually sure he DID sleep, i can count the number of times he wasn't online at some point every day I ever looked for him on the fingers of my amputated left hand! Division 3 and Black Valley exchanged ambassadors to facilitate a close relationship, and who was our ambassador? MAELICE!!! he made an alt and spent countless hours helping out our small guild, teaching us the ropes, getting our underaged into the bars, hitting on our women..... Maelice has left his mark on Rk2, and if you didn't know him, you missed some of the fun. Funcom, he was on so much, his name is probably indelibly burned into some monitor somewhere in your offices. Immortalizing this incredible friend with a drink is a wonderfull idea, please consider this as a serious, and seriously fun, concept that bears NO gameplay balancing issues.

A giant bump from Syd, former director of Black Valley, all around party man.
