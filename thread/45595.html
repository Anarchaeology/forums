Ill try.

My view is a little one sided , but ill try give some realistic points to both.

Anarchy Online:
Good points:

Its a good game full stop.
Funcom wont go out of business for a good few years yet and hopefully with the expansion coming up next year will revive the storyline and bring AO back on track to be a competative game.

Its uses its Level/Skills based system quite well and everything fits nicely to the *in the future* theme.

There is a community of RPers out there , they usually hang about the clubs and roleplaying forum , im not sure about PvP ive not done a great deal of it.

Bad Points:

Its buggy as hell , to date its the buggiest online game ive played and the bugs it has i consider game stopping bugs , the problem is there are bugs still existant from release which is worrying.
Also to note , Funcom dont have a Bug hunting department (paid that is) they are all voluteer players appart of the ARK program.

Game design and coding seems to be very messed up , things like huge computer lag on top end machines , it doesnt handle large PvP meetings very well and the servers seem equally unstable , never seen zones drop as often as in AO.

They make alot of promises and then seem to go back on thier word, this has happened on many occasions
One of the recent worrying things they did was to make item restricted items, this goes against the skills based system that many if not all players prefer.

The storyline was suppose to be a 4 year storyline , it isnt and never will be , Funcom failed drastically on the 4 year storyline promise , months have/did gone by without any word from Funcom in reguards to the storyline.

PvP is unbalanced , Doctors will beat you most of the time on a lvl vrs lvl basis , but in most cases the PvP benefits belong to the lvl 200's.

The biggest problem is , not alot seems to be getting done to resolve all the above problems.

Unfortunatly after about 6 months of playing AO it starts to lose its lust , by this time you have done hundreds of missions , and there seems little else to do.

Star Wars Galaxies:

Good Points:

Skill based system , It has the best system of all , your character is just a blank template , you decide which skills to invest in .. for example you could be a dancing Jedi with hair dressing skills and an affinity to growing plants .. bliss. Complete control which usually leads to balanced classes ,which is one of the most complained about things in MMORPG.

Its HUGE , think of the entire land mass of AO .. times it by 4 (bout the size of Everquest) then times that by 9 planets. Each with its own landscape.

It has the best graphics to date of any MMORPG , however be prepared to pay for that pleasure if you dont have a Gforce card .. forgive me but i cant quite remember what SWG needs but its something thats on the Gforce cards , if you have a TNT2 (which i have)you will have to replace it with a supported card.

Content should be excessive , the company Lucas Arts will be on the ball with this and overlooking anything that gets added stays within the scope of Star Wars and its continuity.

It has the backing of Sony Verant and Lucas Arts which are huge and sucessful companys , with huge amounts of funds to make SWG the best , they say money talks , in this case it cleans your arse for you too. lots of investment will allow the team to be experts , it will have those special little must haves like computer engineering and base structors to ensure that SWG is coded neatly and efficiently.

And about the team .. it consists of UO developers .. UO had the best skill based system in MMORPG to date .. same as SWG , chars had a blank template and you had complete control over your character , the other benefit UO had was the ability to own your own home , along with a complete crafts system to build all the nifty little items to make your home the perfect little nest.

It alos has with it EQ developers , these guys have the expertise of running a huge and highly sucessful 3D game , along with thier excellent faction system and attention to detail , with the UO devs they are going to push MMORPG programming to new levels , its an ideal situation to be in.

Going back to trade skills .. SWG will have more skills based on trade that actual combat skills , this will increase your interest in SWG by at least 2 - 4 years,although with this kind of project i expect that the life span of this particular game will hit arounf the 5 year mark. (UO had about a 3 year life span(before bordom starts nagging at you that is).

Bad Points:

I only have one bad point to put here , but its a big one.

It not been released yet , Barely just started beta , things can change drastically in the next 4 months , solme things might make release, some things might not, when it does get released it could be riddled with bugs it might not, it could fail drastically in many places , Trade skills for example could have serious defects that unbalance the game.

The unknown is what is the thorn in the side of this game but if it does go well , and things do go as expected , SWG will beat ANY game out there hands down!.

The choice is yours , personally I think you will know what your choice is by the time SWG is released.

Ive been playing Online games for about 6 years now , Im voting for SWG as the next big MMORPG,at least for a good few years till someone else with huge funds and expertise comes along and makes another.
