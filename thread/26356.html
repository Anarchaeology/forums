if a simple script is all u want then try this

in notepad

/say Hello there %1 , I am Erin . How are you doing today ?
/delay 2000
/say Ahh i see , well im glad to hear that .... now suck lead from my Pet !
/delay 2000
/pet attack %1
/delay 1000
/pet say " Your Gonna Die Sucker ! "

now save as Attack
place this in your Scripts folder in your Ao directory
Any time you want to use this script just type /attack Erin

the name Erin acts as your %1 so that yuou get this as follows :

" Hello Erin , I am Erin . How are you doing today ? "
slight pause
" Ahh i see , well im glad to hear that .... now suck lead from my Pet ! "
slight pause
Erins Pet "yes master i will kill it for you "
slight pause
Erins Pet " your Gonna Die Sucker ! "

you can make scripts do almots anything you want .
