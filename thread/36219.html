I haven't tried them in awhile (gave them up for GA), but back before 13.8 it seemed that they didn't work when the resolved hit (after AC checks) was below 50 points, and the reflect damage increased with each 50 point increment of incoming damage. A hit for 99 will reflect X damage, while a hit for 101 will reflect X*2 damage.

That meant for a long time, my TL1 and TL2 bracers appeared broken, only working occasionally. I left them on because they didn't conflict with anything else, and eventually (around level 40ish) every mob or player I would fight broke that 50 point threshhold, and it became clear to me what the problem was.

Serious rounding issues.

You can self test it as well on lower level mobs by peeling off your own AC to the point where you're not mitigating so much.
