Zodiac Signs for Anarchy Online

Your zodiac sign relates to who you're to pick for your race and class. The determination of your choice depends on how you want to play the game, and which race/class appeals to you the most. If it has the support of one or more of your friends, you're more likely to recognize yourself in the trait description. Here is the description of each sign.
------------------------------------------------------------
Capricorn (Dec. 22 to Jan. 19)

He has strong leadership ability, generates ideas, delegates details to others and moves toward fresh pasture. He is usually either a Beaurocrat or a Soldier who thinks he is a Beaurocrat. He may be perceived by other signs as a kill-stealer, but he mainly has a desire to accomplish everything with less trips to the reclaim terminal. He levels early in the game, detests the condition of being a n00b and has a tendency toward watching his IP carefully. He sets is aggrobar high and is rarely satisfied with results. He is internally a role-player, but sometimes sets alot of players on /ignore. He is interested only in making credits and is loyal and committed to teams.

Capricorn's tend to be Beaurocrats, Soldiers, or Engineers.

------------------------------------------------------------
Aquarius (Jan. 20 to Feb. 17)

He is very bright and is most comfortable in the world of the forums. He is good at developing his skills at finding a good team and generous about buffing those he feels should meet. He is sensitive to the distribution of phat lewts. He is idealistic and strives to make a difference in the game world. He has a good sense of how long it will take the game to be fixed and often thinks two of three patches ahead of its time.

Aquarians tend to be Solitus or Opifex/Nanotechnicians, Metaphysicists, Agents, and Fixers.

------------------------------------------------------------
Pisces (Feb. 18 to Mar. 19)

Intuitive, emotional, imaginative, artistic and musical, he flows easily between the role-playing aspects of the game, down to perfecting implants. He has a deep need to remain connected to the internet, though often he is not attached to traditional dial-up. He is compassionate and frequently changes the chat colour and petnames. He must learn how to up his defensive armor against chemical attacks. He is very adaptable, flexible, and tends to use pointless weapons.

Pisceans tend to be Nanomage or Atrox Engineers, Metaphysicists, and Adventurers.

------------------------------------------------------------
Aries (Mar 20 to April 19)

He is easily bored and needs constant udpates to content. He is a strong initiator and likes to complete a mission rapidly and move on to the next one. He always want to be number one or to solo the missions, room by room. He has a courageous spirit which tends to make the puller nervous. He is a roleplayer at heart but prefers hunting outdoors than to wait for the rest of the team to come back from the reclaim terminal.

Aries tend to be Atrox or Opifex enforcers, soldiers, or martial artists

------------------------------------------------------------
Taurus (April 20 to May 20)

He takes special pleasure in high QL items and static dungeons. He tends to like nanoformula effects and particularly loves to read the description of items. Slow, careful and deliberate, he doesn't like running into the first room with calms or roots. He must learn the difference between caution and chain-casting. Naturally stable, he needs to work toward an organized plan and prefers with a group that plans to stay awhile. He is especially sensitive to the amount of credits he has and will tend to get missions that have a higher credit reward, with the 'observe item'type.

Taurus tend to be all races, but stick to Nanotechnicians, agents, and fixers

------------------------------------------------------------
Gemini (May 21 to June 20)

He is bored easily and feels uncomfortable in an a indoor mission. Naturally bright and alert, he uses a good grammar skills (as opposed to l33+5p34k) and enjoys spending IPs on tradeskills. He has alot of names on his buddy list for buffs and teaming. He may skim the surface of mission descriptions and he doesn't mind traviling to East Stret Bank. Easy talker, he sometimes embelishes on PvP adventures just to make a good story. He prefers to pull more than one mob at a time, but he needs to learn to calm what he aggros.

Geminis tend to be Martial artists, Engineers, Fixers, and Traders

------------------------------------------------------------
Cancer (June 21 to July 22)

He listens to the emotions and feelings of himself and others in his team. He often alternates between not chatting and spamming the group with pulls. He has a strong appreciation for the spawns and its exp. He is protective of it from KS'ers and also any persons who are included in his team. He is a loyal and devoted leader and backup healer. He will work to make his apartment cozy, but he must learn to not snap at people when they pull aggro.

Cancers tend to be Solitus or Opifex, but choose Docters, Adventurers, or Metaphysicists

------------------------------------------------------------
Leo (July 23 to Aug. 22)

He is warm, generous and open minded. He is a commanding leader and is readily noticed whenever he asks to form a group. He enjoys being in charge, always talks about how much exp he got at this spawn, and is occasionally arrogant. He is susceptible to flattery and is easily embarrassed. When he starts a mission, he will see it to its conclusion, for the tokens. Often a fixer or agent, he is a loyal and dedicated team member. He likes to make fun of his class being gimped, but he won't appreciate others doing so.

Leos to be any class or race that likes to whine alot.

------------------------------------------------------------
Virgo (Aug. 23 to Sep. 22)

He is sensitive to the race differences and tends to play a nanomage. He is particularly susceptible to running out of medkits. He has a strong need for order and a steady stream of pulls. He is a great organizer, though this activity tends to get annoying. He is excellent at finding useful websites and researches on tradeskills, but he sometimes gets caught in details and finds alot of bugs with the games. He will usually find a good team, but is actually happier soloing.

Virgos tend to be Nanomages, and usually play Nanotechnicians, Engineers, or traders.

------------------------------------------------------------
Libra (Sep. 23 to Oct. 22)

He is very bright and uses the guild chat channels alot. Usually, he uses comma's, apostrophes and periods, he is deliberate about using emotes. Good at keeping from getting flamed, he searches for mid-range hunting grounds and missions that make everyone happy. He disarms angry /tells and /shouts, even if it means being quiet about his own complaints. He avoids PvP and He is more comfortable with PvM, and performs best when teaming with a partner or or guildmate. He struggles with dealings with n00bs and their constant begging for credits.

Libras tend to be Solitus or Atrox, and usually play Adventurers, Martial Artists, or metaphysicists

------------------------------------------------------------
Scorpio (Oct. 23 to Nov. 21)

Deeply attuned to his class and the levels of others, he is accurately aware of words or people who are using nanoformulas that aren't as effective. He enjoys exploring Rubi-Ka and tends toward the more remote areas of the planet. He is slow to troll, but tends to add a lot of people to his PvP blacklist, once he is offended. He is attracted to playing a doctor or a metaphysicist or he may take the road of the engineer.

Scorpios tend to not play Atrox, and play Doctors, Metaphysicists, or Engineers.

------------------------------------------------------------
Sagittarius (Nov. 22 to Dec. 21)

Of restless nature, he is bored easily. He loves to travel and often needs to hunt outdoors. For him, exploration is the ****e of life, whether it is mental or in the body. He is always optimistic and often hopes to have a patch for just his class, especially when it is needed the most. He is interested in finding out what the good nano's are and how effective they are in combat. He has difficulty with finishing a mission, procrastination, and sticking to teams. He prefers to keep more than one mission at a time. He often leans toward soloing.

Sagittariuses tend to play Opifex or Solitus, and play any class.

------------------------------------------------------------
