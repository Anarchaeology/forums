Old American Proverb

KISS

Keep It Simple Stupid (FYI, stupid is in reference to no one.)

My charm proposal is the simplest solution for Funcom (other than to delete our profession entirely.) We can give grandiose solutions that will only make the overworked Funcom developers roll their eyes and stop paying attention to what we are saying. Yes, I would love ranged pets and other tweaks but the crux of the matter is we need quick and effective solutions NOW!

The charming change applies only to the caster's droid. You do not need to hit %t free or anything. Simply cast your Droid, buff to your taste and press your Charm hotkey. Rinse and repeat for the unstable charm. Charm expires, so does the droid like any charmed mob pet. To prevent exploits, if the charm fails or fumbles the droid deactivates. The mechanics to do this are already in the game.

Will it be preferable to charm mobs instead of your droid? Yes from both a damage and time standpoint! My proposal does not alter this one bit. But look at these latest patches. Funcom keeps making areas with NOTHING to charm (or nothing worthwhile). They have their own vision of the game so we can either roll with them or go against them (and the only effective means I see atm is to cancel and say good-bye).

Charming your own Droids is basically going to maintain a minimum floor on crat power so we can maintain our usefulness in all the new situations Funcom is going to throw at us.

KISS

1) Funcom please change the pvp/whatever flag on our droids so a crat can charm his/her own droid. (nuff said!)

or

2) Funcom please insure there are charmable mobs in all zones equal to the min/max QL of the Notum mining areas up to a player's max level +-10. That means you must reverse your policy of making mobs in Camelot, Foremans, etc. etc. immune to charm. etc. but o well. (Also useless in most pvp if you're in a 80-100 area trying to fight 200s. So to make it effective for pvp you would need to sprinkle mobs of all levels in all zones!)

Funcom will only listen to simple changes.
