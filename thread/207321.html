Just another murder...
He sat at his desk, the small pot-bellied frame of a short Opifex man and the characteristic glowing green eyes stood out through the fog created by the smoke of many a Nano-Cig(tm) over the past few hours. The figure sat, his feet up on his desk as he twiddled his thumbs.

*breeep*

-"Oy, this is Vector Blue, Rizz speakin'"-

The chatter from the commlink could be ehard from quite a distance, a distressed voice was obviously...well... distressed...

-"Wai.. wa.. wait... slow down... What happened?..."- he said, trying to calm down this caller...

-"Another murder at Reets?... What do you take me for, Omni-Pol? I don't care if some drunk with a switchblade got an adrenaline rush, bud...... What?.... What about robes?...."- He was getting annoyed, he had better things to do than listen to some crazy woman jabber about a routine barfight...

-"The cult of Yxxir died off a long time ago, lady, it was probably some clown with all his friends in a robe.... Call me when you have somethign worth twenty seconds of my lifetime.... I said bye..."-

Abruptly, he turned off his comm, wrinkling his nose and shaking his head, as he loaded the latest nano-game... just another long day at the office...

Access Gridmail, Priority VB-43522S
Seryk Blake glared at the holographic console displayed in the air above his face, he blinked as lines of distortion creased the otherwise immaculate and perfect blue textures of the screen. Seryk smacked his wrist unit where the display was originating from. The screen popped, then flickered before coming back to normal and losing itself of everything that detracted from its otherwise pure, static free blue. Seryk shifted his weight around to get a more comfortable position as he perched atop the cliff face that looked out over the Wartorn Valley. He hefted his Yatamuchy X-3 off his shoulders and placed it on the ground next to him.
"Piece of junk" he muttered around a mouthful of food.
"Command not recognised" the display shot back at him in an automated feminine voice. Seryk frowned at the voice, a look of annoyance crossing his normally blank face. He took a gulp and swallowed the reaminder of the food he had been chewing on.
"Sybil, bring up all new mail" Seryk answered back in response. He watched as several new icons popped up in the new mail box. He glanced over the names of senders....and settled on the one that he had been expecting. Rizzior. Seryk poked his finger at the name and watched as a text display slowly appeared on the screen.
"Seryk, needing ya to look into a murder over at the reet retreat. Some random drunk partyer got sliced open and bled to death all over the main dance floor. Doesn't look to pretty only lead we have is that three people in dark robes walked in, one walked up to and cut open the stomach of one of the partyers. At this point I have little to no information about anything else including who was cut. It seems they never came forward about this and most of the partyers tended to be rather tight lipped....just find out whatever you can and report back to me with whatever you find out. Rizz out."
Seryk punched a button on his wrist unit shutting down the display. "Another day, another job." Seryk picked up his rifle and swung his legs off the cliff, laying down so only his head was showing to any who might look up, he raised the X-3 and aimed it at the doorway to a small house on the outskirts of the junkyard behind the once beautiful Omni-2. Seryk lay in wait patiently...
