No more mob camping
We all knows those special mobs which drop special items and nodrop items we all want to get. some of them are of a low interest, "Georges" the reet , "Ding" the leet in greater tir and tir county for example.
Those one are often available but for all others we could never even see because of heavy camping make us frustrated and upset against the uber guild which camp the donjons and locations where they spawn. Allways the same location.
we definitively want random mobs with random loot (special unique or nodrop items) of different QL and even sometimes no item at all to improve chance to have something very special.
They could spawn randomly in playfields but with only one rule, not to spawn in the same playfield from one time to an other and with just enough information about them to be able to recognize their special status among the other mobs.
This could resolve the camping and give a chance to any player of any lvl to get special/unique/nodrop items.
This could be done probably with little code and scripting routines if mobs AI works this way.

thanks for any reply from FC dev team , Cz or Cosmik about this possibility.
