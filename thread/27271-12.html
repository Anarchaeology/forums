People who play for six months really ought to be better equipped and perform more efficiently in the game than players who've only played one month.

However, this thread is about a specific topic. And I'll try to make it clearer.

Certain people who play for six months should NOT be able to completely block out other people who play for six months simply because they are able to log on all day long or have joined a group whose sole purpose is to monopolize content.

Both players have paid $12.95 a month for six months. Neither is more entitled to have fun than the other is. Both made their commitment to FunCom and FunCom is morally obligated to return in kind to each of them.

We have player X who has now played for a year and given FunCom $150.00. He is now level 140. He has no chance at enjoying any of the new content.

We have player Y who has now played for a year and given FunCom $150.00. He is now level 140. He is unemployed, lives with his mother and has 8 alarm clocks at his computer desk. He talked his way into a guild that has 8 level 200 players and over 300 players in total. His client is almost never disconnected from the game servers.

The way the entire game is set up right now, the Player Y is rewarded in-game repeatedly.

The question that FunCom needs to answer to themselves is 'is this the kind of player that we think should 'win' this game?'

FunCom has given off hints of having a moral backbone. I haven't seen this air around a game developer since Origin's early days. I'd be surprised if it's their intention to continue down the path of developing a game that clearly is devolving into a system of 'haves' and 'have nots' with both groups having equally developed characters played by equally good people.

Likewise, 'fun' should not be reserved by any particular sector of the playerbase. Never. You should not have to 'work' in order to have fun. 'Working' in this game should be fun in and of itself. The fact that Tradeskills are not 'fun' ties in hand-in-hand with the fact that people are getting bored. Let me check for a player-poll regarding this:

http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=25684

What's right up at the top? You got it, 'boredom'. Now, what do you do in this game? You do mission after mission or engage in some PvP. What happens when you've done your millionth mission or fought you zillionth duel? Nothing.

Except.

If you are a member of a significantly small percentage of the playerbase that happens to be in one of three or four organizations.

Then you can have fun. You can have a blast. Day in and day out there is always something to do because you 'own' the game. And trust me, we're not talking dedication or player skills here. It's simply being able to manipulate the game mechanics in such a way so as to exclude the remainder of the equally 'dedicated' or 'skilled' players.

OK, so you don't have a chance at killing a unique monster and your group can't find a corner of Smuggler's Den that you won't be molested in and you don't have 100 lowbies in you guild that you can pay a million credits to reserve a spot in line at Alvin or Dodga till you decide to show up.
