The definative thread. Who is gimped, who is just whining.
Differing opinions are welcome if backed up by good reasons.
Flames and personal attacks will simply be ignored.
If you aren't smart enough to argue over the facts of the post and make valid points of your own then you don't deserve to post here.

NT:
Ignore all the flaming low level NT's on these boards. Ask any high level NT if he's a gimp. They aren't. The NT is one of the most powerful classes in the game at high levels, in both PvP and PvM.

Each of my nukes does damage equal to the crits of my teammates. Now when I am blasting away with my X-3, doing as much damage as the agent next to me, while throwing out nukes and calms, and raising nullity when things get rough while the agent gets beat into pulp, you try telling me with a strait face that I'm the gimp out of the two of us.

The NT has some issues and bugs to address, such as black holes and lag not allowing them to get off nukes fast enough... but at the core of the NT's balance is powerful enough.

Fixer
The fixer is overpowered with grid armor+HoTs. That wouldn't be a problem if they didn't dish out so much damage on top of it. The new SMG's are just as good as any agent rifle, plus the fixer SMG buffs are much better than what the agent has. Supreme defense matched with superior offense, it's ridiculous.

FC even pandered to the whiners by giving fixers the highest nano skill buffer in the game (+60 at QL 200). Now the fixers get to have high weapons skills, high speed skills, AND high nano skills all at once...

The whining fixers failed to realise that everyone has to sacrifice things when IP is tight, the fixer should have been no different. So when do soldiers and enforcers get their nano boosting items? They have IP troubles to. On top of it they only get to raise their nano skills 3 per level, while the fixer raises his 4 points. Or how about giving the agent's and NT's some evade and HP boosting devices to make up for their high IP cost and low caps? That just shows how ridiculous giving the fixer that item was.

Agent
The agent is still gimped. The only thing in the "agent patch" that truely made an impact was the X-3 (the rest was useless fluff). Of course it's not really a boost to the agent because everyone can use it just as good as an agent.

The agent has NO defense whatsoever, NO agent nanos a team wants, and NOTHING worth casting in agent form.
So then, what good is an agent if he can't outdamage everyone
in the team?

Everyone around him is doing the same damage but with the benifit of superior defenses, and their own unique class nanos+abilities.

Don't start on aimedshot. NT's and doctors are getting 10k aimedshots with only minimal points in concealment.
Aimedshot damage is determined by the gun. While a higher aimedshot skill will unlock higher AS multipliers, accually getting them is NOT dependant on your AS skill or your concealment. It's based on your crit chance. Total BS.

That's not even getting into the fact that 90% of aimedshots only do the max damage on your gun. Ignorant players see the 10k aimedshots and their little brains click that the agent is uber. They don't take note of the fact that the last 40 aimedshot were only the max damage on the agents gun (making them much less than bursts, on par with flingshot really).

So you think the agent offsets this with false professions? WRONG. The false profession is a handy tool, not a balancing device. It's like saying the fixer was fine (pre-patch) because he had green B&E, trap disarm, perception, and teal sneaking.
Much like false professions, those are handy things to have, but they don't make you any more powerful.

A teaming agent has two choices:
1. Do margionally higher damage than the other X-3 users in your group, but offering up absolutely nothing in terms of useful skills or nanos.
Or...
2. Go into an FP. Thereby losing your crit nanos and doing either the same or less damage than the X-3 users around you. So you decided to go FP? Well you quickly realise that anything worth casting has been made off limits to the agent. And 95% of the things still worthwhile to cast are things you will not be using in a mission. This means the agent ends up casting gimped versions of a handful of nanos throughout his life... Not the uberness and flexibility people imagine it to be.

Other classes get to have both their damage and unique nanos. Because the agent has nothing worthwhile to bring to a team on his own, he must always go into FP. Thereby living his life as an extremely gimped version of a crat with gimped damage because the only thing teams want are the XP buff. He would have been better off making a crat that uses an X-3.


Soldier

Soldiers suffer from the same problem as the agent. Anyone can pick up their guns and do just about as much damage as them. At least with assault rifles this isn't possible until very high levels when you have reems of IP to blow on all those requirements.
An assualt rifle with specials is also very difficult to implant for. Favoring those greatly who can self equip all those requirements and specials.

Even when players are doing damage close to the soldier, it is still has a solid class structure to fall back on. Having good evades, HP, and excellent defensive nanos.
Unlike the agent who has nothing to fall back on if he can't deal the most damage.

The soldier has some bugs to address, plus some issues like specials being totally gun based instead of skill based (favoring those who barely fit into the guns they use), and the issue of others using their guns too well.

But overall the soldier is a solid class that is equally good in teaming, solo, and PvP.

Engineer
The engineer is even more gimped than the agent. Something must be done to the engineer immediately.

The engineer is dead weight to a team. Offering up nothing at higher levels.

The only reason these guys are still around is because they make the best tanks on unique mobs.

Issues to address:
-AC means nothing past a certain point. Fix this or remove the AC buffs.
-The engineer should be able to stack multiple auras at once. This would sap up all of the engineers NCU to run multiple auras, but it would be a good sacrifice for the team.
-Lower NCU on damage shields and AC buffs, or increased effectiveness.
-PET PATHING.
-More gadets. Engineers should have gadets to do all kinds of stuff for a team instead of nanos.

MA

Funcoms new lovechild.
Uber damage, uber defense, healing.
The MA is a powerhouse, yet funcom continues to pack on goodies for the MA (All the MA only items and armor that keeps flooding the patches.)
Plus they are getting area fears now? Come on!
Don't ever give them something to break roots. It's the only way they die in PvP. A small price to pay IMO for that uberness.

Traders

The trader is the "new agent". Once powerful, now gimped and useless to a team, and because the traders use to be kings of the ring people will refuse to take their issues seriously - forever insisting they aren't really gimped.

The fact is, when healing was made useless on the trader it became quite clear how little they had to bring to a team.

The trader wouldn't be gimped though if FC gave their transfering nanos more usability and worth.

Lower their NCU DRAMATICALLY. Like 2 or 5. Also increase their duration to about 10-20 minutes.

This would make it viable for the trader to truely trade off his stats to boost teammates. Boosting their AC, HP, nano, and attack skills at the cost of totally gimping himself.
That is what the trader was suppose to be able.

Until this happens the trader is just a soldier with a shotgun that happens to be good at tradeskills.
