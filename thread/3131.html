Advisor / Board Member Chan
It's a well known fact that Organisation leaders can't be on 24/7 - so I suggest an advisor/Board chan so that organisations can keep up with events, co-ordinate etc. Many organisations have multi time zone members so it's logical that when the leader is off they can still stay in contact - at the moment we try to pass leadership within the upper members of the organisation, but can prove a complete pain doing this. I think this is a logical and good idea and would welcome your feedback.
