Stuff
1) How about a zoom feature? We can pull back very far until our characters are tiny but we don't have zoom. Maybe add it to a telescope item you wear. A regular telescope would give the best zoom. A night-vision or infra-red telescope (2) would have less. Perhaps make a small circular window that pop up when you activate the telescope. What you see inside is the zoomed view that's too far for normal eyes to see. Snipers would actually be able to snipe from max distance. You could even increase the range of weapons to make telescope use better.

2) Add infra-red goggles, telescopes and implants so we can see thermal images of people/creatures. Ya, I know it's more work but still...could look cool. We could then have creatures that are virtually invisible with normal vision. Maybe have creatures that sneak around so those with high Perception are the only ones who have a chance of seeing them unless you're wearing an infra-red item.

3) Make certain missions into caves pitch black unless you have night-vision or infra-red goggles/telescopes/implants or the floating torch. Have special creatures in the caves (mission) that only come out in the open (non-mission) at night.
