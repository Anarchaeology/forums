Find your way around.
Couple of things some people overlook when trying to find the mission area. 1. Look at your compass, there will be a yellow bar indicating the direction you need to go once you are in the correct area. Keep you compass on this bar and you will head right to the mission X. 2. Bring up the world map; you will see a crosshair representing your location and a flag representing the mission location.

As for the difficulty setting I personally have found no exact pattern this follows. Yes the mobs will be harder but not all of them. I have done missions set above mid and found mob higher/equal to my level as well as gray mobs. Maybe someone else has researched better than I.
