Extract specifically about Inner Sanctum ...


Content upgrade: Inner Sanctum (14.9)
The three main content additions in this latest upgrade was the Inner Sanctum dungeon, the Hollow Island camp, and a multitude of new and interesting items - many of them linked to the dungeon and the camp.

We've gotten some great feedback after the upgrade; mostly confirming what we had already heard during the time it was on test-live. I would like to thank everybody for this, and look forward to see you figure out the game-play, tricks and puzzles out there.

"And if at first you don't succeed, then dust yourself off and try again." - Try Again, Aaliyah

Some comments to the feedback so far:

1. Some items are not possible to over-equip, but are deactivated (all bonuses turned off) as soon as you don't meet any of the requirements. This is intended.

2. A bug causes some items to be deactivated upon changing playfield like described above, even if you do meet the requirements. Unequipping and equipping the item again will make it function properly. We're looking into fixing this as soon as possible, but don't consider it a showstopper that justifies an extra update right away.

3. We'll be evaluating the feedback and development around Inner Sanctum in the coming weeks, concerning temporary keys and the game play inside. We currently do not consider any of it in need of adjustment, but will be keeping an eye on the dungeon (i.e. we'll be there with you, trying to best it ).

4. The issue with members of the same team ending up in different spawns/copies (also named Group Selectors - GS) of a static dungeon is something we're looking into, and hope to change in the future.

---

INNER SANCTUM - your calm, constructive feedback

So basically, they are aware of certain bugs and issues but will continue to monitor feedback and development for Inner Sanctum. On that note, I'd like to start a thread here to gather feedback from fellow Adventurers as a significant amount of Adventurer-specific items was introduced via this dungeon.

Remember, they already know about general equipment bugs and GS spawn/copy issues, so let's provide them some other feedback about Adventurer items, loot drop rates, as well as any constructive suggestion for improvements.

Here's a very rough list to go by (feel free to add other relevant points in your own feedback):

* Adventurer-specific or related equipment/armour - usefulness, effectiveness
* Adventurer-specific or related equipment/armour - loot drop rates - excellent, good, fair, bad, etc
* Difficulty factor - from the point-of-view of individual/team/guilded/unguilded
* Fun factor - rate on a ranking from 1 to 10 (where 1 = yuk!, 10 = yay!)
* Revisit factor - whether you'll be back for more or whether you'll swear never to return, or anything in between

It will be useful to say what level you are and which dimension you play on as that will help explain the perspective of your post. And please keep your posts calm and constructive. Thank you.

Let's have the thread run for, say, about two weeks, then I'll bundle up a summary for FC.


Oh, if you do come across bugs concerning Adventurer-specific or related equipment/armour, it would be best to submit a descriptive email (with screenshot if applicable) to anarchy-bugs@funcom.com

So anyway, here we go, over to you now ...
