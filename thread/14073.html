IDEA: Perception tools should work for stuff over your level.
My suggestion.

With out dumping massive amounts of IP into perception these tools should work on mobs AT LEAST 2x there level.

Why? I tested out my level tool and HP/Nano tool. QL 22 both.

I can level ID and HP backyard lets - lev 1 with 20 HP

I can ID Trade trash bot - level 16 but can't ID HP (with a QL 22 TOOL!)

I can ID a trader in 20K - level 8 but can't ID HP (with a QL 22 TOOL!)

My suggestion revolves around the idea that until these tools can ID MOBs at least 25% over the level of there QL they will remain useless and only toys. Buff the tools!
