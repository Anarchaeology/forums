Autopsy idea
I originally posted this over in the doctor forum, but it probably belongs here.

Ok, how about we use these portable clinic things for an autopsy type function. When mob goes down, you use the portable treatment clinic on the body prior to looting. This gives a chance of finding an additional loot item that will be either a mob part or an implant, depending on the mob type. The QL of the loot item will be determined by the QL of the mob and the clinic.

Possibly give the screwdriver a similar function on robot type mobs.

Of course, there would need to be a check to make sure it's only done once per kill. It may also be desireable to limit it to those times when you have looting rights.
