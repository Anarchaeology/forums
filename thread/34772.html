Team boss loot for solo players
Nobody seems to like the fact that all new content is team mission boss loot only. I ceartainly don't. On the other hand, I understand FunCom's desire to keep these new items special, as in hard to aquire and/or expensive (actually, I don't, but let's pretend I do). So, I introduce my suggestion to let solo players have a go at the phat lewt: Linked Missions

I think an example would present this the best:

Mission 1: Locate and retreive Contained Sensitive Information from Greater Omni Forest
Mission 2: Decrypt CSI using Codebreaker-deluxe-what's-it-called-pro in Andromeda
Mission 3: Bring decrypted message to Alvin^H^H^H^H^Ha mob in Lush Fields

Each "link" would reward the player in normal solo mission manner, but upon delivery of the decrypted message, the player would be rewarded an item from boss loot table.

Why is this more difficult than any other three solo missions? Well, in order to get the final reward, you have to follow the linked missions, meaning you can't select your next mission's location and neither can you use ClickSaver to get the reward you desire. Either follow the next link, or miss the boss reward.
