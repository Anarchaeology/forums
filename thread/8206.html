Suggestion for an armour change.
Currently armour seems to block a set amout of damage, roughly it blocks 100pt of damage per 1000pt of AC.
This make faster low damage weapons unviable. The result being they hit for min damage constantly. MA and pistols suffer the most.

I suggest to make the armour block a percentage of damage per hit instead.

It should be roughly 10% of damage blocked per 1000 ac.

This would make fast low damage weapons viable.

At the highest levels, armour would block about 55% of the damage, assuming an AC of 5500.

As someone pointed out somewhere else, this would make armor useless at low levels. Personally I think both models are inadequate, having minimum damage and letting armor "absorb" damage by percentage or absolute amount has some weird mathematic consequences. In games like eq damage is calculated alot different, if you have good armor you can still get hit for max damage by any mob, however its very unlikely. Good armor means hits will be low on avg, there is no min damage, so you wont see the same number come up 1000times in a row. I think AO needs a more random system as well, where likelyhood of weak/hard hits are influenced by the ac, and chance to hit is more dependant upon defensive skill/atk. Today its almost completely predictable, if my evades and armor is good, I can say that mob a will hit me for say 128damage every 2 sec. Crits almost never happen, he almost never miss unless its a very low lvl mob.
