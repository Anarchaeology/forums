Dancing Atrox/Relax!
The Dancing Atrox in Omni Forest has been renamed the Relax Bar, due to the huge neon sign out front that says Relax! We here at Relax! Inc. would like everyone to check it out, its a happening little place on Tues, Thurs, and Fri nights. We have Live DJ's spinning tunes, taking requests, and generally wreaking havoc and having a good time. We have contests, prizes, and lots of interesting people show up. Last night I gave away a million credits just for knowing the name of the group that did a song I played.

To get there, you need to get to Omni Entertainment. Go out the East Gate. Go out past the 2nd wall there. Head South. You'll pass two small groups of buildings. Keep going. The 3rd group of buildings is where we are, neon sign, cant miss us. The coords are in my sig below, as is a link to the Neon Zebra site, where Tuxx so kindly snaps photos at some of our parties and posts the (sometimes) incriminating shots there.......*snickers*

Hope to see some of you there!
