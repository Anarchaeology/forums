Gathering of Souls Festival (GOS) - every Sunday
Sundays is the Gathering of Souls (GoS) Festival in West Athens on the bridge outside Old Athens West Gate.

A day when Clan Leaders and their members come together to share services with fellow Clansmen. Its a time we can answer questions and build relationships with other Clansmen.

Each week, the GoS is growing larger and larger with more participants offering:

- Buffs of all types from every profession
- Making of implants
- Converting of monster parts
- Weapon creation
- Be turned into an animal

The GoS are now having added features that the folks are offering:

- Fashion shows
- Swim meets
- Highest Jumper
- Best Dancer
- Nano shows to dazzle the audience young and old with brilliant display of colors
- Live tutorials of all the moves on Rubi-Ka
- Murder Mystery
- Boat races

Each week, there are loads one can do either by sitting and watching or taking part in any of the activities being offered. These are things that have been done at previous GoS, they may not be done at all of them in the future. It all depends on who shows up and gets something going.

The day of GoS  if anyone wishes to do something  they simply do a shout to get contestants and judges and they are added to the advertising shouts over the various channels.

Not one guild makes this works. This is a team effort by all Clan Houses that make this a success. It takes off on its own once the people arrive. Its not hard to do this type of event when you have so many so willing to take part.

It can last several hours and ends when the last folks depart. Start times are usually:
GMT  1600 - 4pm
EST  1100  11am
CST  1000  10am
MST  0900  9am
PST  0800  8am

So if you have not attended one yet, this is an invitation to stop by and check it out for yourself.
