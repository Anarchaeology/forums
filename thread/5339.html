Ip and skill attribution change? up and down
Hi,

Beeing inspired by UO which i played for almost 4 years *sights*, i was wondering why a "up and down" system hasnt been thought about for AO?

Many people want a IP reset because of the various "nerfs" that came along, and we all know it's near damn impossible NOT to waste ip's. Simply because you didnt know that nano you just bought was crap (read : looked good but results = bad) and you boosted your skills to get it, or because you want to upgrade your crap weapon for a new one waiting for a better one later and that your new one needs this and that skill the previous one didnt need In fact the IP system makes is that you cant choose for variety in the game. You chose that shotgun way? Dont even think of trying the rifle you just found or the nova someone gave you. You are stuck in the path you HAD to choose at the beginning of your char.

Instead of an "once-a-century" IP reset, why not impliment a way to decrease skills once you pass from level? Same as when you increase them. Not all of them of course, but lets say :

- when you get a new level, you get X amount of IP to spread among your skills right?
- each skills has a maximum of X points increase per level ok?
- Why not make that for example, you could lower the points of 5 skills per level?

concrete example :

You pass from lvl 50 to lvl 51
You distribute your IP, then decide you wish to lower some skills (5 max). Just get them down by the max you could get them up (ie 3, 4 or 5 points). The points you got down just add ip's to your pool, exact same amount it costed to raise them. If you try to lower more skills you just get a message like "you already adjusted 5 skills, wait for next level to make more changes". If you dont change your skills before next level, it's just lost, too bad for you.

That wouldnt change the shape of your char fast and directly, but could add variety and help people change path during the life of their chars without getting gimped because this or that skill they raised isnt usefull anymore or whatever...

Err, i hope i made myself clear..
Bare in mind this is just an idea..dont flame me, i hate that and i would have to cry

Another Idea...
Here's another idea...

Take this scenario....

I read patch notes, which state that the new patch will utilize this "scaling" IP method (as outlined in the first post on this thread, by Lyriam). More information is of course found on these official Anarchy-Online boards & in patch note details.

I log into AO and there is a message posted...

"Due to many changes in the game mechanics, Funcom is offering you a one-time chance to wipe your IP and reallocate. This is optional. When you exit this window, you will be brought to the Stat screen. You will see your current stats and may reallocate as you see fit. Any decrease in stats will dump the IP into your IP pool at the top of the screen. If you wish to keep the IP allocated as you have it presently, you may exit this window. NOTE: YOU WILL ONLY BE ABLE TO DO THIS <ONE TIME>. Funcom will not re-set any IP after you hit the ACCEPT Button. If you hit ACCEPT without making any changes, you will not be offered this option in the future."

...My IP allocation/stat screen appears. I notice there is no [CANCEL] button, only an [ACCEPT] button.

After I hit the [ACCEPT] button, whether I chose to keep my current stats (for RP purposes), or if I chose to reallocate (due to many changes in game mechanics over the past several months), I will now, like everyone else here forward, be subject to the scaling IP -as outlined in the first post on this thread.

While I know it isn't realistic to think you can please everyone - all of the time, I think this scenario may offer the most benefit for everyone, whether they be RPers, or people who's character's have been nerfed. I think this, or something like this, may satisfy the greatest number of players, because it leaves the option up to them, as to how they want to handle their own character.
Some may want to just leave their characters as they are, and if so, this option should allow that.

What does everyone think of this?
Let's hear from everyone? Pro's ? Con's?
