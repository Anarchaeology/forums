Okay, a few comments and suggestions.

You shouldn't have less than 3 moderators per forum area. Otherwise the personal opinions and occasional conflicts (everybody has them, nomatter how rare or common) can become out of control.

Moderators should have a seperate moderator account. Everybody develops a reputation over time and that shouldn't limit their ability to moderate without biased balls of bias.

Professionals shouldn't moderate their own forum. I'll be the first to admit that the professionals team is mostly topnotch and generally they're the first "people" you'd want as moderators. As "professionals" though moderating their own forum would be a conflict of interests. The professionals can't be an accurate representative of the community if they're censuing or are even given the mere appearance of censuring the that same community.

That said I hope some professionals would moderate other forums that they're not active professionals in. Just because they're professionals doesn't mean they're only interested in one profession (*cough* Letah).

Criticism and flaming should be carefully seperated. Things like (The mez pet bugs suck, my team lost 4 hours gameplay due to this bug) are legitimate complaints even if they may be passionately worded because of the players' frustrations. Not open to any criticism = not open to improvement. Funnel the player frustrations into appropriate constructive criticisms, but just corking it will create an explosive backlash against FC.

New Forums as I'd like to see them:

Suggestions > Not as a general forum as it was before, but a specific subforum of the Testserver forum. One thread for discussions on 40 patch suggestion areas is a dismal failure.

Roleplaying (Rimor and Atlantean) > There should be a general roleplaying forum for stories and things. Then there should also be server specific forums for events, org activities, recruiting, etc.

Mad On (Garbage Bin) > Sometimes players just need to vent the frustration of carefully spending weeks decorating an apartment to have the next patch delete it. FC employees should have this forum hidden from them.

Breeds > "This isn't the X Breed forum". Getting real tired of reading that. What is the best armor for an Opifex? What professions is Nanomage good at?

Teaming > People not wanting to go to Broken Shores can't just go to Newland or Tir South gate and LFG. "Can somebody help me with Neleb?" and "Anybody want to go kill Jack?" belong here.

Them be my suggestions.
