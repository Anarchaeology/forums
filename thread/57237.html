Finally you kinda reach a level plateau where you just can't take any more missions. Then you have to ask yourself what your REAL goal is, and how badly you want to reach that goal.

When I hit 175, I decided my goal really *is* 200. While all of my friends are dreading missions as much as I am, they simply can not bring themselves to do them.

I on the other hand decided came to the realization that if I want to hit my goal before 2005, I'm going to have to spend a month or so just doing mission after mission. Sure it's not as fun, but so far it's given me a sense of accomplishment to hit 190 after just a few short weeks. I hope to hit 200 in the next 3 weeks or so, but these are going to be some of the hardest levels to do.

I know for me, as I get closer and closer to my goal, my motivation to level is actually going up. But that's probably just because I want to reach my goal so badly. Most people who want 200 just want to be 200 and are not willing to put in the work to get there. They don't want it enough, so they get depressed, realize they don't have the perseverence required to get there, and stop playing their characters entirely.

Kinda unfortunate. The levelling blues certainly aren't fun, so ask yourself what you really want out of the game I'd have to say I do actually have respect for those 200s out there for biting the bullet and being able to spend so many mindless hours missioning, and I hope to join them all soon.

Exp per kill
Exp per kill doesn't really go up after level 160.

By level 160 you've hit the next title cap and pretty much all your stats are also capped by your breed selection.

This means you can only tackle missions under the same level of difficulty and thus earn the same exp per kill from levels 160 to 200. This is probably around 45K exp per kill in a group of 6.

It isn't worth tackling missions outside of the level 190 to 194..

Missions under 190 have lower level mobs which give less exp.

Missions over 194 have Real Meanies and Ace type mobs which can unleash some major hurt. Real Mean Bullies critical hit for 1K like every other hit sometimes..
