Init changes in 14.6 (not confirmed by FC, this is my BEST understanding)

Fixes to various weapons with bugged inits (BBI, Manex, etc.)

The bonus to Recharge will no longer be determined by the IP spent in the skill unaffected by trickle-down, implants, buffs, debuffs, etc. Instead, it will be affected by the players current init skill. For weapons only (i.e. no change to nanoinit).

In other words, scopes will now increase recharge time instead of leaving it unchange, implants will now reduce recharge time, and ability trickle-down will affect the recharge as well.

Doc debuffs are about to get nastier, given that they've only been affecting half the weapon cycle to date.

edit: The part that is my understanding is that Recharge will remain -1/300 skill. The rest has been confirmed by Cz in LucidFlow's "Fix inits" post.
