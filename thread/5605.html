It really depends on your class. At level 50 i can stand toe to toe with any level 60 MP and take him down every time. I can simply do damage faster than his pets can heal the MP. Agent's are tricker with mp pets because they do enough damage to usually require me to heal 1 or 2 times. This will cost me attacks and allow them to get back a lot of the damage I've caused, but I have taken down 60 agents with MP pets too. I have only met doctors at my level never had to face one much higher. I can take them down too. About the only thing that really challenges me are traders. Personally I think traders are by far the best pvp class. They have many options and can really be very tough. Yes the vektors are nice, yes you can buff into uber armor, but the real strength of the trader is the combo of roots, de-buffs and wrangles. Every lost MP pet in 2ho is a wrangle waiting to happen. Traders have some healing abilites too with their health freeloaders and can also steal nano from their targets. Traders are, IMO the most complicated class to PVP with, because you have many options on how to fight, and each class you face will require you to adjust your tactics.

My guess is you are playing a soldier or an agent, some class that has no healing abilites at all. Below 150, those classes do poorly in PVP now for the most part, but as you have stated agents have many options.

Sub 150 Soldiers can do well in pvp but go with a doc friend to 2ho and avoid arenas all together. You need someone to heal you if you are going to PVP. If you have a soldier and you want to pvp seriously, level him past 150 to 160 or so, and then you'll have more fun.
