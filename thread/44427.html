Here's an idea that might make soldier PvP balanced vs healing classes!
Ok first off lemme say: I'm not a soldier. I'm a MetaPhysicist.

But my first char to lvl50 was a soldier, I team with a couple very skilled soldiers every day, and I often fight/watch soldiers in PvP.

I've read it all... all the whines, flames, etc etc about healing, reflect bracers, and the general gimp-hood of soldiers.


I truly like soldiers and what they're capable of, and i would truly like to see them accell in PvP. I, like you, think it's wrong that any class that can heal can easily destroy a soldier in a fight.

So I brainstormed a bit. I took into account why they get smacked down so often, and thought about possible counters. Most don't work. 50% healing wouldn't work... nerfing bracers might help a bit, but would unbalance other aspects of PvP... 100% dmg... uhg I don't even wanna think about that.

In the end I narrowed it down to a simple nanoformula that might be able to fix the entire healing vs soldiers problem. I've talked to a few different high lvl (160+) soldiers about it, and they love it. I've thought about downsides... and so far I can't see any. But I know I have a habit of missing things right under my nose, so I'll put the idea to the test by introducing it to all the soldiers here. Feel free to tear the idea apart. If it ends up being horrible, I'll go back to the drawing board.

THE NANO!

The idea is for a simple line of nanos, spaced about as evenly as the TMS line... each one would have a longer duration, but not increased affect (like the TMS's reflect dmg).

It's just an anti shield. The nano would have a nearly insta-cast speed (like TMS), and would be offensive (so it can only be cast on an opponent). The shield would simply shut down any healing on the target. I toyed with the idea of reflecting any hp healed as dmg, but the thought of a doc massacreing himself with CH was too much to bear (pretty damned funny too).

It could be like layers, that absorbed any healing instead of dmg, and had unlimited layers.

The lifespan of the nano would be similar to TMS... 20 seconds up to around 90 seconds.

THE DOWNSIDE!

Just like TMS, the nano would shut down your nano-using capabilities for an ammount of time... perhaps a flat 2 mins like TMS.

THE CONCLUSION!

In a fight, soldiers would be given a choice. Against non-healing classes they could stick with TMS and soak up the dmg while dealing out as much as possible. Nothing changes.

Against healers however, instead of using TMS, a soldier could choose to rely on his own dmg taking capabilities, and instead make it a dmg war with the opponent by shutting down the opponent's ability to cast. This would make docs MUCH weaker targets, but not entirely, as they still have nasty DoTs, and init debuffs.







That's about it. I apologize for the long post, and I hope i didn't leave anything out.

If you like the idea, lemme know. If it sucks, lemme know. I'm all ears.
