Instant healing / restore, and unlimited nano for self buffing. This would not seriously affect gameplay as you cannot really do much damage right next to the apartments anyway. This would be very helpful for the putting your game face on / self buffing that comes as part of every mission.

Expanded item interaction. I want to be able to furnish my apartment, and then actually recline on my furniture. some of my characters want to make there apartments into mini temples, some into major shag pits. (complete with shag carpeting and wallpaper)

Allow others into your appartment, but not take advantage of special features there.

Expanded bank access functionality. I often find I have many backpacks to sort through after an evening of missions, It would cool to be able to open four or five backpacks at once, have the bank behave more like a backpack when you are in your apartment and so on.
