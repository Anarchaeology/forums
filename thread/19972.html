Solo?

Well I'd recomment what used to be the most popular hunting area but is now deserted.

Galway

Head out from Rome Blue follow the road, head right when it forks at a BB, zone at a bridge and from there on there is fun stuff. Ob your right you have lots of snakes and bulls. Keep following the road and you'll find lots of harder things like night reets and stuff and if you go far enough on your right you'll find Hammers & thistles (watch for the aggro, they are far too hard for you at the moment).
