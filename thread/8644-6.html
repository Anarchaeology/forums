I never have, nor never will have a complaint about someone being able to self equipp something, and able to maintain that level of skill. Thats by design. Those professions are supposed to be the best at those skills, thats why they get buffs to put them above what other professions could normally do.

Being able to buff yourself up higher than other professions to be able to wield a higher QL weapon than other professions is by design. Nothing wrong with that at all.

Being able to get wrangles to equipp a weapon above and beyond what you could normally equipp fully buffed, is overequipping.

This is not rocket science here. Seems to me the people who are for overequipping have the hardest time being able to see this simple concept.
