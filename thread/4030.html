Idea to make pvp interesting and get ppl motivated toward it
Right now pvp is really boring, you either get wasted by some grid campers or wander round the pvp zone for half an hour trying to find someone near your level...

So here is my idea(some of it based on my time in Tribes):


Have a bunch of small playfields that only lvl x - y players can enter, this ensures everyone in a particular playfield can fight each other. A uniqely designed playfield for each set of lvls so they arent all just identical.

When player zones, all buffs drop, this ensures noone is uberbuffed by a higher lvl player. Omnis would zone in to one side of the playfield at a small omni camp with 'very powerful' gun turrets to protect them while they buff, similarly, Clanners would zone in to the other side of the playfield with their gun turrets to protect them while they buff.

Now in the center of the playfield there would be a fort/tower/etc.. It would have high walls around it and only 1-2 gates, the current defenders would be able to have their ranged weapon players man the walls while enfs and pets could protect the gateways, etc..


Now here is the crunch: there needs to be some real and tangible motivation for players(every player) to come and defend/assault the stronghold. Some meaningless title doesnt do it for me and I doubt it does it for 95% of the rest of the players either. I have some ideas but don't really know the implications on balance and gameplay that they would have:

1)neutral vending machines in the stronghold(outside where shoppers can be wasted if noone is set to defending) could buy your loot at a better rate and sell some things at a cheaper rate(armor comes to mind, do you think anyone pays the shops 500k for one piece of armor?)

2)there could be a stationary device in the stronghold, when clicked on by a clanner it becomes clan, when clicked on by an omni it becomes omni. This device would periodically(per minute or something) deposit money to each friendly player in the playfield. higher lvl playfields would give more money obviously.

3)Participating in the playfield awards tokens in some way.. dunno what though..

4)defeating an enemy in the playfield awards you with lottery ticket which you can use on npc/machine/whatever in the stronghold to win a totally random item(with the possiblity of getting some really cool items).

ok, server should be up by now 8)
