Open Letter to Funcom/community/events/and so on
Hello.

First I want to start by thanking Funcom for deciding to keep the RP Forums, as I believe that a RP community is really important to the survival of Anarchy Online. I did not get as concerned as many people on this board did, but then again, I am not as active on this board as many people are. They spoke out, you listened, and the result is one of promise.

With that in mind, I have decided that I would make a post in this forum (and will e-mail as well) with some concerns I have as a Role Player, and customer of AO. For the last several months I have been watching the Forums and reading about people not satisfied with the state of the storyline, upset at the lack of Social content and a general feeling of "they don't care" when it comes to the Role Play community.

I wanted to try and make a really constructive post, so bear with me if I go long, as I sometimes get sidetracked when typing, which is part of why I get flamed a lot

1. RP vs PVP : OK, we all know that PvP'rs have been the loudest voices of complaint in these forums, and I believe they have certainly been responsible for a lot of changes to the game (Notum Wars) But I think it is really important to not forget that they are only one aspect of the game community. They may have perhaps been the most vocal, but from my in game experience, they are not the most in numbers.

I lead a org on Atlantian for a long time (was E-corp, then Omni-Com as Jesska) and we tried desperatly to be a RP organisation. The guild fell apart after a period of time, because the members started leaving the game, due to a lack of flexibility, story and progress made to further the experience of the world (as a living breathing planet). I know that my org was only one small group of people, but from reading around the forums for a while, I have seen a rapid decline in Role players for these same reasons, and even more so, who are begining to lose thier faith for these reasons. It is no secret that a lot of your customers, are fustrated with not being heard. Without a RP community, AO is just like any other MMORPG, even if you do get the story going again...does it matter if no one is left to want to interact with it?

I am NOT saying the pvp'ers should be ignored. I am saying that the Role Players, Pvp'ers and Casual Players should all be treated with the same respect, and be heard. Not just be heard, but see steps being taken to show that them being here matters. Saving this forum was a step in the right direction. Don't let it stop there.

2. Story vs Events

This one is trouchy for me. I see people all over the boards (and other boards) complaining that there is no story, yet I myself have witnessed a lot of events in game, created by Green named people, and that has been a real bonus to make the world a more interactive environment. After giving this some serious thought, I think I may finally see what my confusion has been, and what the real complaint is.

Yes, these "greenie" types add a element to the Role Play in game, and sometimes thier small involvement in what we as players are doing, can make a world of difference, but they are not Furthering the main story that we are all waiting in anticipation to feel a part of. News Articles have been posted...chapters of the book leading into the game have been posted...However, I get the feeling people didn't sign up to read a story, but be a part of it. Sometimes we see the game world impacted by what is posted, but where is the transition....where is the involvement.

From a couple other posts I have seen, I can thknk of a couple things that may have been able to help in this area, and they really don't have to be huge. I will use the sentinels taking over tir for a example. Servers went down for a patch. when they came back the sentinels were all of a sudden there. No one saw them come in, silverstone never made a speach...it was set up in a way, that we are left to guess how it happened. Obviously, a change like this is planned, so why not, before patch time, bring in the sentinels, and let them start to wander through Tir, shooting Omni who are there, shouting what they are doing. This goes on for a while...servers go down...patch made...servers come up...there they are...liberated Tir.

This would no longer be a "oh gee...when did that happen" as much as a "wow...this really happened". I understand you will not always reach everyone with a event like this...there is no way you can...but for the people it does reach, you have just improved the belief that there is a story. Now lets look at the other end. Omni tek. No response when the sentinels liberated Tir...you think their would have been. How can that be improved? Even a statement from Ross's office....in the form of headline news, could have made a difference there.

I don't think what Customers are asking for is too much. It is little things like this, done more often that can make a world of difference. how about even a sentinel in Tir (non NPC) now and then to talk to the people....see what orgs are doing...spreading Sentinel propaganda...now and then...not always...but once in a while. This too can make a difference.

This is only one suggestion, and I am sure many other people in the RP community have others, but all I think we want is steps to be taken to involve us more, and make these world changes, and story changes feel more like things that are really happening.

I want to point out however, this does not mean we want the Greenies to go away. I love Role Playing with them, and would certainly miss thier presence. We just want to see more of the StoryLINE rather than just stories. I also understand you probably have somewhere you want to go with the story, but please don't close it off to players, so they can have no impact. There are ways to get to the end you want, even with letting other things affect the world we play in.

3. new armour and weapons vs Social clothes and Guild housing

New armour and weapons and items are very cool things to have, and they certainly play well for the mechanics, and pvp'ers...but RP'ers have been pretty much ignored for the most part when it comes to items we have asked for for a long time. I am not going to say much here, as they have been said a million times before, but Guild housing and Social clothes/items even more social environments would really add to a Role Players experience in Game.

Please do not take lightly these things. I understand from a perspective the order things should go with patching. Bug fixes first, and I will not dispute that, and I don't think anyone else will either. But content should not be just for the casual players, pvp'ers and lvlers...but should also be for the community players...social players...and role players. Give us a couple small things in each patch....doesn't have to be 100 mb of social clothes...maybe just a item now and then....really will make a world of difference.


I thank you for reading and hope I made sense. I really hope you take some of this into concideration. Again thank you for the step you took in saving this forum, and please do not let the steps end here. Thank you and good day
