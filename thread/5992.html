guild houses, guild cities
I like the idea of guild houses and all, but you will ned OPTIONS. you should be able to get a mansion, or a military barrack, each with its own bonuses. Mansions should have a theater or meating room for anouncements while barracks have a lunch hall or training ground. Both places should have PvP PRACTICE zones (the kill/death do not count for anything, no freshmen, no novice) through the rest of the place it should be 25% suppresion. You should be able to have rival guilds in each side, like the OoC and the Dark Carnival can fight each other, and so on. You would get a better defended guildhouse with the military design, but a much nicer one with the mansion. You should also be able to put in guard towers, sniper posts, and training buildings for each type of guild house at will (as add ons). You should also be able to hire guards with one initial payment and in hourly payments. The new guards you hire should get there in an hour and start defending the place, but guards can only defend and not attack a rival guild house. you should not be able to steal anything from guild houses though, well not much at least but you can gain PvP skills and stuff like that. Balconies would be very nice and also a lot of the fun stuff, like a guild armor, weapon, other things shop so you can give stuff to who ever needs it, you will have to pay for it, but less then what you would at a store. This is set up so that no one goes from guild to guild and steals everything they have. guild masters and a group of the higher ranking people should be able to buy the guild house itself, with all the included people aproving, but the guild master or the higher ranked members can each buy guard buildings and upgrades. You should be required to pay rent for the land, not building. There should be a maintinance cost or similar after an attack to fix the place back up and repair the machines(maybe fixers can do some of this, it would make them much more valubul to the guild if they could cast a few nanos and repair the entire building instead of having to order all new machines). Guilds should have temporary goals posted in a record hall. There would be a lot less stuff for a cheaper guild house which would require less land but the military/luxury places should have most of the same type of stuff (give or take a few luxury items/armory).

this would add a lot to roleplaying and make the game much more fun.

Ok, Just a little bit of explanation of the name 'fixer.' Maxtor, its apparent you have little understanding of the street slang.

Fixer does not refer to an ability or affinity for repair. Fixer refers to the ability to 'fix' or repair a situation, not a device. Someone is coming after you? The fixer finds you a gun with a filed off serial number. You in trouble with the law? The fixer finds a way to get you out of there - as quickly as possible. You need your fix of illegal stims? The fixer has your back there too. You need someone removed? Well, the fixer knows someone who knows someone who would be willing to do the job - and he'll get the job done, for a price. Now, say you need a modification on your gun, the fixer may indeed have and know how to install a 'splatter pack' - but that device was outlawed by both OT and the CoT due to the horrible disfigurements it left its victims with. The fixer is not the cable guy, the fixer is the guy who sells you the little box to steal cable.

But, light and heavy construction? Leave that to the atroxes, thats why they carry around the support beams and hammers. The fixers would only be interested in stealing the hydraulics off the cargo loaders in order to make their Yalmahas hop.
