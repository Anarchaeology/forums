Greed is an interesting thing. I'm gonna dust off an old psych experiment I'm sure anyone who ever took psych 101 would recognize.

The scenario is this: A group of people are sitting around a table, the guy running the experiment places a bowl of cash (equal to the amout of people at the table) on the center of the table.

He goes: alright, when I say go, you guys can take money from the bowl. Here's the thing. As long as there is at least 1 dollar left in this bowl every 15 seconds or so, I'll add another dollar. Up until a maximum of (whatever, a hundred bucks, 200 whatever) total.

So. Logically, if the group went in turns, each taking a dollar one after the other, and spacing it out so the guy kept adding a dollar, they would each end up with a fair size of loot when the test was over.

What always happens?

Well, as soon as the guy says "Go" everyone lunges for the cash, sometimes even fighting over the dollar they may have grabbed the same time as someone else.

Instead of waiting for the big score that benefits everyone, people usually grab what they can, as soon as they can. Screw systems, screw fairness, screw long term view
