What is a good two player duo?
A friend of mine wants to start playing AO. I am going to start a new character with him so we can level together. Right now I have a level 103 Fixer and to be honest, I am just tired of being gimped. No. Lets not start that debate here. Id be more than willing to do that in another thread. I simply want to play the game and have a good time without having to wait for some magical patch to make it enjoyable. I also want my friend to have fun and continue to play.

So here it is. He wants to play a fighter type so that means a Soldier or an Enforcer. Right now I am leaning towards recommending an Atrox Enforcer since the only comments I hear about them is that they are good all the way to level 200.

I want to pick a complimenting profession that I would have fun playing. My only requirement I have is that is has healing capabilities.

Candidates:


Doctor
Pro: Cant go wrong with tank-Doc combo. When we start grouping, getting a team would be really easy.
Con: Not sure if the class fits my personality. I like a little more offense. I can see myself just following him around with my finger over the cast heal button.

Meta Physicist
Pro: Well, they are uber.
Cons: Chasing down pets that get lost could get annoying. Nano buffs dont compliment an Enforcer well (I think). Not sure if healing pet will be enough.

Trader
Dont know enough to say anything except that I got the feeling that an overequiping or shotgun nerf will happen and make this profession less powerful than it seems right now.

I am leaning towards an Enforcer/Meta combo. Comments welcome.
