GUILD Houses
I know is has been several discussion about this topic, but I want everyone to post what they want to see, but don't repeat yourself :-)

1. Every member of a guild get an apartment in the same house and have his/her key to this department. It's possible to invite ppl into their department by typing his nick.
2. The guild house is perhaps devided into sections for the leaders and ordinary members? And a common area in the guild house for every player at Rubi-Ka (can set up restrictions, eg. no clans, only one profession, etc etc)
3. The look of the guildhouse (inside) will change when your guild bank (type /org bank) increases in value. Perhaps the guild can only get a guild bank when it reaches 500k creds? I was told that the bank draw 1 cred of each member that play longer than one hour, not sure..
4. Possibility to make a guild uniform of a set of clothes that is available.
5. Guild bank and shops, but only when the guild is rich (again /org bank)

Please make a point or two, but it can not be things that can be different for each guild (such as guild clothes, cannot design own clothes, but u can make a guild uniform of a set of clothes available), it has to be common or a set of stuff that is available for a guild (because of the design of AO)

I put up an "expanded" version of this a while ago, I included attacks, guests, guards, training grounds, PvP practice ones, auditoriums, and tons of other stuff. Sadly, most of the thread was ruined by a group of flaming fixers who did not want to become more powerfull and important.

I think the guild houses should be somewhat like a city inside of a huge building but it is very unlikely that Funcom will ever make them like that.

I also sent a few e-mails to FC suggusting they could throw in proffesion-specific meeting rooms so people could get advice and not spam the entire guild chat, as they would use the vicinity chat.

The idea for seperating the house between lower ranking members and the leaders will most likely be put into the game as long as FC makes the guild houses correctly and offers several types of them. These houses have been on the envisioned and in-progress lists for a long time but very little resources have gone into them, if any.
FC should begin these houses after the 14.2 patch while working on balencing proffesions, but it will take them a long time to complete these. Also, an entirely new zone would be needed to hold the guild houses once they are completed.


Many problems can appear with this; lots of guilds have exploiters who can give the guild bank an almost unlimited supply of credits and would ruin the entire balence of guild houses. Most of these exploiters have run out of stuff to buy, but if things as expensive as guild houses come along major imbalences will enter the game and exploiters will get the better of it.

the guild clothes should be in shops inside of the guild houses, all the clothes will look similar but have the different guild names printed on them along with the symbol of which faction they are in.

I hope they add this stuff in someday and put it in correctly, I also hope that I can make sure there are no bugs with it and have fun exploring the houses on the testserver.
