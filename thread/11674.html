COSMIK, shield suggestion to hand over to the dev team!
In one of the latest news (not the fixer's, the other one with Gaute), Gaute pointed out that we should have the best deflection shield in the game.

To help you out with that idea, I have a few suggestions.

My suggestion is: raise all that nano line's buffs by 10 percent, 15 at most.
Then add those 3 features:
_The shield absorbs the damage PER HIT or PER ROUND;

_When going full def on the agg/def bar, the shield absorbs 50 percent more of what it absorbs originally. For example, a shield that absorbs 80 damage would absorb 120 damage instead. Of course, you could go a tad on the defense bar and get a bonus, say every 10 percent? A little on def would add 10 percent, going a bit further would go for 20 percent and so on, to a max of 50 percent when reaching the full def. Remember, every shield only absorbs one type of damage, so this suggestion would be very fair IMO and not only that, but you would still have problems if you would want to heal yourself since you would cast the nano program slowly.

_When going full agg on the agg?def bar, the shield would increase the damage sent back to the enemy, up to 50 percent more when reaching the maximum in agg on the bar. For example when the enemy attacks and get hit back by the shield that would do originally 40 damage, it would do 60 damage instead if the adventurer has set his agg slider on max.

_Also, the damage sent back should be doing something about between 30 to 50 percent damage of what the absorbtion does. I mean that if the shield absorbs (the base score; not counting the modifications mentioned above) 50 damage, then it should hit back with 10-25 damage the enemy.

Those suggestions would definitely make us the 'masters' of such shields and sought upon within groups. Imagine the tanker in the team being agged. Going full def, you would 'self defense' with the damage the shield sends back and absorb valuable damage, hoping that it will take the agg off you or waiting for a precious good ol' doc heal. Now that shield would help us much wether going solo or in a team. Heck, it would really help in making us 'shine' when alone... Get it? Shield... shine... no? You don't? Oh you did get it...
