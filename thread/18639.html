Well, I've experienced insane lag in a number of zones. Some I have firgued out a way around though:

Pleasent Meadows: This is a problem with the number of players. When I enter from Harry's instead of whompa to 20K, I have no problems.

Broken Shores: Same problems as PM. Once away from people, I have considerably less lag.

Clon****: This is a strange one but I've verified it, SOUND. Yep, the Notum Cannons over in the OP in Clon**** are actually the cause of the incredible lag there. Aparently, the effect which causes my screen to shake from the cannons is also turned off when I turn the sound off. I fly through Clon**** with absolutely 0 lag whatsoever now that I mute the sound.

Those are the 3 zones which are actually lag caused by either players or client-side problems. Elsewhere however, I have noticed that I get very erratic pings lately. This is only somewhat recently as I did not have this problem in 13.8 even. My ping will many times sit at 20 for several minutes, then spontaneously jump to as much as 16000. It will then stay that way for a few minutes and go back down again. It jumps around all over the place like a mad bull. I have reduced this effect by muting sound and lowering graphical detail. This has led me to believe it is in many cases an IRQ issue. That is because my video card, my sound card, my USB bus, my serial bus, my PC card bus, AND my network card all use IRQ 9. Darned Windows 2000 *mumbles something obscene*.

The funny thing is, as clogged as my IRQ 9 may sound, I have no problems whatsoever with any other games, including online ones, and as I said before, I didn't have this problem before 13.9. That means that although there is a way for me to reduce the rubberbandlike ping, it may actually be more of an issue with the way AO is sending data through my network card.
