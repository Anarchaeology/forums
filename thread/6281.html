Economy of over-equipping leaves some classes out
The economy of Rubi-Ka currently circles around one phenomenon: over-equipping. The only sellable skill (if clicking an icon can be considered a 'skill') is selling buffs. The tradeskills that Funcom have attempted to introduce have all been bugged and horribly over-priced. Yet people are making as much as 200-300K on the higher buffs that allow for overequipping of weapons, implants, armor, etc.

So by far the best way to make money is to buff people (MUCH more efficient than missions). But there are some classes completely left out of that loop because they aren't given the buffs necessary to over-equip (a strategy that Funcom has made mandatory with the increase of mob Health in response to the over-equipped classes). Most notable of these are the crats, adventurers, NTs, engineers, MAs and fixers. Most ironic is the fact that three of those groups, the crats, engineers and NTs are doubly gimped because they can neither sell the buffs necessary to over-equip nor over-equip themselves since they rely on nanos and Funcom has decided that only certain professions should be allowed to over-equip.

NTs and engineers can make implants, of course. Not as good money as buffing, but it helps out. But Funcom has to face the economy that they have created and either do something about over-equipping or give all classes over-equipping style buffs. Or, if they are feeling really creative, implement a reasonable tradeskill system (though that still wouldn't help the adventurers or fixers).
