*Rant warning* a little respect wouldn't hurt
Ok, rant on

I run a fairly succesful AO help website for those who don't know me, and today I found a couple of things that, I must admit, have got me a little riled.

While scanning boards and other sites for tid-bits of info about the new patch, I stumbled across a few guild and other sites that had copied and pastes large sections (in one case complete guides) of my site into theirs.

*I'm not going to say who as I'm not into name calling, sufice they know who they are!*

Now I have no problem with this as long as the site is credited, and preferably with a link.

One of the culprits in particular disappointed me, a major, well known clan, and they had even copied the screenies I took for the guide in question as well. To top it all off it had been signed as produced by a member of that guild.

Now maybe I'm being a little precious, but I spend a lot of time, reseacrhing, taking screenies and publishing the site, keeping it bang up to date, with twice weekly updates.

The lack of respect this shows (at least in my eyes) has really wound me up today.

Maybe I'm being unfair, but is a link too much to ask????
