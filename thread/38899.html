Nanostealth's Avengers is recruiting
I am happy to announce that Nanostealth's Avengers is now open for new members.

Nanostealth's Avengers was created because of the great loss of Nanostealth. Nanostealth was a good and mighty Nanotechnician, and is missed all over Rubi-Ka. But many of the evil citizens of our great planet made fun of him, called him names, and eventually made him leave Rubi-Ka forever.

We are here to avenge this loss, a loss that can never be replaced. Without Nanostealth, Rubi-Ka has lost a person of great value.

We, Nanostealth's Avengers, will avenge him, we will do everything in our power to take revenge on the persons who didnt treat him with alot of respect. We will gank, grief, ninjaloot, KS, and in general, do everything possible to make life as hard as possible for those involved in Nanostealth's disappearance.

Nanostealth's Avengers is a Clan organization.

How to join? You need to know the basics of the game, like how you send a /tell, and how to equip a weapon. Basically, you need to know the manual of AO, and how the game works. You dont need to be an expert, I just want you to know the very basics of the game.

This guild is ideal for alt's, if you got an alt you want to have some fun with, I promise 100% discression, I wont tell anyone who you really are, and you dont need to tell me either, so it shouldnt be a problem.

Ok, if you want to join, you send a /tell to one of the following fine persons:

President: "Garzuperman"
Unit Commander: "Caernd"
Unit Commander: "Ashkenaz"

Or of course, you can leave your name here, and I will contact you, or you can send me a private message.

Regards

Garzuperman, President of Nanostealth's Avengers
