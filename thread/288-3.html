my comments on Doc playability:

Seems like those team heals for safe xp are a thing of the past. I really considered putting my doc to rest after the patch. People are saying doc's are not playable anymore.
Well i don't know the doc your playing with----but this doc Adjusted!!!

I hated the change and did my share of complaining. It IS furstrating to loose 800k+ xp when 6+ mobs aggro you after healing and no one can get the darn things off you long enough to sit and recharge that nano.
After a few days of constant dying (something new to me) I came to the conclusion that the doc profession were simply not playable. But I liked playing my doc and I enjoy AO, so I simply changed the way i played. In fact the whole team has had to change, not only the doc. You now work together and watch out more for each other. By the way /assist works great!

Team heals were the norm, the safe thing to use since the doc wouldn't get attacked. I sure used it alot--why not--cut down on having to save, and i was guarenteed to lvl. I could get 10 lvls in and never die, everyone on the team was basically safe as long as the doc didn't fall asleep.
Now its a challenge to keep myself alive as well as the team. I rarely use my team heal unless more than 2 members of the team are being attacked. I am enjoying my doc alot more because of the challange. When I get attacked by 6+ mobs now, no sweat...I know what I have to do to stay alive and keep my team going. Having a macro to quickly inform the rest of your team your in trouble is a big help. Communication and teamwork is the key to staying alive.
Yes, I've been on teams that don't work together and yes its hard to keep them healed, but you learn to work around it.

If you don't want to play a challenging game.........go haul out the old Nintendo for a relaxing game of Mario Brothers.

You doc's that say you can't play anymore, either change your strategy or start a different profession, because IMHO the doctor is very playable. You other doc's who are giving it an honest go...if your having problems and can't figure how to get around it, ask someone how they managed it.

BTW - I haven't changed my armor/weapon/implants since the patch except to up by Bio/Matt Met. I've invested in a greater single heal and I've gained quite a few lvl's by only changing the way I've played. The risk of dying is still there and I do occasionally visit the reclaim terminal, but I just save more often now. I'm having no problems with lvl'ing, and it's a joy once again to play Doc.

Well since I haven't pissed anyone off for awhile, I figure this should fill my quota til next time

To All Docs:

Obviously the hate list adjustment in 13.x changed the way mobs looked at us. It was quite obvious, that right after the patch something was definitely wrong. I don't care if a tank (and I travel with a few GREAT tanks) is pulling well or not, it is funny how mobs are instantly pulled to us. Even the best tanks in the game pull a few on accident, or have an extra spawn during the pull.

I have seen a tank run into a camp, he is so far from me that he diseappears from my team list (as all docs keep up always). Then bamb! Mob after mob is now displayed in my text window, and they are all coming after me. Keep in mind, the tank hasn't even returned (he has not yet appeared back on my team list). I am not even in the mobs line of sight; But they now know I am there, and they are sure pissed at me. Does this suck? Yes, of course it does. Will I stop playing my doc? Hell no.

I will admit that things got frustrating a few times. It kind of sucked hunting for an hour or two, being a kill or two away from leveling, then have a group of mobs come over and gleefully kill me in a couple of blows.

My point is, healing should affect the way mobs aggress. It should affect 'the hate list'. But the ratio should be set differently or something. Like a healing point should be calculated at 2/3 or 3/4 of a damage point for mob aggression.

Example -
Current:
Tank hits mob for 100, making him #1 on list.
Doc heals tank with target heal for 200.
Doc is now #1, is screwed, dies quick if mulit-mobs present. =)

Proposed:
Tank hits mob for 100
Doc heals tank for 200 (but is calculated at 3/4 for 150 on the hate list)
Tank just needs to hit again for 100 to put himself back up on the top of the list.

In this fashion, the tank gets to play his/her role, the doc gets to be a doc. I like to heal, not hurt. I carry a crappy shotgun, mostly just for looks. I heal clan, neutral and omni alike. I don't care about the "war", or about politics at all (with this character at least). I simply heal all who need it... please let me continue to do so. =)

I thought they were being attacked, I went over to help so they rooted me and killed me. Now, this incredible display of courage was AFTER I had put away my weapon. And AFTER I had asked them not to harm me. So the brave clan members killed me, while I was defenseless. So, all of you clanners, please pass on praise to Realmedic, Nutryno and Moobia.
