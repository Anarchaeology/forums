Shield/Reflect types and what they do:

ShieldAC - Sheild AC only does that amount of damage to your attacker. Shield AC does not reduce damage done to you. These are commonly referred to as Damage Shields.

ReflectAC - Reduces damage by that %. ReflectAC does not inflict damage on the attacker.

MaxReflectedDmg - This is the damage that gets reflected back on some Reflect shields (Like Soldier reflects). Pretty much the same as ShieldAC, only it's calculated as a different shield type. If you don't have a MaxRelectedDmg stat, your Reflect shield only reduces incoming damage!

ReflectAC with or without the MaxReflectedDmg component are commonly refered to as reflect shields. (As far as I know, there aren't any shields with just MaxReflectedDmg)

AbsorbAC - You will absorb this many points of damage. Once the shield has absorbed that many points of a perticular damage type, you will take damage normally. Commonly refered to as Absorb Shields.

Viable 2 Hand Edged Weapons

Seems like everyday there a post about what to do weapon wise. What works and what doesn't, where I should go after this, whats Uber and whats not. So I decided to throw together what I know. If you want to dissagree, thats cool, as long as its constructive and healpful.

2 Hand Edged normal (By normal I mean shop buyable or missionable)

Blunt Merchant Warblade Its fast and does decent damage. Very suitable for lower levels.
Infact my weapon of choice until you are able to reach Tsunayoshi Smith

Which brings us nicely to...
Meibutsu Katana of Celerity Good sword, a great option from ql 1 - 135
Meibutsu Dai-Katana The more astute among you will note this one has no FA req. Cheap on IP but means you don't have any IP in FA which is groovy for using a Flurry of Blows. I guess its just a question of your own preferance.

Smiths weapons are about your best choice up to ql 135 (Your level 90ish) After that you really want to be doing more damage.

Longmoon I wouldn't go so far as to say this a good weapon but it'll do untill you find something better. Like....
True Katana of the Loyal Samurai Its big!, its mean! and its slow as hell!.

I got mine equiped at about lvl 115. You need to dump IP in to melee init like it was going out of fashion but my goodness the damage is nice!
Big Katanas can't be bought in regular stores, but they are missionable and buyable in Trader stores they are also in good circulation and therefore fairly common. They really do benifit from having a large Flurry though.

2 Hand Edged UBER! (By UBER I mean special drops off Unique mobs or boss drop or just Feckin hard to find)

Energy Scythe This is good, but this is better....
Frost Scythe of the Legionnaire A serious bit of kit this. If Uber is your Game then this is your toy, until that is you can get

this....
Stygian Desolator If you thought the Scythe was mean you need to try this! (I still see folk my level weilding these and I'm 121!)

Those three can be found from various MoBs in the Temple of the Three winds. Also worth a look though are these...

Tangleblade Its good! Its real good! but its Boss drop only and its pretty rare. Not as rare however as...
Cuspidal Corpse Cutter Which is a real bad boy. Again though your going boss hunting for a rare drop.

Top of the line End game damage dealers...

Frost-bound Reaper Arguably the best 2HE weapon available in the game, certainly for use in Shadowlands. Massive Min damage means those hecklers will all love you teh most!

On RK however the FBR is beatable by these
Queen Blade
Enhanced Queen Blade
But only if your properly set up.

Other wepaons of note are
Abyssal Desecrator More based for PvP I gather friad I don't know much about it
EoT The deposed King Once the most awsome weapon we could aspire to but now relegated 3 or 4 places on the damage chain and so rare I wouldn't loose any sleep over it.

Coming soon updates on SL weapons.

Meantime though the Ax violeta is looking like it maybe worth a look.
