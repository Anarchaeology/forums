AO RPCentral Goes Public (again)
RPCentral Press Release
====================

Greetings everyone,

Another year, another forum closure. *sighs* I would have liked more time to work on it before going public but since we're up against a wall, I'd like to let everyone know about RPCentral

located at http://rpcentral.no-ip.com

It's a player-run site with the intent of catering to roleplayers of all types. The forum system has been up and running stably for the past couple months, as is the character sheet system. There's also a story system for those who wish to write and organize their own fan-fiction.

Future improvements in the works are a recommendation system ala Amazon where the site will highlight stories, forums, etc that match your likes. Org pages, etc.

But right now, I'd like to invite each and everyone of you on this board to visit and hopefully make it your new home. There's a community Lounge forum that I hope everyone here will continue to post to.

The site does require you to register your character via an in-game chat bot, but it doesn't require any email addresses or any other player specific information. Posting is done through characters, and for those of you running multiple characters, you can tie them together to make your visit easier.

Well, that's about all I wanted say. See you there.


Tap
