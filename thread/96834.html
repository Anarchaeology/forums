Mercury Dragons Clan - Roleplaying Org Looking for Members!
We're looking for lvl 50 or higher roleplayers who are able or would be willing to roleplay military roles. We're very active roleplayers, we have 3 Platoons (Delta for lvl 50-75, Alpha for lvl 75-120 and Dragon's Claw for lvl 120-200) and a very active Command Staff (lvl 100+), all mercury dragons are always willing to help the lower level members to improve and grow.

Only serious gamers/roleplayers should apply, it is hard for casual players to be in the clan, due to events/meetings and the happenings (events) in the clan which are mostly planned and partly improvised or dropped on our laps by our friends, allies and sometimes our enemies.

This is a strictly military clan, it requires maturity and responsablility even at the rank of Private. You may be called at any time to perform a mission/operation or assist a mercury dragon.

Been Active since Sept 2001, making it 16 months to date. The organization is made 100% of roleplayers or people who want to get into roleplaying.

We have our own Timeline=> Mercury Dragons Clan Timeline

A vast library of resources and tools, a dedicated Screenshot Archives of MD Adventures and Clan Bulletins.

Private Forums divided into two types, one set for ALL MDs, and another for Officers (They access normal and Command Staff forums).

Solid Bylaws to encourage roleplaying and the military role we've uphold.

Mercury Dragons are extremely loyal to the clan and to eachother.

We've gone through ups and downs like any other clan...but unlike many others, we're still around and will continue to be for years to come.

Very positive experiences from within the clan...join one of the most respected clans in existance.

We are one of the oldest clans around, and among the most respected due to the fact we take our position seriously (our clan is run by roleplayers, and it is required you be a roleplayer to join).

Do you want to make a difference?
Do you have the courage to fight against the odds?
Do you seek the discipline needed to be a true warrior?
Can you adapt to military life?
Do you want to join a clan lead by experienced officers?

If you seek to join our clan, please visit the Mercury Dragons Clan Grid Site.

To apply, please visit http://mercury-dragons.org and read the FAQ, Charter and Bylaws in our Sections before sending an application, it is required.

Thank you.
