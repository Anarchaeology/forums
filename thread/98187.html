ooc: RP and death (or lack there of)
ooc:

Talk about immortality through insurance on another thread got me thinking. From a RP standpoint, each time you did, you "revert" back to last insurance scan. That includes memories, thus the loss of exp.

Now, I have tried on occation to Rp this loss of memory after dying, but I have found I end up explaining why I dont know somoene I just met to much, but I think its alot of fun to do. It would be alot of fun for Rpers to do this, especially in a PvP setting. Imagine dying, but "not knowing who killed you". Could be fun Rp to try and figure it out and get revenge. Would you know if a person or creature killed you? Would you know where you died or what you were doing?

Obviously you could retain some knowledge, maybe of dramatic things that happen (like reaching a new skill level fo instance), but Rping memory loss has alot of potential.

Just looking for input/discussion about Rping exp/memory loss and insurance scans.

Any thoughts?

I've always thought of XP as, well, an experience or experiences. You do something to better yourself, to train and the experience of doing something improves your skill.

Take an example from RL that mirrors fighting in AO:

I study Martial Arts in RL. If I want to get some training in and do some sparring I should probably fight someone (spar with them, not just beat up a random guy on the street). If I fight a white belt, I probably wont gain much from the experience. Sure I can take the time to work on my technique, but the white belt wont be challenging me or showing me anything new. But, if I fight another black belt, the fght should be more challenging and I should improve my sparing a bit because of it.

Its like fighting a grey mob or a yellow. 1 XP or 5k. Of course in Rl you dont actually get points, but you do gain an amount of actual experience as in "Ah, that was a great fight, I really learned to keep my guard up when I kick!".

As for gaining levels, well, I usually do the same sort of thing. To me a Level in a game is like a "Certfication" or "Qualification" in RL. There are certain jobs or sports in Rl that rate skill and require testing to advance to the next certification level. I try to think of levels in a Game as the same sort of thing or simply as a "new level of ability". I'm sure we've all heard someone use that phrase. Maybe its not certification or anything actually, but in-game levels are a mark of ability.

My point in all of this, is that it is the actual experiences that lead to improvement, hence the name "Experience Points" which are in game mechanic for tracking and simulating RL experiences. There is no reason not to RP it if you can.
