Phantoms...
"Have you ever wondered if you're loosing your mind ? That something is wrong upstairs ? If so that means your safe....for the moment at least. Now on the other hand, the day you stop wondering if you're loosing your mind is when the real trouble starts."

"End log." spoke valaik as he sat in his chair deep within his Warehouse, the lights dialed down low.

Valaik leaned forward slowly, the events of the past weeks having taken their toll on him. Members of DA being kidnapped left and right and finally his own abduction. He still got phantom pains from the torture he had endured at the hands of Lehx but if he concentrated he knew it was just that, that the body that had been tortured was long gone and his new one was unharmed. What didn't help was that he found it harder and harder to focus lately, starting the same night as his abduction by Lehx and Chadul when he had come back to conciousness wandering the streets of West Athens, his head hurting badly. Later he found out that he had been kidnapped a second time that day but this time the people responsible were not known, his memory of the last couple hours having been wiped by some sort of stim that he found out about later at Baboons. Rumors were rampant about who could have kidnapped him. Don keep thinking it was SPARTA and with Bonefish's comments last night in The Cup, as well as the threats that he had made, caused the Spartans to be high on the list of possibles. Another less mentioned theory was the Dust Brigade, members of Devil's Advocate having reported Valaik saying that he was at the Barracks in Perpetual Wasteland, a warehouse he had abandoned because of Dust Brigade troops having moved into the area.

Valaik sighed and ran his fingers through his dark hair as he leaned back in his chair, another possible kidnapper comming to mind, Thelonis. Though why Thelonis would leave him alive if captured was beyond him but the possibility was there. Maybe Thelonis had tortured information out of him that he could use for whatever reason he had come here, maybe he just wanted Valaik to suffer since he had lived through his last attempt to kill him. Too many maybe's. All he knew was that he knew he had been stimmed and left in Athens after rambling on his Org's frequency about the Barracks in PW. Kidnapped twice in a single day and not even knowing who had done the second, it was more on his mind that he didn't need with everything else happening lately.

Valaik stood from his chair and moved to the door, letting it slide open at his approach, and moved through it hearing the door slide shut and lock behind him. The halls were quiet this early, most of the workers here still fast asleep on one of the lower levels. They had sealed off the lowest level of the facility after the attempt on his life by Thelonis and to keep any of the creatures that lived in those caverns from making their way into the facility. The level above that had been converted into better quarters for everyone that worked there since they were never allowed to know the way in or out, leaving them in the facility for long periods. Valaik tapped the Elevator controls and stepped into the lift, tapping the controls to take him to Storage Level One where he could look over the new merchandise that had arrived the previous night.

"A little work will help me get my mind straightened out." said Valaik to himself as the lift stopped at its destination.

Stepping out into the hallway Valaik saw that this section was still on the lowered lighting the base had during the quiet hours. Glancing both ways Valaik moved towards the section that contained the recently aquired goods, as he passed a cooridor he caught movement out of the corner of his eye. Turning to see what it was Valaik took a step back and drew his Modified Fusion Sprayer from its hip holster, pointing it at the where he knew a person had just been, a person named Thelonis. Valaik slowly made his way down the hall, his weapon held at the ready for any sort of trouble, as he approached the spot where he had seen Thelonis he found nothing. The area was a circular storage area with equipment hanging along the wall, no where for anyone to hide, and the area was empty except for himself. Shaking his head valaik rubbed his temple and holstered his gun.

"Damnit, I keep letting this stuff get to me and I'm gonna jump at every shadow that I see." he said with a sigh and moved back to the main cooridor, making his way to his destination.

Valaik made his way there without further incidents and quickly busied himself checking over all the recent arrivals. New items from Sol Banking Corp. and a few other Corporations lined the far wall, Various Food items were held in cryolock storage pods closer to the door and they all look to be intact, and finally a few items from Omni-Prime that he had hacked an order for the previous week in the far corner. Smiling Valaik tapped the last of the numbers into his wrist comp and made his way back into the hall, his hand moving to his weapon unconciously. Making his way quickly to the elevator Valaik climbed inside and tapped the control that would take him to the main hanger level.

The same headache that had plagued him from the time of his mysterious kidnapping was slowly creeping back, no matter what he did he couldn't rid himself of it for long. Rubbing his temples slowly Valaik stepped out of the lift as it stopped, making his way towards the hanger itself to find his Yalm and head home. As he came out of the tunnel into the hangar Valaik lifted his head and listened, knowing he heard something but not sure what, but he heard nothing and continued across the large circular bay to where his Yalm was parked between two huge cargo lifts.

"It's just business..." the words floated from out of thin air and Valaik spun around trying to find their source, but no one was anywhere that he could see.

"You're a dead man..." again the words came to him and he spun again, knowing they had come from behind him, and again seeing nothing.

Slowly Valaik drew is weapon and held it up next to his head, knowing something was seriously wrong.

"Hello Old Friend...." Valaik spun and fired his weapon, bolts of energy leaping from his gun to impact on a Heavy Transport on the other side of the hanger.

He knew the last voice and was positive who it had come from, Thelonis, and he looked for his old partner as he scanned the hanger, his hand slightly unsteady, trying to find the man who had tried so hard to kill him. Then he saw him, standing no more than twenty meters away, a smirk on his face and his arms behind his back. Valaik didn't waste a moment as he fired, the shoots passing through Thelonis. Valaik shook his head and blinked and his target was gone, again he looked around and saw nothing. Slowly he backed up and started to make his way to his Yalm, then spun as he heard movement behind him. Standing before him dressed in his grey Omni-Tek uniform was Bonefish, he too was smiling, standing there calm as Valaik leveled his weapon at him.

"What the hell is going on here Fishy ?" yelled Valaik to the figure who just began to laugh.

More laughter came from behind him and Valaik turned to face it, bringing his weapon around to take aim. There stood Lehx, the same soldering gun that had killed him in his hand, laughing maniacally. Again he heard laughter, this time from his right and he turned to see Thelonis, standing like the others, laughing deeply. Valaik's head began to hurt badly, sharp burning pain tearing through his mind as the figures continued to laugh at him, starting to slowly walk in a circle, faces toward him and their laughter never stopping. Valaik screamed and dropped to his knees, the pain in his head getting worse by the moment, then suddenly his gun rose and began spewing energy, shot after shot tore into and through the figures around him. His weapon beeped moments later, indicating its power cell was exhausted, and Valaik looked around. Again he was alone, the laughter silenced. A large figure moved into the hanger seeing Valaik on the ground and quickly ran to him. Flatbed slid to a stop as he saw Valaik there on the ground holding his head, his weapon aimed directly at Flatbed's chest. Slowly Valaik recognized him, his weapon falling from his grip and falling to the smooth rock floor, as Flatbed slowly approached. Valaik collapsed to the floor then, the pain in his head becomming too much for him to bare, and as he lost conciousness....he heard laughter.
