MP Quest-Spoiler
I realize a lot has changed since September 2002, and that people still use this post as a guide for the MP ring quest. So I figured I'd update it since it was written 10 months ago.

* I've updated the reward level. It's 130, not 180.
* I've updated Jack. He's much much much easier to kill now.
* I've gone ahead and put the Jack spawns in this post as well.

Mob: Elmer Ragg
Location: Mort 1731x942
Level Range: Reward is level restricted at 130. Mort is also full of cyborgs who will aggro you. It will involve killing Jack the Legchopper, which is easiest to kill with a full team but can be done in smaller groups. I managed it with an engi, 2 mas, myself back when he was uber. So I imagine you can probably do it with two people, and possibly with 1 now.

Target Elmer

Say Hi

He responds with a hi.

Say Who are you?

He responds with "I am Elmer Ragg"

Say What do you do?

He will either say:

I do important experiments...

or

I was doing some very important experiments before you interrupted me!

You say What experiments?

He tells you what experiments. *

You say How can I get the seed?

He tells you how.

You say Yes

You recieve the mission to get the Glowing Amagdaloid from a Jack the Legchopper clone. You have 17 days, 12 hours to complete this.

Jack Clone locations I know of, all in Varmint Woods:

2500x400
2200x1100
2200x1300
3800x900
4000x350
2800x2700

Not exact coordinates, but close enough.

Kill the mobs that are at those locations. You then have a random chance of Jack spawning. Best bet is to just go from camp to camp killing the placeholders. Respawn is very short, so it shouldn't be too time consuming.


THERE IS AN ALTERNATIVE METHOD AS WELL. However, I do not know if this is a bug or not. On the day this patch went live months ago, things were fairly buggy. I have never gone back to actually see how things are now, so if the following no longer works, let me know.

You say:

Hi
Why are you here?
How can I know the answer?
Yes.
