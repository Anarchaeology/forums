A few more ideas
Here are a few other things I would like to see.

  • Umbrellas - These would hover over a character, like a floating torch does. They could come in different colors and patterns. Ideally, they would automatically pop open when it started to rain and put themselves away when they were no longer needed.
  • Visible Implants - These would use the same nano clusters as regular implants and have the same affect on abilities, but they would also change the appearance of the character. They would be for players who wanted to give their characters that cyborg look.
  • Pet Emotes - There is a /pet social command, but it just causes the pet to say it can not perform the action. I would like to see this work so pets could perform more actions. Of course there will be some limitations. An anger manifestation probably would not be able to do anything more than /nod, but a robot should be able to do most things a human can.
