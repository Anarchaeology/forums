Sell Terminal
There have been suggestions on an in-game "sell terminal" of some sort, where players can sell their items and others would be able to buy them from there. Give us your feedback on how you would like us to implement such a feature.


I think each guild building (or clan org) should have there own sell terminal and the leader set the price for all items in the terminal (40%, 50%, 80% off cost or +10%, +50%) and have one price for members of the org and other for non-member. all money goes to the org bank.

By doing this the org can keep equip for there members and at the same time make money off the sell of the items so the org can make money to buy thing for the org.

The location of the sell terminal and guild office should be out in the wild unless you have the money to get an office in the city.

good idea
Two replies to this from me, one concerning regular sell terminals, the second a "item terminal" for guilds.

Sell terms, which actually would (i hope) work as a auction terminal, is a real good idea.
I upload my item(s) to the terminal, maybe a terminal which is zone-wide(not based upon omni or clan, city or area, but GLOBAL), and if i want a minimum price of what i want for the item, i make first bid (easy as that!). Then the person searching for this kinda item sees it, and enters the sum he would pay for item, and the bid then raises. Next person then sees the last bid, and he can enter his... And so on..
Of course must items be set with a certain period, or duration, of how long the auctions continues.

How the final sale is done, can be done in 2 ways, but only one seems usable i think (#2)
1)When auction is ended, the winner and seller is noticed of this, prefferably via game AND mail, and you can then set up a meeting to transfer the item
2) When you upload the item to the terminal, its locked there till end of auction. When auction ends, the winner must pick up the item, and money is deducted from him and transfered to the seller. If no bid or winner, the item is transfered back to seller, and he is noticed of this.

There are several solutions and also more addons to this, but i think its a good idea to keep this as simple as possible. To many features=more bugs, more work, more time spent to learn it,etc etc..

Guild terminals for items:
Store item, no more. No prices, no nothing. The guild is supposed to keep store of stuff to be handed downwards to players, and help out.
If a member wants money for the item, he should get the price he wants or needs, and this must in any ways be done manually.
There COULD be a second terminal, to keep sales items, for example if the guild buys stuff for their members, to let the members buy it from the guild terminal...
Oh well, this can be explored and discussed, and some probably have more ideas on this?

Regarding the visual look of it: That is secondary. Im sure FC will come up with some good idea how they will look
