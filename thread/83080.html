Build on the land control idea.
rather than just have a bunch of towers and turrets which are pretty easy to attack and are pretty dull, why not allow for a fortification to be built around the towers?

actual fortresses would be a lot more fun than standing and blasting a practically unprotected bunch of towers and having them all down in about 30mins to 1hr.

it doesnt even have to be a huge fortress just a few bunkers around only the owning guild can enter, and also some proper defense fortifcations would make for fun, for instance guard towers for agents to climb in and shoot or scout from, trences on the approach for people to dig in and fight each other, anti aircraft guns to obliterate scouts if they aproach by yalm (would make use of the currently useless cloaked ground vehicle)

of course itd be also nice to see appropriate assault vehicles like hover tanks (like the manta but bigger and packing turret fire power as well as energy lasers) and of course drop ships to bring in a large force.

the land control as it is is pretty bland and is basically the same thing that we do in camelot or 2h0 but fighting to blow up a few towers, that gets a bit tedious.

bringing an actual war-like style of pvp (as we were all led to belive with the intro movie we were all greeted with when we all first got this game) would bring a more fun aspect to pvp, and probably lead to more people particapating in land control.
