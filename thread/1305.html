The Whine-O-Meter
Before I start my ramble I have to say this, funcom people need to PLAY THE GAME AT ALL LEVELS FOR ALL CLASSES to understand what needs to be done for balance. That includes pvp and pvm. Start from scratch, dont use your super commands, learn the character. Say one class for each of the dev team? One for each of the balance team? Get a clue, you people dont know how the game works.

Now for my Topic:

I have read in a number of posts and heard from a number of players about ark's and GM's saying "It will not be changed because not enough people complain about it".

This I will now dub the Whine-O-Meter!

This is not the way to balance the game folks. Look at enforcers, I hear none complaining about how they suck or need this or that.. EVER, and I have seen not one change in the class in 90 levels. (besides the removal of q tapping which was needed)

*Disclaimer* PLEASE DO NOT TOUCH ENFORCERS, YOU BREAK EVERYTHING YOU TOUCH* */Disclaimer*

Lets start from the beginning please (not beta, though I was a beta player)

MA's had capped damage and YELLED ABOUT IT.. well they fixed that. Now that MA's are getting up there (first class to 200) they realize that the caps still exist only in high levels. MA's got the first boost and need to be "fixed" for high levels too.

Soldiers were CREAMING people and mobs. So everyone jumped up and said NO FAIR.. nerfed fa/burst

Agents were CREAMING players in pvp. Everyone screamed about it. nerf1.0, nerf1.1, nerf1.2 for them!

NT's said they were too weak.. bump beef bump beef. All they REALLY needed was their EXISTING nano's to be workig properly (I have an NT, I know) because the piddly hp and ac are worth nothing compaired to the NCU costs really.

Meta's were complaining of being unviable, now they are as powerful as enforcers + a doc + and underpowered NT up to level 40 (I have a 40 meta for testing purposes and they are three characters in one, all powerfull)

People SCREAMED about being killed by people that they could not tab (NT nerf 1.0) range is now capped

Now It seems that 90%ish of all the changes are based on player feedback. Dont get me wrong, player feedback should be listened to, but then take that to your "balance" meetings and say "hmm, ya they have issues, but they dont understand X"

X of course being any number of factors such as trade skills vs damage, or agro rules, or nano abilities vs damage.. whatever.

You cant have traders (for example, not singling anyone out) who are MERCHANTS and BUFFERS doing the damage of Melee classes. Why you may ask, because what advantage to the melee classes get? Everything about an enforcer (again just for example) nano line is about damage and HP.. not true of a class that can buff ANYTHING but abilitys (well almost) and mez and ... well i dont know trader nano's all that well... there is a balance issue.


Right now I am highly dissapointed that the squeekyest wheel gets the grease (and UBER grease to boot). And the Q200 nerfstick of DOOM gets pulled out every time that there are people complaining on the boards.

It seems to be the opposite of verant. you guys need to realize that YOU WILL NOT MAKE EVERYONE HAPPY. Stick to your guns a little, and TELL US when you are. Dont leave us in the dark. You cant appease everyone, but you can tell us what you are doing and why.

The Whine-O-Meter will be the death of you, because EVERYONE is figuring out that they have to complain to get "fixed" (dont even know what that word means anymore). Do you really want everyone to complain about everything?

PVP needs a LOT more work, and you guys need to figure it out, not listen to a SMALL sample of people that come to your boards.

PVM is totally messed right now with almost everyone capping on yellow mobs.

I am now an avid poster here on these boards, just because I know that if I dont, nothing I THINK will enter the game mechanics at ALL. I am completely dissatisfied with the way you are dealing with things and am BEGGING you to reconsider.


You do realize that only the "hard core" are here most of the time whining or complaining or begging for something. I dont blame them.. IT WORKS.. of course your going to get tons of flaming if it gets the DESIRED RESULT.

bah.. rambled enough now.
