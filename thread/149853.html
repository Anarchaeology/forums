New radio station
Dear patrons of the Cup,

for months on end now I have been a regular in the Cup, my favourite watering hole in the Clan lands. I have been thouroughly annoyed by the music they play there and had tried tuning my personal comm to other music channels, like GSP.

Though I normally like Rubi-Ka's finest Radiostation best, listening to it in the Cup made me a little edgy after a while. The beats tend to clash with the ambience of the locale so I sat down and thought how to change that.

Thats why I came up with an idea...

Starting Sunday, June 29th, 29477 a new radio channel will go on air.

Aptly named "The Cup", it will be playing house, internaional electronica and chilly stuff, caterng to the bars laid back and relaxing atmosphere many of us have come to enjoy, while sitting on the cushy sofa and talk to other patrons.

Suggestions are always welcome.

I hope you'll enjoy it...

Best regards,

Col. Simon Western
