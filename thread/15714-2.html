Darkbane:

While I believe you are correctly divining the minds of the developers in your crystal ball, to me the whole balance thing is reminiscent of the chicken and the egg.

"Which came first: the balance or the nerf."

Because - in every MMORPG to date we have suffered countless downward spirals where some good this is declared unbalancing thing and is nerfed only to expose the next thing ad nauseum.

AC has realized their mistake and has "un-nerfed" GSA back to pre-patch days. Why? Because it wasn't unbalancing at all - it was just a victim of the whiners who cried "balance."

To me, when the word "balance" starts coming out it makes me roll my eyes and want to puke, because the game is about to get fubar'd for no good reason - only someone's perceived weakness at some low level. Now that we have people even past mid levels how unbalancing would FFAs be? They wouldn't be at all, but low level non-pistol people cried "balance" and *poof* they are gone.

Sorry, but "balance" in a MMORPG is always an excuse for a nerf in my book.
