Looking for PvP advice..
Ok, I stopped playing AO about 7 months ago at level 145 (I'm an NT), when I stopped I was apprentice. Despite the fact that I was VERY squishy as a level 145 NT, I managed to relatively well with long range AoE tactics, hit and run kiting people with a fixer runbuff on, and other sorts of various non-head to head I-know-you-can-kill-me-in-one-alphastrike tactics. Hey, I even managed to kill Snarf solo once... ;P (Yeah, when EC was in its prime, haha). And no, I was already apprentice then and didn't get any title ups during that 2 day EC frenzy. I didn't use a weapon at all.

I came back to AO 6 months later, still a 145 NT, and saw the average level had gone way up.. so I decided to level up a bit before I dove into PvP again. I leveled up to 173, maxed various dodges and defense skills, got better armor, a gun and a scope, etc etc.

I went out to fight again with renewed vigour.. only to be stomped on viciously like that unwanted cockroach in your garage.
I was using similar tactics, but I guess people have either wisened up alot or are just alot tougher in general.


Heres a quick rundown of what a fight might look like for me. My stats are about like this :
200 carb, 200 Abl tank, rendering about 4500-5500 in most ACs.
~ 4700 HP, 5500 nano
~ 720 attack rating (with a 200 Missile Pistol), 1 init, 8% scope
~ 1200 nanoinit, 910 MC
~ 780 dodgerng, 750 classic, 500 duck (for now), 780 nanoresist
~ 850 runspeed

For refference, I go into all the below fights with :
RRFE, Cell Crashcart, GSF, Infuse MC, MoP

I see a level 200 MA in the distance, I pop a root on em, I get AS, Flingshot, normal shot by their bow (all crits). With rrfe and my fixer HoT, this brings me to about half life. I back off for a sec, firstaid, get some health back, move back in and unleash a flurry of missile pistol shots, a crown of frost, then a Grav Anom. I crit not once, do an insignificant ammount of damage, and I see them coming towards me with their big nasty fists. I run and get away :P

Here's another one (with all the above buffs again). I see a 190 something doc. Crown them, blast a flurry of missile pistol shots (no crits), Grav Anom again in hopes of squeezing in some crits, flurry some more, pop em with an Izzys, get hit by a big initdebuff, realize I have barely done any damage to them and I am now basically a sitting duck, and bolt off.

Also under these buffs, I fought a certain 200 fixer who was
healing very quickly with some double HoT action (faster than I could do with my nanos really, weapon was useless) and relentlessly chain rooting me nonstop for 20 or 30 seconds until he whittled my poor only-way-I-can-heal-is-this-fixer-HoT-I-nabbed arse down to dead.

Anyway, you get the idea. I see that what I thought I knew and thought worked well 7 months ago is useless now. To be honest, I have trouble even conceiving what an NT would have to do to be competetive against some of the people I've fought. I just hope someone is willing to fill my brain with knoweldge!! ;D

It took playing AO to finally realize that the only games that have respectable PvP caster classes are those that involve some twitch skill...probably the only gaming environment where the low hitpoints, big damage character can actually be played right.

Nano "tech" as an applied concept in this game is an absolute...and utter...and complete...failure.

All 3 of the main caster classes (NT, MP, Crat) in this game are a joke. I define "caster" class as those classes that must use their nano pool as a main source of damage.

The ability of classes to convert their nanopool into hitpoints (doc, MA, adv healing, soldier's TMS) far, far, very very very far outstrips the ability of other classes to convert their nanopool into damage.

The comparative ratios are already by themselves quite ridiculous. Then when you add the 50% dmg nerf and the fact that we must use other nano expensive programs in the course of a fight (roots, snares, debuffs), our ability to do damage with nano pool is so insignificant compared to other's classes ability to convert their nano to hitpoints...its hopeless.

You also suffer from item "creep" meaning that funcom keeps adding items to the game that adds to hitpoints which exaggerates the 50% damage nerf against your set amount of nano pool (virral eggs, decranum armor). And furthermore, any new items that add to nano pool (albrecht tank) exaggerate the healing classes superior conversion ratios. So, its a long term lose-lose situation.

Then you are also handicapped by stupid things like your friggin casting animations are actually slowing down the rate of fire of your weapon. It drives me crazy that when my crat executes Rule of One (the top end crat nuke), I have to sidestep to break the animation so I can fire with my gun normally.

Sometimes I think using the damn nuke is actually LOWERING my overall damage because I have 1016 self buff attack rating with my weapon. Combined with a scope and MA buff, I crit often enough so that if I pause my character for the 4 seconds it takes for the casting animation to complete I actually have the feeling that I'm losing out on damage.

So, do I have any advice for an NT? Yes, first you need Nullity mk II, and second, lose the raid-x pistols. If you can't find Nullity II then reroll another class because this game's balance is utterly stupid and without sense or coherence. The two best classes at pvp are Trader and Doctor the exact opposite of what funcom intended and leads you to believe via their official strategy guides. And the next best pvp class in the year 30,000 AD, with all the unlimited technology in the universe, is a class that uses their fist. This may or may not change with upcoming patches, but this is how things are now. Good luck.

Without flames, and without being your level, I'll try to help you some.


First and foremost, without the crits the way they were, drop the raid. Its a useless weapon -- especially for a NT. They are fast, and I'm sure WHEN it crits its nice, but don't bother, get a v3 in one hand and something else in the off if you HAVE to use pistols.


**always run at full defense -- max evades **


Against MA.

Crown of course, then root and back off, take a big nuke.

Root again, get crown off and start AE nuking (3-4 times)

Root again, crown, same deal. Always works for me if roots stick.



Against Docs.

If you have good nano resist (mines at 742 atm) then hope to counter their init debuff or open with a crown and hope they won't have enough for it.

after that a mix of crown and fast AE should take them down. If they are high enough to cast CH after crown, or if you didn't counter the init debuff you're dead. Docs are just too powerful.


Against Fixers.

I actually have a real easy time against fixers. I use the biggest (but faster) nukes I have like IEF and LDI and they usually go down pretty easy.


I don't know, we are pretty messed up. I have won against all professions and have lost to all as well. I think a NT makes a great team PvP'er, probalby why Funcom stated that we should be the "masters of area effect damage" instead of DD like we should be.

Hope some of this helps.
