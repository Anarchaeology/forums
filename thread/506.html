Complaints justified?
Ok, I have seen nerf complaints for nearly every single professions (except metas who were pretty bad until recently). What this tells me is that no-one is getting nerfed, the game is just getting harder and more balanced.

For example, NT range - a big problem before was that NT's could attack stationary monsters without fear of getting hit - even deep red plants double their level. Clearly this is a large balance problem, as NTs could level much faster than any other class, especially now that the range nanos work. Now one may say that killing stationary mobs that can't fight back is prefectly fair, but it is in the best interests of the game that no one profession have a technique to level absurdly quickly (which they could do).

So, there are two solutions - eliminate stationary monsters, or cap the range so that monsters can retaliate when attacked - thats it. They capped the range at the standard range other classes are capped, for the same performance reasons. Funcom is not "out to get" NTs, they just had to make an important balance change. I'm sure they don't nerf for the sake of nerfing.

Same thing for pillows - they were giving casters an advantage that FC though was unfair - this hurt NTs and metas. You must see through the anger of getting your personal power reduced and see how these changes affect the overall gameplay.

--Xente
