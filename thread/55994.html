Raise weapon ql cap
The sugestion:

Is to raise the QL limit on mission available weapons to QL250?.

The reason

To increase the damage potential of primary weapon classes vs those who can equip weapons as well as have nano skills.

For example. (A soldier)
MP/NT etc can squeeze in to a ql200 gun and do reasonable damage.

A soldier is way over skilled for same ql200 gun (the extra attack rating doesnt mean a huge amount), give us a gun that closer matches our skill levels. (Soldier probably had ql200 on at 120ish)

OE rules would stop the over equipping problems.

The numbers for example (not projections just my guesses)

Ql200 Novaflow IV
assault rifle >= 785
burst >= 654
ranged energy >= 524
fling shot >= 437
Damage 1-362 (500) - energy ac

Ql250 Novaflow IV
assault rifle >= 1050
burst >= 820
ranged energy >= 655
fling shot >= 547
Damage 1-462 (570) - energy ac

As a level 200 soldier i could use the theoretical ql250 gun above, chances are a non combat class would struggle to equip one without damaging/gimping other skills.

This would give a weapon specialst a little boost, without it being profession specific. ql250 beam for an enforcer . MAs fists continue to increase in damage with their skill.


Note: I read this idea in another thread somewhere and thought it made good sence

There is, however, one serious problem with including QL201+ weapons that is easy to over look, and that's one handed weapons with Multi wield requirements.

There are already too many ranged and melee weapons with ridiculous MR and MM reqs that QL190 is already something only a super twink character could manage (more like 170-180 for most).

QL201+ weapons would either need a static MR/MM req, or even a decreasing one (so that a QL220, would be easier to multi than a QL200) since only specialist in one handed weapons (advents, enforcers, soldiers, and some fixers) would be benefiting. So a big YES to QL201+ weapons, a big NO to exponentially increasing MR/MM reqs on QL201+ weapons.
