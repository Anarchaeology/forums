RP points every few patches?
I personally think it would go alot smoother for everyone if the game added an RP point every few patches. Otherwise the high level characters will eventually become obsolete and untenable.

Tweak for balance. Let's say that we want to give the points only if the character has been active (in some ratio or other). Perhaps a certain number of hours as a guideline, adjusted for level (high level characters would have to have more clocked hours to seem 'active' and thus stand as losers every game-balance change)

Currently the problem is that we can give an IP reset and everyone is happy for 3 months. But as the balance changes roll in the consistent players lose out as they have already spent their IPR's. Eventually you need to create a new character to maintain a competitive edge.


You can always play with the ratio. Maybe 1 more IP Reset point every 6 major game balance changes? I dunno. Something to think about anyway..

Any thoughts?

I personally don't go in for 'flavor of the month' either. It's not that it's so risky. It's more that I'm too lazy to spend 40 hours re-equipping myself every patch or two.

However, I would be inclined to agree that a game-balance change does not give anyone a right to cry for an IPR IF and only IF there was a disclaimer or something mentioning that things change all the time, and that part of the strategy is to spread your IPR's over several years worth of playing.
