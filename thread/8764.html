"Omni-Tek military will not operate within the Council allocated districts with exceptions:
Exception 1: Omni-Tek will be allowed standard secutity forces present at their notum mining facilities.

Omni-Tek is recognized by the Tir Accords under Annex 1A as the "Superior Governing Body of the entirety of Rubi-Ka."

That establishes the corporation as the ruling party, the government, of those areas not under Clan rule.

While it may be gauche to use a definition to support a statement and argue semantics, it seems to be in order.

A nation could best be understood as a "relatively large group of people organized under a single, usually independent government".

Therefore, those areas under Omni-Tek control could most definitely be considered part of a nation, whose ruling party happens to be a branch of a corporation.

Now...your concern over the bias of those in power affecting their call for a national emergency is a valid one...unfortunately, the problem is unavoidable. For a nation to be effective in defending its sovereignty, it must be able to act in a quick, decisive manner. This requires executive power to be embodied in a small circle of people.

Rubi-Ka is, and will be for a great stretch of time, in crisis. Mutants, Cyborgs, and rival corporations such as Sol Banking remain a source of conflict on this planet. It is by the grace of a non-aggression pact between Clan and Omni-Tek that we have been able to repel the relentless attacks. Let us not destroy the unity that gives us shelter from the storm.
