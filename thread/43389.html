Originally posted by Zardock
2- Any jackass that owns a company knows that the client is your god. Working in a tech support I have had to say thank you to people who called me an "*******" and I wouldn't see FC do that.
That's the kind of nonsense that they peddle at business school. "The customer is always right" is not literal dogma; it simply means that a customer buys what he wants (and can afford) and refrains from buying what he doesn't want and can't afford. Or: the best way to judge a customer's preferences are by his actions. But to say that a customer's pronouncemnts should always be swallowed as gospel is just daft. Bad business practice, too.

If anything, Funcom -- or any game maker -- should abandon or avoid any pretense of "incorporating the gaming community into the development process." It only frustrates the customers and adds endless backlog to the developers to-do lists. I'm more of the "build it and they will come" mentality.
