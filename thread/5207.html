Hello Cosmik, thanks for this opportunity. First some background. I'm back here to try out the free month. I played in beta4 and then from release until September. I had high hopes for the utility/scout nature of the Adventurer but I feel severely let down. My character is now level 68, I have little interest in PvP, I'm a PvE player and I use Pistols but I do follow the story to some degree. All my ideas come from purely from this perspective. I have no interest in what other classes have or don't have. If I overlap on other classes here, then bad luck. I'm only interested in improving the Adventurer this time. (BTW - I see you're an Aussie, well I am too, so you have option but to listen to me )

My issue What I see is that my fights are taking too long! Given this situation, we really need more offensive output. I can stand in a PvE fight with most monsters that are even to my level but it takes way too long to drop them compared to other classes. I have to heal myself way too much and I use up a whole bunch of nano kits and end up on fairly low health at the end.

Pistols There is a big issue here, the damage output is way too low. It's not just a little bit low, it's a huge problem. There are options like the Khemtech and Desert Reet. These have an adequate damage output with an unusual damage type. BUT - these pistols require a high level of Ranged Energy and Burst respectively which drains an unacceptable amount of IP. I can see now that Funcom altered the muti-wield requirement in an effort to fix some issues but it wasn't really acceptable and players saw it as a nerf. Would it be a possible solution to drop the multi-ranged skill completely and make multi-wielding a profession based restriction. e.g. Only Adventurers and <insert other classes you vision for multi-ranged> can use the offhand?? And/or add restrictions on the weapons themselves to have more control - "The grips on this weapon do not fit the left hand". However if you wish to keep the skill based thing happening, a simpler solution would be to make the multi-ranged skill an incredibly cheap one for the Adventurer.

I think in summary, I'd like to have plain pistols with Flingshot and good output (matching Kemtech and Reet), and I'd like Adventurers to have a low multi-ranged cost to equip these types of weapons.

Melee Weapons I'll one of the melee experts add their bit but it does seem that they need some work. Too many associated Blue skills seems a problem.

Heals I originally thought we would be the mini-doctor but it hasn't quite worked out that way. The lower end of our heals needs to be upped a fair bit. There's nothing worse than running a nano with a top value of 500-odd and it heals for only 200. The Team heals come way too late, they should be available at the same skill level as the single heal.

Buffs I'm just not sure about these, I've seen the multi-ranged buff proposed so that's good. Anything new is good! Give our Wilderness protection a slightly less nano cost and make it more effective. Could we have a group version of this? The damage shields are a bit low too, maybe have them absorb a bit more, or up their output a bit.

Morphs These need a work over because they're almost useless at my level. We seem to get them way after their associated skills are any use to us. I haven't bothered with morphs because of this. Too many IP's to put into a skill for so little value.

Adventuring Well, what can I say? I like to adventure but all this skill does is let's us wear a bag on our back, correct? Something went missing here in the flurry of problems I think. Time to revisit this one and add some things to the Adventuring skill. Maybe the ability to be completely invisible and the higher this skill is, the faster you can move. To balance it out, whenever you do ANYTHING apart from move, the invisibility drops.

So there are a few things I feel strongly about, I'll leave some room for the other great ideas I've been reading lately.

Finally, I'd just like to add that Funcom has fixed a lot of things since September and I congratulate you. The release of the game would be so much different if you did it right now Make me more competitive and useful in PvE and I'll click the re-subscribe button!

FYI, I'm a level 76 Pistol Adventurer...when I say pistol, the same thing applies to 1he - I don't mean to exclude anyone.

The way I envision the adventurer is a top of the line explore first and foremost, with decent combat and healing skills. I also see the adventurer as the loner type of character (Ranger from AD&D). I believe that most adventurers can agree with me here.


Explorer: Currently, the morphs seems to represent our explorer nature. These need some SERIOUS work. I would like to see some new morphs added, but first I'd like to start with the ones we have.

Leet Form: Never use it much except to get laughs at bars. I think that this form should increase our evades alot (and have some higher levels of this as well). It would be a good newbie fighting form. I see this as a defence combat form morph.

Reet Form: Make the 2nd level of this have the flight ability. Other than that, it is a nice nano. I would like to see more versions of this, so that there isnt such a huge gap between them.

Playful Cub: +7 damage and +100 runspeed. Increase our combat initiatives in this form by a good amount. I see the Hunter as being the fast combat form, and the Sabretooth as the power combat form. I think that these two should both be comparable, and you use whichever morph suits your style the best.

Grinning Hunter: Nice damage output, can't complain here. REMOVE THE RUNSPEED PENELTY. And also, give us more AC in this form, and buff up the Crit % as well. This should be a tanking/power combat form morph.

Pit Dragon thing: Never used it...can't comment.

Quick Recap:
All morphs should be useful during an Advs life so make more levels of them!!!
Leet: Defensive morph (concealment, evades)
Reet: Speed/Travel Morph (good runspeed)
Wolf: Fast Combat morph (damage, initiatives, and speed)
Tiger: Power Combat morph (damage, AC, and crits)

The ADV that focuses on Nanos/Healing would be in Leet form
The ADV that is middle ground would be in Wolf form
The ADV that focuses on fightings would be in Tiger Form.

Key thing...make all 3 morphs pretty much equal to each other in usefulness. Give us a good choice.

Combat: Well, as you know, pistols do not cut it. However, there is a danger to making them better, because other profession use them as well. In order to keep it fair, I think there needs to some self-only pistol enhancing buffs. We should have Pistol Mastery, and we should have multi-wield buffs. Since we are supposed to be the dual wield masters, make them very good (I'm thinking +50, +100, etc). The reqs are insanely high (they shouldn't be higher than the pistol skill...I know its meant to keep the other classes from using 2 guns, but geez, let us use two guns please!)

AC Buffs - continue the line further on...don't stop it, we like it!

Damage Shield - damage output is good if you buff up our morphs. I'd rather see the shield absorb damage every hit instead of having a limited number (of course, at a much smaller value). Unless the absorbtion is just supposed to be a little added treat.

Healing:
I like the extra healing...but I'd like to see them go off faster. When soloing, many times I heal myself but get whacked a few times and end up with a 0HP gain. Now...if the Wolf form idea is implemented, you could make it give us a nice boost to nano initiative which would solve the speed problem a little.

Also - give us more team heals. There is a big gap there, making them almost worthless. And fix the bug where casting a team heal prevents teammates from casting nanos at the same time.

Loner:

I think that Adventurers should have their morphs be a little less potent when doing (other) and (team) forms of them - assuming that the morphs get the big overhaul that they need. The (other) forms should still be useful, but not so much so as to make it too overpowering. The morphs stack with every other buff out there...

And finally:

Make morphs fit in doors! Morphs should be to an ADV what the pets are to MPs, the bots are to Engies, etc. I think that all of our problems can be solved by making morphs great. And to prevent abuse, the (other) forms can be reduced somewhat.

Few more things:

I noticed that ADVs are good at Mech Engineering, so I started trying that out. I like making rings, but it doesn't seem very profitable. I actually lost money today. A ring that was worth 255cr I decided to make into a new one. I paid 226cr for the template and furnace, went through all the motions, and ended up with a 299cr ring. I would have made more money leaving it alone.


Also, keep our runspeed nanoline going. Don't stop at Free Movement, continue it. It seems like, based on the AC and speed Nanos, that you forgot about us after a while. Started out with some good nanos, but they disappear when we get to higher level.

Things I'd like to see for the adventurer class:

I'm speaking as a pistol user and from that perspective.

1. Multiwield buff. I think this would solve a great deal of our problem as multiwield is a huge IP sink even with implants. Being able to wield 2 pistols of equivalent QL would make dealing damage to mobs and other players more equivalent to that of other classes.

2. Morph issues. Being able to use morphs indoors in a mission is a necessity-the damage buffs given by grinning hunter are essential, otherwise I couldn't complete a mission. However, it is an unwield solution as I have to cancel the morph, enter the room, recast it, and hope I get in the first shot against an orange enemy. Frankly, I think the speed penalty with grinning hunter is a fair tradeoff for the added AC and damage, and isn't an issue to me. I see grinning hunter as the "tank" morph and cub form as the "puller" morph. Cub form would be much more useful without the night vision, we all have the night vision goggles and don't need that. Low light scopes impair daytime vision bad enough as it is. That should be nixed as well. That feature of the game is an annoyance and should be killed.

3. Class defining nanos. Early on adventurers get a speed buff, and then we get free movement. But nothing after that with the exception of morphs. A higher grade speed buff or two would be useful, and I think reasonable.

Now, what do I like about the class. I like the morphs, they are useful and I like the freedom being an adventurer gives one. I think the freedom is the downfall because its hard to concentrate in any given area. Its a fun class to play in the lower levels but gets much more challenging as one rises, but I've seen high level advents so I know we competitive with other classes to at least some degree.

I submit the changes/improvements suggested below with one thing in mind...To fine tune the Adventurer in such a manner that they are afforded the same opportunity for success enjoyed by most other professions.

1. Either improve/broaden/fix/add capabilities to the Adventurer's line of Morphs, or eliminate them in their entirety.- The leet/reet/sabre/lizard/etc; forms that are unique to our profession are argueably useless. By the time that we have leveled enough to use flight, we can already afford a plane...and with much lesss IP investment to boot. There is no distinct offensive or defensive advantage as well (Unless you consider a rolling leet an advantage). Guys, give us some morphs we can USE or let us use them as a MP would use their pets. They are plain window dressing in their current state.

2. Give us some credible Run/AC/1H/Pistol buffs- Our current buffs are a sad group indeed. The only thing more insulting than our weak selection of buffs is the amount of IP's required to run them. Face facts...In order to survive, an Adventurer must invest nearly all IP in melee/ranged or healing related skills. With that being said, we are expected to invest tons of IP's into blue Matt Met etc. by level 40 simply to get a +75 AC buff? That is embarrassing! What good is it for a shield to absorb the 1st 60 Health points of a hit and 5-8 after that per hit, when We are taking blows of up to 100-200?

Also, since we lost pistol strengths, give us a legitimate buff as compensation.

I wield Riders...the ONLY legit weapon available to us at the moment. Problem is, I SELDOM see any in the 60-90 Qual. range! I spend HOURS looking for just ONE GOOD RIDER. Can you please add a better selection?

3. Since Concealment is worthless, I have a great suggestion: Add a new nano formula/morph/skill..whatEVER to the Adventurer class. I would call it "Camoflauge" because it fits our profession theme. "Camoflauge would enable us to completely blend into our surroundings, like a chameleon for example, and would wear off after a 2-5 minute period of time. It could only be used every so often in order to prevent abuse (kind of like Speed Stim units...when the effect wears off you can't use again for a period of time). This way we could avoid getting whacked in an emergency situation.

4. Part of our advantage was SUPPOSED to have included command over the elements. The related skills offered- Swimming,Running,Air, Adventuring(HA!) are all green to us...YEAY!..unfortunately they are all USELESS as WELL! I suggest that you either expand the scope of these green skills or get RID of them!...What good is Swimming skill? And Adventurer Skill?...hahaha & Climbing? Whats up with THAT?...Either make these skills worthwhile to use or DUMP them.- How about Command over WEATHER? :-) Let me strike a Medusa with lightning & see how she likes it...hehehe

5.Adventurers are encouraged to solo far from the comforts of home. What pray tell is our incentive to do so? To have the privilage of getting killed 10 times a day? - Give our profession a distinct edge over others when it comes to exploring. Expand our map skill capabilities by offering us map skills unique to our profession. Reduce IP req's for First Aid/Treatment so that we don't have to take a Doctor with us when we are hunting "solo"..ahem....


If we could get 25% of what I suggest It would help tremendously. Us Adventurers aren't greedy. We don't ask for much. All we want is to have fun just like everyone else...yet be able to compete & merely SURVIVE like everyone else

I won't even get INTO PVP...we aren't supposed to be able to PVP well...hmmm so I guess we shouldn't leave our apartment since we don't have the ability to defend our home territory during conflict. I can always be a volunteer nurse...hehehe


If you made it this far, thank you for taking the time to read my suggestions. Hope we all can work 2gether to improve our profession & not cry & whine about it! PEACE......
