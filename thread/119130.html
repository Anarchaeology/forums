Re: Hunting/outdoor Missions
quote:
Originally posted by Devlex

There should be mobs of all levels in all playfields, it would really give a sense of danger when hunting outside no matter what your level.

Outside hunting used to be so much fun, now everyone does missions. <snip>
Amen.

Thing is, the playfields are really just that - different fields for different characters to play. Not only do they nicely divide up the world, they delineate "If you are at _this_ level, then you can try hunting in _this_ playfield." Pros and cons, sure. But the farther you get from civilization the tougher the mobs, and that seems about right.

As for sense of danger... I agree 100%. And we even have something like that in the game already.

When my doctor was quite young, hunting was the "thing to do" to get xp. Since he was OT, that meant trips to Lush Fields. Ever been out to the Northwest mines? Nice red mobs that can be taken down by a decent team... but watch out for the Lifebleeders! They were mean, they aggro'd everything, and could take out an unprepared team. If one was spotted, it sent a chill through the team, and sometimes teams would combine efforts to clear one out before it pounced on someone. THAT I'd like to see more of - good hunting spots with a bit of danger involved. When he got a few more levels and "graduated" to the VBs and BHs of Pleasant Meadows, it was a let-down - it was much safer than the Northwest mines because there was nothing like the Lifebleeders.

Bump for the idea of more brontos. Not only is bronto armor nice, but the Brontos themselves are a nice animation that some folks have never seen. Put in a couple of high level ones with a few Thunder Lizards around that will aggro and eat the unwary.
