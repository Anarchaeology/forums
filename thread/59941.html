Sick Of It All
My very first post on the forums.....Of course, I have to whine. I tend to sit back, read these things, and not comment, but lately, I've grown very tired of a lot of things I see daily. This is no new news to any of us, but maybe I'll feel better after I post it.

PvP brings out the worst in everyone. Maybe we should leave it alone? Maybe I'm no better for posting my rant here in the forums. I'm just so sick and tired of not being able to fight a fair duel. PvP is my favorite aspect of this game. I can't take one of my friends to any PvP area to duel anymore. Someone's there, teamed with a level 75+ (should it be one of my sub-level 75 chars) just to gank me for the fun of it? In the arena, it's never been about Omni Vs. Clan, which I could try to understand if I was ganked by a member of the opposite side of the conflict. It's about some punk 12 year old with nothing more than time on his hands, ganking me and thinking he's better than me because he spells "Newb" with zero's. It's about immaturity and disregard for others trying to find something fun to do in this game. I think it's great that you have spent the time to earn the credits and equipment you have. Wonderful. Don't gank me and brag when I play once in a while and try to have fun. That's what I used to think the game was about anyway, but seems like that's been long forgotten. Your in-game status doesn't make you all that cool, contrary to popular belief. Your title and level does NOT mean I need to pay you any homage when I walk near you, whether you think so or not.

There's no challenge anymore. I see a few 1 on 1 duels, but too many times have I seen a fair fight turn into a senseless gank. If you asked someone to help you gank, whatever. But sometimes, you think that people may come to the arena for a little fun with friends? I dunno.. seems almost... possible. Leave it alone. Outside buffs.. fine. Gank me.. fine. Just know that when you fought me 1 on 1, you lost. I didn't gank, I didn't buff beg, I didn't team. Do I hold it over your head? No. Better luck next fight. Just dont b*tch to me post-fight about why you lost, and how you'll kick my ass after you get your <insert random "Uber" item>. I honestly don't care. I'm sorry you took it so hard, didn't mean to ruin your day. Get your <random "Uber" item> and I'll fight you again. Hey you won! CG dude! Good fight!

Does it end there? Nope. It's a tell from the classy winner. What's it say? ...... "HAHAHA YOU N00B! I'M L33T AND I TOLD YOU SO! KEEP YOUR PUNK A$$ OUT OF MY ARENA! D00D WHAT'S WITH THAT N00B WEAPON? IT'S NOT UBER LIKE MY QL <X> blah blah blah....."

There is a small community of PvPers willing to help train those who may not be familiar with aspects of PvP. Guys like Sehne and the rest of Yagyu, Shaded, and any of the MaryJane series (on RK-2, to name a few of my heroes) can make the experience fun and worthwhile. It's a battle of tactics for those very few, who can both win AND lose graciously (though I've not seen them lose often.) The type that will point out your weaknesses contructively and suggest a change, not bash you for having a low evades or a slow weapon.

The rest of the lot, which is just about everyone else, sure can dish it out, but just can't take a little of their own medicine. They get frustrated that they spent time on their character and can't hack a loss. Why resort to language and idle threats? No, you're not hurting my virgin ears, but there's no place for name calling and language. None whatsoever. You just continue to prove what ignorant fools you are. If you need to feel superior by ganking me when I just finished a fight, fine with me. I got the win I was after. If it makes you feel that much better.. glad I could help.

To all of you who have helped me ignore the griefers and learn to beat them back.. you guys were great.. glad to have met you. The help was much appreciated. =)

To all of you who have nothing better to do all day (you know who you are, because you do it DAILY) than spam the Newbie OOC chat with derrogatory remarks to fresh players, or hang at a PvP zone and Gank lowbies via teaming, only to laugh at your hollow victory, or just to prove your "Uberness" by bragging to me what item you beat me with.. Reality check.. It's only a game.
