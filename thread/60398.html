Really, I can't understand these posts or at least some of them. I can understand that either Atrox or NM isn't happy with the specific changes that are going to be implemented, since those are the two breeds being changed. Other than that, this stuff makes no sense to me at all.

Solitus has no drawbacks. None, zero, zippo. It has no advantages, either. That's why I have picked it for my characters (although I do admit that my next enforcer is going to be nano mage, after the next patch, because I want to see how that works out).

When I made my choice for my solitus characters, I did it with the no advantage/no disadvantage in mind. Every other breed has some advantage and some drawback. I was unwilling to make the tradeoff, so I elected to go the vanilla route.

As far as the specific changes to the two breeds, it seems to me that the Atrox one has got to have something to do with strength/stam/body dev and that the NM one has got to have something to do with int/psychic/nano pool. Those are the strengths of the respective breeds, and the change is being made to further improve them in the areas where they have existing strengths. It does make sense to me that some people aren't too happy with the way those strengths are being further improved, but it just doesn't make sense to me to call them imbalancing at all.

Furthermore, as I have stated elsewhere, I have seen nothing that indicates that this will be the last breed change. I would never, though, realistically anticipate a change for solitus because it is designed to be the middle of the road.
