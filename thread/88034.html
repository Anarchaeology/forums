Well, most guilds keep this information (at least for the other 18h+ uniques) private and amongst themselves so as to have as little competition as possible at each spawn. Less competition = more chance at winning the loot.

As for Tara though, that's a faction-wide thing. Clan on RK1 is looking for ways to better notify people when the next GS will spawn, but its rather difficult to do, and the motivation to implement something like this is rather low at the moment. There's a command to find out the last time Tara died in a specific PF, but that's for admins only, and the command/output is really bloated

It's also difficult to automate something like this on a website. The information would have to be manually entered, and its much wiser to manually enter it into your guilds' forums or whatever other means of communication you may have. You can automate the monitoring of server/zone uptimes but not whether a mob is spawned or when it dies.
