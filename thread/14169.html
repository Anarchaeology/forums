Are there plans to implement REAL vehicles?
With REAL vehicles I mean cars, dirt bikes, dune buggies, hovercrafts, gyrocopters and all that cool stuff seen on the scifi movies. NOT the current shortcut toilet seat thingies that fit into your pocket.

Yes, it might be realistic to think that the future automobile or flying car would be pocket-sized, noiseless, self-contained and unbreakable but it just lacks any CLASS.

You could have garages outside the towns where you could park your car much like stables in fantasy MMORPGs where you can leave your horses.

With the REAL cars you could race the dunes with engines roaring and sand flying. Now THAT would be CLASS. Also you might later implement a real vehicle combat system with drive-by-shooting, vehicles blowing up (and needing repair) and all that kind of things.

Or is the current system the final and we will have to put up with the noiseless toilet seats forever?
