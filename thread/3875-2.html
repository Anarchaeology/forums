Let me start this off by saying that I do concur that the suppressor is the best option for fixers that want to pvp before I start defining the parts that 'suck'

Suppressors are only viable at high levels (like title cap after 132) simply because the FA req is almost as high as the SMG req. Pair that with the fact that FA is a dark blue skill and stamina (which comprises a big part of your FA skill) is also dark blue (if opifex) or light blue (if solitus), and you have a HUGE ip investment just so that you can potentially hold your own in pvp.

Now on to the gun itself:

It does check against duck-exp...this used to be good...now it really doesn't matter that it checks against duck, because just about anyone over level 120 that pvp's has high duck (or doesn't make it to political zones much).

It fires at the same rate as the mausser 1s att/3s rech, so no bonus there...

It has lower damage output (10-218 for suppressor and 7-342 for maussers, both ql 200)

They both check against projectile AC so no vote either way there...

Basically the only redeeming qualities of the suppressor are the facts that they have a higher crit modifier (450 vs 250 on the mausser at ql 200), the fact that it has an 85 round clip, and full auto...

Now the 85 round clip isn't realy that big of an issue, because I don't think I've ever been in a fight with my fixer that has lasted more one clip of the mausser...it's not like the fixer is going to stand toe to toe with anyone (and live).

So while the suppressor as a whole doesn't 'suck' as it is the best possible weapon for a fixer in the smg class because it has that extra special for alpha strike....it certainly doesn't have that much going for it....

And before someone mentions Argulace ripping off a 10k full auto with a suppressor with UVC running, sit there and add up all the damage that arg did for the 10 minutes before and after that attack and give me the value it added up to....

then try it without a crit buff...
