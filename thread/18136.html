Killed by Omni Outside Newland Gate!??
So, I was running back to Newland on a couple days ago after a successful mission in the desert... 10K XP in the bank, and headed for the INS terminal. I switch to my social clothing, and I walk up to the front gate. A second before I zone into Newland City, the "death music" plays, and I get a message about loozing 10K + XP. I zone into the city, and promptly die.

WTF? I respawn at the reclaim in NL, and run back outside... very confused at this point as to why I've lost so much XP in a 75% zone without even a fight to warn me.

As I get out side the gate, and stand by the lake, I see a Green AoE poision nano shockwave headed twards me... Bang, DEAD. I respawn AGAIN, and run back outside.

And, there they are. A couple of Omni MP's, or NT's fighting the guards outside the gate... and hitting THEMSELVES with AoE weapons, and taking hits from the guards (the guys were lvl 156 or something, so the guards didn't do much). I looked around, and there were at least 6 player bodies laying on the ground at the gate... Most likely, noobs that were getting wasted by these guys.

I didn't get the names of the 2 Omni, but it really left a bad taste in my mouth. It *felt* like they were doing this on purpose although I have no proof, and no screens. I was to mad to think clearly.

Anyone else had this problem? Is it a regular occurance? Can anyone just cast AoE nano's at a guard and kill everyone else on the opposite side as them.... in a 75% zone?
