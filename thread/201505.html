Omni-RK E&E launches! (Rimor)
Omni-RK Events & Entertainment is a newly-founded daughter company of the worlds-famous Omni-Tek Events & Entertainment, galaxy-wide leader in events production and the main entertainment industry under the wings of Omni-Tek. Under the leadership of director Meda Shenkel (daughter of the CEO of the main branch of E&E) and guided by the vision of OT representative Rocco Flummer (a long-term artistic co-ordinator), Omni R-K E&E will bring the E&E parties, public lectures, sports events, fairs and much more also to Rubi-Ka!

We are now looking for both managers and staff for the company, as well as interested in contacts with future business partners.

For more information, please see our recruitment leaflet, send a message to director Meda "Grues" Shenkel, or follow this forum for information of our upcoming launch party.
