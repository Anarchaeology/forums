I'm not sure of all of these spots but you could have a look at :

* West end in the Longest Road (dead forest)... about level 100 I believe
* Striker Stalkers in Belial Forest (green to a 120)
* Andromeda, all over the zone
* Milky Way might have some good spots.. there's a mutant camp near the crashed spaceship but I'm not sure of the level.. 110ish ?
* Southern Fouls Hills, southeast end near those ruins... tons of mutants, some bileswarms, babyfaces around level 100
* Around the 2ho outpost (25%) lots of mutants I believe around 80
* Broken Shores outside Home... Breiflabbs, Shades, it has tons of monsters all over the zone

A little higher and you can visit
* Mort, snakes & spiders in the craters
* Perpetual Wastelands, Omi and Demons
* In and around the Enigma Forest
* Belial Forest, snakes and Fossegrims
* Southern Fouls Hills, Anvians, Wolves


Hope this gets you started, good luck
