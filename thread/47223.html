Just another night
The laughter quickly turned to groans as Zanke climbed up on the table, wearing nothing but a g-string and a pair of heavy boots. To a chorus of eewww, my eyes!, make it stop!, and NOOOOOOOs, he began to dance, grinding his hips to whatever bizarre music was going through his head. Two of the guys picked up their weapons, trying to fire on him, but with 100% suppression gas, it was a futile, if heroic, effort. She doubled over with laughter as her friends tried to find various ways to get Zanke off the table, and into adequate clothing. Ignoring them, Zanke continued to bounce around, his pale buttocks jiggling in the dimly lit restaurant. An attractive blonde, clad in the skimpiest of swimsuits, joined him on the table, and, after failing to shove him off, began to dance as well. The guys who had so vociferously protested Zanke now settled back in their seats to watch Layleigh move.

Perched comfortably in Edwins lap, Kiyoko turned and playfully covered his eyes with her hands. Dont look, she said, laughing, as she indicated the pretty blonde, whose swimsuit had strategically slipped, exhibiting a bit more than was perhaps, legal, in public, anyway. He laughed too, and she slid her hands downward to cup his face, as she kissed him softly. You know I only have eyes for you, he responded, returning the kiss, never doubt that. She looked into his eyes, smiling. You know I dont, sweetheart. A fresh burst of laughter rang out, and they turned their attention back to their friends, hysterical over some round of insults that had been hurled at Zanke.

This had been a fun evening. It had started out as a handful of friends, enjoying a quiet dinner, and mushroomed into a riotous gathering of people. She looked around at them all, laughing as Tuxx stuffed a piece of cake into Sezmras mouth, just as she was opening it to argue. She felt, rather than heard, Edwins yawns, and turned back to look at him. He did look tired. Shall we go home now? she whispered. He nodded. Its been a long day. They got to their feet, and made their goodbyes. Leaving Rompa, they flew back to Rome.

They walked in the door of their apartment, and he turned to her, taking her hands in his. She smiled, pretending to be a bit cool. Im not convinced, she said. He pulled her close and kissed her. She slid her arms around his neck, and kissed him back. Then she shook her head. Nope I still have my doubts. He laughed, and quickly swept her off her feet, carrying her into their bedroom.

Later, as they lay in each others arms, half-awake, she stirred and said, I didnt really doubt you, yknow. I was just teasing. He gave her that smile she loved to see, and said I knew that. They kissed again. She snuggled close, and they were soon fast asleep.

Edwin sighed to himself as the door to their apartment hissed shut, concealing her from his sight.

A group of Militant Clanners had taken control of a small, hidden watchpost in the Greater Omni Forest, and Omni-Tek seemed to feel he himself was ideally suited for the job of clearing it out.

Just his luck that at that time of night, none of the AF's Majors, Luietenants, Sargeants, or even Privates were available to pass the job onto. Not that Col. Edwin "Stormrazer" Greble would have assigned it to them anyway. It was his job to do, not theirs. He'd call them from their individual assignments if he needed backup, but he doubted he would.

He was an employee of Omni-Tek, and if Omni-Tek of Rubi-Ka didn't care about him, it's actions said otherwise convincingly enough. Omni-Pol had it's flaws, some employees were corrupt as all sin, and Philip Ross himself often seemed to severely lack knowledge of how to best manage public relations and internal morale, but the loyal employees never failed to work hard, the Omni-Pol guard divisions fearlessly fought, killed, and died to protect Omni employees daily, and the caretakers of the whompas and insurance technology worked tirelessly to keep things moving smoothly and with minimal to no loss of life.

"You clanners are going to pay for drawing me out here tonight... Oh yes, you're all going to pay..."

She smiled. She was dreaming, and it was a funny dream, although later she wouldn't be able to recall why. It did, however, feature Blitzwrx as the Lord of the Dance....

But then a discordant note crept in.... something out of kilter. It was that feeling you get, when the little hairs on the back of your neck rise, because you can feel eyes watching you. She looked around, but couldn't figure out where it was coming from.

The sense of urgency heightened, and she gradually woke up. And immediately knew she wasn't alone.

She saw the figure in the doorway, and smiled happily. "Edwin", she murmured, still more asleep than awake, "I didn't expect you to re..." Her voice trailed off. There was no one standing there.

Fully awake, she snapped on the lightswitch and was out of bed before the neon light flickered to full brightness. Creeping warily toward the door, she crouched, ready to spring. She eased herself through the open door, and quickly flattened herself against the living room wall. She drew in a deep breath, letting her eyes scan the room.

Nobody was there.

She laughed, a laugh containing both relief, and a sense of foolishness. Relaxing, she turned on the lights and padded over to the fridge. She poured herself a glass of juice, sipping it slowly, then turned to head back to bed. She had taken perhaps three steps when she stopped cold in her tracks. The glass slipped from her hand and shattered into a thousand tiny pieces at her feet, the juice splashing across the tiles, before settling into a sticky orange pool.

There, on the chrome-plated bedroom door, the condensation rapidly evaporating, was the imprint of a hand...

She was frozen, unable to move, as she stared at the imprint, which was starting to fade. With a start, she suddenly realized that the owner of that print could very well still be in the apartment.
