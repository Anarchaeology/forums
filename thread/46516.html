how to get into an org
gehn2k,
Here is what I did:
As you do your thing in the game, you should bump into some nice people and some jerks. Take note of the Orgs that these people belong to. (If you ID a person, it will say what Org they belong to, if any.) If you find a person you like, ask them how they feel about their org. If they love it, look up the org from the anarchy-online home page. See if it has the right number of people in it for you, see if they have a web page & if its ever updated. give the leader a /tell. If the leader is not on, they will get the message when they next login. If they dont get back to you, then you probably dont want to deal with them. Eventually you will find a group you like and they can recruit you.
And remember if you dont like an org, you can always
/org leave
