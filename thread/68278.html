How long will Multi Wield be ignored?
Lets restart the countdown.

CZ has posted today that he will attempt to have our Multi Wield concerns addressed. It is Monday, November 11, 2002.

here's where he said it: http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...371#post840371

It has taken over a year of complaints about the MW system, and still no changes. (i do not consider our buffs an acceptable change - if we still cannot get into a ql200 pistol on our offhand the system is flawed). multi ranged requirements have not been lowered, and the items that have been given to us still don't allow us to get to the ql200 requirements on the "good" pistols (BBIs, V3s, and XPs).

now, there is some hidden nerf, or some bug with MW. advents are reporting that dual wielding weapons that are maxed on with the initiatives so the attack/recharge is 1/1 are only getting 32-34 attacks per minute.

priorities on what needs to be looked at:

1. the new bug must be fixed immediately! fix it in an emergency patch and fix it now!

2. increase our buffs to allow us to buff into a higher level "offhand" pistol.

3. allow independant special attacks. i don't think allowing 1 extra fling (there are no high level pistol/fling/burst weapons worth mentioning - even the desert reet's burst is pretty pathetic) is going to create unbalance in favor of our profession.

how long will it take to make these fixes? my guess is that it won't be fixed ever.

this patch is the worst patch for advents in a long time. not only has one of our primary weapon skills been hacked apart...but now our damage shields are pretty much worthless in missions because the G-DAM PETS WON'T STOP ATTACKING US!

thank you funcom.
