The childrens souls are leaving, the hunt have begun
The darkness had placed its satinblack veil over Newland City, the once so busy city now asleep, with exception of the local tavern, filled with drunkards, hired thugs and cheap whores, selling their "groceries" to the highest bidder.

A couple of thugs on patrol, but other than that, it's all quiet, with exception of what you would espect to hear during nighttime, critters running around corners of the houses, drunk people singing, the outbursts of gunshots towards random people, during suspect of being a clanner.

But in the shadows, the patience is great, a man awaiting the right time to move, when to stand still, when to run. The thugs doesn't notice when he passes behind them, they busy lighting their sigarettes, trying desperately to poison themselves more than the nanobots inside of them can handle.

"It's cold, eh?" The atrox thug scratches himself on the leg, before he makes a cracking sound with his fingers.
"Indeed it is.. How the hell did we get nighshift today?" The small opifex lady in bionic armor shivers a little, rubbing her hands up and down her arms.
"Well.. I volenteered us both, why?"

The sound of the gun safety releasing, followed by a gunshot towards the atrox head breaks the conversation apart.
"DAMN IT! Look what you made me do! Wasting a perfectly fine bullet on your idiot brain!"
A kick is lunged out towards the corpse, before the nanobots dissolves it into dust, catched by the wind.

The lady turns quickly towards the shadows, her instincts telling her that something was just there, but no longer. A step is taken towards where she felt the presense, before the atrox returns around the corner.

"Damn, girl, that hurt, ya'know!"

The surprice makes the lady turn her head towards him for a slight moment, long enough. The feeling she had can no longer be felt when turning her head back, gone in the shadows.

"What?" The atrox bursts out, looking around a bit nervously.
"Something in the shadows.. I don't know what it was, but it's gone now.."


Young Marsha grinned widely at her mother, tucking her into her sheets.
"Good night, sweetie, remember, tomorrow is your big day, ok?" The mother smiled with a loving smile at the daughter, who's wiggling a bit under the sheets.
"Mommy, are you sure he will return tomorrow? Promise? Pleeeeeaseee?" Two teeth missing, two poneytails and two big eyes, little Marsha was the heartstone in her mothers heart.
"I promise, but you have to sleep now, or else you will miss it" The mother grins back, as she tickles the little girl. "You don't want to that, do you?"
Marsha squeels of laughter, kicking the sheet halfway off.
"Now, go to sleep."

As the mother leaves the room, she hits the light switch, her body halfway turned back towards the bed. "Sweet dreams, sweetie"
Then the door closes, everything fading into darkness.

"MOOOOOOOM!!" Marsha screamed, a bit scared. The mother opens the door quickly, peeking in through the gap "Yes, Marsha?"
"Can't the light be on?" Marsha looked over at her mother with big eyes.

"You're a big girl, Marsha, what will he say if he hears you are still afraid of the dark? Now go to sleep." The mothers words were a bit harsh, but still comforting enough to convince the little girl.
"Ooookey.." The little girl pouts, before she halfway jumps over to her side, pulling the sheets up tight around her neck.
The mother smiles warmly as she closes the door. "Oh, my, that girl is never going to grow up, is she?" She grins slightly and steps down the stairs to the livingroom and the kitchen, it was time to do the dishes after tonights supper.

In Marsha's room, the shadows now dwell. Covering everything like a fine sheet of the finest silk, soft, embrasing and cold to the touch.
He smiles as he steps in through the shadows outside, to the shadows inside, cautious of not making any sounds or to touch anything as he floats over to the bed, hovering next to little Marsha, still not asleep.

"Chiiiiild..." The voice dry and whispering "What you have been waiting for is here..."
Marsha blinks in the darkness, unable to see anything, a hand reaching out for the lightswitch, but there is none to be found, to dark, to scared, yet so calm.
"Come, my child, your fate lies with history, it is now time to meet your desteny.."

The next day, the mother bursts out of the house, screaming for help.
"Marsha! Marsha! Marsha is gone! Somebody help me! Somebody!"

Trust me, I'm a scientist
Having been an only child for 26 years, Dabblez pretty much assumed that was the way things would stay. It was therefore something of a shock for her to learn that, at the ripe age of 47, her mother had become pregnant again. And though she never would admit it, she was a little more than just annoyed that the little brat had chosen to come to the world the very same day she was awarded her PhD with first class honours from Rome University. Wasn't that supposed have been her day?

With all her attention focused in the subsequent months on starting of her new business with a group of college buddies, a robotics company called RUR, she never had much time or inclination to get to know her new brother. To her little Ethan remained a red, noisy, smelly, fleshy and utterly uninteresting intruder.

All this changed that morning her mother had called her and, sobbing hysterically, revealed how little Ethan had simply just disappeared without a trace.

Isis In Darkness
Sitting in one of the comfy chairs facing the large baywindow, a cup of hot tea left on the little table near her, Jen was absently staring at the dark sky, hands resting on each side of the flat keyboard of her Grid-deck. Communications were so lousy here in Jobe, when it came to the Grid, but she had managed to link her own laptop to the terminal situated in the hall of her apartment, and having her good old connexion back had really been a relief. She wasn't working at the moment, though - simply looking at the sky, at the night that had fallen more than one hour ago. She wasn't working... simply thinking.

The Fixer had spent the early evening with Lemmy, helping her with the homework, still angry at the conversation she had had the day before with her main teacher, at the way this damn woman had of talking to her, so contemptuous and cold from the start, in her attitude and the tone of voice - and Jen had to admit that herself tended to display quite a temper when she was treated this way. Then things had finally been sorted, and she had done the best she could to free a few hours from her timetable and stay with the child; now that Lemmy - Jennifer, she remembered just in time, knowing that given the situation, it was time to get used to publicly use this name for all administrative purposes - had eaten dinner, taken her bath and gone to bed, Jen thought she could work some more before trying to sleep as well.

Gaze and thoughts wandering though, she was thinking to Lemmy again, to the cheerful little girl who always managed to make her smile when everything else was just feeling so terribly wrong, to her quirky way of talking, to their growing relationship. Weird, how the child had taken so much place in her life during the past two months, how much love and affection the young woman had discovered in herself every time it came to dealing with 'Lemmy'... Jiselle... Jennifer... whatever name she'd like to wear. Weird, how much she could love a child who wasn't her own, when her own mother hadn't been able to love her. Another irony of fate, or rather a good thing, for a change. Jen sighed, without noticing it. She shouldn't have grown so fond of Lemmy, especially not with a job like hers, and she knew it; what was done, was done, however, and if anything or anyone had now stepped between them, she'd have been ready to crush it fiercely, without any regret.

Sighing again, she tilted her head, then frowned, briefly looking around her, a slight apprehension suddenly seizing her. No stars in the sky... no sound outside... only the soft breeze entering by the open window of the balcony, and the shadows creeping in the vast appartment, sliding on the walls, both the real ones, and the ones that were visible to her eyes only... Alone with the shadows, and with the soft whispers in her mind, alone with herself, alone to face her pale reflection, Jen was beginning to feel... Frightened? Tensed? In this state of alert she knew so well, after the years spent in the InternOps? There was something, something she couldn't understand well, a worried instinct that was quite new to her and that she couldn't recognize. Something...

SHADOWS!

The dull ache exploded in her chest without any warning, causing her to gasp for air, half stumbling to the ground, barely managing to let the Grid-deck slide along her knees to softly land on the thick carpet at her feet. A hand clutched at her throat, tears welling in her eyes under the violent assault, she tried to breathe, to force the air back into her lungs, and to control the fear, her whole world obliterated by this unique feeling. The darkness was growing, around, all around, growing, more and more, more and more, covering the living-room and the place where she was sitting... then further, further and further...

Moved by her instinct, she jumped from her chair a second before the scream echoed in the hall, her slim Fixer body reacting in a slight fraction of time, hitting the table in her move, spilling the tea all over - and not even paying attention, her whole being suddenly tensing toward the unique thought.

"MISS JENNYYYYY!"

And the door of the bedroom banged open, leaving room to the young woman, her eyes already nervously darting around her, trying to pierce through the shadows. For a second, a very brief second, she almost saw it - the thick darkness, looking so much like an actual shape, there, all on the walls, even blacker than the shadows she had been used to see for months now... She could almost grasp it, feel it, realize that... something... was there, looking at her, quietly. And then it all was over; with an eerie slowness, the light from the corridor seemed to wipe the dark away, entering almost lazily, as if nonchalantly assuming its lateness. Lemmy was sitting in the large bed, half-hiding her face behind the sheets, her frail Opifex body still trembling, finally managing to mutter another "Miss Jenny?..." One could have thought she just had a nightmare, but...

"...It's okay, hun. I'm here. It's okay..." As if in a dream herself, Jen walked to the bed, instinctively taking the little girl in her arms in a protective gesture, letting her dive in her embrace, still shaken. Shadows?... It wasn't the first time Lemmy was sleeping at home, and yet... never had the shadows been so threatening before...

"... Shh, hun. It's okay. Miss Jenny's here... Everything's gonna be allright..." the young woman whispered again, softly rocking back and forth with the child, comforting her the best she could, unable to prevent herself from looking at the walls again, at the corners in the room, at the crawling shadows that were looking so much like the usual ones, now. This just felt so different, and yet... yet, the feeling was already vanishing, like the few remains of a slight fog in the morning. Had it been nothing but a feeling, an echo of a nightmare Lemmy had had?

Jen closed her eyes for a little moment, going on hugging Lemmy, slightly biting her lip. Maybe it was time for her to accept the fact. Maybe she was becoming a danger, not only to herself, but to the child as well. Maybe Heiji was right. Maybe she should simply accept her shadows, before her control on them would completely slip through her fingers, before...

Maybe, also, she had simply fell asleep in her chair, and it had simply been a nightmare, like the ones she was having almost every night.

Yes... nothing but a nightmare... and everything would be allright, now.

A gaze upwards towards the clear sky, the moons slow travel across it, casting a dimm light over the streets of Borealis.
Cold winds, dark shadows, eyes red, this was the night. The hours of darkness and sin, delightful sin, neverending, embracing those who long for it gently, tempting their souls to more and more sin.

Two atroxes stumbles down the street, a bottle in each hand, their singing not exactly a serenade by mermaids. Omni-Pol soldier off duty, on the good way to get wasted. The hood covers his redglowing eyes, the lurking smile, ready to bite. A adventurer appears out of the jobe whompah, this new land, calling out for him. It was not time, not yet.

He steps back into the shadows as the adventurer passes, although she notices him, turning around, taking a few steps backwards as she winks at him, a nod returned, along with a bow and a gentle voice "Ma'am.."

A grin from the girl before she turns around and runs towards the newland whompah is followed by eyes, without the glow.

"Whe.. Where am I?" a surpriced look around, before the darkness overwhelms him for a slight moment, the balance lost, making him hit the wall with his back, sliding down to the ground. The hands up infront of his face, the gaze empty staring at them, like if they were not parts of his body, but to a monster.

"What.. Have.. I... Done.." The voice halfway cracking with crying, a tear running down his cheek. He staggers himself up to a sitting position, leaning back towards the wall, breathing heavilly, looking around, trying to regain his senses, dull from a time long not used, just those of the shadows.

"FEEEEER we be beerdrinking soldats, ye scumbag fencesitting cowards, CHEERS!" The two atroxes almost stumbles over his legs, heavy Omni-Pol Armor boots slamming into the ground next to him. "WHAT do we hea.. hea..HAVE here? Ye be toasting with us now, lad! Bwahahaha!"

Before he could summon his thoughts over what was going on, he was dragged to his feet by the enourmous atrox, drunk, not seeing the red flashing icon in his side sensors,"Warning, clan member. Unregistered ID. Warning."
His breath was agitated, would this be his death? Caught between two members of those hitting his side the hardest.

The one picking him up shoves a bottle towards his lips. "Drink, lad! Or I'll smack ya real' good, ya'ear?", accompanied by the laughter from the other one. They seemed to be brothers, or so they gave a solid impression of, both by the name tags showing bright on his own hud interface.
Scared of refusing, knowing these guys could beat him up really good, with very little effort, he drinks. Leet piss.. Yuk, never been his favorite.

When the bottle lowers and is offered back, a smack to the back of his head makes the darkness come back again, the hood covering his face even more. A slight red glow shines out with a small flash, before a deep laughter erupts from a soul not belonging in this body.

"You were chosen, you were chosen, now do my bidding!" The atroxes blinks and stops drinking for a moment, looking down at him, as regained strength flows through his limbs and veins, the hood falling off as he tilts the head back, a low rumble from the bottom of his throat, erupting into a scream as the shadows starts to manifest around them, creeping across the street like black snakes.

The sound of four bottles hitting the concrete street, along with the calm steps of boots moving away from the broken shards of what was once two atroxes, then the silence fills the street.


The next morning a scream can be heard from several houses in Borealis. Screams of lost children, the screams of silence from two dead atroxes, caught by the shadows, torn asunder by nothing.

"Master, your request is requested..."
