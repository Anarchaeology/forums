-=Combat Drugs=-
Combat Drugs for soldiers only

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Type I - Battlefield Frenzy

This is another creation from Hades Inc in an atempt to create the ultimate soldier. The nanobots creates the drug and injects it directly into the soldier bloodstream. The drug stimulates and increases the soldiers aggressivness and body metabolism by a huge amount. The downside with the drug is, that it puts huge strain on the body aswell as being highly addictive.

Duration 10 sec
ReUse 2 min (Locks first aid for 2 min)
Self only

HoT 100hp/3sec (300hp)
DmgMod +10
OffMod +25
RangeInit +100

After the duration of the drug, the drawbacks hit. (for 30 sec)

DoT 25hp/3sec (250hp)
RangeInit -50
Str/Sta/Agi -20 (may cause OE on armor and therefor lower AC)
RunSpeed -100

Nanoskills: Maybe BioMet/PsyMod.???
