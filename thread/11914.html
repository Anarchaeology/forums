You're right and you're wrong at the same time.

You're right now that dual pillows are gone and the conventional wisdom is go with shotguns (for extra damage) cause they are only single skill.

But, there are a boatload of different pistols out there (various Galahad types and others) that buff various combos of nanos skills (some almost as well as pillows) and these can still be dual wielded. And as far as the ip sinks that the various dark blue skills (bust, fling, dual) turn into, after level ~125 you have ip to spare and can probably even boost burst high enough to do some real damage (the equivalent of a decent one-shot nuke).

I've been accumulating the pistols and plan to go that route for precisely the reason you bring up - and for the ability to unload a burst along with my first big nuke ;o)
