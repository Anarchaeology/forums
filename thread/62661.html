If you're experiencing heavy lag, you can check if it's FunCom's servers or routers along the way that are to blame.

1. Open a DOS window.
2. Do a 'tracert www.anarchy-online.com'.
3. Watch the trace and the reported ping-rates.

If -only- r092.d1.funcom.com [216.74.158.92] has a huge spike, then it's FC's server-park. Usually, however, the problem lies in routers on the way.

Today, Telia's routers, beginning with ldn-bb2-pos0-1-0.telia.net [213.248.64.66] have caused a huge slowdown, my pings having been as high as 27k before I decided to log and wait for better times.

Don't blame FunCom out of hand if you find lag, do a little checking first.
