Can you actually walk to the sea? Why is it dangerous? Seamonsters, giant squid, sharks, eels, devils rays? That would be cool.

The most scared I have ever been in this game is when I first visited 2holes. I kept trying to run out the front gate and the guards would gun me down. I then learned to go the opposite way toward the water. Actually jumped into the water and swam for the ferry dock.

I could not seem to get up on the dock itself, so I swam for shore. About 20 feet from shore, I saw a huge mechanical shark prowling the shoreline. I swam for it(the shore), as I had no choice. Just like JAWS, I could here the music in my head. Not for long though, as I became a victim to the beast.

Something very powerful about the fear of sharks. I think it relates to their "dead" eyes. No fear, no mercy, no compassion. Plus the feeling of vulnerability we get when in the water. I really should sue Peter Benchley for writting JAWS, I havent been the same since
