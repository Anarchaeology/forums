I was wondering...
For starters, I thought long and hard whether I should use the lightbulb or the smiley face to characterize this post. That's important as, I'm about to propose the stupidest thing I've ever thought of...

First a little background. I went out to hunt at shades over the weekend. It was my first time there and I thought it was pretty cool. The mobs were definitely interesting and it kept a certain level of excitement which I was looking for. Now, as we approached further north, we came to this desert and someone said something about a sandworm. I immediately thought 'ha ha - yeah this does look a lot like dune.' But, no they were serious. Ther are apparently sandworms in the game. I thought for a while and went 'Oooooooooooh very cool. What level are they?' The guys looked at me kind of funny and estimated between level 130 and level 150, and I jokingly said, 'so we can take 'em, right?' (I am a level 60 char =)) No, we didn't try... they finally understood I was joking and relaxed, as they'd gotten very nervous when I suggested it.

So here's my question: How many level 60 characters would it take to successfully defeat a sandworm? I admit this is completely stupid, I can't begin to imagine who would even begin to think about doing something like this, but at the same time - I soooo want to see what the corpse of one of them looks like. I just gotta take a crack at one some time... Its a major irrational jonesing which I have developed in the past 2 days... I just gotta know... So, anybody? Anybody on RK-2 wanna take a crack at one. Sure, its a great way to find yourself back at a reclaim terminal, but wow! Wouldn't it be fun just to try. Death isn't permanent on Rubi-ka... sometimes you need to have fun with it.
