MAP improvements
After testing 14.4 last nite and trying out the new map, which is very nice and detailed, I do see the need for some additions to make it really useful:

1. Toggle for whompa routes
2. Toggle for markers for cities/outposts + other places of interest (differently coloured dots)
3. Toggle for zone and area names
4. Toggle for marking political zones/affiliation (slightly different hue on OT, Clan and Neutral zones)
5. The ability to put your own markings with names on, for instance monster spawns
6. These toggles need to be independent of each other

Anybody else have other suggestion, feel free to post them below.

I made the point to some new teammates about how the new map is not very informative. And this was my experience:

They said "the map is great."

I said "but you can't find anything. There are no city names on the map."

"You can use F9 to know where you are."

"But what if I am not there?"

"Then hit F9 when you're there."

/me sighs - I left that group immediately.

What kind of nonsense answers are these? I mean can you seriously make a map with NO MARKINGS ON THE MAP.

What is the point of a map. Its supposed to have information about the area illustrated. They focused so much on the mechanics of centering and zoom that someone forgot to add the damn city names!

I think they have forgotten what the basic point of a map is.

I cannot understand how they could call that an improved map. Jeez how long does it take to plot the Whompa and grid access points and political zones etc. Considering they are the developers and have the information right there I think it is just irresponsible. They are banking on the community to make maps.
