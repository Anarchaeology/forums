Cyborg Invasion on RK2
Tonight has been great fun on RK2. We finally got something to do! I took many screenshots of the events, so here goes.

Firstly I hear news over guild chat that clansmen were being called to West Athens. Most of our guild were out in mort at that time. The ones that wanted to go were taken directly into the grid. After a quick stop off to save at Tir I gridded in to West Athens. Many cyborgs surrounded the grid.

Upon first entering there were a few clansmen there, but not enough to take down the borgs.
Screen1

Many died trying to secure the city.
Screen2

Then a major wave of damage released on me and killed me instantly
Screen3

Upon my return to West Athens we recieved this message.
Screen4

As I approached the barracks a few cyborgs came into view. The report was true...
Screen5

A large group of cyborgs suddenly appeared from nowhere, taking everyone by surprise.
Screen6

With a few casualties this group were quickly eliminated.
Screen7

Thinking it may all be over I wandered around the perimeter where odd skirmishes with remaining cyborgs were taking place.
Screen8

As I approached the other part of the barracks more cyborgs were found.
Screen9

Without warning many more cyborgs began to appear and a huge firefight erupted around me.
Screen10

We were quickly overwhelmed as a huge army of cyborgs came over the hill. I quickly fell back to alert the other players.
Screen11

A fierce battle was fought, it seemed we were loosing...
Screen12

The army was briefly halted
Screen13

We had briefly lost the battle, the cyborgs invaded the barracks once more. Heavily damaged I retreated into an armor store...
Screen14

I came back outside only to find the barracks totally under cyborg control.
Screen15

Players eventually killed the army and secured the barracks once more.
Screen16

Many headed back to the other part of the barracks to check if it was secure. On the way there we encountered the person behind all of this.
Screen17

The general was quickly brought down by the mass of players.
Screen18

Finally...the obvious question...Loot?
Screen19

After that it was all over. The whole thing lasted around 2 hours. Omni and Clan fought side by side against a common enemy. Great team work between us fought off the cyborg threat. Special thx to Dairy for keeping me alive during those tense moments

Now if only we could have things like this happen more regularly, I think this was the first ever event on RK2 where anything interesting happened. A storyline without action is not what players want. Many had a great time tonight.
