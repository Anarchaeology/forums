Remember what we're fighting for.
I've seen clan and neutral persons state clan has "lost their way," "forgot what clan is fighting for," or even "neutrals are the true clans." I'm disturbed because the very persons making these claims, themselves, don't know what clan are fighting for, feel clans are fighting the wrong cause and even feel clans are going about each method of fighting the wrong way.

Clans are fighting for the ability to own, wear and speak what we wish; abolish all slavery; distribute the income from notum mining equally amongst the people of Rubi-Ka; elected governing body over the entire planet; free and impartial press; and free travel throughout the planet without persecution.

As to which cause is most important, that is for each clan person to decide. As clan, you may fight for all, or you may fight for one. Fight for that which you hold the strongest feelings for. Fight for that which you have the best ability to accomplish. But, as long as you fight for one of these causes you are clan.

As to how, that too is for each clan person to decide. If you feel you have a strong mind, then take your fight to the podium. If you feel you have strong body, then take your fight to the battle fields. But, as long as you fight for one of these causes you are clan.

I used to think, along time ago, that for clans to suceed we had to combine our efforts and focus on our goals with the one best method. This, I know, is not how we'll win this battle. We each have to put forth our best efforts in our strongest fields and push. As long as we keep our goals in mind and never give up, we will win.

Remember what we're fighting for. For we are clan.

First, neutrals are NOT the "True Clan" whoever says so is lying or ignorant. Neutrals are neutral. I don't see the word "clan" in neutral. I believe that the neutrals are natural allies with the clans, as they do not work for OmniTek and value many of the same values as we do (freedom and independence the biggest ones). However, they are NOT the "True Clan."

Second, yes, that is what we are supposed to be fighting for. That is what many of us still fight for. However, there are some clans that seem to be fighting only for their own advancement and glory. The Sentinels are a good example.

Third, we will need to unify if we are going to be sucessfull. There is a possibility we could become completely free from OmniTek and completely self governing while still being fractured and disorganised, but what will we do after we have our complete autonomy? We will have no infrastructure, no government, no military, nothing. We will need to unify for the good of RubiKa, not just the clans.

Fourth, yes, people should fight where they are strongest. Some fight with guns on the battlefields of RubiKa, some fight with words on the battlefields of politics. Guns on the battlefield would be nothing without politics and, in this day of conflict, politics seems to be nothing without guns on the battlefield. Until we have unity, until we have peace, until we can have understanding where disputes can be settled in a peaceful way instead of escalating into battles, we will have those guns on the battlefield.
