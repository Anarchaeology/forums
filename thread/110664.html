I can tell you how I do it and let you decide whether it's of any use.

I don't take blitz missions way over my level, just 3 steps above normal diff, ie did 104 at lvl 80, 117 at lvl 90, 130 at lvl 100. I do it this way because it makes it less dangerous and the armor I get is equipable by my own buffs; selfsufficiency. And so far that has been good enough for me.

I usually go for missions where you have to observe, not kill, an individial...by far the easiest. I set the mission sliders to Open, Head On and Money...that way you get 3 missions with Person Observation, 1 with Person Termination and 1 with Object Application (put this gizmo on the Radar Display missions) to choose from.

Next step is clearing a path straight through the mission dungeon to approximately opposite the entrance on the map, because 9 out of 10 times the object of the mission is close to either side to this point. I kill what I can, ie the lower level mobs in the mission, and the tougher ones, that I have to kill on my way, I pull to the entrance, hurt them as much as I can before having to zone out, heal up and go back in to finish it off.

I usually don't search actively for good mission spots, where I can sit outside in safety for healing when I have to, but you could do that, but this means you'll have to try and get lucky enough to get both the item and the mission site right...bad odds. Have tried more and more on higher levels to have the mission situated in a tough zone, where high level mobs roam around the entrance making it impossible to use the harm-mob-zone-out-and-heal-up-go-back-in-and-finish-the-job routine (unless you're fast enough to Yalm to a safe spot, get out, heal up, get back in Yalm and return before the mob has regenerated to full)...so I have to improvise at times.

Sometimes you might have to take routes around certain points with tough mobs on your way to the opposite spot to entrance in the mission and sometimes you just have to face the fact that you're gonna have to try actually running this....happens rarely for me though.

Now, all this takes a hell of a lot more time per mission, but as far as I recall I havn't died once on my last 3 blitz sessions (at lvls 80, 90 and 100). I simply just don't care to take my chances against roots and snares.

A bit of info on the mission objectives I prefer:

Person Observation - as said, by far the easiest. Find her/him, target her/him and after a few seconds - woohoo!

Object Application - almost as good, but you have to go into the room with the Radar Display (or whatever) in it, and mission objectives are often located in bigger rooms with 2 mobs in them. But the beauty part is that you can apply the object to the Radar Display even while under attack, so do it and run like hell.

Person Termination - since almost all persons targetted by your employer for termination are weakass martial artists, I've been able to kill them rather easily even though 30+ levels higher than I. If you can't do that in one sitting, use the pull-to-zone tactic if you can.

Item Observation - well, should be almost as easy as Person Observation were it not for the small size, but hey, if your eyes are those of a hawk, no sweat.

Item Retrieval - Never tried it, so not sure you can pick them up if under attack...that could indeed complicate things.

An small advantage to do it like this is that you'll end up with a small to decent chance (dependent on how big a percentage of the mob population you killed on your way) of being rewarded a mission token for your blitz mission; have tried that occasionally - a neat added bonus.

All my blitzing I have done with own resources only, I must add. But you might wanna go to places where the high levels hang out, NLC and such, for buffs to help you run through rather than fight through. But I must also add for reassurance, I have only ever backed down from a blitz mission once in my carrer, but that was one of my first times, and I probably could have done it now with the newbie anxiety gone.

Almost forgot this. It's a handy tip for solo pulling mobs out of a room with more than one in it...just if you don't already know this. To do so you have to try and let only one (usually the closest) spot you and make her/him/it run to you, and here's the catch: You have to not attack the mob while it is still in the room with its friend(s) and you must not have it hit you with an attack before it is out of the room and in the room you're pulling to. This way the others won't aggro. With mobs that have no ranged attack this is rather easy, but it's not impossible to do it with mobs having ranged attack; just be sure you can move inside the room you're pulling to before they charge up their weapon and fire it...usually takes them 2 secs, give or take half a sec, from spotting you to their first shot...if you're fast enough this is plenty of time. Sometimes you have to wait for mobs clinging together like lovers to roam apart to be able to single pull one of them. Just an added tip that helps clearing the road.

In addition, if your mission is Person Termination and that person is in a room with other mobs, you can attack him without having the others aggro (given that you don't aggro them with your presence, of course). The normal mobs in the room are not aggro linked to her/him, so you don't have to worry about difficult single pulling.

Guess that covers what I have to say about that - Good luck with it

I'm currently blitzing 230+ missions. No problems. Get a 750runbuff, get a behemoth essence and HoT or even a doc hp buff. As long as you have the target selected, you dont have to stand there literally watching him.

Put up tms when needed and unless they root you you'll be fine. Aces hit hard, but take BS missions and there so small you'll be in and out in less then a minute.

It's all about speed, precision, and proper usage of Tms.
