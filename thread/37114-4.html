I don't know if this is on anyone's list, but:

I'd like to know whether player actions are ever going to "again" be able to shape the world and the outcome of the story, etc..

Yes, Gaute released some general comments indicating that "the story" and such will be "turned up" with the expansion, but I do not believe we have a hard, straightforward and direct statement about what direction the _mechanics_ of the story are going to be taking. ie: _how_ is "The Story" going to proceed beyond this point?

Ragnar talks about the eventual addition of better NPC conversations and things related thereto, but again, there exists no hard and fast outright explanation of how the in-game story will proceed and whether the players will merely be spectators, as at a movie, or participants.

I think this is one of the things that AO "owes" the players at this point.

Also, something Funcom seems to have failed to notice: a lot of players generally _do not buy_ Expansion Packs. That's why they are "Expansion Packs", rather than, you know, the game itself.

Funcom _appears_ to be expecting every single player to purchase the Expansion Pack. I'll assume that Funcom needs this to happen to meet various business goals.

Funcom needs to address what is in store for players who choose not to purchase Shadowlands. You will note that "they" are already having their game changed in fundamental ways for an expansion they may never buy, and are getting "hurt" by changes to their "un-expanded" game because of things that will be available in an expansion that, again, they may never buy.

This looks like yet another area where FC is making assumptions based on what FC needs, rather than on what the actual human customers are likely to do. This has caused rather nasty problems for FC so far.

Official statements regarding these two issues would be professional and welcome.

Concerning the storyline and RK2.

I have a suggestion, but it might be untenable. Make RK1 the sole environment for the story, BUT FIRST, ensure that players can move characters between RK1 and RK2 for free.

The reason this might be untenable is the need for unique names. Before this could be done, the names from server to server would have to be syncronized, with the younger character told he needs to select a new name.

What I'm really suggesting is to have RK2 as an annex of RK1. If you want low lag, you move to RK2 for awhile. If you want to see the storyline in first person, or even participate, flip back over to RK1.

Although the map is ridiculously small, (I can run across a zone in about half an hour, I'd guess, minimally buffed, no vehicle,) I suspect that this small area is supposed to represent a much larger environment. Walking across town is something to do to check the prices. Where I live, you'd have to consider driving, (most don't consider walking further than their car...usually parked as close to the building as possible, despite the weather. Some people can be rather limited, they look like MOBS.)

Thus Tir, for an example, would probably be a couple miles from wall to wall, at least. In a real situation, it'd be quite possible for someone on the wrong end of town to not even really be aware something serious was happening outside the gates until the morning paper was delivered.

My point is, if both Tir's were one Tir, for storyline purposes, both Omni1's one Omni1, then people could choose the server of the day and whether or not to play "story" or "low-lag", based on the moment.

I'd let them pick the character, then the server, instead of picking the server and then choosing the character available there.
