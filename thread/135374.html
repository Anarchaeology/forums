What's up? (May 23rd 2003)
Another Friday update to be written, hopefully with some interesting content for everybody.

Shadowlands
I see people are asking about the release date. It's July 22nd 2003. It will most likely not be available for download right away, as we like having a good relationship with our retail partners. I don't think it'll be a long wait for a download version though, and at least shorter than you had to wait for a Notum Wars download. Do note that this is a quite big download.

Items from the database not being found
We get a lot of questions on whether a given item is available in-game or not, based on people searching the sites containing ripped client databases. This last week it has been a lot about the "Custom Made" rings. Previously it's been other things.

Answering all such questions is simply not possible, and considering that this is work created by people ripping our database (which really isn't a nice thing to do ), we simply do not answer such at all. Nor will we change our dev team's way of working with items (e.g prototypes, test items, items made without any set release date, etc) based on this ripping of the databases. We acknowledge them as great tools for the community, but the community should also be aware of the disclaimers to go with those tools.

Basically, any item you see in the database may or may not be available in game. The majority of items are in-game, of course. Others are for GM and/or ARK characters only (whether they have a GMlevel requirement or not), some are for press visitors, some are for the events team to use as event rewards, and some are meant for later add-ons like a new dungeon coming 3 updates down the road. And yet some are simply never ever gonna be available, for one reason or another - be it the designer made it just for trying out a new effect, or it was deemed to powerful and not adjusted due to lack of time. This all may change in the future, but we won't answer on "will it drop later" either. Wait and see.

Item requirements and exploiting
Just wanted to point out that yes, being able to equip an item when not meeting the requirements is of course an exploit. If you equip an item with "Perception >= 800" or "Side = Neutral" while not meeting these requirements, don't act surprised if you're suddenly missing the item, or can't log in one day. This is such an obvious exploit that I find it hard to believe people honestly thinks it's not cheating.

A good rule of thumb: If you think it might be an exploit (e.g. you feel like asking somebody's opinion on it), it most likely is one.

And remember that exploits is not a topic on this bulletin board. If you want to ask somebody about something to be sure to be on the safe side, exploits@anarchy-online.com is the address.

Neutrals in Shadowlands
I've heard numerous concerns and rumors about neutrals in Shadowlands. I just wanted to kill off a few rumors with some info on neutrals and Shadowlands. Note that this is how it's intended to work right now, and it might change before release.
- Neutrals can enter the Shadowlands (Shades and Keepers must start inside the Shadowlands, and are thus neutral by default).
- When starting down the road of Shadowknowledge you must choose a side. You can still choose neutral here, but doing so will limit how far to one side you can tilt your faction.
- The more you tilt to one side of the faction system, the faster you'll be able to gain shadowknowledge. Thus the path of balance (neutrality) is a longer one.
- Neutrals will not gain access to the strongest Shadowbreed powers, but the minor powers available to both sides.

Market forums and searching
I'll split off the market forums to their own forum group later today, to make it possible to do searches in the community forums without getting 70% trading threads. This has been requested a few times now. Making the "new posts" link on the main forum index ignore the market forums is a bit harder, and won't be changed now.

Crowd Limiting will be back
Due to servers choking and crashing, we need to turn on Crowd Limiting again, though with higher numbers than before (talking ~150 people or so). We'll see what we can do about the system itself in land control areas at a later time, but right now, getting rid of the crashing - which can affect far more people than those in the battle itself - is the top priority.

I'm not 100% sure when this will be turned back on, but it'll be announced in the launcher.

Professionals
The ranks of the Professionals have been filled, and they now consist of 24 people again - 2 from each profession. We'll have some tasks for them soon (concerning Shadowlands), and hope to put them to good use both for you and us in the future (/me cracks the whip on the poor souls). The feedback they give us through the program is already very valuable, and not only concerning profession specific matters.

---

That what I had on my list for this update. Have a nice weekend everybody.
