Give us better scopes or new shotguns!
It's fairly obvious that the llts'es were made with traders in mind (ws/ee reqs), and that most shotguns were designed for this fact.

Now that scopes just add 8% to our crit chance, the only viable shotguns are vektor and home defender (and maybe kick, if you're a brute)..

I'd like to see some new, fast, shotguns - ithica makes a good template - with decent average damage instead of silly crits.

Either that, or give us back some scopes. I can see how bringing in new, fast, weapons which are balanced for 8% VE's could make those with old-style scopes even more overpowered.
Consider letting us at those ql400 VE's. If 15% is too overpowering for other profs - just make anything over ql250 trader only. As it is now, traders and ma's depend on crits for decent damage, while it's just additional damage for other profs. Ma's have their crit buffs, while we have nothing..
