MA if he knows what he's doing.

Ever since I started pvping (around lvl175) I have never had problems with enforcers, even those lvl190+.

Basically, to prepare for an enf (I'm assuming self buffed, at lvl200 like you said), equip 2 melee bracers at all times. At title5 I had 32% reflect, at title6 I would have 40-41% with the 2 bracers I have. They, on the other hand, cannot reflect everything you throw at them since you can change damage type at will.

I start with a Junior Reet Tech bow for Aimed Shot+Fling Shot+1 normal bow hit. If they all crit thats more than 6k damage alone. (I'm using chem damage first, tho sometimes no fist buff, cuz that will cause projectile damage with that alpha, and NO one uses proj bracers against MA).

Then I unequip that and start pounding away starting with brawl....if I see myself gettin reflected I quickly switch to another damage type in between heals (and always using first aid kits).

Since a MA vs Enf fight is all about lasting the longest, don't use shen, use flower of life for a 2.5k heal in emergency... after you use that equip your claw or tool.

If they are glowing red, they are using their assault tank armor which gives them 2.5k hp or so absorb for a few seconds... wait until that's gone, then dimach them for the killing blow =).

You should have more than half your life remaining =). Hope that helps.

I always use ql125 first aid stims (highest ql in stores) to restore 670ish hp every 30 seconds or so.

In a duel with an enf, there's no way a MA will either A) run out of nano before the enf or B) run out of nano before the enf is dead.

A few reasons... when an enf is dueling, he will probably start off with mongo, chain cast layers to 'heal' himself, and maybe cast challenger for a hopefully quick finish.
Failing Impregnability: 384 nano
Mongo Crush: 676 nano
Mighty Challenger to Behemoth: 1165 nano

The only things a MA will be using that requires nano is Enlightened Aura of Healing (257 nano) and the occasional low ql fist buffs (100-200nanoish).

Considering many enfs are atrox as well, they will have nearly 1k less nano than the solitus/opifex MA. As you can see there's no way the MA will run out of nano first, also taking into account flower of life is a 2.5k heal requiring only 200 nano as well.

Most likely the enf will die before either side run out of nano anyway, due to the MA's alpha and crits. Like I said, I usually end with about half hp left, and almost always have more nano than hp remaining.

I forgot to add, use a deflection shield as well, start the match with it to raise your melee ac over 9k, then their alpha (brawl+sneak+fast attack) does very very little damage.

edit: forgot to add, I also use a 14% llts and a torturing tool, which is why I'm always at 100% aggro. However, with maxed evades I almost never get critted by self buffed enfs, even if they use a 8% vision enhancer.
