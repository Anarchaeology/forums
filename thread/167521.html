A big thread of suggestions for making story, roleplay, and events come alive
I'd really like to see a lot more RP placed back into AO being a MMORPG. From the ARK expo today a lot of different things and ideas came up.

This is all from a Rimor perspective.

First off, I'm told that a lot of stuff depends on feedback on the forums. Well Atlantean dominates the forums here and Rimor people don't even want to post. Because you don't see it doesn't mean roleplay is dead on Rimor though! Its going alive and well by many orgs.

There are many RP orgs and FC needs to take advantage and promote org based roleplay. People can't roleplay alone (or aren't psychopathic enough to) and events are always going to be too rare. Take advantage of the roleplay orgs! They'll volunteer for all kinds of stuff and it is possible to set up systems of doing this. An event team of 5 can't reach hardly anybody. An event team of 5 directing orgs in participating to create events can impact a lot of people.

Reporting. There used to be a lot of official FC reports on the website for Atlantean events back when they did that kind of thing. Roleplayers thrive on publicity or notoriety. Rimor has seriously lacked this. We need more reporting support for Rimor events, and not just ARK driven ones. There should be support for inter-org events.

If enough players who are org'd participate enough for general players to feel the impact then I wish you'd sit up and take notice to start following up on it. Remember the days when you changed the Tir moat because of a marathon swim? we need those days back where players feel they impact Rubi-Ka and can change it through events. We need more patch support for these stories. We haven't seen a real story based patch since the mercenaries invaded newland. We need more story background and buildup for patches. Why did OT command Juggernauts suddenly appear? Why is there now a plant camp in Belial? What prompted Jobe to place billboards all over Rubi-Ka? explain and create a story and a real environment please.

You're making a booster for "roleplay", but items and places are only tools for roleplay. We need a better story/event support and mechanic to support people to use them in the first place. Otherwise roleplay is nothing more than Counterstrike where each side dresses differently and has different weapons.

If you need to attract people to events (and I don't think you do, I just think you need more events for the people who want to go) then those rewards should be roleplay rewards not "uber loot" which attracts a bad crowd. Roleplayers want publicity and to be recognized, uber stats aren't as important. Roleplayers would love to be recognized as a leader by the clothes they were, they don't need Uber PvP stats for it.

If you tell me when and where an event is you'll get so many people you'll crash it. If you don't give us any clues nobody will find it (unless its in a big public place and they'll crash it again). If you just tell a couple people then its favoritism and most people are being left out. How about just giving us a general place and time when something is going to happen so that the people who want to participate really have to search it out? In example: sometime this next week there will be an event in Avalon. Big area, big time. But the people who really want to be included will be in Avalon somewhere and have a good chance eventually of being in one of the events. Kind of like camping for events instead of loot, but at least its a fair chance.

Make the orgs feel like they exist by giving them recognition and impact on Rubi-Ka (and I don't mean tower battles which is a never ending, not really changing situation).

Try to stretch out your impact in events without stretching out the ARKs.

Always have a story explanation. Even fixes and content patches should be worked both into events, reports, and stories.

Lets make AO a real virtual world and not just a holographic fighting place. Thanks.

Okie, well while sitting and (argueably) abusing the ARK today I heard several excellent suggestions, and I have a few of my own as well, not only to increase real participation, but nice little conviences for players and ARKs alike.

Ymlisa made the suggestion today at the ARK expo of having storyline charicters have names that are not green, but some other eye catching color (bright blue, red, orange, something). Not only would it be obvious at a glance if that glowing name in the distance is a petition or an event, it would also, in my opinion, assist new players. Several people today mentioned they thought they were all regular ARKs and have not approached because they didnt' want to interupt a petition, a way to instantly know the difference would be great help. (Although I'd sure miss calling them greenies. )

Second thing she suggested was a way to have an altered name tag for your charicter that you can apply for. Much like the /name function there would be a /roleplay function that you could turn on and have a little tag added to your title so that other players would know just by looking at you. (Because admittadly being new and trying to RP with just anyone can be a humbling and discouraging experience)

Now, something that has always bothered me personally was the inablity to give feedback on events in-game after an event takes place. It would be an excellent way for players to voice praise and frustrations, and vise versa. Not a way to get extra information on a story, just simple straight feedback. Be that via player representivies selected by ARK to relay messages to them (Like the way the Professional's program works), or just an ARK that is available post-event to talk to if need be. While it does take some of the 'charm' away from the IC environment, it would eliminate a lot of frustrations and problems on both ends that it might be worth while to at least look at.

Dynamic event lines! When trying to get people to come to an event, a lot of people will refuse because they don't feel like they make a difference if they are there or not. Long storylines can be a frustration if you feel like you're just playing off a script rather than being your charicter. I can see this might cause trouble in that Im fairly sure events are approved on a storyline, but even if there were several directional options that would be great. People participate more readily when they feel like they don't know what's coming because anything could happen. Even to some degree I think it would fire people up more.

I know it's been suggested/pleaded/begged to be brought back... but... yes, bring back the player submitted articles in the launcher. There are a LOT of players that dont like the forums.. heck, I'm one of them. It would help people have a sense of what is going on around them on their server.

Visible RP items would be great. Notum you could hold in your hand, datapads, comm units.. the possiblities are endless, and could really be nice to create a more visual experience of what's going on durring an event.

Advanced notice for starts of storylines. Now, not suggesting that every time there is an event everyone gets spammed, but some way to get the word out, as Sallust mentioned that "something" was going to happen in an area "sometime soon" would help those that really want to be a part but don't know how to get in to show up.

Someway of griefers of events being disaplined/removed whatever from events. We all know what they are, in great numbers they can litterally destroy an event setting. Even a temporary ARK run 'mute' button... something.. pretty please.

*thinks* I'm sure there's more that I've thought of from time to time, but this is what springs to mind now... short list huh? *coughs*

The biggest problem facing MMOs and RPG is the complete lack of Game Master <--> Player interaction.

True RPGs (tabletop and even some LARPs) allow the playerbase to maintain a completely OOC relationship with the Game Master, and well they should. This is the channel for feedback and improving the campaign for everyone (player and GM).

Things that would help make RP a positive experience in AO:

1) Set up player liasons on the Events team. These players should be among the best role-players, be able to 100% separate player and character knowledge, and ideally have some game master experience. People relation and discussion group contribution skills are also a must.

2) Find out who all the hard core and soft RPers are on each server and which characters they play. Then maintain a database of characters and players. This is something to be delegated to your Player Event volunteers. Each should have several coordinators under them with each coordinator responsible for tracking a number of players (10-20 max ideally) and thier characters. They can coordinate player disposition and make recommendations for which characters may be ideal to try to get involved in certain events.

3) Events should be player and FC-sponsored. The goal should be to incorporate enough events to allow each player to participate in an official or semi-official event every 2 or 3 months, sometimes more depending on event size, frequency, and number of players.

4) Creation of OOC channels for Role players to get know each other (ARK event coordinators too) outside of the game. It is much easier to role play with people you know.

Also I think it's imperative that RP-guilds and free lance RPers organize themselves, get to know who the players are and where the characters fit in.

There's tons more stuff that can contribute to more engaging and enjoyable role playing in AO (and any MMO, really), and as a community I believe role players can uncover them.

I should be asleep and resting for my trip tomorrow... but I wanted to get this idea down while it was still thought out in my brain. It probably won't sound thought out as tired as I am though.

I remember about a year ago Cz getting on and asking all the leaders what factions we wanted our orgs to associate with. Don't know what this was supposed to be for, but apparently nothing ever became of it unfortunately.

It would be nice if this could be brought back in a more official capacity. So here is a two-fold idea for it.

Org faction alignment.

At first it could simply be chosen which group in AO your org aligns with. In example Sentinels, Redeemed, Terra Firma, Eco Warriors, ICC, Ergo, Mercenaries, Independent, Omni-Med, The Followers, Omni HQ, Omni Prime, etc. There are tons of them for a player's true alignment.

For those RP orgs wishing to participate as actors in events the events team can then use this "alignment" to systematically determine without bias which roles they can play in events as needed.

The second part of this would be to eventually institute a mechanic for it (maybe its already worked on?) with the org system. Orgs should have an option to select a faction alignment. This faction always has to be positive with the members standing for them to be in the Org. It would also give all members of that org a bonus in that faction standing maybe, or allow them to wear special equipment of that faction. It would give the factions of each side a lot more character and be a helpful mechanic to support RP orgs.
