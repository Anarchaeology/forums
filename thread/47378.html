ST: Alpha seeking members. Omni Only
Greetings fellow Omni-Tek citizens. I'm Alex Zander also know as Wyldhawk. I'm currently forming a small group of people to help serve in the betterment and protection of Rubi-Ka and Omni-Tek.

Strike Team Alpha, is a loose organization that is more of an extension of Omni-AF and Omni-Pol. While I am simply a young agent, I am looking in for like minded rookies that want to work with others to achive excellence not only in characters but in roleplaying as well.

The Strike Team will be small hopefully ten to fifteen players(No alts). Nothing is required to join at this time other than a Omni character from levels five to fifteen. If the team grows beyond the given number. They will become Strike Team Beta.

Professionalism, honor, and a good sense of roleplaying is desired. Perks and payroll will be figured out once the team is established. As it stands the team unit is going to be the greatest perk. As we develope the ST concept will grow.

Any questions? Look for me in the Rome Blue district, Highrise 7, or page me.

We protect, so you can prosper.
