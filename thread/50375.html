We need to do something about Tir Grid
See if this scenario seems familiar:

You're a lvl 160ish MP (or any other profession with buffs or trade skills in demand), you come to Tir Grid, see some friends, say hello, and sit down to talk to them. You've been playing for hours, and need to relax a bit, and just small talk with people. But every 30 seconds you are interrupted by someone who needs a nano buff.

For every nano buff request you do or implant work you complete, you get two or three new ones. Some people take exception to being told that they have to wait until you finish the current job, and start sending aggro tells. Business as usual, in other words.

Well, sunday night (GMT), I snapped, and basically told everyone present at Tir Grid to go FOAD.

I know that this means that some engies didn't get the buffs that would make them able to keep their team mates safe(r) with their slayer droids and so on, and I offer my apology to those people for that. I know that people spend time, hours, occasionally, camping the place to get buffs they really need. I am also aware that my outburst makes me seem like a bigot, since all my toons also sometimes need that +80 treatment or a Rifle Mastery and so forth. But sometime steam needs to be vented, and afterwards i got lots of sympathetic /tells. It seems I'm not the only one who views the current situation at Tir Grid as problematic.

So, what can I do?

I could of course max my concealment and stay hidden at Tir Grid at all times, or i could start charging outrageous fees for my services to stem the flood - it is after all business, and i've spent untold hours and died untold times to get my trade skills up to a point where i can make any implant and process any instruction disk, like a lot of us have. Or i could stay out of Tir Grid altogether.

But i would rather try this first:

Would it be possible for us to divide Tir Grid in some way? Let's say that we keep the main area as a recreation area, and that people there should be spared the interruptions, while we make a market place out of the Happy Rebel (We aren't using it for anything anyway ;-?), and thus everyone in there will know that the people you see in there are in there to do business, and therefore are available. The area division could of course be done any way the majority sees fit.

It will of course not change in a day, but habits can be broken. I think it's worth a try. Otherwise I'm setting up camp in Enigma Forest
