How stupid can these quests get?
My complaint is about many (all) of the so called quests. You call spending days gathering parts, or sitting and waiting 2-18 hours for a spawn a guest?

How about the 5 other people in the team who need to finish a quest waiting those 10-36 hours longer?

I have been camping the Trash King over 4 (game) days, only to have some higher level group show up at the moment of spawn kill him. loot and fly off. How the hell is this supposed to be challenging or fun?

Wake up Fun Com! Having players run around, or sit around for days on end is NOT entertainment! Fix the damn spawn rates, loot drops and establish some order to the game!

New games are coming and your time is short, unless you make things intertaining. Fix know bugs, faults and shortfalls before you lose all if not all of your business.

OK. I am done venting....anyone else want to join in?
