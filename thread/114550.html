>> New Shadowland professions unveiled <<
Let's pretend we can talk about that in the Test part of this forum...

For those who don't know yet :

The Shade
The Shade is a profession infused with the shadowy presence of the Shadowlands. He will move with stealth and will be infused with the legends, the powers of the old civilization. He or she will not be able to use implants at all (Implants are an integral part of Anarchy Online, boosting your character). Rather, he will capture and find spirits that he will "persuade" into boosting him in an esthetical-technical process. Where technology harnesses souls, power is born.

He will drain his opponents by touching them; slowly but thoroughly, he will make them kneel. The last thing his enemies will see is his hands reaching into them. The Shade will specialize in close combat, using fists and piercing weapons. He will not be an easy character to play in solo compared to some of the other professions.

The Keeper
This is the other profession. He is also shaped by the matters in the Shadowlands - but as the Shade is dark, so is the Keeper light! An aura of goodness shines around the Keeper, and he lends freely the powers of his auras to his team members. The Keeper will also be a close combat specialist, focusing on two-handed weapons and heroics.

He will well play the role of the tank, albeit not as well as the Enforcer.
