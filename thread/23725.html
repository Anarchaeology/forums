Removing Nanos from "Memory"..
I'm sure this has been suggested before, but I'd just like to reitterate the need for this functionality. It would be SO incredibly nice to remove/delete old nanos from your "memory." Part of my reasoning is in the clutter that the nano selection window can acquire. I am an agent, and I have a ton of nanos. I've come to the conclusion that some are worthless to me, while others are more worthwhile. I have to dig somtimes to find the ones I need. Secondly, there's no need for spells once you're moved past them. I have rifle expertise and proficientcy. There is no need for proficientcy, but it lingers around in my nano list. I've accidentally used the wrong nanos on several occasions.

I guess I'm partially just a neat freak, and it'd be nice to "take out the trash."
