Size of Team Missions
Any chance of toning down the size of team missions? I have done planar raids in EQ that took less time than some of these. The long raids were the primary reason that prompted me to leave that game.

Maybe at least tweak one of the sliders to control the size and make it my choice down to a point?

I am only level eighty and one hundred and fifty mob missions(which seem to be the minimum we ever find) are a bit more of a commitment to play in one stretch than I really desire. Missions that size seem to pretty much burn out everyone on the team as well, so I am guessing I am not alone in feeling this way. Fifty mob missions are a good size and doable by a group in a reasonable amount of time. Hundred mob missions are great if you have some time and are more focused on the experience than anything else. One hundred and fifty to two hundred mob missions are quite tedious and everyone is pretty much burnt out by the end and does not feel like doing much more of anything, most tend to log off afterwards.

I can still grab twenty mob single missions with no problem all the time, why do my team missions have to be five to ten times larger? If it is because of the rare loot, well since I have done tons of team missions and have yet to see a boss mob drop anything worthwhile, let alone rare, I think it is rare enough to not worry about.

Anyhow, my friends and I are about to go back to doing single missions as a team because it is just getting too tedious to do the team missions. So tedious in fact, that we do not mind giving up better loot, the cash reward and the tokens just to avoid them.
