12.5 Live on Test...what I've noticed so far
I jumped on with my lvl 2 MA, just to see what changed. This will be very disorderly:

- Mission monsters no longer melee as well as shoot (melee mobs only use their melee weapons...no extra attacks)

- Mission terminals in Rome Blue have moved. They are no longer near the entrances, but farther back. There are also new terminals in the middle of the zone.

-New Icons for many things: Monster Parts, Hacker Tools, Weapon Parts, Bows

-You can buy the Basic Token Board at the Tools machine

-Weapon Skills and Trade Skills vending machins both sell trade skill items now

-Armor vendors fixed. Less armor available than before bug, but more randomness (i.e. low QL MA, Raven, and Forest armor available in basic stores)

-New Item: PILL OF INSURANCE Will insure your exp from death. Does not insure items. Only insures a percentage of your experience when eaten.

-New Bags: Small, Medium and Large. All have a Adventuring skill associated with them, so ALL can be worn. Small: Adv 21, Meduim: Adv 100, Large: Adv 21 (this seemed bugged as the description of the large bag said it was small) Oh, new Bag Icons as well.

That's all I can think of. Looks like a good patch so far.

More changes
Monster now drop all kinds of new stuff like Ember Sphere, Glow Art, Soul Sphere, etc. Don't seem to have a real purpose except for selling.

Armor:
BIG CHANGES!!!

All armor now has at least some protection for all the different damage types!!! Now everyone doesn't have to wear Bau/Waitt to get decent protections. Organic and Graft armor is now available above QL1, it requires STR/STA (yea for enforcers!) but does a bit of damage when you equip it or un-equip it, at least that is what the description says.

Clan Superior shops were selling tank armor.

Omni-Pol armor types (desert, forest) are now Omni ONLY (thanks to Mangofett).

Trade Skills:
Gobs of new parts, graphics and manuals including instructions and parts for building melee weapons (again yea for enforcers!)
Saw a manual on how to build the OT Basic Weapon Skills thingie, still no idea on how to use it or those field primers.

Nanos:
Advanced and Superior shops were selling all the nanos (didn't check all professions) up to QL 120ish.

1HB Expertise is now being sold (was only selling Proficiency before).
