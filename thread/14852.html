RK2- Division 3: Omni Reclamation seeking General of Trade Services--RK2
Division 3: Omni Reclamation, a member of Project Revenant on RK2, is actively seeking candidates for its open position of General of Trade Services.

Preferably, we would like a trader level 40+ to fill this position, but will interview any traders out there who can role-play and devote enough time to develop trade services.
Responsibilities will include management of all Division 3 inventory (assignes to a LT or Major if you wish), and collateral duties of Public Relations (again assigned to a LT or Major if you prefer) and Internal Division Relations (dealing with the investigations of reports on wrong doings by Division 3 members).

To get an idea of what Division 3 and Project Revenant are all about, go to www.projectrevenant.com.

You can view info on Division 3, as well as access some of our forum boards.

If interested, send an email to mbrock3200@aol.com or send a /tell in game to nortsrum.

Thanks for your time.



*Note: We are in no way affiliated with Division 9 [RSGE] on RK1.
