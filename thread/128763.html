First entry, personal journal of Hisa Kurusawa
Has only really a week passed since the corporation sent me out on the streets of Omni-1 to get familiar with the habits and likes of our employees on this notum-infested dirtball called Rubi-Ka ?

The management here surely has losened it's grip on many of the inhabitants. Today I saw with my own eyes how OmniTech employees sat down in a bar together with some filthy scumbags who with no doubt were clanners, to freely exchange opinions and even ... food dislikes. If I wasn't in the need of allies to fulfill my assignment, I would already have reported them to the authorities, to get them drawn before court.

What has gone wrong here ? Were the leaflets about the secured life on Rubi-Ka nothing but lies ? Is loyalty nothing more than a bad joke between the drunkards who sit under the bridges of Rome at night ? Even some elder employees, when asked, could not remember the name of our CEO.

I will have to do some research on how a situation like this could have happened without the knowledge of OmniTech headquarters. Therefore, I'm in need of a broad connection network, equipment, and of course, fellow loyals. I can't be the only one in this city who noticed something is very wrong ...


Personal note: Do not forget to find out why the slang in Omni-1 is so different from home.
