Announcement: 24-7 is now recruiting omni of all proffessions on Rubi-Ka 1
Greetings, loyal employees of Omni-Tek.

Today, we (24-7) can offer you a great opurtunity.
We are currently recruiting, and would like to see members of all proffessions amongst our ranks.

24-7 is an independent trade organisation, settled on over a thousand different planets.
Many of them are populated by Omni-Tek, and we have had many successful cooperations with this firm.
After running things on Rubi-Ka and gaining experience on how operations could be conducted,
we are re-opening up for recruitment. All it takes to join, is loyalty towards Omni-Tek and a wish to live the Rubi-Ka dream.

The main goal of 24-7 is money. Profit.
Excess funds from the buissness will be put into leisure, entertainment and scientific expeditions.

That is why all proffessions are welcome. Every proffession has something unique that defines them, so to maintain a broad variyty of services, we need representatives from every proffession.
Amongst our operations, we have so-called "buffinstations" with the goal to provide the customers with every buff available to makind and every variation of such.

Currently, our main place of buissniss has been by the South Bronto Burger Bar in Omni-1 Entertainment district. In the future, we hope to offer our services at several locations.

So, if any of this sounds interresting, let us know. Contact can be made to Xeni (director).

Remember, money are there to be earned. What else are they there for?

Skybert, Board Member
24-7

OOC:
We are currently not a very big organisation, but are hoping to fix that (with YOUR help).
Rubi-Ka 1 is the place, and we are mostly european players at the moment.
