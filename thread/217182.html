Tearfall's Return, A Longing for old friends
The man in the white Omni-Pol armor stood silently,
smiling softly to himself behind his black visor.

Finally....
After so long a time...
He had returned.

There were a few people he longed...
No.
Yearned to meet.

One of them he had fallen in love with,
Although...
So long a time had passed,
He knew that part of the relationship was most probably long gone. If not.... Then the smile slowly playing on his lips would grow all the larger, and burst into a joyous laughter.

If it was as he suspected, and feared....
Well...
Time can heal some wounds.
Perhaps if he gave it enough time?

...The following days, weeks and months would tell.

With that,
He gave a respectfull half-salute - two fingers held together, not the entire hand.

Tomorrow,
He said to himself...

Would be the first day,
Of my old life.

Only this time,
I promise myself.

I won't do the same mistakes again.

I know now, fully.
Where my heart lies.

To those of you who remember me,
Reading this.

I dedicate the above to you.



Yours Truly,
