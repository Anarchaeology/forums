Soleet
They didn't half UVC, they halfed crit chances in PVP.

It's kinda like this;

Before the -50% change - If you were running UVC, and you had a Globe of Clarity you would have a 25% chance to crit. Every time your character would strike your opponent a 4 sided die would roll. Only one side of that die would read "crit", the other three would read "not" crit.

After -50% crit chance rule - After that 4 sided die is rolled and you land on "crit", a coin is flipped. One side says "crit" the other side says "no crit". If the coin lands on "no crit" it cancels the die roll.

Since MAs are so heavily crit dependant (and shotty users too) basically they've halved our damage output in PvP with this nerf.

Halving MoP wouldn't do much, the ratios would just be slightly different. Aside from everything that has been listed on this thread so far......the other way to handle the problem is to make UVC 80% chance to crit. But that would only balance us in PVP....and heavily unbalance us in PVM.

That's why the suggestion to raise base damage was made. Crits are too undependable now.
