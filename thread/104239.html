OOC: As I was there from start to finish I will give you the basic overview:

Yxxil was a summoning event done by the Events Team, a gathering of MPs summoned a huge sentient Demon Lord, there was a battle which included some names such as Diamondcut, and eventually he was slain, but it was no more than a 3 days event if I remember rightly, after that he was no more, a few weeks later a green named character called Xzique decided he would play a person possessed by this demon, he gathered a cult around him, and we gathered an army to fight this cult, the story was really starting to take off, then from what I heard through the grapevine people complained about the satanic and fantasy nature of it, and Funcom put the whole concept in the bin, and they were all forced to drop the subject, a few players could not understand this, and tried to drag it out, but those of us that knew just dropped the subject, a few months after, Tarryk whom had come in contact with people from this cult trying to drag it out, used the name Yxxil and modified it to his advantage, which all in all was a pretty good idea, as it was not being used for anything else.

That is pretty much the story from start to finish, there never was a Yxxil storyline, it was just a one shot event, that was based over a few days, and the end.
