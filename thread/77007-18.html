Re-reading some of this thread... yes it is a very, very slow day at work... I see a pattern emerging.

The people who want low-level battles to be safe from interference by high level players are talking about battles where the players "should be" under level 100. Indeed, under level 80.

The people (me included) who want to defend their mid-level bases are mostly talking about bases and areas that are level 80+ or so; and most particularly, the 100-150 range.

That seems to suggest, from a design standpoint, that level 90 or so is the 'balance point' between what we might want to consider "high level" and "low level" for an LC area.

Imposing restrictions on attacking, buffing, and healing by higher level characters in level 89 and under LC areas, while letting people wage totally unrestricted war in level 90+ LC areas, might be a compromise solution worth exploring.

I still hold my position that as a matter of philosophy, organizations should always be fully able to defend their own bases. But in the interest of trying to compromise and increase everyone's happiness, I'm willing to put ideology aside and focus on a mechanic that will work well.

So, here is my suggestion:
Go with Cz's proposal in LC areas where the controller is QL 89 or lower. Inform any people who are too-high level to fight there that they are entering a LC area that's "too low" for them along with the mid-screen message, "You are entering a controlled area." That'll let them know right away that they'll probably be wasting their time.

But if the controller is QL 90 or higher, that "entering area" message should tell anyone entering the area that they're entering an unrestricted war-zone, and can be helped or attacked by anyone of any level. This will allow people who aren't willing to risk being slaughtered by level 200s to steer clear, but those who don't mind, can still go ahead and fight.

I'd be willing to entertain suggestions that that be raised to QL 100, but not very much higher, unless the power of towers is increased dramatically in the ql 100-150 range.

Well Hypos... I don't see how this is squeezing the middle level people out of the equation, to be honest.

If someone's level 100 and has level 80 friends, I think they'd be in his pvp range, and so he can go to a sub-90 LC area and fight alongside them. And anyone is always allowed to go to a higher-level LC area and fight with the gloves off, even if they're probably just committing a swift suicide by it. Granted, level 125s wouldn't be able to go to a level 80 LC area and fight... but on the other hand, that's sort of the point behind the restrictions.

The cut-off point would be the level of the controller in the LC area, giving defending guilds a choice about which set of rules they'll be defending under when they are going to be building on an area that spans QL 90. So if you're all sub-100, you can build a lower controller; if you're all 100+, you can build a higher one.

As a programmer myself, I think level scaling would be very difficult and time-consuming to code, and the more I think about it in practice, the more I think that nobody would like it very much. High levels don't want to be 'nerfed' down, especially if it's done in such a way that they'd have to swap equipment to fight in a lower-level area. Making high level folks swap their gear will keep them out of the action almost as effectively as simply warping them away.

But, even if high levelers do have to change clothes, low levelers probably still wouldn't be able to really "compete" with people who, though scaled down, are still far better equipped and far better developed/rounded.

As for the debuff-tower thing, something to consider is that towers can be destroyed. And those towers surely would be first on the list to take down by an attacking force if they had high levelers backing them up. Given the weakness of QL201- towers in general when undefended, I think it's safe to assume that at least one such tower will be destroyed before an effective defender response can begin.
