An idea could be... to make timed missions - not in the current meaning of missions, where you have 9-10 hours to complete them, but missions that would need these 2 'joined' teams (or 'big team divided in 2 parts') to complete tasks in the same amount of time. Like, one of the teams would have to retrieve information from a boss, while the other would have to place a bomb or repair something in the mission area/near the mission area/elsewhere... and so on. And of course, make it so that it's not possible to complete the mission if the efforts aren't coordinated.

That wouldn't be so easy to implement, but it could add another dimension to the thing.

"Team leader 1, do you receive me?
- Yes! Where are you?
- We've found the main reactor and placed the bomb. From now on, we have 2 minutes to evacuate before te base explodes!
- Okay! Hey, the others, did you hear this? Let's go out!"
