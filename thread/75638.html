Social Decay - Merely a thought
Lately, within the game, and certainly within the forums, there has been a constant state of argument that only continues to increase. Rp'ers versus Rp'ers, gamers versus gamers, and a mixed medley therein. There are people taking their in-game disagreements to the forums, and people in the forums taking their disagreements into the game. As I read so many of the posts, I cannot help but find so much of it sad and disturbing. AO is a game, and though it fulfills other roles, such as a meeting place for friends, it still remains nothing more than a game at its core. In that vein, it should be enjoyable, but such arguing clearly shows it is not enjoyable for a great many, or at least is headed in that direction (Unless one enjoys getting them self worked up, which is the case for some I am aware).

For those of you who know me, you might be aware that my involvement in AO has declined somewhat from both and rp and gaming perspective. Much of this is simply due to real life responsibilities. However, some of this is also due to all the player conflict going on. When I log onto AO, I log on for two reasons: To spend time/chat with friends, and to enjoy myself/relax. When I am not on AO, I have a real life, filled with all the complexities and turmoil that befalls us as we go about our daily routines, I certainly have no intention of adding insult to injury by logging into AO and getting myself worked up over something that exists only within a game that I am supposed to enjoy. In real life, I'm certainly less tolerant than I am within the bounds of this game, both from an rp and gaming perspective. Nevertheless, that is because AO is just a game to me, as it should be to everyone.

When I run into a situation where there is a disagreement between myself and another, I attempt to work it out as much as possible. Should this not come to pass, I simply drop the issue in whatever way would seem the most suitable, and then avoid contact with that individual. Of course, there are situations where people can't let an issue drop and have to not only pester you, but tell the world as well. Of course, perhaps ARK can be helpful in providing some type of solution then. Unfortunately, there will always be whiners, griefers, and the lot, but in the end, how much can really be achieved by argumentation? In the virtual world, people can hide behind a computer, and type away to their heart's content, saying things that would often certainly result in them getting physically pummeled in real life.

For just about every point there is a counterpoint, and realistically, a great deal of what goes on here is completely subjective to perspective. However, from the tone of many of the posts, you would think otherwise. Certainly I see a great many things I disagree with, but to take the time to argue and eventually get worked up about it would be fruitless, for most people retain their opinions regardless of what is said. Indeed, they might be made to look foolish in the eyes of some, but a great deal of the time, they'll still make that last nasty post, holding to their guns. Despite some posts being very civil and reasonable, there never seems to be any real closure until someone simply drops the issue.

I've been playing AO now for close to a year, people come, people go. The only thing that seems to remain constant is the arguments going on in the forums, and within the game. Any social congregating point is almost certainly going to be filled with some kind of mud-slinging on a regular basis. It is especially disconcerting when it involves shouting going back in forth, as if to notify everyone of the justification of the matter. Entire guilds get hauled into this type of mess, only making a bad situation worse. Meanwhile, everyone is subjected to something that they should not have to be.

As it mirrors real life, Rubi-Ka is far from a perfect world. Still, it would be a better world if people could take their individual arguments and keep it in a /tell setting. Indeed, one might ask, "What is the purpose of this post?" To that, I reply only that it is a collection of observations, perhaps to reach out to people who feel like me and let them know that there are others, or perhaps to offer a different perspective to those who see differently. Simply put, this has been on my mind for some time, and as I had the inclination to finally post, I opted to.

In the end, AO is but a game, filled with a variety of people, hopefully all with the goal of having fun. Unfortunately, as this game involves other player, our fun is often dependent on others. From a personal perspective, I have learned to enjoy the game regardless of what goes on by being selective on whom I deal with, and simply dropping arguments. When I log off AO, the real world is waiting for me, a real world that consists of the things that really matter. The people I care about in AO are still in my thoughts, and those who choose to hassle me are but an afterthought.
