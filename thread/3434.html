Ideas to get more people into the Disco Clubs, & Add new clubs..
When I went to the Disco clubs the first time, one in Newland city when it first appeared, tons of people used to be there... Now there isn't anyone there anymore... So then I would advertise, telling people tons of people are here, food, drinks etc, in Clan and OT Shopping, and about one person to ever 1-2 mins came, more like 15-20 within the hour or so... Then after they see no one is there they leave..... WE need to make the disco clubs more happenin... Heres my IDEAS...

1) Get real celebrities like music celebrities to come and talk to people there..
2) Advertise the clubs a bit more in the game
3) Have dance contests, give out prizes,....

4) Maybe add new features to the disco clubs, like sports clubs?
Or turn one or two rooms in the Stret West Bank into a Sports Bar. Have some TV sets up big screens or hanging from the walls.
And we can watch real sports on our home TV sets, not in the game, and talk to other people there thats watching sports at home too... Just like a real Sports Bar.

5) Have a PVP Ring in a club. Like Boxing and Wrestling, we can have real pvp rings in a sports club...
6) Add outside tables in the arena, and a bar so we can eat and watch pvp fights..
