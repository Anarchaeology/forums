Attention: Unguilded neutral Enforcers ((RK1))
The neutral guild, The Healing Hands, has now opened up the recruiting of all breeds of enfocers for its Protectors division.

For those of you who might not be familiar with us, The Healing Hands is an organization of Doctors and Protectors whose goal it is to aid all of those in need on Rubi-Ka, regardless of alignment/breed/gender. We do not allow PvP and do not assist in tower attacks or defense. We strive to remain truely neutral so that we are better able to assist citizens from every part of the planet. Since its creation back in November of 29.475 ((2001)), HH has strictly recruited only Atrox enforcers for its Protectors. With the introduction of the Notum Towers and the draw of war and bloodshed now almost too great to resist, it is even more difficult for us to find these kinds of people who are willing to dedicate themselves to aid and protection, rather than destruction.

If you are a neutral enforcer and feel you would like to join us, please leave a reply here. Or contact our Chief Training Officers: Zeroorez and Linaa; or our Chief of Staff: Billybob2.
