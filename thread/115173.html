Fable Reading in ICC
My dear friend Katpaws and I would like to invite anyone interested to join us for a recital of several of our Rubi-Ka Fables.

Katpaws will start off with the story of the First leet, followed by the Leet and the Viper and she'll finish with The Dreamer.

We'll then have a short intermission as I prepare to read from my collection.

I'll start off with Lies and Truth. I'll follow that with Destiny. And finish with my latest creation Being You.

Kat will get things started around 6:oo pm Eastern US Time (that's 11:00pm GMT). We'll go to the back of the ICC area (away from the whompas so as not to disturb anyone who isn't interested in hearing our stories.

We're doing this in ICC because we want all of our friends (Clan, Neutral and Omni alike) to be able to join us for this small event.

((The Rubi-Ka Fables only exist in-game. I've never posted them outside and I have no plans to. If your curious about them, or if you've never seen stories told in-game before, please stop on by.))
