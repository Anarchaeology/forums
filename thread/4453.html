Agents Envisioned: Read, Funcom, read!
Hi,

I wont' get into the whole 'agents being gimped' subject. Or at
least try to. I only wish to add a few interesting ideas:

1)Make chemistry useful. My idea? Make it to where you can put
different 'tips' on your ammo, allowing you to alter the TYPE of
damage that your weapon does. It's so obvious, it's funny!!

I still wonder why that hasn't been implemented, FC. Hop on it...

2)Move Ranger Increasers to 'slot' 2, so it doesn't interefere, and
unequip our yalmahas. Some of us want to buff a good one on.

3)Agent only armor for the back! Chille suits, baby! Not only gives
AC's, but ups concealment. Also cool for RPing the agent on the
front lines. Gawd, this would so own it all! Other agent only armor
too, that add to AS, Conceal, Rifle, etc.. but, to be fair, make other
profession armor lines, as well. Not hard to do... just make the
'bonuses' vary by QL. I think it'd be nice to have more prof specific
armors, that could be available in shops. Whataya think?

4)As stated before, a 'go prone' option. Have it increase crit% by3
and raise conceal by 50, at the penalty of lowering evades for,
maybe... 30 seconds, or so, by 30? This could work....

5)Stated by me long ago, before I could post here, and has
quickly formed into a 'no brainer'. Make a hotbar button for reload,
for those with small clips. That, or fix the RELOAD BUG!!!

Still, it'd be nice.

6)Lower suppression zones in 'capitol cities' like Tir, Athens, and
the Omni equivalents to 25%, with amnesty still in effect, if need
be. Then give agents a 'False Identity' line, that makes them look
like NPC's to other player characters. Ex: I get targetted, after
using "Assume identity: Male Omni Guard.", and to the player, it
shows up as POD: ----0----! Conceal Vs. Perception will be
what keeps us hidden from NPC's. Great idea! SELF ONLY!

7)Make sharp objects this: Trade skill-weapon extentions. Make
Bayonettes that can be added to weapons as a secondary melee
attack, which can be used as 'Sneak Attack'. Kind of like backstab
in EQ, I guess. Since As only cycles once a battle, if we can re-
conceal in a group, we can get off a 'sneak attack', too. Every
other class has more than one crappy special, why not us?

That's all for now, as I'm incredibly tired. But mark my words, I
sha;ll be back to add more! Feel free to do the same!

Tip of the iceburg, baby! I'll be back!! =)
