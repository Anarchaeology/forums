Just a few quick notes to Dworkin.

First show us your "proof" in your screenshots. Don't keep just mentioning them and never letting us see them to judge for ourselves.

In EQ we weren't allowed to take screenshots from players as evidence. Screenshots are too easily altered. While the screenshots very likely get us to keep an eye out over the person that is the subject of them, it's so easy for someone to fake out screenshots. So something like that would be a cause to keep an eye on the person, but it wouldn't be able to condem someone or cause any penalty action against them.

Next, some games purposely buff up the volunteer players along with the normal GM's. It is more likely that you saw a buffed ARK and were thinking it was a player that got that high.
