My idea for Agents
SO i had this idea for agents, that i think most non-agents are going to hate off the bat (just remember, i'm a non-agent too guys) I think would be really interesting and just plain entertaining.

So, apparently the agents now have the ability to switch sides basically at will (although i've heard a lot of conflicting stories about the ease of this) and can false profession. I'm thinking this is ideal for assassins.

Why don't the agents (and maybe fixers too, being that they are sneaky grid guys) get, as an offshoot of the switching sides ability, become immune to supression fields. That way you could actually have assasins in your area at any time.

I think it would really add an element to the game if wherever you went there was always that chance that something could happen. That any of these guys around you could actually be a clan assassin waiting to pop you. Now, the remedy for safety would be that although you would be a target in the cities there's guards and other people there to sort of stop the agent.

But i'm really into the idea that one moment you're walking through rome, waving to a omni engineer, then the next moment he's a clan assassin, he pops you, and then runs away, and if he's lucky can conceal before he's caught.

Now, there's always the possibility that agents would use this to camp mission area where there are no guards and just gank people, so maybe just make them immune to suppression in cities.

I don't know, i just think it would add a real element to the game.

Oh, and when i say that they'd be removed from supression, they could attack AND be targetted.
