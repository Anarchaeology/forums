Super-Pol's Super-Cop of the week!
This award is given to the cop with the most busts for the week. This week the award goes to Lovielilly for the arrest of one Pete "Thoram" Frost. Also she played a key role in the return of Omni-Tek property. And OT-Army officer pistol from a Minh "Minatt" Matt.
