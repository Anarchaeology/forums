I'll tell you why...because we've also been ranting for new social items for years now, and we just recently got them (in the form of new shades and a few new hats...not much but it's something).

Roleplay patches are rare, but possible. While this is a technical issue as far as lag, it is occuring in a RolePlay area--hence, as far as FC is concerned, this is a RolePlaying issue. And that's something that's at the bottom of their priority list.

That's understandable, considering that for every roleplayer you have complaining about some limiting social aspect of the game, you have 3 or 4 HUNDRED different kids screaming because their weapon got nerfed. Priorities lay in the numbers, and while Anarchy Online has one of the highest numbers of RolePlayers than any other game in the MMORPG genre, there is still a relatively small fraction of us compared to the powergamers.

So all we can do is hang tough, keep up our forum petitions for content changes and RP-bug fixes, and eventually it just might happen.
