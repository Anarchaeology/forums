The Daeva sisters
Shifting nervously in her biomech armor she watches as her older sister's hands work at the console... her fingers moving with amazing speed, obviously aided by the implants just below the skin. A faint glow from the trader's eye tells the tale of nanobots collecting information from the screen and piping it directly into a small receptical placed near her brain. Looking over her shoulder the doctor checks to be sure no guards are around... earlier a giant leet had stormed through the gates, drawing a large croud. The sisters had decided that it would be a good time to execute their plan.
"Just about done... you ready to scan?"

This was it... the moment she sisters had discussed several weeks back, then just a plan, now a reality. Slowly she steps into the cell scanner, a familiar beep is heard. Stepping back she watchs as her only sister, a respected merchant in these lands, moves to stand before the scanner. It is done. Stepping away the older sister disconnects the console, packs away the lock pick and interface borrowed from a fixer that made all this possible, and looks up.

"Do you have the stims?"

nodding slowly the surgeon reaches beneath her cloak to withdraw with two stims.

"There should be no pain... the nano-contaigeon I used is quick and effective. There are mild pain killers in here as well..." her voice trailes off speaking the last word. Reaching out she places a stim in her sister's hand. "No longer will we defy death."

Reaching to her belt the older sister pulls out her personal communicator. She sends a farwell message to her longtime friend Amnra, wipes a tear from her face and takes a deep breath.

The sisters walk to the hill above the grid terminal, overlooking the lake. The soldier is already waiting. Daevadame hands him an access card "This will retrieve our belongings. Please distribute them to good people."

Taking each other's hands, the sisters sit to watch the moons set across the water. Just before the first sun is to rise, they each take their stim, place it against their neck under their chin, and, with a calm smile simultaneously inject. The soldier watches as nanobots break down the remains of the two sisters, only this time there will be no reconstruction. They found their peace... that is all that matters.
