Happens occasionally, but you get to know the zones. Some mission entrances have always been camped by big nasties though (Mantises in Avalon and GTC for instance, Lifestealers on TLR etc.) although "big" is obviously a relative term in this context. Other missions require to to travel through zones that are distinctly unhealthy at the levels you can pull those missions at. Then there's MMD, which is camped by the players...

There are still plenty of un-camped mission entrances, in fact there are more entrances un-camped than camped. If you genuinely reckon you won't make it out alive, consider:
  • What are the odds that I'll ding this mission?
  • Is it worth it just for the reward?
