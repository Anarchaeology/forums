Guess whos back..
As my shuttle flew through the atmosphere above Rubi-Ka, I couldnt help but feel like I was truly coming home.

My shuttle docked and the doors opened, I couldnt help but run out of the exit, pushing a few people out of my way, and taking a deep look at the world I once knew so well, it was my home, and it felt damn good to be back

Things had changed alot, without question, I noticed mercenaries posted throughout Newland, and not a clanner in site. I was born and bred Omni, but this was like a new beggining for me. I didnt know quite what side I was on anymore.

I reached the gleaming towers of Omni-Trade and filled out a few forms, I checked my bank and everything was still there. I contemplated taking the old gear out and trying it on, but I decided I had enough fighting for the time being, maybe later.

I walked down the street to the center of Omni-Trade, and saw a few familiar faces, and thought back to the days where I knew everyone I saw.

I sat down and took everything in, where to go next, I thought.

I looked up and saw a shuttle disembarking into space, I remembered how I had felt when I left, and thought about what a big mistake it was...

I was back, and hopefully for good
