Stats/requirements on any given QL of item (well, MOST stats):

(Value at QL"N") = (Value at QL1) + ("N"-1 * ((Value at QL200 - Value at QL1) / 199)

This is how the database knows what the stats (again, most of them, AC is a bit different but well known and multiwield requirements on weapons differ as well) on an item is, without needing to know any individual values.

If George *always* gave a QL20 pad, then they would have only one QL in the database. But since he is giving out a variable QL pad, it is a lot more convenient to have the stats for QL1 and QL200, and then let the game calculate the stats for the QL he drops. The alternative would be to have a QL20, QL21, ...., QL40 if that mob drops from QL20 to QL40. As you can see it is a lot more convenient to put two items instead of 20 items in the DB.

No one ever said that the item exists in QL200 versions. FC never planned to have people looking at items in their raw form in the DB.
