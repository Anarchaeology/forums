Xenoguile:ReBirth (a FanFiction)
The world was good, experiance was plentiful, death... was not fatal. Then it happened... assasinated, by clanners, with a Ritual Knife Xenoguile died, thought to be lost forever, his being not returning to the net computers for reconstruction, for weeks teams slaved over pages and pages of computer code trying to find even the slightest hint of what Xeno once was. Eventually they were forced to end their trials and tribulations and accept that he was gone. Time moved, as it does, flowing past like an endless river not stopping for anything, but perhaps; changing its course for only the biggest of obsticals.
"It was like death, but not. There was nothing like there had been before, the matrix, the code, the layer of submeta-physical and nano-life that pulsed over the real world was gone, and there was nothing. I myself was there but I was nothing as well, a part of it, a peice of it, all of it. I was nothing. As so it went slipping through and in and out of my own consciencousness I felt myself giving into the nothing, slowly (as if time were such a thing there) becoming nothing more than, nothing. I saw it. At first just a dream, a shadow, a wisp of what once was there. Like a dot in endless space, like a black hole in the darkness of eternity. Hope. The knife had been flawed, and there it was, me, my DNA, my nano, my power. Through nothing, everything, something, I came to it envoloped it, and it became me again. And the nothing, just stopped. Millions of lines of code and volts of electricity sugred through my being, lost in the endless computers of Omni-Tek. I lived in the computers, a ghost in the machine, gathering power, anything i could hold through will or force became me. I could feel it and it began to hurt like a searing pain of flames throughout me, my nanos would try to recreate my flesh yet it was quickly torn from me atom by atom. I could not sustain my flesh inside the mainframe the flow simply would not allow it. And so I trained my self and my nano companions to hold their power so close, so tight, as to not lose a drop, and I slept, still fearing the nothing, still not much more than nothing, I knew I needed time. I slept. Reawaking I found something I almost could not believe, I had it, I had enough, to try, to live again. Like a lost spirit I drifted, seeking the ReClaim that I had once connected to, like a beacon of light it shown brighter than anything I had seen in an enternity. My digital self almost smiled in the system just behind the ReClaim. Like lightning I shot through the system heading for the real world, dodging code of the dead who were to be resurrected. For those who were there, they have told me it was like nothing else, my body had not be resurrected or respawned as they had been and seen millions of times. I was made, nano by nano, screaming in pain of birth as my body molded out of raw energy and flesh was made and torn off and then made again untill it stayed. I was. Reborn again into the real world, my nanos slowly completed their work as I stood to spread my horrible but happy painful smile for the witnesses now huddled around my molding body. 'I am Xenoguile,' I could feel my skin sinking into the correct places as I become completly whole and solid,


'From the depth of nothing I have returned, and I live once again.'"
