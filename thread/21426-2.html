*Invisibility is now implemented for player-to-player interaction. This means you can hide from another player, and he will have to hit "?" (Search) button to see you. When you hide, your Concealment skill is used against Perception. (With 75% chance of being hidden if the skill level is equal). When someone is hidden and you search for him or her, you use perception as the attacking skill. (Thus having 75% chance of finding the opponent.) Also, we have found a bug in the Concealment code that we have fixed, increasing the chance of you being hidden from green mobs. The search code is changed when looking for hidden characters. You have a base radius of 5-meters which increases to a 40-meter radius (or all visible characters with 100% char view distance) when you get 1000 in perception.

You people must realize that all of this is based off a percentage. By hitting your search button to scan your search radius multiple times you are increasing the odds of spotting someone even with high concealment if they stay in your search radius too long. If there is a chance of being spotted then you will be eventually. As an agent I get detected by mobs all the time. Not just red mobs either...grey mobs detect us currently because mobs of any level seem to have a 40m+ search radius and at least a 20% chance of spotting us regardless of how high our concealment is versus your perception.

The way I see concealment working for PvP, perception skill seems to be more important than even your concealment skill until you reach 1000 in perception. After reaching 1000 in perception, your scan radius increases to 40m. Concealment skill then starts to matter more because as your concealment skill increases so does your chances of sneaking further into the attack radius of your enemy without being detected. However one thing to keep in mind though is that if you see someone that is concealed they will know that you see them due to the concealment feedback. They just won't know where you are or even if you are a threat to them at all due to level differences if you are concealed.

We do have a use in the group by using our high perception skills to spot concealed players in PvP zones and root them out so to speak. We attack a hiding player and both of us become visible to everyone. If someone doesn't want to be found then we can find them. We may die as well but we take the first shots...and help out our team that way.

Will PvM concealment/perception checks work under the same principle as PvP concealment/perception checks in the future?
