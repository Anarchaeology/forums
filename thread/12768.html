static mission
Best in Brass is used in a special mission, there are 9 npc's:

Best in Brass (galway shire, outside rome blue)
Greasy Joints (north of newland)
Live Metal (bot mountain nnw of tir)
Metalomania (inside harry's)
Nuts and bolts (warton valley near rising sun)
Trash king lackey (athen shire)
Galvano (greater omni forest east close to shore)
Elektro Unqiue (?)

and the Trash King (athen shire) himself...

All should be over lvl 125 and have the "boss" stats with way too many hp for their level.

The Trash King drops a "Communication Processing Unit Rack with 8 empty slots", the others drops chips for this device. I think you can give the fully "Complex Communication Processing Unit" to Dodga (clan) or Alvin (omni) and get a "Shoulderpads for the Learned" with higher quality.

I've not killed any of these npc's myself yet, and I can't find the Trashking anymore (used to be in athen's junk yard west of athen, perhaps now in greater tir). Also I don't know where elektro (or is it spelled electro?) is...

Any hint to this "2nd dodga mission" would be nice. Please post if you spot any of these npc's.
