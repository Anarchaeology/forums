Then he starts complaining to me about how we couldnt kill him etc. This carrys on him calling me names (not sure if i can say on boards) and all that telling me i'm a title hugger and whatnot (which i'm not), eventually he says hes going to leave the arena....

But it doesnt end there.

A Few mins later i decide to leave to log off in the grid but i see him *trying* to sneak into the arena to get me, so i head to the grid.. half way there i turn around and see him behind me looking alot like hes following me (i didnt take any screenshots, foolishly)
