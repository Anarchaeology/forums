Restoring the Old Athen Restoration Project
Quite some time ago now that its mostly forgotten in the collapse of the Council of Truth and other critical events was a project to raise money for the restoration of Old Athen.

Old Athen is the first and foremost of many Clan cities, but its still littered with Omni-Tek garbage left from centuries ago.

Its time to unlock those funds, donate more if need be, and get clan artisans and constructors in to fix up Old Athen.

I propose that:
  • All Omni-Tek signs and propaganda be permently removed
    We construct our own statues and reface some of the drab Omni architecture.
    The some of the more disruptive debris is removed


Old Athen should resemble a city for all the Clans, because it is. I propose that each major clan donate its own architects for the project.

I would like to see Clan Knights redo the architecture on and around the Cathedral on the north end of the city. Restore and reopen this site as a place of religious freedom.

Clan Eco Warriors should take the east end and remove the rubble and replace it with the plants and natural attractions they so love.

I propose that Clan Sentinels take the south end around their tower store and fix it up. Remove the OT statues and put in your own. Time for your actions to live up a little more to your rhetoric.

Maybe even we can get the Athen city council to approve a few of each Clans independent guards and use them as an opportunity to teach everyone about our different Clans.

Please, lets show what a real city belonging to all the clans should look like and complete this long forgotten vision of the Clans.
