Fixer nano: False alignment
Agents got their false profession nanos, since their characters are supposed to be impersonators.
Fixer, on the other hand, are the underworld, and are supposed to be dealing with any sides, regardless of conflicts.
I posted this idea a while ago, but little response, so im reposting it now.
The idea is basically a "False alignment nano" for fixers, to switch alignment for a duration of time. This will or should not have impact on items requiring alignment (armor and alike), but will be useful when entering cities and maybe using terminals.
As per today and the current amnesty, it is NOT that kinda useful, but when (yes, i say when, its unevitable) it falls, it can be useful.
Another aspect of this which probably will strike people, is the PvP part of a False Alignment. YES, let the opposite side be able to shoot the FA'er(FA=False Alignment), meaning if a clan changes alignment for the small duration the nano lasts, he will in combat terms/means also change sides. (dang, this can cause some trouble for fixers who take advantage of this, and helps out the opposite side if its discovered)
When it concerns Token board: Of course no affect on that.

I thought I would make this a little clearer reguarding my previous post. The caster can turn back into their original identity anytime they want to, but when they use the nano it will only change them into the one identity that they have chosen.

---

Ok I have figured out how a cover could be blown. If the caster changes back into their original identity when someone else, NPC or player is within 40m and have a line of site, they will be discovered. The false identity can be remove voluntarily by the caster or made unstable if the player's info is queried by another player. When the player's identity is unstable it has a random chance of dropping. The chance that the identity would break is dynamic. It goes up while the character is being inspected. It goes down while the character is in their alternate identity and not being inspected. The rate that is goes down will be a lot slower than the rate that it goes up.

Overall the chance that an identity would fail would be low. If you suspected that another player wasn't who they said they were, the best way to break it would be to constantly have them targeted and to get as many of you friends to do the same. These valuse could also be affected by the perception and concealment skills of the players.
