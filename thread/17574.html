Realism: Perception, Psychology, and checks for level, profession and alignment
Okay, this might have to wait until the distant future, but I think this will make the game much more realistic and provide for some interesting gameplay. I'm going to discuss it in terms of a player experience, so please keep in mind (I also think this might have been part of the original design but wasn't implemented.).

You're new to Rubi-Ka, in the backyard, and you see a guard and a guide. They are both red to you. You can't tell what alignment they are, but they seem nice enough and they aren't shooting at you. You see other players. Most are yellow to you, some are orange. You can see their names, but you don't know their exact level, profession, alignments, guild affiliations, etc.

You level once off some leets and increase your stats some. You happened to pick nanomage as a breed, and your perception is a bit higher than most. You notice that you are able to discern more information about those that are greenish-yellow to you. For many of the new arrivals, you are able to discern their level, profession, and alignment. You are even able to discern the level and professions of some of the enforcers and soldiers that are yellow to you. Still, you can't tell what some yellows and many oranges are. But they aren't shooting at you (except for that damn Fresh Clan Functionary, what's his problem?).

Okay, you get the idea, as you level and increase your perception, you are able to discern more about your surrounding players and npcs. Also, and this is important, the npc's are able to discern more/less about you. See the following example:

You're a level 151 Agent, Clan, and you'd like to check out what's going on in Omni-1 Trade. You pop out of the wompa and the guards don't say a word. The guards are around level 100-120, and don't have the perception to beat yours (or the psych check level to beat your concealment, someone will have to flesh this out some, please post your thoughts). In fact, many of the Omni players in trade can't tell what faction you're in. They might think you're an agent based on the rifle and general appearance. Heck, they even grub some feline grace buffs off you and pay you for them. Smirk. Then a level 170 MP shouts: He's clan! Your cover is blown. She beat your basic concealment (this is separate from an envisioned agent "mask/change alignment" nano program), and was able to read your alignment. You could shout back that it's a bug, your still running a nano program since you just left Tir, etc. In the end, this is all role playing, and you could have played a bit better by staying out of her sight, etc.

I think some work/features along these lines would be great for the game. You would get a real feel for growing into a character as you level, of becoming more aware of your surroundings. The con-bar would be all important for accessing possible risks on certain missions. At high levels, you could wander past guards who would be unable to discern your alignment, but you'd still be subject to 'law enforcement' if you attacked an omni-sided/clan-sided player/npc in their sight. This would make the contested zones more interesting, especially if joe-pvp would have to start an attack on someone without actually knowing their alignment, and possibly getting an error message if they were running a nano program, and possibly being in a bad situation if they guessed wrongly. For example, clan enforcer attacks clan doctor in 25% zone, both get message of the attempted attack. Clan doctor gets some griping rights. Clan doctor is actually omni agent using false profession and change alignment.

The change alignment nano could also open you up to other risks: using the above example, the omni agent posing as a clan doctor gets successfully wasted by omni fixer a few minutes later. Omni agent posts his information to the fixer using a reference command on himself while at the reclaim terminal. Nice job, Mr. Fixer, could you just root me next time and chat with me next time?

Also, this would put a premium on names in pvp, at least for the agents who are doing a lot of the sneaking. There should be a name masking nano program that would allow people to put in any name they want, like naming a pet. They would still receive tells for their real name, and wouldn't receive tells on the masked name, so an agent could pretent to be Spyo, but wouldn't get the tells and if Spyo was online at the time, his cover could quicky be blown. (Sorry, Spyo, for using you in this example, I hope you don't mind. You rock!) Once again, a perception check could see past the masked name.

Okay, that's a lot for agents, and some for people who want to be able to see/perceive more about their surroundings. I think other classes should be able to use some of the above suggested nano programs, perhaps different ones that perform the same function, but require more skills (thus giving agents an advantage of being able to use them earlier, but not an exclusive license in the high level realm).

Please let me know what you think,
Ecce, Abunari

In thinking about it, I think it would be perception and concealment that would be most involved.

I'm not sure how that would work with "hiding/masking" nano programs, but if agents get profession specific ones that work at a lower level than the non-profession specific ones, then that's great.
