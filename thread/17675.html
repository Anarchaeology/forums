I think they have been trying to level the playing field all along. But I also think that 14.2 is the beginning of a change in direction for thier leveling. It seemed before they were trying to level it by raising the overall difficulty to suit the grossly overpowered characters that the original system allowed, which caused difficulty for normal/average players. Now they seem to be trying to lower the bar on the grossly overpowered players to a more normal/average level, which I think will be a much better solution overall in the long run. Traders will still be a bit of an issue, but not one that can't be reasonably fixed, and all the average players should eventually reap the rewards of the lower overall difficulty.
