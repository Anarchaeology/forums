Many ways...

You can buy it from a shop (v.expensive)
You can do a mission for it with the slider set harder (difficult)
You can get given it (nice)
You can buy it in the trade channels (best bet)

Look on the bottom of your friends window you'll see a communication channels called trade, tick the 0-50 one of them and watch the adverts flood in.

-- works best if you are somewhere busy like trade for omni or tir for clan.
