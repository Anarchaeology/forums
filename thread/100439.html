..... but .... what's this?

Expecting to have to kill the Gerber baby to get a heart-shaped lollipop, Asterik was dumbfounded and confused by this strange turn of events.

It felt odd, having the credits subtracted from his account as he purchased mementos for his loved ones. Had someone learned the meaning of celebration after all? From the horrors of Hallowe'en to the decidely lowered level of drudgery involving the winter holidays to this. Each celebration had become subsequently more humane. Instead of killing babies for their lollipops and candy bars or mugging housewifes to take the flowers from their vases, he was witnessing people smiling, ladies blushing, and love in the air.
