I think many people are being mislead by the word "syringe" in the description. You don't need a syringe; I've never even seen one. Here's what you need to make first aid stims:

Receptive Bots
combined with Mimicing Cellular Oil (becomes some pink thing afterwards)
lastly combined with a Nano Knot of Restoration

While these do not heal for as much or as consistently as a store-bought first aid, they have the added bonus of restoring nano points (although I have never actually tested one.) A ql 145 one took about 580 pharma tech skill to make, restored 500-700ish hps and nano in one shot, required over 700 first aid skill, and cost a low low 800k to make (gak).
