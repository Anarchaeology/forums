Leetas
PvP flagged persons can not be attacked in 100%. But they can be attacked in all other areas.

You will be PvP flagged if you're not in a guild or if your guild has no towers. If you do have towers then you will not be PvP flagged, but your towers will turn to 25% gas in 1 hour.

And I think that when a person is PvP flagged the level rules slightly increase. My trader alt could not attack a trader before the person attacked a tower and became PvP flagged.

The rules have been changed a bit.

At NW launch, you could attack any level player that was flaged in any gas, including the grid, now you can only attack players in your range in 75% gas or less.

You get flagged when you attack towers and your guild has no towers, or if you are not in a guild (therefore you have no towers) and attack towers. I haven't tried leaving a guild (since your towers stay) and attacking towers, but you may or may not get flagged depending on the check.
