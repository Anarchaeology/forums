New Management for The Dancing Atrox!
Rubi-ka 1

Hello Everyone!

I would just like to let everyone know Crystal Line is taking management of The Dancing Atrox Bar located in Omni Screening Area. We are in the final stages of preparation and hope to be up and running in full swing shortly. Anyone interested in the following postions please contact me or come tonight at 7pm EDT(GMT -4) at The Dancing Atrox. We will be having live DJ's weekly starting tonight at 8:00pm EDT (GMT time -4) Stay tuned for there is oh so much more to come!

At the present time we are looking for staff. The following postions are needed:

Head Hostess: An Executive position within Crystal Line. Will work closely with Management to handle waitresses, bartenders, and waiters. Will be special events service staffing coordiantor as well. Uniform required and paid for by management. Bonuses rewarded.

Waitresses and Bartenders: Fexible hours and available for special events. Uniforms required and paid for by Management. Bonuses rewarded based on work record and attendance.

Just Head out the east gate in Omni Entertaiment District and make a hard right. (292, 1863 Omni Screening Area)

We look forward to seeing you there!

"RELAX!" - Dancing Atrox Bar
Greetings.

"RELAX!"/Dancing Atrox Bar is definitely my favorite bar on Rubi-Ka.

I really hope to see a lot of events there. It's a tiny place, with laser lights and an illuminated dancefloor, and two seating areas, which affords for a wonderfully cozy, comfortable party environment.

A few months back, Bethani had a spur-of-the-moment party there, and it's still the best one I've attended on Rubi-Ka. So many people showed up to that party--and the atmosphere of that club was absolutely fantastic--it gave it a truly intimate (house) party vibe.

(As for RPing...Gridstream Productions hosting parties there would a great way to annoy the hell out of Omni-Pol. It's an Omni-Tek bar...but it's NOT in Omni-1 Entertainment, it's just outside the gates from Omni-1 Entertainment and down the road (south)....and... *chuckles* ...down the road from the bar is the Omni-Pol Barracks. *grins* How fitting.)

("RELAX!"/Dancing Atrox Bar Location: 290 X 1860 - Omni Screening Area, Omni Forest)

I am thrilled that "RELAX!"/Dancing Atrox Bar is finally going to be frequented more. I think it's the best club on Rubi-Ka for a great, intimate party.
