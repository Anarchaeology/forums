Weapon shops need to be seperated by weapon type.
What do you hate more. Never finding a weapon because shops are full of weapons you don't want. There is too large of a mix of weapons in one shop that you can never find Assault Rifle or 2 Handed Edge or whatever you are looking for.

So I propose this. We need weapon shops like the trade shops. Seperate them by type. Shops that hold only:

Assault Rifles
Rifles
Pistols
SMGs
Bows
Shotguns
2 Handed Edges
1 Handed Edges
1 Handed Blunt
2 Handed Blunt
Piercing

If I left out a type of weapon FunCom don't forget about it.

This can go a long way in really improving the shops of Rubi-Ka and help people find the weapon they want in the QL they want.

This can be done as FunCom has done it for trade skills. Now lets get this going and really help out the shoppers. So we can get back to what we really want to do instead of shopping.

Bumpity bump if you think this is a worthy and much needed request.
