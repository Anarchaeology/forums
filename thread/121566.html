Allow people in same organisation to see who owns towers
Hi,

Another suggestion that I am sure has been made before but I would lurve to see implemented:

Can towers be changed so that if you are in the same organisation you can see who owns each tower in land areas you control please ? (by doing an info on the tower when you select it)

The reason for this is tower maintenance (e.g. terminating a badly placed tower and not knowing who owns it right now) and also in larger guilds being able to better manage who places towers (e.g. in larger guilds you might want to have a rule that folks should only place 1 tower each when you dont have much land )

On a related subject can anyone confirm that if someone is kicked from an organisation that their towers are destroyed? The guild I belong too removes players that have not logged on in several weeks to keep our guild roster current - but I never see any messages about towers being destroyed and of course we can't see who owns the towers anyway so I'm pretty sure tower spots are being taken up by people who are no longer in the organisation

Clearly the information already exists because you can see which towers you own yourself "You own this tower!" message when you do an info on a tower you own.

This really would be a big help
