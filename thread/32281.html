My research to Epsilon Purge with PvP
Because I still wanted to give that new nano a chance I did some testing in the Arena with it. First lemme explain what thew (new) nano does:

- Takes away all buffs from NCU that takes less then 35 NCU
- aprox 5s cast, 10s recast
- low nanocost
- 700 sensimp req

That's about it for the most important specs.

I didn't get to test it on all classes yet but here are some of my finding's, I'll add more later:

Soldier
Tested on: Solitus lvl 180 selfbuffed, Rookie
Results:
- Removed all expertise buffs
- Removed masteries
- Removed AT
Reaction: OMFG whats this? You removed all cept my Reflect and Offensive Steamroller!!! ()

Verdict: Always, always use this on soldiers! Takes away a lot AR, their dmg ability and MAYBE even their ability to swap to Flashpoint. Combined with Wnr dot the lower AR will HURT. Cast it on a soldier using MK7 (even lvl 150 solds use it now and then) and its gone


Martial Artist
Tested on: Solitus lvl 172 selfbuffed, Rookie
Results:
- Removed fists
- Removed all expertises
- Removed their str buff
Reaction: Hmm is that all? This isn't hurting me very much, just annoyance..

Verdict: Outside buffed ma's will be hurt more and MA's not using weapons as well (mine used some weapon that did fire dmg so the removal of fists didnt help anything). It may be good but since ma's tend to crit a lot you do not have time to cast it IMO. Maybe only good against <lvl 140 ma's.


Doctor
Tested on: Nanomage lvl 200 selfbuffed, Freshman (drkinky)
Results:
- Removed expertises
Reaction: It was irritating that I had to (re)cast MC expertise to cast WnR on you but that's about all.

Verdict: Useless. We doc's have huge NCU reqs on HOT, short hp, teamhp etc, we'll only loose expertises. Maybe better if the doc glows blue (HE) but nah, not worth it.


Nano Technician
Tested on: Nanomage lvl 150 selfbuffed, Freshman
Results:
- Removed EVERYTHING
Reaction: OMG what the hell?!!!!

Verdict: *grin* Always cast this baby, say bye to most buffs lol. Add a nice debuff and you have a 100% winchance (not that we didnt have one already). Yes, it also removed Nullity



Okay, 4 only but I'll try get some other classes to test it on as well. The reactions were so darn funny cept the ma one, darn such little effect on them. I don't pvp docs anyways because it takes ages I especially loved the reaction of that 180 soldier when he saw nearly all his buffs go away, some other soldier rightfully said: Okay, this means every soldier will get owned badly agaisnt docs using that. I must agree. It's even more fun to use it against TMS MK7 and lower soldiers

I think we underestimated this nano, in PvP it rocks. It will not only lower some stats, its a psychological move too: a lot people panic and think they will get creamed bad now. Not many out there who dare to PvP UNbuffed eh? That's how it will feel to them. My advice is to use it against non crit-happy classes when you can; you HAVE those 10s time because they can't kill you so quickly without crits. It's a good surprise effect and people will fear you for it

I'm not saying I WANT this nano to stay like this, don't get me wrong, hey, I started that Eps Purge thread. But as long as they didnt change it I'm going to use it. It's still worthless in PvM so don't bother if you do not PvP. I'll still nag Funcom in the proff-forum to change it to what the old description says

It's so darn funny to see ppl /say "wth" "wtf" when you cast it on them during a duel
