The Current State of Rubi-Ka
Ammicus, locked in his appartment in Omni-1 and unable to get out, flips open the datascreen on his desk

The Current State of Rubi-Ka

Much has changed in the world over the course of this year, yet as the ancient adage holds, "the more things change, the more things stay the same." From the fracturing of old alliances to the continued wars enveloping the world as we know it, and worlds that we are just discovering, to revivals of old ideas and the resurrection of ghosts from the past, Rubi-Ka and the Shadowlands continue to intrigue us.

The Notum Wars

Battles rage across the countryside on a daily basis, as Omni-Tek, neutrals and clans all vie for control of the ever-lucrative notum mining rights. Officials on all sides, from Omni-Tek Corporation of Rubi-Ka (OTRK) CEO Philip Ross to the petty Sentinels Clan leader Simon Silverstone, have been almost silent on developments occuring on the battlefield. Omni-Tek officials are undoubtedly wary of drawing the ire of the Interstellar Confederation of Corporations (ICC) for making statements seen as inflamatory towards the conflict, and Simon Silverstone and the rest of the Sentinels seem preoccupied with counting the vast sums of credits inflating their financial accounts to care about encouraging their clan brethren forward.

Yet, on balance, the war has essential held in stalemate conditions for some time. While various mines change hands daily, with key flashpoints of the conflict being in Stret West Bank, Newland Desert and Galway County, there has been little marked gain by any faction in controlling a majority of these. Much to the dismay of Omni-Tek, the clans have held a slight advantage in the numbers. However, this has never lasted for any appreciable amount of time. The neutrals continue to make marginal gains, but are most often overwhelmed by the larger conglomerate force strength of either of the two "declared" factions.

The Interstellar Confederation of Corporations

The ICC, after implictly condoning this war with its vengeful decision to deregulate OTRK's market for notum mining, has also been enveloped by a pervasive silence since its November, 29476 decision. Isidra Derousse, the woman almost equally hated by both the Clans and Omni-Tek, seems to have vanished into insignificance. Undoubtedly the ICC has at the very list heard of the carnage sweeping Rubi-Ka as a result of its decision, yet has done nothing to redress the injustice.

Is the ICC even relevant any more? It naturally regulates and supervises all hypercorporations in the known universe, yet it has done very little of late. I would argue that the ICC is a non-issue to Rubi-Ka now. It is far, far away, back on Old Earth, completely oblivious to this planet's needs and the plight of Omni-Tek at the hands of the vicious, blood-thirsty clans.

Though their Peacekeepers remain, they are tremendously ineffective. After suffering a humiliating defeat at the hands of the Sentinels in Tir, they have retreated to safer pastures, content to simply amble about, ever dodging the bullets, fists and bug legs of all factions. They were usurped from Newland, perhaps it is time to remove them entirely from Rubi-Ka.

Silly, Silly, Simon

As mentioned above, since the erruption of the notum wars in November of last year, Simon Silverstone has been remarkably silent. Let us not forget that Mr. Silverstone and his gang of thugs are only able to participate in this grab for land as a result of their stealing, and then producing illegally, the patented personal notum mining technologies from Omni-Tek.

While Mr. Silverstone trumpets the "clan cause", it is readily apparent that his only true concern is wealth. The Sentinels are currently the only supplier of mining technologies to the clans, and see business hourly. They are, I would reason, perhaps wealthier than Sir Galahad at this point in time as a result. Perhaps even wealthier than the amalgum of all clans. All at the expense of their clan brethren, who struggle daily in squallor and filth, their streets uncleaned and their buildings going without maintenance.

Mr. Silverstone has also displayed blatant arrogance towards the clans. Earlier this year, as a bit of a spillover from last year, an overwhelming number of clans sought to have him and his thugs removed from de facto political and military control of Tir. The Sentinels were attacked constantly, culminating in all-out offensives against Simon Silverstone himself. Politically, virtually all of the more notorious clan leaders have spoken out against the Sentinels' occupation. It is quite clear that none among the clans are happy with their reign of terror.

And yet Mr. Silverstone has not even uttered a word of rebuttal, nor any statement to his defense. He has not even commented on the reappearance of Henry Radiman, nor on the opening of the portal to the Shadowlands. Conclusion: Simon Silverstone wants nothing but the credits of the clans. It is grand larceny in its most devious form. If he was concerned with anything else, he would have made those interests known.

Henry Radiman is back again. Or is that back again, again?

After what was perhaps the most foolish decision of his life (the abandonment of Tir by the Council of Truth), Henry Radiman has yet again returned after a prolonged absence. Rumours persisted that he had been murdered; others believed he was taken into sanctuary by Sir Galahad or the ICC, while still others claimed that he had been captured by Omni-Pol. And while Mr. Radiman has returned, yet again, continuously invoking clan propaganda, many questions remain.

The first is whether or not the Council of Truth will actually be reborn. The former Council was always criticized as being far too exclusive, a type of "old boys club" in which only those clans held in favour by the Council's leadership were admitted. Mr. Radiman has uttered statements regarding his intent to revive the Council, but this has been met by clan leaders with both scepticism and su****ion. Naturally, Mr. Radiman's obsession with power prevails, but why would the members of the clans trust a leader who has twice abandoned them? Whose leadership is patchwork and who cannot even reign in the militants?

Regarding the militants, Mr. Radiman has yet to answer year-old charges regarding his co-operation with the terrorist Dust Brigade. As such, OTRK completely refuses to recognize him as little more than an old, tired priest, fallen from a grace he never completely attained. It is unlikely the clans will see him as more so long as his ignorant silence on outstanding matters persists.

Then again, Mr. Radiman has lately tended to preaching about the "reappearance" of David Marlin, whom he believes is some sort of prophet. This fact alone illustrates Mr. Radiman's evident mental distress. David Marlin was a leader of Old Earth, who brought about an end to the reign of the Omega, allowing the Homo Solitus society to once again resume a pattern of normal growth. One fact Mr. Radiman seems to care very little about is that Mr. Marlin was, in fact, the official responsible for the creation of Omni-Tek. It is also interesting to note that Mr. Marlin also mysteriously vanished from his post as leader of a society oh so many years ago. Perhaps Mr. Radiman needs a new "prophet" on whom to base his actions.

Loren Warr Protects Newland; Who will Protect Loren Warr from Newland?

Much controversy has been generated of late regarding the presence of Loren Warr and her Mercenaries in Newland City. Originally hired as a response to Mr. Silverstone's blatant aggression towards neutrals, many neutral leaders appear to have capitulated on their own vengeful ways and are now seeking to remove what has, arguably, been the most effective legion of guards ever to grace the soil of Newland City.

For their part, the Newland City Council has been silent regarding the recent demands made. It is unknown at this point whether or not they are actually taking this meagre uproar as more than unchecked boredom on the part of those speaking out against the Mercenaries. However, it is worthy to note that, for her part, Loren Warr seems unaffected by the cries for her ouster. Ms. Warr is, after all, a neutral mercenary held under contract. Being trained by Omni-Tek has naturally taught her the respect for the law of contract that is noticeably absent in certain societies on Rubi-Ka, and as such her silence is not surprising.

Yet one key fact remains: the Mercenaries were hired in protest to the Sentinels' treatment of neutral citizens. It is apparent that the clans are wholly-incapable of inducing the Sentinels to leave Tir, yet many of these same leaders have instead turned to arguing for the removal of the Mercenaries. The neutrals are naturally handicapping themselves by initiating this action as a result. They will still be slaughtered if they try to enter Tir, yet they would commit these same warmongering, disrespectful individuals to grace the soil of one of their most important cities? Should they prove successful, it will be interesting to see how they react should Mr. Silverstone elect to take a day trip to Newland City.

From Silence to Dust...

One interesting revival that has yet to completely play out is the resumption of attacks by the terrorist Dust Brigade. After close to one year of hiding in the Outzone, they have yet again emerged to carve a path of permanent killings and slaughter across Rubi-Ka. However, whereas during their last campaign they openly targetted notable leaders from all factions, it is interesting to note that they have only targetted neutral leaders in this round of carnage.

It is also interesting to note that they only emerged when the calls to remove Loren Warr and the Mercenaries began. Though little information is available at present as to their intentions, Omni-Pol should be watching the situation intently. Capturing a high-ranking member of the Dust Brigade would undoubtedly prove invaluable in bringing a degree of finality to the terrorists' association with Henry Radiman and the Council of Truth, and may further undermine any future attacks.

Jobe, JAME and other Four Letter Words

Perhaps the most world-shattering development to date has been the opening of the Shadowlands portal by a group of former Omni-Tek scientists calling themselves the "Jobe Association of Metaphysical Exploration". High attop the floating city of Jobe, they had been working on this technology for a great deal of time, finally reaching fruition in September of this year. Since the announcement, Rubi-Kan citizens have flocked to Jobe and the Shadowlands in search of answers to riddles that are only beginning to touch our ears.

For instance, consider the "Xan", the original inhabitants of these Shadowlands. They have undoubtedly been dwelling in the chaos endemic of their surroundings, and suddenly they are faced with an onslaught of Solitus, Opifex, Nanomage and Atrox citizens running rampant. For their part, the "Redeemed" and "Unredeemed", as they call themselves, seem to have aligned themselves with the clans and Omni-Tek, respectively. Similarly, there is Ergo, a massive super-computer which selectively allows access to additional areas of the Shadowlands to those who are willing to dedicate the veritable lifetimes it takes to complete its silly little tasks.

What is known to date is that, somehow, these Shadowlands were once a part of the known universe. However, through what must have been a conflict of meta-epic proportions, the lands were fractured from the "real" and cast into its own domain of spacetime. Thus far, the lands seem endless; citizens of Rubi-Ka are increasingly progressing forward, through legions of monsters and odd metaphysical landscapes which seem intent on snaring any and all who are unlucky enough to grace its surfaces.

Little further information has been presented publicly regarding the origins, intentions and basic characteristics of the Redeemed and Unredeemed, and to date none of their representatives have spoken to Interstellar and Rubi-Kan media. Furthermore, none of the officials on either side of Rubi-Ka have issued statements concerning these individuals. As to the reasoning, Omni-Tek is undoubtedly studying the matter before making comment, and Mr. Radiman is simply being Mr. Radiman.

Will it Ever Change?

Visitors to Rubi-Ka are often quite puzzled when observing life on this planet. On the one hand, we have a centuries-old conflict that rages, terrorists attempting to assassinate citizens, arrogant clan leaders disappearing and reappearing or ignoring calls to disappear, and a portal into an entirely separated dimension in spacetime populated by a civilization that individuals know very little about. On the other hand, we have people attending parties at Reets Retreat, conducting commerce openly, sitting at the various Bronto Burger stands socializing without a care in the world.

To the visitor, this seems completely contradictory. How could a planet in such chaos still act with such complacency, living their lives as normal? The answer I often give is only on Rubi-Ka my friend. Only on Rubi-ka.

Ammicus saves the entry, taking the last sip from what has become a cold cup of coffee. Rising from his seat, he proceeds to the exit to his appartment, and begins banging his head repeatedly on the door, muttering "let me out...let me out..."
