Agree
I agree that it can be very frustrating trying to find the proper QL item you are looking for. I have thought about this alot. I'm thinking that in the year 30000 there would be a better solution. So I came up with one myself.

I am also fairly sure that all the brands represent by products on Rubi-Ka are based here. This is backed up by the descriptions on certain items expressing concern over how they ended up on Rubi-Ka.

I think that each brand should have a master outlet somewhere on RK. A company store if you will. So if one really wants to have a particular QL of a particular item, they can make the trip to the store and purchase that exact item. If an item exists in that QL and is made by that company, it can be purchased there.

Sure, there are some items that don't have a brand attached to them, btu I'm sure something can be done to remedy that.

Another idea I had is one that only really seems to fit in with Omni-Tek. It seems that everything is controlled by the company. There is numerous mention made in trader NC descriptions of the master supply channel. So I think that one should be able to go to the home office of the OT branch that controls the supply channel and for a filing fee and an acquisition, file a requisition form with OT to receive the particular item you are searching for. The item is then beamed into your inventory and the credits are deucted from your account. The filing fee should be a small nominal charge to cover office fees and such. Say 100 credits. The acquisition should be along the lines of 10% of the items retail value.

The last idea might not make clanners or the neutrals too happy, but big business does have it's advantages =)
