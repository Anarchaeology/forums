Guild banks/stores
I don't know about you but my guild is very big into helping each other out with gear. However we find it very difficult to keep track of everything we are holding for each other and many of our bank accounts are quite full. So ... we came up with the following idea.

In guild houses you could place a room off of it that would function as the guild store. You could go into this store and sell that gear you want your guild mates to have access to for guild credits. These guild credits could then be used to buy some gear that your guildmates placed in the store.

Guild leaders should be allowed to award guild members guild credits as rewards and sometimes for pay.

This would give guilds a lot of added functionality and give people a big reason to belong to them and participate actively. This would also free up alot of space for me in my bank account.

What do you all think?
