A note to those who protect the soul of Rubi-Ka
Omni-Tek is the heart and soul of Rubi-Ka. Before Omni-Tek, this planet was a swirling nightmarish hell, unfit for life of any kind. Yet now it has become a place where people can live without artificial assistance, and a place where death is not the end it once was.

Omni-Tek brought all of these things about. The green and pleasant hills you see around Omni-1, Rome and the Clon**** facility are green and pleasant precisely because of Omni-Tek's efforts. The deserts around Tir, Athen and Newlands are - in turn - becoming green and pleasant themselves, and with time all of Rubi-Ka will be a paradise for humankind.

Omni-Tek provides the jobs for the people who live here, and it provides a life that is worth living. Yes, Omni-Tek has a material interest in Rubi-Ka - the notum is too precious to ignore. Yet without that, there would be no Rubi-Ka. It would be another uninhabitable world in a vast sea of them.

Omni-Tek are polluting the natural ecosystem of Rubi-Ka. We are making it fit for human life. Every life-form on Rubi-Ka, from the humble leet to the mighty bronto, is here by the will of Omni-Tek. We have created an ecosystem. And an ecosystem requires everything - the squishy annoying things as well as the cute cuddly ones.

Omni-Tek has created something wonderous - an Eden from a desolate wasteland. And we will and do share that with others. But we will not have the planet we created taken away from us by those too ignorant to understand how this planet needs us.

Rubi-Ka will, one day, grow old enough and mature enough to leave the protection and guidance of its parents. But right now is not the time. It is now the adolescent - wishing independence but unready for the responsibilities that independence brings.

To all Clans, I say only this: remember, Omni-Tek is your parents, and your parents care for you and want you to be safe.

Omni-Tek is your friend.

An interesting, yet flawed, comparison
To compare the Omni-Tek ownership of Rubi-Ka to the colonial wars of Old Earth is a gross simplification. In the case of Old Earth, the colonials you refer to were going into a land already owned by others. Historians agree that what they did was naive and showed no respect for the cultural heritage of the indigenous people.

However, there were many instances on Old Earth where people went to lands where none were before. In many cases, those 'indigenous people' were originally travellers, seeking to settle new, uninhabited lands.

Before Omni-Tek arrived at Rubi-Ka, there was nothing.

Nothing.

A planet *unfit* for life. There are no indigenous people on Rubi-Ka, no indigenous life-forms. Everything you see has been introduced by Omni-Tek - the trees, the grass, the leets and reets, the people, even the air you breathe.

Omni-Tek claims ownership of Rubi-Ka because Omni-Tek *made* Rubi-Ka. If you were to fashion a jug from a lump of clay, is it your jug? To say no belittles the effort you put in to making that jug - the time, the creativity and the artistry.

And maybe I should point out that after the aforementioned mining incident involving the Atrox, not only have Atrox been recognised as people, but Omni-Tek has also recognised the legitimacy of the miners rights on Rubi-Ka in general and made phenomenal steps to improving the rights and quality of living on Rubi-Ka.

Since then, one of many things we have done is to sign the Tir Accord. A document that outlines how the Clans and Omni-Tek can live in peace. But as long as Clan terrorists continue to harras and attack our mines, our troops and our lands we must remain vigilant, we must protect ourselves.

Omni-Tek protects its interests against an increasingly hostile force. But we protect what is rightfully ours - built with our own hands. A farmer would protect his fields from raiders and brigands. What we do is no different.

This violence can be ended so easily. All you need to do is put down your guns.
