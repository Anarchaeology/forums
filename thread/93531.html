((OOC)) How to Link Factions?
((This thread is to remain entirely OOC))

Fixerben presented the idea of the RMC (Rubi-Ka Mining Cooperative). I am already familiar with it and I love the idea, but I have not IC-ly condoned it yet because I can't find a way to rationalize it. I was wondering if others might be able to help me in this.

Being a clanner gives a lot of leeway, because you can follow just about anyone and still be a clanner. Sentinels, COT, CoA, Dust Brigade, heck, they're all clanners by title. So it's easy to justify a Clan org being in the RMC.

Neutrals are sideliners, but it is also VERY easy to promote one's neutrality by supporting the linkage between the clan and omni factions, so it's even easier to see a Neutral org in the RMC.

But omni's stump me. If you are omni, you are listed as "An Employee of Omni-Tek", who's Rubi-Ka CEO and Chairman is Philip Ross. But the RMC denounces Ross as a viable leader of Rubi-Ka. So anyone in the RMC would technically be supporting the downfall of Philip Ross's power. But being an employee of omni-tek means that you are supposedly supporting that power.

I'm sure you see the paradox. But can you see through it? How is this playable on an In-Character level? Help me out here.

((Tarryk: doing this "IC" and "OOC", because it's a bit different from both sides of the RP coin. First, the IC)

I was involved in the initial consultations for the RMC with Nelida, the now-departed Benjacrat, Daimoness, et al., as a representative of the corporation (until business called me away, unfortunately), and one thing you must note is that the opinions expressed regarding Messrs. Ross and Silverstone, although perhaps shared to differing degrees among the RMC members, were those of Fixerben.

The RMC is quite simple - non-aggression among neighbours of legal mining landtitles. A "you don't attack me, I won't attack you" dynamic, with an option, at the discretion of any guild, to provide defensive support to their friends and neighbours.

((And now the OOC:

From an OT perspective, and given that the statements about Ross were, to my knowledge (and correct me if the RMC stance has changed), Fixerben's own, it makes a great deal of sense.

First, think about the situation with the ICC. Any further oppression by OT of the Clans or neutrals will most certainly elicit the ICC wrath and thereby reduce OT's lease. However, if "peace" is achieved under adequate, acceptable conditions, OT thereby secures a strong moral position from which to demand the lease be extended. Remember that OT still retains its exclusive monopoly on the interplanetary sale of notum; it is only required to pay the costs plus a reasonable rate of return on the landholders' investment (even though this doesn't actually happen in-game, it's the only thing that makes sense in terms of what the ICC said about the NW).

Furthermore, it has both economic and, particularly, strategic benefits for the corporation. In the case of the former, the less the number of credits spent on building/rebuilding/rebuilding/rebuilding towers, the more available for OT merchants and vendors, for example; in the case of the latter, fewer rebuilds by the clan landholders means fewer credits going to the Sentinels (who are selling the towers as a result of their theft of the technology via the kidnapping of an OT scientist in 2002).
