Santa Leets: Where and How!
Okay guys, this is a walkthrou for the Santa leet quest!

THE HOWTO:

You need:
1. Golden Sphere or Ember Sphere or Rubi-Ka Pearl (foreman's / BY's)

2. Some candy. (chocolad muffin or cake) (can be bought of different salesmen all over RK)

Just give the candy to the poor leet and he will give you a Pine tree

Then talk to the leet and give him the sphere or the pearl and he will give you a box with Christmas Tree Ornaments

Combine those two items into a shining Christmas Tree

WARNING: The Christmas Tree WILL vanish from the ground after two -2- min.

Anyhow, one sad day your tree will become Dead Christmas Tree, nothing last forever, right? ;p

The Santa Leet can NOT be killed and does not drop anything else. No hat from Santa Leet this year, but there is ARK events happening where they give out hats

THE WHERE:

Boralis: At the fixer shop
Athen: NW corner
Newland: NE part
Wine: Between whompa and the waterfall
Omni Ent: Garden south of the whompa - the one with the spoiled brat npc, se corner behinda tree
Lush Resort: north end center of the square with the grid
Versaille (sp) - the place east of 8x8 in PM, he was inside by a door, not actually inside the castle itself

There is also supposed to be one leet in every sector of SL

The Santa Leet and Engagutten wished everyone Merry Christmas & A Happy New Year
