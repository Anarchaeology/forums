Gold rush
After spending 10 (!) hours tonight flying around every nook and cranny of Rubi-Ka, I have discovered a few things.

1) Theres not nearly enough open land. Basicaly, if your guild hasent set up a control tower by now, forget about it. Theres nothing left.

2) The Land Control Status map is hoplessly out of date. Areas that are supposed to be uncontrolled actually are currently controlled. And yes, I checked the dates that the towers went up. Dozens of them. The map is also quite vauge. Areas that appear to be avalable on the map do not seem to exist.

3) Roughly half of the areas that =could= support a control tower CANT because, and I quote, "The area is not cleared for mining yet". This is incredibly frustrating. I seem to recall reading somewhere that 'some' of the land-control areas had been turned off, but I cannot find that information again. =/

Come on, Funcom. Open up some more land. Not everyone managed to jump on the bandwagon in the first 2 weeks.
