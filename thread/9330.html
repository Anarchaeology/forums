Boring Armor Coats
Am I the only one who is sick of seeing everyone and their uncle wearing the exact same armor coat? When you go in a city, you see the exact same thing everywhere you look, and its a shame because alot of armor looks really nice. A full suit of heated plasteel is very cool =)

I think that somewhere in the future, armor coats need to be changed. At least with tank armor you could see many other peices of armor as well. With the armor coats, all you see is the coat (boring!).

My ideas:
Add specialized armor pieces for the back that give you a healthy boost in one or two AC types (say, double what the current armor coat gives you). This would add strategy and make combat a bit more fun since you'd have to choose what armor you wanna wear for what battle. Things like a Radiation Suit and such.

Also, Tank Armor doesn't differ much from the armor coat, except it has alot of penelties...lets get rid of the penelties, or make them less extreme!

Or, just add armor coats for each type of armor out there.

Or...make armor coats invisible (lowest layer) so that we can see and appreciate the cool armor underneath (higher layer). It may not make any sense to put your armor on OVER your coat, but I'd take anything to add some variety to the player appearance!

(Of course, having the social clothes tab would be great, but I don't think that is coming soon).
