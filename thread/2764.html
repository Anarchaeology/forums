Dogleg, The Search for Luggage
"I is Dogleg, I was told about the nanos by some bloke, he sed that I could get stuff done wiv 'em,
so I learn'ds 'em bit by bit to help me and my friends, I helps you if you need it, I don't really want credits, just cakes are best.
Then someone once pinched my luggage, some bloke sed they woz Omni-tek, so I sez, alright I'll not always help them, but I like them really, and everyone else as well.
My brothers sez I'm a bit slow but I got good idea of whats going on, Dogleg is all about a man of action.
My mate Juzza, he's big, sometimes he's reaalllly loud, sez I'm rare, 'im and Dawson help me when I'm out of cakes, my old boss, Mr.Brassband, sacked me because i made his shop on fire whn i was practising my burn stuff nano.
I use nanos evun though I'm quite big because hitting things hurts my hands, I've got soft hands."
