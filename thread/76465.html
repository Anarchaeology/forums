NW and Scanning suggestion
As many people are now having to grid directly between missions, raids and PvP zones at a moments notice, a method of scanning in the grid is now a necessity.

It is not an option to just zone to Old Athen, Tir or wherever to scan with the zoning/lag issues and people need to get to their bases asap at times.

Make a scanner you can pass through in the grid so people can move quickly about RK without having to risk their xp and loot while helping their Orgs.

Just a thought, thanks
