For those who haven't actually bothered to read the above mentioned news clipping...

It starts with the Headline

"Notum launchers destroyed!"

Then the Tag-Line...

"Independent clansmen admit to destroying Notum launchers near Clon****."

Then it actually proceeds into the article...

"...a handful of brave clan members gathered to eliminate Samson, the overseer of the notum rail launch facility and render the launchers inoperable."

and then...

"[M.O.T.H.E.R - an OT department] proved their true intentions, opening fire on neutral guards, as if this could make up for elimiting Samson and sabotaging the rail launchers."


Now, if you bother to read the entire article, you slowly start to realize that nowhere does it say the launchers were vaporized, blown to bits, or anything to the like. It just reads as some clanners killing the local guards, planting charges to *disable* the launchers temporarily (which IC, a small charge on the rail instead of a cargo container should do). Logically, you would make the leap that OT can and WOULD fix such things rather promptly, leading to the fact that the launchers are still there now and firing (afterall, it is the life-blood of why Omni-Tek is here on Rubi-Ka). The key point of the story was that they took out the guards...

So does this one article where the headline is obviously over-hyped and embellished ("destroyed" instead of "disabled") prove anything? Don't newspapers in the real world try to twist HEADLINES into something catchy? Haven't you ever read an article because you were enticed by the headline only to realize that it wasn't exactly as you had hoped by the headline? Does having a misleading headline that make what was described in the article false?

Why am I even writing this counter-rant? Well, I'm personally sick of seeing this same article headline pranced back and forth as proof of some evil plot of FC. I won't deny that FC has dropped the ball on numerous occasions, but seeing such a weak argument constantly being bumped rubs me the wrong way, especially when there are more important and applicable things to talk about.

I have nothing personal against Cloudeh (he's not the only one touting this), no affiliation with the mentioned clans/factions, nor do I actually really care all too much about the events that happened in the article. But like many RPers, including the official FC and the RNN sites, I've come to acknowledge that the article does not violate any consistency rules of roleplaying.

It takes nothing more than a little critical thinking to make that leap and realize the headline (on a news site) is just that... a headline.
