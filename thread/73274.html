The Life of a Neutral.
A year now has passed since I came to Rubi-Ka. At first I came to the planet not knowing what to expect, which side to "pick". Since then I've grown more and more certain with my decision, not picking at all.

For that is my decision. I remain neutral, I decline the calls to war, the calls to shed blood of another human being simply because he's of the other side. That is what the call is to me.

As neutral I dont see myself in a position where I am shut out from making a difference, should I choose to. But I have no dreams of making a change in the conflict between Clan and Omni. I feel confident in myself and the little space I have made for myself on this planet.

Its others who invade that space who makes my life hard. Clanners and Omnis both calling me fencesitter, calling me names, telling me to choose a side. I chose. Im not indecisive. I do not lack information, I already know enough. I wont choose Clan to have a council rule me. I wont choose Omni to have a boss dictate my actions. My thoughts are my own, my actions are my own.

I have in the past been a part of guilds, neutral ones. I left them. As neutral, alone, guildless, I can be myself. Noone blames me, noone tells me what to do, what to think, who to kill.

I can be friends with whomever I choose, not having to worry about a big brother watching me and noting my actions behind my back. No records are kept saying Im disloyal to my boss or council. No notes state that I might work against the cause.

Other neutrals have found that they shared common beliefs. They formed guilds to work towards common goals. The NLF wants to end notum mining. Elite Operations wants to earn what they can on their prowess in war. Others made their choices too. But they are all different from mine, with only one thing in common. We are neutrals.

The Clan and Omni should not forget that all neutrals are individuals. We all chose neutrality for one reason or the other. You should not call us Clan supporters, you should not call us Omni lackeys, for we are all different. Some neutrals might be undecided about which side to support, others might support either Clan or Omni, and yet others might not want to make a choice or support either side. Some, like me, just wants to experience this uniqe world, not part of a greater cause or war.

When you call someone a fence-sitter, you make them dislike you, for you are ignorant of their reasons for being neutral. If you call me a fence-sitter, I will treat you as an ignorant. Those who call out "You're either with us or against us", yes, Im certainly against You. But I have no gripes with the Clans struggle for freedom. I have no quarrels with Omni-Tek in their mining of notum.

Im not against anyone, until Im pushed. And those who are pushed usually fall on the other side of the fence.

/Jereziah
