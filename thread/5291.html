OK back to reality...

This was "project M" from EQ.

It went like that :

- you spawn randomly in a monster (usualy low level : oh a Leet) ;
- you cannot save and loose control of the M when you try to zone ;
- you cannot talk or emote ;
- you have all the special abilities you should have, spells included ;
- you gain XP you level up (ok no save remember ?) ;
- you die you respawn randomly ;
- you attack another M and you get aggro by surrounding Ms.

Basically it was fun for a few hours ...

Now you can dream of playing a Borg or a Medusa, but it would really mean a new race / profession : where would you shop ? where would you save ? where would you go (you would be huntable in any 75% zone) ?

Mixed feelings - I think it's a nice thing for toying around when you are bored, nothing more.
