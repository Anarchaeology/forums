Ot Attack On Rhinomans Cockpit
Hey there, my felllow mercury-dragons and I were hunting the grounds of Rhinomans cockpit looking for some good trophey animals when all of a sudden an OT employee along with a group of omni gaurds then began to assault THE NEUTRAL GAURDS there in Rhinomans Cockpit. My fellow clansmen and I retaliated and helped to repel the attack. The OT employee either died or retreated we lost track of him in the chaos of the battle. Many neutral gaurds and young clanners were killed in the attack but luckily they had saved their souls before going into battle.
