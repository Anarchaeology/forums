Why Not? I'll Tell You Why Not
I have some idea what it would take for my fixer to survive 60 seconds in MeetMeDere. I know, I have higher level guildmates (go Mockers!) who do it for fun.

I would need to slightly tune one of my attributes more towards armor. I would need to buy a couple of megacredits' worth of armor at way more than twice my level. I would also need to buy an MPS at about three times my level. I would need another half-megacredit set of implants (not counting the quarter-megacredit I'm going to pay some engineer to assemble them) designed specifically to raise my armor attributes, and another half-megacredit set of implants specifically to raise my MG/SMG and Burst skills at the expense of everything else. I would need to tip various professions another couple hundred kilocredits each to buff my attributes so I can equip that armor once I get the armor implants in, bribe some 200th level soldier to give me the top of the line MG/SMG and Burst buffs, and get a largish trader wrangle. And if I managed all of that before the first sets of buffs ran out, I would need to pay another 200 kilocredits to a doctor for a +80 treatment buff, and having done that, if I had done all the math just right, I would be able to install the first set of implants, put my armor on, install the second set of implants, equip my weapon, and finally install my daily-use implants, which would themselves have to be tuned differently (maxing nano resist, for example, at the expense of more useful skills).

And what would this enable me to do more effectively? Survive in PvP. Would it help me in missions? Trivially, by maybe upping my damage output by 25% to maybe 50% over what it is now. It wouldn't help me with incoming damage; monsters and NPCs get much higher minimum damage than players do. That's why, for PvM, the point of diminishing returns is around 20% over your level, not the twice your level I'd need to survive in PvP.

What I have done instead is spend about three quarters of a megacredit to tune a single set of implants so that I can comfortably self-buff into a set of armor about 30% higher than my level, which I'll blitz into when I get the time (right now, I'm about 5% over my level). The same set of implants also lets me equip the best MPS or MCS I can self-equip, which is about 75% over my level. Once in a while, I get a small trader wrangle to my MG/SMG to make up for the fact that MG/SMG requirements rise much faster than Burst requirements, and the fact that they converged near the title level cap. My key implants are at just short of double my level.

And trying to cross the 25% part of MeetMeDere, I am zero for four. Not even trying to get into fights; with my highest run buff on, I can't blitz across a short plaza without getting ganked - rooted and blown off the map, in a total elapsed time of under 20 seconds.

(The last time I /terminated out at 5% health, to deny him the satisfaction.)

When 14.2 comes out, and all you 20th level characters with a full set of QL200 armor and weapons find out that the toys you bought with your millions and millions of twinked-down and/or eBay-bought credits are suddenly worthless ... then maybe I'll be willing to enter a 25% or 0% suppression gas area on foot. Until then, screw you all. What you went through to get that "uber" is not my idea of fun, and I won't do it just so I can pass through your playgrounds on my way to real fun.
