Opinion question about Heal Announcements
I've been announcing heal in the team chat using macros when I heal since pretty much forever (Basically, you'd see "Valen: Healing >>> Bigenforcer <<<" every time I healed in team chat). I do the same for team heals (Healing TEAM). The main reason I do this is so that people can see who I'm focusing on, and when I cast a heal (so if they see Healing pop up, and it's not them, and they are getting hit, they can decide to run, or stim themselves since they will have to wait until I recharge to get another heal off).

Generally, I get one of two reactions from my team (I just always do it by habit anymore). One is I get comments that they really like it, or two they don't say anything at all (probably most common). But last night I got my first complaint (and a rather rude one) from another doc, saying I was spamming team chat, and he didn't see any purpose in it other than to annoy everyone.

I'm a little curious what the general consensus on this is, and any experience other docs might have with it. The very limited number of people I've asked have all said they really like it (except for that doc). But I'm curious if there are more people that think it's useless spamming (because if people don't benefit from it, I can free up 2 hotbar keys that would help me out...I'm not doing it for me...I'm doing it for other people).

There are times I team with the same people a lot (so I know their preferences) and times when I team with many different groups. But when you get into a habit, it can be hard to break, so I'm curious partially so I know whether to change my habit or not. And I'm also just curious...
