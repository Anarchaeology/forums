There's lots of fun you can have as a Melee damage MP. As most of you know I am one so I speak from experience.

You need to invest in a whole bunch of stuff if you're to really get the best out of it though. So on top of the base weapons skills you'll want specials skills (brawl, Dimach, Fast and Sneak are common), parry (for defence/shield) and fast attack (for flurry) as a minimum I'd have thought. Then if you're going to PvP your FA would have to be maxed for the Free Movement stims (as they're the best way you'll have of getting in range). When riposte gets fixed you'll want that too. So all in all it's going to be IP heavy compared to ranged.

It's always seemed to me that the biggest problem with most normal one handed melee weapons is that the minimum damage tends to be quite low on most of them. Take the Blood Mace as an example: 50-520 (150) - Energy AC... or the (hypothetical) Q200 Vento 20-400 (160) - Melee AC... or the Parry Stick 50-350 (350) - Melee AC. Without a high attack rating pushing you regularly into the top end of the damage range and multiplying the damage by a lot - they're not actually massively good damage dealers compared to many of your ranged options.

Add on top of that, your lower attack rating will also make you miss more often and get parried more often and even get riposted more often when it's fixed.... and you see the issues you might have with closing on PvP melee lines. Remember, with the melee player you're only fighting the person right in front of you - and the person right in front of you will very often be another melee player who can parry, riposte etc too...

So all in all you'd want to choose one of the three melee skills that maxes high: 2 Hand Edged, 1 Hand Blunt or MA. If you want to use the creation shields alongside your weapon then you're left with 1 Hand Blunt or possibly an MA based weapon.

Don't get me wrong here - if you're asking is melee damage a real way to go I'd say yes in a shot if you're not going to focus on PvP and don't mind not doing the utter best damage possible. There's a huge amount of fun in melee I find and quite a broad range of toolset alongside it with parry shields, flurries, riposte shields, MA specials, good dualling options, being able to use the shields on occasion etc...

But if you're asking whether the normal high level MP goals of PvP strength and excellent damage with minimal IP investment can be met by Melee... well then I'd probably have to say no. You have to put so much IP into defence on top of the weapons skills themselves for a start. Secondly, you have no real root/snare protection. Thirdly, you'll find your low attack rating will make it hard to hit the other melee specialists you're toe to toe with - and you'll simply not get the damage out of the one handed weapons because your attack rating doesn't give you the multipliers/high end of the range damage that you'd need.

As has been pointed out there are a couple of really excellent 2HE options on the melee side that give really good base damage - but of course then you can't use the creation shield alongside it. 2HE is probably the best option all round for the MP who wants to go melee in PvP I'd say.

Nukes (well, all nanoformulas) take precedence over weapons. If your weapon is half way up the attack bar and you execute a nano, your weapon will stop charging, wait for the nano to execute, and then continue to charge while your nanobots are recharging.

You can always execute a nano. So for someone who chooses to be a melee MP to have fun and mix it up with the mobs and really feel like part of the team, you might want to use your nanos situationally. Picking and choosing where and when to drop a stun, init debuff (to draw agro), shutdown (for the few NPC's that are affected, or nuke (cold or energy depending on the situation).

Yup Bio,

I think there are two choices you can make. You can choose the primary Melee Damage route where you focus on the damage from your melee weapon and use some of the situational nanos in the MP arsenal. Or you can choose chain nuke/pet damage and let the melee weapon damage be secondary. I'd suspect that the intention would be that the MP should (if anything) choose the latter pet/nukes first and just add a little extra damage with the melee weapons.

One other aspect that hasn't been covered here perhaps is the real damage of the two choices. I'll give an overview of my experiences which are based around fist experience, parry stick experience, melee energy crystal testing etc.

In PvM, the pet/nuke MP can be powerful. Slamming away 0.0/1.82 with a mindquake for an everage 850 is pretty good damage all in all. Your min damage is 736 and you can be faster than 1/1 with no lag. Against higher level content you'll need to use the Glacial Lance which has min damage of 602 and averages around 750. You have no nuke-crits really though and no nuke-specials of course.

If you use a fast attacking weapon like a fist, 1HB weapon (including 1HB creation weapon) or 1HE sword, that you can get down to 1.5 second attack speed or less than you can hit with the weapon during your nuke recharge if you time it right as I say pulling 1 hit per nuke out of the cycle. It's a challenge getting that perfect rate all the time but doable particularly if the chat messages are not too lagged (because seeing the chat message for your fist hit helps time the start of the next nuke). More realisically you get 1 weapon hit every 2 nukes. With most one handed melee weapons I've tried I'll get around a 350 average normal hit. It can be a higher or lower but 350 is a good average hit number for average content.

So if I add up my basic melee/nuke damage I get with optimal timing 1100 damage at 1/1 speed. More normally with some slightly lesser timing on the nuke/hit rhythm you'd get 1000 damage at 1/1. Add in some crits on the weapons notably when you flurry and some specials and you're doing pretty damn good consistent damage.

Then add in your pet on top of that with the enmity hitting for around 1K without an evo and for 1500 average with a good evo more like every 3 seconds. When you evo you nuke a little less due to recharge - but in that recharge time you also hit more with your weapon, still overall you lose more damage through not nuking than you gain through hitting a little more for 6 seconds.

All in all you can quite easily get 2.5K every 2 seconds with pet, nuke and melee weapon and specials. So you're really not doing badly at all. When everything runs optimally and you get some nice crits it'll go up to a comfortable 3K every 2 seconds.

On the whole I've calculated conservatively here. Against Vet mobs my enmity regularly hits for 2K every 3 seconds with my QL177 evo. I'll MindQuake more like 850 average. My fist hits are more like 400 average and I'll crit more. Although the parries used to be really bad, they're not so bad any more and against Vets I will rarely miss. Even in high level missions I'll hit with much more regularity, though being in a team with a crat is a great benefit there...

The thing changes quite a lot when you go into PvP though. In PvP your nuke damage is halved firstly which is a big hit on your damage profile. Your pet is unreliable at best. You're far more likely to be hitting at min damage with your weapon. You find difficulty in getting in range and you'll crit more rarely. Your damage just disappears in PvP really if you're a pet/nuke specialist.

If you focus on the weapon damage then things change quite a bit. You only nuke occasionally during the weapon recharge and focus on the weapon damage. So your average weapon hit needs to be much higher. to account for the fact that your nuke rate will suffer. I'd suspect that that's partly why most MPs that do go melee go more to the 2 handed side than the 1 handed side. You'll get a far better average damage per hit.

There's an advantage of course to longer recharges in that it allows you to nuke more reliably, still even the 2HB creation just doesn't hit often enough. You'd want a max of a 5 second recharge after inits and agg/def really otherwise the damage just comes too sparsely to have a real effect, Then of course you miss all of the damage from the specials which you'll absolutely need if you're going to fully offset the lower nuke pace with this style.

Thing with all of this though is the following: you're not going to make the chain nuking/melee choice if maximising damage is what you're after. You can still get a great deal more out of a powerful ranged weapon on the whole. It's a gameplay choice that you're making based around the core consistent damage of the pet/nuke. But the lack of specials on the creations and low damage really mean that you can't begin to get that extra bit of damage that makes all the difference to your overall contribution - and the gameplay options are only middling compared to MA stuff.

I reckon MA is the way to go if you've made the choice for gameplay - and then the creations equip too slowly and the shield stops you hitting with your fists... so the creations aren't an option mostly.

If you're going to focus on weapon/pet damage then you're far better off with a high base damage weapon - so really the 2 handed weapons are your best bet. 2HB is expensive and low maxing, the 2HB creation is just too slow and once again no specials.

In PvP the lack of specials is a killing blow to the creations and for the nuke/pet specialist the pets aren't effective or 'there' enough due to pet control and crowd control issues. The weapons damage on the 1HB choice is too low and you just plain need big weapon damage to offset the losses in pet/nuke.

For the Melee Weapon/Pet specialist the situation in PvP is better. The focus on 2 handed weapons gives them better base damage, the crits help them out and as Bio points out they can use some of the situational MP nanos to good effect. But the general issues with PvP and melee and with pet effectiveness remain of course.

And for all types of MP the issues with melee of low HP, lack of defences against weapons users and heavy IP Investment are true for PvP.

The creations really fall into the "nearly but not quite" category once again when it comes to melee stuff - not a good gameplay choice and not a good damage choice. The existing MP Melee Energy/Piercing Robinson crystals with a creation shield combo would only add heavily to your IP burden because you'd need 1HB, Mult Melee, Melee Energy, Piercing, Melee Init and also the base melee extras of Parry and Fast attack at a minimum and for that you still have a low base damage, it stops you from using MA stuff and no specials. They're pretty hopeless all round.

That would certainly be right for a solo PvM encounter.

It's more complicated with team enounters due to pet targeting issues of course. You also have more of a role in teams with switching, stunning, aggro mgt with taunts etc - so the gameplay specialist will probably focus less on maximising damage. Putting up the demon pet when crowd control is missing is useful too. I'm thinking of killing enmity and recasting demon specifically for Camera rooms so that he can take the brunt of the sentry attacks since the sentries are non-calmable.

Also with MA specials I'll make an assessment of which and whether to use one at the beginning of a battle when soloing. Against a tough Enf or Soldier type I'll quite often pick Dmg/Init debuff and reserve the MA skill for a possible BBCS or FoL. Although I'll often throw in a Snake with fast recharge near the beginning. Also judging whether to reserve an MA special towards the end of a battle because you never know what's coming next can be a tactical thing.

When I solo there's just no chance of either the Enmity or Demon taking aggro off me even when evo'd. I'm fast nuking with MQ (taunt), hitting fast, brawling etc... so for me it's absolutely standard to have the aggro 90% of the time. It's one of the reasons why I max first aid - to get an extra nuke or two at the end of a fight. It's also the reason why I switch to parry stick when I run out of nano: increase defences while adding some extra hit damage and without my nukes interfering the dual damage off stick and fist is pretty good - especially if I have a flurry left to speed up the stick.

As to including stuff in the beginner's guide - it's a little difficult. I try very hard to keep the beginner's guide stuff free of any specific gamestyle bias. It's one of the reasons why the Weapons Guide is how it is. It can be hard to structure information such as we have here in a non-biassed way that doesn't make judgement or decisions for the reader or assume basic things that may or may not be true.

Most of the basic weapons info and how weapons work stuff is in the weapons guide section though - so the info is there for players to use if they want it. I'll take another look at the how weapons work section to see if there's some extra bits and bobs I can include. In the end though, I think I'd prefer a player to ask their own questions in their own threads here to make real and final decisions based on the basic knowledge that is given to them in the guide.

On the whole with all of the guides I prefer to give the reader the info they need to make their own decisions so that the basic mechanics knowledge they gain can be re-used again in the future whatever profession or playstyle they choose. They're guides aimed at enabling rather than at teaching really.
