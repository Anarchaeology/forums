Personal opinion follows. This is not the viewpoint (to my knowledge) of Funcom, just what I think they're thinking. Heh.


*blink* I've always thought the reason the mission terminals were that way was to intentionally cause you to waste as much time as possible. Consider it this way: they only get paid as long as you play. If you level to 200 quickly, you'll get bored and leave. So they want to keep you from levelling to 200 quite so quickly.

How do people get to 200? By doing missions. Doing missions for experience, for gear, for creds. The missions themselves take a goodly amount of time, especially at higher levels. Getting to missions takes additional time. Yalms used to drop a lot more often in chests. Now they don't. Mort used to be 75% and everyone levelled there quickly. Now it's 25% and that's a lot less of a sure place to go for levelling. I bet Home will get changed to something like Mort at some point soon.

I think mission terminals were made to take as much time as possible. I don't know. Call me paranoid if you like, but I tend to think they're just looking out for their bottom line here. Make players take as long as possible to get to 200 as possible without out-and-out slaying them. So it's in their best interests to put obstacles in your way to your levelling. That includes getting rid of stuff that takes a huge chunk out of your downtime, like mission-hunting does.

P.S. Am I the only one who remembers that during the initial talks of OE fixes, they said they'd adjust the mobs' hitpoints and ac at the same time and not fix OE until they could do both at the same time in the same patch?
