Add All Defense is a direct addition to your defensive skill.


Typicaly things that add to defensive skill depend on the situation. If you look at most weapons, or nukes (Nano Resist) they have a stat which determins what skill is used as the defensive skill.

For example we will take the Novaflow, which has a defensive skill based on Dodge-Ranged (An evade)

The shield that you talk about will add an aditional 210 to your defensive rolls.

I dont think (and highly doubt) that it is a direct corelation between the amount of skill (dodge ranged in this case) and the defensive number. but, what the shield does is raise directly the same thing that raising evades does, it just does it across the board, without you seeing a skill increase.

Another good example of this is Fixers Grid Armor, it makes it so that people attacking have a hard time hitting fixers because (at mk4) it adds ~2100 (i might be slightly off here) to all defensive rolls. This works the same way your shield does...


If your still not understanding please further clarify your question.

Edit: It doesn not work the way you thought it did, i.e. removing damage directly. It helps you to take less damage by dodging more attacks, and getting criticaled less.
