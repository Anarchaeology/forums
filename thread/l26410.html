Time's up.
Well, I gave it a try. I bought AO on release day, and here's how things have gone:

Day 1) First-day patching woes. You've all probably heard this story a million times. Connect, patch, drop, connect, patch, drop, connect... connect? Connect? Drop. FTP'ed down the beta 11.1 patch. I get into the game. Play for ten minutes. Drop. Play for ten minutes. Drop. Play for ten minutes. By now, it's 2am, and I need to get some sleep before work. Log out.

Day 2) Official, release patches are available. I uninstall and reinstall AO to make sure there are now no beta elements left in my install. I connect. Connect? Connect? I CONNECT. Play for ten minutes. Zone. Hang. Drop. Connect. Zone. Hang. Drop. Connect. Zone. Hang. Drop. I'm starting to get very tired of the AO login screen.

Day 3) See Day 2.

Day 4) See Day 2 and 3.

Day 5) People on the boards suggest that getting away from the cities might help. Hmm. Now there's a thought. But I'm only level five. I've been unable to stay connected long enough to advance. Won't I be endangering myself? Might I not get stuck somewhere?

Day 6) Make it to Meetmedere. Bind myself to the cell scanner there. Then I look for something to kill. Well, there's a leet. A single, not-worth-the-effort leet. And a bunch of rhinomen, which are so far beyond me that I cannot attack them. Okay, I'll zone back to Newland. Only I keep getting killed along the way. Maybe I can make it to Tir? Nope, I die again. I was just lucky on my first trip. Epidemic Rollerats, scorpion-things, seed pods, and just about anything else that's NOT a rhinoman attacks me. I die easily. They eat the creamy center of my green-around-the-collar metaphysicist.

I log out.

It really doesn't matter whether or not Funcom resolves their game issues now. I'm just weary, I guess. The beta worked really well for me. The release product has been a nightmare. Every time I see the AO login screen, I'm filled with trepidation instead of expectation. I'm burned out. On day six. Why?

I called Electronics Boutique this afternoon. They said taking the game back for a refund is no problem at all. I'd cancel my AO account, but even the "Account..." button on the login panel doesn't work yet. It takes you to a website that endlessly insists you don't exist. I've read Funcom's notice about getting the account page into place "soon," but honestly, isn't that a piece of infrastructure you build BEFORE you take on paying customers? Especially when you have no phone support, and no other visible means of canceling?

I really, really, really wanted to have a fantastic game experience. Or a good one. Or even an okay one. AO is the only sci-fi MMORPG that caught my fancy, and now the fancy has passed; not because I'm bored, but because I'm burned.

I hope the few who stay with Funcom finally receive the product they paid for, and the fun they deserve. In my case, I just can't endure this anymore. The fun's gone.
