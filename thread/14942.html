game mech.: impact of agg/def on AMS / DMS
** disclaimer: all this is what i gathered from various posts, "studies" by some players, common sense, experiements and equation solving . Nothing official from FC, and all this could be very wrong . Also, this may not be exactly the same for professions other than MA.


What i read until now is the following:

the agg/def bar imapcts AMS/DMS, by adding between -100 to +100. Basically, full agg adds 100 to your AMS, and -100 to your DMS. The reverse if full def.

First point:

agg/def impacts your chance to HIT. It has been said that if AMS= opponent's DMS, then your chance to hit is 75%. The exact formula of how it evolves when there is a difference is unknown.
But what is sure is that if you are full agg, your AMS is higher than at full def, and you will hit more often. You will also be hit more often, as your DMS will be lower. On the opposite, at full def, you'll miss quite often.

agg/def also impacts your chance to CRIT: with the formula
crit%=3%+(AMS/DMS-1)/10+Buff,
you understand that 100 change of AMS changes your crit % by 10/DMS (of your opponent). eg if your opponent has a DMS of 500, 100 change in your AMS modifies by 2% you crit%.

agg/def however DOES NOT impact your MA dammage, as proven by experiments. (cf other post on the subject). THis typically may not be true for non-MA.

Second point:

is the impact of agg/def +100/-100? Well you can measure it if you believe the crit% forumula. Basically you go fight leets in the backyard, kill hundreds of them, half in full agg, half in full def. You wee then your crit% in both cases.
You then obtain 2 equations with two unknown variables (the DMS of your oponents and the total span of the agg/def modifier to your AMS). And you can therefore solve the system.

I did it and found that it is more likely that the impact of agg/def on AMS/DMS is lower than thought.
I find a modifier span of 100 (instead of 200) so either -50/+50 or -100/0.

I think AMS is modified by:
- offensive skill used
- level
- items (rings, 250+ tokens boards, some weapons and misc. items)
- nano progs (challenger line for enforcers being the best in this case)

And for DMS:
- defensive skill used
- level
- items (see above)
- nano progs (not sure if there's any)

Modified by:
- level because without changing my evades, mobs who used to hit me often miss a lot more when they turn low green or grey.
- skills for an obvious reason
- items and nano progs because they say so in the description when they have an impact.

Higher AMS means a better chance to hit and crit.
Higher DMS means a better chance to avoid hits, not being crit and resist nano progs.

A simple increase of +20 in AMS from a challenger nano for example makes a major difference in both damage and chance to crit (my enforcer who uses a fast weapon gets great results from this nano line and I often cast it over mongo).
Since I'm using defensive rings and a 400 tokens board I also notice more misses and much less crits from mobs: I did 20 solo missions in a row this weekend and was crited only once (at lvl 118, 50% diff missions) with ok evades too (a little above 500).

I won't go back to my melee rings for sure.

Edit:
About the effect of the AGG/DEF bar:
I'm sure it modifies DMS but not quite sure it changes AMS.
At 50% agg my enforcer hits at the max possible speed with his weapon thanks to a high melee init and don't hit or crit less often than at 100% agg but he surely evades a lot more.
