Hummm... I think the more simple a pvp flag system is the better it will work out and the more plausible it will seem. Two choices, on or off.
Duels and the ranking system is what got the whole nerf and balancing ball rolling and is the one reason why people consider pvp to be broken. I think that pvp in AO originally was meant to be mostly a team affair, just like missions are. Most professions have (or had, at least) a fairly clear cut role in a team. Now everybody wants to be equal to everybody else and anyone should be able to stand toe to toe with any profession, which is kind of stupid if you think about it.
Of course, the reason for all these duels are the rankings, whoever came up with that idea should be ashamed of himself. Either take rankings away or make them attainable even if you are in a team.

AO has a good system with the 25% gas, which should be applied to the whole planet with some areas even at 0%. The 25% guarantees that you won't be constantly killed by players from your own faction, which keeps griefers at bay.
I think it would be a too big task to rebuild the pvp system from scratch so it provides protection from your own side killing you, so 25% is good enough.

So how about it? On...or...off?
