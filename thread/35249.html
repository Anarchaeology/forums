Banded Ring Of Hope Spoiler
Ok I'm about to tell you the specifics of how to get the Banded Ring Of Hope (for those that asked). If you want to find all this out yourself STOP READING NOW!

BTW you WILL be disapointed by this whole thing (if you only want the ring that is)

Ok the 'quests' are actually for parts of Living Cyber Armour. This looks exactly the same as normal Cyber Armour except that you can increase the QL as you level (the same way as the hacker NCU). It wont let you upgrade it beyond your ability to wear it and only goes to QL50 (apparently - although the database shows up tp 200)

Ok it all starts in Newland down at the east end by the Bronto Burger. There you will find a guy call the good time party mixer (mixer from now on). Say Hi or Hello and he will rattle of loads of stuff ending with offering you living cyber armour.

There are only 3 bits available right now (boots, gloves and body) and I've only bee able to get 2 (details in a mo) and I'll start with the one that has the ring so those that only want that dont have to read too much :-)

Gloves
Say gloves to him and he will give you a mission. Its a 'find person' mission and always seems to be in a Rhinoman camp in the Newland desert (I've done this twice and had 2 different camps though, one NE and the other NW). Just go into the mission and find the NPC, once selected they well despawn and will leave you the ring (NOTE: since this is in a mission area the despawn will NOT prevent anyone else from finishing this part!). If you want the gloves beware that finishing the mission starts a 1 hour timer to complete the next part, so I advise you clean out the dungeon first, Omni and Clan can get tokens from this mission.

Once the mission expires (I didnt delete it) you will still have the ring (I left mine in the bank) but you cant put it on for some reason)

Stop here if you only want the ring.

Once you have the ring you have 1 hour to get it to Sarah Winter who hangs around the North edge of the mountains north of Newland lake (the one out the Newland west gate) - just go to the end of the lake, turn north and go to the North edge of the mountain range. Half the reason for the short time is that the Boots should really be done before the gloves. Only because the boots mission desribes how to find Sarah and gives you 5 hours to do it

(NOTE: There are no directions given to Sarah Winter in this mission since it is assumed you have done the Boots mission and already know where she is).

She doesnt talk and you cant right click the item on her. Just trade her the ring and she will give you Living Cyber Armour Gloves

Boots (NOTE: Read all before starting)
As I said before this is actually the first of the 3 missions :-). Just say Boots to the mixer and he will give you a mission and rattle off some text about some one wanted protection money from him. No surprises then that this is a 'kill person' mission. This mission is in Varmit Woods (south east of Newland). Once you kill the NPC you get a crashed implant. You have 5 hours to get this to Sarah Winter (5 hours since your not supposed to know where she is yet - the mission description gives the directions). Again Sarah doesnt talk and you cant right click on her. Trade her the implant and then you are told she will 'upgrade' your boots to living cyber armour. Thats right kids, you need to take some normal (not bau or the Waitt one) cyber armour boots with you. Just trade them with her and she'll give them right back having upgraded them to living.

(NOTE: The directions given in the mission description are WRONG - Newland has no East gate, its the West gate, other than that they are accurate).

Body
Right this one is a real pig. Personally I think it needs changing and have emailed support and posted in the 14.4 board to try and have this changed - read on to find out why.

The overall objective of this mission is to give some carbonrich rock to a guy in Wartorn Valley (the mixer actually mentions this guy in his opening ramble as he thinks this guy sent you). However you cant just use any old carbonrich rock it has to be from this mission.

The first part involves going in to Old Athen (Grid req Comp Lit 130) and doing a mission for a personal ore extractor. This can be a real pig for Omni or Neutral since its a clan city. You can get in and out without dying (may take a few attempts) but it just gets worse.

The second part is in an omni mine in the longest road (the instructions are to 'use' the extractor on the ore while it is still on the floor). Forget the wompa to bliss, on stepping out of the wompa you are immediately attacked by guards unless you are clan. So its a long run through LTR to the mission and back (hardly low level territory) and then off to Wartorn valley with the refined rock to give to the guy in exchange for the body piece.

So given that this armour is only good up to QL50 (~level 50) its going to be real hard for Omnis/Neutrals to complete this bit (and presumably TLR's wildlife will make it interesting for Clanners too)

I'm angry about this quest, and a bit ranty, so bear with me, here.

Oh.. my.. god.

This is the WORST quest design I've ever seen. As a relative noob (no characters over 30), I figured this was one of the only quests I could actually complete. But no, my biggest threat wasn't the monsters, but the quest design itself.

To start off with, I'm Omni, so I can't fully complete it anyway. But that's okay - I figured I'd do the bits that were in Neutral territory, like the Boots.

Now, with that aside....

I've played a number of online games. Ultima Online, Everquest, Asheron's Call, Dark Age of Camelot - and I loved the quests in all of those games. (Granted, UO didn't really have "quests", for the most part. But still.) And while I was never high-level in any of these games, I got a good feel for how their respective quest systems worked.

In Asheron's Call, a number of the quests involved NPCs that weren't in dungeons. It's important to note three very vital things about them:

1. These npcs DID NOT WANDER OFF (with a few exceptions - sometimes they spawn in random locations. But in that case, they always stay put, and there's multiples of them all over the place).
2. These npcs COULD NOT BE KILLED.
3. These npcs WOULD NEVER, EVER DESPAWN.

In Dark Age of Camelot, the npcs would wander around a bit, sometimes a fair distance from where they spawned. While that's annoying, their respawn time was always very, very fast. If someone kills a quest mob before you do, no worries - just wait a few minutes and have a go at him again.

But as for *this* particular quest.....

I've just been running all over Newland looking for Sarah Winter, and she doesn't seem to exist. (By the way, did anyone else notice that the mission directions are just wrong to begin with? Exit Newland City to the *east*?) I've been to the mountain range, and I ran farther east than the crashed shuttlecraft, and the same distance to the west, north, and south of the mountains, in increasing circles.

She's just gone.

Funcom, if you absolutely must have have NPCs outdoors for quests, then *please* nail their feet to the ground. And if they're able to be killed, have them drop no loot and give no XP (so there's no incentive to kill them), and make the spawn timer very short.

I'm under the *impression*, and I could be wrong, that Miss Winters only spawns when a certain part of the quest is completed.

That would be all well and good, were it so - and if this were a *single player game*. Didn't it occur to anyone that multiple people might try this quest at the same time?

I play these games to have fun - hunting for an NPC that might have walked miles away since she spawned, or even worse, gotten herself killed, is *not fun*.

And incidently - according to this write-up, Miss Winters tells you that you need to give her cyber armor boots. Where in the initial quest descriptions is that, I ask you? Pulling such "surprises" on your players isn't fun, either. There's nothing worse than *finally* finding an NPC after hours of searching, only to discover that you lack a component, even though you've followed your given instructions to the letter.

And I'm only writing about the Boots phase - I haven't made a shot at the Gloves yet. But I noticed from the description on this page that the npc will DESPAWN if you click on him, in that phase of the quest. Again, what if more than one person attempts this quest?

Sorry for ranting. I feel a little bit better now.

Ok, I have now completed all 3 stages with my Omni main and I will tell you for free that the last part (body) is a nightmare pretty much regardless of what faction you are. I have updated the original post with more details.

As I said before the first part of the body mission is in Old Athen. Neutrals seem to have a much easier time getting in than Omnis and after several (and I mean several) attempts I found a way in that didnt run into any Guards or cannon. It does however involve swimming and about 4 times on the trot some bloody shark ate me.

Anyway once you have the ore extractor its off to Omni Mine in the longest road. How they expect low level characters be pull this one off is beyond me!!! But you do have a day to do it.

Best advice....forget the whompa to Bliss unless you're Clan. If you're Omni or Neutral grid into Athen (dont know what to do if you cant grid yet - sorry) and run east from the grid point and its possible to run up over the wall using the hill. Turn south and stick to the waters edge like glue until you see the zone border to the south. Turn west and keep going until you see the zone border. Turn south until you find the road, then zone west.

IMPORTANT - Stay on the road.
If you stay in the middle of the road you will be just out of range of all the nasty stuff (and I mean real nasty stuff) youll encounter on either side of the road. Occasionally I 'jinked' to one side or the other when I thought a MOB was a bit close to the road but I only got agrroed twice. To be fair I could have killed both MOBs (only green to me) but since it had taken me this long I didnt want to risk it or waste more time. With my modified run speed of 203 (general runbuff + my Mid) I outran anything that aggroed me (got 1 on the way back). Ask a firendly fixer for a runbuff but the duration needs to be quite long since it is truely 'the longest road'. But a runspeed of less than 200 will probably get you killed.

Soon off to the south you will see a raised hill (zoom in on the main map 'p' and youll see it), dont bother trying to climb it, you cant, but your mission is in the centre. The entrance is further to the West but my advice is to follow the hill round to the south (youll have to back track a bit east before it goes south). It takes longer but youll miss any more nasties (the MOBs around the south side of the road dont aggro (well they didnt agrro me) and you'll miss some clan MOBs by the entrance since your coming from the west now).

There are a couple of Omni alligned guards on the gate, this could be a real problem for Clanners since theres no way to get around them, there are also more guards inside depending on where the mission is.

The mission itself is straightforward. Youll find a BIG crystal in one room, right click on it with the personal ore extractor you got in the last mission to complete. Youll end up with a 'gold bar' which is refined ore and you need to give to a Bjorn in Wartorn Valley.

You have about 5 days to complete this mission, so time shouldnt be an issue. Since you probably havent been able to save since gridding into Athen you could take the easy option and terminate (if you dont mind losing the XP). I'd lost enough trying to get into Athen so I decided to try the trip back. Same as before, work your way back West and South around the hill, then just follow the road when you reach it. Once you zone get off the road to the North and keep going east until you hit the water.

Ok you can hop back over the wall and Grid if you want to save. Otherwise its off to Wartorn Valley.

Wartorn valley is on the North & East side of old Athen (the big city bit that looks like Omni 1). You can go North from the waters edge (where we just left off) but getting over the hill just before the zone border can be tricky. If you dont youll have to go back South just before then zone, run West until you've past Athen, then run North until you've past Athen and then run East (basically we've just gone round Athen in a big clockwise cirlce)

Swim across the river and then zone south once you have passed the cliff, you are now in Wartorn Valley

Mr Mixer hasnt given you very accurate directions (even worse than for Sarah Winter) so you now play 'hunt the NPC' and this is a BIG place. Hes at 755.2,582.7. Once you have found him trade him the bar and he will give you both Male and Female Living Cyber Armour Body sections.

Thats if folks, reverse the instructions to get back to the grid point, pat yourself on the back and wait for the legs, helmet and sleeves to turn up :-)
