Fixing Foreman's lag - new dungeon
I've got an idea to fix Foreman's dungeon from the horrible lag deaths every has in there.

A lot of people go to Foreman's and it has the potential of being a lot of fun, however it isn't because of what the lag of 30 or so people cause in there. Plus the mix of lower level explorers with high level campers.

Can Foreman's get remade into two entire seperate dungeons? It has two themes going on there anyways. Keep one as the Omni-Mining Facility and create another Omni-Med facility somewhere else. Split the bosses and have two in each, take out the quest NPC (the extra lag just isn't needed) or at least put it in the Omni-med facility. If there were two same level dungeons to go into, then maybe people could actually enjoy being there at the appropriate level.

P.S. - changing the way you handle the bosses there too would help a lot.
