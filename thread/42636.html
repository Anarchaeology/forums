Organization for active players!
Dark Fusion, a newly-formed Organization, is now recruiting applicants. Qualifications are as follows:

- Play more than 10 hours per week. This is to ensure that all of our other members can rely on you for groups and events, hunts and missions.

- Be level 30 or higher. Level 30 is not difficult. It merely shows that you have mastered the game to some degree and are now able to play your character as well as we would expect you to. Also, at the moment, the majority of our players are levels 30-60. This ensures that during times when our players are active, you will be able to get a group.

- Be self-sufficient. What we mean by this is that you do not join the organization to merely leech off of the other players. We are here to support you, yes, but not spoon-feed you.

We are a semi-RP organization. What this means is we do not force RP on others, however we acknowledge the fact that the high-end game is either boring repitition or mild story-telling/playing, which we prefer. We do plan on being PvP and RP at higher levels, so this is something to keep in mind. We do not encourage 'u r gh3y d00d'-type players. Proper English (or at least an attempt) is a must

If you are qualified, and are interested, we have around 60 members mostly 30-60, up to 101, and are actively recruiting. Give a /tell to Xuric (myself, General, or as I prefer it 'the Law', responsible for org discipline), Yuric (my alt), Belana (our events coordinator/recruiter), Bigmeatball (our war planner), Nantaku (our treasurer), or Fropower (our illustrious and hair-endowed leader). We look forward to hearing from you!
