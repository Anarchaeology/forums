It was fun for a while
Just cancelled my account for the 2nd time, this time for good. Am waiting for SWG to be released, and will finally get to do something useful with my computer until then.

The world of Rubi-ka could have been a lot better. Ok so it has nice looking landscape and various monsters, but where is the RP part in all this? I never found it, all I found was players doing PvP or missions. Hunting and missions was fun in the beginning, but after a while it got boring, as felt it was never tied up with a good main story to back it up. No urge to continue as I feel I am doing nothing but fighting (the kind of non-interactive fights too).

I really wanted to like this game as it was developed by a Norwegian company (live in Norway myself), but it does not manage to make me feel like I am doing something that contributes to the story at all.

My highest char in those weeks is a lvl 51 fixer. Ok not much, but enough to decide whether I like the game or not. If I had to play to lvl 100 I would throw my comp out the window. I never powerleveled, at least not more than 2-3 lvls top, and when so many peeps do that, it should be pretty obvious how boring this game is in the long run. Why not just buy Tekken or Virtua Fighter if PvP is all you do?

Good luck to Funcom and all of you, hope the story will actually be part of the game again, but I´ve had it with AO anyway, and will not return here, flame me if you want, I really don´t care.

Bye all, thanks to peeps like Toughgal, Mirii, Nausicae, Pimozide, Ratedr, Caberham +++ would love to have you as friends i.r.l. Maybe we will meet in SWG? I hope so. Will be a musician in SWG.

I´m out of here
