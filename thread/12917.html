Save Rubi-Ki 2 Party Mad March Hare Party
Time : 9:00 pm GMT
Location : Reets
Server: RK2 !!!!!!!!
Date Friday 1st March

In the light of my interview with guild leaders on Rubi-Ki 2 and subsequent report on the issues Here.

http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=12814

I have decided to run a Save Rubi-Ki 2 party in order to highlight the planets cause and have fun at the same time. The party is open to all players, GM's and Arks I hope to see most of RK2 there. It is also a good opportunity for players on RK1 to visit RK2, meet the nice people there and show your support for their cause.

So this coming Friday Night the 1st of March 2002 I am hosting RK2 first Mad March Hare party. The party will be in Reets which should allow both Omni / Clan and neutral to attend. The party will of course have the normal drinking, dancing, chatting and special Mad March Fashion and dance show. This is a combined event where players will be judged both on fashion sense (or the lack of) and ability to show your stuff on the dance floor. The event will be in two stages a walkway section and a free for all dance.

We will kick the event off at around 9:00 pm GMT with the first catwalk show starting around 10:00 pm GMT. There will be prizes on offer I hope for the contest ( Looking for some rich RK2 people or guilds to sponsor the event. ) and if my connection holds up I will try to DJ (unless anyone else offers )

Directions to Reet's Retreat
For those of you who do not know how to get to Reet's Retreat, here is the Directions:

Get to Newland by either whompa (clan) or grid (omni) and take the whompa to Borealis. Take the Whompa to West Stret Bank. Go through the archway to your left and make a left as soon as you pass into the corridor. Go straight through two archways and Reet's Retreat is the big building directly in front of you. You will have to wind your way through the maze inside but you will know when you have found the right room as it is the only one with a glass 2nd story dance floor.
