10) Redo our proff. Make it possible to pvp before lvl 150. Make us truly masters of ranged combat. We lack any other options in a fight other then killing our oponent, we should be able to do this better then classes with roots, and calms, and debuffs, and heals, ect. Never have I seen a team saying "team of 5 looking for a soldier", make us usefull to teams. Make it so that it's possible to do more damage then a doctor can heal for in pvp, or an mp for that matter. Make us interesting to play again .
