The taming of the shrew (A Rubi-Ka novel, Part 2)
The small appartment was completely shrouded in darkness. Only the soft azure glow of the comp set on the simple working desk cast eerie shadows that played their slow graceful dance on the walls of the darkened room. Some message silently fluttered past on the holoscreen, making the shadows twitch more rapidly for a few moments.

The figure lying rolled up in a drab army issued khaki blanket on the cot could have been mistaken for being dead, hadn't it been for the slow, rythmic rising and falling of his chest. Suddenly the blanket stirred, a leg twitched. Simons eyes began to move behind closed lids.

Drifting into his dream, he found himself hovering over a green pasture mottled with high trees, their branches slowly moving in the breeze coming from the north. Sunlight was streaming through the foliage, painting a gay scheme of illuminated patterns onto the forest floor. Leaning against one particularly tall tree, Simon spied a slender figure in shorts fatigues and a sweat soaked Tee, panting from exhaustion.
Her long, graceful legs twitching from overexertion, undoubtedly from some morning workout, she was intently staring at something affixed onto her wrist.
Lazily coasting closer, he recognized the young woman as his guildmate Jolynn, scanning her wrist-comm as if reading something on the tiny screen.

The serenity of the morning wood suddenly was pierced by an outcry of anger.

"JUDGEMENT??!!"

Jolynns face was flushed with a deep crimson colour, only part of it being from having run at a harsh pace for several miles. Her slender hands balled into tight fists which she thrust out as if punching someone in the guts.

His senses heightened in his dream, he caught her furious mutterings which she spat out with such vehemence as if trying to rid herself of something unimaginably fould she had tasted as if he was standing right next to her.

...Jesus, you would think the arrogant prick would get a hint, I would rather have dinner with a rapist on prison furlough then him.

As Simon drifted past Jolynn, he noted with mild curiosity the clarity and liveliness of his dream. Drifting up through the canopy into the warm sunlight, he tried to hold on to that dream. In vain... the fleeting images eluded his grasps and after an instant, everything had been washed away by the bright light.

The figure on the cot continued breathing calmly and more regular again, now. A small wicked and content smile was playing around Simons lips. "Gotcha again, Munks!" he whispered in his sleep before turning around and starting to snore softly.


*****

a little later

*****

Dinner alone again, this was getting on her final nerve. Munkie was not one to enjoy the silence or solitude. She was a people person, driven to either make lifelong friends, or entrenched enemies.

At this point it didnt matter, she would spend the evening as she spent the last few since her re-acquaintance with Rubi-Ka. Sitting at her computer, searching for nothing in particular.

The buzzer of the oven startled her; she was intently staring at her monitor, reading Old Earth text about the history of the planet. She completed last the sentence.

and from that point forward hope would not be found.

She forced a bit of air through her nose in a defeated chortle. Figures, friggen hopeless, nothing like prophesizing our future from out past

The door to the oven opened slowly, as she moved at her own languid pace to retrieve tonights nourishment.

Uhmmmm. great, some more half frozen glazed Treanu Sticks and Murzon, my favorite, she mumbled sarcastically to her invisible audience.

Munkie moved back to her computer, setting down the one course pre-prepared meal on a magazine, as to not damage her plastic table. She picked up the Treanu stick and brought it to her nose, sniffing, hoping that it would taste like real food. After realizing that it was still the concentrated vitamin/protein crap she had been eating for the past five days, she surrendered and dipped it in the Murzon, bringing it to her mouth.

In a fit of disgust, she shook her head, picked up the recycled food tray and threw it against the wall. She had all she could stand, of the food, the solitude, and the lack of response from that idiot.

She moved the system cursor with her eye to her mail inbox, still nothing.

That smug bastard, well he can go **** himself, I dont have time for these games.

With that she powered down the only object that she had interacted with over the past five days, and slowly walked to her bedroom.

As Munkie entered the well lit room, she barked, DARK

and the room lights dimmed, as did Munkie for this evening .


******

the next afternoon

******

It was late afternoon when Simon finally woke up, startled and disoriented as if he had had a bad dream. Scanning his sourroundings, he relaxed after he realized that he was indeed only in his appartment and with a sigh, he swung his legs out of the bed and got up.

A sudden jolt of pain raced through his left leg. With much clamor the pile of armor scattered all over the floor.

Cursing under his breath, he ordered the built in domestic comp monitor to switch on the lights.

Scanning down his left leg, he saw that he had cut his large toe on the dented and serrated edges of his battle-worn armour chestpiece. A small puddle of bright red blood swiftly gathered under his foot. Moaning in frustration he hobbled over to his personal sanitation cell in the small room adjacent to his bed/living room, marking his path with small droplets of red blood.

After rinsing his foot with warm water, he examined the deep gash before taking a can of band-aid spray and covering the wound with the gooey substance.
Even with the use of nanobots, even in 29 Millenia of medical history mankind still had to come up with a desinfectant that wouldn't burn like hell, Simon thought, his face contorting into a mask of pain.

After he had finished his other morning ablutions, Simon opened the refrigerator and took out a small metaplast container brandishing a morosely grinning Atrox on its label. After vigourously shaking it and activating the heat inducing nanobots in th process, he tore the small container open and steam started to rise from the cup of processed noodle broth.

With the container in his left, he aimlessly walked to the small desk with his comp unit and holo system and dropped on the rickety metal frame chair in front of it.

Taking a sip from the container, the scalding liquid burned the tip of his tongue. Simon cursed and set the soup down on the desk and activated the comp.

Navigating to his message inbox he found a message from Jolynn. He activated it with a grin of anticipation on his face.



Message to: {alias Simon} (ipnet 12905.12453.23.87.ad)
From: Jolynn Creehan

Simon, while I appreciate your offer, for me to represent your department I respectfully decline.

And as far as dinner with you

 I would like to leave you one thought, even though I am not sure where you will put it. DROP DEAD!

Truly,
Munkie!

*** message end ***


There it was.

She declined his offer to work with him in his department. "Undoubtedly for personal reasons" Simon muttered.

"She has always been one quick to anger and acting on her impulses and intuitions. I believe thats what you become when you spend your childhood in some monastery being trained by some shrivelled old farts with bald heads. Hrrmm... I couldn't care less." he continued speaking to himself, a strange habit he had adopted through too many nights of lonely watches.
He would have welcomed the challenge of having this loud-mouthed rabble-rouser in his department, never having been one to step down from a challenge.

Now as for the second part of the message, Simon was torn between bursting out laughing or having his pangs of loneliness that started becoming ever more common lately get to him.
He had never been one to question his decisions afterwards. His whole life as a soldier had taught him to take problems head-on. If you started thinking things over, you were as good as dead since that second of delay for pulling the trigger of a gun would make the difference between living on to fight another day or ending up as fodder for the vultures out on the field.

"Bah. Women!" Excelling in so many fields, Simon had never gotten around to effectively tackling the mystery that made the opposite gender tick.
He was simply not good at it. Try as he might, he always ended up fumbling an encounter.
Scorning Jolynn and to a small part himself, too, he struck the desk with his flat hand, causing the container of now luke-warm broth to teeter precariously close to the edge of the desk.
"Such a shame that you can't shoot women in order to get to them. I would need to be an adventurer. They have the experience in the field of taming wild creatures."
Staring at the screen, Simon considered replying to Jolynns message in kind, but finally decided against it, a sigh of exasperation escaping his mouth.

He started typing a short reply, his fingers flying over the keyboard.

***

Message to: Jolynn Creehan
From: Lt. Simon Western, Unit Commander

Dear Ms. Creehan,

it is with regret that I have read your declination of the offer to accept a position in the Member Services department of Whisper's Edge.

Your decision is being accepted.

Yours truly,

Lt. Simon Western
Unit Commander


Addendum:
Munks, I take the second part of your message as a refusal to my previous offer regarding a night out in Tir together. Sorry to hear that.

*** message end ***


Simon pushed himself away from the desk, turned around and started to get dressed in light combat fatigues. He slowly walked down the hallway and out through the door, shoulders slouching and deeply lost in thoughts.

On the small desk in his appartment the soup slowly turned cold.
