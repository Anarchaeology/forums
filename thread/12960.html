Should CLAN and OMNI be able to Team?
Currently a Team can consist of players from either side of the present conflict as well as neutrals. From a roleplay/storyline point of view should this currently be allowed.

As the nature of the game changes (Cause there is conflict between CLAN and OMNI now does not mean that will always be the case) shouldn't the ability of CLAN's and OMNI's to team also be similar changed.

When CLAN and OMNI are hostile, they shouldn't be able to team.

When CLAN and OMNI are neutral or even allys, they should be able to team.

Neutrals can of course team with either side.

You could even make it so that in order for CLAN & OMNI to team they require a NEUTRAL in the team.

Anyway just me 2 cents.

Whats everybodies elses opinions?
