Family Names?
Luxurea comes into her apartment and tosses her cloak and hood on the small stereo against the wall, punches in a few buttons on her inventory and makes herself as comfortable as possible on the cold concrete floor.

hmm well now what.. Im not in the mood to mission and Im setting my comm-link to afk .. just a bit of down time is what you need Lux ...your mind is traveling in way to many places lately.

Nodding as she thinks to herself she turns on her stereo and rummages through a small bag taking out a streaming music crystal that was her mothers favorite songs. Sitting back she connects her lap top computer base which she uses primarily as a journal ,to her comm link opens a bottle of water and vents her thoughts into words no one will ever see

Journal entry # 12998 ..subject .. Family (yes once again)

Why did mom give me his last name? Shone?? Is it a common last name, does he have a large family...or is he another one of them men that go around and getting women in the family way and then leaving.. kinda like he did to mom... but why give me his last name then? Unless she knew she was ill and her time with me wasn't going to be a very long one?
Maybe she thought as she became stuck on this planet that I would choose to stay here and maybe search for him? Thinking that maybe if I have his name it would be easy to find.. err I mean he..
Ive seen so many around here with that last name. Its awkward to think I could have family out there somewhere. What is even stranger is that they may see this man as a good person. While I don't know him and really don't care to... well I don't think I do anyway. But really what could I say to a man that coaxed my mother to this planet ,promising her connections in the medical field and to help with her schooling.. when all she got was a nice See ya later when she told him they were expecting, and her passport back to Old Earth had expired.
Yeah .. Id have nothing to say to him. So I guess I shouldn't even think about how I got the name ..or why mom gave me his name insted of her own "Dale" ..its not a bad name.. hmmm well so much for that. I guess I am curious ..though what strikes me more is that of all the Shone's Ive seen.. I don't think any really look like "Family"... either they are fair skinned Solitus, or Optiflex..

takes a sip of her water and chuckles

Guess Id be the black sheep of the family... how would that be for a shock... ehh its just a curiosity anyway...Oh yeah ...I took some time out to let mom know how Im doing carreer wise. I sure hope she knows I think of her all the time.

looks at her comm-link and tilts her head curiously seeing an urgent message ..

Ok ok .. afk is now off so I guess thats it for today journal.. good thing my signal is secured... Id have people thinking Im a lunatic or something..

presses down on the save button without realizing her comm port her old lap top was into was hacked.. giving access to her most recent journal transcript, though also allowing for others to hack into her comm-port and message her as well.. Standing up and putting her cloak and hood back on she leaves her apartment and goes to see what was so important.
