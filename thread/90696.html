R.U.R Delicate Situations- ( From the Hubble Sue's Storyline)
Layla sits at her desk staring at the screen of her laptop. With a slight sigh she turns in the chair and glances at Bot.

" So how should I word this memo? "

Bot continues cleaning for a moment then pause and for a brief moment Layla could have sworn she heard it sigh.

" Well that depends. Yiis is in financial correct? Perhaps just to inform her.
The reason I suggest this is due to the fact that other members in the guild may not approve of "hand outs". Though Yiis being the member in charge of financial responcibilities she knows where the money is most needed."

Bot turns and looks at Layla..." Then again, you can tell then you "got a loan" and you want to invest in the guild, since that is also the truth too."

Layla smiles at Bot and nods to it. " Thank you Bot" she says as she glances up and watched Bot nod back then go back to his cleaning.

Setting up her open gridstream she leaves a memo.

Hello R U R members I have some good news. No unfortunately it does not involve Milly, but it is still good news.

Also you may want to inform anyone working on this Milly case, she may have bonded with the children first. You may be able to find out how she was really working out with them. Seems the parents were far to busy with work to really be the primary caretakers... Oh well can't hurt to try if its handled delicately.
