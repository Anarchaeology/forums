An open letter to the town council of West Athen
It is with disappointment that I have watched the ongoing press coverage of alleged problems with the federated cell scanners and mission terminals in the great city of Old Athen. I am a little surprised to see the Omni-Tek propaganda machine so interested in the state of clan machinery, but would like to thank them for their concern and whilst the Old Athen terminals have always seemed to me to be perfectly adequate to the tasks for which they are intended, it would be remiss of us not to ensure that the facilities for our people are well maintained. To this end, I would like to invite the town officials for my home town of West Athen to contact me, in order that I might provide any assistance, financial or otherwise, to guarantee the good working order and cleanliness of our fair town. Let it never be said, that we of the clans forget our duty of care to those citizens who choose to dwell in the beautiful north.

In peace I remain,

Isobel "Ailish" Nottoris.
