A call for writers, historians and academics of all kind.
THE NEON ZEBRA, the Grid's premiere Social Club, is getting set to open it's latest edition, "The Historical Rubi-Ka Athenaeum and Research Center". The "Center" will open with a large collection of collected works and documents from all over the world of Rubi-Ka that will be easily accessable from their original authors through our multiple Terminals. From current affairs and historical events, to personal essays to fiction and biographies, the Center will hopefully become the leading provider in tools for research and historical studies on Rubi-Ka.

In addition to collecting and cataloging the multitude of existing material, the Center is also looking to build its own library of unique works. With this in mind The Neon Zebra is putting out a call to the writers and academics of Rubi-Ka.

We are looking for three to four individuals dedicated to recording the living memory of this world we all occupy. Each of these authors, historians or novelists will have a unique place in the library, spotlighted in customized environments for visitors to enjoy. They will also have complete editorial freedom, free from censorship of any kind (in keeping with current accepted social laws). We would very much like to have a nice, diverse group to begin with, although we will not be turning anyone away too soon. Rather you right historical documentation or dramatic or humorous fiction, the call is for you.

In time, as both the Center and its resources grow, we will invite more writers to join with all the same privileges of the firsts. Please note, although we are not planning at this time to collect or display any of the fine artwork being produced on Rubi-Ka, please do not hesitate to submit your work if you would be interested (many exceptions or accommodations can be made).

Thank you for your time and for all the support that has already been shown to The Neon Zebra.

Please contact Tuxx or Domu through the Universal Commlinks or by grid address if you are interested.

tuxx@neon-zebra.net
domu@neon-zebra.net

((ooc: Well, there you have it. We want writers; whether its fiction or world events. Each writer will have his or her own uniquely designed page with customized graphics. To ease the update procedure we are using Blogger for the update software and each writer will have individual accounts that only the administrator and themselves will have access to. Each will be costumed designed and have a unique appearance, for a sample of a page click here. Writers are more than welcomed to supply images and ideas for the layout. This will be a space for you to let your imagination run wild and have fun.))

*Due to the agreements with my domain host and the need to protect the site from any damage, I will have access to each blog (basically to keep out any pornographic or racist material from the site). At no time will anyone at The Neon Zebra nor I ever edit or change in anyway a story, essay or report by any author, instead any author that posts something that goes against my hosts limitations will be notified that the page has been temporally taken offline, the reason why and we will then, on an individual basis, discuss what can be done.*
