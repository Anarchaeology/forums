There's been a lot of talk of this. Basically, if you fire off Aimed Shot while not concealed, you get Flingshot'-like results. You still lock the AS timer, however so it's not like getting a second Flingshot on your rifle for 'free'.

Agents feel hampered at times in missions where setting up your ambush position and firing off a sniper's shot into your target's head just doesn't mesh with the idea of racing from room to room bashing monsters dead in 10 seconds.

With this change as discussed on several forums, the Agent could just let loose whenever the AS icon lights up with the proviso that he can't expect to get the 10K shot in such circumstance.
