Anyone Remember this?
I am bored at work and reading the fourms, and started thinking of how AO was back in aug 2001, when I 1st started playing.

The 1 thing that poped up was when I 1st meet 2 fixers, Jeik and Scyles. I was around lvl 25 agent. Well to get to the point I was following them running for dear life from a hammer beast (my 1st time seeing it) that they agroed. Well they both had thier speed buffs running. On my screen they both were disapearing and reapearing as if they were teleporting from spot to spot. Before they told me about the run buff I was amazed at how they could do that, and was still amazed at funcoms imagenation for making that happened. I told them what I saw, to find out if you didn't have the buff runing you would see it, then once you was buffed they would stay on your screen as you followed. Which I thought was really cool because if you read the description of the nano it states that the buff will transport the person in and out of the grid/ time&space to make them run faster (I know its not the exact description, but you get my point)

Well many months has passed (almost a year) and I haven't seen this since. Did a patch remove this cool feature? Or was I such a newb that it was just server lag (same as when in plane and doing /follow) and I didn't know better?

If anyone else remembers this also plz post so I don't feel alone, and/or if you had something like that happen and noticed it missing plz post it here.
