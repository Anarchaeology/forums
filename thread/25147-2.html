I had a Cabalist in DAoC and thought it was a wonderfully clever way of implementing a healing class. I thought the Trader would act similarly. He'd sacrifice his life to save yours. Often in DAoC, I would act as a 'life battery' keeping the team healed up and stepping back as I was about to die and waiting for a Cleric to fill my red bar. The Cabalist heals were very good in terms of being able to pull them off with the tradeoff being that you hurt yourself.

They key thing about being a 'life battery' is that your Agro for healing can be set very very low by the game designers since you are doing an equal amount of harm to yourself. It's pretty close to a Null Sum Trade (life for life). The Healer works on the Battery and doesn't get as much agro either as he's healing a target that is low on the MOB's agro list. At least for my close-knit group, it was a great way to mix things up a bit. The Cabalist and Cleric in my group were good friends and didn't whine about each others abilities.

I think this all got broken when the PvP damage multipliers went into effect. I have to admit I was surprised to nuke myself as an MP in an effort to get a root off and find out I did hardly any damage. Then I pulled out my little Trader and noticed that something was wrong and the damage listed on the Nano for my single target heals wasn't happening to me.

The Nerf they propose removes the ability to act as a 'life battery'. Besides the fact that whining has caused harm to a profession, I don't think that removing the ability to play in this role is good for the game as a whole.

FunCom. Fix the bug first. Then reassess. Try to pretend you are a professional organization for once and not some high-school science club.
