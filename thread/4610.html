Request for an inquiry into the apartment bombing in Omni-E
Red Tape would like to issue a request to the other loyal Omni-Tek guilds out there to pool our resources and conduct an investigation into the cause for the recent explosion in Omni Entertainment that resulted in the destruction of an apartment complex.

It is imperative that we find those responsible for those actions and bring them to justice. With the tensions between Omni-Tek and the Clans running high this event could threaten to shatter the amnesty or offer excuses for the hostilities to begin anew.

Before the finger pointing can begin, I ask that everyone do not jump to conclusions. This could very well be the work of the rumored "Shadows of Rubi-Ka" that is playing both Omni and Clans against each other.

Red Tape and its members will do all we can to assist those that have been victims of this unfortunate incident. We can help you file your Reclaim Certificates to retrive your insured belongings more quickily and we will waive your processing fee. For those that have uninsured items, we can offer assistance in filling out form OT-R02251 at a reduced processing fee.
