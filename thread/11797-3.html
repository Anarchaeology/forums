Bows in the new patch
I am probably one of the few people that has actually gotten to a semi-respectable level while using only a bow as a main attack. I've been more or less satisfied with it, and while im not the most powerful agent in the world (Yes agent, I'm not a martial artist) I can outshoot all but two of the rifles.

Few things
Arrow cost should be left at 400 credits per 20, This is not that much of an amount (I spend more on Nanokits with my NT) and since you don't go through arrows extremely fast it makes up for it. Also this keeps the bows in the rich hunter catagory instead of little younglins running around with no idea what they are doing.

Another, people are posting information and speculation about armor..What the hell!? This is 20,000 years into the future, you don't think we can invent something that can STOP an arrow? Hell we were able to do that with chain mail and shields, why would we not be able to do it now?

Bow special attack: From the data I've seen it looks like I'm going to be putting some points into it eventually. Though I am a little disapointed with the delays (4 minutes just for 900 damage?). Since armor is going to be a big factor in reducing the damage. And it sounds like there are no crits...which basicly means they are an extra flingshot in PvP, ONE extra. Hopefully the damage goes up a reletive amount past QL 120, while I don't expect FA or burst damage at least normal weapon crit damage would be nice if I have to invest in a totally new skill.

People use bows because they want to be 'cool', running around a world full of high-powered technology and using an old weapon to kill people/things. Like all weapons the majority of people talking about them have no idea what kind of bows are useful in this game anymore then they have an idea of what pistols/shotguns/rifles/assualt rifles are any good without copying other people. The poor little MA's that are desperatly trying to seperate themselves from the sterotype should do a little research into what bows are usable instead of posting ignorant things like "My Venom Tech bow is cool , but the IP cost is killing me!".

Which brings me to another issue, people complaining about how IP is worthless once you reach level 150. There are only about 300 characters that are ABOVE level 150. Many of these characters are doubles or triples for those people who know how and are able to level. Most people peter out around level 110-120 because of the tediousness of gaining levels in this area and the halt of progression in a character since few if any nanos are left to obtain.
