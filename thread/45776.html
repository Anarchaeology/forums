Vengance Theory Accepting New Members
Vengance Theory, Clan Guild found on Rubi-Ka 2, is curently accepting new members to the guild.

Professions that are most needed are Enginers, Traders, Adventurers, Enforcers and Doctors.

Higher levelers are prefered (lvl 50+), but any level will be accepted as long as new applicants meet the guild requirements which are posted here: http://gweezlebur.com/~gshippey/vt/join.html

New applicants to the guild will remain at "Applicant" status for a period of 2 weeks after gaining entrance. After this probationary period, the new applicants will be reviewed in a Q&A session regarding guild knowledge and other matters. At that point the question will be asked if the new applicant wishes to remain in, or, leave the guild.

Access to guild funds and inventory is limited until applicants receive full membership into the guild.

If you are interested in joining this organization please contact one of the following members:

Nasar (Guild President)
Knightofni (Guild Advisor)
Lordedd (Guild Advisor)
Ttademon (Guild Advisor)
Lasana5 (Guild Advisor)
Kallipar (Guild Advisor)

Clanners posting a reply to this thread requesting entrance to vT will not be accepted. This thread is only for public awareness.New applicants must contact one of the guild representatives.

Thank you for looking this information over and we hope to see you as an honored member of Vengance Theory.
