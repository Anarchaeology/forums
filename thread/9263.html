New RolePlaying Guild - RK1
Here we are, a new European Clan Guild on RK1 named Psychedelic Freedom Fighters (abreviation : PFF). Why Europeean ? just because of GMT reasons. (Our zone is from GMT-2h to GMT+2h).

Our goal is to promote Role-Playing inside Rubi-Ka. Way too many players are focused on looting and xp-ing, and this is why we'd like to organize a lot of RP-events to try to show to others who don't know it yet the RP dimension of the game...

This guild is also here to promote players initiative, and we'll help our members to fulfill their desires (organizing a party, any kind of event, find a special item....).

We like to dance, to party, to have fun, but we like to fight as well...As much PvP events as possible will be organized in time. We don't want to be a big guild, but a happy group of people who know each other, share ideals , friendship, and time together.......to get rid of the omni scum!!!!!!

And of course, professions will really have an interdependant activity. For exemple, nanotech will make any implant to other members, meanwhile trader/doctor will earn XP while making PlasmaBlood with the monster-parts given by a soldier or an enforcer. Well, i can't explain all inetrdependancies here, but we really wanna play on this...


Our rules of conduct are quite simple...It could be put down to 1 main principles: ROLEPLAY!

-But i think a few more must be implemented for things to work right No real-life talk on the guild channel
-attend the events(unless youu have a VERY GOOD reason...not "i was hunting too far")
-help fellow members (You never sell something to another member)
-A PFF doesn't live for money nor power....he lives for his own ideals, he respects natures, and he tries to understand it better and get into its spirit through trance&dance... A pff member love travel and is goal is not "have a yahlm. and be lvl169"

But a PFF will also do all his possible to prevent the enemy
from destroying the planet and its people. Because freedom is the opposite of OmniTek, we will fight them the best we can.

Our only requirement before agreeing to your application,
(since we are a roleplaying clan before all)is that you submit us the background history of your character (his/her family, friends, the reasons he's against omni-tek, his beliefs, hopes...let your imagination work!! a litlle story, just to know who yuo are )

So, once you're ready, send all this to :
