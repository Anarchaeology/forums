The Order of Chaos Wants You, Join Today! (RK1)
The Order of Chaos wants you to join today!

Are you looking for a guild of people to team and have fun with?

Are you new to the game and need help from some new friends?

Are you tired of guilds who are arogant?

Are you tired of guilds who only accept high-level people?

Are you a high-level player who is looking for a change?

Are you tired of guilds that promise cooperation and don't deliver?

If you answered yes to any of the above questions, then The Order of Chaos wants you to join!

The OrderOfChaos is an Omni-Tek guild located on RK-1 and is lead by LostLogic. Considering the guild was formed in the first couple of weeks of AO, it has many diverse members of all classes and levels. Currently over 200 members (I just cleaned house) exist in the guild averaging from level 77 up to level 187. Everyone is welcome regardless of experience (some additional benefits are available for high-level players.)

Of course, there are some requirements/rules:

Requirements
1. You must be a team-player.

2. Members of OOC do not sell items to one-another. Everything is free to guildmates. (Unless for fund-raising or in times of real financial need)

3. Members of OOC do not charge guildmates for services.

4. You are expected to help guildmates run missions/acquire items/and team in hunting parties. (within your abilities of course)

5. No grief players or guild hoppers. If you have many OT enemies in the game, then we do not want you. Only nice people need apply.

6. No instigators. If you argue with people on a regular basis, don't bother applying.

7. No moochers. Don't come to OOC looking for a free-ride. You are expected to give as well as receive.

8. You must be level 50 or higher. This is to make sure you are a dedicated player and not joining for a free-ride.

Mission Statement
The Order of Chaos is devoted to ensuring the profitability of Omni-Tek. The leadership of OOC does everything in its power to provide security and sources of revenue for OT. Any threat to OT profits is dealt with swiftly and completely.

Recruitment Process
If you feel you are OOC material, please visit the website at http://www.lostlogic.com/ooc_join.shtml or /tell LostLogic in-game. Once you are accepted, you will be added to the guild as an Applicant. Your 3-week trial begins at that time. If you prove yourself OOC material throughout the 3-week trial, you are granted full membership.

Roleplaying
The Order of Chaos has been around a while; therefore, it has been involved in many of the in-game events such as: The Galway Pact, FunCom Sponsored Guild Leadership Meetings, The Amnesty Protest March, and The Black Alliance. If you want to join a guild that has an active role in the game, then OOC is for you.

Contact Information
You can email the guild leader at webmaster@lostlogic.com.
You can also visit the guild website at www.lostlogic.com/ooc.shtml
Or give the leader a tell by "/tell LostLogic"

Leader Profile
LostLogic is a nanomage nano-technician at level 144 as of 05-06-2002. LostLogic has operated guilds in other MMORPGs such as Merdian59, Ultima Online, Everquest, and Asherons Call. He has also published a book on multiplayer game development.
