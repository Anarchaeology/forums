Well now what, found GA 1
Lol...just when you think you have your toon perfectly set up......
I have ranged and cls evades maxed, I have all my base stats maxed other than Pyc, and Int, and Nano pool. SMG/burst maxed, great comp lit, plenty of NCU, Very nice HoTs, decent roots/snares, in short, I really did a nice job on this toon and I'm very happy with the way it handles..........

Now of course, would be the perfect time to find that drop you never thought you were going to worry about (Mat Crea is 51 right now lol). The ONE stat I have ignored above all else is Mat Crea. My fixer is 56 now, with 51ish *Untrained* Mat Crea. The thought of raising this skill ....well...all I can really see it doing is destroying what is now a great template, and one that is working very well.

Now to throw another monkey wrench into the works...had someone offer me 30m for it....it's GA *1* for cripes sakes. 30m buys a lot of weapons, for a lot of twinks...lol, not to mention my fleet of yalmahas for every alt.

I've always sort of planned with this toon, to not really even worry about GA till post 150, when IP get's a bit better to deal with. I know the levels from 50-100 can put a real squeeze on your template, and the last thing I want to do is gimp everything else, just to get into GA.

So there's my dilema....30m creds, or the destruction of my template to get into level 1 GA...... Hard for me to justify reworking my template for this, especially with that many creds on the line. I do have all my IP resets (5 now), so I could actually try it and see what gives, and make the change back...but I am really hesitant to use those IP resets, I would just love to get post 150 and have them all unused...who knows what FC will throw at this game.

So what would you do? It's a real dilema...GA is sexy, there is no doubt about it, it would be fun to have, but is it worth it, especially GA 1?
