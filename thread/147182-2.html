Speaking as a soldier... this is what always gets me killed VS MA's


1) I have died in a MA alpha before i even got chance to hit TMS.. most soldiers are forced to start the fight with TMS.

2) TMSX lasts 1 min 20 secs, using fists move out of range but keep aggro on them so they cant sit and use a heal kit should you of damaged them any before they used TMS.

3) The key is to NOT attack through their TMS, MA damage is good and fast.. but its not enough and too fast to risk getting 170+ reflect back per hit for 1 min 20 seconds while not moving the soldiers hp. Sit on defence and heal up their damage with heals + hp stims.. will probably need to use FoL towards the end of their TMS to push your hp back up into the "safe zone".

4) TMS is down / about to run out, swap to Bow ready for when it drops, soon as you time it as being down, AS - de equip bow, brawl etc etc... soon as tms drops, a sol is easy to pwn.


Only time i see a MA die is when they attack through TMS... they literally nearly kill themselves heh
