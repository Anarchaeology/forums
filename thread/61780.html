Mharc
Supa Leet
The fact that the DB goes to 200 doesn't say much. Many types of armor have a certain range up to, or starting from a certain level. The two sets of values corresponding with the QL1 and QL200 versions in the DB are used to interpolate the values for all QLs in between.

Also, many types of armor have different versions. For example, Decus armor under 100 is called Senpai Decus (primus, secundus or tertius), while above 100, it is called Hanshi Decus.

The reason Kenlon is blinking, btw (), is because Low-tech armor isn't very good, really. If you're going for mid-higher level armor, a much better AGI/STR armor would be, for instance, Flowers tech. That starts at QL75 and goes all the way up to 200.

Hope this helps some.
