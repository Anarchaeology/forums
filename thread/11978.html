Lowish level Soloing spots
As a lowbie Crat (22) I've found that soloing with my redish pet seems to be the fastest way to tack on levels quickly using the /pet hunt command and occasional breaks. Of course I'm refering to green-to-yellow outdoor mobs.

At 14 the road trash mobs just east of the shops West of Tir (near the flea pits) were wonderful, especially with the like-mob group agro.

At 19ish I found a beach to the south of Athen with Sand Fleas, Malles, Bulls and Rollerrats that was really nice (despite quite a few greys there as well). To get to this you simply follow the road west out of Athen and continue on it until it heads south through a zone. After the zone, follow the road south until it bends West, and when you reach the bend, run East off the road and you'll come across the beach I'm refering to.

I know my geographical knowledge is somewhat weak, so this may all be old news, but then again it may be helpful to some looking to rush these annoying early levels. I also realize that not every class is adept at soloing, so perhaps this is only helpful to the pet/crowd control classes.

If anyone else has any to contribute it would be greatly appreciated, because it's definitely nice to have a spot with unlimited mobs rather than the limited ammount found in missions.

Mid-teens to early twenties can't beat the areas around the Northwestern Outpost in Tir. I spent two whole days with practically no competition beating up the Biofreaks and other goons wandering around there. Also, if you zone northwest of the Outpost (as opposed to due north, which takes you to the Outcrops and the Temple) and run northwest through the hills, the forest around Bronto Crater is a really nice spot too. (That area might be too much for teens, though; I went there in the twenties. Still, there are no aggros if you stay away from the dunes--red spidies--and the OT camp.)
