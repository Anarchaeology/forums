Alvin/Dodga Bots
First off, well done FC for removing the stupid de-spawn on Dodga and reducing the spawn time to 15 minutes on the other robots.

The only problem now is that these bots are going down so fast with all the people camping them that people cant identify and tag the bots in time, therefore having to wait in some cases for hours before they can move on.

To solve this problem I propose a s simple change. Make it that for 30 seconds (or more) after a bot spawns, no one can attack it. This should be more than enough time for people to identify and tag then move on.

Please look into this simple but very much needed change. The way things are noe many many people are getting upset.
