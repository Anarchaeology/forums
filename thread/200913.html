Apocalypse RK: Within Stars
((Hello, Usually I just sit back and read the steady influx of messages, but I've been convinced to give posting one of my "hobby" stories here. I usually just post on the Nanomage Liberation Front's story forum. This is taking a huge leap for me so please enjoy and let me know what you think.))

Disclaimer: I took a large amount of creative liberty with this story. Events that take place within are not possible in game.

Within the Known galaxy there are legends and legends of legends. Some are true some are not. Some live, some died long ago. Two legends earned their fame by similar chance. Two legends are warriors. They have no equal. . .accept each other.

Those legends will come face to face and decide the fate of an entire world. . .

Cyrus Long, sat in a chair that was positioned at the head of a large Oak table. 14 more small but no less ornate chairs lined either side of the table. The Table sat just forward of center in a room that was of similar shape and gave plenty of room for movement around the table and allowed for a quite grand appearance. The ceiling of the room was several stories up adding to the illusion that the room was bigger than it actually was. Mirrors lined the long walls which also gave the room a deceiving grandeur, while two large paintings covered the walls at the tables ends. It was quite clear this room was designed to give the impression that whoever sat at the head of the table had power. Cyrus Long, liked that feeling. He liked the look in the eyes of men and women who stepped into the room. The look of self-doubt. To Cyrus that was the beginning of victory. Once he saw that look he knew he would get what he wanted, whatever he wanted. Despite that most welcome satisfaction he used this room for very few occasions. Very special occasions when breaking the will of a man who has a backbone is not done easily, or when he wants to make sure that his victim is completely crushed under the illusion of absolute dominance. Cyrus enjoyed the game of causing what he considered the ultimate pain. The game of breaking a man's spirit. And he did it sometimes just for the pleasure. Most of his victories were over suits. Men in slacks and ties who carried briefcases and a lawyer or seven in their pocket. Those suits who thought of themselves as great sharks of business and politics. Cyrus was a predator of these sharks. To him they were just prey.

"Mr., Long" A voice box in front of Cyrus chirped up.

Cyrus pressed a button inlayed on the surface of the table. "Yes?" He asked commandingly. Cyrus never had a very powerful or booming voice and it seemed he should be wearing glasses and crunching numbers in accounting by sound alone. What he did have was a supreme and overflowing aura and tone of confidence, which he knew well and used as a tool.

"Your guest is here." The box replied.

"Finally." Cyrus sad with a grin then pressed the button. "Show them in."

Cyrus leaned back in his chair and straightened his suit, then clasped his hand on the table. Now the Game begins.

The ridiculously large doors at the end of the room that climbed the full length of the walls to the ceiling opened slowly. This was an effect Cyrus loved. Hit the challenge with awe from the very start, make them feel weak before they ever set foot inside. A figure with a wide brim old looking black hat and a long black leather coat that ran the full length of his body down to his black boots stood at the entrance. His head turned from side to side scanning the room. Cyrus could not see his face. That was a disappointment, he liked to watch them crumble from start to finish.

"Come in, Sit down." Cyrus invited slyly.

The Man strode forward slowly but confidently his boots echoing his authority in the great room. Cyrus blinked but did not change his expression. Though this lack of outward insecurity was uncommon it was not unheard of. He would have to just double his efforts. The man stopped several feet from Cyrus and stood even at that distance towering over him.

"Please, sit down." Cyrus gestured to a chair invitingly.

"I won't be staying long." The man spoke with a subtle light but stern voice.

Cyrus paused and leaned forward. No doubt, there was not a single ounce of fear, awe or doubt in the man's voice. It was solid as stone. This was perhaps a greater challenge than he had expected. "I'll get to the point then." Cyrus pressed another inlayed button on the table and a monitor rose up in front of the seating position where the man stood. The monitor Flickered on and presented a picture. The man's head turned and looked down at the monitor.

"That is one of the reasons I have asked you here. It is becoming a problem." Cyrus explained.

"I don't care about your problems. I'm not a Mercenary." The man said calmly.

"The other reason is the box. We feel that it may be of some value to us. We have made a handsome offer." Cyrus was trying to control the conversation. This game was not over yet.

"The box is not mine to give. Even if it were I would not give it to you."

The calm nature of the dark mans response irritated Cyrus. "I requested to speak with the Demon Slayer, the guardian of the box which swallows souls of those poisoned by darkness. If you are not he then I have no business with you. I'll speak with your master underling. Go fetch-"

The loud strike of the mans boot on the stone floor as he stepped forwards shocked and silenced Cyrus. The towering man seemed to grow somehow expanding his very presence. "Do not presume your position Mr. Long." The man's voice now deep echoed in the room commanding absolute attention. "I am the Guardian of the Demon Slayer. I am the Harvester of the darkness." Slowly and steadily Harvester's presence returned to normal as Cyrus struggled to regain his composure after realizing he had sank nearly to the floor from his chair. "You will talk with me." He said simply and calmly, his voice no longer booming but soft.

Cyrus straightened his tie and cleared his throat. "I have n-n-no problem w-w-w-with that." Cyrus stammered out the words. 'What just happened? What in lords name was that? ' Cyrus thought trying to steady his hands. "I didn't know you were the Harvester, I thought-"

"It doesn't matter, continue with your presentation." Harvester gestured to the monitor.

"Of course." Cyrus nodded and pressed his button again changing the images on the monitor as he spoke. "We would be willing to double the price for the box, but we can discuss that later. The thing on the monitor is what we will focus on for now." Cyrus struggled to gain control again. Control of his own doubt. "The creature is similar to the homonano we have here on Rubi-Ka. Built on the same genetic concept but altered to allow for a sturdier frame and greater energy intake as well as increased speed. It was supposed to have been destroyed. The project we thought was a failure. But due to a rather unfortunate chain of events the mutation still lives."

"This has nothing to do with me or the Demon Hunter." Harvester stated sternly

"But it does. This creature has slaughtered innocent men women and children. Murdered them in cold blood in fits of blind rage. This beast carries a darkness that could very well rival any demon."

"Never the less it is not a demon. Regular men, given enough of them can deal with this."

"We've tried. Omni-Tec has spent millions on bounty hunters and mercs to bring this thing to its just end. Other corporations have also aided us in our attempts. One corporation hired the hunter Wallace to find and destroy it. As everyone knows he was one of the best, but now he's dead! That monster is a demon." Cyrus leaned forward pointing at the monitor.

Harvester took a few moments to study the image on the screen. Then turned his attention to Cyrus. "We will discuss this further." Harvester then turned and walked a steady pace to the large doors and out of the room leaving Cyrus Long staring as he left.

Alone Cyrus placed his head in his hands and began to cry. The game was over and he and been more than crushed. His spirit felt like it had been ripped to shreds.
