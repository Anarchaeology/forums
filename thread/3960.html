Summoned weapon gone - In the middle of a fight (Cosmik plz read)
Ok here we go -

A couple of patches ago us (The fixers) got a line of nano's that could summon Weapons, armor and Ammo.

There were much negativity towards this line cause of the insane high Matt. crea requirements that no fixers have ever raised and also because of it was very hard to to see what we should use these nanos for.

Now that I and other fixers in the guild have become fixers that needs weapons over the ql125 shopping cap, a fixe rin my guild decided to pump hundreds of thousands of Ip into Matt crea. buy ql120 matt crea. implants for loads of money. And getting our highest trader to wrangle him as high as possible. And he got to a point were he actually could summon ql 160 stuff - "GREAT" we thought - We can know get these weapons that are so hard to come by - And at least one of our fixers is doing exactly what a fixer should do -Get stuff easyer that is hard for other people to come by-

So.. After hours of tries he starts getting maussers and some other SMG weapons - just what we need.. "Hurray!" - He summoned a ql 160 Rebuilt supprosser for himsellf and a ql 140 mausser for me. For the first time in my fixer life - I really thought that we could start making a difference... Finally - these Summon nanos are usefull after all - It has cost so much work for the fixer that did this and loads of cash.. but now it works.

For a couple of days we were allright - I had my ql140 mausser and was happy.. very happy.. - But somehow FC allways manage to screw up my happiness.
After a couple of days.. tonight this is - I was borg hunting in Mort together with a group - And in the middle of a fight I cant target the borg anymore.. ?! - I run around him.. jump up and down.. but I just cant get the red "dots" on the target name. So I try left clicking on my mausser - and what happends.. It dissapears.. Out into thin air - its gone.. So now I stand in the middle of a huge battle without a gun. Luckely I had a crappy ql117 mausser in my inv - that I can wield without even buffing...

I looked though my inventory and found a summon weapon nano - and there is written something about it instantly grid warped back to some place on a random check up..cant remember exactly what it said, cause I was so upset - and so Depressed over this - It cant be true that We have just twinked a high lvl fixer towards this ?!!!

The fixer wasnt online neither the trader - So there was no chance of getting a new weapon just yet.
So I logged - If it wasnt for some very good friends I have made in-game.. I would have logged and never ever looked back at this game.

So the bottom line is:
That the sole purpose of this nano is to screw the fixer around to drop a lot of Ips and money into Matt. crea - and then screwing him again by taking the thing away he has worked so hard to obtain.

I cant believe on earth How u could have come up with this idea. But I realise know that the Dev. that has made this have never ever played a fixer to more than level 10 - He cant have.. Cause How can u make a nano this way if u just have a slightly insight in what fixers are.

Ideas to change the summon nano line:

Remove all Summon armor nanos and all Summon weapon nanos.
Make a Summon SMG nano and make the freaking weapons stay forever!!
- I cant believe this.. In the middle of a gd firefight in Mort my Mausser stops working..


the tiny bitsy fate I had in this game is now completly gone - and as stated earlier, the only thing that will get me to log in tomorrow is my great guild and great friends that I have online.
