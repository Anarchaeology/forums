Reflect Shields and Bracers: The Next Crit Buffs
Crit buffs are being nerfed! Run for the hills! It is going to balence PvP! (or so they say, though not really)

However, other items are just as powerful as crit buffs, though they are only effective level 150+ or 190+, they are still extremely powerful. It is possible to get 72% *CONSTANT* reflection against a damage type.

2 Ql 200 Title 6 Bracers = 42% Reflect Chance (one damage type)
1 [best] Soldier Reflect Shield = 30% Reflect Chance (all damage types)

Even now reflect shields are the second most used buffs behind Crit Buffs in PvP, and that is why. The only small reprieve is that 42% of the damage is against one or two damage types; though it doens't matter if you are doing fair duel.

"Hey, that guy is using an ithaca.." Pull out 2 21% Bracers for projectiles.
Flashpoint.. Energy ones.

As more and more people reach Titlelevel5 and especially titlelevel6, reflect shields are becoming more and more powerful, especially to healing classes.

Take, for example, a doc dueling a soldier with the things explained about.

A 7000 Non-PvP Full Auto - Halved, 3500, then only 72% of the damage is 980 damage. (Anyone below level 100 or 75 will think that that is a lot, but any nanomage above level 146 [aka MKII armor time] has over 4k self buffed HPs without any trouble). A 5000 regular burst, 2500, and then 700 damage burst, and 5k crit fling for another 700 damage. 2380 damage. Slap on Complete Heal or something similar and the most powerful instant damage in the game was shrugged off.

Soldiers, after Title Level 6, can get 100% reflect for 1 minute and 20 seconds, or 96% reflect for two minutes with their MK shield.

Just my two cents.

Actually it isn't 72% reflect because they don't all roll in one whack. The reflect sheild rolls, then one braccer, then the other braccer.

So you got a 100 dmg hit is reduced by 30% which would be 30 making it 70

Then you got a 70 dmg hit reduced by 21% of 70 which is 14.7 making it 55.3.

Then you got 55.3 reduced by 21% from the second braccer which is 11.613 making the amount of damage you take 43.678.

Meaning you really only got a 56.313% reflect sheild i recon.

shoobie doobie doo, oh the wonders of math.

By the way, since everyone can actually use both the braccers and the reflect sheild they are pretty balanced. Really the only thing they do is draw fights out so the only advantage would be to people with high first aid skill since a doc will be doing 56% less damage to you than he would if neither of you had a reflect sheild and they can already heal till OON unless your alpha strike or crits kill them right away.
