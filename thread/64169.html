No full patch notes for 14.6 yet, but...
...here's some of the stuff that's in 14.6:

*New character creation and arrival hall

*New NPC chat system

*Newbie dungeon; Condemned Subway

*Tower / vehicle shops under construction on Rubi-Ka

*Initiative fixes on these weapons:
-BBI Faithful
-Nova Flow Mark I
-Arwen MO-404 Grenade Launcher
-HSR Explorer 661
-M-1 Bipower A
-MTI - Russian Good Day
-Manex Catastrophe
-Collins IX Bio-Energy Rifle
-Netgun
-Schuyler Bow

*A boost to the Queen Blade

*All bows set to use Physical Initiative (Pow Bow is a crossbow, and uses Ranged Initiative)
{Edit: Oups. Being a bit tired here. I meant the Bow-Blaster, as some people asked me about it a while back. And the Reet-Tech Junior Urban and Oneida Razor Half-Bow too. Sorry 'bout the mix-up. }

*Thrown grenades implemented (throwing knives didn't make it)

*Wen-Wens now work

*Level 200s can now use the side application forms

*Nano Repulson now buffs Nano Resist, as it was supposed to

*All outdoor mobs now give more XP

*Evades lowered on the outdoor glowing bosses

*Pet profession NPCs now summon pets in combat

*Soldiers recieve nano buffs for several weapon skills

{Added:
*Enforcers recieve nano buffs for several weapon skill}

*Nano Programming nano buffs for Nano Technicians

*Revamp of Martial Arts special attacks (Bird of Prey and such)

*Missions requested from booths by Fixers give higher QL loot

*Coffee machine available through trade skills

*Solar Pistol can now be build and improved by trade skills

*Organization names now display over people's heads, colored by side

*Atroxes get more health, while nanomages get a bigger nano pool

*Chance of scoring critical hits in PvP halved
{Edit: We're increasing the crit chance on the Agent's Concentration line to 200% to leave this at 100% in PvP}

*Fixed a range bug on concealment from other players

*Monster info (shift + left click on the mob, or target it and press 't') updated

*Recieving team heals no longer breaks concealment

*Fixed a range bug on Da Taunter

*Repaired a bunch of broken trade skill processes

*Other bug fixes, tweaks and new content. This was just a taste. More to follow tomorrow (I hope).
