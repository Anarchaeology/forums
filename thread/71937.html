CCI President Arraigned on Conspiracy Charges
ROME, Rubi-Ka -- Ex-Conquistador Coffee President, Vandamar, is arraigned today for ordering the death of a journalist.

His lawyer said they would ask the Court of Rome to rush through the case and quash any su****ion of his clients guilt.

Vandamar, who has held every top job in Beverage Distribution and was President of Conquistador Coffee Industries, denies the charges and says he was framed.

The prosecution alleges that Vandamar's aides, in collusion with Conquistador Coffee Employees, conspired to kill Rubi-Ka Times Journalist Pl4y4K1ll4 Pecorelli because he was about to publish revelations that could have exposed criminal involvement of Vandamar's flagship company, Conquistador Coffee. It is not known what the information was.

Vandamar has long maintained that cases against him were false, saying he was targeted by political enemies and possibly Bronto Burger Executives getting even for his lucrative stranglehold on food industries in Rubi-Ka.

Vandamar told state radio: "I'll wait and be patient. I'm used to thinking in the long-term because this affair has been going on for years. It is ludicrous, however, to assume that myself, or anyone associated with Conquistador Coffee Incorporated would have a hand in any such sordid affairs."

If the case is not given special fast-track treatment, it might take many months, or even years, to reach a decision.
