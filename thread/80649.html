Splitting Ammo - How to do it fast
Did you know...

If you ctrl-shift click on ammo (or other splitable items I would imagine) and drag your mouse to the right you can split off any quantity you desire in a couple of seconds.

Now be honest, whenever someone ran out of ammo you were ctrl-clicking on that stack and pealing bullets off one at a time and *****ing about how bad it sucked the whole time, weren't you?
