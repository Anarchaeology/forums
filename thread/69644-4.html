In a word: wire. Garroting is an art form, perfected by generations of Sicilians and Calabrians back on Old Earth. Plus, it's quick and simple! To clothesline the fixer of your choice, you will need to select the gauge wire that you need for the job. I like to do this according to the degree of enmity I have for the fixer in question. Once you have decided on the thickness of your medium, you will then want to stretch it across the entrance door of a mission the fixer is about to blitz...

Degrees of Enmity:

Deep abiding hatred: you want to use a high-quality piano wire, preferably the thicker type used in a Steinway Class M Grand Piano. Feel free to keep the head as a fine trophy for over your mantel, or to decorate your office as an added stimulus for your employees to not goof off.

Contempt: Heavy-duty fishing line, of the sort used for your larger fish, such as tuna or mahi-mahi. While they'll be whistling through their throat for awhile, eventually the fixer will *probably* recover, possibly even able to speak without a voice box. But listening to that electronic voice-box is so funny! The fixer will be the life of the party!

Not-worthy-of-contempt/Vicious practical joke: Cheap Wal-mart fishing line, more commonly used for hanging toy planes from the ceiling, rather than actual fishing. Leaves a nice-looking bruise and a raspy voice, that will only grow more hoarse with time, answering everyone who keeps asking, "hey man, what happened to your neck?"

Acme(tm) practical joke: Use dental floss, grease the entrance floor, and place a patch of thumbtacks just beneath the floss. Ideally, the fixer will skid into the floss, get pulled back, and sit down hard on the tacks.



Just an idea, for those of you who wanted something a little more unusual....
