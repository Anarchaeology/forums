The reason i am buying
Personally, I hate PVP, but one of the few times my heart starts racing in the game is when I am running through MMD and i see the message "lameGanker became visible"

Anyway, the reason I am purchasing the game is because of my background in software development. When a company makes a booster or expansion the view of the product as a whole changes. From that point on, the "full product" is now the original plus additions. All the effort is going to make the full product better, and the lesser product (original) is given a second place in the development streams. It does not matter if we are talking operating system, telephone systems or games, the fact is that the more expensive version will receive the most attention, and often the problems are only solved there. For example, when you complain that x is broken, the answer you will probably find is well, if you had bought the full product, then you would not have this problem.

I dont want to end up being treated as a second rate customer.

Also, I might be more tempted to PVP in a group. There are some cool ground based combat cars that i think my engie will like, which will change hunting. I want to see the skies full of cargo ships, and to hear the voice emotes that are included.

Lets look at this another way.

Dollar for dollar, what makes better value, getting the booster pack or going to a movie. The movie lasts two hours only, but the booster pack will take longer to explore than a movie.

Another thing to realize
From the laws of online gaming, the quality of players tends to increase in proportion with the money they spend on the game. And there is a reward in gameplay.

Another thing to realize.
There is an Xp bonus for land control, people are paying millions for a 5% ring, whereas you will get that for free even if your side is losing terribly, as there is a total of a 25% xp bonus to be divided allong land control lines.

So,
Its not much cash, its going to be fun, its going to be worth it, its cheaper than a movie.

I think Cz is closing down threads on to buy or not to buy, so consider what I have said, and please join me.
