Customizable weapons
Funcom has been on the right track with adding new weapons and armor to the game, such as the sealed receptacles. The problem is that most classes still have only one or two decent weapon choices. Adding tradeskill weapons just adds one more weapon option. What they really need to do is allow players to design their own weapons. This would add a whole lot more variety to the game. Obviously FC has some sort of formula that they refer to when creating new weapons. (At least I hope they do) Just let players have access to this formula through tradeskills. I.E. Bigger bullets means more damage but smaller clip size. The base component could be boss loot with the same drop chance as a div 9 or an x-3. That way the custom weapons could be as powerful as these 'standard' weapons that everybody uses, but they would be customized to the player.
