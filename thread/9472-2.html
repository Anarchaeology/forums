Leetas
I can see why everyone wants the NoDrop removed, but it really is there for a very good reason. The high-end game simply has too much money. Players no longer buy expensive things from stores, rather from eachother. Money enters the system and never leaves. It's simple enough. I know it's frustrating not to be able to sell your ql 31 yalm after you upgrade to your ql 77, but in this case I view it as a necessary evil. After all it is the most expensive purchaseable item in the game (to my knowledge). To make it only availible through kiosks and dumb luck is a start attempt at maintaining the value of money.


It's a hard point to see, and I can't honestly hold it against someone if they can't see it. If there were a more suitable way to get money out of the system employed, then we could have this discussion. But as is, I would say no ammount of votes, no ammount of petitioning will get the NoDrop off your cars.
