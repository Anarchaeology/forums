2 - The seconds remaining "bug"

I am pretty sure I read somewhere that time in game is half the speed of RL time. 1 in-game second = 2 real life seconds. In any case, your skill delay timer isn't actually slowed, the entire game is.

9 - Aborted nano spells (voluntairly) consume the nano nonetheless.

That's because the nano is consumed as the spell starts. The same is true for spells interrupted by monsters/players.

11 - Doctor team buffs dont work if you are solo (ie a team of one =) ) Since they dont have equivalent solo spells, makes it difficult on them.

Also, if you volontarily leave your team, you will receive all team buffs. Team buffs also have infinite range. Plus they will be cast on you regardless of NCU limitations.

14 - Unavailable skills like brawl/burst/dimach ect... appear to be available on the action bar when in fact they are not.

This has to do with server-client relations. The timer on all skills is client-side. After 20 "seconds" (40 RL seconds due to server being at half-speed) your client will tell you that your Brawl skill is available. However, if the server was lagged at any time in those 20 seconds, your brawl skill will not be available on the server, and if you, immediately after receiving the notification, hit the Brawl key, your client will perform the animation, lock your skill, and reset the timer, but the server will say "Hey, your brawl isn't ready yet you cheater!" and nothing will happen. Since the brawl is locked client-side, you don't get another chance to brawl until your timer is up.

33 - CPU upgrades are said to lower cost of nano programs but in fact they raise it . (by Duvlin, have NO idea what he is talkign about)

CPU upgrades (called "CPU Upgrade") are like coolant upgrades (which reduce the chance of your spell being aborted/cancelled/interrupted by movement/hits/etc.) and NCU upgrades - they go on the deck slots and require a belt. CPU upgrades supposedly lower the cost of nano programs but according to this guy they actually raise it.

Also if you remove your trenchcoat it looks like you have no armor on the places the trenchcoat was on!

38 - Unable to change melee/range target in combat. If you get attacked by a second more dangerous mob, you cant change targets and go after it. VERY BAD.
