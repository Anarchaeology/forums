End the pain, cut to the chase.
Instead of slapping breed, profession, level, etc restrictions on everything, why not just do the following:

1) Remove IP
2) Remove skills from each class that you dont want them to ever use:
-Enforcer Weapon Choice: 1hb/2hb/1he/2he
-MA Weapon Choice: Martial Arts
-Fixer Weapon Choice: SMG
-Soldier Weapon Choice: Assault Rifle
-NT Weapon Choice: None
-Crat Weapon Choice: None
-Doc Weapon Choice: None
-Adv Weapon Choice: 1he/melee energy
-Engy Weapon Choice: Grenade Launcher
-MP Weapon Choice: 1hb/melee energy
...etc
3) Make each skill raise x/level automagically
4) Remove nano skills. Level restrict each classes nanos. (Prof=LevelX, Agent=LevelX + 50)
5) Make all buffs self only
6) Make all armor have stat requirements and (since all stats increase x/level automatically with number (3) you no longer have to worry about over equipping.
7) Rename Game Anarchy Quest Online
8) Get verants customer list and email all of their customers about SciFi EQ.

Quick and Easy.

Here I was thinking that this was a skill based game...may as well get the pain over with and let us see what we are left with...

Don't get me wrong, I wouldn't post something this sarcastic if I couldn't stand the game. You all have a game with tons of potential...it just seems to me that you are throwing it all away. Contrary to what marketing droids want everyone to believe, word of mouth works. If you would just take the time to fix the core issues with this game (there aren't as many as these boards would cause one to believe) instead of slapping band-aid after band-aid on it, you would have a fanbase that would probably to get any friends that are even remotely interested in this type of game to give it a try.

I have quite a few friends currently that like these kinds of games, but sadly can not reccomend AO to them due to the dire future that seems to be looming ever larger before us. If I wanted a cookie cutter game, I would still be playing EQ.
