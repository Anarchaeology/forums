An interesting find in Stret West...
The ineptitude of Omni-Pol Bureaucrats never ceases to amaze me. I can easily see why InternOps bashes them on a daily basis.

While enlightening myself one night at the Omni-Pol Base in Stret West, I came across a standard document security container. I didn't think much of it at the time as it was only a Security Level Delta.

Delta is usually reserved for Outpost Manager communicae back to Omni-Pol HQ and is generally my bread and butter. Most of the time, I find material requisitions in these and alter them to add more equipment. When the shipment arrives at the OP, I intercept it and "liberate" my order.

Anyhow, I hacked the container as I normally would, but was quite surprised to find a Level Omega17 document in there. Talk about a security faux pas.

For the uninitiated, Omega17 is the second highest security level on the entire planet. There are only about two dozen or so people on Rubi-Ka with that kind of access. Mostly Omni department heads, with the notable exception of Omni-Pol High Command (and after this fiasco, I can see why) and some very high ranking InternOp operatives.

Omega18 is the highest level and I know of only two people in that catagory. Ross and Janus. That level is reserved for off-planet communicaes though, so in effect Omega17 is the highest on planet level.

Well, the toiler-bots on this thing were not an easy task to defuse, but with the help of Xaeriq and a few other meta-physical people, I was able to finally hack this thing.

Imagine my surprise when I found the subject of such a high level document. You can ignore the warnings on the cover sheet, the toiler-bots are defused.

Here is the document: rossmemo.041829476.pdf
