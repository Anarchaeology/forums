Envisioned - Equippable engineer bots
Since AO is all over customization like a Grimwolf on a doctor, wouldn't it be amazingly amazing if, rather than casting bots, engineers actually made their bots?

Now I don't necessarily mean make bots in the sense of making weapons... I propose that engineer bots become something like a mini-NPC, in the sense that they are equippable just as your own character is albeit with fewer slots. For example, instead of than right-clicking your nano-program, by taking the appropriate torso, leg, arm, and head components, you could customize your bot to be a melee, ranged, fire, radiation... (ad infinitum) attacker. Equip that Gauss rifle, equip those PPCs! The customization possibilities are endless: unique armor, weapons, legs, and controlling units (head, I suppose) could all be untilized to make the engineer truly a master of machines rather than specialized nanos. Oh, and rather than use the Time & Space and Matter Creation skills, engineers would become more dependent on the Electrical and Mechanical Engineering and the Computer Science skills. Engineers would still need use of the T&S and MatCrea skills because what engineer wouldn't tweak and improve (i.e. buff) their own creations?

The methods of obtaining parts would be fairly easy: primarily mob loot and the new tradeskill department. Buy a torso, leg, and head and you're ready to go -- who ever said your bot has to attack? Eventually, even more customization like insignias and tags would have to be added, multiplying the fun even further.

As always, there are other realms to be explored -- bots and nanos, selling/trading bots, what to do when Bad Things happen to your bot and it dies... These I leave for you to ponder.
