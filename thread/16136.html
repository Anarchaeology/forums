A clearification on the "24-7" name:
Greetings loyal OT employees!

Lately i've been contacted by people and recevied question due to a misunderstanding that have been spread in Omni-1. Someone has gotten the idea that the "24-7" title is linked to the expression "24-7" which is used by certain hardcore computergamers on people who play 24hours 7 days a week. I was even contacted by a reporter from Rubi-Ka Times and asked to evaluate some of the new hologames in Entertainment. That would been fun, but i have no knowledge on such games at all and they usually make me feel sick, so i had to say no.

The title "24-7" is however connected to the main goal of our organization, to be able to provide logistics and services 24hours 7 days a week to our customers (I know the day is longer than 24hour on Rubi-Ka, but 24-7 was founded on Earth).

I hope this have made thing clearer, thank you for reading this.
