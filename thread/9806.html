Token suggestions
I figure that funcom needs to work on their token system some so I'd like to start a thread to gather suggestions.

Since anyone over lvl 6 is likely never to see the 1000 token boards since missions seem to be the only way to get tokens, there needs to be something added. Now funcom is working on adding GM provided missions, and NPC missions. I think the rewards for some of these, granted they are more difficult than just run through and kill everything in sight, should be multiple tokens, 5-20 or so, or even more, a real reward that means time saved for the players. There needs to be a faster way to gather tokens at high levels given the impossibly long time it takes to clear out a mission. As it stands now, you can make a huge impression on your faction at very low levels, but your services at high levels barely get noticed? Now that's messed up.

Also, since funcom's Jail concept seems to have failed, or at least still needs a lot of work for implementing, I believe in-game crimes should result in deduction of tokens. For instance, killing a civilian (neutral) player should be a deduction of lets say for example 10 tokens. That's enough to sting and possibly teach a player a lesson. Now the only way this will truly adversely effect the player is if there is a check made on the token board each time the player zones, or a deletion of the current token board and a fresh token board be dumped in the player's inventory.
