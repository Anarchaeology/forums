There's a simple fix though. Open your mission window and right-click-and-hold on the mission. You get a popup icon menu. Hmm, well even without the map, the yellow marker on the compass will get you there. Just run in the indicated direction. Most mission sites are fairly obvious, but not all. If the marker suddenly vanishes, stop and turn round, chances are you ran past the mission entrance. Move about that area and get a fix on where it is.

Also, if the mission description gives coordinates, you can use F9 to find where you currently are. first coordinate is east/west and second is north/south (IIRC).

Finally, once uploaded, you get a mission marker on your main map (p key) which will get you into the general area.

Make sure the mission key is in your inventory and not in a backpack (unlikely in this case I know, but worth bearing in mind).

Some mission doors don't look like doors, some are caves, some tree trunks, some pipes... Once you get the map and get the visual marker on it it becomes easier, but some are still tricky to pin down, and sometimes zoning into them is slow which might lead you to believe you've not found the right place.

Persevere and you'll get there.
