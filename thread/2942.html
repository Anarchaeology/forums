An Idea...
Possibilities but might be a little difficult to implement...

1.) Actual jobs or mission varience? Omni and Clan both have different departments and organizations. We should be able to apply to one of these organizations and depending on our abilities and game standing obtain a position within them. In return a steady income in credits based on game hours doing a task. Create some type of in game check in and check out. ie.

Soldiers check in and get stationed in pol zone areas and must stay at post to get paid. If said soldier gets killed he is automatically checked out. Granted that this sounds like the most boring job in the world but you do earn credits, it's a price you pay just like any rl job.

Other jobs can work in fields specific to their professions.

Doctors can work in omni med building treatment kits and first aids. Engineers casting and providing a certain amount of bot shells or weapons. etc. etc.
