Nascence routes
The routes you have are... um... ugh. I have no idea how you jumped along the wall like that.

If you look at where the red and blue routes join the main isle south of Jobe Research, they actually join up in a path that is easy to follow leading right south of jobe research (but not accessing to it). If you go to the spot under the bridge you can't go to, and then go east just a bit, you'll find a hill of black rock you can climb. Look for a triangle of rocks that look like you can't fit under. If you follow it up, it is the start of an easy to follow path that tracks to the left all the way to the ergo area.
