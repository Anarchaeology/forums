Yalmaha's and Mobs
Ok, before I start and get moaned at by everyone, let me say I do have a Yalmaha and I do like it.

BUT, as one of the GM's once said on here, the whole reason some missions are harder than others is the place they are, and the Mobs people have to encounter, avoid to do these missions. Fair enough...adds to the difficulty and makes it more fun. But whats with Yalmaha's? As soon as you have one no more worries ever again about mobs getting to you enroute to a mission, or coming back. It is a bit of a dissappointment. Sooner or later I can just see everyone having them and the whole planets going to be boring

So my little idea. Make a few mobs, namely vultures, dragons, and the like aggro things in the air? It won't make a massive differance, yet will make people more observant when they are flying and give that little element of risk, and something to avoid. Heck maybe add an Anti-aircraft missile or two to enemy bases or to cyborgs mwhahahl.

Just an idea.
