Here are my tips to successfully blitz missions..

Firstly turn off all the uneeded nano effects, show buffs, names, enviroment effects. This will help reduce any possible lag.

Get a runbuff from a kindly fixer.

Buff yourself with all your defensive buffs and set AGG DEF slider to full def.

Close all windows accept the map, and keep inventory open with key in top corner.. ready to unlock doors.

If mission looks big, do a scouting run. That is run around the main room and a couple of rooms into each doorway to try and get an idea of which way looks like it will the right direction, then zone again. Heal and then head back in.

Run like a mad man!

p.s Cloudeh the message you get about key not accepted is your B&E skill is simply too low.. raise it to around 300 to open doors in ql200 missions everytime. Also that pause at the door happens with certain map layouts and not with others.. try and get BS missions.
