The idea is that the mission terminal would detect which profession are in your team at the moment, and so you would never have to hunt for certain professions. It wouldn't be too hard to develop certain mission aspects tailored to each profession, then when generating a mission, detect which professions are in the team, and add elements to the mission according to those professions.

Profession specific missions also need not only be purely team missions. If there is only one person gettng the mission (not a team), then they would get a mission that uses only aspect of their profession. There may be multiple problems that need to be solved in pretty much the same way, but that would just be an issue of creating as many puzzles for each profession as possible. Also, the mobs in the mission should be tailored to the profession as well in terms of difficulty of the mobs. If a Doc takes a mission, then the mobs that must be killed should be much easier than the mobs that would appear in an enforcer mission. Also, attack power, ac, and hp should be tweaked seperately. To have mobs with a very high attack power in a mission with an NT would be unfair, but it would not necessarily be unfair to give the mobs a larger than normal number of hitpoints. It might also be interesting to make mobs in NT missions have very high AC to all except 1 or 2 types of damage. That way, the NT would have to use different nanos in their arsenal to take into account damage type, not only the amount of damage alone.

Also, I think one of the most important things to have with these missions is the possibility for failure. Current missions, there is no real possiblity for failure, other than waiting too long, deleting the mission, or simply extreme and total stupidity (selling the return item in a find-return mission). I dont' care about what rewards would be offered. In truth, these missions might be a perfect way to get IPReset points. Gaute mentioned that they might be available as rewards in the future.

Once again, these are just thoughts. Feel free to comment. Try to keep things constructive.

One way round the problem of people leaving the team is to make the mission completable by more than one skillset.

For example, the locked door mentioned above could be lockpicked (fixer), sabotaged / demolished (engy), or just broken down (enforcer).

Similarly, a "stop this guy from using his horrible PK weapons"
mission could be resolved by 1. sneaking in and sabotaging the weapon (fixer/agent) 2. killing the guards and taking the weapon 3. killing the boss that intends to use it (enforcer)

This could be taken to the logical extreme where we don't need class-specific missions, but all characters can draw on the unique talents of their class to complete missions.
