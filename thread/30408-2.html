*** WARNING: This post uses the word 'paradigm' ***

For good or ill, as a programmer, I see the world in black&white terms.
PvP in AO is for 1 of 2 things but not both. (XOR) :

1) For people to fight other people in a manner like they fight monsters.

OR

2) To increase the feeling / fighting of Clan vs Omni.

Pick one. Not two, not zero, just one.

Clearly, if AO is to be paradigm #1, then tighter level restrictions are the way to go. But if it is to be paradigm #2, then there should probably be no level restricitons at all in PvP.

I think FC is trying to reach a balance between the two.
Should FC have picked 1 xor 2?
Is it a good idea to try to balance the two?
Did FC manage to reach that balance?
Is the Euro a good idea?
Is Jackie Chan better than Jet Lee?

All matters of opinion.
