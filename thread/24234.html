All other classes in this game (with the minor exception of the Adventurer) have clearly defined roles as to who they are.

Trader - Buffer/Debuffer this is his strength and is what he brings to a team
Crat - Crowd Controller
Enforcer - Melee Tank
Soldier - Ranged Tank
Fixer - Thief
Martial Artist - Unarmed Tank
Nano-Tech - Nuker

Engineer - Same as above but with more focus on trade skills
