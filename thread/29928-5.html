Do engineers need help in pvp?

Maybe, a tiny little bit.

Should engineers actually be good at pvp?

No.

I'm sorry but as has been stated by someone else in this thread no single class should be good at everything. Face it, there will always be a class that is considered the worst in this or that department of the game. For pvp that might just as well be the engie.

No engineer can honestly say that he picked the class because he or she wanted to become a great pvp player. Hell even if you only read the very very disinforming information in the creation chamber you could never have gotten the idea engies would be a great class to go pvping.

Engineers make decent soloers (right behind crats imo but better than mp's considering a demon can't take an ace (except a functionnary maybe) and apparently a slayerdroid can) and are second best in tradeskills.

If you are now angry because your engineer sucks at pvp all I can say is ... tough break but as I stated before you can't have been thinking of pvp when you created him/her.

Complaining that engineers suck at pvp is imo as silly as a hypothetical atrox enforcer complaing that he has such lousy tradeskills.

It's a tough bullet to bite when you have invested so much time in a char but it is nevertheless true.

The main issue is imo that there is not a lot of fun things to do besides pvp at higher levels, which makes it double worse that engies suck at it. FC should focus on that, not on making engies a good pvp class.

Besides that engineers need one thing though, they really need some help in being a desirable class to team with at higher levels. Because they rank lowest there as well. And imo every class should be desirable in teams, but not every class should be good at pvp.

So to sum my thoughts up: Do engineers need help? Definitely, but not in pvp.
