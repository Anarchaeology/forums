Thanks for the free month
(Sorry, not sure where to type this, there doesn't seem to be a place for these messages. So.... Envisioned sounds good )

It was a good move to invite cancelled accounts back for the free month. Thank you for this opportunity, here are some thoughts for you. AO has improved a lot but it is still not quite ready for "release".

Graphics/Client lag This is the main killer. You play for a while and the game gets slower and slower until it's so choppy you have to reboot your PC. I'm starting to think this is a design flaw in the core game engine, it's not looking good.

Stuff stops working I've noticed this mostly in a missions. Heal and Nano kits stop working. I can't loot corpses. I can't open/unlock chests. I have to zone out which makes me lose the exposed mission map and wastes time.

Reduce PvP to a few areas You're going to hate this but check your own polls where 33 out of almost 1000 players think PvP is a good thing. Players want to be entertained with eye-candy, character progress, useful trade skills, nice loots, good friends, big challenges, etc. PvP is not a money maker for you. It has been proven time and time again that PvE is the money spinner for online games. Pop the PvP into a few areas and open up the game a bit more for PvE. Keep the conflict going - two factions and a neutral, it will still make the story work. Omni hates Clan, etc. If you moved your focus to PvE then players will care a lot less about cheaters, etc.

Experience loss on death Despite the fact the player has some control over this, it's still bad. in a big mission at level 70, I will do something like 20% of the xp required to get the next level. All it takes is one mistake (sometimes caused by the game) and I've gone backwards a long way. Players don't like that. Asherons Call had the best system. Make it a stat reduction for a while, plus downtime when you die. Due to the death penalties, your players are currently looking for the "easy xp" path and they aren't pushing your game forward. All the players seem to do nowadays is set up in a safe, known spawn that generates good xp for minimal work. Please re-think this situation - make death stall or temporarily gimp your character, but stop making it go backwards in such a drastic way.

Over Equipping Why do players do this? Because they can - and you guys nerfed the classes and the game to cause players to take this action. The fix is simple, just STOP players equipping items beyond a certain level above them. Currently, you are trying to change the gameplay to stop over equipping. Don't be silly, this is hurting the "play as intended" players. Fix the cause, not the effect.

More Diversity with Environment You need to add some diversity to the world. It is very, very good but you need to think up new ideas. The world zones are mostly green with trees or some rocks (with lava), or desert (with more rocks). The missions are brown caves (with acid) or grey buildings (with toilets). You guys are the experts but you need to add in some new environments. There are pockets of great areas but you might need to make zones with a more consistant theme to them.

Monster Spawns I'm not sure if this has changed since release because I didn't play outside very much this time around. There used to be aggro high level monsters mixed in with low level ones. This needs to be sorted out a bit, so the further you travel, the harder it gets. There's nothing worse than getting wiped out by an unkillable mob after setting your group up to play. Pop more spawns into clusters (or camps) too. There are a LOT of roaming mobs that are good fun to fight but you need to run around for ages to find them. Once you've clustered the monsters, change the spawn timers to make players move around more.

Multi-part Missions The current missions are good. But they can be better. Why not string a series of missions together and make better rewards for the effort. Maybe even give the player a shopping token to take to a booth that is filled with goodies to suit everyone. a) get the access code for the computer b) hack the computer (make it a puzzle) c) disable the tracking system and dowload the information to kill enemy01. d) infiltrate the installation and assasinate enemy01. e) return his ID tag to General Putz and get a reward token. Get some new ideas happening here and create a little "story" for players to complete every now and then.

Trade Skills These are combat related (you need levels for IP, you need combat to level), and that is just plain stupid. Try making trade skills a different set of IP skills that can be advanced only by practising skills. Players shouldn't need to dip into their combat IP's to up their trade skills. At character creation you could even make a "Tradeskill" profession. This profession would not be able to combat in any way and can only do trades.

Game Information I think you need to pop a game guide online. This should be to the point of almost being a spoiler. You have introduced things like new dungeons, missions, etc but they are impossible for the casual player to find. Get some information online, don't spoil everything though. The idea is to get players playing the game, let them discover the finer points.

IP Adjustments With the classes changing a bit and with the game community constantly discovering new ways to do things, you need a way to let players change their IP allocations. Yes, I understand it could be exploited if it's not done properly, so the solution is to do it properly. There are a couple of good threads around about this, have a read of them. This would make the game fun.

Put some fun back The concealment nerf was silly. This was one of those things where players said, "OMFG this is so cool". I think you need to revisit sneaking. In the beginning just about anyone could sneak past those 3 mob spawns in missions, add the buffs and skill and they could walk right through any mobs. That was hella fun. And yes, I agree there does need to be limitations but you went overboard. That's not the only thing. Go back the start and revisit the ideas you had... soldier shield was being abused so instead of fixing the cause (stupid monsters that suicided on them), you took away a great escape/survival tactic for soldiers. The are more fun things you changed, too numerous to mention.

Ah dear, I typed more than I wanted. There are several other things but I didn't come back to beta test your game and report on it. I came to try out a game I bought and a game where a monthly subscription applies.

A lot of what I typed is from a "casual player" point of view. You have been listening to, and catering for, the hard core types and where did that get you? The have left. They came, they saw, they conquered. And now they're in Mythic's ear *****ing about their game.

AO is conceptually the best MMORPG out of everything released and anything currently in development. But with the technical and gaming issues at this time, it is not worth the money.

Well, I'll break my own rule about not posting twice on the same thread. I need to clarify a few things and go on the defensive a bit.

I'm not sure what sort of computer I need and I'm not a propellor-head, but I have a Pentium 4, 1.8Ghz, 512Mb Ram, GEForce2 and <some fast 40gb hard drive>. Granted, the GEForce 2 may be lacking but there's definately a client issue happening. I *think* the last patch messed up something up but I'm not sure. I only played a day or two prior to the patch but it seemed better than after the patch.

As far as PvP goes, I'm not into PvP as I indicated above. The best model for PvP/PvE co-existance is DAoC. You have the care-bear areas and then everyone meets in the middle to fight. Unfortunately that entire game hinges around the war and, quite frankly, it's boring (played to level 39 in DAoC before tearing my hair out). It also suffers from one of the main reasons why I'm not into PvP and that is random sniping and ganking. The teams idea mentioned above may be a good one, I'd like to hear more about that.

Anyway - my point for PvP is that a couple of areas (zones) should be set aside for the AO war, rather than patches of areas here and there. If the PvP is consolidated into a couple of good battle zones, complete with shops and save terminals, etc, then players who want that action will go there. They could even throw in a few juicy missions/quests for PvE into those areas. Although I don't like PvP very much, I still get a thrill from going through the "war zones", potentially being food for the enemy. And I agree, players should be rewarded for PvP.

Experience system - I'm from EQ actually, played 2 chars to level 60 and another to 55. The fact is that players want to play and progress. They don't want to go backwards. Personally I don't mind some downtime due to death but xp loss is the "old way" of doing things. I quoted AC, but I actually mean the no experience loss situation. Yes, I do prefer to come back to life with my stuff How about this idea? Remote Scanner Devices. You could use these to remotely upload your character data to the terminals. Can't use them in combat and they take 15 seconds to upload the information. Limit the supply by making them a mission reward, or a quest reward or something. Now THAT'S a mission worth doing If that all seems too much to give to players then make the remote devices save only 50% of xp earned since the last save, or base their effectiveness on QL or Computer Literacy or something.

Thanks to the others for letting me hear different opinions in this thread. Opinions are good. The game HAS improved a lot and I may have been a bit too harsh to start with. I hope nobody interprets my message as bashing the game. Far from it, I want to play the game because it's a good one. Hell, I'm here typing about it, so it must be having some influence on me

However, even with the current system, it could be made so that people can advance using solely tradeskills. All they need to do is make the tradeskills worth more exp, and make it possible to earn money with them. I have quite a bit to say on, but this isn't exactly the place for it, so I'll hold off, hehe.

They would of course be quite expensive, and have some sort of requirements, and the lower the QL the less they would save (i.e. low QL would only reduce exp loss by 10%, whereas higher Ql ones would reduce it by 50%, or whatever). I have a feeling they probably decided not to put these in because
a) people don't seem to have any trouble leveling whatsoever. In fact, I'm pretty sure the playerbase is leveling as fast or faster than they hoped/expected, and therefore why would they try and speed it up any?
and
b) they decided to give these types of abilities out as profession skills (eg the crat nano mentioned above) rather than just items which are accessible to everyone.

Hopefully they'll continue to improve the game as much as they have been, and perhaps before long you'll decide it is worth playing Small =)
