White Knights

Special Division

White Knights was formed after the fall of a few major divisions. They were made up of the remaining few who fought through the battles that lead to the divisions fall. These few pledged to fight for what was right even if it surpassed and law. Do to the nature of the new Division, and the need for such a Division that could Operate above the Law in a time when the Law itself is corrupt the White Knight take no orders from Omni but from there own internal government body.
The White Knights are an Elite tactical group. They put there lives on the line for what is right regardless of whom it is they feel they need to protect. All new member are required to make a pelage to always among all else uphold the natural Law of humanity, to always protect life and kill only when it become the last chance and to never run but to face there fears head on.
White Knights name is a symbol of who they are, the color white repressing peace purity and hope, the knight representing something long forgot in this time a person of honor one who up hold the law and courage. The White Knights serve as a beacon to any who needs help.
If you see a white knight you are in the presents of something long forgot in human history resurrected in this time to put back what is right and purge what is wrong.
