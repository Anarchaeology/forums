from another perspective
The adventurer has some outdoor strengths that are often times overlooked and under-appreciated.

Our best run and evades buff come in the outdoor forms of the Sparrow and Flurry. We cannot root or charm mobs but we most can certainly try to outrun and evade a hostile mob in the wilderness. And we can also help others do so - that is one of our strongest strengths out in the wilderness.

Example:
While outdoors, if I should see someone (not a teammate) being chased by a tough mob, I can either:
a) take down the mob if I can
b) heal the person to buy him/her time to fight back
c) and if the mob is too tough and the above 2 will not help the situation, I can Reet (other) or Flurry (other) the person so that he/she can get away.
(and yes, for the cynic, there can be a possible 4th option:
d) do absolutely nothing to help)

An outright nerf on this aspect will be taking away one of the strongest outdoor aids an adventurer can provide, besides the obvious healing aspect.

If you're so worried about PvP (which affects what percentage of the playbase again? Hmm right, that's what I thought) ... I suggest something like a PvP PC tag or flag (or whatever it's called) be initiated ... something to prevent such a chain-casting tactic. As for the griefer example, tough luck, you met a jerk, move on - there are plenty other griefing and ganking activities to worry about but I don't see FC policing those.

So, in essense, I fail to see why something that isn't likely to be widely practised - would warrant an outright nerf on one of the strongest aid features of a profession. There are very few things going for the adventurer at the moment, so do consider other perspectives.
