Methlon's RP Demise. Aye it is finally over
As i stare at the stars.. The beautiful moon in the dark endless night... I hear the onslaught going on around me.. Tousands of brave warriors and soldiers.. Clashing.. Falling to the ground.. I find myself lying in a pool of blood my own blood... I feel the coldness inside my hearth as the last imagine flashing in my head is that of my beloved ones... What drove me to this point is of no matter... Perhaps it was bloodlust.. Perhaps lust for revenge.. It does not matter.. Slayed by my own kind the one i used to serve i feel my essence sipping out on the cold floor in the Omni-HQ and utters my last dying words... "Long live... Freedom..."



-Veldrin "Methlon" Peak Leader of the Sinister Anarchists Freedom fighter... "I will always remember you my love"-
