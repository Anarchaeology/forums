Tab-targetting and enemies screwed up in multi-alignment teams...
Not sure if this was pre-patch or post-patch, as I just recently had some friends create neutral chars.

My char is Clan, however we often team up with a few clanners and a few neutrals. This creates a "neutral" team, but causes some strange glitches.

1) Even though the team is neutral, Clan mobs will ignore all clan party members unless they spot a Neutral party member, then they will attack me (Clan) even if unprovoked. This may be on purpose, but it is a bit odd.

2) The biggest problem is that the Tab cycle will target my neutral team member!! Bad, bad! Since we're always grouped up, instead of selecting the closest or next enemy, it will cycle through my teammates first. This is a real pain...and the tab cycling should ignore teammate no matter if they are of different alignments.

3) When in a neutral team, Omni gear starts appearing very frequently in missions and mission rewards, despite that no Omni team members were involved, the mission was recieved in Clan territory, and the mission target is in Clan territory. (Avalon -> Newland) This isn't a huge deal, but slightly annoying. Why give us crap we can't use?
