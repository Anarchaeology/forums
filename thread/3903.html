OK, so FC is suffering through financial woes. I can accept that and any resulting slowdown or degradation in service. I'm very reasonable like that, and I won't badmouth or drop AO because of it. In fact, I'll probably support it more.

But dropping anything, especially something as vital as the storyline movies, would be folly. Many people have a hard time role playing as is, and the movies are instrumental in developing the world, the conflict, and its characters. VERY important.

If need be, cut back to voiced comic strips, as mpegs, as Flash, as whatever; these could be turned out fairly quickly for many ingame events (such as the trial), and for larger storyline events. They would do just fine in keeping most people with it, and they would even add to the artistic flair and character that you have developed so far (the first movies, even though the animation was spotty, had a very nice comic strip-ish flair, with the blurred paused animation and voice overs, which is actually a lot what I'm talking about.

Don't drop the story any more than it is, FC. It is the only thing keeping a lot of people around at all.
