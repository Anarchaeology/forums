Shadowlands release date: August 26th
Yesterday it was confirmed in a press release that the Shadowlands release date will be August 26th 2003, approximately one month later than first expected.

We know you want to get your hands on it as soon as possible, but we're also sure you'll be happy about the expansion pack being of absolute top quality. Hence no rushing on this. If you can't wait at all, feel free to join the beta.
