Minimap improvement!
This may have been offered up as a suggestion before, but I was thinking this might be neat.

Would it not be possible to build in a system whereby a player could enter coordinates, and those coordinates would then be "locked in" so to speak on your minimap.

You could then use your compass to navigate to the location just like a mission.

Just thought it would be cool. Imagine, you zone in & friend tells you coords, you lock them in and autorun or fly to your team.

Thoughts anyone?
