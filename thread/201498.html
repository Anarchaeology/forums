The strange medical puzzle of the 14.6 Patch
First I thought that the Nanofreak Urine was connected to HI, but now after I searched the 14.6 DB, it seems like it has some other, tradeskill connection, probably ending up being some kind of Medical item(Healing in some way), as most of these items look like they end up like that.

From what I can see, Leet Blood, Gargantula Blood Plasma and Nanofreak Urine has some common use. Their items can be combined at some point in the tradeskill, as evidenced in "Mutated Le(e)t Blood Essence" and "Strange Gooey Blood Stew".

My conclusion with this, is that this is items needed in tradeskills for making various stims and first aids.

Note that this listing is not complete, and not organized very well, but its a start, and probably easier to look up directly at the same place than clicking on each link in the database. The questions are:

-What is "Nano Robot"
-Where do you get Leet Blood, Gargantula Blood Plasma and Nanofreak Urine, not to mention...Atrox Eyes?!

Healing Leet Wax
========================

Ashes of Leet Fur
This is the powder that remains after burning the fur of a Leet. It has gone through many purification faces, and is now ready to be used in the production of medical products.
+
Ethanol
A substance believed to have been discovered many thousand years ago. It isn't known when, but probably some time in the earlier days of humankind. It has many uses within medication production
=
Leet Fur Cocktail
This is the product of mixing ethanol and ashes of Leet fur. It has a strange consistency, but it is not a finished product.

Bubbling Essence of Leet Blood
Leets have some extraordinary abilities physically, specially in concentrated form. When blood is concentrated, it automatically bubbles. It can be used as a component to make medical products under special conditions.

Bubbling Nanite Brew
This bubbling brew is the result of combining essence of Leet blood and positronic nanites. It is teaming with life, but needs something more to make it useful.

Positronic Nanites
Positronic nanites has a wide range of uses, both in the production of medication and other fields of science.

Foul Smelling Lightweight Notum Liquid
Adding the Leet blood to the foul smelling paste has turned it into a foul smelling liquid that is unnaturally light weight. Priming this item with nano robots would make it usable.

Ground Leet Bones
This is the powder of ground Leet bones. It can be added to liquids to make strange results.

Hyperactive Leet Powder
Adding even more Notum powder to the mixture has made the Leet DNA go bananas. Priming this item with nano robots would make it usable.

Mutated Leet Blood Essence
Mixing the Nanofreak urine with the Leet blood has removed some of the impurities of the blood, and has left the mutated essence of it. It cannot be used for anything as it is, but perhaps by mixing in another component, you could make something valuable.

Mutated Let Blood Stew
The combination of chopped mutant liver and Leet blood essence has turned the blood into a strange almost living organism. Quite alarming, but also quite useless. Perhaps combining it with something more could produce something interesting.

Notum Enhanced Leet Fur Paste
Adding the Notum powder to the Leet fur cocktail has produced a thick paste with quite strange properties. Priming this item with nano robots would make it usable.

Potent Leet Powder
The mixture of powdered Leet bone and Notum powder has strong effects, but does nothing on their own. It needs another component before it can become anything useful.

Scrap of Paper
The crumpled piece of paper only contains the following information: Concentrated Nanofreak Urine, Bubbling Essence of Leet Blood, Gargantula Blood Plasma.
Strange Gooey Blood Stew
The mutated Leet blood essence has turned into some strange gooey liquid now that the Gargantula blood has been added. Priming this item with nano robots would make it usable.

Gargantula Blood Plasma
Gargantula blood has a regenerating capability widely known within the scientific world. The plasma have a wide variety of uses.

Strange Gooey Leet Stew
The mutated Leet blood stew has turned into something strange and undefinable now that the powdered bones of a failed experiment has been added. Priming this item with nano robots would make it usable.

Nanite Notum Concentrate

Horrible Smelly Liquid
