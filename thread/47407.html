Anti-camp ideas! Cz, Cosmik, Funcom, read pls.
I posted this as a reply to another thread, but I really thought this deserved its own thread because I think these are decent ideas.

Cz, Cosmik, Someone, please read !

Something needs to be done about ridiculous camp fests like the Trash King. There are people standing in line with tickets to the TK spawn. Let's try and not make AO another EQ please. I have a couple ideas to avoid this mess.

Why not make the junk yard and other boss mob areas (outdoor ones) a zone that uses the same dungeon rules as Steps of Madness??? You will mitigate the amount of frustration people experience with waiting forever with their tickets for a spawn.

Another idea I thought about was to do a Final Fantasy/The Realm style encounter for bosses. If you attack/or wish to attack a boss mob, you and your team and are zoomed into a fight with just your team and the boss (like zoning into a dungeon). Everyone else will just see the team vanish after attacking the mob and nobody has to wait if they want to fight him or otherwise since they would be warped into their own fight scene.

Something like this would be nice for boss mobs only. We wouldnt want to burden the servers with this for all encounters and also deny people the opportunity to be helped by passers by.

Just my thoughts for a camp-free world. I think the first idea would be easier to go with since the base code is already there, but the second suggestion would simply rock.


Your thoughts??? Cz, Cosmik, someone at funcom??? If we don't do something about this, well you know what the result will be.....
