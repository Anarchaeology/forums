PRESS RELEASE
The Mockers ink deal with Baboons' management

The Mockers announced today that they have signed an agreement with Baboons' Nightclub Executive Manager Dave "Diamondcut" Duster and the club's new manager, Angel "Thyme" Garasha, to provide security services for the popular Omni Entertainment club.

"Due to the increased interest by a certain rogue faction in Omni Pol we have felt the need to increase security arrangements inside our club," said Thyme. "What this means is that we will be creating a unit with that single goal in mind, to keep Baboon's safe for everyone who wishes to visit. We do not believe that any of our customers should be harassed for any reason, and we will be taking measures to prevent this."

Also part of the deal will be the uninterrupted supply of food and beverages for the club.

"This is all part of our expanding operations, which stretch the length of Rubi-Ka," said Mockers boss Cyril "Trellame" Croteau. "The Mockers are a large, fixer-only organization, with a varied clientele. Despite rumors to the contrary, all of our businesses are completely legitimate."

The Mockers have placed Garret "Valaik" Clasen in charge of the security operation.

Rubikans interested in booking Baboons for events and/or parties should contact Thyme to make arrangements.
