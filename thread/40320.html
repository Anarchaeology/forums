Crippled Tab Use in Team Missions When Grouped With Both Clan and Omni
Maybe Funcom should think about changing the way you target in Team Missions when both Clan and Omni members are part of the Team. It poses big problems. It also discourages grouping with both Clan and Omni.

For instance, I am Clan. In Broken Shores Team Missions we have grouped with Omni as well as other Clan members. During the Mission the Tab Key filters through the Omni members as well as the mob we are trying to target to fight. It takes so much time you find yourself trying to manually target the mob to start attacking. Not an easy thing to do when you have a big enforcer, a big pet, and 5 other team members all attacking.

Also, a problem occurs when into the Team Mission you are confronted with Clan Mobs to kill, and you receive no experience. So you abort the Mission after you have spent a lot of time getting a group together and finding a mission and starting it.

These problems cause discrimination against grouping with Omni. Almost everytime we get a group together someone or several say "don't invite any Omnis". I hate that because it certainly does not matter to me. It is only because of these game problems that affects us in trying to play.

Please Funcom think about this.

/macro AST /assist %t
