Chimera Recruiting
Greetings.

The Omni organization Chimera, based in the Atlantean sector, is currently accepting new Warriors into our ranks.

Chimera is a group of military personnel dedicated to ridding Rubi-Ka of the radical Clans, as well as any Neutrals who would dare attempt to impede our progress. We are dedicated to eliminating any and ALL threats to the Corporation, so that we may one day live in peace without fear of attack from the terrorists of this world.

Those who join and help us do the Greater Good will be remembered for their Deeds. If you wish to assist us in Purging the Heretics from the planet, if you wish to Bathe in the Blood of Omni-Tek's Enemies; then Chimera is a group that would be proud to have you. There will one day be Confession, and True Belief. And the loyal and the brave shall stand in the Light on that day, and know that it was their deeds that brought Enlightenment to their Allies and swift Death to their Foes.

Those interested in joining the Cause should report immediately to General Healsike.

Submit to Chimera...
Fight for Chimera...
Conquer for Chimera.

((OOC- Chimera is a fairly large group (about 80) of players on the Atlantean server. There's a lot of fine individuals in this guild, all ranging from the Pre-100s to level 200. We see to it that our members have the best equipment possible, and I can say from personal experience that Chimera is a fun group to play with.

We roleplay as Zealots. IC, we believe that anything that even *remotely* threatens Omni-Tek should be dealt with in the most extreme circumstances. There's also more to us then meets the eye, but that's sort of classified at the moment

Roleplayers should be advised that Chimera is not -strictly- a roleplay organization. While the majority of us roleplay, there are some who don't. Also, we do not really roleplay with each other. You won't usually see an IC discussion or anything like that on the Chimera chat channel. We do all of our roleplay with other groups or individuals. Over Org chat, we just talk with our buddies, discuss raids, ph4t l3\/\/tz, and the like So if you're looking for a really hardcore RP org, we're probably not for you.

We do raid quite often, and as I said above, we try to get our members the best equipment available. This process can take time, however. So those who -really- want to be a part of Chimera will get their reward in the long run. It's not easy acquiring the loot everyone craves, so don't expect to join and immediately have a Heavy Notum Tank delivered to you on a silver platter. And we don't appreciate those certain individuals who like to join an org long enough to get whatever loot they were after, and then quit. We remember these people, and you can expect to be strongly disliked by most everyone in the guild when they see you again.

Also, applicants need to be level 75 or higher to be elligible to join. If you wish to join, contact Healsike in-game.

And that is the end of that lengthy post. And to any interested in joining: I hope to be fighting Clanners with you soon ))
