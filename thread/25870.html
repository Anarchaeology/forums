Changing Factions... Can't go Neutral
Most recently I have begun looking into changing the faction of a couple of my characters. When I first started AO, I went primarly clan with all my characters, thinking I could change later if I wanted to. Well yes, I can go Omni if I wish, but I can't go Neutral... why is that? Why can't I decide, "You know, this is a sicking war and I just want no part of it any more!" and go Neutral? Why? Because there is no application, no form, no implimented way to go from Omni to Neutral or from Clan To Neutral.

If you want to go Neutral, you are out of luck. Let's see a change in that Funcom... just add another application and away we go!
