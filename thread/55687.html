Armoury - Produces Weapons
Barracks - NPC Guards are "trained" and "produced" here.
Turrets - Defensive Towers
Mine - Produces either cash (each player recieves a small amount daily and the guild bank recieves the majority) OR produces notum then each player in the guild will recover nano pool MUCH faster.
