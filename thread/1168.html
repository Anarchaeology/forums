The whole point about the Amnesty is that it is a unilateral offer, from Omni-Tek, for any clanfolk to join the Company fold regardless of what may have happened in the past.

An olive branch, if you will.

What an amnesty is not, and by definition cannot be, is a "cease fire". A cease fire is a BILATERAL agreement: it has to be reached by agreement from both sides. Until negotiations have taken place between fully accredited Company and CLan representatives there can be no cease fire.

The true significance of the Amnesty is the idea it represents: that Omni-Tek's leadership see a possibility of reconciliation and are willing to take the first steps in pursuing it. Until and unless the Clans make a positive response via their elected leadership, we cannot expect the situation on the ground to improve.
