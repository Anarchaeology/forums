Cyani, I usually try to not speak out of turn, but Uwen has a point about mining speed.

Before, when OT had a monopoly on mining it was bad enough. Now everybody here can - and do - mine. This is worse.

If and when OT-RK fails to deliver _any_ notum off-planet, then ICC will come and get it themselves.
And that will make it a mad scramble of all possible corporations coming here to get the most notum in the shortest time possible, just like a swarm of locusts on old Earth. Many times worse than it is now.

This is a likely - in my mind - scenario, but it cannot be proven until it is too late for all of us.

Of course, this scenario could be avoided if humans have learned to take care of Nature while carefully extracting only the necessary resources - which we have shown in history to be singularly unable to do.
And I don't see any sign that we, here on RK, have found a way to do this.

That said, no, do not close your eyes to this mining madness.
Notum is vital to Nanomages, and for most of us living here it is not an option to move to some strange planet. This is our home, wartorn though it is.
But for any regulation to be effective, there has to be a compromise, some way to give as much as possible to all involved. And keep our world safe for us at the same time.
