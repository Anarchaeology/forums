Hmm.. yes. Well we've got a name..ST49 GuardMaster. Nice ring to it, huh? And we have a rather impressive promotional holo-vid to go with it. You might have seen a sneak preview of it at one of the recent Borealis meetings.

There are of course a lot of issues. Our main problems at the moment are with the robots deterrence systems. We don't make military bots. The challenge for RUR is to design an effective guardbot that still complies with the Three Laws of Robotics. We are looking at how Old Earth police forces in countries like Britain and Norway operated largely unarmed and we hope to come up with some imaginative solutions.

I'll ask one of our engineers to post a a full revision history of GuardMaster. That ought to give you a good indication of the progress to date.

You might find that getting the political will and unity to affect this change may prove much more of a challenge than to get the GuardMaster up and rolling off the production lines.
