Quick and dirty run through of how to setup IRC with mIRC
The following should get you connected to the Funcom IRC server if you are using mIRC:

  1. Download mIRC from http://www.mirc.com/
  2. Click File -> Options
  3. Click on "Connect" from the list on the left
  4. Change the information in the 4 text entry boxes
  5. Click the "Add" button
  6. Put in "irc.funcom.com" in the "IRC Server" field, you can leave the others blank/default. Click "OK" when done
  7. Click "Options" from the left list
  8. Check the "Connect on startup" and "Reconnect on disconnection" checkbox
  9. Click "OK" at the bottom
After you are connected, the "mIRC Channels Folder" dialog box would come up. Those are just some predefined channels for other networks. You can select all of them and swiftly click the "Delete" button to clear your list.

You should add the following channels to your channels folder list for easy access:
  • anarchyonline
  • meta-physicist
Channel names are prefixed with "#" for easy identification. You can also check the checkbox for "Join channel on connect". Then you will be connected to those channels automatically when you connect. There are a lot of other channels on it, you can click the "List channels" button to get a list.

I also suggest you to go to "Help" -> "Contents" and click on "Basic IRC Commands" for some basic commands. There are also a lot of good startup information in the help section
