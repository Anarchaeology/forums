Not enough info for new players
I've been playing AO for a couple days now and am becomming very frustrated. I am new to MMORPGs and AO is the only one I've tried so far. While I like the look and general idea of AO, I'm having a hard time figuring it out. The manual and in game help gives almost no information on weapon types, trade skills, skill use, or much anything else. I realize that many of these things can be learned from asking other players of trial and error but it would be really nice to be able to read about them in the game manual. How hard would it have been to put things in the manual like what types of weapons can be found in the game, how to use skills like concealment, or how to get implants and how to use them. The great thing about AO is that they offer a free 7 day trial but I get the feeling that I wont have the game figured out by the end of that 7 days so most likely wont keep playing. IF there is a place to read more about game features please someone let me know. I dont really feel like running around in game for hours just to figure out stuff that should have been in the manual to begin with.
