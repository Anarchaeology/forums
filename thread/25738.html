Player trading
A long time ago those merchant machines we are all so familiar with to purchase our armor guns ammo and such would rarely drop in mission chests I know this because someone I know has one.

We want player trading and shops right? it was one of the original ideas for this game so lets get these things back to dropping in the game.

A person can come to say Trade (gasp really?) and set up shop by dropping their merchant maching full of items That they have collected from missions etc that people could browse and purchase if they wanted. Just set it up so it sells and buys at some range close to or prefereably below for the sell price and a little higher on the buy price. Some variance would have to be set up for non shop buyable stuff perhaps make the player able to set the price some how. Make is loadable from inventory so you could drop it down for buisness full.
