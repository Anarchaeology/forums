BAA vs. BORPAC
Borealis Pact and Borealis Aid Accord.

One wants to halt all mining, the other gives Omni a 25% stake in Neutral territory.



With no mining, clans are on equal ground with Omni-Tek and the neutrals can remain on the side lines in the war.

With the Borealis Aid Accord, the current state of Omni vs Clan mining are equal until you factor in the extra 25% Omni is getting from Neutral Towers. Also, the situtation is volitale as Omni-Mining will not defend a Neutral Tower from an Omni led attack. So this BAA may be a smoke screen to set up the neutral territory for eventual 100% takeover.

So from a clan perspective, which is better? I say BORPACT (the
lesser of two evils).

Being a former neutral and a member of the NLF, I can tell you that the bigger threat to the clans is definitely the Borealis Aid Accord.

Please, we must find a way to eradicate the BAA!
