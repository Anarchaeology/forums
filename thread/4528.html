I don't think it's a good idea, mainly because it looks like FC is doing yet another scramble to "fix" something that shouldn't have been broken in the first place.

Take the Conceal nerf, the AS/fling/burst nerf, the Adv dual wield nerf...they did those things because players were complaining about getting ganked in PvP and complaining about balance. Well, I personally don't think a Crat should have any chance of taking out a Soldier in PvP, and I don't think a Doc should own agents...

the point is that FC is obviously scrambling to make sure everyone renews their subscription b4 the 6 month period comes, and this is a great way to give ppl something they THINK they want, when in fact it's just going to likely result in a collection of "uber" cookie-cutter characters running around using shotguns and wearing those silly cloaks...

What I mean is that it is only going to allow some folks to change their characters without actually FIXING any of the flaws in the game like MONOTONY, broken tradeskills, high-lvl game etc. The same complaints will be heard before and after the IP wipe, and if FC is really committed to the 4 year plan and not the quick fix, they'd be telling folks to relax and learn from their character building mistakes and start afresh, like most ppl I know have done. It's like any other game, learn the system and then go off and make another chr once the dust clears and charge up the levels 4 times as fast as the first chr took you...
