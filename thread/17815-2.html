All co-ordinates are approximate. Thanks to Tsannik for the information. The directions are how I got there as a high level clanner. These routes may not be for you if you're Omni or lower level.

Best In Brass:
Galway Shire, Rome Stretch. 400x750.
Grid to Galway. Head South West (I think - use the compass and F9). Watch for the omni guards loitering around.

Electro Unique:
Wailing Wastes, The Boka. 880x1370.
Whompa from Old Athens. Head west. This one wanders a *lot* but does tend to stick around 400-1000 E-W and 1000-3000 N-S area. I've spent hours trying to find this one.

Galvano:
Greater Omni-Forest, Omni Forest Grassland. 2000x2250.
Grid to Omni Ent. Exit Omni Ent, east exit, and head east (use the compass and F9).

Greasy Joints:
Newland Desert, Newland. 890x950
Grid or Whompa. Greasy is pretty close to city (can't remember which direction he is from Newland - use the compass and F9).

Nuts & Bolts:
Wartorn Valley, Junk Yard. 715x665
East exit from Old Athens. He does wander all over Wartorn - but it is a smallish zone.

Metalomania:
Lush Hills, Harry's Outpost. 2950x3250
Grid to Harry's. Metalomania tends to stay fairly close to the wall.

Trash King Lackey:
Athen Shire, Cobol Sands 1600x950
Grid to West Athens. Head west and a little south to zone. Then head west and little south through the forest and over the desert.

Live Metal:
Greater Tir County, Rocky Outcrops. 1700x2200
Exit Tir west entrance and head north and a little east to zone. Continue heading north and a little east
