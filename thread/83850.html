Regarding the mission
For the 2nd mission I suggest you have the ability to fly or it may not be possible for you to finish it. Also I strongly recommend that you bring someone with you to search for the bots. Since this is the only mission out of the full quest that you really have a time limit, (For the 1st and 3rd missions you can collect all the parts before actually getting the mission) it would improve on the time you spend looking, to have another pair of eyes on the look out. Good luck!

Regarding the TK spawn. It is my experience, though debated by many, that if you leave TKL alone and don't kill him, it will improve the chances of TK spawning. 3 out of 4 times TK spawned within 10-15 minutes after TKL because we didn't attack.

As I understand it there is a 20% chance every 20 minutes that TK will spawn. Perhaps by keeping TKL alive it increases the percentage?

P.S. For those who don't know, the abbreviations stand for the following.
  • TK = Trash King
  • TKL = Trash King Lackey
