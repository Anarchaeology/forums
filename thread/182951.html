Press Release
For Immediate Release: September 24, 29477

Schuemann and Associates' Senior Partner Severs Ties with Devil's Advocate organization

Racheal "Blackpetal" Schuemann and Devil's Advocate yesterday mutually agreed to end Schuemann's association with the organization and to end her legal retainer.

"I have been with Devil's Advocate virtually since it's founding," Schuemann said. "Nothing could be harder for me than leaving an organization that has become my surrogate family on Rubi-Ka. However, recent developments have made it clear to me that the organization, and Omni-Tek as a whole, are moving in the wrong direction. I hope to have a more detailed statement at a later time, but suffice it to say that I bear no ill-will towards either the membership or the Board of Directors of Devil's Advocate."

Schuemann and Associates was establishedon Rubi-Ka in 29475 and has been at the legal forefront of issues ranging from human and employee rights to reaching a sustainable peace settlement with the Clans.

Devil's Advocate is a world-class leader in information and data retrieval, and is recognized as one of the premier organizations on Rubi-Ka, with a diverse and talented pool of employees in all branches of corporate service.

Back in her modest office-apartment in Borealis, Betji finishes reading the press release Petal had mentioned the previous night. Her comm sounds in her ear.

Yes, yes, Ive read it. No, I dont have any official statement. I can say that she wasnt clan last night, since her redeemed garden key wouldnt work! Remember, all her friends, acquaintances and associates have omni papers. If she does change, its not going to be quick or easy.

The comm sounds again.

Yes, yes, I know my papers read clan at the moment. Petals still are omni. In fact, her redeemed garden keys and passes didnt work last night because of that. She had to terminate to get in.

As for my papers, it was simply a convenience to make my garden keys work. Its a shame that the Redeemed and Unredeemed have taken sides in the Rubi-Ka political squabbles to such a degree that garden keys are linked to citizenship papers. All this talk about personal deeds and faction levels being important seems like propaganda and JAME marketing spin. At the core, what counts is what your papers say, not what youve done. Just a moment, I have another call

Yes, its true, an attempt was made on Professor Drakes life a few days ago, and its also true that I was in the vicinity at that time. I noticed quite a few energy blast-holes in the vicinity, looking a lot like X-3 fire. It was futile, of course hes well protected. But you can quote me as saying I wish it had succeeded. That mendacious fool treats people no better than lab leets. A scientist with no regard for humanity is as dangerous as a Star-Nova device in the hands of a fanatic. Just a moment, Im on an earlier call...

By this time, all the live channels, waiting channels, and mail channels on her comm. were clogged with calls. Betji smiled, flipped open the back of the unit, and slid in a small erase chip as she toggled the afk block button. In a bit over a second, peace reigned once more.

Poor Petal, Betji ruminates, If my comm is this bad, I cant imagine what hers must be. Perhaps its a blessing that shes so busy back on Old Earth.

With his fingertips pressed down upon the upper lip framing the monitor, Gabriel shut out the information from his eyes. Yet the words still lingered in after thought from the replies that slotted in after the main headline and ghosts from his history revealed themselves once more that night to him, tearing down what safe havens Gabriel pressed himself to in times of doubt.

There was no doubt.

"There is no doubt..."

Light spilled across this skin upon his face and neck as a doorway opened and words... spoken. Moments crawled past the tensed agent as each letter from the sentances born through his ears from the entrant, began to filter into what the intruder had said to him. Taking his physical actions back to his meditational cleansing of his rifle, Gabriel considered further what could possibly befall such a being that was blind to the future, cut away from individual possibilies. All save for one.

Gabriel was to be the tool he always remembered being; one to carry the messages of finality, of an end to objectives who also held no future in mind, body... or spirit. All in accordance to the body members that led Gabriel through the pitch void he traveled in from soft slumbers and non-existant rest.

He stays with the company. There is no redemption for the unredeemable.
