This is quote from Vampi's page, which quotes the Sybex guide:

According to the Sybex guide, your chance of scoring a critical is always at least 3%, increasing by an additional 1% for every 10% by which your Attack value exceeds your opponent's evade value. So if your attack value were exactly twice that of your opponent's evade, then you have a 13% chance of scoring a critical on any given attack.
