Eleet
You hit for what is normal for your level with avarage implants.
We do hit faster than people who wield weapons, we are kind of a living dot (damage over time), so to speak.
There IS a boring period if you solo around your level, but at level 70+ you should be able to take one step higher than 50% missions, and then get better armor upgrades that way.

What about your buffs? You should have the sg buff, wooden or greater wooden skin and fists of shocking touch.
Put on all ma buffs, expertice, mastery and lesser controlled rage, and buff your brawl too with brawl expertice. The more in those skills, the harder you hit.

Have you maxed your str skill too? I believe it helps give more damage. And keep the bodydev maxed too for max hitpoints.

You should put points into evades too, that way you wont get crit hit by mobs so often. But only put into melee and projectiles evades. Put on Limbo buff for better evades too.
