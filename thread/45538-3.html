The Sleeper Awakes
Lawyers...tiresome spawns of evil, every one of them. Coporate Lawyers? They are the worst of the breed. Which of course meant that ICC Corporate Legal was the very pinnacle when you were aiming to be the Cream of the Crap. Sejanus chuckled to himself amused by the thought. He'd have to remember to use that when the Boss asked him how it went.

"I'm sorry Mr Thompon," said the ICC lawyer across the table from him, looking over his glasses like an annoyed schoolmaster. "I didn't mean for that to sound funny." The portly lawyer puffed himself up as he let his subtle sarcastic tone hang over the conference table between the two sides. Omni-Tek on one side, ICC reps on the other.

Sejanus ignored the sarcasm. Sarcasm was one of the first tools a 'crat mastered in school. He smiled and waved his hand lightly in a dismissive gesture. "Think nothing of it Mr. Beedle, no worries, you're doing just fine. Please continue." Feigned genial sincerity was something a 'crat learned later, and it was much more useful than sarcasm.

Mr. Beedle paused for a fraction of a second and went back to reading the contract agreement aloud to the assembled group. Sejanu's com-link vibrated lightly indicating an incoming message from his aid. The message read "Boss, lotta weird stuff going on, lots of activity over the last few days. Tell you about it when you get back to the office. Until then, I'd stick with secured comm only." As soon as he read that one, another message came in, this time from Jaxxa. He read the message and smiled to himself. "How do you accidentally buy two refridgerators?" He thought, looking back across the table to Mr. Beedle who appeared to be wrapping up his oration. When the meeting was finally over he hit the grid and returned Jaxxa's call to arrange a meeting.

They met in Rome Red near the wompas as the moons were high in the sky. Sure enough, two refridgerators. He'd forgotten to warn her how potent some of the drinks were in Omni compared to the watered down variety found in clanner towns. "Drinking and shopping, a dangerous combination" he cautioned her lightheartedly. She was visibly concerned about something and then she mentioned a problem with hackers...and mysterious funds transfers.... Sejanus thought to Tag's warning message he had received just before Jaxxa's. He reassured her that he didn't believe she had anything to do with the money transfer. Not because he was generally a trusting fellow...because by nature he wasn't...but because it didn't fit. The timing...the amount...the knowledge required to pull it off.... Nope, no way it could have been her. The debrief and scans, those she was aware of and unaware of, both indicated none of the skillset required to pull off this sort of job. The amount...40,000Cr.... Too large to be the start of some long term plan to suck off money over a long period of time, 40,000Cr isn't round-off error. Way too small for a grab-and-run. 40,000Cr was hardly worth the effort, he'd given her well over that from Mr. Ross. Nope, 40,000Cr was the sort of number you picked when you wanted someone to notice. Besides, why move it back and forth like that? THAT was the puzzling bit. What was this unknown hacker up to?

The "leaky pipe"? She asked.

"Still being looked at." He replied. According to Tag, nobody at GridNet would admit to knowin anything about the story. He'd have a mutual friend pay them a visit individually to see if they happened to remember something. He would have his aid include her comms and files in his investigations if they weren't already. "Too many coincidences" he thought.

He wished her well, thanked her for the gift of the fridge and started back to his office. He reached for his comm-link to call ahead when he remember Tag's warning to stick with secured comm only. He decided to do one better. He quickly and securely purged his comm-link of all data and call lists, wiping everything but the unit's identifier assigned to him. Closing it up he placed the comm-link into secure comm mode, disabled incoming calls and made a brief detour into Rome Green, glancing at the mission terminals and finding what he was looking for. "Ah," he said to himself, "a level 3 Engineer." He approached the young opifex with a genial smile.

"Welcome to RubiKa young man. Here, compliments of GridNet news for drawing the 10,000th mission from this terminal." he gave the young man a jovial pat on the back as he handed him the shiny executive class Omni-Tek Comm-Link. No charge for the first 72 hours so make sure you use it while it's free!" The young man grined a wide grin at his great fortune as he fingered the delicate and obviously expensive gift that had fallen into his lap.

"WOW! Thx man! You rock!" he said, his friends looking over his shoulder at the device. "Hey! Can I have one too?! Plz?" the others began to ask, coming up to Sejanus. "You got any armor you don't need?"

"Sorry folks, that's all for tonight at this terminal." Sejanus grinned like a used Yalm salesman. "But who knows, what some lucky person my find at another mission terminal...like in...oh idunno...Omni-Ent." He winked very obviously and they scattered...running to the wompas. The gate guards paid little attention. Once you've seen a 6 foot 400 lbs atrox dancing in a blue string bikini waving Leet dolls, not much else will phase you.

Making it back to his office in Omni HQ, Sejanus went to see Tag. He noticed a couple of lvl 200s in the office from the Data Forensics group. "So what have you got?"

"Looks like someone was poking at your honeypot, sir." Tag said.

Sejanus blink. "Tag, why is it you techie types can't seem to speak galactic standard like everyone else?"

Tag chuckled. "Sorry sir. You're publica dataset. You know, all the fake records and accounts that we setup for people like you on the public networks to give hackers something to go after...as opposed to your real records and accounts that are on the closed executive network...which of course, doesn't exist as far as anyone else knows. You're honeypot has been drawing some bees."

"Uh-huh...and?" Sejanus said.

"And, looks like we have two hackers, not just one. So sayeth the guys down the hall in security who are supposed to be good at this sort of thing. And either they are running some very complex plan that we haven't been able to figure out yet...or they are working totally independently. AND, according to our two friends here," Tag nodded to other two men. "One of them is defintely clan...or at least clan trained. The other, got Omni training."

"How do you know that?" Sejanus asked.

"Trust me. We can tell." Said the taller and wider of the two. They seemed mildly annoyed that he would even ask.

Sejanus called Tag out into the hall to talk. "When the UberTwins in there are gone, I want you to borrow some NM Fixers from OSec and put them in the Rift. I want them to just sit there quitely and look for these two like silent ugly spiders in the middle of a web, waiting for someone to jiggle the wrong electron associated with myself, Miss Craddock, or any of the files that you have found a trace of them touching. Just close enough security holes so they don't smell a trap, but leave them a door inviting enough for them to want to jiggle the handle. The NMs are to do nothing to give themselves away if possible, but I want info on these people. Work 'em in shifts if you have to but there is to always be one on duty in there. Got me? And if they're discovered and the troublemakers make a run, I want them to at least leave with one mother of a headache to remember us by."

Tag smiled. "No problem sir, consider it done."

Sejanus had a new secure comm-link setup for himself and left the office for the evening, deciding to take a walk around Omni-Ent.

Coincidences sometimes can be amusing.

This night however, it had been even more than this. It had been... how did it have begun? She had been caught in a storm again - the green ones on Broken Shores, the ion ones in Mort, and now a green again over Twili8ght Plains. Then Malcom had invited her to have a beer at Rompa's with young Corrine, so she had Gridded there in a blink, thinking that she needn't be in the Rift all day long to let the subprocess run. That hadn't begun as a very good evening, in fact. Heiji was scowling once again, making her feel really tired all of a sudden, between this and the incessant babbling around them. A little frustrated, also, at Malcom's laughter when he had told her that she now appeared as green security level to him on his datapad - darn it, he must have had trained all day long to reach his new clearance stage, but monitoring both levels of the Grid took her too much time to do the same. All in all, Heiji's simple s****** hadn't made things better; there were moments she'd have wished to be able to slap him in the face, just to force him to swallow those words back. She had spotted Sejanus in another booth, chatting with some other Bureaucrats, but talking to him now wouldn't have been a very good idea. Her friends didn't need being involved in this for the moment - not when she had such slight evidences, and no real proof between the hands.

Lazaro "Gridgrinder" Gruesbeck warily smiled back, but said nothing as he took a seat near her.
