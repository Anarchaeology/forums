Probably already forgotten in people's minds ... but the findings of my Project Fire-Breath co-relate with Xyberviri's poll (as of 4 months ago) -- one-third of Advys do not use 1HE or pistols. With patch 14.4, it's safe to say there would be a slight change in numbers but I doubt enough to radically change the percentages by a lot. One of the primary tenets behind Project Fire-Breath was always to bear in mind - that the Adventurer profession is the least homogeneous of the 12. This profession with its inherent template will tend to bring out the most variety in combat/healing styles. That naturally also includes weapon choices as a consequence.


Next, no flames, just my own observations for academic discussion.
----------
If Funcom chooses to over-emphasise pistol and 1HE any more than it has, it would, without a doubt, inconclusively stamp the Adventurer profession into the pistol/1HE cookie-cutter moulds.
However, look also at what has been introduced or what has been attempted -- advy-only Black Petes, advy-only Eye of Hunter, advy-only Heavy Chops, advy-only cat heals, advy-only cyclic shields, etc. Note also the variety of the newer non-profession-specific weapons like Zastaba, Yatamutchy X-3, the various Wind Katanas, Byom, Schuyler, KIWD, Original Electronicum, Heavy Suppressor, Light Suppressor, etc. And there are still the old weapons like True Katana, Ithaca, Home Defender, Reet-Tech, Nova-Flow, Gamma, etc.

So let's see what we have now ... leaving aside the actual availability issue and actual usefulness of the item/buff (hehe, I can almost hear some of you chuckling) ...
- Eye of Hunter can be used by any melee advy
- Cat heals can be used by any melee/range adv - they are not locked into weapon usage type
- Cyclic shields can be used by any melee/range advy (well, those who bother with damage shields)

Personally speaking, I would say that they have attempted to factor in some sort of balance when they brought in all those buffs and items - for a profession that is oh-so very not homogeneous.

This profession surely cannot be the easiest to fix/patch for. If anything, we have to keep giving them feedback as to what is effective and what is not ... for a profession that is non-homogeneous.


Next, no flames, just my humble personal opinion
----------
While I respect those who attempt to table profession buffs for comparitive reasons - I personally think that it may not be circumspect to compare buff with buff per level or QL. The main reason I think that way is because -- I am of the firm belief that the Adventurer profession is a very late peaking one, hence profession nano/buff comparisons on a QL basis can be skewed somewhat. Hear me out for a bit.

This is one profession you have to take all the way to Title 6 to truly enjoy/use all of its abiltities and features (well, yes, a few are still broken and mangled unfortunately). As rough examples, the MPs and Enforcers, as they are now, can peak before Title 5. Yes, the Fixers have also been hit with level-requirements. Their extended Suppressor line is what they badly needed - to improve attack rating (similar in reasoning to the advy 1he/pistol/multi buffs). There are 4 versions of Grid Armor, ranging from mid to high level usage. But, in the end, has their profession peak move significantly higher? Questionable, isn't it.

Now let's see - Advy wants to self-cast the top profession morph - Pit Lizard? Heh, sorry you have to level, there's no short-cut, no low-level version of a dragon available. If Advy wants all his self-cast morphs, he has to level, no choice. And courtesy patch 14.4, the top morph now has added offensive buffs (which are not locked to any weapon usage type btw). It does not matter that we happen think the top dragon morph isn't that hot after all, what matters is what FC thinks. Do you now see the big picture I am getting at?

And, oh, we can of course, keep giving them feedback about current issues.
