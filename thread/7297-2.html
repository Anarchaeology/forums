An explanation of the bombing.
Dawna, the building that you saw that was under construction was in fact being repaired after its almost complete destruction by the Dust Brigade, as explained by Omni news feeds after an impartial Omnitek investigation into the matter.

This investigation would likely involve not only forensic evidence but secondary sources like sound-recorders placed here and there in Omni - 1 for this very purpose - not, as some have argued, to illicitly tape people's conversations without their knowledge. Likewise I expect Omni-Pol had several different videos of the explosion from different angles gathered by the camera emplacements nearby - especially the large one on the sewer section separator wall near the north end Whompas, which would have recorded the exact moment of the explosion even if it couldn't get a clear view of the apartments.

Thankfully the superior construction efforts of Omni Engineering, and the generous use of nano-constructors, working in a sterile nano-pressure field which of course would restrict the entry not only of vehicles but of any form of material contamination, have been able to replicate the original building precisely and it now stands as a testimony to the tenacity and moral strength of our corporation. I for one am applying to Omni-residential control to have my assigned apartment relocated to that very building as a sign of my support of the corporation and its efforts to stand undiminished in the face of continual, though totally ineffective, clan attacks.
