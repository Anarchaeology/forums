Far out in left field or the way to go for AO?
This might have been proposed before, but I tried to find some posts about it, but no luck, so here we go....


The main issue now, seem to be the lack of confidence Funcom has with it's players and the lack of feedback to the player community. I have read a great number of posts on this board and there is a great deal of very good suggestions out there and most of them would be easy to implement into the game. (Se the bottom of this post for a link of the type of posts I am refering to.) Another problem, is the lack of content for higher level players, as well as the other players and the ramifications of this.

It is all like a vicious circle that never ends. The community cries for lack of events and a story line. Whenever a event occurs, what happens? All the high level players that are bored of nothing do, round up and smothers the event for everyone else at lower levels thru the mid-range level players and gives them a sense of beeing left out. This again makes it impossible for Funcom to make events that fit certain level ranges as the event charachters woul dbe to easy to kill. And before all the higher level players now start to flame me, relax, I understand why you do it and I would do exactly the same. It's not your fault. But what can we do to solve this? Well, in my opinion it can be solved very easy, together with the lack of things to do at higher levels, lack of content and the fact that alot of players grow tired of only beeing able to level, since there is nothing much else to do.

A well proven consept in my opinion would be to make more quests available in the game, in range from level 1 all the way to 200 as well as more static missions and a level block on the last mentioned. Meaning the static Dungeon of Steps Of Madness (For example) would have a level block of no player below level 45 and no higher than level 75 can enter.

To use this consept on all the missions / static dungeons / quests would make it more attractive for players to gain a certain level , stay there for a while to explore, do the quests before they outgrow them and it would also enhance co-operation between higher to lower levels of players, as a lower level player now might have to go and get just that special item for a high level player. This will again, in a better way than "nerfing" solve the problem of people leveling to fast, as this isa problem for Funcom, considering they had anticipated that it would take the players 4 years to reach level 200.

That is a way to solve a great deal of problems concerning content and boredom. When it comes to events, that point will prove to be a bit more difficult, since naturally everyone would like to participate and it is impossible, well almost anyways, to make a event that would fit every range of players. A possible solution to this, if applicable for the game, would be to also have a level restriction on the events. The way something like that could work would be that the zone with the current event has a "lock" on it. This lock could perhaps be no player higher than lvl 90 and lower than 40 can enter the zone while the event occurs. Of course, any player higher or lower than the given level will not be transported out of the zone, but such a thing would limit the number of higher level players at a lower level event. When trying to zone into the current zone, a message would come up informing you of this. Of course, those who has picked a mission in this area will suffer from this, as well as those enroute to a special shop, but like I said before, it is almost impossible to please everyone. Sure the higher level players will cry out at this, just as the lower level players will, when they can not participate.

I feel that this will solve a lot of the displeased community when it comes to the lack of content and events, though the remaining, such as fixking the balance between players and classes, should be almost equally as high on the list, however, the game would be , once again, in my opinion, more enjoyable, if this was to be implemented.

I also encourge you to read the boards better Funcom and dig thru all that really is not interesting (There is a lof of that unfortunatly) and find the posts that actually have a message for you and good creative ideas, from the players them selves. Posts that show what we the players would like to see.

Some good ideas there. I also think quests should be limited, so that no high-level players ruin the fun for the low-levels. There should be much more quests, more static dungeons and things to do. I mean, how hard could it be? All these buildings in the towns could housed their own special missions, with chances of dropping for instance a special ring, a scope, a recompiler, special stuff that couldn`t have been bought. Not only that, missions could be much more varied. Let`s say that three, four Omni-folks take a mission, they could get a mission where they had to blow up or destroy a clanbuilding in an Outpost or in a town. When the mission was activated, the building or item that should be destroyed could be targeted with lifebar and destroy. This could also increase the motivation when the two sides collapse, if things could be permanently destroyed, which again would change the towns. I guess it would be too hard too do for Funcom....but, it would been fun.

Closing zones however...Well. There would be very much ruckus about it, and I, would not accept that a whole zone would be closed just because there would be an event there. Of course, some will, some won`t. If I had used three weeks searching for a specific item, and finally found it in a zone that would be closed due to some event, I would have been really, really pissed off, because that is NOT how you share joy in a game. Now you may call me egoistic and selfish, but excuse me for a while. How the heck are FC supposed to keep their high-level players if they are too not only offer them VERY FEW quests, but also shut down zones and prevent smooth gameplay? If they choose to do, Final Fantasy XI would be my place to stay for the next years.
To make others enjoy the game, you can`t make it worse for others. There could be a solution though, by only making missions appear in the zones that not are closed. That way I think it is acceptable.

Oh, I am soon to check out if the zones in the west are passable. Couldn`t Funcom increase the amount of cities, spread out over the map? Make some other climates, some mountains maybe and get some variation in the climate? Oh well, just my thinking.
