again, why such healing rates and DOT... ?
After all my encounters against various mobs, I still wonder why they heal so fast (for example: if I hit them with my pistols + fling shot they gain hp over time - nonsense ! and it was stated in various old post I've seen) and why they have, even a green mob, better damage over time nanos than a NT (no idea for a doc, maybe a doc has something equal to what mobs have, but I have doubts about that, really).

Suggestion: 1) lower their heal rates - can you explain why, I mean in a logical, biological way, why they heal much faster than anyone (= players) ?
2) decrease the effectiveness of their DOT - again why so effective for NPC and not for players ?
3) decrease melee range, everybody knows that and has been hitted by mobs 10 meters away. Again it's nonsense.

Healing mobs were a huge problem when they first gained the ability to heal. Back then, they automatically fired off a heal whenever their health dropped to a certain point. It was impossible to kill them unless you had a lucky string of high damage hits. They've since been toned down and are much easier to kill than before. So I suspect that mob healing is "working as intended", as FunCom likes to say.

There does seem to be an issue, though, with mobs using nanos higher than a player could. This is aggravated by the fact that mobs are immune to debuffs and have infinite nano.
