Uprising: Or, the war to be Uber pt1
The twin suns that gave light to Rubi-ka were slowly setting over the battle scared planet. A tall black garbed figure watched the suns and wondered if this was the day. Behind him, sunlight sparkling in the setting sun a figure ran at unheard of speeds. The black garbed man turned around.
"Is it time?"
The blue faded and melted, revealing the visage of a thin Opifex. The Opifex raised up his Submachine gun and nodded. The black garbed man said only one word,"Good."

Meanwhile, across the empty wastelands and deserts of Rubi-ka, north of the Mort crater another kind of hustle and bustle was underway. Atroxes and Solitus figures, along with a few random Opifexs were polishing armor, tuning weapons and were doing inventory checks.
"Sir."
The general turned around."Yes cadet?"
"All the soldiers of Rubi-ka are amassing here and in Wartorn Valley sir, as per your commands. We have several command groups and strike forces ready to undertake the siege on both the CoT headquarters and Omni-E."
The general nodded.
"Sir?" The cadet asked.
The general turned around"Yes cadet?"
"Why this display of force? I understand that both the clans and Omni aren't at war, and you called in alot of favors to bring soldiers from both camps of the conflict together... but why?"
The general smiled."Too long the core weapons of this conflict have been neglected by both sides. All they care about is politics and feel that we're fine. We're going to show them we aren't."

A small breeze drafted from the Mort camp and the the sands shifted... a figure appeared admist the darkness, dropping her concealment cloak and sought shelter underneath the carcass of a sandworm. She raised her comm piece up to her ear and spoke softly, lest her voice be overheard by a wandering sentry.
"This is agent Dane reporting."
"This is Inter-Ops."
"The soldiers are up to something, and it's not good news for anyone sir."
"Heh, the soldiers questioning their place... I suppose we should have made their purpose to them more clear... they're simply here to die for our ideals."
"If you say so sir."
"The prototype cloak held up?"
"Yes it did sir, didn't get spotted by any leets." she grinned.
"Good, here are your orders..."

The smell was heavy in oil and machinery as Alexander stepped into the workshop. Around a drapped table the other engineers were already seated. Feeling the eyes gaze sharply on him, he couldn't but feel overwhelmed for a moment. But he was the chosen one to speak, so he held his posture.
"Greetings my fellow engineers. As you're all well aware, we aren't the true masters of devices, or even robots." There was a silent murmur of agreement amongest the elder engineers.
"A large part of this is due to the shoddy equipment of Rubi-ka, thus leading to actually creating weapons or armor, or even improving exisiting weapons far too expensive for the end product. However this is further limited due to a simple fact... the Traders of Rubi-ka have access to far superior nano formulas." Alexander shook his head. "Our strengths are further undercut by the fact we rely on another caste, the Meta-physicist to even summon our strongest creatures, the feared Slayerdroids."
The room went deathly silent...
"However... I have found a solution... the end to the reign of being reliant on the Trader and Meta-physicist..."

Just want to say that I've been keeping up to speed on this thread, and to also bring some news about Trader heals.

Currently when executing a team heal the Trader is hit with a DOT (hit with an amount of damage over time). This amount isn't all that much, and Traders can keep executing the team heals without their health getting dangerously low.

A change is going to be made to the Trader team heals. This change will mean that Traders have to wait for the DOT to run its full duration (from execution to finish) before another team heal can be executed. So, over time, a Doctor will heal for more (as should be the case). This change is scheduled for an upcoming patch soon (no, not 14.2). -Cosmik
