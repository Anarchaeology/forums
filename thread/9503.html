Looks to me like the random loot generator is working pretty well, it's just not working on the entire loot table with any given character. My experiments seem to show that different characters tend to get different things at different levels. It looks like it is basically a random number generator, but with a different seed for each character, which changes about every 5 levels or so. I would estimate that you have a pool of about 100 possible slots or so at any given 5 level stretch, maybe 5 of which are completely random implants (using the whole table of all available implants), 5 of them are armor (using the whole table of all available armor of that ql), and the other 90 or so are all static items that are chosen by your current random seed. You will keep seeimg those 90 items over and over and over if you pull enough missions, but a few levels later those items are changed. It might even be per level.

That's kinda how I think it works, anyway, if that makes any sense. It's definitely limited to only a sample of items, and that sample seems to shift every level or so, but remain a relatively limited sample. I would guess, like you said, that this was designed to promote trade, which it does. It might even be designed to give you a lower chance of getting some things that are specific to your class (like nanos), although obviously not in all situations since I have gotten nanos for my own class frequently at some points. Maybe it's just that with 12 classes, it's only a 1 in 12 chance that nanos for your class are going to be in one of the 'available nano slots' on any given level...
