Just want to add 2 things... 1 from a trader and one from a
1. MP/Engie perspective
I have a lvl134 engie that was very dependant on MPs for buffs so that i could keep up with the rest of the team on damage. Sure you can solo kill a mob 25% higher than a team your lvl but the kills happen soo slow that a slightly lower exp would give you more exp/time. I have found MPs to be either very kind or very rude when it comes to buffs. I think a lot has to do with the ignorance on a lot of players. My lvl102 MP cannot cast mochams, sorry. As an engineer pls be aware at what lvl a average MP can cast which buffs. If you can self cast a buff with relative ease, (maybe buff up to cast) you shouldn't expect anything from it. A tip is nice, but come on, money is really pointless in AO anyway.

2. Trader perspective.
I find that some lvl150 people are still ignorant on how trader wrangles work and the requirements for them.
I have full ql200 implants, and am skill capped at title lvl5 and cannot do a 132 wrangle without debuffing something. Everyone seems to think that since a lvl105 trader can do a 132, a lvl165+ trader must be able to do one without debuffing. We can't. We just don' t have to ladder up the debuffs as far. I won't even be able to cast it at lvl200 without debuffing. This coupled with what some believe of how little of a hassle it is to go out and debuff something, makes a lot of traders seem like a-holes. When you can first do the 132 wrangles it was fun to help people out and make a little side cash by advertising. As you get higher in lvl and money becomes so abundant that it is only horded for rare items, a 132 wrangle becomes worth millions to us instead of 200k (that the lvl110ish traders are charging). phew...
sorry for the rant.

For my MP i will never charge for self cast-able buffs unless they want to pay a tip.
For wrangles i ALWAYS do the wrangles i can without debuffing for free. always.
If i have to debuff to cast what you want, i will respond with "not doing wrangles at the moment sorry". You can try to persuade me with a VERY large tip (I really don't need 200k so don't bother) but yelling and screaming at me isn't going to get you a anything but a /ignore. 132 wrangles are a hassle to me more than a "money cash cow" like some people think.
