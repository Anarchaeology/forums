Generally, they get hard at title caps, most noticably 85ish-100 and 130ish-150. Thats simply because you can improve the character through raising (green) skills. Thus, a crat is stuck at lvl 149 with the calms/charms he used at lvl 125, an MA is stuck at lvl 99 with the MA skill he had at lvl 82 etc.

There are also some "gaps" in nano-lines for the various professions; MA's as an example have MA buffs of +12 / +20 / +60 / +140. Just before you can use the +60 MA mastery, its a bit tough, same with the +140 one. This can be helped with getting special items and/or buffs, but it hurts nonetheless.
