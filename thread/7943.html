This is how I deal with the first room...
Its not trick, just a pain in the @$$. Enter the mission....scout first room (map reader upgrade: monster = very helpful). Attempt to pull one of the monters into entrance...usually doesn't work. Do what damage you can and run out before you get below 30% health. Then heal and go back in...

Three things will have happened. 1) All mobs are gone. 2) All mobs are still there. 3) One mob is still there in the entrance room. If 1, repeat pull and run. If 2, try to do as much damage to one mob as possible and run out again. If 3, nuke that bastard until he's dead and repeat for other two mobs. Once that is clear you move on and clear mission normally.

This worked for me, I went from lvl30 to lvl 42 in two or three days or 'light' gaming bye doing this on 65-75% missions. Have fun, and invest in some treatment kits.....

Soleet
About the borgs, I think you guys have misunderstood. There are borgs in greater tir county(NNE of the city)they are lvl 70ish. to get there, go west from the temple, it is a fairly long walk if you don't have a plane. I was there last night. team of 5 from lvl 49-56. We had no problem. XP isn't great (2500 average) and the spawn is kind of slow. There is a static dungon there, but it is quite harder, especially if you have a wonderer on the team. You'll need to pull no more than two at a time. They do drop cyborg armor though, which isn't bad stuff. It is dangerous inside the dungeon though, and the xp isn't worth it, just go there if you want borg armor or a change of pace. For your level, I'd reccomend the Shades that the earlier post was referring to. About 4500 xp per kill, but it is over camped, safe though if you don't get adds. The best I know of is the vultures. They hit hard, but die quick. You need a good tank and a decent healer. they give 5500-7000xp per kill. Just stay away from the sand worms. That is an ass whoopin' just waiting to happen. The bridge bully gives about 4500xp, but is too boring, sit, wait, shoot, heal, sit wait, ect. And it is way over camped.

a seldom used area that Ive found is in Varmint woods. take west gate from tir, go way down road (save at OP on road). Continue on road till you zone to VW. Go down road in VW for about 2km. There are orange and red (to low 50's) OTs all over there. Have a good puller though, and select target carefully, the add really easy. They drop some nice weapons sometimes. Also, you can continue on that road until you get to impact crater site area. Go south about 200 meters. good orange mobs there too. Don't go inside of impact crater. Blaster Beetle will kick your ass. He is for teams of 65+. I fly around exploring a lot, so I know some other areas, but I prefer to keep those between me and my teammates. My suggestion is to do ql36 missions constantly until you find Yalm in chest (36 is the first ql of yalms), that is if you don't already have one. Me and all my regular teammates have gotten ours this way. Then just go fly everywhere. Also, you may want to check the mantezes at Poole in Galway. hunted there full team at lvl46, may be good for small team in low 50's Haven't been back.

One last thing. If you go down the road in VW from GTC. Be careful! There is a unique mob on the side of the road named Jack "the leg chopper"Menendez. He is right on the side of the road and is aggro. Go WAY around him. A full team of lvl70+ people will have their hands full with him.

Still, the best spot is one you find on your own that no one (that you know of) knows about. I have two spots like that and my teams always enjoy plenty of room with no other teams around.

good luck, hope this helps
