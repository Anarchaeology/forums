You, you were talking about the end of the world...
SPARTA
Omni-Tek Department S1

Mission statement:
To ensure the security of the Omni-Tek Corporation of Rubi-Ka against all threats, internal or external.

Who are we?:
Department S1 (or SPARTA) is unaligned to the company's core branches, instead serving, supporting and enforcing the direct orders of the CEO's office with unquestioning loyalty.

SPARTA is Omni-Tek's security intelligence department. Its function is to ensure company security from internal and external threats such as terrorism, espionage and the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction, to safeguard its economic well-being from the anti-competitive actions of corporate rivals, and to act in support of the various law enforcement departments in the prevention and detection of serious crime.

SPARTA's key responsibility is for security-intelligence work, which is defined as; collecting and disseminating intelligence; investigating and assessing threats, and working with others to counter them; advising on protection, and providing effective support for those tasks; and where necessary conducting operations independently of support, to further the companys security interests as the director's office sees fit.

In addition, the department provides security advice and intelligence briefings directly to the CEOs office to help reduce the companys vulnerability to all threats.

The department's core officers began arriving on-world 8th September 29475 carrying orders from the Omni-Tek Corporation on Omni Prime to form Department S1 and undertake the above mission.

In February 29476 SPARTA became one of the founding members of the Omni-Tek Security Council (OTSEC).

How do you fit in?:
Whilst we have an obvious emphasis on agents, we are actually always open to all professions, and your potential is far more important to us than your previous experience.

Recruitment can start as early as security clearance level 15, though we pride ourselves in only accepting the very best people, the very best Omni-Tek employees as candidates for interview.

So if you believe you fit this description, if you believe you have something to give back to the corporation above and beyond what the average company employee does, if the below organisation content interests you...
  • Events & Role-play
  • Teamwork & Organisation
  • Co-operation & Political Credibility
  • Storyline involvement & Omni-Tek event participation

...then get in contact.


Security is everyone's concern.
