Roleplaying : Capturing enemy prisoners
I would like to propose 2 new nanoes that will enabled the capturing of organisation members, for the purpose of roleplaying.

The first nano is called "Capture Prisoner", is general and can be cast in PvP areas by anyone. In a fight between omni vs clan, this nano will be cast on the enemy and enabled as soon as the enemy dies.

Casting time is 20 seconds and it should last for 20 minutes, being recastable.

The nano requires a group of at least 5 members to be in effect. This to secure misuse by single persons.

A "capture prisoner" nano will automaticly cease to work, if the prisoner is being taken to non-pvp area.

The captured prisoner will act like a robot, and will react to orders like /pet follow, /pet wait, and /pet release


The second nano is called "Release Prisoner", is general and can be cast by anyone at any time. This nano will stop the effects from an active "Capture Prisoner" nano. Casting time is 20 seconds.

The structure, organization, and community of roleplaying should not be the point of hard code, but rather should be the point of the people themselves. You can't make people roleplay, but you can encourage it to a certain point. To clarify I provide a reference of a MUD called dragon realms (www.dragonrealms.com) now, in this MUD, people by rule usually roleplay.. and its told in the intro screen to their new character that they should roleplay, and that it is required. It's something that the whole community upholds.

Now when a newbie wanders in and starts acting in a fashion that is not in keeping with some form of roleplay, then they are usually ignored, or usually if they are dead, the last person to get resurrected. so really, its up to the members to do it, and can't be something that is enforced with real code. It has to be community run.

The population is about 1/40th the size of AO, which I don't have to say is a great deal bigger than your average MUD's population, but a great deal smaller than AO itself. The unique thing is that they are actually able to advance beyond the genre of hack and slash.. and actually utilize some rather successful methods of advancement unique to their trade.. but anyway thats another post..

But anyway getting back to this idea of the nano that captures someone as a prisoner. Although it sounds like a good idea at first, the only problem is, as of now and probably as of in the future there will be no information so important as to make someone talk. It's been theorized quite a bit that we are all just "extras" in a larger story and we have no real affect on the main storyline.. so theres no real benefit or urgency to actually capture opposing operatives.. Plus the idea of insurance technology, really nullifies this whole idea of taking prisoner.

Let's just assume for the moment there is information worth keeping.. organization "A" captures operative from organization "B" using this nano formula. Ok so we got him.

1.) what is to stop him/her/it from /terminate
2.) He/She/It can just say, 'suck me' till he/she/it is blue in the face. You can't hurt him/her/it in any way. And unless he/she/it is a dumbass to begin with he/she/it wont say anything.
