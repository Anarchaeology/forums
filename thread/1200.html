non combat tradeskills
Ok, what do I mean?

I've often wondered why we couldn't pick up some trade skills that aren't all related to nanos and weapons and the like.

I'd love to be able to come up with a viable tradeskill to make money with, such as a dye maker (make clothes and bags so you can change the color with dyes), or painter, sculptor, or tailor or the like.. even if you didn't want to make money, it would be a great RP feature..

I don't think it should cost a lot of IP or anything to get into a tradeskill like this. It would more be just for fun and to pass the time.. then again, some enterprising person could become the next Picasso and sell his paintings for mucho creds..

There are probably a lot of neat trades you could implement such as those I've listed..

Anyway, just an idea..
