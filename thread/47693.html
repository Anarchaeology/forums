Here is what I am thinking, after looking at some numbers today:

First of all, yes, the QB is horribly horribly underpowered, and I apologise for that. The Queen Blade is meant to be a slow, heavy hitter that doesn't rely on crits for the bulk of its damage.

All calculations based on having 1023 attack skill, the same inits, and 100 aggdef (ie no FoB running). Also not taking AC into account. All numbers were based on the average hits, plus various critical hits (between 0% and 30% crit chance), on a per-minute damage calculation.

New stats will be something like: 320-1003 (66), speed of 400/720

Weapons I compared with (all level 200): True Katana, Sledgehammer of Doom, Support Beam (comments on that later), and the Edge of Tarasque. The EoT is supposed to be the top of the line weapon as of right now.

Given the same attack rating, this makes the QB better than the true katana at low crit levels, with the true katana edging past at crit levels of around 30-ish. Given that the QB also buffs Life and has a proc, that is OK. The sledgehammer trailed the TK, but not by a whole lot; and moves fractionally ahead of the QB at 30% critical. The EoT is always better, because you are getting a whole lot more hits in, and really pulls away from the QB, TK and sledge as the crits go higher. As everyone knows, the Beam is very good. Very, very good. Perhaps too good It is only marginally worse than the EoT at 30% crit.

So, it will end up with a nice average damage, and excellent minimum damage, but still be fairly slow, and be quite comparable with other weapons around it, apart from the beam and EoT.
