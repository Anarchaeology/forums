Evil in the wind..
Cherelle Nursingevil McChristion Knew she was dying, Her medical training told her the various injuries her body had endured to get this far. The Ironic thing is that shed endured it to betray those whod give her enough Monies to retire in style if she wished and here she was with three weaponsfire wounds trying to get to someone she could trust to get the word out to people who could stop her Killers. The two Bullet holes in her lower abdomen and chest were the fatal ones over time but they didnt hurt as bad as the Radiation like burn wound on her right leg. The burn flesh there cracked and bleed with every step and agony spread in waves thru her body which each step.

But she wasnt going to give up. Not now..Not ever. She had to warn people about the weapon she had helped to make. She had to make sure it never could be used otherwise shed never be able to live a life of ease if she is hunted for all time. So despite the pain and agony she moved onward. She reached the town of Hope in Mort, Somehow she had evaded all the creatures that would have killed her without challenge that would have sent her back into the clutches of her Employers' She staggered past the gates limped to the whompas. She dares not use the Insurance terms here theyd know shed gotten out safe and would hunt her down. She had to get word out and then hide, which was her only hope.

The whompas took her Borealis eventually; She was where she needed to be. Now to find a member of the Nanomage Liberation Front. She may despise the appearance of the hairless Freak shows but she could trust that at least that crew of People would want to stop a weapon designed to kill all Nanomage Breeds on Rubi-Ka.

Cherelle Nursingevil McChristion
Merc Medic for Hire.
Everything has a Price.

She remembered finding Ramiyama of the Nanomage Liberation Front and telling him about the weapon. He helped her with the pain with some pills and tired to use a hunk of notum to help the bodys nanos speed heal her wounded leg. She had slapped the notum hunk away twice before he understood that the weapon she had help design and build was a Nanobot that used Notum itself to replicate more of Nanobots and then generate heat to the point where the nanobot explodes killing surrounding cells. She had designed it to be a pinpoint killer of cancers and cells infected with viruses. But her pinpoint nanobombs have been turned into a horrid weapon. While the weapon would only sicken and perhaps in some cases cause death in the Soloitus, Opifex, and Atrox Breeds the nanomage breed with theyre bodies natural reserves of notum would be killed as the nanobombs had all the notum they needed to replicate to critical mass levels.

She told him what she knew about her Employers, which was little. She never asked but knew that they had come from offworld and even though the gear and boxes had Omni-Tek Logos they werent Omni-Tek in an Official way. She told Ramiyana about the Base in Mort and how ecstatic her Employers where to find out that there was a Notum rift nearby for them too gather notum for theyre own uses and riches.

She told him of Mr. Johnson the man in charge of the group and told of Sam the nanomage who helped her make the Nanobots and how he died when they tested them on him, as he didnt want them used as a weapon. Shed have Nightmares forever from that. Watching him slowly melt to death. That was her fate she told Ramiyana as she looked at her leg where theyd shot her with a ball of goop that was infested with the nanobombs. The wound showed signs of the slow destruction of her tissues via burns so small it was impossible for the naked eye to see.

Ramiyana arranged for her to be transported in secret to an Apt where she could recover from the wound. She told Ramiyana the sign for the Notum Rift and its ID number. The last bit was that her Employers had a hold of her Insurance records and she couldnt die without returning to the base. She couldnt reinsure elsewhere, as theyd know where to start looking for her again. Ramiyana brought her some clothes and some water before leaving to let her sleep.

When she was sure Ramiyana was gone for a good while Cherelle sat up and opened her backpack and pulled out her medical kit and got out two catheters. She rolled up her shirt and exposed the two bandages shed covered the Bullet wounds with in her torso. She removed the bandages exposing the puckered Black sores that oozed black clotted material. She inserted the catheters and let them drain out the rotted tissue and the nanobombs that the bullets delivered into her when she was shot.

Cherelle drank the water shed been given and waited till the catheters stopped draining before removing them and cleaned up the mess. When done she curled up on her unwounded side and tried to sleep. But sleep didnt come easily; she couldnt help but see herself slowly melting as Sam had.
