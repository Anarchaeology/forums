static dungeon idea
I was thinking that perhaps a static dungeon located in OMNI HQ and one in TIR city would be a nice idea.

Well and not just any old static dungeons.

Make these dungeons multi leveled ala a team mission and well.........make the final level contain none other than phillip ross (HQ) and radiman (TIR)


IN order to make this feasible make the dungeon mobs extremely difficult 150+ players should have trouble getting through.


construct about three- four large levels based on present mission schematics.

After these four levels are cleared make 12 single room levels
one containing a super duper version of each profession i dont mean bulk rate mission mobs make these super guys act like real players in their abilities and actions ie
1st level you face super adventurer
2nd super agent
etc ...etc...

make the drops on these super bosses rare i mean rare to the point that this is the only mob that drops this loot (preferably a unique nano crystal or disc)
lets say super adven drops a unique morph or a disc to such
super agent drops a super concealment or crit nano or disc..

etc..etc..

and if you survive the super bosses the next level is radimans or ross's office and well you get to kill them (hey the respawn like the rest of us so no permanent loss to leadership and/or story)

the reward for making it to the top and killing ethier radiman or ross should be something unbelievable
like i dont know a super IP ring( off the top of my head something better maybe i dont know though) or weapons of unbelievable power for the team/teams invovled.

also if radiman or ross is killed a message should be put out on a universal channel (much like server notices) that radiman and or ross has been killed to effectively hurt the opposing factions morale and raise their own
as a reward for the victors in the static dungeon

maybe make it a 25% zone so players could persue would be attackers into the dungeon to ward off an attack and oh yes make the mobs only attack those of opposite side to facilitate this but of course dont make it possible for players on the same side to walk through the dungeon unscathed and kill the leaders for loot ( obviously)

the dynamic that these types of dungeons would create would be one of massive team work and perhaps some pretty epic battles as it will take a lot of players tro make it to the top of these dungeons

and yes lag will be an issue but i hope that can be factored in and perhaps mitigated

the main thing is that these dungeons should be incredibly difficult to complete and have lots of variety (super bosses and such) being that they would be the pinnacle of achievement to kill the opposing factions leader

with these man we would have a grand goal to attack not just shooting each other for titles in PvP zones.

anyway feasible or not i think it is a good idea


thanks for reading

Whatever they do... There are a few things they must do...


1. Make it 25%.

PvP dungeons are the closest things we have to controlling territory as the omni and clans struggle for control of the boss mob loot.


2. Remove the camping, this can be done with a combination of things:

--The boss mobs must drop their unique loot everytime.

--The boss mobs must spawn at least every 10-20 minutes. This bullsh1t with day long spawns must stop.

--Put a quest timer on each player. If they pick up a quest item then they cannot pick up another one for a set time (anywhere from 24 hours to a week, to a month)

Combined with the fast spawns this will clear everyone out of the dungeon at a nice pace so that everyone gets to expierence the dungeon the way it was meant to be expierenced.
