A request of Lord Galahad
Lord Galahad, I'm not sure you've noticed a looming problem in your Inferno armor sets. It seems that the trademark armor of the Knights is wearable by Omni-Tek employees.

I'm sure this was an attempt to keep it in the hands of the Knight Clan only, but it is past its time.

I request of Lord Galahad to make his Clan armor available to Clan members in exchange for armor of the Dark Knights. Similar to the exchange the Sentinels have set up.

Allow all the other smaller Knights Clans to stand up in support and identification of Clan Knights. I also can't bear the thought of Omni invaders marching into Avalon wearing Clan armor if this is not stopped. Allow Clan members to be seen as Clan and march in support of you in Knight colors!

Your friend,
