The easiest way to get involved in roleplaying is join one of the RP guilds. Its not obligatory, but the guild structure does help maintain some measure of continuity.

There is a (incomplete) list of roleplaying friendly guilds at the following thread. How each guild interprets as "roleplaying" will vary. For some roleplaying equates to PvP. Other guilds try to emulate table top or Live roleplaying. For some roleplaying is purely social, going to parties and hanging out in bars while others aim to strike a balance by doing the usual levelling stuff, but staying in-character while they do so.

OOC

I found it pretty easy to get into. When i started this character i'd never played Everquest or anything like that. Never done pen and paper stuff either.

I just decided i was gonna create an Adventurer and roleplay a nutty explorer

It can be difficult at times when everyone is talking about the real world but i did my best to stay in character, and i ended up quite a few times sitting by a reclaim chatting in character to other reclaimers about what had just killed me this time.
It was at these times when i could really properly roleplay my character. Telling them about a fantastic new creature i'd just discovered for myself - then got killed by it. A few times the other person wasnt a RPer, but when they saw i was RPing they tried their best to be a part of it, which was a nice thing i thought (Either that or they were just humouring me )

After a bit of this i realised i need to join more people who RPed cos there was never enough about to keep it going.
Thats when i applied for the MD and after i was accepted into the ranks ive never looked back. Its been fantastic. I can still RP my explorer ... but now im the wild explorer who is trying to adapt to military life aswell

Anyway (i started rambling there sorry ) .. the point of this is, create yourself a character and do your best to RP all the time. Then when ya feel yer ready look for a guild that is heavily based around RP. Then hopefully things should turn out ok and you'll have more fun

I guess Rompa Bar in Onmi Ent is the closest thing to a roleplayers hotspot. I am not a great fan of the place, the /ooc socialising seems to crowd out the roleplaying and it can get a bit sleazy. You can also check out Baboons (the room behind the dance floor) also in Ent and Reets Retreat In West Stret, though these can be quite empty unless a planned event/party is going on.

Ultimately I do not think there are any short cuts. Powergamers have it easy, they can just meet up and go hunting. Roleplayers need a context to roleplay in. It just not enough to say "Hi, I hear you are a roleplayer, I roleplay too, lets roleplay together!" In the long run you need something to roleplay about, or the conversation goes stale pretty quickly, which is where roleplaying guilds can offer that extra bit of context.

As for other RP boards, there were a couple created when FC had shut down its boards, but now this is pretty much it.

Anyway if you wish to check out our org, the URL is in my sig.
