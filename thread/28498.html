1) Atroc docs are very viable, just not top-of-the-line

2) Advantages of nanomages are pronounced and they all come from the specials they and they alone can wear (combined a little with the int/psy bonus). These things make a difference to a doc, 1K hp at lvl 200 against 100 NP? I'll take the NP. With a 1K token board I would be able to self-buff 5.5K+HP at lvl 140. I don't think I need more, At lvl 146 with MKII armor HP will be 6K+. Wearing Q190+ Flowers now, so the str/agi deficiency is easily combated with a few implants and the AC's are more then high enough.

I think the point in this this thread is a bit turned around. People say solitus would be a better caster class because they have more HP. I think this is flawed reasoning. Casters are casters and not damage takers. As a caster I want to get as much out of my (doc) class as I can at any given level. In my book this doesn't contain being able to tank vets/real means and Aces eventhough I could if I wanted to. It means (for me) being able to heal as good as any doc at my level could, twinking rather biomet/matmet/t&s then HP/AC/damage output.

If you look at the nano breed specific specials (vest/shades, for instance) they are heavily geared towards casting and upping int/psy. The vest (20) and shades give 30 points at least to any given nanoskill (xcept treat and FA). Don't tell me this is only a trivial advantage because it makes a huge difference. Combined with nelebs robe and 2 eggs (non breed specific) it can go up to 45+ points! Of course you will pay some in AC, but noone ever said casters should be front line characters. Everyone dies on occasion. Casters just can't take much of a punch (exagerated imo). I think it would be smarter to learn to control aggro (if your HP's aren't good enough to take any form of punishment, which they should be) then to blame the breed you play in being inferior at taking damage since that is obviously not what they were meant to do.

I love my nanomage and I was to reroll my main, I would deffinately go nanomage again, heck, I think I would go nanomage for every high nanoskill dependant class just for those bonusses. HP are useful but not all defining with mobs dropping in 10 seconds and still, I think 5.5K selfbuffed at lvl 140 is more then enough to counter anything I come across besides huge dragons and rediculously sized mantisses, but then again, is a doc/MP/NT meant to tank those? I think not.
