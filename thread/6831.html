Player to Player trade terminals
Dunno if this has been posted before, but:
I have for a week, tried to trade some pants with a friend of mine.
Only problem is, he lives on the other side of the world, so we are never on at the same time. I'm on - he's on java, and the other way around.
So, my idea is to make player-to-player trade terms.
Like a bank. U open it up, insert an item, type name of player and price. Then, when he logs on, he can go to the term and get the item. He just has to pay the machine for it, and the creds are transfered to me.

Is this hard to implement? Or is it just an upgrade of the bank term?
Would like a FC answer on this on.....like we all do
