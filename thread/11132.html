Suggested/Proposed/Possible Fixer Features
I know that all of these won't happen, but some/most would be cool.

1) Grid Flight - the ability to fly around in the grid, only works inside the grid, must be cast prior to entering the grid.

2) Supply Line Hack - add the supply lines high overhead in the grid, so that with grid flight (see 1) a fixer can fly up to them or terminals for them and attempt to hack into them to obtain items, credits, and exp (like converting monster parts). Failed attempts can result in the loss of some life/credits. Success shouldn't cost anything. You can put various Quality Level Supply Lines at different levels inside the grid (if Grid Flight is a ql 90-100-ish nano, then the levels would make sense).

3) Grid Armor Change - put the lower level ones in the store, make the higher level ones just like normal nanos (mission items, etc.), and balance out the matcret requirement a bit.

4) Fix the Root/Slow nanos so that they work on MOBs like they work on players, unless the MOBs are auto-casting debuffs to remove the roots from themselves.

5) Summon/Pull Nanos - to pull someone from the grid to your location from your team.

6) Catch Up/Locate nanos - to allow you to teleport to the location of another person in your team.

7) Put into grid - to allow you to pick someone to put into the grid from your team (you don't need to go yourself or put the whole team in).

8) Confuse/Mass Confuse - confuses the MOB or MOBs so that they run around lost for a bit, possibly because they are partially in the grid. This should work like a calm nano and break the attacks, but not root them in place.

9) Damage Buffs - can be self only if you like, but just something to give us half of what soldiers and agents can do, perhaps call it a Spray with Bullets nano. Hey, maybe if can also stun things.

10) Un-wrangleable Buffs - some skills can't be wrangled up, perhaps the Fixer could have a line of nanos that would increase some of these skills for a short period of time.
