Hello everybody!

I just got myself a mezz pet after reading through this thread. I wanted to check it out for myself and draw my own conclusion.

I must say that my third pet strikes me as something positive and possibly very usefull! I only have a low ql one, at around ql 42 (I am lvl 54), but it is still green to me. And its effort is quite obvious. When I set the mezz pet on [Guard], and I get aggroed, I can clearly see the mob is quite confused. The mob starts to change targets a lot, or reselects targets. If I leave it on guard, it continually works the target if I have gotten aggroed. This is kinda useless, and even dangerous as it could get me or my teammates unneccessairly killed. BUT if I tell it to [mezzpet-behind] after it successfully managed to make the mob switch target from me to my attackpet or another tank, a lot of the time the mob sticks with its new target. And that makes me happy

As for when the Doc gets aggroed, I just cast a couple of debuffs on the mob and I normally get insta aggroed. Since I have the mezzpet on guard, it will immidiately respond by trying to make the mob switch target, and since I also have my healpet on myself, I can stand a fair bit of beating. Normally my attackpet gets the aggro back within a couple of attempts of my mezzpet and my attackpet buffs. Distance between the mob and myself should also be taken into account, as I find myself often reselected by the mob if I stand too close. But my findings show that it isnt really a problem as long as I just stand in the other end of the room, or even better - in the next room (as long as it isnt crowded with Hardned Scoundrels or something)

I would expect even better results with a higher ql mezzpet!

To those of you who said that the third pet is useless, I ask: On what grounds do you base your conclusion?
If you use it in the manner I did, I would think you would agree with me that the bonds between our pets are kinda similar to the bonds between a tank and a doc? The tank leeps into danger because he has a doc backing him up. Now WE got both a healpet, means to controll aggro, AND ourselves if the situation gets interesting. Isnt that positive? Of course it doesnt work as planned all the time, but what in Rubi-Ka does?!

I also usually stick my healpet on myself all the time, and only stick it on my attackpet in emergencies. This is because of the healpet's unwillingness to leave its target untill the target's HP is fully restored (+ a couple of seconds). If i stick it on my attackpet from the start, and there are adds, the adds might stick on me, while my healpet is stuck on my attackpet (which is taking damage, but not as rapidly as me) and my healpet starts blabbering something like "Oh sorry, my dear Mr.Master! I'm afraid I have my tenticals full already"...

I have found that it is better that my attackpet dies than me. If it dies, my healpet normally lasts long enough for me to recast my attackpet. Normally that is... You could also experiment with terminating your attackpet just before it gets killed - and recast immidiately, or if you feel like it, shift the healpet from yourself to your attackpet when the a.pet is at 50% hp. (This is just my humble experience as a meta to those of you reading this who might be newbies.. I wish someone told me this when I was low level and all green - or gray to you!)
