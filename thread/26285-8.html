The guy that is really motivated and wants to spent the time doing it, can swap out implants from different slots that are a QL or 5 higher until he gets the desired result. The only difference is you will not be able to do it with store bought implants since they will be to far apart in QL. So the uber twinks will still exist and own everyone in PvP, the only difference is that the regular player who does not want to spend days literally finding the correct QL implants in missions to pull this off will be left out in the cold.

All Funcom is doing with this change is making it harder on the regular guy who likes to engage in some casual OEing to further his/her enjoyment of the game. If they really wanted to limit folks, they would just hard cap the level of QL you were allowed to wear at any given level. Of course if they did that, it would make their entire skill system look pretty pointless.
