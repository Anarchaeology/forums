Actually, they do not have to be the only self inflicted dot or negative nano of any sort. Cast the same debuff on yourself, pick any one you'd like right out of the general booth. Like every single other nano in the game, it will refresh or overwrite itself. It's the way nanos work.
They are the only DoT directly tied to a simultaneous benefit. And normal debuffs/DoT nanos aren't applicable because the person executing the nanoformula isn't the intended target. Of course there aren't any complaints if a debuff refreshes itself against an enemy. Normal DoTs do not stack because it would be unbalancing for Doctors and Agents to chain cast them against targets.

Trader DHPs, however, carry a benefit along with the penalty: healing. The penalty is the price of the tighter healing range than all other nanos in the game. The higher HP cost to ourselves than actually healed is also the price of the tighter healing range than all other healing nanos in the game.
