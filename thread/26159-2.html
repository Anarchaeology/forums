Red Mobs
Agents Aimed Shot doesn't work against red mobs.
Adventurers weapons do minimum damage against red mobs.
Bureaucrats usually can't calm or charm red mobs.
Doctors can't heal damage as fast as red mobs hand it out.
Enforcers can actually hurt red mobs - and almost, but not quite, have enough hit points to survive going toe-to-toe with them.
Engineers get crushed the first time a red mob hits them instead of the bot.
Fixers are way too fragile to fight red mobs.
Martial Artists do minimum damage against red mobs - but at least their minimum damage has been brought up a bit.
Meta-Physicist heal-pets get swatted like flies by red mobs.
Nano-Technicians hurt red mobs just enough to invite counter-attack, and die on the first return hit.
Soldier weapons do minimum damage against red mobs.
Trader shotguns hurt red mobs just enough to invite counter-attack, and die on the second return hit.

And pretty much all of that is how it's supposed to be. You're not supposed to be fighting red mobs safely. That's why the status bar "cons" them as red.

Having played all 12 professions, I've come to the conclusion that the reason I'm having fun but so many other people are whining as that people are convinved that if they can't solo a red mob, their profession is gimped.

Nobody is supposed to be able to solo a red mob. Red mobs that are barely red are supposed to be able to be fought by full teams of six, or well balanced teams of four or five.

If nothing goes wrong, Meta-Physicists can solo red mobs ... from around level 15 to around level 45, and probably never again thereafter. So can a few of the weird hybrids, like Shotgun Doctors (maybe) and Shotgun Martial Artists (maybe). A massively, massively twinked Enforcer can probably continue to solo red mobs, taking advantage of armor and weapons far above his level and the fact that 2HB weapons have very high minimum damage.

But I think it's unfair to hold every other profession up to that standard - not least of which because come 14.2, those massively twinked Enforcers are going to be just as unable to solo red mobs as everybody else.

FAWF: Fixers Against Whiny Fixers
Look, I don't want to get dragged down into number crunching game mechanics in this thread, but show me a shotgun with a 1.0 attack speed like the MPS, or even a 1.5 attack speed like the Sol Chironis.

And if you compare them at similar skill levels (not QLs), you find that the Mausser Particle Streamer does more damage per shot than either the Vektor ND or the Nova Flow Mark IV, at half again the speed, with similar crits.

I have a level 55 Martial Artist with maxed out & triple-implanted MA skill. I have a level 51 Fixer with maxed out & double-implanted SMG skill, using an MPS that's actually about 4 levels below what he could equip. Both are wearing damage buff rings totalling about +10. The MA is using higher damage buffs than the Fixer. And the result in my experience, against the same mobs, is that the Fixer does more damage per shot, gets more specials, does as much damage per special, and all at the same speed as the MA. And the MAs are advertised (falsely) as the "premier damage dealers."

And with that 1.0 attack speed, every point you put into Ranged Init means you can move the agg/def bar just that little bit more to the left, upping all your defensive skills with just one skill.

And until all professions get up to about 1000 init, that 1.0 attack speed gives you the same advantage it gives adventurers and martial artists, namely, the maximum possible chance to interrupt the other guy's attack.

And no, not just anybody can blitz missions way above their level. Enforcers maybe. Adventurers, yeah, up to a point. Anybody else? How? Not enough defense skills, not enough run speed, and/or not enough body dev.

Now, you're absolutely 100% right about the near total lack of hackable objects in the game, and how badly that sucks. But given that hacking cuts the nano skill requirement for an object by about 40%, this sometimes does bring the requirements down to below when those professions can afford the same nano. Too bad there aren't more of them, at least in blister pack form where it wouldn't be as imbalancing.

And you're absolutely right that by the time a fixer puts maxes out the essential skills, he can choose between either having good but useless tradeskills or minimal but somewhat useful nanos. (Hint: Take the nanos.)

I thought about mentioning kiting and RD, but both struck me as too technical for a flippant and intentionally brief summary. (And agents can kite, too.)

No profession, not even adventurers, agents, fixers, or NTs are totally crippled. Imperfectly balanced, yes. Underpowered compared with enforcers, meta-physicists, and now maybe traders, yes. But there are 200th level characters in all 12 professions now, and they didn't all exploit their way up, probably not even most of them.
