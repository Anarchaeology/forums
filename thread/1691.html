A New Nano
How about calling it "Sea Chest". Basically here's how it works. You buy an article of clothing, run "Sea Chest" and nano-bots scan said article of clothing. Once the clothing has been scanned it dissapears from your inventory and appears in another inventory just for scanned clothing. When you want to wear clothing you just goto to the "Sea Chest" inventory and choose your clothes. Any clothes you have on will be replaced by the new choice as nano-bots build it on your body and any armor you have on will be placed in your main inventory.
This way the clothing we have can be carried with us in the form of digital files instead of being stored throughout several bags we carry or hold in our bank boxes.
This would save us from having to run to the bank to get our clothing and it would save on space in our ever-jumbled inventory and bags.
