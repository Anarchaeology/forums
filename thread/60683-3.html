Psiraven
Supa Leet
bah, small corrections to myself...

I just noticed that Nano Init is 40% Agi and 60% Sense; not 40% Intelligence like I mistakenly thought.

So, that means under altered dribble down Nanomages actually take a moderate penalty to Nano Initiative, and Opifex becomes the fastest casters, but not necessarily the best. Under the 0/100/200 rule it would equate to a -50 to 60 penalty for Nano and +120-ish for Opifex. Solitus also would become naturally faster casters than Nanomage.

Nanos would gain small increases to all weapon intiatives since they are all 60% Sense, 20 Intelligence, 10 Psychic, and 10% Agility.

So, an enhanced dribble down doesnt "quite" work perfectly in all cases. Nanomages gain speed increases against the Solitus and Atrox as far as weaponry is concerned and lose Nano Init against the same.

So it kind of works the reverse of how one expects, at least in the case of 4-5 Speed tab skills; due to how they were defined.

Opifex should take a dribble down penalty to one of their Normal Blue abilities to balance everyone else having 2 or 3 Dark Blues. That leaves Strength, Psychic, or Intelligence to choose from. This would go a long way to address a lot of the advantages that Opifex would have over Solitus.

Sacrificing any 1 of them is going to hurt.

Probably Psychic would hurt the least, but it does affect a few key skills namely Nano Pool 70%, Nano Res 80%, Martial Arts 30%, Quantum FT 50%, Dimach 20%, B&E 30%. In the case of Martial Arts, Dimach, and B&E the effect is to tone down bonuses that are already large; so it wont act as a penalty. Psychic will hurt nano skills slightly more than sacrificing Strength. The difference between Solitus and Opifex in Nano Pool and Nano Res will be very pronounced and would amount to an 80-90 point difference under the 0/100/200 rule.

Sacrificing Intelligence will result in huge nano skill losses as expected, about -90 under the 0/100/200 rule as well as a -115-ish penalty to Comp Lit as well as lesser net bonuses to Treatment, First Aid, evades and weapon initiatives.

Sacrificing Strength would result in moderate weapon skill losses mainly on guns and melee weapons turning the effect on Assault Rifle skill into a ZERO net effect and SMG would become a small net penalty. 1HE and Piercing become moderate penalties. However, the penalty on Martial Arts is slightly less than if Psychic was sacrificed; net effect is still a bonus however. Taking dribble down penalties for both Strength AND Stamina creates a HUGE penalty (-120-ish) for Brawl and Full Auto. Nullifies bonuses to Burst and Parry. Makes bonus to Shotgun trivial. Heavy Weapons and Weaponsmithing now moderately penalized.


Over all, if I were to make a suggestion Psychic should be sacrificed but then again, I don't play Opifex. If Funcom ever decided to enact enhanced dribble down, I'd leave it to the Opifex players to choose their own poison.
