An old timer returns... and had a dream !
Kroll was level 80 since augustus 2000, and retired when Funcom buffed the mobs to a point playing a non-twinked fixer was an exercise in frustration.

I played a meta (created on release) till 137, and now play a 161 trader as my main.

I was missing my ol' trox fixer, so I gave him another try.
To my surprise, he's doing fine in 50% missions, with self-equipped gear. Well... nearly self-equipped, I wear armor 125 which is ok even after overequipping patch though.

I think I'm the only fixer wielding a Slugger.
A self-equipped one, QL130.
I always used a Slugger, because it's fast, doesn't need a crit buff because it doesn't have crit bonus and looks like an Uzy or Ingram, the weapons I always wanted my fixer to use.

My HoT is maxed for my level, as well as my implants, and 50% missions are just fine.
Very slow to level, but would team with a fixer ? so better get used to solo

The point of my post is twofold.

First off, I wanted to say I'm able to play, even if I should be level 200 by XMas 2013 playing him 8 hours a day, and I'm glad to play him again because I hate slow characters.

Second off, Funcom doesn't have much to do to make fixers a viable class, so for the 87th time, I'lll summarize my thoughts.
Feel free to flame, but it's just how I see it.

1° Increase damage on fast weapons, like the Slugger, Urnaium Sprayer or Michael Patriot. Speed is fixer defining, so make those weapons viable. A 30% damage increase would be fine.

2° Improve our HoTs. Mobs hit hard, and HoTs were designed before mobs got buffed. A healer can heal himself when needed, while a fixer can do nothing but hope his maxed first aid skill and gimpy HoTs will keep him alive.

3° Reduce nano skill costs. It's on the plan, but if it goes on HUD3, it's not a wise move.

4° Make Grid Armor available. I made about 380 levels with 3 characters and never found any. I camped the shades and eyeswithout result too. I will never buy a disc from a player, because I will never be anyone's biotch. I don't have to, do I ?

5° Allow fixers to recall zones, just like Recall and Gate in UO.
No need to mark a rune to a specific location, a warp (both self and team) to different zones will be ok.
Display the general map, point a location, and teleport there.

6° Allow fixers to hack nanocrystals. Make the success rate very low, even with high break and entry. A hacked cristal will be destroyed and the nano transfered to spellbook, just like now.
To keep it balanced (I think about agents here), you have 2 solutions:
a) lower the QL of the nano by 30%, so the nano will be much less powerfull than what it could be. If a nano doesn't belong to a "line" (like Nano shutdown for example), the nano can't be learned.
b) restrict hackable nanos to those who have some nano skill as requirement. SO, for example, only nanos who have Bio Met for example. The fixer should get a UNIQUE NODROP NODESTROY item with this nano skill written on it, so whatever nano skill he chosses, he must stick to it.

Comments wellcome
