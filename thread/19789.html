A little advise, that might go for real life too.

If your only goal is that 1000 token board, then you are bound to be disapointed. (Oh aye, I got my board what do I do now?) Since you are new to the game you will probably be broke for a while And find that oh... I can do those little easier mission so I can get credits and in fact afford to get that next level. That was when my tokens started to roll in, I leveled just as fast, since downtime in between MOBS where reduced, and chest xp became a major factor in there too. (If you have to use 20 tries to open a door or chests, raise you Break and Entry, you will not regret! (if you do mission...))

Have fun on your journey, because when you reach your goal, that journey is over... There are real people behind the characters you meet!
