Night Assault Legion is recruiting.
Night Assault Legion is expanding it's ranks. Our organization belives that the clan threat can not be solved by diplomatic matters, therefor we belive in the force of violence to get rid of the Rebels and any other Omni-Tek hostile force.
We want every member to know each other and to be well organized and therefor we have a 1-2 weeks recruitment period. When first recruited you will not be promoted Unit Member until after this test period. You will be urged to attend to any meetings during this time and also to get to know the other members of the organization and team with them as much as you are able to.
If you want to apply send either me (Ugluk) or Juggernort an in-game tell.
We only accept serious players and therefor you must be at least level 50 to be able to apply.

Hope to see you on the battle fields against the Rebels.

/Ugluk
