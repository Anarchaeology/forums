Personalizing armour
K,

I am Back in Black, pure black, Nano armour black.

There are alot of us. Except for my fetish for summoned weapons, you could not pick me out of a crowd.

I would like to see a couple of personalization slots on visible armour. This way you could add in personality touches so you could tell people apart visually even if they change armour, or have simular armour with other people.

These slots may or may not have any combat differences, but would allow you to be destinct.

I would suggest
Armbands
necklaces
scarves
armour paint (like tattoes)


do other people have an idea how we could personalize armour without destroying what we have?
