Add a [NO FLY] flag to outdoor dropped items
There has been a large outcry in the AO community with outdoor ninja-looting of dropped items from high level mobs. Can the developers add a [NO FLY] flag to dropped items from these mobs to prevent people from zooming up in Yals and stealing hard earned items? It's terrible seeing the fruits of a large raid go down the drain when some schmuck flies up and steals the dropped item (most likely it's an item with a [NO DROP] flag on it.)
