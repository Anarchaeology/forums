Unusual clanner activity sighted in Borealis
To the employees of Omni-Tek and citizens of Rubi-Ka,

On February 14, 29477 a meeting between two unlikely groups were observed. The place, inside the Borealis branch of a Fair Trade shop. It was in this public space that one of the sections were cordoned off by armed clanner guards from Mercury Dragons. Innocent shoppers were interrupted in their routine and told to leave the area. The reason for this? Apparently, it was because the leader of Mercury Dragons, Theo "Windguaerd" McCovery and Gabe "Sumokan" Hernandez, the leader of the neutral organization Elite Operations were holding a private meeting inside.

The first red flag were using the words "private meeting" while in a public space like the Fair Trade store was an oxymoron. Secondly, for a neutral group their relations with clans have soured considerably after publicly coming forward admitting that they were responsible for hiring mercenaries that shoot clanners on sight in Newland City for 100 million credits. The clan Mercury Dragons' track record have been aggressive towards Omni-Tek and its employees.

What do these two dissimilar groups had to talk about and why in the obviously public setting? I was able to snap a few holophotos as proof before being forcibly escorted out by the armed clanner thugs. An Omni-Tek employee threatened in a neutral area that is technically still considered under Omni juristiction. What is this planet coming to! The request to Omni-Admin has been made to launch an investigation.

Sincearly,
Charles "Kithrak" Houston
Red Tape of Omni-Admin
