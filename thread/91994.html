There are two mobs at that location. One, Major Dalton, is on the platform in the circular cement area.

The other, Major Copperfield, spawns between the two buildings.

Each Major has a placeholder which is a normal guard. So you actually have to kill the placeholder to get the Major to spawn.

The spawnrate is 10 minutes (if I remember correctly).

And no, Sergeant D i c k (the swear filter cut it out, heh) has nothing to do with the Major spawns. He is just there to throw you off and drop a token.

Good luck. The droprate on the Majors is 100%, gloves or a sleeve, since getting them to spawn is the part that requires work, being that the Majors are lvl 170 and easily soloable by anyone.
