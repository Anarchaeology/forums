Team Fixer Grid Quest Broken Too
The first fixer quest is mentioned a few times but seen no-one mention the team version yet so here goes.

Yesterday i decided to get of my lazy backside and get my 141 smurf the team fgrid for my guildies on raid nights weell, off to spawn some borg i go.

First one at the clearing, eeeeasy pie, killed the lunaspell, *poof* borg, wooho! Off to SFH, er, with the clipping changes and jump changes you CANNOT LAND ONTOP OF THINGS. Now this might seem a minor issue, but two of the lunaspells spawn ontop of two HUGE rock towers. (If you try to exit yalm above them you land miles away from where you were hovering)

So, i found a way up, you don't want to know how many hours i spent jumping at that blasted thing. Killed the lunaspell a few times and got a borg, w00t!

Back to SAV to spawn the cyborg fixer. Er, this guys on a sheer sided tower of rock surrounded by water and you can't land on it anymore... Another few hours i managed the impossible, using a combination of careful jumps and then using burst at the very top of my jump i was able to land bursts on the lunaspell, sadly this was a manex so took 3mins for the thing to die.

Managed to spawn the final borg, have it! Run off to Sirocco, goes through his spiel...

"You already have this unique item"

*^*%*&^*&^&%�%^"*%�"�$%^&*!!!

Aha, i mean, oh poopies he won't give me the first incomplete crystal. Five hours later my petition reaches no.1 (possibly think about bumping quest problems up on the ole' priority pwease ) Well as usual the ARK was very helpful, within mins he had Sirocco handing out a crystal, i thanked him in a huge hurry, he bellowed RUN! at me and off i went.

First borg easy, 2nd borg, er, where's the 2nd borg gone? OH JOY! It's despawned somehow. Well, long story short it took me 3 hours to spawn anotherone, 20m per lunaspell.

Right, now to try again. First borg, easy, 2nd borg, easy, back in tir casting fgrid for the final borg... Hey wait a min, wheres the crystal gone?

Oh lord, i nearly exploded IRL, the pain, the anguish that bug after bug had caused me, i had overcome them all with brute stubborness, i so badly wanted that nano, there, in the reward menu it sat, taunting me but i could never have it...

The 2nd borg of course couldn't return the crystal...

"You already have this unique item"

So here i sit, a broken fixer, sobbing to myself and likely in need of counciling, i tried SO hard to make this bug ridden quest work but when my 2nd cast of GA wore off i realised that this was just insane.

I am sorry my fixer friends, i tried to prove a fixer could overcome any little bug but im tired, i feel so worn out it feels like i've just run a marathon.

Oh, by the way, don't try the team fgrid quest, s'bugged.
