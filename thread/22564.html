Buy Terminals!
As an addition to this thing:
""There have been suggestions on an in-game "sell terminal" of some sort, where players can sell their items and others would be able to buy them from there. Give us your feedback on how you would like us to implement such a feature. ""

Why not let people be able to goto a terminal, enter the name of the item, the QL they want and the Max price they will pay, deduct the price from the buyer. Then others can go and look and if they have it, set a reasonable price and it is sent to the buyers bank upon completion of the transaction!
