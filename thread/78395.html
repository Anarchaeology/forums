Xyber Mod- CS Voices
This mod adds SOME Of the counter strike voices to Notum Wars, you and your friends must have this installed inorder for it to be avalible for them to hear. You must have your voice set on military in order to /voice <command> these extra commands.

You must have X-Pack 1 IE. Notum Wars inorder to use these commands.

Dl the file, extract to your anarchy online folder, making sure "use folder names" is checked. and walla.

in anarchy online type /csvoice or /csvoice_menu for the rest.

Download
Load up ao and type /csvoice for the help menu.
