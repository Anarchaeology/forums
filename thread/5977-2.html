Update 2002-01-07
Heres my attempt to put together a Complete MA Bug List. Updated (2002-01-07) with things I've missed (thanks all on the MA-forums) and cleaned up a bit. (Ill fill in my personal opinions and some explaining within paranthesis below. All Quotes within [brackets] are taken from a larger context. Information about Funcom official statements are taken from www.thepunchingbag.net )


MA Special Attacks:
Attack of the Snake (AoS): Does not work over QL100. Hits for 1 damage a large amount of the time.
Bird of Prey (BoP): Requisites seem to be the same whether its a QL10 or a QL200. So is the damage. There is a recycle time change from 20sec. to 30sec. at higher QLs.
Blessed with Thunder (BwT): Stated by Funcom not to be working correctly, as the DoT part is doing too much damage, and the reflect shield seems to be a bit over the top sometimes.
Angel of Night (AoN): Requisites seem to be the same whether its a QL35 or a QL200. So is the damage.
Flower of Life (FoL): Hurts the MA using it, is believed to be a lifetap attack.
Bright, Blue, Cloudless Sky (BBCS): Well, the damage seems a bit low for the duration of locking MA skill, no?
Shen: No personal experience here, but I assume it to be the big brother of BwT, therefore facing the same fate.

All of the above can be "Resisted" by a MOB with high nano resist skill. For no apparent reason they sometimes won't execute.
They are No-Drop.

(The damage output on these fellas was fine and dandy to about lvl 50ish. Over that, its kinda embarrassing to hear teammembers ask you what was that 1 point nanobot damage? And AoS sure hits for 1 a lot. And I can't give outgrown MA attacks to my friends of lower lvl. Hm. Anyway from MA specials we move on to the famed: )


Book of Knowledge:
Currently doesnt work.

(Its rumoured that it would be a special little bag for us MAs, so we could use MA specials from the book instead of having them in our inventory. Therefore (at least I do) we have 3 or 4 items in the inventory taking up the much needed space. Add to this the hints of more MA special attacks, and we have one full inventory indeed.)


MA Specials/Unique Items
When having a MA Special in main inventory, it is not possible to pick up another one of the same kind. This seems to apply to other "Unique" items as well.

(I sometimes use a QL99 Attack of the snake. When my skills are ransacked/deprived, my MA skill can go below the Req for that attack. This bug makes it very unwieldy to have a lower QL "backup" AoS stashed away. Not to mention that upgrading MA attacks becomes... how shall I put this... cumbersome? Of course theres a workaround, you can put the MA attacks in various backpacks, but inventory space is not a thing that MA's have lots of. Combine all of the above with the damage output of MA Special Attacks and it becomes quite unwieldy.)


Weapon wielding:
If an MA zones with a weapon wielded, his fists cease to function.
If an MA buffs his MA skill with a weapon wielded, his fists cease to function.
If an MA runs out of an MA buff with a weapon wielded, his fists cease to function.
If an MA opens the skill menu with a weapon wielded, his fists cease to function.
If an MA is debuffed by a trader with a weapon wielded, his fists sometimes cease to function.

(This has been the case since launch of AO. Thats quite a long time.)


MA Melee Weapons:
Handblades have very tough reqs on Sneak and fast attack compared to their damage output.
G-staffs have very tough reqs on Sneak and fast attack compared to their damage output.
Taichi/Wallblades/Parry Sticks have a very long equipping time, making them unviable in conjunction with MA specials.
Burning Crescents do not have Brawl/Dimach req.
Click stabbers do not have Brawl/Dimach req.

Wielding MA weapons (with the possible exception of dualwielding Acroblades) brings your damage output down at lower lvls.

(If you compare the requisites vs. the damage output against most non MAweapons out there, MA weapons fall short in all aspects. Add to this the utter unability to overequip them for more then 2.5 minutes (wrangler duration), it doesnt quite cut it. And, please consider the damage output of the Handblades. At higher QL they add to melee init and MA skill, but frankly, making [8-32(43)] at QL 200 with a 1HE req of 993 is simply strange.)


MA Distance Weapons
Sharp Objects have never been in the game.
Bows use a massive amount of dark blue skills (fling, aimed shot etc). Equipping/Unequipping time is quite long.
Bow Special attack causes your MA to wave goodbye at the wasted arrow instead of causing any damage.

(Its nice and all that we were given some pulling ability, but unequipping the bow doesnt exactly go fast. I wont mention the sharp objects anymore.)


MA skills.

Dimach
Description: "Dimach is your character's soul attack. It is extremely expensive to train and will only be effective on higher levels." It is commonly parried, evaded or simply whiffs. For the damage it causes, the recycle time seems a bit long.

(Maxed out under lvl100 it hits for appx. 1700 damage. It'll dent a green MOB some. If it hits.)

Riposte.
Description: "Riposte is the skilled martial artist's ability to turn damage back on the opponent. It works well both on melee and martial arts attacks." Currently, it becomes a damage shield of about 1 point per 100 in skill lvl. Stated by Funcom "not to be working as intended" many months ago and has supposedly been under revamping since then.

(It seems well worth the IP spent, especially since its a req for the MA special attack AoN, which locks your MA skill for 450 seconds and is pretty equivalent to the QL70 NT nano "Expanding Neutron Pulse", but wait... didnt that NT nano do radiation damage? And the NT can use it again in less then 10 seconds? Well, gosh, at least I locked the First Aid skill of all MOBs in the Area!)

Parry
Description: "A very useful skill for melee combat specialists, as it will enable them to prevent incoming attacks from melee and martial arts. The higher the skill, the higher the chance." It requires (undocumented) that a weapon is wielded to function. The chance of parrying doesn't seem very high, except for MOBs.

(I maxed this baby when I got my first BwT, and I was using Handblades at the moment so I spent a lot of time with weapons in my hands. A parry then was extremely rare. I havn't parried a thing since with Parry skill at 393.)

MA "Items"
Though not specifically designed for MA's we use these items since the skills involved are needed for our MA attacks. I'll quote Nutbar03 here:

[Deflection Shield
Description: This 'shield' should be placed in the utility inventory slots, and used when you want to avoid damage. It shields melee and projectile damage for 10 seconds - plus 10 percent of a second per Quality Level of the shield. It locks the Parry skill for 5 minutes before it can be used again. The amount deflected varies with the Quality Level of the Shield.
Reality: The shield adds 150 Melee and impact AC for a short time regardless of QL. The exception is very high QL items that add 5000 AC for a short while, but if you use one of these you will get an e-mail accusing you of being an exploiter because your AC was flagged as being too high.

Deflection Shield Projector
Description: This fancy tool should be equipped in an utility slot, and activated by using it. (Right-click) The shield deflects close-combat damage back at your opponent physically hitting you. It locks your Riposte skill, and can be used once every 10 minutes. The amount of damage deflected depends on the Quality Level of the Shield. The highest level shields actually turn more damage back at the attacker than they would have inflicted. Duration is dependent on Quality Level.
Reality: The shield reflects 5 points melee damage regardless of QL. The description says that amount reflected increase with QL but it doesnt. I dont know anyone who has raise their riposte over 600 to use the advanced version.] End of quote.


Graphic Glitches
Martial Artists does not kick. Bird of Prey animation is broken.

(During 93 levels my MA has been waving his fists and performing one (1!) single kick. I remember it working well in Beta 4. The talk about kicking becoming part of a mission sounds a bit vague, I presume Funcom and Martial Artists are talking about different things here. I mean the graphic kick, the snazzy "I'm a Martial Artist Baby!" move, not an MA Special Attack or an additional brawl thing. Regarding the Bird of Prey, making a funny little cangaroo jump toetapping the enemys kneecaps just doesnt feel... right. Especially since this is the only viable alternative to Blesse with Thunder when it becomes "fixed".)


Minimum Damage:
This does not apply specifically to MAs, but to all others (are there any?) wielding fast weapons with a low top damage. Whats believed to be the cause is that the Targets AC reduces the weapon damage to the minimum level. Though not as common now as a few patches ago, I personally run into it at least once per mission.

To recreate; Create a 55-60% percent mission and find a few Adventurer or Soldier MOBs in there. Wait a while so they can buff a bit. Hit q. Enjoy the show. Or simply go watch a lvl80+ MA fight any combat class humanoid MOB's more red then orange.

(I have personally (lvl91 with QL130ish implants) run into yellow unbuffed MOBs I can only hit with min damage. My QL99 Acroblade does min damage of 123 all the time. Against any MOB except grey. But hey, its better then making 97 damage all the time, eh? Its some variation at least?)

I'll quote Zindel here:
[I team with a good enforcer friend. We started our characters together and have leveled side by side to 93 doing missions and hunting with each other almost religiously. From creation to about level 50 we were both very equal in damage over time. From 50 to 93 our damage over time progressively developed large spread. What I notice is Mas have consistent damage with good min damage and medium high-end damage. Other classes such as my enforcer friend have a poor min damage and high max damage. Problem is after 50, mob AC isnt even challenged by our med to high end damage, so we suffer with only min damageconsistently however ] End of quote.

If more evidence is needed about this "annoyance", search the MA forums or approach any MA of medium-high level. We know the issue well.


I'll skip the issues of our Nano Lines since they are more of a design issue then actual bugs, and finish off with a quote from Ming, he sums it up quite well:

[It's not even like were asking for for something extra. We just want our class to function. I agree there is no reason to pay for the game if this can't be granted. Our class isn't useless, but seriously needs repair.] End of quote.

Sent to Funcom Support 2002-01-07.

/DaveDread
