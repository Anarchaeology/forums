Camelot grid in relationship to Tarasque & Crowd/Land Control
Well, the last time PvP at tara has gone down again after it looked like it just came alive again. The problem lays in the fact that any omni trying to grid to Camelot 10 minutes before a tara spawn or later for sure, has a big chance of getting disconnected and not being able to login again for atleast 10-20 minuts usually. Which result that the fight never will take place, not because people don't want to, but because the servers make it impossible.

Therefore I make this simple suggestion: in terms of Crowd & Land control, make it so that Camelot is seen as controlled land from clanners, meaning there can be 40 clanners there, and more will be teleported away, meaning the omni can have 35 people at camelot before they get teleported away. This would leave camelot, and more important Tarasque open for both Clanners & Omni's. Which would hopefully bring back again the struggle for the beast alive again as it once was a long time ago.

The balance between clan & omni is for us not important, a 20 omni, 60 clanners would work too for us, just if we can't loot Tarasque, make it so that it was the clanners that putted us to a halt, and not mainly & mostly the lack of server support.
