The "Team Level"

When you enter a team, take notice that there is a field in the "Team" panel called "Team Level." It always starts at 1, and has the possibility of reaching 5. The longer the team sticks together, the higher this number gets. Some of the areas where you receive bonuses with higher Team Level are:

Chance of landing a successful hit with a nano-program;

Bonuses to AMS/DMS (chance of hitting with weapons and chance to avoid hits); and

Chance of successfully picking a door or disarming a bomb.
