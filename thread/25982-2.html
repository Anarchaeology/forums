As I recall it was this:

If you were Sneaking, then Aimed Shot would not work any differently at all than it does now. End of Story. Detection checks ("xxx is aware of your presence"), the whole nine yards.

If you were NOT in Sneak Mode, when your Aimed Shot special timer cycled you could fire it off and it would act like a Fling Shot. Just 'bang'.

Simple, really and a fine addition for the Agent who might be in a group quite often and is running through mission rooms so fast that an Aimed Shot is rarely feasible causing him to lose a large portion of what makes him special.

Having the Aimed Shot work as a Fling if you are not Sneaking goes in a small way towards remedying this.
