RETHINK THE PvP LVL 75 FREE-FOR-ALL
Since it appears that the OE patch is here to stay, I think we need to review the free-for-all associated with attaining the uber level of 75.

Before the OE patch, some of these level 75 characters were doing awesome crits several times per battle. That and the squeezing into high ql armor and implants made things out of scale.

For example: It was possible for a level 75 Engineer with an uber bot, and a high ql Ithica to kill much higher level characters then himself.

Now with the OE patch, the weapons and pets are much lower in ql, and the 19%+ crit buffs are gone. I don't think there is much room for argument there.

In todays Rubi-Ka, even a level 110 character has little to fear from that lowly lvl 75 Engineer. What chance does that poor guy stand against a lvl 140 MA with buffed 8K AC and 6K health? The first thing he will see is the MA tagging the Engi with 1.6k dimach and up to a 1.1k brawl crit, and a 300 point Shen? Forget running away, the MA prob has 700 run speed w/o any Fixer buffs.

Don't analyze this example to much, I'm just trying to make a point. The PvP system is not only arguably broken, but it's free-for-all level is ludicrious as well now.

I think the level spread for PvP might possibly be similar to the numbers you get for teaming when you do the /tell helpbot level 75. The spread for teaming is fine, but the spread for PvP needs to reflect something more realistic then 75-200.

I'm just not sure about the best way to go about it, do we make the levels specific, like 75-115, 116-150, 151-200. Or would it change as you went up in levels like the "team with" level spread that chages each level?

So far, IMHO, the OE patch has leveled the classes out some. I know there are many people that hate OE and have quit or threaten to quit every week. I'm not here to start a rant thread about OE, so let's keep it out of this thread, pls.
