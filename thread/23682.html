Holstering your weapon vrs unequiping
This post goes along with celestiness's post, "sweet idea for social cloths

I go to a club.
I'm holding my weapon.
I spent time and credits to equip this weapon.
I feel like dancing.
I dont feel like smacking the person next to me with my weapon while dancing.
I want to holster my gun.
But for some reason its glued to my palm.
So no dancing...
How about a drink?
No, that gun is still stuck to hand, so I can't quench my thirst or hunger.
I'll just sit here on this couch and listen to the music.


Concept:
Removing the weapon from your hand(s) with out unequipping it. This is whats known as holstering your weapon.

Why?
So you dont see people dancing, etc with giant shotguns and sniper rifles in hand. Someone is going to loose an eye! ;o)
So you dont have to remove a weapon that is overequiped. Re-equiping is a pain, which leads to people just not unequiping at all, which leads to people dancing with meter long weapons in hand. To make my point: Imagine someone IRL dancing while holding a bookshelf, or a door. Thats just plain..wrong.. ;o)

Implementing:
Make it as hard (adding new square in wear menu) or as easy (using a text /command) as you want. A nice way to implement this would be to just add a small button on your wear menu for holstering a weapon. Like this or something similar

Take it a step further so you actually see the holstered weapon on the side and/or back of the person. Pistols, small ranged weapons, certain blades - on the hip. Large weapons, certain blades, bows - on the back.
Though I don't see having a Vektor or Novaflow sitting on your back as being very realistic or even better than holding it in your hand. Visually holstering a weapon might only apply to smaller weapon types.

But still, start basic with the root feature of removing the weapon mesh from your hands without unequiping it fully. If theres time for improvement later, great. If not, oh well.


How does eveyone else feel about a feature such as this?
