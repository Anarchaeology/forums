For me, opening locked chests is a choice, while opening locked doors is often necessary. Thus, I have no problem with severe traps on chests, but being killed by opening a door necessary to progress in a mission is unacceptable.

Also, it would make sense to have the severity of the blast, and/or the difficulty of the lock vary with the richness of the loot inside. Nobody would guard a toothpick with an expensive device, but they are willing to spend huge resources to guard things of value.

Another approach is to have different sorts of locks. In addition to the traditional key-lock requiring B&E, you could also have electronic locks breakable with Comp lit skill.
