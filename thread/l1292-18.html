Grid... Im a bit foggy on it.

It is a form of transportation, is it also communication?
Why grid, versus subway?
How is it used? Level restrictions.

I guess just more on it... or simply rephrase your description to see if it clicks a light on me head.
I'll try to finagle some screenshots today.

Think of it has a 3D spider web or latice with the various strands ramping towards the heavens like pulled taffy. You travel along the strands and certain intersections have nodes. If you move your avatar over a node, you will port to a specific location. there are signs above particular nodes whi thell you which town or city the node corresponds to. Some intersections have stations where you can find out information about the area, like demographics.
It's cyberspace and much cooler than a subway.

no details on the various level restrictions. You can find out about that in game. All I know is that the various levels of the Grid are restricted by Computer Literacy skill.
