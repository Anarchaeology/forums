Ode to the Assassin
The wind was blowing through the city at a rapid pace now; it seemed that a storm would soon be arriving. Some of the people in the streets were heading for shelter, others still stood at the mission terminals or on the roof of the house near the entrance to the city. For those who were out, things were still just business as usual. That was good.

The man atop the roof adjusted his position slightly, trying to make himself as hidden from view as possible. Newland City was a busy place, so he thought he was relatively safe from any nearby prying eyes. It was nightime, and the black cloak he wore over his Obtru armor helped to ensure he wouldn't be noticed. Having settled once more, he gazed through the scope of his X-3 towards the Bronto Burger down the street. The wind was blowing the smell of burgers and fried toward him; he felt a pang of hunger in his stomach. He thought that after this job was over, he might head to the local burger shack in Tir and grab himself a bite.

The scene down the street hadn't changed much. Two Enforcers clad in what appeared to be Kevlar and Omni Pol Elite armor were standing near the Bronto Burger. One was devouring a cheeseburger with what appeared to be an exaggerated amount of enthusiasm. They were mere nuisances however, they were not what the man atop the building had come here for. Loren Warr was. He was standing at his usual post, gazing down the street every so often while watching his bodyguards with what could have been either contempt or amusement; the Azure armor helmet he wore hid his facial expression from view. The Atrox walked over to the food shack slowly, and began to talk to one of his fellows.

The would-be assassin continued to watch all this with a hunter's eye, keeping Loren firmly in his crosshairs. Warr's slow Friday evening was about to be shaken up... that was the hope anyway. The agent fingered at the trigger absently, waiting for the Atrox to return to his post. Beads of sweat were beginning to break out on his forehead and back, despite the chilling wind. Loren Warr was not known as a merciful person... the agent thought it wise to leave the area after one shot, whether or not his target went down.

The man was brought back to attention when Loren began to amble back towards his usual spot. The agent followed him with his rifle, until the mercenary had come to a complete stop; and then without another moment's hesitation he pulled the trigger. A muffled shot sounded, and the muzzle flash from his X-3 temporarily lit up the scene around in lurid yellow light. The agent wasn't paying any attention to this, though, he was still watching Loren through his scope. By some horrible stroke of luck, the Atrox has shifted his weight to one leg just as the shot was fired, and he was staring right at the man on the roof. The agent couldn't see through Loren's visor, but he knew it was true nonetheless. He could *feel* those eyes watching him.

"Assassin!" Loren bellowed. "Lee! Johnson! Assist me with this puppy!"

The two enforcers sitting at the Bronto Burger sat up suddenly, the one dropping his cheese burger on the counter. Loren himself was already charging towards the building. The agent lost his cool at the sound of the Merc's heavy footsteps falling the stone path leading towards his hideout, he simply threw down his gun and leaped. He hit the ground in a roll, and began to make a dead break for the entrance to town. The other mercenaries around town had already figured out what the ruckus was about; the Opifex was soon to have a platoon of them on him. His escape came to a sudden stop when he encountered two other Enforcers from Loren's happy little band already waiting for him at the city gates.

The agent came to at the reclaim terminal in Athen a few minutes later, with a queasy feeling in his stomach and a horrible pounding in his head. He didn't so much sit down as collapse to the ground beneath him, and waited for the sick thud in his head to dissipate. He had a hinting su****ion that the image of Loren's swirling blood bat was going to haunt his dreams for quite some time to come.
