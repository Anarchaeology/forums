RK2 OT Leader assembly
The OT Leaders of RK2 gathered last sunday. Were represented Omni Elite, Omni-Tek Secret Service, Inner Circle, BlackOps Interpol, OmniTrade Guards, The Tarkus Divsion, GODS, OmniTek Wolf Pack, Project Starlight, Seekers PI, The Praetorian Project, Obsidian Underground, Project EDEN, Internal Audi, New Order (Formerly Omnipotens, but now WOBB WABB?), and The Dojo with me.

This meeting laid the ground of a coordination of all RK2 OT leaders that will slowly shape itself and grow.

All RK2 OT Leaders are invited to register on the dedicated forum on http://www.guildportal.com/guildHome.asp?GuildID=647 ("OT Leaders RK-2").

/tell nikh, Kromos, Shadowtracker ... ingame, or click on the "register" link to request permission to join from the website.
