Total n00b Enforcer Guide(A work in progress)
Before I start I just gotta say: Anarchy Online really is a great game. But it is rather complicated. I can't stress enough the idea of creating a character and playing it to lvl 50ish, just so that you can get the feel for the game, and figure out whats going on. I did that...I re-rolled about 6 times before I got it "locked-down." If u're not a physco-17 year old who's bored during the summer, by all means don't. But if u're like me, you want to be strong, and if you don't wanna be gimped, u should really make your 1st character with the sole intention of figureing out WTH is going on in this game.

Also, make sure that you like what your character is. Don't be a MP cause they are currently the only class that is rather well balanced ( and thus Uber). Be what you wanna be, that way, you'll have fun when playing. Which, after all, is the idea, right? So anyway, on-to da guide.



What is an Enforcer??

An Enforcer is comparable to a warrior or a barbarian from other RPGS. Basically they are the tank, who keeps the aggro away from the other, weaker classes. Enforcer nanos are all oriented to increasing our HP, Weapon skills, or Aggro abilities.

What breed should I choose?

Obviously, you can choose any breed you want, but some work better than others. Nanomage is the least common enforcer breed, followed by Opifex. Nanomages are not commonly used because they are weak in Str/Sta, which are key abilites for an Enforcer. However, if you want to have outstanding nano skills, you could play as a nanomage. Opifex are also a possibility, but once again, not recomened as they are weak in skills that Enforcers need to be strong in. The two best, and most common, choices are Atrox and Solitus. Solitus are chosen because they are decent at all abilites, so they get nanos slightly earlier than the Atroxes, and more nano, but less HP and strength. Atroxes are arguibly the strongest breed for an Enforcer as they can use the famed beams, and Da Taunter! Atrox are stupid, but very strong. The breeds will be mentioned again when we get to choosing your weapon.

What side should I choose?

Your side is irrellivent, but most people play Omni/Clan, Neutral isn't a common choice. But there is something to be said for being different in this game....

What weapons should I use?

This is the big question. There are 4 main catogories of weapons for Enforcers: 2 handed blunt (2HB), 2 handed Edged (2HE), 1 handed blunt (1HB), and 1 handed edged (1HE). Each offers its own strengths and weaknesses.

2HB- This is possibly the most powerful choice. Your best bet for weapons if you use 2HB are Hammers and Beams. Hammers are best for low levels and Beams are best for high levels. The reason for this is based upon the weapon speed, and maxing it out and upon the avaibilty of critical hit buffs. So until you are about level 120 don't switch to a Beam. Note: The beams are Atrox only so make sure if you want to use one that you are Atrox. Choosing the 2HB path, you should always keep Str/Sta at their max. Agility is important for your melee init. so keep it at about 2/3. Body Dev. should also be maxed as it determines your hp. Nano pool is a nice extra, there is no definet amount to put in her, just place some points in here. Sense/Int/Physic should be kept just high enough so that you are not prevented from raising skills.

1HB - 1HB is as powerful as, if not stronger, than your other options, but if you want to be unique, this is the choice. The number of 1HB enforcers that are past level 75 is probably less than 30. For abit in there your damage will be capped until you get enough multi-melee to dual wield qlvl200 weapons, and you can self-equip them. Once that happens you should be very well off. For your early levles use the faster wood staves, as your melee int. won't be high enough to take advantage of the slower weapons. Then gradually move to alloy as your melee init picks up. First one alloy+one wood, then both alloy until you can no longer feed the multi-melee ip monster. Then, a premium alloy in one hand and a premium pipe with a lower multi req in the other. With an attack skill of 850+ alloy/pipe combo is supposed to be rather effective. I'd recommend being solitus if you choose this path, as mult. melee needs agility, and your nano skills/nano will be higher, because your not a "stupid" Atrox. You'd only be missing out on Da Taunter! which isn't really all that much considering what you gain from being solitus.

1HE - 1HE is basically the same as 1HB except that you need to put IP into heavy weapons, as the best 1HE weapons are Rider Executioners. This is a very good choice, but as stated in 1HB I'd recommend taking the solitus route, so your agility is kept high for mult. melee. Once you use dual qlvl 200 Rider Executioners you can dish out alot of damage, plus dual 1HE looks really cool. And, until FunCom gets its act together and comes up with an edged version of Headcracker & Brutal Thug, you won't be able to over-equip too much.

2HE - After 13.6 there are some new 2HE weapons. They are hard to get, but if an enforcer is your twink character and you can get your grubby little hands on these, u'd kick alot of butt. These swords are the equal of a rather large, sharp beam. For 2HE you should be Solitus/Atrox, as multi. melee isn't an issuse, either choice would be good. And, as said earlier, until FunCom gets its act together and comes up with an edged version of Headcracker & Brutal Thug, you won't be able to over-equip very much.

Piercing - I'm not even gonna write much about this until Funcom makes some Legit Piercing weapons. Don't use this as your weapon skill. Piercing weapons suck bad. (currently)

Skills

This is going over key skills (not all of skills, but by far 99% of skills that enforcers use..) to raise with IP, which you get after leveling up.

Body - - - -

Brawling - This is important no matter what kind of weapons you use, max it, as this is an excellent skill.

Dimach - Since this is deep blue to us, either decide to spend ALOT of IP and keep up with it, or just get enough to use your current weapon.

Melee - - - -

This is self-explanitory, only raise the one that you have decided to go with, and if your raising a 1 handed skill, make sure that you raise mult. melee.

Fast attack - This is a note worthy attack skill in this catagory. Even if you weapon doesn't require it, think about raising it. There is an item called Flurry of Blows (FoB) that raises your attack speed based upon this skill. It is rather useful, so if you have spare IP, Fast Attack is a good choice.

Parry - This is something of an interesting skill. This skill determines what your chance of blocking an attack is. Make a choice, max it, or don't touch it. Anything in the middle is just a waste, as you won't really notice its effect unless its kept maxed.


Misc. Weapon - - - -

Heavy Weapons - If your weapons require it, raise it. Rider Executioners require it, but not many other enforcer weapons do.


Ranged - - - -

NO. Unless u're lvl 120+ and just wastin' extra IP.


Speed - - - -

Melee Init. - For any/all melee weapons this skill is necessary. This skill reduces your weapons attack time. At high levels this skill helps sooooo much.

Dodge-Rng - This skill lowers the chance of an enemy who uses ranged attacks of getting a critical hit on you. This is a very good skill, keep it as high as you can.

Evade-Clsc - This skill lowers the chance of an enemy who uses Close Combat attacks of getting a critical hit on you. This is a very good skill, keep it as high as you can.

Duck-Exp - This skill lowers the chance of an enemy who uses a varity of weapons, (Shotguns, beams, Throwing weapons...) of getting a critical hit on you. This skill isn't used much in PvM (Player vs Monster) but is a NECESSARY skill for PvP(Player vs Player) or else u'll get owned....

Nano Resist - This skill is good...but expensive for us Enforcers...Not recommened for low levels, but if you got the IP, its ultimately worth it.

Run Speed - Determines how fast you run, worth raising some, but maxing? no.


Trade & Repair - - - -
Computer Lit. - this skill determines what Belts & NCU's you can use. Get this skill quite high, as belts & NCU's determine how many nano's you can have running on you.


Nano & Aiding - - - -

Mat. Metamorphasis - Keep this as high as you can.

Bio. Metamorphasis - This is "the" enforcer nano skill. Keep it way high, as almost all of our nanos use it.

Psycho Mod - u need 696 for the last mongo nano. Make sure you get it. Raise it in spirts though, as only a couple nanos need it.

Matter Creation - Keep as high as possible. You need it, trust me.

Time & Space - No enforcer nanos require this. Raise it enough so that u can recharge your nano though, as nano-rechargers require this skill. How much you want is up to you.

Sensory Improvment - For 1HB or 2HB users: Raise this skill to 149 (Headcracker). Then stop for a bit. Use implants to get the rest for Brutal Thug(366) For non-blunt users there are only 2-enforcer nanos that need that skill. they're good..but not worth the huge IP cost unless u're high level.

First Aid - First aid allows you to heal yourself in battle. At lower levels it is very useful, but at higher levels it falls behind the amount of damage that you are going to take. (most likely, anyway....) definetly raise this skill some, but don't keep it maxed as you get to high(er) levels.

Treatment - This skill allows you to heal yourself after battle, and it is part of the requirement for installing implants. I'd recommend keeping this skill close to maxed. There are lots of buffs for it, u can get +200 or more easily for installing implants, so don't worry if you don't keep up with it.

http://antiguardians.org <--- look for your-self, nano & item list.


Spying - - - -

Break & Entry - Put a few points here. Not many. This skill helps your ability to open locked chests with the lock-pick. It is by no means a necessary skill, but sometimes you just get mad at those st00pid chests, so you put some points into it and hope for the best.



Navigation - - - -

Vehicle Air - For a yalm. this is a n00b guide...sooooo its not necessary for you guys. It's way expensive, and way 1337

Vehicle Ground - This is a way more practicle method of faster transportation for n00bs. It might be worth it if u just HAVE to have a car, or just can't handle running everywhere.

Vehicle Water - uhhhh...I dun think so. But hey, its your character...

Map navigation - This skill, when raised, allows you to upload maps and map-reader upgrades. Definetly worth putting some points here.




Please feel free to critize this guide all you want, I'll even change it if you make a good point. And if this guide was useful in anyway, *bump* it with a reply, & keep it at the top so other n00bs can read it if they need 2. (At least till its a sticky.)
