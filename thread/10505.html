Funcom since your forcing PvP on us fix the title system first
Currently the title system is pointless and stupid, the only thing its good for showing is that the person either
  • Is an ubertwink and ganked lowbies in their range for ages or maybe did the arena thing for months.
  • Camped a grid/whompa.
  • Can gank a bunch of grays/greens that are hunting someplace like ace camp or borgs.
  • Is one of the small handful of people who don't run across zone lines to escape combat, engage people that are fighting in PvM, or gank grays, for untold months killing/being killed by people.


If I as an omni see a clanner with a title in a 75% zone I or any other omni regardless of our title (or lack thereof) should be able to engage him for the simple fact that this person has already proven that they are a threat and may warrant the effort of eliminating them just like the rest of the common monsters we see on rubi-ka in 75% zones. The same should hold true for the reverse situation (omni with a title can be attacked by a clanner). To prevent untitled attacking titled ones without fear of others joining in set it so once you engage in a PvP fight under these rules you are able to be attacked by anyone of the opposite side as if you were in a 25%.

75% zones would still be impossible to engage a player who has no title since the gas would still keep us from taking action against them. Those who want to walk the walk and get a title should live with the responsibility of their actions or stay in those 100% zones if they dont want to fear potential attack.

This would have the added bonus of eliminating those irritating people who do things like
  • run into shops to recharge MK shields/heal/etc
  • zone->zone back->alphastrike->burst->etc and run back if it didnt kill someone and their odds are grim
  • running through the whompa in 2ho or anywhere else for that matter to a totally safe 75% zone that doesnt require touching a pvp area again to go somewhere productive to escape combat (true omni-entertainment probably isnt a great place for a clanner to be but a few high enough level clanners would be ok probably)
  • Quite likely a whole host of other complaints that PvPrs have.


All those PvPrs that complain that nobody PvPs or that the game is all about PvP can go looking to gank their rivals whenever they want under these rules and all that would need to happen is for someone to tip them off as to their location. While Funcom can quit doing things like making mort 25% and everyone can be happy.


Not enough people with titles to PvP after the change?... easy, go temporarily enlist for a temp title with omnipol/council of truth/etc and get your level times 4k credits (i.e. level 100=400k creds) after being in 75% or lower zones for 8 in game hours and getting at least 1 pvp kill, as well as a good bit of EXP based on how many and what level people you killed (i.e. 2k per level of people between 50-99, 4k per level between 100 and 149, 8k per level between 150-190? etc (numbers might be too high or low, exp should not be awarded till after the 8 hours in 75% or less and should cap at a maximum of 50%-100% of your level). Each time you get killed you loose the value of 2/3rds your grey kills 2 greens or 1 of anything else depending on what you have killed.
