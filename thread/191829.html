Itel More Focused than Ever
Reporter Mudrake

Its been three days since the Dust Brigade attempted to permanently kill Itel. Although he is still recovering from the effects of the weapons used on him, he agreed to allow FJRK an exclusive interview. Itel reveals how his brush with permanent death has affected his plans for Newland, and his new determination to see these plans through.

Although not fully recovered from his attempted assassination, Itel was more than willing to discuss the effects it has had on him. When asked if his plans had changed, in light of his near brush with permanent death, Itel said, Well my Newland issue will not be suffering from this, I will never ever back down from that, actually it just made me more focused on my issues.

Itels sense of Irony appears not to have suffered either. The spot he chose for this interview was no more than thirty feet away from Loren Warr, the Atrox at the very heart of the issue at hand. The removal of the mercenaries is one of the major steps Itel would like to see the Newland Council take.

I got tired of seeing this city falling apart from lack of trade. So I started by going over what might be the reasons for the loss of trade and came up with one thing all the time, the Thugs.

The council refused to even listen to Itel, and in the end he set out on a mission to recruit more people to his cause.When I failed alone I started to talk about it with others, sharing my idea and way of seeing things.

For now it appears the Newland City Initiative (NLCI) is still going full speed ahead. Itel did however, have a few words of caution for everyone involved. Be careful out there, Dust Brigade is a really dangerous group of Terrorists, keep clear of them, they shoot to kill and dont care who gets hit.

When asked about the upcoming fundraiser for the NLCI, he said only that it was still going ahead, but refused to comment on any extra security precautions that may or may not be in place.

With all the attention the NLCI has been subject to in the last few weeks, they can no longer be ignored by the Newland City Council, some form of action is necessary.
