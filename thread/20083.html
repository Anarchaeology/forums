No, no, no. The damage reflected back is actually the amount. However, the damage inflicted is actually reduced by percentage. Go look at the Soldier's Reactive Reflective Field at http://antiguardians.org/itemdb.php?...ano=1&id=70314

modify target reflect energy damage 30

And it reduces damage inflicted by 30%, not 30 points.
Same goes for the modifiers on the bracers.

modify self reflect energy damage 21

The bracer also reduces the damage inflicted by 21%.

Leetas
Ok I was "slightly wrong".

There is no percent included. Not on bracers and not on Reactive.
I was wrong though, because Reactive doesn't "cap" the reflect. The reflect is capped on a "per item or nano basis" not combined.

So 2 QL 200 bracers + Reactive = 42 + 30 = 72 damage reflected.

That 72 damage still hits you. But Reactive will "Deflect" 48 of it and the bracers will deflect 42 (effectively negating a shot of 90 damage or less)

The only nano the deflects any PERCENT of a shot by design is Total Mirror Shield. The other shields that soldiers use reflect and deflect a set amount.

Reflect = it hits the target and bounces back and hits attacker.
Deflect = it never hits anyone unless specified (TMS states the damage is "deflected back onto the attacker")

Heck, knowing the way stuff works in this game....I could be dead wrong about it all. I'm going on my own experiences ingame, and the description of the nanos/items.
