I have played both to the mid 50's and what little I know about the two professions is this... they are completely different styles of play.

My Crat can control/charm almost anything that I come across (I know, the charming ability fades around 100th), but doesn't take a hit too well. The flexability is awesome at times.

My Fixer, he wades into combat and pulls for team missions when ever asked. But, IPs are tight and the Fixer is a VERY popular profession right now, meaning groups are somedays hard to come by.

Bottom line is... you need to ask yourself what kind of player are you and which profession do you closer fit? right now, I love my fixer (more now then when I made him at release) but 3 months ago I would have said my Crat was my favorite.

I have one of each and they are both a lot of fun.

Combat is really simple as a fixer. You target and attack and occassionally hit your burst or maybe even a root or snare. Very straightforward and good if you don't want to think much (particularly good on a night of drinking ).

Crats have a lot to do. They have to worry about crowd control *and* their multiple pets while dishing out damage of their own. You really gotta pay attention when playing in a team or solo. There's no going for a glass of water or a smoke while you wait for a mob to die.

I am really liking my crat right now, though.
