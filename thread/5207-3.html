Top 10
1) Morphs

A) Fix bug where intended indoor morphs cannot be used
due to door issue (Playful Cub, Grinning Hunter)

B) Reet Form #2 (Flurry of Feathers) need to FLY AND
needs to be knocked down to about a Level 60ish Nano.

C) Need to lose the Infra-Red on the Morphs, nice at night
but an abomination during the day. Not a good tradeoff.

D) Playful Cub needs a moderate boost to it's damage output.
Grinning Hunter could use +3%ish Crit, and either more
A/C boost or a Life Bonus. Perhaps go this route
Cub: +17 Dmg, +10% AMS, +1% Crit, +100 Run
Hunter: +34 Dmg, +25% AMS, +3% Crit, +100 AC, -100 Run
Dragon: +68 Dmg, +50% AMS, +6% Crit, +400 AC, -400 Run

E) There needs to be some morphs to fill the gap between
Dog & Cat, and Cat & Dragon. A scorpion as featured in
Beta would be a good intermediate step between Dog
and Cat. Perhaps an "Anun Tearer" for the step between
Cat and Dragon. (See section D, their bonuses would fit
in there somewhere).

2) Damage Shields

A) Alter Absorption to always aborb X amount of damage for
the given specialty. So for instance Firefly's Fury would
ALWAYS absorb 11 points of Fire Damage AFTER standard
Armor is considered. I say after because many "Mobs" have
insane minimum damage output especially in missions,
where they seem to strike for 65-85 dmg no matter what my
AC rating is for that class (and of course they strike twice
to 3 times as fast).

B) Add greater diversity to the damage shield line so that
all the various damage types are covered, stepping up
every 20 levels or so. This would allow Adventurer's to
keep Tanks and Docs covered with nice protection that
no other class can provide without imbalancing things
to much. Anyone can appreciate having less minimum dmg
wrought upon them.

3) Standard AC Buffs

A) Need a general boost to the existing Nanos.

B) Need a greater diversity, there are large gaps in the
coverage between existing AC Buff Nanos and will keep
them in better demand for team mates.

4) Standard Speed Buffs

A) Need additional steps after Free Movement, perhaps a
slight increase in existing values.

5) Weapon Oriented Buffs

A) If the Sybex Guide is still fairly accurate, would like to
see: (Stackable with Morphs and General Nanos).

+33 Pistol Buff
+66 Pistol Buff
+33 1HE Buff
+66 1HE Buff

I could accept a *reasonable* Psy requirement for these
buffs - there needs to be a trade off. I choose Pistol and
1 HE because as green skills, these are the natural paths
that Adventurer's tend to take and would hence benefit
from.

B) Multi-Ranged / Multi-Melee Buff, +20, +40, +60, +100

6) Root / Snare Nanos

A) Add some single-target and AoE style Roots, shorter
duration than the Nano Classes but still long enough to
be worth while.

7) Damage Nanos

A) To be honest this is more for diversity sake then jealousy
over what anyone else has. Would be ideal to have a
new "DD" every 20-25 levels, that is quick firing but does
decent "minimum" damage. Put a long refresh timer on
them [as opposed to "gimping" the damage they do],
hence still better to be an NT if you want better DD's
BUT still useful enough to be fun and worth while for
the adventurer. So put it on like a 3-5min timer but
does reasonably decent damage (similar to the Crat line,
though playing a Crat the diversity is a little to limited,
need to gain too many levels before getting the next
DD).

8) Healing Nanos

A) Decrease execution time on existing Heals

B) Add to diversity and availability of Team Heals, there
simply are not enough "steps" between the existing ones
to provide help. However, my self heals are pretty decent
but frequently way to slow to save my teammates life.
Obviously well below Doc's Nanos but more competitive
with MA's.

9) Abilities Buffs

A) Alter Eagle Eye to add +80 to all Evade Skills

B) Add Hawk Eye to add +100 to Melee/Ranged Init's

C) Add smaller [and larger] buffs along this line so there is
a reasonable progression (roughly every 25 levels or so).
The actual stats used should be a little less than the
Engineer oriented Nanos [for a given level] that do
something similar to this. Going along the lines of Jack of
all Trades but Master of None.

10) Adventuring Skill / Misc.

A) We have a dark green Adventuring skill but really very
little to do with it except bigger bags. Several have
suggested setting up "base camps" for various reasons.
I love this concept but not clear on how to best make
use of this idea. Another poster in this thread mentioned
the idea of creating a settlement where many of the
different classes can contribute/add to the settlement,
Adv's add a save terminal, fixers add a grid point,
traders add a shop, etc. What a killer idea I love it.
And when you get your first Yalhama you'll understand
there is plenty of room for this.

B) Add Plotting abilities to Maps or to give waypoints to
teammates since scouting (Adventuring) is our forte.

C) Temporary pets, someone mentioned a dog - Cool idea,
though still I prefer a furry Leet friend. The pet cannot
exceed your current level [ie will always con yellow to
you] and hence cant be "buffed". So again not as
effective as the Pet Classes but by no means useless.

I hope you will find these suggestions to make Adventurers more diversified and fun to play, but do NOT make us "Uber". We would be better equipped to handle solo play and be equally desirable as an addition to any team and without slighting other classes. Of course the drawback is playing an Adv might be so fun nobody will want to play any other class!

Thanks,

Avatarius

My main problems are with weapons.

Advs natural choices (green skills) are pistols and 1h edged.

Pistols are underpowered, and if you go 1h edged, going for anything else than riders is asking for trouble.

One really fonctionnal weapon out of dozens...

The problem is simply because the fighting rules doesn't work for quick, low damage weapons. That's why pistols and most 1h edged are gimped...

If you take slow, heavy weapons, you notice that the people using them have their damage decrease as they attack higher level mobs than them... With fast weapons, you can do damage really competitive to slow wepons over time against mobs with low ACs.

If you go against higher mobs, you notice that at some point you're capped to min damage and do virtually nothing to the mob, while heavy weapons still do something significant.

The problem is that, when grouped, you naturally go fight red mobs.... But with fast weapons, you can't contribute to effort of killing the mob anymore, as you damage over time gets ridiculous compared to your teammates. It makes such weapons worthless in PvP too.

Find some way to fix this (not by nerfing slow weapons, nor making fast weapons uber either, but by finding better rules to compute damage depending on ACs), and you'll make tons of people happy (and not just adventurers)

Beside that, I think that fixing indoor morphs should be done quickly. It looks like only a problem of too big collision boxes on the morphs models, shouldn't be too hard to fix... Or as somone suggested, scale down morphs when indoors, or always.

I don't think that we should get more fun than other classes, but some additional possibilities would be good.

Maybe some sort of very little useful pet, or some kind of roots, or something on their own that advs can do during fights.
Currently, ADVs (and some other classes I believe) have nothing to do when they fight - you just hit Q, launch specials when they're available, and hit the heal key whenever your life bar goes below a certain level.

I think that the debuff morphs someone suggested above are a very good idea, and it would bring some fun in all those mindless battles.
