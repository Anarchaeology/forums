Clan Leader experiences permanent amnesia
An accident has unfortunatly caused Judson Wagner, known as the leader of the Journeymen and having been regarded as a Counsel to the Council of Truth, who had been reportedly gearing up for a new attempt at peace amongst the Clans and Omni-tek, to suffer a bout of temporal delusion, followed by a state of amnesia to which Clan doctors state that there may be no possible recovery from.

Judson Wagner was out in the Mort Wastelands fighting Cyborgs, when he had reportedly come across some kind of radiation bomb, a relic from the old civil war. The bomb itself was situated in the desert wastes like a land-mine, causing a detonation once Mr. Wagner had stepped on device. The resulting wave of radiation that flooded his body, and the concussive blast from the ensueing shockwave, launched Wagner's body back into the wastes and left him uncoinscious.

It was several hours before another hunting party had come by and discovered Wagner's body, still alive. The radiation had poisoned his body, and he was immediatly rushed to the nearest Clan outpost for medical treatment.

After a through checkup and detoxification procedure, Wagner was in stable condition, but he had apparently no clue of his wherebaouts or who he was. The memories of his former self were apparently washed out by the shock to his body, and Doctors were fearful to try killing Wagner in order to restore his memories from a recent insurance copy, since they had not known when he had last done this.

Wagner is currently in stable condition, and, in light of the recent "loss" of their leader, Orionnt of Clan Journeymen has taken over as Clan Leader. Judson Wagner is currently recovering in Tir, but it is not known if he will ever fully recover from his condition.
