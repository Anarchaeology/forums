Technically speaking, Atrox appear male because of their muscle-mass. However they are, in effect mules. Atrox are a mix of human DNA with... something else. The combination for whatever reason does not make an adult that can reproduce, so the Atrox is condisered genderless.

I certainly know of Atrox that are male in appearance, but ladies at heart. Don't judge a book by it's cover.

Colonists from out of system are exclusively Solitus, and Atrox are produced by genetic modification of volunteers Soliuts coming on to the planet. These volunteers are mostly men, but some women do choose to undergo the process. Some Atrox are produced by modification of specimens from a stored egg-bank, but only the older Atrox are likely to have been created this way, due to the recent boom of new colonists.

The same genetic modification can be used to create a Nano or an Opifex from volunteer Solitus, however it is far more likely for members of these breeds to have grown up as Opifex or Nano, as the breeds are capable of reproduction. The children are carefully sheltered. However, you can find some teenagers out and about if you look (and don't shoot first).

Technically speaking each of the four breeds are a distinct species, as cross-marrages don't produce viable offspring "naturally." However, since we are all based on Solitus, an incompatable couple who really wanted to have children can get some assistance from Omni-Med (or their neutral or Clan counterpats) to create children based on the genetic codes of each of the parents. The process would consist of extracting the Solitus parts of the code from each of the parents, combining them into a new complete Solitus code, and then either applying an Atrox, Opifex, or Nano modifcation on the parents' (or Administration's) requrest, or letting the kid grow up as Solitus to make the decision herself which breed she wants to be when she comes of legal age.
