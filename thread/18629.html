RK1/Clan : F.L.S. (Social Guild)
Heya there !

If you feel an alone clanner and that you are wondering for a guild where you could develop your character, you might take a look at the Fellowship of the Lost Souls.

We are a social guild, mean that we are not pure hardcore roleplayers but we tend to act like real cool people, not playing in the hurry of leveling, and having good times with other fellows & clanners.... Having parties, attend to most of the public events, hunting/missioning with fun, get trip over the planet (adventuring), that's the way we live !

We are not that powerfull in terms of strenght, but some of us try to be good in trade & repair skills to earn their life in other way than missioning. Others just have forget to get levels, and just groaming in the city for months, gettin' fun with the people they talk in the street ! Some also wanna get at higher level to be free to do whatever they want, including having great trip over RubiKa or just be able to make better trade&repair things, or just be enought powerfull to clean the OMni Junkbots from our cities !! Just be sure that all the way to play are fit our guild if at least you stay "social".

Note that we are not active in PvP "attack mode", but we are ready to fight if our cities need to be defended ! Add to this, we are always trying to help the Clans by doing some secret mission with guildmates ! We fight the OT representatives, but we still do a difference between OT and people who lives in OT Territories. That explain why we refuse to attack without reasons any OT employee.

The website designed for the Guild is all open for the members, mean you are free to add all contents you want once you are a member ! Just check it at www.lostsouls.t2u.com. Starting with your own story, you can also write articles, maintain a Diary or just give your advice or whatever on the forum... There is also a newsletter at irregular intervals, to sum-up the main events of the week for those who forgot to check out the website. All is made to involve you in the guild and furthermore, in the game !!

There is many things i could tell you about the guild, but i won't write that here. If you are interested, you will find all the informations on our website : www.lostsouls.t2u.com.
You can also mail me for any questions (rahzelk@hotmail.com), i will answer fast !

We are recruiting because we really need a boost of members to start making some r0x0r things ! To make kewl things like Basher Ball or just doin' some static dungeons.... Well, you have understand that we really need you ! All applicants are welcome, whatever level (just note that we are currently a 50 to 90ish level guild) and most of all whatever profession !

You can also send a /tell at rahzelk

See Ya Soon !
