A better way to join teams...
Shouting "*level* *profession* LFG" is becoming old. There could be a better way to join teams much faster and thus make gameplay more action and less sitting and waiting. My idea would be to make a menu or an access terminal that lists all the teams within your level range or a specified levle range that are in your area. Once you find one you want to join you can send a request as well as your shift-click information. This will make teams much much easier to create and get into. This would be an excellent thing to implement... If there's any questions I can answer them, I have this all planned out in my mind
