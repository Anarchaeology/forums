Monster Parts: A detailed selling price.
If you dont know already, many people have taken up the the trade skill of monster part conversion to rake in some pretty decent cash. In order to do this, first you need any QL monster part (of coarse) and a biocommunicator within the level of the monster part. Bio communicators are found in any trade skill department in the chemistry tool terminals. They are a bit costly but the potential profit is just mega. A Ql 50 bio communicator should convert up to QL 100ish monster parts so you dont need to buy a really good bio-communicator. After you have both items, you simply take your biocommunicator and shift right click on the monster part- producing blood plasma that is MANY MANY times more valuable than the monster part itself. This does require the pharmecuetical skill though but i still raise it with my MA even though its dark blue because i make so much money. A QL 80 monster part takes about 200 pharmecuetical skill. This can be buffed by few engineers by 50, most agents by 50 and traders by like 150.

Now... lets look at mathematical calculations to show how benificial monsterpart conversions are.

The mathematical formula for monster part conversions is most accurately modeled by this formula:

C=1.377506130(x)^2 + -0.5481763(x) + 64.0914

Where x=the QL of the monster part and C=the amount of credits it will yield
