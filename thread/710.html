Nano casts interrupt ranged init
This seems fairly important to traders, so I thought I'd bring it up here and see if anyone else has noticed. I have a pretty slow firing rate due to my low light scope as I'm sure several of the other traders do as well. I time my nanos between shots as I'm sure most others do. It seems that now the nanos interrupt your ranged initiative meter once upon casting the nano at which point your shot meter restarts..

Fine whatever, the two meters don't work seperately now for all intents and purposes. I can see why they would want to do this. The real problem for me is that it also restarts the meter when a spell is cast on you now. So when casting a ransack the cast interrupts and then if you don't get the shot off before the ransack goes into effect the buff interrupts the init again.

Same thing with the health drains.. On cast and on heal it resets your shot meter effectively making you take twice as long to shoot and depending on the level of your gun probably making it not worth it to ever cast anything due to a much higher damage output by the gun effectively saving you more health in the long run. Our two part nanos make us the most badly hurt by this change from what I can tell.

Anyone else noticed this?

another discovery
Hey, I think I've figured out a good coping mechanism for the initiative reset. Since I've found out this was all working "as designed," I've played around quite a bit trying to figure out good timing and what could be done to offset the problem.

So, nanocasting resets the timer. If you cast right as the ranged timer is about to recharge, you eliminate the weapon's recharge time. =)

If you cast at this point, you have cut your recharge out of the picture and effectively are shooting then health draining at least as fast as previously. You still take longer on your debuffs as the timer resets again when the buff part of a ransack effects you, but not 3 times longer. Just 2.

Thx for some thoughts on low light scopes and the other confirmations. Sounds like we can try to make the best of this. Personally, I've just been upgrading my scopes as I go up in level of trade skill buffs and ranged init implants to keep myself in the positive on ranged init.

Can anyone with fairly high ranged init actually get off a shot before the ransack sets off the buff in your ncu? It seems really close to getting a shot off for me but it stops just short every time. If I could get that shot off, it might be worth investing more ip in ranged and dropping my low light scope to get a higher average rate like Sielar was suggesting.

Oh, and if you haven't noticed this problem you might set yourself a tad more defensive and see if that slows you down enough. It would be nice to hear if we can get past some of this as we level and get higher shot rate. I was much more successful in my missions last night after that one small discovery.

What i've noticed:

Weapons and nanos have an attack and delay value.

The attack value for your weapon is now paused when a nano is started.

The delay on the weapon is unaffected.

Delay on the nano does not stop the weapon timer.


Workaround: To run programs between shots, initiate the program immediately after firing a shot (not when your client thinks you shot, but right after you see damage from your shot register on the server). The nano attack bar will not interrupt the weapon delay bar and if you have anywhere near decent nano-init (very nice btw) your fire rate will hardly be effected at all. Unless you are using an OT-Windchaser or Triplejolts, they fire too quickly to get nanos in between without some interruption.

Vektors and Krutts give you plenty of time though.
