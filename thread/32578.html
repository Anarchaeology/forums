Am I here yet?
This seems like the thread to ask:

What is the attraction in fighting in an arena?

I honestly don't get it. I mean, I watch boxing IRL (which is the closest thing I can think of to arena fighting). And I respect fighters like Lewis, Tyson, Ali, ect. But I respect that street punk down the corner more, who comes out on top when he gets jumped outta nowhere. The fight wasn't pre-arranged. There were no 'rules' restricting the people intent on whupping his ass. So for him to come out on top shows more clearly his superior skills.

There seem to be 2 concepts battling for dominance in AO and because there's no clear direction it's ruining everything: PvP and all out war. PvP, which is best represented by the arena fights, promotes rules and fairness and equal chances. All out war, which is best (or do I mean 'worst') represented by the guy sniping people exitting the mission doorway in MMD. Whatever rules get implemented will affect both concepts and it's the fence straddling that is making things so damn frustrating for people on both sides (and me not participating in either).
