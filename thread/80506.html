black doors have been around for a very long time.

it seems the changes they made to reduce lag in missions have had some unfortunate effects. we get warped often in missions now.

i think this is a big part of the problem with the doors. the client loses synch with the server and you see black doors. the rest of the team can see you in a different location than you see yourself when this happens. it used to be a matter of waiting a bit, now it is a long wait; longer than most people are willing to wait. relogging, getting warped, and terminating are some very effective ways of dealing with this.

i'd like to stress that this is a bug(s) in something FC is doing to make our play lives better. i give them a lot of crap, but they are trying to do something good here and we should recognize that; granted they should have made sure it was working well before patching it to live ....
