Adding my voice to the indignant choir
While I appreciate what Funcom is trying to do, obviously they are working far too hard on the game... their brains must be completely fried from caffeine overdoses, lack of sleep, and knows what else to think that restricting or randomizing character names would be a good idea.

So, can someone here clarify on exactly what is going on? There seems to be a lot of conjecture and misinformation flying around.

What I've read so far seems to indicate that there will be first name, a nickname, and a last name for each character. Some component of this, most likely the nickname, will be the same for all the characters on that player's account.

(At least, I hope so. If "Jimmy 'no-neck' Jones" PKs me, and 2 minutes later, "Gunter 'no-neck' Schultz" offers to sell me replacement equipment, I'm far more likely to realize I'm being scammed by the same person (I'm quick that way) than if it's all completely different from character to character, rather than from account to account, and develop a permanent grudge against 'no-neck'.)

Hopefully there will also be a fourth component, the Guild, Clan, or Family name of the group the character belongs to, that of course will be set by being accepted into a group. In some ways, this could supplant the 'last name' field of the character.

A great advantage of a clan system that can be enhanced by readily visible affiliation names is that the reputation of the group is immediately affected by the reputation of it's composite players.

The name I plan to game by is Amichai. In full, Amichai ben Honnan. I suspect, if I'm allowed to, it will have to be "Amichai Honnan" in this system. I have no idea yet what to do with the nickname field, although I fear I may end up with "Foo 'Amichai Honnan' Bar" under the system as I currently understand it.
