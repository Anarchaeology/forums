Mobs are not as intelligent as most players (now...I said most...not all...)

Mobs don't use nifty tools, like first aid kits, and such. Taunts to keep the weaker in HP folks alive, etc..

Mobs don't use thier nanoprograms to thier best advantage (root/calm, run away, heal up, etc.)

When was the last time you saw a Crat mob calm the entire party, sit down, use a treatment kit, then procede to beat down the party one member at a time while everyone else just stood there drooling? Or a Fixer Mob actually do anything other than root you and stay standing next to you while your melee character just shrugged and kept pounding on it. An Enforcer mob Fear your Doc and then wipe the floor with the rest of the group.

Over all, if the mobs we fought were equal to us in HPs, there would be no challenge since the AI is very simple, and players are expected to be using the tools available to them.
