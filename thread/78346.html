Me and a guildie was doing some missions, and at the end of one mission, she got the black door syndrome.

Now, my client reported her to be 3 rooms away from what her client said she was. I managed to guide her so my client reported her to be at the exit, but that didn't help at all.

Anyway, she petitioned, and we got an ARK that ported her, which worked nicely. We also asked for a workaround for this.

The reply was that this is a server/client sync problem, and themost secure way to defend ourselves against it, was to wait about 1 second or so before entering each room, so the server and client could get time to "agree" on your whereabouts (not exact words of the ARK, though).

Seems abit daft to have to do that, but..... just thought I'd let you know. Oh, relogging also helps, but not allways.
