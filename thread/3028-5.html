Leave the agents their MP pets.


My main for a long time was a Meta and at the time I was somewhat annoyed with the Agent ability to FP into a Meta and summon pets. So, eventually I made an Agent thinking I could have meta pets and better combat ability than metas.

This is what I found out:

1)Metas take a looong time to buff up every 30 mins.
~ Agents in Meta FP take soo mucchhh loooonnnggger,
-2000 nano init + 250% of normal nano cost is just painful.

2)As any good pet owner knows, the only good pet is a blood red pet.
~Thx to the penalty to nano skills that FP inflicts, its highly unlikely an Agent will have very useful pets, being mostly orange or even worse.. light yellow!

3)Metas have green nano skills, and can raise 5/lvl.
~As was mentioned previously, Agent nano skills are normal blue while TS is dark blue, so natural nano skills are less than metas(which contributes to stopping them from getting blood red pets too).

4)Metas have new MP only weapons
~Agents can't make these weapons(something I found out the hard way since it doesn't really say in the description).

5)Finally, I found MA FP much more useful: just crit the crap out of everything, works much better than green pets anyway.


<edit> Also, someone mentioned doing deprive/ransack as FP: Trader before assuming FP: MP. Have you actually tried doing that?!

Assume FP: Trader, wait 28.5 mins so you have like 1.5 mins left before hostile nanoprogram runs out. Very slowly cast TS expertise, very slowly cast BM expertise, very slowly cast MC expertise, very slowly cast deprive, very slowly cast ransack, very slowly cast ransack again since nanoskills in FP suck and the leet resisted. As soon as FP: Trader runs out cast FP: MP, slowly cast TS mastery, slowly cast BM mastery, slowly cast.. doh! deprive ran out...

And thats not including the several times you had to sit and use nanokits(250% of normal cost is worse than trying to cast while wearing tank armor).


<2ndedit> One more thing, I play neither MP nor Agent any longer. Why?

1)Agent has been nerfed so much its utterly boring, it stated in the manual somewhere that Adventurers are the 'jack-of-all-trades', I say the Agents are, they are ok at everything(access to most nanos) but excel at nothing(too many penalties to excel at anything)... boorrring.

2)MP has become the most powerful profession in the game, almost to a fault, after killing hundreds of frothing blood red mobs(after the first meta-loving patch) while just standing there watching and shuffling my feet, I realized how boring it had become to play Meta, heck I didn't even bother to try out the 3rd pet, probably make things even easier than it is and thus more boring... sooo, I don't know why Metas are whining about this agent with pet thing, they are nowhere near as powerful as a real meta.
