Every buff in the game. ( using ql 200 items at level 80)

Many of the original isues with Agents using other profs (notably MP pets) before the "native" profession has been resolved by FC by either a) making them not avaliable to the Agent (as with higher level MP pets) or b) placing a level requirment on the new nanos.
Agent can only cast nano's of another profession while in either:

False Profession:
Assume Profession:
Mimic Profession:

The only differences between these is the debuffs on skills and time the profession runs for, the areas effected are:

Nano Cost Modifer
Nano Init Debuff
Nano Range Debuff
Casting Interrupt Debuff
HUGE debuff to nano skills (-25, -80, and -140)

This means that False Profession is mainly used for buffing yourself and others. Because of the BIG hit to init, it's not feasible to cast anything during combat while in false prof. It has a low hit to nanoskills, which means you can cast all those good buffs on your team mates before going into the mission.

Mimic profession has a big hit to nanoskills (it's big no matter what level you are), and therefore is not recommended for buffing purposes. But because of the (relatively) low hit to init, it is more useful for combat casting. Eg. mezzing, TMS, team-heals, etc.

Assume prof is supposed to be some sort of middle ground. It has very little use though, because it has both quite a large hit to init and to skills. I only ever use it, for example, if I want to grid a team out of a mission. Mimic Fixer isnt on my list of to-get nanos, but with assume, I can grid the team out quite a bit faster than with FP. That's the only worthwhile use I have found for assume, and to be honest, if I didn't have it, I could do just as good a job with FP. In other words, assume is useless as it is today.

All of them rely in the Agent plowing additional IP into skills in order to try and counterbalance the debuffs / mods, we do not get them at zero cost and are stuck in that profession until the nano wears off (so dont ask an agent for a Fixer Speed buff and them immediatly after anyother prof buff as we can not do both close together).
