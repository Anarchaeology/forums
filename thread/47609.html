/me hugs Cat

Let's get back to the origional topic, shall we? Cat is entirely correct, I have been seeing more and more of the same thing myself lately(possibly due to the fact that we know many of the same people, but still......). There are usually either one of two different scenarios people tell me when we're chatting about how they would like to become Neutral:

1. I used to be Neutral up until <insert level>, but I switched because <insert thing> and it didn't work out/wasn't right for me, and now I can't be Neutral again <sad>

2.I've been <insert faction> since day one, but now I'm just sick of <infighting/griefing/PvP/RP/KSing/whatever> and don't want to be <insert faction> anymore, but I don't want to join <other faction> either <sad>

oh, and I forgot one:

3.I used to be Neutral but I kept getting ganked by <insert faction> so I joined <other faction>, but now I've decided I can't stand them both <sad>

What I believe my dear friend Cat is trying to accomplish is to figure out exactly how this is happening, so it can be prevented. After all, that's just one more unhappy player out there, and who wants that? And no, being a Neutral is NOT to be taken lightly, as there are hardships aplenty awaiting you.....it takes COMMITMENT and RESOLVE to play a Neutral, something I'm very surprised the Factions don't understand. Labeling Neutrals as "lazy" and "fencesitters" couldn't be further from the truth. Sorry, I got off the subject......this is not the place for where my post was heading. If you want further insight into Neutrals, check out the link in my sig. My post is on the first page, and yes, you can quote me(from that post) as long as it is taken entirely in context. And by the way, the scenarios above are not meant in any way to trivialize or be offensive, I'm using them as standardized examples only.
