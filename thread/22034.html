Request for 14.2: Fix the Grenade Weapon Line (fast fix below)
Ok, so grenade launchers don't work. As an engineer I would LOVE to be a grenade user instead of a dual pistol or <shudder> a ubiquitious shotgun user.

The problem with grenade launcers is the reload on single-clip launchers. This is obviously a problem. Here is my quick and dirty solution.

Solution #1: Make all grenade launcers use a clip of 6. In RL today most Rifle-style launchers use 6-clips. To make up for this the reset time may need to be increase. Go for it. This would be a fair tradeoff for our launchers.

While you are at it, think about this:
Suggestion #1: Grenades of a given level don't seem to do much damage. What? Shouldn't that massive reload (or reset) time be made up in massive damage per hit?

Suggestion #2: Grenades should be AoE. It's an exploding grenade! should it be doing AoE damage?

Suggestion #3: Since Grenades are AoE type of damage (even if to a single MoB), have only 1/2 the damage of a grenade affect the hate list.

With the 14.2 OE change engineers will no longer be the kings of PvM. That's cool. I have no problem with that. But let us use the weapons that were designed for us appropriately. Currently grenades are simply unusable, for the simple reason of a reload bug. Increase the magazine and give us our launchers!
