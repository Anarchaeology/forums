Housing again
My first experience with a MMORPG came when I started playing Ultima Online at launch in 1997. Today I still have an active UO account and I still enjoy my adventures in the lands of Britannia.

I started playing Anarchy Online after hearing about it from a friend, a couple of months after launch. So far I am enjoying the game more and more every day, but there is one thing I think is absolutely necessary to keep interest in such games over time. That is to feel that you belong somewhere, finding your identity and place in the world.

In UO I have my house where I can relax, store items, decorate the interior and meet friends. This possibility also exists to a certain degree in AO, but very few people find it useful enough in its current state. So here's a few thoughts about what housing in AO could be:

1. Every citizen of Rubi-Ka should be given a basic apartment, maybe like the one we get now. But in addition I think it would be nice if we could rent some of the finer houses that are located all around Rubi-Ka.
Pay enough credits every month, and live in one of the fine houses around the Last Ditch. The you have a short walk to Reets Retreat and home again. And if you can't afford it, rent a shack somewhere further out in the wild, or even in the junkyard.
Number of rooms and layout should vary according to what kind of house/apartment you are renting and how much you pay for it.

2. We should be able to place more furniture, and different kinds of furniture. And storage containers, like chests and closets. Give us the option to give other people access to our apartment and containers too. So friends and organizations easily can share items, and so that it would be much easier to transfer items between your own characters, not least.

3. Add some interactive apartment items, like a television set with the latest news info. An insurance booth perhaps? Or maybe a cleaning bot or a wine-serving bot. Both attackable, of course.

Having storage containers and the possibility to transfer items betwen your own characters is perhaps what I miss the most, comparing AO to UO. Oh, and give us an extra month if we forget to pay the rent. I need that in RL too.
