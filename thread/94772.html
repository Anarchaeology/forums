Mobs will often head to the doctor on spotting a different teammate if we used the team health buff. This is because there's a slow, small HOT effect on that buff, that is tied back to you as regards aggro. When the HOT ticks, I believe the mob builds "hate" towards you as if you were healing the person in question. Even if that person is at full health.

At higher levels this is less of a problem, because players start dishing enough damage to get NPC attention while the amount your buff ticks for is pretty negligible.

Bosses (and other mobs) hold on to their hate list order when you go between levels with a button. This is because you aren't zoning, you're sort of warping from one area to another inside the same zone. The boss stops attacking you becaues it can't reach you (you're out of range), but once you come back into range it will re-aggro based on your old hate value. If someone else hasn't out-aggroed you before you go back up, you'll get aggro back.

The only solution there is to team with people who do lots of damage. Aggro enhancers and other taunts don't really work that well. Also, low damage hits generate less aggro than high damage hits, even if the low damage hits are coming faster and doing more damage over time. So, an enforcer with a hammer or a beam would outaggro an enforcer with a pipe/staff combo, unless the pipe/staff enforcer gets a good string of crits.

MAs with crit buffs tend to generate lots of aggro when they crit a few times in a row, two seconds apart each time.

At lower levels the key is having a higher level enforcer that knows how to taunt. Dont leave home without it! And dont team with lower level enfs when you yourself are in your fragile levels (50ish, 90ish, 120ish).

Also be aware that your hp buffs (long hp, short hp and HoTs) have a taunt to them. If your team doesnt do sufficient dmg, you will get aggroed. This is especially true if you do dmg in addition to your healing. And of course team heal does taunt also. So does single heal, but not as much as team heal.

Always use best hpbuff that you possibly can on your self, and if you have ncu issues (too little ncu) then choose hp and defense over +dmg buffs.

Find the right aggro/defensive setting for your slider that will let you cast your heal as fast as you can, but without being at full aggro if possible. Always max nano init, and use nano init buff on yourself always.

Doctors will always be near the centre of attention, because we are the key of life in team, and mobs doesnt like that. Above are ways of dealing with this. In addition I reccomend implanting shining, bright and faded max life.

You cant avoid aggro in all cirumstances, but you will soon learn to deal with it, and recognize situations where you most likely will get it. Planning ahead is the key to survival.

Good luck in your adventures! I hope you can use these pieces of advice.

Ya, this is pretty much the mantra of the lower lvl doc.

Your team HP buff builds up agro over time. thise little dinky points add up. The only way to avoid that part of it is just not to buff HP. I doubt any team would accept that.

Secondly. Single heal as much as possible. A 1000 point heal is the same agro as hitting a mob for 1000 points. its a numbers game as far as the math is concerned. it adds up all the numbers you have for "your" agro, and then the mob goes after the person with the highest number. so.

1 gun attack = 1000 points of dmg = 1000 agro points


1 heal = 1000 points healed = 1000 points of agro.

HoT = say.. 100HP every 5 seconds. after 15 seconds. = 500 agro points

Team heal = 6 people all healed for 1000 points = 6000 points of agro.


you get the idea.

As for armor. use Cyber Armor, Bau/Waiit if you can get it, untill you can put on Biomech. use biomech till you can put mkii on.
