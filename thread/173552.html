Planetfall day celebrations delayed by Omni-Tek Rubi-Ka CEO Philip Ross
Omni-HQ, RUBI-KA (Sept. 3, 29477) - Unexpectedly a few minutes ago, we received the following short recorded statement from OT-RK CEO Phillip Ross. Here is a transcript of the statement.
quote:
Due to the events during the last week, and disturbing reports from Omni-Tek departments on unresolved and unexplained matters, I have decided to delay todays celebration of the 775th anniversary of Planetfall one day.

I will therefore personally address the citizens of Omni-Tek in a speech tomorrow.
CEO Ross did not reveal in his statement neither time nor location for tomorrow's speech.

Rafreal
Senior Reporter
