There's a lot that could be said, but let's start simple.

- I like to solo missions
- I like building relationships with other players for hunting
- I like to be rewarded for the time I spend fighting
- I like to be rewarded for planning and playing smart

The pull of this game (IMHO) is that by being smart I can plan implants/buff combinations that maximize my return on the time I invest in the game. It adds a "thinking" aspect to a game that would otherwise be rather dull. This also allows me to gain a decent amount of experience without investing 15 hours a day.

A heavy penalty for overequipping removes much of this flexability. People want to be rewarded for their determination (if playing brute force) OR smarts (if playing buffed). Please consider lowering the penalty for those that aren't radically overequipped (<75%).

This is a high-tech world and those who employ that tech to the fullest should be rewarded, not hindered.


If you want to fix something laughable, look at the MAs. How is he hitting me from 5+ yards away? Why can a MA hit me with his fists for more damage than I can hit him with an Assault Rifle? Why can they hit while moving and I can't fire rifle at a guy right in front of me while I'm walking? One would think that the game designers have been spending too much time on DAoC...

For more fun, look at Agents. An "aimed shot" can be fired instantly...where's the "aim" part? Wouldn't it make more sense to have a few second buildup time once the attack is initiated? That way they could take the time and do an aimed shot during regular combat. If a MOB is attacking another team member, an agent should be able to take his time and pull off an aimed shot while the MOB is distracted.

Not many Crats posting on this thread... I think I know why.

As a lvl 91 Crat I don't think this 14.2 patch will really affect me as bad as it will other Prof's.

My equipment:
- I wear the Cyborg Armor (ql 77) for the NCUs it provides - AC isn't all that important as I don't get hit frequently.

- I use a ql 40ish SA Home Defender just so I don't hit very fast if something aggros me (after mezzing, etc.).

- I use the Nuke and Mezz more than any weapon anyway. I always charm the best blood red mob i can find.

- Sure, I will end up using a drk orange pet (about ql 110-115) after this patch goes in. Really, the Crat pets are pretty lousy when I can charm a ql 125-130 mob pet that has better AC/Buffs and 4x HPs.

When SOLOing, I've found that the Mob pets are so effective that sometimes I won't create the bot pet. Why bother?! Don't get me wrong. The bot pets are useful, but aren't of the caliber of the Mob charmed pets. Hands down, the mob pets rock. The OE cap doesn't affect my ability to run the current charm(s) either, but could be an annoyance when I decide to run around with Mocham's BM/PM/SI to do the next long term charm... still, the short duration of the charms at that level (about 11min) makes it a moot point.

When SOLOing, I do like gettin Mocham's though, and usually can get through a hard mission in an hour or hour and half. I suppose I would just use the big bot pet with a charm pet until Mocham's wears off and then downgrade to the best bot pet I can do on my own and still have the insanely powerful mob pet too.

So, at level 91, I can (and do) solo ql120-130 missions (about 80% hard) - either fighting or mezzing, depending on how I feel - without a lot of trouble.

If I am on a team that I am not leading, I usually just chain-mezz and attack with the bot pet. I won't charm a mob unless the team is cool with it (some people have a strange aversion to using a great mob pet ... go figure).

Perhaps this "correction" patch will help Crats to fulfill their role as "crowd controller" and make them valuable to teams who are reeling from the OE nerfs. If a Crat can solo an 80%+ mission, then a team can easily get through missions of equivalent or higher level.

So, if anyone needs assistance with a hard mission run - I'll be happy to assist if I can - just send Bravvo a tell (RK1/Clan).

Unfortunately, the 14.2 "OE Nerf Patch" (as it will come to be known) does mean that my mid-to-high-level Soldier, Agent, and MA could be rendered fairly whimpy unless the AO team drops the AC/HP of the mobs to a reasonable amount and raises the exp value of yellow/orange ones.

In these other classes, I've felt a mandatory need to OE as much as possible just to be able to fight monsters WORTH killing. When you need 800k-1.2mill exp, 2-4k exp per mob is pretty funny (in a bad sense).
