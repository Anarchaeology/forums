Apartments..we need an official response
The apartments are "envisioned" to see some changes. However, these changes are vague in general. After reading the boards and experience in the game, here are some matters I wish to suggest..alright. I insist on these:

1) New furniture types such as...
*sofas
*loveseats
*chair/ottoman
*coffee tables
*dining room tables & chairs (sold seperately of course)
*TVs (holographic models would be a good idea for top-of-the-line)
*beds (many different types and sizes; don't forget bunkbeds)
*more art (paintings, statues, vases, etc)
*more lamp types (is strobe or lava possible?)
*microwaves (standing and wall mounted)
*table items (small plants, drink sets, plates, books, etc)
*bookcases/dining cabinets

I think everyone would agree, that beds and sofas are the most desired.

2) Increase the item limit for apartments from 10 to at least 40 (yes, 40)! With 16 items, I can make my hallway look sleek.

3) Design-an-apartment! Give the option to allow people to choose the style of their apartments, from either a studio up to a 3 bedroom apartment (or more). Also, allow texture choices per room please (would you like white or blue walls?). A fee for any of this is understandable.

4) Bank access in apartment! If this is truely the land of the future, shouldn't we have access to our accounts from our own home? If not, let us have a Chest of sorts to allow storage of items in our humble homes. Making the Chest (maybe different sizes) a shop item would be acceptable.

5) Theft protection! A few people have had the misfortune of inviting people into their homes to have their furniture stolen. Adding a rule about only the owner may pick up an item in his/her apartment would help.

6) Bathroom! Missions have them, so shouldn't we? Also, if this is implemented, allow toilets, sinks, mirrors, etc. to be sold at Furniture Stores.

7) FREE Cell Scanner! Let us save in our own homes with zero cost. Making this a furniture item wouldn't be a bad idea either.

8) Food items don't dissolve in apartments! To coincide with the dining items suggested earlier, let us set up a meal and not have it disappear. I'm not sure if they still do or not concerning this matter, but I thought I'd mention it to be safe.

9) Shop-at-home! In designing your dream apartment of the future, allow us to have a furniture kiosk in our own homes. Using the old story of "nanobots converging to produce" line would make it stick with the elements of the game.

I would appreciate an OFFICIAL response from Funcom's Development team on each point. I would also like to hear, if I forgot any, other general requests for apartment improvements. The homes are nice, Funcom, but it's time they get an overhaul!
