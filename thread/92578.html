The only advantage an atrox Soldier has to a Solitus is his HP - its about 700-750 HPs because breed advantage and about 200-300 more with Cyborg implants.
Any other stuff can be worn by Solitus as well.

Solitus advantage is the earlier ability to use the highest nanos with a better implant setup. Even at lvl 200 Solitus have a better flexibility to change implants for better setups. I.e. Solitus Soldier can get about 960 Nanoresist w/o gimping themselves in attackrating or in TMS - almost never possible for Atrox unless they have superior towersetups and guildadvantages. Nanoresist is one of the most important skills for Soldier. This makes you able to escape real bad situations. Atrox will have about 105 (a shining headcluster) less in Nanoresist, which makes them more vulnerable for roots and debuffs. Thats seems to be only a small disadvantage, but it makes a huge difference if you ever get in fight with those classes.

80 pts less in intelligence compared to a Solitus means 16 pts less in each nanoskill. Doesnt sound much, but a simple incompetence could destroy your TMS ability...

Atrox Soldier are better in duels against non-debuffing professions, because their advantage in HP takes full effect, but Solitus are well rounded and their defensive ability due to NR is much better. HPs are good, but not everything in a fight - choose your breed wise and you will be satisfied.
