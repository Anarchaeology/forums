Omni-InternOps call for Applicants
To all loyal employees:

We are spies. We are assasins. We operate best in shadow, and shine brightest in stealth.

Our purpose is knowledge. We excel at determining threats in advance. We are known for our ability to remove threats before they might harm the Corporation.

We do not make friends, we keep employees honest. This often means trailing and dealing with those who appear loyal to the Corporation, yet who may truly not be. This often means making enemies; yet our only enemies are those who threaten our beloved Corporation.

It is under these key principles that we operate. Any further information is classified and will be proffered on a "need-to-know" basis.

We use our skills to better the life of Omni-Tek, its employees and its operating subsidiaries. Our goals are dynamic and shift with the times; whereas at one time, the likes of SPARTA and Special Interception Troops concerned themselves with the threats posed by the Dust Brigade and the Council of Truth, we now face more uncertainties. We have the Notum Wars, Jobe and Shadowlands. We have a reappearence of Henry Radiman. We have many unknows that need our eyes watching over them, and our hands guiding them.

These are glorious days for Omni-Tek. These are glorious days for mankind. Use your gifts wisely, always show loyalty, and above all else, always keep a watchful eye on everything around you...

Those interested, please visit Omni-InternOps gridnet message board, register, and follow the instructions outlined by Director Craddock on the "Recruitment Board".

((OOC: We are a new 100% RPing org, led by a couple of old-time RPers, looking to fill a void Omni-Tek side left by the departure of most (if not all) of the Omni-InternOps organizations. At this time, we have no minimum level requirement. However, we do require that you, at no time in your career, have been a clanner, in order to stay as in-character as possible. Only those serious about RP need apply.))
