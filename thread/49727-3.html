"Click"

Stormrazer's world shook around him as Deckrd pulled that trigger...

"Click", Deckrd whispered, his madness apparent in even so small a noise...

Deckrd then shoved her away, and discarded the empty gun, swiftly replacing it with another that Edwin had no doubt was loaded.

He aimed this gun at Barlau as her knees buckled, and she sank to the floor. Snarling, Stormrazer lunged, his weakness forgotten in rage, all he was saw was red and Deckrd's insane, laughing face...

Deckrd was caught off guard and began to swing the gun around. Edwin realized he wouldn't get the gun around in time, and roared at Deckrd...

"How DARE YO-" and was cut short by wracking pain that clouded what remained of his vision.

Deckrd had racked him with a swift kick that stopped Stormrazer cold. It was all Edwin could do to remain standing, but he continued to do so, glaring at what he hoped was Deckrd with pure malevolence while Deckrd doubtless had the his gun levelled at him.

If I can just keep the gun on me... If he shoots me, he has no reason to hurt her... Death is nothing new to me... he thought to himself as he fought to keep the pain from blurring his thoughts as well as his sight.

"You have no right... I did nothing to your girlfriend, you know that. I didn't even know she was a Clanner... Yet you persist in this..." Stormrazer hissed through clenched teeth, he could almost make out Deckrd's face now, and the large gun aimed right at his throat. Deckrd pushed Storm back a few steps, and then began to pull the trigger...

If I can just keep the gun on me...

"You have no right... I did nothing to your girlfriend, you know that. I didn't even know she was a Clanner... Yet you persist in this..." Stormrazer hissed through clenched teeth...

She had seen Deckrd's kick connect, heard Stormrazer's words, but it all seemed sort of distant, as if she were watching from outside it all. She saw Deckrd push Storm, aim the gun at him. She even saw his finger tighten around the trigger.

Get up get up get up get up get up, she told herself. She got to her feet, and scrambled to stand in front of Stormrazer. Deckrd's finger relaxed again, and he gave a laugh. If she lived to be a thousand years old, she knew she'd never forget that laugh. She hoped she'd never hear another laugh like that again...

Deckrd held the gun pointed at her face. "I can shoot her here, and she'll die instantly". He lowered the gun, pressed it against her belly. "Or, I can shoot her here, and she'll die a slow, incredibly painful death.... which will it be?" Behind her, Stormrazer was trying to move her out of the way, put her behind him. She stubbornly refused to be dislodged, her eyes fastened on Deckrd's face, his features distorted with rage.

Stormrazer started to move out from behind her, but she was too quick, keeping herself positioned between him and Deckrd's gun. Deckrd gave another short, harsh laugh, and simply aimed the gun over her head, right at Stormrazer's.

"Isn't that sweet?", he sneered. "Both of you, willing to die for the other... but not to worry, you both will die".

She swallowed hard, but didn't move, except to slip her hand into Stormrazer's. He pressed it reassuringly.

The expression on his face shifted, became almost crafty. "So... who dies first?"

There was no response, her eyes locked painfully on Deckrd's. He repeated his question, sotto voce, "Who dies first?"

She didn't understand what happened next. His face crumpled, and he raised the gun, pressing it against his own temple.

"Me", Deckrd whispered....

Barlau seemed stunned by Deckrd sudden move to put the gun to his own head... Stormrazer was less effected, he knew Deckrd was insane, and he understood why. It didn't make him any happier about it.

"You narcissistic bastard..." Edwin growled silently.

"First you interrupt my recovery, you threaten both her life and my own..." He snarled.

"And now you're going to kill yourself? No..."

Edwin gently pushed Barlau away and behind him, before continuing.

"Let me."

Deckrd looked up at him with eyes brimming with tears, and silently handed Stormrazer the gun.

Stormrazer held it for a moment, then levelled it back at Deckrd's forehead.

Edwin had only said what he did to get the weapon out of Deckrd's control, but now that he had the gun, all the pain and chaos Deckrd had caused him in that short time howled for vengeance...

Deckrd glared at him with clenched teeth and damp eyes, daring him to pull the trigger.

Edwin tightened his grip on the handle, his vision still blurred from the effort of keeping the pain he was in from weakening him.

He looked over at Barlau, various emotions played across her face like shadows in candlelight, fear, concern, confusion...

He caught her gaze, and for one blissful second the pain didn't register, and order was restored to the chaos within.

He clenched his teeth and looked back at Deckrd. Stifling a sigh, he lowered the gun and stepped back...

And then, just like that, it was over.

She could hear a loud humming in her ears, and she swallowed hard, forced herself to take a few deep breaths. Stormrazer was still standing where he was, gun lowered at his side. Deckrd had turned, was walking out of the room. From behind, he looked like a defeated old man....

She found her voice then, addressing Storm.. "Where is he going?"

Stormrazer gave a little shrug, his voice hard. "Probably off to kill himself somewhere".

She stared at him, without actually seeing him. She was thinking of the look on Deckrd's face, when he had handed the gun to Storm. Grief... loneliness... sorrow... she understood all of that.

She ran to the door, just in time to see Deckrd vanish around a corner. "Wait!", she called to him, "Deckrd, wait!"

Stormrazer was already next to her, his hand on her arm. "Are you crazy?", he asked incredulously, "he was going to kill us!"

"But he didn't", she replied, "and now he's going to go do something rash. We can't let him do that. He needs help".

So they ran up one hallway, and down the next, catching up with Deckrd. Who turned to look at them with empty eyes. "Deckrd", she said, "are you going to be all right?" Next to her, Storm made a little sound of derision, muttered something that sounded like, "when is he ever all right?" Barlau ignored this, her eyes still on Deck's face. "Deck, it's going to be okay. If you want to search for her, I'll help you".

Deckrd's face suddenly showed terrible terrible remorse. He seemed unable to speak for several moments, then said, in a sort of wondering voice... "I was going to kill you, and now you're offering to help me... you're so kind.... and I've been such a fool"...

"It's all right", she said softly, "I understand".

She didn't understand, really. Even when her father had died, when she wished she could just die, too.. well... it had never occurred to her to kill herself. Or anyone else. His thinking was foreign to her..

She wasn't certain what to say next. She bit her lower lip, trying to think of something, when Storm moved next to her. She turned her head just in time to see him collapse. She caught his weight fully, staggering. "Help me", she said to Deckrd, and he slipped his arm around Storm's waist. The two of them lowered him to the ground, sitting him up against the wall. She studied Storm's face, worried. He was conscious, breathing, but his skin had a bluish tinge. He looked exhausted.

Storm managed to smile, even to talk, "I'm all right, just a bit tired. I just need to rest for a minute". She already had a stim out, and plunged it into his tricep. She watched anxiously until the color entered his cheeks. She pulled a bottle of water out of her bag, broke the seal, and pressed it to his lips. "Drink", she ordered. He obeyed, sipping at the water.

Deckrd and Barlau sat with him for some time, while he slowly regained his strength. The three of them sat in silence, no one saying anything, until she felt as if she couldn't stand it another minute. This place... these two men... the day it had been... she wanted to leave it all behind.

She cleared her throat, they both looked at her. She said softly, "I'm sorry, I have to go. I can't bear this any longer". She got to her feet, managed a small smile for them. Both of them opened their mouths to say something, but she held up her hand, shaking her head. "I know you will both be fine. If you'll excuse me, I need to leave now."

She slipped out the upper entrance of the Reet Retreat, walked down the ramp, and stepped into the warm afternoon sun.
