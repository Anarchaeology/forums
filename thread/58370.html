Bug Hunter Dept Communications
Greetings everyone,

As some of you may have heard, the ARK Bug Hunter Department has been undergoing some changes. It was felt that the members of the Bug Hunter Department needed more abilities in order to better assist you, the members of the Test Population, in uncovering bugs in existing and new content.

First thing to be tackled was opening more lines of communications between the ARK Bug Hunter Department and the Player Base. Part of this was implemented by granting members of the Bug Hunter Department posting access to the public Test Server sub forums.
