Tales of a Merc. part 1
The pay was good, no doubt about that. 500k creds for what seemed an easy mission, would go a long way when it was time to upgrade my old Nova. Half up front, and half when my client received proof the job was done seemed fair enough.
There were also no doubt that the "job" to be done was a dirty one. My yata-scope told me that my target was exactly 24.56321 meters away, and he never moved more than 2 meters from his original position. He never stood still either, guess that's one of the things that made me able to spot a fixer a mile away. They all have "fleas in their blood", and this one seemed to have something else running through his vains too, probably a highly concentrated form off meta-amphetamines or something like that. Didn't really matter, if he was "high" it would only make my job easier.

My back was aching from sitting crouched for several hours behind the run-down shack just outside Borealis. This place was the place to go when having a fix as target, as this was the Core of Rubi-Kas blackmarket. This guy, Tyranos Erke had been here pushing his syringes and NCU fantasy progs all day, and I was just biding my time till he was alone. I couldn't risk interference from outsiders, my client had demanded absolute confidentiality on this hit. I didn't ask why, only who and when. Besides, my chances against one of these guys was already slim, I sure as hell couldn't handle two. I had to time my attack to perfection, or else it would only be dust left by the time I managed to haul my trox ass into gear.

As the last of his customers walked happily towards Bor, the twin suns were setting on the desert horizon of Rubi-Ka. An old song I heard as a kid somewhere came to mind, something like "...every junky' s like a setting sun" I couldn't help but smile at the irony. I gritted my teeth and fired up my combat progs, as silently as I could. Erke was too busy gloating over the creds made from an "honest" days work, to notice the high frequency sound my old Nova made as I fired it up.

The rusty metal plate that had been my cover, both against the suns and curious eyes, hit the ground with a deafening sound as I made a sideways roll to a kneestand position. Through my hud-reticle, I saw my target immediately understood what was going on. It took all my concentration to keep him "locked" as he turned, and bolted for the safety of the city. He started dodging left and right in a zig-zag kinda pattern, and I thought: Goddamn he's fast!
The tell-tale smell of ozon filled the air, as I fired off my favorite Nova combo: one shot-flingshot-one shot, followed by a quick burst. I cursed as I saw the first two shots streaked inches to the side of his head, as he suddenly changed his direction of movement. But when the third shot connected solidly with his right leg, the whitehot plasma cutting right through his flimsy brontohide armor, he knew himself "playtime" was definately over. He screamed in pain and stumbled for a sec, that was more than enough time for me to get "lockon" again. I felt my muscles stiffen to prepare for the jolt I would get from the burst only millisecs away and I was momentarily blinded by the piercing lights created by my attack. He fell to the ground a moment later sporting a big, black hole in his back where my burst had hit him.

I walked over to the corpse, almost loosing my dinner to the smell of ozon and charred opifex flesh. I muttered a: " sorry kiddo" as I bent down to cut off his right pinky...
