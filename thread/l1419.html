Freelance Investigator - New Rumors Land in the Territories
In Rubi-Ka there are all kinds of people, many of them fighting each other for the same reasons. Maybe if they took a moment, they would discover that there is no enemy, and the peace they long for is just one treaty signature away. At least that is what many freelance reporters thought, or at least one reporter we know of -- searching out the truth to prove she could make a difference; typing out a message to readers she may never meet. It didn't matter, somehow she felt connected.

**Grid uplink connecting Password accepted Welcome to the Rubi-Ka Grid

I'm Lacey, a freelance reporter for the citizens of Rubi-Ka. I had been in the territories for months, trying to get information on the mysterious medusa and enigma mutants. For some reason I did not have much luck, almost like they were avoiding me or I was just looking in all the wrong places. We all heard the stories, about how some medusas or enigmas have helped an adventurer from certain death, but any contact with them directly has been unsuccessful.

Out in the territories you don't see many other travelers, so when you do you usually settle around the campfire and swap stories as long as no one tries to kill each other. One such traveler was from my hometown of Newland. He shared a rumor about some new organization forming in the neutral city. This was enough to spark my curiosity. The secrets of the medusas and enigmas could be found out another day. I asked for more information as I decided my destination for the next day. I would begin the long journey back to Newland and ask anyone I meet about this new organization.

I wasted no time on my new investigation. Beginning at first light, I traversed the dangerous swampy lands of the territories in my effort to return to Newland. It had been a long day of avoiding death, and I finally have a moment to think over what my campfire companion of the night before had told me.

He mentioned ICC and that caught my attention more than anything else he had to say -- something about their program to help individuals with problems. Baffled me to think about it. Questions popped into mind, Why? Why now? If the ICC cared at all about Rubi-Ka why did it let so many people battle over land and politics? It was welcome news but so unrealistic that it couldn't be true. No one helps anyone without reason. That is just how it was, but imagine what it would be like to call on someone you could trust and know they can help you back on your feet, without expecting anything but a thank you. Maybe it was just rumor and no truth to it. Maybe the story had so many telling it that it no longer resembled the original news I am determined to find out.
