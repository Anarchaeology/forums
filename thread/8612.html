for starters, I'd have to say, if 20 seconds isn't long enough for you to cast 2 nukes and refresh the root, you're using to big a nuke. Those 'lifestopper' nukes are very inefficient for pvp...longest cast time nuke you should use is 5sec. Not just because they cast insanely fast for an NT, but also because they take far less nano than the big nukes and your damage over time/nano cost is alot better off.

With regards to your damage...as of a patch or two ago they halved ALL damage done in pvp, not just specials. With regards to specials, a special attack can never do more than 40% of the targets total health in damage...they may have also included this rule with regards to all attacks, but I"m not sure...

edit:

I forgot to add that pvp for NTs in the arena is generally bad as it is...you simply can't get far enough away from someone to not get hit...NTs have to use their range for any sort of advantage...
