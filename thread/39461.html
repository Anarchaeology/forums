Trying to be constructive, I believe there are two types of RP, and every type of RP can be split into these two camps:

Type 1 is "Social, which is only interesting up to a certain point. It is largely typified on AO by "meaningless" social interaction in the bars, parties, and by people getting into relationships. Before I am flamed for saying it, please everyone note I didn't say any of this was bad, for example, we (SPARTA) do a whole bunch of it ourselves.

Type 2 is "Conflict. This doesn't necessarily mean fighting, nor PvP, nor even "events, but it does mean there are two sides to a certain issue. We disagree on the issue, then we work to achieve our own objective and that means we come into "conflict", be it arguing, fighting, PvP, or "events" in the way most people expect them. It was this kind of RP / story / events that OTSEC was created IC'ly (as a cover) to OOC'ly achieve. That and pretty much nothing more.


I'll take a moment to impart some historical information... "We" tried to organise RP & story & events on a bigger company-wide multi-org scale before. It was called the Omni-Tek Security Council (OTSEC). Older players will probably remember it.

Ancestors of Atlantean, Cabaal Group, Division 9 RSGE, Draco Legionis, Omni Sentient Eye, Omni-Pol, Red Tape, SPARTA, Special Interception Troops, and at one point SkyTek & Omni Shadow Ops (i.e. no small number of 'highly proactive' orgs) were all involved and going in the right direction with the right desire to do things well before the storyline froze up in mid-Feb. We were highly organised, had meetings, discussed things, brought up ideas for 'play' etc. It was looking back, exactly the kind of forum we meant it to be.

Unfortunately with the sole exception of Azazzel's easy-to-pick-a-fight-with TBA, no-one else was even the slightest bit interested in organising some significant RP, events, conflict, confrontation, sub-stories etc on a counter-side side to us. We were a security council with great heaving sacks of enthusiasm and 'can do' attitude, with sadly no one to be secure, work, and act against.
