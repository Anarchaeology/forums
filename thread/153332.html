Well Glara... that's sort of the idea behind the flagging - to make attacking a base harder.

As it is the attackers can all meet and gather right on the edge of the base and buff up, form teams, pick out doctors and NTs to target, and then rush in a coordinated manner... just because of an invisible line there seperating the 25% from the 75%.

Because of that, the attackers control the flow and duration of the battle. And that is frustrating and stressful and non-fun for the defenders. Flagging the attackers gives the defenders the chance to take initiative and challenge the attackers actively, instead of being passive base-campers waiting on something that might or might not happen.

Edit - ql 200+ bases would have to be dramatically nerfed in power or else they'd never fall if this were implemented. But the concept is sound - give the defenders more options in defense as opposed to sitting around waiting on the sitzkrieg.

Edit 2 - Flagging attackers would also solve the "level 20 with HNQ" problem since the HNQ couldn't be refreshed once the character was flagged. Also, flags would solve the problem of doctors sitting in 75% healing people in 25% with range extenders.

Which is something I do all the time by the way... just like I have often engaged in sitzkriegs... because everyone else does. The gas camping is a result of people exploiting flawed pvp engagement rules (the gas lines, the grace period, etc) for an advantage. And as my signature says - in this situation the exploits become the rule.

Simple suggestion ::

There must be some way to keep the flow of battle going. These delays occur when one side starts the attack and then get repelled. Its that simple. You want the fight to carry on on a steady pace without waiting for backups?? Then heres the solution.

Funcom must instigate someway for attackers and defenders to save and res on a temp basis near the battle field. Get an engie to build a portable reclaim machine that can be deployed every 6 hours so people can save at one central point and carry on the battle without having to grid, fly or fixer grid. Its this constant travelling that slows the battle down while everybody is waiting for re-enforcements to turn up.

Problems ::

1) Lag. Since everybody is ressing and coming back into the one zone, lag will shoot up exponentially, so much so, that chances are that zones will crash. One way to minimise this is to setup the temp reclaim machines in an adjoining zone.

2) If people find out that PVP is now very accessible, you'll get more people there outright, so in theory, since the hassle of dieing and coming back is eliminated, you get more people, hence more lag. What this also means is that in the end, most battles will be time specific, so attackers will try to attack bases in timeframes that have the minimum number of defenders on. You'll get zerg and steamroll tactics.

Now Consider ::

Consider if this was now applied to Org v Org battles. Attackers and defenders could continue a very high speed pitched battle where death means nothing, where attackers and defenders can regroup at one point and launch attacks and try different tactics to dislodge the opposing force.

Also consider that the temp reclaim could be destroyed by the opposing force. A system could also be implemented whereby ever time the reclaim machine is destroyed, the number of people that are able to save to that one machine is reduced. Could be used to setup a capture the flag scenario of some sort at tower battles.
