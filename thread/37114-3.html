In regards to the tradeskill XP resonse:

I will agree with whoever was consulted that the item created is the primary reward for tradeskills. I will further agree that XP being increased really isn't feasible in this game because of the extreme possibility for exploit, and the basic unfairness of having someone level 20 or 30 times by sitting in the store building things with a few million credits.

My problem with your answer is this: If the item is the reward, why is it such a punishment to do? By this I mean mainly weapons tradeskills. I personally am incredibly pleased with the recent changes to tradeskills, namely the reduction in price of nano crystal tools, and the increase in charges for the treatment/stim packs. You guys are on the right track, don't stop now. It should never, I will repeat NEVER EVER, cost more to _create_ a weapon out of tradeskill parts, than it does to outright buy it from a terminal. Unfortunately, it does. There needs to be a serious reduction in the cost of weapon creation parts.

Of course, some may ask, "Well then who will buy weapons from terminals?". Answer? Anyone who doesn't have the time or energy to go find someone to build it for them. I suggest that parts be reduced to the point that it costs have as much money to BUILD a weapon as it does to BUY a weapon from the shops. This would allow the players who chose to simply do tradeskills, or to put a serious effort towards tradeskills, to sell these created items for say, a 25% markup from cost, still leaving the weapon at only 75% of the store bought cost. This would stimulate a player based economy, and possibly spread some of the ludicrous amounts of credits around.

Also, please please please please PLEASE consider adding more weapon recipes to the game. Particularly _popular_ weapons. Nobody uses the Blackened Miniblaster. But plenty of people use other weapons that have no recipe. Given the sad state of the Rubi Ka economy, this might just give people a reason to up tradeskills, and to spread around a few of those Ebay credits.

Catch ya planetside.
