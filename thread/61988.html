"The Lifters" are actively recruiting qualified members
I stand before you all today, to let any guildless Omni fixer know we're here if you are looking for a family.

We are opening recruiting for the time being. We are a fixer only organization and have no plans to deviate from that path. So if you aren't a fixer, you can stop reading now. We are looking for true fixers, no twinks that are in it for the "flavor of the month" frame of mind.

Just as with any organization, you get out of it what you put in to it. You show your detication to the organization, and you'll be well recieved.

A lil about us:

- As mentioned, we are a fixer only guild.
- We roleplay and have as much fun as possible.
- The stronger we grow, the more we can achieve.
- We do help each other out emensely and work together as teams for specific goals.
- We live by the fixer code, but aren't affraid to take one out when the need arrises. After all, even though we're Omni, doesn't mean a Clan Fixer is safe from us.


A few rules:

- You can join at any lvl, however you will be an applicant until three requirements are met.
1) You must blitz a 75% mission.
2) You must have in your possesion the hackers club card
3) You must be registered on our website.

- You must remain an active member. Long periods of inactivity will get you removed from the organization.

- No begging, whining, sniveling will be permitted on the org channel.

- If you are offended too easily, this may not be the correct organization for you.

- Do not expect someone to drop everything they are doing in order to help you with something. If you need help, feel free to ask. If there is a member that isn't occupied, I'm sure they'll help. But if not, oh well.

- If a member blitzes an item for you (which we have been known to do when we can if you ask nicely) and you turn around and sell that item, you could be removed from the organization.



This may all sound a lil strict, but too many "flavor of the month" players have taken advantage of some of our members and it wasn't appreciated.

There are reasons for the rigidness of the rules, and when you become a full fledged member you will understand why. Alot of members, myself included have put alot of work to helping our organization as a whole and thus wish to protect our investments.

If you are interested, you may contact myself, or Heletic.
