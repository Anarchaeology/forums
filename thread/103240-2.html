From the desk of Major McCovery
Leader of Desert Winds

Rubi-Ka was indeed a barren wasteland up until relativly recently when Omni-Tek began the terraforming process. However, it is also a fact that the terraforming process has not elevated the 'quality' ((for want of a better word)) of the eco system to the accepted 'colony standard' as seen on other terraformed worlds.

Indeed a recent change to the weather control system had the unforeseen consequences of exterminating all aquatic life on this planet, created many strange 'fogs' and altered weather patterns in the northern terraformed zone as seen by increased number and virulence of storms in that region.

Should the weather control system totally fail Rubi-Ka will begin to return to the pre-terraformed state, for most of us we can simply emigrate. But what of species that can not leave this planet? For example the nanomage? I do not support mass genocide of a group of peoples.

Rubi-Ka's ecosystem is young, barely 800 years old. It is developing, it is evolving. In some cases evolving far faster than normally expected. Into this young devleoping system we have introduced mining and all the wasteproducts and side effects that are associated with it. The ICC, in the infinite wisdom of their corporate members, have even increased this level of mining. Our recent report on Stret West Bank ((see Operation Adustum thread)) highlighted radical changes to the eco system in that area.

Mining is polluting and destroying our eco system.

We are poisoning ourselves.

Stop the mining before it is too late.
