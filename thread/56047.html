stromm
Supa Leet
Levels 1-100:
Soldier: Div9, x3(there is a very good level 74 RK1 pvp agent who uses the x3. it does not conflict with implants, as assault rifle does.)
Fixer: Sol chironis/kerans, gamma ejectors for a short bit, dualing them is a viable option as well.
Agent: ithaca, X-3 Rifle, Gripo-COM AKR (only goes up to ql 50 though)
Enforcer: Without a doubt, a beam.
MA: Regular fists.
Nano casting profs: Ithaca i guess...seen some use MCS too though..
Adventurer: Fire executioners, Freedom arms, BBI pistols.

Levels 101-200
Soldier: River6, Nova (if those two will do any good with 8% scopes...) IEC flashpoint rifle, and in the future whenever it gets added, the Norphex II.
Fixer: MCS, CHS, Manex (once the init is fixed)
Agent: Ithaca, X-3 rifle
Enforcer: 1hb sticks, beam, IEC flashpoint
MA: River6, claw, fists, crossbow
Nano casting profs: Ithaca, river6(would need buffs), CHS, possibly the manex, IEC flashpoint
Adventurer: Fire executioners.

Once again as someone stated above the llts nerf will change a lot. So i may be wrong on some of these.
