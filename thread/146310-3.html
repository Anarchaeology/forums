Ah I see something in need of adding :P

Say you are teaming with a doctor, and find yourself with tanking status. To make the doctor focus more on their job, take whatever good armor you have off and put on the noob equivelent.

For example, if you had ql 200 CAS on, substitute it with ql 30 CAS.

Of course to alert the doctor, be sure to "discover" that you have ql 30 armor on half way through the mission, and then make an excuse as to why you cant put your ql 200 armor on (eg: left it in the bank). Continue tanking.

Putting on low armor and tanking through a mission shows the doctor you care about his/her work, and want to make the most use of them in your team.

If you die while doing this, it is of course the doctor's fault.

Powerlvling for Docs

To lvl you chosen doc as fast as you can loot garbage and all the other new things to get tons of salamanders, rats and fleas. It is most save for you because the doc will start to teamheal once you get attacked and take all the aggro you are able to give to him.

It is important to do this while the team fights at least on regular mob (but docs will perform better with some adds present). He will love the xtra xp.

Docs tend to underextimate themselves. If you ask what lvl of mission your team want to do always ignore the doc - he always can do higher missions! This is especially true for nano mage docs that have tons oh hitpoints.

If you want do help him to lvl up extraordinarily fast do 100% missions in areas that the greedy doc can easily reach with his yalm (oh he has no yalm - what is he doing with his money?). By foot it can be a little rough but docs are good they will make it This is best done at lvl where the doc cant team grid because the challenge of getting back to the save booths appeals to all docs.
