Excuse me, but could someone help me figure this out...
I play a Bureaucrat currently 51st level, I use my DD nanos instead of ranged weaponry. For the past 10 or more levels I've notice that I fumble a HUGE amount. I've tried petitioning about this a few times, but hell, who has time to wait on that. From the combats I've logged it seems to me that out of 10 casts I'll fumble anywhere from 3 to 5 times, and out of the ones that get cast I'll be countered 3 or 4 times. I know that if I only take the minimums of that I'll still get 4 good solid casts out of 10 that are neither countered or fumbled, but on average I usually only get 2 or 3 DD nanos to stick on a mob. I'm not sure if this is an overall 'crat problem, (hard to tell as there aren't really that many of us compared to Enforcers, Engineers, and MPs) something I am doing wrong with my attributes, or the fact that I'm usually fighting dark yellow to deep orange mobs.

I first started really noticing the countering and fumbling when I began using Drill Missile, and Bend Will. I've even had a few group members comment on the fact that I do an awful lot of casting to be having no damage effects on mobs. It may very well be that in 51 levels I've simply overlooked something that is causing this, or it may be that I'm not the only one out there with this problem. Either way I'd really appreciate any feedback you guys have on the issue.
