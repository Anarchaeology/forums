When the first static dungeon was released, it would spawn a new dungeon when certain numbers of players entered. Unfortunately, this was causing groups to be split when the threshold accidentally fell in the middle of a 6 person group who entered. This would cause people to have to go in and out and in and out until thier whole group was allowed in at once.

Panic erupted on the forums, and many many guilds threatened to quit in unison if something wasn't done to fix the problem. Funcom said "Ok, we will fix that later. Until then, the number that can enter before a new dungeon spawns will be raised."

So presumably it is still on the docket to be fixed at some point.
