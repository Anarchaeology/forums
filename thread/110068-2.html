Thank you all so much..
I can't begin to tell you how much it means to me personally to see how many people have at least visited this thread - not to mention the great number of friends who have taken the time to share their kind words.

I'm truly touched that those of you who don't know me so well - also find it in you to lend me these kind words.

I would really like to post a personal reply to each and every one of you, but I feel this is not the place. If you send me an email, on the other hand..

I miss all of you very much. The game itself had little left to offer, but what has made it worth its while all this time is each and every one of you - each in your own big or small way.

So for all of those who hope or wonder, I have to tell you that neither Electronix4 or me will ever return to RubiKa. I'm sorry. His final request would certainly be that you all please help support The Healing Hands - and Billybob2 who has taken it upon herself to try and raise it back to its former glory.


I will certainly be watching this thread for as long as it gets to live. Each and every little post or visit puts a little smile on my face as it helps me to see that you offer E4 and myself that last bit of dignity even after his sudden and permanent death.


With love and respect,
