Reet Retreat - Under New Management
In looking through the news I have come to discover that no one posted the most recent change of management at Reets Retreat. Perhaps it is the busy schedules on Rubi-ka that keeps reporters from getting to all the news, so I thought I would take a load off their shoulders and give the announcement myself.

***** Newsfeed 7/5 ********************************
As of the last week in June, Reets Retreat took on a new assistant manager. Many know or have Gabriel "Cayll" Baptist, who was for a time the Assistant Manager of Reets Retreat, however after holding a meeting with the Manager, Eddie "Ignis" Wright, Cayll stepped down as Assistant Manager. It was within the same evening that Eddie contact Lynette "Wyldthing" Strimple, who was more then pleased to take the position as Assistant Manager. Neutrals throughout Rubi-ka have expressed a concerned as to two clanners leading their #1 night club, but when asked what she thought, Wyldthing had this to say. "Reets is open to anyone and everyone, we just hope to make all our customers comfortable."
**************************************************

Okay, so maybe it's a bit hokie, but what can I say? Feel free to contact me at any time concerning parties at Reets Retreat.
