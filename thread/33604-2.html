I applaud many of the comments and tactics here, but I do want to point out one misconception. Funcom is not setting a new precedent with level restrictions on nanos in 14.4. Two agent nanos in 14.2 also had level restrictions. One was a ql 146 nano with a level 100 req; the other was a ql 169 nano with a level 180 req. There was very little uproar about that fact with most agents focusing on the MP pet restrictions and the fact that the nanos were going to be team mission drops instead.

Personally, I have no problem with level reqs on items and nanos as long as they are reasonable. The level req should NEVER be higher than the ql of the nano, though. And the ql of the nano should be relative to the other nanos for that profession based upon its nano skill reqs.
