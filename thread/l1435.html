Report from E3
Hello everyone,

I am now sitting at the last day of E3 and I feel good. The show has so far been a huge sucess for us and I would like on behalf of everyone at booth #2332 (thats our booth ) thank for all the support we have gotten from fans, press and people just popping by our booth.

It is fun to meet so many of you guys and we truly appreciate your support. We also hope we have lived up to your expectations with what we have showed at this year E3.

We will very soon, after E3, start the beta 4 and we hope alot of you guys are ready to help us put the finishing touch on Anarchy and make it the best game ever.

Thanks.

Marius Enge
