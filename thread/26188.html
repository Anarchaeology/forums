Do traders really have it that rough?
Now, having never played a trader past 10th level and having avoided PVP lack a plague I can't really speak intelligently about PVP unbalance. But, I've sena lot of traders complaining that with the "OE" rules their buffs will no be in any demand anymore. I just don't think that's true.

See, there's 20% of slack built into over equiping. That means, if I want to use a ql200 weapon that requires a 1000 skill I only need 870 weapon skill to use it with a +132 wrangle. So long as people use the wrangle for only 10-15% of the weapon reqs its still worth having. The only difference is that the people getting the wrngle won't be as tough. If someone was willing to spend money in order to be more deadly with an over equiped weapon what makes anyone think that people will stop doing that. I actually think that people will be more willing to OE. The simple reason is that with the new -25% rule there's a top limit to over-equiping. Currently a lot of people don't really bother with it, because they assume that no matter how twinked out they there's still someone that's twinkier. But now there's a ceiling to being over-equiped. Now that there's a ceiling there's more incentive to over-equip up to that limit. Once you hit the limit you're the best at your level, which is way more desirable then being really twinked but unsure of whether or not there's someone more tqinked out there. Now, once you get to "x" ql weapon at "y" level you know you're maxxed out and nothing can mess with you.

I just think that this doesn't cripple traders as much as they think it does.

Re: That's not really true
quote:
Originally posted by Thugdoom
Plenty of people care about PVP. I don't, and the focus of this thread had nothing to do with PVP, but that doesn't mean that nobody cares.

My point was that the traders that were complaining that this patch makes wrangles useless were way off base.
Actually it does reduce wrangles almost to the desirability level of them in actual team situations: almost nil.

Pre-14.2, a one time wrangle would assist you in equipping a weapon until you changed weapons. If your intent was to equip a QL 200 weapon, a wrangle could do so permanently without any ill effects after the wrangle wore off. This is what people were willing to pay credits for: the ability to equip a weapon by meeting the skill requirements 1 time.

Pre-14.2, wrangles were wanted in team situations usually to buff a teammate long enough to use a buff of their own, for example buffing a MA so they could use UVC to buff the team. Once they player used their buff, they usually cancelled the wrangle to make room for other longer lasting, less generalized buffs.

Post 14.2, a one time wrangle will only assist you in equipping a weapon within the 20% OE zone so that when the wrangle wears off, you are not missing more than 20% of the required skills. If you wish to equip a weapon while lacking more than 20% of the required skill without penalties, you will now have to bring the trader with you to constantly refresh the wrangle every 3 minutes. This is something people have shown little interest in having for free when the trader is on their team, let alone paying anyone for. This will be due to the problems with keeping them refreshed every 3 minutes (impossible to do without refreshing Divest/Plunder first, which takes time) and the amount of NCU space they take up while running constantly (most people cancelled the wrangle immediately after equipping the weapon or using the buff they wanted).

So post 14.2, wrangles become a little desired ability of the trader. Buffing a person so they can in turn use a buff of their own, usually lasting much longer.
