Funcom - people may be complaining about the server being down but...
Thats just because they are so addicted to your game they can't stop playing it for 6- 7 hours.:P I'm bummed about it as well but I know you guys are doing all you can to get the server up and running, better it be working than laggy when ya get it back up. I'm amazed at what the game has accomplished in the short time it's been out. The beginning was a disaster but most MMOGs are when they first come out, the game has improved 300% since then. UO still has bugs and exploits after what 5 years?

AO has it all, depth.. a dynamic ever changing world and an actual story. It feels more like a world than a game and I'm not a roleplayer.. I'm an ex-UO PK Roleplay-Hater but AO tempts me to roleplay. It's very easy to immerse yourself in the game and get lost in the depth of it all, that hasn't happened to me in any other MMORPG. I could careless if everyone is more uber than me, I play the game to have fun it's amazing. To all those that think I'm some newbie that is dazzeled by the games 'newness' thats not true. I've been around since beta, infact I was a doctor in Beta just as I am now. I may only be level 32 now but I was one of the higher levels in beta. I recently came back to AO after a very long break and the changes that have taken place are stunning.

I'm far from a "fanboi/girl" whatever ya wanna call it.. I've never been like that.. I state my opinion always.. no matter what. If you guys would just stand back and look at the scope of the game and all the things Funcom are doing, the long strides toward what "future" gaming will be like. They are laying the groundwork for some amazing games. AO in itself is an amazing work of art, Funcom is going through uncharted waters.. they aren't following any set of mmog rules or guidelines they are writing their own. I just can't believe they've come so far so fast. Yes there is much work to be done but what they've accomplished is something that no other game has or probably will for a while. So, I'll gladly give them my 12 dollars a month and my patience and see how it all turns out.
