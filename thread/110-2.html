Fixer top 10
Weve already established that post 70+ Fixers have a hard time fighting thus gaining experience and further levels. Well to be honest I never really saw the Fixer as the best fighter etc but thats realistically the only way to get experience at the moment.
So maybe as an alternative, if Funcom wont give us better combat abilities then it stands to reason there should be another way for us to get levels.
Instead of changing the skills we have why not utilise what weve already got.
Heres my top ten:


1.A mission system that utilises the Grid with missions such as:
 Terrorists have planted a virus in the Grids matrix, Fixer hack into the grid and remove it (whatever) Said Fixer then enters the Grid the usual way and enters a
mission zone. This dungeon should look more futuristic much like the grid and not like usual dungeon type missions. Upon entering the mission a timer is set and you have X mins to find and remove the virus using trap disarm. This will be in keeping with the hacker type role.

2. Another mission type could include many more locked chests and doors, more traps and MOBs with greatly reduced perception thus making it at least possible to sneak around the mission and getting the equivalent experience from disarming a trap or unlocking a door as you would get from killing a mob. Chests/doors could also con different colours and give experience much like monsters. This will be in keeping with the thief type role.

3. Make future nanos partly comp lit based thus negating the need to change current skills.

4. Nanos which allow the Fixer to mark a certain area and teleport their team there thus giving Fixers a role in PvP.

5. Nanos that allow the Fixer to warp into a special Fixer only Grid based black-market/hangout. This will allow Fixers to make supply runs mid hunt when combined with the teleport Nano giving Fixers a role in groups.

6. Maybe someplace in the Fixer hangout where the Fixer can carry a sample of the groups DNA to scan mid hunt also giving the Fixer more of a role in groups.

7. Comp lit buffs.

8. Smg and burst buffs.

9. Conceal buffs.

10. Bigger variety of weapons.

Regards

Fade.
