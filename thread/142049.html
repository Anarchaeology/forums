Feeding the Puddle- Curiosity Killed the 'Crat
It had been a regular day at the office, a rareity these days for Andy French, a man seemingly cursed with the sort of "wrong place, wrong time" luck. However, amazingly, he had always pulled through one way or another, how ever, Mr. French's luck was about to change. ((FYI, Andy Is Prezidential))

Andy leaned back into his office chair, yawning deeply as the sun began to set. In the back of his mind rested the recent perma-death murder of Fyarr, a high ranking general he had met many times before. The concept of death angered Andy, he failed seeing how someone could be capable of ending anothers life, he didnt feel they had the right, it just couldnt happen, and he refused to believe it.

The evening birds began to chirp and Andy started dozing off, it was gonna be another late night at the Red-Tape offices, he could afford a little break. Just as his mind began to wander, the big screen in the lounge suddenly turnt on, indicating breaking news (scaring the crap out of Andy, and sending Deuscloud and his coffee flying to the ground)

Andy lazily rolled his office chair into the lounge, where most of the other employees had relocated themselves.

There was news teams, lots of them, infront of Rompa's. Something huge had happened..something horrible.

Andy's su****ions were quickly confirmed, as he felt the breath leave his lungs as the names were announced

"Stindy and Shadowgrider, their nicknames being all we can release at this time, have been found dead in this popular nightclub's restrooms. Theyre insurance has failed to reactivate itself, and this work is believed to be the doing of dreaded perma-death weapons. The strange thing is, scientists report that their grid signatures and life signs are weak, but alive, proving that their existance MAY be true..however, theyre no longer of this world, as crazy as it sounds."

Andy buried his face in his hands, shaking his head. At the loss of two people he knew very well, one being the wife of a long time friend, the local deth-addict known as Aoick, who had been an invaluable source of info in the past few months. Prez and Aoick were once great friends before nano-deths trashed his brain.

The reporters then stepped inside, where the cameras struggled to keep focus on a shirtless man, unidentifable by his rage, who was causing havoc among the employees and customers.

An Omni-Pol officer attempted to calm him down as the cameras got a good focus on him....

"Hey French, isnt that that drug head buddy of yer's?" taunted a voice from the back of the room

Andy smacked his forehead and headed for the garage.

"Ill be back later folks, I gotta bail him out of this one, and losing Stindy and all...he's gonna need me"

Mr. French pulled his bright-orange Yalmaha out of the bleak gray car park, speeding far above the limit and nearly colliding into several other drivers. All the talk of perma death had him frustrated, he wasnt thinking straight tonight, and he had this horrible feeling in the pit of his stomach that just wouldnt go away...

Andy landed his Yalmaha in the brightly-painted Rompa's Parking lot, which was cloaked with an eerie calm. He quickly locked the doors and bolted for the entrance. Inside he found not the usual sounds of celebration and lust, but the sound of police-comms and cleaning droids. A massive bolt-lock covered the door to the womans restroom. He made his way to the bar, where Aoick was in the process of being cuffed.

The overweight police cheif struggled to control Aoick, and completely lost control of him as Andy tapped him on the shoulder. Aoick bolted for the door, but being tazered into submission far from the entrance.

He tried to explain the situation, but to no avail. Relunctantly, Andy payed Aoick's bail, still some how seeing his old friend in those crazy eye's of his.

Andy advised Aoick to go home and think about it, but in a drunken stupor he refused, spewing the blame at Rizzior, the newly elected bar owner and president of Inferno Entertainment.

As Mr. French was about to leave, practically dragging Aoick behind him, Rizzior notioned for him to come have a seat.

Andy took one last look at Aoick, he figured he had done all he could.

His thick, uncut hair dangled wildly, and his scarred chest was clearly visible through his torn shirt. With fire in his eyes, he glared at an officer enjoying a drink and began to approach his the officers table, with clear intent to really, really piss him off. Andy was about to stop him when he realized a nice night next to Big Bubba in the Omni-Pol Jail might smack, or smash, a little sense into him. ((Aoick))

A well dressed waiter led Andy to a comfortable booth in the back of the bar, where Rizzior was waiting. The waiter notioned Andy toward the menu, where he chose his drink of choice, Hit The Floor Jack.

They discussed the recent death's of Stindy and Shadowgrider, and touched base with the murder of Fyarr. All their names ultimately led up the man everyone wanted a piece of


Dave "Diamondcut" Duster


With every sip, Andy slipped further and further into bliss. His keen edge was slipping, and his luck was running like sand through his fingers. The discussion became heated to the point where a some-what drunker Andy heaved an empty bottle towards the wall, the shattering glass arrousing everyones attention. Mr. French stumbled up and lunged towards Rizzior, grabbing him by the collar

"Why....why did people have to die,.. no one has that right.....no one man..."

Just as Rizzio was reaching for something to bat Andy away with, Zenishaku spoke up, revealing a very good proposition to Mr. French.

Zenishaku was lucky enough, apparently, to know the location of Diamondcut... information that had Andy paying practically everything but the shirt on his back for. When they had made the deal, they shook on it, with Mr. French promising not to bring up Zenishaku's name.

Midnight passed and morning was upon them, Rizzio grew tired and decided he best leave.

He said his goodbyes and handed Andy a large, bolt shaped key.

"Go and take a look for yourself, they were your friends afterall"

Rizzio headed out for the evening, leaving Andy in the eerie silence. Andy said his goodbyes for the few patrons still left and headed for the restroom.

He took a deep breath and gulped as he put the key in the lock, it took all the guts in the world for him to turn the key and look inside.

It didnt take long for Andy to discover the gooey remains of his once very-solid friends, and once he did he dropped to his knees and began praying. He couldnt even bare to kneel anymore... he threw his hands out into the goo, completely oblivious to his surroundings. He wafted his hands through the thick substance, feeling for any piece of jewlery or clothing left behind to remember them by..... and before he knew it, he had come across something...

There was one at first, but then he discovered many. A handful of cloth shards that once assembled a badge of somesort, he began to furiously piece the shards together, forming and E at first...wait, theres no way.....

He was completely oblivious to the door opening as a masked man stepped in. One pull of the trigger and Andy was on the ground gasping for air, staining the goo with a deep red tint.

Wrong place, wrong time Andy thought, his luck would get him through it.

But tonight Andy wasnt so lucky, and as he reached for the bright lights of reclaim, his existance as he knew it was cut short.

A soul binder bullet in the back, Andy had fed the goo tonight, the puddle would be a little bigger in the morning.
