quote:
Originally posted by Nietya
Seriously, how hard is it to make a node-based AI system, folks? Point a to point b, the whole node searching thing (that was certainly an assignment in CS that I DIDN'T like)...
Heh, it's HARD. I've worked on a couple of game projects. The problem is processor time tradeoff. You can search every node if you have the processor time (like a CS project, making a game where one object needs to find it's way through a maze, you get 100% of the processor for that one object.) It's much harder when you are trying to limit the number of nodes that are searched while still making good predictions that will be useful in the present, immediate future, and distant future. That's why so many games (and not just AO, but most of them) have very poor pathfinding, or very slow pathfinding. I'm not a programmer, but I made the mistake of conversationally saying "How hard can it be? My screensaver can solve a complex maze in just a few seconds!" to a weary, frustrated programmer on an RTS project I was working on a few years ago. Needless to say he filled me in on the difficulties he faced. lol!
