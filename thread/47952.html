Yuttos (SoCaEI) Rally for clan unity!
Our Rally for Clan Unity is scheduled for this coming Satuday, August 31, 29476, at 20:00GMT outside the doors of the Council of Truth building in Tir city. (RK1)

Topics addressed will be the CoA/CoT dividing the clans, current ICC/Omni-Tek movements, and Yuttos environmental concerns. This started out as just the concerns of our small group, but quickly has gathered momentum as several clan leaders have shown great interest in our cause.

Now this is bigger than just us and we invite all able clanners to make this important assemblage.
