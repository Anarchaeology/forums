Apologizes from the SoS
The SoS, and me Everwatch as leader, wish to adress an apology to all those concerned by the recent trouble around our org.

After a period of confusion, which lasted several weeks, our internal investigations led to a series of conclusions that reveal severe failures of our security system. It appears now very clearly that the SoS has been undermined by a criminal group.
The following persons were involved:

Stefano. The person who invited himself a series of spies inside our ranks. Ex general of our org for long, his loyalty was all faked. His poor results as a general, his unefficiency to fight the evil that was plaguing us convinced us he was implicated. We now prooved that point. Additionaly implicated in a series of murders against Sensuality.
=> He was removed from our org today.

Serter. The brain of this plot. So called 'friend' of the SoS at first, thanx to his spies inside our ranks. He was the one to start the mess involving the SoS and Ylemmae, always hiding his reasons, now revealled. He also added more hate and confusion to any situation we have faced since, against Devils Advocate in particular, with side conversations and plotting. He was the one to decide to implicate his brothers Stefano and Sephiroth05, the Kenleys family, into a plot which only purpose, as far as we can tell, was to undermine our pacific org, and to make as many members as possible leave our ranks.
=> The SoS ended all relations with that person.

Sephiroth05. Invited and welcomed to the SoS. He received friendliness and help, from goodwilling people. His aggressive attitude hurt different persons inside and outside our org, though we do not consider this person as really plotting against us. Accomplice nonetheless, he helped in undermining our org by supporting his brothers.
=> He was removed from our org today while he was trying to defend Stefano's position. Due to that, he is now claiming everywhere he will assassinate all SoS members.

Senfo. Invited and welcomed to the SoS as Sephiroth05. He was the spy working exclusively for Serter. He revealled himself when information transpired from the SoS at incredible speed. He is a direct accomplice, and his spying over an org as the SoS is illegal, passible of Marshall law.
=> He was removed from the org today.

Malachii. Long member of the org, Malachii prooved his commitment by taking part of the series of murders against Sensuality. He is also an accomplice to the other actions of this group and conspired to spoil the general atmospher of the org.
=> He left the org a few days ago. The SoS ended all relations with that person.

Further investigations may lead to increase this list.

We wish to explain that the SoS was victim of this plot and lost many members and friends due to it. Our loss of reputation is also an important damage. The confusion and arguments that occured also damaged much of the fun we usually get been members of the SoS, and our usual goodwill and friendship was spoiled due to this during those weeks.

The persons involved in this plot showed no regret, no apology for the damage done. Most of them already formed a new organization called Kenley Criminal Syndicate, which this time shows more of their personality.

Therefore, we adress the following messages:

Sensuality: Assassinated several times by this group, only to bring discredit on the SoS. Even though our org took measures against those persons, we send our sincere apologizes.

Wavebreaker (unarmed) and Ylemae (10yo child): Threatened by Sephiroth05 who shot with his gun inside the Rompa bar to scare them. I had the foolishness to defend Sephiroth05's point of view myself. I am personally sorry about that.

Ylemmae: Serter added to an unfortunate incident we had with this person, making a massive argument out of it.

The org Devil's Advocate, Doncarnage in particular, and Ylemae: The plotters added to the importance of an argument we are having at the moment. The situation would settle way faster without their constant trouble. I adress my personal apologizes to Devil's advocate, Doncarnage and Ylemae for that amount of additional trouble, and call them for a calm and contructive conversation to settle our matters.

We do not deny our lack of cautiousness regarding this group invading us and abusing our friendliness. This is why I decided today to submit my leadership to a vote from my members, who are therefore free to demote me from my current position of leader. Decision will be final in 10 days.
To explain a bit our lack of cautiousness, I have to say that we were astonished at the blattant will to harm our org, just for the fun of it.

... Gabriel recoiled himself in thought, placing himself away from the world in a deep meditative glance over what he scanned, the words that trailed upward across his viewscreen. Decryption and scatter-signal software already manipulating any security traces on his access working their programmed magic, a work of art from none other than Dericads mind.

Software was not his forté. Other skills were held by the Daemon Unit member.

Subterfuge and the ability to blend, to adapt, knowledge of his fellow man, workings of the mind, of fear, of hatred through others; these were all fine weapons he knew how to recognise and use to evade possible fatal circumstances. Implement them as tools, weapons, against any objective.

Gabriel had further tests to proceed with. Communication had been received from those who governed a partial amount of drive the assassin had been implanted with upon creation. To attain successful results from all objectives given in any given moments offered to acheive optimal result.

The Kenley brothers, of course they had become involved in his work. Why not use such valuable methods of indirect approach? If the reasons behind the work was simply put down to "criminal activity" then more-so would Gabriel promote such motive.

Of course... this was not the case.

An offer to join the Kenley family had been made. Stefano was an excellent accomplice and his progression into the darker depths of his being kept Malachii proud. Yes, Gabriel was indeed proud of watching these things develop.

In darkness, images cannot be seen.

In the shadows, everyone fails to see the clear vision. That is, unless one grew to understand that shadows are created through light and opened themselves up to use this understanding to focus through potential obstacles.
In darkness, reaching far beyond that which casts shade through light, nothing... can be seen.
But you can feel.

Gabriel understood this to a perfected art.
