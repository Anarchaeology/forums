/assist and non attacking classes (NT's)
as a doc i have to /assist more then possably any other class in the game to make sure that i have the right team mate selected and since SL i have noticed a problem when teaming with NT's with cyber decks... /assist doesnt reselect the mob again and i am left with tabing or quicly selecting another team mate, /assisteing them to get the current fighting target (if there are calmed adds) then /assisting again to get that mobs current fighting target.

so, my suggestion is for /assist to select the last target of a hostile nano if the target isnt fighting so docs can easierly keep track of agro with less button mashing. This would also be usefull in making sure someone doesnt accidentally debuff/dot the calmed mobs since just hitting tab from the NT does not guarentee that you will select the current fighting target insted of a calmed add.


as it is now
/assist enf to select the current mob
/assist to find out who the mob is attacking... selects the NT
heal the NT
/assist the NT to reselect the mob.... sorry, target is not in a fight
now i have to reselect the enf by hitting F_, /assist him to get the mob, then /assist the mob to get the player with the agro

as i want it
/assist the enf to select the current mob
/assist to find out who the mob is attacking... selects the NT
heal the NT
/assist the NT to reselect the mob
/assist to find out who the mob is attacking...
heal whom ever is getting attacked
... lather, rince and repeat.


as you can see, the second method combined with making /assist work on yourself is a far more relayable method in the hectic combat of SL (and even in RK missions) where you might have several adds and trying to find out who has agro and which mob to debuff/dot.
