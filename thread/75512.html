General Nanos
I would like to see an Advanced line of the general nanos. They would still buff the same skills to the same amounts they do now, i.e. +20 to SMG, MA, treatment, etc, but they would last twice as long as the current ones to keep pace with the increasing length of profession-specific nanos. To balance out the extra length, make them use 2.5x the nano points and NCU (125np and 10ncu instead of 50np and 4ncu, for instance). I would gladly give up the extra np and NCU for hourlong general nanos. They could be sold in the Advanced and Superior general nano shops, and keep the shorter ones in Basic shops.
