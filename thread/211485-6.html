CoT Policy Proceedings: A Proposal
To The Council of Truth:

I do not believe that Clan organizations on the whole would disagree to send a representative to discussions of Policies that should be adopted by the The New CoT under these following terms:

  1. CoT Policy adoption proceedings are open forum discussions: In no way does presence of clan representatives at these proceedings are to be considered "in support the New CoT" but are in support of creating a dialogue amongst Clan organizations and the New CoT in order to objectively achieve a list of policies that are mutually agreed.
  2. Voting on Policies: 3/4's vote of the representatives in atendance is needed to pass a policy
  3. Adoption of Policy: Once a policy is passed by 3/4 majority it is considered adopted and becomes the domain of The New CoT in the future.
  4. Untouchable policies: Policies that are voted on and recieve less than 3/4 vote are to be considered the domain of the individual Clan organizations on Rubi Ka. They will set their own policy on such issues.
  5. Right to Leave Forum: Clan Organizations can at any time leave the forum if they feel they are at an impasse with policy discussions


Humbly yours,
