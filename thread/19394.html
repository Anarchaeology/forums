Key to uber characters
As I play more and more, I discover little things here and there that give you an edge. Now these tips are going to be more for the experienced player. Not an "all knowing" type, but someone who has started a second character, can fund the new one fairly easily, and knows where to put IP and where not to.

For some of you this will be very old news. If you are still on your first character and haven't reached lvl 50, don't worry about the rest of this thread because you need a whole lot of cash to employ these methods.

There are really only 3 things that you have to do to keep your character superior to the majority out there:

1.) Keep him twinked.

I made a thread here about how to twink a character. Some people hate having to stop every 25 levels or so and taking out a couple days to redo it all, but it makes such a huge difference. I actually like doing it since afterwards, everyone tends to notice a bit.

2.) Stay ahead of your nano's

This part starts out cheap, but gets very expensive fairly quickly. It is key though, to be able to upload your latest nanos as soon as you can use them. You go through levels so fast sometimes that by the time you get around to shopping for nanos again, you have already skipped some. Staying ahead of them keeps you strong. I have a backpack full of nanos I can't use yet, and check it every level for new goodies.

3.) Get your tokens before the title

Tokens help quite a bit. When you hit level 15 and you have your 60, your HP and nano almost double. Suddenly those minibulls get alot easier. The same goes for the rest of your titles.

With those 3 strategies employed, I have had a considerably easier time leveling and have leveled very fast.

Hope it helps...
