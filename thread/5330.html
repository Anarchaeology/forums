Hammer:
Hello friend,

Funcom have updated their graphics card FAQ to include the nVidia Detonator XP drivers (23.11).

You can find them at: http://www.nividia.com.

I have been playing on the 23.11 drivers for three days now. No crashes ... but I run on an AMD Athlon-C CPU <grin>.

Another suggestion is to turn Compressed Textures OFF ... if you have that AO graphics option turned ON. Turning Compressed Textures OFF seems to have helped many AO gamers since the 13.4 and 13.5 patches were installed.

And if your TNT2 has a hardware "z-buffer" in it (the nVidia GForce cards have one), please turn the AO "w-buffer" option OFF so the game will use the hardware z-buffer in your graphics card.

I suggest you make these changes one at a time to see which of them fixes your problem.

Hope this helps ...
