Clan Guild Doing Limited Recruiting
Dork Guild is now recruiting 10 new members. Requirements are that your character be at least lvl 125 and that you play regularly (daily preferred).

Dork Guild is currently a small group of 25 or so players, mostly lvl 150 and above. We currently have an admin for Tarabot, are able to assist one another with killing most unique mobs, and can build all QL towers for the upcoming Notum Wars.

Those are just a few things about us, but most of all we are all dorks - Just Like You!

If you are interested in learning more about our guild, please visit our website at www.dorkguild.com and leave us a message on our Guild Recruiting forum. Thanks for reading and take care!
