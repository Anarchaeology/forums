What happened to the Answered Questions list?
Has the 'Answered Questions' thread been yanked? Does this and the long-promised but never delivered Unanswered Questions list join the Daily Updates as another highly publicized and appreciated attempt to provide better customer service which you later found you didn't have the ability to support?

For those that weren't around when Savant and the Themis Group came in, one of the things that they were supposed to do was help with customer service. Shortly thereafter they announced that Funcom was going to be giving us something that would help us not only get answers, but hold them accountable. They were going to have an Unanswered questions list and, when a question was answered, it would be moved to the Answered Questions list! Needless to say, those of us that had been desperate for answers were thrilled.

The Answered Questions list came but the Unanswered questions list never did. We were never told why, in fact all questions about the Unanswered questions list were ignored. While the AQ list wasn't nearly as helpful without the UQ list, it was still something that people could point to for authoritative answers.

So where did it go and why? And, for the umpteenth time, where is the Unanswered Questions list that Savant promised?
