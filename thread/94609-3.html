I feel that the market forums are vitally important to the player economy. They provide a centralized place for selling, but more importantly, a place where buyers can be reasonably confident the items up for auction are legitimate, and where sellers can be reasonably confident that the bids on their items are serious.

Confidence is the real basis of economy, and a third-party site just wouldn't be able to inspire the kind of confidence that the official site does. If people don't believe they'll be paid, they won't sell. If people don't believe they'll actually get an item or service, they won't buy.

Among other things, because third-party sites can't block non-AO players from posting, and can't prevent the same person from registering infinite numbers of accounts, malicious interference with the market becomes easy.

On a third party site, people could register extra forum IDs to bid up their own auctions, to post fake auctions, and to screw up the auctions of others anonymously. People who don't play could bid up legitimate auctions to screw up people who do, post items they don't actually have for sale, and so forth.

And at least a few people would. It wouldn't take more than one or two high-profile incidents - and most incidents will be high profile, for big ticket items like grid armors or unique drops - to rock the sense of security in a third-party market forum, and drive both buyers and sellers away from it.
