Kenley's Perilous Times
"Serter" was in Scheol searching for some components for his new armor when he recieved a message from "Cashaura", member of the "Knights" Clan. She demanded he meet her at Rompa's, for his life was in danger...... again. After he arrived at Rompa's he took all of his equipment, not knowing of the danger that lingered inside. "Serter" approached "Cashaura" without even checking her background. He was too tired to do anything at the time.

"Serter...I know your future..please. lets talk alone."

"Fine...just make it quick. I have a meeting to attend to."

"Cashaura" and "Serter" engaged in what seemed to be a friendly conversation for a few moments.

"Your life is in grave danger Serter. I'm afraid your going to die if you do not become more knowledgeable of your current surroundings. Have you ever been infected with Notum Gas?

"Serter" replied hastily, "Whats it to you?"

"Cashaura" walked behind "Serter", wrapping her arms around him, while concealing a syringe filled with the Notum Gas she spoke of. She swiftly swung "Serter's" arms out of the way and jabbed the syringe into his leg, infecting him with the deadly Notum Gas.

"You will die a slow, painful, and permanent death, 'Serter'."

"Serter" yelled to the other Kenleys present at Rompas to attack and apprehend "Cashaura". They chased her outside and around Omni-1 a bit until being led back to Rompa's and finally getting ahold of her. After engaging a few threats between one another, "Cashaura" escaped and fled to the arena. "Stefano" kept chase and infected her with his custom made virus, Essence of Pure Jealousy. "Serter" waited in Rompa's for a report from "Stefano" after recieving word of her infection and him killing her in the arena, he relaxed and then informed fellow leader "Ammicus" (Omni-InternOp's {IC}) of his infection and coming death. He asked him to investigate anything he could find on the Notum Gas. "Ammicus" agreed to help, but did not promise any results, as he was not a doctor.

While heading to Broken Shores to help two of his students to take his mind of things, he recieved a message from a man who we will refer to now as "K". He informed "Serter" of who ordered the attacks on him. He claimed "M" and "V" to be behind the scheme of wanting "Serter" dead. He reported these findings to "Ammicus" and Nathan began looking into relations between the two people. "K", luckily, had a cure for the Notum Gas "Serter" was infected with. After testing the substance, "Serter" injected it into his system. Within a few minutes "Serter's" cough had vanished and he felt his strength coming back, slowly but surely.

Serter put a message out to the community.

"Be on the lookout for a member of the 'Knights' Clan, 'Cashaura'. She is armed and EXTREMELY dangerous. Also be on the lookout for 'Lehx' who was involved in the bombings of Omni-Entertainment Platform 5 months ago. Also be on the lookout for the infamous underground criminal, David 'Diamondcut' Duster. He has been sighted on Omni grounds and is considered very deadly. Any information about these three individuals, or the acts of 'Cashaura' will be rewarded promptly and greatly. Thank you Citizens of Omni-Tek."

"Serter" looked up at "Stefano" and said, "This has been one crazy week. 'Aoick' is dead. 'Eternalforce' has come back from the dead. And now we have a new enemy. Can this get any worse?"
