It started December 12th of the year 29475 everyday is a hot day on Rubi-Ka I suppose 2 suns are mostly to blame, I came here to Rubi-Ki with my husband Jeff Waldram just over a month ago. Jeff worked for a sub division of Omni-tek a Huge corporation that has its hands in just about everything now days on Rubi-Ka, He works within the mining and subterrian division of the company. On Dec. 27th OT(omni-tek) sent Jeff to an unmarked site about 35 miles north of Broken Shores to take some sample core readings which turned out to look promising, within a few days all manner of equipment was moved in and Jeff was made site boss. Everything looked typical and normal until on Feb 3rd workers started to come down sick all the signs showed of radiation poisoning but every test monitor showed no contamination within the mining shaft we lost 9 opiflex workers within just a few weeks. OT sent out doctors and test platforms that kept showing the site to be clear but Jeff wasnt convinced he shut down drilling due to worker hazzard and went into the shaft one evening himself, when he came back he was distraught saying he had to contact Omni-Tek and that the Restored Earth Federation (the newest government of earth at the moment) needed to be made aware of the dangerous material that was in the shaft. Feb 27th Omni-Tek stormed our house.....and...and killed Jeff, the official statement said he shot first but I know the truth I was there, they also took me away to a re-education center.
I never knew pain like they put me through, it was as though my whole body was alive with pain while some damn Omni-tek medical tech kept telling me to watch the screen. I thought I was going to die there but my will had caught the eye of a Major Danter who thought himself a ladies man and an expert in psychology. I learned fast that unless I ...pleased my new master I would suffer through another education session but whats more I also learned that pleasing him made him boast about his accomplishments and that meant information. It took just a month for me to learn that I was good at making him talk, the things I done gag me but I kept thinking that If i could survive then maybe I could find a way out. Major Danter was a ego driven man as such I played on that, he would tell me things and show me things on his terminal and I would feign excitement which would drive him to discuss even bigger things to as he put it 'light my fire'. On March 27th I hit paydirt when I told him I was tired and uninterested he kept on and on with his toys until he hit on something that froze my blood. Technical and filing briefs on what was now called site F my husbands drill site. The Technical profile showed a mineral being mined under the upmost care and its process though the final phase when mixed with a certain batch of chemicals produced a explosive device that dwarfs anything to ever exist...if.....if i read it right one ounce of that refined mineral could without a doubt destroy a city the size of omni-1. Danter expected a night to remember and i gave that to him complements of a vase to the head, I downloaded all the information onto a disc and with his keys and access cards found my way out of the complex.
