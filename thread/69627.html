Caols suggestion to an upgraded Banking system...
Seems it's the flavour of the year to mod this games GUI / Maps etc, so I sat down in photoshop with an idea on how the bank system could be better..

Probably fuel by my anger and dispair since 14.6 turned my bank on its head. I can't even begin to think when I'll have the energy to go thru my bank and sort it out again... (only to wait for the next bank-shuffle bug )

Why not have a look?
