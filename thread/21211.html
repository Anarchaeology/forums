Give Me New Items Or Give Me Death
)> Alright, I hate the crit buff change and it can do dirty things to me with it's mouth all day for all I care, but nothing I say or do is going to change it short of physical intervention - and I lack the ambition requisite for the sheer magnitude of physical labor involved with serial killing.
)> I know that whatever changes are made, I have to either cope or quit. My opinion, words and actions go on largely ignored or overlooked because, hey, I'm just a player of this game, what do I know?
)> So, I cope. But, maybe this idea will get picked up and carried into the annals of fame and history, as something Funcom should have done in the first place.
)> For the love of all that is Good, Holy, Decent, Acceptable or just plain Not Sucky, stop making all the cool new nanos, or stuff that is actually useful that everyone wants... nay... NEEDS to do anything akin to real levelling, so idiotically hard to get.
)> Nullity Sphere MK I - ANNOYING/EXPENSIVE TO GET
)> Nullity Sphere MK II - See above, exponent power of 30
)> Grid Armor MK I - 6 mil
)> Grid Armor MK II - 40 mil
)> Grid Armor MK III - Never even seen GA MK III for sale
)> GA MK IV - See above, but I've seen it in use. And it's bugged, imagine that...
)> Now, if the new nanos are dropped as infrequently, unreliably and easy to recall as those above - WHO WANTS THEM ANYWAY!?
)> They're a fortune to make. They're a hassle to find. But perhaps best of all, THEY'RE A REQUISITE AND IN-LINE PART OF THE CLASS.
)> Grid Armor r0xx0rs j00. Why do you think so many people want it? Perhaps because it, at least occasionally, works.
)> Nullity Sphere MK I has saved a friend of mine's butt so many times I don't think fingers, toes or hair follicles have kept track anymore.
)> Non-self crit buffs... non-self crit buffs... oh, you mean LMA and UVC? HA!
)> No one but my best friends will buff me to buff myself. The best part about being buffed for UVC, to me, was Four Fists of Kali. The one I have to pull off 9,000,000 tricks to cast, and unless I bother to get MORE ultra-rare and MORE ultra-camped items, I get the luscious, luscious task of contending with casting my best nano at 199. That's several, hundred, million XP away... AND IT JUST GOT HARDER.
)> Maybe they never wanted us to reach level 200, but maybe the 'big boys' upstairs should take a nice, long break from making these changes and realize that we're going to make it anyway. What they should REALLY do is stop making it so hard to get to 200, and start making some things that make getting to level 200 worthwhile (aside from the 'look at my massive... "skill"' measuring stick).
)> All of this, in line with a 'to be scheduled' mob HP adjustment. To be scheduled sometime after we're all stuck levelling once a week. I want my new items, and I'll work for them, but people who don't know a rich MA personally, to quote a guildmate, are 'scr00d'.
)> Oh, and PvP is so unbalanced now it's idiotic at best. I can't WAIT until 14.2.
)> Maybe the first trick is just to make critbuffs work in a less drastic manner... perhaps (gasp), lower the requirements and make them easier to get, while at the same time factoring them into the engine differently so not having a critbuff doesn't mean doing crap damage all the time, and me rolling my aggro bar back actually starts to DO something to me getting crit.
)> The problem isn't what's there already, and the solution is not to stack new and more u84r gear on top of the crap that's already flooding the planet.
)> The solution is to make what we have work RIGHT, not change it entirely. Believe it or not, you can swat a fly without a Buick. Sure you might miss, but you'll also not take down the house.
