New Nanos and removing useless
First of I'd like to point out that MA's are the class with the fewest nanos in game - I am not crying nerf, but still...

We have 92 nanos and after removing the two useless lines of Give Energy and Give Life, there is only 84. I know MA's are a figthing class, but still Soldiers have 100 nanos and Enforcers 111. [edit: figthing class ~ well.. they have the least nanos of any class]

Source Jayde's 14.6 Item Database http://v020u46eff.maximumasp.com/AODB/

I'd like to see the two useless lines (Give Energy/Life) removed from game. They are useless and are only annoying to pull in missions ect. [edit: if they was obsulete already in the beta, further the more to get them removed now!]

Please take two seconds to attend this poll
http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=72563

But hey... that leaves us with 84 useful nanos. That is 16 less than the next in line. I think with should put our heads together and make some ideas for funcom to use. And lets try to stay realitic - I don't think any übernano will be put in.

I have a few suggetions myself.

#1 - Make the First Stike nano into a line. Which MA wouldn't like a quicker atk even at lower levels? [http://v020u46eff.maximumasp.com/AOD...asp?AOID=28875]

#2 - Make a bow version of the Agents nano 'Snipers Bliss' - adding 50 bow and 15 bow spc atk. The QL and reqs could be the same. We are supposed to be the top with bows - no? [http://v020u46eff.maximumasp.com/AOD...asp?AOID=32064] [edit: add dimach and parry buffs as well to support our special attacks]

[Note on special attacks - maybe we need a special attack line for bows?]

#3 - Make a snare/root break line or to be realistic maybe a line that shortens the periode or makes it harder to snare/root the person. I am uncertain, how this should be made without tipping the scale to the other end.

#4 - Make a self only healing line that would help fill out the gaps between the heal other line. I read somewhere that the MA's are supposed to be one of the top healing classes, soo it would only support that statement.

#5 - An extend short damage line.

Lets make a brainstorm and put all our ideas together - I'll gladly edit this post over and over to sum up peoples ideas. Feel free to bump the tread as well, if you'd just like to show your support of it.

Edit: Any response would be nice. Is it just me that looks at our nanos this way? Are my idea worse than the crit nerf in 14.6? Do CZ like strawberry? C'mon ppl! Someone must have something to say!
