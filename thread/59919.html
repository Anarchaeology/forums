It's not about a "race" you're right-- but Funcom seems to have very little concept of risk versus reward. It's a basic game design concept, even a life concept.

People don't hunt outdoors much because the rewards are not there for the risks. And for the time required too. Missions give well over twice the XP of outdoor hunting in general. And far better items, due to chests. Many outdoor MOBs are very incorrectly valued. It is brutal fighting a Desert Eremite at lvl 140, for a measley 18K XP. A Cyborg Captain is actually easier and gives 40K XP! Monsters give very little XP compared to humanoids, and this is the fundamental problem.

Players needed to be reasonably and properly rewarded in XP and/or items for the things they do, otherwise they just won't do them. Insisting that something in a game should be done "for fun" isn't realistic.-- reward, reward, reward.
