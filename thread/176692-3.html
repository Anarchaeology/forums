Some advice for the soloing Shade (im lvl 60, have played for 2 weeks, and have soloed like 80% of my xp - and yes i started with no extra cash/items):

I have concentrated on thees two perklines (and have avoided the perks that are good in groups - like Piercing):
Totemic Rites
Spirit Phylateracy

Theese perks give cool over time damage to mobs (the same way Doc/Agent nanos do) and they deal instant damage to mobs and at the same time heal you.

Also two other tips - stuff that helped me out of a lot of dangerous situations:
- Buy a "Flurry of Blows" item if you can find it. It gives you a short but massive boost to your Melee Initiative. Its nice for dealing out a lot af Damage in a short time.
- Try doing a Teammish or two and get your hands on a Web Cloack. They gives a lot better armor than the Cloaks you find in the Temple of Three Winds (see a lot of you running around in theese brown things).

Adding bit of info most ppl prolly know, but

Flurry of blows adds inits and crit chance 10% of flurry QL I believe. While it offers great boosts, it also compromises your evades as it 'moves' your agg/deff bar, so you will experience a higher hit/crit ratio while using the flurry.

You farm Spec 1 the easiest in nasance, spec 2 in ely/scheol, spec 3 in adonis/pen, as well as some parts for spec 1,2,3,4 in penumbra. They all drop off sided (redeemed/unredeemed mobs), so get busy at those temples! Once you've gotten a complete book (spec 1 and 2 you combine into 1 book), head back to Professor Dedlock who is residing at the front desc in the IPS building and he'll happily take your books in return for specializations.

There are also NODROP weapons (one worse than the next) dropping off the minibosses and bosses in the archaic dungeons below temples (run into the spirit rooms and run either left or right, run all the way into the end room and jump into the hole in the floor.). Don't expect to be able to solo this though.
