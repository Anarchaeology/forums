The only person I know who has 1000 tokens spent several months gathering them and terminating endlessly to reach their goal. There are probably characters who have 1000 or are close to that number from buying tokens through the bag trade bug that was fixed a while back (not many of these folks left, I bet).

As for the person I know, I feel they achieved their goal in a somewhat "okay" manner, and I commend their... insanity and determination, but I hardly feel this is the way we should achieve this um, honor. If this individual had levelled normally, there's no way they would have received 1000 tokens. Hell, I token farmed to the 120 board with one of my characters, and that was the single most boring thing I've ever done in AO, even though it took a scant few days and was over soon enough. I could have gotten up to 250 or even 400 very easily, but this was as a very twinked level 6. Anyway, I didn't have the patience to continue -- I wanted to play the damn game!

When we have to think of crafty ways to come up with a 1000 token board, it seems we were never meant to have one or it's something for level 200 characters to do when they can't level anymore - gather up tokens until their brains bleed from the time and effort it would take at that level. But at level 6, you can do a mission in 5-10 minutes. The choice seems obvious to me. And because of this, I think the token system should be re-evaluated.

Personally, I'm glad I got the 120 board with my Meta before I started levelling. I'm just shy of 200 tokens now. About 70-80 tokens gathered in the amount of time it has taken me to level 6-71. And only about 10-15 levels were gained through hunting. The rest was solo missions. If I hadn't gotten a head start, and with my rate of levelling as a meta (which is high, even soloing), I wouldn't be much over my 60 token board.

I don't know what to suggest, though, because any solution I can think of will ultimately cause problems in the longrun.
