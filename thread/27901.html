My experience is that Roger Wilco is very efficient with bandwidth. The sound is VERY compressed. This is why it ends up sounding like a "walkie talkie". But it REALLY adds to the game. Does anyone actually play AO on a dial-up modem? I cannot even comprehend that.

When they integrated voice into TFC (Team Fortress Classic it was such a HUGE improvement. When someone on your team transmitted, a little sign would float above their head. It really added to the experience of being able to "connect" actual voice to the 'toon. This was particularly true with people you played with often - you got to recognize their voice, and developed an even greater rapport and team work.

You have to establish rules, though, because it can end up getting VERY chatty. But with teams in AO limited to 6 people, it would not be too bad.

There is nothing like the experience of being on a good team using Roger Wilco. With a strong leader issuing commands, thethe team members snapping into action. Playing TFC, me and two or three other mates on RW could DOMINATE an entire team of 6 or 8 players on the other side. To the point where it became laughable. (Scores like 150 to 8)
