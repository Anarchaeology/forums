Team worth scale looks something like this:

1. You bring nothing to a team in terms of buffs or special abilities. You don't fit into any role. You are only chosen if there is no other option.

2. While you still bring almost nothing to a team, but you have a few redeeming qualities that let you contribute something. However you would be dropped from the group in favor of most any other class.

3. You are a valueable asset to a team. You bring something to the team that few others can. While you bring much to a team, you aren't required for teaming and they could fill your spot with another person of equal value.

4. Teaming without you would be very difficult, but still possible. You can do great things for the team that no others could.

5. You are indespensible to a good team. Any team leaving without you stands little chance.


----------------------------------------------------------


I think the NT is in the right spot. They may be good damage dealers and calmers, but a good team can go on without them. They also are very limited in useful buffs, HE being their only one.


The Agent ranges from a low 2, to an average 3 depending on your level. Overall the agent is just as bad as the fixer if you take away their FP's. Even with FP's the Agent is usually nothing more than a crit buff dispenser for when the team can't find a Martial Artist. Things get better when you get to level 150 and have mimic professions, but even then you can't even come close to filling the shoes of another profession.

Fixers Aren't THAT Useless
OK, if that's your scale, then a fixer should be at least a 2 in teams, and 3 if the player is any good.

Run buffs and green evades, combined with good hit points, make the fixer the best puller in the game. Period. I wish the people I teamed with would, on average, understand this. People just don't seem to realize how much easier it is in team missions if I bring them the mobs one at a time ... and I can do it, too. With a bit of care, I've pulled one probe droid ... out of a set of 3 that were all within 1 meter of each other! And that's without even Grid Armor ... a fixer with grid armor makes an even better puller than I am, because they're almost immune to being hit or rooted.

But no, nobody wants to wait ... so they all rush in right after me, and we end up with 2 to 4 mobs on us all at once.

I also insist that of all the professions, fixers come in a solid 3rd behind enforcers and soldiers in total damage output. SMGs rock. Yes, so do shotguns ... but ransackable/deprivable/wranglable shotguns don't have special attacks. Fixers with SMGs get two burst special attacks, one at regular rate and another every 20 seconds.

At about the level where the NT gets calms, the fixer gets Team Grid Phreak, shaving several minutes off of the team's return to the mission terminal. Not indispensable, but darned handy.

Now, I'm not insisting that a fixer is indispensable, not in the same way that an enforcer or a doctor is. Yes, almost any profession can pull, if not as well. But this prejudice against fixers as team members is an irrational over-reaction.
