OOC: RP and Tower Attacks
The Nanomage Liberation Front occasionally attacks towers for RP reasons. Usually the defenders don't bite our RP but yesterday was different. During Opperation Adustum we spotted Obsidian Orders Towers. They were Vulnerable to attack so we decided to clear them. The suppression gas was at %5 and 20mins from 100% so we knew we weren't going to be able to Destroy the whole structure, but hopefully against all odds maybe an interesting conflict would emerge. When OO defenders arived it was like any other battle, not many words were spoken, all were too busy fighting. Only one member of OO decided to trash talk but stopped once he hit reclaim (grats Areezz, for a PvP noob your pretty good). Anyway once the tower gas went to 100% I returned from my own trip to reclaim (my alt is a Doctor named Charthos and he is in no way a Twink heh I have no patience for twinking I'd rather be RPing) and to my surprise Obsidian Order listened to our RP reasons for attacking and bit the story a bit. What a refreshing change from the usual "you are such a gimp" "we OWNZE JOO" crap you usually see. I think there is a lot of potential in NW if you try. Thanks OO for making that attack worthwile and remember it isn't persontal it's a ROLE PLAYING GAME . I look forward to making more in character enemies and even friends too! Have fun everyone and if you can don't forget who you are when attacking and defending!
