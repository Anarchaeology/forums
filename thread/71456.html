Omni-Com Investigations Division
"Omni-Com
Omni-Com handles all communication channels on- and off-world, including the Grid, as well as the operation of insurance terminals and the storage of insurance patterns." - as posted by Cz

We are a division of Omni-Com dedicated to the smooth operation of communications on Rubi-Ka. Our investigations include anything where com units, the grid and Insure terminals in Omni-1 may be compromised.

We are now hiring staff to assist in the new workload we have, with the new towers that are arriving, communications will be more inprotant than ever. Please visit our grid site here for more information.

OOC: We are a Role Play Guild looking for people to help shape the guild. We require 3 or 4 mature responsible players to be on the board of Directors, and up to 20 members (for now).

We require Fixers, NT's, MP's, Crats, Soldiers and agents..but we're not going to turn anyone down if they have a solid interest in this and act in a mature possitive mannner

Please send a /tell to Hostbody (my alt who is now my main) and we'll get you set up, or apply at the web site.

Goals : 100% fun, helping each other, and be a part of great stories.

This org is only on RK 1 at this time
