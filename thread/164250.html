Written in Crayon
'Written in Crayon' is a small collection of what I think are my better AO stories. Sure, they've all appeared on this forum at one stage, but as some are over a year old and I thought it would be nice to have them all in one place.

Each story is pretty short, mildly amusing and self-contained. They dont require prior knowledge of specific characters or events to enjoy. Quite a few involve robots. I guess I was just living out my Isaac Asimov delusions.
