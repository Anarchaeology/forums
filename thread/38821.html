A little OOC question about your Roleplaying
OOC: Where did everybody start? What is your history in roleplaying? Heres a little run down of mine.

1996-1998 - AOL(dont have it anymore, thank god) chat rooms, I roleplayed in Star Wars Cantinas.

1999 - Began roleplaying in AOL rp called "Rhydin". Played as a soldier in the La Cosa Nostra. Eventually, the leader commited suicide(IC, not OOC =) and the group disbanded. I went on my own to create some various small groups. The name i went under Hans Lyden. 2001 was my last year in Rhydin, in which time I had started up something that would eventually grow when I left AOL.

2001 - Begin looking for a "fix" on roleplaying. Played IRC Star Trek for about a week, wasnt my type of game. Moved onto MUD's, which kept me content for awhile. Thats when I first registered my Anarchy Online account. I played for a few months then cancelled. Later on in the year, I came back.

2002 - Early this year I cancelled my AO account again, went to WWII Online for a brief period then about to Anarchy Online. I cancelled yet again, and came back to where I am now, and i am quite pleased.

Now that Bone kindly pushed my nose on this thread, I felt like I should add my part to it.

1982-83 around that time, I saw a RPG for the first time. The name was "Bushido" and I was interested at first cause it played in Japan and I was a fan of Japan at that time. Well, RP got me quite fast, and the added benefit of Ultima I & II on my brand new C64 pushed me into that direction.

1984-1991 Was the year when I started to roleplay regularly with a decent group and it lasted until I left school and moved into another town. We played D&D (I remember the DM trying to kill our lvl 20+ group and failed), Das Schwarze Auge (very popular in Germany), MERP, Rolemaster, Spacemaster (one of my fav), Warhammer (fantasy battle & roleplay, 40k, Epic ... well, bring 'm on, GW), Midgard (also a German game), TORG, Cyberpunk 2011 and 2020 (still one of my favorites, SciFi and Dark Future especially are my great passion, maybe that's why I chose AO over UO and EQ), Call of Cthulhu, GURPS and I'm sure I forgot a lot of others. Does Paranoia count as RPG?
Ultima 3-6, Bard's Tale 1-3 and some other computer RPGs were interesting, but I still prefer people to a preset system.

1992-1998 After a time of rather no playing (only computer games, mostly strategy) I found a great group and I never played as much as I did in that time. I still miss them. Our master created two system (well, by picking good ideas from other systems and combining them with his own ideas) and a huge own fantasy world that still grows and evolves.

1998 Was another remarkable step. I moved again and left my so far best group behind, but I discovered live roleplaying. I had seen it 10 years earlier but back then it was weird people with no idea what to do tried to make up for the lack of virtually everything. It had changed, and it's become great. Germany has a very active live-roleplaying community, and though most of it is fantasy, we recently created our own Cyberpunk live (grab your nerfgun, baby) and it's working great.

Today, pen&paper is something I just started again and I can't yet tell where the journey will go. I've cut back fantasy live-roleplay as the last 1-2 years brought a lot of new players, and I'm sad to say, immature and bad players which have caused most of my friends to quit. Cyberpunk live is still happening, but it's only 3-4 events per year, as we're from all around Germany and it takes a lot of time, money and work to set up a plot and the surroundings.

Oh, and of course I play AO - I had seen beta, then moved and lost my connection for some time and then came back after I got the impression that AO had at least become semi-final by the time I joined in September 2001. I'm still here, so I must be liking it. I keep ranting about it, but I don't quit. Why? Perhaps because hope springs eternal. But surely because it's not the system that matters, it is the people. This is true for all games and it certainly is here. And I've found a lot of great people around here.
