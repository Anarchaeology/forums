I remember this very clearly a good long time ago. When I started playing AO hunting in general was a good way to get experience. As I remember it you would hunt outside for experience and then do missions for money. Out door mobs never provided much cash but they were great xp. You would move from one camp to another killing eyes for a while, get some money then move to mantz or other mobs. It was FUN and it added some excitement. People really got to see Funcoms beautiful world. I still remember turning around one morning and gawking at the sunbeams in the mist through trees. It was incredible, something I had not seen before in a game, let alone an MMORPG. I chose an adventure so that I could go exploring and it was exciting. I miss those days, the game is not as new, and it may not be as graphically beautiful as the next generation MMORPGs coming out now but I still find things I have not seen and I still have not been some places. I was disappointed when I returned to find that almost nobody hunted any more, it was always missions. I still do missions as they are one of the best ways to get experience and money at the same time. Shadow Lands has brought back that wondrous feel for me. I still show it off to all my friends who come over and cant wait for someone to have not seen it. Some of you may have read my other posts, maybe not. I often get caught up in the functionality of the game and profession I love, and many times try to add ideas or at least suggest why something is not likely. Dont get me wrong, I have strong feelings and ideas some times but I really do intend well.

Fixing Shadow Lands mobs is a must. I cant explore with grays and greens killing me. I cant continue with the quests if I cant defeat the monsters. I enjoy these things as much as gaining the next level or finding new loot. I dont often show it but I do look.

*Dont forget the programs the monsters run on you, those are almost as killer as their hits.

Sorry, not much for suggestions at the moment but if I come up with any I will append them. This post I guess was more of a guide to why it is so important to some, at least me, to have this problem fixed; above and beyond the shear playability factor. Also to show some one, other plays, Cz, maybe even the devs that this game really does mean something to people beyond just being a game.

Hi All,

I agree, missions are pretty much the way to go for cash
and prizes, but I still like to hunt.

I'm a L 102 MA, currently.

DynaCamps are easy enough to solo up to around my level
(been doing it since 70 or so...) If you have a good
run speed just agg the boss and run. The adds eventually
drop off and you can solo the boss. The XP and loot
is very nice, esp since some of the loot is wanted by other players. I'll admit that the running does get more interesting if
there are other, non camp, mobs floating about...

On SL hunting - at 102 I can take the L80 mobs (ok, so the mobs MY level make me feel like a gimp... and hecklers are a far of dream) and it's fun wandering

L75 mob that hands me my cute little ***. Over that next hill, tho,
I can take on 3 L80 spirits (7HP left, but I can do it.)
