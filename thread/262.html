Results of In Game Poll on Patch!
Since for me this patch tops the frustration level on my behalf I decided to conduct a quick in-game poll on the new patch. I asked players:

"How do you feel about the new patch? Good - Undecided - Bad"

16 People responded: BAD
7 People responded: UNDECIDED
5 People responded: GOOD


I've been with AO since Beta4. The only reason I did this poll..and it certainly is a very small sample... is because I really had hoped that something would come out of AO. The last patch with it's unanounced DRASTIC changes to game mechanics for me - Conceal being useless (read this post: http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...p?threadid=101) really killed my agent at this point. It also somehow shows that FCom seems to be ignorant about their own game. I don't understand at all how they could do such a thing...it's beyond my comprehension.

This and the fact that so far the storyline at best has been cosmetic, really leads me to believe that they are not capable of generating a story that will actually impact GAMEPLAY.

As to storyline, so far there has been:

Spider attack on Tir
Cyborg attacks
'Static' Dungeons

None of this actually matters to anyone..who cares? Other than some cute screenshots on ao-basher.com it is inconsequential even if you were on for the all out lag attack of the mutant thingamajigs.

For instance: Nano is the basis of RK technology and economics (so the 'story' says). Now in order to make that impact the Game mechanics, I would make it necessary for Clan and Omni to hold and defend certain Nano mines against the 'enemy'. Less nano mines that are held by a faction....well, for instance, that would make Nano recharge kits a rare item in stores..etc.

But so far: There is no such thing as storyline impact on the game world. The story is nice..so are the movies...and if I drop some acid I might actually 'feel' it in-game!

Now, I still have a tiny slice of hope left (otherwise I wouldn't post here) - But I'm asking all you fellow players: Do you think that they will be able to make this work? So far they have won the 'Bork Award' on my scale of game producers.

Had an idea, wrong forum, but see it as a continuation
I just came up with an example... its an easy one for a solution for a big problem in this game, stupid nerfing that destroyed at least one class, while others were unaffected, now they busted another class because of the same problem, others are unaffected... Ok, I am just a simple 3D designer, I have only worked with minor games and such... But try this:

Range cap Agents, Meta physicist:
Problem: With "Tab" Agents and Metaphysisists could shoot people they could not see, and that could not see them.

Funcom sollution: Longrange rifle, and Metaspells have the range of a blowgun, or throwing a rock.

Another sollution: "Tab" only react to people in sight.

Another sollution: Remove "Tab" to find players or monsters or both. Make it What you see is what you can shoot. That would mean a lot of paranoid people in political zones, looking over their shoulders all the time, yes... Shouldn't they?!! It should be scary out there!
