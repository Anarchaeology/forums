Omni Press Conference - Uncovering The Truth
Time: 10:00pm Eastern Time
Date: February 8, 2002
Location: Rome Green

Directions from Newland using whompa system:
Newland > ICC > OMNI-Trade > Omni Ent > Rome
Go to Rome Green. Once in Rome Green go to point 325,313 in front of the Omni-Admin building. That is where the press conference will begin. If you don't know how to use the points, the Admin building is located in the middle of Rome Green.

Extended Description:
I would like to invite all Omni Organizations to come out tomorrow for what I'm calling the press conference of a lifetime to release information on the clans. It will occur tomorrow at 10pm Eastern time. Located at position 325, 313 area facing the Omni-Admin building in Rome Green. Wewill release the findings that an organization, that I can not name due to our contract, has discovered regarding the clans. It is very essential to our fight against the Clans and the creatures in their areas (and some of ours).

NOTE: AD Police Members are to arrive 1 hour early for reherrsals.
