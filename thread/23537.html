With regards to the tokens, we can see that to the average player they mean nothing.
The fact this post has slipped way off page one shows this.
The high level token board is no longer a case of "If I get it" it is now a "When I get it".
I'm really not sure where the original change to the token system came from, if it was in response to the "Token Farmers" then initially it failed due to tokens not being unique and even now people can still do 1000 small and easy missions followed by a terminate.

Before the changes I do not remember seeing one post along the lines of "1000 token board is too difficult to get" so I can only assume those that found the board too difficult to gain must have been e-mailing Funcom directly.
The fact is this, some people will always feel they should be able to get everything in the game and a lot of these people want an easier route as possible to ownership.
Fact, if anybody in turn posts a message along the lines of "It should be difficult to gain" or "I've almost got mine and I don't think it should be made any easier" they are then told to "Stop moaning" or "You exploited and cheated your way" or "You just don't want anybody else to get what you have".
On the flip side, yes AO was designed to be a multi-player game, nobody is disputing this fact, however a lot of players cannot find teams whenever they want to, they may play at different times, they may only have a short amount of time to play every day, they may prefer to play the game as mainly a single player one and only get into teams occasionally  it is all down to individual game play styles.
There was a choice to be made, get into teams, do a lot of fast levelling, and play the "Get to level 200 first" version of AO.
An alternative style is playing the game at a much slower rate, do a lot of missions, sure I maybe many levels behind but I'm also gaining tokens.

The token changes attempted to move those playing at a slower rate or playing a lot of single player sessions over to teams.
Suddenly they found that tokens were no more difficult to gain at higher levels, in fact they were a darn sight easier and whereas they had an advantage, lower level but more tokens they now find themselves both at a lower level and having less tokens per mission.
It is too late to change the system back, the day that patch went live the old token system was destroyed and no matter what Funcom told us, the old tokens were massively devalued.
In fact most of the token boards were also devalued, what would have been a difficult task to achieve has now become a trivial task that even the most lacklustre or total team player can achieve with ease.

To fix the token system.
The amount of tokens you earn at each level now has to be set in stone, there must be no changing of the way things currently are.
There is no point attempting to add higher level boards if as soon as you do so Funcom get a load of moaning about "How difficult it is" and they then go and double the amount of tokens given out.

Although Hobbes' idea on making higher level boards available but they only give a cosmetic bonus rather than anything truly useful is nice, I personally don't think this should be the case.
That suggestion seems to be one to keep the "moaning population" happy, something added that doesn't make them "ubber" so they wont necessarily want it.
I would like to see the next board added at the 1500 token mark and I would like to slowly see additions to AC & NCU's.

The token system was the only goal that a player who mainly plays single had.
It did not reward "boring or exploiting characters" as so many people liked to say when this threads were initially posted.
During normal play the mainly missions player will gain a lot of tokens, she/he tends not to choose missions where he knows that there will be just five green MOB's and that is it.
Some of these missions are extremely difficult and take many hours of play; not rewarding the mission player is actually more unfair than doing so.

I would like to see the proverbial "carrot" dangled in front of my nose, the opportunity to gain higher level token reward boards that do add some useful things to my character.
Token Farmers do not effect me, so they decide to do 3000 small missions at level 6, then so be it, that is their chosen game play style, good luck to them.
Those that have already exploited to get their 1000 token board having an easier ride to the higher level boards, again these don't bother me, they want to cheat it is up to them, but to not allow the legitimate players access to higher level boards because of exploiters is rather letting the cheats dictate the game, they shouldn't be allowed to.

If you do not do missions, if you are 100% of the time a team player then the addition of higher level boards shouldn't concern you, you always have a choice in this game and if a higher level board takes your fancy they get out there and earn it, don't sit back moaning and hoping Funcom will make it easier for you  you wouldn't like it if us "token hunters" were suddenly awarded a patch that doubled the amount of XP we received just so we could have an easier time and catch you up, so please don't expect the same with tokens.
