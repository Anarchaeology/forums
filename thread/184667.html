New Sport: Jobe Bracer Jumping!
A new fad is sweeping across the Shadowlands newbie training area. (and by "sweeping across the Shadowlands," I mean 'I have done this a few times myself'). All you have to do is go to the SL newbie area with a character that is low enough to use the warping feature on the newbie bracer. Simply activate the bracelet, wait a second or so, then jump off the starting platform. You will fall towards near certain death only to be teleported back to safety at the last moment. I suggest you look down while falling and that you yell something like "Banzaaaaiii" as you jump. If you're really coordinated, get a friend to stand below and look up as you try to drop right above him.

Please note that Zanthyna, Zanthyna Corporation and it's subsidiary Woefallen Industries are not liable for any death or injury resulting from jumping off the platform.

History: Jobe Bracer Jumping was created by me after I discovered that you can safely step off the platform right next to Drake (if you fall back towards the center of the platform). If done right, you will land in water and take no damage. (but if done wrong, you will 'land' at reclaim). I soon began using my start-up bracer a second before I jumped and the rest his history.

Have fun fellow SL adrenaline seekers!
