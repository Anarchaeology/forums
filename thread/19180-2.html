I just wanted to point out a trend. Seems like everytime you sign on the game there's a new FunCom change to beat you on the head.

I hate exploiters as much as the next guy. I didn't go into detail on each of my items. It's just that every single solitary time that FunCom chooses to address something they take the easy way out and bring out the Nerf bat. It makes you wonder if they're some kind of sickos with castration fixations.

All FunCom knows how to do is Nerf. They aren't original, can't fix a problem properly, and have yet to show me they deserve my respect as game designers.

Me? I prefer positive reinforcement. Likewise if a challenge is too easy for me, then I prefer that greater challenges be presented for me to tackle. I don't want my challenge to be increased by attaching a ball and chain to my leg and poking one of my eyes out.
