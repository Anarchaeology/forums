Wanting us to travel is understandable on Funcom's part. They built a very large world, and they'd like us to see it, instead of packing into one zone and crashing their servers.

However, the incentive isn't there.

5 things we might stand to gain from travelling far and abroad:

1: Mission destination is sometimes by a unique mob, so the mission becomes a tool of discovery.

Unfortunately the mobs are dead and not there most of the time. And most of them are well known anyway - so there's no need to look anymore.

2: Mission is occasionally by an interesting hunting ground, and again, allows us to discover this wonderful place to hunt.

Most of the hunting grounds are lvl 90 or under. The few over that are so far away, so far from a save, so dangerous, and so low in xp/minute that their not worth it. As for the low-lvl hunting grounds, if you hang at one lvl 30ish hunting zone for 3 hours you'll be lvl 40, and all the other 300 lvl 30 hunting zones in the game just became useless. No need to look for more.

3: Mission rewards (money and xp) are greater the further away you travel.

While true, it doesn't address the basic issue that mission rewards are crap.

4: Travelling can give us a break from the monotony of leveling.

Not sure if this is good or bad. I don't need a break from leveling, i need a break from monotony. The 2 don't have to be the same thing.

5: Gives you an opportunity to do some sight-seeing.


But of course you can do some sight seeing faster if you dont waste time in a dingy brown cave.



More or less there is no reason to travel at all. The incentive isn't there. I'll see you in Broken Shores

1. Spend 50k for a kodaik already...the 100 or so points in V.Ground won't kill ya, especially with IPR on the way. (Also, don't give me any business about it being a dark blue skill for MP's...a very good RL bud has an MP and I bought him a Kodaik back in the day when we were both low level and MP's were gimps. With a few implant adjustments, he equipped it without too much sacrifice)

2. Get a runbuff. Most fixers give them for free and even the lower ones can almost double the runspeed of a level 53 MP.

3. Do more research on the mission terminals. The mission locations can vary signifigantly. Ask others your level where they find good mission locations. Eventually you'll find a good terminal to get missions to suitable locations for your level. The travel might not be "easy", but it won't be the slaughterfest you seem to be experiencing.

4. Get maps to all the places you frequent and be sure you have the people and monsters map upgrades to spot potential aggros before they spot you. Learn avoidance.

5. Put your agg/def bar on full defense when travelling through dangerous areas.
