>>if the missions actually worked,

not sure what exactly you mean, but I do believe I can safely say that there are hundreds if not thousands of missions completed by Rubi-Kans every day:). As for having trouble finding item, that's just luck/happenstance. There are few ways you can remedy this - reroll a new mission. Team up - it's easer to find item with 6 people, not to mention XP is better and you can get higher quality item:). Try to find missions in places you are less likely to get the brown cave. Items are easier to find in office/urban settings. Or, skip item completely - get missions where you have to kill or describe a person; or bring item to a machine.

>>there were absoutly no noteworthy features added since I left last year

Well, I guess depending how you define "noteworthy", you can certainly say that:).
However, for example, Team Missions are brand-brand new. They are still controversial, but many of us nevertheless enjoy them:).
There have been numerous small tweaks literally too many to list here - check the individual patch notes. So it all depends what you expected... after all, of course, AO is the same game that it was 6 months ago - it's not going to turn into something completely different just because you gave it some time:).

I hope you find fun on Rubi-Ka:). And if not, I do wish you luck in finding a suitable game outside of this world:).
