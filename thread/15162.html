Well, at least there's one customer who seems to be satisfied with the current state of the game. Apparently, except for the chest loot issue, the above poster is not experiencing any of the other problems that most of us seem to be having just since 13.9x such as:

1. Items no longer available in shops such as MA's Blessed with Thunder, Biomech armor, ect. and shop inventory rarely changing.

2. Increased lag and framerate reduction that's getting close to being as bad as the week the game was released.

3. Increased crashing of missions (causing the mission to repop and getting the player killed) and shops (causing the loss of items which GM's refuse to reimburse).

4. Generally more random crashes, even when sitting still doing nothing, often causing the player not to be able to log back in for some time either due to crash looping or the server not releasing the character from the server and allowing re-login.

5. Horrible sync with the servers, especially while in combat that causes mobs to continue to hit the player even after the mob is dead, lying on the ground and the player has already gotten the experience from the kill. (This is typically followed by the appearance that on the next fight the player is doing 10-15 instant hits on the opening shot that are actually holdovers from the previous fight)

6. Insta/chain casting mobs (okay, this is really from 13.8) that allows mobs to either continuously cast root/snare/deprive or to cast nukes in rapid succession with no animation for casting and still being able punch/shoot while casting.

7. Weapon/fist bugs that either caused people to start punching with weapons when they shouldn't, or worse, have stopped players from punching while wielding mele weapons.

I'm glad someone's happy with the way the game is currenty working and I'm not trying to slam the original poster, however, for many of us, the game is almost unplayable now. Sure, I'd like to see the new team missions and some of the other new features listed in the 14.0 patch notes, but, as I'm sure many would agree, it would be much better to fix the problems above (which are all brand new) as well as the myriads of other major issues that have plagued this game since day one.

Engieman, keep playing and have a good time. I'm happy for you. Truly. If only the rest of us were so fortunate.
