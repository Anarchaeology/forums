Guild Chat
I would like to be able to make announcements to my guild, perhaps using a seperate chat menu, just for buffering messages to guild members on events or emergency meetings.

Everytime you wanted to make an announcement to your guild, you could type your message and then it would be buffered for all of the members that are offline.

That way everyone is aware of the meeting or event.

I am a bit fed up of sending tells to each and every person in my guild! So this would solve the problem.
