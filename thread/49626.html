And as for high-levels being evil, I think that's a load of bull. I can name many high-levels, past and present, clan and omni, who are great people. I won't name names so people won't tell them to death, but I think the issue is this:

When you're a bad person, you're a bad person. If you're a bad person in-game, then your behavior becomes more NOTICABLE at higher levels, because there's comparatively few high-levels and because there's so little for them to do. High-levels tend to aggregate in clumps doing the same things, which have wider repercussions, because they affect a tighter, smaller group.

As for the ganking thing, I have no idea. I explore. I hunt. I team. I don't PvP. There is no war, as far as I'm concerned, so I don't have any reason to PvP, and even if there was, I have little interest in it anyway. What I do agree with is that if you're in a 25% zone, conflict is to be expected. If you're in a 0% zone, you have more important things to worry about than the battle between OT and the clans, since you're likely surrounded by Eremites, Omi, Medusae, and so on wherever you step.

However, I disagree with the whole "shoot any clanner" or "shoot any Omni" just because they're the other side.
