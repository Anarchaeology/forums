I wrote about this in another thread: http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...0&pagenumber=3

My idea was basically to use a skill req on the reciever instead of a level req. After all, it is a skill based system and a Lvl 20 putting all his ip on comp litt wouldn't be any better at fighting than a Lvl 5 going for weapons skill (unbuffed).

Trying not to be too drastical, a 25% req (needs some work) on recieving end seems nice. A char with 100 MC and 100 MM could recieve a AC-shield that requires 400 MC/MM to cast, if he has the ncu for it.

Note: For all nanos in your teaming range you probably won't be affected unless you have a very extreme char.

If NCU is a computers memory, your skill in the different nano skills is the cpu/video/sound and so on. Noone expects AO to run on a 486, or even a P166MMX, no matter if you have 2Gb memory, right?

If you can't fit the highest Ess, but you can sure fit a Ess a lot higher than you can selfcast, but instead of an 100% increase in Str you'll maybe be limited to a 30% increase.


This could be implemented in two ways:

- either as a general requirement, affecting all players, both PvM and PvP. Normal teaming won't be affected, but power levelling and twinking will be a bit harder as the highest programs can't be used. (is it intended for a lvl 20 to fit Q75 implants?)

- As a pulsating tower (liked that idea) with a NCU Overload that scraps all programs not fullfilling the requirement above.

Another note: Twinks do and should own, uberbuffed chars makes it impossible for a new player (or any player not making a twink) to enjoy PvP, thus limiting it to old players making a second, third, forth char.

I don't see how this could be "dumbing", you still would have to find the highest Ess to be the best at PvP and a twink would still beat a non-twink with the same buffs but maybe a self buffed twink would stand a chance against a uberbuffed vanilla/casual char. Instead of just going for the gold (the highest Ess there is) you'll just have to go for the highest Ess you can wear instead.

(Ess being general powerful program, change to HoT, DS at will)

Reasonable?
