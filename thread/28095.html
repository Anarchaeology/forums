List of Features Not Yet Implemented:
Greetings, forumers. The recent topic of the online instruction manual being re-done has caused me to wonder, in total, what features have not been implemented yet. So I was hoping that we could compile a list, for our own reference.

I am relatively new to the world of Anarchy Online, and I believe that many new players could benefit from such a list, at least until the release of the new online manual.

There are a number of things that have not yet been implemented, along with numerous things that are different than how they are stated in the pack-in manual. Typically, the only way for a new user to find these things out is through experimentation (which sometimes leads us to wonder if something's been done wrong on *our* end), to ask Funcom customer support (and I don't think they should have to be bothered by every little question that we have), to endlessly scour poorly-written online FAQ's, or to ask fellow players (from who we occasionally receive hostile or abrupt responses).

So, if you'd like to participate, I think that we can make things much clearer for the newer, and even a few of the veteran players.

Note: Remember that this is supposed to be informative, and helpful to each other. This isn't a complaint list for Funcom. Running a game such as Anarchy Online must be incredibly complex and difficult, so please refrain from Funcom-bashing, something that I see far too much of. This is our resource, so that we can be helpful and knowledgeable as the world of Anarchy Online continues to develop and grow. Thank you, everyone.
