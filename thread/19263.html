Total Mirror Shield
The mirror shield line should *always* count as a better nano than the reflect shield or be additional to reflect shield. Soldier Weapons
More assault riffles suited for soldiers. Its boring if everybody uses a novaflow.
a) assault riffles or miniguns with soldier profession requirements
b) better versions of Nova-like weapons with HeavyWeapon requirements and give Soldiers a self only big HeavyWeapon buff

d) soldier shoulder mounted mini cannons


Reload Button
I would like to click a button to reload the weapon currently in use. Dragging ammo over the gun in the open wear window is not very handy. An ammo status bar would be nice too.

Simple: 3 bullets should do more damage then 1 bullet. Increase burst minimum damage. A crit burst hit should be more than a normal crit.
