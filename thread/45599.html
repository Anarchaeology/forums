Just one request
I had an amazing day today......What was going to be just a average trip to Rhompas to see what was happening, turned into a really kewl event. I was handcuffed and threatend by someone, and then saved by someone else..and then followed by a guy who made a point of talking into his comm everytime i was doing something...I was able to talk someone into distracting my tail so I could slip out and get away without being followed (this all in one simple trip to rhompas ) and then when i was on my way to my HQ, one of the members of my guild reported the person who was tailing me telling someone that they knew where I was...so I went to a secret location, while my guildies followed and gathered information on the person.

You know, this is the stuff I love about this game....role playing to me makes the game worth every penny I spend on it, especially when these things happen that I have no knowledge of before.

So there I was, getting all my guildies working on this, and it lead to a point where we had him, in a position where it would be very easy to capture him and question him.....

But instead of ending the day with a bang, all the role play goodness ended with a whimper. We approached him, and began speaking to him, while he appeared to be trying to score with a girl, ...and I got a tell saying something along the lines of "we can talk about what happened in rhompas later, please this is my lucky night"....

well...i don't want to sound like a spoil sport here, and if there are people who use this game to "get thier rocks off" fine...whatever turns your crank.....but If you are going to role play with me, you have to remember that it is more than just about you....as soon as you interact with people in a RP situation, you can expect that they (if good and dedicated role players) are still playing...the next time they see you..

My one request is this. If you are going to do free form RP with me, please stay in character, please understand that I intend to continue the RP until such time as there is closure...and if you don't want to, or are busy with something else, send me a tell after the RP and say something like "I have other plans for later, do you mind if we pick this up tomorrow?'. I can completly respect that, and leave you be....if I know nothing, other than, for example, you have been following me, I am going to RP my heart out to find out why, and especially when you start it, I expect you are willing to finish it too.

Thank you for reading. I look forward to many more fun adventures with everyone.

Jesska,

I'm not privy to the particulars, but I would say that unless you gave a ooc tell to the person who was tailing you that you'd be dropping out of sight, you're the one who broke contact first.

As such, it shouldn't be surprising if that person thought the RP session was over and resumed to do other things (such as 'get lucky' or whatever) I think you should take heed of your own saying,

"If you are going to role play with me, you have to remember that it is more than just about you"


Sorry, but I thought this should be said since I think it's dangerous for the RP community as a whole if we get locked-down into viewing every encounter only from our personal point of view and ignoring the fact that there's another person out there (with their own thoughts, plans, and time-table).

What gets me most is when someone proceeds to get annoyed at another for some imagined faux-pas, when there clearly wasn't any established communication of intent for the encounter.

Bad roleplayers? No. Just poor communication and I hate to see you (or anyone else for that matter) rip apart others, and their respective roleplaying styles, because of it.


P.S. Just in case other people are curious, I'm not the guy in question - I don't even know who it is. As for Enerbias' comment about being positive... I normally am quite supportive of anything RP, but somehow the tone in paragraph 5 just gets me really really concerned.

You make a good point. My intentions were not to start a fight, or to create mass debate over it. For me, when I log in, I am role playing, and when i log out I stop....a large majority of the people I have learned to RP with in AO are the same way, so it would never occour to me to send a tell to the person follwoing me to say "i am disapearing now"... and on the other end of the spectrum, I suppose for someone who doesn't RP with that same structure, it may not occour to them to send me a tell either, so as you pointed out, yes...i should take my own advice.

Just for the record I was not intentionally trying to rip someone else apart for thier role play style, it was more my own fustration to the situation at hand. Perhaps I should have rephrased, and thank you for pointing that out. My intention I suppose was to let people know how I role play, so if they decide to take part in freeform, unplanned RP with me, that they know the show isn't over for me til i log, unless they tell me it's over for them for the day. I was trying to establish a way of communicating this, so I can avoid the same kind of let down I had from this event at the end of the day.

This is how I look at it, and I could be completly off base here...but given the situation...being followed and don't know why..It makes quite a bit of sense that I would be concerned about why, begin my own investigation that would have that person followed (especially concidering at one point he put something shiny in his hand, and could very well have been an attempt to kill me) So with that in mind, was I off base for proceeding to approach him when he was in a less public area? I don't really think so...was he off base for not playing IC when I did....well...depends on perspective, but concidering what you said....I'll go with no. The real problem was communication...you are correct

But is the solution to send a message to the person and say "we are going to capture you and question you about the events today, is that alright?"...This i find generally a spoil for the suprise element in RP. Half of what I liked about this particular event was there was no warning...it just happened, the same way things in life happened. Is the solution to say "well now that I got away from you, i am disapearing while I send my agents after you". Again, this is now taking away the freeform aspect to it, and turning it into something planned. freeform RP is a hard egg to crack, when people take part in it, when it is conveinient for them, but when faced with the same kind of situation, all of a sudden it is a "no I don't want to role play anymore today" well all I was saying is, perhaps, they should let me or whoever they are Rping with know. I could certainly say "i;m dropping out of sight" but I fail to understand the reason I would...of course I am dropping out of sight.....i was being followed. I don;t want to be followed, i want to know why I am being followed. The problem with sending a lot of OOC tells to tell someone what you are doing, now confines the RP to a structure, which, if I remember correctly, this began as freeform.

My solution could be wrong, but it makes sense to me. I am all for other RP'rs to share thier thoughts on what options there are for it, but keep in mind....what I am talking about is unstructured, unplanned, completly freeform Role Play....to much knowlege to the happenings of the players involved, takes freeform out of it, and makes it structured....which at that point, maybe we should hand each other a script and time table.

Please don't get me wrong. I am all for both types of play, but if it starts freeform, I am under the impression it intends to stay freeform.

and many appologies to my previous comments regarding good or experienced RP'rs....I can admit it was a poor choice of words to use, and perhaps I was more fustrated than i should have been at the time of the post

nothing but respect for the RP communities in AO...even if we don't always like each other, i have nothing but full respect for people who want to RP.
