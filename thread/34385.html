NEW In-Game Map Available!
Greetings!

A new in-game map is available for download!

The download will provide two files to replace the current in-game map files with maps of Rubi-Ka which has:

  • playfield borders with zone lines
  • playfield names
  • Whom-Pah System routes
  • Grid Access Terminal locations


These maps function just as the original maps do, allowing for zooming in and out, and centering the map to your current location.

To download the new in-game map, go to http://travel.to/rubi-ka and click on the "IN-GAME MAP FILES" link.
