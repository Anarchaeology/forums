Announcing!: Old Athen Restoration Rally!
Once again its time for all Clanners to rally around the restoration of Old Athen into a city for all the Clans!

When?: Saturday, November the 8th at (provided I got this right with all the daylight savings changes)
  • 11am PST
    2pm EST
    7pm GMT

Where?: Old Athen of course! We'll meet by the old prison just west of the grid exit in the north end of the city.

What?: We're going to rally and we'll have some fun contests with prizes again. You might even learn a little something about the Clans as well.

Why?: To support making Old Athen resemble more of a Clan city rather than just an old wreck with soulless OT markings. To educate people about our main city and the diversity which is accepted among the Clans.

Who?: You! (If you're Clan or Neutral. Security will be present to protect against Omni insurgents). This event is currently hosted by Clans
  • Veritas
    Vox Populi
    The Pilgrims


This is not an event to miss, so come one and all!
