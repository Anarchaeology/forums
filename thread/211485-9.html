Yeah, that was pretty silly of me to assume that Clanners might mean what they say.
One would think that a person with your verbal capabilities and lingual prowess would know that what someone says is not always what they mean. It boils down basically to semantics. Pretend that you and I are talking about pizza, and you hate pizza. For you the word pizza would have a negative connotation in the taste category, or maybe you dont like greasy food, so the negative connotation is in the grease. I on the other hand, being the smelly Clanner I am, enjoy greasy food. So for me the grease has a positive connotation. This is what results when 2 people with opposite ideas of how a word is inerpreted come together. Its called a reality gap. The reality of pizza for me is "Greasy good pizza", for you its "Ewww greasy pizza". So before taking what someone says and literalizing it, think about the semantics of what the person is saying. Usually it will help you a lot in negotiating or even in just relating with other people. This works very well on people you already know a lot about, it will help you to predict how a person will react to a certain phrase you might say, and then you can know what they will say and do after you say it. Try it on your friends its fun.
