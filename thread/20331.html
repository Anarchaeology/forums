Fixer only Mission Terminals
Expanding on team mission setup: People could come to the fixer to pull a mission for them, and the fixer gets to come too (or he'll delete the mission, can't have fixers being left out now).

Where the pickup items are limited to loot-only items that other classes dream about, including nodrop items. The reward items can be normal stuff and maybe extra tokens for the fixer.

These include: carrier craft, gunships, other vehicles, rings (of all kinds), higher level ICC coats, grid armor, high level NT nanos, Decranium's armor and other NPC items (hey, these are special team missions).

Of course, I don't know if FC would do this, but it would be easy to code, since it just works on existing elements.

I'm also torn over whether agent could FP into the terminal, maybe not, make the terminals an actual person-npc, and have the missions cost more to get, like 10x normal price, and have the npc state the missions: I heard that they were storing a gunship here...
