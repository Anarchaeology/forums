Party on 27 DEC with ARK and GSP
Hey all,

Well, the three year anniversary of ARK is coming up fast, and naturally that can mean only one thing.... PARTY!

Now, while you may have seen us do impromtu parties here and there, this is nothing like that. While we're not doing the EXPO thing (yet), this will be big.


Details:

27 Dec (THIS Saturday)
2200 GMT on Atlantean and two hours later we move to Rimor.

Reets Retreat.

Tarryk of GSP will be DJing and causing mass hysteria as always.

Item give-aways sponsored by ARK include:
-Childhood Memories (omgz teh teddy bears!)
-Wrapped Christmas Gifts

Santa Hats will be given away to anyone who wants them. See the ARK at the Reets entrance for your hat.

Lag Permitting there will be a dance contest in addition to the normal give-aways. (if the main glass floor is too laggy we'll use a different room or even go outside).


So come celebrate with us! The ARK 3 Year Anniversary Party! h00t!
