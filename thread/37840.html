Omni MP's and the q200 Custom Heavy Suppressor
This is a repost of a long convoluted reply to the question: can an Omni nanomage MP ever equip a q200 Custom Heavy Suppressor (without switching to clan in order to wear the Kaehlar Sleeves).

I haven't necessarily organized it too well. Reading it may make your head spin.

The short answer is yes it is possible. So, all those omni soldiers who say it's "impossible" are simply wrong, but it is a lot of work.

-------------------------------------

Well, let's see. This is a long, complicated post. So, you have been warned. In some ways, I have to walk through the process backwards to help illustrate the problem.

I know I figured this out a while back. The hardest req to meet is of course Full Auto. The real question is not can you equip, but can you maintain enough skills so that you are not over-equipped.

The reqs for a Q200 Heavy Suppressor are:

833 Assault Rifle
833 Full Auto
695 Burst
556 SMG
463 Fling Shot

http://auno.org/aodb.php?t=ag&cmd=view&id=160140

SMG and Fling Shot are no problem. If you maxed all abilities and maxed these skills to cap, you are at ~550 at the title 6 skills cap. The wrangle you would need to boost Assault Rifle would automatically raise SMG above the limit.

Burst can be raised to around 550 as well. Riot Control gives another +110 (EDITED). General Expertise gives +20. General ability boosts give another +3.

So, you are at 683.

Then, you construct this implant.

-------------------------------
Right Hand - q200
Bright Warfare Cluster (+63 Assault Rifle)
Faded Bursting Cluster (+42 Burst)
-------------------------------

So, now Burst is at 725. Bingo, blew this req away, now move to next reqs.

ASSAULT RIFLE and FULL AUTO.

Assuming both are at 550 at title 6. Let's take care of the Assault Rifle req first.

Along with the Right Hand above, you should construct a

---------------------------------
Eye Implant q200
Shining Long Range Weaponry Cluster (+105 Rifle)
Faded Warfare Cluster (+42 to Assault Rifle)

Left Wrist q200
Faded Long Range Weaponry Cluster (+42 Rifle)
---------------------------------

I'll explain the Rifle clusters later.

BOOSTING ASSAULT RIFLE

550 Assault Rifle Base score
+132 Wrangle
+63 Bright Warfare Cluster in R. Hand q200
+42 Faded Warfare Cluster in Eye q200
+60 Assault Rifle Mastery (Soldier Buff) (EDITED)
+20 General Expertise
+3 General Ability Boosts

So, right now you are at 870. Construct these implants:

-------------------------------
Leg Implant - q200
Shining Nimbleness Cluster (+55 Agility)
Bright Endurance Cluster (+33 Stamina)

Chest Implant - q200
Shining Endurance Cluster (+55 Stamina)
Faded Strength Cluster (+22 Strength)

Left Arm Implant - q200
Bright Strength Cluster (+33 Strength)

Feet Implant - q200
Bright Nimbleness Cluster (+33 Agility)

Head Implant - q200
Shining Surgery Cluster (+105 Treatment)
Faded Empathy Cluster (+22 Sense)

Waist Implant - q200
Bright Empathy Cluster (+33 Sense)
Faded Unloading Cluster (+42 Full Auto)

--------------------------------

These 6 ability enhancing implants will give you an additional 19-20 points to Assault Rifle (and Burst). Naturally, you are using the q200 Surgery cluster in the head to equip all these other q200 implants.

So, now you are at 890 Assault Rifle (without even the benefit of the Shining Warfare cluster). Blew this req away. Next...

The Full Auto req...833...how do we do that?

BOOSTING FULL AUTO

Construct these additional implants

---------------------------------
Right Arm q200
Shining Unloading Cluster (+105 to Full Auto)

Right Wrist q200
Bright Unloading Cluster (+63 to Full Auto)
---------------------------------

You already have a Waist implant with the Faded Unloading Cluster in it.

550 Full Auto base score
+105 Shining Unloading Cluster
+63 Bright Unloading Cluster
+42 Faded Unloading Cluster
+20 General Expertise
+3 General ability boosts

We're at 783. The ability implants add 17 points or so. So we're at 800.

Get the following buffs.

Iron Circle (Doctor +5 to Full Auto)
Essence of Behometh (Enforcer +7 to Full Auto)
Prodigious Strength (Enforcer +6 to Full Auto)

At 818.

oh no 15 measly points away...can we do it...no...

...Wait! Yes, we can.

Introducing the Galahad Police Phoenix Rifle:
http://auno.org/aodb.php?t=ag&cmd=view&id=124037

Notice the +30 Full Auto buff on that weapon. Now you know why I had you put all those Rifle clusters in those implants.

You are at 848 Full Auto. Bingo! Slap that q200 Custom Heavy Suppressor on.

But...

First, you need to equip that Galahad Police Phoenix Rifle and do it with no IP expenditure. ;(

Galahad Inc. 055 Police Phoenix
579 Rifle
579 Full Auto
387 Shotgun
323 Aimed Shot

The Full Auto req is laughable if you've maxed Full Auto and are wearing just the Faded q200 Unloading implant listed above.

The Aimed Shot req is laughable. If you've maxed all ability scores your Aimed Shot should already be at 120 or so. General Expertise +20, General Boosts +3, Take the Shot (Agent) +130. Max out Perception (which you need to do anyway for tons of reasons) and wear one of those new targeting scopes, another +100 Aimed Shot at least.

Shotgun is already 120 from maxed ability scores. Temporarily switch in a q200 Shining Gunning Right Arm implant and q200 Bright Gunning Right Hand implant. (you don't need all the Full Auto implants to equip this gun, just the faded Unloading cluster). So thats +168 from implants, +132 from wrangle, +20 from General Expertise, +3 from General ability boosts. Total of 443 Shotgun. 3 reqs taken care of and we haven't even spent any IP.

579 Rifle Req...whew...how can you get that without spending any IP?

Temporarily use a Right Wrist Implant for the Bright Long Range Weaponry cluster +63

120 from maxed ability scores
+132 Wrangle
+210 from Implants
+110 Unexpected Attack (Agent)
+20 General Expertise
+3 General ability boosts
+18-19 from the 6 ability score boosting implants

614 Rifle Woot!

(Remember, we're only equipping this Rifle for its +30 to Full Auto bonus.)

We've equipped a q151 Rifle and haven't even spent any IP on the main weapon skills. Switch out the shotgun and bright rifle implants for the Unloading and Warfare implants and then...

...Custom Heavy Suppressor here we come.
