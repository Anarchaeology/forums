Polymorphs in Shadow Lands
How about re-skinning the adventure polymorphs, when in shadow lands, to reflect faction rating? I think this kind of change would not be all that hard to do. You could have say 3 to 5 different skins ranging between faction rating level 1 to max. As you increase in faction rating for the redeemed or unredeemed your look would become more like the shadow lands version of the monster. This is just a suggestion for a visual effect that would be cool. Perhaps at the max level the redeemed parrot would emit the white smoke and unredeemed would release black smoke. Any other ideas suggestions in this vain please feel free to deviate.

Note: a bit of cleanup and clarification here after talking a bit on testlive
-Skins are just used to make the models look like something
-Models are what the skins cover, they are used for the animations, dimensions etc.
Due to lack on knowledge of how Funcom does its models and skins this suggestion may be easy or hard. It _looks_ like they are simply reskined models but I may be wrong. If it is simply skins than this should be easy to do, else it may be a bit more of a challenge and probably not worth dev time.
