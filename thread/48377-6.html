What I find amusing is how many 'experts' post in this thread asking for trader nerfs.

I'm afraid having a level 3 trader does not make you an expert. I'm afraid having a billion characters, but none over level 160 does not make you an expert.

Traders have drains, roots, and mez.

Funnels are basically worthless. They require insane nano, and heal for a piddly amount. I'd compare them with using a first aid stim. I don't think too many people consider that 'healing.'

Single heals are fine for a team. They're not great, but fine. However, they are worthless for soloing.

Team heals are fine for any team with secondary or primary healers other then the trader. An mp paired with a trader makes a great doc replacement. These were nerfed yes. But it was one nerf I actually felt was right. Yes, this is the only point I agree with the nerf mongers on.

Tradeskills are rather dubious since tradeskills are basically halfased implemented. I think it's great that there are a few people who care about tradeskills in this game. I think it's rediculous to consider them in any prof comparison seeing as the only way to advance is through combat. Not shift right clicking.

Ac drains. They're ok. Maybe even worthy of joining roots/mez/drains as a trader's useful tools.

Charms. Um yes. Do I have to explain how pathetic they are?

So lets see. Sure. Nerf drains. Trader then becomes even worse then an engi with no teamability and no solobility because their other nano lines are quite worthless for pvm.

Ok, so the crybabymartyrsyndrom kiddies get their way. Without drains a trader can hit as hard as..a doc. They can't heal. They can use a crap mez untill they hit 150+. They can use a crap root untill they hit 150+.

As for pvp. Not to burst any bubbles, but finding someone to duel you in tir/ent/wine isn't pvp. It's dueling. Frankly, I wish they'd lose the pvp titles for the arenas and make 25% much more common. It just miiiiight force people to venture forth out of ent/tir/wine.

But oh wait. You're all 'experts'. You've all 'played' a trader or have characters that figured out how to make it out of the backyard. I'm sorry. I'll rejoin the other 99.2% of the traders who don't bother posting here because it's become the nt/soldier whineboard.

Oh wait, I have a few questions. If you think traders are gods and being an nt/soldier sucks so bad, why do you play an nt/soldier? Is it the martyr complex where you just have to do something so you can cry about it? I'm really curious what motivates you people to keep playing a prof that you spend all your time crying about being the suck in every prof board you can find and then graduate to asking for other profs to be nerfed.

My second question is why do you duel traders? If you think they're so godly and that you don't stand a chance, the simple answer to me would be not to duel them. Of course I think you're slightly addled in the head anyways if you think dueling is pvp. But there's no accounting for life these days.

Oh, and here's a suggestion. Just a tiny one mind you. While it might be easier to ***** and moan and cry cry cry, I've been told by a little bird that looking for fixes to YOUR prof can be quite therapeutic.

*yawn*
