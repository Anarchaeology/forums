World changing events
I do not think that the travel system is that bad, it makes it nice to get to where you want to go quickly. Problem is that everyone else is there too. Expend the little cities that are out in the middle of nowhere, give people a reason to want to go there. Like some small business just built a new nano, gun, armor, or item, and the only place to get it is from some little shack in some town in the middle of nowhere. It is that there is no reason to travel to these places just to find nothing there. Traveling to places through areas that lvl 100+ people have trouble with and you get to a town that sells basic armor and guns? how does this make sense? But don't take away what is already there, just give us a reason to want to go there. And even add new ways to get somewhere, Whompas are expensive and Grid terms must be hard to get, seeing so little of them, that the underground subway needs to "re-open".

But what I think (and a lot of others that I know came from AC) is needed are world changing events. There was a story about an apartment building that got destroyed. So...why can't a whompa get sabotaged and ether get destroyed, or maybe even re-route you to a clan or Omni city until the maintance crew gets it fixed? I hate making references to other games, but in AC there were towns that got destroyed, not just an attack on a town and everything is ok now, but the town got destroyed, there was nothing left but a crater. Others got taken over for a while by one race of MOB's. Things like this made the story line more interesting, not more fun for everyone, but for most of the people, they liked those kinds of things. It made them move around and say, now where do I go?

Also, yes, there should be things in the sky that you have to watch out for, but I do not want them to just appear, put some kind of story behind them, and start some really big and hard hitting things out in the zones that seem to be ignored and ahve them spread out into the other zones gradualy like a population increase forces people to do. Or even some kind of air defences. I have gone through athens many times and I just point my yalm into the sky and fly away from all the danger. With the new planes coming into that have some fire power, it would be nice to have to try to avoid some turrets or some guards on a tower ready to shoot at anyone who is not suposed to be there.

Just some thoughts...

Arrrgg. I just got done with another thread on this topic. I'm all for touger travel.

Summary of my thoughts in that thread

  • Travel needs to be made harder
    1. Yalms should only be good for traveling from one general area to another general area
    2. Ground vehicles should be the best for going to an exact location.
      Think of it like this. You take an airplane to go from New York to Los Angles. Once you get there, you rent a car to go to your moms house.
    3. Weather can affect air travel
    4. Anti aircraft guns can enforce a no fly zone. The no fly zones can be controlled by Omni, Clan or NPCs and can dynamically change based on players actions.
    5. More flying mobs need to be added
    6. More snaring/rooting mobs in wild
  • The time to complete a normal mission needs to stay the same or go down
    1. Dungeons need to be smaller
    2. Closer missions need to be given out
  • Bonuses need to be given to players that take on tougher challenges
    1. A bonus/penalty value should be kept for each playfield in the game that will affect loot and experience. The more players hunt in an area, the more negative the value will be while the fewer players that hunt in a playfield, the more the bonus will grow.
    2. Separate values should be kept for each side in PvP zones. So for example, a Clan will get huge bonuses for hunting in a zone that is Omni controlled.
    3. The modifier should also apply to dungeons in the playfield
