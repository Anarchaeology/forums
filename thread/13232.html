Generally yes.

When you fight you will see the attack bar move up and down, the time it takes to go up (to the right) is the attack time, the other way around is the recharge time. The attack speed can be lowered by raising your inits (ranged init for guns, melee init for melee weps, phys. init for unarmed combat and nano init for nano programs).

Also, the agg/def slider affects the attack speed. AFAIK at full aggro it lowers your attack time by 1 sec, at full def it raises it by 1 sec.

The cap at attack speeds is 1 s (both attack and recharge).

So basically the added time of atk time and recharge time, influenced by your inits and the agg/def slider is the final time it takes between 2 shots.
