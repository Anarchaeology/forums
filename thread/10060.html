Low lv MP $$$
I am playing a low lv MP, currently 34, and have found out that hunting mobs in Lush Fields first at the pipes and later on at Mutant Domain is fairly easy.

The mobs drop loads of loot, items and monster parts.
For MPs the Pharma Tech is cheap so converting monster parts to blood plasma adds to the loot reward significantly.
Ok you have to invest in a Bio Communicator, but it pays back within an hour of gaming.

When you level up and mobs get gray just change hunt area to harder ones. In Lush there are hunting grounds all the way up higher levels. I suppose that at NW caves you could hunt even at lv 80 and the mobs wont be gray.

But then again if you wanna have cash, why not hunt greys that can easily be taken out and your char wont level. Wich keeps you from needing to invest in new items just to keep up with levelling.

I have "some" money... :)
I started playing this game two weeks ago (love it!).

My first char I tossed after a day (learning the ropes).

My second char, an Omni Fixer, is what I've been playing. In the two weeks (minus one day, see above), I have made it to level 25.

I don't have high level friends to twink me. (Yes, I have been given the occasional hand-me-down by helpful higher levels; and one even built a low-level chem mausser for me [Thanks!] - but I don't have anyone handing me millions of credits and casting big buffs on me)

I have gone team hunting only once. Team hunting appears to allow you to rapidly advance in levels (power-leveling).

I don't try to power level. Why? Because I think you then advance faster than you can financially keep up. The team I went hunting with says they hunt all the time. They were basically broke - needing to spend all their bounty on better armor and weaps in order to stay ahead of their rapidly advancing levels.

I have instead taken to doing missions. I set the sliders a particular way that suits me, and then I "shop" for a decent mission. I go to a certain place for my missions. That's because I can get nicely rewarded missions there (higher paying). Sometimes I get one right away. But there have been times where I've shopped for seemingly forever (maybe 20 or so "requests" of a mission) before one came up that looked good. I'm patient.

I try to get two or three missions in the same area.

These days at level 25 a decent mission is typically in Lush Fields and pays around 11K. That's with the difficulty slider about 25% towards half-way from the middle. That misson will typically have some oranges and reds in it. Yes, I fight the reds.

All my armor (except a few occasional hand me downs here and there, see above) has been from missions. And I've been finding very decent armor. Better armor is one of the main things I shop for as a mission reward. A few times, in the mission, I find better armor as a treasure in a chest than I will obtain as the reward.

I also get plenty of exp from killing *all* the mobs. And those same mobs have plenty of credits.

So in a typical mission I end up gaining plenty of exp, and maybe double or triple the already decent mission reward. These days I might make 20K - 35K per mission.

Couple all that with not needing to constantly spend credits on improved armor, and I have probably made well over 200K cr's since I started. I've spent some of that on goodies (such as the nice 3-deck belt I just bought, or the 20-buff MG and Burst nanos).

I also purchase most of my weaps. I've even purchased three weaps that I need to "gro into". The first of those I am using right now. But weapons purchases are not expensive at this point of my game.

So right now I have just about 150K cr's "in the bank". I'm not rich, but I feel "comfortable". Meanwhile, it seems those out hunting and power-leveling stay nearly broke (unless they have twinkie friends )

So if you are not making money, you might want to try a different approach.

Cheers,
Martek

p.s. As a side note - I also try to save IP's. I have over 50K IP's unspent so far. That has not held me back from improving important skills nor prevented me from taking a bit harder missions. I'm sure these saved IP points will come in handy later when I gain certain key items (nanos, etc.) that need certain skills. I'll be able to bump my skills right up at that time.
