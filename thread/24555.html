Grid Diplomacy
Meeting inside the grid itself works fine, actually.

But if one of the two parties has comp lit 101 and the other doesn't, here's something else that works:

1) Omni character with comp lit 76 or above - grid into Tir. The Tir grid point does not, yet, have guards on it.

2) Clan character with comp lit 101 or above - up to level 2, grid into Omni-Ent. Omni-Ent has two grid terminals. Everybody exits the grid at the southern one, which has no guards.

I'm more sure of #2 than #1. Tir gridpoint plaza is one of the most crowded, most busy places on the planet. There could easily be a guard buried somewhere in that crowd and I wouldn't necessarily notice.
