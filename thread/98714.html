I've rolled up Konstas in the first days AO's release, which makes me one of the oldest (if not the oldest) active Atrox doctors in AO. I love this character and it is the only one I play, since none of my alts made it past level 20, but there are days when I wish that I made him a Nanomage or Solitus. Realistically speaking, Atrox doctors work, but compared to their Nano and Solitus counterparts they come up lacking.

The first major drawback is a considerably smaller nanopool. With the nanopool I have (around 5k) I rarely encounter situations where I run out of nano, but in those rare instances a couple of extra thousand nano would come in handy. In retrospect, most Nano and Solitus doctors of my level have about the same number of hit points because while I'm implanting to increase my nanopool, they implant to increase their health, thus cutting down the small difference in hit points all the while retaining superior number of nano points.

The second drawback is less nano skill points due to trickle down. At breed attribute cap both Nano and Solitus have several levels' worth more skill points than we do. Add into the equation Nano-only special items such as Prof. Jones Vest and Shades of Luc, and Nanomages come out way ahead. The Notum Wars booster pack has not rendered this point completely moot like some would have you believe. Surely with towers and organization bonuses provided by Redemption I can self-cast pretty much anything, BUT not everyone is a member of an "uber" guild with QL 250 bases. Moreover, towers get destroyed and org contracts are buggy.

Quote:
Originally posted by ManiacMD
Bah, you get a major advantage, been wearing Kick Pistol since lvl117 and luvin every minute of it.
As far as ManiacMD's point about being able to use Diamondine Kick pistols is an advantage, it really isn't. At most, it is a perk (thrown to all Atrox like a bare bone to shut some of us up). Pain of Patricia is a qualitatively better pistol that can be used by anyone. It is slightly harder to get, but it is not like anyone can go and kill Diamondine Soldier on his or her own. Having said that, even these pistols are not exactly the best weapons for a doctor to use outside of PvM. Implant wise they conflict with such important clusters as bright/faded MC/TS and a shining nano regeneration cluster, which no doctor (especially Atrox) should neglect. In NW battles they are slow and lack range, which means that in addition to raising pistol/shotgun/fling/burst/(multi ranged), you would also have to raise weapon smithing and perception in order to be able to self-equip a Yatamuchy Movement Predictor as well as rifle/aimed-shot for the optional TIM scope.

Keep your trox doc. The secret here is to build on the skills the trox is already good at. In other words, implant to increase your attack damage and your hit points. So you miss out on a team heal or so, who cares? No doubt there are already nanomage and solitus docs in your org, so let them worry about being a true doctor. Focus on having the most HP instead of nano points and your ability to augment that advantage by healing yourself will help you drop anyone who stands in your way.

In every MMORPG that I've played, the issue always boils down to how many HP you have and how well you can heal. An atrox doctor has both abilities, perhaps not the best of both, but both are there. Don't think like those other wimpy doctors--be an atrox.
