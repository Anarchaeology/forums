Controller needs to warp whole teams....
We (Tradeskillz) came under attack last night while i was about to start a BS mission. Obviously i stated i needed to go to my Tower and fight off the attack, and my team we very understanding. They offered to come and help, which is great, and then i contacted some of my friends, who also asked if i needed their help.

The answer was a big YES all round. This is where the system started to break down in my opinion. I am able to grid straight to my controller, but the best i could do for the group of people who offered their help was to give co-ords in a zone and hope for the best.

I suggest that in times of trouble, the Exit to Controller should give you the following choices:-

1. Exit to any one of your orgs tower location.
2. Exit to the location of any of the towers controlled by your team leaders org.

This would mean that when a team members tower came under attack the team of mixed guilds can make him leader, and follow him to the point of contact via the grid.

It would also give you the chance to form defense forces at Tir grid for example, and facilitate easy transport to the threatened zone.

Just a thought.
