So at what level does a soldier feel gimped?
I have been playing since August 2001 and my first char was a Soldier...which I just recently reborn as a Solitus Soldier.

Now granted I am only level 12 but so far have found the Soldier to do very good at soloing known red mobs. I have even done a bit a PvP and am undefeated.
