MP's aren't uber in PvP. You'd think there would be a ton of apprentices by now if that were the case.

I don't even remember the last time my demon managed to land a hit on a class that can root and that includes the 'crat class who is supposedly so much weaker than us as well as all those "gimpy" NT's.

As far as Nano Shutdown is concerned it gets resisted a bit too often for my taste. Max your nano resistence and you'll see. Besides, in the 11-12 seconds it takes for me to attack and recharge with Nano Shutdown, Id rather unleash my specials, nuke you 3-4 times, shoot you once normally, and let my demon hit you twice (assuming its not rooted or zoned away after some fleeing soldier/fixer/etc.). Most of the fights I win are due to me using skills that really dont have much to do with my class: mainly my weapon. Unless Im fighting a healer, I wouldnt waste 11-12 seconds with Nano Shutdown. Nano Shutdown is for MP's with nothing better do to.

As far as heal pets are concerned. They can be rooted and are also on a long recharge cycle. They don't heal nearly as fast or as much as people like to complain about.

We aren't gods, enough classes have abilities that aim directly at our weaknesses: particularly those with an area slow or area root.

NT's are relatively easy for me if they dont have a good weapon with specials on it, but if I kill them, it probably had little to do with my pets (which are neutralized in 2-3 seconds) or debuffs (which take too long to cast) and more to do with the fact I can unleash an alpha strike on them while nuking them.

If NT's who use pillows or pistols want an even fight, they should go find an MP using those silly creation weapons (a clever way of Funcom from diverting MP's from truly worthy weapons and keeping their damage output in check).

I fear soldiers, traders, and any pet/nuking/healing class with a ranged weapon that has 2-4 specials on it. That pretty much covers everyone with a character designed to PvP well. Rooting classes can neutralize me easily enough, not necessarily kill me. I don't have too much experience fighting melee based characters though. I dont see them too often in political zones.
