Re: Oh no!!!
To start, lets get this out of the way: I can overequip. I certainly know how. I've got good, appropriate skill implants. I generally don't equip more than I can self-buff for because I don't believe in the need for it. Only a broken game mechanic would make something like that necessary.


quote:
I personally don't like the idea of changing the "over-equipping" abilities. Let's face it, there are some profession/breed combos that are very difficult to solo with, especially at low levels.

Is there some limitation in the reading comprehension abilities of the AO populace? Does everyone come directly to the boards after they read about the overequipping reduction without reading the part where - please god finally - mobs wouldn't have 3x the HP of players anymore? I suppose this doesn't affect anyone's solo capabilities?

So quick everyone is to cite their characters' solo abilities (or lack thereof) as a reference to why overequipping is fine the way it is, when in fact it's the prime example of why it needs to be changed.


Important news flash: Not everyone overequips! Maybe half of you do. Those that do, and are complaining, are telling us that:

An X level player with X level gear should never be able to kill X level mob.

I ask, what's wrong with that? Why can't a level 75 char with level 75 gear have at least a tiny chance against a level 75 mob?

With implants and/or self buffs, and maxing your skill, you'd still probably be able to equip QL90 or above items. If monsters were changed in such a way that you could kill a level 100 mob as a 100 char with 100 gear, why does this strike such an ill tone amongst you people?

I saw the proposal amidst a lot of waning interest in AO and thought to myself, 'Thank god, something that can save AO's gameplay,' and came to the boards and posted with optimism.. now, from what I see of this message thread, I see that the ignorant shout down what they don't seem to grasp (since nobody ever seems to notice that monster changes would make things very different and you wouldn't NEED to overequip) and doom us all to think about what could have been. How typical to succumb to the weakening fear of what may happen, when they're comfortable with what is.

Some of you see this as taking out some of the game. I see it as putting back 1/3 of the game. All those levels that you were wearing level 200 gear but probably shouldn't have been. Or the ones where everyone around you had level 6x gear on at level 20 and you had to scrape by on what you could get from missions.
There'll still be twinks. There'll still be overequipping. But why can't you people let it be an aspect of the game instead of the focus of it?

That's all there is to say. Time to go shiver in the corner holding onto some tiny hope that the only MMORPG I find vaguely tolerable isn't ruined by the unthinking masses (again.)

P.S. In case it wasn't obvious, I've already given up hope. I've yet to see an online game team make a hard decision that's the right one.
