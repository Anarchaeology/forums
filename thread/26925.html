Elected Officials in each AO Town?
Well, eventually I knew I would post on here but I did not know when. So after playing AO for almost a year now, and being a casual player (I am only a level 32 Agent) I think that having elected officials within each of the respective cities and towns could help bring along the story and the interest in the game.

By having elections, and electing someone to represent the people of the community for each town will help move the story. The people will be in a sense the middle men between the players, and the story. In doing this make one the prereqs being a Crat. Furthermore, you need to obtain electronic signatures and a certain level to be elected.

This will definately not fix the big problems, but it will add an element that is lacking here.

Well as I am stuck up in NH without my computer and only my laptop, I thought I would go into more detail regarding the idea of elected officials within the world of Anarchy Online. I definately agree with Miss Maul too about the player interaction would rise.

As for what XED LRK's comments about OT being not the sort of company of to allow democratic elections, I would dissagree with you on that. In every company there are elections for different positions. And if there were not elected mayors and govenors, there could be people who are game players promoted to those positions for their achievement within the game.

Below however, is a sort of model for this idea that I have proposed:

Elected Officials within Omni Tek:

Requirements:
Level 40 Crat.
150 signatures (electronic)
5 member guild (to provide support for the mayor)

Each term would last for 6 months, and in being mayor for a specific town, you would receive weekly updates from Radiman (Clan) and Ross (OT). Each mayor would be able to establish office hours for their constituancy and so forth. The mayors can have guards and what not since political figures are always targets, and these people will be able to help shape the destinary of AO. Also there would be fixed housing/offices for these people, along with taxes that each citizen pays in credits a month.

While this is an ambitious idea, I think a lot of the problems that we see with the lack of player interaction would improve. It will provide an chance for the game players to interact with the story line, and provide a more fluid like society for AO.

Cosmik,

I am glad that you responded on behalf of Anarchy Online. Obviously this idea of having mayors and regional managers/govenors would take a lot work behind it. I find it interestiong though that you mention a player who has "self-appointed" himself as mayor. So already I see a demand in a sense of this.

Being mayor would not just only be a title. I would like to see special clothing representing both factions, guards provided by each respective organization, and so on. Also in order to become an elected official, you must work within the organization. So lets say the mayor of Tir remains the "Mayor Of Tir". He or she would be required to meet once a month with Radiman, and help stratigic planning (like in a real society). Also, the people can do missions specially tailored to that position. Whether it is a diplomatic mission, or what have you. Ultimately, this will give the players something tangible to see instead of doing the randomly generated missions. If the ultimate goal of Omni Tek is bring down the clans, have missions where the mayors once have assignments to try to organize major groups to combat, steal, or what have you.

As for people holding elections, make the position that is available for a period of time (whether it is 6 months) where there is a chance of promotion too. Have the elections through an electronic ballot that can be picked up at stores for a nominal price or for free. When these officials are elected, have major characters show up for the post party. make it something that really shapes the environment.

Ultimately, if things do work out, you would have a functioning organization that is taking part of the game and really interacting with the story line. This would only be the beginning too, if Funcom could, would, or is willing to take this game to the next level, they need to really develop the inner workings of all three factions. Make it so people can advance through the ranks of Omni Tek... Have soldiers enlist into an army put together on Omni Trade. The possibilities are endless, but the effort and the leg work needs to be done.

Once again Cosmik, thank you for your vested intrest in this topic, and I am sure that this idea can work. If you have any questions please do not hesititate to email me through the profile page.
