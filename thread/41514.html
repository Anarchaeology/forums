Currently there are 2 kinds of scopes:

1) Old school ("(Extreme)Low light targetting scope")

These use EE/WS to equip, give a boost to Crit chance (max 15%), aimed shot and weapon range, with the drawback of lowering inits (-800 for the ql 200)

These were "nerfed" in terms of they simply dont drop anymore.

2) New scopes ("Targeting Scope - Vision Enhancer")

These only use perception to equip, also give a boost to the Crit chance with the max being 15% also (ql 400). The thing is the highest ql i have seen in game is around 250, which adds 7% to your crit chance. Also adds to aimed shot and increases weapon range, while lowering inits.

Since the highest ql of the new scopes in the game curently is 250 (correct me if I'm wrong), the max crit increase is 7%. So in case you are a veteran player who got his ql 200 old school ellts on, you got a high advantage over others. It really makes a difference if you crit every 2nd/3rd shot or once a fight (even more in PvP).
