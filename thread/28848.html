Crat suit not giving XP bonus
All,

Okay, I've focused on XP bonus since before the term 'XP crat' was termed. But I've always wondered, giving the bugs in the game, whether they were working. This weekend I was finally able to test it and the good news is that they almost all DO work.

I teamed with a friend of mine who was exactly the same level as I was. We did a green mission and I recorded the XP gains. We did kills where he had a 9% and I a 21%; he had a 9% and I a 10%; and he had a 3% and I a 21%.

The 9/10 numbers were perfect. But in the 2/21 and 3/21 cases I only got a 20% instead of a 21% bonus. My bonuses consisted of:

3% Rt. MTI B-94
3% Lt. MTI B-94
4% Clan Merit Badge
1% Crat suit
4% IQ Ring
6% Baton
---
21%

The most obvious answer is that the crat suit isn't giving its 1%. Since I am only short 1%, it isn't the B-94s. His 9% was coming from Baton, a +2% ring, and a +1% badge. So we know Baton isn't the culprit. While theoretically possible that my badge or ring was giving less than it was supposed to, I find is MUCH more likely that the suit was giving none at all.

The very good news, however, is that everything else is working right.
