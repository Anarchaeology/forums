Cyborg Assault
((This is my first attempt at AO writing, so any constructive critisiscm would be appreciated. I hope you all enjoy my meager offering! ))

Warning klaxons blared. Half asleep troopers stumbled out of their bunks, rushing to put on their standard issue armor. A sergeant bursts in, swearing at the top of his lungs at the soldiers.

If you dont get yer yeller asss outside in the next 5 minutes, yer all gonna be the main course for a bunch of stinkin cyborgs!

The troopers scramble to the armory, where they are handed assault rifles, grenade launchers, and pistols. Outside on the drill field the soldiers, boys and men, line up and await their orders. Its a sunny day, with a clear blue sky. If not for the gunfire ripping through the air, it would be a beautiful day.

An especially large atrox in Omni-Armed Forces armor and wielding a Hellspinner Shock Cannon walks to the front of the formation.

Men! Women! Atrox! We stand here today to repel one of the greatest threats to man kindthe cyborgs! These half men, half machines have the audacity to strike at Omni-Tekthey have made a grievous error! Here we will fight them backhere will we prove that true flesh is better than any machine! Fight, soldiers, for humanity!

The gathered soldiers let out a cheer, then rushed to take defensive positions. A deathly silence falls as the last of the forward defense positions falls to the cyborg onslaught.

And then they came.

First one or two tentative pushes, but then the full brunt of the cyborg force charged the Omni-Pol outpost, like waves against a breaker. Energy and bullets streaked back and forth, filling the air with death. Medics rushed from soldier to soldier, desperately trying to heal as many as they could. But even with these efforts, many were being sent to the reclaim.

Theres too many! One soldier shouts in fear.

Keep fighting! Well fight them until theres no one left standing! the commander yells. As he yells, he lets loose a burst of violent energy at a Cyborg Brigadier General. The borg spins from the impact, a pirouette of death, and falls to the ground, a black liquid seeping out from beneath its armor.

The fight is fast and furious, but the odds are against the warriors of Omni-Tek. Many begin to echo the despair of the first trooper. Then, like guardian angels, troop transports come swooping down from the sky, dispersing their deadly cargo behind the cyborg force. Armed Forces shock troopers rush from the bellies of the transports, and charge the rear of the enemy. The cyborgs, taken by surprise, try to fight the new force, but the tables have been turned, and the cyborgs, trapped between to walls of death, are slaughtered in minutes. A few borgs run, and the shock troopers give chase. Through the woods they run, until they come to an opening. Here, they stop dead. The cyborgs are no where to be seen, but in front of them stands a giant ring, with a blue vortex spinning in the center.

Sir, the squad commander calls over the comm.
Go, squad commander.
The cyborgs are gone, but weve found what appears to be a portal. Your orders?
Destroy it.

The commander turns to his men and nods. The portal is engulfed in fire, until it finally explodes in a blinding blue flash.

Back at the outpost, the cyborg corpses are stripped for valuables, and troopers stagger back to base from the reclaim. Flesh had won over machine again. The questions waswould they win again?

A knock at the door.
Enter.
The lieutenant opens the door, walks in, and comes to attention.
Lieutenant Janisko reporting as ordered, sir. The colonel nods to the chair in front of his desk. In response, Janisko closes the door behind him, and takes his seat. The colonel shuffles some papers on his desk, and then finally looks up.
Do you know why youre hear, lieutenant?
I do not presume to read the commanders thoughts, sir, Janisko replies stiffly.
Dont give me that bull. Why do you think youre here?
The portal, sir. You want to learn about the portal. The colonel leans back in his seat, staring calmly at the nervous lieutenant.
Thats right. So, tell me what you saw.
I saw a circle, sir. A black circle, with this blue spinning thing in the center. It was big, and hummed slightly. It was in a wood clearing, and there was no sign of the cyborgs we had been chasing, although that was where they led us.
I see, the colonel says, rubbing his chin in thought. Unconsciously speaking aloud, the colonel muses I wonder where it came from
Sir, Janisko says, it looked to me like what those Jobe people made. The colonel freezes, and slowly turns his head to look at Janisko.
Really?Tell me, lieutenant, have you been to the Shadowlands? Janisko shakes his head no.
But surely youve seen the grid reports.
Yes, sir. Thats why I think it looks like the Jobe stuffthe thing I saw looked like what
I saw in the grid news. The colonel nods, and dismisses the lieutenant, who practically runs out of the office before the colonel could call him back.
So, it's as we expected, Colonel Harold Maloy thinks to himself. The cyborgs have truly out done themselves this time. Shadow tech? Amazing. The question iswhats their plan? All theyve gone for is insignificant outpostswhat are they after? Maloy gets up and walks to the window behind his desk. He gazes out over the Rome cityscape, which always takes his breath away. If the cyborgs ultimately want this, theyre going to be sorely disappointed.
The colonel walks to the desk, picks up his cloak, and strides out of the office. His two body guards take up familiar flanking positions, as Maloy steps into the lift and descends to the world below.

A-boutface! For-wardmarch!

The double column of soldiers began their march. They marched

through Rome Red, where the gothic sky scrapers glared down

at the soldiers from their towering height. They marched through

Omni-1 Entertainment, where bystanders paused their hurried

lives to stand and point at the soldiers, whispering to their

neighbors. Where are they going? What are they doing?

Lastly, the soldiers marched into Outpost 20k. Here the soldiers

halted. Here they quickly had a last bite to eat. Here they took a

last piss in the bushes. And here, they waited. A speech was

given, a war-cry was yelled, and the soldiers were on the move

again.

Out the gates. Down the road. Slowly, they marched towards

their destiny. The veterans were grim, familiar to the

nervousness before battle. The younger, greener recruits

whispered among themselves, sharing their fears and praying to

whatever god they could think of.

Stomp, stomp, stomp. The march of boots on perma-crete. Step

by step, taking their owners to either a life of glory, or a simple

death. All the soldiers knew what they were going to face. They

knew the chances of survival. But they kept marching forward.

Stomp, stomp, stomp. A cadence is started, and soon all the

soldiers take it up. Together we march, side by side, step by

step. Together we march, brothers in arms, united till death.

Together we march, to the end, to the end.

A halt is called. A shudder runs through the column as the news

is passed down. Theyre over that bridge! Were almost there!

This is it! Last minute orders are given, and then the soldiers

make the final leg of their journey.

As they cross the bridge, the valiant Omni-Tek warriors are met

by a withering hail of fire as turrets pump out energy. Men fall left

and right, but they push on. As they reach the outpost walls,

cyborgs begin to spill out the main gate. Laser blasts, bullets,

and explosions fly every which way. Any previous battle lines

disintegrate into one big melee as cyborgs and human breeds

square off.

The cyborgs gain the upper hand, with their turret defenses, and

superior combat abilities. But soon, the turrets fall, and the

cyborgs are pushed back. The human breeds cheer in triumph as

the last cyborgs fall. But their cheers are soon cut off by the sight

of a new wave of cyborgs pouring from the captured outpost.

And so battle resumes. Another wave and another wave come to

fight the Omni-Tek soldiers, but each falls to the might of

humanity.

Suddenly, an unnatural peace falls over the battle field. Dead

and dying moan quietly as they writhe on the ground, and the

still living move amongst the bodies, helping those they can, and

finishing the misery for others. The cyborgs have once again

been defeated. The portal has been destroyed. A sense of peace

falls over the soldiers, as they realize theyve won. Again the

unstoppable might of Omni-Tek has vanquished evil. Again justice

had been done.
