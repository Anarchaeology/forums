Axis....of evil!
I am addressing everyone hereClan, Omni and Neutral and warning them not to trust the Clan organisation known as Axis.

As part of a initiative to try and ease current relations between the Clan and Neutral populations ourselves, and other Neutral organisations have been socialising, and working more closely with the Clans in ways to show that in reality there is no enemy between the two. Ourselves, the IR have done various missions with members and advisors of Axis, and other Clan organisations (and Omni), and also been in groups to the Inner Sanctum and various hunts to the outer reaches of Rubi-ka. And there have even been plans for parties and even a Clan v Neut game of footleet. Many were feeling that the hostilities between the Neuts and the Clans were finally easing.

Unfortunately on Thursday a force of young members of the Axis Levitations, backed back members of Axis assaulted the IR towers. The same people who have been working with them for weeks before with no explanation why. And with communication with the same advisors and leaders whom was teamed with co-operatively only days before ignoring communications. Thankfully some kind Omni citizens who saw the messages from the attacks came over to assist as did other Neutrals helped protect the area. Yet again, a day laterand still with no communications being answered they attack again. Such sad pitiful people when they cant even bother to answer people who considered them as friends days before other than with the words were gonna shove that fence your sitting on up your ass neut.
You really have to wonder about the education of these people.

We, and many other Neut organisations still do believe that it is possible for peace with the Clans, and simultaneously with Omni-Tek as well. There have been actions by other so called Neutral organisation who have attacked Clan and Omni, and those I would say can not be seen as Neutral due to this. But peace between, Neut and Clan, and Neut and Omni, and even Omni and Clan is obtainable. We all just need to get along together, and work together. And actions as have happened here are doing nothing to help the situation for anyone on Rubi-ka. Will the actions of these people, and Axis stop those who want unity and co-operation across all from working together? No and I, and others are still working with the Clans, and still respect them for what they are trying to achieve.

Many people look down upon ninja looters and kill stealers for being the lowest form of life on this planetit would now seem that even they, have something to look down upon. And we even warn the Clan or anyone else who see these people as a frienddont put your guard down with them, they will no doubt knife you in the back also.
