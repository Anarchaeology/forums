Quest Issues
Aside from the fact that I would like to see a number of new quests added, I see there are a number of NPCs that as of yet do nothing so I recognize the room for future expansion.

However I would like to see some new quest (low and mid level) that are in ONE sides controlled zones.

It is a real pain as a low level omni to try and complete some of the lower level quests. Since they are in part of not completely within clan and nuetral areas. As an example, the living cyber armor quest: This is a great quest, however by the time a OT character can do it safely he is either one of two things: 1) Too high of a level for it to matter or 2) has a Yalm, and thus has enough cr that he does not need the reward from the quest (as he can buy better) making it a pointless waste of time.

With the exception of the Alvin/Doge quest, I believe the above to true for every quest in the game and is true for this one as well once you advance to the 2nd stage.

On that alone: PLEASE set TK to spawn normaly. Waiting days for the possibility of a spawn is not fun (waited 4hrs before logging last stint only to find out that he still had not spawned 4hrs later when I relogged to wait yet another 2hrs and still no spawn).

It is hard enough to complete the quest when you have just about every character in the game attempting it (potentially since it is open to all sides). Thus you have everyone camping the same mob and competing for loot and tagging. Limiting the chance to spawn just adds to the camping. Camping does not equal fun.

So I do think the existing quests are a great addition and hope to see many many more. However I would like to see some that are side specific actually take place within that sides territory for the lower levels and the addition of some useful to mid level (50-150) characters as well as some high level 150+ only quests. And once again please do not repeat the camping that is required of TK.
