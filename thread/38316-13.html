This game doesn't need another nerf, especially to a profession that's just had one a couple of patches ago. Noer is also correct that people are reading a little too much into the descriptions. To demand that Engineers be better at tradeskills than Traders based on the blurb in the manual really isn't fair.

I've also stated my opposition to a balancing buff, mainly because I think it's impossible to get such things exactly correct. Whatever we do, one prof or the other will end up on top, be it ever so marginally, and the other prof will cry foul. The situation would be improved by closing the gap, but balance will never be perfect. Which profession you believe "deserves" to be in top position I suspect depends largely on which profession your main tradeskiller belongs to.

The problem for Engineers, as I see it, is a little less straightforward than inferiority in tradeskills. It can basically be summed up as having no skill, buff or property that other players really want. This is not strictly true at lower levels, before the Traders' bufflines have really started to kick in, but at the upper levels things seem to be different.
  • Engineers are not good in teams. The bot gets lost too often, and doesn't deal damage fast enough to tank properly.
  • We have no buffline or other nano that is in demand.
  • For top-flight tradeskill work, a Trader is a better bet.

None of this means that we're bad at tradeskills, merely that there's someone better. We solo adequately, if slowly, which prevents the profession from achieving gimp status. We don't PvP, but then that was never an advertised strong point for the class anyway.


I think what the profession needs is something, anything, that will make another player say "We need to find an Engineer". We also need to do this without stealing another profession's thunder, whether that's the Trader or anyone else.

Suggestions:

CodeMonkey suggested a line of Engineer-only nanos/widgets for customising weapons and another, similar line for Traders in order to balance things. I think a lot of Engineers would be happy with a solution of this nature.

Non-self-only buffs. IMO an iffy solution for the reasons stated above, but Funcom have already announced they're doing something similar for NT's in the next patch. Could be implemented as a straight buff, a nano that plunks a time-limited nodrop item into the target's inventory (helperbots) or as a tradeskill item that can be built, perhaps using Chemistry to make it slightly easier for an Engineer than a Trader.

Something for team play. Fixing the pathing would be the ideal solution, but this is harder to actually implement than it is to whine about.

An alternate would be a "pet grapple" nano, that ties the attacking mob into combat with the bot for a short time. This would give the Engineer more ability to control the fight, something which the Bureaucrat and even the MP have to some extent but which the Engineer is totally deficient in. It's a blatant kludge to get around the aggro problem (see "Bots, not holding") without going so far as a full-on root/mez/charm crowd control library. The bot changes from being a reliable but slow damage dealer to an asset that can help the team manage the fight properly.

Cheers,
