A request to FC from all those who dont like PVP
Ok I have seen several threads here on the boards about people who dont like PVP. I happen to be one of those people. I tried PVP and found it to be not a matter of skill but a matter of equipment. PVP also tends to bring out the most immature people who seem to only be able to trash talk and gloat/whine (depending on if they win or loose).

What I am asking for is either an option at character creation or a form that would permantly remove a character from PVP. The way I see this working is that once this option is used a player can no longer attack or be attacked by another player no matter the gas level and that the tower gas level has no effect on the player so hunters can still use the areas.

I have thought hard on this and some will say that you gain an unfair advantage with towers then and to that I say your wrong. Towers are for the PVP'ers and I will gladly leave it to them. You will say you have a grace period to get out of a zone or area and to that I have two answers. Yesterday doing blitzes I come across two areas with towers that were in 25% mode. Both times I was down to within 15 secs of the timer running out even though I was in my yalm and flying in a straight line. The second answer is I pay my monthly fee's the same as everyone else and to suddenly have to dodge areas because of an activity I dont want to participate in or to close of whole portions of the game world is not right.

Now the last big argument for this being included. If FC would implement this into the game then at that point the whole world could be opened up to 25% gas and the PVP'ers could have the wars the desire while those of us who dont want to participate would not have to.

Im sure that Im going to get flamed for this and Im asking that you stop and think about the idea before you flame me. I respect your views and your play style all I ask is that you respect mine. No MMORPG should have open free for all PVP unless there is a way to permantly remove a character from PVP.
