Excuse me..? I believe I have some related information.

Two days ago I was flying across Newland Desert looking for some rumored mineral deposits. Quite valuable I'm told. Anyway. As I passed close to the NLD Temple, I spotted some odd-looking tracks in the sand. On a whim I decided to look closer. I'm no tracker but I could tell that the person who made them was stumbling and falling a lot.
Being the kind soul I am *cough* I followed the tracks to see if I could help the injured person.

I found him lying unconcious in the shadow of the Temple, propped against a wall.
I quickly jumped out of my Yalmaha with a first aid stim in each hand. The man was a mess. Blood all over him. A bone sticking out of his arm. His face smashed beyond recognition. I almost retched at the sight, but I steeled myself and injected both stims into him. As I leaned in to do so I noticed he was wearing a tattered Omni-Tek uniform. The stims seemed to help but not as much as they should.
Not knowing what to do I ran back to my Yalmaha, grabbed my comm set and contacted Drtiny.

"Doc! Doc! I need your help!"
"I'm kind of in the middle of something. Can it wait?"
"Erm. I'm afraid not. I found a man in the desert. He's hurt really bad and my stims aren't helping!"
"Hold on... Okay I can spare a few minutes, but there's not much I can do from here."
"There must be something you can do! He's dying!"
"I'm afraid not. I would have to examine him myself."
"I have an idea. Hold on"

I ran to my Yalmaha and yanked out the bio interface unit, quickly juryrigged this on to my portable surgery clinic and connected the whole thing to my NCU.

"Doc, I'm going to link our NCUs via a grid route. You should be able to operate my makeshift bio scanner via the link"
"Hold on...yes. Exellent! Strap it to his chest... Okay, just a moment."

I waited in suspense as Drtiny diagnosed the man via my hastily constructed gadget. After what seemed an eternity he told me what he found.

"He should be concious but there's some severe nerve damage. The stims should have repaired that."
"Well why didn't they?"
"I don't know. The bio scanner is very limited. I have to examine him myself."
"How soon can you be here?"
"I'll get there as soon as I can, but I have a small emergency on my hands. Do what you can in the meantime"
"Okay. I'll try"

I didn't know where to start. How so I do what the stims can't? I keyed my comm set again.

"Ike? I need your help!"

My brother took a few moments responding,

"What's up Kirk?"
"You can get into Omni-Med right?"
"Can and do every week. Why?"
"I need to know about nerve damage that can't be repaired by stims. It's urgent!"
"Hold on guys... sorry. Yeah I'll get on it. One moment.

Again an eternity passed. I tried to make the man as comfortable as I could

"Still there Kirk?"
"Yes!"
"Found something, Im in a hurry so I'll upload it all to you."

I didn't answer, but threw myself at the file immediately. Nothing on the cause or details on the damage. A theory on how to treat it though. "break the code seal on a blank stim with receptive bots so it will accept non-standard programming. Very illegal due to the nasty uses it can be put to"
Looking at the injured man I knew I didn't have time to run and get the parts I needed and I just wrecked my Yalmaha to make the bio scanner.
Again I hit the comm. This time to the open DW frequenzy.

"I need help here!"

For a change somebody responded immediately. Tazze and Djinnis.

"I found an injured man in the desert. I need some medical supplies asap!"

I named the parts I needed "mimicking oil, receptive bots, etc. The wounded man was getting worse and I silently urged Djinnis and Tazze to hurry up. They finally arrived with the components I needed. Adding some more pieces of my yalmaha I was able to make a stim that was able to repair the man's nerves. I stabbed it directly into his heart and after a few minutes he came about and was able to talk.
