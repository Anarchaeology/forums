10% wont cut it. That will get members of my clan to go out with me for one hunt to see how much has changed and then later one more hunt two weeks later as a sort of sympathy hunt, because I can't take doing nothing but Home missions.

I can understand the problem. They cant raise the xp on the high lvl critters without raising xp across the board. I also understand that they would prefer half the population to be in missions at any given time to save the load on the playfields.

But, that makes high lvl play what it is today. If you can tolerate the "Canned meat" taste of missions for hours on end, then there isnt a problem.

I just think that something is wrong with missions when a lvl 130 team can Sprint through lvl 193 missions, only stopping for the Big buffs and never because of the enemy's capabilities. While outside, that same team wouldnt even consider attacking the lifestopper camp in CAV for fear of getting demolished by the rapidfire double striking snakes that give 65%(75% after the patch...oooo) of the xp of a veteran engineer.

Sadly, the only solution I can see is making missions harder. Even if the xp was equal, the fact that you can sprint through missions and must crawl fighting outside high lvl camps will never get them close to equal.

People often forget that it isnt xp per kill that is important. It is xp per hour that rules decision making in MMOGs. Even with a 10% bonus....there is no contest.

Re: My thoughts...
quote:
Originally posted by Szentasha
I love hunting outside! Its damn hard to find someone to do it with though. It is a lot more challenging and takes longer to hunt outside. I think the XP increase should be more than 10%.

Better yet, here is an idea: Add a line of adventurer XP buffs that increase XP outdoors for the team. That would be a real bonus, and makes sense too!
Give Adv's that and the Crats will scream bloody murder....

But in the case of indoor vs outdoor xp?...

The outdoors should be the XP haven. Indoors should be for loot / cash / tokens and the like. How can you justify giving decent XP rewards for humanoid mobs in missions that are simple to knock over and proceed where outdoor monster mobs (who hit either twice as fast or twice as hard) don't give nearly enough XP to make it appealing enough to get a team of people out of BS?? It just can't happen at this point....

First, I think that ALL missions should be relatively the same size as a typical BS mission. Second, outdoor mobs should give WAY more XP than indoor mobs do and here's why:

When you do a mission, the exit of that mission functions literally as a "pause" button. There is no danger involved in leaving a mission if your team is overwhelmed. Where you gonna go outdoors when the same thing happens?

Missions have chests, which often contain loot, and sometimes even decent loot. Outdoors...monster parts .

In a mission, we have some control over what types of mobs get placed within said mission, control over the locked/unlocked status of doors, etc... Outdoors, whatever is there is there...

There is much more danger outdoors than there is indoors, thus there should be much more XP outdoors than there is indoors...

and a pathetically inferior XP and loot reward.
This has to change if the game is going to offer more
option than just endless mission after mission.

10% is not even close to making the outdoor world viable
in the level 100-200 game. Anyone who thinks otherwise
hasnt tried it for the length of time I did. It's not if you can
kill a mob and earn XP, it's for much XP can you earn and keep.

The problem is not just XP,
It's world-design, spawn patterns, population control,
mob behaviour and many other issues that also need work.
Increased XP outdoors is a nice 'bandaid fix' but 10% just
will not compensate for all the other risks/disadvantages.
