Any poets / writers out there???
Hello. I am interested in holding a poetry recital featuring the works of the citizens of Rubi-Ka. Two members of my guild will be reading a work, and I am looking for others who would like to participate.

Works can be poetry or free-verse, and should be reasonably suited for the world of Rubi-Ka. Meaning, that they can't have contracitory content. Also, they should be a reasonable length.

If you would like to participate as a presenter, or would like to attend, please send me a tell in game or email me at szentasha@darksphere.com.

A date will not be decided until a fair number of writers have signed up (I am thinking 5-7).

I probably won't be making any sort of grand announcement once it is finallized because it is better that this be a small word-of-mouth event.

Thanks!
