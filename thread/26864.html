Asked to roleplay, yes, pressurised into roleplaying, never. We are a very laid back bunch. We don't force anyone to roleplay or take part in events and we certainly have no roleplaying police running around beating our members into shape.

But it ought to be said that roleplaying is the only thing we are any good at, and even that is open to debate. There are plenty of other guilds who are larger and way better at organising hunts or securing equipment for their members than us. The Society can be a good home if what you are want the most is an environment that helps you connect with your character and with the setting.

If that sort of thing appeals to you, by all means give me a tell. Alternatively, for a friendly, more well-rounded org, you could try speaking with Omni-Executives for instance.
