A Big OOC Thank You
I would like to take a moment out of everyone's busy schedules and troll-postings and rantings and serious debates, and thank the RP community of Anarchy Online. I have had my account since May of last year, but never played that much until around the end of November. To me, it just seemed like a very pretty EQ clone. Sometimes I even wondered why I kept my account open when I played maybe once every month or two.

Then, I met all of you.

I know a lot has been said about "the death of RP"-this and how AO is just a PvP arena and a cyber-haven and even "The Sims: Combat Edition." But I would like to take a second to thank you all for the wonderful work you've done in keeping this community alive and well, and the main reason I now log in daily.

I would like to also take this time to thank several guilds and individuals in particular, those who I've met on-world and those I've met through the forums, for truly enriching both my in-game and my out-of-game life. This is not all-encompassing, as many people have been good friends of mine, but I just felt it was time to say thank you.

-Whisper's Edge (of course, you guys are the best)
-Vixentrox (you know I love you, and we may fight, but it's always for something better than either of us, and I'll always buy you dinner at the Cup)
-Templus and Elviria (The only Omnis who have ever bothered to RP with me in a conflict, even if it was indirectly. It means a lot to be respected enough to be given that opportunity)
-Valor Eternal (you have always been there for me personally and RP-wise and that hasn't gone unnoticed. I'm always here for each and every one of you)
-Emiliy (I've known you only 2 weeks but you make GC so much fun )
-Nealandbob (You were my very first true friend on Rubi-Ka and have been there through everything, cheering me on and helping me up when I'm down. You will always have a special place in my heart)
-Rhogue (You always have a fresh look on things. I hope I can find your strength to change things for the better in myself.)
-Devil's Advocate: Namely Nevver, Missmaul, Ditzie, and Don (you guys have made the board postings fun and original. I'm always looking for what you say next)
-The entire crew of GSP, especially Tarryk, Wulfven, and Trystalyn (WOW! That's all I can say to you all. Thanks you for introducing me to the wonderful world of RP here.)
-Sezmra, Katieblu, Lizor (My party buddies.....my Truth or Dare mates....You and I all have had a blast with off-the-cuff RP, and I love it)
-Thedeacon (You and I never RP, but I have to list you cause TEH FOOTNESS still scares me)

I feel like I'm typing a goodbye message, but I just wanted to tell you all and every RPer out there that your efforts are all noticed and appreciated. Too little do we take the time to say thank you for whats given us and let the people we care about know how we feel.

You have all touched me in your own unique ways, and I am forever grateful for it. I love all of you so very much.

I'll see you all on-world.

All my love,
Kyrstina
