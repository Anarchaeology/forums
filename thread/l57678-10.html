Betadine
Please Understand
Rintrah and staff:

Most of what is displayed here is anger and frustration, but though it all I see people generally disappointed. We had such high hopes for you. We all needed a change from the "hack and slash" fantasy mmorpg. You were our first hope, your initial screenshots were beautiful and captured an entire audience of people willing to give their time to test and retest your game. The AO Community blossomed and grew in anticipation for something new and different, banking on all the promises that you gave on what the game would be.

Beta was tough, and confusing... lots of crashes, bugs, zone problems, everything a normal beta usually is. We played, understanding that someday you would get to the point where you would have a finished product. The entire Beta community was a little shocked when you announced that you were going live when we knew that you weren't ready. Unfortunately, you don't have a finished game and it's still about 70% complete. The bugs are still very common, and with this new patch you introduced many new bugs that again will have to be fixed. People still have many problems with the client crashing and with the Auto-patcher. Characters are still getting stuck in Voids and Gaps and are therefore unable to log in. You nerfed things that didn't really need to be changed, and you "fixed" things that made the game unplayable (i.e. "Take it easy" and the Mob changing targets and being rendered invulnerable.)

Visually, the game is stunning. The artists did a wonderful job creating a virtual world that was different and amazing, however, you missed the target entirely because only powergamers and computer geeks like me can run the game with any efficiency. Take Omni-Trade for instance, it takes a long time to load, it lags while running and turning because it's loading, and since it's the spot to find everything you could ever need, people lag the zone. It seems like a huge oversight on your part, it's like putting a lump of coal in a bright pretty box with a shiny ribbon around it.

Overall, I am supremely disappointed in what you have given us and made us pay for. We all bought into your sales scheme. We all believed in you and wanted to trust that you knew what you were doing. It's painfully obvious that this is not the case. But then you further insult us by not saying anything, or even apologize for the mistakes you make. You have paying customers now, and since you are charging for the game, you have an obligation to provide a service as promised to your customers. You are not doing that, and in addition to your disappointing the entire community, you continue to charge us for finishing the game for you. I'm sad, but I am also angry at your blatent disregard for your customer, and your general avoidance of problems and lack of care. You don't seem to take responsibility for all the mistakes and for the fact that you put out an unfinished game, and charged people for refining your game... which is what a beta is for. You dashed our hopes, you took our dreams and distorted them into a world blanketed in lies and deceit, and I am sorry I ever believed in you.
