PvP joint Clan/Omni WAR this friday/saturday - 4 holes starts between 7 - 9GMT
Event planned for Friday 22/ Sat 23 in 4 holes.

Objective - Clans hold the grid area, Omnis wait at the wompas, when ready both parties try to take and hold the bridge.

Clans members will be allowed to get in position before any Omnis are given the exact time of the event to discourage "Ganking at the grid".

The general time is between 7-9 GMT but exact timing will be issued to Omnis 5 minutes before the Clans are at the location in order to give them time to set up.

Event will be fully coordinated with both Omni and Clan faction leaders.

Credit to Azazzel for the original idea and to the clan faction leaders for their support.
Thanks to Omni guilds FTC & TBA for supporting the event.
Thanks also to Clansmember "Anti" for coordinating the clan side.

We hope this to be a very major event suitable for all levels who like team or single PvP.

Hope to see you at the War!
