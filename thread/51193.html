¤The Compilation¤
A list of suggestions, ideas, bugs, and whatnot.

Profession Related

Doctor:
  • Increase DoT damage.

Enforcer:
  • Add 1HS, 2HS, and Piercing buffs.
  • Add a Mongo nano between QL 66 and 132, preferably two.
  • Change Challenger line to include "Recovering from Challenger"-effect for 2 minutes instead of nano shutdown--24 second reload is long enough.

Engineer:
  • Add small self-only Tradeskill nanos that stack with the Trader's targetable nano line.

Fixer:
  • Decrease GA's DMS bonus and it's rarity (personal request).

MA:
  • Add MA buff between QL 50 and 169.
  • Add team MA buff/aura boosting only team MA's skills.

MP:
  • Extend the Infuse-line nano duration to 45 minutes.

NT:
  • Add "Max Nano Pool +xx"-effect self-only nanos that are stackable with the Trader's line.
  • Increase DoT damage of nanos.
  • Add a quested nano that creates a "Add Nano Damage +xx"-effect No Drop, NT-only item.

Soldier:
  • Change Mirror Shield to include "Recovering from Mirrior Shield"-effect instead of nano shutdown--20 seconds reload is long enough.

Traders:
  • Make the Tradeskill buffs slightly stronger and self-only.
  • Add a targetable line of buffs of the same type, but not as strong.
  • Add two Siphon AC nanos pre-QL86.


(Note: I haven't played a Soldier, but to my understanding, not being able to heal or have a crit buff is a serious drawback.

Weapons
  • Add better variety of decent melee weapons that do not require Sneak Attack.
  • Increase Bow damage.

Items
  • Add QL 101-200 Positive/Negative Aggressive-Defensive Trimmers.
  • Add Physical init bonus to Flurry of Blows (since some melee weapons use it).
  • Add various QL 1 newbie items to startup shopping terminal.
  • Add a greater variety of newbie armor (see above).

Breeds
  • Add Atrox-only Armor.
  • Add a few more Atrox-only weapons (perhaps 2HS that is missionable).

Trade Skill Related
  • Add recipie for QL 1~200 NCU units.
  • Add recipies for various Trimmers.

Miscellaneous
  • Add preferences and exact QL selections to the mission terminal interface (perhaps make this interface dependent on your CL).
  • Add underwater static dungeons and/or dungeon-like areas requiring the Adventuring skill to enter.
  • Add general buffs that last 1-hour.
  • Enhance interface to make chat window resizable.
  • Enable AFK messages to be customizable.
  • Add facial surgery clinic.
  • Add a /quickbar <x> command (activates the respective slot in the quickbar).
  • Add a /target <x> command.
  • Delete all level 1 name-placeholders of expired accounts.

Fixes/bugs
  • Fix pathing problems.
  • Fix Yalm shrink bug.
  • Fix bow intiatives.


(I'm sure there are more bugs, requests, ideas, etc, so... reply what you have and I'll try to keep this post up to date. Remember, no flaming, keep it clean and constructive. Thanks.)
