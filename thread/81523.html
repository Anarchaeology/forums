Thank you Omni-Tek
Logged on today and asked the question "anything interesting happening atm?"

The reply came back "Omni in Camelot".

'Omni in Camelot?' that didn't seem possible, so I rushed over to see which Omni it was that decided to stand at the top of the stairs and watch us clanners disappear through the magic door.

When I got there it turned out there were more than one Omni!! Yes, it's true! There were 3 or 4 of them up top. Quickly I raced down to the box in the hopes these few Omnis had more coming and were going to make a push on us. As I hit the end of the hallway, the 'wall of lag' set in as players in the box room loaded to screen.

To my complete amazement, the dots loading up on the map were not yellow, they were blue!! That's right! Another 20-30 Omnis had taken over the box room!!

The battle between Clan and Omni started on the stairs, people shooting back and forth from both sides. As Clan pushed Omni into the box and bunch of us were at the bottom of the stairs, some waited for alpha to recycle before jumping in front of the door and letting of some rounds. Others took this time to let off some debuffs, drains and Domninates. Pets were going back and forth, slayers and demons. The action was intense.

Finally, enough Omnis dropped that clan was able to enter the box room and get a better look at our newly revived foe. As their spirits broke, Omni retreated in haste to Tara's chamber. The chase was on with both sides have run speeds fully buffed. Omni ran into the chamber and drew a line, Clan hit the door and dared to cross it. The battle errupted again with clanners coordinating properly on same targets. Quickly the Omnis were dropped as Clan broke into Tara's chamber and cleared out the blue dots.

To Sublim and his people in AdastrusII and all the other Omni-Tek employees that came to try the impossible, many thanks to you all. This was some of the best fun we have had yet!!
