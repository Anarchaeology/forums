Fixercon I: SCHEDULE - Saturday, July 13
Saturday, July 13, Start time: 19GMT

Time zone translation

Gathering spot: Borealis. Look for Taboo near the insurance/bank that's on the way to the Wrecked Shop. [Taboo, being one of those slimy agents, has decided to pay his penance by standing out there and grouping people for warps for the whole event.]

Level limit: You must be 25 to attend. Exceptions made by contacting me or Nettie in advance, and will mainly be granted to those joining us from RK2.

Schedule
18 GMT - Hashbang begins work on his masterpieces.
18:30 GMT - Begin warping
19 GMT - Start, Tarryk goes on
19:30 GMT - End PvP registration
20 GMT - PvP start
20-21 GMT - Experts available
21:45 GMT - AO-Fixer contest winner
22 GMT - Playa voting
22:45 GMT - PvP tourney award
23 GMT - End

19-21 GMT Tradeskills/swap meet open (swap meet will be held in a seperate room to avoid extreme spamage)
19-22 GMT Random contests
19-23 GMT DJ Tarryk spins tunes [yes, he takes requests ]

Tradeskills available: Implant assembly, implant stripping, weapon assembly, nano creation. NOTE: Please bring the disposable components for the tradeskills you would like performed. Tools will be on hand. We don't want to overwhelm the warpers with people running in and out looking for Space Symbol Libraries.

Contacts for info: Trellame, Nettie, Raline (Poizun), Hydeo [PvP]

First, let me apologize--this may sound a little harsh. This isn't so much directed at Gorotsuki as any people who really start whining about not getting to Fixercon (for the record, the don't think Gorotsuki is whining. He disagrees with the lvl 25 thing, but accepts it):

quote:
Originally posted by Gorotsuki
I just after 14.4 made a new fixer, and am working with my agent to farm 100 mil so I can buy GA But I cant attend now =(


It kind of stinks, Gorotsuki, but this is the exact thing I think the level limit is there to guard against--people who created fixers right after 14.4 While the rest of us were struggling through with our fixers pre-14.4, these people went off to play something else until the band-wagon swung our way. Then--Whooooa, Nelly!---here they come!

It's not that I (or anyone else, I think) is against people creating new fixers. Just that when those people start a fixer and think that after charging fixers for buffs and not paying for runbuffs, or kicking fixers out of teams, or refusing to team with fixers, etc, they will just be a part of the community and no one will care about the past.

Again, no complaints about new fixers, but be prepared to put in the time as a fixer. If you're coming to stay, great. If you're just passing through....move along.
