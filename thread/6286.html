Improvements....
How about adding some new professions or making some type of multiclass option like in AD&D?

This game is getting extremley boring waiting for new things to be created. There are no secrets to anything, nothing special, ect... the only reason to play is to get EXP and to level so you can use better equipment or nano's, most of which sucks... depending what class you are.

How about even some special features or nanos for breed/class combos?

For example, my character is a Nanomage Adventurer... why not have a special nano for that particular combo, it would be nice to be able to do something unique or special. It would be nice to see more breed restrictions to make items or nano's more special and not just something anyone can use.

have something to make the game more life like. we have different weather conditons, nice changing skies, ect... why not add a little more random chaos to the game... an under world or something.

( i realize this is alot of work, but look at the amount of people who play and subscribe to this service... results would be nice... i'm getting tired of reading posts with no answers, and when they do get answered...which is few and far between, the Forum Helpers are very selective as to what they say and how often they actually reply to post)

i dunno..lol... maybe i'd be happier trashing my PC and going back to a Commodore 64 and playing Ultima IV and The Bard's Tale?

it's getting really boring sitting around waiting for a new patch to see what new things may come or go....

Fantastic Idea
I really like the idea of NanoFormulas that can only be executed in NCU's equipped by certain breeds. We have General and Profession specific NF's. It would add a little ****e to have a few Breed specific NF's. It could help to accentuate the differences between the Opi, Atrox, Nano, and Solitus a bit more and add some ****e to character creation. Hell, leave Solitus out of the mix if they like.

I'm all for options and variety.
