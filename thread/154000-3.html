This is the summary I emailed Cz ...
Thanks everyone for all that lovely input. Cool, calm, collected feedback, gotta love it, you guys & gals are super
FYI, I just sent Cz the following summary:


INNER SANCTUM - the Adventurers' perspective
A summary of feedback from the Adventurer community
-----

Adventurer-specific and related equipment/armour
(usefulness/effectiveness/drop rates)

Top of the list NO-SHOW items:
- Right Hand of Entropy
- Impious Dominator

Armour ...
The Bloodseal/Unhallowed Bloodseal is considered useless back armour for its complete lack of AC values.

The basic Carapace is generally considered as good armour for level 150+ Adventurers. The drop rate of the basic Carapace pieces has been stable so far.

The drop rate of the Ichor of the Immortal One is very bad, which in turn affects the upgrading of Carapace pieces very badly. The difficulty of Hezak plus slim chances of organised group revisits combine to make the Unhallowed Carapace armour set an impossible option to plan for. Given time, effort and organised raiding opportunities, Title 5/6 Adventurers are better able to obtain Azure and/or Sentinel/Omni-AF armour rather than their own upgraded Adventurer-specific armour.

Suggestions so far:
1) tone down Hezak
2) let the Ichor drop randomly from final room mobs

Weapons ...
Many of the weapons dropped in IS would benefit Title 4/5 Adventurers the most, particularly the pistols as they fill the miserable gap level 100-175 Advy pistol users face currently. The drop rate of the weapons is uneven, rated from fair to good.

Dungeon difficulty/player-levels mismatch ...
Ironically and unfortunately, the level range of Adventurers who would benefit the most from the weapons and basic Carapace armour are the ones who most likely won't be in the dungeon. By the time Adventurers are of the levels to stand a chance of survival in IS, they are already late Title 5 or Title 6 and way past the optimal effectiveness of the equipment. They would also already be wearing better armour and wielding better weapons by then.

If the weapons and armour are pitched for the needs of Title 4/5 Adventurers, then the current level of difficulty of the dungeon is a gross mismatch in terms of player level ranges and effective use of items.


Difficulty factor

The mix of mob types and attacks are generally considered a good challenge. Adds/aggro factors are on the high side as very little crowd control can be done. This can overwhelm small teams.

Organised groups, with time to spare, generally have a better time in the dungeon than pick-up groups. Individuals and casual players have little to no chance at all.

Hezak and the final room mob spawns are rated as too difficult and frustrating. Loot spread is also too narrow, not attractive enough for more professions - this compounds the difficulty of organising good team composition for the boss room. The effort-versus-reward here needs tremendous improvement otherwise there is no motivation for revisits (especially once this initial new/novelty phase is over).


Fun factor

IS offers varying and challenging mob types which contributes to the fun factor.

The XP gain is not bad also, so this helps to keep the player motivated (and happy).

However, dungeon size is generally rated as being too small:
- a major detraction to the fun factor is the prevalence of Kill Stealing incidents which is unavoidable in such a small place.
- another major anti-fun factor is the prevalence of mob training
- a trip to this dungeon is nothing more than a enter/kill/loot experience devoid of a sense of journey or exploration.
- the size, location and singular theme of the dungeon is out of place with the faction system and does not lend itself to the main story line.

Temporary key pass and GS/team issues ...
The sentiments of the Adventurer community echo the unhappiness of the general playerbase - no one likes the current system.
