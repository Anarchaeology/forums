pvp 1 on 1 feature plz
would be nice to be able to challenge somone to a fight anywhere on rubi-ka. Something along the lines of:

/fight <message to targeted player= Player Snuffy has challenged you to a fight. To accept type /accept.

this would allow 2 players to spar, test items, armor, and equipment in the environment of their chosing. Would also limit unwanted engagements in the Arena while testing equipment. Would also add imo a unique experiance to fight in areas or settings otherwise off limits to pvp. This would allow you to challenge a player to fight and at the same time give the other player the option to accept the challenge or decline depending on his / her mood at the time.

I know sometimes I would like to just have a friendly spar with a friend, but when you enter the arena you are accepting the fact that its a 5% gas area and while some players will simply watch you spar or test equipment there are those that get enjoyment killing or filling your ncu with junk buffs for entertainment. When I go into the arena I do accept this as reality and If I get ganked I might come back with friends or i might not.

But I think having a feature like this avalible for us to use would greatly benefit the PvP comunity as well as the people that are more causal pvpers.

Im not asking to change the way the arena works, or change how PvP works. Just add this feature and I think it would add a big boost to the enjoyment of PvP for everyone.

I think a common concern for people with PvP is being able to chose who they are fighting.

But there are situations where this is not reasonable such as tower battles. During a tower battle you are either the attacking force or the defending force. Who you fight is not realy a option, and it shouldnt be and the system inplace for those types of pvp action imo need no tweeking or help.

Like tower battles arena fights are kind of the same thing. You willfuly enter an area where you may or may not be attacked by anyone within lvl range. I know it is hard to watch a fight from outside the Arena and you may be tempted to go in for a closer look. I am the same way, but I accept that once inside there are no spectator rules and while most people will ask if you want to duel, there are those people that will attack you without asking. And to be honest I think thats how it should be. You shouldnt have to ask to scrap out with someone in the arena.

With a feature like this in game it would give people more options when it comes to pvp. I like pvp, Its good the way it is now, but its more set up for team or large scale battles.

I think the Arena and Tower wars should remain the same as far as the rules go. This function would be more a supliment and independant from arena and tower battles.
