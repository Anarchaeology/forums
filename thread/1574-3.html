No, _level_ doesn't affect even that.

Your base abilities affects that. Level affects how HIGH you can get those base abilities, and thus how high your ability bonus to said skill is.

If you are level 200 but have not spent any IP into anything, you would still have exactly same skillset as a level 1. You'd have lots more HP/Nano, through level bonuses, but your abilities and skills would be exactly like that of a lvl1.

And no, not every person maxes their stats (atleast until what, lvl 150+), and also every breed has slightly different hard caps and starting stats, which also make a (very minor) variation into it.

So, level does not:
a) affect skill costs
b) affect directly how much it will cost to have a skill at a set point

Level does:
a) affect skill cost to a set point of skill value indirectly by allowing higher maximum in abilities that effect said skill
b) gives +hp +nano depending on combination of class and breed on each level-up
c) sets temporary caps on skill raises by the way of 5/4/3 per lvl caps and title level caps

Otherwise, AO is totally skill based game except those 3 up there. And skills are totally class based in cost. Well there are also the items that require a spesific title, and thus are level capped, so lets say those 4 things...
