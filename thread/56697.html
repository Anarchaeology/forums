Exploits...
I see you (Funcom) making posts that basically state the following:

---
We know about an exploit related to X.
Stop doing it or you will be banned.
---

This kinda bothers me.
Why? Because we (the players) have no idea what you consider an exploit.
Giving us information like the above is like playing a game of chess without knowing what the pieces do, but knowing that you will automagically lose if you move one the wrong way.

Can you instead do something like the following?
------
Create an AO exploits board.
Post exploits as you find them.
Note that the exploit is a bannable offense.
Log people using the exploit and ban them.
When you find a new exploit, make a popup ingame that gives the information from the post so that everyone knows about it.
Fix the exploit asap so that people dont have to worry about accidentally doing it.
------

Would that really be so hard?
I can see it being hard if the logging mechanisms are not in place...but otherwise it seems like it is just silly to tell people that there is a known exploit, not tell them what it is, and tell them you will ban them for it.

Sure, most are probably things that are common sense, but it isnt far from what you are doing to acting like verant and dubbing valid ingame mechanics as exploits.

I have no idea.
Since I have no idea what the exploits are, I have no idea which (if any) are just valid use of mechanics.

My whole point is that it is rather silly to tell us that there is an exploit and we could get banned for it...but to not tell us what it is that we are not supposed to be doing.

If they have the logging facilities, it would be simple enough to log exploiters after a warning and ban them.

That way, you dont have someone who accidentally does something getting the ban stick.

I really cant believe that no one can see this...who cares if everyone knows about the exploit...so long as you ban everyone who uses it after they are given fair warning.

And no, I do not think it would be bad to ban exploiters. Why would it be bad? Subscription numbers? Bah. Easy enough to fix. Combine all servers.

Companies seem to always see the $$$ lost rather than the increased loyalty of decent customers and the lack of $$$ wasted by devs having to constantly change things to make up for any damage caused by exploits.
