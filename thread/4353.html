Preserve professions
Just few word concerning professions and game play:

I would prefer this game with professions even more expertized. My biggest concern is nano crystals. Already Agents possibility to make false-profession makes me little wondering. Ok, its agents expert skill, but i would have liked if agents were more on concealment skills and maybe false breed, sex and clan/omni skills.

Never let there be possibility to hack crystals. Maybe some rare items, guns, etc. which could contain nanos without profession limit. But every profession should have something great, something unique which any other profession cant achieve any way.

Also something small "inside things" to professions: mountain or/and church just for Meta-Physicists, black market chat-channel to Fixers, workshop only for Engineers, skycreaper to which only high lvl Bureucrats could access and so on. There is never too much different places, nanos, guns, armors,...

There are magnificant suggestions in profession boards! Please consider them all carefully but also without prejudice. Game will be developing all its life time, things are added and removed. Hope this world would preserve differency like that other civilized planet we live in.

Thanks!
