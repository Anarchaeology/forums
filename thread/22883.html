What do you do when you are not leveling ?
Im currently level 196. I used to be a powerleveler, gained 5-10 lvls a week. Now I only gain 1/2 or 1 lvl in a week. Im in no hurry getting the last few levels as I cant cast any better nano even at lvl 200. So everytime when I log on, I ask myself, what can I do today ?

- Socializing - Well if I were to socialize Id prefer to go to real bar or something, I do socialize in game mostly when Im doing something else like surfing. Im an active person, I cant imagine myself sitting in a cyber bar doing nothing but watching the screen and typing all the time.

- RPing - This game RPing is ridiculous. Look at how those people RPing, killing Tir guards and claim to take over the city. Or people who claimed to be negotiating all day but achieved nothing in the end. Im not into this kind of RPing. I seek RPing that have 'real' actions. I did build myself an image in my 2 weeks of EFP RPing.

- PvPing - I love PvPing, I geared myself towards PvP even Im not a combat profession. Then again, PvPing in AO is stupid. Most PvP dont relate to skills at all, whatever UVC+Behemoth+Doc HP+Reflect+Run buff is the skill. Often times there are more verbal war than the actual conflict.

- Unique Mob Camping - Another thing I could do, like I said Im an active person, I cant stand myself sitting all day watching comp screen for spawn. Im glad Im in a good guild and often there are people to send me tells whenever a unique mob is up. Ive gotten a lot of good gears and the gears Im currently wearing are better than 98% population have out there. I still join raid when theres one, mainly to help my guildies and friends to acquire their equipment.

- Helping guildmates - spare my wealth with my guildies tho I dont have much myself.

- Reroll new chars - I tried a couple, none made past 50. Now they are all placed in the unique spawn or for political zone spying purposes. I dont even bother to twink em for pvp becuz I believe low level pvp is even more stupid than high lvl pvp.

- Ebaying - violation of rules, not my style.

What else can I do ? Id like some advice from the high lvls who dont level much anymore and still like the game and community.
