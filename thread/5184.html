One thing that always bugged me is mouse scroll wheels and the info panes. Panes like your actions (ctrl-2) and uploaded nanos (ctrl-8) have relatively few things to scroll through yet your scroll wheel works with it. Others, like your friends/channels list (ctrl-7) can have many pages of info, yet you have to manually scroll through it by clicking or dragging. PITA.

Another thing that always bugged me were Esc levels. For instance you have an info box open, two of your bags, a chat box going, and a target selected. I don't remember the exact order, but hitting Esc once will close the info box, again will close your chat box, and a third time will close your bags/loot windows and deselect your target, not to mention stop you from attacking it! IMO, Esc stopping an attack should be last on that list, and they should all be separate levels. No two things should be closed with one hit of the button.

And not to hijack the thread, allowing all of the inventory windows to be resized even vertically would be a big help. Bank management would be less of a chore. Banks should also let you pick up items when you click on them, like from bags, instead of just dumping it to your inventory when you click on it, like loot and reward windows do.

Uh... yeah.
