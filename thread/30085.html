Major IPR System Bug
A note to Funcom, reported through feedback as well:

I've tried 3 times to report this through petition and get it resolved, I'm about to give up. There is a major bug in the IPR system. Here are the details:

I spent an IPR point and reset my Perception skill to base level (it was at 250). I got the points back and raised the skill back up to 200 but did not hit "accept" (so it showed that I had spent 30k or so IP to the left of the skill name). I then used another skill to reset Time and Space and similarly raised it back up a bit. I spent a third point to reset Run Speed and rasied it back up a bit in the same way. I did not hit "accept" between any of these expeditures, and all showed that I spent 30k-ish points to the left of the skill. I noticed I still had points left over, so I spent those on other skills. I then hit "accept", and discovered that my Time and Space, Perception, and Run Speed were all at base level -- they had not been raised to 200-ish level.

What I believe happened is that when you spend two IPR points without hitting "accept" in between, it resets the first skill again -- but does not show this in the skill display (i.e., it still shows that you have spent 30k IP or whatever and shows the raised score, even though it gives you the IP back in the total window). This is a major bug. If you spend all your IPR (as I did), you can permanently gimp your character with no way to get XP (if, for example, you had reset a combat skill). Note that this bug only affects the individual IPR points, not the full reset. I suspect that if you hit "accept" between each IPR point expenditure, the system will work fine.

I petitioned Funcom, and waited online for 3 hours until there were no petitions ahead of me in the queue. No one showed up. My petition was removed from the queue by Funcom. I petitioned again. This time someone showed up, but after I had logged off. I petitioned again. This time an ARK showed up, I explained the problem and he said he would go "confer with others". After 3-4 minutes of silence, his character faded out and he was listed as off-line. I assume he crashed, but no one ever got back to me afterwards. Frankly, I'm pretty pissed off, not at the bug, but at the treatment I received.

Hopefully Funcom can find and fix the bug, but I'd like to have my 5 IPR back so I can un-gimp my character (I spent my last 2 trying to get my Time and Space up to a semi-usable level for nano rechargers).

Regards,
Keith
