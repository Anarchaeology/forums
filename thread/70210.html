Greetings all, thanks for checking out my post.

So I'm back. For real, I'm back. You probably dont know who I am, and why should you. I quit AO over a year ago. I wanted to start up again but I knew i would pay $50 bucks for 6 months registration where I would play a few nights and get fed up with all the regular bugs and lag.

So I'm back now, and why? Well i heard AO had gotten a lot better. Less bugs, less exploits, less stuff-that-annoyed-people. Some things havent changed though. I can still enter a fight with a green creature and see the whole scene freeze. I run outside, and it takes about a minute for it to load. And when i reappear, I seem to have died. Fun.

But thats not the real issue. Im still lvl <20 with my new character so I dont lose XP or need to wait for my stuff to reappear in the what-you-may-call-it. (I've actually forgotten).

Why did I start this thread? Oh yeah now I remember. I've started a beurocrat (I'll probably be able to spell that correctly in a few months). I play around with my nitwit robots and send them off hunting while i play solitary. I get some xp, a few times. Mostly my robot dies because its green and my enemies are also green. (Who would have guessed that the main ability of a class would be seemingly useless).

Now, now, I'm being silly. Ofcourse I'm new to being a bjurøkrætt, bjeurocrat, beurocrat, barrykraut, and I assume I'll get the hang of it some time soon. At least i know I know my Atrox Soldier did swell the last time i played. I made friends with a few people that were 10 levels lower than me and helped them out with missions and money, and two weeks later they said "You're gray, sorry".

What do I need. Hm Hm Hm, yes indeed that is a question.

Well one thing is for sure, my clan seem to have died. Nobody left. Which is sad, I guess. Vision of Disorder. Anyone remember? Oh nevermind.

So anyway, I guess I need a new clan. I like clans that are serious and well-organised. Have good leaders where there is mutual respect. I'm as loyal and serious to a clan as the clan is to me. I'm omni now, by the way. In case anyone is actually interested in hiring me.

I have some extra bonuses ofcourse. Im a webdesigner, I'm good at graphic design (not just webweb-design, but design in general. ahem) and im fairly competent in PHP and mysql so if you want some messageboards and interactive userinfo then I'm your man.

Well if you've read this far then you're probably as bored as I am. So let me close off with a joke.

What does a fish say when it hits a concrete wall?

"Dam".
