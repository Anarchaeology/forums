Gridrunners Guild -- Starting
My friends call me DASGee, I am clan. I am starting the GridRrunners. The GridRunners will be dedicated to the exploration of the Grid and the manufacturing and sale of Nano-Programs.
There will be regular meetings, times will be set upon vote of all members. There is a web site in progress were the public will be able to see more information about our Guild as well as contact us for services.
Under the current amnesty, we will be offering service to Clan, Neutral, and Omni.
Please contact me in game or reply to this post if you would like to join, or just to meet and discuss further.
