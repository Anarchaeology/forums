Mission booths
I think the mission booth system needs a real overhaul. Its just completely irritating to camp the things when you need a specific item. You need to:

* right click
* hit the drop down
* adjest 3 or 4 sliders
* hit request mission
* shift-left click mission
* scroll down
* shift-left click reward

And then you have a 1/200 shot of getting what you want. Its really not fun! I would suggest that the system be overhauled a bit:

* right click booth
* adjust number of missions pulled (up to 50 or 100)
* look at browsable long list, lists mission icons next to reward icons

One possibiliy is to offer exactly one of each reward type - that is, one purple crystal, one pair of pants, one blue crystal, one item of clothing, etc. The current system is just too slow and too hard on the wrists

--Xente
