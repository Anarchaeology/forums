Just Say No!
I have a HUGE aversion to this random naming! So much so that I've sent an e-mail to the entire community and I've started a poll on the Polling Board:

http://aoforums.funcom.com/showthre...=&threadid=1390

My objection is two-fold:
1. What's to keep some wretched individual from beating us to the naming booth and holding our names hostage? We who are in the community, and have been for quite some time, have developed personalities, stories, histories and guilds based upon our names. I have no doubt that names will be held for PayPal ransom under this system. I've seen it done in the opening of a new server for Asheron's Call.
2. Allowing random naming detracts from the interpersonal relationship you develop with your online character. I have a strong affiliation with my past characters and that is because I CHOSE the name and it was very appropriate to the type of character I created. To say that I could become ATTACHED to some randomly generated mumbo is ludicrous! What's to keep Mr. Server from deciding that my characters name should be Jane Doe when I have created a Male Meta-Physicist?

a quick reply
bcbdla, I am in debt to you for the wealth of knowledge you've shared with me and everyone else from your visit to Funcom at E3. I share your passion for this game. I believe we both are going to play this game no matter what the circumstances surrounding final release are. To put it as clearly as I can, here's my problem with the random naming:

1. I am known as Manus Fari, the humbled patriarch of the Order of Paladins of Rubi-ka. Secret information pertaining to my discovery of ancient earth documents have put a price on my head because, as a former employee of Omni-Tek, my eyes have been opened. I no longer have the luxury of walking the streets of my beloved homeworld without constantly looking over my shoulder. But, this burden I bare and bare willingly, for it is the greater cause of freedom to all Rubicants that weighs more heavily on my heart than my own personal feelings of security.
2. I have spent the last 4 months of my life dedicated to the unfolding story of the conflict on Rubi-ka as Manus Fari. I have brought to life a guild of believers who stand alongside me in the endeavors of freedom, as Manus Fari. I have been ridiculed, mocked, shunned and loved in Rubi-ka as Manus Fari. To force me to be Joe "Manus" Jones, if even for a short time, is something that would destroy my vigor in the pursuit of our ultimate prize. And then, to let me change my name to Jack "Manus" Fari does little to boost my confidence.

Granted, most will call me Manus on Rubi-ka, and I have no doubt that most will pronounce it wrong from the safety of their keyboards, but this just shouldn't be. Let me keep my dignity, LET ME HAVE MY NAME!
