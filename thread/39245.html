Keeping People Honest
A couple good ideas from other games, which never made it to AO, in the interest of keeping people honest:

1. Show loot. When in a team, show in the chatbar what each person loots. This is especially needed for Boss MOB loot, as the person who wins the looting roll on the Boss MOB has no accountability. Nobody knows what was on the Boss, and the looter can keep all the rare stuff with no consequence.

2. Show bag contents. When trading with another player, it is often times easier to trade a set of items in a bag, rather than piece-by-piece. This would be possible if bags could be opened for viewing while in the trade window.
