What do you guys think of this idea to ****e up guilds and pvp
Well there are two parts to this. It would be aimed at making AO a bit more strategic, heres what i was thinking
AO should give a bonus to the person on high ground, now im not saying it should be a huge bonus, but it should still be a noticable bonus, maybe a noticebly higher accuracy and maybe a tiny dmg bonus.
But also along with that they should implement forts which guilds could hold and other guilds coul raid. The forts could have ramparts which would give the guild holding the fortress the height advantage which in turn would require the attackers to think out their attack a bit more.
And maybe for the team to take over the fortress they need to get into the center of the fort and access some terminal to take it over, or maybe it could require an engineer ( or someone with high comp lit) to take hold of the fort.
Also i think the incentive of holding a fort might be that it accumulates some money or maybe gives a stat buff to the guild.
i know that this idea is kind of vague, i just think that it would give more of a purpose to guilds rather than just a social thing.
Tell me what you guys think of this idea.
I'd like comments on what you guys think might be bad about the idea and what would be good or make it better.
