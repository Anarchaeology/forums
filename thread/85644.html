My Little experiement
HI everyone. I have Just put up a new set of forums online that I was hoping could be usefull to the story writers and role players.

Basically it is a fan fiction and event planning forum. Now, I know that we already have these forums, and they seem the most logical place to post that kind of stuff, but there are a few reasons I set these new forums up.

Here is what I am thinking. hear me out

1. It not only has sections for AO fan fiction, but also for unrelated fan fiction written by AO players. I like this cause it gives a option to show what other ideas float in your head, and won't clutter these forums

2. Events planning. The event planning area is private, so you need specific access (which is not a hard thing to get. all you have to do is let me know you want access) which means events can be planned without spoiling it for people who stumble accross your post. As well, it's main purpose would be for different groups to discuss possible ideas for larger scale events, involving clan, omni, neut..whatever.

3. Events secton planning has seperate forums for each server, so there is less confusion as to who you are planning with and for where.

4. NPC section, so you can volunteer to play NPC roles in peoples events, as well so event planners can see who will be able to help them out with NPC roles.

5. A AO related news section, which I know is not such a big deal, but you can post your own articles and read other peoples articles.

6. Moderation. Well, although it is a moderated forum, I believe it is a little less strict than the official forums. That is good and bad I guess, but I have a system in place to handle it and all posters will be informed as to why something was changed if it was deemed offensive or I recieve complaints. But I like to keep people informed so I won't be just going and changing things on you without your knowlegde.

7. there is a really easy to use PM system built in to the forums, so you can communicate in private with others who post. I love this feature personally. anyhoo..

so anyway...there you go as to why I Created this forum. I suppose I won't know if it is a waste of time or if the community will use it for a bit, but I invite all of you to join up and use this as a tool.

I in no way want to benefit from this, or expect that people should pay me special attention. I was just hoping to offer something usefull to the community

I hope you check it out and use it, and if not no worries. It will be there either way.

http://www.anderson-web.com/cgi-bin/jyabb/yabb/YaBB.cgi

is the address. Thanks, that is all
