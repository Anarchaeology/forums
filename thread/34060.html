This is one of the saddest days in my gaming career. I actually wish for Anarchy Online to fail quickly. The events of the past year have shown me how horribly a game can be run.

I am saddened to see such a great promise shattered by inept handling.

I realized the date myself and only had to laugh. Just December and January we thought things were getting better. The Story was rolling along and bugs were getting fixed.

We end up now a half year later and the game is turning into "EverQuest meets Dark Ages of Camelot". I've never had worse feelings for a company than I have for FunCom as a whole.

They've disrespected their customers by providing lackluster service in many areas, not the least of which is taking care and consideration and providing a degree of professionalism when loading up the code editor. Lack of originality in terms of game design and lack of effort with respect to providing a product one can be proud of top off the list.

I won't go into Customer Service. I'll only briefly mention the board wipes.

This year has caused me to re-double efforts towards treating clients I have to deal with in my business life with respect and honesty. I have been treated like a worthless dog by a company I have sent well over a hundred dollars. I never want anybody who deals with me as a customer to have to endure the feelings that FunCom has caused me to endure.

I'm now at the stage where I can't wait for my friends in-game to decide where to go next. I have the feeling of wasting a year of time/money/energy similar to when you know you are going to break up a relationship that you had worked hard at and thought had long term implications. Yes, this does feel like I'm breaking up with my girlfriend.

This has been a horrible year. It had a shining period from October to about February.

Something happened. The screws fell loose and now the game is out of control. A game that was fun to play has been systematically turned into a sorry rehash of the stale and tired 'levelling treadmill' so many of us are horribly sick of.

Good luck to all the decent people at FunCom who know what makes a real game (assuming you still work there). Tommy Strand, where ever you are, I know you must be heartbroken.

This is a very Unhappy Birthday, indeed.
