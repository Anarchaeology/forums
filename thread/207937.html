Notum Infused Suit Could Allow Nanomages to Leave Planet
Teeku

An Omni Sub-department unveils research into technology that may free Nanomages from Rubi-ka at recent Science Symposium.

A recent Science Symposium was a huge success, with topics ranging from mining operations to the protective qualities of toothpicks against giant killer reets. One of the more intriguing subjects was the development of an environmental suit allowing Nanomages to leave the planet, which is also a technology that may not be readily available to Clans or Neutrals for sometime.

Towards the end of the Symposium Director Kithrak announced the Omni-Tek guild, Red Tape and Magi-Teks scientists have been working on an environmental suit for the last half year that could potentially allow Nanomages the ability to leave the planet. The suit hopes to create a completely enclosed environment that would recreate the atmospheric conditions on Rubi-Ka.

The notum, that all Nanomages are dependent upon for survival, would be supplied from a pressurized canister on the back of the suit. In previous tests, the suit has replicated the Rubi-Kan atmosphere so well that it has actually created a more ideal environment. Market analysists projects that the suit will be not only very expensive, but also popular among the Nanomage population.

When asked about the availability of the suit to Clan and Neutral Nanomages Kithrak replied, This will be a patented Omni-Tek Corp. technology. That will be for the Omni-Marketing to decide. Obviously not what the few Clan and Neutral Nanomages attending wanted to hear. Later I asked Clan General Arikel her opinions about the Clans gaining access to the suit, but she seemed more interested in the rate at which the alchohol was leaving her cup.

Do you think there is a need for the Clans to start work on an environmental suit of their own? I asked. Arikel answered by saying, Tough to say, but I dont think so. I mean, when we talk about the Clans, were talking about a diverse assortment of individual organizations, many of whom arent necessarily interested in the well being of the Nanomage race. Given the limitations of the Clan scientific community and the fact that we dont know just yet whether the suit is going to be available to Clan or Neutrals just yet, I think our resources are best spent in other areas, and waiting to see what happens.

While no direct answer was given from Director Kithrak on the availability of the suit to Clans and Neutrals, there is a very strong option that we wont have access if it is released. However, that didnt seem to dampen General Ariekels spirits. After all, how many Omni-Tek technologies have seriously remained in Omni-Tek hands? Odds are theyll license it out if nothing else. Or someone will just steal it and duplicated it themselves. It worked with the notum mining equipment."
