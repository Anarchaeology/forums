visions (warning, objects in mirror are farther away than they may appear)
Ok, this IS the envisioned board, so I'd just like to get my 15 minutes of fame, and list all my envisioned.

I could off course just have called this post: "If I had infinite time and money, and ran Funcom"

- Trading items into computer terminals actually has an effect on the economy... i.e. supply and demand

- Reverse Engineering is in the game... I can take stuff apart, and my skill in engineering has an effect on how many parts I get out, and how many may be destroyed in the process

- Weapon manuals are no longer in the game... if I want to learn how to build something, I'll have to buy the manual from another player, or take the thing apart myself, so many times, that there is no doubt as to which parts go into it.

- Weapon/item/stim/whatever construction does not only have a requirement, but it is quite possible for me at a higher level of skill, to influence the final result. Not just the QL of the highest part, defines this.

- All trade skills get a timer... it now takes time to turn this monster part into plasma, just as it takes time to build a nanocrystal. IN RETURN... trade skills suddenly get a major boost in the exp gained. The delay timer is used to prevent abuse. At the same time, I can actually use the time I spend waiting for a team or similar on something valuable... and don't tell me this is not doable Funcom

- Trade skill usage is no longer so limited in the QL range of the parts that go into it, making it easier to build items, and find the components. On the other hand, items may be lower general quality, something that my supreme skill in trade skills is able to offset at least somewhat.

- There's a mission setting on the terminals called Individual <-> Faction. The faction missions are harder, take place in 25% areas, maybe even in enemy territory, but on the other hand they have an actual effect on the game. Examples include, but are in no way limited to: The faction which first completes 10000 faction specific missions, gain access to some faction specific item/weapon/armor. This could be written in as part of the storyline, could be used repeatedly, and would allow people from all levels to actually participate.

- It's possible to upgrade your guild's standing with Omni/Clan provided that you have a regular performance rating in Faction specific missions



I guess this is part one... if anyone has comments, post away. I haven't done the details here, because.... well quite honestly, until someone shows an interest, then I won't bother with a doctorate on the mechanics of modern economics as applicable to virtual worlds.
On the other hand, if someone here is interested in number-crunching, or if (perish the thought) a Funcom representative indicates an interest in any of my ideas, I'd gladly give them the details

/NetSapiens
