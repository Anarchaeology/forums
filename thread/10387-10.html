As long as I can solo 50% missions up to lvl150 Im happy.
This I think should be the goal to balance the classes agaist, not PvP or stuff like that.

So, how do I define soloable?

#1 First 3 mob room
This is normaly the hardest part in the mission. If you get lucky you get no or lowlvl mobs... This part is ok if you need to zone several times to kill them all. After all, they are 3!

#2 Normal 1v1 combat
Against a 50% mob you should NEVER need to zone&heal to be able to kill it, if you do something is not tuned right.
And that should be with a nonoptimal char, not a IP perfect, uber twinked one. This to ensure that casual players can still play and enoy the game. Yes, for the twinks it will be easy... nothing to do about that!




#3 progressive mob dificulty
As it is now, people level like bats out of ....., this I think is due to the fact that mobs difficulty doesn't scale up fast enough. Was is needed here is a system that looks at char/team lvl and changes the mob behavior after that. Ex, for each 10% higher the mob is against the player, you increase the mob casting by 25%. This includes healing, dots, dds and so on. For noncasting mobs, like Anuns, something else is needed.


Hum.... I wouldn't mind a lowering of the 10% xp cap to 5% or lower. Normaly no players should hit that 10%... A lowering of that limit will also hamper exploits somewhat... those people just can't get the full benefit of the uber xp mob they just killed!

All that I ask is that you don't turn AO into Everquest.

In everquest, all through the game, you have to have full health in order to kill something two to three levels under you. Then, once it's dead, you have to sit for 15-20 minutes to regain your health/mana before starting again. The only way to get ahead in the game is to:

1) Get in a group and camp rare spawns (which is difficult to do because everything in the game that is worth camping, is already camped)

2) Twink your character (which is even more difficult to do because you either need to know someone who plays a higher level character on the same server as you who is willing to give you better equipment or have a higher level character yourself that you can do this with)

3) E-bay!

In Anarchy Online, you can solo just about any mob in the game that is even or lower level than yourself. You don't have to worry about camping, because you can just do missions and know ahead of time what your reward will be. Grouping would be icing on the cake, it would just be fun to get in a group, do a dungeon which had an uber mob at the end.

In regards to the social aspect of the game: If I want social interaction, I can just go to one of the many bars for my wining and dining needs. Think about it... would you rather talk in the middle of a desert while wearing combat armor, or in a nice club wearing the latest duds? I think you have my answer.
