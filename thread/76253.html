Do Neutrals Stand A Chance?
There is a finite amount of areas to be controlled you have two roughly equal powerful sides Omni and Clan then you have a small number of neutrals.
If the goal of Omni or the Clans is to dominate the planet then it will be easier with an edge, obviously . Now in battles between Omnis and Clans other people rush in from all sides to give an even match, or closeish to one, and I would imagine that as more peeps get the booster that this will become increasingly more equal in response. But If Omni decideds to go all out against the Neutrals they can gain a slight but distinct advantage over the Clans. Of cousre the reverse is true. So do the neurtals stand a chace?
People from both sides have argued both ways as to wheather the neutrals should be left alone and respected as neutral or be considered the enemy and as such killed. But by and large they tend to be left alone or not thought of.
But will people have the same attitude to the Neutrals taking a share of resources or will the attitude be if ya wanna be neurtal then don't mine. Already Neutrals have taken to mining in areas dominated by and acepted as Clan teritory is this because they get more sympathcy from the clans?
As locations for mining are taken will wither side tolerate the Neutrals in what they see as their areas?

A few thoughts on this.

Attack a Neutral installation and you now bring down the entire Neutral community upon your towers. Unlike attacking the tower of a single Clan or Omni detachment, you are effectively attacking 5% of the playerbase when deciding to destroy any Neutral tower.

With Neutrals holding only about 8 locations, it might be wiser to simply attack a single installation on the opposite side of the conflict and deal with only that guild or a joint defense force for a couple days. The Neutrals have a very long memory and without many bases to defend in the first place will likely be willing to pester your towers till the servers shut down.

Although the Neutrals may lack the huger superguilds, they do indeed have enough 170+ players to put together a single 20 to 30 attack team at a given time. The target would not be chosen randomly or even strategically from the various locations available. It would most likely be against the guild that took down another Neutral's installation.
