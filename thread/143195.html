On Professions and Diversity  A Reminder to Funcom Designers (warning: long)
Disclaimer: This post is specifically focused on discussion concerning the professions that rely on weapon damage and which have a skill template that allows for multiple, viable paths in the current game.

There are several professions in the AO world that have templates that create more than one viable weapon path in the game that lead to diversity of the profession. These skills are either favorable in skill rate, can be buffed by the profession, and/or have weapons that allow the templates to scope in favor of a particular set of skills.

Many players will agree that they want to look different, that they want options in which path they choose. Unfortunately it appears (and players can only react to what they see and what the developers speak) that Funcom has taken a stance on a only one path system. This is evidenced in the description of the Keeper and the Shade professions: Keepers will use 2h Edged weapons and Shades will use Piercing weapons (possibly in conjunction with Martial Arts, maybe there is hope). Also, support for two of the three professions (the Adventurer and Soldier) with the widest versatility in weapon choices seem to be focusing them in on one or two options which given the reported skill value maximums for Shadowlands of 2000+ could reduce people following a currently viable path, but not cookie cutter path, to under performance at the best or gimpdom at the worst.

Adventurers have their strongest skills (through IP only) in Pistol, 1h Edged, and Multi Melee. Also viable in the skill template for Adventures is 1h Blunt, Piercing, 2h Blunt, Melee Energy, Assault Rifle, Rifle, and Multi Range. They receive buffs for 1h Edge, Pistol, Multi Melee, and Multi Range only, however. But Adventurers also have Profession-restricted weapons for Piercing, 2h Edged, Melee Energy, and 1h Blunt. But without buffs and continued support for these weapon options, an Adventurer would be a fool to pursue these weapons since they need high skill to have high Attack Rating which enables one to hit and do damage. Adventurers are also the only other profession with a decent Assault Rifle template, but are generally being locked out of many the viable Assault Rifles, detracting from pursuit this path.

Soldiers have the strongest skills in Heavy Weapons, Pistol, Assault Rifle, and Ranged Energy Weapons with Grenade, SMG, Shotgun, and Multi Range being very viable IP investments. They have buffs for Rifle, Pistol, AR, Shotgun and RE and a buff that hits all ranged weapon skills. Soldiers also have a Profession-locked pistol, which is one of the best pistols in the game pre-Title 5. The room for diversity in the Soldier weapon skill template is second only to the Enforcer. However, despite assurances from Funcom for support in the way of buffs, Soldiers still lack weapon skill buffs for SMG, HW, and MR, and still lack a high end buff for Pistol and Rifle to balance the budget. Also, the only new weapons aimed at Soldiers have been AR-based weapons.

In contrast, Enforcers have a diverse skill template on par with the Adventurer and Soldier templates, but Enforces received buffs for all weapon skills (equal buffs for the 4 main lines  1h Edged, 2h Edged, 1h Blunt, and 2h Blunt  and also near equal buffs for Melee Energy and Piercing) and weapons to use these skills such as the Slayerdroid Crystal Claw, Improved Tango Dirk, and Frost Scythe of the Legionnaire (taking the Enforcers away from the 1h Blunt and 2h Blunt only paths). Only Melee Energy seems to have been neglected, but 1h Edged also lacks high level support. The key point here is that Enforcers should be the standard that diverse templates follow in that the diverse template profession should have buffs for their alternate skills equal to their current lines and weapons utilizing these skills.

Moving forward to Shadowlands it is imperative that Funcom provide equal Perk benefits and skill buffs through nanos and items to these professions. Failure to do so will cater to a world with very little diversity where profession means not only what nanos you use but also what exact weapon you will equip. I do not believe the player base wants to live in a world where Adventurers only use 1h Edged or Pistols, MAs only use fist, Enforcers only use 2h Blunt, Soldiers only use Assault Rifles, and Agents only use Rifles.

Unfortunately without the full support in buffs and perks, players will be forced into the mold in order to remain viable. The reason all boils down to Attack Rating, or AMS, which determines both damage and hit chance. The game will be balanced for the highest AMS possible for a profession using the cookie-cutter mold. If Adventurers can hit 1800 Pistol, then 1800 will be the standard used to balance Adventurers. The Adventurer with the unsupported and capped at 1200 Rifle will have a very difficult time playing, meaning s/he either has to abandon the character concept and conform to the cookie-cutter mold or play a new character.

The only way to address this is for the designers to support the viable paths of the diverse template professionss. This includes Adventurers (1h Edged, 2h Edged, Melee Energy, Piercing, Blunt, Pistol, Rifle and Assault Rifle), Agents (Bow, Pistol, Rifle, and Sharp Object), Enforcers (1h Edged, 2h Edged, Piercing, 1h Blunt, 2h Blunt, and Melee Energy), Engineers (Grenade and Pistol), Martial Artists (Martial Arts, Bow, Piercing, 1h Edged, 2h Edge, 1h Blunt, and Sharp Object), and Soldiers (Heavy Weapons, Grenade, Pistol, Assault Rifle, Rifle, Shotgun, SMG, and Ranged Energy). On behalf of the players with characters that add to diversity, I ask that Funcom not forget us in the move forward with AO.

Remember that this trend is not just a threat to combat skills and options but can also apply to other aspects of the game (such as trade skills and how Funcom seems to be aiming that only the Engineer and Trader play styles should have access to high end trade skills).

MBS is not an Attack Rating cap (that is a common misnomer). Rather it is a cap on the max damage you can do from AR. Also, the 1k+ Attacking Rating multipliers also only affect damage.

Therefore Attack Rating still affects your ability to hit and to crit. Plus there's the issue of equipping items which is based on skill, and completely a separate issue to Attack Rating.
