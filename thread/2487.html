I'm a crat, and I like my profession. You have to take every prof. for what they are though, if you compare your chosen prof. to everyone else it will seem sucky.

If you accept that every profession has it's own advantages you'll enjoy the game so much more

well...
I started an MP 2 weeks back. I have not gone back from this char. She is now lv 61. I still only have lv 50 implants, but thats ok you dont need them. Once you get to the stage where you can use the mastery line of nanos, the whole world opens up and you can then use red pets.

My main tips for using an MP are, keep your bar on full def, stay back from combat when u send you pet in to attack, (and if you are soloing) keep an eye on /pet report so that you dont get attacked with no healing pet on you ! You are readily accepted into teams for your healing pet, if you set it to heal the tank. If you get into trouble, you can always pull it to heal you, then set it back to the tank. I have noticed recently people asking for an MP over a doctor. It might just be the teams i have been with, but as i have been so addicted to my MP i havent broken out my Doc to find out.

They truly are the most enjoyable class i have played. (and i have played one of each char now). However, if you are after PvP, i cannot advise you on how they go. I havent tried my MP in PvP.

The main reason for making my MP origionally is because they look so cool when they FLY !! hehe :-)
