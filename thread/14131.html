Create Mission
One of the main reasons I was excited over AO was how I was promised I could create my own missions.

I do realise you de-activated it for exploit reason, but this is one of your promised feature and after 8 MONTHS, this feature is still not in-game!

To tell you the truth, I had intended to remain a faithful AO subscribers for years and told myself at launch, that it would take 6 months for the game to work properly, but seing so many basic features that were promised at launch and that are not yet implemented makes me wonder how much longuer I will stay!

The fact remains that FC distributed the game and boosted some of its features on the box, in the manual and on web sites.

While these features were mention, it did not say that said features would be implemented in a year or activated in a year, therefor, I question the legality of selling a product that does not include all it claims.

Now, I could find my manual and box and enumerate the features promised that aren t in the game, but I feel it would be a waste of my time, just mentionning what is missing in the clan organisation would be a pain.

There is no denying it, AO is the most technicaly advance MMORPG out there, but sooner or later you will loose alot of customers because of these types of issues.

There is no way that players will accept features such as guild housing and Create mission that were suppose to be included at launch as new features or content.

Come to think of it, you justified the monthly rate of the game as part of your ongoing events team, well I can safely say I have not seen more events in your game that I have seen in other games of the genre.

One can only wonder, why we are paying more for AO then other similar games, why?

PS: I wouldn t mind paying 50$ a month if I only had the promised services. (
