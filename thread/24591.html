Dungeon and hunting solution
Ok, I have been dying to go to a dungeon for several levels now but I can not find anyone willing to make a dungeon group. I have been wanting to go hunting, but nobody can afford to go hunting or take the risk of dying out in BFE only to have a 20 minute run back to the camp, at level 47 there are a lot of yalmahaless people.

My solution is this, drop the HP on mobs in dungeons by 1/2 or more. Increase the amount of mobs, in dungeons. Lower the aggro range of mobs in dungeons, and add wandering mobs who follow set paths inside the dungeons to make a little more variety over set spawns. Change the loot tables to allow good loot to drop off any mobs in the dungeon, allow boss mobs to have a much higher rate of dropping special items.

As for hunting, introduce spawns near outposts that gradually increase in difficulty the farther outwards from an outpost you go, keep them away from main roads. Introduce XP saving pills as a trade skill item, let them save a percentage of XP as originally intended. Make all mobs drop six times the amount of loot they currently drop. I am talking credits, items, everything. If you have 6 people hunting together, they only get loot 1 out of 6 kills. This will make hunting in groups against high level mobs worth the risk. Currently many nano intensive classes cannot afford to hunt, missioning is the only option because of the cost of new nanos for all the levels that you gain while hunting. Increase the XP earned in PvP zones propotionate to the risk involved. Lower the aggro range of fauna. Remember the old saying "They are more afraid of you than you are of them?" Well generally in nature, if one of a group of creatures is about to get eaten, the rest of them scatter to the four winds, not gang up and attempt to attack the predators.

Finally, I hope dynamic camps are introduced into the game. I would love to be running to a mission out in Greater Tir County one day and see an Omni-Tek special ops camp appear over a hill rise... perhaps even reward tokens to anybody in a group who manages to destroy the Omni-Tek super mega grid disruptor particle cannon sitting in camoflage under the main tent...ya thats teh stuff.


In short -- NO MORE MISSIONS PLEASE!
