Is Training a Bannable Offense?
First of all, let me say I'm *not* asking this because of a certain incident in Camelot.
Second of all, let me say that personally, I think it is a lame and inacceptable way to deal with the enemy.
I also want to add that I'm only talking 25%-zones here, I think there is no doubt that it is bannable in 75%.
The discussion arose last night in our guild, and there are few ppl (but not as few as I'd wish) who think in 25% zones all means to kill off the enemy are acceptable, and that includes mob training.
I'm only talking about repeated training as well, as if it is a one-time-thing who is to say if it was an emergency run or really a train?
We are an org with very few rules, but one of those rules is not to exploit or behave in any manner that could get you banned and I threatened ppl with kicking them should I hear they've been training.
Before I resort to doing that I'd really like an official stance on this. Thank you!

Training mobs because you are outnumbered and try to run away without being able somehow to /shout that your are accidentally training in a given direction, is clumsy and can piss a lot of people off. That's it.

Training a group of mobs in such a way that you have the intention of making them aggro a single player or a team (for example by running to him / them and terminating) is an offense.

If it's easier you can compare this to the difference between killing someone in PvP and shouting "I OwNz U Dud3 !" and camping a reclaim terminal killing 15 times the same guy shouting "Oh I FoCk U soooo hard !!!".

I am no official by any means, but hope that helps.
