An Offer to the Clans
After reading Ammicus' notes on The State of Rubi Ka, it got me to thinking. I said to myself, "Self, there must be a better way" and this lead to the usual thoughts of chopping off the heads of Clanners and such, but these were all old ideas - fun ideas, but it's like "been there, done that, got the t-shirt." So, after much thinking, I have a proposal for you Clanners.

I would like to buy Omni-2 from you. That's right, no shooting, no hitting, no space leaches and bloodletting, just straight up credits. I will give each Clanner 30 credits and two Pestilence Rollerrats to move out of Omni-2 and turn over any land holdings they may have to me. I feel that this is a very fair exchange since you Clanners paid nothing for the property and, in fact, were able to make use of all the buildings and utilities already in existence when you fouled up the place... er... "moved in." You are essentially gaining 100% profit from the deal.

As for the future of Omni-2, I plan to open up a Yalm fuel refinery plant. This will be an ideal location for my venture since the existing housing will make for an excellent campus atmosphere for my employees and the nearby waterways will work well for disposing of byproducts.

All you have to do is sign your name below and state, "Yes! I wish to take advantage of this incredible offer! You are the best, Nevver! The BEST!"

Tell your friends!
