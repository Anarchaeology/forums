Slow Business
Whealer sits behind his large antique desk, hand carved from the best cherry woods that could be found. He reclines slightly as he glances through the business analysis handed to him by Boinky. After careful review Whealer sets the report down on his desk and looks up.

"Lovely desk don't you think?"

"Sure is boss."

Whealer glances around the room looking at his arms collection displayed along the North and east walls. He had always taken an interest in weapons and was proficient with most any he picked up. Reorganizing his thoughts he looks over again to his advisor and long time college and friend.

"It appears that business has dropped again. Any guess as to the cause this time?"

"Well, we have been cut off from a large portion of possible clients when the Newland City Council hired in Ms. Warr and her crew. We've also invested a lot of money into the Shadowlands project headed by Solstiare."

"True. How is that project going anyway?"

"Better then expected. We've already begun researching the fiery area know as Inferno, and investigation is being done into the possible existence of a possible area yet unknown to us."

"Very, good. That still leaves us the problem of Ms. Warr though doesn't it? Why exactly didn't we question the hiring of her in the first place?"

"Well, if my memory serves me correctly Elite Operations but up a large sum of money to hire them in an attempt to protect the city from the Sentinels."

"And?"

"Well, the whole deal was done rather hush, hush."

"I see. Well, perhaps we need to convince the city of Newland that they are not as safe as they feel with Ms. Warr as there guard."

"Sir?"

An ominous silence fills the room as Whealer picks up a True Katana lying on a display of deep red silk.

"You know there is a common misconception about Katanas: That they should be fast and light. This is totally wrong - the original katana was rather slow and heavy, made for killing with one blow, not several small slashes - like a short sword."

"Whealer, you don't mean what I think you mean do you?"

Whealer grasps the Katana with both hands and swings towards Boinky's head stopping inches from his neck.

"Do whatever is necessary."
