Making Guilds more interesting
I just posted this in the mass suggestions thread and then realised it was probably never gonna get read, as I typed up a bit of an essay I thought it was worth reposting, so here goes :

This is probably never gonna get read, and it may have allready been said but as A Guild Leader These are some of the things I think of as ESSENTIAL to be able to run my guild better.

1) Guild housing - I don't care if we all have to share the same door or whatever but we need this, and we need to have ones that are bigger / smaller and vary in cost / upkeep

Guild housing needs :

1a) The abilty to invite people not in your guild inside (maybe kind of like the way you invite people into your home.)

1b) A meeting hall - a place with chairs centred round a table for meetings (the size may be related to guild size or how much you pay for your housing

1c) A reception

1d) guild shop / vault - This is vital it could either take the form a a vault into which anyone can drop items and guild leader / ranks leader sets can determine the value or amount of items that can be taken at any time, and members would have to replace those items or items to that value in the bank / vault before they can take any more. If it is possible to implement then being able to mark items that have been placed in the guild bank as Guild owned and not be able to be sold / traded

The other option would be a guild shop, and this may be a little harder to implement, basically your guild leader could set a general rate which could be greater or less than the normal shops, when you put items in you are paid creds from the guild bank, when items are resold the money goes back into the bank. Again guild leaders should be able to set up a limit on the amount of Identicle items that can be in the shop at any time, and if the guild bank has no money, then it can't offer you anything if your trying to sell. The guild shop could be used by people from other guilds, provided they have been allowed into your guild house.

I am sick of trying to manage a load of backpacks in my bank and inv to find items for guildies that I have collected or been donated.

1e) At least 1 office (maybe more offices the more you pay)

Offices need to be attached to accesing a terminal that allows you to control guild functions - the leader set up what ranks in the guilds have access to what - it may be he wants his Generals (for example) to have access to the same functions he has. Functions should include granting access to guild vaults / rates on guild shop items. ANYTHING I have said or will say a leader / leaders should be able to decide on should be accessable here, from a special interface window, that is ONLY accessible from there.

1f) A grid terminal

1g) Workshops - places where members can create tradeskill items, maybe they get an area effect buff on tradeskills when they are in the workshop

1h) Traing area A small 0% area where members can practise pvp, maybe a small area like a backyard in which players can kill monsters for exp, but gain no money or loot. The monsters spawning there could depend on the levels of players there.

1i) A club / recreation area - this would greatly be enhanced by the adding of casino's and casino style minigames and maybe some sort of trading card game - which could also give players something to do outside there housing - while waiting for afk team members / while team leader is recuting / while people are derezzing / while people are camping mobs etc

I see a - f as being essential to every guild, g to i should be available to the Guilds paying the highest prices for their housing, and maybe some of the less essnetials (b - f) should also be upgrades.

2) Message of the day - this small feature would be so helpfull in keeping the whole guild informed of recent events.

3) Land control - being dealt with in the booster pack

4) some sort of allinece system to make Alliences more official, maybe include a chat channel for the higher ranks of the associate guilds, and access to each others housing.

5) some sort of Guild War system basically the opposite of an allience, with the option of sending rival guilds treatuies. Even simple things like Your members may not attack our members or the treaty is broken would be cool.

6) Some sort of Guild medals/rewards system - adds no buffs or bonuses to players to prevent exploiting but aloow guild leaders to give medals/rewards to players that other players can see (this may be in the form of general Medals or specialised medals - guild leader could set what they are called and a description of what they are for

Ok thats all I can think of right now, I'm sure I had more ideas but I can't think right now. Ideas 4 and 5 may not be implementable and are definatley less important than all the ideas related to guild housing. Also 1i) has suggestions that may or may not be implementable but could have a postive effect on the game as a whole, little gambling mini games or a trading card game may not be original but could draw people into the clubs (in the case of mini games) and make downtime more enjoyable (in the case of the trading card game)

Using the chat commands as a means to mamnage members, taxes and voting is poor, time consuming and annoying we need the offices and some sort of leaders interface, even if it only handled the commands that we allready have, it would be a great impoovement.

Like I said I dunno if this will get read BUT PLEASE look at making guilds more interesting, and easier to run Inside the game ie, me not being responsible for endless backpacks, and my own pieces of paper in which I have scrawled notes on my guildies.
