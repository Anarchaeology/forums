Why the clanners need to be punished
I feel that all clanners need to be punished as lawbreakers, for two main reasons:

1) They are trying to commit armed robbery.

You want proof of that? Just look at the northern section of the planet. They have managed to steal that much. They call it theirs, but I beg to differ. After all, who made the planet habitable? Thats right, Omni-Tek. Rubi-Ka (or at least it's air and life) are the property of Omni-Tek, and the clanners are doing their best to steal it, using force if needed.

2) They broke their contract.

You want proof of that? Every human (or roughly human equivalent) on the planet is either now, or a descendent of, an Omni-Tek employee, and as such, they, like me, must have signed a contract of employment. So either they, or their ancestors, broke their employment contract with Omni-Tek. It doesn't take a lawyer to see that. They were employees under contract; now they don't work (for Omni-Tek).

-----------

Their goes the Clanner's so-called Moral Superiority. How can you be morally superior if you are a contract-breaking thief? It simply cannot be done. I feel that Omni-Tek should evacuate all their employees, and blast the planet into shreds (as per the loophole in the I.C.C contract), and build a swarm of robots to scour the dustcloud for notium. It would probably be more efficient than trying to fight a war when nobody on either side can die (unless our skilled hackers break into the Clan insurance companies and release a virus that will wipe all the insurance records).
