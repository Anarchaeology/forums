These prices apply to RK2 - prices on RK1 may vary.

Stuff I sold at real low levels to make cash:

QL 10 (exactly) Concrete cushions - 100k each
Shades - 10k
Novaflows & Support Beams - 1k per QL
Anima nanos - 1k per QL
Engi pet heal nanos - 1k per QL
Senpai Armor Coats - 1k per QL

Pretty sure there's other stuff, too. If you want to do armor suits do Omni-Pol Recruit Issue if you're omni, and sell for 10k per QL for a full set. For instance a full suit of QL 35 = 350k. Remember to get both sleeves and if the armor is a type that comes with a back slot item, include that as well.

That's all stuff you can do with clicksaver.

If you are doing team missions, web cloaks, sealed gun containers (both of which are chest loot), kirch kevlar, X-3 rifles, etc sell quite well.

Don't bother with selling individual pieces of armor at low QLs unless it's a boss drop bit like Kirch.

And don't got crazy on the pricing because often your buyers will be low levels who are strapped for cash (Unless you're selling cushions, which are mostly bought by higher level players).

Using the above methods I had a yalm at level 30 on my first character. Good luck!
