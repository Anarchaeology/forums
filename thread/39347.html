The Great Old Ones...
(OOC: First off, a question. Are you familiar with the works of one Howard Phillips Lovecraft? If not, stop reading. This post will make no sense. If you are, please continue.)

My name is Ottus "Kyres" Ritchert. And I write this only because time is running out. I have seen horrors and blastphamies that the Opifex mind was never ment to comprehend. We thought that in science we were safe. That through the use of technology we could fathom the secrets of the universe and make ourselves as gods. But what we uncovered was secret for a reason...

It started when I was taking a walk through the Pleasent Meadows seeking monster parts I could sell. After a few hours with little success, I started back toward my home city of Omni-1. At this moment, the sun became obscured from my view. I then thought it simply the passing from day to night, but as I checked my interall timekeeper, it showed clearly that it was 3:00 in the afternoon. "Hmm, strange..." I thought to myself. Luckily I still had my compass so finding the nearest road in this darkness would not be difficult. Or so I thought.

I soon found that whatever was causing this unnatural darkness was playing with my compass heading as well. The map cordinantes that I used to determin my location kept changing. I stumbled on through this daytime night, hopeing that I might come across a recognizable landmark. What seemed like an eternity had passed with me feeling my way along the ground like a blind Nanomage when I saw a group of lights in the distance. I picked up and ran toward the lights, thinking they were the lights of 20K. Sadly, this was not the case.

As I came closer to the lights, I saw in their glow the shape of what could only be described as earth's Stonehenge. Within this circle of stones sat 12 figures in black robes. I could not make out what they were saying, even with my hearing enhancments, so I made the biggest mistake of my life. I moved in for a closer look.

With expert stealth and soundlessness aided by nano machines, I moved toward the circle of stones. When I finally came within hearing distance, I sat myself down and listened to the figure's speach. Even this close, it was hard to make anything out. It was as if they were speaking in a strange tongue my translater could not recognize. I heard words such as "Cthulhu", "Tsathoggua", and "Yog-Sothoth". These words filled me with a great sense of dread, though I did not know why. The figures then stood up and their chanting increased in volume. I had no clue as to the consequences of my actions then, but the entire situation was simply wrong, and I felt I had to do something. Taking aim, I fired my rifle at the nearest black-robed figure. The shot passed straight through him!

Their chanting stopped and all 12 hooded faces turned to look at me. There are few words in any language to describe what I saw next. The group moved toward me and I turned to run, but one of the figures had already placed himself in my path! He threw off his cloak and I beheld a face so hideous it could not have been human or anything else made by the creator. Where eyes should have been there were two blood red thorns portruding from the skull. Where the mouth was, only a limp piece of skin. The remainder of his body consisted of rotting flesh in which living worms corsed in and out of. I gave a scream of terror, dropped my rifle and ran as fast as my legs could carry me.

Strangly enough, these creatures did not give chase, and after a minute of hard running, the sky returned to its normal, afternoon briliance. I, however, did not stop until I had reached my apartment in Omni-Entertainment and colapsed on the floor, exausted. It was much later, when I finally awoke. At first I scolded myself, "How could you run when you've faced things much worse then, well, whatever it was, in the past?" But for some reason I could not put their infernal chanting out of my head. It had seemed strange and yet familiar. Like I had heard those words before.

It then dawned on me. I had SEEN those words before. Going over to my bookshelf (I always enjoyed the feel of a good book in one's hand and reading it then just simply having the thing placed in one's NCU) I purused my collection until I found what I was looking for, "The Call of Cthulhu" by H.P. Lovecraft.

"So it was all fiction," I thought, "a group of wannabe cultists playing at summoning Elder Gods."

However, I could not put the feeling of uneasiness aside. And I could not deny what I had seen. I resolved to return to Pleasent Meadows to investegate the site of the creatures actions. I fould it easily enough, this time with no darkness and no black robed figures. But it was what I found at the center of the circle of stones that led me to write this letter and so chills me to the very core. At the center of the stones was my name writen in blood with the words, "Tsathoggua has need of your blood. We will come for you at midnight."

As I write this note, midnight is approaching fast. Soon they will come for me and I will be subject to horrors unimaginable. I went to Omni-Pol with this information, but they have done nothing. I can only pray that this note reaches someone who cares and can stop whatever is happening on Rubi-Ka. But if this is what I think it is, we are doomed from the start. Maybe if...wait a minute...I hear a knock on the door...it must be them! I can see the door open! They are coming! They are com....
