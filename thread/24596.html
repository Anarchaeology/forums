Make Crit damage class and level based.
Rather than base crit damage on the weapon it should be based on the players class and level. Make the damage four times your level or QL of the weapon (whichever is lower) with a favored weapon and three times with other weapons. Each class of course has its on favored weapon set. Soldiers favor all ranged weapons except bows.
Enfocers favor all melee weapons.
Fixers favor SMGs.
Traders favor shotguns
Adventuers favor one handed sword and pistols
Docs, NTs, and crats favor pistols.
MAs favor unarmed and bows.

I've never played an MP and I dont see many with weapons so I don't know what are class weapons for them. Blunt I guess so they can use the staves and shields.

This would make levels and class count for more and slightly lower the effect of twinking. A lvl 200 soldier with a QL 200 Novaflow would crit for 800 extra damage but a lvl 100 soldier with the same rifle would only do 400.
