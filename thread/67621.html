Apartments
As a citizen of Rubi-ka I feel that it is our rights as such to live and work in any apartment, shack and home that we wish to. In any city or county we feel suits or individual needs.

I suggest that apartments should be capable of being traded, sold, and purchased.

The benefit to the game will be to control character population in some of the more populated cities and townships.

Secondly I suggest that apartments and their furnishings be more interactive.

As in furnishings that are storage devices need to be useable as they are designed to, you need to have use of chairs, beds, and tables again as they are intended to be used.

I would also like to suggest that we be given the opportunity to loot the mission areas of their furnishings.

This would have to be within reason, carrying capacity based limitations most likely.

Furthermore I suggest we be given the capability of inviting our teams and individuals in to our homes.

This would give everyone the ability to meet in private, and create safe houses in hostile areas. It will also allow married couples to share living quarters.

Having a large walk-in closet with limited storage capacity just promotes a nomadic life style. We as individuals have a need to base our selfs out of our homes. The Clans and their underground movements need this more so.

The benefit to the game will be to control character population in some of the more populated cities and townships.
For me, the interesting aspect of cities is exactly the population density. It is simply much more fun with a whole crowd of people doing their thing and/or interacting. The only reason you'd want to limit it is due to the technical problem of the server being unable to handle the load. So, limiting poplulation in an area is a work-around to the meta problem of supporting the environment, not a desirable feature to be sought after.

In terms of apartments, I'd like to live directly downtown in Trade. The highrises are too far out of the way for me, and I wouldn't use one even it they worked.

If they worked, and were conveniently located, then yes, I like to see all the features of a real apartment: beds, sofas, tables, kitchen appliances, etc. etc. I'd especially like a closet with my clothes accessible so I could dress in a well lit area.

Being able to have friends over would be an excellent incentive to be creative and furnish your home with style. Then new rare items that had nothing to do with combat could become valuable. Perhaps people would be more inclined to take off their armor then too.
