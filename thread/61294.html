There are some really sweet tiny 0% areas in the middle of nowhere in 75% zones. In fact it almost looks like FC did this on purpose to encourage some PvP in some of the grander outdoors spots. Two examples are a waterfall in Broken Shores (can you imagine fighting on a vertical playing field?) and the mesa of a rock tower at Last Turn I think it's called. It's on the SW corner of Deep Artery Valley and is it's own little zone. No monster spawns in either spot, they are ripe for PvP =D

Granted these places have no save/reclaim spots to get back to, so a tournament is not really a good place for them. But what about the idea of something like ... a 'Guild Wars' event.

6 on 6 in some of these remote places. Last team standing (or partially standing wins).

Not sure what a prize/reward would be other than fun and some practice for Booster Pack =D
