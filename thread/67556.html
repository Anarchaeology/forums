Thanks for the link, Omegadsl, the article does have information that is interesting and useful to me!

I'm excited about getting a date (11/28) and I'm excited to get more info on some towers. It is interesting to read about the new professions and profession specialization.


But, why aren't more people excited? I'm not sure about everyone, but I am a bit tired of false starts from FC. I have wasted time posting about issues that turn into nothing. I'm not going to do it again. So, I am cautious about what I post about.

Along the same vein, when there is an issue that is important and garners significant interest FC isn't very forthcoming about answering questions that pop up in the forums. So, we end up answering each others questions (with our own guesses!). I like seeing other peoples guesses and ideas, but it gets a bit frustrating when their is no official info.

The lack of official info on specific issues related to Notum Wars is frustrating! The last post by Gaute on Land Control in the Notum Wars was lacking in a great many details, and the forum posts requesting more info went unanswered (as far as I could tell). So, what is the point? When Notum Wars is realeased we will get our answers. And you can see from the number of posts in the Suggestions area that people are more-than-willing to discuss concrete gameplay issues. Notum Wars has no concrete gameplay issue at this time. It is entirely speculation.

For instance, what are the skill requirements to cast a Controller? And, if you are in a Organization, can you cast a Controller, or does the leader of the Organization have to approve placement of Controllers? Can any member of an Org cast a Shield Disruptor? Or, does the Org leader have to cast it? THESE ARE VITALLY IMPORTANT ISSUES! But, we have no idea how they will be handled. Thus, as an Organization, we have no way to prepare for Notum Wars. When Notum Wars is available, we will have to figure out what it means, and try to figure out how our Org wants to be involved. Its sucks! I imagine Notum Wars issues will destroy many an Org. Not from battle, but from internal struggles over the gameplay ramifications!

So, we sit and we wait. FC won't give us detailed advance info. So, we hope for the best. What is there to talk about?
