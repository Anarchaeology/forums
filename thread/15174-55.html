I am am new player to AO though not to Online games (think the old text based ones like MUDD through till today!)

There seem to two issues to the debate of OE and twinking.

OE is where your ONE of the REQUIREMENTS of a weapon/ability is more that 20% above skill of the character.

WHY?? if you are able to self buff great! there are nano's that add up to 20 points for EVERYONE....these are available to everyone (yes yes 61 skills (bearing in mind that skills for title level one cap at 70ish))

I know another game that allows TWINKS. Asherons Call.

That game is now owned by an elite class that can travel anywhere pretty much without fear of anything, and is reliant on people knowing exactly which skills to take AT CREATION to have any chance at all of lvling at the highest levels.

TWINKING IN MANY ONLINE GAMES IS ILLEGAL!!!!!

What is TWINKING?? Twinking is the movement of money or equipment from a superior character to a lesser (it is also called an illegal interaction between two characters on the same account - for older gamers).
