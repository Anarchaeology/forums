1st Team PvP Tourney
Well, seeing as I'm pants at PvP, I though the least I could do is to help those that are good at it. Theses are purely an idea at this stage, and any comments on what goes and what doesn't go would be greatly appreciated.

The Tourney is 4 separate championships, firstly taking place at Omni-Ents arena:

1. Team Minor

&nbsp;&nbsp;1.1 Teams must consist of 5 or 6 players.
&nbsp;&nbsp;1.2 Teams must not total more than 100 levels for the whole team.
&nbsp;&nbsp;1.3. Individuals in a team must be level 24 or lower.
&nbsp;&nbsp;1.4. No outside buffing is allowed.
&nbsp;&nbsp;1.5. The following items are also not allowed:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1.5.1. Nano-Critters
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1.5.2. Symbio-Grafts
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1.5.3. Blister Packs
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1.5.4. Pills
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1.5.5. Nano-Fingers
&nbsp;&nbsp;1.6. Only one of each class may be in a team. (i.e. 2 Doctors are not allowed)

2. Team Major

&nbsp;&nbsp;2.1 Teams must consist of 5 or 6 players.
&nbsp;&nbsp;2.2 Teams must not total more than 300 levels for the whole team.
&nbsp;&nbsp;2.3. Individuals in a team must be level 74 or lower.
&nbsp;&nbsp;2.4. No outside buffing is allowed.
&nbsp;&nbsp;2.5. The following items are also not allowed:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2.5.1. Nano-Critters
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2.5.2. Symbio-Grafts
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2.5.3. Blister Packs
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2.5.4. Pills
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2.5.5. Nano-Fingers
&nbsp;&nbsp;2.6. Only one of each class may be in a team. (i.e. 2 Doctors are not allowed)

3. Team Twink

&nbsp;&nbsp;3.1 Teams must consist of 5 or 6 players.
&nbsp;&nbsp;3.2 Teams must not total more than 60 levels for the whole team.
&nbsp;&nbsp;3.3. Individuals in a team must be level 15 or lower.
&nbsp;&nbsp;3.4. Outside buffing is allowed.
&nbsp;&nbsp;3.5. Only one of each class may be in a team. (i.e. 2 Doctors are not allowed)
&nbsp;&nbsp;3.5. Pets of an attacking nature are not allowed.

4. Team Open

&nbsp;&nbsp;4.1 Teams must consist of 5 or 6 players.
&nbsp;&nbsp;4.2 Teams may total any amount of levels.
&nbsp;&nbsp;4.4. No outside buffing is allowed.
&nbsp;&nbsp;4.5. The following items are also not allowed:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;4.5.1. Nano-Critters
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;4.5.2. Symbio-Grafts
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;4.5.3. Blister Packs
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;4.5.4. Pills
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;4.5.5. Nano-Fingers
&nbsp;&nbsp;4.6. Only one of each class may be in a team. (i.e. 2 Doctors are not allowed)

A team wins when they have done any of the following:

a) All members of the opposing team have died and/or have fled from the arena.
b) In the case that people from both teams are alive; but cannot compete in combat, the team with the highest total of levels still alive will be declared the winner.

Pet Classes should note that you must cast your pets once you have been buffed by your team. You will not be allowed to use pets that have been created prior to the event.

Let me know what you reckon...
