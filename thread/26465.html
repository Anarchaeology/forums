Redo Specials In PvP
Just now was chatting with a guildmate who was sitting at a reclaim booth. He had been killed by a level 150 soldier's alpha strike, which did over 5k damage for an insta kill. Granted, he was level 90ish, but if that had been me, or another low health breed, it would have been instant death.
How can we, as agents, stand up to that these days?
That 100% crit nano will not help, as it does not last that long, and that is assuming you are lucky enough to get it.
A: Make our 1 special be able to do upwards of 120% of a persons damage.
B: Make all specials do no more than 40% total, no matter how many they have.
C: Make multiple specials have a timer, i.e. say 1.5-2.5 seconds between use of each. Use full auto, 1.5-2.5 seconds goes by, Burst is used, repeat for anyother special.
I am level 141, agent. This is what is keeping me from pvp'ing. Instant kills. And, I do not want to hear, well, you can do 40% special damage, then a 50% shot which would be 90% total. Sorry, with the guns the way they are, I average in pvp 150-250 damage per regular shot, which is about 1/4 my pvm damage.
And this is with an attack rating of buffed of 947, and using a QL 200 Disafiliation rifle.
And, with agent crit buffs being boss mob drops, I do not expect to see any of them in my inventory any time soon.
Do something about pvp.
