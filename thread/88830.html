The best suggestion I've heard when this type of thread comes up is upgrading General Severus' two SMG's in a similar process to the Hellspinner Shock Cannon upgrades (since all three weapons are found in the same place). A feasible dual-wielding setup for TL3/4 would be IP intensive enough that a player would actually have to choose between very good damage (dual-wielding the new Severus SMG's) vs. very good defense (GA). Until, of course, TL5, where a player has IP to spare and could get a second upgrade of the guns to keep them viable.
