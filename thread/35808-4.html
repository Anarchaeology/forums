I understand that agents were not meant to be masters of the arena...we are long range experts and the arenas are far too small to utilize our advantages with range (roots, excellent distant weapons). I can also put out tons of damage for a short period of time so all my other options should be used to setup that moment of high impact!

Do I have any chance? Would I have a chance if there were no bracers? Yes. I could adapt if I could do damage.

I don't think PvP should ever be thought of as class A should be able to take any other class on any given day. I like to think of it more realistically...Class A can take B but would be hard pressed to take class C. This is how real military engagements are handled, this is how sports teams overcome advesaries etc. This adds an element of strategy to PvP where the ability to think and react, instead of just standing there trying to outdamage *insert any profession*, will rule the day.
