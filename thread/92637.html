Funcom, having laid off staff a number of times over the past year - when the player population was much higher (and hence had more money coming in from subscriptions), now has a smaller subscription paying player population and hence cannot afford for any/many more people to leave, if they want to avoid making any more redundancies, or hell, even surviving.

Sadly, and I wish it were otherwise, I don't think any company can exist on a player population consisting solely of RPers. In a perverse way, we (the RPers) need the kidz, griefers, idiots etc, for any company hosting a MMORPG to financially exist - and hence give us somewhere to RP.

A RPing community (given we're usually small) - doesn't pay the bills.

A Level-Junky and/or PvP community (significantly larger) - does pay the bills.

Why do you think the story and RP content has slowly dwindled dwindled dwindled over AO's lifespan, whilst the PvP and levelling content has been added added added?

I think Funcom had a great idea, AO with a 4-year story, loads of content etc. But I don't think it financially works, given the to few of us who care about those kinds of things vs. the costs of operating a business on that model.

Arguably, you could say the same about Atrox here on AO. Every 'warrior' seems to be a 'trox here. Every 'warrior' on SWG will likely be a wookie.

I'm excluding RPers and the more realistic/serious players from that statement.

quote:
Originally posted by Viray
Eh, I like AO... we create this world, we breath life into it from a vast history, heck we even make some of that up...

StarWars has history, a past, present, and future... its all laid out.

At least here we get a chance to do what we will.
As you say, one could argue that only 'local' events & RP will take place and happen on SWG, and players won't control or affect anything on a galactic scale or to do with the theme. Nor will there be any storyline (other than the time period it's set in, between SW and ESB).

But then... here on AO... we only affect our own 'local' events & RP anyway, and there are no players who've made any decisions or had any effect on 'The Story'. Currently, the storyline on AO pretty much matches what SWG will have, i.e. there isn't one.

As I wrote in another post somewhen, back in the day I used to say exactly the same things that you've just said. But simply put, they're not true.

That was me looking at AO through rose tinted glasses and wanting to dislike/hate SWG on principal, ever loving the former and slamming the latter, finding reasons that are convenient to do both.

Listen, I'm not panning AO. I'm just trying to point out, virtually every bad point levelled at SWG can be levelled at AO, and virtually every one of AO's alleged saving graces can be said of SWG too.

AO's one truly unique saving grace, its killer plus-point, was the ever evolving world, evolving through the once-touted now-forgotten 4 year storyline.

If the 'golden age' of Halloween 2001 to mid-Feb 2002 came back fulltime as opposed to coming back for 3 day appearances before disappearing for another 3 months (repeat ad infinitum), then AO would have it's killer edge again.

Without it, AO is just another MMORPG.

All be it one with (the remnants of) a great RP community.
