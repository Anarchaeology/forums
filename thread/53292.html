Use the news service...
This is something for the events team...not anything for the devolpers to deal with.

Rubi-Ka has poltics, the CoT, Omni HQ, the Clans of the CoT and Omni-Tek departments, but all of these elements come into such limited play in the actual game. Now and then, someone will bring out one of these elements, deliver a scripted speech, and then there will be nothing for weeks.

Outside of these events, Rubi-Ka is pretty uninteresting planet.

So here it is...inspired by an event. One day, I stumbled upon the body of Legchopper in an odd place (Varmint Woods maybe? Don't remember) he was lootable, but had nothing on him. And his body didn't fade away. After poking awile I sent a petition with his location and went about my business.

So then, find some story elements...some real story elements, like the death of the unknown stranger awile back. But put them where anyone can find them.

Someone discovers a body along the road somewhere, they should be able to report it and somone from the ARK/GM staff or events team able to respond as a member of Omni-Pol or the Sentinals, whichever is approiate. And don't just do the ARK/GM poof out of thin air thing. Show up in a Yal, a Kodiak, etc. Confine yourselves to the limits imposed on players (for the most part anyway)

Bounty Hunts

Missing Persons

Dead Bodies

Loan sharks collecting on loans

Assisting with the smuggling of SOL Products (they have to get onto Rubi-Ka somehow...hehe...mmm...cookies)

Simple message courries through hostile
(0% and 25%) areas...with other players on the lookout for the courrier in order to stop them. (C'mon FunCom, you know you want to give us a real reason to PvP) Hmm...do something like force the carrier to save, then give them 1% XP, then an item. If they don't make it, item is lost to PvP rules. If they make it, carrier gets to keep XP. (Keep logs...the GMs aren't 100% trust worthy...I've seen a fixer in grid armor running around with a SOL Fire Executioner. C'mon!)

Heck, even expanding the Clan and Omni missions to be alinged with FunCom's official
Omni-Tek departments and Major clans and clan structures would be an improvment.

Some of these can use the news system obviously, some players will just have to be randomly find.

Then again, maybe this is all going on and I've just been missing out for 154 levels.
