Stay with your friends!

Get into a team with some other people around your level (remember that the PvP bracket of the whole team, is the same as the PvP bracket of the highest level player in that team) and stick together on the battlefield. Concentrate your firepower on the same target as your friends, and protect each other with heals and such.

Communication is important. Telling your team "I'm being attacked by <name>" or "<name> is a trader, kill kill kill!" can help them pick out their next target.
