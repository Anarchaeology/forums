I explained in depth why Tarabot was taken down in:

http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=90282

The fact is, very few people have a real sense of integrity. In real world situations, people learn the limits to which they can abuse other people without long term consequences. These consequences can be very harsh, which results in the general public having a common level of integrity. In a role-playing game, internet relay chat, and any other online community, there is a layer of abstraction through anonymity that protects the participants from long term consequences. Subsequently, people that do not have internally-driven integrity will fall to the whims of their greed or other faulty character traits when the opportunity presents itself.

You can spend all day, all month, all year, or whatever trying to find technology solutions around these problems. You will get nowhere. Integrity issues should be handled by the community, not by game restrictions.

What I would like to see is the development of tools to enable the players to deal with other players like this. An example would be the ability to maintain private and public blacklists at the individual and organization levels. This is something I've been considering as part of our organization bot, but I would love to see it as a function of the game. People would be far less likely to ninja an item or be abusive ingame if it resulted in a record in three different organization blacklists with "ninja looted azure breastplate" that any player could read at any time.

If there are no social consequences to these actions, people will continue to abuse the technology to get what they want. It makes no difference how much time and effort you spend writing code to "fix" the problem.
