The ship gave a sickening lurch as we entered Hida’s workshop, and I had to grab the doorframe to keep from stumbling. The place was a wreck – it looked like a huge piece of plassteel had exploded. The place was strewn with bits and pieces of who-knew-what.

I glanced over, and there was Hida, getting undressed in the middle of the room. “Erm… what do you think you’re doing?”, I asked. He said something about armor, as he moved behind a screen in the corner of the room.

When he emerged a few minutes later, I had to bite my lip hard to keep from laughing. He was dressed like some knight of old, in brown armor with red detailing. He even had his wrench attached to his belt where a sword would be. A helmet tucked under one arm completed the ensemble.

“Somebody get me a tranquilizer gun”, I thought to myself wryly, “looks like we’ve got a live one here’. Ah well, it takes all kinds, I suppose….

Even as I was thinking this, he was rummaging around in a bag, and pulling out pieces of some worn-out looking armor. “Here, try on some of this”, he said, “it may save your life”. Oh, terrific, just what I wanted to wear, somebody else’s nasty old used armor. But… he was right. So, I tried not to think about the probably countless unwashed bodies that had sweated inside it, and let him fit it on me. I must say, he did behave himself – no wandering hands. He really was a nice guy, after all….

The armor wasn’t as bad as I expected, much easier to move around in than I thought it would be. As I was admiring the blue color, he opened a large footlocker, and started pulling out weapons. “You ever used a gun before?” Hmm… only if target practice at the range with Dad, back when I was twelve counts… I nodded, and he handed me a pair of pistols, and a holster. I fastened this around my hips, and took a look inside the trunk. I chose a laser rifle, and a SMG, thinking they looked fairly simple. They didn’t look that difficult to use in the movies. In the meantime, Hida was similarly equipping himself. Opening another, smaller trunk, he handed me ammo clips for my weapons, and showed me how to load them. Then he collected what he would need for his weapons, and we left the workshop.

I was more and more impressed with this guy. Clearly, he wasn’t the usual engineering nerd. I wondered how often he’d come across this, working for Trans-Galactic. Either he was a real conspiracy-theorist sort of guy, the paranoid sort, with a mini-arsenal, and his own militia… or this wasn’t the first time TG had something like this happen. Hmmmmm…makes you think….

As we turned the corner, a man screamed from somewhere close by…a terrible, blood-curdling scream. It made the hair on the back of my neck stand up. As Hida and I looked quickly at one another, the scream was suddenly cut off. He put his finger to his lips, and I nodded. We slowed down, trying to move silently up the hallway
