*** about 12 hours later ***

Oh my god, oh my god, OH MY GOD! The most awful, heinous, disgusting... where do I start?

I went down to the buffet, and found, to my disgust, it was mostly seafood. I HATE seafood. They had the most disgusting things, too, looked like giant roaches - and most of the damned passengers were scarfing them down like they were the most delicious things. Wearing bibs, dripping butter. I wanted to retch.
I stuck with the salad.

The dinner show wasn't half bad... singing and dancing various showtunes with an ocean theme...

After the show, I crashed, only to be awakened a few hours later by frantic knocking on the door. I cracked the door open, since I wasn't really dressed. There was a man there, he introduced himself, Hidareign. Apparently he was now the acting Chief Engineer, since most of the crew had acute food poisoning. As did nearly every last passenger.

Everybody who scarfed down those sea-roach things...

He was talking rapidly, and I wasn't even half awake. "just a sec", I mumbled, "let me get dressed". I pulled on some clothes, and let him in. I held up my hand, forestalling any more conversation, and pressed a button on a panel. Immediately, the fragrance of fresh-brewing coffee filled my room. I poured two cups, handed him one. He accepted it, and we settled down to talk. The long and short of it was, they had three engineers, including him, who hadn't succumbed to seafood-barf-fits, which left them desperately short-handed. Would I consider doing working for Trans-Galactic, until we arrived at Rubi-Ka?

HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! Trans-Galactic, asking *me* for favors!

Then I looked at Hida's face. He looked really tired, and really stressed. And... if the spacecraft didn't have its engineers, how could we expect to safely arrive on Rubi-Ka?

"Very well", I said, "I'll help. But I'll need to have you show me around the place." Something else occurred to me, "What about all these sick people? Are they being taken care of?"

"Yes", was his response, "fortunately, the medical staff, minus a nurse or two, didn't eat the tainted food. And the armed escort, they're at about half-strength at the moment, but they're able to man their spacecraft."

So I wouldn't have to hold somebody's hair out of their face while they barfed up sea-roaches. I hoped the relief wasn't too apparent on my face. I finished off my coffee, and got to my feet. He did too. "Well then, shall we get my orientation started?", I smiled at him. He nodded, and we left my room.

I was about halfway to the quarterdeck, when I remembered Dolphie. I sincerely hoped she was all right....
