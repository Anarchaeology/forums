Stromm, time and experience gained on mobs 20-30 levels above your own level versus time and experience gained on mobs 5-20 levels above your own level. I've found that when soloing the latter yields more experience, and tends to be less frustrating.

At 192 when I solo a Real Mean OT Diversionist (level 205ish) the process takes about 3-5 minutes and yields about 100k experience. Whereas, when I solo the same type mob in Veteran form (level 180-195ish) I can usually kill 3 in the same 3-5 mins for about 135k experience. Same holds true when I solo with my level 50ish trader. I can solo level 70ish mobs as well as level 60ish mobs. But, the time to experience ratio doesn't justify soloing the level 70ish mobs. So, I solo the 60ish mobs. They don't yield as much experience, but I'm killing more and faster which in turn makes up for the lack of higher xp per mob. (hope that made sense)

In otherwords, I think you're better off attacking mobs 5-20 levels above your own level instead of worrying about double hits.

I've found that on my alts under level 70, I can gain levels solo at twice the rate I can in pickup teams by just running regular indiv missions. The difference is experience. Lower-level folks are often still figuring things out and learning, which reallyslows things down. They make more mistakes, etc.

Sometimes I'm in the mood and have some time, so I'll actually get into a low-level team. If they let me lead and show/tell them some tricks and methods, things tend to go more smoothly. If not, I end up frustrated and/or the team ends up breaking up for any number of reasons.

It's not really elitist, it's just that I'm used to teaming with folks that are more experienced, already know the game mechanics, know how to play their characters most effectively and know how to work in a team.
