You need to meet the multi-ranged requirement of the Defender in your left hand. If it has NO requirement (just a multi-melee one), then it's >1000 and is pretty much unreachable. Defenders multi-range skill becomes insane past Q125. So when dual-wielding Defenders, you can wield one that's as high as your Shotgun skill allows, and a low one for your off-hand.
