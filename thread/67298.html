Red Tape/Omni-Admin is hireing.
Greetings citizens of Rubi-Ka, Red Tape a sub-department of Omni-Admin is hireing. We are specifically seeking to bolster our Security forces and Supplies and Acqusition departments to work on a new research project. Only loyal employees seeking to build a brighter future for Omni-Tek need apply. As a member organization of OTSEC the security of Omni-Tek is of our upmost concern.

We have had a distinguished history in politics and offer our employees exposure to many resume building opportunities. Below are the two departments seeking recruits.

Security
Red Tape Security will be used to protect fellow Omni-Tek employees and innocents from the ravages of the violent clans. The Security department is only deployed for defensive or sanctioned operations. Those seeking to join in the mindless cycle of violence will be dissappionted.

Interest in politics but not being afraid to use force should the need arise a plus. Having recieved a title for the containment of clanners a minus.

Currently this department's leader position is open.

Acqusition and Supplies
This department maintains and develops new technologies designed to enchance a wide variety of things. They are the record keepers and maintain the department inventory.

Currently, there is a new project in development and we are seeking people with skills in notum manipulation and/or knowledge of the insurance systems. People with good record keeping skills are also highly desired.

Currently this department's leader position is open.

Security and R&D not your cup of tea you say? Then take a look at the other departments Red Tape has to offer. Pre-existing requirements for recruitment still apply for more information see our Red Tape Recruitment Flier

For more information please contact either /tell Kithrak or Deuscloud or Beria

[OOC Note: We are an RP oriented guild. You will need to go through an interview to determine your elegibility. There will be a trial period and you will be expected to attend guild meetings to stay informed of current happenings. We are not pvp oriented.

The interviews usually take about 15-30 minutes.

Powergamers need not apply.]
