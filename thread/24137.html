Story Mission & Sub-Plots
After reading the post by FC, that talked about story missions I began to wonder as to how FC would come up with the multitude of stories for the missions and other sub-plots without them getting as repetitive as their Solo and Teams missions are now?

Will the players have the option to write small scenarios for Anarchy? When I played Cyberpunk, Shadowrun and Gurps I wrote scenarios for people to play in all the time or I bought modules for them to play in.
If FC would allow this we as players would have somthing to do in between the major storyline events. This is a planet, and the only things I see going on are the few major storyline events a few events put on by guilds and such.
If FC allows this to happen, I wonder how to exactly implement it.
We write stories, then:
1. FC has the task of working it in?
2. We use a mission building program to design the whole thing
and then submit it to FC for approval. If approved FC places in-
game and everyone has the same odds of running across it.
This eliminates a possibility for exploiters.

In my opinion 2 sounds like the best answer for FC and the players. However, will FC take the time to write a Misson Builder for us to use. Thats the big question.
If they did they could concentrate on their major storyline and game mechanics and we could amuse ourselves with our own small scenarios.
Too make this tastier for FC, I would pay for a mission builder!
Anyone have any ideas on this?
