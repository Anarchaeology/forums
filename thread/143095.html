Low Level Tower Battles Balancing Suggestion
As we all know, low level tower battles have degenerated into twink wars and exploits of game mechanics. Twinks with uber NCU and uber outside buffs are dominating tower battles and make bases undefendable and undefeatable.

I suggest we put level requirements on all outside buffs on the recipient. Not at casting time, mind you. But as soon as a player becomes PvP enabled, a level requirements check is performed and all buffs that a player doesn't meet the requirements for are terminated automatically.

I realize this is a new mechanic to implement. However, I believe it is a single, simple to understand and elegant solution to our somewhat flawed PvP system.

A new item "Combat Pass"

-Equip item in any util slot
-All beneficial buffs are cancelled when fist equipped.
-You will only be able to receive buffs from those within your PvP range.
-Allows you to attack another player or tower when within a tower controlled area.
