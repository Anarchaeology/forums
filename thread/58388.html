Some arena Rules
Obviously i dont expect people to agree with these rules or for them to ever be used, but this is just basically what i would like to see them like and maybe you can all give yur ideas on some rules.

Tir Arena - Now that there are no hostile guards in TIR so even low levels can whompa there i think this arena should be a twink only arena where people can only fight if it has been agreed beforehand and all fights must be 1vs1 unless both fighters agree otherwise. Also leaving the arena during battle is not allowed unless you are running away to zone, trying to confuse your opponent by leaving the arena is not allowed. Obviously non-twinks are allowed to come here also, prehaps if they want to team up against a really good player (as long as the player agrees)

Ent arena - This should be made accesable to clanners and be used for non-twink pvp (by this i just mean pvm players wanting to have a quick fight with a friend, or players which do not have a gun twice their level). Other than that the same rules apply as above.

New arena in Newland desert - This is just the same as the arenas are now, anything goes basically and ganking is allowed. This arena is just a patch of ground with some of those red flag thingys around it.

25% zones - People in these zones kill whoever they can regardless of level (this just depends how nice you are) but killing somone in one of these zones is not to be considered wrong (unless they are on same faction)
