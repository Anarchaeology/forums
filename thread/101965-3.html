"If God created the Heaven and the Earth. Then God created these gods of which you speak."

That is what I would call retro active rhetoric. Those words/beliefs sprang from three ancient particularly violent earth cults. The "one god" that these cults followed was once only a god of one tribal people,More or less you have a war god of a nation.

What I find amusing is the three ancient cults I have studied supposedly followed the same god, they claimed was one, YET they murdered each other with more abandon than any non believer.

Ancient earth history is fractured but it does refute the first of these one god cults, were older than pantheons, and even other faiths that pointed to a non sentient energy that bound all, etc.

My point is, what you state is formal rhetorical teachings of the cults, it has no historical weight.

Gods were first personal to particular peoples and cultures, monotheism was a developement. Of course all nations and peoples have claimed their god is the right one, and the one on the side of 'good'.

I happen to agree with you on one point, that all beings, supernatural and natural did extend from one source. I do not think that source was sentient, male, looked like us, and was quite contrary. Personification of the inscrutable One is romantic tripe, often drummed up merely to lend power to certain groups and corrupt inviduals by motivating the masses and to legitimize their abuse/exploitations.

As you have said, there is no one answer, however the history and action of the three one god cults refute their claims to mercy justice and good. I will wage war against any and all of them I encounter.
