Tutoring calculations are a little off...
What burns me the most about tutoring is you can never really be a *useful* expert in this skill. For you to use a QL 200 tutoring device [1000 skill], you would need:

719 skill (max with maxed stats)
150 from the backpack
20 from expertise
which leave you with a deficit of 111.

While you can add implants to improve your tutoring over 1000, you then suffer by REMOVING the implants for the skills you were trying to boost in the first place (So, in order for you get your tutoring over 1k to give you a +50 boost to Mechanical Engineering, you have to remove your +103 Mechanical Engineering Implant!). I'd like to see tutoring be QL*4 instead of QL*5 (that way folks without the pioneers packs can still reach near-max tutoring, and folks with it don't have to remove all their useful implants to buff tutoring). 800 skill to boost my Mechanical Engineer for 2 minutes makes sense to me...not 1000...

Incorrect.

The other possibilities in that spot are Aimed Shot, Map Navigation, Rifle, and Vehicle Airborne. As a martial arts engineer, I have -no- use for any of those. Well, maybe when I get my yalm But I can switch it out easily enough if I'm as high level as you when I'm doing tradeskills.

The Ear Bright spot is even easier. Concealment, Psychology, and again, Vehicle Airborne. For an engineer, this is a no brainer, again, unless you have a yalm

Faded Head - shared with Perception, Sense, Trap Disarm, and yet again, Vehicle Airborne. Damn, they REALLY don't want good tutorers to fly yalms, do they? Still, for the faded spot, I will go with trap disarm.

But now, at level 67, I have tutoring in both shining eye and bright ear as standards.

I consider tutoring a VERY useful skill. I'm willing to bet the IP cost of taking one green skill to 500 is a lot cheaper than, say, taking 7 green to light-blue skills from 400 to 425 (for example).

I am heavily into tradeskills tho. I play solo most of the time, but I convert all my own monster parts (pharmatech), make most of my own implants (I do get someone to put the shining clusters in for me, but heck, I should expect to when putting in QL110 implants at level 60!) which covers nanoprogramming, I make jewelry out of all the rings and gems I find (mech engineering). Sometimes I'll use the comp lit one, may get me access to one or two more grid points or put in a better NCU/belt. Those tutoring devices I use all the time. The others I use more rarely, such as Chemistry when making my aggression trimmer, but hey, they're there, and they are IP I don't have to spend yet directly on those skills. At level 67, I hoard every IP I can. Later on, when I'll be swimming in IP, it won't matter anyway, but I'll sure have gotten my use out of 'em.

I wouldn't really recommend it if you're not going to be using more than one or two tradeskills tho. As someone who plans on getting decent at all of them, though, tutoring is very useful.
