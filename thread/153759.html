More Fair Distribution of Crafted Armor to all Skillsets
As a opifex trader, there is very little in the way of crafted armor that I can use - and precious little looted armor.

There are several strength or stamina based armors that can be made. The only agility strength set caps out at QL 130.

In order to fit into Barter Armor I would have to train counter to the strengths of my race. The only viable agility/strength sets out there right now are flowers and servants of the eight. Agility/Intell would be the best for me, but that combo does not even exist.

Please add some nice crafted armor that will cater to all of the races and classes instead of making several sets that suit just one or two races/classes.

Even doing different versions of some of the existing armors would be a step in the right direction.
