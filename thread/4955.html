repost Not truely roleplaying
I deeply apologize for the last post here is my repost in an easier to read format. Ok I got some concerns that I think players need to be aware of. Ok first off we all know that anarchy online is a skill based MMORPG we like this idea that is why we are playing and investing our time and money in it. Now we all also know how difficult it is to use equipment that we need for our lvl. First you need implants and skills to use those implants and buffs needed to use implants, armor, and weapons which all need skills as well. All good indeed but what happened to our trade skills. What would make it worthwhile to invest costly IP's and credits in. Now comp lit and nano programming maybe worthwhile to invest in based on your profession. However what about other professions that have light blue to green trade skills, for instance a fixer most of the trade skills are light blue, or for a better example a trader with lots of green trade skills. Now a trader would make sense to invest in trade skills so he could trade, hence, a trader. Now what would make it worth his or her while to invest in these green skills whereas he has mostly dark blue other skills. What can he or she do to use to trade skills to trade for loot or credits that no one wants to trade for, hence making the trade skills not worth the expensive IP's or credits to maintain. Now the trader is not using his trade skills to trade therefore not truely roleplaying, because its not worth while. The same thing applies to other professions. In my opinion trade skills should be worthwhile to the professions that the trade skills apply to for both high lvl and low lvl players, and thus turning the game to a truely roleplaying game as well as making players happy in that they have the pleasure of roleplaying at the same time enhancing his or her character from the low lvls to the high lvls and be worth our while to invest those IP's and credits in trade skills. Making to the game more enjoyable to those that have just started the game and for those that are high in lvl and have been playing for a long while. Hence a truely roleplaying.
