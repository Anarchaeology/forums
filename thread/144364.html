PvP-Points - Small suggestion.
ATM u need to kill someone with no more than 100 points more/less than u (100 points = 1 title).

I see this as a little problem because I just got 9 registered kills while fighting tonight but only 1 of those kills gave me pvp-points. I have Rookie so all the non-titles and novice's that I killed today where a waste. Sure, "PvP isnt about titles".. but since we have titles in the first place, shouldnt they mean more than they do now?

Right now it seems like noone will get the highest titles before AO ends. Everyone is looking for people with same title as themselves so they can get more points, and I have seen plenty of people that are leaving players with too high or too low title alone..This isnt RP and not getting "pvp-experience" (titles) from killing someone with way higher title than u is really wierd!

Suggestion:

Make it so that:
*I will get pvp-points nomather who I kill.
*I will loose pvp-points nomather who kills me.
*The amount of pvp-points recieved will go up if I kill a high-titled player.
*The amount of pvp-points lost will go up if I get killed by a low-titled player.

What title u have will still matter tho because:

If u have Freshman and u kill an Experienced player u will get the regular points as u get them today but u will allso recieve a bonus because u killed such a high-titled player. The bonus will go down the closer u get to his title and u will recieve no bonus once ure the same title as him or higher.

If u have Experienced and u get killed by a Freshman u will loose the regular points as u do today but u will allso loose extra points because the player that killed u had such a low title compared to u. The amount of extra points lost will go down as he gets closer to ure title and once he is the same title as u or higher, no extra points will be lost.
-------

The point with all of this is simply to make titles mean more than they do today and allso make people want to fight each other nomather what their title is. With this system u will feel good if u kill a high-titled player and u will feel bad if u get killed by a low-titled player. Everyone is gaining/loosing something as they should and this creates more RP.. I would like to believe.

Edit: If FC feel that it should be so and so hard to get a new title then they have the power to change the points recieved/lost so it fits whatever they have in mind.

thoughts
I have thought about this a million times, but I believe that if I'm ever going to criticize something I better have a solution in place to back it up.

In terms of pvp points in general...I do not think you will find one person in the game that can comprehend how a fresh or untitled can kill a higher titled person and receive nothing. It makes no sense whatsoever.
I am not a programmer for AO so I'm not sure how complex the coding is for this, but it seems to me that the way CZ described the point system is in fact accurate (I've tested it on many chars) and doesn't strike me as something that would be that complex to alter. The issue is coming up with an equitable system.
As it stands right now, people seek out others with the same title...I'm not saying that's a serious flaw, but it does tend to take from strategy in tower battles. If you have one side doing nothing but title hunting..then the sway of the battle is completely altered because people are not working toward the great good of the faction..they are working toward personal gain.

Here are two things I'd like to see..and one is infinitely easier than the other I'm guessing:

1. A way to view current pvp points
2. An equitable system that awards points based on lvl/title for all kills. It is a serious game flaw that lower titled players gain nothing from smashing higher titled players.

On the other hand..I'm still unable to come up with that system change suggestion myself...How in the heck can you prevent 3 casts of Kels to make every NT in the game Expert +. As it stands right now you can get Apprentice on an NT faster than any other prof....above that things get trickier for nts, but if they were able to gain pts off all kills ....I have no idea what the result would be.
