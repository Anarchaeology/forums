Lets use a bit of reason here.

NoDrop exists so you can't trade the item. This is done to prevent certain items from proliferating in the game world or to reduce the advantages to camping in the static missions. This also has a hand it stopping certain type of duping.

Unique exists to keep *you* from having more then one of something. This is to prevent imbalances from certain items and to promote diversity of items (would you wear anything other then 2 IQ or 2 Rings of Power if the same type?). They also possibly exisit for certain game mechanics reasons, to prevent multiple activations of the same item without resorting to global timer on your character. That last one is pure speculation however. There is the added bonus of it keeping a lucky spawn from giving a character a unfair advantage in some way (such as a mission generating 3 IQ rings), and also killing any chance of *self* duping items (abet, your bank is considered a completely seperate inventory from you)

NoDrop Uniques combine the two into a neat little package, that works to prevent just about anything bad that can happen with a item happen. Not that it does it 100%, because well in the rat race of hacking, its the rats who are always in the lead.

Getting to the point about shells (and yes, my primary is a Eng), I honestly think that the lack of the multiple shells is primarily a PvP issue. I would just *love* to be able to target someone, hide behind something, and send bot after bot at them till they died. In PvM its the same concept. Sit down, generate 20 bot shells, get at max range vs a deep red, and deploy bots till dead. Those two abilites would just compelte throw the Eng class out of wack against power levels of the other Pet classes, as there is really no "deploy" time on the bot shell, once you have the thing cast.\
