Omni-Pol's Attack on Mort
(Recapped By Trellame And Kylee6)

On Friday the 16th, Omni-Pol agents Forwards Western, Pretium Irenicus, And three other agents, Started rallying
recruits for an attack on mort crator, at the Omni Enterainment Arena. Clan Members were tipped off to the attack, and rallyed their troops as well.
The clan members gathered south of the Sentinals outpost and proceeded to the outpost.Commander LucyGreen Rouse lead the clan defensive. Along with members
from the mockers, Redemption, and Noble Order.Omni-Pol agents gave cords just south of the Outpost and gathered there. The five omni-pol agents and A dozen Omni-Tek Citizens gathered and charged to the outpost.
The Clan defensive was strong, Thirty or so Clan Members attended and attempted to secure ramp to the outpost. As The Omni-Pol agents and citizen approached the outpost and realized they were greatly out numbered.
With the ramp now secure the Clan members had an edge, firing broke out between the to alignments. Lack of organization on the Omni side, contributed to the Losses they would soon have. With the Clans controlling the ramp.
Their was no way for the Omnis to win. ALl five omni-pol agents were killed, along with the rest, of the Omni-Tek citizens, those who survived retreated back into MMD. Commander Lucy Green Rouse praised the clan members for their excellent victory.
Omni-Tek had lost the battle at Mort. What is the next move for Ross, he must now deal with a Loss against clan member with the icc breathing down His neck. What are his next actions, Both sides ask.This proves that Clans Members can come together in times of need. Hopefully the clans have grown together alittle more with with victory....
