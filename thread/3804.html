New mission types
Dunno about everyone else but i'm getting seriously bored with the same old missions, most of mine now are always the brown cave. I never even knew about the subway mission till recently when I helped a friend with his reroll in one. Its been months since I wandered about the mouth one.

Of course you may have some great ones in the works but here are some ideas for new ones:

Use key on ground/orbit teleporter to start mission in the orbiting spacestation/spaceship/satellite/etc. These missions could have large windows with panaromic views of space and Rubi-Ka, multiple floors connected by elevators, etc..

Also, Underwater 'Aquatic base' mission! big windows with panaromic views of the ocean and little fishies swimming past.

Missions in some of the city's skyscrapers- multiple floors, windows with nice views, elevators, stairwells, nice furniture, etc..

Anyway you get the point, missions with great, scenic views!

no more bland brown cave missions! -die brown cave, die , die!
