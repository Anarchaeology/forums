Here are some additions, all low level items:

YES Support 1010 buffs sense +5 starting at QL 1.

Red Line Grenade Launcher buffs ME (best QL is 50) and drops only in dyna-camps I think.

Construction Sleeves for engineers buff tradeskills and are found on architects in the Subway dungeon.

Division 9 Plasmaprojectors are coveted boss-only assault rifles and are valuable in all QL levels.

The Original Electronicum is a boss only pistol of some value favored by casting professions as a back-up weapon since it uses only 1 skill but does decent damage, and without the aggro pulling crits shotguns are prone to. You can buy them up to QL 10 from the second hand peddler vendor in the training grounds so around QL 20 and up are the lowest ones you'd want bother with for resale.

Morphing Memory, also from the Subway, has been known to fetch good prices.

Exarch Robes from Temple of Three Winds can be converted into Inner Sanctum passes and sold to the high level players who aren't refusing to go there in disgust.

Irreparable River MV is a pistol sought after by tradeskillers to make the engi pistol. It can be found in shops sometimes or rolled as mission reward. Robotic Self-Preservation System and Technoscavenger Brain are other low level parts and drop from low level bots. All can fetch good prices from impatient high-level players making the gun who don't want to go looking for them.

Boosted grafts of Sparrow Flight and Enhanced Senses are desirable and sometimes sell for 500k or more.

Notum chips and fragments are always worth saving. Even fairly low level ones can be sold at prices worthwhile for a new player.

The Robot Instruction Disks someone else mentioned are quite desirable and should definitely be included.

Salesman's Chips are QL 20 and of value to traders for making a special hat.

IQ Rings give the wearer an XP percentage bonus from 1% to 7% depending on the type of ring and begin dropping at low levels. I've seen the 3% and 4% type going for a couple mil. They're unique flagged and all types are considered to be the same item by the game so you can only have one at a time.

Advanced Head Skinchip - Physical Protection is a nice item to have if you don't like wearing a smelly helmet around all day. They're probably comparable to the web cloaks in value and they mostly drop from bosses, although I've found a couple in chests recently.

Biomech hats and cloaks can be worn along with the med suit and add some treatment, not sure what the best QLs are though.

There are dozens of mission-only and boss-only nano crystals at low levels which are valuable. Example: Presence of the Master QL 43 is boss loot only and sure to make any martial artist on your gift list happy.

Instruction Disc (Summon Grid Armor Mk I) and Programmed Photon Particle Emitters (Shape Hard or Soft Armor), all QL 60 items, drop in team missions you might be doing at fairly low levels. I hear they sell for a few creds

Sorry you asked yet? One suggestion: you might want to define a QL cut off point for the list or it's going to be a whopper. Great list, thanks for doing it

As for other items a lowbie can get and sell:

Eyemutant parts - Eyemutant Sinews, Eyemutant Eyes, Eyemutant Massive Chest. Sinews are used to make nice Enforcer only armor parts, I personally pay at least 100K a sinew for them. The Eyes I'm not too sure on price but can sell to Engineer twinks, can be made into Eye Orb Laser or something. The Massive Steel Chest makes a great levelling chest armor piece for male (not atrox) characters. It has Str/Psy reqs so not the best for most people anyway, but great PvM ACs.

Monster Parts - While the low level monster parts aren't really worth your time, you will do team missions and come across dead monsters with parts on them. If they are above say QL 100 loot them and store them. If your a char that can get a Pharmacy skill then process them yourself, or becomes friends with a Doctor who can do them for you. They get a bit of XP from it or you can agree to go spare the profits.

Tok farm and make money - If you solo alot or are just token farming, the best way to make some more money from the mission (apart from looting everything) is to roll for Battle Suits. Battle Suits won't usually sell on the player market but they do sell well to vending machines (probably because they count as a full set of armor or something). If you have a trader friend over 25, get a few suits together and get them to sell them to the Trader Specialist Commerce Terminal for the premium it gives.
