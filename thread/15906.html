Ok...Now HERE is a useful fix...
On the first login screen that asks for a user ID and a password, put the focus on the password. I don't want it saved (for obvious reasons), and like most people I only have one account, so why is the focus on the Account DD box?
These days I spend a lot of time entering and re-entering my password on that screen, something I think everyone can attest to. I click on the password box, start typing in my password, and the focus shifts back to the account box. The DD list is filled my old passwords and partials passwords.

Minor, I know, but all I can do is suggest it...
