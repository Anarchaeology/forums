OT Kerans Automatic Grinner versus Mausser Particle Streamer (character lvl < 40)
You guys weren't joking, this is the best weapon for a young fixer. I was using an MPS for a while because I couldn't find a Grinner at the store... then it finally registered... OT = Omni-Tech. I kinda felt like such a dumbass, but I think it had more to do with me never noticing the 'OT' than anything else. So I ran off to Omni-Trade and found one that I could just barely buff into. Then noticed somebody on the shopping network looking for one, and since there just happened to be one of the QL he was looking for purchased and delivered it to him (felt like doing something nice ).

Anyway, tested it right alongside my MPS of similar, if not same level and I was giddy with the results. I know the speed difference is about a second for both guns, inherently... but it just seems like the Grinner still faster than that, perhaps my Ranged Init would have something to do with it? I was getting up to three shots in per attack on me against yellow mobs and the faster burst recycle is certainly refreshing. ...the fastest I could attack with the MPS was at least one attack on the enemy versus one attack against me.

Damage output was similar, although the Grinner seems to hit at it's highest rated damage value more often than not, while the MPS would draw up quite a few low hits. Disease AC versus Proj. AC?

This weapon instantly made my Fixer worlds more fun to play, as I no longer feel helpless with the high rate of fire I'm spitting out. The increased ammo capacity is also very very nice as I don't have to reload between each and every firefight.

This weapon will do very, very nicely until I can get my hands on a Gamma Ejector. I can't wait until I get off my lazy ass and do up a good set of implants so I can equip an even higher QL Grinner.

PS - I created this post as a reference for newer people researching the profession as I know some of this information is something that may be totally obvious to most of you already. I already ran all my searches and read through many threads for this information, but I thought it might be nice to have a thread floating around that you don't have to dig through a ton of crap in order to get to the meat.

Actually, the Kerran's is substantially faster than the MPS.

MPS is 1/3, Kerran's is 1.5/1.5. That's before you aggdef setting and init is factored in. Now the MPS does benefit more from RI than the Kerran's since RI reduces recharge more than attack. But the Kerran's needs very little reduction to it's attack speed to reach 1/1, so you will reach that speed sooner with the Kerran's. Also since the burst recharge time is based on the recharge, that is why the recycle on the Kerran's burst is so fast right off the bat.

It's a great gun at low levels, just a shame the damage scales so poorly at higher QL's (to the point where shooting anyone with reflect and damage shields can cause you more harm than your inflicting).

Reason you will see the Kerran's hit more often in it's higher range than the MPS is the damage type. MPS does projectile damage, mission mobs, and most mobs in general, will have very high projectile AC (as well as energy and melee AC's). Kerran's checks against poison AC, which often seems to be the lower AC on many mobs (though how mob's AC's are generated is not really known to me, this just matches my personal experience).

I'm a huge fan of the Gamma Ejectors. I've been dualing them for about 20 levels now (I'm level 94 at this post) and just when I think they are getting tired, they pick it up again. SMG and Burst have been capped for a while, but whenever I can I put IP into RoF, they get hotter and I stick with them. Also, just like the Kerrans, they hit against an AC that is generally lower on mobs (Radiation in this case).

I don't have any idea what other Fixers do PvM around my level for damage (I have a really hard time finding teams that want a gimpy newb Fixer, so I'm stuck solo most of the time) but my "round trip" for the two Gammas is at the same speed most mobs take one shot, and does an average of 600 damage between the two of them. I'd bet that sounds pretty weak to most Fixers, but I'm not having any trouble toe to toe with same level mobs in missions. Just recently I got my RoF high enough that the Recharge looks about exactly as fast as the attack (the Gammas are a 1/3 cycle for those that haven't seen 'em).

Also, they are very nice for dualing as (if ya' watch the meters) when attacking akimbo time stops for one gun when the other gun goes through its attack time, but both guns move while either is recharging or at the firing stage. This is to say that dualing is better (or more efficient) with weapons that have a low attack vs. low recharge. You sacrifice Special Attack frequency for banking on the Multi Ranged IP (or that's the way I see it).

Keep in mind I'm a newb, and any experienced player will tell you that Akimbo is more for style that effectiveness (and I'm not disputing that) but this might be of help to anyone that is bent on dual wielding (like me ).
