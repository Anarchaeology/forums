I think part of the problem is that currently there is almost no chance of dying after level 150.

I would so much prefer better xp with a greater chance of death. At earlier levels it may feel like you have a high chance of dying... but the later levels are pretty sickeningly easy... that's why so many high level players keep quitting the games... It's out of sheer boredom.

On the flip side new camps are currently being released to help make the high level game good again... but it seems like it wasn't completely thought out. The reports from the test server are that the camps are significantly harder and give a lot less xp than missions in BS... It seems sometimes that risk is not associated with reward. It sounds to me like the player above thought this was funny, because how monotanously easy the missions can be.

I, on the other hand, miss the days in which I had to wonder if I would die... For the first time in over 25 levels I saw someone die in a mission recently... it was also the first time I had to actually use an MK shield on a regular mob... it was kind of neat to meet a challenge for a change... rather than the standard missions now, where have the team can go afk unannounced and no one cares... and anyone in the team can solo most of the mobs we see...

Something needs to change, because eventually the game will get stupidly easy for you too.... saving xp is fine... but trust me... it won't be an issue soon enough... I don't even save between missions anymore... I've had times when I'm tanking boss mobs with 25mil xp on the line and don't care, because I know I have no chance of dying....
