I want bounties on players!
I hate it when someone scr*ws you over and can just skimp out of there like nothing has happened.
Make it so that anyone could put a bounty on everyone else.
And when you have a bounty on your head you are fair game to anyone of opposit alignment anywhere in the world.
Allso make sure you can only have 1 bounty on you at anytime and that you can only be marked for execution once a weak and only be marked by the same player once a month.
Sure you can have all your friends put bounties on someone but make it cost like a million creds so that you cant afford to put bounties on everyone and their mothers.

Lots of ppl will hate this but i think that this would promote good manors and roleplaying to a great extent. I sure woudnt wanna make tons on enemys in broken shores if i knew that every omni there could wax me for some easy (well it would be hard earned cash for the omnis ) cash.

This would allso make guild affinity count as more ppl would be reluctant to mess with you if you are part of a powerfull guild.

Lets say that someone puts a bounty on my head then i will get a msg saying that player x issued a warrant for my head. my option would then be to msg that player and ask him to remove the bounty and appologize for whatever it is that i have done to him or simply face the consiqenses of my actions.

This would ofcource make for unlimited griefing action but if i know that playerx has it in for me i could simply let someone in my guild retaliate or put a bounty hunter on him. There must ofcource be lots of rules or mayhem would ensue.
For example only one player may attack the target within a given lvl range and he would be allowed to retaliate but no outsider could interfer when the fight has started and when the fight is over no one else can target the player untill after 30 minutes has passed or the player has been killed.
