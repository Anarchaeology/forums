Curazon, you might proclaim your innocence here, but what I saw at the scene of the battle does not match the story you presented here.

Regardless of what time you arrived at the battle (And according to the logs, your guild attacked 2 hours after the first 3-4 guilds started their attack), you can not claim to have had more influence on the outcome of the battle than all the other clan guilds combined. Therefore, I feel that the constellation of guilds have more "right" to decide the fate of that piece of land than your guild alone.

I realize that you weren't on the chat during the attack, so you didn't get to take part in the discussion that was had there. To give a brief summary, the land was first offered to Diveristy, since they were the original owners of that piece of land. However, they found that particular spot to be more trouble than what it was worth, so they declined. It was then offered to BD, as they had taken part in many battles recently, and did not yet own a single piece of land themself.

Now it's easy to come and say you didn't know about this, since you weren't on the chat. But the fact that about 30 clanners from various guilds were hanging out in the area for the 20 minutes after the tower blew up should be an indication that plans had been laid for that land. It would be a simple matter to ask someone there in vicinity chat what was going on, and voice your opinion if you disagreed with the decision that had been made. But you choose not to.

Instead, a member of your guild walked a little away from the rest of us and placed a controller down the split second the grace period ran out. Everyone also went dead silent after placing the CT, not willing to answer even polite questions about what was going on. That looked to me like someone trying to make up a cover story before wanting to comment on anything.

I'm sure we all appreciate your support at the raid, but in order for the combined effort to succeed we need some system where things like this is decided by all guilds that takes part, and not just one guild on their own. And from what I have seen, there has usually been very little arguing over theese decisions, people usually come to an agreement very fast, even if there are 10+ guilds present.

I'm glad to see it all worked out in the end, and you have my respect for choosing to give the land to BD as was decided.
