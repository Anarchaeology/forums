Back in the day, there wasn't a problem with only 10 items. It was nearly unlimited. Problem was database bloat. FC keeps a copy of everything you have in your apt, and when many, many people have a good 30+ items in there, it tends to increase the database by a rather HUGE amount. So the limited us to 10 items.

A easy fix (but one that's easily exploited) is storing the apt locally on people's machines... then load it into the servers whenever people go into it... but that's what encryption is for, is it not?
