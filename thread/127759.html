quote from dev chat form this weekend
gaute - umbrood: seeing as ga are going this way, can you give us an aproximation on how much of our current technologu will be rendered obsolete within sl, and i dont only mean yals
gaute - Ok, first let me state something!
gaute - ALL of these changes will ONLY be inside the Shadowlands themselves. Nothing, NOTHING will change in Rubi-Ka!!!
gaute - People have misunderstood this before...
gaute - 1. All treatment and nano recharge equipment will be obsolete. It will be exchanged with other, just as effective, equipment based on Spirit-Tech instead.
gaute - 2. All flying equipment and nanos will be obsolete. So will all other cars. (I guess fixer-runspeed buffs will grow somewhat in popularity).
gaute - (This includes also flying effects from Adv. Polymorphs).
gaute - (but there will be new / other polys for the Advs

gaute - 4. GA will not work as today. We hope to have a spirit-tech version of it ready for launch. It can most likely be gained through a quest. It will not work in the same way, but will still make the fixer very distinct.
gaute - 5. Some nanos will work differently. The shadowland beasts are less affected by some nanos than others... This is a more detailed consideration, that we do ongoing... More details later...

so we may not be that bad off
NOTE our run buffs are stil going to work
and our evac will be replaced
and a replacement for GA AND IT WILL BE A QUEST ITEM
