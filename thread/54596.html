What I would like to read in next patch info!
What I would like to read in patch Info 14.6. Please post any additions.

Social

Apartments = can now hold up to 20 items
Guilds = Have a range of new commands for management and social purposes

Game-play

Pet Pathing = While we are still fixing the actual problem we have decided to give all pet classes /pet warp  command, this warps the pet in front of the owner. However, the pet cannot be engaged in a battle when executing this command.

Solid objects = Made all the objects inside missions to be translucent. This is to help with various in-game problems.

Weapons = Fixed various problems with different weapons, these weapons should now perform as intended

Ninja Looting = Have been addressed. After killing a monster, it is now possible to <shift-right click> (mouse) on the corpse to get a full list of loot. This can be done by everyone in the vicinity, so be careful

Boss loot = The duration in which the corpse disappears have now been increased to 20 minutes. This only affects bosses.

Fumbles = Have been removed from the game

PvM damage = Mob against player damage is now capped at 45%. However, it is still possible that a high level encounter could get you killed in three shots

Hunting = Increased XP from all encounters past ql200

Items

Implants = Refined-implants and clusters are now available as a mission rewards, sometimes they can also be purchased from the trade departments at advanced or superior shops.

Specials Damage

Aimed-shot = only agents are able to get their AS hits up to 10k. All the other professions have this skill with a cap maxed at 6k.

Full Auto = only soldiers are able to get their FA hits up to 10k. All the other professions have the damage with this skill capped at 6k.

Fixers

GA MKIV = Slightly reduced some defence abilities of this armor

Enforcers

Edged and Piercing Weapons = This is enforcer only buff, but not self only

Soldiers

TMS = only locks MC skill
First Aid = Soldier are able to recycle this skill in 15 seconds
Full Auto = New soldier-only buff
Note: Soldier will be focused more on next coming two patches

Engineers

Tradeskills = We have included a new item that should give a hefty boost on all tradeskills, this item is engineer only and can be made through trade skills. The item can be upgraded once per every title level

Nano-Technicians

Nullity Sphere = Protects NT also from all hostile nanoprograms and damage shields
Enfraams = Have been halved in terms of NCU cost
Range = The cap has been increased to 50 with according buffs
HE = will now tick almost twice as fast, but only for NTs
Direct Damage = New nanos have been introduced (880-990 MC requirements)
Dark Movement = Two new versions available and will also increase targets nano resistance
Blinds = Reduce the crit chance by 25% and all evade skills by 200(with highest version, toned down versions apply for lower level blinds), keep in mind that it is still difficult for NTs to use these higher level blinds (without outside buffs)
Note: NTs will be finalised in the next patch

PvP

Damage = All damage is back to 100%. However, the capped damage is 40% of your maximum health per hit.

Debuffs = All debuffs have been reduced in duration. The maximum debuff penalty period in PvP is now at 30 seconds. It is understood that under current PvP environment it was unprecedented to have professions with ability to attack targets with these long duration debuffs. This should balance and ALSO add more interaction to the playing field, while still keeping the debuff effects as they were before. It is commonly understood that most fights are over in a less then a minute apart from mass encounters. This should also add more interactivity to mass encounters, as you would no longer have ability to Fire-and-Forget.

Engaging in battle = Players can now only attack those that are on the same title level. With an exception of Title Level 5 and Title Level 6, which are allowed to engage in battle against each other

Next Patch

- Will focus primarily Soldiers and secondly on Engineers after that we will also focus on crats
- NT fixes will be finalised
- Improved social content
- Addressing numerous bug fixes.
