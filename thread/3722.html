Call for a Neutral Taskforce for the 'Steps of Madness'
Hello to all fellow Neutrals (and neutral-minded faction members).

I (we) would like to visit Mr. Neleb and his 'folks' to check out what he is brewing in the 'Steps of Madness' down there in the south/east. Furthermore we would like to 'disturb' him a little bit in his plans. Therefore we would like to gather as many 'Neutrals' as possible for this task.

I explicitly want to encourage all Neutral Organizations (The Healing Hands, Blade Corp, and all the others I am not aware off) to participate or even take over in the lead of this adventure.

Feel free to tell me (Maximilian), if and when you want to join out little Taskforce. (I can mostly be found in Borealis and/or Newland when not travelling the lands Rubi-Ka)

Envisioned time frame:
- Somewhen around the weekend of the 15./16. November.
- Early evening GMT (20 hours and later).
- Rubi-Ka 1.
- Level 35-xx (any level that fits to into the team)
Could and will be postponed/repeated on demand.

Yours Max, the Adventurer


What we can offer:
- Neutrality. As we clearly stand for neutrality, we don't oppose to team with any faction's members (although neutrals are preferred ).
- Fairness. Loot (items, cash, etc.) is split evenly, if possible according to the needs/preferences of the team members.
- Integrity. Who could you trust more than a Neutral - we will never attack anyone besides self defense .

Organisational notes:
We are a small team of (fun) players. Our group consists of an Enforcer (lvl 49), a NT (lvl 46, close to 47) and myself (lvl 51 Adventurer). Although not specialized (we don't have high level support) we have decent take down capacity in our range (10-20 levels above) combined with a good survival rate. We have NO preferences concerning the profession of our potential team mates - all are welcome.

Hope to hear from you - Max(imilian)
