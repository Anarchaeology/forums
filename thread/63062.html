A discussion of the nitty-gritty details about life on RK.
Ok, there's a few things that I often wonder about. These are just little things that developers never fleshed out, but they matter all the same.

1) What do you think money physically looks like? Or does it have a physical sense at all? Does the world rely on credit cards?

2) Do Rubi-Kans all speak the same language? If so, what language do they speak? English? A whole new language? And it's fairly improbable that the WHOLE WORLD speaks the exact same language. So do Clanners and OT have their own languages as well?

3) Does time still function in the same way; as in 24 hours a day, 7 days a week? Or is it different?

4) And then there's the huge discussion of new arrivals. Do you arrive as an immigrant, then totally remap your genetic makeup? Or are you created? Let's not rule out the thought that you are compressed into some odd form and then shipped to Rubi-Ka to then gain a physical form...or is it a combination of two above?

5) Does anyone else think that it'd be nearly impossible for a leet to stand? They'd be rather topheavy. This suggests that perhaps the gravity on the surface of the planet is different than the gravity here on earth. The fact that higher-level players can jump insanely high would support this as well.

6) What do commlinks look like? Is it a detatchable wristlink? Is it built into an appendage, like an outer implant? Is it implanted within you and controlled with the brain? On a similar topic, can people around you hear the commlink or is it inside your own head?

7) Simply: Stock market.

8) What about movie theaters? Are movies still 2d projected pictures, or is there a 3d effect? Or do viewers almost 'go inside' the movie to witness it firsthand, as though they are there?

9) Why aren't there any amusement parks in the game? Did OT/Clans cut entertainment funding?

10) What does notum look like? Is it a liquid, a solid, does it take multiple forms? And how is it used? Does it 'radiate' power sources, or must it be melted down in some way? Is it used by mixing into other materials, or raw? Or must it be employed in a generator of some sort?

11) Is there slavery?

12) How are engineer bots carried around? Do they fold up into a transportable lump? Do the nanobots construct them on a whim? And what's the explanation for terrible pathfinding- bad programming?

Please use only a number 2 pencil.

Nice choice of topic, Ms. Maul. Here's my 2 credits' worth:

1. I agree with the majority of opinions, that the monetary system is essential based on electronic funds transfers. I would imagine that a citizen's financial accounts are tied somehow to the NCU, which seems to contain virtually everything about us. Accessing terminals would initiate a scan of a person's NCU (making it virtually impossible to pirate an account) and add or deduct credits according to a person's activity. The same would apply to all terminals (i.e., missions, insurance, etc.)

2. The sociological evolution of Rubi-Ka would indicate that, in all likelihood, there is only one uniform language. The solitus are all derived somehow from the original colonists, and the other breeds were created on RK. Thus, the created breeds would have been taught the colonists' language, which would have been passed through generations. There may, however, be regional variations in dialect (i.e., north RK vs. south).

3. Estiria is correct. Further, the RK date follows the RL date (i.e., as of the writing of this, the date on RK is 28 October 29476).

4. We know for a fact that everyone of Nano breed is created on RK, simply because they can't exist anywhere else (they could, however, be born to parents as well). We also know that the Atrox are neuter, meaning they have no sexual organs and thus cannot reproduce (although they could reproduce asexually via some bizarre form of cellular division...not a pretty sight...). As for the other breeds, anything is possible.

5. Awww, stop picking on the leets. Kinda look like penguins with their little wobble, though.

6. I agree with the group so far.

7. The fact that one is an employee does not automatically preclde such individual from owning the securities of the firm, contrary to Esiria's beliefs. Stock option/investment plans are one of the principal means of motivating a corporation's workforce, and are a form of remuneration that not only pervades society but that essentially costs the firm nothing. Also contrary to Esiria's post, there are other corporations legally resident and validly subsisting on RK - the most obvious being BrontoBurger. If there wasn't, why would Omni-Pol maintain a subdepartment explicity to monitor these corporations? The absence of a stockmarket is due to the fact that the largest shareholding, OTRK, is a 100% owned subsidiary of the OT parent company (hence, its shares are NOT publicly traded on any market, as they are all held by the parent). Stockmarkets are created and maintained to FINANCE ACTIVITY from the capital funds of the investing public. This activity almost always involves expansion, which I would opine is restricted by OTRK, because it wants to ensure that it remains the dominant force on RK.

8. and 9. Maybe movies theatres aren't popular enough to warrant their addition. The same would also apply to amusement parks.

10. I agree with Estiria on re: notum. In its gaseous state (as it exists atmospherically) it is obviously fully transparent, and would be inhaled and absorbed into the bloodstream (this would probably the principal method by which Nanos subsist).

11. Slavery was effetively abolished by the rise of the Clan movement (the unionization of Atrox notum mine workers).

12. No opinion on the bot issue.
