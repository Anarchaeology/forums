The Pain...
It had been a while since Ben had thought about this...But it seemed to be calling him again. He was wandering about when he stumbled across a portal. He looked over it, and saw the name "Jobe". Heaing about Jobe from all his friends he decided to take a little trip. Stepping through the portal into the strange new place. The beauty of the towering blue buildings, the huge floating peices of rock, supporting a great city. A floating fountain of information, technology, and knowledge. An altar to all he worshipped. As he stood there staring in awe he felt a fire inside him...A burning he hadnt felt in a long time. Something deep inside him burnt through his veins. His eyes began to burn and he crumbled to his knees...Blue light emminated from his eyes as they burst open in main, his mouth cracking in a violent scream as he fell to the floor...

What is this...Whats going on...

He felt something erupting inside him, that he hadnt felt for a long time. Something deep, ancient, and inhuman. A strange voice rang in the back of his head, with all these horrible feelings coarsing through him the voice comforted him saying "Welcome home Ben...Welcome home...".
