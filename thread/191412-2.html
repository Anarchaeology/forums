Nascence Quests
I play on the DNW Server, but since we all have the same release I assume you guys are seeing the stuff below. Apart from everything mentioned I would like to point out the following:

- Nascence Garden Quests
Donna/Veronica send you out on a quest to find information. Once you have completed the quest they don't seem to be interested to hear anything about your quests. This breaks continuity. Both quests should be completed by returning to them, which would also be a perfect time to set up the player to visit Ergo.

- Nascense Ergo Quest
Usually NPC's open up their 'give item' window when they ask for something. When Ergo asks to see the Mark of Menace he doesn't open his window. Took me forever to figure out I could just open the give dialogue myself.

- Nascence Trial of Passage Quest
The Mission Key vanishes when leaving the Trial dungeon prematurely. Showing the Mark of Menace again to Ergo does not produce a new Mission Key!!! I could only solve this quest by copying somebody elses key and taking my share of the pellucid crystals. This is a show stopper!

- Nascense Gardens
In both gardens there is an NPC which responds 'You do not have the key yet to this garden. Come back when you have the blessing of the divine'. Funny, I do have both keys. What is this NPC supposed to do?

General Quest Suggestion
All quests should give a mission entry. That way it will be clearer what I am supposed to do, and how much time I have left to do it. Also, I can easily see when I do in fact have accomplished somethink. Right now, sometimes there is, sometimes there isn't a mission entry. Very confusing. I did the Baby Oil thing for Ergo with both Yuttos, not realising that once is enough to progress.

Text Bug
- Jobe Market Portal NPC tells us that the portal leads to Nascense, when in fact it leads to Elysium (bad case of the old copy/past 'g').
