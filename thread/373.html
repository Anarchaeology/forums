Concealment vs missions broke
Hello Fellow citizenz of Rubi-KA!!!!
-------------------------------------------------
Im a lvl 57 MA, who the last couple of lvl's have boosted Concealment,added "sneaking"implants and used a lot of cash in a cloaking device. (and the imps of course)
My cloaking device is now lvl 69, and so i see a connection in ql and monster-lvl's.
i had a ql 55 cloak before too, and when i used that, about 50% of the npc's was able to see me.
now, with the ql 69, all green npc's don't see ****, while approx. 30% of yellows attack me, (depends on it's perception).

In the 12.9 version, i ran missions for like 20k, 3 missions in a row, on 60% diff., and sometimes i got caught, sometimes not. but ill tell u, that was the most exciting minutes of my AO-life, sneaking around, wondering if i'd stepped on any monster-toes..... real kick to see that concealment actually worked for others than agents too.!!!
money was half the fun, sneaking was the rest.

But then came 13.0.....

-Concealment still works on npc's, but in a "pick up the item" is totally broke. if i pick it up, i'll prey to god, tha ill get the "ta-taa" signal before they hit me.
If not, i have to kill them all, before dropping the item, and pick it up again.
-As my fists do less more damage now, im in big trouble. (i actually have problems with greens now, if there are 2 i have to use my dimach to get rid of the first. i took on orange before, with just enough hp to breath afterwards :-)

-The sneak mode seems to be bugged too, almost everytime i enter "h" (sneak-mode), i just run off after 5 secs, and of course aggro everyone in the room, and have to go outside, to try again, but it usually happens again anyway, so i just delete that mission, and run back.....aah..
-And no way if i could pick the item up, when being attacked, get a stupid error msg, to kill first then collect the money.

Everything costs a fortune in RK2, so where should i now get the money from?
Im not gonna take 3 missions, who then takes 5 hours, to get lousy 60 k....maybe, if im lucky.
I know this game have a lifespan of 4 years, but who wants to use 1 year to save up 1 million credits?
not me, then i rather play solitaire for 4 years.....

So lower the prices, or give us working missions again.
i thought AO was fun like hell, until yesterday, when you ruined my daily needed income, and flushed my ip and money spent on Concealment, cloak, and implants down the drain........

But i feel more sorry for the Ppl who left yesterday, they invested so much time in this, and got nothing left, than bad memories.
sad to see.....
