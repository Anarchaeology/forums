More tradeskills
Tradeskills galore.

1) Distilled water eyedropper (25) charges. Combine with a QL X med kit or nano kit to reduce to QL X-1. That way we can stack all those odd sized kits we find in missions. Requires Pharmatech.

2) Mixed drinks. QL1 bottles of booze and glasses that combine to a variety of mixed drinks. Oh--and you need skill reductions/increases from drinking. No skill required.

3) Camera bot. As an engineer, I'd like to combine a camera with my bot shell and then see what the bot sees and control how it moves. Even if I can't attack until attacked. Requires Mech Engr Elect Engr.

4) Non-combat bot healing tradeskill items that require and Mech Engr and Elect Engr to activate.

5) Fix all tradeskill recipes. Make it easier to improve QL of weapons so instead of replacing, we can update our guns.

6) Tradeskill recipes for blister packs and pills and grafts.

7) More makeable trimmers. Bot info trimmer which lists running buffs.
