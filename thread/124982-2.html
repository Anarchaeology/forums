The guy could be an outright jerk and yet he gets to be a 'freedom fighter'. That's just so wrong.

You got your faction system in the Shadowlands. I look forward to it. I look forward to it coming to me, however and not me being forced into one or the other. As stated, I want to 'discover' my shadow breed. I don't want to right-click an item and 'become' my shadow breed. If my friends end up being Blue Gurgles and I find myself a Red Gurgle, them mebbe I've discovered something about my friends. That's cool.

But, please no +50 Str items for Blue Gurgles only. And lay off the token board idea. That's so yesterday. That's so Rubi'Ka. That's so 2001.

I gotta amend my comments a bit. If all you want is phat lewt and levels, I have no problem with that. Not at all.

What I'm saying is that it skews things. When you are supposed to be having a simulation of a real-life political 'situation' is gets messed up, by not only those who don't desire to roleplay, but those who don't even care about the game at all really.

It's not the fact that the people who would blithely switch from Omni to Clan for the sake of a gun or a bigger XP bonus don't have any loyalty. It's the fact that FunCom has based based the game around two factions and the collective 'everyone else' and allow the actions of those who don't care to affect the in-game enjoyment of those who do care. That and the catering to those who don't care much to the detriment of me.

It's the same situation to a degree as the the 'PvP vs. Carebear' argument. One way of enjoying the game impacting another. Look what Notum Wars has done. Look at the game population after its true impact was felt. I thought Anarchy Online was actually on the verge of extinction. But, that's not my point. My point was that FunCom tied your PvM (or 'carebear') success on how well you could kill other players. That was a horrendous mistake in my opinion and the empty servers and grey friends list seem to underscore my commitment to that belief. Look at 14.8 and especially last weekend and all of a sudden there is an excitement in the air. Hmm ... getting a bit lost here and it seems I'm espousing one way of enjoying the game over the other. I'll rephrase:

It's a well-known fact that only 10% at most of the MMORPG community ever has and ever will fully embrace PvP as an enjoyable experience. Beyond balance and mechanics there are 'human' reasons why people don't enjoy it. OK. So, my point is FunCom put out something that directly catered to 10% of the population. I'm 100% cool with that. But, what they did was have a flawed implementation. In order to be a better missioner or Van Horn camper or Smuggler's ninja looter, you had to engage in something you found distasteful. I know people left in droves. Maybe they didn't cancel, but they just stopped logging in. That 10% of the playerbase was ecstatic because not only was their playstyle catered to, but they got bonuses to Mercenary killing efficiency as well. The rest of the playerbase was 'forced' into an activity they had varying degrees of dislike for.

So, we come to the token boards. If yer just in it for levels and loot, then no problem. But, take the analogy with the 'PvP vs. Carebear' argument above. We got people who hate the Clans, can't see themselves as part of the corporate machine either and yet are 'forced' to go one way or the other by the lure of these things. That shouldn't be so. Your political choice (if you enjoy the political game) should be dictated by how you feel and not because FunCom has decided you can be a better missioner or Mercenary killer by going against your better judgement.

It's not the fact that these things exist. It's the fact that FunCom seems to put them in to 'drag' people to one side or the other rather than letting the people be in a faction they are comfortable with.

If you ask me, now that the Council of Truth is gone, shouldn't all Clan token boards be deleted? Or is Henry Radiman somewhere riding an exercise bike to keep the electricity running to power them. If anything, let the Independant Rubi'kans and Circle G and Elite Operations go hunt down Mr. Radiman and get his stash of unused token boards. Those guilds and their ilk sure have a hell of a lot more in common with the spirit of the founding clans than the situation we have now. As an independant character, I always had the utmost respect for Radiman and the Council. I saw them as intelligent and inspired. When the Council of Ares and the Sentinels drove them out, I lost a ton of respect for the guys who were supposed to be forming a new society up north. I kinda understood why the corporation wanted to cleanse the planet.

Bah! What does any of this have to do with a Token Board. Oh, yeah. Politics. Story. That's how I enjoy the game a lot. FunCom's trying to force the betrayal of my beliefs with carrots.

I never liked carrots and never will.

And returning to the orignal point of the thread...

For me there is a very compelling reason to stay netural, two in fact. The first comes straight from the storyline. When you have two sides locked in a long, bloodly and meaningless war, neutrality is the clearest way of staying clean nad staying sane. The situation on Rubi-Ka reminds me much more of WWI than WWII and any nation who had the good sense of sitting out the pointless carnage of the First World War has all my respect.

The second reason for not giving up one neutral status in AO is that you can never go back. Omni and Clan can swap sides and revert at will, but a Neutral who gives up his or her heritage has lost it forever. I've known a lot neutrals who ended up bitterly regretting changing side. If FC refuses to give neutral tokens and other benefits, the least they could do is allow neutral application forms . The fact that you cannot ever become neutral again is very harsh indeed.
