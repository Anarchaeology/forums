.....Freaky.
I was coming back from a mission in Greater Tir County, cutting through Brainy Ant Forest on the way back to Tir City. I'd just leveled too, and was finally able to play with my Leet morph.

In the deepest part of the forest, I came across an Omni-Tek camp. "What the hell's Omni-Scum doing here?" I muttered to myself. I moved in for a closer look, sneaking through the tall grass in my new Leet-form.

Suddenly, a massive, spiked club slams into the turf next to me, kicking clods of mud into my disproportionately large Leet's-eyes. Jumping back in surprise, I immediately pop into my normal form to defend myself... And wiping the dirt out of my eyes, stand face-to-face with a towering OT Enforcer. Suddenly, shots ring out all around, echoing off the trees; I'd been spotted!

My twin BBI Old Faithful 22's blazed into life, mowing down any melee-fighters before they get near, their sound adding to the cacaphony of gunfire from the ranged fighters. As the seconds ticked by, one by one all OT who attacked are mowed down.

A haze of cordite and residual nano-machines fills the air, my trusty pistols smoking; I was nearly dead, but I had won. Around me, the remains of at least fifteen OT employees littered the ground. After a quick heal, the application of first-aid healing stems, and a quick check of my surroundings for any remaining hostiles, I stepped forward to loot the remains.

Suddenly, as I'm retrieving a Shining Gunslinging Left Hand Implant from a corpse, the whole area is surrounded in a blinding, golden light. (OOC: Sorry, no pictures available. ) Gasping, as the light intensifies, I look up for the source. As the light fades, out in the west, I spot yet another light, spreading dome-like across the horizon, expanding silently towards me. Then another. And another. And ANOTHER.

I don't know how long I stood there or how many lights passed; I was too dazed to keep track of such things, or even snap photos to share with others. But after it was over, I stood there for several moments, utterly awed by the experience. The high, trilling song of a bird finally snapped me out of my revirie.

When I finally got back to Tir, my head was full of questions; namely, what the hell just happened? What caused it? Some sort of explosion? An experimental radar-type device? Eating the wrong kind of mushrooms?

I don't know. I'm not sure I want to know. Jesus, this planet is outright freaky sometimes.

WGTs
Yep, they're called WGTs, or Weird Glowy Things. (Variously pronounced "dubya gee tees" or "wiggets.")

If you get a good look at one, you'll see that it looks like an old-fashioned flying saucer standing on edge, buried halfway into the terrain, flat side facing the direction of movement. They seem to be made of some kind of pure energy. They travel at a pretty good clip, around 250-300 runspeed, usually from southwest to northeast. They're huge, usually around 20 meters tall by 40 meters wide, and probably about 4 or 5 meters thick. They usually travel in pairs. So far, they are harmless, although long-term exposure to one may have unforseen consequences. Nobody that I've heard from has seen them more than twice.



OOC: They are also widely believed to be a graphics glitch.
