We spend tons of time, cash and ip optimizing our characters to crit as high and deal as much damage as humanly possible. We've done field testing to determine what weapons are worth using and which ones are just toys. And to equip our weapons we self buff using every trick in the book including going from FP Meta Phys (for psy mod and spacetime masteries) to Trader FP and ransack/deprive, divest/plunder our way into higher quality weapons. We do understand how your attack skill/nano skill buffs work and we also know that you can get more skill by deprive/ransack, divest/plunder chains than you can with mere wrangles alone.

Wrangles were never intended to be used to just overequip. Wrangles were meant to be used during combat to transfer attack rating from you to your team. You don't have to use your highest team wrangle you can cast when you are trying to help the team. Use a wrangle you can cast without ransack/deprive, divest/plunder first. You can then ransack/deprive, divest/plunder chain your way back up if you so desire for your usual damage and high nano skill. Team wrangles even cast on you as well so if you do have ncu free you can get some of your skill back with your own wrangle. Attack rating does add up to crits and frankly I'm quite surprised if some of you can't grasp that. Why don't traders wrangle while in combat? Because it lowers your skills for the duration of the nano? Please that is rubbish and we all know it. If ya cast a low easily castable wrangle you can easily replenish any skill lost by the wrangle by using maybe one more deprive/ransack or divest/plunder chain...with a minimal loss in skill. Is that concept hard to grasp?

Just like our team heals that dot us for health and each additional heal merely refreshes the dot. Just deprive/ransack divest plunder after team wrangling first...then its all a matter of refreshing the wrangle...no real loss. We'll still have more attack rating and nano skill than our base skill anyway and by using a wrangle that we can easily cast while wrangled you could keep a low ncu, easily casted buff on your team at all times. Heck agents always have sniper's bliss running and it only adds 50 rifle skill...with a little aimed shot skill thrown in.
