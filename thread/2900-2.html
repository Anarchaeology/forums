Summation
Well, it seems like the reason the Vecter is so good is a combination of many effects.

1. The damage multiplyer from attack skill has a bigger effect the higher the max damage on the weapon is, so high damage weapons have a bigger advantage than the damage numbers indicate, because defense from armor is done by subtraction and damage increse from attack is done by multiplication, but the bonus for each point of attack seems smaller than the bonus for each point of weapon damage, so at any level there is a kind of magic number where the weapon escapes the min damage quagmire. Vectors seem to be one of the few single skill weapons that escape that damage range.

2. The shotgun duck skill helps the vector get into this non-min damage range (other shotguns I've tried are not so lucky) by increasing the average of damage.

3. The energy damge type essentialy lowers the min damage cap by ensuring that you go against a slightly weaker armor type than most weapons using projecticle.
