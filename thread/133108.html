I personally have never liked raidbots.

However, at the request of a friend, I decided to give another a try recently.

We put in our numbers before we began fighting.
three items dropped.

I joined helpbot because I do not trust the integrity of anyone I don't know extremely well.

Someone did the first roll
Then they did a second

My number came up for the second roll, however the person rolling did not immediately say what items were being raffled for which roll.

Only *after* the two rolls were performed were the items named. Of course the raid organizer (whose number was the first drawn) claims the better piece of loot.

So the raid organizer's number was drawn BEFORE the loot was listed, basically giving him the opportunity to pick the item he wanted.

I didn't ask for a reroll or anything, because this is about what I expected from a raidbot. Needless to say, a couple more people go on my ignore list and I'm left with the satisfaction that my initial assessment of raidbots were correct.

Hell, earlier at a raidbot organized merc camp raid, someone *may or may not have* ninja looted a set of azure gloves.

The thing about these raidbots is you may not even know who you're raiding with, so how can you trust them?

People, raid with your guilds.

THAT IS THE PURPOSE OF HAVING A GUILD

The purpose is to support its members. If your guild will not raid and support you, find a different guild that *will*. Just as simple as that. There's many many good guilds on clan and omni side run by fair people. Storm, Synergy Factor, Tranquility, Midnight Reveries, etc all are well known and active guilds with many fair people. Find a nice guild with the numbers to take down these mobs without outside help and you will save yourself months of stress, anger, and headaches.

Raidbots are run by people and you are at those individual's mercy to remain honest. Now get real. This is AO. A video game. Greed rules here and that will never change

I'm not too upset about it, as this was more of an expiriment for me, but were I not already in the best guild on rubi ka, this would have probably pissed me off to no end. The piece of loot I got jacked on was nice, but I'm sure I'll eventually get it via my family, Storm. I've got all the time in the world and tonight's experience only makes me happier that I'm in a guild that cares about its members and distributes loot fairly.

I can't imagine the amount of frustration I'd feel if I actually needed to rely on raidbots. food for thought
