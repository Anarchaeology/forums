Here's my thoughts on why FunCom implemented this Amnesty and when we can see an end to it. I don't think the players can do anything about it right now:

Personally, I am holding on to the slimmest of hopes that FunCom has introduced this Amnesty to tide us over until they can implement a REAL faction system. My guess is that the Amnesty won't be lifted until they can:

- stop Omni/Clan players from killing same-side NPC's.
- allow Omni/Clan players to buff/heal same-side NPC's.
- prevent Omni NPC's from appearing in Omni player missions.
- stop the Clan/Omni NPC's from attacking Neutral players in non-political areas.
- address the other Neutral issues such as shopping/defending towns.
- provide for the _true_ capturing of Outposts up to about the size of Meetmedere.

The lack of a real faction system is absolutely killing the 'war' side of the storyline and gameplay in general. The game just doesn't work right now in terms of the politics of war _or_ good game design. Those Omni NPC's roaming around are there to aid and assist fellow Omni players and attack Clanners (and Neutrals if they are in a sensitive area). From a game design standpoint they are supposed to create an adventuring area that is safer for one side than the other (Territory control). They are _not_ there to be experience factories for Clan and Omni fighting side-by-side.

I think FunCom realizes this. I only hope that this 'Amnesty' was put in for the short term as a way to address the deficiencies of not having a real faction system in a game based on a war between two opponents and involving others who choose to be non-combatants or despise both sides equally.

Remember, it's only been 3 months or so since the storyline started. If what they say on the 'envisioned' page is true, we can probably see factions truly implemented around the time they put in the capturing of territory. If it's this July, August, or September, what's the big deal? We'll Have patch 14.0 at that time and a nice storyline launch of the official end of the cease fire.
