Inhabitant run transport systems and mobile shop
Hiya all,
Onestbob here, your friendly trader trying out a bit of market research (so please take the time to answer).

1. If I was to set up a mobile shop at some prime hunting locations with items such a Nano, First Aid and treatment packs available alongside ammo for most weapon types would you be willing to pay a slightly higher cost to not have to get back to a shop to stock up. Items will be available at all quality lvls. What would be the most popular hunting grounds that are a decent distance from shops be?
2. I have plans to implement an independent transport system for Rubi-Ka. The system will be based on using Warp Beacon from places such a Tir Grid to TLR dung entrance and would run on a regular basis. Cost would depend on the distance covered (in theory any place to any place is possible), however from Tir Grid to TLR would be 5k each way for example.

Dependent on the level of interest I will approach my business manager at the Royal Bank of RubiKa for a loan to set up these business opportunities and confirm with my Engineer associates that the plan is valid.

It's a matter of trust
In the end, it's a matter of trust. How can player A trust Trader A to buy/sell items at a reasonable price? If player A hands over some good loot to Trader A, how will Player A know that Trader A is only taking a certain percentage (say 20%) or a larger percentage (70%)? In sellling ammo/med kits/nano kits, I find it rare that people would venture out on a mission or hunting without stocking themselves up (when I say stocking I mean like 2000+ of bullet/shotgun/energy ammo and 200+ med/nano kits).

In the end I just don't see how this could be profitable. Convient maybe, but not profitable.

Now if you want to talk about profitable, Wrangles near PvP zones are pretty good and so are UVC (until they make them self only).

Actually here's a side note. With Bulk Trader and Frequent Customer, it is possible to buy ammo/kits cheaper than normal (normal is with 0 comp lit) and then sell it for the "normal" price you would get from a terminal.
