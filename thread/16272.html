Where's the love?
I am an agent lvl131 and here are some observations so far about a number of things....

OE patch. Needed. I was first quite ticked off at first, then I realized that I had to OE because agent rifles suck and mobs are insanely powerful. The mobs are uber because of the OEr's. So yes I do agree, we need this patch.

Professions lacking direction. Agents, uh, right, what is our purpose? NTs, gods of nuking. RIGHT. Adventurers? Fixers, uh, yeah ok.

I see so many great things here in this game yet it seems every single profession gets nerfed, lowered, loses direction etc. Why are the support classes doing more damage than heavy/light combat profs?

If I am teamed with a trader, doc and engie, I feel safe, warm and fuzzy. If I am teamed with a soldier, enforcer I squirm.

I really hope (and I know this is the intent) that FC balances out ALL professions soon after this patch. OE or not, each profession is in need of attention. The only profession that seems to be stable is Enforcers, I may be way wrong however.

I love playing my agent and I have a great time teaming with the different professions. I could single out each prof and givew a summary off these boards on what's wrong with them. If anyone has played this game for some time you will get a good idea oin whats wrong with everyone. But that would be pointless.

So what the heck IS my point? We need love. Everyone does. Give the Fixers some cool toys! Give NTs their damage/range back. Give agents better rifles/nano's. Soldiers need to be ranged weapon damage kings. Give MAs a root breaker. Take Docs off the hate list. Blah blah blah...its all been stated before....

/me rereads his post...
/me hopes he did not leave anyone out...
/me hopes FC gives back to what they have taken away..
/pray


I hope I made some sense. This was not a complaint nor rant, just a quick summary of what concerns me.

but what do I know, I'm just a player.
