Psi's going away party!
Greetings fellow citizens of Rubi-Ka

PARTY PARTY PARTY!!!
Reet Retreat
Friday Night
10:00PM EST

I would like to invite everyone who knows Psiraven to her going away party at Reet Retreat. Psiraven is taking an extended leave of absence from the game as building so many implants has left her weak and in need of a vacation.

Psiraven has served the community both clan and omni in building up to QL 200 implants for a very long time now. It is well known that she has dominated that market in the way of both availability and skills. Psiraven also served the community in spear-heading the event we all have come to know as Battle Royale and even after she leaves us for a time, Mercenaries of Kai will continue her work in bringing even more exciting events to the community.

SO! Bring out your best suit/dress, its time to get down and boogie with all your MoK friends. Clan/Neutral and Omni are all invited to this event.

Please do not request Psiraven making your implants at the event, such an action would get you 10 lashes and a firm /flip. This event is social only, no business, politics or combat will be discussed or acted upon.

Thanks!
