My only RP experience.
Desperate for GA long long ago when they finally fixed it I heard about a GM played character called Diamond Dave (or something). I was told that he can get GA discs for people but you gotta RP with him to get it.

So someone tells me thet DD is in the bar over by the old Tir Grid (forgot name). So I go in there and start listening to these people RPing. Man it was really spooky and I was a bit freaked out. I mean this is a game right?

Anyway... so there I am all freaked out about this RP stuff when now I figure well it IS GA and short of blitzing missions for the next few weeks it was worth a shot. This was also at the height of the org bank credit scam and GA prices were skyrocketing and the Phixx3r population was also growing. So I jump right in and start to type this stuff and just cringe as I hit ENTER. Not really my style.

So I sit there for a while not wanting to say the wrong thing because I heard stories about this guy making people do all sorts of weird things and sometimes hooking them up but most times not. But its worth a shot.

So he splits after promising me the GA3 for a highly reasonable fee of 30mil. But I have to wait for it. "I am expecting a shipment within the hour" he says.

So I am pretty damn excited to the point my palms are sweaty with anticipation and I am nervous. I go into Slaggie's office and chill there so as not to miss a /tell in the midst of all the buff begging. I tell some friends and they are happy but wary. they have heard about this guy and some of the things he has done.

So now I get more nervous than excited. It goes past the hour and I send him a /tell something like "so has the shipment met with trouble?" ENTER /me cringes

"Yes it has" was all he said.

I never heard from him again nor did he ever respond to one of my /tells despite being online.

RP Board over a PvP Board.
Gimme a break!!

Quick EDIT: not quite the ONLY RP experience. There was this one time when DarkMystica was wearing this hot pink thong and tube top. She sexorized me right there in Tir Grid. I have some screenies but I will hold them back for the sake of the kiddies
