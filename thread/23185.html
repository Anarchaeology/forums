An arguement against the newbie changes.
Okay an argument against the newbie changes, specifically the no rez shock and no experience loss. Okay I tested this last night on test to see if you could pull it off as an exploit. Get to your mission, start your mission, finish your mission / command terminate. Now head to your next mission make sure they are in the same zone and close by the respawn point. Err okay great now you have a quick way to get from point a to point b without a penalty. Here is a better example; you have a mission in West Athens and a mission in Wartorn Valley you get the missions in Old Athens your bound in Wartorn Valley. Do your mission in West Athens first /terminate and then you heal because you purchased a better treatment kit and nano kit takes two click and your ready and in the zone that you need to be.

It was really nice to get to level seven in four hours I used this trick once to get back to an area I was familiar with, but I dont think this is the sort of thing you guys had in mind when you came up with it. My advice drop rez shock to level 7 this is when you start to see missions is other zones if you max the difficultly.
