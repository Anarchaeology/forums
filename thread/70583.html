Move outdoors bosses away from mission entrances
No, I'm not whining (well, actually, maybe I am), and I don't mind a little risk and danger, but it's kinda annoying to stick your head out of the mission you just finished and get ganked by a boss and his minions. Especially when you're alone. One specific instance of this I can think of (since it just happened to me) is in the area of Giant Green in Aegean -- got jumped by 4 or 5 level 60'ish mantezes (I'm level 60). Lost all of the 100k xp I made in the mission.

Same deal with the pipe mission entrace near Rising Sun in Aegean. At least that boss is placed a little further from the entrance than the Manteze.

Guess I know which mission areas not to pick now.

Anyway, getting back to the original point of the post, move the bosses more away from the mission entrances. If people want to fight outdoors bosses, I'm sure they will find them just fine -- I'd rather they don't find me.
