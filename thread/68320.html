About 9 months ago, I ran a bunch of missions for QL10s with my MA alt. Sold them by the pair and for 300-400k. I've heard reports of much more (up to 1 mil) and much less (100k). Prices have dropped a bit, but you should be able to sell a pair for around 300k with not too much trouble. Remember, for a high level player, that extra couple of 100k won't matter nearly as much as that +16 str/sta, so you might want to try selling in places high levels hang out. Made a nice amount of money selling items from the newbie dungeon that way.
