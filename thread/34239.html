MA Fist Buff Suggestion
Instead of making it a straight addition to damage, why not make this a secondary MA skill buff? IE, instead of Stellar Harmony adding 23 fire damage, have it add, maybe, 50 or 60 to MA skill + change fists to fire AC. Back before the MA loving, having an additional 15-23 damage was sometimes life and death important, however now it's just kind of... not so important. I mean, there really isn't much point to using Sorrowful Toad over Corrosive (more NCU for a meager increase in damage). As it stands, there is no point in raising Bio Met above what you need for the best MA heal (658 I think). I bet more people would increase BM if the higher level fist buffs added to MA skill.

I hate bumping, but... bump if you agree
