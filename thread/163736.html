Against people that disliked me for what I was, for people that were to busy to give a new person a second glance. Mostly against the emptyness of being a visitor , a tourist... a "noob"...
We are individuals, independent thinking people capable of deciding how to live life to the fullest for ourselves and our families.
Family is not only determinded by dna... but a bond to people. I take the acceptance of a guild as a birth into a family. Which is why I can justify and proudly say Im a SoS member. Regardless of what others think.

So enough of my rant. Once against my emotions have gotten the better of me.
