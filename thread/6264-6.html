Huntingground for Dodga part one
If you're lokking for a place to hunt robots for Dodga part one, I suggest the wall. That is north of Tir and north of Bot mountain and the Temple.

You will find A-4000 soliders and elites by the openins in the wall, and sometimes a bit north of the insides. The give away items fairly regularly. If you're under 70, avoid going too far north of the wall, there're Vultures and Sandworms there.

Good hunting!
