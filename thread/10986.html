Another idea about IP reset
Hey!

1. Remove all weapon/armor/implant
* I'm lvl111 (this title is 40000 IP each lvl), have Rifle skill 400 (max skill) and Pistol skill 300 and have 1000 IP
2. Click on machine (In shop, for example)
3. Confirm "you will LOST ONE LEVEL and 'inverst' your IP?"
4. Click YES and i have 15 min (example) to possible decrease skill.
* I'm lvl110 now and Rifle skill is 390 (+5 green skill each lvl, as usually) and of course also other skills, but Pistol skill is still 300 (which not max skill), and have 41000 IP (because lost one lvl)
5. Decrease all pistol skill to 150 (inverst up to 40000 IP)
* I have 81000 IP
6. Increase again some skills (example use 40000 IP) and Rifle skill is 395
* I have 41000 IP, kill mobs again (it take some time) and DING
