no...
There are pre-designated areas for certain ql level towers but NONE that are assigned Clan, Neut or Omni.

You can attack and destroy a tower not of your faction during it's 25% gas time. During it's 5% gas time you can attack and destroy towers of the same side also.

You can immediately (if you destroyed it) replace an enemy controller with one of your own (provided it is of the right ql for that area).

The areas rotate between 100% gas, 25% and 5% on some basis that I don't fully understand...the cycle starts when you place the controller tower.
