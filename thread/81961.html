Well, a lot of people don't think Biledrinkers are good hunting either, as they give about 30-35k per kill for 150+ players. I started taking teams around level 100 out there a couple weeks ago, and they LOVED it. 40-60k per kill, plenty of spawn.

I am still 100% convinced that biledrinkers could be the fastest xp in the game for lvl 150+, but I have yet to get the right team together that understands how to pull it off and is willing to try. The strategy is identical to the one I saw used at the eye camp a year ago for sub 100's in 20k. While other teams were getting more xp per kill at shades (red), we were getting more xp over all killin greens/yellows en masse.

Need a doc, enforcer, and as many NT's as you can get. Enforcer pulls ENTIRE CAMP, NT's wait til mobs all get a hit on enforcer, and unleash area nuke hell. Doc keeps enforcer alive. If a high level team could pull off 5-10 mobs per pull, with 3-4 kel's NT's you're talkin 150k-300k xp in the time it takes to take down 1 real mean.

After seein a cute lil crat's trick for pullin mission mobs, I'm tempted to revise my strategy for mission teams..... but then again, getting people to do something different is a challenge in it's own right.
