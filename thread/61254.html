Fix the Mid range suppressor gun!
Currently the Light suppressor and Heavy suppressor are great guns.. while the mid range is total crap now due to the change of removing crit dmg in burst/fa attacks.

To give you an idea here what we're looking at the following are the guns in the line and thier description:

Professional Light Suppressor
dmg: 1-410(410)
SMG/Burst/Ranged Energy requirements

The Light Suppressor was introduced as part of the Suppressor 'pack', but it wasn't too popular because it's so hard to master. It's a beautiful weapon in the hands of a professional though.
_______________________________________________

Premium Suppressor
dmg: 10-218 (450)
SMG/Full Auto/Burst skill requirements

The Suppressor is a twin-barrel submachine gun. It fires off many, many rounds per second.

_______________________________________________
Custom Heavy Suppressor
dmg: 1-455(320)
Full auto/Assault Rifle/Burst/Smg/Fling shot requirements

The Heavy Suppressor was introduced as part of the Suppressor 'pack'. It wasn't too popular because soldiers thought of it as 'too heavy', but with the recent boost of ability enhancing nano-technology it has become more useful and popular.
____________________________________________

Now... just going from the descriptions the light should be low dmg high crit, mid should be high dmg low crit, and heavy should be very high dmg moderate crit.
