Trade Skill Manuals
As of now, there are very few useful trade skill manuals in the game. The only *possible* useful ones would be the following:

Mausser (SMG kits are still broken, by the way)
Dissafilation Rifle

And maybe an E-Beamer, but unlikely.

The other good weapons (Hammers, novas, riders, et cetera) are not in manuals.

Yes, these weapons are powerful and wanted, therefore make them hard to create. (make it so maybe one of the items cannot just be bought from the store)

However, there is so little insentive to go into trade skills (except perhaps nanos - but even then it is a little useless unless you already have most skills [Comp Lit, Mech Engineering, Nano Programming, Field Quantum...]) when nothing is very profitable or helpful.
