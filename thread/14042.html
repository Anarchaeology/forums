PSA from Tourism Bureau of Newland & Mort
In the past week, we here at the Tourism Bureau for Newland & Mort have undertaken a massive advertising campaign to raise awareness of our fine lands. We've even gone so far as to remove the loot from your mission chests and replace them with MAPS to Newland and Mort. Didn't find a map in the last chest you opened? Don't worry, there are sure to be several more in Each and Every mission you take!

In the likely event that you wind up with more than one of either map, we request that you Please don't sell them to a vendor. Instead, distribute them to friends & co-workers to further raise awareness of the wonderful landscapes of Newland & Mort.
