The story of a nano enforcer.
Name: Bloodwire, nothing more, nothing less.
Organization: President of The BlackHand
Age: 36
Profession: Enforcer
Breed: Nano
Born: Omni III (Rome)

It might seem like a contradiction, a nano-enforcer, but the explaination to this is really simple. Most of my youth was full of joy, love and happiness. I was spending my days with my best friends Shodu and Muni, not knowing that my parents were Clan traitors working within the system to bring it down.

But at the age of 13 reality struck. My family had taken a vaccation, visiting uncle Shenshen at his home in upper street east bank. It was night, I woke up and needed to go to the bathroom. As I went in the hallway past the kichen I heard voices. I peaked in and saw my father, mother and uncle talk to a stranger.

The stranger was carrying dirty clothes and had a rather rugged look. My father handed the stranger some papers, then he started talking about OT forces and strength at various outposts. I was shocked at the discovery, my parents were Clan traitors, I just could not believe it. I went quietly on with my business. When we returned to Omni III, I contacted Omni-Internops and told them the story. My parents were sent to prison, and I never heard from then again.

Omni-Internop took care of me. I was soon sent to the Clon**** boarding school where a mistake was made, I was enrolled into one of the enforcer classes. I actually didn't mind this that much, aslong as I did not end up like corrupt bureaucrats like my parents. I just wanted to get along with people and integrate nicely into the OT workforce.

In the begining I was teased quite a bit for my weak body, but I had soon surpased every other student in skills of nano technology. It takes stamina to take large amounts of damage, but it turned out that to be a good tank, you need intelligence.

After I had finised my studies I joined an Omni organization called The BlackHand. I swiftly gained ranks and serve now as core and leader of the organization. If there is one thing to be leared from my history, it is that our enemy have many shapes, some which as easily identified by their allegiance and emblems, some hard to identify because they walk within your midst. The rot within OT must be flushed out and every Clan on this plannet must be burried.

I am Bloodwire, if you see me, you are my friend. If not, then you are already dead.
