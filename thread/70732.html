One of the expressed reasons for the NODROP tag was that people were dropping their cars and planes on the ground then getting killed or zoned out of missions.........never to see those items again.
Another problem was that some people would accidentally trade a backpack with the yalm in it and never see the yalm again....Yalm? In the backpack? What Yalm?

Still, there should be a way to sell them back at least. Then you could use it as a "trade-in" allowance for your new vehicle. Or just take the creds. Like selling it to the car dealers.

Another option would be to make the vehicles require a prompted answer to drop, trade, or sell. Similar to the "Are you sure you wish to delete this item" prompts. Then you could give it to someone if you wanted or could work out a deal.
