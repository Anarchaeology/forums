PROPOSED: Controlled Destruction changes
Ok, this is a more elaborate version of an idea I originally posted in another thread. One of Hayake's comments about an insta-cast nano got me thinking...

First of all, I'm aware of the other suggested changes to benefit other MAs and I'm not saying ignore those or that any of them are bad ideas, I'm simpy throwing out another set of ideas. Secondly, I don't think that asking for the world so to speak will get any results and I feel that the following changes would help us significantly but not seriously alter game balance.

Proposed: Additional effects of the controlled destruction line of nanos.

1. One-time root/snare breaking upon casting.

Upon stirring his/herself into a force of controlled destruction, the MA breaks any roots/snares currently in his/her ncus. This will allow MAs to break roots/snares once every 15/20 seconds but at the cost of not being able to cast anything (i.e. heals) for the next 15/20 seconds. I know there are vague rumors about whether the line already does this but I'd like it set to 100% chance. I don't feel this is overpowering in any way due to its nano-shutdown effect.

2. Addition of Armor Piercing %.

The nano will also allow the MA to 'bypass' some of the armor of his enemies, using his/her knowledge of critical attack spots. The net result is that when striking an enemy, the target's effective AC will be reduced by a certain %. As an initial proposition, I'd suggest making the IgnoreAC% equal to the amount of damage added for that particular QL nano (19% for Crash of Thunder, 94% for Inner Peace, Outward Rage). As an example, here are some of my estimated numbers from elsewhere.

Average level 200 MA (1100-1200 MA skill) against an average armored player (4000-5000 AC) with no reflects.

Non-crit punch: 150-300 (average 225)
Critical hit: 1200-1300 (average 1250)

Some rough numbers for 100% ignore AC.

Non-crit punch: 150-500 (average 325)
Critical hit: 1400-1500 (average 1450)

For this example, non-crit damage would increase by approximately 44% and critical damage would increase by approximately 16%, a nice boost.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

So there you have it. I really like this idea myself and think that if implemented, the controlled destruction+upon a wave of summer combo could be an effective but weaker sister combo to the enforcer's rage+challenger combo. I leave it to you guys to debate implementation in PvP or PvP+PvM. I wouldn't mind it in PvM and believe the changes would be in-line with the current and ongoing addition of more and more powerful weapons for other classes to use. In addition, the nano would be extremely useful against the uber boss mobs and their insane ACs. Melee fighters are already extremely gimped against them because of their insane parry skill as well, and this will increase the damage incredibly for MAs at least.

Thoughts, concerns, points of clarification all welcome.
