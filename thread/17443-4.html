By the...
By the sweet sweet coconut monkey gods! Cosmic, if it's at all possible, could you please ask the programming team to take a look at the mobs evade/parry abilities? They are way too high for the mobs level.

Even yellow's are getting between 4 to 6 parry's on me now, and that's with maxed out MA and brawl.

Parry is already a slightly unfair nerf against all melee users. People useing guns can parry melee'ers, and melee'ers can parry melee'ers, but melee'ers CAN'T parry gun users. All melee attacks can be totally resistsed in ways ON TOP of evades. Melee attack/brawl/dimach can all be parried, and the MA specials can be resisted. Gun users don't have to deal with this. Burst/Fling shot/FA/Snipe, none of them can be negated exept by evade.

I feel it would make more sense if parry could only be used by players and mobs who were equiped with melee weapons or fists ONLY.

I can accpet that I can't parry a bullet, but someone holding a 70 pound shotgun shouldn't be able to parry my fists.

I'm very grateful for all the attention the MA's and the MA board has been getting lately, and I'd like to thank the moderators, and the dev team. But, like every other class, we still need some work.

Later
Virind
