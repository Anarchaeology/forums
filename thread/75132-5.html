as a general rule, as long as your off-hand is at least 50% as powerful as your main hand, then you are not gimping yourself. anything lower and you are, anything higher helps. this is based on the multi-wield formula: damage = (main weapon x .67) + (offhand weapon x .67) and is only true when multi-wielding same-type weapons.

there may be init values that affect the accuracy of this statement however.

Well, you would want to count dribble-down from Agility and Intelligence. 60% Agi, 40% Int...

Here are just some ideas...

Intelligence:
+12 General Buff
+15 Advent Hood
+30 2x VT Eggs
+110 Implants
(More options here if you are Nanomage)

Agility:
+12 General Buff
+25 Feline Grace
+110 Implants

Could also get various extra points from Sukutek/Dillion/etc...but that depends on what armor you use, if you need buff weapons to get it on, etc..

Total Int: 167 * 0.4 / 4 = 16.7 points of MR
Total Agi: 147 * 0.6 / 4 =22.05 points of MR
Total: +39 MR

327 + 80 + 39 = +446 MR

Advent's MR maxes at 659 in TL 6, so...

659 + 446 = 1105 points of MR

River Seasons XP is 1147, BBI Faithful is 1155.

Doable? Yes. Doable before TL6? Not really.

1) No level 195 MR buff
2) No bracers (AFAIK, a TL5 bracer has never dropped on RK-2 at all, and doesn't drop any more on RK-1)
3) 619 base skill instead of 659
4) Not many TL5 folks have 2 VT Eggs
5) Advent Hood can be hard to get for certain folks
6) Juggler's Treat is a pain in the arse to get
7) Requires NW buffs

So... -40 from skills, -70 from Bracers, -15 from MR buff ends up dropping the total to 980 MR. This is assuming one is high enough to max MR (level 162) and has hit Breed caps in Int and Agi.

And, that's at level 162. Start stepping back and it gets worse and worse. Compare this to Melee, where one can easily Multi QL 200s at level 125. (Don't have an exact level..may even be possible sooner than that.)

While it's well and good to say that a level 200 toon can get it... Most Advents aren't level 200. The main problems with MR kick in around level 110. MR needs to be adjusted between level 110-190--more toward the bottom than the top. TL4 is a nightmare for MR, and TL5 isn't so rosey either.

Edit: ACK!! Big mistake. I forgot to multiply all Int/Agi attributes by .25 to account for 4/skill dribble down. This makes a HUGE difference!
