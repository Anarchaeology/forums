* FAQ *

How can I talk to people & keep track of buddies?
You can find, make, and delete buddies from your Friends menu, found at the top-right corner of your screen.

To bring up the chat box hit ENTER.

To send a private message:
/tell PLAYERNAME MESSAGE

Notice the /
The / means you're sending a command to the game. For more information try /help

To reply to a private message from the last person that sent you one:
SHIFT+R

From the friends list you can filter out channels you don't want to listen to, simply deselect them to mute them. Clicking on the name of anything from your Friends list will allow you to interact with that directly as well.

Example:
Clicking on VICINITY, every time you hit ENTER and type normally it'll send whatever you say to VICINITY. If you click on a friends name or a shopping channel it'll ALWAYS send whatever you type directly to that. Remember though if you use / first it'll always over-rule this function.

What is a whom-pa?
The whompa is esentially a transit system between cities, towns and outposts . There is a whompa net for each faction and they all meet at the ICC. The whompa's have a sign over them informing you of your destinination. Just step into them and you'll be transported to your destination. No skill is needed to use the whompa system.

What can i do if i find myself stuck in the worlds geometry?
If you find youself stuck in the geomtry of Rubi-Ka and are not able to move there are two common ways to free yourself. The first is use the /stuck command. To use this command you must be in the seated position, so press X to sit. Then open you chat box by pressing enter and type the command /stuck. You will get a message that you situation will be resolved in 30 seconds. Remain seated during this 30 second period. You will then be moved to a safe location after the 30 seconds has expired. The second way to free yourself from a stuck position is to use the /petition command. To do this open you chat box by pressing enter and type /petition <insert info here> and an ARK will come to help you.

What is the Abandoned Subway, and where can I find it?
The Abandoned Subway is a run-down area that you will receiveinstructions to help "clean up" upon reaching level 5. The Subway can be a good source of experience and items for new characters.Neutral characters will find their subway inside Borealis City,Clan characters in Old Athens, and Omni characters just outside the gates of Rome Blue. Players over level 25 will not be able to enter the Subway.

Where can I get missions? How do I find the mission once I've received it?
You can get missions from Mission Booths, depending on your level every time you use a mission booth it'll cost a small fee. The GUI is rather strait forward. A word of caution though, the higher you go the harder the mission monsters are. So you may want to start with something around the middle of the difficultly slider, but not under the mid-point itself as your mission would simply be way too easy and lack much reward.

Once you accept your mission you'll need to click your MISSION button, this can be found among the top-right icons on your screen. After opening this you'll see your mission listed there right-click and HOLD then goto UPLOAD MISSION. Once you do this you can follow the YELLOW LINE now on your compass (bottom of screen) OR open your world map and locate the mission from there if you're in the wrong area.

We highly suggest team missions...you'll level much faster.

Where can I buy weapons and armor?
There are NPC (Non Player Controlled) Vendors located around the cities and outpost areas where you can purchase equipment from. There are also Shops ranging from basic (items for lower level characters) Advanced (for mid level characters) and Superior (For higher level characters.) You can also find nano programs, implants, and trade skill items in these shops. To access an NPC or machine vendor simply right click on the NPC or machine.

How can I change my name?
This is quite a simple process, just type /name ingame for information. But be warned, you CANNOT change your nickname after LEVEL20. You can change your FIRST/LAST name however at any time with the /name command.

Where and how do I save my character?
You can save your Character at any Cell Scanner (located in cities and outposts) as long as it is aligned with your faction side. To use the cell scanner simply approach it and right click on it, your character will then be saved and you will receive a message letting you know it worked, this will cost a small fee based on your level. Wherever you last saved your character is where you will respawn until you use a cell scanner again somewhere else.

Remember when you die anything that's not a NODROP item will drop and be gathered at a RECLAIM TERMINAL, so be sure to stop by a reclaim terminal after dying as well to pick up your stuff.

What is the grid?
There are two types of "grids" in AO, get used to this one fast it'll become your friend. The main one is simply called "grid" - nothing special there. You can find a grid at many points in Rubi-Ka, once you access it (assuming your skill is high enough) you'll be able to travel to many places in the game quickly.

Some AO world map upgrades show spots where grids are, however we cannot support any specific one or another here. But a good starting place would be "Travel To Rubi-Ka," found on Cz's Useful Links thread.

Do I lose items or credits if I die during PvP?
Any items that you have Acquired since the last time you used a Cell Scanner (saved your character) can be looted by a player who has killed you in PvP. If you have Acquired nothing new since your last cell scan then no items will be lost due to a PvP death.

How can I get an Apartment? what if I want to get a new apartment? Can I have more then one Apartment?
You can get an apartment by stepping into any apartment door in a city BACKYARD. Backyards are generally right next to the area where you first started the game.

You can only have ONE apartment, however those who get a Shadowlands apartment will have their old apartment PLUS their new one.

If you want a new apartment then you'll need to visit a store and purchase an apartment application. Target yourself and use it; this will allow you to pick a new apartment somewhere. Be careful, these forms are expensive (for newcomers) and will remove all your old stuff from your old apartment.

How do I get help if I'm stuck or need assistance?
If you find yourself stuck in the physical geometry (example would be stuck in a ceiling) or ever need assistance with a bug, quest problem, lost items, or harassment you can use the /petition <description of problem> command at any time to receive help from the ARK staff. If you just have general questions please check these forums or in game on the Newbie OOC chat channel before using the /petition command. Also, don't be afraid to ask others for help and advice ingame, there are many a good citizens on Rubi-Ka who don't mind helping out.

Please visit this thread for more information on ARK, GMs, and petitions.

What is Over Equipping (OE)?
This is the process of bulking your skills up (temporary!) higher than normal in order to equip a weapon or something. Once the temporary measure has worn off, i.e. a buff, then the item is OE and subject to OE rules.

Some items cannot be OE and will fall off. Some items once OE will have OE penalty making them less effective.

How do I install implants?
To install implants the target slot must first be empty, the slots for implants can be found in the tabs where you find your armor/weapons.

Then you must locate a Surgery Clinic which are located inside most shops and in Apartment Areas. To use the Clinic you simply right click on it. Note: you must have the 300 credit charge it costs to use it. Once you have right clicked on the Clinic then you can either right click on the implant you wish to install or pick it up and drop it in the target slot. You can add/remove as many as you want so long as your clinic buff does not expire or you do not leave the playfield of the clinic machine.

Using a Surgery Clinic will give you +100 treatment points! Keep that in mind while choosing your implants.

How do I trade with players? Is it secure?
Trading with players is very secure. Simply right-click on someone to begin a trade. Once the trade box opens you'll see the areas where you can add items, where your trading partner can add items, credits given to you, or an area to give credits yourself.

We do NOT suggest buying items you cannot see! For example, if someone gives you a bag in a trade and says there are 5 pieces of armor in it, you should request they be normally put into the trade window where the items can be seen.

ALL TRADES ARE FINAL!

How can i see a description of an item or person?
To see a description of an item you can either hold shift+left click over the item (this will work on people too) or you can hold right click over the item until the menu opens and select the Eye icon.

What about socializing and parties?
There are several night clubs, bars and coffee shops available for each faction. Reet's Retreat, Baboon's, The Cup and others often hold player or guild hosted parties, contests and get togethers. Some guilds broadcast music that you can tune into via your internet conncetion. Keep an eye on the player events calendar for some of the public events.
