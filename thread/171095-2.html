The sun above the desert known only as the Wailing Wastes beat down on the massive form of an atrox among it's endless dunes.

Sweat pouring from every pore on the surface of his body, Kanatach flowed from stance to stance wielding a massive blade.

Seemingly against the very laws of nature the blade made swishing noices as it rounded it's lethal arcs, only to be stopped in midair as if it struck the very bones of the planet.

Muscles bulging, every fiber stretched to their very limits, ever nerve in his body screaming for some rest, to be given a chance to recuperate and regain a semblance of peace. No such thing was to happen. It was midday, and a long time until sunset.

In Kanatach's mind the news rumbled around like rocks down a hill: "14 days until the portal opens for the masses, 14 days until the unknown is unleashed! 14 short days to prepare for the end, the beginning, everything..."

The arrival of the news had shook him, as well as his clansmen, preparations had to be made. And they started here, where he learned the forms so long ago. Here, where his long dead master taught him about the blade and how to use it to kill his adversaries.

The thought of his master made Kanatach work harder, the blade flew through the air even faster, the swishing noice it had made earlier changing to a whistling noice.

In the air, high above the heat, the dust and the wind an old man looked down at the sweating giant in the sand. Leaning back he gestured towards the android driver of his vehicle. "Ready, " he thought, "he will be ready..."
