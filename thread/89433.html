A new way to travel: Waldram's Stutter Warp
Hey Engies!

On the "Next Patch" forum I got into an argument whether or not all profs should have remote grid access. At first I was for it, because I didn't see the big deal, it's just a travel accessory right?

The Fixers didn't see it that way of course, and one them, Lilnymph, actually managed to change my mind, with the argument that asking for exactly what others have is un-original and copy-cattish behaviour.

So I took up the challenge, and I think I managed to come up with a new way for Engies to get around, and I posted it in the "Suggestions" forum, under the same title as this thread.

I made it Engie because as an Advie I don't really have travel issues, and although Engies have Beacon Warp, that is of course only good for OTHER people to get around.

Check it out, and see if you like it.

I got an Idea
I read the stutter warp idea and thought that it was OK but there are other things that engis need more.
---------

It's late at night and my mind wanders and some time doesn't come back right away, and it found this gem out in the eather....

I was thinking of what Revelator said in the post about getting new travel but not stepping on the fixers toes and I thought of what an enginer's "fixer's grid" would be like. A SPACE STATION!

A space station that the only way on is the Eng only nano. It would have a trade Dept. and bank terminal in side it. and the whole place would give an area buff to all trade skills. The way off would be one of three Whoopa gates (exit only, no Whoopa-ing in) that drop you in either Tir, Borialis, or Omni-Trade.


It's not the fixer's grid. The fixer's grid is for travel. It can get you from most anywhere to most anywhere else. The engi station is for trade skills. Sure, you can use it for travel, but only if you're going to one of the big cities.

Secondly, it gives us the damn trade skill buffs we want over the traders. It might not help with placing towers, but anything else it would help. It would also give us a good place to rub elbows with other enginers. Share secrets etc.

Third, It's something we know Funcom can implement. They've added new dungons and the fixer's grid via patches, this is no different. And the patch that came just before Notum Wars gave us area, hell GLOBAL, buffs from the towers.

It could look a bit like the new character builder that came out before NW. All shiny and off-white, cool expancive windows that look down on Rubi-Ka and out into space. Ships going by...


WHat do you think of that? Am I too sleep deprived or am I genieus? (there is no inbetween)


I got nothing for PvP. Sorry but I think we're F_cked there
