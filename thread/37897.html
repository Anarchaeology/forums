Class Hurricane Now open for applications
Division 3: Omni Reclamation is now recruiting for Class Hurricane. Classes will begin on Aug. 1 so you will have plenty of time to find a sponsor and recommendations.

If you enjoy a military-style, role-playing org, then we are what you have been looking for. We have levels from 6-143.

You will begin your care er by entering our Academy where the basics will be covered. From implants to teaming to geography, all of your questions will be answered.

Once you complete your 2-week training session, consisting of 5 classes offered multiple times in different time zones (including Prime Time US and GMT), you will be the guest of honor at your graduation ceremony with your classmates at our Division Headquarters in Upper Stret East Bank, graduating with the rank of private (or Private First Class if you are the honor graduate).

You will then interview for one of the four departments you wish to enter. These include: Special Forces; Science & Technology (with both an Engineering and a Medical Branch); Covert Operations & Central Intelligence; or the Academy (serving as a drill instructor).

The week following graduation will be filled with excitement as you will be assigned your department, meet with your new commanding officer and mingle with fellow department members.

Once you enter your new department, you will receive advanced training from the command of that department, as well as assignments to help fulfill the missions of your department.

With time, work and loyalty, you will be rewarded with promtions up the chain of command.

Division 3 is a member of Project Revenant, a group of role-playing orgs on both the clan and Omni side, dedicated to making RK2 a more interesting place. We are looking to add neutral factions in the very near future.

Check us out at www.projectrevenant.com (Please ignore the news section. It is outdated as the creator of the site has left the game. The forums are still active, however, and we are currently working on a new website. The forums will serve the purpose for now).
