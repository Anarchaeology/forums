Freelance Investigator - Intercepted Communication
Conversations cross the galaxies in an invisible array of information. One such conversation between and Omni-Tek and ICC employee was tapped and sent to our reporter.

**Grid uplink connecting Password accepted Welcome to the Rubi-Ka Grid

I'm Lacey, a freelance reporter for the citizens of Rubi-Ka. I recently received an intercepted communication from an unidentified source. "Advisor Program" was specifically mentioned. It brings to light some interesting questions, the Advisors of Rubi-Ka would do well to bring rumors to rest with a public interview. I have noticed some citizens feeling skeptical towards the group's ideology.

I would like to thank the source for submitting this to the public, though it is my duty to inform you that perhaps this is not a tapped conversation. I can guarantee that I did not create or tamper with the file. I will attach the audio file as I received it. You choose what you believe.

!Audio attachment detected

quote:
"What do you have on this Advisor Program? I want details, the experimentation with Neo-Technology has not been disclosed in full. What are these lab rats capable of?"

"Didn't you read the report I sent you? I don't like talking about this topic openly."

"It's a secure link, don't be so paranoid. You know I don't understand all that scientific jargon. Give me the facts."

"Look, don't even try to convince me it's "secure". Facts, they are clearly written in the report. The process is so intensive that many don't survive the initial surgery of a neural network bonding with notum, coupled with strict mental training they evolve into the psychological profile. Any deviation to the profile would see an instant breakdown of the notum fusion. They will simply cease to exist."

"Are you serious? They can't perform anything outside the profile? "

"Nothing outside of the profile, they are the perfect humanitarians."

"That is crazy, what use are they to us?"

"I am not surprised you miss the point. You are so focused on the galactic profit you fail to see the small units that you must rely on."

"What are you talking about we are a family for each employee."

"Do you know how much red tape your employees have to wade through before they get any help? This program will cut your waiting list and costs on processing new arrivals. You will have happy employees that know your systems, so less chances for error."

"Finally you said something I understand. This had better go to plan. I want to see an improvement to profits asap."
