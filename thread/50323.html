There has been a lot of talk lately about trader drains. The most popular idea is for the negative effect to last 30 seconds and the positive effect to last the normal 3 mins, it has been suggested that this would benefit all in PvP (PvM mobs are unaffected by drains). The question would be would this apply just to trader debuffs or all professions debuffs as drains effect nano skills as well and so do some other professions debuffs (some of which last currently 5mins). Another idea is to make it so drains wont stack, ie a trader won't be able to cast a higher divest/plunder if the have a lower one running. This of course would mean that there will be fewer traders casting +132 (871 PM & TS req). If a change was to take place to the last self benifical nano line of traders (wrangles are for the benefit of others and do nothing for a trader), it may reduce the popularity to play of this profession further. IMO F/c would be better putting thier energies into game content and the possiablity of nano resistance for all professions (note divests are 115% and plunders 190%), wouln't it be great to resist dots and roots in team missions.
