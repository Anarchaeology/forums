If a solo Fixer at 113 can handle the Avalon camp (albeit with unspectacular results), two level 100 characters should have a fairly easy time.

The PW camp will be significantly more challenging, but is still possible if you are careful to only fight one at a time, and avoid the other mobs in the area, if necessary.

The cyborgs are relatively easy to kite, especially the ones in the PW camp, since they will want to be within melee range to get melee and ranged attacks.

See the threads about CDS armor in the Fixer forum.
