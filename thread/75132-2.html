Supa Leet
Likewise, my idea has always been that the main hand weapon should fire at 100% RoF no matter what.

I suggest the sliding off-hand scale for a couple reasons...

Offhand RoF:
Skill 1-500: 25% RoF
Skill 500-1000: 25%-50% RoF

25% RoF on the off-hand would probably not be enough for non-Advents/Soliders to bother multi-wielding Pistols, which generally have lower damage than other weapons.

To get more than a 25% RoF increase (which is LESS than current), one would have to raise skill past 500. This requires quite a bit of IP for other professions, and would probably not be an attractive option.

To get back to the base (current!) 35% output, one would have to get about 700 points in MR. 659 is the current non-implanted/buffed max for MR even for proficient classes, therefore this seems like a decent target.

Since one is not GIMPING their damage output with lower RoF, lower-level Advents would still be able to add damage by multi-wielding, even if they didn't achive the current 35% RoF increase now.

The post level 135 Advent, however, would then be able to *improve* their RoF due to more investment in the skill. Even for us, raising MR is an IP sink, and our MR buffs have very high requirements. Considering that there is currently no bonus for raising this skill, the Pistol Advents that have to max this every single level are getting shafted, IMO. It would also give Melee Advents a reason to invest in the skill if they wish, instead of just stopping after 823 to get their Super Sol Fire F equipped.

At 1000 MR, which high-end goal, yet attainable with work...multi-wielders would be able to have a 50% RoF on their off-hand--which is the traditional benifit from multi-wielding weapons with high proficiency.

Unfortunately, I think that while this is a good proposal, Funcom's devs may find it a bit too much work to consider implementing it... But, one can hope, can't they?

Currently, however... Pistol Advents have to dump almost 220,000 IP in Multi-Ranged, triple-implant, use at least 2 items, int buff/implant, agility buff/implant, etc... and get over 1000 points in a skill, just to raise their damage by around 20% maximum!! Sorry...but that's just wrong...

I can think of no other combat skill which is required to get up to 1000+ with little realized benifit from having that high level of skill whatsoever. Even special-attack skills contribute to lowering the recycle rate, and thus raising the damage of the weapon as the skill goes up. We have to constantly raise this skill just to maintain the status quo as we upgrade our main-hand weapon, and to protect ourself from lowering our damage.

-Jayde

Supa Leet
Even at 100 MR< my proposed "fix" would give your off-hand weapon a 25% RoF as well as a 100% RoF on your main-hand weapon.

Remember, that dual-wielding like QLs is very difficult even at lower levels--if not impossible. Low-level Advents often gimp their damage without even knowing it!

Given 100% RoF and 25% RoF combo, it would probably still be better than the current situation. Basically, we have a 135% increase total, which ends up being 67.5% RoF on both weapons.

In almost all configurations--especially low-level players who don't know how MR works--a 100%/25% setup would do more or similar damage as a 67.5%/67.5% setup.

Since off-hand weapons range from 40%-60% strength on average of main-hand weapons, we'll just say 50% for the sake of example.

Current:
67.5% * 1 + 67.5 * .5 = 67.5% + 33.75% = 101.25% damage

Proposed:
100% * 1 + 25% * .5 = 100% + 12.5% = 112.5% damage

Not only would the proposed system give a slight increase in damage, it would keep players from killing their damage output accidentally. You would always be main-hand at 100% damage, plus off-hand at 25%-50% effectivness. This, for pistols, is around 140% damage in an ideal situation with current requirements. So, between 101%-140% is better than the current setup of 85%-125% damage.

-Jayde

To Meepmeep:

i'm back...for the limited purpose of explaining where the 33% increase in rate of fire comes from, and the 12-20% increase in damage over time comes from that Jayde is referring to.

here is how multi ranged works -

attack time (main gun) + attack time (off gun) + recharge (both at the same time)

so, if you are equiping 2 guns with 1 sec attack/1 sec recharge (1/1), you get this...

1+1+1 = 3 seconds per attack cycle, with two attacks....OR...40 attacks per minute.

compare this to equiping only 1 gun with 1/1.

you would have 1+1= 2 seconds per attack cycle, with one attack...OR...30 attacks per minute.

the increase in RATE OF FIRE is 33% (30 versus 40 attacks per minute). and IN GAME tests show that this increase actually varies from 25%ish-33% depending on how well the game is functioning at the time.

now...DAMAGE OVER TIME is a different concept.

YOU WILL ONLY ACHIEVE THE 33% INCREASE IN DAMAGE OVER TIME WITH EQUAL QUALITY WEAPONS.

SOL users typically have an easy time doing this because the multi melee requirements on ql200 SOL "F" types is only about 800...which is achievable at relatively low levels especially with our buffs. So, it is possible to carry two ql200 SOLs.

IT IS IMPOSSIBLE TO CARRY TWO QL200 V3s, BBIs, or XPs, SO IT IS IMPOSSIBLE TO GET A 33% INCREASE IN DAMAGE OVER TIME.

You are able to equip a ql200 V3 in your main hand at around level 130ish. But, you can only equip a level 135ish-145ish in your offhand. As such, you are NOT getting a 33% increase in damage over time. You are getting something MUCH less. In fact, as Jayde has explained, a pistoleer advent needs to have a weapon that does at least 50% damage in the offhand just to BREAK EVEN on damage. At level 200, an adventurer can enjoy using only a level 170ish (if you have lots of extra equipment 180ish) pistol in the offhand.

A ql200 pistol/ql170 pistol combo will only give you about a 20% increase in damage over time, with optimal conditions (i.e. assuming you are actually getting the 33% increase in rate of fire - which you do not SEE BELOW).

Another NEW problem affecting RATE OF FIRE, and as such DAMAGE OVER TIME, is the fun and new reloading bug. Take a look at the V3 and River Seasons XP respectively. They have a clip size of 20 and 25 respectively (or that could be mixed up). During a long battle, you can expect to reload a number of times. EVERY TIME THE WEAPON RELOADS YOU LOSE 3 ATTACKS FROM THE RELOADING WEAPON. This, in turn, decreases the actual increase in rate of fire.

So, to recap, and as my final post, here are the problems:

1. Multi Range Reqs are too high so the 33% increase in damage over time is impossible for pistol users;

2. You can gimp your overall damage if your offhand weapon is too low;
3. The reload problem;

additional problems to look into would be the complete lack of the "alpha strike" from pistols. there are no pistol/burst weapons that would allow use to have a fling/burst opening salvo.

Finally, Jayde has never stated that you only get a 1-12% increase in damage.

Jayde gave an example of how as multi wield currently works, IF you are carrying an offhand weapon doing only 50% damage of your mainhand, you only get about a 1% increase in damage over time. Under his proposed "fix" you would receive about a 12% increase in damage over time even with a 50% weapon.

Please re-read everything that he has written and absorb it. Also, enable your Personal Messanger and i'll be happy to go over any multi ranged numbers that you want.

Good bye again. This time, for real.

Borrace, thanks for the explination on my behalf. =)

As Borrace stated, there are a few factors to consider here...
1) "Technically", dual-wielding raises RoF by 33% on two matching x/x weapons
2) "Realistically", dual-wielding raises RoF by 28%-30%
3) One will recieve roughly a 30% DoT increase wielding identical QL weapons
4) No good pistols can ever be wielded at identical QLs

Borrace actually understates one fact... Given an 895 Pistol requirement, if one can track down an Engineer and maybe a small wrangle, an Advent can get an Electronicum V3 on at around level 110 without OE.

Unfortunately, at level 110, an Advent can only get on about a QL 120 River Seasons XP offhand--maybe a QL 130 if they try very hard. At level 135 caps, an Advent can usually get a QL 150 XP on. At level 145, using Dichotomy of Nature, an Advent can usually get a QL 160 XP on. At TL5 caps, an Advent can usually get a QL 170 XP on. At TL5 cap/TL6 with many odd items, Int/Agi buffs and implants, and a lot of work...an Advent can probably get a QL 180 on. (Assuming the Bracers even drop any more.)

So, let's break it down:
code:
Level		QL		Damage Increase
Level 110	120-130		-10% - 0%
Level 125	130-150		0% - 10%
Level 145	150-160		10% - 15%
Level 165+	160-180		15% - 25%
Level ???	180-200		25% - 33%
Really, the only thing that keeps this from being a problem sooner is the Customized Desert Reet. While it will gimp damage with QL 200 weapons, in the pre-100 levels while wielding a QL 100-160 on-hand weapon, its low multi-wield requirements, high min-damage, and Burst/Fling combo make dual-wielding very advantageous. Without this mid-level weapon, pistol Advents would never really have a "good" dual-wielding situation. Unfortunatly, the CDR ends up gimping your damage post level-100 with no real replacement. While wielding a QL 90-150 V1/V2's, this combo is awesome...and makes mid-level Pistol Advents very effective. After level 100, though, it's back to using Pistols with insane multi-reqs at much lower QL than your main-hand weapon.

Melee is a whole different scenario. With an ~820 MM req, one can get 2 QL 200 Super Sol Fire's on at around level 135 very easily. At that point, a 30% DoT increase will be realized rather easily.

Also keep in mind, that DoT increase--even if RoF was 33%--will be a bit lower, since specials on one weapon override another. The Brawl/FastAttack on a Sol Fire contributes a nice chunk of damage to the main-hand weapon, but it is not factored into the DoT increase.

Dual-wielding works well with Melee! There are many viable Melee options... Sol/Byom, Sol/Sol, Sol/Scissors, Scissors/Gutting Hook, Sol/Gutting Hook, etc... Even 2x Black Petes will do pretty well.

Pistols are a whole other world, however... Mostly because every viable and/or interesting pistol has multi-wield requirements at 1100+... That is the biggest problem facing Pistol Advents.

Funcom has stated they don't want folks to dual-wield QL 200 Pistols. We ask WHY?? They won't tell us... Tetra asked Cz point-blank, and Cz replied that he was not allowed to tell the "real" reason for the insane multi-reqs on Advent-only pistols.

The idea that IP is the reason is total bunk! Look at Sol Fires... The C/F have multi reqs at 830. The T/E have 1285 reqs, if I'm remembering right. What's the difference? A couple hundred points in Dimach!. How is a couple hundred points in Dimach--at the LOW end of the IP cost spectrum--worth almost 400 points in MM at the HIGH end of the IP cost spectrum. (Because, as you know, skills are more expensive per point the higher they are.) The Dimach req on any C/F can be 100% implanted without a point in IP if the person so desires! Even if they do spend the IP, the differece is totally minimal. 208 points in Dimach? At level 125 cap, an Advent probably has almost 100 points in Dimach just from dribble-down. Do you know how much 108 points in Dimach costs? 25,272 IP. Do you know how much 455 Multi Melee costs at the high-end of the spectrum? (Assuming one could raise it that high over the cap) Between 250,000 and 300,000 IP--maybe more!

Basically, Funcom is telling us that:
455 Multi Melee = 208 Dimach
1 point of Dimach = 2.19 points of Multi Melee

Now, at the LOW END this may make sense... Dimach is an "expensive" skill... However, when one looks at the IP cost curve, any 1 point of Dimach between 0-208 is worth FAR LESS than any 2.19 points of MM between 800-1200. One can get around 830 MM/MM at level 135 caps with buffs and implants, with a base skill of around 591. At that point, each point of MM will cost 686 points of IP. Each point of Dimach post 208 will cost about 356 points of IP. As any skill goes up, it becomes much more expensive than lower-end skill--even Dark Blue ones. 208 points of D. Blue skill starting from 0 is worth much, much less than 455 points of an Auqa skill starting from 830.

-208 points of Dimach starting from 0 costs 90,272 IP
-455 points of MM starting from 800 costs around 300,000 IP (although you can't actually get it that high)
-208 points of Dimach is very, very easy to get via dribble-down and implanting
-1200+ points of MM/MR is impossible to get by any means whatsoever, including triple-implanting, buffing, and dribble-down/ability buffs.

So, I'm not quite sure who came up with this Dimach == stupid amounts of MM theory at Funcom, but it simply does NOT add up!

But, at any rate... Something needs to happen for multi-wielding with Pistols to become valid...

1) Give us Pistols with lower MR requirements. Heck, give them Dimach! I don't care! I'll gladly put 30K IP into Dimach.
-or-
2) Give MR protection against gimping damage because we have to wield lower-QL offhands

-Jayde
