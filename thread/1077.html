Would you buy an Emergency Medpack?
Emergency medpacks lock treatment twice as long as normal labs.

Adjusting for this, emergency packs provide about 1/3 more healing over time. However, this is guaranteed lower, as emergency labs require about 10% higher treatment skill to use.

They blind you for an unknown amount of time, or at least I don't know how long. Anyone want to spend the money to build *one* lab and try it out? :0

Given that, would you even be willing to pay store price? Again, I haven't done any calculation, but I doubt a doctor would be able to make a reasonable profit selling at store price.

If you wouldn't buy at store price, what price would you buy them at?

Note to Funcom: player-made items should always be better than store-buyable items. The whole point of people making things is that other people will want to buy them. That is how an economy works, even an enormously simplified game economy. No one wants my QL35 Triplejolt or blinding treatment labs or triple cost nanos any of the other crap I could make if I bothered to invest in the appropriate skills.
