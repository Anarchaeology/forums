I think the real problem here is within AO itself. If people are looking forward to killing their own guards then there is obviously something very wrong. That may be a bit extreme here, but at the very least I think if you attack your own guard than you should be branded a traitor. Then you'd have to choose a new faction, or maybe have to pay a fine or something along those lines.
