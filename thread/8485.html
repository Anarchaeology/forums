>> Hero's Mourning <<
Many of us Enforcers are shy and do not dare to propose new ideas for our own profession. We all feel that we are balanced and somehow fear that the Dev Team starts to tweak the fragile settings of our skills.

Still, an idea surfaced this morning with the conjunction of the future new Crat nano (saving XP a few sec before a death) and the fact that when the fight is really too though and the main tank dies, the whole party is pretty often wiped out.

The idea would be this Enforcer nano :

Hero's Mourning
Cast. time : 1 sec
Recast time : 45 min
Duration : 15 sec
Nano cost : 10
NCU : 10
Description : As a last hope to his party the Enforcer just before dying can cast this nano. The evil foe that is killing him will then go on attacking his dead remains for 15 sec, obvious to anything else.

As to be perfectly clear the nano once cast will be active for 15 sec and if the death occurs within this 15 sec, the mob will attack the Enforcer's remains for 15 full sec. Just enough for the party to run away, equip a plane or try to land the final blows.
