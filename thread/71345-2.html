Two new professions
The two new professions will be

1. The Hairstylist.

Receiving small bonuses to agility, and armed with access to many weird and wonderful melee weapons, the Hairstylist suffers large penalties to Intelligence and Sense. Using their innate lack of intelligence as a primary weapon, they bore their opponent to death with banal conversation. Startup items include :

Nanocrystal (Bad Hair Day) - Causing their opponents appearance to change so much that they are not recognised by their own side and risk being shot by guards.



2. The taxi driver

The taxi driver receives incredible, almost dangerous bonuses to speed, but finds it difficult to take shortest route to anywhere, and often zone into a completely different area than intended. Taxi drivers think they have a green intelligence skill, but its actually dark blue. As is their Map Nav skill. They have a natural resistance to roots, snares and calms.

Startup items include :

Nanocrystal (Road Rage) - All other vehicles in the area are forced to zone within 10 seconds or take damage.

New profs:

N00b:

A class specially designed for new folks, it is loosly based around the enforcer, with a few slight modifications:
  • Lower skills caps; the TL6 skill caps will be set at a low level of 100. This will help the economy by providing high level chars with no money to balance all those ultra-richies. We're also altering the IP awards for levels gained. They will be 1/10 of current rates for each TL.
  • N00bs will also benefit from an altered GUI, ensuring that they never know whats going on and can't learn the game properly.
  • Apart from that, we're gonna keep everything the same as enfs, because it would take too much complicated coding to actually make a *new* profession.


Ebayer:

Actually, the Ebayer isn't a profession as such, but rather an option to start a new character at any level you want!

Benefits include:
  • Having a brand new toon with all the most uber gear you want, plus unlimited creds! Choose your equipment straight from te database!!
  • We've totally removed the need to know how to play AO with this prof - you just wonder around and get other people killed!
  • Actually - we put this in to re-balance the balance that the N00b prof will create with regards to the economy, because we're not sure we want people to be able to buy useful things for under 500m creds, and we certianly don't want unique mobs being killable by anyone other than our most dedicated, on 24/7, no-other-life-to-speak-of, funded-by-their-parents players. We're also doing our best to get rid of these people by carefully reading their posts in the forums, and doing the opposite to what they want. It seems to be working quite well atm. We may nerf the ability to post constructive ideas in the forum, so that only whiners get through to us - it'll make easiere to figure out what you lot don't want the most.


The basic idea is to ensure that new people keep coming in, while old people leave quickly. This way, we can be sure that no one hangs around long enough to see how badly this game is balanced / built / planned, thus ensuring that we can market the product with slogans like: "x new players every week!" and avoid having to say "2x players leave every week!".
