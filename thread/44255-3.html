While it's a thoughtful idea, I doubt Funcom will go for it. While I realize it has happened in the past, to show favoritism in such a way (providing a free lifetime account) trivializes the role the entire community plays in the development of the game. Auno created an incredible resource, yes, but it was done of his own free will and through his graciousness the community received an impressive gift. Does it deserve recognition? Of course it does; I'd say that it's gotten quite a bit since its inception. Maybe FC could even go so far as to create an NPC merchant in one of the neutral cities named Auno the Item Master, or something along those lines. However, I think a line needs to be drawn somewhere, and I believe giving out a free account for something that was done on his own time is inappropriate. Like I said, it trivializes the contributions that others have made to the game.

So... instead of the free account, how about an NPC, or perhaps a bar of some sort (Auno's Treasure Trove?). Maybe even just a kind thank you on the main page.
