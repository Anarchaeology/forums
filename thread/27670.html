Clan victory = destruction of Rubi Ka
If OT were driven off-world entirely, the end result would not be pretty. There are more than enough interests out there (including the rest of Omni-Tek) to cause some considerable havoc. Firstly, the main reason many clans do not turn on each other is because of a common enemy. Remove that enemy, and you suddenly have some rather extremely different groups all vieing for 'their way' on Rubi-Ka.

Add to this the backing of various other hypercorps, and the warzone would simply just continue.

*OOC* *OOC* *OOC* *OOC*

Omni-Tek are an oppressive organisation, and capitulating to OT would result in the same situation as 2000 years ago - horrendous working conditions, poor employee safety, etc.
The only real way to a victory would be to convince Omni-Tek that it is in their best interests to let Rubi-Ka be self governed while maintaining their mineral interests. That way you get a planet of your own, and Omni-Tek military assets to protect it from other predators.

But that's asking for the moon on a stick, since most clans wouldn't want to trust OT with anything, and most OT wouldn't want to relinquish control.
