Just got this in an e-mail from Advisor Aegiel with ARK-Events. And he ask me to share this, so here is it is:


quote:

Hello!

For those of you who do not know me, I am an ARK with the Events Department,
in fact I am the writer and producer of the Gypsy event series, I just
wanted to reply to this thread because I believed it needs to be explained.
To start, the events are not static in any way, when we do something like
this we have a few goals in mind, but we never have the paths to those goals
chosen, that is up to you the players, and of course there always happens to
be the surprises that you the community give to us, once the concept of the
event is shared with the actors, it becomes an improv event, in this
particular event my intentions were simple:

1. To make a presence, ie. Have the Gypsies noticed.
2. To take a page from your book and to share stories that I have written or
have been given by other ARKs, as well as stories we read here on the
forums. (Yes I read everything you write, webpages, and forums.)
3. To create a fun roleplaying atmoshpere that could possibly lead to a
solid story progression.
4. To interact with the player community in a unique way.

Those were the goals I had laid out in my mind, from there I inform the
actors of what needs to be done, then it is completely left up to them, I
trust in their abilities and talents to such a point that I know they will
each add an interesting flare to the story.

Now that is only 10%, the other 90% comes from you the player community, in
this case I was quite happy with the event, it was not cancelled in any way,
in my eyes and that of my cast we consider it a total success, we completed
the goals that I had chosen.

You are probably thinking "But you left?" Yes we did leave, for a simple
reason...RP...If a guest you had invited into your camp starts to attack
your herd and drag them away, how would you react? Combat is not possible as
the Gypsy people do not control the gas, so all they can do is leave and be
disgusted, hence my decision, a roleplay reaction to a roleplay action.

Why did I not set the herd to level 200 and a 10k damage shield? Well I
shouldn't have to, level 200 means a large amount of experience for killing
it, which is more of a temptation to attack it, 10k damage shield isn't
really needed, why would an Ottous have a 10k damage shield, it goes against
the RP, so as our job calls for we improvise a reaction, I knew of the
dangers of having the Ottous there, but I decided it added that little bit
extra to the event so I chose to go with it.

I hope this answers any of your concerns, and thank you for attending the
events and making Anarchy Online and the world of Rubi-Ka what it is, we do
this for you and I personally feel honored that you enjoy what little I can
give to you.

Keep up the amazing work.

Aegiel
