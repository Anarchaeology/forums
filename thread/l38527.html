Problems with the latest patch, list'em here
Ok, there has been a lot of improvements, but looks to me like a real half a$$ed effort since it broke more things than it fixed.

If you got'em, list'em.

Here's a few of the problems:

- crashes, lots of them. Zone crashes (oh god don't even remember how many times clondyke went down today), client crashes, you name them.

- disconnects, lots and lots of them, especially in missions. I thought patches are supposed to fix problems, not bring back OLD ONES.

- zoning timeouts: my god, dumped like a dozen times going from trade-1 to broken shores. A lot of people experienced similar problems.

- search bug: hit search and you'll know what I'm talking about

NEW ANNOYANCES

They just nerfed ranged classes in missions by a lot. Ok, fine, fix the shoot through wall problem. But its still there, I can still shoot through the walls in some instances, so have mobs shot me through walls. But now, in addition to the mob's ability to call upon "scotty" to beam them wherever you are if they're stuck on something, they've all the sudden made it so ALL mobs regardless of type will run around and engage you at melee range. So if you engage a soldier mob, it'll run up to you point blank and beat you up like an enforcer, while blasting you with their weapons.

Funcom, what's the point of using weapons if everything and their mother will fight you up close? Can I be a soldier/Enforcer like your typical mobs so I can blast things from range AND beat them to pulp up close at the same time?

Are all your missions going to be nothing but hack and slash from this point on? First you nerf concealment so it isn't even really useful now (god I wish I knew so I wouldn't of dumped ANY IP into that now useless skill), then you broke search, now you make it so everything is melee.

What gives?

And I thought you said vendors are going to be able to talk to multiple users...it still isnt working.

Just my two bits...
