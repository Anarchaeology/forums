my $.02
nice ideas all over this thread...from all...not sure if FC reads this stuff...but here goes anyway...


WEAPONS, PILLOWS, AND LEET DOLLS OF DEATH:


* WEAPON DAMAGE/MUZZLE FLASH - when firing fire/cold/toxic weapons, it would be cool to get the effect - you shoot an el dieu, a succesful 'crit' might actually freeze the baddie for a little - which would surely make more people use the diablo/dieu series - those poor left out, seemingly 'could be cool' guns.

* NEW TOY - rocket launcher - slow and steady, the *new* soldier weapon and yes, it should fire an actual rocket with a gentle tendril of smoke that follows it to the target. and btw, soldiers/enforcers/ma's/adv's should be able to beat the bloody snot out of just about anything.

* NEW TOY - and i hate to sound cliche/lame - a lightsaber...or as we called them in Gammaworld, a Vibroblade...oh the sweetness...and yes, it should humm and whirr when you swing it. you think it's lame...but you know everyone will want one.

* NEW TOY - starship crowbar/monkeywrench - forget the beams and hammers ...how about an oversized (and i mean HUUUGE) year 30,000 black&decker-techer-wrecker - for the joy of being and enforcer - and to back it up, an enforcer should be able to use this bad boy to force open doors/chests. forget the whimpy lockpick - leave that to the fixers/agents/MA's.

* NEW TOY - EMG gun - no, not the BFG, ala quake - Electro-Magnetic-Pulse-Rifle/Gun - used against machinery/bots to stop it dead in it's tracks with a succesful hit could also be used against opponents to shut down/off protective shield/buffs they might be using.

* NEW TOY - Sonic Disruptor - a new toy that *doesn't* use typical bullets/energy ammo - i'm thinking about the toys they used in 'Dune' - '*Moadib *Moadib *Moadib *Moadib * - My name is a killing word' - could either inflict harm or perhaps stun/shut down an opponents shields or something neat like that - would be a nice crat toy and should use only the pistol skill.

* NEW TOY - Heavy Assault Laser - i'm talkin' the gran'daddy of all guns - immense, slow...something that was probably used on the port side of a small sub-light cruiser. only atrox soldiers can carry, and it's slow as hell but breathtaking on the battlefield. something in the line of the Wave Motion Gun - remember Starblazers? if that tells you how old i am.

* NEW TOY - Predator Frisbee/Disc of Tron - god, shame on me for plugging all these movie ideas...but...they work. a toy in the hands of someone capable...a small, vorpal, laser/heat guided disc that the user throws, it does a number on the target, and comes back. there is simply nothing cooler. and, there should be a slick chance that it does a killing blow and possibly even gets stuck...hence, the user better hope the target is dead to reclaim.

* NEW IDEA - the agent should be able to equip a 'pillow with important stripes' to silence his attacks. ooo...creepy.

* OLD IDEA - i *wish* grenade launchers and shotguns 'worked' as in area effect...hurting more than 1 mob...they have so much potential...i'm so tired of having to resort to 100% shotguns...and besides, a shotgun 30,000 years in the future? good idea...but not great. and if indeed a grenade makes a succesful hit, it should inflict harm on something nearby...and if you shoot too close, you better hope you have good armor on.

* SICK IDEA - the MP, ADV, and MA should be able to use a leet doll as a 'voodoo' doll. i leave the way it works up to you. >


CHARACTER CREATION AND SKILL SETS:


* ENGIE - the poor, poor engie - granted those bots do rock, but...dump the crat pet and give it to the engie. give the engie what he wants - he should be able to build 99% of the machines that live in the game and possibly even make his own. and engies should *rule* the game as far as the 'man-at-arms' master of creation. in fact, i saw a thread about character owned/run shops and the engies and traders would surely come into their own. it's not much fun interacting with machines/terms all the time and killing boss after boss looking for that cool toy/gun/armor/crystal you want. engies should also be able to use their skills to construct vehicles...and why not. it would be a bit expensive and time consuming...but it would beat the hell out of trying to find a hot-rod in a mission. engie's should also have a nano line or be able to use a skill to instantly repair armor or weapons that have seen too much battle...and perhaps, even improve it's lifespan or buff it up.

* CRAT - crats, of all people, should be able to 'beacon warp' friends and allies. is that not their gift? to summon a robot from the office? sounds cool...but the only somewhat cool looking robot guy is the ninja bot...and he should belong to the engie. no no...see, the crat should be able to pick up the cell and call up Brutus the skullsmashing enforcer to come lend a hand when the going get's rough. the crat, agent, and fixer should all have some similar line of nano. i always thought the fixer's summon armor/weapon line was somewhat cool...these guys should all have it. they have 'connections'. they should have all sorts of NPC's at their beckoning call.

* MP - MP's should be able to master the elements - not just emotions...fire, cold, lightning - and use them well. don't get me wrong, i think NT's rock as the 'mage' - but the MP is the true 'high priest' of the game - and his gods should offer him those abilities. summon a giant, burning, flaming skull...feel the *wrath* of the MP...not just the cute little meatballs/demons. i have felt that the more 'spiritual' characters (MA/MP) of the game are the alchemists - they should be able to roam the wild and find resources to make new 'spells/concoctions' that enhance their abilities. not just used some store bought, pre-fab creation.

* HEALERS - docs (and possibly MA's/ADV's) should have the skill and knowledge to revive fallen friends on the battlefield. that would be *the* skill that would set them apart from other classes make people turn to these professions for the right reasons. what is more irritating than forgetting to click that save term and you don't have a yalm. of course, it wouldn't work all the time and would require great concentration - and you would only have a certain amount of time to do it.

* BOUNTY HUNTING - agents (and possibly MA's, enforcers, and maybe crats) should get bonuses for 'bounty' - the ability to take out a target and maybe hand in a 'reference' of the target's *corpse* to a person/mission terminal - and they should certainly not be limited to the use of rifles...that's a sniper...not necessarily an assassin. the agent and fixer should also be the masters of traps, chemical concoctions, and sabotage.

* SNIPING/SUBTERFUGE - MA's/agents/adv's - the more limber outdoors-ey types...should be able to naturally climb trees/scale walls/move upside structures to gain a slight advantage over their quarry.

* SOMETHING TO EAT - i love the fact that i can make my guy play for days without eating/drinking. it should be part of the game and it should affect you in combat. period.

* SOMETHING TO READ - you should have the ability to acquire certain skills with reading. this should also be part of the game. not just getting good at it because you put IP into it. how lame. casting a new pet spell? read the 'ancient inscriptions'. i think books should also be a part of chests and monster drops. books should enhance something you've invested IP into.

* SOMETHING TO WEAR - MA's should get futuristic ninja suits or samurai armor sets...plain and simple. enforcers should get wrestling spandex outfits, mean looking biker jackets, and broken football armor ala Legion Of Doom. traders should be the ones wearing the nomad armor and dusters...as their profession reflects. MP's and NT's should have a nifty variety of capes and robes...not just the nano armor - albeit utter coolness. crats AND agents should be able to sport the suit - 'bond...james bond'. engies should be able to sport the 'dickies'...docs - well heck...shouldn't they come with a damn med suit? soldiers should be able to wear camo, light urban, and space-dock looking outfits/armor. adventurers should be able to sport wilderness backpacks and be able to set up a 'tent' which provides a temporary place where his group can 'zone' inside to safety and they should also be able to sport the avalon knight's armor. fixers - good luck finding your grid armor.

* WILD PETS - the Adventurer, being the nature boy he is, should be second in popularity with the wild only to Tarzan. the Adventurer (and i'm even thinking trader - the more nomadic types) should have the innate ability to call upon the monsters in the wilderness to do their bidding. not a charm or calm...literally, an obidience line of nano. Adventurers should be able to use 'call of the wild' and summon a cool wolf or even a pack of leets to be at their side. this would also be an interesting line for the engie or fixer....the ability to 'reprogram' a robot in a mission or outside...to have it do your bidding. and make it last a long time...not 30 seconds or something like that. as a trader, i always thought it would be cool to have a dog/feral animal to help me protect my stuff since i'm so slow whipping out the shotgun.

* TELEKENESIS - the NT - oh, how i could think of a million things cool for this guy. the living weapon - the nukes - how could you possibly change anything about him. well, for starters, i always thought of NT's and MP's as the 'Darth Vaders' or Emperors of the game. they look feeble, maybe weak (except for you crazy atrox lovin' foo's =] ) ...but they are not to be messed with *especially* at higher levels for *this* one reason - NT's (and possibly MP's) should have the one striving bonus that *no* other class (except maaaaaybe MA) has - the gift of telekenesis...the ability to move objects/people with the wave of a finger. that would probably be the coolest thing i could possibly think of in the game. the ability to not destroy a monster, but literally pick it up and throw it about a 100 yards away. to pick up a chest, boulder, chunk of machinery and hurl it at your enemies...is it a nano program or an ability? i would buy that. NT's should also have the unique ability to warp not just themselves, but enemies too...zap them into some rift...gone...forever. *poof* scary and cool. the dark gift should literally give psionic classes the ability to rip the gun right out of your hand. 'We would be honored if you would join us' *hawww* *haaaacchhh*.... >

* TRADERS - last but not least, as far as character wish lists go, my beloved trader. i don't care much for the healing nerf...i didn't like it in the first place. that's not what traders do. maybe heal themselves at best. traders are *not* that combat savvy...but they're no wusees either. the fact that i have a hard time slinging the only weapon i'm good at is beyond me. when i think of trader, i think of a dude/dudette who's seen a lot...traders are merchants....pirates...possibly even thieves/bounty hunters. i think the ransack/deprive stuff is a very slick concept. but traders are *very* resourceful...to the point where they should be able t shuffle through garbage and find something nice. they should be the ones who get *fat* bonuses to monster drops, they should be up to par with engies, NT's, and fixers as far as tinkering/NP'ing (which, for the most part they are). and this whole thing about traders being such wusses solo-wise...you ever come face-to-face with a disgruntled shopkeeper who has a gun or baseball bat? they make criminals think twice. i think the trader in AO should follow suit. they're not the meanest, but they ain't there to blow wind up your skirt either. IMHO, traders, should be the ones who find the goodies in the chests...yalms...nice weapons/armor...etc. *that* would make them a highly desirable class to want to party with.

* NEW CHARACTERS - introduce 2 new characters you can play - an alien race, and cyborg.


OTHER RANDOM GOODNESS:


* MISSION COOLNESS - when doing missions, you should not only be able to fight monsters, it would be insanely cool to bump into another group and be able to either party or fight. a neater variety of traps in missions too...falling floors/staircases...rooms that close in on you...more devious perils await! lol

* HEAD BUTTS AND DROP KICKS - enforcers should literally be able to pick your ass up and slam it to the ground - causing some serious damage. when i think enforcer, i think about the football guy who didn't make it because of a bad knee and instead became a pro wrestler who retires around the age of 40'ish and who won't hesitate to put your face through a wall or even throw you out a door. simply beating an opponent with the ol' beam or hammer simply isn't enough. this game rocks but it would be *so* insanley cool to see an MA or ENF literally send someone flying across the room with a dropkick or haymaker punch. a little more kinetic...a little more movie action. i really like the new super MA kicks...and there should be more to follow.

* TRAVELLING - more ways to get around....how about a 'motorcycle'...and i want to ride a gofle/pit lizard just like a guy on the web site.

* TANKS - there *should* be armed/armored vehicles that *do* have weapons.

* BAR ROOM BRAWLING - you should be able to brawl in the bars...but you need to be plenty drunk to do it and to make it slightly less appealing, you need to dump your armor and weapons at the door.

* ITEM DAMAGE - armor, implants, and even weapons should have the ability to be damaged. along comes those tradeskill/item creation characters who haven't done a DAMN thing since this game started. they will finally come into their own...and for good reason.

thank you all for your time...this game STILL rocks and i hope it continues to do so.

Peace, Fleece, and Chicken Grease...
