Pet professions, then belts.
What I'd like to see is either pets getting renamed right at casting, maybe by having some text box to fill the first time ever a pet is cast or by putting a /summon command that could be put in macros.
Plus it would be nice if timed pets (talking as a MP) issued a 10 sec warning before popping out of existence.
Then I'd like to see more complex commands in macros to allow some more intelligent behavior of pets. Their pathfinding is already bad enough without them being totally dumb. AND/OR/IF/THEN commands usable in macros to make them more complex could help give them a bit of intelligence, and that sure couldn't hurt.

NCU belts drop your memories because the game thinks you have none at some time. Having a full inv when I switched to a higher version the memories dropped, but one just popped out of existence. I had to struggle with buffs to make them fit my reduced buff space until I had a shop show what I needed.
There's logically no reason why one would switch NCU belts for one with less spaces. Could it be possible to make memories check the kind of belt that is about to come replace the older one and have them drop only if it's of lower quality?
Normally when switching belts it's because you're casting much higher nanos, thus consume more NCU space.
When a memory decides to just pop out, you can't buff correctly. If it isn't possible to have the memories check for the new installed belt, could we have a belt overflow window holding the memories?
