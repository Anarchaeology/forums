Lvl requirements on Nanos
My two cents on this issue.

I have been playing this game for quite a while....basically since release and i have watched things happen that were a good thing and somethings that were very very bad things. This is one of those very bad things that happened to this game. I started my first character way back when with no CLUE how this game worked. Since then i have gain much knowledge about how the game works and how to build a character that is fun and at the same time very well rounded. My main at the moment is a 129 doc who can solo lvl 160ish missions. I have in no way exploited or cheated with this guy (i have known about almost every cheat in this game and have NEVER used ANY of them because i feel that is unfair). My doc at this moment has the skills to use heals that are many lvls over his because of my implant set and the fact that i run expertises when i fight and heal. He also has a 900 attack rating (MA style) and can hold his own with vets and the occasional Master. I feel that this is in no way cheating because i have placed my skill in such places where i havn't wasted IP on skills that i don't want to use ever. I don't care if I can't swim or if i can't drive a car or what cause i can run fast and fly my yalm.

No the reason i think this is a bad idea is because this game was originally advertised as a SKILL based MMORPG. Adding lvl requirements to nanos or items or anything completely destroys that and changes it to a LEVEL based game which is not the game i signed up to play. If a person spends their IP wisely they should be rewarded with the ability to cast a nano that is over their lvl.

Now i know that most of this was done because of balancing of the professions. PvP is hard when you get someone who knows how to play the game and all the little ways to get better equipment on fighting someone who doesn't have that knowledge. I personally don't PvP at all because i just don't like it. Maybe when i get higher i will but that is besides the point. I think what needs to be done to balance PvP is to make a challenge system. You see someone that you want to kill in a PvP zone. you target them and hit q they get a message that sez you have been challenged do you accept? they hit yes the fight starts. they hit no they walk away. There needs to be a way for ppl who like to pvp and like the challenge of being ambushed to automatically accept every fight they are in so we don't have to do that if say we have a PvP event. But lets face it. Most of the people in the game aren't in the game to PvP, most want to play their character, get items, and make friends in the game. Gridding in to MMD and not being able to get out because you get GANKED everytime you try to get to the grid entrance can be frustrating and not fun at all. When i started out i did that and almost quit the game cause i got GANKED 5 times when i was walkin to the grid entrance by some guy who i talked to and said hey i'm just tryin to get out of here man i don't want to pvp. He let me get close to the grid then rooted me and shot me and as i died i saw "OMNI OWNZ JOO!! DIE YOU FILTHY CLANNER!!" I made note of the guys name and if i ever meet him again in PvP i'm gonna Destroy him

Anyway a summary. I think the idea of Lvl requirements on nanos is a VERY poor way to balance the professions. It completely removes this game from the game that i started and love to play.

Let me start off by saying I don't post a lot, but I read the forums 2-3 times a week, and have been playing AO since July 1, 2001.

Introducing level requirements in this game has got to be one of the most asinine, ill-conceived ideas I've seen since I started. Most of what I'm saying here isn't new, and has been said better by others in their posts, but I feel that I need to at least voice my displeasure with this change and "stand up and be counted" so to speak.

I really enjoy playing AO, but every time Funcom changes something, it seems like they are trying harder and harder to destroy what little bit of fun and creativity that is left in the game.

Like many other AO players, I come from a background of level-based games, and was attracted to AO due to its distinct, innovative and (to me) very interesting skill system. I thought that I had finally found a game that went for something other than the least common denominator in building your character--namely, the mindless "to use item/ability 'X' you have to be level 'Y' ; otherwise, you're totally out of luck"

The skills in AO would seem to allow a way around this, and I really enjoy creating characters that are somewhat off the beaten path (Nanomage Soldier who uses a bow, Katana-weilding doc, Agent based on maxing nanoskills/nano init who can actually do something useful in false profession besides buff, etc). "Uber"? Of course not. Fun? For me, absolutely. The myriad of skills allows me to focus on areas that might not normally be looked at, but that can provide me with a different avenue for survival and progress.

Also, for my more mainstream characters, nothing is more fun for me than to devise ways to squeeze out that ONE extra point of treatment or Strength or PsyMod or whatever that allows me to use something new and cool. I get much more enjoyment out of trying to build and optimize a character a certain way than I do from killing Veteran Functionary #459689895689435435 in mission #9348498545423.

Sadly, though, it seems that the powers that be at Funcom have decided to "change horses in midstream" it seems by adding (to me) completely illogical level requirements on items in addition to the normal skill requirements. I've been playing a lot here in the last 2 months, and my bank is slowly but surely filling up with items and nanos that my skills say I can use, but the @#$$!@ level requirements tell me that I can't.

Bionitrous had a very good point in another thread about what an illogical, lazy cop-out level requirements are, and how poorly they work in a game that has heretofore relied on skills. They are nothing but a slap-dash design decision that gets implemented when the designers are too lazy or apathetic to come up with any other requirements, ones that would make more sense and fit in with the existing game. Plus, the level requirements on most of the items strike me as wholly arbitrary, and pulled out of thin air, by people who never have played the game--"Uh, lvl 75 is a nice number, let's slap that on this new item." Never mind that I can use the item, self-buffed, many levels lower.

Also, can someone please explain the logic behind assigning level requirements to new class-balancing nanos???? Buffs that are comparable (or even slightly worse) than buffs that exist in the game??? This has got to be the single worst decision yet made by a company that is sadly becoming synonmous with bad decisions. I've read the official justification for why new nanos need levels, but old ones that are comparable, or more powerful, don't, and it makes absolutely no sense whatsoever. It is a completely indefensible position, and one that every designer at Funcom should be ashamed of. Screwing over 2 classes, one of which was universally considered to be one of, if not THE MOST, broken class in the game, in the name of some future expansion pack that we all have to pay for is a travesty.

I really don't like where this game is heading. I've stayed put through such debacles as patch 12.6, because, despite the awful bugs that got introduced, there was no fundamental shift in how the game is played. Now, however, some people at Funcom seem intent on mucking around with the core mechanics of the game, despite the cries of an outraged player base. I've put up with all of the well-documented problems with this game because I thought it had some interesting features like the skill system; Funcom now seems hell-bent on wrecking these.

The day my character's defining ability becomes level rather than skills is the day I quit and go back to another level-based game that actually works and doesn't suffer from all the bugs and problems that this one still has. I don't expect my $12.95 will be missed, and that's not the point of this post ; I just want to express my extreme dismay and outrage at the way the designers seem to be taking this game that I still love (although less and less each passing gaming session).
