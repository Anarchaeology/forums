2HO Battle AAR
Greetings fellow citizens of Rubi-Ka!!

Permit me to introduce myself. I am Antiphon, a hard working OTRK employee of the nano crystal research division. I am also an ardent hobbyist with many interests and I am communicating with you today to discuss my most recent addiction, military strategy. I am a novice and do not claim to be a subject matter expert, more of an enthusiast of a sort. To be more to the point I would like to give you the reader a few of my observations on the Battle of 2HO which took place on the evening of 27 October. I will place these observations in a format more readily recognizable by citizens of Earth in the late twentieth century than by our beloved employees of Omni-Tek. What follows is an After Action Review of the Battle of 2HO. I will point out issues and offer some recommendations and conclude with a summary.
First allow me to set the scene as it were: I was mulling over a bront meat sandwich at the burger bar in the Entertainment District when an urgent call from Commander Thornson came over the communications channel stating he needed assistance to repel a clan assault which had succeeded in taking the 2HO facilities and that all OTRK employees available should meet at the Outpost 10-3 staging area. Myself along with many others immediately made haste to the outpost.
I will now breakdown the events into the following B.O.S. systems or more commonly known in the late twentieth century military circles as the "Battlefield Operating Systems": Intelligence, Maneuver, Command and Control.

INTELLIGENCE:

Issue: Upon arrival to the Outpost there were many OTRK employees gathered in various stages of preparation. They did not appear to be organized or conducting any type of information gathering activities, although I did overhear shouts of individual employees claiming that they would go in and scout out the area, I am unsure it was clearly communicated to the chain of command or at least to commander Thornson.

Recommendation: According to military strategists of the past it is imperative to understand your enemy and what your enemy is trying to accomplish and how your enemy is doing that. Therefore, accurate and timely information must be made readily available to the leadership. This can be accomplished by designating employees as reconnaissance elements whose sole task is to determine the enemies dispostion (how he is arrayed on the battlefield), composition (What the enemy element is made up of) and strength ( Numbers, how many). The recon elements then keep their attention focused on the enemy reporting all information directly to the leadership in clear concise reporting formats.

MANEUVER:

Issue: After a significant amount of time had passed, Commander Thornson ordered employees into the fray. Some of the employees went through the Whompa at Outpost 10-3, some utilized the Grid, still others utilized rather unscrupulous and risky transportation through the "fixer" network (Not recommended for citizens of good standing). All of the above transportation modes were in piece meal fashion, there were a few who did not go at all.

Recommendation: Leaders must task organize based upon the situation at hand. In this case it may have been possible to assign teams in the following manner: Breach team, Support team, Assault team. The breach establishes the "beach head or footing" (as it is referred to in ancient infograms), The Support team lays down the suppressive fire thereby fixing or holding the enemy, the Assault team (heavy hitters) flanks and destroys the enemy. It is highly recommended that a leader quickly assess the situation at hand, task organize his/her forces, fire and maneuver on the enemy and all the time keeping the action moving and flowing.

COMMAND AND CONTROL:

Issue: Although this observer was not privy to conversations that may or may not have taken place between Commander Thornson and other high ranking employees it was quite evident at the lower employee level that communication was a challenge. Other than an idle shout to take the hill, no plan of action was ever effectively communicated from the leadership to the "Grunts" (An unfamiliar term that may have to do ancient vocalizations emitted by soldiers?) on the ground.

Recommendation: Task organize and communicate. When a leader is presented with a problem, he/she must identify the problem, come up with possible solutions and war game solutions to pick the best possible answer. Formulate a plan, develop the plan based on the intelligence gathered, organize his/her elements to execute the plan and then execute. While gathered in the assembly area (Outpost 10-3) Commander Thornson should have ordered employees to form into teams and then have team leaders report directly to him. During that time, reconnaissance teams are immediately dispatched to the mission area and begin feeding reports to the commander. The team leaders will operate two communication channels, one for their team and one to their higher superior in order to effectively communicate to subordinates and superiors alike. It is important to keep conversation on these channels concise and to the point, a technique is to apply standard reporting procedures for situational reports, spot reports etc..

Issue: Once I dove into the fray through the Outpost 10-3 Whompa to 2HO I found it difficult to acquire targets and avenge my fellow employees. There was a lot of people pointed every which way of all different races, breeds and genders with a multiple variety of weapons, armor and such, I was extremely concerned that one of my fellow employees would strike me down.

Recommendation: Fratricide can be an issue during any conflict. Fortunately for the citizens of this century it has become somewhat simplified, but if our employees would wear a recognizeable uniform it would be even easier to quickly target non friendlies and also to provide an adequate assessment of progress such as casualties sustained in order to order further reinforcements. A method that could be utilized is to ensure that our employees enter combat in appropriate company attire ( Such as Omni Issue Elite Armor, Recruit Armor etc,) or perhaps the entire main effort of the operation puts on white gloves.

SUMMATION:

As members of a huge and might I add successful corporation we all recognize the necessity of efficiency. In my humble opinion the Battle For 2HO was "Efficiency Challenged" to say the least. After researching late twentieth century writings from Earth I believe that we can apply some of those ancient war fighting tactics, techniques and procedures to our modern day era. If you are to become a commander or are currently holding the title of command you should be aware of the following things: Identify the problem, Develop a Plan, Gather Intelligence, War game solutions, Choose a solution, task organize, communicate effectively(to the lowest level), execute. There is also noted that at all times you should "Rehearse, rehearse.......REHEARSE."!! I find this new hobby of mine extremely exhilarating. A most interesting and intriguing topic of discussion. I would be more than happy to discuss my opinions over a cup of coffee or perhaps with your friends at the local night club. Thank you so much for reading this, look for more in the future, and long live Omni Tek!
