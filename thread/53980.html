Anarchy Online Timeline - To Suggest or To Not Suggest
I think from an MMORPG standpoint the game would make a lot of money if it had a weekly debut of idiocy on how not to run an MMORPG. Send it to David Letterman and let him do a comedic stint on it.

The topic of suggestions comes at the end but here have a look. Let's have a familiar run down of Anarchy Online on the comedic level.

quote:
Anarchy Online does not have phone support
25 June, 2001
Editor
Anarchy Online does not have any phone support for the simple reason that we have found e-mail, and in-game support more effective.
Recently there has been an influx of people trying to get a hold of the support team by phone. If you did not already know, we don't have any phone support. Why? You may ask. Our experience is that supplying support in-game or by e-mail is a lot more efficient.
Considering this, it is important that we are consistent. If we weren't, people calling would effectually be jumping the line, ahead of people waiting for a reply online or by mail.
If you call Funcom, you will get to the reception. The girls working there are not in the Anarchy loop, and they don't know how to solve your problems. We would appreciate it if you contact our Support Team through the right channels, or ask other players at the Bulletin Board.

quote:
Problems Yesterday
30 July, 2001
The Anarchy Live Team
Last night we experienced some problems when we patched and released the new dimension, read here for more information.
We decided that we wanted to push this patch out as soon as possible. It had become clear looking at the number of players compared to server capacity, that another dimension was needed, since with only one dimension we saw that there were players that were unable to play at all during peak hours. We had tested the patch several days on the test server, so we were confident it was in working order. We wanted to release the patch and the new dimension, however there were a few issues that became apparent.
The first thing was that people were unable to zone. After this problem was fixed, another problem occured. Our server manager crashed several times, making it difficult for people to login to the game. We fixed this as well, but then experienced new problems with the server manager. At this point, we decided to bring the servers down while we worked on the problem. After several hours, we had everything working correctly again.
We apologize again for doing this in prime time. We see that we might have been a little too hasty about this, since we wanted to release this to you as soon as possible.
-Anarchy Live Team

quote:
2.1 - Are we too easy on exploiters?
I've received several mails stating: "I really like AO, but it bores me annoyingly when I am in teams or see people who exploit as much as they can, and even doing so without fear because they feel there are no consequences." As this article will show, there are consequences!
ANY society, also Anarchy Online, is shaped by the rules of conduct. People behave differently according to the laws. It's a simple as legalized drunken driving. More people would do it even though they knew the risk of accidents would rise tenfold. It's that simple.
Funcom has a different banning policy than any other MMORPG. It might be based upon naivety.
How we handle exploiters
If a player's "record" is clean when we find that he/she exploits or is abusive, they get a notification. The next time the player is found out, he/she will get an official warning.
On his/her next offense, the player gets suspended for a varying length of time, based upon how grave the offense was. This will happen a maximum of two times.
On the third strike, the player is banned.
We have so far banned 628 accounts.
We do not ban all other accounts on the same credit card.
It might happen in cases of "heavy" exploiting, like severe duping (item-duplication), that you do indeed get suspended on the first offense. This is rare, but on certain occasions we have no choice. Let me add that the reason for banning is explained to the player.

Original Topic Here
We only banned 628 accounts but we are sure that we gave them all a fair shake. We didn't go out and er accidentally ban a few hundred folks on accident, not without going through our official rules.

Our official rules state that we will give you many ample warnings and chances but that if we feel it is too severe, a loose term we learned from Judge Judy of the Peoples Court, we will simply ban you. I know it seems strange that we seem to just ban everyone without giving them a warning like we show but we promise you it was all justified only we cannot show you our findings because they are confidential.

(SUGGESTIONS)

Learn from your past mistakes Funcom.

1. Don't be hasty
2. Don't release something that has many bugs.
3. Learn how to communicate properly with your customers.
4. When you promise something, keep the promise.
5. When you come up with a system, stay with the system.
6. Don't change policy or improvise and create it on a whim.
7. Listen to the Customer for they are always right.
8. Progress only takes shape through creative minds.
9.. Even if a person is creative it doesn't mean they actually can "DO" something with that creativity. Perhaps you should find the successful DOers.
10. Last but not least, if at first you don't succeed, try and try and try again.
