Agg/Def woes and NanoCasting - What can be done?
I've been wondering what can be done about the situation of casting vs aggressive/defensive sliders in this game. Why is it that caster classes get stuck pushing their slider to full aggro just to cast at their max speed? They already have a disadvantage (most casting classes) in that they don't have the IP or eases to up their defensive stats, yet they don't get any benefits from the little that they do have as they have to be max agg to cast at their fastest (untli their nano C init is high enough to compensate that is).

Here's a possible solution to this problem:

Remove the agg/def slider from the casting requirements and add in a slider for casting as well. This new slider would be based on the same times (2 to 0 or is it 1 to -1 ? errr different topic) as the agg/def slider, but would be only for casting.

At max speed you'd be able to cast at the fastest possible cast time, but as a penalty for the quck cast time you'd have a greater chance to fumble or get interrupted.

At max caution you'd get a MUCH smaller chance of getting interrupted/fumbling (as you are concentrating so much, interruptions have less of an impact on you).

I know that the slower setting seems to give little advantage, but there should be some reason to consider using the slower settings. There should also be some penalty to having it set to max speed. This way casting classes could have more defense going for them, while still casting at max speed if they need to, and slower if there is some reason why they'd need to make sure that the cast goes off well (that opening blast for example).


Any comments/opinions on this as one method of getting the casters working a bit better?

I guess people miss the point of my posting.

1) There would be no "casters get all benefits with no negatives". The negative side would be less reliable casting at a faster cast/recharge speed. This helps both fighters and casters.

Agents/MAs who may have high inits for their attacks would not be stuck with slow casts just because they want better defense (for example).
NTs/Crats could chain-nuke at max speed and not get slowed down cause they want their puny defnesive skills to work better.

2) Casting classes would be able to slide to defense, getting rid of that annoying auto-attack back against mobs who hit them.

Again a Crat for example is busy trying to mez, charm, root, fear, etc. and while trying to crowd control, a mob hits him from behind, swinging him away from a vital target at the time when he's about to charm a mob killing one of his team mates. IF he could have been on full def, the teammate would have been saved instead of him swinging around and cancelling the cast.

3) FunCom would then be free to fix up various issues with both weapons and nanos without the changes having reliances on each other (as they do now).

I'm not saying introduce a slider with just benefits for the casters and no side-effects, I'm saying introduce it in such a way that people would still have to still make choices about how they want it, with those choices having positive and negatives points.

(I'm pretty sure most teaming people would love to have a doc who could survive a bit better due to his ability to go full def while fighting Cyborgs in Mort).
