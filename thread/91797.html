Economy
Im thinking the economy could really use a money sink or 2.

I've heard a couple good ideas like randomly selling ga4 or ns2 or pppe in shop stores at the same rarity as the drop. (i support pppe the most)

Couple of my ideas are:

Make a cluster shop that sells all QL200 clusters but at like 2.5x the price. I think alot of people would use it to save the time and frustration.

Make a special store that you have to fight through a dugeon to get to where QL 125-200 chargers and stims and other goodies are sold. (yea that idea is kinda weird i know)

Or you can make a store that charges like a 5m cover to get in that sells QL125-200 chargers and stims and other intresting stuff.

make the new yals more reasonable a price so people will actually buy em. And make it so the higher the QL the less skill required. Make the manta vechicles actually worth the price so people will want to buy em. Keep em the same price though.(increase AC increase dmg add reflectAC etc)

You can sell a item called a hologram envelopment suit. Basicly you can wear any armor you want but the hologram suit thingie will allow you to look like your wearing any social clothes or armor. So you will have a secondary wear tab where u put the items that you want to look like your wearing. You can sell that for a moderately hefty price like 20-25m or so.

All these ideas dont add anything which will affect balance so i think they would be effective money sinks.

Nice ideas, all of them.

One thing that would seem perfectly reasonable to me, and which someone pointed out earlier, is why the vehicle prices go up with QL. For something like a yalmaha they are essentially the same at all QLs. I have never noticed a significant different simply in someone having a higher ql yalm. Because of this, everyone buys the lowest ql ones they can find. If, however, the lowest ql ones had a req of around 200 and counted down to the one that costs 10m but only has a req of 80, then people would sink tons of money into their alts to get low ql yalms and the higher levels would save the IP by getting as high as possible. With the other vehicles that have better and better additional stats, it isn't so much of an issue. But still... those use reqs are unpractical at high qls.

Anyhoo... even with all these nifty ideas, would it start to lower the price of things like GA, PPPEs, Tara Loot, etc? Or would they just stay so high?

I think the major problem is the ability of higher level characters to make so much more money than the lower level characters. In an hour with my fixer I can make a quick million without much effort, which will support a twink till at least lvl 25 if I give them cast of items and other outgrown things. That is where the economy has it's problems. Once you have a higher level character, you get into the upper classes and make all your characters like that, which leaves those without high level alts feeling unable to get anythign good.
