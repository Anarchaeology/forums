Gonna go against the grain here somewhat...

I don't believe the damage on fists should be increased much, or at all. In PvM MAs still do very nice damage and that aspect doesn't need to be changed very much. Using MA specials with MA weapons is one change I do like.

My proposed change would be an item/nano that gives an MA in PvP a sort of 'piercing' damage that ignores the armor (AC) of the target. As the MA increases in levels (or uploads higher QL nanos) the % of the target's AC being ignored increases. As a pure example (not considering balance), you could have something like:
Title 2 - 20% of Target's AC ignored
Title 3 - 40% of Target's AC ignored
Title 4 - 60% of Target's AC ignored
Title 5 - 80% of Target's AC ignored
Title 6 - 100% of Target's AC ignored

This would significantly boost damage in PvP but not change PvM.

Some estimated numbers:

Average level 200 MA (1100-1200 MA skill) against an average armored player (4000-5000 AC) with no reflects.

Non-crit punch: 150-300 (average 225)
Critical hit: 1200-1300 (average 1250)

Some rough numbers for 100% ignore AC.

Critical hit: 1400-1500 (average 1450)
