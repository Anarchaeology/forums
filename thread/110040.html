Poem
I felt like writing this, and it seemd as good a place to put it as any....feel free to comment where you may..

And though it may be late
when you see the rising sun
I know now right here in my heart
my winter's just begun!

Cold and broken dead and frozen
heartless from despair
Heart is breaking lungs are aching
breathing this arctic air!

Locked in the embrace of winter
heartless glitter
shimmering flitter
I dance upon the lake
as hard as stone
as cold as death
I need fire to burn me
A heart to turn me back
I need a love to turn me back
I need time to take me back!

To where
my soul lies in spring...
