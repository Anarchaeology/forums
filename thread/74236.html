Agent Modifications...(I think these are cool)
Ok, I have been an agent ever since I have resided on Rubi-Ka. I have always believed that an Agent should be actually able to spy, scope out enemy positions, and be a significant part in the story unfolding before us on Rubi-Ka. Here are some of my proposed "ideas":

1. Add a False Alignment Nano. This nano, can make an Omni-Tek agent turn into a Clanner for a brief period of time, say one hour. Since agents are spies, and if we go to spy on Tir, the Sentinels will shove a couple of energy bullets down our throat. With this little nifty device, we can actually spy! With this thing, there might be a bit more more chaos, but hey...this is called ANARCHY Online.

2. For God's sake, give us some sort of special ability other than Aimed Shot. Funcom all ready managed to [u know what] us over. At least you can have the decency to give some sort of attack that takes an aim (unsneaked) at the head causing massive damage. I have told other people about this, and they have said that the game will turn into the favor of the agents way too much like that. As my response, I ask you this question: Does anyone really moan and groan about the Martial Artist's Dimach abilities? I don't.

3. This is not my idea, but I would like to poin it out. We have a car called a Mid (but of course you knew that). It looks a lot like a probe. So when we go inside it, why cannot we take up the identity of a probe? I mean if we are spies, why can't we? We can just float up to an unsuspecting Clanner and say: "Join Omni-Tek today, Omni-Tek protects." and immediately run into a Wom-pah. I think this is more psychological propaganda. This can make even a 200 doctor start checking every probe around him for a pesky little Agent. By the way, a pesky annoying agent was what I had in mind when I arrived on Rubi-Ka.

4. This one is a lot like #3, but with a little difference. If we are spies, then we can really go into enemy territory with safety, until the [you know what] hits the fan (which is when your cover is blown). So, if we did my new False Alignment Nano, and donned the Sentinel weapon and armor, we should see a name change from [your name] to Sentinel. Like that, you can still patroll the streets of Tir or Athen, BUT AS A SENTINEL. Same thing goes for the ICC Peacekeepers and the Omni-Pol guards.

5. To go along with these new modifications. We could have missions DEEP into enemy terriroty or into cities where normal OT emloyees cannot reach because of the patrols. But, agents, could recieve this special mission and go into enemy territory, disguise himself in certain clothes, profession, and alignment, and either assasinate, convert (from OT to Clan or vice versa), or stalk.

I tried not to make these suggestions seem like I am a pure agent lover, but other professions can have some some of their own benefits as well.

Please, reply with thoughtful comments, and feel free to add to my list. Be sure to give details. Farewell.
