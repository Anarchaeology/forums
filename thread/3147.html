To TBA and Azzazel
When it comes to politics I am as opened minded as I can be on such issues. I have read your recent press release and very nice it is to. You make clear as you have done may times that you do not support Ross and yet you follow Omni-Tek.

I dont see how this is possible myself but then again I have come to the conclusion that perhaps its a matter that I dont fully see your point of view.

See in all this time never once has TBA made its policy clear. I have heard Azazzel talk of length about removing Ross and replacing him as head of Omni-Tek. However in talking to the guilds that make up TBA the matter does not seem so clear.

They did not want to see Ross replaced and seemed somewhat shocked by the fact Azazzel wanted to be put in place of Ross. The seemed to just be against the Amnesty, which if true does beg the question why form TBA in the first place.

This is really the puzzel and the question for Azazzel as the spokeperson for TBA to answer.

What is TBA ?. are you a political group ?,

What is the Policy of TBA ?, its ok to say replace ross but what then, what is the future under TBA, how will you deal with ICC. You claim Ross is doing no good, but how will you change that.

So I am giving you the chance Azazzel to explain to us your master plan. This great vision for the one single empire that is TBA. Myself and I am sure the rest of Rubi-Ki await your reply with interest.

From TBA Wedsite 6th of Dec

Amongst the various Omni-Tek factions there seems to be a few misunderstandings. I hope to lift the fog that has clouded so many minds. First off Amnesty dose not equal ceasefire. Secondly Ross is not Omni-Tek. Once you understand these two things you may better understand why the guilds of The Black Alliance feel compelled to work together for a better Omni-Tek.

Many have raised this point but some still associate the current offer of amnesty for the criminals on Rubi-Ka with a cease-fire with the Council of Truth. There is no such ceasefire in effect. Omni-Tek employees are encouraged to "try out that new gun on a clanner today" or "Kill a clanner today. Be happy tomorrow" whenever they are Omni-Tek cities and outposts. It is every employee's duty to kill every clanner in sight as the opportunity arises. We are at war! Anything less should be taken as collusion with the Council of Truth. Yet many employees discuss treaties with factions of the Council of Truth. Many more think there is a ceasefire in effect and they should avoid killing any clansmen. Believe me. The Council of Truth is not under such delusions. They are bent on the destruction of Omni-Tek on Rubi-Ka. To believe anything less is naive.

The second misconception is the most widespread and potentially the most dangerous. It is common in humanoid history for people to latch on to a figure and associate this person as hero ... or savior. Many times this has lead organizations or nations into tragedy. To blindly follow an individual is dangerous. Ross is not Omni-Tek. Omni-Tek is not Ross. If what Ross is doing is bad for Omni-Tek then will you follow him blindly into Omni-Tek's destruction?

Omni-Tek is bigger than Rubi-Ka. They own many other planets. Ross is only running this one. He is truly doing a bad job of it. Lets take a look at his highlights.

1. Uprising
2. Lose of Athens
3. Lose of most of the northern territories
4. Unleashing Shock Troopers on Omni-Tek employees ( Yes they actually did this. You can thank Derva for this one.)

I don't know about you but I want what's best for Omni-Tek. Is Ross what's best for Omni-Tek on Rubi-Ka? He's not done that great of a job so far. I hope that you all will not blindly follow Ross. We pay for his mistakes everyday. Remember... Ross is not Omni-Tek. Omni-Tek is not Ross.

Recently The Black Alliance has been demonized by some. These people while meaning good are just very misguided. We guilds of The Black Alliance want what is best for Omni-Tek on Rubi-Ka. No matter what the cost. Victory over The Council of Truth is the only option


From TBA website 2 days later



This is the new home of the Black Alliance. When rogue clanners threatened the profits, TBA was formed to defend Omni property and lives.

Our policy is as follows...
When OT is threatened, we will respond in force.
All threats foreign and domestic will be dealt with.
TBA will obey OT law and orders. We also offer our help in any way needed by OT leadership.



So I am now confused we have all heard in the past that TBA would not follow the OT leadership in fact the whole reason TBA was formed to rise to oppose the rule of Mr Ross (This has already been covered I dont want to have to post the logs again to prove this.

Given that TBA has now completely capitulated to the will of Ross one has to question now what is the purpose of TBA. ? If it is formed to defend Omni-Tek then that is the same as omni-tek already had. I think TBA no longer has a purpose, it is not longer any different than Omni-Tek. So I suggest it is time to drop the black alliance as the organistion is counter productive to the unity of Omni-Tek.
