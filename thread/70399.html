I do not think that any system will ever be liked by all and claimed to be "unfair" by all those that disagree with it. Since fariness is a bit of a perspective issue, I don't think holding this to a gold standard is appropriate.

The attempt to incorporate damage teams was an attempt to give those that did not agree with the system an out. (again...www.aoguildleaders.org ) It has since been blamed for the downfall of the clans at tara....an easy target perhaps but not likely the culprit. Those that take a good hard look at what when down will realize that.

My entire intention was to make a system that was less refractive to intervention, ie. more stable. Damage teams to prevent disruptions in 1 and 2, a guild-based system of administration such that people felt that they each had a person on admin that was fair and represented them.

Other suggestions like restricting individuals to 1 or 2 of each item were to prevent people seeing items as the equivalent of credits with better investment potential and so that they people that got the items were the ones that could actually use them. I have realized that expecting people to use common sense is completely over hopeful.

Alas, the only sugesstion that actually got acknowledgement (damage teams) and was agreed upon was not held up in action. All other suggestions, even if they received acknowledgement were NEVER acted upon and no conclusions were made.

Speed, it is thoughtful of you to try to raise discussion on this subject. However, unless a system of Action is put into place where everyone feels they have a voice in what is "fair", all thoughts will dissapear into the ether.
