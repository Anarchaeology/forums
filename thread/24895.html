Funcom, when are you going to admit...
*Cough*

FunCom, I would like to know when you're going to admit that there is a problem with Crat Charms.

The problems are many, but Ill try my best to cover them all.

#1 - The Psy limits on each Charm nano in the line is set too low. Ask any Crat in the game, and they will tell you the same thing. Now, this issue does not have to be fixed in the most obvious fashion. This would be raising the Psy limits on each nano in the Charm line respectively.

A very interesting method for repairing this issue would be to add a new, and COMPITANT, line of Psy debuff nanos.

This means having more than 3 nanos in the line that are just slightly better than Drain Psy. you can buy in stores.

For you see Funcom, that is what makes players angry!

This Psy debuff line would have to have at least 10 nanos in it, with good debuffs rates.

I.e. first nano in line -20, second -25, etc.

Also, dont make it accessible to only high levels.

I.e. pulling one of your trademark- "Oh we'll give you the line, but it starts up when youre around level 90." That's another thing that makes players angry.

This in no way would make Crats uber. It would simply make it so Crats could charm the level of monster they combat. But it would skill have the effect of making it so Crats could not charm godly monsters that are WAY above there level.

This would not mean easy charm of monsters above the Crats level, because the debuff would agro the monster obviously. So charming even a half useful pet would take some technique.

(As a side note  I personally do not think the Scare line of nanos needs a Psy check  There so easily interruptible its not even funny  And the path in which they run away is random, it could save your life or send a horrible baddy on a crash course with your team!  Roots are far more useful, and I dont see a Psy check on those!)

Also, adding a charm timer just might be a good idea. I certainly hope you dont miss the sarcasm there.

And while Im on the pet subject... Why are Crat pets anything but perminat!? We create our pets in the same fashion Engies do. I know engie loose a petty amount of money when they make their bots, but we loose HP's when we make ours.

To be honest Crat droids are not exactly killer Engie bots, and never will be. But making Crat bot perminat would not only makes a butt load of sense,(There both robots for god's sake, not anything like the "living nanoprograms" that MP's cast.) but it would also ease the masses of people who, when they think about how stupid it is to have Crat droid timers, get a massive FC headache.

#2 - Ok... What is up with the huge gaps in some of our nano program lines? Honestly, some of them are just silly. Namely the gap in or DD line that falls between Stinging reminder and Searing bolt. Common FC! There almost 140 points of dead space there! Is this some kind of oversight, or are you just too lazy to fix it?

Given, this issue is not nearly as important as issue #1, but it defiantly is a problem.

Finally #3  Why have you made our XP gain increase nano so pointless? Common, there isnt a team in Rubi-Ka thats going to miss the crappy +3% +6% +9%. No one even notices when its on. This line would work much better as +10% +20% +30%. No ones going to be wanted in groups more than Docs, but this would certainly make Crats more wanted  and would make us the group leaders as we were intended.

 Im not going to go into how stupid demotivational speeches are, but in short  they are suicide agro nanos

If you have read this entire post, thank you  most lazy @sses wouldnt even take the time. If youre a Crat  Plz voice your opinion. I need to hear them.

And because I simply feel for the NTs out there

GIVE THE NTS THE NANO PROGRAMMING BUFFS FOR THE LOVE OF GOD!
