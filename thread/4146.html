Funcoms Approach towards Customers
This is interesting.

Yes there are a lot of problems. And yes sometimes unbalances are fixed, accidentally creating more balance issues.

But sometimes when being the injured party you can easily look past the good.

Im not saying that Funcom is doing the right thing by "seeming" to be ignoring us, and our pleas. Some of you might say that I'm too peace loving or optimistic.

But lets look at this for a moment. The "Good" and the "bad".

BAD FIRST: (Not for any specific reason)

Bugs
Not enough feedback.
Game Balance Issues
Roleplaying Issues.
Note: Actually If you really want to, one could make this list grow and grow... (get my meaning ?)


The Good:

Look at the amount of work Funcom is putting in.
Creating something like this is very very very very complex.
It requires Organization of teams, team leaders.. etc.
Different Depts have to work in sync.
From my point of View Funcom did a great job. Nothing can be perfect.(For the *nix community: Take a look at FreeBSD .. look at how it started, look at what it is today.) It took a long time, slowly but surely they had something rock solid. Bottom line, they are always working on something with the intention to impove things.

The Neutral:

Ok I dont want to slander anyone, but really, how do you expect Funcom to help us, if all we do is scream and diss them all the time. Even online... many occasions I heard Funcom getting cursed. (getting carried away) What I mean to say is, we need to be more organized. A lot of us are adults, or at least mature. The guild systems could really help us. Instead of complaining about the above mentioned issues right away on Forums. Talk to your guild about it. Let the guild Leader represent you. Let that Guild leader talk to other Guild leaders. Then make those guild leaders meet and get all the points finalized in a nice polite and subtle way with suggestions, and ofcourse leaving room for a civilised response from funcom. This way Funcom gets less flames and more intelligent well thought out ideas, complaints, and opinions.

--------------------------------

Off the topic:

Just a note to Diablo Players: Did you not go "am this is not is not right" when you saw a lv 2 with a high level team fighting the ancients in Hell ?

Well does AO have this problem ? Id say More or less no.

---------------------------------

I could go on forever But Im really more interested in the Communities Feedback. I mean does anyone here see what Im trying to say here ? Relationships always need 2 parties to try and make them work. Even a Customer/Company relationship.

Hoping to see some intelligent and mature feedback. Even if you disagree with me. Just be calm about it. Starting flames and such is just not productive. If you have a good point by all means share it.

Anyone who wants to try and develop this idea with others please dont hesitate to /tell Kouto on RK1. Or just reply here.

Sincerely,
