You need to figure out what you want to do first. People are successful in pvp using every setup imaginable. Figure out what random skills you'd like to raise (trade skills, b and e, whatever) then consider your ip obligation to nanos. Then figure out what sort of a weapon/armor you could make the best use of.

Depending on breed and profession, a generally good strategy is to take your least expensive skills and run with them.
