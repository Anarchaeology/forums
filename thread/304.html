What really needs to be done for RP
The only thing keeping me playing Anarchy, after the dull, uninspiring eq-clone combat, is its potential for RP. Funcom has got some great ideas and they have taken Anarchy in the right direction to offer a rich, developed, player-ruled world. The story line is a wonderful idea, the two ever-conflicting sides, players bonding together for a greater cause. This is all grand, but many things have yet to be improved to complete the RP environment I believe Funcom envisioned.

1. Apartments: The one thing that really sets Anarchy apart from other MMORPGs is sadly, it seems, broken. Items disappear frequently, many are difficult if not impossible to place properly, not enough of a selection. Also would be nice if we had items we could interact with, like a couch people could sit on.

2. Trade-Skills: Good start, almost each profession has some type of trade skill they can excel at. However, I haven't really seen anybody out there selling player made weapons or armor. Is it not good enough? At higher levels it should be the best. Also boost the xp players get for using these skills, I've spent almost 8 hours with my 35 NT combining implants only to gain barely 1/2 a lvl (this was with an almost unlimited supply of funds and constant combining).

3. Clothing and Emotes: Done very nicely, keep em coming Funcom! I really like how each character can has his/her own individual style and be fairly unique

4. Bars, etc: A lot of interesting buildings have been scattered throughout the cities, but remain unused entirely. For example, the Happy Rebel, a bar in Tir, contains a main bar, several low lit rooms, and a well used bathroom, the music as you enter the place adds to the environment. People just aren't going to these places. This remains more on the part of the players and guilds who could hold meetings in the areas, but Funcom could do its part by throwing in some important NPC's on occasion. Maybe just to throw out a bone about an upcoming attack or something similar.

Another thing I just thought about for a future improvement. Make tutoring teach xp when used successfully.

For example: DocJane tutors NurseJoe on Treatment. NurseJoe uses his newfound treatment knowledge to successfully put in an implant. DocJane earns xp for being a successful teacher and allowing NurseJoe to do something he wouldn't be able to do under normal circumstances.

This could give xp to both players in examples like tutoring NanoProgramming or other TradeSkills.

I don't think I've ever seen anyone use tutoring yet and it seems like funcom is trying to encourage it by giving us packs that boost 150 to the skill?
