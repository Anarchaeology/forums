You also get the new /org contract bonuses that many are forgetting about, as well as the graphics/music updates (though I know many don't listen to the ingame music), ground assault vehicles, the new ambient visual which add a lot of atmosphere, and the voice commands.

However, if you do not think that PvP and/or towers is worth your time, I don't believe that there is a gun to your head making you buy this booster. It's intended to enhance the PvP aspects of the game, and if you don't PvP, then the booster is probably not for you.

The next "expansion" is actually going to be the first one. This one only added some features, no new zones or drastic changes to gameplay. I assure you Shadowlands will be a much deeper expansion as far as content goes, seeing as how it's almost an entirely different planet.
