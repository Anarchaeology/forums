Rimor Event - Return of the Dojo Dance Party!
Yes friends it is time once again to dust off those boogie shoes and head on out of your cramped appartment to strut your stuff for the second official installment of the Omni-wide Dojo Dance Party! Here's the scoop:

When? - June 15'th - 6pm GMT/2pm EST

Where? - Galway Pool House, Galway County (2450, 1325)

Who? - All Omnis and Neutrals are invited and encouraged to participate in the various contests. To keep things civil, clan members who wish to attend with their omni/neut friends must be cleared through me first.

Why? - Well, as our org crosses each century mark in membership, we get all excited and use the occasion as an excuse to party, so we recently broke 500, and here we are

What? - Besides the usual random dancing/drinking/embarrasing behavior, we will hold at least 2 separate organized contests. The first is a simple best-dressed competition for all 4 breeds - 1'st place prizes for each only, with perhaps some honorable mentions as well.

Second will be the main event of the party - the return of the couple's dancing competition! Rules are simple - find a partner (any breed and sex combo will do!), practice your routine, and then show up and impress us all! The top 3 couples will receive cash prizes. Last year the Inner Circle combo of Deng and Cyberbob blew away the competition with their highly coordinated efforts - will they repeat as champions this year? Only way to find out is to attend!

Those wishing to enter the couple's dancing competition should /tell Chronita in-game the names of the couple who wish to participate, and also make sure to practice (no last-minute routines or entries pls!).

Also, we are proud to announce that the entire party will feature music provided by Gridstream Production's very own DJ Otori! Otori will be able to take your song requests throughout the event via gridbot (/tell gridbot help for more info). For those entering the couple's dancing competition, you may also request a certain song to perform to when you officially enter, and we will try to accomedate you. To find out how to connect to Gridstream radio, please visit http://www.gridstream.com/.

And that's about it I hope everyone attending will be able to kick back, relax, and enjoy themselves at an event that will be totally free of any frantic loot-whoring or bitter PvP action Hope to see you there!
