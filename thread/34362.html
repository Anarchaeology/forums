PvP (Player Vs Player) and RvR (Omni vs Clan)
I think one of the things that make lots of ppl NOT playing AO and going to other games like DAOC is the inexistance of RvR and poor PvP in AO...

You should HAVE a conflict between clan and omni, not every1 playing together and making some Pointless and rare PvP'ing...

Here's my sugestion for improved PvP and RvR:

Make ppl gain something with PvP (like some IP points with each kill)

Create something ppl should guard...like.... make some outposts with whompas to it, and some Big statue in the middle... that statue gives some advantages to the faction owning it, like inits , %xp or dmg. So Omni's and clanners would WANT to control as much outposts as possible. The faction that kills all the guards will make the guards respawn their side, and the bonuses would be aded to their faction....This way Every1 would rush to save the statues every time it was atacked and we could really have good PvP and RvR...

You could also make soem outposts scatered about the entire map that would give the GUILD owning it some bonuses..this would make guild playing interesting..

PvP-
The level restrictions need to be fixed like YESTERDAY! I'll say it again...it makes no sense that level 74's can be attacked by as high as level 95's, yet level 75's can be attacked by as high as level 200.

And what's the point of having titles? One only has to have two accounts with chars past level 6 and they can get a title. Titles are useless.

Rewards for PvP? 1 token only for each kill but only if you manage to kill someone in a faction other than yours and only if they are higher than you in level.

RvR-
Outpost control should have been in there since day 1. Controlling outposts should give ALL members of the controlling faction a skill boost and exclusive access to the shops at the outpost.

Overtaking of cities should also be there. It should be difficult to do but should be possible. Let the players decide who controls the land. Possessing a city should give the same benefits of possessing an outpost, but on a grander scale.

ARK/GM alts-
One disturbing rumor that I've heard is that a good portion of the ARKs have alts in *STORM*. If this is true, it is problematic. ARKs/GMs should NOT be allowed to have ALTS PERIOD, END OF STORY. Allowing this corrupts the internal balance of the game and I don't have enough space here to list the reasons why...
