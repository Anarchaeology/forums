Before this patch a player was able to solo in missions. For ex. a 110 mp would search for a team, and when no teams where available after 10-20min maybe, he would grab a mission and solo for a while until either guild had more players his/her lvl or someone you knew was starting/wanting to start a team. Some people even soloed because they didnt want to team just that day. It feels good to go out on your own sometimes.
Now after this crap-patch, its virtualy impossible to solo. You stand around in nlc, ent, trade and 20k for hours in search of a team. No teams, you grab a 120 mission and dead! WTF Funcom, you guys have destroyed the game with this patch and I am not the only one who thinks so. I know many people who have left because of this non-thought through patch of yours, many of my long term friends I made throughout the year I have been playing AO.

I understand him. He didnt want to spend 500 hours just to get one item that is good for his profession. Funcom, please fix your major F-up and let players be able to solo because its NOT FUN loging on after work when you have 3 hours to play sitting around hunting a team for 1-1.5 hours and sometimes have to leave in the middle of the mission because you cant stay on for more than maybe 3-4 hours per day and even that is alot dedicated to a game that started out as a stress easer and turned into a friggen stress machine!

As someone who's spent a load of time out in the wilds, and who's been out there hunting and exploring, I will say that the risk/reward ratio in 14.6 is actually less than it was before 14.6.

With every animal and plant now apparently hitting twice for every attack they make, the risk factor has increased 100%, but the XP reward has only increased 10%, and they weren't on even keel to begin with. Add to that still poor loot from most mobs (though the 14.6 database indicates some intriguing possibilities), and hunting is, for most people, less of an option than it was before.

In fact, since you now absolutely require a team (in early to mid 100s) to hunt the big game, I don't think that you'll be able to convince most people to leave the NL/BS mission runs for a jaunt to DAV. Though, I'm glad to say you'll see me out there periodically.

It's all about fun and danger out there. And that's why I enjoy it. While I agree with the sentiment (get out there and hunt, people!), it's a rough sell to many players with only one thing in mind.
