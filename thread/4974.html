You'll notice in the description I gave, that the Michael Patriot is right-hand only, and doesn't give a multi-wield requirement.

That means, it is a two-handed weapon, and wielded as such. It isn't a valid gun for dual-wielding.

If you are interested in actually using a SMG in a dual-wield setup, your options are the OT Viper, and the Duke Lugger SMP. You can find the Viper on missions and in OT shops, but before level 80 or so, there's no real reason to other than style. The Duke Lugger shows some promise, except for one little problem. It can only be obtained by the Bootleg Beamers 'n Bolters nano. Because of what happened to poor Duke, Omni-Pol has the entire supply in storage. That means it isn't in the shops, and isn't in any of the missions.

One of these days I'd love to organize a solid raid on Omni-Pol storage facilities. But until that becomes possible, BBnB is the only way.
