Minion snakes: 6 of them, each goes down in 5-10 seconds, each gives 50-60k per kill, spawn is 1 minute. Easy to pull, rarely get adds, but even with adds, it's not too tough to fight 2-3 at a time

Boss: This is interesting. The boss always drops 800,000 to 900,000 xp and spawns every ten minutes or so. Only some bosses are way tougher than others.
