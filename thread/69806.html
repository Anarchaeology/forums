Want a Org house with keys, Take the following Steps:
==============================================
Make a noober alt

Level to level 2

Escape Back yard

Log off noober.

Log on main.

Roll mission in a good location.(team or solo, any Ql, any Location, any type(though i stick to the ones in the city with a buildingesk feel to them))

Get gulid to help clear a mission, or do it solo if you can

clear mission BUT DONT complete it yet.

Dupe Mission key

Place Duped Mission Key in a Bag

Place Bag in Bank

Close bank

Give Noober alt TWO copies of the mission key.

Place one copy of the mission key in a bag.

Place Bag in bank

Move noober into mission zone with the other Key

log noober out INSIDE the missions Start room.

Now complete the mission with main.

Original Mission key will dissappear from your inventory.

Now run to bank and get that bag out and look inside if you luckey the key is still there.

Try and re-enter the mission zone.

If it works the mission zone should stick around indeffinatly.
==============================================

Why does this work.....

Well The AO server can not delete a zone unless all player characters are no longer in it.
So if you stick a noober alt in the mission zone, then the ao servers see this and can not remove the zone.
some one(either ark or gm) has to go on and move your character out of the zone before the system can remove it from the game.

How do i know.... a little ark told me... dam there lose lips....

Alternatively Prior to the uhm i think it was 14.3 patch, when mission keys where not removing them selfs. well the mission zones connected to the keys were not removing them selfs either.

SO, if you happen to keep the key around then you should still be able to re-enter the mission zone. I found 4 such zones with dupeable keys prior to the fix i gave copies to of the keys to every one in my guild when i was the leader of the Omni-Tribunal.

To this Day those mission keys/zones STILL WORK and are STILL ACTIVE. these are like 6month old mission zones.


WARNING: My arkly ex-contact(whom is no longer employeed at my place of buissness so i cant get any more arkly info from him) warns me that doing this may cause invalid items to appear on your character and could only be removed Via Ark/Gm help.

But like i said 6mo no bugs .... yet......
In case your wondering NO chest/mobs do NOT respawn.
