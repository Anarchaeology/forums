Loss of Players, Time for Change!
This is directed at Funcom. All I ask is that you listen to our posts, no matter who posts them. There are some DAMN good ideas floating around. Yes we all understand implementation is not easy, specially with a smaller team, but these ideas are sound. Remember, your name ITSELF has the word FUN in it, I think although you retain that element in your story, you are so wrapped up in that, and in your attempts to make things FAIR and not over or under balanced. You are forgetting to keep things simple and fun. I have friends that are dying trying to take on a green! Why? Because they are not powerplayers, they are average joes that want to occasionally have fun and move on. When you force us into fighting creatures that are always close fights, we start resenting you for it. In Ultima Online I could also go beat up on an Ettin for example. and STILL get benefits. Not much, but it was STILL fun. Anyways, I don't have much more to say except please heed what I have listed below. Remember, we all care and will stay with you for a lONG time, as long as you cater to the average player and not the power player. What kind of thing is it to hear, when a power player, informed me that at 75NT, she can barely take on a yellow, and does just OK with a green. FULLY implanted, and powered up. Please read on. Everyon, support me on this. Let Funcom know how we feel. Its the only way.


· Missions: Improved and expanded to allow more choice (areas to travel to, Items more towards mission getter (for NTs, MPs, etc.. per stats), fixing team missions, prompting of random missions while adventuring across the world, I have more to say but not here). Prices catering to getter of mission are critical, trust me you will have many happy people.
· Team Missions: Make those be much more beneficial than singular, harder but much better, a lot more money. Encourage us to team besides killing things and getting experience, and killing the SAME stuff over and over again.
· Teaming: Allow people to team regardless of level gaps. why? Because we all will go where our friends go. Setup an equation to take account of levels. Example: level of player/highest level of player in team, OR, if level of player is 30 less than highest, then experience is level player/(1.1*highest level of player)
· Combat: Yes, all classes have to be fair and equal to each other at equal levels, in CERTAIN things, however, what is important is MOBs. If you remove the idea of greys, and everything still gives experience, then things would be better. first of all, an average character, has to be able to take on a yellow. close fight, but should be able to win, if he plays things smart. An Average character is one, with ql armor= level of player. NO Implants, implants should be an added benefit. LET people feel powerful, let them take chances with reds! its more fun that way, isnt that what you are aiming for? Your name is FUNCOM afterall.
· Items on Mobs: Have mobs hold more items, even if useless, its ALWAYS fun to find stuff .
· Money and Tradeskills: The missions section above will help in this, but how about new ways of making money? MAKE tradeskills easier. I was attempting to make a ql30 nano sometime back,. and it cost me and im not even done, 55k! I am not that rich. The item wont even sell for 10k. Make tradeskills count, make them easy! There is no need for everything to be hard. Look at Ultima Online, the consultants that worked on that are working with you, arent they? Mining was easy, so was making armor, but it was worth money! and didnt cost US money It was grand fun to be able to armor and equip your friends. Decrease costs, the game need not be as hard as real life. We play it to get away from worries . Make money a bit easier to make , or have missions worked better, so that we can find items of our quality level .

Ease: Ease of play. Make things easier. Your game is forever... if nothing else, make it muhc harder to reach level 200! I dont mind about level gaining being very slow, but make the everyday life more fun and enjoyable. You will keep so many people if you do. They will also be the average players,. and not the powerplayers.
Organizations: Expand this quick, it is of HIGH imprtance. It will keep everyone involved. Commands could use adding: Organization list of members, our own number of ranks (12, 15, etc...), our own NAMED ranks, and many others.
I am an average player, level 45, not in a hurry to level, but when I logon I JUSt want to have fun, and not struggle. I want to be able to make money and not feel like its a task of hufe proportions, I want to adventure, and explore knowing it would be fruitful, I want to be able to go on a 30 minute mission, because I have no more time, and cant spend 15 minutes travel time, I want to be able to know which ones of my guild are on at this time, I want to create items on my side to help my guild mates, and newbies.

...a different point of view
Ok... honestly *not* trying to flame, here. I disagree with everything here, except for the tradeskills & org's bits. Tradeskills should be more a bit more lucrative, I agree. And org's are just broken, for the time being. As for what I disagree with, please let me explain:

Funcom, please *don't* listen to us, the players. I'm dead serious.
Provide us with customer service, yes (not meant to be a gripe... I understand what it's like to be a part of an understaffed customer support team). But... I fear what would become of this game if the Dev team tried to satiate everyone. We'd have watered-down schlock, & no one would be happy. So many half-decent authors have started churning out crap (or worse, Hollywood scripts) because it'd be what the common populace wants (hence the term 'common'... something that is 'common' can never be brilliant, or extraordinary).

I say let the artists behind AO stay true to their vision. So many of these changes that FC has made to their game have been implemented as per player demand. Then people get angry over what they have brought on: 'I've been nerfed! Oh, the tragedy!' players will wail. But weren't some of them chanting the 'balance the professions' mantra, just a bit prior? Hmm...

The rest of the post seems to call for making the game easier. Easier combat & better loot... but make it harder to reach level 200...? Isn't that a bit contradictory? If they make the game easier, then people *will* reach level 200 quickly. Then the players with level 200 characters will quit, because the game no longer offers any sense of reward. Also, those with high-level characters will no longer experience that feeling of uniqueness. So you've reached level 150? Big deal, if you're surrounded by a thousand level 160's. That's what we'd be seeing, if we were to be given an easy game. We'd be bored, unchallenged, & all at high-levels. Who here remembers combat prior to the double-mob-health patch (can't recall which number...)? One could clear out a mission, prepare dinner, & read the paper at the same time...

If combat & missions were as easy at level 100 as they were at level 10, then we'd have an equal number of level 10 & level 100 characters running about Rubi-Ka. There'd be no feeling of accomplishment for attaining levels 100+.

Don't cater to the 'casual gamer' by lowering difficulty. We would quickly grow bored. A tactical gamer will stick with a challenging game longer than a casual gamer will stick with an easy game.

I could care less about profession balance; the only way perfect balance could possibly be attained is by making all professions the same (lame!). As long as they're all useful, & somewhat reasonably close, I'm happy. And they've all been useful, & somewhat reasonably close ever since I bought the game in July.

I want risk and I want reward. I want a challenge, not boredom. I want nothing served up for me on silver platters. But most of all, I want to experience AO as the design team envisioned it.

Just my two creds

-Templar Red

Well said. Whole-heartedly support many of the ideas posted here!

I too find trouble in the level spread. At 50 the spread is from 38-62. I would like to recommend the *creation* of additional level categories in the color/level representation continuum:

White-Gray-Green-Yellow-Orange-Red-Purple-Black

relative to lvl 50 for sake of example, where:

White <under 30> - gives no x.p. and grouping is not allowed with yellow.

Gray <31-37> - gives 10-20% of yellow mob x.p. <more than 1xp in any case>; gray PCs can group with yellows - their xp is scaled for their level though.

Green <38-45> - xp as is; easily taken on solo/charmed; if a purple is in the same team xp is scaled for these players

Yellow <46-54> - xp as is; reasonable to solo/charm

Orange <55-62> - xp as is; difficult to solo/charm, yet still possible with luck and planning

Red <63-69> - xp as is; near impossible to solo/charm - reasonably easy to take in a group - *charm changes to affect red mobs when in teams!!*

Purple <70-75> - xp as level; impossible to solo in any case; difficult and rewarding in groups; charm very, very difficult
