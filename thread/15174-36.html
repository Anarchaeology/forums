Lifedrain -

The problem is that the extreme range *legitimately* allowed for makes it hard for FC to balance the game for people who don't wish to twink. I don't think I (a solo-ish casual player) should level as fast as someone who's treadmilling for all their worth, and has worked out the absolute best implants possible.

But I do think I deserve a fun game, meaning one where I can usually fight against a yellow and win, almost always beat a green, and if I'm lucky take out the occasional orange. Reds should be out of my reach unless I get so lucky it hurts, or play it real, real careful.

The problem is, if they balance the game for me that way, as OEing stands right now, then OEers will have no challenge at all.

Under the new ruleset you can still max your implants - no one will stop you! You just won't be able to use buffs to bootstrap yourself into higher items. That's it. In fact, this patch *enhances* your implant ability, because it becomes even more important to getting the best gun.

For example, let's say you and I are both level 55 Fixers. We both have the same base SMG and Burst (call it 300 SMG and 250 Burst, those are just random numbers.)

Now, you've worked over your implants carefully, maxing everything. I've got hand-me-down implants haphazardly assembled from random monster drops. These implants bring me to 375 SMG and 300 Burst, and you to 415 SMG and 330 Burst.

Now, if we can't get hugeass wrangles, then you've gained about 50% more from your implants than I have.

Now, let's count +132 wrangles in, which are relatively easy for both of us to obtain as compared to a perfect implant set, right?

Now I've got 507 SMG and you've got 547 SMG. Now, you've only gained about 20% on me.

You see why this patch actually makes implant design a MORE important skill?

Yes, you'll have to use lower QL gear. But implant design becomes more, not less important, to squeeze yourself into the maximum possible gear.
