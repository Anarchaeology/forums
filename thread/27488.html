Yes i agree 100%.

Currently the system brings out the beast in all of us and that is not fun. The only way to stay above this dirty selfish existance is to give up your rights to some of the best loot.

1: The system encourages people to take as often as they can as no one knows what they have taken. If a player finds a reflect bracer in a chest they are going to be sorely tempted to just take it and say nothing.
2: Since the system is 'He who loots first gets' and there is no '/team chest alpha' we end up in looting sprees - competing withother players. This leads to:
A): Calmers who run in a room, calm the mob and loot the chest while your team is fighting somewhere
B): Tanks who loot whatever chest they can provided the room does not have an agro mob in it.
C): The players who follow just behind the tank, letting the tank take the agro and then opening a chest in the middle of combat and looting it before joining in in the fight.
D): An interesting one i saw the other day where a player will hit Q to cancel his attacks shortly before the death of a mob to give him a seconds advantage in looting a chest.
E): Players who don't even fight but simply run along behind your team looting everyhting they find. They don't have time to fight.
3: Since everyone is on a loot fest it makes good people paranoid as well. Did that Mantis breeder drop an egg? Who knows. Maybe the team leader is lying. I personally hate to think this way as well, but there is always a possibility that you are being ripped off if you dont have foreknowledge of a players honesty. It's contagious too. Once you voice your su****ions others will begin to believe.. Bleh. I hate this it makes me feel dirty.
4: As an end result we all end up slaves to greed or else we opt out and never get our fair share. The only way to remain above su****ion is to NEVER loot. I was in a team the other day that didn't tough a single chest as no one wanted the 'loot-hog' stigma.

Honestly this is not good. I prefer to enjoy the game. Lets have a message that says who what is in a chest or on a boss mob when it falls. Better yet - let's just put in a message when rare loot drops. I dont care what peices of armor or health kits are in a chest. I do however care that a reflect bracer was in a chest and player X took it without raffling the loot to the team.

Also, lets put in a message saying who takes it:

Player XXX takes a Bracer of Reflection: projectiles form the chest.

If you can tell us a roller-rat wakes up the least you can do is tell us when we've been ripped off.
