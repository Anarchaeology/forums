Back when people would pay millions for a pair of katara sunglasses.

70% of the playerbase seemed to dual wield FA 3927s. Most of the rest seemed like beam or hammer enforcers or MAs.

NTs could nuke things from so far away they couldn't be aggroed.

Mausser Particle Streamers had more range than lots of sniper rifles. And MPSes were the only viable SMG type weapon, period.

Martial Artists wielded sledgehammers alongside their punching in order to do worthwhile damage.

People hunted in teams outdoors alot and it was much better exp than missions.

Stacking snares on yourself would increase your runspeed almost infinitely.

There was no option to set 'character view distance' so you took full graphics loading lag every time you whompa'd into Athen, Newland, and Tir.

You were lucky to play for a full hour before having to reboot to clear the evil memory leaks.

You got tokens almost every time for mission blitzing.

Waitt cyber armor was bar-none the best protection in the game. Bau was second best.

Some fond memories
