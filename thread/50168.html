No centurion, i will tell you where that came from. The '25 or 30' came from me because i was in a big battle for a long time and as time went on there was more and more ARKs spawning. I seriously have no idea how i survived anyway, i was so lagged but i could still see what was going on. Sort of like a slow death since i ended up going LD shortly before it ended.

I was there the whole day. I know. I was in the 25% bunker near the hill & Ron McBane with Vergil and Kilvus when they decided to spare me because of the BS going on outside. I had first noticed it when Vergil made me zone out of the bunker after nuking me, i looked over the fence and i saw over 15 ARKs in battle suits and omni pol armor, recruit-issue (white?) and omni-elite. Of course, the guy in omni elite and the name that starts with a 'T' coldstrike is referring to is Tennek. These guys played meatshield and escorted everyone from 10-3 in. Clanners on the hill were scared out of their socks and trying to hit them was futile since they had insanely high HP, 100% nano resist(I couldnt get off roots, snares, debuffs, traders couldnt divest.), no-weakness armor (chem didnt do anything to omni elite) even for the greys, yellows, and oranges i saw. We got pushed back to the bunkers in 2ho, and the guys in the battle suits remained as well as players. Most folks got gridded out, i gridded out as well when there was 50 omnis ARK and Player surrounding us. Mostly everyone left. Then we came back an hour later when the ARKs started to die down. And they came again soon after, but only for a shorter period of time. This time it was atroxes in Recruit-Issue armor, and the infamous Tennek. Many of the characters had similar names and as soon as we took down one, 3 more came. We took out another, more and more just kept flowing out of the bunker. After we waited a little while, we went back and retook 2ho. I saw an incident happen with an omni player and an ARK in a bunker but i won't get into that in this thread. We had 2ho for about 30 minutes, and on the other side of the 2ho whompa, in 10-3, i saw an amazingly big group of omnis buffing up getting ready for what i knew would be the 'tet offensive' of the evening. All i saw there was players. We numbered about 25 clanners on the hill, camping the whompa, waiting for omnis to come in. Then, Tennek came in. And after that, the atroxes, over and over again until there was so many, omni players came in. I guess being that we got wiped out the first time and not the second, we knew what to do and aim for the players. We held up a bit more of a fight than before, but everyone ended up dying or zoning to the bunker. I won't complain about this but all those ARKs caused so much lag even if their damage output was low. People with cable and dsl i talked to said they had terrible lag as well, finally i crashed, and when i relogged in corpses all around the whompa, hill, bunkers, and omnis were all sitting around the bunkers telling each other 'Don't attack karmacappa!! Stop shooting!' I found out this 'karmacappa' was a GM. level 300. I had no idea what he was doing there, i still don't. Not even the omnis knew why he was there, that still remains a mystery to everyone there last night. After that night 2ho was pretty much over. A lot of people were pissed off at what happened the whole day, but there was still a few small skirmishes later after midnight between solely players. The way it should be.


That is my full account of 2ho last night, and i can tell you that anyone who was in 2ho as long as i was yesterday will agree with me that it was not just '3' ARK omnis, but rather invasions in rows of 3 spontaneously. Some had short intervals, some had long intervals, the whole way it worked was pretty strange to me. I'm sure different stories could be heard by people who didnt spend the whole day there, died early on, or went linkdead. If it really was '3 ARKs' the whole day it would not have been at all a problem for clanners to ignore or even kill these characters.

For the record, i have 3 guildmates that saw a behemoth and some omni units in 2ho last week, one behemoth was charmed and it was deleted and respawned with aggro. I eventually checked it out, some of my friends were there too. I was a bit late, i only got to see the last behemoth before it mysteriously 'despawned.' It had a green name, too. And was hitting for 900, according to a doc guildy of mine. The ARK units were still a pain but not really much of one compared to last night. Was fun the first time, not the second when the so-called 'event' was really just an unbalanced slaughter.
