Balancing PvM (mob nukes/DoTs)
AO mobs generally have high health and low damage output compared to PCs. I consider this fairly well balanced at the moment considering their low damage affect our low health and our high damage affect their high health. Fair enough.

At my level, a yellow mob generally hits me for about 100 points of damage. On average, I hit them for about 450 points with my Caterwaul.

Agent mobs have a nasty tendency to run the Mysterious Causes nano which hits for 51 points of damage every tick, or about 50% of their weapon damage. If I were to run a comparable nano, it would have to do 225 points of damage (0.5 x 450) every tick. Not even the best agent DoT, Delayed Assassin (QL 169), has that kind of damage output. A doc mob stacking a couple of DoTs on me is outright lethal, I can barely keep my health up with treatment labs.

The other day I was nuked for 600 points of damage and promptly killed by a yellow nuking mob. A comparable nuke for me (if I had any) would do at least 2700 (6 x 450) points of damage. I'm no expert, but I think that's a QL 160-ish nuke.

IMO, mob nukes and DoTs are far too powerful considering our low health.

I don't know how best to fix this, or if it's even considered a problem, but I think something should be done about this.

Here's one suggestion:
1) Double our (PCs) hitpoints
2) Double everything affecting our hitpoints (treatment labs, implants etc)
3) Make PvP damage 100%
4) Double mob weapon damage
5) Possibly other tweaks necessary

It would of course be possible to reduce mob nukes/DoTs too, but I kinda dislike the 50% damage in PvP and doubling our health would kill two birds with one stone.
