NTs in teams
It seems to me NTs have a problem in teams right now.

First of all if you look at total damage output over time, NTs
are only average among professions (of course this varies
at different levels.) This is a inconsistent with the design of
the NT profession as a pure damage-dealer, and is especially
annoying considering low NT hit points, but I mention it
mainly as an aside, since it seems to be a problem for the
profession in general as opposed to a team-specific issue.

As regards teams, though, the NT problem is that any given
NT DD is likely to be on a per-hit basis very high damage
compared to tank or other support profession damage. It
is very easy to be aggroed as a NT. There are of course ways
to avoid it, but those tend to reduce the NT damage output
over time still further. An NT trying to play it reasonably safe
by for example keeping his aggression slider at 50% and only
entering combat after the target has taken some damage might
as well not be on the team, since his damage contribution
will be so low. To be even slightly worthwhile to a team, an NT
really has to be in there zapping away constantly at max
aggression to reduce casting time. But this causes much aggro,
and with low hit points (and as a nano mage poor armor) this
also leads to a disproportionate amount of NT deaths.

Of course, a skilled team can ameliorate or even eliminate these
problems. But as Gaute observed, first of all most teams are
not skilled. Secondly, the need to be so careful and skillful
is eliminated if the team just doesn't add a member of a flawed
profession. If I was a non-NT team leader with one slot open
I don't know why I'd ever choose a NT when a soldier, enforcer,
martial artist, metaphysicist, doctor, or engineer was available
no matter if those professions were already on the team or not.
I would also consider that adventurers, traders, and agents
would be at least as desirable if not moreso, depending on
what professions were in the party mix.

I don't mean to suggest by the way that the NT is in the worst
shape of all professions as regards teaming. Surely that honor
has to go to the mid-level fixer these days; at least NT DDs
often have nice visuals.... As regards bureaucrats, well that
xp boost thing and the death loss reduction thing should compensate to some extent for their doing less damage....
No? Oh well. Sorry crats. There are actually so few bureaucrats
in the game I don't remember being on a team with one
since their recent changes.

Anyhow, all that being said, I have to say that whoever came
up with the idea that MPs would enhance NT performance had
an interesting thought. The synergy effect of an MP boosting
an NT's ability to cast was a good idea. Of course the idea
doesn't actually work in the current game implementation,
since MPs by themselves are now significantly superior to
NTs in almost every facet of the game including pure combat,
but back when MPs were lame and NTs were better, that
idea must have enabled many MPs to find team slots in groups
with NTs. The extension of this basic synergy concept to
bureaucrats and engineers has certainly improved (or will
improve) their positions as regards teams. It's just a pity
that the only useful buff NTs generally have to contribute is
humidity, which pretty much any team could easily live without
if they could add another tank, pet class, or damage dealer which
won't die if it gets attacked.


-Strycker NT 80
