great discussion
i didn't see anyone mention the fixer grid quest, and i think it's very pertinent to the point originally being made.

i solo. a lot. hence, i don't get very good loot, unless i solo team missions, and then, i don't get loot that's great for my level, since i can't solo bosses much higher than my current level. since i lean towards overequipping, much of this stuff is only good for selling or twinking my alts.

enter the fixer grid quest. a static mission, in the sense that you always go to the same locations to achieve your objectives. different, however, in that your objective changes half a dozen times before you actually get the final item. also different in that it was both challenging and FUN (well, challenging at level 60ish without a yalm.)

why do we not have more missions like this? clanners have their ring quests, and even though you still feel like you're on the treadmill, the result is an item that you feel a sense of accomplishment and acceptance after having obtained it.

oh. if funcom does decide to add more quests like this, they need to be realistic for solo players and at an array of level ranges. (i threw that in there so someone can't say "what about the ring quests?") i don't pay any less for my account than the folks who belong to massive guilds, so i'm not sure why content for soloers is so much less than content for people who like to team.
