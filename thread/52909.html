Saving Options/Window Positions/etc.
I would like to suggest that you come up with a way that options are saved per character.

For instance, my MP - I keep others hits on so he can see his pet damage. But when I play an NT I don't want to see others hits. This is just one option but there are tons of small option changes that I want to have with certain characters and don't want to have with others and yet you cannot save those per character.

Also window positioning - if you move your window or chat bar to one spot then it shows up for other characters also.

My suggestion is to allow all client options (everything) to be saved locally with the character.
