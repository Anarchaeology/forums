I also think some of the players disagree with you. I'm one of them. I'm opposed to level reqs and all for profession specific items.

Level reqs are exactly the thing that makes people feel they can't play the game they want it to, ie focusing on a particular line of skills to get something early on, while sacrificing another line.

Profession specific items help balance the profession and should make it easier to choose a profession, as you don't have to worry about any profession being significantly weaker than others (ideally, of course ).

And to answer your topic: no, it hasn't yet. ETA: September 5.
