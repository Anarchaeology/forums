Children of Rubi-Ka



Part 1



Johan scrolled through his teams roster on the data pad. Five names, five faces thats hes never heard of or seen before in his life. Johan glanced up from the pad to meet the gaze of the tiny opifex sitting behind her red oak desk. She looked back at him with eyes that seem to glow with a soft blue life of their own.  I dont get to choose my own team? asked Johan, trying not to be distracted by her eyes.
As much as we respect your judgment mister Washington, we thought that it would be best for the mission if we made the choices for you. Youll find that the team we have given you will be more then adequate.
Johan looked down at the data pad again Melvin Rechling, age 28: Atrox. Has spent the last five years in omni-reform for the killing of four guards and three civilians in a drunken brawl. Johan looked up to the opifex who answered with a shrug, he continued on. Andrea Linx, age 19: opifex. Has over twenty arrest warrants pending for data theft and other non-disclosed crimes. In and out of rehabilitation since she was twelve.
Johan tossed the data pad onto the desk. You call this a adequate team? A bunch of ex-cons!
Both have gone through rehabilitation and are, in the companies opinion, ready to live a peacefully and lawful life with the rest of society. Besides, they both possess skills that Omni-Tek is going to need in the future as well as skills that you may need on this mission.
Has the war gotten so bad that we need to rely on common thugs? It would seem weve become no better then the ones we fight.
That is not something for you to worry about. Answered back the opifex with narrowing brows. The room seemed to grow a bit colder to Johan.
Perhaps not. Careful not to look directly into her eyes Johan reached for the pad he tossed away. Johan read the rest of the roster to himself, walking away to the far side of the room. Anything to escape the maddened opifexs glare. Thomas Scott, he had built himself a solid reputation for his excellent marksmanship and his scouting skills. The other was a field surgeon fresh out of Omni-Medical. Alexander Toskof, this would be his first assignment for the company.
The opifex had taken her attention off of Johan, much to his relief, and had taken the liberty to bring up holo-monitor in the middle of the room. Johan watched as the world of Rubi-Ka began to form in mid air. This will be your target area. The holo representation of Rubi-Ka shifted suddenly and zoomed in on an area about forty kilometers outside of the western barrier wall. Johan stepped closer to look at what appeared to be a very large dust cloud. This Commander Washington, is what you are being sent out to investigate.
What is it?
That we dont know. every probe we sent out has lost contact before we can get within a kilometer of the thing. The holo changed to a direct feed video of a probe approaching the cloud at high speeds. The closer it got the more the video feed was distorted. Pause feed. this is as close as we have been able to get to it using a direct wire feed. After this point all electronics fail.
Someone or something doesnt want you looking, eh? Johan looked closer at the feed, the cloud moved slowly in on itself. What is it made out of, it doesnt appear to be just dust.
Good eye... but again thats something we dont know; but scanners have been able to determine that there are high levels of notum and nano-bots.
Nano-bots? I didnt think they could survive very long in the outlands.
They werent designed too. Thats why we are sending you out there.
Johan Grinned Your technology failed and now its time to send in the old man, eh?
The opifex rolled her eyes and hit a few keys on her desktop. The image in the holo changed once again, a sickly looking nano-mage appeared before Johan. This is Christian Birch, a highly skilled Nano-Technician. It is your highest priority that you protect this man, understand?
You want me to baby sit this man?
Mister Birch is not exactly the spitting image of a healthy man. He is able to command some of the most powerful nano programs out there, unfortunately his body cannot take too much physical stress.
Not exactly someone you want to send out to the outlands. moaned Johan, taking a seat in one of the offices chairs. So Im to take him and this band of thugs and fresh cadets out to the outlands so we can see a cloud?
The opifex smiled. Yes. Thats about the extent of it. Here, youll also need these. She reached into a side drawer of her desk and pulled out an envelope. She tossed it to Johan.
Johan opened it, inside where six passports; all with ICC verification. Were pretending to be ICC?
Youll bee traveling near clan territory. The last thing we need is a confrontation. If Omni-Tek was to send a team out there, which we are, the clans would get su****ious and want to stop us at all cost. However, masquerading as ICC officials the clans will be in no place to question you and thus your mission will be able to go on without interference.
Your team is to not anything on them or in their possession that could link them to Omni-Tek. The last thing we need right now is a military action brought upon this group.
Johan stood and saluted the opifex. Understood, maam.
Good luck commander, I dont think I have to tell you the importance of us finding out what this thing really is. Find out why those nano-bots are out there and report back immediately.
