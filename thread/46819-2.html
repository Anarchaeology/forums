No one mentioned Moonwalking? Surely a classic dance move.

/(if they put disco in there with the mashed potato and flamenco I could live with macarana and breakdancing)

And all the wonderful things others have suggested. Someone, Missmaul I think recommended -freeze

I think instead what they could do is allow any emote to be frozen by adding a switch to it like /strong1 -f or /read -f or /pray -f or my favorite /kneel -f

Although interactive emotes have been suggested and I want them I think it would be easier if they just added more singles while they worked on something interactive. Anyone play the Sims? Interactive emotes of slapping, kissing, dancing, giving presents holding babies etc. Can't be that hard to do. You could make it a flag in option to turn it on or not.

Since the EULA states you must be an adult to own an account and if you're a minor you must have a parent agree to it then they should cater to a more mature group with emotes. That means some "politically incorrect" emotes like:

/smoke
/pat behind

Heck they have enough obscene emotes in there already like /moon /drink /finger so why not?

I like missions every now and then but I stay for the Roleplay. I would like to see the next Booster Pack be a Living on Rubi-Ka booster pack.

Where they can finally tackle ideas we've all expounded upon and like to see:

Add some social skills to the game that don't take ips but relate to use for improvement (more you do it better you get at it) so it will not effect current skill set:

Cosmetologist: Change people's hair, facial hair, tattoos, markings, hair color, eye color and skin color. Have a selection of do's. Good for agents, parties, laying-low and a fresh look.

Mechanic: Customize vehicles, add detailing, colors, tweak speed and handling, add hardpoints for weapons or be able to create motorcycles and cars or more customized Engineer bot types.

Interior Designer: Make furniture, lights, paintings, all kinds of furnishings, specialized bureaus that act like banks, themed designs, install security devices etc. Includes making items for use in roleplay as props, may have limited cycle in which they disappear after 24 or 48 hours, have the ability to rename a common furnishing or object for a limited time to help rp.

Tailor: Take the close you have and change their color or materials, design outfits, hats and hoods, make bronto and hide armor and perhaps things like Leet fur coats, headgear with keet feathers, boots out of viper skin, etc.

Vehicle Expansion: Different types of vehicle plans available at your Mechanic, more background vehicles appearing like the npcs moving about cities, larger vehicles for group movement

Housing Customization: allows for upgrades based on title or money in the interior look of the apartments. Allows for different models of layouts to choose from. Allows for a full range of furniture to be used (say a lesser version of the SIMS again) Many of which you would have to get a Designer to make.

Allow for greater housing possibilities maybe tied to static quests, or something, maybe dependent on profession. Crats can get lofts up to mansions, engineers get small shop/apartments up to a tool shop with bedroom, Docs can get apartments up to labs or clinics, etc. Regardless you need to tend it upkeep it (like a guild tax) and have the ability to share it.

Further detailing of social areas:

Add NPC's with more scripts, create "social static missions" missions that involve social interaction with other npcs or pcs like having to find missing records for OmniMed, or finding and interrogating a npc criminal in the Prison.

Fill out the other bars, restaurants, shops, subways, prisons, etc with appropriate personnel and environment such that they will react to you outside a mission. What is the purpose of this? It allows more areas for Roleplay, speaking of which add the limited ability to customize items and npcs or give that ability to ARKS so we can tailor areas for an adventure.

Which leads to great customizability for roleplaying purposes: For instance if I want an OmniPal employee, allow me to rename for 24 hours or 6 hours or so that Omni Fresh Bureaucrat to: Susan Underhill, OmniPal secretary or something to interact with. Perhaps allow limited programming of scripts like (will respond to "form" with "I'm sorry Sir you must have the Green Form x-2349G to check out records") Doesn't have to be in the hands of the players could just be in the hands of the ARKS but then let them use it for us to create our own events. Greater tools to create your own adventures.

Places to see in Rubi-Ka that aren't there for mindless slaughter and loot camping. Like a massive leet prairie dog village or a bronto graveyard or such things.

The massive amounts of emotes people here suggested

A wider range of foods and how you eat them, social clothes, social items like cigarettes, pipes, makeup, cards, dice, personal computers, books you can hold etc.

Ah well I have more like an interactive news service or computing system so you can exchange messages, leave mail, read local news, post want adds or missions, etc.

There are some great ideas out there, lets try for a social booster.
