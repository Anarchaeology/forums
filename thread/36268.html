Weapons...
I think the topic of weapons is one of the low points of Anarchy online. The fact of the matter is that most of them are far too weak and you'll not be successful using them, then some are hard to get, then the other weak ones are weird combinations like.. flamethrower's with aimed shot, or rifle pistols, what would use rifle and pistol anyway? Aren't those like the opposites? hur hur.

Anyways, I sincerely think that is one of the biggest issues that needs to be looked at. Like for instance, the soldier profesion, a decent profesion (I play one, and I like it) but, assault rifles are seemingly one of the rarest types of guys there are. The ones that have full auto aren't very strong (I do see why that is, but I mean I only hit for 6 bullets from a 50 round clip already) The most popular one ends up being the novaflow, but the fixer's have the chemical streamer which does chemical damage and fixers have a better damage buff (and mausser partical streamers are much easier to find in stores, although then the thing has to be built) and the adventurer's have the bbi faithful, even stronger, albeit projectile damage, and then they have two of them. (Those are realatively hard to come by) so the soldier (the supposed big guns heavy hitter) is outdamaged by other hybrid classes. I stress the point of there not being an issue with the other classes and they need no nerfs. And so perhaps more soldier specific weapons... But that still sucks for lower level people, because it takes forever to roll them up in missions, and you can only do missions so hard, and your friends and guild mates are too high level to get things for you, and since they're not found in stores you're out of luck.

What I think needs to be done is alot of weapons re-done, made stronger, more of them, less poor combinations like rifle+shotgun and so forth. And make them available in stores, in a more consistant way, like the nano clusters, so it's not so much chance and there aren't times where people are forced to go without. Because from where I see it the advantage to training in the use of powerful guns and weapons is to be the strongest, but then the people who train in unarmed combat do better, don't need to aquire hard to get weapons, and on the role-playing aspect don't need to worry about being disarmed or being caught without a weapon. So what is the point of training in the weapons? For me, it's just hoping they improve them for a little balance. What does everyone else think? If people agree back me up, please, for all of our sakes.

-Shion

I cut and paste from a thread "Fixer Q and A with the designers" in the fixer board. Although they are referring explicitly to SMG, I think the answer may apply to the general.

quote:
> Will adjustments be made to already existing weapon to make them more effective?
> · Weapons like the Kang Tao and Viper are hardly used.

Jim Salabim
Not all weapons are that useful, some will always be the "worst", and others will only have special uses. Some of these will become upgradeable, some will not.

> · Why are pure SMG/Burst weapons better than the hybrids? Shouldn't the higher IP costs of the hybrids to equip suggest that it would be better?

Jim Salabim
In general this should be so, yes. Most of the weapons you see in the shops today were made during beta. Code and balance have changed since then, and players use weapons and play the game in other ways than we could foresee. We do want to balance those old weapons out eventually.

> · Shotguns have much better stats compared to a SMG. A large portion of the Fixer community as well as everybody else is planning to switch to shotguns when the IPR comes. Will Funcom consider this a problem and rebalance them?


Jim Salabim
Shotguns are far more effective than we envisioned. As a result we are now using those more as a standard to compare other weapons with. (We do not currently wish to nerf Shotguns though). The damage ratio of other types of weapons has increased, and you should gradually see the effectiveness of them raise even more in the future.
