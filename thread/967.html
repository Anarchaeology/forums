Arul Saba does not yet walk the lands of Rubi-Ka, thus you can't get the blueprints required to complete the bracelets.
However, you can assemble them to a certain level.

*goes digging in forum archives*

Ah, there we go:

quote:
By Arul Saba

This book will give you details on how to make one of my famous bracelets.

The first thing that you need is a blueprint for the bracelet, which I will be happy to supply you with in return for certain help. You will then need to adjust this bracelet so that it can be worn on the left or right wrist. This is a one-time operation and cannot be undone. I will gladly sell you the tools to perform this adjustment.

Depending upon the type of balanced blueprint you started with, you can now attach a number of pieces of bracelet circuitry. Each section of bracelet circuitry will allow the completed bracelet to hold one gem.

To make a piece of bracelet circuitry, you need to weave some Silver Nano Circuitry Filigree Wire onto an Interfaced Nano Sensor. You make Silver Nano Circuitry Filigree Wire by combining Silver Filigree Wire with Nano Circuitry Wire. You make an Interfaced Nano Sensor by combining a Nano Sensor with Nano Circuitry Wire.

You can complete the bracelet without attaching all the possible pieces of bracelet circuitry, but doing so will limit the number of gems that can be attached to the bracelet. Once you have attached the desired amount of circuitry, cover the bracelet with Liquid Silver to complete the bracelet.

You can now attach the first, and most important gem. The first gem that you can attach determines what other gems can be attached  only gems of the same type can be attached. This gem is called the Arbiter Gem. Once the Arbiter Gem has been attached, you can then attach the other gems, but must attach them in the correct order. The bracelet will tell you what gem you need to attach next.

Burning Plasma
Contained Energy
Corrosion
Impaling

Longevity

Searing Flame
Silent Death
Smashing Blow

A Bracelet can have 1-5 different gems/stones each increasing it's 'output' of that sort.
A bracelet can only have one line of gems/stones mentioned above.

Arbiter Gem (QL80)
Monarc Gem (QL110)
Emperor Gem (QL150)
Stellar Jewel (QL180)
Galactic Jewel (QL200)

So basically the lowest QL of a Arul Saba Bracelet would be QL80.
