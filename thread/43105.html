1a)Mechdog - Remote Taunting K-9.

Description: (the general idea.)

A mechdog *created* by agents. It does no damage other than
1-1. Why? Because it is a taunting device, for solo agents, and a
means of handling adds in teams. The agent uses perhaps a
nano program to create the mechdog -" husk", and then uses
mechanical engineering (or a relative skill) to insteal the mechdog
robotic brain. Much like respressors, the maintainance skill for
this is psychology, to keep it "trained". If your psychology ceases
to meet the requirements, somehow, your doggie does not obey.

Basically, and agent's best friend, who takes aggro, when told to
attack adds. This is an AMAZING idea for agent's who cannot solo.

That being ALL agent, Funcom... ALL! One add and we're toast.

It looks like: (I know the first part deserves to be in #2... but..)

Mechdog Husk- Remote Taunting K-9.

clan agents using omni-tek property, such as this, are commiting

2e)Absorbtion shields. Agents have no healing.. low evades, low
HP, and no means of protecting themselves. What we need is:

Nano: Unwarranted Remedy:

A nano that put up a shield around the agent for 30-1:00:00
minutes, depending on QL, and heals the agent for a set % of the
damage dealt to it, as a sort of DoT. No shield or reflect..

Here's a situational expample:

I cast Unwarranted Remedy:

This is a 3/7 ATK/REC nano. It lasts 30 minutes, and absorbs a
fair amount of *20%* damage dealt to me.

I go into a mission. Luckily (heh..) only one mob is in the 1st room.
This mob hits for 200 damage. I go in for the kill...

You hit Hardened ass kicker for 1280 AimedShot damage.
Hardened ass kicker hits you for 200 proj. damage.
You were healed for 20 points of health.
You hit Hardened ass kicker for 6 points of proj. damage.
Hardened ass kicker hits you for 450 proj. damage. Critical hit!
You were healed for 90 points of health.
Hardened ass kicker hits you for 1200 burst damage!
You were healed for 240 points of health.

As you can see, this is not unbalncing. It basically acts as a %-ed
HoT, that is reactive. 20% of the damage, per hit recieved is not
a lot, when you get hit for much higher amounts. But it makes
things much easier on agents. Fixers have more HP, evades, and
have HoT's. We have less of all 3, but with this nano, we could
actually have a form of healing. We still TAKE damage, fully, we
just recover a set amount, per hit. I think it's ingenious, really..

But, then.. that's me, and people never take me seriously...

3a) AP needs fixed. As a sub 150 agent, who can cast it, I don't
honestly see the point. -80 skill and -1500 init is painful. It does
not offer anything for teaming, OR soloing aside from buffing. Is
that FC's vision for my class? I'd say not. I hope not, at least...

FP: -2000 init. -25 skill. Perfect!
AP: -1250 init. -60 skill. Improved!
MP: -1000 init. -100 skill. Improved!

Fact is, at the level you can cast MP, init is no problem, it's skill.
It's the opposite for AP. Skill is bad, but not too, but you don't
have nearly enough init at that level to accomplish anything.

3b) Friendly nanos/shields/assist nanos breaking sneak. I hate
the fact that my team HURTS if using a helpful nano breaks
sneak, and I cannot AS. Actually.. not only does my not ASing hurt
but if they decide to not use it and let me AS, the fact is, the
benefit of the buff isn't given to the team, and they still suffer.

This is unfair.

All nanos should not break sneak, unless cast by the concealed
person. If the person is in sneak, then they are not under attack,
now are they? Then they do not need that heal, or nano? Okay!

In that case, if concealed, no friendly nanos can effect you, period.

Simple as that, I'd hope to think. It's certainly a logical idea...

3c)Concentration nanos are fine as is... shot lines (True and Sure.)
are not. True/Sureshot need to be made longer and a bit more
frequent. At the pace we kill in team missions, it's cast once every
good 5 fights or so. This is unacceptable. Increase the duration
to a minimum of 25 seconds, with a cool down period of 1 minute.

Casting over and over is bad enough.
