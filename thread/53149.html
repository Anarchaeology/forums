CCI Rubi-Ka Book of Haiku
Hello poets of Rubi-Ka!

Conquistador Coffee Incorporated is extending this unique oportunity to all you sensitive artist types. In a special section of our CCI page, we will have a book of Rubi-Ka Haiku for our fine citizens to paruse while relaxing with a fresh cup of our flavorful coffee. If you'd like to submit a Haiku for the book please post it here and we will consider including it.

(NOTE: posting a Haiku does not guarantee it will be included.)

Since we realize that poverty and strife can often be a great inspiration for artistic ability, we will be extending this oportunity to Clan members as well. Please realize, however, using this as a forum to insult or criticise our great employer Omni-Tek will only guarantee you're poems deletion. (and possibly violent retribution)

Good Luck!
-Lano-

Kuiper, co-founder of the South Rubi-Ka Cella Dwella Atrox Poet Society reads the CCI's call for poems and gets all excited. This could be a glorious opportunity for the whole world to get to hear atrox poetry. The term "haiku" does not really seem to register. He resolves to go to the nice lady at the public library for help.

"Scuse me, what is a haiku?" he asks her.

The nice lady at the public library replies, "A haiku is a very, very old, pre-Omega form of poetry. A haiku poem conists of a three unrhymed lines of 5,7,5 syllables respectively."

"Right! Gottcha! Thank you!" says Kuiper and runs straight home, to a little hut in a shanty town in Omni-Forest.

The next morning he returns to the nice lady at the public library. "Scuse me, what's a syllable?"
