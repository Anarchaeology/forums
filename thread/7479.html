If your weapon does 20-300 it doesn't mean you will hit in the range of 20-300. that number gets modified by your attack rating. So lets say you got about 800 Attack rating. Your damage without any rings or +dmg spells will be (20-300)*3, yours hits will land somewhere from 60-900. Now, if you wear +dmg rings (+4) and have +dmg spell (+10) on you that damage is modified further on, so your hits will land somewhere in 74-914 range.

There's something wrong with some of the reflect shields, maybe all of them. They are in the in progress patch notes for the next patch as something that will be fixed.

As the other person said the monster you were fighting must have had a deflection shield going. A weapon with 20 minimum damage will never hit for less than 20 and probably will hit for a minimum of 40 or more when adjusted for your attack skill if you are over level 75 or so.

% Add Dmg spells are bugged and stop adding damage after you zone (so you need to recast them every time you zone if you expect to get benefits), so that may be one part of this issue you are having with lower than expected damage.
