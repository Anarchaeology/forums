Agro without Agro'ing - How is it decided...
Ok, so me and a good friend decide to try a team mission, the first 2 we chose were too easy, all green/yellow and we ended up basically walking through until the boss which was just an extended fight and was working fine. The 3rd mission we chose, I ramped up the diff a bit and we had all orange/red's. A little more downtime, usually about 40 seconds or so after a fight we were on to the next room. Well, we finally clear it all out and get to the boss.

We both end up leveling twice during the bulk of the mission(from 30-32) go up and target the secondary mob, a now yellow saboteur, take him out and head back down to buff for the big guy. When we zoned up the first time the big guy started attacking me which was just one under red into deep deep orange even though neither of us attacked him. Through out the rest of the very lengthy fight every single time we zoned up, no matter who damaged him, he attacked me every single time hitting me for 70's a pop. For clarity, I am a Sol Enforcer, my friend was an Opifex Soldier, I was wielding two decent damage Aluminum bats and my friend was duel pistols who before the boss could take agro from me if he really wanted to damage wise.

So, I would wait like 30 seconds and then zone up to the boss room. The Soldier has been damaging him this whole time that i have not even been in the room. I don't attack when i pop up but as soon as I get into the room, bam, he attacks me...every time. We must have been up and down about 10-15 times to re-heal and re-buff...and every single time he attacked me whether i attacked him or not and if I was in the room he never attacked the soldier.

Anyone have any idea why this may have happened? A big Kick Me sign, or perhaps I was wearing the boots of his buddy or something. The only spells I had on were Bristling Shield, Thugs Delight, Essence of Vitality and a soldier Absorbtion Shield...basically the same nano's were on the soldier...we have mediocre equipment, that which you would normally find on two low levels with no guild or older characters, I had no aggression enhancers or anything, this guy just hated me...

Thanks for any insight...
