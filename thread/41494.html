Assuming level limits won't be raised... (not that I know anything one way or another here)

LvL 200 characters still gather XP, but have no real use for it. The game has become static, essentially. I can see why limits were imposed - the world has its limits after all, in terms of MOBs, equipment etc. Even if you could increase in level post 200, what would you fight? What new gear could you equip? The database can't be completely open-ended.

Why not simply give level 200 characters one IP reset point every time they "level" post 200? You've now got a fixed amount of IP to play with, but over time you can shape your character in ways you might not have tried when you didn't have the leisure to do so. Now though, you're finally off the levelling treadmill, so why not relax and have a little fun?

Comments?
