While I don't want to be misconstrued as flaming, I did want to make some comments...

Number one is the explanation of people being attacked by NTs from so far away they can't be seen. I can understand that, but there is a little flaw. The cap is set to 40m. Problem is, the character view distance slider goes up to 80m. Why is the cap set to half the view distance (based on this explanation)?

Number two, the 40m cap can be better explained with PvM. Quite simply, mobs' field of vision only extends about 40m. Beyond that, they can't see you. The explanation is that NTs could attack outside of the visual range, thus preventing the mob from going aggro. There is a problem here too, though. Wouldn't it make more sense for the attack itself to make the mob aggro, rather than the fact of it seeing you? I'd rather have every single mob I root go instant aggro than have my range capped.

While I'm elated that the NT range NFs are now working, I'm a little disturbed that our main line of defense (distance) has been limited. Against Enforcers and their root-breaking NF, we're dead meat. Our only other defense was the distance from us to them.
