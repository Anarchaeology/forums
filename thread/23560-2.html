Some comments and questions ...
1. New versions of all the existing damage shields Adventurers have. These will inflict slightly more damage than the other ones. The major difference is that the absorption shield effect on them now extends to all damage types and will refresh itself every 2 minutes. Naturally, they don't stack with the existing ones. They are self-only and have a lower NCU cost than the others.

This sounds good on paper. The lower NCU cost will be much welcomed I'm sure. If this new version shield only refreshes every 2 minutes, then the absorption factor better be good otherwise it is just a cosmetic buff taking up NCU space - hopefully not as this is a major issue brought up by Advys.

Project Fire-Breath poll response summary:
Adventurers use damage shields
A) when doing individual missions because "every little bit helps" and not because the shields are that effective.
B) sometimes when being main/secondary tank in teams and only because they have NCU space to keep it on. If no NCU space, the damage shield is the first to go to make way for better buffs from other professions.

--------------------

2. Damage shield upgrades. These are secondary damage shields that will stack with the other adventurer damage shields. Self-only. Can only be cast if the adventurer has another damage shield running. Later versions also provide minor damage and HP buffs. Last one also provides 2% deflection.

This looks good. Will certainly help the Advy on solo missions as well the Advy who plays tank role in teams. I hope it is as effective as it looks otherwise it fails to solve the NCU issue faced by Adventurers.

---------------------

3. Buff line for 1h-edged. Also does a minor fast attack buff. Last ones do a minor damage and parry buff. Self-only.

4. Buff line for pistols. Also does a minor fling shot buff. Last ones do a minor damage and perception buff. Self-only.

5. Combo multi-range + multi-melee buff. Self-only. Last ones in the line also do minor evade buffs.

1HE and Pistol buff-lines. No details?
Combo NR+MM self-buffs. No details?

Fact:
IPR reset is coming before the Advy Loving - no details means Adventurer has to plan BLIND. Some Advys are worried and upset about the IPR concept change. For reference, check out the poll response at Project Fire-Breath.

Fact:
These 1HE and Pistol buff lines will have more meaning if there is a better variety of good and viable weapons ... otherwise it fails to solve a major issue/problem Adventurers are facing - POOR WEAPON CHOICE. For reference, check out the poll response at Project Fire-Breath.

---------------------

The rest are nanos that can only be cast when the adventurer is in a polymorphed form:
6. Nukes when in pit lizard form. One single-target nuke. One AOE nuke. One AOE DoT.

7. Massive HP buff when in pit lizard form (7000HP). Useable once every 20 minutes. Self-only.

Nice to know that the ultimate polymorph will finally get a proper beefing up. However, of what true real benefit is this to an Adventurer?

- It cannot be used indoors. Therefore it is useless for missions ... missions which are the bread and butter to get items/nanos/money.
- Outdoor hunting at that high level is so poor that its laughable. Therefore this is useless for hunting.
- That only leaves high-level PvP ... lol, which affects how many Adventurers?

- Pit Lizard has been bugged and broken since Beta. Will this aspect ever get fixed?

---------------------------

8. Damage and evade team aura when in wolf form. More effective on people who are also in wolf form. Later versions also do minor runspeed and amsmodifier buffs.

9. Perception buffs when in wolf form. Basically the opposite of the agent's Ruse of Taren line (top level version if +600 perception). Can be cast on others if they are also in wolf form. Also provides minor evasion and HP buffs.

These look promising as the current lack of solid team contribution abilities is a major issue for Advys especially at high levels. For reference, check out Project Fire-Breath.

--------------------

10. Instant-cast self-only heals when in sabretooth form. Can be used every 25-35 seconds. Very low variance in the heals so the adventurer knows what to expect from the heal.

11. Self-only damage+aggdef buffs when in sabretooth form. Can be used every 2-3 minutes. Increases damage and amsmodifier. Later ones also increase dmsmodifier, aggdef and eventually initiatives.

These look promising. Can't comment much without details of the modifiers.

------------------------

12. 12 hour leet charms when in leet form. This is just for fun. The adventurer can charm a leet for 12 hours and have a little pet. Higher levels of this mean that you will be able to charm higher level leets - Leet, Eleet, Leetas, and finally Soleet.

Cute. More of the circus act routine I suppose. And how can you forget the uber Supaleet? He will be most upset at being left out of the leet family tree.
