I just want to say "Man,I hate 2holes"
Ever whompa into 2holes in a plane and head for the grid? If the lag is too bad or you misjudge you distance to the grid, your grace period ends. You are then engaged by our favorite players, the grid campers.

Twice in 2 days it was the same person. Found out he was 80 levels above me.

Whose fault, mine. I could have used another route. I just enjoy knowing that there are poeple who just log on to annoy other players.

It takes a real competitive spirit to attack someone 80 levels below you who is defenseless in his plane.


Yesterday, I tried my luck again. This was awesome!

As you know, when you exit the whompa from 4holes, you need to elevate over the wall, but ususally you just fly into it because you are lagging.

Well, I was trying to elevate over this wall, my active cursor sliding up the screen as I attempted in vain to control my plane. It found its way over my inventory and selected a backpack. Since I was in a hurry, I just headed to the grid. When I clicked on the grid I got this message- "Item has been dropped". I gridded, then realized that the backpack, inadvertantly selected and not replaced, had been left at 2holes.

Which backpack I wondered? Oh, no big deal just the one with my ql 200 helmet, ql 194 cloak, ql154 tank armor, etc, etc, etc. Just millions of dollars spent on this stuff.

Gridded back to look for the backpack. Tried this 10 times, looked everywhere. Gone, poof. What a way to begin a evenings entertainment.


Anyway, this is not a rant or a flame. All this happened about three days ago. It is just that everytime I select a mission and see one in that area, I think, "Man, I hate that place".

My posts are usually funnier, sorry. But my sense of inner peace could not quell this nagging aggravation and only a cathartic post could bring this issue to closure.
