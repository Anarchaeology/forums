Bleh, I'm not going to make you camp the entire sewer area for three hours, that is stupid. I don't have the exact coordinates, but this should help you find it.

When you get into the sewers, and run past all the non-aggroing Beibblefish Floaters (err, whatever ), you eventually get to a room with four to six floaters that agro you. In this room are two ramps, one of which leads back to the main dungeon area, and can be used as a small shortcut for getting to the Foreman.

Tri Plumbo spawns every 3 hours (I did time this, it is 3 hours on the dot) on the west side of this room, in the water, just a few meters south east of the pillar in the water. He is a giant mutant looking MOB, so it will be easy to spot him (he looks nothing like the floaters).

Good luck. He drops the ring anywhere from 1 in 100 to 1 in 250 times depending on who you talk to. I know someone who got the ring after killing Tri Plumbo 80 times.

Not worth it for only 220 HP IMO.
