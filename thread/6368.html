Octo, what you just experienced is a major bug Martial Artists have been contending with since beta. If you have a weapon in your hand and you zone/get debuffed/possibly even get buffed/so much as look oddly at the gun it will disable your ability to use MA skill at the same time. See if your martial arts ability is over 120 you should be able to use whatever weapon is in your hand AND punch at the same time but due to this bug you need to zone remove the weapons, re-zone, put the weapons back in your hand and then continue and you should (in theory) be using both whatever weapon you are holding and punch at the same time, though sometimes this does not even work and you need to relog. Funcom is aware of the issue, and they have been "looking into it" for a very very long time now.
