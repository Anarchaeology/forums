Ideas for static dumgeons...
I realize that there are static dungeons already, but I hve a couple of ideas for some new and "plot related" dungeons:

1) Alien Artifact/Temple/Spaceship: Somethng to do with a non-human race, that would give whoever side who controlled it a new weapon or nano because of the technology found there. Nothng to powerful, but useful none the less. I see a good way to control it is to fight your way to the center through guards, robots, and creatures. Once you get to the center, you can fight a boss character that is on the enemy side. If you can beat the boss, then you can activate a security panel or something to convert the ship to your side.

2) Mines: This is rubi ka. The notum is the reason for the conflict in the first place. The mines should be interconnected,and seperated into zones, which can be controlled by either side. The more mines a side controls, the more money they would make, the cheaper the things for that side would be. Also, people could hire themselves out to mine i they were low on cash.

3) floating island: Actually have a good use for flying vehicles. I can't really think of anything plot related for a floating island, but it would b an interesting way to add a zone.

4)Terraform Rubi-Ka: Not really a new zone, but it would add diversity to the forever desert world of Rubi-Ka. Make the planet colder, and actually have some snow some places.

Just some ideas, add anything you want to them.
