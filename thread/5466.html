They are supposed to be like this:

You ask a NPC for a mission. NPCs always stays in one place. They are always there, unless someone messes them up. They always offer the same mission.

You get a specific goal (sorry, no examples here) which has to be solved in a specific way.

You then get a fixed reward for this.

Thus, they will be the same no matter which player gets them. Sure, someone with higher runspeed might have an easier time traveling to the target, but you still got to do the same thing.


Those static dungeons has been in the game for a long time. They are just a general hunting place where certain mobs spawns... No missions there.

Asked support about it. Lots of people get this wrong. Static missions have little resemblance with dynamic missions.


You can only get them from certain NPCs. You talk to them and in some way use certain keywords to get a mission. You then get an icon in the mission menue (hence the 6 slots).

You will get certain clues, but there is no actual coords shown.
