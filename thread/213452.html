Cooking up Symbiants
“Blacks” Yea Old Symbiant Cookbook

QL 190 Enduring Brain Symbiant Support unit

Basic Starting solution: QL 150-200

Ingredients
1 Antiseptic Jar QL 150+
1 Canister Liquid Notum QL 100+
1 Immortal Fungus 150+
1 Blood Plasma 150+
1 Notum Chunk or Fragment 150+

Add the Liquid Notum to the Jar to start then drop in the Fungus. Mix in the Bloodplasma till the whole mass becomes black and slurry. Crush and add in the Notum fragment/chunk and mix till little glowing bits are seen in the solution.

Your starting solution is ready for the next stage.

The finishing touches

Ingredients
1 Head Implant (Empty) 190+
1 NC Poison AC 170+
1 NC Comp Lit 170+
1 NC QuantumFT 170+
1 NC FirstAid 170+
1 NC Intell 170+
1 NC Mat Cre 170+
1 NC Space Time 170+
1 NC Mat Meta 170+
1 NC Max Nano En 170+
1 NC Bio Meta 170+
1 NC NanoC. Init 170+
1 NC Psychic 170+
1 NC Psycho Mod 170+
1 NC Sense Imp 170+
1 NC Treatment 170+
1 NC NanoResist 170+
1 NC Dimach 170+
1 NC Elect. Eng 170+
1 NC Nano Pool 170+
1 NC Sense 170+
1 NC Trap Disarm 170+
1 NC Perception 170+
1 Support Bauble 100+

Add first the Head implant and wait till it sinks into the slurry. Then add in the Nanoclusters one at a time in the given order from the Ingredients list. Deviation from the list will ruin the mixture for support Symbiant Still may be used for another if you make a mistake. Once all clusters are added to the mix it should have formed a dark black ball with glowing bits in clear fluid. Drop in the support Bauble and the Symbiant should awaken and take its final form. Wait a few minutes before Pulling from jar and allow time to dry before installing it.


(I didn't list the Tradeskilling requirements to make it but they should be high but doable. This should give people without Tons of cash for bidding in auctions another way to get the symbiants they want. Also it will not ruin the symbiant market. Just give Tradeskillers a good chance at make the tradeskills they spent IP really WORTH something.)

The basic solution works for all Symbiants But the Finishing recipes are different. stick to them ardently to avoid makeing a mess.
