Dissection of Serrephim (Sepentura)
Serrephim lay on his back watching the stars, his thoughts were wandering. His face grew grim as his mind lead him to a painful day three months ago. The day he chose to move into the portal between the dimensions, a day that would forever be a part of him, the day he had to choose his identity forever.

He remembered the day he first chose to switch, and how hard it was to leave the dimension he had grew up on. There were problems he knew, so it seemed like the only choice. He had hoped it would be the best choice.

His mind brought him to the day he first landed on Rubi-Ka 2, when the ICC told him he would have his name changed, because of ICC regulations, certain names were to be left on the first dimension. He recalled being told to think of a new name for himself. He had always been known by everyone as 'Serrephim' and now he had to leave that identity behind, he had to choose his new one and move on in this brave new world. He chose the name "Sepentura" to be forever known as, or so he thought.

Now here he was, reborn on Rubi-Ka 1, and unable to use his identity once more. He is forced to go by his old identity as 'Serrephim' and be forever torn in a circle of names...

Who is he, Sepentura or Serrephim? The hunted mysterious figure of Rubi-Ka. He is both, passing between dimensions, until he is able to stay on one for good, switching aliases to avoid detection, and lurking in the shadows.
