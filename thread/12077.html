My mother was on a flight from San Francisco and was having severe nausea from a stomach ailment. The woman next to her was wearing too much perfume and smoking constantly. After 3 hours of this she deplaned and puked her guts out. She has never smoked again. This from a woman who had quit 10 or so times previously.

So if you can link something unpleasant to the experience of smoking you can develop learned aversion. Not fun to do, but it is quite effective. The strongest motivater is being diagnosed with lung cancer, avoid this one.

Good luck, everyone you know will thank you. You will finally become aware at how your house, clothes, car, skin, hair, and especially breath stink. And you will have more money for beer.
