Ergo in Penumbra (Getting access to Inferno)
As of the server update today, we have decided to remove (at least for now) the trial to get into Inferno. You will still have to complete the rest of the quest Ergo gives you. It's only the trial that will be disabled.

To get the ring you need from Ergo, give him your old ring and "A slightly dented molar from the great ice golem".

Those who have already traded in the Molar to get the "Mark of the Ice Golem" need to contact Customer Service to get a new molar. Make sure to not delete the Mark, as Customer Service probably needs to verify that you do in fact have it before they will give you a new Molar.

Please be patient about the petition, as there will most likely be a queue.
