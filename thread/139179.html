How about bus?
hehe. I dont know what I have been thinking, I think I have been dreaming or something...

Well, I came up with this crazy idea. How about having busses driving between the towns? Let me explain.

Each route has a couple of Bus stops, example, between Rome and Galway. There would be small bus stops between the main destinations. At each Bus Stop, there was a terminal where you could buy a ticket to a bus stop on the route. The bus would circulate between the two different routes, and it would stop at each bus stop for a minute. Then you could click on it, and be transported to the bus stop of your wish. This could be a good way to travel around Rubi-Ka, to places without Grid and Whompa. There would have to be roads though...

Still think it could be fun. And those not riding the bus, could see the bus driving down the road.
