If keeper of team mission goes LD...
make it so that the new team leader is automatically the keeper of the mission.

Why? Does this happen a lot? Yes it does. This has happened twice in the last 5 team missions I have done.

No reward, no tokens, this has gotta be changed! And the sooner, the better! Thanks in advance.
