If I manage the required 30 more levels to 200, it will be guaranteed 1000% more satisfying than dinging as one of the two catered-to sides.

Us Neutrals are a thorn in the side of what I consider to be small-thinking game designers. Our very existence at high levels has forced them to accomodate us in the Notum Wars development process. They seem to think that having two sides is best for the game. Perhaps they are right considering the storyline and all (I mean post-Shadowlands storyline. Not the quibbling children stuff we have now).

But, assuming the game lasts until Shadowlands release, I have to state unequivocably that if any 'side' were to appear Angelic in the Shadowlands, it would be the Neutrals who refused to engage in the petty, often vulgar, almost-never reasoned, squabbling that you see on the 'story' pages. Bunch of silly nonsense all that.

The Neutrals for the most part remain true to themselves and those they care about. They don't harbor hatred based on labels. They never prejudge you. 'Smelly Clanner'/'Omni Drone' just don't work for most of us. You are measured on your merits. Somewhere between the Devil and and a lunatic religious fanatic, you'll find harmony and enlightenment. It's sure to be found in the home of a Neutral if it's on Rubi'Ka.

Sure as heck ain't gonna be found at a guild meeting of some Omni or Clan guild. And if David Marlin comes down out of orbit and puts his strength behind the Clans as they currently act, I'll have lost all faith in the book Prophet without Honor. Just look at the Council of Ares if you want to see a group formed not out of enlightenment but of the more baser and darker elements of humanity. Surely not an example of all that is right with mankind. Omni-Tek is known to be influenced by Vanya and Roman Dragov and as such an extension of what caused the near extinction and subsequent enslavement of mankind. Yet, even within the ranks of Omni-Tek, I'm sure there exist good and honest people who would flee to the ranks of neutrality if they ever found out the truth behind their company's power and future plans. But, not to the Clans. Equal evil and ambition over there.

Who was it that kissed Jesus on the cheek? Judas, right? For 13 silver coins or so. Well, that's what selling your soul to either of the two feuding sides for a token board seems like to any Neutral. Either you have no morals, misguided morals, or simply look at this as a game that you need to 'win'. Kinda like those who side-hop to wear different side-specific items.
