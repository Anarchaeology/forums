Hit points and Nano pool definitely do not have the same value.

If a character has 3000 hit points and 4000 nanopool (like I did, not too long ago) then if you add 800 hitpoints it will effect my game far more than if I added 800 nano.

Therefore, if nanobreeds get a nanobreed advantage, it should be huge in order to compensate for the diminishing value of adding more nano pool the higher you go.

Hit points do not have a diminishing return. It's always good to have more hit points.

The only situation where nano pool is more important than hit points is of course class related, named Doctors. And that of course is the perspective that Destria is coming from, being a doc. The healers have programs that convert nano to life...for Docs, this can go up to over a 7 to 1 converstion ratio.

But for most classes their nano pool is not an extension of their hitpoint total. Add 1000 nano to my total or that of my alts and I wouldnt even blink. Add 1000 hitpoints and I will. Thats the difference to me.

If solitus has 800 more hitpoints. Then nano mages should have at least 1600 more nano along with other nano formula related benefits.
