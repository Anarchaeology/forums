Neutral Quest suggestion
I have what I consider a good idea for a neutral quest. This should be a multipart quest, that is difficult to complete. It should not be combat based, something tricky to figure out that takes thinking and perhaps willingness to go places that aren't always the safest but not guaranteed suicidal. Upon completing the quest, the person's faction standing will change, from Neutral to ICC Member. The benefit of this would be that the player is removed from the tab targeting of all factions, as well as removed from the aggro lists of faction specific npc's. There will still be neutral players who either aren't interested in the quest, who can't complete it or simply don't know about it, these people can still be considered innocent civilians caught in the middle of the struggle.

The ICC Member is someone who's supposed to be there, to observe the struggle, they are recognized by both factions as observers, and hence not supposed to be shot at. Therefore no longer in the tab targetting cycle. This will not be immunity from combat, if someone manually targets the player they are still up for grabs, but this would provide a miniscule barrier for players whose purpose for being in where the fighting is, is something other than fighting.
