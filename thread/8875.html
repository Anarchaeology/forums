New Fixer Nanos (Discussion)
I just got my first fixer to lvl 65 and I've made some observations. I'm sure that some nano options were done to balance/trade-off the fixer with the 'crat.

1) I have an individual and team/group slow down, and only an individual root. Should we have team roots? (Are they available but not found in shops?) I guess this might not matter since -1000 run speed might be fine if the team/group nano works right.

*2) My real suggestion is for Grid travel. It would be cool to be able to "push" someone else, teammate or hostile, into the grid. If the person isn't on my team, then they can try to counter. But this would be great for getting rid of someone camping an area or just someplace and they need to be put into the grid, just put them into the grid and move on, also a nice way for fixers to make a quick donation for services without having to go into the grid themselves. No real damage done. I suppose this wouldn't work on mobs, but maybe it could just phase them into the grid a bit so that they couldn't attack for a period of time, like the PROJECT CALM for NT, but they could still follow me around.

3) HOT nanos - these are nice, but I was suprised to see them on the fixer, out of place, but till okay.

4) Supply nanos - It would be cool to have a wider selection or randomness to these nanos. After all, we're hacking into omni's supply chains here. There should be some nice/exotic stuff, new clothing, etc. (You don't always get what you want.)

Thanks,
