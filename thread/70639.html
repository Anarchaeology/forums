New Bank Terminal setup w/transfer function. ( FunCom plz!)
I would like to see a Bank Terminal setup were I can transfer Goods and Money between my different characters. This would get rid of the problem of ppl running off with your stuff, and getting rid of that awful feeling when giving it to some stranger because no one on your friend list was online.

This is how I picture it would work:

1. U click on the bank and up comes a list of your charaters names.

2. U click on the charaters bankaccount u would like to access. This then opens up the well known little bank window, but on the top it will say your charaters name so u know whitch account u are in, also there will be 2 "slots" on the bottom were u can type in money amount with a accept button (just like in trade mode).

3. To transfer Goods: Open your Account window and click on another charater name to bring up its Account, then just place the Goods in that Account (or ofcourse just plop it into another Account directly from your invitory).

4. To transfer Money: As I mentioned there should be 2 displays at the bottom of the Account (like in trade mode) with an accept button under.
The top display will show how much money that charater has and the other is were u can type in an amount. Now the thing is u can ONLY have TWO windows open at a time. This is so not to confuse from which account the transfer is going from and in. Lets say I want to transfer from Account X over to Account Y.
*Open X. Open Y.
*Type in 100 000 in the bottom of X Account window This will then display a minus symbol infrount (-100 000), at the same time automaticly the 100 000 shows up at the bottom af Account Y but with a pluss symbol (+100 000).
*Then just push the accept button. =)

So what do u all think? I would also like to say if u want to transfer from two different accounts (not from the charater u are using) then the Account that is FIRST opened is the FROM Account, this could be showed with the charatrers name on the top displayed in Green and the IN Accounts name shown in Red.

I think I got it right. Did this straight on the go from the top of my head. I think this would be a great idea. I find it strange that in the advanced world of RubiKa that they can't transfer money in there banks (what no network??) and can't send Goods to eachother.

Plz FunCom let me know if this is a valid idea to be taken under consideration....
