I think the idea is an excellent one. The problem is, of course, that there are currently very few items that could be sold on a black market. Almost everything side-specific is explicitly restricted to members of that side.

One way to do this would be to rebalance items so that each faction has significantly superior items of one type or a much broader selection. This way you could create demand. For example, say, Omni has better Doc nanos and a broader quality selection, Clan had superior and a broader selection of soldier nanos, while Neutrals specialized in MA nanos, etc. Weapons and armor would follow a similar pattern.

This would certainly promote the player economy. One issue though, is that its not really an underground economy, rather is an extension of the normal economy. To make it an underground economy, you need to make it illicit; that is, it would be illegal to sell the special items of your faction, and doing so and getting caught results in sanctions.

Now the problem gets harder because 'getting caught' would take a fairly sophisticated AI, and there is currently nothing capable of it in the game. One way around this problem would be for players to sign up for police duty. They would get a temporary identity and get paid and xp for catching illicit sale of items beloning to their faction. The mechanisms for proving an illicit sale would have to be incorporated to avoid griefing and harassment (.e.g. interaction transcript with item transaction log, etc.). The person who was caught would, say, get a hefty fine, etc.

It could be a lot of fun and some non-combat lifesytles could result. Especially if you got xp as well as the cash for every successful illicit sale
