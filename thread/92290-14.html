It's true, Cz, that in predicting that there will be further cuts in customer service and customer relations areas, I am guessing. However, it's a best-guess based on the information at hand:

1. The company president changed.

2. Developers have been cut.

3. Customer relations has been cut to one person. Customer service emails already seem to be handled primarily by one or two people.

4. The Live Events team disappeared, and Ragnar was "let go." Story activity dwindled and then disappeared.

5. The main webpage hasn't been updated with any player articles, let alone story updates, for over a full month.

6. The NW launch was an almost unmitigated disaster with the existing playerbase, and doesn't seem to have increased the subscriber base by much.

7. Funcom is now in the process of taking a step with its customer relations (ie, closing forums) that you, of all people, had to know would be wildly unpopular - for many very valid reasons - with just about everyone who uses them.

8. Funcom refuses to release any statements about its financial situation. Even you, while (correctly) pointing out that I was only speculating, did not directly refute me by saying, "The company is financially secure and is making a profit on AO."

9. SWG is coming.

10. The economy in America, and in the world at large, is generally down.

So yes, I'm not 100% sure that more cuts in customer service areas are to come, however, it is fairly logical to predict it. There is a minimal amount of programmer and developer resources that are required to operate the game at all, let alone continue providing updates to it - but Sony Online Entertainment has proven with EQ that an MMORPG can effectively cancel Customer Service and Customer Relations both, and plenty of people will keep playing, if unhappily.
