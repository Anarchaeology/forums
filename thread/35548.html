Saturday, 13 July - Doctor Conference
We'll meet at the Bronto Burger in Newland City at 3 pm EST on Saturday, 13 July 2002. This hopefully is both a PST-friendly and GMT friendly time to start, I don't want to exlcude people based on timing.

In the event that there is a problem related to the location - lag, bad weather, annoying people running around - I will arrange for the preperation of a private alternate site. I'll also arrange for waiters to move about with backpacks full of fine food and drink to distribute. Only the best for us respected healers.

I envision this as a conference both on In-Character matters and on Out-Of-Character matters. I think it would be best if we kept the two segments seperate, dealing with OOC business first and IC business second, with an intermission in between to chat and so forth. That way people who aren't interested in roleplay or the storyline and so on can leave during the intermission.

Anyone who wants to give a speech or presentation during the event is welcome to send me an email: Jynne's Email. Please note whether you will be speaking IC or OOC and give a brief outline of your position so I can create an agenda grouping related topics together. Try and keep your speech under 10 minutes in length, prepared as a script if at all possible. I'll update the agenda on this thread as it develops.

I'd like to invite interested persons of other professions to attend and to speak as well. Dress should be semi-formal, please put away your armor and weapons if at all practical.


Agenda:
I. Greeting and Introductions
.....a) Welcome: Jynne
.....b) Ground Rules for Debates: Jynne

II. OOC Topics
.....a) Lack of Doctor-specific items: Razishlyat

.....last) Level limits on Doc nanos?: Jynne

III. Intermission


IV. IC Topics
.....a) Should doctors fight in the war?: Razishlyat
.....b) Doctor Association: Luxurea
.....c) (potential) Doctors and teammates: Hueso

V. Closing Remarks and Reception


I hope it will be both enjoyable and useful for everyone who attends

Well I'm going to suggest some topics for discussion so I'll maybe get some more emails

OOC Topics:
1. Level reqs on nanos. FC has stated that all nanos in 14.6 and on will have level reqs. How will this affect our gameplay, especially in relation to level reqs on new doctor nanos?

2. Shadowlands expansion. What do we know about it and what will it mean to us?

3. Doctor-related bugs. HP bars, line-of-sight especially. Lag and its effect on trying to cast a nano.

4. What would we want to see in a 'doctor patch'? What would we not want to see? New stuff? Bug fixes? Changes to existing?

5. Doctor strategies: Tactics for teaming, soloing, and PvPing. Equipment and implant schemes, nano choice. Ip allocation. Etc.

6. Tips for new doctors.

IC Topics:
1. Should doctors be fighting - directly killing people - in the war between OT and the Clans? Is it right for doctors to be killers?

2. Are doctors ethically bound to heal anyone who needs it? Is it ever right to let someone die when you can save them?

3. Buffing... what are the standards for charging for buffs or asking for tips? When and why do you refuse to buff?

4. When and why do you quit a team?

5. How do you deal with greedy teammates and ninja-looters, especially given team missions, chests, and boss loots?

6. Would it be worth it to set up an in-game doctor network or association of some sort? How would this work and what would it do? Could we have a secret handshake?

7. Are doctors under-appreciated? Do we sometimes under-appreciate our teammates contributions?

If you have something to say, email me through the link from the top of the thread, I'd like to have things worked out a few days before the 13th so attendees will have time to read over the agenda
