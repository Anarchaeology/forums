Init Fixed - Proof! :)
Yes indeed, the 14.04 update on test has fixed our fists so that they are ruled by phys init as they should! For your viewing pleasure, here are my latest numbers to crunch :

Me (lvl 195) vs. 190 engie in the arena
Phys Init = 950

100% aggro - 26 punches/minute

50% - 26/min

25% - 26/min

20% - 26/min

15% - 24/min

10% - 24/min

5% - 22/min

Full Def - 21/min

Wow! is all I can say. 950 phys init btw is with ql200 implants and first strike running, not that hard to reach for a higher lvl MA. Earlier when fists were running on my 726 melee init i kept the aggro bar at half to get the full effect. Now it can be set at around 20% with no slow down at all

Now here is the even more interesting part. . . I have been experimenting with Flurry of Blows on test as well. With my ql133 FoB on I hit full speed again at full def as well So FoB certainly modifies your aggro setting as it says in the description. How much exactly we may never know. Also it does NOT break the 1s/1s barrier like some think, as i have tried many times at full aggresive and only get the same speed and number of attacks. Now if it is SUPPOSED TO then we could really see some carnage, but i doubt this is going to happen.

One other thing. I have been testing number of attacks when wielding my torturing tool as well, and have come to the conclusion that the maximum number of attacks you can dish out per minute it 40. Fists and the tool have comparatively the same atk/recharge, and alternate evenly during my tests. So where as an MA that uses just fists would lose 6 of the 26 fist attacks per minute, he would gain 20 tool attacks, for a net gain of 14 extra attacks Taichis would have the same result I believe judging it by their atk/rechg rates. So are MA weaps worth it? I think so!

Another kudos to FC for getting this issue resolved quickly! Gives me hope for the future
