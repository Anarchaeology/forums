Healing Class vs. Non-Healing Class (PvP Fix)
Everyone knows that PvP is messed up, definately needs some attention.

13.0 fixed nothing, just switched polarity on who was more dominant.

The healing professions have so much of an advantage it's pitiful. If attacks are halfed and healing stays the same what chance does a non-healer have in outdamaging the healers recovery?

Let me answer that for you......... Slim to None !!!

Some say that if you half damage you should half healing. But then all the healing classes who have gotten used to dominating PvP now are gonna cry "nerf".....

Here is what I propose.... A compromise:

Reduce the effects of healing to 70% efficency and keep damage at 50% efficent. That way the healing class still get's a greater amount of healing power, but the non-healers have a fighting chance.... now they do not.

So please post: Everyone, healers and non-healers... what do you think about 70% ? I think it is a compromise and would contribute to greater balance of PvP.... which like it or not is what the game is all about, eventually we are going to be in a war and if PvP isn't fixed the whole game will be garbage... not just PvP.

Also while you are at it.... Nano Professions are at a loss..... Now battles last twice as long and all casters run out of nano before the fight is close to finish..... It is only fair that if you reduce thier damage by 50% that you reduce the nano cost by 50%.

Summary:

1. Reduce Healing by 30% in PvP to make it 70% effective (a compromise on the parts of both healers and non-healers)

2. Reduce Nano Cost by 50% in PvP

3. Also, lastly: make it to where people in a team can not attack people that are not in a team..... there are people out there who are lvl 30 teamed with a group of lvl 60 and they can attack a solo lvl 35..... I'm tired of getting ganked by teams of griefers.

The 1st two should be a requirement, the 3rd is a mere request LOL
