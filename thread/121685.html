RK2 activity?
Hello people!

I am quite new to Anarchy Online and I have been fascinated by the freedom of the players from the first day I started playing it. Before AO I played an annoying game called Counter Strike. When I grew tired of the large number of immature people playing CS, I took down AO from the shelf (It'd been lying on a shelf since summer 2001, and I'd been unable to play it since it required creditcard number) and found out I didn't need creditcard number anymore!
I have now read the timeline, most forum threads, lots of guides and much more. People are actually EDUCATED and SOCIAL here, which is great! Everything seems fantastic, except a few things, of which the major thing is annoying me quite alot. When I looked at the player calendar, I saw that all the fun events took place on RK1 - and I'm playing on RK2. Due to this, I'm considering to start playing on RK1 instead.

Well, I guess this is a request to the organisations of RK2.

Organize some fun events. I beg you!

I am caught up in my leveling, so I won't be interested in organisations yet. But I hope those who's been doing their leveling for a while now could take the initiative.
