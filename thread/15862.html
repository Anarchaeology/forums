How Are People Affording Some of this Stuff?
Over in the over-equipping thread du jour (1600 posts and rising), I keep seeing throwaway remarks from people about how at level 45 they had level 90 implants in, or whatever. Now, OK, I can see easily how it could be done. Pay an NT or an Engie to assemble them, get a high level wrangle to cover the attribute requirements, get a surgery buff, and bang, done.

What I can not for the life of me figure out is how people are paying for all of this. I've got four or five characters scattered from level 15 to 52, and a complete set of implants at their own level is enough to clean any one of them out.

Same thing with NCUs. To run a decent set of buffs for any given character level takes about six times the NCU space you can have, if you're using belts and NCU chips anywhere near your own level. That means mostly mission rewards and loot are useless - and NCU chips are expensive relative to the level you need them.

How are people paying for all this stuff? They can't all be buying credits on eBay!
