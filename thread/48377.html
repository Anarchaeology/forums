I am an NT and my problems with traders are the following....

My damage is halved in PvP, but traders still get full-effect debuffs, which lasts 3 mins each vs. my debuffs that lasts 20 secs and less. Trader can take me down to level 70ish by landing all debuffs on me.

If a trader lands on divest on me, I am still functioning. Albeit it does affect my top range of nanos and attack rating, but I am still functioning. I can survive until...

If the trader manages to land a plunder on me, I am back to effectiveness comparable to level 70 or so. However, nano resist does make it slightly easier to resist, so I still have a chance (more about luck), if I can take the trader down before he lands a plunder on me. If I am plundered and divested for 3 minutes, I might as well terminate to save myself from emberrasment.

The duration of trader debuffs is not professions specific, same thing with docs and MPs. The duration of these debuffs on target should not be longer then 20-30 secs...even if the benefit stays with the trader for 3 minutes.

To be hit with full effect of debuffs when my damage is halfed is a big issue when it comes to balance. Divests and Plunders pretty much kills my ability to use some key defensive nanos...aka NS MKII and Advanced Layered Protection/Fleeting Immunity. So trader have effectively not only disabled my offence, but also my defence. I could survive a bit longer without other, but not without both. Not sure what the best thing here would be, probably make divests with a shorter duration and plunders with longer duration but with slightly decreased effect. While the trader would still get the benefits for full duration.

I agree with Dharin that traders definetly should NOT be nerfed when it comes to PvM, those nanos are needed in there. However, I would say that duration when debuff is in effect on target should be carefully thought out for PvP...3 minutes there is eternity
