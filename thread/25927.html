Well Cosmik, the BureauCons used to be disscussions about crat issues (BureauCons 1 - 5). But to put it frankly, we've run out of things to talk about that haven't been talked to death already. Our profession haven't had much of anything happen balance wise since the fear nerf and crat suit. We have posted to the Crat Top 10 without replies from the ideas we've gathered during the conventions.

So we've decided to shift the focus on the BureauCons to IC instead of OOC. From BureauCon 6 to 8 so far the focus has been changed to politics. The last BureauCon 8 was an attempt to show what is possible with a career in roleplaying politics on Rubi-Ka with the formation of the Diplomatic Assembly.

The Bureaucrat Conventions are basically takeing whatever direction is necessrary to continue to enchance the bureaucrat experience while on Anarchy Online.
