Where to find Reflect Bracers
Locations:

1) Missions (Chests)
2) Steps of Madness (Title Level 2 -3)
3) Enhanced Sharks (Title Level 4) west of Old Athens
4) Smugglers Den, Mantis Queen (Title Level 6)
5) Spiders: Creepers (GTC, Newland Desert and TLR), Gargantulas (GTC, VW and SAV), Fearspinners and Red Widows (PM, Mort and Andromeda)
6) Forest Eremite & Forest Cenobite (Title Level 4-5) Belail Forest, CAV, DAV (~1500x1500)
7) Bileswarms & Ottous (Title Level 4) Longest Road (Dead Forest)

Post other locations if u know them plz

BTW, when u guys see a bracer that reflects X%, it does not mean it will reflect X% of damage.

What actually happens.. is that the bracer will REDUCE the amount of damage inflicted on u by X%.

At the same time.. it will reflect back an amount of damage back to the opponent, and this reflected damage is CAPPED at the absolute value of X.
