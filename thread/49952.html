Org. Applicant Hazing!!!
FunCom:

This is just what every org. needs! We must be able to use hazing while we have a new applicant in our guild!

Hazing is a fundamental element of all modern day groups!

Hazing should be implemented so that Unit Leader or higher positions could do such actions to applicants as:

-Kill them at will whenever/wherever.
-Unequip their items
-Force them to appear as though they are wearing certain items (looks like they are wearing a dress, etc.) even if they are not.
-Cause their weapons/armor to become a certain % OE
-Force them to shout/say things
-Lower their max HP
-Lower their max nano
-Lower their stats/abilities
-Force them to /fblock (or other emotes)
-Dump their NCU of all beneficial nano programs
-Make it so the applicant can be attacked anywhere by anyone
-Swap their directional controls so forward is backwards, strafe left is strafe right, etc...
-Warp them to a random spot in a random zone
-Make their money disappear/un-usable for a period of time
-Control them as if they were a charmed NPC pet
-Force them to always have a DoT running on them dealing a certain amount of damage

There are many parts to a fully-featured hazing system that must be considered and implemented! This is not an option, FunCom! Your customers are demanding this! Please review and implement such a system ASAP.

Thanks,

-Treydor

While it's not really implemented, my org has some interesting methods of dealing with people who join and turn out to be idiots.

Here is an example:

Open a private group chat and invite everyone who will participate in the hazing, with the exception of the victim. Let everyone know what is going on so you can announce a "Really Cool Event" in org chat and invite the new member to come along. Get the member to meet you and the rest of the org members who are participating in a certain location. While everyone is gathering in that location, have an org engineer fly out to the middle of CAV or other appropriate out-in-the-woods mayhem zone. The engineer then uses beacon warp to transport everyone to the mayhem area - one person immediately roots the victim and the rest beat him senseless.

(Have done this, it worked great.)

Another thing you can do is tell the applicant you have found a really cool exploit, and they should type /org bank add (all their money) and then /org bank remove (the amount they put in) and it will dupe the cash. Be sure to tell them it doesn't work right anymore unless they have zero credits exactly when they remove it the first time because FC only half-way fixed this exploit. Then since they are an applicant, they will be able to add the money but will not be able to take any money out of the bank. Tada, there went all their credits!

(Haven't tried this one yet)

Also, find creative ways of talking the applicant into using the /terminate command when they haven't saved. For example, let's say the applicant just bought a yalm and is asking how to equip and remove it. Explain that he should right click it to equip it, and to remove it, type in the command /terminate yalm operation.

(Didn't do this personally, but saw it done and laughed a lot)

Tell the applicant one of the benefits of your org is Bronto Burger hacking services. Explain that due to the time required you will not hack individual Bronto Burgers, he must bring at least two full packs of them to you. Invent some special property the Bronto Burgers have after being hacked, such as that they work like a pill that casts an HoT on you. When he goes to the burger stand and brings back two full packs of Bronto Burgers to be hacked, mock him mercilessly and make sure the entire org joins in on making fun of him. This works better if you are a fixer because you will be expected to hack things anyway.

(Did this one, and more than once)

Mind you, such activities within my org are only performed on members who have proven themselves to be complete idiots (not just lacking knowledge, but annoying dimwits).

Enjoy!
