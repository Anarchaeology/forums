Sounds like you're actually in the desert area outside the city of Newland, which is not the same as Newland desert, believe it or not. The oasis in Newland Desert only has the full sized Young Brontos, not the cute little mini variety.

At level 8, Young Brontos are not a viable proposition - they're around level 20 IIRC, so a team in the mid-teens is your best bet. I'd do the Inventor Bobic quest if you haven't already (just down the hill from Nodda Greg) and then take missions until you're about level 15. Then grab a team and go bronto hunting...

The odd thing is that the second part of the Nodda missions only require you to hunt Minibrontos, which are much easier to kill. Fortunately the missions are on a five day timer, so there's plenty of time to level up a bit.
