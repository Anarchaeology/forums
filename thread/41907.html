The teleporting is a result of client side lag. I have a crat that when played at the office on broadband, the sliding/teleporting effect is greatly reduced. You execute the root on your client, but at that time a lot of information is going around (root time, nano resist of mob, your own skill (yes your skill has a small bearing on whether or not the root is broken)), and sometimes the client can't keep up with it, meanwhile the mob moving path is still running on it's own over at the server, and by the time your client catches up, the mob has moved.
