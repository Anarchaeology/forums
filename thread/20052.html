They didnt change the requirements to equip implants.

They just made it so that if you are removing one implant to put in another, the one you are putting in will not take into account any stat boosts that you had gotten from the one you are taking out.

If you ask me, this was a bug... but a bug that we have lived with and used for 10 months now. All Funcom did by "fixing" this bug was make it so that those people at lvl 120 who had already equipped their QL 200 implants will have an advantage over those who didnt. This will even out over time as everyone levels up and those with QL 200 implants in get to the level where they could have put them in anyways, but until that time its a low level character nerf.
