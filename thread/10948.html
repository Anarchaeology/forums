Exactly what is wrong with the NT
Here is what is wrong with the NT....
nothing.

Wait, hear me out...
The problem is not that NTs suck, it is that just about every other class can overequip to hell.

Let me give an example and explain....

I was out at the scorpid pit (GC) last night as my MA... our team was...
2 MAs, lv30ish
1 Enf, lvl 25 or so

In the "old" days, before overequipping was so bad, we had to have a full team out there to live, and we all had to be lvl 35+ or so. Fights usually went like this...
1. someone pulled a mob
2. My NT would start his long nuke
3. All team members would do some damage
4. My nuke would knock half the mobs health off (and often result in me aggroing the mob and having to run around like a sissy)
5. The rest of the team would finish the mob off

Now, it works like this...
1. We run from mob to mob (we can handle up to 3 at a time)
2. Our hammer enf takes a swing with his QL90 hammer, knocks around 60% of the mobs health off
3. We finish the deep-red mob off easily, and any buddies he brought along.

So... what's the problem?
In the old days, my NT was needed for his big damage nuke, but when he'd pull aggro, he'd be in big trouble (often)
Now, we have Enfs who do more damage than my old nuke did, in the same attack/recharge time AND they can sit there taking the hits. Essentially, overequipped ENFs are the new Tank-Mage.

So, it's not that we suck... it's that we are completly not needed. Who needs an NT when you can take other classes that do just as much damage AND have 10 times the health? I'd just a soon not have an NT in my group, except for humidity extractor and roots... so, we're a support class now :P
