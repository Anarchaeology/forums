starknaked
Supa Leet
There are almost certainly titles after Novice.

Except, Funcom has created a nonsensical PVP system which makes it virtually impossible to get them.

The way it seems to work is, you're an Apprentice, say, and you fight a person with no title. You gain 1 point, say. Now you fight another person with no title and you lose. You lose 10 points.

So you have to get like 10 kills for every death yourself just to keep from losing ground.

This is why it becomes almost impossible to progress up the pvp ranks after a certain point.

This sort of system works fine in chess, because in chess the better players really are that much better than a beginner, and could beat beginners 100% of the time. Also, the higher ranked chess players will only play against those of their own rank, so they don't have to worry about losing huge numbers of points per loss.

In AO PVP, though, you have to fight whoever is there, and this person will almost always be of a lower rank if yours is high. Plus, you are not 10 times better than someone with a lower rank. If they're a soldier with total mirror shield and the best equipped weapons who can slam you with a huge flingshot/burst/full auto combo, it doesn't matter if they're got no rank, they're still going to do quite well against you.
