The end is near.
In this shade of his world he saw, or more felt the color. Blue, deeper and deeper but always blue. Tendrils of blue reaching out to him and embracing him. The tendrils where always cradling and caressing. Complete calm, complete serenity. Pondering thoughts drifted about in the blue sea of nothingness. Perhaps this what they meant by a mothers love?

Bless this shade of the world. Free of the constant barrage of the buzzing insects that inhabited the waking hours. No, not insects, the bipedal inhabitants of the other shade deserved not to be among the insects. Its was almost amusing, the irony of it, the insect knows its place and is free from the tethers of freewill where as that with freewill knows not its place. They deserved no moniker other then a word or two. From henceforth they would become it. There was no need to confuse the issue. It was all the same. It would appear in different forms but it was always it. The bickering and the squabbling would end. It wanted peace and he would provide it that wish.

Concealed for now, thats where he needed to be, a current flowing deep in the ocean. He would feed upon its hate and its anger. He would channel and harness it. He would well up form the depths and he would become the cleansing wave.

Perhaps it may even put its petty differences aside when it understand its fate. That would be grand. It would be much easier to clean the stain if it were only in one place.

But for now, he would just float and enjoy the peace of the blue.

Xyrrg awoke from the dream.
Beads of sweat rolling down his sightless face. Had his featureless face been able, it would have produced a grin.
