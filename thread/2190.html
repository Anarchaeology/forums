The Euro Coordination Group
I've submitted this mail to the most important AO information portals (AOB, AOS, AOforge, AO.it, AOwacry, Aofrance, JOL, AOhandbook, AoRealms, etc...)

-------------------------


Hi, i'd like to present the "Euro Coordination Group", we are European Guild Leaders who want to represent the interests of European AO Players.

Our goal is to improve the contact between Funcom and Euro players, in the organisation of events, timing, story etc..

But also improve the communication between European Guild Leaders themselves, with a forum where we will have discussions about sensitive issues, with a Mailing List to be informed of what is going on, on Rubi-Ka.

The E.C.G. has NO political colors, there are Omni & clan side guilds present in the organisation. We like to represent all the Euro Guilds and Euro Players, no matter on which side you are.

We want to let Funcom know that there are also a lot of European Players playing Anarchy Online, and in a public letter to funcom (will be written soon) we will ask what Euro Players consider as a "normal support" for Europa.


Ok, well here is the website for the ECG : www.ecg.fr.st/

And here is the forum : www.ecgforum.fr.st/

If you are LEADER of a Euro Based Guild, please contact us as soon as possible.

Thank You,

Hi,

We'd like to present the "Euro Coordination Group", we are european guild leaders who want to represent the interests of european AO players.

Our goal is to improve the contact between Funcom and european players, in the organisation of events, timing, story...

We also want to improve the communication between european guild leaders themselves, with a forum where we can have discussions about any issues and to communicate about player arranged events, happenings etc.

A mailing list has already been established to improve communications of what is going on in Rubi-Ka. Email href="mailto:nynavene@tackman.net">nynavene@tackma n.net about details and a small presentation about you and your organization.

The E.C.G. has NO political colors, there are Omni and Clan guilds present in the organisation. We want to represent all the european players, whichever side they're on, even neutrals

We want to let Funcom know that there are also a lot of European Players playing Anarchy Online, and in a public letter to Funcom (will be written soon) we will ask Funcom to provide us , the european players with what we consider equal support and communication as the US players do.

One of our main concerns is support and event times, these are both timed to US needs and we would really like for Funcom to improve on that.

Please note, we are NOT trying to eat away on the enjoyment of US players, we just want the same quality of experience in this timezone.

If you are leader or representative of a Euro Based Guild, please contact us as soon as possible.
