It says on the desc something like three perfectly cut gems.

blackmaneD
Leetas
Oh, that's easy:

Because of the RDB gets parsed pretty much as soon as it hits testlive, it is difficult to make anything secret or that needs to be work out. And I wanted making the Triumvirate Virral Egg to be something people had to work out for themselves, rather than just go to Anti-Guardians and look at the description. So, the descriptions for the various stages of the egg are kind of vague in describing the look of the egg, rather than just spelling out that it is made of X,Y,Z.
