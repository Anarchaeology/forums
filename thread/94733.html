The dawn of the Black Sun
Akudama sipped his drink as he read over the most recent report. Business was looking up for a change ever since they had taken over this neighborhood. Now that Black Sun had a fixed home, the gambling profits had almost doubled overnight. Stim sales were always good on Rubi-Ka it seemed. People wanted escape from the harsh realities of war, or the extra edge in battle. Either way, it was good business.

Glancing around at the quiet plaza one could almost forget what went on here after dark. A quiet dusty little plaza, filled with the muted sounds of the city, and the smell of the nearby forest and waterfalls. As usual, the plaza was virtually empty, save for a few passersby and the two men lounging near the terminals. At a glance the two seemed to be nothing more two friends having a quiet conversation under the canopy of the tent., but Akudama knew what was under those coats, and in those hearts. Akudama smiled to himself. Loyal people were so hard to come by recently, it was good to have a few who could be counted on.

One of the men absently rubbed his arm as he watched the street. A new initiate, marked only two nights ago, the man had proven himself quite well. Akudama doubted if the would ever have trouble with that merchant again.

Akudama tried to remember when he received his first mark, it seemed so long ago. He was young and a bit reckless at the time, but the will to prove himself had been strong. He had almost died proving himself to the council, but it was worth every scar. Akudama had been so proud to wear the snakes that he didnt cover them for weeks. In the years since then, Akudama had been marked many more times, his body a testament to his loyalty and will. He shrugged his shoulders at the though, in his mind he could feel the snakes that covered his back.

Some of the newer initiates scoffed at the old ways, the ancient ways, but it was this past that gave Black Sun its meaning. It was the history, the beginning in an ancient land that filled Akudama with pride and honor. He was part of something that had existed since the dawn of mankind, far away on Earth. The millennia had changed the organization, as time does, but the roots remained the same. Ties to an ancient and honorable past that could never be forgotten.

Approaching footsteps woke Akudama from his reverie. By the mans nervous glances, Akudama guessed he was another messenger and the disdainful manner about him marked the man as on of Rosss boys. Just as well, the last few jobs had been for a small clan in the north, and Akudama was under orders from the council to remain neutral, for now. It didnt really matter though, clan credits spent as easily as Omni ones.

Akudama put on his best smile and turned to greet the man.

Welcome my friend, how can Black Sun Enterprises be of service to you today?
