Boss MOB loot update: June 10
Currently the Boss MOBs are dropping less loot than before 14.2. The reason for this is because of the Boss Modifier item they carry - the Boss Modifier item dictates how much loot they drop, and how powerful they are. Previously, there was a bug that made the Boss Modifier item stack, and this was why Boss MOBs were so powerful. This also meant more loot dropped. When the power level of the Boss MOBs was fixed, and as an unintended side-effect, the loot they dropped also decreased. This leaves us at the point we are at now.

What we are doing now is, as well as creating new loot for Boss MOBs, we're working on increasing the drop of Boss MOBs so that after all the time and effort you put into killing them, you will get more loot.

So, just to sum up, its an issue we are addressing in an effort to get a fix out to you as quick as possible. Thanks for all you concern and input regarding this, and I'll strive to keep you updated on whats happening with the changes.
