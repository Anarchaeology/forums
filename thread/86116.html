NPC buffers
Would it possible to create NPC "buffers" for each of the three sides. These buffers could create implants, armor, or hit people with general proffesion buffs. I know it would hurt the merchants who like to sell their services, but anyone who has had to wait two days to a week for someone with 800NP would appreciate the service.
It's the same with carbo armor, you can find anyone making it up to level 120ish, but then the engie or trader with the ppe has made so much money they stop making it for the general public, and only make it for their guild and friends.
How many times have you asked (begged)a doc for a +80 treatment buff only to have them walk away like you weren't even there.
In order to create balance and to please the cutthroat "merchants" a much larger fee could be charged, say 2k per ql per implant, armor, or buff. This would help people only use these buffers as a last resort.
At least it gives some sort of option.
