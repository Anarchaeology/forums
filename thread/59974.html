The Cavalry Has Arrived!(Omni-Pol/DA Rompa Battle)
Envy walks into Rompa, the bar is silent, a few people talking, making deals, having a pleasant time, but not jumping like it usually is, its silent.....

Envy Sits down to share a few drinks as she talks to Taboo and Altyrna, They share a few words and then she departs to meet a friend in Baboons. Envy begins to make her way towards the door,before she hits the door she notices 2 men standing in front of the door way talking to a man named Eraser, asking about one of their friends.Eraser does not know anything, then they turn to envy.

http://groups.msn.com/RubiKa/funthin...oto&PhotoID=31 -Pic

With a sharp rude attitude, they ask Envy about their friend.(Who shall remain nameless)

"Oh,him, i know what happened to him!" Envy pauses"He got killed by Zaiyne!Upon an arrest attempt, he resisted and was shot!"(Zaiyne being the local Omni-Pol agent around Rompa and Baboons, causing quite a few problems for the locals buisness owners, DA included)

The Men's faces turned red with anger, proclaiming that their boss wont be to happy about this, slamming their fists into the walls, and screaming "The Pols Will pay for this!" loudly echoing through the bar.

"Hmmm"Envy thinks to herself "this is a intersting twist of fate, maybe these guys can have some use, perhaps their stupidity will pay off in our favor..."

"Come boys we have some things to dicuss" Envy says, pulling on Vinnie "Suitster" Shunter's Arm. They both agree to follow and Tony "Roberto" Weston follows closely behind.

The men and Envy sit down at a booth close by and begin to talk about Zaiyne and his attempts to Ruin Devil's Advocate, taking Baboons from them.They all agree, that Omni-Pol must pay for their actions! and begin to chant it across the bar, DA members begin to gather, providing back up for the assualt on Omni-Pol with help from these men. Suddenly a whole platoon of Omni-Pol Agents is standing right in front of US!


http://groups.msn.com/RubiKa/funthin...oto&PhotoID=32 -Pic

The mob quickly calls to arms, and grabs whatever weapons they can find as the agents begin to cuff and drag out the men.


As we near the door Envy notices the men grabbing their guns from their pockets, and as soon as we get outside the men open fire!

The members of DA looked stunned for a moment, and then begin to back up their new found friends, as they promised not even 10 minutes ago!

Taboo opens fire with blasts from his X-3, Altyrna balls her fists, and begins a brutal assault of fists upon the Omni-Pol agent! As envy quickly grabs her gun and opens fire aiming perfectly.

The men and the DA members fight long and hard, with a massive battle of Fire power flying around outside of Rompa Bar,But the battle proves to be to much for the men, and they fall to the ground.The Members of Da hold their place, and continue to battle the Agents, as one after the other, after the other fall to the ground.

http://groups.msn.com/RubiKa/funthin...oto&PhotoID=33 -pic

Finally one Agent is left, She begins to retreat, running at a rapid rate, flying through Omni-Ent, The band of friends try to follow, but with no luck....They run past BB and around the arena, never gaining enough ground to let out a shot. Then the agent was no longer in sight.

The Members of DA group back together to reflect upon the battle, hopefully they have made some new allies....but they have made even more enemies......
