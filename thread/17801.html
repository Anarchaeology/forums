Extremely simple suggestion: change the shopping channels...
Lots of players complain (and with good reason) that there's no market for low-lvl/newbie items.
Part of the problem is that the cash income increases immensely in just 10 levels or so.
The lowbie shopping channel is in a very big range (1-50) which makes the theoretical price range from a few hundred to several hundred thousand credits.

Solution? Make more shopping channels. Make an ultra-low-lvl channel (1-10) where newbies can exchange weapons, armor and the like, and continue with a 10-30 and a 30-50.

Or, alternatively, make the channels based on price, not level. For instance the (in)famous concrete cushions lvl 10-12 sell for an absurd amount and belong more in a high-lvl than in a low-lvl channel. Also bidders would have an idea of the seller's intended price, twinks could get their gear on one channel and real newbies on another. Simple, right?

I wish to stress that all chars should have a theoretical access to all channels within a reasonable range, regardless of level and wealth. That way, the newbie could be on the lookout for armor on a low-value channel and still sell his/her newly acquired anima nano on another channel.

Cheap, easy to implement and fair to all. Not even the PvPers can complain.

What do you think, guys?
