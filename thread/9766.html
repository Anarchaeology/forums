You can add to your friends list by typing:
/cc addbuddy <name>
You can remove by typing:
/cc rembuddy <name>
You can remove all the people you don't know by typing:
/cc rembuddy ?

To change channels you need *not* type the enitire name:
/ch t <- Team chat
/ch v <- Vicinity
/ch <first letter or 2 of guild>
/ch <first letter or 2 of private chat>
etc...

Hope some of that helped, not like it's immediately apparent when you first load this game or anything, took me months and countless questions to figure out what the heck I was doing, hehe.
