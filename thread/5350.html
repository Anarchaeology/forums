Oooookkk - Im soo gonna get flamed for this but:

AO is roleplay, right? The first roleplay was D&D (historians i might be wrong) and most roleplay games take some form of influenece from the original.

Now - D&D had very few class balance problems (in my experience), especially as they had no public beta testing like we have now.

A big part of the system was the class restrictions on weapons and equipment. Clerics could use blunt weapons, fighters anything, mages only small weapons. Mages, thieves etc had armour restrictions. As part of the balance classes also had distinct advantages with what they could do.

The problem with balance in AO is that there are no hard-cheese, thats life restrictions. Only hinderences. My 45 NT could have maxed shotgun and overequipped to use the same shotgun as any other prof at 45. I also get the best nukes.

PLEASE dont get me wrong - Im not saying nerf NTs - check the Pvp to see how we compare to other profs.

My point is perhaps NTs should have to live with limited weapon choice, enforcers with weaker nanos, agents with less armour, and so on. One disadvantage for one advantage - thus a 'balance'. At last..

Oh - and if Funcom takes this out of concept and nerfs us all its not my fault... Im just exploring the idea.
