Hi, I just reactivated my account!
And all I can say is W O W !!! I can't believe this is the same game I (we) all tried playing at launch. One thing I have noticed today though is the lack of people. I have been running around Tir and the Athens cities area. There are only a few people around here and there

I went to west Athens, the newbie starting place, west of the main city. I remember at launch standing outside by that sign with a couple of severed heads hanging from it, people were all over the place. Everyone was standing around the sign teaminmg up and going to the beach or somewhere. Same thing in East and West Athens too. Most people were hanging out by the whompa it seemed. Outside in the junk yard... no one around, no one at the missions, nobody nowhere.

Is something common now? I hope not, cause these places used to be booming!

I fired up AO because I heard how great it plays now. I'll tell you what, it's true. It runs great, I love AO, it's a very engrossing game, and it's fun. I just really hope people pick it up,or come back or try it out like I did. Because I'm staying with it now. The issues I had with AO have been adressed, and I'm enjoying it now. I just hope more people come back, I want to team up again and see people. Glad I'm back though

quote:
Originally posted by reality
AO has reached the point where all the best levelling spots have been found, and that's where most players are hanging around these days.
Well, there's actually a couple factors to this.

First, a lot of the low leveling areas were found by Funcom and nerfed. (Temple? nerfed. OT camps in VW? nerfed. Tooth? nerfed. etc.....)

So then people started finding new areas and hang out there. Plus Funcom has added outposts into the game which tends to spread out parties looking for groups. After launch, you could barely walk at the Tir West OP because of the lag from the 50+ people that would be standing there. Now most of the time I walk by there, there isn't a single person.

But also there are a lot of high levelers now, so they congregate at the areas for the higher levels. Now if you got to Home in Broken Shores, I frequently lose team members due to Link Dead trying to zone into Home.

But basically, find some people to hang with, and you'll eventually find the new hunting spots. I came back at the end of January and it took me about a month to get back into the flow of things, but now I have to tell people "No I can't team, I'm already in one" frequently.
