Calling all newbies.
During the next few weeks i will be holding a series of contests in the great city of Omni-1 Trade. The reward for winning these contests will range from 1 million to 5 million credits. The events will not have set times. So keep your eyes tuned to the shopping channel (1-50) for information.

To be eligible:
You must be under level 50.

The next event will be a race. The reward will be 2 million credits to the first player under 50. 1 million to the first player under 30. 500k to the first person under 15.
No vehicles will be allowed.
the objective will be to run from the southwestern tip of trade to the southeastern tip.

Clanners and neuts, of course, are welcome to come, but beware as there are guards in the race path. Any clanner that wins will recieve a special bonus due to the increased peril.

Good Luck to all,
