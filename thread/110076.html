An Enemy Within! Nanomages beware of...
An Enemy Within! Nanomages beware of... Hunter "Buglight" Locknane.

He is a traitor to the Nanomage people! I thought at first he was brainwashed, but that does not appear to be the case. It was Buglight who tricked me into joining the clans. He was and is "The Voice" in my head. Somehow, someway, he implanted a communication device within me. At first it seemed it was just to help me discover the truth about my past. But that all changed the day Buglight left Whisper's Edge.

While I was settling in to my new guild, a great event took place. My former guild, The Nanomage Liberation Front, led by Queen Fleshreaper and Desert Winds, led by Major "Naxilite" McCovery presented the Borealis Pact to the citizens of Rubi-Ka. This was a great day for all citizens of Rubi-Ka and the Nanomage People. I invited Buglight to join me at the ceremony. While there, I explained the BORPACT to Buglight and he agreed that the BORPACT was what was best for Rubi-Ka. So he and I hatched a plan to spread the word of the Borealis Pact within the clans.

The day we were going to begin our crusade for the BORPACT is the day Buglight left Whisper's Edge. It puzzled me why he left, but soon after he did, "The Voice" returned, and it all became so very clear to me!

"The Voice" was attacking me! The thing is, I know who the voice is?!?! Who is he trying to fool? Buglight was trying to fill my head with so much "truth", as he called it, I could barely stay alive. Thank goodness my insurance is strong.

Buglight is a LIAR!! Everything we talked about was a LIE! He does not believe in the BORPACT! He does not care about this planet or the Nanomage people!

All Nanomages beware of the UNTRUSTWORTHY Hunter "Buglight" Locknane.
