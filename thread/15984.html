A point concerning PvP/Trader/NT balance and 14.2+
If Funcom would implement an incentive for team based PvP, none of this "Trader will 0\/\/nz0r my NT" and "Trader will kill me this way" etc. etc. would be relevant... if we have an incentive to actually PvP en masse...

Scenario for you.

Trader out there. Sees NT. Trader ransacks/divests/swallowsthesoul of the NT they're facing. So the Enforcer with the NT rushes the Trader. The Fixer (yours truly) promptly removes like 319251051539 from the trader's run speed. The Agent in the party gets off their aimed shot at the trader and then swears a bit about how they still need loving. All the while, the Engineer is running in circles, watching their pet in /pet behind mode, hurling expletives around about how he can't be leet anymore...

And when that trader's gone, the hordes of other Omni people holding the outpost rush out and are slaughtered too or something, but regardless, point is, if you have a team, there's tactics. There's thinking. There's assisting. There's thought and reaction. There's a way to circumvent the fact that 1 on 1, not all classes can stand toe to toe against each other in PvP. Can an MA stand up to a Fixer? Well, um, they'll live, but how on earth would they propose to kill the fixer? Debuff el' run speed, fixer flies away. Does that mean they should nerf fixers?

Let the traders take out the NT's, but we need our incentive to PvP on large scale (not to mention server stability that can support that, but that's another story entirely). I personally feel like the ability to capture and hold territory that Funcom is working on implementing at some point in the relatively near future is what is going to add that incentive, so perhaps we should all just cool off for a bit, and NT's should just try to avoid Traders in PvP unless they have a group, no?
