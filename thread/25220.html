What I want to see for OE changes.
Over equipping should be a matter of efficiency. There is no reason why I as a level 50 doctor should not be able to equip some level 200 armor. However, my doctor should not know how to use this armor to its full efficiency, and therefore my own lack of skill is what drops the armors protection. It should operate just slightly better than armor that I could equip myself.

The armor should of course have higher AC's than a QL of armor I myself could meet the requirements for because the armor itself is better. This would solve a problem of having to buy new armor every 5 levels to keep up with mob difficulty, people could purchase armor 20 QL's too high for themselves and grow into it, and it would always offer the best protection for them.

Second of all, you should not have to buff into this higher QL armor. There is no reason armor should not physically fit your body because you dont have enough agility or psychic powers. This way social armor would be an option for those who do not fit requirements for their armor.

Lastly, I find it an idiotic concept for it to be easier to use a Rusty Disrepaired gun than a Flawlessly perfect gun. Honestly, it should be the otherway around, perfect guns should be used at level 1 and tweaked out modified guns should be available at level 200, that only offer the same accuracy and more damage if you have the skill to use it.

But without calling for a renaming of all weapons and insanely drastic changes the only I can ask is that the same model be used for weapons as armor, that you can always equip it however its usefullness is only as good as their own skill, and perhaps slightly more damaging because of the quality of the weapon.

The next thing I'd ask for it Title Caps on armor. Put title caps on all weapons and armor so no level 1 will be running around with a level 200 weapon or armor. The title caps should be very high however....Levels 1-14 you can wear up to QL49 armor Levels 15-50 you can wear levels 1-99 armor, levels 51-99 you can wear up to QL 149 armor etc.

This way there are still goals for people to get equipment at level 150.... "At level 150 I can finally equip my level 200 armor! Yeehaw" The same could followsuit for weapons...that way no level 80 has a QL 200 weapon.

This way, OE doesnt kill customers, PvP is more balanced... Nano based professions are bit more on the same playing field as weapon based professions.

It would also be kinda nice if Nano based professions could cast nanos higher than their level with a chance to misfire. You play with fire, you might just get burned. IE you may hit yourself with that OE nuke, IE you may cause damage with that OE heal. IE you may just debuff your agility if you try to OE a Feline Grace.

This would add an element of surprise / danger to making hasty decisions in trying to save your party with a new nuke you arent quite skilled enough to use etc. This is just a frivolous little idea, but no less amusing to think about.

I like this idea very much. In fact, I had a similar one myself.

First, equipment requirement should be split into two categories: "Equip" and "Use" requirements. E.g an implant could have treatment as "equip" requirement, because it takes a rather competent surgeon to insert one. Once it's in, treatment should no longer be a requirement. Similar with armor; require a little more agility to fit into it, once it's on, lower the requirement a bit. For weapons, the "Equip" requirements could be lower than "Use" requirements, perhaps only a title level requirement.

Next, introduce "Effective Quality Level" (EQL). This is calculated from the "Use" requirements. Suppose I have 95% of required Rifle skill and 120% of Aimed Shot skill. EQL would then be 95% of actual QL. This is the skill cap. If all requirement are fully met, the item would be QL capped to EQL=QL.

In order to avoid unstable EQL calculations, negative item effects (debuffs) should not be affected by EQL, but rather remain at same value as a full QL object.

The nice thing about this is that certain buffs would have quadratic effect. For a weapon, the EQL (and thus weapon damage) is raised, as is damage bonus from skill.
