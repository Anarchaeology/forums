Missmaul dead-dead, this can't be right... but if there is any truth in this, any real factual evidence, then someone was going to find themselves introduced to 'Wyldthing's 1000 Cuts of Pain'. Entering the code, she switches out the items in her backpack and slips into her armor. Only then does she free her Sol Fires from their cases and grip the handles lovingly.

"Someone is going to pay for this," she mutters, sealing her account again. "Someone it going to wish they had not been hatched from breeding tube."

Jumping into her yalm, she promptly runs it into the bank terminal, then takes a moment to regain her control before zipping off into the sky toward the wompahs. Where was Xtrophic? He could nuke the son-of-a-beech into oblivion and back again, but then that might be too good for this "Katrienae" person. A Clanner... a fargin Clanner did this. As if the war wasn't enough, now innocent Omni-tek personnel were being killed by wacked out Clanners.

Through the wompah and into Newland, where the city seemed to be buzzing with life. There were always people coming and going from Newland, off on some mission or another, oblivious to the world around them. Swerving to avoid an on coming Atrox, Wyldthing took to the skies again. Her mind raced almost as much as her heart, the anger and frustration at the death of a friend, a fellow Adventurer, and a truly good person was almost more then her sanity could bare. For months she had kept the 'wild' inside, just like her foster-mother had taught her... but now was the time to release it, to be the Wyldthing that had earned her that name.

"You better watch yourself Katrienae," she hissed through clenched teeth. "Death comes on swift wings, and he tonight he whispers your name!"

He opened up his Commlink to check for messages. None. Damn it. This could be a bad thing. It could also mean that he wasn't needed. He reached the bronto burger and placed his order. He tapped out the Bronto Burger theme on the table top as he waited. Noticing the chef giving him a dirty look, he stopped and turned around. He glanced around the lot at all the people. Most were offering buffs at cost. He snickered and flicked his comm to the shopping channel. This was a favorite passtime of his. He enjoyed seeing all the valuable merchandise going for SO cheap! Silly people! He thought.

"Orders up! That will be 100 credits sir."

The chef sighed and pointed to the sign on the wall

quote:
Due to animal rights issues, the price of the Bronto meat has doubled. Therefore,there will be an increase in our burger prices. We are sorry for the inconvenience. - Omni-Food Service.
Siegemode sighed and pulled out his credit book. He payed the man and grudgingly turned around. He mummbled a few curses as he walked away. He was just about to enter the grid when a yalm came flying out and nearly ran him down!
