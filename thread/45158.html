Pathing the right way when following
AO has always had an on-follow-pathing problem with pets (and also for one player following another, if you are AFK in a mission).

The problem seems to be that the following char/pet trys to follow the leader in a direct path. This is fine in open outdoor areas, but take it inside and you got problems.

If the leader makes a turn around an obstacle (ie. a table, don`t we just loves those), or turns through a doorway, then the follower will try to run trough the object/wall.

Now the right way to do follow pathing, is for the follower to go on the PATH the leader took. This is an indirect route to the leader, but we know this route to be clear,therefore the following char/pet will not get stuck when in follow mode.

I find this to be a very simple solution to follow-pathing, and from programing experience I know this is fairly easy to code.
