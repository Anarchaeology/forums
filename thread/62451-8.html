I guess I could post some thoughts on all this...

First of all, I disagree that there is such a thing as "ninja looting". The programmers made the game such that certain people are allowed to loot a monster; so, if a person does the most damage to a monster, then he gets it. Or, if the team's loot is set to team loot leader, or alpha, etc., then whoever gets the loot then gets it. The problem with the Tarasque is that more than one team competes over the loot; however, according to the game's design, it is fair for the team that does the most damage to get the loot. If a "damage team" states their intentions beforehand, wins, and decides to take the loot for themselves, that's ok. Because they choose to do this, however, this means that there is no reason the other 20+ people have to include them in their big loot roll, if they are doing that. If people want to have a loot system they feel is fair, then all they need to do is play along the rules of the game, and I'm confident that something can be worked out in the end. We can also try to change it by asking the devs to change the programming; this has already been changed a bit by the implementation of loot messages in 14.6. One point I'm trying to make is this: 50 or so people kill the Tarasque every day. Say 44 of those people agree to use normal lottery rolls, while the other 6 do a damage team type setup. That's fine-if the 44 don't like it, they can form their own damage team to ensure that the 44 get it. This will most likely lead to the 44 (or however many happen to be there) winning the loot every, or almost every, time; it seems reasonable that the people that form damage teams outside of the big roll would then choose to be in the big roll again. But they don't have to. It's just part of the game rules.

The Tarasque is different from the other unique monsters in the game, in my opinion. First of all, it can't be soloed by one person, or even one team, as many uniques can. This means that loot must be decided among more than one team. Second, the loot is quite simply the best in the game, more or less. Everyone wants it.
