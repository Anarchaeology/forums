If we can learn one thing from Ultima Online
It is that any limitation on PvP ruins the game.
Before the trammel patch, when i first started playing UO, my heart used to pound at the sight of red pks, id trip up over myself trying to get away, as if paralysed by fear.
That was a great feeling, groups of people would band together to fight the pks, in most cases just chasing them off, but the experience was one of the best I had in UO.
When there was only one facet, that facet was busy, because there was no advantage of doing something elseware, everyone did it all on the same place. If you wantede to hunt in dungeons, you had to go with your guild or face the danger of being PKed.
THen came this god aweful trammel patch, soon the first and PvP facet (Fellucia) was deserted, with virtual tumbleweed flying past what was once a busy bartering bank, gone were the spams of selling magic broad - only 10k!
the folks went to trammel, where you could only die to AI monsters, as a result people died less, this resulted in hyper inflation as people would log on and just kill "liches" etc for GOLD, with no fear of beeing pk'd or looted.
The pks were useless with no one to pk and it all de generated into a farce, in fellucia you couldnt find someone to FIGHT, let alone hunt with.
O$I then introduced the faction system, which did bring some people back to fellucia, but its never been the same since the patch, it also mega increased the number of "KeWL DeWDZ" who made their mouth free from comebacks. IF someone insulted your honour in the time period UO in Loosly set around, you would Duel them, or murder them, in this trammel they wrere completly safe.

In summary, if you limit PvP you limit the game, I for one play MMORPGS because they are so OPEN ended, limiting this defeats the whole point of the game?

If you read this far you musta played UO :P
