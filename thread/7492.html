The article in question!
Omni-Tek, Council of Truth Release Brief Statements
Rubi-Ka Headline Services
January 16. 29476  17:00 RST

The Omni-Tek Corporation and the Council of Truth have both released brief statements regarding the current situation. Philip Ross and Henry Radiman are rumoured to make additional statements either this evening or tomorrow morning, and both parties have promised to keep the media updated on any new developments in the hours and days to come.

The following is a statement from the Omni-Tek Corporation:

"We have evaluated the current situation and come to the conclusion that, for the time being, it does not benefit the Omni-Tek Corporation, nor its local subsidiary, the Omni-Tek Corporation of Rubi-Ka, to continue negotiations with the Council of Truth.

"The clan-related terrorist acts of December 19th have placed the future relationship between Omni-Tek and the Council in serious jeopardy, and the Council's obvious unwillingness to offer their full cooperation in the month-long investigation has contributed to this unfortunate severing of ties.

"The Omni-Tek Corporation is more than willing to resume talks if and when the Council makes a reasonable effort to bring the terrorists to justice in a manner consistent with the Tir Accord. If the Council continues their policy of non-cooperation, however, Omni-Tek may have no choice but to terminate the amnesty.

"We do not wish for the situation to escalate and turn into another civil war. But the Omni-Tek Corporation has a responsibility to its employees and to the galactic economy, and the corporation cannot and will not yield to the lawlessness of terrorists."

The Council of Truth has also released a brief statement:

"We are disappointed and heartbroken over Omni-Tek's decision to terminate our talks. The Council feels that there has been a steady progress in discussions relating to the political and territorial future of Rubi-Ka. To put the negotiations on halt at such a crucial juncture - when we are faced with a new threat to our civil security -is a mistake that may result in renewed violence. We are more than willing to assist Omni-Tek in their investigations into suspected terrorism, but we do not wish for this situation to turn into a witch-hunt against clan-members."

There are already talks of increased violence in locations across Rubi-Ka, and we will continue to follow this story closely.
