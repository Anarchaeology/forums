you can get the parts from a lot of places, the bots around the trashking spawn drop very frequently, Tir you mentioned, if you are sick of dogs, go to lush resort, you can kill either the bucket-heads, the gladiator bot type Pod or the slayers for quest parts if you are bored with grey mobs.

Other spots, the mine in TLR, the ninjas in PM, the a-1000s and a-4000s in clon**** and SFH. The slayers in EFP if you are ambitious, but be careful those monkies bite. The numerous bucket heads around wartorn valley, the helpers and bucket heads in newland near GJ... I can think of a ton more.

People kill the dogs and the waste collectors because there are a lot of them and they don't put up a fight, but sadly since they are the easiest mobs you can fight, they infrequently drop. I find a lot of omni players very lazy. Getting to Tir at any level over 25 is a cake walk even without a yalm, you can just walk there from newland or you could go to the trashking spawn in Cobal Sands. And yes I am an Omni player.
