You can even take it further and use strafe and turn simultaneously. This will make you roll around in circles.
And if you really want to annoy people: use the shift key to make it "permanent" (ie, you don't have to hold the keys to keep the movement going). I think the sequence is shift_down, <key>_down, shift_up, <key>_up, but I'm not quite sure. Anyway, you can do this with both strafe and turn to make you roll around permanently without having to hold down the keys.

When I'm afk and in leet form, I often do this in crowded areas as my contribution to general merriment in the area.
