Right, im back...for a while (had cancelled my account till exams were over).
Ive been keeping an eye on the soldier forums when i could and im most appauled by FC's vision of the soldier.
Dscaper, Sredni and Obergeist have been making very very valid posts and have contributed to the soldier profession quite a bit, but FC have just spat in their faces along with everyone one else.

To sum up the soldier profession, at low levels our nanos are either too weak or we dont have the ip to spare for them and our weapons are mediocre at best with nothing special over other professions weapons.
At high levels our nanos aren't worth the ip except for tms which is comparable to mongo yet enforcers also have essence etc to complement it. Our weapons are used by everyone and their dog, lets face it, ip at high levels is rediculous, not only do other professions have everything their profession has to offer by lvl 140 but they also have a ton of ip to waste on dark blue skills leading to every profession being as combat capable as everyone else with comparable evades, the same armour and the same weapons.

Now to actually focus on the high level game:
Tanking: Soldier does NOT excel at this, for 1min - 1min 20 secs maybe we do, but thats only 1/3 of the time, that other 2/3 of the time we are as vulnerable as everyone elsewith maybe an extra 200 - 300 hp which is about 1 extra hit. Meanwhile the class we are supposed to equal can tank consistantly for almost limitless periods of time.

Damage: Soldier is pitiful at this, currently i have sacrificed tms mk X for a self buffed 966 attack rating and a damage range from 1 - 1165, 3/5 of the time damage is in the bottom half of the range.
Other professions use the same gun maybe with an attack rating of 650 or so with a damage range of 1 - 900, they are still doing similar damage because of the extreme damage range, plus on top they have nukes, roots and pets which makes tms look pitiful (a slayer in 1 min 20 sec can do a LOT more damage than a soldier in the same amount of time and can tank a LOT longer).

Range: This does not make up for damage, an extra hit in the time it takes for a mob to be in your face doesnt make up for a lower minimum damage and an inconsistancy in damage output.

The soldier is a mediocre class at best with no little shine or diversity to set us apart from other professions, nanos are unimaginitive with higher reqs for nanos that give less benefits than similar nanos that other professions have.

I could post more, maybe later, but the soldier profession is dead, FC just doesnt realise it and most of us are too hopeful to accept it. Oh well, i'll bide my time till SWG comes out, hope to see all of you there
