Therion is correct. An exploit is anything that you do which the game designers did not intend for you to be able to do. Sometimes, exploits are obvious. For example, if you have to lag your client to get the same exact mission three times, you should realize that that is an exploit.

In the case of mission running, only the developers can say whether it is an alternative strategy or an exploit. I have yet to see a developer chime in on this issue, and I don't expect to hear anything from a developer until if/when they issue a patch to change this.

Unless you have actually communicated with a developer on this issue, you're only speculating.

The game is touted as having other ways to develop your character besides combat. I really haven't seen too many of them personally, but this certainly seems like one. Maybe my perspective is warped, but it is not obvious to me that the developers intended for you to clear the entire level every time you do a mission (except for the missions that say that, of course).

In fact, since some mission descriptions ask you to kill everyone, and some do not, I think it quite reasonable to assume that you do not have to kill everyone to complete the latter.

Now, if you accept a mission that says "kill everyone" and you don't...that's obviously an exploit.
