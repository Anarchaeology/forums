April 18: Clan Celebration
April 18, 29191 - Omni-Tek signs an agreement with the new Omni-Tek Manual Labourer's Guild, protecting the workers' rights. The groundwork is laid for the rise of the clans.

In honor of this occasion, we are going to be arranging a large gathering of Clan members on April 18th (also known as Thursday) at 2000 GMT.

Tentatively, we will simply gather outside of Tir West Gate and mix, mingle, dance, celebrate, cheer, and light off some fireworks on some innocent critters.

I suggest leaving Tir through South Gate to avoid zoning lag/crashes, should enough people show up.

Anyhow, just pop on by, socialize, dance, and enjoy what is basically a large outdoor picnic celebrating the day the fight for freedom began.
