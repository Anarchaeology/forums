OT 12 is huuuge. Looks like a cannon too. Then again the Arwen 404 looks like a pea shooter.

If you are using a Cowbell looking thing, chances are you need to switch anyways. There are 3 decent grenade weaons in the game:

1. OT 12
2. Arwen 404
3. Chocolate suprise

OT 12: Big and evil looking. When it fires it makes a chest explosing noise, very amusing in missions. Also a 10 round clip. Grenade/HW, fire damage. Goves bonuses to 1 trade.

Arwen 404: Does almost twice the damage of the OT 12. Looks gimpy, has the OT Quartz model. One round clip, slower firing rate by 1sec chem damage. Give bonuses to 3 trades.

Chocolate Suprise: It's a muffin. Yessir a chocolate muffin. Very heavy damage (almost 1k at 200) Bonuses to HP. VERY SLOW FIRING RATE. Were talking a 10 second cycle here. Does not use ammo. Dual weildable (very high). Brawl as special attack. Largest bonus: No one expects to be damaged by a frickin muffin. Fire damage.
