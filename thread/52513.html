TirDrops for the Clans
The glow lamps burned on in the early morning when the farmers and miners were stirring in Tir County west of the City.

Dr. Cifuentes sat hunched at her makeshift desk in her makeshift lab, a squalid stone building devoid of windows and decent ventilation. She had spent the night visiting the stabilized patients for the third time since administering her ad hoc pneumonia antibiotic and taking notes. Now though, she was listening to the grid feed on news of troops moving into Tir.

The Clan guards had left the town possibly to protect the Council members return to their families, and the ICC Peacekeepers were already moving to fill the vaccuum, probably with Omni watching every move.

It was no longer safe for her to be in Tir without the possibility of being arrested or investigated by OmniPol for her activities and breach of contract with OmniMed. She would give the pharmacist next door a special communications signal if she was needed again, otherwise he should be able to see that the drugs were administered correctly and the patients recover as expected.

She began to pack up her vials, her bio communicators, her DNA libraries, mirco array slides, sense plates and all the samples she had of Enigma bark and the pollen.

Being a Clanner had made her vulnerable, but an independent Clanner in the current atmosphere was just an open invitation for a questioning, or worse, a detention. With the loss of the Tir Accord she had no advocates or protection she could count on.

She closed the container for the biologicals and wondered if her application to the Mercury Dragons offering them her skills as geneticist and medic would be accepted. She was feeling like a leet in a backyard training area.

Leaving the small shelter that had been her place for 3 weeks she stopped by the pharmacist and gave him her instructions with a small amount of medicine should anyone else fall sick. She listened to his comments on the situation telling her the ICC peacekeepers were already outside Tir's Gates and commenting when Omni would follow.

With a grim nod she took her leave sending the nanites forth to course through her body and encode her cells to digital media for grid transition. Athens would still be safe for awhile...
