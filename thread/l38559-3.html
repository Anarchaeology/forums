As pointed out, mission running is not an exploit.
For some characters, like Agents, mission running in a more discreet form is totally in character.

However, for Enforcers and Soldiers, I believe mission running can be pointless. They have enough HP to fight anything in a normal mission without dying, and with the use of a Treatment Lab, they can wipe out the mmonster content of the mission, with few problems. By doing so, you greatly increase your EXP and Cr without having to hunt for a site with mobs that are suitable for your level.

For example, I have recently had to restart my soldier (for reasons that I will not go into now), and at Level 5 I decided to start missioning. I selected 3 missions, one was a find item, another find person, and another kill person.
They were all located in Athen (which was where I had selected the mission), as is common among low level characters.
The total reward for all three missions, with cr/exp gauge in the middle was ~500cr and 200 EXP

By killing the mobs, which in total probably added another 40 minutes onto my playing time, I received a total of ~4000 EXP and ~750 cr. Also, I found over 40 items on bodies and in chests, which were all relatively useful, and could all be sold, in total for 3000-4000 cr. Not bad for an extra 40 minutes work.

As you see, mission running is obviously not an exploit, as it can make you LOSE out in some cases, and in other cases, it is totally in character.
