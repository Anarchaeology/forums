My opinion on charm is that the psy check is not necessary. If anything, it should be a resistance check modified possibly by the target's psychic score... not just a flat yes/no.

I agree that there should be a limit on what you can charm (a lowbie crat should not be able to run out and charm a lvl 100 mob) but it should still be possible to charm something that's w/i 10-15 levels... even if the chance is reduced.

If the psy check was indeed added because resists didn't work, and they have indeed made significant improvements to mob resist functionality... then they should be removed. Simple as that to say, but I'm sure it's going to take quite a bit of coaxing for FC to change it... just because the psy component is what we're all "used to" now... and changing it might create a sudden balance shift, even if it's a positive change, IMHO.
