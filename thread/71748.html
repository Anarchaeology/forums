Yeah, Cz's job is tough. But by in large it is tough because Funcom's policies make it tough. A good chunk of the complaints arise because people don't understand how these changes fit into the complete picture. Why not? Because Funcom doesn't tell you what the complete picture is.

The thing with using Riposte for the MA root-breaker is a good example. Out of the blue riposte is now important for MAs. Some hints that Riposte will be useful for other things some vague time in the future have been alluded to. But what they are, and when they will come is anyone's guess.

So now MAs are going to have to somehow redistribute their IP to make use of this important feature. Is it worth it? Unless you understand what else riposte will be used for, you can't make a decision except on the basis of the one known ability. Obviously, people think the choice is a harsh one.

Also, there are other pieces that are missing; being unable to break roots was not the only problem MAs faced. Are these other problems going to be addressed? Or is this root-breaker seen as solving the main MA problems? Nobody has any idea. So they have to assume the worst, that is, assume the present situation is seen by Funcom as a solution of sorts, because the present is all people have got to work with.

In short, if Funcom wants to cut down on the complaints and flaming and whining, then they had better make their comprehensive plans of profession development clear, so people can deal with the facts, and not indulge in negative thinking. As they haven't, and don't seem to have any intent of doing so, they have created their own pit of vipers. They could clear most of it out of they wanted to, but they don't, so I have no pity for them at all.

It makes perfect sense.
To understand why the skill requires Riposte, you have to understand what riposte is. without bringing up the obvious fencing or real life martial arts references, the basic definition of riposte is turning an attacker's attack back on them. They thrust, you parry and return their attack back on them using their own momentum.

In the case of waves of summer, you're reversing the root and adding your own damage and stun onto it. If that's not a riposte, I don't know what is. It's one of the most brilliant things they've done and I hope to see the riposte skill used in regular attacks, now that the parry skill works.

Couple of things:

Range - I'm sure this hasn't been decided, but the only way to make it work is to have the range the same as the caster's nanoprogram. You are, after all, throwing the nano back at the attackers. This is REALLY going to make someone think twice about carelessly trying to root an MA.

Skill checks - I'm assuming that the chance for success will be a roll between your riposte skill vs. the nanoskill of the opponent vs. their nanoskill. If you'r riposte is high, you have a better chance of reversing the nano attack. If their nano resist is high, then they have a chance of resisting the riposted nano attack and you're back to square one

Skill lock duration - Only way this is going to be decent is if there is a zero - five second lock on riposte skill. Something silly like 40 seconds is only going to destroy this excellent idea and will result in you getting rooted on the second try. If the attacker can re-root in five seconds, I should have a chance to re-riposte in five seconds too.

this is a step in the right direction, but in no way is it a lifesaver for the MA profession. I will welcome this move with open arms and give Funcom ALOT of respect for not waiting a year to add it though.

Damage wise, without our crits we're hurting. I can see why Funcom wants fights to last longer and doesn't want us to base our victories 100% on crits. Okay. Suggestions.

1) Raise base damage on fists. Not a whole lot, but some. Lemme rephrase that....I'm not looking for each punch to hit as hard as a beam, but I'd like a bit more kick than 100 damage per hit with 1120 MA skill. Just a little bitty bit when you take my other ideas into consideration.

2) make dimach recharge MUCH quicker (The devs were already thinking about this), based on dimach skill and maybe initiatives. Higher aggression = faster dimach recharge yet you lose out on evades. Risk vs. reward

3) make brawl recharge dependent on initiative, just like burst.

4) MA attacks are very low powered damage wise, especially in pvp, but they have some damned cool modifiers (since the changes). Again, BRILLIANT ideas on the new MA attack changes. SOMEbody there has their thinking cap on! But to add on to those changes, make the different types of MA attacks lock a different skill. Shen and bwt will lock dimach, attack of the snake and BoP will lock MA, bright blue cloudless sky, Angel of Night and Flower of life, I dunno. Make it lock something, maybe first aid and treatment? Actually that's perfect.

5) Make MA attacks useable by all MA weapons that require 66% or more MA skill to use.

All of these attacks are pretty sorry damage-wise if you look at each individual attack, but the whole key is the chaining up of attacks REALLY fast that equals great overall damage.

None of these proposed suggestions overpowers the MA, but it adds so much variety to our attacks and would make the MA ALOT ALOT of fun to pvp with. Versus professions like fixers and docs, we would still have one hell of a time competing, but we'd be on par with an enforcer or melee adventurer.

Variety does not make a class overpowered or funcom's love child, it makes the profession fun and restores balance. MAs were balanced before the patch, but I'd be willing to give admit that maybe we were balanced for the wrong reasons?

they want longer matches because longer matches in pvp are alot more fun. Nobody wants to step onto the battlefield and get owned in three seconds only to have to wait six minutes before stepping back into the ring and getting owned again. That's the frustration of pvp. Making fights last longer gives damage over time professions like the MP, NT, ADV and fixer a better chance.

It still is rock, scissors, paper and I think I'm actually starting to see where the devs are coming from again. Gaute admitted that the crit changes hit the MAs harder than other professions and I think they WILL try to make amends, but we have to give some realistic suggestions.

do these ideas sound reasonable to anyone? Man, I really do hope Cz or someone from the dev team reads this post!!!
