Hi all,

I have had similar experiences. The fact of the matter is that roleplayers are a small percentage of the community. Still, most are respectful enough to allow you to enjoy roleplaying without being disruptive. Don't give up! Believe in what you are doing.

I've found in some cases a private tell is the most effective way to handle disruptive players. If you confront a jerk in vicinity its not going to get you anywhere, because of pride. if you send someone a compelling tell they can back down without losing face. My tells are polite as possible while still cleverly trying to make the person understand what a jerk they are being.

If worse comes to worse, you just have to ignore these people. They will get bored and go away. This happened to me just the other day when I had a group of people gathered in West Athen for an apartment decoration contest. Someone came up, started saying stupid things and mooning me. I just continued doing what I was doing, and eventually he went away.
