Press Release: Free Journalists of Rubi-Ka
Tir City, 2nd April, 29477.

In Tir City today, the management of the new "Free Journalists of Rubi-Ka" publication convened a small press conference to announce they would be "doing their part in the struggle against the oppressive Omni-Tek regime that has inflicted immeasurable pain and suffering on the peoples of our beautiful planet."

Editor in Chief "Kozzie" explained the rationale behind the move. "It's quite simple, people don't know what they're fighting for, and don't realize just how much our actions against Omni-Tek are hurting them."

"Our team of journalists will be roaming the planet and digging up the dirt on these heartless people". When asked how a publication could make any difference to the apparent stalemate between the Clans and Omni-Tek, Managing Editor "Kruickvich" explained that, "We're going to show people the truth. Every day we're wearing down the oppressors, but the average person in the street often doesn't realize just what's going on. By bringing people news on these important events as they happen, we hope to encourage the various leaders pull together and unite against the evil corporation."

Asked how they could ever hope to encourage unity amongst such a wide group with differing beliefs, the Managing Editor rose slowly, smiled an almost toothless grin, and purposefully strode out of the room, saying "We'll see..."

The Free Journalists of Rubi-Ka has been established through an agreement with the ICC to bring news to the citizens of the planet. The ICC is in no way affiliated with the publication.
