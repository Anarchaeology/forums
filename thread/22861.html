What about templates now?
It is usually the fact in mmorpgs that the best templates are usually the ones that are harder to play in the beginning. What is going to happne once you get IPR?

Are templates going to spring up based around this fact. Say for instance an agent that puts all his points int MA and First aid for the first 40 levels and then yanks them all out to put into say shotgun until level 150.

I think it kind of takes something away from the strategy of the game making the game almost too easy. Skills like first aid that pay off in the beginning and then are made useless in the end allow for a kind of determining factor in the game. Do you play it the easy way and level up with first aid, or do you hold out for the end game and hang onto those valuable ips?

It definitely, if nothing else, devalues the IP by alot.
