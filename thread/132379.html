In-Range Indicator for dualed weapons.
At the moment, the health bar of your target shows grey half-bubbles on each side of the health bar itself when you're out of range and red when you're in range. Unfortunately, when dualing weapons with different ranges (say a Pain of Patricia and a Reign of Patricia), you only get to know the maximum range of the weapon with the longest range (in the case of the example, you only get to see where the range of the Reign of Patricia ends).
My suggestion is to change the indicator to show

[============} when you're in range with only one of your weapons and the normal health bars when both/none are in range.
That means
{============} when out of range with both and
[============] when in range with both.

({=grey (out of range). [=red (in range).)

Or anything similar so that it's possible to know when you're at max range with the weapon with the lowest range.
