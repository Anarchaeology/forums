Fixer Weapons and Summon Nanos
Fixer Weapons and Summon Nanos

* Part 1 – Weapons

Using weapons is the only way for a Fixer to do any types of damage, so let’s talk about Fixer weapons.

When you see a Fixer running by, 99% chance he/she is using a Mausser Particle Streamer.

Mausser Particle Streamer at QL200
Damage: 7-342(250); Speed: 1 sec attack, 3 sec recharge

While this is an awesome weapon at low QLs, ever since QL120+(possibly earlier), the damage starts to fall far behind what the mobs are putting out. I propose to change the attack on MPS to 1.5 or 1.8 sec, and recharge to 2.5 or 2.2 sec. This way, at low levels (1-50) the MPS will still take roughly the same time to complete an attack cycle, while at higher levels (50-100) when Ranged Init reaches a rather large value, it will help to speed up the weapon slightly more. Post 100, most Fixers would have over 600 in Ranged Init, and having this change will allow them to equip a slightly better low light scope if they still want to use MPS. This would also slightly increase the number of Burst a Fixer can do in a given time period and further increase damage output slightly.

Now, very recently, a few new SMGs have surfaced. I will list them below:
MTI Aleph 99 – Available only in shops
Michael Patriot Ffi 29A/B – Available only from missions
Duke Lugger SMP – Summon only
OT Rebuilt Suppressor – High level summon only (I’ve only heard about this)
Severus' Fusion Sprayer – No Drop loot from Cyborg Barrack, fixed at QL83
Severus' Void Spinner – No Drop loot from Cyborg Barrack, fixed at QL83
Gamma Ejector – No Drop loot from "Whispers" Dungeon, fixed at QL68 (I’ve only heard about this)

First, both weapons from Severus looked like they are obviously meant to be dual-wielded. But why does Void Spinner have SMG and Fling Shot as requirements? Can we have the Fling requirement changed to Burst?

Second, since both of Severus weapons and Gamma Ejectors are fixed at a certain QL, they are only good for a short while, meaning that Fixers can’t rely on them in their career.

I will talk more about summoned weapons later on in Part 2; I will concentrate for now on the 2 non-summoned SMGs that are available in all QLs

Between MTI Aleph 99 and Michael Patriot Ffi 29A/B, Aleph is the one that come closest to Mausser Particle Streamer.

MTI Aleph 99 at QL200
Damage: 32-280(270); Speed: 1 sec attack, 2.8 sec recharge

But due to the lower max damage, while this weapon gives a more consistent damage output, it usually ends up doing less damage than Maussers with similar SMG/Burst requirements. My proposal to fix this weapon so that it is an equivalent choice for Fixers involves changing the speeds again. If the attack is changed to 1.5, and recharge to 2.0, then this weapon will be boosted significantly and make it a very attractive choice against Maussers. On the other hand, if those number are too aggressive, changing attack to 1.5 and recharge to 2.3 will still make this weapon a reasonable choice against Maussers.

Another thing is that this weapon is currently ONLY available in shops, which means the highest QL we can find is only QL125. Since there are Construction Manuals for this weapon, we could have someone build it… But the SMG Construction Kits have not been working since when I can remember…

PLEASE Fix the SMG Construction Kits as soon as possible!

Now, for the odd ball in pack: Michael Patriot Ffi 29A/B
Michael Patriot Ffi 29A/B at QL200
Damage: 40-175(150); Speed: 1.5 sec attack, 1.3 sec recharge

Now, while the damage from this weapon looks very low at QL200, there are a few things in its favor. One, the faster attack cycle (2.8 seconds total); Two, it does fire damage. But, due to the way AC works in AO, fast weapons with low damage is penalized very badly. That’s why, a lot of times, fast weapons with low damage is compensated with a high critical damage, like OT-Windchaser SM37 PSB Pholler QL190, damage 12-185(875), attack 1.02 second, recharge 1.5 second. Also see Krutt Assault and Ithaca Ki-8 shotguns. While I don’t think that kind critical damage should be put on the Patriot, I DO believe the critical damage on the Patriot should be increased to 500 – 600 range.

* Part 2 – Summon nanos

Let me get one thing out first… Summon Ammo nanos are USELESS, there is no other way of putting it and I really don’t know what to do with it.

Summon Weapon and Armor nanos could be made useful, and they are the ones I’d like to concentrate on.

Currently, the summon timer is fixed and summon items DO disappear after 2 hours. This is as expected, and we can understand that. But, the randomness of summoning of the weapons and armor nanos makes this an impossible situation to deal with. In case there are people that do not know how these work… There are 3 different nanos: "Bootleg Beamers 'n Bolters", "Bootleg Blades 'n Blunts", and "Smuggler Shipment". Beamer will summon randomly a ranged weapon, could be a rifle, assault rifle, pistol, shotgun or smg. Blade will summon randomly a melee weapon, could be 2hb, 2he, 1hb, 1he, piercing. Shipment will summon randomly a piece of armor of random locations; so you could get a plasteel helm on first try and a cyber pants on second try.

To summon a usable SMG will take me on average 10 tries. To summon a FULL set of usable armor, I have no idea how long it takes, but I can tell you it’s a LONG time. To do both of these tasks, it will be no less than 30 minutes IF I am lucky on the armor summons. Then 90 minutes later, they are all gone. I cannot leave my group every 90 minutes to summon weapons/armors!

To fix this problem for weapon summons, there are several ways:

Make the summon items last until we logout for more than 30 minutes. This way, we just need to summon a new weapon at the beginning of every game session.

OR,

Make the summon timer start counting only when you EQUIP the weapon. This way, we can summon a bunch weapons beforehand, and just put on new ones when the old ones disappear.

I have no idea how to fix the armor summoning… it is just too random.

There is another problem with Summon nanos; currently, we can only summon weapons/armors in certain QLs. The first set of nanos summons QL20 weapons/armors; the second set summons QL40, then 60, 80, 100, 120, 140, 160, 180, 200. While this works to a certain extent, would it be better to have only one set of summon nanos, and have the result QL of the weapons/armors to be a function of the player’s current level? This way, we will be able to get upgrade at every level instead of every time we load a new nano, since everyone knows by now that Fixer has all dark blue nano skills. I do realize this will prevent the player from giving out weapons/armors to other players, but at least this will benefit the Fixer more.

Well, that’s quite a bit I had to say, and I hope I am able to get some feedback from Funcom on this, as I’ve genuinely tried to use these additions they made to the Fixer profession.

On another selfish note, can we have Biomech Cloak fixed so that it does go to the BACK slot instead of the chest slot? This bug has been there FOREVER. I am a nanomage fixer, so I’d like to be able to use that.
