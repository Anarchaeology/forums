War against TBA (TBH) and Omni-Pol (player-run) Trade Embargo ended
As of today, the majority of OT factions, including Omni-Pol has ended its aggressive stance towards TBH, the leading faction of the now disbanded TBA. The Black Alliance was disbanded by Azazzel earlier this week, TBH remains as the guild with the majority of the ex-TBA members. TBH has vowed to stay true to Omni-Tek from now on and only fight the clans. This honourable goal will hopefully stay high on TBHs priority list at all times. We will need TBHs assistance in coming times to fight expected clan attacks which are lead by a - as of now - unknown CoT army commander. Once the name of this commander is known to us, we will see what we will do - together as OT forces. The loyal OT leaders are watching TBHs actions closely now, should any treason be done by TBH again, the state of aggression against them will be reinstated. I hope though that this will not be neccessary, as peace among the OT factions is desired to stand united against the clans in times to come. All trade and teaming activity between Omni-Pol members and TBH members is from now on allowed again. We will see what OTSEC as our official political head council does.

The next target to eradicate from Omni-Tek is Methlon, The Sinister Anarchists and all their sympathizers. Among them "Atrum Consensio" and "The Dark Templars (TDT)". Those factions are known among OT leaders for their crimes against Omni-Tek, which will of course not be tolerated by the loyalists - such as our guild. Once Methlon and the other rogue faction leaders are gone from the company, we can concentrate fully on the Dust Brigade and aggressive clans such as "Eternal Fury", "Storm" and "Opposing Force" aswell as the "United Freedom (UF)" forces, lead by Redruum.

Best regards,
