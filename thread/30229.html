Mobs
I remember when FC had first mentioned the idea of the IP Reset and OE. They had discussed remvoing people from excessivly OE'ing and had mentioned an IP Reset and making MOBS less powerful. Now that 14.2 has hit FC has developed these issues, however, MOBS seem to be much more insanely difficult then they once were.
I.E. I go into a mission my level, nothing more, nothing less,
and it is much harder than before. What is so odd about this, is due to IP reset i was able to swtich to a new combat skill, and with this new skill I am dishing out much more damage then i was before. The fact that a mob of my level is making menced meat of me is grossly atrocious, how am I supposed to have that much difficulty killing 1 mob and still go and kill 50-100 more in my mission.

And it is not just that, their seems to be a new agrro on the mobs, though it may be less, it is much more of a pain. As we all know most missions have 3 mobs sitting in the front room, it used to be simple pulling off a multiple spawn area off 1 by 1, now it is much more difficult, and on top of that, when you do successfully pull it, the mob goes absolutly insane and starts to run around like a madman, running through out the mission zone, as if it were paul revere running through the streets shouting "the redcoats are coming" or something of the such.

It has also came to my attention that the range on "Da Tuanters" is far to short, granted its understandable to have a shorter range, but the fact that range is shorter than melee range is absolutely ridiculous. I think that "da tauanter" should have the range of that of ranged weapon such as a shotgun or rifle.

What does everyone else feel about this, and please, let us be serious here and not say things completly outrageous as most people do, if are ideas and requests are to be taken seriously and put into consideration, we must act as serious and professional as we can and make valid points to FC.

Thank you all for listening, and thank you for any input you may have.
