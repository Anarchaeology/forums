Cz, as someone who's done extensive MR tests, here are some numbers for reference...

Multi-wielding increases RoF by about 28-33% based on all tests I have conducted. It depends a lot on how MR is working that day (the devs seem to mess with it a lot), the Atk/Rec allocation (the new system does not work as well under certain circumstances as it used to), and some other factors.

Given the small RoF increase, your offhand pistol needs to do at least 50% of the damage of your onhand pistol, OR YOU WILL LOWER YOUR DAMAGE.

This poses a very large problem.

Even at level 200, an Advent can only wield about a QL 170 pistol. (Of the decent variety.) For reference, a QL 150 pistol paired with a Ql 200 pistol will often gimp your damage. A QL 160 pistol only provides about a 11% increase in damage. QL 170 would increase damage by aout 20%.

Note that the latter situation takes over 950 Multi-Ranged. That's not a whole lot of results for 950 points worth of IP.

When level 100-100-ish Advents can squeeze on QL 200 pistols like the rest of the world, they cannot equip any higher than a QL 130 offhand of the same type. When Advents hit closer to the cap, they can equip a QL 150 offhand. When Advents are able to cast their QL 145 MM/MR buff, they can finally get on QL 160ish offhand pistols. (880 MR requirement)

Basically, a situation has been created meaning that Advents between the levels of 100-145 are actually reducing their damage on a regular basis due to the use of what I would consider a "class defining ability." (We're the only ones that get any serious buffs for it, after all.)

Getting a skill to around 700 points is a heavy IP sink for something that is currently lowering your damage. Especially considering that MR is an aqua, 4-per-level skill--not a green, 5-per-level skill!

Even with an optimum setup, we are unlikely to even gain more than around 23% damage from dual-wielding pistols. This seems like an aweful lot of effort, time, IP, and equipment-swapping/gathering for a marginal to negative damage change. Between levels 100-200, you basically see a range of minus 15%-plus 20% change in damage. This is unacceptable for such a huge IP sink.

Why do Advent-only pistols have 1200+ MR req while Melee has reqs in the 800 range? Non-advent pistols like the Kick Pistol and the Revolving Cold Laser have 800 and 777 requirements respectively. Yet, through all this, the reason given to raising pistol MR reqs was to keep non-MR professions from taking advantage of it...yet the only decent pistols with resonable MR reqs have no profession requirements at all!

Furthermore, the lack of any good variety of pistols combined with the 50% threshold means that if we offhand anything but a BBI or River XP, we will probably be lowering our damage regardless of QL. If we try to be creative and use various Burst/FullAuto/AS pistols, maybe critter pistols, etc... just for the sake of variety. 99% of the time, this will result in us doing less damage than with our main hand alone!

The biggest issues with the current MR setup, is that you are as likely--if not moreso--to lower your damage than raise it. This is unacceptable! A huge IP sink like that should never lower your damage!

In my opinion:
1) Multi-ranging should have an independant on-hand bar that always does the same as normal damage.
2) The off-hand bar should fire at a scaling 25%-50% off-hand RoF based on skill.

This would provide the same 125% RoF increase in the beginning, yet never gimp damage. As skill scaled toward the 900-1000 range, the RoF increase would get up to around 150%. Very much how many skills currently work with the 1-1000 skill scaling progression.

This would also mean that professions using small amounts of skill to throw on low-QL offhand would get at or lower to the 125%-135% "inteded" RoF increase.

Offhand RoF:
Skill 1-500: 25% RoF
Skill 500-1000: 25%-50% RoF

At 700 MR, it would provide the "intended" 35% RoF increase. Getting over 700 MR in a non-MR profession (i.e. Not Soliders or Advents) would be a HUGE ip sink, and limited severely by title caps.

Past 700 MR, professions who invest heavily in MR would be able to raise their damage by sinking IP into the skill, not lower it.

Just a suggestion...it's an issue I've given much thought about... But, likely, the developers won't cosider a change to the system.

Thanks for a reply, and hopefully this post will be able to shed some more light on why we complain about MR so much. =)

-Jayde
