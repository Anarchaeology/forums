Suggestion
I suggested something about backpacks and easier packing a month or two ago, in the Game Mechanics thread.

Not putting things directly into backpacks when you click on the items in the Loot window, but allowing the user to left-click on the item that needs moving, and right-clicking on the container - if there's free space in the container, the item will be placed in it. Else, the usual message about the container being full will be displayed, and another container will have to be selected.

For this to work properly, I think the inventory window would have to be resizable, else one would have to scroll up and down between the items and the backpacks, probably. My character carries 5 backpacks in his inventory these days. Thank goodness the 'special' backpacks appeared into the shops several patches back, making keeping track of what is where a bit easier.
