Sand Mantis Queen Egg
I obtained this Mantis Egg a bit ago from the Sand Mantis Queen in the Smugglers Den Dungeon (1750, 870, SFH), and after a bit browsing through the Antiguardians item database I got to understand the different steps on the way to the Virral Triumvirate Egg.

The only problem is, I am at a loss how to do it. Maybe we can throw some ideas around here, or even get a hint from the devs


First step:

Heat

Petrified Mantis Egg

Somehow the egg must be heated, I tried most of the tradeskill items in the shops, but to no avail. The first item that came to my mind was the Personal Furnace, but no luck. Any ideas ?




Second step:

Filigree wires

Partially Wired Mantis Egg 1
Partially Wired Mantis Egg 2
Fully Wired Mantis Egg

Someone said this should be Silver Nano Circuitry Filigree Wire but it might be any other Filigree wire either. I am not sure myself, and I don't think it can be found in stores anymore. Any ideas ?




Third step:

Nano sensor

Incomplete Virral Egg

I do not know where to get a nano sensor either, but I guess we need this process to make it fit into the egg:
Nano sensor
Silver Nano Circuitry Filigree Wire
Interfaced Nano Sensor




Last step:

Gems

Virral Egg with Gem
Virral Egg with Dual Gems

and after putting in the last gem we get the end result:
Virral Triumvirate Egg

I guess to cut the 3 gems we will need a Jensen Gem Cutter to get Perfectly Cut Gems. Any other ideas ?
