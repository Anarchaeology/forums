Living Breathing World
I had a thought....
(i hear people ducking for cover, but fear not)


Something AO lacks (no, im not gonna bash AO, simply give an honest suggestion) is the essence of real life things in it, i'll elaborate....


People dont own places or are given responsibility of things.

What i mean is that maybe this world needs a few more characters that people can interact with on a daily basis and people to organize things ingame to make it a better community.


if an ARK or GM is free, maybe they could interview people for jobs that ARKs and GMs dont have time to do, like run bars (Rompa, Babs, Happy Rebel, Neuters R Us)
put someone in charge of the place, or if not a player they trust, then an ARK who makes a character and plays him in his non-ARK hours.

They look after that place, organize things for it, help other people organize events at the places, be the contact or manager FOR that place....
then those places would be a lot more lively, and people would get to meet and talk to these minor characters n stuff and feel more connected to the storyline, game and have a fun time.


Also, how about an actual player market? Trade is the hub of, well,....trade but maybe a trade hall. At the moment if you want a buff or buy something, its BB next to Wompa's in Ent.....
and since that is next to the Wompa's it sends lag thru the roof there.
Maybe an indoor (to drop the lag) market in Trade.
A place for people to go to set up stall, offer services or go to buy stuff from players
If that bomb had gone off 20 metres South in the BB stand, the omni-tek trade market would be crippled today


Also, on the note of the people organizing or owning the bars and shops n stuff,
maybe you could do the same to offer positions omni tek (or the council of truth) to organize in world things like a government of cities. Where people can go to debate/roleplay, vent about the bomb or worry that leet fighting is getting out of control etc etc.
And give them RP titles like "Counsellor Biddlington" or "Governor Tel" etc etc

Give some power to the players that have proven themselves responsible so that they can make it more involving for the rest

I thankyou and will now stand aside for someone with a brain to speak......
*me /curts and moves aside*
