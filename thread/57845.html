The Hearing
Dressed in her finest, most conservative suit, Savoy goes to meet the South Rubi-Ka Society's board of trustees. It's just a formality. She has already been told in private both the purpose and the outcome of this hearing, yet she is still nervous.

The hearing has been convened to examine some of decisions and incidents surrounding Savoys presidency of the South Rubi-Ka that in one way or other may have damaged the Societys reputation. The board of trustees are not unsympathetic, but it is clear they are more concerned in the preserving the Societys reputation than establishing the truth.

Savoy is reminded about the rather sordid affair which resulted in a picture of her being included in a nudie WLFF calendar and of how the Society's credibility was torn to shreds by an ill fated appearance on the popular holovid talk show, The Rubi-Ka Show . She is also censored for leading the SRS in a wholly unauthorised raid against a clan penal labour camp to rescue the artist Miroslav Borky , thus putting in jeopardy the Society's non-political status.

After a short recess, the board of trustees inform Savoy that regrettably they have no option but to ask her resign. Savoy just nods silently and signs the letter.

Savoy heads back the SRS's headquarters to pack her stuff. Wodehouse, the butler, takes her coat. May I inquire how the hearing went, madam?

I could have gone better, Wodehouse. Looks like I am out of a job.

"If I may speak freely madam," he says, "this stinks."

Savoy smiles weakly. "Thank you Wodehouse. You'll look after everyone, won't you?"

"Naturally madam. And may I say, it has been a privilege knowing you."

"You too, Woodhouse. You too."

Alone in her office Savoy punches up Stormdragon's number on her comms unit. "Hi Stormy, it's me. I dont suppose the Honored Maiden could use a old, washed-out soldier?"

/ooc One by one all the brightest stars of the South Rubi-Ka Society faded away and disappeared. After a wild, colourful year together we simply did not have enough people left in the SRS to carry on. Of the few remaining active characters, most will be joining The Honored Maidens with me. The SRS will continue to be the home to a handful of alts and seldom played characters, but we won't be recruiting anymore and our regular roleplaying activities have been suspended.

I'd like to thank all those who participated in our past events and in general supported us in game and on this forum. And you might just want to keep an eye out for future THM events.
