When does the XP curve start to become really depressing?
I was wondering when levelling starts becoming a real drag. I dinged 129 this morning before work. Did a single mission and got about 7M xp (5.5 needed to level). Took me about an hour and a half.

When I was low level, I always heard about people getting bored and frustrated about levelling past, say, 100. I don't feel this way at all... yet.

I saw a graph once that displayed the curve and I remember it went up pretty steeply. So far, however, as I'm beginning to get near the high-level range, I'm not noticing a spectacular increase in time needed to level...

Say you're doing solid, back to back team missions. How much time does it take you per level at, say, 150? 175? 190?

Sheffy
Supa Leet
I think I started to need between 12 and 16 missions to level once I got around 195, depending on the average size of the missions.

And I wouldn't say people don't go much higher than 190. If you make it to 190, you should really keep going (and most seem to) because right when you ding 190, you're out of that god-forbidden title cap you've been working so hard to come out of.

The last 10 levels are the best 10 levels of the game, accomplishment-wise. They are also the longest and most frustrating -- to do a team mission and see your XP bar move less than 10% can be a bit unnerving at times, but those who have the perseverence to hit 200 certainly earn it.

Oh, and do QL190-195 missions for fewer RMs and higher xp over time, even when you're 190+.
