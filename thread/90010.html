Kupotek 0wnz Camelot ( a parody but true! )
It was an unusual morning at Camelot.

GS1- There wasn't a clan for miles around, and OT was having a field day. Tara dropped like a first shoe by the bed, and one of our very own ( OT ) Shibbiy, not Shibbey ninja looted her stealthy like a martyered thief. He got off with a bracer of merlin and a GOS, both nodrop items. GMs wouldn't do a thing. It was the sight to make leets cry...

GS2- Tara dropped before the second shoe could fall, no clanners, the box was a hayday of silliness so profound to put a gridstream event to shame.. a dragon circlet dropped. I won it, i fainted and needed foot to mouth resucitations by tehdeacon okay wel deacon wasn't around so i had to settle for a foot massage from Stomy

G3- Tara- was so busy reading a magazine, doing her nails and eating bonbons that she didn't have a chance, even during the commercials between Seinfeld episodes.. a GOC dropped. Zoom entered the raffle, and I thought tomyself, weird, he's gonna win i know it, and he did. Then he said.. well Ive already got 2 so I'll let you guys raffle this again! So we did, and I won. 2 for 2.

GS4- Tara didnt drop she plummetted down the gullet like a chocloate surprise without the kaboom, and it was yummy.. She dropped a HPO and a Dragon Body. Skin was in this raffle, who is also. well I forget who is also , sorry. -_- And I knew he'dwin somehow, and he did. Then I won the HPO. At this point Im getting tells of course by people who want to buy things, or want me to give them something..but Kryos just says.. omg you gotta help me find GA today!! And all in all it was very crazy but fun. I actually thought I was in a dream and was sleeping.

So it was cool that everyone was telling me i broke a camelot record, and everyone did a potato dance at the end.. this story is a declaration of kupocity.. in the land.. of Camelot.

Thanks everyone
