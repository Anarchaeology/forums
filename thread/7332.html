Nanocrystals, and other Technologies of Rubi-Ka: How do they work?
So we all know how Nanocrystals work in real life. You raise your skills, right click the nano, and viola! You got program.

But what about in-game? How do they work? When you right click one, you see your character tap his wrist, does this mean that the nano program is injected into the wrist? Wouldn't it make more sense to inject it into the back of the neck, or spine, because it would get to your neurals easier?

And what about the visual effects on your body that Nano's have? I can understand things like the Challenger series, they are supposed to be visual, the increase in size envokes fear. but what about stuff like Avenging Shield (or any other shield) which glowes at random times. What if you wanted to be stealthy but still have the protection?

These pose some questions that should be answered, and hopefully they will be.

As for your question a lot of it can be answered by "use your imagination". It might seem like a cop out but actually it's part of the appeal of roleplaying, theres no right or wrong as such. eg you see someone tapping their wrist, a homo nano might see it as "moving her hands in complex circular, weaving motions manipulating the millions of tiny nanobots constantly present around her body".

Actually, in game, nano programs are said to be uploaded to your ncu there isnt any mention of injections. My guess is that the programs are a set of instructions for manipulating the generalised nanobots in and around your body. Implants on the other hand are said to contain specialised nanobots.

As for stealth; if you are of a profession that uses conceal whats wrong with roleplaying that the art of concealment involves training to hide not only your biological presence but your nanopresence. After all it is the 30K century surely there exists nanotechnology to mask nanobot emissions.

Like I said "use your imagination".

Have fun and hope to see you in game /ooc end
