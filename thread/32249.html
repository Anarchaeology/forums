And the gifts were good....
In the begining of Rubi-ka...

12 children were born. The parents bestowed unique gifts on each of the children...

"Adventurer, you are given the task of being the jack of all trades, the yardstick by which all others are measured. You shall have heals, and the option of using ranged or melee combat, but excelling at neither. To add to your cool factor, yee saw have morphs."

And it was so.

"Agent, thee shall be the shadow of rubi-ka, blending in though out, being noticed by none, the silent creeping death. You shall have the almighty aimed shot, a true snipers gift. You shall always be bestowed the ability to mimic other classes, thus furthering your own strengths, at a cost."

And it was so.

"Bureacrat, you will be the leader of the battle field. Only a few other children will be able to even come close to the power you shall over the mobs. You will charm your enemies to become your own sword, and those that you cannot, you will simply ease the hate they have. You will also be given your own bot to amuse yourself as well as for combat. You will also be given powerful nukes."

And it was so.

"Doctor, you shall be the healing hands. Other profession will have healing capabilites, but none to match the life saving tools at your fingers. Hence forth, you shall have HoTs, Team hp buffs, temp hp buffs, direct heals and team heals. To aid your poor weapon skills, you will be given init debuffs as well as powerful DoTs."

And it was so.

But hark, did the children grow envious of their siblings gifts. It was not long before the children spread through out Rubi-ka, bringing many seeds, but not foresight. The children did begin to plauge each other with their gifts, the Soldiers being the first...

"Hey, that soldier is using is TMS to kill that Slayerdriod! It's doing more damage to itself then him!"

"What the hell? I got hit in PvP with Full auto for 6k damage?!"

"Soldiers are too powerful!"

The parents heard their childrens pleas and acted quickly:

TMS was changed. Damage in PvP was halfed, and a 40% health cap was added. Burst and Full auto nerfed. The complaints did not end there however.........

"Agents can cast my profession buffs before I can!"

"Agents can sneak any mission they want!"

"Ow, I died to an Agent aimed shot!"

Thus, the nerfs were handed out again:

Conceal and Aimed shot nerfed. FPing was changed.

The whines did not end there.......

"Hey, that engie is using his Slayerdriod at 120!"

"Hey that NT is nuking me from a mile away!"

"Hey, that Trader is too uber!"

"I can't kill that Doctor on one on one PvP!"

Each nerf brings about it's own little death.

Each profession was given gifts, and many of them are long gone. Please don't undermine another professions attempts for a fix or a change. You're free to comment on 'balance', but lets abide by some rules...

Level reqs are bad in a skill based system.
Don't put 'class balancing' nanos in BOSS loot.
If we have a skill system, don't slap on profession requirements on weapons (nanos are fine along with armor)
Please, for the love of God, stop the nerfs.
Camping is bad, don't put uber loot in static spawns. Make it random, or better yet, tradeskill.
As gamers, I think it's safe to say we all like AO and want it to work. Thoughts, comments and flames welcome.
