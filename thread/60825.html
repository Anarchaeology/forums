the GARGAR SHOW!
hello there! i would like to invite all clans on rubi-ka to a series of shows called THE GARGAR SHOW! what is the gargar show you ask? well,it's a weekly game show where you can win money doing easy but entertaining stuff! there will be ten shows and with each show the prizes will get bigger and bigger untill the last show with the magnificent 10 million credit award!

how is the show working you ask? well, the rules will change during each show to keep ourselves entertained but for the first show we will have 4 levels. in the first level there will be 5 rounds, in each round there will be a chance to win a nice 125k prize. all you have to do is be faster then other players! in the second level there will be 3 rounds with a prize of 250k for each round winner but to win you will have to do something a little more complicated. in the third level we will have 2 rounds and each winner will bring home a nice half million credits. and in the last level we will have a cool 1 million credits!

remember you can only win a total of three times! so you may want save one of your chances for later levels!

the show will take place in tir on the roof of the fair trade store where our beloved whompa was once reside. the show will start at 22:00 norwegian time! be sure to be there
