Guildhouse
I would like to see guildhouses too !

There shlould be some things in the guildhouse to help managing a guild, though.

First i would like to see a financial bank in it, so ppl can store money that is supposed for buying guilditems, and to pay the rent.

Second there should be different counters to store equipment. e.g. for armor, weapons and so on. This could be used to get order into the items and to help exchange items netween guild member. This could be done with chests too.

For all those things there should be a access control, which is set by the guild leader or the ppl the guildleader authorize.

That would be REALLY cool!

Outside the guildhouse there should be the possibility to raise a flag or to put a logo on the wall, but it also should be possible to keep it secret, so no sign other than a logo or flag should indicate that this house is a guildhouse.

Access to the house should be granted to all people, wether they are members of the guild or not, but there should be the possiblitiy to keep ppö out too! I don`t know how to handle that yet, but this should be made possible.
