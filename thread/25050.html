May 12th article
Rubi-ka Times-

Sunday May 12- "The scene outside Omni-1 Prison was a ghastly one. Behind me we see Omni-pol covering 3 persons who was killed in what is said to be the worst incident in 12 years. 2 nurses and 1 security guard was killed when a person identified as Meta Waldram staged the escape which ultimately resulted in the loss of these 3 civil employees. This was the scene just one month ago. Now for the first time Director Lucann who ran the mental health department of the facility will go on record to inform viewers of the event that took loved ones.
Doctor can you tell me what happened?
"Of course, As you know our facility houses in both normal inmates and the criminally insane, during our morning rotation one of our security officers was surprised and attacked by Miss Waldram who then shot him. During her escape she also shot 2 nurses unfortunately all 3 died as a result."
What can you tell us about this woman Doctor?
"Meta Waldram is a 26 year old woman who suffers from a rare form of Psychosis, its important for us to not blame Meta for what she has done after all Psychosis is a illness and she deserves our compassion. You see Metas form of Psychosis is harsh in that it manifests itself to the point she looses all contact with what is real, take her posts for example http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=20593 she believes that these persons actually exist and some may but you have to understand that this illness of hers has twisted her abilities to reason thus she has created this fantasy world."
What would you urge readers to do Doctor?
"I urge all to keep an eye out. This woman is a danger to not only herself but to anyone she sees as a threat to her. With Psychosis its not uncommon for the patient to become violent if they believe themselves to be in danger and from the postings I would say that she has reached a dangerous state."

Thank you doctor.....We would like to remind our readers that the woman we are looking for in connection to these 3 deaths is Meta Waldram although we dont have her nickname we do have a general description for you. Meta Waldram is 26 years old, 5'7'' to 5'8'' in height, approx 110 to 115 lbs in weight, she has blue-green eyes and a beauty mark on her right lower cheek and brown hair. We believe given her present mental state that she will try to hide in clan terroritory under an assumed nickname and try to join with a clan. According to Doctor Lucann she will most likely be shy and somewhat quiet trying to not draw attention to herself. We do know that all inmates have an encoded information chip so that even with an assumed name her real name may appear on a close scan. We appeal to the clans not to hide this woman she is both unstable and dangerous, Omni-tek has authorized a 25,000 credit reward for information leading to the capture of Meta Waldram. The public is not advised to take matters into their own hands since Miss Waldram will become increasingly unstable as time goes on. If you have any information as to where Meta Waldram may be hiding on RUBA-KI 2 you are urged to contact Director Lucann or Director Kheys at sethdil@yahoo.com make sure to give a detailed account and your nickname so that we may contact you regarding the reward.
