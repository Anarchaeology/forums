"Two men enter, one man leaves" arena
How about an arena for real males/females/atroxes, not just another 0% zone, but a real arena. Only one person or one team comes out alive. The opponent has/have to pickup his/their gear in the reclaim terminal.

The arena is a stand alone zone to avoid/minimize lag from spectators etc. But from a spectator point of view it will look like a normal arena surrounded by seatings. There will be two monitors, one for each team, with the name, prof and health and nano bars of each of the fighters (no communication posible to/from the arena, radio-dead, messages buffered until you come out).

To enter the arena there are two terminals (like grid terminals), one for each team. When both team leaders have accessed the terminal and team lvls approved the teams are zoned into the arena.

Everybody will be totally debuffed when entering the arena (and auto saved so you come back in front of the crowd for further humiliation). A count down will start (eg. 60-120 seconds, depending on number of ppl on each team). During this count down it is possible for the teams to buff etc. When count down reaches 0 the fight can start (just like normal 0-25% zones, but longer count for buffing).

Everything which are normally shown in the chat window of the fighters are also shown on the monitors (count down, buffs, dmg, chat etc).

The winners will be zoned out with a special (eg. 10 min) "glow" buff casted on them (something like the Boss glow) so they can be celebrated.

To avoid ppl just camping the arena, dmg has to be done, eg every 30 s (enough for all nanos to be casted). And a max fight time of eg. 10 min (dont know what is needed by high lvl teams to find a winner). If not, everybody will be send to the reclaim terminal.

Location of this arena: In the basement of ICC for easy access by everyone.
