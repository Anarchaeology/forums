ARP + GoS Fund Raiser
"We raise funds to rebuild Old Athen, we WILL raise morale. We are talking buildings, streets, and some demolition work. We have looked at these ruins long enough." - Windguaerd


After almost 5hrs, the Gathering of Souls (GoS) came to an end, with a total of 240 million (including pledges) in donations! Our clan was fortunate to reach the 80 Million mark this week, with 220 million to go for our own goal. Hundreds of clanners contributed through credits, services and contests as well as entertainment. Our thanks to Unitsi, Daigalean, Rederon, Calautica, Commander Silvathorn and all the good people who participated during the GoS and the Clan Parade lead by our Commander Windguaerd through Athen, Tir, Avalon and West Athen.

We've shown the people the meaning of unity, and our support for a good cause. Long live the clans!

The fund-raising is still going on, and will continue to do so until we reach our target. We are still accepting donations and any help with events is much appreciated.

If you would like to do your bit for the Clans and the Athen Restoration Project, please contact:

Windguaerd
Kalannar
Unitsi
Silvathorn

Or alternatively visit the grid site: www.mercury-dragons.org and pledge your support


"We can't limit ourselves to military goals, we need to show the clan citizens we can do more than pick up a rifle and shoot. We need to show financial strength, raise morale, show the galaxy we are NOT terrorists. By rebuilding Athen we will raise morale, encourage merchants to increase commerce in our town and have a more social city! Lets bring down the old omni signs, clean up the rubble and make things as they should." - Windguaerd
