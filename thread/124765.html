Sell it...
GA is like crack...once you equip it you will NEED GA II,II,IV. Look at Tasker1.

I'm assuming, since you just started playing, that you have no access to large amounts of credits at this time. So unless you are EXTREMELY blessed, don't count on finding any of the other GA's or even buying them, because they are extremely expensive (well, maybe with the new patch they will drop more frequently...even still, expect to pay 70-700mill for GA II-IV).

I would sell the GA I, since it's really only useful for PvP twinks...if you equip it, you will find that it may only last you to about level 80-90. The 60-80mill you make off selling it will last you and your friends for a long long time.

I found PPPE (shape hard armor) and sold it for 80mill. I almost kept it, but atm, the only useful armors you can make with it are Carb and Junkmetal...both of them are good lowbie/twink armor. I would also have to level an Engie high enuf to make the armor...which at the time was not something I really wanted to do.

The 80mill has been doing me extremely well. I was able to equip myself with the best equipment at all levels and have plenty to help out my newb friends that just started. I level fast now because I have no worries about money.
