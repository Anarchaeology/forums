Leet
The Tir River Swim Competition was a wake up call to the CoT about the need to clean up the river. But, it did nothing to help them clean it up. Long before the competition two of our members Quikrete and Kpax33 started collecting donations for cleaning up the river. They did this without the Staff of Opposing Force knowing. A little after Great Vikings of Rubi-Ka held their swimming competition. Word finnaly got to the staff when the people of Tir started accusing Quik and Kpax of stealing. It was actually quite a buzz around Tir, you might remember it. Utildai the staff and I talked to Quik and Kpax to make sure that it was legitimate. When they told us it was we decided to hold two buffing events for donations, on the same day, for anyone in Tir. We would add that money to the money they had already collected then give it to the CoT for the clean up efforts. It was a long and arduous day for all involved in the buffing as most stayed all day to buff. When it was finally over we tallied all the money from the donations. Added the money Quik and Kpax had already collected plus put in a little from the OpFor bank. We then contacted the CoT. The CoT contacted us back and set up a day and time to give them the donations. Mr. Radiman was the representative there to accept them.

We didn't just dump in cash as it wasn't our cash to dump in. There was nothing posted about our event because we wanted to surprise the CoT and Mr. Radiman. Mr. Radiman and the CoT were very surprised and pleased with our donations. If you remember at that time the CoT had run into monetary difficulties with the clean up effort. Opposing Force did not know of this when we had our event. We were just happy we could help.

We would like to thank Scorus for pointing out the monument. We had no idea it was going to be done and were very surprised that it was. Perhaps they could add a second monument about the Swimming Competition across from ours.
