Metamorphosis of CAS
Clan Anarchist Syndicate has undergone a profound shift in focus and attitude that will greatly increase the gaming enjoyment of our members. Clan Anarchist Syndicate will now be placing a greater emphasis on role playing within the game. We hope to take an increased role in the story dynamic while maintaing our objective. Which is the defeat of the administrative hierarchy of the Omni-Tek war machine and the establishment of a just and free Republican government for all peoples of good faith in Rubi-Ka. In support of this goal we now accept applicants of the 100th level and higher only. If you fit this requirement and wish to truly effect the future of our planet: apply today to join this historic and legendary Clan.
