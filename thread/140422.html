Dead citizen found floating in Omni-1 Entertainment. Sewers

Early this morning, a corpse was found floating face down in the Sewers of Omni-1 Entertainment. The citizen that found the body reported it to Omni-Pol, who quickly dispatched a squad of guards and retrieved the corpse, which was then brought to Omni-Med for identification.

An Omni-Pol spokesperson later notified us that the remains had identified as those of Major Fyarr of Omni-InternOps. The spokesperson also stated that Major Fyarrs insurance records are up to date, but no one can confirm this so far. It is rumored that his killer might have used some kind of perma-death weapon.

Omni-Pol is looking into the cause of his sudden death and they are interested in information about Major Fyarr movements the last few days. A request sent to Omni-InternOps was returned unanswered, and we have not been able to reach Major Fyarrs superior, Colonel Throll.

Several sources we have spoken with have mentioned a rumor claiming that Ex-Omni citizen Dave Diamondcut Duster, a known criminal and convicted murderer could be involved in the death of Major Fyarr. On June 18 last year, Omni-Tek convicted Dave Duster for the murder of two Omni-InternOps, conspiracy against Omni-Tek and theft from the Omni-Tek Reserve bank.

OTPC will use this chance to remind OT Citizens that there is a warrant out for the arrest of Dave Duster. If spotted, please inform your local Omni-Pol station.
