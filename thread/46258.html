Reverse Polarity Reflect nano idea
When you put a normal reflect shield on a target, it redirects portions of attacks back at the attacker but lets healing through. How about we introduce a Reverse Polarity Reflect (RPR) that does the opposite. It would ignore attacks, but would redirect portions of healing from the target back to the caster.

It would be a hostile nano, that you would cast on your opponent in combat. If that opponent gets healed (by anything other than natural healing), the heal would be reduced to 70% of it's original amount with the remaining 30% of the heal benefitting the player that cast the RPR shield. So, if you put RPR on a doc that casts Complete Heal on himself, you'd get 3000 health. The RPR would stay on your opponent for about 1 minute.

Due to game mechanics, you'd probably lose the 30% benefit if either you or your opponent zoned during the duration of the nano. Nanos just seem to forget who the caster was when you zone. But the part about heals being reduced to 70% should remain until the nano ends.

I'd say give versions of this to soldiers and engies, because they seem to be the reflect profs. Also give versions to any other "in need of PvP love" prof, if you can justify it. Then anytime your opponent heals himself in combat, just say "thanks" and really tick him off.

Also, might need to make this only useable against players. Not sure about that part.
