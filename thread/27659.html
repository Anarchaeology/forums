Pistols
If Funcom gave you pistol as your cheapest combat skill, what they mean is, "Don't enter combat." If you do stick with the pistol, use it to time your nukes. (Pistol shot went off, must be time to push the nuke button again.) The PNG M1007A1 is about the best pistol you can get that uses only pistol skill - and as weapons go, it's pretty lame. A real 1.x/1.x speed weapon should have a max damage of at least 1 per QL; the PNG is half that. What this means is that with it, you're doing about half the damage someone with a real weapon is doing.

In the game designers' sick minds, they made this up to you by giving you nukes. Except nukes bite, because you can't reload nano during a fight, like you can ammo. OK, you can - you can calm the target, stop combat, sit down, recharge, stand up, attack again. And lots of people do.

If I were to do it over again with my 'crat, and I'm considering it, I'd dump all my pistol, fling shot, and so on points and get a Vektor ND -- same as everybody else, it seems like sometimes.

I have a low level crat and messed around with pistols 'til about level 25. Granted all pistols I tried were the same level as me, the ones that seemed to work the best for descent DoT with a high crit and very fast fire rate were Minielectroniums.

I simply wanted something that would provide fast supplemental damage along with my droid. The minielectroniums use pistol, ranged energy, and flingshot. The multirange req is not too overpowering compared to others, still sinking ip into it breaks one's heart.

The coolest thing about them is 1) their fire-rate and 2) two of the reqs are wrangleable.

I also tried out the OT Boomer+.90AE. The fire rate is much slower which meant I was nuking with no shots inbetween. The DoT is higher on the Boomer compared to the Minielectronium and the Multirange req is much easier on the IP wallet. Also, only uses pistol and flingshot (1 wrangleable skill).

If only the multirange req were not such a killer (imho) I would have loved to stick with Minielectroniums. So I ended up using a Electrical Pacifier...LOL.

Here's a couple of links to the AG data on these two pistols around the ql20 range, note the fire-rate.

Boomer+.90AE: http://antiguardians.org/itemdb.php?cmd=view&id=123458
