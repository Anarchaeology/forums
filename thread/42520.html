The Ideal Event
I see a lot of people cry and complain about various events, but I never see anyone make suggestions for events. That's why I'm posting this topic.

Basically, I'd like to know what your "ideal event" is. What kind of FC/ARK driven event (or events) would you like to be part of and if you're motivated enough, why? Perhaps by throwing our visions of a good event out, we can be a little more constructive than we are when we post a topic to the effect of "OMFG FC U SUKC A GM KILLD ME WHEN I WAS HUNTING BECUZ I DIDN'T LISTEN WHEN THEY TOLD ME TO LEAVE WTF."

In a nutshell, my ideal event is really anything which works on the Clan/Omni conflict. Sure, PvP could be involved (in terms of PvP against events characters, or PvP against fellow players... doesn't matter -- but it should be an open option for those who don't cooperate, depending on the situation), but it doesn't have to be a part. Basically there's a war going on (as has been stated many times), and the conflict and tension should be pushed.
