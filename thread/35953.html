Well, of course. That's how it is at all levels. If someone knows you want something they will try to charge what they can get for it. At all levels, but generally it is a higher level charging a lot for a lower level because most low levels can equip an item higher than they can actually get and , of course, want to do so.

What the the suggestion does (needs fleshed out more) is balance that situation a bit more. With that suggestion in place, if a higher level really feels the need to have a low level item then the low level can charge them through the nose for it. Price gouging and fairness alike will garner reputations. Eventually it reach a sort of balance. It would suck for a low level to totally gouge a high level that might be able to get them a new weapon they can't via cash or other-wise and vice-versa.

Yes, what I typed above has issues with only talking about certain rare items being capped versus no caps for commons but that's not the point. The point of the suggestion is being:
  • to balance out camping - removing desire for higher levels who do not get exp or the benefit that appropriate levels would (thus harming the lowe levels game experience)
  • have higher's interact socially with lowers more
  • build a stronger, more balanced economy by leveraging lower levels more
  • remove higher level camp competition - sure twinks could work them, but then that would place others at that level in better position to group for the same goals
  • aside from teaming at appropriate levels, solo'er issues vs higher levels would be vastely improved. Griefers will continue to exist but those are dealt with eventually


A lot of benefit could come from the suggestion in form of social and economic balancing.

  • Mobs giving loot according to difference between themselves and the killer. The 'In Character' explanation being the more the attacker outclassed the mob, the fewer items survived the fight intact.

Council of Testers
The entire issue of loot and rewards need to be looked at. Part of the reason for new stuff being boss mob only is to stop people using CS to get them. Now, I agree, nanos and the like intended to rebalance a class should in fact be treated just like any other normal nano, whereas nanos that give an edge or are for fun shouldn't be.

I'm beginning to think that having pre-determined rewards from missions is not a great idea. However, before they could be removed, a simpler and more efficient method of selling and buying loot would need to be implemented. Then players could put any good loot they find up for sale and other players could buy what they wanted.

If you could select a general category of reward rather than search for something specific, and if mobs and chest dropped a greater variety of stuff, then that would help balance the economy. Rare stuff would be rare simply because it didn't drop very often.

Boss mobs could would drop better stuff, and there could still be boss mob specific stuff, but not all new stuff would need to be of that type. A hierarchy of loot types would be implemented, from basic work-a-day stuff which would also be found in shops, to drop-only stuff which would be a bit better than the general stuff, to boss-only which would be better yet. Special mobs could still drop special loot, but these mobs should wander and spawn in different places to prevent camping.

I like the idea of giving MOBs a loot level which would be used in comparison to the level of the players attacking it (use the highest level attacking) to determine the quality of the loot. this would need to be seperate from the MOBs own level to allow flexibility. This would also remove the need for the level caps discussed in this thread.

As to static content, that is probably best managed by hard caps both at low and high levels. To preserve a degree of realism, you could require entry to these places via a key gained from talking to an NPC. This key would only be given if the player was within level range of the static and would be non-duplicatable. Also, if any player in an team (or by themselves) that attack a MOB inside the static are outside the levvel range, the MOB should give no xp and drop no items. This would prevent the use of warps and the like to bypass the entry requirement.

I think FC need to open up this issue to full discussion. At the end of the day, it is their game, but I think that much useful input and feedback can be gained from the players.
