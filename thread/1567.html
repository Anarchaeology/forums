Blade Corp. Official Recruitment!
Good evening ladies and gentlemen and let me thank you for your interest in Blade Corp. We are a mercenary corporation established on Rubi-Ka with two major goals in mind: Providing top-quality services to the citizens of Rubi-Ka and providing great employment opportunities of citizens of Rubi-Ka. Because this is not an advertisement message, let me skip to the second one.

As stated above, Blade Corp is a mercenary corporation, a leader in its field in the entire Galaxy. We have part taken in many conflicts (and some peaceful contracts as well) throughout the Universe and are now proud to announce our presence on Rubi-Ka. We are currently seeking members of all kinds to fill our many departments. There are no age (ooc: level), gender, race, profession and experience limitations. All were looking for is people with great attitude and willing to make some extra cash. Of course knowledge of your area of expertise is greatly appreciated and will be beneficial in your advancement through our ranks, but it is not required as we have professional trainers that will help you achieve all that you can be. We are officially a neutral organization in the conflict, but consist of three main divisions to better suit our costumer and employee needs: Neutral, Omni-Tek and Clan. Therefore you can join whatever division you feel more comfortable in and still proceed with your normal every day life and duties. As such, Omni-Tek employees will never be forced to work with Clan members, unless they personally wish to. We are also looking for people to fill up our leadership ranks, so citizens with such skills are highly welcome. Whoever you are and whatever your background and life goals are, we have a perfect place for you. For this, we have a highly developed organizational structure to better assign you to duties that you want to perform. Also mercenaries are usually mistaken for military people. While we do have several combat departments (such as armed forces and special forces) there are also plenty of peaceful duties you can perform. As an example, we have research departments, manufacturing plants, medical teams, protection and escort and so forth. Whatever it is that you like doing you can do it in Blade Corp and get paid for this! Also, as somewhat stated above, you will never be forced to work for the opposing side. So if you would like to meet new great people, do what you like doing and get paid for it please join Blade Corp now and discover the world of unlimited opportunities on this world. For more information about Blade Corp and an application for, please visit our HQ located at www.bladecorp.com. You can also get some information about us from the interview with our CEO  Major Marone, in the latest RBC (Rubi-Ka Broadcast Corporation) news report of their latest broadcast. Thank you all for your attention and we hope you can fulfill all your life goals together with Blade Corp. Have a nice day!

OOC: Blade Corp is mostly a role-playing organization with mostly adult-minded people that is involved with the story and other organizations (such as our on-going contract with RBC and their RKTV). We seek players of all kinds that simply want to have fun ang get involved with AO and its story line. Our website is undergoing a major re-construction right now so some areas are closed, but all public areas with information about us should be operational. Also our mail server is currently taken down for some changes as well, so please do not use any e-mail addresses that end with @bladecorp.com, as your e-mail will simply get lost in cyberspace and will not be delivered to us. Instead, please use a temporary e-mail address we set up, which is bladecorp@home.com, for all your questions, suggestions and comments. Again, thanks all and have a nice day!
