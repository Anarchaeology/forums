Soleet
ooc) *laughs* This made me recall a roleplay idea a friend and I had when we first started playing AO...

We were going to go with a sort of pirates-of-the-future thing, and demand small amounts of cash. Ultimately, we decided not to do it. Not because we had thought far enough ahead to what Funcom might have to say about harrassment. But because in the roleplay vs. be-a-good-sport, we knew we were straddling the fence, and we didn't want to totally ruin someone else's fun. People expect to be attacked in PvP areas, they don't expect to have to cough up cash or items, or be verbally harangued.

Yes, it's PvP, and yes, it's a roleplaying game. I think your idea sounds a bit extreme, though. And easily interpreted as using roleplay/pvp as an excuse to equip the 75 character.

Oh, and using phrases like "gang-raped"? You'd want to be careful with your audience on that one, because not everybody likes to hear stuff like that, RPG or no... that's why there's a harrassment policy. Besides which, if Funcom allows one person or group of persons a little leeway in the name of roleplaying, it makes it that much more difficult to enforce their policy against the real jerks.

So... maybe you should find some roleplay alternatives, if roleplay was what you were after
