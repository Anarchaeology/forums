OFFICIAL response please.
I am going to bring up the point of high level hunting yet again.

Recently in game one thing I hear continually is complaints about lack of hunting areas and people expressing wishes to do something other than missions.

This issue has being never been addressed since launch, the team missions have been introduced granted, but to very mixed feelings. Gaute stated a long long time ago that the high level game was being looked at and changes would be made, but where are they?

Funcom you have given us a huge and beautiful world to roam around, yet our activities are confined to a few small pockets like BS Home, and Avalon, what an enormous and unjustifiable waste!

Many many players leave the game through boredom as after lvl 120 missions are the only valid way to progress. Personally speaking Im not sure I can stand another mission, single or team.

Give us areas to hunt in where the spawn rate is decent and the XP reward equal to missions and the world of Rubi-Ka will become the diverse place you envisioned it to be.

This is an issue which in my mind and I know many others minds is urgent to prevent the death our guilds and the friendships we have made, through players leaving out of sheer boredom.

Please tell us what you envision for high lvl hunting and what direction you plan to take it. Give us hope that something is being done.
