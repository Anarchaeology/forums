Player ***** is too low/high...
Ok, I'll admit it. A level 200 ganking a level 75 sucks.
Also a Title 6 average guild attacking a Title 2-3 average guild's tower sucks.

If you really want to have limits on players being able to target
players fine. I personally think its too constrictive, but I
understand the need to protect those level 75 Apprentice Traders
in Tir arena. (ok ok cheap shot)

Unfortunately this allows a couple small exploits:
1. Guilds are using low level "spies" to camp way inside a camp's
defenses and report on who's who, who's where, etc.

2. Low level players are attacking high level player's towers, and
the high level players are unable to defend against them.

Sure eventually guilds call other guilds to bring in lower levels to
do the dirty work for them, but often they lose a bunch of towers
and turrets while they stand and watch a bunch of level 100-
140s have their way with the towers.

This especially affects guilds that restrict membership and only
have high level players.

I suggest if anyone attacks a tower, they should be automatically
fair game to anyone of any level in the tower's guild for as long
as they remain in 25% (and vice versa of course)
