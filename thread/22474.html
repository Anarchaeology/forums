I'm not a GM, but I will attempt to give you my thoughts about the mezpet.

When attacking, it wipes the hatelist of the mob.
When teaming, wiping a mob's hatelist means that whoever hits it net time will be attacked by the mob. Not good if the the NT is the one hitting it next (with nuke or pstols or whatnot). For solo use, set it to guard yourself, and it will take care of adds or if your pet looses aggro.


summary:

For team, don't cast it unless you're REALLY skille at using it and targetting the right mob at the riht time. One mistake and a whole team can be wiped as result.

For solo, it's great for selfprotection in case the mob finds you more tasteful then your pet.
