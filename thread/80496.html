Players Attitudes
I don't know if anybody will read this from Funcom, and this is the 1st time I have felt strongly enough to post to a forum regarding peoples attitudes, but here goes.

Having come from DAOC as a player. -I have been amazed at now rude, aggressive and unfriendly people are in the game. Obviously, not everybody is like this, and I have encountered some really nice people who want to help both starting players, and are friendly genuine people who want to do well but obviously are behaving like human being's. and not frenzied aggressive Level Junkies!...

Funcom you really need to implement a Player charter outlining good behaviour in the game., as it will be a shame to see a fantastic game develop a reputation as technically brilliant but dominated by unfriendly people.
On DAOC I have encountered a few idiots and unfriendly people, but on the whole, its a suppoprtive, friendly environment - to be honest i wont say AO is at the present time, and that a shame.
I want to recommend this game to my other DAOC friends, but think they to, like me, will be amazed at the online playing attitude of so many, but fortunately (if that day comes I will quit) not everybody!

We all need to remember treat people the way you want to be treated back...Lets develop the AO community into one where people want to play. not one, where you are open to abuse or unfriendliness.

A supporter still of Funcom and AO. but people need to develop more maturity and understanding...Dont let the bad ones spoil it for the genuine decent people out there

My Theory
I think that the predominate reason why people tend to be rude in this game is that they are frustrated. I bet if you took a tally you'd find that the people that still enjoy the game and have fun playing it are the more helpful ones, and the rude people tend to be the ones still seeking the coolness of their character. Frustrated that their character sucks, they keep striving for the next level in the vain hope that it will be the level their character becomes cool. People are also frustrated that certain issues have yet to be resolved, despite their existance during beta, and then release. Lag is frustrating, warping mobs is frustrating, link deadness is frustrating, spending months online trying to get a single item that is supposed to be one of the defining charactersistics of your character (Think PPPE or Grid Armor) is frustrating, trade skills are frustrating (Finding the peices for the useful stuff is VERY time consuming). It would seem that in AO there is generally a huge time committment for the items that are really sought after, and that is frustrating. These items should have some difficulty, but waiting for a certain monster to spawn when the planets are aligned, at the 3rd minute after dawn for the .01% chance that he may drop the item you are after, is just plain frustrating! (Yes, I know its a bit much, but try finding Grid armor, I hear is about that bad)

Further sources of frustration are game mechanic issues that players feel need to be addressed yet funcom doesn't seem to comment on. Look at the "Fix Mutli-Weild" threads in the ADV forum..LOTS of people are frustrated that its in fact possible to do LESS damage weilding two guns then it is to weild one. It would appear that many things in AO have gone through cycles of becoming better, and then being weakened because they become too powerful, or the mechanics of the game change because something becomes too powerful. A guy in my guild wanted to have his Martial Artist use a shotgun because at one time the MA's crit buff worked with shotguns...so he spent 3-5 levels getting enough IP to be able to equip the gun...only to find out that the rules had changed and the crit buff no longer worked with shotguns! Needless to say he wasn't too pleased.

One thing I miss from EQ is the ability to just randomly give out helpful buffs. In EQ you could hand out a runbuff and not think twice about it, because there was no limit to the amount of buffs a character could have. In AO your NCU limit the amount of beneficial buffs you can have. I'm sure this is done for playbalance issues, but it suddenly makes randomly buffing people a hassle. Now both sides have to worry about whether or not the receipeint has enough NCU free for the buff, as well as any other nanos they want on them. Another difference is that in EQ mana was free, and came back fairly fast on its own. In AO it is theoretically still free, but comes back at a rate of something on the order of the contraction and implosion of our universe. Sure I can use kits, but now that costs me money...which personally isn't a big deal, but it reduces the desire to be kind to others.

As far as guilds go...there are some that exist only to get its members gear or xp.. those are the guilds that use questionable practises and generally don't have a place for lower lvl people. Then there are other guilds which care about their members, but people are generally busy in AO trying something, since it seems most everything takes a good deal of time once you get to be higher in lvl, so they might not be able to help the lower lvl members out too often. My guild has an excellent balance of the later, and are always a great source of information and help. Send me a tell if you'd like to talk about it...although we're clan, so you'd have to leave your omni ways behind
