Weapons Idea
I have been thinking about the many different weapons in AO and made the realization that maybe 15% of them are actually used past level 20. Why level 20? Beggars can't be choosers when you start out.

OK after level 20 most characters are dedicated to a certain weapon like the Stigma or Nova Flow or Masseur as examples.

What does this mean? You go to a shop and look for one or two weapons you can use. Every player is doing it. We all know what the crappy weapons are. Who wants a rifle that at QL65 does 12 to 36 damage with a 24 crit bonus and has a 1.2/1.5 attack/recharge and a clip of 8? Not me. Why? Because there exists weapons with the same use requirements that are doing 4 to 6 times the damage at almost the same speed.

The last part of the problem is that weapons are not profession specific. That covers it pretty well.

So I have a suggestion on how to fix the weapons problem.

1. Developers sit down with a spread sheet of ALL the weapons for each QL and determine valid minimum and maximum damage, critical bonus and speed. This is very important.

2. Decided what weapons will be available from QL 1 to 200. These will be the most common and represent the average weapons of the game. Ranging from sad to very good. You need weapons that are jokes but not as many as we have now. These weapons will be available for all classes.

3. Decided what weapons will be only available from QL 1 to 100. These will range from not so good to great but not uber. These weapons will be divided by profession.

4. Decided what weapons will be only available from QL 101 to 200. These will range from not so good to great but not uber. These will also have a level requirement attached of no less than the QL - 20. This will help with over equipping but still allow you to strap on a better weapon if want to. Also they will have a profession requirement.

5. Decided what weapons will be only available form QL 50 to 150. These will range form good to awesome. Just short of uber. But the cut off at 150 will mean that dedicating to one of these may cost you later in IP's. These weapons will carry some type of profession, level and/or title requirement as they will be aimed at the twinkers whether modest or extreme. Again the penality of using such a awesome weapon will be in IP's. These weapons could have as many as 5 requirements to use.

6. Determine what weapons will be available from QL 1 to 10. These are starter weapons and will vary from all others as they will be very effective with low skill requirements. Just nice choices for someone that is unsure how to get started with a new character.

7. Determine what weapons will be available from QL 190 to 200. These weapons will be rewards for high level players and as such have a level requirement of 190+ and profession requirement attached. These will be with out question the BEST read as uber weapons in game and be monster drop or chest find only. I see one weapon for each class with requirements that reflect the course of skill development that class should have taken. So if you chose a different path to bad.


OK I have been ranting for a bit so lets look at the next section of weapons that I think should be added to the game. A weapon should have one or two upgrade slots in it like implants do. Example: E-Beamer has a clip of 20 and recharge and fire rate of 1.5 and fling shot special attack. So I want a larger clip and burst. So I buy the bursting upgrade for LOTS of credits and have a Weapons Smither install it and bang I have a E-Beamer with fling and burst. I then have him install the large clip option and because I was broke from the burst upgrade I could only get a lower QL clip upgrade. So I have a clip of 27 now.

I agree this could allow for some really uber weapons to be created but remember that once upgraded it is just like an implant and cant be changed. So decided you made a bad choice and quest what you have to start over with a new non-upgraded gun.

OK now on to skill requirements for use.

1. All weapons will have primary requirement.

2. Some weapons will have a secondary requirement if they qualify as awesome weapons from the QL 50 to 150 group as example.

3. Each special will have the proper skill requirement.

You must have the primary and secondary requirement to equip the weapon. If you do not have the special skill needed for the special it will be disabled.

OK that is all I have to rant about. Just an idea that I feel could help the game. Actually just rehashing the weapons to get rid of the useless ones would be nice.
