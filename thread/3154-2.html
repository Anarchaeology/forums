... the point of this thread was about the dungeon. Let's not lose focus please. I went to the dungeon (Longest Road at the Omni Mine). Don't remember the exact cords, but just follow the road until you come upon a pond in the road, then turn left. Thank God for the Amnesty, because if you're clan, you'd never get through unless you have UBER speed. Roughly five guards at the enterance, and a couple of T-60s and Slayerdroids. The dungeon is in the building with a Foreman sign. First room has about eight mobs The mob hp isn't as insane as the SoM, but it's still up there. I couldn't kill a grey there with my alpha right off, but nailed it to about 1/4 of it's life. Don't bother going unless you have a crat, an agent and a doctor. The agent can take the no drop rilfe(off the Gun Beetle). There's also a form there that has some use to the crat class....

BTW Soldier, 70 level (lots and lots of play time)
