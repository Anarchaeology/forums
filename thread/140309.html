The meeting with Doctor Anderson and the Truth
My sorrow and loss of my dear husband had been hard, and I finally decided to go to one of my trusted employees high up in Omni-Tek, for some answers to everything. Omni-Tek Chief Doctor Anderson, who had been my personal doctor for quite some time, had his office in a building in Omni Headquarter.

Passing sterile hallways and doctors, I finally arrived to his office. His room was dimmed and the curtains were pulled infront of the windows.

He stood up and took my hand gently.
"Its good to see you again Engelen"
"You too doctor. Looks like I manage to stay healthy...hope I dont ruin your job" I gave him a smile, but it was only halfhearted.
"Hehe. I see you are troubled. Mind sitting down?"
I pulled out a chair.
"So tell me, what is on your mind?"
"You havent heard? I lost Lsnake...he was killed.....I...dont know how or by who.."
"Sorry to hear that. I assume the ressurection went well?"
"Thats exactly why I came to you doctor. He wasnt ressurected."
Anderson raised his eyebrows and looked over his glasses.
"Not ressurected? But everyone is ressurected, the technology is about 100% safe and complete. If he is not ressurected, his DNA will at least lay dormant in the system waiting for a trigger. Shouldnt be harder than just activating his personal DNA code..."
"You mean there is hope?" I lifted my head.
"Why of course! We simply ressurect him manually. Let me find his DNA. His full name please..."
"Caldon, Salvador Lsnake"
A puzzled look appeared on his face.
"I...uhm to be honest..I cant find him. You sure it is the right name?"
"Yes of course I am sure, I have been his girlfriend for well over a year now. Try again please..."
A moment of silence occured as Anderson punched in the name over and over.
"Engelen...there is nothing there. No Match."
A tear ran down my cheek.
"But...no...he has to be there?! You know him too, you have met him before, you know he exists!"
"I know...come now. Maybe there is something wrong with the database. I`ll go check in the backupserver, all people and registered creatures are stored there. You need something to drink?"
"No....I`m fine..."

Anderson came back 5 minutes later. Not a word was said for the first minute, he sat down on his chair, laid his glasses on his desk and folded his hands, resting his head upon it. I knew what would come, I didnt say a word.
He broke the silence.

"He isnt there."

I felt the salt taste of my tears running down, as I could do nothing more than sitting there helpless and biting the truth.

"It cant be...no...WHY?! HOW?!"
"Engelen, I dont know. Something is not right. I DO have complete access to the database, no creature are stored without my access. I know your husband has existed. You remember the medical check we had on him after his fall from the Broken Shores stairs? I remember checking his DNA structure, everything was intact back then.
Even though someone may die permanently, they would be in the archive, so that we can have proof of their existence. Your husband was there before. He isnt anymore...its like he never existed..
"How is that possible?!" Desperatly I begged him for answers, something to ease my pain.
"Well....there are only one way for one to vanish completly. Someone has to delete him. Manually. It requires a password and an identification, even I do not have that.
"WHO DOES?! TELL ME! I WILL GET THAT SON OF A BITCH RIGHT NOW!" I couldnt control my anger anymore, and Anderson jumped when I shouted it out.
"Please Engelen. I know I cant make you calm down...I dont understand this my self either. But..to be honest, and you know I am, I really dont know my self. Someone probably has to be authorized by Ross himself for an access like this..."
"...So there is..no way..right..?"
"I`m afraid not..please let me make this a top priority case, I will try my best to get some answers, but I cannot promise anything."
"I know...sorry Doc...I..need to go.." Feeling how my stomach turned itself upside down I stumbled out of the chair and towards the door. Anderson quickly got up and followed me, as he always used too.
"Engelen" he said as we slowly walked towards the frontdoor. "Please take care of yourself. I cannot give you any answers now, I wish I could, but I wont lie. I dont know anything. But if this comes out, it could be a problem, and I think we could use that as a point when we are taking it higher up. For now...try to keep going. I dont know if your husbands DNA is completly ereased, or if he is just deleted from the database. If his original DNA is stored somewhere, we could probably ressurect him...although he would not be what he was. No Memories of you, no experience, he would be what he was when he came to Rimor..so..but just so you know.."
"Okay....thanks doc...thank you for your time..and everything you are doing for me.." I took my hands around him and sniffed, and he stroke my back.
"Its gonna be okay. You are strong. I am sure something will come up..."
"Okay...goodbye doc.."

I released my self and turned around, walking towards Trade as tears slowly ran down.

Why...and who...would do such a thing...to my husband..

I dried my eyes and passed the guards at the gate to Trade.

"Have a nice day, and remember, Omni-Tek is your friend" he said.
