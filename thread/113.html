My second character is a Bureaucrat and I wanted to give some ideas even though I have minor experience with this profession at this time.

This is a repost of a few of my ideas that others may have missed if they didn't check the archive.

#8 Leadership Nano line beyond experience buff  Buffs speed initiatives. Give a debuff line that lowers enemies speed initiatives.

#9 Call in Sniper Line  Summons an npc sniper that is cloaked and shoots at an enemy for a short duration, say 15-45 seconds. Is not controllable and leaves after target is dead or after its duration is over or if killed. Can only have one at a time. It will chase enemies if needed to get in range. Takes a search or high perception to see the sniper.

#11 Call in Medic Line  Summons a npc medic that tries to heal the team  Will not fight, can be killed. Lasts for short duration.

#12 Emergency Evacuation  This might step on some toes but I think a high level bureaucrat could have something like this. Not a teleport though. Causes a transport to arrive and automatically pick up the entire team and take them to the Bureaucrats insurance terminal or maybe to a political building in one of the cities. Its an evac. The transport or ship should just appear and hover for a moment and then people load to the appropriate destination. Could be a 10-15 second evac. The 10  15 seconds is waiting for the transport to arrive.

#13 Appropriate Buff Skills  Initiatives, Psychology, Comp Lit, Tutoring, and nano Energy Pool.

#14 Imprison line  Sends out an appropriate aligned sides small police force  3 npcs that have a chance of removing the target from the area. They try to capture the enemy and if successful the npc is gone and normal experience awarded for the capture of the criminal. =) (PvP, low chance of success but can imprison the target in the appropriate jail.) Possibly high level line of nanos. Can only cast every 10 mintues or something like that.

#15 Negates running nano programs on enemies through pyschological guile. =) Your so charming you made them forget the programs they were running. Nano line of course.


Auras

Implement Auras which are buffs for a group, possibly even ac bonuses or anything appropriate. These auras are actually programs running on the Bureaucrat but when people join their group they receive the benefit of the aura. When they leave the group they lose the buff. How you make them work is your choice. I just thought it might be an interesting idea others in the forum could brainstorm from. They could help in nano regeneration rates or something similar as well.



Implement Tax

This line was inspired by one of the posters here. This Tax Nano could be a bureaucrat only buff and what this does is in a group it skims off more money than normal from looted corpses. Say it starts at %10 and the line ends at %30. This gives the bureaucrat a tax break so to speak. It could be extended that the corpse just gives more credits overall for the whole group instead of just the bureaucrats. Let it stack if there are two bureaucrats in a group or more. Could be a way to make bureaucrats stick together as well. Maybe have it max out at 100% if there are lots of bureaucrats in a group.


I think these ideas are worth discussing. Mabye they will inspire other ideas as well.

Cordially,

I have been playing a little bit of Dark Ages of Camelot. There is a class known as Theurgist. Now this class has some unique abilities in Camelot. It can summon multiple pets extremely quickly that last from 20 to 30 seconds and act as damage spells. They run up to the target, swarm it and kill it. Works great, and is one of the most powerful classes in the game (My opinion of course). Personally even if it wasnt powerful I love being able to summon 3 types of pets and swarm an enemy. I get a lot of inner joy from doing so.

The pets summoned take a percentage of the characters magic power. I think it is around 12%. So if you have enough time and the monster focuses on the pets attacking it, which it normally does, you could summon about 8 to 9 pets in a row. These summoned pets are uncontrollable as well, they just appear, chase, attack and harass the enemy until the target is dead or their duration runs out. It kind of acts as a taunt, DoT, Nuke all in one. I really love it. =)

Now the Bureaucrat is the leader of men, but currently its the leader of droids. Maybe a nano wipe is in order. Drop the robot lines and their funkiness. Reminds me of a simi-engineer. Forget the tweaking pets idea like an engineer. Let Bureaucrats summon Cannon Fodder pets. Now I am unsure which pet is appropriate. In Camelot the Theurgist is an elementalist and summons from the 3 areas of earth, water, and air. Now AO is way different. Maybe they could summon Humanoid npcs?, I guess they could summon some type of robots but that is more like an engineer in my opinion, or they could summon mutants. Humanoids make the most sense all the way.

How about being able to summon a few different choices.

Summon Nano Tech - Summons an npc that attacks the target until dead either completing its mission or dying in the process. Attacks for 40 seconds total if possible. The Nano Tech uses Nanos to attack at long range with multiple types of damage. After its duration or the death of the target the NT bows with praying hands to the Bureaucrat and disappears. 20% percent of nano pool used to summon.

Summon Enforcer  Summons a brawler that goes hand to hand with the target until dead, has the chance to stun opponents for short durations as its special. Attacks for 50 seconds or until dead. After the duration or the death of the target the Enforcer turns and bows to the Bureaucrat and disappears. 20% percent of nano pool used to summon.

Summon Soldier  Summons a soldier that wields a mid ranged weapon with burst capability. Attacks for 45 seconds and leaves. After its duration or the death of the target the soldier salutes to the Bureaucrat and disappears. 20% percent of nano pool used to summon.

These pets are uncontrollable. They attack a single target and will not acquire a second target. They come in, they do their job, and then they leave.

Now of course there are stims in this game that quickly regenerate nano pool so obviously this will have to be tweaked in some manner to make it work for AO.

Ok, I'm just going to paste this in here from the "Professions" forum instead of re-typing it all. THESE are changes I think would make us very valuable:

And what IS the big overhaul, Cosmik?

Is it going to be a little tweak here, a slight tug there? You said you play a 'crat...can't you see this class is FUBAR?

Right now, comp lit is BROKEN in shops..any trader will tell you that. Trade skills? You've barely got them implimented, and we honestly don't have the I.P.'s to toss into trade skills that barely work and haven't been there since day one anyways.

Concealment buffs? That's a mission thing, and we all know how borked conceal in missions get.

But here's my favorite. Exaggerated amounts of pets like before? Unless you REALLY up the pet damage..and I mean really really up it...then this is just a stupid nerf with no purpose or history. My red pet gets owned HARD by greens. I help it, I'm lucky to live. This isn't a matter of balance here. Nobody complained that the 'crat was overpowered in any way, shape, or form. This nerf is not beneficial to anyone, and all it does is make people feel even more sorry for us than they did before. Right now, pets are a total joke..and it's not pathing (though that is a problem), and it's not just damage (another big problem), and it's not just aggro (yet another big problem). It's the fact that our pets get killed easy, run around like idiots, and stand there to watch us die. Our pets ALSO have nothing special about them. No new graphics, no special abilities...nothing. Why do we even have a pet?

And the charm nerf. Pointless. There's no reasoning to this whatsoever that you can tell us that's believeable. Tell us how this made us overpowered. The charms don't even last more than 20 seconds! If anything, it was a chore to keep up with them..but for those that could, it was the only sense of crowd control that we had..and that IS what we're supposed to be, right? So where is it? Charming one mob for 20 seconds is NOT crowd control...it's just a surefire way to get a mob to hate you. Not to mention, you've handed out our nanos to other classes anyways. Who needs us?

Which brings it to grouping issues. What use are we in a group, Cosmik? What exactly do we bring to a group? Exp buff? wrong. Nobody wants it..nobody cares. It's not enough to do anything anyways.

Don't you see, Cosmik? This isn't a simple little tweak job. This isn't something you can just play around here with. You have THE most useless class in the whole game that's been waiting patiently for answers while you guys have beefed up all the common classes to godliness. We've gotten nothing, and the last thing we need is some minor tweaks mislabled as an overhaul. We need the real deal here.

But I won't leave you with nothing...if I'm going to complain, I should have solutions, right? So here goes...I've said this elsewhere, and I'll suggest it here:

Pets: Ditch the robot. Have us summon a random NPC type. Make it humanoid only...I don't care. Break up the different types into levels, so that as we level, we have the ability to summon self healing npc types and such. Of course, fix pathing and aggro.

Charm: If you want it to be one charm only, and put a psyche check on it..then fine. Extend the duration to much longer times. 15 minutes to an hour. If you want to exclude the pet while having a charmed mob, then fine. Give us that choice. This 20 second junk is useless.

Grouping: We're supposed to be leaders in a group..give us the means to do so. Give us fast casting, short duration, low ncu buffs that change group damage types and AC resistances. It would be our job to know what the group encountered, and to use the right means to aid the group to victory. Give us the same type of buff to modify crit hit percentages, but only group based.

And fix the mes/sleep lines, roots, and slows. They don't work right...this sliding thing is annoying.

And we need more DD's to fill in the huge gaps between levels. DD's we use become outdated and useless very quickly...mostly because of the casting times.

Oh, and a general suggestion...PLEASE upgrade the nano effects. They really are stale.

THAT would make a 'crat valuable..both solo and in groups. We would be wanted, unique, and viable in the world. Are you listening, Cos? Stop beefing up the big classes and pay attention to the least played, underpowered, and boring class in the game for a patch or two.

And lastly...get the departments working in the game! We're supposed to be one of the most RP classes in the game..but we have nowhere to do it...no reason for it at all. Personally, even if you just opened the departments and put in class specific mission terminals for install missions, that'd be a start. At least we could say we belonged to something for now.
