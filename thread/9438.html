AO is brilliant, can Funcom be too?
I must say first that I love playing AO.

Now for the "however"....However, this complex game with a detailed storyline, intricate world, and amazing graphics is thoroughly under-used. The creativity of this game seems to have completely ended after the concept was drafted. Like Funcom hired a genious, who created a masterpiece, then was fired by Funcom who took over this idea. Now, we sit on a brilliant concept that Funcom does not develop, nuture, expand. AO is a malnurished child.

I am a lvl 60 MP, Petrifying, I just today after two and a half months, read the AO story line. I am greatly impressed with it, as it convincingly explains the many aspects of Rubi-Ka. The mutants, the reason for the clans, the ICC, Sol Banking Co., the corporation wars, notum, the breeds, it is a well created artpiece. Tell me, why does it feel like Rubi-Ka is no more than a series of cubes with little real life?

-Omni Forest is supposed to be a few mere meters from the city of Omni-1, why do mutants never surprise us by entering the city?

-The devices that create the Suppression Field, why can't they be destroyed from time to time? Can you imagine how giddy we all would feel if we went into a shop for a med kit, then when we exited heard that *tick* *tick* countdown to being open to a pvp attack? ok, that's not so unbelieveable, I know, but now imagine the same thing happening in Omni-Trade. I mean, buildings have been destroyed, why can't also the suppresion field devices?

-I read in another thread the concept of a so-called Mama mutant, who would protect her mutant friends, a concept similar to that lifebleeder at NW mines in Lush Fields. Why can't such a Mama mutant rally her "children" to attack cities? Can you imagine how giddy we all would feel to see a crowd of 100 mutants at lvl 80 with their lvl 120 Mama approach the players at Bronto Burger where the monsters had no regard for clan or Omni?

-Funcom recently said (not an exact quote) that it would be difficult to create for us the option to gain xp in a non-combat way. They said that they could not find a balance of risk and reward. That is a cop-out in my opinion. now I love combat, and nearly all of Rubi-Kans love combat, but it's because it's the ONLY way to lvl up and, well, play the game at all. if you choose not to lvl up, you do not get to see all of Rubi-Ka, and if you don't fight something, you don't lvl up. The thing is, having a non combat option would expand the player base. Want to hear my ideas on this? of course you do

1. There should be *missions* where fully completing the goal does not require combat. There can be an option to fight, but it should be an option in such a mission. you enter a mission area, like any other mission, but this time you have been given as a mission item a nano crystal that is not profession-specific. As part of the mission goal, you upload the program, however, this is a specially designed program tailored exclusively for it's intended target, thus making it a useless program for any other reason. So, you must fight, sneak, blitz, avoid, find your way through a maze, or wait (the target comes to you, after a certain amount of time. you must camp a location for him to arrive) for the target, you cast the program, the mission is over, cue the music. the risk is in getting to the target, many things stand in your way, but, now this is important, you do not NEED to fight to complete the mission.

2. In-game events, GM created, that give exp to the players. For instance, we get a message on the Broadcast that an item has been stolen from someone with high influence, like OT's CEO. Say, he's had his statue of the Goddess Buffy Sanders (or whatever that thing is) stolen. No one knows where it is, but the perpetrator is caught, so, lost on Rubi-Ka is this item, the player who finds it gets exp. Being as vast as Rubi-Ka is, the risk is in the amount of TIME spent looking for this item, and the dangers along the way from being in political zones, meeting mutants, etc. since there is only one of the statues, only one person can find it. Obviously, this type would require such events happening all the time, and with that, more resources from Funcom (this could also be incorporated as a mission-type, with the item not being in a mission area, but somewhere on rubi-ka). would be tough to find, and the great fun is in knowing you are on a race with, potentially, the entire player database.

3. This one will not likely be included in AO *ever*. as a matter of fact, the likelyhood of it being in a MMORPG within the next seven or eight years is not likely. But it would prove, in my opinion, to be so huge that it would consume most of our in-game time, much as in real life. Children. (this is going to get a response and a half) Why can't our characters breed? Now I'm not trying to turn this game into and X-rated game, I feel this is a necessary concept. Three of the four breeds on Rubi-Ka can reproduce, why can't we? Ok, before you get confused, the "child" would be treated as a pet of sorts, they would require attention, they would lvl in the same manner that our own characters do, we would distribute his ip for him/her. The risk is in the potential for our child to die before the age of 20 (resurrection doesn't work before 20 or after 76 or so years of age). Losing the child would be a loss for us, not emotional, but that "child" would be a fighter alongside us, we could distribute his ip and develop him as a customized weapon. they would carry items like us, wear armor, etc. I'm not going to design the whole concept right here in this thread but can you see where I'm going with this? a male character (player) would procreate with an npc, like in a shop or something ( a use for the red light district? hehe) then for ever year the child lives, you gain such and such xp, the first year not so much, the second more, the third more, the 20th the most. a female player would procreate with a male npc (the procreation not being visual, for rating concerns) .this would require a "parenting" skill we would have to develop in order to keep our children progressing. Atroxes, maybe you should be completely out of luck, or maybe you would pick up a test tube baby from a genetical engineering lab instead of procreating. anyway, your child would not be able to pass you in ability, and after awhile your child would leave to "be on his own". it would also be incredibly expensive, the risk being losing your investment, the reward would be exp gain from each lvl the child raises. The cost of the child would increase as he progressed, but as he progressed he would become more and more valuable to you. a child should not be able to solo in pvp either, that would be far too easy. maybe the children could fight each other? like a dog fight or something? brutal you say? just remember that this is Rubi-Ka, not Earth.

I like your idea of having players as mission targets, but there are problems with that idea. If the player is not available, the mission is worthless. For instance, if I were a mission target, as soon as I knew about it I would make sure to stay in a strong suppression area until the mission timer ran out. Also, such a mission could be a "revenge" tactic against someone you don't like, if you want to be nasty you could set a fat reward for hunting the target, the target would end up running away, you then set the timer for say, 30 days so he ends up limited for that amount of time, or until he is killed. Also, such a thing would be abused easily: I create a mission to kill my buddy, my buddy saves, we enter a pvp area, I kill him and get mission reward. now he creates a mission to kill me, by the end of the day we each have 90 tokens more than the previous day.

I agree thoroughly that pvp should be more enticing and I do not look forward to having it nerfed in 13.6 (or so I'm told). There should be more to pvp than gaining a title.
