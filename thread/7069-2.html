Learning to use walls and obstacles (running and hiding behind them) in missions or distance out in the wilderness to protect yourself during the period between when you cast calm and when the monster actually stops attacking is an art form in itself. I am a level 127 trader and I use my own calm spell dozens of times each day, so I dont say this out of speculation but out of actual prolonged use of the spell. Calm is a winner.
