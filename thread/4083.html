PvP 'caves' ?
So, I'm a curious guy, and if I'm flying around and spot a cave I havent been in before I like to check it out . .

so far, all I've found is a 'static' missions. not so fun.

"hmmm . . let me think . . I've already done 1000 missions
in this same floorplan but I'd really like to do another one
even tho there is no final reward and no chance of a token .."

um, yeah.

I've actually run into other players in these static-mission caves too.
We say hi, ask eachother if we've found anthing new or special,
always "nope" .. get bored and leave.
So it got me thinking, what if you made some of these caves
empty with no (or very few) mobs and then made them 0% zones.

now, when wandering into a cave,
i might be in for another surprise besides
'will it be human or monster mobs?'

players could claim the caves for themsleves,
and hold them / use them as hideouts / whatever
for as long as they could defend them.

No, this might not be a huge feature,
but i dont assume it's difficult to set 0%/75%/etc
and the maze of twists and turns would allow
for some fun lazer-tag kindof PvP

perhaps new floorplans could be designed with this in mind
with bigger rooms to give the ranged-players space to use
their tactics too.

massive caverns .. old abandoned warehouses .. mines .. etc

?
