General information and starting tips from Martin Amor
Dear testers,

Firstly, thanks a lot for taking the time to test Anarchy Online. It's really nice to see so many people playing in our world now after a long time with empty playfields.

I would like to inform you about a few things and write some starting tips for you.

First of all - right now we are running our servers in full debug mode - meaning that the performance leaves a bit to desire. We are logging every little action and state in the game in order to find the last bugs. When we have found and fixed the last critical bugs we will begin to remove some of the debugging features and the performance will increase. Until then I hope you understand that you sometimes lag a little when playing.

Another form of reported lag is client-side lag, which is low framerate when you are for instance in some of the cities and there is a lot of characters around you. We have not fully optimized the beta4 client you are playing yet, but there are a some things you can do to improve the situation.

If you look under "options" (click OPT icon in lower right corner or press F10) you will find some menues:

Environment:

* Liquid animation - OFF to increase performance in areas with water
* Client wildlife - OFF to increase rendering performance by not rendering birds, rabbits etc.
* Realistic sunrays - OFF to increase rendering performance by not creating "lens flares" from the sun
* Realistic clouds - OFF to increase rendering performance by not rendering complex clouds
* Simple clouds - OFF to not even render the simple form of realistic clouds
* Ground-shadows - OFF to turn off shadows on the ground. This helps when there is a lot of characters and monsters around you
* Show buff/tracers/nano effects etc - OFF to increase rendering performance by not showing our "special effects"


Visual:

* Ground texture size - setting this to medium or low will increase loading time of textures and is advised when playing on video cards with 16 MB or less texture memory
* Other texture size - same as ground texture res.
* Texture resolution - Set to normal for using 16 bit textures (default) or high to use 32 bit textures (only recommand on high end cards)
* Use Continuing level of detail - NOT ENABLED YET
* Use CLOD for anim - Not fully enabled yet, but turning it on will decrease the number of polygons on characters and monsters around you.
* View distance - Set to lower in order to get higher framerate. This will make you see shorter but will increase framerate a lot! Esp. in the cities.
* Ground quality - set to lower to use less polygons on the ground.
* Ground full quality radius - set to lower to use less texture memory on ground


We will enable more options for you in the final client (launch) Hope this helps until we have done a bit more optimization.

Enjoy Anarchy Online,

Martin Amor
Lead Programmer.

Hmm

After figuring out the info in the top of your post (based on previous experiences with AC and EQ), I was all excited and when I logged off for the server reset, I had to post my success.

(Guess I should start reading these sticky posts huh *grins sheepishly*)

Anyway, altering the settings as stated above helps a lot, but the biggest single performance increase in towns still comes from setting the distance that you can see to a much lower number. Cut the distance by about 30% and your frame rate goes from 3 or 4 to about 25 to 30. On higher end machines this alone might be all that's required in order to make cities playable.

One suggestion for the final version: Instead of having the OPT values default to such a high number, maybe the viewable distance should be set with a lower default.
