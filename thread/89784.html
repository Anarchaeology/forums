What is stupid is a MOB with a sniper rifle that is not only hitting you with bullets, but parrying the attacks from your sword. You can't have both the advantage of range and the extra ability to deflect melee attacks. You can't look down the barrel of a gun and simultaneously use that gun to knock my weapon aside.

Equipping any ranged weapon ought to automatically take 10,000 off your Parry skill.

Likewise, if you are parrying my attack, you ought not to be doing damage at the same time. You used up your turn in the combat queue to make me not hit. That's what Riposte is for, taking my attack and turning it back on me so that I damage myself instead of you. A much more difficult move, but once again, one that negates your ability to do damage during that combat turn.

Seeing "Parry, parry, parry, parry, parry, parry, parry, ..." floating over some of these MOB's heads should by all right mean that they are not doing any damage. They are spending all their time deflecting their attackers. If they start fighting, then the parrying ought to stop.
