(The follow story ended up longer then I figured, /me smiles)

Hello Everyone. My name is Traykor. I'm a male Solitus Adventurer, in excellant health, but I've recently came to Rubi-Ka with my sister, Traylia, and a brother, Traylok.

One day all three of us woke up on Rubi-Ka, we each knew each other, but for some reason couldn't remember where we came from, where we were, or how we'd gotten here. It didn't take long and we discovered we were on a planet called Rubi-Ka and were in a dessert near small town of Newland.

Shortly after we had made it to Newland City, we were wondering around learning the layout, when we happen upon a structure with large doors, very bright lights inside the doors, and a tall pipe looking piece extending far, far up into the sky. We stood there a while watching people go in and come out of the various doors, so we decided to try entering the door with a large ICC above it.

Instantly we were in a completely different place, I later found out some people were calling it Andromeda. I decided I wanted to explorer a little, but my brother and sister just wanted to stay near the doors with bright light inside, which my sister had heard someone call a Whompa.

When I returned from explorering Andromeda a bit to the front of the Whompas, I was unable to find my sister or brother anywhere, and after many hours of waiting, I decided to try going back to Newland to see if they returned there. Upon returning to Newland through the Whompas, which by the way are a wonderful way to travel, I was unable to find my brother and sister.

I decided that if I was to go search more I needed to get some weapons and armor as it seems like everyone wore some kind of armor and most carried weapons. I figured it might not be safe to go searching unarmed. Since, I had very little money I figured I wouldn't be able to afford much, but saw lots of people around different machines or terminals that had both a figure of a single person or multiple people. I decided to try the one with a single person since I was alone. I ended up reaching a mission from someone running the machine to go find a piece of leather body armor and to describe it once I found it. The only problem I had was the person running the machine wanted me to kill everyone in the building where the armor was. They said "Kill everyone to cover your tracks, since death isn't perminant anyway", I just laughed at the machine, but I needed money and the machine was offering my money to do this, plus I'd get a nice sword when I finished it. Someone near me heard me laughing and asked what was so funny, when I told him, he simply said "Ya, that's right, just stop past an insurance terminal before you leave on your mission, it will insure you against death."

Since I had already seen many strange things here, I found an insurance terminal before I found where my mission was, it took most of my remaining money to get insured, but now I was insured against death, or whatever that means.

It was easy finding my mission and was just about finished with what it seems like searching all the rooms, when I was doubleteamed. I fought as hard as I could, but they two were too strong. In my last moments, I thought I had failed my brother and sister, but just as I thought I receive a fatal blow, I was all of a sudden back near the insurance terminal I had stopped at. I was so surprise I grabbed the first person I saw and asked how this is possible. They explained the how process of "death", that you insure yourself, then if you die a new body is assembled and you're soul and thoughts are drawn to the new body. They also explained how the reclaim terminals worked. They did say that after a while, you won't remember actually dieing, but only remember up to the last time you were at a insurance terminal. After know this, I got everything from reclaim and quickly headed back to the mission. This time I was able to finish the mission and receive both my reward money and the nice sword promised to me by the mission machine, but I also received a mission token. I later found out that I could join the clans, remain neutral, or become an employee of a company called Omni. Since I prefer the outdoors I decided to join the side of the clans.

Anyway, to make a long story shorter, after enough missions to get fully armored and armed, I started off on my quest to find my brother and sister. After many weeks and months, I happened across my sister one day again in Andromeda. After many tears and hugs, I found out that Traylok wanted to know how the Whompa worked and my sister didn't want to be left alone, so she tagged along with Traylok. My brother Traylok was alway building his own robots, so it's only natural for him to want to know how a whompa works. Both of them ended up in Omni-Trade, where they both went through much the same things I did. Although I found out that both my brother and sister had become Omni employees. Traylia said that Traylok was very happy working for Omni, but she didn't like it and wanted to stay with me. We agreed that Traylia would bring Traylok to Andromeda so we could talk things over. After a very long and sometimes very heated discussion, we decided that Traylia and I would belong to the clans, but Traylok liked where he was, so he decided to remain a Omni employee continuing his Engineering career.

Traylia has done very well for herself so far. I've very impressed how she's able to take on everything with just her bare hands. She says it's very easy for a Martial Artist, since they train to be a powerful force with and without the aid of weapons.

Anyway, I digress, all of you now know my family's story since waking up that one morning outside of Newland in the desert. However, for some reason, our memories still haven't returned from before that fateful morning.

Traykor

(( Ooc: Palla, it isn't as hard as you might think. All it takes is one idea, and you can spin an entire story about your character from it.

So.. Palla is in Gridstream. Start from there... How did she become involved in the organization? Is she a DJ as well? A musician? Or does her talents lie more in planning parties and public relations?

And she's a clanner... has she been one all her life? Did she grow up in Tir or Athen, or did she come from another city or settlement?

Or... just write a piece of fluff and post it That's how my character Lisetta got her Ship of Fools thread started, and now it's snowballed into some sort of bizarre alien encounter. Start a thread, Palla walks into the Reet Retreat, headed to the usual Saturday night dance party. But someone bumps into her, making her spill her drink all over her brand new expensive Mannolos (not to be confused with the ancient Earth shoe designer, Manolo, of course).. Ack! She heads to the ladies' room to try and assess the damage, and overhears an interesting conversation...

So there's one idea, anyway, if you start it, people will usually pick up and carry on with the story
