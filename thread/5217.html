Spookymonster
Soleet
I think you are assuming too much, Mir. I don't think it's supposed to be a charm or calm at all.

From my experiences, and the experiences of others in my guild, the pet nullifies it's target's attacks for a second or two. I ran my tests in o-forest using some gray mobs, away from any adds. I would send in my lvl 67 mind-numb pet, Tigo!, to attack a bio. My other pets were all in behind mode, so they wouldn't attack. I stood just close enough to the mob so it could aggro me. This was to test if Tigo! affected the mob's hate list in any way:

- Tigo! zapped the mob
- Mob aggroed on me
- I received messages and animations indicating I was being attacked, but I wasn't damaged at all.
- Tigo! took what seemed like a 10 second break before attacking the mob again.
- Over and over again, the mob would try to attack me, but couldn't land a punch.
- After Tigo!'s 3rd attack (roughly 30 seconds), the mob finally laid a solid punch on me.

All through the test, I assumed the mob wasn't landing any punches because it was gray to me. I was expecting a charm or calm of some sort. Since my data didn't show either of those results, I assumed the pet was broken. It wasn't until I compared results with the rest of my guild that I saw what was really going on:

- They are built to aggro, even though they do no physical harm at all. A mind-numb pet would most likely aggro higher than a mlee pet of the same QL.
- If you have a melee pet of a higher QL than your mind-numb pet, most likely you won't see any benefit to the 3rd pet.
- The QL of the mob may have an affect on the length of time that the 3rd pet holds aggro. My tests with gray mobs seem to indicate that weaker mobs are aggroed more steadily than equal- or higher- level mobs.

Hope this clarifies things.
