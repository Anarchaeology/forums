Pet cost comparrissons and evaluation
This is a little evaluation for fellow engis and specifically for the proffessionals and FC Community team...

Ok, as an Engi, our main weapon is our bot. Even by FC's own admission. Now it's been proven in a number of tests that MP pets can out-damage engi pets and FC says this is by design. Fine. Crat pets seem to be about on even footing as well, but hasn't had as much testing. Crats can heal and buff thier pets, MP's can buff pets a LOT and have a heal pet, and we can use trimmers, buffs, and heals on our pets. MP's do best damage, we're supposed to do best tanking, and crats are, I think, supposed to be below both. Even assuming that's so, let's look at what it takes to do that for each prof.

Below I'll assume everyone is using the best they can for everything, as close to self-cast as possible. Listed reqs are shown for each skill (as per Antiguardians database) to make the pets, buff the pets, and heal the pets. You notice anything like me? Like how Eng needs twice the number of skills to use all of thier pet abilities? And how the IP for that is roughly 75% more then either of the other two? Or that only the Eng uses a dark blue skill? Or that the Eng will need the most extensive and conflicting set of implants? Or that, with implants and self-castable buffs alone (no items, since they're harder to come by) Only the Eng cannot self-cast the highest pet? (Due to implant conflicts and high skill reqs, you wind up about 50 points short) And that the highest Energy -> HP trimmer is beyond our reach to use even foregoing everythign else in implants (It requires the 951 MechEng, otherwise that's around 801)?

And yet, through all this, the Engi also has far less to bring to a team then the other two outside of pets. Crats can calm / root / nuke. MPs can buff nanoskills / nuke / debuff. We have reflect, but only if there are no soldiers in the group since they do it better.

Our pets are permenant rather then timed, but they also cost more nano to cast as well some credits, so that's not much of a bonus. Any competent MP/Crat can watch the pet timer to see about re-casting pets before a long fight if they're running out of juice, so that's not much of a penalty to them either.

Now let me get one thing very very clear here. I am not asking for a nerf to either Crats or MPs. I point out thier strengths as a point of reference since they are our fellow pet profs, that's all. I am asking simply that if we have to put in this much extra effort for our pet, then we should get a pet that's worth it! If we put in almost twice the effort, then we should get almost twice the pet power. That's the entire reason we don't bring much to a group besides the pet. Our pet is supposed to be the best overall bar none! But it's not. FC has already said they will not increase our pet damage. And they're dragging thier behinds on getting some sort of update on our Kami-bot. So what do we do with our pet? How can we make it worth while for our effort? And to Cosmik: If you need some fuel for the fire to get Devs to re-evaluate our pets, I hope this helps.

ok, so some ideas I've seen in here are:

Well here's a thought:
FC says the bot is the premier tank pet. And the aggression trimmer is supposed to help them hold aggro to tank, right?

Ok, now it doesn't work that great in teams and does OK solo if you've got a good up-to-date one. But it only affects the mob the bot's fighting, and it's effect isn't big enough to be useful in a team situation.

Hmmm, well you can make jealousy-augmented aggression enhancers... why not add a jealousy augmented aggression trimmer? That'd help with holding aggro against one mob. Or maybe another trimmer that can be used with the normal aggression trimmer that does a lower AE aggro effect? Better for teams as long as it doesn't break calms/root. And if it just affects stuff with LOS to the bot, you don't risk tons of adds from the next few rooms over. But the bot could actually keep aggro reliably without upping it's damage, and you'd actually use all those hps! *gasp* Heck, it might even make it worth it to pump up that dark blue BM for higher pet heals if the bot actually got pounded on.

Thoughts? More ideas?
