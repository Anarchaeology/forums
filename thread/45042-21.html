Oh why all the negativity?
This is comments from reading a majority of posts from the 21 pages...

The only thing in this patch that takes something away is a nerf to bracers. Why are people complaining about the other stuff? New content/things to do = good. If you think it's too hard to get, or you don't want it/don't want to do it - then you're no worse off than you are right now so just don't even comment. If you're complaining about how you 'imagine it will be' - save your comments till when you've *actually tried it* with your current live characters and can make a valid objective opinion.

Having played 3 characters to levels 111-152 now, the thing I always enjoy in the levels 1-100 is sometimes teams will want to hunt - and I find hunting the most fun part of the game. In the old days you used to hunt for xp and fun, and do missions to make the cash to support your hunting time. Now missions are the only thing roughly 90% of the player base do. I can't understand why people think it's *fun* to do the same missions over and over ad-bloody-nauseum - especially when they're really no challenge cause people rarely die over level 120 (and thus reason for finding little fun in my doctor at those levels). Oh wait, people need higher levels... so ...? oh yeah, so they can do the SAME MISSIONS again with even less risk/challenge. If you like this then well that's fine, but in that case, save the negative comments on the possibility of more hunting in the new patch, and go back to your BS missions.

I said bracer nerf is only thing that takes away in this patch (except for arul saba - see below), I don't agree that it is good it's being done all of a sudden - but if it is to be done, there's no easy way of ever introducing it without it being all of a sudden. The people that don't already have bracers - why on earth are you complaining? And the people that do - I'm sorry you wasted your time (honestly) - I have some myself, and it's a shame they'll be less powerful. But in the end the biggest negative is for the people that spent the time getting it, and now won't have as much use for it. But what about the positives? First - new Arul Saba bracelets are coming (ok, the old gems are useless - I had some myself - but did you see the stats on the *most uber* of the Arul Sabas? They really weren't that great - the new ones look much more powerful than the old - check AGs) - so where you have crappier reflect bracers, you could now go after new Arul Saba bracers in their place. Secondly... by reducing the amount of power the higher levels had overall at surviving heavy damage there might be more challenge in the game, and healing classes actually wanting to play more often and the possibility of more reliance on dynamic team play. Ok... the soloers take a hit, but if you're soloing at the levels that you really need these items for and still enjoying it then I wonder why you want to play an MMORPG in the first place. Get this... when there's more reason to team - more teams will form, and the more teams forming the quicker and easier it is to get a team, so even if you only have an hour or two to play you can still easily get a team. Everyone has to think negative and say cause they can only play one or two hours they can't afford to team (cause it *currently* takes too long to get a team) but can't open their minds even one little notch to see the answer to that is not to make it possible to struggle through a tiring mission - but to make teaming more viable and simple.

Mostly I'm appalled by the amount of negativity I've seen in this thread. I'm amazed at just how closed minded so many people are and that they can't see out of their own tiny little world and won't accept any changes good or bad. Changes are good, it's like getting a new game to play everytime they come along - even if things get harder. If you want a game with no challenge - then go buy a playstation, and download all the cheat codes off the internet for all the games, and bore yourself silly with unlimited lives and ammo - you WILL be uber then.

There really *is* a positive side to every negative you can think of if you just take the time to think about it and keep your mind open to change.
