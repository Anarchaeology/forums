2 fixes that dont involve nerfs
(posted in a couple of other threads that may never be read)

People want to nerf bracers, people want to nerf heals, and most of it all stems from the a couple of problems.

a) no options to what kind of damage your weapon does

b) the antiquated 50/40 nerf from the 12.x series of patches (i think, coulda been earler)

A) If there was a way for you to switch the damage type that your gun did, then hell, the whole bracer issue would dissapear. The easiest way to accomplish this is with hacked ammo. You have a stack of projectile ammo, add some chemicals, add some depleted uranium, add whatever! Suddenly you have a stack of ammo that does rad damage, or chem damage, or cold or fire, or POO (you hit for 5400 points of poo damage, Critical hit!)!!! =]

This could be done with every ammo type, and adds a lot of realism to the game.

This is the answer to bracers.

You can probably reload with a new ammo type faster than someone can look through a backpack for new bracers. Hit that person once, and then reload back to the old ammo, or with a totally different ammo. This would make bracers next to useless in PvP. Not totally useless as it would be pretty hard for an enforcer to switch damage types mid battle (melee is melee), but I think everyone else would be happy. A couple of mid battle enforcer damage change nanos would make them happy as well.

This would also be something else for people to do with their trade skills, and another money sink. I give my friendly local engi or trader or fixer a stack of 20k ammo to make into chem damage? No reason that shoudn't cost me 100k

Yes this would take a massive restructuring of the whole freaking item database, but really... it's worth it.

B) The 50/40 rule was a responce to the alpha's and aimed shots of the past, or mabye a way to just drag out PvP, I can't remember. It seems to have pissed everyone off BUT doctors and MAs (who by the way can change their damage type mid battle). This needs to be rebalanced, with mabye a tier system.

(As in you're fighting someone 60 levels below you... ya you're doing 50% damage ya gankin ass mofo. 25 levels below ya, 80% damage. Even and above regular damage) or whatever.

But just get rid of it for a patch all together and see what happens. Yes people can still do a 10k AS or FA, but didnt you fix evades?

-Sand
