I, for one, think the crit reduction is a step in the right direction. Crit chance was way too high before, and it still way too high now... In most game systems, crits are rare things--things you can't count on. In AO, crits are common and predictable.

I know with my 4 crit scope + Grinning Hunter that I'll get at least one, often 2 crits per mob battle. With TTS or Mark, I get 3 or 4. With TTS and Mark I get 4 or 5. Predictible and far too common...but, hey... I'll take whatever I can get over the cheating MOBs. However, when it comes to PvP--where PCs don't have the insane HP of MOBs--it's a bit too much.

It's also irritating when someone attacks you with an otherwise crappy weapon (for example, an Ithaca) and is sitting there hitting you for 10-20 damage per hit... And you *know* they've got some uber scope on and that you're gonna get critted in about 10 seconds and lose at least 50% of your HP.

MAs can still get an absurdly high crit rate... We're talking well over 50%! That's waaaay too much.

Of course this is going to irritate crit-heavy profs, but it seems to have sound balancing purposes. 6 months ago, this game was all about crits, nothing else. Now, it's moving away from that...but in PvP it is still a serious problem.

-Jayde
