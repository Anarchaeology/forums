A question for the lvl20 Super Twinks
I don't mean this as a flame, so please don't take it that way. I'm genuinely curious.

Anyone who PvP's see's the general variety of the lvls that PvP seriously. I never PvPd before lvl70 and that was just to get some practice in before I got to 75 where I'd be smashed by any high level that stepped into MMD. After doing some PvP a few days, you get the feel for the levels that those who really concentrate on PvP stick to. Lots of lvl20's, some lvl 68-74's and the 120+ crowd.

I originally didn't intend to PvP, but it got my interest after a while. Like I said earlier I practiced a little at lvls70-74 just to get a feel for it. After I decided that PvP was something I wanted to do, I wanted to be one of the 120+ pvpers, as it seems so much more complex and tactful. You know that at 120+ the people you are facing know what they are doing. In my little stint in PvP in my low 70's the skill level is not too bad, but you still run across people who don't have a clue. From what I've seen from the 20's PvPers is you have the super twinks and the noobs that are just curious. Ever now and then two of the twinks will face off, but for the most part its the twinks waiting around the arena for an unsuspecting new guy to walk up, thinking he may get lucky. In most of the PvP duels I've seen between lvl20ish, its 3-5 shots and the twinks buddies and him/her going "Woot! nice kill man!"

I really don't see the fun in that, I'm not trying to look down on the twinks, I just really want to know what you get out of that? That, to me, would seem to get really boring, really fast. My question is, what is it about lvl20 super twink PvP that holds your interest so well? I'm just curious.

As a second time reroller (NOT a twink), I can perhaps give you some insight. The first toon I made was a soldier. Had a tough time with him, but I learned gameplay, teamwork, implanting, pulling etc. So... you can only do missions and hunting so long. I based my soldier to be a PvMer, and he won't be viable in PvP until he can weapon switch a 200 Nova and Flashpoint. Well, he can PvP now, but he'd get his arse handed to him in a fight by most any other profession.

So, just for kicks (couldn't get a group together with my soldier), I thought I'd roll up a lowbie. I transfered some credits, a few old NCUs and I was simply amazed how much better the game is once you have a handle of it from the start. I PvP quite a bit with him, the PvP scene can be very active on RK2 at times. Last Sunday, there was a huge brawl in 2ho, MMD, Rome and Omni-T. I was at both 2ho and MMD. When I gridded into 2ho, I was literally surrounded by about ten or so Omnis. I had time to grid, but a funny thing happened... I attacked. Not a slip of the finger, but a deliberate act. I knew I was going to lose against such a crowd, but I never really cared about titles, my xp and gear was saved...

On my opener, I hit brawl and my Bird of Prey. I saw a shout the enemy is here! I thought to myself I'm the only one, it doesn't matter... my targets hp was down and fading, there are about five hitting me now, so I heal, keep fighting, recharge is gone, heal again dimach, my target is nearly dead, I heal again, my target is healed, and I finally succumb to the rentless attack of ten or so Omnis.

Restore Esscence has nearly a seven second recharge which nano init will never get rid of. I healed three times, so I lasted for over twenty seconds. It took TEN Omnis to kill me in that amount of time, and I nearly killed the one I was hitting by myself. I never had a rush like that in any mission. To hear that one guy run around, scream like a chicken "Help me! Help me!" from my humble non twinked MA. If it was just me and him... I would have walked all over the guy.

I self equiped ql 30 implants at level 16, and was able to self buff into armor that's 15 qls higher then me without outside buffs. Twinking is alot of fun, and the best, most enjoyable thing of AO is, and will *hopefully* always be, is PvP. It's not something for everyone, but it's something that everyone SHOULD, and probably will do sometime in their AO game time. Hell, I usually get a tell, or talk with the PvP I lose/win against and say, "Great fight, close one" and such. You can also factor in titles as bragging rights, but simply PvP in AO is fun. But wining PvP is even better. =)

I still have my rants about PvP, but my thoughts are fairly typical.

*hint:Crits are bad in PvP
