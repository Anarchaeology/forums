Forming up an Omni Super-Power
The following idea was brought up in our last FTC meeting. As those lines are being written, the idea is brought to several guilds leaders knowledge, including MoK, Silent Night, The Council, Svea Rike, FTC, Neo Core and other high level omni guilds.

The Problem
High level players dropping out of AO simply because they are getting bored. Nothing to do anymore, no one to hunt with since their guilds are mostly offline all the time and if there is something to do (PVP, Camp bosess, Missions for xp etc), they don't have a chance to form a team with the few who are online.

The Solution
What we came up with is this:

Form a temporary Super Power, consisting of only the top notch high level omni factions. I'm saying temporary, since when the expansion goes gold, people will probably want their old faction back so this way they don't feel like they gave up on something (their guild name for instance).

This Super Power will have approximatley 400-500 members, of which approx. 40-50 will be on at any given time (simply how things go). That means you get to do whatever you want, 24/7, and you don't have a problem getting a team together for whatever you want to do.

By saying "High Level Super Power" I don't mean that lower level players can't join. When a guild joins this Omni Super Power, ALL of its players are automatically in, no questions asked.

The only pre-requisites for joining this faction is to have an avarage level of 100+ and to be a well known and respected faction.

As I said, keep in mind that this is done on a temporary basis, you don't permanently give up your guild name, tag or whatever. You only join this Super Power so you'd have something to do and someone to do it with.

Think of the possibilities of having so many guild members on at any given time. You want to PVP? You got it. Boss camping? No one can beat your Super Power. Mission XP? No problems.

The name and leader of this faction are open to discussion. I suggest the leader position will be a rotating position, meaning that each week or so it changes. Maybe rotate the leadership among the original guild leaders. Dunno, need some input on this.

I bet the clans will do something in the same lines, which is good, that way we have more people on at any given time, which is what AO really needs now.

So, if you are an Omni high level guild leader and you think this Super Power can help your guild pass these hard times, please send me an ingame message (Superflu) or an email to superflu@bigfoot.com or simply post it in this thread.

Always glad to get feedbacks.
