I agree with putting lag/lag damage and pet pathing as major priorities.

1. Improve "lag" tolerance of game, in addition to minimizing "lag damage". I'd have to guess that there is likely a correlation between the two at some level. Unfortunately, all online games seem to have their share of lag, to totally getting rid of it is an unrealistic expectation...

2. Improve pet pathing (and monster pathing in general). Everyone knows how easy it is to lose a monster -- just run in an L or a U and the AI gets confused. I imagine the solution would require a good amount of work for Funcom, e.g. to implement room waypoints if they are not already being used. Each room would then have to have a set of waypoints defined (slightly inside room near middle of door) so that when moving form room to room, the AI has a trail of "bread crumbs" to follow. Monsters should also be given the ability to attempt to jump over obstacles in its way, if they are below a certain height. It is silly to see a real mean enf get stuck trying to go around a table and some chairs... You'd expect it to jump right over the table and kick your a^s.
