1 token for every 20 levels
I believe it is 1 token for every 20 levels you are, so...

1 - 30 = 1
31 - 50 = 2
51 - 70 = 3
71 - 90 = 4
91 - 110 = 5
111 - 130 = 6
131 - 150 = 7
151 - 170 = 8
171 - 190 = 9
191 - 200 = 10

(The reason you get 2 at 51 and not 60 is due to rounding (1.5 rounds up to 2)

Which means if you got 1 token at every level when you hit 200 you would have 1019.
