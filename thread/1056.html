it's all about the initial costs:

u of course have to buy a set of tools to begin with (ore extractors, etc), and it makes sense to buy a set of tools of sufficient ql so that u wont have to upgrade much if at all. The outlay of creds for buying this stuff of course is a hit to ur wallet, but u only buy them once -- since ur lvl19, u can prolly buy the nanos u need for less than it would cost to build atm, but once crystals start costing 100k and 200k+, u start saving quite a bit of money.

this is provided, of course, that u can find the carbonrich rocks, instruction disks, etc. of the right ql to build what u need -- which is similar to the problem of finding all the parts at the right ql for building weapons and such -- the benefit here is that many of the things u need only have to be a percentage of the ql of the crystal ur trying to build, not equal or higher.
