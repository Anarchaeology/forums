quote:
Originally posted by Demios
I think everyone agrees that they Docs should be better team healers than traders. I also think we can agree on the fact that at this point they arent.
I also assume that everyone agrees Docs should be the best healers in the game. Thing is, noone is clear what they mean by best, or what they mean when they say Trader's outheal Docs. Thus I would say that doctors should be the most effective healers in the game. I don't necessarily agree that doctor's aren't already the most effective healers in the game - I think it depends at which level (see below) we make the comparison.

A few quick points to try and explain what I mean by 'effective' (and I know some of the below have already been discussed):

  • just because a Trader may heal for more than a doc cast for cast when both are only using Team Heals that does not necessarily mean that the Trader's overall healing output (say HP over a given period of time) is greater than that of the doc.
  • A Docs first aid skill is lighter than a Traders for instance. Thus, relative to Trader, a Doc can add to their healing output using these (I note that this may not be significant at high levels). I do and I find it very effective.
  • the doctor's HOT's and HOT effect on Team Health Boosts contribute to their overall healing output.
  • the doctor's team health and individual health boost can help keep the members of a team alive longer, giving the doctor more time to heal; thus a doc has an increased healing output when looking at (e.g) total HP healed per battle.
  • A Trader's downtime when they are unable to heal (refreshing Divests etc.) reduces their overall healing output (again apparantly not so significant at higher levels).
  • A Trader's health sacrifice reduces their overall healing output.
  • The Trader's slightly higher nano cost for heals reduces their overall healing output, since they must recharge more frequently (I'm not sure how true this point is).
  • There has been limited discussion on single heals. I believe doc single heals are better than Trader singles, but I don't know about doc single vs Trader team (when cast for the benefit of one individual). Thus I don't how these affect my arguments. Aggro may be relevant here - a dead trader who was using team heals compared to a live doctor using singles.


On another issue, as has been pointed out many many times, Trader team Heals (as an example) may cast for cast heal for more, but there are other factors. Some of you have given reasons why you discount those factors, some of you haven't. Many of the reasons given to discount them only seem to apply at higher levels (IMHO). I believe it would be for extremely useful for those who believe Traders's heal better than Docs they are to qualify their statements as to at which levels they think this. Low, mid, high only or all? It would make the whole discussion a lot clearer.

If the statement 'Traders are BETTER at healing than the DOCS' is considered to be true only at high levels (say 125+) then a large number of previously unmentioned possibilities for fixing this become available. As it stands, and as has been pointed out before, many solutions suggested affect the Trader/Doc balance at all levels.

On the Trader vs Doc healing ranges. I actually I believe it fits the concept of Trader to have a concentrated range for their heals. Since the Trader is sacrificing their own health to effect the heal, it makes sense that you heal for a stable amount. That is if you are going to sacrifice a good deal of health to heal someone, you want to be sure that it's going to do the job. The way I see it, a sacrifice requires a predictability of result.
