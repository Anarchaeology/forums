Dunno if it helps, but
evades and resist don't work at the moment.
Cozmik says they do, players say they don't.

Example: my wife plays a 154 MA, maxed all evades with QL200 implants and +120 evades buff, which means over 1000 for her.
I play a trader, and debuff mobs attack rating by 200 most fights.

She never evades.

If your defense skill = the opponent's attack skill, you have 25% chance to evade, that's the rule.
I guess it's the same for nano resist.
But it doesn't work this way ingame, unfortunately.

Bottom line: don't raise Nano Resist, and keep your evades slightly above 60% of your own attack skills to avoid criticals, and you will have a good time
