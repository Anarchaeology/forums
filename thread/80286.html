Will to Fight is a "PvP Dungeon"
Thats what most people dont understand. Players go there to fight. To kill eachothers and have some fun. You dont go there to get some phat lewt, level up or admire the new playfield(which is nice btw). See it as camelot but without Tarasque and box room. Gnuff just drop a crystal that give you some special power for one minute. Nothing uber at all. Just usefull. Its large and very well made for PvP. Alot of place to hide, shoot and run. Its VERY VERY big and easy to get lost when you enter for the first time, almost like a maze. Theres a few mobs to charmfrom ql100-ql150-ql200. Its a big cave that looks like a mix between TLR, Subway and Smuggler Den. Most room are big and easy to move into while some other are very tricky and easy to get stuck while someone is chasing you. If you like 2ho and Camelot, you will probly like the new dungeon. If youre not a PvP'er you can still check it out but be warned that the ppl are there to for PvP.
