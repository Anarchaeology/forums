Just an Idea
OOC: I may be sounding like an idiot but this may already exsist somewhere, and if it does id like to get involved, but if it doesnt then here it is. Why dont we have a player ran justice system in game, where if somebody is convicted of killing an Omni-Tek employee, be it another omni-tek employee, a clans member, or a neutral player, they are brought to a secret location to be reviewed. There could be other cases as well. If somebody doesnt show to be reviewed then there would be squads to go out and find them and bring them with force to the review board. I really like this idea and hope someway it could be put together IC. About 8 reviewers would hear what the convicted and the witness would have to say, and decide from there. If convicted of commiting the murder, they would be taken to another secret location to be executed or be executed in public. Reviewers would vote in squad members and other reviewers if nessacary. Just an idea, i wonder what everybody thinks. If you like the idea ill see what I can do to get it started up.
