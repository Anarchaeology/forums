My Suggestion: PROJECT PLANNING
Project Planning is a great concept.

Test your new code for bugs. Put it on your test server. LISTEN to the feedback and bug reports to avoid adding bugs on your live servers. RETEST any changes on your test server.

Also, PREVENT old bugs from creeping back into your code. You can very very easily set up procedures in-house to avoid this.

Project Planning also includes a "vision" on where you want your software to be in the future. Most companies start with a general wide scope plan and then have a second plan which goes into detail. Perhaps if FC EVER does this, we'll eliminate or atleast soften the amount of nerfs.

I hope this helps Funcom. If not, see ya in SWG
