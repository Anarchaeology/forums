Why am I Omni?
[A real reason, as per a challenge I recieved...]

My reason for being Omni?

I was born on a manufacturing world with one of the lowest poverty ratings in our galactic cluster. The slums there were notorious scum hives, the sort of place you didn't walk without an armed guard. A large one. About 3 years before I was born, Omni began sending peacekeeping troops in, it was a world they had economic interests in. They acquired it when I was 18, and the planet really blossomed. But by then, they had already changed my life a lot more in other ways.

Mama was a good, kind woman, worked as a waitress in a greasy dive. She barely made enough to get us fed, and school wasn't an option. My sister and her had a genetic defect that struck every woman in the family..Jooli still has it, poor thing. Mama died from it when we were young. Dad...well, I never met my father until I was much older, and didn't even know who he was...but that's another story.

So, it was me and Jooli, all alone in a pretty scary place. When I was ten I started learning to pick pockets, earning money for a local gang. When they tried to make some money off my sister, and I'm sure you can guess how, I killed someone for the first time. I didn't mean to...I jumped him and in the struggle he knifed himself.

Soon after that I went into "business" for myself, learning the ins and outs not only of mundane thievery, but computer hacking as well..I had stumbled across an old wrist model someone three out and modified it with help, turning it into a real hacker's gem. I still have that one, it sits on my desk in Galway. I expanded, and learned, and got better..the money poured in, allowing Jooli and I to get a much comfier apartment, which she needed. Her medical condition was getting worse, and it soon became apparent she needed more treatment then I could afford. So I sprun a bold plan.

The first bank of Omni had recently opened on our world, not far from the city I lived in.They hadn't had a chance to shake down their security yet, and a luck probe found a backdoor I could use. I needed to get physical access ot the bank's mainframe to do it, though...and so at 2 am local time I found myself on the rooftop to the bank..and surrounded by more then a dozen omni troops. Somehow, they KNEW I would be there, and had laid a trap! I was imprisoned before I knew it, and Jooli was geting sicker.

A month went by, and I made my first escape. I was caught in three days. Next month, I made it for 12. Each time I learned more and more about their security..and on a suprisingly cold August day a little bug I slipped into their cleaning droids' operational software enabled 423 prisoners to escape our Omni-Reform camp. Well, From what I hear 90% of those were captured that sae day, I don't know. I took three bullets in the chest helping one guy with a sprained ankle. I hope he made it, poor sod.

So I make up bandanged and aching, and surrounded by a small armory worth of firepower. A dour, pale man in the black-on-black of Omni-Internops faced me. His name was Briadier General Zacariah Grey; and before what amounted to a firing squad, he said exactly this, words I will never forget:

"You are nothing. Your life is nothing. You live in nothing. You will die in nothing, unless something changes. Your sister will die, unless something changes. All you've done so far with your life is waste it, on petty thievery and tricks. You have a brain boy. Use it. You can either make the decision right here, right now, to become a man and make something of yourself..or I can let these gentleman shoot you now, and save you the misery. Your choice."

Well, not much a choice is it? And as much as I hated him at the time, the old man was right. I said yes..and my world changed. Jooli got her treatment, she's doing well and now has her own house back home. a real house! I send money back every now and then, but with her job as a secretary she hardly needs it to be comfortable. Omni takes good care of her. I went through the Internal Operations Academy on Prime and passed with flying colors, one of the best in three decades. After several successful campaigns, Brig. Gen Grey, now my mentor and patron, approached me, and told me about Rubi-Ka. I was to be assigned there, to ferret out terrorists and insurgents among our own people.

In four months I was in a tiny cramped apartment in Omni-1, and totally in awe. We, the Corporation, had turned this ball of red dirt into a living, breathing world! Yes, there are a lot of bad things the Corp did, Omni-Med and Mining in particular. I can't say I agree with them, and they shame me. But they've given millions of people across thousands of star systems life, comfort, and hope. And for that I'd give my last drop of blood to Omni-Tek.
