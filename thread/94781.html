Fixer Discussion / Week 8 - OUT of the Box!
This is a discussion on situations where we have taken our profession "out of the box" so to speak.

An example of "out of the box" would be to use a gun not necessarily designed for Fixers such as Assault Rifles or Sniper Rifles.

Please account at least 1 experience you have had with "out of the box" and explain in detail how successful you thought it was or how unsuccessful and explain why you would/would not repeat it.

I'm almost embarrased to admit this...

Back in the day (well before patch 12.6), when I was very low level, I used to dual wield an OT Boomer M30 with a Kang Tao Spitting Cobra.

This was when MAs could cast their crit buff on you. The Boomer was well worth it when you got a critical. The combination was useless any other time. The Cobra does such low damage, not to mention other problems.

I would not repeat any such experiment because:

1) Getting an outside crit buff these days is no easy proposition.

2) No pistol buffs means lower QL guns, and the Boomer M30 series tops out at QL 120.

3) The return on IP investment for dual wielding isn't considered to be worthwhile.

4) IP spent in Pistol and Fling Shot would have been better spent on nano skills.

I suppose it was fun, but it didn't take long for me to realize this wasn't going to do me any good.

Soleet
Back when I didn't know Funcom hates tradeskills, especially people who try to do anything with tradeskills before level 150+ or any tradeskillers with 'E' as the first letter of their profession, I tried making a weaponsmithing Fixer. I knew I'd have to make some sacrifices, so I made some unusual choices.

First off, no PvP. So I put very few, if any, IP into duck and nano resist. Second, I had to choose between nanoskills. One line wouldn't be nerfed, it would just be completely gone. I choose to give up HoT's (and obviously Summon/GA stuff). Finally, I cut corners with things like runspeed or lockpicking or computer literacy.

Tradeskill-wise, I couldn't just simply max out all the required skills. I kept weaponsmithing maxed since it was the highest required skill. The others were raised on a case-by-case basis, except for possibly nano programming, which quickly became a staple skill (I got many low level requests to do implants).

So how'd it work? Well, I think I only got to level 50-60 or so before I quit. It was nice making your own gun (granted, I could only do Maussers). I was also a semi-decent implanter with the help of specialized NP implants. I was also able to do a limited amount of jewelry crafting, but at that level it was more fun than profitable (again, because Funcom is retarded with tradeskills).

Self-reliance was great, but it had some obvious problems. First off, the QL of my equipment wasn't too great. But, compared to a person completely new to the game (someone with no higher level contacts or higher level alternates), I imagine my equipment was quite good. Any person who doesn't know or is against equipping things far beyond skill level (ie. wrangle equipping stuff) would easily be able to make their own equipment to the best possible.

There were some other problems that came up that I didn't think about beforehand (or, at the time, know about). First, you can't make your own armor. I would assume this build would only attract crazy hardcore people (people who don't twink at all) or people completely new to the game. That means no PPPE's, which means no carbonum or bronto. Second, there's very few weapons to make. There's still a gazillion formulae that are broken simply because of a typo here and there in the database. People try to defend Funcom about that, but they're goddamn professionals. Do you think a typo is acceptable in a corporate report? But I guess that's a subject for another thread. Third, consequently from the second, the few formulae that work are furthered reduced in usefulness because the shopping system for trade items is so terrible that one could easily level up multiple times in the time that it takes to complete an implant set or an MCS.

In action, my hybrid Fixer was alright. I was better than anyone without higher level help (ie. newbies) but worse than the average twink (ie. at level 30 I'd have level 50 implants, where a newbie would still most likely have store-bought stuff and a normal twinker would have QL70's installed). My SMG skill was still up there with anyone else's (minus the possibly 20 point difference caused by the implant scenario above). It was great socially. A lot of new people really don't know how to get decent equipment or who to look for if they figure out what can be made.

Perhaps this build wasn't really out-of-the-box, but more of what Funcom actually intended for Fixers. I could imagine it actually being fairly effective play up to level 100 if continuously fed with some gear (like PPPE's or tradeskill enhancing stuff), but then, what'd be the point?
