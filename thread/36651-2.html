Having read through the posts thus far I have heard a lot of good points that I agree with. It is true that no one can own anything in this game, aside from the items you carry on your character... however one thing that needs to be pointed out that I have not seen is the rights of a role player. For this example we will say that Frank and his guild claim to manage Rompas but Debbie and her guild wants to. Who has the right, who has to suck it up? This isn't table top role playing where the GM can say, 'Alright Frank, you manage Rompas and Debbie you manage Baboons.'... this is a game involving thousands of people who all have the same rights as the next person.

So how to we solve this dilema? Who is in the right? I would have to say that for the sake of consistant role play in a world that never sleeps, the rule of 'First come, first served' has to apply. Frank was there first, this it is only fair that Frank be the manager. But does this give Frank the right to tell Debbie to shove off completely? Heck no! Debbie has every right in the world to be there, serving drink, dancing on tables, enjoying herself just as much as Frank.

But alas we come upon another problem that I personally have dealt with, and now our imaginary Frank will have to deal with it. It is Saturday night. Frank and his guild have hired entertainment for Rompas and are throwing one heck of a good party. Along comes Ricky, and he decides at random to start announcing he and his guilds party, in Rompas as well. Frank approaches Ricky, explains nicely that he and his guild are having a party, and what does he get? Ricky tells Frank to F*** off, he doesn't own the club!

Ouch... but true. This has happened to me before. Rude or childish people who feel their fun is more important then the fun of others. They are out there, they will always be out there, and there is nothing that can be done about it. Or is there? The last person I spoke to as Assistant Manager of Reets Retreat in a role play setting, who got in my face with, 'You don't own....'... I /ignored. Why? Because I would not permit that one person, or that persons guild to destroy my fun. If that person cannot accept an In Character, role played situation where the Manager approaches you and asks for something... then that person does not deserve to have my time. Next please.

So my point to all this is this... Lynette "Wyldthing" Strimple is assistant manager of Reets Retreat. Perhaps it isn't what Funcom intended in their storyline, but that is how it is. Would someone who works at McDonalds go to Buger King and sell Whoppers? No. So in this role play world Funcom has created, why is it so hard for others to accept players as managers, assistant managers, entertainment managers, etc of clubs?

That's enough... I think I have said my piece. If you have a problem, most know my Email.
