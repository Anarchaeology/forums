Some changes are a bit to drastic.
For the past few month the patches has been increasingly better. And a big "Thumbs up" to FC for that.

But one thing is when u change/nerf things - U overdramatice it. Take for example the Tank and battle armor, u add 100% nano use for the first and 200% nano use for the second. That is soooo overdoing it and the only thing that cam out of that was that u got all people to camp mission terminals for the Senpei/hanshi etc coats - and now very few uses tanks and battle suits and are using coats instead. This is a pitty I think - Cause I like the look of 3-4 chars in battle suit fighting together or against eachother - It gives it more fealing of war. Instead of now u just see a bunch of people running around in trench coats looking like Bogart.

Set the tank to 30-40% nano use - and the battle suit to 70-80% that would do that people would start using either coats or tank armors. With the current changes u just made all people switch from one kind of armor to another. (I should say that I would never use any tank/battle armor because my stats are for coats mostly - and my interrest in it is purely out of I want to see some differences in the population).

There are lots of other examples on changes that have been overdone. My surggestion is that u should try starting in the small - Changing a thing a bit.. observe the impact, if it has its desired effect, leave it be - If it dosnt, change it a bit in the next patch.. and so on.
