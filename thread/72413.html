Something New
She walked along the curved halls of subway, her last time to be here. She would miss the grim horrors, the vacant eyed stare of the stim users. It was sad to leave this, which had been her home for so long, but she was ready for more now, she was ready to do her part. Her omni uniform, crisp and black as night, the arcane glow of the nanos which infused her slim figure a constant telling of what she was. Finaly she moved to the wrecked train and knelt down and picked up the data disk waiting for her. Yes it was time. The people had tired of the struggleing, tired of the terrorism both the clans and omni-tek reveled in. They came from all corners, some even like her, hideing in the ranks of their enemies, gathering the resources they would need. The fighting left a crack that a wise man could use if he were brave, and had brave followers. Rubi-Ka cried out with the pain of the suffering, and she would help end it. They would continue to attack from the dark, wearing the uniforms of the enemy. She strode from the tunnel, and then the subway for the last time. Her mind was elsewhere as she removed her morphing memory,she kissed it lightly fairwell before destorying it. It wouldnt do for her to be useing one when they finaly went crazy. Things would go from bad to worse before they got better, and many would die for good. She silently slid the data disk into the cell scanner,waited for a few seconds and removed it, then walked hurridly away, her nanos frying the black silken omni-tek cell scanner tech. unifrom, leaveing not a trace, then she moved on, donning a grid workers unifrom and heading for omni trade. There was work to be done.
