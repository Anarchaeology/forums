Little Bo Abbygale
In the news today:

An investigation has been started today in the animal neglect case of Little Bo Abbygale. Last night the said person's collection of leets excaped into borealis, newland and stret west bank. While there is nothing wrong with maintaining a few leets for pets, she had over the permissable number. Sweet little thing that she is, showed great concern for her escaped leets, and inspired bystanders to help retrieve her wayward and sometimes foul mouthed leets. The leets were all found, and she returned to her house. Questions though have arrisen about the proper care and handling of leets, and the number of leets that she is keeping. If Little Bo Abbygale would report to get her leets licensed, we could solve this little issue, and perhaps take steps to stop her leets from escaping in the future. She will also need to present her certificate that her leets have been sterilized, and that she is not breeding leets.

Also, I want my sword back, which one of her leets asked for and never returned.
