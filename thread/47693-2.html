They are (or have) fixed melee init values in 14.5

Basically every attack cycle now has the correct speed attached to it. Previously (as on live now) If you hit Q, and your initiave changes - Your actual speed will not change until you start another attack (hit Q, Esc, and attack again)

What this does is make rage take an instant affect on speed (IE next attack cycle will be faster once you cast rage, you dont need to break off attack and attack again)

It does also totally remove the infamous "infinate flurry" bug. Flurries will now tend to last 20 or so seconds.

It also means that if a doc lands a speed debuff on you, you will be VERY VERY VERY VERY SLOW on your next attack. Currently it has no effect if you are already attacking (but of course it takes effect if you break attack and attack again)

Make sense? Probably not.
