Well I cannot say that I really blame any of the professionals who chose to quit. I can see that being a member of that group would seem worthwile to the extent that you have input that results in fixes to existing problems or improvements. The difficulty is that by participating the players also acquire some burden of studying what their constituents want and some burden of explanation about what they learn in their dialogue with FC. Based on the response to the profession reports, I suspect most feel that they have no real input--certainly I would feel that way. That really puts anyone who is one of the professionals in the uncomfortable position of basically being a buffer between FC customer service and players in their profession. Anyone disposed to cynicism might even think that was exactly what FC had in mind from the start. In any event I know I would not be willing to put myself in that postition.
