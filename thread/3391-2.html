Yes, we discussed it on the #ao-basher channel, but massive support here might get them to consider this option again. So, yes - I support this suggestion 100%.

Btw, I'm a lvl 80 crat, and I plan my upgrades very carefully. But, as said before, overequipping like we are doing now was not planned and FC are looking for ways to stop it. I have no problems with this, if it's done in a nice way like suggested here.

Now, what will happen if they implement this suggestion?

People will start twink-buffers.
A low level trader with extreme implants etc. designed to cast wranglers on twinks. Nice way to make a living, but will annoy new players who can't pay 400k for a wrangle and see that they are less powerful than their twinked fellows.

Some buff-professions (trader, MP, enforcer etc.) will stay low level on purpose just to earn money by buffing others. Their buffs will cost a lot of money, making them unavailable to new players without a high level main.

However, any new player with a minimum of patience will outlevel the twinkbuffers in a few hours, and the be able to get the same buffs from "normal" players. The level of buffs available will also be reduced, as people will have to settle for lower level buffs.

I'm not sure if this will reduce the prices on buffs though, if you make a 50 wrangle the best there is at low levels then that wrangle might cost a million credits (previously the price of a 132 wrangle). The impact of a 50 wrangle is much smaller than a 132 however, so you will not "suck" if you don't get it. Which in turn make people pay less for it - it might work.

Now, enemies will be considerably more powerful after the imagined patch, so they have to be lowered as well in the same patch. No need for another 12.6. This patch should be closely watched by FC so they can remove any super easy or super hard mobs fast. Mob strength adjustment is very risky and will make some people very angry because you can't make them all work perfectly at once. And no, there is no way FunCom can let mobs stay at the same level if they implement this, but they don't have to adjust them at all levels, because high level players already have all the buffs available to them.

Example:
As said before I'm a level 80 crat, with 109ish armor (90 coat) and 110 implants (changing to 125 soon). I can remove all my implants, armor, equipment etc. and put them back on without the help of a "red" player. With this equipment I can solo just fine. To me it wouldn't matter if mobs stayed the same, but a soldier or an agent might find it too hard to fight greens without a 132 wrangle, so mobs might have to be adjusted anyway. Now, they just made my game a lot easier, just because soldiers can't solo without a 132 wrangle and I can.

You see, even if your profession will do just fine without buffs, other professions might not. Agents rely on high level rifles, and without wrangles and other buffs they cannot wear them. I think a combination of upgrading those professions who will need it, adjusting mob strength to compensate for it, and then implementing the buff suggestion would be more correct. Although, they would have to do all this in one patch - and that is extremely hard to do.
