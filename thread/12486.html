RK2 Party March 2, 2300 GMT, Reet's Retreat
Jester's Vengeance is sponsoring a party at Reet's Retreat, 2300 GMT on Saturday, March 2 on RK2. We have lined up the services of the great Djqtpie who will be broadcasting her techno tunes over the net. She puts on a great show and her music rocks!

You can download the winamp player at www.winamp.com and she gives out the address during the party between each song. The DJ (Qtpie) broadcasts most evenings and can be found on RK1 at Babboons.

This party is for clan, neutral and omni alike. This is a social event so leave your armor and weapons at home. If you can not remove them from your main character, make a neutral alt. For those of you who do not know how to get to Reet's Retreat, here is the Directions:

Get to Newland by either whompa (clan) or grid (omni) and take the whompa to Borealis. Take the Whompa to West Stret Bank. Make a left through the archway to your left and make a left as soon as you pass into the corridor. Go straight through two archways and Reet's Retreat is the big building directly in front of you.

Bring your dancing shoes and practice those dance steps! More details will be forthcoming as they are developed (theme, etc.)
