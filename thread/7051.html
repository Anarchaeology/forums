Actually, it is completely trivial. Remember, both Solitus and Opifex get 3 life and 3 nano for each point of body-development and nano-pool, respectively.

Therefore, two characters at level 50, each with 200 in body development would have 800 life if Opifex, and 850 life if Solitus, just from the life per level and body development. That is less than 7% difference. With 200 nano-pool skill on each character, the numbers gained from level and skill are 750 nano for the Opifex, and 800 nano for the Soliuts. Again, a difference of less than 7%.

Nano using Atrox and physical Nanomages do tend to really feel the pressure. Using the same level 50, 200 in body development and 200 nano-pool, an Atrox would have 900 life and 500 nano, and the nanomage would have 550 life and 850 nano-pool.

This is obviously not including other modifiers, like the life & nano a character naturally starts with, the bonuses from max-life & max-nano implants, merit boards, and other sources.
