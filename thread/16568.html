RK2 -- Division 3 (Omni) looking for Euro and Asian time zone players-- RK2
Division 3: Omni Reclamation, a member of Project Revenant, is looking to expand our European playerbase, and add to our Asian time zone playerbase on the RK2 server.

We currently have 10-12 European players. We have a Major in charge of European time zone events, who will set up, schedule and run these events. We also have a drill sergeant in charge of running and overseeing training events for our European time-zone cadets.

We are also looking to build our Asian time-zone player base. We would need someone, who is a dedicated player and plays at least 3-4 times a week, to develop ideas, coordinate efforts, plan events, & oversee and run Asian Time Zone events.

With this job would come the title of Director of Asian Operations, as well as receiving the rank of Major within our Department of Foreign Affairs. A collateral duty would be to recruit Asian players for Division 3, as we ccurrently only have 2-3 players from Asian time zone. Your first prioirty would be to find a person interested in serving as a Drill sergeant in our Academy, to run training events for Asian time-zone cadets.

If you are interested in applying for membership in Division 3, go to our webpage at www.projectrevenant.com and register for the forums. Once registered, go to the Academy forum, and fill out an application. All further instructions on what to do for successful acceptance into Division 3 can be found there.

If you are interested in either the Major position or Sergeant position in the Asian time zone, please send an email to me at mbrock3200@aol.com, after you register for our forums.
