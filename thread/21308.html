I have more ideas...

1) Make it viable for tradeskill classes to gain levels by building things, so they dont have to fight. IMO, an item of a certain QL should give you the same experience that a monster of that level would give you. Also, trade skill items should sell for more than the items needed to build them cost. An engineer should not have to level by fighting. They should be able to build robotic devices and stuff to gain experience.

2) Make MA armor worth using, maybe by adding skill bonuses to evades and MA skill.

3) Fix MA attacks and Dimach recharge, or make them vastly more powerful, because they are worthless now.

4) Check out parry and maybe evades, because they are next to worthless except in making crits less often..

5) Fix the several worthless skills in the game, like hydro vehicles/riposte/sharp objects...

6) Don't make all the good items nodrop unique. I hate not being able to give away my cars, even though im never going to use them. This applies to MA attacks. Why on earth are MA attacks unique?
