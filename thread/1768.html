Nuclear Bomb in Athen!
I was in Athen yesterday (24/11/01) just before 3pm GMT,. on my way to a mission in the junkyard, when I saw a big white box in the middle of the road by the east gate. On inspection it turned out to be a nuclear bomb! As the description warned against tampering with it, I left it alone and went in search of somebody to verify I wasn't hallucinating. However, when I returned to the spot a couple of minutes later, it had gone. Anyone else see this thing?
