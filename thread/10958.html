I disagree with this, conditionally.

Part of the balance of pets being so good is that you are at extreme risk when they aren't right by you. This may not seem like such an issue in a dungeon, but when hunting, I know I scan all around and get myself into a one "red" mob only position, then sick the pets on that one mob. From a long way away.

On the other hand, it would make dungeon crawling go a lot smoother.

Perhaps is should only be usable indoors. There are already several items that check for in vs out doors. Lets make it take 30 seconds or so to cast, just so you can't go running thru the dungeon till you need your pet. I don't see how that would stop scripting the heal/anima/whathave you, maybe if the pet was rooted for 10 seconds after you cast it?

Those are the only ways to bend the idea I can come up with. Anyone else? I think if we try to present FC with a pre-balanced request, we have a lot more chance of getting something implemented.
