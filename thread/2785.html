Well Caster, a lot of people have some bad feelings toward "token farming." But if you spent two months getting those, then I feel you deserve them. However...

Funcom is constantly adjusting how tokens are earned. Token boards aren't supposed to be the end-all-gotta-have-it item that some people are making them out to be. I suspect, but can't be sure, that Funcom might have put in some code to prevent people from earning more tokens if they don't qualify for the next token board. You may want to take a chance and try earning level 15 before token farming again. Or, you may want to wait until you are level 50.

The way the clan merit boards are arranged, you should only have earned about 60 tokens by the time you are level 15. This level of token board is only available to people level 15 and over. At 120 tokens, you need to be level 50 to actually earn and use the token board.

If you seriously have 394 tokens, you have until level 100 to earn 6 more tokens to use the second-best merit award. That is, the 400 token board isn't available until level 100. The next and final merit reward after that is 1000 tokens, which you can use also at level 100, but you still have plenty of time to earn this reward.

Here is the catch: If the development crew at Funcom thought that low-level token farming is an exploit, they would have put in a fix quietly, with no announcement or acknowledgement that the "explot" even existed, unless it was very unbalancing and very widespread. If this happened after you earned the 394th token, then you won't be earning any more tokens for a long time, no matter what you do. Just go out and have some fun! Find a group and learn to work with a team. Explore the world. Earn some honest experience. Have an adventure! As long as you do a reasonable effort at missions every once in a while, the odds are VERY good that you are set for the next 100 levels. Do you think you can get that far?
