What exactly is the reason...
...that first aid skill is in no way tied to how fast you can actually perform the first aid skill?

Many of the skills are like this, and it makes no logical sense to me whatsoever that increasing your ability to do a certain thing has no effect on whether you do it better (that's based on the kit you buy) or faster (since that's a hard-coded timer). Higher skills give me access to better kits, but it seems that 40 seconds is an eternity even at the low level my current doctor is. I can only imagine how much that hurts with a team of level 100+...

Higher skill should equate to faster skill application as a matter of common sense.

(and yes, I know at this point that would severely unbalance everything and waah waah, I know, I'm just saying. The least you guys could do is make it VERY clear that higher skills don't mean faster skills in order to prevent newbies from gimping the hell out of their characters. My doc needs the skill regardless to get the better kits, but does an Agent or a Soldier need the kits, or simply the ability to apply even low QL kits more rapidly? Gaining even 20 HP when you're about to die is much better than gaining 120 HP 20 seconds after you're dead. And considering how easy it is to lose a character at half HP or worse simply because of lag...AND considering that at random times the treatment kits just get eaten and you have no other way to heal yourself...seems unduly difficult to hard code the time regardless of whether it's 30 or 40 seconds.
