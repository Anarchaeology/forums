My new years resolution
Seeing as I resolved to be less stingy this year in RL, I decided that I would try to be less stingy in game too

I want to have a contest open to all citizens of Atlantian, where someone will win a 10 million dollar prize from yours truly. Only problem is, my brain has gone numb, and I can't think of a good contest idea. I would really like to do something fun, and challenging, with as little gaps for griefing as possible.

So I started this thread in the hopes that you guys (being as smart as you are) can help with some contest ideas. Once I have found one that looks good, then I'll plan it, we'll have it, and some lucky person will walk away with 10 mil from my own cash.

I dunno. I play on Rimor mostly now, so I just would rather do something fun with the cash I have on Atlantian, rather than just have it sit there, or give away randomly
