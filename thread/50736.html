September 11th - In remembrance
September 11th 2001 the world was shocked by possibly the most terrifying terrorist acts ever. While people die every day, in wars and catastrophies, and to illness and hunger, the scenario of that day struck deep into the mindset of the whole world, perhaps mostly due to the way it was committed.

We would like to have this thread in remembrance of those who died in the terrorist acts that day. Please keep it free of any enmity. Let those who will post in peace do so. Let this be a day of compassion, not of hate and strife.

In the days following September 11, 2001, I created This Site in rememberance, and to give everybody hope. It portrays people from many different countries showing their condolences for those involved in the tragedy.

I'm pretty certain that everyone can recall, with crystal clarity, precisely what he or she was doing at the moment they first heard about the tragedy...

As an American, I'm sorry to say that our public has a very short memory. For the last 20 years, our armed forces and our overseas civilian workers have been targets for terrorist attacks, worldwide . It makes headline news for about 3 weeks, then everyone forgets. I suppose it's only human. Even natural disasters fade from the memories of all except those who survive them.

If September 11th taught us nothing else, it showed us an enemy who is willing to bring the conflict to us, and rub our faces in it.

I can't begin to express the sadness, the horror, the fury I feel, that there are people out there capable of such atrocities.

So, I say to you, please don't forget. In 3 more weeks, the media will likely have something more entertaining to talk about. Half the politicians will be squawking about what the other half of the politicians are (or aren't) doing. States will be running gubernatorial elections, and everybody will be talking about political agendas.

September 11th isn't about an agenda, it's about people working together, cooperating, and being vigilant. It's about caring, and compassion, and love. It's about continuing to exercise the utmost caution as we go about our daily affairs. Most of all, it's about continuing on with our lives, because if we don't, that unseen enemy has won.

To the people around the world who expressed their sympathy, their compassion, their sorrow, and their outrage at 9/11, thank you.

Please please please, don't ever forget! We cannot forget this, because if we cease in our vigilance, it will happen again. I have no doubts of this. Don't forget the many innocent people who died that day, the courage of the passengers on board those planes, the brave rescue workers who gave up their lives. Don't forget our armed forces, who are out there in sand and trenches, the sea, the jungles, all working to bring a measure of safety to the world. Don't forget all those selfless volunteers, who worked tirelessly to try and bring survivors out of the wreckage. Don't forget our world leaders, who are, after all, only human as well. The decisions they make are neither easy, nor lightly made.

If we forget all that has gone before, then those people who died on September 11th died for nothing.

/me lights her candle
