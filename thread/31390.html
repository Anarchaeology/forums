Bump for entertainment patch!!!!
The best part of AO is frankly the social areas outside combat. The clothes, apartments, resorts, pool, dance clubs, bars, coffe shops ect. all add to make AO the hands down winner for social oppurtunites.But with competition on the horizons, its time to fix the numerous bugs and problems plauging the entertainment portion of the game.
So here it is , proposed changes in an "entertaiment" patch, that fixes other stuff but focueses on entertainment .

Changes in V.---

Entertainment venues-
-All the unopened clubs in Omni-entertainment including Heads and Tails and the Pleasure Dome are now open. Enjoy!!

-Fixed several Bugs with the Enjoy It while it last nightclub in Tir. We have rearraged the corridors to make it more acessible. We had added a bar and opened up the dance floor.

-You can now go up the Lush Hills resorts diving platform. We have also added a changing room, waterslide, a cabana, pool attendants and pool chairs. We have moved save,reclain and mission terminals back so it gives it more a resort feel.

-The clans have opened a Spa in Tir county near Lavadippers retreat.

-Added a working casino in neutral territory. Games include slots, craps, blackjack, roulette, multiplayer poker and wheel of fortune. In order to gamble, you exchange a certain number of credits for casino tokens that can only be used at games and are no-drop(to prevent cheating). By winning games you get more tokens which are redeemable for weapons, vehicles, armor and hard-to-find nanos. The more tokens you have, the better stuff you can trade for.

-we have added several new tracks to be played at clubs, listen for them!

The non-vendor npcs at clubs are now more interactable. This is in the same vein of talking to NPC guards.

Apartments:
-We have increased the number of items that can be stored in an apartment to 40 and have added several pieces of funcional furniture.

-You can now buy new layouts for you apartments at special consoles.

-If you switch sides, your apartment will reset so you can choose a new one.

Clothing:
-Social clothes can now be worn over non-armor clothing. However, you must not be wearing any piece of armor when its on.

-added several new types of clothing including breed specific wear.

-Introducing the birthday suit. It goes in your hud slot and makes it so that the base layer looks like your nude. Its works for all non-atrox and you can toggle off who you don't want to see(males or female only, no Nanos ect.). Parents can lock this feature with a passcode. It only works, however, in bathrooms, apartments, pleasure dome, heads and tails, Changing rooms and at the Lavadippers spa.
Misc changes:

-coffee makers now work!

- added dual ride vehicles. These work by the driver pulling up to rider, targeting them and typing /ride. A pop-up box will apear, if yes the rider will get into car and ride along. At anytime the rider wants out out of the car, the rider or the driver types /getout and the rider will pop-up next to the vehicle. If the driver goes LD the ride ends. If the rider goes LD he gets out as well but pops where the car was before he got out on the ground.

-added dual emotes. These are emotes that when done together allow 2 players to "kiss" each other, ect.
----
Fell free to add anything.
Please keep bumped!! I want an official reply that this is at least forwarded to devs or something.
