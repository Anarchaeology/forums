Why did I ever solo?
Ok... So I soloed most of my time. I liked being out there on my own, providing for myself. And it fit that whole adventurer description. It's really quite satisfying if you can consistently solo 70-80% missions without any outside help whatsoever. I decided I wanted to solo to 100 and then start teaming for a bit.

So, I did. Those upper 90s levels took about 8 hours of missioning, but it felt pretty good when I finally hit 100. Then I 'LFT'-ed a bit at Tir grid, found a team and within two hours, I'd gotten a level and a half! I don't know if I was lucky to find a particularly good team, but the six of us (2 docs, 2 fixers, ma, adv, lvls85-100) blazed through a 170 mission like... well like something that blazes through something else extremely well.

I'll probably solo some in the near future. Grab some Clon**** missions to get those last 320 tokens. But apart from that, I don't think I'll be doing a whole lot of killing by myself. I'll still be a lone ranger, of course. I'll just be one in a team

I tell you this for no particular reason. Just thought I'd share something with the group. Feel free to laugh at me for being so stubborn earlier. And I'm at work right now, but if anyone's up for a mission or two in about 8 hours, I'll be at Tir grid

I soloed my agent to 90ish, then it started taking ridiculous amounts of time so I started teaming. My fixer, at the time I cancelled my account, was level 125 and had never teamed.
