Hot buttons.....
How about a button to execute all 10 of the hot buttons at once - or one at a time. That way you could:

- Put all your social clothes on one bar and change with one click
- Put your armor on another and change back with one click
- Put your buffs on one or two and not have to click each nano in turn
- Put trade items in slots and execute 10 at a time

There could be some other possibilities. Don't know if something like this is hard to code but it would save a lot of keystrokes.
