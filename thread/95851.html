I do not condone of NLF's methods, as I have seen them bring a close friend of mine to his death - but I would just like to comment on the Notum mining issue, which was addressed.

Savoy - I realize that there at present state is plenty of Notum to go around, and that 1000 years are far away in the future, but I must ask you this question: If you're in a car, racing towards a deep crevice, where at the bottom are legions of great spiders, whose greatest desire is to split your car apart, so that they may sink their fangs into your flesh, do you only hit the brake the second before you fall over, and hope that it will be enough, or do you take the safe way, and brake right away?...

Even in my own position, as a Nanomage, I think that thinking 1000 years ahead of ourselves may be just a little bit extreme, spreading the word of doom for the whole Nanomage breed, but I do to some extent understand the motivation of the NLF. I don't condone of their methods, and I think they're panicing before they think, but they do have a point nevertheless.

As for you Trisagiona, we don't know how large a percentage the Nanomages need, nor whether we actually do need any percentage, or just a minor scrap in our pocket - but until any Nanomages start to feel just a bit weaker, due to loss of Notum on Rubi-Ka, I think we'd do wise to just back it up with research before we panic, don't you think?

Think before you act people, not the other way around - that's a good general rule to go by. And don't let yourself get scared about the dim and far away future, of which we as of yet has absolutely no knowledge.

As for the miners - I doubt many of them care about genocide - most of them are in for the profits, and the chance to kill all the people that dare oppose them - it sickens me more that such people exist, who only wish for profits and warmongering, more than the prospect of the Nanomages growing weaker due to Notum mining. Maybe many Nanomages will disagree with me on that, but I tend to think a little broader than that - warmongering kills all of us, not only Nanomages, and it happens every day, yet I don't see the NLF trying to stop that. I personally have a problem with understanding the way the inhabitants of this planet sorts its priorities...
