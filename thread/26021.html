How about grid missions...
This will need to expand items on the grid but it could be nice.
==> Let's call them constructs.

Each offical organisation - and maybe more - will get one on a substytem grid inside each town.
Like other missions you may have to enter a location in a subsystem of the grid - ie Omni-med - to find hidden agenda.

In order to prevent you from getting it, there will be white ice that will root you, grey ice that can kick you (like emergency exit) or black ice that can damage you ==> no need for now to give them special design. Black pyramid can be enough...

No more attack skill, it will be computer literacy here.

Just need to add special nanos line for hacking subsystem by fixers and another general line for all.


Mission reward could be special info about a mob (level, hp, location,...), about the specification of an item (description like helpbot do with "whois" command, drop mob ==>"omg omg i want this f****** Shades of Elucubration which is the mob who drop it??? Plzzzzzzzz"), about a player (location and coord ; nice for an interception team in PvP zone ), for uploading special nanos formulas kept hidden in deep secret facilities but on computers linked with the grid (at least a way to get with still pain Grid Armor or Epsilon purge?) or about an event kept hidden by GMs...


This will give a real touch of Cyberpunk here
I know this will be seriously fixer oriented, but they are the masters of information, hacking and grid, aren't they?
