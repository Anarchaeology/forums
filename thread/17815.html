I cant help ya with all of them and i dont have co-ords but heres a little extra info:

Electro Unique - Wailing Wastes - The Boka - This is near the West boundary, roughly half way up the zone. If you have a map you will see a few sand craters in the area. Look for the largest one. I *think* that is his spawn point, altho i have seen him just North and just South of there. You *will* have to take a bit of time searching

Live Metal - Greater Tir County - Bot Mt. - Heading NNW from Tir will take you into GTC. As ya zone ya should be able to see the Mt. in the distance to the NW. Theres an Op just to the East of the Mountain. Live Metal can be found wandering anywhere in the rocky area of the Mt.

Nuts n Bolts - Wartorn Valley - Hard to find. Apparently he wanders all over the Valley, and because he is so close to a large city he is usually already dead

Trash King and his Lackey - Athen Shire - Cobal Sands - Head West from West Athen into Athen Shire. Keep heading WSW into the desert til ya can see the Ruins. Its not hard too hard to spot

Metalomania - Harrys - Nice n easy to find if he's not been killed recently

Greasy Joints - Newland - Can be found in, or just North of, the valley with the crashed ship. Its not too far NW of Newland City

Ive never actually found Galuzmo or Best in Brass so cant help ya there. Galuzmo is in Omni Forest somewhere and BiB is just outside Rome?

To Seth767 - They dont look that special. Just a tiny version of a Buckethead Techno-Scavenger... with the exception of Trash King who is a huger version of a Junkbot. They are unique tho, so its a nice thing to find And ya can have a few seconds of conversation with them before they get annoyed and jump on ya

Another quick note: I *think* the spawn on all these Bots is 2 hours...
