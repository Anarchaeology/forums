Omni-Tek Public Assistance Program in Rome Park Tonight - 3/10
In an effort to assist current, returning and newly arriving Omni-Tek employees, the first (what will hopefully be regular) Omni-Tek Public Assistance Program (O.P.A.P.) will be held in Rome Park, also known as Rome Red, tonight.

As this is the first, participants currently include... me . I will be assisting any that come by with the following programs:
NCU Compressor - NCI storage increase by 20
Hack Grid vector - Increase to run speed by 190
(lesser program for lower NCU costs as well)
Hacked Diagnosis - 30 min. healing program

Although these are not the highest market programs on the market, I'm hoping these will help out any that can use them and help show Omni-Tek's dedication to it's employees.

The Program will kick off at 7:30pm ((cst)) at the North-West corner of Rome Park. Please feel free to drop by .

*Please no tips or payments, all costs are being funding by Omni-Tek and it's Public Relation Affairs Department.
