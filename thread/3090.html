First DC, It was clear from the trial they showed little regard towards Mr Ross and the rules of Omni. However I think the trial made it clear to them this was not an stance that any guild of Omni-Tek can take.

After which Truwind has offered a formal apology to Mr Ross for her actions and the actions of the DC guild. I have accepted the apology and do believe that Truwind has seen her mistake, however the judgement must come from Mr Ross.

If Mr Ross accpets the apology I see no reason that DC should not form part of the guilds of Omni-Tek once more. It has been made clear to her the consequences if her guild chooses to step out of line.

Having said that I understand there is a lot of bad feeling between DC and the other guilds. This is to be expected so for this reason alone I think it best for DC to miss the first meeting.
