Sounds like we're talking about several issues here, but I'll just stick with the maps.

Making the maps into "nanos" is weird, if you ask me. It's just another nano sucking up my hotbars and cluttering my nano window, and loading up my NCU.

Make them real maps again, and keep following that model. It worked, and we liked it, though I still think some of the maps require just plain too much skill. Anyway...

Once you've done that, to give adventurers some love (And I love adventurers in teams, btw. They do good damage and are great backup healers) add two new adventurer-only map upgrades.

The first would be "Aggro Range". It would draw aggro circles around mobs so you know how close you can get without drawing their attention. This is a PERFECT fit for adventurers, and when you hear your adventurer say "OK, everyone follow EXACTLY in my footsteps!", you darn well better do that.

The second would be "Social Range". It would draw a different color circle to indicate the social range of mobs. Another PERFECT fit for adventurers. When the adventurer targets a mob, social range circles are drawn around all mobs that would give social aggro if that mob was attacked. If those circles overlap, better back off!

Not only have you given basic maps back to regular players, but you've really given adventurers a great set of tools to help them solo, and to help their teams survive in a hostile environment.

The code is already basically in place, as far as the minimaps go, since they do something very similar for towers. I think very little modification would be required.

Comments?
