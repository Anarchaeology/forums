Too tired to shop
I've been a loyal player of ao for quite some time now, I've played just about every profession out there. I've looked past the bugs, and the character/item nerfs and so forth and so on, no biggie. I've never raised my voice in frustration at any of these problems. My one biggest issue however and this one is a bit unforgiving, is finding items over a certain ql. As an Mp my success in a group or solo is dependant on my pets, it's how I make my living, however that existence becomes quite bothersome when I can't keep my nanoformulas up to speed. I camp shopping channels for hours on end when I could be out hunting or RPing, the formulas I need are either not being sold, or the few rare cases when they are being sold, the seller charges astronomical prices for them because they know it was hard to get. I know what you're saying..pull up a chair and camp a mission terminal, tried that, it seems that the machines enjoy generating missions with items that you will never use in your life. It's not just nanoformulas, finding implants is also a pain, sometimes mobs drop empty ones, but they are rarely on the mob in question, and I'm limited to looting rights in most groups.

I'm not trying to complain or anything, and I do know that if implants and formulas were made available up to ql 200 that would take the interest out of the game, that may be so, but at the same time a balance has to be met because if I have to sit on a shopping channel all day, that is equally unproductive and uninteresting.

Since I know no one is probably going to pay attention to this, i guess it couldn't hurt to ask for some ql 150ish empty implants, if you have any for sale /tell shizara
