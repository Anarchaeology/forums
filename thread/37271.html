Some enforcer issues addressed by FC Guru guy, Or something.
This is a MASSIVE cut and paste from the professionals board - Original report of issues posted by Arinia, But arinia = ZZZZ right now, so I'll cut paste it.

The stuff in BOLD is the replies by the FC developer, Original questions are within the ------------------'s, although it should be a quote.. I'm cut-pastin here' damnit! ;P

quote:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Originally posted by Arinia
Enforcer-Specific Bugs:

Taunts
As a class defining ability, these are becoming more and more ineffective. Taunting = Damage now, so we either need to be able to do more damage, or our taunts (Mongo and Single taunt) need to be a lot more effective. I�d prefer a bit of both, to be honest. As it was, Mongo was about the only thing that worked as a taunt, now, it�s losing its effectiveness as well.

Same applies to Aggro enhancers, except some have better experiences with them. I suspect it�s because they are doing comparatively higher damage, they work better.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We agree that Enforcers taunts are vital to the role of the Enforcer, and we are looking at the taunting capabilities of the Enforcer, as well as other professions that require taunts. So, we are currently looking at the taunting capabilities of the Enforcer, in an effort to see what can be done in regards to the situation. I have no definative answer as of yet, but will let you know of developments. Ideas are welcome too, as always.


quote:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rage
Rage needs to be tested with all nanos in the "combat" class. It's also hard to determine which nanos are in that class other than "Hey Mr. Fixer, what class is Luck's Fickle Fate?". Personally I have tested this particular nano and it was not removed by rage.

Rage's descriptions needs to be updated to reflect what it actually does, and how many NCU of offensive buffs will be removed by each rage. It just needs more clarification in this respect.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------



We agree it could use updating, and we are looking at doing it.


quote:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
General bugs affecting all professions:

Special Attacks
"Please wait for your previous special attack to complete" This is annoying, you can only brawl/fastattack/etc 10-15 seconds AFTER you get the message "Brawling skill available...". From talking to others, this appears to happen with all specials. This also can be seen with Treatment/Nano kits. First Aid too. It�s annoying.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------



This is due to how the special attacks are generated on the client. It's been brought up with the programmers for them to investigate. So, it's a client vs server issue we are looking at currently.


quote:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HP Bar Bugs
I can be at 60% health, run into my housemate�s room, look at his screen and I show at full HP. Since he�s the Doc, I can just yell �I�m taking damage�. Others don�t have that luxury. Major Issue.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------



Major issue - Yes, we agree. It's a well known problem, and we would like to fix this as soon as possible. I have checked the bug in regards to this in our bug database and it is being looked after. Once more progress is made, I will let you know.


quote:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Visual Bugs
Yalmaha size issue, growing/shrinking bracer issues, etc. As mentioned, "This is an immersive game. The enviroment[sic]/atmosphere is important." This isn't a game-stopping bug by any means but it's extremely important.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------



This is a tricky issue. The problem here is that for changes to size (wearing bracers, being in a Yalmaha) to appear to everyone, the bracer information must berelayed to everyone. By this I mean for every person to see a smaller Cosmik (if I'm wearing a bracer), information must got from my client to the server, and then to every single person in visual range - and this information must be constantly updated as people go in and out of visual range and as people put on and take of bracers. Essentially, it means alot of traffic, especially in those congested areas like Omni-1 Bronto Burger, Newland and Tir Grid on busy days. That's the layout of the issue, and why it hasn't been totally fixed yet, but to confirm, we are aware of this and are looking at ways to remedy it, but I can not say for certain what can or will happen. I'll update everyone once more is known.


Enforcer Specific balance Issues:


quote:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Need of buffs for all Melee Skills
Primarily 1hb/2hb/1he/2he/Piercing/Melee Energy/etc. Multi Melee is sorely needed due to the high MM reqs on some of the 1hb weapons (Riders have a much more reasonable 1he req). Fastattack and Sneakattack would be nice, but the reqs on these are generally LOWER and therefore it's not as necessary that this is buffable.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------



Enforcers will get buffs for their melee skills. I will make sure your recommendations for the skills are seriously looked at, and can definately see reasons for the ones listed.


quote:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Weapon Damage
Weapon damage is falling off compared to other classes. With the Adventurer changes in 14.4 this will become more apparent. MAs should do more damage than us, just not that much more. We need a boost, whether in weapons, better buffs, or both. Regarding adventurer changes, give that new person in the character creation room a reason to choose an enforcer: �Melee� hmm, advs get all those great weapons and healing, I might as well go Adv for melee�.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------



As stated above, Enforcers will get buffs to enhance their melee skills that will aid in damage. Also, we are looking at adding Enforcer weapons which will do good damage. No current weapons will be boosted, but I'll make sure the new ones kick literal arse.


quote:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physical Dominance is not worth the IP
Go and look at the SI line of nanos for enfs. Raising SI nearly 200 points (DARK BLUE) for about 20 points of init gain is simply *NOT* worth it. We have a hard enough time with IP as it is. Physical Dominance needs to be upgraded or removed. It�d be nice to have a higher level buff as well, but that would mean raising SI even higher. I don�t want to have to max 5 dark blue nanoskills, I�m already maxing 4, with one at halfway.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------



[b]Physical Dominance will most likely not be changed. The reason for this is because Enforcer weapon buffs (Headcracker for example) use Sensory Improvement. That means the new weapon buffs to come will also use Sensory Improvement, and there will be more reason to keep that skill increased, rather than just increasing it for one buff.


quote:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Challenger
There�s much concern that Challenger isn�t worth the nanoshutdown. At lower levels it just isn�t viable because your team lives and dies by mongo. At higher levels it gets better, but is still too much of a special case nano. With more members than any other line of our nanos, you�d think it would get used more, at all levels.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------



There is a possible change to the Challenger line in the works, which also affects the upcoming weapon buffs. I am working on getting the proposal from Andrew Griffin for you to look over because we'd love to have your input. So, more information is coming


quote:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gaps in nano lines
There are gaps in Mongo and Fear lines that render them much less effective between 100-135 or so. This may be due to the playstyle of most people (i.e. start fighting vets around L100); not doing what the designers intended.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------



This is intended (and may be reflective of the type of MOBs you are fighting). The Mongo and Fear lines are not 'full' lines because the Enforcer is not meant to be a real or primary crowd controlling profession.


quote:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rage Nanocost is not balanced with root nanocost
The fact that a startup root has the same chance of landing as Burden of Atlas is *wrong*. Enforcers will quickly run out of nano, unless they resist quite a few. To go along with this, nano resist is unbalanced in this respect because it�s extremely difficult, if not impossible, for an enforcer to raise Nano Resist early in their career.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------



Based on your report, we will be looking at the Rage line, its nanocost, and the line in general. More information right back at you once I have it.

Weapon Variety
We need more viable weapons. Plain and simple. It would also be nice if they were one skill weapons (for enfs at least). The last thing I need is to see a new weapon and say �Wow, that looks great! GRR it�s 33% HW, forget it�. We don�t need any more dark blue skills to max.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------



Related to one of the questions above, we are looking at introducing new Enforcer weapons (and more general weapons that Enforcers can use). No hard stats on what skills they will use, but I'll make clear that the weapons aimed at Enforcers should reflect their skills.


quote:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Armor Variety
Many people express the desire for more �tank-like� armor. I think that this is important, but also keep in mind that it may not fit everyone�s character. Not nearly as big of an issue as weapon variety.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------



[b]I know the cloak look isn't for everyone. Some changes were made to Tank Armor to make it more attractive (and it's good to see more people wearing it now), but I have suggested the idea to the Funcom item guru (official title). No ETA or WTA on implementation, but I'll make sure to follow this through.

RE: Visual Bugs...

All that is needed is a current toon's scale value. This is a single number, can probably fit in one byte. The server knows the current player scale provided it calculates this when the player first logs in. If anything changes this scale, it should be recalculated and sent too the client and to any client requesting details of that toon. I'm pretty sure this all works this way at the moment...

I see that the issue is then with identifying which clients need to also be sent the updated value... presumably the server has to maintain a 'relevant' list for every player so it knows what information need to be sent to that player's client and what doesn't. So, if toon A signals a scale change, all other clients with toon A on their 'relevant' list need to be sent the update too. This possibly doesn't have to occur immediately, scale is probably a low priority variable as far as network replication goes...

Of course, I'm guessing to some extent since I don't know anything about AOs network model, but 'relevant' lists and variable priorities are pretty common in online games.

Oh well, at least they are looking at it...
