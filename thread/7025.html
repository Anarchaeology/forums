Role-playing Dimensions
I've never played any MMORPGs before Anarchy Online, so after signing up for a "Massive Multiplayer Online ROLE-PLAYING Game" i was surprised to find very, very, very little role-playing. But there are those out there who do enjoy a good plot and some in character stuff. And i was thinking, why not designate one of the dimensions for the events and plots and role-playing only, and the other just for hack and slash.
Funcom could finally put in the option of switching the dimention your character is on so people could go to the dimension they want. Then they could rename Rubi-Ka 1 and Rubi-Ka 2 to Rubi-Ka (RP) and Rubi-Ka (killing stuff) it doesn't really matter. The point is, i think this would be a great idea and make the game alot more fun for the people who actually care about the story.

its hard to RP in AO
I've been playing RPGs for a long time now. DnD, robotech, shtuff like that. It's fun going into another world and living through a different set of eyes. And if you had a good GM your character would actually want to do what the "quest" was that day.

But honestly, how can you roleplay hunting? Are we hunting aggro mobs? No its to dangerous. Are we killing things that posing threats to our cities? No, the guards at the front gates can put the hurt on the mobs quite well on themselves. So what do we hunt?

I'm walking around in a nice grass field on a beautiful autumn day. Yes I've been mutated by some horrible accident and forced out of "normal" civilization, but I've found a group of people with similiar problems and quite enjoy their company. They've helped me through this hard time in my life and I'm starting to feel optomistic about my life. I stop to examine an unusually beautiful sunset and BAM! some atrox smacks me in the back of head with a hammer. I turn to defend myself and 5 other "normal, civilized" humans open fire at me. As I fall to the ground dying I see them going on to attack my best friend in a similiar manner and I fear for my new friends. I die. Then some jerk adds insult to injury by walking over, ripping out my guts to sell on the market.

BTW we are the ones that did that to this poor mutant. What kind of sadictic freaks are we?

Mission? Your mission is to go into a company and steal in item. Fine, I could see doing that. But to get the full reward, you have to kill everything in there. Why? To cover your tracks? Bla. How many human mobs have we all killed? Do none of us have a concscience anymore? Yes I know life has been cheapened by the insurance system, but still you think you'd get a bit sick to your stomach after a while. Either that or your a cold hearted, remorsless killer with virutally no manners.

So either we can role play sociopaths (fun for a while but if everyones doing it it's silly) or try to do a on and off roleplay. Well I'm in town well so I'll adopt my "personality" again. Bla. And with the insanely difficult mobs, frequently classes are forced into certain behaviors. You cannot be a jack of all trades, or even a bit eccentric. There are many rules you have to follow, and everyone kinda turns into a clone of some standard for their class.

Yes you can be an atrox, but thats the most involved a character will get usually.

Maybe if we could get experience for doing other things this wouldn't be so bad. Ok, lets change that, if we could get worthwile experience for non-combat action. I know you have said you have decided against that, I for one thing thats an awful decision. This game does get boring... and soon there will be alternative games in the sci-fi genre.
