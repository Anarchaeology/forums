Black Market: Fixer's Domain.
In the real world, think of the black market... You don't want to be connected with it... When you deal with things on the black market, you deal with someone who has connections in that market (Fixer) so that nothing can be traced back to you.

You want to buy a special something? You have to find someone who has connections in the black market. You never have direct access to it unless you're one of the outlaws who handles the merchandise.

If you get traced, poof, now you gotta wait for your stats to go back up, and have to complete some loyalty missions. Your Mission Tokens were deemed as gained only by interest of treason and might be deleted if you do not prove your loyalty within so many game days (that you are logged in). Something like this, or maybe loose a level(head trauma from severe beating)... I dunno.

The point being... Honorable citizens do not have access to underground markets besides through their personal "connections"
