Titles. How they should and shouldn't be attained.
Should be attained by:
Killing people on the opposite side of the conflict.

Shouldn't be attained by:
Killing people on the same side of the conflict

It's that simple. If this was implimented, title farming would be eliminated (or would be a lot harder). Why should you earn a title for killing a fellow clanner/omni? Sparring shouldn't grant any reward...it should only be for testing/fun. This would also end all the accusations for the legitimacy of people's titles and how they acquired them.
