Missions, make them fun:
Missions...when I have half an hour before work I used to be able to throw in one or two missions. That was okay. Now at lvl 98, If I want to go on a team mission I gotta spend an hour getting getting a group, then 4 hours completing the mission. Solo mission still take an hour on their own and are all the way across the world.

I'd like to see an option box in the mission window for "Short Missions" These would have like treament stims or something repetative as a reward. Remeber when the newbie missions only gave out stims? Have them not be miles away in the same zone and consster of 10-20 rooms. No rare prizes or chests, no big cash or xp reward, no token, or maybe just the +1 token no matter the level to prevent exploits. Just some contained fun that can be done in 20 minutes before work. Is this too much to ask? Quite often I don't play AO because I just don't have 4 hours to spare to do stuff. Anything along the lines of shorter missions works for me. Pop in, pop out. I'm sure someone backs me on this.
