My point is that there was a synch issue between his client, the server and your client.
Did the damage indicated on his screen correspond with the damage indicated on yours?


Since the 'ghost' damage is roughly equal to the damage of one crit (including heal delta), we can probably assume that the next hit was a crit that had not yet registered.
The sent data probably hadn't been acked by either his client, your client or the server. What usually happens in AO is that the non-acked data is stuck until the next packet pushes it through.

That's why you see a lot of pseudo double hits when you get laggy.
