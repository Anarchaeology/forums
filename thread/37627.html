Integrity Skill / Karma in AO !
This will add a dimensionality of game storyline creation unseen yet, but also easy to accomplish, with a unique and enjoyable game interaction for the players:

You have a set of Integrity skill points. Like xp they add up according to virtuous tasks and things you do in the game. If and when you die, your points are weighed and where your rezz is delineated from a chart based on your integrity points as weighed against the other factors surrounding your death.


I really like my idea of Integrity points, and will go on toi add another concept to go in conjuction with this.

Based on your acts.. your integrity points will go up or down. When you die, based on your integrity level is where you will resurrect.

High integrity rezz's you close to home and in safe territories..

Real low integrity and you ressurect in the middle of a volcano on a small island in Northern Wailing Wastes, or some other forsaken place like Mayhen Area..

could be alot of fun !

To avoid chain deaths, we could make it that anh death will bring your integrity skill back to medium, or zero, however you choose to create the system.
