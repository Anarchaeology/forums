Tier 1 Keeper Armor
1. Find 18x Visions of the Unshakable
Known to drop from Archaic mobs in second part of Unredeemed Temple.

2. Find 6x Consanguineal Embryo of Annwn
Known to drop from guards outside boss room in Unredeemed temple in Elysium and from Archaic mobs in Unredeemed Temple in Scheol.

3. Find 6x Spongy Novictum
Found on outdoors spirit mobs. Good spot is NW of Port Seven in Elysium.

4. Turn these items into the Keeper of IPS to recieve a Yellow Glyph of Aban
The Keeper of IPS is located in the IPS store in Jobe Harbor, Market, and Plaza.

5. Find a Canister of Pure Liquid Notum and combine it with your Yellow Glyph of Aban to make a Yellow Glyph of Aban - Awakened
Canisters of Pure Liquid Notum drop from all shadowlands mobs. A good spot is from Chimeras north of the portal into Scheol. 550 QFT skill required.

6. Use a Spongy Novictum on your Yellow Glyph of Aban - Awakened to recieve a Glyph of the Knight 600 Psychology skill required.

7. Use your Glyph of the Knight on a piece of jobe armor to make it First Tier Keeper Armor

8. Rinse and repeat steps 4-6 for all armor pieces
The Yellow Glyphs and Canisters of Pure Liquid Notum are unique so you can only hold one at a time.



I got most of this information from this great thread( http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sh...threadid=183504 ) but customized it for keepers.
