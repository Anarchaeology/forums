Wear slot suggestions
I would like to see two new wear slots implemented. A face slot and a backpack slot.

The face slot would double up the head slot items however only social items would be changed to go in this slot, to prevent people from wearing two helmets, basically its the same mount point as the headslot items but it would say, let you wear your favorite pair of katara or keelar glasses while wearing a hood or a hood with a helmet.

The backpack slot is fairly self evident, same mount point as the back slot but given the prevelance of cloaks, coats, robes, tank and the absolute necessity in survivability the added AC gives, its getting to be incredibly rare that you see someone actually wearing a backpack. A backpack only slot would address this, and bring back the backpack into something useful to actually wear.
