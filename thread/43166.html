Story Organizations...
So I was hunting bothackers in Pleasant Meadows with a group of friends when suddenly I receive orders from Omni-Pol.

[Omni-Pol Command]: Clan disturbance detected in Meetmedere. All available Omni-Pol are ordered to investigate and keep the peace. Do you accept these orders? Yes or no?

I accept the orders and a mission icon appears.

------------------------
Mission Info:
Description: Omni-Pol orders you to travel to Meetmedere and ensure the peace and safety Omni-Tek employees for 1 hour (You must work under 25% suppresion gas or lower). You may receive a bonus if your actions lead to the death of several clan members.

Payment: 100,000 credits, 5 tokens
------------------------

My team member receives similar orders by Omni-Med in that they require support in case things get out of hand and violence errupts.

... So we both depart for Meetmedere. There to greet us are not only several Omni-Pol and Omni-Med, but loads of unruly clan members. The battle has already begun, and we jump into the fray. Several of our fellow citizens are injured and my partners promptly begins his healing routine to bring them back to good health. Myself, I grab my support beam and start bashing any and every clan member I see. W00t!!

[OOC] So you may ask how did the clan members get there? Well their clan story organizations gave them a similar tell:

[Knights of Camelot]: Omni-Tek injustices reported by fellow clan members in the vicinity of Meetmedere. All available knights unite and defend our freedom! Will you join the cause? Yes or no?
