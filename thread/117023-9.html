Re: Opening more forums?
quote:
Originally posted by Cz
1. Player moderators. They would have to respect, follow and enforce the social guidelines at all times, and would also have influence on them. 2-4 moderators per forum maybe, in addition to the ARKs and myself having access anywhere. Some system of application and approval would be needed.
On the issue of appointing moderators, a much easier way is to simply let the users of the boards collectively self-moderate them. Root a post at the top of every category explaining clearly what the rules are and ask the readers of the boards to report posts that violate the rules. Be sure to make it clear that not liking what someone is saying is not in itself a violation and that only true violations should be reported. This would eliminate the need for you to spend a lot of time reading through all the posts. You would only need to give attention to the posts referred to in complaint posts you receive, which would be vastly less workload than the way you're doing it now. If there is a violating post, and no one is bothered enough to report it, then it isn't really a problem and requires no attention.

People are really quite good, when give a little leadership, at moderating their own behavior. If you devote your effort to providing that leadership, rather than running around trying to single-handedly monitor everything, your problems will take care of themselves.
