This is an OOC post.

I have to come down on the side of "roleplay is best between OOC friends, even if IC enemies."

Roleplay is a situation that requires cooperation on the part of the participants. It requires more cooperation - and indeed trust - to roleplay conflict or emnity, because without that kind of OOC trust, you get a situation like this, where Players are arguing OOCly with Players over how to play. And instead of arguing, you could be playing right now. The idea is for everyone to have fun, not for anyone to "win" or "own" someone. We can't win, and we're all - OT, Neutral, Clan - in this together, OOCly speaking.

Funcom has already proven their green-named, GM-controlled mobs are going to steam-roll over us in the service of "their" stories, no matter how powerful we become gamewise and no matter what we are roleplaying ourselves. I think it's safe to say that the only part we will be playing in the overall struggle between Omni-Tek and the Clans, will be that of the victims. That goes for everyone, OT, Neutral, Clan, regardless of guild or org. We've all been the victims for raids by the Masked Commandos, green-named stormtroopers, mutants and cyborgs and whatever else... and that is how it is going to stay. We have no power over this, not if we're the head of Omni-Pol and not if we're a Clan footsoldier. We never will.

But what we can have, is fun. Together. Friends (or at least allies) OOCly, while enemies ICly. Coordinate OOCly to get the "rules" straight before you start with a scenario with someone, and then you can keep your roleplay scenario moving instead of arguing, and in the end, we can all have a bit of fun.

So. Soapbox done. Look for fun, that's how we beat Funcom at their little game

Neutrals suddenly become even more difficult to play - in short it is suddenly no fun and everyone quits. So while you can say something _should_ happen due to x,y,z the game mechanics, player knowledge and 'game balance' mean that this is impractical.

I said it before, if Omni-Pol want to have places 'policed' they should police them themselves.
