Boss room -Exit mission- Buttons
As many people are aware, team missions are GIGANTIC. they almost engineered to be a maze. And after completing the boss, you are forced to runn through the many levels to exit the mission. This process often makes the team BREAK up because being lost while some are out immediately is sooo discouraging. What is worse is that sometimes you lose the map because you crashed or something or ran out of mission and you run into unfinished room and get slayed easily. That really really sucks. It often takes around 5-10 minutes for some to get out of the mission because they cant find the exit. Im only level 109 right now but team missions at that level are just massive. I can only immagine level 200 team missions. NO ONE WANTS TO FIND THIER WAY OUT OF MISSION!!!!

My solution----- create an exit mission button in the boss room corner that can only be used when the mission is over.

Why?

1) It saves us a HUGE walk out of the mission and prevents those who have lost the map from wandering into unfinished rooms with mobs in them.

2) It will prevent less teams from breaking up by catering to the lack of patience that some people have towards other teamies.

3) This is perfectly reasonable and wouldnt be hard to implement at all. It doesnt require new content or whatever , just a little programming here and there to warp us out of mission.

4) (as of now) No one seems to dislike this idea and i doubt ANY one would appose this idea, it just doesnt make sense because the walk out of a team mission is , no fun, senseless, time consuming and at times dangerous.
