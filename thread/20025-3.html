My 2 cents worth
In my experience there is just two uses for a Challenger

1. Buff up those skills for equiping purposes
2. Kill the 100%-er or Boss in a mission, and only then if it is not a close thing (otherwise I want to have my Mongo handy)

I have found that when solo-ing I can easily kill an orange in before the challenger expires, which forces me to wait until the block expires before I can reclaim my nano and continue (which takes about 2 minutes). This sort of pointless waiting is something I think FC should be cutting out. When taking on more than one Orange I need to have Mongo handy until the last mob anyway.

Maybe a way to improve things would be to have the nano block cleared by a nano recharge kit (which would have to then be allowed while blocked). That way you couldn't clear it in battle, but wouldn't have to wait afterwards.

If they remove all the restrictions altogether it is just going to become an automatic buff for us guys, and that will mean more hanging around to re-nano anyway.

Ok, I got to say, challenger is very useless for alpha strikers. Why? Because the damage just gets lost in the range of the weapon and the reload time is enough so that challenger really doesnt do much damage.

HOWEVER, for us rare enforcers that are using really really fast weapons, challenger is a god sent. I have 2 enforcers, one is a dual rider executioner enf, and one is a dual MTI sword enf.

I use challenger all the time with my MTI sword enforcer. She kicks serious butt. Her name is Tenstrike(omni), btw.

Just for those that dont know what an MTI sword is: ql 47 3-50 damage, .6 attack, 1.1 recharge (or something very close to that).

With mongo run once and challenger run once, she can solo most just pure reds. Why? Because my challenger is doing 1/3 to 1/2 my damage. I hit over 40 times in 44 seconds. That's over 40 times challenger gets added to the damage I'm doing. And the best parts are: the weapon gets faster as the ql goes up, my initiative goes up as I level, and challenger gets longer. So, I'll hit faster, longer, and harder later on with MTI and challenger nanos.

Challenger is to help fast weapon enforcers, not alpha strike enforcers. Yes, rider executioners are alpha strike weapons.

EDIT: And for those wondering, she isnt twinked at all either. She's using armor(gasp) her own lvl. No tank armor or coat. And weapons that usually she could use better and is killing just barely pure reds. If she was twinked out, they wouldnt stand a chance.
