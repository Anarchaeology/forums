I agree with bcbdla on this point:

We don't have enough to go on to know for sure how intrusive the system, if it were implemented, would be.

However, it is well within our rights to express concerns about how characters will be named in the game, and to let Funcom know our preferences.

From what I read in the past ( and I will try to find sources to back this up) I was under the impression that :

a) We would be able to have a first and last name that was not unique. But that our middle name or nick would be unique so that we could tell who the real 'mccoys' were in case of impersonation.

b) There was no hint given that the first and last names would be forced on us by autogeneration. Far from it, the impression from the devs for most character features has been 'customize customize customize'.


And lastly I find the tone about 'accommodating' us very patronizing.
The devs have shown their interest in what the gamers want before, why should that change because this time it's some roleplayers that are concerned about a possible 'feature' of the game?

How many people seriously 'like' the idea of someone else choosing their name for them in a game?

Given a simple choice of yes or no, I would think most gamers, not just rp'ers would rather choose their name.

Some would not care either way, or be willing to give it a try if that was the way it was going to be.

However *if* you had complete control over what system to use, are you telling me you would choose to force people to have random names?

Explain the positivity of that choice please?

/ooc

You are right, we have been premature in putting any thought or enthusiasm into this game.

Unfortunately it is our way as roleplayers to get excited about a game we have been waiting for for over a year and to form communities and to already start developing stories based on the rich world and history Funcom has given us. And been encouraged to do so with Funcom's equal enthusiasm for our fiction writing and guild and rp fansites.

Like all the other prospective gamers out there we have hit every board looking for scraps of info the devs give us, and followed the ideas and changes that have come about. Most we have been ecstatic about. This possible naming system has only just now been brought up. And it was contrary to what the devs lead us to believe (unwittingly so perhaps).

Like other gamers we have created websites showing our enthusiasm but instead of it being about the skills/professions/screenshots/pvp vs pk etc. It's about the possible story.

This is what happens when roleplayers get behind a MMORPG. It has happened before with EQ, AC, Shadowbane, Horizons, and other games when they were in development. It will happen again.

We as roleplayers follow what is going on on the dev boards. We don't blithely ignore what the Devs tell us and then get all scrappy when it doesn't turn out the way WE want. That's not it.

But we do care about names, traditions and histories. And when those may be threatened by a change that as far as I can see is not really beneficial or called for, then yes, a few of us are going to get very vocal about it. And some more vocal than others.

However, I don't think we are really holding the devs to 'ransom' or making undue demands on them. Heck, they could just do what ever they want.

We are just hoping they will listen to us just like they have listened to other gamers concerns.

And since we don't have much to go by, I think like bcbdla has said, we should not get too up in arms about this until we know the details of the system. Let's express our opinions about possible naming systems, but also trust the devs that what they come up with won't be as damaging as rumored.
