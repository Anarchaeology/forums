Nice thought right? Certainly possible, if Funcom does this right (And I have faith). Possible stumbling blocks tho:

Lag. These things could be super-laggeriffic. However, I've seen 100+ in Omni-Trade and it's passable, so I'm pretty hopefull on this.

VERY IMPORTANT - Make it so that you can't go down in PvP titles. When people are scared of losing their titles, they will be reluctant to enter into Team PvP (because you have to depend on someone you might not entirely trust). However, I believe you'll see alot more action if you simply can't lose your title.

Balance:
DISCLAIMER - I don't know everything there is to know about this game. I do know some however, and quite a bit about the classes I've played (Enf, Doc, Fixer) and I think alot.

Sure, your MA might not be able to wreck that GA fixer, but MA wrecks NT, which wrecks fixer. As far as PvP balance goes, here's how I would group the classes

Melee
Enforcer
Adventurer
Martial Artist

Ranged
Fixer
Agent
Soldier

Nuker
Nano Technician
Meta-Pysicist
Engineer

Support
Bureacrat
Trader
Doctor

So, the idea is:
Melee > Ranged, Melee = Support, Melee < Nuker
Ranged > Support, Ranged = Nuker, Range < Melee
Nuker > Melee, Nuker = Ranged, Nuker < Support
Support > Nuker, Support = Melee, Support < Ranged

So how is this accomplished?
Melee > Ranged - Melee get self-only dodge ranged buffs (might screw up PvM). Hm...
Melee = Support - Crats & Traders just root the tank. Doctors just heal. Standoff.
Melee < Nuker - Nukers can do massive amounts of dmg to Melee before the melee manages to close in.
Ranged > Support - Sure, you can root them, but they can still shoot you.
Ranged = Nuker - Both ranged dmg. Toss-up between who kills the other first. Nukers do more dmg. Ranged has more health.
Range < Melee - As described above, although fixers with their GA scare me.
Nuker > Melee, Nuker = Ranged - See above
Nuker < Support - Support get to debuff Nukers to oblivion. Traders deprive, Docs get a nano init debuff. Crats hm...

You also have to think about this in terms of Group PvP. For instance, sure in duels docs kick ass because they heal like crazy, but when a doc has 3 people on him, he's gonna go down pretty quick. Engies suck atm, but what if you can't FIND the Engie that owns the bot? Same with Crats. Possibly even make then unrootable, but snareable. Also, NTs get to use their funky AE nukes finally. And let's say a MA or Enf gets rooted. Just switch targets. Odds are, there's somebody close enuf to you to hit.

Also, gotta make a few small changes to how PvP works.
Nano Resists need to work alot better, although a resisted nuke should land you with 50% dmg, not zero. Alternatively, Nano Resist determines the % effect. Thus, a root on a person with maxed nano resist lands, but the duration is like, 1 or 2 seconds. Nano Resist should DEFINITELY modify how much dmg you take from nukes though, and not whether the nuke lands or not.
Have to MAKE SURE roots break when you take damage.
Damage should probably be lowered. ACs and evades 2x or something. PvP's no fun when you're an Enforcer and die in 2 or 3 seconds with 3 people hitting you.
TMS & NS durations might have to be decreased.

Oh yeah, and an easy fix: clanners, neutrals, and omni should have different colored names. And outlow colored names on bots. This way, in PvP, it'd be easy to see who your enemies are.
Also, and I'm not sure how easy this is, but how about putting someone's class in their health bar? Or how about this, when you click on a character, it shows the person's health bar. Within the health bar, you see class of the character, and the color of the text is the affiliation of the character. So you might see
code:
 ------------
|  Engineer  |
 ------------
Sounds crazy I know. Maybe one or two of these ideas will get into a patch someday?
