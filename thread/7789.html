Ok, may I try to break it down here?
Alright, again, personally, I don't agree with this story-line. It too-closely resembles a tragic day in current US history. Now mind you...the key word in that statement is "CURRENT".

I'm an American. I feel for every single nation that has been struck with the grief and pain that terrorism causes. I really do. But it seems to me (and I could be off-base on this), the only people that are complaining about the story-line are Americans. The only people that are *for* the story-line are non-Americans. I guess all you non-Americans (and I don't like to generalize) might understand this if you had something this traumatic happen to *your* country RECENTLY, and someone decided to write a "story-line in a MMORPG" about it. Don't you think that would re-open semi-closed wounds for you? Don't you think it would make some people remember something they were trying very hard to forget?

I'll be honest...if this part of the story happened even a year after 9-11, I would be OK with it. I really would be. But, like Drabin said, Time heals all wounds. And it has only been 4 months for Americans. (Which I'd like to add, are the bulk of players in AO)

Perhaps I made no sense to you. Perhaps I just needed to rant. I'm not sure. But what I do know is that sometimes things need to be rethought. Sometimes they need to be changed. Why? Because it would be a sensitive thing to do. Perhaps we all need to learn a bit more about sensitivity.
