One problem I see with LD team members being automatically enlisted back in the team is if the team had recruited a new member in the mean time. I've been on several teams where people have LD'd, and after 5 minutes of waiting, we recruit a new person.

Another problem is that teams cannot recruit if a member is engaged in a fight. So the code to add an LD player would have to circumvent this check.

There are probably other issues too.

I'm not suggesting that these problems can't be overcome, rather, I'm suggesting that it could be more complex than it might initially appear.
