Try reading the last 10+ pages before posting questions, I can answer some of your questions anyway even though I haven't been to E3 or in any beta.

2. Full auto...If you use a "Newbie Gun" the gun you start out with that has infinitie ammo...Can you use full auto and never drain a clip for massive damage?

Only newbie gun with a special ability is Fixer SMG, and it has Burst. Useful, but only if it has a high amount of IP in it.

Whompas are place to place and anyone can use, the grid requires that you get skills (lesser grid jump, for example) to get to higher levels of it. The higher you go, the more specific areas you can go to and the more farther away from main cities you can go to.

Yes.

6. Are there banks? Storage places for money?

You have banks to put stuff in, and your money ALWAYS stays with you (you can die, you still have your money)

Re: Hey
quote:
Originally posted by AnarchyAo


1. If you are in a vehicle and a monster is in pursuit of you (chasing you down) and you jump out of the vehicle, then try to jump back in...What will happen? Can you not get back in because your in attack mode?
You don't ride a vehicle, you become a vehicle. And no, no combat as a vehicle.

2. Full auto...If you use a "Newbie Gun" the gun you start out with that has infinitie ammo...Can you use full auto and never drain a clip for massive damage?
I doubt the newbie gun would do "massive damage", and there's a recycle time in between uses of the Full Auto skill{Edit: And as posted by someone else (can't see the post in Edit mode), only certain weapons have access to the Full Auto skill}

3. Can ONLY fixers access "the Grid"? It seems to make no sense of having a Whom-pa system if there is the grid everyone can access.
Everyone can access the Grid, but only those skilled in computer literacy can reach the upper levels. And the Whompas require no skill to enter, and its much safer than trying to walk the Grid, falling off, and dying.

4. Can you leave the "Backyard" or newbie area you were talkin bout you entered when u create a charchter?
Yes, and there are a few monsters outside the gates to kill, but the backyard is created to get you in a few groups, and maybe find one you'll stick with forever.

5. What if you trade with someone and then you die right after without saving...Do you get the item back you traded? And if that is not true than how will that work? You know, will only things like trading be saved? Not Exp and how much money u get? And also I think you said you lose the money you gained if you dont save and u die? If thats true, where does the money go? And what if you sold something to someone do you lose the money still? And if thats true can someone trade with you in a PVP zone, then kick your ass and get back the money?
If you traded the item to someone, it's theirs forever (unless they die). You only lose what items/xp/credits you had since your last save/level. The items simply disappear, leaving no corpse to find. And, to reiterate, you lose only what you have on you, not what you once had at one point in time. Hehe, what if you died, and got every single item you've ever had in the entire game back, just because you never saved?

6. Are there banks? Storage places for money?
Yes, there are banks. And you have near unlimited storage in your apartment. Stash stuff there.

7. Me and everyone else in the AO community is a little confused about the whole IMPLANTS thing, can you tell us more info on implants or ask the devs? Ask them what the implants can be used for, what are there uses?
Implants are used to increase certain skills or attributes. Most last longer than, say, a buff spell, but none (as far as I know) are permanent.

8. Does having your PVP rating good, meaning your a "badass" good, or bad? Does it effect the way ppl/NPC's trade with you?
I'm sure it just intimidates other players who are thinking about trying to kill you, shouldn't affect your relationship with merchants.

9. Will different races not be able to buy from certain merchants?
The only deciding factor in whether merchants like you or not is your faction (Omni, Rebel, or Neutral).

10. Do the devs think AO will be ready for release with ALL the features and things they were hoping to get implemented into the game actually be implemented when the game is released?
From what we've seen, everything except the minor things (tweaking classes to get them balanced enough) is in and ready for release (like vehicles, which surprised everyone).

11. And finally, Was the lag bad when you played? Could you tell any signs of "Unstable conditions" -quote from Lum the Mad.
His opinions were based on version 9.x of AO. Since then, they've released version 11.x, and from what we've heard, it's MUCH more stable. Besides, ignore Lum, he's only trying to start rumors.
