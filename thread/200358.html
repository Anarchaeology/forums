Memo to the office of Philip Ross
Mr. Ross,

As you know, the Homo Nano(TM) breed of humans is incapable of living outside of Rubi Ka due to the planet's supply of notum which the nanomages are dependent upon to survive. The Nanomage Liberation Front and other fringe groups have been going on about the supply of notum on Rubi Ka and the long term effects of it on the Homo Nano(TM) breed that is manufactured by Omni-Tek. Far be if for me to overstep my bounds, sir, but I believe that we are missing out on some opportunities and a way to satisfy the critics.

What we need to do is set Omni-Med to work on improving the breed so that it can survive outside of Rubi Ka while still retaining it's special abilities in the manipulation of nano technology. I realize that this has not been viewed as economically viable in the past, however I believe that we can not only cover the costs, but turn a profit and make you look like a savior in the process.

I propose that after Homo Nano(TM) is tweaked so that it is no longer dependent on notum to live, that we license the process of creating Homo Nano(TM) beings to other businesses for the purpose of employment in those businesses. What business owner wouldn't be thrilled with the increased productivity that a nanomage can provide with it's advanced use of nano technology? They have already proven to be invaluable here on Rubi Ka with their unique skills and I am sure that, with the proper genetic alterations, they will prove to be a hot commodity elsewhere. To make it even sweeter, those nanomages depend on notum to do their work - notum that Omni-Tek can provide. So, not only do we make profit on the Homo Nano(TM) production license, we promote notum consumption as well.

Now, here is the bonus - YOU will the one to announce to everyone that Omni-Tek really is a caring corporation that wants only what is best for everyone and that it has gone through great expense to ensure the survival of Homo Nano(TM) during these uncertain times. This will satisfy the NLF and the rest of the lunatics so that at least one or two less groups isn't out there trying to blow up our notum mining facilities.

It's a win-win, sir. I eagerly await your response and hope that you might put me in charge of this project should you decide it a worthwhile endeavor.

Regards,

Janella McCallagher

The Nanomage's skill lies with having the Notum be a part of their bodies. To remove the Nanomage's reliance on Notum to survive would be to remove their relationship with it and thereby removeing what allows them to be so adept at useing nanotechnology in the first place.
