Simplified Prospectus and Financial Highlights
Omni-Tek Corporation Simplified Prospectus

Founded in 12272 AD, on old Earth, Omni-Tek Corporation is the largest, most well-established, and diversified companies in the galaxy. Omni-Tek provides an integrated range of services to humanity. The company provides its services through numerous departments, for example:
Omni-Trans
Omni-Pol
Omni-Admin
Omni-Sci.

Omni-Tek specializes in providing these services in logistically difficult, environmentally sensitive, and politically unstable environments.

Although headquartered on Omni-Prime, several departments, including most of the Omni-Tek Board of Directors, have relocated to Rubi-Ka during this fiscal year. The company's common stock is traded on the GSE under the symbol "OMNI".
