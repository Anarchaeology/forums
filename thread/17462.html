A suggestion that may helps some...

I recently upgraded my soundcard to a SoundBlaster Extigy, it's basically an external USB sound card/device.

Since then I haven't crashed in 20k or BS on that machine (althouh it still has a tendency to drop to slide show mode).

Being a semi obsessive techie type I had to figure out why the soundcard change had this effect. I did discover the likely cause.

WinXP/2k and possibly ME/98 use steering to that multiple devices can share resources (most notably irq's). This is a great feature 98% of the time. It starts to cause problems when two devices that are sharing a resource (once agin most notibly irq's) recieve large amounts of data at the same time. For instance, your soundcard and video card share an irq, you zone into a new area and all the sound/video data is sent to the appropriate devices, this causes a bottleneck and neither device gets all of it's data and causes the game to crash and/or lock. This particular issue seems to pop up if any two or more of the following devices share an irq: Video, Sound, or Network connection device (network card, modem, usb network adapter).

My problem was that my pci sound card and my pci wireless network adapter where sharing an irq, by changing my sound to a usb device which was on a seperate irq my crashes stopped. I have since switched the pci wireless adapter in one of my other systems to a usb one which also stopped that system from crashing.

-Xasi

PS in Win2k/XP you by default can't manually change irq's due to ACPI, the only way to disable ACPI is to load a different HAL suring setup. If that doesn't make sense you are better off not trying it as it can really hose a system up if done improperly.
