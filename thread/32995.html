Encouraging Tradeskills from low levels.
One of the disappointing things about this game is the lack of development of the tradeskill processes, and the lack of their use in game at low levels.

So, as a suggestion, how about adding a few items to the startup equipment list to facilitate new players understanding of the Tradeskill processes.

Adventurer - QL1 Gem Cutter
Agent - QL1 Repressor, Limited time (12 played hours?) Lockpick
Bureaucrat
Doctor - QL1 Bio-Commutator
Enforcer - QL1 Aggro-Enhancer
Engineer - QL1 Gem Cutter, Limited Time Lockpick
Fixer - QL1 Hacking Tool, Limited Time Lockpick and Bomb tools
Martial Artist - QL1 Aggro-Enhancer
Metaphysicist
Nanotechnician
Soldier - QL1 Aggro-Enhancer
Trader - QL1 Bio-Commutator

I can't really think of anything for the three other professions, any suggestions welcome.

All the items included should work with the mob loot dropped in the newbie zone, such as Monster parts, Gems and so on, and give new players access to the Tradeskill processes for a little bit, give them extra xp, and up their cash ratings.

You will note that the tank classes (and the agents repressors) are suggested to be given to get aggro enhancers, which means (hopefully) more team work in the newbie areas.
