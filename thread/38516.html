Final bug list and new content
The following is a list of things that should no longer be in this game(some of them should have never been in the game). Also, is a list of my suggestions for improvements too.

1. When someone puts an item down, I want to see the correct representation of that item and not a cube with ? on its sides. If someone drops a bow, I want to see a bow.

2. Give us more types of yalms. Just one style of car is boring. Maybe add weapons and the ability to transport others a la dropships and gunships. Maybe stretch our own bitmaps over our car to customize it at the very least.

3. Title caps. Title caps need to be relaxed a little. I play every day and it took me to 3 months to break the 150 title cap( I was capped at 122). That's 3 months of facing increasing tougher mobs without improving my skills. I almost quit because it was too monotonous. Some of my guildies did quit.

4. Mob pathing. After a year, I still have mobs that run into walls(while dealing lag damage), aggro me from across the entire mission and just pop through walls whenever they feel like it. Line of sight means LINE OF SIGHT. IF they don't see me from 4 rooms away then they don't aggro me and they CERTAINLY don't pop through walls and attack.

5. Lag damage. These days, "LD" does not mean "Link Dead" or Disconnect. It means Lag damage, "keep healing me even though the mob died 5 minutes ago." I don't know what kind of crazy network code you got, but the "tie goes to the runner." Once the mob is dead, he stops damaging people. Got it?

6. NODROPS. The only items in this game that should be nodrops are apartment keys.

7. Missions. Team missions are now boring and they have the added bonus of turning players against each other as they fight for boss loot(a whole different can of worms). Add multiple tasks to missions, occurring in several places across Rubi ka. The fixer has to bribe a man to get the team in location X. They fight their way to a terminal and hack it....

8. "Waiting for previous attack..." This one gets alot of MAs killed. Synchronize your client so that Brawl is really ready to use when the client says so.

9. Blaster beetles. Ummm, these things stick out of walls in indoor missions, attack from 3 rooms away, appear and disappear all over the mission. Remove them from indoor missions since they don't fit anyway.

10. Apartments. I have created several threads on apartments dating back to last year and so have others. Windows on the city would be nice. More than 10 items would be nice. A code to type in instead of a key would be nice(zone crash deleted my key and the ARK reset my apartment because he said he did not know how to give me a new key. I lost some nice stuff). Sitting on a couch like a person and not a yoga instructor would be nice.

11. Decranium MK II. This is one of the most camped armor items and it is one of the UGLIEST too. I just got a full set of Carbonum myself and the helmet looks like a cross between a waste-basket and the Sony robot dog. Make the unique armor look unique and sleek. The MK II Hood looks like low-resolution snot rag pulled over someone's head.

12. Casinos. We have entertainment districts and no entertainment. Casinos with traditional and new games are in order. Bet in-game credits for items and other loot. Even Ultima Online has casinos now and I think someone else posted the idea here as well so it is an idea that is ready to go. I have had the idea for this for months but wanted to save it up while I collected my other suggestions too. This one has the most potential.

13. Mob animations. When mobs are chasing, running, whatever, their arms and legs should move. They don't skid like some twisted Scooby Doo cartoon. One more time, when a mob chases something it RUNS. Arms and legs moving.

14. Unusable nanos. Some of the MA nanos cannot be self cast until 190+ for most people even though they are ql160ish(UVC). The skill requirements are too steep. This may be a problem for other professions as well. Lower the skill reqs to something more practical since all 5 nanoskills are dark blue to MAs(in this case).

15. PvP. heheh, this one will take awhile so let's just say that putting a damage cap on PvP fixed nothing. "We don't know how to balance our PvP system so we will just cap damage because people are complaining the fight is over too fast." That is NOT a solution.

16. Camping. Increasing the spawn time and lowering the chances of items dropping does NOT decrease camping, it INCREASES it. It turns people into psychos too. Remember, this is supposed to be a game. Leisure time, not a boot camp.

17. Swimming. We need a use for swimming. Someone with high swimming can fight in water and dive deep to maybe find treasure chests or quest items/loot. It would be really cool to see underwater missions with their own watery problems and monsters to deal with. Annd of course new underwater clothes/armor/vehicles for our underwater cities.

18. Hunting outdoors. One of the reasons everyone is in Home in BS is because there is nowhere for higher level players to hunt. There are higher level mobs but the spawn is too slow for people to hunt. We killed Enigmas in CAV and then they did not appear again for 30 mins. So we left. We need an abundant, diverse OUTDOOR hunting alternative with decent spawn(I am seeing those green mission walls in my sleep).

19. Storms. When I first saw the storms and the lightning it was neat and even beautiful but now the lag they create often causes me to lose connection. Quite annoying. Make a setting for turning off storms for those of us who have seen them enough and choose to not lose connect when they appear.

20. Mouse lag. Why does the mouse lag so bad in laggy areas? I can understand the graphics but the mouse is on its own thread. You did code the mouse on its own thread didn't you?

21. Token variety. Offer tokens for things other than missions.

22. Sports. Incorporate sports into the game along with appropriate sports-related implants. It would be fun to play some type of team sport based on soccer or basketball or baseball but with a sci-fi twist to it. Give implants that are specific to the game (like Kicking) and give them fairly low reqs so we can swap them out to play. Then have tournaments and standings and world championships, etc...

23. Battle statistics. Give us a setting we can enable to list battle statistics at the end of each fight. "So and so did the most damage with 23,456 damage. Player y critted the most with 5 crits. Player b took the most damage with 45,000 damage..."

24. Loot etiquette. With team loot came loot stealers and it is unfortunate but a system of exposing/blocking loot thieves might not be a bad idea. Change team looting so that once the team is on the boss level the team loot cannot be changed until the boss is killed or the mission ends/is deleted. Or announce to all players what loot dropped on the boss when he dies.

25. Hide coupons for game time on Rubi ka. Every month drop a few coupons for a free month of AO somewhere on the server.
