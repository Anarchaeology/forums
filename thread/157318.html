Summertime
The enemy is close. Very close. So close that the veteran agent can feel his breath. He can even reach out and touch his skin. If he wants to. And the smell... Sure smells like a true clanner. Suddenly. The look on the enemy's face. As if he heard something. Or saw something. Or thought he saw something. The agent holds tight, he knows that if he don't move, he will be just fine. But then the enemy looks right at him. Into his eyes. Blood freezes in his veins as the agent realizes he has been revealed. Slowly he raises his rifle. Gently puts pressure on the trigger. Takes aim, and...

"Oh, sorry sir, did I wake you? Here are your refreshments, like you ordered. Anything else I can help you with?"

Morphex looks up at the young lady, rather confused. The smell of ocean, the sound of seagulls, the warm sun giving his pale skin a brownish colour. Suddenly he remembers.

"No thanks young lady, here, take this for your trouble"

"Oh, that is way to generous sir! Thank you. Enjoy your stay on Omni Prime!"

As the solitus woman walks off to another guest, Morphex takes a sip of his drink, stretches his legs and yawns. Oh, the joy of vacation! No clanners, no work, and no reets taunting him for 12 whole days!

Back in business
As the door slides open, sunlight hits his eyes and burns them badly. When he two minutes later regains sight, he notices the ground has turned itself into a vertical position, hurting his nose in the process. Either that, or he just fell.

Morphex gets up, brushes dust off his coat, and proceeds through the gate towards the whompa. Some Meta-Physicist is performing a wierd act on the ground, screaming in pain, but he doesn't take much notice. Clanners usually do that when he is around.

The blue light illuminates his face as he walks into it, and for a brief moment he is nothing but a data stream floating through the air. Then he rematerializes. Morphex is back.
