Hostile actions by the Nanomage Liberation Front
At 20.00 RST yesterday, October 25th 29477, members of the clan organization Assembly made their way to a notum mining field in the Newland desert. Our objective was to perform a contracted major stress test/efficiency measurement job on the mining facilities of clan Cerberus. Two and a half tons of equipment had been pre-shipped to the location, including hi-tech quantum conductors, regulator readers, GPS equipment, plates to upgrade and strengthen the control tower and surrounding towers. All to the estimated cost of 75 million standard Rubi-Ka credits.

Assembly had sent out five members for preliminary work, setting up service towers and taking level readings. We were not heavily armed, as we didn't expect interruptions, merely protecting ourselves from possible aggressive wildlife. However, at approximately 20.30 RST we were stopped by a very heavily armed atrox, datascans showing him as a guard of the neutral organization Nanomage Liberation Front. Out of nowhere, several other people showed up, about 10 or 11 strong. We were restrained and rather brutally held back, as a man showing up on datascans as Chi "Aazamon" Fadri shouted orders about. His crew were told to find and destroy all equipment we might have with us, which they did. We were disarmed and circled, when Mr. Fadri told us that because of the contract we had signed with Cerberus, we had declared war with the Nanomage Liberation Front and Desert Winds combined. they claimed we were in contravention of the Borealis Pact. Claiming we were aiding the destruction of the nanomage breed, we were threatened with death and retaliation.

Between words, Mr. Fadri got physical with me personally, pushing me to the ground when I refused to cease the work Assembly does for other clans. He made it quite clear that this was a warning and that next time they wouldn't "be so nice". Other members of Assembly were held back rather hardhanded, especially Ryuo "Electronaut" Deckard , who was held between the teeth of a cat-morhped adventurer. It was at this point, about 20.50 RST, that one of the mining towers in the area blew up. We can only speculate in what caused this...bomb or stress we were supposed to have contained and controlled, had we not been stopped. The purpose of this job was to secure the towers, making them stabile and heighten the environmental integrity, something we were restrained from doing.

Luckily, clan Cerberus had noticed the fluctuation in their mining field, and also been called by one of Assemblys members who had not joined the operation. The assaultants were quickly surrounded and my team and I were released. Mr. Fadri was approached by President Anthony "Cogs" McDuff of Cerberus, but was able to escape with his crew before he could be questioned about the aggressive act against clan personnel and property.

Although no one were severly harmed, the actions of Nanomage Liberation Front is preposterous! Restraining and threatening people, using violence and destroying equipment seems to me like a contradiction of what a neutral organization should stand for! Patronizing people because they are not created or born nanomages, declaring war on the grounds of mainentance work?!

Let me quote a few bits from the Borealis Pact;
quote:
(...)It is the intent of this agreement, to put a stop to all notum mining, by reaping punishment on all those who would dare continue this assault on Rubi ka and her people(...)

(...)We, the undersigned members of the Borealis Pact, and the Organization members we represent, vow to take the following actions to prevent and correct the aforementioned wrongs:
A) Band together in defense of the planet and its people, by stopping any and all notum mining no matter the cost.(...)

(...)War shall be declared on all those who fail to meet the terms of the document, and all BorPac members will recognize those guilds and individuals and enemies, and seize any and all controlled lands, and either offer them back when BORPAC conditions are met, or redistribute them to BORPAC members.(...)
Is this the way to go? Declaring war with just about any person who do not agree with their vision of notum dwindling? They have yet to show proof of the planets ecological damage! Actions speak louder than words, and the fanatics of the NLF seems to be unable to both discuss the matter calmly or behave like human beings! If you're not one of them, you're a traitor to Rubi-Ka herself! It will not be tolerated and assembly will not bepushed around like this, bowing down to the demands of a pact we have never signed.

Mr. Fadri, it's time you start putting your money where your mouth is, producing hard evidence that you're indeed right in what your organization and other members of BorPac claim. If you can't, then you will in the end have more than one organization to answer to for your actions!
