Penalty for 'Friendly Fire'
Recently, I've seen a LOT of 'Omni' people wandering about the Omni cities, killing guards and traders.

One would think that Omni-Tek might take a 'dim view' of this diversion.

There is no 'faction system' as such in this game, where you could become less liked directly, but, there is a mechanism for adding a penalty for this sort of thing:

Proposed: Killing someone on your own side (not neutrals) should cause you to instantly LOSE the same number of 'token' points you'd get for a mission token.

This is really fairly minor as a penalty, but at least adds a bit more realism, and since token boards DO check on zoning, and unequip if you can't wear them (don't have the tokens), you could end up with a loss of skills till you 'do penance' by doing more missions to regain the lost tokens.
