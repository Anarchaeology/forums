Friday's Castle Toga Bash
Thunderous music, unbridled shouts, and raucous party vibrations shook the stone walls and floors of the castle in Pleasant Meadows on Friday night, as Devil's Advocate hosted their most recent party. In one of the most unique party events of the year, Director Thyme and her team, have continued to set the standard for parties that entertain all of Rubi-Ka.

Member's of the organization arrived early to secure the inside of the empty, Clan protected structure, and prepare for a Roman inspired Toga Bash, with a Middle-Ages twist. Planning and preparation was needed to overcome the task of getting guests to the site, without being shot by hostile guards.

Pre-party entertainment began at 5:00 PM EST with Gridstream Production's DJ Rythum, broadcasting live from Baboons. Anxious partiers crowded two of Rubi-Ka's most popular dance clubs, Baboons and Reets Retreat, as they awaited the ports that would take them to the final party site. At 8:00 PM EST, the Beacon Warping team of Raline and Abrii began to port the party guests to the Pleasant Meadows Castle.

DJ Rythum continued his broadcast until 10:00 PM EST, when DJ Tarryk took over. Broadcasting from the castle, Tarryk set up on a massive oak table in the main banquet hall. Dancers, partiers and guests crowded around the popular DJ, dancing and mingling on top of and around the table. Providing the throng with drinks and food, the Omni based entertainment company, Nano-Lust, was on-site.

There were about a dozen members of the clan fixer organization The Mockers in attendance to include Director Trellame. These dedicated people helped with the beacon warping team, and grid warped people that wanted to leave the party. The security team helped find people that had left the party and were lost in the hostile territory just outside the castle. The Mockers also made sure the DJ's and organizers of the party were teamed so they could be evacuated in case of a last minute security breech. Both Mockers and Abrii helped on very short notice when scheduling difficulties occurred a short time before the party was to begin.

At almost any given time, roughly 50 guests partied in the normally abandoned castle, displaying Roman Toga fashion, and variations on the white robe and sandal theme. One of the happy partiers walked away from the Bash with a new Martial Artist Suit, given away as one of the event prizes. Surprise visits from some of Rubi-Ka's more colorful personalities included Epin, Thyrin, Wojan and Airframe, who mingled with the rest of the room full of dancing and partying guests.

The main hall of the castle remained packed with partygoers until around 2:00 AM EST, when Tarryk and the remaining guests, moved the party to the more accessible location of Reets Retreat. In a dramatic display of convenience, DJ Tarryk self-terminated, leaving his messy, but still handsome corpse for the clean-up crew.

The last two occupants of the castle sat in the middle of the cavernous room pleased with the success of the night's events. But, there's no rest for the members of Devil's Advocate. DA activities coordinators Poizun (Clan Chapter), and Ditzie (Omni Chapter) were already forming the foundation for the next big event. Keep checking back to see what these two came up with.

When asked to comment on the party, Devil's Advocate Director Thyme replied "I just want to thank everyone that helped making this party possible. I had a great time watching everyone shake their money makers. It was great! I never expected such a big crowd. We are lucky to have such a great team to make these events rawk! Stay tuned, the next event seems like a doosey!"
