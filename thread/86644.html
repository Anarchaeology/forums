“Ode to my Muse”
by Randa "Poetica" Whites

In my darkest hours
In my brightest days
In my times of need
In my times of plenty
In my times of despair
In my times of joy
He is there
Giving me
Feeding me
Tending me
Inspiring me
He is my muse
