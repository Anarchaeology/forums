Suggestions for a better life :)
Hi, howabout adding a 'reload' shortcut? It does get a bit annoying to open the wear menu every time you want to reload that Gripo-com AKR between battles. Or any other 'low-max-ammo-weapon' ...or howabout an auto-reload after a fight finish?

Also, the new map roxxors after you got used to it, but I'd like to have it auto center on the player's location every time you open the map, how's that?

And why not add a 'place target/waypoint' function to the world map, so you can put out your own compass line marker, for easier navigation?

And do make some alternatives to the 'best' armors in the game.
Like another version of the 'Obtru' with the same stats, differnt look, but maybe change the weak/strong points to another AC type? I mean, at the moment every high level Agent, Martial Artist and Fixer looks the same (except GA wearing fixers)

By keeping the stats and just swapping the numbers around for the differnt AC's you do keep the new armor intresting, while making people stand out a bit more.

And please add more 'useless-but-still-fun-to-have-tradeskill-only-items' ...make it fun to be a tradeskill-trader/engineer!

good stuff
"waypoint-device" requiring 250 map nav to upgrade but upon loading it would allow a person to place up to 5 "pins" with user defined tags at varying coordinates in a mini-map.

example: coords 1008,1230 in GTC a person could place a pin and type "team is here". the pin stays until the player removes it, with a maximum of 5, total.


The next in a chain of related ideas was a map upgrade "Tracking device" requiring 325 map nav and allowing a player to encode another player's reference into their map, giving not only the standard yello directional arrow of the player, but a blue arrow facing the direction (even if out of LOS) of the tracked player. Only one player could be tracked at a time.

example: Player a targets player b and hits "g", drags the knowledge icon of player b to his/her mini map and now sees a blue arrow pointing toward player b as long as both are in the same zone. to cease tracking, player a delets the knowledge icon for player b.


various other Map-Reader tools could be implemented also, it really is a skill that most raise right now out of necessity rather than desire.


On a side note and a similar train of thought, I had an idea that for every 100 skill of map nav divided by 10, what if that was the amount of times you could leave a mission without losing your mini map? Makes sense to me.. But then again hehe.



Anyhow, those are my suggestions for mapping. Any thoughts, add-ons ect?
