Washko, it can be more clear than you have stated, unfortunately not in the manner you think.

The reason is that you are yourself imposing an ideology on the neutral community - that of arbitrator and mediator of the conflict between OTRK and the Clans.

From digging through an old text I found in my personal archives, I found this quote from a president of a 20th century Earth nation, the United States:

"I venture, therefore, my fellow countrymen, to speak a solemn word of warning to you against that deepest, most subtle, most essential breach of neutrality which may spring out of partisanship, out of passionately taking sides. The United States must be neutral in fact, as well as in name, during these days that are to try men's souls. We must be impartial in thought, as well as action, must put a curb upon our sentiments, as well as upon every transaction that might be construed as a preference of one party to the struggle before another." (Woodrow Wilson, Message to Congress, 63rd Cong., 2d Sess., Senate Doc. No. 566 (Washington, 1914), pp. 3-4)

I can think of no better summation of what true neutrality is. The definition of "neutrality" needs to be made with respect to the context of the current political situation. Neutrality can essentially be anything, so long as the neutral party takes no action which is biased towards one side over another. That is why neutrals are often branded as mediators, but they are not necessarily restricted to such role. They can also be smugglers and mercenaries, by virtue of their freedom of movement.

Note that neutrality does not preclude the neutrals of Rubi-Ka from taking their own side. So long as it is uninvolved with the current dispute between OTRK and the Clans, they are free to conduct themselves as they see fit. The fact that they are neutral does not deny them the right to posess interests of their own.
