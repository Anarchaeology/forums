Arena Rumble
G'day my fellow Omnis and Clanners.

6 weeks in a row, there's been held "all vs all" rumbles in arena
(you know... last man standing wins)
Most of the people participating are 150+, yet some brave souls
even down to 75 have tried their luck, some even succeeded (somewhat).

This event goes down every saturday evening at 00.00 GMT in Omni Ent arena. Just plain fun.

The rules are:

1: No teams
2: No exiting (also includes no Flee-too-grid)
3: No sneaking
4: No flying
5: When your'e dead, your'e dead. Wait till the round is over before you reenter.

Besides that.. just use common sense and have a good time

For you who haven't participated before, send a tell to me (Deng) and ask for enterence invitation to chat. (everything is primitively administrated from a plain invite to chat)

Have a good one peeps
