Sad Day on Rubi-Ka
I woke up this morning full of hope, another day of peace in the world with the amnesty still in effect. But by 2 o'clock in the afternoon while watching the Rubi-Ka channel news a program on leets, at my appartment in rome I was shakened by a large bang.

I hurried outside to see what was going on, i looked behind me to the west to see a large pall of smoke where omni ent was. Oh my what had happened !!. I ran inside to see a news flash a bomb had gone off in omni ent. Bening a doctor I grabed my stuff and rushed to the area. The building was completly gone only rubble remained of what was once a proud building. I cannot think of the lost of life that must of been suffered in that one blast, to say nothing of the cost in losing the building.

while many including myself jumped at the thoughts of clan outlawers being behind this. We must not jump to such lofty concusions, Omni HQ will do a full investigation into the event. But until then I urge both sides to remain calm until we find more information. Please remember the anmesty and truce are still in effect.

Kirrana
