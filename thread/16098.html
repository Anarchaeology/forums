Competition stadiums
What i hope to see, is competition stadiums, where persons, teams etc zone into.... ->

Capture the flag:
Stadiums where people can play CTF or alike, 0% zones, where person picks up flag, which is dropped if person dies. Team vs team, guild vs guild. Let it either be player controlled event, or "national events" where winners win large prizes (and cool amount of XP?)

Racing stadiums:
Complex racing tracks (tunnels), where competitors fly/run/drive from A to B. Especially cool where 2 persons wanna have some competative fun, or arrangements for guild events!

Chess:
2 players zone into a large field, which is a checkerboard, and gets it own set of MOB PETS which is the objects to move (target pet, then do a "/checker move [cooord]")


This is just 3 ideas.. I guess this should set all on a rush adding ideas for other competition stadiums etc.
