Although I am a total novice at RP, I have held many on-line gatherings.
Here are some suggestions as far as the meeting etiquette could go.

A. The moderator must be very strict in conduction the meeting.
B. Everyone before hand needs to know the rules of the meeting and needs to follow them.
C. During the meeting one person needs to have the floor and everyone needs to respect that.
D. The following are examples of ways for the attendees can let the moderator know they have a question and or comment.
a. Using the grammar symbols as indicators as weather a person has a question or comment.
i. ( ! ) using the exclamation mark tells the moderator the person has a comment
ii. ( ? ) using the question mark tells the moderator the person has a question.

Now, the moderator should have a list going at all times as to who is next in line to keep it fair and to keep it orderly. Now because this game as many avenues of chat It is easy for a person to be able to make comments to other people ie their friend via private tell and or have a private group chat going. So the vicinity chats should be kept clear of all comments or discussions except from the person who has the floor and the people who are letting the moderator know they have a comment or question using the above symbols.

Each person who is given the floor should end all their comments and or questions with the word done to let the moderator know it is ok to move on and or to answer a question asked.

Now I always recommend that the people requesting the floor should do so out loud so everyone can see. This helps to keep the moderator fair and honest. (not saying he/she wont be)

Now, I know that there are times especially in this type of environment that miss-tells and or out of line speak happens. But, If everyone understands the ground rules to start off with and everyone follows the basic guide lines most meetings usually go smoothly as well as all the information is able to be taken in.

These are just suggestions. I again have seen many game, private groups and corporate on-line discussions use this type of forum protocol and have seen it successful each time it has been used.

Good luck in your event.

There is one obvious point in such a meeting: you get to know the people and the faces.

I'm very interested in RPing here - some might know me from my CoA-related ramblings already (and I hope you can make the difference between me and Daimoness). As I'm coming from live-roleplaying and reenactment, I'm all about clear, simple rules.

Once these rules are set, everyone can enjoy more fun because he can play with others. You will need the occasional "referee", but this will hopefully be rare.

I have pointed to to Project Revenant on RK-2 on some other posting, but they do have some rules already. I would like to see this conference a success, but for that, we should try to solve as much as possible beforehand. I have taken my part in a long discussion on this topic on a 4 hour meeting on RK-2, and the outcome was disappointing.

But the most important thing is: You can't play together if you don't like each other. As long as you don't mix the game and the real world, it's okay (though this is much less of a problem here, in re-enactment you can really punch real people which makes it more disfficult).

Okay, after 16 months of playing I seem to have found the forums and hopefully some people to play with and against.

Basically, we should divide this into two sections:

1. Event RP

We need rules for who organizes, what must he provide, what priviledge does he have etc.

2. Battle Play

This means the Notum Wars PvP. I would really like to see some basic rules even in this, not only for diplomatic games but also for the battle as such. Project Revenant has the "no return" rule. Once you've been killed, you're out of the battle. I'm not sure if this is a good system, but in any case I don't like to have backstabbing battles.

Example: Org A and Org B are battling for an area. The majority of org A is on during Australian time. the majority of org B is European, which makes them around 12 hours apart. So when most of A is there, most of B is sleeping / working and vice versa. So whenever one is strong and the other one is weak, the strong one destroys the base and builds its own, then the other one returns favors.

Apart from the fact that it gets booooring, this is something I would like to avoid. I want to see nice PvP battles that actually serve to carry on the story somehow - or at least our part in it.

Now you all must get to think I'm the PvP fan who wants to see action. Well, not at all, but my thoughts race around Notum Wars and it's impact on us. I just want to have fun and enjoy the game. And after all OSTEC and CoA will be able to face of - and maybe we will get to RP something more together than just that.
