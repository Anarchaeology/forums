It is very possible to equip the QB on a doctor, good friend Cublaidoc on Rimor wears a QB. He is an Opifex Doctor lvl192 right now.

But Thyrra is correct, you will never properly use a QB on a doctor because of the Init factor. Enfs have a 5 per lvl melee init, we have either 3 or 4 per lvl, havnt checked in a while. They also have prodigous strength which adds a bit over 100 Melee Init I believe, we can get that from an Enf, but I am talking from a self sustaining point of view. Then they have their rage lines to add init and also can make use of a much higher Flurry of Blows then us.

That is just the Init, there is also a question of skill, we may be able to get enough to equip it, but we don't have the skill to do as strong hits with it, we can barely scratch the 2he to put it on, enfs with all their own buffs can put it on by themselves I believe. With a weapon that hits so slow it is important every hit is a good hit.

I can tell you this also, Cublaidoc at lvl192 wearing his QB and my doc was at lvl151 wearing a single Kick Pistol. I had AODD running during a few missions we did, I outdamaged him by a considerable amount even though he had 41 lvls on me.

Also remember that specials on weapons make a big difference in both PvM and PvP damage, the only real special the QB has (I am not counting dimach) is Brawl, which does not do damage based on the weapon but based solely off its own skill. So you would only have the regular hits of the QB, not any additional specials such as Fast Attack or Sneak Attack.

Yes the chemical damage is nice, yes the +320 HP is nice, yes the look is great (I wanted one of these on my opi doc the first patch the QB was out) but I do not see the QB as a functional weapon for a doctor, we just are not able to raise the skills enough to make use of its potential.

Yes, QB on a doctor is possible - I did it today on my level 161 solitus doctor (named Doctorkain). Melee init is an issue in a sense, but there are a few solutions - firstly, crank in melee init boosting items, raise fast attack to use a flurry of blows, and utilize your cheap first aid skill to use Health Stims (+150 melee init at ql200) - probably the biggest boost you could obtain by yourself would come from a Guardian Conductor of Speed, assuming you're in a guild. Note those towers take Psychology to build, which isn't very cheap for doctors.

If anyone's wondering, the abovementioned Doctorkain used RSleeve of Pest, ql200 implants, Micro-Kinetic Sleeves, Gentle Hands, Essence of Behemoth, Prodigous Strength, Gen Str/Stam, Dirty Fighter, General 2HE and Brawl buffs, and a 132 wrangle to equip his QB. No towers were involved. Can't wait to see what this will play like when he has ql220 implants, 2HE, Melee Init, and Attack Rating towers upstanding.
