Cz (oops),

Yeeess.... it's normal in any sort of Customer Feedback poll (and believe me I know - my working background is in Customer Support Organisations). It has to do with the propensity of people with grievances to voice those grievances much more strongly than people who are happy. They are more likely to go to the effort of voting than people who are happy and more likely to choose the worst rating. They are nevertheless people with grievances....

The other factor that affects polls like this is that some people who are engaged in a persistent relationship to an organisation and are in general unhappy with that relationship will tend to vote negatively on any element of the service as a sort of 'protest' vote. They may not actually be that unhappy with this patch... but they are still very unhappy with their overall relationship to AO and have used this as an opportunity to make that felt.

As a result, 11% Atrocious points to a large number of people with strong grievances with the state of play in AO and 25% in the less than good category indicates people in general unhappy with their relationship to FunCom. The patch itself may have been reasonably OK in terms of its effects.... but the state of the FunCom relationship with its customers must be looked at with some concern.
