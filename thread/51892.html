Clan Fashion show a hit! (FNN News Coverage)
I had the pleasure of attending the 2nd Rubi-Ka 2 Clan Fashion Show at Reet's Retreat earlier today. It was a wonderful event and though there were only a few announced winners, the real winners were everyone who particiapted and attended.

Organized and run by the MC for the event Roberto "Nasar" Houston, President of Vengeance Theory, the Fashion Show/Contest consisted of Six categories.

Male/Female Formal
Male/Female Casual
Male/Female Swimsuit
Overall

The Formal Competition went smoothly, as each competitor stepped forward and introduced themselves, and gave a reason for choosing the outfit they wore. Each contestant looked stunning, but ther could only be two winners, one male and one female:

Female: Kiyoko "Taoh" Matusz
Male: Mark "Maddmark" Andrew

Next came the Male and Female Casual Competitions. Here the contestants took time out of their busy work and fighting to take off the armor, and wear some relaxing clothing that doesn't protect well from Melee Attacks. But then again, they didn't have to worry about Melee attacks today, as everyone was attacked by sheer sexiness. Here are the winners:

Female: Lizbeth "Ernurse" Orender
Male: Casanova "Johndoo" Charm

Next came the moment that everyone had been waiting for...the Male/Female Swimsuit Competitions. Nothing like watching a group of scantily-clad Opifex women all vying for your affections. All the women looked extremely beautiful...well..except the Atrox who decided to compete with the Women..imagine an Atrox in a bikini...but I digress. Many of the Male spectators found themselves short of breath during this segment, or short of vision once Dino "Boomsticker" Fladger came up in its bikini and mooned the crowd . I cannot comment on the Men as I did not watch, but from some of the ladies I spoke with afterwards, the Men were outstanding as well. The winners for this category were:

Female: Roselee "Bayala" Depaolo
Male: Simon "Bubbleloo" Junior

Each winner from each category earned their prizes through tough competition. But at the end of it all, there can be only one Overall winner, and that was......

Kiyoko "Taoh" Matusz!

Congratulations on being the Overall winner of the 2nd RK2 Clan Fashion Show/Competition!

The entire event was a fun time for all, and a wonderful event in General. A big thanks goes out to Nasar, and his crew for organizing the event, and inviting us to relax for a few hours in the midst of all the political controversy.
