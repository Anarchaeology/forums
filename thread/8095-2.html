Asheron's Call fixed this problem pretty well.
Asheron's Call used to have this same problem. Then they took some steps to counter it:

1) Level restrictions on dungeons. I don't totally agree with this one, especially since AO's been out a while. It can screw over those who might want an item, but don't meet the dungeon's level restrictions. Optional in my book.

2) Create a quest timer. This is the most important change. The farming aspect of this problem goes right out the window if, when you try to grab the item a second time, the game tells you "You've completed this quest too recently," and you can't pick up the item. You can't farm an item if you can't pick it up.

3) Make quest item loot no-drop, no-trade. This would require those who want the item to go there themselves and (hopefully) brave the dangers of the place. You want the item? Then take a little risk.

Asheron's Call did all three of these things for many of their quests. It didn't work all the time, but it did work most of the time, and that's a lot better result than what's happening here right now.
