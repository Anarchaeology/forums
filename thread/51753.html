Make missions consistent - a few ideas
love em or hate em there is no getting away from missions. I have a couple of suggestions to make them a bit more realistic.

1. Make the mission design ie the type of mission (grey ccave, office etc etc) be consistent with the entrance. If i enter a crashed space ship for a mission then have the inside look like a crashed space ship and not an office block for example. This helps with suspension of disbelief, makes RP more fun and would geenrally be quite cool.

2. mission types, certain types of mission descs for the different missions types eg crashed space ship, get medical supplies there and save the crew. So the mission desc is tailored to what type of misison you are going into. Good for RP again. There can be a selection to choose from, eg find the pilot, save the crew, find the cargo. This is similar to what we have now but with a little more direction.

3. Mobs in missions, its kind of odd to have clan, OT and neut mobs all in the same mission. How about having a mission have just one faction type of mob in mission populated with humans. this really only affects us neuts and mixed side teams.

I dont know hwo easy to do or wanted these things are but i would like to see em.
