Top reasons you want to be an NT!
1. You get the ability to fumble your first attack and be aggroed by the mob you TRIED to attack who shouldn't even know you tried because YOU DIDN'T HIT HIM.

2. You can't take on more than one mob at a time and if it's yellow or higher then your chances of winning aren't so good.

3. You get to pay extremely high prices on the "black" market for armor that suits your class since it is not readily available in any store.

4. You get told by all the other classes, particularly soldiers that "you are so powerful and don't need to be given any fixes to make your class viable". Yeah, great. We're solid in groups (as long the mobs don't attack us) but our solo viability is nil.

5. You can break our own roots and calms by blinking at the mob.

6. You don't get critical hits.

7. You don't participate in PvP on your own level cause you get spanked. You have to sneak up on your enemy and be the first one to get a hit or you die. That's not an opinion.... that's a fact.

8. Your nanos WILL NOT run if your skill does not meet the requirements. You weapons users would be good to remember this.

9. Our range got capped along with everyone else's. A CAP means absolutely no further than set number.

10. And finally, you can't over-equip your weapons. The one thing that allowed you to slightly over-equip was taken away from you and called an exploit after it was introduced as legal.
