AO - the only game that gets me so mad I see red
I play MMORPGs cause I like the immersive environment, and I like the social aspect of the games. No other MMORPG has been able to infuriate me like AO can. I get soooo pissed off.

Tonight I logged on and I was in Rome green, and I go to zone out to take the whompa to Omni Ent, and I was disoriented so I went out the wrong zone, but the only thing is, I never zoned, I just passed right through the zone. Realizing that I didnt zone, I tried to get back on the other side of the zone but I couldnt get back through. There was another person there in the same predicament... and thats how my night started.

I ran into mobs shooting me through walls in missions (still not fixed), to growing more and more frustrated by the ability of the mobs to continually cast root or a dot right as they die, so I have to stand there doing nothing for several minutes, or I sit there bleeding for a few minutes. Its ridiculous and stupid AI. No PC is gonna waste his time throwing a dot on you, when they are almost dead and you are near full health, its pointless. Same thing with casting root with your last breath. Why? are you trying to keep them from running away from your corpse? Man I cant tell you how much that pisses me off. Not to mention that with maxed out nano resists, greens and greys can land every single nano on me with no problem. Do you even do a resistance check when npcs cast on pcs? I doubt it... I know npcs can resist nanos real well. My MP was getting resisted every other time he cast the other night....

I just continually run into bugs which make my gaming experience less fun, or I run into poorly designed aspects of the game and it hinders my fun. No other game has been able to piss me off like AO. And the sad thing is I am just 1 in thousands of angry customers, so my voice wont mean anything to funcom.


If I wasnt so sick of EQ and if DAoC wasnt so gawd awful boring, I wouldnt even be bothering with this game... plus I prefer sci fi over fantasy... long time to wait til SWG comes out
