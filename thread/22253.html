Armor and enforcer question
Dear Funcom.
1. I am playing a solitus Enforcer, and I am seeing that you have already chosen the Atrox as your favourite Enforcer breed. Almost any item is avalible for everyone in this game, but the atrox is way more fun to play when choosing an enforcer as they have more options. They can use both beam and da-taunter. Beam is one of the best melee weapons in game, the solitus enforcer, however, cant. The atrox can use the "Da-Taunter", the solitus cant. "I want the beam!". "Go dual riders or dual 1th Blunt " you answer. 1. they are butt ugly, 2. They require too much IP . "Go hammer or True katana" you say. 1. Hammer/katana is slow 2. Hammer/katana is bad damage ( if you miss ) etc in . Why cant you make the Solitus and Atrox enforcer equal when it comes to using items and weapons?? Its no fun having restrictions, and always beeing beaten up in PvP against Atrox-Beam users. Why not make it equal for both parts?? Oh, not realistic is it? Is this game realistic at all?

2. Why cant you bring back the Knights armor, Sentinel armor and whatever else armor you deleted? Yes, they were removed as they were way too good. Well, how many lines of coding would you have to change jsut to lower the AC on them just a little? I belive we are talking about a few minutes here. Looks matter in this game too...

Raise the fun FUNcom. There are many easy ways to do it. And make it fun for all. If you ever bring in a new weapon or armor in the game, dont put restrictions on it as some breeds suddenly are not allowed to use it, but insted put Side/Prof restrictions on them. Giving the atrox all these advantages, and not giving any other breeds any, but insted restrictions is just plain mongish. Get yourself together!
