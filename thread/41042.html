About Kraps&copy;:

Kraps&copy; is based on the ancient game of craps. With a few modifications and use of random number generator bots, accuracy and fairness of this game is guaranteed. This game was invented by Elisha Arnall, AKA Rondy and his company Kazino&trade;.

1. The dealer opens trade window with each player. players put in the ammount they want to bet in the window and in vicinty they confirm the ammount that they are betting and where they want to bet. (Examples: 10k on Kraps, 10k on high)

2. After all the bets are received the dealer makes 2 rolls of dice (with /tell <their private helpbot> roll 1 6) the sum of these numbers is the Kraps number

3. Players can make 5 types of bet:
&nbsp; &nbsp; 1: High, Kraps bigger then 7, Pays 1-1.
&nbsp; &nbsp; 2: Low, Kraps smaller the 7, pays 1-1.
&nbsp; &nbsp; 3: Kraps, Kraps equals 7, pays 3-1.
&nbsp; &nbsp; 4: 12, Kraps equals 12, pays 10-1.
&nbsp; &nbsp; 5: 2, Kraps equals 2, pays 10-1.

4. After each roll dealer will post the verification number. players can verify by using /tell <dealers private helpbot> verify <number>

5. After both rolls dealer pay off all players based on their postings in the log.

A small piece of OOC advice
This is an incredibly cool idea.

However, there is one teensy tiny point that I'm going to bring up.

Can I please recommend your 'bots provide a 'Terms&Conditions' message to users informing them that the onus is on them to police themselves regarding real-world gambling laws. Some states and countries outlaw *all* forms of gambling (for virtual as well as real money) and can get very uppity about this kind of thing. All you probably need is:

If you come from a state or country in which gambling in any form is illegal, then you are not allowed to play.

While this is going to stop absolutely no-one, it does cover your a$s legally. Just make sure people can see this message BEFORE they provide any money, or they can reclaim their money safely before the die is cast.

Personally I've got nothing against it and honestly believe these kinds of money sinks are vital for an online economy. But I don't make the law in countries I don't even live in.
