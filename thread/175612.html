Appearance...
~As you walk down an alley, you feel a cool breeze pass you, a shiver running down your spine~

"He shall bring you to enlightenment..."

~The voice fades away as you turn around seeking it's owner...~

"His Darkness is the only path to power..."

~Sensing danger, you run towards the open street at the end of the alley, only to find as you reach the end, you are staring into a void surrounded by what seems to be the universe itself~

"Join us in eternal servitude to Him and find your true being"

~Just then, a group of robed figures appear around you, each one with eyes cold and soulless~

"As the portal opens our journey begins, the journey to completion..." speaks one of the dark beings...

"He shall guide you as well"

"Come the day of September the 13th, at the 14th hour, he shall appear to the world..." ((EST))

"It is at this pillar you shall find him.."

~A vision of a glowing blue pillar that you recognize as the one in front of the Omni-Administration building in Rome Blue flashes through your mind~

"Let your journey to Darkness begin..."

~As the world around you begins to form itself back to the street, you try to gather your thoughts. Confused, you walk into the daylight once again~

((A speech will be given on Saturday, September 13th, at 2pm EST at the blue pillar in front of the Omni-Admin building in Rome Blue. It will be more or less announcing the purpose and goals of a mysterious new "department", with a few little secrets revealed and a plot line left keeping you on edge. Until then...))
