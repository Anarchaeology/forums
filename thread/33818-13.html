Easy way to fix nanos, instead of level req's:
In general, I submit that it is a trully bad idea to put level requirements on anything but the most unusual and rare of items, such as items found in quests. There has to be a better mecahnism to prevent a level 1 character from casting a ql 195 nano with a 3000 point mocham than putting a level 195 requirement on his nano. If a given item is so powerful that it should not be used before a given level, raise it's requirements to ensure that it is not used before. Perhaps just make sure that no level 1 character is ever able to receive a 3000 point mocham (say, if a 3000 point mocham takes 200 ncu's ... And the fixer 210 ncu buff can only be used on targets of title level 5+...) If a given item is to be a reward for reaching a given level (such as the leet charming nanos seem to be for high level adventurers), then make the item no drop, and insert it into high level mission reward tables only. Or state the level requirement along with it's description as a reward. There has to be a way to do this without level limitations.

Perhaps a milder form of level requirement would work best. Say, instead of limiting nanos by level, limit them by _TITLE LEVEL_. On certain nanos, you could even limit the title level of the target. This falls in line better with the stores, too. There'd be no point for a level 10 character to even look at an advanced store - none of the items in there would be wearable for another title level.

A note on PvP: if at level 75 you can be attacked in PvP by a level 200 character, there should be no easilly _known_ limitations on what you can retaliate with. You should be, theoretically, able to retaliate with a ql 240 nuke, and surprise the heck out of the level 200 character.

Looking around you, at other characters, and reading their levels, should probably not be an indication of how powerful they are. At most, it should only be a mild indication of such. Look at it as a way of rewarding your older, and more reliable customers. Those that have been playing for a year, should, if they start a level 1 character, be able to make him stronger than other that also start at level 1. A player with two characters at level 100 should have both more powerful than a player with a single character at that level.

Nevermind the player at level 50 with 5 level 200 friends...

I have a little story to illustrate why level based games are inferior to a true skill based game, or atleast as close as AO was before level req nanos.

I had a low level opi advent, had a couple sol fire execs, two type Fs and a T. Was able to equip all of them in my main hand, but none of them had a low enough multi-melee req so none in my off hand. I had some implants in to increase multi-melee, and had the augment agility, strength, and stamina, but was 1pt away from equipping the second sword. After doing a high cr reward mission, I was able to afford some more general nanos. Of course, there was no general multi-melee buff, for some assinine reason. However, after casting augment intilligence, I was able to cast MatM proficiency, which inturn allowed me to cast agility boost, and then with augment strength I was finally able to equip that second weapon. And it felt great to be able to advance in my combat effectiveness without waiting for that silly exp line to creep across and magically and artificially level up my character.

Now if level requirements, especially as inflated as they are with the 14.4 nanos, were in place, I wouldn't been able to have that small little victory that I had. I would have had to wait until x level before equipping them, and that bit of joy would have been lost.

Thus concludes my little story.

So just remove all level requirements from all items, be they weapons, armor, utilities, or nanos. There is no justification for them. If you are making a seperate product, ie expansion, that requires them, then I submit it is the expansion that needs changing not the present game. Decides, if you take whats fun out of the game, who will be around to buy your expansion?
