Get rid of 5% gas zone period !!!
Get rid of 5% gas zone period please.

A Guild allied to ours was attacked by omni during the %5 zone gas period. We are clan, but it's annoying whatever side you happen to be on.

Many clan guilds responded and we successfully defended the zone and defeated the invaders, but then most of us not in teams were autotargetted by their turrets.

It's very inequitous to get blown to bits owned by the Massive Cannon Turrets by the organization you are trying to help.

In the chaos of the battle there was no way to spot members of the guild and get teamed up in order to avoid this.

In fact, most of the members from the guild that owned the controller were already dead, being first on the scene, so there was no one to team up with.

Also the battle was very laggy!!

There has to be a better way for people on the same side to assist their brethern. The current state of affairs just does not work!

Rival sides are using the 5% period to stage their attacks since that is the time that there is greatest confusion. It is very difficult for the defending side to organize when they can be auto-targeted by their own sides towers.

... Are they exploiting ? During so called 5% members of the opposing sides should not be allowed to attack, if that was the case.

My opinion ... the rule is stupid. There needs to be another way for members of the same side to attack their own without the 5% gas rule the way it is implemented now.

Easiest Fix
Here's the easiest way to fix this problem:

Keep it as 5% zone so that there can be attacks made on same side bases if you want to vie for a spot that another same side guild has taken. Instead of the towers being like a monster mob where it is agro to everyone in attack range, make it like the humanoid side based mobs. To clarify: an OT Sharpshooter will not attack an Omni player because it is Omni associated, but it will auto attack a Clan player, it will however attack an Omni player though if it is first attacked (and call its friends in range over to help out). This way if a tower is laid in a Clan base then it will attack Omni players automatically and Clan players ONLY if it has already been attacked by said Clan player. This way those helping you defend your base during this time of confusion don't get pecked at by your towers.
