I just started to attend at camelot raids a week ago, and to be honest, had a blast so far. Acambot is a great tool, and huge cudos to the makers and admins who run it for providing such a tool.

About the yelling and loot problem, well there are 2 sides of each story.

1) The yelling and orders:

If someone says HOLD THE BOXROOM, KEEP THE DOOR CLOSED, I usually do it. Problem is sometimes its just the wrong tactic. Clanners form up at stairs, get mass people, and at one point everyone lags like **** and we get rushed, losing the box room. If we would have rushed them in small waves while they were forming up, we would have held the box room (happened yesterday for example).

So while generally I agree with the admins, I sometimes seriously disagree with their tactics on how to defend. if there are 5 clanners on the stairs, with 5-10 more in the hallway, why not get the NTs together and use waves of Kels (area nuke) to either remove them or at least drive them back? Not like a rush of 5 clanners could take the box room alone.

But its true, to get the orders through on the bot you have to scream and use caps, or nobody listens.

2) Loot

This is a tricky issue and I usually get fed up really fast. To all those peeps sitting in the grid, in 100%, outside camelot or wherever, go "youknowwhat" yourself. Its those little morons that ruin everyone else's, who fights honestly, chance at loot.

I attended every camelot raid for the last 10 days or so, every day, every GS. I went from untitled to Apprentice in 3 days, and I sure as hell didnt do it by sitting afk in the box or anywhere outside camelot. It sickens me to death to see people work and fight their ass off day for day just to have some lvl 130 moron sitting in the grid winning the GPH or HPO. Call me jealous, I sure am, but thats honestly BS.

People like Hirum, Spyderv, Stopmer2002 and all the other peeps who work and fight their ass off deserve the loot, not your sorry afking in the grid ass.

I know everyone kinda does their part, be it buffing or just standing in the box room in case a wave of clanners comes in. I know the loot and raffle system will never be perfect, heck it'll never be fair. The game just doesnt offer a system to control and administrate such things, and thats why it will stay like it is now for a long time.

For a perfect system the admins would have to know where every bot user is, which GS etc. And since that is not possible, at least atm, there will always be people in the grid or afking wherever, winning some loot.

I personally go to camelot for the PvP fun AND a chance to get some ncie loot. While loot is nice and I would give a lot to get a GPH or HPO (the only things i actually want out of camelot lootwise), its still the PvP what drives me, and what keeps me coming back for more. All that BS with people outside camelot being eligible for raffles, even bringing alts to increase their chance, is so low its not even funny. But until someone thinks of a better system, I guess we have to live with that kind of crap, even if it sickens some of us to death.
