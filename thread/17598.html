How to keep The NEWBIES (A GOOD Customer is A HAPPY Customer)
Please be advised that I have very little experience with this game. I do, however, have a suggestion to help new comers to the game. On my first trip into AO I chose Omni and after reaching second level wandered out into the city. As you may or may not know, the city portion that you enter is the sewers. Now being a newb and having absolutly no real clue, I wondered aimlessly in the ABSOLUTE pitch black night. Needless to say in about 5 seconds I fell into the sewers where I was eaten alive by the radiation. So I died, good ol AO, I didnt have to worry about wasting 50 hours on a corpse run. I collected my monster parts from the reclaim terminal and then forged back out, determined to find the inner city. What did I do? I fell in the sewer again. Third try is the charm? Not for me, fell in the sewer but made it out to the ocean some how (no clue) and then found the banks before I died drowning. Ok now IM on shore and no idea how to get into the city so I roam for 50 years and still cant find it. I type help see the terminate or something command and kill myself. I was so upset I canceled my account. I guess IM fickle because later on I decided to give it one more go after reading about night vision goggles. I log back in, I level to 4 (in the day time) and then get my pair of goggles. All is well with the world. Now whats my suggestion? Thanks for asking, here it is:



Newbie Vision: Give all newbies something that allows them to see at night. Maybe something with charges so it can be eventually expended.


Ok next long story. So there I was in this world, and I didnt want to use a pistol like the one I started with. So I train in something else, but for the first 7 levels I cant afford anything but the newbie weapon. So I am using a pistol which I am not any good at, and I am getting my butt kicked inside out. Literally, I could see my guts and everything. So your asking again, whats this all mean? Well here is my suggestion:



Newbie trade ins. I give you a shotgun you give me a newbie machiune gun. I give you a club you give me a newbie pistol, etc etc. I think this would really help the true newbies in the game. I mean the other players are AWESOME and will give you stuff, but I so hated that beggar mentality back in EQ that I just cant manage the words to ask for help. So please, put in some newbie functions that would really help them.



One last thing, I swear OK so I am an adventurer and I have bow skill that I can now use thanks to the awesome weapons exchange person thingy. Oops looks like adventurers really suck at BOW, no clue why they just do! Ok heres my suggestion:


Give all classes more options in regards to weapon skills. Allow them to move the green skill around to one that they want to specialize in. Do you really want every singly Engineer sporting a pistol? Or every trader sporting a shotgun? Why??? Why not give the players more choices, more FREEDOM!

Thats all I had to say, thanks for reading
