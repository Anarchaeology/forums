I agree there should be a buff like riot control for equiping purposes. I have ql111 ares arms that needs 450 full auto. That was tough to get.

I don't agree that it should hit with the remaining bullets, because that would be TOO strong. My gun, for instance, has a 55 bullet clip. If it hit all 55 of them it would do more than 10,000 damage, assuming no crits. I'd like to see maybe 30%-50% of the shots hit if you have the actual skill the gun requires. That way you can't just implant/buff into a FA gun and do massive damage, you need the constant skill to back it up...but even then, there would have to be a PvP damage cap (if there isn't already). I don't want to see the soldier overpowered in PvP, but in PvM the ability to do 3,000-4,000dmg in one shot, with a long recharge and forcing a reload, isn't as bad. especially since you usually need to keep 3-6 skills up to use good FA guns. Even make it so range effects how many hits you get if you want.
Right now burst is a much better skill. Used by more guns, does reliable damage, don't have to reload, short recharge, doesn't require a very high skill and you can use riot control.
