Social Update Pack
Here's how I see it...

Notum Wars gave some meaning to PvP combat

The latest patch (14.8) made some nice changes to the missions to make them at least marginally less boring.

But, it has been quite a while since the roleplayer or social players had much to crow about.

So, let's start a thread of the sorts of things we'd like to see added to the game to improve the opportunities for roleplaying...

I'll start with some of my ideas....

1) More options for male clothing (shorts, jeans, t-shirts, etc.)
2) Skirts for females other than minis (not that I have anything against mini-skirts, just more options would be nice)
3) Furniture for your apartment that you can actually sit in
4) Some emote improvements (why do I "pop-the-top" on my coffee cup?)
5) Additional non weapon items you can hold and carry. (clipboard, camera, umbrella?)
6) Some additional musical instruments (non stringed... trumpets, drums, saxaphone, etc.)
7) Remove some of the silly "social armor" restructions. Why should a woman (or atrox) have to remove rings and bracers to put on a dress?

Please add your own ideas and feel free to elabotarate on mine. I 'm sure I'm not the only one frustrated with some of these issues.
