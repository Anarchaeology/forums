This is from Megabio and NOT from me.

Okay. I dislike runners greatly. You all can use the "But I don't want to die!" but that is total crap. This is a game. I am sorry, but when you keep putting context into real life - then yes, you are going to have a problem. If you run in MMD or 2HO, that is fine - those are warzones. You probably didn't accept the duel anyway - much like if you were in camelot (assuming you wern't already dead but you are waiting for the servers to "catch up" ).

However, if you are in a duel at the Arena (or agreed to duel, wherever the place) there is a different set of moral standards. If you die, it is because you agreed to a duel, and you knew what could happen. I'm not trying to be ub4r 1337, but I never decline a duel nor do I ever run from a duel. However, when I accept a duel I realize that I have the chance to die and if you don't want to take that chance than keep titlehugging in MMD or 2HO.

Anyway that's my two cents..

Some good ol' rhetoric
quote:
It's not a l33t thing. If you were playing poker with a guy who pulled his chips when he had a bad hand, how fun would that be?
So I enter into every hand of poker expecting to win. We also agreed to bet money on the issue. Now if I get a pair, and my opponent keeps on raising the pot, am I cowardly if I decide to fold early on?

No. Obviously not. What's happening here is that at some point during the hand, I realize that I am not likely to win. Thus, I fold, saving my money so that I can continue playing longer. How is this any different in AO? I go into 2ho expecting PvP. I want to kill people. Someone attacks me. I engage because I think I can win. However, at some point during the fight, I decide that I will probably not win, and thus I run. If I die, I would have to wait around to reclaim, heal, rebuff, and rezone. A waste of time and not exactly fun. If I run, I may get away and be back to PvPing in a minute or two.

Duels are an exception because there is an implied "to the death" in the offer.
