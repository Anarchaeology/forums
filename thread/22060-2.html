UO did this same thing when EverQuest was starting to be just around the corner. They promised a ton of new stuff and halfway delivered on half of it. This was the point where UO stopped being fun and started over-catering to the casual gamer.

Same thing here. However, the announced ideas are, in typical FunCom fashion, actually intriguing. We all know that Star Wars: Galaxies is scheduled for a November/December release. That game could somewhat be construed as SciFi and a direct competitor to AO. In any event, the developers for that game seem to 'get it' to a certain degree and are generating a lot of excitement.

Time will tell if it's gonna be just a repeat of the letdown we got from Anarchy Online. Not that AO is a bad game. I like it a lot. It's just the broken promises, failure to deliver, and misguided direction changes during the paid beta period that sometimes make it more of a heartache than it's worth.

I seriously hope the developers realize that the game needs to be fun first and working and balanced second. If a game works and is balanced, but is boring and/or annoying as hell, you only keep the most devoted of fans. If your game is fun (as AO was from May to August last year and December to February this year), people will fervently work through the flaws and forgive the 'groan factor' in order to play.

Here's hoping the expansion shows that FunCom can put the Fun back in AO.
