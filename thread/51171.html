Thought for balance
Perhaps rather than keeping the trend of new self-only nanos in the hope of fixing a class, FC needs to look at a way to keep classes meant to be strong in certain areas while still allowing other classes to benefit from the new buffs.

What I would propose is to look at the advent wolf nano buff lines (howl of the wolf, etc.). To sum up, these add bonuses to the recipient, but add a better bonus to someone in wolf form. So what I propose is that skill buffs have one base value of buff and a higher value should target profession = x.

Example: Let's create a buff for example's sake, Blade of Valor. It buffs 2 handed edge +80. However, if used on an enforcer, then it buffs 2 handed edge +112. Enforcers maintain an edge with 2 handed edge weapons this way from skill and a bit more in buffs, but an Nanotechnician could still benefit from the buff to swing her True Katana a little bit better (or more likely buff into a higher QL katana sooner).

Also, these nanos will have level restrictions (no way to avoid that at this point in time), but FC should aim the level req as something a nanomage caster could use with equal QL implants and the aid of a similar level/equipped meta-physicist.
