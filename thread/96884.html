TNT goes out on top =(
Hello all.

This is so hard for me to say..

Unfortunatly the day I never wanted to come is here. The last TNT show has been heard and we do apologize for the lack of warning on this. On the plus side, the last TNT show did break GSP's listener record, so at least we can all be proud of that!

GSP shall continue as well as my solo shows on Wednesday night. Threx may be getting his own slot as well so stay tuned for more on that.

I had an absolute blast with all of the listeners, and will miss the show very much. I hold no regrets. It was an amazing experience for me and if I had to do it over again, knowing how it'd end, I would.

Thank you again to all of you for your support, and again, we apologize.

See you on Wednesdays.

/me hangs her head and wipes away her tears as she closes the door to one of her fondest memories.
