I can walk on water!
Not sure if this is the right forum, but thought I'd post this. I tried serching and found no reference to this, but I discovered last night I could walk on water! Well, more accurately I glided over the top while standing. Anyway, I was swimming toward Pleasant Meadows from Harry's and decided to use my first aid kit. My adventurer went throught the normal motions, but after the flash he remained standing instead of swimming! He just kept gliding across the surface of the water standing upright. Didn't speed up unfortunatly, but still it looked pretty cool! I tried this twice and both times it produced the same effect. Try it and see!
