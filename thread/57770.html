PVP Mission System
While hunting in 2h0 last night an idea came to mind, PVP missions

Example of how it works:

One clan player level 58 goes and rolls a pvp mission. If no else his level has a mission then his/her task is to defend the item (Like the boss in a team mission).
A few minutes later another player (omni) around the same level rolls a pvp mission his task is to get the item player 1 is defending.
Now, this mission should be on a timer and the key should not be able to be copied. Also instead of having to goto the mission you should beable to right click the key . Hq will provide trasportation to the mission site.

I think the reward should be cash or experance.
