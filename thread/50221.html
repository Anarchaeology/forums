What you experienced are not just lag, its two common bugs introduced by 14.4 (or some patch later).

1) "nanobots are busy". Seen this often, happens mostly on the first execution for some reason, but I've managed to heal after getting this message. Try to move left/right a few times as this seems to break the deadlock.

2) Double hit by mob after nano. Happens after every nanoexecution from a mob (heal,debuff,nuke whatever). Asked for a confirmation on this bug a while ago from Cz, but have not received an answer yet. One of the hits does not trigger response from reflect/damage shields. Have several screenshots of this.

My best advice would be to do a test heal and keep first aid kits ready at all times.

I'm SO glad enforcer mobs dont cast nanos while fighting...

Edit) Darkbane: Your 2) is a bug. Its even consistent and not situation dependent. I think it was introduced somethimes after 14.4
