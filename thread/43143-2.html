If you happend to close the overflow-window by accident, with items still there they aren't lost. The trick to get them back (ie. open the overflowe-window again) is to dupe a mission-key. If you're inventory is full and you dupe a key, then the overflow-window will reappear, with the "missing" items still there. Then you can just delete the duped key(s), clear inventory to a backpack, and transfer items from overflow-window to your inventory.
