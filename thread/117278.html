Circle G publicly releases Faction Inteligence Reports
I'm proud to announce that Circle G has declassified two very important inteligence reports, that we think are of interest to all of Rubi-Ka. Based on our observations, we have matched many of the numerous Clans and Omni-Tek departments with major Clan structures and OT Departments.

We at Circle G feel that even though our reports are out of date, and based 100% on Circle G's observations and inteligence gathering, they are still useful to Rubi-Ka's population.

And so, for free of charge, we offer Major Clans and Affiliates, and OT departments and Affiliates for public viewing.

We hope that making these reports available to the public will in turn, aid us in getting them up to date. Perhaps those orginizations that were missed will want to proclaim their affilication, or perhaps we guessed wrong on those we have listed.

Either way, Circle G can be contacted to make the changes. In the following weeks we hope to declassify another report listing orginizations who make claims to be Neutral Friendly. If organizations are interested in being included on this report, or in updating the current reports, they shoud do their best to contact Glarawyn.
