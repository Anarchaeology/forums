Uncle Fatty's in trouble...he needs your help!
Ok, now here's the deal.....
There I was, hunger freshly sated thanks to good ole Bronto Burger, lying back in the sun in good ole downtown Newland City. My pockets were full of creds from a mission I had just completed and I had no worries in the world. Erm, well, maybe just one worry. See, one of the stipulations of aforementioned mission was that I find and return a weird looking bomb. And I figured, that stupid ole mission terminal could wait a few hours, what difference will it make if I take a quick nap down here by the Bronto Burger? So, I laid down, with that comfy bomb under my head, and passed out for a bit.
Yeah, doesn't take a genius to figure out the rest. Five hours later, I wake up to find that someone has taken off with my bomb! I have a few suspects, what I need you to do is track down these leads for me and GET MY BOMB BACK!! I have screwed up way too many missions, and if this one goes badly I'll be one very unemployed atrox! I am willing to pay up to 50,000 credits per person for the safe return of my bomb! Uncle Fatty needs you, give me a call on the grid and I will fill you in!

/ooc I am looking for a group of 3-6 Clan Members on RK1, levels 25-30ish. If you are interested send an e-mail to Brontobill@hotmail.com. The bomb search is to go down on Monday, March 11. Include times you are available on that day as well as any other questions you might have in the e-mail. In game, send Fattylumpkin a /tell. Make sure to state you are interested in the bomb recovery.
