An Evening with The Writers of Rubi-ka
Something a Little Different is happening in the Woods!
RELAX! Inc. is proud to present:

A NIGHT IN THE WOODS

Featuring Creative writers of Rubi-ka.
Sunday August 25th at 5pm EDT (gmt-4)

Location Borealis -at whompa head south out east gate and follow dirt path to small lake. Ember Woods 790,560. (Where Dream date auction was held)

We invite all writers young and old to come out for a evening of Def Poet.

We would like to start sponsoring some of Rubi-kas finest writers to share with us their hearts, their visions and their intimate worlds of the spoken word.

Whether you write poems, sonnets or versewe welcome all the creativity to be opened and shared by those who have a love of writing.

The rules are:

Please try to keep each piece no more than 5 minutes long.

It must be your own creation.

Please send a title of piece, author's name, an email where to reach you and approximate time to recite aloud and send to martygarasha2@hotmail.com

Please let us know if you are dedicating it to anyone at all in
Rubi-ka.

Please make sure you sign your name to it. (In character or any particular special acknowledgment)

Entries no later than Friday, August 9th

RELAX! Inc. shall take free liberty to disallow any entries which they deem to be offensive or discrimatory in nature.

We feel there is a lot of talent in Rubi-ka that is not known and this is our way of giving a little limelight to those who bring forth some wonderful thoughts and ideas.
