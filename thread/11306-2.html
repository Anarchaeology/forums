I think pvp is pretty good as it is. I know of a friend, an enforcer, that pvps with no outside buffs, and only equipment that he can reasonably wear. He might have a brutal thug cast on him to help him into his beam (he could cast headcracker himself) but no wrangle. And amazingly, with only ql 50 implants at level 50, he was still able to get his freshman title. just because he used strategy, maxed important skills that people take for granted (nano resist and evades) and picked his fights.

Yes, a level 80 MA will always be owned by a 158 MA, and will have no chance to escape. But you can be careful, you can use cool nanos like grid teleport, you canb get friends to help guard you...

the whole point is that you can adapt and have fun and make the best of it, instead of getting that mission in 2ho, going there, getting ganked when you grid in, and *****ing that pvp sucks.


and lastly, pvp is part of the story line... in AC it was just a thing you can do... in uo (the most popular game, ever) it was everywhere.... ao does a great job of balancing it.
