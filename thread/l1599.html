I'm not sure whether the 20% level restriction is based on the higher or lower character's level, and at upper levels it could make a significant difference: 20% of 200 is 160 and up, but 20% of 160 is 128-192. That's 8 lvls difference at the high end, but that is still a range of up to 20 levels at the high end.

Frankly, I'm not sure whether that's too *much* of a range, and here's why. The reason going into mayhem zones is supposed to be dangerous is because some of the higher level creatures will also be spawning in those areas, putting any 'duelists' at risk of death from monster attacks. People will want to be able to go out to explore when they get to higher levels w/o turning every corner and finding themselves in a life and death struggle with 6 monsters and a half dozen players. Sure, let it be more dangerous, but I support the idea of putting enough level restrictions on pvp in those areas so if I don't want to fight, I at least have a fighting chance of getting away alive, same as when fighting monsters, no more, no less, and since real players will always be more dangerous than monsters, it's nice to have some limits.
I really like the idea of formal 'duels' where persons or groups can have a fight to the death, in an area where there is no escape until death or some formal 'saying uncle.' That lets anyone who really wants to fight other players fight other players who want to fight them, and not have a system like the early days of UO where most players were somewhere near the bottom of the food chain, with hardcore red pkers at the top.
People *pay* to play this game, you don't want it to feel like you're being shaken down, having to pay extortion fees by getting pked everytime you leave the safety of the town walls. You wanna make a mafia online game where that's the rule, go ahead, and we'll see how many people sign up to be at the bottom of that scheme

Well, I'm not a PvPer, never done it. I've played EQ since August '99, and I've been in ONE duel (I won, but it was just against a paldin, so does that really count? lol). However, I have to agree to some extent with Mor and his PvP posse. There are purportedly going to be 50 game zones available at launch ( I *think* bcb said this in his thread, though I could be wrong). Since the zones are so big, I think making *one* truly "mayhem" zone where anyone can attack anyone, regardless of level, faction, etc wouldn't be such a bad idea. One zone is only about 2% of the available game world, and I'm failry certain that at least 2% of the customers would be for the idea.

On the other hand, if they don't do it, I'm certainly not going to be upset. As many others have said, this game is not, and never has been, touted as a PvP game. They have made it an option, in some areas, because it both fits the story, and because there is a sizeable portion of the customer base who wants it. As others have said, if you want all-out PvP action, there are many good games out there for you, and even more on the horizon (Shadowbane, Neocron, Planetside, etc.).
