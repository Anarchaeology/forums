Phear Leet
Well the skill based system they designed basically doesn't work. That's the reason for the level requirements.

The reason their skill based system doesn't work is because skill increases at high levels are small due to having so many levels where they fall under title caps. The buffs available are huge compared to the amount of skill you gain in the last 70 levels or so of this game. The result is that if they ever want a class to be able to self-cast their powerful high level spells, they have to be castable at level 120 by a guy with buffs. Basically that means there's no cookies between level 120 and 200, the longest and most boring portion of the game.

They also really messed it up by not putting skill requirements on buffs. In a skill-based system where damage and capability depend so heavily upon skill, the buffs have to have a skill-based requirement too. They tried to do it with ncus but comp lit wasn't restricted enough due to treatment and stat buffs allowing overboosting of comp lit through implants, which in turn allows overboosted ncu belts and memories plus the ncu belts themselves have a low comp lit requirement for their level. In addition for NCU liimits to work it means that every higher level spell has to have high ncu requirements, which means high level players cant use all the buffs they have available to them. It ends up rendering half the buffs useless because no group members ever have enough ncus for them.

The current system has many flaws. Most of them are solved by instituting level requirements. That's the easy way so the way they are taking. I'll agree its not as interesting to the player. I like trying to maximize myself in the skill based system and level requirements remove that aspect in some cases. In the ideal world Funcom would come up with a skill based system that produces the same results as a level based system, but that's a whole lot of changing they'd have to do at this point.
