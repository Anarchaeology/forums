RK2 Guild Fair!
Hello all RK2 guild members and leaders,

I would like to ask you all to attend and participate in a world wide Guild Fair. The Guild Fair is an opportunity for all people who would like to join a guild to meet and talk with representatives from Clan, Neutral, and Omni guilds to determine what one could be best for them.

For guilds who are interested, please send a tell to my character (Ikia) so we can try to work out a Saturday that works for everyone who is interested.

This is an attempt to get more events going on RK2 for ALL residents, so please show your support!
