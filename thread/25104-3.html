How is a Trader uber?
Ok, I see this whole argument broken into 3 parts:

1. People in healing specialist professions (Docs) don't like being made redundant in teams by a non-specialist healers (Traders).

2. People have been ripped apart in PvP by some low level Trader twinkie who used all the game mechanics prior to 14.0 for uber implants and ncu.

3. People are envious of how 'extremely easy' Traders can make money.

Pretty close so far? Ok, here is my opinion of this:

1. The first statement is only true, if the trader has buffed up his own nanoskills substantially (maybe even using a high lvl MP nano buff) in order to use far higher ql healing nanos than the Doc is using.

Example:
Doc nano ql 103 'Experimental Pancea' heals 446-794
Trader nano ql 99 'Expert Health Haggler' heals 479 with 225 dmg to Trader. So which is the better heal nano? By all means, a fully buffed Trader probably can heal better than a non-buffed (wragled) Doc, but this is hardly a fair comparison.

2. There is no denying that Traders have an edge in twinking chars for PvP, due to the fact that their (nerfed) self-only complit-buffs enable ample ncu for buffs. At low lvl this is crucial for twinking. This imbalance is caused by Funcom making complit-buffs self-only, and is indeed unfair. My advice for this, don't PvP a trader lvl 10-20 unless you are truly twinked yourself, or have good enough tactics. Likewise, I wouldn't recommend PvP'ing a twinked low lvl Agent for that matter, they are also easy to overequip. This edge in PvP is imho NOT a reason to call for Trader or Agent nerfs, most professions have a lvl range in which they can do very well in PvP.

3. If you believe traders make money easily, you should be aware of some facts: Traders get the same amount of cash for selling items to other players, maybe more if they are uber at holding auctions . Providing wrangles to complete strangers while looking for missions or socializing is extremely tedious and time-consuming (I am talking about the high wrangles that need drains in a backyard first - I never ask anything for my lower wrangles that can be given on the fly) so when people ask for a high wrangle and I say 'sorry, I'm too busy' it usually ends up with the person offering insane amounts (maybe 100-300k) for me to stop whatever I'm doing and help him out. This can easily take 5-10 minutes of my already scarce playing time, so I am damn right to demand fair compensation for this. Besides, at lvl 74 my nanos cost me 1 - 2,5mill each, so who is gonna pay for those?

If you're still not convinced, try starting a brand new Trader, forget about friends, begging or gifts, and try equipping your newbie with all the nanos, equipment and ammo (Energy and Shotgun ammo is WAY expensive for a lvl 5-10). Think anybody's gonna tip you for that uber +13 wrangle? You'd be lucky. Up to lvl 50-60 nobody needs your services, even the 85-99 wrangles are rarely asked for, in my experience (hanging out near Tir grid).

-----------------

Chew on this and do me the favour of not whining about other professions until you have played that profession beyond lvl 50-ish - we all know from experience that Funcom would rather swing the nerf-bat on one profession, than adjust the other ones. The coming team-heal nerf is going to seriously hurt our solo play, just because some whiners didnt like being out-healed.
