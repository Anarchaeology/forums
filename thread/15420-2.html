OH DEAR ARE WE ALL THIS MIS GUIDED?
Do people really think that funcoms attempts at over equipping will stop exploiting of the game? HA well you`ll learn, as usual the 14.2 patch is just a knee jerk reaction from funcom to sate the annoyances of whiney noobs who can get on in this game. I started way back when this went live, and was blown away with the freshness of the concept and the freedom of the game. When october came and we had the fist static dungeon this game rocked.. it reached the pinacle of its development. The animated series started, people were mass teaming, pvp was chaos and all was very well in the world of ruby ka.

Then as over powerfull professions were seen to be leaping ahead, instead of adressing these problems properly, Funcom nerfed them, and nerfed them hard. Again another knee jerk reaction, very unproffessional and totally unfair and almost spitefull to those dedicated players who had spent many many hours playing and devloping their characters. With Funcom almost killing off the nano tech, adventurers, agents, fixers and ma`s we though things might just get better after this OVER ZEALOUS stamping down but how wrong we were... The ma had to wait an age to be addressed, although they almost got it right it still neded a fine tune. To be fair though the other proffessions i mentioned above need to be sorted VERY VERY quickly.

This now brings me to the 14.2 patch. If all these funcom lovers are soo happy with the new patch I for one are pleased for them. What you have to understand is, having played this game for quite some time now I have spent moths learning the ways of the world and developing several characters. This has not been easy, alot of it has been trial and error... fer god`s sake i wast aware of over equipping untill a month after playing (due to just playoing a ma). This in my mind although a bit exploited is not too bad, and certainly not as bad as Funcom seem to make out. I cant quite understand why they seem so set on stopping this.. after all it was they who implemented it into the game from the very outset, after all the Trader proffession proves and justifys this point. If I were a developer I would see issues such as proffession imbalances and serious lag problems and server instability as more of an issue. Dont get me wrong I think some of the over equipping people go for is outrageous. But art reflects life.. in the real world people do exploit and some will always have an advantage and I for one am impressed to see such feats.

It seem to be that funcom will not be happy untill everyone is living in a Eutopian Ruby Ka.. where no proffesion is better than another and everyone gets treated the same.. This is not only unrealistic but very contradictory.. as there is suppost to be a war going on.. Not that we`ll ever get any proper battles because any more than 10 people in one place and half of them get spat out by the inadiquate servers. Oh by the way this is not funcoms problem its us as players we should know better than to go to buisy areas. Its also not funcoms problem as they have more pressing buisness nerfing every class in this game with the 14.2 patch. Well all accept a couple, thats why i`m gonna start a meta physasyst. Well any proffession that can self buff EVERY SINGLE NANO STAT has got to be good. SORRY to say this but as an engineer, we have no weapons we can use.. well not any more.. 14.2 will stop me using my nice shotgun, and my bot will be green to me. Only due to my level cap hitting at level 80 and not being able to raise my two most important skills ... time and space and matter creation. But as an engineer surely as my main point of existance in the whole game is to be able to create and maintain a robot there should be self buffs for these two skills??? NOOOOO of course not. However i can heal my pet...unfortunatly one of the nano skills relys on BIO MET?? PLEASE SOMEONE TELL ME since when have the engineers pets ever been evern partly bioloical??? So that screws that idea. Can we charm or take over other bots??? nope... can we nuke like crats or meta phys? nope... do we have good weapon skills in anything other than pistol? nope.. But we do have trade skills... well only 4500 rings to make till i reach level 101 .... HAHAHAHAHA This is becomming tedious now.. i could go through ost other professions and how the patch will effect you all, but I think most will have given up reading by now. All i can say is this.. YOU ARE ALL PAYING CUSTOMERS and YOU ALL HAVE A SAY IN THIS GAME AS WE ARE A COMMUNITY, but if you`re all gonna sit on your apathetic arses and do nothing then remember this. IF YOU SETTLE FOR NOTHING NOW YOU` SETTLE FOR NOTHING LATER.

I think that sums up most of the thoughts of long termers, Please funcom think of how well this game was going back in october and try to work things though from there instead of this over zealous reaction to shoddy patching.
