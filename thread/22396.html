What I'd like to see in the expansion.
- iFeel force feedback for mice that support it.. Easy to implement.

- More detailed mission areas. Office like buildings could use more things in the rooms. Like tables, chairs and such. Even non hostile Npc's roaming around. Like hostages and such.

- - Adding rescue mission types. (hostages, VIP's that sort of thing)

- More variation of weapons per category.

- Up the mininum range damage of weapons more as they grow in QL levels. No fun hitting for 20 damage with a QL 90+ weapon more than 5 times in a row :P

- Greater variation of higher level armour. New designs, more detail in the geometry, but without forgeting LOD (level of detail) models for them to be simple untill you see em real close.

- More "special" missions. Like character tasks. Related to your class/profession with rewards appropriate.
Like multipart missions, where you get a new mission description/key on the completion of the previous one, and instructions to move on.
Good idea to use merit points derived from missions completed 100% (no not tokens, too many people have harvested those at lower levels, which does and should not reflect the characters success rate.), and use those merit points to reward the player with these "special" missions.

- Multi level mission areas. Which from what I understand is getting added. But I'd like to see it both in the form they have now for the team missions (the up/down switches), but elevators would be cool
Also multi-platform rooms. Like cellars/boiler rooms with stairs and such. Already being done in a few missions but only for the looks.

- More affordable vehicles at lower levels.

- New "rational" look to the vehicles. The probe vehicle just makes little to no sense size wise. Takes away from the "immersive" world that someone just goes *poof* and is suddently a tiny flying saucer. (a minor pet peeve )

- More rewards and encuragement towards TEAM PvP.

- More "viable" items for trade skill construction. Possibly make it easier to construct higher QL items, but give crafted items durability. So a trader can maintain a steady customer base for modifications/repairs/upgrades/replacements of items.

- More guild bonuses skills built on the guilds overall "merit" points. The more points your guild aquires through players doing missions, pvp/team pvp and such, the more skills they can buy/upgrade. This gives guilds a more solid reason to reqruit people of all levels, and to encurage teamwork between said players.

- Heat up the clan/omni conflict. People join either side for a reason. To fight the other side! Give people a more motivating/rewarding reason to do so. Be it getting control over bases, zones, new wompa routes or what not.

- Give mission "special" loot unique textures, different from storebought.

- Make more armour dropped in dungeons/team missions/missions unique. Add a random modifier to said armour, but at the same time make said armour no-drop. It should be yours and yours only, tailor made to prevent extra twinking.

- Ballance out some of the weapons currently ingame.
It doesn't make much sense for a gun to be half as effective as another, yet have nearly 3x the requirements on it.

- Allow people more weapon customisation that is viable. IE choosing a slugger over a mausser to actually be similar in damage output, but at the same time cost about the same to use.
To quote a great line from Cyberpunk, "style over substance! Its better to be dead and cool, than alive and uncool."
If people want to dual-wield two SMG's, make it an option thats viable. Same goes for other dual-wieldable weapons.

- Have attack speed give a greater chance to disrupt someones casting/attacking. Be it a player or an Npc. This would make picking faster, lighter weapons a viable option in the long run when considering pro's / cons and damage over time.

- lots more but this is long enough for now

- Aeom
