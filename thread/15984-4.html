Here's how they take on 3 level 170 mobs at level 100. It's really simple.

1) Calm all three
2) BIIIIIIG nuke the first one
3) calm it again
4) heal
5) repeat steps 2-4 until first mob is dead
6) repeat steps 2-5 on second mob
7) repeat steps 2-5 on last mob

As long as you haven't neglected your body dev (for those 1 or 2 hits they will get on you before you mezz) and you've maxed your nano init and use a recompiler (so that your mezz is insatcast), it's actually pretty easy even if it does take like 15 minutes to take those 3 mobs down.
