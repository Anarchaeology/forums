Maybe I'm mis-understanding here?
Perhaps I don't understand the whole point of this thread. I'm currently a level 54 Engi with 122 tokens. Yes, I token farmed at low levels to get that board. When I picked up missions in the beginning, well if I didn't like the mission type I scrapped it and moved on. Otherwise I completed my mission, got my token and moven on. Now at 54 I am running missions to get specific items, well I don't care about the token, so I (we) kill mob's, find the item and leave. If I feel like leveling I (or we) run the entire mission and kill everything in site for the experience, the bigger the mission the better! If you are running missions solo at level 100+ you are nuts anways, cause mob's at that level have 8K hit points and more, certainly not a one-man job. In summation there are several reasons to run missions :

1) Every level token farming - Size of mission is too big - scrap it.
Missions are pretty close anways
2) Item finding missions - Blitz the mission looking for the item
and kill anything that gets in your way ( granted sometimes the
item is in the last room, but that's part of the fun in it ).
3) Leveling type missions - The bigger the better - bring on more
mobs, more experience and who cares about anything else.
Best if run with friends, but even at level 54 I can solo one or
two missions and level once
4) High level missions - Used mostly for the experience or with a
team for a specific item - Solo'ing = suicide for the most part
and blitzing is near impossible.

There may be more to it than that, but missions have a lot of uses, and you know if you hate the size of the mission, go get another one ... what did it cost you 80 credits? Let's not nit-pick
mission size, why don't we take a look at the token board, an area FunCom REALLY needs to fix.
