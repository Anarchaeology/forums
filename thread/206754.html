An Invitation
On behalf of Division 9 R.S.G.E. I extend to all those here an invitation to a casual Meet and Greet with myself and some of the Division's other members. The event shall be held on two days and at two times. First being Saturday Dec. 6 at 9PM EST(GMT -5) the second being Sunday Dec. 7 at 2PM EST(GMT-5). The events shall be held at the Rompah Bar in Omni-1's Entertainment District.

I would like to encourage any and everyone with any questions regarding Division 9 R.S.G.E. to stop by and chat with us. Specific relationships will not be negotiaited at these functions, but other talks may be scheduled. So, if you are looking for an Organization to join, or are in one, or even Clan or Neutral, and simply wish to see what the name on that gun has come to mean stop by and ask whatever questions you wish. I will answer all questions honesty and as completely as I can.

Thank you for your consideration of this offer.
