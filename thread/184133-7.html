You contradict yourself by admitting you raise map navigation for the map upgrades you use in missions.

Imagine for a moment that your minimap and your map upgrades stopped working inside the missions you wanted to do. Imagine that you had to find an Adventurer every hour to turn your minimap and upgrades back on inside your missions. Maybe you would still feel that your minimap and upgrades were unnecessary but I doubt very many people would agree with you.

For me the most necessary function of the minimap isn't the terrain or walls it shows. The terrain is often inaccurate outdoors anyway. The most necessary functions are the arrow, machines, monsters, people, and side upgrades. I actually wouldn't care very much if the terrain part of the minimap was Adventurer only as long as the map upgrades continued to work on a blank minimap.

What's even more baffling about Funcom's decision to make the maps for most playfields Adventurer only is that the minimap works perfectly indoors whether you have the map for that playfield uploaded or not. How can your minimap work on one side of a door but not on the other side?

Another thing that is baffling is that the Land Control part of the minimap does partially work even if you don't have the map for that playfield uploaded.

Sorry. I got carried away. Give me back my minimap in all playfields and I'll be quiet for a while.

P.S. Perhaps if all the map reader upgrades worked on the minimap even if you didn't have the map for that playfield uploaded that would be good enough for most of us.
