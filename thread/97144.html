Professionals Voteing Results #4 Good and Bad news.
    High priority issues
    #1)Roots in PvP being too powerful, and not all can get rid of them.(Adv)
    #2)Healing range in PvP - level 150 Doctors being better than level 200s.(Doc)
    #3)Crowd control heavily limited in PvE due to bosses and monster immunities / high resist.(Crat)
    #4)Multi-wield update - is the gain supposed to be only 30%, and if so, why?(Adv)
    #5)Outdoor bosses being very dangerous, yet they are necessary to take down to get new nanos(Eng)
    #6)Trader nanos look-over - charm rooting, and nano drains and health funnels not paying off.(Trad)
    #7)Black and white nature of Psychic checks on charms create level gaps in charm and fear effect(Crat)


The good news is that we have a second chance to get our crowd control in PvE answered and the second topic closely related to it, psychic checks too. I sincearly hope that the answers to our questions this time will be more in-depth than answering the wrong question(for Crowd Control in PvE, they talked about roots/snares when we meant charm/fear/mez) and "This is working as intended"(for Mez level%, even though greens take 2-3 tries.)

Take a hint, Bureaucrats need looking over. This is the third consecutive time that our issues have made it as a high priority. The responses to those previous 2 times have unfortunately dismissed or didn't acknowledge that they even were a problem in the first place. Can't fix something that isn't recognized as a problem to begin with, no?

Now the bad news is that top priority #1)Roots in PvP being too powerful could have the potential for a very negative impact on our already poor performance in PvP(The one thing we're really good at, more than damage.) And this issue is directly opposite of one of the concerns that affect our ability to use the remaining tools that are functional in PvP. (Charm/fear/mez don't work on players, leaving root/snares. Crat snares are grossly inferior to Fixer area snares thus leaving roots alone as a somewhat good remaining ability.)
Refrence: Disturbing trend emerging in 14.8

There are a wealth of information and suggestions on how to correct the issues plagueing the bureaucrat profession in this forum from the player community.

It is going to take a bit more than a briefcase to fix us.
