The Yang to my Ying
The Yang to my Ying
by Randa "Poetica" Whites

A wise old man once told me
There is a Yang to everyones Ying
He told me this:
Once upon a time
In the dawning of mankind
All male and female souls were joined
As one
He told me this was the way it was supposed to be
A Yang to everyones Ying
One perfect union
But destiny interfered
And they were separated
Now we must all search
Maybe in vain
Maybe in pain
For that other part of our soul
Trying to find the Yang for our Ying
Trying to become whole again
So I search
And I fail
But I never give up
Because a wise old man told me
Theres a Yang to everyones Ying
And Ill search for him
Somehow
Somewhere
Ill find him
Maybe Ill fail
And just maybe.
Just maybe
I found him this time
