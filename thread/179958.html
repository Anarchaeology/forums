My Guides to AO :)
It's been a while since I trolled on through the beginner forum, but in a shameless act of self-promotion I'm going to leave links to a number of game guides I've written here on the forums.

The Guide to Auto-Attack describes how the game's auto combat system works (ie, what happens when you attack something without a special).

The Guide to General Perks describes the bonuses and specials from all the general perks available.

The Doctor Guide to Notum Wars describes how to survive and maybe even be occassionally useful on the notum wars battlefield.

The Doctor Guide to Martial Arts is an explanation of the martial arts skill, martial arts attacks, and martial arts fighting tactics as can be employed by Doctors. Lots of great info for "real" MAs though

The Guide to Doctor Perks is just what the name says

And finally, the Unity of the Rose AO tools - this includes an agg/def and inits calculator, a script editor, and a script to add an entire guild to your buddy list.

All this stuff is linked from my signature too, if you ever need to find it again in another post.
