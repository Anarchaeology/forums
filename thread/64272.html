A solution to Ninja Looting Nodrops
The scenario of this is usually a problem restricted to Tarasque, but one that causes a very large amount of player grief.

What happens is Tara gets killed, damage team gets loot and one person takes all of the dropable items off the corpse. The nodrop items stay on the corpse and are raffled. In order for the person that won to loot, the damage team only looting has to time out and the person that 1st looted needs to close loot.

The problem is that the person that won the loot is now competing with every individual there to get the loot window to open first.

A possible solution to this is as follows and requires 2 things to happen:

1. The length of time for the damage team to loot the corpse needs to be extended for something like 10min. This may seem excessively long, but due to lag, many people keep on fighting tarasque 5-10min after he is actually dead.

2. If an individual joins a looting team, then that person needs to be able to loot during the "damage team only" loot time. I am not sure if this occurs atm.

So the scenario is that one person drops from the damage team and the person that won the item is then recruited to the damage team. They are then free to loot the corpse without worry of ninja looters clicking on it faster than they do.

This seems like a relatively simple solution that would prevent a massive amount of petitions and general strife. There of course could be flaws that I am missing and I would appreciate any constructive criticism.
