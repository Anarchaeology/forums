Betji's guide to Fun and Frolic for younger Omnis on Rubi-Ka
As a freelance omni agent, I've met the most interesting people, without exception, in Rompa's bar. Now, it's true that sometimes it's very quiet, but I've found that if you're patient, and strike up a conversation with whomever IS there, one thing gradually leads to another and you're making friends, meeting new people, etc., etc. It's also true that in my earlier, wilder days I picked up a few people there, and was picked up myself, which led to some VERY interesting situations. A variety of interesting organizations make Rompa's their official or unofficial gathering place or patrol route, depending on their viewpoint.

Baboon's is just coming alive, now that the new airlocks make the place a lot more livable. In fact, dropping into Baboon's last night, I was shocked, shocked to find Templus in chit-chat with some Devil's Advocates, and she herself had brand new DA affiliation instead of her traditional Omni-Pol uniform and frown! What was even more shocking is that she dained to give me a few tips about agent work, when in the past she refused to even acknowledge my existence. Baboon's is the extremely unofficial gathering place for the Devil's Advocates, a great bunch of people.

Neuter's in Newland used to be a place of occasional private meetings and other interesting events, but the presence of those many mercs with contracts out on any clansman have largely eliminated "Newts" as a "neutral ground" meeting place. Yes, I know that a careful person can get in and out safely via Whompas and the side alley, but most clanners are still seriously upset about those Newland mercs, and simply refuse to come.

Reet's Retreat is THE nightclub of Rubi-Ka. Unfortunately, it's more the place you visit with friends, your date, or your steady, rather than go alone in hopes of meeting someone. The live gridstream DJs used to be the hit of the planet, but unfortunately they were just switched to serving their music via a "sign in" grid service, and the transition is a little rocky. Still, you just can't beat the ambiance of listening to a live DJ commenting on the same antics you see going on all around you.

Reets starts to warm up about 9pm Eastern (GMT-5), and hits its stride about three hours later. Fridays and Saturdays are, predictably, the hottest nights. Once you get to know some people, half the fun is noticing who is leaving with whom! The other great thing about Reet's is that sooner or later, almost all the currently active and famous personages of Rubi-Ka will make an appearance. At the moment, the most prominent organization at Reet's, aside from Gridstream, is Valor Eternal. Now, this is just a personal opinion, and my VE friends will probably sputter at the thought, but I've always suspected the organization would be better titled Party Eternal. They certain do throw dynamite Christmas parties.

The Cup, in West Athen (a clan city), as mentioned above, is safely available via grid for omnis. I visited recently, dragging my lady love Blackpetal over there as well. Hey, if she's going to consider doing legal work that might aid the clan cause, I figured she might as well meet some in the flesh! It is clearly the unofficial gathering place of Vitae Covente, since they outnumbered all other visitors, combined. Once you got past the fact that every single one had some horror story about omni, and were very passionate about it, they were a nice bunch of people.

Finally, strange to say, once every blue moon I meet an interesting person in a mission or hunt. I know, I know, there are lots of sickeningly uberd00dz and occasional uberd00detz running around without the meanest iota of personality. But every so often you run into the exception. Cultivating these always pays off. I've made some of my best friends by connecting with such a person, getting accepted into their circle, etc., etc.

Good luck, and happy adventuring on Rubi-Ka.

- Betji, Agent 62nd, Omni, Atlantean

Socializing 101...
Well, my sweetie put it very nicely, and I don't have too much to add... For Omnis, Rompa definitely ends up being a better place to make new friends than the Reet Retreat... it makes sense, doesn't it? You go to a bar to hang out and chat. You go to a club to dance. Hard to dance and talk at the same time, right? Especially with the loud music... ^_^;

The Cup is very cute, but I have to say that the combination of coffee and no restrooms is a very poor bit of architectural planning... ^_^;; My advice: definitely "go" before you go... ^_^ Although, you know the old saw about clanners and hygiene... ^_^

I also would like to second missions with other people as GREAT way to meet new people... I met several of my closest friends, AND a (now ex-)boyfriend through missions, and I highly recommend it. I think there is something about the pressure of a mission that reveals a person's true qualities. A person who is considerate, generous, and looks out for his or her fellows during a mission is probably at least that nice in a social setting. ^_^
