I'm still a bit baffled about this. Nanomages have been screaming for a fix to their hp for 16 months now, and when FC finally come out of the closet on the issue, the only thing they could possibly think of was nanopool? I just hope to god this is just the first meager step towards balancing things out.

If you measure the "value" of hitpoints agains nanopool, I don't think anyone would argue that hitpoints will win out every time. That is, any nanomage will _always go for the hp they can get from items over anything else. Problem here is that any breed normally okay on hp will still go for the exact same hp boosting items, even though something else might benefit the lack of nano/nanoskills more. Add to this that in a way, I feel nanomages are "forced" by FC to use any hp boosting items they introduce, making everything else obsolete for them.

People bring up the nanomage-only items over and over that will "help us", but in essence they are obsolete as such. Nanomages doesnt need more intelligence or psychic, nor a small boost to their nanoskills at all. When you have what you need to cast a program, the usefulness ends. No pogram will get better, or hit more often, or do more damage no matter how much higher your nanoskills are. The Shades or Jones Vest? I'll take the mk2 hood and body every time for the 280hp alone. It doesn't matter in the long run - a nanomage is perfectly capable of getting his or her Intelligence to 650+, but for what? It doesn't do _anything at that point, and the benefit from a higher stamina or bodydev is much more "valuable" for the hp, or even Agility/sense for a higher attack rating.

More HP for Atroxes is good, or better yet, more difference in breeds is very good - I hope this will be built upon to become even more clear. However, when it comes to Nanomages, the lack of hp is so severe they are seriously at a disadvantage either way when not playing the proffesions most reliant on nanopool (Doc, NT?). I play a Nanomage Meta myself, and I can't really say nanopool is important to me at all - without constantly nuking (and why would I?), there's no nanopool shortage whatsoever. And that is even without the countless obsolete ways Metas have to boost their nanopool to ridiculus amounts already.

That said, the point I'm aiming at is that with the addition of extra nanopool, you must balance this out towards something else. For nanomage docs, the hp is already way better than normal due to proffession, and the extra nanopool will help them heal more between rechargers - wich is good, and with nanomage NT's it directly means a couple more nukes for dmg output. You have already stated that the aim with the NT's are for them to be the greatest dmg dealers, and that will naturally rely heavy on nanopool - also good since the dmg output vs poor health constitues a balance of sorts. But what about nanomage Metas? Unless something similar is done so they can make use of the nanopool to compensate for their lack of hp, it's all pretty meaningless.

So what I'm wondering is really, what will do you do with with this fundamental change to gameplay for the proffesions in question? Without further increasing the "value" of a good nanopool for the proffesions that doesnt normally rely on it too much (I'm guessing every nanomage that is not a Doctor or NT), the "fix" does absolutely nothing for them.

Will you make nanopool valuable to everyone, or is this the last we'll see on the issue?
