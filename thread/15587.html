Meeting halls / conference rooms
Everyone who has been to a guild meeting will probably have experienced uninvited disruptive guests.

Since guild still do not have their own buildings, some common structures might be good.

I'm thinking along the lines of a large meeting hall / auditorium working somewhat like a multi spawn dungeon :

- A meeting holder first enters the hall and is given a temporary key
- All members of the guild the meeting holder belongs to may now enter freely
- Guests / recruits are given copies of the key from the meeting holder.

The same meeting hall can be used by as many guilds as necessary, spawning a separate "dungeon" for each person who enters without a key ( meeting holder )

The keys can time out after, say 1-2 hours ?

Another nice touch would be if the name of the hall reflects the guild that hosts the meeting.

"Entering meeting hosted by SomeGuild"

Comments / rants welcome
