Proposal...
(for some reason I'm hard to give up)

Let's see just what people want to do with the concept of freedom.

I propose the formation of the Rubika Free Travel League.

A loose coalition of individuals from all three factions that will, from time to time, form a team equally represented by all three factions (2 of each) for the express purpose of temporarily riding major travel hubs of guards that would impeed free travel.

Specifically for a short period of time this team would calm or kill guards at the Tir, NLC and Trade whompas.

We would meet at ICC and travel to each of the cities and calm/kill the guards there to temporarily provide free access to the citizens of Rubika.

I am fairly sure Fixerben and Cyani would join me in these routine 'pest' control patrols...

All I need to see now is which of you Neutrals and Omni are so inclined... (this will pretty much measure the value of 'freedom' of each of the factions in my mind)
