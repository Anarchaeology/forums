I do want to shed light on why you guys are getting agro alot.

Ok we all know that getting on a hatelist is based on Healing or Dmg output. Now let me explain this to you.

Ill take my Team heal buff ( infuse life , you know the one that lasts for an hour and regens 100ish points of dmg every so often )

You cast that before battle as we all do. Well every 30 secs you get a message saying " You have healed blah blah for X hitpoints"

That heal incurs agro.

Say your tank is pulling 2 red mobs. He shoots one getting 5 point of hate (based on him doing dmg to that mob) while the other is at 1 hate (just for agro purposes).

As hes running back your Team buff heals him. Because the tank already has agro on BOTH mobs when the mobs *sense* anyone helping them (ie doc) you incur agro. Sooo you end up healing the tank for 150 points. Giving you 3 points of hate.

Do the math. Mob 1 has 5 points of hate from the soldier minus the 3 you gave him for healing thus that mob still has 2 hatepoints on the tank

The other mob has 1 hate point minus 3 from your team buff. Thus the mob turns on you and agros beacuse you have the highest amount of hate.


Its kinda hard to explain and I did a shoddy job of it, but I think you get the point.
