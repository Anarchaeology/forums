The 2nd Coming
..the scene is outside the omni-1 spaceport, 2 years ago...

...The skies are rumbling with thunder, and lightning streaks the sky. Rain is pummeling the ground and the small steel awning adorning the front of the space port, two men, a weary looking solitus and an intelligent looking nano-tech, are talking to eachother, talking loudly, almost yelling, to outdo the incredible storm....

"Well, I guess this is goodbye for now, Hans." muttered the tall solitus in the rain-soaked suit.

"Youre making a big mistake, Andy, youre going to regret it..."

"I've heard incredible things about old earth, the government is in shambles, if I play my cards right... I could practically take over the planet.."

The nano tech shook his head.

"Youve always been a dreamer, Andy, nothing is as easy as it seems. Old Earth is a dangerous place, full of mutations and thugs.. not to mention the space pirates circling the Sol System, its a dance with death."

The solitus smirked and said a few off color things.

"Youve always looked out for me, Hans, ever since we met.. all the things weve been through, everything weve discovered, youre like a brother to me, and im never going to be able to thank you for it all.. its been great, but I need to move on.."

A massive bolt of lightning shot down from the dark grey clouds, nearly hitting the nano tech, he dodges out of the way and rolls under the safety of the awning.

The solitus smirked and stuck his hand into his pocket, grabbing out a ticket.

"Hans, its time for me to go... goodbye for now my friend.."

The nano tech scrambled after him, and tried to make one last ditch shot to make him stay.

"Andy, all weve accomplished, all the friends youve made, everything. The memories, the good times. How can you just leave them all behind like this, how can you betray the planet that you loved so much?"

The solitus looked down for a minute

"Youre the only one that came to see me off, arent you? Many of my friends have already left this dust bowl. After the Entropy Dawn corporation collapsed over greed, I have nothing left to stay here for..."

The solitus pushed the nano tech away and headed into the space port.

"Ill see you soon, Andy.." said the nano tech, walking off into the rain...

The solitus fought off tears, he didnt know what he was doing, but it was too late to turn back now. He boarded the shuttle with tears in his eyes.

"What have I gotten myself into...?"

..we now enter the life of the solitus, Andy "Prezidential" French, its been a year since he left Rubi-Ka and thanks to space pirates, things on old earth arent going as planned...

Blaring sirens woke me up, it wasnt anything new, I heard them regularly.. I had almost gotten use to them..

"Hey French, get the hell up, weve got about 200 muties at the Westminster Gate, and theyre not leaving anytime soon."

This was not the life I had expected here, I was a politician, not a soldier, but we all have to start somewhere...

Me and about 23 other men scrambled out of a ruined looking building, covering our heads and ducking behind abandoned car chassis and rubble every few minutes

I heard an explosion from the right of me, and I dove into a blown out super market just in time to dodge a falling 3-story office.

I felt dizzy and heard screaming, I felt around my chest and found a large gash in my side...

"Lookz liikeee teh baztagggez gott balllizzztikzzz." everything was going dim, I could hear anything right.

"Mae gud yav meci un uz." is the last thing I heard before I blacked out......

I started dreaming, I wasnt sure if I was dead or not, but I subconsiously living out memories of my life back on Rubi-Ka.

The first memory I was inside the eyes of the statues that adorned Omni-1 Trade, I saw a hopeful looking kid running under neath me, with a faithful bureaucrat assistant running aimlessly to keep up, running into a few buildings and signs in the process.

Then I saw a bird fly by, I detached from the statue and became the eyes of the bird. I flew all over Rubi-Ka and saw things like id never seen them before, our last stop was over the docks in Omni-1. I saw a squad of Omni-Pol officers strafing towards one of the docks. A sleek looking business man was pushing serious creds in the face of an officer for info, and just as the deal looked sealed, the officers sprang out. The business mans side kicks dived into the water and swam away.

The bird flew down on top of one of the sidekicks, and with that I became the sidekick.

I swam to a beautiful city, a city like none I had ever seen before.

There was a party in the streets, and almost everyone I knew was there...

Just as the dream was getting good I felt a bright burst of light come over me, and I was shot back into reality.

It was a med room of some kind, with bloody walls and floors and rusted tools at my side. It wasnt a very comfortable place to be in, but it had saved my life without a doubt.

Slowly I climbed to my feet, expecting to be ok.


Man, was I spoiled by reclaim


I fell to the ground in wrenching pain, I had been patched up but there was quite a bit of healing that still had to be done.

I layed on the cold steel floor, looking up at the light.. it reminded me of the Rubi-Ka sunset.. it sure was a beautiful sight..

"Why the hell did I ever leave home.." I thought, as I drifted back into sleep...

Back on Rubi-Ka at the Omni-Entertainment East Bronto Burger...

Hans Zanter stands at the Brono Burger drinking some iced coffee. He decides to execute some free mochams and assemble some nano-augmentations for those in need to promote his guild, Omni-Admin's, semblance.

A relatively young bureaucrat walks up to Hans.

"Hey there, i'm pretty new and was wondering if you could help me with these crazy time and space and umm... matter creation skills that I need to make my bodyguard Mr. Zanter"

"Hey there, of course I will help you, free of charge, call me Deuscloud by the way, so if you ever need help sometime later you can give me a call."

Hans casts time, space, and matter creation mastreies on the crat and sends him one the way.

"Thanks a lot Deuscloud!"

As the crat walks off, Hans notices how strikingly similar the young crat looks to his old friend Andy (mullet and all) who left Rubi-Ka what seems like 100 years ago to Hans.

Hans then begins recollecting how he met Prez so long ago in that very place, and everything of the real worrld vanishes for Hans and he can only see his thoughts.

(flashback)
"Hey there Mr. umm Zanter, do you think you could cast some nano masteries on me please; I need a new pet."

"Sure by all means, here you go... Mr. French."

Hans casts some masteries on Andy

"Thanks Mr. Zanter. Heh those are some weird looking eyes you have." (noticing the bizarre slits that Hans has for eyes)

"Umm, yeah I have special eye augmentations they make me more connected to the metaphysical world through my vision, they look strange but I like them a lot... hmm what a nasty thing to say."

Hans half smiles.

"Ahh, I meant it in the nicest way possible" (Andy grins) "As long as I don't end up looking like that!"

Hans chuckles.

"That could be arranged Mr. French."

Hans pulls out a tool resembling a notum-enhanced scalpul, then he puts it away.

"Oh! That's prefectly okay, i'm perfectly happy with my eyes at the moment." Andy snickers. "Hey by the way you can call me Prezidential, my nickname. I really appreciate the nano buffs!"

"No problem at all, you can call me Deuscloud, or Cloud, or whatever you prefer, hey I was just going to the Rompa Bar to get another iced-coffee, you are welcome to come if you would like."

"Sure, that would be fun, i'm not too busy anyway, could use a drink myself Cloud."

Prezidential creates his newest pet

"Wow very impressive Prezidential!"

Hans and Andy go to Rompas and end up becoming very good friends...
(end of flashback)

Hans is sucked back into reality by a low level nanotechnician waving a hand in front of his face.

"Ugg... oh, hi there.. can I help you?"

"Yeahyeah! I thought you were sick or something, but now that you seem okay, could you help me with my matter creation skill please Mr. Zanter?"

(still dazed from being brought back to reality) "Umm yeah, of course, here..." Hans casts infuse with knowledge: matter creation on the nano-technician "Enjoy..."

"Of course I will, thanks!"

Hans suddenly feels a dark metaphysical cloud of sadness and frustration falls over him and his emotions, so he walks off to the Rome Red district to see the nice scenery and think a little bit about what was making him so sad. Walking and thinking alone always seemed to help Hans when he was sad about something.

Hans walks along he side of the big pool in Rome Red, running his hand along the railing surrounging it. He thinks about Andy, and how they had so many excellent times together, he remembers the time that they had to escape from some clanners in milky way with no means of transportation, he remembers when they had to find that coffee thief in central artery valley.

Hans sits on one of the benches and looks at the beautiful perfectly clear and pure water of the pool and how it reflects the stars and the moons and the sparkling muildings of Rome Blue and Rome Green to his left and right.

Hans wonders about what Andy is doing now... is he some excellent leader that is bringing Earth back to the way that it used to be before all of its corruption? Could he be fighting some pirates? Could he be dead in some battlefield somewhere?!

Hans wishes there was some way to contact Andy, but knows that there isn't. Hans thinks to himself a bit...

"It's so far away, there is no way a message could transmit to a place of that much distance... they probably don't even bother sending messenger ships anymore as they are always shot down by space pirates and such.. hmm.."

Hans thinks some more in sadness and with a lack of hope. Hans always tries to stay in touch with reality and doesn't hope for things that he knows are hopeless. He doesn't have alot of faith in anything that lacks credibility, these qualities (or a lack of them) probably take away from his personality.

"If I were a solitus, I would just plain go to Earth, i'd risk it, but I can't. I'm a nanomage and i'm stuck here with my notum. What a wonderful thing it must be to have such freedom, where one can decide they just want to leave Rubi-Ka. I love Rubi-Ka and wouldn't ever want to leave permanently.. but it's difficult in times like this..."

Hans comes to the conclusion that it is absolutely impossible to contact Andy, and that the only way he will ever see Andy again is if Andy decides he wants to come back to Earth... if he isn't dead thinks Hans. There is nothing Hans can do to relieve his sadness, so he heads off to his lab near Red Tape's base in 4 Holes and sleeps. He dreams and thinks and feels frustrated that there is absolutely nothing he can do.
