If you can afford the ip to equip a good ql one, they're... iffy, even with a good attack rating. If you're a weapon switching soldier, this is a large amount of ip being spent ie Assault Rifle, Ranged Energy, Fling, Burst, Full auto. I used to hotswap between a Novaflow and a Flashpoint, but after IPR, I dumped full auto and equiped a 200 Nova.

Reasons being are:
1.More reliable damage
2.FA is like the lottery... sometimes you get a nice phat hit... sometimes you do pitiful damage. On a 100ql Flashpoint, I got a 6k FA on a mob, insta-killed the gun beetle in the TRL dungeon at 72 and made the teams I did in Lush Fields green with envy. However, with the same Flashpoint, I've done 25 points of damage... on a green mob. Most of the time, the FAs were between 1-2k.
3.If you're hit with a Ransack/Deprive or a Divest/Plunder, you're screwed with a heavy hitting weapon that's as slow as it gets.
4.That extra special eats a ton of ip...
5.Implanting hell. If you're a non soldier, your attack rating WILL be @$$.
6.Very easy to counter the damage done by the Flashpoint by using buffs such as RRF.

So... I suggest... Snakemaster+whatever scope you can get into.

Or make an Enforcer or MA. They PvP pretty well. Not the best, but certainly, far from the worst.
