The Agent Story
It is not easy to be raised as a corporate soldier on Rubi-Ka. Death and reclaiming all around you. You do not even know if your inscurance scan is void or not. I work for Omni-Tek, just like my parents used to. They were both Opifexes and my father worked in Omni-Admin and my mother as a Omni-Pol secretary. My childhood was not full of dispair. My father's job was more than enough to afford a luxury Highrise apartment in Omni-1 Entertaintment. Sadly, they both of aging several after they refused Omni-Med inscurance.
I am now an agent for Omni-Admin, but I am not directly affiliated. I have been sent on many assignments including espionage, assisination and infiltration into local Clan organizations, but this new assignment was not the usual Bronto in a bag. I was told to go to Tir and monitor Sentinel activity. Tir has been a hotbed of activity, and obviously Omni-Pol and Omni-AF wanted to know what was going on.
I prepared to depart to Tir. I took off my Omni-Admin Trenchcoat and replaced it with a ragged Black Cloak that offered absolutely no protection, and replaced my Omni-Pol armor with Nadir Ribbed Armor. I could still keep my X-3 though. I was given a Clan identification card and was told to avoid the Grid and the Wom-Pah systems because they were too heavily guarded by the Sentinels. Even though I was disguised, I did not want to take any risks. I took my Yalmaha, and flew all the way from 4Holes to Tir, which I did not enjoy. I entered the city through the front gates without an incident. The local guards accepted my counterfeit ID card.
It has been two weeks since my arrival into Tir, and all seems OK. I have found myself a nice apartment in Northwest Tir with a nice view that overlooked the market place. I even started a conversation with a new Sentinel. I have all ready collected a nice databank of HoloPictures and mapped out the routes of some patrols, but there were too many, the Sentinels seem to have the city under their eye.
I awoke in the middle of the night. 1:43 AM March 2, 29477 to be exact. There was a heavy storm outside and the rain buffeted the roof (my apartment was at the topmost floor). There was a loud bang on my door. "BANG BANG BANG BANG".
"Open this door now!" was the voice that came from behind it.
This was the worst nightmare for an agent. If it are the Sentinels, they will surely kill me, after all, they must think that I am an Omni-Tek employee. I turned off the safety off my X-3 and shouldered it, and loaded a Plasma pistol and put it under the coat I put on...

Riyzzo
Maybe she was overtired and suffering stress; maybe the recent events that had afflicted her had turned her into a paranoid wretch. Whatever the case, Riyzzo jumped awake and grabbed her pistol when the thudding sound echoed on the roof above her head.

Panic took over as she flung herself against a wall, cautiously peering out into the street. The cold desert night air pooled into this little lean-to she had made her home for past few days, where she had made herself vanish into the crowds of Tir. If her pursuers had followed her, they'd have to get past the Sentinals first. Indeed, there was one of them out there now -and her heart nearly stopped when he crumpled like a rag doll before her eyes.

They had found her. The local patroller was dead before her eyes. They were on the roof -they had FOUND her!

It was with a hoarse whisper that she activated her server droid. She had to move quietly, carefully. She thought there was only one person above her head: with her bot, she made two. If she was careful, she could catch him/her/it unawares and take it out before she was struck down as coldly as the Sentinal officer who lay in a pool of blood just down the way.

Steeling herself, she slipped out into the street, her palm growing slick as she gripped the pistol. Her eyes scanned the irregular line of the roof -looking for shadows...
