Should Healing Hands remain totally neutral?
"A neutral movement, which refrains from participating in conflicts and controversies, is ready and in a position to give its whole attention to suffering individuals and help them in proportion to their suffering, without a secondary purpose and without discrimination."

Its 3:25am and I cant sleep. Since the opening of notum mining to all citizens, we have had a dilemma in The Healing Hands. We wish to remain neutral as we always have been, but many of our members also wish to aid their friends in the tower wars. The Healing Hands have always tried to base their fundamentals off of the Old Earth organization, The Red Cross. The Red Crosss mission being:

"The International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) is an impartial, neutral and independent organization whose exclusively humanitarian mission is to protect the lives and dignity of victims of war and internal violence and to provide them with assistance. It directs and coordinates the international relief activities conducted by the Movement in situations of conflict. It also endeavors to prevent suffering by promoting and strengthening humanitarian law and universal humanitarian principles."

You might not see too much of a problem with this mission in regards to helping by engaging in combat. But its not just the aiding of others that is required to let our organization do what it needs to do. We need to maintain our neutrality as well. And I dont mean neutrality as in being a part of the rest of the neutral community as so many seem to believe, but aside from. Total neutrality:

"The word "neutral" comes from the Latin ne-uter and means: neither one thing nor the other. An institution or a movement is neutral when it refrains from participating in a conflict or altercation and abstains from any interference. Refraining from participation and abstaining from interference can be for various reasons: it may be a question of self-preservation and self-assertion, of the judgment that good and bad, true and false are to be found on both sides, of holding back in the interests of a higher purpose or a special task. Neutrality may however have its origin in indifference, fear and cowardice. Neutrality in itself is therefore not a virtue."

No, it is not a virtue. We are not trying to make any kind of statement or prove anything to anyone with our neutrality. From the description above, The Healing Hands neutrality is from holding back in the interests of a higher purpose or a special task. That task being to aid anyone and everyone that we can.

"The motivation of the principle of Neutrality is the Movement abstaining from any participation in hostilities and at all times in controversies of a political, racial, religious or ideological nature in order to continue to enjoy the confidence of all. This motivation is clearly well founded: those who take sides or interfere may estrange or deceive one side or the other, push them away and lose their confidence."

Now more than ever it is difficult to maintain our neutrality. Out of our very front doors, tower sprout up towards the sky. Everywhere, like trees in the growing in the Greater Omni Forest. But now it is even more important than ever to maintain our neutrality. Before the lines between Omni-Tek, Clans, and neutral factions were drawn clearly. But things have been blurred as the fights over the towers have set even formerly peaceful organizations against each other. Omni against Omni, Clan against Clan, neutral against neutral. All fight now, it seems like that is all anyone lives for. But if The Healing Hands joins in this, what will remain? We have kept our neutrality for the good of the community since our founding in November of 29.475, what good is it if we throw that all away now? We cannot allow ourselves to bend to the pressures of war.

"Adherence to neutrality is meant to create and maintain confidence. It is however also a means of ensuring the unity and universality of the Movement. Every disregard of neutrality, every taking of sides in hostilities or participation in controversies of a political, racial, religious or ideological nature, leads to tension, contradictions, fissures and divisions within  the whole Movement. If the Movement is to be a world community, which comes to the assistance of suffering people everywhere and at all times and which  as is mentioned in the principle of Humanity  "promotes mutual understanding, friendship, cooperation and lasting peace amongst all peoples", it must strictly follow the precept of neutrality in the event of armed conflict and observe it loyally "

*sigh* My head is aching and Im quite tired now and need to try to get back to sleep. Hopefully Ive stated all of my thoughts clearly here and it will help you all to understand why we need to remain neutral. To be whom we have always been, even though the times have grown so difficult and more violent than they have ever been before.

Be well,

Dr. Linaa Flowers

Even if 7/8th's of your guild leaves to pursue a different agenda, that's not a bad thing. It's (hopefully) not like they are leaving on bad terms or anything. If you and a few others decide to stay because you want to adhere to what is posted at the very top, then this is the right thing.

Take a look at the Shakers. They were a society that had a particular set of beliefs that they adhered to. They let people in who wanted to live by that set of values and they freely let members leave who no longer felt the same or were children who passed into adulthood and decided to join more mainstream society. They did magnificent craftwork, were wonderful farmers, innovators, and were respected by mainstream America. But, eventually their ranks dwindled as the lure of modern America and a cultural shift away from their diligent ethics drew from their ranks and now there are only one or two communities standing fast.

Are they sad their time is past? Do they regret not changing their beliefs to suit a more 'modern' political/moral/ethical climate? No. For doing so would be an admission that their value system was wrong. It would be tantamount to accepting that the things that drew from their ranks were in fact superior, that their whole foundation was set upon incorrect assumptions.

You can accept that times change. You can watch the world around you heave and buckle. Should this change what you believe in? Let's assume that one of a Doctor's key values is to 'Do no harm'. Should Notum Wars make you change your belief in this? Should Shadow Land? Should Star Wars: Galaxies? Should the paper boy who manages to knock the blooms off your Hyacinth change this?
