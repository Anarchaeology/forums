A real economy in Anarchy
I was reading Scumbug's post on the black market thread and he talked about other players being richer then others. Well I thought of real life economy. What if you set up a system to have things go up and down in price. For example, you would take something like the mausser (first thing off the top of my head) your prob have it now at ({price} x {QL}) = Tprice
price = price per ql
ql = qulity level of item
Tprice = the final price of item

What if insted if the number of that type of item sold in one week was greater then a number, (avg number of items sold in the last qaurter of year (yea the orginal number you would want to just do a week then when you have a quater of info)) then the price per QL would go up and if it was less it would go down in price per QL. Now you should do it for each ql but not be very sugnificant.

This would alow trades to pay attention to prices and wait till there down and buy some of them then sell them when the price went up. This will only work with items in high demand though and it would really have to drop in price enough to make a profit bc player sold goods cost less.

Aparments whould also change in price, mission put out, you would have every thing dealin with money would change bc of a reason, every could use the storyline to play with the economy a little

Also the players like traders will make more money so there will be players like there are now that have money sitting around pointless in your pocket. (I believe some players know what i mean, you just have money and nothin to do with it, you could spend it but there is nothin worth spendin it on).

This is of course would take alot of time to make but it might be worth thinkin about adding just a little bit, or if there ever is a AO2 (lol talk about another AO when the first is still be debugged majorly). But you would of course have to make it small do to small number of those types of items. yea this would be hard to do but i think it is something that should be tossed around a bit. I got some other good ideas that arent as hard. Ill post them some time but let me know what you think of these.
