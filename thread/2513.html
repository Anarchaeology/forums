OOC: You Can't Change the Storyline
How do you change the storyline? You can't. Funcom wrote a book, and have a plan for how the stroryline will progress. We are merely spectators of the storyline.

Perfect example: Do you REALLY think FC had intended there to be a Radiman double in omnihq? Yeah, riiighhht. They invented that crap when they saw that Radiman was dead. Radiman simply can't die until Funcom wants him to die. It is SO obvious that FC made up that multiple-Radiman crap to AVOID players from altering the storyline.

Wait for next week: "Rebels take Rome, oh one second, it was a FAKE rome! It was a holograph! It was an omni trick! Haha! Jedi mindtricks!"

While I WAS interested in doing some good rp, there is nothing TO roleplay, because roles have no effect on the material world.

Game reality: there has been a WAR at 2h0 since the storyline began. HUNDREDS of people die each hour. So why is this ignored in the storyline? Why do the guards still offer amnesty there? I'm waiting for an answer.

We rescue an OT engineer from the borgs. Yay for us. But we can't even interrogate her because Funcom wants us to return her (them and Lorekeep, no big difference there though.) So she gets returned. Duhhhh.

We make an attempt on the cyborg's OT engineer's life in omnihq, instead taking out 6 Omni players. Even mass killing during press conferences doesn't make the news these days!

Or that teddy bear Neleb dude. Ok, I killed him. So why the hell is he shouting outside? (ohhh i forgot, he's got doubles too.. wait a sec, my guildmates killed him too, he's got triples...) Kill all the pumpkin heads, talk peace to all of the nelebs you can find. You know how much you'll change the story? NOT AT ALL.

Why is this whole storyline thing just like watching some hastily put together TV program? Some of you RPers are acting like you are changing things -- you're NOT.

The storyline IS the movie.

Well, I think I'm gonna chime in here as well. First, I know Father Chagidiel. Meet him a long time ago when I started visiting Baboons. I can without a doubt say that he has affected me. Generally, it's an involuntary reflex roll of the eye when he goes into his rants about the great caretaker, but regardless, he does seem like a real person (i.e. not someone who just stands at the mission terms, runs out kills, and comes back).

People keep saying, "you cannot affect the world". "Funcom's story is static". Well, the second statement I would have to say is true, but it is not the full truth.

Is that the only story? No, it's the official one, but not the only one. What about your story? What about the people you befriend? What about the people you make enemies of?

That's the true story of Anarchy Online. It's the story of the players. It's the story about YOU. Perhaps no one else will ever see or experience all of it, but the people you come into contact with will always be part of it. (For instance, the events I describe in The Helpers on the In-Character forum are based on actual happenings and people inside this world)

In the end, I am Tappert Solminski, a tailor. I run a grid-based tailor shop out of my home in Rome-Blue. I walk in the Rome park. I sit at the benches, I talk to people. I have a life, I have a story. And I too have influenced many people left and right. Who says I can't change the real story of AO?




One final thing to note. In roleplaying, whether it's table-top or online, almost everyone wants action, and to be the Hero. We could go into the obvious point that online there are tens of thousands of us that make it logistically impossible for that to happen, but we won't belabor that point. What I see as the real concern and shortfall is that most people here have no sense of role playing ettiquette.

Funcom has *a* story to tell. They have their own core characters, Ross, Radiman, Elenna, et al. that are essential for them to tell it. But what I see are people who try to interact with these characters in ways that go against what Funcom had intended and then proceed to cry foul.

Role playing isn't just about cause-and-effect. If you want that, I'll create a little excel spreadsheet for you. Real effective role-playing requires compromise and cooperation between the people behind the characters. You don't just kill off someone elses character and proclaim yourself king. Such stories are always boring. But sadly, that's what some people here are trying to do.

The bottom line is, the amnesty and the Radiman-Ross meeting are Funcom's babies. Sure, you can say your opposed to the whole amnesty thing, but don't expect that just by saying such it, the world would have to be so. They're playing the leaders. Similiary, in RL, you can go down to your local goverment house and cry all you want, but it doesn't mean state policy is going to change because of it.

Anyway, that's what I wanted to say and get off my chest.


Tappert Solminski
