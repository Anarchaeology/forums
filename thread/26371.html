Sinister Anarchist Manifesto
Sinister Anarchist Manifesto

1. Our Purpose
2. Omni Tek
3. Clans
4. Neutral

1. Our purpose is one which is often mis-understood, we are here to state the truth, and defy tyranny and opression of our people. We can, and will, have something better. Something with more control over the people of the world and less destruction of needed resources.

2. We believe Omni Tek can do far better, they kill the innocent and protect the guilty. They attack those whom do no harm to us, yet those who kill us off run free. Omni Tek has more resources, power, and control than any other group/faction on this planet. Yet they are powerless when their own people revolt against them. This is why we are here, we are exposing the truth that Omni Tek is trying to hold their so-called "Utopian Society" together by destroying those that expose their flaws. By doing some background research, this sounds very much like the Old Earth fascist group, the Nazis, whom by trying to get rid of a certain ethnic group tried to destroy them all in mass-killings within gas chambers, and by pulling them out and shooting them. Strikingly similar to what is happening now. Omni Tek will not win, we will rule our domain and show people your flaws.

3. We are against the Clans because of their ignorance and futile fights which do nothing to Omni Tek. Sinister Anarchists alone is doing more and showing people more of Omni Teks faults than the Clans. Their problem lies in the fact that they believe Omni Tek citizens are also at fault, yet they do not realize it is the governments fault, not the peoples.

4. The Neutrals are too weak and worthless. They are in the same state the Clans are, yet ignoring everything and are just destroying more than they are helping. They don't know the world can be better, but when we rule Omni and once Omni is finished, the Clans and Neutrals will be next.

We will rule, and bring truth to OUR world.
We will not stop, we will not lose,
Dust To Dust.


-Winston "Shojyn" Pauly
Veteran Sinister Anarchist
Dead Cell Recruiter

Note: This is written for being inside the game, not outside. Sinister Anarchists are not against the creators of Anarchy Online, but while they make an amazing world for us to play in, it is our job to immersify and develop the world for other people. This is what we are doing.

(Sinister Anarchists views do not generally carry on into the real world, even though some of us feel the same way about Omni Tek as we do our own governments.)
