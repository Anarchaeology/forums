I'm so glad you posted this, thank you.

I love the sound work in this game and agree with all the appreciations here of it, except to say I think it's reasonable to assume some of the sounds are not used as a stylistic decision. I happen to love the way the cities sound - peaceful and entirely alien - and I wouldn't want to hear the more down to earth sounds someone suggested be present. I've heard those sounds in staging bases and mines and so forth and I think that's where they belong.

Some of the missing spell effects are corny and I agree with leaving them out, but there are a few I agree it's a shame to have left out. Maybe it's that some of you are a little cornier than I am and I'm a little cornier than Funcom's sound team.

My main interest here is in the music, and I'm so used to games coming out with goofy encrypted sound formats requiring plugins to play in Winamp that I never really looked into this, but it's wonderful to be able to look so closely at how the music is set up.

I have a question. I've only played one character, a Clan Agent, and although I've spent most of my time on the Plains I've never heard

.\music\env\plains\day\cel_pan_01.wav
.\music\env\plains\day\flute_harp_01.wav
.\music\env\plains\day\flute_harp_03.wav

I'm wondering, has anyone playing other professions or maybe an Omni Agent have heard these clips of music in the game?

Anyway, I believe Funcom does listen, for a reason related directly to the issue of missing sounds. Some time ago I decided that for me the missing title screen music was the most important issue to me in all the game, so I sent a very carefully worded and passionate argument for its restoration to feedback about that. I don't often get a response to feedback, but I did in this case, and the title music reappeared two versions later after five months missing. I'm pretty confident there was not a tremendous call for this, and it might have just been coincidence, but I've had enough experience with feedback to be convinced now that it's effective to contact that address with concerns provided you be brief, make a strong argument for your case, and not abuse it by using it to vent or contacting them every few days.
