I know that it takes effort and planning on the part of GM's or whoever to make events happen. And I appreciate that effort. However merely making an effort is not enough to accomplish much at times.

Whoever it was that was in charge of leading the event, and the players who attempted to participate, were ill prepared. After keeping a large group of Omni players waiting in 2ok, they couldnt find their way around PM. They sent everyone running off after them to the east of 2Ok, to some supposed clan outpost. They got out near the zone boundary, then realized they didnt know where they were going. A further wait ensued. They then told people to head for the clan castle down near Omni 1. After we arrived, and a large group of clanners had arrived to the south and inside the castle, we waited for some time before being given futher instructions. Once we were told to charge the castle, to which the event leaders did not know where the enterance was located, we were immediately told the operation was a success, and over. Appearantly a group of clanners had been led there as well, and I have no idea what they were told. Probably that they won as well. We were not given any explanation, no thanks for showing up, no notification that our efforts were appreciated.

Perhaps I am selfish, but I think events should include more than leading me, my guildmates, and a much larger number of other Omni players on some pointless jaunt around PM only to end up being able to swamp a few mobs that are present all the time anyway. If there in nothing we can take part in/interact with, how can it be a worthwhile event?
