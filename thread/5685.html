Unique No-Drops
Well, here is an example of what not to do:

Do not be one of the first ones to a new dungeon, especially if you don't know what item will the mob drop. Sure it's fun at first, but then, you pick up a non-drop item that only a different class can use.

So now you are stuck with a useless unique, and can't give it to your alt char, or a friend. Since respawn times for some unique mobs are >4 hours, you are not just hurting yourself, but also a lot of other players.

I've not been to the neleb's dungeon recently, but last time I was there, it looked something like this:

1 team of 5 lvl 100+, and 1 lvl 50 person.

Another team of 3 people lvl 50-75, with one by-stander asking for help in getting neleb's robe.

Now who do you think is going to get that robe? The team with most damage will.

Of course an argument could be made that, well, only teams at neleb's level should get the robe, or that perhaps only dungeon lvl players (i.e. lvl 40-75 dungeon means only lvl 40-75 players can enter)... That would introduce other problems though.

In my opinion, all no-drop items should be hackable, and the no-drop flag should be a toggle. That way I can protect items important to me, and sell mid #27, instead of simply deleting it.

The argument that it's all done for economic reasons is not convincing. Check out the prices on some ql 200 items sold on the shopping channel. If funcom really wanted to remove excess money floating around, then things like nano/elite/obtru armor would be available in the stores. After all, all these items could be labeled unique as well...

The Reason a lot of items are NODROP are because of duping exploits. Early on they realized they had a problem with players duping rare items and distributing them (ie Yalmahas).

So rather than having a bunch of level 150 players stomp through a level 40-70 dungeon picking up rare items, duping them, and selling the dupes for tons of credits on the player driven economy FunCom comes up with the quick and easy fix to this highly possible yet hypothetical situation and decides to add NODROP to a lot of the rare items found.

There is a plus side to NODROP items though. Lets say you take a mission in Stret East. You don't have a plane. So you decided to grid/wompa there. You manage to run to your mission unharmed and head inside. Somewhere during the course of the mission you find a plane in a chest. You head back out and run back to 2ho hoping you won't get ganked. Well you do half way there. Luckily for you the 5mil credit treaure you just found can't be looted. The loss of experience is little compared to all the things you can do with a plane now.
