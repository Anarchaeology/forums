QL upgrade ability? Weapon familiarity?
I don't know if these are good ideas or bad ideas, but I thought I'd go ahead and pitch them.

Perhaps there should be some nano/tradeskill/etc to increase the QL of a weapon/armor/other item. It would probably be best suited for an engineer, since that's what they do. However it would be done, it should only move it up a couple levels, a small but signifigant increase..

Higher level engineers could "stack" these QL enhancements, but it would require cumulative higher QL tradeskill items to improve on the improvement - thus a QL1 Stigma wouldn't be going to a QL 200 stigma unless you wanted to spend fifty trillion credits and ninety-two years on it.

Also, the longer you use a weapon, you could start to gain a "familiarity" level with it, which increases damage, ROF, etc. Of course, if you use an exact copy of that weapon, you wouldn't get the familiarity bonus that you would with the original. Perhaps we could even make nicknames for the weapons

Of course, it would always be cheaper to build the weapon from scratch, but some people might feel attached to their weapons if they've got a high familiarity level with "Ol' Reliable." And the familiarity level shouldn't increase ad infinitum - after all, we don't want a long-used QL5 M-1 Bipower doing more damage than a QL98 Mausser. Perhaps it should depend on how many successful bursts/aimshots/sneakatks you make with it?

I don't know how workable this is, but I think it would work out well if integrated into the game cleverly. Anyway, food for thought.
