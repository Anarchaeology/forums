Well, FunCom did two things in their systematic destruction of the game.

First they destroyed the 'immersion' factor for the sake of balance. One nerf after the other put limits on the character or otherwise added artificial restraints that destroyed my and other's beliefs that we were in a real 'world'.

Simple examples:

People in the first weeks were wisely shooting at Rhino Men from the tops of the cliffs with rifles. Instead of having the Rhinos run away or circle around to get the attacker from behind, they made them teleport to your side. Kiddy-coding amature fix #1.

People were shooting at fish in the lake and were not able to be attacked back. Instead of making the fish swim to the other side or go deep into the water, they just removed experience for doing something smart. Kiddy-coding amature fix #2.

People were using Rifles to take Sniper shots between people's eyes and killing them. Instead of making perception matter in terms of setting of an 'alarm' that someone is setting up an aimed shot at your head, or adding some sort of additional skill required to get a head-shot kill, they simply slashed the damage in half and made it impossible to kill somebody by putting a high powered rifle slug in their brain. Kiddy-coding amature fix #3.

People were sneaking missions to get items. Instead of embracing the idea of an alternate playstyle and adding additional challenges to those shady characters and rewarding them with experience for sneaking past a MOB just like they reward an Enforcer for beating its brains in, they made it so even Leets detect a lvl 200 Agent. Kiddy-coding amature fix #4 (this one they admitted was a hack and it was supposed to be temporary).

People were getting lagged up when seeing too many people. So instead of fixing the sloppy code or loading routines, they simply made everyone 'disappear'. They never gave us the option to set our view distances back to realistic values like in the start of the game. Kiddy-coding amature fix #5.

Smart players were shooting plants from 60 meters away when the plant could only detect them from 40 meters away even if they were being shot at. Instead of fixing the plants so that if they got shot at they could retaliate with their 100m range, they simply capped every damn ranged attack in the game to 40m. Kiddy-coding amature fix #6.

Groups of players used to have the mindset 'Hunting for exp. Missions for items.' People used actually level faster out in the wilderness than in missions. In fact if you hunted too long you could outgrow your gear and have to do some missions to catch up. Man, it was so cool to meet people on the roads as they moved from hunting spot to hunting spot. So FunCom thinks people are leveling too fast and remove all ability to hunt outdoors. The Mort change was the final nail in that coffin. Rather than simply raising the level limit to 300 or 1000 and start balancing on the fly, they just take the easy route and remove outdoor leveling. Kiddy-coding amature fix #7.

I could go on and on and on and on about every supposed 'fix' that they hacked together to try to address these 'problems'. In every single instance, they take some 2-line kludge hacked together hack of a 'fix' that causes further and further inability to believe that this is any sort of real 'world'. 14.2 proposes to add the icing to the cake when it comes to artificial 'invisible walls' in terms of the game rules.

I can't even begin to tell you how sick it makes me as an avid gamer to think that a qlvl101 gun could be WORSE in my hands than a qlvl100 gun while a qlvl119 gun is better than both. They really had their heads up their asses when scheming this pile up. The world isn't real anymore after all the beating they've done to it. I wrote a post last May or June entitled 'What a wonderful world' extolling the virtues of the planet and the gaming systems that went along with it. It was quite long and was an Ode to the people who put the game together. Now the game and world has degenerated into some form of corporate appeasement to the lowest common denominator.

Now, to the second point. They stopped telling the story of the planet. If someone would play Phillip Ross for ten minutes a day as he went to pick up his local newspaper that would be 70 minutes a week on average. Assume the guy who plays him gets 100$ an hour. That's one hundred and sixteen bucks for a company that is getting at least 13 bucks times 10 thousand = one hundred and thirty thousand dollars a month from its customer base.

Phillip Ross has made maybe 4 appearances in almost a year of the history of this planet. I really want to say 'screw you' to whoever dropped the ball on making this planet seem real. I don't care anymore about what's going on behind the scenes at Omni-Tek or in the Clan upper eschelons or in the enigma forest or the Cyborg barracks or anything anymore. The story died up in that outpost in Mort with a last death gasp by the Dust Brigade. When was that? January. I'm personally insulted that they expect me to pay them and they don't deliver in kind.

Nothing my guild could make up would have any bearing on what is 'real'. I could powerlevel a lvl100 clanner up on my account and 'pretend' to be some sort of character interacting with my guys. It might be fun. But it wouldn't be 'real'. Nothing 'real' happens anymore. Ross says nothing. Radiman does nothing. Nothing happens. I'm not interested to log on or check the story site anymore because I know nothing will happen. Back from October to about December I followed the story daily. I wanted to know what was going on. As condescending as it may have been, them Pumpkinheads attacked and 'boom' we had a related dungeon. Now, nothing.

What is presented to me to fill the vacuum left in the wake of the rapidly disappearing story? Posts by Dark Carnival that they and all their members are genetically altered super humans and whoever joins their ranks soon begins to mutate into a superhuman with bizarre powers as well. That crap makes me gag. <sticks finger in throat>. That's what the 'story' devolves into as you let the inmates run the asylum. You end up with people claiming they are the head of Omni-Pol. You get some chick saying she's Phillip Ross' love child and all she wants is legitamecy. All well and good if it comes from the Author of the story. A waste of my time when it comes from someone who has no more authority over the history of the book than I do. Why don't I declare myself to be a mad tyrant and the new mayor of Tir. Why? Because I'm not that much of an idiot to suppose I can usurp responsibility for the plot from the Author.

So .... I have a world that has been rendered impotent and soon to be neutered with an ongoing history that is stagnant. I can amuse myself in this impotent world with its ever constricting ruleset or hope that someone else will have some balls and create a real world that 'feels' real rather than simply looking real. Perhaps in THAT world I'll feel like making a name for myself.

With respect to the early days of UO, I always said that the players could have been portrayed as X's and O's and it would still have been an enjoyable experience. I thought AO was going to be almost as good wrapped up in a beautiful skin and a story written by the author of The Longest Journey moving along beside me all the while.

Looks like I'm getting a bunch of O's and O's that have no character development options in a beautiful world that has freakishly bizzarre rules of physics and time/space mechanics whose history is going to consist of one jackass guild going to war with some other jackass guild and a bunch of disco parties in between.
