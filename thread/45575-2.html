Originally posted by Turin
I think alot of it is the Token board factor... we all want the 1k board. There is no reliable way to work on that while hunting outdoors. I hunted my way from lvl 1-100. I think it seriously crippled me later in lvls cause while everyone else was wandering around with a 1k board, doing more dmg, getting more xp, and running a heluva lot faster than me, I worked hard or the last bit of my tokens. Finally got it though.
Here's the current issue that is creating this problem

reasons people choose missions:
token
better items (boss loot)
faster and higher xp (boss+ mission completion add a ton)
less adds
a set number of mobs in a very low amount of space

reasons to mission outdoors
looks better, more eye candy


Something seriously has to change... right now the game is programmed for you to spend all your time indoors if you want to get to high levels. Everyone most fond memories while xping seem to involve being outdoors... (I'll never forget my time at NW mines, Shades in PM, camps in OF, Ace Camp yet everyday I logoff I forget my time in missions)...

Missions shouldn't provide the best of everything... it would be best if there was a give and take, say.. mobs in the outdoors provide 40% more xp than those in missions... in other words... if I meet a lvl 60 shade in a mission I get 2000 xp, if I meet him at NW mines, I get 2800 xp... this goes on up to and including.. if I meet a real mean in a mission and get 50,000 xp, and I meet the same lvl real mean in the outdoors I get 70,000 xp... people would be forced to make a choice. Do I go to a mission and get tokens and special items or do I go outdoors to maximize my xp. I think you'd have groups of people going in both directions, which is probably closer to what was probably the original intention. In addition, in my opinion, single person mission keys should not be dupable now that we have team missions.
