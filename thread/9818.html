RE:rumors i've heard about reusable nano's getting "fixed"
except for something like one or two of my post ql120 nano's (for lowbies all nano's post ql120ish are mission only) I have gone well out of my way to make sure that I do them in reusable format for the simple reason that 2 of the people I commonly hunt with that have wound up being pretty good friends of mine also happen to be an MP and neither of us are able to do a mission for the nano's we need solo without a headache. I'm not afraid of grouping with a random MP to do a reusable crystal nano for my new pet/nuke/anima/whatever.

Since rumor's have sprung up that the reusable nano "bug" will be fixed in the near future rather than making all those caster type professions compete with each other for their nano's that they need to camp a terminal for a good bit of time I would much rather see it changed to having a mission only pickup and reward nano be made to allow uploading for 15-60 minutes from the time it's awarded. Justify it to where these nano crystals have vital interface parts that are better constructed than typical store bought ones but quickly break down and deteriorate till they are unusable.
