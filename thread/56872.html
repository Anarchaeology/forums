Already got it in aura form.

In fact, we've already got a lot of things in aura form. we've got the most powerful blind in the game, for instance. The problem with auras (single player or team) is the 20 second tick, the short range and the fact that they break if the target takes any damage at all. The team ones also interfere with nano casting and break sneak.

Late adds, gun-using mobs and rooms full of toxic slime all negate the effectiveness of these nanos. However, FC has been reluctant to give us resisted, direct attack versions, presumably because some of these are very powerful indeed. The weakest engi blind is better than the most powerful NT blind, for instance.

That said, if you can think of a good way around the problem you'll be a popular man on the Engineer forum.
