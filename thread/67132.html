I like the idea of being able to take out loans, but I suggest that you can have a more invasive accounting system. That is, if you default it would be a simple matter to garnish your income a certain percent until you pay it back. Further, interrest rates could be linked to your credit-worthiness. I also think it would really interesting for players to engage in loaning to others.

I'm not keen though on mixing social rank, IP, skills etc with the monetary system. Nor do I think that debtor's prision is a viable option. In reality, lenders want their money, and are only secondarlily concerned with punishment, except as a warning and implied threat against default. If you can get your money regardless of default, then punishment is irrelevant.

One key issue though, is that players may take out loans and pass the money on to others, while allowing the loanee to become idle. In effect this would allow players to steal cash from others without penalty. This to my mind is the key problem to overcome, and one way to do so is to link loans to the underlying account, rather than the character.
