The Great Race of Rubi-ka
This will be a very large scale race in which people will have the opertunity to take the high speed tour of Rubika. The race will be set up as follows. There will be a starting location followed by five other locations and then back to the start. There will be a person at each of the six locations to tell the location of the next and give a "seemingly" random item to the contestants as they pass. The race will range across the known world. Any and all meens are incouraged and will be necesary. Participents will have to know when it is better to grid to the neerest city so asto shorten to the trip or wether they should just go to the location by flying or running. It will be entirely posible for a crafty yet slow racer to beat out many faster ones. The race will have an entry fee and large prizes that will consist of not only the entry fees but Items and donations from our sponsers ((if you want to sponser get in touch with Kalitri at Osyrus739@hotmail.com or on this forum)). As of yet no date is set. If you like this idea and want to help out or give any feedback feal free. This is going to be a large event and my be split into two races for more expierienced ((L50+)) and less expirienced racers. With seperate prizes and entry fees. The race would be expected to last at least and hour including all six stops and will cover some of the most breathtaking areas of Rubi-ka. Please feal free to respond with coments. More info on exact details will be posted in a few days some of it based on your input (some is allready set).
-Thanks and hope you participate.
