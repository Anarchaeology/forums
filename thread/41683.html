My 2 cents.
Curtlee,
I'd suggest you doing what makes you happy. Pleasing the masses is always a lot of hard work and very little payoff back. The Entertainment industry is a VERY fickle place.

One wrong sentence, dance move or a simple thing as a side change can make or break you. It's been my experience thus far to find a small group of people you trust with your life, and have fun with them. I was once foolish and naive thinking I had 100s of friends. Turns out when your chips are down the crowds jump to the next best thing.

I do what makes me and my true friends happy now. The days of worrying about the rumor mill and the back stabbing are over. Hell, you PAY to play this game. Why worry with stress and drama? I'm sure you can get that for free in your real life.

Just keep in mind no matter what you do or who you are. There is always someone who's got a beef with you and what your doing and will always try to bring you down.

May not be a Murphy's Law type thing-maybe more of a Thyme's Law. Hold on to that so when you get stabbed in the back by a "close friend" it wont sting as much.
