Doctor Weapon Guide.
Hello everybody i am Julia.
I always wanted to write a guide for AO but never got chance to. Well I guess I am to late explain how to play doctor and so on so I will just write a guide about weapons for doctor. Something that I noticed been question number one on this board.

A little bit about weapons.
In AO everyone can use any weapon they want(exception profession specific). Market is full with weapons but choosing the right one is hard because not all of them are good. Task number one open your skill window and check what weapon skill is light blue. Yes that¡¦s correct only weapon skill that is blue is MA and Pistol. The rest are all dark blue. But It¡¦s not as dark as it seems to be. Playing with skills we actually finding out that some of those weapon skills can go just as high as MA and Pistol. Example is Shotgun, 1 Hand Blunt. piercing and 1 Hand Edged. Some skills can also be well trained but don¡¦t go to high, such as 2 hand blunt, 2 hand edged, grenade, sharp objects and MG/SMG. Few things you want to look at when you picking a weapon.

1) How good is weapon itself.
2) Is it possible to buff it.(wranglers don¡¦t count)
3) Do weapon implants conflict with nano implants?
4) Range
5) Does this weapon need special(rare) items for it to be good? (Example Kaheelar Sleeves, Butcheler Gloves, Low Light Targeting Scope)

I covered 1, 2 and 3 in following chart.
Body:
Martial Arts ¡V Fists do good damage, have 0 conflict with nano skills implants and also easy to buff with Martial Artist buffs such as Martial Arts Mastery or Four Fists of Kali(140 to MA buff)

Melee:
1 Hand Blunt - weapons are decent, clusters don¡¦t conflict with nano skills implants enforcers can buff them.
1 Hand Edged - cluster go to the same implants as 1 hand blunt thus have 0 conflict with nano skills. Only problem highest buff for 1 hand edged is expertise .
2 Hand Blunt - nice for atroxes implants also have conflict with our main nano skills and buffed by enforcers.
2 Hand Edged - Faded Cluster goes into waist and conflicts with Faded BM. No buffs for 2 hand edged. Weapons aren¡¦t nice.
Melee Energy - Shining conflicts with BM/PM.
Piercing - also no good weapons yet .

Misc:
Grenade - no grenade weapons that worse investments.
Heavy Weapons - Is one of equipment req for good 1 hand edged/2 hand edged swords. Conflicts with Bright PM/Intelligence.
Sharp Objects - nothing yet .

Ranged
Assault Rifle - Conflicts with Bright MC/TS and Faded MC/TS implants easy to buff by soldiers +60, and if you clan can raise that skill with Kaehler Uniform Sleeves ,
Bow - no good bows in the game.
MG/SMG - Only conflicts with Bright MC/TS, no buffs for it.
Pistol - Conflicts with Bright TS/MC and Faded MC/TS, soldier can buff it by +40.
Ranged Energy - Conflicts with Shining BM/PM, Bright PM/Int, soldiers buff it by 70.
Rifle - has 0 conflict with nano skill implants, agents can buff this skill by 55 and 120, soldiers can buff it by 50.
Shotgun - conflicts with Bright MC/TS faded BM no buffs for this weapon skill.
