Pet Pathing Confirmed by Dev Team as a high priority issue
Hi All,

Thought you'd like to know that the dev team has officially confirmed that the solution to pet pathing issues is High Priority.

I then asked which of the following "Pet Pathing Issues" this would refer to:
  • * basic pet pathing in general (including pre 14.0 issues like conference rooms)
    * specific pathing issues that the MP community has spent some considerable effort isolating (as in my pathing report)
    * issues that FC has isolated as coming from 14.0 and plan to fix (which is what our experience feels like but we can't be sure).
    * or all of the above


To which the answer was: All of the above.

So, the feedback is good. Pathing has high priority in the dev team right now and they're discussing all of the various sides to the issue.

Labrutte:

First off, given that there's never been an official acknowledgement of the pathing issues on this board (or any other) and that there has never been an acknowledgement that 'pet-pathing' is seen as several different issues which together make it difficult to control and move with pets.... I think that having a clear and definitive statement on both of these questions is very good.

Secondly as to trust... the issues that are acknowledged by FunCom will be tracked on status by the Professionals Team along with the Community team. The Professionals Team will also be going through a process of prioritising issues withe the community team, so we will have our own direct input into the prioritisation of issues and we will report back to the forum on ongoing status as we receive updates.

That's not to say of course that we get to decide the prioritisation ourselves, but that at least we get input into that process that we didn't have before. As a result, I think our chances of pathing being prioritised are better than they were before.

In honesty, from some of the things I've heard - Yes I do blieve that pet pathing has a very high priority with the design team.
