Res effect in pvp rebuttle
i have seen a few people now post on some boards here and elsewhere that they want no res effects after dieing from a pvp fight.

i am against this.

Camelot castle which contains tarasque , a unique mob, is in a 25%. often times omni and clan fight there with each other to see who will get to kill the mob and get his unique loots. in avalon there is an omni tek base. without res effects omni and clan can be like zerglings from starcraft and just rush each other after dieing. this would result in a neverending fight for taraque.

also dont forget fc wants to implement a way for clan and omni to take and lose outposts. if these outposts are in 25% then whichever side holds the outpost can never lose it by attacking right after spawning from death. if pvp has anything to do with gaining and losing outposts this would severly hinder that portion of gameplay.

I can't see any reason for ditching res effects even at lower levels let alone higher levels.
