14.5 Fixer Quest Spoiler inside !!
Go to the top of fixer Grid, you will find a Npc called "Gridman", have some talk with him then.

You : hello
Gridman : hello
You : Why are you here
Gridman : Being here - in this form, suits me far better than the outside world, since my...accident.
You : what accident ?
Gridman : (plenty of text...) If only i could get revenge on Jack.
You : Can i help you getting revenge ?
Gridman : (some text) You don't look like the ... type for it.

Then you got the mission.

Next is to go chop the head of Jack the LegChopper (like ninjas...)
you'll find him in Varmint wood at approx 3200x1400.

Good luck he seems tough...
