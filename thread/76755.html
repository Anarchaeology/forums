Trapped chests now give nearly as much xp for disarming as killing a mob. For example, in a QL105 mission the other day - the mobs were giving 8k-10k xp per kill, the traps were averaging 5k-8k ... It's a green skill for Fixers and we have good buffs for it too. Personally I think it's worth the investment.

Pick-a-Finger +16
CDS Gloves or Handy Steel Gloves ~+9 (varies on QL)
Stack The Odds +79 (or higher if you can run Karma)
Gen buff +20

All add to disarm - Right Hand Shining trap disarm is more useful than MA or Weapon Smithing and in the left hand I don't think I need anymore Fire AC's than I have already. On a QL150 set those two give +126 and it's all Agility based implants (sticking Cold AC into the left hand faded and SMG+Burst into the right hand).

Total of 250 not including trickle down (which will be quite high if you're Opi as it's Agil+Sense+Intel based - all of which you are maxing).
