first off........

LOL!

I don't even know where to begin...

MAs aren't uber, they're just a hella of alot better then many professions. Each profession has their own strengths and weaknesses, and many strengths overlap (ie damage, heals, nanos, etc).

All pet professions have one major advantage over the non pet professions: better ability to solo. While there are (many) issues with pet pathing atm, the pets function like mobs: high min damage, and very high hp. While a demon/slayerdriod/ninjadriod can never match a crit of say, a Krutt Waltzing Queen, pound for pound, and blow for blow, the demon/slayerdriod/ninjadriod will outdamage it over time.

Then the strengths of each of the pet professions is furthered by their unique abilites:

-MPs:Mulitple pets, mez pet to keep a mob distracted, heal pet, and of course, damage pet. Useful debuffs(except for Init debuff), nano shutdown useful on Docs with Complete heal.

-Crat:Calm.'nuff said

-Engies:Can heal their pets, via nanos as well as trimmers.

Yes, I know out of all the pet professions, the Engies probably got the worst of it. However, they can at least offer groups a useful service by warping fallen members back. That's more then many of the non pet professions can say.

Balance between the professions is impossible. Each profession has their own strengths and weaknesses. While some classes are far too underpowered right now, FC is stumbling the right way towards making things better.

Don't try to bring down another profession that's getting new content/bug fixes because you feel yours is/has been neglected too long. It's pretty petty.
