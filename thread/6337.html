RK1 and RK2, two completely different games.
I started playing on RK1 today for the first time since before 12.6

Up to now I had been playing on RK2. Here are some of my observations:

1. There is so many more people on RK1 its not even funny. On RK2 I could run around Athen for 10 minutes before I saw someone, on RK1 it was packed.

2. Shopbot. RK1 has it, RK2 doesnt. This is one of the coolest things Ive seen yet.

3. Anything I wanted I could find relatively quickly on RK1. There is so much activity on the shopping channels, that I was able to get everything I wanted without really trying. And being able to use Shopbot meant I could shop and do missions at the same time

4. Role-playing. I dont know what it is, but I saw a lot more people role-playing on RK1 than I did on Rk2. Could be cause there is so many more people there, but it still seemed like a lot of people role-playing. It was refreshing to see.

5. I got buffed by other players several times tonight... for free, without asking. That NEVER happens on RK2.

6. (not witnessed by me yet, but worth a mention) Events. RK2 is woefully neglected by Funcom. There is never any events there. I read about events on RK1 all the time.

Funcom, why dont you just put RK2 out of its misery? You guys dont pay any attention to it, and there is a sort of apathy among the players there. They cant help but feel neglected.

Plus the fact that there is so many more people on RK1, the folks on RK2 really miss out on a lot of the cooler aspects of the game. If you plan on keeping RK2 around, dont be surprised when almost everyone jumps over to RK1 when you allow them to change dimensions. RK2 really is in need of some attention Funcom.
