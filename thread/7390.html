Well, this is my opinion :

Enforcer - 90% use a hammer/beam. Boring. Slow. Not really damaging over lv 80 (when they can overequip into a ql200 mallet), and if they dont overequip the weapon they arent competitive. Unable to solo.

Soldier - same as above (but with Nova Flow)

Agent - Fun character to play (false profession), is agroed a lot due to high criticals (if you equip the right weapon). Can solo mission (sneak only).

Bureaucrat - Never have really given this class a try, definatly something on my todo list !!

Fixer - Can be fun is you like the speed. But other than that, i didnt see much use for this class. Needs some attention.

Engineer - HIGHLY gimped. Can only really do damage with a RED bot which requires other professions' buffs. Not easily soloable (if you mean soloable as in not using other professions to buff you).

Nano Technician - Ability to solo single reds (with use of root/nuke/root trick). This profession comes alive when in a team of 6 NTs all doing AOE nanos (Area Of Effect) in the middle of a huge green spawn - fun fun fun

Meta-P - Have you seen the mess with a team of 6 MPs??
!Lagville! MPs solo with ease. They can solo anything their pets can take out (send attacker in to kill with heal pet on it - easy). This is the LAZIEST of all the classes. Can solo missions with ease (except those damn missions with water/acid - pathfinding).

Adventurer - An interesting class. Able to solo easy mobs/missions, but not really dedicated for the tougher mobs. But hey, who cares when you can turn into a Dragon ! *dreams*

Trader - We come to the 'math' profession. Not only can these chars do HUGE crits with their Shotguns......they can also buff all the puny professions into their gimpy weapons. Prob same soloing as the adventurer, never had to solo with my trader.....

Martial Artist - This profession has got to be most insanely best profession for soloing. With evade/dodge, high acs and fully buffed with all the nice MA nanos - they can solo reds. By reds, i mean soloing NW mobs at lv 42 without healing. This profession rips through green mobs without even taking a scratch.

Doctor - Soloable if you use loads of ips in weapon skills All a doc needs is a DECENT team that looks after him/her. Stand back and heal....what else can i say....

As for which breed to choose, for any/all of the above I would choose Opifex. Purely because they can equip higher level implants much quicker than the other professions. Also if you look at the costing on ip for the attributes (Str,Agi,Sta,Int,Sen,Psy) Opifex are the best.

Dont shoot me ! This is just MY outlook. (BTW this is PvM not PvP opinion). If you feel different, dont biatch, just explain your opinion.
