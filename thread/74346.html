Mission
All around the lightning kicked up a thick fog of mustard sand. It stung my eyes, cut into my skin. On my arm tiny little nano-machines were frantically trying to seal the wound from a fresh implant, working like mad to keep the dust storm from getting in. I turned the dial on my booster up a bit, and pretended that the pain wasnt there. An eight foot green atrox named Sfrog was shuffling his feet making little circles in the dust. Beside him Nakoi, a crat, stood perfectly still, seemingly unaffected by the swirling shards of sand all around her. She was smiling.

Our great leader Amnra has requested that we take care of a little company business out here in the middle of this dust encrusted city she said. For emphasis, I assume, she removed her visor. Her eyes shone like crystal, piercing through the gloom. Ladies and gentleman we are not mere employees of this company, we are its elite. Its top ranking members. We are the ONLY ones capable of a task so dire

Her lips were moving with the mechanical grace of her profession. A trick, I knew, an illusion of nano-guile indicative to her trade. But it pumped my blood up nonetheless. I wasnt even hearing the words anymore, but I sensed this speech was winding down.

We know no fear, only pride, only devotion to the great cause that is handed down to us from the highest of authority. We will not fail.

Sfrog tapped the dust off of a ridiculously large iron beam and turned to the door beside him.

Wait!

Hayllo, our doctor, was standing behind the enormous green hulk, who up until this moment had completely hidden the young woman. She put a hand on his armor, and stood up on her toes to get close to his ear.

Slow down big guy. Fixers coming

As if on cue there was a flicker of blue light, and seemingly out of nowhere appeared Skeena.

Rdy. He said and smiled.

Buff up Hayllo said.

The air was instantly tangled with millions of nanobots. They twisted and swirled all around, tearing through the gloom. My skin went cool and tacky as they licked at the skin, the hair on my neck standing up at attention.

Rdy
Ready
go
Lets do this
Ready

The door beside us opened with a whisper, and we stepped into the darkness beyond it.

The heavy bulkhead slammed shut, causing a metallic thud to reverberate across the grey rock face of the mine entrance. As the echoes died down, night vision gear and floating torches were hastily deployed in response to the descending gloom. All was silent except for the steady hum of energy weapons, their lethal charge at the ready. Nakois ninjadroid whirred softly.

With a quick nod to the rest of the group, Sfrog slowly edged out of the shadows and crossed the threshold into the main chamber. Empty. Nothing moved at all except for a small contingent of Dengi spiders, which quickly scrabbled into the nearest crevice. The fickle glow of the omnipresent notum, cast eerie shadows off their slender carapaces.

With an abrupt wave of his bulging fist, the burly atrox bade his team to follow. Methodically, the team stalked from room to room, carefully checking corners and scanning blind spots for any sign of movement. Yet no resistance was met. Ever deeper they penetrated the vast complex of subterranean passages but not a sound did they discern.

Finally, Hayllo broke the unbearable tension, softly whispering, What happens if we cant see them?

Lanowel chuckled nervously, Thats not an option, Hayllo. According to Amnra, the cloaking technology could not possibly function in these caves given the large deposits of notum around here."

Without warning, the silence was breached by the sound of stones falling, not far behind. Nakoi pivoted deftly on one foot, her Khemo-Tech Model 200 levelled at the ready. Sfrog growled deeply, hoisting his beam, as Hayllo and Lanowel dropped back into casting position.

Wheres Skeena? hissed Hayllo, frantically searching the murky darkness. He better not have gridded out on us again!

Im sure hes close by, replied Lanowel, her eyes suddenly widening as three pairs of deep, soulless eyes leered out of the gloom to her immediate right.

The room instantly lit with brilliant, flickering yellow light.

Shade, Sfrog announced, with little enthusiasm, thought maybe this was going to be hard

The shade rocked in place, twitching, drooling, dreaming whatever dreams a mesmerized shade might dream.

There were three Lanowel said, I saw three sets of eyes, I know it.

Still no sign of Skeena Said Hayllo. She was apparently convinced now the fixer had taken the quick route to safety, and gridded.

Sfrog lifted his beam high above his tiny head and brought it down on top of the stupefied mutant. It made a crunching sound, followed by a squish, and the shade crumpled to the ground at the Atroxs feet.

Room Clear! He announced, and beamed a toothy grin.

Nakoi stepped to the center of the room and cleared her throat. Members of CCI!, this is a great victory for our established company. We are

Shhh.. Hayllo held up a hand and stopped the Crat, just as she was warming up I can hear them breathing

The group went silent, straining to make out the sounds around them.

I dont hear nuthin Sfrogs voice echoed down the corridors.

Lanowel took two steps back, and a shudder swam down her spine. Nakoi, do that Captivate the Crowd speech you do.

Boring. Its not one of my better..

Just do it!

Nakoi removed her visor again and stood straight. The speech was quick, executed with stunning grace. The effect, however, was terrifying. The team huddled together, bathed in the glare of green light that eminated from an invisible army all around them.

I cant see them! I can only see the effects of the speech! Nakoi, for the first time this evening looked rattled. Theres so many!

The green light faded, slowly dimming the room back to darkness.

Its a trap! shrieked Lanowel. Everyone fall back to the entrance! Nakoi, get that speech back up!

The team swiftly retraced their steps as Nakoi mouthed the words of leadership. A horde of greenish auras promptly materialized about the group, as if summoned from beyond.

Frantically, Sfrog led the team from one twisted passage to another. Doors and hallways flew by, dimly lit by the radiant host that purposefully stalked them through the gloom.

Is this the right way? shouted Hayllo, chancing a furtive glance behind her.

Dont think so, grunted Sfrog, stopping abruptly at the dead end before him.

The team promptly executed an about face as a dozen ethereal figures lurched into view. The shades murmured unintelligibly amongst one another, grating their misshapen claws against the rock face of the narrow passage.

This is bad, exclaimed Hayllo. Very bad.

Assist me, snarled Sfrog, launching his bulky frame at the nearest creature.

The beam hit home with bone-jarring force, producing a horrific eruption of tissue and bone. As it rebalanced for attack, the shade staggered under multiple rounds discharged from Nakois Khemo-Tech. Hayllo's Home Defender pressed the offensive, delivering a critical hit that gruesomely mangled the shade's left hand.

Howling in agony, the shade lunged at Sfrog in desperation. Its razor claws lashed out with blinding speed, tearing a sizeable chunk of flesh from the enforcers right arm. As Sfrog dropped to one knee, a radiant cloud of nanobots streamed towards the shade, emanating from Lanowels highly focused gesticulations.

The shade ceased its furious attack. It began to quiver, imperceptibly at first, and then with increasing agitation. Gaping holes emerged in its flesh as it swiftly dissolved under chemical corrosion from within. The shades eyes rolled back and it dropped to the ground, completely immobile.

Burn, baby, burn, snickered Lanowel, bracing against the heady rush of nano infused by her stimkit.

Three more luminescent outlines staggered into range.
