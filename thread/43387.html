i just visited one of my favorite ao related sites and came across something very pity:

quote:
Good bye, farewell.. it's come time for me to leave AO. My account expired today and I chose not to renew it anymore. The reasons for this are many.. but it's mostly because the game just isn't fun anymore, the content at high level is non-existant.. there is nothing left to do.

Friends made me stay in the game this long, even though I mostly faded away already during the last month.. logging on only once a week, if even that. I cancelled my account back in the end of May.. with over 2 months time left on it. I told myself that before it expires, Funcom will have fixed the game (at least somewhat), given us something to do, and changed some of their policies.. but alas, nothing positive has happened. The last static dungeon opened half a year ago. So.. it's come time for me to leave. I might be back when they've done something to the game.. maybe even next patch, if FC gets their act together.

Many of you wonder what will happen to this website.. and that is something I myself don't know about yet. It'll probably stay online, but if I don't / can't play at all I won't be very motivated to make any updates here. If it causes too much trouble I might have to put it offline.. that's quite unlikely, though.

++ Auno -- <auno@auno.org>
