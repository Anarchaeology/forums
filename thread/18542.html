Tour Guide
Take an adventurer and fill the need for a tour guide profession. Focus on run speed, defence and morphing nanos. Don't waste time with weapons. Point out the interesting sites and events, run from things that might hurt you. People don't see anough of the world and will gladly group with you to get to see all the cool sites. When you get the morphing nanos, slip away from the group, morph, come back and pretend to be an animatronic animal. Sing beloved children's songs. You will level to 200 in no time and soon be a legend on RK1. Good luck, and don't tell anyone you got this tip from me.
