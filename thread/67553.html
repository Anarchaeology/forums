The status of the game (re to "I'll leave because...)
Hi there!

Like so many of late, I found myself to be one of those who "only stays because of the guild", and since I've been unable to log on since the 14.61 patch (that is the game won't run), I've spent some time frustratingly trying to get it to work, some time trying to get help, and finally some time dreaming of other MMORPG's. It is my findings of the last bit I want to share, because I find my energy to keep with AO returning...

Wise of experience, I went straight to the forum of another sci-fi mmorpg to get the temp of the game and the devloper/community relationship.. and I found that "most" players where disgruntled with the game, and I think they even had better cause than most of us here at AO...

As an example, there's only one race/class combination "worth" playing in that game, because it excells in both trading, combat, equipment and just about anything else in the game, so why play the underdog, when you can start an über-character? (Except for the challenge, of course).

One post said something like: "you are obviously new to mmo's. You cant expect to be happy with a char 1, 2, 4 or even 6 months forward in time. Patches and tweaks will most likely force you to change character once or more during that time anyway." Well, I do not know about you guys, but when I invest months in a char, I don't want to find it useless after a patch. In that game they expect it, here in AO, at least it hasn't happened to everybody!(?)



My conclusion is that I will not make the change to another game, because they are likely to be just as broken or worse, and then I will lose the friends and hours I already have here at AO. The grass isn't always greener on the other side, sometimes maybe, but not as a rule.

So to all of you who go "fix x or I will quit and play y instead" -Don't, chances are good that you will be just as dissatisfied with "y".


I'm an optimist, and I belive that one of two things will happen in the future:
1 AO gets better for all
or
2 another mmorpg with a sci-fi setting makes it to the market which isn't riddled with bugs and/or broken and/or imbalanced etc.


Until that, I will do 1 of two things:
1 suffer as I have until now, and enjoy the chats and excursion with my guild friends.
or
2 quite mmorpg altogheter, because the industry really isn't mature enough.


Whatever you do or belive, do yourselves a favour and head in to your game of choice "y"'s forum and spend an hour or so and sift through "" posts as well as "" posts, and don't forget the developer posts. Combined you will get a picture that will help you make the right choice... for you...

I'm staying for a few months no matter what, since I forgot my recurring 6 month thing (or was it 180 days).

Good luck and have fun.

http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=67546
for details on my problem.)
