We made our own zoo...
The National Geographic Society of Rubi-Ka was newly formed comprised of LeeKuanYew, Rubberboy, SoulDaddy, Ohura and Keupon.

Our first Project was to create a ZOO in harry's bronto cage since there was no zoo in rubi-ka.

Many ppl (who found out from the OCC and Shopping channel) came to see the zoo and thought it was great. Unfortunately, at the end, the Hammer Broodling escaped - killing a few noobs.

The Zoo was destroyed by some useless omni scum - Racken, Sockan and Veslan.

Zoo
While I applaud the National Geographic Society of Rubi-Ka for trying to educate the population and hopefully in the future expanding our knowledge of the fauna and flora of Rubi-Ka, I can not condone the keeping in captivity the Bronto.
Historically there is one basic thing wrong with zoo's (or "prisons" as they were called on old Earth before the ban),the animals are often mistreated by the viewing public and suffer both mental a physical degradation due to the captivity.

Holding animals in an enclosure not suitable (especially dangerous breeds) also has it's own consequences to the public viewing the animals, as shown by the Hammer Broodling incident. I do appreciate that you have improved the location from its former use as a culling ground for the noble Bronto, however perhaps a fairground would have been better attraction and far less cruel.

The final destruction of the zoo shows just how pitiful the tyrant rule of OT is.

Support the BLA!
Stop the Bronto killing.
