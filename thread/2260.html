Assassination vs Kill Everything
Why in an assassination missions do you need to kill everything to 'finish the mission well'???

I thought the point of being a professional killer was that you kill only your intended target and no one else. You also don't get caught.

Any soldier can grab a machinegun and spray random crowds...I thought agents/assassins had more style.

Or at least they should.

I think we need to fix assassination type missions to KILL ONLY THE TARGET. That means you have to sneak around looking for your target, find a good spot and bam! Mission over + Token.

It would add much more flavor to being an agent. And it's also more role play too.

Maybe make it so you HAVE to be in conceal mode the entire time (which means no running around rooms and blitzing)

So what do you think?
