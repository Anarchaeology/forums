Sorry.

Fixers are - not - in the same buffing category as post 14.6 Adventurers, Enforcers, and Soldiers. By level 200, any fixer can self cast 100% of their nanos without rare items, and not even fully implanted for nano skills (can easily max implant SMG without losing a single nano).

Paired with the strongest single weapon skill buff in the game at level 195 (Frenzy of Shells +141 SMG and a lot of other adds for a mere 46 NCU) gives fixers a nice Attack Rating. Only a Martial Artist with Four Fists of Kali (+140) and Lesser Controlled Rage (+12) can get a higher primary weapon skill.

Advents cap at +87 Pistol or 1h Edged (+100 if they gimp their healing), Enforcers +100 (if they lose their best Challenger), and Soldiers +110/+120 (AR/RE). The high level Enforcers and Soldier nanos require an MP and/or Trader to cast in the current game.

Fixers should be happy they got their content before the move to make nanos under the new skill budget happened. You lucked out, and I'm happy for you. Too bad Enforcers and Soldiers didn't make the cut before the new policy was made, and that Adventurers got the transition nanos.

Here's hoping that this mess gets worked out by Shadowlands.
