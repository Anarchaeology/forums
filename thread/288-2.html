Well, my two cents
The 13.0 system is awful, in my opinion.

I think that before, when docs never got aggro'd doing team heals, it wasn't very realistic or fair. You wound up with docs that never wanted to target heal, because that added risk.

However, in the new system, the docs get aggro'd way too much!

The best solution would be if monsters aggro'd docs once in a while -- enough to keep the game interesting, but not pile on by the truckload.

For instance, on a pull of 6 critters, if one aggro'd the doc out of every few pulls, the doc could yell "on me!", and the soldier could break off and use his specials to blast the critter. That would add strategy and fun to the game. However, when 5 of the 6 critters hop over to the doc on every other pull, all it does is generate downtime and frustration.

One of my suggestions would be to make team heals non-aggro against level 50 and lower mobs, just to help out newer players. They have a hard enough time learning the game mechanics, and how to cooperate as a team anyhow. At that point, people are still learning how not to break roots/mez, what the agg/def bar does, and many other small things. Take it easy on them .

Incidentally, regarding Medusas: I thik that Medusas are balanced versus Cyborgs because they are in a 0% zone. Cyborgs are only harder in that they do somewhat more damage, but they also give more xp. Medusas are easier, but there is the risk of an opposing force out there hunting you instead of them.

Besides, Medusas are fun because there are all sorts of interesting critters nearby, like big snakes, giant lizzards, and all sorts of other cool stuff.
