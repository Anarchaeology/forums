Suggestions for the Towns Of Rubi-Ka Booster

Funcom is hard at work on their Towns of Rubi-Ka booster, a social only booster pack. It is rumored to have things like org housing and even ownable homes (kind of like towers), but nothing is offical so far. Post your suggestions for what you want to see in the ToRK social booster pack.


My suggestions:

Org Halls
Every org should be given a small hall by default (in the city of their choice). They must pay rent if they want a bigger hall or more then one (and if their are more then one, there should be some sort of whompa/grid like transport between all of them). Orgs can pay extra if they want their hall to include an arena, trade vendors, and if they want a different aestetic.

Bureau of Org Affairs
Each faction should have a Bureau of Org Affairs in its capitol city where players can go to read a list of all guilds currently recruiting and a brief description of it, along with contact information. Perhaps even a message board where orgs can post things like "Dark Angels is having a recruitment party Saturday at Reet Retreat." This would greatly simplify the process for those who are looking for an org. Also, the Bureau of Org Affairs would be where orgs go to upgrade their housing.

Player Owned Homes/Shops
Open new zones and allow players to settle in them. Players will be able to purchase homes and even stores. Homes come in different styles (i.e. you can buy a duplex with a friend, you can buy a mansion (for $$), or even a shack. You can buy a garden and other home accesories. The best part is you get to choose where you want to place your home.

Organizable Friends List
Make our friends list organizable by custom categories--i.e. you can put tradeskillers under one category, guildies under another, etc.

Rentable Nightclubs
Imagine if you could rent out a social nightclub for a day so you could throw an org private party? You get to choose who you let in and even what music is played (i.e. what repetitive techno sound file is looped ). There could be a few different nightclubs, each with a different ambiance, size, and cost. One could be a really expensive one that players rent out when they really want to impress.

Tradeskill Titles/RP Titles
Tradeskill titles given out by how much a person makes (items that require higher skills count more then making low level items). Possible titles include "Tinkerer, Dabbler, Crafter, Smith, Architect, Inventor." RP titles would be nice as well, though it's harder to implement these. Perhaps ARKs could give RP points whenever they see someone RPing. Once the player gets enough, he can choose a title from a list (so that the title remains appropriate to the type of character).

Customizable Pet Messages
Make it so you can create a file where you put messages for your pet to say when it performs commands like attack, hunt, guard, behind, etc. Each command pulls one response, chosen at random, from the list of replies. Ever want your pet to be funny? Quote from The Matrix? Quote Shakespeare? This is perfect for that.

Random Gifts
For 1 million a pop, you can buy unoppened gifts (like the ones the novelty sunglasses and beanie hat come in). When opened, the gifts give one social item at random, with some items being common (75% drop), some uncommon (20% drop), and some rare (5% drop).

Mission Locations
Change missions so that different locations have diffent chances of dropping diffent kinds of loot. (or something, anything that breaks us from our Broken Shores missioning rut).

Renameable Pet Leet or Reet
New pet stores open on Rubi-Ka offering leets and reets for sale. Players can own one at a time and name it. The pet will either say in their apartment or can follow them around, but besides those two things, it cannot be controlled.

List of Suggestions--No Explination Needed
  • holsterable/sheathable weapons
  • more music for nightclubs
  • more furniture and especially more art
  • make players names turn blue when /afk
  • customizable /afk messages
  • social clothes tab so you can look social without taking off your hard to get back on armor and weapons
  • make more doors in cities actually go somewhere
  • labels for backpacks & resizeable inventories
  • resizeable & transparent team window (I need the window open, but I don't need it taking up so much space
  • add more shortcuts per shortcut bar
  • tradeskiller's names get added to items they create (not the name, but to its info)
  • more yalm colors
  • name more items after players and orgs.


Please add your list of suggestions to this thread, and with any luck we can turn this into "The Official Towns of Rubi-Ka Booster Suggestions" thread and get it seen by developers.
