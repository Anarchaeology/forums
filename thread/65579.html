healsrus:

crat: pretty good pet, ok nukes, massive crowd control, some minor pet buffs

mp: best offense capabilities due to massive pet buffs (aenima/instill), heal pet, good nukes, broken mezz pet

engy: pet with most hp by far, ac and reflect shields

best bot damage trimmer adds like 80 dmage for 5 mins, best aenima adds 200sumthing for 40 secs and massive init buff ( dont know the exact numbers here, go check it )

pets in general cant handle adds, in fact, they usually cant even handle one target. ask your fellow engineer how often he gets agroed just entering LOS of the mob, sometimes not even that

the basic difference is the -intended- function of the pets,

crat: one of severeal damage sources, soak up the hits till the enemy is charmed/calmed

mp: damage !

engy: tank

due to the messed up agro system/AMS of the pets, the tanking usually doesnt work (thats why most engies cry so much, only thing they'r supposed to do in combat is broken..).

btw, i'v seen a demon beat a slayer with heal pet of course.
would be interesting how a ninja works out.

edit: a few notes: only the pet owner can buff a pet. there are a lot of different trimmers for different purposes, most arent worth the ip though. aenima/instill have a lot better effects than the trimmers, but a short duration.
