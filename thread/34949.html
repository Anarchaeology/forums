happy gas in cities takes care of crime, dark or not.

NV goggles do help with darkness, but I can take greenvision only so much.

The floating torches are a joke. They don't even illuminate players themselves all that well.

You can get your gamma up, but then it'll appear all washed out during day.

Putting streetlights in cities, where things are quite complex already, will only knock the framerate down more. AO engine is not really very good one. For one thing, I notice it first renders buildings further away, then ones up close, no occlusion culling. I have a GeForce2 Ultra card (my PC is almost 2 years old) , and my framerate in cities is sorta bad. I heard that GeForce3 cards hack it better, but a lot of people don't have GeForce2-level cards yet. If you add streetlights (forget about giving players flashlights, that'd kill even a GeForce4 4600), cities will get even slowers. However, lighting up vertexes around store doors should do the job without performance penalty.
