Reets Retreat, evening Nov 22nd

Vincent sat back and relaxed, enjoying the laid back atmosphere of the GSP party in the rumble room. Well, he tried to relax anyway. Too much had been happening lately, distracting him from his work. He tried to clear his mind of those thoughts, he needed a break...

The com unit came to life with a soft tone, indicating an incoming call. Vincent's HUD lit up revealing the details, an anonymous caller on a secure channel, the call made to Vincent's public ComID. His eyes followed the stream of data from the custom AutoTrace software as he listened to the caller speak.

The voice was devoid of life, computer rendered to foil any voice identification.
"Don't try and trace this, it wont work. I have precautions."

Apparently so, Vincent thought... the AutoTrace came up with a never-ending string of com mirrors, delaying the trace. It was a race against time, but the caller would probably be running some TraceDetector as well if he knew what he was doing, which would end the transmission automatically if his trace came to close.

"You could consider me a friend, sir" the voice continued.

"And who might you be?" Vincent replied, trying to buy time.

He scanned the room surreptitiously, trying to find the caller in the crowd, if he should happen to be in the room. His planted microcameras provided a view of the places his eyes couldn't reach. Suddenly he spotted a Solitus male talking in sync with the voice on the com. Vincent smiled softly.

"The Desert Winds are being watched" The man and the voice said, as one. "If I know more I will contact one of you."

"Watched? By who?" Vincent replied while running an ID scan on the man.

A name showed up on the scan, the man appeared to be a Clanner named Zane "Isco" McKechnie. Nobody Vincent had heard of.

"Who else, someone in Omni-Tek" the man replied, sounding a bit hurried. "That is all I have."

The call was disconnected, the trace proving futile as expected. Vincent made ready to follow the man, but thought better of it. It might be a trap. If it wasn't, the man could possibly be an asset, but not if he felt threatened. Besides, Vincent had identified the man. An extended background check might be in order before any decision was made on how to handle this.

Omni-Tek watching DW? Well Vincent didn't need to be told that... even before he joined, it had been readily apparent to him that this would happen. Still, having the information handed to him like this left him with an uneasy feeling.

The man identified as "Isco" slipped quietly out of the rumble room, and Vincent barely noticed. He was busy writing messages. A report containing the conversation went out to President McCovery. Any official action on the part of DW would be up to him. Unofficially... Vincent wrote a few more messages. So much for taking a break...

STATUS REPORT #5341

The Man Activated by Computer Hypopuncture-power (M.A.C.H.) project has been on going for almost a century now. The aim is to artificially activate a human using a process similar to Chinese Acupuncture. Almost one thousand needles are used to inject Hyper-Power Nanobots into the subject. A CPU implant helps the subject control the nanobots, this is a necessity otherwise the host is driven insane at best due to the untold power surging through his or her body. The subject has incredible strength and mental powers and is able to perform tasks beyond even the strongest Atrox or control nanoprograms with a skill even a Nanomage would envy.

However, the process is still experimental and has been applied to six subjects with varying degrees of success, the unsuccessful applications are too many to number, A seventh subject was installed with an experimental CPU termed Angela. Angela is a neural net processor and learns the subject helping him to control the power even more efficiently and more importantly mainitaing company loyalty. Subject Seven (M.A.C.H. 7) is an Opifex code- named Omegatalon, the presence of Angela has alleviated many of the earlier problems we have encountered and M.A.C.H. 7 entered service over two years ago. He performed admirably neutralizing several threats from around the base in Stret West Bank, one clan squad was destroyed with extreme prejudice.

In August of this year M.A.C.H. 7 turned rogue, reports indicate Angela was faulty and M.A.C.H. 7 was able to override her failsafe mechanisms that prevented him leaving the company or harming an Omni-Tek employee. We lost contact with M.A.C.H. 7 and sweeps by containment teams failed to find him. We suspect he headed to Borealis to join the clans. Units are being sent to that area to contain M.A.C.H. 7 and return him home.

An advanced version of Angela is being worked on, the mistakes of the first Angela CPU have been identified and rectified for future versions.
