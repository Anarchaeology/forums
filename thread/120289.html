Omni-Pol Science Departments Open Day
Greetings Citizens of Omni-Tek:

I am General Tikon Joor Of Omni-Pol's Medical Research, Trade & Engineering Department (MRT&E).

I would like to invite you, the citizens of Omni-Tek to our open day. This will give you an insight into what Omni-Pol does on a daily basis.

We will provide buffs free of charge, and we will have members of our recruitment department there also to answer any questions or queries you have if you have an interest in signing up to join Omni-Pol.

All citizens are welcome, and this Open day will run all day on Monday.

It will be held at Omni Entertainments South Park opposite Bronto Burger.

If you do have any queries or questions, please leave a message here and I will get back to you as soon as possible.



You have my thanks

General Hunter Tikon Joor
MRT&E Dept.
