Suggestions for next Expansion
I've been thinking, and sorry if anyone else has posted the same but HEY! They need to hear the AO community on this one.

1) Make new Races
2) Make new Professions
3) Make more Weapons (For every class)
4) Make more Armor
5) Make more Quests (A lot more)

With a more indepth add-on, they definately need to make a Nano-Cluster & Implant "lay out sheet" which would enable a person to add their Nano-Clusters & Implants on "lay out sheets" that way when and if they wanted to try a new combination they could take out all their Clusters & Implants together and keep them together. So later they could (If they wanted to) add them back onto the charactor all at once without having to deal with each one making it sometimes tedious.

But that lay out sheet thing was just a quick thought. Over all they need to add the numbered list into the game, this would bring people back into the game, bring new people in, and keep existing ones longer. I never really could think of why they didn't have more races, it's usually an appealing idea to have multiple races in an RPG.

POST MORE IDEA'S.
