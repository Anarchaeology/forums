Post for the AO Storlyine Movies!
Earlier today, Funcom released an official statement that Episode 4 was the last in this series and (correct me if I'm wrong) it was mentioned that both positive and negative feedback would be considered on whether future movies will be made...

Well here is my answer: Yes! Please continue with these movies! They give us a great third person perspective as to what is taking place in Rubi-Ka. I realize that this takes money & time = which are major resources, but I've found these stories to be quite entertaining and to give significant meaning to the game. It's very original and reminds me why I enjoyed "The Longest Journey" so much. It also reminds me why Anarchy On-Line stands out from the rest of the MMORPG games.

It would be a shame if this element was done away with since it is also giving us a greater perspective (ie, grandiose) of how our actions are related to an unfolding story which is leading us to something that no-one can quite anticipate. Thanks.
