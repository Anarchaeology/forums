hehe
hehe.. Oscar is a bit of a wild one..

Like we state on the site:

"Found in Greater Omni Forrest, just east/north-east of the mountainous region of the Omni Barracks area"

There are no real exact coords for these little ones - they're a real pain the buttocks to find in fact, especially since they do move around a LOT, and rather quickly as well. By no means are they standing still in one place like, say.. Trash King, hence the vague description. We havent been able to track oscar down to a specific spawn (which we pretty much have for the others)..

If you have the map of the area, you should have no trouble finding the mountainous region and after that, I'm sorry to say, you will have to tab like you've never tabbed before, running around, heh.
