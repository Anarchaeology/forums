I like the general idea, but want to play devil's advocate here for a moment to point a few potential problems with this and see what your response might be.

  • What if not all the guild members online wish to participate?
  • Have you thought of the technical consequences of summoning large numbers of people into a relatively small area not necessarily designed to accomodate them? (I.E. HUGE lag and/or server crashes. It is the nature of ALL true 3D games that the more people you have in a small area, the more lag that is created and the more server strain is created. This is a limitation in current 3D technology, not something specific to AO.)
  • I can see the potential for HUGE abuse of this nano. While it's true that teleport nanos already exist, they move (at MOST) only 6 people, not an entire guild which can consist of over 100 people.
