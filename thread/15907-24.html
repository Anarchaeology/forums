Over Equipping
Okay. I'd like to say thank you to Funcom, first of all, for making AO what we all hoped it would be in the beginning, and for continuously working with the player community on issues that relate so directly to our online alter-egos.
I think the patch is a wonderful addition, and have no problems with it, save one, which has doubtless been discussed many times. I felt I needed to reply, anyway-
The engineer. I have an engineer on Rubi-Ka, and love the class dearly, and am sincerely hoping that they get a fair shake in this whole business, which as of now does not seem to be doing so. I honestly don't understand why the pet has to go into a /pet behind stance once all enemies are done attacking it. This is potentially dangerous for engineers, and certainly not what I would call a "fair" way of dealing with the situation.
Think of our pets like our weapons, yes? Since that is essentially what they are, why would our weapons simply stop working, while a soldier's would continue to work, albeit at decreased efficiency? Why not simply decrease the efficiency of the pet? We don't need to have a larger OE range, but we do need to be able to survive in dangerous situations. If we lose that which is our salvation, you might as well, throw in the towel. One less engi alive in the land.
I mean, in the event of a trader debuff, an engineer would be rendered absolutely defenseless, giving the trader class a WIDELY unfair advantage, in solo, pvp, and team play. Is it really going to become necessary to carry a requisite trader around in my backpack at all times, in the case of severe debuffs that make me lose absolute control of that which my life depends on?
Being mainly a solo player, and truly enjoying that aspect of AO I might add, I enjoy getting the most out of my implants and bringing about the best robot possible by my OWN hand. I have never relied on trader buffs or MP buffs, nor do I ever feel I should have to.
At any rate, I simply think there is a better way, a more fair way, to take care of the OE with regards to the engineer specifically. Decreasing pet agressiveness, damage, AC, on a temporary basis until we can get our stats back up to par- these are all viable options, and ones that would not give engineers an unfair advantage over other classes, but make the OE patch even across the board.
I can only hope you consider the alternatives before making a solid decision.

Now, there is a new thread once again. But this time OE changes will not automatically mean that the mobs will be lowered in difficulty.

And that means that the past 3 months of the over-equipping saga have turned out to be smoke screen. Take a look at all those arguments being made by people who were for the OE patch. Their biggest argument was that if both over-equippers and the mobs are adjusted, then we will be back to square one.

Then I have to ask, why would anyone be for these changes in the first place. How precisely is this going to make things better for everyone in this game? How is it going to balance AO? Those who are not over-equipped now won't see a change after the patch. Those who are, however, will see everything they worked for turn to dust.

The IP reset is about giving back resources lost due to nerfs, isn't it? (see all those who put IP into dual wielding FAs only to see them removed from the game later). Who will refund the time and money spent by people who went out of their way to remain competitive with the changes made to the game play throughout game's history?

Even if Gaute stated that the changes are needed to bring balance to the PvP I will disagree. There is not much PvP in this game anymore, just go and visit 2ho at 8pm EST. I don't believe that there will be more PvP after the patch;

Forget about external buffs for a second. Forget about ransacks. After the patch it will be those who have the best implants who will have still prevail in PvP. Those who have the resources and friends (money for implants, and friends who can assemble them and cast buffs to put them in) will continue to have upper hand.
