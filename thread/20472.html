quote:

"One Question.
Why Can I kill a red demon easy and a Yellow Ball attack pet Is hard? WTF?"
Because the Enmity pets dual-wield and dish out a ton of damage quick. They wear down your hp's quickly to the point you have to respond to the damage rather than keep attacking. Because you have at best somewhere in the range of 4-6k hitpoints that's only about 10 to 12 swipes from a meatball. It can achieve those 12 swipes in about 15 seconds.

The Demon on the other hand trades off damage for hitpoints. If a Meatball has only 12K hitpoints, the Demon has well over 20K. But it attacks half as fast as the meatball. You have twice as much time to do damage to the Demon before you have to think about healing. Because of the way that player damage works, you could very well have racked up a couple crits and maybe even second specials on that Demon before you even need to think about healing.
