More Ma Answers from the team
Ok here you go guys more answers from the Designers.
This time we have Gaute....


Special attacks:

First of all, the special attacks are an additional damage output, on top of everything else. They are not like brawl or Burst, built into the system. They should be compared with Area Burst or Bow Special attack.

Only, they are much cooler than that. What I am pointing to, is of
course the damage balance output. Generally, yes, there will be more. I cannot say when or what, but we have the animations for 5-6 more ready, and we will see how the world looks after the 14.2 patch before we decide. I think it will be natural to see some for the Shadowlands, as the metaphysical aspect of that expansion in many ways goes well with the metaphysical aspect of the purity of the unarmed combat.

We will fix any bugs to the special attacks, of course. I don't think we will do a major change to the existing ones, except fixing bugs, but rather offer new ones through "quests" (I think that is the right word in this context) and upgrades.

2. MA special attacks?
They are such a good idea, stylish and cool, but we're not quite happy with them. Here's a rundown:
Attack of the Snake (AoS) has a high MA req and the QL200 version has a damage output of 1-500 = 1. It very very very often hits for 1 damage.


The AoS is the most primitive of all attacks. It recycles fast, and the ONLY way we can balance this with any variability of damage output is by making it possible to do virtual nothing. If we make it 100-400 instead, it will always do 100. I can make the recycle slower, and up the maximum, but would you like that?


Bird of Prey (BoP) has a low MA req and the QL200 version has a damage of 32-600 = 32. It very very often hits for 32 damage. I can't remember my BoP ever hitting above 300. The Stun effect every now and then is fine thou, but noone uses it once you get hold of a Blessed with Thunder (BwT).

Ok. It is not bugged, it is only not the best. That is fine with me.

Blessed with Thunder (BwT) works rather well, good damage output, reasonable recycle time (40 secs). Always hits for minimum damage as well.


The reason why it hits for minimum, its because they are used fighting red mobs. Used in solo against yellow mobs, they do fine damage. This is the same for weapons, Nano-Tech attack nanos and anything else with an attack range. It goes down, due to AC. More variability means longer recycle for balance...

Okay, these are the short recycle time MA spec attacks. People use these. On to the long recycle timer ones:


Bright, Blue, Cloudless Sky (BBCS) has a recycle time of 180 seconds. While the stun is nice and all, its damage output (450-650 = 450 at Ql200) is out of sync with the skill lock time. You can get off 4 BwT during that time, dealing appx. 10000 in damage with a QL200 version, and get a melee reflect as a snazzy bonus.

I agree, this looks underpower. I cannot promise when it should be changed, but I will look into it.


Angel of Night (AoN), very cool looking area attack, recycle time of 450 seconds. With a damage output of 250- 800 = 250 at QL200 it's useful for killing leets and bronto babies. Same thing as BBCS, too low damage output for it's skill lock time. That it locks opponents First Aid skill isn't really useful in either PvM or PvP.


It should not lock FA. Ill look into that.

Flower of Life (FoL) Ahh, sweet, wonderful FoL. If your lucky enough to have a QL60 or a QL200 version that is, everything else hurts you instead of healing you. This is a rather useful MA attack, the QL200 one has saved my butt a few times. Skill lock: 300 seconds thou. Lots of damage could be dealt in that time. Also, it's time to fix the interpolated versions (QL61-199) now.

I will fix the interpolated versions, those are a bug. It sounds like a code error, since the interpolated versions work fine.

Shen. (uh, Shen) The Big Badabom, right? Stun, powerful DoT, nano drain and utter coolness. Every 17 minutes. Ugh. Noone uses it except for PvP, it deals far far less damage then a BwT over time in PvM.

No, it was not supposed to deal more damage than BwT, it was a "put everything into one blossom", as the esoterics would say. You go for it, and it is a devastating PvP attack. (It is rumored to kill people several times in a row, due to a bug...)

As Id say, yes, ill look into them. But, id rather make new ones than change the existing ones, if you don't mind...


Gaute
