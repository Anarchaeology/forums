The Rubi-Ka Show
( This is the write up of an SRS roleplaying session. I this adventure the SRS are invited on a popular holovid chat show, unaware they are being set-up by their rivals, the Ancient House Eris. It has been edited a little and a short prologue and epilogue were added to make it a make it a better story. )



Percy lies sprawled over the sofa. "Bittsy?" he calls out loud. "Bittsy, where did you save that holovid?"

"What holovid, Percy-kins?"

"You know, the SRS interview one."

"Not that one again." Bittsy frowns.

"Oh, but I am so monumentally bored. You know that vid always cheers me up."

"Surely you know it all by heart by now?"

"Ill buy you dinner at the Canary if you play it just once more."

Bittsy sighs. "Just this once. But you better not expect me to sit it through it again."

* * * *

"We're back in the RKN studio Q with the Rubi-Ka Show and your host, the lovely, inimitable Heather..... Bradshaw!" The intro music plays, the computer generated audience claps and the camera pans to Heather Bradshaw and her guests.

Heather faces the camera directly. "Welcome back everyone. In part two of today's show we have some very special guests. They are Annmarie Posionivy007 McCullors, Major Tappert Slominski and Jillian Savoy Kaba from the South Rubi-Ka Society."

"Hello Heather, glad to be here." say Tappert.

"I am such a fan of your show." adds Ivy.

"And I am glad to have you here today. Now for anyone who's been living in Mort for the past year, who or what is the South Rubi-Ka Society?"

Savoy and Ivy both quickly look at Tappert. "Um, well Heather, " he starts, "simply put, we are just a group of private citizens from Rome and Omni 1 who want to give something back to the community. We all have our regular jobs, the Society is something we do in our free time."

"So, you raise money for charities, sponsor the odd cultural event, things like that?"

"Yes, that's the gist of it." he nods.

"Sounds to me like a cheap copy of the Ancient House Eris." remarks Heather.

"A what?" responds Savoy.

"Just kidding, sweetheart."

"Well actually" interjects Tappert, "the South Rubi-Ka Society has goes way back and in a way predates House Eris. Our roots are with the original Rubi-Ka Society, an organisation that brought aid, medical supplies and education to the poor, early mining towns across the planet. After the partition of Rubi-Ka, it became increasingly difficult for the Rubi-Ka Society to keep its neutrality. So eventually the Society broke up and its considerable assets were locked in a trust fund."

"So basically what you are saying is that the South Rubi-Ka Society was formed to get its greedy little paws on the trust fund money?" asks Heather.

"Now just on damn minute, Ms Bradshaw..." says Savoy.

"Call be Heather. Everybody does, sweetie."

"Now just on damn minute, Heather, "Savoy say pointedly. "The trust fund has some very specific rules governing how the money is spent and we adhere to these rule scrupulously. You can check our accounts anytime you want."

"Oh don't think that will be necessarily. I am sure you do marvellous work on behalf of the community. And besides, it's not my cookie jar you are plundering." Savoy gives Heather a nasty look, but Heather just smiles back. "Now if I may I would like to go back to the early days of the South Rubi-Ka Society. Is it true that Lev Rivers was one of your founding members."

"Erm..yes." replies Tappert.

"Lev Rivers of the Rivers gang?"

"Well yes, but he was different back then."

"And soon afterwards someone called Oshova joined in, as in the revolutionary, subversive cult leader."

"Well, yes. But it was before all mass suicides and anti-Omin demonstrations." says Savoy.

"I'll say. Still, you can't help wondering you will be the next Society member to go bad, can you?"

"That's ridiculous." replies Ivy.

"Is it? I'd say Savoy looks like a likely candidate. Is it not true that only a few months ago you served time in a Borealis jail?"

"It was just one night, Heather. I was out on bail the next morning. All charges were eventually dropped" replies Savoy firmly.

"Just goes to show what a good trust fund money can buy. Mind telling the audience what the charges were."

Savoy sighs. "Assault and battery."

"I don't think our viewers quite heard that, can you repeat it?"

"Assault and battery."

"You basically beat the living daylight out of one Borealis citizen, didn't you."

"It wasn't like that at all. That citizens was Rorybonez, a small bit crook, blackmailer and leet fight organiser."

" Oh I see, so the you ended up in jail to defend poor, cuddly leets?"

"Hmmm.. not exactly."

"Was it not more to do with... this calendar?" Heather pulls out a copy of next year's World Leet Fighting Federation nudie calendar. "I'm afraid we can't show this on the air, but if I were to look up June who do you think I'd see posing in her birthday suit?"

"Give me that thing!" says Savoy. She grabs the calendar out of Heather's hands and rips it in two.

"Tell me Savoy, how does nude modelling mix with you charity work? Do you strip at your fundraiser? I'll bet you that will raises something all right."

"That's it!" exclaims Savoy as she storms out.

Ivy shouts "That picture was taken with out Savoy's consent or knowledge." Her voice is lost in the chaos though as Tappert ,jumps to his feet, overturns the coffee table and somehow or other ends up with his hands around Heather's throat.

Abruptly the picture changes to a Mongo Meat commercial.

* * * *

Percy claps his hand in delight. "Ah Bittsy, you've missed the best part. It's absolutely priceless."

Bittsy emerges out of her room, all dressed up and ready to go out. Absently she asks. "So Percy-kins, are we going to approve Heather's membership to House Eris?"

Percy looks pensive. "You know Bittsy, all things considered, I should like to leave Ms Bradshaw dangling a little longer."

I don't know how much I can add to the story of the Oshova. There are not doubt other people better placed to tell his story.

Yes he was breifly a member of the Society, right back when we started. He used to claim that Rubi-Ka talked to him, and I don't mean in the metaphorical sense. Still, he seemed harmless enough.

Shortly after he left the SRS to form his own underground cult. His manifesto seemed to have changed from the purely mystical to the political as he became an out spoken advocate of the overthrow of Omni-Tek. He claimed to have vast armies reach to march on Omni 1, though I doubt anyone ever saw them. The last I heard of him he was trying to arrange a mass protest suicide in Omni Ent.

On balance I'd guess I'd say he was an oddly charismatic but totally derranged.
