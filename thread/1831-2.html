I Never Really Thought Over-Equipping Was THAT Big a Problem
I never thought over-equipping was that big a problem. Granted, I stay the heck out of zones where the suppression gas level is below 75%, but if for story reasons I jump into a PvP zone, it'll be with the full awareness that I'm disposable cannon-fodder. (I don't go in for mega-combat characters.)

So if that doesn't disqualify me from having an opinion, let me make a suggestion that's completely out of left field:

First of all, buffing your skills with your own buffs is hardly an exploit; that's what those nanoformulae are there for. Same thing with implants, with one caveat: while I don't really care much, I can see where an implant that requires a certain strength, intelligence, or whatever to implant would continue to require that skill to work. (Implants should NOT fail when Treatment drops too low. And surgery kits should go back to temporarily transferring Treatment. If doing surgery other people can't do isn't what doctors DO, why are they called doctors?)

Which leaves us with equipment you can only equip because someone much higher than you lent you the abilities and skills to do so, and that's what most people are complaining about.

What's wrong with rewarding being popular or being in organizations or sucking up in a role-playing game with such an overtly political theme?

But fine, if it bugs that many people, let's fix it. Let's start with this idea: give people a strong reason to occasionally de-equip their items, and have to re-equip them. How are we going to do that? Well, how about this: create another, even higher suppression gas level. At SG 125%, any weapon you're carrying in your hand(s) de-equips, and you can't equip a lesson until you zone back into an area that's at 100% or below.

See, it's always bothered me that even in such obviously social environments as The Cup or Baboons, people are running around carrying guns instead of drinks. Once they open up the City Hall(s), that should apply there, too. Even on a frontier world like Rubi-Ka, it just doesn't feel right otherwise. So set up things so that in areas where story logic dictates that openly displayed weaponry would be unwelcome, you can't carry in or equip a weapon in your hands, only in your Personal Containment Field or your backpack. Then, create reasons for people to want to go there -- say, uber mission terminals, or special stores, or easier grid points, or whatever.

By the way, someone's going to point out that if you de-equip an item and inventory is full, it disappears. This is, as far as I'm concerned a bug, and should be fixed as follows: if a de-equipped item can't fit in inventory, it should drop to the ground, not disappear. Period. No matter why it gets de-equipped.

If you do this, then sure, people will still be able to get themselves super-buffed to carry ridiculous weapons or wear ridiculous armor -- but they'll have to KEEP doing it, which will make that favor harder to come by.
