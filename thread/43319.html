Stance - Pacific, Indifferent, and Bellicose
First off, I realize that my idea is somewhat far-fetched, and something for shadowlands at the soonest.
The Background:
I purchased Neverwinter Nights a few weeks back, and believe I've played the bejeezums out of it for now (not many good mods out yet). There was something in there that made me think heavily about AO, and that is the fact that there are two sets of alignments. Good, Neutral, and Evil (Clan, Neutral, and Omni), as well as Lawful, Neutral, and Chaotic. I believe the 2nd set of alignments, which I will call Stance, can be implemented into AO. Here are my suggestions:

At level 75, when the player is ready to step into the cutthroat world of L200 ganking, camelot, and mayhem zones, he will choose his stance - Pacific, Indifferent, and Bellicose. Your stance will have substantial weight on your involvement in politics (should they ever evolve) and your ability to PvP. The pros and cons for the stances are thus:

Pacific
RP Description:
Omni: You realize that Omni-Tek is superior, and see the clans as simply being equivalent to adolescents - breaking away from their parents in order to form a sense of identity and uniqueness, but in the end conforming to their lifestyle. You believe it is Omni-Tek's responsibility to coax the clans back into order.
Neutral: You actively hope for an equilibrium to be established between Omni-Tek and the clans which will be beneficial to both sides, and the traders caught in between. You are more than willing to act as a middleman for any negotiations between the Omnis and the clans.
Clans: You see Omni-Tek as the evil corporation that it is, and are trying to help it reform itself into a more humane company. You hope to one day have a free democracy on Rubi-Ka, where Omni-Tek is allowed to exist, but not as a governing force, instead merely a standard corporation.
Pros/Cons:
Unable to initiate PvP combat in 25% or 0% zones... can fight back if attacked though.
Not fired upon by enemy guards in any situation
Unable to attack ANY guards
10% additional xp gain in 75% zones. 20% additional xp gain in Political zones. 30% additional xp gain in mayhem zones. This is to make up for the obvious risk.
Able to trade in opposite alignment's shops.
Unable to be tab-targetted, as they are by default "Hostile" to no one.

Indifferent
RP Description:
Omni: You believe it is not your problem what the higher-ups in your corporation do, your task is to file paperwork or work in the mines or manufacture weapons. Leave it to the people in charge to make the decisions. You hope that Omni-Tek will prevail in the end, but will do nothing out of the ordinary to aid or prevent it. Maybe the Council will think of a solution first... that'd be good too. You will try to avoid combat, but won't back down from a fight if necessary.
Neutral: The Third Civil War is just another hindrance to free trade and information passage. But on the other hand, you do make a lot of money selling weapons of war to both sides. You hold no favor to either faction, and are content with simply remaining in the exact middle. It's someone else's job to make waves.
Clan: The Omnis just need to back down. If they'd be quiet for a few hundred years, we could get a good bit of notum shipped offworld, make our money, and be done with it. The blasted Council's not helping either, trying so hard not to offend anyone. You believe someone needs to think of a solution fast or you might just up and leave the planet.
Pros/Cons
Unable to initiate PvP combat in 25% zones, normal combat applies in 0% zones.
Fired upon by enemy guards if there is a Bellicose person of your alignment in the guard's LOS as well.
Able to attack opposing faction's guards, but not neutral guards. 0% xp gain in 75% and zones, 10% gain in 25% and 0% zones.
Able to trade in opposing faction's shops.
Indifferent Neutrals cannot be tab-targetted. Clan/Omni Indifferents can be tab-targetted by members of the opposing faction.

Bellicose
RP Description:
Omni: You believe the clans will never learn, but you're sure as hell gonna try and make them. Omni payed trillions of credits for the lease of this planet, and you're not gonna just let some dirty clanners take it for free. Kill them all a thousand times over.
Neutral: You're making a lot of money from this war. If the pay is right, you'll just as soon kill an Omni as a clanner. It sure beats selling bronto burgers for a living. You don't want to take sides for fear of being killed by the other one, but you're definitely pro-war.
Clan: The Council is a bunch of tree-huggers. This "Let's talk and let the immoral, unethical Omnis who let over 70 atroxes die in their mines tell their side of the story" crap is not going anywhere. Let God judge the Omnis. Your job is to arrange the meeting.
Pros/Cons:
Able to initiate PvP combat in all PvP areas
Fired upon by enemy guards no matter what
Able to attack all faction's guards
0% xp gain in 75% and 25% zones, 10% xp gain in 0% zones.
Unable to trade in opposing faction's shops.

Now here's the clencher: The rating must be dynamic, as well as have an effect on PvP points. If a pacific kills anyone (even though it will obviously be in self-defense), they move x number of points towards indifferent. If an indifferent kills anyone, or the opposing faction's guards, they move x number of points towards bellicose. If a bellicose kills a pacific, they LOSE PvP points. If a bellicose kills an indifferent, their points do not change. Thus, title-seeking bellicose people can only kill other title-seeking bellicose people to get the title they seek If an indifferent kills a pacific, they lose PvP points. If an indifferent kills another indifferent, the gain normal PvP points. If an indifferent kills a bellicose, they gain DOUBLE PvP points. A pacific can never gain any PvP title points ("Yeah I've killed hundreds of people as evidenced by my title, but I really don't support violence"). I have discussed how to move towards bellicose, but how do you move back towards pacific? You will first, of course, have to work through neutrals to get from bellicose to indifferent. This is done through a simple concept, as well as money sink: Giving money to a beggar. There will be a beggar roaming the streets of Borealis, Sleeping on a bench in Rome Red, and living under the toxic waste bridge in Tir. It is a nice gesture, and will of course move you up in the "niceness" scale. The amount required to give in order to change points will depend on title level. 50k per point at title 3, let's say. 100k per point at title 4, 250k at title 5, and 1 mil at title 6. A clan/omni must give to the neutral beggar to reach indifferent, and then to the other faction's beggar to reach pacific. A neutral can give to whichever beggar he chooses. One can also allow to rack up stance points in missions using the *TA-DA* Good/bad slider! You must receive a token though in order to gain/lose the points, so no blitzing for your stance. Your stance will be listed under your faction (i.e. Name, Race, Gender, Profession, Faction, Stance), so all can see it clearly.

One's stance can also be used as a gauge to which static missions he can receive (once they get more in), and perhaps even which events he is able to attend, seeing as how the new Omni-HQ, CoT, and ICC buildings are on the way, you could restrict access to only a certain stance perhaps. No trouble-makers in the peace meetings. No do-gooders in the war meetings.

Them's my ideas. Bump 'em .
