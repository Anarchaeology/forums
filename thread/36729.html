New Items -- Boss Loot Only --- Wrong
Hello all,

I have been thinking about this and have come to some conclusions about this.

1. It is wrong.

2. It _forces_ team play on us.

3. It does not account for every players available play time.

Explaination. Taking myself as an example. Most days during the week I am lucky to get more than 90 minutes of play time a day. That means team mission are out. From experience, it take 15 ro 30 minutes to find a "good" team, get a mission, and travel to the mission. Then figure two hours to do the mission. That is if it is short. Had one recently on a _rare_ power gaming season that took a full team 4 hours to finish.

Again using myself as an example, I get maybe one or two long play sessions a month. And by long I am talking 4 to 5 hours. Not all day events like other players do.

So FC is making all new items Boss Loot Only. This has just ruined any chance I have of getting these items. FC says you can buy them from other players. Cosmik, Cz you both need to sit and watch the sell channels and see just how high the prices on these new items are. I have seen MA suits under ql100 go for over 2 million. Have seen a auction for a ql200 one go as high as 25 million and I realized I had better things to do so I went and did a mission. So I figure that suit went higher. Ok back on track.

In my view which is shaped by my game play and interaction with others I see this Boss Loot Only policy as being counter productive to casual gamer like myself. I cant do team missions often, I cant afford the outrageous prices these items are sold for as I cant earn that much cash quickly with my limited play time.

Now my get on the soap box. As a player I am paying for a product. That product has changed in a way that makes the product no longer viable. I am being told by the maker that I _now_ have to conform to _their_ vision_ of how the product is to be used. Yet they have left the old method of use ( solo missions ) in place. So the maker is actually offering a product that can be used two ways. They however are saying they will only support one of the ways. Both ways are legal, and viable.
Now if the new method of use was part of an expansion that I had to buy seperate then I could understand why that was the supported method of use within that expansion. However the maker is still responsible for supporting the old method for those that did not expand. I think most of you see my point. FC has basicly stopped supporting solo missions as a viable method of attaining the new items. This makes solo mission unviable as a player can not achieve all possiblities without conforming to the new method of play.

If missions where meant to be a team experience then take all SOLO missions out of the game with the exception of the training area. Theses training solo missions would then be seen as prep work for real missions.

So if FC would actually play the game on the live server. Monitor the Trade channels, and do a in game poll of players to determing how much time they have to play a day then maybe they could understand why Boss Loot Only is a wrong policy.

A possible solution. Make solo mission have a Boss. Yes this is a good idea. You could complete the mission without killing the Boss but you can not get the Boss Loot Only items. The difference here being that the mission object be in a different room than the Boss. Make the Boss an addition to the solo missions but not a requirement. You could even keep the current Boss modifer scheme in place. Just toned down a little in requards to AC and HP. Make them killable by a solo but still a long hard fight. This would keep people from getting outrageously high level new items from solo missions. Yet still allow them to get the new items with out teaming.

And to make this solution even more interesting. Make the existience of a Boss in the solo mission be a random chance. Say 3 out of 5 solo missions have a Boss.

This may not be an easy fix but it is viable and added a new level to the boring routine of mission, mission, etc..

I invite comments on this problem and my solution. I am sure that as a player base we can find a viable and realistic method to use as an alternitive to the current method.

Because there are people who can play all day, seven days a week, it is those people that they look to when deciding how hard things should be to get. The funny thing about this outlook is that by their very nature, hard core gamers will never find anything that the gaming company implements...hard. So Funcom will continue to try to curb the hard core gamer from having it too easy, the hard core gamer will continue to not even notice and still have it easy, and the casual gamer will more and more suffer.
