quote:
Originally posted by Xed LRK


The question does occur: What is the purpose of the game?
I mean, I don't mean to whine or anything but the purpose seems to get as high a lvl as possible (which, with the Armour/QL tab idea - would be made much easier).
Oh, and 2nd purpose, to kill as much as possible...
Yup, that sounds like many players purpose for the game for sure. But the reason the game exists in the first place and what will keep it around, isn't rushing everyone to the highest levels or giving them nothing to do along the way. The underlying purpose is for the game to make as much money as possible for as long a time as possible. What many players fail to understand, is that you reaching for your goals in game, is what keeps most of you helping the game companies meet their goals.

As an example, lets use that "one last piece to complete the set" scenerio.

Example "A" (buy it in store easilly): You know what QL you can wear now so you go to the store, buy the whole set and you are done.

End result: You have new armor with minimal muss and fuss.


Example "B" (the current system): You search the stores with no luck, you listen to the auction channel for a while with no luck, you do the same for a day or two and end up finding it in a mission terminal. You put together a team, you go out, you finally complete the mission and you have a full set of armor now.

End result: You have the armor set, you are elated, relieved or whatever other emotions are running high about it. You spent a couple days working at making this happen and basically, for those couple days you actually had a point for being in Rubi-Ka. Funcom also managed to keep you playing for a couple days as opposed to a couple minutes...thus, they achieved their goal as well.



I suppose it could be argued to a point that you have other things to do in the game world, but in reality, this sort of thing makes up a large portion of the time you actually spend in-game, and without it your playtime would soon become very empty. Anyways, it is one of the basic premises of online games and one of their most addictive "features". Look at EQ for example, your entire existance in Verants world revolves around you obtaining the next better item. People spend months going on 8 hour long raids for the "chance" at getting their turn for a single item. Personally, I think this system as is, is a vast improvement over that one by far.


Edit: Personally I find a pure exp grind with no point all the time to be pretty boring. I prefer to have a reason for doing something other than just leveling. knowing that at the end of a mission I will obtain something I want/need, or knowing that the boss may drop a rare piece of loot, is enough to make it interesting for me..and I believe many others as well. Once I have the best gear for my level, it becomes a boring, monotonus grind to get to the next point where I need to updrade again.
