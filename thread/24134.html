Well. There are some new dungeons added after halloween...there is the Tir borg dungeon, The Longest Road dungeon and Camelot Castle.

Some new items are also added, among them a special tank armor for docs that apparently spawn randomly in Camelot Castle dungeon(s)

Team missions are implemented. They are harder than regular missions of same level, has multiple floors/levels and an end boss with a couple of henchmen. MOB's add like mad in these missions too. So doc's are popular in team missions

There are events going on now and then...everything from the large, well advertized zone crashing events to smaller events or story persons walking around. Keep an eye out for people with green names and no "official support ARK/GM" look.

I think the game is steadily improving, but as always there will be no shortage of people on these boards telling you otherwise
