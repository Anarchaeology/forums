I'm bored! FC, you must help me!
I've been playing AO for a few months though and I am starting to loose interest in it. I need more of an incentive to level up. All I've heard about is how much high levels suck becuase there is nothing to do. If FC wants to keep a solid player base they should put some more focus into the high level game. I want to see bosses that you need 30+ people to take out. I want to PvP with mass amounts of people, so laggy and hectic by the time you figure out what your doing your screens faded to white! I mean really, what do I have to look forward to? A couple new places to hunt? The ability to kill guards? If thats all then I'll be gone faster then I came. Please ANYONE come up with some ideas for the high level game. Level 100+ basically. Becuase leveling is one hell of a tedious task that gets old very fast, especially with me and the other folks with A.D.D.
