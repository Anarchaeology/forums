81. The comp lit requirement to use the Tir grid access terminal is 81. It's only 76 to get out at Omni Trade. Stratics has a really useful Guide to the Grid. I copied and pasted it into Excel and printed it out, it's that useful.

The only other alternative is to walk. From Tir, take the whompa to Old Athen. Go west out of Old Athen towards West Athen, but instead of going into town, turn south and follow the road. When you get to 3HO, take the southwest fork towards 4 Holes. When you get to 4 Holes, you can whompa to Omni Entertainment District.

If you're a low level enough character that you don't have comp lit 81, you'll probably never make it. You'll get maybe halfway from West Athen to 3HO before you get slaughtered and eaten, probably by rollerrats. But in theory, it could be done.

(My highest level character is around level 40 now ... and still, if it ain't on the whompa network or near a medium-level or lower grid point, I don't go there. Resurrection shock gives me headaches.)

There is a new alternative route.

From Tir, Whompa to Newland, and then to Borealis and Stret Wast. (The whompas are all connected.) In Stret West there is a ferry along the coast that will take you to Stret East in the 2HO area. All you then have to do is find the Whompa, and you're in Omni-Entertainment. Warning: 2HO is a highly contested political zone. The current truce makes fighting frowned upon, but that certainly doesn't stop rouge clans and rouge Omni departments from duking it out wherever covenient, and they certainly don't bother to make any disctinctions between "the enemy" and "innocent bystanders." Be preared for a fight in 2HO. Most of the time the route should be fairly safe, but the possibility of being murdered in 2HO is always there.

By far the quickest and safest route for anyone with 81 or better comp-lit is the grid. The Tir grid entrance is ground floor and requires better than 80 comp-lit. The Omni-Trade exit requires better than 75 and is also ground floor. Take the convenient Whompa from Omni-Trade to Omni-Ent if you don't yet qualify for the 1st tier Omni-Entertainment exit directly.

Directions
I recently made it from Omni to Newland with my lvl 11 Engineer.


Here's an easy route from Borealis to Omni-

Go through the valley out of Borealis, leading to Holes in the Wall. Go North following the paths(right at the bronto, left at the hill) eventually you'll reach a bridge, keep heading north.

When you reach the stret river go as far West as possible, eventually you'll hit the edge of the map, cross there(beware Pirhana's) and "Stay near that edge"!! eventually you change zones and it becomes a zone border, giving you an easy way out incase you get attacked. The plains dividing the river & 4 holes are full of nasties(Tickler Scorpids, Vulture Squabblers) so hang near the small streams that lie to the west(you'll see them eventually) the monsters there don't seem to be too aggressive..

My directions might be alittle out of whack(seeing as I traced backwards, and I have no compass available). But the route works for low lvl's.

Btw- The Whompa to Omni in located in the south-west camp of 4 holes.
