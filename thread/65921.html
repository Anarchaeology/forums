I'm all for encouraging people to get out and explore other possibilities; forcing them to do so is kinda heavy handed in my view.

I do think your basic idea is sound though. There are tons of interesting things you could do to make missions more varied and fun. One of my favorites is to make missions more realistic, with fewer, more intelligent Mobs, and active defense systems that need to be navigated around or disabled. Similar to something like a mini Rogue Spear or Counter Strike mission for teams, or a Deux Ex style mission for single player. You'd need to add a bunch of other attacks and disabling abilities though, so your suggestion is undoubtedly a more workable one, and a step in the right direction.
