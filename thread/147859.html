Thanks for all the support everyone. It really does mean a lot to me.

I've spoken to the last remaining active members of HH about if we should just put an end to everything, but it has been decided we would give it another couple weeks. In the meantime, HH will be altering its purpose slightly. Instead of focusing only on aiding all of RK, we will be seeking out young doctors to train up in the profession with the knowledge that, once they are fully trained, are free with no obligation to choose their path if they feel it is apart from HH. Of course, we have always allowed this if it was what the person wanted, but now we're just making it official that we're a aiding/training guild.

So, with luck, we can gather some life into HH and revive its old corpse to reclaim. Thank you again for your support and all of your kind words.
