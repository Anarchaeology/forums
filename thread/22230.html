It is done!
Well I finally decided to put OTEC back together last night. I need to get all the Org stuff sorted out over the weekend but I though I'd release the details now.


OTEC is back in business.
While no hard rules have been made yet if the guild is for Engineers only it is certainly a Engineer biased guild with a focus on tradeskill and Roleplay (although neither of these will be enforced).

Minimum entry level is Level 6 but further recruiting details have yet to be decided. I have decided to lower the starting level since I believe that it is the lower level engineers who would really benefit from a guild such as this.

If you are a former OTEC member and have been wandering around in a bit of a daze when the guild fell out from under your feet give Technoman a tell and well see if we can work something out.

Any non-OTEC members who are interested are also encouraged to send Technoman a tell.

While the guild is still small and no hard recruitment policies exist I'm reluctant to 'throw the doors open' since I would rather have a small but dedicated membership to lots of numbers on a page :-)

NB
Its going to take a while to get things sorted since I can only really play at the weekends - GMT) so please dont expect miracles overnight. Also note that a guild is only as good as its members so your input is not only welcomed but wanted.
