Top Ten Soldier Questions and Answers
1.)Fixing the issues with Total Mirror Shield(Referred to as MK or TMS),
these include but are not limited too: Not being able to switch weapons for 20 seconds after the nano has been cast Lower level deflection shields blocking a lower QL TMS being cast over the Top Will any of the above be looked into and possibly changed?


Blackmane:
No. The recharge time on the TMS nanos is 20sec. That isn't going to be lowered.
TMS is very powerful, and this is one of the drawbacks in using it. The stacking isn't
going to be changed either. Stacking is based on 2 things - the 'line' and the NCU cost of the nano. The TMS are in the same line as the other deflection shield nanos (this is not going to change), which means that you will have to remove any normal deflection shield you have running if you want to use a TMS with a lower NCU cost. I could raise the NCU costs of the TMS nanos so they are higher than the highest normal deflection shield (58 NCUs), but I don't think soldiers want me to do that




2.) How do you view the role of the soldier, where is it now and where is it
going in the next 6 months?

Dai-Galean
Answer to this will follow (Just waiting for it from the team)

3.) Added buffs relating to other Soldier skills such as: Heavy Weapons,
Submachine Gun, Full Auto... is there any chance of such buffs being
introduced in game? Does funcom have plans for any new soldier nanos in the
future?

Blackmane:
New buffs are always a possibility, but there are no concrete plans right now.



4.) Burst attack, which was nerfed sometime ago due to PvP complaints and
now does less damage then a flingshot. Will there be any changes to burst
made for soldiers?

Jim Salabim
We do not currently plan to add any special abilities for Soldiers tied up to the Burst skill.

5.) Right now the most popular soldier item is the Frank Khaeler Sleeves,
which adds to the Assault Rifle and Full Auto skills. (Only method of
boosting FA) Many Omni soldiers have turned Clan just so they can equip these. Do you have any plans for Omni soldier items and soldier items in general?


Jim Salabim
We constantly add new items. Many of them are made based on player feedback. Your concerns regarding items buffing Full Auto and other Soldier skills have been noted.




6.) Currently the 3rd highest QL soldier HP buff gives more then the highest
and second highest QL HP buffs; this is obviously a bug. Is there any chance
of this being looked into and corrected?

BlackMane
There is a bug with Titan Physique where it gives more HP than normal. It should be
giving +289. The other two nanos above this are not going to be increased in their
effectiveness because of this, but I don't think that I'll nerf titan physique either. Option 3 looks the way to go - grandfather the existing TP and making a new copy with the correct stats. But we will need to discuss this - might be that TP just gets nerfed.



7.) One major complaint from soldiers has been the lack of VIABLE weaponry,
are there plans for more weapons based on Assault rifle in upcoming patches?


Jim Salabim:
This far we've seen few Soldiers complaining about their weaponry. 14.2 seems it might change this, and we will likely be making upgraded Assault Rifles based on the feedback from this patch.



8.) Certain soldier buffs are considered grossly underpowered for the skills
needed to use them. The Armor Class boosting nano "Phalanx" boosts ACs by
+60 for the level this nano is and the requirements it holds, it must be
clearly bugged. Also the nano "Automatic Targeting" only raises attack by 23
yet takes up 33 NCU. Will Funcom be looking into this and possibly fixing
these nano's to a more useful state?


Blackmane:
Phalanx is changed in 14.4 and is quite useful now in its new state. There are no
current plans to look at automatic targetting.







9.) Soldiers have a green ground vehicle skill, unfortunately even a low
level QL 30 Yalmaha is better then any ground vehicle we can use. Will
funcom be introducing more useful vehicles, ones that would actualy give an
advantage to raising ground vehicle skill and allow for diversity of
vehicles.

Jim Salabim
Yes, we do plan to make new vehicles, but I'm afraid it will take a while before you see them.




10.) Are there any plans for a reload button sometime in the future?

Dai-Galean
There are at this time no plans for this.
