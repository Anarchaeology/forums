my thoughts on ao
i know funcom has been bashed by the whole community( hell i admit i even did some bashing myself). but the truth of the matter is that ao is indeed a great game. it made me cancel my subscritption to eq. even though everyone is complaining(yes even i) about how certain stuff dont work, bugs, gms, etc. the thing is AO really had things going. it seems to me though that this game would have been an mmorpg killer if even half of the original ideas had been implemented and all the bugs fixed. Instead we receive these "patches". Dont get me wrong...i love ao, its just frustrates me tha this could have been so much more. Im sure it would have done a lot better if they had more resources (since funcom is a small company). To all those who complain that fc doesnt listen...it is becuz they listen that tehy decided to nerf certain items...you guys have a bad habit of saying "give us uber items because we are too gimped" only for the other professionso say "nerf those items becuz they are too uber". these are just my thoughts..since i was bored and had nothing else to do right now so i decided to post them.

yes, AO is truely a great game, much better than all the other games out there. for now.

already i have seen a disturbing trend though, this game is rapidly becoming exactly like the rest of the games out there

for example, when i started playing last year, any proffesion of any breed and level could do what they needed to do in order to use any and every item or buff in the game they wanted.

this is not the case any more. now we have level requirements on nano's and armor, breed specific items and armor, proffesion specific items/weapons and nano's.

pretty soon, we are taking yet another step in the wrong direction by making crit buffs and crit enhancing items usuable for only certain types of dmg dealers. no, these things won't be proffesion/lvl/breed specific but will affect you based on the way you choose to play your character.
