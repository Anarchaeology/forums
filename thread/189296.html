Newland Reprise (Ode to Falikos)
Imagine there's no Loren,
the town council we will defy.
No thugs to hit us,
No one would have to die,
Imagine all the neutrals
leveling for today...

Imagine there's no Sentinals,
The time for change is due,
Nothing to run and hide from,
Nor living in a slum,
Imagine all the neutrals
living through the lease...

Imagine no token boards,
No Omni or Clan,
No need for greed or tokens,
A brotherhood of Xan,
Imagine all the neutrals
Sharing all Newland...

I prefer to say I'm a dreamer,
I just want some fun.
I hope some day you can join us,
And Rubi-Ka will live as one.
