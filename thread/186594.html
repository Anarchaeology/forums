Suggestion for getting around Shadowlands Quicker.
Ergo Controls the Major Portals between the lands but what if he also granted access to lesser in land Portals transporting you from Point to Point thru Ergos Network. But the system has limits.

One: Each Access/Egress Point is out of service and must be repaired and calibrated before it can be used again. (Makes you have to go find the Points and look at all that wonderous Scenery. Plus play Dodge the Mob.)

Two: Due to the deterioration of Ergos system you can only transport once every Five minutes. (So you stand an chat like in front of a elevator when you wait for your transport turn.)

Three: Due to the fragmented nature of Ergo Transport system no Across lands Transports can Occur, Such as Elysium to Sheol. (Maybe later a quest to restore the land connections can be added)

Four: Sorry No transports to the Gardens are available. (The Redeemed and Unredeemed were Security paranoid and didn't want any Back doors into they're Strongholds)

Five: The Transport web is weblike so you must make stops before going along to the next connection. (So you can't just jump from one side of Elysium to the other in one Fell swoop, You can do it in three hops though.)

Six: Ergo doesn't do this out of the Kindness of his Heart. He needs the Repairs and is willing to make this service available only to you and gives a Device to use on the access Points. (Another good Quest start. Mayhap like the charaters go and reboot some systems and get the system up and running. And Unlike the Grid only people who have done the Quest for it can get the Device and save wear on the Feet in shadowlands.)

Seven: The Device ergo Provides for the Transport also has a second Funtion of transporting you to the nearest Functioning Access point if you fall off the brink. (No longer do you fall to RK and have to run Back. Now you stay in SL which could suck if your trying to evade a mob and the access is near him.)

No holes to shoot. I've been thinking of something just like this.

The Transport Service Nodes would work like a mini-whompah system inside each zone. Each node would only be available to you after you've visited it and used Ergo's "TSN Tuning Device" on them. I don't see a need for a five minute wait time, though.

I've always assumed that the intent of Funcom was to make you explore each zone entirely. That's great, the first time. Not so fun the fifth or sixth time. This would alleviate some of those problems, making travel easier and hooking back up with your team much less of an ordeal.

Think about it: Funcom wants to encourage teaming, but they've set things up such that once someone in your team is killed, the team falls apart because it can take 15-20 minutes for the team member to get back. They then took away the engineer's Beacon Warp and replaced it with this totally nerfed up anchor/recall system, which is borderline useless. (Two minutes to cast the anchor, where every member must wait until completed, and then two minutes to cast a recall per team member. And the engineer has to wander back to the exact anchor point to do a recall. 2000 nano each, which is a big chunk of an engineers nano pool.)

A fast way to travel, which does not preclude the exploration which is so important to Funcom, would be ideal. If the nodes were designed in branches, like spokes from a central point, the central node for each zone could act like a meeting place for teams.

The whole point of this transport system is to get players back with their teams quickly, while still making them explore.

-Vepr
