Sylvabanes are good for a few things, most especially Instruction Discs and Notum pieces. They are also among the most dangerous creatures in the game, with a 40m range and two poison damage attacks, their tendency to cluster together in groups of 2-12, and the fact that they'll all aggro if you attack another (i.e., doesn't matter if you attack a Lunaspell, a Thanatosflower will also aggro).

They occur EVERYWHERE, and there are at least 20 different kinds that I have found. The hardest ones (Mortefury, Lunaspell, Gravebeau, Thanatosflower) are found in clumps in Milky Way, Eastern Fouls Plains, Southern Fouls Hills, the Artery Valley, and, very infrequently, in Mort and Perpetual Wastelands.
