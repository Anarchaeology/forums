OmniTek Foreign Affairs
OmniTek Foreign Affairs is a Bureaucrat/Trader/Agent guild specialising in trading of information and material goods between the factions. Based primarily in neutral territory in the Newland area, we wish to open Omni-1 and Tir branches as soon as our numbers allow it.

If you're a crat and like to talk a lot (as you should), this could be something for you.
If you're a trader looking for proper organisation in a guild that will take good care of your interests, i suggest you have a look.
Any guild needs some kind of armed forces. In our case we're looking into highly skilled well equipped agents, capable of defending our representatives as well as going on information-seeking missions.

Still in the planning stages as of yet. Give me a tell if you want in.
