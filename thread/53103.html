I use them for various reasons.

Among those, one reason has lately gained significant importance now that there are many high-level dyna-camps with their accompanying high-level bosses. In this case, I provide the hunt team with mob stats - as it helps towards formulating team composition and tactics.

Example: Hunt Team spots a trio of mobs, all con various shades of deep orange.
Knowing that one mob that has 303K HP and another 2 with 90K HP each, one can quickly and efficiently coordinate roles among team members and achieve a more effective hunt. Knowledge is good - it cuts down on unnecessary risk out in the wilderness where many unexpected and dangerous things can happen at any time.

Without knowledge of mob stats, you'll have to use the primitive hit-the-mob-and-find-out-the-hard-way. If that suits you, fine ... whatever floats your boat. However, that is simply too slow, clumsy and unprofessional a method to me - after all, I am a scout and explorer for a reason

Originally posted by Reaperman
A triple implanted perception with all the tools should be able to break through any mob conceal in game .
- Maybe have a 50/50 chance vs triple planted agent with cloak device ( Don't have a clue in this area though )
Both your points are incorrect, I'm afraid.

Firstly, the concealment of the targetted mob checks against the Info Tool itself, NOT the user's perception skill. That is why I'm asking for QL Info Tools to go beyond Q200. We have big perception buffs now but it means nothing if the QL of the Info Tool is low against the level of the mob we're trying to assess.

Secondly, perception vs concealment skill checks between PCs work differently from PvM. A higher perception skill does not mean a better chance against a hidden player in that you are NOT passively and directly checking against the concealment skill of that hidden player. A higher perception skill just simply means a wider radius of your search area when you do active spotting.

And what is active spotting? If you look at your Controls Menu, you will see a Magnifying Glass icon - this represents the search function a.k.a. spotting. And this function can only be actively used every 10 seconds.
