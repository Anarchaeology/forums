You can hunt Clon**** outside esp if you are omni up to level 70, although after level 40 or so you would do better in Andromeda.

Andromeda is fanstastic has a save point at Terra 700, 700, straight north of the Clon**** Grid.

Can you say BRONTO MAMA's. Lot's of them in Ardomeda, great for level 55-70. They are level 65 I think, easy to kill with fair amount of hit points.

Frak ... Brontos are all over the middle part of the zone. There are so many there, especially along the road from Clon**** to 4 Holes. In relation to the Shades camp there are some to the Ne of it but most are SW of it a ways.

Found this ditch place.

Eastern Fouls Plains 2500x3800

Its this valley, around the edges of it on each side is 75%, so hang around that in case some lvl 200 ganker feels like griefing your team.
I was lvl 133 and everything was deep red, gave my 4 member team about 40-50k xp per kill. Granted, not as effecient as a mission, but a lot more fun.

We did very well, a crat, a NT, a trader, and a doc. 3 calmers was very handy, you NEED a calmer, its just packed with mobs, and everything is agro. The snakes (life stoppers) were by far the best xp for how hard it was to kill them. Incubus's (same model as cacodemon) were next best, same xp and a little harder. The mantis's are just nasty, calm them and keep walking. Also the mantis's were VERY fast, and seemed to come out of nowhere. It was a fun challenge doing crowd control.
