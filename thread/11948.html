Soldier specific Weapons
I just wanted to start out by saying that i love this game and i think funcom has a great product here.

One suggestion that i would like to make is a fix for the soldier. Everyone is talking about having the professions do what they are designed to do. As a Soldier, i'm designed to kill stuff...and lots of it. To do that we need the biggest badest gun we can get our hands on. So far i'm using a Nova Flow -IV. I always seem to find a Engineer or some other profession with the same gun as me along with their bots and whatever else they have, leaving me at a sever disadvantage. Soldiers really have no damage buffs...we can't heal ourselfs untill a high lvl and even then its only 200-300 points a pop..which does really no good when i have over 3500 hps and the mobs are hittin me for around 150-700 or so even with high ACs. What i propose is soldier specific weapons. Higher damage with faster recycle times (more fitting of an assault rifle then some of the current ones out there). Maybe the answer is a higher dmg buff or something like that i don't know. all i know is that as a 83 soldier...i can't solo because i can't heal and i can't kill the mobs fast enough before then kick my a**. Let me know what you guys thing of this..
