Fixer shops and the Agent...
Try to understand both points of view.

Fixer point of view: They are weak and need lovin'. Like everyone else they vehemently reset when the agent even touches anything remotely worthwhile to them. The fixers want to feel unique, they want justification for choosing a fixer over an agent.

Agent point of view: They don't want to steal the fixer's ability to access the blackmarket, they just want to utilize their agent like ability to infiltrate it. Being an agent means accually getting to infiltrate stuff. Sneaking in, getting the goods, and getting the hell out before you are caught.

Agent's don't like the fixer terminal any more than the fixers. The agents want a real blackmarket that they can infiltrate, complete with an underground establishment, PC merchants, and guards that require your concealment skill to get pass based on what level of the shop you try to access.

Neither view is wrong. We just need a way to make the fixers feel unique, while making the agents feel like agents.
