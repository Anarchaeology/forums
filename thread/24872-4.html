NTs should rely on their nanos, but since Rubi-Ka has changed since the creation and disabling weapons for NTs might be a dumb choice, these guys needs to get some more nanos, however they should have a requirement 'NOT USABLE WITH WEAPON(S)". Agents, are semi masters of ranged combat. They already got 100% crit buffs, although for a shorter period of time. They can use at least 80% of all the nanos that exist on Rubi-Ka. Which is both a drawback and an advantage. If any profession was the jack of all traits, this is the class.

This is maybe the only profession who actually uses the nano with nanoshutdown on themselves.

Enforcers are those who should deal most damage in one single blow and be able to take more 'pure' damage. The only thing enforcers should get lots of is new, better and more viable weapons.

Opi got 544 agil, 512 sense, 464str and int, 448 psychic and 480 sta.

Solitus has 480 to all except for str, which is 472.

Since nanos has become so powerful as they currently are, intelligence are one of the base abillities that are most important.
