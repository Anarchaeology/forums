Simple suggestion. No stealthy maces.
First off, I'd like to salute you folks at Funcom. You listen to your players, and patiently ignore the idiots. Thank you. I came from EQ to AO, and seeing the actions you folks take to improve the game constantly reminds me why I told Verant they could shove the world of Norrath where the sun don't shine.

You appreciate your players, in action and not just in words, and put Verant and the like to shame. Thank you.

Anyway...

With the Ip reclaim coming out, I've had an opportunity as of late to decide again what weapon I would specialize in as an enforcer.

One of the items that intrigued me was the one-handed blunt line... That is, until I noticed near every 1hb weapon requires sneak attack skill.

I get the feeling 1hb weapons are meant for enforcers. Yet Enforcer cannot sneak worth a damn. Sorry, just doesn't work that way. As such, Sneak Attacks are nearly unuseable.

Putting IP into sneak attack just to use a weapon type that is best dual-weilded on top of that, feels like a waste of IP.

So... I would really love to see the sneak attack requirement removed from a number of one handed blunt weapons.

I understand the sneak attack as a requirement of Blackjacks. It goes with the theme. Still, on a mace? Maces aren't that stealthy of weapons. What good is a stealthy attack on an advanced baseball bat. Isn't that even more unlikely to be a stealthy weapon?

Surely it wouldn't unbalance the game too much to remove the sneak attacks and sneak attack requirements, and it would make the 1hb weapons more accessible to Enforcers.

Meanwhile, until that change is made, it looks like I'm going to have to go with two-handed swords.

Ah! One more thing! It'd be great if there were some sheilds available in the shops that didn't require an energy melee skill... With a Camelot look to them, perhaps?
