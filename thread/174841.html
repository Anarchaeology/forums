Ross addresses OMNI-TEK Leaders
Editor in Chief Raginar, OTPC

Today, among OMNI Armed Forces security in Rome, Phillip Ross addressed OMNI-TEK leaders. “Like the pioneers of old, new pioneers are moving as we speak to the city of Jobe to ensure that again OMNI-TEK may continue its unending quest…” said Ross before ordering all OMNI-TEK employees to assist in a battle being waged outside Home.

Security was out in force, the size of which has rarely been seen, today as Rome Red District was secured for the CEO’s address. Immediately following an announcement to Omni faction leaders, Red District went to an Alpha-1 security state and fell under complete military control. As all points of access were controlled, OMNI-AF support soldiers armed with Hellspinner Shock Cannons were stationed en masse across the park and at entrances. The soldiers were supplemented by the heavy-duty firepower of automated Umpire Opressor Mk III cannons and Slayerdroids, some hidden in statues and shrubs.

Ross began his address with a tribute to past explorers and pioneers--his central theme throughout the speech. “All that you see around you we built in those 775 years, something that happened through the blood, sweat and tears of men…” he stated, then went on to honor such notable names as Captain Yves Boulez and Min-Ji Liu, the first human to set foot on Rubi-Ka.

As he continued, he spoke of OMNI-TEK and mankind taking the first “…step out of the old and into the new; again, leaving an age behind.” He then spoke of a new group of pioneers that were moving to the city of Jobe to ensure that OMNI-TEK continues on its path.

For a brief moment, the speculation rose that Ross was caught in the moment of OMNI-TEK’s glory as he fixed his eyes on a point in the distance and apparently spoke to himself in deep thought.

From this reporter’s perspective in the crowd, I couldn’t help but hear someone speak "I am the ages. I have been here forever and guided you to this place." While it was unclear where this voice came from, it was very evident that Ross had heard it too. As Ross began to reply to this proclamation, an aide rushed to his side and whispered in his ear.

After a brief consultation with the employee, Ross turned his eyes back to the crowd and shared his knowledge- “Fellow employees, during this speech, a force from Omni-Tek Armed Forces moved towards Jobe to put force behind our claim on the portal. They are under attack.”

Almost immediately, anger spread through the crowd and calls for battle were heard. Ross went on to say that the reactionary forces who threatened the peaceful OMNI-TEK exploration were driven by these so-called 'visions' that citizens were reported to have seen.

After confessing that his order must be “clear” and “decisive” in such circumstances, Ross ordered all OMNI-TEK employees in attendance to assist OMNI-AF forces in protecting the party in Broken Shores.

Ross made hasty leave to command his corporation as a column of OMNI-AF soldiers led other OMNI-TEK employees into the Whompa to Broken Shores.

Citizens of Rubi-Ka, Omni-Tek Employees welcome.

That moment was 775 years ago when the first brave pioneers set foot upon OT3934-12; the planet we now know as Rubi-ka.
