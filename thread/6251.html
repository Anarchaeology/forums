Alternative to IP reset
I suggest a "drop level" terminal in the shops.
This is how it could work:

To use it you can not be wearing any clothes, weapons or implants. You have to strip yourself completely for access to the terminal.

Upon accessing the terminal you can select to drop 1-10 levels, perhaps for a suitable price. Then you are taken to the skills and abilities screen where you must lower some skills/abilities to "set back" the IPs that was gained from the levels you are now dropping.

The only problem I see with this is with learned nanos. Maybe it would be possible to scan through learned progs and return the crystal to your backpack, bank or destroy it if you have no room in backpack.

Example
"Example: I raised concealment quite high in the start, and i was probably lvl 40-50 when they (FC) nerfed it. Im now lvl 82. How can that benefit me then?"

At the terminal:
You choose to drop 4 levels, going back to level 78.
4 levels from 78-82 is 80k IP (I think).
Let's say your concealment cost 50k IP to raise to its current level.
You release 50k of your IP and drop your concealment to 0.
You still have 30k IP to release.
Let's say you think you wasted too much IP in stamina.
You release the last 30k IP on dropping stamina.
