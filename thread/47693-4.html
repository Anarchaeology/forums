Leetas
Ok, this will be a post about how I do the calculations for the damage. It is only the damage/minute calculations that I am going to be discussing here, as the indivual calculations for the damage multipliers and item/recharge delay I am 100% sure about.

If you can see anything wrong in these calculations, or something that I can do better, convince me of it, and I will change the calculations and revisit the QB stats again.

My calculations make the following assumptions:
1. You hit 100% of the time.
2. Your initiatives are the same as your attack rating (for the QB calculations, I used a skill of 1023).
3. Your aggdef is 100 (full right and no FoB modifiers).
4. No AC is taken into account (damage reduction assumed to be 0).

Average Damage per hit calculation:
averagedamage = 0.5 * (max - min) + min

Number of hits per minute:
hitsperminute = 6000 / (calculateditem + calculatedrecharge)

Number of crits per minute:
critsperminute = criticalpercentage * (hitsperminute / 100)

The damage per minute calculations (not including crits):
damageperminute = hitsperminute * averagedamage

The damage per minute calculation, including crits:
damage = (hitsperminute - critsperminute)*averagedamage + (critsperminute*calculatedcritdamage)

See anything wrong with those calculations? Anything that I can do to improve them?

Now, some numbers of the new QB and the longmoon generated using the above formulas at 1023 skill.
code:
   Avg   Crit Hits/min Dmg/min 0% Dmg/min 10% Dmg/min 30%
LM  896  2028   30      26880      30180       37068
QB 2620  4088   10      28662      30082       32922
Because of the +320 HP the QB has, I am fine with it doing less damage at the very high crit rates. I will add a DD proc to the QB, however, as it was originally meant to have one (it was left off it looks like). The QB is meant to be slow and hard-hitting, not crit dependent. As you can see, the LM is capped out at 1sec/1sec, but the QB is still quite slow - the speed means that as there are more attacks per minute, there are more criticals, taking the LM into the lead.
