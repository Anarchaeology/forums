I always pictured Omni-Tek Rubi-Ka to be akin to a mining consortium as set up in the U.S. during the Gold Rushes in California and Alaska.

Omni-Tek the parent corporation we have little information about other than the Rubi-Ka branch was set up to develop the Notum found on Rubi'Ka. Being that Rubi'Ka was so far away from Omni-Tek's home planet (Omni-Tek Prime, I think it's called), they needed a structure that would be self-sufficient. Hence we get the divisional structure:

- Omni-Pol: They were charged with policing the miners and making sure that things ran smoothly at the mining camps. They enforced corporate policy. A lot of lawlessness can tend to break out in this kind of 'wild west' atmosphere unless a tight reign is held. With enough Omni-Pol walking the city streets, a night of drinking at Heads 'n Tails is less likely to end up in violence in a back alley.

- Omni-Med: They are responsible for the physical well-being of employees as well as research into how best to adapt to life on the alien planet. Remember Rubi'Ka was a barren dust ball until Terraforming began. They are in charge of developing life forms that are particularly suited to the atmosphere of Rubi'Ka and the needs of Omni-Tek's mining operations. Any non-human life on Rubi'Ka more likely than not was either seeded by Omni-Med or escaped their laboratories.

- Omni-AF: Every outpost needs a military. First to protect from rival hypercorporations and later from rampant mutant attacks. An armed contingent to protect the most important mineral in the galaxy is assumed.

- Omni-InternOps: Yes, you need a department to watch the departments. These are the police who keep an eye on every office in Omni-Tek Rubi'ka Corporate Headquarters. Of course their skills may be called into play for other investigative purposes as required.

- Omni-Reform: Basically public relations. They set up the loudspeakers and signs proclaiming the company's generosity and goodwill. Note that a few loudspeakers still blare propaganda from the last civil war.

- Omni-Trans: Charged with setting up transportation on the planet. The Whom'pas and the Grid are their creation. Surely they work hard each day to find ways to most efficiently get the Notum from the mines to its eventual destination at various depots and refineries throughout the galaxy. I'd assume the Notum cannons in Clon**** where their idea.

I can't see any of these departments having much independence outside of Omni-Tek Rubi'ka's corporate structure. They may have individual charters, yet, undoubtedly all answer to Ross and his bosses. We've yet to get any information regarding Omni-Tek above the level of Phillip Ross and the Rubi'Ka division.

The one I see glaringly missing is the department directly in charge of administering the mines and miners. Who runs the mining operations? Who accounts for daily production and oversees the workers?
