Why do I cringe at patches?
I know Funcom means well when they patch. You want to make the game more interesting and fun for your customers, right? With the latest patch it appears you gave free-range to the omni while limiting the hunting areas for the clanners, at level 86 you practically wiped out the hunting fields. I am limited to Mort where I can be PvP'd(an aspect of the game I do not care for), or missons. Either way both require a team to be formed. I remember that it was said we could form a team if we liked, but that we could also solo. The upgraded green level mobs are a tough customer, so it really means that if your a clanner then you better have friends that can spend the time with you hunting. This is NOT interesting nor fun. Every patch I notice Funcom pays close attention to exploits as opposed to 'fixing' the on-going character flaws and aspects of the game that everyone is concerned about. On another note, it is my opinion that 'IP's be delegated better, 20k a level goes very fast, at higher levels its barely enough to cover the essential attributes of a character. So when you guys host these 'swimming competitions' I feel as though you are mocking us as well as your developement team. No one has any 'extra' IP to put into a useless skill. I would like it if we could change attributes at our discretion, instead of locking us into it. So when someone realizes " Oh damn, I didnt need that skill at all" they could remove the spent IP in that skill and put it where it is needed, instead of having to throw away their poorly constructed character and waste all their time again. Anyway, these are a couple of my concerns, I have come to beleive you guys(Funcom) don't really give a crap what your customers think so I will shut up now as I have just realized I just wasted some more of my time. One day this game will be wonderful!!! ........right?

The reason for what they are doing is :

Funcom needs income to a) keep the game and themselves alive. b) to afford to make improvements to the game.

Therefore one of their current main goals is to implement features that will help them entice new or keep players.

However you look at it, the current playerbase is relatively small, enough to keep the game going, but not enough to make it grow. Hence the need to boost the existing playerbase with new players, hence the new marketing campaign, hence features added like Japanese channel. (The game was released in some areas of Asia/Pacific around September last year).
