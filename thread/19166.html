122 MP seeking European Guild
Greetings!

My name is Sunfactor. I am a 122 years old Solitus Meta-Physicist looking for a guild. Especially interested in joining a guild with a majority of Europeans, as my mentor himself is Norwegian.

I do not wish to join a guild which doesnt reckognize my potential, nor a guild which requires me to send in an application.

I have been an active member in a few other guilds which concider themselves larger guilds, and I have gone through a number of applicant periods, only to be dissapointed by the guilds werent that active or varied as I had hoped.

I am seeking a social guild, perhaps a little roleplaying guild. The main thing is that it's majority has to be level 100+ and not too big - preferrably European.

If you need me to send an application, dont bother sending me a tell. If an online interview isnt sufficiant, then I am not interested.

-------

A little more about my mentor Aforce:

He is an active participant in all my doings and he likes to keep me active. He is humble at times, but I think his powers went to his head a while ago. He is particularly picky with customers, and he doesnt appreciate a rude tell. He is a bit unpredictable that way, since he finds many things 'rude' or 'unpleasant'. On the other hand when it comes to people he is familiar with, he is quite informal, laid back and witty at times. His motto, which he persistantly tells me, is : "Do unto others as you wish others to do unto you".

Aforce is a secret member of the Order of the White Knights, and I myself am a White Knight apprentice. <ooops... I guess its not a secret anymore..>

Lastly (if you've bothered reading this far), I would just like to say that if you find me or my words in this regard somewhat harsh or arrogant, its just my way of setting a quality referance on what guild I am interested in joining. There are a lot of guilds out there that promises more than they can keep.. But do not let me scare you away if you have what I seek!
