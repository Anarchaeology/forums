It's not possible. Thus, OOC-wise, we get killed in Newland for something we didn't do in the first place. We clanners are not the ones who control the sentinels. Those neuts who hired the merc have full control, however.

IC : I believe in the fight for freedom, and I also believe people should be free to take a neutral stand, even if I don't admire the stand they take. The problem with neuts is that you don't know where you have'em .. So, if you're not sure, don't deal with them.

One problem is when the neuts as a whole acts as an extension of Omnitek. They're our neighbours, and omnitek can travel through neutral areas to launch attacks on us. We get attacked whenever we go to Newland by a hired omni-aligned thug.

The sentinels haven't been nice, and I can understand why neuts want to protect themselves against clanners, but think it over ..
Clans getting killed in Newland because of hired OT mercs is *not* just percieved as means to protect oneself. The neuts as a whole are aligning themselves with our enemy. Even if that's not the message they want to get across, that's how things work at the moment.

OOC : It looks as if we can't get rid of the Sentinels IC-Wise, we're at war with both neuts and omni, both IC and OOC. If the neutrals are going to work as extensions of omnitek, I will fight them as well. To me, it may seem like that's where FC wants to take us.

OOC/IC : Anyhow, even if the sentinels disappear and the merc, we'll have less trust in neutrals than ever before. Still, neuts are our neighbours, and I don't want to see us clanners as worse oppressors than the omni already is. If I took a guess storywise on what FC had in mind, it would be .. "The little rebel people against the mighty, oppressing Corporation" .. I kinda like that. When I first started, I kinda wanted to be a freedom fighter. One of the good guys. Now, neutrals are the little people, and in a better Rubi-Ka, omnitek would be their oppressors. Not us clanners. Gods, I'm trailing off here .. Anyways, if I have to, I'll fight neuts too.
