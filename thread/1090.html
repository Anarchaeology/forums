Changing clothing colors
Having played in MUDs prior to playing a MMORGP there is a feature that I think should be added to AO. In most MUDs there is a feature in which you can dye your clothing different colors. In AO however whatever item you get you are stuck (even if it is a weird orange and green wacko combination). I think that there should be a way to dye clothing. This way people can customize how they want to appear (this is a visual game afterall) and not look like everyone else in the game.

What do you all think. Discuss. Funcom, your opinion is also wanted.
