Is your Life Insurance safe?
I have heard reports of an unauthorized cloning facility in the area of the longest road. This greatly concerns me.

Mr. Ross is in the preverbal place between a rock and a hard place. If he scans, he can be cloned. If he doesnt scan, hes vulnerable to assassination.

Scanning isnt sure thing ether, as the recent past has shown. Insurance records can be corrupted (thankfully a very rare occurrence).

It is clear that the security of our insurance records needs to be greatly increased. The ICC (who is the authority in charge of the life insurance system) needs to be held accountable! They are letting unauthorized access (at least I hope its unauthorized) to peoples accounts! Records are being corrupted, and copied!

If high profile people are not safe, how can Joe and Jane Sixpack feel safe in their life insurance? Clan, Omni, or Neutral is your insurance safe?

This is serious business and heads need to roll!
