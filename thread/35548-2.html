I will be acting as a facilitator, and keeping the conference moving and enforcing (light-handedly) some basic debate courtesy rules, which I was explaining to people as they email me with topics, and planned to post as the day drew near. But since it's come up already...

I only envision four rules:
1. Language... please keep it family-friendly.
2. Script - try and put your speech in a script so that it doesn't take extreme amounts of time (especially if you're a slow typist).
3. 10 minutes - I won't enforce this vigorously, but it's not fair to other speakers or to the listeners if you go on much longer than anyone else.
4. Q&A - it will be up to the speaker to open their time to questions after delivering their remarks. They can choose among raised hands for whose question they'll take when. I'll let the time limit slide if there's a good discussion going on, but I'll also intervene and bring on the next speaker if our Q&A starts turning into F&R (flames and rants).

I'd also ask the listeners to be polite and not interrupt in shout or vicinity while someone was speaking.

Please send your emails to me if you want to speak, eq_evelyn@hotmail.com - I will be updating the agenda tonight with what I do have.
