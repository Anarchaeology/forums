Transport Idea!
Hello All, I know That we have many ways of transportation in Rubi-ka.

E.g Wompa,Grid,Yalmaha (for those who can afford one) and the others not mentioned.

But how about this?

The world of Rubi-ka is filled with monsters and animals.
Well how about creating a few animals that we ride?
The animal animations and skins are already there so why not just pop a saddle on it and lets us ride?

This is how it would work, foe example. in Harry's there is a compound where minibrontos are kept like sheep.
Well how about having one of those but for animals that you could ride. You would go there and see the proprietor of the compound and purchase an animal off him. You would then equipp it just like you do, when you ride in a vehicle, except for being inside you will ride on its back.

Not only can you still shoot from the animal, but the animal depending on which type it is could add attack aswell.

Here are some animal types that you could include:

A beast that is Slow getting about but powerfull in attack.
A beast that is weak in attack but can run fast.
And perhaps a flying beast.

I dont want this to be chucked aside, as this would make a great addition to the game. not only would it increase the number of people using their animals go go out into the wilderness to hunt in packs with their beasts.
But it will add a bit more depth and also some veried gaming other than going to missions and getting XP.
Im sure lots of people would find it fun to jump on their beasts back and go out hunting!
I know i would!

But dont make the animals no drop. Perhaps they could be sold at auctions which maybe traders can take up the roles on, as they are traders afterall.
But say for instance if you go out hunting with your animal and you get attacked, then it will take damage too, so if it dies it dies. that way you are not stuck with a completely reliable transport such as yalmahas which nevr break down or go wrong, which means players will have a bit more to do and it will add more depth to the game and atmosphere.


I can see it now, looking onto the Horizon i see a group of Neutral traders on the backs of their beasts walking along in a chain like a caravan, bringing their new goods for sale in Newland.

I would love to hear your responses on this, Please Post!
