The PvP title is already exploited. Whenever I roll a new char, I have to tell off at least one person who asks if I would help them get a title. This would just speed up their effort a bit - like getting TWO friends to do the cycle with (shocking concept) :-p I think that there are many more basic problems with the PvP title sytem to worry about it as the primary block to a logical skill.

Give it a longish cast and reload time, and make the target req be sitting. Don't heal the health and the actual hard coded tag for res shock with the effect - just the ability points. So to the game, they would still be in res shock.

Examples:
Level 50: PsyMod 250, SenseImp 200, 120 sec cast, 60 sec reload, lock PsyMod 4 mins
Level 100: PsyMod 475, SenseImp 430, 70 sec cast, 40 sec reload, lock PsyMod 3 mins
Level 150: PsyMod 675, SenseImp 600, 40 sec cast, 30 sec reload, lock PsyMod 2 mins

So it wouldn't be useful in the loop you mentioned. It uses 2 of the more useless Doc skills (only used for the Treatment buffs).

The main use would be at higher levels, where an Engi could TP someone back when they die, and the Doc could cure their res shock. It wouldn't even be that great an ability - just a bit of a time saver.

See, I can cover my bases.
