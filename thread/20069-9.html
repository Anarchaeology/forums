One little step at a time...
Actually, if these ideas are too hard to implement, there are areas where they could be tested/implemented more easily :

- Dungeons. These are not random generated, they're carefully designed, like scenarii in old times RPG. So, it's possible to fine tune them... Right now, they're mainly hack&slash. It would be possible to add alternate paths in them (you can still hack&slash, but you can also sneak through some part, or resolve puzzles to avoid some of the fights...).

- Static missions. Not many of these yet, and many are broken... BUT the keyword is static : no random element. So, they're easier to fine tune. Each static mission could have a "mood" to it (sneaking, protecting, hacking, grid...).

- Chests. Right now, the chest system is under-developped. No risk, no difficulty, little reward. I had to chance to play a MUD with an EXCELLENT chest designer (Excellent = imaginative and slightly sadistic). They *can* be great fun... People would shiver while you were using your trade on one of these devious things, bow in respect when you actually managed to open one without injuring too many people... Chest don't have to use only B&E, they could use comp lit, nano programming... Or could even be solved though several paths (Disarm to disarm the trigger or nano programming to disable the crystal with the trap effect)... I was thinking of posting some ideas about these to the Fixer boards later...
