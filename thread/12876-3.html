Manually Reloading A Gun
How to Manually Reload A Weapon.

Pick up bullets in the inventory and right click on your gun in the equipment window.

It was brought up in a different thread that this should be basic information and yet many players don't know how to do this. Also I have not seen it mentioned in any of the Newbie tips yet it can be a lifesaver in the right situation.

Loading your gun will save you from the reload cycle during a fight. This can mean the difference between victory and reclaim in a very close fight. Normally it just means you don't have to take a few extra hits or kite out for a short bit. My recommendation is to reload every mob or two with an MCS and every 5-6 mobs with a Mausser. You can probably go longer with a Mausser but it also takes longer to reload so why risk it.
