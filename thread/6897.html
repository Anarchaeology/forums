Overequipping thought
Why not put explicit level limits on the targets of friendly nanos? This is already partially implemented by the fact that high level nanos require more NCU than the average low level character can use. It could be fully implemented by using the same approximate rules for general teaming - so if a nano-formula comes from a QL150 crystal, it can only be used on friendly targets of 110+ (or whatever it is). I would also suggest that a player can always target himself, making self buffing viable.

So what would be the impact? It probably wouldn't affect day-to-day life very much. The range of allowable levels is already pretty large, and I can't think of any scenarios off the top of my head in which a teammate would be denied a buff during the course of normal hunting. However, it would go very far in preventing low-level characters from using high-level nanos to overequip. This would not affect self-buffing/equipping in any way! It would just prevent level 20 characters from getting +132 wrangles and +140 nano buffs - the character would have to settle for the more realistic +25 nano buffs and +10 wrangles, which seems just fine to me. Just a thought...
