MOB Insane Reflect Shield Damage Mystery - Solved!
It's a recurring theme in this forum and in the Martial Arts forum. We keep running up against mobs that have way, way more reflect shield than make sense for their level. This wasn't happening consistently with any one type of mob, but it was happening about once per mission, on average.

The official explanation was that two or more mobs that were friendly to each other were buffing each other - layering a damage shield on top of a reflect shield. But I knew that wasn't it, because I wasn't getting two messages per hit, just one. If that were it, then every time I hit the mob, I would see something like: "Someone's reflect shield hit you for x points. Someone's damage shield hit you for y points." Instead, I was only seeing "Someone's reflect shield hit you for z points," for offensively high values of z.

Then, the other night, I saw a mob (all by his lonesome) prepare to fight me ... and what I saw, combined with another clue I had from earlier, solved the mystery. The scales fell from my eyes, and suddenly I could see clearly.

They're layering three effects: a reflect shield nano, a Deflection Shield Projector, and Bracers of Reflection.

Now, Bracers of Reflection mostly don't work. We were originally told that it was just a display glitch, and they actually were doing the damage. It wasn't true. All you had to do to see it wasn't true was get beat on by more than one mob at a time; when you got to the next one in line it was undamaged. Eventually Funcom found the real bug. (All Funcom employees and ARKs: If you don't know, say so! Making up a story hurts your credibility.) There was a database problem, and bracers of reflection weren't working. This is one of the announced bug fixes for 13.6.

Well, they're obviously implementing parts of the fix in advance on the server side. Because I noticed almost a week ago that my bracers of reflection work - but only sometimes. Bracers of Reflection - Physics work more often than not. Bracers of Reflection - Melee work occasionally. I have yet to see Bracers of Reflection - Projectile or - Nano work.

But at one point, I activated a low level Deflection Shield Generator at just the point where my chemical reflection bracer was working, and realized that for someone with no reflect shield nano, I was returning pretty good damage.

Then, a couple of days later, I watched a mob cast a reflect shield on itself, then get that "glow-y" spherical halo that you get when you activate a Deflection Shield. I attacked - and when the damage coming back totalled even more than should have come from that combo, I realized that this mob must also have a Bracer of Reflection - Melee on, and unlike mine, his works. Heck, for all I know, he has two of them.

Now, here's my theory: Bracers of Reflection have probably worked reliably on mobs that randomly spawn with them, at least since 13.4 and maybe earlier. And on mobs that have the rare combination of all three, the damage reflection is insane. What's more, according to my observations, all reflect shield damage must be completely ignoring AC.

(This raises the possibility that it could get worse: I have yet to see one layering shields the way that was suggested. I'm not surprised, the NCU cost would be high, so this should be rare. But imagine the combination of all four!)

If it gets fixed so that this works normally for players, it will put the final nail in the coffin of people like martial artists and edged weapon enforcers, who concentrate on doing a little damage lots of times. At that point, almost everybody will be returning as much damage as the barefisted MA or swordsman is putting in (or more!), while doing their own damage on top of it.

(Now I see why Bracers were made NODROP.)
