My opinion, Don't nerf the ELLTS, leave them alone. The rarity of them, plus the skills needed to equip them, plus the insane Init penatly, makes them pretty well balanced for the added Crit.


Now, since FC is more "Nerf" happy than a 5 year old in a Toy Store, I'm willing to bet this nerf will take effect, even is 90% of AO's population is against it. So, if FC desides to go ahead with this nerf, here is my suggestions:

1st: Add a damage modifier. Adding a 50-80 damage modifier, while not coming close to the extra Crit damage, would go along with the concept of the scope itself.

2nd: Have lower Init penalties than the VE's.


3rd: No non-combat skill increases. The scope is designed to modifiy damage, it has nothing to due with other skills. Adding +30 to a trade skill makes no since. Anyone over level 135 can easily raise they're perception into the 800's, without spending a single point of IP.

4td: XP increase. While some increase experiance would (of course) be nice, I think the main thing that the scope should increase is damage (i.e. suggestions 1 and 2).

After the nerf, and old +9's and +15's should have the same crit increase (+8), the differance should be the old +15's have much higher bonuses (higher damage modifier, higher aimed shot, higher fling).
