First aids need improving- opinions?
If you play one of the professions which doesnt have some kind of healing nano, you pretty much have to use these. They work ok at newbie lvls: you get hit for 20, 23, 18, 30... you use first aid and get healed for 80 pts.

At higher lvls however they are pitiful: you get hit for 122, 145, 133, 200... you use first aid and get healed for a whopping 200 pts! .. then you have to wait 40 sec to use one again, by that time you either ran away or are dead.

I suggest increasing the amount healed and reducing the recharge time, also have a better range of QL available in the shops. I mean after all you have 1 skill that needs raising and is only any use for using first aids and nothing else.

um, I can't see first aids as ever coming anywhere near close to the effectiveness of healing nanos. First off, you can't really 'over-equip' first aids since the implants would conflict with more necessary implants, this means you are using first aids with a QL very near your lvl. The only professions which can boost first aid skill with a non-general buff are docs and adv, both classes use healing nanos. On the other hand, everyone 'over-equips' to use healing nanos beyond their level.

And also, you say classes become more and more specialized at higher lvls? First aid is a non-profession specific skill... anyone can learn it, a boost to first aids would in fact give docs and adv more since they can use them more skillfully.

Take the first aid that heals for 200hp for example, in that same amount of time a healing nano caster could heal up to 2000hp before running out of nano. I don't see how improving the first aid skill so you can heal yourself of say 600hp total for the entire fight would make docs worthless.. the doc would then have 2000hp + 600hp =2600hp of healing power for that fight, making their healing more effective too.

The only real reason you would not want that change is if you never bothered to raise your first aid skill and don't want to be left behind those who wasted tons of ip into a skill that does nothing else and is pretty useless atm...
