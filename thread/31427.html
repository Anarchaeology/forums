I (and a few others) think we all are responsible for "pushing" FC into screwing up the differences between breeds and professions.

The Opifex needs faster natural regeneration and natural runspeed and evasion abilities.

The Nanomage needs an Intelligence boost gained at every title cap 3 at 15, 4 at 30 5 at 50, 7 at 100, 8 at 150 and 9 at 200 for a total of 36 additional Int over what they have now (+9 to all nanoskills and better armor).
