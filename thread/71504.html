Desert Winds scout cyborg presence in the Wastelands
Less than a week after our last trip we had to again
send troops to both Mort AND Pleasent Meadows to try
to counter the borg threat. Make no mistake everyone,
the borg are a threat to humanity a parasite that
lives of us and would destroy us all no questions
asked. I have seen enough troops in my command killed
by them, their dead bodies reanimated and attacking me
the next day to trust them.

An Omni-Tek mining facility far to the north and east
of Subulum in Perpetual wastelands was recently
attacked by a strong force of Borgs. The borgs
slaughtered everyone in the mine then used the 'parts'
and machinery to make more of their own kind. The mine
is now a strong hold churning out borgs every hour of
every day. No attempt to retake the mine has been
succesful. As part of a future attempt we sent a squad
of the Desert Winds on foot, so to avoid radar
detection, from Hope to the Mine in the Wastelands.

The journey was long and arduous, the wastelands are
not to be travelled lightly. Mantis have invaded the
wasteland and control large parts of the territory,
they have taken over the remains of Subulum, the city
deserted since the war there last year. We found no
shelter in Subulum and pressed on. Attacked every step
of the way by Mantis, we even encountered the
dangerous Nano-Mantis. But we knew this was better
than being detected by the Borgs and giving them early
warning of our arrival.

The Mine itself is divided into two main parts, an
outlying command center and the mine facility itself.
a lake of toxic spill, the waste of humans raping
Rubi-Ka, divides the two locations. the Borgs are
completly in control of both parts and patrol in
numbers. We sighted several borgs and quickly took
them down essentially securing the command center for
the short term, we sent a communication for more
troops to help us retake the mine but none were
forthcoming. Knowing we could not retake the mine
ourselves we instead decided to see forouselvces the
nature of the Borg in the Mine.

They have an army, I do not need to impress on anyone
that they are there in force. It can only be a matter
of time before this army sweeps from the north and
attempts to destroy everyone in its path. Alas we were
detected and a squad of heavily armed and trained
borgs attacked us in the main area. Three of our team
were taken down but we managed to get us and their
corpses into the grid before further casualties were
sustained.

The task remains to assemble troops to try and retake
the mine before we are all borg.
