Well Funcom seem to be starting to recognise the doc profesion a bit more

1. Fix the invisibility bug in missions. People flitting in and out of sight mid fight is not good. People can easily die becuase of this, and with the penalties for dying being so harsh it frustrates players. With the changes to LOS on team heals this needs to be addressed NOW!

4. Bugged HP bars that show someone injured when they are not, and only resets when the patient is injured.

5. HP bars that dont go down when a team mates takes damage until after the fight. then they insta die.

These are problems common to all the heal classes and need to be changed. None of them will unbalance any proffession, well might unbalance us as we will get less grief during the average game.

Please Funcom, i'm not asking for the earth here, not for a new uber nano, or a nerf to another class or to fix my nanos so they are too powerful, Just sort some simple bugs that have been in your fun game since i started playing last September.
