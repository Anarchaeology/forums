Low-light scopes problem - agreed
In all of this, I agree the retention of the old LLS in the game without a nerf is the most dangerous aspect. Don't get me wrong, I do realize how bad it is to nerf something a player worked his/her virtual a$$ off for... however, this is a case where retaining the old item while replacing it with a new one will have tremendously bad effects.

With this decision, you create an elitist atmosphere... where those who have the original LLS will now be significantly advantaged over those who don't. Admitted, I don't know what the new scopes will look like (numbers-wise), but I'm assuming that they will be notably reduced from the old (otherwise, why bother).

Case in point... anyone remember how much work Sony had to do with EQ to "fix" manastones? Basically, they are STILL in the process of removing every zone in the game from the "it will work here" list. If they had just removed the damn stone in the first place, the problem would be solved. Yes, the *****ing would be loud... but in two weeks it would be mostly forgotten and we could move the hell on. Instead, they allowed those that had the stone to keep it, and removed it from dropping. Result: HUGE demand on the item, and tremendous advantage for those who had vs. those who did not. They had to continually remove functionality in zones until the item was made nearly useless.

Bottom line, this is a Bad Idea (tm). They should remove the old LLS entirely, replacing them with an equivalent QL new LLS, removing the imbalance they are creating... or they should just leave it the heck alone.
