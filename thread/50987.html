Shops a good thing
I suggest that the shop be created, the value you get when you consign an item is close to the store value (otherwise who would buy stuff at a store). The "auction" would last a few days, and at the end the money is deposited in the account, minus a sellers fee. I suggest that NPCs also chose to access the store and buy things, that would help the fledgling economy aspect of the game.

I suggest that there be a maximum (low) number of items that each person could put in the store, and that you have to sort it the same way the normal stores are saved. I dont want to have to search through five hundred run stims to find one precious nano.

I suggest bidding - and the money be frozen in your account until your bid is superceded. The item would be delivered to your bank when the auction is done.

Personally, i hate the economy of Rubi ka, filled with twinkers and everyone fighting over a few good items. It really has become a matter of who has the highest alt more than anything else.

as an additional peice of code- you could make electronic billboards outside the shop advertizing present sales rates, that would be cool, and might attract more people to use the system.
