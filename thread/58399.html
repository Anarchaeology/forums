Want Dynacamps on Dovve's Map? Yes or No?
After a a lot of conversation with players I had decided not to add any more dyna camps to the map after the andromeda ones (for the time being anyway). This is for a few reasons:

1) It's a spoiler
2) It creates potential camping problems
3) It will clutter the map quite a bit (I could probably find a way to do it so it not "that" bad but it would still add a lot of noise to what is already a pretty filled screen)

While I did make this map to encourage exploration and get people outside I don't want to have a negative impact on people's experience either. Does it wreck the game to be able to come right in on day one and know where everything is?

I do know that some of the bosses drop some very unique loot. And not many people know where they are right now.

I bring this up as it's the #1 request I get for a map addition. I'm asking here becuase I make this more for the community as a whole than myself, so if there is a big enough tilt for doing it I will do it.

Please post your thoughts, solutions and the level at which you play the game the most (low level game 1-100, high level game 100-200) as it will help decide what camps may be added. Developers thoughts on this are also welcome.

Thanks everybody

No!
People do not appreciate what is handed to them, no matter how much they want it. As it stands, it is not a lot of work to find any of these mobs and the map itself is an important communication device in doing so.

The majority of new boss mobs are already easily located using the markers already on Dovve's map. For example, The One is by the ruins in SFH. If you do not have Dovve's map, you end up going by coordinates. If you do, you actually describe the area: the ruins in SFH, SE of the omni outpost.

Putting a little red dot with The One or a number next to it completely removes people from interacting with their environment and actually looking around to try to find something. Also, there is no need to talk with others to try to get locations of new mobs, which is an important part of community ineraction.

Knowing where uniques/bosses are is somewhat of a point of pride and generally comes with experience outside of BS, good contacts and communication skills, and effort into keeping up on the forums. Putting all of this information on the in game map makes this experience trivial.

Vote Yes Enthusiastically
I was a total newb four months ago. Didn't even know about your map until I'd been playing two months, so it seems that the newb/spoiler argument doesn't hold water. A persistent newb can find the coordinates of anything in 2 minutes here or in the game anyway. I remember the days before I found your map when I was consistently eaten 4-5 times a weekend running to missions. This experience was not "awe-inspiring," Jynne , and more importantly, not fun in the least. Though I got a yalm shortly after getting your map, there was nice interval where I was hardly ever eaten by steering clear of certain danger areas. Newbs get eaten quite enough between 20-80, map or no. More detail in your map = Less newbs eaten.

Now, at intermediate level, I really appreciate the maximum amount of detail in a map. It's not as if extremely detailed maps wouldn't be available on a real RK2. Here in the 20th century, I can stand outside and using a PDA, I can tell you exactly where the nearest Italian restaurant is and how far. On RK2, 30000 years in the future, I can stand in Rome and tell you that a "machine" is a block away. LOL. More detail = better and more realistic gameplay. People are stuck in a rut of BS missions. Mapping the 150+ new spawns might give peeps some incentive to get out and explore the world instead of running another BS mission.

Camping- It's not the people who need your map that are camping. It's the hard hard core MMORPGers that already know where everything is and want to keep it a "guild secret". For example, I see my character going back to Foreman's one day to help newbs get guns/suits/rings. I will never make that dungeon a "camp stop" because the concept doesn't appeal to me. When I do go, though, it is so nice not to have to ask where it is. Same applies to any static game content. /Me questions the ulterior motives of some of the "nay" posters here.

With so many new outdoor camps in the game, having more of them mapped, as one poster has suggested, will lead to less, not more camping.

Well, in any event, thanks so much for your map and skin; they have really made a difference in my RK experience, and if FC employed 10 of you (or even 1), I believe this game would be much much better.
