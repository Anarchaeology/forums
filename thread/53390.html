Transportation suggestion
I like the cars and yalms, they make single person transport easier to locations. And, I see some professions have nanos to allow them to zone at higher lvls (even for teams too). However, wouldn't it be nice to have...say a troop tranport...to transport low to high lvl teams to various locations. It's funny how the animation that is created about Rubi-ka depicts multiple persons in the same vehicle, but we can't actually do it in the game. This would add the the experience of doing team missions I believe. You know, kinda like getting there is half the fun ( a whole concept missing from game play), or maybe something like a team quest (...maybe a surviellance job, or better yet, a seek and destroy, or a search and abduct...).

I don't think I am the first to ask for more content regarding team or organizationally designed activities. After all, this is a socially driven game correct? Add more socially driven content. Not everyone on AO wants to be a soloist. We just choose to be because theres only so many ways to do a mission with a team before you lose your mind.
