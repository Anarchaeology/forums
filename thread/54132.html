Legion of Fire Stages Attacks Against ICC
Angered by the intrusion of ICC Peacekeepers into clan controlled areas (namely Tir), clanners lead by the Legion of Fire staged a brutal assault against the ICC troops located outside the west gate of Tir.

Soon after the attack leadership within the Legion of Fire issued the following statement:

"Fellow clanners, the time has come to act. Omni-Tek oppression was bad enough, but at least the Tir Accord was in place to protect our sovereign rights as free people! Now the "highly-vaunted" and "all-powerful" ICC has seen it fit to deposit some of its shocktroops within our territory, in fact just outside our capital city, Tir!

"True, the Council of Truth has disbanded and the Council of Ares unsupported by most; but are we to fall under the boot heal of the ultimate conglomerate? They claim to be neutral and here in the name of peace! Peace? They care only for profit! They want the rich notum mines and fields of our world so that their members can remain fat and complacent off the fat of OUR land!

Secretary General Isidra Derousse is 'pleased that the precarious political situation on Rubi-Ka can finally be monitored and controlled.' Do not be convinced by their rhetoric and propaganda. They've come, manipulated by corporations like Sol Banking, to take Rubi-Ka by subversion! Mark our words, in due time our "peace loving protectors from Omni tyranny" will turn on us in the name of their almighty god, money! They already turn a blind eye and let Omni-Tek personnel enter our main city at will!

"Our fathers, mothers, friends, neighbors, and children fought against the oppression of one corporation and won our right to self-government! Are we to throw aside the ideals that our loved ones fought and died for? Are we to once again enter years of oppression by a league of corporations! We, the members of the Legion of Fire, bravely shout 'No! Never Again! Freedom through Victory!'

"Tonight we stood together as one clan trying to make a difference. Trying to honor those who are gone. Tonight we struck back at the planetary invaders. Our pre-emptive strike will resound and issue notice to conquerors and tyrants everywhere that we will no longer be used and exploited, that we are a free people able to govern ourselves and are masters of our own destiny! Brothers and sisters, join us! Rise up and throw off your shackles! Fight back against the tyrants! Peace through Freedom! Freedom through Victory!"
