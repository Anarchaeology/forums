I will bump this thread for the post 14.2 world. Yes, I can do decent damage as a soldier due to me wearing LLTS/running crit buffs.

Once the latter are gone, soldier will fall in with the rest of the non-critical world. Take a look:

Classes with crit buffs:
- MA
- Agent
- Crat
- Adventurer

Classes without one:
- Soldier
- Doctor
- Fixer
- NT
- Engineer
- MP
- Trader
- Enforcer

I don't see a reason why all "combat classes" couldn't get a self only crit buff, which matches but does not stack with the new MA line of external crit buffs (with less ncu requirement )

P.S. I don't want to get into an argument about the definition of a combat class, or that some of the crit nanos for the classes listed above add +1 to crit and cost 48 ncu, and some others will only last 4 seconds. That's not the point, take a look at soldier's superb nano Phalanx, and see why +1 is still better than nothing.
