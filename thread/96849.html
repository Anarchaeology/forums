Flight A-346 Crashes; One Missing
Shuttle flight A-346 crashed upon landing today. The shuttle was traveling from the Morning Star Space Station and contained both new arrivals and others returning to Rubi-Ka from off world assignments. While most of the crew and passengers appeared at the insurance Reclaim without other incidence, there was one passenger on the manifest that is currently unaccounted for.

Tanika "Jasziak" Carnalla, a Clan member of the Mockers, did not appear at reclaim, and no explanation is forthcoming. Jasziaks insurance is still intact and it is confirmed that she boarded the shuttle. Officials on the scene are confused as to her whereabouts and an investigation is taking place. However, due to the amount of resources dedicated towards the Notum Wars at this time, it is likely to be brief.
