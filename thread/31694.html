Explaination

Cannot use until Level X(theoretical) due to title caps.
The level X is a rough estimate of the minimum level needed for the average player to meet the nano requirements by self buffing(+20) + implant bonus(varies with level and scaled up as better implants are available.) The theoretical level is determined by questioning other bureaucrats useing charm based implants with a margin of error.

Pratical usefulness <= Y
This is the max level before the nano is useless for soloing missions of a reasonable diffculty, less than 60% hard. Missions with teams are not accounted for because of the vast increase in mission diffculty.

Next upgrade at Level Z
The level needed to use the next stable charm with self buffing only.

Level gap to next upgrade = W
This level gap is found by (Next upgrade level - Useful max level) to find how many levels the player is left in a "gap" in the psychic check where the current usable stable charm's contribution to the player is neglectable but lack the skill to use the next charm.

Useful up to Level missions
Mission level range at which the charm starts loseing effectiveness to the point of being neglectable.
