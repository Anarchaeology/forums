I know this is constantly being brought up but it is for a reason. Imagine if you told Soldiers they had to re-equip their weapons and rebuff every room in a mission, or an MP to recast their pets, or an Eng to make new robots, or an NT to re-equip all his armor and rebuff..etc..

Why? When Mobs in missions are typically harder than Mobs in the wilderness (at the same level). What is the point?
