Epicene can also just mean "neuter". The word "neuter" is repeatedly used to describe the Atrox.

A neuter doesn't necessarily lack sexual organs. Their sexual organs can simply be non-functional or immature. For instance, a worker ant has undeveloped female sexual organs. She cannot reproduce, and is therefore a neuter. But she is not truly without sex.
