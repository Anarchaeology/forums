Resuscitation Device
I posted this in the Doctors forum as well as the Trader/Engineer inorder to gain support as it not only helps Doctors heal their team but also introduces a new trade skill item that would be in high demand. Post your thoughts about it here please.


I've read ideas where Docs should get a resurrection nano but here is something that is more in-line with Doctors rather than Christ.

First it would require slight alteration of game mechanics as when a character dies they immediately go back to the insurance terminal they saved at. What would be required to make a Resuscitation device work is that a character remains as his unconscious character for the same amount of time it takes for his items to be reconstructed.

So, for the amount of time when a character dies and he/she gets reconstructed at an insurance terminal there should be enough time for a Doctor to use a resuscitation device on the dead/unconscious character.

To resuscitate a Doctor would need a "Resuscitation Device" that depending on QL requires:
Profession: Doctor
Bio Met: Skill dependant on QL
Mat Met: Skill dependant on QL
Nano: Amount dependant on QL

Have the Resuscitation device be effective for characters up to ~150% of the QL of the device or at least enough so that a low level Doctor could resuscitate the highest level character in his team. (as long as the QL of the device was the same level as the Doctor. A bit of lee way with this would be a good thing as you wouldn't want to limit Docs from getting into groups because they were too low, didn't have a resuscitation device or it was too low QL.)

Another aspect of the resuscitation device is that it can only be constructed with trade skills or found in missions.
