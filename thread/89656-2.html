quote:
Originally posted by Shatai
*eyes start widening slightly as he remembers where this is*

I explored out around there a while back, and thought that place was so cool. Nicely done, and a little spooky with the wind and setup that I remember. The only disappointing thing was that there wasn't anything to do out there
Actually, about nine months to a year ago this was a major hunting ground. Teams in their 20s would hunt bot mountain, and when they felt strong enough they would venture northward to the area around the temple to fight the human mobs there -- keeping a wide berth of spiders until they finally were confident enough and strong enough to tackle one. Eventually they would grow strong enough to venture westward to Shades.

The area was so popular that people often went out to the outpost without a team and could count on finding one there.

I miss those days.

A few months ago I took a RL friend out to Temple to show him the ways of outdoor hunting. He totally loved it. With the music providing ambience, the moody lighting of night, the necessity of having situational awareness at all times, the movement of the team not determined by following the next door, and the ability of different professions to more fully show their special talents, it all added up to a very immersive experience.

Frankly I don't know whether I would have stuck with AO for so long if it wasn't for the experiences and variety provided by outdoor hunting like this, from Bot Mountain to Temple to Shades to NW Mines and Nanofreak Valley. Team missions really dealt such hunting a grievous wound, one that boss camps and borgs dropping CDS really hasn't been able to fully heal. The experiences are still out there, I just wish it wasn't so hard to get others to share in them.

Sometimes, when the moon is bright, I'll still wander out solo to some wild corner of the world and listen to the sounds of nature and the music of the night and be transported back to the time that really got me hooked on AO. Kind of interesting being so nostalgic for a time that was less than a year ago.
