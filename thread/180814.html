Other revamps for backpacks
If changes are ever made to backpacks, it would be nice if they could go a bit further than was suggested above. :-)

The labelling on backpacks never really made much sense, except in a few cases like "Implants", "Weapons", etc.. What's really needed is a text field on backpacks, so that you can apply your own label to them. Maybe a color or graphic too, as that may be just adornment but it does help.

Secondly, we've been living with the prehistoric 2x3 window through which we peep at backpack contents for far too long. I expected this to be addressed in the new UI, and was shocked when it wasn't. It's time, please, please, all windows should be expandable.

And finally, the holy grail for AO. Other games fill up backpacks automatically when main inventory becomes full, whereas we have been struggling with stop-go looting forever owing to a non-intelligent inventory/backpack system. AO is meant to be the game to beat them all. We shouldn't have to live with this. :-)

Aye, improving backpacks would be nice, in several areas. Having said that though, this thread is best retained for bumping in about 6 months' time, as sadly the devs have their hands full with a bit of a catastrophe at the moment.
