Ultimate Player Event Closures
I've been thinking a lot about player events, especially since the rather fantastic RP meeting at Reets on sunday. (thanks again to everyone who attended, the discussion was quite enlightening).

One thing that continuously springs to mind is the thought of "ultimate closures". Here's what I mean...

Suppose you want to design a player event, but you want it to end with a bang? Perhaps a massive explosion (visual only, of course, no actual damage done) that shakes the very earth and sends a shockwave out for a good two kilometers. Or perhaps the birth of a dragon from an egg as it sails into the sky and away (obviously, again, illusionary).

Basically, gigantic visual hooks that could take the form of a triggered item or a one-use nano or something, that could be the perfect dramatic ending to a pre-organized player event.

The question that automatically arises is: Would this be abused and get old real fast? Well, not if it's kept constrained. Perhaps make such items insanely expensive. Or better yet, require a particular form of petitioning in order to receive them. Or perhaps an extensively dramatic construction routine in order to build one.

Any revisions or ideas on this? Anyone?
