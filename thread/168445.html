Well some notes for the World Design team if this ever gets to them.

The scope of the project is entirely up to them. A lot could be done with minor changes though and without creating any new art.

Specifics:
Major Clan NPCs: This is one of the major changes I'm asking for probably. I'd just like to get some story NPCs in Old Athen for the major clan factions. Newbies come there of course all the time from West Athen but Old Athen doesn't really introduce you to anything Clannish. With the restoration project its a good time to throw in some NPCs that just talk about their major Clan. NPCs to talk about the Knights, Sentinels, Eco Warriors, etc and promote what they've done (or if as placeholders what they intend to do) for the project and their Clan's ideology. This would help all players to get to know those Clans. Old Athen should be the properly diverse area to put them in and a realistic recruiting area. Giving them a couple of faction guards of their own might help people recognize the look of those Clans too.

Insignias and Statues: Please remove or coverup some of the old Omni-Tek insignias, statues, etc. Its a clan city and we'd like to make it look line one. For instance instead of the statues on the north end holding OT corporate logos, maybe we could get placard walls like those found at West Athen or at the Clan Athen Shire outposts with slogans on them? Going the extra mile you could redo some of the architecture in Knight, Sentinel, Eco Warrior themes. Eco Warrior being the hardest because we have no buildings of theirs. But its a good step in preperation for "Towns of Rubi-Ka" right? Old Athen could be the testing ground. New statues of Clan figures with informative plaques would be nice too. Statues of Clan leaders past and present so people become familiar with the Leaders of the Clan struggle.

Rubble and Reconstruction: The rubble in East Athen is excessive. I know its supposed to add to a dangerous missioning atmosphere but there is too much of it and could be improved. Even if you do need the obstacles, it would be a lot more Clannish and would aid the "Restoration" if you replaced them with something else. The Eco Warriors might want to put in a lot of plants or even a park (though the area could still remain Shady). Maybe even attract some Reets or something? Oh, and a Mercury Dragon statue might be really nice in honor of that old Clan and their first project. If I was to request one new thing, that would be it. You could replace the bird statue on the platform south of OA grid with it maybe.

Defense: The Clans should shore up their defense on the eastern side. Those defenses should have been rebuilt long ago. Just a few cannons would be nice and might scare off some Omnis who would loiter around OA too much. Make the eastern entrance to Old Athen look a little more like a warzone with active defenses in place.

I think this could really become a great project and series of events for Clan players. Maybe even some projects on Omni side (opening up some of those closed establishments in Omni-Ent or Canary lounge?) could similarly be done.

-----------------------------------------------------

For the Events team

This project could have a ton of good events behind it. Just having people go around and get feedback about the city could be a good event with Greenies. People would love to have a green listening ear.

Other things could demonstrate the need for some changes. Attacks from Wartorn demonstrating the need for better security. Nasty critters like rollerrats boiling up from the slums. All kinds of stuff. I'm sure it would be easy to drag it on for months.

So hopefully this idea will get seriously considered by whoever makes these decisions.
