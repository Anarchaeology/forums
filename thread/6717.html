I actually like being able to dye armor in DAOC. I would love to have dark blue elite to match my MP shield. More individuality makes RPGs interesting.

In Redmoon, you can change your hair color and your clothes with hair dye and clothes dye.

Very cool I think.

Unfortunately, from what was explained previously on the forums, AO's database system doesn't doesn't really allow "modifers" to an item. The only visible "modifier" an item has is it's quality level.

Each and every item must be uniquely described in the game database. So to have different armors that you can "color", requires # of armors * # of pieces * number of possible colors that can be changed.

Look at the database for implants. There is one implant entry in the database for every possible combination of clusters. The database for implants has thousands of entries, for every possible implant and every possible combination of clusters.

Not very flexible. Probably the same reason why being able to "rename" your backpack will never be a possibility.
