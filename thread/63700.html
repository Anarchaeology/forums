New Suggestion, Banks
Just a small feature request, I would like to be able to deposit items in another characters Bank. I know that transfer is posible using another player but its annoying to find that perfect item for an alt then have to remember where the alt is, move there, find a player willing to wait for you to log out & then in again.
so some sort of bank branch or bureau de change booth that will allow deposits into the other characters bank would solve that issue. just walk into the bank enter the alt's name and stick in the item
what do you all think?
