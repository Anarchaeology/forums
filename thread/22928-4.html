First of all, sure its a war bla bla bla... but its pathetic that Clan actually go to such lengths to legally gank people. I tried to attack one or two guys, but ended up by being ganked by 2 people minimum. I'm sorry guys, but doing so isn't earning any credit it my book.

In the earlier days you could attack 1 guy and the guy either had to run, you killed him or he killed you. And a bunch of people could still attack you if you stood in the 25% zone, but if you zoned out on the other hand, you were safe and could attack only 1 person.

Today I experienced something I will call devastation of PVP, totally. First Storm lay out a bait, I actually was asked by a level 113 (51 levels below me) NT from Storm that wanted me to duel. Just seconds before I saw Wenzell, Hratgard, Punkbum and Wolfe pass by me and enter mmd 'city'. And they were sneaking behind two buildings which was easily to see from where I stood.

The omnis that were there decided to make a go for it, and they first did a mass root and shot us down one by one. They could do this because they were all teamed. They actually got people to go around and check wether or not we were teamed.

It's not strategy nor protection of any kind, it's 100% ganking, legally. Which is silly, I actually were far from the 25% zone when I got attacked by 2 people which of course rooted me and ran away so they could pick me out at a greater distance. Gee that takes soo much skill, I hope you're proud.

However, this had actually somewhat a positive effect and actually this went from being a sad pvp thing to being fun again. After a couple of trying and failing we made a team and tried to do the same to them as they did to us. So we created a team of 6 and did the same thing. At first we didn't succeed, but we actually managed to kill some people, and the clanners went. They didn't dare to be bait again.

The drawback however when we were teamed was that one of my teammates died and was killed by the spawn over and over, until he ended up in omni ent. So White whatever member of storm level 150ish do us a favour and put a bullet through your computer.

I went from apprentice to Rookie again, but I don't even care, having a higher title often means that you go first.

The point I'm trying to make is that Clan and especially the guild Storm should reconsider their approach on MMD. Teaming in mmd where you can attack people that attack your team only ruins pvp. If you continue doing so, you will probably find MMD empty of omni one day. And that my dear clanners will be your fault.

Especially when you're using a low level bait, that asks everyone for a duel. There was even one episode where one accepted because he was the same level as the NT. And whoops Wenzell killed him before the NT could even get a shot at him. And they had this 'act' where the NT said something about Wenzell and that they were in the same guild etc. Just an act.

Now I'm prolly going to be ignored and/or flamed by what I now think is even more childish Storm members. I'm sorry, but you haven't proven worthy of being classified as adults, yet. I knew that some Omni are worse than others to gank people, but after today Storm is the winner.
