Sure thing :

1) Terminals to report not only "offical" news but un-official news (player x takes down dragon in Camelot dungeon, etc). There could be public terminals and apartment terminals. To use the analogy of AD&D once again, it would be like having your DM say "Go play chapter 1 of Buldar's Gate and tell me how it comes out". There is little immersion as it is now. If it happens in-game, tell us about it in-game. Hell, he UO did this quite well with town criers...

2) Again, notes or some other way to pass information from one person to another. Not IMing as we have it, but more like a system of e-mail. That way, you would be able to keep a copy of what you are being told. Sure, I can say "hey, what's your e-mail address" (or RP a nice alternative term), but, again, I want these things to happen in-game. Most other RPGs have, at the least, books or memos to pass information back and forth...

3) Player generated missions. When implemented fully, they will be nothing more than fed-ex missions (to use an age-old RPG term). I'm not asking to allow every player to become a DM, but if we can't change the world in even the slightest ways (and we can't), what's the point?

4) Pre-generated missions with meaning. C'mon, be honest. I realize that your character (at least the one you're posting under) is level 25, but trust me, there's just so many times you can read "Dalph Dunderhead has generated Perma-Kill weapons!". Oh no, not again. To estimate, I would say that my characters have killed hundreds of terrorists, repaired countless radar facilities, and obtained enough "prototype-super-secret-green-cloaks" to choke a herd of starved cows. If the Dust Brigade is causing havok in Tir this week, shouldn't the missions be "Captain Blackknee of the Dust Brigade has been found snooping in the sewers of Tir! He will be there for 8 hours. Kill him quickly!" (just an example, and probably not a good one at that, but you get the point, I think). You would then get mission regarding the Dust Brigade. Are their scripting skills really this bad? Once again, immersion that follows the story-line...

5) Mechanizms for playing the game as something other than "a guy or gal who kills something". Think of the real world. We're at war right now. Is every American on the front lines? Of course not. And to be true, non-killing-type-folk advance in their proffessions just as well on the homefront. I certainly don't want to d-click my shovel and click a rock all day long, but why is there no in-game vehicle for spying, crafting, or reporting (see issue #1) to name a few. We do, however, have vehicles for dancing and drinking...Yahoo...

So, again, yes, I can walk around all day saying "I'm spying on you...Boo!", but we all know that you can just click the guy and target it. ...and besides, if you really didn't want to be "heard", you would just go to guildchat.

I hope this short list helps.

There is little-to-no immersion in this game.

They have presented a great "game". There are lots of pretty background, and lots of stuff to kill (...and kill, and kill, and kill). However, this game lacks the proper TOOLs to allow for reasonable role-playing.

Heh, the unfortunate thing is that #5 is officially out. Funcom has stated in an official statement that non-kill leveling is 'no longer envisioned'. Honestly, I can't imagine how you could have this feature in any level based game. In a skill based game like UO, it is rather easy, and in AC's type of skill/level hybrid it wasn't completely impossible (due to the monarchy system). In AO's skill/level hybrid, I personally can't think of a way they could shoehorn this in. Especially not in a way that wouldn't alienate all the people who have already leveled up by killing stuff, at the expense of all skills unnecessary for killin'. :) I do agree that it would be kinda cool to have that though in theory.

#4 would be cool, and as far as I know is supposed to be happening eventually, although most of the stuff I've seen FC talk about has been sort of PVP oriented. Being able to capture and hold territory, etc. I can see having this type of stuff implemented possibly helping #3 as well, as it is at least possible in theory to drop a character's saved info icon thingy into a player made mission for assasination type missions. I don't pvp much, but I don't mind it really either.

#3 I don't agree that we can't effect the world. Players effect the world all the time. The problem is (and it's a problem with any mmog) not EVERY character can effect the world all the time. Not everyone can be a hero, etc. If everyone is a hero, then there is no such thing as a hero. I have been to many of the FC run events, and if you follow those things, players effect the shifting story all the time. It does happen a bit slowly though, and to me could be expanded a great deal while still rewarding the people who work at it nicely.

#2 Passing information is cool, but I don't understand how it's particularly vital to roleplay. Offline messages are as good as email, as you can tell someone who is online whatever it is that you have to say right to thier face, right? Maybe I'm just missing the point on this one and you should expound a bit more.

#1 I think FC is moving in the right direction on this. They added the news channel, so it's there, but unfortunately they are not really utilizing it all that much yet. I think it would be great if they tie in-game events to this channel to be put on the every-20-minute rotation like they were doing during the end of the amnesty. However, I wouldn't like to see it become too spammy. A news terminal would be cool too, maybe it could be like a mission booth, but when you shift click on the icons (news items) the text popup would just show you the news story? That sounds fun, as well as avoidable if you aren't into that kinda stuff, which is also important to some.

Poor Griemy, only level 24. The squibber wielding nano trader didn't work out as well as I'd hoped. LOL. Variety is the ****e of life! Still my favorite character name though, and one that I used years ago in MUDs a lot back in da day. :) Believe me though, I've done many many thousands of missions. I could use a bit more ****e in them, but for the most part I just sort of make up whatever situation I want for it, I rarely read the mission briefings. I guess I'm just a sort of 'in my head I'm doing this' kinda guy, it doesn't bother me too much.
