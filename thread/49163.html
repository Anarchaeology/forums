the mcs is a good main weapon for a number of reasons.
very nice ATR.
not bad crits and good normal damage.
does chemical damage so its good against people with imp proj bracers (not many have chem ones on as first choice )
with TIMs scope fires at 1/1.
fast burst recycle and quite high bursts (i hit something for 4060 burst once with it).
it is also good due to its low requirements in terms of trader debuffs. u should be able to stay at 100% or at lowest if u unlucky 75% being fully debuffed.

the CHS is quite good for alphas. better than the flash atm it seems cuase most people have energy bracers as first choice. plus the lower ATR usually results in cak FAs.
CHS is not the best choice for main weapon imo.
first of all u got less ATR than any other class cuase they can wear kheelar sleeves.
debuffs from traders will effect u "alot" more than if u were using a mcs.
u have to self equip a lower ql than anyone else. having it as your main weapon and not being able to self equip (e.g. ql 200) means u are stuck with 1 damage type. oponent changes bracers in current setup and u fecked.
u gotta remain flexible and able to change gun and damage type depending on the situation.
