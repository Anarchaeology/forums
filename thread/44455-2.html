I think that the definition of what is or is not "political" is getting us sidetracked from the business of inconsistency when it comes to neutrals in the 25% zones, but anyway...

Saying that a person is not political because they do not belong to a political organisation is akin to saying that a person is not religious because they do not belong to an organised church. A person may hold deep-seated political beliefs, but belong to no political organisation because one does not exist that espouses their beliefs. That individual is perforce "neutral" even though they may be actively political in guild life, for instance.

You can make the argument that Omni-Tech and the Clans are political organisations, but you can't argue that any individual is or is not political on the grounds of their membership or non-membership of either of these organisations.


The inconsistency issue is more important. Either neutral aren't political, in which case they should not con as hostile, or they are, in which case they should be able to attack faction characters. The argument that neutrals should simply stay out of 25% zones is facile and simplistic, since a high proportion of neutral missions are in the 25% zones. As I've said before, I'd rather have a mission to a 0% zone than a 25% zone, since my chances of survival are actually higher there.
