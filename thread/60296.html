I did a quick comparrison of damage to see how the new weapons fare against the old ones. While I don't see any new Piercing weapons. I am hopeful they will be added to give us a full set of melee choices.

For all my comparrisons, I used numbers similar to my current numbers on init and attack ratings & of course I matched numbers between like weapons.

Damage comparrison

The almighty Beam
Your critical hit will be 3667 points.
Your normal hits will maybe vary between 117 and 1833 points.
Over 10 seconds you would maybe do an average of 6004 points of damage. (2.73 normal, 0.91 critical hits)

New 2h blunt Staffs
Your critical hit will be 3533 points.
Your normal hits will maybe vary between 177 and 2649 points.
Over 10 seconds you would maybe do an average of 4995 points of damage. (1.93 normal, 0.64 critical hits)

These have a slightly lower crit, but better normal damage & you get sneak/fast attack .
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Premium Rider Warblade ...looks great on paper
Your critical hit will be 2342 points.
Your normal hits will maybe vary between 41 and 1870 points.
Over 10 seconds you would maybe do an average of 6513 points of damage. (3.75 normal, 1.25 critical hits)

New 2h edge swords
Your critical hit will be 2768 points.
Your normal hits will maybe vary between 169 and 1688 points.
Over 10 seconds you would maybe do an average of 6944 points of damage. (3.75 normal, 1.25 critical hits)

No HW reqs comapared to Rider and Specials + 75-100 hp bonus.
I didn't even bother to compare to EoT since it is so hard to get.
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Quality SSC Byom Blade
Your critical hit will be 2835 points.
Your normal hits will maybe vary between 34 and 1654 points.
Over 10 seconds you would maybe do an average of 5977 points of damage. (3.34 normal, 1.11 critical hits)

New 1h edge swords
Your critical hit will be 2363 points.
Your normal hits will maybe vary between 34 and 1688 points.
Over 10 seconds you would maybe do an average of 6183 points of damage. (3.75 normal, 1.25 critical hits)

Smaller Crit, slightly better regular damage, a bit faster & no HW req. Add Sneak for an extra attack. And you can duel wield 2 of the new ones.
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Premium Native Alloy Staff
Your critical hit will be 2529 points.
Your normal hits will maybe vary between 4 and 1293 points.
Over 10 seconds you would maybe do an average of 5595 points of damage. (3.75 normal, 1.25 critical hits)

New 1h blunt
i used the Schiacciamento Tuono since it has the highest crit mod
Your critical hit will be 2190 points.
Your normal hits will maybe vary between 71 and 1413 points.
Over 10 seconds you would maybe do an average of 5520 points of damage. (3.75 normal, 1.25 critical hits)

Normal damage better than Alloy but crit is lower.. Very comparable to Allow and you can duel wield it with ease.
I didn't even bother to compare to Bloodbat since it is so hard to get.
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Overall, I am very pleased with the upcoming weapon choices we will have..
