One robot does not matter. One notum mine does not matter. Nor does one gram of notum exported from Rubi-Ka matter. The bond between the BORPAC organisations goes deeper then mere superficialities or, as suggested here, pragmatic alliances.

What the members of BORPAC recognise and have to chosen to fight is the unthinking, unrestrained and unrelenting forces of Progress and Capitalism which are willing to plunge the people into a social, moral and ecological nightmare for their short-term gain. The industrial system, the unmitigated greed of those in power, will condemn us to lose not just our livelihoods, but our very souls. We are the few who chose to say "No More" and in time we will grow stronger and more numerous.

In conclusion, before dismissing the Neo-Luddite Movement as "ah, the ones the hate robots", I invite you to either read our Manifesto or save your comments for the sports pages of newsfeeds.
