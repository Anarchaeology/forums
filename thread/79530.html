As posted on the test server patch notes:

quote:
General Changes:
-Rubi-Ka is a desert planet. The days can get hot and sunny, while nights can get real chilly. A cloak, perhaps one of the new hats or a pair of sunglasses are types of gear you can wear to protect you against the climate. A new vendor is in town..

Playfield Changes:
-New Dungeon: Will To Fight. Geared towards PvP gameplay. The dungeon contains three tunnels that come together in a central cave. The cave is called "Gnuff's Lair" after the creature that lives there. Gnuff has a crystal with special powers... Entrance to the new dungeon is in the old mining outpost east of Last Ditch in Stret West Bank.
So, some of the shades (maybe the Arcanum ones), cloaks, and hats (maybe Straw Hat, Repairman's, Salesman's) looks like they'll be on a vendor. Test server folks should probably be able to confirm that.

Gnuffs 1 Minute Crystal of Rift Power looks like it will drop off the "boss" in the cave... If it's a PvP cave, the crystal would be pretty useful, as it randomly casts Shadow Rift stuff. (Basically the AO version of Quad Damage )
