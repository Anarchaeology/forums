marriages in game and why they are fun Rp
I guess your question is really about why people find marriages fun to RP.

I think there are a number of reasons:

playing in an online world the player contract between table-toppers or the rules-contract for live players doesn't exist - so the basis for consensus for RP is sketchy at best... its very easy and safe to establish that consensus with just 1 person - therefore relationships are one of the easiest storylines to RP.

Marriages are also a nice obvious event that brings a guild and groups of friends together - to some extent it may be about attention - but in another sense its a simple boost to a reason for a party.

as for whether they last or not - I think that part of the difficulty is that "happy ever after" is the end of the story - and requires quite sophisticated RP to play unless it is part of a real relationship too... so there's the whole boy meets girl girl meets girl whatever thing - some betrayal along the way maybe and a bit of dithering and dallying - then finally the marriage - then poof - what next... as well as all the RL reasons people sometimes leave the game

I don't think it is going to be setting a trend for younger people believing marriage can work like that - I think its one of the most evident RP situations least subject to confusion (an IC relationship actually risks more danger of confusion than an IC marriage in my opinion)

As for why do all couples storylines tend towards marriage in this game - I guess that has a number of reasons - for its fun to RP something I would never dream of doing in real life (getting married that is) for others I suspect it has to do with the fact that we are not far from the storytale trope "happy ever after" and we will grab it whenever we can - so if there is a RP relationship of course we'll get married - cos we can RP being a couple in certain everlasting love maybe much easier we can ever consider that kind of love, and we still see marriage as that symbol...

just a few thoughts

I think that a reason things go wrong with some marriages is that people DO forget that there are real people on the other end. As Nevver said. People will cheat, will cyber with other characters... and even tell their "wives" or "husbands" about it.

I also know that I have recently found that a lot of female players I have run across admit to being male in real life. Now, this would be a pretty sobering fact if you were a male on the other end as well.. or a female looking for a REAL female companion. It gives the whole situaion a rather sour aftertaste.

Also, it is a game... people like to experience all aspects of the game. They want to know what everything is like... they want to know what marriage is like, even if they aren't really committed to it. Just to say.. "Wow! I got married"

It could also be more personal matters... like, for instance:
Say a girl lives with her boyfriend in real life.. her boyfriend doesn't play the game, but she does. She meets a guy/girl in game and wants to marry them. They get married. The RL boyfriend finds out and has a spaz attack, talking about cheating and lying... So, they get divorced in game. Things like that.
