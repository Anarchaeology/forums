OOC: A personal thank you to all
Effective today I will no longer be the coordinator over the Reporters Program.

In my time as the Coordinator over the Reporters program I wish I could say it has been easy. Finding qualified writers and editors has been (and will always be) a challenge and various other issues have been obstacles I have had to overcome.

I worked to introduce the program as its own entity, and I have worked to gain respect for the program with active and occasional role players alike. I have streamlined the forms and administration portion of the program, and I have worked to even coverage across both dimensions with editing and version control tool upgrades, per dimension in addition to per faction coverage, and better record keeping. It is my hope that standardized writing and community interaction training will continue to create better reporters in the future.

Now it is time for me to move on. While I feel that I am an ok administrator, I have never been a good role player. With the many players I have talked to online in my time as coordinator, you have all probably noticed this. An ARK who is not an active role player (in an online role playing game no doubt!) really has no place in being the head of a program based on role play interaction. What this program really needs now is many people who have the love for role play in their hearts. I feel I have done my job as an administrator, and now it is time for these role players to build the program and make the magic happen. It is this reason that I have decided to move on from this position for one that I feel my personal talents and abilities would be better suited for.

I am very happy to announce that the program is now in good hands. Guardian Aimeh is stepping up to be the prime administrator of the program, and along with guidance from the CR director Xnemth many new and exciting ideas are being implemented. On my way out of the grid channel that reporters communicate with each other in, I was amazed at the flurry of activity surrounding the reporters program that was going on. I expect great things from their combined leadership, and I hope you will all be as candid and open with them as you were with me.

I want to thank all the role players of AO who gave me input and insight on the program. I find that you all are a great group of people, and I know you will continue to support the volunteer efforts of this program.

I am changing departments as I will be moving over to the test server to work with the ARK Bug Hunters group. I value the time I had working with other ARK, fan sites, and role players as the Reporters Coordinator, and I look forward to seeing the future of this program.

Thank you all.
