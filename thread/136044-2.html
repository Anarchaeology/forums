There's certainly an important point in all that from Bio: It's not just about high level content. It's much deeper than that. It's about a profession that has an incomplete toolset at all levels whether you look at them as PvP specialists, PvM specialists or Tradeskillers or from any point of view.

The MP profession is a profession of "almosts". We're almost a good PvP profession - but not quite. We almost have good attack pets but not quite. We're almost great debuffers - but not quite. We're almost a good PvM profession - but not quite. We "almost" have good tradeskills etc etc

The changes to the MP profession have also been "almosts". The evo was "almost" a real boost. The Urn pets are "almost" a really good change for us. The pet root/snare breakers were "almost" worth casting rather than just recasting pets etc. The MP armour was "almost' useful. Even the creations are "almost' worthwhile....

One of the great frustrations of the MP community is that many of us can see a lot of very small changes which are clearly in line with the direction FC would like us to go. But every time a change has come in the recent past, it has carried with it some downside.

The AMS buff comes but takes away our casting time and makes animas useless - so we ask for the same AMS buff for 15-30 mins without the damage add. That way we get the same buff benefit and the animas stay useful. No increase in power, no super extras - just a slight change in design concept that makes the change actually fix the problem it was designed to address - PvP AR and high level PvM content.

The pet root/snare breakers come - but with a long casting time that means we can recast and rebuff our pets faster... so we ask for an all pets one or a faster casting one....

The MP specific armour comes - in three pieces with benefits that don't really fit the needs of the MP and so we ask for NCU (please god) and HP... not a +4 to nanoskills for a profession that self-buffs +211.... or even (still) a +40 swim buff. Pleas god let that swim buff actually make a difference in Shadowlands cos it's just a joke right now...

Let's not even go down the robinson crystal road...

From our perspective we know that there must be some good designer thought behind these things - but every time there's been a change, it's been "nearly and not quite" to the extent that it hasn't fixed what it seemed to want to fix but left us with yet more content or tools that are 'half' useful like the debuffs.... There is something at the very base root of what FC thinks the MP is that just isn't quite right. It's not far wrong - but it just isn't quite right either.

MPs have a potentially great overall toolset - but it just doesn't quite gel into being a proficient profession. It's partly that "nearly but not quite" element that is so frustrating to dedicated MPs because it holds such promise but never quite pays off. Just like the urn pets are shaping up to do once again. They are "almost" excellent pets that could "almost" fix many MP issues - but it's looking more and more likely that they'll fall by the wayside as have so many things in the past for the MP for want of a tweak and a small change here and there.
