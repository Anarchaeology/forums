SERIOUS REQUEST: 0% (zero percent gas) mission option

I think you can all see where I am going here. The bottom line rule is that you can't attack a teammate in any gas level. What better way to have team battles between guilds than allow the mission creator to make the mission 0%. Guild leaders can then PLAN PvP events between teams.

I hope to see incredible support for this from all parties. The only way in is with a key or an engineer. Have the engi summon one team deep into mission with a goal to get out alive and another team enter the front with a goal to wipe out the escapees.. ETC ETC ETC.

Not just teams either. When in 14.2 players can actually concealy themselves(heh) you could pit two agents against each other in a scary game of hide and seek.

Did I just say a place where people could PvP without the twinks raiding and ganking? OMG!!! NO WAY!! :-) :-)
