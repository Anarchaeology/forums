Directions to Club Relax
To get to Club Relax, leave the Omni Entertainment east gate, and once outside the electric fence, turn south and follow it a short while. The club is in a small building with a neon sign stating "Relax". If you are clan, then the guard is going to shoot at you. If you don't have a Yalm, I don't recommend trying to pass through the gate on foot. Best bet it to see if an Engineer will warp you in.
