Idea For Apartments
I dont know if this is new or not but i had an idea. People all want to be able to put sofas and tables and other solid objects in their apts but cant. What if there was a store that sold keys to "stock" apts? You could choose from several apts, pre furnished with solid objects, then add your own touches (paintings, vases, plants etc). Just let the players select what type of apt they want instead of having empty rooms. Would that be hard to implement?
