What it mostly comes down to, is the fact this is a Roleplaying game (that may come as a shock to some of you ). Due to this, your characters should really have no idea about each other. Now bearing this in mind, they should react to each other like you react to any other character controled by someone else. so, say you where playing an enforcer when you came upon a grid armour MKII disk. In all honesty would you:
a: give it away free to some random fixer
b: Sell it for the 25million credits you can probably get for it

I know people in guilds are an expection to this, but if you are in a big guild you probably dont need to swap that much anyway.

This is the way things are handled in most pen and paper roleplaying games. If your charcter is know for giving things away, the GM's usually let you do it betweenyour characters, if they arent (ie they are like 99.999% of players) they arent. Since this game doesnt have enough GM's to know what we all are like, I agree that it should be difficult to swap things between your characters.
