quote:
Originally posted by Moonie2

Is there anything I should add to Locations? Is it easy to get any of those valuable items in Athen or Tyr?



What did you set in the mision sliders for highest chance of getting (for example the concrete cushions)?
There is occasionally some belief that mission sliders would affect the reward offered, but I don't think it is true.



The main thing you should be on the lookout for is when the find item is a useless trinket like "Urgent Sensitive Information" or "Art Container" or "Weird looking bomb." That usually happens for missions where the Good/Bad slider tends toward Good. When that happens you are depriving yourself of the additional possibilities of matching your search items, and you should adjust the sliders until you are getting find items that are normal, usable items.
