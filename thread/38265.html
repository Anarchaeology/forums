After a year of AO, it's gotten to be a little tedious.
Ever since I bought the game on June 27th, 2001 I've played a clan character. I've canceled my account a few times, but I always come back for a month or so to see how the game's doing and if any content has been added that would make the game to be less tedious than it is. I'm just wondering if I made an omni character if that would bring some of the joy back to the game that I haven't felt in over nine months. When AO first came out, it was a blast exploring places and just learning about the mechanics of the game. But now if I want to learn anything about the game I can just come to the game's message boards where there's a plethora of information at my fingertips. Some nights I've just came to the message boards and read for a few hours and not even logged in the game.

Basically, I need to restore some of the umph that made AO stand out to me a year ago. Would making an omni-tek character do that for me? Any opinions?

If you would like to enjoy the game once again...try and make a char (Neutral/Clan/Omni) and see about joining a guild or do things on your own and explore the vast expanse called Rubi-Ka.
