Mail Order?
I love your idea about mail order, Seid. I've had similar thoughts myself.

Perhaps the way to implement it is along the lines of a "delivery warehouse" since there would need to be a new trade-interface added to the game I think. All the General stores have had that "shop-of-fortune' added so that seems like the logical location. You deposit your item along with the recepient's name and the required price (COD, right?). When the item is claimed your account is creditted.

Since I'm sure there would need to be some limit on how long the server maintains a record of the deposit, at the end of a set period (perhaps a week? or determined by item QL?) if it has NOT been claimed, you are credited with what you would have gotten selling to a standard vendor/machine. Or perhaps you are given the option of going and REclaiming it yourself.

The machine when accessed could give a list of items you had deposited, and items deposited for you, waiting to be claimed along with the credit cost. Perhaps the price to retrieve an item before the potetial buyer does, or the 'holding' time expires, could be set equal to its sale price to a vendor This would prevent truly rare items from being destroyed due to careless commitments, but prevent the warehouse from becoming simply a way to extend your bank size.

It would take some work to implement, but simply adding a new type of machine seems much simpler than many of Funcom's other projects. And its potential benefit is enormous.
