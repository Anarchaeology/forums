Blade Corp. Press Release
Blade Corp. official press release:

Today CEO of the corporation Major Shadowolf Marone has announced the official grand opening of Blade Corp. branch on Rubi-Ka. While the organization has been present on this planet for a long time now establishing its presence, only now it is officially open for business, offering its services and employment opportunities to citizens of Rubi-Ka.
Blade Corp. is an inner-galactic mercenary hyper-corporation with branches all over the Universe. Now it is available on this planet as well. Blade Corp. is officially neutral in the conflict, but to better suit the needs of its costumers and employees has three divisions: Neutral, Omni-Tek and Clan. This allows citizens of all kinds to enjoy great employment opportunities offered by Blade Corp. and its costumers can choose from the variety of services from the variety of organization members that would fulfill any of their needs. Blade Corp has a large number of departments each with a specific cause, such as research and development, medical services, manufacturing, protection and escort, military forces, special forces and many more. For more information about Blade Corp. please visit our HQ located at www.bladecorp.com.
