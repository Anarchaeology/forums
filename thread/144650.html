SOL Banking Corporation Weapon Designer goes AWOL.
Fresh from the rumor mill

Circle G's interplanetary intelligence network has intercepted communications indicating that one of SOL Banking Corporations most promising weapon designer has gone mission over the past few days. SOL Banking suspects that he is trying to escape contractual obligations and sell SOL Banking Intellectual Property to a competing Hyper Corporation.

When pressed on the issue, SOL Banking representatives denied all speculation, but security at key SOL Banking checkpoints has been increased over the past few days.

Circle G believes that this man is armed and dangerous, and is thus issuing this general alert to anyone transporting people to Rubi-Ka or any other Omni controlled space, as the rivalry between Omni-Tek and SOL Banking would make Omni space an ideal hiding place.
