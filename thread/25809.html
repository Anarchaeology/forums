What makes Funcom hate players?
Thursday night, I'm running along in West Athen, & I see some guy get shot down. No big deal; I figured someone's killing guards again for laughs, so I keep running by.

All of a sudden, I get critically hit from behind for 1250 points of damage. Oooo-kay... I run into the store. Stores are safe places, right?

Wrong.

Mr. GM-Controlled Masked Commando FOLLOWS me, zoning into the advanced store, & continues to press the attack. Once again, he lands a second crit for exactly 1250 points. Knowing full well that I woundn't survive a third (I had no buffs running because I was in town), I run for the door again, hoping to make it to the 100% surpression apartment area (not sure if it would've made any difference, but what the hell, right?).

I died mid-zone, outside the store, losing my pvp title & 300K exp. 1250-point crit between zones. The exp I could care less about; that's easy to replentish. The title is what pissed me off, as meaningless as it is. After level 75, no way I'm getting that back... not unless I gank/grief-kill other players (not my idea of fun), or wait until I'm level 150 or higher.

The GM playing the Masked Commando is NOT a player. He does not have an account. He does not adhere to the same rules, IP & experience systems, or use the same available equipment as what is dictated to players.

Most importantly, he does not adhere to the supression-gas scheme. Do players initiate combat with other players in 75% zones? ...no? So how can this, in any way, be considerd pvp (thereby impacting pvp rank)?

I presented this arguement in a very civil manner, to "Hadrulf", the GM who responded to my petition. He did make it clear to me from his opening remarks, that he could do nothing to change my prior pvp win/loss setting. The discussion went in circles for five minutes or so... he would apologize, saying: events-will-not-be-like-that-ever-again-blah-blah-blah...

...then I would respond, NO! You're missing the point; I don't care that I died! But, is death induced by a non-player-GM intended to effect pvp rank? Or is this a bug? Or should every pvp-player simply type /terminate as soon as the green names are spotted, just to be on the safe side? (<--a rather crappy way to encourage pvp folks to role-play, & role-players to pvp, if you ask me...)

I didn't get any strait answers from him, except that "it was being looked at" & that he had "to finish up with this because it's the end of my shift".

{T}Bleeder, I wish I had gotten the same GM you had, who would've told me:

"They generally will not attack you unless you first engage."

After witnessing that GM plinking away groups of rez-sick people who were SITTING DOWN NEXT TO RECLAIM (whom he had just killed prior)... hearing something like that would have made me livid.

I suppose someone at Funcom needs to sit down with a few of their employees & explain to them the differences between "ENGAGING" and "SITTING DOWN NEXT TO RECLAIM". Right now, the finer details seem to be a bit blurred to certain Funcom GMs.

To any other players out there who suffer from rez sickness, or sit helplessly next to reclaim, take caution... because apparently SOMEONE at Funcom just HATES that sort of thing, and wants to shoot you for a fixed amount of damage (say... 1250 points, perhaps). Or, they're simply perceiving it as an attack.
