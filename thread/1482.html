Shegrath, the term "reroll" refers to creating a brand new character from scratch. I *think* the term comes from the old pencil-and-paper RPGs where one would roll a die to determine starting stats for a character. In online games, people who "reroll" are creating a new character, typically of the same profession (to do better stat allocation the second time around).

So yes, you can do this whenever you want (per Yazule's instructions ). If you really like your current character's name and want to reuse it, you'll have to delete your current character, but otherwise, you can have both your original and rerolled characters.
