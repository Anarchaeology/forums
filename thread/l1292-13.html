Changing sides will have some time restrictions on your alignment bonuses based on your level. Higher level characters may have to wait 30 playing hours before they can take advantage of the bonus attributed to their new side even while living without the old bonus. People probably won't be changing back and forth on a whim.

1. are there level limits for pvp?
As far as I know, yes.

2. how do skills like tutoring work?

Buy a tutoring chip. Find someone who has the skill you want raised to the degree you wish to match and their Tutoring skill raised high enough to use the chip. Activate the chip and then use any unexpended IP to raise the skill just as you would if you could do so on your own.

3. can everyone create missions for other players? or only the bureaucrat? do we give our own xp and $$ as the reward for those missions?
Anyone can create missions.
The two types you can create are assassinations and FedEx item.
You have to have a ref of whomever or whatever you wish to kill.
You need to be able to pick up the item you want so you can drop it temporarily in the mission creation window....doing so will create a ref of it. Item refs can then be tweaked to indicate the quality level of the item that must obtained an you can specify a range.

If I want a Trader to get me an upgrade of my sword, I would drop the sword in the Ref slot of my Mission Creation window. This would leave a ref/icon of the type of sword I have. Then I would adjust the quality level. I could specify that obtaining one between QLs 40-50 would complete the mission. I would then set the time limit in RL days and hours. Set xp, credits and/or drop in item rewards. Next type in written instructions, if any.
Finally, hit the create button and trade the mission ref with the Trader.

Yes. it is your own stuff that you give as rewards.

4. the grid sounds like a fairly small area...what happens if all 50k people happen to try to enter it? or does it spawn versions of itself like the newbie play areas?
It's a playfield. I'm sure it could hold a few hundred people.
Access is restricted by the Computer Literacy skill. Levels are likely separated into zones.
50k pwople will be split among Dimensions. And there is slim chance that all of the people would ever be at the Grid at the same time. You can't level there. And whompas and other modes of travel are perfectly fine. Whompas are quicker...and you won't have to worry about falling of bridges or navigating the grid.

5. when in a group, can the members automatically follow their leader?
I dunno.

6. are they still going to have that profession-changing machine? (bad idea i think) if so, how will it work and what are its restrictions?
Maybe in an expansion. There will be restrictions. They will only implement this after in depth consideration of possible exploits.
It won't be in at release because they couldn't test this for balance issues.
