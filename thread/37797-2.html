In Essence the soldier class has 1 list of problems that effect the class in general and another problem that doesnt really effect the class til post 125 or so. The first problem is really a 2 part problem :

(1) the nano line outside of reflects needs some work. I personally dont think we should focus our efforts on getting a better ac or hp line as FC has said several times they are in line with thier "vision" of the soldier. They recently "fixed" Phalanx but I dont think were going to get anything much better than a fix or a nerf to a nano in those two lines. However, they have posted that new and improved weapon buffs are coming which seems to be the acceptable line of nanos they will improve for the soldier class.

(a) Get Auto targetting fixed-at 33 ncu for a +23 attack rating its a joke.
(b) Lower the requiremnet on Offensive Steamroller so a solider can actually run this program prior to level 195 or so (and thats with faded PM/SI) Add another Ranged ini program that exceeds OS. IF we cant get crit buff nanos then give us more ranged ini.
(c) Give us more weapons buffs. We should have weapon buffs for all ranged weapons. These buffs should be have a mulit function use from one nano as well such as buffing all the weapon skills (significantly) in the ranged weapon skill tree from one nano. It wont do us a bit of good if each weapon buff is a separate nano that eats up 44 or so ncu because we will run out of ncu befroe we even get our own buffs on much less being able to use other class buffs.

(2) The 2nd part of the first problem that is common to all soldiers but maybe doesnt hit home as much til the later levels is that every viable weapon we get uses a 67/33 split attack rating. If you want to get the same bonus to attack rating/weapon skill that the other "ranged weapon" classes (agent, fixer, advent, trader)get from thier "viable" weapon line we have to implant (6) locations versus (3) for them due to thier weapons are more 100% attack rating based. I dont care about spending the IP on RE or whatever else but make us some "viable" weapons 100% based on a single skill.

The other or 2nd problem that hinders the soldier class starts to ooccur just past lvl 125 , gets more significant as you approach 150 and sticks out like a sore thumb past 160. This doesnt mean the soldier gets worse at these lvls it simply means that compared to the hybrid class which also have nano capabilty to heal/root/mezz/dmg, the soldier simply stands still in advancement. The hybrid classes can start playing with more Ip at these lvls and past 160 can flat out party with it to strap on the same type of weapon a soldier uses (mostly Nova flow) or other weapons and start doing some significant dmg with ranged weapons. In some cases thier attack rating is only 100-150 less than a soldier using the same weapon.

A soldier cant do a thing at these lvls to get more dmg other than a slightly raising attack lvl until your capped simply because by 160 you have every weapon available. Your done..finsihed..nothing much to do but cap other skills that wont do one thing for increasing your dmg while the other classes branch out and increase thier dmg in weaponry by using the same comparable weapon a soldier uses. The div9, CHS, Sleek cannon are simply alternative to a Nova Flow if your bored. They dont provide and significant dmg increase. This problem is truly one that doesnt occur till 125 or so and i think most all soldiers will agree from 1-125 it isnt bad becuase you can still increase your dmg by upgrading the ql of your weapon. Once your in a ql200 Nova etc, you are basicly done with any any signficicant dmg increase. As bad as I hate to say it, we need weapons that have lvl 150 req and higher or weapons that have increasing dmg as you level so we can offset the dmg difference that starts to happen at this lvl. I dont see another way to fix it. IF FC agrred that this disparity shouldnt exist and be fixed but they cant give us better weapons because it would then skew the balance at lower levels, how else will we change this?

I think if we focus on these issues we will at least have a receptive audience as FC has agreed to making changes to our weapon buffs (by adding new) and even tried to add new weapons (they didnt fix the problem). This is the wooden gate we can keep beating on until we break it down instead of trying to push our way through the stonewall.
