Yuttos are cool, my dear atrox.
Yuttos’ Camp

Yuttos directed Ottous with their singing around the camp’s stone wall. The massive animal moved blissfully along, seemingly disregarding three small figures walking alongside it. The wind picked up, making Zorf’s robe flutter and beat against his body. Looking up at the sudden flash in the evening sky, Zorf smiled and stood up.

“Brother has come,” – he says to himself.

A Yalm of pure white descends from the skies. It stops not even a meter away from Zorf. The door opens and a Sorcerer quickly exits.

“Peace be upon you, Zorf.” – the newcomer comes up to Zorf and hugs him heartedly.

“And upon you be peace, Fiqh.” – replies Zorf.

“This is the most desolate place you have ever called me out to, you know. I see you still trying to talk to Yuttos. Listen bro, there is no chance they will talk to you, or anyone else for that matter. To understand their purpose you have to meditate on Ottous not the Yuttos. Look here,” - young Sorcerer points at the passing Ottous and his Yuttos shepherds. – “as you see the Ottous is not of wild kind, it is bridled and saddled. Thus, it has a Master. Yuttos purpose is to look after Ottous, for they are not the Masters. I know that their songs give them control over Ottous. However, Yuttos’ shepherding duties expand beyond mere walking the grazing Ottous. I've once seen a Yuttos leading an Ottous down the Longest Road. So perhaps they take the animals out to learn various terrains. I am yet to find out who the Master of the Ottous is though.”

“My brother knows much of Yuttos.”
“Yuttos are cool, my dear atrox. Read an interesting article the other day. It says we cannot be brothers.”
“But we are. How can this be?”
“It says the Atrox and Solitus cannot inter-breed.”
“Zorf is atrox, Fiqh explain.”
“Can’t get my head around that one either, Zorf. All I know is that Mum and Dad ordered you when I was three. They set-up the tank in the kitchen and in a week you were smashing my toys, eating every single pie Mum bought and shouting “I own j00!” every single time your beam struck our neighbour’s Yalm. But we lived together ever since, we were created by the will of one force, our parents, and thus are brothers. Whatever the article says.”
“That we are, Fiqh.”
