*** Bug warning ***

I don't know, how long this has been around, or how well it is known, but be careful switching a pistol while you still have one equipped . At the weekend I wanted to exchange my ql67 (main hand) gun against a ql76 one. Unfortunately I 'RIGHT'-clicked on the ql67 with the ql76 attached to the cursor (exactly, like manual loading). And indeed, the system considered this action as loading of my equipped weapon. After loading, the ql76 gun dissapeared (like an ammo clip that is used up).
As you can imagine, the friendly GM that responded to my petition, was not allowed to replace the vanished ql76 gun . So, NEVER, EVER right click (until it is fixed) with a gun/pistol on another one that takes the same ammo.
Although this is no major loss, it took several days of searching and about 80k of cash to buy the ql76 Khemo-Tech, that dissappeared. I don't mind the item, as long as I can replace it, but the time I had to spend to search it is not as easy to replace.
