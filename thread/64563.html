Returning to Rubi-Ka
*Begin Transmission*
Greetings Friends.
I recently returned to Rubi-ka to further fulfill my mission to Omni-Tek and her glorious empire. My adventures took me away to Earth for a short while, and sadly I must report their situation is grim. But we cannot help them. We can only sit here amongest ourselfs, talk about the future, and dream of a better Rubi-Ka.

I left Rubi-Ka blinded; blinded by what the Clans were telling me. I visited neutrals territories, even sneaking into Clan settlements only to be met by extreme force that drove me back. I fell back into Omni-Tek, dreaming of evil things to come. I honestly thought the clans provided better fulfillment for me, but I was wrong.

So now I come back, my hands clean of what my mind thought only months ago. After taking my leave, I surly see what Omni-Tek provides: A society made on perfection and nothing more.

Keep your heads up and be glad. You are fourtnate enough to fulfill your needs when you wish, to live in a society which many take for granted.

With that, I say farewell to you for the time being. I shall wander this land once again, setting my feet upon the beautiful soil and this beautiful land which is here for us to enjoy.
*Close Transmission*
