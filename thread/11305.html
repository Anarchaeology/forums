Missing from 13.8 build notes.....
The DoT, Slime Cascade, will no longer occasionally heal the victim.
(this should be edited to have the word "occasionally" removed)

Nanoresist is now working as intended for all classes, players will have a much higher chance of resisting hostile programs if they have raised this skill to a respectable level.

Drop rate for Fixer instruction disks Grid Armour MK1-4 & NT Nullity Sphere MK1 & 2 has been increased slightly. Additionally the type of mob's that drop instruction disks has also been increased.

Players of different or same classes that root a monster at the same time in a team fight will no longer find their roots bugged. Monsters will now stay rooted.

Mort 25% suppression areas has been changed. A dividing line has been placed down the centre of the zone horizontally bisecting the crater area so the South side of the crater wall (MMD side) remains 25% while the North side returns to 75% suppression. The Cyborg factory area will be included in the 25% area. This is to alow players of both sides the choice as to whether or not they REALLY want to throw that 2mil xp down the can.

Soldiers can now only equip pistols.

The MP class has been removed from the game as they are all off on a spiritual retreat and mass meditation quest in order to produce their 4th and 5th pets, Calling Of Orion (blocks 99% of all damage directed at the MP) and Calling of Legitimacy (allows MP's to register FC as their daddies instead of them having to wander round as their "ba**ard love children" all the time).

All class issues with nanos, weapons and skills have been resolved so that everything works as it says on the tin.

kthxbye.
