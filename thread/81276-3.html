Benjacrat lies awake in his bed, pondering the last few nights events. Scanning over gridfeeds from the Herald, and the Voice of Freedom he slowly drifts off...

Not long after he dozes off he is awoken by the same quietly disturbing voice he heard days ago. This time it doesnt come from his Comm unit, but somewhere inside of him.

The voice whispers softly "Ben, wake up, Ben"

Ben begins to stir, and jolts straight up in his bed. His eyes frantically darting into the dark corners of his apartment. He sees nothing, and tries to calm himself. He lays back down, dismissing it as a little wind from a storm.

No sooner has he laid his head back on the pillow than the voice comes protruding into his head.

"Ben, Please do not go to sleep. I need to speak with you."

Ben's muscles jerk him into an upright position again, and he responds.

"Where are you? My Comm unit is turned off."

"I dont need a Comm unit to speak to you Ben, I am a Voice." The Voice says calmly.

Ben hurriedly asks. "How are you doing this, what are you? who are you?"

The Voice responds in a manner that Ben doesnt quite understand. The best he can make out of it is something about "...ending...and death...". The Voice finally becomes coherent, and Ben begins to understand.

"For centuries I have watched as people have destroyed. Destroyed them selves, each other, and everything they come in contact with. I can no longer let this be, on Rubi-Ka."

A short silence, and as Ben is about to speak the Voice comes back...

"Ben, I have seen the good in you, and your friend Nelida. This has come at a great time on this planet. Right now, you have the opportunity to unite all the people of this planet. To bring harmony to its occupants."

Ben sits in his bed, trying to calm himself while he listens.

"I must leave you again Ben. Sleep well, and when you awake, be refreshed in yuour cause to bring peace to Rubi-Ka. I am with you and your friends...for I am a Voice."

Silence envelops Ben's apartment, and he sits paralyzed by the thought of this Voice. What does it want? And why me? Ben realizes that he needs to speak with Nelida, and he needs to get the RMC formed as soon as possible.
