I've played Enf, Doc, Trader, MP, Crat and NT to reasonable levels, IMO (and everyone enjoys different things or finds different things challenging):

Enf - Simple to play, takes more brain power than you'd epect to play a good tank at low levels in proper hunting situations. No brainer at high levels.

Doc - Reasonable challenge when you first start healing a team, gets a lot simpler as you get higher and the healing becomes automatic.

Trader - Pre nerf healing traders was by a long way the most complex. Juggling drains, calms and heals to be able to heal at a high level while fighting was a big challenge. A lot duller now.

MP - Average, didn't really excite me. Well equipped and with a lot of money you can fairly easily get good enough pets to take the challenge out of it.

Crat - Rewarding and challenging in solo missions right up until the point where charms become useless. Average in team missions.

NT - Pretty easy so far but my NT is pretty twinked. I can imagine playing an NT as a first character with poor equipment would be quite challenging.
