Funcom Please Read!!!!!!!!!!!!
please note: this is not in any of the envisioned changes, in progress, or on test server. This is a change I recomend


as many pet owners know, pets cant quite talk like we want them to, in fact they cant talk at all , we have to /pet chat to get them to talk in anything other then their normal; if you wish master, yes! let me kill it, phrases. I would love to see my pet be able to chat on his own without me telling him to do anything (I am not some loony, I know what I am talking about). There is a computer program called teX which is used mainly by people who do not have the ability to talk, those people would type what they want to say and the computer would say it out loud for them, this has been taken to a more advanced level then what I just said. That program can now find the word, phrase, or even sentence that you are most likely to say next. This is very interesting how it is, but it still has "evolved" farther. This program might now be able to fill in a reply to what someone has previously said, here comes the fun part. With a little programing it could be used to reply to actions also, so when some1 else who I am not teamed with starts attacking a monster that I am trying to kill my pet can shout out clever insults. This would let me be free to attack AND I would have much more fun playing the game. This could also be used with other things but the above seemed like a good example. I also wish that my pet could get a full amount of AI so it can do other stuff also (bow/salute to me when it is created and even talk in the guild chat). There would have to be a way to moniter what the pet would say to make sure that insult/joke could not be turned on its master, it would present a small box to the side of the screen of what it "wants" to say and you could hit aprove, retry, or no. If you hit no the pet will say nothing in response to the last phrase and will be silent untill another phrase that catches the pets attention is presented. and the retry would have the pet create a new phrase. This would be very fun to do as the game is getting crowded and I cant quite type 1k words per minute to respond to people and having a pet which would be like a 3rd and 4th hand + a new keyboard would be very helpful. To have this done Funcom might need to buy a new Cray but it would be very cool

Please do not /petition or send emails which would be reserved for reporting bugs this idea countless times in game if you like it, it would slow down improvements and fixes. Just leave a reply to me and hope funcom reads this.
