what I want in 14.0
These are a few things I want for 14.0:

1. Rifles that do good damage and dont require us to waste IP's on shotgun skill.

-seriously our rifles suck when you consider the damage/time. either increase the clip or increase the damage.


2. increase the range of rifles past the 40m visual range.

-The reason scopes are used with rifles are to allow a sniper to cap a guy that he can hardly see with his eyes


3. Increase our DoT damage

-our DoTs are terrible. I have good nano skills and my best dot is like 106 points. Why bother when i can FP MP and use a pet that does 8 times that. Not to mention most of us have fairly low HP's and no HoT.



4. Conceal

-I understand MOBS should be able to buff perception to high values making it easy for them to see a hidden agent; However, dont bump me out of sneak mode. The problem is if we are seen in a mission and we root the MOB we can never, ever go back into sneak unless we zone. When a mob gets aggro on you for the first time he stays aggro on you even if your on opposite ends of the damn mission area. Do they have freakin x-ray eyes and can see me through 25 walls? This makes rooting/concealing techniques completely useless because we cannot go back into sneak mode leaving this MOB stuck while we go on about our buisness. The main issue here is once your seen by one your seen by all. I dont t think thats right.

5. figure out what direction your going with FP

-your main arguement for why we cannot use some FP nanos is that we do not have the 'expertise' to use certain nanos. This 'expertise' is nano skill which, we take a big debuff on when in FP(not to mention the speed we cast at which is even more painfull). If you wish to pick and choose which nanos we can use then remove the penalties for casting false profession and 'allowed' nanos of the false prof. You are essentially penalizing us twice as it is now. We should either be able to cast ALL nanos of every profession and suffer our penalties which, reduces our 'expertise' or remove the penalties and pick and choose which nanos we can use.



Thats all for now,

Just to be frank, if docs want to remain known as great healers why should they have the very best DOTs and HOTs. Agents should have better DOTs and Fixers should have the best HOTs. A fixers HOTs would help relieve some of the burden of keeping everyone healed up. Rarely have I ever seen anyone actually use a HOT in group mainly do to the high ncu requirement. Fixers could use more evasion skill modifiers on their grid jump line...as well as reduced nano skill requirements. Please realize I don't want to nerf a DOC's DOTs and HOTs but rather I want to see an agents DOTs be better than a Doc's DOTs and a fixer's HOTs be better than a Doc's HOTs(reducing ncu requirement wouldn't hurt either). A doc heals he shouldn't be known as the very best person to poison you...an assasin type character would, like an agent. Currently the amount of damage a Docs DOTs deal is just barely enough to be useful...an agent's DOTs just plink away at you (you'd do more damage just firing at the mob instead of casting a DOT atm).

The initiatives of mobs should really coincide with what that class is capable of at that level. Up to level 30 my agent's maxed evasion skills and with implants for evasion skills have reduced the amount of hits I receive significantly. But now mob initiatives are begining to catch up to my evasion skills. Mobs really need to have their initiatives fall closer in line with what that class can achieve at that level. Just something to keep in mind.

Our snares aren't very useful when you hit a mob, regardless of how far away they are and the obstacles in front of it, and they magically just warp right next to you. Area roots are mildly effective in keeping the team alive...area snares just get the team killed. Is it me or does it seem as if none of the debuffs actually work against Mobs? The only time snares and roots are effective at all is when they are used in PvP or when casted on us by mobs. Calms however do work quite well and fit the role that snares and roots are supposed to fill. A calm nano line would be nice or at the very least fix death's gaze for PvP. So many things work well on mobs but then get screwed over when used for PvP...its both frustrating and annoying. I would like to see snares used to debuff mob initiatives and not break when you hit the mob.

I think concealment should have two roles...one while in sneak mode and the other while in normal mode. While not in sneak mode I would like to see concealment reduce the chance of a mob aggroing you over someone with less concealment. The reasoning here is that if your concealment is higher than someone else in the group (an enforcer or a soldier for example) then when you hit a mob with a crit the mob would be less likely to switch from hitting the tank to hitting you because you are harder to find whereas the tank is very visible. If you are aggroed however then it would be easier for that mob to be taunted off of you. In essence the higher your concealment the lower your character would be on any mob's hate list. Concealment buffs would then be usefull to all the support classes in the group and would help keep the mob's attention on the tank.

While in sneak mode concealment would reduce a mob's aggro range depending on that mob's current perception skill. A npc mob class that normally has low perception skill should have a hard time spotting someone with high conceal, whereas a npc class that has a high natural perception skill would have a much better chance of spotting someone with high conceal. In short I'd like to be able to more reliably determine which classes in missions I should be able to sneak past and which ones to avoid completely because I know they'll probably spot me.

I would also like to see cloaking devices only activate while in sneak mode and allow aimed shot and sneak attack to work while cloaked. Anything that disengages conceal would disengage the cloaking device. The cloaking device could essentially be installed and left on much like a low light scope does currently. This way the cloaking device once installed would only reduce health and nano while you are in sneak mode. Once you are attacked or after you begin attacking and sneak mode is disengaged then you could fight back and not be constantly hit with the nano and health reduction due to the cloaking device(it would disengage as well as sneak mode). I would also like to see a modifier added to conceal to reduce the chance of being detected while cloaked.

Since range has been capped I would like to see the removal of the range modifier on low light scopes. A damage modifier should be added instead for all damage types. This would help range users gain back some of the damage we lost due to the loss of our range. A reduction of the amount of init lost while using a low light scope would help as well...but if the damage modifier idea is used then there would be no real need.

The current order of concealment buffs is a little overpowering over other classes. Instead of giving all mobs super detection capabilities I would much rather have our concealment buffs balanced out a bit more. Shadow crown should be expanded into a line of concealment buffs. The entire line of shadow crown should be able to stack with the ruse of taren line and the pronouncement of greatness line. The ruse of taren line shouldn't be able to stack with pronouncement of greatness. As more concealment is needed to overcome the perception of mobs another shadow crown line should be made available to help increase our conceal over time instead of all at once in one big chunk. Shadow crown line would be castable on everyone but the ruse of taren line would be self only. There should be a pronouncement of greatness version that is castable on others just like all the other forms that an adventurer uses. However the Pronouncement of greatness(other) or(team) versions shouldn't give the full 200 concealment, instead it should buff be about 100 conceal. With these limitations we should be capable of sneaking 50-75% missions reliably as long as we avoid the mobs with high perception. High perception mobs should spot us unless we stay out of the rooms that they are in.

If we successfully pick a lock or disarm a bomb sneak mode shouldn't disengage. Also our sneak mode shouldn't disengage simply by opening a chest or a door. If we fail to disarm a bomb or we fail to pick the lock on a door or a chest then sneak mode should disengage. If we open a trapped chest or door and the bomb explodes then our sneak mode should disengage. If we frighten the rollerrat then the rollerrat should see us and automatically attack as well as any other mobs inside the room. If we are blinded then any mobs in the room would detect us and attack because our sneak mode would be disengaged. If the bomb explodes any mobs damaged by the explosion would move into the room where the explosion occured and attack. So in order to be completely successful with sneaking missions all of the sneaking skills should be utilized.

An item that many agents would also like to see sometime in the future is a silencer for our guns. It would reduce the noise of the gun and it would help reduce additional mob attacks. Such a tool would be extremely usefull in pulling mobs and would help define the roles of stealthy pullers such as the fixer and the agent.

By adding in the cloaking device changes more classes would be able to see some of the effects of conceal...especially the support classes. Adding in the low light scope changes would allow anyone that chooses to use them to deal more damage per shot and allow for a better critical chance. By creating a silencer to help reduce adds when pulling for a group both agents and fixers would be able to solo or group more effectively and help define our role within the group. Technology would be used to help exaggerate our natural abilities and make them more effective. With these changes agents would then be able to liquidate unwanted elements through technological means.
