Combat In 75% Zones
One thing that may want to be looked at is combat in 75% zones.
Example: Clan members go to Clon**** to "attack" the notum lanuchers. Thats fine. They go there and attack the guards, while Omni PCs stand there unable to do anything.
Or, Omni personel go to the gates of Tir and attack the guards there while helpless clanners just stand there unable to help.
So, why not allow people to come to the aid of the NPCs that are on their side, allow them to heal, or, attack the attackers.
To me, nothing is more stupid than reading about some battle in some 75% zone where one side cannot defend it's people, or property.
My 2 credits worth.
