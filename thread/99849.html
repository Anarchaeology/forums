Well, the problem is, RP is by nature a social activity. You RP with and around other people, through communication with them. Even if your RPing as enemies, you need to cooperate and coordinate on the OOC level in order to have enjoyable RP. You work together with others to create a story.

PvP, by contrast, is by nature an anti-social activity. You PvP against other people, and win or lose, people being sent to reclaim takes them "out of play." Rather than communicating (trash talking, while it might be amusing, doesn't count as communication) with people, very broadly, you tab-kill people. Because the "point" of PvP is to "get rid of" the enemy, rather than work with them to create a story, it is hard to RP well in a PvP battle environment.

Most successful organizations that mix the two do it in one of two ways, I've noticed - first, by treating PvP entirely as an OOC activity, or else, by PvPing OOCly and then working it in to their character backgrounds and other stories after the fact.
