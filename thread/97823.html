All in a days work
IC:
Omni-Tek protects.
What a wonderful phrase. Dyrge thought to himself.
Letting the apartment door slide close behind him Dyrge strolled across his apartment and flopped exhausted on the bed. The jolt his body took from Grid jumping always left him completely drained, physically and emotionally. It was a small price to pay. As he drifted off to sleep he let his mind wander over the events since he had arrived on Rubi-ka.

Life doesnt always work out the way you think it should but at the moment it was throwing him a pretty good bone.

Since he had disembarked from the shuttle and made his choice, life had been good. Of course, Omni-Tek had been the real only choice. With a corporation of their size, operating under their umbrella was easy. They were predictable. Sure many thought Omni-Pol was unjust but many times they had saved his tail. They did a good job keeping their attention where it belonged, with the clan not on him. One day if he ever met the Generals of Omni-Pol he would shake their hand and commend them on the fine job Omni-Pol was doing.

Yes life was good. The mission terminals, or money trees as he liked to refer to them, had been a very lucrative business. In the early time he had enjoyed operating with another employee Bliqz. Bliqz was a straight up guy. Albeit a tad on the extreme side. But extreme was good. It kept the conflict alive and without that conflict, business would dry up. They had run some jobs together. Once he had done him a favor hacking some terminals for some robot-geeks at a robot factory. Digging up some dirt on a whack job of an employee that was into robot porn, well different stroke s for different folks. But favors were good, better then credits some would say.

He had recently increased the scope of his business. Dyrge had finally made his way into Clanner territory. There where clients there in real need goods he could provide. Thats where the real money was. Omni would never miss the goods and if they did it would be lost in the web of red tape and could easily be shrugged off the bottom line.

Recently a fly got trapped in his ointment. He had heard of some clanner, what was his name againhmm. Fixerben, that was it. Was trying to organize some sort of coalition to bring peace between the Clan and Omni. He would have to put some feelers out on that one. That kind of operation was bad for business.
