Yes, this must be fixed.

Here's a rundown for any non-agents reading this thread and thinking we are simply whining ...

Agents have seven green skills:
Agility
Sense
Sneak Attack
Tutoring
Psych
Conceal
Perception

For starters, Sneak Attack is useless to us as we are a rifle using class for the most part, and I have yet to see a rifle with Sneak Attack. It's a melee attack shining green in a sea of blue skills that are too costly to raise. Ironically, Funcom felt that this should be the only green attack skill we get. Funny, in a sad way.

Second, Psych and Tutor haven't proven their usefullness in the long run yet. Psych seems mostly geared to Agression Enhancers at this point, something an agent won't be using unless he or she feels like taking a dirt nap. We are not tanks. Tutor may very well be usefull in the long run, but as far as I know this skill costs the same for everyone to raise, so we've no advantage there.

What is an agent left with? Perception, Conceal, Agility and Sense. Four skills that are all tied together. Conceal is completely dependant on AGI and Sense ... kinda funny that those are our two green abilites.

The point I am trying to make here is that the agent class was built for sneak/conceal. Our best attacks rely upon it, and it is one of the very few skills we can raise easily. If we were not meant to be sneaking about all over the place, why does our cost/skill level show exactly the contrary?

I do not expect to be able to do a little jig in front of a mob 20 levels higher than I am. I do, however, think that given the limited skill sets of the agent class, being able to sneak past yellow and perhaps orange mobs with the buffs provided by our class is not an unseemly request.

I did not want to be a tank ... I would have been an enforcer. I did not want to nuke ... I would have been a nano-tech. I simply wanted to hide in the shadows and only fight when absolutely needed ... now that option has been denied me.
