Is there a Doctor in the house?
It was a quiet day as the young man strolled down the city streets to the local tavern. The sky slowly became dark as another electrical storm moved overhead. "Damn.", thought the man as he pulled his trench coat closer to his body to shield himself from the heavy winds now brewing.

As the man strolled down the street he could feel the eyes of the several poor families that were now forced to live on the streets due to the expansion of Omni-Tek. Rats quickly scurried around the corners with each step he took, hidng as they watched him walk by.

Music could now be heard as he drew nearer to the club as a sly grin eveloped upon his lips his eyes caught sight of the most beautiful woman he had yet to see.

Soon his whole life would change as a faint but hihg pitched whine was heard in the air, followed shortly by an explosion. Flash of bright light... Burning flames... Sudden darkness...

Was this to be his end, or was it jsut the beginning?
