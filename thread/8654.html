NPCs Need to Be More Like PCs
Having reached one those "hard to level up" doldrums, I went browsing through all of the professions' forums. And you know what? EVERY profession is feeling nerfed and helpless, and all 12 forums are full of people complaining that once they get to about level 75, they can't even take out a single NPC their own level.

Gee, and I thought it was just me.

By using implants and armor at about my own level, and absolutely maxing out my attack skills, damage, and evades, I have gotten to the point where in PvM, I hit NPCs my own level for at least as much damage, with better crits. I hit them on average twice for every time they hit me. They aren't getting special attacks. They're stingy with attack nanos and self-heals. They don't use first aid kits. NPC puppeteers never have their pets with them.

With all I've got going for me, and all they've got going against them, it should be easy. It's not.

I think Funcom massively jacked up the hit points on human NPC mobs to make up for their disadvantages. I strongly suspect that they're giving them all nearly maximum armor. And I want to know where pistol-wielding NPCs at level 45 are getting pistols that do 60+ points minimum damage, because when I had a pistol-wielding character, I couldn't find one.

As of 13.x, combat is tilted way, way too heavily towards the NPCs. We could use a break here, if you expect any of to be able to ever complete another solo mission.
