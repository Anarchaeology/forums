I suggested something like this months ago, I wanted a PvP practice arena in the better guild houses which I wrote an excellent thread on. For some strange reason it was heavily flammed for one small comment that fixers could get the ability to heal defensive weapons during a battle. to anyone who hates me for no good reason from that: I never said clean! I said fix, mobs dont drip blood and machines dont blow up and leave holes in the wall, why must I be flamed?

now that I have covered that, onto the arena. The arena would be a large room where you have the special ability to see everyone else in the room, I can see a quarter of the omni-ent arena as it is . In that arena you could get no new PvP ranking, hence the practice part of the name. Second and third floor balconies would be installed, including a little anouncers booth where whatever the person in there shouts everyone in the room can hear. Another part of this which I had in that thread is that guilds could do battle or invite other guilds into the house, by A: the leaders forming a pact of some type, or B: two of the guild members taking 1 guest on a tour, no PvP would be allowed in this. Enemy guilds would be able to invade, but that is a different condition.
