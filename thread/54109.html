Elyhaym signing off!
Well I am sure 99.9% of you don't know me but I felt I had to jump on the "goodbye post" bandwagon since its the difinitive thing to do when leaving AO. =p

Ahh.. where do I start, unlike most people I am not quitting because of pure resentment to Funcom (well I do have some) etc. but because I don't find much enjoyment in this game anymore, I have done everything there is to do, seen the places that needs to be seen, gone through several days playtime in Broken Shores missions alone, and I finally came to the conclusion that hey... I don't think this is very fun anymore. BUT I never expected a game to keep me occupied for so long, if you think about it, there really was a lot to do, lots of stuff to see etc. AO was definetely an experience I wont forget, and to say the whole journey was boring, would be the biggest lie ever. I could go on and on but I really can't express myself too well. Gosh, if I could copy paste Destria's goodbye post I would do that since she has a golden tongue/keyboard or whatever... But what kept me here the longest were definetely my friends: clan, neutral and omni alike, and they will probably be the reason I come back some day, so thank you all who treated me well, and let me get close and good luck to all of you in the future. You have given me a lifetime of memories, and although this sounds really weird since I only know you from multi-coloured texts and emotes, I find it extremely hard to believe that I will ever forget you guys. If I could I would list everyone that really made this game worth logging on to continuously for a year, but that would take more time than the time i spent in BS missions so i'll keep it as concise as possible:

Big hugs to TC/Rennaisance (ALL you guys are by far the 'finest' group of friends I could have ever come across, we've been through it all and then some), Band of Brothers (always kept 2ho busy), Apocalypse, Cappy, LeQ, LB, and all of the wonderful MOK crew. Cat! And all those from Lost Chapter, Redemption etc. that I have killed or been killed by, pvp was the only thing going for me for a while. There are tons I missed I know... but that doesn't mean I don't lub you.

I think it would be at this point where I am supposed to list what games you can expect me to be in, but honestly I think it will be tough to outdo AO as an extremely great experience for me so I think i'll just end up where my friends go. So for now ill be mostly on irc looking for good trivia. Good luck all, take care, and maybe some day I might just pop up again. *wave*

-Ely
