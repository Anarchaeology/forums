Fire Dragons!!!
Heya all im here to announce the recent creation of the guild Fire Dragons. We are a helpful, and fine young guild that seeks first to welcome all the newcomers to Rubi-Ka and to Extend a welcome to any non-guilded veterans or any Vet's who feel the need to particpate in a guilds construction and outlook and to have a chance to play a key role in the daily workings of a guild on Rubi-Ka.

Fire Dragon's is run by Thortek and myself Munnintekk, This is our 2nd guild endeavor and we already have a better start then our first endeavor. I used to be known as Munnin and we co-lead Assembler Research Group inc. Currently we have 10 active members, most of whom are between the levels of 5-35, we are hoping to increase in size and level range and we would like to be able to participate in pvp (tho not as heavily as some guilds) and the story line. We also want to try to be at most RK events and take part in politics as we see fit.

We are located on RK1 and we are seeking new members of all levels and proffessions if you would like to join please visit us at http://www.guildportal.com/guildHome.asp?GuildID=725 and then contact either myself Munnintekk, Thortek, Drferro or Aprilmist in game or leave a message on the forums at our site OR if you feel that you need to inquire more about the guild in game ask me in game OR email me at unfriendlypsychopath@yahoo.com. We strive to make this one of the best, well known and well thought out guilds on RK1 to date and WE hope that you can help us.
