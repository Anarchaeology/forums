However, if Funcom were to make some major fundamental change, which heavily impacted my character by rendering skills useless through an item nerf or whatever, I feel that warrants extra IPR tokens.

From an MA perspective on the upcoming changes; the nerf to Reet-tech bows should include sufficient IPR tokens to recover all the skills required to equip the weapon. The low light scope changes should include sufficient IPR tokens to reset the required trade skills. This is potential wasted IP and should be recoverable, NOT by using IPR tokens from title levels. As time goes on those title level tokens may be fully spent experimenting with new content. Funcom really needs to create a more dynamic system for earning extra tokens to make that content accessible.
