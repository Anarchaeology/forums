Combo buffs
Make some nanos that could replace some of the general purpose buffing that needs to be done.

For example, a single nano that would buff all attributes by 12 each, cost the same amount (maybe slightly more) to cast as casting all 6 seperate nanos, and use up the same number of NCUs as all of them spereately.

Have "sets" of buffs that would be combined. For example, a "Fixer" buff that would combine the burst, smg, and B&E GP buffs.

A combo buff that buffs all the nano-skills.

The idea would be to have convenience buffs to remove some of the tedium from the general buffs, but not provide any more benefit.

These could be a bit higher level than their components, and would make good quest rewards.
