The issue was that with the title caps preventing the bureaucrat from upgradeing their charms or fears as intended that they are unable to charm monsters even 5-10 levels above them during the larger title caps during levels 127-149. The upper limits are sound in theory but their implimentation is off balance. I did a bit of math during those title caps and found out that had the skills not been title capped and was able to use the next better charm the gaps in psychic check wouldn't be there. So it is an issue of static psychic checks balanced for the nanos that forgot to take the title caps into consideration.

I hope they'll re-answer the issue. I think Gaute mis-interpreted the question as a request to do away with the psychic check system.
