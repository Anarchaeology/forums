I'm a volunteer mod at the AO Vault boards. While the closing of half of the boards on the official forums may seem like a boon to Basher, Stratics and the Vault, I see it as a boon to the players of AO.

Hopefully this means 3 times as much information from FC to the playerbase on issues that are important. Will it happen? I'm hoping it will.

I'd like to clear up a few things I've seen posted about AO Vault and IGN. Yes, you do have to register through IGN in order to post on the AO Vault VN boards. Doing so allows you to post on both the VN and IGN boards. I'm fairly certain that the IGN boards don't have their own AO section, unlike VN.

Registration is free, and all you need to do is register with your name, zipcode and an e-mail address. IGN does not require your physical mail address.

If you're afraid of spam, you are more than welcome to register using a hotmail or other free account.

Yes, the VN Boards are open to posters from other games(AC, DAoC, etc), but trolling another game's boards will result in warnings and/or bannings of the offending party. Our TOS is basically the same as FC's, i.e., no swearing, posting of vulgar content, etc.

We find is that we get lots of people from other games inquring about AO. There are also people who are wanting to come back to AO and are looking for direct input from regular players.

Popups and ads. A month ago the ads were fairly obnoxius. They were scaled back and now appear far less than they did. Someone on the VN Boards posted a list of IP addresses of the ads from IGN, and you can easily put them in your hosts file to block the ads.

Several FC employees do have accounts at AO Vault, standing out with their red user names. FCranium is the most active FC dev, and we hope to get more of them posting soon.

AO Vault has a great and very helpful community, and I would hope that you will join one of the fan sites rather than quit the game entirely. I would hate to see anyone leave AO because of FC's closing of these boards.

These forums are one of the greatest assets AO has going for it. Closing them down is a big mistake. These forums are the cornerstone of the AO community, and weakening the boards will do nothing but weaken the sense of community among players. Sense of community is what keeps people in a game like this. Less sense of community=less to keep people in the game. This will do nothing but give people one less reason to stay in the game.

If lack of man-hours to moderate the boards is really the reason for closing them it would be vastly preferable to simply leave them unmoderated, or just have someone go through and remove the obvious spam and such, which wouldn't take long. Much of the "moderation" that has been done in the past has, in fact, only harmed Funcom's reputation in the gaming community.

This is a game that is dying of a thousand tiny self inflicted cuts, and this decision is a pretty deep one. Funcom is a company that exists in an endless downward spiral of creating wonderful things and then making decisions that undermine everything you've created. AO has been called by many recently the best MMORPG on the market yet you still have trouble keeping customers. The reason is that every time you make a decision like this you drive a few more customers away, and when you add them all together it adds up to massive customer loss.

This entire game could be turned around if Funcom had the ability to make just a few right decisions, but there doesn't seem to be anyone in your organization who can or will make the simple, obvious decisions that are necessary to save it. I guess the gaming world will never know why.
