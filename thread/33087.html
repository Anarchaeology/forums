Please 'Fix' the Team HP buffs!
Recently I've come to realize that the minor HOT component of Doctor team HP buffs in the Health Graft line is far, far more trouble than it's worth.

Therefore, I request that this line of nanos have their HOT component removed.

The very small heal every 30 seconds is worthless, yet the heal ticks build aggro towards the casting Doctor that frankly we just don't need. I love giving my teammates the extra hitpoints from these buffs. But the HOT does nothing useful but get mobs mad at me. It ticks too infrequently and for too few hitpoints to save someone during a fight, and I'll spin off a quick team-heal to get rid of its spam in between fights.

I also believe it continues to build aggro towards me on its heal ticks even when my teammates are all at full health.

Since it's useless - actually, counter-productive - please just remove it.
