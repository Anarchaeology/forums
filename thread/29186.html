Mail Delivery Terminal
Ok, anyone who sells anything, or gives things away to org mates, knows the pain involved of getting to the person, who is either halfway around the world or in a mission. I propose the developers should work on a sort of AO UPS. It could work like this. Put a mail terminal next to all the regular save points, or by mission terms or wherever. Right click drop an item on it, and it asks who you want to deliver too. Make it cost a bit of money like a scanner. When you are done, it alerts the person you sent it to with a tell, and they can pick it up at their convienance, as if from a reclaim term. Make it cost them a bit of money like a scanner too. This does 2 things, makes selling a lot easier, and also is a small way to remove credits from the game. Improvements to the idea are welcome!

What do you think?
