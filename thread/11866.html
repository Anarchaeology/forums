Antiguardians is recruiting RK1 [Finnish people]
REQUIREMENTS:

1. You understand Finnish language

Communication in our channel is 99% of time in Finnish language and we like to keep it that way atleast for now. Thats why we dont accept at the moment anyone who dont read/write Finnish although we are saddened to turn some people down because of this (you know who you are).

2. You are Omni-Tek aligned

After watching that honey dripping cheesy introduction video, how could we have chosen anything else but Omni-Tek.

Ofcourse if you are clanner or perhaps neutral and are willing to step out of the darkness and into the light by joining Omni-Tek, we are more than happy to welcome you into our guild.

Thats it

WHAT CAN WE OFFER?

Heppa ja hyva meisinki! Thats what we offer.

We are not too concerned about role-playing but we are not against it if you wish to do so. Most of the time we are out to have some fun and to chat with people from our nationality while killing some clanners or doing some monster bashing.

We are not very "active" in role-playing sense and we dont have big events. Ofcourse that might change in the future or you may organize some of them yourself by making an initiative!

You can look some more info about us in:

http://www.anarchy-online.com/org/st...1/name/1181697

or in our own homepage at

http://antiguardians.org/index.php

and ofcourse by coming to chat with us at

Official IRC server irc.funcom.com at channel #antiguardians

You are also welcome to contact us online in RK1. I would then suggest you talk to either Auno or Taisto.
