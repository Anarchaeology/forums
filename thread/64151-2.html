Good post, Emiko.

Some people get so greedy, they miss the fun of such events. I, for one, really enjoyed the fact that FunCom took the extra effort to try and make Halloween special for the people of Rubi-Ka.

I would have loved to have gotten a pair of those cool xp/crit sunglasses (my doc HATES wearing an ugly helm), but my girlfriend and I still had fun flying around SFH looking for Uncle Pumpkin-Heads. There were a lot of level 150+ clanners out there trying to kill steal from us, but fortunately each time they tried they didn't succeed. One level 170ish Omni Enforcer was nice enough to actually let us keep our pumpkinhead after we politely asked "Please don't steal our pumpkin! :-(", after which he stopped attacking, replied "Ok", and went off to look for another. Conversely, after we had engaged another Pumpkinhead and two clanners came over and starting attacking it as well, we tried to appeal to them in the same polite manner, and they responded with "And now it is OUR pumpkinhead!" as they continued to attack it. We won the loot anyway...such as it was (a lovely shining cluster of Inner Fire).

Although I wish there had been more of them and that they had spawned ALL over the place so there would be no need for the rampant camping and kill-stealing, I still had fun due to the special atmosphere and uniqueness of the night.

Kudos to FunCom. Let's do something like this again!
