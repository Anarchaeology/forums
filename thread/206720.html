Ashes to Ashes...
It had been but mere moments, mere seconds before he realized what had happened. Feebly, he opened his eyes, the cramping, tight muscled hand baring for grip on anything it could find. he cried out softly as his muscles spasmed in atrophy, his stomach churning as he held back the overcoming feeling to retch. His body had already broken out into a cold sweat, but the pain was starting to subside. He rolled onto his back and tried to focuse his vision. So young and so open, the engineer had been taken advantage of, and was now suffering. If I hadnt have been so stupid! he cried inside himself. My fear...my pain...he fed off them and took control...so many dead...I'll never have the same reputation I once I had...I'll never be able to fix it...Gods save me.. His mind was at ease, slight now of the pain. But still his thoughts were clouded, twisted and churning in the machine that was his undoing.

The room was in focus now. Plain. Simple. One table, two chairs, a bed, and a door. Door. Cmon...Door...lets go...get the hell outta here.. The words were blatant in his head. The functions however were failing off the prime. His legs were ceasing to drag him forward, and he decided the only way to reach his goal was to walk. The boy wiped the cold dampness from his forhead and began to lift himself, first on hands and knees, to kneeling, to standing. The process took a long, strenous time, with countless failures and one completion. Staggering in his new position, with limited equilibrium, the nano mage found refuge quickly against a wall. Balancing himself along, he continued to stride untill he met the iron door.

The hiss and rush of light overcame him, as he shielded the sun from his eyes. Athens..No...Tir...I'm in Tir. he nodded, as he fumbled to gather himself. A heavy thunking came resounding from his left, and when he turned he stared upon the bale form of an atrox.

"Carl...get inside...NOW!" the Atrox shrilled, in a oddly high pitched voice. But the force was enough, as the large arm covered him and threw him through the door. But it was not an Atrox that came through, but a black hazy orb with deep red eyes. The engineer lowered his head. His attempt to escape his capturer again had failed. There was nothing left he could do.

In a swift movement, the spirit was gun, and the Engineer remained standing, cracking his knuckles.

"Bad Lurai...bad bad..." the figure mused. "Oh well...youll never get away from me.." said he again, as he reached for a satchel. "Hi ho, Hi ho, off to Tir we go!"
