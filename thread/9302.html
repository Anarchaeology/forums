Charms, Scares and Mesmerize
Though I do read these forums often. I would rather spend my time playing and socializing in game than posting. I have a L68 Crat which I enjoy playing. I also have a 43 Trader and a 46 Fixer. The Bureaucrat is my profession of choice. I dont complain if I run into problems. I just adapt and over come (to borrow a phrase) to get by.

I am not one who has enjoyed PvP before AO. And even now, I do not go out looking for PvP action. If it happens, it happens. I know more than likely I will probably die. Ive been asked for a friendly matches in the Area, and I decline. Though I do ask if they would like to PvP in a gas free zone. Then they decline and ask me why. I tell them to even the odds, and allow me to charm another creature or such. They freak out telling me thats cheating. Well why should I go to the arena and not be able to use one of my main nano lines. I also noticed that MPs and Engineers will not PvP with me. They dont want their pet to be charmed and used against them. And Engineer told me he would PvP with me if I didnt charm his pet. I said ok, but he cant use Vektor shotgun. He replied, that isnt fair and I laughed at him.

Notice, this isnt intended to start any flame wars for those non-crats that read this. Just my opinion

Now, here is what really irks me. Why can Bureaucrats not charm, scare or mesmerize other characters. Wait, wait, wait Before you start to say it is unfair or unbalancing, STOP. The way I see it is, I should be able to use what has been givin to me, just as every other profession does.

How about they change the way Charms & Mes work. If I get a Charm to stick, that player is mine to control, but I am not allowed to attack another creature with them. I can get him then to follow me, use socials, etc If I get a Mes to stick, that player is in effect rooted, but also may not use weapons or nanos while the mes is in effect. Since root works like this, but they are able to still use weapons/nanos, Mesmerize should basically just freeze them, allowing them to do nothing at all, until it is either broke or the nano wears off. This would at least allow Crats to heal/recharge nanos or run away, while their opponent helplessly stands there watching. Mes can be broken just like the NPCs do, when hit or have a offensive nano thrown on them. How about scares, not sure if they work in PvP or not, but I see no reason as to why the shouldnt be used. I enjoy big events and am excited to be able to participate in major events. Coming up on L75 soon and wonder what it hold in store for me PvP wise.
