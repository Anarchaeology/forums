Ideas, Ideas and more Ideas
Hi there, this is a list of ideas i would love feed back on... This is the only forum I though that would be relevant to post in.

Im just curious to see what ppl think, so be harsh.


* Multiple Professions

You should be able to belong to more than one profession. You can switch between professions but you only progress in your current profession but you retain the abilities of your other professions. Professions should have minimum attributes to join them, and it should cost. Because if you have been a nano tech you should be able to still use Nano-Technician nano-crystals even if that isn't your current profession. More specific ideas on implementation are listed below:

1. It may be a good idea to have a limit on how many professions

2. You can learn up to X number and if you earn level 200 in Y
number of professions you should be able learn a X+1 profession.

3. The second profession you choose should be free but you have to be at least level 20 for example.


* Levels

You should be able to progress your experience level higher in all professions, perhaps indefinitely, well in four years its unlikely you'll reach the max level represented by an integer or long integer.


* Profession Masters

The person who has the highest level in a profession is elevated to the position of Profession Master. Everytime someone gains that title the character should receive a cash bonus and a POWERFUL relevant weapon i.e. if current profession = :

Soldier = Powerful Gun,
Nano-Technician = Powerful Crystal.

etc..


* Forgery

You should be able to forge your details, i.e. your first, last & nickname, characters with a high perception may be able to spot the forgery, or a new skill could be added.


* Wanted Rating

There should be a Criminal Wanted list, i.e. if u kill or steal from an omni-tek employee and the person gets your reference you get a criminal record, and appear on a most wanted list. Using the forgery idea, you should be able to fake the documents.


* New Skills

Pick pocketing, you should be able to view another persons inventory and steal items, whoever back-packs and items contained within should-be inaccessible also, the counter-skill for this would be perception, and based on the opponents perception at timer should be created, when this runs out if you are still looking at the persons inventory they will be notified and you will be automatically targeted.


* Transport

There should be animals e.g. horses that you can use i.e. ride them.


* Secrets Of Rubi-Ka

There should be secret dungeons located in the deserts and forests which contain both VERY powerful weapons of old, spells which can be turned into instruction discs then built and cash. These places should be guarded by old creatures and there should be manu-scripts detailing the life that was once on Rubi-Ka before a great catastrophe which destroyed all life. Some places should give bonuses to the character e.g. max all stats for current title.


* Builders

There should be a non-combative profession, the character obviously can fight, but it should be a Builder, this profession can build buildings, as they become better through building buildings, they should be able to learn more complex layouts. You shouldn't be able to start as this profession, but learn the skills so you can, for example, build yourself a house in the desert. This need not be a separate profession, but it could be a new skill section after Trade.


* Rangers

This isn't a new profession, but these ideas should give you ranger like skills, you should be able to eat fruit off trees, also all monsters you kill should produce Monster Parts which you can also eat. There should then be a book which details the safe fruits/berrys and monsters to eat. Food obviously increases your health unless it¡¦s poisonous. These additions will allow you to make LONG journeys through the land without using the grid or other means of transport. This would, hopefully, encourage people to leave the cities for longer periods of time and therefore reduce 'lag'.


* Suppression Fields

The suppression fields should change slightly:

100% = No Fighting
75% = Monsters
50% = PvP political with level restrictions, i.e. a level 100 cant fight a level 2.
25% = PvP political with NO level restrictions.
0% = Free For All.


* Guild Flag/Colour

There should be a series of flag shapes, colours and patterns which will sit on the outside of guild buildings. Also members own a flag which they can carry into battle (or anywhere). Also a guild colour should be chosen, preferably RBG format, so we can define it like /org colour #FF6501, and the names of members of the particular guild should appear in the specified colour. But you should be able to hide these details if you are on for example a spying mission using forgery skills.


*Guild Housing

If you pay more money on your guild building you should be able to have save points and shops, if you are a member of the guild the save point should be free and shops should have lower prices, if not save point cost and shops are more expensive. The area within a guild house should be 0% suppression field, to allow for PvP combat training. Possibly money spent at shops contained within a guild building should be divided between members of the guild that own it, unless you are spending the money.


* Nano-Technicians

nt's at the moment are not good at all in solo PvP, this is due to lengthy casting, or rather programming, times and a lack of defensive or healing programs. NT's are supposed to be the best at nano-programming, therefore there should be either more defensive and healing spells, or perhaps as an alternative maybe nt's should be able to use other profession¡¦s programs but on a limited basis, i.e. what would heal for 100-200 points with a doctor should heal for 50-150 points etc.. Completely profession specific programs obviously shouldn't be accessible like False Professions or pet summoning. Also there should be some freezing/rooting spells that DO inhibit the fighting ability of the target.


* Nicknames
You should be allowed to change your nickname and re-assign your first and last name.



btw i AM an nt, but i do have a few other characters, i finally stuck with nt, cos i LOVE mages... but i think they should be tweaked a little, we should be major players not support characters, you know ppl asking you for buf's and roots and blinds.. our dmg spells should be more available and we should have more defensive spells.
