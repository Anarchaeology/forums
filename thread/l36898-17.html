Conceal
Another important point a lot of people are forgetting is this.

concealment is not just a agent skill. Time for some math.

Simply put other characters use it and rely on it as well. Therefore it should not be balanced to the point where only a agent with cloaking device/shadowcrow/ruse of taran/concealment expertise/maxxed conceal for agents level can sneak past a green or yellow with little difficulty.

Agents should be able to sneak past most anything within 6 levels with little difficulty under these circumstances. Just as a enforcer that had maxxed 1hblunt and somehow got 250+ skill from buffs to his skills shouldnt have much of a problem consistently hitting something within 6 levels.

ruse of taran=200
shadow crown=63
conceal expertise=20
some odd nanos for only 17 would put you at 300 bonus skill.

Now with putting 5 points of skill in every level, and pumping required stats to max conceal goes up 5.5 points a level, ie 5 then 6 5 then 6, etc so 11 every 2 levels. [I record my characters skill gain from stat increasing alone every level]

So that means 300/5.5 =54.54r

we will round to 54, that is 54 extra levels worth of skill.

Now, thats a crapload of extra skill, under those circumstances a red 6 levels above you should not have much of a chance to spot someone, to say the absolute least.

Saying a agent under these circumstances couldn't and shouldn't be able to sneak past a red, is likened to saying that 300 skill[54 levels worth of skill for 5pts per level skill] in something shouldnt help. Ie a level 55th lvl Soldier should be as accurate at hitting a target as a 1st lvl soldier. Or 500 skill in matter metamorphosis should only let you cast nanos with 200 skill or less.

Credits do not mean anything in this game.

No amount of credits more then the next guy will help you if you do not have the skill/stat requirements to use said item. I personally would rather be leveling and getting money from killing things, and getting the money from completing a mission, than sneaking through it to get the reward and leaving with some credits.

However, Skillwise I should be able to sneak past a red 6 levels above me no problemo with 54 extra levels of skill. To say the least.

Now conceal has other implications than sneaking.
Sneak Attack
Aimed shot

WTF should i not be able to get a aimed shot off at something?
Imagine if you couldnt burst at something, because it saw you. Or full auto. Or Dim Mach. Isn't that kind of, lame?

So yes we have to be hidden to aim at something, fine. At least we should be able with buffed skill and our basic high agent skill to hide from something to get a aimed shot off that is within 6 levels, is that so much to want?

I wont even go on with false profession nanos and crappy rifle range.
