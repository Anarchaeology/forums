Asking people to treat the game like real life won't cut it, Aktrez... not because it's a game, Metalynx, but because people already do treat it like life. That's the whole problem. In life there are people that are rude, selfish, and inconsiderate. There are people who are just young or ignorant and haven't fully developed a sense of ethics and manners. There are people who derive pleasure from causing pain or discomfort to others, there are people who are truly stupid, there are people who are emotionally unstable or mentally ill. There are people who are willing to do things they realize are wrong in order to get ahead themselves; someone like that is sitting three cubes away from me right now.

That's what real life is like... that's what people are like. You can't expect AO to be different... and as much as I would like otherwise, don't. I expect people on AO to act the same as people do for real - selfish, petty, shortsighted, inconsiderate, narrow-minded, arrogant and rude. And there are good people too, who are loyal and caring and respectful and absolutely brilliant, the kinds of people who make it all worthwhile.

I used to think that when I went to university, the highschool melodramas and petty politics would end. They didn't - the stakes got higher and the underhanded behavior got appropriately worse. I thought then that when I went to work, the university melodramas and petty politics would end. Yet they didn't - the stakes got higher and the underhanded behavior got worse still. But each time, there were also people who were better, who were virtuous. I'm not religious, but I am certain I have met some future saints.

My point, in fewer words, is that people will be people, in any setting. There are good, and there are bad, and you have to take the bad with the good. Even in AO.

Metalynx is right about one thing, if about nothing else: Chill out. The way out of the trap set by griefers is for us to treat AO like it's just a game. Brush the griefers off. Remember how trivial and silly they're being - laugh at them. They want you to be upset, they want you to make these posts. Laugh instead. Then forget them.
