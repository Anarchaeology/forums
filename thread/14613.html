Show me my nano damage!
I am an NT, so my damage is to mobs is from Nano programs. I think that it is bad game mechanics when I cast some nano on a mob (see: Nuke) and then I see that,
"X-mob has been hit for X nano/chemical/melee/sillyputty/nerf damage."

Who hit that mob? Me. If I had a weapon, it would say that I hit them for X amount of damage, right? In groups, it's impossible for me to tell how much damage I am doing if others are doing nano damage as well. I am unable to guage how I am doing damage wise and I think that it is bad mechanics.

Another scenario perhaps of more interest to people? Sure, how about in PVP? You are strolling in a 25% zone and out of nowhere you see "<insert name here> is hit by X amount of Y damage." Who hit me?!?! Sure, I may aggro back onto the correct person, but perhaps my first special is wasted because I didn't target them back correctly (see: NT nuking himself thinking that he had targeted the enemy).
