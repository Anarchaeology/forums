Actually, an easy solution is to team up and then kill a leet. If everyone gets exp, you're cool. The problem is that if you have one higher up, then you get exp based on his PERCEPTION of the beastie.

For example: 6 team members, 5 of you see the "Boring Reet" as RED; the final member sees it as GREEN. You will all get exp as if you killed a GREEN critter, not a red one.

You might as well kill green critters for the xp as reds in this case.
