The Ability to Change Your Profession
Now, I'm going to take a deep breath, for I know that I'm going to get myself "ripped a new one", so to speak, for writing this, but...

I don't think that being able to change your profession, an implementation that Funcom wants to make sometime in the next year, is a good idea at all. I think that, despite what Funcom may do to make this idea viable (ie non-exploitable), it will either be exploitable, or it will be none too useful.

For instance:

-If people are able to change thier profession, then I have absolutely no doubt that people may (and note that I say MAY) jump on whatever bandwagons there are concerning classes. (For instance, we may hear a lot of "My level 77 crat now sucks, and I want to change him into a mp, because those things rock.") We don't want that, we want Funcom to fix the classes that need help.

-If people are able to change their profession, they can use this ability to get themselves out of ip idiocies that they have committed (this is not an anti-ip-reset statement, I simply mean that if Funcom wants us to be able to re-distribute our ip, then it should be done in an actual ip-reset and not as a by-product of another, completely different, implementation) by changing to a different class then changing back to thier original (I mean, afterall, if we change classes, than they are going to have to give us our ip back upon the change or else I'll have an engineer that used to be a soldier with my assault rifle raised to high heaven, and very cheaply at that or characters that are useless because all of my ip is in the wrong place, etc.)...this could be easy to guard against if they make the change possible only once, perhaps, but what happens if I change to a new class and then find out that I hate my new class and want the old one back? It just seems like a lot of programming...

-If people are able to change thier profession, then they can start their character as an easy class to level in, then change thier proffesion later in life after they have gained a significant amount of levels.

Of course, these things may be avoided by doing such things as making the profession change take away levels, possible only once, not change your ip distribution but give you negative ips if your skills would eat up a lot more ip than they did with your old profession, take away only some ips, etc. But then how useful would a class change really be then, really? And for the amount of programming that it could take? (I'm assuming that it is no truly small task...)

I know that there are many of you who disagree with me absolutely, and I encourage you to try and convince me of why profession changes actually can be useful. Happy posting, all.
