Omni Tek End Game Vol 2
[In a small station cloaked above Rubi-ka...]

"Project Nosferatux has been a great success. He has already taken care of our small cloning problem, as well as halted the production of the new breeds by at least four months, if not more. The Company will be pleased with this news im sure."

[a small chime sounds and a soft female voice comes alive over the intercom.]

"Sir, sorry to interrupt, but we have some urgent news to report."

[The old man excuses himself from his previous conversation and turns to his desk
reaching for the intercom switch]

"Please, continue"
"Right. We have just received news of a Dust Brigade settlement, ill upload the coordinates to your NCU now."
"Perfect!" [ he sits down in his black leather chair and turns it facing the view screen behind his desk. He presses a small button on the rest arm rest of his chair to open the shutters. A beautiful Rubi-Ka can be seen gently rotating on its Axis]
"I will send Nosferatux to investigate right away, perhaps we can use them. If not ill make some arrangements to have them taken care of in due time. But, for now they are keeping Omni Tek as well as the scattered Clans out of our hair.

[meanwhile, back on the surface, Nosferatux sits in a popular in Omni 1 Entertainment known as The Rompa]

[gazing deep in to the lava lamp on the table, Nosferatux pet, Caligari breaks the silence in the bar]

"Sir, incoming transmission from Sol Banking Corp."
"Patch it through" [breaking his gaze turning his attention to his pet]

"It has come to our attention that a Dust Brigade settlement can be found at these coordinates. I want you to go there and speak to whomever claims leadership. We are well aware of the danger and have provided you with a Nano Technician Nullity Sphere It will only last 15 seconds, just enough time to grid back into your Yalmaha using your modified Wrist Comm. Do not fail us or cross us Nosferatux, we created you, and we will make no hesitation in terminating you."
"That is the end of the transmission sir..."

[Nosferatux pulls out a small back pack full of parts and starts assembling them in what seems like random order. After a while a Salvaged Beetle Blaster was completed. He picks up the gun inspecting his work and loads it with 48 rounds of energy ammo. He glances at his pet, who is starring at him patiently waiting his next order.]

"Our time is short Caligari let us move."

[Nosferatux packs up the remaining parts and calmly walks out the bar into his Yalmaha parked out side. He types in the coordinates to the on board computer, and immediately the craft comes alive and rushes to the location. Once There, Nosferatux types a few commands into his Wrist Comm and is teleported to the ground. Scanning the area cautiously he proceeds to the flashing way point on his map. The air was dry and harsh, almost stagnate. Decay could be smelt from every direction, no doubt the victims of those who tried to get information from these demons of flesh. Minutes pass and his now right on top of the way point with no movement on his radar. Something is wrong. The sound of shattered bones could be heard, And Nosferatux turns around.]

"I scan no organic material in you, which means.... This wont hurt!"

[The Dust Brigade commander shoots a burst from his modified Nova Flow at point blank range. No time to react. everything goes black.]

[System Scan: Powercells 5%, Bio containment -. 01%, Energy output -50% Nanorestoration -50%]
