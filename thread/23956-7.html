I have some slight hope for AO around the Fall timeframe. I think 14.4 might breathe some life into the game as well.

If they wanted to know what to work on it could be summed up in three quick points.

#1 We need something to do. We're bored of mission after mission. We have forgotten why we are doing missions after lo so many months. What are our options? You provided the Static Dungeons, yet they have been siezed by a miniscule portion of your playerbase. Team missions are just missions. Tradeskills are of little value and you must sacrifice you killing ability which is the only way to level in the game.

Tell me what to do besides another mission.

#2 PvP is so broken it's not even funny. You focus on profession balance while none of us even care to PvP in the first place. You provide no incentive for one group to fight another, yet encourage single players to attack other single players. You give us no reason in-game to attack another player. You provide easy outs via shops, and zoning. Even the pitiful Single Player PvP-Titles reward those who have the best running away skills. Ganking is encouraged by allowing one to loot anybody they kill who hasn't saved. All advantages go to the agressor. No repurcussions are to be had for those who engage in illogical actions such as killing their own side (players or guards) or hunting a simple neutral adventurer who happens to be out in the wilderness where the gas gets weak.

There are no options for guilds to 'spar' with each other devoid of interferance from a-holes. I can't even smack my guild leader on the back in my own apartment.

PvP is such a distasteful portion of this game that it doesn't provide the foil for so many of us who are bored that it could if done right.

#3 Partially related to #1 and #2. There is no continuity into the events that are going on around the planet. We get a bunch of crap about what some guild did to rescue some painter, but who the hell really cares. What is our reason for waking up in the morning? Is it to do another mission? Crap, we know more about what George Bush had for breakfast than what P. Ross or H. Radiman are working on. The world is not a world in that we have no clue as to what the f the pyramid in Home is there for. Who created the first Cyborgs. Should Omni support or destroy them? What the hell is going on? The game is turning more and more into a 'game' everyday and less and less of an online world. UO still kicks all the other MMORPG's asses to this very day in this last respect.

Logging on without having a purpose for my actions is getting stale.

- Give me something to do next Tuesday when I log on.
- If there's a battle gonna rage, make sure it's a battle with a purpose, and a goal.
- Start the story already and provide context for all these people running around from place to place.
