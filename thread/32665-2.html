Forgive me if i tread on anyones toes here, or repeat that which has been said before, but i completely agree that there is far more to this "Conflict" then meets the eye. Ask yourselves this: Given the sheer size of Omni-Tek, and the resources that must logically therefore be at its disposal, is it not a tad su****ious that this conflict has dragged on so long?

If Omni-Tek was the inherently evil embodiment of sin the wild rantings of the clanners claim it is, then surely its strategy for a conflict as vital to its interests as the one on Rubi-Ka would have been far more brutal. Why has Omni-Tek not simply launched an all out offensive on the North? Surely manpower/resources are not a concern. Even a war of attrition, slowly squeezing the resistance out of the clans would have been a viable option with such ast resources at its disposal.

Think for yourself, and things begin to seem more clear. The very fact that clanners exist flaws their arguments about the "Evil Threat" of Omni-Tek.

A planet as vital to a companies interests as Rubi-Ka is to Omni-Tek would surely be protected with a zealous brutality. The toleration of a native rebel action seems ludicrous, reinforcing the beleif that there is far more to this story than meets the eye...
