New Content High Cost
Okay, this is not a rant but a mere statement.

Those who have purchased the NW did so for a reason.

1) New vehicles and voices
2) A reason to pvp
3) A reason to hold some land and at last fight over land

Okay consider this, I paid £20.00 sterling for NW. Why?

So I could have the new vehicles and the new content.

Problem?

I have already paid £20.00 for this pack and now in game I am expected to pay a couple of million just to take advantage of the new content E.g. the new vehicles.

So my mission so far has been to locate and purchase a copy of NW, which took me ages.
Now If I want a new vehicle which is in the new content which I have already payed for, I have to bust my ass just to save up enough for the new stuff.

See where Im going?

The requirements of the box should be clearly stated

Cost of product: £20.00
Cost of new content: 10,000,000 Million credits

So paying for something which I have to pay for again within the game does not make sense.

I understand that there are lots of players out there who have lots of money, but what abut the people who dont have lots of money, but who spent £20.00r on an expansion pack who cant even get the new content because it is expensive in game.

I went from level 1-60 saving all the money I could just to buy a yalm a couple of months ago. Now I just spent real money for new yalms and I cant even get them?

Please, review the prices of the current new vehicles in game. The content of the new pack should be available to all players not just to the rich player base.
