14.2 - Welcome to Quake 4
I am a L93 Trader.

I wield a QL200 SA Tranquil Home Defender
My armour is Flowers around QL120-140.
I have a QL130 Secondus Decus cloak on.
I have a 250 token board.
I have Pads of Interest.
I have a full set of Ql125 implants.

Now, in any estimation of how this game should work; I should be moderately "uber". I should FLY through L93 missions, because L 93 missions should be balanced around a moderately equipped character. They are not.

I managed to die (as we all do) in a L93 mission to a chem-shield wearing Nano-Tech from melee damage! Close to the end of the mission (as we all do).

Now....if you implement 14.2. That mission that I died in..would not be difficult. Would not be challenging ...it would be unplayable.

So if you really do feel the need to turn AO into a 12 y.o. standard, console ready version of "Quake 4" with the 14.2 patch, by all means go ahead. You will need to rebalance the game before this is implemented.

You are admirable for listening to your player base, but the views of a level 25 player have little to do with the experience of the higher level players in the game. Unlike some other MMORPGs I have played I really dont see the incentive to level? What will I do differently at L 150 than L 93? After L75 or so - no one continues to fight outdoors. No class of mobs drop any "special" items. I had a QL200 weapon equipped at L80 - which apparently now will be useless.

After the 14.2 patch I certainly doubt I will be able to solo. I think teams used to playing L150 missions will have to play L110 missions. I think Engineers used to playing with slayer droids will be playing with large tin garbage cans again. I think there will be no concept of being "superior" anymore, and no reward for taking the time to make a truly working character. I think a lot of people will get frustrated that that cannot do anymore what they have developed an expectation of.

With the IP Reset I will give up my shotgun and grab a couple of riders executioners. Who really cares after the patch because we will all be brought down to the lowest common denomiator. Level, not planning and knowledge, will determine the power of a character.

...look for the duel wielding 1he Trader out there - ill be coming your way...or maybe I'll get a full auto weapon like a OT Escorpio (they used to be crap)

or maybe...we will have "pickup" weapons in trade with no stats...oh look is that a rotating chain gun!

"Hi I am new to the game? Where can I find the quad damage pickups?"
