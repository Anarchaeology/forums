Shuttles and storms do not mix
Given that the ICC troops are to be shuttled down to the planet not long after the *spanking new uber weather control* has been fitted, the weather control that has already caused changes to the eco system of Rubi - Ka.

Now I wonder what would happen if the weather system *happened* to break down while the troops were on the way down .. I wonder?

Would all the stored / dissipated energy be released in one catastrophic bust (well for the shuttles anyway)?

Could it be that the backed-by-money-we-dont-know-about DB "attack" the weather control as the troops are descending?

Would having all the ICC troops dealt with before they reach the planet be good for OT?

Would being able to blame the-nasty-clan-backed- DB give OT the perfect alibi?
