Saltwater
"Saltwater"
by Randa "Poetica" Whites

My hand is a cup
Filled with saltwater
I take a sip
The salt stings in my mouth
Leaving a taste of tears
Reminding me
Of you
Of us
Of what used to be
Still...
What used to be
Can be once more
If you let me
Back into your heart
Untill then
I stay here
On this beach
Waiting for you
Hoping you'll come find me
Waiting on this beach
Of memories
Where every grain of sand
Is a kiss we never kissed
Yet...
