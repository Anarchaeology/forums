Everwatch:

Trans has roughly 270 members. When I say a small portion roleplays.. that will sum to a number as large as some small roleplay orgs. I would not add the organisation to the list if I thought we couldn't contribute to the rp community.

As far as accepting new RP members... we simply like to keep the roleplayers that we have a close and familiar group. Alot of us have long histories together with past rp storylines, and we are not looking to recruit strangers into our circle. If we build rp relationships with unguilded or other-org affiliated characters and they wish to join us... then we will surely offer an invite.

We have always and will continue to recruit non-rp members actively, however we will not be approaching strangers to join us in roleplay. Not because we are elitists or snobs... but simply because we like to know the people we roleplay with. It's not like non-roleplay recruits where you say, "Welcome to the org mate, grab a gun and blow up that tower!" Good roleplay involves a bit more depth, personal interaction and experience.

Sorry if I gave off the wrong impression.
