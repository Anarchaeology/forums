Neutral? The Independant Rubikans need YOU!
Are you a committed neutral? Do you get fed up of people asking you when you're going to choose a side when you already have? Do you feel that people need to be more aware of who they're shooting when they press 'tab'?

If so, then we need you! The Independant Rubikans have, for a while now, been running the successful Independant Rubikan News newspaper, dedicated to neutral issues, and now we're branching out to form a guild. We're interested in reporters and guild members to form a social, political and roleplaying guild. Most of all, we're interested in putting neutrals in contact with other neutrals.

If you wish to join us, please send a /tell in RK1 to Nyadach or myself, Silenna, or email us at silenna@shee.demon.co.uk .

We look forward to hearing from you!
