Corporate Saboteurs Found - Investigation Underway
Corporate Saboteurs Found - Investigation Underway
Omni-Tek Press Corps. Affiliate - Red Tape press release
Argentum, 4-Holes
December 05, 29477.

Corporate sabotage sets back half year of research for Red Tape, deleted data is valued in the millions and key information on Notum Suit has been lost.

Red Tape, a sub-department of Omni-Admin suffered a major setback recently when their mainframe computers were wiped by saboteurs. Valuable data and equipment was lost for their departments operations and research projects. The people responsible for the attack escaped capture and remain at large.

Among the research projects affected by this act of techno-sabotage was Red Tapes ongoing effort to develop a working environmental suit for nanomages. The suits development was first announced during a recent Science Symposium. It was designed to finally give nanomages the capability to travel off-world by re-creating the environmental conditions on Rubi-Ka. All six months worth of data on the project was lost and among the items stolen, their only prototype suit. The research team has no comment on how this recent act will affect their ability to continue the project.

An investigation authorized by Omni-Administration into the matter has uncovered a saboteur cell working within the sub-department.
Based on the existing information and evidence gathered, the saboteurs are found to be not Clan related or funded at this time. A spokesperson from Omni-Tek said.

It came as a shock and disappointment when the investigation revealed that some of the employees in the saboteur cell have been with Red Tape for a long time.
It was unthinkable that those employees, some of whom have been my co-workers for the past year and a half could be the saboteurs. Administrator Houston replied when asked about how he felt.

Unfortunately, before the saboteurs could be arrested for trial by Omni-Pol officers they were tipped off by an as yet unknown source. The saboteurs managed to erase all employee records for the sub-department before they fled. This has created difficulties for the investigators trying track them down. Authorities say that the saboteurs could be attempting to reinsert themselves into another department, possibly in the armed forces or supply departments. Security analysts expressed concern over a potential "ticking time bomb" scenario where these agents would be able to cause considerably more damage to Omni-Tek through more sensitive measures if they are not found quickly.


During the investigation Administrator Houston has cooperated with Omni-Admin and temparily stepped down as president of the sub-department while further investigations are underway. Cristin Doctorzore Svancara has been appointed by Administrator Houston to take his place until the investigation has been completed. It is expected to take several weeks.

The administrator has been reassigned for the duration of the investigation to a prestigious military unit, Midnight Reveries in their expeditions deep into the Shadowlands.
