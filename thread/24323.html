4 months, 4 years, 12 profs...
I think there's 12 profs (or is it thirteen?) Anyways,

4 years = 48 months / 12 profs = 4 months to get each prof to level 200

So say I don't like a prof or two... that's still about 6 months to level each prof to 200...

So, in light of that, are we levelling too fast? Why shouldn't I be allowed to level each profession to 200 by the end of this 4-year period? (And what if I started a year or two into the game, what then?)

Not that I plan on levelling each profession to 200 personally... but I would like to be able to level at least 4 profs to 200. I'm not saying "Give me G0D M0de plz," just asking "Is the levelling really that out of hand?" Imo, no, in light of the above numbers...
