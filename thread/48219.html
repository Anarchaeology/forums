I have a Geforce 4 Ti 4200, O/c to almost 4600 speeds. 4x FSAA roxxor. and you don't take a "huge" perfomance hit. It is a Very good looking game, the environments make AO look like a 2d sidescroller IMO, the textures are so nice.. the world desiners made it feel like your actually there.

there are towns sitting on hills, you can see them from a mile away. theres a lake and a river.. with birds flying through, you can see for miles

get this.. NO ZONING THERE IS NO ZONING YOU NEVER ZONE!!!

well there are portals.. similar to whompas, but think of the entire world as one zone that you can "whompa" to get around faster.

for fun i set the game to 1600x1200 max details, 4x AA, 4x anisotropic filtering (Anisotropic the nt!!)

and i got like 3 FPS standing still lol.. didnt even try to move. my sweet spot on a 1 gig amd TI 4400/512mb highly optimized system is 1024 x 768 2x anti aliasing.
