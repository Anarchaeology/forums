PVP Gadgets for Enforcers
What makes an Enf better than another in PVP.
Besides armor/Implant choise and weapons, you have several gadgets that matters. One alone makes little difference, but all together (along with the knowledge of when to use them) makes your chances MUCH better. If you need help finding some of them, shout out.

Those haven't been mentioned too much, but things many enfs don't concider and SHOULD concider are these:

- Parryshields.
Eat those alphas, The Parryshields QL depends on your parryskill. I stay at ql 140-150 parryshields to keep my AC's over 8K for 20ish seconds. it's a job to gather them all, but it's worth it.

- Riposteshield.
Adds points to your Dmg shield in Close combat (only). Not *very* much, but helps when you get a decent QL.

- FoB
Get the highest QL you *can*... don't use that old ql 95 FOB or whatever crappy Thing you've had for 50 levels. QL 200 FoB was said to add 20% critchance. Not sure what it adds now

- Stims.
Proper QL on First aid equipment is also good to have in order.

- Grafts
You *can* get a heal graft, Nuke graft (Doc nuke.. around 200 points of damage in pvp), Reflect graft and a few others that can make the edge in a fight. mind the skill that's locked.. they changed it after 14.7 I believe

- Bracers
Spend time.. even though they don't do squat compared to "the good ol' days" when bracers were essential, they still work.

- Assaul class Tank armor
If you don't have this.. don't pvp.. eats up 2K damage when activated (lasts for about 20 seconds)

- Notum Focus
Good thing to have when you run out of Nano and your'e rooted
(A sleeve found in Smugglers den)

BTW, have anyone tested Grenades on live? Used them on test and it was hillarious as I could use them besides my regular weps on "Test-deng".. every 5 sec or so.. was a mad skill to raise tho, but.. who cares on test

I'd like more input if I forgot anything

Remember, an enf isn't as good as his wep or gadgets.. using the noodle helps you a lot
