it keeps going and going....
Do any of the fc employees actually read these? I've checked back here every once and a while and watched this thread grow and grow, I've added my 2 cents, and listened to both sides. Either you are for OE or against it, but one thing is for certain, this issue has generated the most responses than any other topic, I believe secrelty this is just a way for FC to check and see how many people are still playing. Anyway, enough banter, each of us have talked about why they OE and some have said why they don't/want to/can't. Before one side can flame the other for their views, it comes down to perspective.

The reason most are forced to OE is because of mob difficulty, they have insane hitpoints and damage ratios after level 100 especially. and as most of you already know, items, nanos, armor stops spawning in shops over a certain ql (113 for some nanos, I think 125 for armors, could be off...) So the only way to obtain these items are through missions..or if you have lots of money and don't mind programming shopbot (POS in my opinion) Past level 100 mobs get pretty difficult, even if they con green, so the result, people have to OE, this goes back to the chicken or the egg question, in that do people oe because mobs are too hard? or did fc make mobs so hard because they knew people could OE?
Some may bring ip the point that this doesn't excuse the low level twinks who have weapons/eguip/armor wayyy over their levels, and so they attack things that should be handled in a group. However in these cases empathy is important, I myself have twinked low level characters, but my situation is my own, I work a lot during the days and only get a chance to hunt, role play what have you, in the very late evenings, mostly when normal folk are asleep, this is apparent because most hunt spots are devoid of people lfg, now the setup of the game is that it will take you an eternity to fight mobs your own level as opposed to if you can tackle tougher mobs more stronger than you. What of us who can't always get into a group? are we reduced to having to fight greens? thus why OE is such a big issue. Now my situation may not match others, some OE even when they can get groups, personally I do it because I hate being limited in my play time because I can't find groups.


Empathy people, if half the people on this board had empathy, they wouldn't shout or flame out other people simply because their play style is different. I have read some of these posts, some are very intelligent, but the bulk comes down to childish 13 year old comment like "14.2 is gonna kick you OEers ***es" so forth and so on.

I can empathize with why people choose not to OE, and I admit, it does take away from the game, having all the choice equipment handed to you on a silver platter. Truthfully, the game was the most fun for me when I made my very first character and she set foot on rubi-ka, the fun was in the trial and error process, I didn't know which weapons were the best for my money, and many a day I purchased useless items that set me back many hard earned credits (as opposed to the millions I can make now just buffing mochams gift) anyone remember those battlesuits? heh paid good money for one back then they were uber pieces of equipment to have, low reqs and the nano penalty was bearable, a few days later before I could put it on naturally through leveling, the requirements were almost doubled. it made me mad, but I got a good laugh out of it though. OE does rip an imbalance in the game that tears a rift between the seasoned players, and the new players, but personally I feel that a patch won't fix this, now before you say, "hey, she's just against 14.2" and you'd be right, but not because I won't be able to twink the way I like anymore, but for the fact that it won't solve anything.

It will just make people angry thatt hey lose the full use of their weapons, and armor they paid good money for, the people against OE will feel vindicated, until they see something else they have a problem with, they'll find out they still get killed in missions and in pvp, and then they'll want the 25 percent thing lowered, funcom will hear the pleas and then lower it to 15 in a few months, it won't stop, no matter what you do, you can't keep everyone happy, those who are happy with the upcoming patch today will complain against it tomorrow, such is human nature. I respect the fact that some people choose not to OE, it is their right, they pay for the game like anyone else, however, at the same time they have to respect the choices of other people who pay the same monthly fee, I think that in the end funcom should respect both parties. instead of radically altering someone's weapons or armor, why not reduce mob difficulty? cause we all know what this is really about, people level entirely too fast in ao, whether they over equip or not and fc doesn't want this, they want people to stay around for another 3 years. If such is the case then, stop fawning over who has what weapon and armor type, give the players more story interaction, give them chairs and fireplaces for their apartments even, people mindlessly hunt, mission, twink, for lack of in game substance. If players don't have a story to live out, they are reduced to amusing themselves with level 1 soldiers with ql 30 novaflows.

FC can release patch after patch like 14.2 and it really won't solve anything, you will always have problems pleasing everyone, so why not put in content that everyone can take a part in and enjoy?
