Sons of Ether?
Reposted here as not to clutter the RK1 Orgs Thread. Demenzia - Why, oh why has R.U.R. become a synonym of "Sons of Ether" to me? *


I had to look up 'ons of Ether' on Google as I never did play White Wolf games much. In the event the inspiration for RUR is like 80% Isaac Asimov, 10% Fritz Lang's Metropolis and 20% Futurama. Yes I know that doesn't add up, that's the Futurama influence :-)

The name RUR itself comes from a play wriiten by Karel Capek in 1920 called Rossum Universal Robots (RUR) which I was lucky enough to see performend once. The play's claim to fame is that it's where the word 'robot' was first used. It is derived from the Czeck word for worker or serf (robotnik).

For the record, in the play the robots eventually do revolt and take over the world. You've been warned.
