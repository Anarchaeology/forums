First And Last Names
I was on test last week and when I created a character I was not given a first and last name. When I arrived in the backyard, I was able to use a machine that gave me a first and last name. I suppose that if I didn't want a first and last name, I could choose not to use the machine and I could function without them. This is what I am sure many people will do now that they are given the choice (I know that a lot of people do not like the randomly generated first and last names). However, what I would like to know is this: Will there be a way for us to have the first and last names of characters created before 14.6 erased (so that they will only have a nickname)?
