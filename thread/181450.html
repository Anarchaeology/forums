More on Thousand Stings
Thousand Stings got a low mark because piercing requirement is set to low for QL. Consider that at 150 I calculated a Shade could barely fit into the following with 1111ish piercing:

Thousand Stings: ql 166
Sigd: ql 185
Obfuscating Dagger: ql 210

This harms the damage rating on Thousand Stings as well as Obfuscating Dagger oddly enough because of its poor damage-to-requirement ratios.

In addition, try to dual wield a decent quality of Thousand Stings. The Multi-wield requirement is so high it almost looks buggy to me.

Requirements for MA Combined Attack?
The MA column's meaning is that If 1) your wielded weapons all have the "ma combined attack" requirement and 2) that you meet that MA skill for any weapon currently wielded (single or dual) then you will do an extra martial arts damage hit in addition to your weapon attacks.

In our case you'd do piercing attacks and an MA hit with an extra attack bar. In the case of other professions like MAs they would use combinations of parry sticks or claws and other weapons.

Fist Speed
The speed of the MA hits is on a separate cycle from your piercing hits. So upping your Phys Init would make these hits faster. Personally I don't recommend raising phys init at all because you have less IP available than needed, and every other decent weapon uses melee init.

Weapon QL vs MA Attack
Secondly, by the time you meet both MA requirements on your weapons, your piercing, melee init and multi wield will be at a level where you can wrangle into a MUCH better weapon that imho outdoes the damage of your MA attack. So while MA attack is nice, I don't think it has been made a viable feature to take advantage of for the Shade.

Using MA special attacks
The special attacks such as bird of prey and blessed with thunder do not function through your weapon for a Shade, as they now do for a Martial Artist. (At least last I checked) You can take your weapon off temporarily (by putting it on hotbar and keying it) but realize that 1) it is advisable for Shade to wrangle into best weapon possible, therefore you will be stuck with weapon off 2) it is advisable for Shades to wield 2 weapons in most cases, therefore you'd have to take off 2 weapons and deal with equip/re-equip time as MAs do for bows.

MA skill is depended upon for a perk line but nothing else. It's worth maxing it if you can imho for that perk line if you choose
