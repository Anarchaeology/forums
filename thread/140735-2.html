Its really a surprise to me they were removed in the first place. These periodicals were a way of strengthening community and encouraging events and discussion that created a deeper, more involved, and more interactive world. A choice was made to deemphasize the story and story events because they aren't cost productive from Funcoms perspective. Voice of Freedom created an opportunity for players to create their own stories within a story and share them with the communtity. VoF is a recent history of RubiKa told by players involving personalities and events that the players can relate too. By discontinuing VoF, Funcom is losing the opportunity to take advantage of the creativity and passion of its player base at a very small administrative cost.

Roleplaying and player run events has suffered considerably since the introduction of Notum Wars. The loss of such a valuable community resource only served to exasberate this problem.

How can those without a history share a coherent future?
