There was an earlier mention of waypoints so that people could join the march in progress. Something I've seen in real life, when a group wants to do a lengthy march, most of the walk is done only by the core members of the group. There's a point set up outside the destination where others can join in for the final leg of the trip. This helps to ensure a good turnout for that final portion of the trip which is then followed by speeches and such.

If you do want a long walk, you might want to set a specific time that you'll depart the last waypoint. This should permit the maximum number of people to be with you on the last leg of the journey.
