Range cap discussion
I'd like to start an open discussion on the subject of range capping.

Something to get the uninformed started...
Con:
-Characters such as NT's, Agents, and the nanomage race need a high range ability, not only because it is integral to their role, but also because they don't have the ability of the "tank" classes to take the damage that comes with the short range.

Pro:
-In PvP, it seems unfair that a character could be hit from a range of 80m just because they accidentally have their veiw range set to 79m.
-In PvM, it's easy for people to exploit (and i use "exploit" in the dictionary sense of the word, not the malicious "cheater" way) the aggro range of some stationary monsters, in a way where they can power level without the monster ever actually going aggro on them.


Let's be open to new ideas and see where we can find a middle ground (no pun intended) on the subject
