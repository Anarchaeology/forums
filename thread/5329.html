Basically, you tell it to check for certain words, at certain QL ranges.

When it encounters those words, with a QL that fits, it sends you a tell which says who the seller is. No matter where you are, you can easily get hold of someone selling that QL157 Mausser. And you don't have to sort through the spam!


And no, Funcom has nothing to do with it. Someone hacked the client, and put this thing in.
