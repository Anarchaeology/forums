My Proposed Changes For Critical Hits!!!!
this is what i think fc should do with the criticals hits in the game......just my opinion

I think all the crit scopes should be removed and the current ma crit buff line removed completely (even for mas). Also plunder and divest line should be changed to not stack. They should keep the new crit line they are adding for mas which gives a max of 7 to critical.
After fc makes these changes
The critical increase should be mostly determined by the Attack rating of the character and a small bonus for char lvl. I think they should give plus 1 to critical on every 25th lvl. Also when your attack rating reaches 200,400,600,800,1000 you would get a crit bonus of 4,8,12,16,20 respectivly.
What this would do is give a dmg edge to peeps who have more skill with the weopon. Fc's 14.2 patch will increase dmg if you have over 20% of the weps reqs but we all know the amount of crits is the meat of dmg.
This would also mean mps would have less criticals then a soldier using the same ranged weopon which makes sense in my mind.
And the people overequiping a weopon would not do as much dmg as someone with more skill using the weopon.
this would make it possible for soldiers traders enforcers to acually "SOLO" and do good dmg w/o having an ma around. And when an Ma is present its a nice bonus but you can do decent dmg w/o there buff.
My reasoning behind the divest plunder not stacking is traders would be overpowered with the 2 lines stacking with my proposed idea.
this is my opinion please give me your comments....
