On this day of Thanksgiving...
I would just like to point out the irony associated with Clan groups like The Pilgrims. It seems that history does repeat itself.

He we are, milleniums after the first Thanksgiving and nothing has changed. These so-called "pilgrims" and other Clan factions are trying to run us off and steal our land. We came here in peace, built a beautiful, harmonious world that worked and then came the evil Clans. The Clans brought with them disease, they polluted the rivers, they say one thing and mean another and the worst - they killed off the Buffalrat into extinction.

I will be sitting with my fellow employees this evening. We will break bread, carve the turkey, smoke the peace pipe and then exchange ancient, Omni-Tek stories about the way we would hunt the free-range notum, taking only what we needed for profit and leaving behind enough for the world to exist in harmony. But, inevitably, the talk will turn to the beast that lies to even it's own people and continues to destroy the world with it's greed - the Clans.

((Happy Thanksgiving! ))
