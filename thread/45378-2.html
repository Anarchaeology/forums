i dont want to make anyone mad with my comments, so please dont take offense.

My Take on AO and its booster pack:

1. I bought AO on release, and in canada it was $90!!! That is the MOST i have ever spent on any game, but i bought it anyways, cause it was the summer, i had nothing to do, and what they promised was really interesting.

2. I played the game for 3 months as a fixer. i waited, and waited and waited for the fixer to become fixed, and for more tradeskills to come out for other classes, and for all the classes to work! i ended up quitting because it was school time, i had lots of work to do, and lots of clubs to go to, and it just seemed like a waste of time to be waiting for something that would probably never happen in a game.

3. When i quit, they charged me for an EXTRA month, bought i thought, meh, no biggy, i dont want an argument, ill just pay for it.

4.i decided to come back a month ago because i heard it was fixed. so i came back, and to my pleasent suprise the game had become MUCH better. it took me 1/3 of the time it took to get my new doctor to lvl 68, than it took my fixer to get to lvl 38 :O. everything was/and is G, but my main gripe is still that it doesnt have any more dance moves!!!

5. when i came back, they said i hadn't payed last month, when i did, so i had to pay 1 month from before, and another month to start playing again, and now im paying for ANOTHER month now!

6. i like to PvP but i like hanging out at the clubs in AO much more, so i dont think i will buy the booster pack because it doesnt really have anything to do with me. but when they come out with a booster pack with better clubs and 50+ new dance moves, ill buy it ASAP.

7. whats with the price? $40 canadian? thats the price of a WHOLE expansion! this is like just a portion of an expansion.

So lets see how much money ive spent on this game.
$90 for the game
$60 for 3 months of play
$20 for the "extra month"
$20 for the next "extra month"
$20 for this month
$20 for next month

that $240 already, and now they want me to spend ANOTHER $40 for a "booster"? i dont think so. i pay my $20 a month for updates to the game, more content and fixes. i dont pay $20 a month for "funcom fixed an exploit relating to credits" "funcom fixed various other exploits". i dont pay funcom to correct the mistakes that they made in the first place (when is say $20 a month, we have to bear in mind im canadian).

so now i have to pay $280 with the booster, and then another $60 (?) for the xpack. and then another $40 continualy for any other booster packs they come out with? come on funcom, how many of these things are you going to have? i dont mind paying for something that is good, but you still have alot of work to do, and you may start asking me to pay $40 for your booster packs, after you fix your game.

thank you for listening to my ramble.

oh yea and to anyone who says "oh xqiksilve you have less than 10 posts!", it doesnt mean anything, i played this game on release, and posted on these boards before.

peace.
