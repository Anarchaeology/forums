It sounds like sneak attack is every bit as bugged as aimed shot.

So I'll explain how it goes with aimed shot, maybe this will help you.

First of all, you have to be in hide mode, of course. Secondly, if the mob is already aggroed on you, it won't work. Third, if you are in a group, it probably won't work.

Aimed shot rarely works for me when I'm grouped.

Fourth, you have to have high concealment to get it to work.

And lastly, sometimes it just doesn't work at all for no apparent reason. Funcom has purposely broken concealment to make it so we can't sneak through missions. The end result is that with concealment messed up, aimed shot often doesn't work for no apparent reason.
