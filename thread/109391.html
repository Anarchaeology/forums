My resignation...
As of March 13, 29477 at 2:30am I have resigned my post at Elite Operations.

My reasons for leaving are personal and not related to any one person or issue within in Elite Operations and the Elite Academy.

My good friend Nayan has for close to the same personal reasons has resigned his post as acting President of Elite Academy as well.

Nayan handed over Presidency to Schadowen, a respected member of Elite Academy and friend, also nothing malicious was done by Nayan with the Org

Any and all property that belongs to the Orgs between myself and Nayan will be returned promptly.

I wanted to take this chance to stop any rumors, slander, or attacks made on Elite Operations or my own person because of Nayan's and my own resignations.

I look back on my time within Elite Operations and the Elite Academy as wonderful experience and I do not regret ever being part of their Organizations.

I wish remaining members the best of luck and a bright future, I also wish to Thank every member that helped or assisted me and others in every way they possibly could.
