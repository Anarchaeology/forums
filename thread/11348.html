in-game "sell terminal" of some sort
quote:
There have been suggestions on an in-game "sell terminal" of some sort, where players can sell their items and others would be able to buy them from there. Give us your feedback on how you would like us to implement such a feature.
you mean like the omni-bot? the neutbot? chat channel auctions go on now with only the difficutly of having to meet the person on neutral ground. but you mean instantaneous... such as "why go to the store when i can pull up five on the web" ehh?

the fixer answer: walk into a normal mission door, like any other door in the area, but while surrounded by a "passkey" nano. instead of zoning to a small room or not zoning at all he'd find himself in a sort of theives' den. you could find shop terminals in here but perhaps they would allow the fixer to buy / sell directly to the chat channel using grid summon nanos and "knowlege" shortcuts?

if not that, then perhaps the ability to cast a nano on a particular terminal and either refresh the items within or to access for a short time items recently sold to that terminal and buy those items for the same price as they were sold to the terminal for.

the trader answer: a sort of "Central Market" exit on the grid via "passkey" nano in the grid where traders (and fixers?) can come together and buy / sell with each other in the intrest, lets say, of putting together and then selling full suits of a certain quality range of armor. maybe even guilds with "deposit only" boxes could own their own terminal which wouldn't sell until a full set was collected. price and time-to-sell determinable by the owner / renter of the terminal.

for lower level traders, to better distribute low quality armors and such there could be trader-only tradeskills to modify expensive backpacks until they become their own stand-alone sale terminal with flashy neon sign above. kind've like a mule pet. this was actually my answer to trade skill items in august when i took a break from AO. it's a little dated so i wont go into far flung detail, but it's still not a horrible idea... perhaps at later levels they could be connected with other similar qual level devices and the profit / loss shared via community. the Rubi-ka Exchange ;)~

conclusion: the trader does just beg for a way to effect the economy. however, fixers probably need more reason to be wanted by a team than does a trader. lets see some more hacking and hiding and crossing of enemy lines: smugglers only pretend to pick sides!

...seems to me the two classes should work together better somehow. the trader just gathers items at home and the fixer smuggles it to someplace people will be happy to pay more for? hmm, that'd only work if a whompa broke or the grid was closed (except to crafty fixers!) due to war...
