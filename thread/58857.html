To answer your actual question, just do what the game does - extrapolate.

So a ql20 set has an AGL req of ((476-8)/(200-1)*19)+8 = 52 or 53 depending on how rounding works.

In general if x = number at ql 1 and y = number at ql200 then

number at ql z = (((y-x)/199)*(z-1))+x

For items that have multiple ranges (ie ql a to ql b), pick the range your desired ql (z) falls into, find the numbers at ql a (x) and ql b (y) then

number at ql z = ((y-x)/(b-a)*(z-a))+x

So for an item listed at ql 41 (number=175) to ql 60 (number=256) and you want ql50 do

((256-175)/(60-41)*(50-41))+175 = 213 or 214
