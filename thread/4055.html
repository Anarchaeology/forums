The Initiative. (RP event on RK2)
The longest journey starts with a single step. We stand, friends, upon the threshold of perhaps the longest journey in the history of Rubi-ka: the journey towards peace.

I propose, and will work towards arranging, a series of visits by Omni-Tek employees to Clan cities. The purpose of these visits will be to create a dialogue between Clan and Company, and the people who make up each.

If these visits are a success, then, perhaps, we can move towards greater things. Because if we begin understanding each other, then perhaps we will stop fighting each other.

In the the words of Nelson Mandela, a great leader of the 20th century: "If we keep hating those who have done us wrong, they will keep us in bondage for the rest of our lives."

Jamison "Kageman" Cicali
