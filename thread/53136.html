The Most Insane Suggestion Evah!
This suggestion will be broken into parts for simplification purposes:

STEP ONE: release the AO tools. Oh I know how scary that sounds, lord knows I wouldn't. But do it anyway. Release the tools upon the MOD communities. Both the Mapping and the Model and Skinning tools, as well as a pack with all the skins and textures.

STEP TWO: Have a place where MOD developers can display there ideas for new pieces to add to the AO world. It won't be long until there are tons of Static dungeons, monsters, clubs-If it can be done the voracious hunger of MOD developers will create it.

STEP THREE: Have a periodic voting on items that the community creates. Maybe a contest for a new bar, a new dungeon, a new monster.

STEP FOUR: New items get patched in on a monthly or quarterly basis. You just need to decide what sort of timeline is feasable.

This sounds frightening I know. But think of it. FUNCOM would be the first company to release a MMORPG that the players actually contribute to the design of the world. It would be...Welll..It would be ANARCHY!

Oh Happy DAY!

AO was not designed to be extensible or dynamic. It can't handle addon's of any sort very well. The game needs a total rewrite of most of the database structure and handling code to have a chance at long term evolution.

The way it's set up doesn't lend itself to having something quicky hitched to an existing door by dropping in a module and setting a link code. They gotta jump through hoops to get items to drop and appear in stores. There's way too much reliance on human fingers and eyes making sure that all the right entries get put in all the multitude of right places. On the outside it looks like a lot of 80's thinking done by bright and diligent workers to get it to float.

It seems that only now, proper tools are being created to do what was mainly done by hand. This is utterly the wrong way to go about it. We've seen how smoothly CS projects go when 80 to 90 percent of the upfront effort is put into tools and in the final stages of the project a product just 'drops' out of the toolset effortlessly.

I wonder if you can imagine the endless hours spend by some schmuck setting up all the cluster recipes and the first batch of tradeskill weapons. I know what they went through and it wasn't pretty.
