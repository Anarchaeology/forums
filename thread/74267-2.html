A solution for omni's
You might not know this, but there are places to hunt outdoors from level 1 to 170 that are worthwile depending where it is and how you approach it. I'm talkin xp here, and lots of it.

Take for instance last nite. A friend and I took a couple 90's twinks out and built a team to hunt outdoors. We took them to 10-3. In 3 hours, I remember 3 deaths. We were getting 15k for slimebathers, 30k for oozelurkers, and 60k+ for biledrinkers. We almost took a marsh warden. The atmosphere out there is unbelieveabe compared to missions.

The Biledrinkers are ~level 180ish, and can be a source of XP for levels 100-180. Higher level teams just need to kill lots of them and faster. They are approx. veteran xp, maybe a little lower. If you use your strategy right you can make the xp flow in faster than missions (think good tanking + area nukers). Guaranteed.

Last week we had 35ish twinks in Galway county. Hit up the manteze. Insane xp for the level and it was fun. Actually getting to walk around, and root and shoot... etc.

Oh and if you are concerned about loot, both of these hunting expeditions turned up multiple bracers, notum frags/chips, instruction discs. People went home very happy.

I agree there needs to be more places to hunt. I'm damn glad there is somethin out there, and that I can actually convince people to get some fresh air sometimes.

Heh, I remember when hunting was more popular. Then we figured out how to pull missions for 20k (before team missions). The xp was rockin, the save was close. I regret showin people how to do it and I'm determined to get them back outside.

Anywho. That's my bordem rant while I wait for the servers to come back up. If anyone ever wants to hunt outside at any level look me up.

It's because the essence of the challenge lies in the leveling.

Making an alt is easy and definately more fun. What is difficult is forcing yourself to level even though it is painfully slow and boring. Not saying it's a good thing. At some point players will often look at the game and think "All this effort isn't really worth anything, in the real world".
