I Hate Helmets
In the good old days I could solo missions without bothering with about a helmet. Nowdays that is a luxury I can ill afford.

Could we have some alternative head armour that was invisible.
I don't really care how you explain it away, I figure if you can get away with pillows as lethal weapons, you can get away with anything.

Even if it was not as good as a the existing helmet in terms of AC, I'd be willing to compromise a bit of my combat effectiveness for the simple pleasure of seeing my face from time to time, and I am sure I am not the only one. All req combos Str/Sta, Agi/Sen and Psy/Sta should be catered for.

Well, that's my dream...
