I am going to add an idea along the lines of the "pokemon" theme (although i can't stand that show, it did spark a couple ideas).
You can summon 1 leet per Adventurer toon (ever). this leet then turns into an inventory item that can be right clicked on like a engi's bot's shell to be "let out". once you're done with it, it can be double clicked on to return it to your pack.
The positive note to this leet is that the older it gets and more time you work with it (perhaps the more missions you complete with it at your side), it levels up and works like a token board. as long as you have the leet out with you it adds small buffs to you, depending on it's "token level" (for lack of a better phrase).
Naturally, since you want to have your leet for the duration of your adventurer's existence, it doesn't not draw aggro to itself.
