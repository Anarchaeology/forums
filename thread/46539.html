(B.R.O.E.S.) Statement
Omni-tek and Clans:


With the rising threat against the CoT presented by the newly found CoA, B.R.O.E.S has come out of the darkness to provide support for our long time trusted Council Of Truth. We will give all due respect to the warriors of CoA. But in this we ask the same respect. We will remain peacefull until otherwise provoked. In the event that any CoA members become militant or violent against the CoT members, we will retailiate in manners similar to the Dust Brigade. I have no futher statements to the COA.



Dust Brigade: Your continuous attempts to gain access to the CoT building has been monitored. Your warriors are great, and your skills must command respect. Again we ask for the same respect from you. BROES has the coordinates of your base of Operations. We know where you train. And we've watched you for some time now. If another act of violence occurs against the City of Tir , we will also retaliate with great vengeance and furious anger, ...hell will have trouble describing the fury that will befall you and your people. We do not believe in the cause you advertise. And so far 4 Masked Commandos have died at the hands of BROES members. We will take more lives until you are ready to negotiate with us.


Omni-pol: BROES travels into Omni Ent time to time. We are not trying to do 'unjust' or 'terroristic' things. We are almost always hunting a criminal, or providing Surveilance ops for our CO. We do not wish any further violence like that of 6 months ago inside the bar known as the 'Rhompa' . The bomb-device we displayed to you was only a decoy. It was by no means dangerous. Only a method of confusion to prevent further violence. We will however take actions back at you, if you continue to harrass us as we do our buisness. If you want to make this a physical conflict. We will be happy to oblige you.




Edward Cordiero 'President of (The Brotherhood of Eternal Sleep)'
BROES
