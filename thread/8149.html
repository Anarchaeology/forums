Clan Heinlein Micro-FAQ

*** Hello?

Hello! We're still here, and we're still dedicated to securing the freedom of the individuals of Rubi-Ka!

*** What's the ultra-brief on Clan Heinlein?

Clan Heinlein's Mission is to create and foster individual power. The first duty of all members of Clan Heinlein is to learn as much as possible and pass that knowledge on.

*** When are you going to make it official?

Thursday 2002 January 24 at 0200 UTC.

N.B.: That's actually Wednesday the 23rd on US clocks!!!
January 23 1800/6p PST  January 23 2100/9p EST

*** Where?

In the Council Building courtyard in Tir.

*** I have a question about clan organization!

I won't have access to all of the clan in-game command information until the clan is actually formed. Because of this, there will be a lot of questions I won't be able to answer until the first meeting.

*** There's a meeting?!?

Our first meeting is tentatively scheduled for Sunday 2002 January 27 at 2000 UTC (1200/12n PST, 1500/3p EST, 2100 CET). Attendance is voluntary! This will just be an informal get-together to get to know each other, ask questions, et cetera. Also, I should have all the details by then, about clan ranks and so forth.

The meeting will be at Enjoy It While It Lasts, the nightclub just east of the Council Building. Punch and pie.

*** CAN I HAVE UR SWORD?

No!!! I really, really need it!

*** I have more questions!

Please feel free to send a /tell to Gordin or Gaabriel in-game. Also, the Clan Heinlein web site is available at http://clanheinlein.britomartis.net/ for more details.
