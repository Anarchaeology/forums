Outdoor missions
A solution to the boring, endless BS missions, would be the following:

1: Outdoor missions, that could work as quests. You could have gotten them in mission terminals, but it would generate outdoor missions too, with different goals, which was just as rewarding as ordinary missions. They should have been connected to different real locations on Rubi-Ka, like the Clan Tower in Lush, a Omni Tek Outpost, ICC headquarter, locations around the world.

2: Then, you should be able to get missions which didnt require killing. Tradeskills missions, delivering, finding, talking and stuff should all be equally rewarded as the bs missions. People now dont do other quests due to lack of xp and rewards. One mission could be..."One of our top secret spies are trapped within the Sentinels Base, your mission is to find him and get him out the base safely."
Other missions could be linked to your professions, like an Engineer could get "There is a broken machine in Clon**** that needs repairing. With your skills you might get it to work!"

3: Rewards! Make more uniqe and special items as rewards for longer and bigger quests that took a lot of hard work and time. I would love to see maybe a Small Ebony Figurine or special loot in missions too, but they shouldnt be easy, and they should require teamwork and skills to get. But increasing the rewards makes the missions more tempting, no matter how hard the quest is. The best example is the Fixer grid, imagine all classes having quests like that, but in smaller scales, for all levels. Rewards are important!

What do you guys think? Better than the usual BS missions? I would love doing different missions that would require traveling outdoors, hunting, cleaning up an area of nasty fleas or finding a person hiding in Omni Forest!
