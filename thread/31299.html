Alvin/Dodga Quest: Greasy Joints Spawn
Alright, I think we're agreed that there are issues with the spawn on the particular bot named "Greasy Joints".

For those of you who don't know, all of the bots are on a 10 minute timer, EXCEPT for the one known as Greasy Joints in Newland.

This creates a lot of problems. The main issue is that the second part of the Alvin/Dodga quest involves "observing" and "tagging" this bot.

Well, this poor bot just gets destroyed every time that it spawns, because only 24 spawn in a day, and everyone needs that chip to get their final pads...

It IS possible, contrary to what some people believe, to tag this bot while it is under attack. The issue is that in order to get looting rights, you pretty much need multiple alpha strikes on your team to outdamage the other people around.

What this comes down to is that the bot dies in under 5 seconds most times. This isn't always enough time to observe and/or tag the bot.

Unfortunately this causes a lot of ill will between the people at the spawn. The times I've seen it, there are 50-60 people at Greasy trying to tag/observe/kill the bot.

Why don't people wait for some to tag? Noone trusts anyone. My teams have had kills even at other bots taken from us due to concealed people who pop up at the last moment. Moral of the story? If you want the chip off this bot, you have to immediately and without question attack it the instant it spawns. If you wait for people to tag, someone else will shoot it first and get the chip you've been camping for.

It's a classic spawn camping problem. The pads really aren't that great, they just look cool, and ... well... it's something to do. We don't see the length of this kind of problems in the Smuggler's Den for MKII armor because ... well... it's harder to get there. Less people = less problems.

The lower levels get the short end because there is just this single quest in the game, and there are a lot of high levels starving for content as well, and also have the capability to effectively take the quest away from the lower levels. Is this their fault? No. It's just a side effect of Game Mechanics.

So what's the solution? Level limits? Well, then the high level people miss out and get angry. Shorter spawns? Well, then they (the pads) become more common than they already are. It's worth something if not everyone has it. That's just the reality.

What is the solution? I don't know. Make the spawn 10 minutes like the rest? Then you have more people with the pads. Is that good or bad? That's up to the designers to decide.

They just need to know that it's an issue that is creating a lot of bad blood. This is probably because it's so easily accessible. It's also probably because it's the only quest in the game right now. I also think anyone wants more camping, and I'm not suggesting this.

I hope that anyone who responds will do so in a rational manner. That's most certainly not what happened at the spawn tonight, when 3 ARKs and a GM had to show up due to people shouting obscenities.
