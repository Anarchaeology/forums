Nameless Trader looking to join a Shadow-Ops type Org
Omni-Tek Herald Broadcast:

Recently spotted on Rubi-Ka is the mysterious founder of Skycom Industries. Little is known about this reclusive man, but the legends surrounding Skycom Industries' corporate espionage are numerous. No one knows his true identity, going only by the name he gave his company, and yet he may be one of the most successful traders of all time. Skycom Industries competed with Omni-Tek in trans-galactic communications, and Notum technology. Few have been successful competitors against Omni-Tek, and with good reason, Omni-Tek's ruthless CEO, known to all as Boss Ross. Such is the case with John "Skycom" Doe, as Omni-Tek's underhanded political ties have mired Skycom Industries in a swarth of red tape, as ongoing audits of su****ious accounting plague the company. Following an indictment from ICC Justice Division on Syrius 3, Skycom staged a daring escape from the ICC prison ship, as he was being transported to his trial. Most believe he will seek revenge against Boss Ross for the setup, and it's no surprise that this reporter clearly saw Skycom in an official Omni-Tek uniform. What better way to disgrace Boss Ross than to rise through the ranks of Omni-Tek, and perhaps one day unseat him as CEO.

Rumor has it that he is looking to join a Division of Omni-Tek specializing in Shadow-Ops, as it will allow him free reign of Rubi-Ka, and very little supervision.

I sent a registered tell to Skycom, and it was received, so if you are recruiting out there, please, take him in, so that I might have more juicy stories about his exploits for our readers.

Reporting for the Herald, this is Kent Brockman, signing off......
