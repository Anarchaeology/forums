Beginnings in Rubi-Ka
I am Cyronic. My birthname was Quinn Wagner, but since I've taken on many names, Cyronic being the most recent.

Let me tell you the story of how I came to be on this planet. It is not long, and I won't bother you for long.

I came to this planet at the tender age of sixteen. I remember the first time my sparkling blue eyes set their gaze on Rubi-Ka. A land of opportunity. Choices to be made, jobs to be filled, money to be made for everyone. It sounded all to promising then, how naive I was.

Even from the start i didn't have a good standing with Omni-Tek. Omni-Tek gave me the free ride to the planet, but like everyone else that took them up on this offer, I had to sign some contracts. And sign I did. However, even from that early age I knew not to trust anyone. I had read the contract throughly a couple times before even thinking of signing it. As it turned out the moment I got to the planet, according to the contract, I would basically be mining. But I had reasons to go to the planet. No family, no friends that I could trust and count on, no job, and no home. I thought I would be able to get a better life.

The day after signing I took the contract into the local Omni building in my town. Another part of the deal was that I would get a discount on an NCU, and other various peices of neural equipment. With all the money i had just saved on a free trip to Rubi-Ka, I could take the money I was formerly saving to buy a trip to somewhere else (anywhere else, really, I just wanted off the planet and this was perfect for me) and spend it on these implants and upgrades.

Do you remember training for your neurals? Remember how powerful you felt the first tme you had all that information flowing through your head? To look at someone and be able to tell exactly who they were, to look at an object and automatically receive statistics, histories, and detailed descriptions on the item. The mapping capabilities, the bodily monitors, everything! It was like having the whole world in your hand.

The person that trained me came to be a friend after a while. She took pity on me, seeing that I had no place to call home. My last few days on that miserable planet where spent in her apartment. She wanted to see me grow up to be something, to make something of myself. She was the only one that actually cared, and to this day, while I might not agree with Omni-Tek as a whole, I do believe that the individuals that make up OT are very caring and compassionate people. Admittedly there are the bad ones. But have you ever stopped and wondered if things would have been different if you would have met the OT you had just killed in a bar instead of on the battlefield?

Before I left the planet she met me one last time. I'm not going to reveal her name, that is sacred to me, and I wouldn't want any enemies I have to go to extremes and.... The last time we met, she told me "Quinn, Rubi-Ka is a tough place, but you'll do fine. however, I want you to take this." She had given me a Baseball Bat. I looked it over once, held it in my hand, felt it's weight. "It was my brother's. It saved him from a lot of bad things. I want you to take it, it will bring you luck." And then she smiled at me "That and I know you can't shoot a gun worth a dam."

Landing on Rubi-Ka I felt releaved. I had just left my past behind, and I was facing a new future. But of course with no money to my name, I took up residence in a backyard, Backyard 8 of Newland. So Betty (the bat I so aptly named) and I took up a living killing the local leets and what not, getting the little cred that Omni gave. But life there was not so rewarding. I started to go outward. I found some work at a bar, busing tables, and over the course of a couple years I eventually got promoted to dishwasher, and the janitor. So many times where the restrooms cleaned

By the age of 21 I decided that I could do so much better than what I was doing now, so I decided to go back to my Rubi-Ka roots, and away I headed back towards Backyard 8 to start my life anew. And again, with barely a credit to my name I arrived again. So I choose what I wanted to do with my life. I decided, I'm not that smart, and that I've had expeirence with bar life, so I chose to become a Enforcer, because then I could become a "freelance" bouncer of sorts. So with Betty in hand, I vowed not to leave the backyard until I had the money to do what I wanted, or the expeirence to be good at what I wanted to do. And so began my new life on Rubi-Ka.

And that is the beginning of what I came to be on Rubi-Ka. So I, Quinn Wagner, can rest a while. But please, call me Cyronic. I haven't told you how that name came to be, but that will come a different time. For now, remember: Time is but a shadow. It is meaningless, and irrelevant, but things will live on, thus giving it life, and making it seem like so much more. Time will pass and new stories will be told. I promise the next time I come back that I will bring new stories with me.

Cyronic
Time is but a shadow, yet it seems like so much more
