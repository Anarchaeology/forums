Team mission boss only?
Hello Funcom,
I've read the preliminary patch notes for 14.4 and I have logged into Testlive to check out some of the new changes. I have to say that I am impressed with what I have seen. You've given higher level players places to play without forcing them into missions and made it easier to get to some of the more remote areas of the game world. I applaud you on that.

However, what I don't see is what bothers me about the patch. I notice the begining of a trend here. You've placed new low-level nanos in the basic vending machines, but failed to place the nanos into the advanced and superior machines.

Why must you continue to place profession balancing nanos as mission boss loot or worse random mob drop? Unless teamed with guildies, fixers tend to have a difficult time finding teams; some of the new fixer nanos may allieviate that (NCU Buff). Yet to acquire these new nanos, fixers will have to find teams to go on team missions below the level of the fixer (thanks to the dark blue nano skills). No team wants to do that. The only other alternative is to try dealing with the economy by way of the shopping channels.

Also, I have noticed that the Debuff protection nano line has been omited from the patch. Any word on that?

Please fix this. I don't want to be uB3r l33t, I just want to be viable and worthwhile to teams.

~Hoopspro
