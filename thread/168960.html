The 29477 ARK Expo of Rubi-Ka

Greetings and salutations, folks. Very recently, the Advisors of Rubi-Ka (ARK) held their first expo here on Rubi-Ka. For this expo, ARK teamed up with GridStream Productions to provide live music and entertainment, as well as contests for many different prizes.

DJ Tarryk, whom I had a long chat with during the party, had good things to say when asked if this was the biggest party that GSP had ever been involved in:. Saying yes would do it no justice, Tarryk said. This is easily at least four times bigger than any event we've ever been involved in, and easily twice or three times as long.

The party started off at the spacious Reets Retreat in Stret West Bank. The whole dance floor of Reets was rocking with the beat of many a person dancing to the tunes being broadcast by DJ Tarryk and the GSP crew. Prizes given away over the course of the two-day event included millions of credits, T-shirts from Jobe, and many Childhood Memory teddy bears, among other things. During this party, the ARKs held a fashion show displaying their various uniforms that they have available for wear. Every rank and department in ARK pretty much has its own style when it comes to what they were. Coordinator Jenjad of ARK, while sporting a eye-catching black dress of her own, was the MC for the display of a dozen or so ARK members who were showing off their work uniforms, and what the ARKs refer to as their Galla, or dress uniforms. This display took place on the lower floor of the club area at Reets Retreat.

The expo itself was a display of the various things that ARK has to offer, should you ever be interested in joining. Essentially, it was a large Q&A session and display for the people of Rubi-Ka to satisfy their thirst for knowledge of the ARK program. ARK had several tents set up in the ICC area, where every department of ARK was represented. Every person on Rubi-Ka who wanted to talk to an ARK got the very rare chance to do so without having to petition with a problem first!

Mara Doctorum Jobe, an Applicant with Disciples of Omni-Tek, said, I think this expo was a work of art. A party at Reets Retreat with great music and a nice person to person talk with the ARKs.

Based on what many people have already said, this is pretty much an echo of the thoughts of quite a few residents of Rubi-Ka.

So there you have it. An awesome party, an outstanding fashion show and a very worthwhile and informative expo. And lets not forget the entertainment from the DJs of GSP that was nothing less than good to go.

Joshua Hardcharger Mandrake
