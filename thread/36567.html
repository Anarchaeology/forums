Message of the Day function for Orgs
Okay Funcom,

I'm sorry to say but EQ one-upped you in this category. Organizations in AO can be big, and you can't always just be there to tell EVERY member what you need to tell them. MOTDs should be available to the Org leaders to drop a quick important message (i.e. "Guild meeting next week! Important! Please try to come!") as soon as the org member signs on. There's an org website but not everyone knows it exists or what the URL is... ("Visit the website for guild updates at www.guild.com")

I'm pretty sure that I'M not the only one who thinks this is a good, helpful idea that will prove *extremely* useful to Org leaders. What say you?

Love and happiness,
