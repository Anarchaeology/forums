Well all in all the patch notes look good! But the one I like the most is this one right here.

To me, the best thing about levelling is that you can help people lower level than you. You can give them equipment, money, buffs, whatever. As a young doc, I was really dependent on my friends. Now that I'm higher level, it's really nice to be able to help others out.

I understand why some people want this change. In the form described by Cz, it has enormous holes in it; so it won't come close to accomplishing it's goal. The many problems with it were discussed in great detail in Proposed design: If you can't be attacked, you can't assist with buffs and healing.
