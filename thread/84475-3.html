The thing is, though, that we weren't always heavily reliant on nano skills.

A few patches ago, the long HoTs and NCU buffs didn't even exist.

That left us with a starter SMG buff, some bothersome short HoTs, damage buffs, roots and snares that were mostly worthless, grid and speed nanos.

The grid nanos were of occasional use, and nobody ever wanted one of the short HoTs.

Remember that the Fixer was conceived as a generalist class; decent all-around, but not outstanding. This includes trade skills, the green ones of which, incidentally, figure nicely in creating things like the MCS.

That's changed some, especially since the advent of Grid Armor, but FC isn't going to tinker with the basics (skills) of ANY profession, apparently. All they do is add new nanos and gear.

Also recall that someone from FC said that you should not use the QL or minimum level to cast listed on a nano as a guide to what level you, as a fixer, will actually be able to cast it. I think that sounded like a load when I first read it, and I still do, but that's the "official" position on the matter.

If you think Fixer nano skills are bad, take a look at the Soldier's. I think they're worse. They just use fewer of the nanos available, and fewer of the skills because of that, it seems.

Once you reach GPA (Grid Phase Accelerator), I don't see a huge need to move to GSF (Grid Space Freedom) too early on.

I think that those IP, for the 12-18 levels you will not have GSF, are more valuable spent in evades/base atributes.

I started casting GSF really early. Unfortunately I did so, before I had breed capped my INT. Once it was capped, I was over the needed skill to cast GSF.

Since there are other programs now, requiring SI, it's not much of an issue if you have already made this mistake. However, it can be a problem if your a non-traditional fixer and use FA or AR skills. If you do, your really tight on IP between level 160 and 174. You all know, these are the levels where it starts getting MUCH harder to level more than 2 times a day.

Just a bit of advice, from someone who has been there;

Don't go for the best programs in any school till you have breed maxed your base atributes.

NanoAugmented, some Fixer advice, from a lvl 142 Non-GA solitus fixer:

A few people have been posting about the importance of implanting nanoskills...in my opinion, implant as many as you can without compromising the full shining, burst, faded set of MG/SMG, and other important fixer skills. As you know, nanoskills are dark blue and cost the most IP, so its just wise to me to implant them the most as this will, obviously, in the long run save you IP. I have also opted to implant the full set of shining, bright, and faded clusters for BM, so that I can cast the best HoTs, but it really doesn't matter which nanoskill you choose, because either one you pick, you are going to save the most IP. Picking one that is dependent on your base abilities may help too(str/stm, sens/agl, etc.).

Here is some info at my level, that may help you later on, as a solitus and a non-GA fixer, if you don't get your hands on GA. As you'll find out, and FC has stated, its going to be difficult to actually cast the QL nano that actually corresponds to your level.

What nanos I cast:

Run buff: Grid Phase Accelerator, with exp buffs.
Long HoT: Systolic Equalizer, no exp buffs.
Short HoT: Flawless Medical Claim, with BM exp buff.
NCU buff: Recompiling Memory Analyzer (have skills to cast next one but can't cast it until lvl145).
Suppressor: Spray with Lead, unbuffed.
Snare/Root: Gravity Bindings/Prolong Encounter.
Other buffs: Blood Makes Noise, Stack the Odds, Lucks Fickle Fate, etc. and by this point you should have gotten both fixer grid nanos, solo and team.

Weapons, what I have done and has worked out pretty well:
Go with a Kerans until about lvl 40, and if you can get your hands on a Gamma Ejector (NODROP weapon found on Bodyguards in Foremans Dungeon) go with one of those until 75. With a ranged init between 500-900 or so I would gowith an Mausser Chemical Streamer. Once you hit lvl 125 or so, see if you can get a TIM scope from TIM in the Foreman's Dungeon. This will add 180 to ranged init and should put you between 900-1000 at this point. Now is a good time to get your hands on a ql 200 Manex. Theres a huge debate about MCS vs Manex at this point, but IMO with that high of a ranged init, Manex out does the MCS. There is no set path of course, these are only recommendations. Go with whatever you like of course

Armor: This will of course depend on which abilities you decide to choose of course, but if you're not GA by level 75 or so, I recommend looking into to getting some Cyborg Death Squad armor. Stats on it are excellent and it has some nice ranged init, evades, max health, and other modifiers. I used to be a str/stm fixer, but since CDS has come out, I've been all sens/agl, so that I can wear it .

B&E: at 401 atm to cast team fixer grid nano unbuffed. I find that buffed I can open chests in most missions, and I'm might raise these skills a little during my cap right now until 150 so that later on I can strip down higher QL implants, working my way up to 200s eventually.

Yeah and I agree with Insida on the whole NCU buff thing...always have the latest ones. To some people, that's one of the main reasons they want you in their team. Its also good to have the best HoTs as well for blitzing missions and those occasions when a healer class isn't available. And another thing...get used to having to tell people to close their old NCU buffs when you are refreshing it..I'd make a macro if I were you

If you have any questions, please feel free to tell my main in-game, Hollowburn. I'd be happy to answer any of em.
