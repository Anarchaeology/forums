So that there are no questions regarding Nwchat...

Nwchat exists to:

1. Provide a (relatively) secure chat with access control by organization userlists.
2. Provide a relay of All Towers so that people know who is under attack and when (it really annoys me that only organization leaders get this, and our organization bot does the same thing (which is why it's currently the President of SF)).
3. Provide me with a log of All Towers so I can keep track of what's going on in the world.
4. When it's done, provide a relay to my orgbot (and potentially others) so that I can demote my orgbot back to applicant.

It is oriented toward high level PvP (150+). I honestly couldn't care less about PvP below 150.

There are no rules or regulations here... it is an entirely neutral environment.
