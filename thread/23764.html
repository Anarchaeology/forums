The recipe you have is the order you assemble stuff. Just start at step 1 and proceed to the last step ... in the order listed. The problem you most likely are having is that the weapon recipe you are using is broken. Thier are a few that work. The majority of them don't.

So if you are following the recipe steps correctly, have the correct parts and it doesn't work ... well that means the recipe is broken. You can send Funcom a bug report but weapon making is an extremely low priority for them.
