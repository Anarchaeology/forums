Math question about skills
Ok, this is gonna sound wierd, but I am sure there are some math wizards out there that can help me.

I am wanting to figure up how a base stat modifies a sub stat.

For instance:

Matter Metamorphoses is dependent on Intelligence (80%) and Psychic (20%).

Matt Meta: 293
Intelligence: 183
Psychic: 174

When I buff my Intel and Psychic with boost (12 pnts to each) my Matt Meta buffs up to 296.

I am wanting to know how this is calculated.

I tried doing something like this:

y=((.80*183)+(.20*174))+293

Now that is obviously wrong.

Does anyone know what the forumla is for calculating the increase to a sub skill when buffing a base stat?

Attributes add one point to a skill for every four points of the attribute.

If you have 100 points in Intelligence, your base CompLit (based 100% on Intelligence) should be 25 points, assuming you haven't put any IPs into it.

Let's say you have 100 pts in Int, and 100 pts in Psy.

MatMet is, as you said, 80% Int and 20% Psy.

Thus, you get ((100 Int * 0,8)/4) + ((100 Psy * 0,2)/4) = 20 + 5 = 25 pts added to MatMet from ability scores.
