Manana,

The South Rubi-Ka Society is all about roleplaying, and for the time being our bias is European. In game the Society functions as "gentleman's club" ( we even have our own butler now). The purpose of the Society is to organise cultural, charity or educational events for the citizens of Rome and Omni-1, as well as enhance its members social life. Think of real life clubs like Rotary International or the Lion Club and you would not be far off the mark.

In ooc terms, the purpose of the Society is to offer an escape from the levelling treadmill and focus on the life of our characters when they are "off duty" (i.e. not out hunting or on missions). Currently we are running an table top style rpg adventure for our members based on nano-formula drugs in Omni-Sewers and a Society sponsored drug rehab centre Omni-Sewers which is under threat by some local thugs (of course, there is more to it, but that would be telling). Other events have included an after-dinner talk on the theory behind metaphysics, a string of events surrounding professional Leet Fighting (Rubi-Ka's answer to dog fighting) or simple day trip to visit to the ICC HQ in Andromeda. Basically any excuse to interact in character or make Rubi-Ka feel more alive.

There are no level requirements to join the society. All you need is to be Omni-Tek on Rk1 and have in interest in roleplaying. Ideally we aim to recruit people who are interested in creating events as well; in pen and paper terms ideally our members are as much Gms and they are players, but that is not a requirement. A good sense of humour is of course always bonus :-).

If this sounds like something you might be interested in visit our website <http://rubika.no-ip.org/srs/> or give Marog, Nephilous, Tappert, Heksa or Savoy a /tell in game. You can also reach me at the following email address gsoylent@hotmail.com <mailto:gsoylent@hotmail.com>

Alternatively you can check out the thread http://forums.anarchy-online.com/s...?threadid=15307 <http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=15307>

for a list of 50 odd other rp guilds.
