Neutral no longer
Today while attempting to exit ICC into Tir, I was met with a nasty suprise.

Commader Wickham has hit you for 4177 points of projectile damage! Critical hit!
You have died of weapon damage.
. . . .

So, the decidely un-neutral actions of a single organization have removed the only real "advantage" that we had as neutral members of the planet, namely, we can no longer travel freely among the cities of the two factions.

How is this fair? Since when does a single organization have the right to change our relationship to the clans (or to OT)?

It is my opinion that this organization should have their "neutral" status revoked so that we can try and begin to repair the damage their unilateral action has caused our relationship with the clans. By jeopardizing the neutral status of us as a whole, they have shown that they are unwilling and unable to keep themselves from domination by a faction in a war that we are not a part of.
