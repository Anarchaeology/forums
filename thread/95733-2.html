Just pick whichever you want.

A few general PvP tricks that might enhance your survivability :

- Use unexpected gadgets like root / snare boosted graft on an opponent. Many programs break easily that kind of thing, but you can get a few seconds more to reach a safer place.

- Use morphs on the battlefield : even if people to usuallt TAB for targetting, being an /anon Leet among atroxes won't make you a favorite target.

- Stay on the run. Don't spam heals from above a hill, move in circle staying close to your team, use towers and trees to interfere with targeting if you can do that.

I'd like to add a couple thoughts after fighting at the MR base takedown over the weekend. I couldn't hit the towers (only like 3 people from guild total on were to defend if I made us 25% gas hehe) so I spent my time mostly with healing people on the towers, and a little with PvP when omnis were around my bunch of tower killers.

But mostly I healed. And I got a lot of thank-yous for it from people that I saved Whatever FC did to fix lag/CC it seems to help us out a lot for healing others in a mass battle.

At level 200+ battles I suggest you stay far back from the towers with your range increaser - if you go into an area where tower circles overlap, by healing you will draw aggro from the other towers that don't have anyone hitting them. Really it helps at all battles (I mean why tank anything if you don't have to?) So it helps to turn on the tower circles to find someplace to stand, but then I turn them off until I need to move to another tower because there's too much clutter.

QL 200+ towers hurt alot, and a couple 180+ people around me were killed by them on Saturday night. For a while I was the only doc healing tower fighters with the southmost attack team on the MR base, and it wasn't cool watching a couple MAs and even an advy die while I was in recharge from CHing someone else. Remember those melees have to run right up to the tower to hit it and so are almost always getting aggroed by those towers you are avoiding with your range increaser

Don't be shy to ask for another doc to come help you - I did, someone came, and we didn't have any more deaths to the towers while the two of us were there.

If I'd had the chance last night I'd have liked to team up with all melees so I could use A&O on them while they beat up towers. I think that would have been more efficient and easier on my fingers Instead I was crtl-tabbing constantly for hours and my hand still feels kinda cramped

On that note: I found that crtl-tabbing worked alot better than assisting the tower for saving more people. Like I said there are those "add" towers that overlap range with each other, and the occasional defender who takes a pot shot at someone then runs before they get alphad to pieces (sometimes successfully sometimes not hehe). Mixing the two seems optimal: do an assist on the tower to make sure you can heal its target first, then start crtl-tabbing to fix up other people.

So - new NW advice from me: At a high level tower battle stay with the tower killers and heal them. Don't get too distracted by PvPing omnis, trust other people for doing PvP cover. I only switched into PvP mode twice - once while the Omnis were trying to keep us away from the CT cluster and once when so many people were on the CT and there was so much lag for me I was CHing corpses instead of living people so I left.

I also found that knowing peoples' names helped me alot. I knew Ameno and Gothique and Nuuka were NTs so I kept a closer eye on their health for reflects damage. I knew Tinysmasher and Locol were eqb enfs hitting towers and drawing lots of aggro so I looked for them and make sure they stayed alive.

UPDATED: 5/28/03
Rifle, or more specifically, the Aimed Shot special, is coming up for an 'adjustment' in the near future. In other words: Caterwauls and Ithacas are getting nerfed. For a while now, I've been seeing more & more doctors shelve their rifles & ithacas in favor of short-range burst weapons; SMGs & the PoP. Time & time again I've tried to tell the aimed-shot enthusiasts:

Don't get overly worried about it.

Why would I say this, when the whole world is in an uproar over the impending Aimed-Shot-Nerf-of-Doom®? What rumors have I been privy to, regarding this nerf?

First... as one might expect, Funcom is busy with Shadowlands, as in all-hands-on-deck busy. Meaning, they aren't working on much of anything else at this time. The proposed aimed shot nerf is not slated to be implemented neither prior-to, nor with the Shadowlands expansion. In other words, there is no one working on any aimed shot changes at present. Also... there is still some question as to whether or not any changes will be made at all.

Secondly... there is no plan or proposal to make aimed shot a once-per-fight maneuver in pvp. Nor will one be required for a player to be in sneak-mode to use aimed shot vs. other players. The proposed change will require the player using aimed shot to have a certain measure of the skill concealment. I.e., you don't need to be sneaking, but you must be able to sneak.

How will this change aimed shot usage in pvp? Very little, unless said required levels of conceal skill are outlandishly high. If there is a conceal-skill check made vs. the target player's own perception, then aimed shots will be very easy for many players, because it is relatively easy to hide from other players via conceal.

Take note: If this is indeed going to be the case, then it is likely that even a title 5/6 doctor would be unable to effectively use aimed shot vs. title 5/6 agents who've maxed perception. To this I say 'good!' ..because I've met very few agents who stand a chance vs. a strong doc. Agents could use a bit of a boost.

Whatever the case may be, agents are unlikely to be affected, & title 5/6 players might want to consider maxing their conceal skill... not too big an issue for many level 180+ players. However.. lowbie pvp might see some changes, as many lower level non-agent players simply do not have the spare IP to waste on conceal. Or... they might be forced to seek out pronouncement of greatness/cloak of night buffs prior to battle in order to use aimed shot.

Why have I decided to share all of this now? Well... it seems that Morphex has let this cat out of its bag just recently. The report that Cz passed along to him dovetails with what I'd heard. And suffice it to say, I still enjoy my snakemaster greatly ..I'm glad that I did not IPR & I will have concealment fully maxed by next level.
