Harry's - just outside the gates to the grid area on the Eastern side of the road.

The two (Actually four) in Rome are 2 in Rome-Blue, 2 in Rome-Green. Go towards the common wall of the areas with Rome-Red, in the center of the area are two shops, one advanced one superior. The advanced has the yalms you will be looking for.

Also note that there is one in 20K, 2 in Trade, and at least one in Omni-E.

In 20K go East of the Main store it should be on the North side of the road.

Trade - all of the stores are on the outer ring. If I remember correctly one of the southern side and one on the Eastern.

Omni-E - The one that I know about is straight across from the Rome Whompa (Could be more as I've found 4 basic regular stores throughout the area)
