quote:

Checking effects will not generally "find" sympathetic nanos. The reason behind this is that within the nano itself, it casts another nano. The other nano is the one with the crit effect on it, but it is not a castable nano.

Eventually, I may add logic to check nanos that are casted by other nanos, but I wouldn't be able to go too deep since that kind of search would a lot more intensive.
