The Thrilling Adventures of H & H
This will be the start of the stories of two people, one native, one not on Rubi-Ka and how they see the world and its inhabitants. It is our hope to move our characters into and through the lives of all roleplayers on this planet, Omni, Neutral Clan native and not as enemies, friends, rivals or just background. We will gladly support your stories and events with our weirdness please say hi or shoot at us when you do see us. We consider the Timeline to be Canon, we have watched the films we read others forums. We are new to Rubi-Ka and look forward to exploring the world's wonders and making our own tiny mark here. The story will have chapters with the rather slow start of a prologue, hopefully with a little luck it will make for moderately interesting reading when we actually do fall into adventure. -ed.


The Adventures of H & H

Introduction: PlanetFall and Fantomas

Hirunda stepped off the unmarked Sol Banking Corporation ship onto the bare landscape of Northern Rubi-ka on the edge of the City of Athen. A huge sense of wrongness hit all her senses at once; low rusty roiling clouds, the distant crackle of electrical storms promising violence, an odd smell on the dry breeze, a strange taste in the back of her throat when she breathed in, a slight stumble when she walked forward in the pull of subtly different gravity.

So this was the home of the notum? The land where death was cheapened by expensive life insurance. How would that change people? Would they become callous thoughtless killers? Would it make the struggle for world consciousness and a separate identity from the ever voracious Omni-Tek that much more vicious? If people got used to killing insured people, would they think nothing of those uninsured?

What was death like? Would it be a strange metaphysical experience or just a memory glitch?

The shuttle-pilot spoke on her comm channel "Move to a safe 200m distance, we are taking off in 1 minute, count down commencing now."

"Moving out, and thank you for the trip. Good Journeys."

"Welcome. Good luck with your new home. Ending transmission."

Hirunda jogged carefully over the strange new terrain hearing the light whir of the shuttle's steps as they withdrew and the soft clunk of the outer door shutting. As she reached a safe distance she stopped and turned to watch the shuttle shoot upwards into the covering layer of ruddy clouds. She tried not to think of the clear blue skies and soft white clouds of Earth.

She took a deep breath of the metallic tasting air and cleared her mind. Long term mission goals were what ultimately drove her, but for now she had to deal with the short term; food, shelter, contacts and support.

She had a letter of introduction to a young Cuban Doctor not much older than herself. The Doctor, now calling herself Dianechti, had been destined for OmniMed and Hirunda carried the document disk which included various messages from the Dr’s family. These were to make a good contact in case she had needed access to some of the advanced treatments that Omni could provide which the Clans might have trouble getting hold of. However, upon reaching the orbit of Rubi-Ka Hirunda had discovered Dianechti was now listed as belonging to the Clans instead of Omni. Time to find out why later, for now she had suddenly leapt up her list of possible contacts from potential aid, to primary link to Clan society.

Once Hirunda reached the outskirts of Athens, she made her way to the shelter of one of the Apartment buildings and sat herself down on a cushion nestled inside a used tyre and called Dianechti's comm channel.

"Hello there, I know this is out of the blue, but I'm from Earth too; just in. My Dad is a friend of your Uncle's, and he suggested I contact you when I got to Rubi-ka..."
----------------


Destiny Cifuentes was in the middle of a mission when the low pulse of her OmniPerComm XE, a graduation gift from her parents, went off. Her Desert Reet hummed from the energy of the projectiles while her mind raced with the execution protocols necessary for the next group heal program coming online in the chaotic battleground around her.

She had been serving the cause of the Clan and agreed to aid a group of individuals bid to rescue a hijacked weapon prototype Clan engineers were developing to keep up with Omni wizardry. Since leaving the Omni enclosure after being educated by the trader Krozman, the leader of Fantomas, Dia was in a race to catch up. Catch up on the "real" history of Rubi-Ka and Omni-Tek, catch up on the triage skills to keep people alive on a hostile world, catch up on the information needed to move around in Clan territory. She ignored the message to allow the nanite cloud to course from her interface to the rest of the group sealing wounds and increasing clotting to keep them going a little while longer.

A couple hours later she sat by herself in the Happy Rebel a bar on the northside of Tir, exhausted and nursing some expensive bottled water, no doubt smuggled over from the richer side of Omni territory. Finally alone, the rest of the team returned the prototype and began bargaining for their expense needs. She was taking the opportunity to rest her mind and body and allow a blank nothingness to erase her thoughts. Someone’s comm unit went off near her and jolted her from her dazed state and she remembered the pulse during the fight. Digging into her backpack she pulled the sleek black unit out and looked up her messages.

She had considered not keeping the unit when she had changed over to the Clan, she was sure OmniMed would be none too pleased in losing an intern they had supported with scholarships back on Earth, but she couldn't cut herself off from the possibility that her parents might still be able to reach her, somehow. Reading the message containing her tio (Uncle) Ricardo's name from a stranger made her suddenly homesick. Whomever this chica was she was a link to her familia and therefore a potential friend.

She looked around the quiet room, only the bartender was near her and the huge Atrox was known for discretion. She cradled the unit and attempted to respond to the message hoping the woman would still be available to talk.

"Hola? Señora Pauly? Esté es Destiny, Destiny Cifuentes. Call me Dianechti. Sí, como estas?"

She used the familiar term because of the family connection, and listened to what Hirunda had to say.

----------------------

Hirunda had agreed to meet Dianechti in Backyard 16 in Old Athen itself. Hirunda regretted choosing an Apartment in the small West Athen suburb of the main City, it turned out she had much further to run to reach the centre of Clan activities, and the large teleportation network that connected Athen to the rest of Rubi-ka. The run outside on the brown baked earth was good though for acclimatizing her to the planet and the investigations ahead of her.

She passed through a wavering force field, something to keep the vermin out she supposed, not that she had seen much wildlife close to the town.

Athen was a crumbling city, dark-stained stonework, fallen walls, old out-of-date signs and notices, the remains of shops and businesses. In between the ruins some buildings were maintained. There were shops, and the brightly lit insurance, mission and banking terminals were well used and easy to find.

She followed the directions Dianechti had given her into a reasonably well-maintained Apartment block, through into the yard behind. She scanned the area until her eyes alighted upon a thin coffee skinned young woman sitting in the shade of one of the canopies. Hirunda wandered over, a smile on her face.

“Dianechti?” she asked holding out her hand to be shaken in greeting, not sure if that was a custom here. Hirunda thought ::So much to learn::

The woman rose in greeting, she was extremely tall and thin with long wavy hair and large brown eyes, but her smile was big and bright at the extended handshake. “We don’t shake hands much here, more bowing and waving, its more formal and more paranoid at the same time, but I’m glad for the courtesy,” Dianechti explained.

Hirunda continued, “I’ve brought the letters, perhaps you’d like a chance to read them before we chat?”

Dianechti nodded and took the disc and slid it into her own comm. The machine quickly accessed the documents. She looked past Hirunda for a minute, maybe seeing through the grungy plasticrete walls all the way to Old Earth and to her family left behind. Smiling to herself, she refocused on the red-headed stranger in front of her.

“Your padre knows my tio? Then mi casa es su casa. You need help to move here, I needed help, but that is my profession. I will help you, chica.” Another smile was flashed. “I will help you by introducing you to a very important man, el jefe de Fantomas, Señor Krozman, he knows the Clans, he knows Omni, a very knowing man he is and generous for ‘the cause’.”

She spoke warmly and passionately sitting back down onto the makeshift chair and offering Hirunda a can of cola she had picked up at the market on the way here. She brushed a long heavy strand of curls off her shoulder and considered the prickling heat which spoke of a coming storm.

Hirunda smiled and nodded at the warm and friendly welcome and took the can of cola; to think that those letters would have been waiting many more weeks to find their destination if things had not turned out this way. For all the training and dedication to her mission that had left her self-contained and able to walk away from family and friends for what could be a lifetime, she had missed the warmth of friendship. It was rare delight to be offered it so unconditionally.

Hirunda popped open the can and swigged back the cool sweet liquid so welcome in the heat. She listened as Dianechti continued.

“You’ll need papers, registration into the Clan linked to your DNA, identities that Omni will consider a Clanner, maybe a new immigrant that went rogue, enough of those coming in every day now. Your DNA could be checked back home for comparisons with Earth records should OmniMed choose to do so. Do you wish to undergo Gene therapy? We could alter some harmless genome pairs enough to change the sequencing. This will at least stall an instant match that they can pull from an insurance machine if you’re killed and come back.” She let that information sink in a little. It always took offworlders a while to make the mental leap in using the machines and what it signified, it had been the same for her. “I have enough skill now that I could get the programs for a surgery clinic and we could do that for you,” Dianechti offered.

Hirunda paled slightly as the significance of Dianechti’s words sank in; a big hole in her plans so early on, thankfully revealed by a friend and not an enemy.

“I think that would be a wise idea.” Hirunda added quietly.

“You’ll also need money and equipment. Esto es un mundo hostil, a dangerous world. Cyborgs, mutants, lots of Omni-Tek’s failed biological experiments, also OmniPol and others…” Dianechti mentioned with the distaste and disgust apparent on her face.

“I’d heard about those.” added Hirunda shortly as if unsure what to reveal. “My parents were survivors of the ‘419 bombardment of Earth when Omni-Tek took out large chunks of corporate property, including the buildings my grandparents worked in. My parents both grew up in an SBC Creche™, and met and married within the Corp. Your Uncle met my father when SBC started unearthing and rebuilding one of the fallen buildings. It was a cross between an archeology and a construction project.”

Dianechti listened, fascinated at the telling of history as a personal event.

“I have some papers that say I am a new immigrant from an over-populated colony world, and that I came in on an over-packed cheap rate passenger ship that landed in neutral territory. My new name Jacelyn Pauly will not be one your Uncle recognizes, but I suspect neither of us will be calling home for some time.”

Dianechti talked with her for the next couple hours, listening to news of Earth especially in reference to her family. She explained what little she knew of the theories of insurance, of the mining currently going on and how the Clanners made their living on the non-terraformed planet while Omni-Tek lived fat and green. Dianechti accepted Hirunda’s story and took the connection for potential friendship, chatting easily enough with her.

“Shall I call the Señor now?” The doctor picked up her comm. unit.

Hirunda’s head whirled with new information from yet another perspective; Rubi-ka was very different from the ground, it wasn’t just a corporate mining colony it was home to thousands of people battling daily for their right to live on it. She was here to do what she believed was right; the fact she had not signed an employment contract with SBC protected them from any legal reprisals to her actions, but perhaps it also protected her principles from corporate interference.

Hirunda looked up at the stars, not knowing which one was home, she looked over at Dianechti suddenly realizing the woman had spoken sometime ago.

“The Señor? Oh yes, that would be very kind of you.” Hirunda smiled at Dianechti and sipped her cola.

Dianechti got up and walked a ways away talking into her comm. unit at what Hirunda presumed was the head of Fantomas. Shaking her head she came back a few minutes later and sat down looking apologetic. He says he is unable to meet now but will contact you soon he will fly to meet you, I told him what you needed, but he is a trader, yes, he will want you to do something for him too. But he said it will be soon, maybe even today.” The doctor smiled reassuringly then spent the rest of the afternoon going over some programming charts to set up the surgery machines for an operation Hirunda agreed to go through.

The Adventures of H & H

Prologue I: From out of Darkness...

Heket sat quietly amongst the weeds in Backyard number 4 of the run down West Athens neighborhood. It was quiet at the moment without the sounds of ground cars or patrols passing by and the air was heavy with a crackling presence. She felt the ionization around her as her cloud of nanites, perpetually with her, buzzed on the available chemical change in the atmosphere. There would be a thunderstorm soon, she knew, and that always excited them.

The young woman was dressed simply, unadorned in some old black leather pants and a shirt. She was a nano homo, average height, average weight with the signature black, black hair slicked back in a forgettable style to be out of her face. Her face was the only thing remarkable about her. Facial markings were common amongst her breed and hers were asymmetrical curves and delicate scrollings that complimented a softly angular face. Less sharp and narrow then most nano's she had a delicate Madonna-like look. Her eyes were shut and she often wore an expression of intense listening or trepidation upon her features.

"You like them don't you Sylvie?" Heket murmured and the nanite cloud turned an almost imperceptible blue then went back to being transparent.

Putting her hands in her lap she listened to their electronic chatter.

"Yes I forgot, Fred likes them the most," she always spoke warily when Fred was mentioned. The most dangerous and angry of her 'friends' she didn't think it wise to rile him up. Last time he had taken offense at something he blew off part of the wall in Uncle Krozman's apartment and shattered one of his ferns.

She was thankful to Fantomas and the Backyard 4 neighborhood that had brought her up after her parents had been killed in an Omni-Tek raid. She still didn't know the name of the lab assistant who had saved her but she had been told her parents were Omni Tek meta-physicists who had turned Neutral to expand upon their research with a group of like-minded scientists.

She frowned, she needed to give something back, she was old enough now, plenty of her peers could do useful things, have jobs, join the military, explore the planet what was her role? Yet she had never left Athens, never been to a bar, never held a job. She helped on some small errands and was often a worry to the neighbors. It wasn't like she was helpless. She was fine in enclosed spaces, even the city, she could move around freely until she came to large open spaces of nothing. Those scared her, made her want to hide. Sometimes her friends helped her and would describe things to her or hint upon a way, once a Voice had helped her to cross an open space and find a safe haven. She trusted the Voice and wanted to know more about it. She called it Planet. Uncle Krozman said her parents must have been great experimenters, that they even used themselves as test subjects for finding out more about the spiritual properties of notum and what it could do. Perhaps they had been trying to speak with Planet. But he hadn’t known them or the assistant who had rescued her.

Hearing the rumble of thunder, Heket finally got up and made her way to the entrance to the city. "I'll be back, don't worry about me." She said aloud to no one and hoped her letter explaining her errand would allay all fears.

"Come on Nancy and Sylvie, you too Fred, we need to find someone to help or who can help me," The cloud turned yellow then orange then went back to transparency about her head, but no one could see it.

"What? Oh I don't know, I'd like to be useful, I'd like to find and meet Planet and thank him, I'd like to learn more of my parents or repay back all the kindness of raising me to these people here. I want to DO something!"

Leaving the safe haven she ventured forth as the rain came down hard and darkness turned everything black. She didn't even notice the change as she followed the wall to the bridge that would cross over into the main city.

--------------------
Heket was sitting down in Old Athen listening to her Uncle Krozman chew her out about her latest outing and why he had been called to come and fetch her from the downtown guards. It wasn’t her fault what happened. She had been hanging around near the whompahs where people came and went hoping someone might need her to fetch something for them in one of the shops, something she could do. She had felt her body tingle with an invasive nano program, something feral but non-threatening when she had been turned into a large thing. From the shouts of monster, wolf and dog she figured it must have been one of the desert forms she had heard in news stories that she got read to her. All she did know was she was confused and didn’t know where she was in that form too when she stumbled around into Miir’s and destroyed their display. She hadn’t even felt any discomfort at supposedly what had been a disaster, and Fred was laughing himself silly about the whole thing while Nancy tried to soothe her.

“What am I going to do with you? You know I have trades to make and an org to run, girl! I can’t be watching all the time and you won’t stay at the backyard!” He threw up his hands in frustration, a fruitless gesture with Heket, he knew, but it vented some of his concern and stress. Maybe you need a trade, you could sit and program or something…”

The trader felt his comm. buzz and checked it, it was the organizational channel used for business transactions only.
