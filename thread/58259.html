I think you have some wrong info, Cz or that you guys are talking
alil out of synch.

There are three types of Yalmahas now.

Release 11.0
+Yalmaha XL 29478

Patch 14.6 (currently on test-live)
+Yalmaha 29480
+Yalmaha 29480 Gold Flash
+Yalmaha 29480 Vivid Red
+Yalmaha 29480 Obscura
-Theese still have same chassis.

Patch 14.7.07 (Notum Wars beta)
+Yalmaha - 29500 - The Poniard
+Yalmaha - 29500 - Custom Adaga Brutal
+Yalmaha - 29500 - Custom Adaga Elegante
+Yalmaha - 29500 - The Stiletto
+Yalmaha - 29500 - The Adaga
-Theese have a completely new model / chassis.

Now, you can convert your existing 11.0 DB Yalmaha XL 29478
into the Yalmaha 29480 with Paint Kits. I'll give one example.

Item: Auto Paint v. 303 - XL Gold Flash
Description: Use this item on your 'Yalmaha XL - 29478' to
customize it with a decreased susceptibility to 'Root' programs, a
new layer of paint and an overall boost to your defenses.
Price: 12000000

As you can see, Cz, you can upgrade your Yalmaha 29478 to a
new 29480. Adding root protection, defence modifier and a new
layer of paint.

Now, onto the Yalmaha 29500's.

We have the Adaga which is customisable with two paint kits.
Result is two Customised Adaga models.

All 29500 have the same chassis and can be bought, except the
29500 Stilleto. (The black one). I'll list it's info:

Name: Yalmaha - 29500 - The Stiletto
Description: The Yalmaha has always made good, 1-man
vehicles, for any situation. Each new model breaks the limits set
by the old, both when it comes to technology and price. The
Stiletto is Yalmaha's Executive special design.

It has no specific price since you need to build it with a "Yalmaha
29500 - Stiletto Building Kit". The finished Stilleto will be
a "Unmarked Yalmaha 29500 - The Stiletto" which is not NODROP,
meaning you can probably get a engineer to build it for you.

As you guessed you have to register it to use it. You need this:

Name: Stiletto Registar
Description: Use this item on your 'Unmarked Yalmaha
29500 - The Stiletto' - to register it with Omni-Tek's special
executive vehicle registry. IMPORTANT NOTE: Once you combine
these two items you end up with a NoDrop vehicle.

Hope that cleared it up.
