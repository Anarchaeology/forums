just need some more opinions on something..
A group of people get a mission in BS (whoda thought? ;p), during the clearing of the first room (alpha loot is on), soldier_01 loots grid armor 4 from a corpse. everyone is all shocked it dropped of course. fixer_01 says in chat "hey i'll take that plz".
soldier_01 didn't see that in chat or something.

so fixer_01 gives a tell to soldier_01 asking if he could have the ga4 that just dropped, and what he was gunna do with it. soldier_01 isn't responding still, even though hes obviously at the keyboard.

fixer_01 gives another tell to soldier_01 asking if he was just gunna give the ga4 to fixer_02 in the group (who already has ga2 btw).

fixer_01 sends another tell asking what the soldier is gunna do with the ga4.

fixer_01 finally gets a tell back from the soldier saying that he could easily get 600 dollars on ebay for the ga4. fixer_01 is pissed at this point, obviously.
a few other group members are asking if they were gunna roll on the ga4, but all the soldier is talking about is "how lucky" he is.

a week later fixer_01 finally learns that soldier_01 gave the ga4 to fixer_02 who was soldier_01's rl friend.


..now, my question is, should the group have rolled on the ga4?
should just the two fixers have rolled on the ga4?
or was the soldier free to do whatever he wanted with the ga4 just because he looted it?
