Armor and Buffs
OK, suppose that during a mission, I found a piece of armor that I could not wear because my Strength and Agility weren't high enough.

Now suppose I found someone who gave me a super big buff to those abilities, which carred the numbers above the armor requirements.

Would I be able to put the armor on then? If so, what would happen when the buff expired? Would the armor stay on? Or would it fall back into my inventory?

And if the armor stayed on after the buff expired, would I then be able to equip/unequip the armor at my leisure? Or would I need a new buff every time I wanted to put it on?

Thanks in advance.

After the buff you could put the armor on. Once expired the armor would stay on. If you subsequently take the armor off, you could not put it back on.

This is will change a bit with the upcoming 14.2 patch.
If your ability/skill is < 0.8*required skill/ability then there will a a penalty applied to the effectiveness of the armor or weapon.
