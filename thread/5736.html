Jan 2002 the make or break month for AO?
I'm afraid that FC is in for a really big surprise come 2/05/2002. This is the common date for all 6 month subscriptions to renew and to tell ya the truth it doesn't look good for very many of those subscribers to renew no matter what profession they play.

In fact I fear around the first of Feb that RK2 will become a total ghost town and my guild will dry up completely.

To any funcom employee reading this thread: Please be aware that changes made to AO during the month Jan are your key to success or failure. I would suggest that devs start working over time, this will be the most crucial month for AO's survival.

/me keeps fingers crossed that FC gets a clue fast.
