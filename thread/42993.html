Reading this trash that we seem to love to see published lately... http://www.anarchy-online.com/conte...ews/articles/42

it strikes me that our so-called 'editors' have taken to publishing just about any unsubstantiated garbage for no other purpose than increasing their readership.

I'd like to point out that all the interviews with the main clans participating in CoA have indicated NOTHING of a military coup. Instead these interviews have indicated a desire for CoT to either stand up and do the job they were intended to do or to step down and let someone else lead. I see nothing to suggest that CoA is interested in anything other than Clans freely electing their leaders and demanding that current leaders either do their job or get the hell out.

Tomorrow CAS Commander Redruum will be giving a speech. Neither he nor his 2nd in command nor their personal bodyguards will be armed. He will TALK.

Hmmm...talking...is this what CoA opponents fear? Talking? Is this want CoA opponents wish to stop? Simple public outcry over the POOR job our current so-called leaders are doing? Seems that CoA opponents are the ones against freedom...freedom of free speach. Seems to me like CoT are the dictators, reluctant to either do their job or step aside...hell bent on staying in power despite the rising tide of a vote of no-confidence from those they pretend to lead.

And as for the Neutrals? These opponents of CoA would have you think that CoA is somehow against the neutral community. I'd remind you that I, an Advisor in CAS, am married to a neutral and have always been a major proponent of neutral rights. It is a CAS official policy not to, without provocation, kill neutral players, guards or NPCs. And we enforce this. To my knowledge NO CAS member has killed neutral guards so long as I have been in office, and each and ever CAS member knows that they would stand trial and very likely be removed from CAS if they did. CAS leadership also gives notice to major neutral guilds such as IR whenever we are conducting military operations in areas such as MMD. Nyadach of IR can vouch for this policy of CAS. How many of the opponents to CoA can say they have similar official policies and rules in their respective guilds?

No, I'm sorry fell Rubikans, this published trash is NOTHING but empty political rhetoric designed to maintain a status quo that has us forever at the whim and mercy of Omnitek. The Tir Accord was an abomination to the Clan and as to the neutrals it was nothing short of an insult. Today Omnitek views neutrals and their territory as Omni territory and subjects only. They have made this clear. To continue to support the CoT in their effort to fly off-planet on their own little 'personal discovery' vacations for months on end and not leave us with ANY leadership is unthinkable. The CoA has only one goal...to demand that this CoT dead-wood step aside and allow election of true leadership.

To the opponents of CoA I say you are nothing short of cowards and lackeys of a defunct leadership, the CoT. It is YOUR inaction that both leaves the Clans under the constant threat of Omni incurrsion and the Neutrals in a rapidly decaying situation where they can never truly govern themselves.
