The fixer: Making a specialised fixer
Later discussions of this forum have circled round the part of fixer which is a major lack: specialising the fixer.
I will try to come up with some ideas here, on how to do it, and what i would like to see it done.
First of all, it would require some bigger changes from FC/AO, regarding "title".
(Just ideas and thought though..)


All paths would of course include some sort of underworld contact network, organization, channels, etc.

Thief
Skills: break&entry, concealment, trap&bomb removal, perception, run skills (to run away if detected or when mission is completed)
Nano: Here is the lack; Buffs on the skills above, and some non-aggro nanos to calm mobs. Also gridding nanos to grid to area/out of area.
Title level, IF specialised that path would be more like: Lvl 30: Expert thief, lvl 50: Master thief, lvl 100: Grand thief

Courier/smuggler
Skills: gridding, running, concealment, some hacks, navigation,
Nanos: Buffs to help the above of course. Also include some sort of "vanishing" nanos, to either make yerself or even item vanish (to not get caught with that item)
Title level, if specialised that path would be: lvl 30: Transporter, lvl 50: Courier, lvl 100: Smuggler

Sniper/Torpedo/Assasin
Skills: Weaponry, concealtment, sniping, stalking, poisons, evades, maybe some brawls
Nanos: DoT, targeting, roots, and of course nanos to buff the above skills.
Title level: 30: Sniper 50: Assasin 100: Torpedo

Hacker
Skills: B&E, computers knowledge and other electronic knowledge to use, alter and manipulate computers
Nanos: summon computer thingie, where he summons access to computer terminals or networks, nanos to aid or buff the skills, hacking electronic devices (nano to hack running NCU's for example) etc. Easy to get the picture of this one.
Title level: 30: lamer, 50: hacker, 100: L33t

Mmkey, this is 4 paths which could be an option.... Just a brainstorm from me, input and feedback is wanted and appreciated
Ideas for other paths, skills, nanos, etc: GIVE ME!
Maybe we at least could hope for this to come in AO, if its a good enough idea, and we get enough support for this?

Would be ultracool if we could specialise this way (maybe we could choose these paths from beginning of profession, giving us BONUSES, extra skill points from beginning on the skills needed, or maybe the possibility to have 5 ranks per level?) Of course could this easily be adapted to other professions too, but since fixers are kinda gimped, this would be a nice way to make fixers more fun and varied.
Another side of this would be the problem with mistakes on skills, where u put in points on skills, but in mistake: CHANGING professions is as per today impossible, and RETURNING points is also impossible, but with paths in professions could a profession maybe change PATH at least, giving him the extra points or bonuses or whatever BACK from the mistaken skills, so he can change path instead?

Last note on this:
Giving a profession this option to vary would mean a lot, in my opinion, making a personal, varied, specialised character, making it unlike other characters. it could to some degree mean some professions also would overlap eachother a bit, and making professions usable in team missions, to fill in gaps needed in missions. For example could a fixer and agent work well together in an assasin mission, fixer and trader in some theft/smuggler mission, crat and trader, etc etc.. I do guess you get the big picture

I agree all on all parts expect the idea that we are some type of bully or assassin. I you think of the Fixer in underworld terms, then the Fixer is more of the mob boss or the lone rogue working on his own. This is where the enforcer provides the muscle and the assassin does the needed underhanded killing. If anything, the Fixer is the godfather of the underhanded classes.

Obviously a specialization any type of specialization tree would take some time to code and probably won't happen on Funcom's part. But I do think now that a Fixer has to specialize. In fact, I've always said this. We have many options, and we want to be all of those options, however we must specialize in a few of those options and forget the rest.

Like in Diablo II, each class has various skill sets, but try to put points into all of them, and you'll be a master of none and have your skill points spread so far apart, none of the skills will be strong enough to endure the later levels. So you have to specialize with something like a bowazon or pikeazon for the Amazon class. Each class ended up having their own subsets that the players themselves designed.

Thus, I believe its the same with the Fixer. The problem is, our class has never been truly defined. And the other classes don't seem to have as many options as we do. They may have to decide whether to go 2h-blunt, dual pistols, single pistols, whatever. But we've got a lot of options in many other areas that other classes don't have to worry about. To further add to the confusion, many of the tradeskills were broken in the beginning, a lot of our roots didn't work and we have only one choice for a weapon. So whereas, in the beginning, the Fixer was not really a broken class compared to the others, the game itself has changed as well as the other classes but the Fixer remained the same.

So the Fixer is broken by the fact that his environment changed, but he did not. One thing that might help to "unbreak" us, is being able to reset our skills. For in game purposes, maybe we could pay to have one of our skills drained of its IP and reset. Then we could reallocate those points to where we want them. I know back two months ago when I was playing this game, I put points into nanoprogramming for both implants and the thought of when we could make our own nanoprogramms. But now, making our own programs requires three other skills. That's not looking as interesting anymore as making jewelry. I'd love to be able to drain the points from nanoprogramming and put that into other skills.
