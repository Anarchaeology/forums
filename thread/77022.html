Notum Wars. But why ?
Ok. Now it is here! Well atleast for some people and maybe for the rest before x-mas.

But now what ? We can buy IP sinkhole vehicles for million of credits just so we can get "uhh, ahhh, ihhh, wow !"... Yes i know new yalms are only showoff items.

Ground vehicles... Well in Beta i was NOT impressed by them, except for their looks. Why even use them ?

Towers/landcontrol:

In the weeks before NW was to be realeased i could hear most of my guildmates say: "Man, im bored" "Booooring" etc... Now they fight like crazy and building towers almost 24/4. Ok, i guess they aint bored. But how long will this last? As i see it NW will be interesting up to the point where people have fought over some land for a few days/weeks, tried to buy a new vehicle and looked at the "new looks"..

Then what happens next...?

I have betatestet NW, i have not been able to purchase it in any local store yet (old story, wont go into that old one again) . Now i see all my guildies go crazy in these "laggy tower fights" , but actually when i think about it, i really dont need NW, now do I ?

Yes i need it if i want to be fancy looking in a Yalm/ground attack vehicle or if i want to place towers, which might be pulled down anyway by our president just becourse the location nolonger suits the Guild...

So, all in all, i actually cant think up any reason to purchase NW when the time comes. I can still go see the towers/defend them. I still get the "Side XP thing" and i dont need any new vehicle.

So, please tell me "why" I should get a copy of NW for 20$ and what then ? How long will this keep "highlevel" people going.. My point is i cant really see any reason to. The "drilling of notum" well what do we really get from that ? I dont see any drilling..

Just my 2cents... aint a flame just an observation...
