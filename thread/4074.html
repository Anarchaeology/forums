Lifeless Immortality
Why join Lifeless Immortality? Well, why did you become Clan? To fight for freedom right?

That's what Lifeless Immortality is fighting for. In fact, we are doing as much as possible in the fight for freedom. Our primary goals are as follows.

-Unite the Clans (determine the best cause of action for freedom). With many voices shouting the same message in unison we will be heard. Omni-Tek will listen to our demand. We will be independent.
-Locate and remove/expose Omni-Tek Spies. We cannot fully stand and shout in unison if Clan impostors are among us.
-Promote growth through free exchange. Remember, as Clan we are not about wealth. We gladly exchange armor, buffs, implants and weapons with one another to grow as we chose.
-The Longest Road Pact. We don't expect everyone to join Lifeless Immortality, but we do expect Clan guilds to unite in the fight for freedom. Currently, in its initial stage, The Longest Road Pact is our way of promoting Clan unity. We strongly urge all guilds to participate in The Longest Road Pact.

In case you haven't noticed, the above mentioned does involve role play. Lifeless Immortality is not just about role playing. Our secondary goals are as follows.

-Leveling. We've grouped together in hunting parties. Feels good to help out your fellow guild members in reaching the next level.
-Role play support. Granted, you might not be into role playing, but you'll help our cause just by showing the Lifeless Immortality name in your description box. Strength in numbers my friends!
-Swap meets. Again, you might not be into role playing, but take advantage of our "free exchange" program.
-Team missions. We've assisted one another during missions, and we'll do it again. What better way to get that super high level item than by having another guild member assist you with the mission.
