quote:
True neutrality means staying out of it all,
IMHO you are describing True Pacifism, True Non-involvement, or True Apathy.

Your definition doesn't hold for a large portion of people with regard to the conflict on Rubi'Ka or otherwise. One could easily be a Clanner and 'stay out of it all' for their entire lives. Likewise for Omni.

True Neutrality for a ton of people means not having an opinion regarding the conflict. This doesn't mean stroking your leet for your entire life. It simply means that you are Neutral as far the conflict between the Clans and the Corporation is concerned. You simply don't care who ends up the 'winner'. This could be through mutual disdain, alterior motive, lack of information, mutual respect, or any of a number of reasons.

Generally, when a battle occurs between the two warring factions, the Neutral will simply not care who wins.

Now assume this Neutral org has a legal mine operating outside City X. They have a Clan neighbor who has shown them respect and courtesy. There is an Omni organization that has tried to destroy their lifeblood 16 times. When this Omni org attacks that Clan organization, this Neutral org will support their neighbor. Does this make them Clanners-at-heart? No.

You can swap 'Omni' for 'Clan' in the above paragraph and it will still hold true. The Neutral org does not subscribe to either of the ideologies. They don't fight for any of the stated purposes in the conflict, only survival. For all intents and purposes, the attackers could be mutants or cyborgs and the Neutral org would help their neighbor. It has zero, zip, nada to do with the petty 'war' that is going on. They don't believe in the war. If they did, they would throw support to one side or the other. They don't. They're Neutral.

Neutral doesn't mean pacifist. Sometimes 'fence sitters' like their fence. They don't drop down on one side or the other because neither side is very attractive. They deal with having to mend broken posts and rails because of their commitment not to aid and abet something with their moral support that they honestly don't believe in. They value their own commitment to their own ideals. Not the Clan's vision of the future and not the Corporation's way of life.

I'll sit on my fence until such time as I feel good in my heart offering my soul to some cause. Neither cause has any merit at all IMHO today. Until then, I protect my brothers and sisters of like mind and shield them from corruption and harm from whatever direction it may come from. I will attempt to help them and their families to prospers as I'm able.

I won't sit in a corner and stroke my leet.
