Cluster QL's
The minimum qualities for clusters that fit into ql200 implants are:

Faded - ql164
Bright - ql168
Shining - ql172

As stated above, implants don't go down in quality, they only go up and they only go up if the person making them has more NP than required. I don't recall the exact formula that dictates whether implant quality goes up or stays the same.
