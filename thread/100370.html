It's a good way to make money at low levels.
High levels, blood plasma sells for about as much as a normal piece of boss loot.

Higher levels, Pharma Tech is used to combine some of the new JobeClusters, or refined Nanoclusters into refined implants.

Pharma Tech is also used to create Restoration Kits which are stims that add +health and +nano, and can also make emergency treatment libraries which heal for much more health than the ones you buy in shops or find in missions.

I would invest IP in Pharma Tech when you have extra ip, if for the simple reason that its green, cheap, and almost nobody raises it except for Doctors anyway.
