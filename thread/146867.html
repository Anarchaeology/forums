Jerald Carnes found dead in Rompa Mens Room
Ardoyt was in the Mens Room earlier and he was going to wash his hands and he found the sink was overflowing and a body under it and a person in black was standing there.

When Ardoyt tried reacting the figure knocked him out.

Ardoyt awakes to find a huge cut in his hand and he feels woozy, he then sees a wallet and he looks inside and sees Jerald Carnes' ID Card (Jerald Carnes is an Intern-Op of Omni-Tek). He talks to Omni-Pol Shadow Ops and Rejeckted takes over and Ardoyt talks to him about the murder and Ardoyt faints from poison in his body from the cut.

Ardoyt then heads back and spots the figure in the restroom and then he talks to Rejeckted and spots the figure on a shady couch and he rushes to him but the figure just teleports using a strange device like he used in the restroom.

Ardoyt then talks to Hemkhan and Hemkhan uploads the data from the note found in the couch.

Here is the note
quote:
Dear Assistant #6563730004373,

You are hired to kill the Omni-Tek intern-op, Jerald Carnes. Use the provided Sol Banking Corp. Perma-Death gun and the Sol Banking Corp. Teleportation Device. Jerald Carnes will be in Rompa this Friday. Thank you.
If you find any info pertaining to this contact Omni-Pol or Ardoyt using the CommLinks.

This is a public Broadcast by Ardoyt.
