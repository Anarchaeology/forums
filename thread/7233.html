Omni Atmosphere...
I think one unique thing that you guys at Funcom have create (the one thing that sets you apart from the other MMORPGs out there) is the storyline. I think if you want to be successful, and you want to get people engaged and "into" the story, you need more dynamic content to make people "feel" that there is a conflict going on.... I'll give a couple ideas I have for this below:

1) While in Omni--- if say, some clanners decide to go to Omni Trade or an Omni outpost somewhere; have that area sound off alarms and red alert strobe lights in the city. Make a rush of guards come out of nowhere and patrol the entrances.... make it FEEL like there is a real war going on. Maybe have that female voice that is always saying "Omni is your friend" say something a little more meaningful such as: "Warning: Clan Intruders have infilitrated the area! All Omni militia report to the zone borders!" or something like that. Complete with some visible search lights, red strobe alert lights and the movement of OT guards all over the place.

2) I know this has been brought up before--- but you guys NEED to have territorial control where both Clan & Omni can doing continuous battles for control of different areas--- and work that into the storyline if possible. If a certain area is in fact taken over-- make it so the cell scanners and everything in that area gets switched over to the "winning side". Couple this with idea #1 so that you hear warnings and sirens and explosions during the battle and you have some real action happening.

Remember, the mob A.I. alone is simply not going to keep people's interest for long--- you need to dive people into the story (by force if you introduce things like #2). Then you will keep people interested... after all, the story is really the main thing that sets AO apart from all the other MMORPGs.

Just my two credits. What do you all think?
