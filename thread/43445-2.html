There's no question that Engi's are difficult to play. The question is, are they rewarding?

Every time I play my Engineer, the profession's problems irritate the hell out of me, and I end up logging off to play something else instead. However, there's something in my psyche that just resonates with the profession, so I keep coming back. It's probably the fact that the prof is difficult to play that does it - I tried an MA and a Soldier, and dropped both of them as being too boring. The MA was especially bad, because he didn't even really need anything. There was just nothing to aim for.

I have a Fixer who I sometimes dust off, as they're a fascinating class in principle. Trouble is, they have a very boring mission style, so I don't bring him out to play too often.

One class I'm having tremendous fun with right now is the Adventurer. Apart from the fact that I'm constantly missioning for the next piece of equipment, the fact that it's a fragile class with a heal line makes it tactically more interesting than the Fixer with his HoT lines. Even better, with the right equipment (not too hard to get) Adventurers punch well above their weight, which means there's a tendency to tackle fairly tough mobs and missions. Of course, I have no idea whether this holds true at higher levels.
