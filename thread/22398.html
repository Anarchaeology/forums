Audit of Event Calendar as of 23:00PDT June 27, 2002
Funcom, you’ll be delighted to know that I, CompanyMan, have audited your Event Calendar entries for accuracy in treatment of time zones. I have analyzed the data and presented them so that you may see how well your time zone help is working. All events on the calendar as of about 23:00 PDT, June 27, 2002 are used. Here are my results:

RESULTS:
Results are color coded. Completely correct Contains incorrect or misleading information Contains incomplete information
  • 7 events placed time and date in text which matched the calendar. No misleading time issues in text. Congratulations to those 7!!!
  • 8 events were off by one hour because of daylight savings confusion or may be correct but have incorrect or misleading daylight savings information in the text.
  • 4 events had confusing or incorrect time zone related information. (Does not include the preceding 8.)
  • 4 events got the date wrong or had potential confusion in the date.
  • 44 events listed no time in the text forcing us to rely on the calendar time only.
  • 2 events listed a time but not a date.
  • 2 events listed times without a time zone.

CONCLUSION:
Funcom Events Team, people seem to submit on the order on one event per day to your events calendar. It would take very little effort to just make sure that the times/dates are listed in the text, with time zone and daylight savings information correct and there is no other misleading time related information. Time zones are confusing and you have an obligation to help out the community by making sure inaccurate information doesn’t get into your calendar.

MissMaul, the date is actually correct because you are probably looking at the calendar in the default setting which is GMT. In GMT, your event does start on July 31 though it is still July 30 where you are. However, you have not accounted for daylight saving time. If the party is supposed to start at 6pm in Los Angeles time, then it should start at 01:00 GMT. It is listed as 02:00 GMT because you set the time in PST rather than PDT. What this means is that Europeans depending on the calendar will show up one hour late.

I don't think I forgot to include your event as I don't think it was posted yet at the time of my audit. I would love to have included it because it adds evidence that Funcom has to get its act in gear with regard to the inadequacy of their scheduling system.
