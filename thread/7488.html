If I'm not mistaken, its two and a half points added to skills that are 100% based on a particular attribute for every 10 points you have in that attribute. That's why your +12 attribute buff NanoFormulas add 3 to those 100% skills. At lvl 40 or so, 120 points in intelligence would therefore add 30 'free' points in those same skills.

In any event it's in that ballpark.
