The thing is, Yazule, doctors need more damage-per-second output in order to viably solo. The DoTs we have now do nice total damage, and very nice damage-per-nanopoint, but very very poor damage-per-second. The question I am asking is, 'how can we increase DPS without giving doctors aqua and green weapons skills and/or weaponry buffs?'

One way would be nukes, but FC has already turned that down, and anyway, unless the nukes were really efficient, we'd be better of using weapons and saving our nano for self-healing. In other words, we'd either have to have nukes to rival existing nukers, or we'd still be in the same damage-dealing position we're in now.

My personal favorite way would be super-fast DoTs... but would you consider a DoT that pulsed 3 times for 2000 damage on a 6 second timer to be better than a crit buff? Because that's about what it would take to make me spend 1000-1500 nano on casting a DoT in solo situations. For that much nano, the DoT would have to devastate a mob, and fast, to be worth casting instead of saving the nano for multiple heals while I slowly punch and brawl the bugger to death.

But then take that DoT into a PvP situation. It would eat off 1/4 to 1/2 of most people's hitpoints in 18 seconds.

That's how I came to the crit buff solution takes mid-combat casting out of the equation, so we could buff, recharge, and go kill things a bit faster on average without getting DoTs that basically act the same as three chain casted high-end NT nukes with 6 second recharge timers for one keystroke.
