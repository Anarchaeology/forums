I haven't read the entire thread, so don't shot me if I'm doubleposting, but if still needed I'll explain.

If something has "Visual Profession: Nano-Technician" it just means your char has to appear as an NT in the current moment.

If it actually has Profession: 11, it needs the char to be a born Nano Technician.

Simply put, visual profesion means an agent in FP can use the item, while profession means he can't. I don't know of any other effect at the moment, but this is the reason for the difference.
