more alternative pvp
How about an NPC extraction mission like those stated at the beginning of this thread, but with a twist. Opposite side teams of appropriate level looking for missions will be able to roll a mission to prevent you from finishing/completing the mission. NPC is in a locked cage/cell in boss room...key is boss loot. In the mission description include the possibility that releasing the prisoner may set off an alarm, in which case members of the opposite side will arrive and try to prevent the removal of the NPC. The NPC won't actually join the team so can't be gridded out. Once the rescue team has defeated the boss (if an opposing team has chosen the 'stop NPC/team from escaping' mission) and the cage is opened the gas goes to 25%, half the mobs respawn, random doors relock, and rescue team gets another mission. Get out alive. The opposing team enters mission. Both teams have mobs to deal with as well as the 'live' opponents. The mission reward for this second part would have to be greater for the rescue team than for the prevention team, since they have no choice but to forge on. Cash reward for either team would be increased by 50% if all members of the opposite team are killed.

If noone from the opposite side chooses the mission, then no alarm goes off and the original mission is complete as soon as the team hits the outside door.

I have to say that it's very disheartening that this thread has been around for so long with no implementation of any of these ideas. Sigh, once again, maybe Shadowlands...
