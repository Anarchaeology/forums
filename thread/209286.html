Event: Counter-Saboteur briefing (Atlantean)

Greetings citizens of Rubi-Ka, there will be a public briefing on Wednesday, December 10th, 29477 at the ICC HQ in Andromeda at 21:00 GMT. The purpose of the briefing is to organize an effort to root out and expose the Saboteur Cell that has been operating in Omni-Tek territory. If possible, the stolen prototype and data for the nanomage environmental suit are to be recovered.

Further information on possible leads in the investigation will be shared at the rally and possible plans of action discussed. What is known at this time is that the saboteurs are not clan related or funded. This suggests a third party may be intervening in a project that once completed stands to benefit Nanomages greatly. If so, that raises the question of why?

Due to the unique nature of the situation, Clanners and Neutrals interested in assisting will be allowed to attend.

Overview
Date: Wednesday, December 10th, 29477.
Time: 21:00 GMT
Location: ICC HQ in Andromeda
History: The Red Tape's mainframe computers containing vital project information about the Nanomage environmental suit have been wiped by an act of sabotage and the only working prototype suit stolen. The saboteurs have managed to evade capture by wiping out the employee databases there to create confusion and cover their escape.

In all likelihood, the saboteurs will be attempting to infiltrate another Omni department. There is a possibility that the saboteurs may be under the protection of a yet unknown third group. In any case, the saboteurs must be found and stopped.
