My choice
Neutral has truly affected my life on Rubi'Ka. I've never regretted making that choice. 90% of the playerbase plays the same game. Unfortunately, Clan and Omni have no character or defining aspects to them. You really can't tell the life of one of them apart from the other. In the end, they just end up teaming with each other in Broken Shores.

I know that as a Neutral, I led a different life on Rubi'Ka. The early hardship followed by the 25% zone ganking have provided me with reasons to savor every level on my road to 200.

When I get to 200, it will be against the wills of the developers, without a 1000 token board, no easy access to the exploits provided to the two poltiical factions, and mostly due to the fine character of my fellow Neutrals.

Of course I thought I really wanted to be Clan and actually started two Clan characters early on. That was until I learned they were just as much a bunch of jerks as the Omni's that were supposed to be suppressing the planet's freedom.
