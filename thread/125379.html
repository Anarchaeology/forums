quote:
Originally posted by Hawkeye4077
But if NCU were sold in special stores, wouldn't that screw up the economy a little bit for the people getting these things and selling them over the shopping channel?
Not really.

If you look at the price for ql 125 NCU in the store, it's like 250K each (may be inaccurate). Average streetprice of ql 200 NCU is around a million credits a pop. Just design the prices to fit within that aspect of the player-economy.

Regarding special loot, I see no reason why it shouldn't be sold in a store.

a) It would serve as a viable moneysink (Stilettos are not, because I sure as hell wouldn't buy one, but I'd jump a a chance to buy a Black Shirt for my MP). People who have the cash *will* buy the items from stores instead of spending a couple of weeks camping. The point is to drain surplus cash, that cannot be done with foppish things like a Stiletto.
b) It would also serve as an imposed price limiter on certain items (GA would drop in price to below what it sells for in the store).

Certain items that are in the game exist for balance issues or profession improvement. Those are the items that should be targetted by this. Shades of Lucubration, low-end Prof Jones vests, Black Shirt, Grid Armor, Nullity Sphere, etc.
