Keep in mind two things:

1) Implants are expensive.
Example:
A QL90 set of 1HE implants gives +98 in that skill. A QL100 set gives +108. That's shining, bright and faded. So for a full set of QL100 implants, you'll raise your stats by 10 points per skill MAXIMUM. I paid 1.3M for my full set of QL90s. I don't intend to spend another 1.5M in ten levels just for a 10 point increase in some skills. I'd much rather spend that money on armor or NCU.

2) Upgrading implants is not as important as upgrading weapons. Implants give a skill bonus, regardless of level. So if your QL90 implants give you a +98 boost to 1HE (as is the case for me), you will always be 20 levels (with a green 1HE-skill; 5 points possible increase per level) ahead of the curve. I'd say it's perfectly doable to go for 20-30 levels without upgrading your implants. That's hilariously impossible for weapons.

I'd say Hobbes' schedule seems workable. If you have the cash and time to spend, you might want to do one or two in between, but I don't think you'll be utterly gimped if you don't.
