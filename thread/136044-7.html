Khyrene:

1) Stun in this game is defined as preventing movement and attack. The actual word "Stun" is more generally used to describe nanos like Curse of Chronos which are an unbreakable version - so the target stays unabel to move and attack even when you hit it.

The Actual Procs called by the true mezzes and calms in this game against Mobs are:
  • Pacify Target 2 ticks at 1 second
    NPCWipeHatelist Target 2 ticks at 1 second
    Func_53153 Target
    NPCWipeHatelist Target
    Pacify Target

The procs called by the mez pet effect are:
  • Pacify Target 2 ticks at 1 second

    Pacify Target

In fact the two 'calming/pacifying' effects are identical - with the only difference being that the hatelist of the mob is not wiped. This means that once the effect wears off the mob should still be coming after you even if you have moved out of range. For a full mez/calm effect as long as you move out of range of the target before the effect wears off, the mob will forget you were ever there.

A root is actually a completely different effect "Restrict Action Target Moving". During a root the mob can however still attack. This is the behaviour presently shown by the Psychosis pet though through a completely different games mechanical effect. The mob does in fact stay stop moving for the 12 second duration of the effect. Unfortunately, one of the key elements of true roots in this game is that they will not break instantly when the mob is cast upon or damaged. There is only a chance (varying on QL) that the root will break.

So while the MPs can use a 'mez' pet as a rooting pet, it's a very weak root at only 12 second duration and it breaks instantly on any action upon the mob - which unfortunately includes the bugged continued fist attacks of the 'mez' pet itself. So it breaks its own effect. The connected Phantom Damage issues can also then make this a risky affair.
