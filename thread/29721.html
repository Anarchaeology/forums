general nanos- advanced and superior
One thing that has kind of anoyed and confused me since release is that in the nano departments of stores there is the "basic general nano" vending machine, but this is in ALL stores.

It would be highly desirable if I could go into an advanced shop and buy some ADVANCED General Nanos! As well as Superior general nanos for their respective stores and players.

As I recal those general nanos were crucial for most low level characters to make life easier. But as we progressed that vending machine became useless. If there were advanced and superior versions available to all I think it would level off the playing field.

Rather than nerfing and readjusting each char class individually, FC could just let each class have unique nanos and try making things fair with a good assortment of general nanos, for people of all levels.
