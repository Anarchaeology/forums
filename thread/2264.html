Lowburn Interviews Aberic!
I recently had the opportunity to interview Aberic of The Dark Carnival Omni-Tek guild. Aberic was responsible for the killing of the Henry Radiman decoy whom was sent to Omni-HQ in an attempt, according to Omni-Pol, to bring out his assassin. Omni-Pol accomplished what it had set out to do - and drew out the attacker. Shortly after his release from an Omni-Tek prison cell, I was able to meet with Aberic in a secret location - safe from any would be assassins possibly seeking revenge for the death of the clone. Here is what he had to say:

Lowburn: Thank you for speaking with me Aberic. Most people might not be aware, but you were the man who brought down the Henry Radiman 'fake' yesterday. So I think I'll start out with this question: Just what were you thinking?
Aberic: My thoughts are that there shall never be peace between Omni and the Clans. It will never happen. And I will make a stand for what I believe to be the true Omni and shall stand this way forever. The clans are responsible for the borgs and all there problems and I believe also Ross knows more then his is telling even his highest advisors. So I shall kill all Clan I come across - they have been giving a chance to change and it has been long enough time to take care of the rest.

Lowburn: Henry Radiman is a well-known speaker for the Council of Truth. Do you believe you were justified in your actions of attacking - essentially a politician - as you did?

Aberic: I don't care if its a Clan waste collector I'm going to kill it - politician doctor crat soldier - I don't care. They shall all fall at my hands. Omni trained me to kill and that's what I do best.

Lowburn: We all know you delivered the blow that killed this Radiman clone - tell us about the battle. Was it just you vs him, or were there more people involved - and what essentially 'happened' during this assassination?

Aberic: During my first assault he had a 175 soldier with him. I took out the soldier first and then Radiman. Second assault was at the grid location and he was alone this time. I then assassinated him again. Both times where swift and deadly. If I find the true Radiman I will also assassinate him. I am warning all that this amnesty shall never work.

Lowburn: I see, so you alone pulled this off. Some would call this a great vindication, while others may consider it a tremendous insult to the amnesty, both to Omni-Tek and the Clans. Obviously your opinion toward the amnesty is clear though. Did Omni-Pol inflict any sort of punishment as a result of your action?

Aberic: Yes I was imprisoned for a while while some investigating happened and I was found innocent at the time even though I said I was the one responsible for his death. I was found innocent at the TIME. We shall see what the future brings.

Lowburn: Indeed. How did you personally feel when you yourself went up against Henry Radiman. Surely it has to be a monumental moment in your life - most people would be lucky to even see Henry Radiman in their lifetime, much less do what you did.

Aberic: Yes I feel good about it but I expect many deaths such as these by my hands. It is what I was trained to do. I only wish it had been the true Radiman. We at Omni still are working on permanent death and we are not far off from the corrupting of his files, which will result in his permanent death. So I still stand tall and proud of my actions and would do it again if I have too This peace shall never work.

Lowburn: Well - one final question. Do you feel like Omni-Tek made a mistake by alerting Radiman to the assassination attempt, or do you feel that they could have somehow greater benefited from his death?

Aberic: We will benefit from all Clan deaths pure and simple, like the rain that falls from the sky.

Lowburn: Those who share your own views and those who do not are almost evenly numbered at this time. Only time will tell where we are headed. Aberic - thank you for your time.

Aberic: Your welcome and DEATH TO ALL CLAN TODAY.
