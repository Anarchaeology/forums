Omni-Tek End Game Vol 1
[Deep within an underground Omni-Tek HQ Facility]
"Are you mad?" The good doctor said as he was staring at the wrong end of an X-3.
"Mad, MAD?! My dear Sir, im the only sane one here!"
The facility had been here for centuries unknown to the Clans, ICC, and SOL Banking. Within these walls, the most heinous of biotechnology experimentation took place. And now they have reached the apex of all there work. a new class of humanoid life. As strong as the Atrox class and yet as intelligent as the basic Solitus. It was during this time that a former employee of this facility sent vital information to Sol Banking Corp. for a large pay off. Using the information they received from their informant, they sent in a worm to the Facility. Work went on as scheduled.
"This is your dream? Living in fairy tails and creating monsters? What do you hope to gain from any of this?"
"Monsters? even you, your no better then the beings we create!
"............"
"HA! i see you have nothing to say against that argument. Now, sit back and watch as we create the next logical step in humanoid evolution. If I can't be one, I will create a God!
"Im afraid i cant let you do that....{the good doctors eyes start to glow a crimson red} your acts against humanity have gone on long enough!"
"Brave words coming from a man looking at the barrel of my gun...You are of little consequence to me now.... Good Day Doctor Nosferatux!"
{He pulls the trigger at point black range, the gun hums and smokes after its released its deadly shell. Nosferatux laying in a pool of his own blood. The scientist turns back around to continue his final preparations.
"I cant let you do that...."
"What?! wont you die already?! Wait, your not a doctor, who sent you here! Get back! Stay Away! "{the scientist grabs his X-3 and lets off round after round at the being standing before him.
"Dear Sir, you cannot kill, what is already dead." {Nosferatux walks up to the barrel of the X-3 and rips it out of the scientists hands and throws it to the side. He opens up his right hand and in a swirl of colorful nanobots he summons a Nova Flow. "I have let your crimes against Rubi Ka go on much to long. {pushes nova flow up against the scientists head} we are well aware of what you have been doing in the Forman mines as well as other sights on this planet. we can no longer allow such things. We also know that Dust Brigade technology came from you...."
"We? Who's we? Who actually sent you?
" you wanted to create a God, how does it feel to look at one?" {Nosferatux places the gun under the scientists chin, and pulls the trigger, blood splattering on him self as well as the near by electronics.}
"Target Eliminated" [System Scan: Powercells 100%, Bio containment 100%, Energy output 100% Nanorestoration 75%]

{Nosferatux looks out the near by window over looking the tubes with "living" bodies still forming deep inside}

"I wonder if im to late........"
