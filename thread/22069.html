No retreat, no surrender
I'm an agent, I do a lot of agent stuff like sneaking and sniping.

Sometimes, however, this is not enough, and when things go nasty, the nasty gets the heck out of there. And this is when things go outright ugly.

So, I accidently aggro two orange mobs. This is not good, and I know it. Smelling the imminent retreat I run som speed reducing nanos in their NCUs and leave them in my dust.

I take a few hits, of course, and they even get to run some DOT nanos in my NCU (which is strange since I myself can't run nanos while running), but all in all, I should be able to make it to exit.

But now comes the interesting part; when I've put a good distance between all persuers and me, somehow they still manage to get those fatal shots on me, through several brick walls none the less.

There must be some viable way to disengage from combat. At least add a line-of-sight check on mobs before they fire, if they should be allowed to fire at all while persuing. Heck, I'd even sacrifice my own ability to fire through closed doors to get it.

Ok, I'm cranky from getting killed a few times too many, but I still think this is a bloody unfair advantage to mobs.

Mobs teleport to their target (you). I heard they do this when they get stuck by an object in front of them. Part of the improvements to mobs, I guess.

I didn't appreciate this "improvement" though when I was out hunting earlier this week and had to run away because I got hurt bad with my teammates not paying attention. I outdistanced the mob pretty quickly but then it suddently teleported next to me and got in a couple hits. I again outdistanced the mob and it again teleported to me and finally killed me.

Keep an eye out when you're running away from mobs. Instead of shooting through walls, it might just have teleported right behind you.
