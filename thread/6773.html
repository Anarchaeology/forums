A plee to put newbies in other backyards
This isn't a major issue.. just something I, and perhaps others have noticed.

I have noticed this simply because I re-oppened my account over the past weekend.. having closed it in late summer when DAoC came out. (Boy was that game boring after about a month).

Anyway.. I came back.. and since my old character had nothing on him and was 'gimped' (gave everything he had to his guild) I decided to re-roll him in Rubika1, his 3rd Incarnation (Xaielao ~ Fixer.)

When in the newbie zone I realized there were a LOT of newbies in there. I thought 'MAN LOT of people comming to the game if theres 15+ people here in this newbie zone!" Reminded me of release.. day of release actually. All the beta people, myself included, were there the day of release and the backyards were PACKED.

Anyway I soon realized that I was in Tir backyard 1 and so was every other newbie. This seemed kind of cool at first.. until I started talking to people who were new to the game.. gotten it for christmas and such.

My complaint is thus.. since everyone new to the game within the past month (and there are quite a few) they all see Tir as there home and rarely if EVER venture to other cities.

I talked to new people and most of them hadn't even even HEARD of West Athens.. or Borealis or anything like that. Now I don't know about you guys but back in the day (beta and first few months into release) 1/2 the population hung out in West Athens.. and the Beach in Athen Shire was THE PRIME hunting grounds for people levels 5-15!

To investigate.. I roamed all over to see if people where still hanging out at the old hot-spots I so fondly remember. The only people I found in the following areas were lvl40+ (I.E. they've been in game for long enough to remember the stuff I'm talkin bout here)..

West Athens - Empty save a few high levelers
The Beach - Empty
Freedom - So empty I cought the guards playing Hide & Seek.
Bliss - Again empty accept 40+ poeple
Junk Yard - Empty save the bots themselves
War Accademy - Empty
Holes Bronto Stand - Empty


Now you may argue that it's because there just aren't any people in the game anymore.. but that's BS.. there are roughly as many now as there were 4 months ago. Go to the wompa's in Old Athens.. as crowded as ever lol.

It's because all the newbies start in Tir.. and for some reason have no desire to explore. So FUNCOM I implore you to spread them out a little. I'm saddened that my old stomping grounds are baren of life now.

How many of you remember the crowds in West Athens forming groups bye the Omni Sign? Or training V-bulls into the guards in Freedom? Or chatting it up with Sun Tzu at the War Accademy?

Xaielao's 3rd re-incarnation is SICK of hunting in Greater Tir County! I try to convince friends to come exploring with me but they are to busy leveling and becoming l33t. Well not all of them but since they all started in Tir Backyard One they've not experienced the adrenaline of pulling 3 Buzz's and a Jbot at the JunkYard.. or having there jaw drop the first time a Red Ion storm passes over West Athens.. or seen the SPECTACULAR few from atop a building in Bliss. They figure why explore and see new things when they have there Bot Mountains and there Temple

I here and now ENCURAGE anyone who's joined this game in the last 2 months to go EXPLORING for once! That's one of the biggest thrills in this game.. it's so damn HUGE there are some amazing sights to see! Heck even in Greater Tir County (or Tir County Expanse what ever it's called now).

I also emplore Funcom to put newbies into West Athens and other zones.. some overflow rules or something. For ever 3rd newbie that enters Tir Backyard One put 2 others in West Athens Backyard one.. or Old Athens backyard one.

Well lets just say I'm glad your at least exploring at a slow pace. It's those that never leave Tir, Tir County, Greater Tir County (GTC) until they are to high lvl to hunt there anymore (mid 50's actually, very diverse zone) and spend there entire time hunting cramped spots.. going from the Flee pit to the Crater to Bot Mountains to the Scorp Pit to the Temple to the Shades... not even bothering to spend a little tme exploringing other zones.. zones that can be (and often are) around the same level.

You say your level seven? Take some friends with you to the little town known as West Athens (if you don't know.. wompa from Tir to Newland, Newland to Old Athen, then take the west gate to West Athen) and follow the road to Athen Shire. Between about your level and 15th there's a spot called The Beach which is a much cooler hunting ground. It too used to be PACKED (but that was when there were so many players every zone was packed) and it has a very different population. No Scorps.. but Epidemic Rollerrats. No Mutants & OT NPC's but young & adult Malle's and Baby Minibulls (just watch out for there cousin Virulent Minibulls.. you'll only need to learn that lesson once .

After about 15th find the small outpost along the road called Freedom.. theres Young Minibulls & Kea Reets and across from that theres the Junk Yard in the Desert with bots like bot mountain.. every once in a while (rare spawn) the Trash King will come out of his hiding spot and scare the living **** out of ya. Sure you have to run fast and will probably die at his hands.. but so what.. theres a save, stores, bank at Freedom so you can save often enough that it's no big deal.

Exploring doesn't necessarily mean going out into the deep dark wilderness filled with 1-hit=death monsters.. it can be as simple as wompaing to the town away and seeing what it has to show you and finding little waterfalls or monster pit just outside of town.

BTW did you know that there is a spring about 10 paces from the wall of Tir City? It's a cute little spring but it's in an odd section.. but suprisingly close to some mission terminals... people follow the beaten path so much.. that most people I mention to about the spring have never seen it... even thoughs of 30+ lvl.


Every person that reads this post from this day forth.. let me know if YOU know where the spring is.. if not.. I'd say it's a sign you need to do a bit of adventuring.. even in your own back yard.

Things I have done to avoid leveling
Ok, I'll preface this by saying that I'm on RK-2 and I'm level 75. I have played a few side characters but I've logged more time than several 120 level characters (good friends and old hunting buddies), and more than a buddy who is now 138. I play a fixer so, as most of you can expect, I'm causticly bitter about my class, yet I manage to make a lighthearted joke any chance I get.

I too am disappointed when people don't know where things are... Hell, I don't know the names of some of the places I've been, but I can tell you how to get there (survival wise) for some of the trickier spots... Some days I go to the newbie backyards and damage buff and HoT as many of the slow ones that I can so they are invicible against any of the low level mobs there... Somedays I head out to explore. The big thing is - avoid getting bored, it works...

I don't own a yalmaha, in my opinion they make you lazy and lull you into a bit of saftey if you fly high enough (and yes, I could afford one)... Though I did raise my waterskill so I could use my boat. I prefer the runbuff method for dry land, a lot more risk, and you really get a feeling of being there... I ran from broken shores (Home) to Avalon (Camelot) for fun - died a few times on the first attempt, but I made it eventually...

I have traveled around DAV and Milkyway on foot just because I needed to get somewhere and I figure if I can't learn how to get there by foot then its not worth getting to. That affords me some spectacular secenery and several funny anecdotes about the miriad of creatures who have killed me.

I've traveled to Home, to Hope to Seraphim (if I remember the name of the Mantis filled city in PW correctly) just for the hell of it. I've been eaten by a wide variety of one hit mobs.... If you want to know how to get there from here... chances are, I can tell you, or in a day, I can find out.

When I'm not out galavanting in armor which is far below my level, I take my time and kick back to relax in the great city of West Athens. I've tightrope walked the wires between buildings, lept from rooftop to rooftop, jumped over walls, found graphical glitches which made me have to grid out of them...

On top of that, I actively seek out low level fixers and help them out... as well as other classes, though they do not benifit from my generosity as much. And, if you ever need an item - almost any QL feel free to give me a /tell, I'll see if my boys have it in stock, and if not we'll try to to find it for you - sorry no 6-slot belts... (if we found em, trust me, we wouldn't be passing them out until we all had them first). Perhaps I could arrange a slayerdroid rental for you so you might have a shot at getting one, but that will cost you. Bottom line, when you've been here as long as I have - you know people. And when you don't know people who can do something, you know people who know people who can.

Will I run missions? Will I team missions? Will I explore? or Will I hunt? Keep your options open... focus on enjoying yourself...
