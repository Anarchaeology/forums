Guild wars are a very, very, very good idea. Of course the gauntlet would have to be picked up once thrown down or the idea won't work at all. Both parties would necessarily have to agree to the hostility for it ever to be accepted by the customer base. UO did this very, very, well. We always had a couple other guilds that served as our 'archrivals'.

Neutral RP Mercenary guild could decide to declare 'war' on an Omni or Clan guild and play the story out properly instead of having to try to lure them into 0% zones.

Of course, they'd have to tweak the /org invite and organization joining code a bit to prevent having that level 200 Soldier join the org in the middle of a fight and slaughter the other side.

Then you open up the 'blue healer' Pandora's Box where a Doctor that is in neither of the 'warring' guilds is actively healing a combatant in a 75% zone. UO addressed this with the 'beneficial acts' patch where beneficial acts were either not allowed on combatants engaged in PvP under certain circumstances or would actually flag you as an agressor.

I'd really like to see more consensual PvP in this game. We regularly hold our guild meetings in 0% zones so we can have some fun. Arenas, unrestricted ganking, and title hugging PvP hold absolutely no interest for me.

Despite the potential 'quirks' that would arise, I agree that simply having the ability for two consenting guilds to declare hostilities between them and bypass the 75% gas rules would have benefits that FAR outweigh the potential 'workarounds'.

I repeat, this only works if the two organizations agree on hostilities mutually. This puts the decision as to whether or not to open yourself up to things such as the 'Blue Healer' syndrome squarely on the shoulders of the Orgs themselves.

For guilds with a mature playerbase, this opens up a whole new worlds of PvP and RP possibilities. You can have PvP in areas with known mature individuals on the other side without having to worry about the 'kiddies' who inhabit the Arenas, MMD, Mort, 2HO, and EFP.

It opens up the possibility for two Neutral Mercenary groups to play out a deep-rooted hatred for each other as they fight over contracts with the Clans and Omni-Tek. It would put to rest all the arguing about 'Make Rubi'Ka all 25% gas!' since if you wanted to, you could have basically the whole planet 25% for you to be attacked and/or attack people who feel likewise.

I would say that Clan and Omni should be able to declare Hostility with each other and that anybody could declare Hostility with Neutral and vice versa. Omni declaring Hostility on Omni must be prevented at all costs to maintain some sense of realism. If two Omni guilds want to 'spar' then there's Mayhem zones, the Arena, and Beer 'n Booze that suit that purpose within a sensible context.
