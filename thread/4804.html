I hear fixers are good gridders... Is that true? If it is, you could grid to Newland, the area east of the city had some extreme amount of mobs last time I flew over it.... You might want to check out the valley forest there too.... If those mobs are too low level, head east from there, into Newland Desert, from the border you won't be far from the Oasis, a great team hunt place.

If you can't grid yet, I suggest you wompa to 4Holes, then walk the road north to Last Ditch in Stret West Bank. Roads are pretty safe, and even though those areas are 25%, I don't think you'll find many able to attack you.

Newland is surely the best place of the game, a small, pleasant, neutral town with shops in, three wompas, and short way to Varmint Woods
