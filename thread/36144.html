Amoeba II - Destination ICC
This Tuesday at 10pm Eastern, Unity of the Rose will host its second Amoeba party in conjunction with Grid Stream Productions. The timing of this event coincides with DJ Shadowrunrr's normal Tuesday night program. During his program, the entire party will be leaving Reets Retreat and winding its way through Borealis and Newland to its final destination in ICC. We will be pausing at each location along the way to dance, party, and assimilate passerbys into the Amoeba.

An Amoeba party is a travelling party with various destinations along the way. Frequently, party-goers are seen handing out drinks, yelling "Amoeba! Amoeba!" and making a general rukus. The intent is to try to engage the general public and grow the party as it travels.

Please come and join the Amoeba!

Any questions or comments can be sent to Szentasha ingame or to szentasha@darksphere.com, or may be posted on our forums at http://www.bleedingtears.net/uor/vie...294&forum=27&0. Questions for GSP should be sent to Wyldthing. This is an RK1 event.
