Deviation from the intended AO
With all the complaining that has been going on in these forums and on live, I have noticed a change in the game philosophy. Today, all the professions want to be the best damage dealer and win every battle in PvP. So what happened was a tweaking of all professions such that each one was "equal" in combat as the others.

From my understanding, this was not originally intended, since every profession has to have its ups and it lows. Yes, there are some proffessions that have to be good in PvP and great damage dealers. In this same note, there should be some professions that are not good in the battlefield but are good at something else, like making implants or weapons.

I rally for more support in increasing the xp given by tradeskills (by at least 10x) and other non-combat interactions. This way, it would be possible for a trade-oriented profession to be able to successfully level by building stuff and selling it to players. That might diversify the game a bit, making it much more interesting and sticking with the central idea.

I understand the idea that tradeskilling does not involve so much of a risk as a mission. One solution to this is to make the tradeskill items NODROP, and the finished product tradable. This requires people to mission in order to get all the items, to then finally put them together. The risk involved is that most tradeskill professions require a high intelligence, and thus most people decide upon the nano or solitus breeds. Due to their lower HP, these professions die much easily if the tank or pet can't retain the aggro. Since tradeskilling professions are not as good missioning, they run a greater risk while they mission to get the required items, and this greater risk is paid off by the xp gained by tradeskills later. To prove my point, simply check the percentage of tradeskill profession players after level 150 or 190. According to auno.org, engineers make up a total of 7.85% of the RK population, but they make up only 2.75% after level 190. The combat-oriented professions, on the other hand, increase their percentages the higher level you go (10 to 17 for sols, 14 to 17 for MAs, 9 to 11 for enfs).

That said, please bump if you like this idea, to make it heard, and make the game as it was originally intended to be.
