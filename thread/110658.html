Where have I been...?
After weeks of searching I have looked far and wide for something. Something to make me whole. Something to bring happiness into my life. I left the RMC and cut all political ties, wandering in the wilderness to find myself.

Upon my return the RMC has dwindled, and people have lost sight of the things I sought to bring to the public. I have spoken with many members from different organizations.

EG has a nice group, with an internal democracy confined to the Clans. Very good people, but I had tried to bring democracy to everyone. For a time I felt like something was being accomplished. The RMC's membership had grown to encompass 7 or 8 guilds, but none of them were very active in the organization.

BORPAC has formed in my absence, and a new kind of war has begun. People of the planet are sad. Sad because the mining is destroying the planet. Our home...

However I feel BORPAC is going about this in the wrong way. Violence is not the way to solve everything. In the RMC, it was proposed from Szentasha of UoR[EG] that they had been coming up with a new type of upgrade for towers. It allowed the towers to operate more efficiently and produce much less pollution.

Constructive means to an end, rather than Destructive is my proposition. BORPAC if I understand correctly is against off-world exportation of Notum. Please correct me if I am wrong. This is not something that can be stopped by destruction of mines. Talks with the ICC might help instead of just destroying things. Like I said, Constructive means to an end.

Maybe its time for me to start a new organization. Maybe the time for organizations is gone. I dont know...

But I do know I am tired of seeing people destroying things thinking it will make things better.

First... a certain amount of notum mining is inevitable. However many towers you destroy, people will rebuild them. Even if mining notum were made illegal, people would do it illegally. And the reason is simple.

Notum, as the basis of advanced nanotechnology, is the single most important raw material in the entire galaxy. It's more likely that troops from the other galatic powers would invade - with the ICC's blessing - rather than see their notum supply cut off.

Additionally, destroying towers has a severe and negative environmental impact. The explosions release clouds of toxic gases, spill hazardous liquids, and betimes release radioactive materials (depending on tower type) - in addition to spreading polluting, contaminated metal through the tower area.

Bearing that in mind, the most productive and positive focus for environmental protection would be to reduce the level of violence and bring an end to the destruction of towers.

Simultaneously engineers can endeavor to refine mining technology to reduce and hopefully eliminate environmental pollution from notum drilling.

As there is absolutely zero credible academic evidence of notum depletion, I find such claims easily dismissed as the rantings of demogogues.

My remaining concern would be the loss of habitat being suffered by many creatures as a result of tower encroachment, and the effects of highly fluctuating suppression gas levels over time.
