Gathering of Souls press release -RK1
A long time ago Miravlix started a fair, where people could get free tradeskills and buffs, this was done to make the clans stronger. Miravlix later handed over the promotion duties to Calautica, but Cal's military duties meant he couldn't keep promoting it, for a while others tried to continue the event, but after a while it just died out.

Cal has tried in several ways to get the GoS going again, due to his other military commitments he hasn't been able to put his original plan into motiion, that of creating a GoS Foundation (Not a guild) with people from several organizations to take over.

Therefor Cal has handed over the GoS to Lord Llwain Lynx, a civilian and doctor, that has more time to spend on the project.

Lord Llwain has started to announce the GoS and will continue to do so. The event in it's current form is held every Sunday at 19:00 GMT in Old Athens Backyard 2.

Lord Llwain will also continue the effort to create the GoS Foundation, so a broad group of people from the clans can run the event for hopefully many years to come.
