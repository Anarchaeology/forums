Personally, I doubt that this "Immortal One" is either Dragovich or Vanya. Dragovich, although virtually immortal just doesn't fit the descriptions that I have read. He was a calm and collected scientist/businessman who believed in the power of science and reason, not this creepy stuff. Plus, I don't believe that Dragovich would be living with the Cult of the Three Winds, they are after all clan oriented and the description of Dragovich's self-sustained underground complex in the final chapter of Prophet gives me the impression of something more akin to a bunker under Omni-1 rather than a cult run temple in clan territory. Vanya would seem a more likely choice especially given my theories on the two warring "dimensional" races(the dark ones that Vanya represents and the possibly less evil ones that gave the prophets their "truth dreams") in which the dark race of Vanya was kicked out of some kind of extra-dimensional paradise by the other and is trying to use humanity and Rubi-Ka to get back, but I think that it would be to early to introduce Vanya as a character and since you will theoretically be able to kill this "Immortal One"(it is a dungeon after all) then it probably wouldn't be Vanya as I would place him in the "unkillable" category myself. Perhaps this Immortal One is another member of Vanya's race gone insane or trying to rally support from the Three Winds Cult or the clans. Or maybe this is some Shadowlands escapee/exile or maybe a play of some religious figure like Satan. I guess we will see soon enough.
