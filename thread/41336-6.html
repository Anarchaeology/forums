Re: Cz, I think you are missing the point on nanocost
quote:
Originally posted by Corianin
The fact that damage (and this includes damage from nanos) is halved while we still pay full cost compared to healing which works at full effect (at full cost) is what is seen as unfair.

I support a 50% effect of healing as well (and maybe 50% cost to offset the already existing unbalance between cost and effect on damage nanos)
Think about it once more. There are 3 profs that use heals in pvp Doc, MA and Adv (Traders dont count anymore).

If heals where only to have 50 % effect in PVP it would destroy the advent prof. Why? Cause we dont have any offensive nanos.

At higher lvls all profs have the IP to raise weapons skill to a good lvl. You see casting profs using rifles and assualt rifles very well. It does not matter if you fight a doc or a advent using assault rifles, they will do about the same dmg. Even soldiers complain about this, we can all use their guns fairly well.

If heals where 50 % effective :

A Doc would do ok, has a massive init debuff and CH would still be a very powerfull heal.

A MA would struggle more than a Doc, but today a MA is not dependend on his heals to win. He got by far the best critt buffs in game.

An Advent, would be destroyed. Our CH has someting like 16 sec cast time, useless in PVP. We can not debuff, root or uber self buff. We have to heal while taking dmg. We do about the same dmg as any other prof.

So you see, changing heals in PVP would effect advents much more than the Doc or MA. And I have a feeling that ppl want to change heal so they can win a fight vs a Doc or Ma. A heal change wont help you.
