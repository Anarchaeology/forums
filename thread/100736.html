Desert Winds recruiting
The 'neutral' roleplaying organisation Desert Winds are currently looking for active, high quality recruits to join us in our fight for a brighter future.

Applicants must
1 - Be neutral
2 - Roleplay to some extent
3 - Be active

Desert Winds are a small neutral guild formed about six months ago on RK1 (Atlantean). We roleplay as freedom fighting eco warriors. We campaign for a free Rubi-Ka where all can live free and Rubi-Ka is an independent colony, we also work towards an eco stable planet because until then Rubi-Ka can not be independent planet and will simply be dependent on outside influences, such as the ICC and Omni-Tek. We feel the majority of clans have 'lost the plot' with regards the fight for Rubi-Ka and so we go a third way.

Most of our members are European based, but we welcome recruits from all over the world.
