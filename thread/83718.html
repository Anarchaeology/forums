Player run vendors

A whole seperate PF, like a a giant futuristic mall, and players can buy their own machines that can only be placed in there, then the owner has to pay a daily fee to keep the shop standing. The owner drops items into the shop, then it gives you a prompt for how much you want it to cost. You can choose from a list of default names for your shop from the shops "Management" menu, like Armor, Rares, Guns, Melee, Nano Crystals, Misc, to prevent vulgar names.

All the credits are kept in the machine until the owner comes to retrive it. Daily charge will come out of the credits stored, if there are no credits stored it takes it from your char. A Maximum number of shops per character.

The name of the owner will be on the shop so that players can get in contact with the owner if they wish to negotiate something different regarding an item they want from the shop.

Just dragging over an idea which originated from UO. I think it's a good idea and a way to save yourself from sitting in the shopping chanels spamming all your leet ware.

Item : Troop Carrier

Allows the user to transport their teammates in relative saftey, allowing the group to reach their destination together.

Downside of this is the driver is vulnerable, and if the driver is killed, the vehicle returns to reclaim with the driver, potenitaly leaving the remainder of the team stranded.

Advanced models have gun ports, allowing ranged weapons users to defend the vehicle as it passes thru hostile areas.
