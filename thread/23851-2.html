Some of you guys have it all wrong in my opinion...

First of all, let's review what a "Black Market" is...

black market
n.
The illegal business of buying or selling goods or currency in violation of restrictions such as price controls or rationing.
A place where these illegal operations are carried on.

black market

n : people who engage in illicit trade v : deal in illegally, such as arms or liquor [syn: run]

Now let's define "Agent"...

Agent

a·gent
n.
One that acts or has the power or authority to act.
One empowered to act for or represent another: an author's agent; an insurance agent.
A means by which something is done or caused; instrument.
A force or substance that causes a change: a chemical agent; an infectious agent.
A representative or official of a government or administrative department of a government: an FBI agent.
A spy.
Linguistics. The noun or noun phrase that specifies the person through whom or the means by which an action is effected.
.
.
.

Now that we have these basic definitions cleared up, let's go on to the define the "Fixer" and "Agent" classes (Funcom's game definition)...

Fixer
Operates on the edge of the law, acquiring anything on demand if the price is right, and works as an intermediary in the underworld of Rubi- Ka.

Agent
Keeps a low profile to gather information or liquidate unwanted elements through technological means and concealment.

...Anyone see where I'm going with this?

The Black Market should NOT be the realm of the Agent.
Agents aren't meant to deal in illegal goods, and have black market connections...that's the Fixer's job.

Let an Agent sneak into that office by disguising himself as a soldier and get necessary intel or assasinate that polical radical.

These two areas can blend into a small gray area, (both are kinda shady) but the "black market" simply is not the home of the Agent.

Agents need their own "shady Agent stuff" to do...but that's a different topic which probably belongs on the Agent forum
