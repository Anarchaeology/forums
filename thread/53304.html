Oh that post right before mine reminded me of this.

Anybody who has been around since before the newer 50% PVP damage and 15-second grace period rules would know of the.. the.. 2ho GRID!

What a scary thing it was. Whether you lived or died depended on your PC speed, for your enemies could hit you well before you yourself zoned in. For a brave 40 NT with less than 1k HP, it was a big risk. Basically you'd hit the grid node and zone to the reclaim with a "Veev hit you for 1300 points of AimedShot damage" notice in deep red. Veev is my god, by the way. I almost killed him once while he was recovering from a fight in the Tir arena, but he escaped!

Also, having your PC lag a little and you enter a 25% or 0% area by mistake could mean your death. A carefully watching player would be hiding on a roof... watching.. spamming a root nano until you hit the line by mistake and BAM you're in the fight. An aimed shot or large nuke later and death comes swiftly. Oh the joys of 12.x!

PvP is less chaotic and more balanced now, but those old days had their fun.
