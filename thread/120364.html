Making Outdoor hunting more attractive
For now outdoor hunting is practically ignored by majority of players at high-middle level. The main reason - not enough things to hunt.
Dynacamp are quite few, maximum 4 of given levl per zone and no more then 8 of given level at all. What make situation worse part of dynacams are in land control zone and not accessable during 100% time. Those dynacamps and others too close to land control make possible exploits - luring mobs to towers.
Another problem - too big range gap between Boss and his entourage. So even the killing the boss would be attractive, killing his yellow-green entourage make all the work to boring. From the other hand players interested in mobs often have no hope killing boss.
And not related to dynacamps - there is not enough simple, not affiliated high-level (175-200) mobs. Dynacamp boss are too hard, their adds to low, and single mobs of that range are very hard to find.

Suggestions :
1. Move dynacamps from land control areas.
2. Increase density of dynacamps.
3. Decrease gaps in level between boss and his adds.
4. Put dynacamps in every zone.
5. There are huge empty stretch of lands without anything (at NE deserts for example) - put new dynacamps there.
