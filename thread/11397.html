Change Sides, New Pad
One detail left undone is what happens to your living arrangements if you change factions. I started the game as a neutral, with an apartment in NLC and eventually signed up for Omni. Upon realizing that I was apartment-less in Omni-1, I petitioned a GM to help me get new living quarters. He told me that it was impossible. Now, if I want to visit or use my apartment, I have to travel clear back to NLC to see my old apartment (which I kept the key for, thankfully). I would like to use my apartment at some point (and the prospect of getting in a snazzy Omni high rise is pretty nice, compared to the dump of NLC :P), but I don't want to live outside of Omni territory. C'mon Funcom, let those of us who find new employers get new housing!

I imagine it would be much worse for someone switching from clan to Omni (or vice versa, heaven forbid).
