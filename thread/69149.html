The Dust Brigade
Omni-Tek is choking the life from our world. They have squandered our land and our rights, their tyranny must end. The CoT used negotiation and diplomacy in an attempt to deal with OT, and it only failed. We used action, took it home to them, and made it personal. We are the Dust Brigade.

We are looking for clanners with resolve to continue bringing the fight to them. If you don't have the guts to do what needs to be done then go find a clan where they'll wipe your behind for you, we only need those with the will to persevere.

To become a member there are few qualifications:
  • You must be over level 50
  • You must be willing to take any steps necessary to thwart OT
  • You must be willing to kill any OT at any opportunity


Anyone under 50 with interest and resolve will be accepted on a purely probationary status without holding rank or priviledge until after they pass level 50.

In addition to being part of the cleansing of Rubi-ka, members will have access to training, equipment and support from the rest of the clan. The Dust Brigade takes care of its own. We look forward to your joining the fight.

Anyone interested should post here or /tell kivlera ingame.
