No, EFP is Eastern Fouls Plain. Go to 20K and go east till you zone. That is EFP. Manteze, Bileswarms, Brainstealers, Medusa, Sand Mantis, etc. The area with the Medusae is walled with some hills which keeps the other creatures out, although we used to run into mantis that someone trained there occasionally. You do need a team however; that is definitely not solo territory, and you need to have a Yalm to get around. We hunted medusa from 90-110 or so and then moved to other stuff in EFP, and always the Mobs were deep deep red to us. It is very dangerous, but can be quite rewarding. You might also try out in the deserts of broken shores. Lizards of a couple of types, nanofreaks of several types, Eremites, and rageriders. Again you would have to have a group, and a yalm is almost a necessity.

Hope this is of some help. I feel your pain.....I am 158th and we are still looking for a good outdoor hunting area at that level.
