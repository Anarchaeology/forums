Not Enough Penalty for Betrayal
Maybe I'm the only one who's bugged by this, but people in this game seem to change sides awfully casually, and with remarkably few consequences. Here are a few consequences I'd like to see.

Shame

Whenver someone right-clicks on one of those Application Forms, it should generate a message on both the Omni-Tek Herald and Voice of Freedom channels.

On the previous side, it should say something like, "Attention: Firstname 'Nickname' Lastname has betrayed his/her/its friends and defected to Omni-Tek/the rebel clans."

On the other side, it should say, "Attention: Please welcome your new fellow employee/clan member, former rebel/Omni-Tek employee Firstname 'Nickname' Lastname."

That way, anyone who cares will know who to carry a grudge against, or at least who to make fun of.

Punishment

The text for the application forms says that if you use them, it will generate a mission to attack you for your previous side. This doesn't seem to be happening. Bug, or side effect of the cease fire?

Consequences

The way it's supposed to work is that clan characters get organizational benefits, while Omni characters get better armor.

Once they implement the organization benefits, they should add two boolean fields to the character record: BetrayedOmni, and BetrayedClans. Right-clicking one one of those application forms should permanently activate the appropriate flag. (If you switch, then switch back, you've betrayed both sides.)

When rebuilding the character after zoning, the software should check to see if the BetrayedOmni flag is set. If so, then Omni-specific armor should stop working, period. If you're feeling kind, give 'em a text message: "Omni-Net has determined that you are no longer a valid employee, and has deactivated the company equipment you are wearing."

When rebuilding the character after zoning, or at the very least at next login, the software should check the BetrayedClans flag, and if it is set, then you should be ineligible for organization benefits. It should also check that flag when you accept an org invite.

Because as far as I'm concerned, casually having it both ways is an exploit. FunCom would be within their rights to suspend you for being a clanner wearing an Omni-Med suit or Omni-Pol armor. Having the software enforce these rules is arguably lenient.

Obviously, exploiters are going to hate this. Anybody else have an opinion?

Reputation

If you have both flags set, then you should acquire a lifetime rep as someone not to be trusted. And a little bit of gentle nagging would be the perfect way to portray that.

Scenario 1: You're team leader. You target someone, bring up the Team window (ctrl 5), click Invite. If they have both betrayal flags set, instead of immediately sending them a team invitation, it pops up a dialog box saying, "This person has already betrayed your side once. Are you sure you want to invite them into your team?" If you click Yes, it proceeds normally.

Scenario 2: Someone who has boh betrayal flags set invites you into his or her team. The usual text message says instead, "Do you want to join Nickname's team, considering they've already betrayed your side at least once?"

Scenario 3: Apply the same idea to /org invite.

Scenario 4: If either party to a trade has both betrayal flags set, then the other person's trade window should also say, "Before you do business with this person, remember that they've already betrayed your side at least once." (If you don't want to squeeze it in there, at least display it as a message.)

Now the great thing about all of these things is that, unlike the previous section, they impose no actual game penalties. They just nag people gently to stay in character.
