Makes no sense..
I've been reading these posts since the FC press release on the Booster Pack, and honestly I'm more than a little bemused (or is it confused?)... to put it mildly. I am not a big poster (no future Superleet here ) but I finally felt I had to voice my opinion. Seems to me that some people playing this game must be on a very tight finacial string if they manage to get so worked up about paying 10-15 $ (or whatever the price will be) for additional content and features added to this game. To me this does not make sense, it's probably no more than what you throw at a few packs of cigarettes or some 6-packs of beer (depending on your particular vice). So why the intensity, why the flaming of others because of their views and why putting so much effort into getting people not to buy it? If they by doing this also get people to not start playing AO or maybe even to stop playing AO, what then? Unless that is the real objective? And dont go arguing about this being promised and that we are being screwed. I dont buy that argument at all, and I dont feel the least bit screwed (there is btw plenty of stuff on these boards disputing such views anyway).

I dont mind paying this kinda price for the booster and then something similar (or probably a bit more) for the Shadowland expansion as long as I enjoy the game and make lots of friends there (what the heck I've been paying similar amounts or more for add-ons to other games). And I really do like it and I do play it a lot. Hey, of course it would be nice if we could have everything for free, but be realistic. It took plenty of time and money to develop this game, it takes money to keep it running, it takes money to add features and content and just plain maintenance, and it takes money to have guys the like of Cz and Cosmik doing their stuff etc. etc. And finally Funcom needs to make a bit of profit, or there will not be any AO in the end.

Saying this does not make me a fanboy or defender of FC, but I am a fan of the game AO cause I really do enjoy playing it. Sure there is lag and occasional instabilities but they really dont bother me that much. Sure I would like to see some additional means of gaining XP beyond killing mobs. Sure I would like to do other stuff than running to BS 3-4 times for team missions every time I log in. But I only do that because i want those levels, cause there really are plenty of other places to go and things to do, even for a lvl139 agent (which is my highest char atm). And the coming patches, booster and expansion will even improve on that.

The day I dont enjoy AO anymore I will most likely go somewhere else, but with plenty of good memories from my time on Rubi-Ka. And I'll go quietly.
