The enforcer shop..
Well, it might not be an enforcer specific shop. But it do have some enforcer only weapons.. ok here we go.
This might have been posted before (but i didn't find any sticky one about the subject, as i find it importand.) but i hope it will help some newbie enforcers out there looking for that great 2H edge weapon.
To get there you need to whompa from Entertainment to 20k.. from there whompa to Outpost 10-3. After you've got to Outpost 10-3 head directly right and jump over the platform edge. Then take an straight right. You're now heading for the cordinates 3000x3000 (to se wich cordinates you are at press F9). As you getting further you can se to your right there is a big swamp land with water and trees here and there. Don't even think on heading in there as it is some nasty swamp land ppl in there. I have no idea what lvl they are, but as you read this you're prolly below lvl 30. So follow the edge of the swamp lands. untill u get near the cordinates 3000x3000.. You will find an shop merchant in an open field with an really big carbonish rock placed in the center. And surounded by doussins of small Carbonish rock's. The place in very near the swamp lands an are rather hard to miss. Go you to him and right click him.. then starts an small conversation. But you don't have to do through it. Just click the "Shop" button in the Chat window wich came up when you right-clicked him. Now it's up to you to dicade wich weapon you want. Because he got HUNDRETS of them! He got 2H edge, 1H edge, 1H blunt, 2H blunt, with fast attack, without fast attack, with sneak attack, or without both, or with both. He sells melee weapons from about QL 3 to QL 129 something.. Now grab some nasty sword and go kick some!
I hope this helps /Dreed

The Clan approach
It isn't really that hard to get there for clanners. I got there safely at lvl 40ish w/o yalm, even though there was quite a bit of running invovled heh.

First step is to grid to grid to 2HO. After exiting grid, run east to the wall (towards the whompas), and run south along the wall (on the outside). After the wall, continue SE (100 metres or so) to the little tower, then it is safe to go straight east. Remember to keep some distance to the south wall of 2HO, because the female corporal that guards the whompas might attack you if you stay too close to this wall.

After you have successfully passed 2HO, it's just a matter of continuing NE until you zone into SAV, and then continue further NE to the coords 2600, 2900. Alternatively, when using Dovve's map just go to the Carbon Rocks all the way north (and east) on the map. Be sure to avoid getting too close to the Omni outpost 10-3.

Remember to save before you go, and stay clear of any mean mobs that might give you a slap. It might be a good idea to ask a fixer for a runbuff before you go, because it really is a long run.
