The "May Fly Throwing Grenade" (http://64.106.154.149/showitem.asp?AOID=165117) itself never had a UNIQUE tag on them...

The "Secured May Fly Throwing Grenade" (http://64.106.154.149/showitem.asp?AOID=165119) is where all of this nade's problems exist.

May Fly Throwing Grenades are player-made grenades. Last time I checked, there were three steps to using one of them that had to be done each time you wanted to use one because the first step resulted in a unique item.

Step 1: Make the nade - combine Nano Multibenzonoxydant Sulphosalycilic Liquid (which you can get from a chemistry component shop I believe) with a Throwing Grenade Basic Kit and you get a "Secured MayFly Throwing Grenade". This step requires Weapon Smithing (900 for a QL 200 nade so I take it the formula is QL *4.25).

Step 2: Use the Secured MayFly grenade to in effect pull the pin. The result of this step is the un-unique May Fly Throwing grenade. This step requires Electrical Engineering (900 for a QL 200, so again the formula appears to be QL * 4.25). Secured MayFly nades are UNIQUE, thus you can only have one of them on you at any given point in time.

Step 3: Throw the nade. This step must be done within 20 seconds of completing step 2 or you will have wasted it. Throwing the nade requires Grendae skill (again, 900 for a QL 200).

I checked the DB and looked at the Secured MayFly grenade (the one you get after step 1) and it is still unique and still requires QL *4.25 in Electrical Engineering to pull the pin.

First problem: UNIQUE. WHY THE <insert choice four letter word here> IS A GRENADE UNIQUE? WHY WHY WHY WHY WHY? THERE IS NO LOGICAL REASON WHY ANY PART OF THIS PROCESS SHOULD INCLUDE THE UNIQUE TAG WHEN THERE IS A 20-SECOND LOCK ON YOUR GRENADE SKILL WHEN THROWN AND A 10-SECOND SKILL LOCK ON YOUR ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING SKILL WHEN MADE.

Second problem: ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING. HOW CAN YOU JUSTIFY PUTTING AN ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING REQUIREMENT ON PULLING THE PIN ON A GRENADE WHEN PULLING THE PIN ON A GRENADE IS A MECHANICAL ACTION AND DOESN'T REQUIRE THE MIND OF ROCKET SCIENTIST TO UNDERSTAND?

If they haven't eliminated step two from the three step process, the nade is still seriously borked and needs a real fix.
