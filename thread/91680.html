A bold new vision for R.U.R. ...
A restless night. Dixie had tossed and turned for the better part of two hours, before giving up on sleep. She padded into the kitchen and put the kettle on the stove. While she waited for it to boil, she wandered aimlessly about her apartment, picking things up, setting them down again, her thoughts all jumbled.

This Milly/Hubble thing, it just got worse and worse. The press was starting to latch onto RUR employees now. She'd spotted a reporter lurking in the alley outside her highrise, watching the entrance to her apartment building. He didn't see her, so she'd been able to backtrack, and sneak in through a service door.

What to do, how to contain this... could this mess even be contained anymore? Argh. She plopped down on a chair, and ran her hands through her hair. She sat and thought for several moments, to that legendary company of Old Earth, the ones who had continually fought their way through bad press and huge scandals, to create a commerical empire. A company that had been revolutionary in setting up its own legal government and jurisdiction in a way that its host countries couldn't touch. Even in these times, Omni-Tek and other countries still emulated aspects of that early model. A company with the imagination, initiative, almost eerily magical ability to take setbacks, and turn them into cash opportunities. A company who took a small fictional rodent, and made him a god to three-fourths of the living world.

A god named Mickey.

She sighed. She didn't have that sort of skill. She wished she did. What would Mike Eisner do? , she wondered silently, wistfully.

Suddenly... it was as if she could hear little bells. She shivered, goosebumps forming on her skin. She looked around, astonished, the idea bursting upon her like a wish granted from a fairy godmother.

What would they have done? In a word... Merchandising.

She rubbed her hands up and down her arms, shivering again, with excitement. Make Milly a beloved children's icon... Milly plush dolls.... Milly story-books... Milly T-shirts....Milly action figures. Milly-bots specially programmed to entertain children... a whole series of animated Milly films... "Steamboat Milly"..... She reached for her notepad and started typing up a memo to her fellow employees...

The kettle whistled while she worked, but she didn't hear it, lost in a lovely reverie... one that might just take RUR to amazing new heights.....

Dixie finished a memo, to all RUR employees, and was just about to hit the send button, when she paused. Hmm. Perhaps better run it by one or two people first. Just in case this wasn't the idea of the millennium, as she believed.

She managed to get hold of Dabblez on her comm-link. Breathlessly, she explained her vision to Dabblez, who remained silent for some time...

Finally, Dabblez spoke. "Steamboat Milly??"

Hmm.... Dixie thought she sounded a bit skeptical, and hastened to explain. "No, not "Steamboat Milly"... "Steam -bot Milly".

Dabblez made some sound on the other end of the comm-link, and Dixie quickly continued, "Just imagine, a short animated feature, with Milly wearing a jaunty cap, and whistling a happy tune, while she flies a spaceship".

Another long silence. Then... "So... an iconic company mascotbot? That might be good...."

"Mascotbot! Now we're talking!", Dixie bubbled with enthusiasm.

Dixie nearly dropped her now-cold cup of tea that she had been absently sipping. A theme park! If Dabblez had been right there, she would have hugged her! Endless creative opportunities for RUR bots... fashioned to look like feathered reets, furry leets, scary gargantulas, fictional characters... Ooo, and divide it into different themed sections... Yesterdayland, Not-so-Civilized Land, Fantasy Area, Main Strip OTRK, and let's see... ooo, Shadow Lands (not to be confused with the already trademarked "Shadowlands")....

Each "land" could have themed attractions, restaurants, shops, and entertainment. There could even be a "Hall of Administration", featuring prominent historical Omni-Tek CEO's, and end with a robotic Phillip Ross making a speech about the Company... of course, everyone would sleep through it, but it would always be packed due to the air-conditioning, comfortable plush chairs, and excellent make-out opportunities for hormonally-driven teenagers...

Dixie continued typing her ideas far into the night, not seeing a little figure peering in her window, searching for a wayward shadow....
