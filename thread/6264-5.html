Hey, here to add a little more sh!t to the fan: A couple days ago I completed the Dodga quest. My level 67 (at the time) fixer killed scoop- and shovelets in Danger Dunes just north of Wartron Valley, and gathered all eight pieces in about an hour. Not NEARLY as long as I had heard or thought it would take. After that, I headed up to the Rising Sun to check out the Dodga situation. When I got there someone was already camping the brain (hey Stokeley!), so I plunked my ass down to wait too.

About an hour after that, Dodga showed up, the first guy turned in his brain, and left for a couple hours. I stayed there to hold my spot in line for the brain, watched some TV, caught up on some forum stuff, chat a bit. Two hours later there were two card people there (one of whom was the first guy to turn in the brain), so I let them turn in their cards before I gave the brain to Dodga. He gave me the card and disappeared.

I left, did some shopping, sat around and chatted in the OC channel, then came back to turn in my card. Again, there was one brainer there and two carders, me and another person. Note that I didn't talk to Dodga to get a mission icon. When I turned in my card, he gave me the pads, the second Dodga card, and two tokens, one worth three (like a regular mission token based on my title level) and the second worth just one. I don't recall a money or XP reward, but it was so late at that point that I could have easily missed it.

Note also that this was Saturday (I think), and definately after the 13.92 patch.

The instructions I followed were posted on my guild's forum, which was a copy of the initial post in this thread. Thanks muchly for compiling the information, Melchoir!
