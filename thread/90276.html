Give me another beer. Life still sucks.
Strange, really, how something in your past can force you to take drastic measures, like hoping on the next ship to some god forsaken mining colony.

...now how the hell did I do that anyways? And why?

Sure it's one of Omni Teks prized possesions, doesn't mean I'd like to live here.

Course here I am, on this barely terraformed planet, fighting for my survival with the rogue inhabitants. Damn, almost wish I joined the Omni. They have some decent equipment, and seeing how the tides of war isn't going in their favour, I could stand to make some good cash.

I give a short cold laugh, probably making some people edgy.

Not that it matters. Nothing matters anymore, not even death, thanks to the notum here. I have no past, and it seems I also have no future. Especially if I stick around with the clans.

Sure, they claim to have ideals, but it seems to me that the majority of them are just looking out for their own skins. Not that it's a bad ideal, just that self centered people tend to result in some nasty situations for yours truly.

I stop walking for a moment, lifting my eyes from the dusty streets of Tir, to look up at the stars, just wondering why I even existed.

Naturally, the stars have no answers, being mere balls of gas.

Resigning myself to whatever fate may throw at me, I walked on, the horizons tinged with the grey clouds of an oncoming storm.

I aim to please. Basically seeing if I can just make a story based on what's happening while I play this thing.

And for those wondering, the name's Haiiro, on the Rubi Ka server, and I'm seeing if I can't carve my name in Rubi Ka's history.

__________


The mission seemed simple enough when I picked it. Fight through enemy territory to upload some data on their computer or something.

Course, it had to be my flipping luck to find some beaucrat with high tech weaponary and some guards with thus said computer.

As I desperately dodge laser fire, and attempt to keep my gun trained on whoever I see pop into view, I can't shake the feeling that the team mate I'm with might not make it.

I heard a pained scream come from behind me. I turn about to see my comrade clutch his stomach in pain... and... hell... he's bleeding. His pets gather around him, seemingly paniced, if they feel emotion that is.

I shout to him to get out of here. Don't think he can hear me. I quickly down the crat's bodyguard, and shout at him again. I see him stagger for the elevator, only to get cut down by gunfire.

...damn.

Naturally this is when I get knocked down by the crat's assisant, still thirsty for blood. I manage to kick him back and scramble for the elavator myself. Hey, I may be immortal on this world but I'm not suicidal.

Access denied? What the hell?

The assistant reminded me that he was still around with a punch to my face. I should him my appreciation by unloading my clip into his face, body, limbs, and surrounding scenery.

"Heh, gotcha."

Heart pumping, I noticed that the crat wasn't in view. The optimist in me was hoping he ran away.

A bullet to the head reminded me why I preferred pessimism. And once again I'm glad I found this Nomad helmet. I jumped over a desk, glad for the cover as I desperately jammed a new magazine into my assault rifle.

Bullets hammered into and through whatever material the desk was made of. I waited until he had to reload his clip. After all, life's not fair. Why should I be?

Time seemed to slow down as I rised up, desperately trying to track him, as I could see the shock settle into him. Emotion draining from my mind, I pulled the trigger, figuring a magazine full of 80 bullets should do the trick.

Nope.

Grimly, I loaded another clip, and another, and another, not caring what wounds I received.

And then, I realised that was wasting bullets on a corpse. Heh, that's odd... I didn't realise it.

"Yes! He's dead!" I turned to finally notice my ressurected teamate and his pets. When did they come in? Not that it matters.

I replied, "Well that wasn't so bad." as I staggered to the computer, and uploaded the bloody mission.

Mission complete
