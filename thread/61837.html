Wrangle doesn't affect Comp lit. Only affects Nano skills and most weapon skills.

There are Trader Comp Lit buffs, but they are Self-only.

Basically, the only real comp lit buff available is the general one (+20). The rest has to be raised by IP or by some special items (and again, not by much).

Intelligence buffs also help, but there are no int buffs except for general (+12). However there are plenty of int items, including int buffing rifles, etc.

Hope that helps a little. I would post links to items, but I don't the time at this moment.
