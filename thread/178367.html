She ran...
She's been running now longer than most of us know how... Her Mergance with the grid while as reliable as ever not antirely safe.

She Relied on her haven... the grid for so long. Her ability to evac to it's safety was comforting.... Her ability to rely on transmitting her physical presence to the Grid Mark 4 Beacon on Ruby Ka was her strength and her mergance with the grid and the increment in speed that gave her was often her saviour.... the grid is no longer safe.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
To her allies she is little but a memory. Her final insurance record, falsified by the Omni TekArmed Forces 6 months ago shows a failed transportation to the guild, which would have explained the corpse they never found, the Heavily Modified Manex Catastrophe "Half Life" (a Manex Catastrophe December heavily modified to adminster a radioactive nano virus on impact which paralysed her target) enscribed with her 6 winged Pheonix lying abandonned by the grid terminal in Tir.... To her allies and friends she is thought lost but this couldn't be further from the truth.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

A blue haze rushes quickly through the plains of Lush Fields, it steadily slows to a sluggish walk. The blue encompassed human checks for signs of life around it from a small monitor on it's wrist. After deciding it is safe the blue haze fades slowly, and a short naked woman appears in it's place. As soon the transformation takes place she clasps her right shoulder with her left hand and falls to the floor as if being struck by a large bat or club in the back. Her long brown hair falls over her face, wet and disheveled, her eyes open and close several times with the effort to focus. The words "Ashes to Ashes, Dust to Dust." are clearly visable in the small of her back in neat script. Her Alliance with the Original Dust Brigade was well known.

She looks up and scans the area again, comfortable that she is alone, she accesses her personally constructed grid space beaming weapons and various other equipment into her hands. She slides her naked flesh carefully into a skin tight set of armor made from the flesh of Tarasque, wincing sharply as the flesh tries to become part of her body. It's been six months since she started running, the constant movement, no time for rest, the uncertainty playing on her mind has left her little but a shell of what she used to be. She stands in one swift movement, strapping her FDA Caterwaul to her right thigh, and her Mausser in her hands.

The thoughts playing in the back of her mind tell her time is almost up, that eventually she wont be able to run anymore. Her insurance reads that she was permanantly killed in a grid accident, and her comm link has been destroyed. Knowing full well she is being hunted by a team of Omni Tek's Elite she can do little but run, and so she has.

Omni Tek's specially Trained Notum and Diamondine Soldiers are persuing her every movement, and while she is strong she cannot match up with their genetically enhanced Ability... so she hides, but no longer would she run....

She recently passed through Jobe, and met with a young Scientist Named Drake. He offered a solution... and a deal was made.

Her heart beat grew faster, the electronic impulse from her Refined Tech Implants made her arms twitch rythimically in an attempt to calm her down, but this would be the be all or end all of this fixer... She checked for the locations of the Omni Tek AF Forces Leader by the Notum and Diamondine Soldiers hunting her, and made a direct course in their direction. They came together not far from the Primus Camp in Eastern Foul Plains.... Home of the legendary Mercenaries... She fired shots at members of the Omni Armed Forces, killing two of them instantly.

As the Diamondine Soldier drew her rifle, time seemed to slow down for her. She watched the bullet leave the gun, and head on a collission course for her forehead, she could have evades the shot quite comfortably.... but that was not her intention. Her eyes closed a nano second before impact and it all became silent.

When she opened her eyes, she stood before Drake in Nasance.... She was back from the dead, and soon the world would know.
