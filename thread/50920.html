Buffing Machines should be added
I've spent countless (wasted) hours running around looking for a particular buff so that i could install that particular something. In many cases the previous buffs you received timed out before you got that final buff. Likewise, there are those who see fit to charge you and arm and a leg for a buff:

Traders Wrangles $50k - 500k
Doctors Iron Circle $60,000

This is ludicrous and the AO economy does not support such a wealth to afford these types of expenses. With the price of armor, weapons, misc. items, who can afford 300k for a 3 min buff.

Why can't there be buffing machines that provide these benefits at a reasonable RK price comparable to what someone can honestly make in this game.

I want to play, not sell for hours and camp spawn spots for just 1 buff. Likewise, I'm an Omni Citizen fighting for the cause. Shouldn't I receive some form of salary or stifen for my time?
