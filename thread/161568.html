I strongly recommend against seeking someone "new". Many times, "new" people aren't. And people who truly are new won't have a clue what you are asking them to do.

My suggestion is seeking out someone who is much higher level than your main. Most of what you would be transferring would likely be worthless to them anyway.

You might also ask people from well-known guilds. Most well-know/respected guilds won't stand for a member ripping off someone else in a transfer.

Finally, there are some bugs in the trade window which prevents some traded items from appearing until the user "zones". Credits can also appear to "disappear" if you're not careful. Nothing is worse than someone thinking the other is a thief.

I also confirm what is in containers before I accept the trade. I've seen people trade the wrong container, insist some foul play was involved, only to discover the container they thought they traded still in their bank or something.

On a side note, apartments are supposed to be MUCH improved in Shadowlands. What you initially suggested may well be a possibility when Shadowlands is released.

Edit - 2004 August AI Launch

Concerning the process which you can transfer items and money to your alternate character WITHOUT any help from anybody, a big improvement was found.

Traditionally, there is a particular way of opening two clients simultaneously as follows:

The easiest way is to simply do this, double-click the AO icon and then click yes to the eula, NOW, quickly double-click the AO icon again BEFORE the first client finishes reading the 49 files. If you do this in time, then the clients will share system resources evenly and both will respond to the connection port, and so the server requests.

You should have two clients loaded now. Go to first client, type the info and proceed to the character selection screen. STOP! Go to second client, type the info and proceed to the character selection too. Now, click log in and IMMEDIATELY go back to first client and click log in too. Either of the clients will ask something like "Do you want to open two AO instances?". Of course, you click yes.

Sidenote: You cannot open the second client if the first one is fully loaded. This is because AO checks whether the client is in the computer memory so you have to be quick before the check is done.

But after Alien Invasion, it seems that the client has been changed in a certain ways. To simplify that, you do NOT need to take care the sequence of opening two clients. You simply double-click the client whenever you want to use a second account.

i.e.
1 Open 1st account and log in and start playing
2 Open 2nd account and log in and play (Yes, this step is possible now)
3 Close 1st account and Open 3rd account (Again, this is no problem)

However, the game clients do not know how to distribute system resouces (memory usage). As a result, only the first client will use an appropriate amount of resources. You should feel laggy, graphically laggy in second or third clients.
