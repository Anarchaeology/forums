Process in building tier armour
1. Get Visions of <xxxxxxxx>
Ask your IPS what Visions and what type of Novictum you need and head to a (un)redeemed temple, walk upstairs and choose the spirit room. Walk N and zone in the "mud" there. Farm Vision items from the mobs there. You need 3 visions per armourpiece.

2. Get Embryo's
Inside this dungeon the sidekicks of the bossroom drop Embryo's you need. If you are in a higher lvl Dungeon (Scheol+) normal mobs can drop them too. You need 1 Embryo per armour piece.

3. Get Canisters of Liquid Notum
These drop by the general wildlife of Shadowlands. Can only have one at a time so you will need these as last prolly, make 1 piece and get another etc.

4. Get Canister of your type of Novictum
Your IPS tells you what type your profession needs. You farm these from spirit mobs, but not the ones in the spirit room, go outside for em. You need one per armourpiece.

5. Make your Glyph
Head back to the IPS person and tell him/her you got enough for your glyph and hand over 3 visions and the embryo and you'll get a glyph.

6. Activate/Assemble your glyph
Click your Canister of Liquid Notum on your Glyph to activate it. Tier 1 needs 550 QFT for that. Now click your canister of novictum-type on it to make it the final version. Last step needs 600 psychology for tier1.

7. Make your Tier 1/2/3 armourpiece
Click the result of step 6 on your Jobe armourpiece. Grats


Tier 1 only has: Glove/Sleevex2/Pants/Boots/Body
Tier 2 has: Glove/Sleevex2/Pants/Boots/Body/Padx2/Helmet
Tier 3 has: Glove/Sleevex2/Pants/Boots/Body/Padx2/Helmet/Back
*advs are lucky and get a tier2 back too

To make Tier 2 pads/helmet you can use Jobe one. Same with Tier3 back item.

Have fun making these, it is worthwhile. You can upgrade your armour to a certain degree:

Tier1: from 100 to 160
Tier2: from 160 to 220
Tier3: from 220 to 300

Currently a lvl 200 could eqp a 220 set with some effort. QL160 is about the same as normal QL200 armour give or take a few. QL160 body adds 1000 AC usually.
