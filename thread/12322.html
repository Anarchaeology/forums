Update on the MA issues..
First of all, the "dropping fists" fix will not be in the next version we put out on Test Live, but it will be in the version called 13.84.
We are now patching to 13.83, and I expect the next version to be out before the weekend.

When it comes to the questions about Physical Initiative, the martial arts skill is depending on this, and have been since release. However, we are doing some more testing, to see if it has as much effect as wanted.
When it comes to the MA weapons, they will also be changed to depend on Physical Initiative. The first part will be done in 13.8.
I will publish more information about this as soon as my team have finshed testing them.
