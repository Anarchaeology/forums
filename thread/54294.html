This is always problem with official systems.

What you should do instead is to create a Ladder system. Where people can challenge each other for PvP fight. The winner then scores points. Everyone starts for example with 1600 rating and this uses similar ratings then chess. When you lose a match you lose points, and when you win you gain points.

Of course, if you fight someone with much higher rating then you, you will not lose so much points, but can gain quite a lot.

Here you can read more about how the ladder system works:

All you need is:

1. Someone with a little understanding of coding
2. Build a website with ladder features
3. Everyone who want to be a part of the ladder system has to read the rules and register for it.
4. Start competing and see who is the best at the end.

The basic idea behind the ladder is to play whoever you want and whenever you have time. Of course there must be some restrictions and that´s why we have a challenge-system.

Challenges

1.) A player has the right to challenge any other player from the ladder who is in the same title level. You can´t escape from a challenge (at least not without losing points).

2.) After a challenge has been made (a challenge is officially made when it appears in the forum or on this page under "Open Challenges") both players have 1 week to play the game.

3.) The loser has to post the result in the forum within three days after the game is played. If he does not post the result in time he will be punished with the loss of 50 points.

4.) Challenges that aren´t played in time will be judged by a the challenge board. Warning: If both players don´t show serious interest to play the game they will both lose 20 points (independent of the ladder-ratings) as a penalty.

5.) No player can be forced to accept new challenges when he already has two pending matches. In this case the challenger has to wait till his opponent is down to one or zero challenges again. Of course there is no limit if you want to play more games.

6.) Players can neither challenge and play each other two times within duration of 1 month.

Handicap

1. Players can only challenge those that are within same title level from each other.

2. If you want to challenge someone that is much higher level then you. This difference is made out in Handicap, and the points are calculated accordingly.

Everybody starts with a ladder-rating of 1000. How many points you win or lose for a game depends on the ladder-ratingss of the players. The bigger the difference between you and your opponent the more points you will win respectively lose. The formula is:

Basic Game Value = 20 points
+ / -
Difference between the player-scores divided by 10

Game-Value

Exceptions:

1.) If the "difference between the player-ratings divided by 10" is bigger than the "basic game value" the winner still wins at least 2 points (see example 3 "if you win..."). Otherwise there would be no motivation to play these games at all.

2.) You can not lose more than 40 points in one game, even if the "basic game value" plus the "difference between the ratings divided by 10" is more than 40 (see example 3 "if you lose"). Otherwise the risk to play would be too big.

Example 1: You (1000) vs. Opponent (1000)
+ 20 points if you win (20 +/- (0:10) = 20)

- 20 points if you lose (20 +/- (0:10) = 20)

Example 2: You (1050) vs. Opponent (950)
+ 10 points if you win (20 - (100:10) = 10)

- 30 points if you lose (20 + (100:10) = 30)

Example 3: You (1200) vs. Opponent (900)
Minimum points you can win: + 2 points

(even if the "difference between ratings divided by 10 (here: 300:10 = 30)" minus "basic game value (20)" would be less than 2!

(Exception 1)

CONCLUSION

This is the only way to find out who is the best in PvP. Titles can always be farmed. You just need to use a similar system to one I explained above to see who is really the best.
