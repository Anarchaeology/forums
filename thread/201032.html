The Rhinoman Liberation Front (TRLF)
Over the past weeks I have seen a terrible sight, something that will haunt me to the end of my days.

After asking a "friendly" clan trader to get into a more fearsom weapon of destruction so I could fight for the liberation and freedom of the world, I follow him into the Newland Desert

Here I saw an act that terrified me, completely unprovoked and without cause the trader proceeded to attack the poor Rhinoman, draining him of strenght and power and weakening him almost to the point of breaking.

This poor Rhinoman tried to fight, he showed his true courage as a part of a proud people, but he was outmatches like a gnat against the hide of an elephant.

Once the trader had used him for his purposes he killed him, swiftly and mercilessly.

I was abhorred, not merely had I just seen a vile slaughter of a peacefull rhinoman, but I had also been part of the cause.

It turns out that this draining was what required for him to help me, so I have blood on my hands, blood that will never be washed off.

Now, as an act of redemption I have started The Rhinoman Liberation Front.

This is a front to protect the Rhinomen and fight for their rights.

I will hold speeches of ethics, and I myself will go to Newland and do my part in protecting the Rhinomen.

I say this to you all.

UNITE AND PROTECT THE RHINOMEN!!
