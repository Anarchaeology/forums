More mission veriety
For the love of god. I can only take so much brown walls and green slime.

Reduce the chance of getting the "brown hole" missions. Not only are these missions overly large but they also seem to have the best chance of comming up. Like 80% of the time. Making game play repeditive and boring.

As a offset make the "mines" and the "snake" missions come up more. They seem to come less frequesnt. Though you will need to increase the minimum size of these missions for they seem to come up quite small.(3 rooms is the smallest ive seen for both types)

Im suggesting that the missions to be equalized in size and in fequency. It will make for more varried game play and less tedium.
