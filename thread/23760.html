Supa Leet
I'll answer it. 332 layers of protection per damage type is what it is. Each layer is one point, as said in the description. If I get hit for 200 damage I'll have 132 layers left, no damage yet taken on my part. If I get hit a second time for the SAME damage type I'll take about 68 points of damage, while the ramaining 132 layers are wasted away. If a DoT is cast against me, the layers will absorb the damage, it is espciely usful because most DoTs cast against me are poison/chemical/disease or whatever. Most attacks that mobs throw normally only hit the melee and projectile layers. Once all 332 layers get spent you will need to recast the nano to gain the layers back, even though the other layers were still functioning. Quite simple really
