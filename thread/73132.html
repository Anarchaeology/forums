My personal opinion/Warboc has a good point
I must say, I have played an advent for quite some time and I have fiddled with my skills over and over to come up with what I like. I'm not out there to get the best equipment, to advance in levels extremely fast, or to flex my muscles in any PvP setting.

I've made my mistakes, and I've learned from them, but I think what appeals to me with the Advent profession is that it IS forgiving. Advents ARE the Jack-of-all-Trades with healing, decent weapon skills, and a few appropriate non essentail skills here and there.

Warboc has a point tho. All of the things he mentioned are relevant to this profession, and any other profession for that matter.

Q #1 Do I have ql 200 implants in?

A. Yes I do, but I change them around for things that I think I may need, or need at the time. If I find something I want to be able to use, I make a change in my implants.

Q #2 Do you have your nano init maxxed?

A. No, but I have realized the importance of this skill and I am working on bringing it up to a respectable level. For an advent like myself who prides himself on being able to heal very well..this skill is a must have.

Q #3 Do you have your Evades Maxxed?

A. Again, no I do not, although again I have come to realize the importance of these skills and work towards increasing them.

Q #4 Is your weapon Init Maxxed?

A. See answer to question #3

Q #5 Have you bothered to stay up to date on your heal nanos?

A. Absolutely yes. I began this profession with the healing ability in mind as I see it as one of THE most important abilities in the game. Everyone needs to heal wounds, and no team should go without a proficient doctor (and I dont mean just the actual profession)

Q #6 Do you have a scope that cripples your init?

A. At the time of this post..I do not wear one. I have in the past and see some of its advantages. However, unless one has their inits raised high enuf to offset the disadvantages of the scope, this can hinder more then help.

Q #7 Is your first aid raised and do you use stims?


A. My first Aid skill could use a little boost. I do use stims however, and as any skilled doctor should do....I carry a medical backpack with me at all times that holds stims, nano rechargers, treatment kits and other medical sundries of varrying quality level. This is to ensure that anyone travelling with me will always have something as back up should they run out.


A minor point should be noted. I myself have invested a huge amount of IP into my concealment skill. I have it maxxed right now, and will continue to add to it once my level cap is removed at level 150. This was not done so that I might have an egde in PvP combat, but in order to sneak any QL mission successfully and retrieve mission rewards. Even though this skill cost me heavily, I have found it to be an INVALUABLE tool for ANY profession and would urge anyone to invest the IP into this skill.

All of the points that Warboc mentioned play a HUGE part in the makeup of your character and how he or she performs in any situation. His post wasnt to complain at all, but to EXPLAIN certain things within the game that can improve or hinder your own performance.


As for the profession itself, its ability in combat, casting nanos, and other skills, I myself believe that advents do need some things changed and added to them.

#1 A line of advent only armor that does not require you to fight with any guild to kill a dragon on the SLIGHT off chance someone will be generous enuf to let you loot it off the dead body.(I could be wrong here...is there any other type od armor available for just advents?)

#2 A serious look at the advent line of Mezz nanos. Being able to mezz is great...but..this ability is hampered greatly by the fact that mobs CANNOT be in combat when this is done and the Mezz, assuming it works..lasts a FRACTION of the time any other profession mezz does.

#3 A retooling of the Leet Charm line of nanos. This ability, although fun at parties, really isnt all that useful. U cannot zone with your pet, and u do not have access to many of the commands that normal pet users have. One cannot even rename their pet. What is really needed, is for this line of nanos to be EXPANDED to include other animals on Rubi-Ka, such as Rollerrats, Panthers, Wolfs, Brontos, Reets, etc etc...the list goes on. There are numerous species of ANIMALS on Rubi-Ka that can be added to this list, and it seems only appropriate that this Jack-Of-All-Trades wilderness dweller have the ability to have them as pets, even for only a small period of time.

#4 The ability, once morphed into any particular Advent form, to assume the look of a normal mob on the playfirld map, thus hiding from Map Upgrade identifiers when in animal form. This would not eliminate a players ability to 'tab' onto you and identify you thusly, this would just partially mask your appearance in that area. When one assumes an animal form, they should get the ability to become part of the environment like any other animal does, hence, looking like a normal 'red dot' on the playfield map.

#5 Any animal form that the advent assume should be given a chance to be IGNORED by other ANIMALS that you may pass by. Thus. giving you a chance to be treated like any other animal on the playfield, in this case, ignored by other animals mobs as you are an animal yourself.

#6 Reduce the ridiculously long casting time on the Reet Line of nanos. Not only has this completely and utterly removed the Advents one and only UNIQUE PvP attack/defence, it has also made this line of nanos annoying to cast in the first place, as now it takes forever to complete. Not only that, but as advents, this Nano was PARAMOUNT in the defence of people and others such as NPC's (guards specifically). This nano, if not prevented, then seriously limited a players ability to attack an NPC such as the guards. I saw the use of this nano as a unique way to perserve the life of the guards doing their duty to protect our cities and our lives. Now mind you, repeated use of this nano on anyones NCU can and has been constituted as NCU harrassment. But I look at the use if this nano in this manner as Roleplaying, although when asked by someone to cease this action, I acquiesced and moved on. But, catch someone attacking a guard, who has naver had this done to them before, and they quickly realize the dilemma they are in as they cannot attack in this form. The next time they think about attacking a guard just for the hell of it, they will think twice. (and while I'm on it....why do people attack guards anyway? They give NO exp, NO loot, and their death is only temporary as they return moments later...) In my opinion, if you wish to see the use of this nano in a different manner, your best bet it to make it so players CAN attack in this form. Leave the casting time the way it was originally, otherwise...I'm going to grow old and gray before I can turn myself into a Reet again.

Perhaps #4 and #5 could be made into new advent nanos....?


In conclusion, and this applies to ANY profession in my opinion, you are what you make yourself to be. There are NUMEROUS players out there with a WEALTH of information on not just playing an Advent, but on any other profession as well. If you find yourself having problems in any situation, avail yourself of the resources at hand. Dont hesitate to ask questions and dont be afraid to admit that u made a mistake. MOST mistakes IP wise can be fixed with an IP reset of skills and a re-placement of said IP.

The advent profession can be one of the most enjoyable professions to play on Rubi-Ka, and besides..who doesnt like little cuddly Leets and fire breathing dragons?

Feel free to contact me anytime in game should you have any questions concerning the Advent profession. I may not be the MOST kowledgeable person around, but I feel that my experience has taught me alot and I can be of some help if you seek it. Should I not be able to help you, I can surely put you in touch with someone who can.
