ClickSaver and Win2k SP3
Just a FYI for anyone doing a clean install of their computer:

ClickSaver does not start up properly if run for the first time under service pack 3. Learned this by reinstalling windows and going right to sp3 before installing anything else.

On the previous install, ClickSaver worked fine under service pack three, but it had been configured, and created it's database under service pack 2.

Anyway, just figured folks would like a heads up, I sure wasted alot of time on the issue.

I had a rather nasty experience wth clicksaver run under win2k sp2. I tried all kinds of stuff to pinpoint the problem, except changing OS, which is way to timeconsuming. Just something others might try out b4 formatting and changing OS

Just a note, if you happen to have one of many brands of mouse that use em_exec it sometimes will cause CS to hang at the splash screen.

Check your running tasks processes for em_exec if it's there you may need to disable it to get clicksaver running.
