Supa Leet
Ranged Init
For firing, at full agg subtract 1.5 from the weapons fire rate and multiply remainder by 600. That is how much ranged init you need to fire at one.

For recharge the calculation is completely borked. At full agg subtract 1.5 from the recharge rate and multiply by 300. That is how much ranged init you need from SPENDING IP's!!!

Implants, scopes, ability dribble down does not affect recharge. So how can you find out how much you have spend in IP's? Easy, raise your ranged init by 1 pt and note how much that cost you in IP's. Divide that number by your IP cost/pt (for MP's that is 4.0 ouch!) dun remember how much it costs Crats (if you left click it gives you an IP cost but you have to divide that by 5.0)
