<< GridStream Announcement >>
Greetings and Saluations Rubi-ka!

I would like to take a moment of your time to announce the full return of GridStream Productions -and- the grand opening of our Gridpage (webpage)!

That's right. GSP is back in full swing, ready for your events any day of the week! If you would like to schedule an event with GridStream Productions, take a look on our Gridpage: www.gridstream.com - and you will find a new, instant event form. Take a moment to fill it out and away it goes to my Email to be looked over.

Remember, GridStream Productions offers the best in live entertainment for all kinds of events. From your Organizations Anniversary, wedding receptions, dance parties... heck, GSP will play live at ANY event we are asked to attend.

GridStream Production is a non-profit organization, we do not charge for our service, but we gladly accept any and all donations.

So hop on by the Gridpage, take a look around at the new layout and new forums, and plan your next event with GridStream!!!
