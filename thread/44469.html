Booster Pack Wishlist
I figure it might be good to let funcom know some things we would like to see in the booster packs. Like for instance, some suggestions on what the improved graphics might be focused on.

I for one would really really like to see the opifex finally get fixed, if it takes a booster pack to do it then so be it. Like when an opifex wears any coat his head is half way off his neck. Or tank armor and shoulder pads that stick out of the front of the left arm rather than actually being on the shoulder.

Another suggestion would be to give us mood options to our faces, have the art team go through and make a couple variations on the same face and then have a setting so you can appear happy, sad, angry, sick, mean, cold, seductive, etc with just changing a setting, it would default to the original face to the people who didn't go for the booster but it would certainly allow a little more roleplay.

Come on people, contribute your ideas!
