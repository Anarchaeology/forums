Best moments:

Picking a mission above my level, full of red nasties, walking in wearing a bathing suit and sun glasses, mooning them, stealing my mission's goal, and walking out. Realising I just might not be the only one that strives for peace. Or am I?

My first experience with the cell scanner. The world I come from is poor, and outside the influence of Omni Tek. Fear of death rules the lives of men there. After my first pass through a cell scanner, I became addicted. Those that know me well know I am no longer afraid to die. Know that I even, perversely, seem to enjoy it. At any excuse, I'll /terminate, or jump off a clif, or out of my yalm. I'll charge naked at a SandWorm, get into a fist fight with ten Cyborg Generals. Stop and smell the roses!
Zuleica, you are working too hard

You must sometimes just stop and smell the roses. Look at the trees. Pet the leets. this world is there to be enjoyed.

There is nothing more rewarding than flying your yalm in uncharted territory, just to see what's out there. Or running if you can run. I find that by just blindly following my compass to missions I forget what this world has to offer.

I recently decided to take a week off work, away from fulfilling missions and tasks set to me by unknown strangers through an impersonal machine, and just go out and look at the world. Stare at it's beauty. It has thus far been most rewarding. I highly suggest you do that.

try and find a beautiful spot and spend a day in it. Look at the behaviour of animals around it. look at the sunrise and sunset.

Enjoy the beauty! Take some time off.

great thread, here's my $.02

best:

coming out of the Omni training grounds into the backyard area for the first time--I was absolutely blown away by the graphics and music. The little green flame on the distant skyscraper, like it was venting some weird gas--little touches like that really made that scene complete. I sat on one of the couches and listened to the "backyard jazz" for about 15 minutes. Still really like that tune...

finally pulling off an aimed shot that killed a mob w/ my low-lvl agent--my very first screenshot

diving off the waterfall in Greater Omni Forest

getting all buffed up in Athens and then zoning into Camelot to do 3 missions in Avalon--trying to dodge the guards in Avalon was always a risk. This was back when I was trying to save enough money to get my Yalmaha, and Omni-Trade to Avalon missions at my level then paid 100K, so it was worth the risk, but I still died about 20% of the time. Nothing made me more tense than "Attacked By Veteran Male Knight Patroller"

Finally getting the Yalmaha--to me, this is pretty much the "line of demarcation" in a character's life, pre-Yalm and post-Yalm. Flying around in it was fun, but the best part was finally taking missions in all these eerie, ominous sounding places like Perpetual Wastelands, Deep Artery Valley, etc.

Flying to the top of the Clon**** cannons and sitting right at the end of the barrel, watching the sunrise.

Add to that--going to DAV for the first time and seeing the message "No Suppression Gas at all, this is mayhem"--that was a bit unnerving

seeing a Babyface for the first time (hands down my favorite mob, the "whazzup" as they start fighting still cracks me up)

in a team, taking down our first Ace mob
