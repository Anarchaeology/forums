The current theory is that 600 points in ranged init subtracts 1 second from your attack time and 2 seconds from your recharge time, but neither of these can go below 1 second.

So, with a ranged init of 600, a gun with a 2.5/2.5 attack/recharge time will fire in 1.5/1 seconds, or every 2.5 seconds.

This is if the aggro bar is all the way to the right. If you move it to the left, it slows down your attack. I don't recall the exact amount. Probably doesn't matter, you should leave it all the way to the right if you are a weapon using profession unless you are in a group and taking too much damage and don't care how slow you fire, you just want the aggro off you.

Also note that the aggro bar calculations are done after all other calculations are done. Meaning, if you had a ranged init of 1 million, your gun would still fire every 2 seconds, no faster. And if you moved the aggro slider bar to the left, you might fire every 3 seconds or something... all those extra points of ranged init would do you no good.

My new enforcer has been using a katana (1.4 att/rec) since lvl 1 and with the agg bar at 100% he already hit at max speed.
Now with 250 melee init he hits at max speed with the agg bar around 65%.
From the results I had with him I believe in the old school rule :
1000 init = -1 second to attack and -2 seconds to recharge.
50% agg = base weapon speed as shown in its stats.
100% ag = -1s attack/ -2s recharge
0% agg = +1s attack/+2s recharge

My guess is I would need 1400 melee init to hit at max speed with a katana and the agg bar at 0%.

The best way to be sure 50% agg = base speed of the weapon is to use a 1.0 att/rec weapon and see if it hits at the same speed at 100% agg. I haven't found one to test yet.
