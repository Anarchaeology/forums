Sea of Change forming on Rubi-Ka 2!
Sereral clansmen who are determined to change Rubi-Ka 2 are forming an organization to do just that. Sea of Change, as we have decided to call it, will be a Roleplaying guild with several goals.

1. Change Rubi-Ka 2 with our own ideas.
2. Host, and participate in many events.
3. To let everyones opinion be known, and let them express those opinions freely without judgement.
4. To be unique in any way possible.
5. To foster a hardcore roleplaying atmosphere (even in guildchat)

Requirements:
1. Be a clan member
2. Roleplay as much as possible (even in guildchat)
3. Try to be mysterious and secretive about Sea of Change and its intentions.
4. Be willing to be active in the guild
5. Be willing to help with, and participate in events
6. Be willing to make a guildportal name ( -ooc- preferably that of your character) and join our website, which is hosted on www.guildportal.com.

We are a very secretive organization, with many hidden agendas and ideas, we will express those ideas when we feel that the time is right. Everyone in our group has a very unique personality, which makes for a great environment. We also want all of our members to have a say in the decisions that affect the guild. We are going to try to bring a political climate to our Rubi-Ka 2, and be very active in the world.

We haven't formed officially yet, we are still looking for more people, but we hope to very soon.

Anyone who wants to join I will personally meet with and talk.

If you are interested in helping to form Sea of Change then please contact me on Rubi-Ka, I am "Sepentura" or by email: serrephim@hotmail.com. You can also contact "Siirine" on Rubi-Ka for information.

Our guild page is hosted by www.guildportal.com , but to view it you will need a GuildPortal sign-in name, which is free and easy to get. (-ooc- If you are thinking of joining us, we prefer that you use the name of AO character you are joining with, this cuts down on the confusion.)
