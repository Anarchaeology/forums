The Joint was Jumping II (Vixen Entertainment's New Revue)
Ehrynn drifted around the club. The usual crowd had mustered in along with a healthy sprinkling of new faces. She wasnt fooled; the buzz was about the unveiling of Aktrezs new floorshow.

As the anticipation mounted, Ehrynn continued to circulate behind the crowd and make notes. Nevver was present along with Gencop, Powerflux, Tarryk, Leetchop, and Ragingfire. Nothing unusal there, she thought. Wouldnt be Rompas without them around. Finally the floor lights dimmed and Aktrez led out her Vixens, Ashiia, Cesca, and Miss M in their new dance costumes. Ehrynn scribbled down the details, pink tops, black short-shorts, nude tights and strappy black heels. It was tasteful and yet provocative.

Aktrez stepped out in front, bantered with the crowd and then settled into her routine. She started with a blast from the past, the ancient past at that, with a song about a place called the YMCA. Then the show picked up stream with Aktrez stepping out and the Vixens followed her lead through a series of intricate dance routines without missing a beat. It was simply amazing.

Ehrynn cornered Aktrez after the show and learned that this was a warm up for the next Vixen Entertainment virtual release. She also let on that she was gearing up for the arrival of the new Omni-Tek manager, Mr. Eskew. Now this was news, since recently there had been very disturbing revelations about misrepresentation in regards to the ownership and subcontracting at the Rompa. It was clear Omni-Tek was taking a more direct interest in the running of Omnis most popular as well as lucrative night spot.

Aktrez was reluctant to speak any further on the topic, so Ehrynn wished her well in dealing with the new Omni-Tek representative and moved to a booth to have a sit and some wine. It wasnt long before Miss M arrived and reminded Ehrynn that she had promised to demonstrate some of the signature Vixen dance steps and combinations. As usual, Ehrynn watched, memorized, and tried to picture herself up on a table. She giggled uncontrollably, almost spilling her wine, when that image suddenly popped into her mind.

(Next up, an interview with Mr. Eskew, Omni-Teks new Rompa manager.)
