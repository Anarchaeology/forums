The Making of a Model Employee
"Chris? Hello?"

Christopher Russi had just gotten a call from his brother, whom he had not seen or heard from in weeks.

"Oh my god! Fletcher? Is that you?"

"Yes! Listen, this is urgent, I need you to come to Newland and meet me in Apartment 1, Complex 3, okay?"

"Yeah! Yes! Okay! I'm on my way! Its so good to hear from you! What happened? Why am I coming to Newland?"

"All will be explained when you get here, brother."

Russi left his kit at his flat thinking he would not need it to see his brother. That and the fact that the Sentinels that occupied his beloved Tir always gave him a hard time about his visiting neutrals, and they wouldn't recognise him without his armour. He walked through the whompa to Newland and hopped in his Yalmaha to fly to the flat his brother was in. When he got there, he rang the bell and his brother's voice told him the door was open. He opened it and stepped into the room. The lights were off.

"What suprise is this eh?"

Four large hands grabbed Russi and shut the door. The lights came on revealing two Atrox in full OmniPol Assault armour holding him, two Solitus in OmniPol Assault armour and one Solitus that was removing his helmet in full OmniPol Elite. The Elite was his brother, Fletcher.

"What the hell is going on here?"

"I've decided to rescue you, brother"

"Resuce me from what? My freedom? You traitorous bastard!"

"What freedoms do you have, dear brother? The freedom to have inferior equipment? The freedom to live in poverty? The freedom to live in a pest infested city ruled by terrorists? The freedom to oppose the company which brought you here? The freedom to fight a battle you cannot win?"

Christopher spits in Fletcher's face.

"Let me out of here now. I want none of your insanity or OmniTek."

"That, dear brother, is now not your choice to make."

Fletcher nodded to the Atrox guards and they gassed Christopher and he passed out.

"Deliver him to the capable OmniReform. He will soon realise that he should have never quit OmniTek in the first place."

Fletcher wipes his face off with a towel.
-------------------------------------------------------
After two weeks in an OmniReform Reformatory, Christopher wonders why he quit OmniTek in the first place. They gave him a job, they gave his family jobs, they gave his family housing. So what if they had executed his father? He was breaking the laws and suppling the clans with munitions! He had it coming! Russi returned to his flat to find it exactly how he left it four years earlier when he quit OmniTek. Or rather an exact replica made by his brother, but what is the difference? He was home again.
