CONFUSIONS

The tall Fixer awoke at a reclaim terminal near the airport to old Earth.

She blinked slowly, adjusting to new surroundings and the suddenly blinding light. The girl was thinner than ever, pale, and rather sickly, but it was resurrection shock…it would wear off. Everything had gone according to plan.

Yet something was not right.

Suddenly she felt scared, naked, alone.

Half of her memory was missing- the insanity, the torturing endless years. In its place was two years of Omni-Tek Med school, and friends, and family… And she felt normal again, she could feel emotions, compassion…it was as if half her old past had been erased, and replaced with another person’s…

She looked down. Her body looked the same…she still had the same gray, pink tinted skin. Still a freak…yet, though she realized there should be a memory of her mother telling her this…

My mind has been swapped.

The only distinct recent thing I remember is…killing a girl.

The shock came. She didn’t even know why, simply that she had been out to get the girl for scarcely any reason at all.

Then she remembered her father, and a general idea of why- though it didn’t make sense, it was suddenly irrational.

And several other things became less hazy, and came into view…

Half of her said she was seventeen. The other half said she was twenty-six.

Half said she was a Fixer. The other claimed she was a Doctor.

Which do I believe?

She could especially remember the Med school. I never WENT to Med school…

Nobody’s going to forgive me for this…

I wonder if the girl’s still alive.

I have to find her, it’s the only way I can get out of this mess.

I suppose I could…start over. Not entirely, get a new name for myself…DNA reconstruction is too expensive.

In slow motion, the tall Fixer walked to the nearby desk, and grabbed some forms. She filled them out:

Name: Cathleen Shelling
Age: 20 (She couldn’t decide between 17 and 26.)
Gender: Female
Profession: Fixer
Affiliation: Clan
Guild: None

Then the Fixer walked up to a machine and pressed her finger onto a plate. The machine beeped. She then peered into a small hole, where a retinal scan was taken- another beep. Then she quickly clipped a fingernail from her right hand and dropped it into an input slot. Lastly, she stepped into a connected booth.

The booth was all cold steel, and she felt watched. The girl swallowed nervously and self-consciously stripped her clothes while the cameras inside took a 3-D picture of her. The girl was mindful not to look too much like she knew what she was doing; new arrivals were usually confused at Rubi-Ka’s citizen tracking system.

She had registered herself as an immigrant to Rubi-Ka. Her new name would be Cathleen Shelling. The name Gridninja would be abandoned until she really needed it.

There, is Cat okay? You only have to change one letter. It’s a pretty name, easier to spell.

That was the one thing she could distinctly remember: her name. It was most certainly Katrienae Shelling- or it used to be. Now she was Cat- there was only Cat.
For the first time, she genuinely smiled. And for the first time, she was truly happy as she strolled down the Tir street, a ‘Long live freedom’ t-shirt (A gift for those who joined the Clans) slung over her arm.

MM whistled a casual tune as she strolled around Baboons. It was empty, save a few stragglers roaming around. She snatched a can of soda off the employee desk, popped it open. Taste tests revealed what it was; she hadn’t bothered to look.

The notum concentration in Trade was high as usual; the air was hot and sticky and smelled of water. MM waded her way through the crowds and hurried towards Apartment Complex #16. The air seemed ominous; she kept a hand on one of her pistols and walked faster.

Kate-

I went on a mission with Pusher. Be back around 4:15.

Thanks, -Cristin

Who’s playing games?

She just looked at MM, seemed pained and hurt. She looked desperate, and alone, as her eyes rattled with the silence of static...

The Fixer only whispered “I warned you…” as the three disappeared into the silence of the Grid.
