PVP static dungeon could have been a great idea if...
... there were actually other places we can visit without being forced in PVP.

Like many people who have spent some time on Rubi-Ka, I have waited new content for a long time. I have seen the very few different mission layout too much, hunting is not giving the thrill it used to, and social part was what helped me to wait.

Now we planned some raid with members of my guild, got everyone set and entered the place. It didn't take long to have half of the team killed in pvp. It were all fair kills, but still put a stop to our raid, because it's hard to explore far into the place when you have to constantly wait for others to resurect and get back.

I personnaly don't pvp, have nothing against those who do, and having my remains handed to me in 25% zones is not a problem. I won't blame Snarf (or others in the future) for ruining our raid because I don't see why he shouldn't play the game the way he likes.

I will blame FC for adding :

1) A very limited new content
2) A very limited new content which can be rendered unavailable due to pvp.

So I am back to the point where I hope the social aspect of game will make me forget I have been doing the same things for quite some time (and yes, I have been in the different zones, I have enjoyed the landscapes, I laughed seeing babyfaces, I tried to have conversation with NPC...)
