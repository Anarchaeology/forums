Disturbing Trend emergeging in 14.8
I have browsed through the new items added to the 14.8 testpatch and noticed some disturbing trends emerging. There are quite a few new items designed to give the users

resistance user nano strain 145 (Snares)
resistance user nano strain 146 (Roots)

Some of those items are stackable and are part of a set.

Innate resistances

The significance is that these innate resistances work much like the growing number of mobs that have complete or partial immunity to root/snares in PvE. (Think team mission bosses that are immune to roots.) Crowd control is the one thing the crat can really contribute to a team and the addition of new mobs like Secondus, Primus, partial immunity mobs at the Clan/Omni tower research camps (anywhere from 25%-95%) that are immune to that crowd control has greatly diminished that capacity to contribute in the high end.

The level% nerf to mez(but also added to root/snares) have hurt the mid level crat's ability to contribute there.

In PvP the bureaucrat's charm/fears only work on player pets. For pratical purposes, mez also falls into that catageory in terms of usefulness. That leaves root/snare/stuns that are effective against players. The only real crowd control nanos left are root and snares. The introduction of items that give users the ability to ignore these last two functioning lines leaves the crat with only their nukes, which are second to third rate.

In PvE, there are mobs that are completely immune to charm/fear/mez/stun/root/snare. That is everything in the bureaucrat arsenel except the nuke and droid. Pray tell, what is the bureaucrat supposed to do?

Now, in PvP there will be players that are partially immune to root/snare. The only crowd controlling nanos that "work" in PvP. Singly, the items only offer a minor 1%-7% innate resistance. But some of those items can be stacked and don't forget that this is only the beginning of the introduction of these kinds of items. Currently, the innate resistance is somewhat balanced by lower ACs in the cloaks only. In the future, the possibility that more useful and powerful items with those bonuses built in will create a nightmare for crowd controlling in PvP.

The new items that will give this innate resistance needs to be carefully balanced to avoid "best of both worlds" secnarios where the stats and immunities conferred are both optimal.

How this affects bureaucrats.

The bureaucrat profession is the only profession out there where all their main abilities can be completely ineffective makeing them very underpowered. But, when all their nanolines work they are relatively balanced. However, there are far too many artificial walls that create large portions of the bureaucrat's career where those abilities stop working. Psychic checks causes charms and fears to stop working completely. Level% in Mez/Root/Snare causes chain resists in PvE.

That is the heart of the problem. The bureaucrat's abilities work like a light switch. Either they work or they don't/very poorly with nothing else but to fall back to causeing damage.

All abilities in the game can be divided into one of three groups. Damage related, causeing it(conventional weapons, nukes,dots, crit, nano and weapon buffs) and reduceing the amount recieved(AC, reflect, drains) , healing(heal,hots) and crowd control(init debuffs, root/snare/stun, blind). Everything can be catageorized as one of the three main groups.

So far, the crowd control branch have many immunities that cause these abilities to stop working. Not because of high resistance or evades. They just stop working.

The bureaucrat's specality is in crowd controlling. Their nanolines and abilities reflect that. Something needs to be done to compensate this lack of function. The bureaucrat is currently either fairly useful in certain circumstances and fairly useless in others. The uesless circumstances do not make for an enjoyable experience.

How level% works
Theoretical example

If the bureaucrat has
PsyMod: 100
SenseImp: 100

The AtkSkills use 50% pm and 50% si.
Total attack rateing with the nano would be
(100pm * 50%) + (100si * 50%) = 50+50 = 100

Now for crat mez, DefSkills are 100% nano resist + some level%
So for our example lets just say the level% was 200%
Total defense:
100% nano resist + 200% level

To see if the nano lands the attack and defense scores are compared.

Example mobs:
Level 5
Nano Resist: 30

Total mob defense vs mez:
(30nano resist * 100%) + (Level 5 * 200%) = 30 + 10 = 40

Result: 100 AtkSkill vs 40 DefSkill means almost guranteed to land.

Level 10
Nano Resist: 45 (assumeing the mob gets +3/level)

Total mob defense vs mez:
(45nano resist * 100%) + (Level 10 * 200) = 45 + 20 = 65
Result: 100 Atk vs 65 Def - Still good

Level 20
Nano Resist: 75

Total mob defense vs mez:
(75nano resist * 100%) + (Level 20 * 200) = 75 + 40 = 115
Result: 100 Atk vs 115 Def - Not likely to land

Level 30
Nano Resist: 105

Total mob defense vs mez:
(105nano resist * 100%) + (Level 30 * 200) = 105 + 60 = 165
Result: 100 Atk vs 165 Def - Near impossible to land
