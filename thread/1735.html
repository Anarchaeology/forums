Org idea/suggestion: Editable Rank names.
We were talking today in our organisation about ranks... And had an idea we really liked, that should not be too hard to implement.

How about being able to edit the ranks in the organisation? The leader of the organisation should be able to call his members what he like, that fits with the organisation, like.. Instead of member, he could change it so that they were called Thugs.. Or soldiers.. or slaves or whatever... Instead of exec, maybe inquisitor, or something... Not being leader or whatever but.. dictator.

I know this is just cosmetic and would be in the lowest part of the to do list, but.. It Would be cool to be able to call yer members what you want within the organisation. And it would be more fun to read the personal info.

I still want to be inquisitor..
