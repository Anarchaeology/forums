Yalm speed is not bugged.

Of course a jet plane is faster than a car. But there are some jets that are faster than others. Once you get to the point where you are finally fleshing out your character and have mastered two types of weapons and basket weaving and still are looking for some place to put IP's, a top notch Yalm can cut travel time by about 30% over a bottom of the line one.
