The Ghost of Rubi-Ka: Research.
OCC: This is a little story i'm going to be doing on here I figure this is a good a place as any to put it Feel free to post and add in stuff and that will help me add more content as I go .


Shalex sat down at is lab table, adjusting a few things here and there and then marking the results down on his electric notepad.
He was a Homonano Nano-Tech, and doing research and experiments was one great joy to him. The thoughts of being the first to find something, do something new is what fuled him onwards even when they blew up in face (Which has happened more than enough times). This particular test he was running was on some of the novictum and peices of hides dipped into it.
"Ah, very interesting." He mumbled to himself as he poked the semi- puddle of subdued novictum with what looked like a bronto prod. He time he poked the matter with it, it seemed to fade from vision a bit. He picked up one of the samples of hides sitting on the table and examined it closely. It was a peice of normal CAS type armor. He picked up a knife and cut a section of the novictum off and slid it away from the mass of it. He then trimmed the peice of CAS in a perfect square, being careful to not let the inside of it touch him and try to merge with him as CAS tended to do. After getting it cut to his liking, he turned the backside of it torwards the smaller puddel of novictum and watches the CAS's tendrils poked out sensing it was nearing something. The tendrils extended dipping into the novictum, then absorbing it up to the armor where they quickly merged. Seeing it worked as he hoped he set the peice of armor down on the counter top. "Lets try this one at max volts." He said to himself turning up a nob on the prod-like thing. He hurridly put his goggels on over his eyes, pressed the button on the rod, and touched the end to the hide. The armor peices seemed to jump a bit then it turned almost invisable, but not quite. You could see through it, but you could still see it to an extent. "This is some very intersting stuff, but it seems like its missing something." He thought outloud while scratching his head. At that moment his door buzzed. "Ah, Ithink I could use a break anyways." He said standing up and removing his goggels. He about walked away with the prod-like thing in his handm suddenly relizing he was carrying it. He turned back and set it on its end standing up, so the shocking part was up in the air and then continued on to see who was here.


He hit the button next to his door and watched it slide open, reveiling Catteni, one of his friends. "Hi Shalex!" She said waving with a smile. " Hope i'm not bothering you?" "Oh no, i'm taking a break from reasearch at the moment, please, come on in." He said while gesturing with his hand to come in. She walked in holding her hand behind her back and tunring as she entered so to not let shalex see what she was hiding. The door slid shut as got through it. Shalex smiled when he relized she was holding something and keeping it from him. "I bet I know what that is!" He said grinning wider. "Yep! It's more novictum! But this one is a bit differnt then the one you have already, it isn't subdued." She said finally reveiling the cleat canister with the mirky substance in it. "Ah yes, this kind looks very active! Where did you find it at?" He asked bending over to look into the container, then taking it up into his own hands and studying it closer. "This stuff came from the scheol area near a bunch of spirits, quite unfriendly they were." She said smiling. "Yes, but I see you managed to get this still. As promised heres the 500k Creds for the sample. And thankyou dearlym this type should help my research out tremenously." He said digging in lab coat pocket and bringing out his Cred Card. Catteni produced her electro-wallet and held it up for him. He slid his card down the side of it and the punched a few of the keys on the top of it. "There ya go." He said after hitting the last key. "Paid in full." "Great! I really need them few extra creds." Shalex wasn't paying attention to her now though, his eyes were locked onto the new novictum he had. "Well, I bet you want to get back to your studies now, so i'll leave ya be with your new toy." She said moving towards the door. "Yes, I do have alot of work to do. But please, stop back by later, I may have some interesting things to show you." He said momentaraly shifting his eyes from the container to her, and then back. "Ok cool, I have to go get some shopping done, I'll stop by later after that. See ya and be careful!" She said smiling as she pressed the door button. "Of course, nothing but safty here..." He trailed off as she went out the door. "My my, aren't you interesting." He said to the container as he turned to go back to his lab.


He quickly got back t his lab table and open the container slowly. untill now, all he had run test on was subdued forms of this substance, never an active type. The lid was fully removed and nothing happened, it didnt jump out or seem to move at all. " Well, I guess that counts out it being a living thing" He said as he laughed at his own joke. He took his knife and scraped the subdued novictum back into its container, making sure to get every last drop. That done, he tilted the new container and slowly watched the novictum ooze out onto the table. "Ah, this type is more gel based it seems," He said as he wrote some notes down on his pad. The rest of it slid out and made a bit of a splat nosie as it hit the table. He bent in closly to it, examing everything he could see. Then out of his kitchen a smoke alarm went off. He had put his lunch in nuke-o-matic earlier in the day and had forgot about it. The alarm started him badly, for he was only an inch away from the novictum. As he jumped with a start, his legs hit the table, and he watch the prod sway left and right, then finally falling right into the new pile of novictum. As the prod hit the table with a thud it set it off, sending a massive shock into the active novictum. Shalex tried to back away but it was too late, the novictum shifted and them seemed to explode right at him covering most of his body in it and tossing him a good 6 feet away. The substance quickly reacted to his body, soaking right though his lab coat onto his CAS armor he was wearing. Soon as it hit the CAS, he could feel it, penetrating it. It felt like the stuff was alive and he knew what it was going for... the missing componet....notum.. and his body was full of it. It pierced through his CAS then hit his skin, then burrowing into it. The pain was unbearable and he began to black out, but as he drifted his last thought was "I knew that was it.....notum..." and then blackness.
