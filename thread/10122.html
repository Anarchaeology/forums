I think that FC's fatal flaw was combining the skills and combat system in one level tree; many other games have SKILL levels and FIGHTING levels, so you can gain a lot of levels doing skills, and raise those levels rapidly without fighting, but you can't raise your combat abilities (beyond basic stats).

Yes, come to think of it, this is the fatal flaw. Because you can't allow people to level *easily* with tradeskills, without it being abused (example 1: hacking high level critters at low levels).

I suppose that making a gun should be at least as much XP as killing an orange mob. It just should. It takes more time, and you have to spend money instead of make it. That would help a deal. I raised my tradeskills until level 40, before I needed the IP for everything else.

But that just loops back to the problem. Have to kill. Can't rely on trade. Both do use the same skill points.

*Fatal* *flaw*.
