Cumulative odds simply mean that the total odds of it showing up in less than X become greater.

For example, flip a coin 4 times. What's the odd the 4th flip is heads? 50/50.

What're the odds that at least one of the 4 flips was heads?

1st: 50%
2nd: 75%
3rd: 87.5%
4th: ~94%

You see the difference?
