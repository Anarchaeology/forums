Getting to Longest Road
I've no idea where the dungeon is at in Longest Road, but I can tell you how to get to that region.

First, get to Newland City. You can grid there easily with a Comp Lit of 100. Go into town and take the whompa to Athens. Go out the West Gate in Athens and follow the road south. You'll hit a zone and enter Athen Shire. Keep following the road and it will bend west. This is the road called Longest Road, but the zone named after it is still a ways off. Keep going west till you zone again to reach The Longest Road. Once there, watch out for Hammers along the south side of the road and scorpions and spiders along the north side.

Oh, if you want to get some insurance along the way, there is a small outpost in Athens Shire. While heading west, you'll see a road going south. Take that to the town and insurance terminal. You can also find a neutral town in Longest Road called Drumdar. You may come across a dirt road leading south once in Longest Road. Take it and it'll take you straight to Drumdar. It's a quiet town with an insurance terminal and even a mission terminal and some very basic shops.

Good luck.

directions to the new dungeon for 40-70ers
I'm a clanner so I don't know the best way to get from an omni town to Athens, but that's where you'll want to whompa/grid into.

From athens go out the west gate and follow the road until you zone into Athens Shire.

Keep following this road until you zone into The Longest Road

It's a painfully long run but it's worth it if only to see the dungeon...

once you zone into The Longest Road keep running until you get to the 2nd x-road, turn left along this road and you'll see two omni guards. go between them and go into what looks like a rock quarry. on your right you will see a sign that says 'docker', go through the door below this sign and you'll be in the new static dungeon.
