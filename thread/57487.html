Yal nerf.....
Okie right now is traveling over Rubi-Ka not very exciting when you have gotten a Yal...
There is no place where you can get easily and fast on the planet... boring....
And there are no real diff beween a ql30 and a ql200 Yal... except cost.

So... what I would suggest in adding is a "flight time" var on the Yal that depends on ql. It should also lock the skill for a certain amount of time... (call it energy recharge of the Yal or something)
In game terms.... when you use the Yal it casts a morph/flight nano on you, when it times out you drop to the ground and the recharge timer nano starts.
That would give you a nice 10s message when the time is running out, giving you time to get down to the ground without getting damage.

Aka...

QL30
Flight: 5min

QL200
Flight: 15min

Both with a lock of 2 min.

This would result in more planing when using a Yal plus working as a small moneysink for the higher level people.
It would also work quite nicely in a RP way... the better ql the more energy it can hold in each charge... the lock time is for it to regenerate the charge from its internal powersource.
