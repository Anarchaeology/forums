Neutral RP org Shattered Dreams now accepting applicants.
Applications for Shattered Dreams are now open to any RK2 neutrals interested in solid (non-twinked) play and RPing.

We are a group commited to RP, and plan on working with the greater RP community to provide many opportunities for RP events for our members. Currently planned RP events will involve some of the major Omni and Clan RP orgs.

If you are at all interested, please send a /tell Kojiyama in-game, and we can arrange a meeting for you to learn more about Shattered Dreams' goals and policies.
