Advice on new character choice: trader v. doctor
I'm making a new character, to be used almost exclusively in a team with an MA. The character will never solo, and it will be rare that we will be in a team of more than just the two of us.

I plan to be mostly a support character, while the MA deals the major damage. I've narrowed it down to Doctor or Trader. Here's what I see:

Doctor - Dots, more healing spells, more powerful single heals(?), unstoppable high level PvP, maybe good as a pure healer, some damage potential with dots/nukes/shotgun

Trader - heals (but that hurt me), more powerful team heals because of smaller variance(?), wrangles, mezes, (useless) charms that might someday be fixed, decent damage with shotgun

I can't decide between the two, really. I'm tempted to take the Doctor for more of a pure healing supporter, but then again, having wrangles and some crowd control is tempting, too. My main is a 'Crat, so I know how useful calms can be.

Any thoughts are appreciated.

Actually, I would say trader.

With the MA and his heals/teamheals combined with a trader's heals/teamheals you should do fine. Having a doc for extra healing might be good, but perhaps you have enough healing with MA/trader sollution? The extra damage from the trader is a good thing. Besides, Getting wrangled, the MA can upload better critbuffs earlier, and shotguns with critbuffs are REALLY REALLY nice! But beeing critbuffed all the time, you might not want to do what I did, have a nanomage trader. Extra damage=aggro=pain.

oh, and by wrangling the MA while in combat, you are upgrading his weapns, not just his skills, since MA's weapons are his fists.

Well, that's my 2 creds.

But in the end, remember, there is another issues to this. Your preferences for playstyle. Playing a proffession just to keep your team stronger, while you're not really happy about your role.... might be something real bad, and what I believe is one of the reasons people quit playing.

Read what others write, but decide for yourself! Your preferences should go above all else. Even above raw power.

I would say it depends on what you want to do
A docs options are limited. With a doc your going to be (and expected to be) a healing machine. You spend most of the combat paying attention to the health bars of the group, making sure they have some red stuff left in them . Weapon options are limited to mainly shottys or weak MA without alot of the cool ma skills cause they are ip sinks.You can use pistols but they suck right now and all other guns require way to many dark blue skills to use. Other skills such as running, evades, and all vehicle types are dark blue so getting around can be a pain (although a yalm is never an ip sink) Money can sometimes be really thin since nanos cost a ton and they are the mainstay of youre character. However in PvP you can own cause everyones damage is halved and maxed at 40% but youre heals are not at all (big advantage).

Ive never played a trader but from the looks of them they seem to vary a bit in weapons/armor and style of play, however, if 14.2 comes out wrangles will be significantly nerfed in usefulness. However traders do get those BBI Faiths or whatever they are called that are sold at trader stores and the stats are incredible on them (prolly the only really great pistol in the game). And as a trader youre job seems alot more exciting as you get to deal with adds and what not.
