What is the coolest outdoor place you have seen? (Static dungeons don't count)
The coolest outdoor place I have seen would have to be in the longest road. It's a hole in the road with a blown up convoy truck. Even the trees near it are bent from what appears to be an explosion.

I also like that omni outpost north of 20k simply for the fact that it plays a sound of radio com chatter when you enter it. It's really not that impressive to most people, but the reason I list it here is because it is the single most imersive thing I have ever come across in AO. It really felt as if I had entered a guard outpost because of that radio com.

As you can see I favor the little touches that bring the world to life more than the grand things... I am interested to hear what everyone else considers their favorite outdoor sights.

Nice Topic
Here's a few for ya:

A great little scenic area - Corbyte Beach, south of the Athen West main gate (one zone). Funny thing is, I didn't discover this until a hunting trip with my first alt. . Soloing through the first 20 levels of my first character, these hunting grounds were not viable for me (6 members - lvl 5-10). I'll tell ya though, if you travel this riverside beach all the way to it's south end, just north of a small ridge, you can turn and face north and grab a great screenshot (maybe a new desktop?) of a rolling hill topped with some really nice palms.

A 2 way tie for my favorite hunting ground environment - The southern Mutant cave in Lush Hills, the fog effect there is the best I've seen in any video game. Truly a programming effort to be proud of. The other is Mort's Cyborg Factory and it's accompanying "ring" or ridge that connects the outpost and the factory. It seems that 80% of the time, there is a really harsh sand/radiation storm kickin' there and that is eerie on it's own. Mix in the paranoia caused by the cyborgs (who will own just about anybody 1v1 <@125) and the 25% gas and you get a zone where you can't just go /afk. Gotta stay on ur toes out there!

So many interesting places outside (missions, bleh), I'm glad there is talk of rejuvinating this forgotten activity.
