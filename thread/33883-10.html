emotes and roleplaying
roleplaying is mostly a matter of keeping real life out of conversation. I heard one person say, "I will be going off-world for a few hours", when she had to log; that's a good example of how to roleplay. As far as emotes go, I agree that there are not enough "emotional" emotes, but this game has more than any MOG I have played yet (EQ, AC2, Neocron, Project Entropy, Earth & Beyond). You need to be creative; maybe use some scripts.

Here's one I call cheer:

/rocky
/v Woohoo!!


Here's one I call doh:

/slap
/v Doh!


dunno:

/v Ummm...
/scratch
/delay 3000
/v I dunno!
/shrug


yep:

/v Hmm...
/strong1
/delay 4000
/v Yes.
/nod


nope:

/v Hmm...
/strong1
/delay 4000
/v No.
/nono


don't forget simple one's like /greet and /bow and /salute. sometimes the animation of the emote can be used for something other than the name suggests; for example, /fishsize might be used with greeting text to show an exuberant greeting to a close friend:

/fishsize
/v %t!!

If you have Becky targeted, you will have your arms outstretched and say "Becky!!".

If I meet a friend or aquaintance, I do:

/greet
/v hiya %t.

If I meet an org-mate, I do:

/salute
/v Hello %t.

If I meet an org officer, I do:

/salute
/v %t!
/delay 1000
/bow


anyway, you get the idea. be creative and have fun !

I second the motion for emotion emotes!

Yet more emotes
I would like to see some Martial Artist type emotes:

/kata1 (would do a karate style workout routine)

/kata2 (would do a kung fu style workout routine)

/bow2 (would do a chinese "fist in palm" greeting)

/bow3 (would do a bow with hand at sides like opponents do at a match)

--------------

I would like to see many more martial arts attacks with animations that don't suck like the "bird of prey" does. The other professions get zillions of weapons to choose from, while an MA has just a few special attacks. How about a "black fist punch" that does an "internal bleeding" d.o.t. nano on the target. Or an attack that disturbs the "chi" of the target and prevents the target from being healed for a few seconds.

Some more metaphysical type things for an MA would be cool, like a very long casting "meditation" nano that "warps" the MA to various destinations, and maybe a higher level one that warps the team. Maybe in the "Shadowlands" expansion, the MA could have a similar one to "meditate" himself into the shadowlands, or to use the shadowlands to get around somehow like the fixer grid does for fixers (the MA does it metaphysically by meditation, while the fixer does it technically by hacking into the grid).

I see the MA as a "body and mind" profession that would lean to the metaphysical. Already, the MA nanos have a metaphysical bend (Inner Peace, Outward Rage; Subconscious Guiding; Controlled Rage; Transcendent Shen Protection), so it would stand to reason that in his quest for the perfect mind/body, he would discover things like I mentioned above.
