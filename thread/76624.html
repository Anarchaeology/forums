pharmacy skillz need a makeover
1. Make regen cluster more common.

Yes the health regen and nano regen cluster were a nice touch but these clusters are much to rare atm for pharm skillz to be in too high of a demand. btw, is there a reason theres no variety on ql 200+ implants? i always see the same clusters in these high lvl implants.

2.Make emergency treatment labs more user friendly.

The blind effect is annoying enough to steer away anyone interested in using these. Id be willing to add antoher step to the process of making these to make them blind- free.

3.Make treatment/nano rechargers stackable with pharm skill.

It wastes space and i presume memory having backpacks full of these. Please make them stackable if they are close in ql and go to the lowest ql.

4.Make Stims better.

are muscle stims still bugged? If so please fix them. The Swim stim should be replaced with a better version of the runbuff stim which should add an all off mod. The booster stims are good but are extremely expensive, too costly for all but the richest in RubiKa. It would be nice if the final product was 200 stims or even 1000 stims depending on something in the formula.
