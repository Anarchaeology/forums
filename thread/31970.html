An Anarchy Online Story. Pt.1
Outside My cramped cell of an apartment, I heard them shout "Immense Lag Approaching!" Not being able to sleep, I popped out of my straight jacket and exited my cell. The cell was cold and damp, Had bloodstains on the bright white padding and a picture of a horribly ugly man that goes by the name 'Phillip Ross'. The skulls of past victims lied in the corner of the cell and the stentch of digusting flesh was hard to evade too, Better pull up my pants. As I tried to exit the cell, I realized the door was jammed, So I began pounding on it (playshift Error).. "Hey, kock off the Racket! Some of us are trying to sleep!" Exclaimed the Psychotic Nano-mage in the cell next-door to me. "I'm trying to get out of here, the damn door wont open." I said Calmy but loudly. "So, that give yah' Permission to make an ass of Yourself and wake up The others? Shut the hell up damnit! Some of us gotta sleep!" He screamed again. Trying to keep quiet, I gently pounded the door and it opened finally.

I exited the cell with a smile on my face Thinking that Today will be an Alright day, From my cell, I look to my Left and see a Newcomer and the Guide having a conversation. Using my Acute hearing, i Eavesdropped in on their Conversation. "I'm new here" Said the newcomer with a smile on his face. "Welcome to Rubi-ka, BIOTCH!" Exclaimed to the guide as she shot him between the eyes. I Paniced for a minute and then once Again I heard "Immense Lag Approaching!"
Quickly, everyone in the vacinity ran into Cells (apartments if you havent figured out) for shelter. I stood outside, scrtaching my forehead and wondering whats going to happen. I shrugged and Continued out to the city.

As I walked through the Omni-Sewers, I managed to get Nano-guck on my new Black pumps I had just baught In Newland. Anyways, I quickly ran inot the Main city, Omni-Entertainment 1. i stopped by Mongol Meats for a Burger, As I am too lazy to walk to Bronto Burger. "That'll be 59 Credits Ma'am" He said politely. "Why, Thank you sir" I said cheerfuly as I handed him the credits. I looked down at my Pumps and notice the guck was gone. Feeling a bit happier, I neearly Take a bite of the 'Burger' and Realize the Guck form my pumps was in my hamburger. I Forcefully throw the Burger at the Chef and run away. Out Of breath, I cant The Stamina Boost Nano on myself, realizing it doesn't really Do much. Oh well. I decided I'm kind of worn out, and I walk over to Rompas.

Ah, Rompas, Good, Clean fun for the Family... WHAT THE HELL IS THAT?! Not looking at the 'dancers' I walk over too a booth by myself and have a seat. A few moments Later, A 'Female' dancer shows up at my table dancing. I give 'her' an Evil look and she kept dancing. As I looked down 'her' Body, I saw a pair of what seem to look like Testicles So I quickly Cringed and Dimached her into a waiter who died of Impact Damage. feeling Sorry, I left a Tip in his pocket "Get a Real Job". feeling digusted, I attempted walking out of Rompas. Some Horribly Disgusting Level 159 Nano-Mage Blocked the doorway and said "I'll give you 500k if you Cyber me in the restroom". I smiled flirtatiously and I kicked him right were it hurts, Quickly bringing the dumbass down, As I walked over him, My heel left a Whole in his head.. I can't Say that I'm truly sorry though.

Oh well, It's Still daylight, dont wanna go back to my imprisonment just yet. So I walked over near Omni-Hq. Gunshots everywere as More and More ARK's fell to the ground like swatted flies, Leaving a puddle of blood. "Ahh! I've Walked Straight into a Riot!" I thought to myself as Hundreds and Hundreds of People gathered around me Yelling and Swearing. More and more ARK's were deployed with Riot Shields and Stun Battons. "Don't make us destroy you!" An Ark shouted Raising Up A Ql 1 Stun Batton. The angry mob replied "Give us our money back!" "YEEAAAAH!!" Shooting Nova Flow beams into the air. Desperatly The ARK's look arround with a Face of Fear, Knowing they're about to die. All of a sudden There was another Period of Gunfire and ARK's collapsing to the ground. A HUGE Atrox Had the Lead Game master at Gunpoint. "We want our muny back!" Exclaimed the Atrox. "Uhh..uhh. We Game Masters can only make This world a better place for you and I.." Were the last words of The Lead Game Master as the Atrox Sent a Hot Piece of Lead through the Lead Game Master's Head. "Immense Lag Approaching!" Was screamed agian as the mob quickly Broke up, running for shelter. There it was. Coming like a Hurricane, the lag. As I ran for my life, The Lag ate up people Like a Cop Eats Donuts.. QUICKLY! I didn't Wana be next So i ran for my life, Only to See that I am Rubberbanding Somewhat Fiercly, Crying for help I began crying and Screaming Nonsense. I turn around And I was face to face with the Lag.. It was my time..
