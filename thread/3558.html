Need quicker interface for equipping armor/clothes...
Thought I'd suggest this if nobody else has (haven't checked to see this yet), but while this isn't a serious serious problem, it is rather restricting to switch in and out of armor to get into some form of social wear.

At the moment, switching out of battle gear and into more comfortable clothes takes me 10-15 mins (I have really poor fps... does the mouse cursor really have to be drawn in an overlay plane?).

What I suggest is an added tab in addition to weapon/armor/implant... called clothing or social. It would contain the same layout as the armor tab except all that can be placed there would be clothes and such. Then add a button to the controls menu, to switch out of your armor and into your clothes. That way you're not always rummaging through your backpacks trying to find each piece that you have to wear, unequipping all the armor and equipping all the clothes and it could very well get rid of the frustrations of that social armor only error message.

It'd just be a lot more convenient. Even then, maybe no restrictions on the social/clothing tab is needed at all, just a second wear tab where you can have a second set of armor/clothing that you'd like to swap between.

This could go along to weapon swapping too (if people do use more than one set of weapons)...

This would be much more convenient so I thought I'd mention it. Hope to see something along these lines implemented. Any comments, opinions or additions to this?
