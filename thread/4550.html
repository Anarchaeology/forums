Missions and the terminals
Lots of people complain about the camping done by "missionarys", who try to find a good mission with good reward.
Of course, we all would like to get nice items as rewards, problem is that neither me or probably most of you, AND Funcom would like to see that people get items too easy. Work hard and get payed well is how it should be. Easy missions would thus give"junk" or not-so-valubable stuff.
The solution is to change the way terminals work, and let people have an option to choose from low quality missions, to mid and high quality. With quality i mean quality of missions, lenght, distance, payment and reward.

Low quality missions: Pays a small sum, not so far distance to travel, and a low item as reward, and a small amount of mobs.
Med quality: Pays fair sum of credits, a short travel of a few minutes, medium item as reward, and a medium amount of mobs.
Hi quality thus gives more money, better reward, a distant travel (from a couple of minutes to max 10. More than ten minutes travel is way to much in any cases), and lotsa mobs to fight.

Now, peoples wishes might differ a bit, where some low level want a long mission with lotsa mobs, but dont wanna travel far (going to Varmint Woods at level 10 is certain death), so maybe new options in mission booths would have sliders of:
Amount of mobs(few, medium, lotsa)/size of dungeon
Travel distance(short, medium, far)1-2 minutes to 2-5 minutes to 5-10 minutes.
Then, when picking the criterias you want, the terminal generates a mission upon this, and then generates a suitable reward item and credits. The harder the mission, the more likely to get a good, valuable or usable item as reward. Distance gives higher credits.

Another change in mission rewards could be:
-Instead of -a- piece of cloth be a complete set.
-Implants should either be basic implants or cluster, not implants with clusters. Set these to be of higher ql than your level, so you got something to use at later change of implants.
What I also could like to see, would be nanokits, treatment kits and first aid kits, maybe some with lower req of use and/or higher output of treatment. Those pop up from time to time in missions loots, so why not add them as rewards?

The option to pick a cathegory of items will just make it too simple to find ultrageer, so I give no support on it.


This would be nice for people, so they could plan a bit easier how long a mission would take. Sometimes ive got hours to kill mobs, but meet 2-3 dungeons with 1-2 to 20 mobs. That is just to plain simple if reward and credits are ok or good, when i really wanna fight a lot and get xp.
Vice versa will people who just want a quick mission on 15-30 minutes also get this chance, without having to spend too much time finding a suitable mission, running to area and then maybe have to kill for hours to get that reward..
