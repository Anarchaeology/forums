You are certainly correct in your description of Omni-Tek's priorities, but, as pointed out previously by Aazamon, the Clans have become something of a vestigial twin of Omni-Tek. They spout the same pro-profit words while living off Omni-Tek's funds. To my unworthy eye, it seems as though the Clans and Omni-Tek only fight each other for the title of Destroyer of Rubi-Ka.

And for ANY nanomage to put profit over his or her own life seems quite sad to me.

I used to laugh at the following parable:

The last two Nanomages -- one Clan, the other Neutral -- were being chased through the Wailing Wastes by Opifex Omnis riding Yalms. Omni wanted to harvest the minute traces of Notum contained in the nanomage pineal gland.

As the yalms approached, the Clan nanomage cast the general run speed nano on himself.

The Neutral nanomage said "What are you doing? That minor nano won't let you outrun a yalm!"

The Clan nanomage laughed and said "I know that! I only need to outrun YOU!"

/me smirks despite the gravity of the parable.

Of course, we all know that the Omnis would subsequently run down and kill the Clan nanomage before the Neutral mage's blood had dried on the bio-communitor....

But hey, if the profit margin is good enough, even Clanners will get a good laugh from this story.

If both Clans and Omni-Tek suspended their notum extraction for a week, all the Nanomage race would gain is a one-week stay of execution. All nanomages, regardless of affiliation, should oppose mining.

Thank you for your time.

Vytocyt

Notum is an element, a base material not made up of compounds of any other elements. When used Notum is not destroyed, rather locked away in compounds where it is of no use. There are mechanisms natural and unnatrual that can free this notum again. However if Notum is exported off of Rubi-Ka it becomes useless for the inhabitants of Rubi-ka. What's worse is that it becomes dispersed so much it can not even be recycled well. No element is infinate, there is a limit to all matter and energy. Our data over the past few years has shown this to be true. There is also much circumstantial evidence as well that Notum Mining is having a negative effect on Rubikian life forms. Notum Levels on Rubi-Ka are falling, Export is the clear culprit.

The Lands of Shadow actually show evidence of what happens when you over mine notum. That land is being ripped assunder and our researchers are currently working hard at understanding this. We do have preliminary evidence that in fact ancient Notum Mining has created a massive imbalance in space time and is destroying existance itself!

Notum Export is not only a Nanomage problem, it's a Rubi-ka problem.
