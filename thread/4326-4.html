Events
In my opinion, events should be scattered throughout a wide variety of times and game sites. Some events should be chance events that appear out of the blue, while others should be scheduled events that use filters (level, expenditure of game credits, etc.) to limit the number of people to a reasonable number for the particular event.

This combination of approaches gives the most people possible an opportunity to participate, while maintaining the fun that comes from the occasional spontaneous event.

The bottom line is this. Every player needs an opportunity to participate in an occasional event if they are to feel part of the story. At the same time, too many cooks spoils the broth so find a good balance.
