Getting to the bottom of the heal range issue
In a discussion on the Professionals forum I posted the following assesment of the healing range discussion. Letah asked me to cross-post it to you guys.

[edit]Oops... was meant to go on the doc forum... but might interest you all anyways so I'll leave it here.[/edit]

X

quote:
We have decided to restrict the positive influence of healing and buffing of lower and higher level players after the Shield Disabler Item has been used. In LC areas, after this has happened, when you are PvP enabled (after Grace) you will no longer be able to heal or buff people outside your PvP attack range.
This was the patch note on the change in question.

The PvP attack ranges I'll be using are the following ones:
  • PvP Range info taken from this post

    Level 120: PvP range = 96 to 150
    Level 150: PvP range = 120 to 200
    Level 187: PvP range = 150 to 200
    Level 200: PvP range = 150 to 200


So the doctor issue (as I understand it) is as follows:
  • 1) The level 150 doctor has an optimum range on healing. Every level gained after 150 up to level 187 will reduce the level range within which the doctor can heal. After level 187 the range remains the same.

    2) Since the doctor has only one real role in Land Control battles, the effective power of the doctor is reduced as his level moves up towards 187 because the doctor is able to heal a smaller group of his fellow combattants.

Many of us argue that the same restrictions apply to us. That is:
  • * The soldier at level 150 can attack anybody in the range from 120 to 200. Every level that he gains after that will reduce his ability to attack because he can only attack a smaller group of his opponents.

    * The level 150 MP can attack, buff and heal anybody in the range from 120 to 200. Every level that he gains after that will reduce his ability to use his tools because he is able to attack a smaller group of opponents and he is able to heal/buff a smaller group of fellow combattants.

The Doctors' follow up argument to this, is that although further levelling allows the other professions to improve their ability to use their tools within their range - further levelling does not allow the doc to improve their tools within their range, because the doctor gets no stronger as a result of levelling (in terms of strength of heal, strength of debuff etc) with the possible exception of strength of DoT which is a seldom used tool as their key role is healing.
  • * The level 187 soldier may only be able to attack the level 150 to 200 opponents (smaller range than a level 150 soldier) but his attacks are very much stronger as a result of his levelling.

    * The level 187 doctor has the reduced range of 150 to 200 but has no balancing improvement in strength of heal. Although the Doctor does improve considerably in terms of weapons strength during the 150 to 187 phase, the core role of the doctor does not improve.

Because of this, the doctors' point of view is that the reduction in PvP range is balanced for the soldier by an increase in power in their core role. Whereas the reduction in PvP range for the doctor is not balanced by an increase in power in their core role.

My points to this are as follows:
  • 1) The doctor is not alone. Level 187 MPs have the reduced range of 150 to 200 but have no improvement in strength in their core roles. They still use the same nukes, pets and debuffs as the level 150 MP. The one exception for the MP is the very new Evocation line of nanos. These are pretty much unused in Tower Battles and so fall into the same category as the top DoTs for docs - not really a used tool in Land Control battles.

    2) If the high level docs get new healing abilities that the level 150 doc does not have, then the high level docs will experience the very same thing that the soldier experiences: the level 187 doc will have a smaller range that he can affect balanced by an improvement in his core ability.

    So for example, if FC bring out a Level Req 185 nano that does AoE healing for +600, then the level 150 doc will not be able to cast it. The level 187 doc will be able to cast it and thus add power to their core ability as a balance to their reduced range.

    3) Point 2 can be illustrated concretely with the new MP pets that will come when Inner Sanctum is implemented. The new MP pets seem to have excellent Nano Resist which would be an extremely valuable property in Land Control battles.

    At present, the level 187 MP has no increase in power in his core abilities compared to the level 150 MP. When the new MP pet comes out, that MP will be able to cast the level 203 pet. It has a level restriction of 185 - so the level 150 MP will not be able to cast it. This means that the level 187 MP will then have an improvement in strength of his core ability to balance the reduction in the level range that he can affect.

    Just like the soldiers enfs etc etc


With all that said, let me take another look at the FC response to the Doc issue:
quote:
Were fully aware of the fact that Doctors use the same nano program for a long time, and have a wider healing range (in levels) at level 150 than at level 200. As we said last time: Were not looking into changing this. The level 151+ Doctors will have to improve in other areas if theyve reached their top on the healing ability.

Hopefully well give higher level Doctors some new stuff to play with in Shadowlands.
So just as the MP solution to this issue will come with an increase in their core role at higher levels because of the new pets - The doctors will find the solution to this issue with the introduction of new heals that improve their core role of healing at higher levels to balance the reduction in level range that they can heal.

I have been reading many of the threads on this issue since it first appeared in the PvP forums and later on also in the Doctor forum. It's an issue that affects any healer to an extent. The adventurers, traders and MAs all continue to see some improvement in their weapons power which is part of their core role so they are not as affected as the Doctors and MPs are.

I really do sympathise with the doctors. The MP is in exactly the same position as the Doctor. Our core tools get no stronger at all beyond level 150 - but this is because, as was pointed out in the FC reply, we are using the same tools as the level 150 MPs. The core of the doctor issue here is that the 187 - 200 doctors are using the same core tools as the 150 doctor. If the high level doctors get better, stronger heals (or other abilities) then their reduction in level range will be balanced by an increase in power - in exactly the same way that a 187 Soldier's reduction in range is balanced by an increase in power.
