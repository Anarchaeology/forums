Red Tape of Omni-Omni Admin seeks Peace with Borpac but is Unwilling to Negotiate
Members of the Nanomage Liberation Front have started an information campaign in Omni-Tek held areas of Rubi-Ka in a peaceful attempt to end the threat against Nanomage People. We have been harrassed and threatened in our efforts but our own safety is of less concern than our goals of a Rubi-Ka forever, and Nanomages Forever.

When General Zargin, Commander Galius, and Merijynn entered Omni-1 Entertainment district today they brought with them nothing but information. Before they even set up thier signs and beginning to speak our abou the plight of our people they were arrested and expelled from the city forcefully. Immediatly after this I recieved a communication from Commander Galius stating that a high ranking Omni-Tek official would like to have a meeting with me.

President Kithrak of Red Tape of Omni Admin came with his lackies Shuldrich and Collaps to meet with Me, my Generals and a few fellow Borpact representitives of Desert Winds. I must say, that even thier meeting me was impressive. After a long year of struggle of being ignored, and being hushed by all powers that be I was impressed that they came down to Borealis to talk with us.

It became clear after some initial discussions that we had some common ground. Kithrak is a Nanomage, as is Collaps, and he is concerned with his own well being. Kithrak let me know that he is researching personally an answere to the problem of Notum depretiation and Nanomages. This is no surprise really, but to let me know that this is the case means that the problem must be more serious than even I imagined. Omni-Tek has always ludicrusly claimed that the Notum will last forever and that no depletion takes place. Of course we all know that nothing is infinate, and that the Nanomage people are tied to the limited resource of Notum on Rubi-Ka. But this revelation that Omni-Tek is beginning to admit that the notum supply is dwindling, with the clans at OTRK's doorstep, I think there is a changing wind in the air.

Unfortunatly even though the Borpact alliance was willing to negotiate a cease fire for the benifit of Rubi-Ka Kithrak was unwilling to even explore if our demands were achievable. Our demands were as followes

1. To meet with all the heads of the major OTRK departments and Organizations to state our concerns. This was replied with the claim that not Kithrak or anyone had the power to call such a meeting.

2. To begin an indipendant study of the problems of Notum Mining with respect to it's effect on the Nanomage people and Rubi-Ka. this would need to be done with full oversight of the Borpact and a neutral third party (Neutral as in not a party to the dispute). Thier response was that while Omni-Tek was at war with the Clans Omni-Tek can't afford to do any research into this matter.

3. To petition Philip Ross to make a statement about Omni-Tek Rubi-Ka's efforts to ensure the future of the Nanomage People. We know that Mr. Ross is under many outside pressures so we only stated taht for our cease fire that Kithrak pubicly petition Philip Ross to halt the fighting, not to actually have Philip Ross actually speak. We are seeking to change how the departments within Omni-Tek view our dispute and we want to see some action from them to change. Thier response was to say that because Philip Ross would never make any such statement it was worthless to try.

Now clearly we are being reasonable, why would Omni-Tek not wish to deal with us if Rubi-Ka's future were truely in it's best interest? Because the need for short term profits is all Omni-Tek is after at this point. The lease is almost up, so why spend any money or effort to improve the situation? I'll tell you why, the Clans are comming Omni-Tek, don't fight a two front war when we could be working together for the future of Rubi-Ka.
