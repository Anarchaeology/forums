What are your Team Mission nightmare stories?
Okay.. being a naive engineer, I joined a few groups. Most of my experiences with groups were great.... but not with this one (and is probably why I will never trust an Atrox team leader)...

1. I was asked to tank with my 'bot. I didn't think much of it... 'til I noticed the mobs kept trying to kill me first instead of the other team members.

2. The loot order was set to "Loot Team All"... needless to say I never got the chance to loot anything.

3. I got lost during the mission because everyone else left me behind. Finally I found my way to the lift to the boss room... and the rest of the team was waiting for me (apparently none of them wanted to go out and find me)

4. During the boss battle I was told to have my 'bot tank. Big mistake... the boss started shooting at me. I asked for a heal, but never got it... eventually my bot got killed and so did I, losing 7000+xp... but to top it all off..
