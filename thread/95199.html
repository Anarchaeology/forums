Farewell Rubi-Ka
And at last, it was my turn too.

Real life has given me an oppurtunity I feel I can't pass out on.
I don't dare to play AO anymore.

I started out playing AO the day it was released. My RL brother (Evilbaffle on RK2) and a good bud of mine (Cyberbob on RK2) wanted to try out this "Anarchy Online"

... and so I ended up here

Lil' Deng-history

My first character was named "Hyrkania" after a player in Planetarion. was gimped beyond believes as I sincerely thought that there would be IP's to raise all skills
Then RK2 opened up and I saw a "new start" after a bad month.
And so my adventure started. I joined in on a new guild that was about to be started, the "Inner Circle", who initially was a guild for the few highlevels that existed at the time (read highlevel as 70+ at the time). Over the first half year, I got to know Grontha, Antashi, Toorqs, Kirsta, Armycastaway, Rubydoobie, Gizzan, Shtylez, Isnal, Uberfix (as krossarn), Orbakay, Illuvatar (eaze), Ipsa ++ many of what we can call *true* old farts in this game. close to christmas, I was proudly made "General of morale" in IC by Grontha, our boss at the time. This was quite an experience, as IC at the time, consisted of all the level-happy, jumpy people with little interest in others view on any situation. So I ended up getting a good deal of practice on trying to settle arguements.

At this point, we all hunted medusas... Spawn was broken very often and we ended up fighting for meds. Some of us went to Ace camp (which consisted of vets/aces and was 25% at that time) and had some good experiences there

THe spring of 2002 was a new experience.. had a short break from AO, and when I returned, Shtylez, Grontha and all the others had levelled from me like mad. I ended up levelling like mad for 2 weeks, but from there, it went slowly IC fell hard on the fact that we had loads of people who didn't manage to live together in a guild. Many spread to become Total Chaos, Cult of Buffy, Last Hope and other guilds

late spring, 2002 I was given Leadership of IC by Meyra, bless her memory, who had more than enough to do. did a little recruitment push on IC and set some fairly easy rules to follow..
Before I got to shuffle my thoughs, I went from a happy lil enforcer to starting organizing Rumbles, Tara-raids, wars, raids and my friendslist became very crowded.

At this time, I got the urge to start testing Enforcer-weps/armor/strategies as we were a pretty booring class at the time. I met a sweet adventurer I walked to the altar with (Amberrai) who's ment the world to me. Inspiration and motivation doesn't come just like that

as time went by and NW was opened, it became clear the RK2 was imbalanced beyond what was possible to cover for. people went clan as they had tara, towers, special.. everything looked dark. In the end, we did a few stunts that weren't excactly nice and took a lot of concideration, but when we did it, something rang in the head on many Omnis and clanners that it might be time to change things... So we made a few deals, a few Clanners went omni.. and more followed. Many good players. This lead to new wind in Omni sails. Tara-wise and Tower-wise. Never thought we'd be able to fill up Camelot *and* ace camp, but it happened

IC merged with Last Hope to a new strong front. People on both sides have been taking things *very* well, and I'm proud of them.
---

AO has ment a lot to me. Not just a game, but a world of friends on both sides, seing them on good and bad days, in battle and in peaceful situations. Figured it was time to quit when someone called me "Legends of Juggernauts". Felt old...

After some time of playing, seeing people come and go, telling their stories, peeping at how RL affects them, trying to quit, comming back, ending up without job/relationships/social life, I wish there was more I could do for them... Aside from leading org, giving a happy message, some loot they need or.. whatever... I wish I could help them take the decition I have done and stick to it. Because it's obvious that a LOT of people, given enough time, will be changed by AO.
I found myself delivering small white lies, delaying bills, canceling sleep just to join that raid, just to twink that char or somthing simmilar. This isn't something that can continue..and I'd rather quit now that I have the possibility.

Problem with quitting, I think is that many people don't have anywhere to go *to*... they simply go from AO to a strange RL situation where they have nothing to fill the gap with.

hmm 2 cents in the end there

Anyhow.. as a good norwegian I'm holding a "into-the-Grave-beer" for AO IRL on saturday in my house in Oslo. if you have the possibility to come, you are invited. allready have 4 players comming

send a mail to pinny@contraz.no if you want to come
hell.. send me a mail if you want I'm oversocial

It's been a pleasure to know you all here on the board...

Hope you won't forget me that quick... (I.E. name the next Mongo after me. "Mongo Deng" sounds mean)

Deng - old fart
