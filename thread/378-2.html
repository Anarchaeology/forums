Sneaking missions was very fun at one time.

Once upon a time, we could rehide. There was still ample danger involved with sneaking missions, we could take loot.. and a good portion of the risk came from opening locked chests.

We did not receive tokens for this, and as a result ... most agents and Adventurers do not have tokens to this very day.
