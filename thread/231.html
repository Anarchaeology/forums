In-game surprise...Please change to use shoulder slots
You've probably heard it from dozens of people already, but I would like to add my request to have the bag not take up the back slot, which are used by robes, larger backpacks, social armor, tank armor, etc. I think, since there is a serious lack of useable shoulder armor, that you should make it equippable in either shoulder slot, as well as the back slot. You don't need a graphic for the shoulder slot - I'm happy with the backpack only being visible in the back slot.

Please give this some consideration. I can't see it unbalancing the game, and the people that have been supporting you since before July 8th are still here dealing with the bugs that keep getting introduced in every patch. Cut us some slack, please, and make this bag really worthwhile - right now it's just a novelty, not truly useful, because as much as I could use the miniscule stat increases, I don't want to give up my trenchcoat - it's part of the roleplaying aspect for me.
