Omni-Tribuanl Wants you
Anyone out there intrested in joinning a guild check out ours. http://www.omni-tribunal.com

The Omni-Tribunal is a independently owed sub contractor for Omni-Tek. There are reports show that there might be ties to the ICC, but nothing has confirmed this info yet. The Omni-Tribunal was founded on Saturday july 21st 1:06:19(UTC) PM 2001 , this took place at 20k

Currently we are 50+ strong and always looking for others. I am the hiring manager for Section 7 CI-CON the guild inter-ops. Section 7 is divided into 3 subsection. Below you will find a brief discriptions of the sub-sections. If your intrested please head over to the web site and fill out an app. You can also email me with any questions at tyreus@omni-tribunal.com
You will also find information on the other sections at the web site.

7-A Counter-Intel: This department maintains the responsibility to insure the safety of the tribunals operations. Some of the tasks would include sending out false information to confuse a enemy intel gathering network, it would also send out security alerts throughout the tribunal. This group would also carry out any type of assassinations of purposed threats to the tribunals operations.
7-B Survelance: This department will maintain a contact vigilance on all day to day operations throughout Omni-Tech; tasks such as current events major plot line events such as the recent halloween pumpkin head event. This group will also work with section 7-C in a limited capacity. This group is responsibly for making intel determination and assessments and current and future situations.
7-C Covert Ops: This groups primary responsibility is to infiltrate the Clan just as Janis. Because a Omni-Tech agent can not simply stroll into a Clan area without drawing attention members of this group will have to change allegiance from time to time. Once a member of this group infiltrates Clan operations the intel they gather will be a crucial to the tribunal as a whole. Because of the dangers involved and huge sacrifices involved in working in Covert Ops (ie: loss of token board) I would like to request special privileges from section 2.
