How about add a 'Treat' for Trick or Treat?
Why not automatically grant everyone with an active account something for each character for each holiday, with a date on it, or somthing to set them appart. Halloween no-stat masks, Easter Bunny no-stat ears, Santa hats, somthing simple and fun.

Take UltimaOnline for example, each holiday each character gets a bag, with a few minor items in it, depending on the holiday, or special occation. But then there is also unique NPC's spawned *like the Uncle Pumpkin Heads*, but EVERY ONE of them drop something.

Make most of the items, if not all, unique, and no drop. So everyone that is subscribed at the time gets it, and if they go find it, get ONE and then leave the camp.

Personally I was very dissappointed and depressed over not getting ANYTHING for my second holiday while living in Rubi-Ka. I spent hours 'camping' these pumpkin heads, just for a 'minor' pair of sun glasses. Sure the +1% crit and exp are desired by those who think it will actually make their character perform a lot better. Hardly. For one, they are losing a decent amount of AC that would make a difference on their character's performance. But for my character, it was because he has not, nor ever will wear a helmet. And I wanted a simple 'Show and Tell' item, to say "Yeah I was there that X holiday during 29476."

And as the message that was broadcast all day long "This only happens once a year!" Well for once, could the chance of somthing that is happening only once a year, be higher then never all day at getting something?
