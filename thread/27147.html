Tank Armor vs Coats (and how to fix the Tank Armor)
At this point there are two commonly available back slot item options in the game: Tank Armor and Decus Coats.

The AC provided by both are roughly the same, QL for QL, but the Tank Armor is loaded with disadvantages. (Yes, I know the nano-cost penalty is decreasing in 14.2, but the evade and runspeed penalties are still there.)


This leads to hordes of people running around in cookie-cutter outfits.

BORING.


So, how to improve Tank Armor? I'm glad I asked!

Tank armor is supposed to be this enormous, heavy super-tech armor, right? Therefore, let us technically improve it.

Make Tank Armor the equivalent of a Belt Slot, letting us add a NCU mem, or a Recompiler, or a Coolant sink or whatever.

"Enhancing" Tank armor in this fashion should be a hack, not a factory supported option, requiring someone with Electrical Engineering to configure the Belt Component and requiring Comp Lit and B&E to install it.


Good idea? Bad Idea?

IMO the runspeed/evade penalties are realistic since you've

modify self poison ac 250

modify self radiation ac 250
