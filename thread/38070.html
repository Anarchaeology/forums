Purpose:
The purpose of Thorns, Inc. is to establish a presence on Rubi-Ka and increase the corporations foothold on the on goings of Omni-Tek. We will focus on company gain and not individual gains. We will grow slowly but strong.

Who are we?

Thorns, Inc was founded by Robert Thorns in 28702 as a gun for hire group. After several years of successful operations the groups reputation earned them a long term contract with the Omni-Tek Corporation as a security team for high level executives. In 29458 the contract ended and Thorns grandson Thomas incorporated the group and re-organized it into a security firm from which it expanded its clientele to include many large and small corporations as well as the ICC.

In 29476 Omni-Tek once again hired Thorns Inc. for security detail on the companys pet planet Rubi-Ka. Entrusted with the highest security clearance, even higher than Omni-Pol and Omni-Med, Thorns, Inc reports only to Philp Ross. They have been given complete jurisdiction rights over Rubi-ka if the situation is called for.
