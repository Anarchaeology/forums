Social and Custom Personalization Will Save Rubi-ka
Well I've played so many games and am an old fart so I don't know where to begin. Well perhaps I do. Let's start with a game I played for over 9 years and is still active today.

Gemstone III - Started at a base of 50 people and was an original mud owned by I.C.E. and then when put onto AOL started wracking up 2,000 people at one time on peek hours. It became the very first massive multi-player game and had more subscribers than any other game at the current time. Had its own graphical front end and added some nice features to make MUD'ing work well. The reason why its alive today?

Reason 1 - Social Lives - Most of the people playing are mature (ages 20 - 60 in real life) They have their own personalities, and a rich storyline to thrive on. Friends don't leave because they build on the friendships and have Simucons and Vegascons where players meet and form social bonds outside of the game.

Reason 2 - Character Personalization - You can customize and alter any piece of clothing/item you wear to make elaborate descriptions. No two people look alike. You have custom armor, weapons, and even housing. The custom houses you own can have their own furnishings and even though it costs a lot it can be done in great detail. You can choose the area you want to live in and the real estate can go up or down depending on game events. You can add custom descriptions and real time effects to your house as well. You even have guild houses that were custom built from the ground where you can go in and store items in your own private locker and sit at tables and order food etc.

The Downfalls - Its a MUD - well an elaborate MUD. They take up to 2 years to fix bugs and to do simple profession balancing. The reason people don't mind is because they enjoy the social aspects to the game which more than make up for the bugs.

The cost - 12.95 for one character (yep one single character). $32.95 a month for a premium account which allows you 10 characters. $79.95 a month for a platinum account which allows you special services.

How many people are still on at peak hours now today at the present time? 968 people online.


Everquest - I won't go into this popular game because people already know the good and the bad but I will touch on the auction system that was revamped. The ability to sell your items while you are offline is something fabulous. AO should implement a similar feature or hi-tech bazaar.

(NOW THEN)

Things that will make AO better and help the future of the game.

1. Create public housing and guild housing. Elaborate more on houses and guild houses. Allow full furniture customization. Allow for mood music to be selective within your own apartment (zone). Allow for ratio furniture placement and limit the pieces to 50 per apartment. Allow for banking in guild houses and public houses. Allow players to buy entire structures. Allow players to customize their own structures.

2. Fix "custom missions" on the mission terminals. This would allow people to make their own custom missions to ****e up the adventure some. I know all about the no-drop exploits that people used on that feature in the past but c'mon its been months upon months now and its still not working as intended.

3. ****e up the Storyline. One of the greatest things about some of the special games I have played is that when a storyline was present, monsters (mobs) could be created in waves to simulate an area attack and yet I have not seen one large scale area attack or multiple scale area attacks since the attacks on lush resorts with the cyborgs. Correct me here someone but I believe the borgs were the only ones and that was a lot of fun while it lasted. They also pushed again in Avalon during the same time as well. Create "popular" public appearances that are NPC run for notables like Phillip Ross, etc. but don't bugger up the appearances by letting players kill them easily *cough*. Create more "animosity type" figures that can interract and cause some stir in the community at any given time so that people start spreading rumors about the tyranny of such and such.

4. Create more content and rich areas to explore. Add snow. Fix the weather on Rubi-ka. Yes I'm omni and it is quite well known that they originally tampered and screwed up the weather biosphere machines on the planet which causes the sporadic problems on rubi-ka. Allow for unusual texturing in certain areas so that everything doesn't seem so dark and foreboding all of the time.

5. Add more character animations. Add more facial, body, features. Create body chop shops that allow for people to go in and customize their body with tattoos and change their face and appearance for a price whenever they want. Let people control their looks and the feel of their clothing, armor, weapons.

More will follow later...
Feel free to add..

Idea
I posted and sent this idea in the Professionals and to the community staff but I'd like to share it with all of you.

This suggestion can be reworked and reoutfitted in any way shape or form to work with the storyline.

It is based off "the juggernaut". I call it the ICC Juggernaut but because of peace issues we could call the ship that comes to Rubi-ka something completely different like the Juggernaut EC03-11.

Story and interaction:

The Juggernaut EC03-11 comes to rubi-ka amidst the uproar of uncertain outbreak of war in an effort to help restore peace with a one month peace break called the "Festival of Rubi-ka". The Juggernaut is a trade ship and has many wonderful stores and features that can be visited.

Now the brunt of this event is that I firmly believe it should be a "paid" event in that we as customers can decide whether we want to take part in purchasing an entrance fee into the event or not. The event money would have a good portion of it split down into commissions for the designers who assist with the event and for those that work with monitoring the event on a "live" basis.

Now by purchasing access to the event you have access to the stores. Some of the items can be no-drop - some might not be. Some services might be "in-person" only.

What would the juggernaut have in its stores?

How about:

A tattoo parlor for real body tattoos that will appear after your clothes are removed.
Custom Clothes with emblems and special insignias and even naming personalization.
Special trinkets and toys, utilities, etc. that can be used for role-play

Custom Weapons and Armor:

You can buy your own base armor version and then incorporate different design meshes that will allow for personalization but still maintain the same original bonuses. You might even be able to rename the armor to something you like.

Customize your weapons so that they show ownership or even add an extra stock or special design to an already existent one that increases base damage or certain abilities.

Visit the Body Chop Shops:

And change your appearance completely. Upgrade all of your looks from body weight to facial expressions to even new hair.

Buy into a special gift prize that when used or hacked, spawns a completely unique item that no one knows what it will be. Call this vendor the mystery item vendor.

Have wedding chapels and romance supplies for those that want to give flowers or special gifts to their loved ones.

Create Festivities and Entertainment:

Throughout the month, especially on the weekends have entertainment geared with great splashes and interesting quirks like special dances and even mass assemblies.

Finish off the event on the last weekend of the one month stay and create a huge bang or a special role play scenario that will surely make everyone remember the events.

For shops and special vendors you can create specific zones so that if too many fit into one shop, they will go into an alternate shop.

Please add onto this idea as you see fit.
