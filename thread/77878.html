Proposal to fix the multi PF problems
Although it sure is nice to be able to loot Tara 4 times every 18 hours instead of just one time like similar outdoor mobs, most people will probably agree that the way the Tarasque raids are done now is not what FC intended.
Mirror playfields should only open when a playfield is full, everything else leads to the problems we see now - some people considering logging alts to open PFs an exploit, other people consider blocking a PF as griefing ...

I offer 2 proposals to change this:

1. If you log off in the castle, when logging in you are not placed in the PF you previously were, but in the PF you would be if you had just entered through the door.
This would not only prevent people from using lowbie alts to open mirror PFs, but also solve the issue of people logging into already full PFs, interrupting raids in progress.

2. Keep the multi PF system as it is now, but have only one common spawn timer for all Tarasques. Tarasques in all PFs spawn virtually at the same time, and if one Tara is killed all others despawn and all spawn timers are reset to 18h.
Then opening multiple PFs would be useless, and no matter what PF is currently open, it would be the one Tara spawns next in.


If you reply to this post, please try be constructive. I know some of you want phat lewt more than anything else, but the top priority should be to make Camelot enjoyable for all participants.
