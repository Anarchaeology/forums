My Awakening
For the longest time, I laid on that small hill outside Newland. Why? I don't really know. I enjoied the smell of nature around me, and the bustling activity below. My body would fill with the air of a cool morning breeze, and my eyes would slowly open to the grass bending to the wind. I raised myself, and although my arms felt made of lead, I struggled to bring them into the lost position of my order.

Something different happened that day. I returned to my resting when I saw something on my hand. My eyes gave it a quick examination. But I saw something then that I didn't see before.

Then I realized I was aware. Not just aware of my hand, but aware of the ground around me, of the bustling people below, of myself.

I had awaken.

It had been so long since I emerged from the growth vats, I could barely remember the small Nano disks as they were implanted into my spine, or the nurses as they monitored my vitals. They had trained me as a Martial Artist, a master of the ancient arts, and I took my role without consideration. For a countless time I followed their every will, every whim.

I sat on that hill, and realzied that wasn't living. That was surviving.

The time had begun for me to live.
