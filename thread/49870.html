The system works fine as it is today.
All your chars are different persons on RubiKa even if it is you who run them all and have them in the same account.

I think there should be some risk involved in transfering items between chars.
Consider that you have borrowed a friends laptop to play AO away from home and when you get to the place where you were to meet to return it he isn't there yet. You are in a real hurry and see that a completely unknown person is standing there. Q: Would you give him the laptop and tell him to wait a few minutes for your friend to come there?

However, there is a version of a banksystem that would work. You add a "bank shop". A building only availible in the major cities in wich you have "bank booths". These works as ordinary trade shops but you have something like deposit/withdraw.
You put the items or money) on the deposit trade and enter a chars name, click trade and it will emerge in that chars bank, waiting for him to do his withdrawal when he log on/get to the bank.
There should ofcourse be a limit to the number of items a char can have/trade in the bank at the same time as this should not be used for storage only trading, maybe only one item at the time and no backpacks to make it a little more demanding for the twinks
This system will also not allow any nodrop exploits as you can't put nodrops in shops or trade them to others.
It will also make trading over RubiKa easier.

I kinda like it the way it is now tho. It's more personal.
As it is now, if an omni and a clanner should decide to do a trade they have to meet on neutral ground in person. This would go away as the clanner could sit in Tir and the omni in Trade sending tells, coming to an aggreement about the price and doing the trade without even seeing each other. Makes things a little sterile I think, maybe it would work better if it was only omni to omni and clan to clan?
And the risk in trading items between your chars would go away, even if it would make it more "legit" as you have the RubiKa Bank System to trade with instead of a account bank.
