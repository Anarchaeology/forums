Post ideas for new nanos here:

I have a nice idea for a new NT nano:

"Programmed illusion", qlvl 100

"A molecular model of the caster is holographically projected on the surface, extracting nanobots from the environment to digitally represent a duplicate of the caster"

This nano lets the NT produce a duplicate of him/herself. The duplicate has the exact same appearance and equipment as the original. It also does exactly what the original does.
The controlling player can see the duplicate transparent, while all other cant tell the difference.
The duplicate follows the NT, side by side, everywhere it goes.
If casting a nano on a target, the duplicate also does this.
Monsters dont know which is the original, making them target you only 50% of the time.
The duplicate will disappear after a period of time, or if its hitpoints are reduced to 0.

A nano like this will really make the NT more interesting to play, and much more effective in solo play.
