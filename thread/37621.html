This isn't even really an issue over the name superslag. That name is just a rallying point. The real issue is maturity. In a game that requires a credit card (18 yrs or older) or having your parental consent with them holding full responsibility for your actions if you're a kiddie, it's sad that someone can be immature enough to take offense from a word. Maybe I'm just a desensitized individual, but I'm barely in my third decade and can't think of a single word that offends me in a passive sense. I suppose there might be a FEW that do if used AT me, but to take offense at a word in passing etc, is a sign of inflexibility and immaturity. Our world is more and more becoming global and multicultural...if you can't bend, then you're in for a lifelong insulted existence.

Now in the case of Superslag, the only thing I can come up with is this:

You may not use any sexually explicit, harmful, threatening, abusive, defamatory, obscene, hateful, racially or ethnically offensive language. This includes all communication with other players.

Now if you take his name in the slangful way that Funcom seems to want to, then I suppose they have every right to make him switch it. We can all agree with that I think. It's the utter patheticness of the individual who took it in that way, and the utter patheticness of Funcom in supporting that individual that we rant against. Or at least I do.

Face it. Any one of us could take ANY name in game and decide it 'offended' us. We have this 'power' I suppose. Why in the world would we choose to though? Are we so little as to call down the powers that be over someone's name?

I pity the poor individual who did take offense at Superslag. They must live a very closed and sheltered life.

While Cosmik can say it's a dead issue, I will continue to bump my support for Superslag, because to me and I think many others, it is most definitely not a dead issue. Waving a magic wand only works in fairy tales I'm afraid.
