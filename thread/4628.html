Funcom has left low health players at the end of the food chain
This game is built for breeds with high health. There is no denying it. If you are a low health breed...nanomage.....then don't even bother doing anything alone. A extra line should be added to the nanomage profession description: "Warning: this breed should only be chosen if you plan to group only, if not please don't pick this breed."

I'll give me reasons why. Please keep in mind this is from a nanomage/NT point of view.

Nano resistance: is probably in the worst shape it has ever been. Are grey mobs who give 1 point exp supposed to be a challenge? If so why not make them give more exp? I still get debuffed, dotted, drained, and nuked on a consistent basis with maxed resistance. Oh thank you funcom, I finally resisted a grey mobs dot. To bad his next execution was successful. Anythng after grey makes my resistance obsolete. On the other hand MOBS have the luxury of super resistance. This is "HIGHLY" noticable with grey and green humanoid mobs. They do it more then yellow, orange and red mobs. Since green and grey mobs fall under the solo player category, it is evident that Funcom feels it should be harder for the solo player. The nanomage feels this more then any other profession.

Green mobs - super resist when they are below 50 percent health.
Grey mobs - chain resist through out a whole fight.

Why am I fighting grey mobs you ask? Simple, 50 percent missions have yellow mobs that tear me into pieces in less then 5 seconds. I don't have the choice of 50 percent missions anymore so now I am "FORCED" to pick lower then 50 percent missions. Oh yeah that reminds me since low end missions have massive grey mobs I don't get the token for wiping the whole floor. Thanks again funcom.

Evades: So now I max evades thinking it would make up for my lack of health. Wrong again. Sure it slows down the critical shots, but it doesn't slow down the consistent dmg done by the mob. Armor is factor that is suppose to decrease dmg done to you, but since nanomages rely on int/psy they have the worst armor in the game. Nano cloaks have the worst armor rating. Combine this with low health and you have the worst time with "consistent" dmg. Maxed evades won't help. If you change your Aggr/Def bar to the defense end, then you will have a slow attack, with consistent dmg, and poor health. Again, no solution.

Lets see what we have now for a nanomage:

Poor health+poor resistance+consistent dmg from mob+poor armor+high mob resistence+high health mobs = no chance to solo play with a nanomage.

Where is our advantage you ask? Supposedly our advantage was nano pool, but since we have such poor health that cancels out.

Poor health/nanopool = No Advantage


Plain and simple nanomages are worth nothing in the battlefield. They are worth nothing to the NT profession and quite frankly are a worthless race at this point. Since nanomages have poor health these other existing problems were supposed to be our advantage. It turns out it all goes against us.


The only advantage a nano mage has is in nano programming. To think that nano programming would be the momemtum changer in this story.
