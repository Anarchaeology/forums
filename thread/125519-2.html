at your level, twinking won't do anything bad, however, many people often fall into the trap where they spend IP's before an implantchange, where they are left with little IP's to spare, and a bit too much placed in some skills.

example.

"Havregraut", the enforcer, is using premade ql 100 implants. He's in his 80's and is keen on a new mongo. So he maxes PM/BM like a madman. a couple of levels later, he's gotten his hands on OT-rings, changed his implants to 140-150ish, swapped the Int he had in eye and ear with PM and what he sees is that he's spent way too much IP in PM/BM, leaving him not enough skill to raise MM/MC who's allready lowered a bit due to no more int-imps, and his rage/challenger suffers. Havregraut is angry.

the pointer here, is that there is no point in wasting IP's unless you got it covered with your skill after equipmentchange.

1. FoB = Flurry of Blows
2. Carbonium is made using trade skills. You'll need to either buy it from another player get it from a friend (in which case, you'll need to provide parts).
3. Assault Tank can be obtained as loot in the Foreman's static at 1940 x 775 in The Longest Road.
4. If you're omni, you can get the pieces that make up a med suit from a tailor or clothing shop. If you're not omni, you'll need to ask one to get it for you.
