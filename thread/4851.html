Put banks in shops, please!
The new tradeskill shops are awesome. Thank you.

Now, that we have those, can you please put a bank terminal in the room with them?

Here's why:

Now that encumberance is on its way back in, I'll have to stop carrying around all my tradeskill items with me. Which means, every time I want to work on skills, I have to go by the bank, switch out what packs I'm carrying, and then go back to a shop to buy/sell additional trade items.

Banks are usually near shops, but the zoning becomes a pain. Please put banks in shops to help with this.

(On a side note: it's also frustrating to not be able to look in bags that are in the bank. EQ did similar earlier on--you could look in, but not remove items. It's just frustrating, especially given that there are only three pack icons currently, and its hellish trying to keep up with what you have in packs.)
