I posted a 2 page explination on how "double-tapping" works on zeroshifts post then someone deleted the whole thread, which is aggravating. So I'll sum it up here.

DOUBLE TAPPING ONLY WORKS ON NANOS WITH UNDER 2 SECOND RECHARGE.

Clear enough?

Roots have 3 sec recharge and therefor all double tapping does is stop the animation, the recharge bar still goes. What the double tap DOES do on nanos with 1 sec recharge like AOE nukes and Expertises, is it cancels the animation and removes the false waiting peroid between casts because even tho you arms are moving, your recharge is finished.

So NO BODY is instarooting you through an exploit.

Understood and appreciated every word you wrote, Cloudeh. You're underscoring the reasons why so many find PvP in its current form a waste of time. The 'skills' required to be good simply aren't the skills of a good warrior. They are the skills of being able to manipulate game mechanics to your advantage.

We need a PvP patch BADLY. Losing Line of Site should remove Autoface is a start. Providing rewards for team-based PvP where the back ranks can support their Melee tanks is another. IMHO, there's no reason two people should be chasing each other around like idiots anyway. The tired statement is 'we're at War not Duel'.

So speaking of dueling, they need to provide proper Arenas where two people who wish to duel can be assured of a fair fight. The translucent dome comes to mind. Buff stripping terminals to get in is another. Most of us believe in a duel based solely on what your character can do for his/herself.

And Wargames. We need a couple Neutral/Clan newbie zones to be setup as 0% zones so that two six person teams can face off against each other in a controlled 'paintball' type environment.

The game needs a PvP patch something fierce. Your post, Cloudeh, just points out how much the current setup is hurting.

And as others have stated, Traders are not Shopkeepers.
