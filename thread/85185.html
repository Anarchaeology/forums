The top 5 in mass PvP:

1 - The person who does not care about titles but is more focused on the overall goal of the team.
2 - The person who communicates with their team/side.
3 - The person who understands the abilities of their profession.
4 - The person who functions well with others.
5 - The person who keeps on going, even after a visit to the reclaim.

Every profession has their abilities. When combined into a single force, it then becomes a matter of which side functions better as a team. A pack of title-hungry individual d00dz will always fall to a well coordinated team.

edit: There was actually a tie for #5 between "The person who keeps on going, even after a visit to the reclaim." and "The person who doesn't get violated by Crowd Control and lag too much" but because the latter of the two can be attributed to technical problems within the game itself, it was disqualified....
