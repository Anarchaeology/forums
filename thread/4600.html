My Christmas wish list
My list is short

1. Save Mission Booth Settings. I don't know how many times
I have set them sliders to Bad, Order, Hidden, Money. Would
be great if my preferences were remembered by the booths.

2. Make the mission area name appear in the first lines of
text of the description so you don't have to scroll down every
time to find out for how long you have to run.

3. Nothing happens when you right click a mission. Why not
upload the mission coordinates to the map when you right click?
This is also a very common task.

4. One last painfull scroller: the agg bar. When I play my doc
I use this a lot during the most intense seconds of team fighting
and the interface is just too slow and too tiny. All I do is put
full agg or full def. Oh, I can't say how happy I would be if I
could write two macros to do this. Can you add a command to set
the agg bar?

Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year
