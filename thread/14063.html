Not Fair! hehe
would like to say welcome to the world of PVP. Once you get over 74th lvl a fixer's root will be the last thing you will have to worry about. Come back to the forum after you get ganked by a 150+ soldier in a dark alley.

At least you have the ability to heal yourself, and you have a decent selection of weapons (I know ADV is gimped too). Fixers have basically historicly had 1 choice for a weapon. We have 2 now (whoohoo!) and 3 if we are high enough level to waste several hundred thousand IP use a Suppressor. Funcom's combat/skill system is rather whacked, but I do not see why everyone needs a way to break roots! Some people were intended to be able to break roots on their own (Enforcer).
Others are hopelessly defenseless (MA). This is obviously intended. Everyone was previously using a bug to break roots earlier and now you cannot. Now that the game is working more the way that it was intended non root capable classes are whining like babies. Get this the Nano Tech, CRAT, Agent, Trader and Fixer can root. The Enforcer has the ability to break roots with the berserker rage nano. That leaves the ADV, MP and MA without the ability to root or effectively counter roots. So if most classes have the ability to root or coutner roots then why is FC going to take the root away?

The fixer root is the weakest as we cast 20-30 lvls below our skill for some odd reason that funcom apparently is not going to correct. It seems that most proffessions in AO can root. Good luck getting enough support to get this changed. A lot of the IP that I have invested in my nanos is wasted as they do not work as intened in pvp or pvm. It is about time this was changed. I will surely be a victom of a root in pvp. You can be sure that I wont be in the forum whining like a baby because I died. I am 153rd lvl and it is not inconcievable that a 120 soldier could eat my lunch on any given day if we fought toe to toe. THATS NOT FAIR. Hehe.. If I am dumb enough to go blow for blow with an enforcer or soldier I deserve to die. If you are dumb enough to CHASE a fixer then you deserve to be extremely frustrated trying to catch her! Run the other way while you can (Doh!). Wait until they fix our snare. Your whole team will be dropped to a crawl with something like -1000 run. You will then be rooted one by one and then taken out. God help you if your team has no ranged weapon capabilities or enforcers to keep the Fixer at bay. It is all about synergy. Some professions are good at defending against one thing others are not. I think Funcoms implementation of this in the light of nano resist being bugged or not working is rather rude but it is here and it is not fair to everyone. Adjust and go on. Life isnt fair. It never will be. Who is to say this game should be fair??? If I had a chance to go back and change my profession I would not be a Fixer again.
