Dabblez
Trust me, I'm a scientist
For me the key thing is not to make assumptions on how or when other people should roleplay. We all have very different views on what consitutes roleplaying. Not all these views are compatible, but as long as we treat each other with respect there is no reason why can't all enjoy ourselves.

Personally I think PvP goes very much against the spirit of roleplaying. For me the aim of roleplaying is not to simulate reality, but simulate fiction, and usually a cheesey, popular, escapist type of fiction. Normally in this kind of fiction its the "good guys" (or at least the more "likeable guys") that win in the end, often against all odds.

PvP is more like a sport. It has no concept of what makes a good story or who the good guys are; its just about power. If the "might makes right" approach of PvP were to be applied to escapist stories like 'Star Wars' or 'Lord of the Rings', would have been very short indeed.

Of course other people will tell you that PvP is the only tangible expression of roleplaying we have, everything else is just make believe. And that's okay too, well until I get ganked crossing a politcal zone, but nevermind that,

As for Omni Pol, the thing you need to remember is that its an old, old guild. It dates back to a time when Funcon was advertising AO as a game in which players would be running Rubi-Ka and directly affecting the official storyline. As Funcom did not live up to its part of the bargain, its kind of harsh to expect Omni-Pol live up to theirs.
