New Zones, please
As I see it BS and 20K are totally overloaded. Could U guys at Funcom please put up 2 new zones equal to PM and BS? With Whompas and Grid and all. If we could splitt the amount of players on those playfields, maybe we could bring some more fun into missioning again.

For me it's like this at the moment:

Go to BS to find a team. 1 LD between entering BS and finding a team. Takes about 30 mins in total.

Someone in the team has a mission already and gives out the key. Untill everybody is in mission and the team is joined up again 1-2 persons have have multiple LD or LD-loops. Takes at least 1 hour in average.

If some one dies in mission it takes about 20 mins for him/her to come back due to lag/LD.

Mission itself takes 1-2 hours - if you can keep on to your team. Most of the time someone leaves about halfway through the mission and U have to replace him. Takes some more 30 mins.

As a total, I have seldom had 2 missions on the same day in BS with the same team. We have more downtime due to lag than to anything else.

Please Funcom, please. Put up a similar Zome so we can reduce the load on BS....
