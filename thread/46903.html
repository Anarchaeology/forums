Guild tax, lets make it useful.
now those of you how are guilded and have used the guild tax feature. and who arent banned. how useful is it?

answer: not very.

in a guild with high and low members, setting a moderate-high tax means that the lower members will never have any cash, while the higher players dont notice it. a lump sum comes out of your bank every so often, and finding a good figure is impossible.

i propose that the guild tax takes a % of your total cash, so everyone is paying what they can afford, no more, no less. and more money is flowing in to the guild coffers from the higher level players.
