Nope, I definitely think they care it is just that we don't see the bigger picture, e.g. if 60% of the people logged on are playing in Zone Y vs. the 2% of TL6ers in Zone Z then that is a big incentive to tackle Zone Y first.

On top of that you have all us old-timers who stuck with AO even after 12.6. Chances are that those same people will weather the storm. And the longer a change takes, the more _any_ change will seem an improvement and keep us going that bit longer.

Then you have 'quick hits'. Changing some dialogue is a lot easier than balancing faction and SK gains but can make a big improvement to those players affected by it, so a change is made as it requires very little resource even though it is not high priority (which of course upsets some of us players).

So.. there is a very tricky risk analysis and prioritising game going on right now, it is just unfortunate that some of us are already reaching/have exceeded our tolerance threshold.

Upshot is, never think for a moment they don't care. This is their livelihood, and I imagine they take a lot of pride in the work they have produced, they are probably more frustrated/depressed about the bugs than we are.

Oh, and they have almost certainly worked a damn sight harder than most normal 9-5ers of late.
