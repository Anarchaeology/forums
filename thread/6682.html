I quit playing shortly after the game went live. Returned to AC and have been enjoying it. Lately patches werent very interesting so decided to try out AO for a free week.

First thing I noticed. Most of my site links from beta and early live were either dead or hadnt been updated since the last time I played. Second thing, message boards slow, real slow, very bad sign of player numbers and overall interest. I got nervous about even messing with it after seeing what the community was looking like.

Well after a couple days, I can honestly say its a lot better than it was. Graphics running great, missions fixed for less forced grouping, and overall more stable. But the disconnects are still there, or back if they ever were fixed, which is really annoying. Far to frustrating to spend money on at this point. Will return to AC for next patch, but plan on watching AO boards closely for signs of improvement.
