Something Other Than Pet Pathing
This is long. Skip to the end for a short explanation.

There is another issue involving pet classes that I am not seeing addressed as much as pet pathing. I've seen it mentioned, like in a post above this (although very short mention), but the deluge of pet patching complaints has swept them under the rug, in my opinion.

We all know pet pathing can be horrid. Now, then...

The issue I'd like to see addressed as soon as possible is with pet aggro on pet classes. To explain the aggro I mean, allow me to cite an example:

Lush Fields, NW Mine. Two Bio's about 20 feet apart. If I with, say, my MP, walk over and stand next to Bio #1.. then have my attack pet go attack Bio #2.. what does Bio #1 do most of the time?

If you play a pet class you said: He attacks the MP, not the pet. Congratulations, you win a Kudos bar.

Why? If two complete strangers stand next to the Bio's and one attacks one.. the Bio will go over and help. It will not attack the complete stranger who is not in the team of, nor even friends with, the other person.

--------------------

I am NOT saying that pet classes should be immune to aggro from monsters they have their pet attack. However. The pet classes are not, in general terms, tanks. Some people go extremes, such as making Atrox MP's and the like, but the vast majority of MP and Eng's do not, typically, have the ability to tank the mobs their pets can fight (even not OE'd!) for very long at ALL. I believe this was on purpose. I believe that the Engineer and the MP were never designed to be combat-effective. Their pets were. They were designed to provide other uses to themselves, their pets, and AO society at large.. not to be an Enforcer alongside their pet. Which is how it should be, but a problem occurs when the game seems inclined... nay... downright obsessed with aggroing mobs on the player, and not their pet.

I propose that any and all code in your aggro system pertaining to considering the pet and his master being the same person being removed. I propose instead that if the pet class owner, fighting mobs that do not aggro on their own, has his pet kill some... they will under only TWO circumstances attack the pet master:

1) the pet dies. A simple check would be 'ok, pet died.. did it have a master? Yes? what was attacking the pet? Ok, all those things now hate the master'.

2) the pet has more than 'x' number of mobs on it at any time. I would suggest a percentage thing.. the more weak the mobs to the pet, the more it could take.. the stronger, the less. Once it passes a certain point, each additional mob means that all mobs have an 'x' percent chance to aggro on pet master instead of pet next aggro cycle.

Now, I can already hear some of the complaints... let me cut some of them off here:

The pet classes are not tanks.
The pet classes, even the Eng with their pretty good AC buffs, will die fast and hard to things their pets can stand.
... Even if their pet is the same level as them, later on.
The pet classes do not have nearly the damage potential of the more 'tankish' classes by themselves, and even their pets are not typically the highest damage dealers in a party.
The OE patch is going to nerf their ability to have pets WAY over their level.
The people with pets WAY over their level still could die if things their own level got past the bot for too long... it was just that the bot was so powerful it often was able to pull it back on itself or kill it in time... this will no longer happen.
The pet classes were not designed to BE tanks, or damage-dealers as themselves. I cite the fact that Engineers seem geared for tradeskills, and MP's seem geared for buff/debuffing.
Their pet is supposed to be the tank.
Their pet is supposed to be the damage dealer and damage taker.
The pet is supposed to pull aggro (I cite for example the pet trimmers for Engineers to increase pet aggro... an obvious example that it is recognized the pet is supposed to be the tank in a party consisting of a pet and his master).

I personally believe that pathfinding is already an 'addressing' issue. I have seen it get loads better over time, and Cosmik's post above reinforces that belief. I do not however see any mention in his posts anywhere that they're looking into this aggro issue.

---------------------------

To put it simply: I am tired of having my pet run into a room and attack one of the two things in the room, only to have the second mob turn and run down 2 corridors, through a conference room, around a hallway, down a long passageway, through 2 crossways and finally into the main area of the mission... to attack the pet owner, me, who was innocently sipping his coffee. That is so fundamentally wrong its disgusting (and on another level, too... lets see one of our PETS manage to pull off that feat? ). YES, we were meant to be able to sit around while our pets do the fighting, IT'S the way we seem to have been designed! Either make it more feasible for us to become tanks and fight alongside our pets, or fix it so our pets can do what you seem to have thought they were meant to do!

Thank you.
