Takes a LOT of credits to powerlevel a character. You also need a LOT of gear. You need all your nanocrystals so you can upload them ASAP.

AND ... you need a high level to follow you around constantly to keep you out of trouble and finish off monsters once you get them half dead.

Powerleveling is not easy and comes at the expense of credits and the time of a 120+ character who is willing to sacrifice his own time to follow you around and keep you safe and finish off your kills.

As far as leveling yourself as fast as possible, make sure you are a wanted profession (MA, Enforcer, etc.) that can always find a team. Then ALWAYS camp the mission terminals for items that you need and get three missions in the range that people want to do at your level. ALWAYS have missions ready for any group to do. Even if you can't use the items now, you will in a few days to a week.

Learn to be a bastard. Leave groups that are wasting your time. Push the groups into action. Time spent wasted for 'pee breaks', etc. takes away from your leveling time.

Personally, I think it's a waste to push for level after level for a few reasons. If it's your first character, then you are seriously missing out on experiencing the planet. Some level 100's have only been to 4 playfields. That's just not right.

Second, powerlevelled characters usually suck in that they don't know how to play the profession. I've seen powerleveled NT's that don't know they can Calm or powerleveled flavour-of-the-month Crats who know nothing about the Agg/Def speeches or proper Calming technique.

Your first character should really take the time to learn the game and learn the world. After that, I agree it's ok to take shortcuts to the upper levels with subsequent characters.

Big Mistake
Power Levelling to 100+ is a big mistake. If you are bored at level 18 then you are going to be bored sick at level 150.

Sure I want to get there too but the best zone to be in, IMO, is the 50-100 zone (actually probably 50-85). You are just starting to get some really cool progs, money is still a problem but one that can be solved by intelligent missioning, team hunting is great, there are more characters at that level and more hunting grounds. You are high enough to explore more of RubiKa but not so high as to have nowhere to hunt (or minimal places to hunt) therefore the world is both open but dangerous. If you want you can actually save up for a yalm. Sure it will take time but at level 70 or so missions start to yield 150-200K credits. Do enough missions and you will be ready to buy that Yalm. Or, be an MP and work up to Quantum Wings which allows you to travel much like a Yalm. Then the world really opens up. Then you can go exploring those areas that are not accessible by Whompa and Grid without having to run, dodging trees, for 30 minutes or more. Now that I have QW I am taking missions in areas I don't have a map for. I just keep track of directions and coordinates for important places (Whompas, Grids, crossings). I am a fairly conservative player so if you are wanting excitement start exploring early. A couple of early encounters with Super Deep Red's and quick trips to reclaim blunted much of my earlier enthusiasm. Now though I can do the exploring, look forward to leveling, plan my developement. I have to really consider IP placement, work for those extra NCU slots, plan my implants with care. This is what the challenge of AO is. 50-100 is the Bomb (as my kids would say).

My thoughts.
