You'll have to excuse my long post, but I am leaving the game in a week or so (due to financial reasons) and I have a lot of built up stuff I wanted to say )

That is a rather bold statement to make IMO. I have met more new people in the last few months than I had 6 months ago creating my first alt. People are still coming to the game, and it seems a lot of the sources for advertising (game magazines etc) are starting to talk about AO again, which will generate even more interest. Aside from the many funcom flames, mostly from people who have been here a long time, and who I see in a position to not even come in contact with new people, Good things have happened in AO (and some bad things, I'm not going to lie) and word has gotten out.

Is SWG going to be a great game, and steal a lot of the population of AO. Quite possibly, but for every star wars fan out there, I know there are just as many who would rather not have anything to do with it. Some of those people are in game now, and I am sure, as the game imporves and gets even more talked about, more of those people will come.

I find AO a extreemly fun game, until you get to lvl 110 or 120, and it wasn't just because of the easier ability to lvl before then, but becuase most people at those pre 110 lvl's are actually doing things, like exploring, learning about the environment, meeting the story chars, hunting.....after 110, I didn't find it was so much a lack of these things to do, but a lack of people who wanted to join you in doing them. The biggest enemy in AO IMO is a lvl mill.

I see everyone complaining now about this new patch. Some of it is valid. I agree the reflect bracers should not have been single wrist, and from what I hear tara is a pain in the butt, but there were some fun things added. The biggest complaint I hear is about the XP, and I believe that is a direct result of the post 100 lvl mill that everyone seems to fall into. No one wants to do it, because everyone wants to lvl. But that doesn't mean these new camps can't be fun, or that the new content is a waste and everyone should pack up and go to the next big game. Maybe its just that people want to get to 200 so they can say "hey look at me I'm uber".

Which is fine. I've been playing AC2 beta, and people who only care about lvl's and reaching the end of something may have a really good time there. I played the neocron beta, and those who would rather play a shooter will probably have a lot of fun there. I played earth and beyond, and for those who would rather solo a MMO game, well...here is your chance.

Every few months we hear about the "next big thing" that will kill the other MMORPG's, and yet they continue to survive. So saying that the playerbase is essentially done, or that everyone will leave when a certain game comes out, it doesn't hold ground....not yet anyway. Only time will tell.

seeing as this is the suggestion forum, I think I'll add a suggestion. More content between 100-120, other than static dungeons and team misson loot (not sure if there is much now after patch. It has been to soon to discover everything). I think this will slow down the desire to power lvl and keep people interested in the game and not thier lvl. And I am in no way saying we should not have post 150 content, I just think it is the lvl 100 hump that starts the ball rolling on the lvl mill.

Anyway, others will disagree, but this is taken from my experience in the game. I'm on my 4th char, and the first one I lvl'd to 139. I am still having a ball in game..(role play, exploring, hunting, missions...yup all that). I havn;t lvl'd in almost a month, well because there has been a lot to do. I think what is going to make and break AO in the long run is not what new game is coming out (as I try to stop ranting and get back to the topic) but what perspective the players who come and stay are playing from.

And I have no idea if that made sense, and I expect anytime now to have my post ripped to shreds. lol. Like I said, this is only coming from my perspective, but I am sure there are others who share it....I meet them everyday. Whatever game everyone ends up going to or staying with, I honestly hope they chose one where they have fun.

nuff said. I want to play not type, i got 9 days left. Hope to come back though...I'll miss it (not the boards though...they drive me nuts :P)

My prediction of the market stabilizing is because of the fact that SWG is the first MMORPG with real potential to be a Crossover, a game that brings the license fans into the MMORPG genre. License Fans will not react kindly to the License obeying Game Mechanics (all you PvP fans take note: how do you balance a class like the Jedi in PvP when by definition they are UBER?).

And then there's SOE/Verant to sort just those issues out, with a batting average of about .800 with the Ban Bat

The only thing that kills these games are profit analysis and investor panic.
