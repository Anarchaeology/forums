Enno, here's another thing I noticed testing this earlier tonight. In some instances both fist and mele weapons will work but fists will only strike about 1 out of every 10-15 hits. I was testing this with a level 90 MA on test against level 1-10 mobs in Harry's and in a 10% (that's ten percent) mission where every mob is gray to me. Buffing and debuffing, even zoning seemed to not make any difference in this. I could start a fight with no buffs up, be hitting fine, buff myself a moment later, continue hitting fine on the next fight and on the third fight suddenly my weapon is hitting consistently and my fists aren't even coming up as often as my brawls. But then, on the very next fight it seemed to be working again.

Steps I took to try to fix it...

1. Unequip and reequip the weapon (worked about 80% of the time)

2. Switch weapon to opposite hand (worked about 50% of the time)

3. Buff/debuff (no change)

4. Zone (no change)

This was using a level 15 crescent moon (sorry, never put any points into mele weapons) against leets, blubags, rollerrats and all types of humanoid NPC's. None of the mobs seemed more prone to cause this effect than any others.

Hope this info is of some benefit.
