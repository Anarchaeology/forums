Attn ADV Professionals
Given the data in this post by Jayde and Borrace (who rock) and others (who equally rock) one glaring problem shows up. Why is it that its possible to do LESS damage with TWO guns then you could possibly do with ONE gun? I would think that as long as a person has ANY skill in using two weapons that their ability to do more damage is a garauntee. This should be true even if I use a QL 200 BBI with a QL 2 BBI. Granted the added damage would be piddle, but it would still be added damage. I may not be completely on top of things like Jayde and Borrace with MR, but it would seem that one of the key issues with the dual 200 config is that that is the optimal damage output, and if you use an offhand gun of signifcantly lower QL you're actually gimping yourself. This is innane!

It would seem that funcom could fix this by going one of two routes, the hard way or the easy way.

The HARD way would be to rework the mechanics of MR such that dual weilding a QL 200 and a QL 2 would generate higher damage then a singly weilded QL 200 (I think this is the BEST solution, but least likely to happen). Frankly I don't know why each gun isn't tracked seperately for recharge and to hit etc. So both guns shoot and recharge at the same way, since the MR requirements are so outa whack, you're seldom going to be equiping two guns of the same type at the high end so you're not going to be doubling your potential damage with the high MR requirements.

The EASY way would be to significantly reduce the MR requirements on guns so that they could be dual weilded with a more comparable QL.
