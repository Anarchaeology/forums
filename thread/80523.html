"The night the moon fell"
"The night the moon fell"
by Randa "Poetica" Whites

Twilight is gone
The night is here
Darkness
I feel it surround me
Yet i'm not alone
The moon is here
Watching
Watching over me
Giving me comfort
So i walk in the night
In solitude
But never alone
Because the moon is here
I'm thinking of the past
When darkness scared me
When darkness made me vunerable
I don't feel it anymore
Because the moon is here
Watching
Keeping me safe
Showing me the right tracks
All night long
But what if one night, the moon was to fall?
Shrouded in darkness
Dressed only in the nights robe
Darkness all over
Nothing to comfort me
Darkness
Emptyness
Solitude
This is what happened the night the moon fell
My heart is dark
Invaded by the night
Will the moon ever rise again?
Will the dawn ever come?
Waiting
Watching the skies
Waiting...
