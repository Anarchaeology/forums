Implementing PvP capture and hold of outposts
On the subject of PvP 'capture and hold'....

OK, pillaging a few ideas from other posts, adding some of my own, and mixing...

Such a mechanism should work something like the following.

Each capturable outpost should display a flag or banner showing who currently holds it.

In each outpost are several doors. These doors lead into an 'outer zone' which is a pre-designed indoor mission-like area constructed in a maze-like fashion. The point of the 'outer zone' is to provide opportunities for offence and defence and to hold the elevators to the 'inner zone'.

These elevators (and there need to be several) provide access to a large boss-room like area, possibly with some side rooms. In this area is a terminal. Activate the terminal and a timer starts. If this timer expires, then the outpost switches to whichever side last operated the terminal. If someone from the controlling side can activate the terminal before the timer expires, then control will be retained. The terminal room should be arranged such that you need to get close to use it.

The outposts should be level restricted. That is they should have a specific level range they are intended for, and there should be no means of access to someone outwith the level range. This should be easy enough for doors, but with certain warp nanos, there may be problems. It would need code added to any nano that could warp someone into the area to check what level they were and prevent the function of the nano if the target is outwith the level range. If you level over the maximum whilst inside the outpost, you will not be kicked, but, if you log, then on logging back in, you will be placed outside, just as you are for expired missions.

There should be a number of sided guards inside the outpost 'outer zone', maybe on 10 minute spawn time, just to keep things interesting.

Each outpost would also 'control' an area round about it, and affect the 'side' of any terminals, NPCs etc in that area. This should not include insurance terminals, to prevent killed players returning too quickly (without aid anyway...)

These outposts should be in disputed areas, and would provide a more structured form of PvP than the current free-for-all griefer friendly system.

These outpost don't need to be in PvP areas themselves (though the highest level ones should probably be), but should be in areas whose control isn't clear (and I don't mean Neutral areas).

Something like this would make PvP a much more significant exercise, and I suspect increase the games's fun factor quite a bit
