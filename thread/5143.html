I play on RK2. Despite FC not giving a rats arse about RK2 the server has some of the nicest most helpfull players you will meet. Although dont try begging that will just get you stuck on ignore. The population on RK2 is a lot lot smaller but on the other hand its the sort of place where you can easily stand out from the crowd. Post level 100 (or there abouts) most people (at least on your side i.e clan or omni) will know you or know of you. The Majority of higher level players work together a heck of a lot and tend to bond in groups out with their individual clans, more than I have seen on RK1 at least.

It can be hard getting a group at times and because of FC neglect and over streaching themselves there isint much in the way of support by them. No events and massive waits for a GM/ARK. The player events are often good but rarely get reported on the web sites. Again because FC treat RK2 like their Ginger haired step child.

Its still well worth playing on RK2 though and the more people that come the more likely things are to change. Many of the "in game firsts" happen on RK2 first. Like Nulity Sphere being found/made and such like.
