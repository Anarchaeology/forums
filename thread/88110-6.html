The lengths of the fights can have make a huge difference on the effectiveness of a weapon. Plus toss in the randomness of random numbers things all get wonky.

I was playing around with Jayde's damage simulator. Funny thing I noticed when I compared the QL200 Enhanced Queen Blade to the QL200 Edge of Tarasque.

In a 30 second fight, the EQB beats the EoT marginally in damage (though Jayde's calculator can't taking in account the EoT's Bite of Tarasque's effects on the fight). But extend the fight out to 5 minutes and the they reverse themselves with the EoT performing better (almost 17k more damage better). Guess we should be glad it's near impossible for enforcers to use a QL 200 EoT.

The Manex is good but it's not spectacular. And the length of the fight may be skewing people's perceptions when comparing a Manex's damage to their own weapons. The weapon they may be using could be better suited for a longer fight.
