Sexual reproduction requires genetically compatible gametes. The female gametes have all the cellular stuff as well the haploid chromosomes, whereas the male gametes pretty much just have the haploid chomosomes. That is. you get all the cellular components - and thus life as we know it - from your mom.

So, what defines male and female are the ability to produce gametes of the right sort. If Atroxes were not able to produce any gametes, then they would not be males, independently of whatever other male attributes they may have.

That would be one way to explain all the male attribute that Atroxes have, and still be able to classify them as neuter.
