Bloodlines
My origin has been a mystery to me for as long as I can remember, growing up in the slums as a non person. Recently a man has come forth as my father, one Garret Silvestrini, known lovingly as "boney". I have searched for my father and mother for a long time, even falling with a bad crowd and learning a life of crime. It is hard to believe that my father can be none other than Mr. Silvestrini. Fate is laughing if my father is a man that would likely see me dead. I need proof, I am calling on all the doctors out there to help us resolve this..I will participate in any tests needed, I must know!
