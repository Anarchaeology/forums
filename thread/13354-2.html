Just a little suggestion some of you might like (I'll divide this post properly since it is very long)

The Explanation

Have you ever played an RPG named Arcanum?

Well, in this game, each use of a skill (for instance, attacking with a melee weapon or lockpicking) can be met with one of the following results:
a) critical sucess
b) sucess
c) faliure
d) critical faliure

Now, how does this work. The higher your skills, the bigger the % chance a sucess or critical sucess, and the lower the chance of a faliure or critical faliure, as could be easily guessed. When a critical is achieved (sucess or faliure) the ame draws from a list of possible side effects, while regular sucess and faliure have no "extra" bonuses or penalties. 2 examples:

Melee hit board:
a) Critical sucess - Hit with melee weapon, plus one of the following effects:
-50% extra damage
-100% extra damage
-50% extra damage and cripple a limb (limb hit)
-100% extra damage and blind (head/eye hit)

b) Sucess - Hit with melee weapon for regular damage

c) Faliure - Miss attack

d) Critical faliure - Miss attack, plus one of the following effects:
-Normal weapon damage to self
-Normal weapon damage to self plus scar (reduces beauty)
-Critical to self (draw critical effects from critical sucess board)
-Drop weapon plus one of the above effects

Handling of locks and lockpicking

Ok, ok, now what does this apply to this whole topic? Bear with me

Here is the critical board for lockpick skill:

a) Critical sucess - Lock is picked always silently

b) Sucess - Lock is picked, maybe silently, maybe not

c) Faliure - Lock is not picked

d) Critical Faliure - Lock is not picked, lock is instead JAMMED, which means it cant be opened ever.

The point of this post

Here is the point of this long post, critical faliures at lockpicking.
In other words, if one tries to open a chest with low B&E skill, he incurs a chance of permanently jamming the lock, never to be opened again. This can be easily handled by implementing a sliding scale of sorts, like:

Lock difficulty is 200 (which means it is meant to be opened with 200 B&E)

Base chance to jam the lock is 1%

If your B&E skill is 200 or more, chance to jam the lock is 1% (base chance)(on each attempt)

If your B&E skill is 100, chance to jam the lock 50% (on each attempt)

If your B6E skill is 50, chance to jam the lock is 75% (on each attempt)

This could solve your button mashing problems

And once again, pardon the long winded post, hope you liked it

Here's how to make everyone happy:

When you pick a lock (regardless of success or failure) you get feedback in the chat window. Why not just make it so when you pick a lock you get these possibilities:


(1) You fail to pick the lock, try again? (alt-space or whatever keystroke trys again)
a. affirmative yields 3 possibilities: 1 retry 2 success 3 goto (2)
b. negative doesnt do anything as it is the default.

(2) The lock is now jammed. Attempt to force it?
a. affirmative yeilds 3 possibilities: 1 retry 2 success 3 "you cannot force this lock" The lock is now frozen forever.

Forcing a lock is a PvM damage hit. You can use whatever special you want, or whatever nano. So if you want to use brawl, just target the chest and hit brawl, or F/A or whatever. On each failure to force a lock there is a 90% chance of freezing it. Lock would have HP based on difficulty.

The idea being the best WTG is B&E. This would make it so there is just one use of the lockpick.
