.....................There are good crats and bad crats like in any profession. But, I think unlike other profs, there are certain ways a good crat always stands out beyond the color of his bot, armor or weps.

Rules of the Good Crat:

Rule #1
Never tell people to cancel reflects/shields or take off bracers. In fact I usually insist the Soldier or Engie buff everyone else with reflects. Why? See Rule #2

Rule #2
Always run ahead of the team and mez all potential adds. Thus there is never a problem with reflects since you make yourself the target. That includes going up a lift alone. If your team is hitting a mob, put the bot on it and then run around to mez. This means you need to work on AC, health and evades. You're not an MP or Engie who needs to spend all their IP on the best pet possible.

P.S. As soon as you are done mezzing run back to team to refresh their speech aura.

Rule #3
Chain mez mobs when they are beating up the Doc or Agent. Mez is 100% effective in wiping the hatelist.

Basically, you have to be very proactive, lead a team, take tons of damage (but not tank). You get to shape the battlefield for your team. Unlike NT's or Traders, you have a bot that can contribute to killing mob while you run around and mez.

Playing a crat like an MP or Engie is bad..............

The majority of these people that say crats rock with our charms and fears have probablly never tried to use them. There is a glass ceiling at the title caps that are distinctly pronounced before and after level 100 because they last so long. (83-99) (126-149) Psychic checks kill the abilities off into uselessness.

Imagine a reverse level requirement. Mob must be less than level 120 and that is basically what the psychic checks do. Problem is, the player is level 130 and have no way of getting the next nano because of titlecaps.

Mez, that is all we are really good at. Our droid is strictly third rate and our nukes are nice, but limited to melee/energy damage hitting for min damage often. With unmezzable, uncharmable mobs it chips away at the last few things that the crat is good at. And at high level team missions, mezing has no real use with mobs dropping dead every 10-20 seconds.

So yeah, crats rock if we play like a soldier/agent with mez and completely ignore our pets and charms. But when even the mez fails with the addition of all new mobs being unmezzable we are relegated to just wonder what the heck we can do.

The crat profession is bruised and have recieved internal bleeding from each successive nerf/ignore. Slapping a band-aid on it and overlooking the internal injuries do not necessarily mean we rock. Though in the meanwhile, many of the rocking crats are getting by just fine by equipping like second rate soldiers and agents to dish out damage since that is all that matters.
