A few days ago I decided to grid to Omni-1 Trade to do my banking, but I found out that the bank terminal near the grid access had recently been removed, so I asked myself "what am I to do? I always do my banking here in the trade district". I was intent on finding another bank terminal even though I'd never been more than 15 feet away from the grid access, so I set out on the streets of this gigantic maze of highrises they call Omni-1 Trade. Hour after hour went by as I was trying to find a banking terminal amongst the countless buildings and the endless masses of pedestrians, I thought my search would never end. Then suddenly I was swept away with the crowd and ended up in some sort of backyard, I looked around and to my amazement I saw what I had been looking for............ "a bank terminal" I shouted. I rushed over to terminal and started the opening sequence "Retinal scan comeplete........warping items" the terminal said, I grabed the stuff I needed and walked towards the backyard exit........ then a strange black cloud came and shrouded my vision and moments later.......BAAAM!!! someone or something hit me from behind, I was knocked out cold. I was dazed and confused when I awoke from my deep slumber, I didn't know the time or how long I had been unconcious "They stole everything" I cried. I had no idea how to get back to the grid access or whompah due to the size and mazelike structure of this city. So I contacted The Advisors of Rubi-Ka and told them about my problem, and after an hour of waiting for an answer they told me "We can't do anything about your missing items (hehe), but we can warp you out of Omni-1 Trade".

Don't try finding other banks in Omni-1 Trade, I learned my lessen the hard way.

Please FC give us back the bank terminal in the middle of trade, I don't want this to happen to anyone else.
