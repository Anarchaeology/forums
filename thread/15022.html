Just a simple opinion about high lvl Engineers.
I have spent 22 days in hours getting my engineer to lvl 115, and from this point it is easy to see what engineers are all about. I have looked at viral.clansupport.com, and at all the nanos, and high lvl Engineers get nothing cool. This is basically what Engineer's get.

Bot which can be 80 lvls higher with outside buffs.
Trimmers which enhance bots slightly.
Nice healing nanos for pet.
AC buffs.
Some weak reflect buffs
team member warp nanos

There you have it folks. That's it. ya there's a slayerdroid transference at high lvls, but it's not worth mentioning. We don't have roots, we don't have calms or self heals, and we have a bunch of used up IPs. I liked lvling my Engineer because I didn't go wrong with IPs, and it is prety streight forward. I don't have the IPs to have a gun, and I don't want one. Aren't Engi's supposed to let their pets do the fighting?

And when it comes to PVP:
1. Root Engi pet. There will be little if no resistance.
2. Kill Engi. don't be afraid. without their pets they don't bite. BTW, their pets do bad damage usually, so u uber twinkers don't be afraid.

It's just too simple. Personally, I would like calms because I don't care about pvp, and it would make me much safer and I wouldn't have to kill all the mobs in missions. That would be sooo nice. anyways. that's it. thanks.
