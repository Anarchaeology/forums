Graphic Content : Still Looks Beta
When I first played AO in october, I was in awe at the beauty that i found in the artwork thoughout the game. Now that i've played all this time, I've gotten a little sick of the lack of attention givin to improving things like character personality and diversity. Maybe Shadowlands has more to offer but seriously, im not keeping my fingers crossed. I'm refering to the art in the game.

I know there are many other pressing problems in the game like camping, playfield design, and general bugs, But i think that the game could be much more graphically interesting. Thus I begin my critique.

1. I'm an hardened lvl 100 enforcer, but i still swing my beam like a lvl 10.

Im refering to the animation here. Its sad that there cannot be more content here. Currently, a melee style fighter with Brawl, Dimach, and Fast Attack has a grand total of......6 different swing-da-beam animations. They never change throughout the game. NOW, I would think that a lvl 100 beam weilder would use the beam with more style and technique then a lvl 10 lowbie. It would be cool to see this in animation form. Also, if land a Critical hit, I wouldn't be able to tell unless I look at the chat box. I would like to see more dynamic animation content that would be dependant on more things such as, level, agro/def slider (I would be in a defence stance, for instance if my slider was at def), and criticals. I'm sure theres even more ways you could mix up the variety of attack animations. You should also play different Hit animations depending on the relative damage and when someone gets killed by a crit, make them die in a more explosive way. Would also like to address the Repost, which is obviously just stand in content. These things need to be polished up guys. One of my biggest beefs is that I have to watch the (and colored HP lines) to see how the battle is really going. It would also be cool If the classes had a special move of some kind. Go look at a 3d fighting game and pick up some ideas. These at best 6 attacks animations (yeah MA's have more, but its still a pretty half ass-effort on the animation part especially when you compare to /emotes) for the entire game. Lame, Lazy. Mix it up, make the battles a more dynamic combination of animations and more interesting to watch.

2. The only diffence between a ql 10 and ql 180 attack nano is the little number in the chat box.

Seriously, NT's can't be left out of the boredum can they. They have a vast array of attack nano's but limited to just a few nifty animations. In my opinion, all the attack nano animation look very first-in-the-nano-lineish. For example, I drop a low ql AoE nuke in a room full of mobs. I see a little transparent tarus animation that hits all the mobs for a little bit of dammage. then I walk in the same room and drop a 189 AoE, same animation, maybe a different color, with more damage. What does an NT really have to look forward too besides damage. Now think if i dropped the same nano, but instead there was a blinding flash, defening noise, and the ground shook (like when the clon**** cannons went off). All could be topped off with a more complex model/partical, blackened walls, and maybe a lingering smoke.
The NT is just an example, the same lack of diversity is in all attack nano's. Should be addressed. You have lvl dependant animation instances for some healing nanos. would like to see more of it everywhere.

3. Pets, they all look the same.

Ok, pets are just not diverse enough. MP's get 4 different pet models, Engies get 4 different pet models, and Crats get 2. Simply not enough. Should be at least 3 times that many per proffesion. I would be even cooler if players had a choice on how their pets look. Like 3 different kinds of slayers and war bots. More diversity in the MP pets is just a must. Crats might be Ok because their pets are secondary but you cant have too much content or diversity.

4. This is a planet, there should be more varieties of mobs.

It's allways exciting when you see a mob that you've never seen before. especially when its uber. I think that FC should try and produce new mobs every couple of weeks to keep players killing new things (BTW this wont work unless you fix the playfields).

5. year 2002 hundreds of car models, year 29000 5, whoops make it 4 car models.

We used to be able to use a grand total of 5 cars. 1 of which no longer is possible to get and 3 are just utterly undesireable. Everyone either flys around and a static yalm model. Lets have some color and diversity here guys.

6. Look, when i sit down, I cant stick my beam through my stomach.

When a beam attrox sits down with a beam, it goes through the model. Same with riders. A female with tank armor and a nova has the nova partially inside the tank armor. Sloppy, Ugly.

7. My ass is really flabby.

Male models have this nasty jiggle butt effect when they run. I hate looking at it. Some people say they make female characters for this reason. Can you work on it? I want a firm ass.

8. The fighting scenes are kinda unrealistic

The script of a fight: Mob see's player and fight insues, Player raises weapon, mob raises weapon. We all wait patiently as the relatively boring fight animation plays. The mob looses all its HP. Mob lowers weapon, goes into a non combat stance, then a death animation plays. Sloppy and unrealistic. Needs to be more fluid.

9. The Cookie-Cutter Omni Attrox-Enforcer

Definately need more choices in the armor department. All respectable enfrocers use a beam, omni-elite, and primus coat, and are Attrox with some very small variations. I chose a solitus enforcer with a 2he just to be different. I actually get alot of compliments on looking unique. What we need is more usable clothing material. There seams to be 1 or 2 usable armor set for each race, then a few that seem pretty much designed not to be used. I've been called gay before for wearing nail armor pants.
ALSO!, just small detail, i would like to have seperate arm objects. I want to be able to equip just one of the new shoulder pads on one shoulder. I think they look ugly on both and i wont use them unless FC fixxed that little feature. This would allow u to see that u are using 2 different sleeve, if you wanted to.


WELL, thats about all that i have for now and im gonna stop cause this is long as hell. I invite your comments. im curious to know if FC has just laid off all its artist or if they are still playing ping pong and video games pretending to devolope new content. I hope the later is true.
