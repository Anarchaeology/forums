Un-Authorized TBA Strike
Hello Fellow Employees of the Omni-Tek Corporation:

I am Trent Reznoir Sotto, Acting Director of Public Relations for the TBH/TBA. This transmission is a response to the last article that Tisantous had transmitted to the Rubi-Ka Times about The Black Alliance going on strike. We as an organization loyal to Omni-Tek want to clear up this issue at hand. In no way, shape or form was Tisantous (former member and Directior of Public Relations for the TBA) ever given authorization to say that the TBA was going on strike, we have no intention to go on strike now or in the near future.

Tonight the TBA interguild talks were opened up and the TBA strike matter was quickly resolved between the TBH and DRAP and hope that other members of our alliance will participate next time so we can resolve any other problems this issue has caused. We also invite any other organization leaders within the Omni-Tek Corporation who has questions or issues to resolve with the TBA to participate in our next meeting with the assurance that we will try our best to help answer any questions and/or resolve any issues you may have with the Alliance.

We would also like to state for the record that Tisantous was also going around posing as an Official for both the TBH and TBA which he was not and that anything he has done was un-authorized by the TBH/TBA. He has since been handled accordingly and is no longer a member of the TBH/TBA.

We apologize for any inconvenience to all TBA members and associates affected by this issue and we would like to express our graditude to all the many people that stand with us in our cause.

If anyone has any questions on the matter at hand please feel free to contact me and I will try to answer all your questions.
