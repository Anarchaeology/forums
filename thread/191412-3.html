Re: The unofficial Quest bug list
Adonis
Sanctuary quest
Not exactly a bug, but if the right blueprint to get is the Infernal Demon one (which as others said, can't be completed now) for the Gilthar Sanctuary quest, it drops with a good rate from unredemeed mobs in the east camp (where notes for the garden quest drop too). The quest givers says to look for further hints about the diviners disappearence hunting "ghosts and spirits". As hunting spirits beside those around Adonis City is a bit of a pain solo (and noone is interested in going hunting those hitting for up to 4K regular damage in Suburbs), I wouldn't complain, but the hint is a bit misleading (I can't confirm for the reason above if it drops there or from the spirits in the north though, for the reasons above).

Glasses quest
Milliner ask you to get the Bionic Cooling Liquid from sharks as part of the protection glasses quest (those for Penumbra) - this part is not needed in reality. Since there's no dialogue entry to give him the other six items (which is already the max amount of items you can trade at a time to the NPC), and you have to trade those directly, the request of the bionic liquid is a bit misleading, as when you trade the item directly you don't know which 6 of the 7 items you have to trade. Remove the Bionic Cooling Liquid request in the informative dialogue or add an entry to the Milliner's dialogue tree with the cooling liquid as a pre requisite for handing the other six items.

Relieving Milliner's Pain: there are two ways to temporarily relieve Milliner's pain to make the diallogue goes on: handing him 100 credits (which I wonder how the first one who did it found out) - "Whimper" comment follows, or handing him "Soothing Herbs" which seems to really relieve his pain - beside dialoque and RPG considerations, there doesn't seem to be any difference in the two processes (and Milliner won't remember you gave him the erbs the next time you meet him). Opening trade and closing it clicking on "Accept" without adding items or cash work the same as giving cash (bug).
