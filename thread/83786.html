That low a level you should be able to get closer missions.

Secondly, in regards to MPVs, this is why they never will. There are two ways I see to implement them:

1)driver wears vehicle. Members of driver's team right click on car and are forced to do a /follow, and their avatar's vanish and gain flight abilities if it flies.

2)Driver wears vehicle. Teammates right click on it, and are brought to a special room. Driver goes to locale, removes vehicle, and team is dumped out wherever he's at.

OK why is this a problem?

Lag. SO OK we go route 1. we ALL know how well /follow works with lag. You'll lose people left and right. Through in the latest "warp" bugs, an we're realy screwed. Route 2, what happens if someone LDs in the room, where do they come back at? Do they get stuck in there and have to /term? Do they land wherever the driver is/was before he logged? If the zoning server goes wonky, do people get trapped in? Out? No, too much could go wrong.
