Implementing PvP capture and hold of outposts
OK, pillaging a few ideas from other posts, adding some of my own, and mixing...

Such a mechanism should work something like the following. Each capturable outpost should display a flag or banner showing who currently holds it.

In each outpost is a door. Inside this door is the outer zone, this is simply be a number of interconnected rooms which can be guarded to prevent access to the elevators to the inner zone. The inner zone is where it's at. If the only players in the inner zone are of a single side, then the outpost is held by that side (maybe have to hold the zone clear for a minimum time). This is rather like the static mission areas that can be found, except they have a layout designed for attack and defence, and have multiple elevators to a single 'boss-room' type area.

So, the aim is to keep at least one player in the inner zone on your side whilst using the outer zone to defend. There should be multiple elevators from the outer to inner zone, and the outer zone needs to be designed to offer opportunities for defence and for attack, choke points, multiple paths, etc.

Outposts could come in a variety of sizes, and can have level limits placed on the doors to enable a variety of levels to take part in the enterprise.

Each outpost would also 'control' an area round about it, and affect the 'side' of any terminals, NPCs etc in that area.

Any guards at an outpost should be in the outer zone and of toughness suitable to the level range of the outpost, they should also have a spawn time of at least 5 minutes, maybe more. There are still issues relating to PvP around the outpost (bringing in extra attackers or defenders), but that relates to how the PvP system works (or doesn't) currently. At higher levels, engineers would be used to bring in reinforcements anyway...

These outposts should be in disputed areas, and would provide a more structured form of PvP than the current free-for-all griefer friendly system.

However, in order to further intensify the nature of the clan vs omni conflict, more areas need to be PvP (25%) with the only non-PvP areas being neutral areas (75% outside cities, 100% inside) and all backyard areas. Whilst it is reasonably safe to wander many parts of Rubi-Ka regardless of side, the feeling of the 'conflict' is not manifest.

Of course, you'd have to provide all sides with similar content in terms of mission areas, hunting areas, boss camps, static dungeons etc. at all levels before you could really do this. And enable neutral players to initiate PvP, if only so they can defend their guards properly... Perhaps attacking a sided NPC should set a flag against that player for the duration of the combat and maybe a few minutes afterwards, during which any player of the NPCs side can initiate combat against the aggressor regardless of the gas level. This might alleviate having to make many zones 25%, as omni NPCs can attack clan PCs and hence open that clan PC up to attack by omni PCs, and the other way round of course.

Yes, there is death loop protection. The guards would only exist inside, so just like an expired mission, it should be possible to kick you out if your side doesn't match current control when you log back in...

I thought about guards outside, but that would cause too many problems. Simpler just to keep them within the 'mission' area as it were. It also enables them to be levelled at the same range as the outpost whilst protecting them from higher level players, since such players would be unable to enter.

The level restrictions would have to apply across the board and cover all types of entry including warp. And if you ding too high whilst inside, then you get to stay until you leave or are 'removed' at which point you can't return.

I'm sure there may be other issues too, but I can't think of them at the moment. If anyone else can think of anything, do post it...
