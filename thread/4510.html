Meta physicist Charecter Development. What do you think of these Ideas?
The Mp is the Nano priest so the mp needs more role-playing items. A soldier has a gun and armor does a priest really need that. I say make the priest even weaker but give him more supernatural tools.
· Conversion Nano-Ability to convert an opponent in PVP to their side. i.e. Iam in PVP and convert an Omni-tech to Clan. The Omni can go back to their prior government if they find a priest to convert them. The only problem with this would be in the area of tokens. And people finding a way to exploit it. However, the benefit to mps would be massive enjoyment in Role-playing.
· Followers- the follower’s purpose would be 3. They would serve as glorified backpacks. They would act as human shields and take a certain percentage of damage for the mp. And they would speak for mp if mp desired. (preach). “Are’nt pets followers?”….I don’t think so they are manifestations. A follower would not attack or heal. They would really only follow and assist the MP carry items spread his message and take a bullet for him. Here are two scenarios for MP’s being awarded a follower.
1. One Follower would be rewarded in place of hp at every status increase 15,30 and so on. A level 60 mp would then have 3 followers each only having a small amount of hp. If an mp would normally have been given 20 hp for leveling to 15 they would not get that and instead they would have a follower with 20hp.
2. Another scenario would be for an mp to only get 3 followers total. Awarded every 30 levels the first is a speaker only does speeches. The second a backpack and the 3rd a human shield with 100hp.
3. When a follower dies they are gone until mp dies and regenerates. If a follower dies and is carrying items they will be send to reclaim terminal. If a follower is the one that takes the bullet for the mp any damage to mp will be directed to this follower until that follower dies.
· Column of Fire Nano- Be able to call a column of fire from the sky onto an opponent this would take a massive amount of nano and hp. But would deliver a powerful punch. Only available for extremely High level mp’s like 170+
· Gift of Moses- this would produce small miracles; an mp would be able to produce a minimal amount of frogs, mice, locust. This would not cause damage it would just be for looks. Calling of lighting would be great too. Different levels of this nano the higher the nano level the more it produces. Lvl 15 gift of Moses produces 5 frogs’ lvl 60 produces 30.
· Sacrificial Touch- Ability to raise someone from the almost dead. If a teammate is on their way out and about to die the mp could cast this and almost all of the mps life would be transferred to the teammate. Another possiblity is to have this program give full life to entire team but kill mp. 3 level nano one available at 15,30, and 60,

The Mp is the Nano priest so the mp needs more role-playing items. A soldier has a gun and armor does a priest really need that. I say make the priest even weaker but give him more supernatural tools.


Please Post comments and other ideas
