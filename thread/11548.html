New Profession and Breed Template
Evolution Specialist/Evolutionist template is a breed that is based on evolution. It cannot wear any armor or weapons in the game, instead it spawns from its limbs and body weapons and armor. Its nano programmes are unique in that they have drain spells, regain spells and attack and armor spells. It had very few offensive spells but has strong defensive spells. Most of their spells can blind and poison for a along time. They make a very power ally and are great to have on your team. Their limbs can change into metal, ie his arm turn into a sword, while other arm turns into sheild. This is how it would work:

1-15 levels will go through his first evolution
15-45 levels will go through his second evolution
45-90 levels will be his third evolution
90-150 level his fourth evolution
150-200+ his last and final evolution

Whatever skills you have placed on him his body will mold and form those skills and sprout limbs and body armor how you wish.
