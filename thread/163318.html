It is done...
Reports... bad press... forming bad reputations...

Bad reputations... upser people... murderous intentions...

Target acquired... 3... 2... 1...

Lem looked up to the bot. Curiously she stepped forward before having shrapnel rip into her her. Piercing her brain, the shrapnel did not take long to induce upon her death.

Death... rebirth... reclaim...

Reclaim... imperfect... in-complete...

Lem shuddered looking at herself. Never before had she the need of the reclaim terminal... and nor did it seem to work properly with her still 'minor' status at the age of fifteen. The wounds had not fully healed... and in-part... her brain...

Who am I?

Confusingly and frighteningly she checked her com.

Scanning... Jacelyn "Ylemmae" Waldram. Age = 15. No detachment affiliations.

Jacelyn... Ylemmae... Waldram...

Scanning... complete. Found. Employee of Omni-Corp. Omni Inquisitor, Reporter. See articles: "Society of Traitors", "Call for Ross to Step Down? Traitor or Patriot? Two Voices Heard", "Another IS Employee found dead?".

Reporter... employee... omni...

Scanning... information found. Omni-Tek owns Rubi-Ka. Corporation run by Philip Ross, CEO. More details?

Lem looked around her. Reclaim booth?

Scanning... found. Reclaim booth. Death is not permanent on Rubi-Ka. See Technological advances in cloning and transferring of soul to new body.

Reclaim... death... rebirth...

Then why do I not remember?

Scanning... found. Technology yet perfected for those of a growing mind. Was out-lawed for some time for any below the age of twenty-one due to this but law over-turned and however is still highly suggested never to happen.

Technology... out-lawed... age...

Scanning... Jacelyn "Ylemmae" Waldram. Age = 15. No detachment affiliations.

Jacelyn... Waldram... 15...

Maybe it is just temporary...
