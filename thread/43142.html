secrets lost...
[prologue]

....for a moment he saw it all clearly, the reason why he kept coming back to this haunted place, then it vanished and left him puzzled, the images were gone and so was the memory. The young fixer took one last deep breath of fresh air, one last look at the forest around him, one last look at the sun, before he with forebooding feelings descended down the stairs..

It came to him in a late night vision, that much he remembered, how could he forget waking up with the sense that someone or something hade tampered with his fate, the feeling of not having any control anymore, later that summer he stumbled upon the shrine for the first time. The shrine was the best name he could imagine for the long lost ruins the lay beneath the sand several miles from Tir. The ancient main building was just barely visible from the surface, and took him several weeks and many hours of smuggling digging equipment well away from established trade routes, away from prying eyes. Noone would ever find out
about his finding. But it was all worth it when the entrance was cleared from the sand. He had never felt a larger feeling of accomplishment before. The damp and ancient air that
reached his face when the door first opened litteralty reaked of grandness and long lost treasures, but somewhere hidden beneath theese feelings was a sense of decay, death and
a secret that had not been lost, but hidden. Hidden away from the eyes of mankind for reasons unknown...

[secrets lost]

"wake up memphit for ****s sake!" Alyzz´s voice eccoed through the small appartment. Slowly the sleeping fixer came to life.
"what the hell were you doing last night?, you got yourself killed again right? surely from some stupid ****ing break-in"
The still lingering pain in his body made it pretty ****ing obvious that he was killed and reconstructed by a insurance term last night. "yeah well, uhmm..i cant really recall" he replied, the angry reply from his girlfriend, or atleast that was what he stilled called her, didnt take long. Not really paying attention to what she was saying, Memphit tried to remember what he was doing last night but to no avail, the images and memories were all blurred and fuzzy, not making any sense at all, still there was a feeling that something was terribly wrong. Then it dawned on him that today was the day, today he would break the last seal of his well kept secret. With his now uncontrollably feeling of that something was wrong, he arose from the bed. He had to get to the desert, he had to get there now. "Listen, i dont have time for this, i have an important customer waiting" they both knew that it was a lie, nevertheless it was much easier to let it slide than have another fight. "sure whatever" she replied when he disapeared through the door. The mechanical sound from the opening mechanism made it impossible for anyone to hear Alyzz´s last words "i love you".

The early morning sun was slowly rising above the horizon, in a few hours the streets of Tir would be bristling with activity. The desert city lay beatifully calm in the sun as Memphit ran down its streets. He had always loved upp'ing grid surfer and running, especially inte the late evenings and early morning. But today there was no sign of enjoyment on the fixers face, he was on a mission today, a mission that he was sure would make his life better on Rubi-Ka, maybe even so good that he and Alyzz could sort out their problems. As he ran through the west gate, a guard greeted him with a nod of his head. The fixer slowed down wondering, he didnt know the guard, actually he had never seen the man before, and the guards are not know to be overfirendly. The call of the desert came into memphits mind and he started running due north once again.

Every now and then he checked his navigation unit, the coordiantes kept rushing by at tremendous speed, he grinned. He remembered when he first realized how fast he could actually run with the help of his nanobots. The althoug painfull but still fun memory of when he ran through Meetmedere and actually missed the grid term. The smile faded when he recalled being hit by sniper fire trying to find that damn term he hade ran past.

blip...blip...blip blip blip... he was getting close. Slowly the large sand dune the he recognized all too well came into view. He slowed down. The small structure he had spent weeks uncovering lay before him. Triangular to the shape and riddled with strange cracks and signs the building looked somewhat puny as it lay in the morning sun. But wait, something was afoot here, there were numerous footprints outside the door, how could that be? Had someone else discovered the shrine?

The fixer sat in the sand pondering the situation. He tried to recall who could have seen him out here in the middle of nowhere, and the conclusion made him feel ill at ease... The only person that could possible have any idea of what he had found was Alyzz, the woman he still loved somewhere deep inside.
