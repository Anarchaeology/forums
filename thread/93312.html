When the transport ship brought me to Rubi-Ka, I was given a choice. Go Omni, Clan or Neutral. Not knowing much about either "side", I did not want to fall into the stereotypical trap of thinking Omni was "Evil", and the Clans were "Good". So I decided that the best course of action was to be Neutral.

The main reason for my neutrality is that, once I finally make a choice, I will not switch my affiliation, ever. Once, I'm Omni, I'm Omni forever. Likewise if I choose Clan. After four months on Rubi-ka, I'm still Neutral although my beliefs are clearly leaning in one direction. People call us "fence sitters", but I'd rather stand firm in my beliefs, then switch sides every week. Those are the people to watch out for, they are the ones who truly can't make up their mind. I, along with many other neutrals, choose to stand firm in our neutrality until we are ready to fight for one side. For some of us, that day may never come.
