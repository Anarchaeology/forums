For the record, Init debuffs work fine on mobs. Just be aware that there is a 'Breakable' line (which breaks after a few hits) and an 'Unbreakable' line. The debuffs with the larger -init penalty and lower -damage penalty are breakable. The debuffs with the larger -damage penalty will NOT break. The breakable one is handy for adds if you don't have crowd control. Just remember not to attack them prematurely . But... when I know that they've got the debuff on, the effect is fairly noticable.


Apart from that... I agree holeheartedly about Purge. Me want Purge! Me want Purge!
