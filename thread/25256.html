I feel your pain Funcom
Analogy:
I was DM for a long time with some buds playing D&D. The DM's Job is to make sure him and his players are having Fun. As DM, you need to make sure you have a balanced campaign to keep players challenged and Having Fun.

A few times I unbalanced my campaign by giving my players too many powerful items . I could try and balance my campaign by making monsters more and more powerful, or by removing some of those items.

When I did remove items, My players would feel "Nerfed" But I had to count on their maturity to understand it was in everyones best interest. Keeping everyone happy is tough (yes players in D&D compete against eachother, ask any DM ) , a DM has to see the bigger picture.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Funcom, it must be awesome to play DM to so many players, creating a world where so many people have fun.

Funcom, I feel your pain. I can't Imagine what it's like to have Hundreds of players screeming bloody murder anytime they see something they don't like, SPECIALLY since those players feel they have a Right to Bash and Abuse you since they are paying money.

To Everyone: People, this is a GAME. Have fun. Yes FC has screwed up in the past, present, and even future, but they are Human beings and I think you and I know they are doing their best to make things happen. Balancing a game, Any game, is always a painful process. We'll all just have to live with it, and I hope those that are scrambling to get to these forums to flame FC can step back and hopefully see that it's all in good faith.

So I fully support any Nerfing/Fixing that needs to be done, with the faith that it is done to balance and tweak the game further, and not to appease people that are being abussive/loud. Lets all make their job a little easier and have a clear head when we write on these forums.
