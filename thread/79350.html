Zionai returns
A long time ago, several guilds worked together in what was called the Diplomatic Assembly under the initiative of Kithrak of Red Tape. Another guild that was present during the discussions of the DA was called Tranquility - a very little known guild, but still it had its hands in pretty much anything, managing to stay out of focus while still working in the service of peace between the people of Rubi-Ka.
A few months ago, the guild changed name to Black Sun, and decided to enter the trade of mercenaries, from whence it once came - though this had a specific purpose, which definitely had no intentions to collide with their previous peaceful intentions. At the same time I had to leave the post of precidency of Black Sun for an unknown time, leaving leadership with my general Mithrax, one of the best men, and friends you can find.
Now, finally, I can give word that I will soon return, and once again take over leadership of Black Sun with some of the best people this planet has ever bred.
And why do I wish to tell you about this? - Simply because the initiative of peace hasn't died, as it never will. Black Sun intends to return to the peace talks, in the interest of all the Rubi-Kan people - We want the economic prosperity of all people as well as the social liberation for the Clans, in the best interest of Omni-Tek and mutual cooperation.
I intend to hear many harsh words over this, as I have done before, but those of you who wants true peace without the costs of war, knows that no hard words can destroy us.
All feel free to contact me, when I return permanently in January - I hope that we will be able to build a stabile peace, despite the boulders that are being hurled at us, harder than ever.

I'm looking forward to seeing you all soon
