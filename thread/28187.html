Top Ten Ma Answers
Ok here they are everyone. First I would like to say sorry about 1 2 3 and 10, Blackmane is out sick and he is the one that can answer these question.

When he returns, I will for sure post the updates for these 4 questions.

1. When it comes to nano interruption, it is only melee attacks that are able to interrupt nano casting - shooting someone with a gun won't interrupt nanos. However, because of the speed at which most nanos can be executed, the chance of interrupting a nano is relatively small, unless the nano initiatives of the target have been reduced quite a bit.
In terms of damage, the MA is meant to be doing fast, steady, consistent damage. The
enforcer and soldier are more tank-like (with their high HPs for the enforcers and the
deflection shields for the soldier) than the MA - they can withstand more punishment than the MA.

3. MA nanos?
Blackmane:
3. Cohort and Horde have been fixed in 14.4. Cohort is now a +400AC buff for all MAs in the team that costs 5NCU. Horde is a +12 damage for all MAs in the team that costs 5 NCU and also does a minor martial arts buff. These stack with the other damage/AC buffs.

The give nano/life are useless. They are hangovers from beta, and I will probably simply remove them from spawning.

The single-target taunts for all professions are going to be revisted sometime in the future as they are not good enough at drawing aggro onto the caster.

Crash of Thunder. Right now I have no intention of revisiting this nano line. Given their recently reduce NCU and nano point costs, they are in a fairly good state. A +94 damage buff for 1:20 minutes costing 16 NCU is good enough, for now at least. There are no other modifiers to these nanos (so no increased fist speed), and the breaking of roots is probably related to the nano shutdown debuff that it places on you (which can be considered to be a bug).

Other MA buffs are always a possibility, but there is nothing scheduled right now. Same with a bow buff - it is likely something that the MA would get, but it is not on the schedule right now. While the meditation suggestion is interesting, it is not something that the MA will be getting, sorry.


4: MA weapons?
Bugged as usual. 8) Anyways, when you spend IP points you want some benefits, right? That's not the case with MA weapons atm. Zenith Taichis, Torture tool and the upcoming (whopee) Claw weapons up your damage, the rest kinda brings it down. While Wallblades and Parry sticks have a nice defensive bonus, they aren't really worth the IP in my personal opinion. Also, dualwielding MA weapons sure brings that damage output down. Shouldn't we gain from the IP pain?


Jim Salabim
--- I'll have a look at Wall Blades and Parry Sticks. Since we upped the damage of your fists recently - MA weapons might be a bit out of balance damage-wise compared to them.



5: Ranged "MA" weapons?
Bows/Sharp objects. If we're supposed to be good with bows, we need a bow buff. Who is able to selfequip a QL200 bow? Traders. Bow Special Attack is nicely implemented but frankly, kinda sucks from a damage pov. And pretty please, we want some sharp objects to throw around. Stun grenades, smoke
bombs, shurikens and poison daggers. It's a green skill that has no purpose.

Jim Salabim
--- Bows are in the process of becoming better and we want them to be viable weapons, at least for MA. You will get a Bow Buff, not sure which patch.

Implementing Throwing weapons have been a bit down on the list for a while, but you should see the first throwing weapons and grenades enter the game in not too long (I'll try getting the first ones in for 14.6). They will work in the same way that Special Arrows and the Fixer Special Attack does.

Bow Special Attack is an additional attack - it comes on top of all other damage, and as such there's no rules for how much damage it should do, other than it should ideally be worth the IP. To begin with I'll look at lowering the cost of making Special Arrows.



6: Riposte?

Gaute:
We are considering maybe perhaps changing the Riposte skill to an automatic skill. This will stop the attack of the opponent, and give you a free attack. It will be available against all close combat attacks for those using MA or melee weapons. ETA will not be released until it is available in a "next patch" section. Please be patient.

7: Parry? I don't parry?

Gaute:
The parry chance will be upped slightly, and the method used changed. This will mean that it will favor those who put a lot of focus into it. This will be done at the same time as when we change the Riposte skill. (We are going to have a "skill fixing" patch some time in the future.)

8: Dimach

Gaute:
The biggest problem with the Dimach is that it takes too long to
recycle. (Damage per use is OK, we think.) We are going to change it so that the higher the skill, the shorter the recycle. With high skill (2000 or around that - available after Shadowlands...) it might recycle as quickly as every 5-10 minutes. (Dunno exactly how fast, we shall test it...) Most MA's use Dimach to access "Shen". I haven't seen anyone complain about the effect of that, yet.

9: What's up with Nano Resist?

Gaute:
We are planning on heavily upgrading all defensive skills (Nano-Resist, Dodge, Evade and Duck) to make defense a more viable strategy. We have several plans on how to do it, but it will be a method that favors those who invest IP.


10.
Blackmane.
I don't think that we want to give out the particulars of the way the initiative/evasion system works. But, higher evades are always better (reduces not only the chance of being hit, but also the chance of being hit with a critical). And higher initiatives for the MA means that you can have the aggdef bar lower, which is better for evasions purposes, but still attack at the same speeds.
