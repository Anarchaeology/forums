I agree, a distance slider sould be included in mission generators or at the very least have the ability to pick the zone.

In fact I think they should go further by adding sliders for the percentage of grey/green/yellow/orange/red mobs that populate the mission. I know this is what the difficulty slider is supposed to do, but it does not work.

I know this will take a lot of the surprise out doing a mission, but I am fed up with missions either being to easy or to hard at the same difficulty level.
Also each of us will have different ideas on just what is easy and hard and that depends on class and level and play style. If however we have the option of setting the MOB's in the mission, then each of us can decide how easy/hard we want a mission to be.
Perhaps even to be able to set the size of the mission to. On more than one occassion I have spent over 30 minutes running to a mission, only to find it consists of 3 mob's and 5 rooms. What a complete waste of time.
