Clans engage Omni-AF troopers crashed ship at Avalon.
Colonel Elnertz
Report #221

Intercepted a message from a man, claiming an omni-af ship had crashed into a mountain south of Avalon.

Gathered up a troop and went to check it out. Immediately met heavy fire from the Omni-AF troops trying to repair the ship. I estimate the number of Omni-AFs to be around 5.

We immediately sensed someone very powerful had equipped them. People could shoot them yet their health seemed to be boundless! What kind of equipment had these been coated in?

While we started firing at the omnis, a Goliath-Class Juggernaut came out of nowhere to their aid. Needless to say, the clanners provailed.

A higher officer gave us the order to destroy their ship, but it was invulnerable to our damage. While we tried to figure out how to devastate it, Omni-AF reinforcments came in form of 5 more troopers and 1 juggernaut.
Needless to say, we beasted them.

Just as we finished the juggernaut, we could see the ship rising and making its escape. What their mission was, we did not find out.

/Colonel Elnertz
