Devils Advocate wants you!
Devils Advocate is currently seeking new members, now is the best time to join an organization that is known throughout Rubi-Ka as being one of the more elustrious social organizations. DA doesn't believe in having it's members molded into the cookie cutter forms that some orgizations expect their members to become. Instead we provide a "family" type enviroment where members are allowed to be themselves.

Currently we are looking for:

General member: The core of any good org is it members, and in DA all of it's members are listened too and their ideas are incorperated into the overall workings of the org.

Lawyer: DA in the past has been known to "bend" the rules a bit and likewise we tend to come under the gavel of the Omni-Tek courts. Of course we've always came out of the courtrooms squeeky clean due to our excelent legal team. This is one of the more difficult positions in DA but also rewarding in it's own right.


If interested sign up for an interview today by contacting myself (trybes) or DonCarnage ingame.
