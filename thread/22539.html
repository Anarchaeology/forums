MOB Difficulty
I posted a long time ago about my thoughts with upping MOB difficulty being the most imbalancing thing to happen in AO, causing almost everyone to have to overequip massively just to survive. I feel that to slow down leveling, instead of raising MOB difficulty maybe you should just lower the amount of experience given for killing a given MOB by XX%. There is ablsolutley no reason someone should have to use a weapon 30 QLs levels higher than themselves to do more than minimum damage on a MOB the same level as themselves. Someone else seems to have written something to the same effect and summed it up very well in a way I hadn't thought of before. So I'll quote it here:

Happens in almost every game

quote:
Originally posted by Trosida
Every stinking one of the mmorpgs (and even many of the pen and paper ones).

Devs and GMs get into this mode of: Crap if they kill my mob the players are smarter than I am!

Of course this is nonsense. So is the idea that the game must be balanced between the mobs and the players. The *PLAYERS* are *SUPPOSED* to win fights against average mobs with ease. Boss mobs are a different story (no matter the game). MOST fights in an RPG are NOT SUPPOSED TO BE LIFE AND DEATH FIGHTS! Only the biggest most important fights warrent an average or better chance of death.

However...

MOB exp is NOT DEVALUED because of this.

Listen up Funcom! Typical devs with no clue how to actually build a game. They can program them all day long but do not have the slightest bit of game theory.

If the game was designed properly this would be the general outline.

Playing missions at equal to the player's level results in mobs that the player will handily beat (or a team can handily in a team mission) netting the player(s) a solid amount of experience towards the next level. The final mobs at the target (and esp. the Boss mob in the team mission) is tougher and could kill the player if the player is stupid, unlucky, or some combination of both.

Of course any mob can kill a player that is stupid, or unlucky. Boss mobs just narrow that window further and the *boss* mobs would be the mobs that start off more powerful than the character.

With an overhaul of the con system this could be accomplished overnight.

A second side-effect of this is then you could put real meaning into character death. No game at this time has that. Of course this stems from the problem that the lowest mobs can easily kill a player.

All because of the devs not wanting to be beaten by the players. Heaven forbid...
