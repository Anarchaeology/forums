How hard are you willing to work?
Level 0: Claim you are a DJ, but don't do anything else. (No coolness.)

Level 1: Set up a playlist on Live365 or any other audio server, tell people that's your station, and you're a DJ. (No perceptible coolness.)

Level 2: Set up a playlist on Live365 or a comparable server. Find a dance floor or venue that doesn't have a DJ on duty and doesn't have one scheduled. Schedule it for yourself, which usually means figuring out who's "running" the place in-game (if anybody) and getting their permission, plus putting fliers up on the RK1 or RK2 events calendar. Log your character in during the event, be there live, and yell every few minutes that you're the DJ, and your Live365 audio stream address. (Some minimal coolness.)

Level 3: Do everything from level 2, but use Live365's software that lets you control what order songs play in real time. Don't use a playlist during live shows. Using Audacity and LAME (or whatever), record a station ID that includes the character name to send /tells to for song requests. (Some coolness.)

Level 4: Do everything from level 3, but use even better home software and hardware to be able to broadcast live voice from home, and actually DJ the broadcast, including announcing songs, doing shout-outs, and so on. (Coolness)

Level 5: Band together with a large enough group of other level 4 DJs and hardcore event/party organizers so that you can offer a full schedule of live dance and music events most evenings. (Serious coolness.)

Level 6: Do such a great job of being a level 5 DJ, and play such popular tunes, that you end up impressing so many people, including guild leaders on all 3 sides, that your show becomes practically required listening for anybody who wants to "get" the inside jokes rattling around in AO chat. Be so good at it that ARKs and Funcom employees volunteer to do live call-in - which implies having the hardware and software to broadcast call-ins, too. Produce a talk show and/or news broadcast at least once a week while you're doing it. (Compete in Tarryk's league.)
