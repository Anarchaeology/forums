Walk Of Answers
His tears blend with the corns of the sand, and his feet dug into the dunes of the desert at every step.
"Gate... How could he be gone... He's immortal... Like me... He's a Dun... Like me...", Malcom fell to his knees, tired, worn, looking up at the starfilled skies.
"HOW CAN YOU BE GONE!? WHAT ARE YOU DOING TO ME!? WHAT IS THE PURPOSE?!" Malcom screamed at the skies, spreading his arms and locking his fists, wasting all air in his chest in his yell of desperation for truth.
A soft, yet conserned voice behind him.
"Cham.. What's happened? Why are you way out here?" Malcom took a deep breath and turned his head to look, still kneeling. It was Jake, Jake Russi from 'Voice of The People', a growing friend. "Jake... They took him away... Somehow..." "Took who away Cham?" Jake sat down carefully besides Malcom, but he didn't answer, fainted from exhaustion, heat, and depression, he falls onto Jake..

Someplace else, West Athens, Leopus' appartment.
"Can you ..attatch it..? Cer'?" Zack was standing in the door to her bedroom, Cer' was examining Leopus' jaw and head, with help from the lights of Scott's robot 'Dr Data'. "I...Think so..I'm no professional doctor..But..Falsing the profession, I might just be able to bandage it together..Her head and jaw..." He proceeded as told, Scott held Leopus' jaw up to her head, while Cer' carefully bandaged it all together. Her stare was distant, misty and dead, the skin around her eyes were black and blue, the rest of her body was covered in blood from the missing jaw.
"We better clean her up..." Zack said, and approached with a wet cloth. "What do you think happened to her, Zack?" Scott said, packing up his robot. "I don't know...", Zack softly pushes the wet cloth against the stains of blood, "...But I'm somehow sure MithNess has something to do with it..."
The three spooks, "I have something to do with wha....oh..my.." MithNess stared at Leopus. Cer' quickly turned and blocked his path of vision. "Mith, you better go out." "But I'm here to help Leo.." Mith flew through the appartment to the far wall. Zack's fists was burning with a bright red flame. "You stay away from her! You better start running, because I'll be hunting you down when our Lord isn't watching..." MithNess rubbed his burnt chest. "But I..." MithNess exclaimed. "GO!" Cer' pointed at the door fiercly and stared angry at MithNess. MithNess nodded and slowly walked out the door of Leopus' appartment...
"Damned alien..." The three watched him go, Leopus was laid in the bed all patched up, staring at the ceiling of her bedroom...

Perspective Of The Waz'Yul
A creation.. Not really a masterpiece.. A bit of a wasted effort.. It needs a special touch.

There, done. Rubai-Koasho. Write it down, make it.. Make it a book. An eternal book, some writing, they might learn to read, "The Book Of Dun". Place it.. There. Perfect. Easy to find for them once it starts.

(...)

Created, and not raised, Chamaelo awakens on a rather hot day, in the middle of nowhere, nothing has a name yet. He stretches his body in the sand, as the bones cracks and falls into place he looks around. A storm. His eyes won't blink, even if the sandcorns hits him, he touches them, whipes the dust away, his eyes are of crystal. There is something in the distance. Some kind of rock, doesn't look like it fits in at all. He walks over to it, woobly at first, but he quickly learns how to move more perfectly. Perfect back, perfect skin, perfect body, perfection. The rock is cubic, touching it makes his hand burn, it's getting crispy, he withdraws his hand, looks at it, scars of fire. While he looks at his hand, he smiles. No more scars now. Walking around the structure, he ponders about its meaning, why is it there, what is it there for, how did he get there, what is this image. What is the top like, he's on it, looking down at something extraordinary, the cube has a rectangular hole in it, not too deep, but still strange.

"Good day."
Chamaelo kneels down before the hole, puts his hands inside, it's cold.

"Good day Mr Ashwell."
He turns, where did that come from, might be the wind. He keeps studying the hole, he draws around the edges with his fingers.

"Mr Ashwell, Good day."
He looks up and smells the air. Refreshing. A moment of silence, the winds stop blowing, and his fingers seem stuck on the edges of the hole.

"Here, let me help you."
A shadow moves to the other side of the rectangular hole, hands, almost like Chamaelos, fingers pointed out, draws the rectangular the same way he did just moments ago, starting from his stuck fingers. He looks up. There's someone, one that looks different in a way. Suddenly, a blinding light coming from the hole, it's blue, bright blue, they both fall off the cube. He stands up again, brushing some sand off his body and looks at the cube. It's not there, but something is there. He walks up to it. It's the rectangular hole, but now it seems inverted. As he's about to pick it up, it vanishes.

"Hm.. The Book Of Dun.."
He looks around. The other figure is holding it, opening the rectangular. The figure looks at him.

"Come Malcom."
The shadow turns and seems to float off with the newly found item.

"Come along Malcom. It is your name, isn't it?"
He looks around, name, Malcom, or Mr Ashwell. The figure stops, turns and moves to him. It takes his hand.

"You do not want to come?"
He's standing still. What is that feeling, something feels wrong in his chest. He backs away, he runs, in the opposite direction.

"Stubborn...Why did he run off like that?"

(...)

"He keeps making these weird noises..I've never heard anything like it."
A pause.
"No, I don't know what it means... But it's as if..I feel what he's been through... No, just a feeling... Yes Sir... Yes, have a nice day Sir."
Malcom slowly opens his eyes.
"You're awake..Cham..Are you allright? You kept making some strange soun.."
Interrupted.
"I'm fine.."
Jake nods and gives Malcom something to drink.
"Cham... I kept seeing some images..I heard voices..."
Malcom looks over at Jake, something feels odd.
"I..Nevermind Cham.." Jake looks down at his drink, and reaches over Malcom's forehead with a wet cloth. Malcom smacks Jake's hand, and the cloth falls to the floor.
"I said I'm fine."
Water slowly trinkles about the wet cloth on the floor.
"Cham..What's the..Book Of The Dun..?"
Malcom turns in his bunkerbed, away from Jake. Jake watches Malcom in hopes for an answer, yet sighs and leaves the room a few moments later.
The door shuts, the lights go out.
"Book Of The Dun... My purpose.."

"Miss Vallone, are you there? It's Miss Washko."
Leopus opens her eyes from resting to the soothing music from her radio system.
"Greetings Miss Washko, how are you today?" she speaks to her com, sitting up on her carpet.
"I'm..I'm just fine Miss Vallone, though there's something disturbing me."
Leopus looks a little conserned down at her carpet, picking at a small stain. "What's the matter?" she says calmly.
"I met a man, outside your apartment, he asked something of a book that belonged to him, that you have?"
Silence, Leopus looks ahead of herself. "Malcom?"
"Miss Vallone?"
Leopus shakes her head and looks down again. "Yes, yes I'm here.."
"What was this book he spoke of Miss Vallone? It seemed important..And..He died because of Madam Aqwa I believe..But he came back to life.."
"Come to my apartment Miss Washko..I'll tell you about the book.."
"On my way Miss Vallone."
Leopus gets up on her feet and walks into the bathroom, pushing a button to make the showerhead come out of the wall, undressing, and steps under the shower, letting the light water run at her body. The water is warm, steaming up the mirror slowly, the bathroom heats up slowly aswell from the water. Holding around herself, Leopus just stands there under the running water, thinking, deep in thought.
The doorbell rings its soft bells. *Ding* *Dong* *Dingaling* Leopus gasps and quickly turns off the shower and tucks a towel around her, tiptoeing lightly to the entrencedoor, peeking through a small peephole. Miss Washko stands there, looking around.
"Are you alone Miss Washko?" Leopus asks as she peeks. Miss Washko turns to the door, moving her head closer as she replies. "Yes, I'm all alone." "Ok" says Leopus, pushing a button, making a buzzing sound, the door unlocks, and Leopus opens it a little. "Come in, I was just taking a quick shower." She smiles at Miss Washko, stepping away from the door, holding her towel up. Miss Washko enters and closes the door, it clicks and locks itself.
"Don't let me keep you Miss Vallone." She smiles back, taking her coat off and hangs it up on a hanger by the door. Leopus nods and tiptoes back to the bathroom quickly, leaving small wet spots from her feet, shouting from the bathroom. "I'll be right out Miss Washko." closing the door to the bathroom. Miss Washko nods to herself and shouts back, smiling and looks around Leopus' apartment. "Take your time."
After a moment, Leopus steps out, wearing her bathrobe. "I'll get dressed later. I hate wearing clothes right after a warm shower." she smiles and sits down on her carpet by the radio. "Please, sit."
Miss Washko smiles and nods, sitting down. "You look good Miss Vallone, I was very happy when I heard you were healed up ok."
Leopus smiles shyly. "Yes, thank you. I guess it was fortunate..."
Miss Washko looks at Leopus curiously. "I'm only guessing, but does this have..Anything to do with this book, or the man that asked me about it? He died, but seemed to heal up again..Like you?"
