OT PR stunts or Clan screw-up?
There has been much discussion between the clans about whether we acted wrong by retaliating after Radiman's supposed death a few days ago. Some people have suggested that it was just a conspiracy by Omni-Tek to make it look like they saved our leader, and we attack them in return.
Others believe that they actually saved Radiman, but this is all beside the point. Whether or not the assasination attempt was planned By Omni-Tek, the point is that they exploited the situation to get good PR, and once again make us look like terrorists. They knew we would retaliate if we thought Radiman had been killed, and so they led us to believe just that, making us look like fools once again when they revealed that he was safe, and in fact had even been saved by Omni-Pol. They baited us, and we fell for it.

Now, if they are really such nice people, going around saving our leaders and offering all of us amnesty, why did they not inform us immediately after the "assasination", that Radiman was alive? Not only would they have improved the relations between our two factions, they would also have avoided an assault on their own town! This seems to me like incredible disrespect, both for us as clanners, for their own civilians who died when we retaliated, and for the peace they supposedly wants. Our assault could have been avoided if a single Omni-Tek official had cared to enter the OT-trading channel we all have anyway, thanks to their own amnesty, and broadcasting a single short message! The fact that they did not, but instead held a press-conference, again without actually informing us about it, shows how much they care for us, for peace, and for their own civilians, who were sacrificed in this media stunt!

And once again, they baited us. They made us believe that we screwed up again by attacking, to make us worry about this, instead of realizing that Omni-Tek actually provoked this attack by not letting us know that our leader was safe. And once again we fell for it. Yesterday clan leaders spent the whole day discussing whether or not we should have attacked, but it seemed right at the time. They had killed our leader, so of course we attacked. They would do the same if Boss Ross was killed.

Once again we missed the point entirely! Omni-Tek employees attempted to kill Radiman, and their leaders sacrificed their own employees' lifes by making us attack them! Those are the important facts here. They show exactly how much Omni-Tek care.

The fact remains that we should not have attacked, but that was hardly unexpected, and it is by far overshadowed by Omni-Tek's behaviour during this.

Until very recently, I actually believed that at least a few of the Omni-Tek bosses actually wanted peace. Now I am not so sure any more.

For the record, the emergency press conference held in Omni HQ after the "assassination" had no clan members present. After we were killed at the last HQ press conference, we are quite wary to return there while criminal organizations like Dark Carnival are free to do as they will. For this reason, the clans thought Radiman had actually been killed for quite a long time after the decoy story was announced to the Omni.

I was lucky enough to receive information from a number of Omni sources, including Sandfield, reporter for the Rubi-Ka Times, and Derva, spokeswoman for Omni, who told me the entire decoy story and even that Aberic was the immediate suspect. I relayed this info to the Clan Leaders immediately...and was ignored. I sent who I could to all the major gatherings of clan people they could find to spread the information. But even after the press conference by Gelsa, it was obvious people only wanted to fight, and so fight they did. On the one hand, I can understand why they felt the need to physically retaliate. They also had no reason to believe information fed to us only by the Omni, and the Omni refused to let anyone on the clan side speak to Radiman directly. Ultimately, the biggest clan mistake was the horrible PR spin we let the whole ordeal take. I'm fairly convinced that reporter Mayhoon is on the Omni payroll, and he hardly helped to show our true intentions in the resulting attacks, instead he just helped Omni portray us as a group of bloodthirsty idiots.
