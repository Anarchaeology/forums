Lirona
Leetas
quote:
Look, sweetums Omni MADE a clone of Radiman. Ok so all it had to do was walk down a street until what's his face took a shot at him but they did make one.
Something like that would hardly need to stand up to any real scrutiny. A robot with a few packets of synthesized blood could to it, with a marksman who knows exactly where to hit, the security guards wait for the paramedics to arrive, and the medics, paid off in advance, make the DOA announcement and cart the "body" off to the incinerators so it can't be examined. "Duplicate" and "clone" don't necessarily mean the same thing.

quote:
And if somebody was gonna go to all the trouble to make a clone don't ya think they would have thought of the scanning thing? Like maybe they hacked into the system to put their copy of Radiman's scan into it? You think those scanners are secure? Hah!
Let's see, if it was Omni, the only real access to that they have is from Radiman's visit to Omni-1 last year. Meanwhile, Radiman has had to give enough of his genetic imprint to access various files, open communication lines, and the like over the past year that a discrepancy like suddenly reverting back to a particular date, especially an important one like that, would have been noticed, and the clone denied access to resources. And I'm assuming that Radiman also doesn't submit to insurance scans all that often. Sure someone could probably build a more up-to-date genetic profile, but if one got it wrong it would set off even more alarms: Too risky.

If it was someone within the Clans, then we're doomed, because someone would use this as an excuse to dissolve the Tir Accord. After all, it's not really RADIMAN in charge anymore, and he is the man designated for the job.

quote:
Anyway so ok maybe not a clone. Maybe they snatched him and brainwashed him or something. He was gone long enough to brainwash a platoon.
This is a somewhat more likely possibility, but again there's the chance that any rough treatment would be noticed, at the very least by the medbots you can be quite sure have given him a thorough examination. Although a lack of rough treatment would explain why he was gone so long, as such conditioning would take much longer. But this doesn't explain something else: Phillip Ross was missing from the public eye too, and disappeared about the same time as Radiman. It's just as likely they were off finishing their chess game in peace.

quote:
I mean look at the alternative. You really want to believe the leader of the Council of Truth just went traipsing off into the wilderness for months just to think about things? If that's what happened then maybe he needs a little more vacation like in a mental ward.
This was actually a time-honored practice on Earth in truly ancient times, near the dawn of written history. Two leaders might retreat into the wilderness to contemplate options simultaneously, that the situation be unchanged when they returned. Obviously this is from a much simpler time, but I still would not be surprised if they decided to attempt this. After all, many on Rubi-Ka only know the rules for Chess because these two men decided to play a game against each other. In fact, it was quite a fad for awhile if I remember right. If anybody on this planet would try such an ancient custom, these two would be it.
