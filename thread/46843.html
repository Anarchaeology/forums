Recent TAG article
Having been sucked into the event, I feel it's my duty to explain my actions, and involve myself more completely into the RP aspect of AO.

For those that have read the recent TAG article, the quote at the bottom was done by my alt, WanderingX. I didn't expect to be quoted, but now that I have been, I may need to start fearing for my life

So to add on to the recent event that happened in Old Athen, here is an explanation for my actions, and why I said what I said:

(note: I play primarily on RK2, so this might not mean anything to you RK1 players)

(IC)

I firmly support the CoT, not neccesarily Henry Radiman. Omni-Tek may be our enemy, but one thing is for sure, we must stay united to prevail. However, I agree with the CoT that violence isn't the answer. Why sink to the level of our enemy, when we can be above them! The terrorist activities need to stop! The Dust Brigade and others are only damaging our reputation. I will not follow a "clan" that thinks they can win this war by violent unruley actions. What happens when the war is over? Is Simon simply going to move on? Step down and let us live in peace?

I say No! He will be greedy! He will try to take control, continue with the violence! Become the next Omni-Tek! I won't sit by and let one evil deed be replaced by another.

All you clanners need to wake up! The bloodshed needs to end! We are not a hate mongering, violent society! We seek peace, and we will not achieve peace with all this petty bickering. I say Hurrah to the ICC for stepping in. The CoT will rise to it's former glory and the clans will re-unite! I say our enemies aren't the Omni-Tek oppressors. Our enemies are ourselves! Kill the demons of greed. End the bloodlust!

(OOC)

Getting involved in an event like this makes everything worthwhile. Despite some of the problems this game may have, it's stuff like this that pulls you in. You begin to feel part of the bigger picture, not just some drone on a leveling treadmill. Kudos to Funcom! We need more vents like this. We need more people to get involved.

Of course, I'm just basking in my 15 minutes of fame :-)
