IPR Suggestion..
The miniscule amount of IPR that we are given compared to the number of skills available to us just isn't right. We have what..80 something skills and around 15 IPR total (after hitting all title levels). There needs to be a method of obtaining IPR points other than just hitting title levels....

Those who have surpassed all title levels can't get any more IPR points. What happens if they've used them all? What? Use IPR wisely you say? LOL. I say make IPR something that can be earned. It would instantly give us the ability to experiment with our characters (something I know would make the game a bit more interesting). It would also give us the ability to correct our IPR mistakes instead of dealing with gimpness until their next title level (something I know would make the game less frustrating).

Suggestions:
Put IPR shops in the game that allow you to buy IPR points at a rate of 1m per IPR point.

Make IPR endless however set a time lock on the ability to reset so that you couldn't use more than 1 IPR per 12 hours.

Change the mission terminals so that the money / XP slider becomes an IPR / XP slider and allow missions to give out 2 IPR max.

Put an "uncommon" (read: NOT RARE) item in the game that can be taken to an NPC to be redeemed for an IPR point. Make it drop off of anything to prevent camping.

I agree that there should be some ways of getting more IPR points available than the 15 or so that we get by level 200. The game has many years left, and I'm sure enough changes will happen in the future for any level 200 to use all his/her points.

But the alternatives you present makes the points a little too easy to obtain IMHO. A powergamer would be able to completely rearrange their skill template up to several times per week with that kind of access to IPR points.

I saw an idea presented earlier where it was suggested that you could trade in mission tokens for IPR points. The cost here would have to be given a lot of thought, but something like 500-1000 tokens per point would make them obtainable in order to fix a "gimped" character while not being too easy to get.

Another way could be to let level 200 players get IPR points for every n million XP they get after they hit level 200. The number of xp points would of course have to be set fairly high in order to make this something that takes a little work, and not just something you can do in a day.

I realize that theese suggestions leave out a lot of people. But keep in mind that until you reach level 200 you still get more IP every time you level. You also get new IPR points every time you get to a new title level. Only players of level 200 who has used all their IPR points are permanently stuck with their skill template.
