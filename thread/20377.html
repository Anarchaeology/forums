Idea for a new vehicle - Team Van
I was talking to my friend the other day trying to convince him how cool AO was and that he should join when he gave me an idea. A new vehicle should be created. The team van. Vehicles are great when running around solo. But, what happens when you get in a team of six when two people have cars and everyone else has a measly 150 Run Speed. The fast people zoom off and you end up having to wait up on the slow people. This makes traveling as a team a slow pain in the neck. So I propose the team van. (This seam like it would also be cool for organizations, having an organization van that can be borrowed). The team van can be held by one person and then when equiped it lets the entire team inside the van. Increaseing and averageing out everyone in the teams run speed so that everyone can arrive to the hunting ground or mission together without waiting. Write what you think, maybe Funcom will build and sell Team Vans!.

You are not the first to suggest this, nor will you be the last... the more individuals who suggest it the better

Who does not want to see :

APC ( Armored Personnel Carrier ) : Ground Vehicle
Takes 1 driver and 5 passengers ( or maybe 1 light turret gunner and 4 passengers ). Increases the AC of everyone inside by a significant amount.

Troop Dropship : Air Vehicle
Takes 1 pilot and 5 passengers. Increases the AC of all inside considerably, but less than the APC.

Maybe have some owner-less vehicles as well...

Lets say you find an old bombed-out tank from the last war.

Notify your guild, buy parts and get your engs out to fix it up and restock ammo.

Vehicle "slots" :

1) driver, control the vehicle
2) turret gunner, controls the main gun ( grenade launcher type )
3) MG gunner
4) 3 passenger seats. passengers have to get out to fight.

Now, a tank should pack a punch. It is also vulnerable to heavy weapons. If the crew is killed / driven out of the tank, it can be captured by someone else. Tanks would also be fairly slow...

The number of tanks could be finite, and limited to certain zones.

In one zone there would be limited places to hide if someone decides to hoard vehicles...
