i think the only thing people can sorta
answer without speculating are question 2 and 3.

you cant really force missions but you can take note where you spawned grey cave missions since these have 30 mobs max and are thus pretty short.

if you note the general region and pick missions around there, you have a high chance of getting another small mission.

that is why clon**** is/was so popular.


perception is important if you want to equip one of the new crit scopes (but can be buffed with adv and fix buffs), is important for detection checks vs concealed people (interesting for pvpers) and for trap disarm (first step of avoiding a trap is to know its there if you perform an active search from the option menu and your perception is high enough, trapped chests and doors will be highlighted).


about team level there have been alot of speculations but as far as i know never a concrete and conclusive answer

mission duration tends to reflect the relative size of a mission, and it seems to be more accurate with certain mission types than with others. a 7 hour or less repair mission tends to be short more often than not, but i've also seen 14 hour find-item missions that were 5-6% per mob, and 5 hour hits that were .5% per mob.

in general though, you can kind of gauge a mission's size by the amount of time you are given to complete it.
