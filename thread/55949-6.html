If you offer some way for the players to get credits in exchange for the old, now worthless LLTS, there are a few things you should keep in mind:

The prices may vary between RK1, RK2 and RK3. I imagine players on one dimension would be rather ticked off if you used prices derived from another dimension.

The prices are very high (200m+) for the best LLTSs and not so high (1m-5m?) for the lowest 1% LLTS. You might be tempted to make the cost a linear function based on QL, but IMO this would strongly overvalue the LLTSs in the middle - a QL100 LLTS (7%) is surely worth less than 100m.

You'll be introducing billions upon billions of credits into the economy. It might be worth your time to calculate just how much would be added (by checking how many LLTSs are out there) and compute how many new credits would be created. Then compare that with how many credits are currently out there. Imagine a world where the number of credits doubled overnight. What effect would this have on the prices of other highly valued items (such as the rare Grid Armor line)?

4 IPRs might not be enough for some people, but from what I've read 5-6 should cover most everyone.
