I'm not saying other classes aren't challenging. I've played with some crats and NTs who are especially good at crowd control, and many enforcers who are always mindful of the hate list. But these days, there is less need for crowd control in the high level game, and everyone can pretty much tank past 150.

I'm also not claiming that traders are boring. Compared to soldiers, traders are delightfully interesting. But the trade-off is an increased workload. With the impending OE nerf, most traders will probably be expected to team wrangle every 3 mins in missions. It's do-able, just work. I do it sometimes, but I'll probably wrangle all the time next patch.

As far as trader nerfs go... What nerf? I haven't seen anything from Funcom about trader nerfs. Sounds like too many windbags with not enough to do. Yeah the OE nerf will knock out the Ithacas and Krutts, but this hurts everyone with a shotty. Traders will be back down where they should be - not too powerful, but not too weak. Defenders and Vektors are "ok" guns, even with your skills jacked up.

I also see some discussion about divest and plunder being overpowered. Many don't remember, but both divest/deprive and plunder/ransack were heavily nerfed long ago.

A quickie divest is almost a necessity without a crit buff. But it's been my experience that if I divest AND plunder, I'm usually dead before the second nano finishes. You see, if you're casting nanos, you're not shooting. If you're not shooting, you're not killing. And it's not the guy you're landing nanos on you have to be concerned about, so much as the other bastard sneaking up behind you. Who ever fights one on one these days? There's always some other jacknuts who jump in.

Dunno, you guys can argue all you want. But those who chose the trader class to be pvp gods will inevitably lose out - just like agents and soldiers did many months ago. However, those who chose the class for the challenge and maybe some tradeskill fun (yeah right, what am I saying?), will be largely unaffected.
