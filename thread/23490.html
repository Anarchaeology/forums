SOM doesn't drop high QL bracers, but for a majority of people that don't have any, it's a good place to go and get some good sets from that will augment or allow for at least a little reflection. The ones in there are normally +3 / +4 variety, which still adds up to a reasonable +8 if you get your matchups correct. Upto L50 they serve their purpose well. After that you do need a little luck in getting higher ones, but it's certainly not impossible. As for reaching the magic point of being able to chew up 40% via bracers, you'd still need to be TL6 to do that. TL5 bracers will cover 15% to 18% which is still a level 150 pre-requisite.

It doesn't solve the issue of people getting hurt by there own hits, but as many people know, the best way of tackling a soldier is to wait for the TMS to drop anyway, and considering that TMS overwrites the reflect, the soldier is at a disadvantage for the remainder of the 2 minutes that TMS shuts them down for.

Whether or not the soldier decides to run just before the TMS goes down is another matter, but that's a topic that been discussed at length elsewhere.

I'd also like to reiterate that the person with the bracers would have to know exactly what damage type they are going to face, and although people can hazard various guesses, and occasionally get lucky, they can be open against differing damage types.

If people are silly enough to stand and fight a soldier up-close and personal while they've got bracers and TMS MkX running, then they'll get hurt, but how many people do you know that would be silly enough to try this, when they know they can run and wait 1minute 20seconds, then charge back in.

Also, what's to stop someone from getting a deflect from a soldier before they fight? Everyone seems to be doing pretty well when they get MA's for LMA/UVC...

Perhaps it's time people started asking soldiers for buffs. God know's we could do with the business (although I'd still do them for free).
