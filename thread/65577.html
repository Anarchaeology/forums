Actually...
On live, multi ranged and multi melee work differently...

With multi ranged, the attacks take turns recharging (the one stops while the other recharges bit).

With multi melee, both recharge and attack at will (neither waits for the other to do something).

This is why it currently makes more sense for a melee to dual wield than for a ranged user to do so...you don't get much more for an advantage by dual wielding ranged weapons because most people can use 1 weapon of a much higher quality (primary hand) and have to use one of a much lower quality to dual wield. Since the lower quality one slows down the higher quality one, it usually is not worth it for most classes.

Melee on the other hand does not have this problem and an extra weapon generally means extra damage.

Anywise, that has been my experience using them.
