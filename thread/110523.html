100% OOC
I've found that preplanned teams with other roleplayers tend to be much more rewarding than pickup teams for several reasons.

1:The atmosphere is much more relaxed and laid back, no matter how frantic the pace may be.

2:In my experience most RP'rs tend to be better players socially OOC than most "1337" players. You are not constantly being told that "u r teh suxx" when you pick up a peice of "phat lewt" nor are you accused of being a "sploiter" when you win the roll for the ql 150 X-3 you looted off the boss. (Belive it or not both of these things have happened to me in team missions, plus or minus a few spelling errors)

3:In a preplanned team the rules that are agreed upon at the beginning of the mission (or series of missions) are followed with greater strictness then they are with pickup teams. ie: I have been on a number of pickup teams that have broken up halfway through a mission simply because some fool insists on playing "find the rollerrat" when the team is fighting, usually ending up with the death of the doctor and the subsequent death of everyone on the team as they fall prey to the squealing ball of furry doom.

In short, I find that preplanned teams amongst groups of RP'rs tend to work much better than pick up teams primaily for those reasons. Furthermore, they provide a way for RP'rs to kick around ideas in an OOC fashion while getting XP, and yes, the occasional peice of "phat lewt"

So if anyone is ever in need of a high 90's-low 100's trader (yes, I can calm thank you very much ) just PM me and I'll be glad to help out.
