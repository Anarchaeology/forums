Supa Leet
Yeah, it's 'executing a Nanoformula'. Casting is admittedly easier to type. If you look at a character's hands when he's 'casting' you'll see that he is actually making the motions of typing on a keyboard. He's 'programming' or 'running a program' or 'executing a nanoformula'.

You have an NCU (Nanoformula Containment Unit) it harnesses Nanobots that can be 'programmed' to do things. These programs are called Nanoformulas. Some are beneficial (buffs) some are harmful (debuffs). Think of a debuff as a Computer Virus that's running in that NCU belt around your waist.

When a Disavowed Medic 'executes' a nasty DoT in your NCU, what he is doing is turning your own NCU against you. Essentially he's causing your NCU to run a virus that is making the nanobots do bad things to you instead of good things.
