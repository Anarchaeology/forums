I'd like to add the following point to this allready EXCELLENT thread.

Healing yourself.
A doc in youre team will consider it a personal insult if you use treatment kits on yourself. After all, The doc is there to heal you, so you yourself should stow away those treatment kits. (If youre an MA or adventurer dont try to chip in either... Doc=Healing so forget all about youre healing potential when you find yourself in a team with a doc.)
This is especially true in combat. When some dumb mob his slapping you silly, and the doc is throwing you red sparkles (god knows why) and suddenly the mob changes target and you are left at half your health. Some stupid doc will suddenly start throwing those annoying red sparkles at someone else. At this point please do not use a treatment kit on yourself. As i said, this will insult the doc and also give him a more challenging combat session since you're allready at half health when the mob decides to throw a few more hits youre way.

Always be sure to type
HAEL ME DOC!!!!
