Player Made History/Background.

When I read about AO I fell in love with the idea of a story that the game would become like living inside a book. With a book the story is told often over a number of events, in which we learn more about the characters, what they feel, how they react, there strengh and weakness to. We also learn about the world we live and the start of the book is almost focused on background and the history so far, and bits of this history unfold over time to.


In AO we do have some history and a background in the form of a book, but as I understand it the book itself does not cover aspects in game. For example the races etc etc.

I was rather hoping in time FC would build up a background of info from the important history right through to the strange stories.

We do have a time line which helps a lot, but I would also like to see the twists and turns and read things I see in game.
For example whos is Harry ?? Who owns the lush restort. Who made the Tir river so dirty etc.

I have spoken to FC about this and while its not there offical word they said they had no objections to just making up interesting background. Of course they did say that bare in mind FC might well change the details when they do it offically as part of the story. Also the background should at least be conistant with the current story. All of these points are vaild and i see no problem sticking to them.

So I thought about a player run background site, where we collect stories and background writen by the players.


Now you might wonder why this is any good to people well if you have ever GM or done player events oftten the hardest point is where to start, and starting from a blnk bit of paper is hard.

The background is a rich source which provides ideas, and might even spark of a few interesting debates.

There is the other issue that unless you read the website you might not know what the other person is talking about, but maybe thats not so bad as it give to a chance to re-tell part of history.
