Not logging in isn't what the dissatisfied PvP'ers are so worried about.

It's getting a title then never fighting again. Hell, someone with two accounts could get the top PvP title at level 1 and play him everyday for the next 2 years without ever engaging in PvP again. That person running around with that title and never fighting is what annoys some of the more title conscious PvP'ers.

My ultimate choice that would be on the board would have been 'eliminate them until Team PvP titles can be implemented'.

Get that option up there and in the game.

PvP should not be 1 on 1. This whole thing wouldn't be an issue if you weren't rewarded for only fighting one other person by yourself.
