Rockgod, I must contest much of what you say. While an MA may attack faster at lower levels, the punching attack rate quickly hits its minimum of 1.0/1.0 attack-recharge. A soldier, on the other hand, continues to increase his/her attack speed pretty much until level 200. As for an MA's buffs, let's look at each series:

Damage buffs:
As you progress along this series of buffs each one adds a whopping +2 damage while chewing up 6 more ncus. At level 126, I can use fists of the sorrowful toad which adds +19 damage at a mere cost of 39 ncus. When an MA is in a team and doesn't have enough ncu space, which buff do you think is the first one to get downgraded? That's right, damage buffs. By reducing my damage by *gasp* 6 damage, I can free up enough room for a soldier's deflect shield (-15% damage), for example. The only reason MAs upgrade their damage buffs is because they require the same skills as their healing spells.

AC buffs:
You're joking, right? The MA series of AC buffs require a dark dark blue skill. The overwhelming majority of MAs stop increasing the skill (matter creation) early on due to lack of IP. Any MA that has pursued this line of buffs has really sacrificed in other areas. In any case, if you want a good AC buff, you don't ask an MA, you ask an adventurer or an engineer.

Healing Nanos:
I'm assuming you mean healing nanos instead of 'buffs' here. MA healing spells are roughly equivalent to adventurer heals in magnitude, but have much higher recharge times (which are not affected by nano initiative). For example, my greater healing aura has an 8 second recharge time, whereas an equivalent adventurer nano, greater restore health, has a 3.88 second recharge time. In other words, at high levels and with some points in nano init, an adventurer can heal approximately 1.5 to 2 times as fast, with equal magnitude heals. And I don't think I need to make comparisons to doc heals.

Evade buffs:
Other than debating the actual usefullness of evades here, I'll just say that these buffs are big ncu hogs. NTs and MPs also get these buffs (maybe more that I don't know about).

MA buffs:
These buffs aren't just helpful to an MA's ability to deal damage, they are ESSENTIAL. An unbuffed MA does approximately 20-30% less damage than a buffed one. Name me another class whose damage dealing ability drops that significantly when unbuffed. The +60 from mastery and +12 from lesser controlled rage are comparable to buffs that other classes get for their weaponry (except they can overequip, but that's another issue). Basically each combat class gets buffs specific to their chosen weapon so this is nothing special.

Strength buffs:
LAFF!!!!!! If you can't figure out why these buffs are useless...well, I'm certainly not gonna explain it.

Aggro buffs:
Again, I'm gonna assume you're talking about taunting nanos. Every class in the game can use taunts. Nothing special about these either.

Crit buffs:
Without these, MAs would never be wanted in groups. Period. Only problem is, they help other professions more than they help MAs. Yes they're great, but FC's giving em to crats too so there goes that uniqueness.

All in all, these buffs make MAs one of, if not the most ncu dependent classes in the game. MAs are supposed to do the most damage over time, not soldiers. Yes, we have a wide range of nanos, making us a jack-of-all-trades of sorts, but we're a third-rate jack-of-all-trades (in terms of nanos). On top of that, add all of our problems with special attacks and weapons (which have existed since beta), and you can understand why we feel gimpy. We're great at NOTHING, we're just ok at a few things.
