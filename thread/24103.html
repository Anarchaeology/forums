To clarify-

"This offer stands only in the period between 14.2 and the launch of 14.4!!" refers to the one time only IP reset. That is, between 14.2 and 14.4 you may reset all your skills at once and resdistribute your IP. As mentioned, this is a one time only offer. This is called the IP reset.

However, IP reallocation will be a part of Anarchy Online from 14.2 onwards (not stopping at 14.4). At each title level you will gain a number of IP reset points. With one point you may reset an entire skill. So, lets say, if a change is made down the track that affects skill costs, you may use one those these points to gain back all your IP from that skill and use it again as you see fit.

So, you can still redistribute IP after 14.4 (as long as you have the points). If you wish to take advantage of the full reset you will have to do so within 14.2 and 14.4.

Here's my viewpoint:

Does FC want everyone to have 1 main, or do they want us to play like 5 different alts?

If they give single-skill IP re-allocation more freely, then we will more likely stick with our mains and adjust to change over time. This will have these effects:

a. Increase value of time spent on any given character
b. Increase role-playing and community (because people will get to know each other more through playing 1 main than numerous alts)
c. Re-distribute the level spread: I don't know about you, but I play on RK2... and at the hours I play, I either see 90% below lvl 20 characters, or 90% over lvl 100 characters in any given area - where are the mid-range characters?
d. Keep people playing AO longer. If I will only play Fixer, and I don't want to re-roll, and FC nerfs me into oblivion, then what is my option? Cancel, that's the only option. But the IP reset/single-skill IP re-allocation allows a longer life-span for any given character, so there's less chance that someone would need to re-roll, thus keeping people playing their "chosen profession" much longer.
e. Cut down on resources needed: If I have 1 main and 1 alt stored on the FC servers that I play, isn't that less resources needed by FC than if I have 3 alts on RK1, and 5 alts on RK2?
f. A better idea of which profs are actually represented in-game: If everyone has a 'crat alt, but never play them, we might think "Oh, well RK consists of 10% crats" when the number might actually be 2%.
g. etc.

Yeah, playing different professions is fun... But I for one would have stuck w/my Fixer until level 200 is she was actually employed in a viable profession... Maybe I would've played 1 alt for awhile, but I wouldn't be in the situation I'm in now, running around with a bunch of alts...
