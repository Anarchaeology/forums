Gridgrid's Animal Adventures Part 1: Tir
So in exploring the wilds of Rubi-ka, I find that many people tend to ignore the smaller denizens of the zones except when those denizens happen to be larger or stronger than they are.

As a result, I believe these poor creatures to be rather bored, as they will engage in a conversation with whoever walks past. Also, the lack of social interaction seems to have lowered their levels of intellect, as the below screenshots will prove.

Note that these are the actual screenshots taken by my ever-present invisible camera during the conversations. The only words that have been altered are those that may be found offensive to our younger readers.

Venturing outside of the west gate of Tir, one runs into many creatures. One of the first I happened upon was a reet warbler. These bird-type creatures are happily emulated by many an adventurer seeking to gain speed. However, as the shocking footage you are about to see shows, they might not be the best creatures to emulate.

When I responded to the poor reet's pleas for charity, the reaction was so adverse that I was forced to act in self-defense. Who knows what harsh things such a simple mind may be capable of doing.

http://www.wfu.edu/~marelf03/AO/meeting-a-reet-2.gif

Next, I happened upon an Eleet. Being a lower level, he had a much less pronounced vocabulary, but was rather sure of his own abilities:

Due to his arrogant nature, we soon decided to test our might in a duel:

Finally, tiring of the wilds of Tir County, I happened upon a rather attractive creature:
http://www.wfu.edu/~marelf03/AO/shadowmutant-1.gif
Who attempted to lure me into joining his guild.
http://www.wfu.edu/~marelf03/AO/shadowmutant-2.gif
The lack of vocabulary combined with the lack of a definite promise to procure grid armor resulted in his unfortunate lack of hit points. (had he offered a small gift, I would have considered his guild more closely, as I would have if his vocal cords were capable of forming more than two distinct sentences).

For now, I bid you, fair citizens of Rubi-Ka a fond farewell and wish to remind you to take some time to speak to the underprivledged creatures of the world. Though my experiments today were failures, perhaps someday leets, reets, and mutants will be held in as high regard as atrox (or maybe even humans).

This is Gridgrid, signing out.
