*blam* 10k as.
to say that my surprise was immense would be an understatement of extreme proportions, seeing as with my adv using a scissor and having 760+ in sneak self-buffed i've had possibly 6 or 7 10k sneaks over a period of 14 levels worth of missions, with a good few sneaks in every one of them.

as for my trader's attack rating fully drained compared to my adv, my adv still has a 50 point higher attack rating if he's running feline rage (my adv currently being level 160, both are neuts, so there's not many damage buffing items involved like token boards and tar loot )
as far as perception, base skills, and attack rating + skill goes, my adv has my trader beat hands down on everything, yet my trader seems to be a damage machine using AS compared to my adv using sneak attack, and bear in mind this is in pvm.
(slight edit, bear in mind that my adv trounces my trader on base damage, not a nerf-call here, just a AS/sneak damage comparison)
my adv is also using a 5% vision enhancer as well as usually going flurry before i sneak just in case (ql 145-150 flurry, or somewhere in that range, requires 68x in fast attack).
just for the hell of it (as i have plenty of IPR points on my NT, and missile pistols have lost a good deal of their fun in pvp after the crit reduction) i'm going to switch my NT over to a snakemaster just to test AS on him with the lower attack rating, but the reason i posted this was mainly to see if you guys had any input on this, as it's confusing me to a degree as i was under the impression that AS and Sneak were fairly straight-forward attack skills, as in your skill unlocking a higher max crit multiplier as your skill increased, at least i noticed a steady increase on my adv as i got sneak from 500ish up to the point it is now.
