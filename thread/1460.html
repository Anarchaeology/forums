Regurgitation pt. 1
Resurrection shock fills his body and he drops to the floor with a thud. His throat contracts and he starts to get dry heaves. The insurance terminal never resurrects you with a full stomach. Cursing and hammering his fist into the ground he slowly rises.
A few minutes later at Bronto Burger he sits and awaits as the nanosurge from the microscopic nanobots leaves his body. He feels queasy and the Bronto Cheese he ate feels uncomfortable in his stomach. He waits and gradually his sense regain their normal state.

He takes a quick glance down his chest and legs. All limbs accounted for. The quiet humming of a Yalmaha passing by him overhead. The smell of Omni 1 pollution control nanobots filled his nostrils. He removes his Nadir Steel Ribbed helmet and draws a deep breath. This is the third time within 30 minutes he had been killed. Wandering towards the Grid terminal he goes through to motions and tries to figure out what to try now, seeing as his last attempts had failed miserably. He sits down next to the grid terminal and holds his head in his hands for a long time.
After a time he looks up and draws a small holodisc out of his pocket. He inserts it into an OT HDplayer and starts studying the the outlines of blue cylinders and boxes that slowly rotats counterclockwise about 20 centimetres above the HDunit.

Small jittery movements are the only visual sign that he is alive He sits for a long time absorbing, every detail, every little Nook and crevice in the cylinders. His mind is searedby the blue light. The plans are almost burned into his memory.

He pushes a button on the deck and the Image of the blue cylinders disappears, leaving the faint glimmer in his field of vision, as a mirage thats there and yet not. He rises, very determined and quickly lets himself get digitalized into the grid.
The transfer into the grid always feels a little uneasy for him but the following disembodied sensation quickly replaces the discomfort and it is at these times he feels the very best.

Crossing vast deserts in seconds, traversing high mountainranges with the slightest effort of will. He can visit Tir and be back in Rome within 2 minutes. He loves this sensation and at times when he is really focused on his mission it seems like he can feel the incredible speed with which he is travelling. This is one of those times.

His node comes up and he slowes down. He initialises his run and starts his gridcracker. This part had succeeded the first 3 times sofar, the tricky part is to get the security system deactivated and do the run within the set timeframe. He uploads the access code and waits for the green light to come on.
It does.

He materializes into a three foot drop and starts his run. This is the time he feels most alive. This is what he is here to do. This is the way he will atone for his sin. This is the only time he can forget it.

This time he will get it right.

Regurgitation pt. 2
The adrenaline is pounding through his body he can feel his heart beating fast, the snakegenes are kicking in, his refelexes are fast his eyes constantly twistshcing from side to side, up and down. The makers of the opifex race really came through. He praises his maker as he dodges a laser movement sensor by mere milimetres. Hiding behind a file cabinet he recalls the schematics. The blue hologram stil burning in his memory. He knows this place. He was here before. The alarms are still silent. He hasn't ben spotted. The clan diversionist in the next room who saw him the last time is certain that he got his man.

He silently watches from a shadow as the clanner pulls out a thermos of coffee. He checks his implanted HUD for a threat assessment, Dark Orange. This is not good. He recalls the schematics. He has to get through the room to get further in.

The nanosurge pulses through his arms as he forces the clanners NCU to run "No Escape Possible", as soon as it is in effect he jumps up and bolts through the room. Two or three bullets imbed into the wal behind him but he is unscathed, for now.

He dodges the bullets of yet another guard. This one, a fat atrox, is not really a threat to him but he know that he is mere minutes away from the alarms sounding ad he better make the best of those minutes. He methodically searches each room for the Sensitive Information Module. The grid surfer nano reinforcing his leg muscles putting his clocked runspeed somewhere in the late 90s kilometers an hour.

He jumps and dodges the assault of a nearby trader. The shotgun ripping out a rather large chunk of wall where his head had been a moment earlier. The trader then tries to slow him down. He feels the tingling as the nanobots in him works to counter the hostile nanoprogramme.

Room by room he searches. He feels a sting in his side where he was hit twice by a rogue Scooplet. Damn things are supposed to be on his side. Omni Tek material hitting him like crazy. A mystery to most OT personnel who met them in a mission. Must be crappy programming from their makers. And the thing is fast. Right on his tail.

He enters a room with a long table. Chairs are lined up on each side. Seems to be a meeting room. At the end there's a door. According to his map and the schematics this is the last room he needs to explore. A big hulking Enforcer is coming at him swinging a mean looking club. The way to the last room is blocked by the bulking for of the atrox.

He jumps on to the table and runs right past him. A sharp crack followed by a blinding pain in his leg tells him he has been hit. The last 8 metres he jumps in three jumps on one leg. Throws himself into the room. There it is. The Information Module he grabs it in one hand and activates his Reckless Digitization.

In 6 seconds he'll be in the grid. In 6 seconds he will safe and on his way home. The clanners jumps him. Hitting him over and over and as the nanobots starts to disassemble he feels the Scooplet biting hard into his spine and his legs going numb. In ancient days he would have been paralysed. He feels the familiar nuisance an fights the urge to vomit as he is encoded into the grid.

Once inside he feels no pain he knows if he steps out of the grid he will be in severe pain, but now he just feels disassembled. Free.

This sensation is the ultimate freedom for him.

He needs to drop off the Information module. This means he needs to leave the grid. But first he needs to restore his health. The treatment kits are working their magic. Had he been outside he would have felt them resetting his spine and patching up the wounds. In here he feels nothing. He has a general idea as to how long it takes for the bots to fix the damage done.

After a while he exits the grid through the ent node. Quickly removing his helmet and breathing the air. Air which is very polluted but at this point smells very fresh to him.

He starts to walk back to the mission terminal.

It turned out to be a good day after all.

But then again its not over yet.

Regurgitation pt. 3
The nanobots in ent gives the air that very odourless smell, but it seems as if there is something lying beneath it. Some long forgotten scent hidden, like a wallpainting is hidden between layers of white paint. Just because you can't see it doesn''t mean its not there. It is the same as the feeling of being followed.

He wasn't aware of it for a long time, but the sensation crept into his head slowly. He continues as if nothing has happened trying to pinpoint the sensation. He'd tried it before and if he concentrates he can almost sense where it comes from. At BB in ent he stops to see if any of the streetmerchants has anything of value and maybe to sneak a peak at his tail.

Haggling over a piece of Worn Obtru Steel Ribbed gloves he catches a glimpse of a thick moustache and a bald head. Its there for only a second and he'd have thought nothing about it if he hadn't seen it before. Maybe not. Maybe he'd have known it was the guy following him if he was in the middle of Omni Trade at peak hours. He smiles and gestures to the street merchant. "60K! No more!" He gets the armorpants and moves on. Nothing else catches his interest and the bald head is nowhere to be seen.

The sound of the whompah as he passes through to Rome is a clear indicator as to where it got its name. The park is beautiful, birds singing, the lake rolling slowly against the sides of the pool. And for the briefest of moments he has shaken the feeling of being observed. He relishes in the moment, knowing that it will soon return. No doubt about that. And surely enough. After a short while the eyes are upon him again. He scans the area around the whompah, but only the guard is there and a couple of young Metaphysicists experimenting with their nanos. He looks at the meat balloon that materializes as the young girls angrily starts to shout at what appears to be her boyfriend. The pale face with the eyes sown shut flashes red for an instant,then returns to the normal deathlike pallor and the boyfriend smiles and claps his hands. The meat balloon just hangs there drawing deep rasping breaths.

He moves a little away, winking at the young meta girl. Just because its not excactly his kind of preference he can flirt a bit. Besides the innocent anger shown had made him forget the sensation of being followed. Even if it was only for a bit he had enjoyed it. He knows very well that when he sees the bald head again thats when the real trouble is going to begin. He contemplates the different course of actions. he could make a runner for it, but it would only postpone it. He could try to locate him again, but hed most certainly fail. Instead he decides to sit down and wait. He sits on the ledge of the pool and watches the water. Thinking about nothing in particular, mostly preparing for the coming meeting, time slowly passes and after 20 minutes or so an hand falls on his shoulder. He grabs it and in a movement he gets up, turns around while still hanging on to the hand. The punch hits him square in the chest and the hand he was hanging on to for dear life is suddenly not there anymore. He hits the surface of the water with a large splash before even being aware of having been hit. He knows better. He is a Fixer, no match agaist a trained agent. He never stood a chance.

A large hand with black hairs on the back of it is stretched out towards him offering help to get out of the pool.
Following the arm at the end of the hand he sees the large Eyebrows and the thich moustache. For a bald guy he has a lot of facial hairs. He grabs the hand and gets out of the pool.

Major! He nods.

Regurgitation pt. 4
The thick mustache is bobbing up and down as he is talking. The Major is a big man. The military career is evident. Broad frame and muscular arms. But the intelligence is equally apparant. He is talking calmly and selfsecure. Not too rushedly. Like he is comtemplating every word. Maybe if you had never met him before you would think he is slow witted but that is not the case. He had learned this in the early days of his coming here. The major had dragged him through basic training and retired soon after. He still came to some of his former men when he had different tasks that he needed performed. Sometimes he came to Enigma. This was apparantly one of those times.

They stepped a bit away from the two Metas and the Omni Pol guard and did the run down. Apparantly someone was out to get Henry Radiman when he was to meet Philip Ross here in Omni 1 later that week. The Major looked at him and asked gravely: Do you have any ideas what this would do to the state of this planet, where the assassins successful? He had a pretty good idea of what WOULD happen. The Council would falter and the different clans might either start warring each other. Or they would more likely launch a fullscale assault at Omni. And with the current rift in Omni Tek they would likely be succesful. Noone could tell what the black alliance where up to.

Or how they would respond to an attack.

The major was apparantly very concerned about the possibility that they would attack and The Black Alliance would show their true colors. They had on many occasions ben branded traitors. Maybe the Major thought they were. He didnt know. But it seemed like it.

He got his orders and headed back towards his apartment. Plans were forming in his head. He was at work again.

But it would be a few days before he would hear anything, but when the call came it was on very short notice.

Regurgitation pt. 5
The report had been vague at best. The transmission had been lacking a lot. If he hadn't been so preoccupied with the events that were talking place he might had run it through a filter. He was too lazy to actually do anything about it. He had just upped and left. He had called Kirrana, Chimaeri and Rienna. No need to disturb all the troops. It seemed like a wild goosechase anyways. Now he knows better and he wont be caught off guard like this again.

To sum up what had happened in Newland: They had arrived and seen nothing. Chimaeri and Rienna had followed their targets with the Professionality that marks them. As priests they have a tendency to seem, transparant. Himself and Kirrana had stayed back and when the hooded figure arrived he had drawn their attention away from what they were looking for. The man had shot and killed Pilker. He cursed his inability to prevent it, but what was worse, their real target had come and gone undiscovered.

The dropoff had been made, they had failed, and now as he is running towards an Office in Omni HQ he curses his laziness. He should have known better, but you learn by default. He would never let this happen again.

The elevator opened up. Snugglebuns and Rienna was observing the building, Chimaeri was controlling the lockdown, He and Kirrana stops by the door. He signals to her to wait. The lock on the door poses little security and they pass through. The hallway is clear. Eventhough it is late the building is alive. You can always feel, when a building is completely empty. There was people around, just nowhere around here.

They sneak slowly down the hall and end up outside the office, he pulls his gun and signals Kirrana, three.....two....one. She dives inside and he follows they quickly scan the room in a rehearsed movement. It takes a full second or so and they are sure they are alone in the room.

Its a small office and the body lying on the floor takes up a rather large amount of floorspace. The smell of cigars unmistakable and only slightly unpleasant. he kneels by the corpse and checks for a pulse. Shes dead. Recent. Still warm though. He scans the room, noone in here, he glances out the window at the fountains. He touches his wristcom. Snuggles, pull up the security camera video for Rheess door. I want to know when Jackkal left this office.

Hes turning every possibility, every outcome, in his head. And when the message comes back hes surprised nevertheless. He never left sir, hes still there! He starts scanning the office. Bullocks, theres no one here other than Kirrana and I. He looks at Kirrana. Check the...! He stops midsentence. Kirrana, you said he was here for an interview?

Yes Bax. That was his cover.

He turns the thought in his head. Slowly looking at the furniture again. Why then would Rhees not set him somewhere to sit in front of her desk?

he nods toward the overturned chair Kirrana looks at it and frowns. They look at each other Then she slowly looks upward toward the vent.

In a millisecond relization hits him, the full scale of what is happening becoming clear. The seemingly needless death of Rhees. The obscene amounts of Credits being transferred. it all adds up. The message received hinted at it, but he had thought that something of this scale would never be pulled off.

Damn" she says. "We have a gopher!

They bolts towards the door. They want Radiman.
