Broken shores is the most convenient place to reach to do missions at high levels, far more convenient than any other spot. It has a save terminal right near the missions and you can grid right from where you get the mission into broken shores where you complete the mission.

It wouldn't be the end of the world if we couldn't do missions in Broken Shores. We'd all get missions to the Mort outpost instead and we'd be pissed that we have to waste our time flying all the way out there for no real reason except that the 5% of the people who engage in PvP get catered to and not the 95% that dont.

Change the Mort outpost and we'd find some other place right near a save term in a 75% zone to gain xp. Then when all those places were gone, we'd quit because its a pain in the rear end to have to take 10 mins at the end of every mission to go save then fly back to the hunt spot.

The customers here are not hardcore PvPers. No mainstream U.S.-market MMORPG has ever attracted a lot of people with hardcore PvP. Those that do have any availability of hardcore PvP find that the majority of customers have no interest in it. A real good way to sink a game is to cater to the hardcore PvPers and forget about the majority of the paying customer base.
