Interesting Agent Ideas
I see a lot of complaints about Agent development. Although I havent ventured into that class just yet, I was thinking about it and had a few interesting ideas. So far I can see that the most distinctive abilities of the Agent are Aimed Shot and the Sneaking ability. Stealth and Conealment I think seem like the main focus. I was also thinking, that people such as Agents in the AO world are those unknown factors behind the linds. I think that they should be a little more on the assassin type of persona. Not only hitting you from far range with a well aimed shot, but being able to be very quick with weapons like katanas or daggers. Now this may be a bit on the fantasy side, it seems to fit the idea though I think. Elite type of class, very skilled in sneaky ways to kill. Hopefully my idea is being conveyed how I see it. Add a little more excitement to that class. Just my 2 cents.
