cause it doesnt work that way... pretty much at no point in the history of humanity is a small extremist group able to stand up against the might of an army when said army has the popular support of the people... especially when the main thing holding back the firepower of the army is the desire to avoid collatoral damage of unintended property/uninvolved civilians. The more firepower that gets used by the extremists the less popular they become with the populace until you have a situation where your extremist groups are so hated by the populace they are pointed out to athorities and/or killed assuming they don't hold the entire populace in abject fear and terror... which as said above grows increasingly difficult as your populace begins to realize your extremist polocies are worse than the fear you may instill in them.

required reading for anyone asking why the extremist rogue's can't just attempt to take power by force from well funded armies that hold popular support:
The art of war, Sun Tzu
The Prince, Machiavelli

Supa Leet
To answer both mrbunny's posts, when the populace doesnt want you there and would rather endure the concequences of fighting you off, there is really very little you can do. You can level entire cities and keep control for a short while... but in doing so you lose not only whatever support you had left in the populace but will likely start to lose support from your own people and allies.

I made it quite clear to point out that an extremist faction is unable to overpower an army supported by the people, and that the main thing holding back that army and it's tactics was keeping the people's support. Once the people's support has left and turned against you, the only things keeping you in power is fear... which as pointed out only lasts to a point.
