The life of a Neutral HAS always been a hard life......this change doesn't suddenly *make* it hard, as you imply. What it does, is in one fell swoop, it takes away the thing that Neutrals(or, at least, I) cherish the most.......freedom of mobility. And, of course, being actively shunned when looking for a team at times. Yeah, now THERE's a really nice feeling. Staying Neutral requires more discipline, determination, TIME, and just plain strength of character than you could ever know if you've never played one to high levels. And above all, never give up.
Until now, guards have never directly aggroed us.......but, until an NPC is gray *it* WILL, and then the guards jump in as well. As one of the very few high-level Neutrals, this freedom of mobility is my RIGHT.......I HAVE EARNED IT. The ONLY areas Neutrals don't really have a right to expect to go unmolested(nice word choice there ) are Omni HQ, and the CoT/CoA areas imho.
Now, looking at it from the RP/gameplay perspective.......Omni is the easy road, Clan is in the middle, and Neutral is the hard road. Hmmm, who's the fence-sitter again?

Originally posted by Gaute Godager


The role of the neutrals in the story has been the subject of much debate within Funcom. Some feel that the neutrals are essential to the story, and that they will play a part in deciding which side wins. In many ways the neutrals are the victims of the conflict. If or when they rise and ally themselves with one of the opposing sides, maybe the balance will change, and the outcome of the conflict decided?

There are actually quite a few advantages of being neutral today. As a neutral, you may go to all cities without being afraid of getting killed. You may shop in all trade-skill shops, and thus have cheap and ready access to all implants and other trade-skill items. You may listen in on both the other sides' out of character channels, making it easier to find teams, knowledge, and goods to trade.

Of course, there are many disadvantages, too. As a neutral, you do not have access to most of the clan and Omni-Tek shops, you will be hunted by sided monsters, you may not attack first in political PvP areas, and so on. Life as a neutral isn't a dance on roses.
No....life as neut was never intended to be easy, and most neuts I know are proud of that fact. And living between two parties at the brink of open warfare will get even thougher as the war escalates, this is something all neutrals will have to deal with ...so to all of you who choose to stay neutral, all respect, but don't complain when things doesn't go your way.

One of your advantages are temporarely taken away due to a change in storyline....If most of you neuts are such great roleplayers as most of you claim to be (and those I know ingame have proven to be) you should be thrilled about this.
Not complain that you are being nerfed...
