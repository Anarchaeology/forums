OOC: Looking for rpg'ers to play important role in event
OOC:
I am the event coordinator of my guild. I am currently looking for two people to play the main non-guild roles in an upcoming event and several others may be needed. I am looking for clan/neutral/omni people capable of faring a conversation and be threatening. And I dont mean in an adolescent way ("You suck and I'll blast your head off"), but a calm, dark manner meant to scare and emphasize the seriousness of the conversational subject.

You must be logged on to AO every day, as I may need you on short notice. It's preferrable you're European, or at least play on european times (GMT +- 1-3 hours). Every race and profession is welcome, and there is no level restrictions. Most likely there will be no combat, but if that seems to be the only solution to the problems presented in the event, it would be nice if you were able to rally up some high level people to fight it out with my guildmates in a PvP zone.

I really cant say more, but if you are interested in helping out, please contact me on PM via this forum, and I will tell you more about the role you will take on.

Thank you
