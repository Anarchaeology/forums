((Bars/night-club are usually a good place to start. There are several of them all over the world, although some are more 'visited' than others. Omni-Ent has Rompa's and Baboon's, Omni-Forest has the Dancing Atrox, Stret West Bank has Reet's Retreat, West Athen has The Cup... Two other bars in Tir (Happy Rebel and Enjoy It while it lasts), and a nifty... uhhm, little bar in Hope

Also, if you wish to attend paties, check GridStream Productions announcements - on these baords or at http://www.gridstream.com . They schedule parties at Reet's, sometimes in other places too. Meeting people there isn't that hard; then, once you know more, being RPing or simply 'chatty' isn't a problem.

And btw - welcome to AO ^_^))

There are definately places like that. Reets is very popular but you have to check the GSP schedule. Friday night US is very popular night at Reets. Also check these forums for special events.

On the clan side on RK1 the Cup in West Athens is very social. Its right next to the grid so its relatively safe for Omnis as well. Certain backyards are very social too because there is a bank and surgery clinic in them. You have to find the right ones, though. West Athen Backyard 4 right next to the cup is popular with my guild.

As far as roleplay versus social, I've found that the two mix freely in this forum and on world as long as you aren't talking about blatantly OOC topics.
