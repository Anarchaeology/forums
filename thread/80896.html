((Party Tonight)) Meanwhile at Reets...
Epicine hoists a very large PA speaker into position in the rafters of the Rumble Room. It hangs at an hangle to the hulking Atrox, who seems to be straining somewhat to keep it there.

"How's this?!" Epicine shouts down.

Tarryk look up from his playlist generator and virtual turntables to look at the speaker for a moment.

"That's good, right there."

Epicine bolts the PA into place, and finally flops down on the support beam to take a break.

"Are we getting paid for this?" Epicine asks.

Tarryk speaks while going back to work on his sound board. "Course we don't get paid for this. Pretty meager tips too."

"Then why the hell are we doing it?!"

Tarryk looks up, and regards Epicine for a moment before replying. "Because we're gimp fools who love to party."

"Oh. Right."

Tarryk slaps a large red button on the console. It lights up with a time counter.

FRIDAY THE 13TH POWERBASH
LOCATION: RUMBLE ROOM (RAFTER) IN REET'S RETREAT
STRET WEST BANK - RK1
PARTY BEGINS AT 8PM EST / 1AM GMT

Epicine sees the readout. "Um...Friday the 13th? I didn't even realize..."

A creak...a snap...the PA Speaker falls, along with Epicine, to a screaming demise at the floor of the Rumble Room.

Tarryk looks at the mess on the floor as Epicine is wisked off to reclaim.

"Gonna be a long night."
