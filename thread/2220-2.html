Well, I for one am screaming for peace. As a retired Major of the ICC Expeditionary Force, I've had my share of foxholes and real wars. An Omni-Tek vs Clan war? No thank you. Just let me tend to my tailor shop and leave me in peace.

And regarding the earlier comments about why make such a fuss if people can just scan themselves? Well, I have enough credits to do that on a regular basis, but I don't recall my Randa's (my niece) birthday anymore just because an accident happened when I was making some modifications on a pair of Energized armor pants.
