The ability to pull soloes got nothing to do with the profession of the character you're playing. It is simply about player skills...

The pulling singles 101:

1 - Get the people and monster map upgrades. Still doable without, but much easier with.
2 - Try and see the mobs pattern and wait until you got one on it's own close to the door. If they are always close together, do your pull when only one of them is facing you.
3 - The tricky part, may take some practice. Enter the room and pull back as soon as you get one of them to aggro you. Do _not_ attack until you are both back in the entry room and the door is closed.

After doing this some times, it will work on at least nine out of ten attempts. Some have been having problems after 14.0, but I haven't had any trouble with it.

Okay, I finally figured out what was different with the 14.0 patch, so here's the trick:

You have to pull 1 mob to the main mission room, and then step to the side. This causes the mob's flight path to change, and it will stop and fight you, instead of running back into the main room to get its friends. I never had this problem pre-14.0, I always pulled to main room and stood smack-dab in front of the entrance door (to the mission, not entrance to main room) - that way I could zone instantly if need be. Well, pre-14.0, the mob I pulled would come in, stop, and start fighting me. Post-14.0, the mob I pulled would come in, hand me an IOU, run away, and then return with 2 or 3 of his buddies.

So you see, the problem I was encountering wasn't with pulling only 1 mob - I can do that almost every single time, piece of cake, even when I'm half asleep. The problem was getting that 1 mob to stay in the frelling main room w/out running away like a little sissy NPC coward that they are...

Give this a try, it will probably work for you I hope. Just takes some practice.
