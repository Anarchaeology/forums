Omni-Mining Statement on Dr. Verson
Greetings Citizens of Rubi-Ka,

After seeing the reactions of many after last weeks Science Symposium, the board of Omni-Mining has agreed to release a statement regarding information of Dr. Verson.

On Saturday November 22, 29477, agents from the private organization Vector Blue extracted a clan scientist named Dr. Verson from the Science Symposium hosted by Dabblez. This was done on request from Omni-Mining. The extraction was a success and thankfully with out firing any weapons.

There are a few reasons why this extraction was done during the Science conference. Due to the fact that Dr. Verson was under watch by more then just Rebel Clan guards, it would have made an extraction very difficult if it was done in clan areas. Thus we needed him to be on neutral soil to avoid confrontation from clan affiliated personnel.

Dr. Versons liberation caused him no harm and is in perfect health. He is in the process of finishing the paper work to become an Omni-Tek citizen. He will be working for Omni-Mining as a researcher in the research project Infinite Circle. I will not go into detail of this project.

Dr. Verson has told Omni-Mining personnel that he can finally rest after many stressful weeks. He looks forward to starting his job position in Omni-Mining.

That is all I have to say on this manner.

OOC:

Infinite Circle, what is it?

IC is a role playing alliance between the current orgs Omni-Mining, Omni-InterOps, R.U.R and Vector Blue. The aim of IC is to provide mutual support in aspects of role playing and non-role playing activities.

Infinite Circle itself in an in-character point of view is a huge Omni-Tek funded and highly classified research project that these orgs were contracted to do.

To read up more information about IC, go to this FAQ: http://vector-blue.com/icforums/viewtopic.php?t=55

We are always open to start up some RP with others, just contact Swiftmind, Dabblez, Demenzia or Ammicus.
