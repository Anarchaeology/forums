New meeting place in 20k
Just an idea,

Some players that I know do not like to Whompa from Trade to 20k because they find that they disconnect from the game.

In 20k on Rubi-Ka 1 all the players looking for teams seem to pile up in one area; around the mission booths and save terminal. My computer halts for a while, then I see the players load up and there may be a good 20 or 30 players in such a small area.

Now nearby is a shop. Inside this shop is a larger area called the Bar.

Wouldnt it be great if players started to use the bar to find teams, socialise, advertise their abilities for tips and chat. Not being technically minded about computers, surely this enclosed area with a larger space to congregate would create less strain on peoples computers? Also it means that shoppers can still shop in low lag conditions, as the bars seem to pop up as you enter the door.

This would be fair to players around level 55-110 with not so great connections or computers to have a chance at increasing their levels?

I have seen people complain about Funcom not making 20k easier, but perhaps this is the solution, that we change our meeting spot, then the area doesnt get adjusted to have lower hunting spots to make 20k less popular.

From now on, if I want a team, I will look in the bar first. I hope you do to!
