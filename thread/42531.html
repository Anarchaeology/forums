Every other level is total B.S. By the time you reach the level requirement, you're well beyond the ability requirements to equip it and are better served by something else of higher quality level.

Every single time I 'ding' to another god damned level, I look at my pile of So8 junk festering in the bank and compare it to what I've currently got on. Each time I see a piece that I can now where this level that I couldn't wear last level I chuckle and let it fester in the bank some more or just drop it on the ground and let it deteriorate.

And this coming from an MP, not an Enforcer or Agent with Str or Agi buffs.

This line of armor is the most assinine middle finger flipping at the skill system to come down the pike yet. I know hardly anyone who doesn't have a full set or two of various levels that they are fully capable of wearing with the exception of some abstract 'level' requirement. Hence it goes unused.

But, hit the magic 'level' of 180 and then by golly you'll be strong enough and agile enough and have paid homage to the all mighty game designers whence you will magically be granted the power to wear the friggen' stuff.
