GridStream Stands Down
OFFICIAL PRESS RELEASE

Over the past couple months, GridStream Productions has grown to literally become what we claim. We were the top source of live audio entertainment to the world of Rubi-Ka.

We continued to grow, we took on new DJs, we set new schedules and created new standards, and we tried our best to be a legitimate business within the confines of this world. And while our intentions were good, our fortitude was not.

We've had some serious problems within the guild lately, stemming from many different conflicts of interest, and it has all come from a singular aspect that we are just now starting to ascertain:

This is not just a game.

We overstepped our boundaries in thinking that we could maintain a stable structure and keep personal conflicts out of it. We wanted to create an atmosphere of a true business, and a full schedule.

For that, we paid the ultimate price. Several of our guild members have left, and those who stayed felt the aftershock rippling through the guild.

On that note, we are taking a vacation. GridStream Productions steps down. We will not be doing any live shows for the next week or so.

Our sincerest apologies goes out to everyone who has given us such immense support. We would not take this time off if we did not deem it absolutely neccessary, but we are sad to break away from such a great thing.

But we will be back. Count on it.
