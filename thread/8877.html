Scumbug, I'm with you all the way. The numbers game is one of the most original and fun parts of this game. Anybody who decides to hold themself back should not cry foul against those who learn how to play the game and spend the time to do so.

quote:
It is simply impossible to balance the game with such a great diversity in characters abilities.
I honestly hope you meant 'diversity in _professions_ abilities'. Balance between professions abilities is a fine goal to strive for. The fact that one _character_ can be more powerful than another of the same level and profession is one of the strongest points of this game. It shows the stronger character's willingness to study the rules, gather equipment, network with other players, and reap the rewards.

The last thing I want to see is more of that DAoC garbage where every character of a particular class and level is within 1% of every other character of the same class/level. That homogenized pile of oatmeal was a mecca for those who wanted to prove their 'power' by spending more time and leveling.

In this game a lvl50 of a certain profession can very likely kill a lvl70 of that very same profession. It all depends on knowledge, network, and effort. That's a good thing.

Once again, let me reiterate that if there are disparities between professions in terms of overall gameplay enjoyment, then by all means address these inequities. Don't destroy the game in the process by dragging everyone down to the same boring lowest common denominator.
