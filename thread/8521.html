Whompaa locations to bring AO to a stop?
We all know AO gets a bit laggy when there is lot of players in one location. We all also know this is most due to that fact that almost no 2 players look the same.

My concern comes after last weeks 'bombs' in Tir. There was a lot of ppl there to watch what was going on, this caused some major lag but worst off all made it impossible to zone.

I think the location of the Athen and the Newland whopaas will be a major problems as the population of Rubi-Ka grow. Any travelling from Tir to Avalon needs to pass Newland and Athen. The Whompas at both places are crowded and laggy and this will not be better as more players join AO.

I think the world designers should look into this now before travelling on Rubi-Ka becomes a nightmare.

I know that you can aslo use the grid, but whompaa travel is faster and does'nt have any req. The gird are fairly empty now.

Either there should be more grid locations or the whompaas need to be replaced before they evolve into lag heaven.

Lag is part of life
I think of lag as the game version of RL crowding. We don't have to worry about sitting in traffic in game since we can run through each other so the lag, for me, represents city overcrowding and traffic jams. It makes going for out hunting in some relatively quiet area to running through a city really feel like I've come back "roughing it".

Off course, there are two big issues with that. One is that sometimes it's just as laggy in relatively empty zones like Clon**** so going to town isn't any difference from being in the great outdoors. The second is that, yeah, this is a game and not real life so we dont' want real life headaches.

Anyway, that's how I see it and it works pretty well for me most of the time. It sure helps cut down on stress for me.

I adjust my view setting when heading into town
If you aren't doing this you should consider doing. Set Player View to minimum and the general view slider to rather low ... maybe at 15.

For some reason the whompa area of Newland is very laggy even when nobody is there. I haven't figured that one out yet. But people are usually there and having player view setting at zero helps a lot.

After running through a congested area or several towns (even at low view setting) you should consider relogging once you get into the wild. This will clear memory of all those building and player graphics. This will speed the responsiveness of AO quite a bit.
