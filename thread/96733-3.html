Plan B
It wasn't a big cave, and the creeping roots that burst through the roof made it even more claustrophobic. That, and the number of men and materials that crowded the available space.

The sound of frenzied activity, of preparing weapons and armor, of confused and muted talking, of comraderie, was drowned out by the huge exclamations and roars of annoyance coming from the big old Atrox.

Miner Bill, in one last roar of rage, hurled the old comm unit across the cavern. It missed a tubby opifex and shattered against the irregular rock wall. "She has gone! The bloody wench! After all the money...!"

Silence. Silence as the Atrox stalked across the room and inspected the small array of weapons left unused. The tension rose.

It was a long haired solitus who first spoke. "Bill, we can still go on without a mercenary. We have the guns you know... we can still make a go of it."

Bill grunted. "Perhaps. And what would you strike? The programme has cascaded into a virus like you said. No one is listening to us!"

"They are, Bill? Change takes time..." The Solitus drifted off, unable to stare down the hulking Atrox who continued to smoulder like a slow burning fuse.

"The mercenary has buggered off! And my little criminal chit has vanished!" Bill hissed and began fiddling with a pistol. He looked like he was about to use it on someone.

"Actually, Bill..." it was the tubby opifex, rubbing the back of his neck carefully. "I did manage to hack her comm. I downloaded the info she pilfered for you... thought you might need it." He gulped as with three swift strides, Bill ambled otwards him with the subtlety of an avalanche. Whimpering slightly, the opifex pulled out the documents he had retrieved. They were snatched from his grasp.

"Good man!" Bill lay out the documents -plans of buildings. "Now we have something achievable. Gather round..." He sighed heavily. "I had hoped to gas the bastards while they lectured the next generation -but that option is dead now. So this is what we will do... we will sting them in their temple, and we will raze it to the ground!"

It was with quiet interest that the small gathering crowded round the Atrox and his detailed blueprints. His heavy, thick voice took on a hypnotic quality as he outlined his proposal. My God, Miner Bill thought to himself, I feel like I'm back being the foreman again. Smiling inwardly, he forged his few followers into a work gang with a mission.

Lyricia
Engineer Professional
Lyricia released the last batch of nanobots into the air of the Tir School of Engineering's central courtyard. They glowed briefly as they floated toward every centimeter of wall, floor, and overhang in the area. The glow faded as they settled in place. The new coating of nanobots was now invisible to the naked eye, as intended.

After receiving an architectural overview of the School, Lyricia suspected that the NLM planned to bomb the School. Her plea for help to the Sentinels fell on deaf ears. The Sentinels had no sympathy for the School after Lyricia denied them an opportunity to build a barracks by purchasing the property. A bomb in Tir would also give the Sentinels the perfect platform to rally the Clan's around their cause.

This didn't stop Lyricia. "Most people look at a problem as something to avoid or struggle to overcome" She had always explained to new students "An Engineer looks at a problem as a playground for the mind. A chance to let your ingenuity and imagination run wild". She had decided to solve this problem by building something and she did. By lunchtime she had programmed and released nanobots that would coat all the surfaces of the School. These nanobots would pulse out from the walls occasionally and search for the chemical components of 55 known explosives. The nanobots were also programmed to recognize an antimatter bomb, Lyricia didn't want a repeat of the Dust Brigade bombing of Omni-1. These nanobots could even spot other nanobots assembling explosives from nearby components. This was a concern after the infamous "Nano-bombing" incident on Omni Prime in 28717 in which nanobots pulled molecules from nearby buildings, the air, and even people to create and detonate cyclotrimethylene-trinitramine (C3H6N6O6). "I hope I didn't miss anything" Lyricia thought to herself.

There was one final thing to do. Lyricia needed to test the system. She held up a Rubirango-sized can containing an explosive called Tetrytol. She paused a moment and thought about crossing her fingers. "Superstitions are for Atroxs and children" She thought and broke the seal on the container. In less then a second a synthesized voice in her comm said "Explosive compound: Tetrytol detected in central courtyard". She smiled and put the container away.

With the system in place it was time to warn Dabblez. "Dabblez, please contact me once you receive this" She spoke "I've discovered something about the NLM. They may be monitoring our comms so I will tell you more in person."

Stranger in the Sands
Keep Moving... one foot... other foot... keep moving...
The twin suns burned the sky a pastel hue of red and orange, while the air itself was grained yellow with the blasted sands of the Wailing Waste. In sand storms like this, even the great Prowlers that hunted in these parts looked for shelter.

A man struggled over the dunes, tumbling, bumping, crashing into the low scrub and with sobbing breaths. He is a stranger here; alone and without support or aid. He doesn't even ware any desert survival gear.

Tall and lean he is, this man bracing against the biting storm. His grey hued skin is blotched and broken with cuts, sores, abrassions and bruises. He staggers on, numbly; he staggers south across the dunes, blind to the camps nearby that offer sanctuary from the storm -blind to the river so close by.

Why is he here? He forgets for the moment. He must make for the south. South is safety. Away from his pursuers. He has information against them -but what was it?

His head hurts; his body aches.
A lone man, marching south through the sand storm. No one sees him pass by.
Maybe that is for the best?
