They rearranged the Romes (Blue especially) because of this, back when they used to be croweded from people going to/from Galway Shire. But back then I never crashed in crowded areas. This was during the time of launch-hell, as well.

Now I never whompa into 20k from Trade -- I always ferry there from Harry's because if I crash from the whompa, I may have trouble logging in for a few minutes or HOURS. I get dropped to the desktop over and over again. Sometimes this happens in BS, but not to the same extent.

A little bit of advice for those having this same problem:

Maybe this is just a worthless voodoo ritual, but when I'm having the login trouble (happens when I log out in 20k like an idiot -- can't get back in), I tend to re-log in with ALL settings turned down. All textures turned to low (I run with max settings) and my screen resolution down to the lowest possible x 16. It might even be wise to turn view distance, etc. down, too. But I don't bother with that.

I think this helps me get back on faster, but again, it might be psychosomatic.
