I think many of you are forgeting that Neutrals are their to give people a chance to deside what side they want to join. That was the idia in the first place. Hypos had it right. Its suposed to be hard to be Neutral, and they never intended people to stay Neutral long. They figured some would stay Neutral but most would choose a side. Asking for things to be fair is reasonable but you also need to remember that being Neutral is not easy, you get to choose a side but neather side realy trusts you till you sign the paper. Who knows if you are just being nice to give the Clans or Omni info on the other side? If you choose to stay Neutral the whole time then thats your choice and that voice does need to be heard to some dagree. But it should never be a large voice and not a faction at all. Funcom wanted this game to be a war between the Clans and Omni, not Clans vs Omni with Neutrals attacking whom ever they want. Not that what you want is to be able to do that but the resault is similer. Play Neutral for a while and get the hang of the game, then choose a side if you don't like the pains of not being trusted. That was their intent with Neutrals. As for the rest, its debatable and I think funcom is doing a good job of keeping with their original idia, for the most part.

No hostility here just answering the question to the best of my ability and knowlage.
