Troubled dreams
I held the still warm Seburo in my shaking hands, gasping for air.

All around me in the forest lay the bullet riddled corpses of Omni Tek grunts, probably still fresh from boot camp. Other than the sound of my heart pumping rapidly, everything was quiet.

Then why did I still feel that someone was around?

I quickly salvaged their dog tags, as apparently some clanner gave you a reward or something. Naturally I pocketed what little credits they had, still trying to get over the fact that I was ambushed by five of them, yet I survived.

Barely survived, that is.

I leaned against a tree, looking at the destruction and death I caused.

That's when I noticed I had an audience. An audience wearing an InternOp uniform. (And she looked kind of cute too, for a blonde lady that looked like an emotional killer)

Still gasping for air, I asked her, "So, why am I not dead yet?"

She gave a short, cold laugh. Then she grimly looked at me and slowly stated, "To be honest, if I had my way, you would be dead by now. But apparently someone in High Command knows you, Haiiro, and wants you alive."

...someone knows me? Definetly not good, especially when I don't know myself.

"You know, that raises even more questions..."

"And you'll have to learn to live without the answers. I've been told to give you this. Do with it what you will, but remember, who ever your friend is in Omni Tek, I think it would not be wise for you to refuse this gift." Her right hand twitched slightly over her holstered gun. "That will be all."

With that, she placed something on the ground, and briskly walked off, disappearing amongst the trees.

I walked towards the object, rifle ready.

...it was an Omni Tek application form.

Beyond control
Later on, I found myself sitting on top of a the shattered remains of some vehicle in Wartorn Valley, brooding...

I pulled off my Nomad helmet, noting the recent battle scars it received. I felt naked without a helmet on, but then again, how am I supposed to get drunk with it on?

Wishing myself long health, I took a swig of Setting Sun Sake, and began to examine my two recent prizes.

The Watcher of Tir ring, and the Omni-Tek application form.

Couldn't have both, obviously. And of course, both seemed unappealing to me.

It all was a question of which one I disliked more. Restrictive order, or insane chaos.

Lightning struck off in the distance, and I'm uncomfortably reminded that I'm on top of a very metal structure. But then again, I have insurance. I already feel like hell, what's the worse thing that could possible happen.

I held up the form between my fingers, while drinking sake with the left hand.

So, someone knew me, and undoubtably knows about my past. I wonder if it's...

PAIN!

I grasped my head in overwhelming pain... hurts... to remember.... to remember...

Next thing I know, I'm lying on the muddy ground, head throbbing in intense agony, the pouring rain doing nothing to cool my anguish.

I slowly get up, wondering where the hell that bottle of sake dropped, then I realised... I dropped the form.

Despite the pain, I stumbled about the ruins, desperately looking... and then...

And then I felt a sudden desire to throw up when I saw the form in the hands of a clanner guard. And with my luck... yeah, he's one of the Sentinels.

Big guy too, for a Solitus. Moves fast, I thought as I was swiftly knocked to the ground.

"Listen, ah'd kill you right now, but we're going to have a nice little chat with my group, y'hear scum?" he hissed as he clutched my thoat.

"Would you believe I have to idea where that came from?" I choked.

He held a pistol to my head.

"I'll take that as a no."

Having said that, I kicked him off of me, and leaped on top of him, trying to keep his bloody gun from killing me, or worse, wounding me.

He knocked me aside with his fist, roaring in anger, his gun disappearing in the dark.

We both got up at the same time, and then glared at each other.

The rain poured down.

He charged. I stood my ground, waiting...

When he was almost on me, I quickly whisked out a slender black knife from my sleeve, sidestepped him, and rammed it into the back of his neck.

I didn't wait to see if he was dead or paralysed. I quickly picked up the fallen form, retrieved my helmet and gun, and sprinted as fast as I could to Old Athen.

The only constant in the universe...
Despite my fears, I finally found myself at Adromeda. Neutral territory... if you could call anyone neutral.

Everyone has to pick a side sometime. Looks like the decisions has been made for me.

I lean back against the wall, a nameless statue towering over me. It's probably for the best. From what I gathered, the clans definetly don't need me. Course, by now they probably hate me, not that an Omni citizenship is going to help matters.

I hesitate...

Thinking of what the future may bring...

...I sigh.

Goodbye clans, hello Omni-Tek. I held the now completed form in my hand, wondering what to do now.

"Hello Haiiro."

Startled, I look up to see someone in a white uniform standing beside me. He continued speaking, "Well, now we're both shocked in some way. I take it you're keeping your helmet on, yes?"

He dumped a bag at my feet.

"You will find that there is a full suit of Omni Pol armour. Forest camo. There's also a commader's cloak in there. Yours, if the Corporation is pleased with your actions."

I stand up, hefting the bag onto my back. "And what actions do you expect me to do?"

"All in good time. For now, I expect you explore your new home. Oh, and do not accept certain responsibilities too quickly. Your first obligation is to me. Do not forget that, and never, ever mention it to anyone."

"Need to know basis, right?"

He seemed to smile, but then, the night can play tricks with your eyes.

Well, that's what I'm hoping, as his smile didn't exactly seem too friendly.
