Shops and Shopping changes
While the official envisioned list: http://community.anarchy-online.com/...ion/envisioned ,has some nice changes to the shopping terminals, but overlooks my main source of frustration with the current state of things: random shop inventory.

I freaking hate it. Last night, in between crashes, I was trying to find some ql50 clusters. This involved running back and forth across omni trade from one advanced market to the other. After a half hour I still hadn't found the right ql of cluster. They were either low 40s or high 60s, low 70s.

I just want to click on a faded cluster terminal, scroll down to the cluster type I want, enter the ql I want and buy. If that's too difficult then just put items in at set ql's the way you do with the implant terminal. I'd love to click on the advanced faded cluster terminal and see ql20, ql30, ql 50, etc clusters listed for sale.

I'm sick to death of boring, frustrating running around trying to find parts. I did it with my ql 20 implants and hated it. I did it when making my aggression trimmer and hated it. Having to do it now for my ql 50 implants I'm hating it more.

Get rid of boring timesinks! They do nothing but generate resentment among the playerbase. I know i'm feeling it. I put up with this kind of boring timesink in EQ (boats, rare spawns, etc) for too long. EQ's burned up all my patience for this type of thing. It will not make me quit this time, but ql70, ql90? maybe. Eq has lowered my tolerance for boring crap to all-time, new lows.

On some other boards I read the random shop merchandise is almost always listed as one of the reasons for not returning to AO. (the other ones being lingering anger of the buggy release, and no way to effect the storyline/war).

Please let me just buy what I need and be on my way.

Pretty Please?
