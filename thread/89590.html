Item Org Bank
Would anyone else like to see an organization item bank similar to the org cred bank?

Put items in, such as med suits, ql 10 cusions, or sets of armor that you dont want that you think others could use. Type /org itembank and it brings up the same kind of inventory your own personal bank does.

Restrictions would have to be put in, like only one person allowed to use it at once or only certain ranks and such but I would like to see something like this put in.

While there are lots of bugs and exploits that devs should be considering before putting in new content, I'd still like to hear everyone's thoughts on it.

only problem. S/he is not online. or s/he is in another time zone which means that the only viable time for me to pass over said item is over the weekend or by using a middleman that are online when I am and also online when the recipiant of said item is online.

Place it in the org bank and then when I logg off for the night or some other reason and he gets online he can open the org bank and get the item.
