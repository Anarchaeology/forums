The Sentinels - Enemies of Clan and Omni alike?
For several months now, ever since unexpected problems forced me to back out of the political spotlight and later leave Rubi-ka for an extended period of time, I have watched the political situation between Omni-tek and the clans destabilise more and more.

From the exposure of the criminals in the heart of the Council of Truth, the dissolving of the Tir Accord, the clumsy ICC interference and finally the outragous invasion of Tir by the Sentinels.

Obviously this situation is not acceptable for Omni-tek. The Sentinels have absolutely no mandate from Omni-tek to govern Tir, which with the Tir Accord no longer in effect is for all intents and purposes Omni-tek territory once more.
But what the clans should ask themselves now is, are the Sentinels really the people they want controlling their capitol?

The Sentinels are extreme, even by clan standards. The appointed governour of Tir, Fisk, has been quoted for stating he want's to slaughter not only Omni-tek but neutrals as well. A statement which is more than just words, which is something any neutral who has visited Tir recently will know.

Now...it is clear to all that this situation will not be tolerated by Omni-tek for very long. Measures will have to be taken to remove the Sentinels from Tir. Knowing the Sentinels, it will require firepower and lots of it to push them back out. Most likely many clanners will feel a sympathetic obligation to "defend" their city regardless of the fact that they dislike the Sentinels as well.
This could trigger large conflict as Omni-tek pours more men into the conflict and the clans respond by calling upon more and more of their own soldiers.

I'm sure the clans do not want that situation and Omni-tek surely does not.
Therefore, I urge the clans, for their own sake, to remove the Sentinels from Tir. They are not worthy of the responsebility they have taken. You did not ask them to come and take over. The ideals that the Sentinels hold are the direct opposite of what you as clanners are fighting for. Freedom to express whatever you want and do what you want. The Sentinels are far harsher dictators than Omni-tek ever has or will be, make no mistake.

It is not too late to prevent another planetwide civil war, but do not expect Omni-tek's patience to last forever.

Yet again, I find myself agreeing with Gestava's appraisal of the situation.

However, as with all things on Rubi-Ka, it just isn't that simple. The ICC are in part to blame for the current climate in Tir. If their guards had upheld the notion that Tir was a clan city (despite whatever the minutiae of the now defunct Tir Accords may have had to say, Tir is home to clanners), and taken a slightly more politic attitude to the situation, preventing OT Employees from strutting around the city as if they owned it; then I suspect that the Sentinels would not have felt the need to intervene. But they didn't. And the Sentinels came and removed the ICC, claiming the city for the clans exclusively once more. Irrespective of the rights or wrongs of this, their actions have gained them much sympathy amongst the rank and file clanspeople. Memories on Rubi-Ka are short, and many have forgotten the brutality for which the Sentinels have made their reputation. The ICC created a power vacuum, and the Sentinels filled that vacuum.

Consequently, any removal of Silverstone's thugs from Tir must be backed up with some sort of replacement. Looking around the clans, I don't see that replacement. Not yet, at least. All that would happen, currently, were some move made to remove the Sentinels, is that Tir would once again become a playground for the rich and bored OT Employees looking to experience a little clan culture. How, then, could any clanner of good conscience condone that?

Until the clans can organise for a replacement government of Tir (and I know that there are plans afoot) there is nothing that can, or should, be done about the Sentinels. And yes, I'm afraid that this leaves our beautiful city off-limits to the neutrals, but this is something that must be endured.
I hope that the clan initiatives for the governance of Tir will be given time to mature and to be placed into effect, but any demand that the Clans work to a timetable dictated by Omni-Tek's desire for some 25th millenium reconquista is doomed to fail. I would urge those in a position of power to push OT to give us time, but we have seen time and again that OT move at their own pace and without regard for the political implications of their actions. I have also seen the intransigence of employees when it comes to negotiation of timetables in such matters.

So, I fear that OT will attempt to take Tir under their control. And there will be fighting on the streets of our fair city. I will defend our city to my core, even if it means fighting alongside the hated Sentinels. And I am not alone in my attitude.

Given time, the clans will get their house in order. If Omni-Tek persue a quick resolution in Tir, they will not only earn the renewed emnity of the Clans, but also further entrench sympathies for the Sentinels in Clan hearts. One step closer to war, then. The future looks bleak, Gestava, my friend; and I fear that soon we may indeed face one another down the sights of a gun.
