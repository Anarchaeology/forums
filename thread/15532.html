FUNCOM/COS::: -> The Adventurers fix, when?
After alot of talking w/ people, reading forums and tracking rumors, I start to wonder what is true and what is not.

People say Advs will be delayed, and a friend told me that FC'll prolly fix MA's, Agents and Engs first off. Right ... as if they need fixing. Well, perhaps it'll be the most noted change, since ALOT of people play these classes.

I soloed a mission yesterday, 50% diff ... just took me about 2 hours. The most interesting thing was that I met the hardest human mob I've ever encountered! An (orange) adventurer! It took me freaking 20 mins to kill it. I got it down to 10%, it healed up to 25%, three times ... I had to exit to heal and restock nano. I returned and it had healed back up to 75% again, and that's just in a few seconds. I eventually killed it, with 50-60 hp left(out of 900) and no nano. Later on that night, I found another while in team. And yes, it was hard too.

Howcome mob Advs are so über?

I think I speak for most advs when I say:

"GIVE US A DATE WHEN THE PATCH WILL COME!!!"

The least you can do is an approx date. Are we talking 1 month, 6 months, a year? Sure my adv will be easier to play when the IP reset is installed (And I think many others too. If this feature doesn't come, lord knows there'll be ALOT of flaming :P. Thats not a threat, thats fact), but it wont help the metaforms, for example. As I posted in the other thread, why is the cat so darn slow? Cats are F-A-S-T and A-G-I-L-E creatures ... not slow. Atleast REMOVE the -100 if not swap it for a +100 or more.

I also read about the cub form, which I have personal experience of. In it's stas, it says that Bio / Matter Meta and Sensory Imp controls it's attack. Hell it does. I morph, buff 1h edged, heavy weapons and brawl ... THEN I do damage worth speaking of. Doesn't really do any difference if I buff my morph skills (Which already are a good bit over the requirements).

I hear the pit lizard form is useless. According to what I've found out, it's barely worth the time and effort needed to get it.

Furthermore, about weapons, I read a suggestion making Adv only pistols. GREAT IDEA. Why not follow that suggestion? Why not add cloaks that boost perception in shops too? Perhaps in fixer shops.

Another interesting thing, swapping from pistols to the thread about Rangers, is that Advs bow skill is kinda expensive. This is just a thought. In fact, I dunno why I bring it up, since bows are slow. Atleast thats what I hear.

"When WILL the Adv patch come? if ever. Please give us atleast an approxed date." Perhaps us Advs will calm down for awhile, unless it's a year away or so.
