To consider
Though I vote for such a possibility, I have to say, we won't get it (soon, if at all).

According to what I understood from the last 'official' statement from Funcom concerning this, the current technology prohibits such a dynamic model. Every item you can get in AO is in the (local) database. This means, every individually named bag needs to be in the database in advance (!) - we can't generate database entries on our own (there might be several reasons to forbid such).

A larger variety of bags would be very helpful and easier to realise:
- Armor
- Weapon by type (1HB/2HB/1HE/2HE/Pistols/Rifles/Shotguns/...)
- Tradeskill (different types)
- Pharmaceuticals
- Rings
- Crystals
- Gems
- ...

Please give us some more options - my bank is almost full (6 slots left)
