Idea to help out engineers
Something that would work well to help engineers would be the ability to customize their pets to a certain extent. Since engineers appear to be trade skills / pet oriented, it would be almost natural that they would 'tinker' with their pets to make them more effective.

Here is how I see it working:

An engineer has their standard robot shell in their inventory.

First, they take their screwdriver and disassemble the standard robot (using Mechanical Engineering skill) into a disassembled robot shell.

The engineer builds a module that attaches to the shell, (using weaponsmithing and mechanical engineering skills just to attach the item). An engineer would only be allowed to attach one upgrade module to their pet.

They would then be able to activate the upgraded shell. When the pet dies, they have the ability to recover the module (provided they are able to recover the corpse of the bot).

The modules would have to be assembled using trade skills, and these could be some modules for the robot:

(note: some parts should be hacked parts, and I feel that flexible shafts, carbonium plating, and various types of wire should be commonly required parts)

* Targeting computer module
(increase chance of pet's critical by a small %)
(would use something like ME, EE, QFT, and WS to build)

* Ranged weaponry module
(slightly reduced damage, but pet gains ranged attack)
(would use something like ME and WS (and EE?) to build)

* Electric taser module
(slightly increased damage, using energy AC)
(would use something like ME, EE, and WS to build)

* Chemical sprayer module
(slightly increased damage, using chemical AC)
(would use something like ME, Chemistry, and WS to build)

For balancing, the requirements for modules could be handled one (or more, if necessary) several ways:

* Module ql might end up having to be a similar ql to the pet
* Module could increase the pet shell activation cost
(by a fixed amount, depending on the module installed)

Of course, better pet pathing would be a godsend...
(but I know that it is on the list of priorities)
