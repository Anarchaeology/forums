Real Life
The world of rubi ka needs.

Hospitals - so you can fix up your character, get implants installed

Police stations- so you can report crimes carried out by other players

Court room- so crimes if wished can go to trial

Conference building so that different factions can meet and negotiate over the table.

Markets- so you can haggle for goods and by rare clothes and objects

Transport- you should be able to share transport

And if you take on the role of enforcer or soldier then you should be able to arrest criminals in your cities.

I would like to hear your responses from these sugggestions.
