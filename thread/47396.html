Grid Armor takes the hit to the DMS mod and AC when OE, but the +NCU/HP/Damage bonuses are not affected.

I've been Ransacked/Deprived many times while wearing GA1 in missions, solo and team, and the loss of DMS is pretty evident. Green mobs start hitting you more often, and yellow/reds hit you like you are nearly naked.

Granted, 25% or 50% loss of MK1's DMS is going to put you closer to normal DMS than it would if you lost 25% in a higher version, making a GA2+ Fixer a little more durable after going into OE mode.
