Firstly, I'd sooner see FC's efforts going into sorting out the trade bugs that appear from time to time, which as a consequence, caused Claire (L175 NT) to quit, and provided the final straw in Noer (L176 Trader) quitting.

Now... onto the proposal for over-equipping.

The first thing I would do as FC is provide a simulator for people to see whether they are over equipped, and in what areas. This will at least give people a chance to see how badly they are going to be affected.

Point 2. If you're going to throw this little gem into the game without placing checks against implants, then you've only done half the job. Someone with high level friends will be able to get hold of QL125+ implants easier than someone who doesn't, meaning that you've got nowhere in the issue.

Point 3. Traders will become incredibly valued. They can pretty much suck skills from anyone or anything, and therefore can go into battle knowing they are much stronger for their level than anyone else. If the trader decides to suck away at another player, then the other player becomes OE, and even more disadvantaged, and not just against the trader in question.

Point 4. If the requirements for QL200 armour are 551, then you will not be OE when you hit 480. (480*1.2=576). If you want implants above 125, do missions for blanks, and go to the shop. If clusters of 10% lower fit into a certain QL of implant, then QL180 clusters will fit into QL200 implants.

Point 5. If OE is to be put in play, then you need to balance all ability buffs across the board for the associated classes. An enforcer with EoC+ will be able to jam themselves into much higher armour than a soldier would. Int and Psy based classes will suffer tremendously at lower levels too.

Point 6. If NCU is not going to be OE, then traders (again) will be able to get away with being able to use much high equipment than anyone else. I've seen L34 chars get into 6 slot belts, and carry nearly 200 NCU.

Point 7. OE will affect both the social and economic system tremedously, and the amount of social traffic will be reduced dramatically.

Point 8. IP Reset is a good thing. End of story on that one.

This fix is something that I would avoid for a long time for two reasons. Firstly, the problems that already exist in the game that are critical need fixing first. Secondly, I would implement this change on a totally separare box for a good few month, and I may even propose that it be made a separate server altogether that people can play on if they do not like the OE state as it stands.

The first thing that should be done, even has it's own forum now:

http://forums.anarchy-online.com/for...s=&forumid=185

Clear that list first, consolidate, then move forward.

This change is a complete rewrite of the game from the players point-of-view. Everything is affected. I'd go so far as to say that it should be called AO2.

If the change does go ahead before critical issues are challenged, then I can see other MMORPG's getting an abundance of AO players moving over to play them instead.

For the time being, at least with the current system, if people use the various channels, make friends, join guilds, etc, then they too can enjoy and use the various opportunities that open up to them in the current world of OE.

Perhaps it's time that all the FC staff actually played this game with regular players, to see how the world affects them from ground level on a real time basis, rather than having to use forums and petitions which will never really describe the issues that players are having at this time.
