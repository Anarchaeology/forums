Sol Banking Corporation implicated in Dirty Tricks Campaign
The hostile rivalry between the Sol Banking Corporation and Omni-Tek is common knowledge. The well-documented central issue being Omni-Teks sole ownership of the ICC issued leasehold for Rubi-Ka, a planet that as we all know uniquely hosts the galaxy's only source of notum, a fundamental component to all aspects of nano technology.

Last week it came to Omni-Teks attention that the Sol Banking Corporation had instigated an operation to plant wholly manufactured and false evidence of Omni-Tek atrocities against clan affiliated prisoners of war with the ICC, in an attempt to discredit the corporation on an interstellar scale, and add weight to the call for an ICC review of the validity of Omni-Teks continued sole leasehold.

No matter how false the evidence, it is this kind of public image smudging that the corporation will not tolerate, nor this potential threat to its profits, and hence following a meeting of Omni-Tek security chiefs, department SPARTA was assigned to retrieve this manufactured information: to in effect covertly steal it back from the ICC before they could review or use it.

As was suspected, upon investigation SPARTA operatives discovered that the information was being held at Nepal, the ICC Headquarters on Rubi-Ka, an installation located at the eastern end of Andromeda.

An intelligence operative, who will remain anonymous for obvious security reasons, was immediately despatched to penetrate the installation covertly and retrieve the information. This operation was successful, though an exchange of gunfire did take place between the Omni-Tek agent and ICC security personnel resulting in several ICC deaths that we tracked back through successful reclaim terminal usage.

The ICC representative holding the bogus information, an individual we allege to be on the payroll of the Sol Banking Corporation, was amongst those who fell in the gun battle.

Unfortunately from here the situation escalated, and SPARTA was forced to mount a rescue operation to extract both their agent and the information. This was achieved without further loss of life, and only minimal casualties on both sides, in a typically swift and clinical Omni-Tek fashion.

We can confirm that the ICC have protested to the CEOs office in the strongest terms to this operation, and requested that he instructs SPARTA to return the information so they may make their own judgement as to its validity.

SPARTA has not currently received any such instruction from CEO Philip Ross, and hence the Director of SPARTA, Garret "Bonefish" Silvestrinis office has issued the following response to the ICCs request. "No."


Omni-Tek is your friend.

So much strife
Ehrynn reflected for a bit on the news about Omni-Tek, SPARTA and the Sol Banking Corporation. How can one planet have so much strife?"

She had only been on the planet for a few weeks and could not understand how a world with so much potential could be plagued by such terrible conflict. The university had pumped her head full of notions about economic interdependence and the exchange of universal moral values promoting harmony. On Rubi-Ka it seemed the wise had consigned such notions to the dustbin long ago. On this planet, money makes the world go round, and the pursuit of interests was everything. It was all about power.

The South Rubi-Ka Society was a philanthropic group that stuck out on Rubi-Ka like a sore thumb. She wondered about this SPARTA organization. Ehrynn tried to recall where she had encountered that name before. She quickly called up information on ancient earth history and searched for the term.

Sparta: Most militaristic of the Greek-City states. Male citizen of Sparta was obliged to place a priority on military preparedness. At age seven Spartan boys were taken from their mothers to join all able-bodied Spartan males under the age of thirty for a life in the military barracks and training. The two kings of Sparta served as military commanders. See also: Battle of Thermopylae and Leonidas; Peloponessian War.

Ehrynn followed these other links. I wonder if this group holds to the values of discipline, duty, and sacrifice represented by the Three Hundred. She frowned, It could be they just like the image of military skill. She dashed off a quick message:

Dear Director Silvestrini,

I am a new Omni-Tek employee and a member of the South Rubi-Ka Society. The news of your most recent operation has intrigued me. Congratulations on its success.

However, I was curious to know if the use of the term SPARTA indicates an attachment to the high standards of conduct that marked the armies of the very ancient earth Greek city-state of the same name?

Marylynn "Ehrynn" Tedders
Receptionist, Omni-Prime Med Clinic

As for where our nickname comes from...


Historical Context

The life of a Spartan was a life of discipline, self-denial, and simplicity. The Spartans viewed themselves as the true inheritors of the Greek tradition. They did not surround themselves with luxuries, expensive foods, or opportunities for leisure. And this, I think, is the key to understanding the Spartans. While the Athenians and many others thought the Spartans were insane, the life of the Spartans seemed to hark back to a more basic way of life. Discipline, simplicity, and self-denial always remained ideals in the Greek and Roman worlds; civilization was often seen as bringing disorder, ennervation, weakness, and a decline in moral values. The Spartan, however, could point to Spartan society and argue that moral values and human courage and strength was as great as it was before civilization.

The ideology of Sparta was oriented around the state. The individual lived (and died) for the state. Their lives were designed to serve the state from their beginning to the age of sixty.


So I hope that clears it up? I believe the favoured term these days would be to describe us as 'ultra-loyal'.
