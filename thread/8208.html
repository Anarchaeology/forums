Parts-based items/weapons
I dream of a day in AO where I gain a more powerful weapon, not through buying a brand new one and throwing my old one away, but by replacing the parts of which my weapon is composed.

It would be interesting if when you shift right click (or whatever key combination suits you) on an item that is made up of other items (weapons are the most obvious), it would pop up a little schematic of the item with all of it's constituent parts, which could then be removed and replaced (through some trade or nano skill, like with implants). Upgrading that item, then, would simply be getting better parts for it, or perhaps buying a better casing and transferring in all the parts from the old casing. The QL of the item would be an amalgamation of the QL's of it's parts, perhaps with different weights for more important parts. Or, even better, each part of the item affects the overall stats of that item differently. For instance, getting a better blade of a melee weapon would increase it's damage, while getting a better handle would increase it's recover speed, or whatever.

I realize this is quite a leap from the current system and would probably be a bear to implement, but I think it would add greater dimension to the game play, as well as greater customization and variety to items (specifically weapons). Any thoughts?
