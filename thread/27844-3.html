We've agreed and disagreed on various topics before Scorus but on this one I have to disagree with you. The solution to camping isn't as simple as we, the players, make it when viewed from the developers standpoint.

The only way to eliminate camping is one that takes more time than people want to give. That's:
  1. Completely randomize the mob's loot so it is never guarranteed to have an item.
  2. Give the mob a wide enough range of items to have as loot equal to it's difficulty to kill.
  3. Make sure an item has more than 1 mob of appropriate difficulty to spawn in.


So long as people know where to go for an item , are sure to find it there, and that location is the only place to get it it will be camped.
