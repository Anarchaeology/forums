Bionitrous, you bring up some valid points, but I happen to agree with they way Funcom was thinking when they designed the land control.

quote:
I'll be honest with you Cz and tell you that Notum Wars is has NOT opened up a new mode of gameplay except for about 200 players TOTAL. This is not a whine. I just want you to know that your entire playerbase is essentially at the mercy of about 6 guilds who can destroy your base like turning off a light switch and subsequently 'give' it away to some other guild.

We want to fight other organizations who match up with us in terms of average level and membership. We don't want to sit in fear of being targetted by some conglomeration of super orgs who don't even need our land and just 'give' it away to someone else to bump their xp bonus up a bit.
I can't speak for Funcom, but I believe they were thinking along the lines of Clan vs Omni with land control (Not Guild vs Guild). Yes, a huge org can take down your tower easily, but because of the XP bonus, it is in the best intrest of all clanners to help you defend against omni, and vice versa. This is why orgs form alliances.


quote:
This is what we're talking about. There basically is NO challenge to holding a lvl250 base on RK-1. You guild can do whatever they want all week long without having to worry about defending your base. You can level, kill uniques, go to Tarasque, whatever. It would take a MONUMENTAL cohesive force to pull off the miracle. That's just wrong. The advantages from a 250 base are absolutely incredible. It should actually take some resources, work, time, and effort to maintain these advantages. As it stands, a 250 base is the easiest thing in the game to hold, when in fact it should take the most work to maintain. THAT's where the problem lies.

I'm not saying it's not possible. I'm just saying that the equation is wrong. The one thing in the game that gives you the most advantages is the easiest thing to hold and least likely to be attacked because of this.
