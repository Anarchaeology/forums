So, OT killed your friend's family for... errm... no reason.

Seriously, OT's a company, it cares about its bottom line. Randomly killing entire families of clanners (except for one survivor - funny how that always happens) is a waste of time and resources that could be more profitably spent elsewhere. If you really want to help your friend, I'd suggest that you do some digging into the background of her family. I strongly suspect that somewhere along the line you'll find out that one or more of them was involved in activities which hurt OT's profit margin badly enough for them to bother with the cost, fuss and general bad PR of sending a kill team out to resolve the problem. Must have been quite a determined thorn in their side too, for the usual system of threats and blandishments to fail.

Alternatively, look to the clans themselves. OT cares first and foremost about the bottom line, but the clans are fighting to determine who runs Rubi-Ka once OT's lease runs out. Consequently, half the time they're at each others' throats. If a family member was enough of a problem that OT might have taken violent action against them, it's equally likely that a political rival somewhere in the clans might be to blame.

Sorry, but I've heard this "OT murdered my hamster!" story too often to take it at face value any more...
