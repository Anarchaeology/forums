Sea of Change Now Recruiting (RP)
Sea of Change is now recruiting new and veteran roleplayers to help fill our ranks and join in events. We are about to revise some of the organization structure, so we have many openings available, in all branches, from military, to crafting, to entertainment & events, and anything in between.


About Us

We are a fairly small, friendly guild. We all enjoy roleplaying, and we often hold roleplaying events, both public and private. We enjoy taking part in any kind of roleplaying story. Our main goals are:
  • To increase the enjoyment of our gaming experience.
  • To hold, and be a part of, as many roleplaying events as possible.
  • To make a difference.


What We're Looking For

We're looking for those who want an organization they can roleplay with openly and freely, whether you are new to roleplaying, or a veteran. We're seeking those who enjoy events, and would like to be part of ongoing stories.


Our Background Story

Sea of Change is a highly secretive group, with many goals, which we typically keep to ourselves. We started as a small organization based out of Borealis, even though we were not a very big organization, we were still suspect in many happenings across Rubi-Ka, including investigations by The Council of Truth and Omni-Tek. Our secretive nature leads to us being labled criminals in many cases, but we press on with our drive to change the face of Rubi-Ka, our cause is much to important.

Contact Info

If you wish to join, or want to learn more about us please contact Sepentura on Rubi-Ka 2, or send an email to sepentura@icqmail.com

You may also visit our guild website by clicking Here

Questions are always welcome.

Thank you for your time.
