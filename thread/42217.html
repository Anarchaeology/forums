Kazino
I just wanted to give some info on Kazino and how it is operated.


1. We dont have any ideology or political beliefs as a company. We may have personal opinions but they will remain personal and wont affect how we do business. We are not affiliated with any other organization in rubi-ka.

2. As a company our goal is to get more customers and profit and maintain our high customer satisfaction.

3. We do not exclude any player. If you want to have fun, got money to bet and you behave when you are playing with us, we want your business.

4. We do not exclude any organizations. If you invite us to your party or event, we will show up to entertain your friends. We dont care about guild wars, it is none of our business.

5. We are willing to do business with any organization. If you want to arrange some sort of business deal we would love to hear it.

6. We do not tolerate unprofessional behaviors from our dealers. If one of our dealers is not acting professional give me a tell and he/she is out. Somebody complained about one of our dealers and the dealer was kicked out of our company.

7. If you dont want us to deal with your rival guild or someone u dont like, dont expect us to do as you wish. We are like general stores that sell guns to all sides. We have a neutral stance. If you dont like our neutral stance and wont deal with us because of that Im sorry to lose your business but Ill live with it.

8. We have moved to Reets, the room left to the blood room. GSP was generous enough to let us use that room. I wanted to thank Wyldthing for her support. I also wanted to thank Thyme, Nevver and the rest of DA for their support, we will miss baboons.

9. We will cover Reets as much as possible, hopefully 24/7 in near future, but right now we have limited number of dealers but we are working hard to get this accomplished.

10. We need more dealers. Please send your resume to me (Rondy) or dealerchick. And we will schedule an interview as soon as possible.

Sincerely
