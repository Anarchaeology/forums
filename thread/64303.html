Beggars
Well.. Don't know if I'm really in the right forum for this, or if there indeed IS a right forum for whining, but I want to , so there!

Begging.. I was just rezzing in NLC after being lagkilled in Cam and, naturally, I wasn't all that happy with my life at that point. This is when a GUILDED lvl 18 decided to walk up to me and ask me for money to buy such-and-such. I reply: "No, go earn your own money." As I always answer beggars. I do not like them - at all. He then snaps at me for being an a**hole and I should know what it's like at low lvl's, it's hard, I've been there too, blah, blah, blah...
I don't quite know what bugs me the most, the fact that he thinks I'm just going to give him the money, or that he thinks he's in his right to call me names for not giving it to him.

Let me start with saying this; yes, I do know what it's like to be a low leveled poor, good for -virtually- nothing char. We've ALL been there and that. And don't try to tell me that this is an alt, or I have high level friends or what have you. It doesn't matter if this is my first, third, or twenty-eight character. I have STILL been that poor, no-idea-about-anything guy once. So what if this guy I'm on now is my last, as opposed to first, attempt at an AO character. All that means is that I've already gone through the grind of learning the ropes and making friends who help you out. Maybe a blessed few had a RL friend who also happened to be a high leveled AO character, but I'm willing to bet that the majority of us we're n00bs, in the truest sense of the word, to AO when we first started our AO life.
What that reasoning of you-have-it-I-don't-so-gimme-some boils down to is this; YOU consider YOUR time as more valuable than MINE. MY efforts in learning this game and making a char/set of chars with skills, know-how, money and friend is not worth as much as YOURS, since YOU should have it served to you, for free, and instantly. Same thing goes for a lot of things... "Boo-hoo, you're a high level character, leave this kill alone for us lowbies", or "Since you are so much higher than me, you should team with me and help me finish this <insert quest/mission/boss-kill of your choice>"
NO. I shouldn't. I have spent WAY much more time in this game than you have. I might, I'm not saying I never, I just want to point out that I am in no way obliged to. I help people out. I'm not that egotistic. I would just like to point out that I. Don't. Have. To. It's my choice, and I'm not looking for kudos and eternal gratitude when I do, I just want to keep it my choice.

Guess what, my time invested in this game has given me this money, this level and this amount of know-how as to how to get from a to b, and how to clobber a, and give the corpse to b so c can give me money for it. I didn't get it all for free, and if I could choose, I honestly wouldn't have wanted to get it for free either. It was all part of my growing up as a Rubi-Ka citizen, and having it all handed to me at too low a level would have meant that I'd have lost interest in the game I think. There would simply have been too few challenges.

Hmm.. I got sidetracked here. Basically, what I wanted to say was this:

Don't whine when I don't give you money, go earn some instead. Thats what I - and everyone else - had to do in the beginning too. Help you out? Sure, I could give you pointers, but I am not logged on as some sort of alternate ALT. I pay as much money for my time here as you do, and I have most likely paid more in the past, since you don't know all the stuff I do, and believe you me, this knowledge didn't just upload itself to my brain. I got it the old fashioned way.
Don't call me names when I decide not to help you by running around the countryside killing bots and pointing them out to you so you can tag them.
And lastly.. Just don't expect the high level community to be there for your gaming enjoyment only. We're not.

I'm not here to wreck it either, I'm here for my OWN sake. Just like you are. The only difference is that I've been here a little longer, thus I've learned a few things along the way. You will too, in due time.

There.. Now I've finished whining about the whiner that just jumped me while I was rezzing. And I'm done with that too, so I'm going to head back to cam now. rezzed, rebuffed and with vented opinions. I feel better now, thank you very much
