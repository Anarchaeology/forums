The Reformists Present... A New Beginning!
Flyer
Members of Rubi-Ka,

With much pleasure The Reformists are proud to announce that we will be holding a party this saturday night at 10 pm EST. Our parties are open to any faction, any breed, and any dance style (though we hope you will keep your wild dancing in the corner ;P ). Bring a friend, bring a guild, hell you can even bring a faction. Our goal is to provide a party you can join where everything is happening and there are no regrets on going. Whether you join us to dance or watch our amazing table dancers you will be in a stress free environment.

Words of the party...
Socialize, Colonize, Cannibalize, Privatize, Emphasize, and most importantly Recognize.

Dimension: Rubi-Ka 2
Where: Reet's Retreat
When: 10 pm EST
Till: The music stops bumpin and the crowd's stop thumpin.
Who: Everyone

See you there!
