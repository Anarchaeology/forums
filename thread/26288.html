Customer Service Suggestions
I keep hearing from people that Funcom has misbilled their credit cards, that it has billed their credit cards incorrectly and then suspended accouts for nonpayment, that it has suspended people's accounts because they complained of having been hacked. And in every case, the common thread is that it takes forever to get an answer out of customer service, and when you do, the answer is usually wrong or at least unhelpful.

So here's my suggestion: Unless Norwegian law prohibits it, fire the entire Customer Service department (or better yet, feed them to a starving pack of weasels), and outsource customer service to any one of the large number of firms that specialize in it. If that's not feasible, or Norwegians can't figure out how to do it, or Norwegian law prohibits it for some incomprehensible reason, hire new ones.

And tell the new ones that rudeness is never appropriate. Tell them that it is even more important to apologize for mistakes than to prevent or correct them. Tell them that apologizing doesn't get you off the hook for taking corrective action. Tell them that 1% is not an acceptable error rate in a six-sigma world. Tell them that while it is true that some customers are too expensive to serve to be profitable, using that as an excuse to provide poor service means that eventually all of the customers will shop somewhere else.

And while I'm dubious about a world run by people who've read Dale Carnegie's classic How to Win Friends and Influence People, nobody in their right mind should hire a customer service person who hasn't memorized big chunks of it.

I don't know about the rest of the world, but in America, these are things we all hear before we're old enough to sign contracts, usually on our first part-time jobs as kids.
