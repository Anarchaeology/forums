Hello all!

Here you go ....

1) Mails to people.

We have no ETA on this. The reason we want to wait with the rest is to make sure everything runs smoothly. To be honest, even we dont know when we will start to send out the mails with the CD keys, but you all know when the game is going live so, as you imagine, it really cant be that long The mails will be sent out in batches.

2) Circulating of beta 4 cd's

This is nothing we are mad at. Actually we encourage people to spread out the cd. _But_, people should be careful where they download it. Make sure you trust the download site, and always check it for viruses etc, when downloading from a site that is not an official download site.

3) NDA

There is no NDA now, people are allowed to talk about the game, and discuss it aswell as posting screenshots etc etc. We do however trust that people will report exploits and bugs to us if they come across them, and not posting them out on fan sites.

That should be it
