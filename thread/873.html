Apartment ideas
Apartments is one of the things I'd like to see improved as well, but honestly...let's be sure we improve them to something useful. Right now, I never go back to my apartment (even though, YES, you can get other people into your apartment...they must be on your team). More practical for me, I've just kept a mission key copy of a nice mission area I liked, and call it my 'condo'. I can still dupe the key, allowing anyone I want in. It's fully furnished, and even has beds!

-If- apartment changes come, here's what needs to happen, to be interesting:

(NOTE: All these things also apply to the basics of faction housing, when that hits as well.)

1) Make usable furniture.
I like pictures and all, but goodness...they're useless without couches and tables! If apartments are supposed to be social situations, then you need furniture that allows them to BE social situations. Couches, chairs, tables, beds, rugs, etc.

2) Make useful furniture.
* Cabinets and bookcases should be small containers. I'd love to be able to put all the books I've found in a bookshelf, just so that I could easily find them again!

* Let lights actually affect lighting! TUrn off the flourescents and be lit by lamps for ambiance.

* Add computer terminals that you could click on to do stuff...like give you a better chat terminal, or even better, a whole chat/friends interface where you could get info on orgs, people, characters, Omni-TEk, Council of Truth, news from the web site! That would be really cool to see that in-game. (Great place to add email and such too!)

* Refrigerators should be able to store drink and food.

* Stereos should allow you to activate and play music in settings. (Actually, I'd love to be able to do this anywhere, but in apartments would be a good first start.)

*Especially for Faction housing, places where you can put things that OTHER PEOPLE can access...that is extremely valuable.

3) Increase the item limit
Obvious one here. 10 items is nothing. I could put 10 refirgerators in my apartment and it would look empty.

4) Let us edit who's allowed to enter.
Either through commands (/apartment add <friendname>) or by copying apartment keys.

5) Let us buy new apartments!
I'd drop 1mil credits if I could get a bigger area, allowing more items, or other styles. I'd also love to get apartments that aren't in backyards.

6) Trophies.
It'd be nice to be able to display weapons, armor, etc...in your apartment. I think it would be totally cool to have a manequin that you could hang armor sets on and it would show up. It could work like a container, buty only allow one piece per body slot.
