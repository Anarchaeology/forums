about Symbio-Graft
Symbio-Graft items are nano-charged wrist slot items. They can take the form of any nano crystal, and can be hacked for anyone to use, regardless of profession. It is the easiest nano for Funcom to add for several reasons:

1. Symbio-Graft items already exist in AO. It requires no extra coding to add a Symbio-Graft: Face Graft because there are already many other Symbio-Grafts such as Symbio-Graft: AssualtRifle Expertise, Symbio-Graft: Essence of Titan, etc.

2. By hacking Symbio-Graft, the profession restriction will be removed. Thus all of us can use the nano program on the graft.

Because the simplicity to add Symbio-Graft items, a petition to add Symbio-Graft: Face Graft would likely to be successful in getting funcom to add such item. It doesn't require them to program a face/height changing surgical clinic, or anything complex.
