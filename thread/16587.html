Special Loot in Missions
Ok, it seems like to me, the only special loot that you can find in missions are vehicles, such as the Yalmaha. I have in my entire time on AO, having done over 500 missions, only found 2 Yalmahas (QL 156 and QL 157). Now what I mean by "special" means that you cannot get these as mission rewards or as the "Pick up item."

Funcom, can you guys put some other stuff in there - like the Aura Magnifier or Padded Overcoat. It's not going to unbalance the game. Just make it as rare as getting a Yalmaha. It'll be better than two dozen people camping a static dungeon for a boss mob that spawns every 24 hours.

Just think about it, or pass it up the latter. Hey Gaute, you reading this? You think this would be a problem, buddy?
