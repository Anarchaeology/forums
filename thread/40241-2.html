This thread was very helpful to me so I thought I should bump it and post what I did.

Opifex MA self casting FFoK (req 782 PM SI) at level 158 as follows:

maxed abilities
maxed PM SI TS

relevant QL 200 implants:
eye: SI TS
head: PM sense
ear: PM
rarm: strength
chest: SI
larm: strength
rhand: TS
leg: agility

gear:
NCU Robot Reed: +8 SI
Third Eye of Daria: +27 SI
Neleb's Robe: +8 all nano skills
Viral Triumvirate Egg: +15 intelligence
Dillon Boots: +7 intelligence
Sekutek Body, Gloves, Sleeves (2), Pants: +4 intelligence each, total +20
Commander of Tir Ring: +4 intelligence

buffs:
generic int, str, agi, sen buffs
PM SI TS expertise

This gives me 791 PM, 752 TS and 787 SI. Had I remembered to dump some points into MC earlier I could have equipped Neleb's Robe at 157 and self cast then, but no earlier.

Still 25 points shy in TS to cast UVC (req 777 PM SI TS). The QL200 Adapted Notum Crystal gives 12 TS, so if I dump enough points into Melee Energy in theory I could self cast that at 161 or 162. Not sure of any other items that would as useful for that; w/ another egg and a +5 int pistol I might be able to shave off another level off of that but I'm not certain.
