Player A has high evades, fair runspeed and an understanding of line of sight. He brings mobs back to the group to kill one at a time.

Player B has high hp. He takes the mob off player A and tries to keep it on himself.

Player C has hp restorative abilities. He keeps player B from dying.

Player D-F have whatever meager abilites. They add supplemental damage primarily. They also can add whatever abilities the designers choose to have in the game (cc, buff, etc).

This is not a complex system. However, the above makes no specification of hit-rates/damage rates/heal rates. These factors need to be designed well in order for the group to a) succeed and/or b) have fun.
