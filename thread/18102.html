Fitness Matters
With all the wonderful ideas for gameplay and profession improvements, this idea pales in comparison, but one thing I would think might be fun from a roleplay point of view would be the inclusion of fitness facilities in the major towns of Rubi-Ka where our characters can work out, including the possibility of a fitness clothing shop, health food and drink bars, swimming pool, sauna in addition to standard fitness machines and such.

Such an area would provide areas for socialization and roleplay as an alternative to simply going to the clubs to dance. I could even envision emotes for workout actions to go along with such a place.

Just a thought in case someone at Funcom has a little free time and imagination to spare.

Thanks!
