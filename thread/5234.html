My reasons for leaving as requested in the cancelation process.
The perpose of this post is not to complain in any way but to let Funcom know in detail why I have canceled my account (I was asked for a reason in the cancelation process and felt I should post it here because I had a bit to say on the subject).

I will list the things I like and the things that I didnt for Funcom to see and hopfully think about changing for the benefit of other players of AO.

I have played a while and one of my chars has reached level 90 while my other 7 chars ranged from level 25 to 75.


Things I liked about the game:

The graphics and animation are the best I have ever seen and it must of been a special art team to make an entier world look so good.

I liked the large choice of weapons.

I liked the large choice of nanos.

I liked the professions.

I liked the breeds.

Music and fx are great.

The world feels BIG and real.

Screen layout, chat and options work very well.


The things I didn't like:

The skill system I dislike, it is to restrictive and leads classes to follow one path only (yes there are exceptions but as a general rule people tend to follow "the best path" for there breed/prof). For instance I made a soldier because I wanted to try out all the "big guns" in the game. I quickly realised that to put ip into skills I would later not use would "gimp" my char. So like many others I took the Nova Flow/Flashpoint Laser route and missed out on having fun with all the fun looking guns because of the restrictive skill system. It doesn't allow you to "try out" diffrent skills.

I have always preferd the type of skill system the requiers you to use a skill to be able to get better in it.

Missions I also dislike, to me they are boring and repetetive and I do not enjoy them at all.

The time need to play and "get something done" is too long.

Travel is to long. Untill you have 5 million to buy a plane ( I never found one in a mission), or the MP nano Quantum Wings.

Shops charge to much for items.

I found it very hard to keep track of my backpack/bank items and was forever opening bags to see were a particular item was.

The storyline did very little for me.

I felt like it was a continous need to upgrade weapons, nanos and armour and a daily run of boring missions to aquier the items/cash needed for this.

Teams take too long to get together.

I dont like the QL system, it dosen't make sense that you need more skill to use an old item rather than a newer one of the same type. In fact you would probably need more skill to use a worn out rifle than a smooth working new one that is acurate.

I looked at tradeskills early in the game and didnt look at them again.


There are probably a lot of things I have forgoten to add, good and bad but for now this is all I can think of. Also please remember these are my opinions of the game and the reasons why I have got bored of it and I realise it will be diffrent for other people and many will disagree with me.

Perhaps things will get better now, but for me... I played through all the bugs, patches and balancing and the game has lost it's shine for me. Perhaps if I had joined the game now instead of 5 months ago I would have more to look forward to.
