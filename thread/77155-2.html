IMO

1) Boss loot belongs to the whole team; the looter's job is to loot and divvy. Rare loot should be sold and split unless other team members just don't care about the cash.

2) Fixers and NTs have no "higher ground" to stand on with respect to GA and NS dropping off a boss or chest or anywhere else unless it is agreed in advance. They chose that profession knowing full well that certain key items were worth 50-500 million credits. Why should Fixers and NTs be automatically entitled to a 50-500 million windfall because of their professions when their teammates can use the cash just as well as they can use the item? This really only applies to GA and NS; can't think of any other profession specific rares that drop randomly and are so high priced. If the team decides to give them the item, they should be eternally grateful, because it is not their automatic privilege to get it. If they sell the item and split the cash, the fixer or NT should be happy to add their cut to their NS or GA pool. Would I give GA or NS to a friend if found in a mission and teaming with the friend? Absolutely. But playing with people you don't know, fixers and NTs, doesn't give you the right to expect your team to sacrifice 10-100 million credits each so you can have your rare item.

3) That said, the team should always try to work towards a solution that puts the rare needed item in the hands of the profession that can use it. There is, however, no obligation to do so.

Just my $.02
