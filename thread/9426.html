Newbie shops
While elite shops is obviously a good idea, I think newb shops are needed too.

Let's face it, nobody sells anything below lvl20 on the shopping channel, and once you're kicked out of channel newbie at lvl6 you're on your own.

A few shops that sold low Q stuff would be cool - would also free up the basic shops for some more relevant stuff. They only need the basics - poor armour/weapons, medi/nano kits, ncu/belts - that would be enough. Kinda like the basic booth but a step up with stuff up to Q10ish. Make em stick to cheap/low Q stuff as well, so they're perfect for those with few credits (ie have medi kits of 20 instead of 50, a good selection of weak armour, good but not uber weapons etc.).

While nobody really wants to admit it, everyone needs a bargain store every now and again
