Kamikaze bots
From what I saw on the Engie Forum, the Kami bot has two big flaws.

1) Instead of blowing itself up by resetting its HP to 0, it sets the target's HP to 0.

2) The other is a pathing issue. The Kamikaze is a 'burrowing' robot similar to a Scooplet. The problem is it cannot negotiate its way to a target that is on a different level, eg a slope or a table.

Number 1 seems a simple enough fix, but the 2nd I imagine is a real headache.
