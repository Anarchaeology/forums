second off, whoever made the decisions on this fix, clearly didn't get what they were doing. Making it, effectively a 15 second recharge and putting that full dot damage in 15 secs AND the fact that the dot will do more damge than the heal= bad.

Teammate of trader: hey WTF! why aren't you healing us.

eh?

1) First off, of course, fix the dot to 100%
2) add 50% to recharge time.
3) cut dot time in half, making it do full damage in 30-35 secs

That way, the heals are still usable AND it adds that risk that you guys are wanting, without making them useless.
