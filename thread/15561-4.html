A few thoughts
I just read Syrah's post where she(he?) states that she is "the best" pvp'er. Such a remark is bound to make you unpopular but I honestly don't care. Bragging is a sign of low self esteem, and people with low self esteem should be allowed to grow stronger.

In any case, I'd like to discuss the term "the best". What makes you the best? Almost every time i go close to a pvp zone I see syrah and her crew, which mostly is Dankman, Fopus and Etin plus some amateurs that tags along. This makes me think that her neophyte title is based on two things. Investment of time, and team tactics.

Personally I don't pvp much. If fact I almost never pvp compared to the time i spend online, but I have still managed to get an apprentice title. I pvp'ed quite heavily for a short period to go from rookie to apprentice and this makes me think that getting novice is purely based on a decicion I have to make. Prioritize pvp before getting xp. If I had played as much pvp as syrah did, maybe i would have been novice or even neophyte by now? (probably not neo :P) Who knows? If so, would i then be the best? Because I invested time? I think not. It would be becuase I have a flashpoint rifle and mkx.

I know plenty of people that has killed syrah one on one, so obviously she isn't the best when it comes to personal skill. Two times I have died to syrah, and both times it was me against at least two omnies. Does this make her better than me? Or anyone else? Nope, it doesnt.. It makes her crew better than my crew. What beats me is that she takes personal credit for it. Maybe this is the reason why an infamous omni ganker has issued a 5 million reward on her head?

Lets compare "the best pvp'er" to a hero. All heroes has a few similarities. They are strong, skilled and most importantly, brave. A person is generally considered brave when they take a big risk and actually manage to pull it off. The type of risks that makes the rest of us stand and watch in awe. Strong and skilled syrah may be, but brave she is not. Her pvp performances can at best be considered as "effective, but annoyingly dull". Are these characteristics use in connection the the term "best"? Not for me at least...

Syrah's reputation may percede her, but not as the best pvp'er. Her reputation is more along the lines of "the neo omni with shady tactics". Nothing like drzand's reputation, whos pvp performances are still talked about today.

To close this annoyingly long post I would just like to point out that the title "the best" is never self proclaimed. The term is comparative. Just as nobody can proclaim themselves as "cool" and stay "cool" doing it, nobody can build up their own reputation by claiming they are better than the rest.

Just some thoughts =)
