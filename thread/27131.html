May I ask how much RAM you have in your computer?

While increasing your processor speed plays a big part in lessening the lag, the game itself often eats up to 300 MB of resident RAM in my machine (after I've been playing for a couple hours and have gone through lots of playfield changes).

Personally, I have 1GB ram in my machine, but I would recommend 512 at the very least. And yes, I have been telling Funcom to change the minimum requirements and the "recommended" requirements on the game since release (it was 20 times worse at release.. and oh, wait til you zone into Newland )

Good luck fiddling with the graphics settings a bit more. Hopefully you can fine-tune it to a playable state until you upgrade your machine.
