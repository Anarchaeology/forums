OOCish

The virus that causes the cold can change its structure (by altering its DNA through mutation) making it harder for the immune system to fight, one years cold is different to another years.

Secondly a number a symptoms of the common cold - headache, drowsiness, blocked sinuses, temperature are also the symptoms of a lot of other infections, most of which are benign. In fact a lot of the colds you have suffered in your life have not been caused by the common cold virus but by other viruses.
