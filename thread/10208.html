Announcing the start of Battle Royale MoK
The Mercenaries of Kai are proud to announce a program of Battle Royales to determine who is the strongest, smartest, and luckiest character in the Milky Way galaxy.

This program shall be held every Sunday at the Omni-1 arena at 8pm GST/3pm EST/12pm PST starting on February 24 and ending May 19.

PRIZES

1,000,000 for each individual Battle Royale winner
20,000,000 for the Grand Champion winner

Contestents of each Battle Royale compete for a prize of 1 million credits. Champions of the these individual contests are eligible to compete for a 20 million Grand Prize when they face each other in a final battle scheduled on May 19. The Grand Champion will be crowned the Master of Chaos.

Any players interested in participating should visit the MoK website and read the rules carefully.

www.totalaggression.com

Follow the "MoK-AO" link to find our Events section.

Once again, the first contest:

Battle Royale MoK I
Sunday, February, 24,
8:00 pm GST/ 3:00 pm EST/ 12:00 pm PST
Omni-1 Arena in Omni-1 Entertainment, South 600x600

(NOTE TO CLAN MEMBERS AND NEUTRALS - Since the patch, guards will now aggro you, however if you use the grid to enter Omni-Entertainment you should have no problems. From the grid exit in Omni -1, Entertainment South, simply run north to get to the Arena.)
