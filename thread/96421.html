OOC item desc and stories here
I was just browsing the DB a bit and came across some cool descriptions. Maybe someone can use some of them to organize rp events to play out or something.

#1 In Milky Way there lived a man they called Duke, but his real name was Sonny Dodd. Now, this man came to the area - nobody knows quite from where, but some suspect it was from another planet. He settled down and bought an old settler bunker near the north dock ruins - he obviously were in no short supply of credits. A year or two passed, and then a strange thing happened: Duke used to buy blooms and bars from the new factory - and this day, it was short after Landing Day, he were on his way out from the factory area with his helper bot carrying the metal. Just as he came out from the gate he was overrun and killed by a carrier craft. It was, as one might expect, not a pretty sight. The foreman that was Dodd's contact waited for him to come back from ressurection and fetch his metal, but the man never appeared. This foreman, his name was Skip Thorus, was a kind soul, and he decided to bring the metal to the place he knew the man Dodd were living. He arrived there in the evening, after work, and found the place unlocked. Duke were nowhere to be found - and OmniPolice were brought in. Inquiries about his whereabouts were sent out - to no avail. During the ransacking of his abode the OT Police found various machinery that indicated that the place had been used for the manufacturing of weapons. In a hidden tunnel under the bathroom they found a large amount of mass-produced submachine pistols. These were of a kind never seen before - obviously a unique design by Duke himself. They use SkinCom 3rd edition and are very easy to wield. For some reason the gun shapes the bullets into random, odd forms. They aren't aerodynamic at all, but they can pull large chunks of meat from the body of the target - hence the name. OT are currently storing the gun until Duke returns - from wherever it is he's insured.

#2 This weapon was supposely designed by a high-ranking individual of Omni-Sci or Omni-Engineering. Rumour has it that he had an affair with a soldier in Division 9 RSGE. Some say it was Babe Suitt, but she usually denies this. It could also have been Maren Silvestrini. The weapon was meant to be a gift to whoever she was, but Omni-InterOps detected the thing, the production was stopped and the lovesick engineer secretly dismissed. The plans seem to have survived though since somewhere someone is secretly manufacturing this weapon in quantities.

#3 These sleeves were specially designed for Colonel Kaehler of the southeast OT region. It is strictly private property. Any other OT employee trying to wear it, will be jailed, fined and lose his or her OT privileges. In severe cases even exiled from the planet.

#4 In Athen Shire there used to exist a small, independent research lab, under the supervision of Dr.Styve. When they recieved a large set of brand new Bio-Zymolots from Eple, they soon started test-production of biomech weapons. The most successfull of these designs were the Cumulus assault rifle models - produced in a limited edition. The lab was soon after ravaged and pillaged by OT paid mercenary forces, and Dr.Styve was perma-killed. Commander Kelly Frederickson and a band of 6 other clan members then hunted the evil-doers down, taking back the stolen weapons. The Commander since kept this rifle.

Feel free to add some more
