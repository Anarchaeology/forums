Outdoor Hunting
After my first charactr was determined to be gimped about two months after launch me and some RL friend started over in Tir BackYard 3 and at level two we all went out and started hunting outdoors. By level six we were in GTC doing the OT camp by the waterfall getting outageous XP and leveling about every 5 minutes. The spawn was fast and the mobs where RED to us at level 6 and still green to use at level 12. That was 45 minutes of pure fun. Then we al stopped upgraded armor weapons nanos and got supplies and headed out again. It took only 15 hours of outdoor hunting to level to 30. It was fun. It was better than fun. When outdoors teams get to work together.

Like at the temple. Our little group now level 30 to 34 was up there and the ancinet garg attacked us. Ok beyond our team to take. No prob another team jumped and wiith two docs healing my solider and 10 people beating the Ancient garg it died. It was great. SO what happened. The the two teams stay together and we went after even bigger stuff. So with two level 30-35 teams we clean that part of GTC out and all gained several levels. Heck I was leveling so fast that I would go to level up and have 50k worth of IP to use. The game play was fast, furious, and dangerous. But more importantly fun.

Now when my MA came up it was after the outdoor spawn killer patch. So at level 35 my MA could solo the temple and only had to run from ancient gargs. Hello. Solo the temple. That was un heard of at launch for a level 30 to 40 character. The spawn was to fast and big.

There was no reason to stay in the backyards in those days. Outdoor hunting was to fun. And you always got good loot off the mobs. In fact my first character did not by armor or weapons until level 20 or so. Why? Got it all off of mob drops outdoor hunting. When I deleted that character at level 25 I had 10 mission tokens. Had only donee 12 missions. Missions where for cash and hard to get items. Outdoor hunting was about conqueering Rubi Kai.

Oh well my trip down memory lane is over. I am off to the mission grind again. Just a side note. Outdoor hunting is about as dangerous as the back yard is. The spawn is just not big enough and fast enough to take out groups. It once to a well oiled group serious thought and attention to survive the spawn on at the temple up on the platform. Now it takes a solo solider with his MK shield and alpha strike to survive.

What a said game AO has become.

Hunting is not entirely dead, but people are
Started an MA alt the other day.

Soloed him to lvl 10 or so doing 100%missions, very easy and very fast.

Then did some mission teaming with pick up teams - my MA was tanking and on full aggro, mission at 75%. Levelling/Time ratio was good but not exceptional.

Decided to go out to old Robot Mountain in Greater Tir. No one there so soloed Mountain for 5 minutes. 2 other guys turned up and we ran to the Clan Op located North East in the zone (snakes, birds, scorpiods and an occasional OT Hunter). Never seen faster levelling ever on Rubi-Ka, unless you have a high level healer backing you obviously, but that is no fun anyway...

Now, at level 31, I am not sure where to go. At least I got my Yalm now, so I might be able to find some teams out in the wilderness.

Problem is, most people hang around inside cities these days all thinking that outdoor hunting sucks.

It honestly does not, unless you are after special loot from Team Mission Boss. Loot from wildlife mobs is not as good as from Mission NPC's but you will occasionally get the odd Ring and some other goodies.

Main thing is, good XP IS possible.

I cant tell how good the outdoors is for all levels, but flying around Belial Forest (whompa to Wine) there seem to be many really good MOB spawn areas around, probably level 100-150+, but I cant tell really. All mobs was deep red to my level 90 alt.

Outdoor hunting means you can use 3rd eye view camera, zooming in / out getting a very good overview of the area. Nicer scenery while playing!

There is more strategy involved outdoors because of Social mobs, Aggressive/Non aggresive etc. This adds to the fun IMHO.

Hunting in the wild is much more enjoyable for the Pet classes as it has been stated before, mainly because of Pet pathing issues.

Agents will have more problems getting off their aimed shots outdoors now (with the new Line of Sight rules) as there is no walls to hide behind, but if you use distance or vegetation as cover it can work.

Adventurers and Fixers and everyone else with high Run Speed has advantages beeing outdoors as it is relatively easy to do the old "running around the team in circles" if the aggro is stuck on them.

Thumbs up for getting out of the City Walls
