This will need to split into 2 classes.
A: Buffs. Buffing is tedious. I personally need to run 7 nanos before going into a mission for any chance of survival and this whole process is a pain in the backside. Having a script for defensive/basic buff nanos would just alleviate the boredom of it and I can't see a balancing issue there.

B: Offensive nanos. It will never happen, period. Having an NT being able to click a button to run a cycle of roots and nukes or whatever without any more interaction could lead to major imbalance issues. Sure you could do it yourself, but you're going to be slower and a hell of a lot more error prone than a script, especially in the heat of a close battle. This is the whole point of you as a person playing the game fighting your battles, not the script.

I can see why you'd want it as I would love it too but it aint gonna happen. I suggest you/we focus on trying to get the things out of Funcom that we can reasonably expect. ie choose the battles so to speak and in this case it's only a portion of the total.
Get FC to implement automating the buffs which to them has no negative impact and for us is very positive rather than them dismissing the whole idea cos we are pushing scripting all types of nanos which do have possible implications.
