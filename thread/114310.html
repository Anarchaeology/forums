The Bow Special skill is fairly straight-forward.

You need to have a bow equipped to use a special arrow and that bow must be a pure bow with Bow Skill as its main requirement (not rifle etc).

In order to use4 the special you need to meet the 'use' requirements on the arrow itself, which are usually Current Nano and Bow Special requirements.

To use the special arrow you simply right click on the stack (or allot the stack to a shortcut bar slot). The arrows need to be in your inventory to be used. Special Arrows are not loaded into the weapon in any conventional way. They are simply a stacked item that can only be used when you are wielding a suitable bow.

The arrows check against the defensive skill for the Bow in question (so usually dodge ranged).

The special arrow usually locks the Bow Special skill for a period of time.

It would be perfectly possible to have the arrows you describe simply be [nodrop] special arrows and allow MPs to use them with any bow capable of using special arrows. Indeed, the likelihood is high that the existing special arrows are due for updating as they are pretty much useless all in all and this is one of the elements that the MAs are chasing quite hard rigtht now.

Since both of the existing Creation Bow suggestions are pure bows with Bow as main requirement, there is no explicit reason why they might not be allowed to fire special arrows - even if this were limited to Creation Arrows.
