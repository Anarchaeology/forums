Clan call to arms (Vox Populi Recruiting RK2)
Clan Faction Vox Populi is currently seeking a few good Warriors to aid us in our fight against omni oppression and notum control. Interested Rimor clansmen/women can register for an interview on our forums here under the Joining The Vox fourm.

OOC: What you can expect form Vox Populi-

Role Playing
Vox is mainly know for being one of the most established clan Role Playing orgs of rimor taking part in FC run events as well as creating our own storyline and events.

Notum Wars
Vox is also very active when it comes to Notum Wars. Our goals in this respect is to aid all clans in aquireing and defending notum mines.

Hunting and Unique Mobs
Boss camps, dungen, and, unique mob Hunting parties are a fun passtime of vox members.

Guild participation
Members are expected to take an active role in the org's functions. Each applicant is required to complete a training session and pledge their services to a department before becoming a member. The training session consists of a variety of fun activities to give the applicant an idea of what they can expect form the org. Vox departments consist of DoMA (Department of Military Affairs), Administration, Research and Development, and Intell. Each department takes care of specific functions or the org and members of the departments are often called on to complete certain tasks.

Mentors
In addition to completing training, applicants are assigned a mentor from the org who is of the same profession. A mentor answers profession specific questions as well as general questions an applicant may have. A mentor also aids in supplying equiptment to the new applicant.

Community
Vox is a well established org that has been active for nearly 2 years. If you are looking for a fun and mature org with a good vibe vox is the place. We are a close nit family that always strives to help each other out.
