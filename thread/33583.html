Difference between 25% and 0% Gas
Isis,

To my understanding the difference between 25% and 0% is this:

In a 25% zone, combat is allowed between opposing sides and versus MOBs, of course.

In a 0% zone, anything goes. You can even attack people on the same side, with the exception of the other members of your team, if any.

Stret West Bank has an area (Last Ditch/area of Reet's Retreat) which is 75%. Going outside the wall of that small community, I've come across mostly 25% areas.

What I know of gas suppression percentages is exactly what Nivenyrral mentioned.

Gas suppression percentages:

100% - No Fighting Possible
75% - Wildlife May Attack
25% - Political Zone (PvP: Clan VS Omni-Tek, Clan VS Neutral, Omni-Tek VS Neutral) and wildlife attacks
0% - Mayhem Zone (Anything and anyone can attack anyone, regardless of faction. You can not attack a member of your team, however.)
