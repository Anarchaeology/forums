Tne Neon Zebra Social Club is, as the name suggestions, I place for people to come and socialize. We have five main rooms:
  • Zebra Lounge: Our main room will people are encouraged to have a drink, chat and sahre stories or adventures form their day.
  • Lion's Pride: A place for those who are seeking to join an organization, or guild, and for those seeking new members. We encourage people to use the space to meet, greet and get to know each other.
  • Tiger's Den: This room is for the adventurer, both seasoned and new. Rather you are looking for a group to take down some cyborgs or for a massive campaign against whoever (Clan, Omni, etc.), this room is for you. Chat, plan and recruitment is encourage here.
  • The Fox Hole: This space is for the merchants of Rubi-Ka to announce auctions, advertise their services and ask for help or advice from fellow merchants and trades-people.
  • Honey Comb Lounge: For the romantic and lonely alike, this room is to meet your future love or future ex. This is the singles room, where we encourage girls and guys, and even Atrox, to mingle, chat and put on the charm.


For each registered member you gain access to private messaging, event posting on our calender and a private notepad to keep track of whatever you like.

We also provide, within our entrance kiosk, customized advertising for merchants and organizations, our ever growing Grid directory, and access to the wonderful sounds of Gridstream Productions (not affiliated with the club). Although we are not an exclusive club, we do keep a good watch for any trouble makers that might show up.

We hope to provide all you social butterflies a safe and relaxing space to enjoy and expand your experience of life on Rubi-ka.
