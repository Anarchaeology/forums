I think the time is soon coming for a bug fix patch.
Teaming
-HP Bars reset to show just base hp's when someone moves out of and back into visual range.
-Teammates can become invisible sometimes when they move out of and back into visual range.
-Pet pathing is simply atrocious.

PvP
-The hp bar of your oponent does not show hp's gained from items and buffs. Thus you can shoot them for half their health before their hp bar starts to go down from your point of view.
-If your oponent moves out of and back into visual range during combat it causes all sorts of problems. Can't disengage combat, can't see attacks even tho you're still doing damage, can't fire specials or use ma attacks. Ect ect.

Items
-BBI Faithfuls use the wrong init
-A number of other weapons also use the wrong inits.
-A number of ma attack are screwed up.
-Wen Wen's don't attack.


Now these are the only bugs I can think of off the top of my head, feel free to add more my fellow anarchists . The most important from my point of view are all the hp bar bugs, pvm and pvp. Redo the hp bar if you need to funcom, it isn't working right.
