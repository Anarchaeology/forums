Report to NT community on Pest Raid
I arrived on site at 10:30 AM GMT and quickly people flowed to the site, soon allowing a team to be formed of strength enough to do the mission.

Objective JOB THE PEST!

This was however harder than first thought due to him being shy with all the people standing around ready to execute him.

Over a 10 hour camping spree (yes i didnt leave for 10 hours, except for a 10 min food break) the Plague Rot was driven from the face of the planet repeatedly but the Pest failed to show.

He appeared once at 1pm (approx) GMT.

He was sumilarily dispatched after he unfortunaelt broke through our lines and attacked for no apparent reason Zarandia, mortally felling him.

The two NTs eligable for the raffle were Ravenous and Maddiganed. Maddiganed gallently turned down the option to roll and gave Ravenous the nano. All hail Maddiganed! Hydral, a meta-physicist of notable skill won the roll for the Petticoat. I hope he looks good in it!

At 7:50pm (approx) GMT the Pest appeared for a second time and after a much harder battle (due to a lowering in numbers thanks to the camelot raid and the lunitic raid) we again took him down, unfortunately, the heroic meta-physicst Jamespond sacrficed himself onto a Pus to give the calmers time to do their work.

The two NTs who were eligable for this raffle (being the only two there) were Deatheyes and myself. Deatheyes, in what I can only call an amazing act on generosity, stated I could have the nano as I had organised it and been there all day. Words fail me, I cannot thank him enough! hawkeye, whoes accuracy is a thing of deadlyness, won the raffle for, also, a petticoat, again I hope it suits him.
