Well, I'm not there yet, but my understanding is that missions go well past QL200 while players are capped at level 200. So the game can spawn these super mobs anytime the players want.

Problem is, players that high say the time it takes to kill these mobs isn't worth it. That is, the xp/hour ratio drops so that they're better off killing greens and yellows for xp. Throw in the fact that you're not going to die (and lose xp) while fighting easy mobs and no one goes after the big guys without a good reason.

The biggest mob I know of is Tarasque. I'm told it takes two full teams to take him down. But he drops some of the most sought after loot in the game.
