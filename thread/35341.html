A good idea, but...
The problem that I see is that newbies would be dead meat. You get a large group along with a few lower level twinked guys and you would have tons of newbies getting killed. The solutino would be to make certain cities (Tir, Athen, Omni 1) unattackable.

The other problem is one that already exists, and that is zoning in and out of stores etc. to heal teamates but to avoid getting killed. You might want to either have all stores close during an attack, or make stores have a tmie limit (30 seconds) between when you leave, and when you can enter again.
