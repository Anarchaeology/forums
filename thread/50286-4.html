Sariel, if you're using a weapon with a large crit number, you really need to try to raise your crit chance as high as possible to get the most out of your weapon. Asking an MA for a crit buff can help a lot. Agents also have crit buffs. In addition, you should try to get items that raise your chance of landing a crit. The most popular is the Low Light Targeting Scope, but these don't drop any more so they're very difficult to attain. A lesser, but more available, choice is the Vision Enhancer.

Also, increasing your attack rating will increase your chance of hitting and of critting. So get the biggest possible implants you can for your weapon of choice.

Personally, I got scared off of the Ithaca route. The common belief is that if you lack a high QL LLTS, it just won't work well for you.
