Alchemy Blue: Memory Log 13 - Untied
I watched from what seemed to be an unbearably lofty height, unable to breathe and not really knowing why. Everything seems so peaceful when people are so far away that they resemble busy little ants. Peaceful and lonely. It was the only way I could bear to see it anymore. Too many inherent problems, too many unadressed conflicts of interest, too many issues blindly passed by. There was nothing I could do to fix any of it.

I am a proud woman, but I know my limits. I had to face this one, acknowledge it and get on with my life. Why can't I breathe? It's not the thinning of the air. My yalmaha compensates for that. Maybe it's a sense of unbelievable loss. But there really wasn't anything TO lose, was there? In their infinite variety and dissonance, the clans all shared a few very distressing similarities. Too many people fell through the cracks and too many people watched and did nothing. Was everyone like that? I didn't know. I was beginning to think so. Still, there had to be something different. Something better. Something more utopian. Something more human. Or should I say more humane?

I look down at the application again, running a trembling finger over its cold surface. Is this what it's come to? Yes. I think it has. Maybe the answers I am looking for have been hiding in the one place I haven't wanted to look. Maybe Omni IS the way. I'll never know if I stand hanging at the doorstep, afraid to walk in.

Clenching my teeth I jab the acceptance button and am done with it. My clans board falls to the floor between my feet. The sound it makes on the plasteel is sharp and hollow, fragile, like my faith in the so-called cause. My comlink beeps, trying to summon my attention and I look at it with an almost horrified fascination. Should I answer? I don't know. It's Aamuel... and Knightheart... and Leroux... Those three I will answer. I didn't expect more than one of them.

Aamuel sounds tense. Angry. Maybe a little sad. It's so hard to tell with the distortion.

"What was that? And why?"

How do I answer? What can I say that will justify my action to someone who was until only seconds ago a comrade? Do I really want to explain? How much DO I owe him? Well he noticed the switch and my sudden disappearance...

"I went Omni."

I know that my voice is flat and unemotional. It gives very little away. It is at odds with the chaos that roars inside me.

"Could you have at least given us a little warning?"

Yes. Aamuel is angry. I flatly offer a very generalized, uncomfortable and terse reply as I look at the blinking comlink listing. Knightheart's sorrowful "Why?" cuts me deeper than I thought it might. He thinks it's his fault, just as he thinks that the rest of this world's wrongs are his doing. He never did anything but try to befriend me. How could he have driven me to this? But this is hard to explain and I do what I can to reassure him and remind him that my employment doesn't keep me from still being his friend. After all, what the Company doesn't know...

Leroux. I swallow the lump that is my heart to get it out of my throat. This was the only one I expected, really. And the one that I dreaded the most. My mentor. My friend. Was this going to be a goodbye? Or was it going to be something else. My mouth is dry and my fingers are numb. How much of the truth can I handle? Do I really want to know if he despises me for my choice? Can I bear it if he does? I'm not sure. Everything inside me has come untied. The chaos inside threatens to engulf me completely as I force myself to answer.

He's never really going to know. Because there are some things you never tell anyone. Because they have to see it for themselves. Because if you have to tell them, then it ceases to be as pure and there was never any hope of it anyway. Life's a bitch that way.

Borealis winds kiss me goodbye
Fly me where I need to be
Whisper words of wisdom in my ears
And set my weary spirit free

Borealis zephyrs lead the way
Show me now which way to roam
So I can find another day
And my heart can find its home

Fare thee well, and may your hearts burn with valour... eternally.
