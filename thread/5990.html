Whosale stores for traders
The 2 big things that I think traders need are the ability to open their own shop and to buy things from a wholesaler.

Lets tackle the second one first. Right now Funcom is envisioning a black market for illegal goods, this is great for fixers and possibly for shady traders, but traders and engineers need a place that they can buy components at a much cheaper price. Yes I know that the prices in the trade section could be lowered but my next suggestion ties in with our own shops. I would love the ability to buy the vending machines to put in our shops, that way we could offer not only offer our services and the goods we can make but offer the things that we cant make at a slightly cheaper price then the other stores or have more of a variety. They way I see the vending machines working is we buy the terminal then we buy, from the wholesaler, whatever we want to stock it with. In my way you could tailor your terminals to what your customers want Right now if you want to buy a weapon you go to the terminal and sort through all the available stuff there and you may get lucky and find what you want. In my shop I might have a terminal devoted to nothing but shotguns or maybe just sense agility armor. At least thats the way I envision it.

If you dont like the idea of every trader being able to open his/her own shop then make a title requirement on the idea. Lets say that to open your own shop you have to be level 50 (the full trader status) but before you reach that level have a large common area like a market zone where low level traders and engineers can lease space to put up their terminals. When I say lease that is just what I mean to, so many credits per terminal spot per month. The same goes for shops when you can open them you have to buy or lease the building. I could see a whole subclass of trader doing nothing but buying buildings, setting up terminals and other shop equipment then selling the whole outfit to other traders or engineers.

Im not a programmer so I have no idea how difficult this idea would be, but I think there is a lot of potential in it. I see this as a way to sell items you make or take special orders for items, provide better shopping for the other people in the game, and provide for a valid player run economy.

That worked out pretty good in Ultima Online. I ran a few shops back when I played that game...

I'd like to see the Trader limited to just one terminal at a time (but you could close it down and move it somewhere else). I'm not sure where the locations would be though, or if there should be a fixed # of locations or not.

The best solution may be to have something like this:
Have "Player Terminals" scattered about the world, in cities, and in some hunting spots, etc. If a trader wants his shop to be accessed at that particular player terminal, he has to pay xx credits per day/week/whatever (and he can buy access to many terminals, maybe 10 or unlimited).

When a player accesses one of these terminals, he sees a list of all the Traders who have bought access rights to that terminal. It could show the Trader's name, Store name, and a line for description (Agil/Sense Armor). Then the player can search the different Traders' shops and buy to their hearts content. (hope that made sense).

I'd like to see this be a trader only thing. I can imagine Fixers buying black market items, selling them to a Trader, who puts it up for sale in his store.
