Love for All Professions! Old Nanos! Tradeskills! Cheap Prices!
Well, ive had an idea building in the back of my head for around, dunno, a year maybe, about putting yoyur old nanos back to use.

This would require alot of programming, i think, but here it goes...

  • You take an empty nano crystal and place it on the ground
  • You then target the nano
  • You then begin to cast a nano that you have uploaded onto the target empty nano crystal
  • Once the program has been executed succesfully, it is placed into the empty nano crystal, restoring it for use amongst other players.


Now, i understand that if this was implimented as stated above, it would be extremely good, almost too good, so here are the exceptions.

  • The restored nano crystal has increased requirments, anywhere from 5%-15%
  • The percentage is based on your nano programming skill
  • There is a 0%-15% chance to fail the creation of the nano crystal, which is again absed on your nano programming
  • A set amount of NP is required to even make an attempt at the creation
  • The nano crystal can only stay alive, for 12 hours of in-game play time
  • Once everything is complete, you have a new nano crystal in your inventory, and the nano program is deleted from your library


Here is an example of a restored nano crystal...

I think this could definitly help out the less fortunate, maybe theres a level 80 NT, who has been saving up for NS MKI, and just doesnt have enough money to buy it. a NT could create a Restored Nano Crystal (Nullity Sphere MKI) and sell it to him for a cheaper price.

and maybe there are some fixers who have GA MKIV, but dont need I II and III, pop it in a crystal and sell away.




This has been a long post, and im sorry for any typos, but i think this kind of an addition to the game could definitly help.

also, heres a question that im kind of confused with, if you understand all of this, would this decrease inflation?

thanks for your bumps, and your constructive criticism if you see something wrong with it.
