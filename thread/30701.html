If they gave me 10 things to change.....
1. LAG! Now I don't know if this is my machine or what but zoning into Ent, Trade, Lush, Clon****, Galway, or Newland is taking way too long. Comp specs:

P3 1ghz, 512mb RAM
GeForce2 (ick) Pro 64MB RAM
Sittin on a T1.

2. LAG! In team missions, there is much lag. Ever see the whole "starting attack failed, you have been hit for 246 pts of unknown dmg" thing? Or what about "Waiting for previous special attack to complete"?

3. The outdoors! Rubi-Ka has too much unused real estate and not all of us have the tolerance to deal with #2. High level peeps need high level monsters outdoors to kill in a 75% zone. Ever since mort went to 25%...nuff said.

4. The OE. Last night during the patch, I wrote a crazy lil thread titled "They are going about it all wrong". In a nutshell, OE needs to be removed from the game. Why nerf twinking when encouraging it would drastically increase the longetivity of the game?

5. The IPR. Cool concept, bad implementation. IPR points need to be given out PER LEVEL, not per title level. Being able to experiment with different abilities would also increase the longetivitiy of the game. And what is it with having to be completely naked? Problematic to code? It is a simple calculation to subtract any points added to the base by implants / armor / weapons to determine how many points should be given on a rollback.

6. PvP. There is much to fix within the PvP aspects of the game. First, the gankers. Two ways to deal with them. Make the HP display above players' heads disappear when in 25% or mayhem zones and implement /duel & /teamduel.

Second, and this is NT-specific, NT nukes should do full dmg. No other class had their nukes nerfed and its not like we are godly with weapons. Oh...and those nasty huge dmg nukes that we have take ions to cast anyway.

Thirdly, we need insurance / reclaim terminals at the arenas. I know, minor, but I hate walking back after I die and I also would like to have a chance to save my XP before going into a fight if I forgot to do it somewhere else.

7. Item farming. This is a problem and I'm sure those who have been to Steps of Madness only to see Neleb get whacked by a pair of 200 MAs know what I mean. All loot off of static dungeon mobs should be no-drop unique. Also, static dungeon mobs should be restricted to only those who can get more than 1 xp point for killin it.

8. Stats that are broken. Nano Resist tops my list of stats that need to be fixed. I been maxing out Nano Resist since conception and through 60 levels have yet to 'counter' a nano being casted on me.

9. 1 minute belt equip time. Uhhh...are you trying to make this game more like EQ with the additional sit around and do nothing time it takes to equip the belt? The less sitting around doing nothing I have to do, the more I want to play. Belts should only take 10 seconds to equip, just like NCUs.

10. Tradeskills. There probably is a few items in the game that can only be obtained by tradesklls but there needs to be more and the recipes need to work and need to be easily accessible.

Also, doing tradeskills should provide better XP. After doing some calculation, I figure at level 60 I'd have to make 600+ QL 70 implants before I could level up to 61 non-destructively, whereas doing a QL 100 mission would give me the level after killing about 30 mobs. 30 vs 600? I could live with 30 vs maybe 90. But 600?
