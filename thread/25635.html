How to make PvP enjoyable - my <humble> opinion
The main problem of PvP is that players like to kill, but not like to be killed. It's fun for lvl 150 to kill lvl 75, but not fun for lvl 75 to be killed by lvl 150. Is it possible to solve this problem ? IMO yes.
Ther should be device in the game to allow 2-sided mission in which one side has very low probability of success. But reward for complition such a "mission impossible" should far outweight risk involved. For example 2 lvl 150 char should defend outpost for 1 hour against 10 lvl 50-100 players and assising them 10 NPC, functioning as pet (if there are not enough PC they could be substituted by +20 level NPC) if they won they get 10 "token of honor" which could be put into board.

just my 2 tokens
