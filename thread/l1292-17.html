Silidan
the Grid for MAW
The Grid is a 3D angular grid layered many levels deep.
- It's the AO version of cyberspace.
- The grid is based on Computer Literacy. Low levs can only access the bottom rungs of the grid and travel to the main cities, but they can see high leves moving about above them.
- Every part of this area is some shade of light blue.
- There are numerous nodes where you can port around the world.
- Information about various areas will be available from here such as /list players, /list professions or /list levels.
- Fixers and Traders will have the best access to the Grid.
- You navigate a point-down pyramid avatar as a low level grid runner.
- The appearance of this avatar can be upgraded as you gain levels on the grid.
- Grid avatar is different than your character.
- I saw no combat in the Grid, and I assume is not possible.
