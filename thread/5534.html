Top quality of life suggestions
I loved the store changes! No more running around like an idiot looking for the right cluster. I would like to see more such "Quality of Life" changes for things that annoy me on a near-daily basis:

* Fix container gnomes

This is completely irritating - if something is supposed to be fixed, then isn't fixed, please fix it asap

* Fix Pet alignment when zoning

Currently this drops, placing pets in tab-list - annoying

* Allow Resizble windows

3x3 inventory? 3x2 trade? 3x2 bank? Awful!

* Fix Team leader disappearing

Teams should always have a leader

* Fix player dropping team when going ld

If a player goes ld, they should stay in the team until they return - color their name red, remove after 5 minutes. Even better, when they log back in they could warp to the team leader so the team can keep moving.

* Look into alpha loot messing up

This is still buggy

* Automatically put teammates in control-tab

Alignment really has nothing to do with hostility - teammates and their pets should not be in your "hostile" tab cycle

* Do not clear map when leaving mission area

Annoying

* Place zone boundary toggle on big map

Tir is not really north of deep artery valley, and we need to use external maps to figure this out. We should be able to see passable/impassable zone boundaries

* Add distance slider on mission

A "close" mission should have low credit/xp reward, but maintain mission difficulty and reward. Why go to Mort to get a mission in Broken Shores?

* Add grid term in 20K

This will unclog Omni Trade a bit, and life will be better for all

Anyone else have ideas?

--Xente
