Party Time at Reets (FNN News Story)
PARTY TIME AT REETS

In the midst of all the political controversy surrounding the Council of Truth, ICC Peacekeepers,and Omni-tek's accusations, it is nice to know that some people can sit back and relax. And there is no better place to relax that Reet's Retreat. Where ever Saturday at 10pm EST (3 GMT) The Reformists, host a party for their fellow Clansmen.

I attended this week's party, and believe me, fun was spread all around. The beer flowed, as well as the cheer. I happened to get a word with The President of the Reformists, Adan "Basheen" Fadri, and I asked him to comment on the party. This is what he had to say:

"I am here trying to help unify the clans. Omni only stays stronger than the rebels because many clans can't see eye to eye. But since our inception of this party.. the clans have grown together!"

However, this party is not only for clanners.

"Many neutrals have joined the clan side through this party and as I said a few Omni will show soon. And their own words are.. man clanners have way more fun!" Basheen added gleefully.

We asked about the serious issue of the ICC Peacekeepers, and the possibility of their parties being seen as nothing more than a method of recruiting more troops, Mr. Fadri had this to say in response:

"Bah... Party trancends all! This is the one thing all humanoids relate to...that is why we don't fret. We don't truly recruit.. we just take them in if they wish to change their political stance. The one thing all inhabitants of this planet can agree on is FUN and RELAXATION. We need to take our minds off the war every now and then."

A relax they did. A happy Clan/Nuetral crowd partied the night away, oblivious to the Conflict, as if there was not a care in the world. And for once, for these partygoers, everything on Rubi-ka was good. The Clans and Nuetrals will go back into a another week of work, and then come back to Reets again next saturday to party. Hope to see you there!
