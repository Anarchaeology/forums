A whooooole buncha combat questions (not pvp)
Hopefully funcom will answer some of my questions here tho I'm not holding my breath. In the meantime if some of my fellow players could help I would muchly apreciate it.

I'm a lvl 117 soldier using a ql 200 novaflow. I have 700 attack or so when buffed, am using a low light scope that gives +2 crits, and I have 600 or so ranged init.

1) So why do I miss so often when fighting green mobs? I'll be fighting a mob and I'll miss like 6 or 7 times in a row for no reason. I'll think the guns empty or something so I'll check but nope, still rounds left to fire. Or it'll happen after a reload and the first bunch of rounds the gun fires will all miss (I'll check once it starts doing damage again and it'll be at 16/20 or something). These miss jags can carry on for quite some time, sometimes they end on their own and I start hitting again, and sometimes a fling will come up that I use and I'll start hitting again. So why does a soldier with over 700 attack rating miss so bloody often, and in such numbers all strung together?

2) And speed, with just under 600 init why isn't my nova flow capped out at 1:1? It takes roughly 3 seconds for each bit of damage to happen, 3 seconds for each shot (i used a metranome). This is on full agg, I was hoping to be able to move my agg bar down but at this rate it'll never move. Does init do anything at all???

3) Ok, i wield a ranged gun. Is there any advantage to having range? Mobs just teleport right to you, that guy with the baseballbat seems to have 10m of range easy. (yeah yeah roots don't prevent me from fighting back whoopdy do) In a new mission room there's walkways going up 4 levels in the room and I'll work my way up and start shooting a mob and instead of granting me the range advantage I should have while they climb up using the ramps, they teleport right on top of me.

4) What exactly is the RangInc # for range extenders and low light scopes? Does RangeInc 5 for instance increase your range by 5m, or by 5%? And do low light scopes and movement predictors stack? Or does one supercede the other (which one?), or does the better of the two come into effect? (please don't say "it's like this" unless you've actually tested it for concrete proof, I've heard all the speculation, I want to know for sure)

5) Well, I thought I had more questions but whatever they are I've forgotten.

The first four is enough tho, please someone at funcom could you answer my questions. (especially #1, I'm tired of being the "king of ranged combat" and missing so often)

I'm an NT and can only comment on point #1.

NT's have the same problem and have been complaining about it for months. We don't get dodge's like you do, we get counters (resists) to the nanos and they also come in chains. I've gotten up to 7-8 at a time, though 4-5 is more common. Like you, I've found it tends to happen most often with very weak mobs (one's that con deep green to me) and rarely if ever with yellow or reds. Att ne point I actually started calculating probabilities and found that if a green mob mob countered one nano, the chances were 85%+ they would counter thesecond. If they countered 2 in a row, it was ~80% chances they would counter a third, and so on. It's almost as if the AI is cheating (as in non-MMO) computer games to give weak mobs a chance and to make the fight a bit tougher. In reality i think the random number generator for 'save' rolls just gets stuck, though why it should stick for only relatively 'easy' enemies is beyond me.

I remember one fight from back before they got rid of the dozens of grey mobs in missions. I was fighting a grey fixer type (trickster). and using ol' faithful at the time (a nano that gives the caster a 2:1 advantage in terms of being hard to resist). I started the fight by landing 3 shots, then got 3 resists, 2 fumbles, 3 resists, 1 hit, 2 resists, and finally landed 3 shots to make the kill.

Oh yes! one other thing I noticed. the chain resists rarely started until the mob was down to 30-40% health. Even now, when fighting green mobs, once their health drops below 50% I start holding my breath and praying the chains don't start.
