Fixer Shop? Well, fixer and agent shop. I might visit it once a week to buy Cluster Bullets--there's nothing else of value in the Fixer Shop. Cosmik, Devs, fellow fixers--we've got *one* thing going for us as a class, and that one thing has been almost impossible to find, hideously expensive from any indirect source, and virtually career-crippling in nanoskill costs.

Now they're going to add features to it. This is like putting surround sound in a million dollar car--it doesn't increase the usefulness of viability of Grid Armor because it is still all-but-impossible to obtain and wear.

The new "debuff debuff" line? I'm sure the requirements will prevent us from being able to cast them at a level where they make the slightest difference (edit: this is really just a boon for agents, who I'm sure will be able to use them before we will).

We'd be standing there, next to invincible in our grid armor (except for that nasty chain crit bug), taking five to ten minutes to kill an even con mob because our damage output is a joke.

Fixers, *demand* that Funcom actually fix your class and stop throwing this poorly thought out, poorly implemented trash our way.

Sad to say, I'm very disheartened by this official response--Funcom is no closer to understanding the problems of the class than they were when the instituted the universally mocked Summon line.
