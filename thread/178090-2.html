Power Up, Scotty
Nice compilation Jynne,

out of boredom and in hunt for tokens I decided to spend 7 perk points on Power Up (182 Doc) and I love it!

The mobs in my, admittedly low (ql163), solo missions were dropping like flies on a hot day.

The specials never landed until I poured my 220k+ spare IP into ranged energy... hmm, the specials ain't *that* nice but sometimes add another 200-1200 damage every 2 minutes (they recharge fast but never before Energize wears off) - I don't really know whether the stun really works, as the diference is hard to tell when they have Rapid Palsy already running :P
Mobs that have high evades (MAs and Fixers) usually resisted the specials, but RE isn't maxed or implanted yet.
Since I usually PVP at full-def I don't know whether the +105 dmg will do any good there, but I have good hopes for the stun once I get my RE a bit higher.

The reason I didn't do Specialist Healer yet is I don't have any problems healing on RK (sigh don't start about SL lol) and the specials I'm waiting for (Vaccinate and Hale and Hearty) aren't available to my 182 Doc.

Just my 2 eurocents,

cheers,
