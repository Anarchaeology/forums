We're at WAR - give us TANKS
After having read the timeline events and the constant battling, I think the idea of Armored Fighting Vehicles makes sense.

A tank could be used for speed, have limited visibility (to allow infantry some combat viability), and have excellent vehicle vs vehicle firepower.

Haven't put a whole lot of thought into this, but I'm sure its workable.

WE'RE AT WAR! Allow us the tools of warfare!
