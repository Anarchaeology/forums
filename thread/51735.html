I think if implemented right this doesn't have to reduce interaction between players. The ways you do this is simple:

1) All buffs should be extraordinarally priced (so as they are only used on rare occassions)

2) If any buffs are offered by certain proffesions then they are priced well above what any proffesion would charge for them (like 1mil per)... also leave out any buff that is Lvl125+... Personally I don't think you need to add buffs that are offered by other proffesions

3) Buffs are mainly buffs that are not offered by other classes. For Instance a buff that adds +20 attack rating or +20 defense rating could be added.

4) Pills can be offered that are proffesion specific as well (new buffs of course), but since pills are hackable this gives an extra trade skill for the elite hackers out there.

Maybe these pill terminals could only be available at remote locations with nano reqs on them high enough that low level players don't have to have them to be on par.
