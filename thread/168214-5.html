DoctorFun,

You quite correctly point to the difference in IC / OOC. Then again, sexual harassment is such a touchy subject that I can hardly imagine any RPGer wanting to play a persona who is 'into' this kind of thing in AO or any other 'normal' MMORPG.

In other words, there are MUDs where such behaviour is OK. Every player who logs onto them knows that. In all other online games, such behaviour is not tolerated (and even may get you banned) and thus no one who logs onto them expects it (I would guess) - IC or OOC.

AO clearly falls into the latter category.

There is so little roleplay in AO that I would assume any crude remarks or behaviour of that sort to be OOC and thus a bannable offense.

Telling the offender OOC that you are not into IC stuff like that is a nice idea, but IMHO would not work at all, since the offender would only laugh at you and continue his assault. A negative outlook on thongs? Yes, perhaps...

About teaching ethics: a noble cause, and yet...

I think that no RPGer would ever start this kind of thing unless he was abolutely sure it is not unwanted, especially in a game like AO that is not considered 'mature' like some MOOs. IMHO there is just too little roleplay in AO for that. Consentual IC acting between players is something altogether, of course (and preventing 'accidental' accusations of harassment is a moot point unless FC were to implement measures that could result in such accidents).

Other people acting like that are, IMHO, jerks who should be banned at once (and given a chance to explain themselves later). RP ethics, I think, would be lost on them.
