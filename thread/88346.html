The Omega Element. Clan for Agents
A free society is comprised of many elements. People, organizations, political structures and much more go into making freedom work.
Merely the idea of freedom is a complex thing, wherein the general population itself agrees to adhere to the structure of that which makes freedom possible.
There are political figureheads who are, supposedly, publically appointed to represent the best interests of the people.
There are businesses who help service and fund that society by means of creating an economy, thereby helping the average citizen free to live such a life.
But underneath it all, way back in the dark recesses of the inner workings of a free society is one element. An element that secretly toils constantly to ensure that the structures that allow freedom to exist, maintain their hold, no matter how precarious; no matter what the cost.
That one secret element is to be known as "The Omega Element"

Interested Agents seeking to join the cause, Send tell to Commander420.
Any questions or responses may be posted here as well as I check periodically.
