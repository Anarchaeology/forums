Yes, it really is perverse that you have to spend several minutes at the end of every level trying to find the elevator you found earlier. In teams its even worse than soloing , because the team often separates during this process, and if a member finds a monster he's toast. Further, the problem exists on the way down as well as on the way up.

Lastly, LDs also have their map wiped, and that just adds to the aggravation.

Both of these problems are trivial to fix.
