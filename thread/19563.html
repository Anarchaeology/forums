did you go here?
Try out the Borg Dungeon in Greater Tir County with a team. Go North from Tir West gate until you come to the Cyborg Domain region and look for a camp in a rock wall.

Lots of fast respawning Borgs and a Boss named General Jacota(sp). Try and get a complete set of Cyborg Armor for the +42 NCU upgrade and some other nice NoDrop weapons.
