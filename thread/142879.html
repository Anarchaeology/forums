Help Wanted - (Trainee Omni Investigator)
From: kithrak.redtape.omni-admin.otrk
To: public.jobsearch.otrk

From the desk of Administrator Kithrak,

There are one to two openings for the position of Trainee Omni Investigator. Duties include assisting in the handleing of a larger case and detective work. Light to moderate travel is expected to and fromt he office and the field in search of clues. Must be able to provide own transportation. Need to be able to report information found on the field and if needed write reports.

The ability to defend one's self in a hostile situation a plus. Coming from a respectable department or organization will also improve chances.

Other requirements:
  • Test scores of 50 or higher in travelling experience.(Level 50+)
  • Absoloutely no background or affiliation with the criminal elements.
  • Know the locations of all resturants, bars and nightclubs in Neutral and Omni areas.
  • Be able to navigate to a destination useing only coordinates.


This position is an internship and no salary will be offered. This does not constitute employment in the Red Tape sub-department for Omni-Admin. Apply in person by contacting Administrator Kithrak. Position to last for the duration of the investigation.

Do not miss out on this great resume building opportunity!

Administrator Kithrak,
Red Tape, Omni-Admin

------------------------------------------------------------

Kithrak hit the send key on his datapad and uploaded it into the Omni public job search network.

The Omni-Admin Guardian droid standing guard by the door to his office tilted its head in thought. "Sir, shouldn't you inform the potential applicants that position is a bit dangerous for the small benfits recieved?"

He chuckled lightly at his delibrate forgetfulness. "Do you know how much hazard pay or professional investigator costs? I have been doing all this extra work and Omni-Admin haven't paid ME anything yet."

"Aye sir, it would be nice to have someone else take the risks for a change." The Guardian droid nodded mechanically with the electronic equivalent of relief at the thought of not having to be put in harm's way as often.

Kithrak stretched and looked down at the window from his office in Omni-Admin headquaters at the center of Omni-HQ. Six floors below in the central courtyard employees can be seen milling about or moving purposefully to their destinations. "We'll just have to wait and see if anyone will be foolish enough to apply for the position."
