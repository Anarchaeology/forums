S.P.O.C. Guild recruitment for Neuts 9/22/02
S.P.O.C. Single Point of Contact. A mercenary guild. A fighting force. An information and data resource brokerage service. A Team. If you think you have what it takes to be a part of the S.P.O.C. team, you are requested to meet at the sattelite dish in Newland on 9/22/02 at 8PM est. Diatsu along with Fleshgrind will be interviewing any candidates who are interested. We especially need a website coordinator, a recruitment officer, as well as an event planner. All breeds and professions welcome. S.P.O.C. will specalize in combat tactics, recon, as well as information brokering and nano construction. Our unique talents will be sold to the highest bidder, regardless of affiliation or political agenda. The Guild stucture will be a loose one, with an emphasis on individuality. However, make no mistake that S.P.O.C. must be a priority in your life here on Rubi-Ka 1. The team's needs outweigh that of the individuals and certain policies must, and will be adhered to at all times. After all, we have a reputation to uphold. Specifics will be discussed at the meeting. Do not be late, recruitment briefing starts promptly at 8PM.

PS. This is an RPG guild. If you do not enjoy roleplaying your character, this may not be the guild for you.
