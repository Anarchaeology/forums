Suggestions for the NT
Hi there! I'm Naraya, level 55 NanoTechnician. I'd like to suggest a few things about the profession.....

** Nano line suggestions **
Our nano lines don't reflect the profession of us being the "masters of nano technology". We have lots of damage nanos and not enough utility nanos. Here's what I suggest:

Take away the NT DoT line of nanos. They are useless!

We have spells that improve range, nano cost, and recharge rate. Do we have one that increases NanoInit? If not, why not?

Almost every other class has some sort of specialized buff line, be it self or targetable ( i.e. Wrangle, Mastery, etc...). No, don't give us a buff line. Give us a debuff line. Name it "Crack Skill: xxx", base it off PsyMod and SenseImp, and make it drop the desired skill by 50, 150, and 300 as the line gets higher up in level.

Give us a line of nanos that can make us invisible and allow us to see through invisibility. Base the nanos on SenseImp/Perception and SenseImp/Concealment. They should last no more than 20 minutes.

And finally, no other class should be able to out-nuke an NT damage-wise. Make it so!

** Skill suggestions **
Our skill set seems pretty right on with the exception of break & enter not being green. Break & enter is the skill checked when we use the hacker tool. This should be green and not blue for NTs. Perception should also be green.

** PvP suggestions **
I'm 0-3! :-(. Why? My nano's only do half-dmg and only have half the range. WHY? Nanos should do full damage regardless of whether its PvE or PvP. MP nukes aren't nerfed in PvP are they? Neither are Crat nukes. What gives? Our mez line does NOTHING in PvP. To all the soldiers who are pissed at us NTs for carrying nova-flows, I say direct your anger at Funcom for nerfing the NT nukes in PvP. If you're still upset, consider this:

In PvE.....My max nova-flow burst dmg that I've seen on an xp giving mob is 302. My max dmg from a fast-cast nano that I've seen on an xp giving mob is 254. Go figure.

In PvP.....My max dmg from a fast-cast nano is 99. 99!!!! NINETY-NINE!!!!!!! What is up there? I've used the flow once in a PvP fight but never got a shot off because I was dead too quickly (fought an agent 7 levels higher than I who got off a damn good aimed shot).

If giving us back our full damage causes fear, then how about making NT nukes cast twice as fast in PvP?

Naraya, 55 Solitus NT.
