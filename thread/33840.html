This may sound strange, but I learned of this from Hextorr, a lvl200 fixer, and then tested it myself several times...
Basically, if you can get the cluster into the basic lvl200 imp, the cluster gets UPGRADED to ql200.

What I mean is this. Whether you put a ql180 or a ql200 cluster into a ql200 basic imp, THEY WILL HAVE THE SAME STATS!

I've tested this many times. Just last night, I took a ql196 basic implant with a ql180 shining cluster, and a ql200 basic implant with a ql195 shining cluster (both the same imp and cluster, I think it was an arm and a strength cluster).

Then I got a friend to put both of those together.

Basically, the ql200 basic implant with ql180 cluster had BETTER stats than the ql197 (upgraded from 196 due to the maker's NP).

Also, I have about 4 ql200 implants each of right arm/left arm (changing small things like brawling, piercing, str, or phys init), and all the ql200 ones have the EXACT SAME value (when the clusters are the same type, not ql).

Another thing I noticed, I got a ql 174ish faded parry cluster, noted the 'effect' (I think it was supposed to add 38 or so to parry), and then when I got it implanted in a ql200 implant, it said 'parry 42' or so. (Don't remember exact numbers, but I'm sure it was raised).

So basically people, don't worry so much about the ql of the clusters! If you can put it in the implant, they will upgrade to the implant's ql! This should save you time AND money I hope.
