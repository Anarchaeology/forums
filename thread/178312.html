Join The Nanomage Liberation Front ((RK-1 Roleplaying org))
Hello I am Chi Fadri, leader of the Nanomage Liberation Front. If you are a Nanomage and your tired of being looked down upon we have an organization for you. Are you tired of being called the "N" word "Nanogimp" or just plain "gimp"? Are you concerned that the only home for Nanomages is under threat of distruction by the forces of greed? The Nanomage Liberation Front fights for the rights of Nanomages all over Rubi-Ka.

Now not all Mages are right for the NLF, even though we serve the rights of all Mages. We accept applications from Mages of all factions although due to some technical difficulties those Omni and Clan mages need to go through genetic re-engineering ((re-roll neutral)) to join. It isn't as bad as it seems, many of our Mages are former Clan and Omni. All applicants must be serious about helping the Nanomage cause and protecting Rubi-Ka.

We also have some limited space available for our special Atrox Royal Guard unit who's sworn duty is to help protect our Nanomage members.

All members will find that the NLF is also very much an organization that helps it's members. More than just giving stuff and items, we try to teach each member the skills they need to be a success and show that Nanomages are not "Gimps" in any proffession. We have some very succesfull Nanomages of all proffessions including the more combat oriented ones. We share whatever we can to help each other, and will always be there for our members when they need our help.

We are a small close knit society, as close as a family some say. If this seems like an organization you are interested in please read our archives here http://www.zenpushergasoline.net/nlf/ . Pay special attention to our story, guild rules, manifesto, borealis pact, and recruitment guidelines. I know that this is a lot but the path to enlightenment comes with knowledge, and knowledge comes with work. If after reading this information you wish to join, please contact these people:
