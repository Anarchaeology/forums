The Grid:
Well, it is a 3D angular grid layered many levels deep.
I died a couple times falling of the top.
(falling damage is in)

Every part of this area is some shade of light blue.
There are numerous nodes where you can port around the world.
Information about various areas will be available from here such as /list players, /list professions or /list levels. Fixers will have the best access to the Grid.
Ah. You navigate a point-down pyramid avatar as a low level grid runner. The appearance of this avatar can be upgraded as you gain levels on the grid.


Which reminds me about PvP.

There will be an indication next to your shown name which reveals your PvP rating. This is separate from level. You could be low level and have a high PvP rating to indicate you are badass.
