Goodbye
OOC : I am posting this thread to announce my shelfing of Ron.

I've played Ron in times of peace and war, and I have had more fun than I ever thought I would when I started my career.
First as lord of the beggar's guild, then ironically commander of the Tir City Watch ..
Eventually, it all became just a bit too much work for me to handle,
and all the time I'd spent on AO scared me, so I took a brake may last year.
Sadly, when I got back in Desember, there wasn't much for Ron to do anymore ..
But now and then, I'd get tells from people I helped out and people who knew me then, but all in all ..
The things that got me to take a brake in the first place are now the things I miss. Ironically ..

Thus I am shelfing Ron both here and ingame,
but I am hoping that I can have half as much fun with Didymus,
the character I will be playing instead. I believe he will differ a lot from Ron as a character. At least, that's what I'm hoping for ..

Anyways, here's a treat for ya :

Foul Ole Ron - The pic that never reached the compo deadline ..

So, that's it ..
*sniff*

Goodbye.
