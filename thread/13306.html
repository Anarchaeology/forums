Yalmaha's hidden run speed.
The description of that Yalmaha you just bought isn't telling you everything. There definately *is* a hidden run speed boost.

This is how I know:

I have a runspeed of 200. My QL 30 Yalm lists a 150 runspeed buff.

First I debuffed myself with Blizard of Red Tape, -600 run speed. that put me at -400 run. Then I equipped the Yalmaha, putting me at -250 run. I flew around real close to the ground. I was going four times as fast in my yalm with -250 runspeed than on foot with +200 run.

This is how it should be. A Yalmaha should be really fast. But why hide the info?
