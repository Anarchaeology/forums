From Birth to Machine: HiredRunner Story
HIREDRUNNER

On Aug, 15, 29466, I step off of the Orbital Shuttle and was Digitally told to get in line. After a brief orientation my shapeless form was brought to a room. The room had four panels. At the top it said select your breed.I looked over the atrox. To big and husky. Then the nano. To weak for the ideal professions in mind. I was excited that my Application to Ruba-ki was finally approved. I was tired of the formless life state. I had a hard time between Solitus and Opifex but the draw of the paleness was my decision.After I when to the next floor terminal. As I entered the next floor there were a size and shape, and a Face graft. What to look like. A decision indeed. To continue on I looked over my choices of Professions. I saw starting with Crat.: to Loud. ; MA: to inner-peacie; MP: Odd and spiritual ; Eng. Builders of Tech and Science, /writes down possible. After a while I saw Fixer. heard the Shout of NERF. That was the one, Not for the Challenge but the idea of the grid. So one who uses the grid to the full potential and beyond. I applied. After all the paperwork and body choosing I was asked. Please sign your name Citizen _______. I though for nearly an hour. I thought what am I. What with my skill is a Reference. I am a Hiredrunner. So be it.

As the newly formed citizens ship entered the Ground of Ruba-ki I got of on the side of Omni. I felt a sudden cold felt need for money and supplied items. Whether or not I feel to this day is or was a mistake will always haunt me.

I left the safety of HighRise 13 in trade and with what little cash I had bought a paper. "Omni and Clan in peace agreement." Wow I just arrive and I can roam freely to all cities. Nearly a months go by and an explosion rips through the area I was standing in throwing me into the tainted waters of the sewers. Then the attacks of the Masked Commandos. As I picked myself up and found a hidden underground network of tunnels. This was my home.The comfort of the apartment in Trade 13 had lost its warmth. I started to feel a cloak in the dark areas of omni as I sat back and watch the onslaught of the Mindless soldiers rip the place to pieces. I wonder what training can bring that type of emotionless rage into work. "News: CEASE FIRE called of until further notice." Omni sent members of every investigational squad into every crevice and crack in the world. I myself can say one member came into my home. I ran out the entrance as a sweep team entered. I was not pleased with that police action. I needed to take refuge from the from the storm of the investigation. Even though I had never done anything to begin with. I found local Org label Shadow Company. They had minorly headline the papers on the grounds of trafficing weapons. I figure thats what Ive been doing anyway but having slim luck on the sales. So I sent an encoded message to a member. I was to that @#%^^# was not a member anymore. @#%@%^ was incharge now. So I applied and recieved entry. After joining I saw the perks and Flaws of guild life. A leader who was rarely on and most too busy to leave a mission to interact in Social event of the greenie typ. I had my run-ins with Mogdai and the Yxxil and Omni-Pol. Pol Gave me trouble about random yalm thefts and being in connection with the mockers. Then Diamondcut. I smiled and brandish legal protection. After gaining the title Executive, I left. I wanted to continue a underground whisper of the lost Grid City. I realized that it was not near Omni or the Soulless Corps would have found it and ruined it and Controlled it. I though that if the Terminal was anywhere it would be out in the wastelands. I became a Clanner, and Joined a new Family. It was Great. Many people were on and wow. But my research was poisoning my mind. All my hopes and dreams of the City had started to fall apart. I found myself sitting in the Rompa bar almost every day for god knows how long. I realized I was becoming enraged by the failure of the dream. I became cold and isolated. Then he walked in. I pale old Nano with a Black Omni Cloak walked in. I had watched him come in and the large machine walking with him. He had used a Computer terminal. I intercepted "Looking for a place to invent weapons and such without the hold backs of the Corperation." I stepped out of the blackened shadows and his machines eyes lit red. He told me that the machine was programmed to kill and I had a minute to explain why I had approached him armed as he put his shotgun on his lap. I said two words, " Shadow Company". And walked away. As time progress we started to discuss what he wanted to do and what I had been searching for. He explained his lack of interest with my delussion of the Grid City. He wanted to continue the process of Machine Flesh interaction. So I agree to see what he wanted. I left my clan family to the operation tables and rejoined Shadow Company. From the return to Shadow Company I was put to sleep. In a matter of an Estimation of one month I awoke. One of the coldest feeling in the world shot through out my body as I felt pain like Ive never felt before. I awoke with cybernetic grafts into me. I asked what is it for, enhancements? He said " No, Just the interworkings of Man and machine at the moment. You will now understand the machine you use." I asked " Then what the hell is this for?" he responded " You are still your self but you have know the understandings of all the machines the Omni has forgotten. For every peice of data misplaced, every machine left out to rust you now know what its like." I said, " I just dont get what the deal is." He said, " My name is Prospectus, and you will Hiredrunner... ...In time. And when you find your Grid City or anytime you use any grid system, You will truely understand the man/machine theory."

I later as I reflect on this News paper Clip of " Yxxil, Who was he really." Here I can say I remember truely why I defend the grid today as much as I did before the Transfomation of the man/machine. I still kill Cyborg and other machines knowing the hatered they have for man and I have for them but knowing they will not have to live a continue banished and unwanted life. For that I am ... ... Do I see Prospectus anymore. No the old man has disappeared into the shadows. Will I find the Grid City, I hope. It is mainly my goal and now program. A new day is dawned and tho back in omni, with now the Fixer Grid I have a new hope that somewhere in the shadows I can come home.

HiredRunner. {End Transmission}
