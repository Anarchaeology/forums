Thank you Cosmik.

Hints for new crats:
- Always keep Matter Creation, Psycho Modi and Time & Space maxed.
- Spare IPs should go into Nano init, Evades and Nano Pool
- Important attributes: Psychic, Intelligence
- Breed: Up to you, solitus is the most popular crat breed
- Weapons: Direct Damage nanoprograms and your Droid

Important nanoprograms:
1. Droid, always max and get MP buffs (masteries etc.) if possible
1b. Droid Aggro buff (mission only nanos, first one: Mudslinger ql40)
2. Direct Damage
3. Charm (Use the general psychic debuff to charm reds later on)
4. Mezmerize (Sleep, Demotivate etc.)
5. Root and Slow
6. Fear (not as good as mez and also has a psychic check)

Combat:
Let your droid tank (take the hits), you stand back and stop any additional enemies, cast Direct Damage nanos on the current target etc. Use a stable charm (long duration) to get a second combat "pet", this will help a lot.

If you want to use a weapon, find out what others use and what their experiences are. No use wasting IPs just to find out that a particular weapon doesn't work. I use pistols myself, not for damage but for the bonuses (O.E.T. Co. Jess pistol ql100+ gives +20 psy/int).

In teams you should let a player tank (as our droids can't stand up to reds unless you get MP buffs) and use your droid as your weapon. Make sure to keep an eye out for additional enemies attacking etc. and stop them. This is what the crat is all about, controlling crowds of enemies. Mez them, slow them, root them and so on, keep your team alive and let them fight one or two mobs (enemies) at a time.

For more information see Meister's Bureaucrat guide in the AO Stratics forums:
http://ao.stratics.com/content/commu...ureaucrat.html

This is all I can think of at the moment, good luck in your new career as a Bureaucrat on Rubi-Ka!

How to make a suit:
Found in Foreman dungeon in The Longest Road zone. West of West Athen along the road. There's a sign that says Foreman out front and a few Omni guards. The Lab Director upstairs drops the order form. He spawns every few hours, so be ready to have other players jump on the spawn, too. People are impatient.

Originally posted by Starke
  • Hack the form with a hacker tool.
  • Combine the hacked form with ID data (created by using ID-extractor and Expendable Hologram Camera).
  • Combine the resulting personal form with a multiform in the following manner:
    - Omni form - omni multiform = omni suit
    - clan form - clan multiform = clan suit
    - Any form with it's opposite (omni form - clan multi or vice-versa) = Universal suit
  • You'll need 300 Psychology to wear it.

Making a briefcase:
  • Create an ID data. Combine ID-extractor and Expendable Hologram Camera
  • Combine OTSC-490 Group A + personal ID to create Personal Order OTSC-490 Group A
  • Combine the Personal Order with Omni multi-form (clan and neutral don't work)
Result is Briefcase of Holding


Advanced Suit: Find in chests pre-made. Only come in the neutral variety.
