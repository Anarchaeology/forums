LOSES:

Agent - Aimed Shot, Crit every 2 shots, Massive Gripo/Stigma twink

Just let me add here that this was in MMD and I confronted him in Suppression Field and asked him for a friendly duel... I even went in first so he would get first jump.

Thats it, if your waiting to see more wins.... don't. As far as any NT reading this - We should all get together and start a Gimped Arse Club. We could make a guild and call it G.A.G. (Gimped Arse Guild)

Some might read this and say: "Well maybe you should think about developing your character." Maybe !

I'm lvl 34 and have had a Full Set of QL 51 Implants for Soldier since lvl 23. I currently switch weopons in PvP: QL 47 Supernova for aimed shot opener, QL 48 Flashpoint for Full Auto Big Bang, QL 50 Nova Flow for Burst, Fling and rest of duel. Or I buff myself into a QL 63 Nova and stay with that..... depends. My point is that I am a lot of things.... but I am not an underdeveloped soldier.

Before patch, any other profession would have went down hard somewhere in between Full Auto and Burst. I'll be the first to admit that this wasn't right.

But the usless piece of dead meat the Soldier has become after the 13.0 "PvP" Patch is not right either. Soldiers are supposed to be Warriors, the big damage dealers. We have not heals, no major buffs, nothing that makes us viable. All we had was PvP and our Alpha Stike. Now we are ineffective at best, This is not a fix. What would a conflict be without soldiers??? What is the point of this game, the whole storeline of confict and eventually war.... BUT WAIT...... All the Soldiers are Gimped !!!

Let us play our Alternate characters, make new ones... Metas/Enforcers/MAs whatever.
