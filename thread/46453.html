Account Bank
Dear Funcom

While I have never traded items between my characters or muled for someone else, it appears that many people do. It appears that many people don't feel that "twinking" ruins gameplay, but rather the frustration of finding all new equipment ruins the game. Some people feel their characters are a family, and that sharing items in a family would be natural. Others, to whom all have their chars in the same guild accientally twink with guild item sharing.

So my request in that account banks are made where trading items between one's own characters (according to affiliation) would be simple and easy. Endorsed by FC. It might ruin things to transfer cash, but I know that my lower lvl chars would greatly benefit for the dust collectors in my higher lvl chars bank.

I think it would just be an acknowledgement of a widespred behavior, and help "Honest" players be able to have equivalent ease that twinking characters have.

PLEASE BUMP IF YOU WANT TO LEGALLY TRADE ITEMS BETWEEN CHARACTERS!!!
