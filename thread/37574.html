It's a lonely world
Since the day I emerged into the big city from the training grounds the world has filled me with awe and respect. My dreams were always to help my fellow clan fight in the war against the opression of Omni-Tek. However, what was the best way? I began doing missions alone to help with our cause, however something was just not satisfying. What good would any accomplishments I achieve be without friends and family to share it with?! So then I began a new quest. I wanted a clan to call home... to call family.
I looked at many a clan. Some seemed more interested in numbers and strength. There were others that I met that were much more genuine in their feelings towards their fellow clanmembers, however they did not share the same passion I felt for helping the entire cause of ridding the world of Omni-Tek oppression. They acted as if it were just simply a way of life and something that they just do... forgetting the passion behind the cause. Is there no clan out there with genuine concern for the cause and for fellow clan members? I shall continue my search.
