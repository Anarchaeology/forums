Freelance Investigator - ARK Interview
The Advisors of Rubi-Ka put rumors to rest and answer our reporters questions...

**Grid uplink connecting Password accepted Welcome to the Rubi-Ka Grid

I'm Lacey, a freelance reporter for the citizens of Rubi-Ka. I have been investigating the rumor that ICC is starting a new organization in Newland. So far I discovered a newly forming organization called Advisors of Rubi-Ka (ARK) maybe the very subject of the rumor I am investigating. I searched the ICC archives and finally received the following result -

quote:
As the year 29473 AD drew to a close, the ICC found itself under mounting pressure. The ICC, the regulatory council for all megacorporations and hypercorporations across the known galaxies, was under increasing criticism regarding their control over the growing number of corporations. The general public opinion was that the ICC was losing its grip on a few of the larger and more powerful hypercorporations and false rumors of bribery were beginning to circulate.

In an effort to mend public relations, denounce all charges of bribery and to strengthen its image in the public eye, the ICC began to undertake the task of constructing a department to aid the best interests of the public. The Advisors, as they were to be known, were designed to be a department consisting of people that would deal directly with the general public, assisting them with their problems, questions and concerns.

It was decided that in order to aid the public in any foreseeable way, Advisors would have to be nano-enhanced to a level superior to that of current standards. An expensive, and as of then untested, process was put into place by the ICC that enabled notum to be bonded directly with the body cells of the Advisor, allowing them to control nano technology in a way not yet seen before by science. There were hints of reports coming out of the ICC technology division detailing that not all Advisors survived the nano bonding process. According to some insiders, only the worthy were able to survive the harsh process.

This information was just what I needed to spice up my list of questions for the mysterious ARK organization. I made use of a contact address I was given by one of the Advisors in Newland and just received the following answers:
quote:
#1 Rubi-Ka is in a tense political situation with conflicts between Omni-Tek and rebel Clans intensifying. Many organizations have been destroyed in the fray, with that in mind could you please explain to us why you have devoted time and effort into establishing the ARK organization on Rubi-Ka? What would ARK hope to achieve here?

A~ We have no political standing on Rubi-Ka, our interest in establishing the ARK organization here was to be close to the source of Notum. The conflict between Omni-Tek and Clans is most unfortunate. These destructive activities bring anarchy into our lives. We hope to ease the confusion by helping people with every day questions and concerns.

#2 Referring ICC archives:
The Advisors, as they were to be known, were designed to be a department consisting of people that would deal directly with the general public, assisting them with their problems, questions and concerns...
Are the Advisors of Rubi-Ka (ARK) and the mentioned "Advisor department" of ICC one in the same?

A~We have nothing to hide regarding our roots in the Advisor Department of ICC. We reached a stage in our development that required us to build the foundations of a self-reliant organization. We decided it would be much easier to establish our selves at the source of Notum. Once we finalize the development of our infrastructure we will be capable of assisting every citizen of Rubi-Ka.

#3 Further quote from ICC archives:
Advisors would have to be nano-enhanced to a level superior to that of current standards. An expensive, and as of then untested, process was put into place by the ICC that enabled notum to be bonded directly with the body cells of the Advisor, allowing them to control nano technology in a way not yet seen before by science.
Referring to recent rumors:
The process is so intensive that many don't survive the initial surgery of a neural network bonding with notum, coupled with strict mental training they evolve into the psychological profile. Any deviation to the profile would see an instant breakdown of the notum fusion. They will simply cease to exist.
Can you please explain to the citizens of Rubi-Ka what the nano enhancing technology is all about and why hypercorporations like Omni-Tek are not using it? Is it safe for citizens to interact with nano enhanced Advisors?

A~Nano Enhancement Technology or Neo-Technology is a unique process, though many scientists around the galaxies can understand the theory involved, only a few surgeons are fully trained with skills needed for successful results. Risks of Neo-Technology are isolated to the individuals that undergo the training and surgery. These individuals can not harm others in any way. Rubi-Ka citizens can rest assured that Advisors of Rubi-Ka are here to help them.

#4 How do you hope to achieve a balance in working with citizens of Omni-Tek, Clans and neutrals? How will you deal with mistrust issues?

A~ ARK is a humanitarian organization, not politically motivated, we are monitored by the ICC and must follow galactic sanctioned humanitarian guidelines. Our aim is to help individuals regardless of their alignments. We are not here to be involved in the conflicts or politics. ARK is here to provide aid and support in times of need. We aim to earn the trust of all citizens of Rubi-Ka, with the services we provide.

When our plans for offices are complete, we will be staffing them with Greeters to welcome and assist anyone with questions about Rubi-Ka, helping new arrivals and all citizens alike. Greeters will also monitor the "Newbie OOC" chat channel and answer questions there.

ARK also supports an on call service, just by using your normal interface type /petition <then your message>. We will have an Advisor on location to attend any concerns or problems of Rubi-Ka citizens.

#5 Could you explain the infrastructure of your organization? How would interested citizens apply to join your ranks?

A~The Advisors of Rubi-Ka organization consists of four departments plus the Advisory Council. Much of the infrastructure is classified, so we cannot discuss certain details. However, it is important to note the various ranks of Advisors and Genetic Manipulators who will be assisting the citizens of Rubi-Ka.

Assistant Advisors are still in the initial stages of the notum fusion process, and are undergoing extensive mental training and testing.

Advisors are the mainstay of ARK. They have completed the fusion and training processes and are fully capable of assisting the citizens of Rubi-Ka.

Guardians are the management rank of ARK. Their special nano-enhancement enables them to fulfill their administrative roles within the various departments of ARK.

Genetic Manipulators (GMs) are enhanced to a level previously discussed only in theory. This enables them to handle a wide variety of extraordinary situations. There is much speculation about their abilities.

Citizens interested in joining our ranks will need to go through an application and training process. Advisors of Rubi-Ka are a humanitarian organization caring for the planet and all its inhabitants. We are looking for people with excellent communication skills who have experience in customer support, and are able to devote at least 10 hours per week. If you are honest, empathic, dedicated, and interested in serving the community, please fill out the online application form at this address:
http://community.anarchy-online.com/ark/application
ICC have had an interesting hand in the establishment of ARK. I will be keeping my eyes open for public response to this innovative way of keeping individuals informed and supported. If you felt like just another number or slipped through the process of red tape I am sure you will see ARK as good news.

Here is the details of ARK :D
Here is what I understood about the ARK:

ARK consists a group of AO volunteers, acting as guides to new players and GM petition respondants. They answer the player petition, and resolve (possibly) PvP, guild dispute. Also, an on duty ARK Volunteer would monitor the chat frequency, and help out new players and seasoned pros with answers to their questions. Basicly, ARK is a group of dedicated service members (each accepted volunteer must spend a given amount of time, usually 12 hours, each week to full fill their duties). In the retail release, in game /petition will sometimes be answered by an ARK member.

However, it is not known if the ARK members would be given a flesh and blood avatar in the world to physically help out or guide players for a very limited period of time. Since an ARK member has to email or contact a given player or perform customer service duties, it is unlikely that they will be staying in the game world for a prelonged period of time. To obtain more information, visit the ARK official site at community.anarchyonline.com/ark

My knowledge on this matter is still limited, and I believe full detail will be released upon launch.
