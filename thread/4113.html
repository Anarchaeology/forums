I am not sure I understand your question but here is some info on implant and clusters:

A implant can be inserted with a cluster if the cluster has a ql lvl equal or better than -10% of the ql lvl implant.

You do not 'loose' any skill boost when inserting a cluster, but it is the ql implant that governs the skill boost. Ie you can put a ql 65 cluster or a ql 200 cluster into a ql 70 implant, the result will be a ql 70 (or 71) implant giving you ql 70 (or 71) boost. So if ql 65 cluster gives +20 to a skill and a ql 200 gives +100, the finished implant will give you f.ex. +25 if that is what you get from a ql 70. So inserting a ql 200 cluster does not give any better or worse boost to skill.

Hope I got you right and this answer your post.
