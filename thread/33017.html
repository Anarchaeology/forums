Funcom, stop "balancing" the game and start fixing and adding content!
"Balanced" is always a relative term in MMORPG. Chances are what is balanced in one person's eyes is not balanced in another person's eyes.

I cannot believe how much time you guys have wasted in changing the basics of a profession after the game has been out for so long: Take out MA's crt buff, Gimp Traders heals, OMG. People made their characters and picked their professions based on what they were when they first started. Then you totally changed it.

That is not balancing. That is chaos.

Please, spend some time putting out some content and make leveling past 100 fun.

Please, fix all those darn bugs.

Spend your energy where it is needed.
