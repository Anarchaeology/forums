Yup, many of them can be found if you attend rather large parties. I've seen most of them fairly often.

They're cool doods. But don't expect much more than "seeing" them. The unfortunate "greenies" have a little problem with everyone who comes into contact with them automatically thinking that they're now buddy-buddy with an ark.

Hence, they're just a {sarcasm}teensy{/sarcasm} bit busier than the common player. They are there to roleplay, but can't pay attention to everyone at once, so they have to favor certain people and groups from time to time, which means they might not have any room at all to say much more than "hiya" to someone they don't know yet.

Personally, I still think they should lose the "greenie"ness of their names. They'd have a much easier time roleplaying and interacting with the community without entire crowds gathering around, half bombarding with attempts at fanboy roleplay and the other half bombarding with ARK-intended questions.

Scuse me, I got a little bitter there for a second. Ahem.
