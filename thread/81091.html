Real reasons to PvP
Ok, everyone is always complaining that the AO storyline just doesn't engage people, and it doesn't lend to the PvP rationale. So instead I think we should come up with more realistically based organizations for PvP. The idea would be to provide things that will give people more real emotional emphasis to their battles. So here are some of my ideas based on reading the boards, you all add your own

Instead of Omni vs Clan PvP how about:

1) "We Want Our Profession Patched NOW" Vs. "STOP Patching So Often"

2) "We Want More Content" vs. "Fix Bugs First Dammit"

3) "Everyone" Vs. "Traders"

4) "I'm Gonna Blow A Gasket" Vs. "Chill It's Just A Game"

5) "Funcom Blows" vs "Funcom Rocks"
