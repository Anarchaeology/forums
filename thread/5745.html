The Marsalis Reports
PRELUDE

The android butler set the white porcelain teacups on the table between the two men and exited with the softwhirclick of its servos. The men took up their cups and drank from them, the older man smiling and nodding as he set his down.

"That's real tea, Major, not synthesized, not vat grown. Enjoy it. I doubt you'll get anything like it where you're going." Fidel Orasco winked at the young man across the table from him. "But I guess I shouldn't be so mysterious, should I?"

Major Rhase Marsalis shrugged slightly, taking another drink. "Its very good, Mr Orasco. As to where I'm going, I have a fair idea. Sol Banking wouldn't have given me a crash course on Rubi Ka if I was headed into the Core."

Orasco chuckled, his eyes closing as he laughed like a toad swallowing its meal. "Well put, Major."

Marsalis didn't like the Sol Banking executive. Hated him in fact. He knew Orasco's hobbies, knew what the man did for "entertainment". Marsalis was all for two adults doing whatever they wanted behind closed doors--with rez technology a person's sickest fantasies could be played out with a willing partner. But Orasco didn't always use willing partners--or adults. He was also stupid enough to record his adventures and leave them in accessible form.

Orasco stood up, walking to the window overlooking the city below. "I know this isn't the assignment you wanted, Major, but we need more people on the ground in Rubi Ka. This amnesty is a fragile thing, and the more agents we have rapping on it, the more likely it will break. So there you are."

Marsalis put the now empty cup on the table. "And it has, of course, nothing to do with the Van Zant situation." He kept his eyes on his own hands, both flat on either side teacup, noting absently that the deep mahogany of the table was only a shade lighter than his skin.

Orasco cleared his throat after a moment. "That would be ridiculous. We all know what happened on the station. Van Zant was entirely responsible, we hold no one else from the team in a bad light because of it."

"I hear Shaw was sent to Deepwell, Cadderly to Tinkin Bay, and Laird...well, I haven't heard anything about Laird. No one has. And now I'm going to Rubi Ka. I'd hate to see what might happen if the company DID see us in a bad light." He looked up to find Orasco glaring at him.

"See here, Major, its none of your concern what assignments are given to anyone within the company...and considering that those orders are all classified, I might be tempted to find out just how you know these things. But in an hour it won't matter because you'll be on your way to Rubi Ka! And you WILL do your job!"

Marsalis stood up. "I always do my job, Mr Orasco, don't worry about that."

"Get out, Major...you're due at the shuttle in half an hour."


Marsalis strapped himself in as the shuttle's engines warmed up. As soon as he felt the dropping sensation in his stomach that indicated they were away, he took out his datapad and activated a command routine. On the planet below, every terminal in the Sol Banking complex began a 15 minute loop of Fidel Orasco's home holovids.

Marsalis smiled. He doubted they'd have real tea where Orasco was going either.
