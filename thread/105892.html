OOC
If there's little or no roleplaying, it's because people are far too busy sitting in these forums whining and sobbing over the lack of rp, instead of going out there and making an effort themselves.

Don't get me wrong, there's a lot of people who do just that, and who aren't dependant on the Funcom Event team to make up some good stuff themselves. But there's also those who play prophets of doom and naysayers, and just can't see the rp because of their malfunctioning eyes. Even though both the greenie population and the amount of events have increased, some still cry "No events from FC". But the rp community shouldn't have to rely on those. They should be able to make a helluva nice amounts of intriguing, exciting, interesting and including events themselves. The greenies and the FC events should be a boon to this, not something one has to have to survive on Rubi-Ka.

Roleplaying isn't dead at all. If people think it is, they should try doing things or going places or hang with people they don't normally hang with. I've never experienced more roleplaying than I do now, and it doesn't all have to be about drama (although I have to confess I'm involved in a few drama plotlines myself). Get hold of the new faces out there, the new people who wants to roleplay and come to AO in hope of finding it. Not everyone posts here, and that shouldn't even be required.

Try it, and see that it's far from dead. But alas, too many people are stuck in the forums writing posts about nothing while whining about how little to none events there are....*sigh*
