First Meeting of the Neutral Coaltion
On Tuesday 8th August leaders and prominant citizens of the neutral community met in Borealis to discuss the growing tensions on Rubi-Ka. After a long meeting the Neutral Coaltion decided on a General Assembly and an Executive Commitee to represent the neutral population of Rubi-Ka. The Executive Committee comprises of representatives of the 8 most powerful guilds and groups as well as an unaffiliated head. The commitee also has the power to vote on poilicies affecting the neutral citizens who subscribe to the Coaltion. Citizens are free to attend meetings and to bring up items for discussion, provided they let the Coalition know in advance of their points. All citizens sitting on the general assembly are free to discuss the items of business brought up in this manner. The executive comitee will have the power to vote on anything which requires a decision, with a simple majority holding the day. The executive comitee consists of The Indepedent Rubi-Kans, Wolf Brigade, Nanomage Liberation Front, Circle G. The Free Assembly, Celestial Eminations, Saim Dathedi and the Healing Hands. Cathern "Catalyste" Ardman was provisionally named as head of the Coaltion. The Coaltion is due to meet again in one weeks time to discuss and decide policies affecting neutrals in this time of growing tension.

Further details of the meeting including the log (hehe) can be viewed at
http://pub64.ezboard.com/fneutralsofrubikafrm1
The Coaltion will be using the neutral forum for further business.
