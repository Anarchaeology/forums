Rejkavik's Story
The following story is quite long, so if you intend on reading the whole thing, I advise waiting until you have plenty of free time (I was going to keep it short and sweet, but I guess I got a touch of artistic inspiration or something ) Also, I apoligize in advance to anybody who might feel that this story is too graphic or goes overboard, but I wanted to accurately portray why my character hates the clans. Nobody in this story is meant to resemble any characters in game (other then my own, of course) and the Omni-Pol I mention are meant to be the police force on Omni-Prime, not the org Omni-Pol on Atlantean. That being said, on with the story...

Rejkavik sits alone at a table in Rompa, one where he's been finding himself more and more these days. He has a feeling his alcoholism is winning him over again, but these days it doesn't really bother him. The blackouts are better then the memories. He raises the shot glass to his lips, bloodshot eyes glancing over at an attractive young watiress walking by. The glance is a fleeting one, however. There would be no other for him. None since the-

He cuts off the thought before it can even begin to manifest itself, instead rising up and walking towards the bar. A slender Opifex man is serving the drinks tonight. Rejkavik thinks he catches a look of annoyance flash across the bartender's face when he sees him approaching, but he dismisses it.

"Hey." Rejkavik sidles up to the bar counter. "Gimme another shot."

The opi man looks at him, and the disgust in his eyes is unmistakeable. He acts as though he regards Rejkavik as a thing, and the soldier feels a black rage begin to boil inside him.

"I think you've had enough for one night, pal."

"D... Don't you try to babysit me... just do your job and get me another God-damned drink."

"You ain't getting another drop of liquor out of me. Now sod off or I'll bring the bouncer down on you."

With speed faster then one would expect out of a drunk man, Rejkavik lunges across the counter and siezes the Opi man by his shirt collar. The bartender gives a frightened squawk, and a man standing nearby begins to run towards them.

Rejkavik pulls the opifex closer until they are almost eye to eye, the smell of Hit-The-Floor-Jack rolling off of him in sick waves.

"Listen to me, you skinny little fu-"

A hand touches Rejkavik on the shoulder, and he hears the voice of the man whom he saw running towards him a second ago.

"Hey, there's no need for-"

"GET YOUR HANDS OFF ME!" Rej screams, releasing the bartender and turning on the other. He loses his footing in his intoxication, and falls to his knees. The man hee helled at begins to stoop and help him up, but Rej slaps his hands away.

"Don't touch me."

The barfly steps back, and then the bouncer is there, a burly atrox that looks like he has a mean disposition.

"Time for you to leave, methinks." he says. Rejkavik glances around the bar. Everyone is starting at him with wide, fascinated eyes. It's the kind of look people get the first time they see a mutant. The bouncer speaks again.

"Time for you to leave. Go now, or I'll throw you out."

Rejkavik shakily rises to his feet, his hands balling up into fists.

"Why don't you try?" He croaks.

Five minutes later he hears the mechanical swoosh of the Rompa door sliding open, and then he's off his feet and in the air. His body does a sort of 180 degree spin, and he sees the bouncer walking back into the bar. Then his head connects with the pavement, there's a flash of hot white light, and everything is blackness.

And once again he's forced to bear witness to the memories...

Rejkavik had lived on Omni-Prime his entire life, he'd worked a boring desk job and had lived a normal life up to that point in Ferbruary. But the dreams didn't care about any of that, they never reflected the good times he'd had. Only the bad.

He'd met Sara at a cafe near the office building in which he worked. She was an amazing woman, beautiful and classy. They'd hit it off, and a year and a half later they were married with a son on the way.

When his son Brandon was born, it made Rej complete. His life seemed to him like a dream... he had managed to get two promotions and a significant boost to his paycheck, he was married to the woman of his dreams, and he had a son. But all good things must come to an end, and for Rejkavik the end came on the 10th of February.

In his dream he could see it all so clearly... the event is burned into his mind, and the dreams are becoming more and more frequent. He sees himself driving towards home in his Kodiak, he remembers the sinking feeling in hi stomach when he saw the van. It was parked in front of his apartment building, a corporate vehicle with the sleek grey lettering 'Omni-Pol Protects' along it's side. He saw a plethora of armor clad Omni-Pol officers standing at the door to the building, saw the yellow crime scene tape covering the entrance. A man in Elite armor wielding an automatic weapon was waving his hand dismissively at the reporters and specators outside.

Rejkavik runs to the door, and the policemen there move to block him. One of them is saying 'You can't go in, this is a crime scene.' over and over, like a broken record. Rejkavik tries to tell the man that his wife and his boy are in there, and he wants to know what's going on; but the OP officer keeps repeating himself like some kind of idiot robot. Rejkavik gives a cry of disgust and sprints off toward the side of the building, where the fire escape is located. There's no Omni-Pol around, so he begins to climb the ladder up to the third floor, where his family resides. He tries in vain to open a window for a few seconds, then gives an outraged yell and kicks it in. As he climbs through the window he's aware of the dead weight he feels in his stomach, aware of the fear that has been growing in him ever since he saw the Omni-Pol vehicle. He feels as though he may vomit at any moment.

The next set of images are always the same, they flash through his mind like a maniacal slideshow. He sees the body bags lying in the hallway. One of them is partly open, he can see a dead man he doesn't know lying in it. There's a crime scene investigator standing near the open doorway to Rejkavik's apartment, taking pictures. Rej simply walks up next to the man and gazes into the room. At first the CSI is too surprised to really do anything; it takes the man a moment to sieze Rej and pull him away from the door. But in the few seconds that he was able to look into that room, the future soldier has seen enough to scar him for the rest of his life.

HIs wife is laying on the couch in the living room. She's been stripped of her clothes, and it's too painfully obvious to see what has been done to her. A pool of blood hsa formed on her stomach near the navel, and is soaking into the couch. There is blood on the floor as well, although it is not his wife's. Rejkavik's son lies lifeless.

Then he is jerked away from the door, he is being asked what he is doing there, but all he can do is scream. He slumps against the wall and slides down to the floor, and the hot tears on his cheeks seem a little too real; maybe because he's sobbing in reality as he lays outside the Rompa bar. He cried and screamed until he felt his very soul was breaking, but the pain he felt never faded.

The details of what happened that night were revealed to Rejkavik later, after he cashed in on a favor owed to him by an old friend in Omni-Pol.

There were three men that had been involved in the crime. They had all been under the influence of Stims when the murders occured. All three were from Rubi-Ka, all three were members of what was known as the Black Raven Clan. They'd come to Omni-Prime to martyr themselves by setting off a bomb in one of the many residential districts, to 'save Rubi-Ka from Omni-Tek oppression'. These facts were discovered in one of the men's journals in a city called Athen almost a year after the murders. The rest was revealed by the only member of the trio that was captured alive at the scene, and who was later shot during interrogation for attempting to steal an officer's weapon.

The three men had picked the apartment that Rejkavik and his family had called home for the past 6 years. Why his apartment was picked out of so many, Rej didn't know. He'd been off trying to further his career within the Corporation rather then spending time with his family, and he felt that his family was dead because of this. At any rate, Sara had passed the trio in the hallway on her way to pick up some groceries, and she recognized the device they were carrying as some kind of bomb. She did what most people would probably do: she ran into her home and called the police. The men realized she'd seen the weapon, however, and they chased her into the room and took the comm unit away from her before she could explain the situation. They beat her brutally. Perhaps it would have ended there, and Rejkavik's son and and wife would have been able to escape the apartment building and get help when the men left. But it was not to be so. One of the men decided to search the desk in Rej's study, and found his ID. He saw the Omni-Tek logo stamped across it, and the word 'executive', and Sara and her son's fate was sealed. The man showed the ID badge to the others, and then went into Brandon's room and roused him out of bed. The 8 year old had somehow managed to sleep through the sound of his mother being beaten. The Clanner drug the boy into the living room by his hair, instructed him to sit down on the floor in front of Sara, and then shot him in the head. Sara went berserk, and as she wrestled out of assailant's grasp, the man who had murdered her son shot her in the stomach. Apparently killing her son and mortally wounding her was not enough, the clanners ripped off her clothes and had their way with her until she died about an hour later. And throughout it all, the interrogated man said the only thing she did was call out to her son, begging him to get up.

The men were not caught until almost 2 hours after the murders. Omni-Pol's response time was lax, due to the fact that the message recieved by the OP emergency hotline was merely the sound of a disconnected phone. It was thought to be a prank call, and although officers -were- dispatched, they took their time in thinking that all they'd find was a young bored child home from school. Had the trio of terrorists not decided to have their way with Rej's wife, they might have ended up detonating the bomb and killing everyone on the block.

Rejkavik himself spent his next 3 years in a drunken stupor, and lost his job. He honed his hate for not only the three men, but for himself. Eventually he grew to hate all clanners in general. On the four year anniversary of his family's murder, Rejkavik boarded a shuttle to Rubi-Ka.

* * *

Rejkavik awoke with a start. He knew he was near to Rompa, he could vaguely make out the music coming from the dance club down the street. His lip seemed much larger then normal, and he rubbed the back of his hand across it distastefully. When he looked down at his hand, he saw that it was smeared with dark crimson blood. For a moment his mind threatened to conjure up images from the horrific dream once again, but it stopped. Perhaps he had tortured himself enough for one day. Rej stood up a little shakily, using the bar's wall to steady himself a moment, and then he hobbled out of the covered alleyway. It was raining now, but he paid no heed to the droplets of water spattering all over him.

He had not asked for the war with the Clans.
The Clans had brought the war to him.

And now he'd make them pay.
