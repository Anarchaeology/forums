Trade items sort and construction manuals
Items sort
-------------

Wouldn't it be nifty with a sorting ability in shops? It's hard enough to find the Q you need when building something, and when trying to fill that bright slot in an implant it can be a pain in the butt to find one that fits and is good for you.
I know that they are sorted alphabetically, but that only helps if you know what you're looking for.

The ability to sort Clusters according in implant order would be nice or the ability to filter out others, e.g. only left-hand clusters or Agility clusters.

The same goes for weapons shop, it would be nice to sort weapon according to weapon class, since my enf. isn't very interested in flipping through guns, or even piercing weapons. The alpha sorting makes them spread out, as as stated above, it's hard enough to find the Q you need.

Construction manuals
---------------------------

Playing an engie, i've noticed some strange things, there are too few manuals, and of 'strange' things. One of the simplest things to build must be blunt weapons, right? Try to find some blunt weapons manuals, especially 1H blunt. Maybe i'm only unlucky, but i've looked in many shops by now. Is it really that hard to build a torch or a stun baton?

Shouldn't almost all weapons be craftable? After all, someone has built them at one time, right? Why shouldn't i be able to?

If the idea is to limit the availability of certain weapons, i have an idea: you could have the ability to reverse engineer a weapon by taking it apart (screwdriver and/or hacker tool) and make your own NO DROP manual of construction. How that?

Market
---------
A quick mention of the player economy that exists of spamming the trade channel. Maybe that's the idea, that it should be hard for players to trade from each other, otherwise you could have a messageboard or shop where used and found stuff go ...
