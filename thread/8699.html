My opinion:

MP's and engineers are best at gaining experience through soloing and levelling that way.

Doctors are fastest at levelling up in groups. (because any group with a doctor in it is far more succesful than one without a doc, and doctors are always desired in groups)

Agents and fixers are, at the moment, the best at making money through blitzing missions, as they both have access to fixer run buffs. Also, agents have the benefit of concealment, which lasts for 30 seconds or so before it breaks(sometimes more, sometimes much less).
