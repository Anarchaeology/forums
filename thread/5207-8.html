Advent Changes
I'd like to see the following changes, 1-10, in no paticular order, to adventurers.

1) Improvement to available weapons. This is a fairly broad problem in the game but adventurers really get hit hard by it. Rider executioners are good but cost far too many IP to maintain with other skills, and no other 1HE are very viable. Pistols, our other easy skill, are ok if you get good crit pistols (PNG as an example), but otherwise are mediocre. I'd like to see a lot of good base damage pistols added to the game (nothing fantastic mind you, but maybe a 1-300(300) 1.5/2 pistol, or SOMETHING for us to use to have reasonable damage). I'd love to use a pistol, a staff, or a sword, but quite honestly, I'm being driven toward a shotgun because not many good, low IP cost weapons exist for those skills. And that new adventurer only pistol.... not good :/

2) Morph changes. A biggy here. The morphs generally "suck" as the term goes, and they need a massive overhaul.

Leet - Leet form adds concealment and cuteness. Cuteness is great, but concealment is useless to adventurers (great for FP agents I admit but...).

Wolf - Wolf form adds reasonable run speed and some damage. It's not BAD, but I'd like to see it made a little more powerful either on the runspeed end, or the damage end. It also needs to lose its low light vision, because Wolf Form + Low-Light Scope = TORTURE.

Cat - Catform is fairly good, but could use a slight "boost" in its numbers, albiet not a lot. Maybe 2-3% crit rate instead of 1, 300 AC boost at max, and perhaps 70 damage at max. Also, it'd be nice not to take the runspeed hit, as that quite honestly deters me from using the spell line much. It's not overpowered, it needs no counerbalancing "nerf".

Reet - The reet form needs a drastic change, because it's fairly useless. Everyone level 75+ has a plane for the most part, and therefore the runspeed is useless (since you can't fight in this form). The flying form is recieved so late it is practically a joke.

Pit Lizard - Lastly, pit lizard... while the HP, ac, damage, etc aren't bad, the runspeed hit and the defensive drop could definately be done without. It's not overpowering by itself, it doesn't need negative hits to counterbalance it. Also, it'd be nice if you could go from flying to not flying with a key, back and forth, so you can fight in the form then fly, and vice versa.

3) Sense/Agi back armor. This is a fairly generic change again, but it applies to adventurers. It's really the only statset of armor that doesn't have a backslot. This affects MA and Agents as much as adventurers, but it'd surely be nice to see.

4) Changes to the damage shields. Quite honestly, a 90 point damage shield means VERY little when things you fight have well over 10,000 hit points. I'd like to either see this become a reflect shield (cutting the same damage away from the person it's on) or have the shield become MUCH larger, but much more short term (perhaps 250pt but lasting only a few minutes).

5) Continuation and/or alternative to the AC buff line. It caps at around what, 400? And stops at halfway up the nano list in terms of skill requirements. I'd either like to see some other line mixed in with this one that extends much higher in the requirements (perhaps an HP buff line, or something, as an example), or I'd like to just see the line as it exists extend more (to a max of perhaps 600ac buff).

6) Better skill selections. Make either ranged or nano init green or aqua for adventurers or so. I'd like to be able to use points into those without feeling it's somewhat of a waste.

7) Make "adventuring", the skill, useful. Make it do more than arm backpacks, and reduce fall damage. Basically it's unused. I admit, the new backpacks are a good step toward getting the skill used. I'd just like to see some more additions to make the skill generally useful.

8) New map upgrades/maps. Make some advenurer only map upgrades or maps, requiring high skill, but giving large benefits possibly. We're supposed to be the best map readers, right? ;-D

9) More independant runbuffs. Either make this part of the wolf line as mentioned above (focus more on speed), or give a slight extention to the runbuff line (terrain knowledge, free movement). It seems only fitting that adventurers move fast, right?

10) Possibly roots or snares. Not sure about this one, because roots are very powerful tools in game. But it seems to fit from a roleplayed standpoint (roots being a word that has to do with nature and all), and it'd be awfully nice as a balance. The only issue here might be overdoing it, because roots are very useful. But they'd still be nice.

Maybe I should add my late idea here
Gaute pointed out that we should be casting the best shields in the game.

My idea is: when you go further and further in the agg slider, your deflection hits the aggressor harder, up to 50 percent more. For example, an adv on full agg will have his shield that does 40 base damage do 60 damage.
If you opt for going further and further in the def slider, your absorbtion would be more effective. For that part there are different propositions:
the adv absorbtion works on every hit he takes or refreshes every round or something, and when going in the def slider, that absorbtion soaks more, or make its refresh time shorter. Remember, our shields absorb only one type of damage, so this wouldn't be too 'uber'.

To sum up: full agg goes up to 50 percent of additional damage when the shield hits back and full def adds 50 percent of the original absorbtion score, or reduces the refresh time by 50 percent.

If you want, you can make up a catch: full def will substract 50 percent of the damage the shield sends back to the aggressor and full agg will substract 50 percent of the damage the shield will absorb/ adds 50 percent to the absorbtion refresh time.

Another thing about the damage sent back:
To avoid being too uber or so against fast hitting and low damage players/mobs, you could make the damage sent back a PERCENTAGE. The higher the nano shield, the better the percentage. It would start at 1 or 2 percent and finish up to 15 percent or something. That way, the damage sent back would be proportional and then in a way, more reasonable. Test out those modifications, FC? I'm sure this would help us much, and it's a very good 'fix n' tune' all in one.
