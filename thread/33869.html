Bump for /engage(duel) Command or Similar (Fairly Long Read)
I've had it with the state of the arenas these days, the conceal change was the absolute worst change ever for pvp, EVERYONE zone hugs and never duels because of the 2-3 Omni 150+ omni agnets or whatever hid at the edge ready to step in as you start your duel and end it before it starts...

For people like me who prefer to duel at arenas (well nowhere else I can fight at my char's levels), there is nothing more annoying than these little cowards *cough* Biohover *cough* who sit conceaed ganking duels ALL DAY LONG.. They defy the whole friggen point of the arena.

What would be fun to watch is having 2-3 High commander guards Sat at the arena harrassing/killing these people, but I'm not like that NO, I would rather a change that would benefit all in someway so here it is..

I've read about a /duel command somewhere else but can't remember all the suggestions so start afresh sort of,
Basically "Safe" duels can only be fought in arena areas (not belial etc), you would walk into the arena but there'd be no 0% gas, there'd be some other supression field or whatever... anyway once in the arena area you would target the person and type /engage, they would then get a window saying *name* wishes to engage you in combat yes/no.. person clicks yes you then have a 30 second period to initiate a fight with that person till you have to start the /engage again.

Now this /engage feature instead of a 1v1 duel command would let you test 2v2 or whatever fight types because durin that 30second period if you HAVENT STARTED COMBAT (Ie: pressed q or whatever) you can continue to accept challenges, and each successful challenge would refresh the 30s period.

Advantages of this method are:
1: People can still view fights, no need for special indoor private arena's
2: You wont be having fights initiating all around you at mission terms etc causing more Unnecesary(sp?) lag
3: NO GANKS!
4: Some other stuff I'm sure i'll think of later..

Feel free to post suggestions etc and don't forget to Bump, I think about 1000 posts is what we need to get a "I'll look into it" post from Cos.

Oh if what I've said was mentioned anywhere else feel free to slap me with a cold fish.
