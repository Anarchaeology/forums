quote:
Originally posted by Angeni
I agree with WillForce, I thought the meaning of the game was to roleplay the conflict.
The world that they created and the backstory to go with it have no real bearing on the purpose of the game, beyond them hoping there are enough fans of this genre to make it profitable.

The world, the items, the storyline(or lack thereof) are all just the filler. Yes, your purpose is to get online and fantasize that you are in the far future on some remote planet engaged in a civil war of sorts. That has nothing to do with the goals of the game company, which is to simply keep you playing as long as possible. The faster you reach your goals, the sooner you leave.

Yes I understand there are some folks out there that get frustrated and leave because of the system. But surprise to them, all these games are the same so there isn't exactly anyplace better to go. The only game that did it a bit differently, listened to all the complaints of the players and made a simple fun, easy to use world was Mythic with DAOC. Interestingly enough, that game is most likely to be voted the most boring game of the year. Most folks played it, leveled up in very little time(even casual gamers) and then left, there was nothing more to the game worth staying for. Personally, I consider that a huge failure. Now Mythic is scrambling to try and add interesting stuff to their gam,e, so far it is all very lame eye candy and adds about another 2-3 days of interest to their world for the players.

Anyhow, I am just trying to point out, that even though an idea sounds great, would be super convienient and make short term game play more fun, it definately would be counter to keeping the playerbase for as long a term as possible. I think as soon as players get over the mindset that they have to somehow "win" the game and complete it, then game designers will have much more leaway in what they can do with their virtual worlds. Unfortunately, most folks feel they have to get the best gear or reach the highest level and then they have "won" the game. After which point of course they stop playing not that long afterwards. So until game designers can stop focusing on slowing the rush to the top down and making some things near impossible to obtain, we are stuck with the way it is for now unfortunately.

I for one look forward to the time when the players of MMORPG's have matured enough for designers to put new and different types of goals and content into the games. I am guessing that willbe quite a while to come yet though.
