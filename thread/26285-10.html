In what way has the OE changes affected the average player's enjoyment of the game? None. And for people who want to twink? A fair bit is still possible, particularly with regard to implants and NCU, and combined with the fact that 20% gives more leeway at higher QLs than at lower ones, a lot is still possible.

It seems that most people who were severely OEd were PvPers or maybe just plain old-fashioned power gamers. Power gamers are bearable up to a point, after which they are capable of ruining a game (exploiters being the worst case). PvP is another matter.

PvP in the game at present (with the possible exception of Camelot) is *not* what was intended. One day this will hopefully change, but for now 1on1 is what we have.

So for now, the game must aim for a certain level of balance in 1on1 PvP. Of course, I'd rather we just had proper team based PvP working, then profession balance would be more about solo PvM than PvP as it often seems now.

Power-leveling a new toon is still pretty easy if you can throw money at them,OE might slow it down a bit, but level 50 shoiuld still be possible in a few days at most.

Of course not everyone will agree, fairness is after all a perception and hence highly subjectve, but fairness is what this is all about. Pity FCs designers trying to do 7 impossible things before breakfast... including please the entire player base...
