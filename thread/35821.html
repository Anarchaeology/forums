Outdoor MOB Loot Idea
So it seems the only way to get good loot these days is through team missions which gets pretty repetitive and boring. I'd like to spend more time hunting and exploring, but that doesn't seem to be a viable option when I can get more experience and better loot from team missions (especially bosses). Camping unique MOBs obviously sucks and is not really a good way to spend my gaming time. In order to make outdoor hunting more attractive I think the following may work.

At spawn time each outdoor MOB gets a born on date/time. As the MOB ages the chances of it dropping unique loot becomes greater. The loot would still be based on the ql of the MOB but would now be a combination of MOB lvl and MOB age. This would mean that MOBs in less travelled areas would live longer and therefore would have a greater chance of having unique loot. The combination of MOB lvl and MOB age would guarantee that a very old leet would not drop something like a ql195 hammer tourturing tool, but they may drop a low ql web cloak.

Having the opportunity for good loot and good xp outside of team missions would certainly give players who tire of missions another option. I know the outdoor hunting spots suck at the high levels right now, but that could be improved as well with the addition of more high level outdoor mobs with the addition of this feature.
