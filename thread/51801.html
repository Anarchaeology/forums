My Traders 110 Evade buff will be mostly useless unless im teaming without a fixer(very rare). In PvP speed is much more important. Thus almost making evade buffs useless (Give me something else to buff then).

2. It is known that breeds are fairly broken. You can practically choose any breed and the Nano/HP gain or lost is very minimal at high level (i think its like +-200). It is just more costly at low level. And the additions the base abilities add to other skills is very minimal difference (at most +-20 points to any given skill depending on color of base ability).

3. Item customization. I remember awhile back (maybe 8 months ago or so) there was an weapon modification tool of some sort available. It had a description of changing a weapon to some degree (dont remember specifics been a long time). Like maybe giving (said weapon) different damage type, or higher minimal damage (like +50 to minimal damage). Hell i think some people would love just a unique color item. Like a Green Home Defender. Anyhow, If this was ever to be implemented obviously make it somewhat hard to find, and worthwhile if found. This would also utilize some more tradeskills.
