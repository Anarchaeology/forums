What I would do to curb "ninja chest looting"
We have all delt with the people who it seems in missions their first point of business is running around and unlocking chests for the loot (with no regard to other players getting their chance). It's very annoying when a "ninja looter" is trying his/her best to unlock a chest while the rest of us in team are fighting away.

There is no way to stop it but if FC could just impliment a feature that the team gets a message of who opens a chest then that may at the least make these looters (who are not bad people just a little greedy) ASK the team if anyone else plans on looting during the mission.

I know its not a big deal but it could make things a little less uncomfortable in missions

I'd support some sort of (toggleable) loot messages that tell other people in the group what a person gets out of a chest just so people can know if they are getting their fair share of chest loot, but I'm not sure there is any game mechanic that can be added that can adequately address the problems of dealing with chest loot.

Its not as easy as just assigning the chest to a person since the majority of people I have grouped with cannot even unlock high level chests.

In general everything goes the quickest if one person with the breaking and entry skill loots all the chests and distributes the loot to group members who need it, but no one can know if they are getting their fair share of the goodies right now.

Calling out alpha chests in a group is perhaps the fairest method, but the people without enough breaking and entry skill still have to rely on someone else to open the chests for them and the result is that the entire group slows down and earns less xp in the same amount of time. For me at least, xp is much more important than loot so that is almost an unbearable solution. I'd rather have unfair distribution of loot than poor xp.
