Envisioned: Client<->Server Sync
Yesterday I was hunting in medusas - I was pulling as usually and the team waited for me on the hills.
At one time I outran the mob out of visibility and after a few moments the mob cached up with me. This is the perfect situation for instant death bug - And so be it, I was killed by instant death bug. I could see my health dropping by all in my team said I was at full health. I yelled HEAL HEAL.. but it was to late and I died.

This has happend so many times for me dieing either because my doc cant see im dropping on health - Or the "classic" instant death bug that occurs when u solo and ur health drops from full to 0.
Well - after the medusa Incident I got an idea on how u could do a client<->server sync. Cause this seems to be the problem - that the client dosnt now that its being attacked and the server isnt sending any information. Or in the medusa case were My client was updating health but not sending to server.

I got a couple of ideas on how this could be done:

1) Make a shortcut or chat command that allows the player to send 1 byte to the server that sends it back - Confirming to the client it is responding (Much like when u open (u) skills and click accept ur health gets updated - dosnt work when being attacked though)

2) Give the client a repeating command to send hp info every 1 sec - and the server should respond by sending its information back. This could be a bit heavy if every client logged on should send hp info every second - So it could be done while only in combat mode - ie. hitting *q*

This is no flame - Its just a thing I very much would see adressed soon as possible.
