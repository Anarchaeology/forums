It used to work that way. Unfortunately, it also worked that way with team buffs (specifically, doc team HP buffs). A solo-ing doc would not be able to buff himself with the long-term HP buff (45 minutes), but instead have to make do with the 5 minute single-target HP buff. This was absurd. I had to literally ask somebody to team with me before going to do a mission so that I could cast the team HP buff on myself and then leave the team and go on my merry way.

I don't remember if it was this way for team heals as well. For every profession *but* traders, the team heals is much weaker than the single-target heals. So you asking to not allow a solo'ing class to team heal for less HP while not in a team is kind of stupid.

Logically, you are correct in that a team heal should be for the team. And by the definition of the word "team", it means 2 or more people. But this does not mean a team of 1 should suffer because they are unable to utilize all buffs and nanos for their profession when choosing to hunt solo. I believe this is why Funcom made the change, and I support the change.

Plus, Trader team heals are being nerfed in an upcoming patch, so your quest to nerf Trader team heals will be accomplished shortly.
