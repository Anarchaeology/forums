ICC
Sorry, the sarcasm in the last post must have been mistaken for idealistic fervor.

You are, of course, entirely correct - the ICC are too much of a galactic 'threat' to Omni-Tek to be something that can just be 'bombed'. The thing that many people miss about the ICC, however, is that they are made up of all their member corporations with (I believe) a proportional representation system.

Ironically, this means the main decision-making power in the ICC is Omni-Tek! The irony that the one organisation 'protecting' the Clans is, in fact, what could be described as the conscience of the very group they are fighting is quite delicious.

However, what ICC really represents to Omni-Tek is the combined voices of our competition (Sol Banking, TrueSpace, etc.) against our voice - as well as Omni-Tek's source of PR and visible conscience to the rest of the galactic community.

So is there something rotten in ICC? Yes and no. Yes there is in as much as it is the best representation of Omni-Tek's corporate opposition. No in that it is also representative of Omni-Tek, and only the combined might of our competitors can force the ICC's hand away from what we'd want. And like any democratic system where no one group can form a majority, we can - if needed - cut deals and form coalitions with other groups to ensure our own position.

What this boils down to is that in many cases decisions made by the ICC for Rubi-Ka can be seen as management decisions by Omni-Tek's offworld management presented in a way that is 'palatable' for our corporate and local adversaries.

Is the ICC purely to prevent Omni-Tek repeating a Farmatek? I think that is more of a 'side effect' than anything else. I see it as more akin to the United Nations agreement in the late 20th/early 21st century on Earth. Essentially the ICC forces each company to tread carefully in case the combined fear of their competitors cause the ICC hammer to fall. And where one member has more clout, they are able to call more shots. Omni-Tek is very much the ICC's 'America' and Sol Banking ICC's 'China' - the two big players in the ICC who hold all the real power.

I believe that ICC actually predates Omni-Tek. Part of its inception as a concept may well have been an anti-Omega concept, but back then Omni-Tek was one of many small corporations, and as a result were (and I believe still are) one of the staunchest supporters of the ICC and what they represent.

Omni-Tek are also the largest provider of combat troops for ICC operations, and currently the only corporation with any military assets worth mentioning to 'spare'. This in itself makes the very concept of an ICC military presence on Rubi-Ka laughable. Were the ICC to 'send troops' to aid, say, the Clans then those troops would be elite off-world trained Solitus combat squads provided by none other than Omni-Tek. Which is precisely why any thoughts of ICC military intervention will be stopped before they get that far - Omni-Tek is not going to field troops against itself.

Omni-Tek is your friend.

Cell Scanners
Certainly.

((this is all available in the timeline and such, so forgive my paraphrasing and occasional lapses of actual knowledge)).

One of the discovered side-effects of the Notum-rich environment of Rubi-Ka is that the 'essence' of a person - their soul, if you like, has a tendancy to hang around before dissipating (or going to its final resting place, if you like).

Observations made were that if a suitable recepticle was available, the 'soul' could be inserted into said recepticle and preserved. After considerabe experimentation, Omni-Tek researchers discovered that the ideal recepticle was a clone-body of the original person, and the absolute best and most stable results were to put the soul into a clone-body that had been age-accelerated to roughly the same age as the original.

The soul would also carry some residual memory - kind of like past-life memories - hazy and incomplete.

By combining this discovery with cloning, advance-growth technology and making periodic 'mind maps' of people, it was found that a host body which had a recent mental imprint of the original then used to 'trap' the departing soul would result in the same person effectively coming back to life, the hazy memories of the 'soul' filling in the necessary blanks since the last time a memory map was taken - provided the memory map is less than a few months old.

So when you insure, you're actually getting a 'mind dump' of your knowledges and experience at that time. When you die, your old body actually croaks and a new body is prepared with your last insured 'mind' imprinted.

So what does all this have to do with ICC?

Well, Omni-Tek invent this technology and immediately provide it to all their combat-troops fighting against the Clans. This is something of an advantage - Omni-Tek troops can take a bullet to the head and be fighting fit again in under an hour, Clan troops can take a bullet to the head and die.

So the ICC step in and say 'lo! such medical technologies are surely meant for the benefit of all mankind! Give us verily this technology so that we may cure death upon all worlds!'

Omni-Tek, after considerable argument, capitulate.

Unfortunately this only works on Rubi-Ka. Actually I believe it does work elsewhere, provided that you have an environment that is artificially notum-enriched, and the dying person is within yards of their new body. Great for the insanely wealthy, but not so good for use with military personnel who have a wont to die a long way away from a handy hospital.

So the ICC take said technology and build 'ICC Cell Scanners' in areas that Omni-Med isn't covering (neutral and clan territories). And now everyone has it.

If you're curious, try taking a trip to Omni-1 and looking at the cell scanners there. They're not 'ICC Cell Scanners' - they are 'Omni-Med Cell Scanners' And if you're a clanner, remember to turn yourself in to the proper authorities for re-education while you're at it.

Oh, and as an aside: I'm not available to join any orgs. I'm already rather heavily involved in one. However, I do believe that the cell scanner technology is a large part of the problem on Rubi-Ka - it cheapens death and as a result cheapens war.

I'm not stupid enough to want to eliminate a strategic advantage, so my ideal situation is that all ICC Cell Scanners are destroyed, and all Omni-Med cell scanners are left exactly where they are.
