Attention Citizens of Rubi ka, please ignore the following omni propoganda, found in the latest issue of the Rubi ka Suns-Times: It is filled with lies:

"By Laurine Loomb Unsell

(RKS Wire Services) Borealis: The Nanomage Liberation Front and Desert Winds appear to be at it again. Both groups are well known terrorists, and in recent weeks, have volleyed various attacks on mining outfits across the entire zone of Stret West Bank, leaving local citizens frightened to leave their homes. Its not just the fact that they are attacking, but it is the methods they employ. They wouldnt think twice about bombing Reets Retreat if they thought it would suit their purposes. I cant even tell you how many children I have seen sent to reclaim in the last week Says frightened stret West Citizen Roland Killamb Svencara.
Though thankfully most of the attacks have been successfully defended, victory has come at quite a cost, in both Mining facilities, profit, and most importantly, lives.
And now, the Coup des grace: NLF Matriarch Queen Fleshreaper made this statement in borealis Thursday, On Saturday, March 1, The world shall hear a new battle cry, and see that all they have been fighting for was a lie. Desert Winds and the NLF shall present to the World the Borealis Pact. I promise this document shall shake Rubi ka to its very core. Till all are Free! When asked to further expound on this statement, she waould not comment. Spokespeople from dessert Winds would not answer our tells.
It is not known exactly what will happen in ICC on Saturday, but in this reporters opinion, and judging from both groups past records, it would probably be best to stay away from ICC on Saturday March 1 at 7PM EST!

Laurine Loomb Unsell is a Freelance reporter for the Rubi ka Suns Times.
