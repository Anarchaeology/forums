*Yet* another refreshed PVP thread
New and refreshed pvp guide

--------------------

wohey.. I finally got around to refreshing a .. er.. 6 month old pvp thread I wrote... I've seen a lot of things about on Rubika that can work for an enforcer..

If your'e duelling... QB/EQB is the best overall wep I've seen so far... However in towerfights, alphas are more important... I reccomend 1HB/1HE combos then..

The most common mistake an enf does, is to use one tactic alone on all classes. Here's my thoughts on how to get things done with the different classes.

(note, I use a Partial Deflecion shield graft that adds 10% reflection on top of my regular bracers + Parryshield and riposteshield on melee fighters)

Fighting Soldiers:
---------------------
Soldiers put their strength in heavy alphas and the reflect shield.
This is what I do: Get in, Fully loaded with Mongo/chall/rage up and healed, Reflection/Dmg shield and the highest parryshield/Fob I can use.
As they will most likely Alpha me pretty bad, I take the alpha at full def with Parryshield up. After the Soldier alpha, Mongo, Rage, Fob, *smacK' like mad to force up an early TMS (the sooner you get them to do total reflection, the better). During TMS period, you have the following option:
- If you have a high HE or if your'e Solitus/nanomage enf, stay at Full agg, keep rage running, and chaincast Layer (goes *much* faster with rage) and have your mongo heal you up while eating damage with a constantly refreshed layer.
- If you DON'T have a HE, and happens to be a Nanopoor enf, feel free to waste the layer-tank, but I advise you to go at def and try to survive with stims, slow mongo 8also good to have rage running then.
use your first aids to keep your nano somewhat alive, remember to save up some for the finnishing alpha. During the Total reflection, you don't hit the soldier unless you have QB... doing that causes you more damage than him, Therefore, Use the time to coordinate Mongo/layer casting.
if you succeed, you shouldn't recieve more than 1 more alpha which you will be ready for. Eventually, the shield drops. Count the seconds and know what TMS the soldier have.
As the shield drops, so does his former Reflecion shield since this is overwritten by TMS. at this point, I go Challenger/Fob and blast the Poor soldier, not able to heal, to kingdom come.

ATM, Soldiers are on average not powerful enough unless they are properly buffed or have a hellspinner up'n running

Fighting MA's
---------------------------
Fighting MA's is a joke now... I can't see how an MA *can* win a fight vs an enf... Mongo crush heals better than an MA can heal... We hit harder... we have more HP... MA's are wasted and I pity them as I enjoyed the "close to balanced" state we had vs MA's

Fighting Fixers:
---------------------------
If your'e out of nano, you die(root root). As NR works somewhat now, keep rage running and don't FoB untill your'e sure you can land a hard alpha. and... use weps with specials...

Fighting Traders:
----------------------------
Traders are tricky. Maxed Nanoresistance combined with chainrage makes it all a cointoss. Depends on your alpha. Arena fights usually benefits the enf as the trader are being chased with a limited ammount of HP and the need to drain the Enf to 50% efficiency to have a good chance
if you can't get a trader on the first 30 seconds, your'e toast in 99% of the cases

Fighting Crats
-----------------------------
Hmmm.. I have little experience, allthough I remember I was surprised on how much demotivational speeches set me back last time I fought one

Fighting MP's
-----------------------------
If you've seen something tragic, it's an enf with autoattack attacking an MP's pet. I focus on the MPand throws in all I got of "Christmas presents". you will outdamage the Heal pet if your'e good enough. Remember, they debuff your nanoskills
MP's still a decent pvp class IMO... just VERY tricky to play

FIghting other enforcers:
------------------------------
Enf vs Enf is probably one of the most entertaining things to watch. use what you got of shields and buffs and smash till the first one drops. there is no better fight

Fighting Adventurers:
-------------------------------
as a high level adventurer in kitty-shape insta-heals 2.5K (2 heals and FA), you need to do a lot of damage.. fast.. same as before, Full Christmas present and no mercy. I've had some of my best fights with adventurers. do not back to heal up from an adv.. they heal better

Fighting NT's
-------------------------------
The NT's I've encountered are using some sort of NS. there's really no need to waste Good alphas on this, so just be sure save up an alpha to present as it runs out. other than that, do *not* underestimate an NT. they nuke and root like mad.
use the Layer tank with NT's. (found on bodyguards in the Foremans office static dungeon in TLR).
Fighting NT's in the open is hardly fair to the NT. You outrun them.. rage keeps you unrooted... Enfs aren't supposed to loose to NT's

Fighting Agents:
-------------------------------
Enf's don't like agents . But with good enough skill on the rage button, you should be able to land enoudh alphas to whack em all. I especially don't like it if they get 3 crits in and i end up loosing 8K HP in 3 sec.

FIghting Docs:
-------------------------------
BE cruel, hit hard, give em a full present so they keep themselve busy whealing. the sad part is the Init-debuff.. use Mongo and first aids. you might be able to withstand some damage and soner or later, the doc *will* forget to refresh the debuff or you will withstand on full def with rage. I usually rely on being tricky.. saving dimach till when I need it. I hate the slow debuff btw... so much...

FIghting Engies
-------------------------------
Smack the owner, not the pet should do the trick now.
I hate fighting engies and it's a lie that they are worthless in pvp Ever met 2 Slayers at once? BTW, no need for RRF or bracer doesn't really reflection doesn't really exist in engy world
