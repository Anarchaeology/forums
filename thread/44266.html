Do you really have that much faith in ICC?
quote:
Those "ICC dirtbags" are kind enough to allow you to continue your shady lease and exclusively mine this planet. If you know what's good for you, you'll be extra-special nice when you run into them.
I probably should have stated my position on ICC first: Please consider the following facts:

1. At the behest of Sol BC, ICC has invoked emergency powers that allow it to act against member hyper-corproations without majority approval. Not surprisingly, ICC's first dictatorial action has been to dispatch armed troops to Rubi-Ka, under the guise of a peacekeeping effort.

2. This extreme response by ICC has supposedly been motivated by the need to maintain stability between a weakened CoT and a discordant OT. However, relations between these groups are currently not heavily strained. Rather, any recent instability has been as a result of the external influence of the DB and the CoA.

3. OT and CoT have abided by the terms of the Tir accord, whose formulation and signature were observed by ICC. In fact, OT has gone beyond the original terms of the agreement to completely withdraw its mining operations from all Clan-held territories. Nonetheless, ICC continues to meddle in Rubi-Kan affairs on the grounds that the Tir Accord is being jeopardized.

4. ICC was unilaterally responsible for abridging OT's lease on Rubi-Ka and nearly abrogated the contract in spite of a lack of concrete evidence demonstrating wrongdoing by OT. The option to renew this lease is 100% contingent upon OT complying with all future ICC directives.

5. Although ICC's stated mandate is to represent the interests of its consituent hyper-corporations, its actions are constantly in conflict with OT's agenda. You may remember that ICC initiated a failed military campaign against OT, which was abandoned due to a lack of member support in the wake of the corporate wars.

Whereas OT and ICC may have once been in league, it is clear to me now that this is no longer the case. I am currently of the mind that ICC, backed by SBC and potentially greater interests, are behind many of the destablizing events of late. On the surface, it would appear that their interest is to seize the notum concession from Omni-Tek and end the Clan bid towards sovereignty.

In this regard, it is highly likely that they are the unseen backers of both the Dust Brigade as well as the Council of Ares. It would seem to me that ICC has been using these agencies as tools to sow discontent and provide an ideal pretext for an ICC invasion of Rubi-Ka.

As such, ICC no longer has any legitimacy in my opinion. How can ICC continue to function as a respresentative body when it is no longer accountable to its consituents? How can ICC claim to be a neutral arbiter of the situation on Rubi-Ka when it is clearly being manipulated by SBC? How can ICC expect the citizens of Rubi-Ka to recognize their peacekeeping authority when their agenda clearly runs counter to the interests of OT and Clan alike?

In summary, ICC are hypocrites of the highest order. They are not to be trusted by OT employees nor should they be welcomed by Clan or neutral supporters. I would further recommend that you adopt a similar view as the "extra-special-nice" approach is liable to get us all killed.

Wake up people! There are far greater forces at play here!
