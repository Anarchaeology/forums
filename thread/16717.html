Well
Full bad/full money gives:

Three find item missions, one return item mission and one 'fixing type' mission (radar, food sacks, e.g.).

Find item mission are considered quite optimal, because you get two items (find and reward) and the payment is max, because yu get no XP for the full bad/full money-find item missions. You can get find item missions with other settings that effect XP and money and mission setup (closed doors, traps, etc.)

Another setting
You might want to try these settings, if you are interested in locked doors and chests and a higher chance for traps (good xp for the right profession):

order    ----------|---------- chaos
open     ----------o---------| hidden
physical ----------o---------| mystical
head-on  ----------o---------| stealth

This should give you 3 find item, 1 fixing and 1 find person mission (5 good ones ). Interestingly these find item mission give a tiny ammount of XP, in contrast to the full bad/full money find item missions.

My settings are similar, and result in 3 find missions, and may meet your needs:
Code:

order    --|-------o---------- chaos
open     ----------o---------| hidden

Those settings are the ones I usually use, and they are also the settings that have found me two yalms and one hydro vehicle.
