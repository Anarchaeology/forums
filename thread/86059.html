Geez, playing 6-10 hours a day for a week you could have leveled any of your chars to over 100 by now if you had put the same effort into killing stuff as you did to blitzing. Also, like cold said unless you keep your fixer low level, GA 1 has a very limited shelf life. It starts losing effectiveness when you reach about level 80, at which time you need another 100 million for GA 2 (if you have to buy it) or IP reset Mat Crea and switch to regular armor. Buying GA is only worth it for people who have a very high level main char with nothing else to do with their money.

Try this instead: Get yourself the best regular armor your fixer can equip (I keep all my base stats except psychic maxxed and wore flowers/elite/primus for most of my 138 levels), max your evades and ranged init, get a nice gun like an MCS or Sol Chironis, lower your agg/def bar, and spend some IP in other nano skills instead of the black hole that is matter creation. Mobs will still miss you often enough to make it worth it and you learn more about being a fixer than running up to something, hitting tab-q, and going to make a sandwich. I've never worried about getting GA and one thing that gives me a warm fuzzy feeling inside is knowing that all those twink fixers my level wearing GA can't self-cast half the nanos that I can. That and the fact that if they didn't have GA they wouldn't have a clue how to play a fixer and would die rather quickly in PvP or PvM.

You can find CDS pieces in QLs as low as the mid-60s (I think), but 81-91ish seems to be the norm of the lower range.

Half a million a piece is a decent price (at that level), but it's a seller's market. Some will try to gouge, claiming they spent all week just to get that one part they're selling (or some junk). Maybe they did. Who cares?

Full suits are a problem because of the spotty nature of the CDS drops. Sellers aren't likely (if they're Agents or Fixers) to be replacing their entire suit at the same time, unless some serious farming has been done.

Don't sweat GA, and don't sweat CDS either. If you want CDS, you will get it, eventually, either through effort or by paying a reasonable price.

Don't blow a lot of credits just to have either.
