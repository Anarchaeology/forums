OT-DTR lloking for members - RK1
Who can join us?

Omni-Tek DTR is open for all professions and all grades of Omni-Tek employees. We will train the unexperienced and will provide experienced employees with new challenges.

You may however wish to hav a look into our roster to see if you think you fit in. We do not have overly many members within the low level range (1-15), but you still can benefit - and be beneficial to others as you rise.

Join now and make Rubi-Ka a better place.

How do I join?

Just give the recruiting Commanders a /tell. They will come back to you as soon as possible.

See the table for which may be available:

Name Timezones
Midhir Europe & similar
Gennha US & similiar

Our standard procedure is to let you join with the rank of an Applicant. This means you will be part of OT-DTR but not yet full fledged member. This status will last for at least a week!

After that you may become a full member with all support from OT-DTR.


Offplay:
Have a look at our site at:
www.nightfae.de/ot-dtr/

The Guild has just started, so we are still in the phase of heavy recruiting.

We wish for players that want to partake in the story - and things look good for that - we are already involved.

The guild has no fixed objective - things will evolve during the story. We will provide you with gatherings which support role playing your characters, hunting parties, events and stuff.

This guild is best suited for european players, as I reside in timezone GMT+1. But players from everywhere are very welcome, too (especially as we are building a beta section, that will consist of players from US timezones).

If you chose to join, please keep in mind that the guild is not a self serving restaurant where you get stuffed. People will give you equipment to help you to be useful - equipment that they earned the hard way though missions. So honour that.

You should also keep in mind, that guilds are fun only, when you play the game somewhat frequently... No one enjoys a member that shows up once a month.

The idea feeds on individual involvement.
