Classifications
This is an off-shoot from the sticky thread about Rk1 RP Orgs, in which it was suggested orgs subscribe to a classification system to describe themselves.

I have to say, I don't really care for classifications. Besides, there are other criteria you could you could argue are more significant: orgs that equate RP with PvP, orgs that equate RP with socialising, orgs designed to roleplay the Official Story Line, orgs that roleplay thier own stories. Any one wants to learn more of RUR can check out our wonderful website or just ask one of us in game.
