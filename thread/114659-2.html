FC has pretty much already said they won't be addressing any profession issues that aren't "game breakers" until SL is release.

In other words they'll nerf stuff if it's too uber, and they'll fix blatant bugs and exploits, but don't expect any boosts to any professions, unless it comes from items in a content patch, and that is mainly is presuming Inner Sanctum goes live before SL.

I'd guess the bulk of their programmer and area builder resources - probably over 70% - are committed to the SL development effort and QA (hah!), rather than to "production support" of live servers. So only "urgent" defects will probably be getting addressed. "High" priority defects get workarounds of one sort or another, like the engie pistol (the workaround being keeping it out of the game).

I think it will be a lucky break if the engineer pistol is fixed before SL since the problem is a code bug (really, a program logic flaw) with the "Eat" function, not a case of the item being designed improperly. That tends to be much more resource-intensive to fix.
