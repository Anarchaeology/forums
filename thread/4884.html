Why open Omni Departments but not Clans?
Funcom,

I'm just curious why you are envisioning opening the Omni Departments but do not mention the advertised clans (Knights, Sentinels, etc.). I'm not saying you shouldn't do the former, I think your pre-launch literature plainly gave the impression that players would be able to join those and get departmental missions from the beginning. And I know that not listing the clans doesn't necessarily mean you won't open them at some point. But it does seem an obvious omission since you have been asked over and over whether you will be opening BOTH the Departments and the NPC Clans.
