Dust Brigade Strike again:
Borealis Appartment Complex 6 Firebombed:

---

Today, I was assaulted in my home inside complex 6 in Borealis, and the apartment was firebombed. The attack began when a mutant entered my apartment bypassing the security protocols; it approached me immediately and exploded sending me back to the tree of life I had tied my essence to. While I was coming to in the garden the building began to collapse around my stunned guests and they ran for the exit; and upon getting outside safely they were ambushed by a squad of Dust Brigade troops.

The Brigade soldiers sent one of my guests to reclaim while the rest escaped with heavy injuries. While everyone was confirming that no one was permanently damaged; the soldiers escaped in the direction of the mines outside of Borealis. Additionally disturbing is that although they were followed closely they appear to of vanished completely near the shacks by the lake.

The destruction to my home was a total loss, and none of my house guests escaped without taking considerable damage, some were injured critically. Due to the nature of these attacks not only on myself, but on Itel and the random assaults in Athen and Rome; I advise everyone to go about their business with extreme caution. The Brigade has offered no explanation as to the motivation for the intensity or the reason for their selected targets, and therefore are even more dangerous due to their unpredictability.

Safe travels

-Falikos
