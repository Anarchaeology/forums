A simple fix to get people to go outdoors!
Here's the current issue that is creating this problem, where people only do missions from Newland to BS:

reasons people choose missions:
token
better items (boss loot)
faster and higher xp (boss+ mission completion add a ton)
less adds
a set number of mobs in a very low amount of space

reasons to mission outdoors
looks better, more eye candy

problems with outdoors that make them less appealing:
more chance for adds/death
no tokens
less loot
slower xp
much further to go to camp
much higher risk with many camps in 25%


Something seriously has to change the bads so far outwiegh the goods that it is sick! Right now the game is programmed for you to spend all your time indoors if you want to get to high levels. Many people's most fond memories while xping seem to involve being outdoors... (I'll never forget my time at NW mines, Shades in PM, camps in OF, Ace Camp yet everyday I logoff I forget my time in missions)...

Missions shouldn't provide the best of everything... it would be best if there was a give and take, say.. mobs in the outdoors provide 50% more xp than those in missions... in other words... if I meet a lvl 60 shade in a mission I get 2000 xp, if I meet him at NW mines, I get 3000 xp... this goes on up to and including.. if I meet a real mean in a mission and get 50,000 xp, and I meet the same lvl real mean in the outdoors I get 75,000 xp... people would be forced to make a choice. Do I go to a mission and get tokens and special items or do I go outdoors to maximize my xp. I think you'd have groups of people going in both directions, which is probably closer to what was probably the original intention.

There needs to be more adequate return for the risk of hunting outdoors. The more people see the outdoors of Rubi-ka the more fun stories they will have to tell... people always listen to the old war stories of hunting outdoors... any stories about "blah, blah, I was in mission, blah blah blah, then the rollerrat, blah, blah, blah" are so boring and getting old...

I want to here people talking about the eremite add they got... or how they stumbled on a pit lizard... or the swamp warden that attacked them... those were the days... the days we miss most (except for the bugs back then :P)

Well, other ideas have been suggested too, at various times.

I alone have suggested.

Outdoor styled missions. Make a new model group, like brown caverns or office, that is open to the sky and lacking walls.

Put tokens on every 20th-30th outdoor mob. I would suggest that the system check level and if you're not within half of grouping level, or within maybe half of PK range, then deny the token. (Force people to hunt yellows for outdoor tokens that way.) And of course the tokens shouldn't be bravery or service tokens in this case either.

As for boss mob loot, they've done it. They're called dyna-camps. (Maybe just put tokens on the mobs in THESE areas.)

Random swarms of mobs, with an announcement through chat, that appear once in awhile, complete with their own bosses, in areas that don't normally have a concentration of mobs of any type. Call it herd, migration, swarm, whatever, but throw in all the advantages of missions into a small geographic area that's open to all, and only for a short while, (half an hour to an hour.)

A new one, that I was going to post on its own. Limit the number of solo missions a person can take to about half the experience. This requires tracking mission-acquired experience alongside experience to level. If mission-acquired experience, (from killing mission mobs, unlocking/disarming chests and doors, and mission completion,) is equal to or greater than half the experience to level, don't allow another mission to be taken at this time. Personally, I'll take as many missions as I can because I want that token, but if everybody was forced to go outside occassionally, the playing field would be level still.
