An open letter to Mr. Phillip Ross, The Council of Truth and the ICC
Greetings all,
Recently there has been a lot of talk on the the topic of terrorism and the Dust Brigade in general. The Dust'ers are without a doubt rabid animals that need to be be euthanised to ensure the safety of of all the inhabitants of Rubi-ka.

There is one issue with which I have trouble comprehending. The Dust Brigade are terrorists by definition, and they are a product of the Clans without a doubt. This tends to foster a certain belief in a fair number of the non-clan population that the Dust Brigade and all of the other Clans obviously have the same goal or hold to the same ideology. This is not true.

While it may be true that some Clans or Clan-sponsored individuals are every bit as murderous as the Dust Brigade, let us us not forget in the history of Humanity, terrorism is never united under one flag or comprised of one nationality. There are terrorists on all sides of Rubi-kan politics.

Soldiers who shoot at unarmed civilians without the slightest provocation are not soldiers, they are predators and agressors. Indivuals or Organizations that are not within the official Omni-Tek military structure who murder innocents are the same sort of terrorists as the Dust Brigade.

I ask that in order to prevent the current political situation from turning into a War, all sides of the conflict show a bit of restraint and compassion. Terrorism must not be tolerated and any who engage in terrorist activity even if it is unwittinly must be dealt with harshly.

Sincerely,

Mihn Silvenestri

Let us not forget the history of the clans...lazy union workers who wanted more pay and more concessions and throw a tantrum any time it's suggested that they quit their ceaseless quest for handouts and actually do the work they were hired for, and contribute something back to the corporation that continues to pay to maintain the artificial environment that keeps you alive.

We're all entertained by the spectacle of the Council of Truth coming apart at the seams over the dust brigade, a group that most of us know is at least funded by another megacorporation, if not entirely populated by them. In fact, I would bet that the dust brigade issue is of scant day-to-day importance to anyone not in Omni-Pol or Omni-AF. I know I could personally care less, and only hope that one of them can be captured alive so that I might...explore its origins, so to speak, and see for instance what sort of pain tolerance it has and how much psychological damage it takes before it is driven insane. But purely as a matter of interest, you understand, not because I'm affected by their terrorist tactics. I certainly haven't modified my behavior in the least because of them.

As such, your paranoid little plea not to be categorized as one of them is particularly humorous. Omni-Tek is well aware that few clan members are remotely so skilled or well-equipped as the Dust Brigade, and the way Henry Radiman and the other members of the Council are soiling their collective diaper over them makes one thing plain - at least the Dust Brigade warrants some measure of respect.
