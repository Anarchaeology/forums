This scenario lacks accuracy, play with the numbers as you wish.

Twink attacks base (Twink has an Essence)
This character has (for the sake of arguement not accuracy) 1600 HP.

6 Defenders come along, they're not really twinked.
Each defender has 500 HP and no buffs.

Then we'll add some arbitary damage values - the twink is good and can do twice as much damage as any of the defenders and crits a bit. These are all low level characters so we'll assume the attack cycle takes 5 seconds (2.5/2.5).

Defenders engage attacker
Attacker takes 50 HP damage * 6 (1300 remaining)
Defender 1 takes 200 points of damage (300 remaining)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 6 damage again (1000 remaining)
Defender 1 takes 100 normal, 200 special damage (Defender 1 dies)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 5 damage (750 HP remaining)
Defender 2 takes 100 damage (400 HP remaining)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 5 damage (500 remaining)
Defender 2 takes 200 damage (200 remaining)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 5 damage (250 remaining)
Defender 2 takes 200 damage (Defender 2 dies)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 4 damage (50 remaining)
Defender 3 takes 100 damage (400 remaining)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 4 damage (Attacker dies)

Now with a HoT running...

Defenders engage attacker
Attacker takes 50 HP damage * 6 (1300 remaining)
Defender 1 takes 200 points of damage (300 remaining)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 6 damage again (1000 remaining)
HoT Heals attacker for 360 damage (1360 remaining)
Defender 1 takes 100 normal, 200 special damage (Defender 1 dies)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 5 damage (1110 HP remaining)
Defender 2 takes 100 damage (400 HP remaining)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 5 damage (860 remaining)
HoT Heals attacker for 360 damage (1220 remaining)
Defender 2 takes 200 damage (200 remaining)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 5 damage (970 remaining)
Defender 2 takes 200 damage (Defender 2 dies)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 4 damage (770 remaining)
HoT Heals attacker for 360 damage (1130 remaining)
Defender 3 takes 100 damage (400 remaining)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 4 damage (930 remaining)
Defender 3 takes 200 damage & 100 Special (100 remaining)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 4 damage (730 remaining)

Defender 3 takes 200 damage (Defender 3 dies)

Attacker takes 50 HP * 3 damage (940 remaining)
Defender 4 takes 100 damage (400 remaining)

Attacker takes 50 * 3 damage (790 remaining)
HoT Heals attacker for 360 damage (1150)

At this point we may as well give in... the attackers HP is now increasing again, and all remaining defenders will be killed in short order.
