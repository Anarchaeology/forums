IP-reset will happen in patch 14.2 (if something doesn't happen)

It works like this: (cut&paste from Gaute's official article found HERE

One point at Level 1.

2 points at Level 15

2 points at Level 50

3 points at Level 100

3 points at Level 150

4 points at Level 190.

and one point is used to reset one skill

So if you are Level 200, you will actually get 15 IP reset points. (If you are Level 16, you get 3, and so on...)

The reset will work like this: When you reset a skill, it drops down to 5 in base. BUT (and this is a large "but"): we have a system called "dribble-down". This you will have noticed skills being increased as an effect of increasing the main abilities. This is most easily explained by looking at "Body Development". It is inherited 100% from Stamina. That means that, for every 4 points of increased Stamina, you get 1 more point of Body Development. Let's say "Pete" resets his Body Development skill. He has 100 points in Stamina. Even after the IP reset, his Body Development will be 30! (5 points + 25 in "dribble-down" from Stamina).



--oh, almost forgot: when this patch is intruduced, you will receive lots of IPR-points that will be temperary



When this is introduced the first time, you will have about 90 points (give or take a few). So you may reset some skills many times, or all skills at once.

These temporary points will last until the next patch (i.e. 3 weeks). They will then disappear, and you are then awarded the standard IP reset pool. These are the rules for getting new IP reset points:
