Memories
The desert night was quiet, like countless other nights before. The big bird glided silently through the sky, a faint whisper from its feathers as they rushed through the air. She was the reigning empress of the lands around, so she flew where she chose with impunity.

Only the occasional cry of a small creature scuttling around looking for a mate or falling prey to something bigger could be heard breaking the resounding silence. None were close enough to be of any interest to the bird.

She suddenly saw something unfamiliar on a distant hill, a strange gleam. She closed in, warily but certainly, as experience had taught her. Cautiously, she sniffed the air, picking up the scent of something hot, unpleasant. There was more, something more familiar. Faint whiffs of an unknown animal, a new inhabitant, either a threat or a treat.
Ah, there it was, a big thing reeking of something unknown, and beside it a smaller creature, foul smelling as well  but also with the smell of life on it. Large it was, and unknown, so she retreated, using the winds around the top to lift her up and give her speed.

The man sat in the darkness, unmoving like a statue, apparently lost in thought while listening to the silence. This was a false appearance, he was aware of the bird and its potential danger, but left it alone as it did not come close.
He sat on the tall, sheer-sided rock, with his feet dangling over the edge and his back turned toward his vehicle. It was the only way to get up here and it made infrequent metallic noises as it cooled down.

Of course, the craft was not switched completely off, in this world, his world, that would be an unthinkable error. This was a world of danger and sudden death where your only warning usually was the sudden pain from a hit.
So all the passive scanners were operating and the craft was ready to spring to life at a single whispered command, just as his weapons were at his side, loaded and ready for use.
Any possibly dangerous movement would immediately be brought to his attention and dealt with.
Such an interruption was not expected tonight, but then they never were, he smiled to himself under his helmet.

He thought about the strange twists his life had taken over the long years.
He was an Atrox and so his first true memories were from his training classes. He was taught to be an OT soldier, to protect civilians and efficiently destroy the enemy.

He became interested in learning more and spent his meagre free time studying. Naturally he at first concentrated on expanding his knowledge in the area of soldiering but soon diversified his interests. He learned as much as he possibly could, much more than others suspected.

After basic training he was assigned to duty as a guard at an administrative complex. The years at the office block were a good time and time passed quickly.

Then he was reassigned to a place called Area P. It was a mining complex and he was to guard the workers.
But the things he saw happening there were horrible beyond his worst nightmares and his whole world view was shattered. That OT, his OT, could in any way be responsible for these events were beyond belief. He still did not believe that it was so, not all he had learned could be wrong.
It had to be a small minority, unable to handle the power they wielded. History had many examples of such cases.

Atrox are generally considered to be less intelligent than other breeds and slow learners. This may be true but it can be a double edged sword.
In most respects he recognized that he was an average Atrox and like all of them, once an idea had taken hold in his mind, there it remained.
All through boot camp his instructors had stressed the soldiers duty to protect the innocent, even to laying down his life to this end. One officer had said that a real soldier will live with honour, fight with honour and die with honour.

Certain events, even now painful to dwell upon, made him decide that he had to leave Area P. That would be difficult, given the conditions in that place, so he made plans and preparations, carefully taking his time to avoid su****ion.

He escaped one night during a heavy storm and ran through the night, far into the the desert. After a long and arduous journey he came to a clan outpost.
As he lay watching the place to find a safe way in, a fire broke out and trapped a group of people inside a nearby building. So, adhering to his instructors words, he broke cover and ran to the fire where he, alongside two Atrox clan guards, took a big part in the effort to save the strangers.
Badly burned, he then approached the nearest, very surprised, officer to surrender. This event made them slightly more inclined to believe him, so he was finally given a new identity and a chance to live free, even to the point of using insurance  something guards at Area P were not allowed to do.

He stayed away from joining any organizations, fearing that history would repeat itself yet again.
Remembering an ancient proverb, that even one man can make a difference, he started a one-man private detective agency. With a reference to his race, it became known as Gorilla, inc and so he made a living for himself fighting various dangerous criminals, generally trying to make the world safe for ordinary people.
His particular brand of honesty made him on occasion do things which others thought strange.
He would be merciless in his attempts to rid the world of criminals, regardless of gender and age.
But if one of these same criminals came to him, seeking him to, say, locate a missing child, then he would accept the assignment.

Thus he became used to constant vigilance, being alone in the crowd and never really trusting anyone but always trying his best to satisfy his ever more demanding clients.

So it was that he went into the desert to relax as much as he ever could and to enjoy the silence.
Out here he could wind down and release the tension of missions.
Out here he could wonder at the way his life had turned out.
Out here he could mourn for those friends  in fact his only family, adopted though it was  lost in that infernal place all those years ago.

Out here, in the solitude of the desert night, he could remember his real name, his previous life.

Only out here could he spend time with his memories.
