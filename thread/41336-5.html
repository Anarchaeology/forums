quote:
Kenlon, I would say giving out all the formulas and numbers would just make people find the perfect character template, meaning we would have more of the exact copies, not less.
No, the correct way to prevent everyone from having the same perfect character is to not have a perfect character template in the first place. Players should have to choose what their advantages and disadvantages are. To cover up a disadvantage in one area a player should have to lose an advantage in another. That way the configuration of your character will be more influenced by your play style than by some secret template of an uber character.

Game mechanics should never be hidden. All that does is make every player more alike because few players want to gimp their character by experimenting. Also, the uber template WILL eventually be found and everyone will then use it.

Finally, without knowing the game mechanics, players have no way to effectively plan their character advancement or combat strategies. This leads to fewer choices, causing template characters and repetitive gameplay. For example if no one knows the effects of the Agg/Def slider, fewer players are likely to adjust it in battle. For these players a part of the game is effectively non-existent. As more gameplay features fall into this black hole of ignorance, I hope you can see how this can lead to the feeling by some that all the game is, is hitting auto attack and eating a pizza. This is not the best way to maximize the enjoyment of the game for your player base.
