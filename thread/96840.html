I play on test now exclusivly, but I played my MA doc on live to 107, mostly soloing and did it using my fists. I love being able to solo, but by this time in my career I was feeling the crunch. Your damage will begin to drop off exponentially about this time since you won't be able to buff your MA skills other than with Boosts and Implants. Yeah.. I can still do 60% missions solo.. but it takes a long time.. because I just can't put out as much damage as fast as a regular MA can. I can drop reds when I have to, because I can keep myself alive (keep your FA skill maxed).. but it takes a LONG time.

Not only that, but the IP crunch starts to be very bad. You are, after all, a doctor.. so your healing skills have to come first, or you won't be very liked when you do get to team. Your debuffs and DoTs will start to be VERY attractive around this time, too and you'll want the IP for them.

I don't PVP, well..cause I just hate it, but then I'm a girl and it makes me cry whether I win or lose.. but, in PVM, I can say that I have never had more fun than when I took on seemingly impossible odds and won. MA docs are a blast to play, and though I was reluctantly beginning to try to decide what weapon I would reset to at about level 120 (well before I discovered the test server and fell in love with it), I would do it all over again the same way if I had to.
