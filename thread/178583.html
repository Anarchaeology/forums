Shadowlands Newbie Mission Guide (spoilers)
I decided to write this because the newbie missions in the Shadowlands can be extremely confusing. This post contains spoilers, so don't read it if you want to figure it out by yourself. Unfortunately I am not patient or mathematically gifted enough to have exact coordinates and so forth, but I hope this will help some of the frustrated souls out there.


You'll find a crude Neanderthal-like scetching of the newbie area, as well as a guide to the missions and some hunting tips for when you're done with the missions. I hope you find this useful, I compiled it all based on my experiences in the Newbie world and my fellow newbie's frustrations :P

Now that your eyes have stopped bleeding from looking at my map....

The O-------------- represents the tower and energy bridge you first start on. That is where Drake stands.
Drakes Tests:
-Test of Strength: A simple killing task. First make sure that your bracelet of Jobe is on. The first two beasts can be found roaming the area to the right of the energy bridge, marked here with an SL. They look like a giant spider and giant snake. Kill them in the right order, simple enough. Now, make your way down the hill to under the energy bridge. There's a stream under there and you'll see wierd camel things, beet gras, roaming there. They're harder than the snake and spider, so be careful or go up a level. Let Drake record the data on the bracer and he gives you a bracer that adds to strength. Hurrah.

-Test of Agility and Stamina: Get ready to run the Marathon. You have a device you must use on 3 creatures. To use it, take it from your inventory and right click over the creature. The good thing is you can use it from far away, so just use it and make sure you get experience from the action, then move to the next creature. The bad thing is you have about 2 minutes to do this! So, efficiency is in order:
1. Upon receiving the device, look immediately towards the hill (above you on the map). I don't mean go there, I mean just look at it. Where I've marked the hill with ! you should see a big cat thing just sitting there. That's your first target, use the device, it'll work from up here.
2. Once you've registered the cat, run across the bridge and down towards the stream. Don't go into that area, just run past it towards where I've marked H on the map (it's to the left of the tower). Here you'll find glowing lizard things, these are chimeras, the next monster you need. Use the device.
3. Now, run back under the bridge to the stream area. There are humanoid monster things here, some kind of Dryad. This is the last thing you need. Be careful, sometimes there's a mega-Dryad around here, it attacks on sight. Just keep your distance and record a normal dryad. Then, get up the hill and across the bridge.
4. IMPORTANT!!!: You are pressed for time, with your 2 minutes almost up. Talk to Drake. When he asks for something from you, GIVE HIM THE DEVICE YOU USED, NOT YOUR BRACELET. I did this once and screamed as the timer hit 0. You should get a piece of armor. Most get a chest armor piece, my shade got a tatoo

-Test of Sense: Oh this one's so very easy compared to the last. Simply run across the bridge to where you first killed the snake and spider (marked SL-stands for shadow leet). Just use your search power and you should detect a shadow leet. Once you get experience, head back to Drake, let him record from the bracelet and you get a nano program with a map of Nascence. It's easy to do and very useful, just remember to use the program at some point and you should have the map for good.

-Test of Psychic: This one has two paths. First you must find the emissaries of the Redeemed and Unredeemed, then decide which you'll help.

The Redeemed Ranger is in a forested area. Go across the bridge and follow the slope down like you were going to the stream (moving down on the map). Watch the wall for a crack that leads into the forest. You'll know you're close when you see ethereal birds everywhere. The Ranger is marked R. Just say hi to him to register the mission if you want to work for the Crystal Guardian.

The Crystal Guardian can be found by crossing the bridge and moving up on the map. Follow the wall as you go, and keep your eyes open for a tall crack in the wall that will let you pass. It's a little tricky to find, so keep your eyes open. It's along that wall to the right, so if you go far enough to be bumping into the wall to the top you've gone too far. When you go through you'll see a bunch of flaming birds. Follow the passage down and you'll find the Crystal Guardian. He's marked as UR on my map. Just say hi to him if you don't want to work for him.

Part 2:
Now you pick which one you'll work for. If you like the Redeemed go with the Ranger. If you like the Unredeemed work for the Crystal Guardian.

The Ranger's task: The ranger gives you an item with the scent of a mother silvertail (those ethearial unicorns outside the entrance to the forest area). Go outside the forest area and follow the bottom wall along to the left of the map. You'll pass through a large herd of Silvertails, but keep moving, your task isn't here. You should see a large cat-creature perched on a rock, and a little past him on the other side of the rock (marked YS on the map) you'll see a small silvertail all by itself. This is the young silvertail. Get close, target it, then open your inventory and right click the object the ranger gave you. You should get a bit of experience. Go back to the Ranger and talk to him, he shouldn't say anything but you should get another bit of experience. Then return to Drake with your bracelet to finish the mission.

The Crystal Guardian's task: Unfortunately I'm a Redeemed kind of guy so I didn't do this one. All I know is he wants you to kill a particular silvertail. Anyone with more info please post so I can add it here.

NOTE: Whichever task you do, when you finish the task with Drake, you'll get a small faction adjustment, just so you know.

The reward should be an item aligned to the faction you chose. I got a bonfire kit which makes a little campfire. I don't know if it does anything else. The Unredeemed get...well I don't know, someone tell me.

-Test of Intelligence: If you like to look for things and explore, don't read this one. Drake gives you supplies to give to a scientist. The scientist is under that little hill where you first used the device on the cat-thingy (marked !). Go to the top of the hill (top on the map, not top as in peak of the hill). There's a little cave area, you'll probably see a scientist assistant outside in a Jobe uniform, but continue into the cave (and past a big demon-imp guy, he doesn't attack you). The female scientist is in there, give her the supplies and return to Drake, do the usual bracer thing. You should get a ring that resists nano effects. I might have this reward mixed up with the test of Sense.

-The Brink: This final test can only be gained after you finish all the others. Drake sends you to the brink to get clues. The big B on the map, and everything to the left of the line I've drawn through it, is the brink. What you need to kill is the Heckler of Evolution. It looks like a big rock. I've marked it's approximate location with HE. More specifically, there's a little batch of Hecklers of Nature towards the bottom of the brink, the Heckler of Evolution appears a short sprint above them.

Now for the painful part. Apparently the Heckler of Evolution is on a 30 minute timer. Bad. Everyone in the entire zone will eventually be looking for it. Very Bad. Tons of high levels are probably also camping it. Oh that's just Bad. Hopefully the spawn time will be buffed up. For now, you could try going after it at 4 AM, that's what I did. He drops a wierd metal contraption with strange writting. Take this back to Drake. He'll give you a neat backpack that has its own light source.

This is your last mission from Drake. He'll direct you to two of his associates (one for each faction) in Nascence itself. Someone more talented than I will probably do some kind of guide for Nascence, so until then go out and explore.

-Fun things to do in the training area:

-You can get several weapons in the training area. Snakes drop poison vials, Beet Gras drop toe bones. Combine these to make a bone handle. Combine this handle with the horn of a silvertail, or an item from a snake (I can't remember the name) to make a dagger or a 1-h sword, respectively. They're pretty useful, and my Shade still uses the daggers he got here at lvl 15 effectively. They're pretty powerful.
-I've heard that an enemy drops a metal bone of some kind you can make a gun from. I'll update this when I know more.
-Hecklers of Nature drop metal pipes which are 2-H blunt weapons. Ready made bashing sticks.

-There are also implants available. In the Shadowlands, implants are instead symbiotes. I've found the highest proportion of these from Hecklers, though a few unique monsters also have them. There's quite a few to find, though most only work for a few classes.

-Those flaming birds on the way to the Crystal Guardian sometimes drop a big Keeper-only sword. Just be careful, if you kill even one your Unredeemed faction will plumet.

-You can probably hunt hecklers for a very long time. I did so until level 13. They give good experience. There's a large group of them at the very top of the brink area on my map that spawn pretty fast, and a smaller group to the bottom, below the Heckler of Evolution's spawn area. They drop pretty nice things, including the occasional ingot, which sells for a fortune (silver for over 600, all the way to platinum for several THOUSAND, and possibly more beyond that).

-There are three unique monsters I've found so far. The first is the Leader of the Pack, a big cat-creature perched right outside the Scientist's cave. I don't remember what it drops. The second is the Mega-Dryad thing I mentioned earlier, somewhere near the stream. It drops some implants and valuables. The third is a large golem beastie, in a little cave area near the Heckler of Evolution. It's big and scary, and makes a tremor when it falls dead (no really!). It tends to drop implants and other valuables. All 3 are very aggressive to players.

-There are large beast creatures (can't remember their names) wandering the brink area. The rarer, more powerful ones (possibly uniqe) drop nice things like implants and valuables, but are aggressive. The others are not.

-Monsters in the brink area, specifically the beast creatures I just mentioned, as well as all Hecklers, and the golem tremor monster, give positive faction to Guardians of the Shadow (Ergo's faction). It's a nice little bonus, but it goes down gradually the more powerful you get compared to your targets. The common beast creature things give about 1-3 points, the hecklers give around 6, and the tremor guy gives around 30, at least around the levels you can start killing them. You'll stop getting faction gains a few levels before they turn grey to you, but they're great sources of experience and cash (especially hecklers).


This concludes my guide. I hope it assists some of my fellow newbies. Long live the shades, long live the Redeemed!

Nice and helpful. A few quick points:
  • Sometimes, the Psychic quest can not be done because high levels (for whatever reason) feel the need to kill off one or both the faction NPCs. *sob*
  • Combine the bone handle with the Spider Shank (spider mobs drop 'em) to get the 1HE Spider Machete (ideal dual wield for Keepers early on).
  • Primitive whatchamacallums drop two different stones, a Wailing Stone and a Singing Stone. The singing stone is used to make the Chimera pistol.
  • Petrified Bone is dropped by Heckler of Nature mobs. Nice 2HB for enforcers.
  • The Young Silvertail for the Redeemed is almost always killed. He's on a 15m timer (I think), so just camp his respawn to complete the quest before some silly killer n00b gets him again (he's level 2, so not exactly a high level unique).
  • The Unredeemed want you to kill the Silvertail Mare. She is also on a 15m timer (have to recheck) and located right outside the cave entrance to the Unredeemed NPC!. She's also very easy as she is also only level 2.
  • Check Sundance's 'shop' for some good n00b armor. It's cheap and covers you until you can mission some better stuff. Sundance is located next to the teleporter that brings you up to the Jobe Real Estate building.
  • Implants can be salvaged from the 'half-digested' items that various animal/beast mobs drop. To salvage, just right click the item. There is also a chance the implant will break while you're grabbing it, so it's not always a free ride.
  • Finally, a good way to screw yourself up is to use the bracer to teleport back to Drake at the end of a mission (level 5+). Do not do this, as it seems to break the mission and you'll need to redo it then to get the final mission.

Few more fun things to do while in the backyard/garden:
  • Kill "The Dryad Watcher" (he drops some good level 5 to 8 stuff).
  • Find the Shadowleet of Powah (hard sucker to find, I spotted him once and regretted not taking a screenie). He wanders around the swamp area... but may wander further.
  • Kill Tempuratus (orange birds) for the Level 8 Keeper sword (and parts of the Mariner book). Killing them lowers your UR standings and raises your Redeemed standings.
  • Kill Hai-Tempuratus (white birds) for UR standing (lose R standings). I think they also drop parts of the Mariner book.
  • Kill "The Devastator" for some nice symbiants
  • Combine the bone off a Shimmering Chimera with the Energy Accumulator from the Pond Dryads to get a Basic Firearm. From there, you can add a stone (Sining, Howling, Weeping, and ???) to make either a Chimera Pistol, Chimera Shotgun, Chimera SMG, or Chimera Rifle. Too bad the stones are on Primitive Mortiigs.
  • Finally, kill enough Mortiigs and Hecklers to raise your Guardian of the Shadow faction to a pretty decent standing, as this is as easy as Brink monsters will get for you and raising the standing helps with Ergo.

Here's another bit of goodness for everyone (big spoiler):
The 'birds' (Tempturas and Hei-Tempturas) located by either faction camp not only drop some 'rare' lowbie weapons, but they also drop (very often) chapters of "The Mariner's Father". Tempturas drop chapters 1, 2, and 4 while the Hei-Tempturas drop 2, 3, 4 (so obviously you've got to kill a little of both... which is good, helps even out your faction ratings). When you get all 4 chapters, combine 1 with 2, 3 with 1/2, and 4 with 1/2/3 to get the book of "The Mariner's Father". Take the book to the Independent Professional's Society (Jobe Harbor, real hard to miss it) and talk to the scientist at the front desk. Tell him you have something for him and hand him the book, he'll give you a nice little NCU tweak that will last you until about level 12+ (by then you should probably have a belt and a +3 NCU unit).

That just about covers it for the training garden.... remember, if you want some decent starter symbiants, try killing the Dryad Watcher (located in the swamp against the wall where the energy bridge ends) and The Devstator (located at the Brink near the Heckler of Evolution... he's hiding around a corner). Both drop various symbiants for every class (and are equipable when you can get out of the garden).
