This is how "I" as a soldier view my profession.
This is how I as a level 133 soldier view my profession.

When I consider how balanced I am as a soldier I always end up comparing myself to Enforcers. Soldiers and Enforces are supposed to be “Different but Equal” combat wise and this is patently untrue.

“If you prefer to take the fight to your enemies, use an arsenal of weapons, and like the strong, lethal type, the Soldier, Enforcer or Martial Artist would be a great choice.”
“

An enforcer at my level has a better init buff, Focused Anger +72 init VS my Attack Booster +43 init.

He’s got Mongo Bash which gives +1079 hp’s, and can be cast repeatedly so as to act like a heal during the course of a fight. VS the heal that I might have had if I sacrificed and skimped elsewhere to get it “One More Hit Healing”, which heals for 200-300 and inflicts a 10 pt DoT. I actually don’t have this heal, and very few other soldiers do either, simply cause our bio met line’s of nano’s suck.

An enforcer has Essence of Titan (or better) at my lvl giving +590 health and +20 str/stam, VS my Boot Camp Toughness a +58 health buff. By expending a whole lot more ip then the enforcer did to get Titan, I might be able to get Titan Physique, our best health buff, which gives +416 health. A whopping 174 health short of the enforcer buff that’s 20 qualities below it. But seeing as how no soldier below lvl 150 has the ip to waste to get Titan Physique I won’t even count it. Boot camp for +58 VS Essence of Titan for +590.

An enforcer my lvl has a defensive screen, usually Gird For Punishment. Giving 525 screens of blockage. I as a soldier have nothing to compare to this nano.

I’ve no clue what challenger an enf has at my lvl, but lets give them a nice easy Mighty Challenger To Titan. This short term buff is usually used to buff weapon skills while equipping stuff, but is also used during combat when the enforcer wants to win fast. It gives +88 to their skills, +32 to damage, and +25 to Offense. And it lasts 56 seconds. It locks their nano skills for 2 minutes so isn’t used for actual combat very much. I have nothing to compare to this.

An enforcer my lvl most likely has Brutal Thug, a +87 buff to 1hb/2hb, VS my two mastery’s, +60 Assault Rifle, and +70 Long Range Energy.

An enforcer my level has the damage shield Poison Stingers most likely, which deals 15 points of damage each time an enforcer is hit, and gives +161 hp’s. VS my reflect shield Reactive Deflection Shield (Extended), which blocks 19% of damage and reflects 21 points of damage per hit I take. My shield I think coming off better in the exchange, however it loses it usefulness fast cause it’s cancelled when I cast mirror shield.

I have Total Mirror Shield MK V which blocks 75% of incoming damage (though not damage shield damage) and reflects 66 pts of damage in return. This shield cancels out my reflect shield line rendering it useless, and lasts for 41 seconds. Which would be fine if I could actually kill the enforcer in 41 seconds. It then locks my nano skills for 2 minutes leaving me unable to use any other nano.

I also have an evade buff Precognition, which gives +55 to evades, something enforcers do not have.

And I also have an AC buff, Lesser Absorption Shield, giving +112 to all ac’s. Something enforcers don’t have (tho chances are they still have better ac’s then I ).

The enforcer sacrifices some to raise these nano skills because they have a plethora of useful nano’s to use. Every time they raise any nano skill they gain access to bigger and better buffs in multiple fields. Also by going 2hb they free up a ton of ip’s by only having to raise 2hb, brawl, and dimach by a small small amount. I as a soldier however have to max Assault Rifle, Long Range energy, and Burst. Fling I don’t have to max, but still have to raise a lot.

Chances are they sacrificed a little and their evades are a little worse then mine.

Their initiative’s are just as good as mine, rendering their beam close to 1/1, while my nova flow has been capped at 1/1 for quite some time.

Their attack rating is easily 860 or so, VS my attack rating of 798. Beams and hammers are solely dependent on 2hb for attack while my only choice for pvp, the nova flow, is 66% Assault Rifle/ 33% LR Energy. (I might go gamma beamer but it’s 66% LR energy, 33% Assault Rifle, and it’s too short-range for my taste).

They have better AC’s then I due to being able to self buff their str/stam with essences.

And they have ungodly amounts of hp’s compared to my measly 4k.


All this adds up to very one sided fights where I last for the 41 seconds my mirror shield stays up, only getting the enforcer to ¾ to ½ health and falling to ¾ hp’s myself while its up. Then once it goes down I die real quick, and the fight ends with the enforcer still at half health or better. This is the reason I have maintained for the longest time that soldiers are underpowered.

I’ve seen high lvl soldier Vs enforcer fights, and the soldiers win. At high lvl’s a soldier can switch out between his nova and a flashpoint for a hefty alpha strike, and his mirror shield lasts for 1:20. An enforcer can’t last against this. However, for all my time so far from lvl 1, aaaaaallllll the way to lvl 133 enforcers have been slaughtering me with embarrassing ease. I don’t want to play a gimpy Prof. simply because at the top end soldiers with mirror X are really powerful. I expect parity with enforcers.

Well, I know I’m going to get a ton of people jumping on yelling about how soldiers RoXOr, and I should shutup because I’m obviously a nube who SuXOr’s. Well kiss my ass, I want combat parity with enforcers, I don’t care that after all the nerfs nt’s have gone through soldiers can kill them fairly easily (though I’ve dueled an nt who did real good and beat me once while I beat her once, so I don’t really agree with that). I don’t care that fixers are weakened versions of soldiers and I can beat them fairly easily. I don’t care that Agents have gone through a number of nerfs and are now like fixers just a weakened version of the soldier. I don’t care that adventurers have been weak since time began and can be beaten by soldiers. I don't care that MA's deal weak damage and can't take me down. And I don’t care that engineers can be picked off while their bot feebly swats at the aggressor.

I want combat parity with enforcers.

I feel your pain Sredni....I really do.

I have my 102 soldier that was put into retirement a while back that I am getting ready to start up again. Your very correct in saying that pre-150 soldiers are not on par with equal lvl enforcers. Honestly though I think it pays off in the end. The fact is that enforcers are similar to Engi's in that they kind of peak out around 130ish, beyond that they dont get much more powerful while "sleeper" professions really start to come into their own at the high end game. These prof's are:

Soldiers - they just pretty much own a lot of things. They run neck and neck with Traders in dmg output (somtimes more with specials factored in), but their key ability is survival. With the various refect shields and fast/high dmg output, its hard as hell to take a soldier down in either PvP or PvM. Unfortunatly this can lead to some soldiers who believe they are playing in "god mode" and they tend to charge into room after room w/o waiting for the rest of the team to catch up....this can be bad for the whole team.

Docs - once they get into their powerful init debuffs, insta cast heals, and decent weapon skills, docs truly become a force in 150+ play. They are no longer considered nurse for the team, but an almost equal dmg dealer whose primary function is still to watch over the team. In PvP....well we all know they can get nasty.

Traders - they are good throughout, but they really dont come into their true power until they can insta cast their divest/plunder line and keep large chain healing going. Also they finally get out of the OEing problem and actually start letting their attack rating do the work for them, which is much more important. Before 120, traders are really just heavy twinks that can die easy, but after that they really hit thier stride and begin to become team leaders that can fulfill multiple roles in PvM. PvP...well we all know the gripes here.

NT's - also begin to become powerful as they start insta casting some of the higher end nukes, and they may have a nullity spell for those "oh sh*t" times. By this time alot of them have truly mastered the casting art and like the docs have finally got IP's to begin dumping into a weapon skill. This greatly enhances their dmg output to a point where they can steal aggro from the traders and soldiers (note: enforcers have a tough time holding aggro in the 150+ rng). PvP....well a well played NT can do well, others still die easy

The other classes stay pretty much on the middle ground throughout the game like advens, fixers, crats, MA's (though the MA's and Fixers do get some nice boosts later as a fixer gets his suppressor and GA, and the MA gets UVC plus the massive boost to dmg they just got....these classes are nothing to be laughed at even at 150+ and they are very welcome additions to a group)

Some professions tend to peak early and then just dwindle out past 130. These would be:

Engis, and MP's - both can cast their highest pet and have little to look forward to past 130.... really sad

Agents - overly nerfed months ago, if one can hold out until they get to around 160, things pick up for the agent as they get Take the Shot and can cast the crats baton line of nano's so that they make a good addition to a team since there are still not alot of high lvl crats around....the 10k AS in mission is a nice addition too. Plus the fact that most of them can fill in if an MA is not int he group. I have two good agent friends in my guild that are both well built and welcome on hunting teams, but I also think that alot of the reason I like these two is their personality

well thats enough for now....just wanted to share my thoughts. Nothing really needs to be updated on soldiers. If you choose a soldier, expect to be outclasses for a long time but then in return you can basically dominate (or at least be a co-dominator) the high end game. It just a tradeoff that someone has to choose when they make a new char.
