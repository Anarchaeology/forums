Supa Leet
Armor in this game is broken into five segments, really...

1) Low-level armor -- low-level versions or crap armor lines that work fine early on
2) Mid-level armor -- good all-around armor with decent ACs, no bonuses, and not enough AC to be "top-tier" (Flowers Tech is a good example, Graft is another)
3) Top-tier armor -- best all-around ACs with no special buffs: OP Elite, Biomech, Obtru, and Nano Armor
4) Special armor -- Special armor with good AC and buffs, Mk II, Carbonum, Albrecht..
5) Uber armor -- Super-rare items with good to mediocre AC and uber buffs... Expensive Vest, Living Dragon Armor Body, etc.

Now...

#1 is great until level 75ish...this is why there is a lot more variety in lowbie armor. =)

Not only does it not really matter too much what armor you wear early on, there are distinct trade-offs...all lowbie armors have 2 or 3 weak spots. People were different armor, or mix armors to compensate. Also, it's easy to get MOBs to min-damage early in the game as well...so variety is the ****e of life.

#2 is used all the way until the wee levels by some folks... Clanners who use Str/Stam have no OP Elite option, so often wear Graft. Flowers Tech is really popular, as is all the Hanshi/Senpai gear. (Cloak isn't considered mid-tier, though...since there aren't many cloaks.)

Still a lot of varience at this stage...a large array of things to choose from.

#3 is where things start to water down. All Opis wear Obtru, all Atrox (Omni, at least) wear OP Elite, Nanomages wear Nano Armor, and Docs wear Biomech. Not a steadfast rule, but insanely common. Heck, I'm guily of this as well. (Obtru-wearing Opi at your service!)

The top-tier armors are great until the top of the line game-play and PvP. Only 1 weak point, usually Chem or Rad, highest aggregate ACs of any armor, and pretty easy to equip if you're of the matching breed. Generally speaking, they look cool too.

#4 This is where boredom really sets in...when you're tired of #3 armor, want the HP buffs, Nano buffs, NCU buffs...you get Carbonum or Mk II armor...throw on the Albrecht and go to town. Not only is you AC the same or better, but you've gained a ton of HP, Nano, and/or NCU in the process.

The biggest problem is this waters everyone down to *two* sets of body armor and *one* piece of back armor. Boooooooring. But what choice do people have? I could stay using top-tier armor, or I could get a ton of bonuses with the other armor. HP and Nano (if you're a healing class) are what matter in this game--so you're shooting yourself in the foot if yo udon't start using Mk II eventually. Once the nano penalty dies on the Albrecht, it's the best back armor simply because of the bonuses--more nano AND reduced cost. (About the opposite of all other tank armor before it.)

#5 Makes the game a bit more interesting...but the problem is that it's too rare. Hardly anyone wears the stuff because nobody can get it.

So...my brief synopsis...

Level 1-75: Variety is the ****e of life
Level 75-120: People start wearing full sets of armor
Level 120-150: People start wearing only 4 sets of armor and Albrect/Hanshi back items
Level 150-199: Everyone looks the same
Level 199-200: Everyone looks the same except the one piece of their body they've gotten an uber item for

Weapons are the same way, really... In the first 30-50 levels, it doesn't matter what weapon you use. People use all sorts of wacky things! Between 50 and 170, though...you have to use the "good" weapons, otherwise you're screwed. After 170, people start using DIFFERENT weapons (due to lots o' spare IP), but generally just rotate through the "good" weapons from other profs.

It's really too bad that there isn't more choice regarding items in the high-end game. There are about 30,000 non-implant items in the database, and almost all of them stink. =(

-Jayde
