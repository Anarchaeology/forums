giving grid capability to those that don't already have it... Umm. NO!!

I admit I haven't read all posts in this thread, but after reading the 2 first pages I figure I post my take on this.

Some claim that giving a grid type nano to all professions will not damage those professions that have it? Maybe true.

Some are happy if it is a slow one as long as they get it. And the reason they want it? Because they do missions far form a Whompa/grid terminal and are in danger of being attacked and lose their XP.

Well... as I see it thats a risk you take when you pick a mission.
At low levels you can pull missions that are in or around the location you pick the missions from. As you gain levels more areas open up to you. Exactly how this works, if it is level based only or if the sliders affect this as well I dont know.

But the fact is. you as the mission picker are free to make the choice if you want to do the mission or not.

There are some very nice worldmaps you can upload that will tell you where the mission location is in relation to the nearest gridterminal/whompa. Also what faction that whompa have (IE if it is in a neut/ot/clan area)

So asking for a grid nano because you dont want to make the trek back from the mission is not a valid reason. In fact... there are no valid reason.

It would be like having a HP buff nano for those races (not proffessions) but races that have a frail body, simply because it is more convenient for them so they dont have to use first aids or their profession specific heal programs (if they have any) as much.

From the moment that FC sat down and started to discuss AO and the various professions they had a clear view of how they wanted each profession to be. We and FC knows that it didnt happen that way for various reason.

But FC are doing a few things to keep the professions a bit varied. By providing a way to teleport for all they make that distinction of the various professions less.

I got a enf at level 50. I wish he had a heal nano, but he doesn't. He is stuck to use First aid in fights and then sit and heal between fights. But what he does have is a nice HP nano that makes him able to take more hits.

I dont wish for other professions to have that nano.

There are some cross over profession nanos that bothers me. I will not list them here, or even make this into a discussion about them. That is not why I replied.

Those that play a profession that dont have a nano they wish they had, pick a profession that have said nano. If you want more nanos.... play agent. They are the ones with nice access to many nanos.

I know that agents get a penalty for using other profession nanos, but it is still a nice buffer to have access to them

So in conclusion... NO more professions specific nanos to those professions that shouldn't have them.

Read the statement on your professions. Even if it is not 100% accurate, there should still be enough left of that to give you a clear idea of what the limits of that profession is when you roll it.
