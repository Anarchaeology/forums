oh man, I LOOOOOOOVE being 200!

Three days after I hit 200, I sent a tell to a guildmate and friend of mine, named Kinkstaah who had been level 200 since I was....like level 1.

I was sitting in Tir doing absolutely nothing and asked him "Now that I'm level 200, what do I do now?"

His reply was "Play the game."

Took me a few weeks to realize what he meant. In the meantime I levelled another alt to his mid 130s when it struck me that there's so much to do if you're willing to get creative.

Now that refined implants and clusters are blitzable again, I spent over a week blitzing ql 220 missions. the "new" missions. With Hellhounds. I died several times a day, but got so good at it, it barely takes me anymore time than it did before the missions got revamped.

I also think of my character alot and things I can do for improvement. Can I put creation weapons to good use? What implant layouts should I used for different situations and what will the end result be? Last night I had an epiphany on implant and weapon layouts that I'll be trying tonight.

And then there's uniques raids, pvp, mass pvp, chatting, helping folks out...

....and missions. Yes, I go about 6 ql200 solo missions a day now that additional loot has been put in missions. I find jobeclusters, ncu, recompilers, curious items I've never seen before, etc. Many of these items lead to looking up tradeskills friends to have them assemble these items, which leads to good conversations, etc.

I do everything the long way. Sure, I have the cash to buy many of these items myself, but then I lose out on content. I also help guildmates with their raids. When someone needs an item from a unique, I'm usually the first one there.

So I get pretty busy. Alot of times I get tells that I'm unable to answer as a result, or I'm not able to hold up a long conversation, but if I could, I would

So remember to take time and smell the roses. Getting to 200 shouldn't feel like a race. If you rush through your char's advancement, you're going to get bored fast. Look at all the lvl 200s that say there's nothing to look forward to at 200. They never took the time out to really experience AO
