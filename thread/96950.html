Press Release - MoK & Ren Merge
We are very proud to anounce that after many talks between myself and Glopp (Claire) of Ren, we decided that MoK and Ren should not share the boundries of seperate guilds.

MoK is happy to join forces with Ren for the better of Omni-Tek and the entire Omni population. I am very proud to now include Glopp and Yrdaddy in the finest staff of Generals I have ever seen. Their addition will further MoK's abillity to keep me making the correct decisions.

There will be no drastic changes in the way we conduct business on Rubi-Ka. We hope that this union will make us a better asset to the company and to our fellow employees.
