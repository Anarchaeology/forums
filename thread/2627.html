Just what i'd like to see...
No whining, just a simple list from my point of view that i'd like to see coming up.


Guilds - finish them, get the banks to work and all the other remaining commands for them


Guild Houses - Put them in.


Apartments - K, you've fixed the bug that didnt make items appear immediately, well done. Now please remove the "no more than 10 items" fix that you put in (because i doubt its a bug) and add furniture you can SIT on and use. Also you said months ago now that you'd be creating apartments you can choose and buy, bigger ones that look different, and add the apartment blocks in cities into use. DO this, please..
And also add some apartment blocks to every town/city along with insurance so people can LIVE in different cities, like Gal Castle or small towns. Encourage communities instead of cities which are just overgrown shopping malls.


Euro timed events - The only European people that ive heard of that witnessed GM events are those that stay up till 11pm-1am in the morning. I personally play till around that time anyway, so i can catch those events, but i feel sorry for the people who are before that time, give them an event FC....


Spydr's "Resync" command /resync Is a very good idea. To just type it in during lag so the computer and server agree and it just warps you where you should be. It would make the player a much more happier person.


Car de-quipping. My Kodiak *still* de-equips between zones, despite you clearly marked this as being fixed in 13.1
Can you please fix this? You upped the time it takes to equip a car to stop people using it as an escape in Pvp, but now that means that it takes forever to keep re-equipping it inbetween zones....


Add new cars (a personal request, something id like to see) everything is either a kodiak/mid or Yal, yet ive seen sprites of Omni-cars (the white-black ones) the funny crow looking ones you can see in Trade or Athen and other types. Also, make People carriers carry PEOPLE. Allow a car to be 1 or 2 seated so you can take a friend or use a people carrier to carry a team.


Missions - link them, make quests or something, make them interlink into storylines. SOMETHING. i just find it gettin more tedious to just run thru missions killing mobs for an item or person. Missions should at the moment just be called "Indoor Hunting". Interlink missions or do something that requires you to get an item from one mission and use it in another mission in a different place. Just think up some ways to make them more interesting please


Professions - Give jobs to people so the game doesnt solely concentrate on killing. Professions are just disguises for different types of fighter.
The great thing about SWG (star wars galaxies) is that you wont have to fight to progress. Try that in AO. Give xp for things relating to peoples jobs. You cant level a lot just by making nano's, jewelry or medkits.... Give us jobs that relate and give good xp or cash related to our professions.
Killing and fighting is currently the only way to gain decent xp in the game. Fight for money, fight for xp. Make it different, give us some variety plz.

Thankyou for listening (if an FC employee actually reads this)
