Root tedium
When you are in a mission and are rooted, I think the root should end when to player is no longer under attack. I don't think it has to end if the caster dies if you are still in conflict with other mobs, however, when everyone is dead and you are just standing alone in the room picking your nose, the root should end.

Tonight, I have had to endure at least 5 minute long roots. Not very fun. At level 87, I wonder if the roots will become proportionally longer, so I'll be standing there for 5 to 10 minutes.

This is an annoyance at worst, I am just putting in my 2 cents. Bump if you agree.
