"Top Gun" Tournament
Was thinking about setting up an organized tournament for the best of each profession then carrying over to a "Top Gun" tournament. What i came up with so far would be this:

-Hold a tournament for profession vs people of same profession (would be 1v1 self-buffed).

-Winners of however many professions there are would advance to a round robin.

-Every prof would fight every other prof and the best record would be the best overall/ Champion. If there are ties we will deal with that in tiebreaker matches.

This is just an idea i've had to ****e up the game. Comments and improvements are welcome as feedback so i can improve it.

Edit: Changed format to make the round robin conclude with the winner instead of 1-death elim format to give everyone a chance at everyone else and really be best overall.

Also, Nenous is helping me out with this and putting up a page, check out www.tekcrab.com
