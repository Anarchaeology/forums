Realistic Nano Suggestion
Now we know there's a Beacon Warp Nano that warps a person to you, however there is no nano that warps YOU to THEM...

I think there should be a few Variations of this,

Engi's of course should get these nanos Primarily

Maybe a Self only Warp-to-Player Nano (In your Team)

Also a Team Version that would warp you and any teammates In your LOS to the person selected (In your Team)


Fixers could possibly get a Reckless Digi and IE version that would Drain all of your Health and Nano Bar down to 1 point and send You or Your Visible Teammates to another selected teammate quickly.


NT's should also get Variations like the above to go with their Warp Time and Space Line.
