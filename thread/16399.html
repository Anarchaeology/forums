Or how about this?

Have random mobs drop treasure maps or notes corresponding to their level amongst the loot ...
Then when the player reads the note/book/map etc it will hint at the location of some treasure or item.

An example could be:
Bob the level 5 player is sittin outside Tir West Gate and decides to kill a leet that wanders past. He kills it and is surprised to find a small note in the loot. He Right-Clicks it and gets a message that he needs 50 Map Navig to use it. He realises he will only need to level a couple of times to be able to use the note so he saves it for later. Two levels later he rummages around in his pack and finds the note again. This time he decodes it! It hints at a location somewhere in Aegean where <nice rare item> can be found. Altho it doesnt tell the EXACT location. But gives a few different co-ords in Aegean where he could look. So off he toddles to have a look round Aegean for this Cave/Building etc. Once inside he has to battle a few mobs of roughly his own level and eventually finds the treasure in one of the rooms!

Now to me that would add something really special to this game. Obviously have the notes dropped at random by ANY mob and have the level correspond to the mob that dropped it. IE a note off a leet would be for low level characters and so would the loot from the mission. Whereas a note dropped by a Vulture would
correspond to that level. Aswell the loot would have to be of similar level to the player doing the quest to keep things even.
This would make the game more interesting for ANY level of player. Instead of having to be level 100 and sit around camping unique monsters for hours.

The notes/maps themselves should be rare items so when you get lucky enough to kill a mob that drops one its a really special find. The actual mission locations and caves scattered around the world could be used for the quests.
Also make it so only the player who picked up the note can use it ... Altho he can get a team together to help him.


Another way of doing it would be to just have this available for Adventurers only. I was reading on the Adv forum about ways they're gonna be improving them in the near future and maybe this could make the Adv more wanted? Maybe an Adventurer is needed for the map to be read. So a player could hire the Adv as
a guide so he can do the quest. We are Adventurers after all! We're supposed to have adventures!
