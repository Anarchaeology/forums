I tested in a 106 mission last night with the following results:
  • Started at 75 B&E = chests didn't open after 20 tries.
  • Added B&E Expertise buff = chests didn't open after 20 tries.
  • Pushed total (with buff) to 125 B&E = chests didn't open after 20 tries.
  • Pushed total (with buff) to 150 B&E = chests didn't open after 20 tries.
  • Pushed total (with buff) to 175 B&E = chests didn't open after 20 tries.
  • Pushed total (with buff) to 200 B&E = chests opened within 20 tries.

The loot in the locked chests ranged from 80ish to 125ish, so, although it took most of a levels IP, I guess it was worth it (since I'm a 92, that higher level armor is a good find).

I guess we can figure, from this and some other of the posts, that B&E should be roughly double the mission level.
