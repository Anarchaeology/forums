Just a few general ideas
I havent played adventurer(only profession i havent).

But just generally speaking i can tell you this.


More important than what pistol skills to raise, is what pistols to use. Find that out, and then you can figure out what path to take from there. So see what people have to say about that.

As for your nano skills, i'm not sure what all adv. should max, but i can at least tell you this. Your heal is by far your most important skills to be raising. So make sure you keep your heals up, and let the rest come along.

For your speed category. I'd raise run speed to a comfortable level. Keep your ranged init up as much as you can too, since this will obviously make you shoot faster. Dont worry about raising nano init because your heals are quick enough as they are, and raising it would just be a waste of IP.(init does not affect recharge, only cast/attack time.)

Your evades are important too, because they effect you getting hit, and make it harder for something to get critical hits on you. The most important 2 evades are dodge ranged and evade close. Duck explosion is not used by many weapons so many people let that slide, and it really doesn't matter much until you get well past lvl 100 where you have the IP to raise it.

For your trade skills, only raise comp. lit. - Comp lit is important for equipping more NCU so you can get more skill buffs running on you, and also allows access to the grid.

Dont worry about vehice skills, until you have the vehicles. The only one that you should truly raise is vehicle air. Being an adventurer ground vehicles dont help you much, because you can buff your run speed nicely. Map navigation is also nice, but i wouldn't stress it too much. Check out the map upgrades, and when you have the spare cash, its best to use implants to boost your map nav. skill high enough to use the upgrades, and then take the implants back out and put in your normal ones. This can save you some IP on that.

Other than that you'll want some more specific answers, as to what weapons are good. Which main skills(stry/agi/sta) you should be raising. And planning out your nano skills.
