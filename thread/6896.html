I had mentioned something like this a while back. Here is a repost of that suggestion:

When they do finally allow travel between the two dimensions, I would like three things to happen:

  1. It should be a journey to get from one to another. I would suggest a static mission or a new zone that has two entrances, one in each dimension. It should mean something to be able to get from one dimension to another and it should take some time to do it.
  2. One of the dimensions should change drastically. I know the landscape will remain pretty much the same, but they should still do something visually like change the color of the sky.
  3. One of the zones should cater to higher-level players, with more PvP areas and tougher mobs, but would also have greater experience and loot.


People have complained that they have nothing to look forward to. Being able to enter the tougher dimension could become one of a characters goals. It could be a great part of the story too.

Imagine an invading force attacks RK-2. They totally destroy some cities with powerful bombs that kick enough dust into the air that the sky turns red. In an attempt to save the population, Omni finds a way to open a portal to another dimension of Rubi-Ka to get reinforcements and to allow the civilian and lower level population to flee.

After a month, the invading force realizes that the free flow of personnel and equipment through the portal is hampering their efforts to overtake RK-2. Believing that they will never be able to close the portal, or that they might want to use it themselves one day, they decide to sabotage it instead. They sneak a biological weapon (like in the movie Aliens) into the portal to cut the supply lines.

The players are now battling the new force on RK-2. The passageway between the zones has become a dangerous place. Finally, the Omni and Clan are trying to position themselves to be in control of RK-2 after the invading force is repelled.
