More MA Special Attacks are needed at the terminals!
The current number of Special Attacks in the terminals is terrible. Attack of the Snake, Angel of Night, and Upon a Wave of Summer are ok, but what about all the other special attacks?

If soldiers, fixers, enforcers, etc., can find a wide range of weapons at the weapons terminals, MA's should have the same variety to choose from for special attacks (relatively speaking).

What really stinks about this is that even if I find a special attack in a mission, or receive one as a reward, it's the same special attack I can find in a terminal!

Funcom, get off your lazy rearends and give the MA's the special attacks they need in the terminals! It's bad enough that the message I posted like this was deleted in the 14.7.3 temp forum instead of being moved, but to see that 2-3 patches later, you still haven't fixed the issue (which IS a simple one to implement).

MA's are slowly becoming a dead profession because Funcom isn't giving us the support for special attacks that is needed (long ago, finding a special attack for an MA was as easy as finding a gun for any other profession).
