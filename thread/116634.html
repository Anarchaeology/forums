Adventurer Issue #1

By an overwhelming margin, the main concern of Adventurers at this point is the present status of Adventurer weaponry. There are a few facets to this issue, so I have broken it up into 3 sub-issues detailing these aspects of the problem. Please pardon the length, but I think it is important to explain the issue in-depth to avoid confusion.

Current weapons do not match up with "modern" weapons and the "new damage budget"

By and large, Adventurers are still using exactly the same weapons they have since around 13.6-13.8 (Mantis Scissors and BBI Faithful respectively.) Not surprisingly, however, the damage situation has changed dramatically since that point. With weapons like the Manex, EQB, Caterwaul, Pain of Patricia, and Augmented Hellfury/spinner becoming the norm, weapons such as the BBI Faithful simply do not stack up.

Many Adventurer-specific weapons have been DOA or MIA and contribute nothing

While FC has made a point of releasing quite a bit of Adventurer-specific gear--much to the chagrin of other professions--very little of it has actually had any positive impact on our effectiveness. Weapons such as HSR Explorer, Gutting Hook, Heavy Chop, Ithaca Vulture, Black Pete, OT Army Officer Pistol, and Kaen-Archibald Kolt are generally *terrible* weapons that were never good when they were released, and are downright worthless today. Adventurers have also had the fun of being shown various weapons (MTI Grey, Ancient Adventurer Sword, Blazing Pistol) which were never put into the game at all. (I will note that various FC sources, from Cosmik to GMs, stated that the AAS and others would be available in-game via quests 'eventually'--much the same as our promised Cyclic Shields that have still never shown in-game.)

New pistols being introduced for other professions are better than Adventurer-specific pistols, therefore removing our so-called profession Advantage in the skill.

The Master Engineer Pistol, Eyemutant Orb Laser, Pain of Patricia, Kick Pistol, and Revolving Cold Laser are *all* superior to Adventurer pistols. In addition, they all have comparatively low requirements in various stats. (Either because of Pistol being easy to buff, or having very low MR.) After many complains of it limiting our usefulness, the 1100+ Multi Range requirements on Adventurer-specific pistols has *never* been removed, however most of these weapons have MR requirements that are amazingly lower. (800 for Master Engineer, 600 for Eyemutant Orb, 666 for RCL, and 800 for Kick Pistol.) Not only are other professions with secondary damage output sources becoming more proficient with Pistols than Adventurers (Nukes/DoTs + PoP > Adventurer with PoP and Trader/Engineer with PoP + Master Engineer > Adventurer with 2x PoP), but they are also more proficient at *dual-wielding*--the other Adventurer specialty.

We have been told that FC does not like updating old weapons, yet we just saw a completely useless AR (the Eradicator XCI-52, which was previously a 30-186 (350) piece of junk) upgraded just this patch. Despite that, FC can still not bring themselves to lower Adventurer pistol MR requirements down to reasonable levels. We have been told that the older weapons use the "old MR budget" and the new ones use the "new MR budget." However, flying in the face of the claim, is this set of facts: The Kick Pistol was introduced in 14.5 with a 1100 MR requirement. In 14.6, the BBI Faithful had it's initiative type fixed from Melee Init. to Ranged Init. However, after the dust settled for the 14.6 patch, the Kick Pistol's MR requirement was LOWERED to 800, while the BBI Faithful's MR requirement was RAISED from 1109 previously to 1155. Now, logically speaking, how could the BBI Faithful be "updated" in a time when the "new MR budget" was obviously in effect (as proven by the Kick Pistol's 800 req at the time) and be RAISED in requirement?

In conclusion, Adventurer weapons are our lifeblood. That simple. We cannot nuke, we cannot DoT, we have no crowd control (aside from the half-arse calms we were given), and we basically have no offensive capabilities whatsoever aside from the weapon(s) we hold in our hand(s). It simply makes no sense that non-primary combat professions are now able to use pistols with greater overall effect than Adventurers, and all the while become better dual-wielders as well. Adventurers need new and/or updated weapons that bring our ranged capabilities back above those of non-primary combat professions. (But, of course, staying lower than Soldiers by a bit.) While Pistols are most prominantly lacking, our 1HE could also use a refresh--as the original Mantis Scissors is still in *wide* use, despite being pretty old now. (Daitos would help greatly--and many Advents do use these at lower levels now--but going only to QL 125 means that high-level Adventurers are still stuck with Sol/MS combo no matter what they do. EMS was a good idea, but the high level req leaves a large gap between when QL 125 Daitos lose effectiveness, and when an EMS can be equipped--levels 120-176--and still offers little improvement in damage for the huge leap in requirements.)

Here is my reply in the Professionals forum:

quote:

Hmm.. I'm not really sure how to respond to this one..

My biggest issue is with paragraph two. My key complaint here was that Advent weapons are the ones with the insane MR requirements, while non-Advent weapons (Kick Pistol, RCL, Master Engineer, Eyemutant Eye, etc.) are the ones with the small MR req. The facts of the current game are painting an opposite picture than one described here. Right now, it is the other profs dual-wielding, and the Advents that are currently stuck single-wielding from 175-195. So...currently this is a rather sore spot for Advents, not the "advantage" described above.

Also, please consider the multiple threads in the Adventurer forum which show the pitfalls of the current Multi-Wielding system, including that of "gimped" damage during certain periods (100-125 is one of them) given the current MR budget. The impact of the "reload bug" on multi-wielding pistol (an absolutle nightmare) is also a large issue here.

At any rate, I would disagree with Gaute. The only thing the Adventurer is second-best at is healing. We have the 4th or 5th highest attack rating (6th compared to a "hot" Trader.) The 4th best AC buffs. The 5th highest HP. The 6th highest nano pool. We have perfectly average nano caps. (despite having nanos with reqs that are in line with 5/level BM) Morphs offer no spectacular benifit above and beyond your typical buff, aside from nausiating us from all the bobbing and furthermore, there is only one morph that is actually considered useful--Calia's Sabre--which also comes with a pretty harsh run speed penalty (-240) for what amounts to 1 crit, 40 AMS, and a bit of damage. The morph situation is even worse with the fact that Parrot will be useless due to lack of flight in Shadowlands. We come in dead-last in the crowd control department, with only the near-useless calms we were given. Advents aren't even the "masters of damage shields" like we were told to be.

Please keep in mind, I'm not particularly *complaining* about any of these things, but I am curious as to where/why Gaute would be under the impression that we are the "second best at a lot of things." That just simply isn't true. In fact, the "specialty" professions end up being better at more things at the same time than Advents, therefore nullifying the "well-rounded" nature of Advents. (Traders are the true jack-of-all-trades, anyway. But, it only takes one look at Enforcers or MAs, for instance, to see someone who's best or second best at a huge array of things.) Honestly, I can't think of anything we're 2nd-best at aside from Heals and Damage Shields--and we're supposed to be the best at one of those. Saying something like that is nice nice "feel-good" statement, but it really doesn't hold up in court.

The sad fact is, while it's all well and good to say that Advents will be the best at multi-wielding pistols, even if it was true (which it's not right now) it doesn't matter. Multi-wielding right now is such a small advantage overall (33% is *not* the increase in damage...anyone who tells you that is sorely mistaken. Typically is 15% at best, and -10% at worst even under ideal circumstances. And, yes, that is a negative sign before the 10%.) If you don't make Advents the best (or 2nd best behind Soliders only) at pistol while single-wielding, the whole multi-wielding promise becomes little more than a placebo.

Sorry, but I'm using a Master Engineer pistol that was spawned for me on test. It's simply uber. The mods are insane, the damage is great, and the reqs are astronomically low. 1000 pistol and 800 MR?? I mean, come on... Most of the Advent pistols are 1050+ Pistol, and 1200+ MR, and not nearly as effective.

Furthermore, in a previous Professionals issue, the feedback stated that Funcom had no intention to increase the effectivness of Multi-Wielding. So, tell me... How can that, going hand-in-hand with giving out sub-1000 MR reqed pistols to multiple non-Advent professions, ever provide Advents with any noticible advantage?

I'm sorry to ramble on for so long here... But the answer given was terribly nebulous and seemed to ignore the key questions and evidence that I put forward in my initial issue post.

If this is an expression that Funcom plans on taking proactive action to make the Advent multi-wielding situation better and more benificial, then that is good. However, I am currently seeing exactly the opposite in-game, and I would wonder how that fits in with that. Would it at all be possible to get a bit more detailed an answer, simply because as much as that may be the "intention of the designers", it is certainly not the end result in-game.


-Jayde
