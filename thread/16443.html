I found a little low level one earlier
If ya follow the road South from Athen East gate past the Rising Sun area you eventually come to a river crossing heading to the next zone.
Just before the crossing and slightly to the east is a small sandy canyon leadin to a cave. You can see it on the map easy enough cos it looks like a mission location.

Anyway ... Once inside i found a LOT of Asphalt Adders. All were grey to me at Level 31 but i ploughed through em all to see if there was anything worth finding. I'd say its probably a level 20ish dungeon.

It was interesting to find the place but i was kinda disapointed to see there was nothing at the end of it. Its ok if yer a low level just after experience or monster parts but if yer not interested in xp (like me ) then its a let down to battle through and realise at the end that its just like a mission but without anything really to show for it.

Even these little dungeons should have somethin to reward ya with at the end! It makes it much more worthwhile

A real static dungeon
Thus far I've been to one REAL static dungeon, e.g. one with its own unique layout, unique enemies, \ and I suppose, ending boss.

The name of this dungeon is 'The Foremans Office' located somewhere in the vicinity of the Longest Road west of Athens (I won't divulge toomore info on its loc. cuz I'm going to leave the exploring up to you ^_^.

The design of the dungeon is one of a vast adminstrative building for a mining corporation and I can see FC spent a lot of time in making the building look nice and unique. For example, the ceiling to this dungeon is built up high and there are nice little decorative touches here and there.

Btw. This dungeon is ideal for lvl 50-70 ish people. Bring a small team or prepared to get your butt handed down to you on a platter.
