They aren't giving up.

I've spent countless hours with the others working on the Council of Truth happenings, but they aren't giving up.

How much longer am I supposed to sit here and watch these fake impostors get away with what they are doing? Trying to steal the limelight? Grr... if only they knew the real ones. The last of us not known. Amateur tactics and unintentional fumblings in their "assaults". Do they even know why we did it before? Do they even care?

Either way, they aren't really us. They're impostors.

And the next time they come try to impersonate what we stand for, I am going to show them a little something.

It's time for the snake's venom to surface once more. The true Dust. For the angels watch my back always.
