Reasons 13.0 Missions Suck!
I was actually looking forward to the 13.0 patch based on the build notes but the reality is very bad, at least from my perspective. I play a 61 NT and had some difficulty soloing even missions but could always complete them if I was persistent. However, the changes in 13.0 seem to have made it impossible, or nearly so, for me to complete the missions now.

1. The hard cap on Nanos means that I cannot root a MOB from farther away than he can shoot. I don't want to tank; I was fine with low hit points and AC as long as I could play my NT with finesse, i.e. root and nuke, blind, etc. Yes, I can root a MOB and run away, but this leads me to problem #2.

2. Either roots are much shorter than they used to be or nukes are now capable of breaking root because I am now getting off only one slow nuke or two fast ones before root breaks and the MOB charges me. Well, I could live with that - I will simply pull the MOB to the entrance and nuke and zone, nuke and zone, etc. Except that instead of standing in the mission foyer, as MOBS were wont to do, the MOB runs back out into the open area and waits on me there. Well, that would be ok if I could move around in the foyer and tab-target the MOB and root and nuke (see above), BUT

3. I cannot seem to tab-target through closed ( Not locked, closed) doors anymore. This means I have to open the door to tab-target the MOB, at which point it rushes me and the entire fiasco begins again. The MOBS tend to wait very near the door from the foyer so that as soon as it opens they can pound me.

4. Even when the door is open it is MUCH harder to tab-target the MOBS than before 13.0.

I have been a very patient fan of AO. I bought the game on the release date and got my reward today (which I like very much since I have no Nano Cloak so far). I have offered compliments and requests for changes in about equal measure until now. However, the changes in this patch have taken the fun out of soloing missions, which is my primary game play style. I will wait and see if the problems are addressed but if they are not then I will probably not renew my subscription when it comes up for renewal.

I posted earlier about my inability to delete missions since this patch. Another player responded with a tongue in cheek comment that it was probably not a bug but was intentional in FC's part to make progressing toward 200 take longer as people would quit once they reached 200. i hope he was wrong. A lot more will quit out of frustration and boredom long before reaching 200 if changes such as the range cap, changed MOB AI, etc. keep taking the fun out of playing.

I play the game to have fun and get rid of frustrations. As of this patch, the game is not fun and is frustrating. Thus my reasons for playing no longer exist. if the Nano range is a problem in PvP then change it in PvP zones ONLY.
