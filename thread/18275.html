Tokens are given at the end of a mission, IF you fulfill certain requirements like cleaning out a certain amount of the mobs, the difficulty of the mission vs your lvl, chests picked and so on. Right click a token, and it will add to your board. When you reach different totals, like 10, 30, 60, 120 and so on, you will be given bonuses in terms of more hp, more nanopool, more xp and such. The higher token boards ( I can't remember a good link to the full details of token boards right now) require title levels to be higher as well. Title levels are what you hit at 15, 50, 100 etc. At title levels you get more IP per level to spend, and the profession sub-title you have will change. (like "nurse" for doctor profession and so on)

Hope that helps

Soleet
Token boards (available for omni and clan, not neutral) hold tokens, and are worn on um...the neck slot, I believe. If you right click on a board while you're wearing it, it tells you the # of tokens you have, and also makes the little shoulder things visible on your character for all to see.

(They're not really on your shoulder, but floating next to your head, and on top of that, they wave around with your arms, so you can get some really bizarre stuff happening with token boards sticking out of your ears and that sort of thing...but I digress.)

Benefits of a token board:
There are several levels; each level has higher benefits. I think ao-stratics had a nice page with the different levels and benefits, but in short, it'll add to your health and nano bars (more health is always nice)...higher level ones add %xp and other goodies.
