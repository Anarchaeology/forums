Thanks everyone for all your support and kindness :)) Sorry to be mushy but reading some of your posts brought tears to my eyes. I'm going to miss so many of you so much.

I know the site I posted about Internet Addiction is not the best in the world. I just used it as it shows the basic ideas behind it for those interested (they are pretty much the same on any site you visit).

I don't need a website to tell me I have a problem, and my decisions were not made based off something I quickly read somewhere. Internet addiction can be a serious problem just like alcohol or drugs and should not be taken lightly. I can tell you from my own experience that my life is pretty messed up from it right now.

Anyhow, I am now taking medication for my depressive conditions and getting help to work though my problems. These things together should head where I need to go.

Thanks everyone again for being so kind, you all show what great people are here in AO. *gives big hugs to all* :))

-Dovve
