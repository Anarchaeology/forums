New Clan : "Clan Imperialist Army" recruits!
Ladies and Gentlemen!

Allow me to introduce myself, I am Didymus, head of the Clan Imperialist Army, a clan on RK1, Atlantean.
We're about to do recruitment, but first ..


An introduction of the clan

First off .. The name!

Clan - We're clanners, I think.
Imperialist - From imperialism, political structure in an empire *and/or* policy of national territorial expansion ..
Army - Yes, we plan to reach our goals through military force.

To put it simply, we want to strengthen the clans as a whole ..
Expand clan territories, pushing the omni further south and
work for setting the base for a Clan Ruled Empire.

The last bit may cause some confusion as the omni buttkicking bit is quite clear.
Note that an empire needn't be ruled by an emperor. However, we see the need for a strong,
united clans with an effective governing system ..

All in all, we aim to be a disciplined guild and we want to organize
recruits into different teams and give training where needed.
We also want our members to be reasonably well equipped,
and so, we'll provide what help we can to our recruits.

As extremists, we want to involve ourselves in the battle for territory as much as possible ..
And we're gonna do it with style.
We also hope to be both loved and loathed by other guilds and factions.

We have some criterias for recruits.
