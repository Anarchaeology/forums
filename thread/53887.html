My take on profession specific rings/items/quests.
I've been camping Jack for a while now, he refuses to drop the part I need to complete my MA ring quest, and when he does I'm pretty guaranteed to lose the raffle to another MA or the winning team gives it to a guildie. I hate it. Camping is so bad.

So, how would I like it to be? A one man quest. Selfbuffed only. Geared toward the specific profession, and uber hard. I dont mind dying while trying, I could die 20 times and I would still go at it if there is a remote possibílity that I could make it. Something that would test MY ability to play MY profession, not as it is now by testing my camping endurance and the number of highlevel people I can drag along to the spawn.

I would like something like this: A personal dungeon, only enterable when unteamed and zoning in clears all buffs. There you buff up and go 1 on 1 with a badass mob, perhaps one of the same profession (with pets if applicable). Just barely killable at your present level. You would have to use all your tactics, all your skill and have quite a bit of luck to win, and the reward should be worthwhile (eg. not the current rings.)

Prefferably, this quest would test some other things as well, since all professions aren't 100% combat oriented.

For fixers, their dungeon could be trapped with big (fail and die)traps... Engies/Traders would perhaps need good tradeskills etc.

I'd like that.
