The trick is the NCU and Nano Reqs.

Bulk Trader:
psycho mod 456
sensory imp 418

Even after Deprive Skills (Major)/Ransack Skills we only have 297 nano skills, remember? Masteries are needed...but Bulk Trader takes up 30 NCU.

We run into that wall again before we can cast Bulk Trader to up our NCU by a huge margin.

Frequent Customer may help, but I doubt it's enough NCU.

This process would require *heavy* implanting. And if you can do that, why not just implant-step (it's still quite possible) up to super-high implants and twink the heck out of everything that way? Any class can do that.

Not to mention, this whole process would require the maxxing of at least 3 nano skills (psy mod, sensory imp, and time & space) of which 2 are blue, 1 is dark blue.

This certainly looks good on paper, but I think if you actually tried it you would run into so many walls that it would only be possible with a LOT of playing back and forth. May take hours or days to get everything worked out right...by then you could gain to level 60 or something, and have a lot better chances than at level 41. =P

Still, even in your intial phase, you talk about:
"Plus Bulk Trader Which *CAN Be a little Trick to Cast at level 41 as its requires Some Fast Implant Swaping and Infuses plus FP Trader but it can be done +160"

Infuses would need 83 NCU! Bulk Trader 30... That's 113 NCU. Infuses only add +90, so we still need 264 points of nano skill buffs of whatever type to use bulk trader. General buffs cost 8 and get us to 244 and 121 NCU. So... Ransack Skills/Deprive Skills (Major) would add +47/+55 to get us down to 142 required for Bulk Trader. Those add 16/18 NCU to the total, though, and bump us up to 155 NCU. We're still 142 points, off...so, what now? Masteries? Another 30 NCU and we're still 92 points off. 185 NCU. So, now we have to go to righer level ransack's maybe? Maybe implants could tackle this, and a higher level Ransack. But that would edge us closer to 192 NCU.

So...that means you have to find a way to get 192 NCU on *before* Bulk Trader. We have 352 Comp Lit. after uber implants and Frequent Customer (not counting a Pioneer Backpack because that's rather unrealistic.) That's only enough for a 5-slot belt, IIRC. 5-slots * +35 NCU = 175 + 8 basic = 183 NCU.

First major hitch.

And this still isn't considering that we may actually need more nanos running to achive the 456 psycho mod req of Bulk Trader.

So, we now have 182 NCU instead of 248 NCU like you assumed. Starts to fall apart a bit after that...

"Now we hit a wall at Divest skills Minor we have 509 Skill and we need 526 We have 40+40+8+52=140 NCU used so far so lets pop up to Mochams 52+52+8+52=164 used NCU"

OK. So, we have 164 NCU full out of 182. That leaves us with 18 spare. Divest Skills (Minor) requires 35 to re-stack, so the journey is over.

Divest Skills (Weak) is +88 is +97.
Still can't use Unexpected Attack because we don't have 628 in any nano skills. Sniper's Bliss and the Experitise is +70 rifle/+35 Aimed Shot so we're at +255 rifle/+35 Aimed Shot.

Higher than what I said yesterday of +172 rifle and +32 AS, but not by much.

This isn't even considering nano durations, nano casting times with -2000 init, nano cost (and Agent's low nano pool), etc... It also isn't considering that there is NO GOOD WAY to buff Aimed Shot. Unexpected Attack does not raise Aimed Shot, it raises Sneak Attack. Take the Shot is the highest one, and it raises AS by 130--however, it requires 47 NCU and 700 in both sensory imp/psycho mod. Don't know how that fits into your situation. If we can't do Take the Shot, we only have the +15 from Sniper's Bliss. With general, that means +150 Aimed Shot or +35 Aimed Shot.

At level 41 you can have 228 Aimed Shot +150 or +35. 263/378.

Ranee requires 442. Unless you can get Take the Shot on (another big hurdle) that's not even remotely possible.

Also, consdier real-world limitations like...Agents have expensive Treatment and only have 196. This is 296, plus whatever you can equip to buff it. Maybe up to 350 without a Doc. (So, around QL 70 implants) That's only +79 to Aimed Shot. Enough for the Ranee with Take the Shot, impossible without it.

Don't forget that Aimed Shot and Rifle overlap in 2 implants. =)

Also don't forget that Ransack/Deprive has to be cast on an enemy, but only last 3 minutes. Any implant-swapping involved would require running back to a shop (for the +100 treatment buff) and swapping...that eats up valuable time. Frequent Customer/Bulk Trader only last a minute. So consider run time when you talk about "quick swapping."

You would also need a high-level MP on-call for this entire process, and someone to Give Nano: you as well. (NT or Trader) Plus you would need the implants, an nano cryistals, the Ranee, etc...

With that many resources at your fingertips, there are a lot better ways to power-level than spend a couple days trying to get on a Ranee. =P
