Here's what I did to fix it for me anyway..

Right click Desktop ->

Select "Properties" ->

Select the "Settings" tab->

Click the "Advanced" button ->

Select the "Geforce 2 GTS" ( or whatever the name of your video card is) tab ->

Then look on the left to a little menu and select "Performance and Quality Settings" ->

The middle scroll bar should say "Antialiasing" and range from Off to 4x.

You then move the bar all the way left so that Antialiasing is OFF.

Click ok on everything and start up AO.

I do alot of work on computers and I have identifyed the probleum. If you play Ao in 32 bit mode and set the grapics card to 32 bit mode you recieve no probluem using the drivers. However, if you set Ao to anything lower then 32 bit texture (ex: 32/16 or 16) while setting the grapics card to 16 bit mode. You will recieve the blocky letters. If you wish to play Ao in 16 bit mode you most set the grapic card to 32bit and AO texture to 16 bit.
