Sorry for the delay in getting back to you Khun. I have some answers for you now.

The Grenade Launcher has an attack and recharge time of 2.5 seconds each. This means 2.5 seconds from the attack starts until the damage is dealt, and another 2.5 seconds from damage is dealt until the next attack can start (during which you may do other stuff without interfering with the attack, e.g. reload).

However, the Grenade Launcher has only one grenade loaded at a time, which means that after the attack time is has to reload, not just recharge. This takes longer than 2.5 seconds, and is why the weapon can only attack about once every ten seconds.

The animation gets run repeatedly when reloading, which is a bug we're aware of and working on fixing.

I hope that helps you out.
