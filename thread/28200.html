13 general suggestions
Edit : corrected som spleling misstakkes =)

I wont really rant on about me - I'm a fairly uninteresting guy - but I do hope that you will take a look at the suggestions below...they would interest quite a few people I think.. I sorted them into cathegories for convenience (damned, never did learn to spell that word)...

(This is a clip taken out of a much larger document sent to Funcom that our guild took a looksey at - the full document includes profession things as well, but I dont want to focus on that on the board since profession suggestions will get trashed by trolls and whiners and fanboys (I am a happy neutral in this particular case) ... )

General - CITY LAG:
You are already taking some pressure off the most visited zones.. Newland, Pleasant Meadows, Broken Shores... moving cellscanners and banks to various parts of the frequented zones are good. I do, however, think that Broken Shores - City of Home, should be a zone on its own. It would reduce lag a lot I think, not having to load whats beyond the mountains and so on, much like how Newland is a zone on its own (and to some extent Borealis).

General - BREEDS:
Right now, the difference between breeds are rather.. nonexistant. At lower levels, 1-50 - there is still some difference, but at high levels - 150+ there is none, Solitus is the best breed for any and all professions, not the "allround" breed it was supposed to be (not excelling at anything, but be a well-rounded off breed).. One is led to believe that there would be a rather big difference in HP / Nanopool for one thing, compared to the other classes, but.. there really isnt. I've talked to a lot of people about what class/breed they would play if there would be able to turn back time, and 90% of them will say Solitus - which I really think is a crying shame.

Atrox - Should have at LEAST 1000 more HP than Solitus at level 200, and also 500 less Nanopool.
Nanomage - Should have at least 1000 more Nanopool than Solitus at level 200, and also 500 less HP
Opifex - As much HP and Nano as solitus, however - their runspeed as well as their evades should be at least 100 more than any other class (when maxed out) (or get some new Sense buffs out - the existing one is.. phew.. horrible)

General - SAVE TERMINALS
Right now there are a lot of reclaim terminals scattered throughout Rubi-Ka. A lot of these are in places that has no saveterminals however - so I would suggest removal of them, or putting in saveterminals in the places the reclaimterminals are.

General - UNIQUE MOBS:
Noone likes camping, and I have a couple of suggestions about this really. The thing is - I'm quite certain that you dont want everyone to have a certain item, the special items should really be SPECIAL, it should take some effort getting them, I can understand that - if everyone walked around like carboncopies wearing Corona Armor MK2, wielding the Edge of Tarasque - it just wouldnt be any fun. There's also a problem with a lot of people hoarding the same item over and over again, simply because (this is an actual quote) "I want it to be as rare as possible, so I'm doing it so noone else can get it" which is just obstruction I think. Another thing you should look at here is the availability/spawn ratio/drop ratio/quality of the items. I'll give you an example.

Tri-Plumbo drops the Rust-pitted Ring (+220 HP) every once in a blue moon. This ring is one of the rarest items in the whole of AO - its even rarer than stuff thats about 10 times as good. Triplumbo spawns every 3 hours, and has someting like a 2% chance of dropping the item, or once every 50 kills. 50 * 3 = 150 hours = a week CONSTANT watch over that one, I mean not a single kill must be missed, 24 hours a day.
Compare this to Nelebs cloak which is a 30 minute respawn and a 100% drop (together with some other stuff of his). The cloak is genuinly a lot more attractive than the ring. an 8 boost to all nanoskills means the world to a lot of players - I know level 180 players that puts it on just so they can cast that "extra" buff just before battle if they're missing a couple of points.

To the topic of special items - a certain other MMORPG solved this issue rather brilliantly. If you put a mobflag on every person (alone or in a team) ATTACKING (not killing) a mob, it would be solved. It could work something like this.. if you gather a team and want to get a certain item (because for the really nice ones you WILL need a team, they are impossible to kill by yourself - and I do mean impossible), when you attack the mob, you'd get a flag that would last for a certain amount of time - maybe fixed (like a month) or varied (depending on how good of an item it is, from 2-3 days to a month). If everyone in the whole team got the flag, you'd make sure that only the people needing the item in particular would get it, because you cant go "hoarding it just because its cool" or obstruct other players trying to obtain it. 30 to 60 minutes later the mob would respawn, leaving the quest open for another team.

Another variation could be that the flag lasts a week, mob spawns instantly, but the chance of a drop is only 50%.

Yet another way of doing it would be to incorporate some actual roleplaying into the game - like the Alvintype quests - those were a step in the right direction. You would have to talk to a certain person, get the quest, go there and kill the mob. A key could be spawned to a static dungeon where only the actual team members could go inside, and do the mission (this, however - would not work on some quests - like Tarasque, since they involve PvP to a great deal.)

General - EMPTY PLAYFIELDS:
Most playfields arent visited at all. They're empty of mobs (pretty much) or have only level 1-10 mobs in them (even the ones that are really far off) A lot of things can be done here..
Adding roaming uniques, static dungeons, or how about special shops where you can obtain clothes or items? The Fixer shop in Borealis was a neat idea - add some more of these, but that are also available to the rest of the population - I know for a fact that a lot of players want more clothing, make these available in certain shops only.. "Robert Yu's Fashion" for example could have a selection of oriental clothing.

General - ROAMING SUPERMOBS:
This is just a suggestion, that actually your designers brought up on the board once, I think it was voted down though, and I think it was because of the implemention of them. Supermobs are a good thing to break the monotony, but you need to do more than just plop them in, or have them randomly spawn.

I played a game called ANGBAND (very very old and very very fun Moria-type game - if you dont know what I'm talking about - nevermind, lol) and when something unique spawned, you would get a message reading "You feel there is something special about this level". Now - thats a dead giveaway, instead, when the mob spawns, how about you make it so everyone in the playfield gets a message reading "Suddenly you feel this might not be the safest place to hang around in..." Make the mobs into virtual bulldozers, I dont think players would mind - they'd rush off to scan, and then to explore the whole region looking for the mob.
A very easy way to get players to go "EEEP" is just to scale the monsters - make them huge (or have a certain glow to them, or even "rare to the region").. walking around in Lush in the woods having a Forest Eremite on your tail, or a Hammer thats 300% size.

But some kind of warning system would have to be added, and also, it wouldnt have to be a recurring thing, or people would go "Ok, that darned Challenger spawned again, everyone save and we'll hunt it down again". Make them random and very fierce.

General - HOUSING:
I wont touch on the topic of guildhousing, since apparently you are making an effort there, but I'll say I hope they make it into the game soon - having guildmeetings in various places being interrupted by mobs or other players going "Whats all this then? Guildmeeting? Can I join??" is tedious - I sincerely hope that the guildhouses will have multiple rooms and place to decorate a LOT. And thats something I want for the regular apartments as well...

1. Wardrobe - would work like a bank, people can put clothes in there
2. New furniture - sofas, beds, tables.. the actual models are in the game already - in the shops and so on, but sitting on the floor in your apartment while having a party or whatever kind of feels odd.
3. Ability to pick your tilings color / wallpaper. The graphics are easy enough, implementation might not be though.
4. 10 Item limit has to go. 10 items is lamps alone. I dont know how the clan housing looks, but if they look like the Omni ones, and are all white, it kind of feels like one is living in a part of an asylum with all white walls, ceiling and floor.
5. Maybe a bank could be added in the hallway to your apartment

General - SOCIAL MOVES:
Implement a "LOCK" feature and a real "/kneel" - the present one looks rather... well, lets say that its not used very much... "/lock" would mean that if you do "/pointfor" and then "/lock" or "/pointfor lock" it would lock the animation, and render the player unable to move (to prevent people from zooming around doing /adjust or various other nasty things).
Right now, all the commands in ao has to be implemented over and over again, if you feel like using them. "Its over there /pointfor".. "Where?" .. "/pointfor" "Do it again, please?".. "/pointfor /pointfor"

General - NANO RESIST
This skill really needs some looking at - currently it is at, I'd guess about 30% of the power of evades, if that. I will admit that powering this up would make PvP against casters even easier - something would have to be done to the mobs really I think.

We were discussing this and thinking that since mobs hardly use the weapons that they are wielding (they use different ones, special mob weapons that are really souped up) - why are they casting the same nanos that we have? It wouldnt be too hard to replace all the nanos with copies of the ones that casters have, and lower the nanoresist of them instead, so the players has an honest chance of evading them, because with a maxed out nano resist - its still quite impossible to do so.

General - PET PATHING:
Pet pathing is a problem, and I think I have a nice idea about it, but its only about 50% developed, but I'm going to write about it anyhow...
The nano "Transferrance" that engineers get came to mind one day while talking to an engineer and a MA about how wierd it is that transferrance gives the engineer a MA skill bonus.. Pet pathing could be solved rather ingeniously with the implementatioin of Transferrances for all professions. Let me give you an example on how it would work...

Transferring your body into your pet, make the pet 50% transparent and it would follow you, much like wearing an armor, alternatively make it so you actually disappear into the ninja/demon/pet/droid/whassname.

Socials wouldnt be an option, naturally - doing this would make it very harsh on you guys. The pet could still attack one mob and you another, as long as its within range. This would mean some risk to the pet / and you ofcourse. Perhaps make it so that 50% damage that you take is transferred to the pet, but also 25% damage that the pet takes, flows over to you. It would have to be tested ofcourse.

Another way of doing it is to implement "Warp Pet" nanos, as someone suggested on the board. Warping the pet to you from the same zone to your back again if it gets stuck.

Either of these solutions and you wouldnt have to worry about pet pathing ever again (pretty much at least).

General - PET SELECTIONS
Selecting each team member with the F1-6 keys is a very nice function. A lot of times, selecting ones pet is incredibly hard though, especially mid battle in a mission, sometimes even outside its a really hard thing to do, especially if you still havent learned the camera at low levels. I therefor suggest that you assign the following keys to pets :
F7 - Attack pet (crat, mp, engie and the coming pet nano for adventurers)
F8 - Heal pet (mp)
F9 - Mez pet (mp)

General - Bug HP BAR
The HP Bar sometimes will not update correctly for the team members after HP's have been modified with Essence, or other HP buffs, making it extremely hard for Docs to keep track of the members HP's...

General - CHAT CLIENT
The planned chat commands are good. Furthermore, I'd like to see a /aggdef command in % where you could set your aggdef slider accordingly.

Another useful command would be /ncu where you would clean out all non-hostile nanos instead of manually clicking them.

Yet another - /afk REASON (where you can specify your own reason)

The /CC addbuddy command is great. How about a /cc addbuddy GUILD version where it adds all guildmembers?
Also - how about making another list apart from "buddys" and "?" called "Guild" and you could add a /cc addguild XXXX command where XXXX is the name of the player..

Tons of things can be used in the chatclient to provide better communication between people. One thing I definately like is the /ignore command, I dont overuse it, but its a good thing. However, I think that you should:

1. Remove the ignore button from when people want you to join their team. This causes a LOT of trouble if your team is disbanded, and reforming and more than one person is trying to restart the team.
2. Add /unignore as a command, and make a paramater ALL, so you can clear the ignorelist completely.
3. Add a /ignorelist command, which will output all the players on your ignore list into either a local file, or a message, or a textbox.

oh, and name SW corner in Trade "Twicer Alley" - I spend enough time there to know every darned pixel in the game =P lol
