Skill Dependencies and "Soft-Caps"

Each skill has a ability dependency where increases in an ability (ie strength, stamina, etc) can affect a skill level. For example, Body Development has a 100% stamina dependency. My understanding is that an increase in an ability will increase its dependent skill by 0.25 times the dependency times the ability increase. In other words, for a 100 point increase in stamina, body development would increase by 25 (0.25 x 100% x 100).

Furthermore, a skill can only be increased a certain amount until it reaches its "soft cap" assuming that the ability that it is dependent upon is not increased. This is the message you get sometimes when increasing a skill and it says "base ability too low" or something like that. For example, I can't keep increasing body development without increasing stamina at some point. Does anyone know the formula for calculating this "soft cap"? For example, if I wanted to ultimately have say 500 in body development, what is the lowest stamina I could have?

Originally posted by Beria
Maximum Skill Increase Per Level by Skill Colour

Green = 5 per level
Aqua = 4 per level
Blue = 3 per level
This isn´t exactly accurate. Every skill has a multiplier to count its cost in IPs. All green skills have a multiplier of 1, they all cost 5 IPs to raise the first point.

Aqua skills have a multiplier between 1,2 and 1,5, making their first point cost between 6 and 7,5 IPs. If the aqua skill has a multiplier below 1,5, then it actually can be increased five points per level. Just create a fixer and raise their treatment/first aid by five points at once to verify this.

The same goes for dark blue skills, which have a multiplier between 2,4 and for, making their first point cost between 12 and 20 IPs. I think, although I´m not totally sure about this, that dark blue skills with a multiplier of 3,5 or higher can only be raised 3 points per level, while dark blue skills with a multiplier below 3,5 can actually be raised by 4 points per level.

It´s not only the colour of the skill that defines the cost, the cost also varies between skills of the same colour, just create a new character and look at the cost for raising all the dark blue skills the first point, it will vary between 12 and 20 IPs.
