OOC: The barriers to RP in AO (and hopefully the solutions)
Sorry if this has already been discussed to death but its not an easy subject to search for (common keywords)

I'd like to as people to list those things that exist in the game mechanics that they find hard to speak about IC due to their being no "in world" reason. Also I'd like people to propose "Explanations" for the same.

My list:

1. Suppression Gas, why is it lower/higher in specific places (E.G MMD)
2. Why can humanoid NPC's attack at 75% suppression gas while players cannot
3. One word... Nodrop
4. and another... Unique
5. Why NPC's just _stand_ there 24 hours a day 7 days a week
6. Why NPC's can't cross zones
7. Why there are so many things you can't buy even though the NPC's obviously can.
8. Why Omni don't actually have to work at all.
9. Why you can't steal an NPC's armour/weapon
10. Why there are big Yalms/Kodaiks in the scenery even though ours are _very_ small

I love AO and I'd love to RP more but I find it really _akward_ to use the wonderfull backstory with so many incongruities.
