A high level agent in the middle of the final level cap (e.g. somewhere around level 175 where they have been capped for over 10 levels) will have TONS of IP to spare. As such, it would be pretty easy to max out the skills needed to use a Flashpoint. I'm not saying you need a QL 200 one. As I showed eariler, a QL 161 Flashpoint will do much much much more damage with an alpha than an Ithaca ever could hope to do. Also, Flashpoints have an almost instantaneous euqip time, so swapping out the Flashpoint after the alpha is easy (if your target isn't already dead). Please note, you should use a NON-OE'd Flashpoint. Remember, specials are instantaneous. That means Fling, Burst, and Full Auto, would all happen within a span of 1 second (if that), and ALL would be crits. A Burst crit (let alone a Full Auto one) with a Flashpoint at QL 161 would hit the 40% cap on almost anyone in PvP. The only 2 classes who can have a prayer will be Soldiers using TMS, or one of the 20 NTs who have Nullity.

I think this is fair. Why? Because it is difficult to do and not only requires forethought, it requires breaking the mold in terms of IP distribution. It also requires an extremely high level to accomplish.
