Neutral Application Forms
In 29250:
The first "neutrals" appear when Omni-Tek employees - as well as clan members - abandon their affiliations and settle in non-political zones. Fearing more ICC interventions, OT authorities turn a blind eye to these neutrals.

People were given a choice over two hundred years ago to leave Omni and leave the Clans to become neutral.

Why now in the year 29476 am I forced to stay in the clans which are becoming more and more radical?

Let it be known that the organization Conscientious Objectors is here to show the powers at be that neutral application forms need to be made available. You shouldn't have to stay on a side of the conflict that you don't agree with fully.

You should have the choice to be neutral!
