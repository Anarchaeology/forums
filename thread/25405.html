Nerf means to weaken something. For example:

Someone takes a really great skill and restricts the use of it from all adventurers. Basically, they delete it from the game.
Random Adventurer: Man, they totally nerfed adventurers!
Nerf is used because a nerf toy is generally soft, squishy, and totally harmless.
