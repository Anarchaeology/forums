Interesting experience in missions
I did a mission yesterday which used the scheme I rarely ever see (The one where you walk into a monsters mouth) Now I'd done this mission type before but was absolutely amazed to see that it got darker as I went deeper into the mission. Eventually I could see nothing except bullets flying at me. After barely killing what I couldn't see, I decided I couldn't continue like this. But I really wanted the mission reward, so I tried to think of how to light the place up.

I remembered those floating lamp things and night vision so I went out and bought them, came back to the mission, and found out that I had gone past numerous treasure chests I had missed.

Long story short, I love this added mission effect. (And no, my monitor brightness was not screwed up.) The darkness factor is just one of the interesting things I hope to find in the future. Keep up the good work!

Anyone else seen new mission effects?
