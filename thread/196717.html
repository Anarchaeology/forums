Hey, Warlock. Good to see you again.

Now, to answer the questions:

1. Engineers aren't gimped to hell and back. We have to be careful which weapons we use in SL as hitting for minimum damage is quite common against mobs L100+. We've gotten perks, new nanos - at L201, I can now selfcast Shield of the Obedient Servant, and I'm 11 pts short of selfcasting Omni-Pol Pacification Logic System.

2. AO hasn't changed all that much with regards to powerlevelling/camping, it's just that now you have to camp SL mobs to get quest-items so you can actually get to where you can level beyond 200. Does 'quest hell' mean anything to you?

3. Tradeskills have gotten a boost, 1250 QFT isn't nearly as difficult as it was. Tradeskill-perks and new items make it easier for Engineers to reach high values in the skills.

4. Shadowlands has brought much to the game - but roleplaying isn't one that I have noticed, with small exceptions.

5. AFAIK, the free month must be used on an account other than your own, if you already have an existing one.

6. You can choose between the new GUI and the old GUI. Look in the Options for this (can't remember exact location).

7. FC hasn't given much response to the Professionals as of late. We live in hope.

Engineers aren't gimped at all, anymore. At least in terms of damage. I can regularly outdamage enforcers if I try. This gives us a lot of leverage in teams. Perks...

Basically cumulative skill bonus's.

You get 1 perk point for every 10 levels up to 200, then one perk point for each level from 201 - 220.

The best bet for deciding which to choose is to load up Jaydes (AO DB), or Auno's (Auno.org) websites and use the Perk Configuration tools.

Each perk level is locked to a specific level, so you could instantly learn 10 levels of Bio Shielding before level 201.

For trade skill specialists its easy to spend all your perk points at level 200 on Trade skill perks. Personally I have 19 points spent in a mixture of Tinkerer, Gadgeteer and Mechanic, the last point in Genius.

For anyone that likes combat, or just staying alive, Bio Shielding is very very useful.

Hope that all makes sense.

They're fantastic but oh so very hard to find.

For the most part they drop from Blueprint bosses. The bosses themselves are made from a complete pattern, some MP magic, subdued Novictum, and a summoning party at an incarnator of the appropriate QL.

To make the pattern into something usable is quite intensive, QL192 requires 1440 Nano Programming to make and about 1150 QFT, that Nano Programming wasn't a lot of fun getting the first time.

Control symbiants number amoung the lowest drop rate, the most desirable (for everyone else it seems) and the only set we have access to (every other profession has a choice of two or three sets).

Personally I only have one, a QL180 right hand.

I had a left wrist but they really are pitiful and not remotely worth bothering with.

Finally, they also don't provide any bonus to Grenade, making that, at present, a bit of a misleading weapon choice.
