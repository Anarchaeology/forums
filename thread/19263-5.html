I have a great idea for a revamping of a particular line of Soldier nanos. The mastery nanos in particular.

MPs are masters of nanousage. Thus they get a line of nanos that buffs each and every nano skill. There are several levels of them, but at each level, a buff is available for each skill. They get:
+25
+50
+90
+140

Well, how about making Soldier weapon buffs work the EXACT SAME WAY! They should thus be able to buff:

Assault Rifle, Rifle, Pistol, SMG, LR Energy, and Heavy Weapons (and maybe even grenade)

make these buffs have different levels (however each skill has a buff at all levels). Make them the same as the MP buffs:
+25
+50
+90
+140

Aim them at each of the title caps in terms of requirements. Thus:
+25 (around level 15)
+50 (around level 50)
+90 (around level 100, yeah, I know Infuse isn't available till later for MPs, but Infuse is borked IMHO)
+140 (around level 150)

Also, Soldiers should get a full line of buffs for special attacks. The special attacks are:
Fling, Burst, Full Auto, and Aimed Shot

These I feel should also be available in the same levels, however, I also feel the requirements should be a little higher.

The way I see it, a soldier is to his guns as an MP is to his pets. An MP does damage with pets, and a soldier does damage with guns. Just as an MP is able to self buff into amazing pets rather much earlier than other pet professions, a soldier should be able to self buff into amazing guns as well. With the current OE rules now, this would require them to keep those nanos running (and thus taking up NCU) if they want to keep those guns effective, and thus I think these nanos would not be overpowered at all.
