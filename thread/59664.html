Well, it changes as the levels go up. I mean, most people seem to try to look at this as what prof can solo up the highest ql. I think that is an impractical point of view.

Assuming ql 190-195ish missions, then MA. Nothing drops those mobs faster at that level. And you don't need crowd control. Since I duo missions with an MA, I know first hand just how fast they can solo, since I'll be the designated chest popper etc, and she'll drop 1-2 mobs while I move my lockpick over a chest and right click.

Next, I suppose is crat. Sure, they can charm mobs, but between having to deal with pets, or just waltzing up to a mob and critting it to death in a matter of a few seconds...I'd prefer the critting to death part. And like I said, for an MA at least, mezzing isn't an issue.

I'd say enforcer next. They deal very nice damage, and mobs at this level drop fast enough to disregard heals.

Once again, this is considering characters I regularly team with...and I'm 195. I'm not saying these three would be the best choice throughout the entire game at every ql mission.
