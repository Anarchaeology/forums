Bionitrous -

I agree in principle with your statements but I think you should also consider a few other things....

First and most important, the neutrals are not cornering the market on suffering when it comes to Notum Wars. It has had a very bad affect on relationships both personal and public within the Clans, and (I assume) Omni-Tek. I believe that actions against neutrals are not, in a lot of cases, directed AT the Neutrals but rather are a by-product of what is happening to almost every guild in the game.
I'm glad to hear others coming around to the realization that Notum Wars is ruining AO in a particular fashion. My latest 'kick' these days seems to be trying to point out that Notum Wars has essentially poured out all the bad that used to be contained in Tarasque's lair onto the entire planet's surface. Search out some of my recent posts regarding this analogy. Some are pure crap that shouldn't have been written that were triggered by sheer frustration with what this game has turned into while others are a bit more lucid in trying to point out why Notum Wars was put out so totally and utterly wrong when it could have been so wonderfully good.

Not a lot of people are having 'fun' anymore. All there is in-game is tension, distrust, complaining. People can't go about their routines without being disrupted. In-game friendships are being torn apart. Exploits and loophole-utilization is through the roof. Once again, I repeat, this all used to be contained in Camelot and MMD/2HO to a great extent. If you didn't want to be exposed to it, you had no difficulty avoiding it. As much as I personally can't wait for the day my guild's remaining high-level tower is eventually destroyed by some Counter Strike-minded guild leader, the losses stemming from my Org Advantages and personal tower bonuses will be a tremendous burden. FunCom is basing the game's future balance around advantages that are earned through PvP. Well, that puts 90% of the game's population in a situation where they are directly affected by something they'd rather not deal with.

Is it any wonder we have groups like Storm and Synergy Facter and so many others running around the map willy nilly wiping out bases and then 'handing them over' to a guild on their own faction? FunCom in their infinite stupidity decided to not only hand out an experience bonus (most valuable buff in the game), but to base it on persentage of towers owned by your faction. Now we end up with 5 or 6 organizations pumping up their XP bonus by wiping out groups they have absolutely no beef with and 'handing over' the base to some other group they may never have even heard of and which has/had no intention of PvP'ing on any regular scale.

It's so utterly messed up. Yeah, we got between 80 and 200 people having fun with Notum Wars. The rest vary from grudging acceptance to utter disgust to feigned ignorance. Notum Wars could have been a wonderful tool for facilitating inter-guild warfare. Instead, because of the terrible way it was implemented, it has degraded to a disgusting modern-day evolution of UO's housing fiasco. My guild looked forward to having our claimed land attacked. We assumed it would be someone who had difficulty with us personally. We could engage in counter-attacks against these guys and generally have a good time. What we get is red-team vs. blue-team who has the most triangles on the board. It don't matter who your guild is. It don't matter what the impetus is behind your guild's actions. It don't matter the moral fibre of your guild leadership.

All that matters is the color of the tag under your name and whether or not you can be tabbed. So bring it back to this thread's topic: in the eye's of so many who apparently 'matter' in this game now that Notum Wars has been released, nobody gives a damn about Healing Hands or whether or not you are staying true to your charter. Because of FunCom's horrible sense of how to 'encourage' people to PvP, all that matters is that you are:

a.) Attackable
b.) Potentially sapping some of the almighty XP Bonus

and ...

Well, that's about it.

Heaven forbid you ever get caught healing one Omni after you just kept a regiment of Clanners alive the day before. That just doesn't matter. You've just given the power gamers an opening to rip you a new one in forums and torment you with impugnity.

In our guild's case, heaven forbid you launch a counter-attack on someone who ripped your towers down even though you've never once launched an unprovoked attack. I suppose it makes me somewhat sick to my stomach that my guild has had to stray from our charter by not taking even one mercenary contract to aid in an attack on any org that didn't launch first strike against us. Believe me, I'm guilty that FunCom has seen fit to make the advantages from our higher-level base so valuable that we have to cower from engaging in our business activities for fear of the almighty Red or Blue teams. The advantages from land ownership have nothing to do with PvP and are ruining the game. Oddly enough, in our case it's preventing us from engaging in PvP as it should according to our charter.

A final note on how twisted the team Red and team Blue extremists can be. They will link without a moment's hesitation, Tower Hunters, Elite Operations, Circle G, IR, Wolf Brigade, Healing Hands, and all the rest, into one 'faction' simply because the color on our tags is the same. My guild lost a tower because of the actions of what the Tower Hunters did. My guild don't know them guys except in that the founder or founders came from my organization and quit when we put a stay on promotion from our training guild to the main guild to allow us to evaluate several longer and severely reprimanded several for unprovoked attacks in the lowbie areas of Holes in the Wall. We got punished ostensibly because of the actions of people we didn't find at one point were a fit for our organization. Point being, it doesn't matter what the integrity of your guild is. All that matters is the color on your guild-tag. Bear that in mind, make like the three monkeys at the temple, and as much as you can bear it, pretend Notum Wars never happened.

Hopefully, FunCom can address the wave of darkness that Notum Wars draped over their game with some subsequent modifications. I'm praying with all my might that Rimor will be completely overwhelmed by 'Team Red' so that FunCom will hear from both sides as well as the independants how much it sucks to play. Laying non-PvP-specific rewards on this tower nonsense was their first and biggest mistake. Rimor is suffering a potential extinction as a server as a result.
