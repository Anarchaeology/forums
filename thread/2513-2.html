Story / Character / Interaction
Aristotle thought a lot about the whole question of whether plot or character is more important. He arrived at the conclusion that plot is more important.

I would tend to agree. Without a plot to give context to your character then you are just another uber-soldier putting the smack down on MOB after MOB.

Funcom is telling an epic story which I can experience first hand - and to be perfeclty honest I respect them for even trying.

However, and this comment is aimed at every poster who is whining about the lack of roleplaying in AO, Funcom is trying to tell a story in which we can participate. But our participation must react to the game world.

If you want to go down in history as Rubi-Ka's John Wilkes Booth - fine with me. But as long as your actions take point of departure in the story, and you don't expect the story to take point of departure in you, then everything will be fine.

But if you start whining about the game world not reacting to your actions then think about this scenario:

Aberic (or some other charcter) carries out an action which is contrary to the wishes of Omni-Tech (which is, remember a Facist Company)

Omni-tech revokes this person's ressurection insurance, and threatens to do the same to anyone who associates with them.

Omni-tech bans them from entering OT cities.

Omni-tech brings in their military to deal with the problem permanently. (remember the ICC ruling only prevents the OT military from attacking the Clans - not from dealing with "Internal OT security matters")

Now that would suck, right? But it would be the game world reacting to your actions...

And it could be implemented really easily too...

Interaction is a two edged sword - and believe me it gets really sharp out at the cutting edge...
