West Athens Attacked.....
A few moments ago, West Athens was attacked by Mantis like bugs. The prelude to this "attack" was some rumbling and quakes in West Athens.

A guard Captain (Meepels i think)entered the Cup and spoke with us briefly. We were having our regular Sat. meeting with many Red Tigers and DI-RTs present. Meepels wanted to kow if we knew anything about the quakes. After speaking with us he left to scout out the rest of West Athens.

We proceded with our meeting, despite a few tremors. Suddenly, Meepels returned with a cry to "help man the Gates! We are under attack."

With no further prompting, Red Tigers and DI-RTs alike, rushed to the exit of the Cup. Once outside, we were greeted with a horrible sight. There were literaly dozens of Mantis like bugs infesting the streets. The bugs were attacking everyone.

Rypley, the president of the Red Tigers, quickly set about organizing a defense. She had us rush headlong and meet our foe. I was quickly felled (thank god for reclaim). But I did see Zenless1 doing a passable job, imitating a tornado

I'm proud to announce that the Red Tigers held the line, and stood their ground while re-enforcements arrived. Many peeps from many different guilds showed up in time to participate. I'm still unclear on wether we beat the living hades out of them, or they withdrew having accomplished some unknown mision.

Be forewarned! If you experience ground tremors, band together. It could be a prelude to an attack.
