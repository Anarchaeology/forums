Using Conceal to gank greys in arena
Just want to say how lame I think it is when very high level players (eg a 167 titled MA) go into the arena, turn on conceal and then gank greys.

This is the ultimate in lameness - high level players using conceal to gank greys of their own side.

Since the 5 times I have ventured into Omni Ent arena (with my alt) this has happened *every time*. You're not just ganking greys, you are locking out a section of the game that people *should* be able to play. Yes I agree that is partly Funcoms fault for not implenting a duel mechanism. I have *no* problems with people my own level range doing this, I have no problems with people on oppisitie sides doing this - doing it to someone on your side when you are nearly 100 levels higher is *pathetic*. The excuse that 'I am high level and so I am bored' is equally pathetic. I have a high level MP who has never ganked greys of his own side.

Not a moan about ganking in general, I just wish other players would grow up. You should also realise that the grey youve just ganked might be your friend using an alt.
