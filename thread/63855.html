Bait & Trap
1. If you suspect any particular individual(s), set them up with a juicy piece of info and see if it leaks.

2. If you know to whom the info is being leaked, ask around and see if anyone on your Faction's roster has been previously associated with the Faction/person to whom the info is leaking.

3. Find a relatively unknown citizen, who's unaffiliated, and have that person become "nice-&-friendly-like" with the person you suspect to be either the traitor or a person you think is associated therewith.

To prevent being spied on, conduct your "sensitive" meetings in secrecy or via secure grid-link, and always be aware of those around you. There is always a hawk watching overhead.

AiymaNeut
Supa Leet
Just make sure you, yourself, can draw the distinction between su****ion and paranoia. Take a step back and ask yourself if the information to which you believe this "leak" pertains could reasonably be determined by an unaffiliated third person with no knowledge of the subject matter. Sometimes things that may seem as "leaked" are simple common-sense derivatives.

To limit the possibility of stupidity and gullibility, ensure that your Faction's policies concerning confidentiality and the privilege of information are as transparent and clear as possible, and attach consequences to be levied against those in breach thereof.

Before releasing any piece of information, on a per diem basis, state explicitly the sensitivity of the information and the punishments applicable for breaching confidentiality. Often times setting a general policy that is not frequently reiterated dooms such policy to be forgotten.

Above all else, Jynne's suggestion holds: choose your allies wisely.
