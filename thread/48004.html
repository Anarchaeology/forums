I wouldn't call the jones vest a nanobreed "advantage." But if you can get one, then yes it can make your life in AO a bit easier.

Regarding raising nano programming to wear it, its not so bad, Nova.

You get +210 from your token board, another +210 from implants. +92 from an MP buff. And if you've breed capped Intelligence you already have 130 NP as a base skill. Add another 23 from expertise and boost and without spending one point in NP you already have 665 (672 with Shades of Luc and 679 with 2 eggs, 689 with Faded Genius ear implant, 699 with 2 Jess pistols, 701 with two pieces of sekutek...) lol

Sadly, the OE nerf requires you to maintain a 511 Nanoprogramming though (the jones vest itself gives +50 to NP once you get it on). Still, if you have 130 base +210 from token board and +55 from vest, it is NOT a lot of IP at all to raise it to 511, maybe around 20k IP. Think of it as spending IP to raise all your nano skills by 25 points, your nano init by 50 points, your Int by 20 points, your comp lit by 5 points, your treatment/first aid by 2-3 points etc...I'd call it a very good bargain.

Besides, a crat raising nano prog to wear the jones vest is no different from all those other classes raising psychology a bit to wear DeCranum MK II armor.
