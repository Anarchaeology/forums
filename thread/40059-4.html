Mission area cleaned out is a bad idea. I've tried similar things before, and about 50% of the time the mission area 'drops' when you get more than about 15 people in it, then it comes back up again. However when it comes back it is fully populated again - making things rather dangerous for people in the wrong place.

An obscure backyard would make more sense - Newlands and Borealis have more than enough out-of-the-way yards for this kind of thing. It is slightly harder to control access, but this would be the best way to get people in without the additional headaches.

I'm keen to attend any discussion on RP and griefing. However, if we're going to have a meeting of any size can I suggest we appoint a chairperson to ensure the event doesn't get spammed to death with everyone talking at once?

* * *

What we've done in large, long BWO meetings is this:

A private channel is opened for actual discussion of points, and all meeting attendees are invited to this channel. At any given time only one person is entiteld to speak on this channel - all others are required to be silent.

One person is designated as the chairperson. They keep a record of who wants to talk, and in what order.

In vicinity chat people raise hands to talk, make small talk, emote or whatever. Anyone wandering by also tends to talk in vicinity. People would also, in vicinity, indicate when they had finished talking and allow the chairperson to call forward the next speaker.

What this means is that the output of the meeting is a well structured log of the meeting from the private channel in which each person gets to talk 'uninterrupted' and you get a nice, sensible flow from the meeting. We had a meeting like this that - while it lasted about 4 hours - was extremely productive and came out with some very good points and some very readable notes. The log was also quite readably short, as it only included pertinent points and didn't include the 'hellos' 'sorry I'm lates' 'can I say something please?' and all the other things that so often clutter up meeting transcripts.

Without an agenda and some kind of meeting management this will just be a shouting match.

If someone needs to voulinteer to chair such a meeting, I'm happy to take notes and see who wants to talk and when.

I'm a GMT +0 (+1 at the moment due to daylight savings) person.

this is something that would no doubt take a long time, and Mech, just like our BWo meetings, could go on for a long time as well. I expect a number of people to want to be a part of this, and share thier 2 cents, so I would propose the following.

stay with the chairperson idea, but have each topic of discussion decided beforehand (although it is quite possible more will come up as we go) so that the discussion groups can be split up into 3 or 4 different chats, all discussing the same thing. Afterwards each chair person, with a log of that discussion then brings forth the infomation and discusses it with the other chairpeople, and when there is something to work with, a second gathering aff all who were involved is arranged to go over each item as a full group, again with a chairperson.

Advantages.....1. Less hours of your day spent waiting and waiting for your chance to speak. 2. I have always found that taking a topic in 3 or 4 different groups, you can get a very good understanding of what priorities are and what common things are discussed, without any influence from the other groups. this helps in solution finding, and 3. I can forsee a lot of bitter people that after 6 hours of waiting, to say what they wat to say, the majority want to close the topic and move on. I think the important thing is for everyones voice to be heard. As well, i believe with the proper time spent, the better off we'll be with the decisions that are made.

Disadvantages - takes a little longer as a group to come to an understanding, which many want as soon as possible, and there is the matter of finding more than one unbiased chairperson.

change is not going to happen overnight, but all the RP'ers working together i truly believe it can and will.

as well i suggest constant communication in a forum chat is made so everyone is on the same page as these issues are discussed.

my 2 cents
