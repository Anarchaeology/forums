An Update of sorts
Ok, after some consideration, I think this event would best be served, in a roleplaying sense, by being held in Stret West, at and around Reet's Retreat.

Having decided that, the next thing I'd like to think about is what will be actually happening at the event, and to that end, I'd definitely welcome suggestions.

Naturally, the main focus of this event is to create a sort of "trading bazaar", where everyone can come and trade items they no longer want, or have put together... offer their services for buffs/implants/tradeskills/etc... roleplay the buying and selling aspects of the game...all without having to sit around monitoring the Trade channels. I'll be designating seperate areas for specific trading 'types', along the lines of "Tradeskill trading", "Buff and Implant Work", "General Item Trading", and the like, to cut down on Vicinity chat traffic as much as possible.

Some other possible events I've envisioned:

* Scheduled 'hunting trips' to popular spots
* 'Profession Guides' for newer players (more established players helping newer players out through suggestions and such)
* In-game Seminars on interesting topics (for those who enjoy lectures )

Again, any ideas you may have are welcome. Keep in mind though... I don't want to make things *too* mammoth; I'm only one person, and I can't do everything by myself.

Once we've got a handle on the above, my next step will be to assign 'time slots' for people who wish to 'staff' this event (by 'staff', I mean on-duty organizers, not players who wish to trade)... probably in 2-3 hour blocks, with an 'alternate' in case of unforseen issues and emergencies. I, myself, will likely take a slot for early afternoon Pacific Standard Time, as that's the time zone I'm located in. I'll post a list of 'suggested qualifications' later this week.

Oh, and by the way: Puppetfinger, is was nice to meet you in-game last weekend... I'm still shopping for a 'event staff' uniform
