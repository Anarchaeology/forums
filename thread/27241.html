2nd Accounts - /follow in missions
Hi all,
I've been playing with the idea of starting a 2nd account, so that whenever I go solo-missioning with my Doctor, I can have a reasonable chance of survival (if I pick a more difficult mission). I know many out there have and are using 2nd accounts, so can you please tell me your experience with them?

Namely, is it problematic having your 2nd character /follow in indoor zones such as mission dungeons? Specifically I'd like to start a soldier that can target and fire/forget while I focus on healing, buffing, debuffing with my main.

Thanks in advance for any advice/experiences.

Playing 2 at a time is fine...
...but the follower pathing is iffy and downright terrible at times (getting stuck on walls when going through doors, whompas, etc.; follower sometimes gets "unhitched" and is on auto-pilot in a straight line).

You probably do better to use the support class as the follower and the combat class as your "driver".
