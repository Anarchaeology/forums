Omni-Tek Day
Omni-Tek Day.

Saturday 30th March
Time 8pm GMT

As we all know this saturday is Omni-Tek Day ( ok I know I am making this up but hay why not) so we have planned the normal parade of Omni Tek troops and following party.

As the loyalty of many guilds in Omni-Tek is under question what better timing could this have in bring the people together in the honour of Omni-Tek.

This years parade will follow the normal route, starting at Galway County Castle taking the Road to Rome, through Rome, Omni-Ent and ending with a party in Omni HQ.

We hope this year Mr Ross will be able to present his yearly speech but given the current criminal problems, and issues with the clan we understand this may not be possible.

All members are reminded that you must dress in Full Omni-Tek uniform or in offical guild uniform. We hope you all have a nice day out.

Ps can you let me know if your guild is able to make it, also any comments are welcome.
