Omni-Tek Quick Responce Force
Loyal citizens of Rubi-Ka......

The Omni Quick Responce Force currently has opening in all departments. We are a Roleplaying/Teamworking Oriented Guild based in Omni Trade (RK2).

If your idea of fun is Roleplaying, working with a close knit team for a greater good and story line involvement (when the story-line gets to RK2), then QRF may be the guild for you.

The Omni Quick Responce Force by name has been around since nearly the begining, but we are currently reforming and coming together.... we are becoming stronger at each passing day.

Please take a moment to visit our web-page at the following link:

Omni-Tek Quick Responce Force

Our Leader is President Kizzim and we currently have departments in Warfare, Intelligence, Technology/Research, Personnel and Foreign Affairs with Generals Plankk, Tarloran, Encrypio, Lohem and the 5th department yet to be determined respectively.

We are interested in Mature Quality People, Not just great AO Players.

If your interested please contact me in-game on RK2 for a short meeting and interview.

I look forward to hearing from you in the near future.
