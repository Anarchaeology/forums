14.2 Whompa and Travel Changes
Sooooo... we're getting to see "the first" of a series of changes to the whompa system?

Well, um, can we get some info on this? I seem to remember a post or discussion here once positing the use of ICC HQ in Andromeda as a "neutral hub" of whompa travel. The question is, how will this affect the existing system?

Thought #1: http://nakar.freeservers.com/whompa1.jpg

This is a revised system, using only those whompas that currently exist, with the Neutral hub moved to ICC HQ. As you can see, this totally isolates the clan and neutral whompas from one another, just like the OT whompas are isolated. I can only forsee this as being a major pain, but I somehow suspect this isn't what will be happening.

Thought #2: http://nakar.freeservers.com/whompa2.jpg

This uses the ICC HQ as the neutral hub as before, but connects Last Ditch to Athen and ICC HQ to Omni Ent. While this would make getting between territories trivial, it does make a certain degree of sense.

Some other thoughts, not mapped out: Instead of Ent, connect ICC HQ to Harry's. A whompa to Harry's is, IIRC, something Neuts have been asking for for a while, and it makes sense.

So what's up Funcom? How is the system gonna change? Are we going to see new whompas and new destinations? A restructuring of the old? Let's have some answers - or some speculations!
