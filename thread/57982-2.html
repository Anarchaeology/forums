Let me just add some quick comments...

Bionitrous, your complaints have plenty of merit. I do not question that at all. However, I question that the request outlined in this thread (or many others in the MP forums) would help in the slightest bit.

You are talking about key, underlying problems that globally apply to AO as a whole. MPs are mearly the prof that sees it first, since they can buff themselves and have many green skills.

What would solve your boredom? Surely a Nano Pool pet would not solve your boredom. Perhpas for a few days...past that, back to same ol' same ol'--just a little stronger.

Here are the only solutions available then:

1) Give MPs 2 or 3 new levels to all their lines
2) Raise the reqs on existing lines to readjust the growth curve
3) Add level requirements on all nanos
4) Remove title caps are raise all reqs to compensate to readjust growth curves

#1 is not gonna happen. It would make MPs way too overpowered. #2 would come with huge complaints from almost all MPs--probably including yourself. #3 would garner a worse response than #2, in my estimation...I'm sure you would love going through what Advents go through now--being able to SELF-CAST nanos at level 125 cap, but having to wait to level 145 to use the nano because of a level req. (Not even considering outside buffs!! Could cast those nanos at level 80-90 otherwise with Infuses or Mochams...but, nope...145 or bust.)

#2 and #3 artificially string out the level process to make you have something to "look forward to." But, as being a class where #3 is being implemented as a main, this is more frustrating than anything else. I'd rather be bored than frustrated.

#4 is how AO should have been to begin with. Almos tthe entire AO population is bored after level 166, not just you! Title caps drain the life out of the high-end game, and make the game overly boring. That said, it is too late to implement this large of a change without mas hysteria setting in.

So, that brings us to a few conclusions...
1) Quit AO because you're bored
2) Find aspects of the game to enjoy aside from character progression
3) Create an alt

EVERY high-level char faces this choice around level 160. MPs may get it set in a little earlier, but it's not unique to MPs in any way, shape, or form.

These are simply basic flaws with AOs design.

This is why I find it silly when people request "new things" to help the situation. They don't. People would either whine and ***** that they can't get them right away ("The reqs are too high!", "It takes too long to level!", etc.) or would get bored within a week of the initial "oooooh...ahhhhhh" factor wearing off.

The problems lay within AOs stat progression system and lack of high-level content, not the power of the MPs as a profression.

I honestly wish this wasn't the case. AO, especially in the earlier levels, is way too cool to come to such a screaching, boring halt...it is rather unfortunate. It seems obvious that Funcom's choice was #3 on my list, and I rue the day when all nanos have level requirements. =(

-Jayde
