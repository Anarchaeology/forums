Temple of Three Winds Loot List
Hey everyone,
Everyone out there should know that the Temple of the Three Winds has some really spectacular loot in it. I would like to create a list of all the things people have found in it to help me decide what to hunt next. Please include the mob that drops it. If you are not sure of what drops it, then someone else can tell you where it drops it. I'll try to update the list with information people provide me and hopefully we can get a grandmaster list.

Weapons:
Stygian Desolator Unsure what drops it
Phase Blade Exarch's drop these

Armor:
Shadowfade Armor (Body) This comes in sleeves, boots, gloves, helmet, and pants as well. I'm not sure which boss drops the helmet, but I know the rest of the pieces can be dropped by Faithfuls, reverends, acolytes, windcallers, exarchs, and cultists(?).
Robe of the Faithful Dropped by Faithfuls
Acolyte Robe Dropped by Acolytes
Reverend Robe Dropped by Reverends
Windcaller Robe Dropped by Windcallers
Exarch Robe Dropped by Exarchs
Bracelet of Amplified Sound Dropped by Guardian of Tomorrow

Rings:
Bronze Ring of the Three First in a series, dropped by regular cultists and Faithfuls.
Silver Ring of the Three Dropped by Acolytes
Gold Ring of the Three Dropped by Reverends
Platinum Ring of the Three Dropped by Windcallers
Notum Ring of the Three Dropped by Exarchs
Bloodleech Ring Dropped by Guardian of Tomorrow

NCU Belts:
Guardian Circuit Board Dropped by Guardian of Tomorrow
Dark Memories Unsure what this is dropped by

That's all I can think of. Please add to this list and provide locations of dropped items/links of dropped items. thanks!

Other Drops
I spent quite a bit of time in the temple with my Engineer. I found out on one of my last few days in there, that there are 3 Platinum Rings, and 3 Notum rings. The normal Notum ring has Sense Imp and Psy Mod increase, and drops off Exarchs at times. The other rings are rarer, and I got both from killing the Curator repeatedly. They are rare drops. The Notum rings can be worn at the same time, since they are different items, so you can get boosts of 2 points in 4 different nano skills. The second ring adds +2 to MM and BioMet. The third ring adds +2 to MC and TS, which is perfect for pet classes, like my Engineer.

The Shadowfade helmet has dropped twice on Windcaller Donnel in the Demon room. The Guardian of Time drops Guardian Tank Armor, which I have seen drop 3 times, Guardian Circuit Board drops every time, and a Bracelet good for agents, which is not unique, so two can be worn. Memory Loop drops from Lien the Memorystalker, and is rare. Dark Memories is what usually drops. And since last patch, when you kill Lien, a horde of Legionaires come running down into the room, so it makes the fight quite tough. I got everything possible in the temple I believe, except the waist implant from the doorkeeper. I have also heard that Aztur the Immortal drops a book which is used by Doc's.
