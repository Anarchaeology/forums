The best thing MAs have going for them are:
Consistant Fast Damage
Brawl
Evades
Run Speed
Ability to attack while running

Bonus item are:
Heals
Bright Blue Cloud in the Sky
Dimach
Ability to Change Fist damage type

Strategies. This I kind of play by ear. The one thing to always keep your eye on, is your Agg/Def slider. Know when to go Full Agg, and when to go Full Def, and when to run it somewhere in between.

This tactic alone is why my Adventurer can take out most Soldiers at his level.

Very simple tactic is to go Full Agg if you take someone by surprise. As soon as you are rooted go full def and begin to heal while stille trying to move forward. Most of the times roots break while the graphic animation is still shown on you. When you get to your oppenet depending on what weapon he is using will determine whether or not to go Full Agg or about 10-15% Agg.

High Crit, low damage weapons means you 10-15% Agg, Good damage low crit weapons means you go balls to wall.

I know not many people use Bright Blue Cloud in the Sky, but do you realize that it "root/stuns" people? Man it's great on people who are kiteing you. The effect only lasts a second or 2, but I've noticed roots only last a hit or 2 on me anyway.

I normally save BBCITS for when a fixer gets down to 1/4 health. Because if I don't stun his ass, they ALWAYS encode themselves!!!

Use green speed to your advantage. Soldier puts up TMS. Go full def and heal, or run away. When you come back he will be gimped. :P

Stand on, or run past rooting classes. As soon as you attack a fixer, stand on top of him while fighting him. This way when he roots you and back up, you can still hit him 2-3 times before they get out of range. Then when the root breaks, do the same.

Snares are a different story. If you get snared, expect to loose the fight. You can't win them all.

Stay away from enforcers. I've yet to find a class good at killing enforcers.

Any class with less HPs than you, should be easy to take out. Even if they can root you.

There are so many ways to reduce or break roots in the game now, there is no excuse to be root/nuked to death. However, it is still possible for people to root and run. That's life.


If you fight at 10% Agg, you will likely take no crits, and there is a good change people will miss you alot.

(Sorry this isn't organized very well, I'm just typing as I recall PVP fights)


With all the people wearing tank armor these days, it's easy to see what kind of armor they are wearing. Know your armor, and adjust your fist damage type appropriatly.

Heals are nice, but they probably won't save your life. At least they are fast casting, so bust them out at the first time of trouble.

Dimach is a last resort, not a first one. If you know you are going to loose, don't waste it. If you know you are going to win, don't waste. However, if you may loose, and you and your targets health are quite both quite low, nano pool is running out, then use it.

Anyway, there are my tactics. They work great for me. In Mass PVP it's even better.

You can look for a fixer Kiteing someone else, and level him!
