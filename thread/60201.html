Agent Chat Bot
Destructiva contacted me today, and asked me to meet her at "The Cup" in West Athens. After a short meeting, she had convinced me.

We want to set up a chat bot for the agent profession, where people can chat, get information, news, hopefully use some of the useful tools that we will hopefully include in it, etc, etc...

I think the idea is great, not only will it be good for information and discussions, but it will also maybe bring our community closer together. And that can only be good IMO.

However, we need a programmer to make the bot. If anyone think they are up for it, please contact me [EDIT: "and Destructiva"], we would love to get this up and running ASAP.

Also feel free to leave ideas you have for the bot and such here
