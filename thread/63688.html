Sugestion : Moving Outdoor Boss Camps
I've not had as large of a problem with this at higher levels but for lower level players it becomes more of a hinderence then a help. Many of the old outdoor hunting grounds now have boss mobs walking around in them. All in all this sounds like it would be a good idea. In all actually though it isn't. Since the boss mobs agro it instantly pulls every type of that creature onto the helpless low level players who are not even trying to hunt the camp. The Rhino Cockpit in Newland Desert is a good example with the Minibull boss sitting right between the guards and the mobs. Now it is almost imposible to run from a mob and end up alive since you need to either run through the bosses area and pull more mobs onto yourself or run around the boss mob. Either way you just can't make it back to the guards in time to save that little bit of xp you just gained. The Oasis is another example of a boss causes some problems though the Brontos are more easily avoided due to the size of the oasis. Another problem is the boss camps that are right next to mission entrances. Again not as big a deal at higher levels though it can still be leathal. A posible solution that may be easier then moving the camps would be to not make boss mobs agro until lvl 40 or 50. By this level most players should be able to survive a runin with a camp while running away.

In the event the team broke up before it had formed, but just as I was leaving I noticed a low-level hare into the camp and be promptly cut down by the boss and his minions. The guy was too far away to even shout to, so there wasn't anything I could do.
