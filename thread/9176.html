There is a lot of roleplaying on Rubi-Ka, it just tends to be concentrated in small, isolated pockets rather than all pervasive. Also, it is worth mentioning that roleplaying in a mmorpg is very different from table top; better in some respect but a lot less flexible.

The South Rubi-Ka Society is an Omni-Tek rp guild based on Rubi-Ka 1. I guess it is fair to say we are more like a "gentleman's club" than a para-military organisation. You can find out more about the Society at our website http://rubika.no-ip.org/srs/srs.htm

If that does not suit you, there are plenty of other rp guilds. Its a shame there isn't a list anywhere. That would be a valuable resource.
