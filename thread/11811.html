I'll confine this to the ones I've actually been to:

Steps of Madness. Level 40ish in groups. Very easy to solo at lvl90ish. VERY. Neleb will put up a fuss, but you can do it. It's in Omni Forest. Look for the cave with the eyeball in it...

Biomere/Foreman's Office. Supposedly 50-70. Front half is much easier than the back half. Easy to solo at lvl90ish, BUT...try not to get mobbed and the bosses are still out of reach. Only thing green to me at lvl92 were the bodyguards. Lots of hit pts (LOTS), but just hit hard enough to wake you up. It's in The Longest Road. Large mining complex, second door on the right as you enter.

Cyborg Barracks. Stuff outside can be higher level than stuff inside, but mainly green to lvl92. 'Borgs don't hit too hard, but have two tons of hps each. Don't get mobbed. Very small dungeon, but kinda neat. I'd say it's probably groupable at the 60s-80s. It's in Greater Tir County, just northeast of Tir itself. Follow the green valley on your map until the box canyon at the end. 'Borg installation is right there. Watch out for wandering PFC 'borgs.

I was most impressed by Biomere. Very cool dungeon. Definately worth a trip just to see it. Very neat.
