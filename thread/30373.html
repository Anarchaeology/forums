Money is becomeing worthless
A game needs money sinks to have a viable economy.

You can put in all the fluff and glitter that costs 500 billion dollars, but if it dosen't actually do anything the player's are willing to pay 500 billion dollars for.....nobody is going to buy it.

I mean face it there is nothing after level 130 that money can buy. You EASILY make enough cash to buy ......what? nanoclusters and treatment kits? That dosen't come near to costing enough to make a proper money sink.

A good game economy will have a system whereby players are ALWAYS trying to get more credits, or gold, or whatever, because there is ALWAYS something they can buy with them. That's not been the case in any game so far except ultima online. It was true in UO because playermade was just as good at dropped, cost less in terms of time, and all items wore out eventually anyway.


None of that is true in AO. In order to make a viable economy, you must provide players something they can spend credits on, and a lot of them, all they can get infact.

In order to make camping still viable, you need to make it cost alot of credits....alot to the level at which the item is approite.

Well...What's a lot of money to a level 150? How about 50 million? That would make them pretty rare. How about selling de-cranium's mk2 for 50 mils each? Eh hell knock it down to 30 mils. People would _STILL_ camp the stuff becuase it's a lot faster.


The solution? Allow people to buy rares from machines, or merchants. Even the nodrop stuff. At prices that make camping for it more attractive. The vest of van horton? Make it cost X * Y * Z credits, where X is the number of players it takes to kill him, Y is the number of hours bewteen spawns, and Z is the chance he drops a vest.
A good place to start would be around 200 million crdedits. That would make it approitatly rare.

Ahem
I would be willing to bet that the vast bulk of the current playerbase has not yet broken 100 yet. I know I havent, and I've been here since release. I keep hearing about how easy it is to make really big money at the higher levels, but I've yet to see it.

I would like to point something out. AO has whats commonly known as a Free-Market Economy. That is, briefly, that folks will pay for item X whatever the market will bear. Nullility disk for 100 mil? Folks will pay it. Put it out at 200 mil and someone =will= buy it. Heck, I myself paid 10k for a silly Omni-Tek Suit (not the crat suit, the n00b-loot kind), because I thought it was cool and I wanted it. Is an AC 10 item worth 10k? Hell no! But that was what I was willing to pay to own one, and eventually I found a seller. Thats what a free-market economy is all about.

What you are suggesting is artificialy inflating what is already a fairly top-heavy system, by introducing outrageously expensive items that only a small fraction of people will be able to afford anyway. People on the lower end of the spectrum will jack up thier prices, so they they can afford the new Yalm superjet or what have you, and the spiral will grow worse instead of better.

The economy of Rubi-Ka is NOT broken. It is behaving exactly as a good free-market should. Folks pay what the market will bear, or they go without. A more viable soloution would be to fix tradeskills, so that playermade items are better by a healthy margin then what you can buy in the store. =Then= you'll see how a strong free-market economy really works.
