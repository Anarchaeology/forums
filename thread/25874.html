Team Missions are lame...
I think we all have our own horror stories regarding team missions, but this story that I had heard is a classic example of why team missions suck so badly.

The story goes like this. Some Fixer dude was looking for xp and decided to hook up with a team of people for a team mission or two. The mission went well up until the point where the group ran into the Plant boss mob. They engaged the boss mob and as the mob was about to die, the team leader switched the loot to leader only. Then the mob died and it was discovered that the boss dropped not one but TWO Grid Armor MK1 discs...

Did the team leader distribute the super-rare treasure to those who could use it? No, the sh!thead kept both of them for himself, likely selling them for XX million each.

Now the question I need to ask is: Why use team missions to distribute any form of rare or profession-balancing loot?

I've said this before and I'll say it again. The economy is busted.
Who cares if people do missions for battle suits? I sure the hell don't. The thing that breaks the economy the most is the sale of unique and rare treasures and in order to fix the economy these things need to be fixed.

Remove uniques from the game or at least make them nodrop, preferably they should be made nodrop WITHOUT advanced warning. This will take care of the problem of people camping the various mobs that drop things like Shades and Prof's Vest, since people will only camp them if the item is needed.

Place ALL loot that is currently team mission only, in the SOLO mission loot tables. The current system screws the solo player.

Place profession-balancing loot into SOLO mission reward tables. This will drasticly drag down the prices of things such as Grid Armor or Nullity Sphere since they will no longer be as super-rare and will only sell for huge prices to either twinks or the lazy.
