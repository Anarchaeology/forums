/notell option for the wish list.
Something AC had that became useful was a /notell option. It allowed you to turn tells off so that anyone who tried to /tell you would get a message back that said that person is not accepting tells, and you would see nothing.

I think this would be quite useful to most high level players who get harrassed for one thing or another (which is most professions) at times when you don't want to be (in a mission, camping a mission term, working on implants, whatever...).

Sometimes it's nice to get online and just have some time to yourself for a little while before you allow the flood of tells in, or be able to do things without interuptions for awhile.

buddy list...
For those who fear ending up on someone's buddy list, I have news for ya...

Anyone can put anyone else on their buddy list at any time, either from the chat client or while in game by simply typing /cc addbuddy <name> and you'll never even know you've been added.

They don't have to ever see you, talk to you, send you a /tell or even know your name. You can make names up and if someone exists with that name, the system will add the person to your buddy list.

Just a little fyi
