Grid armor was implemented to help balance the Fixer profession. It is not an item that can be bought in stores or found on mobs, like all other types of armor. This is a special piece of armor that requires specific nano skills (All of which are dark blue to a Fixer) to be summoned. Because the Nano skills are Dark Blue, it would take a Fixer 2 times the levels of the QL of the nano to get it on their own. On top of this difficulty, the nano is ultra rare. So in otherwords, by the time a Fixer has the ability to self summon the grid armor, the armor is comparable to someone half their current level. In order to get the armor summoned, Fixers get buffed up to the point of being able to summon the armor, which they then bank for use later on. Unfortunatly with the upcomming nerf, all Fixers with Grid Armor will need to be uber buffed in order to use their grid armor. Every time they use their armor, which removes their ability to bank them and use them whenever they need to, thus being semi self-reliant.

Here is a link to one of the Fixer Nano lists. All of the nano skills are dark blue for them, so they often focus on certain groups of nano skills, specifically the running and gridding nanos.

http://antiguardians.org/itemdb.php?...4&pagesize=500

You will notice that grid armor requires two skills to be extremly high (In regards to the Dark Blue Skill) that are not part of the 'useful' nano lines.

Matter Mettamorphisis and Matter Creation

You may point out that these two skills allow the Fixer to use the HOTs and bootleg line of nanos. Unfortunatly, the skill cost and investment of the nano skills is often to much for a Fixer to support and remain viable.

Now back to the subject. If the Fixer needs to have their nano skills maxed buffed in order to use their Grid Armor, AND they can be debuffed for 100s of skill points which would make the armor fall off, their class balancing enhancement (which isnt enough by far) is lost.

That should have alleviated any confusion you had in regards to what grid armor is any the difficulty it represents. To answer your question as to why grid armor shouldnt be treated like regular armor...

Name Summon Grid Armor Mk I
Description This temporarily creates a Multi-Spatial Phasing Beacon, which is used by fixers to enable them to exist both in Gridspace and the real world at the same time. The appearance of the fixer when in this form has lead to this device being called 'grid armor'. When being worn, the fixer evades most attacks, yet can still interact with the world as normal. The Beacon lasts for 2 hours.


You see? Its not armor at all. It is a phasing beacon that represents the Fixer in the real world while their physical body actually resides in gridspace.

http://antiguardians.org/itemdb.php?...no=1&id=155186

But... if it was armor. It can not be purchased in a store, or found on a beastie. The requirements to wear it are not like any other armor piece, they are instead going to be based on nano skills not attributes. Skills can be ransacked etc etc etc. Attributes can only be lowered by 20 or so. This armor only lasts for 2 hours, all other armor lasts for??? As long as you want...


Hope that clears it up for ya.
