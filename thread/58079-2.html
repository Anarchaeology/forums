While I see the point about nanomage not being the best choice for an MP (it isn't, it's always been atrox 'cuz MP's don't NEED high intelligence, so even the slight HP advantage atrox has was better than anything else), for an NT (who CAN'T buff their main skills) it's important to have as high intelligence as possible. The argument that you don't need a large nano-pool as an NT would be valid if our HE's/crunches worked as well for us as they do for others. THEY DON'T. Ack now I turned this into an NT whine, that wasn't my intention. Anyway... my point is nanopool isn't what's needed to "fix" the nanobreed due to the relative unimportance of nano vs HP.

If some kind of nano-regeneration is built into the nano-breed that'll (as far as I can see) turn out to be one of two things:
a) Too small to matter to anyone
b) So big it'll make nanomage healers even more overpowered in PvP, while infringing on the one thing that's unique to NT's.

I guess balancing nanomage is a bit more complex than balancing atrox, just don't think you can stick some more nanopool on it and everything will be "A OK". As someone said, nanomage is supposed to be genetically created to work with nanobots. That should IMO at least give them a CLEAR advantage compared to other races when it comes to nanoskills/nanopool/etc, while giving nanomage more nanopool is a step in the right direction it doesn't really come close to what the breed sacrifices in HP. The gains are simply too small and the disadvantages too big.

While a raised intelligence could alleviate this "problem", I think messing with breedcaps could get very messy. Clearly the 32 point difference in intelligence between nanomage and solitus (around 6 points to all nanoskills, 40 points total due to BM and MM being both based on psychic nanomages second strong point) doesn't balance out the HUGE dive you take in hp as a nanomage tho'.

Of course you can get your nanoskills more than 6-7 points higher than a solitus as nanomage, but this requires you to get ahold of shades of lucubration and expensive vest of professor jones. While both nice items they're heavily camped, often by people who only wants to sell them and are out of reach of alot of players. The fact that you lose out on 2 pieces of armor while using these, for a non-healer prof like NT for instance losing 280hp (MKII being the "standard" armor to use for a non-healer at 146+, while healers often use carbonium for more nanopool) isn't too nice when you're already severly disadvantaged on hp.

Now we go down the list to a couple examples of professions who don't benefit as much from a large nanopool.

Soldiers don't benefit as OMHH can't outrace the damage being done. His MK shield has a recharge probably as long as a first aid recharge. Large nanopool is way less important to a soldier than health pool is.

Just wanted to lay out some examples of how the Nano->Health conversion is greatly benefitted by a larger nanopool.
