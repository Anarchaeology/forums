Hollow Mountain = Wine
I still contend that What is now called hollow mountain is the last remaining renant of Wine.

On the map that came with the game, the word Wine is printed centered directly below the location of hollow mountain. You can see the shape of the hollow mountain on this map.

This name-centered-directly-below-location holds true for other major cities. Check out Omni-1, Athen and Newland; they all hold true.

This original map can still be found in scanned pieces on AO Basher.

Also note that the original hardcopy seemed to confuse the concepts of neutral areas and political zone...Harry Sheldon would be appauled...opps, wrong backstory.
