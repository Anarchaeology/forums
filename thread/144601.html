Token Boards, spies and reets, the price of Science!
RUR chaired the first meeting of scientists interested in working on developing Token Board Technology (TBT) for the Neutrals. It did not quite go to plan.

We tested our early, experimental prototype of a token board for neutrals Elite Operations president, Thebigguy and Omni-Mining president Swiftmind. It had the unforseen side effect of turning them both into reets for a while. Note for the future: do not use organisation presidents as test subjects, especially if one of them is your client and is actually paying for your expenses.

We put this incident behind us, and just when the conversation was getting suitably gee- erm technical, Evalissa, director of CTU spotted a Borpac spy from the "Neo Luddite Movement" among the delegates. The spy had cleverly disguesed his affilication sporting the org tag "Northern Loom Makers." The spy was captured and taken away for questioning.

Noholdsbarre closed the meeting singing some uplifting Neutral folks songs.

Obviously there is still a lot to be done, so thank to everyone who attended, and if you are still interested inthei project by were unable to attend, do show your support on the "Scientist! Researchers! Engineers!" thread.

Neutral folk songs? The mind boggles...

Sounds like Thebigguy has still got a few fragments of the morph's code running in his NCU. It happens from time to time, when a stray process gets orphaned as the buff cancels. It's especially common with code that's still in alpha.

Fortunately there's a simple solution. Running another morph should overwrite the problem code, and since the reet prog side-effect from the board isn't listed as currently running there should be no problems with superior/inferior nano checks (which take place at the application level. A quick wolf morph should fix the problem ... although to be on the safe side, I'd make sure you're outside when you do this. Psychological side effects can be funny things and Thebigguy has already shown an unusual susceptibility to them. There's no delicate way to put this, so I'll just come right out and say it - wolves aren't always housetrained.

Since wolves also have an uncomplicated attitude towards people they don't like, you might also take the precaution of morphing him in a 100% suppression zone. That should take care of the worst of his aggressive impulses while you let the buff run.

Glad to hear the project is meeting with some sort of success though. Keep up the good work.
