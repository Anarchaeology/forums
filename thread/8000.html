Amoeba Party -- Tuesday 1/21/02
There will be an Amoeba Party this Tuesday at 10pm EST. This is a sort of "end of the world" party, since the planet is on the brink of all-out war.

An Amoeba party is a travelling party that grows in size as it moves from bar to bar by collecting people on the way. It will begin at Reets Retreat, and end at Club Relax.

More detailed information can be found at http://www.darksphere.com/uor.

This party is sponsored by Unity of the Rose.
