Agent Dev Chat Info
We got 3 agent related questions answered this time. They all pretty much boil down to "agents will be the damage kings." Here is what was said:

Question 1

<gaute> Mattie: conceal has been broken or not functioning in some way since the game was released. will shadowlands finally fix the many issues with conceal? inquiring agents want to know!

<gaute> Concealment has been changed and improved several times since launch. There are some remaining issues, like Line of Sight outdoors and PvP use of concealment, to mention 2. There will not be any major overhaul of Concealment as part of Shadowlands.

<gaute> There will not be any major overhaul of Concealment as part of Shadowlands. There will be several bug-fixes going in, but I cant promise any changes to Concealment will be part of that. We shall continue to improve all the skills.

Question 2

<JimSalabim> Mattie: agents have been dying for info on what they can expect to see in shadowlands. namely how will our damage and and core skills be improved on?

<JimSalabim> Agents will have reason to be very happy with their damage output in the Shadowlands - they will be on top when it comes to Damage Per Minute. Skills in general will be possible to improve quite a bit, also Agent core skills.

Question 3

<blackmane> Salmak: since someone stated that fp will no longer be continued, could you give us some sort of an idea as to what your new vision for the agent profession is?

<blackmane> Damage, damage and more damage. The agent will be at the top of the damage range after SL.

<blackmane> No other ranged profession will come close, and only the MA will come anywhere close to it (and only when using fists).

<blackmane> The NT will be on the same level as the agent when using nukes (slightly above because they can't sustain it for long periods of time), but the agent is all about damage in SL.

Desired damage hierarchy (im sure he will love me quoting this part )

<blackmane> Bet this results in some nice flames, but...

<blackmane> It goes roughly in this order: NT (nukes), agent, MA (fist), shade, adventuer/soldier, keeper

<blackmane> The Shade will be relying on the special attacks they get from the perks to be dealing out a lot of their damage, and a MA using only weapons will be way down the damage-dealing food chain.

<blackmane> Those are the main damage-dealing professions, or at least those at the top.

there was other info as well, but more general in nature. you can read the dev chat logs for the rest, im sure the link will be posted soon.
