Thank you, Funcom!
Ok, in the spirit of keeping things constructive, I am going to start a thread on what the guys at Funcom have done right. This is not sarcastic, nor is is degenerative in nature. I simply want to let them know how well recieved their changes have been. So, let's start:

I liked how ya'll implemented the pvp half damage thing.

I liked how ya'll [seemingly] reduced chain healing mobs. At least it doesn't seem to be a problem, anymore.

I liked how ya'll shortened the distance for missions. (I can do "building" missions again!)

I like how every patch or so ya'll add rooms to the missions to keep variety in the level design.

I like how the tradeskill system is [developing]. We can tell you are trying.

I definitely like the plot so far.

Ok. I'll leave a few for the other posters. Remember: this is not for rants or requests. This is to reinforce what we want Funcom to keep in the game. Tell Funcom what they are doing right, and maybe they won't get pulled in fifty different directions.
