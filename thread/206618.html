Cypher
Alts have more fun
Jorji and Kiisu are explaining it perfectly for those people who know what it is "Meep" is a term that refers to the short-cast grid nanos like Emergency Vector Tap. These nanos will put the team in the grid almost instantaneously. This is used as an "evac" (when you or your team is in trouble, this can be used to send them straight to the grid). It is also used as transport at the end of the mission when people don't want to have to walk down through three floor and fly back out of Broken Shores to get to the grid.

The term "meep" indeed derives from the RoadRunner and Wile E. Coyote cartoons in America. When the RoadRunner is about to take off at super-high speeds (ie escape), he always says "Meep meep!," hence the origin of the term. Hope this clears things up!
