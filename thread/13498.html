An article to piss off all you engi's
I've played the game for about 6 months now (I think) and the game started out very different than it has grown into. The pet classes have grown REALLY strong. Not so much the Bureau or the Meta as the Engineer.

It's no secret that to be a killer engineer, you just need as many NCU's as possible. With a wrangle and those nice MP nano's, an engi can have a QL 139 bot at level 60, making max exp every time. This has done some things that I feel make the game less enjoyable.

I consider Enforcers to be the stongest characters....or at least, they should be, kinda like your big brother helping you beat up the bully after school. They are made to go in there and smash smash smash. When you have an engi in the team tho, it throws his role completely out of whack. The bot takes almost no damage and everyone ends up feeling pretty useless with the almighty force. At least when I was fighting with Enforcers the way they are meant to be, they still needed my help. I hate to say it, but either engi's need to be nerfed (complit or bot nano), or wrangles and mocham's needs to take up double the NCU to prevent robot abuse.
