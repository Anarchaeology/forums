Anything in your main inventory that wasn't there at the time of your last save is lootable from your corpse if you're killed by another player. You can get around this by putting stuff in a backpack that was in inventory (or worn) at your last save. However even then, the XP loss still hurts.

To my knowledge, the ferry works from 2HO to 4 Holes, but has had some issues regarding the return trip.

In any case, once arrived on the west bank, I'd recommend heading northwesterly to Aurum, and using the Wompah back south to Omni-1, Entertainment district.
From there, anyone that ought to have any business being in 2HO in the first place should be able to Grid back home easily.

Of course you could use the grid directly from Argentum in 4 Holes as well, but the 180 in Computer Literacy may be somewhat steep for the average fighter.

The Third option on the west bank is simply heading north to Borealis. However, this is a loooong trek. =)
