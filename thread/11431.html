I would say it is 'precisely' the mezzing that makes these dungeons a bit easier for any prof. that can mez. The Foreman's Dungeon is the same way.

I went in SoM solo the other night, just for a bit of fun and exploration. I made it about 2/3rds of the way to Neleb before i simply got overrun and died. I didn't expect to even get *that* far solo. However, I feel that if i had the ability to charm/mez/root adds, I would have been able to get to Neleb with little difficulty.
