Idea for PVP
Well I am new to AO, I came over from EQ. One thing I have noticed a lot, is talk about the PVP and how it is unbalanced and sofourth.

Many people have said that stats rule here and there is no real element of skill when fighting.

Well, as I read, I was thinking of a way that this could be corrected. (forgive me if this has already been suggested)

Why not just make it like a FPS when you go into a PVP zone. What I mean is this:

1) Allow players to move when fighting. (allow for auto targeting or not, up to the player and would depend on there skill level)

2) Make the mouse button become the fire button for your weapon, or attack in general. Pressing and holding it for autofire(if weapn allows it) or fast attack. What ever your primary weapon or attack is. Left click could be primary and right click secondary.
Basically have it so you can assign a weapon or skill or attributes to these buttons.

3) Booster icons could also be added. These would be like armor, heals or speed boosts in FPS, most have seen them. They would be invisible unless you were in a fight.(That would avoid just anyone picking them up)
Quick items could also be picked up. They could only be used in combat and would disapear from your inventory after the battle.

4) Team weapons is another idea here. Weapons that are used together for greater power. Standing close together, or even components.

Well thats my two cents worth. Not even sure if AO reads these things. I don't know I think it just might work. Although not sure how feesible it is...lol

I think that by switching to a FPS feel in PVP they could cure all there woes.
