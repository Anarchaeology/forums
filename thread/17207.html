Posted this in the NT forum too:

To make a good NT, you must choose your weapon carefully. In pvp its crits that does the work, not our nukes. From what I know, the former melee NTs are thinking of IP wipe and get a ranged weapon. At higher lvls such as 150 you can do a combo. First you fire off your specials from your ranged weapon, then you unequip and go MA and use dimach and brawl etc.

Honestly, I wouldnt have a chance without my flashpoint. But im doing IP wipe also, becuase even if I am able to kill ppl with it, I have no staying power, after I have fired off my specials, I need to wait 5-10 min for them to recycle. So im now going shotty, or dual shotty/pistol with as many specials as possible. Specials recycle much faster on fast weapons, flash is very slow.

All professions can be valid, but some got obvious advantages, for NTs the debuffers are killers, soldiers too. Profs with devestating debuffs is: Docs, Traders and MPs. But its possible to kill them, but its really hard, and you need some luck, unless of course you are much higher lvl. But even then those, specially traders and docs can be very difficult becuase of their healing.

I would say NTs is one of the last to be killed, or at least, we are looked upon as a small threat. It depends really, in large battles in 25% zones the NTs thrives very well, becuase of our AE nukes.

Root is a very important nano. Learn to use it against meleers, I havent tried this becuase of my slow Flashpoint, but I htink vs meleers you can just concentrate on rooting them to keep them from hitting you, while your ranged gun hits them for dmg.

NT's are very tough to PvP with... I've rarely had fun doing it and tend to play my lil NT butt outta poli zones asap.

But if you want to...

1) get a fast shooting gun (pistols)
2) get a gun with a good crit (shotguns)
3) make sure you have an action bar with 'backup' low lvl attacks for when you get debuffed
4) Use a fast shooting nuke, not your big ones
5) Use 'Darkmovement' as soon as you can
6) calm/root pets
7) root melee chars, but don't waste you time rooting other ranged chars, you can't out range them (that's been nerfed)
8) wear anything that raises your HP (imps, special items, SA Home Defenders)
9) Do what everyone else does (high evades, beg other chars for their buffs, make sure you know where the zones are, debuff peeps all you can, know what other profs are good at and couter it, over eqiup, twink, blah blah blah...)
10) and you can always be a 'hit-from-behind-grid-camping-ganker-who-only-attacks-people-20-lvls-below-them' if you are into that kind of stuff. I'm not, I know NT's can't go toe to toe with other profs, but I won't attack anyone unless it's obviouse they are looking for a fight.
