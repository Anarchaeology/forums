"Why go through all the trouble of making a chrystal from scratch, when you can buy it in ANY gen. store at the fraction of the cost?!?"

well, it depends on the ql of the crystal. obviously building ql12 crystals is not really worth the effort, but for a ql90 crystal, ur only buying two ql23 parts and one ql45 part. as the ql goes up, the price of building tends to be in the neighborhood of 10% of the store price, which certainly makes it worth it, obviously more so if we're talking about ql126+.

if ur referring to the initial outlay of creds for the three tools u need, sure 2 millionish is pricey, but u pay it once, keep the tools forever ( provided u bought sufficiently high ql tools in the first place ), and can easily make back the money if u dedicate a little time to rock and disc hunting. for me, selling a couple disc-only nanos paid for the tools in one shot.
