An idea I had for the Engy/14.2 problem
Now, we all (I hope) know that in the 14.2 patch when a pet is i use and your skills drops under that required to create the pet that that pet will finish fighting whatever it is fighting and go into "behind" mode. This has been seen as unfair by pet classes, as weapons will only lose 25%, but pets will loose 100% efficiency. Now, as mainly an MP, I don't really have a problem with this, as I can easily keep myself buffed into a deep red pet at all times. Thus, MPs are not, compared to Engineers, very effected by this change. Engineers, however, do not have the buffs of the MP, and must find an MP if they want to be near the equal of an MP in combat. An engineer, will, thus, be stuck with a pet that they can get to using only the general buffs, while a MP, if he can get atleast teachings, will be able to get a deep red and recast those buffs as long as he wants it.

My idea, is, to allow classes to create something along the lines of what would be called a "scroll" in a fantasy game. Maybe call them "Fast Loading Nano Program Discs" or something. You would have to buy an empty disc near the QL of the nano you wish to put into one, they would cost a lot more nano than the spell being cast, would cost XP, and would have a limit of how many may be made in a certain amount of time. These would be limited to non-offensive nanos. This would allow Engineers or whoever to bring along an extra incase the first buff runs out, although they would probably cost a fair amount of creds. This could also be a nice thing for Traders and wrangles, although I have no idea how wrangles work and won't pretend to. What do you think of this idea? I think it would add a nice bit to the economy and, if implimented properly, not create a lot of balance issues, after all, it seems every class has a few nice buffs that could be sold.
