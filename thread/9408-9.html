I don't "hate" the thread
I just see it as a waste of resources that could be better used to fix other problems.

I figure just about anything new added to the game except for items in the DB is going to take a significant amount of time and resources, particularly a visual change the caliber to which you are proposing. Even adding a new item is more work then one thinks as there are loot tables and drop tables that are going to need to be updated and then balanced in order to make sure that things are reasonably dropped for their power level.

Adding a sheathing maneuver is like asking them to add two more doors to your sports car while they change its oil. Its not something easily done while working on something else and its going to have to be its own project and effort which would exist seperate from any other thing they are working on. Thus you'll have them putting effort into this and neglecting some other more pressing and important issue. That's why the continuation of this thread bugs...its because you are in effect of keeping this thing alive are asking them to put aside other issues and put in this change because it'd look cool.

For weapons that sheath on legs, you'd have to create the visuals for the holster's then the make sure that the visuals for the guns fit in the holsters, now you need to make sure that during all the various motions of the character the guns don't poke through the exterior of the holsters. Then you need to create an animation to "draw" the weapon from its spot. Then one to "sheath" them. If they want it to be just a visual thing its a lot easier, but if they want to institute checks to see if the guns are actively drawn or need to be, then that's going to complicate things. What are you going to do if you're wearing an item on your back like a backpack or tank armor? Where are you going to sheath that 2 hander on your back on top of the backpack or tank armor? How's that going to interact with the BP or TA? If you just stick the item to the back of the character model then you're going to see the weapon stick through the BP or TA and look weird. If you want to disallow sheathing in such a case, well now you need to institute a check for back items that prohibit weapon sheathing. This would require you to add some sort of tag to all back items that would specify if they interfere with weapons sheathed on the back.

I hope that the above would give you some idea that this change isn't some simple task that can be done while they balance PvP or reduce rubberbanding and such, but instead its a major undertaking that will take a considerable amount of work. Most of the tasks that need to be done in the game have nothing to do with visual things like sheathing weapons, so its not like they can add this while fixing an existing visual character model bug.

This thread has existed for the better portion of a year, it even got a response from Cosmik when he worked at FC. It HAS been seen, there is no need to keep bumping it.....

Please
