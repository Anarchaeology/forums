Potential!
I think that AO has lots of great potential. I have just signed up for my free month and I am trying it out. At the end of my month I will decide if AO is worth throwing away all my hard work on UO and cancel my UO for my AO acct. But theres a few things that could be changed, and they made the same horrible mistake as level-quest or other known as Ever Quest. Combat is nothing more than click and watch. When you attack someone, there is no running unless you decide to tuck tail and run for a server boundry and hope you take the damage your about to be dealt and live. The fate of the battle is normally decided on nothing more than computer calculated randoms and the sheer lvl and quality of your character. The combat in AO and EQ has no style, no real player skills required, the Ranges on the Guns are very in-realistic and you can be hit with melee damage from a pretty nice distance. They need to fix the distance required to attack with melee damage and make it smaller. On the other hand sniper rifles and larger caliber guns need to beable to shoot farther. Realisticly any gun can shoot farther than the human eye can see, how accuratly is the question. But on AO ive noticed that my target needed to be quite close for me to beable to shoot him, sometimes even too close. Nightvision is a great thing.. but it serves no purpose for you can most of the time see your guns range in any weather condition. When I was reading the book on the way home I saw the advertisement in the book for the Disafiliation Sniper Rifle. It mentioned picking off cowboys from miles away. But my disafiliation snipe rifle cant shoot farther than I can throw a football. I can learn to deal with click and watch combat for great terrain and interesting equipment and upgrades, but when I buy a rifle or a sub-machine gun, Id like to beable to experience some "good ol' flank tactics, shoot'em across the river, you never saw it coming, gun fights." To meet it seems more like standing next to my target while I can only enjoy the sound of my rifle and never get to use its proclaimed "picking off" ability. I hate ending up face to face with some club weilding retard, seems my sniper rifles range factor is nothing more than the distance I can provoke monsters from. Well thats my speal about weapon range and how guns have been way overlooked. It just reminds me of being on everquest and those days Ive spent duking it out with gnolls face to face, now that I got a gun I wanna use it like a gun!
