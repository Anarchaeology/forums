The time tested Omni-Inquisition is recruiting for a short time only.
Omni-Inquisition has re-opened recruitment for a brief period.
We are looking for more dedicated players who are trustworthy, value friendship and above all like to have fun in other areas than the level treadmill.

The majority of our highly active members are above level 150+, however we have put a minimum level requirement of 75 for this recruitment period.

The basic requirements all applicants should fulfil are: -

*Must be level 75+

*Your main char must be in OI and you must not be a member of any other org.

*You must be a team player, willing to help guild members out and share resources with each other.

*Guild loot is shared amongst us freely; you should be wiling to give your spare items to the guild members who need them.

*You will be expected to help out with events, participate in them and promote them.

- Also please note that an interview will be required upon a successful application.

About Omni-Inquisition: -

Omni-Inquisition was founded over 1 year ago, roughly 1 week after release of AO. We specialise in being the gap between PvP and RP, a guild formed around friendship and loyalty.
We continue to stay strong through the changes in AO due to our strict policies and outlook on trust, friendship and decency.

We enjoy PvP events with our sister guild Neo-Core which shares our joint forum with us.

For further information or to apply: -

Check out our website @ www.Omni-Inquisition.net where you can apply online.

You can also get in contact with us on the Inquisitions IRC channel on #omni-inquisition at the funcom IRC server.



Best regards
