Some stray thoughts on this...

I've had a lot of AO characters (since beta4), and finally, a week or so ago, I bit the bullet and levelled one past 74.

I never quite liked the idea of me being fair-game to a level 200 character at level 75. In fact, I really don't understand it. But that's not the reason why I have avoided levelling in AO -- I've done that because of the lack of high-level content and because the game is just more fun at lower levels. That's a fact, straight from the mouths of many high level characters who I know and play with. I've wanted to avoid the crap that Kopo and many others have dealt with for months and months.

I don't PvP. Never have, and probably never will. I *personally* think it's meaningless and I don't really find the idea of serious twinking to be that much fun, either. At level 75, if I spent a few weeks twinking my character to absurity, I *may* survive against somebody level 150+.

But even if I manage to squeeze into QL200 implants (boy, wouldn't that be fun!), and equip the uberest weapon and use the highest level MP pets, well -- I still might lose. But this is what you must do to be a good PvPer in AO. Not right, but that's the way it is right now.

I don't really know what Funcom intended with the level 75+ PvP thing. Like Meomy said, we're never told how the game is supposed to work. Did Funcom expect Level 75ish characters to be decked out in full QL200 armor/implants/weapons/etc. and thus be a match for a level 200 character?

And with the proposed over-equipping changes, those in the first stage of post-level 75 will be even more at risk when they choose to fight in low gas, gank-prone areas. Not quite sure how it's going to work, but I think it may widen the gap a bit.

Hm, I've rambled. In conclusion -- ganking sucks, two wrongs don't make a right ("clanners did this to us in the Ace camp!"), but some people just want their titles. I'm friends with some of them and I still don't get it. Oh well.
