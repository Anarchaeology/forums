Ah, evades. A bit about that topic:

The actual math was supposed to be that evades worked on: Evade = Att skill of attacker, then there's a 1/4 chance we'll evade the blow.

At medium agg/def.

Ok. So:

-3 evade skills to raise
-MA is I think the only class that has 5 growth/green cost
-Said evade skills all rely on a several base attribs (Agil, Int, Sense, and Psy as well I think?) - therefore we would have to max all of those stats every level to get a 1-point increase over the 5 growth rate already assigned to our evades


Alright, on the other hand let's take a few random examples from other places - for pure example, a longmoon/enforcer combo:

-5 growthrate/green cost for an enforcer under 2HE (meaning equal growth level, in absolute worst case)

-2 base stats, incidentally Str/Stam, coinciding nicely with armor selection and giving full incentive to max these stats each level (which ends up awarding another point to 2HE each level they do max said stats)

-ONE and only one attack skill - 2HE. Easily 100% implantable with every level cluster for that skill. About a +138 for ql130 implants.

-Buffable by gen (+20) and challenger line - you may or may not count challenger line at your option, but the higher-end ones add a good 150ish for a short period of time.

-Essence lines intrisnically add a nice +5 or so to this skill by base stat bonuses.

So, the 2HE skill starts at a 5 growth rate, same as ours. The base stats are simpler and regularly raised - an extra 1 point a level. I belive if an MA were choosing the Agil/Sense path, they would achive 70% of an evade point by maxing those two stats each level. The 2HE can be implanted in a snap, and can also be sub-augmented by implanting Str and Stam respectively. Then, it can be buffed by gen +20 and challenger if you desire to count that.

Evades on the other hand take up 7 spots in your implant set to implant - and you can't implant all 3 evasion skills, you must pick and choose. Because they don't rely on simple base attribs, you can't expect to implant the base attribs they use and get any signifigant return at all. Heh, amusingly enough these evade clusters take very important spots for us MA's - faded brawling, shining agility, and bright MA skill all consumed by evades in the first place. Buff wise - MA's have profession evade buffs - about 128 points at level 85 or so from Fleet Foot. This buff replaces the +50 evade buff from before, does not stack with it or other profession's evade buffs. Ironically, it doesn't even stack with the fixer's grid running nanos.

Now, you look at the ease at which an enforcer can jack up his 2HE - I don't really complain at all. He's an enforcer, he's the god of melee, and that's how it SHOULD be. I can do the damn same thing with my MA skill - it has 3 req attribs, I get good buffs for it, and I can implant that single skill quite nicely as well.

Trader? 5/green shottie, and only nanomage breed can't raise the requisite Agil/Str like mad for that extra 1pt-per-level increase. An enforcer using 2HB has a growthrate of 4 - but gets headcracker in replacement, and also an easy str/stam dependency.

So now we have evades which require you to invest in 3 skills - don't grow as fast as general attack skills do, have many dependent stats, are DIFFICULT at best to implant up to high levels, have no stacking buffs WHATSOEVER, and require you to leave agg/def at center just to get 25% hits evades if they have the same att rating as your evades.

Mobs use fake attack stats, period. That 25% evasion rate will never come into effect because they're utterly FAKE. They're not evades, they're "crit reducers".

Player versus player is the only place in which evades can reach close to the attack rating being hit against them - but to do that, the person attacking must likely be much lower level so his strongly raised att rating stays on par with the other player's evasion skill - and what will the person get in reward? 1/4th of the shots a lower-level person will miss.

Middle agg-def by the way, adds 1 sec to the att and recharge time of the weapons any MA is using. If he's fist only, he can outstrip this soon as he gets 375 phys init. If he's using a wall blade, he'll need 900/375 melee/phys init. If he's using a taichi, he'll need 480/375 melee/phys init. If he's using a hammer, he'd need 1200/375 melee/phys init to cap it's speed at that setting - and supposing this person didn't mind the recharge rate on hammers, which doesn't interfere with fist punching, he might only shoot to cap the att time at one second - that'll be 900 melee init at medium agg/def please!

And the irony after all that? His heals are now a most godawfully slow to cast nanos that they are unreasonable at best to use with that agg/def. The cast time for our heals can get cut down with a nice 200+ NanoC init, but the after time gets horribly extended from that agg/def setting - and ironically NanoC init takes the majority of the time out of the casting time, which means we'd have to raise NanoC init massively to cut the backend off the heals. Hey, if you want to jack your dark blue Nano init to 400 at level 75, be my guest.

The summary of all that is that evades are not evades, they're 'crit-reducers' and nothing more. Our 5/green evades are nice later, when we have IP just spewing from every orfice. As it stands however, maxing evades doesn't make us evade much at all. Specializing isn't very functional as FunCom seemed to like randomly assigning Duck-Exp to things like Flamethrowers, Beams, and shotties (shotties I understand) - so you MUST raise all 3, period. And agg/def? Yeah right.

Evade, this is one of the most serious flow in the game engine.
Coupled with how speed and AC works it's the reason most fast weapons in game are not viable.

For example a ql 200 fists:

http://www.hackersquest.org/ao/show....d=43713&item=1

And ql 200 rider warblade:

http://www.hackersquest.org/ao/show....=128994&item=1

now let's just say both have 600 initiative so that the fist is hitting for 1.0/1.0 and the rider is hitting for 1.5/1.5. And the fists user take advantage of the extra initiative and put his agg/def bar at [---------+-I---------] to take advantage of the evades.

now assume both has 1000 attack rating, which I remember somewhere funcom stated you will do 2.5 times more damage (or, because I said so) with such attack rating. So:

Fists damage:
(45-215)*3.5 = 157.5-752.5

Rider damage:
(12-554)*3.5 = 42-1939

Now factoring AC, assuming both sides has 4k AC, which will result in 400 damage reduction. so:

Fists damage:
157.5-(752.5-400) = 157.5-352.5

Rider damage:
42-(1939-400) = 42-1539

Now we can calculate damage per seconds (This is w/o taking critical damage into account, but then rider can critic higher....)

Average damage of fists per second:
(157.5 + 352.5)/2(average)/2(1.0/1.0 = 2 seconds) = 127.35/s

Average damage of rider per second:
(42+1539)/2(average)/3(1.5/1.5 = 3 seconds) = 263.5

And remember the higher the ac, the higher the % differences

So you see, a rider more than double the fist damage in average
(let's not talk about hammer, it would be really sad to do so). and unless that [---------+-I---------] thingy can enable the MA to dodge half of rider's attacks, he is not going to last. And we all know evades doesnt work that well.

So what' the problem?

1. Funcom grossly overestimates the important of speed

2. Funcom grossly underestimates the important of evades

3. Math, some liner addition/reduction(like AC) coupled with some multiplier(skills, how initiative double some slow weapons attack rate) are garanteed to have problems.
