When your RI is > 600-800, this is true.

Until then, Sol Chironis is a damn fine choice, and can be scoped pretty well.

The LS/Sleek series are... well, I don't feel they're viable choices until later on for fixers, as we're already IP tight, but they're pretty good guns I'm told. Dunno how they'd work for crats, where you don't have our SMG buffs to think about (using the LS makes your SMG buffs less effective b/c it only gives a 2/3 bonus to AR). If you're set on RE, go with the Light Supps. If you're insisting on MR, go with the Light Supp.

Otherwise, go Sol Chironis until your RI is very high, then go MCS.
