Decus Coats vs Tank Armor
Take a look around Rubi-ka, and nearly everyone has coats on. Hardly anyone wears tank armor... and can you blame them?

Can anyone give me a good reason why, that with lower requirements to equip, Decus coats give protection better than or equivalent to tank armor and have zero penalties?

What's the purpose of tank armor, if nobody other than the terminally insane (and fashion victims) wears it?

No other "coat" line carries the penalties of tank armor... nano armor cloaks don't, biomech armor cloaks don't... why is the str/sta line singled out for this punishment?

Here's the stats of ql200 decus and tank to compare (yes, different types give slightly different protections, but this serves the point):

code:
ql200 Hanshi Primus Decus Armor Coat    ql200 High-Quality Heavy Tank Armor

To wear:                                To wear:
Agility  546                            Stamina  576
Strength 496                            Strength 526 

Modifies:                               Modifies:
Melee AC      1000                      Melee AC      1000
Energy AC     1000                      Energy AC     1000
Projectile AC 1000                      Projectile AC 1000
Poison AC      875                      Poison AC     250
Fire AC        875                      Fire AC       875
Cold AC        875                      Cold AC       750
Chemical AC    625                      Chemical AC   250
Radiation AC   250                      Radiation AC  250 
                                        Nano Cost    +100%
                                        Run Speed    -50
                                        Duck         -50
                                        Dodge        -50
                                        Evade        -50
Seems a little silly, surely? Double the cost of all nano programs, lowered evades and run speed? Why?

Funcom, please give the str/sta professions back the choice of a 2nd layer armor that makes sense to wear.

Take the penalties off tank armor, please.
