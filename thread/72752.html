Ring Of the Nucleus basalis (MAKE UNIQUE !)
I would Please ask that you make this ring Unique. For one....there are many low level players that are new to the game and are playing in the new subway area but when they want to try and get this item they can't because the spawn is soo heavily contested by high levels Camping the mob getting Ring after Ring after ring to sell for 10+ Mill. It is sad when tons of High levels who want this ring go in to ruin a game for new players because they can twink their character up soo much that they can almost assure themselves that they will beat out everyone else to get this item.

Before anyone starts hounding me on this ( HIGH LEVELS ) I myself play a ATM 98 Agent so I cannot get into this dungeon, but I do have an alt that I could go in and get it, but after reading the frustration that alot of new players are feeling in this area I am choosing not to.

Making something like this Unique will NOT HARM ANYONE....except those farming trying to line their pockets.

Think about the impact on someone just coming into the game..only to know that an item in a area that is restricted and was made just for the low level people is camped with nothing but twinks and they have no chance of getting one of these great items themselves. It will really screw them up for the mindset that they will not be able to get anything in this game worth having as there will always be someone that has a twink or is farming the item from them for the duration.....the frustration level would be intense.

Yes I know this is a common occurance in all MMORPGs....hell I witnessed it in EQ. But some things just get out of control, and this is a solution to the problem.

Flame Away !
