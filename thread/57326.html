Couple of minor statics
Here are a couple I've stumbled across...don't have the coords here at the moment, but do have general areas.

Below lvl 20 (I think) statics:

- At least one in Ent Sewers...enter by trying to run into a drainpipe. Dangerous to get in there though, you have to run through toxic waste.

- Just east of the ferry at Harry's. If you take the ferry from Trade, it's straight east basically (use bridge to the north of ferry).

Lvl 60-70ish:

- One almost straight south of 20K. You run pretty near it on your way to/from the Bothackers.

- A cluster of them at what looks like an old mine along the north edge of Clon**** (east of central if IIRC).
