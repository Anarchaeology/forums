Warning! Major Spoilers Ahead!





















Quest: Dealing With AO Customer Service

Requirements: Depends. This is a DYNAMIC quest that allows for multiple paths to completion.

You gain your quest by finding something about the game of AO that bugs you.

Here, you have two choices. Send customer service an email or post on forums.

If you picked send customer service an email, scroll down to section 2. If not, continue.

After posting with your concerns, two possible things can occur. You get an official reply, or you don't. If you got an official reply, scroll down to section 3. If not, continue.

After a couple weeks, and hundreds of other posts similar to yours, you finnaly get an official reply. Scroll down to section 3.

SECTION 2:

After having sent an email to customer service, you recieve nothing but automated replies for two weeks. This changes you in bizzare ways and you find yourself chewing on your rug at odd times at night. Thus ends your quest. I hope it was fun.

SECTION 3:

Upon reading the official reply, you learn any of the following: It is being looked into. It is not an issue. You are not posting in the correct forum/thread. You now have two choices. Give up every getting a reply with content to it, or go postal and sign up for marksmanship classes with the nearest NRA branch. If you give up, then you've ended your quest. I hope you enjoyed it. If not, continue.

You go to sleep that night with grim resolve to not give up on your issue(s). At 2 p.m. stealth ninjas enter your room and poke your eyeballs out and cut your fingers off. 'Haha,' they say. 'I bet he'll think it's resolved now!'

You are now maimed and crippled for life. It takes 9 years of extensive therapy for you to resort to anywhere near a normal mentality.

The end.
