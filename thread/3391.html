A possible fix to Super Over-equiping
Just an idea on how to fix super over-equiping, ( and I know that I'll be flamed for this one),

After playing the game from day one till now.... I've noticed alot of new characters out there with super stuff there is no way they should be possible to get into and use. this includes a 25 atrox MA in Organic armour, and tank on top. not sure the ql's but from what I'm told these types start at about ql50, sounds extreme to me.

So here's my idea to fix it. (Brace your selves, The DEV's might listen to this one..... ) A person can only give buffs to a person they could possibly group with and recieve exp. this gives aprox 20 level range at level 20....or so. and the gap get's bigger at higher levels. This would stop the Uber twinking and in my opinion exploiting, while at the same time allowing for a reasonable amout of over equiping, with self buffs, and other's specialized.

I think this idea is a fair solution..... but hey I put it on here to see response....
