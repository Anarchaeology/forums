My theory as to why FC is releasing the booster pack instead of Shadowlands this christmas season... Pure speculation mind you...

From a pure marketing point it makes perfect sense to push Shadowlands until 2003 and release the booster pack instead and schedule that for a christmas release.
By doing this they will still get a much needed influx of money and keeping a presence on the market with a box in shops. By adding the game client to the box FC will also be able to claim an increase in the sales of the actual game. Sneaky sneaky...

The reason for pushing Shadowlands back is simply that SWG is also being delayed from the original launch date. FC sees SWG as their main competitor and Shadowlands is AO's counter to SWG's launch. If SWG had been released at christmas like it was supposed to, Shadowlands would have been out there as well, trust me...
On the flipside is that FC will now have the opportunity to really polish Shadowlands until it shines and release it as a very well put together expansion, hopefully.

Unfortunately the booster pack will mean that patches will be fewer and will contain less actual fixes, 14.5 for example seem to contain more additions of possible bugs than actual fixes for old ones. So, content is more of a priority now than fixes it seems...

All this is sound marketing strategy from the "marketeers" point of view but a lot of existing players feels like they are being shafted.
