I think the MA patch is a good idea, and i think that they should come first because they will be thoroughly enjoyable with a better damage output (btw i have an uber MA so i know somewhat about the subject). Maybe they wont be as good overall as the MP, and surely the special attacks and MA weapons need to be fixed, but overall the MA class is pretty cool right now, and these things can wait to be fixed as they will really be a bonus for any MA who didnt nerf himself in the first place.
Same goes for agents. I have some agent friends and i certainly dont want to prevent them from gettin their meager FC boosts where they can, but i know several agents who can sneak just fine if not overly-well, and aimed shot is sooo uber that many other classes use it even tho it is dark blue to them. I'm not sayin "Nerf it!", im just sayin...get a grip.
How i judge a class to be needy of a patch is whether or not they can successfully do what they were meant to do, and if they have at least something unique to add to a group or organization. As an adven i sadly rely on my clan to keep me on par with the rest of rk2, and it is a testament to them that im still here.
On the whole i'm sure there will always be replys of "U dont know what ur talkin about", and i guess that is to some extent true, because i havent taken EVERY class to lvl 100+ so i dont really know how it is for them, but in the few classes i have played i must say that being an adven was infinitely harder EVERY STEP OF THE WAY.

1) seen a lot of high level advens lately?
2) has an adven ever been responsible for the uberness of ur team after lvl 80?
3) when teaming, what proffessions do u look to recruit first, and last?

1) Doctor - c'mon, if yer uber and u know it clap yer hands
