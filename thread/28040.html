Make event chars POSSIBLE to kill!
Ok, I just got finished participating in an event in newland. I am not whining because I was killed, I expected to be given the level of the NPC. I AM pissed because said NPC:

1. Did not REMOTELY conform to the laws under which players operate. She was able to hit people for gigantic amounts of "unknown" damage with no casting time whatsoever (at least none that was apparant). She withstood damage that no level 200 PC could possibly take. If you want to make NPC event chars extra tough, give them extra levels if u must. But this is just dumb.

2. When the mob of PCs finally whittled her down, she WARPED AWAY. This is what pisses me off the most. After all those players fought, died, came back, fought, died, etc, some losing titles in the process, their victory was stolen from them.

3. People lost titles to this NPC. I was not among them, having no title to lose. However, I sympathise with their frustration with this. If NPC event charecters are not going to conform to the rules that players do, then it is horribly unfair to take these titles from them in this way.

I came back to this game recently, as I heard positive changes had been made. I hope that this does not become the "standard" event for this game, I hope I don't regret my desicion to give this game a second chance.
