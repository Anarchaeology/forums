how nanoresist works:

player A on 50% AGG/DEF
player B on 50% AGG/DEF

player A has exactly 800 nanoskill-based ATK
player B has exactly 800 nanoresist skill

player A casts 100% resist nano on player B
player B has a 25% chance of countering

IF AMS = DMS, attack has 75% chance of success.

problem is, there are so many variables,
(AGG/DEF settings, nanos with different resist rates,
uneven attack and defence skills, random dice rolls!)
that people come up with lots of confusing "results".
