Ruse of Taren, a good idea for a fix?
I was just thinking of something the other day I played my Agent. I NEVER use Ruse of Taren, since I use a bow with no Aimed. And I do seldom sneak missions, I love to do them instead. So I thought about it while buffing, what is the point of those stupid buffs then? Well how about fixing it?

This is a logical solution I found out who WILL make sense, and will help Agents greatly, and I would love Funcom to try improve the Agents with this added Bonus.

If you do a Ruse of Taren, you reduce your size. If you reduce your size to a certain point, you will be much, much harder to hit. Now a lot of you already guessed it, you increase your evades pretty much. What I mean, make the Ruse a combined Scale/Conseal/Evadebuff, giving the Agents some REAL use for the Ruse in combat, not only in sneaking and Aimed.

What do you say people? I hope I get some Agents with me now, because we would really need a little "Extra". (Those 5-seconds critbuffs help us nothing really...)
