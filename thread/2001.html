Is it just me?
I'm not happy with this game.

While the bugs and nerfs and so on are annoying/frustrating/maddening, the biggest problem I have is that it is not living up to what it was promised to be.

Supposedly this was going to be a game about the conflict between Omni-tek and the rebel clans, and this was going to go on ingame and individual player actions would shape it.

I don't see any of this happening. What I see are some movie clips and book chapters on a website. In the game itself, it's all about gaining levels so you can equip higher level equipment so you can fight higher level monsters so you can gain more levels.

In other words, it's a game about levelling up, with some bits of Clan/Omnitek conflict as decorations. Do a mission, if you're clan, you fight "Daft OT Clerk", whereas if you're Omni, you fight "Daft Clan Clerk".

Pretty much every player who has actually made it to level 200 has quit or sold their account, because they had nothing left to do.

I myself decided to stop gaining levels, originally because of many annoying bugs which plagued me when I was grouping as an agent. Instead, I focused for a while on upgrading my equipment. When I was done with this, I tried PVP, and found that to be totally lame, because even though I was very successful at it with all my maxxed equipment, it was still pointless and boring.

As of now, I can't think of any reason to gain any more levels, as I've already got plenty of money, a yamalha, and I can go anywhere in the world and do most anything that anyone else can do except fight high level monsters. I have no interest in levelling up anymore, and so now I've come to the same realization that the level 200 players all came to, that there is nothing else to do.

Am I the only one who feels this way? Or are the majority of players happy with the game as it is? Did you just want a game about fighting in groups and doing missions and gaining levels?

To me, this game is an empty shell. It was supposed be filled with the story which would go on in the game and which we could participate in, but this filling was never included. And month after month go by, and we keep being told "big things are coming, just wait!", and we keep waiting, and they don't come.

I have some more thoughts on this.

The game doesn't work as a role playing game, really, because without the story going on ingame roleplaying seems pointless.

So I have been saying that this game is really just a game about levelling up.

What I'm realizing is that the game DOESN'T WORK AS A LEVELLING UP GAME EITHER.

That is, once you get to be a mid level player, there's no point in gaining any more levels as you've already seen and done everything and you have everything, for the most part.

When you're lower level, there are many new things to do, new places to explore. You get your first Total Mirror Shield, or your first polymorph, or your first token belt, and it's fun to try all these new things, and you always seem to have more to do and more to look forward to.

Then you get to be a mid level player, and you're trying to save up money for your yamalha.

You eventually get the money, buy a yamalha, and now you can travel the world and go everywhere, do missions everywhere, which is great for a while.

But now, what else is there to do? What incentives are there to gain any more levels?

Are there any cool places to go to which only high level players can get to? No.

Are there any cool items which only high level players can use? No. The yamalha is it.

Cool nanos which only high level players can cast? Well, a couple, for a few professions, but certainly not enough to make you want to play for months to get them. Adventurers eventually get their flying reet form, but by then they have a yamalha anyway.

The high level game was poorly thought out, it offers nothing to players that they can't already get by the time they're mid level, so there becomes no real incentive to gain any more levels once you reach a certain point.

This is especially true if you're interested in PVP. Once you hit level 75, you suddenly have to fight everyone up to level 200 in PVP, so why would you want to become level 75?

I could accept the fact that the story didn't exist in the game, if at least I had other things to look forward to. If there were new areas to go to, new cities, new lands. New cool items, I have no idea what they'd be.

How about an ocean at one edge of the map, which could only be crossed by players who owned a ship? And you'd have to be level 100 (or whatever) to use a ship, and you could then sail off to the high level area, which would have new monsters, new shops with items which could only be bought there, etc.

Those are just a couple ideas, they're not important. What is important is that there need to be incentives at each point in the game that make you want to gain more levels, if indeed this game is going to be about levelling up.
