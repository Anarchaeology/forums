Deliverance...
After months of solitude amongst the Clans, wandering and making his own way, Hubert his pet...his only friend. Benjacrat decides its once again time. The political scene is beginning to move, and now is the time to feed the fire and watch it burn again.

Ben steps out of his Yalm and strolls calmly through Old Athen. He stumbles through the ruins of what used to be the East side of the City. Thinks of the past, the things hes seen and been through. Hoping he can prevent these things from happening again...

Hoping he can bring the Clans some Deliverance....

((Deliverance is a new RP guild, formed for pursuance of political and legal avenues towards peace, and equality on the planet of Rubi-Ka. If anyone is interested in joining please send me a tell, I'm always looking for interested people to help bring about change on Rubi-Ka.))
