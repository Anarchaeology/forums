Man Down
Robilber sat hiding out in Reets Retreat, worrying over the small disk he had hidden on his person. Dabblez had just come on his comm requesting the disk, and he was more than happy to oblige. After what had happened to everyone else involved in the situation. Dabblez approached Robilber mechanically, requesting the disk calmly. He happily turned it over, feeling a great weight lift off of him.
"Thankyou meat-thing" Dabblez said, shooting a round into Robilber's chest and shoulder. He fell backwards, clutching the smoking hole in his chest. He stood, breathing hard over the shot, firing two of his own at the fleeing imposter. He then collapsed onto his back and called for help. Moments later a handful of neutral medics saw to the minor injuries. He then walked outside, going to the small bank in Stret, opening a safety deposit box and taking out the backup disk, the replacing it and sighing, lighting up a cigarette.
