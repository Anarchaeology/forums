Making jewelry to sell is great the way it is, but it would be nice if I can also find rare precious stones that when used in the construction, bestow various specials on the ring.

eg*

Use QL 50 Fire Opal and you end up with a ring that bestows +50hp.

+nano points

+run speed

etc..

Very rare and potent gems could bestow 2-3 special properties.
