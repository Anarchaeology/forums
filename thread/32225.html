Agree completely
Fotik's suggestion is a good one...

Yes, it does kinda sound like "crit nerf" but it's the best solution imho.

Leave bracers as they are - EVERYONE has access to them, and EVERYONE should continue to have access to them. They are not overpowered, because they are based on title level, so you can't use 21% reflect bracers until you are level 190+. That's fair.

However, anyone with enough NCU can get a 30% soldier reflect shield easily, and this is unbalancing, not only to PvP as a whole, but puts soldiers at a huge disadvantage.

The Soldier's "thing" is ranged weaponry - everyone has access to these, and should continue to have access.

What soldiers don't have is:
Healing capabilities
Nuking capabilities
Skill depriving capabilities
DoT'ing capabilities
HoT'ing capabilities
Pet's etc

Let's take another profession as an example.

Doctor, lvl 200
Self buffed/equipped
2 x 21% Energy Bracers
Flashpoint/Nova Flow combo
HP buff(s)
Healing
HoT
DoT(s)
15% crit scope

Outside buffed
RRFe - 30% reflect shield - all damage types
7% crit buff
Essence of Behemoth
Offensive Steamroller
Automatic Targetting

and compare this to a soldier.

Soldier, lvl 200
Self buffed/equipped
2 x 21% Energy Reflect Bracers
Flashpoint/Nova Flow combo
Crappy healing (300 points) - anyone actually use this btw?
15% crit scope
RRFe - 30% relfect shield - all damage types
TMS - 75% reflect (overrides RRFe)
Offensive Steamroller
Automatic Targetting
Total Focus

Outside buffed
7% crit buff
Essence of Behemoth
Doc HP Buff(s)

Basically what is boils down to is that the doctor has EVERYTHING the soldier has, plus a little bit more.

Now, I'm NOT saying "nerf doctors", in fact, I'm not saying nerf anyone but it's just plain stupid that a doctor should be better at ranged combat than a soldier!

Make the soldier reflect shield self only. Give them a RRFe (other) buff that reflects 7% damage.

No-one is scared of fighting a soldier any more because they have the same equipment, plus everything else that comes with their profession - it shouldn't be this way. This change wouldn't make soldiers "uber", but I think it would level the playing field a bit.

Organa/Protexxor

Well IMHO....
With crit nerfs, halving of dmg and a million and 1 items to add to hp, we're in a situation where people are near unkillable in PvP. Factor in near MK protection for a particular dmg type as well and we're talking fights where first to fall asleep will be the first grass muncher

This situation is ludicrous and something needs to give. However I don't believe it should be making Reflect shields self only, as Sredni pointed out this is a good tool for PvM which makes soldiers and eng(who lets face it have real issues with there pets high lvl in missions so there effectiveness is a problem already) more desirable to team up with. I think the problem arrises from bracers being far far too powerful. 44% protection from dmg from wearing an object that doesn't even collide with anything else useful, thats nuts. Don;t you remember when you first found out about reflect bracers? Didn't you have this feeling of "WTF this is NUTS!"?

My solution would be to halve effective % reduction of bracers and making them unique. A maximum possible 11% protection is still a significant "edge", but then it;s not overpowering either. It would allow soldiers to relish in there class defining attribute (for pvp anyway) MK shields, once again we would be in awe of 75% reduction, "w00t j00 so 1337!!"

I can forsee some cries of "But I worked really hard to get my bracers, why should I be nerfed?!?!". My answer would be on rbk2 anyway, look around see many people pvping ? Sure if you work on your character you should have a more powerful character. But look at the cost, people are too busy continually trying to get the best "stuff" to fight on a even playing field that in the mean time no one is actually brawling(or flinging ).

I think I remember reading on web site about the "theory of good game making" or some such title that rare items such give a moderate advantage, but should not be significantly over-poweringly in themselves. Well I;d say bracers break that rule And in fact FC seems so wanting to appease us that many items are "overpowering", 1kish hp from a armour you got nuthing from before, +400 nano and -15% nano cost from wearing a hood with noly moderate AC loss, and many many more. My point is where it's going ? Is FC going to have make items better and better? Is this just going to become EQ and we're continually camping to hide the fact there is nuthing actually to do ?
