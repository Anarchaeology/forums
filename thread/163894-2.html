I was going to make this same sort of thread until I saw it here. I think bank sharing would be a great idea.

As far as players being a separate entity: it shouldn't be a problem. As far as I can tell my parents are both separate entities and they have joint access to their bank account.

To help prevent twinking: the people who are twinking are the ones with lots of money with high level characters who are most likely in big orgs. In that case they will always have someone to transfer for them. And even if other people (non-high level or non-rich people) want to twink its not hard to find someone to help you transfer (as someone mentioned before).

Trading between omni and clan: just because 2 people are on 2 sides of a conflict does not mean they will have no contact with one another. In the American civil war most of the generals on both sides kept up correspondence regardless of the battles they were fighting. Exchange a letter or exchange a chem mauser, its all the same to me.

Trading nodrops: trading nodrops is a bad idea, but can be dealt with very easily. The reason many things are nodrop is because you arent supposed to have them until you are powerful enough to get them on your own. Just live with it or get someone to take you where you want to go.

As far as I'm concerned I only see the bank situation we have now as a nuisance. I realize this is an MMORPG, but the kind of roleplaying im looking for has to do with free enterprise and tradeskilling resembling real life; not marriages or going to the disco or any other silly thing people want to do. Don't make us pay for other's oddities.

I know for me, and many others, a non-shared bank is only an irriation that detracts from our gameplaying experience....so gimme!
