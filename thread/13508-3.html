quote:
Originally posted by HurfBurf
It would be nice if you could choose your chat channel with a hotkey. Also, your sig is way too long.
There is. Just make a macro. Here's how:

Let's say you have OT Seeking Team (1-50) and OT Seeking Team (51-100) just for example's sake.

To make a hotkey that selects the 51-100 channel you'd need to type this:

"/macro name /ch OT Seeking Team (5" (without quotes)

You only need to supply the client enough of the name to separate it from other names adequately.

If you had channels Vicinity and Vicizoo, you'd have to type:

"/macro name /ch Vicin" to get Vicinity and "/ch Viciz" to get Vicizoo.

Got it?

I would like it to be divided into 4 sections (at the very least).

1) ONline people accepted into your friends list
2) OFFline people accpeted into your friends list
3) ONline people NOT accepted into your friends list
4) OFFline people NOT accepted into your friends list

Each alphabetized, of course.

This way, instead of having to scroll through 700 names in alphabetical order to find the ~40 or so that are on, you can have them all grouped together. If they're not in the "online" sections, they're not online. No more squinting at hundreds of names. It would be similar to ICQ or AIM friends lists

P.S. I would LOVE to have a "Guild" section too, perhaps an ONline and an OFFline guild section, regardless of if you've added them to your friends list or not. Sometimes a guild gets new members and you have to add them into your guild one by one. It would be nice if the game would actually keep track of that one for you.
