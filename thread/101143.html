I am doing bow with my doc right now. Level 131 and have been using my 142 Schuyler bow for some time now. Like you said, you can not compete with the main damage dealers, but at least you wont be totally gimped offesively. I am doing a meager 300-400 damage to level 190-200 mobs with approx 800-1000 damage crits.

It does have its advantages, especially since you have been doing MA, you wont have to change your init because bows use physical init. As you mentioned, it gives you some range. Also, you will save IP because you only have to raise one attack skill (bow) and the init that you raise is the only Light Blue init other than NC init. You can also get a higher AR because it is not a split skill, straight bow.

Also has some drawbacks. One is that you will never do as much damage as you would with say a rifle. Another is that since it uses physical init, you will get parried. Not sure how they go about parrying an arrow rather than dodging it, but it happens.

Theres my 2 creds worth, hope it helped.

I've been using a 167 Schuyler for about 10 levels, and I'm about to move up to 180. The main problem with the bow is that one, it is VERY IP intensive. Bow is about as dark blue as anything gets. I think it TL4 caps at around 450, and ql180 Schuy has recs of around 850. My attack rating is 700-730ish right now, with Expertise, so I'll need a wrangle and some borrowed dragon sleeves.

The other problem is that it doesn't have any specials, and has a slowish attack speed, which doesn't mix well with healing. No alpha strike, and I generally have to be a bit offensive to fire rapidly, even with 600 physical init.

In all, though, it is more "fun" than running around with a gun. It also gives me the illusion of looking like a MA (I wear a MA suit, for style reasons), so hopefully people won't notice me as much in PvP. Probably just wishful thinking. :-)

In all, it isn't too bad. A ql200 Schuy actually has decent damage output, though not enough to make you a damage dealer, as you say. I can solo orange mobs pretty easily, but it takes a helluva long time to do.

With the 167 with ATR of 720, I hit for 600-1000, usually (averaging 800, at least), and crit for 2100 or so.

Previous to Schuy, I was MA and then Parry Sticks... Schuy outdamages them. The real beyatch is Parry, but I'm sure you're used to that.
