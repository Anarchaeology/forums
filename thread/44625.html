Chapter 1
Chapter 1: The Waking
Tyruck opened his eyes. He saw the sun for the first time in his life. All he could remember was a white room with many men in it talking to each other telling each other that hes a definite failure.
He thought about the time in the white room. The people called themselves clans. But then many men in black broke in; he felt a sharp pain in his left leg. He saw a man pull the dagger out of his leg. Before the man in black with the word Omni on his forehead shot him. The man shrieked with pain and fell to the ground with red blood oozing out of his side.
The man in black then took the needles out of Tyrucks leg. He felt close to the Omni man, thanking him in his mind. Right before the Omni man pulled the final needle out of him, he saw a Clanner press a button. Thats all he could remember.
Tyruck somehow knew that he was in Tir County. He knew that the many animals around him were leets and rollerrats. He could not see any other animals. He was in a forest. He wanted to be in the Omni cities. He knew that that was very far away. He decided to sleep. And so he slept.

Tyruck stepped into the mission. The sledgehammer had taken its toll. He needed to rest the hammer was too heavy. He sat down as a bullet shot right in front of his face.
