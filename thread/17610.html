Ok just to put in my 2 cents worth. I recently upgraded my PC from a dual 550 PIII 512MB ram to a XP2000 1Gb ram. In both cases I have a DSL connection. Although my FPS has greatly increased and I no longer get mouse lag I have noticed looking at the actually data comming into my PC, it very rarely exceeds 3k a second no matter what zone I am in or what display setting I have.

This most obvious reason for this is that the game servers limit the connection speed regardless of the load. Due to the number of people online at any one time they would have to, and it is probably much easier to statically limit the connection speeds rather than dynamically adjust them as the load goes up and down.
In less complex/populated areas things are fine and no one really notices, but once the amount of data increases due to more people in the area or more complex areas then things start to suck.
