Division9 R.S.G.E. Recruiting
http://www.jensenweb.net/div9/albums/album16/aaa.jpg

The day's hot suns burned in the warm sky and shined like twin rubies as the croud gathered around the podium set up along the Omni HQ platform with Omni banners laying everywhere. The lower levels of the area were swarming with soldiers as to intercept any unexpected disruption in the 25% area. The man shining in his dark Elite armor with metals by his side finally stood up and got the attention of the restless crowd. Curious as to what was going on, the croud hushed down to hear the respectful looking person speak.

Greetings, fellow Omni. My name is Deoine Jerrikk Conmy, General of Division 9 R.S.G.E. and head of Public Relations. I have came forth today to speak of you of Omni's dire time. Throughout the hundreds of past years, Omni has fought mercilessly with the Clan. Battle and battle again, we have fought a hard war. The Division itself has always held great respect for Mother Omni and have lived to upheld her wims. I come to you today with a notice that Division 9 is opening its floodgates for recruitment.

We have from day one proven our value to Omni and had the pleasure of having a gun named after our Division to aid the soldiers in battle. Recently, we have gone silent. But that is all changing on this day.

We have a reputation to live up to, and ask that any willing or feel like they have the guts to join and survive sign up now at our recruitment office at: http://division9.rsge.net

I am looking forward to all those who wish to aid in our cause, and help us once again become the power we once were.

I bid you all a good day.

With pride showing in his eyes, the General walks off the stage with his personal escort.

[OOC: We are a great Role playing organization. We act more as a family but in story are a military organization that serves Omni. We only accept those players who are nice and will get along in our environment of friends, and this is why we vote on our applications. Recently, we have made more new programs in our org. And once our numbers grow some more, we will begin to introduce more exciting activities in our org such as dungeon raids, and org story events. One which I'm planning myself that will introduce Shadowlands to the game for the Div which will be pretty fun.
So I ask that anyone who wants to come and join the fun of a friendly Role-playing atmosphere sign up today. We are always looking for more members. ]
