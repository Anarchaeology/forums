CYBORGS! Forget Clan/Omni dispute this is more important!
Has anyone been to Perpetual Wastelands recently? Well if you had you will notice a large outpost, presumably a mining outpost. Not too unusual. However, upon closer inspection of the patrolling guards, you will discover that they are, in fact, not human, and are actually hostile CYBORGS!

Hundreds of them are swarming around in this camp, which, to all appearances, seems like a normal functioning outpost. Now, Im only level 101, so every single cyborg on that outpost conned red to me. However, I realized (once they started shooting at me) that these cyborgs did not have a current favorite in the Omni/Clan dispute (a clanner I asked verified this).

This can only mean one thing: the cyborgs have hostile intentions toward us all and they must be stopped! These horrible creatures are biding their time, waiting for the humans to weaken themselves with infighting, only to strike at our most vulnerable moment! They must be stopped at all costs, so I am proposing a plan to stop them.

Next saturday, (July 28, 2002) at 19:00 GMT I propose we humans(Clan, Neutrals, Omnis) go to the Perpetual Wastelands and kill every last dirt-licking cyborg in that camp! There is only room for one Race of sentient beings who carry guns, and thats us!

Directions: Whompa from Newland to Newland desert, and from there whompa to Hope, from Hope it is almost due east to the zone. On your planet map you can actually see the cyborg encampment (it's that large) in the very farthest north-east of the map...

Everyone, please tell everyone you can of the horrible travesties committed by the cyborgs (make them up if you have to) but please be there!
