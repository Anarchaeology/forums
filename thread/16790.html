He's not really in the twink range anymore. Nitsobar you can do it, you need to build yourself for speed a bit. After 100 There really isn't such a thing as a twink. Anyone can put in ql170 plants by around 110. The low hundreds are bad pvp levels, after 130-140 you get in a more competitive range. In the low 100s you are just getting past all the skill caps, most PVPers are higher.

I suggest that when everyone goes to kill trasque you hop on board. Usually the omni just stand at the top of the stairs wondering where their guards are at, but every so often a brave one wonders down. I'd stick to being in a large group till you bust past 130 for pvp, then go to more small group areas.

Docs can be one of the better PVP classes, second to traders and soldiers but when played well able to really dominate.
