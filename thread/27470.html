Death and it's consequences
Hi All,

I was ready through the new threads and saw this poll;

http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=25142

I read this and realized that of course FC will never reimburse IP lost for any reason, its simply too time consuming and difficult to verify. I know this is a very sore spot with most and Im sure that the majority of players have had this happen to them at least once during their playtime in AO. I know its happen to me on many occasions.

Now I know death is really of no consequence ATM with the exception of the possibility of IP loss and that a lot of people think that death should have more meaning. I have a couple of suggestions along those lines taken from my experience in several other MMORPGs.

1)I think you should NEVER lose IP on death Its just not right for someone to waste countless hours and lose that investment due to a server crash or some other bug in AO.

2)Death needs to have meaning Implement a Res penalty of say a reduction of skill level of 5% per death to a maximum of 20% that can only be removed by earning IP points. This would be based on the total IP needed to level. So say earn 10% of IP needed to level removes 5% of the Res penalty.

3)You should drop items that are not equipped and not marked NODROP. The corpse and items dropped would remain on the ground at death site until you recovered them or until your body decayed. Set the body decay to say 1 hour with an additional 5 minutes per level factored in on top of that with say a cap of 3 hours. This would provide you with an incentive to go back and finish that mission or kill that mob to get your stuff back. It would also add real meaning and value to special NODROP weapons and Armor.

I know there are many details that would need to be worked out and Im not even certain it makes since or is possible within the game engine but it sure would make things more interesting as well as remove the possibility of losing the IP you have earned over many hours of play.

BTW: This would be very interesting in PvP as well

Just my two credits...
