dont get discoureaged
i've been playing on & off (thought mostly on ) since 3 weeks after launch (was gutted i didnt get pioneer lol, had to wait for a credit card to come through). anyway, i digress.
it is more than feasible to level up your character to a very respectable level without doing any camping at all. i have one character above level 5, a level 164 soldier. he is effective in PVP & PVM, can solo well. i can go on fun boss raids & go to aces with my friends & have a blast. being high level is'nt all about camping, although to be the uberest of the uber, i suppose you do. by hoods i think you mean decranum MK 2 which can be looted in the smugglers den dungeon, & admittely that does need a lot of work to get a full set. i personally spent about 8 hours there for 2 pieces lol, but then i let about 4 more pieces pass to guildies/team mates. the armour you may be referring to is dragon armour, & that needs pure luck to get, or going to tarasque raids forever. i wouldnt fret about that.
anyway, i somehow dont think i am entirely answering your question. it is COMPLETELY feasible to make a lvl 150 character with doing absolutely no camping, & be effective. weapons & armour can be bought for a reasonable price up to that point (the real campable stuff is only soldier/enf weaps off the top of my head, & they have level reqs, & if you play them, you WILL want them, but thats for later).
hmmm, i am rambling quite a lot but really, dont worry about camping. it is not neccesary IMO.
