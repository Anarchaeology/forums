I'm with you on part of what you have said (actually, restated), but we're till not on the same sheet of music. Let's get on the same page first:

1) Skills have an initial cost to raise, and an incremental value (20%) for every level after that. So, while I call a green skill a '5', you call it a '1'. This makes no difference. I am merely pointing out the difference in our lingo.

2) Base skill is that skill without any buffs, implants, devices, or ability modifications.

3) While the cost of the skill will be reflected in the color of the skill, it does not work the well the other way around (a blue skill can be anything from 10.5~20 - while you can raise 11 skills 4x per level, and a 20 skill 3x per level).

So, now that we're on the same page, I'm confused.

There is a cap of 600 base skill on every skill, regardless of skill color or cose to improve. Even in your logic (A green skill can get to 600, but a 1.0-1.4 skill can only get to 550 - that doesn't make any sense at all)

At level 200, any given class can have 600 base skill in any skill, regarless of skill color or cost to improve (naturally, you will be able to have less maxxed skills if you chose many 'blue' skills).

So, my lvl 200 martial artist can have 600 base MA skill. My lvl 200 MP can also have 600 base MA skill. The only differences are:

1) The martial artist will have achieved the "cap" of 600 base much earlier than the MP. The MA will likely achieve the cap around 165ish, while it would likely take the MP all 200 levels to get there.

2) The martial artist has spent much less IP in the long-run. Given he has a base of 5 MA, he will improve at 1 (20% of 5) each level. My MP, however, will cost 14 for the initial increase, and 2.8, rounded, for each improvement after that. So, the MP spends a heckuva lot more IP for this skill.

I understand that you are familiar with the 20% increase in skills per skill-level (hence you 'quoted' AnarchyZone). However, you have not demostrated that there are any caps above or below 600 for skills. Please, if you find proof of this issue otherwise (with Funcom's name on it - hence why I quoted Sybex's guide) please share it with me.

There are very few facts in this game, other than what you personally experience. Your post is somewhat ARK-like in such a way that you're not quite sure, but it's the best answer at the time, there is no one to 'back up' your information, but you speak with confidence anyway. Anyway, unless you can show me a level 200 MP trying to put 451 (base) points in MA, but getting the message 'you have reached the maximum for this skill' (or something similar), you haven't proven a thing.
