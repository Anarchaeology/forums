I remember when...
I remember when...

Browsing for 3 missions would take 20 mins max. You would see more or less a _full set of the armour you wanted (for me then, bau and waitt), an NF or 2, and many of the popular weapons that are used. Junk items (clothes grafts etc) were rarer, most stuff you saw was useful, creds easily covered cost of kits and ammo used and provided a decent reward. (up until 11.3 or so)

Green mobs were quick easy fights, evens were enjoyable, oranges tough but killable, reds usually won but an epic fight might see one dead. A solo mission wasnt an all night epic fight that saw you gain a level or 2 (up until about 12.5-12.6)

Freedom Arms made pistols viable weapons, and were in the game. (11.6?)

Omni missions werent full of agro no exp omni ratbots (up until 12.6)

What happened ?
