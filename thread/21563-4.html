Well, this is not all correct.

Actually your health is dependent on both breed and profession, this is how it works:

The profession decides what color the Body Dev skill is and how many points you can raise it each lvl. For example a Nano Breed NT got a dark blue skill and can only raise it by 3-4 (dont remember) every lvl, while a nano breed Enf got green skill and can raise it at least 5 points every lvl.

The breed decides how much health you get from every point you raise your body dev, for example nano breed gets 2 health for every point, while solitus gets 3.


So as you can see, a nano breed Enf will have more health than a nano breed NT.
