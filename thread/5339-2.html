MN lock issue
In all honesty, it's not fair to pick some skills that cannot be adjusted. Frankly, I wish I hadn't put points into MN.... why shouldn't I get those back like the NT who went for shotguns?

The original idea, slow change each level, albeit with issues to upper level characters, is the most balanced and fair (and likely easy to implement) suggestion to the misspent IP due to nerf or misconception of ability I've seen. Also, the one-time full optional adjustment of IP fix will not only solve the current problems of high level chars (who granted, by this time should know where they want the points), but resolves the 'it was nerfed all of a sudden, it should be fixed all of a sudden'.

Regular IP resets are not the answer. People make mistakes, but you live and learn from them, and at least with the 'adjustment' factor, you can recover from them. I think that the same #/level that you can bring a skill up, you should be able to bring it down each level, and recover the IP. All other rules should stay the same.

It will mess some folks up, granted, but I think that if you lower something to the point where you cannot equip it, it gets either a) put in your inventory or b) to the bank. the 'loot' window idea could cost some folks some expensive gear... although they could just through out their health kits.

On this note, I think the one time fix should be implemented like the pioneer backpack was.... a special command that you can type at your leisure, once per character.

My $0.02
