Take missions to completely kill and clear the area,you make as much in creds(and more!)for the kills and loot selling as you do for the completion bonus,and of course more xp.
We are based on speed and evades,so make sure you invest well in these areas.Look to our stack HOT,s which really give us a good boost now,and always max the only 2 relevant ranged wep skills we have..SMG and BURST.Ranged init now also effects the speed that burst recycles so put what you can in,but not at the expense of other skills.
The Yakamuchy movement predictor gives us much better range,in HUD one,and can also be worn with Targeting Scope-Vision enhancer(HUD two),although this lowers ranged init a bit it increases range again and crit chances.
Padded Love armour uses Stamina only(greenskill for us)and gives nano skill buffs.Check out the armours and choose carefully the ones that suit what you are trying to become.
Besides the MCS,look at the Sol Chronis,when i have used it,it seems to disrupt the attacks of foes because of its speed.May be imagined!but i prefer to use it against certain Mob.

Uhm...you know you don't *HAVE* to have GA, right?

You know Fixers do just fine *without* GA, right?

This whole thing just sounds like a bad whine to me.

"Wahhh! I'm poor and don't have enough money to buy a Ferrari and I'm not lucky enough to have one drop in my lap! What should I do?!?!?!"

That's exactly what this sounds like.

The answer is: Stop obsessing over GA and just wear regular armor (drive a regular car) until either your luck or your bank account catches up with your desires (GA).

GA is very rare and very expensive (as intended).
GA is not necessary.
You can't afford GA.
You haven't been lucky enough to find it (yet).
Get over it, or get to work making credits.

Obsessing over finding GA can detract from your enjoyment of the game. It sounds like all you've been doing is using your 2 chars to search for GA...that doesn't sound like much fun.

Fixers are doing great these days. You'll find they are great fun to play. Take the advice of others in this thread: Just get out there and have fun playing your Fixer and fate may (or may not) drop GA in your lap. Either way, you will have had fun playing your character instead of endlessly searching for GA.
