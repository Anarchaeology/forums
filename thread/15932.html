Secondary skills and Specials should have different rules in OE. *Bump if you agree*
What happens to 66%/33% weapons when you are debuffed? Funcom has made it clear that if your 33% req (i.e. ranged energy or what not) falls below the 20% mark (these reqs are the lowest of the reqs) then the entire weapon will have a 25% damage reduction.

I suggest you get a 25% of 33% damage reduction. And if the 66% req falls below your oe marker, you get a 25% of 66% damage reduction.

It makes sense.

How about Specials?

This is something that literally sickens me when I read Gautes article. The reason for this is simple:

There is no reason to pump special attacks past where you need then in order to equip a weapon.

Other then Aimed Shot, all other specials should be left OUT of the overequipping check. Things like Riot Control and implants for equipping weapons would become useless.

The logic here is "Why spend thousands upon thousands of IP in Burst when level 1 burst is equivelant to level 500 burst? I can just get implants and buffs for it and NEVER worry about pumping it."

And thats exactly what I do. I only pump secondary specials after i'm implanted with those specific clusters, fully buffed in base abilities and totally sure I cant push those skills any higher. I dont see any point otherwise.

If there were a difference between level 1 flingshot and level 600 flingshot, my tune would be different. However now Funcom is forcing us to pump useless skills. This irritates me so much.
