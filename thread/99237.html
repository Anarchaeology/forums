"Missing Key"
"Missing Key"
by Sun "Poetica" Catt

Sealed inside my heart
there is a room
A closed room
Sealed up
But not forgotten
A closed room
Where memories live
Where a love lives
The key is missing
I can't unlock it
Maybe one day
the door will open
Maybe one day
the key will appear
Until then
I got a closed room
Sealed inside my heart
Sealed up
But not forgotten
