The situation's about the same at the low levels as it is in the high levels, from the sound of things. Walk into a room and, most of the time, you'll draw aggro simply for standing there.

There's aggro trimmers of course, and when these work, they work. The problem is that there's no way to predict whether the trimmer will pull the mob off you first try, or whether you'll spend the entire fight fruitlessly spamming the trimmer while the mob hacks big lumps out of you. Whether you counterattack or not also seems to have little bearing.

None of this would particularly be an issue if:
  • Bots did more damage over time, and killed the mob more quickly.
  • Engineers had more ability to take punishment.
The current difficulty revolves around the fact that, if the mob decides to go for the Engineer and not the bot, an orange mob can kill you before your bot will kill the mob. The frustrating thing is that this is an essentially random occurrance that you can do absolutely nothing about. Most missions I'm fine. Sometimes I run into a mob that I can't handle and which I can't pull off me. I'm left without anything in my tactical toolkit to handle this situation, and there's no way to predict it.

IMO this is why so many Engineers go the shotgun or MA route, rather than sticking with pistol and concentrating on their nano and trimmer skills. You lose out on the ability to trim your pet, which increases the incidence of the type of situation outlined above, but you do more damage which means that you're more likely to survive the situation should it occur. It's expensive in terms of IP though - all those dark blues to be raised to a high enough level to have a decent attack rating doesn't come cheap.
