One shot kills of green Mobs should be common and 1/2 dmg to 3/4 damage against yellows should be the norm, with the occasional possiblility of a one-shot kill against those too.

A line of armour specifically for agents should definately be introduced, or perhaps a tank/social armour that provides a conceal buff

Anybody who thinks that agent's are overpowered because of their FP line should try playing an agent and see what the limitations of those nanos are first-hand. The idea that AP and MP are in some way upgrades from FP is a joke. Why not add a hard QL cap rather than making the lines increase the penalties? make the cap for FP at no higher than QL75 nanos can be run, for instance and other reasonable caps for the other two lines, making the penalties go down as mentioned earlier. I think the best way of treating aimed shot is to make it a shot that takes twice as long to take as a normal shot, but does higher than normal damage and has a higher than normal chance of critically hitting.
