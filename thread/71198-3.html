I AM an Advisor in Elite Operations and I will state why we were there helping the NLF:

We got paid.

Everyone of our attending members was paid 100K for our services.

If any Clan out there would like to hire us to help defend their towers, we will peruse the proposal and perhaps help you.

If any Omni detachment out there would like to hire us to help defend their towers, we will peruse the proposal and perhaps help you.

I kid you not. We may not be the biggest or most influential, but we try to hold to our own ideals. We are warriors who like to enjoy the spoils of war and revel in the cameraderie of our own brotherhood.

We don't give a crap about the piddling politics going on between the Clans and Omni-Tek. We're often shunned by other Neutral orgs for our philosophies. The fact that NLF came to us for some muscle only means they understand that there are tools out there that can be obtained through a few pieces of silver and some of that good Nanomage Notum Wine they make so well.

We set ourselves up as targets and that's fine with me. It's to be expected considering our modus operandi. The only soft spot we may have for other Neutral organizations is out of respect for other citizens of the planet who have the balls to follow their own ideals without falling prey to the doctrine and propaganda of the two prevalant organizations.

Go ahead and hate us for accepting a fee to attack the notum cannons. At least we have a mission statement and stick to it. We have ideals and let them guide us.

OOC: We're working our hardest to play the role that our founding members envisioned when they created us from the ashes of the Borealis Militia. It's not easy being both Neutral and non-pacifist considering the way the game is structured. But, we do our best. Despite all the wrenches thrown in our gameplay by the dev's, we do our best to roleplay a bunch of fun-loving battle-worn merc's who would give their own life for an org-mate. We have a lot of fun in the traditional sense of guild-life and aren't afraid to work our way into the new 'high level content'. Check the bios on Lorepan, Hoopin, Sumokan, Permafrost, Asterik, Provisioner, Kymara, Razz and the rest. Then, think if you might not want them on your side at some point in time. I will assure you that you will not find a group of people more honorable with respect to doing what they say and adhering to business agreements. Aside from that, I really think we managed to get a great bunch of people with fun-loving RL personalities somehow together.
