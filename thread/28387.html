What AO lacks, is the unexpected.
Content I would *love* to see implemented.


Unexpected ANYTHING!


One of the best parts about Everquest was the element of the unexpected that existed each time you entered a zone.

In Anarchy Online, sure you can enter a dangerous zone and immediately get killed by some mobs, but the mobs arn't unique ... they arn't easly remembered.


One thing I miss about Everquest, was the NPC's that would randomly stroll by a popular leveling place just to whipe out a few groups!!

Being killed is never fun of course, but that one named NPC became infamous on any given server.


How about some high level terrorists that spawn and cause havok among the lower levels? ... It would enable higher level characters to periodically "rescue" people in the zone whenever the npc was causing problems.


I think they *tried* to implement this, but unfortunatly it didnt' work out.


To reinterate .... AO is definatly missing the unexpected. When was the last time you thought "Hell, I didn't expect that!" in Anarchy Online?

Reading about NWN, I think it would be cool to have a high-level NPC that wouldn't just warp away and come back with full health. Actually, what I mean is the GM would actually do something besides fight- give a speech, talk, ect. and do something you'd expect a -real player- to do. Here is what they could do, if they really aren't creative enough to think of anything on the fly for a while:

Maybe after enough people died they could keep the NPC at 'red', but put it's ACTUAL LEVEL down about 100 or so. Maybe they could have a few 'normal GMs' join the fight, and the players could all band together to kill what seemed to be an impossible enemy. The players could feel like they actually accomplished something. At least, there's a ton of stuff they could do to make the attacks a lot more interesting that they AREN'T doing. Maybe they could add humor and introduce a roving gang of terrorist Leets, that were much higher-level than expected and would go around killing players until someone defeated them

But we still have the truth that so many players are already unsatisfied with the events. Level 400 NPCs just don't cut it for most everyone. We need to have someone with an imagination working on a consistant storyline- maybe we could even have some sort of epic attack that even Funcom didn't know the outcome of until the very end because players would be able to influence it.
