Names- for Funcom officials
I don't want to stir up problems, but I would like to make my forum name to become my main characters name.

Currently, my name on Ruki-ka1 is taken by a level 2 who has not leveled in months.

I would like to re-name "Purebrute" to "Hobbes", as that is how people know me.

I think a request to the owner of "hobbes" would be appropriate. If he says "yes", the the fix is easy. If he has quit or is away from the game the problem is more troublesome.

My solution is this:

Let me change my name. If the original ingame returns, let him learn that his name has been re-assigned. If he wishes to keep it, he can click "resume name", otherwise he can rename his character.

My desire for a new name is only casual., I would not want any player pressured or harassed to relinquish his name.
