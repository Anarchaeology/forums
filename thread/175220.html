From the Shadows...
Nathan smiled broadly as the display on his comm registered his new official affiliation and rank. They had lurked in the shadows for far too long; and from the shadows they now emerged. The pace of events on Rubi-Ka was accelerating rapidly, taking most along the ride as hapless passengers.

"...we make the pledge of dedication to Omni-Tek, her employees and to the still larger cause of humanity..." Nathan turned the words of the emminent Philip Ross around in his mind. For far too long had Omni-InternOps remained secret and silent. Their division, Division 3, led by his friend Zach "Eternalforce" Craddock, was now tasked to fill a void left by the departure of longstanding notable organizations such as SPARTA...it would be no small task.

Jobe and the Portal; the increased incidence of employee-employee crimes; the mysterious "visions" being experienced by employees; the "visions" motivating even the most pacifist of Omni-Tek's foes, Sir Galahad, to war; the rumours abounding that Henry Radiman was back among the living. So much to investigate, and so little time in which to do it...

Yet it would be done. Omni-InternOps division 3 would see it done. It was their duty, their sworn oath, their passion and their lives.

Glancing at his friend, Nathan smiled. From the shadows InternOps now emerged.
