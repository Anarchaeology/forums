What it is, what some want, unfortunately what it sounds like some want.
Note: The (What it is section lists some obvious problems with said topic, however this should not be taken as a bashing towards all the faults of Funcom in these areas. I am not a demanding person, and am quite content with current state of many things (and like everyone else, will hope it only gets better).

[Story]

What it is:

Currently an unbalanced mix of story text and in-game action, with the scale tipped heavily on the side of story text.

What some want:

More involvment in the story spread out over various level ranges on a more frequent scale. Ability to affect the story on small and large scales. More in-game action to balance the amount of text relating to story on site.

Unfortunately what it sounds like some want:

Give me a Final Fantasy scale epic, everytime I log in dammit!

[Class Balance]

What it is:

A lot of classes need help due to some obvious broken skills, or just lacking a defining role.

What some want:

Fix the obvious broken skills, unique skills/nanos/items that give class a purpose and a sense of individuality. More balance, so as to blur the line between this/that class sucks and this/that class is uber.

More Skills/Items/Nanos that would add some diversity to fighting for some classes rather than just hit 'Q' and or attack specials for every fight.

Unfortunately what it sounds like some want:

My class needs to be able to one hit everything that is at least orange, I need heals on par with a doc, able to tank like an enforcer, and be permanently invisible....else I cannot solo a grey!

[Missions]

What it is:

Large (sometimes), dungeons linking many single sized rooms together that can become mind numbing after 30+ levels within them. They can be very difficult for some classes due to pathing issues or the class is simply lacking the tools to compete with the mission.

MOB HP (and sometimes AC) is too great, perhaps 5x or even 10x that of a player can cause mission to take too long to complete or simply too difficult.

What some want:

- Pathing issues fixed
- Mission diversity (multiple levels maybe? or increased chance of "large rooms" within mission as opposed to "small single ones").
- Shorter missions at later levels (time to complete wise, not necessarily size)
- MOB HP Decreased to reasonable level, with increased MOB intelligence.
- More unique mission types/objectives.

Unfortunately what it sounds like some want:

I should be given 1000 tokens at the completion of one mission, because getting 1000 tokens is just too much!! (And I must have it, since those 100s of exploiters early in the game have it!)

All missions should be within the zone I receive them for maximum rewards. They should be limited to 5 rooms only so that I can complete at least 20 of them in an hour! And they should always give me an item I definately need.

Also the 3 mobs in the front room (and any mob in the mission for that matter) should never gang up on me under any circumstance.

[Weapons]

What it is:

Almost every class is restricted to 1 - 3 different types of weapons (although there are probably more than 200+ different types of weapons in AO). 98% (estimate) of the weapons in AO need to be made "USEFULL"!!!

Some weapons for classes are lacking in some areas and just cannot carry the class at higher levels (more related to class balance here).

What some want:

- Improve the 98% of weapons in AO so that they are actually usefull and can help to vastly improve diversity among players.
- Fix Broken weapons/special attacks
- Unique weapons that are usefull but not too overpowering (no one wants to "forced" to get a weapon from a quest because it is stronger than anything else available)

Unfortunately what it sounds like some want:

Where is my BFG!?!?

Any class I should decide to play, should have a BFG!!!

[PvP]

What it is:

An unbalanced duel city! Unbalanced due to some classes just lacking balance and strength in general, duel city because there are too many safe borders near war/political zones, permitting people to "side line" conflict and organize personal ego tests.

Worthless rewards (titles).

What some want:

- Class balance = PvP balance (to a good degree, not fully though)
- Better design of political areas to prevent "zone camping", "side lining", "store hopping", "wall nuking", etc.
- Rewards that have meaning (should have personal meaning, like boasting or something trivial to prevent exploiting), current titles don't even accomplish either as they can be gained through means of camping etc.
- A reason to PvP besides rewards, (story line involvment)

Unfortunately what it sounds like some want:

- "I lost a duel, therefore my class is unbalanced!"
- "No one else should be able to attack me while I am killing one of their comrades on the battlefield!"
- "I cannot one hit people, I am gimped!"
- "Some guy cheated...he did something called 'Thinking' on me, that's not fair! I should win every fight using one tactic I got killed by!"
