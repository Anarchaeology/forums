Newb safety concerns.
The precious IP. from low lvls you wouldn't know that it's the most precious thing a person can have. I am gonna make a list of things that ppl shouldn't invest in. I would like ppl to add to this list.

Pistols - There is a wide consensus that they do poor damage. unless u know what your doing, don't use them. Use a shotgun with the shotgun req if need be.

Cars - An IP sucker that gives a minor speed boost. you can camp lvl 4 mission terminals for a sparrows flight that gives 190 to speed and swimming and makes you look like a bird. Did you know that every yalm has about a 1400 hidden run speed? So lower lvl yalms are good because of their low reqs, unless you think that those are too slow .

Jewelry making (unless you're gonna put IPs into Mech. Engineering anyhoo) - Be as cautious as can be when it comes to spending IPs to make money. Rings and such make a poor amount of money. Get battle suit missions at high lvls and sell them in omni trade for alot of money. a lvl 180 battle suit goes for about 800k, and it proportionate to the lvl I believe. You can get battle suits starting at lvl 50. just use AOMD to find them quick

--------------------------
Map Navigation - Don't put too many ip into this skill. The monster and human upgrades are important to have, but after that you can go a long way by putting in a temp set of implants to upload the maps you need. It will probably be enough for anyone.

Adventuring - You don't need to meet the adventuring requirement of a bag to use it, only to wear it. Wearing a bag might have it's benefits, but by the time you need to consider this you probably already know what you are doing.

Nano Programming - Do not take up NP on your <fill in profession> to make your own implants. Leave implant making to those who choose to specialize in the field. Theese will probably be NTs or MPs. Disregard this if you want to specialize in this of course, but this is an all or nothing skill really. Either leave it alone or max it.

--By ColdStrike---------------

There are many other dangers. I hope that this thread is helpful and gets more items added to the list. I will edit this message with the new items and the person who brought it to our attentions.

Just want to point out that putting points into pistol, as you say, is ok *if you know what you are doing*. Thats for using pistols for their buffing qualities, not for doing damage.

And as for cars, this means ground vehicles. Using planes is of course a very wise thing to do, but don't get anything but the lowest QL planes until you know what you are doing.

Other skills to be careful with:
Map Navigation - Don't put too many ip into this skill. The monster and human upgrades are important to have, but after that you can go a long way by putting in a temp set of implants to upload the maps you need. It will probably be enough for anyone.

Disregard this if you want to specialize in this of course, but this is an all or nothing skill really. Either leave it alone or max it.

Things you always want to max:
Body dev - You should max this no matter how dark blue it is. People who don't max their body dev are *always* the first to die. No matter what your character is built around, your hp will be your last line of defense against death. Don't ever forget that.
