It was not so much of a vote as an agreement of sorts. There was a lot of bargaining that went on, although the majority of the meeting was flaming and people making personal attacks. There is a log of this meeting on www.aoguildleaders.org in the clannet forum if anyone is so interested.

T3 was decided on as a damage team dungeon.

During the 1st opening of T3, there was lots of namecalling and a general revolt from those in Clannet against doing T3 as damage teams, although it had already been decided. Jasonx was not present for this. All of the people in the ODM alliance at that time were called out of Tara, hence giving the 1st T3 to Clannet. There were a couple that reamined, but only PvPed and did not roll for any items or participate in Clannet.

There was some dissention within ODM that since we were not allowed T3, we should retaliate by taking T1 or T2. Jasonx, acting on this, took the loot on one of those dungeons and put in on the floor to rot.

After that, there was much dissagreement amongst the members of ODM about that action. Jasonx, feeling that he really wasn't able to retaliate very well as clan, went omni and proceeded to harass clan at Tara.

During all of this, there were 1 or 2 sucessful T3 damage team raids. The one that I was on was a lot of fun, although we actually lost However, after the Dragons went omni, the alliance as a whole fell apart and we had all been through too much stress to really want to deal with Tara.

So, T3 fell back into Clannet hands. I still don't understand why T3 somehow changed back to being only Clannet, since there was never a decision to actually do that -- it was only becuase there was no one that opposed them during all of this time.

If anyone wants to sift through the posts on the Clannet forum, you will get a taste of what it looks like when people try to deal openly with well thought out criticisms to try to change the Clannet system. Nemesis was part of that alliance and went through all of that before. They knew it didn't work, why should they have tried it again?

I have to wonder why I even make these posts tho -- every single one I have made gets lost in the flames and no one even bothers to address a damn thing. I thought that among all of the bitterness, you people would at least have some sort of brain that was capable of dealing with an argument. But no, it seems not.
