Chimera admit that we underestimated the defences in Stret West Bank but the operation is still far from a failure.

Clans have lost countless towers and mining areas, but we are having trouble keeping those we take. After much consideration Chimera decided that a bigger force was needed. So general Marlamin and Dr. Grimmy (both two of my closest advisors) have made a deal with a group of Omni mercenaries lead by Milou "Mandarillo" Monaco . They will aid us in our crusade for a pure Rubi-Ka and after less then an hour of talking they decided to join our forces. We are now sure that this group will act as wavemasters and tip the war to our favor.
