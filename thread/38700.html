That's because as you travel, the game tries to store as much as it can in your RAM. After you log, the game frees up all your RAM, and more of it has been used because you've traveled more. That's also why you sometimes need to relog after you've played for a long time. All of your RAM has been taken up, and the game is now relying on swap space to do its job, which is on your harddrive. You harddrive is much, much slower than RAM.

What it's doing is delete the zone cache. It's more or less the longer you play, the more items and textures you see. The more you play, the more you get stored up in virtual memory / RAM. The reason why this is done (the closing progress bar) is to make it so that it prevents AO from slowing down the computer after you log off of AO.
