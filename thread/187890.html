Omni-Mining Response to Recent NLF Attack
On Saturday October 4, the ruthless Prime Minister Aazamon of The Nanomage Liberation Front made a daring and foolish attack. In the evening, he took a few of his followers into Clon****. They were not there for a Bronto Burger or for some sight seeing but destruction and murder. They sneaked inside the Notum Exportation Facility, one, if not the most important base on Rubi-Ka. Crawling like insects between rocks, dirt and walls they made their way towards the center where they created a weak but noticeable stronghold. It is here we seen the true nature of this group.

Security personnel of Omni-Mining were dispatched immediately. The Omni-Pol guards watched the perimeter as my security forces moved in. It wasnt long before their so called stronghold was disrupted. Several members of the NLF were discovered in the center of the base, distracting the security personnel from the main reason they were there. At the central Notum Cannon, Aazamon reveled his hideous plan to end their false struggle. The head of security John Rm and a fellow guard discovered that Aazamon was trying to assemble a Thermal-Nuclear device. Their plan was to destroy the cannons and all around it with the most destructive force they could find. The second they saw John and his fellow guard they trembled in fear and warped out.

I am appalled to discover the destructive force the NLF will go to for their false beliefs. But I am also disgusted that they have such technology. They have finally proven them selves terrorists. They can not be anything more or less. They have gone too far. Just imagine what destruction would bestow on this planet if such a device would have gone off? It is obvious they do not care for the lives of others on this planet. Trying to save notum. Do they realize how much notum would have been destroyed if that bomb went off? No, for they do not care. They are terrorists; they are attacking civilian assets of Omni-Tek for the purpose of stopping notum mining with fear. This indeed makes them terrorists. Rubi-Ka will not be safe with such terrorists until they are dealt with.

It is time this barbaric group of people be brought to justice. I will not stand to see their unlawful acts not be punished. It is time The Nanomage Liberation Front pay for their crimes against not just Omni-Tek but the citizens of Rubi-Ka. For they are a danger to everyone and everything on this planet.
