Martial Artist Armor Gloves
I've read several threads on suggestions for brassknuckles and other hand "weapons" for the MA and while I think thats a good idea and would like to see them added, I keep getting this feeling that people in general except armed MAs should do more damage than unarmed MAs. fist+weapon=more damage? To the unarmed MA it seems a little unfair. Although I understand the current issues that weapons have, I think the bug fixes will make weapon weilding MAs much more competetive than Unarmed.

Maybe this isn't a new idea but what I'd like to see is a pair of gloves that are NOT weapons, rather they are armor. You know, the gloves that are missing from the MA suit? yeah those! Add an MA bonus that progressively increases with QL and a restriction "Cannot weild weapon" and "MA only".

The explaination for the the restriction could be simply that they are woven with high guage titanium threads which makes it impossible to maintain a grip on a weapon.
