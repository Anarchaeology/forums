a couple of new lines of nano's
I'd like 2 or 3 new lines of "School" nano's, each with a TS, PS and 1 or 2 other skills (MA or Brawl plus 1hb, 2hb or whatever) as a requirement. This way, while you can learn the basics of another school at higher levels, you will only be able to master 1 school. The nano itself is self only, and will buff AND DEBUFF IN SOMEWAY the user. All of these lines cannot stack with each other. In addition to buffing the caster, the nano will create a) 2 or 3 MA attacks and/or b) a MA weapon. The attacks and weapons only last as long as the nano. These Nano's are Hostile Nanos and cannot be cancled, like Agent FP's.

Example.

School of "Drunken Fist"
requires TS, PS plus MA and Brawl to cast.
raises caster Evades and Nano resist while lowering HP and Run speed.
Does Damage over time to caster.
Maybe minor damage buff
summons MA attack "Drunken Dance" which does damage and 100% chance of stun or root or snare. Locks MA.
summons MA attack "Breaking Cup" does strait damage. locks Brawl.

School of "Shaolin Fist"
requires TS, PS plus MA and Brawl and 1HB to cast.
Raises caster evades and Run speed while lowering AC significantly (in effect canceling out armor).
Does Gain Nano over Time at higher levels.
summons MA attack "Fist of Budda" does damage and chance of Damage over time. Locks MA. Can be used with weapon
summons MA attack "No Shadow Kick" does damage and chance for stun. Locks Brawl. Can be used with weapon.
summons Weapon "Oak Staff". Requires MA Brawl and 1HB to equip. Has Brawl special.

*edit* wanted to add a pure attack school

School of "Blade Master"
requires TS, PS plus MA and 1HE and Multi Melee to cast.
Raises 1HE, Nano Resist and Run speed, while lowering Evades and Nano pool significantly (making it impossible for MA to heal much during fight).
Damage buff at higher levels.
Increase crit chance.
summons MA attack "Flurry of Blades" does damage. Locks MA. Can only be used if 1HE equiped
summons MA attack "Dance of Blades" does damage and breaks roots and snares. Locks Brawl. Can only be used if 1HE equiped
summons Weapon "Blade Master Sword (right hand)"
summons Weapon "Blade Master Sword (left hand)"
