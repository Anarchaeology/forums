Vaporware: Ally Matrix Linker
Vaporware: Ally Matrix Linker

Just an idea, but how about a device that allows guilds to "invite" other guilds to share exit points to control towers? For example Guild A has two tower sites, T1 and T2. Guild A wants to allow Guild B to help with the defense at tower site T2. The president of Guild A buys the Ally Matrix Linker and right clicks with it on the control tower at T2. Let's say this results in a new item to be generated, a Ally Matrix Key. The president of Guild A trades this to the president of Guild B. From this point, all members in Guild B now has the option of being transported to Guild A's T2 tower from the grid. After a few weeks, if the original guild wants to revoke the travel rights of the guest guild, the Ally Matrix Linker or the key can be destroyed. I would restrict it so only one guild can be invited.
