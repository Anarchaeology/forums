Problems with Matrox Parhelia Video Cards
Funcom:
I realize that this is a relatively small issue, since few people have the brand new Matrox Parhelia cards, however there are some very annoying limitations with AO when using the card.
The Matrox Parhelia card (www.Matrox.com) is a 3 screen card and their website says it is fully compatible with AO. However, I have found that its is impossible to play on all 3 screens with a resolution over 1920 x 480, the lowest (which looks really bad).
It is also impossible to play the game with a single screen resolution over that of your desktop.
When you try to use a resolution besides those listed above, it gives you a message that says something about a render error and the object "INVALIDOBJECT".
I have also noticed, since updating to the latest Parhelia driver from their website, that AO crashes almost everytime i "tab-alt"
Thank you for your attention in this matter, and I look foreward to an eventual resolution of this problem.
-Mike
