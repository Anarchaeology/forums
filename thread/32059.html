Kill Stealing Losers
What's the deal with the kill stealing?
Aren't people ashamed of being a kill stealer?

I was with three other people my level getting the mission item from Nuts&Bolts in Wartorn Valley. There was absolutely no one else around. Some other people show up so I mentioned that we only need one chip then we would leave and they could have the camp. Doesn't that sound reasonable? It's a 15 minute spawn. In five minutes, Nuts&Bolts appears and we could have killed it, looted the item been gone.

But, no......
Evidently 15 minutes and some common courtesy is too much for Boogster and Gallry. They attack and kill steal N&B not just once, but twice. Gallry at least was ashamed enough not to argue but Boogster kept whining about there was no such thing as kill stealing and that it's all Funcoms fault anyway for allowing a level 200 to do the mission. Blah, Blah...

The problem now is that while they are kill stealing from us more people show up with the same attitude ie: if I can take it I will....

So, after the first jerks we now have a parade of punks pulling the same sh*t. Redsnaper and Reznr, and the list goes on.

We ended up leaving.

One of the best things about MM games is all the cool people that you can meet. Unfortunatly some people just don't get that aspect. Personally I don't give a damn about some stupid item for a silly quest. I could flick the switch on Rubi-Ka and not lose a moment of sleep. But I surprised that some people care so little about common decency and their reputations that they would pull a stunt like this over something so trivial...

Kwerk
