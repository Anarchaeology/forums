While UO doesn't stop you from doing rampart ganking and PvPing, it does have game imposed consequences for your actions... not quite as good as AC, but it's better than the crap in AO.

I don't PvP at all, in fact, I think someone has even attacked my party once in now over a year of playing. I do, however, travel through PvP zones all the time on my current server. It is rare, and I mean like less than once a month, that I lay eyes on anyone else in any of these zones. That is already a gigantic squandering of resources. Why throw even more away on an entire server? People don't use what is there to be utilized now;

Because I don't PvP at all, I'm sick and tired of changes being made to this game to accomodate the miniscule number of people who do. It is the source of every single nerf. From my point of view, resources that could be far better spent actually improving the game for everyone are constantly diverted into supporting this tiny segment of the population;

Speaking of resources, customer service on a PvP server takes on a whole new meaning. The amount of effort that is required to properly support the players on a PvP server is more than significant - it's astonishingly so. Maybe you feel that FC has all their ducks in a row in this area, I do not share that opinion.
