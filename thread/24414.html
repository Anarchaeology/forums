DJ Tarryk's Birthday Bash (Sun May 26 8pm RK1)
It's coming up on that time of year. The time of year when I think to myself "What do I want for my birthday?" It falls on a Sunday, and I got to thinking...Sure, there's plenty of stuff I want to do on my birthday, but what do I want to do that night after my birthday festivities are over?

I want to party. I want to party until my head spins off and explodes, or until I pass out, whichever comes first.

And so, without further ado (I like that word. Ado. ... Adooo! A Dew! I could use a Dew right now. Lots of sugar in Dew, though. Perhaps A Diet Dew. adietdo? Without further adietdo...), here's the announcement...


{ DJ Tarryk's }
{Birthday Bash}


That's right, Reetheads! This is going to be the one day of the year that it really is all about me, and I'm going to spread that power all over Reet's Retreat like it ain't nobody's biz!!

I'll be starting off with the usual powerhouse mix of music, and then when things wind up a bit, I plan to blast your ears off with all-new megamixes of techno, industrial, metal, and grindcore that I've been saving for this very day!

The Event: DJ Tarryk's Birthday Bash

The Location: Reet's Retreat in Stret West Bank on RK1

The Date: Sunday, May 26th, 2002

The Time: 8pm EST (12am GMT)

The Duration: Until my ears start BLEEDING from the level of volume my speakers will be set at while I crank out hardcore ragetech and grindcore.

Hope to see you there! h00t h00t!
