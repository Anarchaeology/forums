For non-PvP (referring to PvM/outside boss camps, etc.) - there should be a separate MK shield for them. It should be rare and do a 3 min / 3 min maneuver. 90% not 75% - 3 minutes with 90% reflect with a 3 min deprive. This PvM nano should not affect players meaing that if they try and use it in PvP, all player attacks go through it. This would still maintain the balance and help with some of the lackluster defense soldiers have in the PvM areas.

Also, they should have another weapons buff that increases their AR by 150 points.

I fully support ALL profession balancing and would like to see the NTs get their love in 14.6-14.8 like it was promised, have soldiers and engineers get their love, and then see Crats get their fixes.
