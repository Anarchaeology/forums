Everyone wants the optimal setup. What's wrong with that? How many people IRL say "SCREW THIS! I DON'T WANT ANY STINKING MONEY! EVERYONE ELSE HAS MONEY, AND IT MAKES ME SICK HOW THEY'RE ALL CLONES OF EACH OTHER". Doesn't happen that often, does it? Everyone wants to be the best.
Actually the real point is to have no one armor/weapon be "the best" for all situations. First off, if they had wanted there to be variety in armor, they should never have made it so all armor gives AC for everything. Having most armors be specialized to 2 or 3 different ACs and have NO protection in the rest would mean folks would be required to have multiple armor sets and pick a set right for a group of enemies they'd be fighting. For one thing, it would make AC buffs pretty darn sought after.

As for weapons, if they did the same with mob ACs, and made the mob evades such that most mobs are weak in one type of evade, then you would have to select the right weapon for the right occasion as well.

Unfortunately, Funcom already missed their chance for that, and changing it now would result in a somewhat universal "NERF!" scream from almost the entire playerbase. In fact, a change like that might make quite a few leave. Also, with Funcom ignoring innovative ideas like those posted in the "Dream Mission..." thread and a continued increase in camping, this game is going to actually get even worse.

My little MA hates the pajamas and I have adopted a much more unique and stylish approach while maintaining good AC and no matter what I do, I have HP hanging out my rear compared to my MP, so I never worry about it.

MA solitus, average height (except when i got the shrinking bracers on) and weight

-Back slot: Aura Magnifier (my only truly rare and kinda uber item)
Adds +20 to all damage types and +15 to MA

-Chest: QL200 kirch vest. I lose 80hp versus an mk2 chest (which I also have), but gain 20 to all evades, 20 to parry, which allows me to use a ql200 BwT, 20 to riposte (heh not used yet), and 5 to psychic, sense and agility. No way MK2 beats that...

-Sleeves: Spirit Focus tattoos which have little AC, add a little hp and each one adds 7 to martial arts. Oh yeah, that's a keeper. BS missions are too fast, I don't worry too much about dying so the little loss in hp and ac is worth the tradeoff to pack a slightly bigger punch

-Legs: QL175 kirch pants. looks better than my MK2 pants, 5 to sense, agility and psychic is worth the small loss in hp

-Boots: MK2, it looks like kevlar. I played with so8, which looked decent but I deemed the hp hit might be a bit much with that one

-Gloves: MK2, it matches

-Head: MK2 hood, see above

-Shoulders: Alvin pads (omni pads look SO much better)

So I got this sorta rugged biker look going for me, with some nice bonuses. Best thing about it is if the boss room is nasty, I hot swap for the red pajamas and mk2 sleeves for some more hp.

I recently got slammed by Zylina for wearing Kirch instead of MK2, which I totally did not understand, as I think my look is somewhat unique and somewhat stylish and I stand out in a crowd.

Now major props goes out to Squiggle2, Atrox MA, who I see roaming newland alot. Near as I can tell he's got

so8 sleeves and gloves
MK2 chest
so8 pants
mk2? boots
mk2 hood

On an Atrox, that setup looks pretty pimp and he's an easy guy to spot.

so overall, there's nothing wrong with being a bit different. In this game, there's too much of a drive to be like the next guy, as it is the 'best' setup. My advice is to find something with good bonuses, yet maintain a unique sense of style that sets you apart from the other folks in the game. Cookie cutter can be boring (thedeacon is as generic as they come with full mk2, albrecht and x3).

Create a separate template for pvm regular, pvm boss, pvp, social, etc.
