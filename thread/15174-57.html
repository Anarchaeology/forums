Do your maths!
First of all - thank you Funcom for trying to correct the rules of the game to what it always should have been. I have read some response here in this thread and I have some comments on it.

1. 20% OE IS 25% INCREASED PERFORMANCE, NOT 20%
Do your maths! Wearing a 20% OE item actually gives you 25% increased performance and not 20%, since the 20% check is based on the item that you wear, and not your skill! Hexxia made calculations about the optimal weapon and it was all wrong, maybe except for the conclusion about 19% OE items being the best choice from now. Let me explain!
What is a 20% over-eqiupped weapon? Hexxia: "19% OE = 100% + 19% = 119% w/ No penalty = 119% dmg" Nonononononono! =P
Imagine that your skill is 100. According to Hexxia, a gun that require 119 in skill is the perfect gun for you then, right?
Now, lets start in the RIGHT end and calculate this. 20% of 119 (the required skill of the item) is 23.8 and 119-23.8 is your NEEDED skill too pass the 20% OE check. According to Hexxia this should be 100, but it's not - it's 95.2! In other words if your skill is 100 you can have a better item (actually one that requires 124.999999999...in skill).
Again: Wearing a 20% OE item actually gives you:

2 5 % I N C R E A S E D P E R F O R M A N C E

...and not 20%, since the 20% check is based on the item that you wear, and not your skill.

2. TRADER'S HIGH WRANGLERS STILL NEEDED!
The need for the traders' wranglers might from now on have a limited use, as someone pointed out. There is no need to wrangle any skill when it only lasts for a couple of minutes, is there? Your weapon or armor will loose capacity once the buffs run out. Your pet won't obey your commands.
Well, this is true - and not true! Picture this - lower quality level gear (or nanos) have lower skill requirements, thus 20% OE will be a lot of skill points compared to your base skill, if you are a low level character. However, let's say that you are a level 160 Doc with 800 in shotgun skill. 125% of 800 euqals 1000 - so you can wear any shotgun that requires less than 1000 in skill and pass the 20% penalty check. If your skill is 800, you need +200 points to equip this gun, off course. This is where the higher wranglers come into the game. Suddenly it is worth the trouble and the money to pay for that +132 buff! Same goes for pets!

Higher quality gear require much more skill than lower stuff - we all know that. To compensate for the 20% of the requirements that you need to buff, the buffs must be stronger for higher QL items, such as shown above. Someone said that wranglers should be extended in time to make the trader profession more attractive. I partly agree on this, and with the fact that only 4% of all characters on Rubi-Ka are traders, we must all be very poor.

3. IP REALLOCATION POINTS & ABILITIES
I agree with some posts that this issue with 90 points of some sort have not been explained very well.
Then also remember - there are skills, and there are abilities. If you reset an ability, all the skills that depend on that ability have to drop as well... in other words if there is no way you can do a "complete IP wipe" - I believe that you will not be able to change your abilities, but only your skills. Correct me if I am wrong. I really wonder how this will work once it hits the servers.

4. GREAT ADDITIONS
I have been waiting a long time for the proposed concealment-perception check. Spying and detecting people and objects will finally become a part of the game.

5. INFO-TOOLS (Just a thought)
The info-tools that exist today are very poor. When you encounter a mob in a mission, you know it's aggressive - you don't need an info tool to tell you. When you look at its Hitpoints when you fight it, you will know how much it has in total, roughly - you don't need an info tool! What does it matter what level it is? BAH!
I call for some usable info-tools like:
- What type of damage will the NPC deal?
- What weapon is the NPC using?
- How high is the NPC's AC and evade skills?
- Tracking Device! - Sneak up on someone and put a bug on his coat, sit back and track his movements on Rubi-Ka. This is true spying. ... ok maybe I got a little excited here.

6. READ THE FIRST MSG IN THE THREAD
People should read the first comment in the thread before starting asking all these stupid questions about checks on implants and belts.
