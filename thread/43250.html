I was not born... I was created...
Start recording
Starting Blackglow personal log

I was not born I was created

I am Marvin Blackglow Caris. In a breeding tube at Omni-Prime, I was created to work for an organisation called simply Genesis. They educated me in the field of nano technology and the meta aspect of it. I earned the profession title Meta-Physicist and was then sent to the planet Rubi-Ka. Actually, I did not know then why they chose me to travel to the red and dry planet. But what I discovered later on, when I returned to Omni-Prime was that I already before coming to Rubi-Ka had been assigned to work with the Omni-Tek department SPARTA. And that they had requested my presence before I had arrived on the planet. I still have not asked my dear friend and the Director of SPARTA, Garret Silvestrini why I was chosen. But I guess in time he will tell me.

When I first arrived to Rubi-Ka, I found that the planet held much more than you could see on the surface. I had settled in, in my apartment after arriving with the shuttle to Omni-1 and started going through the packetmails I had reserved from home (still called it my home at this time) Omni-Prime. I got very surprised to hear my door signal sound. I went to open the door. Outside stood a man in a black InternOps suit and black sunglasses. Bald head except for a greyish ponytail in the back of the head. The rain was pouring down over him but before I got a chance to invite him in, he started speaking.

Hello Mr. Caris he paused. You do not know me. My name is well you can call me Bonefish. The tone of his voice indicated no stress, no feelings at all actually. He kept on talking.

I need your expertise in a matter that has arisen. I will get back to you in a few days. As I looked at him mistrusting, he continued. Check your packetmail Mr. Caris. Genesis should have sent you the details by now. I took a step back. I could not believe then how he could know about Genesis. Who was this man? After this, he turned and walked away.

Now I know that the man was and still is Garret Bonefish Silvestrini. A very dear friend and my employer. All that he had said checked out and he returned a few days later. With my help, Garret and the former SPARTA did a lot of high priority missions. (I cannot go in to detail here because I would have to sit here recording forever). Most of them successful. After a time I was called back to Omni-Prime for yet another period of training and studying and to report about the notum and nanobots that are the soul of our planet. After a couple of months I was told that I was going back to Rubi-Ka and that I was to be contacted by Garret Silvestrini and that I no longer was officially was working for or with Genesis. When I later arrived to Rubi-Ka Garret was already waiting at the shuttle bay in Omni-1. I still remember his first words to me Its time for the interview. and he looked at me and smiled.

Now I had joined SPARTA. A beginning I never could have imagined

Marvin Caris
Deputy Director  SPARTA

End recording
ending Blackglow personal log

Marvin turns off his recorder on his arm. Raises his head to look out the window of his office in Rome Blue. He is surprised over how dark it is and a little disappointed to have missed the setting suns. Suddenly a voice alerts him of another presence in the doorway.

Hey Marv you look like you could use a cold one. Marvin turns around to see his old friend Garret standing in the doorway. I think the gang is at Rompa's. Maybe we should go join them? Marvin nods. He is tired. But as always he knows that Garret is not asking him to go and party, it is probably work. Garret smiles at the fact that his friend knows him all to well.
