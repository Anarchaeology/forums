Why no sprinting clusters?
Why aren't there any Nano Clusters of Sprinting yet? Everyone wants them, so why doesn't funcom deliver. Why should run speed be the one attribute that you cannot enhance with implants? I've heard of some players being offered sprinting implants as mission rewards, but never the clusters just by themselves.

I would like to see these in game. Some of the slots on some of the implants don't offer much, but atleast Run Speed is something everyone can use.

As an example, you can take the bright slot on a left-wrist implant. There are a total of four options for what can go in this slot, one of them being sprinting, but due to the fact that the sprinting clusters don't exist, you only have tree oprtions; energy slicing, parrying, retaliation. I don't need any of those, and there's probably only a hand full of people who do. What we do need is a way to run as fast as we can when we're being insta-killed by these uber mobs!

Please Funcom, out these seeminglly miniscule items in the game...

We want sprinting nano clusters!
