One last tip of the hat...
Well, I never PVP-ed much, but most of the farewells seem to be posted here so I figured I'd throw mine in with the lot.

At long last I've grown tired of battling with AO. The lack of content, the endless missions, the infighting, favoritism and politicing is all just too much. It stopped being fun for me quite some time ago. The only reason I stayed was for a handful of people whom I see less and less.

Alas, that asinine scene in PW just moments ago was the final straw... If this is what someone in Funcom views as content then there's really nothing left to see here. It's been almost a year and, well, that's enough time to put into something that is no longer satisfying. It's like a relationship, right? At some point you just need to cut it loose and move on to that next adventure.

To the many of you whom I count as dear friends, I'll miss each and every one of tremendously. You know who you are. I am so, so very sorry for the abrupt departure but I know myself and it wouldn't have happened had I spent time on the farewells. There's no question I would have gotten sucked back on some silly excuse and, well, I didn't want that to happen.

At any rate, I may pop back in come Shadowlands, but that will grow more and more unlikely by the day. I spammed the guild with my email and hope we won't lose touch, though it is probably inevtiable. As well, feel free to ping me at snapuel@hotmail.com.

See ya', gang. Thanks for some great times...
