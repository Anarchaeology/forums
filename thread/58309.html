More health for the Atrox, and more nano pool for the Nanomage
In 14.6 we do some changes to the two most extreme breeds in Anarchy Online; Atrox and Nanomage.

Atrox will get 1 more point in max health per rank in the Body Development skill.

Nanomage will get 1 more point in max nano pool per rank in the Nano Pool skill.

For reference:

All breeds except Nanomage currently gets 3 extra max health per skill rank. Nanomage gets 2. Atrox will, after 14.6, get 4.

All breeds except Atrox currently gets 3 extra max nano pool per skill rank. Atrox gets 2. Nanomage will, after 14.6, get 4.

Thread open for comments and discussion.

Adding more nano pool to the nanomage breed certainly does no harm.


But please keep in mind:


* HP is far more important than NP, even more so at high level than low level. I'd personally trade off NP for HP at a 2:1 or 3:1 ratio. Check out the nanomage population in the database. Just about every last one is using Life implants in preference to anything else that can go in those slots. However many, including me, can do without max nano or nanopool implants.

* Even NTs, the most gluttonous consumers of nano pool, don't generally have real problems with nano pool at high level.

* Nanomage is clearly the most inferior breed.


In other words, I'm saying that adding some more nano pool to nanomage breed is not enough, and I'm also saying that I would happily trade the extra nano pool for HP. I know that's not going to happen, but there should be some other benefit to the breed instead of NP. This has been discussed at great length, and I don't remember a single player post saying that they wanted more nanopool. Instead suggestions like higher nano resistance, more NCU, less chance to be countered, elimination of fumbles, etc. etc. etc. have been constantly suggested.

Please don't let the apparently pleasing symmetry of balancing an Atrox HP bonus with a Nanomage NP bonus distract you from the fact that the Nanomage NP bonus is a very minor and almost unnoticeable benefit added to the weakest breed in the game.

Oh yeah, as regards Opifex, they should perhaps have some new advantage too; the thing that comes to mind offhand is a little higher evades, since they are supposed to be so slippery and agile and all that.... Or maybe a built in don't-critical-me bonnus?
