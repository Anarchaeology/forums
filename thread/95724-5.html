Emiliy
Soleet
Silence. Little else echoed within her relatively bare apartment in West Athen aside from the occasional waft from turning of pages, intermixed with the odd whimper or sob. Night had fallen, both on Rubi-Ka and on her brother, and all that seemed to follow was an abyss of loneliness and fear.

Emiliy could find little comfort in her surroundings; the euphoria that had seized at her the moment she had walked into this new flat - this new home - had vanished without a trace at the moment Lynar had said "he's dead." Taking to the streets - and to Newland - was similarly incapable of quelling the tears in her eyes and sorrow in her rapidly beating heart. Even as she had passed cautiously through Newland, eyeing every face, tuning in on every whisper, and finally arriving at the place that Alamexis had spent his final seconds of life, peace evaded her, to be replaced slowly with an ever-building rage.

And so she sat in her apartment. And she read. Slowly she became enthralled in the words - his words - printed upon the pages. And despite scattering tears across almost every page, Emiliy finally drifted off to sleep with a slight smile on her face, holding the book tightly.

TEST COMPLETE -- NO ERRORS: DNA SAMPLE RECORDED

TEST COMPLETE -- NO ERRORS: DNA SAMPLE RECORDED AND MATCHED TO 100% ACCUARACY

Well, now that there was no doubt that Alamexis was the victim, she continued her tests on the bomb fragments.

TEST COMPLETE: HIGH GRADE COFFEE -- MANUFACTURER: NOT IN DATA BASE -- KNOWN TO BE IMPORTED BY SEVERAL CLAN TRADERS -- SAMPLE CONTAMINATED BY MUTATED NANO-BOTS

Interesting Cathern thought. Why would coffee have been in the bomb? That didn't make sense. Unless...could the bomb have been concealed in a package of coffee? That's possible. The large presence of nano-bots indicated what? She would have to get back to that.

TEST COMPLETE: MARK 7 SPECIAL DENTONATOR -- MANUFACTURER: OMNI-TEK -- KNOWN TO BE USED IN NANO EXPLOSIVE DEVICES -- SAMPLE CONTAMINATED BY MUTATED NANO-BOTS

Well...that was no suprise. Omni-Tek has long been the leader in military technology. Still, these were not that easy to get a hold of.

The next piece looked like an empty receptive nano-bot container. Still, to be sure, into the machine it went.

TEST COMPLETE: RECEPTIVE NANO-BOT CONTAINER -- MANUFACTURER: NANOS-R-US -- SAMPLE CONTAMINATED BY MUTATED NANO-BOTS

Could the container have been used to store the mutated nano-bots instead of the ones normally used?

The next two items were relativly intact. They seemed to be poison injectors. Cathern placed them into the machine one at a time...and got same results.

TEST COMPLETE: MARK 17 POISON INJECTORS -- MANUFACTURER: OMNI-TEK -- MARK 17 POISON USED IN PERMENTANT DEATH DEVICES -- PRIVATE OWNERSHIP OF MARK 17 POISON FORBIDDEN UNDER OMNI-TEK LAW -- SAMPLE CONTAMINATED BY MUTATED NANO-BOTS

Cathern checked the time. The whole evening had past. One more test and maybe she could rest.

She promgrammed the device to identify the mutated nano-bots that was on all the items.

TEST COMPLETE: NANOBOTS -- ERROR -- NANOBOTS ALTERED TO UNKNOWN SPECIFICATION -- EXTRAPOLATING POSSIBLE EFFECTS OF ALTERED NANOBOTS -- RESULT: ALTERED NANOBOTS WOULD BE LETHAL TO HOMO-SOLITUS BREED WHEN COMBINED WITH MARK 17 POISON; RECLAIM TECHNOLOGY FAILURE RATE OF 97%
