Join Nomadic Spirits
Why join us?

We are a fast growing guild in the world of Rubi-Ka #1. Although we have a small number of members currenly, we will alter the course of the storyline greatly through in-game Sunday meetings or our member's only message board. All items posted in our guild chat will be FREE, since helping each other members will keep our clan strong. We participates in many events, such as guilds meetings, where we discuss our current plans for our future, and what road we should take.

Your voice will be heard by us.
