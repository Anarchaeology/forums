More newbie tips
Some more to Keikoa's newbie tips:

Tell your team to use the same person for tanking/pulling all the time, put an extra HP buff and a lasting heal type of nano (that heals every 5 sec or so for 2 min) on him/her.

If you hunt real baddies, tell your team to check if You are ready before they start each attack. Most people understands this without telling them, but it's better to say it and get a "yeye we know..." than a "hey wtf HEAL MEEE!!!111111" and then "omg ffs, you suck doc!"

Don't stand in the middle of the action, move away a bit. Make's the mobs less aggro on you.

Tell your team mates to run in circles if they get in trouble, hate when they all run in different directions after beeing overrrun by mobs and then complain about me not healing. FFS do you run away from the hospital when you're about to bleed to death?.

Use the F1-F6 keys on your keyboard to quickly select the team mates and use the 1-0 shortcut keys to heal.

If someone's in trouble and you need to make a fast rescue, move closer to the action, attack and do some team heals, might save your team mates lives, not yours, cause you will aggro all mobs attacking the team, but you'll be a hero.

Use Nano Kit's early, you need all the nano you can get.

If you get aggroed a lot, put a lasting heal on yourself before each mob, takes about half your nano, recharge, start fighting.

Ok in response to the last post, I will explain my points. If you have a doc you will know that the main healing is Bio and Mat met. the problem is that these share spots on implants. Also if you play a nanomage doc, Int/psy also share spots. This means you have to choose to what is more important. In my humble opinion, it is Int/ Bio met. That means your psy is going to be lacking, and so will your mat met. To get around this, you use a weapon that will raise your psy. That would be the vektor shotgun, 15 psy gain from a ql 73 shotgun is great. If you use nano armour, then you can put it on alot sooner. The O.E.T. Jess pistols, you can dual wield, this is more ip, but when you can put on a ql 200 pistol dual wielded and get +50 int and +50 psy, that isn't something to turn your nose at. The lowest i have found them at is ql 15 and they gave +5.


Also in my opinion, Bio met, Mat met, and T&S are the main nano skills you need for a healing doc. The other one are for buffs, and combat. If i cast a debuff, it ups my agro for the creature.
