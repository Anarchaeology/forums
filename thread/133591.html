"I'll just grab a sandwich and post some more clan hate mail hehe I am sure Pryo wont mind" After making sure the coast is clear he inserts the key card and goes into the secret passage. waking the corridor he stops abruptly. "What the hell is this, the damn door is gone!!" He prods, he shakes, he batters, he curses and he spits but no door.
