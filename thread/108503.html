Name Withheld
As most of you already know last night Name Withheld was disbanded by it's former leader Toggles.

I'd like to let everyone know exactly what occured and what steps I intend to take the keep the newly reformed org alive.

Yesterday evening after a meeting with Toggles and some of the other leaders and members of Name Withheld, I decided to resign after serving for two weeks as temporary leader. This resignation was due to disagreements in decisions and comments made by the former leader.

After my resignation, Toggles disbanded the org for reasons unknown to me. No explaination nor any warning was given to the members or myself before it occurred so we were all left wondering exactly what happened. Not only did he shun the membership but he also dumped several million credits, or took them, before disbanding.

After speaking with Britain of DA and some former NW guildmates I decided to reform NW. With their help and the help of some other DA members we managed to accomplish this. So far I've had several members express their wishes to return and also have had many tells confirming support from other guilds, mainly Devil's Advocate.

I'd like to thank everyone for their support and help and I'd also like to apologise to all the members of NW who were left out in the cold after Toggles rash actions.

I'd also like to let everyone know that NW will not be continuing down the path it was prior to the reformation. Some bad decisions and mistakes were made. The guild was used as a tool for revenge by it's former leader against other Omni orgs. I want to reassure you all that this bahavior will not continue. We have a fresh face and big things will be happening in our future, that I can promise.

Thanks again to everyone for your support, I look forward to more cooperation in the future.
