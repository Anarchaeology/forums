Waning Philosophical
I was bored with AO itself a long time ago. I almost left.

I have been a mother and counselor for so many people here, and it is what has kept me going. Being a passionate person, and sometimes melodramatic, I have been touched by many hearts here, who opened up when they did not have to. I took the risk of opening up to them too, and have been hurt at times, but with no risk, there is no reward. I have learned from many, and have been blessed by many.

I almost left once, recently even, but did not, simply because just ONE PERSON asked me to stay. That was all it took %). I gained a new family, for which I will be eternally grateful, and a best friend.

Now, that person may leave, because many other close friends are leaving. Is one person reason enough to stay? Well it was for me, but I am not everyone else.

Am I hurt by all these goings on recently? Yes. Have I lost sleep over this? Yes. I have been told I care too much, that I am too honest at times. Big deal, it is who I am, and when things work, they are so much better because I chose to take that step. Anyone who knows me knows I love people, and I hope not to change. Was it my choice to reach out to others? Yes. So what happens? I cannot expect some company to change the world, that is up to us. I cannot expect some silly game to make a difference, but it HAS, for some people, this may be all they have. They work as hard as anyone, spending hours upon hours, forming relationships, and meeting friends, feeling emotions, and creating real MEMORIES!

Funcom, what you read in these postings is not all anger at the company, it is frustration at all these emotions and memories being suddenly disrupted by a few simple acts. At least they are saying something (If you weed out the whining). It means we care, about life here, AND each other. This is a wonderful dream, and sadly its as if someone keeps waking us up before it is finished. Regardless of what people say, the answer is NOT in PvP, buffs, nanos, patches, and "profession love." It is in US, in all that we ourselves create here, within the limits that seem to be getting tighter and tighter.

If you look, you will see wonderful creativity that people have. So many have been resourceful in working within the confines of what is here. It is amazing! But it is still something meaningful that we all seek, wheter we know it or not. People to spend time with, to live and die with, even if virtually, THAT creates lasting friendships. Relationships are hard enough here as it is, everything is all intensified, accelerated, it's a soap opera. But the struggle is even harder now. Misunderstandings are too easy, (I know, I have been misunderstood and have misunderstood and that is part of life .

And now my friends are leaving, and there is little I can do to stop it. Yes there are plenty more people, but THESE people matter to me! How can I help? What can I do? I do not know.

I feel like this is a good dream turning bad...I want to scream, but when you scream in a dream, no one can hear you....
