Another route is:

From Athen East gate head SE into Aegean. Keep heading SE until ya find the rope bridge crossing the Stret. Just SE from here is the zone into USEB. Just East of where ya zone is a Rhinoman village. Complete with two huge spires on the shore of the Stret (I have no idea what they are)

There is a small crossing over the Stret again continuing East. From here following the dirt road into the woods will lead ya to a devastated town called Safe Haven. Talk to Delphine Sabbatt to get the history

South of the crossing is a small OT Outpost in Anarchic Hill area. There is a single OT Captain there and i think an Ins term and Reclaim.

*thinks some more*

A long way South (Following the shoreline of the Stret) is Bow Point. This is a major Omni outpost. Last time i was there it was swarming with Omni-Pol, altho they arent usually in that area. I assume that there are Ins Terms etc there.

Other interesting things to see are the Nokkens in the Barchlows Jump area. Its a short distance SE of where ya originally wouldve crossed the Stret. They can be found in a swampy valley. Ive seen a maximum of 6 there on occasions. Very impressive lumps of ..um ... mud and trees

Ive probably missed some bits n peices. Ill try and remember anything else
