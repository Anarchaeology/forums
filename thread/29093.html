Neutral Faction Unites II
Neutral Faction Unites II

ICC Observers vote about joining the neutral faction is done. With nearly 90% of the votes positve, a clear statement for unification was made.

Around Fri., June 14.06., the ICC Observers will join in the neutral faction. With this unification of the two biggest neutral guilds on Rubi Ka 2 the most powerfull neutral organisation is about to be born.

As merging plans will be done by this act, the then formed neutral faction will held an election of their President. High rank officers from NCSO dep and ICC Observers in Advisor rank will asure participation of all joined in groups.

Our major goal to establish an overall respect for neutrality will be supported by a strong voice from this time on!

Due to some changes during the merging procedure, neutral faction will return to action with the begin of July.
Contacts with the represantatives of Project Revenant are made.
