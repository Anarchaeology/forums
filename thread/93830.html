Edit: Thanks!

Lyricia, a mitaar is an old instrument consisting of a wooden resonance box and four to twelve strings made of metal or dried animal guts. The strings are different length and thickness and produces different sounds when struck. The player also presses the strings down against the wood to shorten them, to produce even more tones. It's quite complicated and nothing like modern instruments. It's still used today by some folk singers who want to get that genuine sound however.
