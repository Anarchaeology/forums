Cheetra
Phear Leet
I believe it was Gen Paton that said - No one ever defended anything successfully - there is only attack attack and attack some more.


IF they hit your base - you hit theirs, only harder. NW does not lend itself to the defenders having an advantage. The only advantage you get is that the towers themselves will fight the attackers also - and that's small concilation when you're talking about bases that are under ql 200.

If you want your base to live - here a few suggestions on tactics:

1) you have a QL range for your base - USE IT.

That means - dont' ONLY place the highest QL towers it will fit. There are range caps on PVP that you can use to your advantage as a defender. Place low and place high ( NOTE do not EVER place a 150 tower - at 150 they are open to attack by every level 200 around ).

2) construct wisely.

Place defense in depth if at all possible - keep 3 turrets of various QL's on your CT. and then load that area up with condutors that will effect how well your turrets perform. Some reduce nano skills, some reduce health, some reduce Defense on attackers.

3) the base does not exist to provide *you* with specific tower based advantages / bonues. The base exists to provide your ORG with universal advantages. Cordinate your tower placements and types inorder to make your base as defensible as possible - forget about personal benies from a particular tower if it doesn't figure in well to defending a base.

4) if you have the chance choose land that that's NOT anywhere near a whoompa, grid or fixer grid ext.



War is not about equality - it's about money, time, numbers and being better then the other guy - and booting him so hard when he hits you that thinks twice before doing it again.
