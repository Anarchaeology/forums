Attack Petz...
It's been quite some time since I graced the many pages of the forums, but here I am at last returned after many months away at home. And when I return I find that not all is well.
I hear rumors and whispers that the MP pet...the attack pet in particular, is quite a piece of useless crap after 150th level.
Heh perhaps even before!
I state this because, from what I've heard and observed, the cacodemon is not quite powerful enough to stand up to a lot of the wild life an MP of that level would find themselves facing.
True? False?
I'll let you be the judges of this question and decide for your selves.
HOWEVER!!!!
Should this accusation be in the affirmative, I've thought of an alternative plan that, should the miracle of FC actually pursuing this take shape, may prove to be one of the most beneficial patches for MP pets ever....thus far at least.

I present to you the "IP Pet."
And perhaps there is already an incarnation out there in someone's head or drawing board but...whatever...It's my turn.

The IP pet is a simple and effective idea.
It brings into the game a very real experience with your pet as well as answers the question of whether or not the pets of the Meta-Physicisit are genuinely a part of him/her.
The answer is of course...YES!
At certain levels of experience, every MP finds that they are capable of enlisting the use of a better pet nano.
What I suggest is, instead of investing so much time and effort into a huge assortment of attack pets for use throughout all levels of the MPs life. Instead, remove some of the more useless pets (and there are quite a few) and replace them all with individual Point upgradable pets.
When an MP reaches a new level he/she has the option of spending some of their own IP points into the given "attributes" of their pet.
This way it puts more control of the pet's power level and abilities into the players hands, makes the concept that a pet is a physical manifestation of part of that characters psyche and also erradicates the need for a multitude of useless and unwanted pets. it also, if done properly, could go a long way to bring the fun of the MP pet back into the PVP scene.

Contemplate this of the tree on woe.....
