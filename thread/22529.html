Malibu Battle Barbie - all female clan on RK-1
Malibu Battle Barbie is founded on the idea of having fun and helping others. We are here to revel and level.

We're an informal sisterhood with only three rules:

1) Have fun. If you're not having fun, it's just not worth it.

2) Try to be nice to people. When there's a confrontation, take things in stride and avoid shouting obscenities. Try to see the other person's side and be fair. You don't have to be a saint--some people are looking for trouble and no one prevent them from finding it. Give as good as you get. Give better, even. But keep it civil. Hate is extremely unattractive.

3) Try to help others when you can (especially other MBBs). We don't expect you to entertain blatent beggers (begging is obnoxious and rude). We don't expect you to give up all your playing time or your credits, either. When you see someone in need, follow your heart. Encourage people by your example. Tell them to pay it forward.

If this sounds good to you, please send me an in-game tell to discuss an invite:

Venusrising - 23 Crat

If you have trouble catching me online, you can contact any of our other members about an invite. For the full list, see http://www.anarchy-online.com/org/st...1/name/1720322

You can also find out more about us by visiting our Yahoo group page at http://groups.yahoo.com/group/malibubattlebarbie/

Doing our duty, with strength *and* beauty!
