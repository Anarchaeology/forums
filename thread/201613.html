Fun in the air
Damon walks along the pathway leading along to the teleportation rings in Jobe-Market and suddenly stops. Stretching out before him, the layer of clouds, make an apparently endless fluffy land. He carefully steps to the edge, and crouches, looking down as he leans against a small pylon. Nothing but clouds could be seen below. Standing and grinning slightly, Damon pulls out his black trench coat and a small white device with a red button on it.
"DIYA, stay here. I'll be back very shortly." he says to the large bot standing behind him as he pulls the coat on, leaving it open.
"Wait command received. Waiting..." replied DIYA.
Damon turns and looks over the edge as he grips the white controller firmly. "Gwen is going to kill me if she finds out about this, if it doesn't...” he chuckles as he suddenly jumps forward, his foot hitting the very edge of the platform, shoving him far into the air. As he arcs slightly before falling, he spreads his arms wide into the air, curving as he starts to fall, head first, his hair blown straight back by the wind. A grin forms on his face as he plunges downward, the clouds rushing up at him.
As he reaches the tops of the clouds, he pulls his arms down to his sides, streamlining himself as he plunges through, water vapor collecting on his face. The wind whips past, catching the tails of his coat, causing it to flap wildly.
As he starts to be able to make out the ground below, he stabs the red button with his thumb. In his ear implant he hears "Yalmaha activation failed. Drive failure."
"No...." he yells as he watches the ground below. He spins mid-air, his back toward the ground as he pulls himself into a ball around the yalmaha key, stabbing the button futilely.
"Well, glad I scanned..." he says as he turns back, watching the ground. He keeps hitting the button with his thumb as he falls. Just as he starts to make out the shape of the rocks and fauna of the forest below he hears "Yalamha activating please wait".
"Yes... come on now...” he hisses through his teeth as the ground looms closer. He spreads his arms again against the wind as he feels the nanobots spread over him, forming the skin of the Yalm. As the activation finalizes he hits the throttle hard and pulls back, as the nose rises and engines scream, he sees the tops of the trees loom past. The engines hit in harder as he pulls back, shooting horizontally, blowing leaves and rocks every direction from the jet wash of the Yalm engines. As he just pulls out of the fall, he hears a loud screeeeeeeeaaaach as he Yalm scrapes along the rocks before shooting back up into the air.
Breathing deeply again, Damon points his Yalm in the direction of Newland and puts it on autopilot. Leaning back, he runs his hand through his hair, pushing the dishelmed locks back out of his face as he closes his eyes. Sitting there, after a few long breaths, he suddenly laughs, a huge grin forming on his lips.
"I want to do it again......"

A little leet looks up, then dives under a bush just before a man falling out of the sky is suddenly encompassed by a Yalmaha and shoots over her head.

The little leet presses a button on a tiny bracelet, morphing herself back into a small, tanned solitus woman with shining auburn hair. She adjusts her Ruby-colored specs and activates her own Yalm, hearing a faint and crazy-sounding laughter from the now distant man/Yalm.

She flips open her comm unit, smirking, and sends a little note to Gwen. Then she giggles as she sends another little note to Damon:
