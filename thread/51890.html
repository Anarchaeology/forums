Shootout in Hope Leaves mystery, Reclaimed Bodies. (FNN News Story)
Shootout in Hope leaves mystery, reclaimed Bodies.

An incident in a bar in Hope left more than a few people in Reclaim,
and sparked a bit of controversy between Vox populi, Rogues Gallery,
and Shattered Dreams.

A meeting between General Elizabet "Myiria" Alsaqri of Vox Populi, and Damon "Larsonny" Moscardelli of Rogues Gallery, led to a shootout in which several neutral bystanders were sent to reclaim.

The nature of the meeting is unknown, but apparently to situation got very heated as an unnamed third party came into play during the meeting. The third party was violently sent to reclaim. Shortly thereafter, the sounds of Gunfire echoed throughout the whole town.

I spoke with an eyewitness who says he was there when it happened. He gave his account of what happened:

"Larsonny and Myiria were meeting to discuss something of which I am
unaware. Obviously something shady was going on in that back room...but what exactly it was is still hazy. Apparently there was a 3rd person involved in Larsonny and Myria's meeting. This person was sent to Reclaim by either Larsonny or Myiria (accounts differ), and set the
chain of events in motion. A firefight broke out between Larsonny and Myiria, resulting in Larsonny's trip to Reclaim. After Larsonny was killed, a few Vox troops opened fire on myself, Azyss, and Izis. Allthree of us were sent to Reclaim, while the rest of my people made it out safely. I assume that the Vox troops involved mistakenly assumed we were there to support Larsonny. At any rate, Myiria--stating her side of the story--called in her troops (being the commanding General of the group). She terminated Larsonny, and her troops opened fire on the other Rouges Gallery members standing by. And then her troops proceeded to open fire on us."

Neither Larsonny or Myiria were available for comment.

As of this release, the volatile situation has apparently been resolved through a bit of diplomacy between Guild Leaders. An investigation has been launched, however, to get to the bottom of the situation, and sort things out. I will relay more as the news comes to me in regards to this incident.
