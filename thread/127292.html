There should be a cap of 40% damage on all elements of play in this game. Max 40% that you can do to mobs, and max 40% that mobs can do to players, not just player vs player. Plus, I think this needs some clarification, since your post implies mobs = human mobs. What of creature mobs, that don't really have a reason to be able to do specials? Most creatures are melee, some chemical/poison, even fewer energy/radiation/cold/fire, and if I recall, none projectile (not counting robots as creatures per se). Further, some have "specials" like Stunned, Poisoned, or Open Wound. Even more to the point, being as a vast majority of creatures are melee, and double hit, would you want them to have specials too? Moreover, what is the logic of an Eremite hitting you with Brawl, Fast Attack, or Dimach (for example)?

My question is, when you're asking this, do you mean for EVERY mob, or just human mobs... because with creatures, you start getting into some wierd territory, and they're already not worth fighting as it is.
