I am SO delighted to have actually become visible enough to have made the hate list of the infamous Tobiaswolff. Of course, I'll have to suffer the congratulations, random outbursts of affection, and other embarrassing side effects, as his myriad victims share their stories of woe with me. Truly, though, I'm mystified about the depth of his hate, since he never caused me half the heartache and angst he visited upon others I know.

Now, I must admit that I may have caused a little heartache to a few people, simply because in my early days on Rubi-Ka I was a little too wild for anyone's good. I was the proverbial kid in the candy shop. Fortunately, Blackpetal came along, managed to discover one or two redeeming qualities, and made an honest woman of me! Thanks to her, whatever danger I posed is almost entirely safely past.

Meanwhile, in a perverse sort of way, Tobiaswolff might actually be admired in some circles. I know of at least one omni organization he managed to take over and then completely eliminate – something most clan opponents never accomplish!

Has some perfidious Omni experiment transplanted the soul of Tobiaswolff into Toggles? I speculated about that possibility to a few friends about a month ago, since the way Toggles approached me was remarkably reminiscent of Tobiaswolff. So much so that I was quick to turn him down, and view the entire NW organization with extreme caution. Clearly subsequent events have suggested this was indeed wise. However, my speculations were with people who have absolutely no contact with Meister, so it would appear that two very different people have come to the same conclusion.

Ultimately, though, if the spirit of Tobiaswolff does not live on in Toggles, then the Rubi-Ka will almost certainly be a better place for it. On the other hand, if Toggles develops the same sort of victims and enemies, for the same reasons as Tobiaswolff, it will simply remind us that psychological griefers are a fact of life in any world that encourages social interactions, no matter what name they go under.
