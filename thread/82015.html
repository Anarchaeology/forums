Hmm that depends. There won't be an appreciable difference between a solitus and an opifex doctor at level 90, unless you max agi and sense as an opi and wear agi/sense based armor, instead of the more standard int/psy or psy/sta doctor setups.

My advice, if you're definitely aiming to be a pvp-twink, is to skimp IP in SI and MC (in fact, just be able to cast expertises and don't raise MC at all), but raise PM and TS. Make sure your evades are good and that you have high first aid (and of course the best possible implants). Oh, and get enough nano init to insta-cast with your slider on under 50% agg/def. Use a Recompiler

If you still have IP after getting your PM and TS up, look into a weapon. I'm not sure what to suggest... you almost certainly won't have a lot of IP to put towards an initiative skill or specials, and crit-or-die just won't serve well anymore...

I would look for a ranged weapon that's fairly fast naturally, and is one-skill with one or no specials. For some reason the Flamethrower sticks in my head. Maybe an OT Kerans or Sol Chironis SMG, if you have more IP than I think you will.

I'd really recommend tinkering with the skill emulator before spending much IP.

But again, at level 90, you won't see a ton of difference.

This thread may be a couple weeks old but I am going to have to drudge it up (thx Eunucha for bumping it) and defend the Opifex Doc as I am one.

Opifex doc is quite nice. If you max str/agi/int and raise sta/sen/psy to decent lvl (or max some depending on ip avail) you can put on great armor. With the advantage in agil opi get it makes for putting on str/agi armor higher then others very easy you can implant agil and get feline and then for the str all you need is IC and an essence.

Since you are talking about doing towers I would suggest doing rifle, even though it is only 3 per lvl for us it is also 60% agil and 40% sense so its natural opi skill. Also this will let you keep in the backlines, use whatever good rifle you can get with the best range and stay back as far as you can while still providing heal and firepower.

Since you won't be going up higher then 90 you won't be affected by breed caps, opifex does have a somewhat low psychic cap, i think only besting atrox. But my 174 opi doesnt have any issues with nano pool and I havn't even bothered getting GPH, just have my -15% cpu upgrade in.

As jynne mentioned, scrub MC and SI. SI is mainly useful for outside combat, only good combat buffs are nano init and nano resist, but a recompiler should be decent nano init for you and hopefully in the backlines you wont see too many nanos being cast on you. And DOTs just arent that useful.

Also the PM use depends on what style you want to play. I would suggest for towers focus on healing as your main since your guys will be taking some good damage prob and then use your weapon from the background to assist where you can. Using init debuffs can be nice and really piss the enemy off but thats more nano you are using and unless you put in a Nano Range Inc tool also you will have to move up some to debuff them.

Anyway, opi docs are nice. At 174 I could raise my rifle almost as high as my other weapon skills even though its 3 vs 4 per lvl just from all the agi and sense i have (around 650 agi right now i believe). And for melee, which is what I use, 1HE is my highest useful weapon class. Piercing goes a bit higher but there isnt really anything to use. Even when using byom blade/ryder exec with the lowered AR by heavy weapons I still had around 850.

Btw Eunucha, yes kicks do look nice, my new trox doc might be using those later. I still can't decide if I want to leave the melee route though.

Grats. Must be nice. Personally, my damage output is poor.

Where does that opinion come from? First, from the crit nerfs that have made the traditional doc choices for weapons less effective. Second, from the response of FunCom to our damage output concerns - that we don't need help in that area. Third, from the changes to nano resist that mean we need to go full defense to get the best chance of resisting. Fourth, by the change in FunCom's stance on soloing. For a long time, FunCom maintained that all profs should be able to solo at all levels. Not too long ago, they changed to say that soloing was never intended to be the way the game was played so they'd make no effort to make sure everyone can solo. Fifth, from the attitude of some docs and many non-docs that docs should not use weapons or nukes to inflict damage at all. Last, and perhaps most importantly, from the justifiable desire of profs like MAs, Enfs and Soldiers to do greater damage than profs like docs. As these profs get boosted, other game factors like mobs get adjusted correspondingly reducing the effectiveness of damage output from docs.

Again, only my opinion. You're entitled to your own. But to me it seems like the ground work is all there and we're headed toward a "why bother to even try to do damage" environment.
