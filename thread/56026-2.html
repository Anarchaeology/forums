Dislike of the NODROP thing in general
I have a general dislike of most NODROP items. Basically, here are my thoughts.

Martial Arts attacks: These should be found or sold in "scroll" form which is unusable until opened. Once opened, they become NODROP. They would then funtion kind of like books or Balaklava Helmets (ski masks). This allows much more freedom in how they can be traded or sold.

Vehicles: Ok, I only kind of understand that FC wants you to earn the money for vehicles, but honestly, anyone should be able to sell their car. Vehicles already have a skill requirement, so you could only flood the market with the low quality ones and only to people who have the ips to invest in the vehicle skills. People can already buy vehicles for their lowbie friends by handing them money, why make it difficult.

Senior Intern-Ops Coats: I LOVE the look of these. Ok, they're not NODROP, but they are UNIQUE. What's up with that?!? Again, they already have stat requirements. What, I can't own two coats?!? Maybe I should invite some FC execs over for a peek in my closet to show them just how many coats I can own.

Bracers: These are again NODROP. With the bracer nerf, there is NO reason these should be NODROP anymore. Bracers are not really a rare item (at least they haven't been for me), and I would love to be able to sell or give my really useless, low level ones to someone.

Quest Items: Ok, these are why NODROP should exist. Neleb's coat, cyborg armor, etc. Should only be gained by people who go into the field, take the risk, and get the items. They should not be able to be farmed out to others. In fact, more of these items should be made NODROP. Right now, high levels go out into the field and farm these items to sell, leaving lower level characters who need or want these items in the dust, which isn't fair. Grid armor is a good example. Maybe not all of it, but the higher level grid armor should be NODROP. Then, FC could increase it's drop rate to balance the NODROP ('cause if you make it NODROP, it's drop rate should be raised).

Again, these are just my opinions. Anyone else have thoughts to add on these matters? No flames please.
