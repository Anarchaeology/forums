Rules For Tarasque Raids
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These rules were approved in the Clan Leaders Meeting that took place Sunday 11th August.

Those were sort of agreed upon. The problem was that the organization leaders were not the ones going to raids, and a lot of the people that were actually going to the raids didn't agree with these loot distribution ideas.

As a result, there was a second meeting the next day among the regular participants and these were thrown out.

The problem is that things just aren't as simple as defining a set of rules. Each individual has a different perspective on what he or she considers fair, and no matter what rules exist there will be unhappy/upset people. Based on this premise, we opted with the KISS (Keep It Simple, Stupid) concept of considering each Tarasque spawn as a single unrelated event.

I'm working with Javaschick right now on getting the base set of rules documented and setting up a forum where we can discuss and brainstorm.
