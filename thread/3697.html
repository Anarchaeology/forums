My Reevaluation period. Welcome Back to Rubi - Ka!
Ok so FunCom is offering me 31 days to reevaluate the game. Why not, Diablo 2 has gotten kinda carpal tunnel like on me. What follows is a complete detailed description on my reevaluation of the game.

Day 1: Create a new character. Its only fair that I take a fresh start and experience the game on a whole. Thus, on December 3rd, Poprawkz, the clan Metaphyisicist is created. Kill a leet, grab a newbie mission, repeat till level 2. Go into Athens, grab missions until level 9... hmm I seem to be leveling quite fast, what the.... "You have been disconnected" and with that, the servers went down for approximately 5 hours.

Welcome back to Rubi-Ka, we've fixed everything, give us more money, oh crap, our servers are borked, please hold.

Day 2: Missions, missions, missions... noticed a lot of new graphics, lag is still horrible around whompas...leveling fast as hell, it seems that FunCom actually increased the rate of leveling which was never a problem to begin with, now I'm leveling too fast not enough money, even soloing missions and selling every piece of loot. Quit at level 20.

Days 3-6: Slowed down on leveling, taking really easy missions to build a set of QL 50 implants. At level 35 I've accomplished this. Unless you e-bay credits or twink, credits are DAMN scarce, nanos are impossible to afford, the new nanos implemented for metas sell for 100k-250k and thats just for QL 37-50 animas and creations... TOTALLY RIDICULOUS, the economy is fubar.

Day 7: I have to take my first cave mission today. The mission terminals will not give me any more city missions. My pets go catatonic every room. I cant move past water. Everything sucks. Enemies can track me around corners, through rooms, around tables, over tables, through tables, through walls and even shoot through doors (which I cant seem to cast through amazingly enough) and my pet cant get over a pebble on the floor. I give up this mission and ponder what to do next... send a petition to rant about how pissed off I was about pathing. Decide to go hunt with a clan mate.

I gain 5 levels in 1 hour. I am now level 40. I quit leveling because I fear I will get too far behind financially, since my nanos are so expensive and I only managed 5k in loot while hunting. I rant at an ARK Akusomoto or something for 45 mins about how little this game has changed and I get the same old answer: "They are known issues and we are working on them." He tells me there are other things to do besides missions, that I could go hunt. I tell him I gained 5 levels hunting in the hour it took him to respond to the petition. Sorry mindless frenzied shooting got boring about 10 minutes ago, besides I cant afford my new nanos now -- at this rate I will be level 100 by Christmas at least.

Conclusion

7 days, 40 levels, and a lot of auctioning later I have come to this realization... FunCom has no idea what they are doing. The economy is borked, the only way to afford the things I need is to do missions, I cannot do missions because pathing is screwed up to the point where it is not fun to do a mission, its too frustrating. My enforcer is retired because of nerfs to his nanos and changes to armors, my soldier is retired because of nerfs to his nanos and lack of availability of his chosen weapons (he was designed with no dodges to make full use of mk shield and uses pure assault rifles only, which are still very difficult to find), and now my meta has landed between a rock and a hard place, due to ****e coding, is going to become increasingly frustrating to enjoy.

So what exactly was I invited back for? New icon graphics? New spells I cannot afford? To witness gestapo like tactics of GMs bullying players into submission for supposed "exploiting" (I reference to agents rooting during pvp grace period and dual pillow wielders getting smacked around for 'exploiting' although they broke no rules.) This game is exactly like I left it if not worse! Not to mention I'm not even allowed to hate omni anymore because of "amnesty," which by the way is annoying to get spammed by guards every time an opposing faction runs by 20 guards.

The only thing I've come to enjoy is the people I have met in the game, and FunCom, you didnt implement them.

Peace,
-PoPRawkZ
