Journal Entry: Agent Ehks, for any who care to listen...
Life's a lot simpler looking down the sights of a long-gun. You're quiet and quick, or you're dead. As the world has grown more complex, life has gotten more straight-forward. Go figure. Things are funny that way.

Used to be the other way around. I was an agent for Omni-Tek. Didn't seem to be any reason not to be, and a young man with a few gifts and not many opportunities wouldn't have thought to look anywhere else for a shot. I've always had a sharp eye and a quiet foot. And I've always shot dead-on straight. I'm not from Rubi-Ka. Not a native. One of those poor bastards who believed the hype and came over to keep the peace. Peacekeeper. With a long-range weapon.

Funny how they used to try to sell even those of us on the front lines their story. It was all for the greater good. All by the will of the people. That's where doing well was a disadvantage. The better you were, the higher you climbed...and the less anyone bothered to snow you. Man, you start hearing ugly things when everyone thinks you're "on board." If they were smart, they'd keep agents in the dark at every level. After all, infiltration, subversion, looking like we belong when we're just gathering information...that's our bread and butter. You're not going to know what we're thinking when you tell us the truth. That's why you hired us.

But they let me get wise. Their mistake.

Killing "rebels" had been easy. Seldom even needed a profile to make me feel better. You'd got to some OT outpost and see kids crying cause the food stores had been raided. Or women wailing over fallen husbands who were foolish enough to take jobs as guards. You always tell yourself that "death isn't permanent." Then when you're buying insurance, you remember how poor some people are in this hell-hole, and you realize not everyone has the cash to come back from the grave. And many who do would be better of not and bequeathing their precious cash to their children so they could eat.

Oh, the clans used to be easy prey. They shouted and talked a good fight and protested "peacefully." And there was always that handful of idiots in the crowds that couldn't leave well enough alone, who had to push it too far. That's all we needed. The contracts stacked up faster than the bodies.

But a fella gets tired of watching his own men die for what he knows is an increasingly-thin cover story over an unimaginably complex lie. And the targets grow tougher as they learn from your mistakes...tougher still when you hesitate on the trigger, knowing that they're dying for a lie, too. Fella eventually goes on a mission and doesn't come back. Happens all the time. There's a letter home, maybe a posthumous commendation, and then you're forgotten.

All the better.

Maybe I should have went neutral. Not much work for a guy like me, but it could have been peaceful, at least. Ah, who am I kidding. The neuts have it worst of all. No, if you're on Rubi-Ka, you've chosen a side whether you like it or not. I went over to the clans, wanting to make sure I was on the right side.

Tell you true, they're not much better. People are the same everywhere. There's a core of idealists, a huge body of common, decent folks, and a handful of insecure, frustrated egomaniacs who aren't happy unless they're stirring up a storm of violence and destruction in the name of "justice." That's why I still take contracts at all. Cause someone, somewhere is doing their homework, and it's a better than fair shot that whoever I'm icing deserves it, and that the world as a whole will be better off for their absence. People all look the same in the sights of a rifle, no matter which side they're on. And my old One Time friends (that's what I call OT's...cause they used to be the kings of the hill, but aren't anymore, and you usually only have to hit em "one time" to take 'em out of a fight :^) are just as rife with trouble makers as the rebs. And while the rebs are usually ticked off about oppression, the OT hardcases are usually just arrogant power mongers who never lost anything in their lives.

But understand me, I don't like fighting in the streets. I don't like seeing folks with families slugging it out over some rhetoric or a few credits. I don't play that game; life's hard enough for regular folks just trying to raise their families. I don't gunsling in residential neighborhoods against cocky teen punks with big talk and small weapons. I only hit who I am assigned to hit, and I stay out of trouble. I wish others would respect that, but they don't...not on either side. People like that are starting to make me question my patience and mercy. And it'd be sad for everyone if I lost those. But some people just can't help trying to overcompensate for fat egos and thin self-image.

Like a whining OT Trader named Coyotess who opened trade with me as I was passing by and then sucker-punched me. I ran, cause I don't much like fighting people who haven't been "assigned" to me. I mean, what if she was just mad at some bad hand a Clanner had given her? Then the damn 100% suppression gas failed, she rooted me, and I had to duke it out. Lucky headshot won the day for me. Then when the whiner rezes and starts sending me threats. That's the kind I hate.

Or an OT freshman named Zeman who brained me with his giant hammer the other day as I was trying to grid out of MMD...after I'd just talked a bunch of Clanners out of ambushing him. No gratitude. No justice.

Guess that kind of people didn't get the memo about the amnesty. If it keeps up, I may lose my patience with "fairness" and start throwing lead on-sight. I hope that day never comes.

Anyway, that's my story.

If you're a Clanner, stop and say "hi" sometime. You can't miss me. I wear a black robe and hood in town...like most of my commarades.

And if you're OT, say "hi" too...before you start shooting. Or better still, INSTEAD of shooting. We can have a few laughs about old times. The damn paperwork. The Over Time (what I called OT's back when I was one :^). The hours of boring pep talks. Not everyone's your enemy, you know. But if you don't learn that fact pretty quick, they may be...soon.
