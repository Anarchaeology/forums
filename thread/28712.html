Gambling (and a little on the economy)
I'm bored waiting for the new patch...

AO is my first mmorpg, haven't tried any others. Currently, I'm a lowly 13 omni agent. While I haven't been around a while, I have noticed that there seems to be few ways to get money out of circulation, resulting in inflation and de-valued credits. In addition,
though this is an RPG and not an economy simulator, I believe that there is enormous potential to expand the game's depth by manipulating this economy. I've seen posts suggesting a flat tax (i like that, they can add a new debtor's prison), rent (seems complicated), a stock market (How? Omni-Tek owns everything there would be no market. Bonds, maybe), i think my favorite has been gambling. I must admit, i spent my first few hours wondering Omni-1 Entertainment District actually hoping to find a casino, or maybe card game in the back of a seedy bar. It just seemed to fit. Anyway, here are some thoughts on gambling.

1) Difficulty to implament
I'm not a programmer, so I don't know. Would mini-games such as gambling be horribly diffcult to implament? It seems like slot-machines, or video poker type games would be easy to implament. Games vs. the house (blackjack, craps), or PvP games (poker), I'm just interested in what the gaming-savy individuals would say about their difficulty of implamentaion.

2) Economy
I've already stated that I think there is enormous potential here in adding depth to the game. Slots, and games vs. the house would most certainly remove some of the credits from circulation, adding more value to what we have. Any other ideas on how this might efect the economy?

3) Validity
Would people even play? Judging from the reaction of previous suggestions of this it seems so. I know I would. Also,
I've never been in a bar and found ANYBODY else? People in shops are coming nto get something and then getting back out to use it. Neither of these seem to encourage social interaction. I think people going into a casino and staying there could. I admit I play Rubi-Ka 2 which is a far less populated (i hear, anyway)

4) Continuity
It seems gambling would fit into the world of Rubi-Ka. It doesn't even have to be real world games, the FunCom guys could make something up.

And a few off top things.
Has funcom ever expressed concern over these things?
While writing this I thought of at least a lottery? That could be cool.

I have not been here long so I could be wrong about everything, or not. Let me know. I'm intersted to hear what everyone elso thinks.
