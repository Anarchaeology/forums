Loot
There HAS to be a change with loot. In team missions especially this is a problem. Where you are in a team mission, (Pickup groups especially), the Agents or Fixers (not to pick on them, but those guys have the high B&E) or just whoever, jet through the mission and loot the boxes, and noone has any idea for sure what they are looting. Another part is at the boss, all that sweet boss loot, and one person looting it, noone knows for sure what the loot was, and people walk out of the mission with nice items that others could have used and so forth.

I propose that AO show the loot, like when someone loots the boss, or a chest. They di it in EQ and other RPGs. It's just a fair way of enforcing honesty. Im not one for oppression, but I really can't stand thieves, and people that dont list all the boss loot or box loot are just theives IMO.

Another thing that needs to be implemented is a fair "Rolling" System, where team members can roll the dice so to speak, for certain loot items that multiple team members want.

Now your argument might be to say set the loot to all at the boss, and have everyone look, but that is hardly ever acceptable or practiced, and would be something that everyone playing AO would have to aggree to start doing, which we know wont happen. Or you might say, well the loot is set to alpha, so whomever gets the alpha on the boss is entitled to all of that loot, well, I say BS! Its the teams loot. The only thing alpha is good for is the regular mob looting.

So start showing the loot as its looted please!

Thanks.
