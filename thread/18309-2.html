What about......

The ability to take on more than one monster at a time.

Imagine this:

You walk into the dreaded first room of a mission. Instantly, you are aggroed by at least three <insert random OT baddie here>. Now normally, what do you do? If you're by yourself, like I usually am, you target the closest unlucky guy and dimach. Then you finish him off with a nice brawl. One down, but the other two are still attacking you. Have been the entire time. Except now you are out a dimach and a brawl. Blessed with Thunder (or Bird of Prey, depending on level)! Okay, but he still isn't dead. That's okay though, because you just recently uploaded the newly redesigned Controlled Destruction program. Now, not only does it increase your damage output, it allows you to hit any mob that is attacking you.

With proper animations added in game (i.e. kicks we supposedly have, blocks, parries, etc.) this would be a dream to watch. Not only that, but you would be damaging both mobs at once.

Tell you what. Make you a deal Funcom. On behalf of all other MA's who would agree with me, cut our damage back to what it was pre patch, and make our fists duel wielded. Then give us the ability to target more than one monster in combat. Make it so that our left fist is attacking that ugly atrox nanoshifter to our left, and our right fist is beating on the agent trying to root us.

Of course, the multi target thing should be across the board to all duel wielding players.

What do you think?
