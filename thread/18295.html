It didn't occur to me until this evening how amusing it is that Camelot Castle is 25% in the Morgan/Tarasque area.

This provides another tool for powerful guilds to maintain a stranglehold on the rare spawning of unique items.

It almost looks like the area was designed by Funcom to be a camp/ganker paradise of the already very powerful. 25% helps to prevent less powerful guilds and players from getting access to the powerful items in the zone. This in turn becomes a liability in a PvP situation. Which makes them less likely to survive in a 25% zone. Which makes them less likely to get powerful items. Which *whiz* *spin* *whir* *click* *melt*.

"Help, I'm being oppressed! I'm being oppressed! Look at the violence inherent in the system!"

Hehe.

I guess Camelot Castle is perfectly designed. It really does mirror real world power issues exceptionally well.
