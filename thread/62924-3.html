It's not freestyle arena PVP, it's a tactical operation and it needs a leader. The word "please" does not need to preface every command. If you can't do what you are told, maybe it's better that you don't show, because at that point you are just a liability.

Here is why you stay in the box room and not down the hall:

In the box room you don't show up on the map until grace is almost over with a 1k run speed. This means clanners have to get in harms way to see if we hold it. This means that if they do they die and another omni outside Camelot can get in to strengthen our advantage. You don't stay in the hall because then you can be line jumped ala MMD, and then they can get more clanners into the GS. It's all about ATTRITION. The box room is the nearest spot to stop the clanners before any major forks in the dungeon, and tactically it's a sound position.

*added stuff*

The reason why you keep the door closed is to keep an NT from targeting 1 player and AOEing the life out of the boss room. This is why it's good to position 1 or 2 tanks on the stairs to the box room.

*end added stuff*

Anyway, GG Omni leaders, keep up the good work and get P. Ross on the bot if you can.
