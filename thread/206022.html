Small update
Next game update
As you probably know already, the first versions on 15.3.0 have been deployed on the test dimension. We're aiming to have this one out soon, maybe even next week, though I can't promise anything at this time. This will be a client and server update, so expect some downloading and approximately 5 hours estimated downtime.

A few of the things that's currently scheduled for 15.3.0 (disclaimer: some might not make it to the live dimensions this time around):
- Fixing Smuggler's Den
- More garden entrances and exits
- Access to Inferno temples
- Tweaks on some quest item drop rates
- Tweaking of coil requirements
- Ability to delete uploaded nano programs
- and more...

Cz gone for a week
I'll be away next week, so I won't be replying to e-mails and private messages, gathering feedback for Marius and Johan, posting on the forums, be in IRC, etc. I'm sure you'll all manage without me, but wanted to just let you know why I'm not gaining any post count for an entire week.

There wasn't really anything else I had on my mind right now. I guess the complete build notes for 15.3 are what people are waiting for. C'ya on the 8th.
