Battle Plan for Generals Right hand
Most likely I am way off base and a complete loser for even suggesting it, but here goes. The plan can be editted as it meets battle and certain facts become clear.

Objective: Sustained Damage over time. We are not looking for outrageous to drop it fast, we are looking for damage that can be kept up to beat the regen rate. We are also looking for Aggro control since if aggro goes to someone who cannot recieve it, then we cannot sustain damage. There will be more nonfiring people in my plan than there will be firing people.

# Kill Teams: ?
# Misc teams: ?

Each Kill team will consist of at least - 1 Engy, 1 Soldier, and 1 Doc/Adv.

Jobs:
Soldier: Once the aggro message appears, cast TMS, and tell the team
Engy: once the soldier says TMS casted, start warping. Some delay is allowable as TMS lasts longer than it takes to warp.
Doc/Adv: Keep that Soldier on its feet.

If there are multiple Engineer/soldier/docs per team, then each engineer is assigned one soldier to warp, and only warps that engineer. The Doc can heal whoever needs it. This is best done at range to avoid aggro.

Engineers will be standing completely out of view of The General's Right hand.

Only the soldiers present will be firing. If the Aggression Multiplier works, then they should be using that as well.

The effect of this should be able to sustain 80-90% of the firing force on the mob at all times.

Misc Teams:
Agent team - for spamming the repressor to attempt to control aggro.
Fixer team - for pulling.
Crat team - Have the +7% Crit and +100 Off buffs running at all times to benefit the kill team.

Optional Kill team members -
Traders: cast Divests and Plunders until you get them off, or if nothing will land ever, cast the highest team wrangle that you can cast repeatedly.

NTs, Enforcers, fixers stand near the Engys to protect them from wandering mobs, and to buff and hot soldiers that come back to the rear after TMS has been cast. MP's can keep the Traders and Doc/Advs buffed up so they can cast their best wrangles and heals.

MA's.... I can't think of a use for you. Buffing Soldiers with RI perhaps?

Anyway... thats a really rough draft of something I've had in mind for awhile.
