That is indeed a nicely formulated hypothesis, Wisewise. It would be convincing as well if it didn't suffer from a couple of fatal flaws.

Pro primo:
Why would someone kidnap these people, just to let them out outside Tir and ignore them?

Pro secundo:
These "victims" of a hypothetical kidnapping could apparently leave the area at any time. A journey across Tir County towards neutral territory (where we know they have more than adequate protection) should hardly be more dangerous than remaining around Tir.

Pro tertio:
Not only do these supposed victims stay in the Tir area, but there is irrefutable evidence that they will return to the area even after having been killed.

Ergo:
All available evidence points towards the conclusion that the low-level Omni-Tech employees that can be seen outside Tir are there of their own free will. (This may have some radical implications, as we shall see.)

However, this does not by itself exclude some form of "kidnapping." It is quite possible that these people have been liberated by clanners, and consequently decided to stay in the atmosphere of freedom and individuality that prevails in clan territory.

The fact that they still formally belong to OT, that they don't yet dare to venture inside Tir proper, and that they attack low-level clanners, can all be attributed to confusion during careful deprogramming and mind restoration after OT brainwashing that these liberated souls no doubt recieve from their clan protectors.

This could mean that the clanners seen in OT territory may indeed be genuine clanners, on a brave mission to rescue young OTs from the claws of the corporation. That only young employees that have not yet passed fully through the brainwashing program are chosen is logical.

If this theory should prove correct, it's obviously not advisable to beat these young Omnis to a pulp with mitaars.
