MA specials
Well to the subject of MA specials....

Alot of them are bugged or just not usefull at all... and thats a pity.
The biggest problem is that they just lock MA for too long... anything over 60s is almost unsuable in PvM situations.

They shouldn't boost the MA power too much, just to make them attractive to use!

  • Attack of the Snake, usable at really lowlevel only
  • Bird of Pray, usable up to lvl 50-60
  • Blessed with Thunder, the best PvM attack.
  • Shen, reduce MA lockdown from 1000s to 180s.
  • Flower of Life, should lock First Aid and MA in 30s instead of MA for 300s (aka instead of stims)
  • Angel of Night, reduce MA lockdown from 450s to 80s.
  • Bright, Blue, Cloudless Sky, reduce from 180s to 45s (higher min then BoP but the same max damage, but with MUCH higher dimach req)



Many of these have very cool anims and I really want to use them, but with the current lockdown it just isn't viable to do so.

Then we have the problem with some of them just not working as they should... aka Flower of Life give the same amount of damage to yourself as you deal out to the mob (but halfed due to pvp rules) instead of healing you.
