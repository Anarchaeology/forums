Despondant over merit board
Finally reached 250 tokens to reach next level. This took a lot of time and energy. I figured I should get something for this. But the next board gives almost nothing. Totally not worth the effort.

At one point I thought that the higher ql your merit board was, the higher the level of bracers and rings you could wear. Wrong, only your title level matters and that is based on your character level (15, 50, 100, etc). So I see no use for the merit board.

Lets say, at the least I wanted to display it when walking around. Well, it looks almost identical to the "late night" board. Total crap.

My point, don't waste you time with the merit board.

As I have said before this could be easily used to enrich the game. I should get a title such as a miliary rank and be given some ornamental piece of clothing to display my achievement.

To take it to the next level, you could get discounts in shops, guards should give you a special salute and omni who are low in rank cannot attack you. Like in the real world, a private doesn't have access to fighting a general. The general has underlings to take care of this for him.
