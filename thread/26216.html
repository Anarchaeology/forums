Most Hacked Symbio Grafts will lock one of the major Nano casting skills (T&S, Mat Creat, Mat Met, Bio Mat) so be careful.

I will often hack one and then give it to someone like a soldier etc. who says they don't use nano much but for us pet classes these things make for a good time to chat.
