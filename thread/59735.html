The Merchant of Tir
Thadnuriath shouts: Food for sale! The best of Rubi-Kan agriculture!

Thadnuriath shouts: Carrying a wide array of different eats, only 10 credits apiece!

Rubi-Kan inhabitant: shut up thad

Thadnuriath: Another day, another curse. How long can I stand here, doing my tedious work? I am the Merchant of Tir, official Clan food supplier. But all that I recieve is hostility from the people around me. I must bear it. I am a man, and men were from the early beginning designed to suffer.

Kanshai: And then man created the Android, a helpless macine forced to stand by his master's side, listening to his pathetic wailing.

Thadnuriath: Your mind, like your body, is made out of steel and notum, Kanshai. You will never understand the matters of the living. Nevertheless you shall stand by my side.

Kanshai: What choice do I have? I am but a creation of dead matter, yes. But not by your hand. You have only purchased the program used to create my shell. Who are you, to speak of my mind, and how I calculate my feelings? Your speak distress me, Master. I have heard merrier words spoken by my cousin Marvin in South Artery Valley. Hey, are you listening?

Thadnuriath: The sands are my floor, the clouds my roof. I sleep in the grass under the trees. Water I drink from the mountain springs. But food I cannot find in the wild, even how much I wish. I must contribute, to be worthy a living. This is my contribution. Why will nobody accept it?

Kanshai: Because your shouts are quite annoying. That's why.

Thadnuriath: They are poor, these people. They may have a million credits, but still their spirits are starving for meaning, for some variation from the race of career.

Kanshai: Do you ever listen to yourself?

Thadnuriath: Listening? What is listening, in this world of noise. Speakers everywhere transmitting the message of greed. Money, it shouts.

Kanshai: This is so pathetic. As always, I have to solve your problems.

Kanshai shouts: Level 76 Bureaucrat with strong android looking for team!

Thadnuriath: Be silent! This disease of meaningless spreads fast enough, if not you are to infect me with it! You are forcing me to countereffect your destruction, even if that will cause new curses to hit me.

Thadnuriath shouts: Over here, by the tents! The best food on this side of Rikel!

Kanshai: I thought it used to be Betelgeuze

Thadnuriath shouts: Special of the day: Too-perfectly done Meatloaf! Only 10 credits!

Kanshai: I cannot stand it.....
/pet terminate

Tir inhabitant: bronto burger please

Thadnuriath: Ten credits that'll be.

Tir inhabitant: Thank you. Here you are.

Thadnuriath: Thank you, kind sir.
