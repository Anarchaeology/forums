why do we need Balance?
I wonder how people define balance. I keep hearing players asking for balance, but i think most poeple dont even know what they are asking for. So, i was thinking about what balance was...If balance means equal power throughout all the characters, how is this possible? IMO it is not. Every class is too unique and different with nanos..etc to be ever equaled in 'power'. Each class's advantages is just too different to be ever equalled in power.

I think we should actually be asking for more 'unbalancing', because if 'balance' is what i think it means, then 'unbalancing' would mean more uniqueness for the classes. Of course, its obvious that 'balance' complaints comes mostly from pvp. If one class seems 'powerful' then another, i dont think this means we need 'balancing', it just means that class's advantages just happen to benefit them more in pvp then another class's advantages that doesn't...either this reason, or some people just suck at pvping and dont know how to play their own classes right...i also assume pvp could involve some 'luck' and some 'strategy', and its not just about whos got better nanos..etc...
