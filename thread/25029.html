Official apology from Mayhem after Battle Royale
Mayhem wants to officially apologize for what happened at the final of Battle Royale today.

Former president Aqualimon, who was not in the finals, entered the arena and started to attack the participants. Mayhem was excluded from all upcoming Battle Royale's after this episode.

"The actions taken by Aqualimon was done at his own initiative, and does not represent our guild. Therefor he was kicked shortly after the event, and an apology was sent out to Noer from The Council and Argulace from Mercenaries of Kai. We officially apologize for what happened, and hope that there is no hard feelings between our factions."

Matravardi won the competition
