Ok, I´ve got NW, but why is the box empty?
Dear Marketing Director!

I managed to get hold of a copy of Notum Wars a few days back, but found the box to be nearly devoid of contents...

The low weight of the product already made me a bit su****ious in the shop, but at home I was positively shocked! The contents were:

1 CD (containing AO)
1 piece of cardboard with registration key and installation instructions

Obviously, I had already received the software on the CD in the form of numerous online patches, thus needing neither the CD, nor the installation instructions

Now, I am sure this is a mistake, as I do not believe any person heading a marketing department would let a product like this ship. Please let me make this clear: I am, as opposed to other people in this forum, quite happy with the content of NW, but I fail to see how the physical shipment was in any way necessary. A simple button would have sufficed:

<Notum Wars> by pressing this button you agree to the terms below, and authorise Funcom/Gamigo to deduct $19.95 from your account. Thank you!

No hiccups, no unsatisfied customers wanting the add-on, but not getting it. Frankly this box was an insult. You could have included a lot of useful things:

- Newbie guides
- Profession guides
- New media
- An updated handbook
- A list of Notum Wars features, and how to access them

The fact that even this last item was missing, is the most upsetting to me. I do not think I´ve bought anything at all apart from food and stationary that did not come with some sort of description. Least of all a piece of software.
If I was Marketing Director at Funcom I´d be very ashamed. I mean it. Your programmers are doing a good job, even if some bugs seem hard to fix. They gave you an interesting product, but you made them look extremely bad by marketing it the way you have.

Though playing on DNW, I post this in the official forums, as I sincerely hope this outcry reaches you through Cz or Cosmik, and that maybe for Shadowlands you will rethink your strategy on this issue.

Loving the game, but digruntled by the way customers are treated,

Yours,
