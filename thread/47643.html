Nerf to camping: Pros and Cons
Camp nerf:
All unique mobs should spawn in random locations and after death, should spawn within 5 minutes in a different playfield. None of them should agro on sight...only when attacked.

Pros:
Killing a unique mob would actually require some hunting rather than just camping his spawn spot until the timer ran out.

Cons:
People who find enjoyment in camping might go back to EQ.

FunCom: I urge you to try this...at least with just one mob and see how much fun people have hunting it down and killing it. You may be pleasantly surprised

omg, that would be a great idea, but here's a couple mods that would make the idea work even better. Note, these really are Nar's ideas only changed slightly.

1. Depending on the boss it should randomly spawn or have designated spawn spots. If it's something like a person then they should spawn inside a camp or from a cave. However, since this post wants camping nerfed, the boss spawn would randomize between a number of different camps. For animal types, there should be specific spots in the playfields where the bosses would spawn, such as inside certain forests or in the middle of certain clearings. Otherwise, you may have, for example, a boss in Andromeda be killed and then respawn in the middle of ICC. This way, the hunting aspect is still true yet does not completely remove the camping ability. You can hunt for it or camp a spawn spot as you wait for someone else to kill it elsewhere. And yes, the boss spawn should be no more than 15 minutes, and that's for small playfields or few spawn spots. Otherwise, even instant respawn would work. Of course, to aid those who end up wandering through the boss mid-respawn, there should be a 15 second period in which the boss and the mobs around it would not even move let alone KOS.

2. Only certain mobs and bosses should KOS, depending on their type and their alignment if one applies. If it is, say, a mutant boss and mob(don't criticise me here, don';t even know if there are mutant bosses) it would not KOS unless attacked first. Mutants aren't aggro. However, if the boss spawn is, say, a spider, then it would KOS.
