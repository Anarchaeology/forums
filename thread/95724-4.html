(PART 1 ... Trying to catch up )

Monday night...

His comm kept buzzing incessantly as he passed through Borealis and Newland...and his stress level kept rising...

"Can I hav sum credits?" a strange voice asked.

Sometimes, the adventurer hated suppression gas.

Finally, Braedon made his way to ICC where Wulfven of Gridstream Productions was broadcasting his show. Lounging in sabretooth form, Braedon took some time to ponder the events of the previous night. He was tired...stretched thin...and, on edge. So much had occurred the last couple of days, and he needed some time to sort through it all.

Flashback to Sunday night...

He had wandered to Reets and met with Vixentrox of Whisper's Edge. As the new Vice-Leader of EndGame, he had hoped to have the opportunity to speak with Vixentrox to guage her interest in the Alliance. Taking advantage of the opporunity, the two talked at length. Braedon came out of the converstation hopeful that Whisper's Edge might soon find a place in the Alliance. Yet again, Braedon was amazed at how much the two leaders saw eye-to-eye on many matters. Formalized alliance or not, this was one friendship he felt fortunate to have, and one that had survived the test of time.

Braedon and Vixentrox spoke of much more than Alliances that night, however. She relayed the threat she had receieved from an unnamed source. Braedon felt his fists clench in anger as the words were repeated to him, and he offered to assist Vixentrox in any way he could. The two leaders then compared notes on what they knew.

During this conversation with Vixentrox, Sezmra approached and greeted him. The Omni Agent had the effect on him he had come to expect, but also another he didn't...one he didn't like one bit. He felt distrust. The information Vixentrox and he were sharing was of a...sensitive nature. His heart cried out that he had nothing to worry about from this agent sitting nearby, but his head screamed otherwise. He had responsibilities...and, he made certain none of the conversation could be overheard.

Doing his best to block out the distractions, both pleasant and otherwise, that the alluring Omni brought about...he continued his conversation with Vixentrox. A few minutes later, he watched Sezmra's form as she slowly departed the dance floor area.

Always she walks, as if stalking her prey, he thinks to himself, shaking his head.

After the two leaders complete their conversation, Braedon decids it's time to track her down...and address that uncomfortable feeling of before. He found her in the fireplace room at Reets. Pausing a moment, he stared at her silhouette in front of the flickering fire, then moved to take a seat.

The backrub....was that a selfish request on his part, or truly a ploy intended to distract the agent from his plans to gather information? He tried to convince himself it was the latter.

Sighing softly, Braedon found himself pleased that the Omni agent knew more about the human anatomy than merely which parts are most succeptable to a bullet. Much more. Her touch was actually doing a great deal to melt his tension away, but he was still troubled. He needed information, and assurance....at least as much as he could get against such a worthy opponent.

This would be a dangerous conversation.

Having his back turned to her put him at a disadvantage in that he couldn't see her face, but the rhythym of her touch could reveal a lot. Closing his eyes, he focused on her hands running over his back, and let loose the first name...

"Lesentia"

A pause in her movements, and some agitation. He did his best to thwart her inquiries, and went for the next name.

"Alamexis"

Braedon winced a bit as her strong hands dug into his back. Yes, that struck a nerve...and, striking a bit deeper than he intended.

He spoke to her a bit about the Omni lawyer and his recent goals. Some of her answers gave him pause...there was admiration in her voice, which he also felt for the man. Yet, her guard was going up quickly...the corporate shell came out in full force.

"I don't question my corporation. It's not my place."

"That's just the way it is."

How those two simple statements from her set his teeth on edge. Everything he stood for and believed in raged against those words.

Yet, he expected it. He may be an optimist and a dreamer at times, but he's not a fool. Omni is Omni, and Sezmra isn't some novice employee. He more than had his hands full.

Turning abruptly in his seat, the President of Valor Eternal faced the SPARTA agent. Distrust aside...there was a respect and affection between the two that could not be ignored. And, there were some things that needed saying...

Braedon made it clear that he knew of the increase in Omni surveillence, and who the agents were. He also informed her that threats had been issued, while not specifically towards him, or her....he felt confident that anyone supporting or involved with Alamexis or Uwen is inviting danger. He mentioned the rumors of Sentinel involvement, a group that the two could likely agree on as an enemy. All this he volunteered, doubting he was telling her much she didn't already know, but hoping his openness would lead her to take his next plea very seriously...

"I need you to tell me what you might find out, and I will do the same," Braedon said firmly, his eyes intensely locked on hers. "But, most importantly, I need you to promise me that you will be careful."

That last part was filled with a bit more emotion than he intended. Flashbacks of the nightmare filled his head and lowered his own defenses. She seemed to take his words to heart, however...and, that was all Braedon could hope for.

The two decided to head off to Borealis falls to unwind to a GSP performance and then call it a night.

Braedon prayed he hadn't said too much....

And, that he had said enough....

Back to Monday night...

Braedon was spralled out in sabretooth form with Kirasha leaning against him. This was a favorite form for Breadon, and feeling Kirasha's lithe body laying against him always had a way lifting Braedon's spirits.

Sezmra was dancing nearby to Wulfven's tunes, and drawing appreciative glances from the couple. Glances that were occasionally met with a smile.

"Have you heard?"

"Heard what?" he asked...fearing the news to follow.

"Alamexis...he's dead....permanently," she responded after a pause.

Braedon's hand slipped off of his comm a moment. While he honestly never got to know the man, he felt the loss. From afar, he had admired the lawyer's courage and vision. And, now the Omni diplomat with a vision of peace...had died for having that dream.

After asking Vixentrox for any details she knew, he agreed to meet with her as soon as possible. Seeing Kirasha staring at him with concern, he shares the news with her. As he finishes telling what he knows, he notices something very wrong. Sezmra has disappeared.

"Brae, two of the individuals you mentioned are at The Cup right now," his Advisor, Aameul, suddenly states over his comm.

"Thank you, Aam," Braedon says, glad to have friends he can trust giving assistance. "Please let me know if they leave, I'm going to try to swing by there."

Aameul passes on what information he can overhear as the two individuals talk, but they quickly depart from The Cup. Braedon's search for Sezmra was leading nowhere, and she wasn't responding to her comm. Irritated and feeling a bit helpless, he decides to swing by The Cup on his way to Vixentrox.

Braedon meets with Uwen briefly outside of The Cup and the two share their remorse for what had occurred. Uwen explains that he had just conveyed the news to Alamexis' sister, and his lover. Can this night get much worse?, the frustrated Clan leader wonders.

Vixentrox and Maephina both look suddenly uncomfortable.
