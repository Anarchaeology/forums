I think it's a limitation of the game engine. Kinda how MA specials are 'resisted' and the same with Fixer Cluster Bullets. I'm sure, they'll fix the message eventually to 'Your target ducked the grenade' or something.

Problem with grenades is that they are doing them wrong. There should be a check at about 25% of DuckEXp for the AoE attack (with reasonably low damage) and a second check for a direct hit on your target at about 60-75% of DuckExp for a single target damage with significant damage.

Assuming damage of 40-400 for a grenade:

You make one roll vs. your enemy's Duck skill for the fragmentation. You should have almost no chance of failing this role. It's made agains 25% of his Duck skill. A successful roll lays down a 40-100 AoE effect.

You then make a second roll to see if you mangled your intended target. This is made at a higher chance to miss and a higher range of damage. Check against 60% of his Duck skill and a successful roll adds an additional 40-100 pts of damage to your selected target only.

Point being, it should be almost impossible not to affect everyone in your target area at least a bit. The big question is whether you really sock it to who you are explicitly aiming at.
