Press Release: Omni-Tek Press Corp.
Omni-Trade, 31st March, 29477.

The newly formed Omni-Tek Press Corp. today announced they will be engaging in a mission to present the truth of events on Rubi-Ka to Omni-Tek Employees. Having acquired funds from Omni-Tek, they claim to have secured enough capital to launch a newspaper which will be dedicated to disseminating truth, and allaying the lies spread by those opposed to Omni-Tek's orderly patronage of the planet.

While the focus will be on news and current events, Editor-in-Chief "Zalcoy" said they would also "take time out to speak to the loyal citizens and allow them to share their thoughts and feelings on the terrible atrocities carried out by those opposed to the corporation."

Zalcoy went on to outline the goals for the publication, saying "we look forward to bringing you, our fellow employees, the best possible news coverage of our company's triumphs over all the troubles on Rubi-Ka. Successful conquest of the peoples opposed to Omni-Tek will be a defining point in the corporation's history."
