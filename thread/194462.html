Current Agent Bugs/Issues List
Issues list 2.0! New and improved, now twice as exciting as the first! Order your copy today for the low low price of $19.95 plus shipping and handling.

In no particular order

1) Lack of agility buffing items for agents. None opifex agents lag drastically behind the opifex breed. Its one thing for opifex to have an advantage, its nother thing for the gap to be so large it cant be bridged. Solitus, nanomage, and atrox agents are severely punished in the amount of agility and sense they can get with little to no advantage. Tier3 becomes the biggest example of this problem. While opifex can self equipt ql300 tier3 armor, other breeds struggle or even lack the ability to get it on without incredibly rare items and impossible twinking. Esspecially troublesome for items that have to be swapped due to zone protection pieces being needed.

2) We need better rifles. If we are supposed to be the highest damage range weapon based profession then our weapon needs to reflect that. Enough with the 200-400(300) rifles. We need rifles that are in the same range as the other top damage dealers. This means rifles closer to the 200-800(500) range, which is about the same as MA fists at the highest levels. Either we are given the tools or we need something else to take its place.

3) Our Shadowland damage buff nanos need looking into. Currently there is no tangible benifit to using anything beyond the two "sickening" versions. The higher nanos require tremendous skill investment with little to no increase in damage. A stacking of the proc functions and the scaling of there %'s would go a long way to making these nanos worthwhile.

4) We would like mimic's duration increased. Either through a change to the nanos that has a "friendly" version being cased after the current hostile version expires, or the ability to recast mimic durring down times as a way to increase its duration. No changes to the nano itself are needed, just an increase in its useability and convinience.

5) Agents would like there perks to use the range of the weapon they have if possible, or a longer hard coded range if not. We are a long range profession and as such our tools need to reflect that. An agent can only be as far away as the lowest tool he uses. With some of our perks being only 25 meters this limits our long range ability which is not how agents were designed. We need to fight as far away as possible, not at 25 meters.

6) Extensions to our old nanos is greatly needed. If professions are being given the ability to buff evades by 1k or more, surely our evade debuff should scale higher to acomidate that. Also a SL root would help us improve our solo ability as well as usefullness in teams. We arent asking to be crowd controlers on the scale of Bureaucrat but someplace in the middle would be welcome.
