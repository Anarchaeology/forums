Crit buffs need to be fixed or removed!
Okay, I seriously doubt this will be a very popular thread. But currently, the crit buffs are a severe problem in the high lvl game.

Here are the problems as I see it:

1) LMA and UVC seem to have a much greater effect on crit chance than the 19%, 24%. Unless the formula is really arcane, they are probably bugged in some way.

2) Weapons with high crit damage are currently completely unbalanced compared to any other kind of weapon, including many with difficult to equip specials or reuirements like ranged energy or heavy weapons. Thus shotguns are for the most part better than guns that require significantly more ip to equip.

Weapons that do fast average damage, with no real crit, or that have a high base range, but are slow and have only a small crit, are as a rule sub-optimal, and at worst, just bad. Thus, despite all the names, there are very few real weapon choices. Who wants to hit steadily for a 300 a pop or so when people are steadily dishing out 3500-9000 pt crits?

3) Because only a few weapon strategies are viable, this leads to a big homogenization of the classes, with everyone standing in line waiting for the MA to buff them up. Which leads to a big homogenization of the play experience. Which makes the game really dull.

4) Even worse, a lot of the classes that are not primarily weapon users are forced to hop on the crit train, or just feel useless and underpowered.
NT's are not the only ones affected, but it is the case I know best. I cast the best NT nukes (at least the best that aren't "make only" :/) in the game, but I am consistently outdamaged by a doctor wielding a shotgun when he has lma. Who wouldn't feel gimpy? Nukes can be resisted, blackholed, fumbled off, and you a very limited number of shots. The damage has nice low mins, which should compensate for all this, but when people are critting with regularity, you are completely overshadowed.

5) So the gimped classes are forced to whine -- fix our damage! Let us crit too! But bottom line, when no help is forthcoming, eventually almost everyone jumps on the crit train, and starts pumping some dark blue stats.

6) Crit buffs render most of the other weapon damage buffs (for example, the adventurer, fixer, trader, and engineer buffs) completely pointless. Who wants to add 25 damage to a hit when instead you can add well over a thousand? These nanos are probably underpowered anyway, but compared to the crit buff, you might as well have swimming expertise.


Crit buffs need to be fixed, nerfed, or just completely removed. It might be necessary to do this with some sort of ip reset, since so many people have invested (and invested correctly!) in weapons with high crit damage. But something has to be done. It is too optimal, too dominant, and completely homogenizes the game.

Chromatorg's solution strikes me as a decent starting place.

Take "da taunter" for example. While it certainly does something, in any actual group PVM fight, the mob follows the crits, and frequently ignores the tanks. It becomes a free for all, successful largely because of the high damage output, and for no other reason. There is precisely one tactic.

When there is one tactic, you are doing the same thing over and over again. When you combine this with limited choices of things to fight (i.e. BS missions are so optimal it is ridiculous), and the limited character growth over lvl 125, it is easy to see why there is such a high rate of attrition among the higher lvl characters.
