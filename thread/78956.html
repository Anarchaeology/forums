Ho I have a geforce 2 mx 400, and my friend has a geforce 4 mx 400, is anti aliasing available on these cards and how do I set it 'dcus I can't stand the jaggies.
With a GeForce 2MX 400, you will slow the game to a 4 FPS crawl if you enable FSAA... It just doesn't have the power to do so.

An EXTREMELY simplified explaination of Full Scene Anti-Aliasing:

For every 'x' (I.e.: 2x/4x/8x) the card has to rerender each frame that amount of times, so you are effectively cutting your FPS in half by enabling FSAA 2x

(For all you propellerheads out there, I know this isn't exact, but I'm keeping it simple.)

If you do care to enable it, however.. Make certain you have the latest Detonators from nVIDIA (BOOM!), right-click on your desktop, go to properties, click on the "Settings" tab, and then click the "Advanced" button.

Depending on your operating system and version of drivers, then FSAA options will be located in there somewhere.
