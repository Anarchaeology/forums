Ditch Titles and do a Points System
There are so many title huggers its pathetic. People who wont fight or are afraid to fight unless they are sure they can get the 1st shot. Bunch of babies.

They should just get rid of titles and move to a points system. And have a points boards. With Top 20 for Omni, Nuet and Clan. Also have a top 5 for Total Guild Points for Omni, Nuet and Clan.

Points would be awarded upon the level of you to the level of your opponent. A lower level character killing a higher level character would get more points than an equal level fight.

Therefore all the title huggers ( 70% of all ppl with a Rookie title or higher) would be more inclined to fight as much as possible if they want to be up on the points board and recieve some recognition.

Sort of like EQ has for their PvP Server Sullon Zek.
