No-Twink PVP arena?
OK I really despise PVP in this game, even with my experience in tir arena. Ill share a few stories

-Level 20ish MA (me) gets slaughtered by everyone. Most of the time I just get chain rooted and get slammed with attacks 4 times stronger than what i do in PVM.

-Level 39 See Before, Except I can root. But usually it doesnt matter cause they have a damage shield that does 70 damage or my attacks do 0-3 damage.

Level 20 Agent.... See MA. Sure I can root. But that atrox enforcer with level 60 gear on at level 40 I might as well take tarasque. I fought him 5 times, I only did damage maybe twice.

I would say I exercise the best tactics I have available. But I think the point is, since im not twinked, I really dont stand a good chance at living. So I propose the following...

A Non Twink Arena
Fairly simple. Make a new building or such where people suffer a penalty for having insane OE. Granted some classes can self OE fairly good (but usually those classes have bad weapon selection.) So I suggest wearing anything over 25% reguardless of buffs gets automatically downgraded in the area. Simple no? Granted ill still get ganked. But maybe not in 3 hits. Of course this is just an idea.
