INSURANCE FILE TRANSMITTED -> Rubi-Ka 1
Being born off planet has made my life miserable. I long for the blue sky and non-nanoed air of Earth again. These idiots seem to actually like it here, too strange. Maybe someday I can leave, when "they" think I have done my duty. Being a Fixer seems somehow pointless now. another day, another problem resolved. I used to take such pride in my work. What happened?

Maybe it's because the air here stinks of technology, giving it a bitter metalic smell? Combined with the ever present depressing rain, I half expect the whole world to rust apart at any given second. I keep checking my gun to make sure it works. On Earth I'd did that too, it is a good routine to get into, but it should not be like it is here, where I expect it to be jammed every time I look.

My name is unknown because my friends call me Weazul and I have no friends. It is safer that way. It is neater that way. It is boring as hell. My wife was supposed to join me but her transmission was never sent. Damn it's lonely here. But I have to hand it to myself, I keep busy, so it doesn't matter as much.

Ah, well, more "situations" to Fix, no time to mope.
