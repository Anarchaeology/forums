Illegal 1st Aid Kits! (support plz)
I've been thinking hard about a way for us all to play fixers in the game (in a fixery / non shooting way) and came up with the radical (*cough cough*) idea that we need illegal stuff to sell.

So here is my idea. its very simple.

To make cash we need a cheap fixer obtainable thing that other classes would need. And could be sold repeatedly. So the fixer shop should sell:

HACKED FIRST AID KITS.

These Illegally tampered with 1st Aid Kits have 1/2 the normal reqs for their QL level ( or something eg 20ql lower than normal reqs). They are cheap and banned cos they are dangerous & known to fail. Every time they are used their is a chance they will malfunction (and maybe cause a small bit of damage) and will lock 1st aid for the normal period of time.Otherwise they are as normal 1st aid kits.

This will give us something every1 will want and make us into proper drug dealers. And we will have constant repeat customers.
And its SIMPLE to do.
Give them an "illegal" tag, and maybe they can draw agg from guards etc if u r seen trading them (this can be added later if needs be due to programming time)

Please support this. ( you know u want to)

Emergency Treatment Kits
Just have to add my two cents on this issue:

The Emergency Treatment Kits and the Standard Treatment Kits - compare say, the QL50 version of both. Notice how the Emergency kit's healing is well over twice of a corresponding standard kit? Thus, you get more healing over 30 seconds than you would do if using a standard kit twice in that time.

Besides, if you're in combat and someone pulled aggro off you, you might not have the chance to use the standard treatment kit the second time - thus, emergency treatment labs are the way to go. Good thing there are no penalties associated with the blindness, too.
