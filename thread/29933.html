I play for a couple reasons.

1) Friends. I have a couple of game friends who I like to play with. No matter how bad a MMOG is, it always has this advantage over a single player game.

2) No alternatives in the genre. I, like a lot of AOers, am a FPS convert. Never been interested in the fantasy theme (orcs, elves, ferries). I really like the Sci-Fi genre and there are no alternatives right now (key word there being NOW).

3) Hope. And I don't mean in Shadowlands. I am about to hit TL 5 myself. I'm not too terribly interested in playing alts (I do have a low level enforcer I play from time to time just to break the monotony). I play in hope that there will be some mid to high level content added. It won't be acceptable (for me) if this isn't done until Shadowlands. Reason being, that the last part of SWG beta or the final game will be out by then and I'd rather take a chance on a whole new game than to keep wishing that FC will do something.

4) Items. I, myself, am a bit of an obsessive person. Once I decide I want to do something, it really bugs the crap out of me until I get it done. That, soon, will come to and end though, as I have almost all I set out to get. Note, that acquiring items isn't a good enough single reason to stay with this or any other game for me.


Those are my reasons. In a few months, though, I won't have any of them....

Friends- My two in-game friends and I are going to, most likely, be making the move to a different game together.

No Alternatives in the Genre- SWG and a few other games that are on the way will soon change this.

Hope- While SWG is sure to have plenty of problems, they aren't just making general and vague promises- such as "Player generated missions, trade skills, ways of leveling other than fighting" They are making those promises and giving details about how they will be implemented. Sure, they will fall short of goals in some areas, but FC has already proved that they have and will fall short in every aspect. Every patch, there are tons of things broken/ made worse and a lot, if not most of the new features or items are bugged. I'd rather start a new than to bet on a losing track record.

Items- Like I said, this isn't a good enough reason to stick to a game solely for this reason. And no matter how desirable an item is, I'm only willing to go so far in my attempt to acquire it. While I'd like to be part of killing tarasque and I'd love to have a heavily padded overcoat, I'll never be willing to do what is necessary to obtain it. Within the next month, I'll have any item in this game that I want and am willing to do what it takes to get. And just in case someone from FC reads this and thinks they'll be able to make people like me stick around by making the items I want harder to get, you'll just move one more item from my "gonna get" list to my "would like, but won't spend countless hours to obtain" list and thus shortening my already winding down AO career. (I only made that last statement because they seem to think that this is the answer to making people stay after they get to higher levels, its not... content is)


All that said, I really think that with the policies that AO has and their absurd in-game support AND the total lack of high level content will hurt AO bad when the alternatives do get here. I'd bet that Shadowlands will be an answer to a lot of the high level content questions, but I really think its a slap in the face to release an expansion that isn't an expansion. Its adding content that should have been in place at launch or released thru a patch. It's a bad move. As soon as they get a break from the gaming press (getting PCG's MMOG of the year award) they jump right on it to try and hit us up for more money when we are the ones who were here when this game was getting bashed.

I really place a lot of the blame on Greg Vederman and Chuck Osborn who work for PCG and are obviously the reason that AO got MMOG of the year from them. In their best of the year award article. They said that they picked AO over DAOC because "while DAOC really drops off at higher levels, AO just gets better". I find this amusing, considering that both of these two have lvl 40ish characters and don't know jack s**t about high level play.

Even with AO's strategy, they are messing up. They are aiming for an end of the year release of Shadowlands and that is the same projected time of release for SWG. In order for Shadowlands to have a chance of doing what they intend for it to do (stop the exodus of players for SWG), they need to release this expansion at least a month or two before SWG.

Well, I know I sound cynical, but most anyone who has reached their "Broken Shores days" are just as fed up with this situation as I am. A combination of FC being way behind on fixing things and adding high level content has made me as jaded as I am. So, why do I keep playing? Well, its listed above, but that will all end in a few months and I'm sure that a lot of people are on the same page as I am.
