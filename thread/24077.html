I agree with a lot of your ideas, but I don't know if you understand the point of self-buffs.

Self-buffs are the way to FIX the balance issues. That's right. It means that a given spell will not be used on every Fixer/Doctor/Engi/etc. that wants it. It will ONLY be applied to Advents.

Making the good MA and Agent Crit Buffs self-only will help balance the heavy crit-based, shotgun-only gameplay that many players are falling back on.

You say you want us to be able to Multi-Range buff on others, but Advents also complain about the high Multi Wield reqs on pistols. Do you realize WHY there are high Multi Wield reqs on pistols? Because certain pistols were starting to be used by EVERYONE, so they nerfed them.

A self-only Multi Ranged buff with mean that Advents, and Advents ONLY will be able to dual-wield good QL 200 pistols. If we could cast it on everyone, it wouldn't gain the Advent class ANYTHING. May as well just lower the reqs.

Self-buffs are to maintain the balance between the classes. If everyone could use anything, nobody would be an Advent in the first place.

-Jayde
