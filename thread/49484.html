Entry 1: September 4
"The road to the future is paved with the bricks of the past"

Ages ago, a monk named Conrad Vertigo, who was old enough to remember the outbreak of war against Sol Banking here on Rubi Ka, walked the then barren plains of Duhndur. In his heart, Conrad was neutral insofar as he had no desire to make war. Politically, on the other hand, he was Clan-alligned, as he hated the cruelty of the Omni Corporation.

A man of the earth, Conrad sought to make life on Rubi Ka better for everyone. The Rhinomen, for instance, owe to him their survival as a species. Back when they were hunted for hides, before the luxury of Bronto Hide was discovered, it was Conrad Vertigo who saved their race: he taught them to band together for protection; he revealed to them the secret of fire; he instructed them in building such basic structures as huts and towers. Now their society even has social classes~!
_____

I remember the day he found me, as clearly as is the sky over Aegean on a cool spring morning. It was mid-summer; months earlier, my father Koreth Scola had disappeared (I would later learn that he was arrested by Omni-Pol for violations of some trade treaty...I never really understood why they took him, but I also never stopped hating them for taking him from me).

The days and weeks following my father's disappearance left my mother withered, tired. In desperation, she cast herself from the window of our apartment onto the cold streets below. I was left alone, and being a child, I had no means of paying rent, and so was soon homeless. Alone, living off of garbage in the slums of Old Athen, I struggled just to survive day to day. Rogue waste collectors were my biggest worry, since I didn't have a means of bathing and was often confused with "waste". I was too young to defend myself from them; it was all too often that I had to flee the city in order to escape them~!

One particular morning, three Collectors mistakenly identified me as a rollerrat carcass, and proceeded to chase me outside the East Gate and into Warton Valley. It was then that Conrad Vertigo happened upon me; he was returning to Duhndur after visiting with "an old friend" (as he told me) in The Rising Sun. As you may expect, he saved me from the Waste Collectors, and upon hearing my tale, took me on as his apprentice.

So then did I, Felix Scola, the one called Ubaro, learn the ways of the Martial Artist. Conrad took me deep into the Wailing Wastes. A rigorous training ensued, building both my body and mind. He taught me much of the history of Rubi Ka, from the origins of our species (homo solitus) to the society of the Yuttos. His values he drove into my heart. His philosophies he pounded into my head. And after many years of toil and training, after gallons of blood sweat and tears, after memorizing attack after attack after attack, he left me.

Entry 2: October 2
"How few opportunities for reflection there are as when wandering the barren plains of Duhndur~!"

Nearly a month had passed since Conrad abandoned me, yet still I was condemned to roam the Wastes, lost, confused, desperate. Abandoned...what worse emotion is there than the feeling of rejection? As a child, I did not understand being alone, being unwanted. But time ages more than the body, and the mind grows increasingly su****ious while the heart's hope gives way to dispair.

I wasn't worried about starvation nearly as much as the vagabond Shades that were increasing in number and strength. I ran for weeks at a time. It was the only way that I could keep a sense of direction, though every time I stopped to catch my breath, that direction was soon lost, and my fate rolled over as when a clock strikes midnight.

Eventually, I happened upon the Yuttos. I remembered my lessons on them, but as I felt I could no more trust what Conrad had taught, I was quite skeptical. So tranquil a species I have yet to see again on Rubi Ka! In all honesty, had I not discovered their camp, it is doubtful that I would have ever escaped the lifeless desert. Their's was truly a strange society; their vocal jibberish was pleasing to my heart, and their food...let's just say that I've since made the trip back for their dining alone~!
