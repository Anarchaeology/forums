It depends on how good you are. That is, how experienced you are and the things you should do in particular situations. I.e what do you do when youve just agroed 4 mobs to many, and you are not anywhere near the exit room.

Since your an engie you have the potential to solo by yourself in pretty high missions.

Here are the pros and cons...
PROS
solo:
1.) You don't have to wait for anyone else but yourself. The only limiting factor in how fast you get exp, is how fast you can get yourself into combat.

2.) All the exp is for you alone.

3.) All the loot is for you alone.

4.) You don't have to put up with a potentially bad group. e.g whining and stupid people.

CONS
solo:
1.) You indeed can't kill as fast as a group can.

2.) You cant heal yourself if your bot cant pull agro. except using a first aid kit every 30 seconds.

3.) It will get very tedious very fast, if your not use to going solo.


PROS
group:
1.) You can potentially take on a higher mission, and since there are at least 4 damage dealers in a full group, you can kill the tougher mobs faster.

2.) There is safety in numbers.

3.) Its not boring, unless you have a dumb ass group. (and vice versa)

CONS
group:
1.) You can potentially have a dumb group, and in which case you will probably die losing 10 mil exp.

2.) The time it takes to get exp is limited on how fast the group can organize the hunt.

3.) Sometimes there is discrepancy as to who can loot what.



If you are good enough you can make more exp than a group can. Just because you will be making more exp per second than they will. Of course if the group is better..well they will be making more exp per second. Its been my experience though, that since groups are hardly ever made up of the same people, (e.g every group you are in is 90% of the time an entirely different mix of strangers) that the time it takes for the group to become a well oiled exp gaining machine, (including the deaths from dumb mistakes and the misunderstanding of who does what) you would have already gained more exp had you gone solo.

Of course if your just dumb, then its best if you go at it with a group.
