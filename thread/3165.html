Post service idea revisited
As bank space is limited I'd rather not split it with all my alternate chars.

Another solution that might be more generally useful would be a post service.

Some braindumps ( sorry bout the mess ) :

--- Regular package ---

Buy a box at the post office items in it, label it, then send it by using it at a mail clerk or terminal.

--- Mail order type package ( for trading purposes ) ---

Buy a box ( more expensive than the regular box ), add items, label it with recipient name and price, then send it.

You get a receipt so that you can get your items back if the recipient does not collect it. This is the reason for the higher price on the package box... insurance...

If a box is reclaimed this way you should be able to empty it and re-use it.

--- Money Order / Cheque ---

Buy a folder of these ( maybe a 20 pack ) and you can issue money orders to people and send them in the mail.

-----------------

You will no longer have to keep items promised to other players for a week because you never seem to be able to hook up...

You will now be able to pay fees / debts when YOU play

And in general make it easier for people in different timezones to interact and trade.

And, it has the added bonus of letting you trade items between your characters. If FC does not want this to happen, they can easily prevent/hamper it by checking if the recipient is connected to your account.

As for NODROPS, they would of course not be mail'able...
