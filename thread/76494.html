New zones just for hunting?
Now that Notum Wars has been released, there is a reason for more people to start visiting "the outdoors." However, for those of us who always enjoyed outdoor hunting and exploration, a fairly sizeable chunk of Rubi-Ka's wild zones (at least on Atlantean) will now have 100% gas operating a significant part of the time.

Now, the game world is only about 60% complete from the zones that were on the original map. There are even map items in the game for some of these zones (The Spur, Plains of Salt, the Bay of Rome, and Three Craters come to mind). Does anyone think that it might be good for Funcom to make some of these zones available now, and restrict them to "hunting only" for now, since (in story) we could say they are just now becoming terraformed, and so are not fit for mining YET.

Personally, I'd love to see some of these remaining zones come online. Would anyone else want to see these zones appear for hunting purposes only? If you would, have any suggestions on making these zones interesting as "wild outzones," besides newer and or harder animals (or more of the nasty ones we already have)?

I'd definitely support this. In fact, to keep the rp justification for not having it open for land control yet, make this very early in the terraforming process for those zones - make it really alien.

<warning: off the cuff ideas that haven't been given much thought! Proceed at your own risk... >

Maybe have it count as a hostile environment, like a poisonous gas in the atmosphere that does damage every round (like lava, or the green slime in missions) unless you have a specific type of helmet (or buff, or something) that provides a filter or complete air supply, etc.

Some of the zones could be further along, closer to normal habitable zones, partially "tamed", so they could have lower level mobs and only need relatively cheap air filters - suitable for characters 30 to 120 maybe? The less terraformed zones could have MUCH nastier and higher level mobs, and require more expensive equipment that would provide a completely independent combat-grade air supply - suitalbe only for 120+.

Missions in these zones could be part of the terraforming process - things like "you have to kill this nest of slime worms that are causing problems for our terraformers", or fixing a damaged/malfunctioning terraforming machine, etc.

Outposts with whompas, grid terminals and insurance terminals would be relatively sparse, maybe looking like small domed camps used by the terraformers.
