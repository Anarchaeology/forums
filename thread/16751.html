Since in the high-level game it is CRUCIAL to get buffs to make a pet to use and we have to do this at the moment everytime we log on, I propose a pet change for ALL pet classes, not just my specialty, engineers.

THE CHANGE I PROPOSE IS THIS : Unless we die in the game and the life-line is cut from our pets due to that death then our pets are unable to leave us at all. If we get disconnected due to server resets then we still have our pet linked to us because we HAVE NOT DIED. If we log out for our session then our pet fades when we do because we HAVE NOT DIED, and when we next log in, our pet fades back in with us as we log in, because we HAVE NOT DIED. I'm proposing that all pets are inextricably linked to their owners and that only in death do the pets get separated.

With a 14.2 OE patch imminent it is NOT unreasonable to ask for this feature to be implemented. I for one am TIRED of logging in looking for buffs for an hour then playing for another hour to only have to log off again and comeback later to repeat the process ad inifintum. So much play time is wasted looking for pet buffs when we shoudl just be able to play. What is the game world reason for killing our pets each time we log or get disconnected by the AO server resets? That it's fair? (BTW pet control should be based on psychic ability not 20% TS and MC OE rules.... and NTs should be able to cast any nano they can upload under the new OE rules as well....)

Engie pets are theoretically forever, and for whatever reasons MP pets and crat pets are timelimited but I see no reason for any of these classes to not have this proposal apply to them.
