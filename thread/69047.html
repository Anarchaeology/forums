Yeah, for sure. I'd love to see more context sensitivity in the game. Its a touch that adds to the feel and interactivity of participating in this world.

A good start would be something like a suitably gendered response. Yes, absolutely. Also, its dumb that your bots call you 'master,' and any Mob you charm calls you 'master,' whether its leet, a nightcrawler, or a Disavowed Medic. Personally, I'd rather the animial Mobs and the bots just left off the subservience altogether, and the humanoid Mobs used Sir (or Madam).

Basic context sensitivity, such as gender, is the next funadamental step. Next thing is to have Bots that give you intelligent responses. Not just "I can't do that, " but "I can't do that, because ..." Then you can actually deal with the situation.

Lastly, what I'm looking for is to have an audible dialog rather than a text response from pets, bots and charmed Mobs.
