We now join Mel, and a slighty stunned Andy French, on the bridge of the Liberator- the head ship of the Last Hope Squadron

Andy stared in total disbelief at what he saw, a living, breathing nano mage- he had to be dreaming, there was no other explanation.

"Greetings, Mr. French, I take it your stay on Earth has been, how do you say, an interesting one?" muttered the nano mage in a raspy voice- he was having some difficulties breathing. He had a slight western accent that was obviously fake.

"Uh, thats putting it lightly, but then again, this is all a dream to me..heheh.." jested Andy

"Oh no, Mr. French, this all is real! Put your hand in front of that vent- and be ready for a rush."

Reluctantly, Andy put his index finger in front of the air vent, he was in total awe. Tiny at first, but they grew by the second, little shards of notum, giving off an electric buzz every so often. The air was alive with notum, and that explained it all.

"But how is this done, ive never seen any thing like this before!" exclaimed Andy, who was attempting to cast his robotic assistant.

"Well, Mr French, have you not wondered where all of Rubi-Ka's notum goes? Well, Earth recieved a great deal of it! It was stealing it from them muties that was the hard part."

"The muties?" Questioned Andy

"Yes, the muties, scary bunch they be, some have big brains and big muscles, the supers, it can make for some very dicey situations I tell you what."

"Im glad I didnt have too many run ins with them, they sound nasty as hell."

"Well, Mr. French, that there is all about to change. Were headed to Haven, North Americas last hope gone awry- weve got about 300 OT Pur-sun-nel (he struggled with the word) pinned up in there some where fending off a great ol' batch of muties. Rest up and be ready, because this is the 1st step in getting you home."

Andy nodded, a bit frightened at the thought of facing mutants worse than the lemming-like hordes he faced at the before he met Mel. He shrugged it off and followed Mel to his quarters.
