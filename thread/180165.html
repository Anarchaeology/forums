Valor Eternal 1 Year Anniversary Extravaganza!
Valor Eternal will be celebrating their one year anniversary this Saturday, September 20th!!! Join us at Reet's Retreat in Stret West Bank starting at 6pm Eastern time (22:00 GMT) for a long night of partying!

DJ Tarryk of Gridstream Productions will provide the music for the event (we'll be testing the great Tarryk's stamina on this one...backup DJ will be on standby LOL). There will also be a host of contests for rare items and large amounts of cash! And, the event will feature an auction of prominent Valor Eternal members. That's right...several lovely ladies from Valor Eternal will be up for bid (along with this not-so-lovely org leader). There will also be a surprise guest up for auction.

This auction is held in good taste, and anyone purchased must agree to the terms of the buyer (this is for fun, it isn't a meat market LOL). What those terms may be...who knows? A date? Levelling assistance? Blitzing? Apartment cleaning? It's up to you. Each person up for bids will have a bio which gives a hint as to what they may be willing to do.

The auction will be conducted by the Red Ties Mystery Girls. Their name is just what it implies. Higher level ladies have shed their armor and donned the red ties to provide you with classy entertainment. You just never know who they might be...

All are welcome to this party! I hope to see lots of ya there! We want to top that Christmas Party at The Rising Sun...but, we can't do it without ya.
