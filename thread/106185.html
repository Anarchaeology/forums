"Just for a Moment"
Footsteps
Walking on green moss
Soft under my feet
Removing my boots
Feeling the moist ground
Alive and everlasting
Makes me want to run
To put it all behind me
Just for a moment
And run like a forest sprite
Forgetting it all
Just for a moment
To be at peace
With my heart
With my mind
So if you see me
Or catch a glimpse
of a running forest sprite
Don't try to stop me
Let me
Just for a moment
To be at peace
With my heart
With my mind
To be at peace...
