Ahem

Neutrals are not attacked by any guards anywhere.

They are attacked by civilians, which can cause the guards to attack as well

Us Neutrals, since we have free roam of _all_ of habitable rubika, literally team where _everyone_ else teams. That includes a wide range of places such as Tir by the Grid term, Omni Entertainment by the BB, NLC by the BB sign, Athens West by the Save Term, Home (BS) by the Stairs, 20k in front of the General Store, Lush Hills, Avalon, Greater Tir County, Omni Trade, Wine, etc.., etc..., etc...

It is recommended that you reach level 15 before you start wandering outside of NLC, though. Probably, you should reach level 50 before trying for the far to reach places. Well....
After reading so many posts on the Story board I decided to look at neutrality for myself for a bit.

Being familiar with Omni-1 I figured I'd look around for missions myself.

Here's the list for lvls 20 and under that are easily manageable:

Borealis. Even at QL40 missions in Borealis terminals come up that are inside Borealis. Holes in the Wall isn't much of a walk either.

Harry's. A neutral can get to Harry's very easily (Newlands - ICC, ICC - Omni Trade, run in straight line from whompa and you're there). Obviously almost the entirety of the rest of Lush Fields as well, except near the OPs.

Omni-1. There are mission terminals in some places around Omni-1 that neutrals can use (the easiest ones to find are just outside the Relax! bar). They'll almost always give you missions in or near Omni-1 (Entertainment, Trade, Omni-Forest or Harry's) even at QL40-50.

I try to avoid Newlands because I can't stand the lag.

As for groups, I've never had a problem getting goups when I'm in the mood. Borealis seems to be perfectly fine for a young neutral to find groups. Failing that I've been attached to both Clan and Omni groups when wandering around in Clan or Omni territory.
