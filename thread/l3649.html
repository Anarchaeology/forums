I know not official but you are making some incorrect assumptions with the statement "Can we expect those emails tonight or do we got to wait another day?"

The official web site states as follows:

"We are about ready to start beta 4. The CD will be available for download week 23. The link will be posted here.

All (yes we said ALL) beta applicants will receive an e-mail containing a CD-key. This CD key will grant access to the beta. The CD-keys will be sent out in chunks starting week 23. Please check ASAP that your e-mail address is correct."


This means two things. Nowhere does it state they will be at the beginning of week 23. It expressly indicates that they will be sent out in chunks.


So lets play with the following scenario. A Funcom Employee comes on and says CD Keys will go out tomorrow. That in no way puts you any closer to knowing when you will get it. Yes it does mean others will but it doesn't help you. So what I am saying is even if you are lucky enough to get an answer it won't help to determine when you will get your CD key.
