Fast levelling isn't the same thing as twinking at all. Twinking is simply transferring cash and/or equipment from a higher level to a lower level character (hence the recent overequipping nerf and all the complaints about twinking in PvP).

That said, if you want to level as quickly as possible twinking is probably going to be part of your strategy.

1) Twink the character to high heaven, so that you never have to worry about money, just xp.

2) Overequip as much as possible. Some professions are better for this than others, because of the differences in selfbuffing capacity etc.

3) Solo the toughest missions you can consistently manage, clearing them for the xp. When you can, team with people who are prepared to do the same.

That's about it, really. Probably others can suggest specific hunting grounds or static quests that will be good at particular levels.
