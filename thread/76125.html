I've got virually no PvP experience, this was my first real go at it tonight while helping the clans in Broken Shores. But here's what happened to me as me, trying to be a good little supporter, tried to get near the battle.

As I was still a ways out from the main fight, I encountered a bot that was yellow-green to me, so I sent Grrrrrr, my attack pet, after it. Moments later, a divest landed on me and an orange-con MA started attacking me. I'm not sure, but I don't think the trader actually shot at me at all, in part because the fight lasted even longer than I thought it would. But the trader's divest meant I couldn't use my nuke (not having PvP'd before, I'm unaccustomed to having to downgrade my nuke, so I had nothing set up to do that) and I couldn't retarget the Grrrrrr. 30 seconds or so later I was visiting reclaim.

Don't get me wrong, I'm not complaning. I thought it was kind of cool, and a pretty good indication of how a trader is still a force to be reckoned with.

On a somewhat related note, pet pathing outdoors in a jaggedly hilly area like northern Broken Shores is pretty much as bad as in missions. Even if I had not been divested out of control of my pet, I have very little confidence that we would have actually come to and engaged the MA.

And on another related note, trying to be act in a support capacity (I'm not saying all MPs should be, just that I wanted to be) by staying a the back of the line and handing out MP buffs to the recently deceased was kind of hard for me, in part because it was really hard to find where "the back of the line" was. Maybe this will become clearer as I/we gain more experience in these big battles.
