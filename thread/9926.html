Info for lowbies like myself...
Everyone here is talking about High Ql MCS, so I thought I'd throw in my experience building a Low Ql one. Since I had been wondering this myself and decided to just go for it and find out.

My level = 26 Fixer

Original Mausser = Ql 22 (all I had that I'd be able to convert and equip)

1) Purchased a Ql 87 Advanced Hacker Tool from an Advanced shop. (Figured I might as well purchase what I can afford to be useable in the future). Cost - approx 7,400. Apply Hacker Tool to Mausser. Requires 90 B&E.

2) Compression Chamber for Chemicals - Ql28, purchased from a Basic shop. Cost - 552. Required 92 Weaponsmithing.

3) Composite Barrel - Ql34, purchased from a Basic shop. Cost - 63. 92 Weaponsmithing was fine.

4) Sheet of Curved Carbonum Plating - Ql 33. Purchased in Advanced shop. Cost - 23,889. Required 88 in Mechanical Engineering.

Total Cost (including Hacking Tool) = 31904, give or take

Result = Cheap Mausser Chemical Streamer
Ammo: 50
QL: 22
Require MG/SMG from 117, Burst from 90
Dmg: 1-64 (59)
Attack: 3.00s
Recharge: 1.00s
Rng: 16m

Now this is a bit below what I can use, but I went with what I had, as in one of my old guns.

Also I invested 4,000-5,000 to purchase Weaponsmithing and Mechanical Engineering Expertise Buffs. 20 point buffs saving me quite a bit of IP. Also I had purchased Cracker's Luck to raise B&E with buff, as I hadn't purchased that nano yet.

Have yet to field test it, but my main point was to see if I could make one myself close to my usable level. I'm broke now, but happy.

Anyway, there's the info... hope it will help some lowbies like myself

Just be careful when you make one you don't end up with a higher QL than you had planned for.

Doing the math, I decided I could just barely squeeze into a QL98 MCS with the help of wrangle and stat buffs. So, I tracked down a 98, not the quickest thing in the world, as you know trying to find a specific QL Mausser in stores can be a pain.

Once I found it, I simply bought high the first higher than QL98 chamber, barrel and plate I could find, all ended up being around QL120ish in an Advanced store.

Then of course there was the fun of getting it hacked (needed a 401 B&E), as well as getting it put together. Once that was done, I was handed back my shiny new MCS, and man was I let down. I was all set to get a wrangle and strap it on, when I looked at the reqs I was 9 burst short.

I didn't do the math wrong, what I got back was a QL100 MCS, not a 98! Just like implants bump up a few levels if the parts are overspec, it seems an MCS can too.

I got VERY lucky and found a Soldier with Riot Control, and was able to get it equipped, so it turned out for the best, I'll get a couple more levels use out of it this way. But if I hadn't found that Soldier, it would have been at least one more level before I could equip it.

Gun does not suck, it replaced my QL120 MPS, and does noticably more damage per shot against NPC's I've ran into, not to mention the faster bursts. The range loss hurts, no doubt, but I can live with that, and take steps to correct it with a Yatamuci.

Oh, in another stroke of luck, I had just the day before mission found a couple Rings of Power:Physics of decent QL to replace my RoP:Proj rings.

RE-MCS in PVP.

Yes people usualy have a SLIGHTLY lower CHEM ac than Projectile AC. However its usualy not much. CMS does decent dammage in pvp but not vastly superior. Gamma Ejector is the only way to go PVP against most classes.

At 43, against a highly twinked enforcer (level 51 with a q170 sledge hammer equiped) I got wasted with the chem streamer, (Ran outta nano for keeping him rooted) and dropped him in about 9 seconds with the gamma ejector.

Reason was i was doing around 40-50 a hit with a 120 point burst with the MCS and 200-400 with the Gamma, With a 800 burst. Admitedly this guy had WAY too much armor for his level, but not much in the way of rad protection

Anyhow I guess the lesson there is to know the class that you are fighting, and what armor hes wearing, and what its weaknesses are. Is it rad, fire, cold, chem, projectile. Those are pretty much the dammage choices we have, and proj/chem are usualy peoples top two armors, so know your oponents weakness and use that. Chem till level 41, rad till 71, then choose rad/fire/cold based on what oponent is wearing.
