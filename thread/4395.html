New Stuff in between Blue @@@ Jan 19th, 2002

Apartment/Stores

To add some usability to apartments, why not allow players to have one booth that they can use to add items to that can be bought by other players at a player set price.

For examples, General Apartments can be redesigned so that there is a "front room" (this is where the player vendor machine will reside) and the back room where the owner will enter and store his/her things and furniture etc.

Of course the internal structure of apartments will change, currently a person must carry a key and enter a building with 5 doors or so to enter their apartment. Change this so that when a person enters an apartment complex, he/she enters a sort of lobby like the new trade stores, with many doors on different levels with hallways etc, leading to the many different player apartments. Each door is a new apartment if active that any player can enter (front room only) to browse the owners shop.

1) Players would have to purchase and pay upkeep on these new types of apartments (perhaps larger more expensive ones will be available) which also contain a more improved bank window (more storage and the box itself is huge....10x12 boxes displayed perhaps)

2) Apartment should also have some added functionality just so they are convenient to be in. One example would be the ability to do a player search (like on the AO official site) but it would just show the info of the player just like if you were to create a reference to said player.

3) Give players in apartments the ability to read a text only based version of the current happenings in Rubi-Ka. Instead of having to leave the game to read it on website.

4) Allow players to use their bed to remove all resurrection penalties or speed up the process greatly.

If a player purchases an apartment he or she, is given a key with a room number labled on the key (Key for room 202-1R for example, 2nd floor, 2nd hallway, 1st room on the right). The key will remain in the players possesion (no drop or give) up until 1 week after the player has not paid the upkeep, then the room and vendor will be wiped and the key will vanish.

Players may even be able to purchase add-ons to their apartments or more expensive apartments in general that have special features:

Apartment + Lab: Improves Trade Skills by a small amount
+ Swimming Pool: Personal Value Only
+ Arena/Save Terminal: Fight Club in your house!
+ Dance Room: Personal Value Only (music and lights play within room)
+ Bank Terminal: Different from apartment storage, allows you to access your outside banking stuff.


Using this new structure, there will have to be limits to the number apartments per complex, which is reasonable.


Trade Skills

@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@
Give players the ability to add nano effects to items/weapons/armor.

Example: Give players the ability to create blister packs with buffing type spells, for instance, creating a blister pack imbued with enforcer titan spells or MA crit buff.

Or add to weapons to help make them unique: These could be used to help add some damage to weapons, and really help out faster weapons to some degree over slow hard hitters.

Have a weapon cast a weak nuke similar to NT spells whenever the weapon is shot/swung (I saw some weapon that implied it casted a haggler I believe from the description) so this should be possible.

This could be mixed up to give weapons multiple type of damages. And could be configured to really help the lacking "fast/weak" weapons out there.

For instance, +50 Radiation nuke damage per slow swing is nothing to a weapon hitting for up to 800 like a hammer, but can due wonders to a dual wielder who is slicing/bashing away at super speeds.
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@


Allow players to add improvements to weapons/armor, very similar to the way implants work.

All increase values would vary upon QL of item being affixed to weapon.

Examples:

Poison Injector: Increases Weapon base damage by +15 and Changes weapon damage type to disease/poison (if technically possible for Devs)

Extended Barrel: Increases Weapon range by +5, Base Damage by +5, Main Requirement Weapon Skill by +30 (Increase Rifle for instance if rifle is main requirement for weapon).

Clip Enchancers: Increases ammo capacity of weapon by 15

Nano Impactor (Melee): Covers Weapon with nano bots that inflict X amount of damage type X upon impact.

Chemical Plasteel Plates: Can be affixed to plasteel type armors to improve the chemical protection available to that armor.


Missions:

1) More large open rooms in the missions, and maybe even multi-level building type missions.

2) A small chance per mission to have a boss type mob (unique name and maybe even look) spawned that would always say be dark orange or very light red to the player, after killing this mob it would always drop a random item ranging from 10 - 15 ql above the player level.

3) Lower the number of treasure chests in missions but improve the quality of the items within.

Weapons

1) There are literally hundreds of weapons available in AO, however more than 90% of them are worthless. Please have someone re-evalute the abilities of all weapons in general so that there are not so much favorites among the community. I would love to start seeing enforcers with something other than hammers or beams, soldiers without Novas etc.

2) MA's fists seem to be treated as 1 hand weapons and are very slow compared to fast dual wielders, if possible make MA's dual wield their fists.

3) Give MA's glove type weapons which are nano enhanced to allow them to deal specific types of damage, as well as improve their damage slightly.

Example: Fire Augmented Nano Glove: Damage would be slightly over the damage of the "MA Fist Weapon" used to calculate MA damage and would deal fire type damage. This would have to overlap the MA weapon to avoid MA's still punching while swinging a "3rd Fist". 100% MA skill based as well.

4) Since Fixers are shady type characters, I feel they should have some sort of unique dirty fighting tactics.

Give fixers the ability to use some illegal weapons banned by Omni-Tek for example:

Nano Field Destabilizer:
QL: 200
Range 15m
Attack: 1.5s
Recharge: 1.5s
Ammo: 70
Damage 85 - 220 (400) Energy (Down with HORRIBLE low end damage for all weapons!!!)
Def Skills: Dodge
Atk Skills: SubMachine Gun 100%
To Wield: SMG 920
To Wield: Burst 580
To Wield: Profession Fixer
Special: Destabilizes the ability for the correctly control nano bots (In english = each shot drains some nano from the target as well as does regular damage)

PvP
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@
New Ranking System For PvP: (Benefits too!)

People seem to complain a lot that said individuals with high PvP titles only Gank/Group/Camp other players and do not deserve them.

PvP titles should be based on Points (if not already in the background)

However, who gets these points on a PvP kill should be divided up depending on what % of the targets life was actually taken away by certain players.

This system would also have to reward others non combatant professions in a way too, and be capped in certain measures to avoid abuse. Example below:

The amount of PvP points you receive from a kill will be determined by several factors.

1) Player Level - There should be a standard amount of points players give based on their level, should another player kill them.

1) Con Color
----------------
This can be scaled in some form of %, like a red con player (sometimes they are attackable) will give you 150% of standard points, while a grey con player will give you 5% of standard points.
----------------

2) Percentage of Target Life Removed
----------------
This timer would reset everytime the player is healed to 100%

It is directly related to how much damage is done to the target from a full health bar...if you did 100% of the damage, you gain 100% of the points.

However if the target is not at full health (fighting mobs for instance) the player receives 100% minus the percentage of health the player was missing (granted they killed them alone).

So if you kill a person at 50% health and would normally receive 5000 points, you would receive 2500 points.
----------------

3) Non Direct Damage Spells
----------------
Should another player simply help out by paralyzing/debuffing the target, they will receive 5% for each successful attempt, capping at 15% to avoid abuse. This counter also resets if the target is healed to full health.
----------------

4) Healing You
----------------
So assisting classes such as Doctors are not left out, their alotted points will be based on the amount of life they heal another player and would receive a portion of any points that player receives upon killing another player:

Their portion is NOT subtracted from yours, these are bonus points....you will still receive the full value for the kill, but they will be complimented.

If you are healed:

25% of your life, the Doc gains 10% of your points received
50% of your life, the Doc gains 30% of your points received
75% of your life, the Doc gains 50% of your points received

These values do not stack per heal, it is a one time thing...so healing 25% then 75% of a players life does not net 60% bonus points, it still yields 50%.
---------------

Right now, these titles do not mean anything other than a very slight social status, but some rewards can be given to those who earn titles (small rewards as to not promote farming).

The reward can be something small like a tiny discount at shops.
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@

1) Move instant transportation (whompa & grid terminals) out of PvP zones, place guards near them and turn entire zone into political or mayhem (abosolutely NO patches of %75+ gas in %25 zones). This will make people have to run towards Meetmedere for instance and promote actual conflict (as opposed to duels) at the small town etc.

2) Also give players an incentive to capture PvP areas, such as notum mining caves inside of town, cheap vendor prices, etc.

Players and/or organizations should be able to capture a town by defeating all the spawned guards and player hired guards and recapture of the town main terminal.

The main terminal would be located inside a building similar to a mission building, although it would be static in design. The terminal would be a computer that the opposing faction would have to reach to capture the town. The static mission building would have a few standard guards and some player hired ones as well. Even perhaps include the ability to allow players with high trap disarmament to purchase and set traps that set off on factions other than their own.

After a town/city is captured, players should be able to purchase several services by donating money to the town:
(Granted these type of services can be implemented in AO)

1a) Purchase Guards, there will be many types, weak and strong, area damage, single, long/short range damage etc. All with higher/lower prices set to their effectiveness.

If possible, allow players to set a hired guard item on the floor and use to designate spawn spot (even allowed on top of buildings for strategic purposes in some cases).

Players will be able to set the respawn rate of the guards, but will affect cost. This affects how fast the guard will respawn each time it is killed and how much money it costs for the respawn.

Super Fast Respawn - 200% cost of guard from city money pool
Fast Respawn - 125% cost of guard from city money
Normal Respawn - 100% cost
Slow Respawn - 75% cost
Very Slow Respawn - 25% cost

2a) Purchase trap, land mines, notum draining fields etc, would require some trap disarmament skill to set though.

Traps will be very effective, however they will not respawn like guards do.

3a) Automated turrents, these are very expensive, do tons of damage, and maybe even area damage in the form of a mortar type turrent. These have the same options as guards, however due to it being machinery the respawn rate is inherently much slower than a guard even if set to super fast respawn.

4a) Allow players to purchase one time use combat vehicles, such as tanks, mechs, etc. These will be used like regular vehicles now although they give the player more hp (depending on type - a tank would give more than a simple truck for instance), and if possible a weapon that is used, tank cannon etc. (if not possible to implement, just make the tank increase projectile damage by 200% for instance and weapon range by 20).

These vehicles would cost varying amounts although they are very expensive even from the cheapeast vehicle...but not too expensive to the point they are worthless. After die within the vehicle the vehicle item icon disappears just like a mission key after completion.

Story Improvements:

@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@
I have seen GMs in game as Omni-Pol soldiers harassing (in a story sort of sense) players in Omni Entertainment. Instead of them standing around doing basically nothing most of the time, have them lead active events!!

Example: Have one clan GM soldier rally troops for some "cause" and go to one of the unused outposts in MMD and explain the "cause" to the clan members as to why you brought them here. Then have the omni GM inform omni players of the clan disturbance/cause at said location and actually lead the players into a conflict.

It may not seem like much but I think some people would enjoy a PvP conflict more being led into an armed combat by someone "out of the ordinary" rather than just by other players shouting "Clans in 2HO!"

And, you can actually inform the players with some dialog as well that can turn out to be something used as a TAG news line.
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@

Have some more in depth story interactions for players. For instance:

Have Cyborgs take over a town, they begin at a very low level strike force, of say fast spawning cyborgs within level ranges of 40 - 60 within a specific outdoor/indoor compound with maybe 1 - 5 cyborg commanders in the area/buildings etc.

Announce the event on the main site and give players a time limit, 1 week etc to eliminate 2000 cyborgs to retake the compound or city. However, to prevent too high level players from annihilating the cyborgs, make a kill only count towards the total tally of dead cyborgs if the player receives exp from the kill.

If the players do not complete this assignment within 1 week, the cyborgs reinforce the city and the level of spawned cyborgs increase to 80 - 100 etc. Depending on the type of monster in this event, the level limit spawn can cap out at whatever you wish.
