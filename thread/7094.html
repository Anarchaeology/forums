The trouble with Doctors is....
I considered myself sort of a "Battle Doc." At level 116 I have 3500 AC, carry a QL 154 Vektor shotgun, and Im equipped with a QL 115 LLTS. I can self-buff my health to 3100. I am able to solo all even (50%) missions and can solo many QL 127 missions.

So whats the problem? Let me explain 

Going Solo
--------------
To defeat one mob equal to my level can take 12-14 minutes (actually, this also applies to some hardened mobs at my level minus 1 and 2 ticks on the con meter). This is not an exaggeration. Ive timed it many, many times. I can take out most low greens in a less than a minute but even so, its not unusual for me to spend 3+ hours clearing a 50% mission. My gawd, that is so boring. No, its monotonous.

Specific Issues:
1. DOTs are weak and, for the most part, useless. To do 200 points of damage every 6 seconds to a mob with > 10k hit points is relatively meaningless. Most mobs regenerate at a higher rate than the incremental damage of my DOTs. DOTs had some utility at lower levels when I could do 125 damage to mobs with 2k hit points but now I see them as a waste of good nano. I use them only when Im attacked by multiple green or gray mobs.

2. Direct Damage nanos are underpowered. On the surface, doing 288-560 damage seems like a lot but the nanos are expensive (in terms of nano usage), usually do minimum damage and are often resisted. Even when successful, the health bar of an even-level mob moves down at a microscopic rate. Whether the DD nanos are bugged or simply ineffective against even level mobs, the end result is that I only use the DD nano on green mobs.

3. Debuffs  I dont think they actual do anything, do they? Wasted Limbs is supposed to reduce damage by 5 points and lower initiatives by 1577. First of all, thanks but whether a mob hits me for 400 or 405 points of damage really doesnt matter that much. The 5-point damage reduction might be nice at level 15 but being 100 levels higher, its kinda pointless. And I still dont understand how a mob can have all initiatives reduced by 1500 and still execute offensive nanos while simultaneously shooting me at roughly the same rate they did before I cast the nano.

4. Weapon skills for Doctor are all dark blue with the exception of pistol, which is light blue. I used to dual wield pistols but gave it up when the cost of multi-ranged was increased dramatically. I couldnt justify the IP cost and implant trade-offs to equip weapons that require multiple skills. There is also the issue of weapon speed in correlation to healing nano attack speed timing that make the slower firing, higher damage vector a better choice. My shotgun is okay but as I stated earlier, it takes forever to kill a mob with it.

5. Mission are way, way too big! Im just after a token and maybe an item I can sell. Im not looking for a long-term relationship with a single mission. There are times when I have only 45 minutes or an hour and Id like to do a mission. Forget it. I dont even look for a mission unless I have at least 2 hours to commit to the task.

Teaming
-----------
I take my role as doctor very seriously - maybe too seriously. I feel it a personal failure if anyone in my team dies. After all, keeping the team alive is my sole purpose for being there. By the way, for those who think doctors should be damage dealers in a group, you are idiots and dont understand anything about playing a doctor. In a group of 2 or maybe 3, sure, a doctor can engage in combat. In large groups its an unnecessary and dangerous distraction to the primary task at hand. Let the doc decide on his own whether to engage in combat or not.

Specific Issues:
1. Aggro  I dont have any real problem when a mob were fighting turns on me. I can usually hold my own until a tank pulls them off. What is absolutely stupid is when adds attack me first. How the hell did I get to the top of the adds aggro list if Im not at the top of the targets list? Sheesh!

2. Health bar lag and slow updates. It is so frustrating when, on the team panel, I see everyone at full health then someone starts yelling, "HEAL!" When I select that person I find hes down to 20% health. ACK! Because I cant count on the team panel display, I have to constantly cycle through each member of the team to find out what their health really is. When more than one person is taking damage it really sucks. The same holds true for team members who go out of range then return  their health usually shows 75% on the team panel but until I select that person, I have no idea what their true health status is.

3. No legitimate offensive nanos. Its odd that Funcom wants to pull doctors into the fight but leaves them helpless to do anything about it. All weapons skill are dark blue and DOTs, DD spells, and debuffs are ineffective in solo situations and absolutely worthless when teaming. I have never had anyone in a team say, "hey doc, cast your DOT on that hardened bully" or "doc, hit that medusa with your awesome debuff."

4. Nano execution lag. The most helpless feeling in the game is when I see Executing <nano name> and then nothing - the nano never completes. Sometimes Ill have 6 or 8 of those messages in a row in my chat window. The next message I get is from a teammate at the reclaim booth saying he just lost 2 million xp and asking why I didnt heal him. I feel like crawling in a hole. Saying it was lag sounds like such a lame excuse.

5. Direct heals, line of site, and missions. For some strange reason, team heals work around corners but direct heals dont. Whatever the logic (or lack thereof) behind that is, I can deal with it. What is absurd is that there are many bugged areas in missions where I cant use a direct heal on a player standing right next to me. Sometimes I get a message saying the player is out of range and sometimes I get the message that the nano is firing but it never completes (this is different from the lag related issue). Why did Funcom decide we cant play outside anymore? I hate trying to heal people inside missions.

PVP
-----
I dont care about PvP and wouldnt even if it wasnt totally screwed up. Personally, I think Funcom attempts to fix PvP has unduly harmed other parts of the game.

In General
-------------
I truly admire those who have chosen the play doctors and stick with it past level 100. While playing a doctor may look easy, it requires a level of prolonged concentration, attention to detail, and patience that most people could not and would not endure. Top that off with teammates who seem hell-bent on getting themselves killed and it makes for a difficult role to fill. Plus, there are lots of other professions that bring healing as well as damage to the table (MPs, Engineers, MAs, and Adventurers). Though not specific to doctors, the final straw for me was trying to acquire implants and, to some extent, nano crystals and armor above QL 125. I couldnt get them myself, couldnt afford to compete with wealthy professions to buy them, and felt like a leech constantly trying to beg them off guild members and friends.

My Solution
---------------
To save most of you the effort of responding with "Thats the profession you chose, live it" or "if you think docs have it tough, try playing a [fill in the blank]" Ill end by saying, youre right. I now use my doc to exclusively to cast treatment buffs and IC on friends. Im playing a new profession now (I won't say which because I don't anyone to read anything into it.)

This is my first-ever post here. The purpose of my post is to be vocal about issues with a profession that tends not to be very vocal. Maybe being quiet is the nature of the people who choose to play doctors or maybe there is no problem and I just dont play the profession very well. Ill leave it to each of you and Funcom to decide.
