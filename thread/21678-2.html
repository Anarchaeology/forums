Ok, for those people who just don't 'get it' about the Fixers, I'm going to explain it to you in the most simple way I can...

Fixers are a broken class. Any Fixer over level 60 knows this.

We keep playing our characters for 3 basic reasons:

1. We like the concept of the profession for various reasons (rp for example). Fixers are just cool.

2. We have been told repeatedly by Funcom that the class will be fixed/improved and hold out a small amount of hope that they actually will do what they say.
Most didn't know that Fixers were so crippled when they rolled them up. Eventually we realized it and we hope that the class is fixed because by the time we realized it, we were attached to our characters.
For the record, I started my Fixer on day 2 after release. At low levels, it is not apparent that Fixers are as crippled as they are. Also, Fixers were much better back in October (November?) than they are now due to various nerfs. The soldier burst nerf is one.

3. When/If Funcom's promises are kept and the class is improved, we don't want to then start a Fixer character from scratch. We want to already be a respectable level when it happens.
