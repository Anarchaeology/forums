Please Stand By: Incoming Announcement
My Brethen,

We the Whistle Blowers are the remants of the hope of peace from the council. We are here to deliver what was not said, and dearly needs to be spoken, or else our united freedom will fade into renewed slavery for all who number themselves as the many faces of the clan.

The ICC is poker faced, it has two sides; one public which we see as the alleged saviors for all who claim to call themself neutral. The other is hidden from sight, and feeds the jester that is their public face.

We the Whistle Blowers are ready to stand if need be alone. While our family fights amongest itself as frightened children not knowing what is to come of our fate. We do ask for all who hear this silent tone of hope to pick up your rifle, and die not for us, but for yourselves so our children may live FREE.

We remember the ancients of our motherland of the Earth that was so beautiful, but she has always carried her young with a grimace. Please remember the Ancient Alamo, and it's tale of woes, lest we forget we are doomed to fall like our anscestors have before us, and for us they did this act which was never in vain.

We are only mighty as ONE FORCE united, and not splintered. This is our rally call, does anyone one hear us?

------------------

We have breached a disgraceful officers command frequency line, and have received numerous reports that this first wave of ICC will not be the last come several years of our Rubi-Ka ,who also is grimacing like our motherland the Earth right now.

The poker face of the ICC is the bastards that are omni-tek can not meet supply that the ICC is demanding so as to maintain control of the alleged free universe of man. So our miner brothers of the stars will be brought in by the ICC to be their slaves in production. Before our very own eyes history is repeating itself!!! Can we allow this to happen once more on Rubi-Ka?

This is a mixed blessing however, since ICC has arrived omni-tek fears their contract on Rubi-Ka will become null & void. In consequence due to not meeting supply demands of the ICC.
This can truly become a three way war, with the neutrals finally getting representation by a force that could shatter omni-tek across the galaxy.

This is an ironic twist, since omni-tek built up the ICC, and now they repay them by devouring them whole. Like a snake that cannibalizes itself for self-preservation, stupid Snake!

We must instigate conflict between omni-tek and ICC, and help erupt the tensions between them. That will be our mandate for VICTORY!

Live Free or Die Proud, is our motto.

Undersigned,

We The Whistle Blowers.
