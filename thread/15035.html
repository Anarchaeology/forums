Suggestion for a handy trade skill
Before I go and make a suggestion to Funcom for a trade skill addition to the game, I just want to make sure there isn't something already in existence. It would be really useful if there was a trade skill capability to pop-out clusters from implants so you could put other clusters in their place. Is there any mechanism in game to do this currently? Say, use a screwdriver on an implant and it disassembles the implant? This would be incredibly useful at higher levels where you need implants that are over the QL125 available in stores.
