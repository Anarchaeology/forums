a missing profession...
This may be a stupid suggestion, truth is I am only posting it cause my friends think i should. Basically I have seen a wide range of professions and their comparables in other games... e.g. the fixer is kind of a thief of this game, and the MP is sort of a druid whereas the NT is the mage and so and so forth.... Well I see alot of this utilized in the game, hell we even have enchanters-'crats available to make. The one class overlooked that I think should have been one of the first classes utilized is that of the Bard. Why not a "musician" of some sorts in the game?... I mean come on we have clubs dance steps drinks all that is needed for a real party in this game save the significant entertainment!... Admittedly the bard can end up also being the most annoying and least likely to get a team, but this game has done a good job so far of making each job somewhat wantable by a team. Why not the everfamous minstrel of the future, a rockstar or a DJ or something?... the character that has enchanting abilities akin to the crat yet a singing voice to boot!.. hehe... I mean they could do so much with that prof' that the idea only staggers me as I learn more about the game and all i can do is find myself going "why isn't there some kind of Bard here?" I mean come on, it seems to me that music is only making a bigger mark on society as we in the real world move forward in time. Wouldnt those in the future, those populating another planet have similar interests and realities to some extent?... Now mind you I can totally see where it wouldnt be a daily activity for a big business to buy out a record company, but it does happen and couldn't that explain such a profession available to those in Omni?... I could see where it wouldnt need much rationale in the clanner or neuter zone, but a business might seem a little off kilter for a musician...
Now when I first came to this game I thought "man, there are so many professions to choose from how could they ever expand?" Well time and trial has shown me that alot of these professions (tho each quite unique in its own way) are very much alike. I think something like a bard character might just add the extra ****e this game is missing! j/k.. I honestly dont think it will add much other than new nano's and new items such as musical insturments for such a character. Still as I said my friends thought I should present the idea and me being the sheep I am had to post it. Besides, I thought, lessee what the rest of the community might say about it... So there it is my simple nooB suggestion to the suggestion post...
