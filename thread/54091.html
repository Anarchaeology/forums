hmmm
AO as a game has a huge role-playing community. It may be "make belive" from one point of view, but Role playing is what keeps people intrested once the power leveling buzz is gone.

Not everybody is in a race to make it to lvl 200. The game is set up to encourage role-playing but it is not forced upon you.

There are many hunting grounds for those peple that want to lvl, however AO also has Bars, clubs, parks, resturants and many other areas for role-players to interact in. Funcom also has "greenies" dotted around certain areas to promote role-playing.

When you start the game each profession has a detailed description to aid you in building your character.

Free items like cars as you said are ok, but not great for roleplayers as people will only do what is required to get the item they want and then leave.

Try participating in a event once in a while, joining a RP org or talk to some "greenies", you may enjoy it and realise AO is more than a level race!

I look at role-playing in AO, or any MMORPG, as a fun way to "get away" for a little while. It gives people a chance to be/act how they don't get to because of RL. For example, when I watch a good movie, I alot of time think of what it would be like if I was the "hero" in the movie. Online RPGs are the same thing, but one step further, you can be the "hero" and interact with other people playing and role playing their parts. AO is the first game I've really got into role-playing most of the time as it has so many social aspects of RL that you can do in-game. Like getting dressed up and head to a club for the night. Tack on listening to one of the streaming broadcasts from Gridstream or Vixen(free advertisement ) and it almost feels like you're actually at a club with a live DJ.

As far as vehicles, just like in RL, you have to work to be able to afford a car, so the same should always be the same in an online RPG. You should be able to afford your own vehicle for a while. That's why online RPGs usually have some sort of "public" transportation. AO-Whompas, DAoC-Horses, EQ-Horses.

And guns that kill in one shot to make it more like RL, I don't think that will ever happen in any online RPG, because it would make players very mad all the time. If they constantly got killed with one or two shots, they won't stay around for long until they leave and cancel their account.

I agree Role-Playing isn't for everyone, it's for the people that enjoy it as an escape from RL. I also think that both non-RPers and RPers need to respect the others choice to RP or not, which in AO I think happens much more then other games because of AO's atmosphere.

To put it simply, role-playing offers those that want it an escape from RL, where they can be completely themselves without socities pressures, or they can be play someone complete different. A shy person can become out-going, or out-going become shy, maybe someone with no self confidence can become very confident in themselves when online. To me that is the power of role-playing.
