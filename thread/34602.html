Party at Baboons - CANCELLED
PARTY CANCELLED

An announcement from Devils Advocate's president Thyme:

Devils Advocate is sad to announce The OT celebration party planned for Sat at Baboons is now canceled. No further dates for this party will be planned. Agent "Garrisen" has sent an offline message to me stating, "I have left Rubi-ka and am taking a much needed rest and I enjoyed speaking to everyone even the scum that hated me."

We are also sad to announce that all of GridStream Productions dates at Baboons are called off as well. Much to our dismay, there is a boycott on appearing in OT. Contact Wyldthing in game on how you can find out more about GridStreams stance on this matter and help them out.

I would like to thank Shadowrunrr for the awesome show the other night. Was a blast. A special huggle goes out to The Mockers who came and provided Showrunrr and the party security! There were smurfs everywhere! I cannot thank The Mockers enough for there support they give us when in need. Have you hugged your local Mocker today?!

Devils Advocate wishes to extend Garrisen a heartfelt good-bye. You will be missed. It is always sad to see someone leave our world. Hopefully one day you will return. Anarchy Online will not be the same without you.

We hope that all these horrendous lies and plotting and the never ending rumor mill comes to a close soon. Devils Advocate has always, will always be about great parties and having fun.

Often Immated, never duplicated. That's us!
