New quest idea...and special item reward.
You would have to find a NPC by the name of Lewis Medlock. He will give you a quest that goes like this.

Lewis will give you coordinates to a spot that you have to stand on exactly. You have to stand there and not move for 72 hours...no logging allowed. After you complete this task you will be given a bottle of Leet Daniels to take back to Lewis. He will then give you a Twisted Sister concert shirt that adds 1000 to all your AC's, but has a -200 Intelligence penalty. You will then have a title in front of your name like "Camper Hemicuda".
