Go North from the NW Tir Outpost to the Rocky Outcrops. Just NE of the Outcrops theres a small Outpost that you can save at. Save there, find a group, and head to the Temple thats just north of there.

If the spawns suck, go to the Varmint Woods outpost and team there. Theres lots of places you can hunt in VW for levels 40-60.

Take the whoompa from Newland to Borealis and follow the road out of town, after the Zone you can save at a small outpost near there. Look at your map and find the Volcano along the road close to an Omni OP and have fun leveling on Pixies and Plants that spawn in this area until you reach ~lvl48. My MA had lots of luck in this area!
