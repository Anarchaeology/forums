Logs: Omni-Tek Rally 3
The report for the rally can be found atAO Stratics here.

The full logs of the event can be found here.

A the end of the Rally, two Omni aligned criminals identified as Stefano and Shadowgrider kidnapped Administrator Houston and took him to their apartment in Borealis. They demanded the Dave 'Diamondcut' Duster be given a full pardon and the sum of 20 million credits before the hostage will be released.

After a long stand-off with forces from Red-Tape, the two criminals finally released the hostage and fled from the area. If you see these two be careful as they will most certainly be armed and possiblydangerous.
