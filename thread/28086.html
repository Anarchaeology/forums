About Grenade Launchers and reloading
The recently added Arwen MO-404 Grenade Launchers on first glance appear to be one of the nicer weapons in AO.


They are a bit slow (3.5/3.5) but do excellent damage.
The QL200 does 1-850 (425)

The problem is this:

The clip is limited to one (1) grenade.


If you manually reload after every shot it becomes a 3.5/3.5/3.5 weapon. Which is still pretty good.

If you let the weapon reload itself, it becomes a 3.5/3.5/3.5/3.5 weapon.
This lowers it to the realm of a useless weapon worse than a Techtronica Neural Disruptor... without the specials.



I suggest increasing the clip size of all grenade based weapons to 5-6.

OR

Incoporate some sort of reload command that can be hotkeyed.


------------------

It's incredibly frustrating to have to pick up a stack of ammo and click it on your weapon every 5-6 seconds. Not to mention in the case of screen/mouse lag, you run the risk of missing your weapon or the worst case scenario of accidentally de-equipping your weapon.


------------------

There's already enough useless weapons in AO.
