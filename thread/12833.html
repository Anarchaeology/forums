Before you chose to be Neutral...
This isn't meant to be a gripe, just to point out inconsistancies concerning Neutrals and the disadvantages they have and to warn anyone before they chose the middle road.

Disadvantage 1:
Every Neut, once he starts doing missions in other towns, knows that when he enters an Omni or Clan town, there's a reasonable chance he is going to be attacked by Omni or Clan NPC. So that Neuts are being attacked by both Omni and Clan NPCs. ( Certainly Switzerland was attacked by the US and the USSR in the cold war because they were Neutral? )

Disadvantage 2:
Neuts aren't allowed to shop in Omni or Clan towns. But Omni and Clansmen are allowed to shop in Neut towns.

Disadvantage 3:
Neuts are attacked by everyone - both Omni and Clan. And get this, they can attack us but we can't attack them except if they attack us first. Try defending yourself against a NPC Clansman who attacked you first ( while in a clan town ). Immediately the guard puts you to death.

Advantage 1:
Hmmmm... I can't think of one. If anyone knows of one let me know.

To sum up, why be a Neut at all? Why have that option? Because it's more challenging? If that's the case we should only be allow to fight with breadsticks.

I stand corrected. There are a few advantages. The team thing is definitely an advantage. Omni and Clansmen looking for tokens generally want no one but neutrals in it. Not really something that comes from the mechanics of the game but just developed by the players themselves. Though it's strange that Omni and Clansmen are seeking us out as team mates but Omni and Clansmen NPCs attack us on sight (if they aren't grey). As far as the Chat Channels, we can use both but I've not used either.

As far as being "immature", hmmm...not quite sure where that's coming from. I'm not looking to get into a flame-fest. A part of this forum's purpose is to help correct problems with the game. The other purpose of this post is to let people know up front what they are getting into when they decide on a Neut. I was fairly disappointed when I found out that I couldn't travel "freely" or browse other affiliations' shops. How many times I've died at the hands of assassins and guards, not knowing that now I was the enemy, too. Now that I know, I still choose to be a Neut, though now I have to learn to adjust my playing accordingly ( such as trying to get missions in Borealis, Stret Bank, or Newland, being very careful going into Clan/Omni towns ).

I'm simply stating the facts. I'm not talking about what's fair or unfair, just that the disadvantages don't make sense. For example, if they said, "Neuts can have no factions." I can understand that. It's not "fair", but it makes a sort of sense that neutrals don't have a political affiliation. But the disadvantages listed above aren't logical. It's hard to roleplay a neutral when the game mechanics prevent Neutrality from operating logically.

One of the reasons I chose Neutral was because I had no interest in fighting other players, so I wouldn't want "attacks on both sides in PvP" either. I think an obvious problem is 25% areas. 25% are sort of a double-jeopardy for Neuts, we can't attack but we are in a place where Clan/Omni players can attack us.

Really 25%s are, on a practical level, off limits to Neuts since Neuts could never be effective PvPers ( unless you want to wait for someone to attack you ) in these areas.

Why even have 25% zones? The difference between 25% and 0% zones - Neuts can't attack first. Seems strange doesn't it? Where 0% zones are PvP zones, 25% are really Neut ganking zones. Thank god for /terminate.

Make 25% places where Omni/Clan also can't attack Neuts. We wouldn't PvP there anyway. Sort of an Omni/Clan war zone. It would help support the fact that we are Neutral.
