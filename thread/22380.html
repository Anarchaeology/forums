these are so called haiku
japanese poetry.

strongly limited in form 5 syllables first line, 7 second, 5 last.

the only thing remotely comparable from occidental culture is a sonnet which includes rigid stanza structure as well.

anyway, the beauty of the haiku is that your creativity has to blossom in the rigidity of the form - controlled, guided, disciplined.

it does not contain any rhyme as such but is quite beautiful is it not?

comparable to the cold beauty of a symetric form haikus are something a little alien to me - i never was capable of counting syllables very well :/

anyway.
