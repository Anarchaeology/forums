Mercury Dragons - Recruitment Pamphlet

Join the Mercury Dragons.
Fight for what you believe.

The Mercury Dragons are dedicated to bringing peace to Rubi-Ka by dispatching whatever threats might arise against the Freedom of it's clan citizens.

The Mercury Dragons believe in four things:
Honor, Loyalty, Maturity and Respect.

Do you think you are a person that has what it takes to be a Mercury Dragon?

If you're level 25 or higher, you are qualified to join MD!

If you're level 145 or higher, you are qualified to join MDE!

What's MDE?
