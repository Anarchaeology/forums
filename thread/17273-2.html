The ARK sighed and explained that he does help people with being stuck on geometry and the like, just not anything that involves compensation of any kind.

The "no compensation" policy is to defend against exploiters and cheaters. Given that Funcom has done nearly nothing about exploiters and cheaters (a few bannings, but only in the most grievous cases), it makes sense that they would be very reluctant to open the floodgates to any form of compensation. This way, they screw everyone equally... both cheaters and victims. Let me point out that Verant with EverQuest has a nearly identical policy... compensation is VERY rare for anything in EQ, regardless of fairness.

Bottom line, the best bet Funcom has is to correct issues of this nature as quickly as they can. Customer frustration is never a good thing... and as it increases, their cash inflow decreases. Funcom knows this, and will do their level best to make us happy... but we'll see whether it will be adequate or in time.
