If I'm doing a mission with a team, this is what I do for chest looting. I look at the team window and before we begin the mission I say, ok, this is the order for chests. Then I go through the team window, calling off their names as I see them. If someone disagreed (which I haven't had happen) then I'd say look this is the most fair way to do it, and if this is a problem for you feel free to leave the team. I normally team with the same people, so we're pretty good about sharing the items we find. If an item is more suited to someone else's class, then whoever has the chest at that time will tell them to loot the chest instead.

Even within teams where I don't know the people well I've never had anyone refute this way of chest looting, and its always worked out pretty well.

If you find someone 'hogging' all the chests, politely /tell them that you'd like to keep it fair so everyone else has a chance for some loot, and ask them to wait their turn. Just getting angry doesn't help that situation. If they continue to do it, all you can do is ignore them, possibly kick them from the team and never team with them again. At least with the present game mechanics that's how it would have to work.
