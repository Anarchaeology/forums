I'm not a member of TC/MoK/Trinity but...
I'm not a member of TC/MoK/Trinity but here is what I understand to be the situation:

Awhile back, when I first started going to Tarasque spawns, a raffle system was in place, it worked generally like this:
Each guild team was allowed to sponser 1 unguilded/under-represented guild person.
1 full guild team = 1 number in the raffle system.


Tarasque would die, 1 person who is trusted announces loot and grabs the items that are not NODROP for raffle outside Camelot (done to conserve time w/in a 25% zone) Items that were NODROP were raffled immediately, and the winning guild sent a person to loot the NODROP item.

Now, for reasons unknown to me... some ppl decided that they were not getting enough loot, or were being treated unfairly for their strong participation in the Tara raids... this led to the "Big 3" loot system.


Big 3 system: TC/MoK/Trinity each took turns respectively looting Tarasque while excluding everybody else.


That system was in place for a short time. And now, it would seem that the clans have gathered up and organised themselves, and have come to Camelot En Mass, and basically overrun the place... ie, same thing that omni did to them way back when.
