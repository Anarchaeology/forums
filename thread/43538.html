New Faction!
What we really need is a new Faction; the Outcasts.

You cannot choose to be in this faction. The way you get into this faction is through the /lamer command. Each use of this command adds a point to the target's hidden 'Lameness' attribute, and the command will only work once per day per person to prevent mass-macroing people into desolation. After you gain so much Lameness, you become an Outcast. Now, you can't shop anywhere and all guards shoot you on sight.

This would be an effective way to combat griefers, gankers, beggers, ninjas and other lame asses.
