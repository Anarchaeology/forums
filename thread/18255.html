Omni-Tek Awards
  1. Basic Board - Does nothing.
  2. Ribbon of Strife - 10 Tokens - Title level 1 (Lvl 1+) - Life + 21
  3. Double Ribbons of Skirmish - 30 Tokens - Title level 2 (Lvl 15+) - Life + 49, Nano + 28
  4. Triple Ribbons of Battle - 60 Tokens - Title level 2 (Lvl 15+) - Life + 168, Nano +112, XP +1%
  5. Flag of Sacrifice - 120 Tokens - Title level 3 (Lvl 50+) - Life +336, Nano +224, XP +3%, Defense +1%
  6. Double Flags of Righteousness - 250 Tokens - Title level 3 (Lvl 50+) - Life +350, Nano +350, XP +4%, Defense +3%, Attack +1%
  7. Triple Flag of Humility - 400 Tokens - Title level 4 (Lvl 100+) - Life +350, Nano +350, XP +6%, Defense +4%, Attack +3%, All Dmg +1
  8. Flags of Glory and Redemption - 1,000 Token - Title level 4 (Lvl 100+) - Life +700, Nano +700, XP +7%, Defense +7%, Attack +7%, All Dmg +4, Nano Prog. +210, Comp.Lit +210, Run +210, Concealment +140
