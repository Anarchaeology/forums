Trying to maintain a high QL heal by constantly draining is very prone to resists, errors and bad timing making it dangerous. I get in stretches where I outheal Docs but that is maybe about 1/4 to 1/2 of the time spent in the mission.

Effect of proposed solution (One cast per dot)
I can't remember the timer on the self DoT but I'm guessing if your plan was enacted I would be able to heal once every 2 fights at an average pace for a good 6 member group. I could save the group if things go very badly, that would be the only use.

If this were true, then Traders value to a group (post 14.2 = average damage for a Trader) would consist of:

Single Target calm
Lend Nano
Wrangle once for buffs at begining of mission
Average damage

Calms are great, Lend Nano is good but it will not be missed, Wrangle is good. Overall, a very poor showing indeed. Without heals we need to rely on unique benefits. MP's can buff nano that lasts longer so...our only unique gift to the group would be Lend Nano. The outlook would be very grim for the second least played prof in AO.

Better Solution
Make each cast of a heal stack the DoT on the Trader as I originally thought it would. This would make it almost impossible for a Trader to be the main healer in a group. It would promote situations where the Trader would have to rely on another Team Healer(s) to offset the DoT to cast a heal even semi-regularly.

Fix the minimums for Doctor Team Heal.
