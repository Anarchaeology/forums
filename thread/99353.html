New business venture
I am going into the furniture business. Ever since I arrived on this planet, I have been disappointed in the style of furniture; it's all too high tech and cold looking metal. As such, I have seen a potentially great market for more traditional furniture made from wood.

For the past few months, I spent my time looking for enough investors to purchase Belial Forest and have finally done so. Belial Forest will now be called "Nevverland" from this day forward and the new company is called "Rubi Ka Crosscutting." The sawmills and workshops have been built and we begin leveling the forest this upcomming Monday. Any tenants left in Nevverland when work begins will be moved into nearby Eastern Foul Plain where they will be free to begin their new lives.

Rubi Ka Crosscutting is your friend.
