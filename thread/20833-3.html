Epsilon Purge is self-only, with the exception it works on MOBs as well. The reason for this is because Epsilon Purge is a hostile nano program. What it basically does it strip the target of all hostile nano programs and buffs. So, it can be used on the Doctor themselves to remove any hostile nanos (and buffs) and be used upon any MOB to remove its buffs.

The reason we can't make this executable on other players is due to the possibility of it being abused and used in grief episodes. It would be far too easy, and silly, for someone to execute Epsilon Purge on another player in either the cities or out on the battlefield and strip them of all their buffs without their consent.

I know you all would like to use them on other players but that's just not possible at the moment. We have to take the lesser of two evils. I'll keep suggesting it be targettable in the hope it can be one day.

I promised a post that says more then this sucks, here it is

First is to check what the effect is of using Epsilon Purge.
Not being high enough to use the nano myself I base most of my arguments on the assumption
that it can be used at level 150. I don't take in account humidity extractors, recompilers, NCU upgrades (makes for to many different scenarios)

I assume that the nano init is high enough to insta use nano's

What do you lose if you only use nano's around that level.
Epsilon purge, ql 149, 200 nano, 1.4 secs recharge
the team health buff you run standard
Basic Omni-Med Enhancement , ql 149, costs 940 nano, 5.11 sec recharge
The costs of two team heals to get all your teammates back to max health
Circle of Renewal, ql 149, costs 187 nano per casting, 374 nano, 8.18 sec recharge (total)
General buffs
expertises in biological meta, time and space, matter meta, weapon exp buff
2 seconds, 160 nano

So the use of epsilon purge to get rid of a hostile nano takes
1674 nano energy
16.69 seconds IF you manage to run buffs exactly after one is finished so this is more
likely to be around the 20 seconds or more.

This is what the Doc loses at the most basic rebuff. Add another 461 nano and 5.19 seconds
to that if the doc also runs Constitution Magnification (short term health buff).

To this you need to add the nano costs of everything that group group members have to use to rebuff you.
And this doesn't even take into account the value of buffs that you the group doesn't have but that you got before you went on a mission.

So we got 20 to 30 seconds recovery time (not counting the wait time between the uses of a nano charger) and somewhere between 1674 and 2100 (and a bit) in nano energy.

What kind of debuff is worth that?

Dots?
Wrack and Ruin, ql179. is probably the worst DoT there is. 544 damage every 8 seconds for 2 mins for a whopping total of 8160 damage.
Cycle of Life, ql 149, 807 nano, only restores 3990 damage in that time
Greater Field Dressings, ql149, 204 nano, restores on average 878 hp, which takes 5 castings to do the remainder.
Total costs 1827 nano, total time 25 to 30 seconds.
This indicates that EP is more interesting IF and only IF you solo and you do it selfbuff only. The moment you team, the team spends more nano then this, the moment you don't solo selfbuffed only you need to get back to town

Roots/slows?
A doc has 4 DD's to use, any single one will break those for a lot less nano even if you have to heal afterwards.

nano/weapon Skill reductions?
You already need implants (from what I read on this forum) to be able to use EP so when those hit forget it, just sit the 3 mins out.

init debuffs?
Usually last around a minute and with the extra time you lose on the use of EP (and since part of this time is used in combat) you are better of sitting out the remaining 20 to 30 seconds.

AC reduction?
for a few minutes you get hammered 10 to 50 points extra per hit and any decent tank will have taken the aggro long before that that turns into a problem

Nano reduction?
Forced Bankruptcy, ql 185, strips a total of 927 nano, using 1600 nano to get rid of that, yeah.

Attack skill reduction?
Blindness durations are shorter then the time it takes to use EP and rebuff.

Evade reductions?
the extra damage that result because of this is still cheaper to heal.

Nano cost use increase?
Notum rejection, ql 149 adds 25% to cost. Can be cost effective if you expect to use more then 6696 nano plus four times what your team mates used to rebuff you. But why bother, you can't spam enough heals in the time it takes to unlock time and space from the last nano charger use to actually run dry thanks to a debuff that forces you to increase nano energy use.


So there are two, two specific situations in which it might be interesting to use EP.
The first situation you will never see since I don't expect a lvl 150 doc to solo 185+ critters.
The second situation is a bit more likely to happen, but to have a nano that specifically counters one other nano in the game....


The second point I want to raise is When does a doc get hit with those nano's?
It usually takes a few hits with normal weapons before a mob starts using a nano.
This indicates soloing or adds. That are the only two occasions a doc gets hit with them. Or the tank is not good at keeping/aquiring aggro.
So most of the time (doctor is a teamproffesion) it is not the doc that gets hit by the nano but the tank/person doing the aimed shot.
This further lowers the times that this nano would be used.


Third point I have is that the debuffs it IMO would be actually worth to have to go through the entire process of rebuffing stop you from using EP.
The Sense Improvement requirement is just to high to survive an unmake/dominate/plunder/divest.

The usefulness of EP would be increased a lot if it would only remove hostile nano's. And the game already knows the difference between friendly and hostile nano's, just watch it complain when that DoT is going to run out in a fight.
And it should be usable on others, limited to the team you are in, because if the tank can't tank due to a divest/plunder combo EP would get the tank back on his/her feet. If the NT is hit with Nanoshutdown EP is the nano to use.
