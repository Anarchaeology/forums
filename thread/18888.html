This is what I think happened:

Before, in the day, you'd kill a monster, and there would be some lag just before it died, like the game had to load up the death animation/sound.

So they went and fixed that by making the game have a bit of fore-knowledge on when one combatant would die.

This made the death animations more smooth, but had some quircky side effects:

- Undead Monsters: Oh they are dead alright, you killed the hell out of them, but there is some type of lag that makes them forget that.

- Loot Lag: He's dead, he's on the ground, now comon cursor, switch to the little gear already....

- Battle Destiny: This one is my favorite, since the outcome of the battle is derived a couple seconds before it actually comes, sometimes you can get the XP before teh monster is dead, yet you haven't killed him yet. I have, on multiple occasions, started running away because I see the XP come in, yet I haven't laid the final shot, but as soon as I stop running, no matter where I am at, I hit the creature for the final blow. Kinda funny when it's 5 rooms awa with a pistol sometimes. This one is also fun as a player tho, I have been killed, yet lived to keep fighting for a while. All my buffs cancel, my health drops, but I am still alive...


Try to fix one little thing and look what happens...
