Some PvP tweaking coming up in The Notum Wars
Hello everyone!

I wanted to drop by and leave a couple of notes about PvP and what we are currently working on with The Notum Wars. As PvP will be a big part of The Notum Wars we sat down and looked at how PvP works today, which profession is best in PvP and which profession needs a boost. In some cases we wanted a certain profession to be especially proficient at taking out one other profession. I will give you an example of this. The NT will be the fixer killer and he/she will get a special nuke that will be very effective against fixers in grid armor.

PvP battles in The Notum Wars are meant to revolve around tactics where characters of a certain profession will need to take out characters of another specific profession as soon as possible. I wont go into more details here, but I believe you get the general idea.

See you on the battlefield!

Well, the problem is that Traders (and MPs, don't forget to nerf MPs! /me ducks) are the anti-EVERYTHING. Safe to say that pretty much any class surprised by a Trader or MP in team PvP is kinda boned. Fixers lose GA, Docs lose their uber heals, Enfs lose damage buffs, etc etc.

Of course, the introduction of debuff-cancelling items in NW changes it all around. I'd really rather have had those remain in EpPurge, because now they have to re-balance all of the classes to make up for it. It changes the rocks-papers-scissors.

Doc-specifically, they can't do much. Getting to CH is friggin' easy. So giving us something like the NT's up-coming 2hb uber-nuke item isn't really an option to persuade us from not fighting. Now, since the debuffs can be cancelled, crits are nerfed, etc, what is the only thing "balancing" docs is mediocre weapon damage. In team PvP, it may be enough to make a Doc concentrating on weapon wielding not really worth it, to instead focus on tradeskills and using the debuff cancellers. But in either case, it'll become pretty bloody hard to take out a Doc one on one (have you ever seen 2 Docs fight?) and call for a "balancer"... /me shudders

BTW, rock-paper-scissors works out a lot better in team PvP than you'd think. It is the one place chaotic enough where Agents going into sneak actually doesn't get noticed that much, an Engineer can hide effectively, and you can't quite pick out the healer among the enemy group (and the lag).
