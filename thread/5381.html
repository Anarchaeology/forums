Phear Leet
It doesn't have to be that long of a walk. Yes, you have to make the journey from Athen through Shire to get to the longest road. But there is a clan outpost called "Bliss" in the zone that has a Clan insurance scanner, mission terminals, and several shops, including an advanced weapondealer, advanced armorer, and advanced medical supplies. I used to hang out there a lot, as the mission terminals used to give some sweet cash.

To find it, enter the Longest Road zone north of the Cobal Forest, and look for the big buildings in the desert. It's hard to miss, the structures are about the same size (and style) as Reet's Retreat in Last Ditch, with several present.

A full team of 40s should be able to get some nice loot and have fun in the first level of the dungeon, after that mobs start to get tougher as they approach level 70. The mobs drop great loot, usually armor...Waitt, Bau, Omni-Pol (white) and some Obtru steel-ribbed and Elite later on.

The mobs in the dungeon are not as overpowered as the ones in Steps of Madness, but they do have some nasty weapons that can put you out of business fast..I'm level 89 with 2.5k AC and some evades, and they still hit me for at least 131 damage (the ones with flamethrowers).

Lower level teams should definately bring a good doc
