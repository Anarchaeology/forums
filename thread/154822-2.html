As Gaute said, the Unredeemed are not "evil", neither are the Redeemed "good". They both have a differing understanding of how power should be obtained and used. The Unredeemed preferred to drain the notum source on Rubi-Ka, while the Redeemed wanted to conserve it. Ultimately the greed of the Unredeemed resulted in the existance of the Shadowlands, as the source exploded and ripped the dimensions apart, leaving only a small trace of itself in the Shadowlands - the dark dimension - but a mirror of Rubi-Ka.

Both the Redeemed and the Unredeemed suffered from this explosion and wars against each other for control over the source as I understand. Being greedy might be considered "evil" by some, while it is ultimately the reason why Omni-Tek exists and many great inventions would not exist without Omni-Tek. So the statements that Omni-Tek is "evil" or the clans are "good" is incorrect. It depends on your view of good and evil. I for one do not consider Omni-Tek "evil"... rather profit-oriented, willing and able to get things done one way or the other.

Regarding Philip Ross and him being an unsuitable CEO for the Rubi-Ka department of Omni-Tek.. I can only disagree. While I agree that he should be more visible in the public, the security measures that need to be taken every time and the danger Ross is always in being not insured and openly attackable in the public, do not justify risking his life. I'd rather have a somewhat hidden CEO than one who gets shot by the clans and who would celebrate a victory like this over Omni-Tek until the end of existence.

On a sidenote, as others already mentioned... Omni-Tek is quite far from a democracy. It is not a nation. It is a corporation. You are not entitled to throw out the CEO nor vote for a new one. The only entity able to do anything with Ross is the Omni-Tek Board of Directors on Omni Prime. Luckily, as I can see, you are not part of this rather powerful council.
