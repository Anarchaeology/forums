Following a hunch, I found him on the first try. It turns out to be one of the oldest and most infamous NPCs - Monday Klein

Most of you old school OMNI know right where he's at, cause he probably killed you a dozen times. So the young uns and clanners will have to track him down the old fashion way. I can't hand everything to you

very special deal
special notum crystal
where is the atrox
