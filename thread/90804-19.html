Easy creds with High level friends
Well, I am not really about making money with any of my alts but it's nice to have a nice nest egg. someday one of my alts will get a yalm. Then I will reach for the stars.....

Anyway, a quick tip for making some nice creds in the Subway (if you have a high level friend with lots of time on their hands) is to farm the morphing memories and have your friend upgrade them as high as they can. You don't even need to sell them on the shopping channel. I was getting 35k each for ql160's from a shop term so a whole bag was worth about 1.4 mil creds. Not too bad for about an hour or two of farming.

Luckily I have some nice friends who will help me make to most of my farming. And to think I am an MA (I should have been a farmer!)
