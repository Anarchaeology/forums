I notice in these arrest warrants that all of the clan alliance Endgame has been labelled a terrorist organization. Since some of the least offensive clan organizations belong to that alliance, it seems to me that it would be quicker, easier, and take up less poster space to simply label all clan organizations, everywhere, as terrorist.

Frankly, I'm also glad that I'm not an OT employee, but instead free to pursue independent freelance work. (Incidentally, my current contract is with an omni organiztion.) Despite the hefty rewards offered, it seems that OT's fabled efficiency is sadly lacking. I have been in WE clan territory frequently in the last week, and absolutely nobody had the least interest or concern about the warrant for Vixentrox, least of all the redoubtable clan leader herself. I imagine the same applies for the others. Given the obvious lack of interest by omni personnel in any of these suspects (I noticed no agents, bounty hunters, or other signs of interest at or near any of the usual clan hangouts), I'd say these warrants were a dead letter upon issue.

I also find it unfortunate that neither OP nor OAF have taken the least interest in recent clan offenses against the smaller omni organizations, whose towers have been systematically destroyed, causing dismay and demoralization throughout omni ranks. I would have thought that a corporate-wide security force would take a special interest in defending those branches of the corporation most in need of help, especially when the clan employs proxy armies such as "Demolition Squad," who just this last Friday destroyed a tower in Omni Foest! Dealing with such attacks would do much more for omni morale that pursuing high profile individual clanners whose life or death will have no effect on the activities of tower-smashers.

Frankly, it is failures like this that may explain the recent, steady stream of defections from omni ranks to those of the clan. After all, if a corporation cannot protect its own assets, those assets will naturally seek refuge elsewhere.
