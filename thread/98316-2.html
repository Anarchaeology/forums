Hey, I ain't Dovve or something but here's a few to get you all started:

Iziris Agathon 518 x 597 Omni-Trade
Has basic rumours on the SBC funding of Dusters, but well basic stuff we kinda all knew or guessed at.

Horatio Campbell 298 x 203 Omni-Trade
This guy is probably one of my faves, he's hysterical. Hates Omni-Pol as they framed him, cost him his job and marriage...recently changed a bit but he used to take cash for questions. Different amounts for different stuff. Info on Eva, info on rumours of assassinations of Ross. Also info on battles of Omni-Pol v Omni-AF. This guy sells the lot, all for a price (drop creds in get dif answers). Used to tell about the guy in the fixer grid who seems to be kinda quiet on the subject.

Ida Schuller Walker Omni-Trade
Shadowlands, Professor Maitland and other stuff. Mostly NT info She had a full speil. One old time spoken rant is on the IRN but others have been placed on this thread way back when. Shes actually kinda sexy really, but then again I am a nanomage

Sam Cadena 1541 x 420 Athen Shire
He gives info on the trade stuff, and also on Harry and the Knights etc.


Have to warn you all though. One patch they talk, next they wont. It's pretty hit and miss. Also what they say also changes. Heck Ida has been a real pain, one patch she will have loads, next nothing, then another she has loads on other stuffas said pretty hit and miss.

Also you need to keep an open mind on these guys. The NPCs do tend to crash or generally get buggy. Some dont have half the info on them, or some just generally crash and only give out little bits and shut up quiet quickly. Always worth a walk and to come back later. Its also worth noting that the newer NPCs seem now to be or have been upgraded to use the new click to speak menus, as others still use the old text commands where adding a ? to your questions confused them, so if you ask them something don't add a ?

p.s. lemme know if you all find something
