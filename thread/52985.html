14.5 has failed. 14.6?
In what many were hoping to be an end to the monotone flavor of BS missions, 14.5 has failed to deliver any fun. Forget the fact that there are 140 camps, bosses at the camps who can send you to the reclaim with a single crit, new EQ-like quests all revolving around 1 very EQ-like mob....it can almost be said that AO is an EQ clone nowadays. Sad....very sad.

14.6? Could this be the straw that breaks the camel's back or the orgasm that requires an extra cig afterwards?

This thing called PvM content for higher level peeps is an oxymoron. Do you think XP is among the top concerns for peeps in the TL6 range? I don't think it is. In fact, every TL6 player I know is dying for more focus on PvP. Even the whole make an alt and twink idea isn't appealing in this game. If 14.6 turns out to be another PvM content patch, I'm sure that it will be the straw that breaks the camel's back.

The key is PvP. Yes, polls have been done on who PvPs and who doesn't, but one very important requirement that should have been included there was left out of that poll. It should have been a poll available only to those who have characters in the TL5-6 range because it is in this area of the game where PvM has already bored people out beyond sanity.

The current implementation of PvP is ridiculous. Somewhere in the storyline, there is supposed to be a war going on. Well, lets see...what do you need at the very least in order to breed a war that are in fact absent in this game?

1. Something to fight over or for. This does not exist in Rubi-Ka. It simply doesn't. If you consider Tarasque loot as something to fight over, wait til they turn Cam into a 75% zone....you know it will happen soon. Every 25% zone which got one of those dyna-camps is now a 75% zone.

2. A reason to fight for it. One thing comes to mind here: alignment wide skill boosts.

3. Two or more conflicting sides that need the "something" mentioned in one or two (big emphasis on the word CONFLICTING). Omni vs Clan is fine. Omni vs Clan vs Neuts is even better.

Without any representation of the above three in the game world, there is no basis for war..no reason for it. Putting the elements above into the game will not fix PvP overnight, but will provide a solid ground on which PvP can be developed into what it is capable of being. Doing this would give everyone something to look forward to after the leveling process.
