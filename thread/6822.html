Profession summation + - (Read)
ALCON,
It seems to me that EVERY PROFESSION wants an insta-kill button so they may be GODS in their own right. No one is satisfied with the dmg they do at their particular level, regardless of the equipment currently equipped. Everyone will want to do more damage. Your profession is not gimped because you cant kill something with little or no effort.
I say gain levels. Levels help you do more damage! Levels help you get into that new weapon, wear that new armor, etc... which in turn will allow you to fight harder mobs! If you say you cant fight dark oranges, let me clue you in...It isnt supposed to be EASY!

People want over equippers nerfed because they do more damage than those mediocre players who either refuse to take advantage of in-game mechanics, or are just too damn lazy. Casual players want to be able to kill their opponent regardless of how hard their opponent has strived to better themselves. UNDER-EQUIPPERS, (coined a phrase, I did =P) want to compete with those of us who are well equipped, not overly so. It is not possible for me to equip a weapon of Higher QL unless I had the skill. How can you OVER-EQUIP? If I had the skill, then I was able to equip it. Theoretically, I should be able to remember how to pull that trigger after the buff wears off...
Besides...If we were all the same damage do'ers, leveling would be a pain in the arse! Sometimes, I count on people to do more damage than me. There are times when I COUNT ON THEM to do so. There are times when I cannot equip my armor for a few levels, or cannot upload a nano program to do more damage, and need someone to pick up the slack for my inadequacies.

PVP? I know it is unbalanced at present. I play a nanomage. It is hard, but a friend of mine recently got a title at lvl 113. I say his level because it wasn't at level 7. You know what I am talking about. If a nanomage can do it, anyone should be able to. The choice is up to you to make the right decisions about your IP, weapon, and armor. It is up to you to STRIVE to equip the very best possible for your current level, and to take advantage of all other professions; have them assist you in doing so.
If the person you face sends you to the reclaim, better look within your character. YOU are to blame. YOU ARE TO BLAME. Take it like a man, re-evaluate your character and his armaments. Mission for money to buy anew, and then stand opposed next time. Stand your ground knowing that if you fail this time, it means doing more missions to buy even more uber gear. It is only your fault. You should either stay away, or eventually be the best damn pvper there is. (If you're level 75+, you should know better. You should know who to challenge, and who to save for a better day. Try and get a few more levels under your belt, worst case scenario.)


People want damage dealt and hitpoints of mobs reduced to the point of killing them with ease. Sorry to break it to you guys, but the color code scheme of Mobs is precise. If a mob is yellow to you, it is a sign that you are EQUALS. You should use caution when fighting a yellow for one simple reason: You designed your character to be strong, and if he is your equal; He is strong too.
I am sure there will be people who say, "But greens destroy me!" A green is only ten levels below you. There was a time when he was able to whoop you. There was a time when you had to run for your life from that particular mob. If you can kill one, youre doing good. You shouldn't have this, and you dont: <press here to kill a green> Remember, he is still able to whoop you if his friends collaborate. It is the same as us joining a team to kill a mob 50 levels higher than us. Why is this so hard to understand? If it weren't a challenge, who would play?

I wont even pretend to know the answer to those who say they cant kill a gray mob.

EVERY PROFESSION cries out for more buffs to ease in the self proclaimation of god-hood. No one wants to walk around or holler on the shopping channel to see if someone can help them? They need to ask the game manufacturers for more buffs? They feel the need to argue about other professions and what they have had implemented, (as if a life line), because they cried out; whined even?
To that I say: Play more... Adapt and overcome. EVERY profession has weaknesses...

From experience, I play a 105 MA and a 114 NT. I do my level missions. I strive to cast the best possible spells with my NT, and use a parry stick QL 150 with my MA. What once cut it for lower end monsters no longer cuts it when youre playing with the higher end mobs. Cut and dry.
Greens only pose a problem when youre stumped with no armor available for ten more levels. You do get slumped in that aspect, but...Theoretically each time you level, you are able to fit into something better armor-wise, and weapon-wise.

Proof-reading this after-the-fact I don't feel any better. I skip around a lot, but you all get my point. I am tired of people whining about how gimped they are. You have no one to blame but yourself. If you want to have a soldier's shield, be a soldier. If you want to have a pet, be a pet profession. If you want a nano regen buff, be an NT. If you want to uber heal, be a doctor. All these professions have their weakness...It is up to you to capitalize on it/them.

I like playing the game, and I like to think that I am capable of doing whatever I assert myself to. It is up to me and my frame of mind. I will not sit idly by and make the wrong choices and pray that if I whine enough, say my class is gimped (enough), someone will throw me a lifeline before I go under.
Play it. Make intelligent choices throughout your levels and I guarantee you will be a fine addition to any team.

Peace.
