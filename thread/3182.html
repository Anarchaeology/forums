Status Meter/skill lock timers
I was playing one day, and was thinking, what if there was another bar, similar to the attack bar and nanoprogram bars, that showed A-How long until FlingShot, Burst, Fullauto, Dimach, etc were available? Also, a nice little display that told you how much ammo was in your gun? All would be optional, and turned on or off at will. Thats all I can think of right now, but tehres tons of other things that can be monitored in the game that would be nice if they were on your HUD.

Even if they werent directly implemented, they could be an item or something, lower QL ones could show less, and higher QL ones could show more. They'd have to be a reasonable price, because I wouldnt pay 500k just to see how many bullets were in my rifle.
