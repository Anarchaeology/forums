I have a table in front of me with 3 screens and a table to the right with one screen and table to the left with a notebook computer.

I have one 21 inch screen in front of me with a full size keyboard and mouse in a pull out drawer.

On both left and right front I have a 17 inch monitor. In front of each monitor is a half size keyboard and a mouse (no keypad). On my right on a table is a another 17 inch and mini keyboard. On my left side on a table is a notebook computer and mouse.

I am playing on test with my hydra and having fun. I do team missions but since it takes me a while to position my hydra in the boss room I am setting the difficuly to far left. Still get good xp in mission and am able to take down the boss mob.

It is nice to get all the boss loot and and not have to fight/share with others. A large number of items I find are used by my hydra. I don't have is a soldier so I do not need much heavy weapons. Nor an agent or MA. So these items get sold.

I picked 2 pet classes as it is easy to hot key a an assist puller and send in the pet command. I spank it and then the Q (attack) key and go back to concentrating on my tank, and doctor ( and later crowd control).

The doc is hot keyed to heal whomever he is targeted on.

My fixer is my puller and will be giving damage buffs and run buffs to the group.

My crat is for crowd control and has a pet to boot.

My meta is for nanao programming to combine implants, mochams etc and also has a pet.


I use hot keys to setup /follow and /assist on all characters.

I use two pet classes and their assist setup on the main puller (my fixer) and then send in the pets.

Anyone else playing a hydra setup?
