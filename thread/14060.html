Doctordudes dairy!
Day 1

I feel so horrible here at Omnitech, I wish I was never born. Why is my dad a Omnitech employee? WHY?! I must get away from here, i must join the Clans, Omnitech must be stopped.
Late in the night, when my family was asleep, i ran, far away. I ran all night, then all day, then finally, i saw the beautiful city of Tir. When i arrived the guards wanted to kill me, but i explained my situation to them, and they agreed to let me in. Some sweet girl named Neijaa took me to her home, so i had a place to sleep.

Day 2

Since i was untrained at any proffesion, Neijaa dicided to train me as a doctor. She gave me nano-programs, items and clothes.
I trained all day.
