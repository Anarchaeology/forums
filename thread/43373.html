the real mechanics of evades are unknown, but ip in them are never a waste.

what they do is lowering the chance of mobs to land a critical hit on you.

but if you are at 100% aggro (meaning your agg/def slider is all the way to the right) you still get hit almost every time by a mob, even with maxed evades. if you move the slider to the left however, evades become more effective and mobs are starting to miss you completely.
