Profession restricted weapons. How not to.
Please stop making everything profession / level restricted.

Instead, add tech to the game to make weapons require more than just the core weapon skills to determine attack rating.

Then instead of adding class restrictions, use more weapon requirements:

Here are some ideas for weapons intended for each class while still letting others have the option of using them:

Soldier (33% assault rifle, 33% heavy weapons, 17% smg, 16% rifle)

Adventurer (33% 1h edged, 33% 1h energy, 34% adventuring)

Crat (33% pistol, 33% smg, 34% psychology)

I am sure that the devs are creative enough to come up for some reason as to why adventuring, psychology, craft skills, etc would be required to use a weapon effectively...

This would make weapons fairly profession restricted without actually making them restricted.

Also, make skill / attack rating increase damage / crits for any weapons that they dont (oh, and fix weapons that dont work (non-launched grenades come to mind)) already effect.

If someone wants to make the sacrifice in IP needed to use a weapon, let them spend IP on it that they would otherwise spend on nanoskills/tradeskills/defenses/etc.

What about a map nav weapon?
Say Pistol, Map Nav, Some engineering, and computer literacy for a hacked sattelite weapon controller? Lets see those engineers calling lasers down from orbit on unsuspecting people =)

Implement critical failures for weapons...don't give people OE penalties per se, just give them a chance to shoot themselves (or even have their weapon blow up) if they are using a weapon that they don't meet the requirements for...the farther from meeting reqs, the higher the chance of something bad happening.

Anywise, I think these kinds of things would add a lot to the game, let professions be the best at their intended weapons, and at the same time, keep the game skill based rather than level based.
