Neutrals can get both quests. You get exactly the same mission rewards as you would if you were Clan or Omni, even down to the mission and bravery tokens. Just go to see Alvin or Dodga (your choice), navigate the tortuous conversation tree (with any luck revamped this patch) and do the quest in the normal manner.

One advantage (the only advantage?) to being neutral, you can get both sets of shoulderpads
