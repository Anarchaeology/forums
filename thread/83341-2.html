Name: Vitae Covente {RP}

Leader: President Lizor

Purpose: To fight against the oppressive OmniTek corporation, and the sneaky ICC to release their tyranny from the world of RubiKa1.

Guild Name: Law Society of Rubi-Ka
Faction: Omni-Tek
Director: Malcom "Alamexis" Age, General Counsel
Guild Website: www.aci.on.ca/~robc/lsrk
Email Contact: malcom_age@hotmail.com
Guild Description:
Devoted to all things "legal", including the enumeration of the planet's laws, through the Rubi-Ka Body of Laws Project, the creation and supervision of Rubi-Ka's planetary court system, and the provision of legal, diplomatic and dispute resolution advice, through the Law Society's Advocacy Program. We are active politically, vocal and visible, and above all else helpful.

Guild Objective:
To bring equity and justice to all citizens of Rubi-Ka through peaceful dialogue and effective dispute resolution.

Guild History:
Originally founded by Malcom Alamexis Age and Reynaldo Monilith Griffel as the "Body of Laws Project" in early November 29476, the expansion of the project into the creation and supervision of Rubi-Ka's first court system, as well as the provision of legal and diplomatic advice, necessitated the evolution of the original organization into the Law Society of Rubi-Ka.
