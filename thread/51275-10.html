Here is the true X vs trader list by a trader:

Other attacks trader first not in the arena:

MA vs Trader => MA 95% (Several big specials)
Enforcer vs Trader => Enforcer 80% (Have to root too many times and run out of nano)
Agent Vs Trader => Agent 65% (Because trader can't shoot or cast spells because agent roots reach too far for a trader)
Soldier vs Trader => Trader 65% (Most soldiers don't have resist and spec in shield too much when they really need multiple specials to unload on a trader)
NT vs Trader => NT 95% (long root and big nuke)
Dr vs Trader => DR 95% (trader run out of nano long before a doc does)
Fixer Vs. Trader => Fixer 80% (roots, hots and trader runs out of nano)
MP vs Trader => Trader 60% (mez pet, kill owner)
crat vs Trader 60% (Mez pet kill owner)

Now if the trader attacks first:

Fixer doesn't lose..they just run away.

Easily beets the:
Agent, Nanotech, Soldier, Dr.

Still loses to the MA because they do so much damage early and have a high minimum damage.

Is iffy with the enforcer.

MP and Crats are really really toast. (Can't control their pets)

So basically the trader class is totally reliant on the first attack and stacks up really well against three other classes. The weakness about that first attack is that the spell range is practically the same as the melee range and the shotties only hit close up too.

If your a MA, NT, Enforcer or Agent. DR and lose to traders alot its not the trader...its you.

If your a pet class then your just bad against mezzers.

If your the soldier you have to unload quick on the trader with several specials otherwise your screwed.

In general traders are better than most at lower levels (75 and down) and worse at 75+. Thus traders have it bad for 60% of the levels. Unfortunately once this is found out you can't de-level.
