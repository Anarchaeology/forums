The Dawn of A New Era
A great day has come upon us. We as Omni-Tek employees are living in prosperous times. There could not be a better time for the company to progress. And progress we shall. With the end of the constricting document known as the "Tir Accord," a vast number of possibilities are now upon our doorstep. All we need do is take them.

We all know Omni-Tek is viewed as the superior governing body on Rubi-Ka, as defined in the lease by the ICC. And we as a whole have control and operations in a number of territories to date, but as of today the company has the opportunity to expand planet wide, and here is why:

The Tir accord clearly states that with The Council in affect, they will control Tir, Tir County, Athen, Athen Shire, Aegan, Northern Artery Valley, The Big One, Avalon, Mort, Newland, and Western Endless Plains. But with our beloved CEO declaring this accord invalid, these areas can now become base to Omni-Tek mining and operations. New department bases can be expanded, our mining can be increased ten fold since we were restricted to setting up any new mining facilites in the past, and our profits can soar as high as the eye can see.

With these territories at our fingertips, not only can we indludge in the mining resources that have gone unclaimed, but our military base of operations and labs can be expanded as well. Rubi-Ka as we know it can be turned into a profit generating machine, 24 hours a day, churning the benefits of its rich notum upon each and everyone of us. And While we expand so will our inventions and breakthroughs in the medical field, science, and military. There is now no accord to tell Omni-Tek, "you can not go here, or you can not mine that area." We have the opportunity for global expansion. And as a dedicated employee to the company I would strongly urge Omni-Tek and Mr. Ross to look into these overwhelmingly endless possibilites.

-Sid Tisantous Saar

Legal Clarity...
To clarify and expand on a few of the legal points of this discussion:

Even though the Tir Accord has been declared void by OTRK, what you have to realize is that the Clans, as unified by the Council of Truth, NEVER possessed effective and legal title to the lands as so described in the Tir Accord.

Point 1 of Annex 1A of the Tir Accord quite clearly states that "Omni-Tek is the the superior governing body of the entirety of Rubi-Ka, but will leave to the Council to supervise the areas..." And further, in Point 2 "[t]he remaining teraformed areas on the planet will remain under complete Omni-Tek control and subject to the ICC lease of 28708."

Notwithstanding OT's blanket lease over Rubi-Ka, OT was prohibitted by the Tir Accord from controlling or "administering" Notum mines (Annex 1B) in the Clan lands. However, and what has not been realized thus far is that, pursuant to the terms of the Tir Accord, the Council was NEVER conferred legal ownership over these mines and their endemic notum supplies, let alone the areas in which they were situate. They were merely delegated the authority, exclusive of OT, to "administer" these districts and, pursuant to the August 11, 29470 Amendments, the notum mines. OT was, however, prohibited from establishing new notum mining operations in Clan-administered territory.

The assertions thus far that OT should "reclaim" the Clan lands would be foolish and quite frankly presumptuous. We have not yet received any statement from the ICC; given that ICC peacekeepers are currently the occupying military force in Clan areas, with instructions to "keep the peace", any OT advance would certainly lead to a military conflict in that regard. Furthermore, should OT troops march on Athen or Tir, they will undoubtedly be met by the entirety of the Clan populace - armed and not very hesitant to pull their triggers. OT would thus face a two-front civil war, which Philip Ross stated himself is not what OT wants.

However, given that notum operations in the formerly Clan-controlled north are, with the dissolution of the Council and the Tir Accord, essentially not being administered at present, OT should reclaim these facilities in order to ensure their continued production. The ICC would certainly not object; the market price for notum has probably doubled by now, given that the galactic supply has been cut in half. So long as the only visible and stated goal of OT was reclamation of the notum supply, OT would not be challenged.

To clarify one final point, there are currently limits in place on the level of participation that extra-planetary hyper corporations (like Sol Banking Clan) can have on Rubi-Ka. They are permitted to transport notum and people to, and notum from, Rubi-Ka, but are not permitted to colonize or develop the planet further until January 1, 29500, pursuant to the ICC response to Sol's formal complaint of March 15, 29444. Even in 29500, they are only permitted to purchase "limited interests".

As such, any role that these insurgent corporations would play would be nothing more than passive (i.e., supplying weapons to the Clans a la RK civil war #1, or perhaps supporting terrorist factions...).
