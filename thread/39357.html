New sport on Rubi-Ka!
Ok, me and my friend Scragnoth were tooling around in the backyard...and here's what came out of it.

LEETBALL

RULES
-----------------
2 players enter a deserted backyard. One sets a timer to 4 minutes. The other turns him/herself into a leet and gets their quickbar ready. When the timer starts, the player has to:

1. Find a leet
2. Charm it
3. Buff it with Coat of Barbs or Frost with Snow
4. Bring it back to the blue-gate pen
5. Release it.

Player who ends with the most leets in the pen wins.

STEALING
-------------
We discovered if a player is already charming a leet and another player tries to charm the same leet, the leet will go under player #2. This is called stealing and can be allowed or disallowed.
