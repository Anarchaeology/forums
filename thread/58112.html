The booster will be out in November it seems, the expansion probably spring next year though we have no actual date yet.

If you don't buy them you will miss out on some of the features and new content, particularly with the expansion. But they are *not* essential. The booster's main features are the ability to build towers, guild ones and personal ones, the new vehicles, and some enhanced graphics. The expansion's main features will be new zones, monsters, gear and character options.

Yes you will get benefits from upgrading, but no, you don't have to, there will still be patches and new stuff in those rgardless.

Expansions and the like are standard practice in MMORPGs, Everquest has about 4 for example, and as for fair... from FCs point of view it wouldn't be fair for them to not charge something for their hard work... if you wait they'll probably eventually be released as a single product and less cost anyway...

The choice is yours at the end of the day, pay more money get lots more content, or pay no extra money and get less new content. Only you know whether its worth it to you.
