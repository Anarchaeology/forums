May the truth be known
Citizens Of Rubika,

Several months of independent research and investigation have taken place regarding the Alarming Activity seen around the abandoned mine at Azure Lake. This event occured on Friday July 25, 29477. Herein lies the actual truth of Omni-Mining's presence in Borealis on that day. This information is contradictory to Swiftmind's public release of Omni-Mining's Official Explanation of Presence in Borealis.

This contradictory explination is evident in the classified document that has been obtained from the Rome Blue Offices of Omni-Mining:

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Authorized Personnel Only
Any unauthorized personnel reading this document will be punished under Omni-Tek Law

This Document is authorized to the following:
Administrations of: Omni-Mining, Omni-Research, Omni-Admin,
Omni-InterOps, Infinite Circle


Description:
The following is the investigative report on the accident in the Borealis Mining Complex filled in by the Engineering department head of Omni-Mining.



August 10, 29477
Investigative Report BS420
Reported by Engineer Gutenhi


Greetings honorable Directors. I will get to the point of this report.

On the week of July 25, 29477 the Borealis Mining Complex was put on emergency status. Soon enough we discovered there was an explosion in the deeper levels of the complex. We have been investigating this since that day. I believe we have discovered the cause of this serious incident.

On the evening of July 25, survey team 2 for the other side embarked down to the lower levels to conduct their monthly travel their. As you all know, Project Blue Stone has shown promising results and this night they tested the new and improved C108N-A. On this night, survey team 2 took C108N-A down with them to test out its durability. It seems something backfired. This is the first known accident of Project Blue Stone, so we have investigated it fully.

It seems though C108N-A is a perfect replica, it still doesn't work with dimensional technology. This means either something is wrong or missing. It seems it didn't power it correctly, which causes a backfiring malfunction with the destabilizer. When the survey team tried to portal over it accounted in a massive malfunction which caused the devastating explosion. But this wasn't a normal explosion we have concluded. There was not a lot of structure damage. This was a dimensional breach, between the two sides. This was caused by a deconstruction wave interference between the regular energies and that of C108N-A. The wave lengths appeared to have been 202.0074 which went against each other causing an instable portal. We are lucky that the back up systems developed by Omni-Minings Engineering department kicked in and stopped the breach. Other wise it may be still open. A fully technical report is in BS420-TA.

I fully agree with Director Moscardelli's order to stop surveying teams using different complexes for this. The safest place to do this is the portal in Alpha Base-001. We won't need to worry about such an event happening again. I also recommend stopping the use of Notum from Project Blue Stone for this kind of work. It has proven it self unsafe.

I will speak with Infinite Circle and see what we can improve on C108N-A. I believe this is fixable but requires time. I will fill out another report on what the Engineers of Infinite Circle agrees on.

Gutenhi
Gutenhi Head of Omni-Mining's Research and Production Department

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I personaly find this shocking now that I have learned the truth behind what was really happening near the city I and a number of other neutrals call home. There has been an outright lies spoon feed to us by Swiftmind and Omni-Mining. This again proves that this branch of Omni Tek will stop at nothing.

Thank you,

A slight breeze tickled the air surrounding the elevated platform high atop the Nascence Frontier Training Grounds, and Nathan "Ammicus" Age pulled his suit coat tighter around his body. There was something unnatural and yet strangely intruguing in the air; a restrained power of unimaginable proportions.

Glancing at his datapad at the report from the NLF General, Nathan sighed. It had been kept secret for far too long. Originally an Omni-Mining operation, in conjunction with Rubi-Ka Universal Robots, Infinite Circle had drawn the attention of Omni-Tek's intelligence services....drawn his attention.

"Mr. Age?" A timid male voice called from behind him followed the footsteps that Nathan had heard approach. "Your...test subject has been released." The scientist took a place at Nathan's side, pointing down to the swamps below.

As Nathan focused downward, he admired the manner in which the creature moved silently, swiftly, disappearing into the shadows of the arched alien foliage. Moments later, a nearby Goffle hardly had a nanosecond's warning before a hand reached forth from the darkness and severed its aorta in a swift, fluid motion, spilling its blood on the forest's floor.

The creature glanced up at its master, holding the head of its prey aloft, a sly, evil grin washing across his face. Nathan smiled, and with a slight wave of the hand, urged the creature onward.

"Excellent..." Nathan muttered in awe, watching the creature whipe its black blade on the carcas of the fallen Goffle and vanishing. You will come in handy, Nocce...the NLF wanders too close to our goals...

Oh, what Director? John questioned.

Well as you know, I was moving some items out of my office in Rome. Dabblez came along to help me in the moving. Those bots of hers are very handyfor lifting things anyway.

Did her bots ruin your desk? John interrupted.

Cristin then put up the video log, showing Ramiyana sneaking into the hallway of the office. Well here is the video data. But it seems he did not do much.
