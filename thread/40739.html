With each new game comes the usual cheaters & exploiters.
These are the same people who download a game from some illegal warez site and then switch to "god mode" so that they can complete the game in record time.

The don't play games for the enjoyment, they don't play the game because the game is a bit of a laugh.
They simply play to be "the best" and attempt to ruin the fun for everybody else.
It's sad and all we can do is that these people eventually grow up.
Realise that winning isn't everything and that you can actually have fun and enjoyment in games.

However I am in agreement with you, I wish some of these companies would have the guts to open a brand new server a year after the initial release when most of the bugs and exploits are ironed out.
