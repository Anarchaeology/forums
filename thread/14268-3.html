Tak --

There is a difference between fixing a bug and nerfing an ability into oblivion.

Range. NTs should have the greatest range. NTs have the lowest HPs and ACs and must keep enemies at a distance. It only makes sense that an NT should have a greater range than anyone else, even if only marginally. FC not only fixed a bug, they entirely removed the NT range advantage.

Likewise, they have "over-fixed" a myriad of other bugs too: AEs, LOS, stuns, the list goes on.

I'm not going to argue that NTs were not a bug filled prof (we still are). But FC consistently has taken the EASIEST TO IMPLEMENT route to bug fixing, and have consistently failed to take into account the ramifications of changes.

Other professions likewise think that the removal of bugs have only removed unfair advantages that NTs might have once had. That is not true -- a lot of fair profession unique advantages or tactics have been removed by hastily thought out bug fixes.

You see?
