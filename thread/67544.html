Team Mission Suggestion
The most annoying thing about doing Team missions is after you've complete the mission. If you don't have a Fixer (or a high enough Fixer) in your party, you have to spend half an hour getting lost whilst trying to find the way back down.

My suggestion is that a one-way warp back down to the first level be placed in the boss room - either in a corner, or have it appear after all the enemies have been killed.

That would work, but I suggest that your solution is too specific. For me, the problem is that there are no map indicators of important aspects of the environment, specifically, entrance doors and elevators. So even once you've found them, you have to keep refinding them. Every mission is exactly the same: the team or someone has to spend a couple of minutes at the end of every level to refind the elevator that was discovered earlier. 100% stupid.

Another aspect of this is that mission maps are wiped out if you leave the mission site. Specifically, if you die, you have to spend time searching for the elevators and/or rebuilding the map. Mindless. Maps of the mission need to be saved until the mission is deleted.
