Armor, combat, and melee rings.
OK guys, I have a few questions I would appreciate answers on:

1) While fighting a green soft enforcer i was being hit for a constant 40 per hit. I wanted to test my new senpai coat, so I took it off (-250 ma AC), and the guy kept on hitting me for 40 points. So i took off my body armor (Another 315 AC gone) again he caried on hitting for 40 points. Off came the helmet (About 200 AC-ish), and he carried on hiting for 40 points.

So question 1 is: is armor useless? or is your amror value calulated at the instigation of combat and cannot be changed during combat by adding or taking away armor? IF this is the case, then it doesn't matter if my Wooden Skin runs out in the middle of a fight does it? I can just turn it off when the fight has started and free up 11 (or however many) slots?

2) During combat, the red numbers indicate how many hit points I lose when the other guy hits me. But what are the white numbers? IS that how much i hit for? (Before the enemy's armour reduces the actual attack). Or is it the amount of hit points the enemy loses? I ask this because my MA attack of the snake has a minimum damage of 1, which I often see happening, despite my melee ring (Adds 5) and my Meditate on Autumn Leaf (Adds 28 i think) and my corrosive fists (Adds 11-ish). The same applys for Bird of Prey (Min damage 19). Now these 2 VERY often hit for minimum dammage, but surely min damage should be 1+5+28=34, not 1 (This is if i dscount the corrosive fists which transforms to chemical attack). Either these attack-boosters don't affect the special attacks for some reason (Even though the description says ALL melee attacks, how many are there for God's sake?) or the value of 1 is the amount of damage inflicted AFTER the other guy's armour has absorbed the damage. In which case it is one hell of a coincedence that his armor always manages to reduce the attacks to exactly the same value as the minimum attack value (1 or 19 depending on the attack). Or the min-damage implys that no matter what level of armor the enemy has got, 1 point of damage will be inflicted. This can't be the case as this doesn't happen with weapons, so why should it happen with MA special attacks?

Anyone who can shed a little light on this has my thanks.
