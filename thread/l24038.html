Possible ATI Solution
Hi Everyone,

Here is a possible solution to the ATI errors. This has personally been tested. Thanks Extruder!

You should be able to permanently solve this problem by turning fog off in the ATI properties in the Control Panel. This is how you do this:
In the Control Panel, double-click "Display".
Click on the "Settings" tab.
Click the "Advanced" button.
Click the "Direct3D" tab and here you will see different settings listed that can be turned on and off.
Turn the "Table Fog Support" feature *off* (make sure there is no check in the box).

Please note, you are turning off the "Table Fog Support" feature in the Direct3D. This is not the same as turning off the fog in the game.

You need to have the latest drivers for your ATI video card installed, as the older drivers may not have the Table Fog Support option. If you have older drivers, be sure to uninstall them first, reboot your computer, then install the newer drivers. You also need to reboot your computer after installing the newer drivers. (Be sure not to download the beta drivers; you want the retail drivers.)

This should help fix the problem right now, but we are looking at a fix on our end of the problem and should be fixed in a future patch.

Thanks and hope this helps.
