Some thoughts
I'm also glad that we crats get some attention especially if it makes team play better. Right now (Lv66) I do mostly solo missions due to the problems when teaming. A couple of points I'd like to make.

1. Fix the Speech so that it doesn't spam/interfere. I've no idea how good the bonuses are but the current form of the nano makes it too annoying and disrupting to use.

2. Add a 'Peer pressure' factor to the Psyche check. I've found the Psyche check limit to be very well balanced when it comes to solo missions. In teaming however, as several have stated, we need to be able to affect the more dangerous opponents a group usually challenges. So add a Morale or Peer pressure bonus on the Psyche check. The larger the team the bigger the bonus.

3. Using the droid in teams outdoors are usually not a problem. But in a team mission it often gets a weird area aggro effect. Where enemies come from all directions several rooms away. I see from the attack messages that they instantly targets my droid, and thus conclude that he is the cause. The crat should be a 'crowd controller' not a 'crowd gatherer' . My team members usually request that I don't activate my droid in missions...

4. When implementing new things for us (or any profession for that matter) try not to copy abilities from other professions. This only dilutes the game. I read some arguments for us getting summon team member nanos (like engineer) because we summon our droid. Using this reasoning, since all professions have the same nanoskills, you quickly come to a point where everybody 'should' be able to do anything based on nano. Just look in this thread at all the fixers. I think the main reason they're upset is that you took away their uniqueness. I know I felt similar when traders got charms (even though those were as useful as a paper hammer in a rain storm). Don't go further down this path, please.

5. For the love Chronos give us some writing materials in game!! Every MMORPG I've played (read: UO and AC) had books and papers. It's a great asset in events and roleplaying and should of course not be restricted to crats only. If crats gets any special writing abilities as the suggested making OT/Clan registration forms I'm all for it, though. Preferably I would like a fairly advanced memo pad where I could store info about players and locations, sort of an extension of the reference menu that exists now. Using the /memo playername you could send messages to a player that don't get lost in chat spam. Integrate this with organization functions and player made missions and it'll be great..

I've never played a fixer, so I won't even comment on their plight.

As for the bureaucrat:
(Warning: involves some class envy here)

They have a light blue NP skill...why? Nano-bot programming is meant to be a technical profession, correct? One that has an affinity for things computer and cybernetic. So why does the meta-physicist have a green skill here?

Rationale: MP's are the mystics of AO allegedly so where did their affinity for NP come from? Of all professions in game, it seems that MP's should have the least ability with NP, at least from a logical viewpoint.

Someone who is attuned with his/her "God-side" wouldn't have much of a need for an earthly and very technical skill like nano-bot programming.

A bureaucrat, it could be argued, does. Yes, crats are masters of everything mind and influence. They also summon robotic pets, like the engineer. Not manifestations, but metallic robotic pets. That alludes to some, if not a lot, of technical skill in computers and cybernetics in general.
Our skill set doesn't reflect this.

Request: reduce the skill for nano-bot programming from light blue to at least aqua.

Non-general psy/int buffing.
Currently non-existant except Odin's Eye.
Rationale: bureaucrats are movers and shakers of the political world and are able to influence the actions of those around them.
Therefore, I'd like to see the introduction of a nano line that allows us to buff +15-20 psy/int, akin to the doctor's Iron Circle for str/sta. It is the bureaucrat that has this power over others and would add to the lustre of the profession and well as to its worth.

Voila, instant worth to the bureaucrat profession and a viable way to earn a non-combative living.

We can't wrangle for tips, we can't effectively NP for tips, we can't IC or headcracker for tips, we can't feline grace, treatment buff, buff run speed, riot control, challenger, etc, for tips.

We aren't alone in this lack of non-combative income options and I'm sure other professions can add their voice. Each profession should have at least one skill that can be used to derive an income or proceeds to upgrading their equipment.
Give bureaucrats the non-general +20 psy/int buff, please. We are the logical profession for this type of nano.

My above comments re: meta-physicists aren't intended as a denigration of their profession. Keep in mind that you, the player, didn't invent the profession, Funcom did, so don't take it personally.
