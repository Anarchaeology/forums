14.2 patch planned for Wednesday, 5th June
The 14.2 patch is scheduled for Wednesday, 5th June. We know you've been waiting for this patch for a while now, and we are also eagerly looking forward to its release.

The reason this patch has been in development and testing for a while now is due to one of it's big components - the IP reallocation. Essentially, due to the nature of this change, we want to get this addition done right. If the patch went live and a problem was found with the IP reallocation on the live dimensions, every single character in Anarchy Online would have to be rolled back. Obviously, that's a big hassle for us and for you, so we want to do all we can to avoid it, and that means making sure bugs are ironed out in testing.

This all means, however, that if on the off-chance an issue does arise with the IP reallocation on the live dimensions, it is highly important we are notified right away. If it is reported later than 'immediately', it means a rollback will have a large effect on every character - even more so the longer a problem isn't reported.

But, as said, and to reiterate, one of the reasons for the heavy testing of 14.2 and its resulting delay is more ensuring the quality of the patch and a lack of bugs. We look forward to giving you the option to reallocate IP, and we're sure it will really enhance your Anarchy Online experience.
