Limiting the number of attackers.
Could you limit the number of ppl that can attack your tower depending of the number your of ppl online in your guild. We cant just stay awake or make our daily schedule depending of when the tower will be PvP enabled. Even if we planed the timing, we could not be able to protect it correctly. Limiting the number of attackers depending on the number of the Controlled land Organisation would be nice. For example, lets say that Org A got 15 members online and guild B is gonna attack Org B. Since Org A number of members online is 15, the system make a 'Cap' of 30(maybe lower) for the controlled area. Right now its all about how big your guild is or how many ppl you can bring with you, no skills, no tactic just number.. Might as well call it Number War heh..
