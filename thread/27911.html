Stoping Misson terminal camping Ideas
Nobody likes mission terminal camping, even the people who do it. It begs the question WHY people camp mission termianls.

Originally, one of the great features touted about the mission generators was how they would be tailored to each class. This is absurdly false. Missions are not tailored for anyone other then level.

Asside from change the very nature of missions which would require you to figure out what missions are most doable by a doctor but not for an enforcer, you can still change missions to a great degree to stop mission terminal camping.



1. Mission rewards: A HUGE factor in camping...

This is an easy fix for funcom. Only give mission rewards that can be used by the player. Dont offer a Nano-tek and adventuer nano-crystal. Dont offer an enforcer a assualt rifle as a reward. Don't give a Martial artist a shining implant of Akimbo style (duel wielding guns).

Just this small change can make people camp the terminals a lot less since everythign they will get is potentially usefull to them.

Specalized rewards: Look at the characters class and skills. Does the character have a lot of IP in pistols, burst fire, and fling shot? If so, then make any weapon offered as a reward a pistol with some or all of these properties. Offering armour? Is the characters Sense and Intllegence stats 20 pts higher then all the rest? Then offer cyberarmour, not somethign they cant wear.


2. Mission terminal presets:

Most people have a certain configuration of the sliders they want, make this configuration always the default. If they want a missiont he can just click "generate mission" without manually moving all the sliders.

Also eneabling the generate mission button even after pressing it once is would be nice.

3. Mission preferences option:

Do you want a kill the person, locate the person, locate the information, or repair the device mission? You should be able to select eactly what kind of missions you want. Nobody likes the fetch and item and bring it back to the terminal, so most people could just filter that type out so they never get it.

4. New Slider: Distance from the mission terminal.

Basically this controlls how far the mission is from the mission terminal. The farther away it is the bigger the reward. The reward should be greatly amplified the farther away.

5. Slider explanation:

Exactly what do the sliders mean? What does Pysical and Mystical mean? How about stealth or head on? I know they change the types of missions generated (although I cant tell you what gives what without experimentation). But do they actually have an effect on what is actually in the mission? Does one setting guarentee monsters while the other you get people?


So don't you people think this will make mission terminal camping a lot less common and improve your ability to get find what your looking for quickly?

----------------------------------------

Semi-related to missions a personal gripe:

Pet pathing is Isanely bad. I know you know this. There is NO excuse for this. I am a programmer, and pathing isnt that hard. The quick and easy solution is to make pets go through walls & Objects in a beeline to thier targets. This wouldnt be unbalanced since the player still needs LOS to target a monster to tell the pet to kill. Doesnt it bother you that people dont use pets on higher level missions because of the pathing issue?

Id be happy to provide you with a good solution that would solve all indoor pet pathing problems if you are really listing. All you have to do is ask and ill write it up and post it (or email it). It would make so many people so happy.
