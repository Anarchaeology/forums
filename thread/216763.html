Joint Circle G & RUR Bid for NGI Contract
Statement released by Dabblez, director of Rubi-Ka Universal Robots (RUR)

I am thrilled to announce that today an agreement was reached between our company, RUR, and Circle G to present a joint bid to
the Newland City Council with regards to the Neutral Guards Initiative
(NGI).

The NGI is a movement aimed to oversee the replacement of the Omni-aligned Loren Warrs mercenaries in Newland City with neutral guards. This initiative has garnered support not just from neutral citizens but also from Clan and Omni. Indeed it is fair to say it was Administrator Kithrak who spearheaded this initiative in the first place.

For the last few months we at RUR have been lobbying the Newland City Council for them to consider the use of RUR developed robotics guards. And we were vastly encouraged when the IRRK news revealed that robotic guards were indeed looking at these proposals.

So why now did we feel it necessary to join forces with Circle G at this stage? There are two reasons:

Firstly, RUR is specialized in civilian applications of robotics. While
we feel with the GuardMaster series we have created a state-of-the-art robot with vastly superior sensory and threat-detection capabilities, we never have made warbots. We essentially felt we needed a partner who could provide the military technology required for this project, and as Circle G were already bidding for the NGI contract, they seemed a natural choice.

The other reason is a little more complex and I rather not go into too much detail about it. Let us just say that, as a recent IRRK report has revealed, one of the reasons the NGI has made so little progress over the past months is that the Newland City Council itself feels somewhat intimidated by the presence of the Warr mercenaries on its very doorstep. We believe Circle G may, with the assistance of other neutral factions, be able to remove this obstacle from an agreement on the NGI finally being reached. How exactly this might be achieved is a question you would be better off directing to Whealer from Circle G. I have nothing more to say on this aspect of the project.

Thank you very much for your attention. We hope to be able to invite you all to the demonstration of the new and improved GuardMaster robot very soon.

Dabblez
Director of RUR
