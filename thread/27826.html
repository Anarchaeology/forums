Any pistol shopping tips?
I've found a few things about pistols that help determine which pistols are right for me (I'm an Engineer, and these things can be important to me)

1) Often, a pistol with a high damage rating also has a slow attack speed, so I choose a pistol that does slightly less damage, but a higher attack speed.

2) While I do choose pistols with high attack speeds, I also make sure these pistols have recharge rates close to or lower than the attack rate. From what I've learned, Attack speed + Recharge Rate = Rate of Fire, or how often I can fire off that pistol. I think that a good rate of fire reflects how much damage a pistol can do in a given amount of time.

3) QL doesn't always reflect damage rating. I've seen two pistols with the same QL, but one does more damage at a better rate of fire than the other.

4) Being an Engineer, I try to choose the pistols that have fewer if any functions. I put most of my points into Pistol and Fling Shot, and I use implants to augment my dual wielding ability. I shop for inferior pistols for my left hand which use a different ammo type to ensure I don't run out of shots during a crucial moment. Even though my dual wield skill isn't that great, every little bit of damage I can do with both pistols always helps.

That's all I can think of for now. Post your shopping tips if you have 'em.
