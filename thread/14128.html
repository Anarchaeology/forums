News Ideas to toss around the table.
Man I just wanna start by saying AO Rocks...it's an awsome game. I have only been playing a couple weeks but from what I see it's great. I have a couple ideas that you might wanna put in to expand the game. For one, the things you can do in the game is kinda limited. Yea you can do missions over and over..but missions are many times very similar. If you can get there you can go attack the opposite side people. What I thought might be good is have like tournaments set at different times and in different places throughout the world. Like races (for pets, cars, runners). Or like a tournaments for fighting..of people with equal levels or such. Another idea, is have like side games...mabye make a card game, something you can collect and challenege other people. More games for the masses. Last thing mabye put a gambling house or town or something. Someplace where people can go play at gambling. Craps, poker, slots...really any gambling game you can think of mabye even name them something like "Bones for craps". Another thing that might make the game more, well, interesting. There are missions where you gain weapons that prevent resurections. Granted this is to kill NPC characters. but why not allow like limited use against other players. I know many people will hate this, spending time building up characters and then to lose them permanetly. You could go in a side area for this type. Makes the characters more important, plus there can now be revenge battles over the lose of high level characters in groups. There should also be more interaction between the sides Omni, Clan, and Neutral. Not players vs NPC but actually attacking apartment complexes, battling cities. The game can really take off, lots of ideas things to think about. Mabye even start allowing the sneak ability to make yourself invisible to other players to sneak into cities, and mabye to steal items from peoples invitoryes. Anywayz this messege is long enough. Just a couple ideas.
