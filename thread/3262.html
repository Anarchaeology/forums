Bug with consitently missing mobs.
I hope this is the right forum to be posting this.. didn't see another more appropriate..

I've noticed more and more that while in a mission, I all of a sudden start missing the mob I'm fighting. I've missed up to 10 times in a row, and in missions on average I hit a bit over half what I should. There is a work around, jumping up and down, and otherwise moving around but it doesn't always work very well.

If Funcom plays I'm sure they've noticed it, all the ppl I've asked know what I'm talking about, and they say to jump up and down. It happens in any zone, hunting or on missions, so there isn't much specific information I can give you. It never happens in pvp.

Just for kicks tho, lvl 92 atrox enforcer, ql 162 haxor, 577 melee init (modifying melee init with flurry of blows and rage sometimes seems to help)

I've considered that it could be that the mobs evade vs. my attack skill is causing me to miss, but it happens more against greys and greens than it does reds. It also seems to happen when the mob gets lower on health, several times I have reduced a mob to where there is no health visible on its bar, then I continually miss while it does an additional 500 points of damage before it dies. Very annoying.. annoying enough to make me stop in mid-mission and come post

I also assure you its not the graphical glitch that makes you swing twice and hit once - its nothing to do with that (in case you think I might just be a moron :P).

I hope this can be fixed this soon, it is a serious detriment to my game.
