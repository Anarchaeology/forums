Weekly gathering of souls. (Fair)
Greetings,

In Church of Ascension continued effort to help out the clans we have taken initative to create a weekly gathering at West Athen picnic table.

Each week a new clan (leader) will be selected as the host of the event and except for a few duties, it's completely up to the host(es) to decide how they want to do it.

Duties:
Advertise the event on Clan OOC 20-30 minutes before.
Make arrangement so there is buffers and Nano programmers, weapon makers, jeweler there to provide there services.

Suggested ideas a host can use:
Street theatre, speeches, contests, make it a carnival, I'm sure there is many more possibilities.

Gathering of Souls is held each Sunday, switching between 16:00 GMT and 16:00 EST from week to week. The location is always West Athen picnic table.

To apply for hosting this as a Clan Leader, use the Clan Leader forum created by Unitsi, I have a thread there with dates, times and who host, where people can apply.
