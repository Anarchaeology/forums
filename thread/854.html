Farewell
I just wanted to say goodbye to the people I have met in AO. Most of my friends have left and I am no longer holding the faith I once did for Funcom or AO. Funcom has the great story and idea, but does not have the funding or the drive to fix the REAL problems. What we want as a gaming community is a working product. I would wait for the story and the content, IF the game worked. I will wait for STARWARS, with Sony, Verant and Lucasarts helping it grow I am SURE it will strive. Thank you, Funcom for you attempt at an amazing world. Maybe next time.


Farewell to the friends I made on Rubi-Ka.
