Easiest way to fly.

Dump your character and get an MP. Level 70-75 get Quantum Wings and fly all you want. Not as fast as most yalms, some disadvantages and some advantages but way high on the cool factor and way low on cost (nano is like 250K). Then you can save up the money for a yalm at leisure. Level 97 now and just starting to wonder when I might decide to Yalm it as an additional ability for missions where the outside is camped by tons of deep reds.

Aside from that Quantum Wings is the bomb.

One note, I too think it is great that Yalms have stopped dropping in missions. It used to bug me (still does) when a level 20-30 character was flying around while I was striving to get ahead. With my engie and then my MP I dreamed of a Yalm but to have a guild buy it for me would just not cut it.

Glad you got yours, you worked for it, enjoy it, and explore. And if you pass a MP flying along stop and say hi, it might be me.
