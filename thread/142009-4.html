Omni-Internop's Headquarters
---------------------------------------

Dr. Dominiq "Divvinity" Lyrhea is handed several pieces of paper; standard documentation that she looks at frequently. Another boring case...

Turning on her computer she enters the unique case number found at the top of the document and presses her thumb against the biometric device. Several beeps later and the case information is projected up on the screen before her.

"Wow, less than a year after the fact... that's pretty quick around here" she jokingly remarks to herself as she looks at the date and begins to examine the information more closely.

She quickly scans her eyes through the data on her screen. "Two melted bodies, new weapon, blah blah blah suddenly her eyes stop at the red highlight. A name she has heard many times and seen on far more documents then she cares to remember.

A quick press of some keys and she is staring at a archaic mainframe screen. "You would think with the budget Omni-Pol has that they could actually afford better then this" she thinks, sighing in unison with her thought.

After entering the case information, the screen glows as line after line of investigative details are produced for her perusal.

She examines each line carefully for any telltale signs of misconduct.

Switching screens again she examines public information resources, administrative records, and the Internop's confidential records stored for every Omni-Tek employee.

She cannot find receipts of the DNA chain-of-evidence in Omni-Pol records, she cannot find expert accounts of the crime scene, nor can she find anything grossly incriminating that would suggest a traitor in Omni-Tek midst.

A shame, considering she has a quota to meet.

Thoughts rushed through her as she prepared. Thoughts of Newland, Warr, thoughts of Omni-propaganda and official statements...

"Divvinity" carefully and methodically began typing her non-official and non-public assessment of the events that transpired and the people involved.

A story in and of itself, "off-record" documentation is required in all OT internal investigations. Omni-Internop's, having been on the receiving end of one too many OT-centric subpoena's from the ICC, was reluctant to officially declare anything; "including the corporate cafeteria menu" Divvinity thought to herself as she continued typing the justification for and implications of... further Internop actions.

...While one cannot discount the unprofessional actions involved and the total disregard for procedure, I am unsure whether this was deliberate attempt to manipulate conclusions of Omni-Tek law enforcement or just simple ignorance on behalf of our employees.

The lack of an evidentiary chain and the amount of time before Omni-Pol forensic units were called in, is in a word, frightening. In my humble opinion, it discounts the strength of this case substantially.

I have no plausible explanation for why an official administrator and president of an administrative OT division was investigating a crime, nor is it sanctioned by Omni-Tek policy, nor do I personally find it appropriate.

I therefore recommend in light of recent events, such as the Newland Guards Initiative, that we place President Kithrak under surveillance and investigation immediately.

Pressing the "send" key, Dominiq closed her eyes and tried to focus her thoughts. The technique, one taught to her by an OT martial artist, quickly became a standard relaxation ritual she performed before beginning an assignment.

A watcher had witnessed a sensitive meeting between a Omni citizen and a clan leader for a large organization. Nothing unusual by any means, but the watcher in question had a penchant for getting drunk and saying too much.

Dominiq had traveled to West Athens and killed several watchers in the last week.

Unfortunately, by luck or circumstance, none were the correct target.

Damn it he better be there tonight. Dominiq thought to herself after all I have a quota to meet.
