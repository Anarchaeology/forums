Bump!
I am a person who suffers severe wrist problems from, well, mainly too much gaming and too much computing in general. Every click I am saved is less damage to my wrists. I understand that buying or incorporating Clicksaver may not be feasible, but please do something similar. I've already had to quit two other games I cared about because of forced repetitive action (UO anyone?).

I use Clicksaver. It doesn't allow you to do anything you cannot do without it, but it saves a lot of hassle, and for people like myself, repetitive stress injury. I don't even use the "Shop" feature at all. My only concern with Clicksaver is how it gets the information about the missions and items and I worry if that's alright with Funcom. If FC ever says not to use Clicksaver, I would stop, but I would probably have to abandon missioning and just hunt outdoors.

Almost everyone who understands what it does and that it cannot be used to exploit has no problem with Clicksaver. Please consider including a new mission interface with similar features in Shadowlands or the booster. I'd buy them just for that, so I could stop running AO in window mode and not have to mess with a 3rd party program anymore. A plus for FC would be the ability to time how often mission rolls could be made.

For some reason, opening and closing and scrolling several windows in a row is just particularly painful for me, perhaps because of repetitive motions involved. I am not an isolated case. Many gamers suffer from RSIs.
