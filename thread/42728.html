Read me, Funcom...please? (Apartments)
Ok, a while ago I posted my thoughts on Apartments...I think it needs its own thread.

Comments? Suggestions?

Here's what I want for my apartment. Tell me if you agree with me:

NOTE: The level stuff is just an estimate of what kind of money you might have then.

Okay. When I load my little noobie adventurer into the game for the first time, I'll go get an apartment-for free. When I get inside, it will have a fixed layout that looks somewhat like this:

-1 room with a bed, desk, sofa, and television (Extra space too)
-1 smaller room (like the one we have on the side now) with a wardrobe in it (which we can use to store clothes)
-1 smaller room (same size as above) with a toilet, sink, and bathtub inside.
- A WINDOW. Now, I understand it would be hard to have apartments overlooking the arena, so how about this: You know those fake cities you see in the Highrises? Make a little 'set' that looks somewhat like Trade or Ent. Now stuff it chock full of NPCs. Make them wearing playerish armor and gather around the Bronto Burger sometimes, and you've got a convincing mockup of Omni-Entertainment- with probably half the lag it would take to truly do it.

Okay. On with the sim. Anyway, now that I've got my lovely apartment (with a 20 item limit now) I can go to furniture stores and go shopping for more decorative items- like lava lamps, and maybe some figures of people like Ross or Radiman, (or a leet) statues, modern art..ect. ect. ect. Note: I'd kind of like to buy aestetic stuff like magazines, to put on a coffee table. Oh- and a dinner table.

Fast forward about 20-30 levels. Advnoob's got more money now, and she wants to purchase a better apartment. Her better-quality apartment adds these:

-Better wallpaper
-Carpeting
-A kitchen room (with more static furniture)
-Maybe slight enlargement of all rooms

Maybe she can buy better decorative stuff now...like, a computer.

Wait. Whoa whoa whoa. Just got an idea for that computer. When you used the computer, maybe you could view the AO site! You could even view and post on forums- INGAME! Or, maybe they could have the webmaster do some quick webpages for the Omni- divisions, and perhaps Bronto Burger Aaaaweessoomme.

Okay. Moving on here.

Advnoob is now about level 90-100. She wants a medium-top quality apartment. So she goes to some terminal in the highrises and bumps it again. Here's what she gets:

-A proper ceiling, maybe with some nice hanging lights
-Some more enlargement of all rooms
-Maybe some new static furniture that looked more classy
-A larger window
-Item cap raised to 30

...and, of course, some cooler furniture. How about a fireplace? Maybe a larger bed? How about some more colors of lava lamps? I want some exotic plants. I want to see some more paintings and art. Perhaps a washer and dryer? How about coffee tables, tabletop fountains, lamps- heck, if you want a cryo chamber, you should be able to GET a cryo chamber, just because they're cool. C'mon Funcom, I'm pulling this out of my butt here in like 30 seconds, it's not hard.

Okay. Advnoob is pretty high-level now. She wants a high-quality apartment. She pays a lot of credits, and gets:

-A balcony to replace the window
-Enlargement of all rooms
-A new room, with a DJ booth, sofas/tables and chairs outlining the walls, and an empty space in the middle for dancing.
-A new smaller room, with: Grid terminal, basic shop terminal, bank terminal.

Now. This is a pretty high-quality apartment, and it brings lots of stuff you can buy. This would include rotating disco balls, maybe a giant lava lamp, other cool party stuff. Maybe now we could even buy a bar, with a hired bartender.

And now...THE MISCELLANIOUS SECTION.

Someone suggested you should be able to hire strip-dancers for your apartment from an NPC. Sure, why not add it? It's not like they can be nekkid anyway.

Now we come to the subject of pets. Yes, we SHOULD be able to have the following as pets:
1. A scaled down version of the 2-headed wolf, with 1 head taken off as a dog.
2. The sabertooth, scaled down, with some modifications as a cat.
3. Leets.
4. Reets.
5. A very, very scaled down pit lizard as a 'baby dragon'.
6. Pirahnas (they look like fish)
In the furniture section we could buy pet houses, reet cages, leashes (to take them around Rubi-Ka), tanks (for the fish), food/water dishes, ect. We should, of course be able to name them. And if you die, they run off, so you end up asking around if anyone's seen them :P

/me bows. Thank you, thank you. You love me, you really do.
