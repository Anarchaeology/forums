When is AC damage absorption going to be fixed?
As you increase in levels, mob AC increases.
Damage absorption is a set integer that is based on AC alone.

For argument sake, I'll use a very simplistic calculation. I will not figure in crits, +dmg rings/nanos and attack rating damage bonus. They do help the faster damage weapons slightly more than slower weapons but not enough to overcome the innate flaws with AOs AC damage absorption equation.



Player A is wielding a weapon that does 1-100 damage every 3 seconds.
Player B is wielding a weapon that does 1-165 damage every 5 seconds.
Player C is wielding a weapon that does 1-300 damage every 10 seconds.


Against a mob with zero AC, these weapons deal virtually identical damage over time. A maximum 2000 damage per minute(DPM).



Against a mob with 500AC (50 points damage absorption)

Player A max DPM drops to 1000
Player B max DPM drops to 1380
Player C max DPM drops to 1500


Against a mob with 750AC (75 points damage absorption)

Player A max DPM drops to 500
Player B max DPM drops to 1080
Player C max DPM drops to 1350


Against a mob with 1000AC (100 points damage absorption)

Player A max DPM drops to 20 (minimum damage every hit)
Player B max DPM drops to 780
Player C max DPM drops to 1200


The player with the slow, high-damage is still effective, while the player with the quick, low-damage weapon is useless. This becomes painfully obvious post level 100.


This is why 95% of enforcers are using beams and hammer. No other weapons are as effective at higher levels.
This is why nobody uses pistols.
This is why Vektor Shotguns are the weapon of choice for every ranged profession.



If AC damage absorption was changed to a percentage of damage dealt (ie: 1000AC absorbs 10% of damage inflicted), every weapon would maintain their relative effectiveness regardless of level.

Bionitrous
Supa Leet
One very minor point your calculations neglected is the effect of criticals.

Player A has 20 opportunities to land a critical
Player B has 12 opportunities to land a critical
Player C has 6 opportunities to land a critical

I know this doesn't make it any better in the real world. But, it's a place where FunCom can start to equalize things:

Fast Weapon 20 shots/minute 30-60(250)
Slow Weapon 6 Shots/minute 5-200(30)

This kind of change can _help_ to equalize things while still maintaining the differentiating _feel_ of the big/slow vs. small/fast weapons.

Likewise, the recharge rate for special attacks is based on the speed of the weapon. Player A above has _many_ more opportunities to pull off a special attack than Player C. Perhaps he can pull off a FlingShot (or Sneak Attack) at the same rate as each of player C's every other regular attack.

Edit: And a final point with respect to the different damage dealing modes regards misses. Assuming a player misses 15% of his attacks. player C could very well be in trouble with a minimum possible damage of 5. Player A stands a little more comfortable with a minimum damage of 17. He has the variance factor in his favor.

It boils down to the same principle that gets people all hot and bothered when an Agent hits for 10K once a month and he can't even be happy about it because he's afraid everyone will start crying 'nerf'. They don't pay much mind to his ten thousand previous shots that landed for 100. The trade-off is supposed to be slow, steady, reliable damage (1-hand, pistols, MA) with options for other damage methods (specials, MA moves, Pets, NF's) vs. wildly random often spectacular damage (Beams, Hammers, Aimed Shot) that are usually the only means of doing damage.

Not refuting your argument or the fact that a problem exists. We are all well aware of the real current in-game advantage to slow, hard hitting weapons. It's just not so cut and dry. I'd also not like FunCom to simply have everybody have the same damage over time without maintaining some sort of _feel_ factor to each of the damage dealing methods.
