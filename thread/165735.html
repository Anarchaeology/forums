Memorial Service for our Leader and Friend Ladimir
Thank You all!

Thanks to all for coming to the Memorial, It was a beautiful event.

Thanks to all who posted Kind, comforting words on this forum.

Special Thanks to all the Arks and Funcom employees who came and did a really nice salute with firing squad.

I will be posting screenshots soon as I get some webspace, if you where there and want to send me your screenshots send me a PM.

We miss you Ladimir!


We at TravelingOMNILoveMachine are sad to announce that our Org leader and friend Michael "Ladimir" Kozitza passed away tragically due to a motorcycle accident last Thursday.

We are having an in-game memorial on Sunday August 17th at 5pm GMT (9am PST 10am MT 11am CT and Noon EST respectively) at the Bronto Burger outside Borealis at the approach (this was one of TOLMs meeting spots when the game was 1st released).

Anyone is welcome to pay respects to Mike, a fellow "AOer" who loved the game more than anyone I know. His main character was "Ladimir" an NT who recently dinged 200 and he was very proud of, but Mike had many characters. Mike actually had 2 accounts and was a hardcore player who loved the game, community, and his org.

Mike touched us all in allot of ways whether it be advice on PvP or advice for Real Life. He was a great friend and he will be missed.

Mike leaves behind his wife and 3 kids. Help me pray that God will give them the strength to bear such a tragic loss.

Thanks.
