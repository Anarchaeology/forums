Don't make a rant because you picked a weapon and it's not the best weapon for whatever you want to do.

Why did you pick a Krutt shotgun, it is a crit or nothing gun.

Shotguns, in real life, do a horrible job at damaging armored targets. People use shotguns against human targets for two reasons in real life. One, the spread allows for a better chance at hitting a target, and two, the knockdown power is unrivaled by any other conventional firearm.

That being said, the entire weapon database does need a revamp, it has for awhile.
