Corporate Recruitment ------{-@
Greetings citizens of Rubi-Ka, especially Omni-Tek citizens. ((**Atlantean org**))

Future Technologies Inc. has recently been asked to begin production on Rubi-Ka. We were founded, with the funding of Omni-Tek, to cheaply mass produce most products Omni-Tek provides to the universe. From a simple calculator to the complex parts needed to power the grid, we have at least inspected the parts, if not produced them.

With the upcoming changes reported to be coming to Rubi-Ka, Omni-Tek has ordered us to begin producing as fast as possible to provide the community with adequate resources. In this speed to produce we need capable employees of Omni-Tek to come and work for a corporation founded on providing the community with the highest quality goods for the very cheapest price.

Positions Open In:

Corporate Board
Public Relations
Marketing
Security
Others (to be disclosed upon inquiry)

Our ticker symbol ((official name)) is Future Tech Inc.

I know this post might seem a little pointless so let me explain the premise behind the org. We are a purely CRIMINAL org and PURELY RPing. The org is a corporation claiming to be mass producing cheap goods for OT. Actually they produce faulty, poor quality goods that usually break very quickly and they make a lot of money doing it. Beside this they also have a very criminal underground element that extorts, kidnaps, and smuggles among other things. Now you might be asking yourself how this is any different than any other OT criminal org?

We appear to operate lawfully. There are normal employees that apologize and "brown nose" high level officials to just ignore their activities. While the good side of the corporation tries to cover up things the deep underground criminal element goes raging through OT.

We are not into tower attacks on other OT orgs or anything terrorist like that, unless it is out of itimidation. Also you will notice (maybe by now) the org might only consist of myself and Ikio. Hey you have to start somewhere.

Ikio and I are die hard RPers and we think this could be a really great idea. We realize too that we are not going to get a lot of high level players but any alts or anything you have we would love for them to join. Ikio will be posting soon with a more criminal looking post too.
