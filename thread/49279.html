Hence the renaming of the dimensions, from #1 and #2, to something new...

The current naming scheme implies a primary and backup server to the average user, which is probably not what Funcom wants people to think. They'd prefer to spread out the user load across both systems mitigate some scale-related lag issues.

By switching the dimension names to a more benign nomenclature, they remove any potential bias. It'll be similar to how the shards were named in UO.
