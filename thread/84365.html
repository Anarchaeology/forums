well...
It seems to me that there are a couple of issues that need to be addressed.

First of all, the contract that brought each of us to Rubi-Ka was an Omni-Tek contract. At some point in time, you, or someone in your family, was brought here at company expense and under contract to work--and reneged on that contract. In this respect, it must be said, Clan and Neutral are exactly the same.

Second, I question the notion that true neutrality can ever exist, except in the rarest of instances. If the neutral party represents a large force, and has the capacity to shape a conflict in some meaningful way by intervening, then that party is, de facto, taking a stand by not becoming involved. This is not neutrality. It may be many other things (pragmatism, cowardice, sense of fair play, cruelty), but it is not neutrality. In the specific case of the neutrals on Rubi-Ka, I have seen little to indicate that the larger number of neutrals truly remain free of the conflict. They have even begun to mine notum in recent days, and to fight for the land claims that generate that Notum. This makes the so-called "Neutral" as much a faction as any clan. Neutrals have attacked both Omni-Tek and Clan holdings. These are not the actions of a neutral party; they are the actions of an aggressive third party in a global conflict.

As my Devil's Advocate association might indicate, I am sympathetic to the issues that caused the Clans to break away from the Corporation, and I am sympathetic to the continued inequality and injustice that feeds the growing conflict on our mutually shared planet. Please don't play the innocent victim of a situation not of your making, though. It turns my stomach. Like it or not, whether you have committed crimes agaist Clanners or Omni-Tek employees, forces associated with your cause have. Just as I must unfortunately accept some responsibility for the attrocities of Omni-Pol (and try to work to change that corrupt organization), you must be prepared to accept some responsibility for your neutral compatriots when they draw you ever deeper into a war you don't want.

-Blackpetal
