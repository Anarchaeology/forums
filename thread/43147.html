The Fake SRS
( This is an adventure recently run by the South Rubi-Ka Society. Many thanks to Ghump for playing the role of Johndolk, it made all the difference. )

The Society receives the following message from one Ken Kuiper.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dear Fake South Rubi-Ka Society,

We is da real South Rubi-Ka Society. We is older den ya, so give us back out name our we sue yur pants off.

Respectfully

Ken Kuiper
Da Real South Rubi-Ka Society
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Not overly concerned by the implied threat, the Society none-the-less decides to contact Mr Kuiper to get a better understanding of his grievances and maybe find an amicable settlement. Poisonivy, Heksa, Savoy, Skybert and of course the Society's lawyer, Specchio, head off for Jacobstown, one of the many small shanty towns in Omni Forest, where they find Kuiper and another atrox, Johndolk.

The meeting with Kuiper and Johndolk proves to be more difficult than anticipated. The two atrox are not particularly bright and often misunderstand things. Moreover Specchio, still suffering from a mysterious illness, snaps and gives into an equally sudden and unprovoked fit of rage and paranoia against everyone and everything, including his life long friend Skybert. Specchio storms off in his Yalm shouting threats and insults to the stunned Society members.

Heksa and Poisonivy resume negotiating with the two atrox. What emerges is that Kuiper and Johndolk had indeed created a club called South Rubi-Ka Society (an atrox poetry society) some five "baths" (years) ago and hence have something of a moral, if not technically legal, claim to the name. Kuiper and Johndolk only found out recently of the "other" SRS from a Roman gentleman called Pecry Allsworth Saar who among other things offered his help should the atrox require legal council to recover their clubs stolen name. Johndolk shows Heksa Allsworth's calling card which reveal him to be a member of the exclusive Ancient House of Eris, an organisation much like the South Rubi-Ka Society only older, wealthier and restricted to opiflex and nano-mages.

The Society offer to buy their name back, but the atrox are unmovable. Allsworth has promised to pay them double anything the SRS offers. As negotiations continue, what becomes clear is the what Johndolk and Kuiper really want is for the world to hear their poem. He and Johndolk have been working on one epic poem for these last five years, a poem called "I Cut Me Finger on da Cheesegrater" which he claims to be so moving, majestic and true to life the whole world deserves to hear it. Kuiper then stands up and recites it.

Savoy finds herself thinking that, though words "moving" and "majestic" would not be her first choice to describe this poem, she can think of one or two people who really "deserve" to hear it. More to the point however, she remembers that an important meeting of Rubi-Kan writers is due soon in Borealis which will involve public readings. Even though a public reading of "I Cut Me Finger of Da Cheesegrater" in front of such a sophisticated audience will probably make both South-Rubi-Ka Societies the laughing stock of the planet for a while, it could be a way for Johndolk and Kuiper to live out their dream and for the Society to acquire a clear claim to its name.

The poet-atroxes welcome this proposal. Heksa and Poisonivy sweeten the deal further by helping the Johndolk and Kuiper come up with a new, "better" name. They settle on name South Rubi-Ka Cella Dweller Atrox Poet Society. The deal struck the Society retire to Rompas to discuss the mystery surrounding Specchio's condition, but that is a tale for another day.
