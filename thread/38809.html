Expedition to the Yuttos Village
This is a short write up of an adventure recently run by the South Rubi-Ka Society. For the purpose of the adventure, you need to assume that the presence of the Yuttos village in the Wailing Wastes is not a commonly know fact. From the average Omni player's point of view that is probably not too far from the truth. Please note the "dangers" described below are as seen from the point of view of the main character, Lorna, who is only level 12.

There are some associated images at
http://rubika.no-ip.org/srs/yuttos.htm

==========================================

The Society receives a request for funding from one Lorna Corbin, a xeno-anthropology research student working on her PhD. For the last two years she has been out in the wilderness studying the Yuttos, a peaceful, shy and strangely musical Rubi-Kan people of which little is known. Specifically she trying to find for their main settlement, which she now believes is somewhere in the Wailing Wastes. The dangerous wildlife there has however prevented her from exploring this region successfully thus far and now her funding is running out. Would the Society consider sponsoring her research?

After due consideration the Society agrees to support this research project. To do so they plan an expedition into the Wailing Wastes with meticulous detail. A group of members and friends of the Society consisting of Sightsee, Resetgun, Belisamma, Graverose and Junior are invited to join Lorna on this
journey and ensure she finds this elusive village once and for all.

The expedition sets off on foot from Reets Retreat at night to avoid the heat. They head North West, fighting the elements and many hungry predators. Once deep in the Wailing Wastes, the presence of Ottous droppings and tracks seems to confirm that they getting closer to their destination, the Outtus being large, cattle-like creatures the Yuttos have domesticated. It is Resetgun
who first spots the low stone walls of the village.

The expedition approaches the Yuttos settlement. The Yuttos seem agitated and keep asking to be left alone. The job of gaining the Yuttos confidence takes a lot of time and patience. Using all of her xeno-athropology training, Lorna begins to establish better and better communications with Yuttos and slowly learns something about their current predicament. The Yuttos, the village elder explains, do not normally have any natural enemies. However one very large wolf called Darkness has apparently developed a taste for Yuttos flesh. Night after night, Darkness stalks the camp claiming more and more lives. The Yuttos are totally unable to stop him.

The party decides to stay on to protect the village from Darkness. On the first night, Darkness manages to sneak in and savage a Yuttos before he can be stopped. But on the second night they manage to corner and finally kill the him.

With Darkness dead, all the Yuttos join in their customary song to
celebrate the event. The next day the expedition heads back home, leaving Lorna behind to live with the Yuttos and complete her research.
