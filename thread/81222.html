Better starter equipment
Hi.

I have been fooling around with some alts of mine.

What strikes me is that after the starter weapon, which is only useful to level 3 there is nothing available for new chars.

Going to any Fair Trade or basic shops to look for armor/weapon is not an option. Most of the times you wont find anything you can use until you are lvl 10 or above.

Why not make an extension to the starter weapons and also some more low ql armor?

Place 2 terminals in the backyards (Not training areas since there is still problems with zoning out of them).

1 is for 3 different ql of the starter weapons. QL3, 7 and 10.
All kind of starter weapons for all type of professions.

the other terminal is for starter armor. same QL 3, 7 and 10.
The armor dont have to be the exact same type, but atleast make them avail as full sets using the same 2 abilities

Also makes them cheap. Money is tough to get when you are young, unless you got a higher char to pass some to you. which all of us dont have.

I play on both servers (Rk1 and RK2) Dont bother much to play on the German server.

Doing this will also (I think) clear up some lag around Fair trade and Basic shops
