20: "Hall of mirrors" effect in missions. Seems to be caused by a slight gap in the autogenerated mission 3d model. Always happens at doors and manifests itself as a perpetually open door with multiple imaging. If the door closes, the missors are fixed. Perhaps the doors have no floor ?

This has to do with the way they render missions. While the whole mission is generated when you walk into it, the rooms graphics are not kept in memory, except for the ones immediatly around you. When you get the hall of mirrors effect, it means a) you ran from point A to B too fast and the client couldn't keep up with you. or b) lag/desync. easy fix (assuming you're not running for the door from aggro mobs), just go back 3-4 rooms, let the doors close, and walk out again.

38: Unable to change melee/range target in combat. If you get attacked by a second more dangerous mob, you cant change targets and go after it. VERY BAD.

Hit esc to clear target, turn off attack. target new mob, attack. rather simple, even if a timing nightmare.

bugs
1.Nightvision "works" on my voodoo 5. The problem is that the nightvision NEVER helps, even in the darkest of places. It simply gives everything a green tint, and then you have to put up with the flickering of the night vision. I'm suer this was all by design (you get what you pay for), but they server no practical use right now.

2. low light scopes and range finders DO NO ADD RANGE. I have tested this serveral times with other agents who are using no range adding devices and we both shoot the same distance.

3. The "snare" spells make absolutely no difference in the targets run speed (I can't speak for all classes run debuffs, but I was using the agents "delay of the ineviatable"). I have tried on NPC and PvP alike. I even tried it on myself and was brought down from about 160 run to -70. Even at -70 run speed I was still running at the same pace (Its possible there was a decrease in speed, but if there was it was extremely hard to tell). At -70 you would expect someone to slow to a crawl similar to what happens when you are overburdened.

4. Low light scopes make it extremely difficult to see things in the day time, or in well lighted missions. An option to disable the night vision o the low light scope would ix this (possibly bind a key to the night vision to turn it on and off).

5. The agents holding nanos don't work on NPC's or even in PvP. It's odd, but the holding nanos DO work in missions the last time I checked.

6. Holding nanos have a range of 20m-25m. Now, given that melee attacks have a range of 10m-20m, this makes holding nanos incredibly impractical. You start to cast the nano at 20m, by the time you are done casting it they will have stopped within 5m or 10m if you are lucky and will start to wail away on you with melee attacks. Increasing the range of the nanos would probably upset the original balance, so you will have to nerf the melee ranges to 2m or so like it is suppose to be.

7. The latest sneaking nerf needs to be tweaked. I can understand how you would want opeing a door or chest to drop your concealment, but we should be able to reload and use items in your inventory without blowing our cover...
I personally would abolish the whole idea of losing your sneak for several reasons:
a. It really changes nothing, it simply makes life as a sneaker annoying. I say this because I can still unlock a door, or open a chest with an enemy in the room, if I hop back into conceal quickly.
b. It's not very realistic when you can fire while concealed, yet you can't manually reload or re-arrange your inventory without losing concealment.
c. The funnest part of the missions for me was looting and disarming stuff while undetected... but the recent nerf has made it such a hassle to loot chests and stuff that I usually just go strait for the objective, which that takes all the fun out of it.

8. Rifles will need some love soon... Your standard rifle has less range than a SMG or pistol. Now I'm no gun expert, but I think a sniper rifle should be able to outrange a submachine gun or pistol. The broken range adding devices don't help the problem either.
Another problem: Freedom arms and maussers are very common, but stigma rifles over QL 10 are about as rare as sunglasses.

9. I can't speak for what the higher level agent DoT's do (level 70+) , but If they follow the trend of the first 3 then I can make an accurate judgement on the fact that they are useless. The problem with the agent DoT's are quite simply they don't do enough damage to warrant the time it takes to cast them. In the time it takes me to cast the nano I could have fired off about 2 shots for 50 damage each. Now, this DoT nano will only do 2-5 damage per tick... The battle would have to last a pretty long time for the DoT to do even 50 damage. So you can see why these need to be looked at, they have no place in this game at their current state.
