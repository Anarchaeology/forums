Sooooo what exactly do the skill dependancies mean?
If a skill is 60% dependant on Agility 40% dependant on Sense, what exactly does that mean?

Open up your Skills/Nano and Aiding menu. Then give yourself an Intelligence Boost. Numbers should go up on most all of the skills there.

From observation, the percentages list how much each attribute contributes to a skill. There's something else going on; using your example if Basketweaving is 60% Agility and 40% Sense, and you have 100 in both, your base Basketweaving is not going to be 100. But I have no idea what it'd be.

attriutes give an effective bonus to all skills based on the dependencies for any skill, equal to 0.25 * the skill dependency formula.

ex: an int of 100 therefore gives a bonus of +25 to all skills 100% dependent on int.
