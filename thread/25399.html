even the monthly rate is only 12.95

what you are probably seeing is the end of your 7-day free trial. What you probably didnt realize is that at the end of that free trial, you actually agreed to purchase the game for $19. But the good news is that it includes the 3 weeks left of your free month included with purchase (you used the first week in your 7-day trial already apparently) just like if you bought it in the store. So basically it will cost you $19 for the first month (to purchase game) and then 12.95 or whatever the appropriate amount is for the payment option you chose.
