Funcom: Suggestion for DD NFs. Plz Read.
I Posted this on the nano-technician thread but wanted comments from other classes as well; therefore, I am posting here also.

I ran a test last night using my 118 level NT:

Test Case #1
1. Joined a team for level 190 mission in BS.
2. I set aggro/def to 100%.
3. Entered the mission.
4. First mob, waited until others had initiated combat.
5. Cast Gravitational Anomaly.
6. Was aggroed once it landed.

Comment: It was to be expected as I did the most damage at that point and had aggro at 100%.


Test Case #2
1. Same mission as above.
2. Set aggro/def to 40%.
3. Next mob, waited until others had initiated combat.
4. Cast Gravitational Anomaly.
5. Was actually aggroed before the nuke ever landed.

Comment: Ok, now this is strange. I was aggroed before it even landed (lag maybe?). Also, with a 40% aggro/def you would think I wouldnt get aggroed as quickly.


Test Case #3
1. Same mission as above.
2. Set aggro/def to 0%. (Thats right, full defense)
3. Next mob, waited until others had initiated combat.
4. Cast Gravitational Anomaly.
5. Was actually aggroed before the nuke ever landed again.

Comment: By this time I really wondered why to set aggro/def to anything but 100%. I was aggroed anyway and still took crit hits.


Test Case #4
1. Same mission as above.
2. Set aggro/def to 0%. (Thats right, full defense)
3. Next mob, waited until others had initiated combat.
4. Cast Annihilating Hadron String.
5. Was aggroed when the nuke landed.

Comment: I wanted to see the aggro rate on Hadron so I used it next. The wierd part is that it only landed for around 2k damage yet I was still aggroed. I say this is strange because by the time Hadron landed, others had done over 2k damage to the mob. I wasnt the greatest damage dealer at that point yet I still got the aggro even at 0% aggro/def.


Results
I am not going to bother with aggro/def at anything but 100% any longer unless something is changed. It makes zero difference in the amount of aggro you receive. Now, it may reduce critical hits, but I didnt witness any great reduction. I was still hit for 1200 pts. on a semi-regular basis (and no, my evades dont suck.)

On a positive note, its nice that I can finally outdamage everyone in my team. (Only took me 118 levels, QL175 implants, and moochams MC gift.)

Suggested Fix
1. FunCom, please reduce the amount of aggro an NT DD does. I don't think it is that much of a balance issue since NT's were designed to have low hps, ac, and evades. We don't have range, so please reduce aggro from DDs. Let the tanks tank and let us NTs be artillery.

Thanks,
LostLogic

P.S. Kids, don't try this at home with Localized Dimensional Inversion, you get aggroed regardless of settings or how much damage the team has done.

Yep, I basically going to reinterate what Dave said.. setting your aggressive/defensive slider has absolutly NOTHING to do with determining if you are aggroed or not. It determines YOUR aggresiveness when using a weapon. By setting it full Aggressive you will get a bonus to your Init speed and you attack much faster, but you basically lose most (if not all) of your evades. By setting it full defensive then you get a bonus to your evades but obviously you attack and run nanos much slower.

Basically, when you are starting off there is really no reason to keep it anywhere but full aggressive. As you start raising your Init skill (whether it be Nano Init, Ranged Init, or Melee Init) then you can start backing your slider up as you won't be getting much of a bonus anymore. I've found that once your Init is up to around 250-300 then you can move the slider back about 15% and your attack won't be any slower.. the higher you raise your Init then the farther toward Defensive you can slide it back. Until eventually, if you get Init high enough, you can move the slider into full Defensive and your attack speed will still be as fast is possible and you are getting full advantage of your evades (assuming you are putting points there).

I've got no real proof of any of this, but this is just how it seems to be working for me. So basically I don't bother putting any points into my evades until I get into the higher levels since running full Aggressive is really the only way to go until you get your Init up there.
