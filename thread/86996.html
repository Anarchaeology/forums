Shopping sucks
The organization of the stores really sucks. Is it really that difficult to get them set up in a more logical way? For Nanos I would think having them by level, and/or funtion would be much better. For implant components I would think at the very least sorting by the type of implant they fit into. The way it is now seems ridiculus.

One aspect of shopping I absolutely hate besides shopping for clusters, is shopping for armor. Especially as a low level. Ever try to get even close to a full set of low level (< 50) sense/agi armor such as steel-ribbed from shops? Oh yeah... we have ql 31 boots and ql 49 sleeves in Tir, sorry, can't buy ql 30-50 helmets or body armor today in any clan/neutral shops because, uh, the shipment didn't arrive on Rubi-Ka today, but if you go to Avalon you might find ql 40 gloves.

My point is, I could be walking around with 10 million credits and *unable* to buy a full suit of armor by visiting just one or 2 shops, and I'd be lucky as hell to get 4-5 pieces in a +/- 7 level range by going to every shop I know of in non-hostile territory, which could easily take 1-2 hours of my time (by which time I could have leveled my alt 5 times).

It is apparent that shops are meant to discourage being able to easily acquire exactly what one needs, given that it's 'more rewarding' to get what you need the hard way: adventuring, missioning, blitzing, or trading with other players. But there are many times when ****ing around for hours to get something that is pretty basic and that everyone needs is disgustingly troublesome (read: makes my 'cancel' finger mighty itchy).

My suggestion is to make the non-exceptional low ql armors availble in sets from low level shops. Make them ridiculously expensive if you think you have to, but at least have the option in the game for those who can afford it.

P.S. One thing that cracks me up is that FC undoubtedly wants to put money-sinks in the game. Things that'll suck high-level players dry of millions in the fewest clicks possible. Guess what? Twinking alts is an expensive propostion. What I mentioned above would be a key way to get high-level players interested in spending money. Imagine... ql 30 full set of Titan.. 800k from the shops.. instead of having to wait 3 hours in Tir waiting til someone posts it. Or full set of ql 50 Kirch Kevlar for 1.5 mill.. etc. Of course, putting features like this in the game would require having a clue.. something that apparently is not part of FC's mission statement. Correct me if I'm wrong.
