So, WTF is up with bows?
Hello friends. Just a brief note about something I'm certain must be a glaring mistake.

Tonight my girlfriend and I started some new characters. She made an enforcer and I made a doc. I wanted my doc to look cool, and I wasn't/am not really worried about him using a weapon at higher level. I'm teaming with an enforcer, after all. So I thought about it and realized I have never, ever seen a character using a bow.

Well, then, says me, I'll use a bow. So right off the bat I get offered a mission that has a cruddy bow as a reward. We go and do the mission, I get my bow, hurrah! Now time for some arrows.

Actually, now time for STICKER SHOCK. Arrows cost damn near as much as GRENADES. They cost six times as much as SHOTGUN SHELLS. 449 credits for 20 (twenty, TWENTY) arrows. I used up four quivers of arrows (for those keeping score, that's almost two THOUSAND credits' worth of arrows) in a single mission, at level THREE.

Surely this can't possibly be right. Who can afford to use a bow that shoots once a second when every single shot costs 20+ credits? I realize I am low level doc, but I have a 25 adventurer and HE would go broke, and quickly, if he used a bow. It's just ludicrous how expensive they are. I can understand, sort of, grenades being expensive. They're big and loaded with C-4 and check Duck-Exp for their damage. But arrows? The technology to make arrows dates back to CAVEMEN. So, as the header says, WTF?

I also had a great deal of trouble reloading my bow. The bow I was using only held three rounds. When I would try to right-click and drop my arrows on it when out of ammo, even between battles, the ammo would stay at zero. At the beginning of the next fight, my poor doc would dry-fire his bow about 65 times, "reloading". What's up with this?

It would be nice if arrow cost would be brought back in line with reality in the next patch so I could plink away with my bow. As it is, it looks like I'll be using my dinky solar-powered pistol until I get high enough I can afford to spend 20+ credits for EVERY SHOT.

Silliness.

The bows that were available at launch are bugged far beyond the developer's ability to make a quick fix for. So while they're working on it, some alternatives were introduced wihtout the bugs. Part of the problem right now is "bow special-attack" is just plain not working. Another problem is reload bugs with the specific weapons that haven't been tracked down.

For the low levels, I recommend going out and finding a weapon called the "bow blaster." This is a blaster rifle that simulates the look and feel of a crossbow, but uses energy ammo instead of actual arrows. You will only need bow skill, fling-shot skill, and aimed-shot skill. That is, if you are still serious about using a bow.

For higher levels, don't plan on making regular use of the bow. The martial artists who do use it (and I know of very few) use it to "pull" a target in from a distance, and will drop the bow for something cheaper and better once the critter is in melee range. Because arrows are so expensive, there is little else that can be done.
