Meta meeting Sat Nov 24, 18:00 GMT Newland
There will be a meta physicist meeting on Saturday, November 24 at 18:00 GMT outside the walls of Newland City (1250, 450) on Rubi-Ka 1. All metas are invited to attend regardless of level or politcal affiliation. If you are unsure of the GMT translation, typed "/played" to figure out the time shift (US East coasters is 18:00 - 5 = 13:00 = 1:00 PM).
