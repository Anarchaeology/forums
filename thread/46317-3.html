My thoughts on this...
I very rarely, if ever, give money to beggars. Like so many others on this thread, I help out low level characters with advice and buffs (although this usually means some general buffs and the occasional fun morph... yep, I'm an adv... ).

To those of you who do give money, might I suggest the following:
that you do not give a level 10 character 100k or 1M or some other large amount. It may not mean much to you and will certainly mean a great deal to the lowbie. BUT... giving such amounts will seriously influence the development of that player. Especially if it's a first character, that person will not become as proficient with the game as someone who has leveled his/her character the hard way. Essentially, giving large amount to new players will result in devaluating the player base in the long run. A character that has gotten to high levels the hard way will always be more experienced, stronger, better tuned to his/her environment than someone who has cruised his/her way through the levels on a large pile of donated cash.

Now I'm by no means a power gamer. I've been playing the game since release and my highest character is level 74. I'm sure that if I'd joined some large guild, or begged for a few weeks, and really put my mind to it, I could be at least twice as high level as I am right now. But I wouldn't have been able to look at myself in the mirror in my apartment in West Athen (). I like my character the way it is and I like the fact that he is starting to get rich right now and able to buy high QL gear without flinching with every trade.

Anything that adds versatility in the game is good. And having to overcome poverty is a very fun aspect of the game. So if you feel like giving a lowbie character money, please, give 5 10 or 20k. They will still appreciate it tremendously and it will make their lives easier for a while. But don't take away an important aspect of the game from them.

/ignore

I'm one of these people who doesn't tend to stick with one character for huge durations - I often have a low level alt. kicking about. There have been times I've run across a real newbie (level 2, backyard, clueless) who is looking for directions. For those who really like newbie-helping, I've found a good way is to keep an eye out for green names in the common starting backyards.
