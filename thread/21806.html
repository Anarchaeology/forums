Nano resists?
Out of curiosity, has Funcom ever explained why they simply made the skill Nano Resist useless? As many have pointed out, any enforcer/MA who engages in PvP vs someone with a root is a dead man... since resisting roots is nearly impossible. By contrast, the fantasy MMORPGs all have gone, if anything, to the opposite extreme... it is very very difficult to get rooted in PvP in EQ, for example (assuming you get high resistance).

Of all the things they could do to fix PvP, it seems that nano-resistance might be one of the most important (and most difficult to balance).

Just a thought...

Nano resist works... Just go test it, I tested it while an ARK monitored it. A Trader debuffed me. We didnt bother to test the Divest so much, it got 100% resist and because of that, lands about every time they try. The plunder got 190%, I resisted it about 45% or the time. I got 794 NR (incl Psy) which is the highest unbuffed NR a player can have at lvl 183, the Trader had averagely 1100 Nano skills.

The problem is of course that if we resist the Plunder (45/55), they simply recast it, and odds are that it lands first time, but in 2 tries its even bigger odds. After Divest, they already got us fumbling after some nano to cast, and to be honest, all nanos I got that is 200 lower than my original skill, is useless vs a trader with roots and healing. After they land plunder, they have debuffed us with 415 points in ALL nano skills and ALL weapon skill that effects our ATTACK rating (AMS). In other words, he debuffed my AR/RE from 600 AMS to 185... Try to hit a trader with 900 (DMS) evades with a gun you got 185 AMS with.... Its overkill.

In a private test vs a lvl 118 Trader (he got low evades), I was able to hit him with 10 dmg each hit when I hit, which wasnt to often btw. And when my MC is almost 50% of what I got undebuffed, my nukes dont even scratch. Only option is to run, but thats not always that easy, they got roots. If I have managed to do some dmg, he roots, wait and heal and runs in to finish me off, very easy really, too easy.

The main problem is that NR and Evades work exactly the same if we dont count crits into it, how many times have you competely evaded a shot? To completely evade a shot, is just as difficult to resist a debuff/nuke with 100% resist. But, evades are useful, they make you reduce the chance of getting critted, making it more easy to feel a difference between 0 and 800 in evade skills. We got no such thing for nukes/debuffs, either you resist, or you dont, nothing in between. I promise, the evades would be very useless if they didnt work vs crits, nobody would use 1 million IP to max 3 skills for a small chance of completely evading a shot every 1 in 100.

Further, most of the debuffs got 100% resist, thats the same as NT nukes, do you expect to resist NT nukes? Well, dont know what to say about nukes, im not neutral about that since I am NT, but at least I dont expect to resist them. Now, since most of the debuffs such as (top ones, those I for the most part encounter):

MPs Dominate -100 to a nano skill

Docs init debuff -1500 inits which effectively shuts down all nano casting

Traders Divest -200 to ALL nano and Weapon skills

they all got 100% chance of resist, same as NT nukes. The 100% effectively means FC intended them to land every single time vs an equal lvl target with maxed resist. If you ask me, I would say its crazy. Those debuffs, specially the DOcs and Traders are DAMN powerful, yes way to powerful to have 100% resist and land about every single time they try.


So, I really think the chance of resisting debuffs should be increased by ALOT, and/or remove some parts of them. None of the above debuffs should have lower chance of resist than 190%. And/or they should remove the Nano debuff part of the Traders debuffs, and remove the nano init debuff part of the doc init debuff. Traders plunder should then be increased to 280% resist.

To cripple an opponent in 1/10 of a second is WAY to powerful.
