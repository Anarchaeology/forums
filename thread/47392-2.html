Engineers need love!
My vote is for engineer love - and not just from a mechanics point of view, but from a RP point of view as well.

Engineers do one thing: make a 'bot. Everything they have is focused around that 'bot. Their shields are okay - for the 'bot. Their tradeskills mean trimmer use is easier - for the 'bot.

The problem is the 'bot is a second-rate enforcer who couldn't keep agro if it tried. It's biggest strength is its hp, which isn't a strength at all if nothing is hitting it!

And the engineer is simply the keeper of the second-rate enforcer's leash, who can only really be effective by grabbing a shotgun or SMG and making like a poor-man's fixer.

Engineers need love. They need more trimmers (what about a 'divert energy to damage' trimmer?). They could really do with a small range of different 'bots - scout 'bots, enforcer 'bots, heck even medical 'bots might work. Since an engineer can only have one 'bot, it means they can be the 'second rate' whatever-is-required-by-the-group.

Engineers should be able to do engineer-y things. Just 'bots is both boring and tedious. The fact that engineers are not the premier builders of objects, the fact that engineers can't tweak the armor or weaponry of their teammates and all that is - IMHO - a bit of an oversight.

How difficult would it be to give the engineers a nano or tradeskill item that takes a weapon or armor and turns it into a *temporary* higher QL version of the same (without changing the required stats) which in turn reverts back to what it was when the duration runs out? That'd be engineer love, and engineers would become popular for their ability to do engineer-y things. Hooray!
