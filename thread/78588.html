Re: Another new Creepy BUG!
quote:
Originally posted by Cowe
1) When I am in a duo team, one of my team mate go invisible. I can heal him with my group heal, but not with my single heal. This just started happening to me recently so I am not sure if this is old issue. They come back after a long time tho.
If by invisible you mean "The person hits sneak mode and disappears" then you haven't ever been able to use a single heal on that person. Group Heals hitting the sneaking person shouldn't be either (at least FC stated awhile back that was what they wanted), which is a bug.

If you mean the person just suddenly disappears on you, you can see the damage from them fighting the mob in the room but you cannot see them, then yeah thats the way its been. The Client thinks the target is completely out of LOS, but the server knows otherwise. When you do a single heal it checks for LOS and fails. When you do a group heal, the server controls who gets healed and who doesn't.

Not being able to cast a targetted nano on a target because the client thinks you don't have LOS is a long standing bug. Its one that makes an NTs life a hell of tortures and pain.

FC *please* take LOS/range checking off the client... there are too many bugs for the client to check LOS, the client is often wrong about where another player or a mob is. You check LOS/Range on the server anyway.
