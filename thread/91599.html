My futuristic adventure story
This is my first real attempt at a story. It is very loosely based on AO.

Chapter 1

The loose gravel road crunched beneath Jaltecs boots as he ran against the howling wind. The vast sky darkened as the sun slowly descended in the north. Jaltec turned his attention to the eastern horizon. He looked upon the beloved city in which he grew up in, Riov. Returning home from a long and perilous journey, he was eager for a much needed rest. He knew that he must be wary of strangers baring questions, for his mission was to be known by few. Of the thousands of inhabitants in Riov, Jaltec could only trust the Chosen.

Jaltec was what folks would call an Adventurer. He was a nomad containing a passion for the wilderness and despising the hectic city life. However, the occasional visit to Riov was necessary, be it to take a break from danger or just to receive his next mission. He was not a person someone would wish to meet in a dark alley, or in any place for that matter. He was draped in a torn black cloak, which concealed his travel-worn armor and holstered pistols. The parts of his face that werent blanketed in shadow caused by his hood were rugged and unshaven. His voice was deep and gritty, and sounded as if it hadnt been used in a long while. He was of average build, muscular, but not bulging with vein-covered muscles. He stood at about six feet, which was slightly above average in Riov. Citizens say Jaltec has a presence, and you just know when he is around.

The last streaks of sunlight pierced the clouds before the sun finally vanished from sight. Jaltec reached the outskirts of the immense city and approached the outer gate, where two lightly armored guards were standing.

Halt traveler! What is it you seek? Barked the larger guard of the two.

An inn to calm my thoughts in and a bed in which to rest.

Show me your Clan token.

Jaltec fished a cracked, dirt-stained coin of gold out of his belt pouch. Engraved on the coin was a Tosri, a dangerous, scavenging beast whose affection is difficult to gain. The Tosri is Jaltecs sign, given to him at birth, as all Chosen had been given. He handed the token to the guard.

The guards face instantly softened and he stuttered, T-Terribly sorry, Master J-Jaltec. Go right through. His trembling hand held the token out and Jaltec took it.

The skinny guard piped in. We just cant be too careful these days, what with the recent events.

Jaltec simply nodded and walked through the gate. When he was a good distance away, he could hear the guards chattering. They said something about Jaltec, but he gave no heed to it.

There was approximately a one-mile expanse between the outer and inner gates. This area was a desolate land of homeless people and exiled outcasts whom the guards didnt have the heart to throw out. There was the occasional lynching of one of these pariahs when the citizens became restless. Similarly, one of these vile wanderers will sometimes breach the inner gate and murder a citizen or steal someones valuables; more often than not they will do both of these things.

As Jaltec strolled through the span of barren waste, he heard a scream originating from an alley to his left. He quickly crouched low and faded into the shadows of the bushes near the alley. He reached both hands into his cloak and pulled out his previously concealed pistols. They had been holstered in two connected straps around his shoulders and chest. He slowly crept towards the corner of the stone wall on the right of the alley. He reached it and carefully peered into the shadows beyond the bushes.

There was a man with long black hair and a shaggy beard and mustache towering over another man on the ground. The man standing was clearly a street thug, as he was holding a spiked titanium bat in his right hand. The man on the ground was wearing a business suit, and tears mixed with blood were streaming down his face.

Hand over your credits, ya snooty bastard, before I shatter your face more. Rumbled the thug.

The man whimpered and pathetically attempted to scramble away, but the thug kicked him in the stomach. The thug suddenly lost all patience and raised the weapon above his head.

Say good night! He shouted while bringing the bat down fast.

A split second later, a bullet coated with a chemical designed to ignite on impact traveled through the thugs skull and ricocheted off the wall behind him. Blood sprayed from the wound while his lifeless body slumped to the dirt floor.

Jaltec stepped into the dim light created by a blazing torch mounted high overhead. Traces of a deep crimson glow faded into the air around his eyes. The businessman pressed himself into the corner of the alley, his eyes stricken with fear.

I will not harm you. Go home with haste!

The man stared at Jaltec for a short moment before clumsily running away, towards the inner gate. Jaltec rummaged around in the thugs pockets and pulled out a few credits. He shoved them into his wrist pouch and headed for the inner gate.

Chapter 2

The inner gate was more heavily guarded. Every ten feet stood a guard covered from head to toe in a bulky armor suit made of titanium. The suits were colored with dark brown and green for camouflage. There was a gas mask built in, as well as a bulletproof visor attached to the helmet. Each soldier wielded a sleek, black pulse rifle. The rifles fire a black atomic sphere that disintegrates anything in a fifteen-foot radius of its point of impact. For more discreet occurrences, the guards also each carry two shock pistols holstered in their belts. Furthermore, the guards have nanobot packs strapped onto the back of the suits. The nanobots can be channeled in very peculiar ways. There is no limit to their true power. There were two battle tanks surveying the perimeter of the gate. They were armed with twin pulse cannons, and on either side was a manned nano-grenadier. A nano-grenade, or nanonade, creates a vast explosion from tightly packed nanobots. Nanonades were the most powerful weapon known to man.

The gate itself was triple-layered. The innermost layer was made of stone, and was the whole gate for about one hundred and fifty years. After the devastating War of the Clans, the Council of Riov decided to add two new layers. The middle layer was five feet of solid titanium. And the outermost layer was comprised of nanobots channeled into pure energy to create a, so far, impenetrable force field. Riov was nicknamed The City of Blue, because one can see the nanobots glowing a deep blue from many miles away.

The guards eyed Jaltec curiously as he walked towards the entrance to Riov, for they had not seen a Chosen pass through for many years. Jaltec had been away fro nearly thirteen years on a personal journey, but he had been sent a message two weeks ago. The message had told him to return to Riov immediately, and that their was great danger ahead. All of the remaining Chosen had yet to be summoned, and Jaltec was the first to be called for.

There were but three remaining of the Chosen from the original nine. The Chosen were prophesized by Tique, the eldest of the Order of Foreseers. Sadly, the Order became no more when a band of rogue extremists silently executed every member in the night, roughly three hundred and seventy years ago. Once a Chosen comes into their power, they age no more. They become warriors, bounty hunters, and ninjas of the deadliest kind, their hearts binding them to one another. For his relentless strength and untiring endurance in battle, Quori was chosen. He was the first to be chosen, and the first of the Chosen to perish. He fell at the sword of Doshec himself, the Clans greatest enemy in history. Doshec was believed to be dead, killed in the First Independence War, but his body was never recovered. For his unbelievable speed, in both physical and mental acts, Synox was prophesized. Synox used nanobots to alter his speed, making him able to run, think, and do anything else almost three times as fast as any other man. Poscai, the third of the Chosen, killed him. Poscai was chosen for his ability to become invisible at will, and make his footsteps unheard. Unfortunately, he used his power for evil acts; therefore he was stripped of his power and executed. The fourth of the Chosen was Luborn. She had the uncanny ability to create fields of resistance between her body and objects, giving her the power to fly and move objects at will. She was captured by a bounty hunter employed by the Enemy, and was tortured for months. She valiantly refused to give any information pertaining to the whereabouts of the other Chosen, so she was murdered. For his potency to foresee events of the future, Ardaimic was selected. Ardaimics mind was not as complex as the intellects that made up the Order of Foreseers. He was unable to handle the colossal power of this ability, and he committed suicide. Finally, the most recent of the Chosen to be numbered with the dead was Shide. He contained the most unusual power of all. He could pass between reality and Hell, using passages provided by nanobots. The Order of Foreseers scheduled many meetings pertaining to Shide, and ultimately decided that this capability was far too vehement. They locked the protesting Shide in a dank, squalid dungeon until he died of starvation.

Jaltec thought of the deceased Chosen as he walked up the steps to the entrance to Riov. He had known none of them, for they all perished long before he was born. Suddenly, a hand slammed into his chest, and prevented him from walking any further.

A mysterious traveler at this time of night? State your business! demanded the owner of the hand, a muscular guard with a furrowed brow. He stood almost a foot taller than Jaltec.

Ive already spoken to the outer guards. I am cleared to enter, replied Jaltec.

Well, tell me now. Ive no reason to trust you.

Jaltec sighed. All I am here for is an inn to rest within, he lied. Is that a crime?

No, but Im interested in that fresh blood on the toe of your boot and your glove.

Jaltec looked down. Indeed, there was blood where the guard had indicated. Jaltec thought back to the thug. He must have gotten some on him while seeking the credits. A careless mistake, he thought. Simply a street incident, nothing more. If you must know, I am Jaltec, youngest of the Order of Chosen, on urgent business with the Council of Riov.

The guard snorted. Lies! There hasnt been a Chosen in Riov for fifteen years!

As Jaltec held out his token, he said, Twelve years and eight months, actually.

The guard looked as if he were about to be sick. Tiques Wisdom! It is Jaltec! He began to bow, but Jaltec waved for him to keep his posture. The guard quickly moved aside and watched in awe as Jaltec continued through to the streets of Riov.

The streets were deserted save for a few nocturnal wanderers, and children out playing late. Most lights indoors were turned off, and the only things showing Jaltec the gravel road were a few dim torches flickering in the cool breeze. Memories began to renew themselves in his mind, none of them enjoyable. Jaltec had loathed his years spent cooped up in the city. He had daydreamed his way through academics, waiting for an adventure to present itself.

His thoughts became scattered as he realized he had reached his destination. It was the Tower of Riov, where the Council presided. He slowly made his way up the many stone steps. He gazed up at the wondrous details of the structure. There were jutting ledges, and three small peaks surrounding one seemingly infinite tower that bored into the cloudless, starlit sky. Statues of each member of the Council, as well as the Order of Foreseers, were mounted around the highest ledge to be seen by all.

Jaltec stood at the great wooden doors of the building. He reluctantly placed both of his gloved hands upon the thick, wide doors, one hand on each door. He hesitated for a full minute, and then pushed them open as the hinges creaked.
