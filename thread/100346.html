OOC/IC - a kinda mix

Hehe .. We had some troubles with a a young opifex guildie once ..

I was Lord Ron of the beggar's guild then, with a base of operations in Tir. Anyways, I was out looking for recruits with one of my knights, Rotsnort. Pretty odd fellow, that one. In any case, we were jaywalking and loitering around the CoT building where we saw this young opifex standing. I strolled over to her and started giving her the beggar's talk. A small peptalk about beggars being the eyes and ears of the streets and so on, giving the impression that we actually *did* something .. And we did. Anyways, Rot was interferring my speech, so I said "Rot! Go sit in the corner! And don't stand up or say another word until I say so!" .. He says "Sorry Ron" so I pretend to get a bit more pissed and so on.

Anyways, we thought we'd take this gal to the rebel to see if she could drink like a beggar. In any case, just for fun, I kept whining at Rot just for the fun of it, and he was playing along with it too .. Appearanty, he'd made "yes Ron" and "sorry Ron" macros.

"Rot, you go in last." "Rot, you sit on the floor." "Rot, get me a drink and pay for it yourself." "Rot, give the gal some of your money." .. "Rot, shut up!"

She was starting to ask me why we were treating him so badly, and I told she didn't need to feel pity for Rot. No other guilds wanted him anyways, he was useless. Eventually, the others came along, and so we told them to play along with it. Now Rot was to get us all drinks and sit on the floor. She felt so bad for the man, she ran for the bar, trying to get ahead of him and desperatly trying to give Rot money so that he wouldn't have to pay for the drinks himself, but Rot just said "Ron knows best", so she got more and more frustrated.

Eventually, Rot pretended to get really upset as if he didn't know what to do anymore, and headed out of the rebels .. She followed him and had a peptalk about how he had to stand up to me and the rest of the guild ..

The poor lass was so saddened over our treatment of the poor man, and when we showed up after following them a little, she started crying. To be frank, she seemed a little dim at the time. As if she really hadn't smelled the rat at all. Anyways, we all thought we had gone a bit too far and told her it was a test to see if she was fit to be a beggar. It was a guild for looking after the poor after all, and so she joined.

Anyways, she was a member, and we had work to do .. We were doing intelligence for the clans and sold omni information for money. In the proper way, not infiltration. We had some honour .. however, all she wanted to do was to party. She'd spend all her time at the Reet's retreat, and she was getting on our nerves. "Ron, come to Reets!!!" "We can party!!!" .. And I'd go "No, I have work/meeting to do/attend." Eventually, she managed to get one of my knights to the reet. A fine man named Jopis .. And I met her at the Rebels the day after ..

Seems she had fallen in love with Jopis. And I thought .. "Alright, this is getting weird." (OOC/IC) She also introduced me to some woman she claimed was her mother .. So I asked "(( In AO, I take it? ))" and she said "No, in RL." .. Came as quite of a shock. I asked of her age, and she told me she was twelve years old .. That explained why she didn't smell the rat on the day she joined.

Anyways, she had gotten in love with Jopis for real, and so over a few /tells, I told the man about her age .. He replied with something like ..

"****!!!!!111 TWELVE?!?!!!! NOOOO!!!!"

Furthermore, she had told her mother of her and Jopis and how much fun she had in the beggar's guild. Good gods, what was I to do? .. It was not a guild appropiate for that kinda young souls, but still, the way her mother acted, I was afraid I'd get my skin full if I kicked her .. ;P

And poor Jopis was being stalked and nagged all the time. Weirdness.

Anyways, it all sorted out. We had done an extensive investigation on some issues regarding the Radiman assasination for a few weeks, and we eventually failed in managing to be discreet. So, an omni faction met up with me and told me they would kill all beggars on sight .. Thus, most members except the hard core were kicked for their own safety and RP reasons ..

You remember Shanty from the story? The successful stripper?

She did a job once in the rebel and earned loads of money from tips. I was there, of course, as I was her manager. Anyway .. There was a nanomage fella there who seemed to be a little breathtaken by Shanty's performance. A bit more than usual, one might say, and he started nagging her if she could do a job at a private, intimate party of his. She told him to talk to me as her manager, and he wanted to hire her for 10k or something. I said "No way, she's worth lots more than that." and refused, knowing I could get her better jobs than that.

But he didn't stop there ..
He claimed he'd fallen in love with Shanty, saying he wanted her to be her girlfriend. Shanty eventually ran out to get away from him, and I followed her .. so did he!

I followed Shanty on foot through the dusty streets of Tir down the road northeast where you can cut off right to the arena and left for Miiir. She was standing at the end of her street when I found her. I asked her if she was alright, and she told me the nanoguy had gotten a bit perverse and annoying ..

I turned around, and in the other end of the street .. there he was. He'd gotten one of his buds with him too. Shanty went "protect me from them Ron!", and stood behind me.

There was clearly some hostility between us now .. I was her pimp in a sense and he had to deal with me if he wanted to get to her first. So, he asked me again if she'd take the job. I told him that she wouldn't take jobs from perverts, and then he asked me if I would sell her to him for 250 000 credits!

I told him no, Shanty's life was not mine to sell, but he didn't give up. As I didn't want his cash, he wanted to take me to the arena ..

There, we were to fight over Shanty's life.
I thought the idea was ridiculous, so I refused.
Eventually, after some harsh words and challenges, my trusty bodyguard Josip showed up, wearing a battle suit. He told him that if they ever buggered or laid a finger on any of us, he'd break them in two.

That scared'em off ..
