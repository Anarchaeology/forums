Dual wielding is incredibly complicated. I'll give you the simple explanation.

Weapons have a certain minimum requirement in multi-wield skill to be able to dual wield. At least one of the two weapons you wield with multi-wield, needs to meet that requirement.

In most cases the QL of the weapon you multiwield is limited not by your weapon skill for equipping one of those weapons, but by your ability to meet its multi wield requirement. This is especially true for ranged weapons, because ranged weapons multiranged requirements increase dramatically as the QL of the weapon goes up. And for professions that have dark blue multi-ranged skills, which almost all do, it becomes impossible to wield a high QL gun in the second hand.

This is important because dual wielding increases the number of attacks you make overall by 33%, but half the attacks you make come from each weapon. So if your secondary weapon is too weak, it saps harder-hitting attacks away from your main weapon and you do less damage than if you were only using one weapon.

Finally... the old FA pistols are good because they have low multi requirements. But they also require pistol, ranged energy, and flingshot... they would be horrible IP sinks for a doctor to raise in addition to shotgun and multi wield. If you want to spend that many IP, you'd be better off using a different, heavier weapon type entirely.

A few interesting points here.

1. If you are a doc, below title level 5 nobody cares what kind of damage you do as long as you heal well. CH the tank a few times and he'll think you are swell, even if your 75% OE rifle is going miss miss miss.

2. Nanoskills for docs. BM/MM/TS always raise. MC>PM>SI. Don't get hung up at title level 4 about doing SFA. I could cast it in the 130/140 range but then again I have a nanomage doc. There are tons of 150+ docs running around these days, most all of them can cast SFA, and prior to title level 5 you won't be doing much implant swapping, so you don't really need it. During title level 4 raise your SI high enough to use instinctive control but you can leave that alone for a bit.

3. Think about what your weapon is for. What good is a 125 X3 in a 180 team mission? Are Caterwaul docs currently "owning" on the battlefield? At title level 4 you are trying to do better heals and lusting to do complete heal. What weapon skills can help you do that? You don't want to raise one weapon skill just to IPR it later, you only get 15 IPR. Should a title level 4 doc use an Ithica? No. If you are a shotgun doc at title level 4, use a Krutt, you can get up to 18 intelligence points with one, worth up to 4 or 5 nanoskill bonus. If you have a QL 50ish pepper pistol, you can implant that, get extreme predudice, and a wrangle, and equip 10-14 extra biomet. Hardly any IP needed to get that pepper pistol on at title level 4 if you have a shining small arms pistol implant. Later on down the road when you can self equip your shotguns that pistol skill will come in handy-use a Jess to get that Morphing memory in for example. Also remember when you are using rifle you are giving up 2!! nanoresistance clusters, which means you are that more likely to be deprived and plundered on the battlefield.

Morelian is very wise in the ways of weapons.

However... I have to say that while a lot of doctors like nanoresist... I don't. Not enough to implant it over rifle or AS, if I was using a rifle or an ithaca.

The reason is pretty simple... NR is luck. Unless you can get it up to the 1300-1500 range like an enforcer, you can't rely on it very much at all. From my experience, 900 NR is just as useful as 1000 NR, or 800 NR. If you get targeted by a trader for divest... divest will almost always land. In a mass battle I doubt he'll even bother trying to plunder - if you were targeted for a specific takedown then you'll be dead without being plundered, or if the trader is just trying to debuff a lot of people, he's not going to use plunder on any of them since it's 190% resist. Other debuffs are the same - either you're being targeted and it doesn't matter, or they're tab-debuffing and moving on, and you can virus scan it.

But 1000 AS is better than 900 AS, and 800 rifle is better than 700 rifle. Etcetera.

Still... make your own decisions based on your own experience
