The Freelancer!
Name: Sol Kesagai Ganzer
Age: 25
Height: 56
Weight: 175
Eyes: Brown
Hair: Black
Species: Guess?
Profession: Agent
Alignment: Omni
Group: None

My name and past are unimportant but I will let you in on just this much. I was from earth a long time ago before I came to Rubi-ki I was a freelance spy for just about any price. Until I took one job my last job, I was finally getting out of the game tired of it all just wanting to settle down with all my secrets as a backup. The job turned out to be a trap and I soon found my self-blown out of a building falling two-story building onto the hood of a parked car. I remember wakening up in an omni med hospital being approached by a corporate man and the owner of the car I landed on, he made me an offer I couldnt refuse. After my little incident at the building all the corporations put a rather large bounty on my head dead or alive just to keep all their skeletons in the closet. To the omni corp. the money didnt matter neither did the secrets they just wanted my skills to be useful to them on Rubi-ki. I had to say yes just to get my self out of a tight bind after they healed my body I was stuck in a cryo tube and shipped to Rubi-ki born a new as Sol Kesagai Ganzer.
