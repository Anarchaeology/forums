Black Reet Down
Yesterday an Omni jet went down in West Stret. The cause of the accident still unconfirmed as far as I know. With the pilot still clinging on to life, Lieutenant Nessin ( I hope I got the name right), sent out a general alert asking all available Omni citizens to form a rescue party.

I figured this was a worthy humanitarian mission as well as a good opportunity to show of RUR's Logitronix series robot's all-terrain capabilities. Thus, though I was on my way to interview an candidate, an engineer by the name of Spidah, we decided to postpone our meeting and offer our services to the rescue party.

Unfortunately Clanners also took an interest in our fallen craft and its injured pilot. By the time the Omni rescue party arrived on site, Clan forces were already entrenched into defensive positions and the Omni rescuers torn apart by the incoming fire.

I understand the Clanners were successful in capturing our pilot. I am sure they had very good reason for this and I hope he will be treated humanely. For my part I am not sure how well we proved the all-terrain capabilities of the Logitronix line of robots, but I did quite successfully demonstrated just how quickly I can get myself killed.

Well I am sure every story has two sides to it. This is really all I was able to piece together.
