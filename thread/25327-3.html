The REAL problem is that people refuses to acknowledge what makes a profession good shouldn't be confined to a solitary aspect of that profession.

If Doctor = Healing power in the real world, all Doctors would be surgeons. A well rounded physician is more than someone that can slap a bandage on an open wound. And that should be held true in AO as well. It's still argueable on whether or not Traders could outheal Doctors for me at least. But IMO, we're still just arguing over who can slap the biggest bandage on the wound. That's the sum of the Trader's ability to heal damage: we got big bandages (wrapped in razorblades which cut up our hands when we remove the package). The same holds true for MAs and Adventurers. But the doctor profession has other abilities that pertain to character health besides the ability to close wounds.

But that leads us to the next part of the problem: doctors are undoubtly the best at caring for the health of players in their party, but when are those skills needed? They haven't been. So anyone that could slap a bandage on the wound supplanted the doctor's spot in a team. Traders happened to be a bit more obvious about it (I dare people to tell me they've never seen teams with 2 MAs or a MA and an Adventurer to handle healing in the team). And for a team facing an orange con mob, all that is needed is who can slap on the biggest bandage. More adventurous (or intelligent) teams, however, need the doctor's abilities. A HP buff helps the tank survive the intitial onslaught when entering the room. Init debuffs have a significant effect of slowing damage on mobs that can easily survive the team's opening alphastrike/crit shots. DoTs negate a mob's natural healing which means the team has to inflict less damage to kill the mob (and spend less time recieving damage). But if the mob only lasts 30 seconds, who cares about all this?

If Doctors can only consider themselves 'Doctors' by the amount of HP they can heal, they're short changing themselves. And that's what I think the original poster was trying to say.
