My Ideas...
Well, i thought i would bring the mind of a PvPer to AO...
Blah, anyway, ill just start with abuncha suggestions for the current AO.

Able to do with Current AO.
1. Real guns, i know AO is set in the Future, but what happed to all the guns we know and love? Eg. AK47, M16 Ect. Get the idea?

2. Muzzle flash for Bullet Type guns. (Real muzzle flash, no lazers please)

3. Arg, Cars.... Trucks.... Whatever they are, Why no old Military Surplus tanks/jeeps/Trucks?



[u][b]Things that are Probably impossable to do. AO2?[u][b]
1. PvP...... Jesus, it resolves WAY to slow. PvP should beable to get your adrenaline pumping.

2. Ok, well, here is where the money is at, i kid you not, The targeting system, Clicking on a monster and hitting Q is alittle... Boring i mean, i pray that AO2 isnt anything like that, it worked great for EQ, but then again, PvP on EQ/AC/DAoC was all kinda lame, DAoC had by far the best PvP.... Anyway, look at neocron, its targeting system is perfect, it blends Character skill with Player skill, and that my friends is where the good feeling comes from when you kill a enemy, mostly another player...
I know im baseing this around PvP to much, but whats funner? killing mindless monsters all day? i think not. (well for me anyway)
Buildings, they shouldnt be another "Zone" unless you could "Zone" out to the top.... Lets say, your a clan sniper, And ya sneak into omni ent or whatever, and climb a high building...
now, ya got a long range high power rifle, On top of a building, Many unsuspecting people below. Get the picture?
Anyway. I got to many ideas to type here, ill prolly just get blown off and ignored. but at least i tryed.
