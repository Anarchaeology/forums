Wasting time in game...please improve
Here's a suggestion to enhance (or de-enhance?) wasted time in game.

I know a lot of players would agree with me when I say that a lot of time in the game is completely wasted. Not that gaming isn't enough of a waste of time anyway - sometimes....but heck it's fun. So wasting unecessary in-game time takes the fun out of it.

Here's the two things that waste the most time in game:

1. Bad nano's cast on players from NPC's or others such as "Deprive Skills".

commentary: How many times have you had to just sit there for 5 minutes until you were able to heal and get on with the mission again?

solution: What if the bad nano's disappeared if the caster was killed? To enhance it even further, what if this had a negative side to it and good nano's cast from team member's or whoever went away if they were killed? This would make us watch each other's back more during team-play wouldn't it? I kinda like that.

2. Installing belts takes forever as well as installing other items.

commentary: For one thing, it's funny how implants take almost no time to install, yet belts take forever. Why? Good question. To waste our time while we wait. Get up, get some coffee and food during that time. Then, it's so buggy that sometimes you have to do it again.

solution: I can understand that making it take time doesn't allow us to install it while out in the open hunting or whatever. It would be a little unrealistic (I use that term loosley - think of the size of the planes in the game) to be able to do that. But what if it was installed in a jiffy inside a shop? Or there was a machine that you needed in the shop to use the way the implant machine works? Just a suggestion.

Those are the two biggest wastes of time in the game that I can think of. I think I had another, but it's fleeting at the moment. Please post if you happen to think of some others and a solution of course.

Thanks!
