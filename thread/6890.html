New PVP Game Mechanics - Bump it!
Greetings every1,

the new pvp system turned PVP more into gankers vs. player than into player vs. player. Maybe the old system was better - I'm not sure. However we need some pvp changes soon to make it fun again. Please feel free to add your comments and suggestions to this thread. I'm starting with my own ideas i'd like to see implemented ...

1. When you zone into a 25% Zone there is no 15 sec. grace period. This isnt working fine. Instead of the 15 secs you shouldnt be able to get attacked. This will take sure the client finished loading. All you have to do is doing nothing till your client is done loading. It also brings the benefit you can see if the grid outled is camped. If it is camped you can call for assistance or try to get away instead of running into a wall or wake up at Tir/Omni-1 after gridding in. Of course you can be attacked when you start moving/buffing/attacking whatever.

2. More viewrange. In pvpzones we should have max. viewrange. It makes me sick to run arround and 10m infront of me spawn 20 omnis and instand kill me. Give us the ability to see what is coming up. It just sucks that you can't see anything until it is too late.

3. While in combat you can't be attacked/buffed or healed by some1 else. Players who try to attack a player who is already attacked get a message "This player is already under attack". It's not possible to buff or heal one of the fighting players. Does this affect story based fights or team-fights ? No! You can always choose another target or wait till your target is free for another attack -> killed one of your friends. Will be more fun then to kill him. And at least he has the chance to run.

4. Remove the half-dmg and the 40% dmg cap again. With the upcoming overequipting nerf this is no longer needed and it makes whole pvp "critbased". Already no1 pvps without a critbuff anymore. It's just a question who got a better critbuff than a better gear. Maybe a few people will say now "Whee, he wants instandkills". Well - Let's say it this way. When every1 has the ability to instandkill some1 it's just fair. You always have the ability to grab buffs or go on full defense to prevent an instandkill. Are there professions who don't have the ability to do it ? I think not - Doctors with shotguns, Engineers with Flashpoint Lasers - all pets doing insane dmg without the halfdmg cap also. I think this is fine and it's worth to try it.

It was good to try the current pvpsystem but in my oppinion it failed. Please add your comments and suggestions to this thread. I'm up for any critics and new ideas
