Lost Chapter owns Notum Soldier
Earlier today, a good group of Lost Chapter members (20-30) took a visit to EFP to pick on Notum Soldier, and luckily, we managed to kill it twice.

With Anikitos leading our line of tankers, we dropped Notum Soldier in 10-15mins on its first spawn, dropping:

One Nophex Destroyer II
One or couple Hellfury Augment Chips (Can't remember)
QL220 Cyber Bau armor

After giving out the loot, it spawned again, and it was killed again dropping:

One Nophex Destroyer II
One Notum Infused Kevlar Body Armor
One or couple Hellfury Augment Chips (Once again, can't recall)

-

Overall, the experience was a good one. The two Nophex's went to Sandbag and Elnertz (w00t!), and the chips were given to other soldiers (Cyboot).
