Logical looting (what you see is what you get)
Just a little thing, but I'm consisitently annoyed by the fact that whatever a mob is holding and using during a fight disappears afterwards. In EQ, if I saw a skeleton wielding a sword, a sword of some kind would be part of the loot. It was satisfying...
