Suggestion: 30 second timer on /terminate
This is to prevent two problems:
1. Terminating in PvP to prevent someone from getting the kill credit.
2. Terminating after training mobs to get the mobs to aggro other players.

There might be more, but these are the ones I've seen.

My suggestion:
When you type /terminate to self-destruct, you will be unable to move and cast for 30 seconds - then you will die as usual. Hitting the escape key would cancel the countdown.

There should also be a visual indicator to show others that you are terminating, like a special emote.

I can't see any downsides to this, because you never need to /terminate fast. All you need to do is die, to get out of places if you're stuck etc. A 30 second timer would prevent a few "grey area exploits"/cheesy tactics, without nerfing anyone.

Let me know if I'm right/wrong
