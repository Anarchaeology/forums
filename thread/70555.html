Event!! GTTT (Guild-Team-Tag-Tournament)
Heya Guys,

I just wanted to list an event Im trying to assemble and would like to hear opinions on.

Event: GTTT (Guild-Team-Tag-Tournament)
Place: Undisclosed Mayhem Zone
Event Type: Capture the flag variant
Stages: 1.lvl 75-100 Freshman Tournament
2.lvl 100-130 Rookie Tournament
3.lvl 130-150 Apprentice Tournament
4.lvl 150-200 Ace Tournament
5.lvl 75-200 Neophyte Mayhem
(Each stage isnt based on a chars current title but is
named after the difficulty lvl on the stage.)

Rules: 1. Event includes Omni-, Clan-, Neutral Guilds
2. Each Team Leader has a rooster of 20-30 people
3. Each team has a team color (Colored hoods)
4. Each team has 1 flag (Color dressed char)
5. Each guild can have a max of 6 people on field at one
time. If a teammember dies, the leader can select to
send in a replacement from his rooster.
6. Winning team is the guild that kills of all opposing teams
chars in the rooster or Takes the opposing guilds flag to
their side.
7. The Flag sets /follow on the teams Flagcarrier after a
trade is initiated.
8. If Flagcarrier is killed then the flag returns to his original
position.
9. If flag is killed then the killing team is disqualified and
detucted from the guilds rooster.
10. Each participating guild has to pay a entry fee. This Fee
will be the price money and set on the difficulty of the
stage. This Fee will also cover costs for hiring
(Borderguards, Flags,Judges etc.)
11. Warping to the location will be made by engies at
location and a m8 that will team people up for warp at
a non-pvp neutral site (ex. Newland grid). Every
participant, audiance, guard, flag, judge will be warped.
12. Anyone that is killed on field or by accident will be
warped back to the location. But if participant, then
he/she is out of the tournament.
13. Any mob or troublemaker will be killed on sight and
their guildleader notified of the offence and risk getting
his/hers Guild barred from the contest.
14. Only guildmembers can participate in this event. How
they are choosed is up to the guildleader. This event
will require wits, tactic and a good deal of adrenaline-
tolerance
15. Any mobspawn on field of battle will be considered as
a "joker" and a element of surprise.

This event is an idea to make our lives in Rubi-Ka a bit more fun...Its an idea that Im trying to make happen. But I would like to hear any input u might have, either its flame/bump or modifications.
Please feel free to tell your guildleader about the idea and send a /tell to Chocosan or Mrsanta if u would like to participate or have any questions at all regarding this post/event.
(If any Gamemaker/personell read this, please feel free to give comments and/or advice on possibilities of changing mob-spawn on the location in mind.)

Flame or Bump...Ill check this thread often
