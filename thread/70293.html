Council of Ares: The Truth from a Clansman
quote:
Originally posted by Dr. Ervin "Hayjyin" Houston
OTSEC is demanding that, in short, Redruum and Windguaerd drop their weapons and quiet down. Now, I'm as dedicated as anyone to the clans; even more than most I'd dare to say, but what the OTSEC is asking is perfectly reasonable.

Omni-Tek wants a shot at the Council of Ares? Let them have it. They wanted to be seen as the "leaders" (though despots is more accurate) of the clans? Let them stare down Omni-Tek's guns for once. Maybe if we're lucky, Omni-Tek will get rid of the false Council and we'll all be able to breathe a sigh of relief.

I happen to know on good authority that the majority of clan leaders are opposed to the methods of the Council of Ares. I may not be a leader myself, that doesn't stop me from having an opinion.

First off, The CoA is comprised of criminals. Terrorist attacks, even against your enemy in a ceasefire, isn't legal, and terrorist attacks is the least of what Redruum has admitted, boasted even, to have done. Anyone who bands with him is an accomplice to their group's crimes.

Finally, I - again - am forced to wonder what we're all doing. Are we clans or not? We're letting a minority like the CoA take over without even blinking. Are we all asleep? Do you want to be led by criminals? Or am I the only one the CoA isn't "paying off" to keep quiet on this and haven't been told? They can keep their filthy credits, I'll do what I know is right.

For peace, we need to re-open communications with Omni-Tek. For peace, we need to let them take care of the CoA, as wrong as it may seem to give Omni-Tek carte blanche. For peace, we need to let Omni-Tek know that the clans are not the Council of Ares. I refuse to let myself be killed to further their own little ambitions. You want peace? The CoA wants war. You want freedom? The CoA will have all of us in an Omni-Reform facility, one way or another.

You want a leader? Wake up, be heard, and take a stand. Or be divide'ed et imperia'ed by criminals who think they know best.
Finally, someone in the Clans sees the horrific reality of where the Council of Ares' terrorism and warlike activities is dragging all of us. I applaud the honourable gentleman, and urge other clan citizens to take a moment to read, understand, and see the truth in his words.
