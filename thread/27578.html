Gambling!!.......Solution for too much money.
Yes there is an inflation problem on Rubi-Ka. No, making battle-suits worth less is not a solution. Unless something is done soon, people will start pricing items in damage rings instead of credits. (SOJs anyone?)

I suggest that the time is right to create gambling casinos for all to enjoy. I think the best way to do this is a system similar to Diablo II. A basic item is shown, (for example a blank fixer nano) and for a large bet someone would have a large chance of getting a level 10 nano, a small chance of getting a level 100 nano, and a very very VERY small chance of getting a unique item that everyone will pour billions of credits into trying to obtain. Make the odds 1:1,000 and watch the inflation problem disappear in seconds.

One more comment on the money system. Credits worked extremely well at first because the best item in the game was a Yalmaha, and everyone had to buy one.

Now that most established players can afford an entire fleet of jets, cash is quickly losing its usefullness. PLEASE put something back in the game that even very high level players must save up for. Suggestions below:

A high level Yalmaha with more speed, a new look, and maybe a 1000watt stereo sound system. 100 million credits.

"Pet" mercenaries that could help on hunts/missions, do domestic chores, whatever. 10 million credits per day.

Uber-weapons and armor that can only be worn by level 195 and above. By the time someone reaches this high, they deserve to stop working and have some fun. Not only will this inspire people to climb the ladder, but it will provide the much discussed reward at the end of the struggle. Make it useable in 75% zones only so it wont be used in pvp. Billions and billions of credits.

You get the idea.
