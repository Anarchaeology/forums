There are a lot of journals out there describing character creation, in addition to a few videos.

The jist I've gathered is

You select one of 7 race/genders (4 races, 3 of which can be male/female).

Each of those has ~20 faces, I don't know the exact number.

You can adjust your avatar's height and weight as well, 3 height levels and 3 weight levels.

Then there are tons of armor/weapons/clothes distinctions to help you be more unique.
