Events? something that's not an exp pack
I am about to the point of quitting AO. I like many others have been here since the beginning, promised the world, and delivered very little. Events are as rare now on RK1 as they were on RK2, players are left with massive missioning to have any hope of experence. All features that were promised are now being moved to a "booster pack" , and still have word of an expansion pack to come. How about we make the game what it was promised to be without making us pay, moving features to a pack to get more money from the players isnt right. Content that was supposed to be given , and then moved to pay is in poor taste. With the wide assortment of MMORPGs on the way, I dont see many staying with AO due to no story development, little involvement and a yeah later features still not there or working ( sharp objects? how about weapon skills, etc) not to mention other features that just disappeared from the coming patch lists (kamakazi robot for engies, crat briefcase bank access, exp los nano line, etc)
I hope things go well in the near future, many of my friends have left AO due to poor support ingame from ARK/GM's ( send a bug report to......or "that's not our policy to replace...) , lack of involvement and just sheer boredom from nothing but missions.
