it is normal to run low on IP's every nw and then.
So keep your most important skills/abilities up, and relaq a bit on the rest.

and for first aid, when you get your first mongoslam, first aid is your friend. Not for first aid kits, but for nano kits. My first aid kits does about 350 heal at ql 70 with 318 in first aid. One ql 50 nanokit is enough for me to toss one more mongo, which heals mea little over 1k hp (over time).

Do not reset your first aid, you will raise it again later anyway.
If you're gonna reset something, reset the evades. They're not really useful until lvl 60-70 or so (IMHO), but you will probably want to raise them later on anyway. And from what I hear, nanoresist doesn't do squat. But this is just hearsay, and not confirmed fact.
