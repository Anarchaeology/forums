well, aside from the two bickering fools, perhaps what would be better is if either funcom made it easier for adventurers to learn multi ranged (not the recommended choice here funcom), or to lower their waaay overkill knee-jerk reaction to the too low multi-ranged that the game started out with. There are some pistols that the highest skill requirement is multi ranged. This is a skill that does nothing other than grant the ability to get a second weapon into the other hand, if you have higher skills in this it does not help you in any way.

I would strongly recommend that funcom lower the multi-ranged requirements across the board on pistol weapons not all the way back to their original level, but they are definitely unjustly high. As it is most certainly easier to aim a single weapon effectively than two, there should be some kind of benefit for those that opt to specialize in only a single pistol. I'd say modify critical increase by 1 or 2, not much, but enough of a boost to help the single gun user survivability. Of course when a second weapon equips, this bonus is disabled.
