Roleplaying is about give and take.

You take the good with the bad. That means that sometimes you are going to lose. And sometimes you are going to win.

If you feel that you are not receiving fair treatment from the other person then there is always another choice:

Walk away.

All roleplaying is voluntary. No one can force you to do anything and no one can be forced by you as well.

In the end it will always be your choice as to whom you choose to roleplay with and whom you do not. As should it be for all of us.

No matter how the war between your two organisations turns out you will still be actively trying to keep the Roleplay area dynamic.

Enjoy what comes and good luck to both sides.
