There Are No Events On Rk2!!!!
Hello, let me introduce myself, my character is named Sepentura on the RK2 server.

I have played this game from the very beginning, I started out on RK1 (which apparently was a better place to be) but soon moved to RK2 when it became available. I moved because it was new, and had less hardware problems.

I am a true, true fan of this game and have been from the beginning (accually from way before the beginning) and I desire most of all to be someone on Rubi-KA, to make a difference, but since I play on RK2 I cannot. No one from RK2 is noticed or cared about. We are customers too.

I attended all eight of the debates TAG held the last two days, on both servers. A gm by the name of "Neigek" ran them both, on RK2 he/she barely said two words, he seemed afk for most of the time. But on RK1 he/she spoke very often and joked and seemed right at home. Please do not play favorites. Captfallout and a few others were the spotlight of those events and so was Red Freedom, but nothing like that was happening on RK2. All we on RK2 had to talk about was what was going on on RK1, and that is not right. We need things to happen in our diminsion too, rather than just come to events to discuss RK1! He/She did this because there has been nothing to make anyone famous on RK2, no portman, no Red Freedom, nothing! In other words no influence has been made, because They leave us out of everything.

Rubi-ka 2 isn't JUST europeans because I am american and playing there, so having events on europe time will still leave some out. I think an equal amout of events should be held on europe times, and american times, and on both servers.

I was known as Serrephim on RK1 and still am, but I don't play there much anymore, I want to help RK2 prosper more.

I would like to ask Funcom to please allow diminsion switching soon if you are going to continue to mistreat us. Or merge the diminsions or something, please keep my hope alive.
