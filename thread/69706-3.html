Good idea lets not leave out Funcom
Hello!

I sure hope Funcom will sit in on this convention and listen for a change, so they can get a better understanding of what we the roleplaying community wants. Personally I do like the tea party atmospher type of roleplaying but I also wanted death and distruction.

I hope in addition to this convention. Everyone of you sends Funcom a letter telling them that we want more content and roleplaying in this game and we want Funcom to take an active role.

I hope to attent the convention but my real life family and work takes presidence over the game. Below are some of the issues and concerns I have and hope will be addressed at the convention so as to benefit all players, even if they dont roleplay.

I would like to see that the guilds post their roleplaying events on the calendar and I am not talking just Beauty Pagents. I want to see major roleplaying active such as pvp hunting parties, kidnappings, assassination of characters. I would even scarific my character for the sake of roleplaying. If Funcom wants to assissinate my character so she can't be reclaimed, then by god I would participate. I wouldnt mind starting over again. I wouldnt mind seeing my name in print and be associated with the event. At least people would know that I participated and I am not a whinner and am not afraid of loosing my stuff. I only complain when the game takes my stuff and I didnt get to be utilize it for something that would benefit the game and other players.

All Funcom would have to do is contact me in game and ask if I would like to be a casulty of the conflict. My daughter and son would also be willing to give up their high level characters to stimulate the events. Granted we would then move all the inventory and stuff to our alts. With the exception of Nodrop items which we would be willing to give up.

Also I hope Funcom will attend the convention so that they can getting a better understanding of what the Roleplaying community desires.

I am just getting a little tired of the average tea party type roleplaying. I can do that with my grandchildren. I would like to see a little more hardcore roleplaying taking place. Things that a person would only do in a fantasy. This can only be done with some imput from the events team. At the moment the events team is a joke.

Funcoms article about the city of Tir having a conflict and that the sentinals have taken over the city just goes to show how meaningless roleplaying is at the moment. A week prior Omni soldiers entered Tir and all we got to do was haggle them. They were sent there to investigate rumers of su****ious activites. Did this has some bearing on what has now taken place? Where was the fire fight? Did anyone get to participate in this? Or did this all of a sudden come into existance like poof? Why are the major events that shape the world of Rubika only fought out on paper? These things have to be addressed at the convention.

To effectively roleplay there has to be something tangable for the players to acquire or fight for and have outside forces changing the direction of the event For example: If a guild is roleplaying on the fact that one of their guild members has been kidnapped; Funcom should then come in and kidnap that person. Funcom can kidnap my character and then kill her in the end. Let that be on the guilds conscious. It can only strengthen how roleplaying is suppose to take place.

Plus, since GMs can aware players for contributing to the storyline. I would like to see more of this taking place. Also I do not think just because a guild participated in the story that the reward should go to the guild leader nor just a member of the guild. There are bystanders that participate, even if they are haggling on the side lines. Those players contribute greatly in fueling the fire.


Ok! now lets get off the shuddle and be realistic. Funcom doesnt have the man power to contribute to this. I dont know how many of you players take part in a lot of the activities that Funcom allows players to participate in, such as beta testing, ARK community and so forth. I have always read various responses from GMs when people have asked for something. The response was always negative to the fact that they dont have the manpower to pull this off.

Basically Funcom is working with a limited staff. Anyone that reads the articles that Funcom puts forth and takes an active role in the community can determine this on their own. This is a shame because this will come back to bite Funcom in the ass.

I would like also to tell Funcom to give their players heads up on any events that will be taking place. This way people can make a point on logging in their characters and participating in the event. You dont have to say what the event is about, just indicate there is going to be a Funcom staged event. Just place it on the callendar and also the time. wow! what a way to test server load. Kill to birds with one stone.

This coming year when the new MMORPGs come out I for one will try out shadowbanes, eve-online and SWG. If they have something that interests me and keeps my attention. I will probably let my characters go into deletion. I have done that on two other MMORPGs. AO wouldnt be hard to forget. I have sat around long enough waiting for Funcom to take an active role in this game. Only to be given reassurances that things would change and big things await those loyal to AO.

I guess I am just easily dupped into believing such things. I wonder who will have to the last laugh in 2003.

My thoughts ...
I was at the last roleplaying convention. It was a good experience and an opportunity to get to know some of the other players in the roleplaying community face-to-face. I think the impact it had was minimal, but I still saw it as a good thing.

There are many problems facing the roleplaying community, but the biggest is visibility. Funcom doesn't offer us the support we need because they percieve us as an inconsequential minority. Because our community is spread throughout many cross-sections of players, it makes us seem smaller than we are. I think any effort to raise awareness that we are an active vibrant community that adds depth and flavor to the world of Rubi-Ka is worthwhile.
