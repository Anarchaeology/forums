Just one minor flaw in that tactic when it comes to agents.
Any action taken towards the mobs, be it rooting or fishing for single aggro, equals detection, which means no aimed shot.
Thus no special during the fight and our only "ace up the sleeve" is gone.

Once upon a time we were able to single pull with the help of LOS, almost as if our sniper rifles actually had a silencer equipped (go figure )... don't recall excactly when that changed. Off course, this was back when the mobs you weren't fighting would mostly wander in circles around you until you're present target was dead and you started shooting/bashing the next one.

Multiple mobs are, from my experience, the main show stopper for the profs hampered in the body dev department.
Single pulls with aimed shot (depending on LOS) would help on all levels, including making agents viable as pullers in teams.

There might be some negative factors (in FC's eyes) if a team was able to single pull with high certainty in outdoor hunting spots, tho' I think it would benefit everyone.

So my simple request would be the addition of silencers for our rifles, through tradeskills perhaps, to make our guns go Pfft! instead of BLAM!, to help on the single pulls. Even make it so the mobs you don't target can hear the Pfft! if they're too close (for instance nearest 1/3 of the room in question.)

Just a thought, really
