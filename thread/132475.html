*** Establishing video-link ***
Omni-Com protocol check... OK!
Gridfeed node access... OK!
Logging into Omni-Admin subnet... OK!
*** Video-link established! ***

Beginning transmission...

* Kithrak's face appears on the video-link. The video is of poor quality, low frame rates with static and distortions. A single large floating island city can be made out in the fuzzy background. People moving in the background are clearly wearing clothes not found on the rest of Rubi-Ka.*

This is Administrator Charles "Kithrak" Houston reporting in from the second of the three floating islands in the city of Jobe. You heard correctly, I am at the floating city of Jobe currently. Omni-Admin has dispatched me on a fact finding mission and let me tell you there are some really weird things happening here.

The scientists have all been very hush hush about their pet project but that didn't stop me from hacking their files useing creative access techniques. The rumors floating in Omni-1 about them trying to open a rift into a parallel dimension of some sort are true. I have pulled a data dump on as many files as my hacked account will allow without triggering their security protocols. *grins* I've also baught some souvenirs for my friends when I get back.

To the clan Opposing Force that have attacked us unprovoked in my absence, you are cowards. Red Tape have remained as one of the last departments to favor the use of force. One of your former leaders spoke to us about ceaseing hostilities before. Is this a measure of your millitary prowess and proof of your honor? Your transgressions will be delt with in due time.

*Kithrak covers the microphone, looking and talking to someone off camera.*
I need go to another meeting.

Administrator Charles Houston out.

End transmission...
*** Video-link terminated! ***
