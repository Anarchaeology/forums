Freelance Investigator - Ransom Terror in the Territories
While on her way to deliver the ARK uniform templates Viiir falls victim to criminals...

**Grid uplink connecting Password accepted Welcome to the Rubi-Ka Grid

I'm Lacey, a freelance reporter for the citizens of Rubi-Ka. Viiir was held ransom by a group called the "Fashion Bandits". Known for their clothing fetish and bad dress taste they managed to kidnap Miiir's sister just yesterday. It is believed they planned to force Viiir to create clothing designs for them, but because she refused they sent ransom demands to ARK, threatening the delivery of recently commissioned uniforms.

ARK sent Advisors Chaotix, Horbane and Metusaleh to hire negotiators from Newland, Tir and Omni-1. Two people from each city grouped together to make the negotiator team. After a briefing from Advisor Augur the negotiators transported to the fashion bandit's location. Wildthing, Edward, Thall, Geist, Flint and Elbryan bravely faced the bandits in their dangerous Territories hideout. Sadly Elbryan suffered an attack from a Primal Chirop, leaving five negotiators to rescue Viiir and the ARK uniforms.

Greeted with such quotes as "We're sassy, we're hip, we're the fashion bandits" and calling themselves "The most beautiful people in Rubi-Ka", the bandits went on to claim they were disbanded from a redundant Faction called Omni-Fab. They scoffed at the negotiators dress sense and it looked bad for team but the negotiators appealed to bandits needs and put them off guard. From an initial ransom of 1.5 Million credits for the uniform templates and another 1 million for Viiir, the negotiators settled the bandits ransom on just 800 Thousand credits for both. The bandits last reported they wanted to "open up their own fashion/dance bar! "

This quote from Advisor Augur:
quote:
"After tense negotiations the team managed to secure the release of Viiir and the ARK uniforms for 800,000 credits. At first the bandits tried to trick the negotiators by giving them a white dress, rather than the uniforms, but they saw through it..."
Happy to be free from the Fashion Bandits Viiir gave each negotiator a trenchcoat while ARK paid each of them 800 credits for a job well done. With tragedy averted at such a high cost, we all look forward to seeing these new ARK uniforms.
