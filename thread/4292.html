They were too popular
FunCom changed the game rules so that you could no longer buy Freedom Arms in shops. Also, you could no longer get them as loot. The only ones that still exist are the ones that were already in the hands of players. In some guilds, they have been handed down from character to character.

This change occurred shortly before the creation of RK2. So there are no Freedom Arms on RK2 at all.

I'm not sure if FunCom ever said exactly why the did this. But it seems the weapon was just too effective. Too many people were building their characters based on it. So FunCom just made it disappear.

Nah, he's talking about the Freedom Arms 3297, it's a pistol that does fire damage I believe, and uses energy ammo. It was the reported to be the highest damage weapon in the game at release, and was very common at most QLs. As a result, just about every character in the whole game was dual wielding FAs at one point. Funcom eventually modified the dual ranged skill necessary to dual them, so that cut them down to a 1 hand gun, at which point most people stopped using them for the most part (unless they already had them equipped). Since then, they've become more and more rare, although I still have several I think on one of my first gimped characters.

During one of the loot changes after the first month or so after go live, the FAs just stopped appearing for the most part. I still get one very rarely when I start a new character as a mission reward, usually a QL 1 or 2 or so.
