In game support: Unless you are stuck in a wall, ARKs and GMs are pretty much useless. Reimbursement for loss of items is non-existent. Additionally, if you find a bug and report it to the ARK or GM they tell you do use the bug reporting system - unfortunately, the bug reporting system has has a known bug where it randomly crashes or doesn't report the bug.

Out of game support: I have sent about a dozen emails to funcom regarding various issues from account problems, to bugs, to technical problems. I have received one reply regarding an account problem. The others gave me the auto-responder, but no one ever got back to me. Initially when I purchased the game it wouldn't install properly. So, I went to the funcom website and followed the link for technical support -> phone numbers. I called Norway from my home in the Detroit metro area just to have someone walk me through what I was doing wrong. I was told they would not help me, good bye. This is pretty piss poor.

Communication: The communication between funcom and the community has been lacking from the start. Lately, changes are being implemented with no playerbase input or notice. About 6 months ago, Funcom acknowledged its lack of communicaiton with the playerbase and introduced a team of "professional customer service people" to address those issues. There was a month where these boards were paid attention to by the company, since then they have pretty much been ignored.

Guidence in how to play: On release, Funcom and Cybex published a beginner's guide. However, most of the content in the guide was never implemented or wrong. It took funcom more than half a year to finally post the online beginners guide, and get ARKs into the newbie areas to answer questions.

Patches: There are bugs that have existed since release. The are known and acknowledge as known by funcom. They have not been fixed. Additionally, many the "fix" patches implemented to remedy deficiencies in certain professions were bugged, acknoweldged as bugged, and still haven't been fixed.

I have had more poor experiences than good experiences in dealing with funcom representatives ingame, via email, or over the phone. I still believe the best example of poor customer support are the repetative half-truths and misrepresentations by funcom about what we are to expect as customers from this product. Much content advertised on release was never implemented, and now seems as if it will never be implemented. Believe it or not, land control was a concept that was publicized by funcom pre-release, then placed on the boards as a future implementation. And now, we have to pay for it.

I have realized that if there is a problem with the game technically, I need to figure out how to fix it myself because funcom will not help. Customer Support
One of the more amusing parts of these kind of discussions, is that you will often find
that the same people that are screaming "I wont pay for a patch" and "Funcom is only after
our money", are usually the same people that whines about FC's lack of Customer Support.

Well... Get this: Customer Support (CS) is very expencive. Every minute spent on CS costs
the company money. And, yes, good CS will keep more customers happy than bad CS - the
equation however is: How much money can you spend on CS in order to keep these customers
happy vs. cutting CS costs and loosing a few customers?

The answer is rather simple actually. If the cost of keeping X customers happy exceeds X*13
bucks then it's prolly not worth it. (Well it's a bit more complicated than that, counting in for mouth to
mouth publicity and so forth, but I think you get my point.)

I bet that by raising the monthly fee to like 18 bucks, they could afford better CS. But are you guys
willing to pay that much more - this does not give you more bug fixing, patches or content -
merely better and more professional CS.

I have never had to contact Funcom CS - and I know for sure that I am not interested in paying more for
better CS for the few people who do so. (I would however have no problems whatsoever to pay like
20 bucks a month for more bug fixes, patches and content.)

So FunCom: An idea for you - 2 different subscription models:
1. Standard - the way it is today - 13 bucks
2. VIP - guaranteed CS, prioroty in petitions etc. 18-20 bucks

Point is, you get what you pay for. Some people seem to have highly unrealistic expectations in what
their 13 bucks a month is buying them.

And btw, Spooks And Borrace, this is meant as a general observasion, and not pointed directly at
you.
