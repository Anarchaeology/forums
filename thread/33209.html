Lev, Lupoh and the drug.. Sightee falls deeper...
Data of Synopsis: 20th June 2002 (Real World Date)


Sightsee was lost, he still hadnt managed to find the nanos that Lizor had requested days earlier. He found himself in the squalor that is the Omni Sewers; he walked down an ally, hoping it was a shortcut to the main City. Bins and rubbish bags were against the sides, one of the bags seemed to be moving, as Sight got closer he saw it was actually one of the unfortunates of Society. The man was large built and had blonde hair, he was unnaturally red and was having a violent fit, foam was coming out of his mouth and nostrils and his eyes were rolled up. He was dying and in relative silence, just a low moaning and the rustling of the rubbish bags could be heard. Sight immediately kneeled down to assist, he cringed as he started to hook away the foam and clogged up mucus from the mans mouth, tried to get it out of his nose. A hissing noise emanated from the mans throat, suddenly his eyes were staring at Sights, the noise and movement stopped, a tear rolled down the mans cheek, he was dead.
Damn! Sight shouted, he couldnt stand those pleading eyes looking at him, he closed the mans eyelids and looked away in shame. Thats when he noticed a small metal box, clutched in the mans hand, he opened it to find a few empty stim cases, but one was still full. That Colour! thought Sight as he recognised the distinctive metallic purple of that drug that Lupoh had shown him (Yixxil Part3).
Bastard! Sight said through gritted teeth. He took the box and covered up the body with his trench coat. A patrol would find the corpse, he was going to personally deal with this situation, and he needed to get the drug analysed. The police would (if they got hold of the drug) file it away and dismiss it until a citizen of worth fell victim to it, much as they had done nothing when the White Trash drug killed the down and outs a few Months ago.

Sight was disgusted and annoyed, he couldnt get the mans eyes out of his mind. His thoughts went to Ehrynn, could that have been her brother? He checked the description and was relieved that it wasnt him. He drank another swig of scotch, the bottle was already half empty and hed only been in Roompas Bar for a half-hour. He didnt know what he was going to do, he hoped the drink would have helped him to think with some clarity. He was glad at least that Mogdai, who happened to be seated at the booth opposite, was leaving him alone for once and behaving himself. Ehrynn came by and noticed Sight sitting in the corner, he looking quite tipsy.
Hi Sight.
Oh hi there Ehrynn. She noticed he wasnt his usual jolly self.
Go easy on the drink! She said as she sat down next to him. Sight didnt know whether to mention his discovery. While they talked he decided he would be honest (after all he promised to help, could be something that might be a lead to her brother).
I think theres a new drug either being tested or hitting the street.
Really Sight? She looked at him questionably.
I found a dead guy, a guy in the sewers district. And I found something that looked like what a Mocker showed me recently on the body I cant really say much, but Im sure its new, and could be trouble. Sight sighed and slammed the bottle down on the table, he stared into the muddy hue of the scotch and said, Aw, I hate all this knowing, I grow weary of it Nothing is as it seems.
Indeed She could tell that Sight seemed to be getting more and more depressed lately, better to change the subject than encourage his drunken ranting. Oh Sight, I have a new friend dropping by to meet you be charming, thats a good chap. She smiled. Sight was in no mood for pleasantries, and he was quite drunk as well. Whiskey brought out the sharpness in him, especially when he was in such a good mood.
What, be charming like this? Sight pointed a wavering finger at himself.
Just be a little more suave. Before Sight could complain, Charley sat down next to him, she was quite pretty, but the hood she was wearing, it shadowed her face, she had the look of a 'professional'. The waiter, a fellow by the name of Wiltor greeted them. Asking if they wished drinks, then moved on.
Nice outfit dear catsuit? Ehrynn asked.
Yes it is. Charley seemed the serious type to Sightsee. They all talked for a little while.

Levrivers entered the bar with his gang, after warping into the City (They were Clan). He casually walked past the booths looking for someone, some of the clientele gasped, some looked afraid. He stopped by Sights table.
Rivers! Said Ehrynn, surprised.
Dont worry, Im here on a business call today Ehrynn. Lev said and chuckled out loud, No robbing or stealing tonight Just a bit of the ol rough n tumble. Sight was confused, he was starting to sober up some real quick, Lev was armed and had his friends blocking the way out from Sights booth. Sight cursed himself, as he was unarmed, should anything happen hed just have to run for it. The waiter apprehensively spoke out to the gang, Clanner Please leave the building.
When were DONE Wiltor! Lev stated. Ehrynn got her gun out from her dress and stood up slowly, so that they would see that she was ready to act, should any trouble start?
Err.. Take a seat dude. Said Sight to Lev. But Levrivers didnt, and just stood there in front of Sight.
Now you see my guys with me?
Err Yea?
Theyre here to show you we mean what we say So listen up!
What!? Sight was shocked at Levs manner and was feeling his friendship for this man turning to dislike in an instant.
Now I dont wanna get all angry n stuff, or resort to violence, were cool guys, we think our suits look cool, without the need for the colour of blood on em. Lev laughed with obvious sarcasm. Ehrynn had now manoeuvred to Lev's left side, her knuckles were white from the grip she had on her Gun, Try anything and this unloads into your head. She raised the weapon.
Tell that to the 3 guys with me. Lev said without even looking at Ehrynn or acknowledging the weapon that she had brought to bear.
Now, a little Leet tells me youve been interfering in the business of Lupoh and Metalynx
What!? Sight couldnt believe that they could even know that he had a vial of the new drug. Whats that to you man? Sight said.
Well I heard through the grapevine that youve been trying ta annoy them in various ways.
Well dude, ya better check that its the same vine man! Sight shouted as Lev growled back to him. To Sights surprise, a familiar hissing voice shouted out Hey now Lev, SSSsss! I wouldnt want to sssnap your neck It was Mogdai, HE was speaking in defence of Sight? For a brief moment Sight had to stop himself from bursting out with laughter, and managed to bring back his serious look.
Im here to tell you to lay off Lupoh and Metalynx.
I dont like ya tone of voice Lev What the HELL has happened to ya? Sight said calmly.
Rivers is a mad dog, Sight. Ehrynn said. Mogdai got out his comms link and started dialling up.
Waddya gonna do Mogdai? Do your usual Yixxil trick and commit suicide on us, hehe? Quipped Lev. Its quite simple Sight, you know youve been interfering in the affairs of Lupoh and Metalynx, Lupohs kindly asked me to come down and give you a lil reminder to leave him, Metalynx and their personal business to themselves.
So, theyre scared of me huh? Sight was surprised, seemed they indeed thought he posed a threat to their operation. Lev held up a gun to Sights face, Sight flinched slightly, he was getting worried now.
No no, this is a friendly warning, they arent scared. Lev grinned evilly. The dead mans eyes popped up in Sights mind again.
Well, Im afraid it is my business when it involves people getting hurt from certain side effects shall we say.
If people get hurt from side effects, they shouldnt buy anything then, should they?
Guess you can call me frickking Robin Hood! Sight was pissed again.
This is a warning, my clients are trying to give you a hint, just make sure I dont have to come back to remind you again huh?
Hmmmm, Im a friend of Diamondcut, go check it out yourself Maybe the Mockers agenda is ta do with a trick on Diamond eh?
Lol, dont go throwing names about me, you know very well, me and Dave go way back! Shouted Lev, had Sight hit a nerve?
Well, all I know is that YOU have gone way back now Lev Back into that quagmire called Sh*t!
I hear Omni-Pol is arriving shortly, so Ill bid you farewell. Lets not make me have to come back huh? Lev slapped Sight on the back, as a friend would do.
Just inform Lupoh, I have nothing on him Its been a waste of your time Lev. Sight said as the gang walked casually towards the exit. Lev shouted, Then you have nothing to worry about!

Sight was visibly shaking after the encounter. He found out from Ehrynn that Lev was rumoured to have committed robbery and murder the night before Information that Sight hadnt heard of until now. All he knew right there and then was that he was going ever deeper into the darkness. A tangled web had been woven, when did it all start? thought Sight.

The main problem however, was the fact that he was now working as one of Ignis bodyguards (Ignis being the new manager of Reets Retreat). Lupoh and Metalynxs organisation (The Mockers) were contracted general security there too. Obviously the hadnt heard about the latest employee
