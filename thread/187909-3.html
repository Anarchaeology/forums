The com beeps loud across the room as he sighs finally enjoying himself, expecting Usia to show back in West Athens so he could embrace her. Yet all he sees is Don Carnage's message... as she has been "released", and that she was on "Omni-Tek" property.
One could imagine his situation here, he has lost his sister to one of the most flirtatious men in the world, a womanizer, and as some would consider a bastard in every aspect of the word. He and his canivving bunch now had Usia in their custody, Don said he was going to give her to the proper authorities, he was expecting to himself or Clan, how wrong he was. He misjudged, made a mistake in trusting this man too much, he had him right where he wanted him. Now imagine a few of the names that pop up when the words "Omni-Tek Authorities" comes to mind.
Imagine if you would a man that has been lied to, a man that has lost someone dear to him, for every org member to Zane was very dear. Imagine if you would thinking that everything was all squared away, and it was behind you. Some would have cried, some would have screamed. Not Zane, no. There was only one phrase that echoed through his mind... it dealt with payback. And Zane was already in a foul mood.
