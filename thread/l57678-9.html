This worries me
Though I've had fun playing AO with low-level chars, and haven't personally run into very many bugs with the new patches, it's obvious that there's something very wrong here.

Is this the same company that produced the brilliant and polished adventure game Longest Journey (recommended!)? Does not seem like it, too many things about AO smack of amateurishness and bad understaffing. The game is *beautiful*, I'll give it that, and like I said I still have fun with it. But...

It's obvious that Funcom's quality-assurance / testing process is either nonexistent, in the hands of incompetents, or has been taken over by marketing people. They released a big patch that hadn't been tested *at all* with characters over lvl 20 - or if it was, the testers did not do their job at all.

I like the general aims of these patches, i.e. balancing the game, making red mobs dangerous, and giving mobs more intelleigent. I'm also all for removing exploits and other questionable means that people have been using to boost their characters to use equipment ridiculously above their level. Good aims, hooray for that.

But this is not the way to do things, this sort of shotgun patching reeks of desperation, over-worked programmers, lack of good management/planning, or all of that.

I really do hope this game survives. Even though it's not a science-fiction game as opposed to a science-fantasy game (proof left to reader as fairly obvious), I still like the "feel" of the game world. It has promise. But Funcom has to shape up *now*, or this game will never survive. Oh, I'll hang on till my 3-month payment runs out, but there is no way I'm continuing past that if the support quality does not improve.

Oh, and Funcom: you really need to take an active part in these *official* board discussions. Yes, that will mean enduring some flames, but that's a minor irritation. We need discussion, not vague "press releases" as daily updates.
