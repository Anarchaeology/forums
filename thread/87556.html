The "specials" are:
Ranged: FlingShot, Burst, FullAuto, AimedShot
Melee: SneakAttack, Brawling, Dimach

These skills are required for the majority of weapons available. They are required for wielding so that special attack can be used with the weapon. They don't have any effect on the attack rating, only regulating the special's damage and/or recharge time.


If you take a look at this Premium Nova Flow Mk IV you can see that it requires 524 RangedEnergy and 785 AssaultRifle. This means (as you can see under the slot "Atk. Skills") that it's attack rating is calculated by using 66% AssaultRifle and 33% RangedEnergy.
This means that if you have 800 RE and 600 AR your attack rating (without any items or buffs) is 726. ((800*66)+(600*33))/100
