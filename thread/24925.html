Ouch bro
You raise all your skills equally? Thats exactly the perfect way to end up with a gimped character, choose one weapon type and stick with it. Would you rather be able to switch between 1hb, 2hb and 1he and do hypothetically 20 damage with each, or would you rather stick with one and be able to do 60 damage?

Those arent real numbers, its just hypothetical, but you get the picture, besides with weapons requiring so many different skills you will find out you will end up seriously underpowered.

I'd suggest 2hb Brawling, and Dimach as your only attack skills, Beams and Hammers rock. Plus as an enforcer you can easily increase your skill with these weapons using headcracker and strength and stamina buffs.
