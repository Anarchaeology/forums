The term "buff gun" exists for a reason. All the best ones are low-level or totally useless. The Galahad line, Pepper Pistols, Druids, Quartzs, O.E.T. Co. line, MTI SW500 Geyser, etc.

Very, very few actual guns have decent, working mods on them.

Also, nowadays, I don't think I wold consider the Krutt a very good weapon.

G-Staff and Kiddy raise Agility.
Cusions and the new Black Staff raise Stamina.
Cushion and Black Staff also raise Strength.
Immortal Katana, Compressed Blade, and Black Staff raises Sense.
Chesterfield Dining Chair, Brainchopper, and Mantis Predator Blade raise Psychic.
Brainchopper, Breathing Space, and Black Staff raises Int.

It's also important to realize that just because there are a lot of buff "guns", doesn't mean that they are available to all ranged users. Some require Full Auto, some require Pistol, some require AR, some require SMG, some require Ranged Energy, some require Rifle, etc...

-Jayde
