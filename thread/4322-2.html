I don't PvP much, but I just had to post a reply to Illu's post...

Calculating gear is actually a skill, because you have to balance implants, find the right kind of armor (i.e. elite sucks if you fight chem damage weapons), earn the money to buy this equipment and so on.

If you for some reason think you should be able to compete when you won't bother to spend time improving your char then...what are you saying? Should a level 50 soldier who spend 1 hour a week ingame be able to compete with a level 50 soldier who spends 3 hours a day working on his char? Really?

Same goes for FPS games, if you think you can just buy RtCW, power up, log on and win the game...you're wrong. To have skill you have to practise, to practise you have to play... The more you play, the better you get.

Skill is not just being able to point your cursor at the head of some other player, you have to have experience as well. Someone who knows about the weakness of elite armor would be able to use that to his advantage, an NT could use his chem-based DDs for example. Knowing what evades do is important, and making the right choice between raising evades or abilites could be a lifesaver. This knowledge is not something you get for free when buying the game, this is something you learn by playing. If you refuse to learn, you will loose.
