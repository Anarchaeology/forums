The period of time allowed on non-active accounts before the characters get deleted
I have been playing AO since day 1, however recently I have been hardly playing at all and my account is about to go non-active, so I am writing to say a few words about an issue I think is very important.
If you cancel your account and dont re-subscribe for three months, your characters get deleted (unless this has been changed and I don't know about it...), I think this period of time needs to be extended by quite a lot, and here is why:
I have a pretty high level Metaphysicist as my main character, I've obviously put a lot of time into developing him, but like many players (I have noticed...), when you reach a certain level things start to get a bit samey and basically, I am no longer interested in playing AO. However! Should there come a time when Funcom really sort things out and the storyline gets going PROPERLY, I might well want to return, and I dare say quite a few other long time players feel the same way. BUT, if say, in a years time I check on the progress of AO and find something interesting going on that I'd like to interact with, I am going to be seriously put off by the prospect of having to re-start with a brand new character and spend hours and hours (months, even) getting to a decent enough level to be able to do anything of significance, as I have already done with my main character. In fact I think I can safely say that I would definatly not come back if that was required.
BUT, if I could bring back my character who I've spent so much time developing then I'd be much more likely to return. The point of all this is simple: It benefits everyone, even including Funcom themselves as in the long run it may bring back old customers.
If you agree, let your opinion be known! Wouldn't it be cool a few years down the line when the story is in full swing, to have some of the original characters (and the players who were enthusiastic enough to be here from the start) from the beginning?
