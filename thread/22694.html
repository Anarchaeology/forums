LLTS and Melees
I am a fixer and I PvP, I am one of the few. I perfer to fight melee chars for obvious reasons, I mcuh faster then they are and I can run and shoot fairly well. I noticed the other day that an enforcer i was dueling was having a rather easy time hitting me. After I died I asked and returned tot he arena he told me that it was the Low Light Scope he had on. It increases his attack range on the beam he was using. Now unless I am stupid, and I'm not, the LLTS should not effect the range of a melee character. That takes away the sacrafice that ranged char make when using weapons. Guns get range, but they doo less damage. Swords/beams etc. do a ton of damage, but theoretically, you have to be les then a meter or so from your target. I think it is absurd and unfair when I am running circles around an enforcer in the arena and I am getting pummeled because he stands in the middle and has the range to hit me.

Another little problem I have is auto-facing. I can't turn it off. So whenever I stop to take a shot I turn ALL teh way around and shoot the guy. Then I have to turn all the way back around, or run at him. Neither option is beneficial because it just lets the guy get closer. Sometimes I like the auto-facing feature, but in PvP I hate it. I seem to remember an option in a previous patch to turn it off.

Maybe funcom can do something about this, but more importantly the LLTS issue. I am not sure if it works with the movement predictors but i would bet it does and that would give a melee a range equal to that of a sniper rifle.
