Crit debuff
After really thinking about it I don't want MA crit buffs or LLTS to be nerfed that's why I think the best solution is a crit debuff which could be used by all professions who invest in the right skills.
The crit debuff still focused on the MA profession could be the effect of one of those 2:
1. Special arrow
2. Special unarmed attack (main req MA skill)

Effects:
-1% chance to crit per 5QL of the special, lasting 1sec per QL and it would never lower the chance below the absolute min (3%).
Refresh time equals to the debuff duration and to make it interesting in team pvp make it area of effect.
The chance to resist it should be the same as it is now for other special arrows or unarmed attacks.

Funcom can easily implement it without changing anything else.
