OOC Need Omni enemy. (Revised)
Ok. To makes things a bit more clear and understanding I will go into a little more detail about my past and the story around it. Hopefully this will generate a bit more responce.
Psychogirl grew up on the outskirts of 4 holes. Her parents were know to be outspoken because they were trying to form an alliance between the clans and OT. Needless to say some powerful people did not like that. What I have planned as of yet is that somebody hired a small band of people to kill the whole family.
My parents and two brothers were spared by a reasonably quick death. (about 2 hours of pain and interrogation) After they had all died She had to bury them all. Afterall it increases the fear factor knowing what would happen to her if she didn't cooperate.
She did not have the pleasure of a quick death. She was interrogated and tortured for a week. After that any reasonble person would say what ever their captors wanted to hear. So that is what she eventually did.
The entire time she was led to belive that they were clanners. Probably to hide who they really were. She grew up hating clanners. She served with SPARTA for the majority of her career. Then one day she found out some disturbing info from one of her oldest and best contacts that she has lived a lie. She was serving the very people that killed her family.
I hope this helps. It looks like I have Ognom and Prezidential so far. I would like 1 or 2 more if possible.
