quote:
Originally posted by Glarawyn / Lilredhot
I think the suggested percentages might be a bit high.

Placeholders are on what, 20 min spawn?

I think 6 spawns should bring you a 50% chance. So after two hours, you have a 50/50 chance of getting the unique to spawn, if you haven't gotten lucky before then.

With a 50/50 chance, you would have to be very unlucky not to get the mob to spawn in hour 3. And given that you had 6 chances at low odds in the previous two hours, it's seems unlikely that you'll have to go to more than 2 hours before seeing a spawn.

At least when you get better odds when the placeholder dies you have the satisfaction of knowing that you earned something.

Tweaking the system like this may require tweaking the loot tables on some things, but that's ok by me.
Sometimes the placeholders can take 15 minutes or more to kill.

The other night at Ljutor we killed 6 or 7 Polymorphed Lunatics without an appearence by Ljutor, over a span of 3.5 hours.

Last night at Ljutor we killed 3 lunatics before Ljutor spawned, and then Ljutor spawned next.

Three and a half hours of killing placeholders is a bit much. If they could put it on a fixed 1 in 5 system so that you KNOW the boss will spawn after you've killed 5 placeholders I could handle that much more easily.

Also, shortening the time between spawns would help, again it takes about 20 minutes between Ljutor/PMN spawns, a rate of 5 or 10 minutes would speed things up.

Keeping a group together for 2 team missions is close to impossible, and asking people to stay in a campground for 3 or 4 hours is insane!
