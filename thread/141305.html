Soleet
After receiving a tip from LordFoul2000, Director of NanoTek, that the criminal wanted for the explosion at Friday's Omni-Tek Rally, one Mr. Alexander "Lehx" Western was seen at Rompa Bar, Division 9 RSGE's President, Hershel "Kasimir" Jurik, and General Monte "Daetona" Jr. reported to the notorious club to apprehend him.

Upon arriving, the two Officers began to speak with him, at first assuming a casual and reasonable manner. As to be expected, the suspect fled. Taking the form of a Reet, he made his attempt at an escape, but Division 9 cruisers where quick to arrive, and tail Lehx to the middle of Omni Entertainment.

Surrounding the area, President Kasimir, and General Daetona had the cruisers form a circle around the immediate area, to prevent Lehx from fleeing again, and demanded that he lay on the ground and surrender.

Again, as to be expected, the suspect was uncooperative, and injected himself with a nano-virus that would detonate when mixed with enough oxygen, and made active by movement.

Ordered to lie down on the ground again, he did not comply, and made a move to escape. Now the oxygen in his bloodstream would be active, and detonation would commence very shortly.

Diving over one of the cruisers, Lehx rolled down it's hood, and headed east. President Kasimir followed at a distance, and General Daetona raced to catch up to the suspect.

Pulling a Tac "Breathing Space" Tazer from his side, General Daetona sent a shock into Lehx, making him halt his running. When he failed to bring him to the ground, he delivered another taze into the suspect, but it was too late.

With a final word, Lehx's body detonated, and an outward explosion damaged much of the street in the north-east section of Omni-Entertainment.

Leaping into the air, General Daetona landed, and positioned himself against a nearby wall, blocking the brunt of the explosion. President Kasimir was already at a safe distance.

But, the chase was far from over ...



Immediately searching the Omni-Tek Mainframe, they uncovered the last Inssurance Terminal Lehx had accessed, and received the location of the Reclaim Booth he would return too.

Proceeding to said Reclaim Booth, the two Niners found him sitting in front, collecting his belongings. Ordering him once more to lay on the ground and surrender, again he was uncooperative, and whispered something into his com-link.

Moments later, a young Agent by the name of "Serter" arrived and prevented Division 9 RSGE Personel from apprehending Lehx. Again, he ran, and was tailed, this time to Rome Blue.

Ducking into an apartment building in one of Rome's many backyards, he escaped. His cohort Serter, remained outside, where General Daetona engaged him in a brief conversation. Making sure he was aware that he was now guilty of aiding and abetting a known and wanted criminal. Then left.

End Report............

Daetona
Soleet
"Well, I'm at ICC, if you think you can catch me..." Lehx said over General Daetona's com-link.


"Word of advice, my traitorous friend... When you've got your head in the Lion's mouth, don't step on his balls." Daetona replied, and lept into his Adaga.

Crusing through the city's skyline, Daetona sped for the ICC Headquarters here on Rubi-Ka. Placing a quick call to Division 9 RSGE Headquarters for back-up, he nodded to himself.

Arriving moments later, he slid the hatch back of his vehicle, and dropped down to the ground in front of Lehx. Rising slowly, he motioned to lehx with a casual nod.

"So, am I arrested?" Lehx snickered.

"Shut your trap, and get on the ground." Daetona ordered the criminal.

Chuckeling, Lehx complied, much to the shock of Daetona who had never known him to be so willing to surrender.

Wasting no time, a set of Carbonum Handcuffs where slapped on Lehx's wrists, and he was patted down for anything he might use to try and escape. Removing a lockpick, and three pistols, Daetona let them drop to the gound, and kicked them away from reach.

Then, a Division 9 RSGE Cruiser arrived, and Private First Class Arlynne of StratOps exited. Saluting General Daetona, she greeted him.

At this point, Daetona now standing behind Lehx, pricked him in the neck with something sharp.

"What the...!? " Lehx blurted.

"PFC Arlynne, I need you to deliver this to InternOps HQ immediately." Daetona requested, a sly grin forming over his lips. "And travel swiftly..."

"Roger that Sir!" Arlynne responded, and saluted before heading out on her assignment in the Cruiser.

Returning his sight to Lehx, Daetona placed his foot on him to keep him on the ground.

"Aren't we curious as to what that was, Lehx?" He mockingly said.

Nodding, Lehx struggled to get out from under Daetona's heel.

"It's a simple device really..." He went on. " What it does is takes a sample of your DNA. That sample is then taken to InternOps HQ, and run through an Inssurance Terminal. The machine thinks it's you insuring from the DNA pattern, and over-writes your previous scanning. The next time you die, you go straight to jail... do not pass go... do not collect two-hundred credits."

"Son of a ..." Lehx growled, and struggled more to free himself.

"Yes, that I am... " Daetona again mocked. " So, we're just going to sit here and wait for that little gizmo to reach InternOps HQ, and then you'll have a nice cozy cell to rot in for what you've done."

Twisting around, Lehx managed to struggle free from Daetona's boot, and ran franticly, panicing around ICC Headquarters. Following him around, he was quickly brought back to the ground with a kick to the knee. At this point, a small crowd had gathered of other Bounty-Hunters wishing to see the criminal brought down.

"So, you're going to kill me then?" Lehx inquired.

"Only to send you to InternOps HQ" Daetona answered. "After that, your inssurance is wiped so suicide is NOT an option of escape. So I'd be careful if I where you, and comply with any questions they ask."

Daetona's HUD flashed with text, and he glanced down and smiled. " Assignment Complete, Sir. ~ PFC Arlynne "

"Well Lehx... All is set and ready. Any last words?" Daetona asked.

"Yeah... Frag You !!" Lehx growled.

Smirking, Daetona reached down, and with a single twist, snapped Lehx's neck, and dropped his lifeless body to the ground.

Immediately contacting InternOps HQ, Daetona inquired as to the location of Alexander "Lehx" Western.

"We've got him General. Great job!" A voice came back over his Com-link.

"I'll be in shortly to question him... Keep an eye on him until I get there." Daetona responded, then clicked off his com and called for his Adaga to meet him.


___________________________________

Later that afternoon....


Entering InternOps HQ, Daetona passed through the series of security checks, and ID confirmations, and made his way to the brigg. There he met with the Guards in charge of keeping Alexander "Lehx" Western secure and safe. Handing them a couple bottles of Fresh Tomb Water, he said they can go take a break, and was left alone with Lehx.

Opening his own bottle of Water, Daetona smirked at the prisoner, who only returned a sneer.

"So... you want to tell me who hired you to set off that explosion at the rally, Lehx?" Daetona inquired.

"Go suck a Leet!" Lehx responded harshly. "I ain't givin up mah man to you..."

"As you wish..." Daetona now grinned malevolently at Lehx, and threw some of his water on him. "We'll do it the hard way..."

Daetona's hands charged with the power of the Lightning Crane, and crackled with enormous amounts of electricity. Lowering his hands slowly to the large puddle of water covering Lehx and the floor, his grin remained.

"Oh @#$% Man !! You can't do this !!" Lehx cried out, now sweating up a storm.

Before his hand reached the puddle, Daetona's com beeped.

"General Daetona here." He answered.

Nodding a few times, he sighed.

"Right away... No, just sit tight Serter, I'll be right there." He went on. "Well, if Shadowgrider is about to stab you in the back, we'll beat him to the punch."

Turning his attentions back to Lehx, Daetona scowled.

"Saved by the bell, bub... For the moment." He warned. "I'll be back soon... and I suggest you think about spilling your guts when I return. At least with us, you have a chance... Duster, I doubt, will not be so willing to cooperate."

Daetona left the cell-block, as the two armed guards returned from there break, and looked puzzled by the spill of water on the floor.
