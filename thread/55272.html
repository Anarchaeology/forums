I know that several of the eco-friendly groups on Rubi-Ka have grown concerned over this and are investigating. Desert Winds (www.desert-winds.org), which I am a member of, is surveying the current weather situation in hopes of finding a cause.

The last DW report, which was recently declassified, is that we are also receiving some investigative cooperation from Independant Rubi-Kans, Unity of the Rose, and NanoMage Liberation Front. I have not yet seen their findings.

I personally have a few theories regarding the strange fog.
-It may be the result of Notum depletion or a byproduct of Notum mining on Rubi-ka. The effects of the notum mining operations on Rubi-ka are not well known at this time, and it most certainly has a negative impact on the health of the planet.
-It may be caused by some unusual activity by the Medusa colonies. We have noticed a trend that the fog appears on the eastern zones, and is heavily concentrated in the Artery areas. We know that this is the home of the unusual Medusa's.
-Since we haven't yet concuded that the 'fog' has an insidious origin, we cannot -yet- rule out the possibility that it is simply the result of the natural evolution of our young ecosystem.
