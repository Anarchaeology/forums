Searching for Tyrianno after the firebombing of Sanctuary Centre
She moved efficiently about her dorm room, packing her two well worn travel containers. Her roommate, Kibray, watched her with a look on her face that wavered between concern and disapproval. At this moment disapproval had won out.

"You can't be serious about leaving the university and taking a position with Omni-Tek on Rubi-Ka; you are simply not thinking this through." Marylynn Teders, 'Ehrynn', to anyone who knew her, continued folding her clothes as she turned to Kibray and repeated the same explanation she had given everyone else.

"My little brother Tyrianno has been missing for a number of weeks now. The last thing I heard from him was that he had gotten into some new terrible drug. White Bash or White Smash, maybe it was White Trash, I'm not sure what it is called; it doesn't matter. He was getting some help at rehab centre run by a Dr. Emmet Gray in a terrible sounding place called Omni Sewers. Now nothing, silence for weeks. The research I've done about recent events in the Omni controlled sectors of the planet points to a place named the Sanctuary Centre being firebombed about the same time 'Anno' stopped communicating with me. He has never gone a whole week without sending me a message, since he ran off to find adventure on Rubi-Ka. I can't just sit around here any longer and wonder what has happened to him. So, I'm going there to search for him. Now, why is this so hard for everyone to understand?"

Kibray softened her tone. "Ehrynn, there are organizations better equipped to find your brother than you are just now. Let them handle it. Besides, you still have almost two years of medical training left to complete. I can't see you as some glorified field medic or medical technician working for Omni-Tek. At least go to your parents for help; let them fund this crazy expedition you are so determined to go on."

Ehrynn sighed. "You know perfectly well I can't go to them about 'Anno.' They wont lift a finger to help since he ran off to Rubi-Ka without their permission. They are too old fashioned, too hardheaded, on that score. There is only me and I am going tonight."

Kibray walked over, gave her friend a hug, and helped her finish packing for the trip to Rubi-Ka.

Searching for Tyrianno
The first few days on Rubi-Ka seemed hopeful and the inevitable delays in seraching for Tyrianno understandable. Now, Ehrynn was starting to believe that Kibray was right... she might not be up to the task of finding her brother. As a result, she decided to take her search in a new direction and access the Rubi-Ka Personal ADs.

The following message was posted on the RK-1 personals channel:

Desperate sister seeks missing brother. Last know Location Sanctuary Centre. If you have information about SC residents after the firebombing or Tyrianno, please contact Ehrynn over Omni Net or the User CP. Reward for useful information.

New Directions for Searching
Sitting at the reception desk at the Omni-Tek clinic Ehrynn pondered the information she had received.

The first was a hopeful lead from an individual known as Savoy. A quick check of the clinic data banks, which she knew was a terribly unethical act, revealed among other interesting items of information that the individual was a member in good standing of Omni. It also listed members of an organization, the South Rubi-Ka Society, as next of kin. She tucked that bit away in the back of her mind and decided to contact Savoy as soon as possible.

The other lead was as disturbing as Savoy's was hopeful. Ehrynn rotated the jar with the finger slowly in her left hand. It had been waiting for her when she arrived at the clinic yesterday. The following note was attached:

"The individual you seek is indebted to my employer to the tune of 100,000 credits. Although this is not a particuarly large debt, my employer always takes the position that any amount owed much be recovered in some form. The item you have received is an example of how some debts are paid off, at least a portion of the debt to be more precise.

Since we seek the same individual, it is in your interest to meet with me to discuss other methods for repayment. Instructions will follow."
