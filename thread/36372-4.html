Ah... The heat of the summertime... Although sometimes i find it hard to decide is it hotter here inside my home or hotter here in the forums. Heh...

Most of the unfairness of the current PvP system comes from the lack of objectives of it. Nat and Naraya for example pointed out the PvP title system in its current form: you kill, you score. (Peepee extension? Just hilarious way to put it.. =) You zone in alone or with friends, tab the closest prey you can find and duke away... Thats it. The other party might fall or flee but thats that - nothing accomplished. Well.. The players accomplished excitement and something diffirent activity besides leveling but thats that.

As I said, i see the coming ability to capture and hold territory a very positive and refreshing thing and it will add more depth to the current PvP system as is but more would not hurt. Some months ago i had this weird concept of PvP oriented missions buzzing in my mind and i think this outta be the proper place to toss it out for your reading... And note, its rude, not thought propelly and very unpolished but here goes:

Most of the player base know the 'patrol' -concept of many flight- and space simms. My thought was that this concept could be relativelly easy converted into AO's world too. You get a team and will be assigned patrol points inside for example 2H0 and must patrol through these 'waypoints' to accomplish the mission. The mission would be time triggered and should not last no longer that the average solo / team mission. Rewards from these missions would be cash and naturally tokens, maybe even a title point. Many variations about missions like this could me made: zone patrols, outpost patrols and such. Missions like these would instantly give one meaning for being inside a PvP zone.

If one'd push this concept even harder, you could also have some countering missions: attack outpost, eliminate patrols etc. but if so these concepts would be really close to the original Capture territory -concept so these would not be neccessarilly wise to implement.

PvP points for ''privates'' are different for those given to the ''Freshman'' line of titles.

The private-line titles is a guard-type-NPC killing point, which makes the Guard-Killer a valid PvP target until the guard-killer has been slayed. After that his/her status is returned to normal.

This promotes a storyline, because as soon as a player kills a guard, he/she has decided to fight for their side (wether Omni or Clan) directly, thus becoming a soldier.

If one tries to exploit this system with Damage Shields (''The guard killed himself by my DS, it ain't my fault'' mentality) then the person who had the DS on will get the Guard-Killer point when the guard DIES, or he/she should either:

1. Not enter the opposing sides territory to prevent guards

2. Just run through like forest gump to the nearest grid or whompa.

3. Shoot his/her way through, becoming a soldier him/herself.

Take 2HO as an example.

2HO is Omni. Why should Clanners have the right to just freely stroll around in there?

If a player does manage to kill off all the guards, the player him/herself becomes a guard of that specific place, protecting the other clanners from Omni-Tek soldiers ( Titled players ).

These Titled Players have the right to attack the Guard- Killers first, because the Guard-Killers are Guard-Killers, thus the titled players will not loose PvP points and will infact GAIN some.

The Guard Killers can ofcourse kill the titled players if their good enuff and gain PvP points then, giving them the Freshman-line of titles, which in turn overwrites the guard-killer titles.
