made some scripts to help with bs load lag
there was recently a thread on here about changing a couple of the graphics settings via the chatbox or with a script so i went ahead and made a few to do it automagically at the press of a button and decided to let people download them if they want em since some people have trouble or are just too lazy

click here to download, the scripts come in the following flavors
viewdistance and charactter view distance set to the % of and in a script named the same... 10, 20, 25, 50, 60, 70, 75, 100. as well as a "high", "med", and "low" setting which are 90/90, 60/60, and 25/10 (since the crowds in BS:CoH are worse than the buildings)

all you have to do to use them is unzip the scripts to [i\C:\program files\funcom\anarchyonline\scripts[/i] or where ever you installed AO to, and type /scriptname (i.e. /10 or /med) or to make a clickable macro to stick on your hotkeys type /macro macroname /nameofscript such as /macro high /high and drop the icon it makes on your hotkeys.
