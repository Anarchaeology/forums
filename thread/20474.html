14.2 A Considered Assessment
I was thinking....

When I started AO I created a Enforcer, took her to L57.

I got bored and then created a Soldier took her to L42.

Both times I found that my characters while they had potential to be fun were largely gimped. Primarily becuase I didn't overequip by the standard definition and they were both (particularly the soldier) virtually unsoloable.

S, I stepped back and tried to understand the way the game operated. I learn the importance of equipment or getting the most powerful weapons and armour on to be viable, and then beyong that to actually be superior...to be powerful.

And indeed my Trader was just that powerful, independent, and well equipped.

It should be considered with some irony the overwhelming impact atht the proposed changes of 14.2 will have on gameplay.

and...it just hit me....

I recently went back and played my 42 Soldier. She used an OT Escorpio. This si an assault rifle/SMG hybrid with Burst and Full Auto. As a result it is a weapon that cannot be overequipped and the Assault Rifle component and Full Auo requirement are the same (no benefit from a wrangle).

The L42 soldier suprised me how weak she was in retrospect and compared to my now L93 Trader. But this hit but like a Atrox's support beam. After 14.2 my WEAKEST character will relatively be my strongest, and my STRONGEST character a L93 Trader with a QL200 SA Hoem Defender will relatively become by weakest.

This will be a VERY dangerous change to game balance. I have started playing my soldier again
