New Roleplaying Org (RK2) - Squadron X. Many fun features, please read!
Hi there everyone.

As I am sure you are all into Roleplay (If not then why are you in this forum), I would like to announce the creation of a new CLAN (RK2) Roleplay Org:

Squadron X

I am trying to make this org one of the "coolest" orgs around. Parades will be regular, and uniform (including a weapon just for show) will be issued to everyone who joins.

It would be good to get a range of people to join, all I ask is that you are at least level 10, and that you take it seriously...
I want the parades to look smart.
Im sure you would all be proud to march through Tir in formation knowing that you look smart and well... Uber.

Of course, tower battles will come into play just like any other org.

And ranks will be taken seriously, you should respect anyone of a higher rank.

Its not going to be crazy... Its going to be cool - just remember thats its fun
And try to make a good character personality - if your an atrox you aint the sharpest tool in the shed etc

Remember - look smart, look cool, look uber




Please send a tell to "Halouk" if you want to join
Or send an email to Zoiderland@hotmail.com
Any feedback about the ideas in this post are appreciated... no flaming please though, even if you dont like the ideas leave it open for people who will enjoy it please.

Laters all
Halouk
