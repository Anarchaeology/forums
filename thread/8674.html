The Pvp rules are bull****
Thought I would go to Stret East Bank for some missions. Gridded in and watched my timer ticking off. With 3-5 seconds left I got rooted by some grey ass. Um, what is the damn point of the grace period?

I'm in my car, so as you know I cannot get out of it to defend myself. Just have to sit there and be attacked. Finally, broke the hold, but it was too late.

That was real gamemanship. I called the guy a "****er" because thats what all you grid campers are. It is not Pvp, but ambush the player in his car who is defenseless. I don't think this is what people had in mind for player combat.

My point, nothing has changed at all in regard to grid camping. Funcom has made it worse by altering the use of cars, making them deathtraps. The 15 seconds should be 30 or more. This really pisses me off. You want to fight me, lets go to the arena. You cowards are pathetic.

For those of you who will call me a whiner, please articulate why, otherwise stuff it.
