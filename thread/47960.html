Pvp suggestion
I'm not sure exactly what FC has planned for Land control, or anything else PVP related, but strongly suggest that majority of the "battle zones" be moved indoors.."mission style".

1) If battle zones are moved indoors,and designed properly, they can lower the polygon count and as a by product lower the lag ..making pvp a bit more fun.

2) It would also be possible to filter the indoor zones to let only players within a certain level range in, something outdoor fighting can't do.It would also be possible to limit how many players of each side entered the battlezone at any one time-making for a more sporting skirmish.

3)Indoor "missions" is something unique to AO.It provide an edge to pvp game mechanics that is difficult for rival MMROPG's to duplicate(should it be successful of course).

4)Indoor maps can have a multitude of nifty corners to set ambushes on opposing players.It can have choke points and kill zones---adding strategy to PVP.( as opposed to simply running around trading blows at some sand pit).

5) Indoor maps, with player number limits , are the mainstay of many online pvp games.It is a proven , tested design model for online pvp for a LOT of titles.

6) Indoor maps lessen the chances of non-consent pvp(Ie someone simply trying to travel the zone to some mission being ganked or being killed by a grid camper).This will lessen headaches for a lot of people in the playerbase.

7) PVP oriented players will have an easier time finding other players to fight.

I also know that there are caveats to this..increased development time But I think this might be good for a game like AO. Comments welcome.
