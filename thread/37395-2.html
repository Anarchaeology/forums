My thoughts...
/me looks so angry.

I love Rompas. I knew it from my first night there that it would be my favorite bar in Rubi-Ka. It pains me to say that ever since the night Thyme's pajama party was busted by a bunch of green-named Pol-Heads, I have not been back. This issue opens up old wounds for me (See my article at http://www.anarchy-online.com/conten...articles/2961L). I wish there were good clubs in clan territory, I really do. I know that the clan would never be this unwelcoming. As it stands, I now only have 2 bars to go to in neutral territory .. and this is putting a real crimp on my lifestyle .. (and my enjoyment of the game).

[OOC]

Now I have to go OOC. It is appauling to me that one persons utter lack of creativity and respect for the rights of others to enjoy a game continue to have such a direct impact on how others live their lives on Rubi-Ka. This world is SO dead without the entertainment and roleplaying organizations offering a little bit of flavor to this world. The problem is that we understand that players are players. We want EVERYONE to have a good time. We don't descriminate. And for that we are forced to endure these types of frustrations. I know just how Aktrez feels.

Meister says that we shouldn't tell him how to roleplay. Well, I think roleplaying is a personal matter, and if it makes you happy, do it. But when it interferes with the happiness of scores of other people, then you are open to criticism.

The truth of the matter is that good roleplaying typically REQUIRES conflict and willing participation. The best way to establish a context for good roleplaying are for PLAYERS to coordinate out of game. When OmniPol rushes in, disrupting a party .. many hours of planning on the part of those running the party go to waste. Thats just not fair. The problem I have seen is that there are those who run Omni organizations who have become desensitised against any consideration for the rights of the PLAYERS who control clan characters. If you want to roleplay a conflict with a clan guild, try contacting the leader of the guild out of game to seek a willingness to participate in a roleplaying scenario with you. Do not thrust your mindless hate against us without any consideration for our right to enjoy this game. Hate is not roleplaying. And when it is one-sided, it feels abusive.

Hate is also infectious. I have many friends in an Omni guild whose leader has said that the guild members must stop befriending clansmen out of concern that the guild would not be taken seriously by other Omni roleplaying guilds. Sound familiar? Sounds like a party line to me .. I'm glad that Aktrez has greater strength of character than that.

The last comment I wish to make is that I am baffled by the fact that these Omni 'roleplaying' guilds are trying so hard to rid their territory of clansmen. How are we supposed to roleplay with one another in an environment where we are being discouraged from getting together and interacting? Doesn't it make sense that a Rompa bar that serves the clan offers greater enjoyment to those who play Omni who actually want to roleplay with the clan? And by roleplay I mean a diversification of interaction: like civil talks of politics, a cross-faction love afair, war stories. Not the single minded: "You clan. You evil. Must leave or I kill you now." type of roleplaying.

I am sorry if I offended anyone with this post. I normally try to be more diplomatic, but this is an issue that resonates strongly with me.

[/OOC]

hmmmm
There is a lot of talk about what RP can be allowed/encouraged etc.

IMNSHO the reason for this is that RP is about consensual reality, and Funcom have decided not to sanction anything (they couldn't the no. of players is too big anyway), and therefore establishing consensual reality is going to be a jockeying for position.

I also accept Szentasha's points about courtesy. And that it is possible to play political or non-politcal groups.

However the one consensus reality there is is the world background. Faulty and regularly broken as they are, the Tir Accords still stand. Clan citizens should stay out of Omni territory if they believe in the accords. If they don't they go where they like.

Therefore:
So long as Meister is only RPing that he intends to get Aktrez out of a job - fair play to him - enjoy the RP Aktrez - why complain?
If a clan group decides to RP IN omni territory - they should expect any consequences that make sense according to the world background (they're breaking the Corporation Law) therefore they can't complain when RP that might not be the type they like ensues as a result - the risk is obvious according to the only consensual reality we have.
Likewise - If you can get more clanners in the Omni clubs having fun than you can OT guards patrolling - then hey! thats great.
And as far as tabing to recognise people - I chose to play that as a function of my HUD computer which allows comms and identifiers and maps - seems to fit with a futuristic world - I'm quite happy if some of you chose to see it as not there for RP purposes - in character I'll just assume you have a different HUD model - but don't tell me I can't - thats imposing too far on a consensus reality which you cannot control.

OOC coordination between groups can go a long way towards making this stuff easier, as Szen suggests. But nobody should be obliged to do that coordinating before they begin to RP - everybody RPing should simply go into it with the attitude that they are prepared to take whatever comes - if you aren't able to cope with that, you should consider finding a closed world to play in rather than an open one like this one.

In simple terms - don't assume any rights or priveleges because of the time you put in. Don't expect or require courtesy, but give it whereever you can. By diminishin our expectations of this nature - everything becomes less of a big deal, and we can get on with enjoying the RP.
