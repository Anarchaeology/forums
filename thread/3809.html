Overequipping is one of AO's biggest problems. Some professions can overequip, others cannot. Funcom doesn't like giving away something for nothing, so they have to respond to the overequipping. Their response has been to up the health and such of the MOBs to match the overequipped damage that is being done to them. The result is that those professions that are not overequipped then have a lot of trouble against those MOBs.

And allowing the overequipping of nanos does not solve the problem. Can you imagine everyone around Q100 running around with Q150-200 nanos? It is already to the point where players are as equipped as they will ever get around Q125. And then they complain because there is nothing left to work toward. To add nanos to that mix will solidify the post-Q125 boredom that has stricken so many players.

Institute skill checks for all weapons and all items when 'used'. Overequipped armor is less unbalancing because it doesn't make as much difference as weapons and it is at least available to all classes.

If it is necessary to have checks on absolutely everything, then make it that no item can be used or equipped if it has a QL that is more than character's level+X.
