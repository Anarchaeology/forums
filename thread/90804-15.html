OK. I admit it, I didnt want to wade through the 15 pages of posts to see if this had been mentioned.

I have been gone from AO since Notum wars came out, and have recently came back on. I just could not stay away. So, given that, I am not sure if this is still valid.

Create New Clan Char-

Level 1-5: Collect Rollerrat tails, when you get 10, go see the Roller Rat Catcher outside of Athens. Do the missions, and sell him back your reward armor. You need about 5K credits.

Go to newbie shop and buy 2 switch side cards. (DO NOT SWITCH SIDES YET). Buy a Clan card and a Omni card.

From Athens, goto Tir, then ICC.

Switch sides to Omni.

Enter omni trade, goto the clothing guy and buy a complete Med-suit set. If he does not have a complete set, Whompah to Omni-Ent and Omni-RGB to find them.

Go back to ICC, switch sides back to Clan.

Goto Tir.

Offer the Complete med suit.

Now.... Most people will offer you like 50K, politely decline. There are some players who need it badly and will pay you 300K.

Take that 300K and buy 2 more switch side cards and 6 backpacks. Go back to ICC, switch sides and go fill up your backpacks with Complete Omni-med suits.

Head back to ICC, switch sides, goto Tir and sell them for 300K per. At this point you can dicker on prices.

I think 15 complete suits will fit in 6 backpacks. You do the math.

I had a level 5 char at 5 mil in less than 4 hours with this technique.

YMMV.
