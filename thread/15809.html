TRAP, TRAPS and more TRAPS!!!
Crosspost from Fixer board.

Traps need to pose a real threat. As it is now it would take longer to detect and disarm a trap than it would to simply heal up after it goes off. I realize that you just cant kill a player outright but you can make their life a little harder. In addition to providing a reason to invest time and resources into disarming traps, increasing the effects of traps will add variety to the often boring and repetitive missions that we have today.


New types of traps
  • Poison gas: Area of Effect DoT
  • Alarm: Aggro radius of mobs increases. Mobs will roam more.
  • Explosion: 80% damage to everyone in area. Items in chest destroyed. Chance to alert nearby mobs.
  • Nano bomb: Just about any debuf in the game.
  • Lockdown: Main exit of dungeon will be locked with a very high QL lock for a period of time. Random doors in mission will also become locked.
  • Call for help: Roaming high level mob will spawn in dungeon
  • Teleporter: Teleport part of team to a random location in dungeon
  • Lights out: Lights in dungeon will be dimmed. All attack ranges are decreased and a chance to lose you target after it has been acquired will occur for a period of time.
  • Mob retreat: new dungeon will be spawned elsewhere and mission item will be moved there. Current mission icon and keys will now work for new mission location.
  • Reinforcements on way: Similar to call for help but more mobs will spawn. The catch is that they are on the way and are not there yet. Very low level mobs will spawn first but over time they will get tougher. Players must complete mission and get out before mission becomes impossible.
  • Self destruct: A countdown is started. When it reaches zero the mission will terminate and kill everything still in it.


Traps need to be put on more than just chests. The reason is simple. You can never make a trap do enough damage to kill a player. As a result, any penalty for a trap can be over come by killing all the mobs in a mission first then opening all the chests. Traps need to be on doors or just hidden in a hallway so that they cant be avoided. Any trap on a chest has to be able to destroy the contents of the chest if activated.

Id also like to see clues given to the location of traps in a mission. For example, players could find notes on mobs that give an approximate area for a real nasty trap. The chance to find the note should be based on the perception skill of the person searching it. A mob could be search by anyone even if it was already looted. By finding clues to the location of traps players wont have to repetitively search the entire mission to find them.

The idea of setting off a trap is that you will have to change the way you do the mission. You might have to speed up because it will be destroyed (Self Destruct) or you will have to regroup your party if some of them have been teleported away or even travel to another mission (Mob Retreat)

The loot should be increased if you pick a mission that has a lot of traps to compensate for the extra effort. Id also like to see the chance to spot or disarm a trap to be a one shot attempt. I dont want people to have to search for or attempt to disarm 10 times to be successful. Either you have the skill to do the task or you dont. Extra tries wont help you.
