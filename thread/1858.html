Mean level of eq carried by player = players level.
An idea.

Over-equipping is a problem because it consequently makes the level of the player an unreliable measure of his/her real power.

Ok. Well then don't use the level of the player to measure the power, use the equipment levels.

Thus that over-equipped lvl30 freak who won't level will be pitted against equally strong lvl 60 players.

And that same lvl30 freak won't be able to walz through a 100% mission because his 100% mission will be adjusted to his real power.

Naturally there should still be the a margin both ways, both in difficulty of missions and PvP attack range. But that margin could be brought to serve it's original intent which was voided by over-equipping.

Also, over-equipping would thus still be fun because it would be an alternative way to advance your character to power-leveling, but still retaining the balance of the game.

Am I missing something?
