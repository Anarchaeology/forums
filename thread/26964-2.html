Guard Killers
Ok, I dont like the fact that Omni People kill Omni Guards. Or Clan Killing Clan or Neutral killing Neutral for that matter.
(<RP Mode>Though the clanners can wipe eachother out for all I care, tis their mentality. </RP Mode>)

A Group of us Omni went hunting Clan Guards in Agean once and found that we didnt get any XP for them. That right there put an end to our hunting party.
I know in the past XP was given for all guards, then they got rid of it because people were killing their own guards. I thought it was a good idea you couldent get xp for killing your 'protectors' but after that hunting party I thought it was rather stupid that you couldent get xp for killing 'enemy protectors'.
Killing Neutral Guards shouldent get ANYBODY xp in my opinon but an Omni killing Clan, or Clan killing Omni.... Hell Ya!

As far as player assistance. I to would want to help my guards out if someone is attacking them. They can attack the person, the person can attack them, why cant we attack them? At LEAST put them at 25% range. So if a clanner hops into Omni-Trade and picks off a shopkeeper someone can defend the helpless merchant.
I can see how Same Side could be disallowed though. The 'cops' dont want civilians getting involved in martial disputes.

Penalties: As far as penalties for killing your own guards...
* Neutrals really shouldent have a penalty for killing their own guards. They are the ones that havent taken sides so really they are 'immune' to the whole faction thing.
- XP Penalty: I cant see happening, well at least to a point. I can see them loose xp = the value the guard is worth upon death only to the point of their last save. Yes this can be easily bypassed but it could the other way too. Ok, theyve taken out an entire city and have -500k xp. /terminate and be back to last save xp.
- Merit Penalty: This one I actually kind of like. For every guard they kill they loose X number of merits. X being based on the level of the guard. Plus this would work nicely in conjunction with my Neutral comment above since the Neutrals dont get Merits anyway.
- Faction Hit: Well the first problem with this is that there isnt any "Faction Values" stored as far as I know. Your either Clan or your Omni, that is that. (Ignoreing neutrals at this point) A number of guard kills could be tracked but I dont see it turning you Neutral. Some people would do it just for that. Neutral would join Omni to buy some equipment because he's in trade then kill off some guards until he's Neutral again.
Changeing sides however I can see, though that also could be abused. Clanner joins Omni for gear, dosent want to wait 72 hours to change back (another problem in my opinion but thats another rant) so instead he just kills off some guards until he's clan again. Hence, skipping the 72 hour changeover time.


But thats just my 2 credits. Im only a FunCom customer, im used to be ignored.
