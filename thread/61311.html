Rooftop Revival Party this Friday 10/25
Unity of the rose, in conjunction with Gridstream Productions and Vixen Entertainment is hosting a Party this Friday 10/25 at Midnight GMT (8pm Eastern). It is the Rooftop Revival party. The purpose of this party is to promote a continuation of party-diplomacy that has been disrupted by the recent addition of guards to the grid at Omni Entertainment.

There will be a live DJ, free food and drinks, as well as contests and prizes. There will be a taunting contest where contestants will be judged by their creative verbal and physical taunts (not attacking and no profanity).

Warps will be provided from the rooftop of Reets Retreat and Fair Trade in Tir during the first hour of the party. You can yalm there if you would like, but we do not want tiny people at the party even if a relog is required.

Please come and enjoy the party!

Contact me in game with any questions or send an email to szentasha@darksphere.com.
