PvP Lvl caps to Level 200!
This has been a concern of mine for a while now. Now that they are bringing out the OE & IP reset rules, it has become a bigger concern. At higher levels, there are few places to hunt, and none of these places are safe from random attacks from the opposite faction. It was bad enough having an entire team wiped out by 1 person twice their level, but now it will be impossible to defend yourself from a person twice your level. The caps they used up to level 75 where fair and kept people from goddly levels from hunting people at popular hunting areas. I personally think that these same type of caps should be carried all the way to level 200. Right now my character can team with a level range of about 27 in either direction, so I can still get my butt kicked easily, but would stand a decent chance of defending myself. I know I am not alone on this issue and until more hunting areas come out for the 100+ (and maybe even a safer area), this is a reasonable request to make. NO ONE LIKES DIEING BECAUSE A LEVEL 170 GETS HIS KICKS OFF OF HUNTING LEVEL 75'S!
