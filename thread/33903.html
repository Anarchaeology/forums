Mas Bracers and pvp. what i think should be done.
1) ma pvp balance
My main char beeing an MA the only thing i see that needs to be changed on mas for balancing "in pvp" is the use of uvc with ranged weopons such as nova,flashpoint,river6.... I think it should not work as well with these weopons... maybe make uvc 24% to melee crits and 7% to ranged crits (just an idea) .Mas are not supposed to be masters of ranged dmg and should not have higher crit% chance then a soldier ( or any prof) who has more skill with the weopon imo.


2) Bracers and what i think should be done about them in pvp

As far as Mas beeing able to switch dmg types i see nothing wrong with it as far as balance goes. To tell the truth Nts and Crats can do many dmg types at the same time. Last time i fought Wenzell i was taking Energy proj and melee dmg all at the same time. Really hard to use bracers against that. Nts can do primary weopon dmg and alot of dif types of dmg with their nukes . The big problem comes with proffessions like the soldier, enf,trader ect.. who dont have lots of choices with "good" weopons that do different types of dmg.
Maybe a solution to help enfs get around bracers in pvp would be to duel wield 2 decent weopons with 2 dif dmg types so you could do 2 dmg types at same time, but then the enf also needs more options for "good" weopons with dif dmg types.
Maybe if they made new beams new alloy staffs ect.. same appearance and stats as the current ones but would do dif dmg types like chem cold fire ect.. So the enf could swap weopons to change dmg type and it wouldnt be so obvious which type of dmg they were gonna use. As far as the soldier and ranged proffessions go I have heard the idea of making fire proj chem bullets to use in ranged weopons i think that would be a cool idea also. that way bullet selection would determine their dmg type.
This would not really nerf bracers imo, but would let people have more options and stradegies when fighting someone in pvp.
