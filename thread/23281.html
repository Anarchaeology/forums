Rubi-Ka Summit Meeting
This was not my idea but I have heard it from a number of different people and I think it might be a bit of fun as a roleplaying idea.

The idea is we have a number of people from all 3 sides clan, neutral and Omni-Tek at meeting point to discuss the up and comming war and the condition for peace. Perhaps on the lines of trying to form a new Tir Accord.

This event is not to every ones taste but more focused at the people interested in Politics and debate. I suggest that 2 people on each side give a 10 minutes speech followed by comments from the various people.

I would suggest about 8-10 on each side with myself as the reporter and one person as the chairperson for the summit.
