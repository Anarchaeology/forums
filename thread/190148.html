[OOC] To all Roleplaying Organizations in Atlantean/Rimor
Hello guys, usually I stay IC here, however in this case I got to go OOC, and since this is directed to the roleplaying orgs and their members, I believe this is the best forum for it.

A few weeks ago I created a special section within AO Vault.

The purpose of it was to share the existance and history (short version of it) of each roleplaying org, list a website (if applicable), server they're in, faction, a contact name if interested in joining the org.

Our Organizational Grid Database has a few of the most notable rplaying orgs so far, but the information is incomplete at this time.

We want to offer new roleplayers and old timers an 'intro' to each org to make their options more clear and open to them.

Lots of people are still looking for a roleplaying org when they join or get bored of powerplaying their toon, and with the recent activity of player-run events things are getting better within the roleplaying community.

What do we need? not much.

Name of Organization
Website (if any)
Basic History (check the current ones listed for reference as to how long you can write it)
Basic Goals/Mission Statement
Faction
Server
Contact Name for anyone interested in joining

We would preffer that advisors or members of the leadership of the organization send in the info, as it WILL represent the organization publicly.

Another thing we've got under our sleeves...fictional organizations from all factions. Why you ask? because there have been times when a storyline, event or plot needs a temporary fictional enemy or ally to keep things going. We'll provide information which should give any event producer a fictional org to work with, such fictional orgs will be listed as such, to prevent confusion with real player-run orgs.

Roleplaying orgs can check orgs from an opposing faction and consider agreeing for crossover events, become enemies or call for temporary cooperation. Same side orgs will know fellow rplaying orgs they can talk to for events and plots, or just alliance.

All this...from a simple page with enough information to know who, what, where, and how to contact a roleplaying organization.

So if you're in a rp organization in Atlantean or Rimor, just email me and I'll get the info up there asap.

AO Vault , Home of the Roleplaying Community.
