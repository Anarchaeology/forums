Awakenings ((Graverose's Bio))

** Omni-Med log:55762A-23**

Proposal: Project VICTORY GARDEN.

This project proposes to grow employees capable of serving Omni-Tek in an infiltration and assassination role. Using the Solitus female DNA base (Attached: DNA-Rose Galloway), we will infuse into it a variety of genetic traits using the FastVat cloning techniques.

**Omni-HQ file 57849S-54**

Funding approval for project VICTORY GARDEN granted.

**Omni-Med log: 58724K-82**

We are starting with 20 subjects until the process is perfected. In addition to the genetic treatments, backgrounds will be created per Omni directive 5321-8. We chose Ward of the Corp 15 for its exemplary loyalty factors and Individual initiative allowances.

**Omni-Med log: 60084M-34**

Subject Winerose suffered molecular breakdown during genetic treatment. Remains destroyed.

**Omni-Med log: 61247X-92**

Using an intermediate phase of a degenerating shadow mutant gene, subject Graverose has shown a greater ability to absorb the said foreign genetic material. Only side effects so far are a slightly ashen discoloration of the epidermis. Otherwise subject is quite healthy. The Organi-steele hand grafts were installed with no difficulty and are healing as expected.

**Omni-Med log: 64915Z-71**

Omni-HQ, requests active units as soon as possible. Accelerating growth and gene treatments treblefold. A memo has been sent to HQ warning that success rate drops from 83.2% to 31.8%.

**Omni-Med log: 65288B-65**

Failure of 3 more subjects. They arent fully integrating the genetic material before the next treatments occur. As predicted, rejection of DNA is occurring.

**Omni-HQ file 67283J-00**

**WARNING** Background Program Ward of the Corp 15 has been hacked into. Cease use and wipe from all databases. Using Ward of the Corp 87 will take care of any problems with minimal deviation from projected backgrounds.

**Omni-Med Log: 67581V-28**

Subject Graverose Remains healthy. All other subjects show signs of rejection and genetic trauma.

**Omni-HQ file 67981Z-06**

With the Amnesty over we feel that resources are needed in Warrior class clones rather than Infiltrators. Project VICTORY GARDEN is therefor canceled. Destroy or release subjects as per Omni-Pol guideline A65-73.3.

**Omni-Med Endlog: 68091Q-XX**

All subjects except for Graverose have been destroyed per Omni-Pol guideline A65-73.3. We believe that subject Graverose is stable enough to become a fully productive member of the Omni-Tek Family. We recommend periodic surveillance for progress and possible degeneration.

******************

Graverose woke with a start. She had the dream again, the one where the Clan bombed the high-rise where her family lived. She vividly remembers lying on her back, pinned by the heavy reinforced door that probably saved her life. She turned her head to the side and saw, through the smoke, the broken remains of her father and mother. By a cruel twist of fate she was the only survivor.

The dormitory of the orphanage was usually full, but when she looked around, the only people she saw were a couple of Omni-Med techs walking toward her.
Please stand up Graverose, one of the techs said with clinical coldness, We need to do a few tests. She had Physicals before, but never here. She looked around the room as they conducted their tests. Somehow the place seemed grayer.

After the Initial battery of tests they escorted her to the Gym. There was a nagging feeling in the back her mind. She could have sworn that the gym was located on the other side of the building. The techs had her run the gamut of tests to see how her hand grafts were functioning. In the Clan bombing, her hands were crushed beyond repair, and Omni-Med, in its wisdom decided to use experimental Organi-steel replacements. The color was a glassy gray; they had silky smoothness, and were oddly warm. As part of her childhood recovery they had taught her to defend herself with her new hands. She took to it right away and kept up with her training even today. Her movements were fluid and her strikes, aided by the material strength of her hands, could easily crush a mans throat.

They put her on the treadmill and monitored her heart and endurance. For the first time she noticed the mirror across from her. She moved a strand of her long brown hair away from her face as she took a look at herself, for what seemed like the first time. Staring back were a pair of deep brown eyes that had a sever intensity about them. She was taller than most Solitis and could even look most Atrox in the eye. Her athletic frame glistened with a light sheen of perspiration as she jogged. The color of her skin was odd. It had a grayish tint, but not like an Opifex or Nano. Someone once mentioned that it too was a side effect of the bombing.

The techs were making happy little tech noises as they compiled their data in their scanners. Excellent results the lead tech declared in a Flat tone. Please clean up and report to Dr. Wagnas for further orders.

A few minutes later Graverose found herself in yet another part of the Omni-Med sponsored orphanage that she had not seen before. The air here had a stronger antiseptic smell than the rest of the building. She knocked on the door to Dr. Wagnas office. Come in, said a womans voice, as the door swished open. Ah, Graverose. Continued the elderly woman in the white uniform of Omni-Med. Please, sit down.

Im proud to say that you have been seen fit to leave us. Your training at this point has been exceptional, and Im sure you will do well. There was anger in the doctors voice but it didnt seem to be directed at her. I have taken the initiative to forward your files to Head Mistress Meka of The Honored Maidens. They are a troubleshooting group working with Direct input from Phillip Ross.

Dr. Wagnas Stood up and offered Graverose her hand, which she shook. As she left the office she thought she heard the Doctors voice softly say good luck.


Discovery ((Graverose's Bio cont.))

Why are they always in the damn sewers? Graverose asked herself as she snuck into the hidden Clan hive. These cells popped up from time to time, and since joining The Honored Maidens, she had made a small name for herself cleaning out these nests located in Omni-1. There were two guards up ahead, just milling about, their rifles held relaxed in their arms. She inched closer in the shadows, and then when close enough she sprang out and attacked the first guard with a flying kick to the back. As he attempted to recover she summoned the force of her psyche in a crippling Dimach attack. She finished him off with a quick throat strike, crushing it with her Organi-steel hand replacements.

The other guard took advantage of Graveroses lack of attention and got a couple of good rounds at her. The armor she wore took most the damage from the shots and her discipline kept the pain from effecting her fluid movements as she covered the intervening distance. As the Clanner took aim again, Graverose dove toward him into a tumble and came up with a fist in the gunmans gut. He swung the butt of his rifle at her head, which she ducked easily and responded with a knee to the solar plexus and a left-handed chop to the neck. As he stood hunched over attempting to breathe, She finished him off with a good old-fashioned uppercut.

She went up to the locked door of the Clan workshop and carefully and quietly picked the lock. The door opened silently and Graverose slid in, melting into the shadows. Bent over a wicked looking device was Arthur Somes, the man she came to kill. Arthur was so intent on the final touches of his permanent death bomb that he never saw Graverose until she struck. The fight was quick and inglorious, but necessary. She left a few demolition charges to get rid of the bomb and any other designs he may have had on file. Not that it would stop some other nut job from trying the same thing later on. Graverose sighed. She needed a drink.

Graverose was relaxing with some friends at Rompas when the call came. The code designation meant that she needed to go to the Arena Mission terminal. Sorry hun, she said to Callantha who gave her a quizzical look when she heard the comm unit chime. Duty calls. Callantha gave her an understanding smile. Graverose gave her a hug and a quick kiss before she shot out of the Bar.

The Mission report was cryptic. Normally they mention which department requests the mission but this one didnt. The job was a simple Data retrieval in Rome Blue. She looked at the co-ordanants again, that was close to the Omni-Med orphanage where she grew up. For some unconscious reason, she shivered inwardly.

The Building was easy enough to get into. It turned out to be a warehouse of older Omni-Med equipment. Crates filled the place, with TOP SECRET and CONFIDENTIAL labels on them. After an hour of searching she found a box of used back-up crystals. She ran the scanner on them and one particular file caught her eye. It was labeled Project Victory Garden. As light from the file reader played across her face, Graveroses expression changed from surprise to anger to confused then finally ended with a dazed shock look. She starred at the data crystal in her hand for a moment, then crushed it with her Organi-Steel hands that Omni-Med was so proud of.
