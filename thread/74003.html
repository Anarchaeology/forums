Jayde
Supa Leet
Thanks for the feedback..

-The 1.25 was a typo and has been corrected.

-Weapon speed is capped at 1/1 *or* the base speed of the weapon. If the base speed of the weapon is less than 1/1, it will be that fast. AggDef or negitive initiative can take it above the base, but a speed with base below 1 second can remain there with positive initiative. With 1000 init, you could theorhetically get the G-Staff back down to .58/.5 at 0% AggDef.

-Special damage is the total of all specials. (Some weapons have Burst and Fling, for instance.) There was a glitch in this causing it to get multiplied, which is why the number wasn't making sense.

-Lastly, I may throw together a FAQ page with useful info from various sources. I'll have to contact the folks who originally posted the info to get their permission to repost, though. =)
