Open Letter to Commander Silverstone
Commander Silverstone, with all due respect to your personally overseeing and maintaining the peace and security of our capital city, I request your appearance and involvement in the rebuilding process of the Council of Truth.

Experienced members of old Council of Truth should be present, share past wisdoms and ensure mistakes of old CoT are not repeated.

In addition, The Sentinels have proven themselves to be a millitary ally of the clans. Such an ally may be needed to ensure the peace during these proceedings, and protect our citizens against unjustified warrants.

Respectfully,

Abram Kerans
