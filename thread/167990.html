Nano Structures

For the last couple of months I have been studying the adverse effects that notum has on the plant life of Rubi-Ka. Whilst experimenting with gene enhancement using nano technology, I stumbled across a new cell structure.

The design of the cell structure is currenlty being witheld while its being tested by Scientists at Omni-Prime.

Mr. Cogs,

The ICC is in no way the owner of the Notum. Nor are the miners (whatever their faction) the owners of the Notum they extract. Until the lease comes to an end, the only rightful owner of the Notum is the Omni-Tek Corporation.

The only say that the ICC have is about the mining rights. They had to step in to calm down an explosive situation that could have prived the Universe from its only source of Notum. So Omni-Tek had to give its technology not only to its trusted employees, but to all the Rubi-Ka citizens.

Is Omni-Tek gathering huge amounts of Notum? Doubtlessly, and this for two reasons: first they currently have a complete control on the supply of the Notum. If they sold more, the prices would fall, so they artificially keep the quantity sold low.

But then you say, they could do this by resticting the mining operations. True inded, but they don't do this for the second reason: the lease is coming to an end, and the future owner of Rubi-Ka is not known. So Omni-Tek is gathering stocks of Notum, in case they don't regain the lease.

Military use? Oh, some of the Notum will be put to military use, that's right. The military market is a very profitable one. But you are forgetting the main focus of the Corporation: it is aimed at making profit. Not at war-mongering. This is common sense: even though the Notum military technology of Omni-Tek is outstanding when compared to the one of the other corporations, it couldn't stand against the alliance of these corporations.
