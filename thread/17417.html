Originally posted by TildenAntonini
How exactly is plasma made?

Is it with a bio-communicator? If so, where is that bought? Is this something a level 20 could do?
Use a bio-communicator on monster parts to make blood plasma. Same way implants are made (pick up bio-comm and Shift-right-click on monster parts).

The ability to convert a certain quality level of monster part into blood plasma is determined by *both* the QL of the bio-comm, and your Pharmaceutical skill. The bio-comm can be considerably lower level then the monster part, however. (A QL 20 bio-comm might work on up to QL 60 monster parts for example).

When you attempt to convert a monster part, it will tell you what is too low (your skill or the QL of the bio-comm) if any.

You can buy bio-comms in advanced and superior stores (not sure about basic stores), in I believe the "tools" machine (been awhile since I bought one). They aren't cheap, but you quickly make up the cost in converted blood plasma.

Monster Parts and conversion
If the QL of the bio-communitor is higher than the QL of the monster part, the QL of the resulting blood plasma will be 'pulled up' partway towards the QL of the bio-communitor.

If the QL of the bio-comm is lower than the QL of the monster part, the QL of the resulting blood plasma will be equal to the QL of the monster part.

Thus, if you have the cash to spare, invest in a QL125 bio-communitor. You'll earn the expense back in time.
