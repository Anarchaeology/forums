There's an element of truth to that Vix. Clan have never been that well organised, and our differences have been exploited in the past by various groups. Maybe its time we had a serious think about what we are trying to achieve...
Now while Fixerben has a point about the guards having a seemingly superfast reclaim and return rate, there is still something to be said about clan unity.

Radiman once said, "Divided by rhetoric. United by cause."
Dont we all wish that were true. We are not united. We all have the same cause but we are NOT united. We do not function as efficiently as OmniTek because we can't seem to work together. We have many different agendas and some are even contradictory to others. We need to find a way to put aside our arguing and work together. We need to stop thinking about what is good for each individual clan and think about what is good for all RubiKa, clan or not. What we need is a council that includes all clan leaders and actually has authority. The CoT was never able to excersize authority over all clans because few clans were part of the council. The CCL had many clan leaders in it, but it never got anything done. People argued too much and there was no unity.

In light of our past mistakes, it seems obvious that a republic will not work. We become factional and work against, rather than with, each other. We need to elect one person that has the intelligence to preside over the clans and once we elect the person, we need to listen to them. We need to have a government with some authority that can help turn us into a unified force that fights the enemy and doesn't just argue with each other.

Now I'm not saying we need some kind of dictatorial regime. I am talking about about a leader elected by people. However, that may be difficult to assemble all the people for an election, so I propose the leader be elected by the leaders of the clans. The people in the clans most likely support their leader or they wouldn't be in the clan. Therefore, the leader can speak for their clan. And, while I am not talking about a dictorship, far from it, I am talking about a democracy, we still need to allow that leader and the government some amount of authority. Not too much mind you, we keep our individual freedoms and liberties, but we can have a legitimate government that can deal with OmniTek as a force that can command and the deals that it makes will actually have bearing on the clans as a whole, something the CoT could never do.

And I should think it goes without saying that the Sentinels are not the clans. They are a band of thugs run by a warlord that have invaded and occupied Tir. Most clanners do not condone nor support them, but have to live with them until we can oust them for good, which is no small feat.

Now, to the issue at hand- Once again, it seems, OmniTek is trying to speak for the neutrals. I do not understand what OmniTek doesn't understand about the neutrals. They are not employees. They are not servants of OmniTek. They are also not clanners. They are neutral. NEUTRAL. Let that sink in a little while. Have you got it yet? Neutral. It means they aren't taking a side, for whatever reason. And that is their right to. It is not OmniTek's responsibility, nor right to interfere with the neutrals. It is not the clans responsibility nor right to interfere with the neutrals. The Sentinels, as I have said before, are ignorant hate filled thugs. I would gladly have neutrals in Tir. It hurts not only Tir's economy, but atmosphere as well. The neutrals gave a moderate air to the place that could cool many hot heads, or at least long enough to visit with the tourist neutral.

I think the neutrals have every right ot deny Newland City and its surroundings to the clans, if they feel it is necessary. But I do not think that one neutral that has enough money has the right to overrule other neutrals and become something of a warlord with a band of thugs occupying Newland. I guess the north is being overrun by warlords.

Shameless cross-post from the other thread that summarizes all I have to say regarding this.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Some disjointed comments. Necessarily so as the Neutral community is by design disjointed. The meeting last night which discussed this evidenced this clearly. There were certain high-profile dissenters as well as those who agreed to endorse the funding of this endeavor. Even those who endorsed the endeavor did so prompted by various reasons.

Here is what I know and what I have gathered. I hope this helps you form an opinion:

1. The credits are real. I am convinced this would not have gone down unless the money was there and more than the mysterious 'benefactor' were in agreement that raising the profile of the plight of Newland City and the 'fence-sitters' of the world were not important both IC and ((OOC)). There is an expansion set coming and the Neutral community must be heard or be forgotten.

2. Tir was not only closed off to Neutrals by Simon Silverstone and his thugs, but they were relentlessly murdered. The Clans as a whole have not been able to restrain Silverstone even though many independent-thinking Clan orgs despise this man.

3. This service by Ian Warr and his gang is funded by a Neutral organization as a lesson in 'how it feels' to be treated like scum by people you formerly harbored no ill-will towards. Fighting fire with fire is not always the right choice, but sometimes it's the only choice. Publicity is also a way to get people to listen to your issues. Killing Fisk a few times did not achieve the desired effect of opening a dialogue between non-Omni's and the Sentinels.

4. This is not an offensive contract. No towers are to be targetted by the Warr gang. This has little if anything to do with Notum Mining. It is about an oppressed segment of Rubi'kaan society being (rightly or wrongly) given some breathing space in the one city they call their own.

5. Nobody is making you go to Newland. That's the same response Neutrals got when complaining about being murdered in Tir.

6. This does not represent every single Neutral citizen's opinion. There is no Neutral flag/charter/constitution. This contract represents the sentiment of a lot of people not aligned with either Omni-Tek or the Sentinels or followers of the Council of Truth. However, a lot of them are pissed over recent actions in-game. The day you can show me a Neutral flag and a Neutral President is the day you can start lumping us all together.

7. On a whole all Neutrals (and a ton of Clanners and Omni) are sick to death of the nonsense that goes on outside the NL West Gate. If the only thing this changes is an end to the b.s. you have to endure when trying to go from NLC to the Grid, then it was 100 million well spent.

8. The Neutrals were approached by Ris Lee. How many times has Storm killed his brothers and sisters in arms. If you stay away from their mercenary camp, do they come out to kill you? No. But, certain organizations have taken it upon themselves to harass and repeatedly murder their membership.

9. Many in the Neutral community wished for BOTH Omni-Tek and Clans to be encouraged to leave us to do our mining and business in peace. The offer by Ris was only with regard to Clansmen. The majority of high profile unprovoked hostilities have been uni-directional in the form of Clan orgs attacking Neutrals. An attempt was made to have an 'out' clause included enabling the benefactor to terminate the agreement should Neutral harrassment abate. Apparently, Mr. Lee would not agree to that and will have Ian's people positioned until such time as they feel they've earned their keep or they just 'feel like going home.'

10. The Tir Accord is no longer in effect. There is no Clan land. It's all Omni-Tek land until their lease runs out. Take from that what you will.

11. Ian Warr as the type of business man who has made a name and living for himself Independant of the rhetoric-spouting Clans or Corporation. Sometimes people need to think outside the 'Team Blue', 'Team Red' simplistic mentality. He's obviously done well for himself as evidenced by how well he outfits his brothers and sisters in arms.

12. No Neutral organization would pay this sum of money to Omni-Tek to protect our Haven by the Lake. The fact that Ian is Neutral and is muscle-for-hire made it palatable. It can be seen as Neutrals protecting Neutrals. Whatever P. Ross wants to say and irrespective of Ian's past dealings with Omni-Tek, he's first and foremost a Mercenary and would not have done this without pay.

13. No Neutral organization to date has sided with either Omni-Tek or the Clans. We treasure our neutrality and hope the best side wins. We know it won't be any individual neutral org. As such, we watch our own backs.

14. Nice to see something at least happening on the planet, eh? Even many Neutrals applauded what happened in Tir (myself included) as a sign that our planet was alive. It's possible to detach yourself from the 'game' and look at the big picture and see that a world that breathes is funner to play in than one in which everything goes your way. The groups who joined together in agreement that this is a 'good' thing know full well that their intentions may be misunderstood and life may become a living hell for them. But, the fact that this thread exists in this forum right now is justification enough.
