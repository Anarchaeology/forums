Adventure Race January 20th at 2:00pm CST!
Hi everyone, I want to invite you to a big event on Rubi-Ka2.

Sunday, January 20th at 2:00pm Central Standard Time Siirine, Saebre, and I will be holding a cross-country race across northern Rubi-Ka. This event will take place on Rubi-Ka 2.

Anyone is welcome to attend. Solo runners or Groups of any skill level. Please meet outside the West Gate of Tir at 2:00pm CST.

The race will begin outside the West Gate of Tir, and contestants must run to the 'Reets Retreat' in Last Ditch and pick up a secret item, then run north to Camelot.

Come and see some of Rubi-Ka's most beautiful sights, and win some cash while you're at it
