Even if bio met was all that mattered...

If you use Life giving elixir (786/714), your average heal (assuming that the roll is not curved... ) will be 980 health. A doctor using a much worse heal, i.e. Life Balm (722/650) will be 893 health. That means that your uber nanomage doc has about a heal that is about 8% more (on average), an average 100 more points of health. Most xp'able missions with veterans and masters normally hit for 300-600's, and up to 1200 crits. That 100 health is not significant enough to make a difference. If you are losing the fight with Life balm, 9 times out of 10 you'll be losing it with elixir...

You don't need skill to level. All you need is skilled people in your group. If you are one of them, then cool... if you are not, well, hope the other group doesn't notice.
