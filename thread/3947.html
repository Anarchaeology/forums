Good Suggestion for Guild Improvement, Please read.
I am more of a soloist player and had never joined a guild before in any other games (M59, UO, EQ,..etc) but I recently did and am enjoying it. As a new person to Guild structure I have a few suggestions which I feel would benefit the game and make it more enjoyable.

There are numerous zones not implemented yet; (jobe being one of them). When these zones are put in (preferably they will be a long distance from civilized areas) dont make them possible starter cities. Instead make them guild only cities; in other words, allow guilds to purchase pre-built guild structures.

Included in these structures should be the following:

1: A large number of rooms(multi level) to substitute for apartments(everyone has a apartment that you dont have to zone into). No reason to lock them since the only things which could be taken would be furniturewhich is cheap and easily obtained. Not to mention the penalties of stealing from guild members would being kicked out.

2: The buildings should be tall and as such have a landing deck on topfor pilots; and Adventurers. So if you wanted to walk in through the main entrance you couldor you could land on top and go in through the deck entrance.

3: Guild houses should be in isolated areas or remote cities (not pvp zones but perhaps bordering them or zones that might become pvp zones due to story a later date). This would make them stand out from newbie backyards and give the Guild a place to call their own.

4: Guild houses would have a Grid entrance point (not exit, just entrance)

5: Guild houses would have as a standard: bank terminal, save terminal, reclaim terminal, and possibly a few basic vendor terminals.no advanced shopsyou have to have some reason to go to town and associate with other people.

6: Guild house zones would be connected with their proper alignment chat channels; I.E: Omni shopping lv 1-xx. Which would allow members to enjoy sitting around and talking but still be able to monitor shopping channels...

7: Chests!!! Storage room A place to store excess stuff; separated into different chestsarmor, weapons, trade skill, etc..
