OOC: Synergy Factor and RP
This post was originally part of a follow-up to the "When Clans attack..." thread, but it has a point to it that some of you may find useful.

We are not a role playing organization. A long time ago, we did everything we could to be friendly and not interfere with role players. However, in spite of our efforts, the role playing community insulted and harassed us for every little thing that they felt we did wrong. So now that we've grown up and can kick your ass, you want us to play nice and respect your playstyle when you wouldn't show us the same respect before.

If you want to encourage role playing, I would suggest being more tolerant of those that are new to the MMORPG environment and may not be as interested in hardcore RP as many of you may be. Most new players are struggling enough with the mechanics of the game without having to deal with hostility resulting from the politics of RP. People like myself and many in my organization could have just as easily gone the RP route had it not been for the alienation by many in this community when we were new to the game.

aaronb
Supa Leet
Lyricia, please don't take it personally. I posted this to share a perspective that might help many in the RP community move AO more in a direction they want. This post was not made in anger at all, but more after I took a step back and thought about the situation and how it could encourage learning instead of just more discontent.


quote:
Originally posted by Blix

Obviously Synergy Factor has had some bad experiences with RP'rs. But aaronb, can you tell us a bit more about the details?
Most of our "bad experiences" took place a long time ago, but it's little stuff like hostile reactions to buffing omni, hostile reactions to accidentally aggroing guards with mongo, or more recently stirring up drama on leader chat because we aren't attacking bases controlled by friends we have in the opposing faction.


I don't pay much attention to this forum, but I do stop by every once in a while to see what the RP community is up to. Right now, I see people frustrated by organizations that have no regard for RP in this game (including mine). So, I think back to *why* we have no regard for RP and what could have been done (or could be done in the future) to make the situation better for both "sides".

We used to have a statement on our website that though we are not a role-playing organization, we did support preserving the role-playing environment (basically that we would not interfere or antagonize people that were interested in RP).

If people want to judge SF because we consider guards to be just another mob or whatever, we're well beyond where we care what other people think. My point here is that those people might not want to push so hard on new players or you'll end up with more people against you than with you.

This is really just a suggestion, not a whine or a flame. Please accept it as that.

spookiepants
Phear Leet
OOC:

Its nice and all to say that we must all get along, and its just a game. But in practice, there will be problems.

A non-RP'r main goal is to team with friends, go out, and have a good time collecting loot, killing monsters, battling each other.

As RP'er we have chosen to impose an overriding set of rules upon ourselves. We must stay in character, play the part, make sure that what we do is always consistant with our storyline.

Our success is measured by our ability to be convincing actors. Theirs is measured by experience points, and weath.

Neither approach to gaming is wrong, but because of the difference there will be problems. We need to recognize it and not simply say that 'we should all get along'. That's not enough. We need rules on how to interact with each other to make sure that we can manage.

Sadly, I'd say the burdon is on us, the role players, to initiate this. It's us that have the greater requirements. It is us, that are asking things of non-RP'rs.

Any ideas?
