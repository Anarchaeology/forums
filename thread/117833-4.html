Day 4: Rubi-ka General Knowledge Quiz and Golfing
Well, we had a lot of changes around the world yesterday, and a fair few in the Olympics. Both of the Rimor events have to have the start times moved later in the day due to seismic activities effecting all of Rubi-ka, and after that it seems many people are now busy with new fashions of wearing Rollerat helmets, and Monocles? Go figure, thats fashion for you :P The quizzes which were to be held only to find the smartest Atrox was opened up at the last minute to allow contestants from all breedsit was the only fair thing to do really.

The Olympics, what were all here for. The quiz which was held in Omni-Entertainments Baboons Night Club was popular, mostly with Omni and Neutral but we were also pleased that the Clans came (we did calm the guards by the grid exit to help them). The questions ranged from the simple, to the down right difficult, but after a hard fight, the quiz, and gold medal was won by Hotsy, a Omni bureaucrat. Silver was won by Zaduda, an enforcer in the employment of Omni-Tek. And the bronze went to the neutral citizen Orin. Genais from the Clans just missed out of a medal coming in just behind Orin. Thank you to all the contestants who decided to make this an enjoyable event, and due to the popularity of this quiz, well hopefully be organising a Rubi-ka pub quiz tour shortly going around all the pubs, clubs, and bars or Rubi-kawatch this space for further information on that in the coming weeks

In the Rimor event we unfortunately didnt have as many contestantsbut we did get 2 who fought it out for gold and silver. Gold in the end, in a tie-breaker final went to Cereberial, a recently arrived new citizen to Rubi-ka.big congratulations. And silver to Dervisus who played well throughout the contest. Thank you for both making the best of the event

In the Golfing, it seems that no one, either on Atlanteen or on Rimor wanted to come out and play. And after the organisers sat around in Tir Pavilion for a while hoping for more contestants, sadly non cameand as such the event had to be cancelled.

It was still dark when the suns starting to rise over the Perpetual Wastes desert. There in the shadows of early morning stood two teams of equally badly dressed teams, ready for combat...or was it?

A whistle blew and the two teams ran at each other...but whats this, no one had a gun, or even a blade? they ignored each other and pursued a Leet which ran for its safety across the desert with both teams chasing trying to possess it. With precision footwork both teams stole the Leet from one another as each tried to run with the Leet down to the opponents goal area which was situated between cactus at either end of the pitch.

The two teams, the Independent Rubikan Reets, and the Priesthood of Honour & Guests All Stars fought intently with each other for half an hour. The goalies of both teams being stretched on numerous occasions to some magnificent saves. With the scores tied at one all and half time closing the All Stars made a charge for the Reets goal, only for Terando to be brought down by Mr Lev Rivers of the IR inside the box. Marvin the Slayerdroid in the IR goal was all poised ready for the penalty. Charging forward and making a valiant dive he just missed the Leet as Terando buried the Leet into the top corner of the goal to great praise from his team mates. It was half time, and the score was 2  1 to the All Stars.

After a quick change of ends, the match commenced with a quick second half goal from Yarko of the Reets. The game was all even again, and the teams ran back and forth with shots being saved every minute at either end of the pitch. The fitness of these teams where quiet amazing, or was it just the stims they had at half time? Who knows, aint no sign of Omni-Pol out here to test it

With the end of the second half closing, the All Stars made one last mad dash for the Reets goal. Managing to distract the goalie with some brilliant passing before they managed to sneak the Leet in past the Slayerdroid by the back post. The local spectators where unruly with some of the decisions of the referee and did at times storm the pitch. On the whole they luckily didn't do much damage but for some minor reclaim visitations for the IRs star forward Mr Lev Rivers.
