Do you remember...?
Do you remember...?
Omni Reporter Chilia

 October 12, 29209? Not many do, but I will tell you

It is 268 years ago today that the valiant defenders of Omni-2 were slain by the clan masses under the leadership of a clan man, named Athens. The brave soldiers of Omni-2 struggled heroically to repel the clan army, but were outnumbered and outgunned. The clans had traded raw notum ore to Sol Banking, in exchange for weapons and with the help of traitors and saboteurs the clans stormed the city.

They routed out, imprisoned or executed the inhabitants of Omni-2. Soldiers, workers, women and children; it made no difference. The clansmen did not care about such details as innocent people when they reloaded their weapons for the next execution. The clans have even renamed the once glorious city with the name of their hero Commander Athens who was killed during the attack.

The tactics used by the clans, or lack of the same, completely surprised the city defenders. Sabotage disabled the automated defenses and allowed the clans to breech the walls and storm trough the gate. The fighting quickly spread to the entire city and eventually the city commander ordered a full retreat and evacuation of the city. Most of the civilians got out before the clans took the other gate. Cut off from retreat and with no were to go, the rest of the city guards courageously continued to fight killing many clanners and allowing omni citizens time to flee.

After the guards had been defeated the clansmen began looting and burning the city. Then came the executions of the captured guards and civilians. Some were only imprisoned for the remainder of their lives, but most of the defence forces were slain- wounded or not no mercy or quarter was given. It is unknown why the clan leaders, who spoke of freedom and peace, did not prevent the mass murder. No matter what they claim had been done to them, then nothing can forgive such a bloodbath.

The death of Commander Athens is but a small comfort to the survivors. It is unknown how he died. Some said he was killed while storming a Beam Cannon, along with hundreds of clansmen. Another theory is that he was assassinated while attempting to prevent the murders, which is very unlikely. Others said he simply went missing, presumed dead. There is only one thing we know for sure. The clans are still in control of Omni-2, and most employees do not understand why the company board did not send in reinforcements, or try to retake the city later. Even today, the key factions within the company are rumored to have been preparing battle plans. It is rumored that these people exist within the OTAF and Omni-Pol divisions as well as amongst the employees.

The clans are nothing more than animals, and do not obey the rules of conflict. Omni employees want revenge for the humiliation and the spirits of the dead call out from their graves. The clans must pay for the crimes of their ancestors, no matter what bloodline they are off. Be it Athens descendants, if any exist, or the clans in general, all must pay the price! And this reporter can only support this, and I hope that the company board will hear this plea!
