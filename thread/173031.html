Neutral Token Board - Uncle Bazzit Gets Mad
Hello,

My name is Emmet Bazzit. You may have heard of my family, the Bazzits of Meetmeder, owners of the Uncle Bazzit Foundry which produces a range of rather popular guns. Anyway, I recently embarked on a project to create the Profiteer's Helper, something of a replacement token board for Neutrals. The Profiteer's Helper is a superb device. It is far, far more sophisticated than the rather crude token board RUR were developing. Moreover we were also much closer to being able to deliver it than RUR, even before that unfortunate accident in Newland Desert destroyed RUR's manufacturing facility,seeting back the project indefinitely by my estimation.

However I am not here to gloat. A similar attack was to what RUR suffered was perpetuated on my own workshop, which is very annoying as I was almost ready to go Live with it. I do have a backup facility and I don't expect this set-back will delay the Profiteer's Helper project very long. However I am keen to see justice done and have these heinous saboteurs caught. So I wish call a meeting of all concerned parties to swap notes and pool our intelligence. Uncle Bazzits has not stood for decades in war-torn by just turning the other cheek. Someone will pay for this.

Cordially,

Emmet Bazzit
