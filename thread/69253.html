An Idea for ADVs
Heres something I never could understand, ADVs are supposed to be able to blend in out in the wilderness and be able to scout and spy. I know all players use the map to track other players, see a white dot in a pvp zone and you check it out to jump them. Now why not make it so that when a ADV is morped their blip on the map should turn red like any old monster mob out there, thus hiding them for scouting use. This would give advs a important role for the upcoming towers and mass pvp battle, scouts will be important, spotting scouts is not supposed to be easy by just looking at a map. All you ADVs out there gimme your thoughts on this.

Also I would like to suggest a 2he buff for advs like the 1he ones we already have.
