Bad things about low damage, fast hitter:

1. With initiative slow weapons can hit considerablely faster, espeically those with 2.5/2.5 recharge times could eventually hit as fast as our fist (and we all know how much damage those pos do). 2. AC, we all know how much worse AC is for the fast, low damage weapon than a slow, big damagers.

3. Deflection shield, this type of nanos take a fixed amount of damage out of your hit. So while your alrady slim damage is decrease further more, an enforcer with a big hammer only suffer 1/3 to 1/4 as much damage decrease.

4. Damage shield, the faster you hit, the more damage you take, couple with Deflection shield this is the worst nightmare for a fast hitter.
