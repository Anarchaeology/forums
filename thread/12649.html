Root Changes will Destroy pvp
I guarantee it. It will FORCE players to pvp in groups, making the solo pvper almost impossible to play. It will remove any elements of surprise and simply strengthen the opponents in 1 vs 2, 1 vs 3 situations (which right now are quite fun). Here are some of the scenarios we will get:

(1)
- level 90 trader/nt/agent/fixer roots level 160 player
- level 90 trader runs off to get 5 level 160 friends
- gank.

(2)
Players /terminating all of the time the second they get rooted, knowing that there is no way in hell they're going to survive.

(3)
- player gets rooted
- player gets the snot debuffed out of them, since only dmg breaks roots.
- simple gankage now

(4) Here's a cute one:
- large group of opposing players are AREA-rooted.
- we then, starting at one spot, pick off every single opponent one by one. We target the nearest rooted player and kick the crap out of them from range. Rince, lather, repeat.

(5) slows will be suicide. If you get snared, you might as well /terminate. No way in hell you're going to get out of there anytime soon.

The existing system is, for the mostpart, fine. Every player is penalized for getting rooted (must stop attack, target self, attack self at least once, then target enemy) as is. My suggestion to the current system would be to add a general NF (root breaker) which has a cast time of 3 seconds or something. I'd also repair nano resist or change the resist reqs on the root NFs.

If the change goes through with no ability to resist roots or break them prematurely, I'm probably gone. I do NOT want to have to hunt with a team of 10 people all of the time, and I dont want yet ANOTHER ability that renders your character entirely useless for up to 5 minutes. PVP should be about tactics, strategy, and position, not whoever roots the other player first.

Snarf
