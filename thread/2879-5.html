with a geforce 4/xp setup. There's a few things you have to do to really make any game run like butter. 1st thing is go to tweakxp.com and look for a article title "gamer settings for winxp." After you've set that up, and be very careful when you are doing that, you need to set up your page file settings.

Your page file should be 1/5th of your hard drive space per hard drive. Example would be, i have an 80 gig, and a 40 gig. So my page file would be 4 gigs for my 80 and 2 for the 40. This will dramatically increase the perfermance of xp with anything really.

Another fix that i've seen work with ge 4's for some reason, is dx9.

Ao would constantly crash with the newest drivers for my ge 4, soundblaster live, and dx8. Switched to dx 9, and got a 20% performance increase in Ao and no more crashing.

Finally you have to make sure the sound card and the vid card aren't sharing the same irq. This can be a pain, cuz you'll have to probably move the sound card into a different pci slot (trying to turn off acpi just gave me headaches, so this was my alternative).

Also make sure you have chipset drivers updated. In my case it was VIA.

Currently my system is a 1.2ghz 266 fsb with 512 megs of 2100 ddr memory, a geforce 4 ti 4200 128 ddr video card, soundblaster live, a 40 and a 80 gig hd, on a shuttle ak31 revision 2 motherboard with VIA chipset.

After doing all mention above, I have not come across a game, this includes asheron's call 2, the sims, sim city 4, and a few of the other new games out there, that has caused a problem. Not even the Ea games screw up anymore.
