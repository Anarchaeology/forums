Fun? If you like to save others, get a lot friends, always find a team, barely can solo nor do dmg and be the number 1 victim/target in mass pvp, be the no 1 choice in unique killing then choose a doctor

Before you start choosing a breed for your doctor you need to realize what playstyle you prefer. I'll list all the breeds for you with its good and bad sides:

  • Nanomage: Purely aimed at healingskills and nanopool (your "mana"). This is one of the popular choices for a doctor. You will be weak in the health and strength departement making you weaker against an enemy who tries to take a bite at you
  • Solitus: Also one of the popular choices; a pretty looking breed with a little less nanopool and nanoskills then a nanomage but they get more strength and health in return. Solitus can be just as good doctors as nanomage, but will have access to better heals a little later then nanomages.
  • Opifex: Not used a lot but it's not a bad choice. It's health/nano is between the nano and solitus with a little less nanoskills then solitus but more evasive skills. Not recommended as first character because of its extra difficulty.
  • Atrox: These are very rare. They lack a lot intelligence and nanopool resulting in lower healingskills for your lvl. They get the most health and strength in return though. Not recommended as first character.


What skills to raise?
At the lower level you cannot afford to raise everything you need; so it's prolly better to first aim at your prime healing skills. You will need to max at least:

  • Abilities: Always raise intelligence and psychic because those help you heal better. Raise agility so you can equip implants; those play a large role into enhancing skills you want. Stamina is often raised as well; as it raises your health.
  • Skills: Always raise bodydev/nanopool (better hp/nano), Computer Literacy for better NCU (to hold nanoprograms) and the nanoskills Timespace, Biomatter and Mattermeta (healingskills). I hope I don't need to tell you to max Treatment and Firstaid Do worry about weapons yet, we doctors are loved in teams and we don't need one until much later (lvl 130+). In "speeds" make sure to max Nano-init (castingspeed) and up your evades and runspeed at least 2 times the lvl of enemies you "fight".


At the early levels (once you killed some leets in the backyard and are around lvl 4) you can go out and find yourself teams. A single "lvl xxx doc LFG" will often get you a few /tells asking you to get in their team. Heal your teammates if they take damage but be careful not to over-do it as the enemy will get angry at you for healing; thus attacking you. If you want to know who the mob is attacking make a macro: /macro assist /assist and put it in your shotcutbar, make sure to click the mob and you can "assist" the mob to see who it attacks. Watch your team's HP bars closely! Make sure to tell your team if you are under attack and make sure you have enough nano at all times (use nanochargers and nanostimms from the shops).

At around level 30 you may want to make your own implants instead of the pre-mad ones from the shop. To help you design such a layout you can use mine; which is suited for any doctor that does not use a weapon. More varations can be found too. Click here to check it out Also make sure to read the Doctor forum and namely this thread full of guides and strategies: click here If you still have questions feel free to post your question in the doctor forum, we're all happy to assist you.

More info..
My doc, which is now level 177 was my second character. At the time, before the agent "love" patch, my agent had a heck of a time finding teams.. so I saw how in demand doctors were and wanted a new challenge.. so alas Mediccus was born.

A few things to consider..

In the early goings (pre 100) you WILL get a lot of blame for your teammates death. More often than not, it is not your fault (although I have been caught napping resulting in a death of a guildie here or there .. Nothing you can do really than just roll with the punches. Remember that you play an invaulable roll to yoru team. Courtesy, intelligence, and friendliness are very important to have as a doc. Pay attention to your teams health bars.. Every so often Ill find a "rogue" member of my team off fighting his own mob.. he gets in trouble and being the nice guy that I am will run to his fight, heal him, and return to the group fight. Doing little things like that, knowing your teammates situation will help you greatly.

As letah said, don't bother with a gun until your in your mid 100s.. There are a million threads on here about gun choice. Go with what you like.. Personally I use the x-3/caterwaul combo, but maybe once I can use a PoP without OE Ill switch over.. time will tell.

Doc's are the #1 most sought after profession in Unique raids and now the QL200+ missions. I walk into NLC for 30 secs and I have a handful of requests to team. Youll never have problems finding a team.. just remember to be polite and courteous. NO ONE likes a cocky doc.

Good luck, and let me know if you need any help or answers to more questions.
