Tokens - a major welcomed change
Envisioned ? These things should be on 'Going Live' :

1. More lvls on tokenboards.
2. Breed specific benefits.
3. Prof' specific benefits.

Reason: Noone will never get 1000 tokens legally unless /terminate 1000+ times at lvl 6.

At lvl100is one single mission will take 1-2hrs+ to complete, add time to find it, travel to it, may not get token although that possibility has been greatly reduced if no greys are encountered and more than 5-10 mobs in mission. Most missions consists of many more mobs off course.

Going from 250 - 400 tokens (150 tokens) will take 300hrs efficient mission time alone, + find and travel, shopping socialising etc. We are talking 50 days playing 6 hrs a day doing nothing but missions. Find and travel to them, shop and chat with other players, it will take 100 days. PLAYING EVERY DAY that is. Going from 400 - 1000 token (600 tokens) will probably not be done by anyone not willing to terminate 1000+ time at lvl 6, because Ao will be finished before you will be.

So make smaller increments, ie 10 - 30 - 50 - 75 - 100 -125 - 150 - 175 - 200 - 250 - 300 - 350 - 400 - 450 - 500 MAX. And make the benefits breed and prof' specific so it will be more beneficial for all chars.

And wait - I did not say a char with 500 token should be über because of the benefits. Just make it good enough so it will become a nice gaming factor, today it is not helping the gaming experience at all.
