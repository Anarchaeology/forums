Circle G Secures Reets for The Healing Hands
Want an area locked down tight as a drum? Look no further, Circle G has the experience and tightest security protocol of any company on Rubi-Ka.

On February 8th, Circle G was hired by The Healing Hands to secure the GSP Glass Lounge at Reets for the purposes of "official" business.

When asked to comment about this event, Unit Commander Shane "Sabreclaws" Ashwell replied, "We are in the business of providing security to all citizens. Please, do not ask me to disclose the details of what took place here at Reets. We, at Circle G, take pride in our stringent policies regarding client confidentiality. Afterall...Safety is Our Business."

When confronted about how CG is able to provide such a "secure" service, Gaiawyn "Autumnblaze" WolfMoon replied, "We have state-of-the-art scanning equipment that even the most up-to-date hacking tools can't penetrate, which provides peace of mind for our clients."

The contract between Circle G and Healing Hands was reviewed by CG's attorney Max "Alteredstate" Marsalis and the facility was inspected by CG's Tod "Zarandia" Whites.

Unit Commander Ashwell also mentioned that he would like to thank; Jeffrey "Walzes" Ricks, Gaiawyn "Autumnblaze" WolfMoon, Sylvester "Stercoral" Meloche, and "Caveman2001", for doing a fine job in securing the facility.
