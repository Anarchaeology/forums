Unanswered questions list redux
All,

I don't know what the best forum for this would be in the new arrangement, so I'll try it here. On October 30 I started a list of the questions that were as yet unanswered by Funcom, many dating as far back as Beta. Some of those have since been answered, others have not.
We were told over a month ago by Savant that he and Cosmik were indisposed putting together a list of unanswered questions and a list of answered questions, respectively. When a question was answered, it would be moved from one list to the other. Many of us praised this as a great development. A couple of weeks later the answered question list appeared and we asked about the other. Savant said that he was working on other things and that Cosmik was doing that list. A couple of more weeks later and there is still no such list.
So, I'm reviving the unofficial one. I will start with the questions that were still there on my last list November 13th. Cosmik said over a week ago that that list had gone to Dev and he was expecting answers, but needless to say we have seen none.

1) ETA on an official unanswered questions list?

2) ETA on Story missions? Funcom's two-part "Getting Players Involved in the Story" promised missions that would affect the storyline. They said that these missions would not be money-makers like the others, but that they would allow us to serve our particular side! Many of us expected these at storylaunch (the above-mentioned announcement was well before that). When will we see them?

3) Known Bug List. Now that I am back from vacation, I will revive my unofficial one, but why is there no official one? Every day players are confounded by bugs that could have been avoided if they had only known. Almost every player has been told "that is a known bug" at some point and if they had known that previously then they wouldn't have taken that type of mission or somesuch. This would not only help player morale but cut down on petitions, yet you seem to be reluctant to publish one, or even answer why or why not.

3) Will characters be allowed to join the official clans and Omni departments? This is something else that many of us expected to be able to do at storylaunch and is another issue that we can't get a straight answer on no matter how many times we ask it! Many of us read the so-called manual and designed our characters to fit into the Knights or Sentinels or Omni-Med, when are we going to get to join and get orders from the head honchos on how to serve the cause?

4) Feedback on why tester-reported bugs are NOT fixed before the patches go live. Enno has finally spoken on this issue but I think a more general announcement on a forum that non-testers can read would be very appropriate!

5) Why not transfer characters from live to test so that we can test using realistically leveled and equipped characters? This would encourage testing and find even more problems before they become a PR mess on live.

6) NPC Interaction. Will there be any NPC interaction other than the occassional story event or shopkeepers? What will be the mechanics involved? How will a high Psychology, solitus crat have an advantage in this over a low Psychology, opifex (remember, the race that is not trusted) soldier?

7) More Int/Psy armor in shops? We assume that this is a bug because they keep telling us that it has been fixed. But then we read elsewhere that maybe this is the way you want it? Which is it?

Please add your own questions to this list.

Scorus
