Any LLTS worth having (9% or greater) will cost a lot by virtue of not being available in game any longer. The supply is dwindling as players with them either keep them or cancel their accounts. If you want something that's 8% or lower a Vision Enhancer is the way to go, IMO. The 9% and higher scopes are in very high demand, including by players more than twice your level.

If you want to make money, here are my suggestions:

1.Try to buy as little as possible from other players and don't buy anything from machines save recharge packs and the like. I've never played an agent but I was under the impression they were one of the classes that could get their own gear via missions (sneaking).

2.Buy low, sell high. Most of the time, though, you won't see that many great deals so this one seldom happens.

3.Get items that other people want and sell them for a good bit. There is obviously a huge list of things that could go here, so I'll just throw a few things out: QL10 Concrete Cushions, QL22-25 OT Windchaser M06 Quartz rifles, QL160 6 slot belts, QL121 Poliziotto Druid guns, certain grafts, etc. You have to watch the forums and the channels to know what to get.

4.Blitz missions for money. As an agent you probably cannot blitz them but should be able to sneak higher level missions. If you're looking to sell to players, look for items they are sure to want (QL125+ nanos, Flowers/Obtru/Omni-Pol Elite armor, certain weapons, etc.); if you're looking to sell to machines know what items sell for a lot (battle suits, most body armors (esp. the female Waitt), the newer Anima nanos and some other newer nanos, etc.). At the lower levels I blitzed stuff to sell to players; now at level 159 I find I make more money per unit time by selling to machines (1M/10 minutes or so).

Keep in mind you won't be able to compete with an alt of a higher level player. After a certain level (it depends on the individual and class, somewhere between 80-100 for most people) you should be getting enough money to outfit yourself with good gear even if you buy from other people. And past some other level (hmm, 130-160?) you will have equipped yourself with QL200 gear everywhere (armor/weapons/implants).

Even so, a few very high level players (190+) are similarly "poor" (< 10 million) and can't seem to figure out how to make more money.
