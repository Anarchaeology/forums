If you wish to become a Shovah, read this
This is to all of you who wish to become Shovah

If you see what must be done in the plane of flesh, and with the plane of flesh I mean this world, this galaxy, this universe, and you see yourself as something very special and unique. If you accept the path of enlightenment, and take it too your soul. If you wish to live by it, then you can try to become Shovah.

What is a Shovah you may wonder.
A Shovah is a very special entity, for he knows what must be done in the planes of existence, they are the doorkeepers, the sentinels, the creators and the destroyers. They are not evil, they are not good.
They only do what must be done.
They prepare for the rebirth.
They spread the word.
And they cleanse the planes of corruption.
The Shovahs are a semi-secret society of both newer entitys (( lower level characters )) and older, more powerful entitys (( higher-level characters )) it is not confidential, but we protect ourselves by acting from the shadows of mankind.

How to become Shovah
First of all, there are no restrictions in power (( level )), all are welcome to join the Shovahs. But you must also have some special traits that make yourself unique out of the unique. The paragraphs below are the guidelines of Shovahnism and if you fullfill even just one of them, you should contact (( /tell )) one of the entitys below.

Oshovah
Jehna
Gillem


The paragraphs of Shovahnism.

§ 1
You can make a difference in the world (you are an entity of great influence and control, maybe an organisation leader or a political player)

§ 2
You perceive yourself as an entity with a greater purpose (maybe you have the trait of megalomania, some people perceive you as fanatic. The shovahs now better.)

§ 3
You have special gift or trait. (All entitys are unique, but some entitys have truly amazing gifts. Such as the gift of farseeing, the gift of being more pure in soul, these are all gifts or traits that the shovahs seek to find)

§ 4
You have understod the greater meaning. (You can accept that there is no good and no evil in the planes. Only more or less corruption. All entitys are created in the same fashion and with the same fundamental buildstones, purity and corruption)
