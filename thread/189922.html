Okay, let's see, quick summary of the roundtables...

Basically, it's a more directed way of gathering feedback. I'm trying to pick a balanced assortment of people (spanning classes, sides, and servers), get them together in one place, and arrange a discussion about how to improve a certain aspect of Anarchy Online -- in this case, Shadowlands.

The Shadowlands one will be a one-time thing, though I'm hoping to arrange for monthly roundtables focusing on various topics.

And nobody from ARK plans to send Stilettos out to monitor your every thought, for economic reasons if nothing else.
