ideas plz read
First off I here that the booster pack contains land control. This is the ability for orgs to set up mines , and towers to protect them. So far this is about all they have said about land control.
If this is as basic as it sounds, then it will be a waste of $20. The whole thing needs to be a lot more complicated:
First off, orgs want bases, trust me. Orgs like to have a place of there own where the can do meetings and stuff. Instead of just having a mine, there should be a Base. Bases could range form a small room and a small mine, to huge Lumbering buildings with large add-ons. This of course would cost a #$%^ load of $$$$- (id say for the biggest base, it should be at least the cost of 10 good yalms) Like wise, towers should be the same way.

Secondly base attacking- there should be many different kinds of defenses for bases, not just towers, plus if a base is attack, an alarm should go off and the org is told that there base is being attacked. If a Base is being attack PvP combat should be open between the attacking and defending org (but only in the zone where the base is at).

Only being able to destroy a base one way would suck there should be multiple ways to destroy another orgs base, as well as ways to defend them for example

1.You could run up take out all towers, and take down the base
Or
2. You could sneak past the towers; unlock the front door, sneak past the indoor security system and plant a bomb or something like.

There would also be ways to counter these attacks
1. Get better towers, come and defend the base for yourself est.
2. Buy a better QL of lock for you front door, for buy a better security system, or even patrol inside for yourself

There should be a level cap for who can or cant attack orgs bases. A good way to calculate this would be take all members, make an average, then all peps who are 5 lvls above or under the lvl average, may attack the orgs base.

There also should be rewards or something for orgs who take other orgs bases. Like $$ for heroic effort.

These are just some ideas. I know they would be a lot of work for funcom, but it would be fun. I know some of the ideas sux, but I think some are good to. Please post any ideas you may have.
