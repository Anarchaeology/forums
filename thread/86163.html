Rubi-Ka 29498 AD - Ruins of Omni-1
More fires erupted all around her, and filled the sky with smoke. How many had died in the first attack? She didn't know, but most of Omni-1 was in ruins. They had done their job well with their orbital bombardment. No one had been left alive, no one had been spared. Just as they had told her. Was she the last in this doomed city?

The streets were filled with bodies of dead people. Some of them whole, others were not....

She walked around among the rubble. Looking for any sign of life. A sudden explosion sent her across the street, or rather, what was left of it. She looked at the sky, so blue and beautiful, and yet, it was not different from the day before. She got up on her knees, and looked around again. The taste of blood filled the mouth. She didn't care, she just remove it with the rest of her sleeve. It didn't matter, nothing mattered. She looked at her hands, they were covered in blood as well. Her blood! Or rather....

Their kind of blood... Her blood was different for the normal breeds. Very different....

They had called her a traitor, and still... They had contacted her just before the attack, and told her where she would be safe from the bombardment..... but why? She had whished that she had remained behind with the rest of the people around her, who now were dead. That would have been the best solutions for her mistakes. She walked for a long time, or so it seemed. Hours? Days? On the other hand, it could only been a minuet. She didn't care at all. It was all her fault.... Everything was her fault. If she could change this, then she would happily give her life to do so. An idea sprang into her mind. There was a way to change this, but it would be dangerous. And they would properly try and prevent it. Somehow, she had managed to get to the remains of a landing platform. This was properly where new colonists would be check by Omni-Pol soldiers. But those days were long gone. And that was also her fault. All the attacks. The millions of deaths. Both here, and back on Earth and everywhere.....

They had managed to defeat the combined fleets of Omni-Tek, the ICC and Sol-banking with a single strike. All of their lives, all the blood, were on her hands. She collapsed, crying. A single mistake had done it all. It had to be changed. She suddenly heard a noise, coming from behind. But she didn't even look, she knew who it was.....

She could feel it! It was a man. Dressed in black cloth: He was slowly walked up besides her. She didn't cry any more, but tears were still running down her chin. The man smiled to her. "As you can see, then you were wrong about us. You could not defeat us!"

Cathrin.... her name.....

Cathrin looked at the man, "I failed... in everything...." The man laughed, "Yes! You failed, but it doesn't have to be like that. You knew what they would do, and say about you! Your not one of them!" Cathrin looked at the burning city, then back at the man, "And i am not one of your kind! That was made that very clear to me. Why should i join you now? And go back to being hated?" The man sat down besides her. He was older then her, much older. "Some hate you. But some also hate me." Cathrin turned her head away as the man continued. "Think about what you would be able to do again." Cathrin turned around, and shouted at the man. "I did as i wanted to do! And i tried to do it your way But that was not good enough was it? You had to take my daughter away from me!" Cathrin got up and walked away, while the man just stood there, and looked after her. Suddenly he shouted. "You went behind my back and born a damned....." Cathrin stopped, and turned around. "A damned half breed?" She crossed her arms. "Just like myself...." The man walked towards her. "I didn't mean too..." Cathrin interrupted him. "To remind me about it? No, i have been thinking about that every day since you killed my mother."

Cathrin's hate toward this man had no limits. "I did what i had to do. And you know that!" Cathrin rolled her eyes, "That is what you have told me ever since. But do you know what? I don't believe you for a second! You used her for your own pleasure. I was just... what was it you told the others? An accident?" Silence filled the air. The man looked around. Properly trying to find words. Cathrin continued. "What will you do to me? Kill me as well? Kill my daughter?" the man shook his head, "I Would never be able to do that...." Cathrin waved over the ruins, "You were able to kill all of the people in this city, what would two more do?" the man didn't answer. He just stood there. Cathrin turned around and walked away. Before she walked she was out of view range of him she shouted. "Go back to your kind and tell them what i have told you. And prey to i don't see you again. Cause i will kill you next time! If you are no longer my father!"

Cathrin walked out of the main gates, down the road until she came across a stone, with a cross engraved. Cathrin kneeled down before the stone. She took some grass in her hand. A tear ran down her chin as she said, "I Swear by your grave, that i will prevent this from happening. I give you my word on that mother" She slowly got up, and continued to walk further down the road, but she suddenly stopped and looked back. Omni-1, a pile of rocks of a once so proud city. She had made decision! Much work had to be done. To prevent this, all of this. The timeline had to be repaired! She had do it alone. But could she? Cathrin shook her head, she didn't know, but she had to try. She continued to walk away from the city.

Deeper and deeper into the wasteland....
