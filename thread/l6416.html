BETA 4 FAQ. New testers please read.
OK, after today some common themes have emerged in chat. Please have a read through this list if you are new to the beta. Feel free to ask more questions or add your own answers.

CAMERA

How do I change the view?

F8 switches between first and third person views. The third person view can be rotated by holding down the right mouse button while moving the mouse. '+' and '-' on the numeric keypad zoom the view in and out in third person.

Is there a chasecam view (like Asheron's Call)?

There is something similar. Pressing CTRL-F8 in third person mode toggles between free camera (the default) and fixed camera. Position the camera where you would like it and press '5' on the numeric keypad to lock it in position relative to your character.

CONTROLS

How do I find out information on an item?

Shift + left click.

How do I target myself quickly?

F1 toggles between yourself and your previous target.

How do I examine myself? Say, to see my full name?

Target yourself and press 't'. Alternatively, shift-click on yourself in third person view.

How do I sit down?

Click 'Sit' in the control view or press 'x' on the keyboard.

How do I exit the game?

Sit down and press alt-f4. Alternatively click the relevant button in the control view.

For fast quit, press alt-f4 twice or type /quit.

How do I use the shortcut bar?

Actions from the control view, items, nanoformulas or macros can be placed on the quickbar. Simply pick the item up with the left mouse button and drop it in the position you want it.

To change the orientation of the shortcut bar (horizontal-> vertical or vice versa) right click on the small tab next to the 'page' number.

How do I create a macro to add to the shortcut bar?

/macro <name> <command>

Will create a macro named <name> on your cursor which will perform <command> when activated. Simply place it on the quickbar once it's created.

Where did my shortcut bar go??

Hit 'y' to get it back.

ITEMS

What are treatment kits and how do I use them?

Treatment kits are used to quickly replenish health between fights.

In order to use on on yourself you must
a) be sitting
b) not be in a fight
c) have yourself targetted
d) have the requisite skill in Treatment

When using on another person the target must be seated, you do not have to be.

What is a nano recharger / How do I replenish nano energy?

A nano recharger can be seen as a treatment kit for your nano energy. In addition to treatment skill they require a certain skill in Time and Space. The treatment lab new players start with also restores nano points.

What do Aggression Enhancers do?

When a monster is selected and the Enhancer is right clicked the target will become more angry at the user, and may stop attacking it's current target in favour of you. This is useful to stop monsters picking on the more fragile members of a team.

What are Nanoclusters for?

They are tradeskill components for the construction of implants.

How do I use implants?

Implants can only be equipped after using n automated surgery clinic or a portable surgery lab. Automated clinics cost 300 credits, after which you have 30 seconds to install or remove as many implants as you like.

I can't see my equipment in the wear view..

There are three tabs at the top of the wear view: Weapons, Clothing and Implants. Check you are looking in the right place.

GAMEPLAY

I keep dying! I can't kill anything!

As a starting player you should:

a) Go into the Stats view and move the Aggressive/Defensive bar all the way to Aggressive.

b) Equip your starting weapon and upload any nanos you might have. If you do not meet the requirements go into the skill view and raise them to the required amount.

c) If your starting nanoformulas are 'buff' type programs, run them on yourself before starting a fight.

d) Spend IP on your weapon and related skills, particularly body development. If you use ranged weapons, increase Ranged Initiative. If you use melee weapons increase Melee Initiative. If you use Martial Arts, increase your Physical Prowess Initiative.

e) Heal yourself between fights using the treatment kit all new players are provided. Heal yourself during fights by using the provided First Aid kits.

f) Don't be afraid to run to the guard if you need to. Don't be too concerned about dying though, your character does not need to save and will not lose experience until level 6.

How do I find xyz shop? Where are the wumpapaluppas?

If you're going to ask these sorts of questions, please be specific. At least tell people what city you are in. I would encourage you to try and find the answers on your own however, exploring the game is part of the fun.

How do I find my mission area?

Go into the Missions view. Hold down the right mouse button over the mission you want to find, and select the arrow from the drop-down menu.

If you are in the right playfield a white cross indicating the door will appear on your map and a yellow line on your compass will point in the right direction. If you are not already in the right playfield, you must find your way there first. Checking the world map ('MAP' at the bottom of the interface) can be used as a rough guide, as a flag appears on the playfield that contains your mission.

How do I delete missions? Is there any penalty for doing so?

Do get rid of a mission find the relevant mission key in your inventory and hold the right mouse button down over it. Move the pointer over the 'X' and release the button. Confirm that you wish to destroy the key, and the mission should be removed from your list.

There is currently no penalty for failing to complete a mission.

How do I upload maps?

You must have yourself targetted and right-click the map. You must also have the required skill in Map Navigation.

I'm stuck in the world! What do I do?

If you're unable to work your way free, you can use the /terminate command. This will kill you and you will respawn where you last saved. Bear in mind you may lose experience and/or items as per a normal death.

How do I reload my weapon?

There is currently no way to reload a weapon manually. If you have the correct ammunition and try to fire the empty weapon, it should reload itself after a few seconds. If it does not, the weapon may be bugged and you should report it.

How do I report a bug?

Press ALT-CTRL-SHIFT-F9 to open the bug reporting tool.

CHAT AND COMMUNICATION

How do I send private messages?

/tell <player_name> <message>

How do I talk in / disable other chat channels? (Trading, etc)

Go into your Friends view. There you will find a list of chat channels. By default, your text goes to Vicinity, the area around you (same as '/say <msg>' command). By selecting a different channel or persons name, chat will go there by default from now on. Default can be recognised by red text.

To turn off a chat channel, uncheck the box next to the channel name in the SHOW column.

How do I add someone to my friends list?

Any time someone sends you a /tell they are added to your temporary friends list. To put them on the friends list proper though, you need a reference of them. Target them and press 'G'. A reference to them will be created in your Knowledge view. Pick up the reference on your pointer and drop it into the friends list.

How do I get more information on commands?

Type /help in the chatbar.

COMBAT

How do I attack something?

To begin attacking, press 'q' or select 'Start Combat' from the control view. You will also begin fighting if you start off with a special attack or an enemy attacks you.

How do I use special attacks?

If a special attack is available with your current weapon it will show up in the Control view. Click it there, drag a shortcut of it to your quickbar, or use the keyboard shortcut. (Check the keyboard layout file in your Anarchy directory).

What do these two bars mean?

The red bar represents your health, the blue bar represents your nano energy.

How do I tell how strong an enemy is?

The color of your target's health bar shows how strong (what level) it is in relation to you. White means the target is much lower than you and will only yield 1 exp if killed. Green means it is lower than you but will still yield reasonable experience. Yellow is the same level as you and could prove a difficult fight. Red means the target is a lot higher than you, only attempt it if you have a decent team beside you and you're reasonably sure of the enemies actual level (ie. it's not level 200). There are shades of color inbetween as well.

How can I tell if an enemy is in range?

Small red blobs appear on either side of the target's health bar if it is in range of your equipped weapon.

DEATH

I died. Where are my items? Do I have to recover my body?

You do not have to recover your body. After 1 minute your items will be available from a reclaim booth. Items you have equipped stay with you after death, as do no-drop items.

How does the death penalty work?

After level six, if you die you will lose all experience (and now it would seem items) gained since the last time you saved or increased in level.

A free save is automatically given with an increase in level so a level can never be lost through death. Saving at save booths costs a small amount, which can vary based on the booths position and alignment.
