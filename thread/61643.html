Anarchist Orgs
I am having a near impossible time finding an anarchist org in the clans to join on RK2, which I find a little ironic on a game like anarchy online. The few orgs I found by digging through the list on the anarchy online website never seem to have members that are online when I'm online, except for the french federation, but I'm not french.

So there were two things that I was hoping to do.

First, I would really love it if someone who is part of an anarchist org could tell me about the organization and meet with me online sometime so that I could join if I'm interested.

Second, if there is no one playing actively who can do that, I make a call to all free spirited individuals who believe in mutual cooperation without oppressive leaders and taxes, and without elitist ranks, to join with me and form an anarchist org that is active and can actually help its members. I want to be able to post things I find to others in the spirit of free cooperation and mutual aid, to help the newbies and mission with the vets, and I think that the individualists among us would benefit from forming a very loose and friendly bond so that we don't all get crushed come Notum Wars.

So anyone who could help on the first or wants to take part in the second please respond here or message me in the game, my game name is "Oaria"

Thanks in advance for any info or help!

BTW - If it turns out that the only viable anarchist orgs are neutral, or that people would rather start a neutral anarchist org than a clan one, I am completely willing to go that route as well.
