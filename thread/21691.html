The verdict on the agent changes:
quote:
The Agent now has the ability to swap sides without losing tokens. This is done using an "undercover" kit. Note: Although you keep your tokens (and can even swap to neutral!) you cannot keep wearing your Merit Board. You can put it back on when swapping back. There is also a skill lock on the swap, just as with the normal side change application forms.


Let's give a big W00T for this!

This scores high marks for being useful, class defining, and "agenty". Exactly what this class needed. We can now infiltrate enemy strongholds, access their terminals, and steal forbidden tech... All things an agent is suppose to do. (This is assumption on my part. I get the impression this device acts just like an application form, with the added perk of letting you keep your tokens.)

quote:
Agents are now able to learn and use all the agent-specific nano programs (except for the False/Assume/Mimic profession line) when in a false profession. Essentially you can now execute nanos from two professions - the Agent nanos and the ones from the false profession.


Let's get one thing strait first: FC balanced the agent with the intent that they would use false professions to offset their weaknesses. The flaw in this logic is that when you spend 95% of your life in false professions you don't get to take advantage of your true agent nanos, thus you end up feeling like a gimped version of another class.

So... This change is essential to the agent, and it was a good move by FC.

How good this change is depends on one thing: Do we get a penatly to our nano skills and nano int to our regular nanos when we are in a false profession?
This is important. If we do get all those penatlies to our agent nanos then this change is nothing more than a minor convienence that lets us throw on a few buffs without waiting for the FP to run out.

We should not have any nano penatlies to our regular agent nanos when in FP. If this is not done then nothing changes. We still spend 95% of our lifes as another profession, devoid of combat nanos, the difference is that we can throw on a felines grace buff without waiting for the FP to run out. Big deal.

quote:
Agents: added two new lines of self-only critical hit increase buffs - Concentration and Sureshot. Check out the boss monsters in team missions, they might be carrying one.


Perfect idea, but I will reserve judgment on their usefulness until I see how much they do.

Seeing as how their are only two, it's obvious they won't be as good or as common as the martial artist crit buffs.

These changes are in the right direction. The agent should be all about large hits and crits, but with a slow rate of fire.

quote:
The Agent is no longer able to execute the higher-level Meta-Physicist pet summoning nanos. For the combat pet, the Enmity and Demon lines are no longer executable. For the healing pet, the last 4 pets are no longer executable (The Vivificator, Altumus, Curatem the Grand and Belamorte). The agent can still summon the other combat and healing Meta-Physicist pets.


Good riddence. I never liked being a gimped MP anyway.

I will happily trade off being an MP to have my agent nanos and abilities focused and improved.

You have no reason to feel cheated or gimped either. You can always use the IP reset.


quote:
Agents: added a second line of damage buffs (these are self-only) - the Executioner line of nanos. These stack with the Agent's existing damage buff line. Check out the boss monsters in team missions, they might be carrying one.


The idea is great, but like the crit buffs I can't judge their usefulness until I see the nano.

Damage buffs and crit buffs are perfect for the agent. They fit with our style.

However, we will need some serious damage increase to make up for all our weaknesses. We can't heal. We have low HP and AC. We have non-existant evades. We aren't very good at running or crowd controlling either.

The only thing we do is sneak.

I always thought agents would be similar to NT's. No evades, low HP, no heals... We are both suppose to make up for that by dealing massive damage in a few slow blows.

With our crap rifles that is not the case. Hopefully things will change to better define the agents role in combat, but a small damage buff and crit buff will not be enough. Rifles need to be changed.

quote:
Agents: added some Team Concealment buffs - they do not stack with the existing Agent Concealment buffs, but have a lower NCU cost (so they won't override Ruse 1 until they provide a higher Concealment benefit). Check out the boss monsters in team missions, they might be carrying one.


Um... Worthless.

Lmao. This is just a piece of fluff you guys threw in to pad the list.
This has no more use than the crat concealment buff. When was the last time you had a teammate beg for a concealment buff, let alone your whole team!

Ok, so FC obviously realises that the agent nano selection is tiny and the agent has nothing unique other than the false profession line... Padding the list with a useless line of nanos doesn't help us, and it doesnt make us feel any more unique. If anything it makes us feel worse when we realise how truely little we have to offer a team when the best you can think of is "team concealment buffs"

quote:
Fixed the description of the Face Graft nanos - they don't do damage so they shouldn't say they do damage. Advanced Face Graft is now faster to execute and is cheaper in nano points to execute than Face Graft.


Eh, don't really care. Nice to know it's there if I ever use it.

quote:
We have introduced hologram items for use by Agents. If they can find any... Agents have also got a new unit of clothing fit for the profession.


An agent cloak 'eh? If it's anything like the ICC messenger cloak, then please spare us the trouble My eyes still burn from seeing that thing.

Hologram items? Sounds cool, but they probably don't do anything useful. Just roleplaying tools.

quote:
Invisibility is now implemented for player-to-player interaction. This means you can hide from another player, and he will have to hit "?" (Search) button to see you. When you hide, your Concealment skill is used against Perception. (With 75% chance of being hidden if the skill level is equal). When someone is hidden and you search for him or her, you use perception as the attacking skill. (Thus having 75% chance of finding the opponent.) Also, we have found a bug in the Concealment code that we have fixed, increasing the chance of you being hidden from green mobs. The search code is changed when looking for hidden characters. You have a base radius of 5-meters which increases to a 40-meter radius (or all visible characters with 100% char view distance) when you get 1000 in perception.


... *confused*

So are you saying that unless I raise my preception to insane levels then I can't even detect a sneaking level 1 soilder because he will be outside of my range?
Your detection range should be based on your perception vs their concealment, not a flat value based on your perception.

I don't like having to actively search for EVERYONE either. There should be a passive search that detects people with very low concealment that are trying to sneak. Just imagine it, a level 1 could sneak up on you because you don't feel like pressing the search button every 2 seconds to make sure no one has just entered the area.


quote:
New version of Low Light Targeting Scope has been made. This has no night-vision effect, and a lower critical increase buff. The old one will no longer drop, but will stay in the game. Treasure the ones already there...


Not really an agent change, but I always considered the LLS a toy meant for agents.

Increase to critical chance.
Reduction in speed.
Increase to aimed shot.

Come on, you can't tell me they didn't make that with agent's in mind.
Of course, then everyone and their dog started using one.

I like the change, but I think you should make an agent only critical scope. It could be a quest item. In fact, you should lower the tradeskill requirements and require rediculous levels of perception to equip it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

FINAL VERDICT: B+

Overall very good changes for the agent...BUT, I will be very disapointed if this is the final version of the patch notes. There are still many things that need to be addressed before we can consider the agent fixed.

THings that still need to be addressed:

-- Mission sneaking. We want the bugs fixed, we want the cloaking packs to work, and overall less dice rolling and more predictability in what we can sneak past.
-This includes letting us zone while sneaking. I had to delete countless missions because I dragged something to the front room and could not get past it because there is NO way to jump into conceal before you are detected.
-We also need a way to break argo off something and continue sneaking a mission. I am tired of this crap where I have to run for the exit as soon as I'm spotted. Then I have to delete the mission before the first room is full of mobs. When you break line of sight for a while the mob should forget you are there, so you can root something and run behind a wall.
-Deaths gaze is our ONLY option when it comes to breaking argo and sneaking the rest of the mission. Unfortunately by the time i becomes available we have everything we will ever need.

-- Lock picking. We should be able to loot chests, defuse bombs, and unlock doors without breaking our sneak. Only when we FAIL to defuse or unlock something should we drop out of sneak. I think that is a great compromise.

-- Rifles. We need better ones. Current rifles have microscopic clips, pathetic crits, wide damage ranges, slow attack speeds. Overall, just very bad weapons.
Even pistols crit better than rifles!
Rifles should have the best crits in the game, period. These are armor peircing weapons that take skill and timing to fire, and when they hit a vital area they are gonna hurt. How is it that a tube wildly spraying out pellets has the largest crits in the game?
I don't mind the slow speeds, wide damage ranges, or small clips. I just want the gun to make up for it in other areas. I want massive crits, a damage variable with a high max, and a better chance of crits.
