Omni-Tek Organizational Pact
The Omni-Tek Organization Pact

Omni HQ  3 August 29476

The parties of this pact reaffirm their faith in the principles and purposes of Omni-Tek Corporation and their desire to conduct business with all peoples and all organizations.
They are determined to safeguard the autonomy, financial status, and corporate structure of Omni-Tek, founded on the principles of accounting, entrepreneurship, and the rule of law. They seek to promote stability and security on Rubi-Ka.
They are resolved to unite their efforts for collective defense and for the preservation of profits and property.
They therefore agree to this Omni-Tek Organization Pact.

Article 1

The Parties undertake to settle any disputes in which they may be involved by peaceful means in such a manner that business operations and profits and assets are not endangered, and to refrain from the threat or use of force in any manner inconsistent with the purposes of Omni-Tek.

Article 2

The Parties will contribute toward the further development of efficient and profitable relations by strengthening their organizations, by bringing about a better understanding of the principles upon which these organizations are founded, and by promoting conditions of stability and security. They will seek to eliminate conflict in their inter-corporate policies and will encourage collaboration between any or all of them.

Article 3

In order to more effectively achieve the objectives of this Pact, the Parties, separately and jointly, by means of continuous and effective training and cooperation, will maintain and develop their individual and collective capacity to resist armed attack and terrorist activities.

Article 4

The Parties will consult together whenever, in the opinion of any of them, the autonomy, corporate mission or profitability of any of the Parties is threatened.

Article 5

The Parties hereby establish a Guild Leaders Forum, on which each of them shall be represented, to consider matters concerning the implementation of this Pact. The Guild Leaders Forum shall be so organized as to be able to communicate at any time. The Guild Leaders Forum shall set up such subsidiary bodies as may be necessary.


Article 6

The Parties agree that an armed attack or terrorist activity against one or more of them on Rubi-Ka shall be considered an attack against them all and consequently they agree that, if such an attack occurs, each of them, in exercise of the right of self-defense, will assist the Party or Parties so attacked by taking forthwith, in concert with the other Parties, such action as it deems necessary, including the use or armed force, to restore and maintain the financial security of Omni-Tek corporation.

Any such armed attack and all measures taken as a result thereof shall immediately be reported to the Guild Leaders Forum. Such measures shall be terminated when the Guild Leaders Forum has taken the measures necessary to restore and maintain business operations and profitability.

Article 7

The Parties may, by unanimous agreement, invite any other Omni-Tek Organization in a position to further the principles of this Pact and to contribute to the security of Rubi-Ka to accede to this Pact. Any Guild so invited may become a Party to the Pact by signing this agreement and submitting it to the Guild Leaders Forum.

Article 8

The Parties shall, if any of them so requests, consult together for the purpose of reviewing the Pact, having regard for the factors then affecting the efficiency and profitability of Omni-Tek Corporation.
