Ideas for AO
I find PvP (and really combat as a whole) in most games of this kind predictable and lacking in motivation. If this were an FPS (first person shooter) that might be different, but it isn't. I KNOW the guy that cons red to me is going to kill me. There's no guess work at all. Unless I can somehow get away, Im GOING to die. It's predestined by the way combat works in this game before the fight between us ever starts. I know, I know. . there are exceptions to every rule, but I'm speaking in generalities here. Also, there is no real motivation here to fight NPCs accept to make money. . . and once you have all the money you'll ever need, why fight anymore? To level?

It's not that I'm knocking the skill, combat and targetting systems here, it's just that they aren't being used to their best advantage. Instead, AO should play on its strengths (gorgeous graphics, huge environment and awesome customization abilities of characters) and create a heavily factionalized environment where every single thing you do and every single creature you kill effects your standing in the world. In games like this it should be not so much about "how am I going to kill that creature?" but more "what are the implications of killing that creature?" That to me is what makes games like this one interesting.

Heres an idea:
Keep the idea of 2 factions. But make the core of each faction a non playing force or entity. Make this a mystical battleground over the souls of humans. Have a core evil and a core good, no shades of grey. The two biggest problems with the conception and implementation of Omni vs Clan, have always been the fact that 1) Clan isnt all that good and Omni isnt all that bad, and 2) What seems like about 90% of the storyline has relied on players to create, which is a really bad idea because players come and go. You can't count on players to develop the game.

Also, players need motivation to fight for their factions. Have a reward system for gaining ranks within factions which involves special skill and abilities, and even new SKINS for players! If a truly good character were to take on more of an angelic look and a truly evil character were to look more demonic, the skins alone would entice people to participate in the battle. Im not saying we have to deal with angels and devils, I'm just giving examples to help illustrate the idea.

And we need to be able to adjust our faction in ways beyond just killing each other (PvP). If someone kills a good NPC their character should become more evilly aligned, and vice versa.

I really like where you guys are going, thinking about making things more mysterious and mystical. Its a good idea. . . and hopefully it'll give us more of a reason to fight.
