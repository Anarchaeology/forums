I suspect the amount of nanopool influence has a great deal to do with there being a set "timer" for how long it takes for you to regenerate your entire pool. The NanoDelta might be getting an increase from your "Nano Pool" skill as well as implants.

By this reasoning you're looking at what seems to be NumberA and NumberB where A is your entire Nanopool no matter how big and B is the set time to regenerate the entire pool.

Lets try some theoretical calculations:

A lowbie:

A is 150 and B is 10 minutes (Just an example)

By this reasoning you'd regenerate 15 nanopoints every minute or 7.5 every 30 seconds.

A highbie

A is 7000 and B is 10 minutes (Still just an example)

You'd regenerate 700 points everyminute or 118 every second.

Now add to this some different variables, like an opening for instance for an increase in NumberB for every 1000 extra points of nano in your pool. Or levels or Psychic or simply that you won't be getting 700 every minute but rather lower amounts every second and we're somewhere in the proper vicinity.

It just makes sense that to fill the nanopool you have a set time and to increase the "ticks" you increase the nano pool. The nanodelta will then shorten the time it takes to fill the pool thus giving you faster ticks. The amount again is of course set after the amount of nano.

If there weren't any increases in NanoDelta based in Nano Pool the time it'd take to regenerate X amount of nano would be a set time. thus making it take 10 times as long to fill a 1000pt nanopool as it would to fill a 100pt one.

I think this is how it works.

Am I making sense?
