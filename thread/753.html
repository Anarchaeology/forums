Strange mission bug, somewhat serious
i just went to 20k to get a few missions, and i found 3, one in andromeda for a nano armor hood (first time ever for me), one in clon**** for a layered protection crystal, and one in stret east bank for a nano cloak (another first, and something i've been looking for quite hard, not being able to afford the rather steep prices in player trading)
well guess what, when i zone out from 20k to omni trade, two of the missions (the cloak and crystal missions) had changed locations to southern foul hills.
to say this was a bit annoying would be an extreme understatement, especially since i had just spent over 10k cycling through missions to find something i could use.
