Screw zone the leets. I say eat the leets!

Leet Mediterranean
(serves 2)

1 leet, preferably a soleet, cut into pieces
1/2 cup white wine
1 cup onion, chopped
1 1/2 in. piece rosemary, fresh, minced
1 cup celery, chopped
1 tsp. fine herbs
1 clove garlic, minced
1/2 tsp. basil
10 Italian olives, cracked
1 tsp. worchestershire sauce
1 T. capers
1 1/2 cups chicken stock
1 T. sugar
1 tsp. tomato paste
1 tsp. wine vinegar

Saute leet pieces in olive oil in a dutch oven (or very heavy bottomed pan).
Remove pieces and set aside.
In same pan, saute onions, celery and garlic until limp.
Add sugar, tomato paste, herbs, wine, vinegar and remaining ingredients. Cover and simmer 15 minutes.
Add leet pieces and simmer 30 minutes.
