An Idea for perception and criticals
I have posted this before I am fairly certain but could not find it in the search feature so I will post it again because I still think it is a great idea.

Perception, should be linked to critical hits. And not as it is rumored to stop aimed shot criticals, to increase YOUR crit chance. It should be that for every 100 points of perception you have you have a 1% chance to hit a critical. Maybe 100 per 1% is too much, and could be 200/1% or something. The current problem I see however, is that perception stops making any increase whatsoever after a certain point. The only use eventually is for information tools, which I don't think are often used. Yet there are many items in the game that increase perception. Shouldn't the skill do something? Monsters don't sneak and you can just click on players or show all names above head so why should anyone raise this skill? This idea would change that. People WOULD raise perception, and perception items would be useful. Sought after even.

Secondly, this would make agents happy. Agents have perception as one of their few green skills and live or die by their crits most of the time at higher levels. Most fights my agent enters alone are accompanied by prayer for a crit or two.

But before all the agent haters condemn this post, remember EVERYONE has perception. Just like everyone has nano skills, shotgun skill, or pharmatech. Not only that but Agents don't even have a buff for perception, Fixers do. As well as Crats I believe. Doing this may also assuage people's fire for the talk of making the MA crit line self only.

EDIT: I forgot to include that this would make sense because perception would allow you to see an enemies weak spot and use it to your advantage. It falls in line with the Universal Vulnerability Compendium type thing, which from it's name sounds like it teaches you to attack weak points in enemies.
