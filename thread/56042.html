Weapons Manufacturing Firm announces Defective Component

------------------------------

The Omni Herald
11:31 October 2, 29476 RST

Omni-1 (TOH) - Weapons manufacturer Intelliwipe (ICCDX:INTW), makers of the popular Extreme Low Light Targeting Scope, today announced that a component used in the scope has been found to be defective.

"Now that the scope has been out in the field for about a year, we're starting to get reports back that part of the targeting system is defective," said Intelliwipe spokesperson Devin Shelling. "As a result, customers are experiencing a loss of accuracy." According to Intelliwipe R&D, commercial scopes may begin deteriorating in a few weeks.

When asked if customers would be reimbursed for the faulty scopes, Mr. Shelling replied, "Intelliwipe regrets that we cannot reimburse customers for the price they may have paid for the scope. We currently don't have a way to fix the defective component, our R&D division has gone back to the drawing board on that one. However, we are coming up with a plan to allow customers to bring their scopes back to Intelliwipe to be retrofitted with some improved features, as a sort of compensation."

Intelliwipe was criticized earlier this year when it discontinued it's popular "Extreme" line of scopes and replaced them with the less-effective "Vision Enhancer" version.

ICCDX reports that Intelliwipe stocks are down 1215.75 points, or 20%, at 4663.13.
