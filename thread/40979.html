In honor of a fallen friend
Form the office of Gen. Lyda "Seloth" Carnalla


"Be Generous.

There is the sort of person who is impoverished, who will make rich paupers of us all. If you know them, you will be glad for what you have and sad for what you have not. And then you will wonder why you could not give some of your time back to them when they are gone.

It is not our way to deny anyone in need. This is our creed and we follow it. If a family is put out of their home, we will shore up their wants and put them on firm feet again. We do this to be humble.

And yet humility still stands to be humbled. Among us at a time lived a man who humbled us all, and we did not know it. He gave gifts to the gifters, life to us all. He gave us his time. There is no finer gift than time.

He is gone and so is our chance to honor him in person. We make a belated effort to thank him for his generosity. He was generous and he was honorable.

In the memory of Damon Moodyman Kosar, Division 9 RSGE creates the Order of Kosar.

This award will be given to members who exemplify the standards Damon Kosar set as a member of Division9. To be considered, one must be generous beyond ones means of the things one has, both tangible and intangible. Take Moodyman as your model; give when you know you are not watched, when nobody will notice. Give quietly and not as a means to another end. Only those who can wear Damon Kosars name on their chest, and deserve to wear it, will be considered."

on this date Moodyman, Rosbif and Ariana are inducted and named the founding members of this order



(Damon "Moodyman" Kosar passed away recently in an automobile accident. The members of Division 9 RSGE as well as those persons outside the Organisation who had the pleasure of knowing him will miss him and wish to express our deepest sympathies to his family in this their time of great sorrow. He will be remembered for the acts of generosity and kindness that he bestowed on those he met. Our lives are lessened by his passing.)
