I agree that gameplay mechanics problems; pet pathing, for example... are more important than implementing the proposed "weapon-holstering", above.

But here's the problem: The pet pathing problem is more than just a "bug" per se. Mob AI could easily be improved to the point where mobs/pets would "navigate" smarter; programming them to plot vitual paths & search for the shortest route from point A to point B that does not lead through a table. :P

As I understand this, higher-end pet/mob AI would utilize ALOT of server resources. It takes a damn powerful computer to coordinate thousands of satans, slayers & meatballs "thinking" their way around tables & doorways in hundreds of missions... all at the same time, no less (not to mention the mobs, whom I imagine use the exact same nav engine).

Pet AI could probably be fixed right now, but at what cost? This would eat up server mermory as fast as anything, lag/sync problems would be at their all-time-worst.

When pet AI finally does get fixed, I imagine it may even involve some sort of hardware upgrade on Funcom's side.

Weapon-holstering would entail rendering a few graphics textures at different locations on the 'toons. I'm no expert, but I can't imagine that this would be overly difficult. Don't think it would take up much time for the coders, nor would it require a server upgrade.

My point... little fixes needn't be shelved just cuz the big ones are still pending.

Still and all... if it could be coded up in a few hours, and I'm sure there's at least one or two programmers at FC who probably could code this in a few hours and forward it to QA, I don't think it'll detract anything from the next big powergaming patch.

Really, all they'd have to do is make two "holster" slots for your weapons in your "wear" view. You can move your weapon to the holster slot manually, and then move it back from your holster to your hand manually, to holster or unholster it. Items that are not weapons and that are not in one of your hand slots cannot be moved to the holster slot. And, if there is a weapon already in your holster slot, you cannot equip a different weapon in your hand slots.

But... why not make this a mechanics fix as well as a social fix? Give some love by it. Let people holster weapons, then equip different weapons (as for an alpha strike), and then remove the new weapon and unholster their original weapons again. So Bob Soldier can have his wrangled-on Div9, and still get his flashpoint alphas in too. Or Jill MA can wrangle on a bow, then holster it and fight with fists, and whip out the bow when she's rooted, or to take an aimedshot to start out a fight.

As for the clothing/armor tab, it could work on the same principle - mechanics love and social love in one. Give people a "clothes 1" and "clothes 2" tab, identical in every way. Give a 1-minute lag time for swapping between them. Then let people equip whatever they want in either, and just switch between them with a toggle button. If you want to put on two different sets of buffed-on armor and switch (say, one for PvM and one for PvP) go ahead. If you want one armor and one clothing, go ahead. Easy
