Organization Questions
Hi all,

I am a general in a departmental organization and have a few questions/issues..

1. There seems to be an extreme lack of information on Organizations either in game or on the website, other than a list of commands..

2. What happened to the organization banks?

3. I heard some talk of a "Guild hall"? Some place for the guild to hang out, a house, or something, is this available yet?

4. Guild MOTD?

1. Type /org help in game. A window with a commands list and explanation of them will come up.

2. A few things happened with the banks over time. The most recent was an exploit that allowed people to duplicate unlimited credits through their org banks.

3. "Guild halls" are not available yet. Many guilds use a city backyard or a fairly accessible outpost as their guild halls.

4. There is no guild MOTD feature at this time; I have not heard that Funcom plans to implement one.

Hope that helps
