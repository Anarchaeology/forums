Hmmn, i thought i was offering advice... Basic advice, sure, but lobbies, while tricky, can be managed by ANY class. But others besides toast have expressed some interest in how to handle them, so i will go into greater detail.

Look, single pulling is quite possible, and if you screw it up, you can always zone and try again. As I mentioned in my apparently useless post, it can be done is 99% of the missions i have tried over the last 140 lvls.

Basicially, the key is to get seen by a mob. If you attack, you will pull all 3. If you are not a melee class, you need to go full defensive, so you will not automatically return fire. This requires patience sometimes, since they have to be somewhat separated. But they do not need to be as separated as you might imagine, especially if the other mob is not facing you. You also have to be able to move the slider bar back later -- if you have a very slow connection this might mean zoning then immediately rezoning.

If you screw up and pull two, immediately zone. If you are quick enough, often only one willl have successfully made it into the room. If not, you need to start over.


You will need to pull it out of the visual range of the other mobs. So fight to the side of the door, deep enough inside so that the door does not remain open.

Again, if you cannot beat a single one of them, even using the zone, then you need to work on damage output, or other features of your character.

Other tactics depend entirely on class -- roots and snares can make things easier if you have them, as can the calms. You can use roots to split a pair of mobs, e.g. or snare and the zone. This is more effective after you have killed the initial member of the triad.

Again, missions are often much more rewarding in pairs or trios. Find a partner, or a guild. But soloing is possible, as long as you can handle each mob individually. There is some randomness about how hard any given mission will be on any setting -- pull 3 at a time in the same area, and if it is more than you can handle, just go to the next one.

Once again, hope this helps someone ^^

How to pull only one mob from main room to the first room:

1. )adjust your agg/def bar to around 40% (or less) so you will not auto fire back

2.1)
If you can root:
Go in, quickly root two of the mobs and zone out of the mission.
There will usually be only one mob in the first room after you zone in again.

2.2)
If you cannot root:
Scout the mian room and check the map. When one of the mob gets close to the door (while the other two are either not in line of sight or far away) step into the mian room and step out quickly for a few times until you get the aggro. When you do get the aggro retreat back into the first room. *attention* do NOT attack the mob until it entered the first room completely. It is recommanded that you zone once after successful pulling so the mob will get closer to the mission exit after you zone in again. (just so you wont accidentally aggro the other 2 mobs in the mian while fighting it).
