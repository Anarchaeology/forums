Another forgotten storythread ..
Hmm .. *rants*

It seems Funcom is forgetting about the threads they start spinning. Quite an amount of months ago, I was a clan leader. When the Radiman clone asassination happened, people was happy (although perhaps not in-character) because something was happening. There was some sort of lasting storyplot from Funcom back in the game, so people started wondering about what had happened, who did the killings, etc. It had an impact on the politics in the game as well, and a lot took the opportunity to spin the Radiman clone's death into their own storylines with their own tales of whodunnits and whys. In the end, there were a damned lot of conflicting versions of the murder, but somehow, Funcom never seemed to catch up on all the su****ion and investigation. When Henry showed up to clean the team moat, people had mostly gotten bored with the thing, but some of us still wondered if it really was Radiman or if it was the clone that showed up and so on .. Sadly, when Funcom seemed to drop the story about Radiman and noone got to find any clues relating to the murder or who actually got killed and so on, people got tired and forgot about it. Radiman never showed up after the moat cleaning anyway ..

Then the Dust Brigade came. Just like before, people think "Yes! A lasting new storyplot from Funcom!". Suddenly, brigadeers were there to kill clan leaders and attack the omni and do general havoc in Tir and so on .. It had a major impact on politics, clans started arguing and splitting up in more alliances than before, people started investigating Dust Brigade, hoping to find them or some secret base .. Scouts were sent out lots of times to trace the vapor trails that appeared in the skies and people were generally happy something was going on. (Although again, I might add, not necessarily in-character) ..

What happens? The Dust Brigade disappears .. There are no clues because Funcom weren't planting any. People got tired of it, the dust brigade don't show up anymore. All the players are left with is disappointment.

I could say the same thing about Yxxil the marauder, but he didn't really have the impact the brigadeers had.

It makes me pissed to see Funcom throw away their own agendas like that. People expect more .. They should expect that if the dust brigade shows up, they're there .. a part of the story they can take a stand for or against. A mystery to unveil. If you ask me, Funcom should damned well take a lot more care to follow up on what they start.

I've been away since may last year .. Now I'm back, and I really hope things have improved with the storyline and all ..

Well, that's my rant.

Story Line
Hi, although I am relitivly new to the game, I have read where FunCom doesn't follow up with the storylines they create. If they know what is good for them, and thier business, they will start! I dont really care if I like the way the story is heading or not, I just want to know that someone is in charge of the storyline and has enough sense to finish what they have started.

I played for a week about 3-4 months ago and then stopped. But now I'm back and I cant get enough of the game right now. If the players aren't allowed more of a roll in directing which way the story line goes, or at least how events that occur are dealt with, then its going to get boring, maybe not today or tommarow but eventually.

Afterall we live, we eat, we sleep, we breath, we bleed and we die in this world FunCom has created for us to play in and get addicted to. We should have more of a deciding factor in what happens.

I would like to see where if one organization takes down 20 towers in a day, then it should have an effect on the story. Or have NPC's there that are the leaders of storyline organizations that we can kill, I'm not saying make it easy to do so, just make that option availible. In real life we have the options to do whatever we want, most of the time the consequinces of our actions keep us from doing so. But it this game, you can never die, so there is anything to worry about.

Yes the Notum Wars is a great add-on, and I like it very much. The whole tower and land control thing is great, it gives opposing factions something to do. But I would rather see more storyline additions (and completions for that matter) that we can have an impact on than cool new gadgets.

Thats my 3 cents. =)
