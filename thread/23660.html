This is rediculous
Has anyone looked at the post count on all the profession forums? Soldiers and in the bottom 3 of post count. And you know what, that means we get the least attention. We do the least *****ing. Why? Because soldiers are lame. Boring. Every class in this game is a soldier that is expanded in some way. Its getting really old that anyone and their brother can run around with the same weapons we use.
Not only can they use the same weapons and deal the same amount of damage as us, they can root, heal, stun, nuke, divest, plunder, have pets, get special armor, get special weapons and the list goes on. What do soldiers have? TMS and a good reflect line. WTF is this? Was this the grand plan for the soldier? The master of the same ranged combat the anyone can do?
Soldiers need some attention and soon. We have been nerfed more that any other profession in the game. We were strong at one time. But with the improvement of other professions and constant nerfs we have fallen to the bottom of the list as far as power is concerned. One crits buffs go self only we will fall even lower.
The one suggestion I have is regarding TMS. They basically took out the Total and Mirror part when people used it to exploit. Now its a Mostly Shiled and Reflect 136 damage. Now dont get me wrong, it is a great nano. BUT, its the only good nano we have and its defensive. Correct me if Im wrong but should this not have a 75% chance of stopping hostile nanos too? Its a shield right? Its obvious that we dont ask for alot. Thats apparent in our post count. Look at the fixer board. Look at the NT board. As a soldier I should be the combat master. Thats all a soldier does. I dont care about heals or roots or divests, I just want to deal some massive damage. More than other professions. But as it stands now I dont.
Maybe if they would fix heals in PvP I might feel different, but as it stands we are weak at best. At 177 using a QL193 flash I rarely kill anyone with my alpha. 3 specials should kill almost anyone from a 177 soldier.
Now I dont want soldier to be the most powerful profession because Im a soldier. I chose a soldier because I assumed they were the most powerful profession when it came to ranged combat. We are not. When you take into account that all we have is good ranged skills and a shield, that doesnt measure up to the benefits that other professions have.
I do not wish for other professions to be nerfed, I only ask that soldier be improved. The way I see it now, a soldier is a base template for all other professions. Everyone is a soldier with some added bonuses. Its hard to comete with a class that can heal at 100% when you can only hit them for 50%. Its hard to compete with a profession that can stun you, shut down your nano, heal themselves at 100%, and have their pet pound you, all while you deal 50% damage. Its hard to compete with a profession that can root you, steal your skills, heal at 100%, hit you with the same gun you are using or a crit crazy shotgun. Thats just 3 professions.
I grow weary of trying to justify why I should keep paying money for empty promises and many shortcomings. Flame is you must. Post please if you agree. The only way to get attention it seems is to ***** the loudest. And it seems we are not making enough noise.
