Unfortunately, implied in your question is the notion that Funcom saw the release as a disaster. A few of the more vocal users saw it as a disaster, but Funcom has a very different point of view.

They sent the game to the distributors, and the distributors got it out as fast as they could, given individual country laws and customs. That hurt some players feelings as they wanted to be the first on the block with the new toys, but it didn't cause Funcom any real bottom line issues.

Unless enough people canceled their accounts to make up for the new revenue from the sales of the game, Funcom wins. Bottom line. I suspect that not nearly enough people canceled their accounts to make this a problem for FC.

So again, the implication that the release was a disaster is subjective, and it isn't clear Funcom would classify it as such. At the end of the fiscal quarter, if sales were good and they got some more subscribers, it is what most businesses put in the "success" folder.
