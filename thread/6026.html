Thoughts on AO
When I first got this game, I really liked it. Swarming with bugs but I could live with it. First the bugs started to feel more and more annoying. New patches come and when I'm downloading I think "Yes! some nasty bugs fixed and improved shops". After that patch my framerate drops alot. Boohoo crappy patch FC. I get my new graphix card; no more low fps(Whopee I like AO again). Then comes the fatal strike, "server maintenance" after that I have been more or less unable to play. My system freezes every few seconds no matter what I'm doing. I'm still trying to come up with a solution. My recent thoughts on AO: "How can this be laful? this is all about scamming people with 30% finished products".

On the CD cover it says "The most impressive online RPG to date" -ign.com.

That sure is true, AO never fails to amaze me with it's new "patches" (translation: bugs, crashes, lowered performance etc, etc).
