Anarchy Online is on a collision course
With Shadowlands a year away and the onlything to look forward to a bunch of towers I dont care about, all I can think is, anarchy online is going down the tubes.

I have alot of fun playing this game, but you know what that has about 10% to do with the actual game, and 90% to do with me.

The game really doesnt' have my interest. I mean what is there to do? There's just no storyline, and no real quest.

I really loved the Nintendo 64 game, Zelda the ocarina of time, cos it's an amazing story and it unfolds as you go and as it unfolds the entire world changes !! That's beautiful


Rubi Ka is meant to be so life like, but if you petition an ARK and ask em " how can I make an appointment with Radament" or anything pertaining the story, the fact is, you cannot.. why? Because the truth is, AO isn't living up to FUNCOM's promises.

What's there to do on Rubi Ka?

1 - Explore the map.. well, in Zelda you could explore the map but you could do it on horseback ( Why Rubi Ka has no rideable horses is beyond me i.e. Gofie?? Ottous?? )
The reason Zelda beats AO hands down is cos if the only quest in Hyrule was to explore the map youd die of boredom, and guess what FUNCOM, thats exactly whats happening on Rubi Ka. Your players are dying of boredom. Obviously Zelda is easier to make since its not multi player or online in realtime

2 - Missions.. A hum drum of inanities. why? Because when I go into a mega huge mission and kill a boss named so and so, it should have an impact upoon Rubi Ka. I just took a tea of 6 into asecret place clan/OT and took out one of their ELITE, but doing so makes no shred of impact on anything on Rubi Ka, which leaves me with a bitter aftertaste once the day is through.
It proves to me this game is placating me with cookie cut out missions that do not reflect on a storyline or the universe, since there apparently is no storyline or universe

3 - PvP.. Yet again , no t a storyline based concept but something youcan find in any game online, except that most PvP games are skill based and I havent seen anything even remotely to do with skill in reference to PvP on Rubi Ka that your average 12 yr old wont master in a few days.

I guess that pretty much wraps it up for Life on Rubi Ka. I would love to say things will get better, but do I really want to wait 5-20 months waiting to find out? I might as well do what I did in 12.5 and cancel my account like all my friends and I did back then when the ***** really hit the fan, some of you remember that cold war.

If I do quit, I'll likely come back when Shadowlands comes out to see if FUNCOM got their act in gear, but I'mnot veryh hopeful right now.

If this were a movie review, I'd say thumbs up at level 1 and slowly moving to a thumbs down as I reach level 150, seems like the thumb hit terminal velocity and nothing is going to help this game get back up on it's feet. Just not enough content and nothing to keep the interest.

Thankyou.

~kupotek

I am looking forward to 14.5 and the introduction of all the content described in the patch notes. Quests. Outdoor hunting. Big scary level 300 critters. I bet those critters will give us docs a reason to level up

Like everything else, this patch won't live up to expectations, neither will the booster pack, neither will Shadowlands. For that matter, neither will SW:G or any of the other new MMORPGs. That is not because Funcom sucks, or because Verant/Sony sucks; it is because we set our expectations too high. Higher than any game designer can deliver on.

But, the new changes coming to AO also will not be as horrible as people fear. That is not because there won't be problems, or bugs, or social changes as a result; it is because we fear irrationally when faced with changes to the way we play and therefore assume the worst from the start.

The only long-standing complaint I have about AO is the lag I get in highly populated places, and I believe some of that lag is the fault of my system's obsolete and underpowered graphics card struggling to render and animate many people. Of course, there is true network latency induced by crowds as well, and even if it requires a rewrite of AO's networking engine, fixing it would result in incredible returns for Funcom.

I think that Emiko has touched upon a very important point here and it is that AO is, as a game, confused. AO doesn't seem to know whether it wants to be a social game or a combat game. Right now, every patch has worked on building up the combat portion of the game--unfotunately, the actual combat in this game is dull, repetitive and just plain sucks. On the other front, the social side of this game has been largely ignored by recent patches even though it is the stronger suit of this game.

I suggest that the combat in AO should go more in the direction of Quality over Quantity. Instead of fighting twenty-three billion leets to level up, combat should be only for important fights (ie. defending your side's town) and sport. Unfortunately, the flaws in the combat in AO run deep, and I wouldn't count on any changes.
