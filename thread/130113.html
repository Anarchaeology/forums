First Game: Hide and Seek
You will have 2hours to find me. I'am gonna be hiding in Borealis somewhere. Might be in the country, might be in the city, might be anywhere in Borealis.

Prize for the first person to find me: Globe of Clarity + 50 mil Creds!

Rules and/or conditions: All lvls and factions are welcome to this game. I'am not gonna be hiding in sneak mode and I won't be in missions.

Start: Borealis Grid at 9am Est Time Wednesday, May14th, 2003




Second Game: Tournament of Arms
I'll be hosting a massive contest of strength and power at the tir arena.

Prize for the strongest: 3 mystery backpacks with lots of goodies in em +100mil creds

Rules and/or conditions: To participate in this event you need to be at least lvl 180. This Game is open to all factions. I will not tolerate any ganking while 2 people are dishing it out. Everyone that participates will be in a line in front of the people that are battling sitting and watching the good show. I plan to take about half the arena for this event hope people will understand.


I'am only gonna accepte a maximum of 50 inscriptions to the tournament so post here with the following info: Name, Level, Profession and Title. I'll try to keep in mind at least for the first round profession inbalances but I'am not saying that you might not be fighting your worst prof enemy. After that all bets are off. For the first few rounds you'll get enough time to recharge all your attacks but as you progress your recovery time will be shortened SO BE WARNED! (Hint: Dimach).

If your not there and we call your battle the person what was supposed to fight and isn't loses by deflaut

Last round we will wait till both contestants are at full power.

If you lose during the first rounds you can either come back and watch the rest or leave all up to you

One more thing. I and a few people will be closely watching the duels because there will be a few buffs I'am not gonna be tolerating if ther're not your profession like:

Reflects: If you want to use reflects you'll have to take 4 hits from you oppenant first to make sure you don't have any reflects and then you can equipe your reflect bracer or use your Hacked graft. If not your disqualified. Only Sols and Engis can have reflects to fight with from the beginning.

Shield: Since we can see em if your a profession that isn'nt supposed to have a shield buff on and you do your disqualified If you want to use your hacked graft you'll have to wait 4 hits from your oppenant. Shielding bracers are ok you can have them on already.

HoTs: Same goes for HoT's we will be checking if you have a Hot befor you fight if you do and you are'nt the following profs you'll get disqualified. Only Fixers,Docs, Advs and Agent as Fixers, Docs or Advs will be allowed to have HoTs befor a Match. You can use your graft after you take 4 hits from your oppenant

Note about Adventurer HoTs: You can only have one if your using the Kin of Tarasque Strategie

Form Changes: Kitty, Doggie, leet, etc. I don't want to see them befor your fight unless your a Adventurer or an Agent as An adventurer. Wait 4 hits from your target befor you use your graft

Layers: Not much to say here if you start absorbing damage and your not a Layer prof we stop the battle. I am aware that Enforcers have Tank armor that absorbes a given amount of damage.

Speechs: They are cool since both people will be getting em if they are crat or engi.

HE: Your not an NT you don't HE ^_^

Note: You cannot be in a team while dueling we will be checking that befor you fight and during. To avoid Wrangles and Adv speechs and anything else I'am missing

All other buffs will be near to impossible to detect sadly so you can get those if you want. We will be checking if during your fight anyone buffs you. If you are buffed in any way with Reflects, Hots, shields or Healing if the person that did it was in the Contest he/she will be disqualifed but the match will go on but it will be up to you to cancle what buff you got in the next 8secondes if not your disqualified.

If it's a heal, Root, Deprive skills Dot and whatever we will see if they came from outside or if your oppenant used a graft and stop the match if it came from the outside. We'll have to wait and start again in these case. I hope this isn'nt going to happen thou cause it would be a shame to use a dimach and then get healed and have to stop. If cases like these do come I'll act on the spot and make sure that the proper actions are taken.

Overall I think it should be fun and hope that all the complexe stuff will not have to be used ^_^


Start: Tir Arena at 12Pm Est time Wednesday, May14th, 2003
