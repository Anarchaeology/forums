Lyons, we, the South Rubi-Ka Society, are a roleplaying guild pure and simple. Lord knows, we aren't much good at anyting else.

In game the guild is sort of a "gentleman's club", we even have a butler now, Wodehouse. Speaking in character our members mostly have jobs outside the Society (Tappert runs a tailor shop in Rome, Heksa an agency called Inter-Activ) The Society is where our characters meet "after work", in the way you and I in real life might join a gym or bridge club.

Again in character the purpose of the Society is to run cultural, charity or educational events for the citizens of Rome and Omni-1, or just social events for its members. Think of real life clubs like Rotary Internationl or the Lion Club. We are not as a guild directly involved in the clan-OT conflict, though of course as individuals it affects our lives in different ways.

What does this really mean in game terms?

We are roleplayers in the SRS and we are looking for ways to expand our characters' experiences beyond the Clam-Omni conflict. Our focus is not levelling or PVP and we do not tend to hunt together as a group. What we aim to do as a guild is meet up in character for, say, a informal drink after we are done hunting, or for specific roleplaying events we organise.

Currently we are running an adventure for our members based on a Society sponsored drug rehab centre Omni-Sewers which is under threat by some local thugs (of course, there is more to it, but that would be telling). Other events included an after-dinner talk on the theory behind metaphysics or simple day trip to visit to the ICC HQ in Andromeda. Basically any excuse to interact in character.

There are no level requirements to join the society. All you need is to be Omni-Tek on Rk1 and have in interest in roleplaying. Ideally we aim to recruit people who are interested in creating events as well; in pen and paper terms ideally our members are as much Gms and they are players, but that is not a requirement. A good sense of humour is of course always bonus :-).

If this sounds like something you might be intersted in visit our website http://rubika.no-ip.org/srs/ or give Marog, Wiretrip, Tappert, Heksa or Savoy a /tell in game. You can also reach me at the following email address gsoylent@hotmail.com

If not, check out the thread http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=15307

for a list of 50 odd other rp guilds.
