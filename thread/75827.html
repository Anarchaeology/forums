hmm /me scratches head in confusion.....I hate to sound like a broken record here, but I don't think a lot of MAs see the full scope of the problem. The word "Crit" seems so burned in everyone's head as the MAIN philosophy of the MA, it clouds our minds and hinders our ability to "think outside the box", it keeps us from realizing alternative solutions. Every suggestion always leads to "more-crit-more-crit-more-crit" and I can tell you, the only way to make "more crit" a semi-positive fix is if Funcom makes the MA an exception to the -50% crit rule that was applied in 14.6. And it only solves PvP damage problem.

Celestiness, no I don't think your suggestion is overpowered. Quite the contrary, I believe it is severely UNDERpowered. A ring that gives 6% would equate to 3% in PvP, hardly worth considering. My previous post was somewhat unclear and maybe misunderstood. What I was trying to point out was, the only way a crit ring would help an MA's damage in PvP, is if the ring gave upwards around 40% chance to crit. Add that 40% to our 15% scopes and our 24% UVC you get 79% chance to crit (say 80 for the sake of this argument). Now divide that in half for the -50% rule and you get 40% chance to crit which is where we started before this stupid nerf. Nevertheless, a ring with 40% crit would be too powerful in PVM so it's silly to suggest. BTW, the other effects on the ring were nice

The answer is right under our noses, we see it all the time.
quote:
...my fixer with manex allready does the same or more dmg then my MA in pvm.
Yeah my fixer does too, and she's 45 levels lower than my MA. The difference is the base damage in the weapon. With the manex you could care less if you crit or not, because high non-crit damage is about the same and just as dependable as an MA's crit damage @40%. The other beauty about high base damage weapons like the Manex is that, you're almost never evaded. The problem with boss mobs evading MAs.....directly related to low base damage.

Yet another problem with being crit dependant is its indirect effect on OUR evades and resist. The low light targeting scope has become a religious icon to the PvP MA. Scopes are almost a necessity for the MA in PvP just to be a contender. I'd go as far to say that without one you could be considered gimped. The cost to wear one? Fighting at near full aggressive, forfeiting your innate ability to evade and resist nanos. Want a better chance at resisting roots and plunders? Want to evade more damaging blows from that enforcer? Take off the scope, fight full defense at 1/1.

The New MA (just my dream) Higher base damage (around 65-450 as opposed to 65-280), lower crit modifier (280 as opposed to 500), No scope, Add an "All Offense +100" buff to UVC which raises your Attack Rating (similar to the soldiers Automatic Targeting). The result? More frequent and higher base damage, slightly less frequent yet equivalent crit damage, slightly more damage over time, evade more, resist more, fists hitting at 1/1. To me, that sounds more like an MA but it's just my opinion based on observation.

Back to playing my fixer...
