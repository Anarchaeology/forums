We know originally:
  1. Delayed Health Payments were intended to heal the team immediately (the heal portion did not attempt to distribute the health over time)
  2. Delayed Health Payments were intended to extract more HP from the caster than was given to the team (the total damage done by a single execution to the executing trader was greater than the health restored by a single execution to the team)
  3. Delayed Health Payments were intended to extract the 'full payment' over the span of a full minute.


Item 1 works as intended. Item 2 is bugged. Item 3 is either a bug or a bug AND a tremendous design flaw. If it were originally intended that the traders were to be unable to execute another Delayed Health Payment while the DoT was active, then it was originally intended that a trader could only execute a team heal once per minute. This would place the use frequency of Delayed Health Payments, including the QL 1 version, beyond that of any nanoformula in the game including team heals that restore full health. This would be both bug and design flaw. Fixing the bug portion would be to make it so DHPs weren't capable of execution during the DoT for the intended length. The bug being in the fact that the traders were capable of reexecuting the nano during the DoT. The design flaw would be in thinking that anyone would find the entire line of nanos useful for more than pretty nano effects.

The other option for Item 3 is that the trader was supposed to be able to reexecute the nanoformula during the DoT. The only way for Item 3 and Item 2 to work together would be to ensure every execution of a DHP extracted its payment by having the DoTs stack. Depending on implementation this would leave the trader vulnerable to hours worth of damage from stacked DoTs (every execution queuing its DoT so that when the currently running one expired, the next one ran). Or it would leave the trader vulnerable to possibly highly damaging 'ticks' (each DoT adding it's damage per tick to a stack for its duration, with each tick doing the total damage in the stack). Fixing this bug would require making the DoTs stack.

To do absolutely nothing but fix the bugs in the nanos would have meant making the nanos do full damage and making it impossible to reexecute for the full 60 seconds under the current implementation. Or it would have meant making the nano do full damage and making the DoTs stack so the 'price' of each individual nano was done over its intended time. Neither approach was being taken so the nano was 1) Having the bugs fixed and 2) Getting balanced at the exact same time.

Now the people that are stating just fix the bugs need to look at the break down carefully. One way to repair it would make the nano line completely useless (60 second delay between executions). The other, which I personally would have no problem with, would mean traders could easily kill themselves by chaincasting as many PDHP in a 60 second period (I did the math and in 60 seconds, the final tick for the 1st execution would be in a tick worth over 2k damage with a total damage over 13k. With 11 more DoTs still remaining). I'd have no problem with this solution because I think myself controlling my executions of a nano through fear of fatal consequences is better than an arbitrary control made through game mechanics. And having the time of the DoT stack would be vulnerable to tons of bugs (365 days later, you're still taking DoT damage) .

But Blackmane said that stacking would require redesigning how nanoformulas are implemented. And as much as this nano line involves me, that's a bit much to ask for over a single nanoformula. The next option presented was to be locked out on all DHPs for 15 seconds. This was unacceptable to me because it would leave my team vulnerable, especially to chain executed AoE attacks, by not allowing any team healing from me for too long a length of time. Team health status is too dynamic and fluid to refuse healing for that long.

So without completely redesigning the entire nanoformula implementation, Funcom offers this option: locking out the specific nanoformula in question to allow its price to be paid as intended but leaving team healing open by allowing executions of lesser DHPs. To me that's fair.

Traders stating they'd need pages of shortcut bars are exaggerating things. You'd only need to keep track of 3 nanoformulas. With a 1 second execution time (through nanoinit) and nearly 4 second recharge time, you'd only be able to execute any nanoformula every 5 seconds. Best DHP + 2nd best DHP + 3rd best DHP = 15 seconds. By the time you're through executing the 3rd, the 1st is availible again.

Traders stating they shouldn't have to pay a price need to look at the profession again. Almost all our nanoformulas that give something to someone else comes at a price higher than what's actually given. Skill wrangles take more skill from us than they give by design. The same with AC transfers. This is what the Trader Profession is all about. And most real Traders knew the prices we pay for our skills and were willing to pay it when they chose the profession. We just want the price to be reasonable for the team. I haven't heard a trader state they want a skill wrangle to give more skills than it take. They just want the damn things to last longer and not eat as much NCU. Same with all our transfers.
