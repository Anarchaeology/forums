OMNIPOTENCE - The friendliest Omni guild on Rubi-Ka 1
If you have watched the news on the Anarchy Online Community Site and AO Stratics, then you know we are a very active guild.

Omnipotence, an Omni-Tek guild, is about loyalty, honesty, respect, help, assistance, family and closely-knittedness. There are no minimum induction levels, there is no need to play 16 hours at a stretch every day. We welcome all members, big and small, professionals and newbies. If you want to be part of a professional guild with nice people who want to play an active part in the Anarchy Online story, then this is the guild for you.

If you're after loot and power leveling, then maybe you should look further

GUILD BACKGROUND
The founders were among the first free settlers on Rubi-Ka. Taking note of the savage environment they decided to team up to help eachother survive. The founding of the guild as it looks today is a direct response to the clan uprisings, to protect each other and attempt to expand the guilds authority. Although every member of the guild is expected to rise as one when one or a group of members are threatened, no one is forced to do so...

GUILD OBJECTIVE
We are in a world of turmoil and chaos where friend or foe can change as swiftly as the wind. The members of Omnipotence were drawn together to go beyond the old worlds laws and bring justice back to Rubi-Ka!

Interested? Head to www.omnipotence.de.vu, read our guild charter and write an application on the message boards (Application Forum).

We look forward to hearing from you!

Galious
Omnipotence Guild Director
www.omnipotence.de.vu
