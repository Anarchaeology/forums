Character Notebook/Filofax!
There should be some kind of notebook with your character. I hate having to write things down on a piece of paper. Its a big hassle.. and most of the time you never find that note again..

Some kind of personal filofax would be very cool. Example: You found this cool bar you would like to visit again, only you are totally lost.. Would be nice if you could just save your current coordinates, provided you have a map, with an entry in you filofax/notebook.. Favorite places/town/category.. ex:

Favrite places/Omni-I/Leisure
1.Baboons nightclub
2.Rompa bar

Also, a note system and to-do lists etc. etc. And you could incorporate the existing reference menu thing into it. What you think?
