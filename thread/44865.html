Late Game - BORING
The later game, past 100, the game gets really boring. I mean really boring... I haven't done anything in weeks, I think I level'd twice... I have the best things I can get on( almost... due to stat limitations), my attack rating just max'd till 150, I'm running good nano's and I have all my best weapons, only one thing missing( GA of course, fixer specific, but lets say I was NT or ENF or MA etc, dare I say Engineer).
And I have to ask, what do you do after this point? I mean, you can level more? You can get more ip to play with? More HP/Nano?
A better title bar? PVP?
All these don't really justify spending more time doing the same missions, over and over and over again. There is no outdoor content, and little coming, unles 14.5 is drastic as 14.2 & 14.4. But with the "BOOSTER PACK" announcement, I see really no reason to continue playing.

PVP? Yeah it is fun but it's all about throwing as many specials out as you can and getting criticals. Even then, the system is still broken imho, and I think many others think so also.

I have 2 weeks left to decide, I'll just level and see what happens, but like most... I've become a whiner, and I think I can justify this because 1st: lack of content 2nd: promises keep getting broken 3rd: shadowlands delayed(last straw...) 4th: pvp broken 5th: bad implementation of new content 6th: bugz, ever since launch...

Oh and Camelot is laggy and kinda stupid, I mean 1 entrance determines this freaking place... and Omni never tries... I mean put some effort into it. I used to be omni and went clan because of this... lack of "cojones"

See you at reclaim. 75-200 pvp gap, kinda unfair
