Player to player shop machines?
I know player to player trade shops have been suggested before but I am going to do it again, how about a terminal where you drop links to your items for sale, enter some info and enter a price.

Players who want to buy something use the same terminal to "search" for items (by name, QL and/or stats) and then contact the seller to negotiate or just to buy. Item references stay in this database for a fixed period  say 1 week  or until deleted by the creator. Also it would be better if items didnt show up unless the owner was online.

If you made access to the sellers side (NOT the buyers side) dependant on comp lit versus the QL of the item for sale (Or just make it trader/fixer only) it would really enhance those classes.

(Because then traders really could act as merchants, buying bulk stuff (their junk from missions) from other players at better prices than the NPC merchants and selling it to other players without having to spam the shopping channels for the rest of eternity)

At the moment the shopping channels are OKish for advertising buffs and services but a REAL pain to buy from or sell even slightly obscure items like specific nanos. You have to sit there watching the spam scroll up your screen for ever and ever waiting for the one item your looking for. Of course the odds are the item you want will turn up as soon as you switch the channel off and go back to your mission or whatever.

I know this has been suggested and discussed a long time ago but I couldnt find any threads talking about it so I thought I would start a new one.

(Please post a link if I missed a thread talking about this specifically)

I think we're talking about two different, but related, concepts: an actual shop terminal and a bulletin board for advertising.
