Loot, level, GA and Ol' Uncle PH
I'll try to keep this short.

I'd like to see a loot system where players don't get loot from mobs much lower than their character.
Yup, you heard me. Level 150 NT nuking for NS I - no loot. Shooting dyna camp bosses for GA 1 when you're level 100+ - no loot. Killing level 30ish Unce Pumpkinheads in Lush fields (this was me last night) - no loot.

I'm not sure where to draw the line, setting it to the same as the XP seems like a bit narrow, but maybe twice that.

This is only for mobs in the wild, where highlevel characters can spoil the fun for lower level characters. No risk for that in missions.

I'm not sure if this is doable, but it's something I'd like to see (I think).

The condemed subway is a good start I think when it comes to letting the lowlevels keep the lowlevel action without risking getting their kills/loot stolen by a high level camper.
