Simple Question:CZ or Cosmik (and side note)
I am sure this has been asked and answered many times, but seeing the questions regarding the Trader (Sta) req on new armor.... it reminded me again to ask this...


Why no non-general INT buffs?



As a side note, As I have been keeping up with SWG for these last many months (with the limit of Jedi) I think AO (funcom) has one major *potential* advantage. The *potential* advantage is for the player who likes to have a somwhat classic ranged *caster* type char. I know there are higher priorities for the team (some other professions) *but* would suggest that finding a way to better meet the needs of those players who enjoy the *RANGED* casters classes/professions playstyle might be a good advantage point for the funcom team. Unless you soon find a way to address those concerns, you will have lost that *potential* playstyle choice advantage. IMHO

This was not meant as a rant, complaint, troll bait..on any other negative behavior just a thought.

Find a way to better meet the needs of the *ranged* casters, otherwise a second mass migration *might* take place.

(It *could* be your advantage, you might want to use it before it's too late

The reason I mention this is in reading the Q&A session feedback posted on the NT board I noticed a few more "working as intended" and "Not going to change" type comments than I think the games current/future positioning would warrant. I really do like this game *but* will change if somone else does a better job with ranged casters, I don't imagine I would be alone. One might argue that SWG will provide more for (crafters, mele, weapon and H2H folks) but as of yet not ranged casters......will AO keep that *potential* advantage?? Guess time will tell

dB

Pure "ranged casters" should IMHO have/be the following

1. Highest DPS DD in game *WITHOUT QUESTION*
2. Low Defense
3. Low HP
4. Highest range (other than one shot sniper types) but significant range advantage to all others.
5. Special infrequent to use but available to have "get out of jail free card"
6. Hard to level (see # 2 and #3) but nessecary for high end encounter due to #1
7. Need to manage aggro activley (see #1 again)

And some ability/skill that sets them apart from hybrid and pet users.

As I see it Number 2,3 and 7 are being met currently... I hope # 1, 4, and 5 are addressed soon as I think the folks at Funcom have worked hard to create a great game....and a advantage is only a advantage if you ..."take advantage"
