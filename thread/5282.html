Neutrals: no-tab flag?
Someone mentioned this.. somewhere. One of the other sub-boards, in one of Shadowolf's cross-postings of his recent neutrals thread. ;)

The idea was that neutrals shouldn't show up by tabbing (that is, pressing the TAB key - or whatever you've changed it to- to automatically cycle through visable targets); the client would just skip over them.

I'm beating myself over the head about this. It -seems- like there should be something fundamentally wrong about the idea, because it looks too good to be true. There's no appreciable downside.

I think the main reason this was conceived was as a safeguard against being attacked in PvP/low suppression gas zones. Since neutrals can't attack first - and after the first hit or two it may be too late - it seems a pretty fair tradeoff. It's not like we can sneak up and do terrible amounts of damage and you can't target us. ("we" and and "you" being rather fluid; work with me here ;).

It's also pretty easy to reason out "reality"-wise. Consider that the TAB function is sort of an automatic lock-on that a character's nanos/implants take care of. They pick up alignment signals and specific genetic traits out of the surrounding area. This is obvious for Clan and Omni; for monsters, guards, and the like, their geno-types are already registered and catalogued. Neutrals maybe, by some quirk, are passed over; or they invest in some basic type of jamming-bot that blocks it? I don't know. I'm sure a plausible reason could be hammered out, I'm thinking as I'm typing here.

Regardless of the how.. what's wrong with it? Again, there must be something I'm missing, a basic flaw. What is it?

And to be fair, I don't see this having any kind of high priority; or, indeed, even programmingly (is that a word? ;) feasible, depending on how AO is written (no smartass remarks, please). So it's more of a conversation piece than actual hope, thus Game Mechanics and not Envisioned or such. ;)

My take on it, as a neutral, is that I'm not an enemy so I should show up on your target as one. It's unfortunate that we are nearly in the year 30k and to this date modern AIs can't figure out who's friendly or not.

My reasoning for it is basically RPing... I'm not part of the war and have declared myself out of it. I'm not included in the story, for the most part. I don't want to be included in the dying over the planet.

As I see it, I'll still be a valid target, you just have to look for me a bit harder if you are that intent on killing neutrals. Otherwise, there is no point to it for you to kill me. You gain nothing, except maybe a title and the dislike of yet another neutral. As it is, I already have nothing to do with anybody that has a title in front of there name: title = neutral killer in my little private universe. I think other neutrals agree with that statement.

Reality wise, I don't think it would be that hard to figure out who's neutral... There must be some implant that identifies a company employee as Omni-Tek because they are the only ones that show up as friendly. There's one that identifies rebels the same way and for the same reason. From that it's easy to figure out who's neutral.

Funcom has done very little to give us a way to defend ourselves. Our citizens, NPCs and Guards, are hunted and killed in the streets of our cities and we are helpless to do anything. I know many of us want to help them somehow - either by letting us defend our towns when our MOBs get attacked or by making the cities 100% like the rebel and OT cities. At some point, OT or the rebels will be able to take over our cities and we'll not be able to participate in defending it. If we are to be excluded from the War that way then we should be taken out of PvP.

Anyway, that's my ideas on why I don't want to be a target... I hope my rambling made sense. I can't help but follow up with some thoughts on how to do it.

Game mechanics follow:

Personally, I'm for being removed from the [tab]. I've seen others mention adding some sort of icon to identify affiliation along with the health bar & name and leaving us as targets. That would work; though, I personally distrust it because in the heat of combat it'll be easy to shoot first and check icons later. Another option is to let the player decide wether neutrals will show up on his/her [tab]. This way if you wanna play as a neutral friendly OT or an either-your-with-us-or-against-us, fence-sitters-are-worse-than-rebel-scum OT then the choice is yours.
