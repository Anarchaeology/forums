Size and Shape: Hidden attributes
I chose my characters appearance thinking they wouldn't affect his peformance at all. I thought these choices were purely cosmetic, but that doesn't seem to be the case.

Short characters appear to run faster by default. Test this out yourself in first person view (so you're not swayed by the character animations) or have a race with someone with newly created characters. One tall, one short. Differences in Internet and CPU speed may not give an accurate indication so it's best to just test this out yourself in the clinic by changing between tall and short and running around a bit.

Is it just me, or do shorter characters just run faster?
