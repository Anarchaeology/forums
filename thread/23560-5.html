After being somewhat disappointed of the proposed changes in the upcoming Advent lovin patch I read it again and give it some comments:

quote:
1. New versions of all the existing damage shields Adventurers have. These will inflict slightly more damage than the other ones. The major difference is that the absorption shield effect on them now extends to all damage types and will refresh itself every 2 minutes. Naturally, they don't stack with the existing ones. They are self-only and have a lower NCU cost than the others.
Sounds somewhat good, but most Advents dont use them at higher level. And whilst it says that it will absorb all damage type there are some questions here. How much will they absorb? If they are anything like our current ones they still wont be any much of use. And why so long refresh? Will the damage shields give different type of damage, i.e chemical damage, poision damage, fire damage and so on? As it is now it only give damage. It would be real nice to see for example chemical damage shields.

quote:
2. Damage shield upgrades. These are secondary damage shields that will stack with the other adventurer damage shields. Self-only. Can only be cast if the adventurer has another damage shield running. Later versions also provide minor damage and HP buffs. Last one also provides 2% deflection.
Damage shield upgrades. To me it sounds that these are our current ones And if they are our current ones will they or will they not stack with the new damage shields? At point 1 it says it wont stack with the existing ones. Or are we getting two new damage shields  giving us total 3 different shields? Later versions also provide minor damage and HP buffs. How much is minor damage and how much HP?

quote:
3. Buff line for 1h-edged. Also does a minor fast attack buff. Last ones do a minor damage and parry buff. Self-only.
1HE buff is nice, but it would be even better if it also buffed 2HE. Many advents are using 2HE and there are also adventurer specific 2HE swords coming in 14.2, so this is a must. How much will they boost? Anything less than 50 pts will be a joke. And make it stack with the generic ones.

quote:
4. Buff line for pistols. Also does a minor fling shot buff. Last ones do a minor damage and perception buff. Self-only.
Pistol adventurers will for sure like this one. A boost less than 50 pts will be a joke. Make it stack with the generic ones.

quote:
5. Combo multi-range + multi-melee buff. Self-only. Last ones in the line also do minor evade buffs.
Same here a boost less than 50 pts will be a joke.

quote:
6. Nukes when in pit lizard form. One single-target nuke. One AOE nuke. One AOE DoT.

7. Massive HP buff when in pit lizard form (7000HP). Useable once every 20 minutes.
Self-only.
Sounds good perhaps at FC, but since no advent really uses CF: PL. I suggest lowering the HP a bit and give it when we are morphed as a sabretooth instead. Btw. how long will the HP buff last? Is the nukes when morphed self-only or available to the people we eventually cast it on? How much damage will the nukes do?

quote:
8. Damage and evade team aura when in wolf form. More effective on people who are also in wolf form. Later versions also do minor runspeed and amsmodifier buffs.

9. Perception buffs when in wolf form. Basically the opposite of the agent's Ruse of Taren line (top level version if +600 perception). Can be cast on others if they are also in wolf
form. Also provides minor evasion and HP buffs.
The only time when I use wolf form is when I blitz missions. But the perception buff might come in handy when equiping the new LLTS that will come in 14.2 Hopefully it will stack with Eagle Eye (unless FC decide that they will remove it). At point 8 it says that later versions also do minor runspeed  buffs. Does this mean that the current runspeed buffs on the wolves will change?

quote:
10. Instant-cast self-only heals when in sabretooth form. Can be used every 25-35 seconds. Very low variance in the heals so the adventurer knows what to expect from the heal.

11. Self-only damage+aggdef buffs when in sabretooth form. Can be used every 2-3 minutes. Increases damage and amsmodifier. Later ones also increase dmsmodifier, aggdef and eventually initiatives.
Will the insta-cast self-only heals interfere with our normal healings? Will it interfere with First Aid- and/or Nano-kits? Do we have to be selected to cast them? (Casting all self-only morphs does not require that we are selected). Why cant we get a HP boost when morphed as a sabretooth? Why do we have a negative run speed on our cat morphs at all? Will the later ones also increase our critical increase chance?

quote:
12. 12 hour leet charms when in leet form. This is just for fun. The adventurer can charm a leet for 12 hours and have a little pet. Higher levels of this mean that you will be able to charm higher level leets - Leet, Eleet, Leetas, and finally Soleet.
This is probably fun when socializing  but it must also be fun to play the game. And this is not my idea of having fun playing the game
We really should get a charm or root nano imo. Being wilderness masters, we have learned how to control animals (and this also should work on humans as well). For Petes sake, we have learned how to morph ourselfs

Finally some questions to FC:
We are masters of outdoor, why dont we get a Map Buff?
Why cannot we have more variations of our morphs? (3 parrot forms, 3 wolf forms, 2 sabretooth forms)
Why cant we get special attacks when morphed?
Why doesnt we sound like a wolf or sabrethooth when morphed?
Why are the most of the proposed changes self only? We still dont get any real good to offer team members imo. Still no valuable buff that we can offer other players.
Why dont we get any more AC buffs?
Why arent the Gutting Hooks available in missions or in shops?
Why is some of our Advent only stuff (ring, hood and cloak) dropped by the dragon?
Where will the new nanos be available? Shops? Dropped as instruction discs from the Pit Lizards that roam Rubi-Ka? Dropped only by Boss Mob when you pick a ql200 mission?
When is the patch coming? Are we next after Agents?
