First, I would like to thank everybody for the support they have shown us during our rebuild.

I have tried not to post much during this time for a couple reasons:

1. Web had the board admins delete my posts (the first one accidently had his real first name in it - the second one I'm still waiting for an explanation). CZ did respond to my email asking why my posts were being deleted - tho he seemed to be under the false impression that this was just something personal between Web and me.

2. Once I read Web's posts I realized there was nothing I could say or do to him that he wasn't already doing to himself. I haven't seen that much spin outside of a centrifuge.

However I think I can manage to say a few things about what happened and stay within whatever guidelines the board ops have in mind. Hopefully after hearing what started all of this you will understand how truly pathetic this all really was.

This was not a personal fight that spilt over into the game. Up until Web demanded leadership of the guild we had no issues other than Web's occasional outbursts and his tendancy to get into fights both in guild and out. Web has made a lot of trouble for me both in this game and in AC. I have always viewed this trouble in light of his accomplishments and our friendship and I probably gave him too much of a pass on his past actions because of this.

We had a similar incident occur a while back - a general logged on and ran a kick script. I was able to log in and kick the general before too much damage had been done. While I don't excuse what that general did, Web caused the incident. The general had not been logged in in about a month - so when he did log in Web confronted him and told him he needed to /org leave and be reentered at a lower rank. They had fought with each other in the past. I had told Web not to make contact with the other general on numerous occassions.

There was a Squad Commander who had not logged in in a while who came back. I told Web in no uncertain terms that he was not to contact the SC. Web explained his position on leaving people in positions of authority who we not recently active. His biggest fear was that they would run a kick script (anyone smell the irony? ). I told Web that I understood his opinion - I just didn't agree with him, particularly since I knew the SC and he didn't, and that he was not to contact the SC. At that time, I made the comment to him that there is only one President of the Obsidian Order, not two. He has twisted that statement around quite a bit - but now you at least see what it was originally.

I logged in and found he had confronted the SC - who not knowing he was speaking only for himself, did /org leave and was re-entered at a lower rank. When I found out I told Web he had earned his demotion. Web's response was demand the leadership of the guild - saying I had promised it too him. If he had thrown in a couple of "it's my birthday presents, my precious" it would have fit the way he was ranting. Web began posting on our internal leader board about how I should step down and give him the guild. I was fed up with Web - but in order to save what friendship we had left - I decided to post that I would step down and allow elections for new guild President - provided Web was not a candidate - tho I also allowed that if there were more than a couple of leaders who wanted him - he could run also - just I would leave the org if he were in control.

I thought that would be the end of it and the guild would just elect a new leader. What happened instead was I was asked to remain President by all of the guild leadership. Since it was Christmas and Web was filling our leader board with the same type of posts people have seen here - I asked everyone to put it all aside until after the hoildays. When that didn't work, I ended the discussion by telling everyone that I would stay. As a peace offering to Web - I said I would leave him a general - but that he had to behave like one.

What happened next has been pretty well covered in this topic. Web waited a couple of days - then ran a kick script - told everyone I had either done it or that I had left the game. He locked all of the leaders out of the leader board and the guild board. He destroyed our towers and replaced them with ones from a new org he created with a similar name. He used the guild bots to spam all old members with his story and began recruiting them into his fake guild.

It worked for about a day - then people started to notice there were too many holes in his explanations. Not the least of which is why I as guild President would run a kick script (/org terminate is easier) and why if I ran the script were all the SC's and Generals still in the old guild.

It has been a lot of work putting things back together. I have had to take time away from my family in the early days to stay logged in just answering questions. Then I had to spend time with them to make up for it - leaving the org alone for a couple of days at a very important time. Somewhere in all of that I had to fit in two trips to the cemetary. I will never forgive Web for ruining my vacation and taking a good part of the joy out of the game for me, but other than that I have no malice towards him. It is sad to see someone commit reputation suicide. All of the things he took from the org can and will be replaced in time - and largely improved upon.

Once again thank you all for you support - even the stinking clanners :P
