A good night ends badly
This was sent to me from one of my many contacts. quite interesting no?



Report of events at Rompa's Vixen Ent. party.

What is to be expected?

This is of course a planet where Anarchy is ruling right now is it not?

The party held by Vixen Ent. and Gridstream Productions, as always packed the house at Rompa's tonight. DJ Tarryk spinned the Mpegs as always to a crowd of willing listeners. Aktrez the host of hosts, made sure everyone had a good time, even letting them into a bit of her life.

then things turn for the worse, I do not quite know the full story, getting to many different ones from many sides. But, I will tell you what I know.

Being a silent observer, I watched as events unfolded before the crowded dance floor of Rompa's as the tale of Aktrez played it's final chapter.

Being threatened the night befor by unsettling knews that the clans where after her thinking she was thier property, and after having been kidnapped then released, What happened next blew my mind.

In the midst of a crowed of Omni-Pol, Omni-Tek Special Ops, and other well known Omni-Tek orginiaztions, Aktrez, in the club she and her orginization serves at, was kidnapped yet again, by Lupoh, Dudicas and Kaghelion of the clan aligned org. The Mockers.

After attempts made by Gridstreams President Wyldthing, and Omni-Tek employee and friend of Aktrez, Missmaul. Lupoh stated his terms of Aktrez's release. but no avail the demands where not met.

Omni-Pol nor any other aligned Omni-Tek org officials seemed to make any attempt at uncovering this tragidy. Well, except for one of Omni-Pol's members, Gencop. Who after a half hearted attempt of getting information from the fixer Lupoh, proceeded to dance the night away.

From here I am not quite sure exactly what happened. Where Aktrez went, who was looking for her, or even who had her. It seemed that no one in the immediate area even cared. Yes, I am sure a few cared, but from the crowed it did not seem like alot.

After following a few people, a new element in the story arrived, A Metaphysicist known as Noraxu entered the scene, and proceeded to warn all of the clansmen in the bar to enjoy them selves for it will be quite different very soon, and they are not welcomed there. (interesting no?)

After his brief but expicit warning, he proceeded back to the booth area of Rompa's and entered into a discussion with some members of the crowed (I will send you the names later).

The information I gathered was more of bragging and boasting in many of the onlookers eyes, to me it was more. (I will explain in person.)

Now, after I recieved your communications I left the bar. I will report back when I have more news.

Sikkes..........



After reading this I headed straight for Rompa's to see if there was anything I could do. Upon arrival, I suppose you could say that Sikkes was a bit wrong about Omni-Pol, or was he? It seemed led by an Omni-Pol operitive, Aktrez had been found, and was led back to Rompa's. there at the footsteps shots where fired and one of Omni-Teks own Cayll of The Corterie was shot from the Omni-Pol officer, Aktrez was then led into the bar.

What happens next saddens me greatly, Although I am not sure exactly what happened, But with out warning, Aktrez announced her departure from the Planet Rubi-Ka. And the story ends there.

Although I have had recent disagreements with Aktrez, She has been a mainstay in the landscape of Omni-Entertainment and of Rubi-Ka its self. Her role as an entertainer has been nothing but filled with troubles and turmoil, she has been a bright light to those who have visited the popular bar known as Rompa's sinces it's opening.

If for what ever reason it was that caused you to leave the planet Aktrez, consider this, Regardless of whose toes you stepped on, regardless of who had a different opinion, regardless of anything else, The friendships you have made, the stories that will be told about you and your surrounding cast of characters will always in my eyes be one of the best things that happened on this planet.

I wish you only the best in what ever you decide to do.

Sincerely.
