For me, at lvl 30-40 I hunted at an oasis in the rhino cockpit. Just east of the outpost on the acidic river in newland. The acidic river btw is a LOT east and some north of newland city.
For lvl 40-50(which i just hit, yea!) I killed in lush fields/mutant domain. To get there, grid into Harrys, and head southwest. Its southwest of the omni outpost along the road. Theres a hill there with a LOT of mutants, and a lot of people killing there, pretty easy to get a group going.
