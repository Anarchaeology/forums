Adventurer Suggestions/Thoughts
Below are a few improvements for the Adventurer Profession.

Please note that these suggestions are a collective of personal as well as those suggested by a few of my fellow Adventurers.

1. Weapons - Adventurers, like all other professions, are not bound to select a particular weapon. We DO start out with a pistol in hand and with green bars for pistols, so pistols seem to be the likely choice. Unfortunately, MOST 120-150+ Adventurers cannot survive using pistols simply because there do not seem to be any powerful pistols to supliment the higher levels of this profession. Either add more powerful pistols to help 150+ Adv's, or provide a green skill alternative from LvL 1.

2. Adventurers are supposed to be the jack-of-all trades. We know that we will never heal the best or fight the best, or PvP the best or run the fastest or have a great selection of nanos...Having said this, what exactly DOES the Adventurer have as a distinct advantage? It seems like most other professions have at least one profession related skill that cannot be matched by others. The common arguement would be the Adventurers' morphing ability. We all know how completely useless they are so I won't even go into detail. HOW ABOUT Adding something to our profession which is PROFESSION related such as the ability to summon wildlife (outside of cities) to aid the adventurer when attacked. this would be a great addition!

3. Soloing- The Adventurer is supposed to be adept at soloing. Doing missions as well as being self-sufficient enough to run long solo missions. If this is the case, then why don't we have any special (EFFECTIVE) skills that would enable us to do so. Most Adv's have a hard enough time GETTING to a distant mission due to our lack of decent speed buffs. More importantly, when we DO attempt to explore the outer portions of any city, we don't have enough power to handle more than 3-4 light oranges let alone a SINGLE red monster. If we are supposed to excel at this, then please develop/provide us a tool to do so.

4. Flying- This is a joke to most Adv's. By the time we reach a level high enough to take advantage of the more powerful bird morphs, we already have enough experience/cash to dump into a top of the line flying car. What therefore is our advantage in Morphing? I suggest that the skill requirements to morph into an effective Reet/Leet/Sabre/etc; be reduced enough to make them a viable enough tool for the Adventurer.

Well..I could go on, but these seem to be the biggest concers amongst many of the Adventurers that Iam friends with or that I RP with. We are not seeking omnipotency, simply the tools that we need to survive within the logical structure of our profession.

Thank you for listening!

Map Upgrades
I've seen several recommendations for "Map Upgrades" for Adventurers as something to give us a unique edge or purpose. So what could we really get thats not available or currently in game that would not require a serious recoding of how maps work in general?

#1) Monster Type Identifier

If the Adv. has this upgrade, perhaps he could type in the console

/map show "Nightcrawler" color yellow
/map show "daft nano addict" color blue
Then if any nightcrawlers are in radar range yellow dots will show up instead of the normal red dots for "Mob", daft nano addicts will show up as blue dots.

#2) Aggressive/Passive Identifer

This upgrade lets the Adv. distinguish passive vs. aggressive Mobs and NPCS in radar range

Passives show up as minus signs, aggressives show up as plus signs - this concept can stack with upgrade #1 so that if you defined nightcrawlers to show up as yellow plus signs.

#3) Identifier a player

/map show player Avatarius green

The player named avatarius, if in radar range, will show as a green dot.

#4) Set waypoint! [lots of people have been begging for this]

Lets you set a x,y coordinate then puts the pointer on the compass and a cross hatch on the map - just like for missions. this lets you set arbitrary points then follow them.

#5) Set Team waypoint

Same thing except all your team mates will get the same waypoint and designator on the map

#6) Set homing beacon

Lets other players "home" in on, your position will show up as a mission type cross hatch on the master map and regular radar map and team mates can follow their arrows on the compass to arrive at your location like missions.

/map set beacon on
/map set beacon off

/map find beacon Avatarius

Hence, if my beacon is set on, someone could do a /find on me, get the waypoint uploaded then follow their compass to my position anywhere within a zone.
