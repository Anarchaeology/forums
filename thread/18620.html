Rant about Camping, and Uniques in general
<rant>
somthing has to be done about uniques, and mission mobs...i dont wanna see AO turn into another camp-fest like EQ was. More and more unique locations are getting out in the open, which means more competetion at the spawns.

Prime example is dodga. i dunno how bad alvin gets, but dodga can be a mess most the time. 2 or 3 people sit around the dodga spawn at almost all hours of the day, and most of the time at least 1 or 2 more login minutes before his spawn. turning in cards to him doesnt depsawn him, getting the mission or turning in the brain does. Yet people dont have the decency to let the card people go first, as soon as he spawns it becomes a clickfest and whoever has the fastest reaction time and connection, gets him.

Simple fix to this, put unique missions on a PLAYER timer not a spawn timer. for example, instead of dodga despawning for EVERYONE, just put a flag on the player that doesnt allow them to do the next part of the mission for a certain amount of time. the mission would still take just as long and be just as hard to complete, you just wouldnt have to wait in line.

Unique items....who knows how to fix this one but it needs somthing done, because it sucks how it is. whoever is the highest level, or whoever has the best damage output (im guessing camping uniques can REALLY suck for support classes who dont have friends willing to help out). Just spent 7 hours at Tri Plumbo spawn in foremans office, after the first three hours to my amazement i was the only person at the spawn...but then SECONDS before the spawn, a soldier and a fixer both red to me come out of nowhere (just logged in) and muscle me easily out of the spawn with a couple quick alpha strikes. theres 3 hours of my time spent at the spawn gone, then after another 3 hours at the camp, same thing happens this time with an agent.

the thing is, it seems when it comes to uniques nothing matters anymore, side,organisation, friends who knows i know of friendships and orgs destroyed because of ninja-looting at uniques.

look at shades of lucibration....10-200 million credits for them just because its so hard to get in on the camp.

it gives powergamers a huge advantage over casual gamers. dont like ebayers? crap like this is the cause of ebayers.

I dont have a problem with rare items, in fact i think its a decassary part of the game. but the way FC is going about doing them is a pain in the ass.

Maybe a fix would be to have the monster that drops the items spawn in random spots? Maybe have the unique items spawn on random monsters? Maybe move more of the rares to 25% zones so you can do somthing about ninja looters and jackass's? (the 25% thing would lead to whoever has the most firepower again though...) Or maybe making more of the uniques not just be loot, but a quest?

the worst part about it is, a lot of the uniques are some of the best items in game, so its almost a necessity to have them to outpower others in pvp.

14.2 is going to make this even more fun too, because without the powergamers being able to overequip themselves, they are all going to have to turn to the best items in game...mainly uniques and rares.

im sure everyone who reads this thread has at least 1 story of a ninja looter or getting screwed out of somthing you spent a lot of time working for.

Xenok 110 Disgruntled Martial Artist
</rant>
