Cz, Cosmik, Funcom events team, Funcom story team, anyone that reads this,

You have seen in the poll (not to mention in the reaction to the game launch, the reaction to the supposed storyline launch in October, and the high profile players such as Lorekeep and Gummiluv that have left because of the lack of progress) what your players think of your storyline efforts thusfar. My question is a simple one: what are you planning to do about your storyline in the near future (say, BEFORE the expansion)?

I am not looking for a "we can't say, but you'll love it" answer (re: the really great fixer thing that Cos announced months ago but would never elaborate on or produce). I am not looking for a laundry list of things you would like to do (re: the crat fixes that were announced over 6 months ago and never implemented). I want to know what you are planning to do. If you have no plans, say so.

I joined this game to be part of a big storyline involving the clans fighting for independence from Omni-Tek. I joined in order to take orders from and maybe, if I was a good player, even interact with the many important NPCs and clans/departments that you said were going to be in the game. I joined in order to be sent on important missions that needed to be done to advance the cause and bring my side a centimeter closer to victory.

I simply want to know if I should keep playing.

The storyline events that happened before generally were One-time events. This would be an attack on city X or outpost Y and all players from a side would come to defend against the NPCs and usually end up crashing the zone. There were also a couple of more rp oriented events that would supposedly lead toward storylines (Manteze declare war on clans; rogue OT employees try to assassinate leader of clans) but those storylines would never develop. See Lorekeep's thread on these forums for a good discussion of that problem.

Recently they have been toying with a storyline involving the Dust Brigade, but the only 'events' that that has really generated are when GMs occassionally change their name to 'Dust Brigade' or some such, make themselves level 250 and start ganking players in a city.

There have also been GM and ARK run events, but nothing that could be called a story. A story requires continuity and we haven't seen that yet.
