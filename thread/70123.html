A few suggestions on more work = reward at high levels
Almost everyone level 175 and above say that there is not much high level content. I can see what they mean after having to do over 16 missions or more to get a level, camelot being repetitive, not many in 2ho, etc...While i understand that Notum Wars will be adding a lot of life in the game, there are a few things in the PvP basics that need some attention.

A prime principle in MMORPGs was rewarding the player who worked harder with better bonuses. Look at the token system, for example. Someone who spends less time in the lower level bracket token farming will have a hp/nano/dmg bonus over those who don't take the time to token farm. Same with implants, i hear level 50s complaining all the time at other level 50s that their implants weren't as high as the person who they just got killed by. More work = reward.

However, tokens and QL's of implants and their benefits stops at 1000 tokens and quality 200, even the new jobe implants are either unequippable, unmodifiable, or not worth the extra 4 or 5 skill.

The LLTS is an item of extremely high price and is not dropping in the game anymore. HOWEVER *enough* of them dropped in the past to the point where they're not 'unobtainable,' i see LLTS/ELLTS being sold all the time. People whine about the prices and the fact that they can't obtain that much cash so they asked FC to nerf the LLTS. "Nerf crits, because i can't raise my ac, adjust my agg/def bar, raise my evades." So with these two arguments in question, the LLTS is soon-to-be nerfed and so is critical hits in pvp. Currently you can only get 10ish critical chance without self-crit buffs the MAs have which is reportedly and what i have seen not to be enough in PvP. With the LLTS nerf, this hits rock bottom even more.

And bracers. For so long the pvp community suggested making soldier reflect buffs self only and leaving bracers the way they were. Most of this was because with bracers and soldier reflect buffs you could get 72% or 52% reflect to certain damage types, almost equal to a soldier in total mark shield... FC saw this flaw and reduced the amount of reflect they gave. This way, the high level players who spent hours and even days looking for bracers to give them a boost in pvp would not have gone to waste because of some nerf on an item that took so long to get just to please those who want reflect from a buff that comes from one of the most popular professions in the game. Currently you can get maximum 7% reflect against any type of damage through bracers, with the highest soldier reflect buff you can get 30% to all, 37% to one. I have always thought that reflect at high amounts should be the soldier's primary thing and something at their disposal as opposed to other professions. Their damage is lacking already in pvp because of this and damage is precisely what they rely on. The hellspinner does help with this though, i won't deny that. However the reflect issue is affecting everyone, and disappointing those who spent so much time looking for reflect bracers. What's even worse is the fact that professions that can instantly heal all their HP or half of it can use a simple buff to make them have to use their healing less. Damage is way too nerfed against healing. high reflect should be a quality of a soldier, a profession who has to rely on survival and fast damage output, one thing that is accomplishable by other professions and the other, hurt by the crit nerf. The 50%-40% rule is already a lot of restriction in PvP.

Tokens. I have 2,147 tokens right now. 1,147 tokens ago i recieved a large and rewarding boost to my profession, i often think that i should just delete the tokens but i figure there may be some future bonus. There has to be a point to uploading tokens to your board beyond 1,000. Maybe convert them to exp, since missions are such a drag these days. Or maybe add some motivation to doing missions besides them giving an exp reward, how about a 2,000 or 3,000 token board? I know people my level who farmed tokens to 1k many levels ago, and they have something like 3k or 4k tokens and nothing to do with them. There needs to be some type of greater bonus to this.

Critical chances, on a grand scale. With new items like the manex catastrophe and queen blade, they totally calculate the critical nerf to be a nerf on all professions as a whole. These weapons allow non-hardcore gamers to do very high damages, but at slow rates. The regular damage of some of the new weapons is higher than the crit damage of weapons like the ithaca, river series 6, and martial artist fists. This allows more shifting in game preference, whether you want to rely on crits and hit fast as an MA (not to mention with root fix you have to have more guts running up to someone and punching them, the damage SHOULD be worth it) or do a lot of damage in one shot, and rely on your ranged initiation or melee initiation. A lot of the weapons used previously are now not used anymore, and the availability of a large assortment of weapons at one's disposal is even less. 2 weapons do not make make 'variety' when there is 5 or 6 not worth using anymore. Also professions such as the agent who are very fragile hp wise but are designed to do a lot of damage cannot do much of it once their concentration nano is gone. Bear in mind that 30% is a large difference from 100%. Not to mention, 11% of this critical chance is gotten through outside buffing.

I can understand that many games these days are more and more directing to please the casual gamer. Now i'm not one who plays the game over 30 hours a week but i think that, the principle of MMORPGs, their complexity and nonlinear original qualities, needs to be enforced in AO. Adding new goals and harder to reach items will only add more life to the game. My suggestions:

1. Leave the low light targetting scope the way it is. Even the init negator was a real pain to professions using 15% scopes with weapons that could normally shoot fast on fully defensive, and not to mention with the recharge fix coming up, initiation negators will hurt users of this even more but rewarding them with more damage per occasional hit.

2. Restore critical chance. The life of an MA and people without ipr points is completely 'crit or nothing.' Every profession can beat them in PvP now because of the recent nerf. Healing and evades are even more powerful now with the watering down of damage done. Specials seem to be all pvp damage is now, and even that can be reflected and healed by considerably larger amounts. people still had a problem with healing when critical chance was high, so healing will remain good. Also the thing that FC was wrong about in the 14.6 patch notes was 'critical chance' being the deciding factor in PvP victory. Those who played with their agg/def more, used their profession's disposal still had a chance against those who had more critical chance than them. Healing will be much less an issue than it is now if critical chances are restroed. Bear in mind that, having a higher critical chance also means you worked harder to get an item that enhances it, you should be able to do more damage, more often. In order to balance things for professions with low-colored evades, this can be discussed in the future as professions get new things chronologically as the game flows.

3. Make bracers at max, 20% reflective against one type of damage, and make a bracer of each damage type unique like it is labeled now. This will make it so people will be rewarded for seeking out different types of bracers and will switch their items depending on the situation. For example, projectile bracer against someone using a projectile weapon. Fire reflect bracer against those using fire AC. This will make people emphasize the situation and the combatant at hand. Make all of the soldier reflect shield line self-only. Soldiers will be able to finally get the highest reflect without TMS, like it should be, against all damage types. Even though soldiers can get 72% reflect through the use of total mark shield, many experienced and high level soldiers will tell you that they refrain from using it often because of the fact that it makes them even more prone to rooters that just wait it out and come back and gnaw off on someone with low reflect because of the nano shutdown for 2mins using tms does not allow you to refresh. This would then require players to work at getting a good reflect item boost to their profession and one that definately pays off.

4. Make a higher ranking level or two of token boards. This way the people who previously token farmed will have gotten to the highest board faster than those who haven't.

While I do not expect my ideas and suggestions, as an individual, to be top priority with a company with a lot of customers, i understand that everyone has different opinions on game balance and how things should be done, but i ask Funcom to atleast consider these suggestions as many of the 175+ crowd would find this a lot more enjoyable than cancelling their account like so many have. Thank you.
