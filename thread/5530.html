I agree, there needs to be more role-playing in the general populace. It is, after all, a role playing game. I think that doing missions is a part of role-playing. You are, after all, going out and serving your faction by doing this job for them, for which they are greateful enough to compensate you for your time and effort. People who arent on missions need to roleplay a little more, though. I can sit around the Tir grid terminal all day and see maybe 3 emotes (/me) that arent mine. There's a lot of bowing to other characters, especially at the beginning and end of a trade, but it needs to go further than that. Quit referring to yourself out of character (like telling people what grade you're in in school, there are no schools on Rubi-Ka as far as I know). If you have more than one character, don't refer to them as "my 52 NT", say something like "My big brother, the NT". Pet classes have something that gives them a major edge, RP wise: /pet chat. I'm a crat, and so I have an Assistant droid named Peon. Peon loves to run around complimenting female characters (he's a particularly lonely little droid), and at times I have to tell him to cool it a little. The reactions I get from arguing with Peon are great, sometimes other characters jump in and take Peon's side of the arguement (I had most of Fair Trade involved in an argument Peon and I had about where we should go hunting next). The point is, role-playing is what this game is all about. It adds a lot to the game to see some random person having a full scale debate with their own pet. Plus (I dont care about this part), if an ARK or a GM sees you role-playing really well, they'll give you a prize for it. I have no clue what the prize is, I never bothered to check and haven't gotten one yet, but the fact is you can get something (presumably) kewl just for doing what you're there to do: roleplay.

These are all good suggestions and stories. I typically use /ooc when talking out of character, but I really like the brackets idea, I think I'll do that from now on.

I have come up with ways to deal with non-RP talk, when you're trying to RP. ex: "Do you want MatCreation Mastery?" or "Send them a /tell." There are two ways I like to deal with this sort of thing.

1. (this is the most fun.) When they talk OOC treat them like they are talking like the town drunk. ex: "I'm token farming the missions, wanna help?" you say: "umm...no thanks...I think you've had a bit too much to drink. I can't make heads or tails of what you're saying."

2. You can just take what they are saying and register it as RP talk. ex: "I'm token farming, wanna help?" you say: "I've been awarded five tokens from the council, I suppose I need more merits, but I'm busy packing for my upcoming trip."

Usually people will catch on after a few min, if they don't then don't be discouaged by the non-RPer's, continue about what you're doing.

Use the area & events around you to your RP advatage. LIke last night outside TIr West Gate, a man tryed to shoot the guard and was killed, his body lay right outside the gate. Some people came by and looked at the body confused by the whole thing. I had been watching from afar the whole time, so I used this as a beginning of a great RP experience. I shouted:

"I saw the whole thing."
"Dead man shoots guard, guard kills dead man."
"It happens a lot in this area."
"New recruits, sometimes they can't keep their heads on their shoulders."

This ended up in a full RP discussion about the event. It was a very...Refreshing experience.

Keep up the RP, it makes our Rubi-Ka great!
