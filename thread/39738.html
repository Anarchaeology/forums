I'll recap it for you real quick if you like.

AS is damage modifying skill ranging from 1/4 to x6. This is multiplied to your base crit attack. The multiplier itself is random, but affected by your AS skill. Higher skill equals higher chance to achieve a high modifier.

The dependancy on AS skill is however so low it really isnt advised to spend IP's on AS skill at all. Firstly because AS is the worst special in the game, partly because of recharge but also because of of the difficulty of using it in practise. Secondly AS the modifier is about 50% in the 1/2-x1 spectrum with 1300 AS skill(me).

AS should only be raised to equip new rifles until you reach the late 170's. Evades/Body dev/Nano skills are far more effective pr IP point.

So if youre twinking your newbee agent, dont waste points in AS.
