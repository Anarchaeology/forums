Someone has mentioned that Reflect Shields are useless as 30 points of reflected damage is not that big of a deal. Also mentioned that this makes them unworthy of the cost in MM.

This is very confused. A soldier's AC buff is based on MM, not a soldier's reflect. Both TMS and Reflect shields use MC and TS and not MM.

But now - on to the 30 points of reflected damage: That is not in fact what reflect shields do. The stat of (30) in Reactive Reflective Field *directly* equates to the stat of (75) in TMS. Thus it is not a 30 point reflect. In fact, it reduces damage against you by 30%. The second stat in reflect shields is the maximum damage you deal back to your opponent. With RRF this is (48), meaning you will deal a max of 48 damage back to the person who hits you.

The stat: ShieldAC used in damage shield's such as the Adv uses (as opposed to reflect shields) simply negates damage against you by that stat and reflects that damage back. This is called a damage shield as opposed to 'Reflect' shield and it is an important distinction in that the 2 are stackable.

Now let's be clear about this: Reflect rules PvP, and is far better than AC's in PvM as well. When you add your 30% to a couple of 20% bracers and are facing an opponent taking only 30% of his damage normally, or 0% if you shield.. well.. You have won.

Even in PVM, i would take a 30% damage reduction of 500-1k damage over an extra 1k AC's anyday. The reason is that 1k AC's i believe reduce damage by 100 (not sure about this one) whereas Reflect reduces 1k hits by 300, and even 500 damage hits by 150. I dont know about the rest of you, but i am far more concerned about taking less damage from the crits Regular damage doesn't kill soldiers - sudden unexpected damage does.
