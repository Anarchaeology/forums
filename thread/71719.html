I just wanted to update you all on the feedback I've been getting.

1. So far everyone is okay with a weekly updated comic, with a couple people having said daily. My intention is to draw at least 3-5 pages before putting any of it up to get an idea of how long it takes me to complete a page.

2. The general consensus is that it should be in color. This does not at all come as a surprise to me. ^_^

3. No one at all has said that they want the story to be very involved in the main storyline. This is very comforting for me, because I don't want to be competing with Funcom for major events in the world.

4. There appears to be a great interest in everyday life in Rubi-Ka, rather than just killing and conflict. I was hoping to hear that. There is also a lot of hinting that there should be at least some humor, and I will certainly do my best.

I will probably be putting up some character concept art in the near future. Solarax from AO-Basher.com has been generous enough to say he would write a news article on his site about this, so any such art will most likely been found through AO-Basher.

Not many of you have supplied e-mail addresses, but that's okay. I made it optional for a reason. ^_^

And finally, my favorite comment thus far:
"9. Additional comments (optional):
Sounds cool, as long as I can print it out and bring to school to read. "

Thank you all for your feedback, and keep it coming. I have gotten some excellent suggestions so far. Please tell your friends online who may not read AO-related website about the survey.

-the Sober Panda
