Reality Check OOC
I decided to write this post because as it seems, a lot of the things I say are taken out of character, and a few people seem to view what my character says as a personal attack on themselves. I want to clear this up right away that I by no means actually in real life mean anything that my character actually says or does. The character of lexus was an invention of my own because the rubi ka conflict really lacked any movement. The clan side floods daily with omni refugees leaving the Omni tek side of the conflict serverly lacking, that coupled with most of the major rp orgs..who rp regularly that is are clan. This left no clear enemy to fight and things just became rather stale. I believe this was pointed out in a post made a while ago entitled "Ao post Swg" or a name similar to that effect, it stated that conflict as it were began to die down because of lack of an enemy or a "Villain" I attempted to remedy this by becoming one of, if not the only real vocal sentinel sympathizer.

As I said though, over the course of time this has not worked out to my liking, my character is very extreme yes and says a lot of things that can get under the skin. I am by no means excusing it or saying 'take it" but that it is only roleplay..and none of it is meant as an OOC attack. However as many times as I had tried to point this out, a lot still think that what is said is an out of character assault and therfor take it personally. A depressing reality sank in when I realized that the roleplayers of the game have to be reminded that it is just that "roleplaying" and by no means represents personal real life feelings...if I had meant anything that my character has said in real life, or ooc, i would have been banned a long time ago, but I do not in fact mean what i say. It is a game, i saw a need for an enemy and I filled it, I regret sincerely that it didn't work out as grand as that. I apologize whole heartedly if I offended anyone. I may not be playing ao much longer, the challenge of starting over in a new place is quite intriguing to me so I'll start playing shadowbane on tuesday. I again apologize if anything I said was taken literally, it was not nor will it ever be. Enjoy yourselves, and maybe when shadowlands comes around I might come back on an alt or something, until then I will most likely just play on the forums.

((Lex, I definitely see where your coming from, it's the plight you'll find everywhere you RP. Should you choose to RP an absolute cold hearted villan many times your statements will be taken OOC and you will be personally attacked for making them. This is by no means good RP and really subtracts from the overall experience as it forces players to conform to a certain standard rather than allow them to do as they wish.

Imagine how worthless AO would be if there weren't people like Bliqz, Neboja, yourself and anyone else who chooses to RP a villanous character around. I think primarily what causes the OOC animosity to villans is that they are often confused with greifers with their more "negative" posts. I've found that a good way to counteract this problem is to put a disclaimer in your signiture (like Bliqz's or my own) that states that everything you say is IC unless specified otherwise, this will elimiate all doubt as to whether you are speaking to the character or the player. Another thing that I've found to help has been to be very selective about where I RP my darker side. Announcements ment to be congratulatory or humorous in nature I respond in an OOC (or at least a "lighter" IC) tone as I've found that even with a disclaimer making a "dark" post can be touchy at best. Another tactic that also works is to respond as "darkly" as you wish, however, add an OOC tag at the end expressing your congratulations or amusement at the post.

I don't want to tell you how to RP your character as that is a very singular decision and only you should be the one to make it, however I just wanted to share some tips that I've found work very well when RPing an "evil" type. The same holds for the community in general, please don't discourage people from being evil in an OOC format as it makes RPing flat when everyone's a good guy.))
