King of Fools!
<Start Personal Log>

4 April 29477

Tonight, is my first party as a member of Whisper's Edge. I wanted to make this a party to remember. The premise of the party sound fun. "Are you a Fool?" Well, of course I am a fool. Now is my chance to show everyone. But what if I freak people out? Ah, who cares. Its all in fun, they'll understand that I'm not really wierd! At least I hope so.

I overheard a comm from Vixentrox to another member of WE, stating that they should mismatch their clothes to look like a fool.

I bought my outfit from Miir in West Athens. Hows this for mismatch, Red Boots, Pink Gloves, black thong, black short sleeves, & a back tattoo. Top it off with the Trader Hat from Zoftig Blimp. I spend money like its water. When I got the gift from General Ramiyana after leaving the NLF I bought the Trader hat cause I saw Aazamon wear it and I thought it looked cool.

After I put the outfit on, I sure felt like a fool, so I guess it was perfect. Now its off to Reets.

Apparently I did scare people off, as I danced by myself for a good part of the night. I tried to even request "Dancing with Myself" from GSP but they didn't have too much normal music. So my request went unanswered.

My one shining moment came when a costume contest was announced. Vallikat was my personal favorite to win .
But to my surprise, she lost.. TO ME!! I won the contest, and 1 million credits!!

I am truly the KING of FOOLS!

<end log>
