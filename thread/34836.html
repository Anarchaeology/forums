Profession channels
I'd like to suggest in-game Profession channels. Such that all clan MPs, for instance, had a "Clan Meta-Physicist" channel that they could access. This would only be for characters of that profession (make your own decisions about False Profession) and would mainly be a place where people could ask and answer profession-specific questions. Since each person would only have one channel, it wouldn't add too much to our current large selection.

I suggest this mainly because a lot of players don't read/use the boards for getting advice and I hear a lot of bad advice given out by people who have clearly never played a character of the profession they are trying to help.
