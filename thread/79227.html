Just my thoughts
Brothers and Sisters

When our beloved profession is in need of a fix, I sat down and though about what the MA as a Close combat profession is all about. This is just my thoughts of how the MA gameplay should be. Im not speaking for you ppl. Its quite Pvp oriented since NW is so hot right now.

Order of things
Close Combat
HP and evades

Close Combat
In a SF game like this Close combat fighters should have a big disadvantage, the range aspect. A strike with a Tiger Claw or parry stick does not reach that far. And if you fight guys with guns the range should be the biggest problem for the MA. Today It does not work like this. The problem is the damage. When we get in there, when we are able to touch the opponent with our bare hands we would be the most lethal Profession in the game. Ppl should say, whatever you do, dont get close to an MA. To protect oneself from us there is roots, snares, damage absorbers and reflectors. Of course Range is still an obstacle to work past but that should stay that way.
Its our Job to strike hard and where it hurts the most.

Right now its our Big Brother profession Enforcers that strike the fear into the hearts of the Ranged weapon users. The damage output is incredible. But what should the enforcers biggest advantage be? Their HP if you ask me, and not their damage capabilities. The Enforcer is Tank profession. They have so much Hp they dont know what to do with it. In my world, if I where fighting an enforcer my lvl, if we both do everything right, the timing is perfect and we use all the special profession skills we can to survive and win. We should both die at the same time. He from striking my little fragile body (compared to a essenced, mongoing enf) and I striking him where it hurts the most (you know where) that is balance if you ask me. If you get sloppy you should die, If you miss timing and dont have the skill to "read" the fight you should die to. But the chance of winning should be 50/50 if both guys play perfect.

I know this is impossible and that it would never happen. It is hard to tune a game this complex to perfect balance with some simple changes. Lag often ruins Timing that should not be forgotten eather. So pure balance will never come.

So a High damage over time is first and foremost important for us. We know where to strike and why, We should do critical damage when we are close to our opponent. I really think this where intended from the start.
That is where the beautiful MA crit line comes to work. We crit, therefore we do more damage. Everyone can crit but we depend on it. If we dont crit like we have been thought in the dojo what use are we? I think that we should be able to crit as much as we did before the patch. We live and die with the crits and everyone knows it. We lack in good Alpha attack weapons, And the MA attacks is not much to get exited about. One more time: Crits.

Ma HP and Evades.
We have nothing to boost Hp like Soldiers or Enforcers, yet we are considered one of the tank classes since we often take agg when fighting ordinary MOBs. To make up for this we have Green Evades. And Evade buffs. As someone in another thread stated we dont have the best evades. Should we have the best evades? Its hard to compete with fixers but After fixers I think we should be the best evaders. Its the MA way. I dont think we should loose the AC buffs or anything but I would rather se us have better evades then AC. Ppl suggest that ripost should be changed for some better effect not just the skill to use for some MA attacks (read Wave)

We dont have Reflection or HP advantage. Thus we should be more like fixers in the AC/HP/Evade aspect, not as extreme of course. If we get hit it should be felt .

Close combat professions - "Something like this in my dream world"

MA = High Damage, Good Evades, Medium Hp, Medium Healing

Adventurer = High Healing, Good Evades, Medium HP, Medium Damage

Enforcer = High HP, Good Damage, Medium Evades, Low healing


Hang in there Guys...
