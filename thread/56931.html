List of nano formula you can't buy (QL <125)
Here's a list of Nano formula (less than QL 125) that are not available in stores. (All nanos over QL 125 are not sold anyplace).

Now you have an easy way to see if those nano crystals or instruction disks are possibly worth keeping/selling to players, not to booths. The QL range is about 4 to 123 so something for every level.

Info notes: It's based on a client-side database dump (ver. 14.5.1) so some nanos may not be in game at all (yet, or ever). Don't use this as a wish/shopping list. Use it as a list to check against when you find something you're not sure about.

My working document is in the format "[nano formula] tab [profession]" so the profession colum isn't lined up here. Sorry, I'm too lazy to make it pretty.
