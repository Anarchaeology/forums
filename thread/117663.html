OOC: Timezones
I have discovered that I chose to live in the wrong timezone... I might be imagining or just have no clue, but I get the feeling that so many people are in a very different timezone than me. Most parties are held in the middle of the night (for me), most meetings are done very late at night (for me), and so many of the people active on this board are ingame when I sleep or when I'm at work. So I hardly ever get the chance to talk to any of you, which is a shame.

So...with no further adue...let's list our timezones. Only getting some nice rp during weekends is silly, and I want to change that for myself. I have met so many great people and roleplayers in this game, but I think I must be very "unlucky" since 99% of them live in a weird timezone for me. And hardly being able to talk during weekdays and only ever planning anything during weekends is getting rather boring. From now on, I'll steer clear of the US/Canadians, and befriend the Europeans . You know what I mean.

'GMT' like 'EST' technically never changes. However most of Europe do have their own equivalent of daylight saving.

In England we are now on British Summer Time (BST) which is GMT+1. So at present the relative time difference between London and New York is still 5 hours, regardless if you calculate it as GMT->EST or BST->EDT.

Of course actual dates used in Europe and America move their clocks forward and back are not synchronised, so there is always that confusing week or two when one continent is on Summer time and the other isn't.

All of which suggests that the only sensible people are the Icelanders who simply stick to GMT.
