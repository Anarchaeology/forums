Zanthyna for Keeper Professional 2003

As many of you may have seen (in this thread), I have just announced my candidacy for Keeper Professional. I'm sure, once you get to know my qualifications, you will agree that I'm the best choice to run our beloved profession.

My Experience
I have been playing AO since October 2001 when I rolled my first character, Zanthyna. Sure, some of you may have noticed that I was away from the game for several months, but that was due to my time in the Peace Corps--I was away from my computer feeding the starving children in Africa (and not, as my detractors allege, locked up in a mental institute following a 'psychotic, Whitney Houston-grade breakdown'). In addition to having played for nearly two years, I have leveled numerous characters to high levels, including getting my agent to lvl 56 and my doc to a staggering lvl 129!

My Dedication
I have dedicated myself to the betterment of the AO family and will continue to do so when elected professional. I run a very sucessful web site, www.zanthyna.com that gets literally up to 30 unique hits in a single day! Numbers like that are unheard of for most fan sites and my hit counter is rivaled only by those of free porn sites. Also, I am well know on many forums for intelligent, incisive comments and hard work. My modesty prevents me from saying more, but I consider my humble dedication to you, my AO family members, to be my strongest asset.

My Mad Skizills, Yo
On top of being a well known AO celeberity with impeccible credentials and excellent name recognition, I also have mad skills and phat l3wt. Not only are all of my characters uber twinks (my doc's gear), but, if you combine the bank accounts of all my characters, I'm a millionaire almost five times over! I bring this up not to brag, but mearly to point out that I won't have to sell myself to special interests to get campaign money and thus no one will own me. I will fairly rule over the lot of you as I build up my super army to take over the world...muahahahah...(edit: ignore this last sentence).


My Platform
If elected, I vow to keep all of the following campaign promises. Absolutely. Guranteed 100%. No way will I renig on any of these.

Once elected, I promise to...
  • change the name of this forum from "Stronghold" to "Pimp Daddy's Lair"
  • make fun of those wussy shades for not rolling a real profession
  • start a "Finders Keepers" program to help keepers find teams faster
  • root all my threads
  • lead us to victory over the shades in the annual keepers vs shades softball game
  • ignore real issues and instead stir up peoples emotions on unimportant issues to distract everyone from my bumbling incompetence and evil, fascist agenda
  • petition FC for a /bitchslap emote useable only by keepers
  • create and run in game social events, such as "Zanthyna Day"
  • rule with an iron fist (edit: oops, I meant "treat everyone with respect and help those in need")
  • cut all your taxes while doubling spending and ending the deficit
  • invade despotic third world countries and liberate their notum mines from opression
  • placate the masses with trite platitudes while secretly hoarding wealth and power for myself
  • and finally, if elected as your Keeper Professional, I promise to actually roll a keeper.


What You Can Do To Help
If you want to help me get elected, you can help me out greatly by liberally posting (but not spamming--CZ already chastized me about that once ) supportive statements and by suggesting to your friends and guildmates that they vote for me. You can also enlist to join my Goodwill Outreach and Operations Network squad (G.O.O.N. squad) and help 'encourage' others who aren't onboard to join up now before "it's too late." Remember, if you oppose me now, you're name is going on my enemies list and, if I am elected, my wraith will be furious as I get retribution, biblical style. So I strongly suggest you get on board now if you know what's best for you.

Conclusion
So, in conclusion, please remember Zanthyna when you go to the polls to vote for your first Keeper Professional. Together, we can make AO great!
