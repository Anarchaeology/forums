Thursday Night - Special Event!
Hey Party Peeps! Heads up! Special Announcement from Relax! Inc.

Every Thursday, we at Relax! Inc. are proud to present DJ Tastyvixen on the air from 9pm EST, usually going until whenever she passes out. This Thursday the 22nd however, she has the night off, but DO NOT DESPAIR!

We are happy to announce that we will have a SPECIAL GUEST DJ on the air in her regular time slot this week! It's no one you've heard before, and I guarantee it won't be the last you hear from him! Who is it?? I'm not tellin.....nope....can't pry it from my lips, no matter how hard ya try.....you'll just have to tune in this Thursday at 9 pm EST to find out! I can tell you that he plays extremely cool tunes, he will take requests as we all do, and he babbles almost as much and as incoherently as me, so what more can ya want!

You know the time, you know the place. The Relax! Bar in Omni Forest. If you don't know where it is, send a TELL to one of us Relax! Inc. members, myself, Liss, Threx, or any of our DJ's and other members. If you are Clan, you can still get there if you have a yalm. If you don't, it might be tough getting past the Guards at the East Gate of Omni Entertainment. But send us a TELL, we may have some warpers on hand....

Nothing much else to say except be there for this exciting happening, or read about it afterwards, and kick yourself for not being there....
