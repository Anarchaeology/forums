Maybe... we could have a pet that follows us around constantly?

You'd start off without having a pet, on level one. When you get to level, say, 15, you'd get the normal "Current Level: 15" and "You have gained the title of ___" (Yea, I forgot what it is... Hunter?) messages, along with something like "You have gained a friend!" Then you would get a weak, low-level creature, say, a Leet, and it would follow you around, having no set time limit on it. You could command it like a normal pet, perhaps...

If it died, it would give you a message saying "Your friend has been killed!" or something. You'd then be without a familiar for... about an hour (Or you could use an in-game day, might make more sense). You'd then get a message saying "Your friend has returned to aid you" or something like that...

Then at say, level 30, you'd get something like "Your friend has become stronger". Your pet would then turn into something a bit stronger, like... Well, I don't know, but something more damaging in combat. (Not to good with level 30ish creatures.) Perhaps a Rhinoman?
Anyway, it would be just like the first form, just stronger. Also, this would allow you to use the '/pet polymorph' command to switch from Leet form to the new form.

Then at level 50, you would get the "Your friend has become stronger" message, and turn into something new (Perhaps a Greywolf?). You'd also get a message saying "The forest has given your friend the power to heal."
This would then allow you to use the '/pet polymorph' command to choose what typeof pet you wanted. There could be different categories (This level would be Attack and Healing), each with pets of that type, and one category could open per title level.
At 50, your Attack pets would be Leet, Rhinoman and Greywolf, and your Healing pets would be Silvertails (That unicorn thing...). And you could only have one type of pet out at a time, not an attack AND a healing pet. One friend at a time...

You could get a new attack and healing pet at level 75, then at level 100, a healing pet, attack pet, and a new category would open up (Never played a pet profession very high, so don't know much about pet-types ;P). It could go on like this until level 200, where we'd get our best pets.

Just some ideas I had. Anyone like it? ^_^

Level 30 - 20-40(75) 2.1s/2.1s
Level 50 - 40-80(120)
Level 100 - 80-160(240)
