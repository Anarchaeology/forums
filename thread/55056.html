Killer for Hire(Part1)
The Party Raves on, the lights of Reets Retreat fluttering, people dancing wildly with excitement, as the DJ Tarryk plays on through the night.Envy Sits on a couch In the back enjoying an Omni Lite(Tm), taking small drags and exhaling the thick smoke into the air, the lasers catching the smoke giving it a glow. She watchs everyone dance, wanting to join, but she isnt Here to have a good time, this....is...business....


A tall man walks up the ramp onto the Glass floor, A very tall man, wearing a black coat and an Omni Pol Elite Helmet, almost Trox in formation but not quite. He moves through the crowd at a slow pace, as the dancers part, making way for this massive figure gliding through the crowd.Envy catching sight of this man glaring at him, as he makes his way to her couch and carefully sits down.

The Man takes out A small package slowly placing it on the table and slides it towards her.Envy drops her Omni Lite(Tm) on the floor stepping on it. and Slowly takes the sealed package.


"I trust you know what todo with this?" the man says in a deep tone over the loud music.

Envy nods and the man gets up and dissappears into the crowd from which he came.Envy take the package putting it into her coat,getting up and slowly walks towards the ramp giving Tarryk a wave as she walks past.

She walks outside and starts up her Yalm, flying up above Reets, she slowly opens the package.upon Opening the box she found 2 items.
The first, A standard Ncu Memory chip with encoded lettering, the other a small energy gun cartridge The lettering: A.N.V. Envy picks up the chip carefully placing it into her belt, punching a few buttons on her wrist com.The chip loads and a document appears before her very eyes reading:


"Upon Success of the mission you will be rewarded 50 Million credits wired into your account, Cordinated: 626.4,426.1 Omni-1 Headquarters 8pm your target will arrive.
The energy cartridge holds a Advanced Nano Virus, it only has one round,One shot,hit your mark, and we will do the rest..."


"Omni-1 HQ!" Envy thought to herself knowing her target was A Bureaucrat of some sort"This is risky,good thing the money is good" envy grins at the thought of the 50mill and takes off to Omni HQ, being one to never turn down that kind of money.

Envy heads to Omni HQ, scouting her Sniping area, Guards posted on all sides of her except for south of her cordinates, A sign of course, that this is where her target would be.Envy sets down her yalm and sets up camp for the night.

Placing a small silver sleeping bag out and a small lamp, she takes out a black box from her pack with a cloth attached, slowly opening it and dipping the cloth into the slimy substance taking the cloth and rubbing it onto the barrel of her X-3 rubbing it in to a shine.She takes out the cartridge from the package and places it into ther X-3 arming the gun and placing it next to her.
Envy lays down into her sleep bag and stares up at the stars in the omni skys, her mind wondering with thoughts, all centered on thing.
The man who came to the party, Big of course quite shady, of course envy had come A custome to shady types giving her Jobs but this man was different somehow, she could feel it in her heart.The man wearing an Omni Pol helmet "Quite Odd" She thought, "A job within the company?" Envy knew that if she messed this up, it would cost her life.
So she slept uncomfortably that night, with nightmares of Omni Pol charging at her placing her under arrest in the very spot she layed, not getting any shut eye, when she finally fell to sleep dead tired.

When she woke, it was nearly 7pm, sleeping well into the day, she woke, and almost instantly prepared her rifle for the shot, the shot, that will make, or break her. Placing the rifle carefully on her shoulder waiting, waiting for her target....

8 Slowly approached and the thought of her arrest never let go, plaqueing her mind but never letting up on the trigger. Finally 8 had arrived she clinched her fears within and began to concentrate heavily, her sights directly on the door to the south.

The door slowly opened and she tensed up clinching the trigger softly eyes dead within her scope, A figure enters the door talking and waving to the people within the building, she couldnt make a shot with her in the doorway, something stopped her, she had to see who it was.

After a few moments.. the figure stepped out and the door shut behind. The figure slowly turned and envy casted a quick nano program through her NCU "Utter Concentration" so her shot would be dead on. As the program finished running, Envy looked dead through her scope again, eyeing the figure.


Envy stopped in a gasp, looking shocked, breaking concentration, she backed away from her rifle for a moment.... almost in a dreadful fear..... "Alun...." she thought to herself

Alun, Envy's sister who she had hated all of her life,whom she envyed for her Easy life, Envy was reminded of this hatred and quickly grabbed the gun, her target still in sight for a clean shot, but something stopped her, she was posed ready, to strike, thinking, internal conflict buzzing through her mind..... Blood thicker than water, Money more than she would make in a life time.......

To shoot, or to save..... what will She do......
