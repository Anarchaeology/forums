What is balance?
I have seen a number of postings that talk about balancing. I am sure this will sound like a very naive question to most people, but what exactly is balancing?

I assume it means trying to make the different professions equal in some sense. Since people have different feelings on what is good or bad about a profession, it would seem almost impossible to make an arrangement that everyone would consider equal. And since everyone is free to pick whatever profession they want, is it a problem that they consider some professions better than others?

Balance is making every profession more or less equally able to participate in the game.

No one is arguing fixers should ever, ever outdamage soldiers. But fixers should be able to play the game from 1-200 with about the same difficulty as soldiers.

Abso-fragging-lutly!
Balance at its most extreme would be very, very, dull. What most people really mean, is as is intimated above, the ability for every class to play the game with a similar level of challenge/reward.

And, as also intimated above, PvP balance and PvM balance are two entirely seperate things.

So, with that in mind, what can be done to improve balance without upsetting the apple cart?

Idea 1 - have the 'colour' range (and hence xp gving ability) of MOBS vary for each class. Non-combat oriented classes would get greens over a wider rage of levels than combat-oriented classes. This allows these classes to get xp for lower level stuff which might still be a challenge to them. This really needs the OE patch in place first. Actually, any balance attempts will NEED the OE patch.

Idea 2 - Generate a 'challenge'rating for MOBS and players based, not just on their level, but on thier known nanos, equiped gear, etc. This is much harder and would require both extensive data collection and much testing. It would also need to handle many possible means of exploiting. However, this would be the fairest method as reward (xp, treasure etc) would be directly related to the difference in challenge rating between the combatants. This would work for both PvP and PvM.

I'm sure others have views on this, and their wn ideas.

Balance for me
For me there are different kinds of balance.
All of them should be juggled, and hopefully not
mutually exclusive.


1) PvP balance. Given a large ladder tournament,
it should not be a given beforehand what profession should win. It should also not be given which profession should NOT win.
( Rock/Paper/Scissors for example, or equipment/player skill count for more than what profession you have chosen. )


2) Solo PvE balance.
There should be only marginal differences in different professions possibility to play solo.
( There could be professions that are clearly marked as not capable of this, but I consider this a failure. )

3) Group PvE balance
Having another member in a team should be an asset regardless of profession of that member. Every profession should have something that makes the group stronger.

4) "Playability" balance ( Fun factor )
All of the above could be satisfied with every profession having a one-shot kill ability. It could also be achieved with no profession being able to outdamage natural healing.
This would however not be fun, nor challenging.
The balance to strike here is between fun and tedious. One way to do this is to try and set up goals of how long should a PvP fight last, and make sure damage-dealing/healing is balanced so that fights take that long.

Bah, I am rambling. Best to stop here.
