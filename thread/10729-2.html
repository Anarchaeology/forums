I understand those who /terminate to stop some ganker from getting a kill, but that in itself is a small problem (you'll die anyway) compared to my main reason for suggesting this: people who train mobs on others and then terminate so the mobs will attack other people. With a timer you can choose to kill the trainer or run away while the mobs do it, that is an option you don't have right now.

The PvP consquences are not all good I agree, with a timer you wouldn't be able to terminate away from a ganker, but it would stop people from doing the same thing when they realize they can't win a duel. I don't PvP much myself, and I can live with a timer even though it means letting gankers get more kills - but I'm not saying that's the way everyone else should feel.

The thing is, we use /terminate as a form of transportation now, as there are no real penalties for doing it (besides the rez time). I'm not out to stop /terminating home from missions etc. (I do it myself sometimes), but I want to stop griefers who use /terminate as an easy way out.

How about this:
The timer will only be used if you are under attack by a mob (non-player)?

Player attacks you -> instant terminate
Mob attacks you -> 30s terminate
Mob + Player attacks you -> 30s terminate

It's not really what I wanted, and a timer is not logical in any case, but something has to be done to stop griefers. If the above rule goes in someone will try to find a way around it, but I guess it's better than nothing.
