Not really. Have you seen the requirements?

http://v020u46eff.maximumasp.com/AOD...sp?AOID=203108

Who has that kind of Mechanical Engineering to throw IP's around on? The other point is that they apparently hit like a wet noodle:

http://v020u46eff.maximumasp.com/AOD...sp?AOID=201917

That's the one I could make at level 172 if I spent points in Mechanical Engineering. But, that's for a quality level 400 version. You have to multiply that damage by 15/40'ths to see how awful it really is. Puts it in the league of a Wen Wen. Also should be noted that once you place it, it doesn't move. So if you want to get the benefits of it, you best be in the middle of a spawn somewhere or be prepared to do some long range pulling once you run out of mob's in your immediate vicinity.

Likewise, they can't be placed in a mission or any indoor playfield. Not that this is a surprise. Just needs to be pointed out regarding their situational usefullness. Basically, IF you are a soloer out in the wilderness AND happen to have a nonstop spawn within range of your tower AND you dump points into Mechanical Engineering you can probably get a small benefit IF you are a profession who is able to solo out of doors with your character right now.

I'll give the benefit of the doubt and hope that Service Towers tailored for the various professions will be added in the next patch.
