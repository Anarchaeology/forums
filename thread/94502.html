Omni Leader victim of Omni-Pol
Alexander "Lehx" Western, ex-leader of The Agency was arrested yesterday night by a group of 7 Omni-Pol officers, led by General Templus. Western was escorted, followed by several onlookers to a location in Omni-1 Trade district, where he was supposedly abused. Upon returning from a reclaim terminal, Western was found to have no eyes, and it is believed that these may be impossible to replace.

The incident was supposedly caused by his being suspected of selling confidential Omni information to a criminal organization, whose name and alignment are as yet unknown. Rumors speak of torture and coercion occuring in the location where President Western was taken.

Lehx was also found to have been forced into handing over leadership of The Agency [IaI] to an as-of-yet unknown person. Omni-Pol is able to bring Western into what could be one of Omni's first trials on Rubi-Ka if the new Agency management investigates the matter and brings proof and certain names to Omni-Pol officers.

This has been an Omni-Newz announcement, January 26th
