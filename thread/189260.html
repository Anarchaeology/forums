So...What can I do? If I am going to continue with this speed, I will not be able to be 201 before earliest 2005. I cannot see any light, any "Woot we can do this to get SK!" or anything.

I am simply giving up, slowly. Giving up because all my efforts are useless and by the end of the day, wasted. I am giving up because I dont see any fruit grow on the tree I have been working so hard with all this time, ever since the NIGHT SL was opened. Giving up when I look at my XP bar, seeing 4300 points and thinking one month of pure gameplay.

I beg of anyone, help me. Help me by telling me how I can progress, how I can level. How I can feel that my efforts mean something, that I get something out of my time in SL. I cant go around killing green hecklers for 5 SK points that takes me one minute per kill, I cant kill yellow, because they are impossible to solo. Which means...I have to kill grey mobs in Nascense, because thats the only way I can gain something. But thats no fun! I want a challenge, but I want to be able to do it myself, and I want a reward that matches it.

No, I am not leaving or quitting AO. I love AO. Its the best game ever, I have never, never spent so much time on any other game ever, and I never will. And I dont mean to rant or flame Funcom with this post, because I am very satisfied with their Efforts and Expansion pack. This post just isnt about anything like that. It is simply about a player who cannot progress anymore, and need and want help to feel that I am back on the road and that it leads somewhere.

I feel where you're coming from on this Eng, not sure I have any helpful advice, but I'll try anyway ...

From the sound of it you're stuck on deuterium and/or dust right? Both of these are extremely hard to impossible to do solo, depending on profession.

For the deuterium I'd say get an NT to help you, can calm the extra parasite when 2 drop (might take some tries, but it works), keeps you with nano for heals, and can chainlayer you in an emergency. I know I got my deuterium with an MA and a soldier, haven't tried with just MA and NT, but it should work.

Dust is both a bit harder and a bit easier, not sure how it works on RK2 ... but at least on RK1 people seem more or less inclined to cooperate on getting past this. While it is possible for you as an MA to solo one golem at a time, 2 or more will likely kill you so your best friend here will likely be a 'crat ... charms work wonders for handling adds and taking down the main target Biggest difficulty with dust is maybe the pulling, I'm afraid I don't have any good advice for that, but I've heard that getting a 2-3 golem pull while leaving the rest undisturbed isn't all that difficult at least.

After getting your dust and deuterium there's two more parts that (likely) requires help, and that's the sharks and the slithers. The sharks are relatively easy as they don't social on you, but hit hard. The slithers arer just pure pain in a big package. For sharks I'd bring the same NT you used for deuterium (same trip preferably ), for slithers an adv or a doc would be great ... maybe especially adv for parrot and fast travel

With extensive use of CiB and acrobat you should be able to do these things with 1 or 2 teammates, while not the solo experience you hope for at least it's doable I hope

Then again I could be completely off my mark here and these are not the problems you're having at all, if so I apologize for assuming and hope someone else will give you better advice for solving your problems.

Good luck

One thing to remember about the dust, it isn't your fault that you have a hard time with getting it. The dungeon is broken in at least 2 very important ways:

1) It doesn't instantiate (still, even tho' they tried to fix it), so everyone gets same PF ... making the camping and competition worse than it should be

2) The golems all spawn in the entrance room, in a big heap .. instead of spread out so you can kill them one or two at a time.

If I were you I'd get 2-3 friends together and go hunting slithers, the slithers are actually quite fun beasts to wrestle with and their cave is a pure marvel of ice. The cave is quite far north in the abyss (slightly west of centre).

On the other hand, if there's few people willing to give you a hand at the moment I'd say bring a friend and roam the abyss to find a Lone Shark (personally I prefer the one that swims around some way outside Dr. Pons' dungeon).

I'm leery of saying more than this, as it seems to me you'd like to discover where things are for yourself ... if you do want to know more exact locations tho' there's always Hero's "walkthrough forum".

Again good luck.
