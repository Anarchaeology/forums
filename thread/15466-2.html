* Cohort/Horde nanos are broken.
* Useless Nano Lines (Give Energy:100! Whoa!) / Oversensitivity to roots / Broken animations / Bow Spec attack kinda sucks / MA weapon bugs... Etc...

I could go on for quite a while here, but at the moment we are reasonably balanced. A little continous tweaking and we'll be all fine and dandy. To bad that an Engineer will out-MA any MA though. That bugs me.
