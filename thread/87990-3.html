I find it highly entertaining that all these suggestions are STILL being made. Some are variations, but none of the ideas are new.

1. Vicinity loot messages. There was quite a bit of discussion about making people from various guilds part of the team that got loot for Tara in order to combat this particular problem. Funny, it never got implemented.

2. No repeat rolling. This was discussed and at least brought up officially by Jasonx on the aoguildleaders site. Funny, it was met with flaming and no vote or decision on it was ever made.

3. Have admin hosts that already have all the loot. Well, I think this has been brought up, but in no offical way. The essence of it to have unbiased admin or admin that at least some people can trust, however, was specifically brought up by myself as a severe problem of Clannet.

4. Using helpbot to roll. I brought this up immediately after Clannet started. Granted, helpbot is player made, but it is for all intents and purposes Independently player made. Labrutte's idea of how it would be implemented seems incredibly simple and something that any programmer could set up easily. I find it odd that there is so much resistance to this suggestion.

And finally something that has not been said, but I think I make clear from this post:

5. Clannet has no concise system for making decisions on changes to its system. Random meetings with no one clearly representing Clannet turn into boxing matches and no real decisions ever come out of them other than "well it seems we have no alternative, we are taking tara"

Also, there is no real system for dealing with people that do not agree with Clannet. Banning an entire guild, and one that you can not say holds no power on Rimor, will only result in them having the open opportunity to kill Tara on their own.

Honestly, I would rather Tara was level locked 100-150, since the mob is obviously a joke for everyone higher than that. And granted, I doubt Nemesis would really enjoy spending the hours of meetings it would take to make Clannet a true community based system, but in the end --

By making it virtually impossible for anyone to make a real contribution to Clannet, ignoring repeated suggestions, and leaving the only action open to people that disgreed to take tara for themselves -- Clannet brought this upon themselves.
