Make your own descriptions...
Little idea:

It came to me when I was (for the 23528th time) going through all my 5 backpacks of nano's after someone asked what Crat nano's I had...

Figured it out yet? I'm a nano trader. (Yes, they excist) (Yes, it makes good money) (No! We don't cheat ppl with prices).

Back to my idea.
I petitioned about it recently and they told me nothing was to be done about it.
Yet.

I SAID BACK TO THE IDEA!!!!
Well, here we go:
Why not make an option to make your own "description" windows.
Make it like an item which you can shift-click on to see the details. Instead of details, there is a list in it, with (In my case my range of Crat nanos) blue, clickable links to the descriptions of the items listed in there. (In my case, you get the description of a particular Crat nano.)

Get it?!

Now, it can be that such a thing already excists...
Comments welcome and/or links to websites where this is described.
