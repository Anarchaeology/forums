Enforcer born...
I wrote a bio for my character. I think it is pretty good and decided to post it here.
Be gentle.

Roland Portello, like most Atrox on the planet Rubi-ka, was created by Omni-tek to work in the Notum mines. A freak accident left very young Roland alone in the deserts of Newland wandering for days and almost dead when Hyman "Exstedt" Rouse found him and nursed him back to health. Hyman was a doctor that lived near Tir. He was traveling in the Newland Desert to visit a professor in the Rhinoman village who had fashioned him a special vest. This vest was made using nanotechnology from the purest sources of Notum on the planet and would help Hyman greatly with his practice. Having no family and no way of knowing where he was meant to be, Roland went to work for Hyman who raised him like a son. Roland lived with the good doctor for years and grew to regard him as a father. He learned to help the sick and downtrodden and he learned a passion for life that Atrox are not supposed to have. Most people were alarmed by Roland at first but grew to accept him over the years. Roland was happy. One day, while traveling near the Stret River a squad of Omni-tek soldiers stormed into Hyman's camp demanding treatment for a wounded sergeant. Hyman worked for hours late into the night but could not save the man poisoned by one of Rubi-Ka's deadliest spiders. On hearing the news of the sergeants death, the soldiers became furious and began to burn the camp down. When Hyman tried to stop them, he was murdered without a second thought. He was, after all "just a dirty clanner". Roland returned to the camp just as Hyman was struck down. He fell to his knees in horror and let out a howl of despair that, in legend, could be heard from Onmi-1 to Tir. As the soldiers turned their attention to Roland, he was filled with a great and horrible rage. He turned to the supply wagon full of building supplies that they were transporting back to the clinic and pulled out a 7 foot long steel beam. Some of the soldiers were unfortunate enough to get too close to Roland and 3 died in the first mighty swing of the beam. Another found himself unable to breath with crushed lungs and a 4th looked down at his pulverized legs before seeing a massive form swinging a huge piece of steel aimed for his head. The 2 that remained tried their best to shoot the monster down before he overtook them, but they might as well have been fighting Ian Warr himself for all the good it did. Bleeding and alone, Roland took his father and made his way back to the only home he had ever known. Hyman's partner, Tish "Fryli" Timmer, healed Roland and helped him bury his father. Roland soon left his home behind to seek out and train with some of the greatest warriors on the planet. He learned the ways of combat from Ismael "Kayoss" Kiewiet, Daria "Shani" Catt, Sonny "Toktok" Flummer, Nikodemus "Nikoz" Puller and Dustin "Smooth" Catt. After a time, he chose to mark his face with an ancient warrior symbol. When he was ready, Roland Portello died and "Obitus", a name from an ancient Earth language meaning "death" came to be. Obitus lives his life now as a Clan Warrior. He seeks nothing but the total annihilation of all things Omni-tek. He will not rest until Rubi-Ka is free and "Obitus" has come to all that oppose.
