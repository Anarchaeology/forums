My thoughts ...
I have been having the problem that although I am the leader of my org, I haven't been getting attack messages. I spoke to a GM about it last night, and here are the conclusions:

It turns out that the Clan Leaders channel overrides the tower channels. I don't like leaving the Clan Leaders channel open. I had it muted, and when I muted this channel, I no longer recieved tower attack notifications even though those channels were on. Now I am forced to listen to the clan leaders if I want to know when I have come under attack.

It occured to me at the time that perhaps the reason other people in the org are not getting tower channel messages is because the program code ties those messages to the Clan Leaders channel. Since other members don't have this channel, they get no messages.

I hope they fix this problem soon.
