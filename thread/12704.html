Docs and 50% - SHOULD not happen (but not why the other guy says it "can't")
OK....I dont usually post or read here........but I am at home right now and this PC cant play AO (oh well).


I play on RK2 (silly me).....some may know me....most probably won't.....do I care.....not particularly.


My 2c.

DONT make doc heals (or any heals) 50% for pvp.....Why?

Well...this may have been mentioned before....but since I cant be bothered (yes I am quite lazy) looking for them, and cos its mostly just bi0tching (yes I see lots of bi0tches here).


Its pretty simple....Legitimate hunting in pvp zones....Yes, there are people that DO try to do that in non-suppressed zones.

Team hunting....managing to survive with doc healing team while they are engaged with (relatively) tough monsters.....Along comes bored/title hunter/whatever person(s) wanting to kill anything they possibly can....Team may or may not notice them, a zone may or may not be handy for the XP team....but regardless they get engaged.....doc/tank/anyone really gets attacked by a player(s), heals and damage are now at 50%, meaning the monsters that were previously engaged have basically become 200%+ "stronger" due to them maintaining 100% damage, and any damage being done to them is now 50% and damage being healed on the players is only at 50% effectiveness.

Potential end result....XP team dead and most probably minimal to no pvp point gain by the attackers if the XP team was already decently damaged by the monsters.


What am I saying.....Basically....Heals shouldnt be dropped to 50% just because damage is....but only because it just makes ganking a lot easier (like its hard now anyway).

What I WOULD like to see in pvp is debuffs made 50%.....granted, its not as "good" for the majority of profs that dont heal, but in my opinion....its still "fair".

There is a simple sollution to all that. Damage only gets halved against monsters. It doesn't matter if another player is pounding on you, you still do the same damage.

Why not do this for heals too? When you take damage from a player, that part of the damage is flagged as PvP damage.

Damage from monsters are still healed up as usual.


Say a monster hits you for 75 damage.

A player then hits you for 50.

Now a doc heals you for 125 HP.

This heals up the 75 damage from monsters (which has a priority), and then heals up 25 (half of the remaining 50) of the PvP damage.


Would this be better?
