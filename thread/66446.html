VERY difficult to say this
I'm not here to rant, I'm not here to bash, I'm not here to complain. It's all been said and done before and it accomplishes nothing but cheapen the real message behind my post.

I'm here to give my thanks to all of you for being a second family to me for the past 15 or 16 months of my life. Saying goodbye is the

I'm going to forget alot of people here and I don't want you to think that this makes you any less important in my book. I care about you all equally

Zylina: Strange choice to put at the top of my list, you might think, as I know that I usually rub you the wrong way and I seem to enjoy rubbing you the wrong way a bit too much. Actually I just enjoy rubbing you any way I possibly can to tell the truth. But I admire your courage to speak your mind no matter the consequences. If more shared your courage and resilience we either would not have war or the Earth would be a smouldering pile of ash. I can't figure out which. But you defend yourself, your class with a fire that is nothing less than amazing.....Yet underneath it all there's a decency and a noble quality that I respect a great deal.

Lordmat/devilsown/devilsson/and the other dozen or so citizens running around: You were one of the first friends I made in this game, we go back a looooong way and out of anyone that comes to mind, you always had the most even temper and positive attitude. I sincerely hope our paths cross again, my friend

Exekias: Same deal bro. I remember last year playing my gimped adventurer in metaplast armor and you in the green cloak and blue waitt helmet outside of 20k. You spotted that nasty red plant and sent your pet out after it. "Don't attack the plant. Just keep me healed and I'll keep making new pets to send after it". Hehe, I never listen too well. Those were the days, weren't they? I really hope we keep in touch

Kupotek: You always made me smile. To this day I still run around saying "Meet the street with the hippity hoppity happy feet" and garner odd looks from those around me. Such a colorful and vibrant person, you always know how to cheer a person up, even at the expense of your own happiness. I wish you the best and advise you to keep smiling. The world can't get through a smile and will fall to pieces at a good laugh

Nitsobar: One of the best people I've ever met. You defended me during an 'incident' a few months back and it meant more to me than you'll ever know. Definitely keep in touch man and you'll have to get me roaring drunk the next time I'm in your town. You think I'm bad now? Cram some booze in me and when we wake up in jail sixteen hours later with nipple piercings and a new boyfriend named 'Bubba'---um never mind there

Stromm: The voice of reason in any forum! Such intelligent and well thought out posts, you've always had me nodding my head and saying "yup! exactly!". You're the ultimate sparring partner as well. Fair, good sport, excellent player. you're the role model that any new PVPer should choose

SuperSLAG: heh, one final bump on the name You're the best there is at what you do man. I own a computer repair shop and some of my past employess have also been AO players. I did a test run during work and had my two technicians and my service coordinator have you make various items. After the transaction was over, I told them "this is how you better deal with your customers. Fast, polite, clear service". So although you never knew it, I used your excellent business technique as a template for my company's customer service policy. For kicks and gigles, tomorrow I'm having a sign made that says "Customer service the Superslang way". I wonder how many customers will kno what I'm talking about? hehe

Ack, I can't type anymore. There's too many people to thank. Javaschick, Steffani, Microx, Fatcrusher, Stomper2000, hundreds more. I'd be here all night if I listed you all.

There's slight bit of hope you'll see my ugly face back on Rubi Ka, but as I've stated before, certain things need to be changed in order to see that happen, and not all of them are game related. So please forgive me if I decide to hang on to my 'stuff' until that day may come.

I only wish I had another week or so to accomplish everything I wanted and to say goodbye to more folks, but unfortunately when I hit the cancellation button, I saw that my subscription will end on Saturday the 9th

Goodbye and I love you all, my brothers and sisters.

In the meantime, if you see me bouncing around newland or tir, please just say 'hello' so I can have the chance to thank you prsonally for all you've done for me. Barring that, im only an email away at xcaliburpc@yahoo.com or icq #77976789.

*hugs* You guys are the best friends a guy could ever ask for. If I could, I'd rip your pixellated bodies right outta my monitor and chain you up in my basement so you could never get away. Um wait, that last sentence sounded wierd, didn't it?

I guess I'll just have to settle for printing out pictures of you all and kissing the pictures until the cherry red lipstick smears off of my lips---er, wierd again, wasn't it? Oh shoot, my mascara is running and my wife is sure to find out I've been borrowing her dress again..
