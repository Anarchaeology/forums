Hi all

Some comments about the damage team situation. After "sbje's team" won loot and kept it, emotions have been running hot. As far as them being ninjas and stabbing everyone else in the back, I think that depends totally on one thing.

Did they or did they not state their intentions up front. If they were in clannet chat and kept their intentions a secret, they were in the wrong. That's because they might well have been included in rolls had they not won loot rights. As I understand it they clearly stated their intentions to Jasonx well before the raid. By doing so they forfeit their chance of winning the usual roll should they not win loot. It also gives the ones running clannet a chance to organize a damage team to ensure they win loot.

In my view there's nothing wrong with making a different system/chat than the one currently being used if you're not happy with it. This is because it's impossible to make a system everyone agrees with. The thing that is wrong and I would consider ninjaing, is to pretend to follow someones system and take the loot once your team wins. And as far as most damage wins I don't see that as dividing clanners in itself. It is after all the current rule with all the different unique mobs, without people hating each other for it. There's nothing wrong with friendly competition, as long as all parties are aware of the situation.

Lordb mentioned some problems with pure damage team wins in the case of tara raids. This is of course true, and if omni show up in significant numbers, damage teams will fail. Another problem is that professions lacking in pure damage output would have a hard time getting into teams. That's unfair imo. However I see the damage teams as a temporary push towards a change. People are fed up with the arbitrary rules and items being looted without being posted.

The system Marlark proposed is a system I've been in favour of, and probably one that will get the broadest support (as long as a reasonable level limit is enforced). I think if we can get this system running, it will ensure the loot stays in clan hands as well as make it more fair to everyone. The rules need to be posted and always enforced. With the coming 14.6 loot messages, ninjaing will atleast be exposed.

I for one play this game for the fun it provides and at the moment tara raids aren't fun at all for me. No risk involved, and although I don't care too much about winning loot, I do understand people that do, and I do care about it being sorted fairly and the system not being exploited.

With all the drama going on now, I've decided to stop going and do things that are fun to me. Losing friends over this loot is not worth it at all. I do hope the proposed point system gets up and running, and I'll be happy to give help out if asked, as well as give my opinions on it. However I don't think I'll be going back until there is atleast a chance some omni sends my ass back to reclaim

Please, calm down everyone and do the things that make your gaming day most enjoyable.
