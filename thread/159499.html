Public Warning!
This is a warning regarding some unusual and disturbing sitghtings of a character called Demoneyes. Little information is known on this figure, however we know that he is extremely dangerous. He typically flies around Omni 1 (in quantum wings form, since he is.. was a metaphysicist) and has a very unusual, dark aura constantly surrounding him. If you at some point encounter this fiend, please report any information you may find to Deuscloud or Arakiba.

Here is the little bit of information we know:
Demoneyes apparently used to be an Omni-Tek Metaphysicist.. he was at some point exposed to some metaphysical disease, which causes him to have this aura surrounding him. Before this disease occured, he was in contact with various people.. who he now refers to as those "marked" by him - he also calls them his followers, however after talking to these people it is clear that there was never a strong social connection between them and Demoneyes, but maybe a psychological one (through dreams). Now whenever he speaks, he speaks only of shadows.. and a great force that will crush Omni-Tek, but he is deceptive and nearly impossible to catch, we don't know anything else, but he is a great threat, and we are trying to learn more, so again, and information you manage to attain is greatly appreciated.

Thank you citizens.
