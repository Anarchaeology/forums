Security Camera Woes, Part II
Security Camera Woes, Part I started here.

Glarawyn and Chester are still in Glarawyn's office

Glarawyn: And how about the Spring Fair, is everything proceeding as planed?

Chester: Mostly, there was a small delay with the cookies and, heh, chili...

Glarawyn waves as Gaiawyn "Autumnblaze" WolfMoon strolls past her office

Glarawyn: Good morning Gaiawyn!

Gaiawyn: Good morning, good morning. Can't talk, much work to do!

Glarawyn: Good good, keep it up!

Glara glares at Chester

Glarawyn: And just why is my door set to open when someone is in proximity?

Chester: I don't know. The contractors must have...

Glarawyn: What contractors? The security system was supposed to be an internal job!

Chester: Well, the accountant determined that it would be cheaper to subcontract the instalation, and then we could add the Circle G touch later.

Glarawyn: And this wasn't cleared by me?!?

Chester: You were..unavailable at the time. The cost savings was significant, and...

Glarawyn: Is that camara supposed to be on? I notice it's been running since the door opened...

Chester: No, the security system wasn't supposed to be activated until we had tuned it to our prefer...

Glarawyn removes a large gun from her desk and aims at the camara

Elsewhere on Rubi-Ka, a com link crackles.

Unknown Party: Recon one, we lost signal.

Unknow Agent: Yes, the feed was errm...bursted.

Unknown Party: Bursted?

Unknow Agent: Errr...yes. I'm getting out of here before they track down the signal.

The sounds of gunfire erupt over the agents head as a team of Circle G employees emerge from the Fixer Grid.

Take him alive!
