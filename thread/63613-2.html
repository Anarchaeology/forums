quote:
Originally posted by Dominata
Instead of being the usual pompous prick that you usually are, maybe you could actually help out for once and point out the cons, eh?
Just this once cause you asked so nicely.

1. Spam. If you've got a semi-high volume of chat on your channel the bot is going to get spam-blocked. The only workaround I can think of would require having multiple logins to the chatserver, and that wouldn't be fun to do.
2. Not your guild. You can talk to another guild, visit their forums, but when all is said and done it's *not your guild*. If some bot like this was set up between my guild and another it would be like looking in someone's bedroom, and they're looking back...not very good.

In the end, you want Funcom to implement a framework for something that can easily be done yourself.
1. Decide in your guild whether to merge (there *is* a voting system if you don't have forums.
2. Decide when your two guilds will merge.
3. Merge.

Now, as previously suggested you can even make this a big event, with inductions and such...a good way to meet your new guildmates. Anyone who can't make it, well grab them when they come online.

A couple difficulties with simply having a merge infrastructure:
1. Rank structure. Not every org system has the same number of ranks. Both orgs would have to have the same org system to merge. AFAIK when you change org types all ranks get reset to base, and then to re-promote you have to see everyone anyway...
2. Backend implementation. It's definitely possible that something like this would be difficult for FC to implement as far as the guild database goes. I can't claim to know how it works, but it's always a possibility.
