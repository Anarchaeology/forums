http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...?threadid=7741

When I saw your thread saying a majority of the players want this I decided to go and see if you were right.(I'm at work and have nothing better to do than count peoples votes in a thread)
The thread started out good then turned into an argument between some posters. I was very liberal when taking a Yes vote, if the poster hinted anywhere in their post that they thought this was a good Idea I counted it as a Yes.

Keep in mind, that many of the people I counted as saying yes said something like.

"I think this would be a good Idea as long as you can first do something to make a PVP title mean something"

So my results:

Yes Votes: 16

No Votes: 21

The majority of the posters felt that either spending time on a webpage devoted to this shouldnt be even considered till the game was fixed or said something about Gankers and Campers making a PVP title worthless and that Funcom shouldnt reward these people.

I think it would be a good Idea as long as a PVP title meant something. A person should not be able to gain a title from killing people who have no title already. This would allow those who dont wish to PVP(either because they dont like it or because they are playing a profession that is bad at PVP) to be left in peace.

If a person could only gain Rookie by killing people who already had Freshmen or above then PVP titles would actually mean something. As it is now, any lvl 150+ can go to a popular hunting area that happens to be in a 25% zone and wipe out people less than 1/2 their level and gain a title.

The Poll you speak of may in fact have shown that the majority of people wanted something like this but seeing that you said it closed "ages" ago and the Thread I got my results from is newer, I suggest that perhaps the people's opionion has changed.
