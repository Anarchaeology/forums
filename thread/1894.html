Try this
With a team, try the hammer beasts in The Longest Road. Enter from Athershire and hunt south of the road. The further in you go the denser it gets. Watch out for aggin too many mini bulls while you have a beast war going on. Past the road that branches south there are these big turtle things that I wouldn't hit if I were you, but get close to one of their herders and turn your sould card on to hear the singing (when you get bigger you can kill the herders, some of them carry Rings of Luck). The older you get, the further west on the main road through LTR you can go. There is also a Neutral City where you can save nearby: Drumdar, The Longest Road, (3642, 567) 75%: Missions, Insurance (15/1), Bank, Health, Weapons.

In your 50s more to the shades in North West Greater Tir County through the mountain pass, but watch out for vultures and sandworms.

When you hit 60-65, just move to 20k...you'll understand once you get there.
