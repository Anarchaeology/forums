Did I say the game works fully now? No, not in any of my posts. Did I say it works much better than at release? Yes, and if you stop to look at it, you'll have to agree.

In fact, I'd suggest that instead of asking if it works fully (not even EQ players after what, 3-4 years now, think that game works fully), ask if it works reasonably well.

Did the game work reasonably well when it went live?
No.

Does the game work reasonably well now?
Yes. There are few exploits, fewer holes in the world to fall through, I haven't crashed out of the game in a long time, content is at a reasonable level though I'd love to see more events and static quests, classes are mostly balanced (with a few notable exceptions that have been told when to expect their luv) to the point that Dai-Galean can get 10 main questions per class to address in the forums. Yes, PvP needs to be worked on, we need more content and hunting spots at higher levels (adressed in the expansion you denigrate), and there are those issues with each class that... hmm... they are addressing.

Is the patch rate slow? Sure. But having been involved with testing other games, I am impressed with the degree of testing Funcom does on each patch before sending it live. I wish they'd pay attention to the few bugs that they miss each time, but they have generally been minor bugs and they do have a player base like... oh... you for instance, that clamors for patchs to come out more often to fix more things. So they balance bug-free patches vs. speed of release and have struck this balance of about three weeks of testing.

But... do the patches continue to address the concerns raised on these boards? Yes, they actually do. Even if you don't get dev responses as often as you'd like, they are apparently reading these boards and prioritizing issues to fix. Yeah, theres things that have been broken for a long time (take a look at any of those top ten question lists in each class forum), but they are relatively minor things in many cases... as in the class is playable even with that bug, though not as good as it will be when they get to it on the list.

So... is the game broken? Not by a long shot. Does it live up to all that we'd like it to be? Unfortunately not, even though PC Gamer decided it was the best of the year. (I don't even want to think about what that means for the other games on the market).

Having said all that... Plush Leet Dolls? No, I don't know what they are thinking highlighting that on the main page, though apparently from the Official Poll there is a lot of interest. My only hope is that the person working on this is in management or marketing, and none of the programmers are doing anything with this project...

You'll actually discover that people give more weight to your views when you express yourself without swears or potty-humor.
