These are probably associated with boss camps. Whilst these are currently at fixed locations, they were implemented in such a way as to enable them to be moved around, so the 'scenery' is made of normal items which cannot be picked up and which don't vanish after a short while. This would enable FC to move the camps easily if they decided to do so as the 'scenery' can simply be despawned and respawned elsewhere, along with the MOBs.
