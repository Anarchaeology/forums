I'd just like to see some more 'moves' that can be used in regular combat. Hitting Q and waiting for brawl to recycle is BORING. Let's add some more hand to hand moves!

I detailed some info on this in a previous thread, but here are some of my ideas.

I think it'd be nice to have three 'lines'. Attack, Dimach and Defense.

Let me give some examples.

Let's say you have three 'slots' to put these special attacks in. You have to buy and upload something to use them (I'd like to have to go train with a sensei, but I know Funcom and NPCS Even if it was a nanocrystal or something you had to upload, and it became a move in the same menu brawl, sneak, dimach etc are in, that'd be fine.)

The requirements on them could be things like '400 MA + 200 strength + 150 phys init' for example. That one could be something like a very fast repeated series of punches (like a close range 'burst') that does three very fast hits (like, in 1 second) and recycles every minute.

Some other examples could be something like..

400 MA + 500 Dimach + 200 psychic (for example.)

That one could be a 10 minute recycling dimach that does 2000 fire damage (Fire of the Soul?)

Another, perhaps.. 400 evade-clsc, 300 MA, 250 Run Speed, 100 Agility. This one could be a 10 second 'move' that makes you evade 50% of hits. Takes 5 minutes to recycle? Like a TMS but not quite the same

All 'attack' moves should be on the same recycle timer, all dimachs on same and all evades on same, so if you use your 'Fire of the Soul' dimach move, you can't use any other dimach move for at least 10 minutes (the length of time it takes for 'fire of the soul' to recharge.)

I just think it'd be kinda fun to have some actual moves - I'd probably pick up a nice kick and a chem damage dimach or something. Would make playing an MA *way* more fun, anyway.
