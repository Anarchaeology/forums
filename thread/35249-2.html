Thx for the great thread Warlock! I'm a clan doc and was able to complete all 3 parts at level 15. The first 2 were a breeze (altho' the assasinate was a little difficult for me, I finally got it). The third is very difficult at that level. The omni guards at the gate are no problem, however there are a couple of Male Lt's, one of whom lit me up the first time I ran in. There are also a couple of Slayer droids which can really rain on your parade. That being said, with the fixer run buff and a brave soldier who drew alot of aggro (yes, he died =( I was able to *barely* get through on the second try. I think that for the last 2 parts (gloves and body) the mission location is variable based on your level.....

Oh for the record, I found Sarah just around the N edge of the mountain range -- definitely not on the East side, but I think if you just circle around the range and avoid the rollerrats/plants you can find her without much trouble, assuming nothing catastrophic has happened.
