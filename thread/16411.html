I like the idea - mainly for RP reasons.

In "RL" if I walked down the street and saw 2 cops jumped by thugs out of nowhere, would I run to get help? Would I try to help them myself? Or would I sit there like an idiot helpless and not do a damn thing.

The answer would of course be A or B [assuming there isnt another side to the story I witnessed]. I want to /assist our guards when appropriate. And I do not even LIKE PvP!! It just makes sense though.

I almost agree with Snarf sometimes I wish it was 0% even though I dont like PvP, but a guard /assist option would go a long way to helping things.

I suspect code wise this would get messy as it would mean we are consenting to PvP in a otherwise non PvP zone. Hence, each player would need a toggle flag for a "go" or "no go" status on PvP. People firing on guards get flipped to "go", and my targeting the guard and typing /assist flips mine to "go" as well, allowing me to target and attack.

If someone attacks a guard aligned to their side, they should flip to a neutral status temporarily hence omni can attack rogue omni attacking omni guards this would be a big help during events.
