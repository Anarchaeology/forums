All new questions for Cosmik and Company
Cosmik,

Actually some of these are several months old, but since you haven't answered them yet:

1) What are crats supposed to do Psyche-check wise when we are grouped with parties? I'm sick of leeching off my friends when their guns and nanos are just as effective against higher ups and I get "Target has a Psychic score above X".

2) What are crats supposed to do with ransacked? My MP and NT friends switch to the lower level nanos, yet you have prevented us from being able to do that.

3) Were the cratsuits and the new nanos given to us to try and balance our profession? I believe that was stated but I would like a clarification because.

4) If they were, why are they not available in the shops? If they were to make us equal with other professions who have all their nanos in the shops, why continue to gimp us with this policy? Isn't it enough that we had to survive for several months while watching the fully implemented professions zoom past us, why continue to gimp us?

5) If they were, why are you putting so many hoops for us to jump through to get the suit. I love the idea of profession-dependent equipment and having to earn it, but not if the equipment is necessary for us to finally acheive balance with the other professions!

6) Why have you thrown salt into both the Psyche check and the 'earn your suit to acheive balance' wounds by making even the putzy grays in the front room of the TLR uncharmable? Would it SO unbalance us if we could use our only really good nano line ONCE in a freaking dungeon? You claim to play a crat, take your crat in with a soldier, MA, MP, NT and Enforcer. You will have one useful line: Mez. And guess what, LOTS of other professions have that one also. There is NO reason for a group to take you in there over an equally good player in any other profession whatsoever.

Going with the normal timetable, I look forward to your answers in April.
