Daily Update September 27th
Today's update will be short, and about our need to do an emergency patch today that fixes a couple of exploits.

The most significant change is that we fixed a bug that gave players a higher chance of landing a critical hit on a red MOB than they should have, and this was important to fix as it allowed players to level a lot quicker than they should. We felt we had to patch that today to avoid patching on a Friday again.

We also removed Knight Armor, Sentinel Armor and Divaad's Armor. We had to take them out of the game because of the high stats and low prerequisites they had, unbalancing the game. People who had already acquired these items will lose them.
