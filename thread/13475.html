The dismal state of designer clothing
How often do you see 80+ level folks wearing all those fancy and wacky clothing available from Miiir? Mighty rarely, I wager. Reason: overequipping. Now don't take me wrong, I am not saying that overequipping ability should be removed. As we all know, the process is simple: find someone who can buff skills/abilities to needed level, equip a higher ql item. Buffs expire, item stays equipped. Catch? Should you happen to remove something that's overequipped, you can't put it back on unless you get those buffs again. This has been part of the game pretty much since day one, and personally, I think overequipping is OK. Heck, my lvl 90 MA sports a set of ql 112+ custom implants, and wears ql 125+ Flowers Tech armor. Except for helmet, which is ql 110, and which I can put on without getting a Feline Grace from an Agent. Why? Sigyn doesn't like to cover her good looks with a pink pot while in town.

What does this lead to? Well, the grand clothing selection offered by Miiir goes unused, for the most part. Ditto for tattoos. Also, about equipped weapons: anyone else think that dancing ymca while you got that huge Nova-Flow or 2 pillows in your hands looks awkward? People don't like to look awkward, and since they can't just drop that overequipped Stigma into a bag and grab a Martini glass... well, there go all the drinks, foods, bars and nightclubs. All these players locked into Elite armor and Primus Decus coats just don't see themselves dressed to party.

What I been thinking of is that equipment panel (Ctrl-1) would benefit from an overhaul. Yes, I do realize it's a fairly large change. Basically, add another tab: Social. Have it have slots for head, sleeves, chest, pants, gloves, boots, back, left and right handhelds. Anything worn in those slots would visually obscure armor/weapons. Introduce a 'empty' item that can go into any slot on social panel. Any unoccupied slot in social will allow the items in armor/weapons tab to show through. So, if you think those Waitt boots look swell with blue fret pants, you just leave the boots slot in social panel empty.
Of course, only ql1 bonusless items should ever fit into social slots. Not sure where Omni-Med clothes should go, though.
A nice touch would be a 'Hide Socials' button, which would hide those pink boxers, Katara shades and full-body dragon tattoo, and reveal your full Omni-Elite suit, complete with Premium Sledgehammer of Doom.

So, thanks for having the patience to read this, tell me what you think. Personally, I can't see how this could be an exploit, I do see how this can be a hurdle to implement.
