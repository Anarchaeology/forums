Healthstatus for pets
One thing I miss in the game is to be able to see my pets (I'm an MP) healthstatus easily without the need to do a /pet report or wildly alt-tab'ing through anything and everything.

This could be done with an extra set of healthbars just like the one you have for your own character. To be able to tell the difference between the pets for an MP the bars could be named A (attackpet), H (Healpet) and M (Mezpet).

I do think that this addition to the game would be appreciated by Engies and Beaurocrats too.
