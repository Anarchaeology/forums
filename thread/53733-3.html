MM walked slowly back to her apartment, opened the door, and flopped onto the bed.

It'd been quite a long day.

Had started normally, of course. She'd woken up at a Reclaim terminal around 8 PM, cursing the mission boss that'd killed her- then found people swarming around, saying they were 'glad she was back'.

Of course, it had all been quite frustrating at first. In the course of five minutes, she'd learned that not only had she been dead for a week, she couldn't remember the past month. MM had won Miss Omni-Tek, and lost Miss Rubi-Ka. (She grumbled a little to herself recalling this.)

Then again, seeing as she couldn't remember her talent, either, MM wasn't one to complain.

MM grabbed the nearest box of tissues and blew her nose a few times to clear her head. After a week of being dead, or at least almost dead, she still had reshock- worse than ever. It felt very, very good to lay down in her bed.

Realizing she was hungry after a week in cryo-stasis, MM got up and crossed the room to her refridgerator. She pulled out a cold piece of Leety's Pizza and stuck it in the microwave to heat up, grabbing a can of soda to go with it. Content, she hopped onto her bed again, and flicked the gridvision ((Television )) on, taking a swig of her soda.

MM grinned a little as she heard more coverage of Wyldthing's Miss Rubi-Ka win. MM couldn't get too depressed about losing, seeing as for all she knew, she'd done horribly. Whatever happened, I'm sure Wyld earned it.

The microwave beeped, and she hurried across the room to retrieve her pizza. MM wondered wryly if the competition of Bronto Burger could keep up with good ol' Leety's. You couldn't get much better than double cheese, pepperoni, sausage, mushrooms, and olives. Taking a bite of her pizza, a sentence scrolling the bottom of the screen caught her eye.

Today, Jack and Linda Locknane, ambassadors to Old Earth, were honored as helping Rubi-Ka prosper in harmony with Old Earth.

MM paused and nearly dropped her pizza.

She didn't know why it was there...but did it really matter?

MM smiled, and looked out the window. Hi Mom and Dad...I know you're out there. I don't know where you are, but you said it's for my own good...

Thanks.

MM finished off the Leety's Pizza crust. She licked her fingers, and turned the gridvision volume to low. "Apartment light level: off." she murmured, and the ceiling lights obeyed. MM settled underneath her cozy, warm covers, a ray of moonlight falling gently across her face from the window.

And as the gridfeed reports stopped, and the gridvision switched to a late-night cartoon, MM drifted off to peaceful, uninterrupted sleep, with one last thought.

It's good to be alive...

The End

Gridfan sat in his bed, putting his comlink down after a detailed roundup of info by Aktrez on what happen while he was incapacitated.

"Hmm! Dang it's over? Some always get to be where the fun is!" He said to himself. Stretching his almost healed body.
"Welp. Seems I'm almost good to go for Corgan and Nealandbobs reception show tonight"
Putting on his mask again, hiding the old plus the new scars caused by the almost direct hit by the storm.

The few who had seen his face did tell him to get surgery,
but he liked the scars. It was part of him.
Every scar had a story behind them,
not to mention the odd wound on his cheek that refused to heal
properly and always opened when under huge physical strain like fighting.

Looking out the window in the Reets building in Last Ditch he glances southwards.
"Hmm! They got her somewhere down there, I wonder if she really is...Ah! Silly idea. Oh well I'd better hit the shops. Wyldthing's damn leets stole all my food again *Sigh*".

Putting on the vizor to protect his eyes, then his as he liked to call it Technosmurf outfit. Making sure it was nice and tight not letting a single piece of sand get in he headed for the platform outside Reets and to the yalm garage there.

Taking straight off into the blue he fails to see seconds later a blue figure, coming in from the south and rushing thru Last Ditch and heading north, towards the forrests near Athen and Wailing Wastes.

MM finally pulled the refridgerator door open with her tail, and fell to the floor panting. She was already beginning to miss arms and hands. Working to get a door open for 15 minutes wasn't fun. She'd had to try a butter knife and warm water, and was about to resort to small explosives when she finally pulled it open using feet and tail.

She crouched on the floor, picking at the pepperoni on her pizza thoughtfully, and trying not to panic.

Maybe life as a leet wouldn't be so bad. I'll get tons of adoration, and leet treats.

But no arms, and stubby little legs forever...

MM picked herself up and waddled across the room to her commlink on the floor, falling down twice in the process. Painstakingly pressing several buttons with her nose and tail, her only hope was to get a message to a Doctor before it was too late. Of course, she had no idea if it could be cured- MM hadn't read about Permenant Leet Syndrome (As she'd dubbed it) in any of her schoolbooks.

But MM didn't know any Doctors...who could she call?
