Looking to start a new neut guild
I've noticed a lot of guilds here, but most are clan/omni, and neuts that post here are usually persuaded to choose a side.

Well, that's all fine and dandy, but for those of us who like playing a neutral and want to stay that way, but still find want the fun of guild-play, tell me sometime. If I can get the group ready, I'd like to start one.

Right now, I'd want pretty low level people, so that the guild can both grow together, but I'd like the guild to be one based on finding a lasting peace between all parties. I think that the peace to be won isn't through battle and bloodshed, but through understanding and reconsiliation. I want to make this clan a leader for the Clans, Omni-Tek, and other neutrals to see that peace can be acheived and that there is a way to resolve the conflict without annihalating each other.

The important thing is the planet, and making sure that all of us will have a place to live and friends to be with when this is all over, and that peace starts with a single step. I plan to make that step, and I hope others will follow.
