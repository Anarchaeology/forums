The little things I'd love to see in Anarchy Online...
I love this game. I hear lots of complaints about it but at least I can solo whenever I want and not have to run around Rubi-Ka for a hunting spot (did this in EQ so much and for so many hours it got frustrating).

Anyway, there are things I'd like to see in AO... they're not really important, but they would add a bit to the realism in the game...

-All edible food items when consumed should give a tiny amount of HP healing. Like maybe a Bronto Burger giving 5hp. The drink items should give less HP, and if alcoholic should lower your intelligence score a lot

-Trenchcoats and cloaks should give some AC as they cover up everything else

-A jukebox option in the techno dance clubs should be nice.. I don't want to keep listening to the same stuff inside all the time

-Team missions with puzzles... I know I mentioned this before, but don't you think it'd be fun to find a passcode to open the door leading to the next level's portal?

hehe... that's all I can think of for now
