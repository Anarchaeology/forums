Social nano formula's
I would greatly enjoy seeing a more social use of the nano formula's. When someone casts a program on you, the "nanobots" swarm around the target, creating a nice show, this could be used as an appearance enhancer.

Create some neat animated lights, not like the little lines that zip around your body ever few seconds from normal buffs, but rather a more dramatic appearance, say the creation of a holographic-like image on top of your own that makes you appear larger (appear being the key word, I'm not talking about a skill increaser). like, make my character look twice his height with 50% more muscle mass, the veins bulging a little more, but have my characters real image "inside" my own. the holo image would behave just as I do, but larger, etc. and I understand completely the required steps that it would take to animate such a thing, it would be consuming for an already overwhelmed funcom.

Something less dramatic would be the creation of bodily features that don't normally exist on our characters, like putting an extra head on us, adding a tail, claws, or some gag formulas that make it appear we have a Sword of Sir Galahad through our chest. or the ever popular arrow through the head.

Even less dramatic would be to simply extend the life of those pretty lights that float about right after you cast a buff. not extending the "show" from the actual buff, but creating a completly new buff that only has those animations. If anything, this would be a fun social thing to do when we are camping Omni-Trade for those non-store items. And better still, it would make us able to see better since those floating torches are pathetic.

Now, how do the players get these formula's? it would be too easy to just have a few in the general vendor machines in shops. maybe title levels would enable new social buffs. so a newbie wouldn't get to do a social nano until he reached his next title, and at each title you would be able to use the new social buff. perhaps there should be profession specific social buffs (all self cast only of course, any other way would spoil the fun). like a doctor could create that symbol around himself that has the human anatomy with the four arms/legs and all. or a soldier/enforcer be given the one that makes it appear you are huge.

a friend of mine (Tizzy) brought to my attention that when you cast several buffs in a row, you get a many colored light swirls that looks like something from the disco fever era. why not a disco buff? seriously, get a swirl of purple-ish blacklighting, some green, blue, and a little yellow or something like that. and have it swirl around us for like a minute or so while we dance.

another idea is to have a "nanocloud" of lightning, imitating Thor. or have your body appear as water to other characters. have our eyes pulse devilish red. steam floating off our head and shoulders. flames floating off our head and shoulders. maybe make a run buff that makes your feet appear as though they are on fire.

here's an interesting thought, a nano that gives the caster the appearance of the target (thoughts of agents come to mind).

just remember I speak only of appearance, in no way an enhancement of skills, just a way to make ourselves look different.

Anyway, this game is currently lacking in events and the missions are always the same. hunting never gets more enjoyable. the best part of this game is socialization (for me) so why can't we show off a little? have some fun? groove?
