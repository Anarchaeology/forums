Funcom balanced MPs.. now were are they putting the rest of us?
Meta-Physicist Changes:

MPs can now have multiple pets! You can now have up to three pets at the same time. Count'em, that's 3!

The three types of Pets are: Combat Pets (the first you get), Healing Pets, and Mind-Numb Pets.You can have one of each simultaneously. And they say three's a crowd, hah!

Reduced the nano cost of MP Pets.

Slightly increased the duration of the MP Pets.

Enabled zoning for all Pets.

Other MP Changes:

The Chant Pet-Buff line now also affects the "AGG/DEF" (Aggressive-Defensive) skill of the Pet (the one you change on your character with the slider in the Stats View). This can be used to increase the aggressiveness of the selected Pet, but will also render it more vulnerable.

The damage caused by the MP's "Instill" nano line for pets has been increased.

The duration of these buffs have been increased to match the lifespan of Pets. They only work on the Combat Pet.

The Healing Pets will now heal more often, better, and faster.

Added a new line of MP programs called "Teaching" and "Infuse With Knowledge."

Added another line of Pet programs for the MP Combat Pets called "Anima." This is a short-duration damage increase buff.

We have fixed several bugs relating to the Healing Pets. They should be working correctly now, please let us know.

MP pets will now heal correctly when you use other healnanos at the same time.

MP pet buffs now work as they should.

Improved Pet path finding. They should now be able to follow their master better in dungeons.

The command /pet report will now give feedback that includes the pets name. This is part of the support for multiple pets at the same time.

Metaphysicists can now summon weapons! There will be a line of weapons that are unique to the MP that can be summoned. They are not like ordinary weapons -- They do not do a whole lot of damage, but will have other effects on them.

Odin's Missing Eye should no longer inflict damage each time you zone.

Lull Wrath will now use the correct icon (used to look like a buff icon).

(Whent hey fixed Lull wrath they did this too: "Agents using false profession will no longer be able to learn or cast the engineer or bureaucrat pet summoning nanos. They can't use the shells anyway, so there is no reason they should be able to learn the nanos.")

The 3rd pet will be added. It is a psychosis/mesmerize pet.

Healing pets will no longer go into a coma if their target dies or fades away.




Ok so they fixed and balanced MPs.. When are they gonna fix the rest of us?
