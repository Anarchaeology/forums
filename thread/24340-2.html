Big question... Do you pvp as sport, or do you do it because you want to hurt people? Personally, I do it as sport and part of roleplay, and I'm genuinely not interested in hurting other people IRL. No more than I want to actually kill someone I'm going bowling with or playing poker with.

I really do think that someone who is out merely to upset other people is being a jerk, plain and simple. Hiding behind the anonymity that you get online and using it as an excuse to be mean and cruel. I don't feel a need to put on a mask and go somewhere to be mean and cruel to people who won't recognize me on the street. Online meanness is cowardice, no more respectable than calling up random people in a phone book and threatening them.

Back to AO....

In character... they are the omni scum and I'm fighting 'em. Out of character they're people playing the game like me, only for the other team.



Going along with this... I have a rule for myself. I don't attack anyone lower than me (especially untitled and lower) unless attacked first. I admit that I have done it in the past, and I generally felt stupid about it. I made a firm rule for myself some time ago to never do it anymore. When I come across a lower-level guy in the political zone, I'll sneer at him, turn my back and walk away.

In character, it's because I play someone with a lot of honor and attitude. My character wouldn't attack someone who looks weaker than him... He figures that if he wins, he hasn't proven anything except that he's too cowardly to find a worthy enemy. If he loses, he looks weak and pathetic. Hence, nothing really to gain, but something to lose. On the other hand, fighting tough opponents means plenty to gain, and little to lose. (Of course, the actual pvp title may or may not reflect this, but who cares? This is a RPG. Besides, I should get rookie any day now at the rate that I'm going. I win more than I lose.)

Out of character, this is a game, folks. Regardless of how other people play, I'm a nice guy and I don't want to ruin anyone's day. Yes, I've been shot up and ganked by characters way higher than me, and I hate it just as much as everyone else does. Just because other people do it doesn't mean that I should start. There's no shortage of jerks on Rubi-Ka... I don't have to be a jerk also.

We're not lions, they're not gazelles; we're not real soldiers fighting a real war; none of these metaphors are meaningful to me... we're normal people playing a RPG that's wildly unrealistic six ways from Thursday. The goal of AO was never to make the most realistic experience possible. It's to make the most fun experience possible. Realism when it's called for, but realism is and should always be sacrificed in the name of fun. Why is there an insurance reclaim? Typical PC-style 'save-game' feature is not realistic, no matter how you slice it or what explanation or excuse you give it. This realism sacrifice makes the game more fun. Every single time you find something "not-quite-as-realistic-as-it-could-be" in AO, it's that way because it's intended to make the game more fun.

I really enjoy pvp... love the adrenalin and the challenge. But, like I said, this is a game. People my level and higher are fair targets for me, but I generally consider lower level folks to be unwilling participants unless they challenge me or shoot first. I don't want to play with people who don't want to play with me.

In the game, I'm fighting omnis. IRL, the omnis and I are playing a game together. I wouldn't acknowledge that in-character, but I'm careful to never forget that out-of-character.
