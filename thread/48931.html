Hmm........I'd have to(kinda) agree with Cloudeh here, people. The only time I usually use Challenger is in the boss room, tanking the boss while the rest of the team beats down the minions........and it *does* give a dmg boost, *and* I seem to crit a bit more frequently. For the record, I'm using ql250 scope with ql200 Byom/Rider combo. Unfortunately, I never really played with Rage too much before today, except as a runbuff hehe. The main problem, is that I need a big scope to keep my damage up........but it hurts my inits too much. I missioned with a lvl200 MA today, and started playing with Rage and Challenger during the mission, just to see what would happen. I don't have hard data to back this up, this is just my experience........for me, using Infernal Rage(top Rage) was better on the mission mobs than Mighty Challenger to Behemoth(top Challenger), but on the higher-level(compared to the mission mobs) boss, Challenger pulled even or maybe a little ahead, depending on the boss type.The reason is this: Rage adds a decent amount of damage, and a little more to the crits, but is a HUGE init buff........I was doing a good bit more dmg than normal, more crits, and hitting at 2X normal speed(mission mobs). Chain-casting Rage, I was able to get and hold aggro roughly 50% of the time from the 200MA........not to mention the NT in the group as well. Using Challenger on the mission mobs increased my dmg even more, but because I was hitting at 1X speed, Rage was actually better for dmg; not to mention the fact that I was still able to Mongo, if need be. Now, as to the boss..........Rage didn't quite cut it there. Yes, I was still hitting at 2X speed.........but simply had LOTS of misses/parries; my puny(due to HW req of my weapons) 814 AR just wasn't enough, even at 2X speed. However, because Challenger boosts AR, I was doing more dmg,
and missing less; so, even hitting at 1X speed, Challenger was a better choice there. Again, this is seat-of-the-pants, and I have no hard data to substantiate what I've said.......just my experience to fall back on. Bottom line: for mobs slightly or medium difficulty above your level(or, most likely, PvP), Rage is a better choice. For mobs greatly above your level, Challenger is better. From a pure damage perspective, that is. I think I just found my new favorite toy
