one of the things a lot of ppl who look at database and ask why certain items don't drop at whatever QL, is that they made the item with 2 QLs. high and low. then the item also has it's droppable QL depending on what drops it. (like notum metaplast armor which the sheets drop only for really low levels, but in the database it shows from ql1-200). it gives them a way to do

((lowQL + highQL)/(highQL-lowQL))*currentQL.

and an easier way to add mobs that drop a different(perhaps higher) QL later if they need to, rather than pushing up the QL later and having to find new balance numbers to test out.
