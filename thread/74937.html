This could be used as an exploit for titles because you're getting PvP points out of nowhere. For example.

Player A has 1400 PvP points
Player B has 1400 PvP points
(1300 is the base)

So.. let's say PlayerA kills PlayerB for 15 points. The 50% buff then adds 7.5 points, for a total of 22.5 points. Let's just round it down to 22 for simplicity.

So, PlayerA has 1422. PlayerB has 1385.

Player B kills PlayerA. PlayerA loses 15 points, PlayerA gains 22.

So now we're at:

PlayerA: 1407
PlayerB: 1407

So they both just gained 7 points for killing each other. Do that 100 times and they've all progressed 7 titles.

So... unless it equaled out so that both gained/lost them (which would be unfair to the opponent imo) then it wouldn't work.
