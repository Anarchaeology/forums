The Party!

Perhaps she was being hypocritical, after all, she socialized with clanners all the time. A smirk flickered across her face at that thought and she found herself peering out of the corner of her eyes now, not at Malcom, but at someone else. She wondered quietly just how dangerous her 'relations' with him could be, and she just stared quietly at his form sitting there on the couch, her body still dancing. He was oblivious of her gaze, and Raquel naturally preferred it that way, especially at this moment. Could this clanner pose danger to her... she wondered, he was a popular fellow indeed, always dealing with some kind of organisational business it seemed.
Suddenly he looked up at her and Raquel was caught by surprise. She worried that somehow he had read her mind, but he merely flashed her one of those smiles and gave her dancing form a good look. Alamexis sighed and Raquel continued. "I'm not sure which part's more amusing.. this so-called love triangle or your klepto of a sister...," she struck a nerve purposefully, and he responded in turn just as she thought he would. He was protective of his sister, obviously, and Raquel reassured him that she had nothing to go on. She barely even knew his sister...

"What I do know is that I am being accused of being a traitor to Omni-Tek, my sister has been assaulted by a member of Omni-Pol in the cup...," he seemed annoyed as he finished, and the opifex studied his every move, inflection, expression. Moving to turn around and lean against the table, she turned her eyes to the party. Alamexis explained that his sister met the famous Szentasha and wanted to repay her kindness with Omni-Med suits. He explained the girl, this Amacia, as some old friend of his he had to discuss "business" with. Bah.. Raquel's mood was quickly frustrated as the pair was approached by Whisper's Edge folks. Indeed, lovely shadows he had picked up.

Soon, Quinn entered and Raquel was glad someone desirable showed up. He kept his distance, lighting a cig and scanning the area.

"Business?", she asked Alamexis.
"The 'business' is a man by the name of Radein. He was somewhat of a friendly rival of mine back in school, but he holds quite militant views...in favour of the clans. He doesn't like Omni-Tek much," he mused.. great, another Omni-Tek hater touches down. Just what she needed.. still, Malcom continued as her thoughts wandered, "He was taught by a Professor Kolin on one of the outland colonies - a self-proclaimed "expert" on Rubi-Kan history, and even more militant towards the clans."

Definitely a new problem for Rubi-Ka. Hmmph. She mused to Malcom.. mused back to him about someone having a problem with him obviously. Who that was, she didn't know. Either he was guilty of something highly questionable, or someone didn't like him. Something stirred beneath him though, something ultimately bad for him and those he cared about. Her eyes flickered back to the clanner male. He was dancing now, with his girl and a few others. She watched his solitus form, always admiring of the 'breed'...

The meeting eventually ended with Alamexis needing to take care of some business.. and Raquel ended up back at the party until she went home.

She never misses a Tarryk show...but, it was just a dream, dammit. She's just being fashionably late.

Pulling Kirasha closer to him, he tries to take his mind off of the nightmare and removes his hand from his comm...surprised to find it there. Although he had never told Kira about his penchant for sometimes dreaming of events that eventually take place, he noted a glimmer of concern in her eye when he mentioned Sezmra. It was clear that the Omni agent had made an impression on her as well.

After putting a request in with Tarryk, Braedon looks up to see Sezmra walking into the room. His heart skips a beat as it often does when he sees her, but more than anything...he's filled with relief. Trying to act normally, he smiles and greets her...yet, his mind remains troubled. This was a dangerous woman, and for a reason he had trouble putting a finger on, he found himself drawn to her...and, oddly protective of her. Of course, beautiful opifex women have always had a way of drawing his attention, but this wasn't the same. Was he genuinely starting to care for this woman who could very well be a viper looking for the time to strike?

Braedon shakes his head and chuckles to himself.

Developing a fascination for an agent of the enemy. You're getting soft.

Finally able to relax with the metal pounding in the background, Kirasha pressed up against him and Sezmra nearby swaying to the music, Braedon turns his attention to the crowd. Several of his guild members are present, as usual. He also sees many members of Whisper's Edge about, including Vixentrox, the organization's leader who he respects highly and considers an ally. Whisper's Edge had also been in the news lately, yet he had not spoken to any of their members about the rumors he had heard.

Braedon decides tonight is a good night to listen in and observe...especially when he spots Alamexis entering the room. A stir seems to begin almost immediately, which Braedon finds fascinating.

Uwen of Whisper's Edge makes for Alamexis quickly and they engage in a discussion about a new constitution. Braedon isn't able to catch all of the conversation, and certainly doesn't want to make it look as if he's trying to hear more than he should.

Glancing up at Sezmra, he catches her watching Alamexis closely, as well. It seems the agent is here for more than partying tonight, and as usual, has her finger on the pulse of what's happening on Rubi-Ka. Braedon knows the two have been seen together on numerous occasions, but isn't certain what the nature of their relationship is, exactly.

Alamexis looks troubled and on-edge. Rather understandable considering the press he's been getting lately. He's certainly drinking a lot tonight, also...but, Braedon has no room to judge someone about finding escape in the bottle and he knows it.

A militant presence next to him comes to his attention, setting his teeth on edge like an annoying fly. Glancing over rather directly, he sees Elviria, heavily armed and staring his way. Braedon mutters a greeting and tries to ignore Omni-Pol, something he's normally quite adept at. This one seems to be taking a bit of an interest in either him, or one of his guild members.

"That's the last thing I need," Braedon mutters to himself.

Suddenly, the Adventurer has a feeling of being watched....and, it wasn't from the diminuitive Omni-Pol next to him. Looking quickly over his right shoulder, his eyes meet those of Sezmra. He tries to tell himself that his sudden smile was meant to distract her from the thoughts swirling through his head, but realizes it was on his lips before he had a chance to think about it.

Silently cursing beautiful opifex women, Braedon decides dancing is in order and pulls Kirasha to the dance floor.

Returning to his seat a few songs later, a bit breathless from Kirasha's dancing...he realizes Sezmra is no longer around. A bit alarmed, he searches the room and spots two figures huddled in the far corner across from the dance floor. The distance was too far for him to be certain, but he had to know. Excusing himself, he mingles as he crosses the dance floor...inching closer to the tables across the way. Finally, unable to use the crowd as cover, he walks alone towards the couple until it's clear that it's Sezmra and Alamexis, talking privately. Is she involving herself in the obvious danger and trouble surrounding this Omni lawyer?

Braedon quickly returns to his seat hoping he wasn't spotted, and curses himself for being so indiscreet. It's not his business, and Sezmra can more than take care of herself.
