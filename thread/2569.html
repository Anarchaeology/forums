Use your imagination.
I left AO for a while, as well. Got tired of running around and just doing missions. Then, a friend of mine sent me those episodes they are making, and I got hooked again.

When I came back I found a greatly improved game, with much less bugs, more professional players, and the mission centrals had started giving me missions close to home, FINALLY. So no more running around. I pick a mission in Newland, while I am in Tir, close to the Newland city whompa, and i cash out 6K missions at medium difficulty. For a lvl 16 thats pretty ok, I think.

You can do anything! With all the options you have here in AO, you can even start your own taxi-service. Get people to /follow you and you can run to their missions out in the zones.

Start your own shop?
Pick high-level missions and sneak around, until you find the goodie stuff, get high quality items and sell them to someone who needs them. Get lots of cash, buy yourself a bikini and go swimming with the pirahnas. GO NUTS!

In conclution, I don't know when or why you left our world, but I can assure you, it's a lot more fun now. I for one got all my intrest back, just by picking a different kind of character, the second time around.

Also, things get more fun if you Roleplay a little. Try to take a personal intrest in your character, and interact with other players, as if the world was a little bit more real that it "really" is.

And best of all, you are always welcome back. :-)
