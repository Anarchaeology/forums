Fixing the NT profession
Fixing the NT Profession

PART ONE:

Before I thought about writing this I asked myself whether or not Funcom would read it or even take the time to seriously understand the NT profession and why so many of us want positive changes done. I realize by not writing it, I wouldnt be offering my ideas to them as both a customer and a gamer that wants to see Anarchy Online improve and conquer in the MMORPG community.

AO is a competitive game and like many games, the competition not only ranges between the professions but within our own NT ranks as well. We play an NT for many reasons and sometimes we compete against our fellow NTs and other times we compete against the other professions outright. When the profession you play no longer matches the description or idea that it was meant to be, the profession needs to be fixed, not dodged or set aside and given distance. Most of the gamers that have stood painfully by the NT profession and its idea have done so at the cost of being ridiculed by other professions both in game and out of game on channels, message boards, and even profession websites. We have sat by for months waiting for some type of official reply from you, Funcom, in the hopes that you would offer at least a recognition that we have problems and a general time frame on when you will try to tackle some of these issues. All we hear is silence and most of us cant help but wonder if there is an underlying reason for this. People have told me to play other professions and while I do to some point, I wish to continue playing an NT because its about role-play and the idea of enjoying what it is you want to do.

Now, as most of us are mature gamers, we do understand that you have projects that are very important in enhancing game balance and overall enjoyment. The patch for 14.0 looks promising for team play and for initiating more profession instruction discs in the game. The patch for 14.2 looks promising for re-balancing skills entirely with the IPR and over equipping while not perfect is an attempt to balance out the professions where armor and weapons are concerned. So, we know you have a lot on your plate. All we really want is an official statement acknowledging that you are looking into things.

As for our profession at a glance, I want to show you some general concerns and where we might be able to improve on them. In the following article below, I am not going to berate another profession like some, including myself, have done in the past. On the contrary, Im just going to concentrate on a few things that we need looking into, or fixed. The list is in no particular order but all of it needs to be viewed. Remember, these are just ideas and concerns.


Instruction Discs  I know that our nano, Nullity MKII and the upper echelon nanos have been placed in the game via instruction discs. The first and foremost problem that we see as a community is that the rarities of these discs are extreme and that the number of times that they spawn in the game has been seriously curtailed. I am hoping that in 14.0 with the introduction of boss mobs and the note that you produced about instruction discs  that this is your way of saying that our discs will be introduced more frequently. If not, we ask you to please look into this.

The second problem with instruction discs is the ability to assemble them. The skills you implemented with construction are noted and while I agree with the overall process because you went to some detail to implement it, you didnt provide an avenue for NTs to have flexibility with obtaining their end product. Part of this reasoning is that if an NT were to find a Nullity MKII instruction disc, the going price for it is close to 200 million if not slightly more. How can an NT trust an engineer to help in the assembly if that engineer decides to steal it and sell it? This is not berating the engineer profession mind you but just stating the obvious about any gamer. If a gamer needs money in the game and wants to steal something, this is an easy way to do it. I am hoping that you will provide a new automation service by implementing it in the trade skill section. You could have the service be a machine where you place the instruction disc into it and are charged a hefty price for the assembly. I for one would rather pay a machine 1 million credits to assemble my nullity MKII disc (if I ever find one) than an engineer that has the skill to do this due to the rarity of such discs. Please understand if all of our upper level nanos are in instruction discs, the engineer might charge us considerably more than 2 million per assembly. In the long run we will be paying too much for assembly because we are being forced to rely on another profession for our end result.


Range Extenders and Range Increasing Nanos  Range extenders and range increasing nanos have been in the game for some time now. Both of these are designed for the outdoors where distance does become a major factor. Inside missions, the finite space and the ability of mobs to hit you from almost any distance outweighs a comparison for extenders and range increasing nanos. In the outdoors, an NT with a range extender and a range increasing nano would be a deadlier foe to face. While I understand your position on monster aggro and pathing I dont quite understand why you cant fix this. Both of these things were designed to help an NT deal damage from a range to compliment the fact that the NT has low health. It was designed as an advantage to compensate for the low health. With these factors in mind, I dont believe you ever gave us an official reason why monster aggro and pathing could not be fixed. You cannot leave an NT with a range increasing nano and a range extender that does not work. Its not mechanically viable from a designers standpoint. Range Extenders need to be either removed from the game or substituted with a new improvement that benefits the NT substantially or fixed. We would rather you fixed this issue and allow range to be a factor once again.


NCU costs for nanos  One of the more severe problems an NT faces is the ever-increasing cost of nanos, as he gets higher in level. With a full assortment of nanos self buffed, an NT easily finds himself nearing the 230 to 275 mark in NCU costs alone. This means that we have to have a great deal of NCU outfitted and even when we do, we do not have room to accept other buffs from other professions. The idea of placing the nano formula recompiler in the 6th slot of the NCU belt only diminishes our NCU capability to 5 NCU chips that starts to cause a more severe NCU problem. I am asking that you reconsider two things here. First, lower the NCU costs for our nanos by a considerable margin especially for the humidity extractor, enfraams, crunchcom, and nullity lines. If you wanted to implement a new nano that reduced the NCU cost by 25 percent you could do this by allowing it an NCU cost of 1 so that it didnt interfere with our other nanos and also make it highly available in the game as a completed nano crystal.

Fumbles and Countering  I know many NTs that have very deep concerns where these two problems that seem to surface and compliment one another with overall ineptitude. Most NTs thoroughly enjoy the ability to use fast nukes or multiple fast nukes when executing nanos. The problem arises when out of a sequence of 10 fast nukes you get the following: Hit, fumble, hit, hit, hit, fumble, hit, counter, hit, hit. For each fumble and counter, a large amount of nano is lost, further complicating the issue that mobs have with health factoring. Mobs have high health. If we were to use a device that was placed in our computer deck it would simply create more problems with NCU as stated above. I really believe that the code for fumbles needs to be looked at as well as a serious bug with countering at higher levels. Real mean mobs and Aces have the ability to counter almost all of our programs with regularity. There are also bugs that I myself have reported to your staff where there are pockets within the game as you are fighting a mob where the mob counters a nano every time until you move a step backwards or make the mob move a step forward. I propose that NT nanos have their fumbling reduced to 2 percent due to our exceptional professional ability when using nanos and that our nanos as a whole be given a very low resist rate versus all targets.


The Attack Nano, ATK, Recharge, Radius, and Damage  Ive thought long and hard about what I want to say here and have listened to my fellow peers with an open mind. The Nano-technician is and will always be the most damaging profession where nanos are used. Funcom you promised this and have kept to this idea and we have some valid concerns here.

Firstly, lets start with attack. As nano-casting initiative becomes great, attack rates are very fast and this process has been fairly smooth. I dont feel many people are complaining about 3 to 4 second attack nukes because with high nano-casting initiative, these nanos are almost instantly cast. We do however believe that the higher damaging nanos that have a large attack delay is definitely out of proportion. Izgimmers Last Word is a good example. Its attack delay is 20 seconds. In missions, mobs have a lot more health than the damage this nano does and by looking at other nano comparisons, the damage would be average anyways. So the long attack delay is a penalty. In PvP, fights dont last 20 seconds. So this is our highest and best nano and cant be used in PvP. Attack delays should stay around the 3 to 4 second margin so that we have a fighting chance.

Secondly, lets talk about the recharge delay. The best tactic for a nano-technician is using fast nukes to damage a foe. The problem is only a few of our nanos, mostly area effect nanos, are low recharge rates. Our highest AE nuke, Kels Neutronium Plaything has a 7.5 second recharge delay. Our highest direct damage nuke, Izgimmers Last Word has an 8 second recharge delay. Recharge delays on most of our mediocre to decent single nukes have a recharge rate from 3.4 to 7 seconds in delay. We want considerable reduction in recharge delays. Our recharge delays for all attack nanos should stay within the 1.5 to 2.5 second margin. There are valid reasons for this and most of those will be discussed in the damage portion of this segment.

Thirdly, lets talk about radius of area effect nukes. The radius for our best area effect nukes have been seriously diminished and in turn has given us cause for concern in the PvP political areas. As it stands now due to nano costs and attack and recharge delays, area effect nanos are our best way to conserve nano. It was one of our greatest selling points into becoming an NT  in that we would be dishing out a lot damage in a large area. This large area has collapsed so much that we are subject to dismissal in political zones by any formidable team that uses and calculates distance when attacking. No team stays together but we have no valid tactics for attacking a well-rehearsed team that knows if they stay x amount of distance apart they can still hit us with their range attacks and not get killed. Our AE nukes are more or less becoming the equivalent of direct damage nukes in that we are only attacking one person rather than multiple persons. Why was this ever changed to begin with? In addition to this note, (LIKE-SIDED) mobs are attacking us in political zones when we use AE nukes. If like-sided players arent hurt by our nukes why should they hurt like-sided NPCs? As it stands in this particular note, experienced players are standing near NPC vendors so they have assistance and this is an exploit. Please work on this a great deal.

Finally, we are going to talk about damage. Ill start with mission mobs first. The average damage for our nukes versus MOB AC makes almost all of our nukes do minimum damage. There is no Minimum-Max damage only Minimum-Average damage. I have never in the last 50 levels of playing an NT and Im 137th currently seen a max damage ever against a mob. It simply does not happen. Most of this is probably due to the way MOB AC is calculated and could be easily remedied. In addition to this note, we have no critical hits calculated in our damage nukes. There should be a critical hit modifier with every nuke that we cast. We dont have special attacks and even if we used weapons that offered special attacks it would greatly diminish our IP due to weapons skills costs and also relying on those weapons would hurt our casting effectiveness. We need a critical hit modifier or a special attack. This is very important. As for PvP, further complications arise. In PvP we have no special attacks or critical hits with our nano casting. In addition you have placed a half damage cap on all nanos. With AC factored into this equation and with the half damage cap, all of our nukes do (Less than min damage  up to min damage but not greater) against any properly prepared foe. What this means is on those super nukes with the long attack and recharge delays we would see damage slighted considerably. With an example being our very best AE nuke, Kels Neutronium Plaything  the damage is supposed to be 2,400-3,155 to all enemies. Due to the nature of half damage the new min-max is 1,200-1,577 damage to all enemies. Further complicating this damage, when AC is factored in at upper levels (4500 AC as an example) you diminish damage by 450 points. Kels is now a 750  1,127 damage AE nuke with a 6s attack delay and a 7.5s recharge delay. If I were to use this AE nuke in PvP I would more than likely do 750 points of damage and have to wait almost 8 seconds to use another nuke. This makes our best AE nuke obsolete. I recommend removing the half damage cap on AE nukes especially and add a critical hit modifier on each cast whereby they could inflict max damage without caps or max damage +25% without caps.

PART TWO
PART TWO:

Nano Ability Buffs and Debuffs  This is a very delicate subject that almost every NT becomes enraged at when discussed. We do not have the ability to buff and increase our nano abilities but we do have the ability to have our nano abilities debuffed by others. I believe that NTs should always have been able to buff their abilities to the point equal too if not greater than the mastery, infuse, and mocham lines currently in game. In addition the ability to debuff an NT should be extremely resist able. After all, nano abilities are our meat and potatoes and if we cant use them we are dead in the water. An NT is only as strong as his best and most effective nuke. If deprived or dominated an NT is now only as strong as someone 50 to 75 levels lower than him. This takes a level 137 NT such as myself down to an 60th or 85th level NT in consideration of nano casting. In addition to the damage as stated above in PvP, now Im using a half damage nuke from an extremely low level. Ineptitude is the best word I can now use to describe such an NT. Nano Resist is broken in PvM and in PvP versus ransacks/deprives it rarely if ever works. In missions this becomes a serious issue because with one dominate or deprive, we are heading for the zone. In the case of PvM versus a tough nanoaddict, as we run to the zone we are being swiped from behind for 400 to 1000 points of chemical damage to only die before we can reach the exit. Keep in mind you cannot calm a nanoaddict that just used nano shutdown on you. In PvP we are cannon fodder for any profession with debuffing abilities and even with the OE balance you are placing in 14.2  we still do not stand a chance against these types of classes. People are always crying nerf, nerf, and nerf. I dont want to cry nerf anymore. I would just like you to fix nano resist the way it was designed to work, take a serious look at resistance modifiers versus all debuffs, especially in PvP, take away debuffs from PvM missions altogether, and consider an ability buff line  especially for matter creation for the NT profession. Once again, Im not asking you to nerf or take away anything from another profession but rather take a deeper look at resist balancing and ability buff/debuffing.


Improvements and Concerns for (not-yet implemented) and (implemented) nanos and devices.  Funcom could you please take a look at the following nanos and make improvements to them:

CrunchCom Line  If range is not going to be fixed increase the reduction of nano cost for every nano in this line.

Cohesion Line  If range is not going to be fixed, atleast reduce the NCU cost of this nano by 50 percent.

Enfraams Line  Reduce the NCU cost and increase the health benefit.

Dark Movement  Either reduce the NCU cost or increase the benefits to 120 instead of 80.

Nullity MKI and MKII  Reduce the recharge delay from 6 seconds to 1 to 3 seconds. Fix damage from reflect shields, damage shields from entering the sphere. Fix hostile nanos such as debuffs, and DoTs from entering the sphere.

All the Root lines  Allow breakage to be reduced by a considerable amount and keep mobs rooted for the duration if not attacked. Mobs are breaking roots before their duration is up even when not attacked. Furthermore, fix the ability for a player to debuff himself in order to exploit and break roots.

+Add a nano resist line  this line of nanos would allow us to resist debuffs very effectively and keep the NCU cost low please. This is intended if you are not going to fix the previous mention concern.

+Add a critical hit line of nanos or nano modifier line  this line of nanos would allow us to critically hit or modify the critical hits for existing nanos.

+Add special weapons that act as special attacks and require only (nano ability modifiers and dual wield) to outfit. These weapons could be hand held and/or dual wielded.

If anyone has any further ideas that are positive, please respond to this article accordingly and Ill add your statements. My thanks to every NT that has been patient enough to deal with the problems and concerns that we all have had for a long time now and I hope that you stick it out and achieve your goals.

Funcom please reply to this letter and article as I am placing it on the board as well as emailing it to you. Just an official statement please. You could also make this a sticky thread if you deem it worthy.
