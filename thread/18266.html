Off the top of my head:

/s = shout
/say = vicinity (it's annoying when someone shouts that's standing 1 foot in front of you )
/g team = team (useful for macros so you don't spam your guild channel announcing targets )
/g charname = char group (chat group created by charname)

other than that, you pretty much have to change channels. I wish they'd at least add "/gu" for guild chat, and "/ooc" for OOC. Other than that, I don't really care about the rest. I'm only on them when I need something specific.
