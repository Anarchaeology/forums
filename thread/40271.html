Balance Issues?
Guys about balance.

This is just my opinion.

When you look over these boards, what do you see? You see 1 profession going after the other because they cant beat them. What you don't see, is professions bragging about who they are able to beat.

NO single profession in AO will EVER be the "Super pvp profession."

Some people say it should be Soldiers, others say it should be NT's who are uber. Fact is, EVERY profession should have a nemesis.

Of course Soldiers think it should be them. NT's think it should be NT's and other professions think they are the ones who should be uber.

Fact is, in games such as AO where there are so many varriables to consider, how can you ever possibly have a "superrior" profession in every way shape and form? You cant.

What would happen to this game if Soldiers (for example) were the beginning and end of pvp? Well, you would be LFD not LFG. Cause there would be 5 soldiers per group.

Take a look at the misconcieved uberness of Grid Armor. How many new fixers are running around atm? Tons, it's all about bandwagoning.

Take other games for example, games that pvp was in mind from the very start.

Diablo 2. Played it? Barbarians (Soldiers) were UBER mean vs any of the other mana based classes, however they had trouble under certain circumstances where those mana professions were able to make armies (see Necromancer with Iron Curtain) or Sorceress with cold damage and really quick on their feet gave them problems too.

It's all about how your character is setup and WHO your setup to fight.

Take me vs Kink for example. I'm fully implanted vs melee at the moment. Kink has trouble hitting me sometimes. He looses, calls for fixer nerfs on grid armor.

What Kink doesn't know is Abid OWNS MY BEHIND in every possible way shape and form. And it has nothing to do with his debuffs really, it's the fact that my Duck Exp is not implanted well atm (I'm changing this, so i dont care if people know).

While Kink and I can fight for 10 minutes and either one of us can win, Abid can jump me, throw some debuffs and with 6-7 shots out of that shotty of his, i'm running like heck.

The point is this. There will never be a "be all, end all" profession. I know that these words fall on deaf ears but I say them for the benifit of those who would listen.

I been pvp'n basically since I passed the lvl 75 cap cause I used to hunt in DAV allot, back then 2ho was constantly camped by clan and omni couldnt do much about it.

I've seen soldiers in pure domination of pvp, I've then seen FC realize some mistakes and fix some systems in the game. Most recently evades which was quite literally a waste of IP before this patch came.

I tested some things with Fiix and Gellanna in the arena last night. MKIV grid armor is not what ppl think it is. Gellanna was able to evade me 50% of the time last night and my atk rating was REALLY REALLY high.

ANYONE can be uber in 1 or two catigory. Soldier, crat, agent... It makes absolutely no difference in the end.

Every profession has a specialty. In the case of soldiers, their specialty is in direct non mobile combat. Face to face, head to head, pound for pound, you wont beat a soldier setup properly unless you can out heal their damage output by yourself. That leaves VERY few professions who would give them trouble head to head. Doctors specialty is long term combat with low damage output and the abillity to massively slow your abillity to debuff and damage them. Traders are best at sucking the life and damage outa anyone, again for the "long term combat." Agents adapt. MP's are the destroyers of nano skills.

The list goes on....

If your looking to be the uber pvp profession, you got the wrong game because I don't think it will ever be possible and I'm happy that FC has made it so. I always want to fear someone and I want also to know that I'll likely win vs. certain others.

n.

A state of equilibrium or parity characterized by cancellation of all forces by equal opposing forces.
The power or means to decide.

A state of bodily equilibrium: thrown off balance by a gust of wind.
The ability to maintain bodily equilibrium: Gymnasts must have good balance.
A stable mental or psychological state; emotional stability.
A harmonious or satisfying arrangement or proportion of parts or elements, as in a design. See Synonyms at proportion.
An influence or force tending to produce equilibrium; counterpoise.
The difference in magnitude between opposing forces or influences.
Accounting.
Equality of totals in the debit and credit sides of an account.
The difference between such totals, either on the credit or the debit side.
Something that is left over; a remainder.
Chemistry. Equality of mass and net electric charge of reacting species on each side of an equation.
Mathematics. Equality with respect to the net number of reduced symbolic quantities on each side of an equation.

In one hand a Fixer pretty much owns a crat, in the other hand an MP eats the fixer for breakfast.

In one hand a Doctor may defeat an Agent but in the other hand, the Doctor is owned by the Trader.

In one hand a Soldier will defeat most Enforcers, but in the other hand an Enforcer may defeat a Fixer.

This is my point. All who cry nerf the MP debuff, all who cry nerf grid armor and those who want roots nerfed, RRFE nerfed, etc. These people (imho) need to spend more time pvp'n to see who they can defeat, what odds they can beat and how good their setup vs a nemesis might be.

My argument is that this game already has a certain level of balance. Before 14.4 there were a few primary professions for pvp but now, there are many.

Changes being implemented by FC are positively effecting the pvp community because they are giving us options and before we didn't have many.

Ever hear the term "every dog has it's day?"

I wonder how many people viewed fixers as an easy kill before 14.4 and further I wonder how many of those people are the ones crying for nerfs.

I wonder, how many people used to consider adventurers cannon fodder and I further wonder how many of those people now cry for Adv nerfs.

These people do not seek balance, they seek equality. Nothing is equal in any pvp unless you play FPS Genre games.

I view someone like myself who build a pretty much (by description) non pvp profession as someone trying to defeat the odds and win anyway. Someone who builds a crat for pvp would be viewed the same way imho.

Just my opinion.
