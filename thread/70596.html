Disco, I imagine the probs you're having are due to some problem in the drivers. If you've gone through several driver updates and the problem persists, you may want to make as detailed as possible a bug report and send it to funcom and to the vidcard manufacturer. It may be that the manufacturer is unaware of that problem; so, if you and your friends, along w/someone at fc, reports the bug, they'll probably fix it.

EDIT: Ah, I just remembered... make sure you have the most recent version of DirectX and that Winblowz is fully updated. If that doesn't improve stability, I read somewhere - maybe on this forum, if not, then on VNboards for sure, that Microslop has a special version of DirectX, (8.2, I believe), that is especially for those who are having probs w/online games.
