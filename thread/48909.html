Rubika Taxi Service
Would it not be cool if you had a flying craft that could carry other players?

this could be used as a way to get your team to that far away dungeon or getting your crew, collected, on the battlefield. You could even set up a taxi service for the yalmless..


I was thinking this would not even be hard to to acomplish as the game stands now..


Here is an idea for a people carrying craft,

One guy is the pilot...he flies the craft just like a yalm.

the passengers sits in the the hull which is entered just like when you enter a mission...get a key and zone into the passenger compartment. The passenger compartment is a kin of limbo on a different server so you do not have to get into a lot of hassle with alligning different connection speeds with that of the pilot. So when the pilot anounces on the craft-chat that the objective has been reached, people zone out...

I know that an egi can do this allready and more with the beaconwarp, but a peoplecarrier would be so much more Sci fi and well...just damn cool, especially if the model was a big bulky design like a dropship or something

still, this would not take away anything from the engis since a craft could never be used indoors where, as I understand it, beaconwarp is mostly used. I for one would buy such a craft even if it cost me 30mil
