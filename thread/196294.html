EVERY single class is and must be soloable. Some not as well as others. Reasons for the difference will vary depending on what *you* mean by BEST. Best does not always equal easy. Best does not always equal cheap. Best does not always equal interesting even....

Is there any profession that hasn't been mentioned in this thread? If there are, those professions probably just haven't gotten around to mentioning it.

Even docs can solo. It's not like they'' die easily. It will just take forever to killa mob.

So... what do *you* mean by best? Just asking people to list their top three won't quite answer the question if we don't know our target audience.

In a sense, as some have already mentioned, pet classes are the best classes to solo with. Why? Cuz they don't have to get in the direct line of fire. They send in their minions to do their work for them. Now... the question remains whether everyone wants to deal with the old/current/future pet-pathing and so forth.

Or do you want to be just you, yourself, and your fists duking it out?

just a few more thoughts on the matter. gl
