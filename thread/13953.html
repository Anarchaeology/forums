Rk1 Clan Guild Recruiting
Hi all,

For many years, I ran a successful guild called Dork Guild in a previous game, UO. It was always a close-knit guild in which everyone knew one another. We took pride in that. We also took pride in not taking guild structure, rules, etc too seriously.

Founded in the summer of 99, Dork Guild moved to AO in August of 2001. We currently have characters ranging from 10 to 120 and make an effort to team together regularly.

For those of you laughing at the guild name, you just don't get it. And for those of you who remain serious, you know you are a dork just like the rest of us, so why not consider joining.


Send a tell to Warboc in game if interested.
