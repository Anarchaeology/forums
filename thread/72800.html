OH MY GAAAAAWWWWD - accounts reactivated
Well, sometimes good things DO happen at the most unexpected times.

It's irony because I had just made a slightly depressed post about how I've given up trying to acquire anything really cool.

As many of you know, I had cancelled my account for a few reasons (not listing them here, as it has no place) and so I've mostly just been kicking around the world for the past couple of weeks, waiting for both of my accounts to run out.

I actually fired the ol' meta back up and was gimping around, etc. One thing I hadn't tried in a while was getting the jack legchopper ring. The MP ring ain't that bad actually. Adds some run speed, adds some hp, adds some sense (good if you use a rifle, which I do) adds some 1h blunt (well that last part is cool if you want to consider using creation weapons), all in all a pretty damn good ring.

My guild mates and I were all camped at the various jack locations.

Now here's the funny part. I almost never loot when I have another purpose (such as getting jack's ring), but SOMEthing told me to check a soul swapper MOB I had just killed. Instruction disc on it, I pop it in my inv without checking, thinking it might be something to sell to the shop for a couple bucks.

then the message: thedeacon has looted SUMMON GRID ARMOR MKIV comes up in big pink letters!!!!!

I remember the first thing I said was "lol". For an instant, I didn't think it was real...And then it sunk in..I had just looted GA4!!!

O M G

I would like to give a HUGE hugglez to my guildmates, the wasteprowlers of Rubi KA for coming out to help me with Jack!!!

O M G

Before I kinda gave up on Rubi Ka, I had rolled a cute little fixer girl with an amazing rack and sexier feet than Zylina. I parked her in west athen wearing a thong and went on the hunt for grid armor MK frickin ANYTHING. Now I'm a lazy man, this I will admit. My attention span is short and my tenacity is even shorter. I gave up rather quickly, I will admit. I absolutely LOVE fixers and me being somewhat of an obssessive/compulsive person, I don't think I could stand playing one without having EVERY fixer item in the game, so this is a gigantic treat for me and expect to see a brand new smurf soon!

Yes, I know this makes me a wannabe, bandwagoner, Ph1xX0r smurf, whatever type person, and yes I know certain classes will own me like a prison lover, but this little stroke of luck is like a godsend to me and will keep my ugly face in this game for a while longer ))

Oh btw, if you're wondering what happened with Jack, the first one dropped the doc part. After I went and saved exactly 126 times in varmint woods and newland, I went back out and we killed another jack...I won't say what part it dropped, but ask to see my MP profession ring sometime.

For once, for ONCE, something GOOD has happened and yes I'm a bright and cheery sunshiny dork who is rambling far longer than I should be.
