Don't show colors/levels
Here's a thought. Use a neutral color for ALL players instead of grey, green...red. And, don't show what your level is on the profile unless you want it shown.

There are ways of finding out these things with gadgets ingame. This will make PvP more interesting. This will also prevent someone from looking at a person's color and ganking just the weaker characters.

Heh. Wow, Psyphen, you sure are in a reform mood lately.

I have to agree with you here, too. (Not that that's a bad thing.) I'm tired of being obsessed with my level. It shouldn't matter as much as it does; and maybe it wouldn't if I didn't feel so self-conscious about the entire curious population of Rubi-Ka checking my level all of the time. (I'm assuming that I'm not the only person who does this.) I wish that level wasn't displayed in our character information pop-ups (or at least when we clicked on characters besides ours); in the real world we don't go walking around with our IQs posted on stickies on our foreheads, and I don't enjoy the similar feeling that being in Rubi-Ka gives me. Our profession titles would be enough of a level indicator, in my opinion.

Or perhaps they could tie the concealment and perception skills to character information, ie a higher concealment and people will see less about your character and a higher perception and you will see more about theirs. (Asheron's Call did that, I think, in some fashion.)

I like your posts. I'll try to remember to keep them bumped, although I'm sure that I won't need to.
