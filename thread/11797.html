Ok I have done quite a lot of bow testing on my MA on Test. I'll answer some questions. First note that I cannot make 'all' arrow types as they can only be made in a certain QL range (for example cold arrows are QL80-120 I believe) and I don't have the ability to make some of the higher QL arrows. Also they are pretty buggy right now.. but should by all means work bye release day.

1) Have i wasted IP on Bow Spec. Att?

No.. although the skill req has been removed from all bows (well one or two have them still, but thats a bug) special arrows require the skill. The higher your skill the higher QL spec. arrow you an use as well as what type you can use... apple blossom, poison, cold, fire..



2) Does this mean that all arrow based ammo requires special
attack skill, or only certain kinds?

Only the new special arrows require the skill. Regular arrows work as they always did.



3)For the ammo that uses special attack are there different ql's based on skill lvl?

Yes.. and no. I've seen many people say spec arrows cost 35k per bundle and require hefty skill to use. Well those people appear to have only spent about 10 minutes investigating the new arrows because 90% of the new arrows must be made. They come in 2 parts (the shafts and the heads) and must be combined. The QL of the shafts deturmines what requirement in Bow Spec. Att and the heads must match or beat the shaft QL. Note that making arrows requires Agility and some Intelligence (so you need not pay thousands of credits to an engi or trader to get them made



4)What type of damage output does Special attack
do?

There are 4 or 5 types of arrows. Chem, Poison, Fire, Cold (and one other I believe.. cant remember now.) Each type requires a certain base skill lvl in Bow Spec. to use. Although not all are working properly we believe that each type does a special effect. Each type also varries in dmg. I do know that Poison arrows of about QL45 do about 350 dmg (the dmg is static, no min-max) and I have heard Fire arrows do the most damage, although my Agility and Int are to low to make them myself.



5)What is the refresh rate of special attack like?

Each type has a set frefresh rate. The lowest QL ones (apple blossom I think they are called) have a 2 minute refresh. Poison Bamboo has a 4 minute refresh. Unfortunately do to bugs these are the only two types of arrows I've been able to test so I don't know the refresh on any of the others.

6)For current crossbow users (Urban Junior / Clan Patriot)
Would you recommend saving some IP or start investing
in Special attack skill?

I myself have not tested any crossbows so I couldn't tell you just yet. I'll try to find an answer for you. I do highly suspect that spec. arrows will become important to melee users in PvP situations (being able to inflict good dmg while rooted) so it may be a good idea to spend a little IP if you use a bow at all. Remember, the new arrows are optional and the Spec. Att. req has been removed from all bows so if you don't want to use that IP but still use bows.. bye all means go ahead



7) Are these arrows for bows that only use arrows or any bows?

I have not tested any energy bows but if they currently have Bow Spec. Att. requirements they can probably use spec. arrows when 13.8 goes live.



8) How do you use say the special arrows over normal ammo in your pack?

When you purchase or make the arrows (oh and bye the way QL60 poison arrows cost roughly 2k per 20 pack to make) they create there own icon in your inventory. To use a spec arrow simply click on a target and right click on the spec. arrows. You can also put them in your hotbar and press the coresponding # key or right click them in there. Also for any Fixers reading this, the new Area Burst bullets work the exact same way.



9) Clip size and arrow cost is a downer...

That it is However I don't think bows were intended as a primary weapon, and with the new spec bows I think most people will switch out to a bow to use there spec. arrows. then switch back to primary weapons... like when rooted in PvP.



10) As an adv, what kind of bow selection should I look into most of the time?

Um.. I believe Reet Techs use fling.. not sure. Kevlar Bows have a fairly low Aimed shot req. but that's there only special. The only bow I know of with burst also has every other special under the sun lol. If you don't mind using a bow with no specials (save the new arrows) I suggest an OT Limp Prototype IV. They aren't very common but are the only bow I know of that only requires Bow.

11) How does using these arrows affect your regular attack?

They have no effect on regular bow attacks. The recharge timer is completely independant.



12) As for the price of arrows, that will be fixed?

As of 13.81 on test.. normal arrows cost the same as ever.

Hope this answers everyones questions. I'll do more testing as soon as test comes back up (hopefully some of the fixes are to the new arrows.) As to the energy bows and crossbows.. I'd have to say if it requires Bow Special Attack on live right now.. it should be able to use the new arrows.. if not.. chances are it won't.
