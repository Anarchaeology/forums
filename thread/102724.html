OOC - Big Thanks
Hey Everyone,

I just wanted to drop in and give you guys a big thanks.

The ARK events crew and Funcom want to throw out a big thanks to those of you that show maturity and patience in events. Today I witnessed a line of people waiting for a 'reward' in an event and I was incredibly impressed by the order shown by most involved. Seeing this type of thing is what keeps the team moving and believe me they appreciate it.

The producer of the most recent "Shade" event wanted me to express a personal thanks to everyone there and a thanks from the team as well. They work hard to do what they can for you, and it really makes them want to keep going when people show understanding and continued support.

So.....

THANKS!
