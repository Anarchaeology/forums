"Third Party Tools" article: clear as mud
As of this afternoon, there's a posting on the launcher web page that says the following:

quote:
Third party tools, (14.05.02)

We get a lot of questions about 3rd party tools, how we feel about them, and what our official stance towards them is. Well here it is: Funcom does not condone the use of 3rd party tools. We know that there are 3rd party tools out there that players use on a regular basis and that these tools help players a lot, but...

We can, and will, make changes to the game that might affect how these tools work, and if they work at all. Although going after these tools isn't a high priority for us at this time, we won't consider the functionality of the tools when we alter the game.

Please make sure you read point 9 at http://www.anarchy-online.com/conten...fconduct.html, which says: "You will not attempt to interfere with, hack into, or decipher any transmissions to, or from, the servers running Anarchy. You are however allowed to use status pages provided by Funcom." and point 9 in the EULA, which states: "You may not use any third party software to modify this Software to change game-play" and that you are aware of the fact that you might risk getting banned from the game by using some of these tools.
Clear as mud. And now you're threatening banning, without making it clear what you consider to be the meanings of "modify" or "change game-play"? WTF?

It was a Funcom employee who asked me, under test 14.0, to see if AOMD 2.0 with auto-search would break the servers. Now ClickSaver has the same feature, but does it in a way that doesn't require packet-sniffing. It does, however, require patching the copy (in memory, not on disk) of one of the game's DLLs.

Does making it possible to get valuable rewards "change game-play"? Yes -- and no. Yes, because otherwise, you'd go stark raving mad trying to get a useful reward out of a mission terminal. No, because if you were willing to go stark raving mad, you could do it.

So, are you going to ban people for using ClickSaver? Or not? Or are you just making vague threats, without any intention of enforcing them, in hopes of cutting down usage by the timid?

How about skins? There are now at least two skins that replace the map with actual useful maps. Do these modify AO? Not the copy on disk, but they do (through a now-approved method) change what actually runs. Do they change game-play? Absolutely. They make it possible to tell in advance whether or not you can safely make it to a mission, and give all kinds of free hints in-game as to how to get there. And no, because in theory, you could get the same information on the chat channels by asking around.

Your clarification doesn't clarify anything. Instead, it muddies the waters. It then raises the stakes by threatening banning. I would appreciate it, very much, if you would either clarify these issues once and for all ... or drop the threat. Please. Thank you.
