I recently started a little alt for just the reason of playing in the subway

It is not intuitive how to go about finding a team there tho...one method i used was I did a /tell helpbot level (my level) to find out what level I could team with. Then I did /list 7 12 (if i could team with 7-12) and proceeded to send tells to anyone in my level range in the subway to see if they needed someone for their team.

I found quite a few teams that way, not full ones mind you, but 2-4 people generally and we had a blast.

Other than that, Fair trade is usually a great place to find a team. Once you get a few going, you will start to meet more people that you can just send tells to as well
