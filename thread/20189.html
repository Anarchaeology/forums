SPARTA
Omni-Tek Department S1

Mission statement:
To ensure the security of the Omni-Tek Corporation of Rubi-Ka against all threats, internal or external.

Who are we?:
Department S1 (SPARTA) is unaligned to the company's core branches, instead serving, supporting and enforcing the direct orders of the CEO's office with unquestioning loyalty.

The department's core officers began arriving on-world 8th September 29475 carrying orders from the Omni-Tek Corporation on Omni Prime to form Department S1 and undertake the above mission.

In February 29476 SPARTA became one of the founding members of the Omni-Tek Security Council (OTSEC).

How do you fit in?:
We are always open to all professions, and your potential is more important to us than your previous experience.

Recruitment can start as early as security clearance level 15, though we pride ourselves in only accepting the very best people, the very best Omni-Tek employees as candidates for interview.

So if you believe you fit this description, if you believe you have something to give back to the corporation above and beyond what the average company employee does, if the below organisation content interests you...

  • Teamwork
  • Organisation
  • Co-operation
  • Political Credibility

...then get in contact.

Project Revenant RK-2
Due to a lack of official events on RK2, a group of players, several from Day 1 of release on June 27, 2001, have banded together to create player run events on RK2. The sum of this idea has been named Project Revenant.

As with the official story line, Project Revenant encompasses both an Omni Organization and a Clan guild. The Omni faction is known as Division 3: Omni Reclamation. The Clan Guild is known as Vox Populi (Voice of the People). At this time, there is no Neutral faction in Project Revenant. However, with enough interest and player support, one can be added sometime in the future.

Our first goal is to recruit enough members to make events have importance. Whether you choose to play Omni or Clan, it doesn't matter. What matter is your support and willingness to make RK2 as good as it can be.

If interested check out our website www.projectrevenant.com
