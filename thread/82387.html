Kyrstina's Story - Prologue

December 17, 29476

Today starts a new beginning, or at least I hope so. I finally decided to move into one of the cities, Newland. I have wandered the forests and deserts for so long, it was quite a shock to actually claim a city bustling with people and buildings as my home. It is to be expected though. One cannot truly feel safe, not in this day and age anyway.

As I was handed the keys, and actually set foot in my new place, I had to stop and think of how much things have changed, how I have changed... and grown too. My life has not been the easiest, although I'm sure there are others who have gone through worse. This war has left many people I've met scarred to the point of oblivion. I remember walking into the city for the first time, and noticing a man sitting on the floor, mumbling and sobbing about his loss of his clan, as they were recently slaughtered in some sort of raid on his complex. At least, that is my guess anyway, since I couldn't get a word out of him. It's times like this when I have to think back on my own life, and be grateful I am still here, still living, and still moving on.

I remember my family, and how they would probably laugh if they saw me now. This is not the place they would've imagined their daughter living in, and probably not the way they would see their daughter living either. My life has been so full of things that have turned out the way they shouldn't have, and I certainly have been no exception to this.

We lived out in West Stret Bank, in the woods, earning our living through hunting the wild creatures here and selling them in the city, at least that is what my dad would do. I never went with him, as I was too young and I had to stay behind and tend to the house with my mother and brother. He would constantly tell us of the wonders in Omni-1 and how glad he was to be supporting the cause.

My brother, on the other hand, had a quite different set of views. He would keep them quiet, and would nod in agreement with my father, but I knew better. I had seen him sneak off at night heading for a Clan settlement to the north. I knew he thought he was working for the freedom of Rubi-ka. I knew he thought my father was a fool for believing Omni-Tek was a blessing for us all... and I knew sooner or later, that both of them would end up in confrontation.

It took longer than I thought. I think it was because my dad was in denial. He wanted to believe that his son thought as he did, that he would one day grow up to be just like his old man. Even I was surprised, though, when my brother stood up and announced that he would be moving up north to Tir to help the rebel clans. I had never seen my father so furious. They fought for most of the night. Even though I ran to my room and tried to sleep, you could hear them throughout the house, probably through the whole bank. I remember the slam on the door, and the most intense silence I've ever encountered.

From then, I guess it was only a matter of time. Father left for Omni-1 to enlist and to fight for what he believed in, the same day my brother was part of a huge raid on an Omni encampment. Mother and I stayed behind, and tried to tend to the daily chores, hoping that one day, they would both return to us.

Things were to turn out much differently.

I'll never forget that day. The day I should've been there. The day the raid happened near our house. I remember the smell of blaster fire everywhere. Bodies laid strewn around. This hadn't been a normal battle. Someone had used something they shouldn't have. I wouldn't be going to Borealis to meet my family at the reclaim terminal. None of these people would be.

It was kind of ironic... the whole thing. My father and brother had been at odds for some time, over a great many things. It seems almost appropriate now that they were both there. What I did find was quite interesting. To this day, I don't know who was responcible for so many permanent deaths, but it seems both my father and brother forgot who they were fighting for, and rushed to protect my mother, an innocent civilian that just happened to be in the wrong place at the wrong time.

"I should have been there. I should've been at my family's side." I remember thinking this then.

From there, I headed out into the forest, and there I have stayed, wandering the lands, trying to find a reason to it all. Is there even a reason at all? But, as I look back, I do take a lesson from that day, as I have taken lessons from so many I've met and so much I've seen and done. Even in the bitterest of conflicts, both my brother and my father were able to put aside their differences to fight side by side for what they believed is right. For that moment, the two who has always been at war... found peace.

I may not be successful. I may be shunned, laughed at, or even possibly killed, but even as they did, I must fight for what I believe is right. I must go where my heart tells me. I must try to find a way to stop the violence, to stop the hatred. There has got to be a way, and damn it, I plan to find it.

And now, I shall save this entry, step out the door, and begin my next journey. Time may not be on my side, but I must be successful. I have to be. There is no other way.
