This is a subject which comes up time and again, but to be frank I've yet to se a 100% satisfactory solution to the problem. Currently we have /team loot alpha, but that only works on mobs. Bosses I believe can only be looted by the team leader, which means that you have to rely on the team leader playing fair. If bosses were included in alpha, I think we'd see a lot more teams splintering, as people attempted to make sure that they were next in the loot queue when the boss came up. It wouldn't always work, but that wouldn't stop people trying.

Then there's the problem of ninja looters and chests. Now that rollerats in traps actually aggro, ninja looters have to be a lot more careful. /team chestloot alpha would sort out the rest of this problem, if it was set up as a separate command to /team loot. However, you run the risk of ninjas simply resigning from the team in order to ninja loot, if that's all they're interested in or the boss has already been dealt with. Still better than the current situation, but there's no 100% fix.

The solution you suggested, all loot going into a pool, the pick in alphabetical order post-mission, has its own problems. For a start, what's to stop the team leader from kicking the rest of the team and keeping the loot for himself? You might suggest that the loot pool remembers who was in the team when the loot was taken, but that gets more tricky on the database side. You also have the risk of wierd situations, such as someone refusing to take their pick as a form of protest, to prevent the other team members from getting their share of the loot.

My point is that no system of loot division is perfect, although I do agree that the current system could use some improvement. Nothing will protect you 100% from unfair loot division. Ultimately, Wal's right: your only real protection is to team up with people you know and trust.
