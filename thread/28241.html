my suggestion
Well, I think my suggestion for adding an AI Level to the monsters and an AI Suppression Level to the zones would make the game a lot more interesting and still very playable for all level characters. And as a programmer I don't see this as a very difficult patch to the game. Wouldn't it be cool if about once every day or two one city somewhere in Rubi-Ka was suddenly attacked by a mob of monsters? It would hit the emergency news chat channel so everyone would know about it. And then there's the time you're roaming the desert for rats and come across one with armor and a chainsaw in his hands, and before he attacks you he whistles to all his friends to come join him. That would make a fun game.
