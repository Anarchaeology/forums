PvP update
Well PvP the big question on everyones mind it seems. Well I just talked with Gaute Godager and here is what he had to say.

When AO goes live we will have a few new things to do with PvP.

First: A wider range of PvP level. (It will be lowered from 100 to 75.

Second: PvP Rating (More to come on this as, when I can actually understand Programmers)

Third: PvP Title (This will be displayed over the player)

Fourth: You will be able to loot all the items on a player that they have gained since their last save.
