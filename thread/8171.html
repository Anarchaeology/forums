Funcom, SAY NO TO CAMPING
As I have posted elsewhere, there is the problem of camping that raises its ugly head on AO. All the other games on the market that I know of, EQ, AC, DaoC have understood that camping is bad. There is no reason for high lvl character to keep on killing a grey mob for hours on end to get that ubr l00t. (spectre of my lower guk archmage 30 hours+ straight camping spree still haunts me.)
Let the folks that are in the range lvl kill it. Besides, instead of putting a k00l items on some multi-hour spawns instead fix the damn trade skills. This is not a fantasy game where u cannot make squat and got to kill that mob to take that cool item. AO is in the future where folks can make stuff? Right? So let them make stuff! I know times are tough and budgets are fixed but damn.....At least mirror the server that runs the dungeon. There are got to be some folks that can put together some load balancer for your servers if the server space is tough.
