I for one don't even remember the last time I got healed by a doc who isn't in my team. It's a exceptional case which doesn't need to be fixed IMO.

And if you really want changes, than at least propose a solution, instead of repeating the issue over and over. But that's hard, because there's no solution which doesn't imply another problem. For example, IF they allow level 200 docs to heal level 120 people, then a bunch of level 120's can attack a base, get heals from high level docs, and the level 120 opposition can't do anything against that ?

This isn't a doc issue, every single prof suffers a reduced range of people they can help by buffs or simply attack after level 150.
