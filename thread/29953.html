Price Control in the year 30K !!
After Playing this game and seriously getting into some trading ( which for me is the fun part of this game) I can only think that a main problem with costs of things is that alot of players (newbies) really have now idea what the current price of there item is worth. Sure they could do a quick shop run and see what it goes for, but that can get to be a hassle if you like to trade in alot of stuff.

I propose that all items come with 2 prices built in to the description, one that shows the current price that you would expect to pay at a shop, and another price that shows what the shop will give you. That way a newbie can just look at an item and have an instant reference and then set prices accordingly. Sure certain items like grid arm. are gonna be inflated because of supply and demand ... but at least a newbie can have a an instant reference of the current market prices, and laugh or beg accordingly. I have seen too many newbies sell thing at too low a price and also laugh at other players asking high prices (in the millions) for other items.

Whether it makes alot of difference to experienced players is not the point; isnt it the year 30k? If I can grid around as a triangle and teleport instantly to different cities (and the rest), surely I can have the same satellite that updates my minimap, to update current average prices for my items. Dont we do it today with commercials?
