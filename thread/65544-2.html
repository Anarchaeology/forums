Gentlemen TMS is a % - based nano. The amount of damage reduced is directly proportional to the damage done to the TMS'd soldier. Fact.

Now if TMS lasted indefinately, then longer fights would make no difference whatsoever on the capability of the soldier. However, given that TMS is limited in duration, it only reduces damage over that period of time. Thus: The more damage done within the duration of TMS, the more damage overall the nano is able to reflect. Fact.

Honestly, soldiers were never that uber to begin with. Only in 2h0 were they so efficient, due to the easy zoning which is the only tactic FC has seen fit to give soldiers. TMS, alpha, keep shooting and try for insta-kill, zone if not. That's it. On any other playfield soldiers have never been 'uber'. Just about any profession with roots could polish them off.

However, now it is a different story. Soldiers will still be able to zone in 2h0, but there is slim-to-none chance that they will be able to kill anything. Basically they are going to be running around shooting people and then running away - more like a pesky gnat than anything else. On other playfields? Haha. If there is no zone around soldiers are dead meat to any otyher proffession you care to name.
