Mob Condition Icons
I posted this a while ago on the test server forum, but since we had a suggestions forum now i think this would be a better place for it:

As a crat my biggest problem is trying to mezz in extremly busy areas in missions when there is like 4 mobs and 4mob pets and all the pets and mobs have the same name and are crammed into a small room.

Trying to target and mezz adds is almost impossible because you can't tell which mob you have targeted or if you have calmed that mob yet or not. When you calm/snare/root a mob a small icon should appear beside the mob's health bar to signify if the mob is calmed, rooted or snared or a combination of the three.
It could be a simple square icon; blue for root, green for snare and yellow for calm.

Bump to this, I often serve as the primary calmer in many missions and as such I often run into the problem of recasting my mezzes on allready mezzed targets. Primarily this is a problem in the first room of the mission where mobs have a nasty tendancy to stand in the doorway right on top of each other, in which case mezzing the adds without recasting on already mezzed mobs (and 9 out of ten times getting a healthy dose of aggro) becomes rather difficult. The situation gets even worse when there are two calmers on a mission team as most times mezzing become a game of aggro tag as Calmer #1 breaks Calmer #2's mezz. Status icons would, for the most part, eliminate this problem and make life that much easier for all us Traders/'Crats/NT's .
