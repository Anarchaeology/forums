Fixercon PVP Tournament Rules/Signup
Here are the proposed rules for the Fixercon I PVP tournament, which will begin at 20GMT, on Friday, July 13, at an undisclosed area, 1 hour after Fixercon begins. Engineers will be available to warp you back to the Con.
Please reply to this thread to sign up for the tournament or send me a private message, or an email. (we will be taking registrations up to 19:30 GMT, Saturday, July 13. Also, please reply here with any comments/suggestions on these rules.

As it stands now, the prize for the tournament will be 20 million credits (10 to each team member.)

I'll be posting specifics on how to get to the Con, and the Playa of the Year contest within a day or 2.


PVP Tournament Rules
First Draft:

 Paired Team Tournament

 The team with the last one standing wins. (it doesnt matter if one of the teammates dies).
 Single elimination.
 Individuals are responsible for finding their own partner and submitting their team before the event starts.
 Teams must consist of two players that are at least 25 levels apart.
 Everyone needs to be at least 75 to participate.
 Immediate disqualification for healing from anyone outside of your team or if any member of a team grid evacs.
 If the highest person of team A is a lower level than the lower member of team B, then team A gets 10 seconds of attack time without team B attacking back.
 If the highest person of team A is higher than the lower person of team B then a referee will use the 10 second countdown script to initiate the fight for both teams.
 A person 50 levels over the highest person of a team needs to fight the other pair by himself/herself.
