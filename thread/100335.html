hehe i was curious so. . .

ql200 WWK - 30% crit chance, 1020 AR - 20,985dmg/30 secs

bear claw + 15% LLTS (-800 inits) - 45% crit chance, 1020 AR - 19,740dmg/30 secs

fists with 30% crit - 19,950/30 secs

fists with 45% crit - 25,080/30 secs


so the 2 weaps do about the same dmg, the only difference would be the enhanced crit chance with your fists with the LLTS would make it more damaging. of course with the high init penalty you would have to stay near the 75%-87.5% area depending on your init, whereas the WWK can be wielded at around 37.5%-50%.

of course, you can equip a scope with a WWK as well I figure you can get on a 10% scope (-500 inits) and hang around the same % aggro as the bear claw, so.. .

WWK w/10% scope = 24,075/30 secs

Fists w/10% scope = 23,370/30 secs

so WWK remains the most damaging under the right circumstances, and also performs very well at the lower agg settings sans scope
