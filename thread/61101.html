Grid Exit Beacon
How about a device for Fixers that you can plant anywhere, acting as an exit point from the grid? It could be one use only, or perhaps something you can pick up and use again. Hell, would be funny if someone else could pick it up and move it (after a search finds it and an appropriatte skill is high enough in comparison to the quality lvl of device or the Fixer who planted it).

Just an idea, but I think it would be pretty cool.
