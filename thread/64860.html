The Illuminati: Now starting back up recruitment.

If the following describes you, and you are looking to join a well established, active organisation consider us.

1) Level 100 or higher.
2) Active player
3) There to help out when you can, as well as be helped.
4) Don't act like your 12 (we are looking for people that will be mature, while still having a great time. If you have no manners, or just want things your way, please don't waste your time).

We usually do about 1 raid on a various Dungeon or Camp per week, as well as help with getting various quests done (Alvins etc). Org teams are one thing that we do quite often, we have 100+ members so teaming is rather easy. One semi-unique feature that The Illuminati offers is that we have our own bot (illumibot) which has many features such as a list of all members online, a walkthough of all quests, an items database, and many many more commands. As for what we stand for the main thing is to help out your fellow Illuminati. It's all mutual, we love helping when and where we can, and the same would be asked. Our webpage will be up shortly, until then you can check out our stats on the AO Page Click Here. If you like what you see feel free to /tell fretter



edit: One more thing, our main member base is mostly from the United States, and Europe. We do have members from all over the world, and usually always have a few members online during the hours that our main group isn't online, just wanted to clear up any confusion on that.
