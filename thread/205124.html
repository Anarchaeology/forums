Ah yes, I remember the first time I encountered the Nevver...

There I was, a fledgling shade, revelling in my first tentative steps outside of Jobe research. I was running around, eager to explore the fractured landscape of Nascence, when I found myself plunged into a great darkness, a huge shadow. And at the center of that shadow, a lady of truly epic proportions, regally surveying the landscape...

To this day, I don't know which cast a greater shadow, her ego or her, erm, "formidable armaments". Either way, for just a moment, I was tempted to send my resume to OT. She is one truly insidiously influential person....

Omni-Tek is a great company, but that company has threats from a far. We need to unite and maintain Rubi-Ka as one world, and become allies.
