Computer Gaming World reviews Notum Wars
That's right kids, got my issue in the mail today and our first huge-ass media outlet has reviewed our beloved booster pack... with good to great results. I shall do the honor of transcribing the article

"A few months ago, to the dismay of many Anarchy Online subscribers, Funcom announced the delay of the Shadowlands expansion. Instead, they'd be releasing The Notum Wars, a booster pack add-on. What was that? Who would want to pay for a mini-update? The answer came shortly after installing Notum Wars - any AO player would want this. More than mini-update, it's a major change to the way Anarchy Online is played.

Notum Wars' biggest innovation is land control for organizations. The big advantage to having land control towers is the stat bonuses you gain: organization-wide bonuses for all your org's towers, individual bonuses for any towers you own personally, and experience point bonuses based on the percentage of land held by your faction. So when I say there's a major changed int the way Anarchy Online is played, I'm talking about the promotion of teamwork, I'm talking about fighting for a common cause, I'm talking about all-out mass player-versus-player battles you'll only see in the likes of WWII Online. In the early morning hours on the first day of release, the massive pitched battles of the Noutm Wars began and Anarchy Online has been different ever since.

Land control isn't the only addition. Funcom has added new ground-based fighting vehicles, as well as visual enhancements like starships taking off and landing on the planet's surface, and new weather effects. They've also rebuilt the character creation system and NPC chat interface, making it easy for anyone to join the MMORPG world. Notum Wars has renewed Anarchy Online with added play mechanic's just don't have. If you haven't given this game a try, now's your chance. There's a free seven-day trial available online at www.anarchy-online.com

Verdict: 4 stars - Enhanced, updated, and all-around fantastic."

I think this is a great boost to AO... compared to the 1 and 2 star ratings it got from many magazines at launch, this is a welcome improvement. Doubtless we'll see a boost in the player base from this.

edit: This is the March issue so non-subscribers won't see it on the shelves for a number of weeks.
