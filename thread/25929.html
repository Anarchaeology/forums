MMD
From level 50-70 or so I missioned from Camelot to MMD almost exclusively. You get good money missions this way. Just like Nightstalker said you have two options.

1. Run from save terminal (and it is very wise to save with this option) to Grid when leaving. I have been ganked a number of times but more often I get through.

2. Take the safe way, run around the big rock to the gap between them. From this location you can get to mission before PvP timer is off, or to grid. When exiting mission go right following rock until you hit the same location and it becomes 75% again, then go for grid entrance.

Warning, stopping to smell the daisies usually ends with pushing them up.
