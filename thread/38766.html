Ok, 3 times the chest level is the bare minimum skill needed. Less than that and you will never disarm the trap. Chests can be higher (and lower) ql than mission ql.

I recommend that you try some very very low ql missions to see if you can disarm those chests. If you can't, then something is wrong about what you are doing (hold tool in mouse cursor, right click on chest).

I have 560 trap disarm at level 95 after all modifiers (3 clusters, ICC cloak, pickafinger, fixer buff). I can disarm any chest with a few tries (in ql 171 missions). There is no way I can detect these same traps since my perception doesn't cut it.
