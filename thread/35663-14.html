Subpar, take a good long look at Fixer nano skill costs, and the reqs to use Fixer nanos. Take a long look.

The NCUHI was implemented because our nanos were horribly overcosted and our skills horribly priced. It was one of our biggest problems pre 14.4, if not THE biggest.

Nobody spent more IP to use nanos than a Fixer. Nobody..even those classes with blue skills only had one or two lines to keep up..we have to use all SIX. And return on IP investment in Fixer nanos (prior to 14.4) was generally poor, further aggravating the issue.

Even with the NCUHI, ALL OF OUR SKILLS ARE DARK BLUE..that means that if you subtract the .3/skill/level bonus the NCUHI gives a Fixer, you're still looking at a minimum adjusted 10-11 ip base cost on every nano skill.

What it means for a fixer is that we can save a bit of IP, and be able to cast nanos only ten levels below us, not twenty.

I'm fine with the fact that they didn't cost the upgrades to the NCUHI because, well, Fixers already pay through the nose for GA, and this was not an OPTIONAL fix, but a needed, necessary, core upgrade to Fixers.

The other solution would have been to muck around with the core character code and reduce the IP cost of Fixer nano skills directly, something they didn't want to risk.
