Trybes' bio
((OCC: This is basically a summary of a story that I was writing a few years ago when AO was announced and after I started playing it. Never did finish it. ))

Full name: Cedrick Trybes Clyburn

Age: 36

Currently employer: Devils Advocate

Brief History:
Was born and raised near the small farming community of Terra north of the Clon**** Mining Facility, father raised both him and his younger brother, Gherritt, to be skilled in various forms of martial arts and healing skills; their father had forbidden them from every firing a gun. It was there in Terra where Trybes would meet a young Omni Bureaucrat that would forever change his life. Gavisdin Denmont was a low-level intelligence officer for Omni-Teks mining operation on Rubi-Ka; on a business trip to Terra Gavisdin meet with both Trybes and his brother about a possible future within the company. Gherritt quickly joined up with the Omni-Med branch of the company, under the watchful eye of Gavisdin, while Trybes stayed home to better hone his skills.

Three years had passed since his brother leaving and Trybes too felt a hunger to escape from the mundane life of the farm. Trybes moved to Omni-Trade and enlisted with Omni-Pol; after four grueling months of training Trybes was released. He was told by his instructors that he was not fit to be a member of the Omni-Pol family. Facing the aspect of having to go back to the farm Trybes was once again approached by Gavisdin.

Gavisdin had started his own smaller organization named the Intelligent Few; a small group of agents and informers that bought and sold intelligence data to any willing party. Gavisdin was in need of field agents working in clan territories, he promised to pay Trybes six million credits plus labor and board to work for him. In exchange Gavisdin would be ale to get him onto the Omni-Pol roster. Trybes excepted and spent the next ten years acquiring tower and guard placements, troop movement, and political power shifts.

Trybes spent the years constantly watching his back, making only a few friends and allies. Fighting Omni only when he needed to protect his own agendas. During those years Trybes met a solitus woman, her name Montyne Reynolds, she was also a spy sent to Tir under the direction of Gavisdin. The two spent most of their free time together and soon became almost inseparable. But lucks fickle fate was not a kind one. Montyne would vanish, leaving Trybes alone once again.

With his tenure in Tir was over and Trybes returned to Omni. Gavisdin had been true to his word, upon his return Trybes once again enlisted into Omni-Pol and successfully made it into the investigation bureau . His time in Omni-Pol would not last long however, for his past would soon resurface and a name that he had learned to trust would soon become his enemy. Gavisdins group that had run secretly under the nose of Omni-Tek would soon make a fatal flaw. A failed execution in Reets Retreat would leave a victim to name him as his would be killer. Not only Gavisdin, but Trybes brother Gherritt as well.

The investigation into the Intelligent Few would leave twelve OP officers perma-killed; but Gavisdin was caught and brought back to Omni-HQ for sentencing. Trybes had managed to talk officials into letting him be the one to interrogate Gavisdin. In the interrogation Gavisdin gave Trybes an ultimatum, Release me or I will tell Omni-Pol about your joining the Clans.; if Omni-Pol were find out about his true records, Trybes would be imprisoned just as Gavisdin had become. Going against his principles and his duty Trybes help Gavisdin escape from HQ in return for his silence. He helped Gavisdin flee as far as Andromeda, from there he left him on his own; Gavisdin asked Trybes to come with him for one last time.

He refused, asking only that he cut ties with him and his brother. He agreed and headed west. On the way back to Omni a message came across all police frequencies, there was a traitor in the ranks of Omni-Pol. A full manhunt for Trybes was started, Gavisdin had betrayed him and cost him his peaceful life once again.

With Omni-Pol on his trail Trybes hunted down Gavisdin, his hunt would take him to the floating city of Jobe, a place Trybes thought was only a myth. Chasing Gavisdin through the city he would be stopped by Gherritt, his brother was determined to stop him from getting to Gavisdin. The two fought, using all the teachings their father had taught them to destroy the other. In the fight Gherritt fell over the side, catching himself on a support beam, as Trybes reached to help his brother Gherritt let go. He plunged into the clouds below, a grin deeply etched in his battle-scarred face.

Trybes finally found Gavisdin inside on the science labs, he stood in front of a vortex leading to a place Gavisdin kept referring to the Shadowlands; between Gavisdin and Trybes stood Montyne. Gavisdin had used her as a puppet to keep tabs on Trybes and make sure he didnt get too comfortable with the clans. She had never really loved him, it was all a doing of Gavisdins brainwashing. Trybes went for Gavisdin but was stopped by his one time love. As Gavisdin entered the portal to the Shadowlands Montyne brought the shotgun she carried to her own head. Her body fell into the arms of Trybes, her soul scattered by all the equipment used to open the portal, she was gone from his life forever. The man the had ruined his life had gotten away, Omni-Pol had tracked him down. Once again Trybes would have to run north to live with the clans.

Three months passed, Trybes tried to settle back in the north country, taking odd jobs to make ends meet, drinking most of his left over wages away. The clans were not his people, the place wasnt his home. It was only a place for him to escape too. After sobering up he headed south once again to rebuild that wich had been destroyed.
