The now massive problems with cash are actually all due to Funcom forgetting some of their own words. Loooooong time ago, back in beta, when we were all off on other lands battling stupid lil trolls and orcs, when we only had the ideas to go on that what was to come. One line which always took my interest after coming from such a screwed up economy as UO, was a comment about rares, and that they won't effect they economy as they will either be only for the person who found them (no drops) or wont be rare as they will be readily available.

Sooo, why the hell are mega rares like the instruction disc for Nulity Sphere Mk2 (since there is non of them in ready to roll nano form) got a street price now of 200-300 million credits? And its smaller brother, the ql90 Nulity Mk1 selling for 25-35 million?
WHY? its simple, because its lucky if one or two of these drop each month, and Fixers have to suffer this too with their Grid Armour. The way in which these things drop is whats causing the imbalances in cash. On top of this, you also stopped multiple charges on the same nano, so now it will only work once. So the "mere" 5-10 milion credits that was being paid for Nulity Mk1, and the "very small" 50 million for Nulity Mk2 is now made even worse as ppl can't sell on the NF.

It's simple supply and demand guys. Everyone wants these formulas, but you've limited supply so much that the only way to get your hands on them is to pay prices which force others to inflate prices or farm items just to make enough money to buy these things. And its wrong.

As for lack of funds for docs, you what? every doc I know makes more cash than any NT out there, but this isn't getting into Doc bashing it might just be one of those things. Why? all I can really see is a HE for Doc is alot more effective than it is for an NT, and also Docs can make huge sums of cash from there pharma tech skills for making plasma. Meanwhile the NT's use of there pitiful nanopool is a joke, look at a minor example here of a 30secs of fighting from an NT, in this time he can usually use up over 1300 points of his nano pool, yet the "incredibly useless" HE in that same time will have recovered *drum roll* a huge 266 points. Thats less than a 5th of whats used. You can get the idea here an NT runs out of firepower very very quickly, which I am not saying is a necessarily bad thing as with nothing to stop them, the amount of carnage would be great...urm I mean a problem ;P Current HE's are pathetic for an NT (well even when they work which they don't half the time when they suddenly decide to turn themselves off).

Yes the money is recoverable from the missions, but the amount of disposible income is very imbalanced. The simple, logical way I could see a balance on this is different ql of ammo. The usual ql difs would be nice, and work with say a ql150 rifle could only then use ammo which is ql150 or above.
