PvP action below lvl 75
Well heres what i see.

It is a lot cheaper to twink a lvl 15 than it is to twink a lvl 120

This makes the PvP element accessible to a lot more people who dont have lvl 150 + alts to fund their fun. There are a lot of people PvPing in this lvl bracket, and yes, a lot has to do with outside buff begging and ncu capacity.

That is one of the things that make traders so successful as PvPers - as they have superior comp lit buffs - my trader had a ql 160 6 slot belt on at lvl 55 or something stupid

If you want to be a self buffed killing machine you need to sit just on the level cap at lvl 74, where you can enjoy protection from anyone above lvl 96 or so. Go Trader without a doubt, the superior power and ability to make opponents OE with 1 or 2 deprives cannot be touched imo. Having said that, an MA doing fire or radiation damage in combination with a flurry of blows and a crit buff can be equally devastating at times.

At the end of the day, PvP success is directly related to the cash and time you spend fine tuning your character. I tend to take down most opponents in 3 or 4 shots, so an essence isnt compulsory. In fact i dont have the ncu capacity to outside buff, as i dedicate 2 of my belt slots to a nano recompiler and a deck range modifier.
