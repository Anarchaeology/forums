A way to increase the player base
Folks,

I am trying oh SO hard to return to AO after a break of some 6 months, but there appears to be a conspiracy to prevent it!

I'm not going to write a novel about the lengths I have gone to, and problems I have encountered, in an effort to get the game re-installed (on the machine I used to play it on), but I have just one, constructive, suggestion:

Somebody at FunCom, PLEASE grab a machine which does not have AO installed on it and try to install it from scratch. Fix all the problems you find, and make sure they stay fixed.

It should be entirely possible to just pick up the CD, stick it in the drive, walk away for a few hours while the patches are applied, and then come back and play the game. If ANYTHING more is required, it's broken.

I even went to the lengths of ordering a more up-to-date CD, but even that doesn't fix the most basic of problems (the patcher overrun bug).

Yes, I know how to download .exe patches (don't get me started on that...). Yes, I know there is a "fixit" tool linked right here. Yes, I have read all the pertinent threads. Yes, I had some pain when I first installed it late in Beta.

NO, I do not expect to jump through hoops to even get the game running when it's been live for over a year!!!!

For now, I give up again.

Regards,
Scelto.
