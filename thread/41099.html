Bronto Liberation
Those who have been to the Bronto Burger factory in Galway county understand the horrible conditions the brontos are kept. What is worse is that these brontos are slaughtered while they are still babies. Think of that when you bite into your next bronto burger.

Our clan was successfull in saving 26 baby brontos from slaughter by relocating them to the Galway County Nature Reserve. I urge others to take similar measures against Bronto Burger and other entities who would commit such cruel acts against the innocent animal inhabitants of Rubi-Ka.

Pictures are available at http://www.koopa.org/ao/uor/events/b...to_rescue.html.
