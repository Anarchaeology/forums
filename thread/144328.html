GA answers and a snap shot of a lowbie fixer
Right. This is what my "newest" (Opifex) fixer looked like the first time he equipped GA Mk I at level 27

He needed a QL 50 sta/agi leg and a sta foot, essence and feline grace, boosts and a med suit to get the imps in / armour on.
Had to get a Riot Control for the last points of burst because of the implant layout.
Used a QL 70 Comp lit head implant to get the NCU memories in.

Also, he had to use a pair of QL 10 Concrete Cushions, and a pair of QL 1 Second-Hand Old English Trading Co.

Equiping GA Mk I takes Lend Nano: 250, Mocham's Gift: MatMet, Mocham's Gift: SpaceTime, Mocham's Gift: MatCrea, MatCrea Expertise and MatMet Expertise.
