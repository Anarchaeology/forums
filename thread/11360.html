Guide to getting Shades of Lucubration
The shades add +30 psy/intel

On February 14th at 9:20 PM EST, Nuggetz appeared at
1121 x 3706 in Clon****. If you grid there, go South toward Nano Guns. Anyone can pull him to the guards which are extremely high level and will kill him pretty quickly. Then you can wait (approximately 1 minute) and loot the corpse for your new QL165 Shades of Lucubration. His spawn rate is 18 hours. So his next few appearance times will be

15th 3:20 PM EST
16th 9:20 AM EST
17th 3:20 AM EST
18th 9:20 PM EST <-this day restarts the cycle.

Enjoy going out there, just if you want to see what he looks like or maybe get a few shots in on him or try to loot him after he dies.

I personally have no use for the shades, so I don't care who gets them.... and the reason I am posting this is simple. I showed up to see Nuggetz, because he is one of the few Unique Mobs I have never seen (he's always so overcamped). It just so happens that he was about to spawn and a guild had 10 people there to attempt to take him. I was just sitting in my Yal waiting to watch the fight and see what he looked like, when I recieve a tell from "HAGLER", it said "we just killed and are camping him til next spawn 17 hours and 47 minutes", I knew that was a bunch of crap so I hung around...

Anyways I got to see him and you should too... I'm tired of see a bunch of people think they own him and no one else should know when he spawns. Hopefully there will be no new server resets for awhile so a few of you can get out to see him too.

And if you see Hagler around... probably wise not to trust him... he's a bit dishonest.... And dumb to think that one Level 130 Agent was going to KS from 10 people from his guild and of which a least one was a higher level and there were two demons who were deep red to me.

I hope you guys enjoy getting out there... I was glad to finally see this unique mob, despite the attempts of the guild "The Syndicate" trying to lie to me to trick me leave the area.

Good Luck getting your shades! and hopefully you won't have to pay one of these guilds 30mil+ for them!!!
