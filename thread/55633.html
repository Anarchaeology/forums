Cz, please lets have a community discussion on trade skills.
Many of us enjoy the idea and limited uses in game of trade skills. It is fun to be able to tell people you "made" that Uber item you're using.

Unfortunately the profession's uses of tradeskills seems more than little messed up and the applications for them are limited. What is the status of the problem? Something being done about it? Are the various professions roles in tradeskills being considered?

I've got a lot of opinions, but I think it is a matter for the entire player community. It is also something we need an official funcom response on to let us know what direction you are going to take with it.
