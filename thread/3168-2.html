Yes, Metas are "powerful" - in their own way. Most people seem to judge a proffesion on what they do best. An agent firing off a succsessful AS, or an enforcer pulling of a huge dimach/brawl hit and still take a pounding with 5k hp must be equally powerful no?

Thing is, all proffesions have their strengths and weaknesses. I'm a little tired of ranting off the weaknesses every time someone "attacks" one of my proffession of choice, but take a look at the all powerful Meta:

* We can hardly do missions - the only way is to find a city/indoor/house type one if you're lucky, and traverse really slow and controlled. The minute something happens, bet on your pet getting stuck and causing you to die.

* Nearly ALL our useful clusters go in the SAME spots. Having played almost all the other proffesion, I could hardly believe my eyes when building my first meta set. Intelligence and Psychic, Matter Creation and Time&Space occupies the same spots - our 4 main skills. Psymod and Sensitivity also pretty much occupies the same spots. Once you finally manage to get some resemblance of a balance here, guess where the treatment clusters go to enable you to get em on? All this makes the Meta a constant sacrifice and balance issue, and we know we can never overbuff/overequip a damn thing. A level 35 enforcer in a full Q75 Elite outfit? No problem. I'm lucky if I get into my Q75 nano armor before lvl55.

* We burn through nanochargers at the same rate as Doctors do healing. Remove the ability to run missions for money and you quickly have a very poor sob. Not to mention the extreme cost of these new nanos that make us so "uber" - expect to pay a quarter million for a "lowlevel" nano other proffesions most likely find in shops for around 20-25k.

* We have no combat skills whatsoever. All combat related nanoskills are blue. Fair enough, but it doesn't quite justify the fact that we have very little diversity in programs besides the pets. The creation nanos are all nice and interesting, but the cost even for the simplest one is outrageous - the Metas you see around with creation weapons have most likely just given in to the curiosity. One thing is certain, once our pets go down, we're out of nano, and one of the million programs we have running times out, you better run.


But hey, it's fair enough. I adapt and overcome. Naturally I envy other proffesions for their strengths, but never try to bring another proffesion down to match your own proffesion's weakness. Agent's AS anyone?

Cheers
