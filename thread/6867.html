Improving the quality of life for Engies and Crats.
Firstly, probably to stop some exploit or other, you can only put one unique/nodrop shell in a particular backpack, this means your main inventory gets cluttered with shells ... a real pain.

Solution: The MA special attacks can be put in a special container for them, why not have a special bot shell carrying container to put all the spare shells in? Obviously nodrop, which has the added plus of staying in your quickbar after you visit reclaim.



Secondly, the Crat bot is so feeble, even you at Funcom acknowledge this and have been throwing around the idea of letting it cast nanos from time to time. Well, it seems that, since it uses Martial Arts, why not let it periodically use random MA specials? Just imagine your droid doing Blessed with Thunder!
