The constantly recurrung discussion about metas and weapons....


Metas can be very effective with no weapon.

If you choose to use a weapon, you need to continually invest a lot of IPs in a dark blue skill. The skill needs to be kept maxed every level in order to keep a decent QL weapon equipped or you will getting minimum damage hits. Dark blue skills get disgustingly expensive at higher levels.
Unless you are a twink, keeping your weapon up-to-date is an unneeded expense.
Using the best possible pet is paramount.
When you get up to level 60-65, your pet nanos are going to be costing 150-200k.


If you simply must use a weapon, a Vektor ND Shotgun isn't a terrible choice.

Miir and I have an ongoing disagreement about Metaphysicists using weapons.

If you're a nanomage, do what Miir tells you. He's the authority on standard pet-and-nuke metaphysicists.

If you're solitus or -- it does happen -- Atrox, then you have the option of being a variant of the Metaphysicist which has gotten the nickname 'Tankmage'. The main difference between this and normal MPs is that you want to tank the mobs, leaving your pet free to make the kills. A good weapon helps with that.

For MPs, the best weapons are 2hEdged/Martial Arts/Bow (very slight affinity with these weapons), Shotgun (only one skill required; massive critical hits), or 1hBlunt/2hBlunt (to use the new Creation nanos). After seeing the creation nanos, I'd have to say they're definitely worth the effort. I'm now transferring my character from a 2hEdged user to a 1hBlunt user, mostly by changing implants. You'll do more damage with Shotgun/2hEdged (and thus, tank better), but the created shields *significantly* help a meta's survivability. An atrox meta with a deep red pet, maxed evades, tank armor, and a Solar Guard is one of the hardest things you'll ever try to kill.

Whatever you choose, have fun.
