I have an MA that is 90. (112 agent main.)

Gotta say:

Pets? No. Pets classes have those. You have no need for them.
Morphs? No. There we go infringing on others territory.

Sharp objects? Not a bad idea, but a martial artists isn't really a ninja. A ninja is an assassin, which actually fits the agent class more. But considering a ninja is a martial artist, but just focusing
those skills to be such. (Assassin.) I don't see the problem there.

It's more of a character thing. If an MA wants to be a ninja, evil
guy, why not? I think Sharp Objects would be cool for us. (MA's.)

But bows!? Nope.. agents. They're assassins, and many "old
school" people of that "profession" used bows, "back then".

A)Martial artists are melee. Period. Projectile weapons, that are
not thrown by hand are not native to them. Ninja stars! Sweet!

Bows.. nu-uh.

B)Agents are assassins by trade. Experts at ranged attacks.

C)Many bows use what skill? AimedShot. Many bows also have a
secondary skill, now, which is what? Rifle. Bow = Agent assassin.

D)AGT ranged init is cheaper. Admitably, your bow and bow special
are cheaper, but our init and aimed shot are cheap in return.

Let me just sum it up this way: Sharp objects don't have to be
thrown. They can be shruikens(Sp?), yes. But they can also be
daggers or small concealed blades. SO this is my idea.

Agents need something other than a damn rifle to use, if it's
their choice. MA"s can have an additional one, themselves. Seeing
as Agents actually have cheap melee and ranged init than MA's,
and a VERY cheap sharp objects, and fairly cheap bow skill(10),
why not give one of these skills, and needed buffs to each?

(Seriously, everyone that casts or has pets use an x-3, now!)

So
A)MA's get a new ranged toy, and agents get sharp objects.
MA's have bow special, and great regular damage. (HUnter KIWD)
while agents can still use sharp objects, such as daggers, while
still using conceal for sneak attacks. (10pts cheap than MA's.)

Or
B)MA's get sharp objects, like shuikens(Again, sp?) and daggers,
so the choice is theirs, while agents get bow buffs and bows,
so they can remain ranged, but still have an assassin's weapon.

Seriously, agents need variety? What competent agent DOESN'T
use an X-3? Not many.. they need a bit of interest, too.

By the way, I'm making an MA content improvement thread, myself,
because there's honesly a lot that can be added that would be
pretty interesting. Not that playing an MA is boring, at all...
