How I would've improved Dodga/Alvin Quest II
Well, as we all know now, Dodga/Alvin's second quest involves going from one unique bot to another and tagging them. They must be visited in a specific sequence in order to finish the quest. The decoder item that is given when you take the mission is supposed to enable you to ascertain which bot is next in line.

Sounds like fun, except that everyone knows the sequence by now, and no one bothers with the decoder.

I think the mission would have been better if the sequence were randomized for each player. This way, everyone would have to chase the bot around, use the decoder, and figure out where to go next. This was supposed to be part of the mission originally, but the unchanging sequence makes it so that there is no reason at all to bother listening to the bot yammer.

As it is now, the fun and discovery of the mission was gone already as soon as it was live. The way most people do it is with a team. Everyone goes together from one bot to another and tags as a group. I don't think this is particularly fun, and I don't think it was meant to be this way.

Because of this, most of the players miss out on a big part of the mission. There's no sense of discovery. It's more like a series of chores.
