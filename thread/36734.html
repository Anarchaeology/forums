just something to think about....
just an observation....

I don't post often, but I read all the boards I can often. I notice that a lot of the whining stems from one class being good at certain times. One moment everyone gangs up on the traders cause they are owning people left and right in pvp...then traders get nerfed, and fixers get something good which makes them more competitive than normal...and the majority of the population screams "Nerf the Fixer!"....

I am pretty sure than eventually all the nerfing noise will lead to fixers getting the chop and then everyone will be off to the next class to whine about. nerfing is not good.......I say if someone turns out to be very good when before they were not...then we simply have to adjust fire and find a new strategy, a new way to defeat said class. there will always be a way to beat someone. you just have to look for it.

with that being said, take me into consideration.

I read a bit further down the board, someone made a post with an outline of all the class pvp abilities. You will notice one class is missing from that ENTIRE thread. the class that I love and play.

the facts are this:

I am a Bureaucrat. I own almost everything I see in PVM and Missions. 75% of my nanos have no use or purpose in PVP(I think nukes, and root are the only ones). And that's being generous. I can't do enough damage with weapons in PVP to ever warrant attacking. I have no HP compared to other classes and dark, dark blue Body Dev. My pet is generally ignored by anyone who decides I am an easy mark, which I am.

with all the whining and complaining about pvp that I see around the official forums, just consider this pvpers:

You could be in my position pvp wise.

I am a Bureaucrat. I own almost everything I see in PVM and Missions.

In PVP I DON'T EXIST!

so it could be worse.....
