Yes and No
Your level does determine the QL of the bracer, and the QL of the bracer determines the title level requirement to wear it.

So, with this in mind:

You're level 10
You find a QL 16 bracer
This bracer likely has a requirement of title level 2
You save it for a few levels.

You're level 110
You find a QL 99 bracer
This bracer likely has a requirement of title level 3 (whereas a QL 101 bracer has title level 4).

Why do they do this?

You're level 1
A level 200 guildmate hands you a QL 200 bracer
(* It used to be this way, hence the title requirement *)

So, yes, the title levels of bracers that YOU FIND off mobs your level will be, more or less, around your level and title level.

The only major exception to this rule are for rings of any QL that you cannot take off until title level 6. Basically, they're saying "this is permanent" (title lvl 6 is lvl 190).

Hope this helps
