Bateman & Fletcher, LLC Expansion
Bateman & Fletcher, LLC Consulting Group Expands
Omni-Tek Trade District, R-K1

Bateman & Fletcher, LLC offers a wide variety of general services and consultation to organizations and corporations galaxy-wide. These services include public relations and press management, superb research and analyses, competitive intelligence & counter-intelligence, integrated countermeasures, strategic event management, employee screening and evaluation, tactical political guidance, topical legal advice, and a history of getting results.

Expanded services on Rubi-Ka are coming online daily:

Bateman & Fletcher Arbitration Committee
Settle inter/intra-guild disputes once and for all with Bateman & Fletcher's confidential conflict resolution services.

Bateman & Fletcher Escrow
A nominal fee will ensure that transfers of goods and credits will be handled discreetly and honestly. Take the worry out of selling or transferring your assets. Guaranteed.

Bateman & Fletcher Travel Agency
Need to get to a remote location? Bateman & Fletcher's engineering department can teleport you there instantly avoiding hazardous and laborious travel-time. Travel safe with Bateman & Fletcher.


Bateman & Fletcher is expanding -- New Employment Opportunities now available.

Bateman & Fletcher, LLC is a diverse, tightly knit organization on Rubi-Ka1. We are currently looking for people to fill positions in the offices of Creative Services, Research, Engineering and Security.

Contact Ravenwulf, Japella, or Cratsworth on Rubi-Ka 1 for further information.
