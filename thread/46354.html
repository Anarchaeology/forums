What to do about reflect bracers
I know that FunCom is going to be changing reflect bracers. I wish they wouldn't. I don't see the need for just flat nerfing. Here is the idea I have:

1) All existing soldier reflect shields will be made self only.

2) New soldier reflect shields will added which can be given to others. These new reflect shields can follow funcoms idea of level requirements if they want, doesn't really matter to me.

3) Reflect bracers will not be changed.

4) TMS will stack with a soldier's regular reflect shield so that it is not killed by TMS.

5) TMS will not add to the soldier's amount of damage reflect but instead simply sets the amount reflect to 75%.

6) The amount of NCU required to cast TMS will be reduced

What this will mean is that a soldier level 190+ could potentially have 72% reflect for a single damage type all the time. The TMS will be beneficial because it will set reflect to exactly 75% across all damage types. Once TMS wears off the soldier's reflect amount will return to normal and because TMS will stack with the existing soldier reflect shield they will not lose it.

This idea would make me happy and I should think it will make many others happy as well.

Okay - flame away or give your ideas. I just don't see the need for this change. This just puts me one more nerf closer to the cancel button.
