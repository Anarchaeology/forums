Deep Winter Fashion Show
Deepwinter Faction RK2 chapter launches the first of a series monthly competition for the title:

Deep Winter Fashion King and Queen

Date: Wednesday, December 12th, 2001

Time: 7:30pm EST(6:30pm Central Time, 4:30pm Pacific)

Place: Lush Resort (Right at the pool off the grid)

Deminsion: RK2

Prizes: 1st place: 100k credits plus the title of King or Queen
2nd place: 50k credits and 1st runner up title
3rd place: 25k credits
