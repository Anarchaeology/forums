Funcom...do this and make all your (remaining) customers happy again.....
First let me start by saying that I still love this game, mostly because of the friends I have made during the time I have played. I am not threatening to quit, that doesn't work, I'm just trying to help my friends find reasons to stay.

That being said, I truely believe that this is the idea that could save AO: The ability to change professions.

Don't laugh, it could be made to work quite well.

1. Make character unequip everything, a la IPR. This would have to be done to get rid of proffession specific armor/weapons/items.

2. Have a special terminal in a shop somewhere, probably Superior shops in major cities.

3. Make said terminal have a cost, say 10% of your total credits. A small price to pay for a new chance at playablity.

4. Upon activation of terminal, have it display the available professions, giving the option to either select a new profession or stay with your current. Want to make sure someone doesn't get stuck with a new proffession because they selected the terminal by mistake.

5. After selecting a new proffesion, force a total IPR reset (Save Map nav) and whipe all "Learned" Nano forumlas. Then you can start uploading your new proffession's nanos.

6. In addition to the credit cost for using the terminal, have a penalty of say 10% of levels. Ex: You want to make your level 100 doctor an adventurer. After selecting adventurer as your new proffession, your level drops from 100 to 90 because, face it you couldn't be as good an adventurer as you were a doctor from the second you switch, you'd have to grow a bit first.


Sure it would need to be tested forever to make sure there weren't any bugs. And someone would eventually find a way to exploit it, prompting an "emergency" patch during peak game time, but it would help to eliminate some of the tensions and anger built up by the damaging nerfs.
