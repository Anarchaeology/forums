Alliances?
So, here's my question about the Booster Pack. Has anyone considered alliances yet? It seems that the big concern that people have is that the few uber guilds are going to be able to just run the whole land control thing and that will be the end of it.

Will us smaller guilds be able to allign with another guild? Maybe cap a size that a guild or group of guilds can be (just take the single largest guild and add half of the second largest guild, and that's your number. Keep it dynamic so it changes as the guilds change in size). That way the Biggest guild stays the same size but the smaller guilds have a similiar, if more fragile, ability to participate.
