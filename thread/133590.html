They want to go there for the same reason that we want to go to Tir.

I remember as a very young colonist, having discovered how hostile some of the locals in Athen could be, Tir was recommended to me as a safe haven for trade and general discourse. It was refreshing to find a clan city that didn't treat me as hostile, and the physical fabric of the place was fascinating to someone who'd grown up with the sheer concrete walls of Borealis.

Newland was the neutral equivalent, a safe city one step out of ICC (after the whompa revamp) with pleasant surroundings where all three factions could gather to talk and trade. Of course it was mainly used as a jumping-off point for missions, but that's another issue entirely.

Personally, I have mixed feelings about the move to exclude one faction from the city. On the one hand the Silverstone episode hurt us, and despite the actions of a few the majority of clanners obviously didn't care enough to do anything about it. There was more than a little jubilation in some circles and small groups were observed gathering at the whompas to jeer at the corpse of the next unlucky neut who just hadn't heard the bad news yet. In the face of that, it was heartening to see that perhaps there was something we could do that would make our displeasure felt.

However, the fact that the mission network in Newland now gives only the most basic and undemanding of missions does suggest that perhaps Newland's economy isn't what it used to be as a result of this move. It's also made detente with the clans trickier, since neutral "aggression" in Newland always comes up at some point. Despite the message that EO tried to send out, most clanners still don't really understand why you did what you did.

Never mind "treaties for neutrals" etc. It's about time that the more influential members of the clan and neutral communities simply sat down and sorted this whole thing out. Silverstone can't be shifted except by force, but the clans have shown time and again their ability to deal with the toughest opposition and come out on top. At the neutral end things are even simpler - end the contract with Loren Warr.

You've sent your message. Now it's about time we did something about it.

Cheers,
