Kaviera Family Mafia and its new new face..
*turns on a mic*
*clears his throat*

Greetings,

With the new president of KFM(Porsche 'Alilee' McCovery) and with Neo 'Xeroscope' Kaviera turned clan and created a clan based KFM, called Kaviera Family Mafia(Dead Cell). During the elections Alilee said in her speech one of her biggest concerns was the Role-Playing aspect of the KFM (specially with them around Rompa Bar), She has decided that KFM would take on a new face in the Omni-Tek world. With that said, KFM is had very few RPers' and impacted alot of the Role-playing organizations in and around Rompa, in a bad way. Which is why Omni-pol is restricted to RP with them, for there would be punishment to whoever did. But a few days ago, Donte 'Zariken' Kaviera (Forum's Advisor for KFM) had a meeting with the president of Omni-pol and discussed its new plans and with a generous decision Omni-pol has lifted is RP ban starting next Saturday. Which I invite all of you to please stop and say hello. Though we still have some members who do not RP with us, we have more and more join us everyday. Some of the RPers will still take the role of 'bad guys' and dislike OP and several others, while few (alilee) welcome everyone with open arms. We hope to get a better view from the public besides the rude and un-welcoming, and hope to run into some of you in the near future.
Thank you.

*Turns mic off*
