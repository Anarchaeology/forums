From Desert Winds
-----Start transmission-----

Rubi-Ka was empty before we came here, thats not to say nothing had ever happened. The planet had accreted, melted, roiled and cooled, leaving a surface scarred by enormous geological features: craters, canyons and volcanoes. But all of that happened in mineral unconsciousness, and unobserved.

People of Rubi-Ka look around you. Look at your home, what it is and what it could become.

Once Rubi-Ka was a lifeless dull ball, we have begun the process of turning her into a living place.

But we have done more, we have started to mine Rubi-Ka dry.

The precious notum is blasted off planet, sold to line the pockets of a vast corporation.

Mines cover the planet now, but what have we done?

The notum is so precious it is a mill stone around the neck of Rubi-Ka.

How so? Let me explain.

Rubi-Ka has an artificial environment that was created by the same company that mines and exports off planet the majority of the notum.

This company has a presence here sanctioned by the ICC, The Intergalactic Cartel of Companies. Omni-Tek is just one member of this cartel. They hold the planet by the balls.

Notum is the most economically important substance in the galaxy, whoever controls the notum will make vast profits.

Control Rubi-Ka, control the notum and thus control your destiny.

The corporations will never let Rubi-Ka be independent of their hold.

It does not have to be this way. People of Rubi-Ka, those who call your self Rubi-Kan fight the machine!

Halt the mining and Free Rubi-Ka

- An ecosystem that works, no more dependence on off planet companies.
- A government for Rubi-Ka, not a company.
- Free to decide our own fate.



And so we came here. Rubi-Ka had her consciousness and was a place.

*italicised words are paraphrased from Red Mars by Kim Stanley Robinson  pub. Harper Collins

-----End Transmission-----
