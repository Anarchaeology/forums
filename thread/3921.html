Join the Rubi-Ka Free Trade Union today!
The Rubi-Ka Free Trade Union is looking for clan members! If you are honorable, fun-loving and a craftsman, salesperson, or have a general interest in trade and the economy of Rubi-Ka, then the Union is the place for you!

Though the general purpose of the Union is of course to facilitate clan tradespeople and salespersons, don't think that the Union is only a place for "a bunch of traders to hang out!" Everyone knows how much trade and the economy affects all other aspects of the political, social, and military climates on Rubi-Ka, so the Union will take a very active part in all spheres of interest, and will therefore need a diverse range of skills and abilities available to it. There will be room for people of all breed, profession, and background in the Union!

If you are interested, reply to this thread, contact Forum in-game, or send an email to RKFTU@aol.com.

Hunter "Forum" Olveda

(Out of character notes: the goal of this clan is to gather a good group of fun, honorable people to enjoy themselves and interact with the storyline in a fun, interesting way, from a relatively unique but potentially very influntial angle. I look forward to hearing from all interested parties! The sooner the better!)
