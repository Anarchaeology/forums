Ok Psychology fans, here's some brand spankin' new info!

- Ideas on new uses for Psychology include 'DNA linking' as part of making certain weapons (e.g. a very good rifle, but locked to the creator, i.e. NoDrop) or other items, and more 'paperwork' trade processes.

- Regarding the Aggression Enhancers that uses less Psychology, they have been made to give an optional taunt device, which is not as effective as the original Aggression Enhancer. The new device uses Psychology to a lesser degree, but relies more on another skill (as you pointed out). It also has a special requirement. Those who have already invested in Psychology skill might be better off staying with the original one.
