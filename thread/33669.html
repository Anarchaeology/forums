RK2 Guild Fair
Hello Fellow Citizens of Rubi-Ka,

This is to let you know of a Guild Fair being held this Saturday, June 29 in Reet Retreat in Stret West Bank at 20:00GMT.

A guild fair is an opportunity for folks who are not in a guild to talk with members of guilds to find out various information about them so they can decide if they'd like to join them or not.

This event is for Clan, Neutral and Omni alike, and there will be representatives from all sides there.

Please feel free to show up and check it out!
