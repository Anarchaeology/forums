Here's the problem as I see it:

It's impossible to make professions completely balanced in PvP versus each other. Why? Because some professions are based around tradeskills. These classes have opportunities that combat classes do not. Therefore to compensate and be fair, the combat classes should be better at combat since that is their field.

In duels then, it should technically be perfectly fair for enforcers to win every PvP round: They're character is made for combat, correct? Would it be fair for an Engineer that focuses on trade skills to excel against combat classes? If you think about it, no actually, not at all. Then again, engineers suck at everything, but that's beyond the point.

This is why I don't like 1 on 1 fights. They're Purposely imbalanced FOR THE SAKE OF BALANCE. If you understand what I mean. Sorry, I'm tired. Notum Wars however, is ok since you have buddies to compensate for your deficiencies.

Some people might interpret this differently, but I think that balance should be the ability for every class to become very good at the thing that they were created for. To have something that other classes can't dream of, and to be happy about it.

Bah, all of this shows just how stupid it is to try to balance all of the professions and to give them new stuff in the process. Tell you what: Stop single duels and only allow team battles or something, then you can give professions lots of new stuff without worrying to have to balance the whole mess out as much.

{Disclaimer} These are just my views of the situation, not a serious analysation as you can see. Just a tired and lazy view of the problems with AO class balance and PVP. Don't flame me please, just say that you disagree and that can be it.
