I'm not surprised you only see 2 "Classes" in the game - You are looking for Archetypes.

There are more or less only 3 Archetypes :
-The Fighter
-The Supporter
-The Tradesman

Only the Tradesman is often not seen a lot and in this game doesn't really exists. Ultima Online had enough alternatives to fighting that there existed characters that had never fought a single rat his entire life. While it's theoretically possible in AO as wel, I've never heard of a character in AO that never killed anything.

Fighters are the ones that do the Damage - MArtial artists do it with hands and feets, Enforcers like it up, close and personal, preferrable with a spikey baseball bat in their hands and Soldiers use machine guns and assault rifles from a distance - Yet they are all Fighters. Thus they pretty much become just 1 "class" ... an archetype.

Supporters are characters that can heal, buff, debuff, snare, slow and do crowd control such as Calming monsters.

So, there, did that make any sense to you ?
