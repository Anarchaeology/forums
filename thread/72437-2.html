After asking in tir how to get to DAV someone said take a grid to 2HO. So here i am at reclaim and quet mad becasue i died so i decide to go back and fight him since grid wasnt an option so i flew all the way from newland to 2HO i unequip my yalmaha i slowly got close to that enforser then i AS him and yes he falls dead! It was such a great feeling i got my revenge but mostly becasue he died so fast.

So Ao became for me i spend some time getting stuff for my character and then pvp at the bridges. Thats lasted for almoust 2 weeks it was so much fun.
Ah those good old days at the briges when only buffs people were asking for were AC and HP no one knew of crit buff power. We died alot we killed a lot no hard feelings when i was Aimed Shoting soldiers and dieng to reflect or when two NTs wiped out 3 clan teams in the matter of 10 seconds.

When i fianly finished equiping my character she was uber. Full set of ql 110 implants and 75 armor was good backthere. Decided to go hunt got to know some people that we used to pvp at the bridges we hunted with them. Been to lots of places was fun hunting A-4000 in tir country and rember when we tried to kill sandworm. OR ehen we went to hunt in CAV and saw The Desert Emmite we thought its a toughest mob in AO.
Lvling wasnt fast maybe we were getiting good Xp for killing but we died as fast as getting XP so after couple hours of XP we go and pvp in 2HO or MMD. Ah those good old days when 2HO and MMD seemd to be so big.
