I definitely like Wal's idea.

That is: if after shields are disabled, no towers are destroyed within an hour, then gas should go to 75% and start a new cycle. This not only eliminates sitzkrieg, but has also another advantage - you can shift the time period of when a base becomes attackable.

I know a few high level bases on RK2 that rarely gets attacked simply because they become 25% at extremely "bad" times - when only a small percentage of players are on. Implementing Wal's idea would mean you can shift gas cycle period, and hence make a base turn 25% at different time.
