Well, first off, I suggest you repost your message as a new thread in the doc forum since I think you will get more responces.

Having a doc as your main character is expensive and until TL4 (Title Level 4, i.e. level 100+), you will be struggling for money. Don't worry about armor until you are 60+ but you should go to Temple of the Three Winds and get an Exhaurt's Robe. Also, don't bother with using a weapon, instead get the doc Chalice from Temple.

I suggest you do not beg for credits, but you can ask to have all the 'junk' loot no one wants because you need to save up for better heals. This will sometimes get you a kind soul who will buy you a few new heal nanos. Also remember that you don't need to buy every new heal--every second or third will be fine.

If you are interested, I suggest you join a newbie-friendly guild--they will often help you out with getting stuff. You can look for a good guild on the Life on Rubi-Ka forum.

(btw, I hope you aren't taking the Guide to Teaming With Docs too seriously )
