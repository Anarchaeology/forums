"What, you don't have the matching shirt?!"
Hello friends,

I am here to discuss something that has been bothering me for a while. I guess I should rephrase that. I am here to discuss with you something that has been bothering me for a long time, but I have only recently figured it out. Some of you might be saying to yourselves, "What in tarnation?!" so let me explain. Since I started playing AO something had been bothering me about the game. It was/is fun and all, but there was always something on the edge of my consciousness that just didn't sit well with me.

But I come before you today with exciting news! I have figured it out!

It is the way of procuring armor. Do you have to go searching for general nanos? No. Do you have to go searching for a full set of ql 70 implants (Just the implants, not the clusters and what-have-you)? No. Do you have to search for a basic weapon? Kinda... but not really. Do you have to go searching for armor? HELL YES!

I seriously have never purchased armor from a vendor before. I have graced various shops with my patronage when in need of purchasing nanos, weapons, implants, nano clusters, trimmers, and other nicknacks. So why are we forced to drudge through an abandoned sewer system, kill enemies whom find OUR piece of armor to be worth their life, and otherwise jump through hoops. Or the worse scenario to which we all eventually succumb: shelling out gobs of cash to our own allies for a simple set of plasteel armor?

The answer? I don't have a freakin' clue?!

And I'm not talking about things like Omni-pol elite or MK II armor. I'm talking about plasteel armor, metaplast armor, the most basic of armors. While I agree that some(maybe most) armor should be gotten through heroic deeds and other such nonesense, but as an employee of Omni-Tek or a loyal Clanner (or a neutral Neutral) we should be able to spend our cash with EASE to procure certain basics of living on Rubi-Ka.

So I was thinking with the introduction of the various new types of armor in 14.2, why not make certain COMPLETE sets of armor available in the shops? I mean, some sets of plasteel/metaplast armor at ql 20, 30, 40 or whatever would add a sense of "RPG" to this game. Have you ever played an RPG where you couldn't go to a town and upgrade your armor to something fairly basic, then go on a quest to get a new super-duper-cool-ultra-sword-with-lasers-and-optional-nuke-add-on?

Lets face it, getting a set of crappy armor (either because you're too poor to afford the prices your fellow RKers sell things at, or terminal camping isn't your cup of tea) is just a pain in the ass.

Maybe I shoulda put this in the "next patch" forum... oh well!

So if you made it through my lengthy tirade, please, leave a comment.

Lotsa Luck,
Soozan
