I'm quite certain that all the high level 'gods' don't remember this stuff, so I'll go ahead and dispense my godly lowbie experience.

Since AO shipped, I've probably leveled at least 50 characters up to level 25-50 or so. This is no exaggeration, and it was almost entirely done solo.

MAs are easy to the point of being unbalanced for the first 10-25 levels. I have been able to get the first 10 levels in under an hour with an MA soloing, no problem. I did this with a character who started with no twinkage other than they had the personal healing nano, 25 low QL chargers and 25 low QL kits to start with. I think it could be done faster.

Traders, possibly the most versatile class in the game, are the absolute hardest class to start bar none. Remember, there are no deprives/ransacks until level 7 or so. In fact, there is no nothing until level 7 or so...heh

Crats are bar none the second easiest class to solo after level 2. Once you get a bot, you have the combination of solar pistol, MA bot, and 15 pt dd that you can cast 4-5 times. If you have 5-600 creds to spend on chargers, crats are unstoppable until about level 8 or so, then unstoppable again after they get mez. Nobody, and I mean nobody, appreciates how powerful crats are.

Doctors aren't too bad as long as you have nano chargers, but once you burn through those (you start with 15 chargers, so usually you are out after 15 fights or 1.5 levels) then you are relegated to either begging NTs for humidity so you can continue to solo, or actually SITTING DOWN and waiting for nano to come back. Or maybe you are lucky and you have saved enough phat newbie loot to drop 300+ credits on some more chargers. Difficulty ramps up considerably at about level 5-8 or so for solos, and continues going up slowly from there until the end of time.

NTs start out weak, then get extremely powerful in the first 50 levels. Remember, the first nuke is a debuff/nuke, so it does very little damage. They start with the dreaded solar pistol. At the point where you get shockball, though, the fun really begins. It's usually not too hard to find a place where you can shockball 2-3 mobs at a time to death, gaining levels pretty quickly but with extreme risk. NTs will benefit greatly from no xp loss to level 30. :) I predict sales of shockball will skyrocket :)

Enforcers are pretty easy, especially after you get the first rage nano. Second only to MA in ease at that point. Not being able to heal is hard, but enforcers have no nanos, and a pretty easy time finding compatible armor and weapons in missions, so kits are easy to come by.

Soldiers are rediculously hard at low levels considering that they are a combat class. The solar AR is good because it can burst, probably the most powerful starter weapon in fact, but the killer is no healing ability. Soldiers go through the first 15 kits as fast as docs do, but luckily they don't have to spend as much to replace them since they don't need nano chargers.

Engies are a total joke for the first level or two. The first bot can barely solo a leet, even with your help. If the bot dies, you die, period. Luckily engies get 2-3 new bots a level, so it doesn't take long before you can upgrade to something that is more suitable.

Agents, another extremely difficult start. Solar rifle is major suckage, with it's 2 pt aimed shot as the only special. Not too bad if you go MA until you get a better rifle. Pretty much worthless otherwise.

Fixers, another class with a bursting starter gun. It only bursts for about 15, but that's enough to almost kill a leet. You can solo pretty well for the first 3-4 levels if you are patient enough to wait for burst between fights. This also saves you creds on kits.

Adventurers are pretty much like Doctors...they burn through kits healing, so they need chargers fast. Advents aren't too bad if you dual pistols and forego all but the healing nano skills and damage shields at lower levels. Any IP wasted on morphs, etc will cause you to be hurting for IP at around 50. And ESPECIALLY don't use 1he, it looks like an interesting idea, but it isn't. lol

Without a doubt, the first 30 or so levels of the game are the hardest. You are playing with inexperienced people who frequently get you killed, you are making poor choices with your IP, and unless you are just soloing missions, the gameworld is daunting with rollerrats that chase you down and kill you every time you leave town.

Of course, that is assuming you don't make a beeline for lush fields mine the second you come out the sack, like most folks do. I find camping spawns boring so I don't do that.
