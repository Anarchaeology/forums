Damon "Ugluk" Howryla
Damon was brought up in a lab in Newland, as he was an experiment he didn't have to go through any childhood as he was grown-up at the start. After awhile the Scientists took no intrest in Damon what so ever and finally he was kicked out in the warm desert of Newland. With no knowledge where he was or where the closest city was located he started too wander about. The scientists had been good enough to at least give him a small weapon and with this he shot small animals so that he could survive the day. After countless miles out in the desert he was nearly dying of thirst, at the horizon he thought he could see a large wall, he did not belive his eyes but had nothing too loose since he was soon dehydrated anyway.
But when he got closer he saw that his eyes were telling the truth and it really was a big city he had found. He stumbled into the gates and screamed for help. People just looked at him and he saw that they were all wearing some sort of symbol he had never seen before. Some didn't care, some laughed at him and some even spit on him. After that he passed out from thirst, later, he did not know how much later, he woke up in a small rather hard bed. Bent over him stod a large Atrox, just like him self. He gave him water and some food to eat and told him where he was and what had happened. He had apperantly passed out on the streets of Newland, the clanners (Damon had no idea who these was, but thought it might be the rude people that didn't help him) hadn't helped him a bit only staring on him then ignoring him. Luckily for you, the big Atrox said, we came to Newland and saw you laying there so we took you to our small camp.
Who are you, asked Damon rather confused.
I am Nick Dinnen, a faithfull Omni-Tek employee, and so are the rest of the people that helped you out at when you needed it at most.
Damon was really tired and could feel Nick's hand on his head, and distantly he heard Nick say: Remember, Omni-Tek is your friend.
Later, he did not know how long, he opened his eyes and he was still in that same bed. No one was in the room though and he walked out. There he saw some sort of camp and a man came up to him and said: You must be Damon, eh? I think this is just the place for you. Come now, follow me to the shooting range and I'll teach you some basic information about being a soldier.
After several months he came out of the camp, he had been given directions to Newland so he went there. For the second time he entered the gates, but this time he was neither thirsty nor hungry. He saw the rebels drinking beer, having street fights in this dirty city. He had never hated anything before, but now he hated, he hated so very much. He hated how they looked, he hated how they acted, he hated how they talked but mostly he hated how they thought.
For awhile anger and hatred clouded his mind, he pulled out his gun and blindly started opening fire against the rebels.
After that he ran, he ran very far and very long down south. On the way he met up with people that had his way of thinking, his way of view of the dirty clanners. Their company was now getting bigger the further down they came, and at last they arrived in Omni-1.
The grace and wonders of this beutifull city astounded them all, and they just stood there, watching security guards walk proudly around in black and white armor with a symbol on it. Damon recongnized the symbol as soon as he saw it. It was the same symbol Nick and his company had, the ones that saved his life.
He knew that this was his home, he knew that he had to defend it at all costs, he knew that there would be no safety before all clanners was rotting corpses in the ground that they had soiled with their stupidity.
And so, he and his companions gave a pact to protect Omni-Tek forever more even if it means death. They started Night Assault Legion.

Damon "Ugluk" Howryla
