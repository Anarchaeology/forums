Fix The Hunting Areas
Please fix the hunting areas and spawn rates around Tir and Athen. We Clanners should not have to fly our Yalmaha to 20k, just to hunt.

Before you party crasher types start crying, we clanners have NO PLACE TO HUNT after level 35. We have to go to 20K or Lush Fields, or well, anywhere in Omni lands.

So, how do I know about 20k? I just found out about it, and if you don't have a Yalmaha...... you aren't going to 20k.

There are so many places to hunt in 20k alone, with 40k xp a pop, for level 80 guys, and it's easy xp for the most part. You can hunt in teams all the way to level 100 as an Omni. As a Clanner you have to do solid missions after level 35.

There are a few lousy hunt locations in GTC, but the spawn rates are too slooooooooooooow, and too few by far.

It's like going to a Clanner friends house to play Frogger, or going to the arcade at the Omni Mall. And that is about the correct comparision. Don't believe me, then check it out yourself.

Even if you returned Clan hunting to the pre 12.7 patch rates and quality, the Omni areas are still 200% better now. Funcom nerfed the clan spawn rates and number of mobs in patch 12.9

Forget all the other crap you guys at FunCom are working on to make things balanced, because there is a gross inbalance in the two factions. An Omni player can level his new guy to level 80 in 10 days, and not even need a kodiak to get around in.
