And one of my points is that the clans have not had a unified goal in quite some time. I do not doubt that many of you have the same goal. Some of you doubtless even agree on the method that is best to achieve that goal. However, I would think you would also agree that there are many clanners that do not share your goal. The Dust Brigade for instance. Omni Tek was willing to deal with the Council of Truth, until elements within the clans sought to promote their own view and attacked the Council, driving them into obscurity.

For every well-intentioned clanner who is willing to seek change peacefully but who will fight in defense of their lives and the lives of those they care for, there is another who wants nothing but the destruction of all that is Omni. I have asked several clanners, under the mildest of interrogation, why they fought against Omni Tek and what they hoped to accomplish. Their answers were very similar. They blamed Omni Tek for all that was wrong in their lives, and vowed to continue to fight until Omni Tek was destroyed. While I will not lump you in with their kind, I hope you can see how those of us that are charged with defending the Corporation must take statements like yours, assuring us that you seek a peaceful change, with at least a grain of salt.

I will also not lie to you and tell you that all employees abide by the enlightened directives of Philip Ross when he ordered us not to seek conflict with the clans, and to avoid attempting to impose Omni Tek law upon you. I will not even go as far as to say that all that do abide by the directives do so of their own desire. However, for the good of the Corporation, and out of a sense of loyalty, they do so.

If I might, I would like to point out one thing that I think all sides have agreed on here, in one way or another. To try to put everyone from any faction into the same category is not only foolish, it is hopeless. All sides will have disenters. Some clanners want nothing but war. Some Omni employees serve for the same reason. And the same goes for neutrals. There is profit to be had in conflict, there is personal glory. But there is also shame and dishonor if the cause is not just, or the methods abhorent. All sides have those that want peace for the sake of peace. All sides, unfortunately, have collaborators and sympathyzers. If you say you want to bring about a peaceful change, then I am in no position to gainsay you. But I have experienced firsthand that not all clanners are as enlightened as to seek a civilized resolution. And it is with them that Omni Tek must fight.

As Director Ross has stated, there is a much greater profit to be had here on Rubi Ka if we have the ability to do business in peace. That, if nothing else, should tell us all why Omni Tek says it wants peace: simply because it is better for business.
