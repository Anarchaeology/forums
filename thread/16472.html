Possible solution to OE + Traders wrangles
You guys are really hitting this over-equiping problem pretty hard. I, for one, like the idea a lot. For some classes it is very easy to get waaaay over equiped. For others, like an NT for example, good freakin luck! Last time I looked there were no huge Int/Psy buffs for you to put on that uber armor. Oh sure, there are matter crea buffs for better attacks right? Ahem, in case you people didnt notice, unlike weapon buffs the nano buff has to be RUNNING for the attacks to work.. Hmmm, I think the nano guys have better things to do than use up 40-52 NCU for the equivelent of a 100 wrangle a soldier, enforcer, fixer, etc only need ONCE to equip their weapon.
Now as I understand it, you will still be able to overequip with buffs (ie wrangles) as long as the nano is active. Thats fine.
The wrangles intention is to strengthen an individual on the Trader's team or the whole team, right?
Why not enforce the OE a bit harder by making a new wrangle requirement: must be a member of the caster's team. Then make the nano atuo-shutoff if you leave that team.
This, along with your % rules, will prevent all overequiping except where it is intended to be taking place.
