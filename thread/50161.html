Dabblez
Trust me, I'm a scientist
Drugs was a natural subject for the SRS, as it is one area where our in character charity work can get dangerous. One of the first South Rubi-Ka adventures invovled the fire-bombing of a a drug rehab centre we sponsor in Omni Sewers. This consequently got us mixed up with a drug called "White Trash" and a shady land redevelopment company, the Highrise Constructions Consortium.

There is a somewhat confused write up of the adventure at this link http://rubika.no-ip.org/srs/srsadvent.htm

As a roleplaying device, White Trash is not your garden variety of recreational drug. It created for a very specific purpose. It is only found among the dealers in Omni-Sewers and among it's side effects is rapid, premature ageing. I really can't add more without we still have to play out the final chapter of this adventure.

There is a "vibrant" drug scene in the Rubi-Kan underworld, but you'd proably have to ask (ooc!) the likes of the likes of the Mockers, Devil's Advocate and the inevitable Diamondcut about it.

Try this thread for more info on the subject
http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=42226

Dianechti
Leet
I would think that stim use and drug use in the medical professions would also be a common occurence, especially given the amount of death and pressure to perform.

As a doctor I've several times casually emoted applying a stim in public situations when I've "looked tired"

It is a rather known fact that those professions that easily access them often easily apply them.

Stim addiction for combat and doctors is a good storyline character trait to delve in to.

Speaking of drugs why not add the social smoking going around that seems to be increasingly popular. Those little death sticks with different colored smoke have got to be addictive or long term lethal.

In a world that lives fast, dies young over and over and over and over such things normally destructive would probably find a good hold with the masses who need that "high" in their life.

Stims and drugs are a way to get it, I would also imagine that people might become addicted to the feeling of certain nano programs that MUST feel different because of the changes in your body they produce.

I'm beginning to think people asking for Iron Circle really don't care if its improving their strength of stamina...

Then there's the natural items on the planet. How many people have read the descriptions of some of the fruit items on this planet that you get from food vendors? Well some of it is poisonous which probably leads to interesting side effects as weak poisons do, and some of it is Incredibly enriched in notum and probably would give hallucinogenic side effect to nanos and the sensitives.

I've used some fruit before like that.

The experiemental medicines you can pick up for mission assignments are good to have in the inventory for such things in roleplay too.


If anthropologists are correct in their theories that the primary reason human development switched from nomadic to pastoral or stationary was to cultivate drugs (with bread being a side effect) then I see no reason in the course of our evolution to abandon such a hoary tradition.

Drugs for healing or harm add a dimension to explore in the dregs and heroic depths of the human condition and triumphs and learning can be a lesson to us all whether virtual or real.
