First off. It the traders that sell wrangles for 100ks.

Almost every class can sell their buffs, Traders for their wrangles (formerly the comp lit.), Mps for their masteries, Adventurs for their polymorphing, Docs their treatment, agents their sence/agil buff.

All these professions arn't really all that strong os running missions so to compensate most sell their buff to make the credits they need. Other people are glad to pay because it lets them do things that they normalls couldn't. A doctor's +80 treatment buff (average price of 200k) will let people put in implants 20 levels higher then they could.

The most expensive and one of the more popular ones are the trader wrangles. They allow for overequipping of weapons and for the pet classes to cast really tough pets. Thos are the ones you seed going for hundreds of thousands (I charge 350-400k and make a fair business at it.) Some people if they are desperate have paid me upto 1 million for a +132 wrangle and have been more then exstatic (sp?) to get it.

None of these buffs cost credits to cast (to the best of my knowledge), the payment is more of a 'Thankyou' for the service they lets you do all these things. Traders tend to get so much though because it costs millions to get the buffs and the buffs to use them (7.3mil for all the ones i needed for a +132 wrangle) THEN they have to goto an area with low level monster jsut to cast them. One top of that these wrangles don't work on traders so they are only good for using on other people. Most other peole can cast them on the spot and use these buffs on themselves.

Preface: I have neither and adventurer, trader nor MP character! Nor do I have a Soldier or a Doctor or any other profession that is constantly bombarded with buff requests. I have only my lowish level (126) Engineer self, so I do not sell buffs, cuz I have nothing to sell. On the other hand, I sometimes do take tips for implant creation, but not unless the implants is at least level 80ish (used to be 50ish).

First, from what I understand, Adventures don't sell wrangles, Traders do, but that's a minor point.

The primary generic reason any player would take money for anything is to make money. A second generic reason would be to discourage future requests.

After that, the reasoning is really just a personal thing, I am sure.

I have noticed that more Traders charge than MPs (being an engy I am most familiar with requesting these buffs). But if you think about it, it makes sense. The type of person that plays a primary character of a trader, may have similar traits irl or be roleplaying the character well. Traders are supposed to be interested in making a profit. MPs are supposed to be more in tune to the balance of nature, not profit, so whether it is an extension of their rl persona or good roleplaying, it fits. This is not to say that there is anything wrong with a Trader giving freebies nor a MP taking tips. Its just how they play the game.

Although I don't enjoy paying (who would), I do understand the extended economics of it.

First, it does cost them something. They must buy nano kits to have the nano energy to give the buffs.

Second, they must spend the time buffing themselves to give the buff and then buffing you. Valuable time they could be using to level or obtain important items.

If this cost seems out of line with what they charge, consider the concepts of supply-and-demand and value-added-retailing.

Supply and Demand: There are no where near as many buffers as there are those wanting their buffs. Those that want to go to the head of the line offer to pay and pay well. Low Supply + High Demand drives prices up!

Value Added Retailing: The buffer consumes resourses and supplies me (the buffy?) with a product, a buff. The actual value of this buff which creates its market price is what I get out of the buff. As an engineer, If I buff myself to build a bot, I can do missions that will net me, oh say, 500,000 to 1,000,000 in credits and loot. If I get Mochams from an MP and/or a good Wrangle from a Trader, I can up that to 2,000,000 to 5,000,000 mission. Yes, alot of that increase is of my own effort, but the net value added by the buffs is somewhere between 1,500,000 and 4,500,000. A couple of hundred thousand credits does seems like that bad a split of the spoils. [Note: These have all been rough estimates, I have never actually kept track.]

I will add that I have never met a player that charged for buffs in team and would probably leave the team if I did.

So, basically, if you want the buff, you have three choices.
  1. Suck it up and fork over the dough, they have every right to it.
  2. Develop a close group of friends that you always play with that will never charge you, always be there when you want to play, never get sick, never go on vacation, never have network issues, never go to work, don't have lives.
  3. Suck it up and fork over the dough, they have every right to it.

Charging vs. Tipping
I have nothing against people charging for buffs. Its their buff, they want cash, you pay - simple really.

But its interesting that as a genralisation, some profs charge and some classes ask for tips. For example, the majority of traders I've seen advertising wrangles have a set price, whereas the majority of MPs seem to either give masteries away free, or ask for a tip. As an NT, I get asked for HE all the time, and the majority of people don't even think I want a tip - its a "non-payment" buff. Wrangles are "payment" buffs where even those given free are usually tipped for. As I said, interesting.

As a side note, tradeskills are not covered by this tipping / charging rule. NTs have one of the most frequently used skills - nano prog. I ask for tips, but would never charge for my services. I've never seen anyone charging for any tradeskill, but most ask for tips. I do know of v high NTs charging for implant work - but thats countered by the insanely high reqs of building ql200 imps.

Just an observation.
