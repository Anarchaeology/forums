So you want to hear what goes on with the storyline while "in game" ... idea
Just a little idea that poped into my head while roaming the boards...

A lot of people complain that the storyline is missing from our daily expericence in AO, and i totally agree. Appart from personally witnessing the event in question and be online at the same time. The only way you'll know about what happened and goes on/what to look for, is to read the storyline on AO's website or download the small movies that they make.

Now, i'm not bashing the great storyline so far or bringing down the efforts made by Funcom to build a good story. I just think that there are ways that they could implement a news system in game so everyone can know what's going on and feel a little more an integral part of Rubi-Ka. ( i personally feel that to have to go to AO's website to find out what's happened in game is a turn-off for immersiveness)

Here are some of the things that i could see beeing used as information tools for the community:

-Add some NPC's in the bars that talk about the events of note
that happened in Rubi-Ka, make the bars places of gossip for
us news deprived players. Would also make a good reason for
people to go and hang out there since it would be a good
place to learn about what's going on.

-Add newsreader booths or msg boards accessible in game, or
maybe a scrolling marquee that reads out the headlines of
events that happened.

-Make it so people have a clue to who is who on Rubi-Ka and
what mischeef they've been up to (well the public stuff at
least =).

These changes would not be too hard to implement and would make a world of difference in the immersiveness of the game. More interaction with NPC's that would make them feel more a living part of the world than as lamposts. As it is people interact more with mission terminals and shop booths than they do with the world.

With these small changes, i can see for example me walking in Trade district of Omni-1 and as i go by on my way to a shop i see a scrolling markee sign saying something about the resignation of Anthony "Cogs" McDuff ... (the President of Rubi-Ka Freedom Fighters for those not in the know). Now i might be puzzled about this but go on my business... only to stop by Baboons for a drink, and there not 5 feet away from me 2 npc's are talking about his resignation and the details on how it happened...

Just with these small additions i am more involved in what goes on around me =). True it's a passive participation since i was not there to witness the events but i'm still participating in an immersive world.
