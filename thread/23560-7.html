Adventurers Changes
I have not read all of the posts but I did get upto page 6 or so before formulating an opinion. I really really don't understand why advs are getting so excited over the announced changes.

1) Damage shields....Damage Shields are useless against the type of MOBS most players fight. What good is it to hit a MOB for 51 or so hp when they have 10K hp? I don't see this as useful. Good for PVP perhaps, I don't know however since I don't like PVP and avoid it (PVP in AO is just plain stupid imo). Second, they are supposed to absorb x amount of damage but again whats the point? They don't even absorb enough damage to block a single attack from a MOB. So now they are going to recycle every 2 mins...geez who cares. To top it all off, they are going to be self-only??

How about increasing the damage of all shields by 3x, forget about the absorb part and the new lines altogether. This is so much easier to do and might make our shields useful.

2) Weapon buffs and multi-wield buffs. Nice, but then again they are self-only. They are also going to force all adv to be the same. What????? you mean not all adv use riders and pistols??? Can't be!! As a hammer wielding adv, this does nothing for me (yes 2hb is aqua). Now I would like these buffs alot, even though they wouldn't help me, if I could cast them on others. This at least would make me valuable in a team. Funcom is completely ignoring the importance of team dynamics. The weapons buff lines as stated (self-only) will not help us get into groups easier.

What I would like to see is the self-only part of the nanos removed and the silly requirements for riders changed so they are easier for us to use and not ip hogs. As for the pistols....easy fix...BRING BACK THE ORIGINAL FREEDOM ARMS PISTOLS and make them adv only if necessary.

3) Where are the AC shields???????? Why was the line not extended past supreme??????

4) Where are our runspeed buffs?????? Why was the line not extended past +100????? Yes I know morphs will do this but NOBODY WANTS OUR MORPHS except for parrot to get into the den or maybe dragon just to try out for interest. First useful thing Funcom could do is change their stupid position that getting into smugs etc. using parrot is an exploit. That is just plain dumb. Parrot form is the only real buff request I get from people. Second useful thing funcom can do is fix yalms. All yalms, regardless of ql, have the speed of a ql200 plane. Don't believe me? Look at the runspeed bonus on a ql30 yalm and a high level yalm. Now all of a sudden our morphs (bird and wolf in particular) are very very useful.

5) Dragon form? Bonuses to dragon form? Again who cares (unless perhaps you are into pvp)? NOBODY HUNTS OUTSIDE ANYMORE. Where do most people spend their time to get xp??? In missions or perhaps in static missions to get goodies. So much for dragon form.

6) The changes to wolf form and sabretooth form sound interesting. We will have to wait and see how well these work but for goodness sake reduce their sizes. I get so many ppl asking me to drop my morph in missions because it blocks their view and targetting. I get the request despite my efforts to always move aside in a battle so I don't block others.

7) Pet Leet???? You gotta be kidding me right? Instead of giving us something useful you are going to waste programmers time to allow us to tame leets? Funcom employees must have been smoking crack when they came up with that idea. The idea that is much much much better has been stated by several ppl in a number of posts..... Allow us to charm any and all wildlife MOB critters. Could you imagine at say lvl50 or 60 (whatever) walking along with a hammer in tow???? Now that would be cool and actually useful!

8) What about changes to our healing nanos? We don't heal as well as mp pets or traders but we are supposed to be good at healing? How about eliminating the team heal lines altogether and making our single person heal lines team heal lines (more like the MA). That would make us very good at healing indeed.

9) Wasted green skills.... Many of our green skills are skills that are essentially useless. Swimming, adventuring, water and land vehicles???? Bah! Change some of the other skills to aqua or green to compensate. For example, it seems we are being setup as the class that can readily spot agents (ie we are getting some serious perception buffs). So why is our perception skill blue (dark blue?)? Since many adv use riders why not lighten up some of the dark blue skills required to use them?

Sorry, I just think funcom has completely missed the boat with respect to what adventurers need. Funcom just does not seem to understand group and player dynamics. I don't want fluff and things that make you say neato the 1st few times you see them then are completely useless thereafter. I want serious hardcore changes that improve our ability to pvm (pvp too if you are into that sort of thing). I want skills and nanos that make it so teams that are forming say 'HEY there's an adv lfg....get him/her quick!' not 'Yeah there's an adv...we will get him/her if we can't find a doc or we can't find anyone else to join the group'.

Anyways that is my take on the proposed changes......
