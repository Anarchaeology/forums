The Fall Classic of 2003
(( OOC: Well I'm a sports fan and just figured hell why not. This is a really odd post for me, I would LOVE for some IN CHARACTER back and forth, if this works out I'll start a few more. I'm also using my alt Nexeus for most of this. Remember this is an In Character thing and enjoy))

In an age and world of leet-ball, technology simulated sports, and Olympics that included flying cars, Nexeus stood glared to his Holi-vision(tm) where he watched games from Old Earth. Nexeus would follow the ancient sporting schedule, watching a game on particular days, and times as they were given to him on disks to watch. Every Sunday from the beginning of August, he would watch the game of Football at either 1 pm or 4:15 pm or at 9pm or on Monday at 9 pm and the possible Thursdays or Saturdays, depending on how it was scheduled. He would also watch the pregame and post game information, something that was called ESPN and Sportscenter. But one of his most favorite moments was something that was called The World Series, and during October, based on the Old Earth schedule, he would watch the ending of the baseball season deemed the World Series. Even though he still could not understand why it was called a "World Series" when the rest of the world did not participate, but one could guess that it was only something that you had to understand being on old earth of that age and time.

"Nexeus, what are you doing. Why are you watching those Old Earth sports?" Zane would ask him as he would visit him in his Luxury Apartment in Jobe

"You have to understand cuz, there's a beauty to this, it's a time when machines, technology and implants did not waterdown a sport. This is where a guy took his raw self, his raw ability and fought against another guy. The winner was stronger because they were able to throw, catch, pitch, receive, run faster than the other," Nexeus replied looking at Zane turning away from the holi-vision terminal as the Florida Marlins began the second inning against the New York Yankees in Game two of the World Series.

"Okay, I guess I can understand that, but why watch it. You watch it at the exact same time they play it, at the exact moment, at the exact day. That was thousands of years ago. How you got the full broadcasts as well is way beyond my knowledge or understanding... but why not just search the grid for the winner and move on?"

"Because I want to live it out bro... you don't understand, living out how those fans must have felt at that time when the Yankees lost in the first game, or how Jets fans felt when they started as 0 and 4, or how the Knicks fans feel every season. That's passion, that's heart man. I rather watch the game as if it's going on (of course if two games over lap, I'll just watch one at a time), and feel the same way as the fans did."

Zane could only shrug as his explanation but looked at his cousin continue to watch the game with enthusiasm. "You need help Nexeus... maybe it's just an Old Earth thing, I me-"

Zane was caught off as Nexeus soon then hollered "HOME RUN" as Zane only shook his head and left Nexeus to his own entertainment. Even with the explanation, he still did not understand how Nexeus could enjoy himself.

Nexeus was further glued to the tube, so-to-speak as Pettitte was pitching a shut out into the ninth where the Marlins scored a run. At that time it did not matter though, the score was 6-1 and Nexeus has full faith in "his" Yankees that they would not collapse. The Yankees now remained 3-0 in game two's that post season, having always lost the first game. With the series tied 1-1 they now head over to Florida where they face the Marlins at home.
