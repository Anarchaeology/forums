ok this wasn't my idea, (i forgot who's it was, anyways..)

I don't think title's or PvP Status should be given to anyone that kills someone on the same side of the conflict.

Think about it. you see someone walkin around, and notice that they have a PvP title, you'll say "Hey right on man, you must've killed alota omni to get that, "

---- this system would allow, clanners to duel eachother (or omnis to duel eachother), on a MUCH friendler basis, allow for testing of weapons/nanos/etc.. without the grief----
