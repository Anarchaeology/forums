Bombing in Tir! Suspect Identified!
A bomb rocked Tir tonight, across from the Happy Rebel. Rumour was a Omni Tek offical was seeing his illicit lover, but the apartment was found to be empty when the bombs went off. No injuries, and no dead. Minor interior damage.

However. One witness says she claims to have seen the bomber.

"He was pale. A nano Mage. Bald. Wearing Blue armor, looked like that Hanshi stuff. He kept talking to "Carl" telling him to shut up. He ran like heck after he left something in the mail recieve area."

A scan by a nearby insurance reclaim revealed the bomber to be identified as "Carl 'Lurai' Shone." He is armed, extremly dangerous, and considered mentally deranged. Use extreme caution.
