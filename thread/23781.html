Fix or freezing and crashing in populous areas: low-quality avatars
I have a frequent problem with getting completely frozen when entering a densely-populated area. Others have told me in-game that they have the same problem.

When I arrive at a spot where there are a lot of people, my hard drive starts spinning like crazy and my character completely freezes. Sometimes I'm stuck for over a minute before I can move.

Places like NL and Tir are typically killer to walk through. I recently went to Broken Shores for the first time and was nearly unable to move--my teammates ended up warping me to the missions rather than waiting for me to get control back. Last night I attempted to participate in the fight with Omni's invasion of Majestic Bridge and Newland. I was frozen most of the time, ended up killed because I was unable to respond to anything, and ultimately ended up crashed/dropped from server.

It seems to me that the big problem is characters are fairly detailed and have a lot of textures and polygons to keep track of, in addition to the fact that unlike structures they're constantly moving. When an area is overpopulated, I think all those character details overwhelm my virtual memory and cause me to freeze/crash.

With web pages, sometimes sites include a low-res image which downloads quickly and appears right away, and then the high quality images build in slowly and eventually replace the low-res ones. I wonder if this technique would help reduce the frequent crashes and freezes.

For example, instead of loading ALL the chars in an area whole, you would load, in order:
1. Full details of your teammates and pets, if you have any
2. Full details of whatever character/mob you have targeted
3. Full details for anyone who's attacking you, if appropriate
4. A set number of characters/mobs (5? 10?) that are closest in proximity to your character in.
5. Low quality avatars (a single flat polygon, roughly anthropomorphic-shaped, little or no texture, along with the data for name, level, profession, side, breed, guild, etc.)

Then the game would let the keyboard and mouse have a turn at the control loop before trying to download anything else. The details for the rest of the chars would download in the background, with the closest undetailed player being downloaded between each player control action. Anyone you target is immediately loaded.

Yes, the game wouldn't look as good sometimes, but I don't think there's any doubt game responsiveness is more important than graphics. I would take a game with 1-bit graphics over one that constantly freezes and crashes any day.
