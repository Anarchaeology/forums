I totally agree. Those Miiir fashions are slim pickins, and when it comes to trying to make your character unique, you have maybe a few dozen outfits to play with. Most of the female outfits are trashy, and my female character wears the male suit so she's not a skank.

The cloaks look like christening robes or mumus, and the lack of overall variety is really discouraging. What about some hats? :P Just helmets without the armor. There are few or no *nice* shorts, cloaks (over the shoulders?), casual clothes, skirts, and most of the colors are hot neons and ZEBRA PRINT!

I mean, isn't there an eye implant for fashion sense? How about an eye implant for Color Blindness Reduction?

Yes, there is a need for the Miiir fashion store. They provide the ONLY civilian-like clothing for the entire planet. (Notice that there are Miiir shops in clan, neutral and omni? What about trade embargos?) It would be great if each of these shops was different than the other, though. It would make running to Trade for some new pants worth the trip. It would make things less predictable. Everyone has sunglasses, everyone has a cloak. It gets old.

Also, characters shouldn't always have to dress like they just walked out of the Matrix. It was cool, sure. How about some (tasteful) color for men? What about some stylish and professional-looking outfits?

I don't like having a character that is a complete Power-Gaming hulk. Customization and a little bit of style can go a long way towards expressing one's personality, which in turn, allows the player to become attached to the character and get involved with roleplay and the storyline.

Bottom line: No more zebra print, get more anything. :P
