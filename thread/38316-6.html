From what I've read, I'm led to believe that anyone with the skills will be able to make A Kamakaze bot, but engineers will be able to make the same kamakaze bot again, and again, and again, by using a nano-program made from a snapshot of the prototype.

Raymote, my idea, which I've posted elsewhere as well, was to give the traders an enhanced version of the window for buying, so they could see how much it really was worth, without having to alt-tab to look it up on a self-generated table or web-database, then buy it. The part that didn't make it into this posting was for the autobuy capability to be opened up to ANYBODY...provided ONE of the parties was a trader. So your trader could buy merchandise from other players from zones away, and then sell it to other players from zones away.

Two scenarios. In one a conventional player asks me how much I'm asking for the watchamacalit. I don't really know, as I don't deal with the thing, so I tell him some nice round number which is either woefully low or ridiculously high. Let's assume he accepts. Next question, where to meet to make the exchange.

In the other, a box pops up telling me that an improved trader is offering x amount for my item. He also /tells me he'll give me x amount for the watchamacalit, and that it is about 10 times what I could get for it in the shop, (probably omitting that the shop, if it had one, would sell it for 20 times that,) I accept. Transfer made. No muss, no fuss. Much harder to miss in chat window spam.

In the other side of the second scenario, that same trader puts the item up for sale, stating quite clearly that he'll part with it for, oh say 1.5 times what he paid me for it. (Let's assume that he alsk knows that it IS carried in a shop, so he can't charge "full price", he's trying to snare a bargain hunter. One bites...from the middle of some out in the back of nowhere zone without access to a whompa or grid terminal. THAT player, because he's dealing with an improved trader, gets the same interface the trader had used to buy the item in the first place.

Basically, traders would be able to buy and sell across zones with less hassle.

Actually, just give traders a /trade command that they can drop a reference into, or specify cash. Or both.


/trade nameofsomebody watchamacalit 1000

"You have been offered a watchamacalit for 1000 by Trader" (or just open up the trade screen would probably be better. best would be both. The message and the trade window opening)

click on watchamacalit (By doing this, the client presumably can tell both the what and the who, so those fields would not need to be filled as in the previous example.)
/trade 5000000
"You have been offered 5000000 for your watchamacalit by Trader".

Of course, if you did this, you'd definately need to make a test to ensure the item was still in the offerers inventory before completing the transaction. Otherwise, it could theoretically be possible to sell items you have never had. But then again, if it opened the trade window to do this, and supplied the traders component straight into the window, the other player would still have to drag and drop, so maybe it wouldn't be as hard to do.

How about THIS for an idea?

The idea has been brought up before about giving your bot a weapon. I like the idea. However, that might get expensive giving your bot weapons all the time. It might get difficult too. I think there should be a way to make a "bot setup" and then apply the setup to your bot. It might work like this:

1) Apply data for a weapon to a MRR. For this example we will use a Premium Support Beam. The result of this is a "MRR (Premium Support Beam droid modification)."

2) Modify a Trimmer so that it will add the necessary skills to your droid. Seperate Trimmers for seperate skills might be annoying but would definitely be easier to code than being able to make customized trimmers.

3) Use the droid shell

4) Use the trimmer(s) on your droid

5) use your MRR on your droid. At this point it will do a skills check on your droid to see if it has the skill to wield the beam. If it does, then it will now be equipped with a Premium Support Beam.

The process should be such that it either drains AC from the bot or possibly hp. It would also be nice if they made a complete set of menus to check out your droid's stats instead of just treating it like a regular pet. Engie bots are supposed to be basically permanent. Also, since engies have beacon warp, there should be a way for Engies to warp their droid to them.

Yeah I know, coding this would be insanely tough. There might be easier ways to go about doing it. But I really think it would add a ton to the game if engies could do this. It would also be awsome if there was an entire line of tradeskill-made bots. These bots would be much much weaker than regular engie bots and wouldn't be customizable after creation (trimmers won't work). Why have them you say? They would be tradeable. It should be made to require some skill to use like QT, or maybe a combo of Mechanical Engineering and Electrical Engineering. That way it would require extra IP just like any other weapon. Wen-Wens are almost like this already, so I don't think tradeable bots are too much of a stretch. For one thing it would make engies rich, that's for sure.
