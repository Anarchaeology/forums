Windows Hots Keys
I think it would be *great*, and I don't think I'm alone on this, if AO had the option to disable Windows Hot Keys. As a software developer, I know this is easy, but at the same time probably not top on the to-do list. However, I think I am one of many who have cursed themselves, their computers, their dogs, etc, when in the middle of hunting you hit the Windows Key on your keyboard, only to be kicked out of the game, leaving your computer in an unstable state.
