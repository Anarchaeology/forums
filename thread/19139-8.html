Thank you all
I have really enjoyed reading these posts for a number of reasons.

1. They are very funny. I am sorry that your frustration is my humor but I am sure you are laughing along with me (now).

2. They are very informative. My main is an MP and I solo a lot. However when I team I try to be very thoughtful to the other people.

It is my sincerest hope that no one who teams with me feels about me the way some of these people make others feel.

One thing that has not been mentioned much but should. Skill and knowledge of one's job is important. I read these forums to pick up on tricks I never knew. It was from these that I learned about the agg-def bar (always had it at 50% before), the /assist, the team window for tracking (I have always used Function keys), and many other things.

What I did not learn here that I feel contributes to a good team mate is respect and manners. I don't charge team mates because it would be rude and tactless. I don't loot chests that are not mine because it is impolite and, IMHO, stealing. If the team has a good leader then I follow, if not then I offer to lead (as a decision maker not a tank/puller/etc.) If my chest has something someone else needs, I give it to them. Good teams work on more than one level, bad teams can fall apart at any one level and cause frustration.

Thank you for all of the good advice.
