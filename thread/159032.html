Crats are good because they can calm mobs, and charm them as well. A lucky crat has 3 pets; his/her robot and 2 charmed mobs, that's pretty hard to beat. Still, a crat is pretty weak so if a mob ignores the pets and goes for the crat, and the calm fails, you're toast.

MP's have 3 pets, but normally only use 2. One attack pet and one heal pet (the third, the mez pet, doesnt work, ahem, very well). So the MP sends in the attack pet and the heal pet heals it, which makes it... tough. Still, same as the crat, weak in HP and if they get aggro they normally cant take it for long.

MA's is another type of profession. He's a combat machine himself and does not use pets at all. A MA will do good, even great damage with his fists, he has HP enough to live for a while and he can heal himself. Also he has good evades so if your lucky the mobs wont hit you much, they sure wont crit you much anyways. The MA is also cheap to run, no need to buy new weapons, they grow with your levels and the number of nanos you'll have to buy is a lot less then the Crat/MP.

If you want to solo I would only reccomend one of the pet classes if you are able to give it a load of start up money.

A crat can solo quite easily for most of their life but they are expensive to keep indipendant. I have a level 40ish Crat now whilest learning the trade and keeping her in updated bots she had spent 1,000,000 creds (plus what ever she had made for herself) by the time she was 20! I think Crat is the hands down best soloer. There's nothing quite like soloing VBs, 8x8, then borgs However, they are very difficult to play. Unless you really know what you're doing and have a strong grasp of game mechanics, I'd say that crat is not the prof for you.

MAs and Advs are the easiest classes to solo. As a MA you're a buzzsaw at lower levels. Your damage output combined with your heals and evades make you an extremely good solo char. Both are a lot of fun and are active combat classes to play.

Crat requires a lot more juggling and can be quite taxing to maintain your charms, mezzes and pets long enough that you live through the experience. But, it may be the most fun I've ever had with a toon.

I noticed no one said Fixer's, are they no good at soloing? I was thinking of rolling up a Fixer but if they're not good soloers, I really don't wanna waste any time leveling one.


Fixers are very good soloers. Automatic healing (2 strains of stacking HoTs), enough NCU to hold all your own buffs and then some, a little extra damage, wonderful speed/evades and decent crowd control (roots/snares) which work well if you are soloing, but not so well in a team when you can't get others to leave the room.

Fixers also have the advantages of being able to get from anywhere to the grid quickly - and after talking to Ofoz, getting from a grid terminal TO anywhere quickly. Combined with our speed and other abilities, fixers are great at blitzing and thus its easy to get cash to buy the few things you can't blitz for yourself.

Juggling your nanoskills (all dark blue) isn't fun though, and you kind of don't have much IP room for error until very late in the game. All in all a great soloer class.

I think I've solo'd most of my fixers carreer, and only now in the 100+ game is it getting more beneficial to team up.
