Actually, as far as I know, merit board upgrades simply reflect your rank within your faction.

For example, the ranks for Omni-Tek employees are the following:

Omni-Tek Award - Basic Board (1)
Omni-Tek Award - Ribbon of Strife (2)
Omni-Tek Award - Double Ribbons of Skirmish (3)
Omni-Tek Award - Triple Ribbons of Battle (4)
Omni-Tek Award - Flag of Sacrifice (5)
Omni-Tek Award - Double Flags of Righteousness (6)
Omni-Tek Award - Triple Flag of Humility (7)
Omni-Tek Award - Flags of Glory and Redemption (8)


As far as I know, your rank doesn't really do much outside of giving your the bonuses from wearing the upgraded merit board. It doesn't have anything to do with being able to wear various items unless those items have a token requirement of some kind. For example, the Omni-Tek Armor item has a 10 Omni token requirement on it.


Your characters profession title changes based on the following scheme which is strictly character level based and are the following:

Title Level 1 - Level 1
Title Level 2 - Level 15
Title Level 3 - Level 50
Title Level 4 - Level 100
Title Level 5 - Level 150
Title Level 6 - Level 190

Of course, they may have changed something...
