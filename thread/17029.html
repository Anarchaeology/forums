Mission Treasures
I am relatively new to AO, but have played Asheron's Call and Everquest for over a year each (now Rubi-Ka is the only world for me). I think over all this is the best online game I have ever played but I have a couple of things that bother me. The first is that mission treasures should be more profession specific...Mt MA character is level 52 and has over 90 tokens (but many more missions where no tokens were awarded due to finishing before killing enough mobs, my fault not yours) and maybe 3 or 4 of them actually had MA type treasures. The other MAIN problem is spending way too much time trying to unlock an EMPTY chest. Now lets face it guys no one keeps an empty chest locked (except maybe a paranoid schizo).
I have 5 or 6 characters of different professions and find more treasures geared to them than to MA's.
