quote:
Originally posted by Ysrafel
Right...I'm Omni, but that answers my question anyway. Thanks.
You should be able to do the same in Trade. Just bounce between the advanced and superior stores.

quote:
Another couple of general questions; how far up/down in terms of cluster QL should I be willing to stray from the QL implant I'm trying to compile?
A cluster can be as much as 15% lower than the implant or as high as you want it to be. So a ql 85 cluster would fit into a ql 100 implant as would a ql 160 cluster. Keep in mind, though, the higher the cluster the higher the ql of your finished implant will be. This means higher treatment and secondary ability to equip.

quote:
Also, the abilities/skills given by the implant do depend on the clusters used as opposed to the QL the implant ends up being, yeah?
Ending requirements are completely dependent on final ql of implant after all clusters are in. So unless you have a lot of points in the secondary stat, try not to get clusters that are too high above the ql of the implant you are trying to use.

I wish this topic had been discussed before, last week I spent quite awhile searching through this forum on how to lay out my first *full* set!

If you don't start out with alot of credits (I didn't) then you can get by on a smaller subset of the implant combinations mentioned above. You'll be levelling so fast that it won't matter much anyway.


Here's a simple scheme that I used up to around level 40. I maxxed AGI/SENSE/INT. The setup is low cost (4 implants, 2 shining clusters), and is entirely Agility based. It focuses on weapon skills.

Format is:
LOCATION (SECONDARY REQ): Shining / Bright / Faded

R ARM (AGILITY): SMG /Chem AC /Radiation AC
CHEST (AGILITY): Sense / - / SMG
R WRIST (AGILITY): - / Burst / -
R HAND (AGILITY): - / SMG / Burst


At level 40 I decided to add some to my abilities so I could equip the armor sets that I felt obliged to blitz every few levels. =) The general advice is that you should swap out ability implants for a skill implant set, but at low-mid levels you change armor so often, plus I still had no money. I added the following to my list above (note it's still entirely Agility-based). You can substitute whatever nano cluster you want into the head, I chose MM because I hadn't been raising it as much.

HEAD (AGILITY): Matter Met. / - / Sense
LEG (AGILITY): Agility / Evade Close Combat / -
FEET (AGILITY): - / Agility / -

At around lvl 50 I added some QL 90 nano ability implants to the list above. I was able to skip QL90 gun implants since I'd be using a Gamma Ejector to lvl 60-ish, at which point I could upgrade to 125s. Note the entire set still is agility-based.

EAR (AGILITY): - / Concealment / Psy Mod
EYE (AGILITY): - / Sense Imp. / -

*****

At level 107 I finally decided to get a set with health, evades, AC, and runspeed, almost the whole nine yards. After trolling the boards for non-GA implant schemes, I went with the following. I had only been raising stamina in order to max body dev., so I had to finally dump some more IP into STA. Luckily it is very easily buffable, but even so the 2 stamina-req implants had to be lower QL than the rest.

EYE (SENSE): Map Nav / Sense Imp / Timespace
HEAD (AGILITY): Sense Imp / - / Sense
EAR (AGILITY): - / Concealment / Psy Mod
R ARM (AGILITY): SMG /Chem AC /Radiation AC
CHEST (STAMINA): Max Health / Mat Met / SMG
L ARM (SENSE): - / Radiation AC / Mat Met
R WRIST (AGILITY): - / Burst / -
WAIST (SENSE): Chem AC / Max Health / Bio Met
L WRIST (AGILITY): - / Runspeed / Nano Resist
R HAND (AGILITY): - / SMG / Ranged Init
LEG (AGILITY): Dodge Ranged / Evade Close Combat / Max Health
L HAND (STAMINA): - / Fire AC / Cold AC
