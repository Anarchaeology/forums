ICC Space Station
ICC Space Station

Pictures like this defiantly make me want to chill on a space station.

An all-indoor dungeon, without mobs (except for maybe a few level 1 thugs in a forgotten cargo bay).
Players would see ships docking and leaving (ie: watching a ship through a window approach the station, then flying through a force field wall in the bay.)
Many windows, half of which have a view of rubi-ka.
Notum containers being launched from the surface might be seen from one side of the station(?)
Maybe the new character creation could be seen from a few windows. (ie: NPC "new arrivals" boarding a ship that disappears in the distance, or real players, whatever is possible)
A Station lounge would be great.
A promenade, with various venders. (im thinking DS9)
Even if it were a small zone to explore, the point is it would be in space! Thats all that matters. Its a sci-fi game after all, and there isn't one bit of OuterSpace in it.

Story Explanation:
The ICC's presence on rubi-ka could now be seen in space.
and
Its the first thing you see in character creation for crying out loud, what more reason do you need to make a space station zone?

Its the setting, and the little details that make it so great. Its a crane moving cargo around, the little cleaning droids, station-announcements in a muffled voice you can understand, an NPC drinking at table in the back, NPCs boarding and unloading from transports. Its just fun to be around. To be immersed in a station atmosphere.

/wishful thinking
