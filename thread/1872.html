Hmmm. Well, take a look at it from a different perspective. By the time you hit 125, most are already fighting the toughest mobs in the game as groups. When you hit 150, you're still fighting the same mobs. So you should be stronger right?

That seems to be the way it works out. People continue to fight meds and vets as groups, they just kill them faster and with a slightly better margin of safety.

There are many ultra-nasties that no one seems to play with much. I don't see anyone aspiring to stand toe to toe with an Ace (is it possible?) or those higher level mantises, not to mention that other goofy stuff that hits for ludicrous damage. It's just not very efficient experience.

So unless the gremlins at Funcom are tinkering with the mobs, I'd say it gets slightly easier as you go... as long as you accept the fact that you won't ever solo again and resign yourself to fighting the same mobs over and over with a group.

Of course, the game is incredibly stale by that point.
