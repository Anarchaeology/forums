Well, I guess I'll answer my own thread in case any other newbies like me are curious. It turns out the mission terminal I was using in the backyard of the Omni-Tek newbie area only gives out the "find So-and-so" missions. Once I left the newbie area and entered Rome Blue, the mission terminals there offered all kinds of missions.
