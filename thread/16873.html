One of the many possible solutions would be to randomize the spawn of MOB's that drop precious items. I've spoken at length with several long-time MMO players that are friends of mine, and our general consensus (although we're not like MMO gods or anything, or the authority, but hey) is that randomizing spawn location and timing would COMPLETELY negate camping problems. Say, there's 5 locations hand-picked in the world where this guy *might* spawn (i.e. let him spawn in about a 10000 foot radius from the hand-picked spot, within reason), in a time-window of 2 hours to 20 hours. It would take a vast amount of people to even think about camping this person, however, you COULD go on a hunt for them and try to find them.

A nice addition to that would be some form of indicator, some NPC, that when asked, will tell you either A: "Yeah, I saw that guy wandering around a few minutes ago" (meaning he's still alive) or B: "It's a good thing they took that guy out... he was a real menace" (i.e. dead) or something to that effect...

That way, you don't waste time looking for him if he's dead, and people will stop camping the hell out of static spawns. Static spawns are bad, no matter how you look at it. Even in static dungeons, loot should be randomized, or spawns should be randomized, or both. That way, returning to the area presents a fresh challenge and experience each time you come back.

If we wanted to play EQ, we would pay to play their crappy game. Please take the word "Static" out of the Rubi-Ka vocabulary.

~Nietya

If I have the most precious gem in the world (in real life, mind you) I could give it to some bum, or toss it in a gutter. I could. I could own more than one of them, and I could give them away, etc. etc. NODROP was a band-aid used to try to patch a gaping chest-wound, and as such, should NEVER be adopted by any other companies. Again, I point the accusatory finger at EQ.

So, in addition, please remove the NODROP and UNIQUE tags from any item in the game, and just make it so mission tokens automagically load into your board when you recieve one... there's no reason not to, honestly, and that'll keep people from duping them.
