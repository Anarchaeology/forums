The Leet Fighting Debate
A formal debate will be held on the subject of legalising Leet fighting on Sunday, December 2 at 21:00 GMT (4 PM EST) at Galway Castle (Rubi-Ka 1) next to the mansion (north side of the Galway Castle 2453,1315).

Speakers from the World Leet Fighting Federation (WLFF) and the Friend Leet Foundation (FLF) will be presenting their respective cases to the audience who will then vote. for or against the motion. Light refreshments will be served afterwards by the Galway pool and members of the public will have the opportunity to talk WLFF and FLF representatives.

"Leet fighting" is a sport which involves staging a fight between
two leets in an arena, often for the purpose of gambling. It was
originally conceived as a creative form of pest control among in notum mining communities. The sport however grew bigger and bigger and soon regional leagues were formed and commercial sponsorships deals agreed. Eventually these different leagues all joined up under the umbrella organisation WLFF.

Problems started for the sport when it became apparent promoters were actually breeding and training these leets for the fights. The training methods were particularly cruel. Documentary evidence shows test subjects being driven into a murderous frenzy by recordings of other Leets going "I ownz joo" played over and over again.

The practice of leet fighting was eventually banned by OT special directive RKSD-345-ZZZ9 as it was deemed barbaric and a bio-hazard. For the last 15 years WLFF was been lobbying for
the ban to be removed as well as fighting off allegations of arranging clandestine leet fight events.

More information on the Rubi-Ka 1 guild FLF can be found at http://www.ihastus.com/leet/

For other queries regarding the event, of if you have another hot not war related topic to debate, email gsoylent@hotmail.com or send Savoy a tell in-game.
