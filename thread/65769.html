A Face In The Crowd
The crowd inside The Cup was unusually large that night, drawn together for one event, Tristalyn and Threx were playing a game of truth or dare live on Gridstream. A figure in a long cloak and hood entered the bar and moved slowly through the shadows, his eyes steadily watching the back table, hoping to catch a glimpse of the woman he had come to see. Making his way to the back corner he finally found a clear view of her, Tristalyn, one of the DJ's from Gridstream that were hosting tonights little event. He watched her intently, never letting his eyes glance away from her for more than a moment. She was amazing to behold, glorious to him, the most beautiful woman alive and he had to have her. He feared to get close to her but wanted nothing more, she was his world, he never missed a chance to hear her voice carried to him over the grid, soothing his need for a time.

In the distance a man named Doncarnage was kneeling before an Atrox, performing some horrible act that the hooded man could not stand to watch, but the crowd seemed to love it. His eyes drifted back to her, he was deeply in love with her, all he wanted was to run up to her, take her into his arms, and rush with her out into the night. Slowly as the night progressed he made his way closer and closer to her through the crowd, her voice drawing him in. Then he stopped, watching the woman sitting next to Tristalyn lean over and kiss her, the womans hands roaming over Trist's body. His heart grew cold and he remembered the woman now, she was Xellina, the woman Tristalyn had married days before. How he hated her, she had claimed what he wanted dearly but could not have, not at the moment anyway. He would have his Tristalyn, the lovely Tristalyn, and if he had to kill Xellina to do so then so be it.

His eyes shown bright with hatred as his gazed at Xellina, drawing a blade from beneath his cloak as he moved through the crowd, but he stopped realising that if he killed Xellina in front of Tristalyn she would never be his. Slowly he sheathed his blade and sank back into the crowd, holding his killing blow off for another day. He would find Xellina alone one night, he would strike from the shadows, he would steal her life. First he must find someone that could hack her insurance and that would not be against a dead Mocker. He had much work to do before Tristalyn would be his, he loved her so and he knew that given time she would learn to love him. He had planned to kidnap her and force her to live with him, keeping her locked away from the world until she grew to love him as he knew she would.

For the rest of the night he watched, smiling as he listened to her sweat voice, and feeling a shiver run through his spine as she completed a dare that she struggled so hard to get out of. He loved her and would never rest until she was his forever, so he would wait, he would plan, and when the time was right he would act. Xellina's death would be his first task, to rid the world of the fraud she was, for no one could love Tristalyn as he did. Next he would steal Tristalyn away from this world and take her somewhere safe, where she wouldn't be harmed for not working for the Corporation, and teach her to love him. Then he would live forever happy and content, knowing that his life could get no better. There was much work to be done, reluctantly the hooded man stood and made his way out of The Cup, planning for the time when one would die and one would learn to love him as he did her.

/ooc

I thought the original post was excellent, in a creepy, unpleasant, "Silence of the Lambs" sort of way. However I can't help feeling most of the replies pretty much missed the point. The way I see it this post is not about the author telling you, the characters, that he has murderous plans but just a story as seen from the stalker's point of view and as such it is not something the characters involved would be aware of. In fact you could argue the whole point of the story is that the stalker decides not to make his move that day.

I also don't think this story was posted on a public form to tip off or provoke Xellina and her Mocker friends. It's there for the audience to enjoy in an ooc sort of way, maybe just a s a "mood piece" or maybe to foreshadow events in the future, who can say. As for whether the stalker could really kill Xellina or not, again this is missing the point. This story is viewed through the eyes of an insane stalker, not a dispassionate discussion regarding which player is more uber.

I guess it all comes down to the age old roleplaying issue of keeping separate what player knows from what the character knows. I appreciate this distinction is not always clear cut and as it is in any event subject to interpretation, different people will read things in different ways.
