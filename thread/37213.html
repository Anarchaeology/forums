PvP as a microcosm of society
Well, here I sit at work, 4:39, and I can't make myself do anything productive, so I'm going to post some thoughts I have had since I started PvP about a month or so ago.

Let's face it, when it comes down to it, a lot of people are complete a**holes. I think I'm a pretty nice guy, I have made lots of friends in the game since I started playing last January, and in general I am willing to give just about anyone the benefit of the doubt, but a high percentage of the lowbie PvPers are rude, dishonest, and in general don't have much honor (or much of a sense of humor) in the arena. Granted, I have been sucked into ganking people who act like jerks whenever I can, and I think I must have at least 20 people on /ignore at this point - all of whom I will gank on sight whether they are in rez, fighting, anything. A good majority of these people I can't even remember what they did to me in the first place, but if they are on /ignore it's a sure sign it was something really lame.

Take, for instance, Misterganker - this guy sits at the edge of the Tir arena and attacks anyone and everyone at any time for no reason. He runs straight for the door any time he goes below 1/2 health and talks major s**t constantly. Now, he has the right to do this, but damn - it is so lame! The sad part is, hes actually pretty good, and could probably do just as well without being a complete idiot.

Now, to my point - none of this is a suprise to me at all. Look at what goes on in RL, is it any wonder that the game is full of jerks, idiots, a**holes, title huggers, etc. etc? I would love it if there was a place where we could have fair duels where people couldn't run away and I wouldn't have to fight against their NCU belt! It would be even better if people would stand there and take it when they are losing a duel (MMD and 2HO aside - this is part of the game, there IS a war on). But, just like RL, people will do anything (including cheat) to get ahead and further their own interests (re: gain the next title level), so why should this behavior be a suprise to anyone? (maybe it's not)

Personally, I get almost as much enjoyment from listening/watching all the jerks and idiots. Sometimes they go too far (but thats what my /ignore list is for), and sometimes, occasionally, someone stands there and takes their punishment, and afterward you get a "good fight" or "nice duel" tell from them and for a minute I start to believe that maybe it's not so bad, then I see Misterganker (or one of his kind) again and it's back to the same old thing.

Anyway, just an observation. I can't say I haven't been a jerk, but I can say that I'm not always a jerk, and if someone beats me in a fair fight I will congratulate them, and maybe even ask them for some pointers. The fact that most people suck makes the ones that don't that much more interesting to me, and saves me from being a complete cynic, even though my gf and my parents would disagree (about the complete cynic part).

Like you pointed out, this is the way the world works, unfortunately. This is further compounded by the fact that, in this game, you interact with people of all different ages.

If I see someone who behaves rudely or selfishly, I will do what I can to alleviate the situation. It's also not that hard to recognize when someone is behaving badly just to get attention. I treat them like I would little children, and I just ignore them until they stop. By the way, it doesn't really help to gank people who behave poorly. Although I admit I've done that several times in the past, ganking people that gank only serves to convince that person to behave even worse.

It's not only PvP. This entire game mirrors RL in many ways. At the core, it's based on social interactions. You will always find bullies, cheaters, etc. I just sigh and accept that this is the case, and I chose to focus on the people who deserve respect. Sometimes, you have to look hard, but there are plenty of people who play this game who are good, decent people.
