Random Idea 2: Reload Button
I'd like to see some kind of reload button available in the GUI. Using rifles with 5 round clips makes it a real bother having to bring up inventory and weapons tab after each fight (I like to keep my screen as clean as possible during missions). I also tend to forget this, which is very annoying when trying to get an aimed shot in with an empty clip.

Alternative: automatically reload weapons if the clip is empty, even when not fighting.

Extra bonus feature: Reload All Weapons button. Good for those of us that like to switch weapons depending on the tactical situation.
