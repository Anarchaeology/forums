If you have one weapon equipped, and want to equip another in your other hand, you only need Mult.Melee skill listed for that other weapon.

Either that, or you need Mult.Melee high enough to satisfy the lesser weapon's req.

So, if one needs 110 and other 30, you need only 30 to wield both.
