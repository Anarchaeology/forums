Pardon but that statement is incorrect and needs to be clarified.

- The Boon and Freedom nano lines of the Adventurer profession are innate resist buffs and do not work the same way as the nano resist skill check system. Simply said, the innate resist values do not stack with the NR skill.

- The Boon and Freedom buffs represent a flat % chance to resist roots or snares only (and nothing to do with any other debuffs or drains or nukes).

- These innate resist buffs work on top of the NR skill check system (as mentioned by the devs themselves).

I'll add some info about how nano init works, since i realized even many mid/high level players aint aware.

first off, nano init is crucial for any character casting nanos in combat. high nano init is one of the things that set apart a good healer from a bad one. so if you intend to use more than an emergency heal now and then ( and frankly, then you should play and enforcer/soldier imo ), this shold be one of your primary skills.

general effects of nano init

ok, lets have a look at how it works. i will use moonbeam as example, as this is the nano most advs use in the high end. it shows:

attack time: 3.5 s
recharge time: 4.74

( meaning that at 75% agg and with 0 nanoinit you wil cast 3.5 secs before your actually healed, and cant cast another nano 4.74 secs after )

now wouldnt it be nice if you were healed right when you click the button, as this is probably the time you need the health ? well, thats exactly what nanoinit allows you.
for every 200 points in nanoinit, the attack time is reduced by 1 second. yes, only the attack time, theres no way ( atm ) to reduce recharge. this reduction is no linear decrease however, but works in steps. i.e., raising nanoinit from 1 to 199 will help you not one bit, you have to jump over the 200 mark. same goes for 400, 600, etc.
so, to come back to our example moonbeam would be instacast with 800 nanoinit ( at 75 % agg, see below ). obviously, the healing over time is increased by a huge amount, especially since nano init can be boosted by items very high ( see below ).


effects of the agg bar setting

unlike for your weapon, the agg bar doesnt work ( more or less ) linear for nanos. its more like nano init, working in steps. there are just three different settings that matter:

100% agg: attack time is decreased by one second
1-99% agg: attack time is as shown as in the description of the nano
0% agg attack time is increased by one second

again, theres no way to lower the recharge time.
using that on our example moonbeam, you would need 600 nanoinit to instacast it at full agg, 1000 to instacast it at full def.

items to boost nano init

first and foremost, there is the nano formula recompiler. this nifty item goes in deck slot 6 and requires complit to equip. so you cant use one until you equip a 6 slot belt and it will cut down on your ncu, but seeing that the ql 200 one gives you +400 nano init, the ip you save are well worth it. recompilers can be bought in shops and are also missionable.
a complete list of items to boost nano init can be found here, most of them ( leet dolls, crystals, etc. ) are of very limited use to an adventurer, you might want to have a look at the carbonum armor and the albrecht tank though. also, "the mister ash's" specs give a nice boost, you will miss a lot of AC though.

final word

as already mentioned above, if you not only wanna smack stuff but be the dmg dealer/healer hybrid the adv is designed to be ( just my guess ), nano init cant be valued to high. however, the aforementioned 1000 init to instacast the high heals at full def are really sufficient. and while the loss of 64 ncu for the recompiler hurts, i dont think theres another way to get to said 1k init with any reasonable setup. given the easy availability of ncu buffs, i'm even known to use recompiler + hardcore upgrade ( - % nano cost ) and even a range increaser ( on toons with offensive nanos, pointless on an adv of course ) at the same time. its more than worth it

Shadowlands - Maps
Shadowlands - Maps

Travel in SL is basically a linear progression. The basic design intention is to lure the player progessively and in a linear fashion through the SL playfields, lower to higher and each step of the way is harder. That is why flying is not allowed and why the minimaps work differently from RK because they want you to have an immersed experience within each zone. There are also inter- and intra-zone ways of travelling, and this involves a network of gardens, statues and special portals. All will be revealed soon enough as we explore SL and help each other out along the way. (And we will have to because SL isn't for soloers, it is geared toward teaming.)

Ok, now about SL maps. Everyone with a SL-enabled account will have access to the SL planetmap which shows good detail of landmarks and terrain. As for SL minimaps, they come in the form of nano programs. The first two maps can be uploaded by anybody provided they have the skills and an SL-enabled account. All the rest can only be uploaded by Adventurers who have the skills (and SL expansion).

(Note: With the fix that came with the 19th September client update, Adventurers are now able to cast their maps on others)
