Izgimmer, its time to get up and start some serious work + LOTs of other stuff
A nano resist buff for NTs is very much needed. Since we suck vs weapons and all our evades are dark blue etc, and the fact that we are the masters of nano technology tells me we should be damn hard to land nanos on. Nano Resist should be one of our strongest fields, and everything with the word "nano" in it we should be the masters.

ATM we got the highest unbuffed NR, and after some testing today with my 794 NR (incl Psy) it turns out NR infact works, I resisted quite some debuffs, about 45% vs Plunders that got 190% resist, which in turn means my NR vs Plunders are effectively 1500, this was monitored by FC. My opponent got buffed 1100 in nano skills. IMO my 1500 NR vs his 1100 skill I should resist even more. This was at max def, we didnt have time to test at max agg, but the results of max agg is much more depressing, done some private testing on it.

Now, NTs needs a NR buff really badly, any debuff totally cripples us, the Trader debuffed my MC down to 500, and I got 925 unbuffed MC... Not only that, he debuffed my (sucky cause I use AR) AMS from 600 down to 180. WHich in turn makes it impossible for me to hit the trader with my gun. Now, after the OE patch I will also suffer from OE penalties....

The problem is of course the fact that if a trader will most certainly hit me with the divest (100% NR) about every single time he tries, then he will Plunder me (I am already fumbling after some nuke to cast). THen he stacks up with a plunder (190% NR), if he miss on the first try (50/50) he will most likely hit on hte second, only 6 secs later.

The doctors init debuff (100%) is better than an MP nano shutdown (160%) IMO. The init debuff is "meant" to land every single time, cause it got 100% NR, same as our nukes, and yes I really think our nukes are not supposed to be resisted. Besides, the init debuff is instacast and the shutdown is not.

I hate to be crippled down to nothing in 1/10 of a sec.

NTs are way to vulnerable, at least vs what we are supposed to be the experts of: Nano Technology; Nano Technician. Hmm, dunno, perhaps I got it all wrong. We suck enough already vs weapons, no need to suck against everything.

And what i quite frankly cannot understand is why Fixers gets this very nice anti debuff buff: Bluff Debuff. And that is only to prevent an MP or Trader from debuffing their Grid Armor to uselessness. Which I find strange, cause Grid Armor is a buff already running, does this mean if I am debuffed that my HE Crunch and all my NT buffs dont work anymore because I cannot meet the reqs anymore?? Will my Nullity stop running because a Trader/MP debuffs me, which Nullity should have stopped in the first place?


NTs need NR buffs. We need a whole new line OR two of NR buffs. I have suggested them earlier, but I do it one more time, and btw, NTs dont have a single ability buff at all, and an NR buff would be the right thing for us.

1. A Enf like NR buff like Rage, which increases NR by 400 points, the NT one should be perhaps 600. This buff should also include a HE, and perhaps some Dot included. Or maybe the other way around, that we loose a little Nano, and gain a little health over time. Anyway, we need a kick ass NR buff. This debuff should last maybe 3 min at a time. Self only.

2. A long lasting NR buff, the top one adds maybe 200-400 points to NR, and lasts maybe 1 hour. Self only.

3. Add some NR bonus to the Enfraams line. ATM Enfraams is a waste of NCU, and needs some stuff to make it a valid buff. This one is already self only. And Enfaams sucks, Izgimmer have already proven it:

quote:
Nano Crystal (Izgimmer's Mockery) Nano Crystal - When Julius Enfraam proclaimed during a lecture that the coding for his 'Perfected Fortification' nano formula was crafted with such skill that no one would ever be able to match it, Mehar Izgimmer took it as a personal insult. In a single sitting lasting 37 hours, Izgimmer took apart Enfraam's coding, and added his own code to enhance the already 'perfect' code, further enhancing its effects. When Enfraam received a copy of the code, he is reported to have flown into such a rage that he had a minor stroke.
If Izgimmer used 37 Hours to do this, think of what he can do in a week...

I suggest Izgimmer takes a look at the whole Enfraams line of buffs, and add some additional stuff like some extra NR. I also suggest he takes a look at our nukes.

So, Izgimmer, get your ass up and start working. This time dont mess it up with 20 secs execution and 8 sec recharge, make the damn nukes useful.

NTs are the profession htat should have nano debuffs, dunno what the MP got them for, but it sure fits the NT much better, we are a combat profession with only our nanos to trust in, no pets.

Izgimmer, while you are at it, give us some Hardcore Nano debuffs.
