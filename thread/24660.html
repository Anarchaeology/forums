Pease Help The NT's in PVP...FC PLEASE READ!!
Ok FC surely this is not how you planed NT's to work in PvP. 2 things come to mind that gimp NT's to hell and back and to hell again.

#1) Why on earth is our PvP dmg capped at 50%? I was gone from the game for a while so i was not here when this change came into play.

First off we have very few hp's. That means that we can't have an all day battle with someone to kill them, which is what we now have to do because of the 50% cap (not succeeding BTW). Hitting an enforcer for 1.3 k (on a good hit) while they hit us for 1/4 to half out hp's is just gay. Our dmg is supposed to make up for our low hps....guess what IT"S NOT CAUSE IT IS 50%.

#2) Example: Little 50% dmg NT walking along in a PvP zone. Trader runs up and depive and ransacks the poor NT. Now instead of the poor little NT doing 50% dmg he is doing 0.

Is this how you invisoned this FC? Sure a trader can deprive and ransack any profession BUT it does not take away their main weepon when he/she does.

Nano resist you say? my reply to that is "MAKE IT WORK". Without a viable defence against debuffs NT's are low dmg dealers and no dmg dealers.

I read posts by people at FC saying well this class can't have this or that because that is not the way we invisioned the class. Well did you invision the NT class as being good for nothing but PvE (and not very good for that)?

When other classes reach high levels and go out and PvP what are we supposed to do? What about us? where do we fit in this PvP Clan VS Omni tek Fight for freedom?

All i am asking is that you make us viable in PvP. I do not want to think i wasted all this time playing this game because i can't kill PC's. For me killing boring NPC's over and over again is crap.

My job is to pay you guys pick a profession and level him the best I can. FC your job is to balance the game out where any class can kill any other class at any given time. NO GOD CLASSES. I didn't buy trader online i bought Anarchy online.

Thanks for your time.
