The thread on marriage and love prompted me to post this for Orgela...
This series of haiku (or haiku-like poetry for you purists out there) was written over a period of a month by my sister Orgela after the blinding love-at-first-sight of Angelynne settled into a never-ending fascination and longing ache for Angel's lovely Opifex butt. Angelynne and Orgela are both Meta-physicists ((on RK1)), their hearts and souls forever entwined. Today they are also engaged to be married...a wedding date as yet unset but, nevertheless, as certain as the rise and set of Rubika's own Moons. Orgela has started on Volume 2 of her Angel Moon Anthology...
Hope you enjoy them!

Chasing breathlessly.
Angel Moons Anthology
Laughing in my eyes.

My Angel, woven
From inexplicable Void.
My heart, Moon-caught, Sings.

Gale-shaken leaves torn
From complacent wandering.
Angel-kissed new Dawn.

An Angel Moonrise!
Sensually framed between
Long legs, arching back.

Summer Moon-gazing.
Wide-eyed imaginings of
A still further Shore

(chasing Agelynne, wearing much-too-short shorts, across Stret West Bank)

My eyes sigh, then chase
The steamy-locomotive
Of my Angel's butt.

My Angelic Love
Sensibilities undone
Scent of Rainy-Moon

Sleepy-eye's view
Past the rise and fall of an
Angel's morning-Moon

Pooled summer rain in
Hollows of an Angel Moon.
Fingers wandering.

Across Angel's Moon
Glistening delectably;
Freshly-squeezed peach.

Two ripe pears peeking
From under a fallen leaf.
The thrill of tasting.

(running toward Bliss, at night, from the LR outpost...Rubika's moons hung heavy over Bliss as I ran behind Angelynne, as I like to do)

Moons-framed in Bliss, an
Angel Moon rises tonight
Against both. I gasp.

(upon me leaving Independent Rubikans to form Celestial Emanations with Angelynne)

Bright Stars in my Heaven.
My Angel's Moon-lit path calls,
Yet I roam not far.

(Angelynne in Yalmaha...stuck in a tree)

Gloomy gnarled tree,
Snatching at my Angel's Moon.
Scratchy fingers.

Moon-gazing through leaves
Fingers caressing it's Orb
Shining white Angel

Angel's Moon rising.
Drenched in another day,
My sleepy eyes wake.

Moon-captivated!
Unaware of Angel's eyes
Over-shoulder smile.

Head raised, gasping air.
Falling still into night-Moon
On lips: Angel-rain.

(The following four I call the Mission Haiku Quatrain)

Chasing vapor trails.
My Angel's metallic-ass
Across Rubika

Impetuous Moon!
Angel dancing close with Death.
Nostrils flare with thrill.

Suddenly alone,
Lamenting-not brave death.
Fallen Angel Moon.

Reclaim tenderness,
Gazing into Angel-eyes.
Two Hearts: Ecstasy

(outside Angelynne's apartment waiting for her to arrive)

Scurry little roach!
Only the Moon is patient.
Stomp! No Angel home.
