Roleplaying and going too far.
To all you people who take RPing too far, you may not know it but you do ruin the enjoyment of the game for casual players or just people who just like the setting and aspect of the game and who don't RP. For example someone like me who does play the RP part once and a while but i play it lightly and never take anything too far. Right now as it stands with some guilds not trading or buffing TBA members I will still make implants for anyone who asks if I am not busy or buff them if i am not busy for the exception of people who have made it to my blacklist (but thats another story).

The Trade/Buff ban on TBA for example is pretty ridiculous considering that a majority of people in TBH don't even RP. Why make these peoples lives difficult because you want to RP and they don't. It's like saying "I am suffering you have to suffer aswell". Not everyone RP which makes situations confusing and even volatile at times. Because person_01 asking for the buff will get mad because person_02 refuses to buff them because there is a Trade/Buff ban on that persons guild and/or organization. So person_01 may get nasty or upset at person_02.

There has to be a point where people should know when to stop it. I myself play this game to have some fun with friends and just kill stuff together and advance my character. Not to deal with in-game politics. I deal with enough of that in Real Life.

And just for the record why hold the Trade/Buff ban still if the TBA Strike when it was never authorized by the TBA. I have made a statement about what Tisantous did and how it was never supported by the TBA.
