~The Front~ The Macgyver Massacre
The crunch of sand and dirt under heavy armored boots.. slowly moving up along the ridge line the squad spread out evenly enough to provide a good distance from mass attack. The Low foliage blending in well with the dark green of their armor. mostly all new recruits fresh out of the tubes. having set foot on the front no less then two hours prior. The Captain shook his head.. no matter what type of genetic training these kids had it didnt replace good hard training and experiance in the field. He watches the point man silently through his forward observantion Cam. and turned his head to examine the terrain. For the past 48 hours they had pushed hard to get into place a supposed flesh farm was on the other rise. "Point.. ease up there.. where getting close sensors read 5 click South by South west over the rise is a sentinal outpost.. Keep Sharp" The Captian moved along beside the squad his Rifle held in his hands lightly not the most likely weapon to use but he liked the Nova series. "raw fish.. on a assualt patrol.. whats higher ups thinking... probably not" he spoke quietly on his off set channel. then he picked up chatter on the squad Comm then rapid fire of energy and Projectile weapons

"CONTACT FRONT CONTACT FRONT AMBUSH!!!" The Captain spun and ran for the front of the column waving the others forward "Online online keep dispersion easy on the trigger.. " remain calm remain calm the Nano in his system trying to slow the adrenaline release into his system but it never seemd to help this was what he was made for. He sprinted forward finding the point man laying on the ground crimson splatter all about him his arm and left leg ragged his right hand completely gone. "Flesh Farmers, Keep your aims tight to the chest Disable them we cant kill them" He spun at a sound and snapped two rounds off quickly catching a Fleshfarmer in the chest. sending it peeling over its own feet crashing to the ground. "Status report Fire Team Level GO! " "Fire Team Alpha Green Green Green holding fast Sir" "Fire Team Bravo Green Yellow Green Holding sir request Medic as soon as possible" "Roger Bravo Doc you catch that? " "Yes sir inroute to their pos now." "Roger Fire team Charlie status? " " Green green green sir and holding" "Roger all teams Green good to go Dig in and hold positions. Intel as usual was Snarfed. " the Captain switches frequency to the Battalion comm net and broadcast his status and position to higher command and waited futher orders. the Squad channel sqwacked at him. "Sir.. there coming in hard and fast Left flank.. " " Roger Hold fast. hopefully help is in route." " SIR! Rear to the Rea.. *muffled scream* " The captain pulled up the over head relay map display to his hud and swore Fire team Charlie was all down leaving their rear open to attack He moved the Waypoint beacons closer together to form a circle on a low knoll area the Teams automatically moving upon recieving the new orders he Marched up to the center and started digging in with the rest of the men. " We hold here.. until help arrives oohrah?" "Ooorahh!" the Comm sounds sharp and loud in his ear.. Fresh Meat..


Lex sat up on his rack and looked around his new apartment and groaned wipping his eye and the sweat of his brow. he swung his legs over the edge of the bed and let his feet hit the cool floor. he blinked and looked at the time on his implant hud. 02:30.. he had only been asleep an hour. He rubbed his forehead slowly and padded over to the bathroom splashing cool water on his face and looked up into the mirrored display his face pale and drawn.. deep pockets of fatigue under his eyes. "I got more sleep on the front lines.. then I do here.. why.. " he turned and moves back to the bed. passing his armor rack and uniforms.. the old Pressed fatigues of the Omni Tek Marine Corps.. his Dress uniform and the new creased Uniform of Omni Pol. He lays back down staring at the ceiling and rehashes his transfere to the Omni Pol division on planet. "I didnt get them perma deathed.. I didnt get them perma deathed.. the flesh farmers where not supposed to be there.. it wasnt my fault. " Lex closed his eyes again and slowly drifted back into a troubled sleep.

"Start recording: Log entry 43453.

"Though the evidence is circumstantial, most experts do agree the appearance of cyborgs on Rubi-Ka was the direct result of a Clan experiment which went out of control; much like the mutants population was the result of failed Omni-Med experiments. And yet this does not in itself exclude the possibility of parallel evolution of cyborg organisms on other worlds.

"Which really brings us to the question, what is a cyborg? In this day and age most of us choose to replace tissue with cybernetic augmentations. Some say in doing so we trade our humanity, but there is little evidence of that. In our drives, desires and urges, we remain as human as our forefathers, with much the same virtues and all the same failings.

"The true cyborg organism however has shed its humanity, and I do not mean this is romantic, anthropocentric sense. Fundamental changes to the organisms bio-chemistry result in the equally fundamental changes in its psychology. The organic portion of the cyborg brain was been, for lack of a better term, rewired to interface more efficiently with the inorganic portion and no longer functions as a human brain. Instinctive human reactions to pain, pleasure, stress or boredom, the primal instincts to mate, to feed and even to just survive are either absent in a true cyborg or present in forms completely alien to the human psyche.

"One of the strongest, most evident drives of the cyborgs found on Rubi-Ka is its intense, perhaps even pathological, xenophobia. Logical and pragmatic in most instances, the cyborg will not tolerate intelligent organic life.

"Like I said, these findings apply for those cyborgs found on Rubi-Ka. Yet the concept of, and potential benefits from, a true cybernetic organism are such that it is easy to imagine different cyborg strains being developed or evolving on different worlds in parallel.

"The question is how would an off-world cyborg be different from a Rubi-Kan one? Would off-world cyborgs display the same traits, the same level of xenophobia? If the opportunity arose for two separate cyborg strains to meet what would happen? Would their instinctive xenophobia prevail or would they recognise their kinship and join forces against their common enemy, mankind? Should this ever happen, would mankind stand a chance?"

"Stop recording."

Dabblez regards the alien artifacts on her examination table, a test tube containing a codenamed Liquid Glow and a cybernetic arm. For an moment she considers destroying them.

"No, not before I learn everything I can from them." she says to herself. "The day we fear to prevail over our thirst for knowledge is the day that all that is worthwhile in the human race will have gone."
