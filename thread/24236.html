I discovered how to combine liquid charges. You're all going to laugh. Here is how it works:

1) You use a screwdriver on a flamethrower ammo
2) Combine a Simple Tube with a the stripped ammo
3) Use a screwdriver on 2 other ammo charges
4) Combine 1 of the stripped charges with the charge that has a tube in it
5) Combine the last charge with the double liquid charge
6) Combine a key detonator

So what's the problem with this process? Well, because ammo only come in QL 1, the highest QL charge you can make is QL 2. GREAT WORK FUNCOM!!
