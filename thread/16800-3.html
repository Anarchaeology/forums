I would love to see Engineers be the first profession to fly. I think that a limited capacity would truly enhance the low end game for all the 'Johnny Rockets' of the world. In all honesty, if even a lvl 2 Engy could piece together a 'Decrepit Rocket Pack' that would get them from the back of the Newbie Zone to the shops and then fizzle out, I think that would be very cool.

I think of them more as 'jump suits' which allow a single 'take-off' and 'touch-down' within a zone before requiring some maintenance.

I bet that the designers could put this together quite quickly for a couple day whirl on test before getting down to proper implementation.

Add Flame Thrower Ammunition (liquid fuel) to make a 'Fuel Jet Pack'.
Heavy Tank Armor as the model with a modified skin.
Some silly nano effect shooting out yer feet simulating propulsion.
Appropriate EE, ME, Str, Agi, Int requirements to wield.
One item property is 'CanFly=True'
Negative runspeed modifier of mebbe -250 to start with.
Make the 'Fueled Jet Pack' 'timed' like the Meta creation weapons so that Robut Junk actually has a continuing use. Make the duration short as part of its largest tradeoff. Perhaps 5-minute jumps could be the maximum. Still usefull. Not unbalancing.
