Galway Shire - A waste of space.
Galway Shire as it is now sits outside Rome Blue. It contains the Omni entrance to the subway and a handful of Tower areas. A few mission entrances in the form of caves. It has a save point some mission terminals and a burger bar and bartender.

Sounds like the Perfect place for newbies to try out hunting doesn't it? Well it would be apart from one thing. All the mobs (Apart from best in brass) are level 2 or 3.

What is the point?? by the time most people get there they have leveled until they cant get anymore XP in the backyard. Why fill a zone with mobs that nobody will want to kill?

To make it usefull I would suggest upping the level of the mobs to the range of 12-17.

That would make far more sense in my book.

What do you think?
