Launchers - when it comes to ammo - aren't so bad because:

A) The clip size is usually pretty freaking small,
B) Attack and Recharge time is slow, so you go through your limited ammo at a slower pace, and
C) Launcher ammo is fairly common in mission chests; I find at least one clip per mission.

They're not more popular simply because there are "better" (i.e. more streamlined/popular) strategies already out there for the heavy combat classes.

Bows aren't used more because Bow Special Attack is totally useless; only thing they're good for at the moment is if an MA wants to do some pulling. Some of the bows have extra abilities (Oneida has Burst, Fullauto, and maybe Aimed Shot and/or Flingshot), but again, MAs are the only class that would really use 'em, and all those extra skills are blue/dark blue.

Why arrows cost so much
Everyone knows that arrows are made from special oak that is only found in a small section of the brazillian rain forest on Earth. Each arrow sharft is individually made, packaged, and shipped first-class from Earth to Rubi-Ka.

Then, arrow-heads are imported from a planet in the far reaches of the galaxy, where the last remaining ore mine is found. These arrow-heads are special, because they require a new mold for each head which you produce. They are also individually packaged and shipped first-class to Earth, which are then re-packaged and shipped second-class to Rubi-Ka.

After that, specially-trained ICC Hunters are sent into the wilds of Rubi-Ka to collect Reet feathers. These feathers are collected and shipped bulk-rate back to Earth for processing. After processing, the feathers are sent to another planet, bulk-rate, for colorization. After colorization, they are individually packaged and shipped all over the galaxy, including Rubi-Ka.

Finally, some old guy in Omni-1 sits around all day and assembles the arrows. He uses a kit from circa-1324AD, which he purchased at an antique auction years ago. He painstakingly assembles each arrow by hand, sharpens them, and packages them for re-sale on Rubi-Ka.

And there you have it, the reason why arrows cost so much...
