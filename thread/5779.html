The shovah call for an official discussion/meeting
The organisation known as The shovah wish to call for a meeting among willing OT, neutral and Clan guilds/organisations. Those who are interested may call Oshovah and we shall arrange this meeting the best way possible.

Topics on the meeting will be:

The amnesty
How should we stand against this so-called amnesty. I know that this topic is "out-of-date" but still, there are smaller organisations that should take stand against it and make themselves heard.

Trade agreements/Alliances
Guilds arrange trade agreements and alliances, this will be better for the not so large guilds, it will make their voices heard.

Merging of guilds
If anyone would wish to maybe improve their status as guild, this topic will also be discussed as I see this will be important in the future.

Future
It is important to see what can be done and then act in such a way. This is maybe the most important topic.

If anyone wish to add something to this list of topics, give me a tell.

/tell Oshovah
