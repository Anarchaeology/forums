This belongs in the game mechanics or beginners forums... but I will answer your question.

The 'base' chance of scoring a critical hit is 3% if I recall correctly. Several factors modify that 3% to make it either higher or lower.

Your attack rating and the target's evades are a major factor - if your attack rating is higher than the target's evade skills, your chance of scoring critical hits increases. If the target's evades are higher than your attack rating, you will score fewer critical hits. Your attack rating is determined by your skills for the weapon you are using. If you're using a weapon with two skills for its attack rating, or if you aren't maxing your weapon skill every level, you will tend to score fewer critical hits.

Buffs and items: Some items and buffs directly increase your critical hit chances. It's very likely that your teammates are using these buffs or items while you are not. Martial Artists and Agents have many crit buffs, and Crats have a crit-buffing speech. There are also 'vision enhancer' items that increase your crit chances.
