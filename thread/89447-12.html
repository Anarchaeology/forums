((While I enjoy roleplaying, I don't consider myself to be all that great at it and I'm even less of a good writer. Plus, I was just never interested in or felt the urge to come up with a background, so, personally, I don't have any for my characters. While I might not have a history for everyone to know about, I think that my character's actions in the present say enough about them without having to go into their childhoods and every other experience they might have had up until now. They are who they are regardless of how they got there. Sure, maybe a background might provide some insight into why they are the way they are, but it wouldn't change who they are.

I think it's great that some of you have come up with elaborate backgrounds for your characters and sometimes I read them if I stumble across them, but not everyone has one for one reason or another.))
