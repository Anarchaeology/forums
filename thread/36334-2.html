Yeah that works for the most part...
What Dhurdahl did works for the most part, its a great extraction of the files.. the only thing is the naming system makes it a little harder to put back together with a DOS copy command. Remembering that the graphic is several png's layed out in a grid I will show you the difference. Lets take for example the small map, the 3X4.. Dhur named them this way.

1X1 1X2 1X3
2X1 2X2 2X3
3X1 3X2 3X3
4X1 4X2 4X3

Left to right, however they need to be named top to bottom in order to run a regular DOS copy ex: (copy /b *.* mapfilename.bin)

1x1 2x1 3x1
1x2 2x2 3x2
1x3 2x3 3x3
1x4 2x4 3x4

Also in the naming convention for putting them back together... if you notice in the text file the small map comes first, so don't name your files for example (Large1x1.png and Small1X1.png) since alphabetically L comes before S you will get all your larges in the bin first, instead I named them Larg31x1.png and Asmall1x1.png and so forth.
