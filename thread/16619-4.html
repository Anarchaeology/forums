If the NT is putting in 3 implants and the MP 2 (max) then the NT gets more.... they just do.

Add to this of course, that the MP pallette is much more difficult in terms of implants. To optimise our damage and use in teams we need:

1) PM/SI for buffs (for team and to get our best pets),
2) PM/SI for skills debuff,
3) BM and TS for heal pet
4) MC and TS for attack pets
5) BM MC and TS for the anima
6) BM and MC for the instill attack pet buff,
7) MC and SI for the chant attack pet buff
8) MM and TS for mez pet (optional)
9) PM and MC for our nukes
10) MM and PM for the damage debuff...

Nearly all of the clusters for all of these skills fall into the same slots as others. The NT is supposed to do damage and can implant for that purpose specifically if he wishes. The MP is expected to buff the world, do damage in nukes, heal the tank and attack some with a pet.... each of which require minimum of two skills and all of which together take all 6 skills.

As a concrete example, at the lvl 100 point nearly every MP gives over all of their key spots to PM and SI to try to get to the infuse buffs by lvl 110ish at the earliest. It makes sense for us, because we can use the infuses to boost the other skills and also buff others in a team to make them more effective, so helping ourselves too...

When we do this, all of our pet focussed implants come out and we gain only a little on those skills with the infuses compared to what we had before with lower QL implants and masteries. I'm level 114 and imped to the max pretty much (QL 170ish for the key ones). I can get all of my infuses, but still only reach to a QL 139 attack pet fully buffed with infuses/Expertise (if I could only find the crystal) and cope with a QL113 heal pet. I have to buff high to get to my QL 116 nuke.

So my reality is that I could reach a reddish attack pet but can't find the crystal, I have a heal pet lower QL than my level, a nuke only very slightly higher QL than my level. My self buffs like nano cost and evades take other skills again and I have QLs lower than my level for nearly all of those too. My damage debuffs were lower QL than my level too until 114 and are really glorified aggro prods too. My animas, instills and chants are lower QL than my level. My creation shields and weapons are way lower QL than my level too.

NT's can reliably take QLs higher than their level with full MC imps and deal the damage they want to. With MP buffs from their peers, they can take higher QLs for their nukes and other nanos too. Whereas, for the average MP, even imped to the max and fully buffed with our own buffs, we're rarely more than 20 QLs higher than our level on one element (usually attack pet or heal pet) and the other elements are usually around or well below our level.

Very occasionally we manage to get two elements that have QLs moderately higher than our level... attack pet and heal pet usually, but it doesn't happen too often. To keep everything optimised to its fullest and get the best things we'd like to reach, we'd have to change implant layout and level every 10 levels minimum (with the associated costs).

Oh and keep in mind that for the MP two of the nano skills are light blue.... the ones we need for our buffs....

NTs definitely have an advantage on the implant and skills side. You can equip nanos of higher QL than your level for your main weapons and a couple of other nanos too. With MP buffs, you could have several seriously higher QL nano lines at your disposal.

For MPs we're lucky if we can manage a higher QL nano for one line when fully buffed. Near skill cap times, we can get maybe two nanos of seriously higher QL if we're lucky. That's it.

a simple fix for NCU shortage
As pointed out in the original post, one of the big NT problems is lack of NCU for buffs, etc due to the need to equip our various gadgets (e.g., recompiler, hardcore upgrade) in deck slots instead of NCU memory units.

One solution is to reduce NCU costs, as suggested in the post. Another suggested one is to create an NCU buff. Both strike me as unnecessarily complex and FC may be reluctant to do either for fear of giiving NT's some kind of percieved advantage.

A simpler and safer solution - and one in keeping with the system for the gadget's other professions have, is to move the darn things out of the belt slots and into utility slots (HUD slots would probably be inappropriate for RP reasons). That puts us on an EVEN footing with other professions with respect to having 6 slots (max) available for NCU units and allows us to still have gadgets (again) like other professions. Problem solved! No advantage to NT's!

--------------

An afterthought. Moving gadgets like CPU upgrades (to cut nano costs) to utility slots would also benefit other nano using professions. I think this is a logical move and would improve things all-around
