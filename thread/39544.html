Something wrong with MOB con colours in Steps Of Madness
I suspect this has come up before, but since I've not seen a thread on it I thought I'd start one. I assume this problem also extends to the other static dungeons but as I've only been to SOM I cant say for sure.

At level 40 I went to look around the SOM. I didnt really expect to get far but figured I needed a change of pace from constant missions. Most of the MOBS conned green to me and as long as there was only 1 in the room at a time me and my bot (level 50 ish bot) didnt do too bad. Still got canned my multiple MOBs in a room so decided to get some levels and come back later.

So now I'm level 51, and have a newer bot (level 60 ish), went back to SOM last night and the MOBS con either grey or green (neleb was yellow for the brief moment he was alive before the group of high levels killed him) however killing one of the grey is actually harder for me than killing a normal red MOB. I'm assuming this is due to the numer of HP's the MOBs had since they didnt appear to be doing alot of damage, but since they were alive a lot longer that damage was worse than a red could deal before my bot killed it. The worse thing is the fact you only get 1 xp even though it was more dangerous than a red!!!

Please fix the conning system in the static dungeons so the colour of the MOB actually reflects the danger it presents since it is no where near accurate at the moment.

As an aside it was nice to not have to battle my way to Neleb as the higher level players had already done this (an upside to a single dungeon instance) but given that there were at least 2 groups of high levels camping neleb, me on my own didnt stand a chance at getting the robe (having said that I admit I probably wouldnt have stood a chance on my own if I'd have to have fought my way to neleb and taken him on with the add in the room with him!!)

and on the subject of class balance, the was a level 30 or 40 crat in one group (all the other group members were 100+) who had no problem charming nelebs MOB and using them against him. How is this balanced to a level 50 engineer with a maxed selfbuffed bot who has trouble killing 1 MOB!! (mainly because the bot cant hold agro but thats another story)

Tiran
Phear Leet
Since con also determines experience values, they're not going to change it easily. (grey means 1 xp). Right now, it's merely your level vs. creature level, which is a value they give the thing when it's created, and does not have to have ANYthing to do with its actual skills, hit points, damage, etc.

There is a 'usual values' guideline for level and those values, but it's not fixed, and any one or all of the creatures capabilities can be set wildly different.

The SoM creatures are uniformly given FAR higher hit points than 'usual'.

It's quite possible for Funcom to create a creature that's level 1, has 500k hit points, hits for 1000 a pop, and has a 1k damage shield on it, to boot. Obviously, this is one they don't want you killing. <grin>
