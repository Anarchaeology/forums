ClickSaver 2.0
Released version 2.0 of ClickSaver.
It no longer uses winpcap. It now patches directly one of the game's dll (in memory) to get mission packets. It has a good chance of working for anyone, including people for whom AOMD never worked. However, it will probably actually not work for many people because of dumb bugs, as usual ;)

It also works in areas where packets are compressed, like newland.

I've tested it successfully under winxp and win98.
