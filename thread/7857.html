To address the starter of this thread and to answer their question regarding "interdimensional" between the two servers,

the answer is no.

Why? Well, let's look at this from a less than logical point of view...

First of all, there is the question of time difference. Whatever happens in RK1 doesn't necessarily happen on RK2. It's like that dumb as bricks t.v show "Sliders" where people would move between dimensions or.. time periods and they would, have one little adventure here and there and what have you.

I guess I could diverge on contempoary theories of temporal mechanics, and the "reality" of things, and how that relates to certain events happening on RK2 and RK1 and vice versa and how the story will unfold. But I won't bore you with that. Theres probably a much simpler reason why FUNcom won't implement a travel between servers...

It's cause their working on other changes to the game,(supposedly, really how are we to know they aren't smoking it up)
and from what I have heard their director talk about how they work on things, my guess is that they work on whatever seems to have priority.. say, character classes. So it's my guess that travel between servers is very low on the priority.. plus they are going to be working on things that will by the middle of this year (supposedly..) will have tremendous affect on how omni and clan factions interact with each other... e.g capturing territory..

And that will probably eat more of their resources up. So, unless they have a company the size of microsoft working on their coding, its my guess they probably won't ever ever get to the idea of travel between servers. Sorry.
