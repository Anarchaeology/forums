We already have the Reet Retreat...what about the leets?
Ok. Many of you have probably gone to the Reet Retreat one time or another in Stret West. Well the fact is, most people don't pay much attention to Reets. (As in, the creature)

But boy-o-boy, look at all the attention LEETS have got!

So I propose we not only keep the Reet Retreat but build, somewhere near it, a Leet Lounge. In fact, I have blueprints and a ton of ideas for the Leet Lounge so I guess I'll just put them here.

FIRST FLOOR
1. Entry room. I was thinking we could have like, a bouncer to kill anyone who attacked someone else. I mean, it'd be SO much cooler than suppression gas. Also, what about a small admission fee? (Don't throw stones, I'm just saying...)
2. A main dance floor with lights, maybe even a ROTATING dance floor. (I think this would be really, really cool) It should DEFINETELY have a DJ Loft.
3. Bathrooms on either side of abovementioned dance floor.
4. An indoor pool complete with slides, diving boards, and maybe a floating raft.
5. A playground with swings, slides ect. reserved for those cuddly little balls of fluff known as LEETS!
6. In the back, two conference-style rooms for guilds and such to have meetings, or a dignified dinner party.

SECOND FLOOR
1. The official 'Leet Lounge' inside the LL. This would be a very classy coffee shop/lounge, much like Rompa Bar but with higher capacity. I was thinking there could be glass walls so we could look out over West Stret. (Or wherever you build the Leet Lounge)
2. On the right side of the lounge, a generic 'bar'. I was thinking it could also have a stage for people to perform.
3. Here's what I think would be UBER UBER AWESOME. A holo-deck beach simulation area. It could have a small bit of water, and a nice sandy and sunny beach, complete with plenty of lawn chairs, umbrellas, picnic tables, and other beach stuff for everyone.


Feel free to add onto my ideas. We are still lacking some more attractions on Rubi-Ka and I think a Leet Lounge would be a good addition.

LEETS ARE PEOPLE TOO!
