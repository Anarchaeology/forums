To clear up some confusion:

Some seem to think debit cards are ATM cards while others are using the same term to refer to Visa/Mastercard check cards.

If you have a check card, one that will have a Visa/Mastercard logo, then you can use this just like a regular credit card. A debit/ATM card will not work.

Spectre your choices seem to be getting a Visa/Mastercard check card in connection with your bank account, using a prepaid credit card, or getting a regular card. Most credit card issuers give fairly quick replies to applicants. If you are turned down for a regular card, try a secured one. I recommend going this route as it is the only one of the above methods that will help you establish a credit history for future credit.
