Don't move it!!!
I am a regular at Reet's Retreat. Please do not move it just because one or two players want it moved. Its a great place to hang out with friends, and its in a wonderful location. This type of place SHOULD be off the beaten track. I wouldn't mind having a grid location in a closer location (like the Bronto Burger in 4 holes), but there should not be a grid location right outside.

As for a previous posters comments on roleplaying .. we do the most with what we have. Funcom developed a game centered around missions and hunting. There are not many roleplaying aspects to the game at all. The "story" does not provide roleplaying opportunities to the common player. The only tools for roleplaying we have been given that are not mission/hunting related are these dance clubs. People often complain that "thats not roleplaying", or "oh no .. another dance contest/swimsuit party" .. but thats what we have been given. Once Funcom expands the variety of roleplaying content, roleplaying opportunities will expand. For now, Funcom's focus is game mechanics, bugfixing, and class balancing.

BTW for those who care about roleplaying, what we REALLY need is the ability to write a note and hand it to someone. This would change the roleplaying environment overnight. Think about the possibilities Funcom!!!

- Szentisha
