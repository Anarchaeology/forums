It's time for a REAL! boss mob
Ok, now all I hear about boss mobs is how there's always a huge group around them racing to kill them (I, luckily, am not high enough level yet to participate in this), that to kill them first is the goal, that they actually dying is a foregone conclusion. What I think should be done is a secret (No patch notes about it) power up of all these boss mobs. I'm talking like those 2 teams camping it get to crowd up the reclaim and wonder what the heck happened.

OR, my other, and I think better idea: Let's face it, everything on this planet can and has been killed. Killed so much it is obvious it will die everytime. FC, create a mob, a big mob, a BIIIIIIIG mob, that will take an army to kill. Something like this: A mysterious (and big) spaceship crashes somewhere in Rubi Ka. Teams are sent by Omni and the Clans to investigate, all that's heard are screams over the radios. All the aliens on the craft are dead, their pet, however is not. This should be the original mob-from-hell, Cthulhu, Godzilla, and King Kong rolled into 1. I'm talking 2-4 ideal (good set up of proffessions) teams of all lvl 200 was the best equipmeant possible has, at best, with tactical thinking, a 50-50 chance against this thing. It's strong (Makes the big dog droid look like Bill Gates compared to Superman), it's fast (Higher QL Yalms could outrun it, barely), and it's BIIIGGG (like use whompa attenna for a toothpick big, well, not quite, but taller than one of those). It wanders from zone to zone, including civilized ones, and just terrorizes and destroys, guards, people, machines, clans, omni, it doesn't matter. Have an AI on it that makes it just aggro and kill and wander away evtually, but GM controlled for whenever something big happened would be nice.

Make it so it could be a threat for a good long while (Kind of like you first time playing Resident Evil: Nemesis, it could show up at anytime, anyplace), and make whatever guild or group or whatever (I don't mean a simple matter of how much damage they do or who gets the last hit, I mean a human being on hand to see who really does it, and it will require many teams teamwork) be heroes! Not 1 or 2 nice items, fanfare, parades, statues in their honor by their side or both, credits, items, etc. Most of all, make this thing hard enough to be worth all that, and make it take the organization of a large group of people (Perhaps 2-4 level 200 teams is too little to kill it, on second thought) to destroy the thing. None of this camping bull crap either, uh-uh, it's a one time thing.

Ok, there, paragraphs.
