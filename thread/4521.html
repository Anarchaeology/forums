Capture of Dr. Certes
I was at the Tir grid yesterday when Gelsa came to announce the capture of Dr. Certes (sp?) yesterday.

As I sat down and watched Gelsa prepare, a few things came to mind.
1. Why were her guards Omni? Then after much ruckus, finally decided to be clan.
2. Why did she have guards in a 100% zone.
3. Why did she decide to have a clan gathering, right at the grid entrance. I must admit though, people gridding into Tir right in front of her was not only distracting but annoying. Especially the engineers with noisy bots.
4. Who is Dr. Certes and why should I care that he's been captured?
5. And last but not least. How does someone get captured anyway?

I sat and listened for a while, sending tells to Lorekeep and fellow guildmates who were also trying to understand the situation. The only thing I learned was that he was captured by (presumably) Omni and Clansmen working together.

About 45 minutes into it, I got really tired of Gelsa's slow speech/explanation and the lack of any viable questions about the situation being answered. A guildmate stayed in my absence and filled me after it had ended about 20 minutes later. Her summary was this:

"We lost this doctor guy, found him again. Don't know why he's captured, but we'll fill you in later. Thanks for your patience."

I sighed as I read the message from my guildmate and wondered how a simple announcement took a whole hour to tell and continued my desperate search for yet another elusive polymorphing nanocluster.

There were some accusations about Lorekeep being a part of the party that helped capture Dr. Certes, but like most of the asolescent shouting that was going on, I took it with a grain of salt.

If anyone could be so kind to enlighten me as to who this Doctor is and why is he that important, please reply. I spend most of my time outdoors hunting and exploring so I don't get to hear a lot about clan celebrities.

Dr. Certes was the clan affiliated "mad scientist" responsible for the giant mutants attacking Tir, Athen and 4 Holes earlier this fall. (Spiders, rollerrats, baraton, anuns, etc).

After some investigation the Council found Dr. Certes was the brains behind these mutants, and imprisoned him. He escaped once, but was captured again.

Since he's now captured, I guess he must have escaped once more (missed that one).

The reason you might not have heard about him, is because these events took place before halloween [story kick off], in August/September I think.
[these giant monster attacks were used as a test on how events/big battles performed in game]

[..also, the reports of these events are at least 2 board wipes old ]
