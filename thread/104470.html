I would like to apologize to the members of the AO forum community for the post that started this, the original post had been deleted from here by a moderator. I was given the link to this article and was told that it was the one that was deleted, so I reposted it here and removed the link to the logs after Boglwe's suggestion that it was the link that got the post deleted. Unfortunately, the link was to the wrong article and was a post written just after the downfall of the Angels when many were still very very angry over the events.

While most of us regret our actions in what happened to bring about the downfall of the Angels, it's very hard to give a clear idea of what happened to people outside that were not there. More went on than is just in those logs, leaders kept information from the members and the members thought they were well informed about how thier org was run. Members grew angry that they did not feel listened to and closed thier ears. There were mistakes on both sides, and my friends and I aknowledge our responsibility in this whole mess.

I'll not sugar-coat this, some of our members still hold anger or resentment towards our former leaders. That will fade with time, though. What's done is done, and cannot be changed. For now, we let the past lie silent and we heal. Maybe some day we will invite Boglwe and Dooley to join us, as they helped create what we were before this happened. Maybe not. I cannot say just yet, but time will tell.

So, I've deleted the content of the original post. Well over 90% of the original members of the Angels have come to form another org named Vagabond Winds after three long days of thinking and discussion. We think we have created a unique org with no limits in what we can accomplish together. We do not wish to look back on our past mistakes but instead to the future and what we can do to make Rubi-ka a place of fun and laughter for everyone.

I hold no ill will toward Boglwe or Dooley, without the Angels I would not have met my friends and family I know today. With that said, I think it's time to bring a close to this.

R.I.P Angels of Light.

-Yiphyin, member of Vagabond Winds
