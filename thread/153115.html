The Diary of a Pissed Off Fixer.

"What day is it today...Oh, this thing is on. Well, whatever day it is today I have once more been ganked by that stupid Omni scum Evilessence. I swear I should just find a group of people and create an organization strictly about partying. I wonder if that would go over well..."
