Tradeskills: Making weapons.
First, fix the damn construction kits, they have been broken since release.

I would like to see some unique weapon smithing.

I am too tired to get into detail but I'll give youi a run-down of it.

Basically you take a general weapon template and add many things to it to build your unique weapon. Aside from the general items required to build each weapon, everything you add to the weapon template will do something (Like add burst, heavyweapons, set damage type, speed, range, etc)

There will be dozens of "generic weapon templates that determine what the base skill of the weapon will be. (IE. Choose a rifle generic weapon template and you will come out with a gun that's only requirement is rifle.)

But of course that generic rifle will suck on it's own so you have to add additions.

There will be three basic types of additions you can make to the generic template.

1. Things that change an aspect of the gun. Like adding a fire injector will make your gun do fire damage. Or a "bursting chip" that gives your gun burst ability. Techincally you could make a gun that has one of each special ability but you probably won't want to.

2. Things that increase some stats at the cost of adding a skill requirement. Like increasing the maximum damage range at the cost of adding heavy weapons to the requirement. Or giving it an area of effect damage at the cost of adding grenades/explosives.

3. Things that give the gun an advantage, but also give a disadvantage (To encourage tailoring a gun to your needs instead of everyone running around with cookie cutter guns).
Like something that increases maximum damage range at the cost of lower speed.

Number 3 is also unique in that there will be several "additions" you can use to increase damage, each of which will hit a different attribute. Like one will make you sacrifice speed, one will make you sacrifce ammo, and another could make you sacrifce range.
All three add the same boost to damage, but let you tailor your guns advantages and disadvantages to your needs. Hell, you could even use all 3 damage increasers and take hits all across the board if that's what you want.

Or modifers that you can put on the gun like +5 to agility, or +20 to health, etc (of course the value will be higher based on QL). The attribute modifers should also have a negative side effect. Like + 10 to agility but -5 to stamina. There should be many options so you can totally customize you gun... So if you want +10 agility on your rifle you will have a choice as to what atrribute you want to be decreased as a trade off.


Naming

Nothing fancy, just give all player created weapons a generic name with maybe a number at the end to signify some of the changes done -- Like: Assualt Rifle XL-520 B. I just made the number up, but you could make it so all the possible weapon combinations will give a unique tag number.

conclusion

I think this is just what the weapon tradeskills need. No one wants to build a carbon copy of a crappy gun when they can buy a better one in the store for cheaper.
