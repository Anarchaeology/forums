Fallen
Davis looked around as he sat in his booth in Rompa Bar. He was finished, everything he had worked for was gone and a sense of doom covered his soul like a black haze.

Omni-Pol was no more to him, kicked out for a gamble at peace..

SoS was no more to him, left in shame after his peace efforts exploded in front of his face.

What he once thought to be friends now left him in the dark, Lunya detested the man, Avrik didnt trust him, and Arimathea just sat by idle.

There was nothing left for him, not even the though of Ariel could save his soul from the depth of darkness and decay. The end was soon, but he did not know quiet when.

The planet rejected him, the clans rejected him, the omni-tek emplyees rejected him, all who has ever come into contact with the petty soul has rejected him.

He started to fall into a blackness not even god himself could pull him out of. He began to mumble to himself "It will end for all...It will end for all...It will end for all...."

He quickly lost all of his once flouishing compasion for his fellow humans, he allowed all hope to slip away, he grasped at the thought of revenge on those who had betraid him...They called him a traitor when they themselfs where no better.

"It will end for all" he continued to mummble softly as he sliped away into a slumber...

Glennie "Arimathea" Niss was relaxing in the Rompa bar having a nice talk with some friends when she saw a man dressed in black, approaching with a large blade in his hands. She recognized the face. Earlier in the day, while she was sick from resurrection shock, this man had come to ask for employment with her organization. Marcai. At that time earlier in the day, she knew her boss, Doncarnage, and Marcai wouldn't get along, and she was too sick to argue with the guys to make them listen to eachother. So, she got labelled a "traitor" just like the rest of them. And there he was now in the Rompa bar with that large blade. Glennie's hyper-sensitive nose could catch the scent of fresh blood.

"I wont do it, I cant" he said. Glennie looked from him to his knife and back at him again. He had a smirk on his face that told her he had something evil on his mind.. but what?

"Are you going to knock yourself off, Marcai?" she asked.

"Knock myself? No, not at all..."

"Well who then?" she paused, waiting for a response. This conversation had led to what might have been a frightening silence, if indeed Glennie were actually in fear of the guy. She had gone on jobs with him in the past to clear out some buildings of clanner riff-raff. She knew what he was and wasn't capable of.

The silence continued... "Well Marcai, don't keep me waiting!"

Then he spoke.

"Waiting... Meta has tasted this blade."

Glennie froze, for a moment pondering what it must have been like to see the expression on Msice's face when he sliced through her with that knife. Not quite something Glennie would even think of doing.. but in a sick way she understood why he had that smirk on his face. "Now why did you do that Marcai?"

"They are traitors."

Slowly, people began to filter away from the once-jovial Rompa booth. The air had gone cold and Glennie's heart was fluttering. He had used that word.. traitors.. when she had last seen him.

Then Marcai continued, "Much like Lunya and Avrik... Everwatch, Meister, Ross... Radiman, Vixentrox." Glennie was quite sure there were more on his list.

"Are you going to kill me too?" she asked.

Marcai's eyes flashed, and then he said, "You, perhaps... but the time isn't right."

Glennie shook her head... what a freaking idiot, she thought to herself. She kept her eyes on him as she rose from her seat. "Good, then I have time to walk away then."

She heard him calling goodbye as she picked up her pace down the stairs.

Into the Crowd
Defiantly, Nathan pushed his way through the horde of shoppers ambling around Jobe Plaza. There was something about the floating city that unnerved him; was it the fact that so few powerful minds could accomplish so numerous of feits, or was it that those powerful minds were not under control? Probably the latter. As he crossed the main concourse, bound for the Harbour, his wristcomm flared alive.

"Sir, are you there?"

"Yes, Dannielle. Go ahead." Ammicus stopped, leaning over a nearby railing and looking out over the top of amber evening clouds.

"Sir, there's been an...incident." There was some urgency in her voice, and though his assistant, Dannielle "Whispe" Griffel tried her best to convey calm, those efforts were betrayed by her relative inexperience. "I've succeeded in locking down the mainframe as best I can, so that should be safe now, but Marcai..." She trailed off, hesitating.

"But Marcai?" Ammicus asked, somewhat annoyed.

"He...his movements...they're weird."

"Elaborate." Ammicus responded coldly.

"One minute he's in Rompa...then the next he's in Nascence...then he's in Omni-1 Trade District...then Jobe...then..."

"So the fool travels, what of it?"

"Well, like I said, it's all a matter of minutes. He was also seen taking samples from the Brink in Nascence, and taking photgraphs of the Void. He was asking some really bizarre questions towards the Jobe nanonuts, too." Whispe finally paused to take a breath. "And like I said, it's weird."

Ammicus rubbed his eyes with the palms of his hands, sighing heavily. There was enough trouble in Omni-Tek as it was without having to deal with this lunatic. His mind played back to the scene at Rompa the evening earlier, when Marcai had entered, interrupting his dinner, spouting absurdities about ending the world. Naturally, as Eternalforce pointed out, Marcai was simply another wack-o suffering from excessive notum exposure. But, what if...

"Analysis."

"Inconclusive given current information, sir. But...I don't like it. Not one bit." The strain in her voice was evident, and pervasive.

"Very well. Put out a warning to all Omni-Tek departments concerning Marcai. Have them keep their eyes wide and ears open for any information concerning his whereabouts and current activities." Ammicus moved back from the edge of the platform as a sudden chill seized him.

"Yes sir."

"Oh, and please make reservations for two at Baboon's for dinner this evening. Say, Five-ish. And clear your schedule if you have anything planned for that time."

"Sir?"

"Well, I'd rather not eat alone." Ammicus smirked, crossing through the Whompha back to Rome with a sigh of relief.
