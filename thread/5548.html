Since Cosmik asked:

Trade skill planes and cars.

1. Planes and/or cars crafted by players can possibly have lower "to use" requirements (like the emergency treatment packs made by doctors) or faster speeds.

2. New vehicles crafted by players, perhaps similar to the mid that have pros and cons. One example that I am thinking of - the "nano pod". This vehicle raises run speed slightly and hovers the player in an orb above ground. The player can still cast nanos from the pod BUT NOT fire ranged weapons (obviously it would puncture the fabric of the vehicle). The pod increases nano skills but decreases other skills (perhaps evades?? - perhaps some other skill???). Many of the parts are available in the famed city of Jobe (hint, hint.....).

I envision other vehicles crafted by players that enhance benefits to a class. Perhaps one oriented towards doctors, etc......

3. New looks to vehicles. Several thoughts here:

a. A craftable upgrade pack that would permit a player to "upgrade" his existing vehicle and, in essence, exchange its avatar.

b. Different looks for clan vehicles as opposed to Omni-Tek vehicles.

c. New looks for the "class specific" vehicles that I mentioned earlier (not exactly "class" specific - but ones that would be used by a particular class more than others).

d. Guild vehicles. Yes - vehicles linked to the guild system. A way to identify fellow guild members - and simply a cool way to improve the guilds. The "hows" of implementing this idea are much harder.... but come on, Cosmik - you got some good programmers there (plus you can beat DAOC to the punch on their "guild" emblems - competition, competition).

e. A contest for would be vehicle designers...... a contest to outdo the "screenshot" competition, etc. Accept entries, judge entries, the best entry finds its way into the game.

That's it for now.
