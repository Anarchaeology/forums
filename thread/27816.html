Invitation Only
I'm normally not into organizing people but after a fun night of team pvping in 2h0 i think there are a lot of ways we can improve on what we're doing. Despite what you might observe, there are people with restraint on both sides of the "conflict" (hey we need someone to fight).

So here's what i'm thinking - an invation only team pvp contest. Mort might be good as both can save. We set up 1 giant chatgroup for both sides (can have side only chat too but need one that everyone's in). We could have one neutral team made up of both, and one attacking team. We'd need some way to decide on team makeup - lvl and prof wise - and then can pick some coords on where to meet and have at it.

Sounds complicated, but figure if we start small we can learn from our mistakes and eventually expand it as there are people with enough RESTRAINT to be involved. So think it over and offer some suggestions. Might have to keep the first one on the DL but who knows - just as omni and clan unite to fight each other, and players unite to biiatch at FC, we can all get together to have a better time shooting each other.

So to all the people i'd feel safe standing in the arena with, I'll let you know how its coming, or just lemme know if you got a few peopel that wanna scrap - im almost always down.
