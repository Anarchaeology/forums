game mech.: crit% and defensive rings
** disclaimer: all this is what i gathered from various posts, "studies" by some players, common sense, experiements and equation solving . Nothing official from FC, and all this could be very wrong . Also, this may not be exactly the same for professions other than MA.

The crit calculation is understood to work like this:

crit chance = 3% + (AMS/DMS-1)/10+bonus/buffs

e.g. if AMS=2xDMS, crit chance = 3%+(2-1)/10=3%+10%=13%

After testing, I confirm that SG for exmaple does increase ths figure by 8% - meaning in the above example with SG you would get to 21%.

It is important to understand that your MDS is (among others) modified by 2 things:
* the agg/def bar: it is supposed to work by adding betweenn 100 (full def) to -100 (to full agg) so your DMS.
* the defensive ring adds its bonus to DMS too (not to AC as some ppl may think)

Now how less chance to be crit does a +10 defensive ring give you?

the answer depends on the AMS and DMS of the 2 fighting ppl:

% of crit chance gained
DMS 100 200 500 1000
AMS
100 0.9% 0.2% 0.0% 0.0%
200 1.8% 0.5% 0.1% 0.0%
500 4.5% 1.2% 0.2% 0.0%
1000 9.1% 2.4% 0.4% 0.1%


Forget DMS of 100 compared to AMS of 500/1000. You do gain 5-10% less chance of bering crit, but your chance of being crit when AMS/DMS is 1000/100 is 93% without buffs

So basically a +10 defensive ring should give you about 0.5-2% less chance of being crit.

Oh and here is the table for a +20 defensive ring:

DMS 100 200 500 1000
AMS
100 0.8% 0.2% 0.0% 0.0%
200 1.7% 0.5% 0.1% 0.0%
500 4.2% 1.1% 0.2% 0.0%
1000 8.3% 2.3% 0.4% 0.1%

so: no big difference! for crits, a +10 or a higher defensive ring (or 2 defensive rings for that mater) do not make so much of a difference.
