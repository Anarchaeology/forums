Yeah, bump to this guide!

I agree that if loot is set to alpha, then any rares you get is de facto yours. However, I think if it is a profession specific rare, if the profession is in the team, give it to him.

Hogging a GA disc will get you some mondo credits in the short term, but look at it in the long term:

You pick up a GA4 disc from some random mob in a mission.

MrLuckyInsertNonFixerProfHere: Wow! Didnt expect that...

MrLuckyInsertNonFixerProfHere: Well, it's for fixers only and I'm not a fixer, so I guess the fixer should have it.

MrIncrediblyLuckyFixer: WTF! WOW! w00t! THX THX THX!


Think of the reputation THAT would give you in the general populace! The man/woman who just voluntarily gave up GA4 to a team member.

And if that fixer has even an ounce of honor in him, he will be your personal blitz/HoT/Run Buff/NCU ***** for the rest of eternity.

The rest of the team might protest that it should be rolled, but it would sound really pathetic if the item was given by the looter to the person in the team who could/would use it, rather than sell it on eBay.

My 2 cents, but the guide should definitely be required reading for anyone who teams.
