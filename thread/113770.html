Well, if we are going for reposts, here is, in its entirety, the epic atrox poem, "I Cut My Finger on da Cheesegrater" by Ken Kuiper and Johndolk of the South Rubi-Ka Cella-Dwella Atrox Poets Society. A few of you might have been unlucky enough to hear this poem recited live one or twice.

Here goes...

"I Cut My Finger on da Cheesegrater"

"I cut me finger on da cheesegrater
whilst making me diner last night
I wuz making spaghetti bolon-.. bolg-..
.. spaghetti wid tomato sauce
Which I think is really nice
though it is even nicer wid
grated cheese on top, I think.

"I cut me finger on da cheesegrater
It cut of a big chunk of skin off me finger.
It hurt and it bled.
Drops of blood fell on da table.
They might even fallen on da spaghetti
but I could not tell cos da bolo-.. bolg-..
... tomato sauce is also red.

"I cut me finger on da cheesegrater
but I think I said dat before.
The hurt wasn't so bad
but by da time I took care of me cut
the spaghetti got cold
And you just can't reheat spaghetti bolo-.. bolgo..
... spaghetti wid tomato sauce.
That just ain't right."

When I was younger, I attempted poetry to subdue the demons in me...This is the story of a childhood sweetheart...the last of my tolerance for the clans died with her in yet annother clan attack on the frontier.

Long years gone

I dreamt about my love last night
but woke up all alone
her scent was in my nostrils
but she is long years gone

Her hair is tied in ribbons
her face is glowing fair
her smile shines like summer
but she is never there

A lad I was, and she a maid
she told me we would wed
what childish games we playd
but now my love is dead

She whispers sweet and gentle
"I will never leave your heart,
ever we are together.
Death cannot make us part"

I do not feel she left me
I only lag behind
when my bones are cold and dry
my true love I will find.

She stirs my sleep in ripples
like a gentle swiming swan
