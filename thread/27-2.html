MOBs might have several possible items they can drop, each with their own percentage probability of dropping. Some of them will exclude other items from dropping, some not. Most MOBs also have a chance of dropping nothing (excluding credits). So, this "equation" allows MOBs to drop certain items at random times. This defines "unique" because the chance of getting an item is less that the chance of getting an item of a MOB that drops loot all the time. If MOBs were designed to drop loot all the time, the probability of finding certain items would be increased. Would they really be all that unique when someone can kill a MOB over and over and be ensured of a "unique" drop every time? Unlikely. So, the reason MOBs don't always drop loot (in this case unique loot) is to ensure that loot stays unique.

Sorry for the delay in getting back to you Khun. I have some answers for you now.

The Grenade Launcher has an attack and recharge time of 2.5 seconds each. This means 2.5 seconds from the attack starts until the damage is dealt, and another 2.5 seconds from damage is dealt until the next attack can start (during which you may do other stuff without interfering with the attack, e.g. reload).

However, the Grenade Launcher has only one grenade loaded at a time, which means that after the attack time is has to reload, not just recharge. This takes longer than 2.5 seconds, and is why the weapon can only attack about once every ten seconds.

The animation gets run repeatedly when reloading, which is a bug we're aware of and working on fixing.

I hope that helps you out.

Regarding Adventurer concerns:

Multiple ranged buff: Yes indeed, there are plans to include a nano program to buff Multiple Ranged. There's a very good chance it will be an Adventurer profession nano, although not self-only.

Team Free Movement: This will definately be fixed. No estimate at the moment, but it's on it's way.

Polymorphs: There are no current plans to change them at the moment (or to add more that I'm aware of), but if you guys and gals keep giving suggestions, I'll keep bugging the devs. Muaha.

Heals: Adventurer heals have much shorter recharge times than Martial Artist heals, and also go to higher levels. This helps balance out the large heals that Martial Artists obtain. There are no plans on changing any of the healing nanos at this time.

Damage Shields: There are no current plans to change the damage shields, but like above, I'll keep voicing your concerns along as long as you keep passing them to me.

Pistols: New pistols will probably be included later on, though like the other new items coming in patches, they will be available through missions and tradeskills. I know pistols are of a major concern so I'll be doing all I can to make sure the devs know that.
