Also, about the damage/healing problem. The problem is that at some point low-healing classes only have slightly more damage than healing classes. So they can do one of 4 things or a combination of them:

1) Decrease the amount of healling all around.
Very bad idea that.

2) Increase the amount of healing to those classes that have little.
Not ideal, but possibly the only answer.

3) Decrease the damage that healing classes do.
The very worst idea yet.

4) Increase the damage and damage caps of low healing classes.
Very difficult to balance.

Whatever they do, they need to balance all the classes as a whole in their ratio of damage/healing. I worry about their policy of hitting one or two classes at a time. Bug that needs to be fixed
This has been mentioned multiple times on the NT board (and most likely others as well) but since it's not a profession specific issue, it's better to have it here.

The bug is that when casting and recharge time of a nanoformula is less than a certain timeframe (I estimate 2 seconds or so), The casting animation seems to be blocking the next nano from being cast.

When you have casted the nano (for instance a buff or an AoE nuke) and watched the nano-casting bar go up and down (pretty fast though, which is when the problem occurs) you still get the message "waiting for ... " (don't remember the specific feedback, but it's the same you get when trying to cast a nano when another one is in progress of being cast)

In essence, this severely lowers damage over time when using instacast (due to high nanoinit) nukes like AoE (which has short recharge time) Instead of being able to cast one AoE every 1,5 seconds or so, you might get one off every 2,5 seconds or so, primarily due to this bug, although other issues like lag/human reaction has to be concerned.
