The Sol Media Conspiracy
Chapter 1 - My New Job

************************************************** **

Hello, my name is Mitchell Matusz, but most people know me as "Monswuki". This is a story, of my life on Rubi-Ka. But you already know about life on Rubi-ka, don't you?

************************************************** **

I come from a mining colony on Mars owned by Sol Industries, my family was well taken care of, and in turn so was I. Now my parents always had ambitions of me becoming a doctor or bureaucrat, pretty much anything that I would have to go to a fancy college for. But I was restless, I wanted to go see things outside of Mars, whatever the universe had in store for me, it was'nt on some mining colony. No sir, I was going to be an explorer! I wanted to see new frontiers, discover uncharted territories. So when I was twenty years old, against my fathers will, I joined the Sol Industries Campaign for Exploration and Terraforming, the grandest pioneering venture ever organized by a single company. But when I got to the recruiting station, things were'nt quite what I had Imagined.

"You! Yeah you blondie! Over Here!", A man damn near screamed at me from behind the reception counter " You here to sign up for the campaign?", he asked.
" Umm... yeah, I guess." I said with a puzzled expression on my face, how had he known that? But if I was puzzled, then I could'nt tell you what he was. He looked at me as if I had commited some sort of crime. And suddenly he burst into laughter.
"Ha! Your like, what, eighteen? You want to be an adventurer? Right! Mabey you should go to college, and become a doctor or somthing!" More laugther ensued at my expense.
" So, err... where do i sign up?" I said unceartantly. The strange man was very sober all of a sudden.
"Oh, your serious are'nt you? Well then go to the basement, and look for Emil Bunkes' office, you cant miss it." He said and quickly looked away, as if I was diseased.

So I headed down to the basement and found Bunkes' office, the man was right, you couldnt miss it because the door was almost fouteen feet high! I knocked on the door.
" COME IN IF YOU DARE!" resounded a voice from inside. I walked in and came face to face with the largest bipedal organism I had EVER seen. Emil stood almost twelve feet high, with short legs and arms about as big around as I was, his face was heavily scarred, and tattooed. But despite his appearence he seemed like a very gentle person for some reason.
" Emil Bunke's the name, pleased to meetcha!" He extended his hand, I took it and he procceded to crush it for what passed as a handshake.
" Dont be alarmed boy, you have never seen an atrox before? Or at least not one as handsome as I!" He boasted. So this was an atrox, I had heard of them, but never actually saw one, I was told that they only resided on one planet, though I could not remember the name. The bohemoth continued, "Well, I wont go into details, but lets say I am a refugee of sorts." He paused in thought for a second, " but none of that matters, lets talk about your assignment"
" Assignment? " I had thought I would be trained before I started to go out on my own, mabey I really did'nt know what I was getting into.
" Yeah, you will get some field time under your belt before you know it, your not scared are you? This is the chance of a lifetime here boy!" I guess it was.
"Ok, then lets hear it." I said, trying to sound confident.
" Good! Thats what I like to hear!" He wasted no time and shuffled his paperwork until he came to a picture, wich he held up to my face, " This is a Leet." He said matter-of-factly. The picture was of a rodent that had big eyes, and no arms, it was unlike anything I had ever seen. And for some reason it was annoyingly cute.
" Leets are indiginous life forms to the planet of Rubi-Ka, they were engineered by Omni-Tek, and just too damn cute to kill. They are intellegent species who have structured societies, and even thier own language." These Leets were interesting, but I could not figure out what- if anything they had to do with exploration of the galaxy. Emil continued " and what do these cute creatures have to do with the Sol Exploration and Terraforming Campaign, you ask? Well the awnser is simple, nothing!"
Ok, now I was really confused. And Emil could see that further explination of this was required, so he continued, "Leets are just what Sol Industries needs to beat out the compitition, they are the next big thing! And since Omni-Tek does'nt want them we could easily turn thier existence into the greatest advertisement tool ever!" I wasnt sure I really understood what he wanted, but I had an idea.
" So you want me to go find some Leets?" I asked.
" Well we already know where they wre boy, we just need you to go to Rubi-Ka and convince a sizeable amount of them to come back with you. You see boy, as long as they work for us on their own free will, Omni can't touch us with any red tape." And with that, Emil rushed me out the door and on my way. So all I had to do was go to a planet owned by a rival of my employers and ask some genetic experiments gone wrong to acompany me back home. Sounded easy enough, but I couldnt help thinking that there was somthing Emil would not, or could not tell me. He gave me a gridlink full of information on my mission and escorted me to the shuttle wich was taking off as soon as I got there. I was told to study the info on the way there so I could complete my mission as quick as possible. And as the shuttle took off I thought abuout what I had gotten into. I had better adapt fast, I said to myself, because whether I like it or not, this is my new job.
