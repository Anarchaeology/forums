Dawn Clan is recruiting
Dawn clan is looking for new members! Several of the active members of the organization decided to quit AO, so I am looking to revitalize Dawn with some new recruits.

We are a clan-aligned organization. Newbies and vets alike are welcome. The average level of our members is about 50, but we have a wide range of levels, from 1 up to 129. We have no level or expertise requirement to join, all we ask is that you are a nice person who we can all get along with easily.

We are a very laidback organization, we try to have fun and not take ourselves too seriously. Our main purpose is to make good friends who we can hunt, do missions, or just hang out and have a good time with. If all of the above sounds good to you, contact Cayana or Tann in-game to be recruited.
