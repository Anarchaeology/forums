A Ghost From the Past...
A Ghost From the Past...

The waters of the Stret River were calm and relatively smooth as the moon hung high above Rubi-Ka, bathing its surface in blue tinted light. A silver dart shot through the air above the peaceful waters, A Yalmaha travelling at full speed towards its unknown destination. The pilot dropped the Yalm down to skim above the waters, throwing up a spray of water 30 meters high behind him as he passed. Two beeps sounded in the pilots compartment and Garret "Valaik" Clasen pulled back on the throttle cutting his speed down to a third of what it was as he angled his plane over two rocks near the shore towards a wall of rock ahead. The yalm flew towards the wall of rock with no signs of slowing and then dissapeared inside, a slight distortion marking the place where he had passed through the rock. The Yalm moved slowly down the corridor inside the wall of rock, gliding into the large hanger that took up most of the lower level, and parking among the collection of broken down Yalmaha's and heavy transports along the far wall.

Valaik shut down all the Yalms systems before releasing the hatch and climbing out and down from the Yalm, stretching after his long flight he made his way to the large blast doors that were set in the south wall. Tapping a code into the panel next to the doors Valaik took a step back as the Doors squeeled open, allowing him access to the rest of the base. Moving through the doors when they were half open Valaik was pleased to see that the work on this tunnel had been completed since his last visit, the workers moving on to the next level up. With a smile spreading across his face he made his way to the lift at the end of the hall, glancing into the parts storage rooms that lined the hall seeing a few mechanics at work on different projects. As he reached the lift he keyed in the command that would call the lift and then take him to the command level. The lift arived with a hiss of escaping air and Valaik stepped aboard and was hurried up to the top of the facility, smiling as the lift doors opened fully he made his way down the Hallway to the Command center. The mainframe computer took his palm print and retinal scan at the door and allowed him access, moving inside Valaik took a seat in the chair in the center of the room, bringing up the diagnostics and system readouts for the whole complex.

"Good news all around so far, Seems the techies are getting more systems up and running each day, Missmaul is back from the dead, and Corgan is getting married tonight. Now that's how a day should go, not a problem in sight." said Valaik with a content sigh as he leaned back into his chair and relaxed.

Valaik smiled happily and tapped a few commands into the control panel attached to the arm of his seat and brought up a list of music from his archives, picking an ancient earth group he let the computer pick which songs to play at random. As the music began to play he leaned back into his chair and let his eyes fall shut, enjoying the music and letting the previous days tension slip away. Valaik fell asleep quickly, dreams of his beautiful wife Ravia comming to him, of quiet times alone with her and away from the chaos of the world. A smile crossed his face as he lay dreaming, always happiest when he was with her, even the dreams of her made his life seem that much better.

In a moment everything changed, The entire facility shook and the muffled sound of an explosion could be heard through the walls, sirens began to scream and Valaik was torn from his dream.

"Warning: An Explosion has been detected on Storage Level Two, Section Eight. Cause Unknown. Security Personel En Route." said the even female voice of the computer as the music was cut off.

"Computer, Cut those damn alarms off!" yelled Valaik and a moment later the alarms quit screaming inside the command room.

"Ok, now what the hell is going on? If some smartass was screwing around in those storage bays and caused this they better hope they didn't survive the blast." growled Valaik as he began typing in commands, trying to bring up the scematics of the base and the cameras in that area so he could get a look.

The base schematics came up on the monitor on his left, glancing at the three dimensional image he saw the computer had updated it to account for the damage. At least three sections of Level Two were decimated, the roof above that section had also been weakened by the explosion and there was a cave in warning for most of that level and Level Three above it. Growling Valaik saw that all but one of the cameras in the Section Eight were down, damaged by the explosion, the only surviving one was a drop down cam that was protected in its compartment in the roof. Slowly the Camera slid free of its enclosure and began to scan the room, the picture comming up on the screen in the command room. Valaik surveyed the destruction on the screen, the entire area was a blacked scorch mark now, Melted plastic containers were pressed against the walls, and the door to the main hallway was missing, probably blown out into the hallway by the explosion. Motion caught his eye as the camera panned back around the room, forcing the camera back to the door a large figure made his way into the room, A battlesuit clad atrox by the size of him. He shook his head and cut the camera feed for the moment, instead bringing up the inventory log for Section Eight.

"Now what the hell did we have stored in there?" muttered Valaik as he continued typing into his controls.

Finally the inventory list came up on the screen and Valaik began to skim through it looking for anything that could have caused an explosion. All the merchandise in that section just arrived this morning, Fresh offworld goods that were not even sorted yet. No explosives of any sort, No hazardous materials that could cause an explosion, most of it was Armor from Sol Banking and a few assorted luxury items, nothing that should have caused that explosion. The comm at Valaik's elbow chirped and he tapped the command that would let the call through.

"Val, I'm down here in Section Eight, One of the Engineers thinks he found the source of the explosion. Seems whatever it was was in Container XJ-484-L7. I've got everyone in Battlesuits to protect them from cave in's and I've got the Engineers moving in here now to reinforce the roof." spoke the gravely voice of Flatbed through the comm, noticeably clearer than the last time he was here.

"Good work Flat, thank's for the info, I'll check and see what was in that container and where it came from. See if any of those Engineers can find out what kind of explosive it was and why the hell it didn't show up on the scanners." Responded Valaik.

"Gotcha, I'll get to work on it right now boss." said Flatbed as he cut the signal.

Valaik sighed and keyed in the container number to see what had been listed as its contents. The computer quickly brought up the contents, it was supposed to be a crate of Rare Sake from offworld, an order Valaik himself had placed nearly a month ago. It came from one of his regular offworld suppliers, but there was a link to the original owner. Opening the link Valaik looked at
the picture and felt his heart stop. He quickly glanced at the name and then back to the picture, The name not matching with his memory of who this man was. Images began to flash in Valaik's mind, scene's from years before on another world. A woman's face, fear showing on it brightly.....A Firefight in a Hanger, Bullets and bolts of energy lighting up its dark confines.....The womans face again, this time covered in blood, her eyes staring blankly at him. He shook his head to clear his mind of the memories, looking again at the picture of a very old friend, and enemy. Thelonis, his old partner, The man that had killed his first love and nearly killed him before comming to Rubi-Ka.

"Seems you're still trying to finish what you started all those years back....old friend." spat Valaik, anger dripping from his voice. The comm beeped again and he tried to compose himself before hitting the button that let the call through.

"Boss, We know what caused the explosion. The Engineer says it was some type of low yield Liquid explosive. The damage was fairly minor so if it was deliberate they need to work on their technique." spoke Flatbed, his voice echoing slightly, Valaik guessed he was the one in the Battlesuit from earlier.

"No Flat, He knows what he's doing. It wasn't designed to do more than annoy us, it was a message, for me. A ghost from my past seems to have found me and I doubt this will be the end. Flat, you're in charge here till I get back. I've got to get dressed, I have a wedding to get to. Keep me informed." said Valaik with a hint of sadness in his voice.

"Sure thing Boss, have fun and cheer up." said Flatbed jokingly, followed by a deep laugh that was cut short as he closed the channel.

Shaking his head Valaik stood and made his way to the door, passing through it and down the cooridor to the quarters he kept here to change for his friend Corgan's wedding. His shoulders hunched forward as he walked, his hands in his pockets, lost in thought at why Thelonis would choose now to come for him. Five years had passed since the night his world had been torn apart by a blast from his best friends Rifle, Taking the life of the woman he loved and nearly killing Valaik in the process. He had ran that night, ran from the fallen form of his love, ran from his friend turned enemy, got on a shuttle and set a course for Rubi-Ka. Valaik would get a chance for vengence after all, Thelonis wouldn't use some bomb to kill him, he would insist on doing it himself.

"Come and get me."
