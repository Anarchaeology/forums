Rate Yourself: Player Energies
(Warning: Mass Doses of Personal Opinion Follow; Take it or Leave It)

I actually sat down and tried to reason this out a few nights ago, and came to a conlusion that there are four general "energies" of players in AO, any one of which can occur at the expense of the other three:

RP Energy: The amount of energy expended on personal roleplaying. This includes chatting IC, dancing, socializing, emoting, getting involved in player-run events, and being as "real" on Rubi-Ka as possible.

Level Energy: The amount of energy expended on levelling a character. This includes hunting and going on missions.

Storyline Energy: The amount of energy expended on following the official AO storyline. This includes checking the AO webpage for news bulletins, reading guild postings, looking through the BBS, and acquiring info through guilds and guild leaders.

Participation Energy: The amount of energy expended in the seeking out and participating in events. This includes player-run events or official events; those that won't affect the storyline or do to any degree.

I figured this was a good method for rating the type of player I am (and sometimes others when I'm feeling judgemental .

Rating Myself:

RP: 50%

Level: 10%

Storyline: 25%

Participation: 15%
