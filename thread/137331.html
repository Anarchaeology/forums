Suggestion to semistatic quest
Ok. We got Alvins quest. Both in game and on net. It is on the "todo" list for almost any player 50+ that knows about it (Ok, not part 3.... But)
If you like to know how then just look at some webpage and you got the resipte. That is 1 quest, 1 soultion, 10000 players that can find it. Same for Jack Leghopper etc.

So? What is the suggestion?
Ok. Lets say there was ONE reward. Just like in Alvin. All wants it, just like in alvin. But there is 10000 ways to get it? Sure someone would get up with some kind of resipt so you get the general idè, but you would still have something to thinkabout so solve it.
How shall this be done?
Let say it is one startpoint. There you could get a quest. The quest will be one of the following:
1. Get a spessific item of someone.
2. Kill someone to get an item
3. Tag a tower of org XXX
4. Get hold of an item that need a tradeskill prosess. (You may already have it, buy it, build it, and so on. It is just a item involving a tradeskill prossess.)
5. etc.
You would get one of those random. And for some the result shuld be bringed to someone. That someone is also randomly chosen all around RK.

In case of quest 1 you could get an additional quest to do something else random before you get the thing.

When you then finaly locate and get to the next person you may get a new mission on the same terms as above. And so on. Finaly you end up somewhere with the final request and you bee awarded.

This would lead to a lot of possible ways to do it, a LOT of possible person to visit (There are a lot of person on RK with spessific name that do nothing for the moment) so you would perhaps get something to work for, not just follow a respite.

Just a thought
