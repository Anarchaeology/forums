I hate people who wish for no PvP. They've gone so far as to make it RESTRICTED PvP, do you really want to step on everyone who likes PvP competition's toes and remove an entire aspect from the game (thus making it EQ in the future) just because you kept wandering away from safety without any backup? Please. If you're going to walk into griefer territory without anyone to watch your back you DESERVE to be ganked. Know the risks, or don't take them.

In EQ, there were PvP servers. 3 of them. Each usually had about 1000 people on them, but i'd imagine that number has gone up since I last played EQ. In it, I played a Dark Elf. Regularily, the Dark Elves would all gather together, and attack Freeport. We wouldn't go so far as to attack the guards, but we'd attack and kill everyone outside of Freeport. With us were lvl 50's, and lvl 1's. Was there any point to us attacking? Nope. We'd just go over there and have a LOT of fun trying to keep the zone for as long as we could. You're just plain ignorant if you think people won't go to a free PvP zone. What do you think all the lvl 200's will do? Sit around all day counting the grains of sand on Rubi-Ka? Hell no! They'll go out and kick some ass! I really hope that Funcom listens to this and impliments one or two zones like this.

Oh, and don't talk about coding. They've made AO, they can make one minor adjustment to a zone or two without killing themselves i'm sure.
