My vision ... Motorcycles!!!!!
I am a motorcycle enthusiast. Sport bikes and otherwise. I really think if different types of motorcycles were offered in the game it would be great. And onsidering the size of some of the robots I see i dont think full sized bikes for people to ride would be a problem. You could have the bike ride it and people could still see what you look like aswell. And it wouldnt be a problem of running over people because this game doesnt cause collsions when people move against one another.
There should be 3 classes of bikes (in which each class would have custom colors and different body styles). The sport bike type (i really like the bike that Kaneada had on the classic anime movie Akira), the chopper type (ya know like a harley), and then jet bikes for the rich people or the techno freaks (kinda like jet moto). Now when I say kinda like a harley or whatever that doest mean i suggest copying a harley i am just using that as example as the kind of bike. I think if funcom took this and did soemthing with it it would be great but they would have to make thier own concept models for the bikes for originality.
If bikes were added then there could be motorcycle gangs and all sorts of cool stuff maybe even races if that could be setup correctly with the current physics system.
