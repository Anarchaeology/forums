What could be more simple than /coords
I guess I'm not the only one who presses F9 constantly while flying in his Yal to find out what coordinates am I in and where do I go.

Instead of that, why don't you FC guys implement a script that will enable us, the players, to see any inserted point on the map and compass.

It will work like this:
Users type /coord 2050 1938
Then it shows this coord both on his zone map (if it's installed), the planet map, but most importantly, a yellow line on his compass!
