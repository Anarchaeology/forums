What will you do?
What will you do when Rubi-Ka is nothing more than a toxic wastland? The unending advancment of 'new' buildings leaves behind it a wastland of shattered homes and run down shacks. Athens, Omni1 it matters not! Both are just as guilty of pushing out the boundries of their cities with no concern for trying to hold them together. Look you to the sewers of Omni1 and ask if those are trully a 'desirable' place to live.

What will you do when the world has lots its Notum through the thoughtless gouging of that one natural resource? Once this most precious material is gone, what will happen to this world? It is not on any major trade route! It is the -reason- for the trade routes, once the Notum is gone -no one- will want to come to Rubi-ka any longer. Pitty those who need its natural abundance of Notum to egsist, the Nano-breed; pitty them for their time is limited.

Egsistance was, before Notum. And it can be still, without. Rather than strip another world of its resources and then move on like the 200kilo cockroaches you are; leave Rubi-Ka now.

What will become of any one, so inclined to say on Rubi-Ka when nothing is left; but Dust and Ash.

Think on it. You'll know what to do.
