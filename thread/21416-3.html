Remember, the regular IP points, beyond the one time full reset, only reset ONE skill! Is that going to be enough to allow people to experiment with a new weapon type or skills they haven't dared put IP into? No way. Is it going to be enough so that people who make gimped characters not knowing what they are doing can start fresh? Nope. Is it even enough so that an experienced player trying a new class can get comfortable and correct mistakes? No.

If the were going to give us 90 IPR points as promised, then allow us to earn more at title levels, that would be what they promised. How many of us would have used those 90 points to reset EVERY skill? I would venture to say Zero. To say that a full reset is comparable to what they promised is just a disgusting ruse.

A one time full reset may allow people to fix some gimped characters, but even that isn't certain. That's a lot of work to redistribute IP to every stat in your character, with out making any mistakes and assuring that you will still be able to re-equip all your implants, armour, weapons and still be able to use all your nanos! What if you make a mistake, not even one in calculation but just a dumb error? You are totally screwed again.

This does nothing to address character gimping moving forward, the small amount of points they are giving us at each title level is NOT enough to fix a broken character and definitely isn't enough to allow people to experiment with new things.

We end up with so many cookie cutter clones of people in every class because if you don't follow a tried and true template and decide to try something a little different, the risks are to high. A real IPR system would allow people to try new variations and not be affraid that if it doesn't work out they have a hopelessly gimped character to show for it!

Alternative One: Every character, not just existing ones but new characters moving forward, should be given a stash of IPR points from the start. How many? I don't know, but enough so that people can try different paths a number of times and still be able to reset enough stats to try something totally different every now and again.

Still allow the accumulation of further points moving forward, at title levels.

Alternative Two Award IPR pojnts at title levels in the amount given in the patch description, but allow each of those points to be spent on a total IP reset. This limits the frequency with which people can totally rework their characters, but still allows them to try different things with the ability to recover from mistakes.

Alternative Three With the exception of the one time, full reset offer, make it so that IPR points only reset a single skill. However, award IPR points much more frequently than just at title levels. Once a level, once every five levels? Either would be better than what is being offered, would allow people to try truely different paths and still recover, while limiting frequency and scope of changes over time.

I would be happy with any of the above, the system as it stands now is not going to do much for anyone, doesn't allow people to fix mistakes moving forward to any large degree and does nothing to allow people to try different alternatives to established templates with out fear of ending up with a totally gimped character.
