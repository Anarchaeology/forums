Division 9 RSGE, are YOU up to the challenge?
With the restructuring of our Initial Entry Training program now complete, we are capable of training more recruits than ever before. We now seek to fill up our current class so we can begin processing them quickly. Optimal class size is 30 persons. Training time takes about 4 weeks. Training includes field excersizes, pvp, and classwork. If you're not up to all of that, we're not for you.


A little about us,

First, the basics. Omni faction, Rk1 server. We are an Omni-Tek para-military organization with a long and storied history here on Rubi-ka. Initially created to monitor and enforce laws regulating genetic experimentation, and researching the various mutant strains on our world, we quickly realised that to study many of these hostile creatures we would have to defend ourselves. Our military aspect grew quickly and has become one of the most well trained Special Forces units deployed outside of Omni Armed Forces.

Our mix of science and martial power has been a potent one, and we have earned our place in Rubi-kan history. Now, we offer you the chance to be a part of that history.

If you think you have what it takes, you too can be Division 9 RSGE. Those looking for handouts, freebies or an easy ride, hit the BACK button on your browser now. But if you have integrity, pride, and honor.... if you are selfless, dedicated, and motivated then you might just be for us.

We are a heavy Roleplaying org, if you're not into that, we're most likely not for you. But if you crave structure, a military sense of discapline and a group that values comradery check us out. . . We're looking for a few good troops.

If you have questions, contact myself or Chaplain Valarken in game. We'll do our best to assist you.

http://division9.rsge.net/

Let me bump this by adding a bit of information for those of you who don't know us.

Division 9 is a mid-size, currently expanding guild with a deep, rich history. We were one of the first major Omni orgs, and have often worked hand-in-hand with other orgs and groups such as Omni-Pol, OTSEC, and others. We have a tradition of pride and excellence, which is displayed in the gun that bears our name; The Division 9 Plasmaprojector, well known and loved, was named after us to honor our Division for our dilligence against the Clans over the years.

We have participated in a great number of large scale events, most notably the signing of the Tir Accords and the campaigns against the Dust Patrol in Mort and elsewhere.

We are currently recruiting in a bid to expand our numbers and our power once more, and we need you. We have a tradition of strong roleplaying, and a tight military organization; however you don't need to be a hardcore RPer to join us. We seek Omni employees from all callings, and welcome the experienced and unexperienced alike.

The majority of our players are from the US timeframes; however, European and Asian players are indeed welcome as well. The only thing we demand of our members is dedication. To join Div9 is to be one of the best. As Lt. Trantor put it, Are you up to the challenge?

Come to our website, http://division9.rsge.net, and come see for yourself. Or ask one of us if you like, we're a friendly group and always willing to lend a hand.
