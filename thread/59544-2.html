Well, i still find 2ho fun. Despite the zoning, which we ALL do no matter how much we hate it, it gives a battlefield warlike experience having 6 people rush 6 others. I like the urban setting and the 'hotspots' of 2ho, and how they're set up, too.

I think the people saying both sides need to shape up are abseloutley correct. Of course it's true that omni has less pvp-dedicated people, but im talking about the 'tactics' being used. When the clanners camp the whompa, omni becomes uninterested because there is simply no way to penetrate all the people we place on the hill when we DO camp it, grid entry or not. It's been like this ever since TC broke, i've noticed - I remember when we used to have 20 people at the hill and there would be big battles near the whompa, which would sometimes result in clan losing...it just doesn't happen anymore. What happens is that all the bandwagoner pvp'ers you've never seen before think that 2ho is all about zone camping, clans get bored, omnis there give up, numbers on both sides decrease, however more will be called into action by omni because clan's victory goes to our head...The next thing you know all our numbers have reduced to 5, because of the boredom, and 30 omnis sweep in. It happened last night. We held 2ho for about 4-5 hours, and i must say it was a very disorganized attack on omni's part. I'm pretty sure its what inspired Johiyat to make this thread. Then at about 10 o clock eastern Jacksr and firedragon rounded up an omni army 25+ strong. The remaining 5 of us were forced back into the bunker, and again, the bandwagoner pvp'ers are what destroy these battles. Let me tell you what happens.

You'll zone out as a clanner, and see a ton of omni faces you've never seen before, no titles(not that im saying this matters a whole lot), no nothin - you've NEVER seen these people hit another player before or even enter a 25% zone. The more honorable omni pvp'er are moving back to the hill to give clans a chance similar to how clanners tell each other to move back to bunkers, but the noobs keep zone camping...Soon enough you're reduced to being able to do nothing, and we blame each other for leaving? Or blaming each other how lame we are debuffing and zoning the people camping us, or me alpha'ing all the brats who run into bunkers saying 'clan sucks' even though the rest of their friends were the ones who did the work? I'm telling you, having a fully automatic weapon rocks. Nothing fills me up more than seeing one of those trash-talking bandwagoners do a polish dance to a bunker in a root or a snare and then get alpha'd for 40% hp a special, and then send you tells about how 'lucky' you are or what a 'gimp' you are. There would be a lot less rant posts about 2ho if some respect was taught.

Now i'll be honest - I bunker hop like a rabbit when i'm outnumbered...For someone to come to me and say i'm a ***** because i won't alpha someone who's HP will definately be only 50% after it and i'm killed immediately after, im sorry but that's simply ridiculous. There are reasons for zoning, reasons for debuffing and then zoning, and its all to stop people from camping. As far as my 'bunker hopping' method i find it extremely effective. The better group of omni players usually know what i'm doing when i bunker hop. I've seen some higher level omnis find it so funny they just sit there and let me do it to the small ones. The brats are so new at pvp that theyll follow me just to keep their own satisfaction...their feeling..."I 0wnzor stromm." Soon enough i'm out of dangerous debuff/gun range and i have one, two, three even sometimes 4 less problems to worry about after a gunshot or two.

This dude last night named 'Spear1,' never seen em before. After all the omnis saw i was the only one who was willing to stay in 2ho, they all just left. I caught this guy when he was alone. Rooted his tms, came back, and he ran for the zone. Then he calls me a *****. Then i see some green NT...she runs instantly because of the simple fact i am higher level than her, when i've been killed by green NTs that use crown/IEF on me. She didn't even try, and i nailed her. I go back to the bunker, this MA who kept following me left, and then i zone out to see this level 140 agent who is standing right beside the bunker, not even sneaked. I decided to test his pH..perhaps he was afk. Grace ran out, and he attacked me. i alpha'd him, and he started running immediately, and killed him. The whole time i was thinking 'could he really be this stupid?' The point of this isn't to brag how i raped the heck out of lower levels, its to show that they are lacking pvp knowledge and they need it before they can pvp responsibly, and do well. It isn't fun to fight people who act like mobs.

Clan does this too. And i think it's time we taught the new pvp'ers to be a little bit more sportsmanlike. I can remember being such an ******* to all the arena gankers, sending them hate tells when i was a lower level and just getting into pvp, but now i know a lot of these people, and i can call most of them my friends in AO now. Of course some of these people will whine at me after i got them back for ganking me at level 90, but hey, they had the choice to shake hands and they were the ones that said 'no' the way i see it.

However i find pvp fun when me and other omni like Maddiganed can exchange each other tells of 'nice shot' 'good fight' rather than '**** you ganker' from a person i never did anything personal to, or say anything personal to from an antagonist stance. These are the people who i'll be a complete ******* to and i feel it's within reason.

2HO is most spontaneous when it comes to the fun gauge. Sometimes it can be considered 'lame,' 'empty' 'fun' 'awesome night.' Different things happen. All lame tactics will happen to lame people, and then they are the first to cry for nerfs. I have but one thing to say:

PvP is a blood sport, if you can't bleed then shape up or ship out.
