quote:
Originally posted by Snarf
I've always wondered WHY people in clans and OT care about peace. So there's fighting. People die. But they instantly resurrect. So what's the big deal?

Who cares if people kill one another if the biggest problem is that they've got to find some change to get a bus home from the reclaim terminal?
Spoken by one who has never been in a war. What we are in now is not a war. In a war you don't go home to your warm apartment at night. Wars are spent in mud up to your knees or marshes up to your chest, with rats and snakes and leeches nipping at you constantly. More people die of disease and dysentery in a war than ever fall to hostile ammunition. These are slow, painful, debilitating deaths that damage the mind as much as the body. And the mind doesn't return to its scanned state, you keep the memories of all the horrors you experience in war.

Do you think that a child who has felt what it is like to be blown to smithereens a few dozen times doesn't take that trauma with him or her? Or a mother or father that has seen their child suffer from hunger and cold after their house was blown up and they were forced onto the road as refugees? I have seen many jackbooted military theorists or rear-echelon officers yearn for some kind of never-ending eternal war. I have never seen anyone that has been in a war do the same.

Which Brings Up The Question ...
All of which brings up the question ...

Like most of my clan characters, Pluggenplay hates the company, has no intention of ever working for the company, begrudges the profit on drinks she buys at Baboons, and will cut and run the second the Council makes the first gesture towards recognizing Omni-Tek's monopoly on notum.

On the other hand, she also has no intention of declaring war on her own, not with an official cease-fire on. Yes, there are no shortage of Omni-Tek saboteurs in the cellars under Tir; she's got an apartment full of Weird Looking Bombs to show for it. But if the cease-fire breaks down, it won't be at HER hand, not unless the company provokes it by doing something really, really outrageous. (Say, disabling the Clans' Insurance Terminal system. Or crippling the Suppression Gas dispensers and reclaim camping in downtown Tir. Or actually using the Permanent Killing Weapons we keep hearing about. Or maybe if one of these Weird Looking a-Bombs actually goes off.)

So what should she have done when, accidentally whompa-ing into Athen on her way from Newland to Tir (wrong turn), she finds herself in the middle of an organizational meeting for yet another clan attack on 2H0? Planned for 20 minutes later?

She thought about griding to Omni Trade and making an announcement -- but that'd mean selling out her own side. So ultimately, she decided to keep her mouth shut, go home to Tir and take a nap, and read about it in the papers tomorrow.

Right decision? Was there a better one she could have made?
