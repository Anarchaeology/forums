We are currently on 13.9.

14.0 is up on test. You can easily check it out by yourself, or read the patch notes. The highlight of it is team missions. Other than that, there are quite a lot of technical/graphical changes, that not everyone care about.

IF there will be a 14.1, it will be released to fix any extreme issues that will arise from the 14.0 patch. Maybe someone find a way to exploit, or someone find that textures are missing... The odd (.1, .3, .5 and so on) patches are never sheduled. They are not really wanted, but it is much neater, and easier to organize. They can already now refer to the 14.2 patch as just 14.2, instead of saying "It will be the next patch, unless we have to patch in between."

After this comes the confirmed patch 14.2. No words on when it will be released.
