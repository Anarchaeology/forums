This is just an idea on how the new boards can be moderated.

In my opinion, moderating could be done by the player base itself.
In order for this to work:
* The lists of the moderators for each forum have to be available at the top.
* Moderators should have an increased PM capacity and have their PM enabled.

How it would work:
A point system. Every time a player sees a post that they think is offensive and shouldnt be there they PM the mod with the location of the post. The mod then, in his PM inbox, keeps track of the number of replies citing a certain person (this could be done by players putting the the name of the offender in the title). All of the reported posts will be evaluated to be moved to the "hidden" thread. After say, three reports the player gets a warning, and after six, he gets banned. Of course, these numbers could change depending on the feedback involvement. After a certain time, like 2 months, the numbers could be reset.

Why do I see this as an alternative?
I see it as a viable alternative since it transfers part of the work to the player base. It also allows moderators to know what it is that forum users consider offensive, off-topic, or shouldn't be on the forums.

How to keep the burden off one mod?
By rotation. The moderators that have PMs sent to them could rotate weekly.

Please remember that this is only an *idea*.

I think Jynne pretty much nailed it. The boards are a great venue for player-to-player discussion and they function adequately for Funcom-to-player announcements. However, they really should not be considered as a vehicle for player-to-Funcom communication. It is my understanding that the Professionals Program was intended to serve this purpose.

Unfortunately, there is a widespread perception that if you scream loud enough on the boards then the devs will hear you. The old PvP board was a classic example of this in action. The fixer board has also been demonstrating this tendency in the past few days, following the Stratics dev chat. At one time or another, practically every board has degenerated into a shouting match for FC attention as a new nerf (either real or perceived) hits the community.

Ultimately, these discussions usually ensue because players feel wronged by the decisions of Funcom or by the actions of other players. In most cases, the discussions tend to peter out after the concerned parties take the opportunity to "get it off their chests." By this point, the discussions usually degerenate into a one-on-one flame war until a) the topics are moderated/removed or b) the flamers run out of vitriol and energy.

The net result is that players clearly need a venue in which to air their discontent. With this in mind, I would suggest the creation of a "Gripes, Grievances and Vendettas" forum. Disgruntled players could use this as a venue to clear the air about issues that are affecting them and existing moderators could use this as a dumping ground for off-topic content from the rest of the boards.

You could even go so far as to not moderate the discussion on this board alone. Allow users to criticize, namecall, flame, disparage, etc. to their heart's content with the clear undestanding that a response from Funcom will not be forthcoming. In this context, the proposed forum would go a long way towards cleaning up the boards as they are right now by eliminating the overtly negative discussions that are increasingly diluting the more valuable topics.

Last but not least, by providing a focal point for trolls, flamers, spammers and the like, it would be so much easier for the rest of the community to ignore them. That's my 0.02 credatz.
