It's dependent on missions settings. You need to set max XP.
Do lllmmr settings and missions 1-3 gives most XP reward.)

It's also very lvl dependent.

My 162 trader gets just short of 1M xp.
My 132 gets 200-250k.
A 140 gets about 400k.

If a higher lvl person than you pull the mission, your reward will be capped at 5% of what you need for a total lvl. That's one reason the highest lvl player in team should fetch missions. A lvl 186 pulling 50% mission will give more xp than a lvl 150 pulling a 193-194. (XP from mobs doesn't scale with difficulty, much bigger xp reward)

So below lvl 130 or so, don't concider mission-reward, it's just 2-3 mobs, which you would kill in the time you use between two short missions.

Another point is that BS missions give 3.5-5M xp, or 5 missions per lvl at 160. Most people like to save with 5M xp. At a HUGE PM/HQ missions, I might loose 10-15M on Boss.

Others have already nailed it about the BS missions.

At level 171 I can pull 1.something million exp mission rewards. I require about 27 million exp to level to level 172.

In the average BS mission I gain between 3 and 4 million exp from the mobs, and then another million+ from the reward bumps the total mission yield into the 5 million range. Usually a good team can blast through a BS mission in about 20-25 minutes, 30-35 if there are more than a handful of Aces/RMs.

So, 27 million divided by 5 million is 5.4. It takes 5.4 missions for me to level. Assuming 25 minutes per mission, it will take me about 2.5 hours of actual mission time to level.

Bump that up to 3 to 3.5 hours factoring in lag, people going afk, pulling missions after the first two, and needing to refill team slots on occasion.

If the missions were places we actually had to travel to, it would probably take 5 or more hours per level, from the additional travel time and rounding up people who get lost and go LD and such, and from the fact that there just isn't much good hunting.

Now... RL, I have a job, and I do like to sleep, eat, and do things besides AO. So I have about four to five hours of play time per night; six on occasion. If my goal is to level, the BS mission option is far superior, because I'll gain about 1.75 levels per night at it. From Monday through Friday I'll gain 9 levels. Fighting outside I'll gain at most one level per night, so in the same time, I'll only gain 5 levels, and possibly fewer.

So that's why alot of people mission in Home.
