Damage-adding gloves seem dandy to me.
I've just started playing a martial artist (taking a break from my other character whilst a friend catches up levels ;), and while reading through message board posts and the like, the main comment seems to be lower and lower damage as martial artists go up in level, as compared to other classes.

Now, you can throw a rock any direction you please and hit a similar "conversation" (haw) going on about -any- of the professions. Mostly it revolves around skills or nanos.

For martial artists, the solution, to me, seems pretty simple; create gloves (two or three brands, for variety's sake) that add X amount to minimum damage, based on quality level, et cetera, like any other weapon. The trade-off would be that it only added that minimum damage, not a range like others.

Maybe have one brand add basic physical damage, while others add slightly less damage, but of a different type; radiation or chemical, or fire or cold, or whatever.

I don't know. I'm just surprised there aren't even something like brass knuckles. =p Thoughts? Opinions? Flames can be directed to the nearest brick wall. ;)
