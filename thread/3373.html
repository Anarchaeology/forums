NT suggestions - Q & A
Should NTs get a range advantage?
Yes they should. A max range of 45-55m for NTs could be implemented and tested on the test dimension

Should NTs have have a larger nanopool than other professions?
Yes they should. They are the only profession that is 100% reliant on nanopool to deal damage. Maybe a new line of nanos that buffs max nanopool.

Should Nanotechs have a lower resist/fumble rate?
I don't see why not. As I mentioned above, NTs are 100% reliant on nano to deal damage. A few resists and fumbles equals failure for a NT. If a melee/ranged/pet profession has a few rounds of 'blackholes' it usually means the fight is just prolonged a by 15-25 seconds.

Should Nullity sphere be more common?
Yes. It's currently the rarest nano/instruction disc in game by a huge margin. The drop rate of Carbon Rich Rock could also be increased
