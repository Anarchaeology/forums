I am crashgod... thou shalt have no other crashgods before me.
Unfortunate as it is I beleive i have crashed the game a whole lot more than every other player on the planet. Here are a few suggestions in case support does not happen to know the answer.

1: If you just updated your video card you'll need to uninstall and reinstall AO. This is the "quick fix" I used when i went from voodoo3 to geforce2. Afterwhich i update my drivers, defrag my system and do general all arround "housekeeping" on my computer.

2: If you tend to crash while in a specific area, slowly start avoiding that area for a while. Usually it's because your video card starts to overload (personal experience) so adjust the view sliders a bit to get more reasonable movement rates in mass populated zones.

3: When crashing, try and add in what you last did. I used to crash every single time after accepting a mission and attempting to leave borealis. This gets the "bughunters" to find the program glitch without much effort.

For a player who has crashed the game over 1050 times I feel that theres been some severe improvement. My last crash was 2 weeks ago (from the time of this post) and players are more likely to help you out with problems than the email staff. Ask us every now and then. We might be able to solve your problems faster. Try these out and it might just fix all your problems.

ps. everybody envisions a stable game. even EQ isnt perfect still and they continue to watch for and fix bugs on a constant basis.

AO has plenty of people attempting to fix the bugs. Wait... it'll get better. I know it will.
