Just to elaborate a bit on the clan rules on rk1, some people have been pushing for weighted raffles, but so far that hasn't flown.

Asmoran has written a bot which we use to raffle items which is entirely random. XX people enter the raffle via a bot command, then the bot rolls a number between 1 and XX, then goes through its database for that particular raffle and finds the person listed as number Y (the random number) and that person wins.

lvl 125+ only. Many people win on the first few times to Tara, those are the lucky ones. I however have been to countless raids and entered countless raffles and I've never won a single thing. And since my guild president wrote the bot, this can assure you that there is no funny business going on, or else you would think I would have won something by now

All in all, it works very well, and 99% of the people seem to be very happy with the way Clan does things currently. Faster than picking numbers and rolling, the old way we did things, and more "random" giving everyone the exact same chance at winning loot.
