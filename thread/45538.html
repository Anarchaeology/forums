Revelation
While sipping Java in the food court of Newland, Sun, known as "Snyyper" in some circles, digests what she has just read.

It wasn't supposed to fall into her lap. She was just on a simple mission, assasinate a mad bomber. Mad? In this office complex that is so orderly and clean? It was just a book, locked in a chest. A nifty conversation piece, she thought at the time, for her new table in her new apartment.

She was just a simple war-orphan. She was raised neutral, but that's another story. She was befriended by a young woman named Danykalla, watched the woman die a final death and decided to follow her friend's fervor for the Clans...

But the book....the history....the past....

She sees a path before her, and wonders if she stepped the wrong way.

When Danykalla died, first Sun was angry at Omni's screwed up technology when the 'insurance" shorted out and Dany never re-appeared.

After reading her stolen book, she wonder at the Clan's expectance at having gifts such as "insurance" given to them as their do right. She began to see the warring clans as spiteful children. All the destruction by the terrorists.

"Perhaps it is too one sided, this book, perhaps there is another side," she says aloud to no one, emptying her java mug.

She pulls at her lip in thought, and decides she must find more about the truth in this war before she further binds herself to one side or the other.

Sitting in an impressive metal chair with moniters blinking steadily, Jax is asked a multitude of questions. She's calm and fully expected this treatment...after all, one must be careful when someone professes to defect. They have to be sure of her intentions and she happily supplies all they need from the deep recesses of her mind. In fact, they've dug up some things she hadn't even remembered.

They chuckled good-naturedly when they found how she was named Sun Botos by a 34-1 Helper digging for scraps after finding her under a sign that read "Botos, Sun" until the grime and rust was later rubbed off and found it really said "Go to Sunny..."

They laughed when they found that "Snyyper", her nickname, was given to her as a teen for her dead-aim with pebbles shot via rubberbands.

The most interesting was how they dug up from the recesses of her mind where she came up with her new name. Flashes formed within her mind....Jaxxas was easy to recall...before her talent with rubberbands was discovered, she used a more defensive trick to be left alone before puberty. She always carried a collection of jacks. Often she could be seen carefully sharpening them on the concrete. She hated having to throw any in defense so she could run-away...they were too precious to her. When ever she needed rest, or to feel safe while sleeping, she surrounded herself with the needle sharp jacks so no one could step near her. If she sharpened them enough, they could pierce through even the best boot soles.

Then deeper in her mind, flashes of a small raggedy blanket with letters on it that she now recognized as reading "D. Craddock" neatly marked on the corner being handed to a daycare worker along with the small blond tot sucking her thumb. Funny, Snyyper thought she had just made that name up. Seeing the Omni symbol emblazoned on the blonde woman's breast that leaned over and kissed her good-bye, and then hugged her tight when later, she picked her and her blanket up from the day-care. A tall, dark haired man, she rememberd, she called him "Dah", ruffling her hair and asking her "How was your day, Dani."

Tears streamed down her face, saddness and relief. She didn't know her parents name, but she recognized the white uniform her mother wore as being the same she saw now on the doctors performing the tests while the InternOps people asked questions. Then the crash came. Riding along with her "bee-bee" as she was calling her blanket...tucked securly in a tot-seat behind her parents in the air-car...being shot down over the junk-yards.

She tried to wake Ma-Ma and Dah up, they wouldn't wake up. She was so hungry. She climbed over pieces of the air-car, burned her wrist on a piece of metal that was still hot from the crash (So THAT'S how I got that scar). She found the water bottle the next day, it helped but it wasn't food. She wanted her jar of bananas. It wouldn't open. Ma-Ma and Dah had flies all over them and were so stinky. She had to find something to eat. She walked and walked, dragging Bee-Bee behind her through the dirt. Finally she fell asleep in the shade under some more metal.

Then a voice. A metal day-care worker? It carried her to strangers who fed her. People without uniforms. They wore bright colors and smelled of fried food. She didn't care. As long as there was food. Where is Ma-Ma? Where is Dah? Never loosing Bee-Bee. And finally, being placed in the care of that awful guardian. Neutral was a perfect alignment for her. Her only alignment was to her steady supplier of Vodka.

Dani was honest with her dealings with the neutrals where she grew up. She told them all the missions she had gone through while aligned with the clans with her friend, Danykalla.

The Omni InterOps were able to find the Craddock family, off-world. Her parents had been stationed here just before her birth. Their air-car had been found, both dead, but the child had remained missing.

Danica Craddock was now home.

"Craddock, Danica...lost presumed dead...daughter of Mr and Mrs...attack by clan faction...parent's remains....", he skimmed over the file summary, thinking aloud as he was apt to do before his second cup of the day.
