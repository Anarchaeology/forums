I've always said that a game that had 48 Breed/Profession combinations that were viable and unique is a better game than one that had 12 Breed/Proffession combinations that were viable and unique.

As it stands AO has about 16 or 17 good combinations and perhaps one or two actually play differently.

The Opifex Agent does not play significantly different than the Solitus Agent. The Solitus and Atrox Enforcers gameplay differs perhaps a smidge.

With different breeds that had strengths and weaknesses that offset each other we would have a much richer world.

Instead we have an excess of uniformity and few options with which to enrich our team with.

Atrox should be rougher and tougher. Enough to offset the lower mental capacity.

NanoMage should be far more apt in the use of nanotechnology in order to shield themselves from physical harm.

Opi's should be slippery as an eel in order to avoid damage that might snap their frail frames.

But, all should be able to capitalize on their strengths to excel to some degree in any profession they choose.

Playing an Atrox Bureaucrat should not be like playing a Nanomage Bureaucrat which should not be like playing an Opifex Bureaucrat and each should be off center from the Solitus Bureaucrat. But each should be fun for the person who chooses to enter the bureacracy.
