What better way to feel like a wizard than to call yourself Gandalf?

Its all about roleplay. Its easier to roleplay and easier for other people to understand where you are coming from when you are playing a well-defined role with some background history. Now you could make up your own role, but then no one else would know your background history and you certainly aren't going to have time to explain it to every person you meet. Naming yourself after things people already associate in a certain way allows you to be associated in the same way (at least till they figure out your are a jerk and a loot whore not a wise and benevolent wizard).

When someone calls himself Winnie the Pooh you automatically have a different expectation than if he calls himself King Arthur if you have any knowledge of popular media. However, you have no preconception of what someone named Telzibidri is supposed to represent so a person who names himself a "creative" name like that has wasted an opportunity to give you information with his name.
