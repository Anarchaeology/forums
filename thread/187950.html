Announcing...the Shadowlands Community Roundtable!
On behalf of the ARK-CR Direct Relations team, I would like to announce a community roundtable concerning Anarchy Online's newest expansion, Shadowlands!

This roundtable is open to anyone with an active Anarchy Online account.

If you are interested in participating, please e-mail crsubmissions@ark.anarchy-online.com with the name of your main character and when you can be ingame. We will attempt to contact you for an ingame interview before we make the final decision as to who will be invited to the roundtable, and when it will occur. Be sure to mention what dimension you play on -- both Atlantean and Rimor folks are welcome, but I want to make sure I'm trying to get a hold of you on the right server!
