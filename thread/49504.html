Roleplaying Group Seeking Members
Sea of Change, an active, dedicated roleplaying guild is seeking more members to take part in events, and enjoy roleplaying with our organization. We're looking for anyone interested in roleplaying, and having a good time. We are a Clan guild, and our focus is to make an impact on Rubi-Ka 2 through Roleplaying.

We have done a variety of events, and want to continue to do what we can to further roleplaying and story for Rubi-Ka 2. We do many events for our members that are part of a guild storyline that we have been running for quite some time. We also want to do many public events in the future, and we would love to see you among our ranks at these events. Members are encouraged to hold their own events, if they choose.

We've been around for many months, though we are still a small, friendly organization. We aren't focused on any certain number of people, and we have no level requirements, because afterall, this is a RP group.

We simply want to provide the best RP playing experience we can for our members, and would like you to be a part of that.

You can view our website HERE

If you are interested in hearing more about us, or wish to join, please contact Sepentura or Siirine You may also send an email to sepentura@icqmail.com

Thank you for your time.
