Zoftig Blimp problems
Nicely done hats... A bit on the primative side as far as texturing goes, but nothing is perfect. Everyone loves silly hats.

The shades were a very nice touch too. In fact, this is what the game has been needing for a long time. New stuff that isn't there for leveling in missions, isn't there to give you the ability to twink, it's just there to be fun and amusing.

HOWEVER: I noticed the shades don't sit right on a lot of faces. The amber and pink shades both are slightly to one side, creating an awkward pushed to one side look, at least on my model, a female solitus. The blue shades weren't even near the eyes, instead the nosebridge somehow pierced my forehead. Interesting if somewhat painful looking fashion statement there, but certainly not what was intended.

FunCom, could you please take a look at these sunglasses?
