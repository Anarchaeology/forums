Official answers to PvP questions PLEASE
Ok, the game has been out far too long to have so many unanswered questions about something that is an integral part of the game itself. Why are these things kept as a secret and concidered "spoiler" info? I have asked many ARK's and GM's qyestions that they just dont know the answers to. How can this be? How can there be a system in the game that no one knows the working of?

Questions that need to be answered:

1) Why do the only 5 titles seen in the game so far mean the same thing? (n00b)

2) Why is there no benefit for team based PvP?

3) Why do you sometimes lose your title randomly? In this I mean you got apprentice, killed a ton of people, then die. Bam you lose apprentice. Surely youhad enough kills to have a safe buffer. But nope you didnt apparently.

4) Why dont we have a way to track PvP progress?

5) Why is this system such a secret?

6) How exactly does the point system work? We have many theories but everytime i progress or lose a title my theory is shot out of the water.

7) What is needed to progress to the next title? ie. Do I need to kill someone of equal title and have them lose their title to move up? Can I move up on any kill?

8) Does level of opponent have anything to do with the points given for a kill or is it just based on the persons title?

9) Why arent there some sort of benefits for having a title? ie defensive bonus, damage bonus, hp
whatever. These would only apply to PvP situations.

10) Why have you changed things so that we have become more reliant on crit buffs to feel viable in PvP?


Those are my top 10. Please can we have some official answers to these and other questions? The lack of contact and info doesnt make me feel like a valued customer. All I ask for is answers.

Please if you dont have extesive PvP experience dont reply with theories. I am one of the more experienced PvPers in the game and I know all the theories and have a pretty good grasp on how PvP works. I really want concrete answers from FC. It has been a long time coming that these questions be answered. This in not spoiler info, this is something that will make people PvP more if they knew why things happen the way they do. Right now people are turned off because of the lack of knowledge on how PvP actually works. I am one of them.
