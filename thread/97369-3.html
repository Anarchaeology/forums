This may be true, but there are also rules that some clans follow that resemble that of the corporation.Sure you can delve into lawlessness, but you also notice how people who follow that path lose respect on both sides. Respect and some resemblence of order must be maintain on both sides to come out of this conflict as unscathed as possible.

c) Weak minded individuals who believe the lies clanners spew out more often than not

And you mean to tell me that everything Omni-Tek tells you is the absolute 100% truth? Even I know that corporations and guilds are usually concerned with one thing, their control, power and their money. Heck, we all know certain clan guilds that are concerned with such more than some OT's are. This is the same as believing some of the old leaders when they said, "Our state has never excecuted an innocent person." There are always exceptions to the rule. There is always going to be human faults and frailties, and you cannot escape that, no matter how "perfect" the corporation may or may not be.

Now, if these points are invalid, why do some choose to join the clans over Omni then?

Being as honest as I can be, the truth of it is that it is a personal choice we all have to make, for our own reasons. Some may do it because they believe they are fighting for something better. Some may do it because they like the less rigidness of the clans. Some may even do it because they have a grudge against someone/some guild of the other side. Whatever the reasons are, the point is that it is our jobs as the leaders on both sides to make sure that we recruit the right people. The people who serve under us need to be ready to follow us, and it serves us better if the people who don't want to be there leave us.

It is also our job to keep the people who are under us happy and satisfied. Those who want to stay need to have a continual reason to stay. They won't stay if you do nothing for them. I know a lot of ex-employees who left because they didn't get any respect or recognition for their actions, and to me, that shows how we as leaders failed them. It is our job to recognize them, and to make sure that their accomplishments go noticed.
