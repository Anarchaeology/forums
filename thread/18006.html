Autoaiming is also to help those with a slower connection. The speed of the connection could mean life or death for a character. My first multi-player experience was on Descent 2 using a 56k modem, but my phone line could only achieve 28.8k. I got beat bad because my connection was so slow. EQ changed everything for me because I could play on my 28.8k phone line with almost no lag. My first try with AO was last May and the lag was killing me so I gave up on AO and went back to EQ. I am now on a cable connection and am back on AO giving it a second chance and it is going great so far.

Making it based on player skill also takes away from the role-playing aspect of the game where your character may not be as good as you can make him using your mouse. Also it allows for those whose physical skills are not as good as most to compete on a more equalized playing field.

By the time Necron, Endless Ages and Project Entropia come out, broadband may be the majority, but right now, phone connections are still too common to not be accounted for in the game.
