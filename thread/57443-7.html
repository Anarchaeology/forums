Hi, Mega, nice to see you back. One should listen to as many voices as possible, even if they are inconvenient. Thanks for the 'real' numbers.

Ok, folks, you may be right about the numbers you see, but that doesn't actually mean, these are the ones that actually affect the game. Gaute is the boss of the guys who program this game, so he should (in theory) get more and more detailed information than we have, keep that in mind. And actually, if he would be concerned about the exact % values, he would not be the game 'director'.

Actually I made some research on my own, and aside all the other incorrectnesses, no player on Rubi-Ka should be able to equip a ql200 FoB.

In the end, the same facts stay: People do crit too often (Funcom's view). We can name it as we want: fast leveling, PvP imbalance, etc., the end result will be, that the crit system will be reworked. After reading a lot of opinions and having one on my own, I just would like to ask for thorough consideration of that step. We have a number of weapons, that depend on the crits you can inflict with them (player view). If you (FC) scale the whole crit system down, you may cause changes you can't even imagine right now (reducing the ammount of viable weapons even more, etc.). Although I am still in favor of a downscale of crit chance (~30/40+ percent is not what I would call a critical, more a special) try to keep such things in mind.
Maybe this discussion should have been undergone 'after' the Inits work correct (if that ist still coming!). Maybe the idea of an overall crit max chance cap (-> Bionitrous) is an alternative (PvP damage caps do work, or?).

Nevertheless, LLTS should be removed, changed or made available to all. An item that powerful should be either available to all or not - and no, just being ingame for definite period of time isn't a privileg and doesn't give you the right to own a specific item. I own two low ones and don't want them to be changed (perfectly balanced for my needs), but the actual state is unacceptable.

So, don't focus on numbers (player's view), try to see it from an overall game view (Gaute's view).
And Gaute, if I may say so, try to prepare your articles a bit better, you are the boss, and all - hear, all - will look at the things you write. (just the old 'Klugscheißer' Max, you know)
