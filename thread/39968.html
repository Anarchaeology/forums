Why less clan PvPing? Its a relatively simple explanation.


The only real reason players had to choose clan when they started was for RPing reasons. Every guide around told folks that Omni had better access to goods and paid less for them. As such, folks that had a real interest in exploring all the game had to offer, including the RP aspect, leaned towards clan, while nearly 100% of the powergamer "Kewl D00dz" choose to be Omni.

Now, I'm not at all saying that some peopel didnt go Omni for RP sake, but I am saying that nearly 100% of the more immature LEET moron types did.

As such, this is why you see a larger contingent of PvP players on the Omni side. It is an inherent trait with those types of powergamers to be like that. (NOT that PvP is bad, just that most BAD players PvP) You'll have to be perceptive to understand the real meaning here.


So, now that you have this situation and add to it the incredibly limited areas to PvP, you find situations where most of the times clan players are horribly outnumbered. This, believe it or not, gets bloody tiresome after a while so folks just stop doing it.
