Triffnix
Lag => high FPS
There is one little mistake in the article.

In the world of Anarchy Online, a lot of lag means good FPS. The reason is simple (and embarassing for Funcom): whenever new players pop up, the client spends a lot of time generating the textures - making it freeze for up to ten seconds in heavily populated areas.
But if you are lagged, you will not see the players surrounding you, because the server simply does not send out the information.
