It's an extremely smart idea (even if they don't know it) to force a box onto store shelves in the near future.

The majority of the gaming populace doesn't even know Anarchy Online exists.

If there was a box on the shelf and I went to buy it and some guy saw me picking it up, as I rounded the corner, he might actually pick it up and read the box. I know if I see somebody heading straight to a particular game and grab it and walk away, I go see what all the hubbub is about.

Anarchy Online has very poor penetration into the market consciousness. Whether they will admit it or not, it's a niche game now one year after release. You don't see it on any store shelf and it's not mentioned in many articles that discuss MMORPG's.

Example:

http://www.foxnews.com/story/0,2933,59959,00.html

Where was AO mentioned?

A 'box' will by default raise discussion in the various gamer magazines and make it onto the 'release schedule' sections of these publications. It also will warrant a review forcing further consideration by the gaming public.
