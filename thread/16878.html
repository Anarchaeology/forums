The Main Issue!
The main issue of the game is to make it fun. Well lets look at what high lvl ppl r doing. They are doing missions over and over again to lvl because it is the fastest way, and it's impossible to find another. I'm talking lvl 100+.

*Missions are boring and simple and clostrophobic.
*People majicly finding some knook somewhere in the wilderness to lvl is not realistic (people don't do it, how could they find it?)
*The People who play this game alot, the dieharders, are high lvl; There is nothing to do for them.

The fun of the game is the main issue. There can be many suggestions on how to fix this. I hope that the information above was obvious before reading.

Here are some offhand ideas of how to fix the game:
1. Fix PVP so that people can make legitimate wars without feeling gimped by a cheap prof or cheats, and make it so that there are actual rewarding goals that people can have. An idea comes to mind where each org can have their own place, with features that they pay for like a guard, and that it can be taken over by other factions, but the guard's massive health will give the members of that org time to get to their base to defend it. (Not a well thought out idea, but there Might be possibility; kinda doubting it)
2. Make a more elaborate system of play at high lvls to get xp. Maybe make multi step missions that give away rare loot that is both rare and unique(as in not many people have it or talk about it) (and hopefully nodrop most of the time).
3. Make a way of adding roleplaying to the game, mabye by having several corporations at war with eachother that have their heads as employees of Funcom. This would both tightly connect players to funcom employees, and make roleplaying possible, and make the game more random. As it is now, the people of RubiKa don't even look at the news because it never applies to them.

Oh, and btw, what are we giving lower lvl people to look forward to at high lvls besides money?

I would love to see what you guyz think.
