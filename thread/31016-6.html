But we shouldnt be penalized which we are with the 67/33 split. If you (FC) are adamant about keeping ranged energy as part of the req for a soldier only weapon then how about looking into this suggestion:

Make the new soldier only weapon attack 100% based on assault rifle and 20% based on ranged energy. Yes i realize that is 120% but what it does is give the soldier a bonus (20% of ranged energy) for having to invest in RE to use his weaponry. I cant possibly see this as an imbalance as it corrects a penalty weve been striken with since day 1. 20% bonus to attack rating based on your RE is not going to make anyone a god. If you think 20% is too high, then maybe 15% but please stop making the weapon 67/33. This in iteslf will correct several problems we have been asking to fix as it get rids of the 67/33 split and allows us to get our attack rating higher than the other professions using similair weapons so we can in fact do more dmg with assault weaponry.

I realize we have new nanos coming sometime but that will not fix the 67/33 split on attack rating that keeps the soldier class penalized.
