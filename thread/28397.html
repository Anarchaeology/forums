About the server downtime - Thursday, June 30
Last Thursday, June 30, the live dimensions were taken down quickly and without prior warning. The reason for this abprupt downtime was due to a rather bad exploit. It was something that could not wait, and it needed to be addressed immediately. We did not want to announce why the servers were being taken down, in an effort to keep the exploit under wraps.

However, the lack of any announcement for the downtime was an oversight, and we're sorry for not giving you fair warning. We will continue to keep you informed of server downtime in fair advance.
