I can't provide coordinates for any of these, I am at work, but most of them are marked on Dovve's map.

Check out the Leets camp in andromeda when the boss is up. A waist-high glowing leet!

Check out the 'Maze' in Broken Shores. It's not super special but it's nifty and fun to run around in.

Head to the Three Brothers Volcano in Avalon. Very nifty. So is the southern lava basin, and some of the nano freak camps that are nearby these two spots.

Check out the Medusas in both their areas, Deep Artery Valley and Eastern Fouls Plains. They are one of my favorite monster types.

Travel down the river from Wine in Belial Forest on foot and you will encounter some Eremites or Anchorites (the mobs that come out of the ground) and Fossegrims (huge tree mobs with three faces). If you get to Hollow Island you might even find a pit lizard or two wandering around. At the end of the trip is a wrecked outpost sandwiched between some neato waterfalls.

You can also find Eremites in Deep Artery Valley, and their cousins the Cenobites can sometimes be found in Mort, Milky Way near the crash site (big wrecked space ship), and the desert of lost people in Broken Shores.

Check out Golem Island and Eremite Altar in Deep Artery Valley. Nifty look, nifty critters. Enigma Island is cool too, but it's just a teaser of the big gem, one of the coolest/weirdest places in the game in my opinion - Enigma Forest in Central Artery Valley. I think it'd be possible to spend days there, just looking at things, but I believe the enigmas are aggro against Omnis.

The Temple of Three Winds in Greater Tir County is nice. Not very big, but something to have seen before you go.

The Fixer Grid. Get someone to upload you to it if you haven't already.

The inside of the Council of Truth building. Find an engineer with a key to warp you in, and check out the meeting chamber.

I'm sure you've seen the big cannon thingies in Clon****. But if not, go see them.

The miniture village you mentioned is in Galway County. Also in Galway County is the Bronto Burger Factory (Boycott! Boycott! Unsanitary and Cruel!) and a Nature Preserve.

The Lush Hills Resort diving platform. Jump off the high board a few times for max falling damage *giggle*

That's what I've seen in person and remember
