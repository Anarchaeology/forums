Well, the simple answer is MR curves.

Take, for instance, the River Seasons XP...

code:
QL Range	Start	End	MR/QL
1-65		11	192	2.78
66-132		195	655	6.87
133-199		662	1139	7.12
200		1147	1147	8.00

QL Range	Start	End	Req/QL
1-65		6	337	5.09
66-132		342	684	5.10
133-199		689	1031	5.10
200		1036	1036	5.00
Now, throughout the life of the Advent, one can only raise MR 4 points per level. This is a constant. One may also raise Pistol by 5 points per level. Additionally, there are more buffs for Pistol than there are for MR.

So... let's look at the ratio here...

code:
QL Range	MR/QL	Req/QL	MR/Req
1-65		2.78	5.09	0.55/1
66-132		6.87	5.10	1.35/1	
133-199		7.12	5.10	1.40/1
200		8.00	5.00	1.60/1
Now, let's look at character skills...

code:
Level 	MR 	+Buffs	Pistol	+Buffs
1	11	0	12	20, 40 		  (Exp, Mastery)
66	298	45	335	20, 40, 100 	  (Exp, Ballad, Wrangle)
133	551	80	591	20, 65, 132, 120  (Exp, Ballad, Wrangle, EP)
200	659	120	719	20, 100, 132, 120 (Exp, Ballad, Wrangle, EP)

Level	MR	Pistol	MR/Pistol
1	11	72	0.15/1
66	343	495	0.69/1
133	631	928	0.68/1
200	779	1091	0.71/1
So...let's consider these facts presented:
1) MR reqs per QL raise from 2.78/QL at QL 1 to 8.00/QL at QL 200
2) Pistol reqs remain constant at 5.10/QL
3) MR skill ranges from 0.15-0.71/1 compared to Pistol skill

What does that translate to?

1) Multi-wielding requirements per QL quadruple between QL 1-200
2) Pistol requirements are very linear
3) The MR requirement on QL 200 pistols is *higher* than the Pistol requirement, despite the fact that an Advent only has roughly 71% as much MR skill as Pistol skill at level 200.

Therefore: The disproportionate amount of MR/Pistol ratio will cause off-hand weapons to begin falling behind as soon as the skill ratio (~.65 on average) is smaller than the requirement ratio. This happens at roughly QL 75 on average.

Despite the fact that Advents have 32% *more* Pistol at level 133, the MR requirement is 140% per QL compared to the Pistol requirement per QL.

Liaker pistol is good because it is 1000 MR at QL 200, which is doable by a mid/high level Advent. Aside from that, it honestly isn't all that great since it's slow, and doesn't have much better damage than existing pistols.

So... let's lay it all out...

Primary Issues with Pistols/MR:
1) Pistol MR reqs are disproportionatly high compared to MM reqs, with little reason for being so (Dimach is NOT a reason, as explained above)
2) Even with that being the case, MM maxes at 719, while MR maxes at 659
3) MR requirement curve and handling of MR mechanics leave many periods of time when players can lower thier damage
4) Reload bug is a problem, especially in MR situations--causing 9+ seconds of downtime using like-clip weapons mid-battle
5) Lack of specials: lacking a good Burst/Fling pistol after the Customized Desert Reet, all Aimed Shot pistols die early or suck. (On the other hand, it is very easy to get Brawl, FastAttack, SneakAttack, Dimach with a melee config and still be using good weapons.)
6) Outside of PvP, range means nothing due to warping mobs and melee mobs with absurd range--making the reason for "tradeoffs" using a ranged weapon pointless. Furthermore, none of our good pistols have very good range anyway... 15m isn't very big of a deal..most mobs have longer ranged "weapons."

Also, to reassure as asked above... MM should not be nerfed! It is working about how multi-wielding should work, IMO. MR needs to be rebalanced, at least for Advents and Soliders.

I will present another issue... While other professions can, for instance, do Nova/Flashpoint swapping for specials, we cannot. Since MR requirements are checked on (almost) every equip of your primary weapon, it makes it nearly impossible to get into a situation where we could swap weapons. (For instance, using a Galahad for FullAuto or something) Since Pistol wielders are forced to temp implant, temp buff, and generally pull amazing tricks to get the highest QL offhand on, it makes swapping weapons in the field close to impossible. If Melee Advents had to (which they don't, since they can wield Sol/Scissors to get all specials easily) they wouldn't have to worry about this, as 830 MM is easy to maintain in the field.

Somewhere along the line, Funcom made the decision that Pistols, and Multi-Wielding Pistol would suck. This is probably fallout from the whole Freedom Arms debacle back in the day... However, why these opinions were retained within Advent (and Solider)-only weapons I cannot fathom. Even the BBI Faithful recently had its high-end MR requirement raised just recently. This is absurd!

Cz and/or Funcom, we need a real reason as to why this is the case, and feedback of it is ever going to be fixed at all. The Reload bug needs to be fixed, as well.

Feedback is very important.
