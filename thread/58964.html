Irresistible plagiarism
COMPLETELY OOC... just for giggles, some humour for those that were there to share... just some fiction, no reality here. The real report will follow some other time.


Surrounded by anarchy and chaos, Garret "Bonefish" Silvestrini and Luis "Manamatrix" Ye face each other on a gantry, high above a satellite installation in Borealis.

BONEFISH: There is no escape. Don't make me destroy you. You do not yet realize your importance. You have only begun to discover you power. Join me and I will complete your training. With our combined strength, we can end this destructive conflict and bring order to the galaxy.

MANAMATRIX: I'll never join you!

BONEFISH: If you only knew the power of the dark side. Fleshreaper never told you what happened to your father.

MANAMATRIX: She told me enough! She told me you killed him.

BONEFISH: No. I am your father.

Shocked, Manamatrix looks at Bonefish in utter disbelief.

MANAMATRIX: No. No. That's not true! That's impossible!

BONEFISH: Search your feelings. You know it to be true.

MANAMATRIX: No! No! No!

BONEFISH: Manamtrix. You can destroy Omni-Tek. We have foreseen this. It is your destiny. Join me, and we can rule the galaxy as father and daughter. Come with me. It's the only way.

Bonefish puts away his pistol and holds his hand out to Manamatrix. A calm comes over Manamatrix, and she makes a decision. In the next instant she steps off the gantry platform into space. The *cough* Dark Lord *cough* looks over the platform and sees Manamatrix falling far below. The wind begins to blow at Bonefish's cape and the torrent finally forces him back, away from the edge. The wind soon fades and the wounded nanomage begins to drop fast, unable to grab onto anything to break her fall.
