Bank and Inventory Improvement
The bank and inventory system of AO needs serious improvement. From a usability's standpoint, it scores 0 out of 10 The following tweaks and changes will make it much more usable. Please post your ideas and keep this thread bumped. I hope we need some major GUI improvements in Shadowlands.

Executive Summary For Core Ideas

1) Bigger/Resizable bank window
2) Take away one-click transfer for easy reorganizing
3) Open bags from bank window
4) New windows don't replace bank window

Commentary And Reasonings

1. Make the bank window resizable, if not, at least make it taller. I understand that the inventory window should be smaller because some people keep it open, but there's no reason for the bank window to be that small. It can span half the screen for all I care.

2. Items reshuffling needs to be fixed. As we all know things get shuffled inside a bag when you put it in the bank. Most of us store multiple types of things in a bag and like to organize them inside the bag. This makes it pointless.

3. Let us be able to rearrange things inside the bank. Right now when you click on an item in the bank it goes into your inventory automatically. The cons of this feature outweigh the pros of it. How many times do you take more than a few items from the bank? Make the bank window work like a bag so you can swap position of things within the bank window. This will also allow you to take things from the bank window directly to a bag in your inventory.

4. Exclude the bank window from the two containers opened at a time limit. It's extremely annoying when you are opening bags from your inventory to look for things to put in bank and it replaces the bank window.

5. Let us be able to open bags directly from the bank window!!! No justification needed really.

6. Give us more bags! It's a no brainer on FC part to make some new bags. Give us say 100 more bags with a number tag at the corner. Don't tell me this is time consuming to do There's no game balancing or testing needs to be done. For all I care (and 99.99% of the Rubi-Ka population), they can add no stats and even give you a penalty when worn! Don't make them insanely expensive either, there's no reason why the price tag should prevent someone with limited resources (like a new player) from organizing his or her inventory.

Wishlist entries suggested by others (I don't necessary agree):

7. Universal bank account for all characters on an account. Currently the game is not preventing someone from transfering an item between characters on the same account. All they have to do is find someone trustworthy to do so. Why not make this an in game feature since it's not an "exploit"? Also, I have heard more than one stories of people getting ripped off, even by guildmates (happened to one of mine). It's unnecessary strain and pressure on the players. You earned those things with your own characters, so it's only reasonable that your newly created characters can enjoy the work you have already done on other characters.

8. Sharable Access to Apartments for all characters on an account. Not necessary all characters to have the same apartment, but allow them to enter each other's apartment to obtain things dropped inside. Possibly restrictions on what you can share.

9. (Wishlist entry removed for NODROP items transfer due to popular demand)

10. Have a cash deposit box for the bank to deposit credits for the "impulsive buyers" type And if the universal bank account is implemented, then this allows sharing of credits between characters on the same account as well.

11. An alternative to universal bank account is to have public safe deposit boxes. You can "rent" these boxes at a fee and can put a certain number of items into them plus credits. Then you can identify who can reopen the box (besides yourself) by typing in the nickname of the person (with verifcation of course). We can associate a relatively meaningful cost to using of such box to prevent overusage. These deposit boxes will facilitate transfers not only between characters on the same account, but characters on different accounts as well. We can even extend this further to include a cost to open the deposit box. This will facilitate offline trading. Merchant A can deposit Buyer B's items into a safe deposit box, specifying how much Buyer B needs to pay, then Buyer B logon and can open the deposit box by putting in the specified amount. This will definitely solve the "not being on at same time" problem commonly faced by merchants/buyers on the forums.

12. Divide the bank up into nameable 'folders' or somesuch and just give us 20 different folders that we can set the names for. When you click the bank you get a list of folders. When you click a folder you get a bank window just like the ones we get now.

13. Optional automatic sorting of items inside the bank or bags in alphabetical order. This should be an option that you can turn on and off so players who want a particular order of items in their banks/bags should be able to do so.

14. Related issue, an organization bank that allows org leader (or appointed individual to access) so items can be shared among members of an organization easily.
