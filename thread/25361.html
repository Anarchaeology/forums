Expanison?? For gods sake, fix the simple bugs first!!!!
Please don't divert any programing time or talent to an expansion while you still have such unsurmountable bugs in the game system like the contest of packs getting scrambled as the are put into or taken out of the bank!!!

I know this must be a very tricky piece of coding since it is not working and it is almost a year since the game has gone live.

So please have the programmers you would assign to the expansion work on this problem. I cannot tell you how frustrating it is to put implants/crystals/armor in a pack in the bank in order by level to use as I "grow into it", and have it be in a different order when I take the pack out of the bank. I just want to scream.

I know you can't be putting your best programmers on the expansion since the best must be busy day and night on the challenging coding problem, but can't you have the second string bring coffee or something to those slaving over this pack rearrangement code rather than creating a buggy expansion??

Get these tough bugs sorted out first...then you can put your best talent on the expansion.....and then I will feel I can recommend this game to my friends and look myself in the mirror in the morning...
