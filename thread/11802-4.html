I think the formula is (or at least is suppose to be)

Nano Att Skill of Caster / (Nano Resist * ((100+Nano AC Mod)/100)) = % chance to resist

Therefore if a Nano is cast by a player that relys 100% on MatCre and the caster has 100 points in MatCre and the Target has 100 points in Nano Resist AND the Nano resist mod is 100 then the Target has a 50% chance of resisting.

If the Mod was 130 instead of 100 then the chance to resist would be 57%.

Bottom line, unless CZ deigns to chime in here, we will just have to wait and see. But I will give you my reasoning.

What is being fixed is a bug -- that non-damaging hostile nanos break roots. Damage is still supposed to break nukes. All taunts now do damage. Therefore, the general taunt should still break nukes. Not every time perhaps. I imagine the same holds for dots.

So, this could be rendered false by three things:

1) There is a minimum damage needed to break the root -- certainly not true now, and I doubt it is likely to be implemented, since it would give a big advantage to low damage/high crit weapons and roots in combination.

2) You cannot cast hostile nanos on yourself anymore period.

I think we would have been told about 2).

3) Self-inflicted damage is treated separately from non-self-inflicted.

This sounds tricky to implement, to say the very least.

So, I suspect that this will result in almost no actual change, unless damage breaks nukes with less regularity than,e.g., swimming inexperience. Which is matches my own limited sample, for what it is worth.

It is also possible that the taunts were deliberately changed to allow roots to be broken -- I highly doubt it, mind you, but the timing is suggestive.
