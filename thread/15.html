Athen Accords
From the Opposing Force Press Department

The Athen Accords

This press-release from Div9 and Opposing Force is just in: With the current situation on Rubi-Ka, the present rift amongst the clans and the threat of imposing war, two factions from the opposite ends of Rubi-Ka will meet on the evening of November 15th in an atmosphere of tranquility.

Since their first meeting several weeks ago, Clan Opposing Force and Omni-Tek faction R.S.G.E. [Division9] have been at a standing order of ceasefire. In that time they have been working on the Athen Accords, an agreement of non-confrontation that will help to unite the clans and Omni-Tek in a posture to seek out a viable and peaceable solution to the conflict on Rubi-Ka. In a summit of these two organizations, to be held at 10pm CST in Borealis City on November 15th, they will sign the Athen Accords, thus binding them and any other organization (Clan, Neutral or Omni-Tek) that is interested in non-confrontation in this time of chaos.

"There is only one way to end the growing problems among all of Rubi-Ka's citizens," said Utildai, President of Opposing Force, "and that is to come together as a people and not as factions. With the Council of Truth's indecisions, lack of clan support, the clashes among our peoples and the recent conflict with the Cyborgs near Tir and Athen, this is a solution we are hoping will benefit all people of Rubi-Ka and finally start to bring us together."

"We believe this to be a step in the right direction," stated General Barzillai Craddock of Division9, "one that might help to secure a positive resolution to the growing menace of chaos and uncertainty on this planet. My hope is that others, who feel that war is not a solution, will join us in responding to the threat of Red Freedom and the indecision of the Council of Truth."

Although these organizations will be the initial participants in the signing of the agreement, others -- regardless of affiliation -- are welcome to participate in the agreement. Those interested may contact press.xchange@rsge.net to request their inclusion in the summit. Or alternatively they can contact Utildai or Barzillai in game.

The full documentation of the Athen Accords can be viewed at this address...

http://division9.rsge.net/rsgeNET/division9/spec_docs/Athen_Accords.htm
