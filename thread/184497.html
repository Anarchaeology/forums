Neutrals: Newland Guards Initative
From : The office of Administrator Houston, Argentum 4-Holes
To : Citizens of Rubi-Ka,

Subject : Newland Guards Initative

This is Administrator Charles 'Kithrak' Houston of Omni-Admin here. I am submitting a proposed plan for the eventual restoration of truelly neutral guards to the City of Newland.

Right now at and around Newland City a neutral city, has non-neutral guards. They have been ordered to kill any Clanner on sight. To a large degree they have been successful. This needs to be changed.

Therefore, I propose that a funding initative be started to collect credits to train a new set of neutral guards for Newland that will eventually replace Lorren Warr and her hired thugs. The funds will be an effort to help the neutral community gain recognition from both Omni-Tek and the Clans. How will this be acomplished? By designateing Newland City as a 'Neutral Zone' and createing a set of laws that both sides must reognize while in the city.

Neutral Zone Code
  • Everyone entering the city must respect its neutrality.
  • While inside Newland, all disaggreements between Omni-Tek employees and Clanners are left at the city gates or whompa.

These laws are only preliminary and may be subject to change.

Those laws will bring economic prosperity to Newland City by turning the area into an area where everyone on Rubi-Ka can get together to exchange goods and services without fear of conflict.

The Borealis Charity Event was organized by the neutral group Independent Rubikans. I am donateing the sum of 25,000,000 credits recieved during the Borealis Charity Event to start this funding initative. Negotiations are in progress with several Neutral and Clan parties to make this a trully untied effort to recognize neutrals' neutrality.

For the sake of neutrals, Omni-Tek employees and Clanners should support this initative. Having a protected neutral zone will be one more step where both sides may meet to exchange ideas and perhaps one day co-exist together.

Regards,
Administrator Houston
Red Tape of Omni-Admin
