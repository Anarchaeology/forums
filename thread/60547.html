GA armour from docs point of view
Hi folks, I play a doc on RK-1, currently in 120s. I have been grouping with a lot of different people and have pretty much seen how people cause agro, take dmg, how good they can take it, what overall impact AC and weapons have, etc. etc.

These are things that I havent seen before playing an MA or Soldier or NT or Trader or anything else, cause I never payed so close attention to peoples health. Now that I do few things have become very apparent and I see the game in a little different light.

The one thing that has struck me however, is the effect of Grid Armor in the game, to pt it plainly, its rediculous, wrong, fubar'ed, real real bad. It is unbalanced taken the the most utter extreme in every feasible way, and here is why.


During my time grouping with people, I have had some real excellent groups, with experienced players (alts) such as myself. Several times, overall group composition was something like this:

Doc - lvl 120-130 (me)
MA - lvl 120-140
Enf - lvl 130-150
Sold - lvl 130-160
any prof - lvl 120-160
any prof - lvl 120-160
Fixer - lvl 120-140

Once I was in group with 2 Fixers. Missions taken were ql 184-202, with all the apropriate mobs for the mission levels, including of course Ace/RM Poachers and Enf type mobs.

Now here is the amazing thing. On average, a lvl 130 Fixer in GA3, took a fraction of the dmg that a Soldier 20 levels higher then him, in better armor, with more hitpoints, and runing TMS. I couldnt freagin believe it but here it was. Fixers didnt take dmg. Soldiers AND WHILE RUNNING TMS did, not much, but more then the Fixers in GA armor. This vs the Ace/RM mobs (the hard ones, not Lab assistants etc.).

Then, as if that wasnt enough, I saw in front of me a lvl 130+ (I forgot exactly liek 134 or 137) Fixer, solo an Ace Enf, in a ql 198 mission. While I do not envy people soloability, isnt soloing a mob that takes an entire group with lotta healers to take down a bit in the rediculous range ?

Seriously, I play a doc, but man, I feel real horrible for Soldiers and Enforcers. Whats the point of being a Soldier when GA3 alone outdoes TMS and it lasts a long time vs the 1 minute of TMS and without any negative effects ? They seem so useless as tanks in comparison with GA Fixers its not even funny.

I dont know if FC doesnt play their own game, or they have zero people in QA departament, or maybe dont have one at all, or what, but how the hell did this get pass them ? If its intentional and working as intended, I understand taht its hard to balance thinsg for PvP, but this isnt PvP, this is regular xp missions, how the hell is this balanced ? Could someone in FC please explain to me the logic and reasoning behind the GA armor going live ?
