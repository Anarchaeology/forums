Its kind of funny, one of the reasons I started my fixer was because I wanted to have a really high B&E for hacking stuff. But I find that I have developed him differently than imagined because of the rest of our hurtful IP cost. His first 20 levels were totally focused on B&E though, including triple implants.

Here is what I would recommend: early on, raise B&E enough to get into the fixer shop (using Cracker's Luck and B&E expertise). Then lay off a while, until you are planning on doing the Fixer Grid quest, at which point raise it enough for the nano (which is not much more than the fixer shop). Then lay off it again some more until you start having trouble opening chests in the missions you are doing, and raise it enough so the chests usually open on the first try (rough guideline: 2 times the mission QL). Next raise it when you are about to do the Team Fixer Grid quest. Then lay off it again until you have some extra IP (level 130+) and want to start doing the B&E tradeskills.

Because its green you don't end up saving a whole lot of IP by being conservative this way, but every little bit helps.

Trap disarm is useful for Small Titan Message Containers. I think you need 500 to crack it, but I'm not sure. I haven't done one for a while.

Another side note: I think B&E and trap Disarm are mostly useless without a hefty Perception skill. Every door you pick in a mission may be trapped, and you'll never know without having a good score in Perception. I don't think 400 cuts it.

You'll want a really high B&E if you plan to clean implants. Really high. For a QL 135 implant, you need 630+ B&E.

Speaking of every little bit helps, buy a Pick-a-Finger from the Fixer store. It sounds like a nosepicking device, but it gives you a 14-or-so point increase to B&E and Trap Disarm.

I've learned to hate this skill, because everyone and their mother pumps as much IP into it as possible, for chests and doors (ninja looting optional).

I agree with what was previously said about spending IP in B&E, except that I would wait until after 150. Like it or not, abilities, evades, and nano skills are still of utmost importance, and having the 135 SMG buff at 135 is more important than B&E.

1x QL in nanoprogramming
4.75x QL in B&E

I raise my B&E as I go: my eventual goal is to be able to clean QL200 implants without swapping implants around (might not be doable, to tell the truth), or failing that only swapping self-buffed.
