Team Chest Loot/XP Sharing Proposal
It really gets annoying to be in those groups where one person takes it upon themselves to loot ALL the chests, and I would like to propose a simple change to 'fix' them, once and for all.

PROPOSAL:
1. Distribute the experience points from unlocking a chest among the whole team, just like MOBs.
2. Make a new set of commands, exactly like the "/team loot" commands. So, for example, when in "team chest loot alpha mode", anyone can pick the lock on the chest, everyone shares XP from it, and the person allowed to loot the chest is given a message saying that they can loot the chest.
