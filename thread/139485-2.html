Originally posted by Savoy
No whiners in the old days? Does the name Cptfallout mean nothing to you? :-)
OMG!!! I haven't thought about him in AGES! That boy whined enough for any 150 people combined!

Oh to be an agent like we were back before they started the nerfage... Remember when they nerfed conceal completely for a week? Oh how we howled about that one.

There was whining all right. LOTS of it.

"Agents are too powerful!" Over and over and over again.

What about the nifty little soldier reflect shield exploit that we watched go unchecked for just about ever? Funny how no one ever talks about that.

Or the "crash of thunder" MA nano bug that killed its victims repeatedly after they respawned at the reclaim following a little game of PvP in Tir Arena? LOL!

You didn't see many crats back then because, let's face it, you had to be determined, stubborn AND crazy to play one. But some of us just HAD to hold on, in hopes that they'd actually make good on that "Crat-made Mission" feature that we almost had. I wept bitter tears when they finally gave up and got rid of it entirely.

I still have my old ql 200 Stigma Rifle. I couldn't bear to get rid of it. Too many memories. It's been passed down from deleted main to deleted main through my many incarnations.

I remember hearing "take it easy" until I was ready to put my fist through my monitor.

I remember having to jump up and down to attack a mob. It was FunCom's official temporary fix. Honest!

I remember having to pass around a form with ten other people to create my first org. And I remember orgs actually MEANING something.

I remember 10k credits really being a stack of dough.

I remember plummeting to my death the first time I zoned in my brand new Yalm. And the funny little bug that made Rederon look like a flying leet.

I remember trying to swim across a river and being eaten by pirrahnas. And trying the same thing somewhere else and being one-hitted by a shark.

I remember seeing mysterious, very scary looking mobs out in the middle of the lake just outside Newland City.

I remember when complete strangers would talk to each other without begging for creds or buffs or loot.

I must need my head examined, coz I miss it all.

But do you know what? I still love this infuriating, bug-ridden crazy ride of a game. Because I'm still finding new, fresh and and totally entrancing things to remember. And nope, I'm still not Uber. Because I think that the second I actually hit Uber, the fun, the joy and the reason for playing will be nothing more than a memory.

Ah, the good old days.

Started July 01 2001 with an agent, had no idea what I was doing, and the some of the best times for me were back then.

Omni-Pol barracks region being full of people shouting about selling their uber ql 40 Omni Forest Armor. Getting a group there was easy, and then meeting up by the big rock a little south, and heading off...just to hunt random spawns, hoping you didn't run into a bloodcreeper . Being able to use a stigma rifle and actually pull something from afar (unless you were teamed w/ a mausser-using fixer w/ the same 45m range, stigmas outdistanced everything).

Galway County was another fun place full of people and easy to get a group, I actually remember seeing a doc trying to auction his services to 3 different groups there one night lol. Killing mantz..anyone remember the "peace treaty" or whatever it was that happened I guess around September of 01 with the manteze? I really thought this was going to be some sort of major shift in how AO was played.

Sneaking missions w/ my agent for cash...O-Trade to Avalon for 100K per mission. Back then Avalon was a scary place where no one went...I'd go up there for hours on end and see no one, and msg my friends and org mates about all the cool stuff up there--seeing the domed Omni bases, Camelot (pre-Tarasque), the lava lakes..etc.

Getting my yalm and flying around non-stop for a whole week, taking screenshots. That in a lot of ways was my proudest achivement in AO, getting that yalm--I was one of 3 people I knew that had one, and they were still rare enough to be "oohed" and "ahh-ed" over...I spent the better part of a month sneaking missions and not leveling so I could get enough cash for one.

It was mentioned earlier, but I think the best part of those days was the feeling of the unknown, that every time you zoned you could come face-to-face with something new and breathtaking (or lethal)....the first time I took a mission in one of the Artery Valleys and saw "No Suppression Gas at all...this is Mayhem" it made me feel afraid...

I could go on for hours...heh...AO was a strange magical place when it was new, I've never been so immersed in a game as I was during the summer/fall/winter of 2001. Randomly being stopped by a clanner in Varmit Woods when I was heading to a mission there and having a 30-min in character discussion of Omni vs. Clan...keeping a small bit of IP in reserve and bumping a skill up by one point every few minutes to save your xp before Lush Fields crashed...the first time I saw a hammer beast...4 Holes beach hunting...being a leet for the first time...watching the sunset from the tops of the guns in Clon****...cyber armor being the best since it was the only kind that protected against every damage (all my early screenies, my agent is in waitt/bau head to toe)...ok if I don't stop now this will become several pages heh

I still love the game, and play it a lot...but the sense of discovery isn't there anymore. I'm really hoping that SL will bring a little of it back before all the mobs are slain and wildernesses explored.
