First Order Recruiting Again! (Omni-RK1)
One of the few guilds that has stood the test of time, is looking for some more qualified members to join our ranks.

Although First Order was at one time sporting a 700 Man Roster. We have trimmed that number down significantly.

About a week ago, we kicked all members that were more than 1 month inactive. So check out our roster, and understand, that what you see is what you get. All the players on this list, are still playing the game:
http://www.anarchyonline.com/org/stats/d/1/name/2398209

But be warned. First Order is not for everyone. So let's get the *not*'s out of the way first.

  • First Order is not a powerleveling guild. Although we do have our fair share of level monkies.
  • First Order is not a role playing guild. Although it's quite fun to go into clan territory shouting "Join Omni-Tek, We will give all you dirty people soap and free showers!"
  • First Order is not a PVP guild. But we will be active in the Notum Wars, and as well have a great many titled members.


So I know what you are thinking. "With all those not's... What the hell is First Order."

Well let me tell you. First Order is a family.

The guild was built on friendship, and it's been our number 1 goal in the game.

Our members are helpful. Funny. A joy to be around. We aren't clouded by stupid rules and regulations. (note: We do have some rules)

If you are a high level player with alts, you'll love FO, because we don't push your alts off to a "sister guild" where they will have to live all loney and stuff. And as a matter of fact, if you enjoy playing your alt, and you meet someone who is friendly in your alts level range, they are welcome in the guild as well!

The most important aspect of First Order, is that you be mature, courteous, and friendly to one another.

We have members who's main character ranges from level 10+ to level 200. We truley have a place for all level ranges, and all time zones.

In Summary
We aren't catered to one one play style. Instead, we are focused on making friends. We all understand that this is a game, and as a game it should be fun!

The main things we, as a guild offer are:
Friendship, Groups in many level ranges, No Level restrictions on mains or alts (However we have very strict begging restrictions, and understand that high level characters do not like being bothered every 5 minutes for buffs), NO GUILD TAX.


Our friendship also expands past AO. We still have lots of members that are faithful to FO, and are starting up FO guilds in other games, such as AC2, SWG, etc...

We have a very community driven website at http://www.firstorder.net/ and if you wish to join, that should be your first step.

I know it looks like anyone can enter FO, I mean hell, it appears as if we have no requirements to get in!

That's just not the case. If you aren't friendly, and people don't get to know you. You either will never be accepted into FO, or you will end up leaving.

First Order is what you make it. Nothing more, nothing less....
