Talk of an expansion _was_ batted around back in May/June. We had different people from FunCom communicating to us back then. Talk was mostly of 'turning on' additional zones and the like as added content. Areas that early beta players could get to but are now inactive and the like. The 'magical' island of Jobe for example. This was before FunCom got such a cold dose of reality as to how bugged/unfinished the game really was.

I think all you'll hear is a lot of backpeddling when it comes to this issue now. After the message board wipes, patch disasters, Agent ping-pong, Adventurer/pistol fiascos, mass player exodus, and everything else that they were responsible for, I don't think they could in good conscience expect their existing players to fork over more money for an add-on at any time soon.

One has to hand it to them for crawling back into the game after all the heartache they've caused. They'd be much better served by opening additional landmass for free as a token of gratitude towards their faithful playerbase. Additionally, no drastic changes in gameplay should be considered at any time soon. The recently-discussed skills vs. requirements changes (whether valid or not) could seriously spell the end of this game forever. Any changes such as introducing encumbrance should be carefully thought out. Weigh the 'annoyance' factor against the benefit to the playerbase as a whole. AO is probably supreme in terms of cutting out the pain-in-the-ass parts of a persistant world game. Try to keep it that way.

Continue the work on stability, continue to add content in terms of items and areas to explore, don't stop the storyline both in-game and out, help the weaker professions ('crats) a bit, improve the identities of the professions (Agents really don't want to be MetaPhysicists. The inability to operate like a 'real' Agent forces them to do anything to be viable.). Whatever you do, don't fiddle with major game mechanics. People seem to be having fun.

Come back to us in a year and maybe then try to sell us a ticket to one of Rubi'Ka's moons for $19.95.
