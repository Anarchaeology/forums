Give everyone a shot at rare items, heres how...
A fairly simple solution to give more people a reason to venture outdoors and also give a lot of people who never gets to see any rare loot the chance to obtain some for themselves...

Apparently we need a reason to explore this beautiful world of Rubi-Ka, instead of spending all our time in missions.
Creat a system where every 'UNIQUE' item in the game has a chance to spawn as loot on random mobs outdoors, although sparingly. Design the system so people hunting for it will know on what type of mobs they can be found on but not in which zone. No stupid dyna camps, that is just camping all over again, but outdoors.
This could surely be done while letting the spawn and drop rate be the same in the already existing dungeons, you know the ones reserved for big factions...

Create a system where you can manually insert a wandering mob into any zone carrying a specific rare item. Broadcast a news bulletin about it; 'Attention, Omni loyals! It has come to our attention that a clan spy has been spotted deep in Perpetual Wastelands carrying an assembled Nullity Sphere MK II NF. He has to be stopped at all costs, as the reward you will be allowed to keep anythibg he might be carrying.'
The first team that finds him and starts to attack him will have the rights to loot him, no stealing aggro away by a stronger team. Make him move all around the map so when you find him he might be hipdeep in red monsters, do you take the chance of attacking before any others get there or will you wait until he moves out of the area? Heck, make him not show up on the map in order to make it more challenging.
Make them drop anything from a padded overcoat to professor Jones vest. The point would not be to stop people trying to get the items from the regular places but a system like this would at least make it possible for people who would ordinarily never see these items at least a possibility of obtaining them. They would almost be like mini quests or treasure hunts if you will. Rubi-Ka is a big beautiful world with a lot of neglected places, like PW, DAV, CAV and others, it would be a shame if they will stay unexplored for ever.

A system like this would go a long way in making us breaking up our routines and be on the alert for actual ingame happenings...
