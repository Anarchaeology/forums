if you don't have it yet, go buy a map over newland city. Upload it, then go upload your mission coordinates.

If you dont have enough Map Nav for the map, just upload the mission coordinates and follow the yellow line at your compass. Since you have a problem already, I take it you dont know how to upload your mission coordinates. Well, if I remember correctly, this is how it's done:

Open up the mission window (located at the top right of your screen). Right click your mission and hold the mouse button down. Three small icons will appear, one of them giving you the mission description, the next will upload the coordinates, and the last will delete/cancel the mission. While you hold your mousebutton down, move your mouse over the icoc in the middle. This is the one that will upload the coordinates. Release the mouse button, and you should get a message in your chatwindow. It will tell you if you are in the right place or not. If you are, the yellow line will appear at your compass. You might not see it at once, so just do a 360 degree look around to until it appears.

Hope this helps
