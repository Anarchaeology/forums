Change is a good thing...the alternative is what you see in a lot of your friends online: they aren't logging in to play their main characters. They have filled their alts, filled alts on a second or third account, or switched entirely to the alt account.

You can stay enf, that's great! In a world of change, you can be the rock, and I'm sure others will join you.

If that long time playmate doctor, who you left the backyard with, decided to go trader, and asked for your help in getting trader nanos, would you help him get them? Wouldn't the game be a bit more interesting, in that these missions would mean something because you're friend needs the reward item?

An rpg based in the distant future should allow for some creative character possiblities.

What if you were able to keep some of your old profession nanoprograms? Maybe only those of a QL below 50, if you are above level 100, or below 15, if you change below 50.

What if you're skills and abilities didn't change color when you changed professions? Whoa! Then profession changes would really have to be thought out, since you could start NT to get all green nano programs and then change to Agent so that you could be the best nano agent ever, only to find out that you have less hps, less nano, and no ranged init or rifle skills. Doh!

Of course, these are just ideas, and profession changes are different from an IP Reallocation system, but there are some interesting possibilities for the game, even for an enforcer.
