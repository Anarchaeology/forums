I agree with having the option not to disclose your faction and guild, however your name, breed, profession and level should be viewable at all times.

For the guild display, though, there needs to be an ability to view the people in your own guild, even if its only for the guild leader. One reason for this is that in my guild, we have some members who haven't registered with our roster, but of course are on the AO roster. We need to compare rosters to keep track of who is not on our roster and contact them about registering. It's also good for simply keeping track of members.

Personally, my opinion, is to do the same thing as another MMORPG is doing to enhance the pvp experience. IMHO its brilliant. You can only have characters in one faction per account (this lowers possibility of people playing both sides for info, but does not kill the possibility of in-game infiltration...). Also, you cannot send tells or communicate with people in the opposite faction (no more harassment from opposing side sending you tells and no traitors helping the other side; again, does not stop the possibility of an in-game infiltration). What I mean by in-game infiltration is say, Agents using in game skills and nanos to try and infiltrate the enemy 'posing' as the other side, etc. On that note, I also think to make it more realistic, cities of opposing factions should be political zones or simply off limits to the other side. I mean, come on. Were there a lot of American soldiers walking around yelling LFG in downtown Berlin in WW2? Same with teaming, etc. However, this could/should change as the story changes (peace, war, cold war, etc.). I guess at the present time, with amnesty, that clanners could walk into an omni city...anyway, that's a whole other thread lol
