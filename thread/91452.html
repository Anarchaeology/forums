Hey Blackmane,

I think, on the whole, that the MPs will love the new pet. It looks great, does great damage and has some features that will make life a little more interesting for us. I've quoted a few things of concern below, but all in all it looks good.

OK so feedback so far is:

1) Pet not responding in chat to commands is interesting - but could cause us some issues when pet pathing is problematic. Many MPs use the responses and or lack of responses to judge range of pet, what pet is doing at this time, mode of pet etc which all gives us good information regarding what's going on with it.

2) New pet doesn't seem to fit through mission doors too well (bit wide?). It's in the right place and running - but the longest I timed for it to get through a door was 35 seconds....

3) No noticeable improvement in pet pathing - seemed to like running into the wall next to a door just as much as the other pets (hadn't expected it to be fixed in the pet itself though )

4) Damage is excellent compared to a similar fully buffed enmity. The addition of cold damage on the Revenant I tested (scythe damage) is a nice touch.

5) Damage speed was excellent too and the animations are definitely cool. (overall the look is excellent ).

6) Hitpoints: seemed a little lower or about the same as a meatball of similar level (greater enmity used as comparison for the 162 Revenant). Given the damage it's clearly more a meatball type than a demon type (thanks for that btw ) so that would make sense. You also said you were still adjusting the stats on the higher level ones - so I guess the stats are still a work in progress to an extent.

7) Pet doesn't seem to zone with the owner. Could be an interesting change to the game dynamic for us - but once again with pet pathing issues, sometimes zoning is used as a way to retrieve a lost pet (because pets usually zone with us from wherever they are)... with this pet not zoning with us it's more susceptible to irretrievable loss. Given the eat flag on the energised urn, this will sometimes force MPs do go hunting for lost pets to get them back under control which may be irritating, particularly when teamed and people are waiting on you. It's not a technique used by too many people or too often though - so it wouldn't have a huge impact.

8) Duration: Given eat flag on the one hand but also given non-zoning on the other... the duration seems excellent. I can't think of too many situations where the pet would time out before I needed to zone. Most likely would probably be a very long Notum Wars battle but on the whole duration seemed good.

I'm going to spend some more time soloing the Inner Sanctum tomorrow to get a feel for how easy the urns will be to access. The last time I was in the Inner Sanctum the agg and damage on the mobs wasn't really working as you know. One other thing - are there any new commands that we'll need to use with the new pets? Just checking so I can test more fully.

Blackmane,

A little more feedback on the new pets' defences. The Revenant is doing better damage than the other pets and does seem to attract more aggro as a result - which is a good thing. However, given that extra pull the hitpoints/DMS do seem a little low overall. They seem to die pretty quickly from an MP perspective - perhaps just because we're not used to our pets holding aggro so our old pets died less quickly anyway. It could just be a perception thing comparing one to the other. The new pets seem to have similar HPs to a meatball of the same level but they seem to die more quickly.

Given the requirement to find new urns to recast the pet though, I'd say that its defences probably need some tweaking upwards. I haven't been able to test with the level 203 pet yet as I'll be needing a wrangle to reach it. I'll let you know as and when I get one.
