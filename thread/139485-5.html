During BETA (Omni) :

- Hunting near the rocky maze in Greater Forest and being utterly owned by the Gargantulas or the Vultures which sometimes walked out of the rocks.

- Running all the way from Omni One to Athen with a level 8 MA (took AGES !). Seing things like Red Widows (scared me to death !) and the nice Bronto Mamas !

- Planning to explore broken shores, and finally managing to get to Home all the way from the Omni wompa (I was around level 15)

- PEOPLE IN BETA ROCK ! Even all the bugs, downtime, crashes, didn't change the fact that this beta was huge fun. No leet doods, only testers/roleplayers. Every beta is like that (the fact you lose your chars at release helps a lot).

AT RELEASE (Clan)

- Being level 30 and being UBAH !

- MA sucked so bad ...

- The main PvP spot was that bridge near Borealis, south of the bronto bar in holes in the wall. The bronto bar itself was a major gathering place for both PvP and hunting. Now, there is nobody there

- An omni enforcer named "Bowtie" owning everyone at that same bridge. All clanners were pissed against him, I chatted a bit and we became friends ... we ran all the way down to Omni One from that bronto bar, made many friendly duels (some of which my gimp MA managed to win, dimach helping a lot ). Bowtie, if you're still around, send me a PM please !

- Almost insta death by dimaching into a soldier's MK shield (still laughing at the memory )

- First run in the longest road. Finding bliss, then continuing (dogding the uber hammers, scorps and spiders) to the place with the lifebleeders and getting atomized by 10 of them

- Seing one of the few yalms. Seing a yalm in a shop for 5+ millions. Dreaming. And a friend Agent finding one in a chest (lucky 'stard !)

- Hunting in that rocky hill near the outpost SE of Tir.

- Hunting at Crater Farm.

- PvP in 2HO as a clanner ! Teamed with my soldier friend (MK shield was uber at that time), gridded there (UB3R ! I can GRID) and killed all the newbs while the high levels can only look. Until finally (after over 3 hours of Omni massacre and shop healing) the scums came with a huge force of char of our levels and whiped us out, even if my soldier friend killed quite a good amount of them before he was sent to reclaim too

- Many many lost friends

- Many many more good memories

I remember my first char, Kemal, made on June 28th, 2001.
He was bugged and didnt have a side chosen, though I went into the Neutral BY.

He couldnt initiate combat or bring up his skill window, because the game kept asking him to choose a side.

He had the level 200 MA Title, "Legend."

I took a screenshot of it, but had to delete poor Kemal =*(

My next character, Jenslove, ive had since July 3rd, 2001, and he is only level 91. Bawk!

Jenslove spent a few days stuck under a building in Newland City.

He had his apartment in Newland City Backyard 3, which was at that time over-clipped by the Mountain, so it was like a "Secret" Backyard.

I remeber the awe I felt when I looked down in the BY and saw the roach crawl over my feet.

After I changed to clan, I ran token missions non-stop from Borealis to West Bank for like 5 days...

Then, remember the bug that caused missions to not delete/time-out? Yeah I had that =*(

I remember PKing my friend in Tir Arena, and he hadnt insured, so I looted his Swimming Trunks that he was carrying. Every now and then ill whip em out of the bank and go "Nyah nyah!!!"

MAs were so gimped then it wasnt even funny..And MP's only had one pet if I remember.

And I remember as a Neut I tried to run from Newland to OMni-1 and failed miserably.

And then there was the very first time I set sights on Home..I almost fell over in my chair, it was so amazing.
