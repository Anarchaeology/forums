One of the interesting features of AO is that your "attack rating," computed from your skills, adds to the damage you do with a weapon. If your target doesn't have adequate armor (which is more of the case than not with mutants and critters below level 80), then yes, you can reguarly hit for better than "maximum" damage.

If you still have your Solar Powered SMG, try it out in the newbie area sometime. I bet you'll be hitting better than 2-6.
