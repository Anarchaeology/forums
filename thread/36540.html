Fond farewell
Decided it was about time I said my goodbyes.

As those who know me know, I havn't been on a whole lot, in fact not at all for sometime now. Mainly because of work commitments and real world commitments, and secondly because I have lost all interest in AO as a game. Felt more like a chore than a game in the end.

Have been playing since release and in that time have made some good friends, and also seen alot leave the game. Hence I am reluctant to leave but hope to see you all in SWG. Im going to name a few, Kimberlie, Host, Bhustanut, Santacharm, Substatic, Reikosan, Nalaar, Whygirl02, Hunter, Lephi, Barbiegirl, Chanel5, Hextorr, and all the people in Apocalypse. This is a list of just a few I will miss. Oh and add Caol to that also, enjoyed our arguments, your not a bad bloke after all. :-)

Apoc was immense fun to be in as a clan and without any doubt the best clan on RK1, with an awesome atmosphere.

This is not a whinge post about AO, in fact I will remember my days in AO fondly, but I'm done with it now.

Want to wish everyone good luck and hope that many AO'ers will show up in SWG in the future.

Till we meet again...

Signing out,
