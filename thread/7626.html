A threat to our way of life
As the new year begins, new challenges unfold -- challenges to Omni and the future of our world. This calandar year will see the clans withdraw -- preferably by choice; by force, if need be. It is my most sincere hope this is a year of peace. I've seen the hideous face of war and counted the costs of conflict in friends lost. I remember this all too well, and have no greater concern than the well-being of our men and women stationed in clan territory. True, their morale is sky-high. True, if they are called upon to fight the aggressors, they will do their job courageously, professionally and, in the end, decisively.

But we should go the extra mile before asking our men and women to stand in harm's way. We should, and we have. Philip Ross, with the full support of Omni, has already tried to peacefully pressure the clans into an Amnesty, to no avail.

This week, we've taken one more step. I have offered to have a bounty placed on the head of the clan guild leader "Snarf". We received word that Snarf has been killing Omni employees throughout the Amnesty. First hand proof of this has been supplied to us. This will not be secret diplomacy at work. I have, in person, a message for Snarf: Withdraw from Rubika unconditionally and immediately, or face the terrible consequences.

Snarf will either leave, or he must die to appease the civilized world. This is a deadline for Snarf to comply with Mr. Philip Ross and Omni. Time is running out. It's running out because each day that passes brings real costs.

Snarf already poses a threat to the areas of DAV, 2h0, and MMD, as well as our own men and women in the Stret West Bank region. In fact, Snarf has used weapons of mass destruction against innocent unarmed employees observing an Amnesty. Each day that passes increases Snarf's worldwide threat to Omni.

Our own economy is suffering, stemming from Snarf's aggression.

Each day that passes, Snarf's forces also fortify and dig in deeper into Rubika. We risk paying a higher price in the most precious currency of all -- life -- *if* we give Snarf more time to prepare for war.

We have seen too often in this age how quickly any threat to one becomes a threat to all. At this critical moment in history, at a time the war is fading into the past, we cannot fail. At stake is not simply some distant area controlled by some clan members. At stake is the kind of world we will inhabit.


Snarf must Die!!!!!
