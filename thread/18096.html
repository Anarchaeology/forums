I've gotten a striped pillow and a concrete cushion from missions, but they weren't rewards, they were objective items (like Tsuneko said). But, you don't need to delete the mission and lose the reward and token. Once you find the item in the mission click on it once (not twice like the mission asks you to) and hit T (or whatever the shift-click equivalant is). You'll complete the mission AND the item will remain on the ground. Then just take it.

Sometimes I take missions based not on what the reward is, but instead on what the item I'm suppose to find is (knowing that I can just keep it, eh). The reward is then just icing on the cake : )
