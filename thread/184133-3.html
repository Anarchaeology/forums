Two sides to the story
There are two sides to this story. The first side is that throughout the whole history of AO, adventurers have been teased with new items that never showed up and struggled to find their "class defining ability." Adventurers could heal, but not as well as docs, they could buff run but not as well as fixers, the adventuring skill was pointless except for carrying more in packs and reducing fall damage. The adv gripe list goes on & on, and like most gripe lists has a few valid points mixed with a lot invalid points (morphing didn't count as class defining ability, for instance). In some ways SL is a giant Adv patch, and give them the love FC has for so long denied them. They now have run buffs that matter, mophs that might be used for more than eye candy, and they have the maps that require adventuring! The skill is finally useful to the profession it takes it name from. I am sure the adv thread is now free of gripes!

The other side to the story is that many players liked the local map, as opposed to the world map. Players spent IP and used implants to get their map scores high enough for whatever maps they needed. Some players had lots of maps, and some had no maps. It was a matter of your own preference. Map & Nav was the ONE skill that could not be reset, and therefore people spent points carefully in it. However, since that skill can't be reset, and since many players have invested IP into the skill, those players tend to feel cheated with the SL adv only maps. If I want to play my character primarily in SL, all the IP he spent on Map & Nav is now wasted except when I am on RK. And even if I dump all my remaining IP into map I can't have them for SL. Some characters also have worked hard not only to get maps, but to get their map upgrades, which will work with adv maps, but offer no value without a them. Now to me, I always upgrade my maps and spend IP in them, because I see it as a valuable part of the interface. Others do not feel they need the maps so they don't. It does seem unfair to deprive the people who have spent lots of effort and IP making sure they could use maps, and now tell them that their spent points are only useful when an adv is around, and they can never get their own maps. It is a skill after all, and I would assume that since I am good at it, if I get good enough, I can get a map of anywhere.

I have read a couple of solutions posted in this thread. For instance I like the idea of having a black screen in my map window that shows things like mobs and doors, but doesn't show terrain. Adv map nanos have buffs in addition to giving a local map, so perhaps FC could release map nanos that just function as normal maps for the rest of the population. My own idea includes making Map & Nav resetable. Make maps not show up if you don't have the skill. So I can reset all my IP used in Map & Nav and play AO with the new non-map oriented interface. I think it is good that FC finally decided to give adventurers love, I just wish they had come up with a cleaner solution, that would not have made my IP spent in Map & Nav only valuable when I am on RK.Sorry this post is so long winded. I just wnated to make sure I was clear on my points.

-Lest
