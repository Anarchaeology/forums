TvT
Something I'd really like to see in this game is some sort of meaningful PvP. Dueling in the arena, or "ganking" in 25% zones has very limited appeal for me.

When I first played AO, I was struck by many of the professions' similarities to classes in Team Fortress (I'm most familiar with Q3 Fortress, but I'll assume the template is pretty consistent between engines).

- Enforcer = Minigunner
- Soldier = Soldier
- Fixer (or maybe Adv)= Scout/Recon
- Agent = Sniper
- Doc = Doc
- Engineer = Engineer

It's been a while since I've played TF, so I don't know if I'm missing anything; but the point is that AO has a lot of potential for team-vs.-team (TvT) play, if the success of TF (or other similar games, like CS) is any precedent.

I'm not sure of the best way to provide an incentive to engage in such a thing. One idea I've had is some sort of TvT missions. Using regular team mission terminals: say an Omni team takes a mission to go to some indoor location. They have to occupy/garrison the location for maybe 1 hour; while they're there, a corresponding mission would be available to Clan/Neutral mission terms. Say a clan team accepts the mission to retake the mission site: they go there and have to fight their way through as with a normal mission. Maybe there would be some random mobs so they couldn't just walk through. Maybe they would have to recover some object (e.g., capture the flag) and return it to the mission terminal, without necessarily decimating the opposing team.

If such a thing could be implemented, I think it would have the advantage of effectively obviating debates about "balancing" classes for PvP. Good team tactics -- not to mention team composition -- would be at least as important as in PvM/PvE team missions or hunting. In the scheme I have in mind, I could see classes like agents and engineers (or traders, post-heal nerf) being more in-demand than they are now. Most important, it seems like a good way to realize the fact that there are many (thousands?) of players, each in one of three factions which are supposed to be at war with one another.

I'll freely admit that I don't have a lot of experience in team settings; and none in PvP. What experience I have in teaming tends to make me want to avoid it altogether. With that in mind, comments and critiques are welcome.
