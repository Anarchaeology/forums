FYI ...
WinXP or Win2K do not have TSRs, per se, as they run the NT kernel. TSRs exist only in DOS-based OS's like W95, W98(SE), and WinME.

Be careful when you install the SoundBlaster drivers for your sound card. Do not install the DOS drivers, as these drivers are all TSRs and will get loaded whenever you boot your PC.

I recently switched from W98SE to WinXP for my gaming OS because I wanted to run more than 512M memory for other things. AO ShadowLands version does not like Win98 compatability mode at all.

If you are running a DOS-based OS (i.e., not WinXP or Win2K), do not put more than 512M memory into your PC ... the disc caching in these DOS based OS's is limited to a 512M address range. There is a work around via CacheMem, but for the best reliability, I suggest you remove the extra memory.

And here's another one for Athlon users running Win2K. There is a registry patch on the AMD site that fixes an AGP address range bug in Win2K. MS may have included this fix in one of their service packs, but I don't know for sure.

Hope this helps ...

Maybe you run out of RAM - 256 is what you should at least have, 512 is what you need to run it smoothly. The only hint I can give you is that on my old computer, the mainboard had a bug and was randomly crashing. But that was with everything, even without running programs. Maybe try a complete reinstall or just install a 2nd "clean" WinXP and just drivers (latest but NOT beta) and AO. If problem still persists, contact support.

@MrCheese - Laptops do not have top gfx cards. Check the tuning tips. If your friends sees you running against a rock while you are standing, then it is a "lag" problem, which can be either with you, with him or with the server. You should check for your FPS/Ping to see if the lag is on your side. FPS < 15 is very bad, should ber > 25 ideally. Ping > 400 is bad, it should be < 200 ideally. There's an option to see this in the game, but forgot how. Server-side lag is usually in crowded places (many players and/or many monsters) around.

General Graphics Tuning Tips

Particle Effects - are heavy on performance. In the F10 menu, turn off Muzzleflash, Tracers, Buffs, Nano Effects. If that alone does not help. turn off names/org names and all other effects (liquid animation, client wildlife, clouds). Spaceships toggling is bugged but I guess you can turn it off again soon.

View Distance - If you experience lag, try turning down the Character and/or World View Distance. Many people operate with a Charview of <20 and even set worldview and charview to minimum in mass PvP.

GUI - The new GUI is heavier on performance as the old one due to the opacity and other things. Try perhaps reverting to the old one just to test performance.

General Info - AO is an old game and only utilizes DirectX 7 functions. Thus, GF4 is about the best card you can use for it. Always use the latest certified drivers (never use beta drivers unless you know what you are doing) for your card. Notice that some graphics cards do not use the standard ATI/nVidia drivers. For example, ASUS does not use nVidia's reference desigen and thus you will need the ASUS driver. Always check with your manufacturer or dealer.
