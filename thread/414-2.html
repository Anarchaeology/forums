I know that meetmeder was mentioned as a neutral town, though I want to add that it has some basic items, mission and insurance terminals and a grid access point in the center of town.

Superior Nano Crystals
Seems to be a confusing question.

If you are looking for nano programs, no neutral store sells nano programs higher than ql 100 (approx.). Clan and Omni stores sell nothing higher than ql 125 (also approx.). Also note that many of the important programs cannot be found in any store at any price. For example, for engineers, the warps and auras (at least as far as I have seen) can only be found as mission rewards. Yes, you can use a mule to get the 100-125s, but since you're gonna need to doing missions for the items you need eventually, why not just get used to it early. Besides, its the quickest way to make money at these levels. (I still use the 1/5 rule...1 level doing solo missions for every 5 in power level teams).

If you are looking for nano clusters to build bigger better implants, they are available in any of the neutral advanced/superior shops (newland, borealis, 20k, hopeless). Enter one of the side doors off the main room and go up stairs and click on a machine. If it says advanced, just go through the other side door off the main room. I think its usually to the right facing in.

Phear Leet
Made change to name of Stoltz Outpost. Thanks!

On the cluster/crystal thing, I still contend that at some point you will be required to complete missions for these and might as well get used to it early. The program crystals just plain stop at 100 (125 if you are using a mule) and while you can get implant clusters all the way to 200 in stores (any store clan, neutral, omni I have never seen one that wouldn't sell to neutrals), the basic implants themselves have the same 125 cap.

About attacking guards...I have been the target of unprovoked attacks from guards in Omni Trade, however, the attack doesn't com eimediately upon entering the zone. This has caused problems getting to Pleasant Meadows, but the following steps can make it fairly safe (most of you kow this, but for those that don't): board your jet before entering the grid. Grid to Trade. Imediately upon exiting the grid angle straight up and hit the 0 (ins) key for continuous forward travel. Once above the building (out of guard range) stop forward travel and wait for zone lag to end. Then look down and maneuver so you can fly directly into the whompa to 20K (it will be a steep angle, but it will work). Since I have been doing this, I haven't been guard attacked once.

As to places to get missions, I agree that Drumdar has its weaknesses. Meetmedere is good, but dangerous. But, its not the last hope!

Consider Hopeless in Mort if you have a jet. It has everything a neutral could need, including a slow, but steady flow of medium high players coming through (mostly non-clans doing the sentinels dungeons).

Also, don't forget the neutral mission terminals outside Omni Entertainment and at Versailles. Both are a short safe ride from 20K (if your careful) and I have gotten some very good missions there (particular outside Omni Trade).

Keeping tabs on the best mission spots for Neutrals. I routinely make the rounds to most of the best terminals every couple of levels to see which is best. So at lvl 55-56 ...

I have never been attacked in the imediate vicinity of Versailles, but are right, while I run around the castle itself on foot, I have always approached it via my Yalm.

To find Sir Lancelot, approach the castle from the north. There is a ramp on the north side running uphill from east to west. Sir Lancelot is on this ramp.

There are two sets of mission terminals at Versailles.
  • There is a clan only set inside one of the tower rooms. These are the only mission terminals I have found on RubiKa that are indoors, but it gives hope that there are more neutral terminals that we just haven't found yet.
  • The neutral set of terminals is on the road heading south out of the castle toward Omni forest. Perhaps it is a misnomer to say these are at Versailles since they are probably about a half a kilometer down that road, but it is the best landmark around.
