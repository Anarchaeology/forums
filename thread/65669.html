Hi Dairium,

Like you, I am new to Anarchy Online and was looking for an org a week ago. I checked out Obsidian Order and joined because I liked their attitude about playing this game.

http://groups.msn.com/AnarchyOnline/guildfaq.msnw

Basically... it's about being normal folks with RL family and commitments who just want to get together with others and enjoy this game. No roleplaying, no guild commitments, etc etc. Of course, it's about having fun also.
