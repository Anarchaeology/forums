EDIT: oh you were asking about Dmg Types , well at lvl 73 I was soloing the front rooms, but had to team with a higher lvl because of the adds in the rest. Hehe, some of the most challenging and graphically interesting fighting in AO I have had. However, the exp sucks, the cyborgs have massive hitpoints, just go there for fun and loot. After 2.5 days there in order to get a full cyborg suit, I did not level up once.
