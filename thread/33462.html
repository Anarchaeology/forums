You dont say what ql mobs you where fighting. The general idea of a mob about your own lvl is, i has more HP than you and i should do less dmg than you.

Mob : High HP , Less dmg, AI vs You : Less HP, higher dmg and human intelligence.

Now its your job to make you char do more dmg and take lower dmg hits. If a mob your own lvl does much higher dmg on you then you do to the mob, then you need to take a closer look at your char. Talk to expericend fixers , get some hints and tips.

You have to choose the best weapon, upgrade your armor and work on you evades and inits if you want to survive. Each prof usually have one or two templates that works. And remember AO is a team game Good luck
