How to save AO from SWG and the rest.
I have recently come back to this game after a hiatus of over a year. Im sure few at Funcom forget the riot I raised in these very forums. After comming back to the game, I find that it is finally in the state it should have come out as. Most things work and any lag can easily be attributed to player congestion and wholly unavoidable until we all have fiber runnign to our houses. Lets talk about what is good.

1) Graphics. They flat kill anythign on the market. Even SWG will have a hard time topping AO graphics. And playing Cartoon2 ... uhh .. I mean AC2 isnt a substitute.

2) Missions: Now that they work, they rock.

3) Variety: Thousands of things to get, hundreds of ways to outfit a character.

Now what is bad:

1) Population: The early releases of the game drove off huge numbers of people that would have stayed had the game been released as it is today. Level with us Funcom, how many play at peak times on RK1? 5k ? And everyone is clustered in town. To fix this, fixing the rest of the bad points has to be done.

2) Selling and Buying: They are a pain. Stand there and auction for an hour. No-no ... people hate that. All new games are going to automated commerce systems that allow a user to bid on auction items or outright purchase them. Searchable computer terminals would be great.

3) Mission terminals: For god's sake put summary info like in Clicksaver in the game. I want to see that at the top of each mission. I dont want to miss out on the role play of reading the missions but dotn want to have to go through 5000 to find what I need. I feel cheesy usign Clicksaver but it is a must.

4) Outdoor Moster Exp: Increase it. Drastically. Tir county is now totally empty and that is really sad. Give peopel incentive to go out there and hunt. Also throwing in a bit of cash wouldnt be a bad Idea. Id suggest that outdoor monsters offer good cash and good exp but no items except for trade skill drops. Missions offer items.

5) Increase trade skills drastically: Give us 1000 new items to manufacture. Stims of all varieties.

6) Reduce trade skill cost for items: Make it economically feasible to make medical kits or weapons that people will want to buy.

7) Make a couple new craftsman professions that are all about trade skills.

8) Increase nano varieties: Get on your thinking caps and put
out some more nanos.

9) More mission tiles to make for dynamic missions. Give us some scenery here guys.

10) More 3d. Build missions on multiple levels. Allow people to climb ladders and fall in holes and go through tunnels that wind under each other. EQ dungeons have the 3d aspect right. Tir is a beautiful but one story city, let us climb up to those lofty platforms. Stairs arent so hard.

11) Make vehicles Much bigger and dont let folk use them in town unless to fly to an aireal platform to get out of it. Within 10m of ground level they should be forbidden.

12) This one would be tough but would trump the upcomming games. Get multiperson vehicles.

If you would do these things and do them quickly, this game could make a comeback. To be brutally frank, as you know I always am; If you dont do these things, even if SWG is a crappy game, it will put the nail in the coffin. The question is really, do you want this game to be an alternative to the upcomming verant project? Or do you want i to go away. If you do the above, word will spread around and people will come back. If SWG turns out to be crap, people will surely come back but not to EQ or AC or DAoC but here.
