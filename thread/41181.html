Also, how do clanners get down to the The Steps of Madness dungeon without getting killed by omni guards?
[list=1][*]Grid into Omni-1 Ent.[*]Go east.[*]Go inside the south garden by the southern East Gate.[*]Go North inside the guard free garden.[*]Aim at the East Gate, close your eyes, and dash in like mad.[/list=1]

This method will reduce the number of guards that'll shoot at you to the 2 standing right beside the East Gate, and the one outside. But I found the Southern East Gate guard in the Omni Forest is usually not at his post. Otherwise he is standing just south of the East Gate.
