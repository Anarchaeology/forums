AO lost its golden touch ?
Once upon a time I got the explanation of AO...

You know , the game where you choose a profession but you have COMPLETE freedom! Even though you choose to be a doctor you can still choose to wield a rifle. You won't be as good as a professional agent - but you have the option to choose it... That's soo great about the game - YOU get the choice!!

I'm sad to see that those days are almost gone now. I see weapons just becoming "profession" specific. In Patch 14.8 there's a new rifle ONLY for agents. I shouldn't complain - I'm an agent...

I think it's a bit pity that we're becoming more forced that X weapon can only be used by X profession.

If you choose for doctor, you can't wield weapon X because you need to be profession X, you can't wear armor Y cause you need to be profession Y, you can't use item Z cause you need to be profession Z...

Why not just go for the strenghts ? Agents are supposed to be good at critting !? Create a rifle that is more crit dependant & faster - making it more beneficial for agents to use since they have their crit nano, etc. BUT other profs can use it as well. Since they miss the crit nanos, etc - they won't be as effecient.

This is not the evolution I would've wished for in AO...
