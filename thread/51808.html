Declarations of war?
Y'know, all wars I've ever seen don't usually start with some guy stepping forward, pointing at some other guy and saying 'we are now at war.' Not, at least, until both sides have been fighting for quite some time.

If this is going to be run like any of the other millions of wars throughout the history of mankind, then we can expect to see neither side actually declaring war until the war is well underway.

Neither OT nor the Clans will state loudly that they're at war for some time. History may well decide to call the period we're in now 'the beginning of the war' but no-one is going to cry war even if they're merrily sending troops against the other. As soon as you do so, the rules change, the ICC peacekeepers get uppity and you lose any legal or jurisdictional rights you might have - at that point it is purely down to force.

What both parties would prefer to see is their rights being maintained so they can cry foul to the galactic community *while* taking territory from the other. The whole deal with Omni-Pol (the official ones) stating that these criminals are going to be arrested and claiming right to 'police' clan cities is just such example. Of course the CoT are going to say no, but all this posturing isn't for the CoT - it's for the ICC's benefit.

I think you'll find that both sides are practically at war. If you're looking for a good excuse to go serious raiding of enemy outposts, then work something up in the context of the situation. I'm sure Omni-Tek would love to send an armed investigative team into Athen to declare martial law while the citizens would love nothing more than to kick the a$$es of any OT folk who tried.

The situation at the moment bears striking resemblance to Northern Ireland about 20 years ago, or that in Vietnam just before the start of the Vietnam war. And if those historical (and rather horrible) events are anything to go by, then the war we end up with is going to have more in common with the Vietnam war than, say WWII.

Full-blown war can't be far away, and in the meantime we get to play 'war without actually being at war' - cold war if you like, if cold wars involve moving troops into each others cities for 'peacekeeping' or 'protection of the law'.
