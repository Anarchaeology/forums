Challenge option
Hi

I've been roleplaying alot around the bars and discos just for fun.
I and i've seen that alot of others including myself have made some spesial social chars, who might not always get along with everyone else.
Be that a missunderstanding, political diffrenses or things like that somtimes you're chars not getting along with another char.

What whould be cool, not only in the bars but in the wildernes meeting people from other factions as well would be a chance to challenge them to a fight.

Now i now you have the oppertunity to go to the arena and such, but see it like this:
You're an omni soldier exploring the wildernes, when a claner shows up, you immediatly start to argue with each other.
This is not uncommen, what sucks is you cant do anything about it.
Therefore it whould have been cool to have a Challenge option for such cases, you challenge the other player, and he decides if he accepts or not, If he does accept you have the ability to fight him, but no one else can interfere.
If this option is used, you should be able to fight anyone of any alignment and lvl.
I mean, you know the risk of accepting a challenge from a lvl 100 if youre lvl 10, but from a roleplaying aspect i would let some of my most stupid chars do that just for fun :-)

Let me know what you think.
