100% PvP damage and no pvp downtime!
Remove downtime from PvP deaths and Make damage 100% again so that dying in pvp becomes more of a minor annoyance rather than a big pain in the neck, and people can just have fun fighting.

Effects from a change like this are...

1. Healing goes back to normal strength and healers are not automatically at an advantage to other players.

2. High crit weapons are not totally unbalancing because there is alot less time for crits to happen hense the crits would become something thats extra like they should be, not the core reason to use a weapon.

3. A more satisfying fast paced team deathmatch style of pvp play that makes first person shooters so popular while not effecting the pvm aspect by not nerfing single proffessions that dont accomidate fare play in the current system.

If this where combined with the ability to gain title in teams pvp in my view may as well be considered fixed. Futher additions such as capturing and holding territory are great too.

I would also love war zones where npc clan and omni are fighting eachother as well as player fighting eachother where large scale war like combat could be achieved. Granted some of the players with slower systems couldnt enjoy that as much but i think it would rock. Being able to participate in large scale battle is something i wanted ever sinse i saw the intro movie.
