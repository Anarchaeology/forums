New Playfields...
...a promised thing for some time now. Let me revert you all to these beta era links.

Salty Hell.
This place sounded intriguing. " A brave fighting merchant could earn a lot of money by bringing food to Salty Hell." That's a pretty fair idea for a static mission or in-game storyline. The entire playfield of The Plains of Salt (that gap in your map between Tir County and Perpetual Wastelands) sounds like a great desloate place just itching to be explored, conquered, exploited, you name it.

The West

This whole area sounds unreal. Especially the stories behind the various mines like Beatrix and Roulette. The story of Kapar sounds a lot like Tir with the exception of Omni interests in the city. You see? Put your mind to it Funcom, and you could make Kapar a hotbed of Ruritanian or Machiavellian politics, which would in turn lead to profession-specific goals for folks like bureaucrats, traders, etc. And any city worth its corrupt salt needs fixers to...fix things.
"Sometimes they appear to be willing to sell out their cause for political power." Of course they are, and the opportunistic player can be in the heat of it all...

The Gangly Mountains sound like a wicked place to visit. You can have cave-ins on the mountains, leading to static missions, mad scientist labs, robot factories, the whole 9 yards.

Endless Plains

Once again, Funcom have mapped out a great place with The Big One. Is there some underlying cause for the Tris? Who is behind it? What can players do to find out?
Not to mention Sparta and the code of honor Kolokotronis has. What code of honor are we talking about here?

OT Controlled

What happened to 3HO? According to the map in the link, it should in Stret West Bank, but there's no such place (as far as I know).

Jobe is coming soon with Shadowlands so I'll skip it.

The Reck sounds like a cool place to hunt with all the monsters.
"The owner seems to have good connections with the Jobe inhabitants, but he uses most of his time trying to stop crazy people from swimming to the island of Jobe."
Indeed? What of good connections? An idea that begs for a story. And, for what it's worth, will we be able to swim to Jobe?

1HO outpost. Come on Funcom, give 2HO a break here. On the road from Rome to Kapar, you say. Interesting. On the road between two allegedly enemy cities. Another idea begging for a story.

The Plains of Divaad.
This bit: "There are tales of clans that have established big domed cities in the wasteland, but it is impossible to find anybody that has discovered such a city."
Really? How's about giving us this chance...

Looking forward to the day these places are in game with a good story and tons of player-gained potential in them...
