Flat Beat of Mindscape
She was eying the backpacks at her feet, curiously, not really realizing that they were hers. Well, Rickert had told her they were hers, as well as the guns, and all the items inside, but it simply... looked irreal. This knowledge didn't ring any bell... it didn't feel like it was hers, plain and simple.

"...Mine?..." she asked to the young man, and he smiled, a little too cheerfully, nodding.

"Sure. Master Elm says he found them in the plane with ya. Must surely be yours then. That's logics. I mean, unless ya have st..."

'Rickert", Elm interrupted before he could say more, and most of all say something he shouldn't have. "Let her look at them, is all. Understood?"

"Yeah, yeah, Master", he grumbled on a sulking tone, then shrugged, handing her the first bag.

She hesitantly took it, looked at it again, for long minutes of silence, while both men were going back to their own business, still looking at her from the corner of an eye, awaiting her reactions. All of this seemed so distant to her, even if she could understand why they were doing this, in the hope that something, anything in these bags would help her in remembering a little. They had already checked for an ID card, although they had found none, and their own ID scanner hadn't given any useful information, only labelling her as <subject not classified in database>, which had made Elm look even more careful and concerned. He had reassured her a little, telling her that he wasn't necessarily meaning anything, that they hadn't access to all the citizens database from where they were, in the cave, and especially when an ion storm was still raging outside, for two more days now... yet she had understood the understatement hidden behind these words. That she may not have any insurance left. Or that her true identity was classified enough for him not to be able to read it. In both cases, it didn't help in making her feel more at ease, nor the two Engineers, and for this reason, they were trying something else, while waiting for a better opportunity to reach for a town or an outpost.

She rummaged through the bag, pulling out a few medpacks marked with the Omni-Med symbol - nothing really new, such packs being regularly smuggled into every part of the world... - and some other tools, recognizing them for what they were, yet still not understanding why she owned them. What the hell was she supposed to do with a lockpick... and a set of bullets with an odd, faint smell of chemicals, certainly not adapted to the bulky guns they had found in the plane as well... a few empty instruction discs, seemingly awaiting for someone to copy programs on them... carbonrich rocks, the kind of which used to craft nano-crystals...

"Too bad it ain't a personal lockpick..." Rickert sighed, half-turning to her. "Would have your name on it, sure..."

"Rickert. I told you to go back to work, boy."

The apprentice shrugged, but kept silent again, and she dismissed the mention of this 'personal' instrument. Too bad, indeed. There were no other items left in the bag, and thus she took the second one, pulling out a few clothes. Women clothes... a pair of blue pants, black shirts, the kind of which would certainly fit very closely to her thin body, black boots too, and what looked like an Oriental-styled blue dress, long and silky, though having certainly known better days, after having stayed stuffed in the pack for so long.

"Wow. I know this kind of clothing!" Rickert said again, before Elm could snap him shut. "Eddie sells them blue pants in Last Ditch. Guess it's even the only place where ya can find them..."

"Mine..." she frowned again. The clothes of a stranger... and nothing more. She slightly shook the head, careful however not to make any brutal move. "I'm sorry, Elm... I... I dun remember. Looks like... ya sure they're mine?"

"Well try them, ya'll see if they fit or not", Rickert chuckled with a mischievous smile that made him look even more boyish, and attracted him a stern scowl from his master. In fact, the comment made her smile, for in a way, he was right, and she was now doing well enough to feel the need to get dressed and take a walk, even if the weather wasn't allowing it for the moment.

"Not a bad idea", she answered, attracting a slightly surprised look from the old Engineer. Elm simply cleared his throat, and put a hand on Rickert's head, forcing him to look elsewhere - elsewhere, anywhere, but not at her.

"Aaaah", he said, slightly coughing. "Your trenchcoat and armor are there, in the pack between these two seats. They were what you were... wearing when we found you."

"Thank you... Elm..." she whispered with a shy little smile again. Armor. Why would have she been wearing armor... and under a coat? Usually, when in armor, one was expected to wear the whole set, not to hide it under civilian clothing... unless one had something to hide, or a need not to appear ready for fighting... So, why? Why her?...

She decided on the blue pants and one of the black, sleeveless shirts, one that let appear the tatoos circling around her arms. These also were weird, dark lines intertwining with each other on her pale skin, without any other meaning or aim than to look... pretty, she figured out. Well, they were nice, though she really didn't know why, when or where she had them done. Then, she looked at herself, at her reflection in the darkened window near which she was sitting. At the two white locks striking her dark-brown hair. At her so pale face, still marked by tiredness, the almost black rings under her eyes making her appear even more fragile. Her smile slowly froze, then disappeared; even her own face was the one of a stranger... even her own features were distant... dulled... unknown. And she wondered. What if she was in fact someone... terrible? A criminal, a killer, whatever - a person with blood on her hands, a person who would have caused horrible things to happen...

Maybe not remembering at all would be better...

"Wow... Suits ya well!" Rickert exclaimed from behind her back, bringing the smile back a little. Suiting her well... yes, she had to admit it, the clothes seemed exactly cut for her. They had to be hers, indeed, yet... something was missing. She slightly frowned, thinking that combing her hair a little might make her look better as well, and looked if there was a comb in the backpack too.

Her hand suddenly touched something else inside, something cold, something shaped in a circular form. When she got her hand out of the pack, she was holding a golden bracer, ornated with rusty-colored spirals and intricated lines... symbols of the desert, of the sky, of the wind... symbols of her past, also. She kept looking at the bracer for a long, long moment, barely hearing Elm whispering something about a "Bracelet of Ka", and how one could still find some, in the desert or in the ruins of towns abandoned by ancient settlers for decades now. Something was on her mind... the shadow of a man, a strong one, handing her the bracer... the echo his voice, of his thick accent, too... kind words meant for her only... She frowned, frowned, trying to remember more, to keep grasp of his presence, of what he was saying... "Ah thought it'd suit ya well..."...

... and the shadow flew away, and the memory was gone.

"...What's happening?..." Elm asked, his comforting hands on her shoulders once again, and she turned to him, giving a slight start.

"...Uhm. Nothing... I dunno... I think..."

"Then why are you crying?..."

She looked at him in confusion, raising a hand to her face, distraitly wiping the sudden tears from her cheeks.

He had been right. She was crying.

But now, she couldn't remember why...

Incommunicado

It had a meaning. "I love ya, Heiji. Yer always so kind to meh!" little Jen had once said. But he couldn't even remember the last time he had been that kind to her...

...Gomen ne, Jen-chan... he whispered for himself, knowing that she wouldn't hear him anyways. I... I beg your pardon... I didn't mean to... I didn't mean to make you sad... I...

Such a fool... such a filthy, pathetic, pitiful piece of crap he had become! Maybe she simply had decided to forget him in a last attempt of not hating him. Or to be finally able, after all these years, to really live her life, without him flaring his anger at her each time she did something he didn't appreciate. When had all of this gone wrong? When had he let himself be engulfed in this downward spiral of hate again, bringing her with him in his fall, forcing her to escape in any situation - her work, her dreams, her long wanderings, the nights of partying, the days or running, further and further, the more and more dangerous operations requiring her attention... To escape in letting her own conscience split, sometimes, leaving him deal with the child she had been rather than with the woman she now was... To escape, and at last be able to avoid him a little?...

It hadn't been Cratty's fault... only his own. The Soldier had just been another lame excuse for him to vent his frustrations at her, his despair and anger of being locked within her conscience like he had been in the last fifteen years, his growing need of a life of his own as well. And this had obliterated what should have really mattered to him; this had obliterated what she needed, she who would so scarcely complain, knowing how he felt, trying her best to soothe him, until... until she simply couldn't bear it herself anymore, and went falling in the spiral with him, again and again.

He felt the harsh cold surrounding him again, just like the first time, not even aware of what he was babbling now, his conscience painful with madness and contained screams. Not like this. Not like this! He didn't want to disappear... he didn't want her to forget him... he didn't want to fade, he didn't want to lose her... Please, Jen... Please...
Remember me...
Remember me!!!

How had he only be able to think that he could be more than that... do more than waiting for her to find Cyrus back, or to simply find a solution to their mingled state of being.

Boku no baka... Baka, baka, baka! Such a fool!... Such a piece of crap! he desperately cried, alone in the silence. I...Gomen nasai, Jen... Boku no daisukina Jen-chan... Gomen ne... Forgive me... forgive me...

He had loved her, too - in a way slightly different than he had for Deannah, true... but loved her all the same, even after - or maybe because of - all these years spent existing within her, entangled within her.

And now that he was realizing it... he was also realizing that he had lost her for good.

Mr Sandman, bring me a dream...
She was dreaming again, yet not of the faceless shadows this time. The dream was slightly different... more vivid, and however, bearing a ghostly countenance, colors slowly fading in the background, until all was falling into darkness again.

She was standing in the middle of this void-to-be, her booted feet silently treading on greyish grass; when she took a step ahead, she confusely noticed that where she was walking, the grass would become green again, though only for a few seconds, and this made a strange feeling arise in her heart. The place... the place had something familiar, a scent of nostalgy that wasn't as disturbing as she would have thought at first... colors she had already seen, a long, long time ago. Pools of water - no, water streaming under small wooden bridges. Black and grey buildings all around. Grass, trees - a small park, without gates, freely accessed by all, except that there were no people to be seen. A bulky mass in the distance, like a little house, dulled by the lack of memories, and upon seeing it, a quick thought crossed her mind - Damn, this thing was already so ugly at that time?... - and was gone again.

She turned to the wooden bridges again, her gaze attracted by a silhouette. A child, squatting on her heels on the bridge, looking at the water with a concentrated expression. When she walked toward him, the child looked up, and she saw that it was in fact a little girl, clad in a worn-out poncho... a little girl with deep brown eyes, and dark hair striked by white locks. Just as hers, she realized in shock, but then the child gave her a scornful smile, and rose on her feet to face her.

"Why ya comin' back?" she said, an insolent epxression on her features. "Ya didn't want to. Then why?"

She stood silent and unmoving, looking at the girl, frowning at her words. Coming back? If only she knew what this place was...

"Ya have forgotten this, also..." the child said again. "Well, screw ya. Go away. Leave us alone!"

"Leave... who alone? I dun understand! Who're ya talkin' 'bout?..."

She stopped. She had exactly the same way of speaking than the little girl... or maybe was it the contrary. The girl... yes, so familiar, and so sad at the same time... There was a sparkle in her eyes, almost impossible to see, a sparkle of despair, of sadness, of broken, destroyed hopes. She was living here, because it was the only, the last happy place she had known, before... before?...

"... Go away..." the child began to cry all of a sudden, turning from her, her little fists clenched so strongly that their joints were becoming white. "Ya just makin' 'im sadder! Why ya came back, if ya dun wanna see 'im again? Why... why ya can't leave us alone?..."

But she simply couldn't. Not when she looked at this girl, and realized how lost she really was, as lost as herself when she had woken up at first, injured and confused, in the crashed plane. She took a few more steps toward the child, hands outstretched as if to show her she was meaning no harm. She didn't understand it all, but what was clear to her was that the little girl wanted her to stay - not to go away, as she was so firmly claiming.

"What's the matter, gal?" she whispered, kneeling in front of her and looking at her pale face marked by tears now. "Tell meh... I can help?..."

"Only..." - a loud sob - "...Only if ya remember... Dontcha wanna remember us? Ya really... really wanna forget?..."

"...Forget... No! I'm tryin' to remember! But... but it's hard... Wait, how ya know that I..."

"I know", the girl sobbed again. "But ya dun wanna me 'nymore. Dat's why. Ya dun really wanna me. Ya dun wanna memories 'nymore. They gonna wreck it all... this place... they gonna kill half the people... but ya dun wanna remember this, right?..."

She slowly shook the head, saddened by her own lack of knowledge. Yes... she knew the place. These waters, the slight smell they were carrying. These trees, where she had herself sat. Someone had been looking for her... a man, a parent, stern and angry, but loving her, doing his best to make her happy, to...

"I'm tryin', I swear... But I... I jus' need time!..."

"Ya dun have time!" the little girl shaked her head before escaping her arms and beginning to run away. "He's goin' away now! An' ya dun have time 'nymore!..."

"...Jen-chan...!" she whispered, a call to a past that was fading again, so quickly. This girl was looking so much like her that it was really frightening...

But not the crashed plane, nor why her NCU and implants were still down to fifty percents of their capacities.

Well, she had a few more hours, at least, to think about it while they'd be travelling under the sand again. She dismissed her worries with a shrug, and simply asked Elm if she could be of any help to them for the moment.

Ura no ura...
Another dream...

Opening her eyes, she immediately jumped on her feet, awaiting the attack... before realizing that she was back in the so familiar park. She let a slight sigh of relief; the landscape and buildings now seemed a little duller than in the previous dream, but it was the same place, no doubt about it.

No sun, no moon, no day or night - only grey tints, dullness, smoothed edges. A terrible feeling of loneliness seized her, all of a sudden, a feeling that wa related to the surroundings themselves, a feeling pertaining the dream only. A dream, or a memory?

Are wa yume, soretomo... omoide?...

It sounded... so sad, so attuned to the place, however - soft words, spoken in a soft voice in the middle of a soft world. Wasn't she supposed to be able to do more than this in a dream?...

She needed him, as much as he needed her, and nothing in the world could change this - yes, he was understanding it now... nothing, not even her love for other people. They had known each other for so long... gone through so many trials together... how could this be erased?She had loved him, him and everything about him, and she still did, even if there were days when it was hard, when none of them would give any concessions to the other. But that was... that was being human, after all! Simply human... a humanity his death hadn't completely destroyed. Jitsu wa!... Boku wa... boku wa ningen yo!...

Wait for me, Jen. I'm coming back!
Wait for me!...

Nothing would prevent him from coming back now.

Nothing.

Out of the Depths of Sorrow
They were all gathered around her, smiling and cheering and happy to see her back. A whole gathering of hugging, shaking hands, laughing to tears. And Malcom was there too - looking at her with this so kind smile of his, his apparent roughness always giving him a little awkward attitude when he was looking at her like this... She was smiling too. Smiling in her half-sleep. For once, this was a pleasant dream, though she didn't dream very often, and her usual dreams were much more darker.

Smiling...

Slowly, so slowly, she opened her eyes again, the pleasant vision fading from her sight to be covered by the one of the leperish rock above her head. It was still shading her, but the suns had now moved descending upon the horizon, letting her realize that the night was soon to fall, and to replace the terrible furnace of the desert by the freezing cold again. She slightly stirred, forcing herself to sit and to gather her mind, hand clutched at her side; the wound was bleeding again now, and certainly beginning to be infected after having been exposed to the sand, throbbing even more than it had before. It was too late; the night would bring other worms and creatures, and she couldn't fight anymore, not in such a bad physical condition. All worthless...

She managed to grab one of the backpacks lying near her, in an attempt to find something that'd be able to soothe her dry throat and aching body. A few medkits... ones she had been keeping for emergency cases... nano-stims... In a vain hope, her shivering fingers took one of them, injecting it directly in her wrist, resting her back against the big tall rock while waiting for it to act. Nothing was happening; with this odd awareness she had of her own vital functions, she knew the nano-bots were trying to make their way through her body, with no result; it helped easing the pain a little - slightly, so slightly... - but her own NCU was still acting high and low, somewhat blasted again by the use she had made of it against the Worms, preventing bots reproduction, and even destroying some in the process. It wouldn't work. Before any stim could work, she needed to repair this, and one usually had no chances of finding a surgery clinic in the middle of the desert.

She gave a sad little smile upon noticing how little water she still had. Of course... she hadn't been prepared for the desert, although it was hard for her to tell what she had really been up to before they took her - little bits of memories, again, drifting in her mind... and the feeling that there was so much more behind all of this. Trellame... yes, Trellame would work for anybody, as long as the payment was high enough for his expectancies. She didn't remember having had anything to do with him lately, unless they simply needed to make an example of an Omni-Tek employee like her... so maybe they had simply... simply... a trap... It looked like... a trap... damn...my head...

"...Guess it's gonna be the end now..." she muttered to herself in the deep, muffled silence of the vast desert, her voice sounding horribly croaking, her throat rasp and aching in spite of the few sips of water she had taken. "Heh... If I had thought... it'd end like this..."

"...won't!..."

She closed her eyes, trying to force the dream to comeback, a last vision of what she'd never come back to. Home.. She wished for her friends to be here with her in that instant. Most of all, she wished for Malcom to be with her. He'd never know what had happened now, or maybe over the time, when all would already be too late... There was no need to lie, no need to hide now. Running away from her felings... running away from people, for she knew that she would never be able to find happiness, not as long as the shared state of mind would go on - But it's too late?... I made 'im disappear. I... killed 'im! Killed... Killed... Running away from Raquel, cold and steady when it came to work, but always the first one to be ready for a party or for lending a helping hand. Running away from Jo, who had smiled to her and simply decided he wouldn't let her sit alone that evening in Rompa's. Running away from Chris, from the Director she was always enjoyin to tease with her odd Fixer quirks, from the kind Mr. Caris, from the wild and surprising Corrine, from...

Running away from Malcom, pretending not to see, not to understand, pretending she was happy and always smiling and...

"...It won't, Jen!...
Jen...
I'm here, Jen! I'm..."

The two only men she had truly loved in her whole life. The one she had killed by her fear of remembering. And the one she had been running away from...

"...Gambatte ne, Jen-chan... I'm going to bring you home. No matter what..."

"...Heiji?..."

A slight start, a slight frown, and the darkness in her mind, the heavy guilt, the evergrowing despair, slowly retreating... His voice. She remembered his dislike for Malcom, but also how he used to make her strong when she needed to be. His anger, but also the kindness he was capable of. His despair, but also his love. Heiji. Together, no matter what. Together, whatever the odds would be, whatever the price to pay. Together, as they had been for so long now. Opening her eyes needed much effort though - was it an illusion, or had the time passed so fast, night almost completely fallen now? - yet this new hope... this new hope was like a flame. Burning, but sustaining, also.

"Jen. He's waiting for you... You need... you need to go back!..."

"I dun' even know... where I am..." she whispered, weakly. "An' I... I dun' even have a comm... I dunno where... Wait!..."

Blinking, clearing her vision the best she could, she slowly put a hand to the pocket of her coat. Elm's voice. He had told her something, something about using a private channel - told her something, given her something. Something that was turning out to be a rudimentary communicator, the kind of which were used by the workers in the Mines or factories, with a short ray of action, but still enough power to join someone in a vast building, in a whole area, even. Cyrus had had one like this, in the old days; she had played with it long enough to remember how it worked. Maybe she could at least try to join Elm or Rickert, and...

"...I haven't their comm ID..." she said, her voice even harsher than before. "If... it's... their first names, and not their IDs, I'm..."

She felt something stirr in her mind - a brief flash of irritation, of anger maybe, stiffled the second after, replaced by concern. Heiji.

"...Call him", he said, and she knew what it was costing him to say such a thing. "Call... call your Soldier. He's in the desert. I... think he's in one of the outposts. He was. He told me to go... maybe it was Sabulum. I'm not quite sure. Call him, maybe you..."

"Ya... called... 'im?..." Another frown, as she was slowly understanding that something was sounding horribly wrong in all of this. But he went on. "Call him. Maybe your other friends are too far, but you have a chance with him. Call him. Call him. Call him."

She turned on the communicator, her fingers numb with pain and tiredness. Inserted the vocal command initiating her logging to the private CommNet. The line was desperately static, even when she then entered Malcom's ID, asking for a local search. Static. Static. Static. And if the comm was broken... And if he wasn't here anymore!... And if... if... if... She felt the tears in her eyes, not even caring for keeping the most water she could in her body. Not caring. Malc... answer... Please... Please... answer meh... Please... Again and again, a silent prayer, a silent wish, a silent, already fading hope...

A crackle on the line. A faint buzz. And then a voice.

"...Jen?..."

She raised her head to look at the sky, crying in relief.

He was there. He'd come for her. If she could survive long enough, he'd come for her.

He would come...
