Concentrate on big issues first.
OK, the devs looked at LLTS...why?

I don't own one and don't care if they work in an imbalancing manner. Why? Because there are way more important issues than people equiping llts and pvp'ing.

They need to prioritize their bug list on the basis of how many people a fix will effect combined with the seriouslness of the problem. If a problem isn't CLEARLY bigger than another then you should look at the problems that effect the biggest amount of people.

Thus here is a list that should have been looked at WAY before any LLTS nerf!:

1.) Negative spells cast upon yourself.
(Yeah EVERYONE casts spells and this ruins rooting classes root spell.)

2.) Several classes nano's simply don't work at all!
Example : trader's mind takeover spells....used to work briefly...but now are completely broken.

3.) Monster AI is really poor. Instead of making monsters harder to fight because of HP's maybe they could wonder around. Have real stats that can be debuffed. Use their spells more effectively at higher levels....etc.

4.) Tradeskills.....completely broken...seperate trade skills from fighting IP's completely.

5.) Add spells that address PVP issues of rooting. Give all non-rooting high nano-cost root-breaking-preventing-root-for-2-seconds spells or something along those lines. The really cheap root breaking debuffs allows melee types to drain a rooters nano.

6.) No one likes fighting over a special mob that spawns in the same area all the time. If you have a non-dungeon mob that people need to complete a quest, then its spawn point better move around alot otherwise big guilds just camp him and the solo player or small guild has no chance. (IE. The trash king.)

7.) Nobody wants an Agent in their group. The devs response was to give agents a useless utility that uses a skill they wouldn't invest in normally. Thus it gimps the agent such that he doesn't need the util! The current agent false profession scheme is a farse. Just make the agent able to cast a subset of the assumed professions spells with no cast time penalty and disallow all agent spells from being castable. Then agents could be useful in groups, yet would still be less powerful than a person of that profession. -2000 to casting inits and reducing the nano-skills is going over-board in such a manner that agents NEVER cast in a group except for a buff.

8.) There needs to be a magic reflect shield introduced. Give it to the soldier and make its effect to reflect a spell onto the caster depending on its strength vs the casters stength with some randomization. Then make the spells duration halved. Have this an NCU intensive spell that has a short duration. This is a counter for debuffers and rooters.


After you fix those things....everyone will be happy to buy an expansion pack. If none of them get done, then why would I want to play the same buggy game with unbalanced pvp and boring pvm in a new scenerio?
