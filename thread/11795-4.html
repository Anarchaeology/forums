I have more MA skill than some Soldiers my level have AR, and I do 1/3 of their damage. Make there be markers where the fist weapon changes (100, 250, 400, 600, 750 and 900 MA), and gets better than it was before. Make our weapons do some at least mildly respectable damage too, and for God's sake, make them at least SLIGHTLY less like Enforcer weapons. (Side Note: Cut the attack time down on Wall Blades. I'd need 1200 melee init just to make it a 1/.8. But if you make it PhysInit, well, I might start using them. They do more damage after all.)

I'm not asking for 5 Growth here, I'm just asking that you make the heart and soul of the MA's design stop being as expensive as our ability to metamorphose matter. And while you're at it, take Psychic OUT of the required skills for MA. Make it Sense, or heck, take it out and make it a pure Agi/Str skill, or maybe Agi/Str/Sta. I hardly think my ability to balance and attack with accuracy is based even dimly on my control over my personal nanobot cloud. And don't prey on the token attack of 'it's your inner strength', that's where Dimach comes in - that's my 'inner strength' attack of being an MA.
->Lower the nano cost on LMA some. To go from VS at 696 to LMA at roughly 1100 (I don't cast it often, give me a break for not recalling offhand) is just silly. After all, UVC is only another 150-200 points more expensive to cast, a far cry from the 300-400 increase of VS -> LMA. Oh, and make the high level MA buffs more easily self-castable. I shouldn't need to go get a Neleb's just to cast my stuff. I have heard of -ONE- MA who can cast it, and this skill should not be a 190+ trick to pull off. There should be something ELSE to shoot for that high up the ladder, something more class defining than Form of Tessai.
->And while I'm at it, give us a defining characteristic ASIDE from our ability to critbuff. Maybe some nanos that alter our skills based on a fighting style? Sacrifice Evades for AC, and vice versa. Some damage for a max health sacrifice, perhaps even a nanoskill penalty to gain some in our martial artis ability. We need CHOICES, as it stands we're the masters of the slap fight and stationary combat with open palms. I hardly think admonishing a Veteran Grid Saboteur for a bad pickup line is the attack of a highly trained martial artist.
->Yes, I was getting to 'more attack animations'. I've seen Konkrete kick a whole TWO times, and it was a thrill both times. He leaned back, and let fly with a split-kick to a mob's face. It was cool, he looked like he was fighting, not proving his ability to wave his arms around in a silly manner. Maybe a few lower kicks, split kicks, backhands, heck, a roundhouse or a flurry even. Some attack animations for MA weapons would be nice too, long drawn diagonal strokes, a swift left-right motion... you know, the kind of things that make us look articulate, one of the hallmarks of martial arts.
->Yeah, some of my thoughts could be... unreasonable, but, oh well, that's the price you pay for thinking. Salt liberally, and please consider us.

nano-resist works.

but if your nanoresist skill is dark blue and not maxed or implanted
then the ATK vs defense roll is going to almost always come up
in favor of the casters maxed and implanted green nano skills.

the resist % of the nano is very important too.
a 100% nano will almost always land against
a player with equal or lesser resist vs. casters skill.

a 190% nano will be resisted by players
very often, even when thier resist is much
lower than the casters skill.

it's all about skill-checks and odds.
it's not supposed to shut-out every nano,
just like evades dont make every hit miss.

if allowed multiple attempts,
the caster will eventually land it. that's just odds.
but my trader with his 190% plunder debuff
will tell you that it's very frustrating to watch your
HP dropping while he's just standing there,
not shooting, waving his arms, and getting the message
"Target countered the nanoprogram"
"Target countered the nanoprogram"
"Target countered the nanoprogram"
over and over again.

taking hits without shooting back. (cant shoot while casting)

and that's the point of nanoresist,
to waste the casters time while you get closer / get hits in.

could just be totally immune to an NT; so it's more subtle.
