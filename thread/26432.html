Divide the shopping channels
At the moment the shopping channels are pretty useless if you are at a level below 30 or so. you have to watch a flood of items in order to find items in which you might have even the remotest interest. The newbie channel works until level 6 but from there to level 30 at least you basically cannot use the shopping channel 1-50 unless you can afford to sit and concentrate on nothing else. I have even had people send rude messages accusing me of spamming the channel when I try to sell items of lower QL.

The team channel is divided in a fairly rational way (10 level increments). I would prefer that the shopping divided along lines of at least 20 ql level increments. The player commerce is already broken enough as it stands. Why compound it by rendering the shopping channel fairly useless. Shopbot does help, but it is offline to frequently.
