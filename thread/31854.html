Animal Ecology
Something I'd love to see is a little hunting, not just the player hunting mob variety but the mob hunting mob kind. I think it'd be really cool to see a pack of scorpoids trying to bring down a bronto or see Bloodspinner or a Gargantua or something like that going after some of the blubbags.

I wouldn't want it over done where the mobs themselves were depopulating the zone but it'd be nice to be out hunting and everyonce in awhile see some mobs actually hunting one anouther. It'd really help with the immersion factor and I know I'd have a blast going around and taking screen shots of mobs going at each other like on Wild Kingdom or something. I think it'd be just awesome to see how the Rubi-Ka ecology supposedly works.
