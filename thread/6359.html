Why W2K (and WinXP) Files Keep Getting Larger
Both W2K and WinXP have a system service called the "Indexer" The Indexer is but one of many nasty Windows services in these two OS's that can bog your system down ... there are more.

The Indexer does the same thing that "FastFind" does; it indexes all NEW files by location and by content. "FastFind" is a tool that gets activated whenever you install a MS Office package. It lets you find files by what is in them, or who created them, or ???.

Since the Indexer gets installed by default, you need to stop this service before playing any games. And you should stop the scheduler service, and the maintenance service, and a bunch more services too. They are not required for playing AO; therefore, they should not be active.

Check the W2K Tweak sites for more info. I think http://www.anandtech.com has a link to a MS KB article about which services to stop to maximize performance for benchmarking and other gamer tweaks.

I think clientr.exe is getting the blame for something the Indexer (or another W2K service) is doing to your system.

As for WinME, I cannot say whether the Indexer exists in that OS, or not, because I refuse to use that piece of trash. If you have ME, switch to W2K or WinXP ... either one is faster than ME when it comes to playing games (it's been proven more than once).

I play AO under W98SE (it's still the fastest Windows OS for playing games) and do not crash. Ever!
That does not mean I have not crashed or burned, in the past.

Hope this helps ...
