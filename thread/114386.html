Those are all the ones I know off the top of my head. Lvl ranges I gave are approximates and vary wildly depending on the playstyle of the player. Also, I listed only the highest buff I know of, for example Prod Str is pretty much useless since you usually need stamina at the same time, and it does not stack with an essence. Also, adventurers have treatment buffs at high levels, but SFA is much bigger. You can also find lower level versions of many of them, often from different professions, crats and sols for example have smaller pistol buffs at much lower levels than an engy, thereby it's often easier to get a Pistol Mastery from a Sol, then find the extremely rare EP. Anyhow, I'm sure many can add onto this list for weapons I've never used and skills I've never tried. hope this helps someone...
