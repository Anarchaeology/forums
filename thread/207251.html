Jenny looked through todays messages. Lots of silly advertising, some omni friendly clan propagandaing for the "poor people left behind"...sheesh...garbage all of it. Tapping the holographic screen, she brought up a framereader and quickly entered a password. Not too longago, Eo and Ryuo had set up a link to the Omni-Pol files, so she could follow what happened down south. It was a small bug, not really that noticable, and she hadn't had any problems logging in yet. As long as she worked quickly, no one should notice.

Blablabla...drunken fight in that bar, Relax...blablabla...some moron crashing his Agada into a building in Rome...blablabla...someone being continuously torn apart by a fault slayerdroid....a neutral agent sniping some woman....blabl...wait.

She moved her finger over the holo and went back to the last file and read through it more thoroughly. Names...lots of names. She had no idea who these people were and she didn't really care. But one name stood out in neon. Zerosnake. She had heard that name before, she was positive. A name mentioned on the streets or in The Cup or something.

Quickly finishing reading the report, she closed the framereader and opened a new one, a personnel registry.

...Angel Nihilo...callsign Zerosnake, Agent SCL 206, Squad Commander of Storm...

That was it...she had heard the name! From the report, it didn't seem like the Omni-Pol officers knew it was a name, though, or they missed it. But why would a clan squad commander, member of one of the largest organizations on the clan side, be involved in something that seemed like a scorned lovers revenge on some woman? Couldn't be as petty as it seemed at first sight. Couldn't. And what's with all the agents? This Zanzoon guy, Zerosnake, Malachii...

She had to talk to someone. Last thing the north needed now was an invasion of trigger happy agents. The supression gas was simply a toy in the hands of a skilled agent. But who could she contact? Who would believe her when she connected the dots between a clan agent, a neutral agent and an omni agent? They'd laugh at her, pat her on the head and tell her to go buy some ice cream. She hated being patronized for her age!

Frowning, she shut down the holo-screen and stood up. She'd talk to him herself. Yeah! She'd talk to this clan agent and ask him herself!

He entered the cafè and she looked up, put down her cup and got ready. The last thirty minutes had been spent thinking through what she would say. She felt ready, even though the question was hard. However, instead of approaching her, he walked past her, straight to the counter and ordered a cup of coffee. With his back to her, he spoke three words.

"So...you're Tussa."

She looked at him, and before she could say anything, all the blood left her head and she felt like she was fainting. What was this? It couldn't be... Every pre-thought thing she was going to say was gone. She couldn't speak a single word, do anything but look at him with eyes that felt as big as plates.

"Y-yes...I am" she managed to stutter.

He came over to the table, calmly sat down and set his piercing eyes in her.

"So..you wanted to talk to me?" he asked in a mellow voice, keeping his gaze at her. She shook her head slightly, to get rid of what must have been a dream, and focus on him. She tried uttering a question...catching the thoughts she had had earlier, but she failed, so it was best to just spit it out. Quickly, she told him about the report she had come across, catching his name in it and how she had wondered why he had even been involved. Why his name had come across a report that included the death of a neutral agent and the mention of an Omni-Tek agent.

She struggled for word, stuttering them and felt rather embarrassed. How angry she was with herself, so completely incompetent in asking questions and so intimidated by him...by what she had thought she'd...no...lose that thought. It wasn't true. Couldn't be.

I had to..."
"..had to..what?"
"Kill him"
"Well...uhm...that's your...business..I was more worried about all the agents popping up.."
"Hah. Agents popping up. They have all been there all the time. Waiting."

She frowned. Waiting. What did that mean? Waiting for what? However, he wouldn't indulge her with answers. Instead he snickered, playing a game of 'guess the answer'. She knew she wouldn't get more out of him, and it very nearly freaked her out. He was a cunning, clever one, this high ranking agent. Not someone to mess around with. Exchanging formal, polite questions and answer didn't make anything happen. She asked him bluntly...would it lead to open street war, snipers on every roof, trying to take out the next person? As an answer, she got a laugh and a shrude smile before he lowered his voice.

"I'm a cold, Killer.. we both are.." he whispered. "..wanting to make a black mark on the world of Rubi-Ka."

Black mark? What did that mean? It sounded ominous. Agents...sniping...secrets....bad secrets....

"Oh you will see soon. I dont wanna ruin the fun just yet. You'll find out soon..."

She frowned. Was that a threat? She was jumpy around him. Wanted desperately to know more, but was too shaky to put any sort of pressure on him.

He got up from his chair slowly, looked at her, reading her like an open book, it felt like.

Oh and...How's your mom and dad?"

She nearly bit her tongue off. Her parents! How...dared he!

"Wha....?"
"They just got to this planet didnt they?"
"How do you know about my parents!"
"I know much about you Tussa. You think I'd meet you without knowing who you were?"
"Don't talk about my parents! I don't like it!"
"Glad we had a little chat. But the black mark will come.. Infact...it's already here!"

And with those parting words, he executed a gridbeacon signal, digitalizing his body, coding his DNA into the grid. He was gone and she was left with a thousand more questions.

He was bad news. He was up to something bad. She had to talk to someone. This was way beyond her...nothing she could handle.
