Looking good
I don't care what anyone says. Funcom has come a huge way in improving AO since last year. I'm here to stay!

As one of my earlier posts had said. I played AO through the stress test, through the first free month, and even payed for a month. But laggy play, bugs and other issues, I just didn't get into it. Well now, I think I've gone and got addicted, heh!

Sure, it's not perfect but it's fully playable (for me), looks jaw droppingly gorgeous, and a blast to play. My only complaint is the soloing. Sure it's fun, but why is it so hard to find people to hunt with? Or do team missions with? Stop being anti-social people! This is an RPG for goodness sake!

One thing I won't be doing is stay around these forums. I'd hate to be the only one here who actually enjoys this game. It's just a damn shame that one of the best places to talk with the community is nothing more than a giant complaint forum. I won't argue with some of the posts, and can agree that Funcom has done some wacky things, but this is THEIR brainchild to mold as they see fit. They can take some ideas from the people, but maybe they already have a full plate and just can't fit it in? The way I see it (and have always seen it) I pay my monthly fee to keep the servers online. If I wanted say in how the game is built, I'd become a shareholder.

So, this is my last post. Unless I see some noticable improvement in the general attitude here...I look forward to playing with you (those who enjoy the game for what it is...not what you want it to be). No doubt I've pissed you all off, so it's a good thing I won't be here to read the results

Long live the Clans!

Nightlith, Soldier for the Clans
