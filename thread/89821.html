Combat System
SPAMcrit_tables.pdf are 5 "Critical hit!" tables that have been corrected for Spacemaster RPG (pen-and-paper). Spacemaster is published by Iron Crown Enterprises. Does this make AO seem basic?


Critical hit!'s and Special Attacks could do more than just subtract health from a target. Such an attack could first be run through a formula that result in bodyparts (implant chart). A second formula could decide whether or not items, abilities and/or skills (relating to the part) would suffer a negative modification, either temporary or permanent.

Include some attack nanos in this system and make it work in PvP only. A change like this would certainly favor attack classes rather than support classes. It would however make support profession more valuable both on and off the battlefield. Each support class could get access to a new nanoprogram that works as an aura. Simply run up to the proper Support Class and your side effects will be removed.
