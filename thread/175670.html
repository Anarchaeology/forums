Clans To Defend Jobe Scientific Community Rights Against Omni-Tek Reclamation Claim
We recieved this release from an unknown source in Old Athen
quote:
CLANS TO DEFEND JOBE SCIENTIFIC COMMUNITY RIGHTS AGAINST OMNI-TEK RECLAMATION CLAIM
ATHEN CITY, RUBI-KA (Sept. 7, 29477). Several Clan factions held a rally in Athens today in support of the Jobe Scientific Community, developers of the Jobe Portal to the Shadowlands. The unidentified clan leaders stated that they would come to the aid of the beleaguered scientists.

Stated one unidentified spokesman, We the clans have taken up the calls to defend the freedom and justice that has so far reigned in Jobe. We will defend its community! We will defend their way of life! We will defend the portal to the Shadowlands that should be for all to benefit, not just for OMNI!

Continuing his angry speech, the Clan spokesman stated that ICC will not handle as usual and prove ineffective to the pressure the Moloch that is OMNI can put on it to make it bow to their wishes. We will trade blow for blow, shot for shot, casualty for casualty in the defense of this breakthrough development that will herald a new era for all of Rubi-Ka, not just for those who OMNI deems worthy.

The portal shall remain for all! Live long, live free!

Several demonstrators fired weapons into the air in celebration of the remarks and a loud raucous rally continued. Noted one bystander, I havent seen this many clansmen gather together since Henry Radiman was running the show.

Noted another rally-goer, We have put up with the heavy company hand long enough. This time, they have gone too far.

The Jobe Portal to the Shadowlands was recently opened by the Jobe scientific community and was promptly reclaimed under ICC commercial law by the Omni-Tek Corporation.
The spokesmen for the Jobe city scientists were unavailable for comment, but a source says that the overall development of events is worrisome and the inevitable response from JAME is being weighed and worded with the utmost gravity. The same source says that the population of Rubi-Ka can expect a statement before the portal is opened.

A worried frown.
Carefully folding the computer printout and tucking it away into a wooden file cabinet, the pale skinny man rose and slowly walked to the window. The sun was shining outside, there hasn't been a storm in West Athens for quite a few days. The rabbits jumped around as happy as always. There would be a storm now allright.

"Not unexpected. Still, unfortunate." he murmured.

Moving toward the exit door, his eyes darted to the side, to the shelf where swords and daggers, sticks and bows were placed, in symmetrical order, polished by years of practise and careful maintenance. They seemed to glimmer and shine more then usual today, thou it must have only been a reflection from the sun outside.

"Most unfortunate."

Gracefully, the skinny pale man donned his armour and stepped out the door, heading to the War Academy for contemplation. He knew that it would take hours of meditation to loosen the worried frown from his face. There must be a way to avoid bloodshed. It simply wasn't necessary, not for this.

The cheers from the crowd and the gunshots in the air could still be heard from Old Athens. Indeed there would be a new storm. Soon.

Dawn, several thousand meters above Galeway Shire. Gentle zephers tossel the blonde hair of a woman riding the currents. Famin silently exults in the feel of the wind in her hair and on her face. "Let the others flit about in thier flying contraptions, thousands of meters up with nothing supporting you but your own will...this IS flight!" A soft chime sounds. Famin activates the small hud display worked into her Keelaar Sunglasses.

A sad expression crosses the young womans features as she takes in the news from the streaming vid broadcast. Jobe. The source of all life, or so her studies had indicated. And now, Omni-Tek wants this too. If anyone could have seen the woman at this moment they might wonder what could cause a look of such utter saddness in one so young. 'We'll be going to war...again...for the fourth time.' she thinks. 'and this time, I will be alive to see it...'. A finger of fear races down her spine as she contemplates the reprecussions of this latest move of her enemy, her recently completed mission was one of sabotage. Innocent people trying to live thier lives died down there. That little voice inside her head picked this moment to begin it's haruange anew...

'Can you live with the fact that you just killed innocent people, Deary?' The voice of her consciouse was always in that hated wheedling, nasal tone of her foster mother. 'Can you live with the fact that someone you went to school with could be laying down there dead because of you, Deary?'. 'Sugar? Do your new friends know that you were once Omni...do they?'. Famin bore this internal assualt on her sanity as she did all the time, with thin lipped silence. But then, 'I Do Not 'Live with' it!'. She shouts in a silent rage against that inner voice. Then aloud. "But I will endure it. I will endure being labeled as a killer, a terrorist, even a coward, I will endure it all if it means that one day all of Rubi-Ka will be free."

Suddenly Famin terminates the program allowing her to be lighter than the very air itself, as gravity reasserts itself she tucks her arms into her sides and hurtles downward quickly reaching terminal velocity. She used to do this for fun back when she had first learned how to fly, the adreniline rush was fantastic. Now, however, she did it more often for some other reason. Her timing was still exqusite and the program kicked in just in time to avoid disaster. She is at ground level now, below treetop level moving fast. Moving north toward home, the past was dead and buried. The future was ahead in a town called Old Athens. No one saw the tear that slide from beneath the expensive sunglasses to be whipped away in the wind. In the future, there was war.
