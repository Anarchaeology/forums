If any of you have played any combat game, first person shooter like quake or anything you'll know 2 things:

a) Choke points (ie where all traffic in an area is forced through a small region) are where all the action/fighting takes place.

b) Defending "choke points" is the heart of any defense. Why spread out all over the place when you can elimiate all threats at their entry point.

Whoma/Grid = Choke Points.
