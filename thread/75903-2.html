Great map! - Nice to see someone continuing the legacy of Dovve.

One comments though on the Fixer exits overview on the right side. It's a nice adddition for newcommers to the Fixer Grid - however it does add a little confusion. The old typography used by Dovve (you still using it i see) was IMO great - simply the best ever, simple and easy to navigate by. Exits are marked with a floor number and a letter L/M/R - simply Left/Middle/Right. So when looking on the map seing an Exit Marked "9-L" in Mort, you would go to Floor 9, Find the Mort exits and take the Left one when looking at them. Simple, fast and efficient.

This new typography you are introducing does add a bit of confusion - primarily because now we have 2 different typographies on the same map. IMO the Dovve standard was easier to grasp .

As i'm loving your new map, I would also like to see it improved. So my suggestion: change it back , or make a seperate map without using the space on the right-hand side - then mabe add a small explanation at the buttom of the map ([L]eft/[M]iddle/[R]ight).
