Gsp Dancer Auditions!
-- OPEN AUDITION FOR DANCERS! --

The GSP Dancers are looking for new recruits to help strengthen their numbers and add flavor to the weekly shows and events. These auditions are open to everyone; male, female, and atrox, as well as all breeds.

The first audition will be held Monday, August 19th at 8pm EDT (00:00 UTC/GMT). This audition will be held during DJ Tarryk's LTK Deviant Radio show upstairs in the Rumble Room.

The second audition will be held Wednesday, August 21st at 6pm EDT (22:00 UTC/GMT). This audition will be held during the Lance Orbin show upstairs in the Rumble Room.

There is no need to contact anyone. If you are interested in trying out to be a dancer, merely show up at the appropriate time and you will be added to the list of those present.

All persons trying out will be asked three standard questions:
* Where are you from?
* What are your likes and dislikes?
* Why are you interested in the GSP Dancers?

Each participant will be asked to wear a social outfit of their choice, then perform a dance routine.

If you have any questions regarding the auditions, feel free to contact Anake or Wyldthing in game.
