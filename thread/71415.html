Boosted Hellspinner - A gun which soldiers have to obtain from the Cyborg Dungeon (easy step). Then we need to upgrade it by killing the Obidiency Inspector (a bit harder, requires 2-3 high level teams to do). Requires 3 skills, average damage ~900, Burst for 2.5k, FA... Your mileage may vary.

Manex Catastrophe December A gun which can be had as a mission reward. Requires 2 skills, average damage ~1.5K, Burst of 5k.

Boosted Hellspinner was introduced as a soldier only weapon, sort of a "balancing act". At this moment a soldier with manex does better damage than soldier with a hellspinner. Both are outdamaged by Manex-wielding Traders/Fixers.

This MTI Martins was considered to be too powerful and "Unbalancing" and removed from the game. Strangely enough, my tests showed it inferior to the Hellspinner. Alas, those were just my tests.

Finally, there is a rumor that NW will finally introduce the Nophex Plasma Destroyer. I've not rolled a soldier on beta, and I still don't know if they will be mission only or available through some silly quest.
