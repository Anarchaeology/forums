Ehm. Shadowrun?

Try William Gibson's "Sprawl Trilogy": that is, _Neuromancer_, _Count_Zero_, and _Mona_Lisa_Overdrive_. Father of cyberpunk; I enjoy Shadowrun plenty, and Cyberpunk 2020 (a similar, older game, minus the magic and races), but don't fool yourself into thinking those games came up with the ideas.

The Grid's an obvious iteration of Gibson's Matrix, updated for nearly thirty thousand years in the future. ;)

With that said, yes, a more matrix-like (please note that this does not, in -any- way, refer to the recent movie; read the above books) Grid would be welcomed and interesting, but hardly something we can/should expect. Maybe a couple years down the road we'd see something like that, but I'm not holding my breath.
