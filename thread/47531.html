Vanity
Soleet
Economy
Well of course, the main real problem is that RK doesn't HAVE an economy. The is no method of manufacture, supply and demand. Technically there is an infinite amount of credits and an an infinite amount of items, even Team Beacon Warp nanos. However, because of the overvaluing of everything and credits being too freely available, people pay too much for everything. There is also no international trade, so day-by-day the humble 'credit' devalues even more.

As far as I can see (and as far as my little knowledge of Economics takes me) the best way forward would be the following :

1a) All vending machines to have a FINITE number of stock items. These are not automatically replenished, but topped up by the players themselves who collect the various bits in missions. Funcom claim that each item has its own unique ID number anyway, so this should not be difficult to implement. Junk in chests now becomes important. The randomness of the supply of items will ensure there is a fluctuation in the value of certain goods. For instance, in week 1 a Nano Pylon will be worthless because there are so many about; but later if supply dries up, then their value will soar.

1b) Because of the above, more items would now have to be built - giving the Engies and Traders more to do. Forget the 12 step process in making guns, reduce it down to 2 or 3 like the weapon containers so it is manageable.

2) This will be a totally unpopular idea, but its the best solution for fixing this screwed economy. SALES TAX or VAT - whatever you want to call it. I suggest 15% of any sale is lost to OmniTek, Clan Leaders or ICC depending on your faction. Any time cash changes hands, 15% is added, but only the original figure goes to the vendor. This, by nature will bring prices down. 15% of 950,000,000 is a lot of cash to lose. 15% of 1,000,000 is peanuts in comparison.

3) I also agree with the earlier post that Clan, Neutral and Omni credits are introduced. Also make certain items spawn only in Clan, Neutral or Omni areas. Introduce a proper system of Trade between the factions and when conflicts increase - Trade embargoes and sanctions can be introduced, making it illegal to Trade. Now the Fixers come in with their smuggling operations.

Perhaps I am getting ahead of myself and this is just a pipe dream, but why not?
