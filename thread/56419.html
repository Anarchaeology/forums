Secret to Beating Ian Warr Revealed
Ok, one warning to begin with, all of the items needed for this process are difficult to get and will require a large force of about 20-40 people. Required Professions are 1 crat, 5 agents, 5 enforcers, 5-10 fixers, and a doctor in case you make a mistake and have to run. Fill the other slots with whatever profs you wish.

First, you need a crat that has one of those miyashiro coffee machines. You have to combine it with one of those enhanced coffee blocks preferably the fragment fink kind. Now you use the machine to produce enough coffee cups to fill 5 backpacks, any size. You then hand the 5 backpacks to 5 agents that have at least 700 in aimed shot and 750 in WS each. They must also each have an alsaqri chemical rifle and a container of liquid ammo.

Now each agent must combine the coffee with the container of liquid ammo one at a time. After all the coffee has been put in the container, you will get a coffee filled ammo container. Combine the container with the alsaqri rifle and you will finally end up with a Superior alsaqri chemical coffee rifle.

Go to the ace camp in efp and make sure you completed the quest to make ian and his buddies spawn. You must have the crat charm a human mob, any lvl mob will work, and bring the charm to Ian. Make the charm do the emote of /moon in Ian's general direction and you will receive a message saying "As Ian sees this act of disrespect, he is surprised at the monsters incompetence and make a face like this :O". All of the surrounding mobs such as Nelly Johnson will make the same face. This is where you have to move fast. Have the five agents use an aimed shot with their coffee rifles so they can aim the chemical coffee into each of the 5 mob's mouths. If done correctly, each of the mobs will go into a caffine shock and start spasming. They will start shaking and sweating for about 2 minutes. (If you have 5 enforcers equipped with paper hammers, you can fan off the mobs and prolong the spasming for an extra 5 minutes.) The mixture of all the movement and the sweat will make the joints between the armor pieces decompress and they will fall off the bodies of the mobs. You must have the fixers (or any other character with very high run speed, +1200 speed) run in and pick up every piece of the armor. *Note* Don't attack any of the mobs while they are spasming. The effects of the coffee raises their body temperature drastically and gives them a 3000 damage fire shield.

Run away before the spasming ends and head to 20k. Give all of the armor from each mob to Milo Chatter. For every full suit of armor from the mobs you give to milo, you get a unique item. For Example, if you give all of Ian's armor to Milo, you get either the blood bat or the blood mace. Give him all of Nelly's armor, you get the Dress of Nelly. So on and So on. After you give him all 5 sets of armor, your group will receive 10 million xp and 50 million creds. Also around 50-100 tokens depending on your character level.

Congratulations on Completing the quest of Ian Warr and his buddies!
