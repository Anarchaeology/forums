Ida Sculler/Professor Maitland and the Shadowlands
Ok, while everyones running around looking at Radiman and Co, been running around looking into well, my usual NT things and looking to see if there's any news about Jobe, Shadowlands, and well an NT which doesnt fumble and does decent damage :P

Anyhow, here's a copy of an interview I did a while back with Ida Sculler, a Nanomage NT working on T-Dim experiments for Omni-Tek (NPC found in Omni-Trade if you want to chat with her yourself).

--------------------------------------------------

Nyadach: Hello

Ida Sculler: Hello.

Nyadach: Might I enquire who are you?

Ida Sculler laughs nervously. A sort of high pitched whine that quickly gets annoying.

Ida Sculler: I'm Ida Sculler. I'm a nanotechnician. Although I do more research than fieldwork. I see a lot of dedicated people utilizing our latest research, and that makes me proud. I am assisting the most notable of technicians in their research at the HQ labs. We've got stuff there that would blow your mind. Literally.

Ida Sculler: Anyway. I talk too much.

Nyadach: What kind of research might that be?

Ida Sculler: Oh, we're working on some fabulous stuff. I've had the pleasure of seeing it myself. Professor Maitland did this amazing T-Dim experiment a while ago, and actually burned a hole in space. I was so impressed!

Ida Sculler smiles

Ida Sculler: Anyways, I digress. We have a lot of really good projects going, which will really help out the general public once we've made it safe to use. We even have Eva Pourais come by one of the clone la...

Ida Sculler gives of a little whine of a laugh.

Ida Sculler: Well, I guess I shouldn't brag about it too much. We're far from done with our research in most departments. We're on the bleeding edge, you know.

Nyadach: The leading edge of what?

Ida Sculler: Hmm?

Nyadach: Ok, subtle hint taken. What kind of fieldwork?

Ida Sculler: I don't care much for that. It's dirty and it's definitely not a controlled environment. I'll gladly let you guys do all the fieldwork.

Nyadach: What has Eva Pourais to do with thing?

Ida Sculler: She comes in every now and then to see how we're doing. She seems to always be in a hurry and is constantly bossing people around like she was on a schedule or something. I don't like her.

Nyadach: Professor Maitland? Im unfamiliar with his work, can you tell me anything about it?

Ida Sculler giggles.

Ida Sculler: Professor Maitland is the greatest. He's really dedicated, and sad to say, he doesn't get out much. I make sure to get lots of air. The labs can get quite stale after a while. Maitland is the best scientist in in the field of nanotechnology. I really admire him. He's so smart. Plus he's really cute too, in a distracted and detatched kind of way.

Nyadach: Whats the T-Dim experiment?

Ida Sculler: It's short for trans-dimensional. But I really can't talk about this.

Nyadach: Trans-dimensional you say?

Ida Sculler: I really can't talk about this, Ill get in trouble.

Nyadach: I dont suppose I can help you at all with these experiments?

Ida Sculler: Eh? I don't think I got that right.

Nyadach: Can I assist your experiments?

Ida Sculler: Hmm I dont think so?

Ida Sculler: Omni-Tek will employ you, just look for the nearest mission booth.

Nyadach: Its been nice meeting you, and thank you for the time in answering my questions.

Nyadach: Goodbye, and may your nanites not fumble.

Ida Sculler: Bye
