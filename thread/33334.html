Omni-Tek vrs Omni-Pol
Greetings Citizens.

Born in our home Solar System , curious of information , I am Yorkforce an Agent employed by Omni-Tek.

I come here today to report some news of events that I have personally experienced first hand during my time on Rubi-Ka.

As some of you know I used to be a law enforcement officer within the rank of Omni-Pol,when I first became aware of the conflicts between the Rebel Clans and Omni-Tek I felt it was my duty to protect those unable to protect themselves ..the reality soon became apparent that this was far from the truth and that in my belief Omni-Pol do not protect the innocent but that they exploit thier power and enforce thier own personal laws.

We all know of the *bloodbath* within the Rhompa Bar a few moon ago,when pyjama clad party goer where shot down in cold blood, from my understanding it was told to me that certain individuals were to publically speak against the Omni-Tek cooperation and rally support for its cause,this to me was enough of a reason to raid that evening and thus I did my duty.

What I witnessed was the very scope of the entire agression and killing that started when I saw with my own eyes an official Omni-Pol officer shoot down Thyme ,a lovable Rebel who was unarmed and un protected.

I could not believe my eyes,It was publically stated by Miester the President of Omni-Pol that his officers were attacked first and to prove this he explained the use of the supression gas in the area and that this was the only plausable explanation to why the shooting started,this was an out right lie,I voiced my concerns very clearly to my General of what was happening and made a point that he understood my feelings.

The actions of the Omni-Pol officers were condenmed right across the planet that night ,I know, i was the target of that comdemnation and i felt ashamed to be an Officer of our fantastic company.


Now , Id like to make very clear of what my Political Stance is within all of this, I will never compliment the actions of anyone, Clan , Omni or Neutral that start a mortal agression against another, I do however feel that everyone has a instictive right to defend themselves and thier property.
I will not and have never broken any Law within my company of Omni-Tek and I will never intentionally do so,
I believe we should have laws and that those laws should be upheld and respected.
I do have many clan freinds .. and also have many Omni-Tek freinds but my views upon them are not in relation to thier political stance but of who they are.
I will always strive to protect any innocent citizen of Rubi-Ka from another who starts a mortal agression towards them.

This being said, Last night I was present at the Rhompa bar when low and behold but who enters .. you guessed it Omni-Pol officers made yet another raid, but were not supported by the presence of the Official Omni-Pol dept,thus a another bloodbath was averted.

During the comotion,I was standing outside .. playfully and non agressivly teasing the Omni-Pol officers with little comments .. such as ..*If I push such and such an Officer will they all fall over like domino's (they were lined up outside,and i was sure they wernt breathing..stood like statues) another comment I made was where i tapped on the armour of an officer and asked if there was anyone inside, it was then i heard the voice of another officer , named Metallica86 .. who shouted in full presence of over 50 omni-tek witnesses and I quote * You will be the first to Die Yorkforce* my first reaction was to shout back that they had indeed falled to an all time low to make a death threat against a lawful omni-tek citizen who was only out to join in the celebrations that were going on in the rhompa bar after a hard days work in galway,I was out with my soul mate and eternal love , Liss.

I tried pointlessly it seems to ask Metallica86 privatly as to why a law enforcement officer would make a death threat against one of his own citizens like some kind of yob,he wouldnt reply, In the end i sent him a rather long message that I would report this to a General or the President himself because i could not fathom as to why Omni-Pol would want to execute or murder me.

So what does the title of this post have to do with all this i hear you ask?

Well .. after the many reports of the brutality of the actions of Omni-Pol I no longer feel that Omni-Pol represent the law of Omni-Tek .. they have continued to break thier own laws like anarchists,and they would rather pull thier triggers then speak civilally to another who does not share thier agressive views.

So I call upon an Omni-Pol high ranker to reply to my concerns and to prove me wrong by lawfully dealing with Metallica86 and his vendetta against a respectful ,lawful and non agressive Omni-Tek employee.
Or .. I would accept a personal apology from Metallica86 to me and my partner Liss (it wasnt very nice for her to see an Officer make a death threat against her partner) and take it no further.

My question to all other Omni-Tek citizens .. do you feel that Omni-Pol represents the law of our companys? .. if so , in what way? and if not .. why?

Reguards
