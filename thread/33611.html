*** ARK/GM Fun day!! Hosted by Vixen Entertainment *** (RK1)
Vixen Entertainment is pleased to host the first ever ARK/GM fun day!!

We all know how hard these citizens of Rubi-Ka work for us, so now it's time to celebrate that hard work and effort with the biggest party on the planet!

The festivities will take place at the Reets Retreat (with an after party at the Rompa Bar), July 12th at 8pm est. The evening will include a beauty competition, Leet dance, Rompa Race, Dance contest and much much more.

So, invite your favorite GM and ARK and let's "Get this party started!!"

For more information, please contact Aktrez at AKTREZ@hotmail.com.
