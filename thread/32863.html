The Joint was Jumping (Tales of the Rompa bar)
Still feeling some of the effects of yesterday’s mood elevating nano, Ehrynn ventured back to the Rompa Bar. The joint was jumping.

She bumped right into Lev Rivers and a few of his companions. Rivers was attempting to strong-arm her dear friend Sightsee about some new drug that would make White Trash look like a blessing on society. She had no idea how Sightsee was involved but it was also clear that he had no idea how Lev was involved.

Sight was completely caught off guard when Ehrynn drew her pistols. It was clear he had not heard about the River’s gang hold up at the advance store earlier. Unfortunately, as soon as she pulled her weapons she found herself looking down the barrels of Lev’s gang members. She swallowed and tried to hold on to her anger; it wasn’t working and she hoped her knees would not buckle. To make matters worse, another friend of hers, Charley was sure to be caught in the crossfire if things went sour.

Then almost as quickly as it started...it was over. Rivers had concluded his business with Sightsee and left with his gang in tow. Ehrynn slumped onto the bench seat and sobbed. Much to her surprise, Sightsee was paying her a compliment for standing up to Lev Rivers. She smiled weakly and desperately hoped he would take it as a sign of humility and not the washed out look of someone who had been on the verge of panic.

Ehrynn did her best to bring Sightsee up to speed on Lev’s activities, the robbery and the murder. She even pointed out the waitress, Kinkajou, who had been robbed by Rivers at the advanced store. Sightsee then asked about her brother. She began her now familiar catechism: “No she had not heard from him; no she didn’t have any new leads; no she had not located Melba; no she had not given up hope.” The last statement was a lie. She had given up hope.

She smiled when Charley and her friend Ladymatrix offered to aid in the hunt for Melba and Tyrianno. People were always nice about wanting to help.

Ehrynn excused herself and went to the restroom. “Time for another one of these nice little mood elevating nanos," she spoke to the face in the mirror. “It makes it so much easier to deal with my brother, the University, and my ever so loving parents.”
