The Professionals are rolling again! Here are the answers you have been waiting for!
As the story once again, suddenly, jumped out of the closet it has been hiding in, with numerous events daily, the professionals program also woke up again. That is, the professionals have been active all along, but now Cosmik is back in action, with lots of posts and discussions. Amonst them the long awaited answers to the 10 questions every profession submitted a while ago. This might also have to do with Mattie (you all know Mattie) had a little talk with Cosmik's superior

Anyway, here it is, and I think it is much better than I had feared, actually very promising, something to work from to get the agent where it is supposed to be.

First of all the agent hood. Cosmik stated to me that the agent hood will not change at this time. They have however noted that we want it changed, but the graphics team is very busy with Shadowlands now. So we better get used to the current look for now.

This brings us on to Aimed Shot. I had several questions about Aimed Shot, one of them was to get the calculations for it. What skills are involved and such, as there is much confusion about this in the agent community.

FunCom will not give us this calculation, because they dont want the game to boil down to just numbers, with everyone following the same dull skill template. I also brought up the damage of the Aimed Shot (or the lack of high multipliers with high Aimed Shot skill), and Cosmik said he will look at the damage, possibly do some tests with me on the testserver. It is a fact that Aimed Shot hits far too often minimum damage...

Moving on to more Aimed Shot, I asked him about the 40% cap in PvP. If it was possible to do some changes there as it hurts our Aimed Shot pretty bad, reducing it to a Flingshot. Cosmik is open for discussing changes, possibly rising the cap on Aimed Shot and Full Auto to for example 50%. Also he was open to the suggestion to add put a cap on healing too to balance PvP more.

The cap issue will probably be discussed a lot in the Professionals the coming weeks.

Several agents have submitted to me the idea of a few seconds stun on Aimed Shot, this will not be done. FunCom wants Aimed Shot to be about damage, but they are appearently open to discussion on the damage Aimed Shot does, like stated above.

My next question was about an issue a lot of agents have, the lack of defence in agent form. I submitted two ideas players have given me, the mechdog that draws aggro from the agent, and extending the stun nano Death's Grace into a while line. Cosmik like both ideas and pushed it on. He did however state the classic "we have the defence of other professions", but I will make it clear that as long as we cannot use Mimic Profession, we can't do much more than buffing and using MP pets... It seems that FunCom have the illusion that we can in fact act as doctors for the team and ourselves in FP. He did however say that as soon as my character is set up on the test server he will set up some tests with me to test defence. As my agent is a Mimic using level 180, I will probably have to make him start a lower level agent to do the tests, but the important thing is that we will probably do some testing.

Another nano related suggestion was to remove our useless DoTs and extend our evade debuff nano into a line of evade debuffs. Cosmik talked to the nano designer, and this is not a change that will be done now, but they will keep it in mind for the future.

Going on to the Ruse of Taren 2/3 speed bug in grid, it is up for a fix, but hard to tell when. At least they know about it.

For all the agents out there with an X-3 you know how well it performs and what a great rifle and weapon it is. I asked for more rifles of the same quality, some of them maybe with an added Flingshot special for those wanting that. Cosmik said that new rifles of the same quality as the X-3, some with Flingshot on it too along with Aimed Shot was something the weapon designer at FunCom liked a lot, and something we will probably see added in future patches (new weapons are added every patch mostly, so keep a look out for it).

FunCom will however not, as some agents suggested to me, add a damage type change to our existing damage buffs. This is because we already have lots of rifles with different damage types so we can easily change with them. At the current time this is probably not true, as the old rifles with different damage types cant really compete with the Caterwaul and the X-3, both energy nowadays. But with more rifles probably being added the coming patches, we should expect to see more different damage types in there

And finally, the fixing of Aimed Shot in teams, or rather, sneak mode being broken by team nanos. As I stated earlier in a post here, we have a solution for it, and it is being implemented in one of the coming patches. Cosmik couldnt give me an exact patch, but very soon. "It is being put in the first patch it can be" Cosmik stated.

So what happens now?

I will be working more with Cosmik about the FP-line, especially getting a change for Mimic Profession and Assume Profession. What many agents wants is nano skill debuff on Mimic Profession changed to -100, as the current -140 reduces a level 200 agent to a level 120 doctor or trader or whatever.

Also I will make sure to follow up on the Death's Grace nano being made into a whole line of stun nanos. I think it could help us out a lot on defence, and it seems like a change they are open to. Other ways of defence will also be a priority to me.

And finally Aimed Shot. It is a big issue that I will keep working on, first making FunCom realize the problems Aimed Shot has, both PvM and PvP, and then trying to get a change we can all live with.

If you guys have any more issues with the agent you want me to work with, just reply here, or make another post about it in the forums. I read the forums every day, snapping up ideas, bugs and issues. Especially now that the Professionals program seem to be back on track.

On the other hand, you are partially right about some of your complaints. As an Agent Professional my job is to find good suggestions and discussions and bring it on to FunCom. If I am to do my job well for FunCom, I will of course have to filter stuff. If I didn't filter, they wouldn't need me. Still, I don't stop a suggestion someone make if I don't like it myself. As I said before I try my best to represent the community not myself.

I bring on suggestions to FunCom that I think are doable, things that I think might be something FunCom would actually do. When applying this filter I also have to think about lots of factors, balance between classes is one of them. I try my best guys, and I must say I think I have done a pretty good job so far. I also think that you guys have done a great job. You come to me with so many great ideas and suggestions, and you make sure I am aware of all issues agents have. I try and make sure all voices are heard and that all discussions and suggestions gets through, but as I said, my filter will cause some of them to fall out.

My point here is that my job for FunCom is to pick out agent discussions, issues, bugs, suggestions that are doable, and that is balanced. If you have good reasoning for your suggestion, or there is a good discussion going on I will push it on. If there is a suggestion about giving us godmode nanos, "just because", I will not. I am a human being with feelings and opinions, so I can never be perfect. This is however a job that could not have been done by a machine.

I usually post replies to threads I bring on to FunCom, so if you have a really good idea, and you feel that I am, unfairly, overlooking it, you can still use the old route, go directly to FunCom reps with it. And the FunCom reps still read the boards, picking up good suggestions and discussions.

I dunno if this all makes sense, or if I am just talking woodoo here, I have at least tried to defend the program that I believe in, and that I think can do a lot of good for the game as long as FunCom are active and listen to us.
