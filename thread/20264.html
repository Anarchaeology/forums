Im sorry to say this but there just isn't much to look forward too at the moment when it comes to hunting. From lvl 90 - 120 the best and fastest xp is hunting borgs in mort if your clan or the Ace camp in EFP if your omni.. however they both lie in 25% zones so expect to get ganked fairly often.

Personally speaking I say try either place... as long as you regularly save when you do get ganked then you won't lose much xp.

After those places hunting is non-existant anywhere, with missions becoming the only real option. This is an issue FC must address as it has been b!tched about since launch and has yet to be resolved.
