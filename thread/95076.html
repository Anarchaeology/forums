We can't go neutral for the OOC reason that Funcom doesn't want any Neutrals, long-term.

The Neutral faction was intended to be a way for new players to start up, look around, and then make a choice by the time they reached level 50 or so. Neutral characters start with both Clan and Omni applications in their inventories.

This is why, until recently, neither side's guards attacked Neutrals, and why Neutrals weren't allowed to initiate attacks in 25% PvP gas, and so forth.

It's also why these benefits are starting to be taken away from Neutrals and why Funcom is pushing Neutrals into a conflict with the Clans. They want to make the Neutrals choose a side.

Jynne said it best.

quote:
The Neutral faction was intended to be a way for new players to start up, look around, and then make a choice by the time they reached level 50 or so. Neutral characters start with both Clan and Omni applications in their inventories.
To be more detailed about it, the game is based on conflict. It is Anarchy Online. Not Peace and Liberty Online. It is about war. The entire point upon which the game is based is the struggle between two sides. Funcom knows that there will be people who will want to remain neutral for roleplaying purposes, but they're not going to make it easy.

Honestly, the OOC ways that Funcom has restricted neutrality (and the inability to turn neutral) helps to improve the RolePlaying in the game as well as increase the entropy of the combat (hence the enjoyment of the setting).

Fact is, in a world such as this, it WOULD be incredibly difficult to remain neutral. I think Funcom has done a good job to reflect this both RP-wise and OOCly.

I agree, but it is definitely not impossible. Right now, it's right at the stage it should be (that's my opinion of course, nothing more), the "Very Difficult" stage. Being neutral and promoting peace is so very difficult.

Welcome to a realistic portrayal of War and Peace. None of it denies your right to remain neutral and/or peaceful, nor does it remove the possibility you have of doing so.
