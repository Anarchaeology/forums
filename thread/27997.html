Two Things I Hate
Ok, first I'm a L79 Engi that has been a L79 Engi for way to long because of things like this:

(1). Soloin a medium level mission. First room has 3 Yellow mobs. I send my QL100 (approx) bot into the room to kill a couple or all three of them. He takes care of 2, so I go in to assist on the third. We kill the last one and I get exactually ZERO XP points for any of them. There was a little lag, but not too bad. The message box says I can loot all three of them, which I do, but not a darn thing about XP. The way I understand Engi's is that my bot is my main weapon, but because he killed the mobs and not me directly I get no XP. What's up with that?

(2). Soloin a medium level mission DEEP in Andromeda. Clear the enitre house and have the mission item to take back to Omni-E. Woot! I'm up 250K XP and ready to run back (no I dont have a Yalm, I know lame). While getting ready to leave the house, I get booted! The whole mission I had green ping, no lag, nothin, then I get booted when mission is at 100%. The agonizing minutes pass as I try to log back in before my bot shuts down. What do you think happened? Of course, I get back in and my bots gone. Also, of course, I needed at LEAST mastries to cast that bot. Cuss, curse, cuss, gripe, etc. Oh well, I only got attacked once getting here and that mob was just green. I'll just cast a lower bot and take the same way back, np! I step outta mission cave and look around. I have just enough time to realize that there is 6 RED mobs that wanna make the cave I just came out of their new home. See ya at the reclaim -250K XP. What's up with that?

The only positive on the second scenrio is that the mission item was a Warp Time-Space Borielas NanoCrystal. Delete mission and head to the shopping channel to salvage some of my dignity.

Some things just never change!
