I have a lot of crazy ideas, more like had, pertaining this stuff, so here we go:

Since you can now place stuff on the ground (ie back at release a sofa apartment item would be a ? box), and even better, retain ownership of items on ground, why not allow players to set up their own shop terminals? How great would it be to have player run shops? You go into a shop, that allows you to then access player run shops by typing in /entershop "yugi", and can only be accessed from inside the static shop. Inside my shop I can have terminals set up, and any kind of decorations I want. The way tradeskills are really taking off, this would be a fantastic facet to tradespeople. If you wanted to, you could hang out in your shop, or just let it run manually. To save on coding and confusion, you could design it so the shop owner could check his balance his shop has generated, and then make withdrawals from that, from a terminal inside the static shop access point.

I had come up with this idea back in the day, but tradeskills sucked, apartments weren't really working well, etc. Now it's all in place except the shop system. I know this is a WILD idea, and would take some work, but wow wouldn't it be nice to have your own shop with your own decorations and whatnot?

A totally different idea I had was to have missions either generated or static, where at the end was a special "stolen" or "underground" shop terminal, where you could buy special items from. You could only buy one item frrom it per mission, per person lets say. It wouldn't be game breaking stuff, but say it might randomly spawn things like rare nanos, armor, etc, but it was a hefty price. You could even have class specifc or breed specific missions, etc. This was another neat idea that some people liked, but I never heard anything about.
