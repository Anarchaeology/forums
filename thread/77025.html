Hi,
The article that describes the the towers is HERE.

I think you can buy premade towers up to ql 50. Then you have to make the ql 75 & 150. There is a ql 225 & 300 but those are unmakable at this point.

The steps are fairly easy and require Mech Engineering (maybe some other skills which I had enough of at the time). Here is example of ql 150......

First you have to do a quest with the Supplier at the Clan/Omni base. Give him Gift Wrap Basket and he will give you a Multi Purpose Tuner.

Use the Multi Purpose Tuner on the Semi-Sentinent Service Tower Library (the different QL's have different names ie. ql 75 is called Android Service Tower Library) to get Semi-Sentinent Service Tower Brain.

Use Service Tower Shell on Semi-Sentinent Service Tower Brain to get Inactive Semi-Sentinent Service Tower.

Now all you need is a sweet spot where you want to drop this bad boy.

All the parts can be purchased from the Tower Shops. I can't remember the exact amount but I think each of the ql 150 pieces cost 50 - 75k.
