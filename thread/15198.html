Umm.... I don't think you can really even attempt to read anything out of the size of the wrangle buffs. If you look at the top buffs for most professions, you'll tend to notice that they're all NCU hogs. The top Doc hp buffs (Team and Temp) and HoT are all 55 NCU+. The top wrangle is 58 NCU. So it's pretty much 'par for the course' NCU-wise. The only diff is that Traders can use their top buffs a hell of a lot earlier than most other profs (with Plunder/Divest stacking.

The Wrangle lines were NOT solely designed for overequipping... of that I am sure. They were designed also to enhance the abilities of others (the whole team in the case of Team wrangles) at the cost of the Trader's own abilities. That loss of abilities can in fact be compensated for with skill drains on the mobs. If you don't have the NCU, make them. Having teamed with a Trader who pushed his ATK rating over 1200 with them, I can say they're well worth it even if you're not wranging the entire team all the time.

Also note that... for a character to be able to overequip using the 132 wrangle without suffering from penalties when it expires... they need 528 self-buffed ability in that skill. And that is for the TOP wrangle. You can get 210 points from implants (less if there are clashes), and 20 points from Gen buff.... so it really isn't hard to get within 'range' of making that 132 wrangle useful.
