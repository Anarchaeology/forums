I don't want to fight mobs in missions. The Thief games were some of my favorites, and I would like to play missions like this. I would like to sneak through missions, grab whatever gold I can get, get the reward and get my actual experience through grouping.

I would have thought that the whole point of having a bunch of different classes, doctor, agent, enforcer, fixer, etc, was so that people could chose their own style of playing and roleplay and do what they want within the game. One person could make credits by buying and selling items, or making items and selling them. Or, a small group of 2 or 3 with buffed tanks and a doctor could do red missions together for money and items.

Some characters could make credits by installing implants for others, or making implants, or even casting high level buffs on people.

Some groups could decide that doctors have too much trouble making credits and deserve to be paid for their services, and so decide to let the doctor in the group have half of the loot from the mobs they killed. (I have no idea if this would be appropriate or not, just a hypothetical)

And one would have thought that an agent could make credits by sneaking through missions and getting the rewards without having to kill anything.

I'm not demanding to be able to sneak through all red missions with zero risk, that would be silly. But I assume that I should be able to sneak past greys, greens, and yellows with very little risk, and past oranges with some risk. And if massively buffed, perhaps I should have a chance at sneaking through a red mission. Not a good chance, but some chance at least.

If the only acceptable way to make credits in this game is by killing all the mobs in missions, then what's the point in having so many different character classes? You wouldn't need more than 3 or 4 if all anyone was allowed to do to was kill mobs.
