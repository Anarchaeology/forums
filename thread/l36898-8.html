Okay, I've read approx the first 5 pages of this thread and I have to speak up before I read anymore because I'm going nuts.

First of all, I would like to say that the first character I made was an agent. I absolutely loved the concept. Well, I made it to lvl 10 and realized that the character was NOTHING like what I invisioned. Aimed shot didn't work at all, and I realized I was a more proficient killer with martial arts than any of the rifles I found/could afford lol. So I created an adventurer because they seemed like a more versitle class.

I got my adventurer into the mid 20s range and got the spell Pronouncement of Greatness. From that point forward I was having huge amounts of fun. If I couldn't find a group, no problem. Set a mission to hard and sneak through it. Now, as an adventurer in leet form, I was still very often getting caught by the mobs I was trying to sneak past. It was very VERY risky for me to attempt a mission like this, and I died quite often, but it was FUN! I got next to NO experience for these missions and would have byfar made a lot more money actually killing the things I was sneaking past.

Now, I realized that I wasn't a MASTER of deception and sneaking the way my agent should have been, so I had no problem at all when I got caught. I figured an agent could and SHOULD be able to get past the mobs I couldn't with my adventurer. It fits with the entire agent concept. Sneak in, fix what needs to be fixed, sneak out, mission complete.

I have a problem with Funcom's entire concept of stealth in the first place. Ever notice that if you set the slider all the way to stealth a lot of the missions require you to KILL everyone in the mission in order to complete the mission? Please someone explain to me how this is stealthy? It doesn't require any sort of stealth skills at all; it makes hack and slash unavoidable. Another reason this is completely illogical, in Rubi-Ka DEATH IS NOT PERMINENT! The "kill em all cuz we need to cover our tracks missions" don't make sense at all. Wouldn't all of those that you killed remember that you killed them and be able to report to their superiors that you killed them and took their "highly sensitive secret info" or whatever? It just doesn't make sense. Steath should require that you sneak in UNDETECTED do what you came to do and get out UNDETECTED!!!

Leet form by itself didn't let adventurers do this in harder missions, and an agent's unbuffed sneaking skills by themselves didn't allow agents to do this in the harder missions. Combined leet form and master sneaking abilities SHOULD (imo) allow you to accomplish the more difficult missions in this manner.

Also, does anyone else find it ironic that Funcom has stated over and over and over how little credits mean but the second someone finds a way to make money by non-traditional means (ie hack and slash) they nerf it? For example, here is a quote from today's (July 23rd) Daily Update "In fact, credits are not that valuable. When you get to around level 100, buying a nano formula will cost you almost one million! There are in fact no items of higher quality level than 125 in any shops besides nano formulas, so you will have to buy all items off other players or find/make them yourselves past that level! I feel really sad when I watch people spend real money buying in-game pocket change on some of the better known auction sites." So if all the money we are making now, even sneaking through the reddest of missions is "pocket change" why the need for the nerf at all?

I just don't get it. <boggle>

Funcom, you have a huge amount of irate people on your hands over this. And I don't blame a one. All this nerf has done successfully is 1) destroy what little agents had going for them 2) handicap other classes (adventurer/fixer/other classes with some concealment affinity) who actually used (with great risk) their stealth skills 3) Made the game in general that much more geared toward hack and slash when so many of us came here to get away from that in the first place. And I do believe that even those that are completely uneffected by this nerf would agree that it would be nice to be able to gain a little advancement through a means other than hack and slash, be it sneaking, trade skills, exploring, whatever...

Finally, why was this done in the first place? At least when Verant nerfs a class they do it because they get massive amounts of complaints from players that a certian class is too strong. Had anyone even complained that agents with their sneaking abilities and leet form combined were too strong? Did I just miss that rant? I read the boards every day and have not heard a peep on the subject from anyone. At any rate, I think the nerf has by far upset more people than it pleased.

Just my 1 million credits worth...which FC has already said are worthless.
