* Party Saturday, April 13th at 3pm PST @ Neut's! *
Next party will be held @ Neut's this Saturday, April 13th at 3PM PST!

I hear the Neutrals club has a pretty rocking stereo setup and I would like to give it a try! You know the mix, you know the place, and you know you want to come!

We are planning on having a dance contest during some sweet tracks by DJ Icey for breakdown hour! So far the current prize is 500k, but I am taking small donations towards the prize. I am collecting a panel of seasoned dancing judges to rate each participant on a 0-10 scale for their performances. Each dancer will be given a few minutes (depending on the number of dancers) for getting their groove on!

I will try to make a post near the day of the event with the approximate time for the dance contest since the party will be fairly lengthy (from 3pm-3am!)

I have purchased a Live365 stream that I can relay through. I will be working on the mix this week and hope to see you all there!

Clan, Neutral and Omni are all welcome to attend of course!

Please contact me if you want to be a judge or donate to the contest prize. I would like to be able to reward the top 3 dancers with the highest scores in the contest if possible!
