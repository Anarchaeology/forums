Neuters - I still not get it - I talk about dumbness - I am dumb ?
I like my nanotech. He is neutral. I wanted to and I intent to stay neutral.
I tried an omni Enforcer and a clan Metaphysician. Both are interesting. I especially like my MP.
But I still prefer my neutral character (if I want different aspects I play with my other characters, simple).

I prefer him mainly because I hate stupidity. A lot (I don't know how many or what is the %) of clanner and omnis are dumb (sorry I may be wrong, but I like using "strong" words and provoquing a bit). Killing everything, every mobs (without asking themselves if they are of the same side, or even killing people in the streets of cities, the best of stupidity is to kill guards, and they do that).
Leveling and leveling all the time. Running without talking (there is maybe a part of responsability of Funcom, in the way AO works, in the way AO does not put the roleplay in front of the encounters between players...). Spending hours to overequip to have more power (!!!! here I do not say that overequiping is dumb, I intent to say that I do not like that part of the game. Don't fire me for that please, and stay constructive, as I try to be)...

I think neutral have at least a propension to think. I'm sure a lot of neutrals are dumb too, but the % seems inferior, to me.
I like difficulty and I'm not disapointed with the absence of advantages like token or not PvPing first. What I want to say here, is that something is wrong in the conception of the neutrality for the Funcom company. They say "neutral is hard".
I'm sure they think neutral are not so important in the storyline. They must also think "neutrals are not played by many players". It's true we are not numerous (something like 4-6 % of the total community ?). But why is it so inconsequent. Did you ever saw a neutral country in a war being without any advantage ?

I do not mean, for the game, advantage in hit points, rewards... but advantage in communication, in trade (that requires max. communication to be effective), in roleplay, in possibilities to move around, in spying, in shops (more, costs inferior...), in events...
I don't know, I'm sure there are a lot of ways of giving different advantages to neutrals. These advantages should not give neutrals more physical power, but more depht and implication in the world of Rubi-Ka.

The way to do it has to be slow, otherwise I already hear omni and clanners saying "What !! you focuse on 3 masochists players in place of fixing bugs. I'm a level 254 and I only do 4567 damage every second. Why don't you fix important things first. I will cancel my acount right now".

The way to do it, I think, according to the story and to initial plans of Funcom, is to keep neutrals a minority. With something more and a lot less than omni and clan. For me, improving the roleplaying in neutrals is the best advantage of all. And there are a lot of ways to improve roleplay.

A part of the problem is that we are few. We cannot have a lot of events from neutral players. To have an exciting game, we need more than clanners and omni the implication of Funcom.

I like AO. As I play far less than the majority of players, I have time, to rise in level slowly. To discover new zones. I'm not so impatient (sometimes when I look at the posts around here I think that I play a diiferent game !).

I realise that I know much less the mechanics, the world of Rubi-Ka and so on that a lot of other players. But I wanted to give my opinion, to see if it is shared. Hopping to participate to improving AO, to have more fun, for everybody.

But one day wil the status of neutral change ? Or are we going to stay the way we are, Funcom considering we are crazy ones to stay neutral above level 10 ? That we have to choose between clan and omni, as they have so much advantages (in many ways)....
