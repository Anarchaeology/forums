Ergo Ring, Part 3 Walkthrough - Scheol->Adonis
I'll skip all the yadda yadda and explain the numbers on the map:

Use this map:

Map


1) Ergo.

2) Shadow Spawn, drops item needed. It's not far west from the mountain base heading up to necropolis.
The other mobs down here also has a chance to drop. Shadovy Bravado, Demonic Apparation, Demonic Mirrage and Crooked Casuist.

Itemname is: Antigravitational Metal Sphere

3) Tower, to get there, goto the northern entrance of the spider circle, marked as 5, go down into the mountain and keep left, you'll see a tower not far down, go on the backside of it, find a yellow light, right click it, you'll get ported up to the top of the tower, each corner will spawn a named mob, you'll get 2 unique items up here.

Itemnames are: Navi's Multi-Gyro Component and Ravin's Multi-Gyro Component

4) The book, 1 hour spawn, Undead Engineer. Basically head into either of the temples, go through the first room, then to the end of the 2nd. Buff up, head into the 3rd room, loads of spirits, get ready to run or nuke if you are NT, run straight north, to the north east part, along the eastern wall you'll find a passage which leads into a room north of the one you are in. Head to northern wall, head in the door and down the hallway til you end up in a small room, this is the spawn room. Camp him get book.

Itemname is: Widget to Gadget

5) Blossom Yutto, stand next to the northern entrance of spider circle, look down on the northern side of the entrance, you'll see a portal, jump down, go into portal, head up the rocky passage and jump over to green island, mr. Yutto is over by trees, hand him the 4 items, get another item.

Now either head to Ergo and give him the part from Yutto to get Mark of Lost Souls, then goto temple at marked as 6, kill spirits, get 6 soul globes, or roll soul globes as mission rewards. Now head back to Ergo, tell him you are ready for challenge, you'll get a mission.

Bring some friends, we did it with 3, just copy keys to your team mates and they will enter same dungeon as you, find a deamon, give him 6 soul globes, he'll spawn a HUGE deamon, kill deamon, HARD to solo, he'll drop 3 spikes, you only need 1, give rest to friends, then head back to ergo, give him spike + mark + ring, get ring +3.
