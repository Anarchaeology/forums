Look at it this way...
Here is the way I look at the issues of MMORPG's monthly payments and expansion costs.

I am a gamer...always have been, always will be. Before MMORPG's, I was buying games left and right. I usually bought 2-3 games a month (I gave myself a $100 game allowance per month). Ever since I fired up AC for the first time, the number of single player games I purchase has dropped dramatically!

I have purchased maybe 4 games so far this year. So, I do the math:

4 games @ $40 per game = $160
8 months AO subscription @ $12.95 = $103.60

Total 8 month cost for me to game = $263.30

If I was spending $100 per month before, I would have spet $800 over the same 8 month period.

So, AO is saving me $536.70 for an 8 month period or $805 for a year. Now, how the heck can I comlain about that!
