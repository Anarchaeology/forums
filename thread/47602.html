A Use for Adventuring Skill
Well, as wel all know, you need adventuring to wear backpacks, and all backpacks are the same size on the inside, and what im suggesting needs that changed.

I think, if the backpacks were changed in size, you could use the adventuring skill, to open the backpacks. If you wanna use the nice large backpacks, you would need 200 adventuring or however much, this suggestion is useless if the sizes arent changed. This is pretty much a nerf, but that skill needs some love.

From the tale of the magical pony:

"[Novice Rebooter throws a small grey pack on his back and loads his pistol. The Daft Rebooter fumbles around to load his pistol as well. His eyes move to the grey pack on Novice's back.]

Daft Rebooter: I have a pack?

Novice Rebooter: No, these are kinda tricky to use, I'm pretty sure you can't wear one like this.

Daft Rebooter: Don't just put it on?

Novice Rebooter: No, its pretty complicated, trust me.

Daft Rebooter: Let me try.

Novice Rebooter: You can't do it, just trust me. More skilled men than you have struggled for hours to put this on.

Daft Rebooter: Just two straps.

Novice Rebooter: ITS NOT JUST TWO STRAPS! Its a complicated piece of technology, I have had to spend considerable time training to wear this! I will not argue with you further.

Daft Rebooter: [grumbling] Just a bag.

Novice Rebooter: [regaining his calm] I am your superior. As a daft, you must listen to me and have some trust in my command. You cannot use this bag. Its hard. You will trust me or I will shoot you. "

My point: Using adventuring skill to be able to open a bag, would be plain stupid.
