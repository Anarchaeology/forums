Level 64 Looking For Guild/Hunting Partner
For starters:
1) Rubi-Ka 1
2) Adventurer
3) Neutral
4) Level 64
5) Casual Player
6) United States Resident
(Don't want conflicts with the above to waste people's time by having them read this post just to find out that we are on different dimensions or sides of the globe or whatnot.)

Okay, let's see. I've been getting a tad bored with the game now that time has been wearing on...and I'm now interested, especially with team missions out and looking to be improved, in finding a hunting partner, or even a guild. I'm not sure how role-playish it is for my character to be joining a guild...but I would really, really like to team with some people; the solo game is becoming a boring chore as opposed to an entertaining game.

So, I'm going to "list" things here which I think are important for anyone to know that may possibly be interested either in hunting with me regularly or recruiting me. If, after reading what I have to say about myself, you are still interested in filling either of these roles, or you would simply like some additional information, then please post a response here, or you can send me a pm.


All right. I have two characters that I am absolutely positive that I will keep till I stop playing this game.

-They are both adventurers; one is a non-rider executioner melee advent on level 64, and the other is a dual-pistoleer on level 10. (I would much prefer to play with the higher level advent, but I am willing to play with one or the other, or both [both is better than the other].)

-They are both neutral and on dimension 1. For those of you who care why I am neutral, it is not because I believe that "fighting is evil" or any other such thing. It is more because I enjoy that most neutrals are not powergamers and because I think that both of the factions we have to choose from are...undesirable to join. I don't trust the Clans and their fanaticism, and I plain just don't like Omni-tek. That's the best answer I have.

-My connection is DSL, and my computer is decent. I do experience lag in Newland City, but it is not terrible and I do not usually have problems with link death.

-I live in the United States, in New Mexico, which is, for your information, indeed a state; it is located between Arizona and Texas. Trust me, check any map. This means that I live in the Mountain Time Zone.

-I play casually. I usually play for 1-2 hours Monday-Thursday, 3-5 hours Friday-Saturday. I am usually on anywhere from 3 o' clock in the afternoon to 10 o' clock at night. My schedule is by no means like clockwork, and there are stretches of time where I may play double as much as I normally play or periods where I am unable to play at all due to real-life events and/or general busy-ness.

-I have been playing since the first week of September. This should give you an idea of the rate at which I level. I know how to play this game, but I admittedly still post threads in Beginner's Corner and ask questions that others who have been playing far less know the answers to. I have never teamed; I have gotten to where I am by soloing only. This is where I admit that I have absolutely no experience with teaming. That means that whoever wants to team with me will either have to learn with me or teach me. I'm not an idiot, and I am willing to learn and to learn quickly; I do, however, not have experience. I do know common terms like "puller," "/assist," etc., and I do have experience with games: I have been playing computer games pretty much since I was capable of operating computers; I just have limited experience with teaming and no experience with teaming in AO.

-In relation to the above statement, I'd like you all to know that I am not anti-social, nor do I have any mental illness of which I am aware (I'm trying to be funny, but I do realize that some of you may have read the above statement concerning my lack of teaming and took me to be in some way "messed up.") I am actually pretty easy to get along with and I warm up nicely to people once I get comfortable with them; it is only that I take longer to warm up than most people do. I am basically just introverted with a touch of shyness.

-I am not a power gamer, nor am I a twink or an over-equipper. Admittedly, I am also not a strict roleplayer, although I am by no means a L33t D00d. I speak in normal English, except for in battle when I may use shorthand. I do not have qualms about speaking of real-life matters in-game, though I do usually inform the person that I am going OOC, and I do not talk OOC in public without an indication at every line unless I am not speaking in the general vicinity (ie not using tells, etc.) as I do not wish to ruin other's gaming experience.

-I can type. I'm not a veritable whirlwind at the keyboard, but I am certainly not using solely my index fingers or looking at my hands.

Okay, now some more things about my character.

-My higher level character owns a Yalm. Actually, two, both of which I found in missions.

-I usually take on missions of 50% difficulty.

-I use all of my nano-lines, including the one requiring psych-modi (for those who may not know, advents usually don't train in the psych-modi line as the buffs aren't all that useful to advents and psych-modi is good for nothing else), as my weapons require them.


And those are the only important things that I can think of at the moment. (Of course, knowing myself, I probably left something glaringly obvious out of this post.) Please respond if you have any questions or are interested. I am loyal, dependent, and responsible; I have been in a guild before and am aware of the importance of coming to meetings and such. I have e-mail. I will give the address to you if you are seriously, seriously interested, though I will not post it here (if need be, I will give it to your character personally or by some other semi-private means).

Thank you for your time.
