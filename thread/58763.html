I'd like to see a new map upgrade...
After playing with several low level alts, I would like to see a new map upgrade.

I would like to see a Map Upgrade: Team. All players/pets in your team would show up on the map. This should be a startup upgrade, available at level 1.

I realize that your team shows up with the map upgrade: people.

However, this doesn't help newbies of the game out, nor does it help lowbie alts. Without implants, you can't get that upgrade until at least level 15. You shouldn't have to wait until level 15 to be able to find your group. Group missions are difficult when your team keeps getting split up due to lag and other factors, and normal hunting isn't much better.
