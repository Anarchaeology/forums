Well, you forgot one BIG bottleneck in gaming performance in an MMORPG. Hard disk speed. I run 2 IBM GXP ATA 100 drives in Raid 0 to achieve maximum performance while the textures are being read from the drive. If you have a drive read status light, you will notice that in large crowds, your machine is accessing the hard drive to load textures to memory.

Hard disks are VERY slow compared to memory access speeds. If I were you, I would get about 1 gig of RAM, run at least 2 drives in Raid 0 so that you can nearly double drive performance. It makes a big difference. These days, IDE arrays are becoming fairly common among power users. Most of the coontrollers support raid 0 (striped), 1(mirrored), and 0+1 (mirrored stripe set).

The other thing you can do if you want another performance boost, is to get a platypus qikdrive or cenatek rocketdrive. They are essentially huge, persistent RAM disks that will not lose their data after reboots. If you put your AO install on one of these puppies, and eliminate the page file, you will see a significant performance boost.

The bad news about this is that if you want a cutting edge, maximum performance gaming machine, you are going to pony up some serious cash.

By the way, it's too bad you upgraded to a TI4600. They are old news. Radeon 9700s should be in stores this weekend, and MSRP is 399. TI4600 retail boards are still 340 to 380 in many places, and a Radeon 9700 obliterates it in performance if you have an AGP 8x board (they should be available this week also).

You better believe I'm going to be upgrading my system. Right now I'm running:

Athlon AXP 1800+
Gigabyte GA-7DXR+
(2) IBM GXP ATA 100 60 gig drives in raid 0
1 gig micron pc2100
Radeon 8500 retail (128 meg DDR).
Sblive
Leadman LP-6100E 500 watt PSU

(the rest is insignificant)

I run AO with texture res set to medium and smoothvision (anti aliasing at 2x) at 1024x768x16/32 mode. I dont have lag issues, but crowds definitely cut down the frame rate. Even in heavy crowds, my system still plays AO VERY well.

One other thing you might want to check is if you are doing FSAA at something taxing like 4x or more. FSAA is a frame rate murderer, so think about how much you really care about not seeing jaggies on edges and such and how much you care about an acceptable frame rate. A Radeon 9700, I'm sure could easily do FSAA and laugh it off in AO. I can't wait to get one. A ti4600 is a waste of money now.
