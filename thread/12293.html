random ideas.
I'm just going to post some random suggestions for funcom that I think would be cool.

*Make it so that two teams can share their chat channel with eachother so that more than 6 people can be in a logical group, or something like that.

*Make a Away From Team system like shopbot where whenever people are not in a team and want to be, they are posted in an organized way so that people making a group could find people easily. It would get streight to the point because I have a hard time finding a group.

*It would be cool if there were outdoor mob missions, where a person had to kill certain mobs in a certain area outside and the compass would have to help the entire team.

*upgrade the map system. Make it so that people can zoom out. It would also be cool if you could identify the difficulty of mobs and distinguish teams in the map.

*What would prevent cheating, is if people could identify the equipment of other players. If you saw a lvl 10 person with a lvl 80 hammer, you would probably not pvp a person. This would be nice. Mabye make a command where people can show their AC, Life, QL of weapon and weapon name, and buffs that they are wearing, and ql of highest implant. People are probably drooling at this, or something, because it would make pvp more fun.

*let it be configurable what a person has to target. I hate targeting things that I have no intention of fighting. TABing neutrals sucks. Let me select what I will target.

*Cars suck. It seems they are their posted speed. Thier are boosted graphs that people can easily get at lvl 4 that increases speed and swim by 190, and doesn't require IPs usually.

I think that's enough by now. It didn't take too much effort to think of these, just sat down and decided to write. It's so easy to come up with these ideas, and they would help alot. why can't funcom do this? I would be willing to be that only one programmer would be working on AO right now because FC is sucking right now.
