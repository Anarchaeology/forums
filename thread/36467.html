Biggest Problems in AO and my suggested solutions.
Well, I have been in the world of Rubi-Ka shortly after it launched in late may last year. my combined played time in AO from all my chars is well over 100 days. That doesnt even include the time I spent in forums and researching on the net. Yep, I am a hopless addict. But telling you this is not to show off how hopelessly addicted I am, but hopefully I can establish some credits here in what I am about to suggest.

I'd seen way too many people come and go. So what are the biggest problems in AO that make people leave in the first place? I will try to suggest solutions to each problem I post and link up polls to the different solutions so you can vote on it. Is it something you absolutely disagree? Vote against it! Is it something you think AO must have? Then vote for it!

If theres enough interest in this artical and the polls, I will try my best to keep this updated to the response I get in this thred and the polls. Its going to be lengthy, but if you are like me that still has faith in AO, please read it through and join in the discussion!

Game Client Performance -
The lag, omigod the lag. The game world has great graphics and effects and all that, but whats the use when all you can do is admire the flashes of light dance before your eyes and not be able to do a darn thing? Memory Leak(HD grinding), long zoning, server/client crashes, already login bug, stucked at "Loading", black doors in mission, invisible teamates/mobs, etc. Too many of
my friends have quit due to game client performances with the major culprit been lag.
I will not be able to provide any suggestion to this as its way too technical and out of my field. Only thing I can think of is... get more and better servers?

Customer support -
This topic is too personal I believe. People who suffered the games glitches and couldnt get satisfactory answers/response will simply say the customer support is utterly incomeptent. And those who are fortunate enough to not suffered the wrath of the game's bugs will find customer support up to their "expectations". So I will leave this to you to decide if its the biggest problem in AO. Vote Here.

Story Content -
When is the last time you feel been part of the story? Or simply do you see any "story" in the game at all? The story "potential" of Rubi-Ka was what attracted me to this world. But where the hell did it go? Do they seriously expect the players community to make up their own without any support?

I personnaly think this is the biggest problem. God knows how many players out there, like me, have endured the lag, lost of items through bugs/crashes, nerfs, unwanted changes to game
"rules", etc. But my faith in the game is wearing thin. I sincerely do not care how wonderful the graphic or new stuff will be in the expansion. What I want is to feel been a part of the game and
play a role in the "history-making". I have long lost motivations in leveling and accumalating "rare-items".

I strongly suggest FC to seriously consider hiring a team to create stories that players can be a part of IN-GAME and permamnently help/support/monitor long/continous running RP events that will involve large numbers of players. I am sorry if they already have such a team... I don't see any work of this genre been done at all.
To vote for your satisfactory on AO's story content, Click Here.

I believe the RP/Event Team should be more directly involve with the players community. And implement rewards/recognitions to those who make an effort to encourage more players nvolvement.


Reward/Punishment system -
Simply put, the only things-to-do in the game now is level and level. If you are in a big high level guild then possibly you can get your hand on some of the rare drops. PvP rewards you meaningless Titles that is so hard to achieve/maintain. I hope FC will come up with a better reward/punishment system.

For example,
1. Killing NPC/PCs from the same alignment will get you banish/blacklist from your government, get lesser rewards from missions, get attacked by guards, unable to use the transportation system(whompaas), pay more in shops, suffers penalties in PvP due to your lost of "status" in your side of the conflict.

2. If a level 75 will never be able to kill a level 200, then why should a level 200 be allowed to kill a level 75 or even get pvp title points at all? In my opinion level restrictions should still apply but upward restricted. For example a level 75 can pvp with anyone from level 60-90, but can still initiate attack on a level 200 but not vice versa. Just like a neutral Vs clan/omni. A Level 200 should be able to atack anyone 170+ but can only return attack if a lower than level 170 attack him first.

3. PvP Rewards, is titles enough? I suggest activating Specialized Pinky Cutter and make xp finger with names
of the victim, a sick memoribila(how u spell it? ) concept but i believe it will be welcome.
And also rewarding XP/Creds acording to victims PvP points. The more he kills from your side of the conflict, the more xp/creds you get, in accordance to your level versus his. Where a neutral should get more rewards since they can only return fire upon attacked.

4. Rare drops, reduce camping/farming by introducing different spawn/drop systems. 18 hours boss should randomly spawned at different locations and time.

5. In-Game social standing, there should be an in-game mechanism to record/show individual/guild status in all sides of conflicts. I suggest a reputation system that not only shows
your social standing in rubi-ka but also directly affects an individual/guild in the game world.

E.G. A clan guild that has high numbers of confirmed omni kills, should get discount at clan shops, periodical "tax-deduction" from their government in the form of added creds to the guild's bank.

And how about a terminal that shows your "dedication" to your government too? Sorted by confirmed kills, missions accomplished, total tokens(even beyond 1000) accumalated, richest players etc. It should have atleast a top 50 list to ensure more players will be "involved". No point if its only top 10 and all months long you see only the few elites. This I believe will also
improve the content aspect of the game. Making players feel been part of the story by making their presence felt among their peers.

If AO has a better reward/punishment system, I believe it will make the game more appealing and interesting. Vote here for these suggestions on reward/penalty system.


In conclusion, I think the biggest problem in AO is the inability to keep peoples interest. Most people I know still visit Rubi-Ka only because the friends they met in this world still hangs around. Almost everyone of them told me the same thing, theres nothing to do in AO anymore. I don't think AO needs new items, nor new graphics. It Needs New Content! And it needs to Involve Players in the content. To let players feel their presence felt in the community.

So, whats your take in this? Please feel free to vote in the polls I made in this post. And suggest any new polls you feel related to this topic. Thanks for reading!
