There may be more than one mini-Galway. I didn't find one on a hill, but I *did* find one in the middle of a swamp... hmmm.

People know about this, but there is an Omni outpost in the southern part of Avalon. It's within a giant crater and covered with a transparent dome. Outside you'll find mini-domes and ninja/slayerdroids on patrol. Inside, you'll find a set of basic shops, two sets of advanced shops... and a juggernaut. Oh, and this is one of the only places I've ever seen trooper NPCs in battlesuits... they're known as 'Shock Troopers.'

Andromeda has tons of cool surprises. Zone into it ASAP from Clon****'s eastern side and go north and slightly east towards the Stret and you'll find the fabled ICC headquarters. Not very interesting, and hard to find on foot or w/o a map, but fun. Due west and somewhat south, aaaaaall the way across the zone, is Terra, another OT city. Boring place, but large and very pretty. Up in the north and center is a small OT village with advanced vendors. Due north of there is a zone to 4 Holes... and, if you go just across the river, you'll find a great little hunting spot for a ~30-35 soloist. Easy mobs, and it's fun, quiet, peaceful, meditative hunting alongside a river. Just don't go across the eastern creek or northwards.

In the OT outpost in Broken Shores, in the northwesternmost building, you'll find a small dungeon of sorts with one large room and a closet. Inside the closet is a VERY aggressive Hardened Criminal who will probably hit you with a sizable dot. Don't go in there unless you're 60+... or don't open the closet.

In Borealis, near the fixer shop, there's an outhouse. Serious. A bathroom with several urinals and stalls.

Got a yalm? That outpost you see across the river as you zone into Lush Fields from Trade is the OT outpost to Pleasant Meadows due south of Mutant Domain. I always thought it was there for show, but you can actually choose to go to Harry's *or* this outpost, relatively quickly.

Speaking of boats, if you fly north of 4 Holes to Stret West Bank, you'll find a little neutral dock with a boat to - of all places - Stret East Bank. I presume there's a sister dock in SEB. Good way to cross the river if you're a young neutral who can't use the grid (if, for some reason, you WANT to go to Stret East). West of the docks is a VERY cool bridge leading to Holes In The Wall.

Hey, did somebody say Stret East? Fly east of 2HO until you come to the cliffs with all the dungeon caves in them. Now fly south. Eventually you'll come to a little town around a bay and an interestingly-named peninsula...

We all know about the City of Home, right? No longer such a cool little secret place like it was way back when, is it? Well, there's still something fun to be had. Start running right out of the west gates of Home and just keep going 'til the dirt road curves northwards. Assuming you don't get eaten by vultures or sandworms, you'll eventually come across two pillars in the desert, covered in skeletons. They look kinda ceremonial, but their purpose is inscrutable... and if you go north of there, you'll find those cool canyons.

There are several Clan outposts in Wailing Wastes that are just sitting there. Just FYI.

You can get on top of the buildings and towers in Omni Entertainment, and probably in most other cities as well. Fun place to hang out, and great to freak people out if you jump off and fall into the BB crowd. Banzai!

Tons of things to see in Southern Fouls Hills... make sure you bring a plane or are pretty fast, though...

Check out some of the stops along the irrigation canal in Galway County. You can even head downriver, for fun!

From the mass drivers in Clon****, go south. You'll eventually find an OT outpost and a little town. Quite a walk though.

There's a little watershed just south of the West Athen/Old Athen bridge in Athen Shire. Before I got my plane, I would always jump off the bridge and swim to the watershed and heal up from the shots the Athen guards took at me.

In one of the towns in 4 Holes (not the ones with the grid or whompa), there's a building marked 'Guild Houses.' Inside are tons of boxes, a few symbols, and one sign saying "Omni-Tek Officers Only!" Also in that same general area is a little building marked 'Computers Inc.' which has inside it a secretary's desk and a larger desk with a portrait behind it. It's a nice, large area, perfect for a guild meeting, and very well secluded.

In eastern Newland desert there's a river of toxic waste and a tall mountain range (go north and east of the Rhinoman valley). At one point there is a precarious rope bridge over the sludge river, as well as a couple merchants. In addition, there are tons of clan guards protecting a tunnel through the mountains. Presumably, this eventually leads to the Plains of Salt, which aren't there yet.

Check out the rows of Enigma Trees in CAV. Spoooooooky!
