Leets Carry the Day!
The citizens of Rubi-Ka have voted against leet fighting in today's debate. More specifically they voted against lifting the current ban on arranged leet fights. Though this vote is in no way legally binding, it is a indication of how our citizens feel on this issue. Clearly the passion of the opponents of this sport among the audience was plain to see.

I wish to thank out speakers, Tatjaana and Nanubis from the FriendLeet Foundation, Rorybones from the World Leet Fighting Federation and Father Chagdiel of the Church of Omni-Tek, our sponsors the South Rubi-Ka Society and all those who participated.
