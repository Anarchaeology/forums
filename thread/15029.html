The only reasoning i can think of for this patch is thus: When the game first came out, all those players who were there the first month, they had A LOT of time to get high levels while others were just learning about the game. In the process they acquired wealth. This money, they put towards other lower characters they wanted to start, but they wanted to level them faster or have them stronger sooner than they had first been when they started playing.. IS this a bad thing?

A parody i can think of is purely hypothetical, here goes. Guy moves to the united states, he is poor, he gets a job and is part of the working poor class of society here. He eventually raises a family, he somehow manages to save money and get better off, he learns new skills or whatever. When his kids meet the age at which theyd go to college, he can send them (with the money he saved). Those kids get better jobs, make more money.

This is exactly the same thing. Our first characters in AO are our pioneers or immigrants. Our second characters are their children, and leech off their daddy or mummies(whatever the case may be ) to have a better start in AO.

Is this a bad thing? I think not, the only bad part is new players dont have this option.

Thats TOUGH, people who slaved over those hard levels to be able to better another character they want, have more interest in or whatever should not penalized for duking them out with the most powerful gun or armor they can possibly put in their hands.
