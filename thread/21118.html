Money makin tip for new players.
Ok, so you started the game, and leveld up a bit. Now you need a weapon because the one you started with is total junk, but what to do? You cant do missions because you need a new gun, and you need a new gun so you can obliterate everything in your path.


Well, its simple really. Remember all those monster parts you scored as loot in the backyard you started in? Well, if you need some cold hard cash, go to the backyard, and kill leets, reets, and anything else that moves for a little while. Collect those monster parts. At this point youre probably thinking that you only get 2-5 credits for them, why work so hard for so little? Well, if you can find someone kind enough to convert them to blood plasma for you, you can sell it to the booths/vendors for about 80-100 credits for each monster part that was converted.

This should earn you a couple thousand credits, and really help you get started. Once you have that shiny new gun, you can do missions for gear, and money.

hopefully this should you all very much.

The value of the blood plasma you get is dependant on the QL of the mob parts you start with -- so if you kill tougher mobs, you'll get better parts, and you'll make more cash. You need increasingly higher QLs of Bio-Communitors to process the higher-QL mob parts, so your QL47 communitor will serve you for quite some time. Unless the Pharma skill is green or light blue to you, though, you'll find it to be a too much of of an IP sink eventually.


Once you have the gear to do a mission, don't forget to do long-range missions. Past level 6, you can pull missions from mission terms (in town, not in the backyard) for other areas (so from Tir you might get missions to Newland City). The cash reward (do all-cash missions for this) is tied to the distance between the mission terminal and the location of the mission itself, regardless of whompas or Grid access. So if you're omni, going to Rome and getting a mission in Trade will give you 4-5x the cash of getting the same mission from a mission term in Trade. If you're Clan, getting Borealis missions in Tir (or vice versa) pays really well, too.

just to add to the discussion on Pharmatech and plasma. This is somethin a doc pointed out to me. If you can buff yourself with any and all pharmatech buffs you have BEFORE making your plasma. Just collect a bunch and then do 1 big mob parts----->plasma conversion. The reason for this is because with higher level pharma tech you can actually RAISE the QL of plasma you make. So far i have tested this with some of my characters and here were the results.

LvL 50 Agent Makin Plasma with a lil more than 200 skill after buffing. I was able to turn QL 44-50 into something like QL 54-65

LvL 20 Crat with around 100 Pharmatech skill. I was able to turn monster parts into EQUAL quality plasma.

The point of this is that its not necesarily a waste to put points into pharmatech if you got the IP to spare (once you hit title cap) because in the long run you can make much more cash by upgrading monster parts that you found into plasma.

*** Generic Newbie Money Tip***

A great way to make decent money starting at level 2 if you are Omni is to go to Omni Trade, take the Whoompa to Broken Shores and look for missions back in Omni Trade. They pay a couple thousand each(unlike the few hundred the local missions pay) and it adds up quickly. You will be able to afford a new weapon, armour, etc in no time.

Same deal for Clanners if I remember right, take the whoompa from Athens to Broken Shores and grab Athens missions...it pays quite well at low levels.
