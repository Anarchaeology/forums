Actually the nanocrystal tradeskill process is somewhat split on who's best suited. A Trader can process the ore and create a pure carbon crystal. But nanocrystal creation requires heavy nanoprogramming, which traders have at a higher IP cost (and lower cap, I believe) than other professions.

As for the other cases you mentioned (dragon-armor, Sols, Ithacas), I've been trying to point out these aren't particularly useful tradeskill items. Dragon armor is barely worth a comment. You can't base an entire ability's value on a single finished product that 75% of the people in game will never see.

The sealed weapon recepticles are a nice try and do provide some value to weapon smithing related skills. But the demand for those tradeskills will always be dependant on
  1. The supply of the sealed weapons recepticles
  2. The current 'Weapon of the Month' demand.

Weapon and armor will always be dependant on the people that have the skillset to use those weapons/armor. Which automatically means a limited demand. More general use items would be better to add to the tradeskills in AO for them to have value. Nanorechargers, NCU Items, Rings, Bracer, ect. Things that everyone uses and tends to upgrade regularly as they climb in levels.
