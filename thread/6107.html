While you're waiting for that 24 hour reswitch to pop up, I recommend getting missions from the Broken Shores area, using the Whompa in Trade to get there. That should get you missions anywhwer from Galway to Stret West, depending on your level. That should pay relatively well.

Whether making the switch from Clan to OT is a mistake may very well be how many tokens you lost in the process. If you haven't already noticed, your token count gets reset to zero each time you switch sides.

Between the tokens, and how well the Clans pay me for missions, I found little reason myself for applying for a position with OT. What I've seen of OT equipment doesn't look all that good, if you aren't an Atrox, Enforcer, or Soldier.
