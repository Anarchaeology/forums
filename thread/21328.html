Neutral issue, plz all read
Hello all

The 14.2 patch will have an effect that few have thought about but will shake the foundation for the neutral community (a very close knit community, more so than most people realize). When a person starts off as a neutral they have a very hard and difficult road to travel. It is a road that is chosen mostly by those that put thier moral code before them and are willing to go through hell to get what comes easily to clan and omni. When you are neutral everything attackes you on site and you are always looking over your shoulder (this applies mostly to lvls lower than 100, which are the hardest for most neutrals). Heres a small example of what it was like in my early days just to illustrate:
Borg hunting in mort:
-find team
-scout for clan npcs in area while your team is getting ready to hunt. Find the wandering clan npcs and lead them away in plane(takes a while, your also getting shot and no heals at this point, team is getting xp and you are not). This has to be done or else you will get shot in back while your team is consintrating on borgs. The team also refuses to help clear them out, which they could do easily.
-go back to team( which is miffed cause your not pulling your wieght) and procede to hunt for 30 min.
-npcs wander back, repeat from step 2

What the AO community doesnt realize as a whole is that the neutral community is built on respect, and that respect is earned not given when a person signs on the dotted line. When you give an established agent the right to switch to neutral, you slap the face of all neutrals who have gone through hell and back to get what little xp they could. It undermines the very foundation of respect that the neutral community is built on. If you dont believe me how hard it is look at stats. How many neutrals are above 100? a handful? above 150? 7 or 8? So much respect exists in fact that i can go to a neutral i dont know and place the life of my chaacter in his hands and know he would go to the ends of the planet to protect it, as i would do for him. This patch will allow an agent to lvl up omni or clan and then switch to neutral on a whim when he is a high lvl and is on the easy road. I am a well established neutral (lvl 173), my concern is for my fellow neutral agents who have worked VERY hard to get where they are. This patch will only serve to lessen thier acomplishments in peoples eyes and that should never happen and never be allowed. Well thats all i have to say. I hope you can look at it from a neutrals point of view. High lvl neutral agents sudden materializing would not go unnoticed my the community and i doubt they would find any respect from my fellow neutrals, i certainly would give none.

I have nothing against the premise (in fact i think its pretty cool)but i assumed if something like this was put in the game it would be something simple like a nano formula and timed, just enough to fool npcs and people around you. As it stands now from what i can tell its a semi permanent deal where your actual affiliation can change for as long as you want. This really is unfair too neutral agents, its been a much harder road for them and the only payoff has been the respect of the people around them. I hope you can all understand this and pass it along to people who can make a difference, it really is important that this be known. Bump too plz =p if nothing else.
Thanks
