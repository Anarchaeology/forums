Content proposal from an oldtimer
Hi all!

AO needs something to provide constant action for all players, other than running missions or camping for items. So I have this proposal!

Add NPC's to some of the 25% areas, and later on the 0% ones. These NPC's should be set up in camps like an army in the field. Guarding eachother against the enemy nearby. Players can choose to defend their area, or attack into enemy territory at the disadvantage of facing both NPC's and players!

Now, to promote players into taking this terrible risk of beeing killed in pvp you place unique no drop omni-only items on the clan npc's, and unique clan-only items on the omni npc's. The power of these items should be staggered so they become more useful and interesting for the high level players the deeper into the enemy camp you can penetrate. A few of the items could even be placed on "boss" mobs in the camps, while most of them would be randomly distributed among the npc's inside an outer line of defence (so players dont get anything for picking on single npc's but always have to fight to get to the reward).

These areas would also need to be fairly close to a grid or whompa, but not very close!

This idea has some benefits, you dont have to make new areas or dungeons to implement it, only need to place npc's in existing areas, and you need to make a few items worth fighting for.
