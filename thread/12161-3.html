I was thinking of trying to tie in a political democrat/republican quasi example at first, but since republicans and democrats dont usually shoot at each other (on a regular basis), I couldn't quite pull it off

The way I see AO, In a sense you dont claim an affiliation, it makes a claim on you. Once a side has made a claim on you, the only way to get rid of that is if the other side makes a claim on you. Neutral, isn't a side, so it can't make a claim once you've left it. Gives me headaches :P

I think it's about time we left folks go neutral. I've never once believed the argument Funcom makes that says something like "once you've been in a faction the opposite faction will condisder you an enemy".

Unless you are Radiman or Ross, chances are most folks on the opposing side don't know who the heck you are or that you haven't always been neutral. If you are well known as a former Omni or former Clanner then folks that feel strongly will still come after you, whatever faction your in. Why have the game mechanics force it on them?

It really doesn't make much sense really in an RPing sense. I seriously doubt the reason for not allowing folks to go neutral is anything other than RP sense their is no gameplay reason to dissalow it.

If going neutral should not be a simple thing then implement one of the options that have been suggested before. Let the person do a hard quest to earn that application. Make a person lose a few levels (and therefore status) for going neutral. Make the application really expensive.
