Battle Suit value is illogical!
Battle suits are the equivalent of 7 pieces of armor, yet most styles are now approximately equal in value to 1 piece of body armor of the same QL. Certain tank armors are as valuable if not more than some of the battle suits as well. Sure battle suits were worth too much, but this is rediculous. This change is only reinforcing the imbalance in the economy, as the people who took advantage of this before the change have a distinct advantage over all those who did not. Please correct this imbalance by increasing the value of battle suits to bring their value closer to that of a full suit of armor of equal protection & QL.
My 2 cents.
