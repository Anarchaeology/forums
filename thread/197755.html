I am using the 2nd deck and Entropy's Advance now mostly in missions and especially on raids.

First, I can't self PNH woithout the deck and I can not swap the rifle that easy. Our guild has some tower problems and I am not thinking about swapping it out soon.

Second, I am more effective in teams because I do OK damage, do great mezzes, and do great overall team support with Grav. Anomally and blinds.

Third, If I wore a gun (nanomage) and used IEF, I would be more of a hinderance to the team as the tank couldn't keep aggro from me. And with rifle, when I get constant aggro that doc can't heal, what do I do? I still have to either waste time on calms, layers, blinds etc...

Fourth, I am on raids often, so my nukes rarely counter and I almost laugh at NTs throwing RB and CC knowing that 30%+ of my 70% NR nuke is resisted by Fred. I wonder if they land any. At 182 (my level) I could only use ol' faithful or FIM.

Fifth, I often have to watch for ads and not look towards the mob I am nuking. Rifle makes me turn, and thus again hidering my effectiveness.

With MANY perk points spend on regeneration, I can now pretty much sustain long nuking without recharge.

It's not all about damage sometimes. It's about working with your team. So since you are an atrox and if you can tank easily, I would strongly suggest you not not even try using the deck until much later.
