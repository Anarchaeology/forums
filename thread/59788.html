Interesting question.
Well to answer your question directly. It would take less than 20 milli-seconds to purge names of characters on accounts not active for say 6 months and could be done requiring no downtime. However, we have discussed this to a great extent and have opted not to purge these names as in so doing we would have to purge these characters. Now everyone knows the problems that AO experienced with it's launch and certain patches who's name shall not be uttered... Long story short, we currently maintain all accounts and characters indefinitly so as to allow individuals to return to Rubi-ka and pick up where they left off. (*Disclaimer - Funcom also reserves the right to delete all account and character data after a three month window, at our discression*) Now some say, well what about just deleting the level 1 characters and freeing their names. Sorry no can do as even those characters may have been used as mules for additional storage and should be available if one were to reactivate their account.

On a more posative note, we are and have been deleting characters and freeing names from abandoned trial accounts after a specific period of time. Characters on these accounts are maintained for a duration to allow us to retroactivly search for potential exploits vs losing the data when a trial account is inactivated.

Hope this helps explain our stance on the freeing of character names.

As for the previous posters statement that the database is holding names of deleted characters, well yes and no. There are two seperate systems in place, one holds character data and a deleted character is indeed deleted and the name is freed. However, the chat system will continue to reference a characters name to the orriginal account until that name is reused by another account. At which time, if you query the chat client the deleted character will no longer show as being associated with your account.

One last statement, as for renaming a character on an inactive account. We've opted not to do so as a returning player would want to reclaim their orriginal identity but would be unable to do so if someone else had their handle. All of this may change, but for now we opt to preserve characters and their names on inactive accounts in stasis awaiting reactivation.
