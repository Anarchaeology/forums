A couple of my worst experiances...
Scenario #1: Thou Shalt Not Kill Your Friends... Even If They *ARE* Stupid.

A couple months ago, I talked some good r/l friends into starting up accounts on AO (they were disillusioned EQ players).

Imagine my suprise when, a couple weeks later, they're almost at my own level. I find out that they've exclusively done missions together (as Martial Artist and Adventurer), taking on red missions... so I chalk it up to good grouping abilities learned in EQ, and grab my Fixer alt that is at their level, to form a threesome.

Can't remember where we were, but we were wandering about, hunting. We're taking on red Mobs fairly well... and then I'm noticing that we're consistantly drawing serious adds from all over the place. What's worse is that I'm continually cycling through my teammate's healthbars (via f1-f2-f3)... and their hitpoints are rapidly going down... down... down... and nobody's healing. A Martial Artist, and an Adventurer... and nobody's healing. This is happening almost every encounter.

...which leaves me desperately trying to keep them alive with my miniscule little HoTs. And my First Aid kits.

And then I get aggro. And, of course, my First Aid skill is locked. And of course, I die.

Friends: "Where'd you go?"
Me, grumbling darkly: "I'm going. To smoke. Alot. And maybe get very, very drunk."
Friends: "We're sorry!"
Me, sighing: "It's cool. Say... did you guys know about cycling through your teammate's healthbars via f1, etc? Or using the Team Window to monitor who needs heals? That might help us stay alive longer..."
Friends: "Uh, ok!"

Upon my return, nothing had changed. 4 deaths later, I called it a night. :/

#2: Beam Me Up, Scotty.. This is Taking too Long.

I'm with a good teammate I've met online - good guy... even forgives me when I screw up. We get lucky enough to grab another good teammate who happens to log on just as we're preapring to grab a mission. We manage to get a fourth, because Teammate #3 knows him, and he's looking for a group our level.

I grab a mission in Stret West.... to be exact, the north-west corner of the Stret West zone. "It's going to be a bit of a run," I say... and they're all ok with that. So we set out.

5 minutes later, Teammate #4 is harassing me that "We're not in Stret West! We're in Central Artery!" <I'm using the Omni GUI... and I'm the guy with the mission. Additionally, I've been here several times.... I think I know where I'm going.>.

5 minutes after that... we get jumped by a green mob, but since we're on something of a r/l time schedule, I call out that we'll ignore it and head straight for the mission - we've all got enough runspeed to have no issues.

A few beats later, I hear Teammate #4's death rattle. 'WTF??' is going through my head... The remaining three of us turn and kill the mob. Which is when I realize that it never hit him.... he'd self-terminated.

Utterly flabbergasted, I /tell him: "Why'd you terminate?"

"It was taking too long!" was his reply. :/

#3: "Errrr... He hit me really hard, and I flew out the door??"

<chuckles> Actually, that last one was my own fault. A 6-man team of us is trying out a 'Borg site near Greater Tir. It's my first group (I mainly solo), and I'm really nervous.. I don't want to do something inappropriate, and get people killed.

So, I hang back and let the pros do the work that they do well, adding my damage in support as best I can. Then we come to a dangerous room with 4 red Cyborgs. "We need to pull them one at a time!", the leader says.

I realize... I'm an Agent... that means... Oh. They're Gonna Want Me To Pull. <gulp> I'm *really* nervous now, but when the inevitable request comes, I'm ready to step up... praying that I don't screw this up.

...And wouldn't ya know? I manage a spectacular... no, a *stellar* pull. 4 'borgs, all clustered together... and I draw one. I get compliments. Boy, I'm proud of myself....

...Which leads to my mistake. We've got three to go, and I go down the hall to attempt to pull one more again. This pull doesn't go quite as good - I get 2 out of 3, luckily - but we've got a good support crew, and we're handling it pretty good. I'm adding my damage in support, as usual.

...and the Engineer's Bot steps right in front of me. For a split second, I don't know what's happening, and when I suddenly am taking damage because I've acquired aggro, I do the natural thing... I attempt to step back just slightly, so I have an idea of what's going on.

What I don't realize, of course, is that I'm standing right in front of the mission entrance. I step back... and zone. Right into the waiting arms of all those 'Borgs we'd killed on the way in... respawned, and happy to see me.

NT: "Hey... where'd you go??"
Me: "Ah, sorry... I bit it."
Doc: "You died??!? What?? I'm sorry!!"
Me: "No.... No... not your fault... I accidentally stepped out..."
Team: <laughs>
Soldier: "It must have hit you so hard, you flew out the door!"
