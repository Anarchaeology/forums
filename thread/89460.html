consider....
Self-buff: buffing within your capability, and only within your capability, with no outside help.

I think everyone would agree with that statement.

For every profession but trader and FP trader, this is a no brainer. But, to pre-drain, an outside source is required (leets, reets, other backyard animals). Since an OUTSIDE SOURCE IS required, by definition, pre-draining is not self-buffing.

Pre-casting HOT, essence, blah, blah is not the same as pre-draining, as it only requires your character.
