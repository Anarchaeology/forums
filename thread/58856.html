combat vehicle idea
a poster in my thread about adventurer pets got me thinking....this idea will work with both animal pets as well as combat vehicles.

to make vehicles worthy, which is one of the main hopes of the booster pack, they must be quite 'different' than being a soldier standing on the field (infantry vs vehicle). would it be cool if vehicles have a check for attack?

1. infantry attacking vehicle gets 10-25% of regular dmg.
2. vehicle attacking infantry gets 10-25% bonus dmg.
3. vehicle vs vehicle gets no bonus or penalties.

would make sense as infantry would be severely limited in attack/speed/power compared to a combat vehicle.

i just really hope they don't just put more vehicles with an attack in and let the booster out as that...that would really suck.
