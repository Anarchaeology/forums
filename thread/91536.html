I have a cople of tips for you folks with "lag"

DEFRAGMENT your HDD.
consider getting a second drive if you dont have one already, and try to put AO on the D Drive, along with the windows swap file..
Yeah, AO and the swap on D.

make the swap size a "fixed" size, for example 500mb, by setting the min/max size to 500mb.

this will help a LOT, I have just spend a good cople of hours defragging and moving stuff around, and it helps

and I have "only" a amd tbird 1.4ghz, 512mb ram and a Gforce 4 TI card..
but if I put AO and the swapfile on C, things lag like crazy.....

so folks, defrag and move

remember, you can have the fastest PC on earth, but with slow HDD's it'll still lag like crazy..
