Less thongs, less trenchcoats, less boots
I posted to the other clothing thread, but I'll post here so it gets bumped.

Okay, what if they just had 50 outfits in 10 different colors? It's the exact same outfit, but in a different color filter. That's an easy way to add SOME variety. Instead of a grey catsuit, you'd have a blue, green, red, white, etc. We have thousands of different types of weapons and armor, what about a few dozen different types of clothing? It's really a bonus for everyone, in the long run.

More uniforms would be nice. Uniforms for soldiers only, including medals on the shirt would be great. Grass skirts, wooden shoes, thigh-high boots, sarongs, flip-flops, sandals, togas, feather boas, pajamas (med cloaks look close! :P), t-shirts and jeans, socks and sneakers, jackets (NOT TRENCHCOATS FOR THE LOVE OF ANYTHING SACRED), professional and executive clothing, knee-length skirts.. male clothing should come in something other than black, too. Not everyone wants to be a lame Matrix-wanna-be or some lingerie-obsessed whatever. Some people just want to have a professional, unique character.

Come on, if the SIMs can have 30 zillion websites for skins, can't AO add a few more clothing stores? (unique, hard-to-find, lets have some variety in cities!)
