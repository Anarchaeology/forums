LLTS Changes - Another Nail In The PvP Coffin?
Yes, there was a simple solution to the LLTS issue. Change every LLTS that is currently in circulation, so that it has a reduced crit bonus, but instead, FC have decided to make a unique that will define and destroy PvP as we know it today.

At the moment, PvP between newer and older members of the community is more or less balanced, even though the implant issue was tackled stealthily, it's beginning to circulate.

But the new uniqueness of the old LLTS's is going to be a game killer. If you were fortunate to get a QL195 LLTS before they were removed (again, steathily may I add), then you'll have a foot up on the members of the community that don't have them.

The question is; and I hope FC will answer this, but what exactly is going to happen with the new LLTS's?

Taking a bit off the crit, and knocking out the lights on them is one thing, but you need to at least add some value to them, so that they will balance out their usage against the old versions. Just by nerfing the new ones, and leaving the old ones in game, you'll break PvP for good.

One idea could be to increase the range modifier on the new LLTS's, which would at least give them some value against the decreased crit mod.
