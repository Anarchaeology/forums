Next Meta-Con Announced! 2002/01/05 @ 18:00 GMT

Topics so far:

The MP's Guide to Surviving 75
- moderated by HonorGuard
What's left to do after you've explored all of Rubi-Ka? We'd like to
know! Our most ancient and respected brothers and sisters are invited
to come speak at this open discussion.

Mystify Me: The Third Pet Explored
How effective are they in PvM? In PvP? How do you script for them?
How many different species are there? Get the answers straight from
the experts! Tactical demonstrations will also be performed, local
wildlife permitting. Please note that, at the request of Sister
Tatjaana of the Friendleet Foundation, the targetting of leets will
not be allowed.

Closing Ceremonies: Gifting of The Lesser and The Ghost Dance
Following any scheduled topics open discussions, all brethren are
invited to stick around the bank terminal for our traditional Gifting
of The Lesser (read: swap meet). Or just hang around the rock for our
like-wise traditional Ghost Dance summoning ceremony, wherein we
summon pets and drink ourselves unconscious (read: crash the server).


Hope to see you there!


Location
As usual, the convention will be held in Newland at The Rock In The
Desert, just east of the grid exit.


Information
If you would like to host a Meta-Con, or if you have a topic of
interest to the MP community on RK1, please feel free to send your
suggestion to aohoc-owner@yahoogroups.com.
