Guild Question (Please reply)
Hi everyone, I don't post on here very often since i'm normally playing, so I don't really know if anyone has thought of this before, but here's what's kept my little mind entertained for the past 10-20 minutes... Would it be possible to put in a way for guilds with sub-guilds to chat with each other ? Example: If a guild called Freedom has a sub-guild, something like Freedom-Med or Freedom-Security, the leader of Freedom still is the leader of the sub-guilds. At the moment however the leader of the main guild Freedom has no way to chat with either of the sub-guilds without talking to the leader of the sub-guild and having them relay a message to that specific group. What I suggest is that guild leaders, in this case the leader of Freedom, have the option to select different guilds to be included in the guild chat, the leader of the sub-guild is sent a request after the leader has done this and if the leader of the sub-guld agrees then it becomes a standard chat channel to all members in guild Freedom, and all members in the sub-guild now have guild chat Freedom aswell. If the sub leader says no, then it's no go and nothing changes. Is this a good idea or not ? I feel that it would make things much bigger by letting different sub-guilds communicate with the main guild so all people within the guild could talk with each other as well as letting all it's sub-guilds talk with each other and the main administration to get items, help, friends, tips and anything else from each other. I'm probably just crazy but I think it's a good idea. Secondly, I think that guild banks would be a good improvement too, just something that comes with a guild house though. I'm sure guild houses are probably way off in devlopment somewhere so if it's possible, why not put in banks into the guild houses? Perhaps the more money the house is the bigger the bank ? Something like that. Anyways, post what you think about it and any improvments you can see or any thing that you think would be cool. -- Quicken --
