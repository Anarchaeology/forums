Ok, you'll obviously need to up those nano skills. Really all of them will be used at some time between calms, layered, HE, blinds, roots, and your DD nanos. Focus on MC with implants though, that's what 99% of you DD nanos will use.

I don't know your breed, but always up Body dev. I recommend Stamina as well. Int, and Psy for equipping armor. And of course nano pool as this is your main source of ammo.

Nano int. is very important. The faster you nuke, the more damage you do. Run speed is nice to up a little (i have 300 at level 128 with implants, not fast, but i can keep up with a group.)
Evades are a point of debate. I have very low evades, but I don't PvP much. In PvM I don't die often, with calms and nullity (hope you can find it) I can almost always avoid death.

Trade skills the only one you need is Comp lit. Nano prg can be nice, but since you'll probably be using implants well above your level, you'll need outside help anyways after a while. It's not a very good source of income anyway (unless you get it high enough to do lvl 200 implants.)

As for weapons. I would recomment either pistols or Shotties. It's up to you. at higher levels pistol raids have great crits and fast attack. For shotties i don't think you can top a good ithaca.

I hope this helps some, and remember there's no great way to go about it, have fun. The IPR will be implemented soon allowing you more flexibility to experiment.
