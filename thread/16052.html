Those are good sentiments but wouldn't work. That's how Ultima Online was originally supposed to work, players were supposed to police themselves, build their own natural community where laws and enforcement would just evolve. It never worked out because gankers seem to far outnumber those who want to clean up the place. I had friends early on in UO had founded anti-PK guilds but they eventually got tired of it and gave up (on the effort and the game itself). The anti-PKs were usually motivated to do their jobs because they wanted to enjoy the game without having to deal with every town being put under siege by a tight ring of PKs; they had lots of other things they wanted to do in the game. The gankers by contrast almost always played the game solely to gank newbs. So, fighting them becomes a full time effort in order to be effective. Once this sank in, people gave up, because now you're playing the PK's game and not your own.

This stuff works in real life because death is final and thus a more effective sanction. Since there really is no dreadful consequences in MMORPGs, there is no deterrence.

Note: I am not arguing against PvP here, but I am arguing that player-made sanctions just don't work. Killing your own faction side is out of the bounds of legitimate PvP, and something that can't be tolerated at all. Funcom will have to do something about it, we can't.
