This question was actually answered by one of the developers a while back:


"Briefly about Tarasque loot:
The EoT is in the Rare drop slot, and is there with 5 other items. So if he drops a rare piece of loot, there is a 1 in 6 chance that the EoT will be chosen. The other items in the same drop slot are: Aura Magnifier, Hollow Bone Bracer of Merlin Ambrosius, Robust Backpack, Sinew of Tarasque, and the Smelly Butcher Gloves."
