profession houses?
I know there's been a lot of talk about org houses. I'd like to throw out this idea. How about profession based houses in each major city? Only that specific profession can enter and they could have a lot of different uses.

Inside would be special mission terminals for profession specific missions. If you're a doc, you get missions to heal someone or make a medicine or the like. An agent would get infiltration or assassination missions, a trader would be sent on a difficult mission to trade goods, etc. I'm sure there could be several ideas for every profession. These could be more like static mission rather than the run of the mill generated missions we have now.

Also, if machines are ever installed for players to sell items to other players, you could have machines in these profession houses where you could sell your profession specific items to others. Outgrown that SMG? Maybe the next fixer to visit the "Fixer house" will buy it..


Inside should have nice places to sit around and relax and a big area for large meetings.

I'm sure there could be a lot of ideas that these could be used for.. what do you think?
