...bump?

I'm kind of losing my fire. It seems like that for us on the advent board as well. We're stopping our suggestions (as we really can't think of any more that are realistic) and now we're starting to go at each other's throats, or just re-hasing old news for the sake of posting something while we wait and see what happens, or sharing ways that we have gotten around the problems and continued to play the game. Given, that is a general statement, but our board is not what it was two months ago, or longer. I think it results from a combination of having nothing else to say about the whole matter that hasn't been repeated several hundred times, the filtering in of new weapons, some advent-only, and the comment from a high-level Funcom official that we are going to be fixed eventually. (Hopefully before the game has been around a year, which will only be in a three months, I believe. I wouldn't know exactly, because I started in the first week of September.)

Just thought I'd bump this thread; I saw that it disappeared and it wouldn't hurt for it to surface again.

Oh yeah, and here are two ideas that haven't been tossed around a lot by advents that were thought up by other people:

1) Dual-wield specials. There has got to be something that a person can do with two swords or two guns that he/she can't do with just one. I don't want to make dual-wielding uber; I do expect that dual-wielding is more effective than single-wielding a weapon that can be dual-wielded. It does, after all, require ip. It would be nice to have specials added, even if just for the sake of style. I wouldn't want to see everybody and his brother running around with two *insert weapons here*'s like they currently are with shotguns. (I like your crack about "Shotgun Online", Lacaan. ) (This is Lacaan's idea [the dual-wield specials], as I first saw it.)

2) Weapon specials and such that are profession-only. Like anybody can use a Nova Flow, but a soldier should be able to do something with it that makes it rock even more. Sort of a balancing equivalent to profession-specific nanos (not everybody can nuke, but any nuker can use a shotgun [though to a less capable degree, I admit, than a trader]) that would make fighter-classes more interesting (they seem to be thought of as newbie classes sometimes and I think that that is wrong) and that wouldn't take away weapons from support classes that want to use something powerful. They should be able to, but fighter-classes should have more of an advantage with those powerful weapons than cheaper skills and higher attack rating. (I hear a lot of people saying that ip becomes more abundant in the high levels and the cheaper cost doesn't really matter, but I don't know personally as I am a mid-level character and always in an ip crunch.) Of course, an implementation such as this would probably require much balancing, but hey, it would be a welcome addition and perhaps would negate the need for new weapons (which adventurers need for their green skills). (This idea was mentioned by somebody before, but I don't remember who. I'm not taking credit for this idea.) {Edit: This idea is definitely not advent-only. The first one is geared more towards advents, who have their cheapest weapon skills in the two most dual-wieldable weapons classes, but it isn't advent-exclusive either.}

So, there you go. Kind of a long bump, I admit, but hey, this thread hasn't been visited in almost a month, so...
