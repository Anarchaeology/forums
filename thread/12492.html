Zeroshift, it drains your inner energy to do the attacks. The ones that involve basically changing life force and matter take more inner energy than a jab or jump kick. Everything else can be done over and over again with a set source of nano bots that can be reintroduced to the system; life energy can't be. The MA description outlines this concept.

I think FC's MA system is poorly done, but your criticism lacks focus and solution. I'd rather see special "style" attacks that you can string together that take the place of the normal sock to the face unarmed attack - with small recharge times, so you could side, roundhouse, jab, trip, in the time it the stead of 4 punches, with their own bonuses and damage (side is a rapid kick that does normal damage, roundhouse takes longer than a normal attack,jab is 2 fast attacks but has the chance to miss and does less damage, trip does very little damage but stunds for a few seconds and locks the style attacks for longer). The other special MA attacks would operate in the manner they do now.

If they had their own recharge time, you would launch a DoT, DD, AE, and health drain in rapid order... the trick is using hte right attack in the right situations. The problem is that the special attacks lock out the basic attacks, which my outline above fixes, or a simpler outline; have 2 tiers of MA attacks that lock independently; Physical and Spiritual.
