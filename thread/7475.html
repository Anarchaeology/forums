yes this is an important thing about RPgame
A real roleplaying game (IRL like ADD, Vampire, Shadowrun...) has no end. Never. Only missions, or temporary goal.
RPgame (MMORPGS) on computers inherit that. I understand your point of view, and I gess you never played to a RP game IRL ?
A few questions are always the same when someone asks the mechanics of a RPgame (IRL) especialy: what is the goal, what do you try to do...
Answers: no goal, just fun, you try to have fun.
On a computer RP is more accessible, to every kind of human being (with a computer and, for a MMORPG, with an internet connection). You do not have to gather 4-8 people together for a long time in the same room. And beside, computers games tend (and are in my opinion) to be less intellectual, more automatic, with less human contact (of course !).
A goal is leveling or having more power (armor, guns, credits). But it is only this way because RP is poor. If RP was really implemented, you will have fun only with chating with others, exploring, discovering, listenning to a great story, really interacting with it..., your level is of no importance ! the challenge will get to your level, smoothly.
I'm almost sure RP games IRL will stay the best way the do great RP, with friends, and live great moments (shared) more stronger than any computer game. But we'll see, with time maybe...
