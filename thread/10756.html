Useful list of 'crat issues. Look Dev team!
Ok, let me start out by saying I LOVE the crat class, this isn't a whine post ;D. I just leveled my new 'crat to 80 and think I've learned enough to post a useful list of issues the crat class has. So here they are, in no particular order.

1) Demotivational speeches and calms : Calming is one of the most useful abilites a crat has, as a crowd control charachter. I spend a large portion of my time in fights calming at many spawns. The problem is, demotivational speeches and AoE roots knock the calm off monsters. So Im running around calming adds, and my Demotivational speech hits and POW, they're all uncalmed. Not fun, for this reason I dont even bother to use the Demo speeches. AoE roots/slows do the same thing btw. Not fun either.

2) Charm durations : I *really* wish they'd display time duration on your charm left in the "/pet report" command. I've actually taken to setting my stopwatch to beep 20 seconds before my charm wears off, so I dont have my former pet all of the sudden kicking the **** out of my pet or my party without me noticing it. Other than that, the durations are perfect ;D

3) Charm Psychic Checks : I understand why they implemented these, something like this was completely neccessary to prevent crats from charming ridiculously high level monsters and running around smashing everything up with them. The only problem is the dropoff of charm usefulness is too severe.

Heres a scenario : Lets say at the level 125 title level cap on 5 per skills, I twink into the absolute best charm I can and have a great time hunting oranges. I gain 15 more levels, my skills dont go up, we move on to harder monsters. My charms no longer do anything at all. I delete them off my macro bar.

The thing is, all classes encounter this cap at 125. All classes stop getting more offensively powerful for the most part for the next 25 levels. The problem is, other classes are still useful. My NT has been using the same nuke since 125. It may drop off in effectiveness severely against higher and higher level mobs, and maybe even become nearly useless against special super tough mobs with monster nanoresist, but it will never really be totally useless and I will always be somewhat effective with it. Not so for a crat, their charms go from being fantastic, to useless instantly against tougher monsters. I just wish there was some kind of decreasing effectiveness on the charms instead of a complete dropoff of use. Say, if the resist factor were to go up exponentially the farther the target was above the psychic check number, to the point where something significantly above it was immune to it.. or something, anyway.

4) Snares : This is more of a general issue, snares vs roots. The problem is, both are breakable. It is unlikely that anything you snare OR root will remain snared or rooted for very long, or if you're planning to flee and leave it or so, its unlikely it will take you more than a minute or so to get away, so a 10 minute snare is overkill in the utmost.

My NT COULD use better calms/roots than he does, but I usually don't bother. Why? Because when hunting nothing stays untouched for more than 60 seconds, so there is no reason to have a calm or root that lasts longer than that. Why waste the extra nano? If snares were roots too and lasted 10 minutes, would I use em? No.. who needs a 10 minute root?

So basically, nobody uses snares.. ever. Why make it move slow when you can stop it? The only way I can think of to get any use out of snares but not overbalance them is, give them the same duration (maybe even half) as roots, and make them unbreakable! Or maybe just leave them alone at risk of messing something else up..

5) Fear Nanos : I haven't figured out what these are good for, much less why they implemented psychic checks on them. They're basically a calm that makes the monster run away for awhile. Sometimes they even come back after the time is up and beat you up again. The point is, who WANTS the monster to run away! Its much more convenient to leave them standing there stupified while you finish off their companions, then move on to them. Who wants to deal with running after the thing to find it, or having your pets go whacko running off into the distance chasing it. Maybe there is something I'm missing about these, but I doubt it. I managed to get a friend killed hunting today when I chose to Fear a monster instead of calming it. It came running right back about 40 seconds later and finished him off before I noticed it.

Anyway, I guess my only real greivance about them is that they would have such a high resist factor, such a shord duration and have psychic checks yet at the same time have less utility than calms, which are harder to resist, last much longer, and have no psychic checks. I think some crats would be happy if you got rid of the psychic checks and made em a lil harder to resist. I think many more enforcers would be happy. Maybe the psychic checks were put in to stop some powerleveling or bug exploit, I dont know, maybe not. I can't imagine how they could have been abused though.

6) Uhh.. uhm, I can't think of any more! I love 'crats ;D
