Re: hms..
Houses -- Being held up because FunCom is planning to introduce housing for rent/purchase in the summer. This is why they won't remove the 10 item limit on "new arrival" apartments - they want to give incentive to buy a new one (as if no one would buy a new one anyway)

missions -- Well, we got team missions, and people still whined. Go figure. They aren't perfect and need some tweaking, and FC really needs to look at their AI pathfinding again.

organizations -- For being the sole largest group of dedicated and active gamers, organizations seem to get shat upon a lot. We have poor government choices, no guild housing (should be about as difficult as spawning a permanent mission), no events support, etc etc.

yalms and other vehicles -- Yalms are disgrace enough. While other vehicles would be fun, from FC's POV, they simply aren't anywhere near the top of the to-do list. I would say to look for them on an expansion pack/content addition bonus super thingamajig CD.

addons for gameplay -- When FunCom changed around their DEV and directors a lot a few months back, they switched to a much more strict patch tree that probably has logic to someone there. I presume that it involves stability and balance fixing before things like capture and hold territory (read: mass PvP) come in.

story-based content -- FunCom does not have an events team. I guarantee this. They have some part time writers, and people who double up positions when needed, and ARK. Hell, all of the statements by Radiman etc come from community@funcom.com. Once again, I suspect that the recent halt in all activities has a lot to do with getting 14.2 out.

subquests -- ARK is restructuring how they do events, and preparing for handing out quests and being active daily ingame. I do not know this for sure, but that is what everything points to.

clothes, faces -- Um, AO already has the largest assortment of both. More are always welcome, but again, bottom of the list.

brooms -- Um, ok...

emotions -- AO's emotes are motion captured from people (makes you think about /moon, /scratch, etc). Adding more would be a big step... content CD. Not like just coding it... they would have to hire models again, probably rent a studio or pay someone, get them recorded, etc etc.
