*Poof* Done. Anything else I can do for you?
OK, I'm exaggerating; all of your wishes haven't come true. But most of 'em.

Tattoo parlors: I agree there should be, and there aren't. On the other hand, tattoos are pretty commonly given out as mission rewards; they aren't that hard to find. Just time consuming. Hmm. Come to think of it, a mission is about the same amount of time as a tat takes IRL.

Cool clothing: Miir isn't everything (in particular, about half of their women's wear is just plain ugly) - but it's everywhere. By the time you get your character level up to 30 or so, it's even affordable. There's plenty of cool clothes to party in, and not all of them are hard to find.

Club events every week: Baboons, in Omni Entertainment, has something going on most weekends. And Reet's Retreat in Last Ditch, Stret West Bank, has good-sized crowds in it almost every Friday and Saturday night, even if there aren't announced scheduled events.

Change the music: I know of about five DJs who use Live365 to mix music specifically for club events. One of those, Reet Radio, is on continuously. When you walk into a dance club on Rubi-Ka, /shout What audio stream is the music on tonight? Odds are better'n 50/50 that somebody will actually be streaming music. And as dated as it is to be running 20th/21st century music in a 294th century dance club, it really is more fun when everybody's listening to the same songs.
