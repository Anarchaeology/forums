Level 1-100

Keepers have no Init. Buffs, how do I raise my Melee Init.?

It is possible to get some very good init. on your keeper, while it might not be quite as easy as stoping by the genral store and picking up a nice +100 melee init buff here and a +240 there, it can be done though the use of equipment.


-Intelligent Titan Plasteel Shoulderpad
These are some pretty nifty little items. These boost your Melee init. From about 4-21 depending on Quality Level. While most keepers will probably opt for their Quest pads, these could quickly become a keepers best fried considering one of the best keeper weapons, the Frost-bound Reaper, has a -1110 init. Debuff, and keepers have no self init. Buffs. These are boss drop only, but their not to expensive, they go for about 3k per quality level.
-Journeyman Durable Boost
These are another great way to get your melee init. Up, they boost from about 13 to 15 per booster, you can only wear up to two. While this might seem like a very small boost, when stacked with all the other items they help allot. These are probably the most expensive item on the list, they dropp off professor van horn, one of the most camped mobs in the game. If you want to take a shot at camping some you can find a good amount of information on him here, at Anarchy Arcanum. Other wise your looking at about 10 to 15 mill per boost.
Guardian Conductor of Speed
This does require a guild with towers to use, but, the good news is it has a very nice melee init. Self buff on even the lowest tower. The owner of the tower will get a +100 melee boos regardless of quality level. So, if your guild has some towers just ask whoever is in charge of your towers and I�m sure they would be able to walk you though it. The tower shop for Omni-Tek players is located around Rome Blue Basic in .. Rome Blue! The clan tower shop is in Old Athen near the whompas to Tir/VW/Bliss. The Neutral tower shop is in Borealis, somewhat near the Jobe whompa. Thanks to Aameul for clan and neuter tower shop info.

These are another great way to boost your melee init, at quality level 200 they boost your init. By 15 each (+30 total). This item gives quite a large boost, it actually gives more then Heavy Notum Tank, +50 to be exact. This item also has a nice HP boost on it, very helpful. I'm not 100% sure on the drop area, if any one would like to post more info that would be great.
