Then there was the big event, the Yalmaha Valley race in the Dead Maze, Broken Shores. The idea of this was for everyone to race down the valley from the same start point, and meet at the exit point given by co-ordinates without the need of flying out of the valley. The amusing thing was, the race was possible, and was tested multiple times and it takes about 8-10minutes to do. Unfortunately it seems the navigation training in Rubi-kan schools these days isnt what it once was, and instead of heading North (as a rough direction considering they are inside a maze), it seems all the entrants went South. After 30 minutes search partys were sent out and the contestants were located at the far south edge of the maze ((I had to admit I was creased up laughing by this point)). They attempted to find the way north but it seems they failed, and so a compromise of who was the first to the place where they all meet on the south edge of the maze was madeso in such case, the results are:
