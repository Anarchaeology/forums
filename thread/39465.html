We need a /busy
I think a /Busy command would be a great idea. When you don't want to be bothered you have little choice here. /afk doesn't stop the tells from rolling it and sometimes add some nice spam to your screen. How many people want to actually ignore everyone on there list JUST to have some time to be to themselves?

If I miss tells on the rare occasion that I'm hunting. People think I'm rude. If I'm in a meeting and get 343423423 tells while I'm afk for it. People think I'm rude.

A /busy command that blocks any incoming tells to you at all would make so many peoples life easier here and cut down huge amounts of stress. (Sadly, those one of a kind MT's might be avoided as well.- oh well. Take the good with the bad )
