I don't think the game should impose these rules on players. For one, it would be a logisical nightmare to re-code. If you're going to duel someone in the arena to test your metal, simply tell them you won't fight them until they're self buffed. Don't trust them? Simple, you have two choices: 1) don't fight them, or 2) ask then to save and term.

Now, if someone asked me to term to fight them, I'd laugh in their face as would many. So basically, it's unlikely you'll ever get the luxury of a sure fight unless you know your potential opponent.

What I'd LOVE to see is an arena set up for PvP from the ground up. Meaning:

1) No hidden areas where people can exploit. For example, in Tir there's a place inside the arena where a player can stand and literally be invisible. You cannot tab them, and even if you could you'd have no LoS. On the other hand, they can see you in plain LoS and attack all day long.

2) A debuff machine, kinda like an airport scanner to get into the pre-fight waiting area.

3) Nowhere to run once the fight starts.

4) A way to register fights, and even wager on them. This would also allow true tournament ranking, which would be SUPER cool. I'd probably never leave the place other than to level and restock.

Of course this is a pipedream.

My personal opinion regarding other's buffs is... anything goes. I'm willing to fight someone with all sorts of buffs on. The agent with the healing pet, however really bothers me. That's BS. I'm sorry, but an agent shouldn't get the knowledge of the class they're impersonating. Even as it is, an agent shouldnt be casting a meta pet. They can't do engineer or crat pets, why metas? I know agents are reading this and going ballistic, but come on!
