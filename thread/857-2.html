Jug baby
I've killed some nasty mobs, level 150+, but I gotta say.. the Goliath Class Juggernaut is probably the nastiest I've tried my hand at. I clocked the beast at around 5000 energy AC, I'm sure it has at least as much in the other mains. Thats the same as a QL 200 battle suit guys, the highest possible AC any player in the game can have w/o AC buffs or implant/equip buffs.

1500 damage hit to this sucker barely moved his lifebar. He would resist my roots at LEAST 30% of the time even with 600 nano skills, making the root and shoot trick basically impossible against him. He seems to do about 800-1500 damage a hit (discounting armor). Its hard to tell whats a crit and whats not from him though. I'd estimate his min damage is around 700-800, even if you had 6000 or so melee AC. He hits fast as hell.

Its hard to tell what else comes close. I know a fossegrim doesn't, his crit damage is almost less than a jugs min damage. A Doombringer might, LBer variants hit fast as hell since they seem to have multiple weapons (at least 2, maybe 3). Anchorites are pretty nasty. Some of the higher level cyborgs are bad too now that they have all seem to have guns with nasty ranges.

Kinda makes me glad I'm Omni Tek :P
