Suggestion: Static Missions(quests) with an AO twist
There should be seprate mission terminals for Static Missions, one for team, one for individual, and one for "epic" Static Missions(raids, extensive camping, etc.....).

When one of these terminals is inspected or it is used for the first time, it should have a disclaimer. It should read: "DISCLAIMER: Somebody else may have the same mission as you. They may be doing the mission at the same time you are. By using this terminal, you are agreeing that the ICC((funcom)) holds no responsibility for the conflicts that may occur as a result of this."

Here is an example of a individual/group static mission: "Hello, I am Omni-Pol mission 0279-C. As you know, leets have a uncontrollably high population; they have no natural enemies and an extremely high metabolism. I want you to kill 20 of these creatures. Omni-Tek is your friend!"

This would work like an assasination mission, each time you kill a leet that would get counted toward your quota. When the quota is reached, you or your team would recieve the reward. The reward for static missions should be a special item. In this example an appropiate reward would be a special leveling "Omni-Tek hunting rifle".
