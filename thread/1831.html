I was unable to find a posted sticky thread in the official sections so I am posting here.

quote:
Gaute Godager  Exploits, Nerfs, Banning and Overequipping - A foray into the difficult.

Summary
To sum up but a few of the many inputs I would like to get:
1. Should we do a pre-emptive strike on the exploiter community?
2. How hard and in what ways do YOU think we should crack down on people who exploits?
3. How should we handle over-equipping?
Dupers that do not report dupes should be automatically banned on their first offense. If they have other accounts on the credit card those accounts are also banned. It is assumed that all characters on that credit card shared in the duping of the original dupers account and are in violation.

If you have warned people about doing something there is no second chance, they are banned on the second offense. If the offense is totally unrelated to the first offense it should be given consideration based on the game style and current AO community. If everyone is doing it then the problem is with the software, not the players. Otherwise ban them permanently.

Over Equipping is part of the game and even promoted by implants and nanos. Over-equipping needs to be handled with patches and refinement of the AO software product. I suggest a system like Dark Ages of Camelot. If you have not played it you should do so, it is an excellent product. Items above the characters level perform poorly. The higher the item is above the level of the character the less benefit it yields to the character. Not only do the items operate poorly because of the lack of level but they also wear out faster or break often. This allows players to mildly over equip which would allow the AO developers to better balance for overall game play. With huge differences in players abilities at this time the game is impossible to properly balance in PvP or for PvM. AO needs to define how much over-equipping is reasonable and then make sure there is a max that is unable to be crossed.

And banning is for life, the account and all its data is removed from the servers and stored elsewhere for testing and or legal reasons.

Cordially,
NanoFixer

The base problem is that the current AO rules only check skills when equipping. Besides not making any sense, this leads to the current situation where some people have insanely high equipment because someone else cast a short-duration buff on them. On the issue of equipment
How about a procedure like:

Everytime you

a) zone
b) log in
c) die and get resurrected

all your primary equipment (the cloth and weapons sheets) get checked for skill requirements.

If they are not met. the piece of equipment is FULLY deactivated and visibly marked. (I'd prefer to have the piece unequippped, but I assume that it would be a problem with the inventory, just as we had it with the vehicles)

General remarks

This solution would still allow you to get VERY well equipped for a short period of time, to prepare for a certain event (battle against a hard monster or readying yourself for the final assault of the other faction).

It will also remain consistent with the world concept, as the intelligent use of nanotechnolgy will permit you to achive feats beyond your normal capabilities.

But, depending on the time the skill check is done, it will LIMIT the benefit of EXTERNAL buffing.

All those classes with the capability of self buffing in certain areas will retain these advantages.

Classes, who are now providing external buffing will still be sought after for certain occasions


Specific remarks on TIMING OPTIONS:

a) zone

This would result in the most short-lived effect of overequipping.

Overequipping will most likely be limited to adventuring while in your group or on event occasion like raids of certain locations, when both side take care of their own first to achive a maximum advantage.

Getting buffed and then moving out alone would practically be impossible.

But it would also hassle all the classes with self-buffing capabilities very much. (I intentionally left the option of continous checking for skills out, as this would just be a plain pain and would require excessive NCU capacities)

b) log in

While this option would make buffing and equipping longer lasting, it presents problems and disadvantages for two groups of people with causes beyond their control:

*) Occasional players, who only log in for a short time
*) Players with an instable internet connection

Also, in a game sense, it is not justifyable just like option a, as both zones and logging are artificial components not "native" to Rubi-Ka.

c) die and get resurrected

Which brings me to the last option, which in my opinion would be the most beautiful one.

1) It would be completely consistent with the game world.

2) Items not meeting the requirements could be unequipped, as the inventory will be empty.

3) People would have different downtimes depending on how they were equipped, thus balancing the effect of overequipping in the long run.

i) you meet the requirements with your body stats and implants :
only 30 seconds downtime until your remains are reconstructed

ii) you need self buffs to equip certain parts:
As long as it takes you to be able to cast your self buffs. Maybe as long as all the resurrection effects wear off.

iii) you need external buffs to equip certain parts:
Well, you need to go and get buffed again by someone else. As long as this might take.

On the topic of skill checks for implants, I am not quite certain. As you might need a surgery clinic (mobile or stationary) to re-equip even the implants you put in with self-buffs, I think, it would be too much of a hassle. The game should be fun after all.

As you might have noticed, I am very much in favour of option C.

What is your opinion?
