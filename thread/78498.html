Open letter to the ICC
From the desk of Major McCovery,
Leader of the Desert Winds

ICC

The planet of Rubi-Ka has a young ecology only recently terraformed by Omni-Tek, despite troubles the terraforming has been going apace for 800 years. Now YOU of the ICC have opened up the entire planet for mining with NO THOUGHT for the consequences that excessive mining will have on the DELICATE balance of life here. A GOLDRUSH for a quick credit has blinded you to the facts, Rubi-Ka is not ready for EXCESSIVE public mining. I ask you in the spirit of HUMANITY slow or even stop the mining before it is TOO LATE.

The Desert Winds have established monitoring stations to PROTECT areas of scientific interest and to OBSERVE the process of terrforming. If our data tells us Rubi-Ka is spinning out of control we WILL take steps to protect the DELICATE balance here.

We are brothers and sisters of the land UNITED
ONE
INDIVISIBLE
FREE

Rubi-ka for the Rubi-kans

Major McCovery
Leader of the Desert Winds
