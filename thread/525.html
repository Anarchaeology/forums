Here are some nerfs I would like to see:

1. Remove all creatures from the game. This will keep those darn power-levelers/exploiters at bay! This would also solve the crap HPs of caster classes, no more dying.

2. Remove all weapons/armor from the game. Haha, the joke is on you enforcers and soldiers. Now you cant kill me anymore in PvP. This would remove armor-envy too.

3. Remove all nanos from the game. This will keep them pesky casters from killing stuff once and for all! Crats cant gripe anymore if no more nanos!

4. Nerf the interface by getting rid of all buttons. Well, exploiters cant cheat if they cant control their character now can they?

5. Nerf walking. Players cant get too many levels if they cant move! This will also get rid of the peeps who complain about missions, now they wont have to bother since they cant walk!

6. Nerf sight. Players cant level too much if they cant see their targets! This will solve the blind bug for NTs, everyone will be blind.

7. Buff the chat system. We all know that is all we want in the end right? A game that we can connect to and chat with one another on an equal basis? No one will be too uber or too lame, just you and the ol' text window.
