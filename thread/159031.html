I've been messing up Tab's for........umm, almost 2 years now, and it sucks :/

As a Neutral, you eventually get used to always mouse-targeting; but messing up Tab is a big inconvenience to the Sided players we MUST team with, and the main cause for not being picked up for a mission team many times.

The one redeeming thing is at Ace camp.......the mobs there are Neutral, so Ctrl-Tab works wonders for me there Also, I'm of the thought that being Neutral makes aggro stick to me just a touch better when I'm tanking Uniques, due to the mob's natural aggro tendencies towards me. I think I've got a permanent spot in Ian's hate-list hehe.....right under the heading "R.I.P."

Of COURSE I'm all for this suggestion. There's nothing I'd like better, in fact. But the major balk I see from FC is that it would remove one of the hinderances to being a Neut........and being Neutral is *supposed* to be hard. Very hard, evidently LOL

Let me reiterate........I'd LOVE to see this idea implemented. I'm just giving my speculations as to why it isn't, if it never comes to be :/
