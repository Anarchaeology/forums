Well, my number one choice for a game improvement would be:

1. Get rid of random shop inventory.

Either let us enter the ql we want or put in fixed ql's like the implant shops. Running from store to store to find the right ql cluster, trimmer casing, etc. pisses me offf more than anything else in the game.

Put in all items that are sold in shops in the shops. If I want to buy a ql50 of a specific brand of pistol, armor, trimmer, etc. Let me do it without having to run around for god knows how long.

The rest, in no particular order:

2. Autoattack on/off

3. Integrate aomd into the interface, or let us choose our own rewards and/or locations. Rerolling missions is one of the game's money sinks, so charge people level*500 to customize their mission quickly. That's the same as rerolling fifty missions. Retains the money sink and gets rid of boring downtime.

4. Get rid of all that extra info from F9. Just give us the coordinates. Or make a new command that just gives the coords.

5. A new Social tab in the Wear menu that lets you display social clothes instead of armor. Every slot, including weapons, should have the option of displaying either the armor or social item equipped in one of the two tabs. Very few people switch from armor to social clothes due to the hassle of oe'ing. Even if it's self oe'ing. Item bonuses and protections would only be calculated from the current weap and armor tabs.

6. Let us holster our weapons. Same as in Daoc, just attach them to our hips or back, no need for actual holster graphics.

7. Deliver on the implied promise of those soft porn "I've got implants" ads and give us some booty!
