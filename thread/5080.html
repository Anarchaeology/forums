Fix the 0,0 edge of the world problems
Once again, I found myself at the edge of the world, this time with my regular hunting partner. This used to happen frequently where I'd get warped to 0,0 in Southern Fouls ... but has occasionally happened elsewhere. Tonight it was between um I think Upper Stret and East Stret. Once there, there's nothing to do but petition or terminate. 73 petitions in the queue and well, after almost an hour, not much to do but terminate and lose a bunch of xp from clearing some very tough missions. Get your stuff from reclaim, sit and ponder the state of the world and up pops an ARK who gets defensive and, frankly, rude and you wave your hand and say "shoo, go help someone who is waiting in the queue; thank you for stopping by."

Please fix this. My collection of screenies of me and friends sitting on the edge of the world is getting too big.
