The truth about Nano Shutdown From an MP who uses it.
OK. Everyone is scared of the UBER MP nano shutdown. I have tested nano shutdown in many different situations over the several months. I think perhaps I got this nano sometime back in September, so I've had quite a bit of experience with it. Heres the low down.

Nano Shutdown is resisted in this manner...

It uses the folling as it's attack modifier.

35% T/s
34% BM
31% PM

So if my stats are t/s 900, BM 900, and PM 850. that means the attack rathing of my shutdown is 315+306+264=885.

885 is the attack # for this skill. It's resisted 160% according to Funcom. In real world numbers that means your nano resist is multiplied by a factor of 1.6 to come up with your defense score. If you have a 500 nano resist - (500x1.6=800) compare the two mubers and you would not resist this nano. A slightly higher nano resist and you would in fact resist it, most of the time at least. There seems to still be a small chance of the nano landing and I do not understand AO's combat system well enough to explain it any better than I just did.

Now, as to this being the UBER end all buff. HA. Look at the nano. First off it takes 9.3 seconds to cast. As you know - that is a VERY long time. You can probably kill me long before I get the spell off. Especially if you see me first. If I get jumped - by the time I tab me target and hit shutdown - usually I'm dead before I complete the nano.

Secondly, this nano has a 15M range. Enough said. Use your head and dont close to within 15 M range.

THe only time you have to fear this nano is if an MP surprises you and the first warning of combat is this debuff landing on you. If that happens - RUN. Remember, we dont have a root.

Also , remember pets have 0 nano resisit. Stand 20 Meters from me, root me, root my pets, and then kill me. Simple.

THis nano is only deadly under extreme rare circumstances.

I've surprised a few people with it, but I find that it's not that useful in normal fast paced pvp combat.
