Thanks everyone for the compliments I really appreicate them!

I have made any zone with political areas in them, no matter how small "political". I think that SFH has more than one area that is political, ill have to go back and see if they changed it.

What I am trying to avoid is people who think a zone is safe accidently walking into a gankfest. I don't plan on drawing out the tiny supression areas becuase once i head down that road i have to do them all, and they change too often and are too difficult to map.

Nobody will get mad at me for saying its political when its mostly not, as no deaths arise from that. But people do get mad if i tell them its safe (when its not) and they get ganked.

Basically a political zone means "be on your guard".

[edit]I went back to SFH and the omni base there is also 25%. Since this is the only save in the zone it gets a good amount of traffic. This in addition to smugglers den is what is earning SFH political status. The same way that meetmedeere earns newland dessert political status.[/edit]
