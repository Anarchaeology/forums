2 Things from me


1. Im sorry for putting this in the RP forums, I didnt know where to put it, and it affected alot of people.

2. I won't get bogged down with discusion anymore with "so and so this, so and so that"

Its nice, I have spent the last 9 months dealing with everyone elses problems, as well as my own on a daily basis.

Running a good bot, that is not only fair, but secure, is hard. I tried my best to keep things in line, but everyone is not perfect. Things could of stuck together, if everyone would of been civil about it. All too often people let thier animal instincts get the best of them, so be it, but no longer is it my concern.


Now I don't have to worry about acambot, my guilds politics, and running 32049823 raids every week. Maybe now i will get to sleep more than 5-6 hours a night.
