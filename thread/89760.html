R.U.R Contract for freelance hunt for 'Milly'
Memo: Clearance for the below Authorised (reluctantly)


R.U.R. Has contracts for a Search and capture Job.

Recent developments require freelance operatives for a highly sensitive search and capture job. Target is a P.H.E.W.Y Type 6 model known as Milly, last viewed wearing Womens undergarments.

Risk factor is dubbed minimal to moderate. Skills in careful questioning and surveillance essential.

Crattyjack to detail those interested and skilled for the operation.


Crattyjack eyed the screen, and smiled to himself. Yiis had obviously cleared the idea, and Dabbz (Dabblez) had authorised the motion. He decided to create a table to note the future candidates for this job. Looking at his stained desk covered with various enterprises started, but never finished, he fought to find room to write on some small scrap of paper. His Bot Harry would have gladly cleaned his bedroom, but Cratty liked it just the way it was, cluttered and stinky. He sighed when he realised hed have to make room.

Cratty picked up his half made pottery piece of what looked like the bottom half of a kangaroo, (it was supposed to be the bottom half of his then lady friend Come to think of it, it was quite accurate). He gently placed it by the overflowing bin full of coffee cups and Bronto burger boxes. Next he picked up the pile of paper, and magazines of Time and chucked them on his bed, (which already had piles of paper and his chequered red and blue nightwear with Momies little Tiger written on the top garment). Then finally he moved some dusted electronic boards, which if finished would have been a completed CPU unit for a singing Leet, 10 hours to complete, started 5 Years ago.

He smiled to himself then drew his table, nice neat columns with bold sharp lettering. Placed the scrap of paper, placed it in his trouser pocket, then had a shower, placed his dirty laundry in the washing bin, changed into Momies little Tiger, fell into a deep sleep (on the piles of paper btw).

Later that night Harry picked up his clothing and put them in the washing machine, scrap of paper an all.

To Be Continued (Maybe in a few Years time?)

He woke up and dressed up in the now clean clothing that his Bot had made sure of. He got the memo from Omni Pol and was shocked by the bluntness. But then he had visions rise in his fickle mind... 'The RUR saga..... a tale of betrayal, lust and fervour! A family caught in the struggle of a free enterprise based on igenuity and brevado!' He saw himself beaming a nice side view of his serious yet sexy demanour in the dust ridden mirror.

He lauged at the idea. Omni Pol surely were more concerned with other more dangerous affairs?

Doubt then settled; a slowly as a forgotten dream of a long time ago remembered by a trigger of thought. That being the collapse of yet another started and unfished cherade.

But was it a charade? No cheesecake with Pollum topping was, the kick of the sting and the injecting of an antidote part of the fun. Where would the antidote be now?

No he must find this 'Milly' Bot, must see if such a silly thing was possible. Could this Mr. Hubble be right? could he be wrong? And what of his digestive problems? Without finance, hed surely grace all with his specialised fragrence once more? And be smacked over the head by his old sorority with a kipper?

The Police... The Police had embargoed now. It was no laughing matter. Did they have proof? Did they have 'Milly'? Were they to raid the offices? the Companies that manufactured the Quantum Positronic Thingy TM? He shook his head and wondered how... How would he sate Dabblez now?
