OT needs the Clans
The very presence of the Clans on Rubi-Ka gives Omni-Tek carte blanche in so many activities. The fact that Omni-Tek is fighting a war with rebellious citizens - citizens who continue to revolt despite efforts made by Omni-Tek to calm things down (the amnesty, amongst other peace offers) - means Omni-Tek has no obvious choice but to defend itself militarily, and to increase security measures across all parts of the planet.

Were the Clans to stop attacking Omni-Tek, then Omni-Tek would be forced into conflict with the ICC - a conflict that could not be fought with guns, but would rely on clever words. A conflict that Omni-Tek may not win. To keep the monopoly on notum alive, Omni-Tek needs the Clan conflict. After all, with insurance scans the cost of a grunt with a gun is much lower than the cost of a trained bureaucrat who can fight Omni-Tek's corner against an educated opponent.

The Clans hold 40% of the terraformed territory on one planet. Omni-Tek hold countless star systems. When it comes down to numbers and military hardware, Omni-Tek could crush the Clans like a bug. But to do so would end the conflict, and probably draw the attention of the ICC.

The wonderful irony is that the conflict is being driven more by the Clans than by Omni-Tek. So not only do OT have a conflict to their advantage, but also get the moral high-ground of being the victims of the depravations of madmen.

Peace on Rubi-Ka is going to involve more bloodshed. Even if parts of Omni-Tek and certain Clans were to declare a peace and unite, it would not stop the more militant Clans and the subsequent armed response from Omni-Tek. And as long as we have insurance, we have a guarantee that the same psychopaths will come back again and again.

We have unending war precisely because death is meaningless. And until that is rectified, the only incentive to stop fighting rests in the hands of the Clans.

Good luck with your peace process. You're going to need it.
