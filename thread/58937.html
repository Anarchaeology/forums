Relatively Easy Mission Improvements (long read though)
Basically I'm starting off with two improvements, maybe 3 improvements. I'll update the list if someone adds something neat later on. Here goes

Number 1: Redesign Mission Layout

The reason I find missions so boring is because of the small, linear, confining rooms and the painful procession of 1 mob, 1 mob, 0 mobs, 1 mob, 2 mobs, 1 mob, etc. My suggestion is to liven it up by making a more action-y FPS-y layout of having having more big rooms with lots of mobs, making the whole thing tougher and more exciting. You'd have something like the big opening room with 4 mobs in it, and then maybe 3 halls branching off of that with no mobs in them, each hall leader to another big room with 3+ mobs in it, etc. This gives the effect of running into a room, guns blazing, killing a bunch of people, and then healing up and preparing for the next fight, and then repeating. Each room might have a unique challenge. One room might be full of trox enforcers ready to pound your buttocks. The next room might have a stationary cannon that hits really hard. The next room might have an agent or 2 up on ledges that are sniping you in addition to the regular mobs. In my mind, the purpose of the missions should be to prepare you for PvP in the real world, not as hunting. That's what hunting is for. For that to work, the mobs have to act smarter....

Number 1.5 (or 2): Improved AI

This affects mainly the methods of the mobs buffing. There is no logical sequence to it. When the players enter the room, soldiers should put up shields and use their burst/flingshot, enfs should Mongo and/or rage and/or challenger, docs/MPs should cast debuffs, etc. etc. This would seemingly be quite simple. Something in the AI script that just says OnAttack() Use.nano.Total Mirror Shield MK X; Target=self
or
Use.Attack.Burst; Target=PlayerWithMostHP. Something like that. Of course, assuming the code lookes like that Add ****e to the mission and maybe make it TOUGHER. Teams of level 80-85 doing level 180+ missions makes no sense.

Number 3: TEH UBAR HIGH LVL CONTENT: PVP MISSIONS WITH STORYLINE EFFECTS.

This would require some effort on the ARKs part, but I think it would be fun. We (supposedly) have an ARK event team, but they haven't done anything in like 5 months eh? My suggestion: Every few days (or more frequently), they randomly pick 6-20 150+ players from each side to engage in a "story mission." To maybe make it sound more exciting, I'll give a bit of a narrative of what would happen .
