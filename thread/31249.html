Armor Issues, Seriously - Its insane!
All over Rubi'ka, in all the Stores there is the same kind of Armor, when I want to buy a full set of IE, Plasteel Armor I want to find a full set in the store, not run around for 10 hours only to find out that, yes, there is the same Armor in EVERY store, just a few QL ups and downs, and NO, the sleeves and helmets cant be bought in ANYONE of them... they are only available in missions for the moment.
