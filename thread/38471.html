quote:
Cweef, I never got into UO really. What do you think made it so addictive (like crack)? What did it have that AO doesn't?
For me it was the Skill system, and the balance in PvP. There _were_ magic items like mad, but people rarely used them in PvP (except LJs, my 7x GM Disarm thief has scores of Magic axes).

People usually use player crafted items, most of them were self made by other characters on that persons account(s). Cheap, and no biggy of ya lost it.

When you died you died, and all your sh1t got looted. This = motivation to play well lest lose all your stuff. VERY few items were unlootable.

No dependance on teams. You could (and most did) make PvP characters that would solo very well. And none of this "317 ways to deal damage" redundancy you see in AO. There were very specific stratigies you had to use that basicly meant you had to deal damage fast, keep stamina high and keep health up. Max HP you can have is 100, maybe 120 buffed with a potion. This means that even a newer character with only a few maxed skills could (and often did) compete in PvP.

Over all the PvM scene is slim to none. The game is a great game for interaction and PvP. Its fair, fast moving, and entertaining.

But after nearly 5 years its just old.
