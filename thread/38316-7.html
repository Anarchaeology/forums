One of the advantages that Traders have is their very own shop terminal in the general stores. Fixers have a similar advantage, if you know where to find it. Why not have something similar for Engineers? Put it in the Trade section of the supermarkets, just to drive the point home.

What might such a shop contain? For preference, items which allow Engineers to use their tradeskills in ways that other professions can't. Sure, a fully-buffed Trader can build you a really nice gun, but isn't it a bit ... mass-produced? Wouldn't you prefer a custom version? Make that Sleekblaster hit against Radiation Damage, or that Kolt 58 against Poison. Nice flamethrower mate, want it converted to a Chemical Sprayer? All for a small commission, of course.

This could be implemented as a tool that works like the Hacking Tool, stripping specific weapons to a base which can then be customised. Have it Engineer only, working off Weaponsmithing.

You could use the same tool on Weapon Kits, to produce a Customised Weapon Kit that could be used to construct weapons that can't be constructed using the present system. Want to build an Original Electronicum? Start with a Custom Energy Weapon Kit, which only an Engineer can make for you.

Finally, the manuals themselves could be sold from the Engineer terminal. Not strictly necessary, but it would make it easier to track which weapons can be made by anyone with sufficient skill and which require an engineer's special touch.

Once you have an Engineer-only terminal, it becomes easier to implement ideas along these lines. Maybe converting your Kolt to Poison damage requires a part that's only sold in the Engineer shop, or perhaps sell Custom Weapons Kits directly through the machine rather than "hack" them from the conventional kits. I'm sure you can come up with your own ideas.
