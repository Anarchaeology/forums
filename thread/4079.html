Lifeless Immortality and The Longest Road Pact
People of Rubi-Ka. I am Clan. As clan, we do not value Omni-Tek polices and procedures. We do not value wealth and political gain. For this, Omni has labeled us rebels and branded us criminals. Omni-Pol Guards have attacked us on site. When the amnesty took effect the Omni-Pol Guards ceased fire as they were ordered, but Omni-Tek Employees assumed their position.

Even through thick and thin our demands remain the same. Respect our decision to live as we please. Let us roam freely throughout the planet, without persecution. Or, revert control of the planet back to the people. Either way, our goal of freedom can be achieved.

As Clan, we all want this. But we are going about it in different ways. Some people attack Omni-Tek employees at will, others with peaceful debate or protest, and some ignore the world around them. Unless we can come up with a common method of protest our voices will be scattered and passed off as random noise. Our message won't be heard.

Clans people. We have to unite with one common method. Unless we can shout freedom in unison our random noise will fade. Omni-Tek will defeat us.

Lifeless Immortality has formed for the following purposes:

-Unite the Clans (determine the best cause of action for freedom). With many voices shouting the same message in unison we will be heard. Omni-Tek will listen to our demand. We will be independent.

-Locate and remove/expose Omni-Tek Spies. We cannot fully stand and shout in unison if Clan impostors are among us.

-Promote growth through free exchange. Remember, as Clan we are not about wealth. We gladly exchange armor, buffs, implants and weapons with one another to grow as we chose.

-The Longest Road Pact. We don't expect everyone to join Lifeless Immortality, but we do expect Clan guilds to unite in the fight for freedom. Currently, in its initial stage, The Longest Road Pact is our way of promoting Clan unity. We strongly urge all guilds to participate in The Longest Road Pact.

We are seeking guilds to step forward and assist in the unification process. The frame work for The Longest Road Pact has begun, but it is the unity of the Clans that will create the pact and put forth its use.

Abram "Cemetarygate" Kerans
Lifeless Immortality, President
Martial Artist, 97
