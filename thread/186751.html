SPECIAL REPORT: Warning Omni-Tek Employees
October 3, 29477

Rubi-Ka Independant News Agency has just recieved a warning from The Nanomage Liberation Front concerning an attack at an unnamed Omni-Mining facillity. Earlier today I Erika "Thescoop" Barnes was boing over some notes of another story I was working on in the coffee shop in Fair Trade, borealis, when a hooded and cloaked figure dropped a message on my table. Being the curious person that I am, and my duty as an investigative reporter, I opened the package. This is what it said

quote:

Omni-Tek has for too long ignored the please of the Nanomage people. We have tried negotiation but as long as thier profits roll in Omni-Tek will not negotiate. So today we take action and this warning is to ensure least number of casualties possible. Today at an unnamed Omni-Mining facillity the Nanomage Liberation Front will detonate a Thermonuclear device. We are taking the extreme action because our backs are against the wall. Omni-Tek's continued mining, refining, and export of notum is a direct attack on the Nanomage poeple, therefore we are going to completely obliterate one facillity. I strongly urge all Omni personal at any of the large Omni Mines, transport, or refining complexes to abandon your posts as you are in great personal danger.

Too long the solitus lead governments, lead from Omni-Prime, have ignored the Nanomage people. We can only live on Rubi-Ka, and only as long as there is enough notum for us to survive. We demand all off world notum shipments stop imediatly. Notum can be used safely and to great benifit of the galaxy should all notum based industry be conducted here, and the notum stay here. As our preliminary investigations into the Shadowlands proves, the stripping of notum from the planet could have dire concequences for all life, not just Nanomages.

Rubi-Ka for the Nanomage people! Her blood, notum, is in our blood, she is our mother. We must protect her or die. There is no option, Until All Are Free!!!

Aazamon Prime Minister of
The Nanomage Liberation Front

As you can see this radical madman plans to use one of the crudest and most destructive weapons known to man, a thermonuclear bomb! I have sent this message to the leaders of several Omni-Tek organizations to make sure it gets to where it needs to be. I sincerely hope that this is a hoax and no one gets hurt.

This has been a special report of Rubi-Ka Independant News by Erika Barnes.
