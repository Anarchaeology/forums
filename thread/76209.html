neuts take cdr steel to reclaim with toe tags and body bags for all
they say what goes around comes around.

normally neuts get to enjoy their automony and ablity to move around in all but the most secure of clan/omni areas. but when simon silverstone deided to "liberate Tir" he murdered innocent omni's and neuts who were just there to meet friends and go about their trade. these overly aggressive actions even took the more aggressive counterpart to the decessed CoT, the Council of Ares to speak publicly against them condeming the Sentinals actions, esspecilly on the part of neutrals.

earlier today, things came back around.


The neutral merc guild Elite Operations , under contract from unspecified members of the neutral population made the Head of one of silverstones top generals, cdr. Steel, top priority on their things to get for christmas list. unlike many of Steels men who had stopped attacking neuts at the whoompahs and the grid terminals shortly after taking Tir, commander Steel had made a name for himself for his unabased brutality in killing those of neut affilaition. rumors of this operation was leaked intentionaly underground to the more influential members of the neut community and by the time EO and Elite Academy members had gathered together, large numbers from every major neutral guild and some of the more powerful unaffilated neuts had arrived to assist what was sure to be an extreeme endeavor. Sumokan , headed the attack by first bringing the more powerful fighters off the grid in yalms and attacking cdr steel head on from the east. the second wave entered battle from the grid shortly thereafter with a squad of buracrats to calm Sleels minions before they could assist, with the 3rd wave on their heels to provide medics and backup to the first wave.

the inital onslaught ran cdr steel at least 50 yards before taking his life about 45 seconds later.

After taking steel, the remainder of the sentinal watch guards that had been calmed earlier were swiftly done away with. with the mission accomplished and the head of steel in a sack, sumokan called the neutral fighters to pull back and return to borealis for a debrief. just as the first of the cleanup crew (me being one of them) started for the grid term, commander steel, fresh from the reclaim booth reassumed his post outside of southgate and began his rebound attack. unfortunately for steel, the majority of the attack force was still present to dispatch him a second time before heading back to home territory.

at the borealis Grid term, Sumokan thanked the neutral population for what has been the largest single neutral attack for our autonomy and safty in decades. after the formalitys were out of the way, Hoopin, the top agent for EO, under the guise of a clanner took the head of commander steel to the doorstep of simon silverstone, as a 'gift' from those who had been murdered without cause in the streets of Tir for doing nothing more than simple trade and travel.
