.
Actually, as far as I know there are no "grenadier-thrown"-type grenades in this game (things you lob). While there may be items that look they way, they are all actually just guns that fire grenades, similar to grenade-launchers used in the world today.

So, every grenade weapon prior to 14.2 is a one-shot deal, but you simply reload the weapon as normal (although grenades are bugged and usually require a manual reload after every shot to fire properly).

I hope that wasn't too confusing
