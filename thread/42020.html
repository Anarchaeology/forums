I want to se PvP become something that can be used to further the RP elements.

PlayerX wants to Kill PlayerY (maybe to collect a bounty, or out of vengance...)
PlayerX sends a Challenge to PlayerY

PlayerY has the Option to accept or decline the Challenge (this is to protect the Non-PvP players).

The Challenge should have a Time Out Default , based on the players current Agg/Deff position (in case PlayerY is asleep at the keyboard). If PlayerY is set to Agg then it defaults to Yes! If PlayerY is set to Def then Default is No!

This makes those people who have a Bad Attitude (walking around in full Agg) more likely to be killed by thier own kind. And forces the rest to Calm Down (pull Down the Agg/Def Slider) while in populated areas.

This type of play would allow for fighting in the streets (i think it should be available in any zone with 75% or less Supression Gas)

This Style of PvP should not give titles, save those for the Arena Duelers (that way Ganking is discouraged and the Gladiators of the Arena are Awarded).

just my 32 cents.
