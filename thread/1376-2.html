Well from what I have seen by monitoring the posts, the gripes and the changes that FunCom has been making to the game, and finally implementing a lot of the things that were missing for the first few months of being live, is that:

The game is stable. The music tracks, and visual effects, weather and terrain simply blow the competition away, and it keeps looking better all the time.

The early casual player base that initially migrated to AO has come and gone for the most part, and like me, a lot of us are still watching from the sidelines, looking for a major series of patches (or just one major fix) to integrate/fix the things we need to finally say, ok, thats it, I'm cancelling my EQ/DAOC/Whatever account, and going back to AO. It may happen, because I know many who are disappointed with the aspects of other games.

But:

Or are you also beginning to see the foundations laid for a greed-based system here? The bulk of the trade skills are so complex as to be a major turn off, but these new goodies that FunCom is offering us are only accessible through them. Once again we are down to rare drops, and Phat Lewt.

As a Fixer, I turn on my HoT, go afk, grab a soda, maybe look for fixings for a sandwich, come over, hit the first aid button, walk away, make my sandwich, come back and loot the corpse. Well, what do you do if you have 30 mobs in a mission like that? Or more? I'm not going to eat that many sandwiches sorry, and I'm not studying while I play, I don't have to study anymore, I work for a living.

Give this thread some bumpage if you're also sitting on the sidelines, I think its important for FunCom to know that there are lots of us out there, looking for a real reason to come back, not a backpack that adds 150 points in a broken and worthless skill.
