Pardon me but what game were you playing? EQ had horrible pathing (speaking from somebody that played a mage from beginning, then a beastlord to 54 before quiting).

There were spots where the pet would just Poof (bedroom of guk, never fixed). Spots where pets would follow bizarre paths to get to a creature thus pulling everything and anything back to you.

The introduction of Summon Companion was a start, but took too long to cast and too much energy. Why? You had to get inside the mob, summon, then attack.... Otherwise mob train.

Mages were used to wandering off so mobs would kill them and wander away rather than killing the party if a pet went awol. These were facts of life from EQ beta through Planes of Power.

AI with that number of mobs and best path deduction is non trivial. Most games handle it by building pathing right into game zone, the mob, creature, etc beelines to closest "lay line" that runs by , then finds the closest point, and turns straight at target.

This is one reason that FC's fixes to pathing so far have been to eliminate rooms that really messed pets up.
