When you going to add some more 2-Hand Blunt weapons?
The ONLY worthwhile 2-Hand Blunt weapons are the Support Beam and Sledgehammer (btw its spelt wrong in game - Slegdehammer). The ones i think are TOTALLY useless are the Blackjohn and 2-Handed Blackjack which require Sneak Attack (Has anyone actually got Sneak Attack to work?), and the damage is laughable.

The 2-Hand staffs are reasonable but require to many skills to raise (Fast Attack & Sneak Attack), though the Fast Attack can be VERY useful.

There is also the novelty 2-Hand Blunt, The Banjo! But this is exactly that Novelty.

Whats getting me down is the fact the Atrox Enforcer has become a clone profession. The only main difference i see is whether they wear a Decus Coat or Tank Armor.

What we need is a few more GOOD 2-Hand Blunt weapons, they would be better if they had a 'Fun factor' that the Support Beam has. Maybe "A Park Bench", something BIG and makes people turn around and say "That Atrox was wielding a Lamp post!!".
