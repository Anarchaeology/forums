What is truly funny to me is this Evil Storm mentality that tends to churn the rumor mill and high drama of these boards.

In my EQ days there were the "uber guilds". In order to be in a "uber guild" you had to be invited by the officers/members cause it was a exclusive club. These guilds dominated unique mobs and ran over people left and right in order to accomplish their agendas. They to got raked over the coals in the boards and had plenty of public disputes between themselves.

In AO we have Storm a guild that until very recently let anyone in that was lvl 110 and had 3 sponsors. Wolfe telling us officers that everyone deserves to belong to a good guild and to not exclude any person that seemed cool and agreed to our one rule.

As a guild we only stand for 3 things; Killing omni, having fun (while killing omni) and helping each other out. Alot of the members are power gamers and they have pushed this game to the very limits of what it can do, hence the consecutive tar killings and uniqe mobs.

Back when Dark Carinval was around we even got together with them and played out our own player generated story and had a few large pvp battles. That was alot of fun. A shame really that all that ended.

Now I'm not saying that their aren't other guilds like this because of course there are. Yet Storm, much like bigfoot and EQ "uber guilds" generate the tabloid rumors.

To the point of the original poster, fotik pretty much summed it up a number of the old timers have quit or are taking breaks.

As a side note to all these title farmers posts on this board, I read in our forums why the few people did farm titles. My impression was that a few of the guys were just plain bored and wanted to accomplish 2 things:

1. Define concretely how the PVP ranking system worked.

2. Prove that the PVP ranking system was a joke.

A few of these bored storm members are leaving the game now as there is nothing left for them to do.
