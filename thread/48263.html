NCUs Made by Trade Skills Recipe
1. Get Notum Nugget (NN) of QL=q1
2. Use Jenson Ore Extractor on NN -> Raw Notum (RN) of QL=q1
3. Use Isotope Separator on RN -> Refined Notum (RfN) of QL=q1
4. Use Neutron Displacer on RfN -> Notum Crystal Fragment (NCF) of QL=q1
5. Repeat 1-4 to get NCF of QL=q1, q2, q3, q4
6. Combine NCF q1 and q2 -> Notum Crystal Segment (NCS) of QL= min(q1,q2) = Qa
7. Combine NCF q3 and q4 -> Notum Crystal Segment (NCS) of QL= min(q3,q4) = Qb
8. Combine NCS Qa with Qb -> Notum Crystal (NC) of QL = min(Qa,Qb) = Qf
9. Purchase NCU Casing from trade shop machine.
10. Combine NCU Casing and NC -> NCU of QL Qf
