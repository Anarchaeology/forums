I play because it satisfies my obsessive/compulsive tendencies. Just mindless, automated fighting to level, lather, rinse repeat.

To me the game is like online-solitaire. You play it not because it is fun, but because it occupys part of your mind. I usually listen to the stereo and watch TV while playing.

I have gone off and played other games, but usually I finish them within a week or two, and AO is like a 2 a.m. girlfriend, who is faithfully awaiting your return and is available at your beckon call.

I think a system of leveling conflicts to some degree with content. Many people, myself included, will not put a lot of energy into quests which are roleplay related and not level related, as they feel everyone else is leaving them behind. Most people have the mindset that will reach 200, then roleplay.
