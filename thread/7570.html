Ammunition
Hi,

As a Soldier I get through plenty of ammunition and when I restock I usually buy 5-10k rounds at a time. Having to buy ammo in 25's may be fine for the basic shop, but perhaps the quantity sold could go up according to the type of shop? For example, the basic shop would sell in 25's, the advanced sell in 250's and the superior sell in 500-1,000 increments.

I'd love to be able to walk into the superior shop and buy 1k rounds with one click or something like that.
