Misson reward and Neutrals
I play a Neut MA lvl 55, my first char so i guess im a newbie (so correct me if im wrong about something.... =). So just a few questions around mission rewards for neuts.

When you finish a mission, you get X amout of money, and item and some exp usually, depending on what criteria you choose for the mission (max money max bad, usually doesnt give much exp =)
