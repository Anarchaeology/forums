The problem why init effects are confusing is that there IS a difference wether the init is buffed/debuffed or wether you raised it by spending IPs (same thing as jump hight, if you know what i'm talking about ). Lots of ppl just wouldn't sacriface their IPR for this tho..

Best way to test it is to reset the init skill you want to test with IPR, put your agg-def bar on 0% (so you make sure you don't cap anywhere), use the stopwatch to time the attack/recharge bars, then raise the init a ton and test again.

When there was full IP reset available I did it and tested Physical Init the way I described above...it was exactly 1 sec off attack and 2 sec off recharge for every 600 init. I'd guess it works the same for the rest except NanoC Init.
