Condemned Subways
Level Range 5 - 24
Note: You will be unable to enter once you reach level 25.
http://ao.stratics.com/content/guid...ways%20OT.shtml

Temple of Three Winds
Level Range 25 - 60
Note: You will be unable to enter after level 61 without a warp and then you will be booted at level 71.
http://ao.stratics.com/content/guid...hreewinds.shtml

Steps of Madness
Level Range 30 - 50
No level limit in dungeon
http://ao.stratics.com/content/guid...ofmadness.shtml

Biomare Foreman's
Level Range 50 - 70
No level limit in dungeon
http://ao.stratics.com/content/guid...s/biomare.shtml

Cyborg Barracks
Level 70 - 90
I don't know if there is a level limit on this one.
http://ao.stratics.com/content/guid...gbarracks.shtml

Camelot Castle
Level 100 - 200
I don't know if there is a level limit on this one.
http://ao.stratics.com/content/guid...s/camelot.shtml

Smuggler's Den
Level 120 - 200
I don't know if there is a level limit on this one either.
http://ao.stratics.com/content/guid...gglersden.shtml

Inner Sanctum
Level 125+
Note: Unable to enter this dungeon unless you are level 125 or higher. I don't know if there is a level limit that will get you kicked out.
http://ao.stratics.com/content/guid...ersanctum.shtml

Will to Fight
Level: unnoficially 100 - 200
Note: It is a 25% gas zone. It is a PvP area with very few mobs. Each faction enters the dungeon from a seperate entrance. There are lvl 100 and 200 mobs inside.
http://ao.stratics.com/content/guid...lltofight.shtml
