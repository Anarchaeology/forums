Funcom true or not....
http://www-csag.ucsd.edu/individual/pakin/complaint


Rather than engage in a point-by-point response to the textual interpretation of Funcom's sophistries, I want to respond to the more general issue at hand. First off, with that kind of thinking, Funcom makes it sound like it's some perfect angel of unstained ethical standards. Now, that last statement is a bit of an oversimplification, an overgeneralization. But it is nevertheless substantially true. Funcom not only lies, but it brags about its lying to its subalterns. I find it sickening to watch Funcom ascribe opinions to me that I don't even hold. Funcom will almost certainly tiptoe around that glaringly evident fact, because if it didn't, you might come to realize that I want my life to count. I want to be part of something significant and lasting. I want to serve on the side of Truth. In the past, organizations like Funcom would have been tarred and feathered for trying to base racial definitions on lineage, phrenological characteristics, skin hue, and religion. If Fate desired that Funcom make a correct application of what it had read about metagrobolism, it would have to indicate title and page number, since the pigheaded organization would otherwise never in all its existence find the correct place. But since Fate does not do this, from secret-handshake societies meeting at "the usual place" to back-door admissions committees, Funcom's hirelings have always found a way to leave helpless citizens afraid in the streets, in their jobs, and even in their homes. Perhaps it sounds like stating the obvious to say that we can't stop Funcom overnight. It takes time, patience and experience to give our young people the values that will inspire them to create a world in which authoritarianism, unilateralism, and pessimism are all but forgotten.

You may balk at this, but if we're to effectively carry out our responsibilities and make a future for ourselves, we will first have to resolve a number of lingering problems. Although Funcom wants to engender ill will, if we fail to make it answer for its wrongdoings, then we have no one to blame but ourselves. The great irony is that if Funcom thinks its modes of thought represent progress, it should rethink its definition of progress. There can be no doubt that if Funcom can overawe and befuddle a sufficient number of prominent individuals, then it will become virtually impossible for anyone to indicate in a rough and approximate way the two shabby tendencies that I believe are the main driving force of modern larrikinism. To recap the main points made in this letter: 1) Funcom uses vulgar language and makes obscene gestures at those whose opinions differ from its, 2) Funcom treats people as objects, and 3) anyone who says that merit is adequately measured by Funcom's methods and qualifications can be branded as both muddleheaded and ostentatious.
