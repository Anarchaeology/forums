Sided Combat
I've come to be very sick of people killing their own side. Anyone else seen this? I'm sure you must have.

How many Omni-Tek, I wonder, have murdered their own guards? Or destroyed dozens of slayerdroids just for the loot?

And what of the people who directly attack Philip Ross, or whatnot?

I'm proposing they change that. I think that killing Omni-Tek employees if you ARE Omni-Tek should have negative consequences, as killing Clan should for a clan member.

I've got a few ideas, but I'll just sort of throw them into the open. I think killing your own side should have consequences based on the severity of the attack (who you attacked), and your previous record. For instance...

You are Omni-Tek
You attack a guard (not elite), and are given a warning
You attack another guard (also not elite), and are kicked out of OT for 12 hours
You attack an elite guard now, and are removed from OT for an additional 40 hours
You kill 2 slayerdroids, and are removed from OT for a full week and a half.

Or...

You are OT, but never do anything wrong
Philip Ross, our CEO, holds a speech... you shoot at him
You are permanently banned from OT

Or here's a real example of something that happened. A prisoner (clan) was being escorted to Omni-HQ for questioning. Two OT employees attacked her, and killed her. The OT would shun them for ruining a possibly informative interrogation, and the clan would shun the people because of their brutal murder of a clan member. At LEAST a month out of both factions should be the penalty, if not much more severe.

Why all the bother about this? I'm sick of it all.

I'm sick of people trying to disrupt plotline events for "kicks", and having no real negative response affect them as a result. I'm sick of seeing random OT employees at the BB in Omni-Ent tearing down guards because they're bored. I'm sick of people asking me to group a clan member just so I can kill Ninja-Droids for better XP.

I'd like to see it where killing creatures of your own side has negative consequences. They can start small (everyone makes mistakes), but they should grow fast enough to really hit the people who do this is a regular act hard, and keep them out of OT forever. I for one don't want to be part of an organization where it's OK to murder your co-workers.
