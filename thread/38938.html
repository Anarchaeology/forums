Grid Point for Stret West Bank!!!
The number one Neutral club in all of Rubi ka is Reets Retreat. It is the club where Omni Tek employees, Clanners and Neutrals alike go to mingle, role play, listen to live music, and enjoy themselves. In the few months that I have been playing this game, I have watched how things transpire at Reets Retreat, how people interact and role play, and most of all the socializing that takes place. There is just one complaint that I hear over and over again, "Why is there no grid point to Stret West Banks? It's such a pain to get here."

So this is a petition to Funcom to implament a Grid Point to Stret West Bank, for the purpose of making the major Neutral club as accessable as the Clan and Omni clubs are.
