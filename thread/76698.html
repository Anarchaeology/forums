Perception vs Concealment - explanation in a baby mantishell
I've seen several perception/concealment misconceptions posted around the boards lately, so I've culled my previous posts into a summary and hope it can be read as a handy reference without having to jump all over the boards looking for relevant threads. Perception vs Concealment
- explanation in a baby mantishell
---------------------------------------

The attacking/defensive checks work differently for PvM and PvP situations.

PvM
-----
* Basic attacking skill versus defensive skill checks at work here. Simply stated - NPCs and mobs have Perception Skills which check against your own Concealment skill when you are sneaking.

* There is also another factor at work - a roll-of-dice - every 10 seconds. This roll-of-dice determines the chance of the mob detecting your presence. (The actual percentage of chance has never been revealed by FC and I do not know anyone who has managed to figure it out to any accurate degree.)

PvP
-----
* A player can hide (ie. sneak, become invisible) and other players can do active spotting (ie. use Search function) to see a player who's trying to hide.

* When you sneak, your concealment skill checks against perception skills of others around you - with a 75% chance of being hidden if the skill level is equal.

* When you actively search for a hidden player, your perception is now the attacking skill with a 75% chance of discovering a hidden person if the skill level is equal.

* There is no roll-of-dice feature for invisibility in player-to-player interaction but there is another code feature at work. We all have a base radius of 5 metres - ie. your radius of spotting. This increases to radius of 40 metres (or all viable characters with 100% character view distance) when you have 1K in perception skill.
