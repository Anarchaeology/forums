Proposed changes
Current AO Manual:

"The Martial Artist is a spiritual warrior, mastering the silent bow and unarmed combat. Some Martial Artists may even decide to follow their path to illumination with melee weapons as an aid. His focus is absolute, making him a deadly foe, especially when he puts his arsenal of nano programs to proper use. However, the same focus makes the Martial Artist pay dearly in all other fields."

Suggested new version:

"The Martial Artist is an outdated warrior, who has no place on the modern battlefield. His best role is as a second tier healer and bodyguard for a doctor. Some call him the ninja-nurse. On his off days when he fights monsters he can have some fun, but during a notum war he must wear his hospital clothes. The least effective of the damage dealing classes, who's so called 'arsenal of nano programs' have been infected with nerf-virus and are ineffective. No longer is his focus 'absolute', he may try all manner of weapons including sniper rifles, shotgun's and inappropriate melee weapons..anything else to try and find his former glory..but alas in vain. A tragic figure relegated to the edge of the conflict"

This crit nerf was a mistake in the first place, FC should have the stones to acknowledge that and remove the nerf from the game. The problem with LLTS and other crit rates was stacking for insane crit damage with uber items, not the average critting MA and Agent.
