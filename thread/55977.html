Elevator Locations in Team Missions
Okay...

This one is a rant for everyone who has to use a pet.

In the same type of missions you get from Broken Shores (Brown offices) there is a room...

In this room sometimes there is an elevator (normally on the floor you're going up to).

The elevator is carefully positioned behind a little partition wall (/me wishes I could draw on here). Sometimes there's a chest there, which is fine of course, but sometimes it's the lift.

Now when you zone up to a new level there is generally one mob waiting for you, and a very high chance of aggro from the surrounding rooms.

Clearly this hasn't been tested becuase the only way to get a pet out from behind the little wall is to go far enough away for it to warp. This holds for every bot from Automations to Slayers (yes i have tested it).

You only have to remove one little bit of that wall to allow pets to get through... but which idiot decided a lift should go there??


Go on.. try it...

Make a room below with the up button.

Make a room above with the stupid moronic wall with the down button in the usual place and stick a Veteren Enforcer in the room.

Now make yourself a level 150 Engi or something.. trade skill specialist so no real weapon skills yet, no evades etc etc.. you know the type... 100% dependant on that bot.

No you can't terminate the bot, you need it to defend you. And even if you did you're like every other Engi, you got buffs and got a slayer... and oh.. look... those buffs have run out...

Press the up button and try and get the bot out without dying... go on...


This room does make me angry every time I see it... but since petitioning takes so long for someone to come along and say "so what?" then why bother. Especially since I would have to hold up the entire team for that period.

I can be patient and understanding about fixing pathing, I don't imagine it's easy. I can be just dissapointed about the rest of my profession and again maybe it's not an easy thing to just fix a profession.

But this? This is just gross stupidity, there is absolutely no need for an elevator to be there, and I can't imagine for a minute that's it's even hard to fix.

Please bump this thread.
