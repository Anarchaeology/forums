DaveDread

Sorry, here's a clarification:

Traders can do wrangles, a temporary nano-rasing nano. Max version is at +132 to all nano skills.

MetaPhycisists can do nano skill buffs, from lowest to highest level: Teachings (+25), Masteries (+50), Infuses (+90), Mochams (+140) They dont stack.

To make a good pet you need high nano skills, so you can make pets that are very high level compared to yourself if you use these buffs.


Hope it helps.
