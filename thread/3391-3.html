I'm very divided on this issue.


First off, I believe the mobs are very tough. Something needs to be done about this -- but, you look and say if they were easier, then everyone would be soloing oranges, which is clearly wrong. BUT it doesn't seem right that everyone should be soloing oranges, and greens -- and we all know that the levels come really slowly when fighting those one at a time. I'm confused, I'll admit it.

One one hand, overequipping and buffing is a large part of this game. Changing it now will change the mechanics of the game EXTRAORDINARILY. A lo of people will quit, if they do not have the patience to stick it out and get used to the changes. Personally, if the game I have grown to love, changes now, I may have to think long and hard about it.

On the other hand, things have gotten out of control. The reason that so many people don't want overequipping changed is that they can't see that if it's done right, the mob strength goes down, and everyone is more balanced. Or, they PvP, which is another whole topic.

Cycle:
1) PvP people figure out how to use the game mechanics to get the best possible equipment. L50 implants at L7 not uncommon, even after treatment clinic patch.
2) PvP People get sick of PvP and go fight mobs
3) PvP People cut through red mobs like there's no tomorrow
4) FC Increases mob strength
5) Normal players without QL50 implants at L7 get owned by green mobs.
6) Goto 1 and figure out how to get 70s in at L7.

My L9 enforcer has QL27 armor, QL40 hammer, QL20 implants and using L14 nanoprograms for self-buffs.

She has to pick her fights VERY carefully to avoid getting owned.

Starting to level last night, she kills greens and yellows in one hammer swing and one brawl at the most.

There are 2 sides of this, and they are polar opposites. Find someway to make everything gray and a BIT more balanced, and more people might have more have fun.

But don't pi$$ off everyone in the process :-).
