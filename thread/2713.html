how to make the game more fun
The higher I lvl and the more I play Anarchy Online the more it looses its luster and the more time consuming it gets in order to do nothing.

I am a lvl 91 soldier an the abandonded RK2 server. As many of you know we are the red head step child of servers.

I my personal opinion there are a few thing in the game that do nothing more then consume time in things I dont particularly see as fun. I will list them all below.

1. Making Implants

Problem: To make implants you have to run around like a mad man to 2000000 stores iding every cluster to find the one you need. Run around like a chicken (this applies to any shopping you do) you can spend 4-5 hrs running in and out of stores. I do not find this to be a lot of fun. That is why in the real world there are places like walmart. People do not like to go around town to every store looking for 1 item they want, they enjoy 1 stop shopping.

Solution: The stores in the game should have everything that is sold. If I am looking for a cluster, gun, or armor I should be able to go to 1 store and find it (unless it is a unique item).

2. Higher lvl shopping

Problem: it doesnt exist. How in the world am I supposed to build implants for a lvl 130 soldier (when I get there) if I cant buy anything at the store. And well, for a lvl 130 he will be using implants much higher then his lvl so this would make me have to find 5 other people willing to go on 12 missions to just get my implant parts. Then on 30+ missions to try to get the clusters.

Solution: have a shop for the higher lvls, you let us get up to lvl 200 you should have shops that sell these items.

3. Hunting (lvling)

Problem: I some ways it is fairly easy to lvl in this game (until you hit higher lvls). I dont mind the game being harder to lvl but they way it is now my obsticle to lvling is not spending more time having fun killing monsters. It is standing around spamming for HOURS to try to find a team. 3 days and I have yet to find one at medusas at RK2 that has room. I have out lvled all my friends and am 110% dependant on teams and I cannot find them so basically I spend most of my time in game not having any fun at all.

Solution: Have a different xp scale for single missions to make it worth while to do them in order to try to lvl. Missions (that I can do as a gimped soldier) give me 3k xps per kill, when you need 1 million well 3k doesnt really cut it. Make monsters generated in missions give higher xps. An example: If I am hunting at medusas they medusas give me around 50-105k xps per kill so make the monsters in the dungeon which will be easier to kill give me something like 25-35k xps. That way the days that I cannot find a team I am able to play and have fun without having to stand around for hours yelling LVL 91 soldier lft!!!

4. Role Playing

Problem: those who know me know that I am a super twink as far as armor overequiping goes. The reason is because if I want to play and kill anything or take a hit I need to be over equiped (and soldier still suck after that). This means putting no social armor and having a party at baboons in to appealing because I need 10000 buffs to put all my stuff back on again.

Solution: Make an armor check, if you dont meet the reqs with the implants make us take it off. Also make the monster EASIER so we dont HAVE to over equip to play this game. That way we can role play more, and have parties at baboons to encourage people to get into the RP of the game.

These are the reasons that I feel like quitting AO and I think are the same reasons why so many other quit ohh yeah.. make my soldier not be such a gimp.. remember we cannot over equip our guns so we cannot do damage.
