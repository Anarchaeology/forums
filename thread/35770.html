how to get a Personal Lock Pick no drop lockpick

get a id-extractor and a camera combine them and you wil get id-data now combine the id-data with a lock pick and you will get a Personal Lock Pick witch is a no drop lock pick

how to get a Personalized Basic Robot Brain
  1. combine a Screwdriver with Robot Junk and you will get a Nano Sensor
  2. now combine the Nano Sensor with a Bio Analyzing Computer and a MasterComm - Personalization Device and you will have your verry own Personalized Basic Robot Brain
  3. both the Bio Analyzing Computer and the MasterComm - Personalization Device are sold in the Trade section of shops in Mech and Elec Supplies
