Lizzara, it's possible but very difficult to put on grid armor at that level. You will need to implant MC and MM with the highest possible implants you can tweak, likely sacrificing certain core skills to keep the implants focused on one skill line (probably agility).

Each time you want to put it on, you will need MC/MM/TS mochams as well as a 132 wrangle and, even then, you may still be OE if you haven't done your homework correctly. No matter what happens, you will be surely be 50% effective when the mochams wear off.

Obviously, breed is a consideration here, but you have a lot more to consider when planning a level 25 GA twink.

Extra nanoskills could be good for the extra few points towards some stuff but in the grand scheme of things u will still be needing at least a 100 wrangle regardess in order to get it on.
A downside of a nanomage is your weapon damage would suffer slightly and u may have a good deal more difficulty than i did with implanting. If u are on rimor feel free to get in touch if u need any help or information and good luck.
