Servers, Events & Roleplaying
Hi there fellow AO-players and of course the mods here...

I have realised that RK-2 (where i am located) does not have the same amount of events nor do the majority of players in RK-2 choose to roleplay as much as the peeps in RK-1.

I do know that there are some (in RK-2 a large amount of peeps) that do Not want any events nor do they care about roleplaying, likewise there are some (a rather small amount of peeps) in RK-1 that do not wish to roleplay nor do they like events.

So my suggestion is to give everyone a one time offer whether they want to be in RK-1 or RK-2 server, where RK-1 will be the server where the gamemasters can really focus most events on and where one can expect that the majority of players actually roleplays.
Those who do not want this will choose RK-2 where they can do their powerlvl and where they will not be bothered with events and such.

Well, i sincerely hope that i will get support with this idea.

Regards.

Actually, it's just Dust Brigade massacres we're worried about. I think the majority of people on either server would be happy to see events in which the players had an instrumental role to play. What's been causing people to say "Thank God I'm on RK2" is the fact the the DB gank-fests have largely been confined to the RK1 dimension.

At the end of the day, I don't see why anyone should be bothered by events, as long as they don't have to take part in them. It's the ones where you're dragged in against your will and have no power to affect the outcome that have soured people on the idea.

More events for both servers, please.
