LLTS Petition to FC
This is a petition to either keep the old LLTS in the game or to Change ALL Scopes to the new one. (prefer the latter) However, either of these is fair to the GENERAL PLAYING populace. If you keep the old scopes as they are but make the new ones significantly weaker. It does nothing to solve the problem of crits (old ones are still there) In fact, it will only make the Uber people in the game EVEN MORE SO since they're the ones that HAVE the damn scopes to begin with. Using the current solution is just going to widen the gap between the uber and normal players.

Now for the reasons why FC is doing this. Please everyone see the light. True reason is that FC is afraid to piss off the old player base. so they keep the uber items in. Well, that's not going to solve the issue then. so why even bother to make the outward attempt at balancing the game? Does FC really feel like newer players will keep playing the game when they know that they will NEVER get as powerful as the older players with their UBER equipment? Anyways, just my thoughts. Hope FC changes their mind on this.

Conclusion for those who skip to the bottom. MAKE ALL LLTS the same.

Bump if you agree. Bump if you don't. Either way, let's give this some visibility and see some reasoning here.
