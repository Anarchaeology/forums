It seems to me that the real issue here is leadership. Omni thinks that they should have leadership over RK. The Clans think that RK should be entirely under clan control. The Neutrals think differently. Many support all sides of this argument.

And it seems to me that this whole incident is geared towards showing clans as terrorists, barbarians, unworthy to lead and control the peoples of RK.

But leadership is all about doing what is right for your people, taking care of them, helping them to prosper, recognizing the dangers that exist and avoiding preventable disasters.

For these omni leaders to get slaughtered in this manner casts serious doubts on their abilities as leaders.

By your own admission, you know that there are militant clans like Storm out there, clans full of people just waiting to take up action against Omni. Just putting yourself in that compromising position causes you to lose credibility. And if this was a staged event, then its even worse.

And so now you come here and post this story of the May Day Massacre. Was it a tragedy? Probably. But the real tragedy is your failures as leaders. This causes many of us clanners to question your competence and judgment. How could you take care of the people of RK if you can't even see the obvious danger lurking around the corner? How can you guarantee our prosperity, our well-being, if you cant even take care of yourselves?

My clan is a peaceful guild. We do not seek out war with Omni. We do not want to control RK. All we want is to enjoy this planet. And we are certainly not the only clan guild that feels this way. Some of you know me and my guild, Black Orchid. I have personally signed treaties with a few of you. And, you know that if you wish to discuss this with me in-game, you can because I am always available to help my fellow citizen of RK, no matter what his faction-choice is. After all, we are all in this together.
