growing frustration with rebels
Despite its massive military support, Omni police have still been largely unsuccessful in halting the rebel activities within Omni-1, as the attacks against Omni guards continue today, led by the outlaw leader Methlon of the Sinister Anarchists.
Methlon, who has been among Omni-Pols most wanted criminals for some time, claims that he is making a stand against the corrupt rule of Omni-Tek. Unfortunatly for police this individual, while no longer associated with TBA, has clan and omni support for his cause, making him difficult to bring to justice.
It seems that the police are limited as to how they can deal with these situations, due primarily to outdated Omni-Tek legal policies.
There have been several possibilities discussed by Omni officials as to rectifying the problem. They have considered converting neutral lands into a penal colony where unlawful citizens will be banned for crimes commited. Others have talked about banning these criminals off world permanently, but there is concern that this might be too harsh a punishment, and would agitate citizens who already question Omni rule to the point of revolt.
Unless Omni-Tek changes its criminal policies soon, there doesnt apear to be an end in sight to the civil disobedience that is rampant in the streets.
