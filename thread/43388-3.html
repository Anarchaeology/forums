But, nobody's talking about 133. The Atrox starts to suck majorly around 150 or so and becomes a bigger and bigger gimp till the end-game.

Up till the mid 130's the Atrox is simply a larger size Solitus with little to no difference in skills or abilities. This is forgiveable. Basically till 130 Atrox=Solitus.

At 130 a clever Atrox who spends a ton in expensive blue Abilties (Int/Sense) can get most of his 200 implants in. He'll have to keep the faded Surgery cluster and might not get the head in. But, implants are not the issue here. Smart players always manage to gear up well enough.

The issue is that our Atrox's not only are not distinguished from the Opi's and Soli's. It's that they actually become gimpier and gimpier each level past 130 or so. By contrast, the NanoMage becomes stronger and stronger as health becomes more of a non-issue in the high level game. Hitpoints are so easy to come by as the NCU buffs and Essence of Behemoth (not to mention Doctor buffs) are standard-issue in the first room of a mission.

Nobody ever dies anyway. Mind you we are talking PvM here.

The problem is that we have the Atrox getting worse and worse every level with nothing to balance against it. I've not heard of an Atrox who wants to be able to self-buff into the best nanos. That's the hit we decided to take. What every Atrox wants is something to balance against the inability to execute the best nanos.

A larger heal delta, a bit of 'natural AC', some more hitpoints per level, hell even a natural runspeed increase would even the scales a bit more post 130 where the game finally starts getting interesting (or boring depending on your point of view).

Ever notice the length of an Atrox' legs? He ought to really motor on the open field once he gets going.
