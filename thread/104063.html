Good day, Rubi-Ka,
This is reporter Jon Grim of the ONN media service. I would like to announce to the public the release of two new publications

The first is Omega News Network's propoganda free newspaper. It's attempt is to report the news fairly and impartially.

The second is ONN's "TALK!" magazine, where we interview some of the movers and shakers of Rubi-Ka and bring it to you in full color high gloss magazine form. You won't want to miss the first issue. Bad-Gal "Templus" graces its cover
