Just some easy advice
As far as NT goes, you can solo missions quite well early on, as soon as you get calm. The you can use the calm to isolate mobs, and regain your nano. Monsters/melleers are best, since you can root, although in missions, the location you see is not always the one the server does ;(.

But for lobbies, it works best to be patient and pull a single. In 140 lvls, all done in missions, maybe only 1% have an arrangement where it is impossible to pull a single. Use the monsters line of sight against them. And dont pull with an attack! Then it is just a question if you can beat one orange-red mob.

If you cant, then all your resources need to be committed to better damage output first and foremost, since you can zonetrick if necessary. For weapon classes, this includes good weapon skill implants, and using the buff resources of your community to overequip them. If you take a lot of crits, evades then become crucial. You dont need to be twinked to manage this, (i wasn't), you just have to focus on one big improvement at a time. I had only 4 implants in my body until about lvl 80. I basically could cast only one nuke at any given time. This is fun that twinked characters never get to have, to plan, to achieve, etc. Do not neglect the shopping channels!

Another option is mission teams -- 2 person teams are very effective, and a very fun way to play that avoids some of the hassles of large groups. People often neglect small groups in AO, and with some reason, given the experience structure. But they can still be deeply efficient, since there is less to coordinate, and a better split of the rewards.

Hope this helps ^^
