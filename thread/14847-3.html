Nice way to ask
God, I hate to sound pompous by even suggesting ways to help you ask. Lemme point out I've gotten a sum total of 5K I think for all the Teachings, Masteries, and Infuses I've done. I just don't want the credits. Maybe Mocham's will justify a tip in terms of NanoChargers used to ladder from Infuses to Mochams. But ...

I think the one thing that would have me more than happy to stop what I'm doing and drop a buff on you is if you somehow acknowledged the inconvenience that the constant buff requests are:

"Excuse me wise and powerful MP. Would it trouble you to execute Infuse or Mocham's in my NCU? I'll be sure to repay the favor once I reach your stature."

You see you have acknowledged that throwing buffs around is not usually an MP's hobby. You're sympathizing with him or her. That earns a free buff every time. Now, to help you out a bit more, make a 'buff beg' macro and put %t in there so that you personalize it:

"Excuse me, %t. You are obviously a wise and powerful MP. Would it trouble you to execute Infuse or Mocham's in my NCU? I'll be sure to repay the favor once I reach your stature."

I know it sounds pompous to suggest to others as to how to beg. But, this is what I will be doing with my little Engineer. (Not, the exact phrasing as it's not her style. But you get the idea). The fact that you have their name in the request shows that you have searched out an appropriate profession to help you and actually are close enough to target them.

Here's another thing to try:

Instead of just hollering 'can some1 buff me ts/mc plzzzzzzz!', create a reference of yourself. Target yourself and press 'g' (I think that's the key). Then take the reference of yourself off the Info window and drop it into chat before your shout for help. That way you can get:

"Arthur Nutsnbolts McKechnie" really needs some buffs to make a good 'bot!

The part in quotes will be clickable and will give information about you. This will personalize your pleas for help and probably give you better results. People in general like to click on the purple things in chat anyway and will make you less ignorable.

Hope this helps.
