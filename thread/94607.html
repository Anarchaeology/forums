Tisantous Departs From AO
***Please Read The Following Speech While Listening To The Opening Music From The Trailer To The Movie "Pearl Harbor"***

It is a sad and grim horizon. I have been with AO since its very beginning, and braved the heaviest of stroms it has had, trudging onward in the persuit of a glory that was only a broken promise. I saw it in its best time, and have been there through its worst. Saddly, the worst of times has not left, and has only increased with the recent chain of events and blows to the RP community.

I can say from a personal stance, that I gave my all in this game. I was given a place, I used it to the fullest power. But my hands were always, somehow tied behind my back. Why? Because without the tools, without the foundation to build upon, I can only do so much. And as the days grew darker, and the hope of a story began to die more and more, I tried harder, but was only beating my head upon a brick wall. My resources were taken away one by one, my building blocks decayed, changes that could only hinder the RP community were made one by one. Slowly, I came to see that this was AO, this was the reality that so many were affraid to face. We were living in a world of unfulfilled promises, broken plot lines, and deception. And I had been party to it, blindly.

So here I stand, in my final act, before the curtain falls, before my spot light fades, and before the seats are empty and the only sound one hears is that of the eternal hum beneath Omni grounds. Here I am saying to all of you, the RP community, all of those I have interacted with, and met, and become friends with, those I have touched, those I have given my devotion to, that I Sid Tisantous Saar, must bow out gracefully, and exit the stage. For one can not act in a role that has no foundation, no depth, no inspiration. It once did, but that was in the good ol' days. I am not a negative person, I always kept my heart going for the sake of the revival of the story, but I and all of you know, that this is not to be. So I bow out, and exit stage left. My final line being:

"Shame to you Funcom, your deception to your loyal fan base will prove to be the most damaging in the long run. I gave you my all, and you took it with disregard. So you now lose me, one of your previously faithful and devoted role players and supporter of the never to be story. A fate you brought upon yourself."

I wish you all well, and may you live in BETTER times.

-Sid Tisantous Saar *slowly backs off stage, curtain falls, fade to black*
