Well, you don't understand nano technology very well, then. It can't make an island fly. For starters, it needs material to feed from to gain energy, and it needs constant reintroduction into the environment, as the bots are used continuously, in themselves having a fairly short lifespan. THey are incapable of providing the massive amounts of thrust pushing against air that would be required to lift a house, much less an island. The energy requirements would be incredibly large. Nanobots work in the "human" body fairly well, by manipulating biological material as they are coded to; to heal injuries, to make injuries, to enhance or disrupt things like neural pathways. In Rubi-Ka times, IIRC, the NCU belt is the key to controlling the bots, and keeping them at a stable state. Massive uses of bots, of course, drains the body of its set supply, needing them to be recharged.

They can't do everything, they can't exist outside the body for longer than it takes them to transfer between bodies at short distances, etc. They could probably form a funnel to propel an object by taking in and rejecting some material at higher velocity, but then, that is what a jet engine does, and it lasts a lot longer.
