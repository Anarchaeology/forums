Maps beyond Elysium are Adv-only. End of game
After all that work and frustration figuring out SL, questing garden keys for Nascense and Elysium, and finally obtaining the +2 rings from Ergo for access to Scheol, I zone in and .... no map. No problem, let's find out how to get one.

So I look at the forums, access the details of A Clear Sense of Scheol from the database, and discover that it's Adventurer-only.

This is the end of SL for me, and it makes me wonder what the point of continuing with AO is at all.

There is only one thing that engies can do pretty well despite the nerfs, and that is to solo. It was never going to be easy to solo in SL, but Gaute assured us that the solo game would be supported just as well as team and full raid play. Never in my worst nightmares did I think that in SL we would be cut off from seeing what mobs are around us on the map, which is the basis of any solo play that doesn't involve the rezz terminal.

Maps were the bedrock on which AO play was founded, and this was not only for solo play but for all forms. The ability to see side colors and machines and your team members and even the lowly direction arrow, it all contributed to making the AO experience enjoyable, and in many cases, possible. And now, after all that investment in Map Nav, the facility is removed from us. Of the many arbitrary restrictions made in SL, this has to be the most blinkered design decision yet.

But back to the narrower issue of soloing. As far as I can see, this is the end of my gaming in SL, since I've only ever even seen one of our org Adv's in SL once in all this time because of the linear format that splits us all apart. Unless I'm wrong about the maps being Adv-only, I cannot see any way forward for my mainly-solo game at all.

Since most of the time you're in SL you probably won't be teamed with an adventurer and since the buffs last only one hour the end result is that the majority of time the player will end up not having mini-maps. From the players perspective all this does is remove one of the valuable features of the game interface. It probably seemed like a cool idea at the time to have adventurers be "keepers of the maps," but it wasn't a very well thought out feature since the end result only degrades the game experience.

A good way to fix it might be to have the adventurer-only map crystals be easily available at a special vendor somewhere, then have versions usable by all professions which can be found with more difficulty in the environment. That way adventurers still have their map advantage but there would be some relief for other professions when they found the maps.

The best solution though would be just to give us good installable maps like we used to have in the old game.
