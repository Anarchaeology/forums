you will only reset the IP spent. (hence IPR -- IP reset).

After you get your IP back, the base skill will still be affected by "trickle-down" from your abilities.

so, let's say you have 200 in "sharp object"

100 is because of your abilities
100 is because of IP you have spent

When you spend an IPR point on this skill, you will get IP back for the 100 points you raised that skill. However, "sharp object" will still be at 100 because of your abilities.
