When it hits the fan...
"Sir...we've lost contact with the subject."

"What? How? I gave you strict orders not to let him out of your sight!"

"I'm sorry, sir. He just...disappeared."

"For gods sake, Agent. Must I do everything myself?"

Only silence answered. General Craddock turned off the vid link in disgust, and turned to the look out the window behind his desk. The sun was just beginning to set behind the Rome city scape, and below the office citizens scurried back and forth, finishing up their buisness for the night. But for InternOps Division 3, their buisness had just begun.

They had been monitoring Bret "Serter" Kenley for a month already. Suspected of criminal activity, despite his official pardon, InternOps admin had considered the man too be a risk, and had ordered two agents to watch him at all times. All fine and good...untill now.

Without warning, Kenley had disappeared. No trace of him was left. No grid messages, no bank deposites, not even a bar tab. It was as if he had never existed. What game are you playing, Kenley,Craddock thought. Something wasn't right.

A blinking light at his computer display interrupted the general's thoughts. He pressed a button, and was greeted by the face of one of his agents.

"Go."

"Sir, we got a lead on Kenley. Word on the street is he grabbed the first shuttle he could and high-tailed it to the Morningstar."

"Good work, Agent. I want you to continue observation, and let me know about any updates. The moment Kenley shows his face, I want to know where, when, and why. Understand?"

"Yes, sir."

Craddock shut off the grid link, and turned back to the city scape. You'll be back, Kenley...and I'll be waiting for you...

Sirs,
As you well know, subject A-49-21 has "dropped beneath our radar", to use an archaic term. Suspected residence is the Morningstar space station, as previously informed. Agents on location there reported an outgoing grid message from "B.S.K.", which we easily identified as the initials of Bret "Serter" Kenley.

What we're not sure of now is if he's still on the station, and if he is, where he's hiding. As I transmit this message, two of my men are searching the shuttle database to try and pinpoint when he arrived.

We will not stop working untill we find Serter.

For Omni-Tek.

***End***

General Craddock deleted the message and returned the small datapad to his belt. Satisfied with the update, he returned to main room in Rompa.

"I'll have a Combat Can, barkeep. Put it on my tab."

"Yes sir," said the opifex behind the counter as he ducked beneath and produced the beverage.

"Thanks," Zack said. He cracked open the can and chugged half the drink. I guess some habits die hard, he thought to himself as he contemplated the choice beverage of Omni-Pol.

Much had happened in only a few days. The sudden onslaught of information had been withering, and so Zack had come here, to the Rompa Bar, to try and relax and reflect on what he knew. Well, we know Serter was in danger of assassination from multiple parties. That would be reason enough to run. Or maybe he knew we were watching him...maybe he's testing the waters, trying to gauge our reaction. Whatever it is, Kenley is doing a damn fine job of staying out of sight.

He finished the beer and left the bar. Pulling his cloak close, he stepped into the chill night air and wandered into the shadows of Omni-1.
