I agree 100%.

I've said it before, Funcom should not be trying to screw the solo player. It seems that everything in this game revolves around teaming.

Team missions - better loot and quicker XP

Outdoor camps - team mission loot and special loot

Loot Mobs (like Nuggets) - some of the best stuff in the game which is near impossible to get without a team or guild support.

Now, if Funcom plans to never allow a solo player to take missions they should at the very least start adding some of the loot to solo missions (either as chest loot or reward).

However, if they are willing to make a change, I would suggest the following change:

Any player may roll a team mission regardless of teamed status. The team mission will have a selection of six random pieces of reward loot. This player may then invite up to 5 other players if he or she chooses and updating will give the mission to all team mates. From then on, it works like any the current team mission setup. If the player does not chose to invite other players, he will only recieve one random reward upon completion of the mission.
