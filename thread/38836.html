Me (tasai) and several others in a team (drgreenton, sjok, specnaz, zienelle and asyzi) in a high level mission together. We progressed to the third level when all of us were booted from the game. Most logged back on to find ourselves in the same mission but not the one we were in. This is because all of the monsters respawned. Two of us perrished, me and zienelle. Specnaz, was fortunate to find a safe spot. Knowing that this is a technical issue I petitioned. Explained to GM Kharoon that specnaz was in an awkward situation. The only reply was "I can't help".
