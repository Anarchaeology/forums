14.2 Patch Changes
There are a few things that I think should be changed in the game. For one, I think that there should be more variety of clothing. Perhaps adding onto the Miiir Fashion store or creating a new one altogether? Also, I think that access to the upper half (neutral and clan) territories shouldn't be restricted from the people who don't have 75 computer literacy. Perhaps a portal (just like the ones to Rome, 20K, etc.) could be made that leads to Athen, Borealis or Newland. Of course, I'm not asking for a portal to a clan city, but I think that just one portal to just one of these neutral cities would open doors for many people who feel confined in the Omni-Tek territory and really just want to go and see something else.
