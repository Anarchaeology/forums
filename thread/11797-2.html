More Info
I have been testing special arrows with Fion on test and i must say that it is really hard to give any concrete information since they are still really bugged on 13.82. I'll answer what i know tho-

AdvClown

"If bow special attack skill is removed, then what use would I have in raising it? Is it still needed to create those special arrows? Would that mean it became some sort of skill trade to make the special arrows? "

The ql of the spec arrow determines the amount of bow spec atk skill you need. I think the ql 30's i made required somewhere around 100 skill.

Nutbar03

" Can we use a QL1 bow to fire off QL100+ special arrows?
Do the varius types of arrows share the same refresh timer or can you use one of each? "

I believe you prob can use a real low bow, since the arrows themselves are based on the bow spec atk skill. It's really best to think of them as like a MA special. They have a req and cost nano to use, and they have timer before you can re-use them. And like MA specials, you have to wait for that time to expire before you use another spec arrow, even if you want to use a diferent type with a lower timer stat.

OTHER INFO

There are the 4 types of arrows Fion listed, then a type sold in the atks kiosk in stores called "Pestering Gadfly" Unf. this type is bugged right now and has the same reqs for every ql you see. I don't believe you can make this arrow, and it seems to have a taunt quality to it. Cold arrows are ql 60-120 only and Fire are ql 120?-200. An ark made a 120'ish fire and i believe it had a range of 300-900 dmg. Not sure about cold dmg, and i think maybe it might have a root aspect, since dmg isnt listed in its discription. Chemical arrows have 1/2 the dmg of a poison of the same ql but also 1/2 the recharge time. Also i think the recharge on the poison arrows is 4 & 1/2 mins instead of 4.

I have only got these arrows to work 1 day on test, every other day i get a generic message saying i cant use the item. When they were working i tested all 8 bows available in shops. The intersting thing about that was that all "bows" (black icon that act look like a bow when equipped) worked and all crossbows (gray icon) did not. I haven't got any feedback yet on whether this is supposed to be or not.

Finally here are some reqs i got when trying to make arrows before i had the skill -

ql 34 needs 93 AGI and 41 INT
ql 69 needs 189 AGI and 84 INT

pls list any other q's you have and i'll try to provide answers.
