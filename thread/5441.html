Sentinel may not have been as reduculously overpowerd as Knight or Divaad, but there was one seriously overpowering flaw.

It has no weakness.

With every form of armor in use today, there one of the 8 damage types that just isn't protected against. Omni-Elite and Nano armor don't protect against chemicals. Waitt and Flower's Tech don't protect against disease. Obtru Steel Ribbed and Biomech don't protect against radiation. Standard Omni-Pol has defense against chemicals, disease, and radiation, but doesn't protect against either fire or cold. Since Sentinel had strong protection against everything, it was overpowered.

I imgaine Funcom may take the opportunity to rework these three armor types and release them as part of the in-game story. However as is, they had good reason to take them out.
