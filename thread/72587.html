In an ideal world...
you would be right.

Unfortunately the AO world/trade system is not strong enough to support advanacement from anything other than combat (something that has had people banging their heads against a brick wall since the beginning [1]).

So if killing MOBs is the only way to get levels (using a skill doesn't increase the skill unfortunately [1]) then you need to be able to do one of the following:

1) Fight Solo
2) Fight in a team

2) is not always achievable so we need to provide for the ability to solo which means... exactly what you have noticed. Everyone gets the ability to kill.

Most weapons are usable by all professions if they can spare the IP (which is hard until 150+) so most high levels players will have a weapon of some kind to help deal out damage.

So, the core focus on each class then becomes _how_ they kill and what support abilities they provide or have access to.

Docs are the best healers and also get various Debuffs and Damage over Times, MAs are fast and nimble, Enforcers are brute strength and damage soaking, Fixers are masters of grid technology, NTs get Area of Effect nukes and Calms, Crats get AoE calms for crowd control and support bots, Engineers get Droids (Slayerdroids are awesome) and so on.

Originally I was very strongly against allowing all profs to fight on almost equal footing but the more I play the more I agree with it. They are not there yet by a long way but they _are_ gettign there. (whether it is fast enough or not to survive past SWG is another question)
