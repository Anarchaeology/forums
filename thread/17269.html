Getting to steps is going to be a little tricky.

You'll have to go through omni-entertainment.

There's plenty-o-guards there. If you go to the grid there, you'll probably have your best chance, because there's no guards at the grid. When you zone in, you'll just have to try and sneak your way around.

You might want to see if you can find a high lvl person to help "distract" the guards for you, while you make your pass.

Once you get outside the gates, theres one guard that patrols by the gates, so you might have to do some running from him too.

Just follow the road out a little bit and then head off to your left. The coords are either (800x2800), or (2800x800). I forgot which, but i think its the first one of those two.
