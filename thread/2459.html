Rejoice Meta-Physicists, for a new age is at hand!
Rejoice, for a new age is at hand! From the ashes of the legendary Council of Chronos, The Hand of Chronos born!

The Hand of Chronos is devoted to advancing the goals of Meta-Physicists across Rubi-ka I. We are dedicated to presenting a powerful, unified voice for Meta-Physicists, both in-game and on the development boards. To this end, chapters will open on both sides of the conflict:

- The Hand of Chronos guild in Omni-1, headed by Prime Minister Spookmonster
- The Fist of Chronos guild in Athens, headed by President Nerath

The Hand of Chronos will also be the first guild on Rubi-ka to introduce the concept of FELLOWSHIPS. A Fellowship grants you access to guild councils, RP events, and festival events, WITHOUT being an active guild member. This allows our brothers and sisters already in guilds to maintain their bond with us.

The Hand of Chronos also offers impartial arbitration services as an independent third party anywhere on Rubi-Ka I. Please contact your local chapter for fees and schedules.

For more information on either guild chapter, please visit our website at aohoc.tripod.com.

With Mocham's blessings,

Damon 'Spookmonster' Scales
