New bug on pet buffs.
I don't know how many other people have noticed this since the last patch. Anyways in last patch we all know they introduced a new feature where buffs stayed on your pet when you zoned so you didn't have to rebuff every time.

At first i did not notice this but it appeared some of my buffs were timing out randomly on my pet. At first i just attributed it to forgeting to put that buff on the pet at the start of the missions.

To make a long story short. Now when you cast a buff on your pet if it already has that buff running it will say that the nano cast is succesful but it will not reset the timer for the buff as well.

For those who don't get what i mean i'll give example

Buff A lasts 25 minutes Buff B lasts 35 minutes.

You enter mission buff pet up clear mission in 30 minutes and move on to next mission.

you enter second mission mission and rebuff pet again. Because Buff A expired when you recast it the timer goes back to 25 minutes. But because Buff B has not expired you cast the buff and the timer remains the same so in 5 minutes when your in the middle of the mission the buff suddenly wears off when you don't expect it.

Now this definately isn't a game breaking bug but i find it slightly annoying and hope it will get fixed soon. If you are trying this yourself you need to use reflect or damage shields so you can tell when they expire.
