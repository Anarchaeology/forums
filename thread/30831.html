Melee needs help!!!!
ok honestly this is for all the enforcers, adventurers, ma's and whoever else uses melee weapons

The following are very big issues with Melee

Parry-
I dont know how the heck mobs parry sooo much in a fight. I can see an ma and enforcer mob parrying, maybe an mp mob but if a mob is using a GUN it should not be able to parry. Forgive me if im wrong but parrying a hit with a gun would DAMAGE the gun.

For balance issues parrying should only work while weilding melee weapons (and fists/shields) and not work when using a ranged weapon (unless you like replacing your gun with its barrel damaged)

Sneak attack-
Its the hardest special to pull off in the game. Unlike aimed shot we have to be VERY close to use it. And since no class (even the adventurer) can obtain the concealment of an agent it often isn't worth it. In addition since sneak attack damage is determined partly by sneak attack skill our damage PALES in comparision to any aimed shot. the only sneak attack buffs available are a +64 from fixer, +30 from agent and +20 from general. Compare that to an agent with +100ish aimed shot and low light scopes that add 300ish aimed shot and are easy to equip. Likewise the average damage of a weapon with sneak attack pales in comparision to aimed shot. Agents have aimed shot weapons that are 1-500(500) ish. While the BEST 1he weapon with sneak is 95-346(110). Ok the breathing spaces arent bad 1-100(675) ish. But melee energy is unbuffable and in addition its range is even worse than any other weapon. The best 1hb weapon (native alloy) isn't that bad considering its about 1-400(300) and does decent sneak attacks

To balance this the following should be made.
1. Vision enhancers/lls should add to sneak attack like they do aimed shot (vision enhancers could allow people to see weaknesses in the armor to gain more powerfull attacks)
2. Better sneak attack weapons for 1he.
3. Ability to pull off sneak attack in mid combat if the opponent is stunned or possibly as a backstab like attack
4. BUFFS for sneak attack (adventurers have aqua and are the most profficient of the usual melee users (aside from fixers and agents who prefer ranged)

Buffs-
Granted this is a heated debate. Enforcers should get buffs. But so should adventurers. Making these self only is counterproductive compared to the buffs that other ranged classes receive. To make it fair enforcers should gain the 1he, 2he, peircing buffs for use on OTHERS in addition to themselves. while adventurers should gain the 1he, melee energy buffs castable on OTHERS. Aside from the both gaining multi melee buffs etc.

Multiple weapons-
The outrageous multi melee requirements of some weapons with pityfull damage is shocking. If anyone has ever seen a q200 tear blade (i have on in the bank) it only does 60-150ish damage (dont remember the crit) but has a multi melee value of 1800ish!!! and yes thats one thousand eight hundred!!!! Dual weilding must be made more effective. It gives a mediocre gain in damage for the skill cost

other issues such as the cost of the heavy weapons melee skill (cough otherwise known as thrown grappling weapons) being required for a 3.7 weight rider but not being required for heavier weapons as well as dimach being parried 90% of the time now are big issues.
