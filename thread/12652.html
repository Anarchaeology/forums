To give a perspective on the original question (rpg wise):

Omni-Tek is a big, well funded corporation which has dedicated engineers working to improve their armor and weapons 24/7.

Us clanners are the "peasants" of rubi-ka carrying pitch forks for arms and badly woven cloth for armor (figure of speech )

Da thing is, when you play Omni and have that well funded organisation behind you, you have certain advantages. To name just 2 small ones, you get more money for loot at an Omni store (a thing us clanners are left without since the end of the amnesty) and the trade part of the shops have 2 levels (less running around for that implant or cluster). Things you would expect from a large company.

Us clanners have the council of tediousness. They have neither money, nor recourses (and the little they do have they squander in their precious little tower) so they will not develop any clan specific armor. They honestly couldn't be bothered, and if they did, Phillip has em on such a short leesh, they wouldn't dare.

In conclusion: I think the lack of clan specific items adds to the realism of the rpg part of AO, it might not be quite fair, but then again, what in life is?
