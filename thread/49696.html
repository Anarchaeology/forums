The Healing Hands: Looking for dedicated Doctors and Enforcers
The Healing Hands, an organization of neutral Doctors and their Atrox Enforcer protectors, is looking for you!

If you are a young Doc, or even a seasoned one, looking for a place to get together with your colleagues, the Healing Hands might be just for you. Being a Doctor is one of the most difficult, but most rewarding, professions of Rubi-Ka. Endless masses of wounded can be found everywhere from the constant battles that rage across the planet. Dedicated Doctors are needed to help relieve some of this pain and suffering. The Healing Hands can help you to learn about the basic aspects, as well as the intricate ones, of being the best Doctor you can.

The Healing Hands also employ Atrox Enforcers to accompany the Doctors into dangerous situations. These brave Enforcers protect the Docs, putting their own lives on the line for the Doctors sake so that they can administer healing nanos to their patients.

If you are interested in joining or finding out more about The Healing Hands, please feel free to visit our infolink, or contact Electronix4 or Linaa on-planet via comlink.
