Trying my hand with a very short story.
I'm not expecting much, but I figured I'd try my hand at writing something. I'll keep it short.

====================
Shizhima sat there. His hand resting on her head where she sat on the floor next to his chair. He was fond of her and treated her like a daughter.

"Razor... You have to let the old pain go." he said. "Not all men are bad or out to hurt you."

"I know uncle, but everytime I start to feel comfortable with a man the pain comes back and all I can think of them as are filthy, double crossing, backstabbing, self centered Double-Ys." she replied. "There are some good men in DA, and on RK-1 in general, and I've treated them wrongly, but I can't help it."

Shi looked at his neice. "I know you're trying. Maybe if you talk to some of those good men more often and curb your rage the pain will go away."

A tear rolled down her cheek and her voice shook with frusteration. "I do, but then some geneticly deficient dren head comes up and rubs salt into the unhealed wounds."

He took her chin gently in his hand and had her look up at him. "One day you will meet a man who take that pain away. He'll be there through your fits and rages, the calm center in your storm. He will ask you to calm down and say things to sooth you and you will hate him for it... at first. But he will allways be there and eventually you will be able to look at him and not see the pain that made you what you are now."

"How will I know him?" she asked.

"You won't know him immediatly." he smiled. "The realization will come uppon you suddenly."

Razorclevage smiled at the kind old face. "I hope you're right." She looked at her chronometer. "I need to get going. I promised Thyme that I'd drop in on the party that DA's throwing at Babb's. I wonder what's on her mind. She knows I hate those Double-Ys that show up at times."

Shi nodded. "Giver her my regards."

"I will." she said.

Shizhima watched her as she walked down the street in OT and wondered if any man would be willing to put up with her eccentricities long enough to allow her to get past her pain.

*Only time will tell.* he thought as he returned to his appartment leaving the bustle of the streets behind.
====================

Well, there it is. Comment's are welcome. Good or bad.
