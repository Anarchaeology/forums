Shoulder Mounted Cannons and Wrist Blasters!
Making wrist blasters would not require anything special, just some new art work.

They would go in the normal weapon slot but appear to be on your wrist. It would simply run the attack animation for pistols.

I'd like to see a lightning bolt arc out of these things, or a strait laser beam, or maybe a sphere of plasma that appears to home in on it's target. (I am not talking about that crappy lightning on the vektor either. I want to see a convincing bolt arc out and lance at the target, leaving a puff of smoke as it singes the target.)


Sholder cannons would require a little more effort, but they wouldn't have to be too complicated. The basically draw a polygon of the gun on your shoulder You could even have the gun pivot to move with the target.

When you start an attack, there will be no animation. You simply stand there and it fires.

If you guys REALLY want to make this cool, you could require us to wear it in the back slot... thus having a shoulder cannon to supplement your weapon. You want to balance it? Well... Make them require heavy weapons, ranged energy, explosives... and all kinds of crap... and don't give them specials. That makes them simply a little extra damage.
