Sea of Change (RP)
Hi,

Anyone who is interested in a unique roleplaying experience, please check out Sea of Change .

We offer regular events for roleplayers to particpate in. We also run our own independant storyline to encourage roleplaying and add a sense of immersion to your time on Rubi-Ka. All members are roleplayers and enjoy the spirit of RP.

We encourage events, and hope to make Rubi-Ka 2 a more active place. We want to change Rubi-Ka 2 for the better and leave our mark. Our goals are important to us, and we are looking for people who feel the same.

We have strong leadership, and good activity amoung members. Everyone is extremly helpful, and dedicated to making the planet better with our own activities.

If this interests you, please contact 'Sepentura' on Rubi-Ka 2,
you may also email me at serrephim@hotmail.com .

Thank you for your time, and stay safe.
