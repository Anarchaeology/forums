Here is a soldier TMS/First Aid idea:

1) Change TMS nano lock down duration to be equal to the duration of the TMS plus 30 seconds.

2) Significantly decrease (e.g. from 20 seconds to 1 second) the recharge time on TMS.

3) Modify TMS so that it has a percentage chance to both deflect hostile nanos such as debuffs and roots and reflect such nano programs (would need to carefully determine those percentages to prevent them from being overpowering).
