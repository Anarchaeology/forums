Proposal for Metaphysic Transference
Proposal for Metaphysic Transference
==========================

Here's a proposal for another defensive measures idea for the MP.
  • Emotional Void ql 100
    nano cost: 2000 nano point
    requirement:
    title lvl 4
    T&S:500
    MM:500
    PM:500
    attack: 2 sec.
    recharge: 20 sec.
    duration: 30 sec.
    effect:
    add concelament of 400
    add all evade 400
    add nanoresist 400
    run speed -2000
    and stun for 25 sec.

    Description: With great self-control the MP is able to empty his mind completely of emotions... through the power of meditation the MP is able to phase himself onto a metaphysical plane. Any movement, command or action other than using conceal will break his meditation.

    Explanation: Even when the meditation is broken his state of semi-meditation cause his slowness.

    Emotional Oblivion ql 150

    nano cost: 3500 nano point
    requirement:
    title lvl 5

    PM:800

    recharge: 20 sec.
