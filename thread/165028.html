a clanalizer is an item that started dropping in the 14.9 patch. There are a couple different uses for them. First, you can use one on the lvl 3 dodga shoulderpads to increase them one more time. Also you can combine them with carbonum armor to make storm carbonum armor, this adds max nano I believe. I think anarchyarcanum.com has a guide on these so you might wanna check it out. You can find them in QL 50+ missions, although they are sold quite frequently on the market boards and in game.

As for a dictionary...there really isnt one, you just gotta play and when someone says something and you don't know what it means, ask. As for your examples-

mocham- MPs buffs that increase nano skills by 140

twinking- getting characters into gear much above the lvl they would normally be able to get for themselves. An example is getting QL 100 imps in a lvl 20 character (never seen this, just an example).

any other questions, just ask
