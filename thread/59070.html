I really feel that the entire shop interface needs a massive overhaul. The existing shops are so frustrating. Alphabetical order is not the way to do this. It's a mess, plain and simple. Things should be grouped together logically. All shotguns should be together, all ammunition should be together, etc. Maybe even arranged by QL.

I'd love to go to a shop terminal and just ask to look at the shotguns, not have to remember that certain types are rusty, cast-off, over tuned, etc.

Running around Omni looking for one item of a particular QL in a shop is not my idea of a fun afternoon. This is the future after all, shops should have a wider selection, and people should be able to make nearly everything available in the shops.
