Just remove GA from the game. The fact that it's in at all borks fixer balance - it's really powerful, so the fixer class has to be balanced with it in mind. However, most fixers won't ever get it, resulting in a situation where they have to be weaker because otherwise the GA fixers would be too powerful.

Thesis: Fixers with GA are a hell of a lot better than fixers without. Hence, to make up for this fact, FC buffs up normal fixers.

Problem: GA fixers still get the benefits from being conventional fixers *and* the benefits of GA, resulting in something even more powerful and the end result is the same between non-GA and GA fixers. The only thing is that balance relative to other profs is more borked (though admittedly less than it used to be).

Solutions:
A) Find a way to equalize GA fixers vs. non-GA. Perhaps introduce some nifty little items that improve a fixers' combat ability but can't be worn with social armour. Adding stuff that buffs our non-combat stuff, like trades and B&E, would be a waste since GA fixers could just take off the GA for a couple minutes while they needed the non-social item. Alternatively, nerf GA, but this is a weak solution.

B) Remove GA. It creates more enmity, camp-fests, obscene price gouging, and forum bickering than any other fixer-related issue. It shoots balance to hell and makes the non-GA fixers feel less useful. Once it's gone, the fixer prof can be balanced like the rest of the classes, instead of having this big GA issue weighing down on it.

My guesses as to FC's thinking:
1) Fixers are (apparently - I've never seen a non-fixer outkite me) the best kiting class. Since when kiting, it's unlikely you're going to get hit anyhow unless you screw up, the benefits of GA aren't as tremendous. However, I think I'm one of the only fixers that actually does kite. Group xp is going to be at least twice as good at all times, so it's best to do for loot and a little xp. Not a popular combination given the way the game is now.
2) Mobs yet to be added will have ways to deal with GA. Perhaps something like the anti-TMS proc many dynabosses have; something that drops Add All Def. by a huge amount, but caps out on the lowest it can drop you to at a Def. level that's higher than anyone without GA will ever reach. Complicated but I think it would work. Unless damn near all mobs are given this, though, I don't see it having the desired effect.

It's OK to have something that's vertically much more powerful and rare than the normal stuff a prof gets. I can live with that, hell, I'd encourage it - the best stuff should go to the players with the most time/resources/biggest org/most 1337 skillz/whatever. However, GA isn't just a vertical improvement - it's a huge horizontal one. It changes the role of the class and gives them an ability that they don't have. It's not a non-combat thing either, like the Shape PPPEs that Engies can get. It directly affects combat class balance.
