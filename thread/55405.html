Social Anarchy
AO needs to have a lot more social content.. a few things including Furniture, Apartments, Guild Houses, Clothing and Vehicles. It would be great if we could have RK Car Shows with different vehicle types being different colors. You need furniture you can interact with in you apartment.. like if you go to a chair you would sit down.. or if you had a bed you could lay down. More clothing would give people a wider variety of how they want to look. They could make a Bronto Burger restaraunt or something where an npc takes your order and brings you food. An actual restaraunt.. They could also have some kind of stadium where you could watch an event. They could have a slow river cruise up and down Stret River. People should have a reason to go to zones that don't get any attention.. They also need to make a subway in Tir! That would be cool. How much better could this game be. None of this is probably ever going to happen.. but I'll keep dreaming Won't you?
