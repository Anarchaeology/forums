News Report: Yalmaha Manufactering shortage!
Today Yalmahas R Us reported a great shortage in it's shop, the shop sells the world's largest supply of Yalmahas and even sends out the Yalmahas to other shops in the galaxy from it's warehouse on Rimus Galaxy Sector 9. The store recieves millions of Yalmahas a day and takes them into the store and sells them to people who come in or order and then ship off the rest to people and shops around the Universe

The owner of the store said this

quote:
Steve Jenkins
The distrubtion center reported a massive halt in the delivery. We're still trying to contact the makers of the Yalmahas but there is no reply in the corperate headquarters. We will get more Yalmahas as soon has possible, We're sending out the last batch to Rubi-Ka due to the demand for them there.
Yes this is terrible news but atleast you can find the Yalmahas at the shops but don't travel off-world looking for them since Rubi-Ka got the last 5 million Batch.

We arrived at the Yalmaha Warehouse to find it was closed and empty and the Yalmaha's weren't inside.

The entire building was empty so we have two theories: (A) They closed and moved to a new Warehouse in a secret part of the Universe or (B) Their bankrupt.

So we walked around and found the For Sale sign posted outside.

In a recent market report we found Yalmaha's are double the price of sale price. The company's famed Adagas and famed Stilletos and totally famed Yalmaha XL are only avalible on Rubi-Ka due to the company's deal with Omni-Tek. There is a strong supply though.

In other news: The Yalmaha JR is for sale in Zanus Sector 4 since the company who makes Yalmahas are on Hiatus.
