the biggest reason why you will get hit harder at that level by agents is because their rifle/as skills are so far above the reqs of their gun that they start getting really nice bonuses to damage...

as a point of reference, my level 78 agent with 125 implants in and buffed with his own agent buffs (snipers bliss, feline grace, enhanced senses) plus generals (rifle and as expertise) has 658 in rifle and 518 in aimed shot...

rifles:

Premium Stigma (ql 200)
rifle 859
as 430

Premium Disaffiliation (ql 200)
rifle 939
as 470

Tsakachumi PTO-HV6 (ql 175)
rifle 842
as 421

OET Co type 77a Ranee (ql 188)
rifle 794
shotgun 530
as 442


as you can see from the reqs, I can already meet the AS req of any high level sniper out there at level 78...I"ll cap at 678 rifle for title level, but at level 120, I'd be at 778 rifle self-buffed (with the implant upgrade I'd be doing before that, I could easily get into any one of the guns listed above by then with self buffs only (without going FP:trader) so by tht time I got over level 150, my chances for crits greatly improves with my increase in those skills, add to that LMA or UVC and you've got alot of damage rolling down the pipe at you.

if you remember the sound it made and the color/graphic of the tracer coming at you, I could tell you exactly which gun he was using...but in all honesty, it probably wouldn't matter...agents have really high attack ratings and being an NT, they will probably have quite a bit more att rating than you have evade skill...which would account for the fast and hard hitting...

the reason they differ from soldiers is because an agents damage is based 100% on rifle...soldiers are 60% RE and 40% AR (for most guns...) and so their attack rating is actually lower than someone with a pure 100% in one skill for their weapon...
