Halloween Party!!
It's halloween. You've just finished giving away all that candy to the little ones coming to your Jobe apartment door. Most had great little costumes, some told funny stories or sang. It was kinda fun, but not excatly what you look for in entertainment.

What to do now......

..... come to the Red Tigers Halloween Party!!

We are hosting a party to cellebrate this most confusing of days. It will be held on Halloween. That's Oct. 31rst for those of you without calendars Party starts at 10pm EST untill midnight

The place is still to be determined. Blood Room at Reets is going to be the place! If we it gets crowded, we can always move to a bigger room.

Please wear costumes. Go to Miir fashions and see what they have to offer. Make up funny, zanny, scarry or just plain matching outfits

We will once again appeal to GSP for a DJ (they always do such a great job). There wil be prizes awarded for contests(you have to come to know what the contests will be).

I hope to see you all there!!
