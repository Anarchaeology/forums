Actually....
Smilodon was more similar to the Hyena, rather than the Lion. It had a hunched over back, powerful fore-arms.

The thing was a scavenger.

So was T Rex. T-Rex skeletal structures have been analyzed by biomechanical engineers to show that it couldn't possibly run. A real T-Rex trying to run as it would in "Jurassic Park" would fall on its ass & break its neck.

Same thing with the Sabertooth.

Scavengers don't run fast, for the most part. They kick whatever's eating a carcass out of the way, though. So the bonus/disadvantage config on the cat makes sense, unfortunately.
