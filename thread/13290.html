Saving mission slider settings
Ok, this idea just came to me tonight, i searched the forums and couldn't find anything similar so here it is :

I just discovered AOMD, and i LOVE it, but there is still one thing slowing me down on the mission generators - having to reset the sliders EVERY TIME.

We all have our preferred settings (full bad/full money, etc.) and if you're like me, you have to reset those to the same settings over and over every time you generate another mission set, right?

Why not have the mission booths remember the last settings you used so you don't have to redo it every time? It couldn't be that complicated to code this in, and it would be sooo much easier (especially if you get the mouse-pointer lag).

Imagine just having to hit 'Generate Mission' every time without having to set the sliders! (unless you want to try something different)

Somebody please bump this into the next patch! I would think it would be simple to do it on the client side by just setting some flags or something.

(If there is already a way to do this somehow, and i just don't know about it, then yes i will feel like an idiot... but please tell me!)
