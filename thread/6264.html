Mini-guide to the Dodga static mission. Don't read if you want to find out yourself.
-WARNING-
This quest takes a LONG time. And it's just killing, killing, killing during that time. If you can stand that, then please feel free to check out this guide.
-END OF WARNING-

Unless I got everything wrong, this quest is for clannies only. The Omni variant seems to be in Lush Fields, but I have not tried it out. Look for Alvin there.

If you have some corrections or additions, please post them and I'll update after I've confirmed it.


1: Wait until you are above level 55. There are some nasty things there. As long as you avoid them, you should be fairly safe. Don't forget to scan before.

2: Get to Old Athens, by wompa or otherwise, and exit the east gate. Then take the southeastern road and follow it until you zone into Agaean.

3: Look for a place called the rising sun. It's an outpost on top of a round mountain. You can access it by swimming through the waterfall to the southwest. Just follow the river and you'll find it.

4: There is a person called Dodga in a 'hut' in the middle. If he isn't there, wait until he gets back. Seems like he vanishes after someone completes this quest. If he is there, ask him 'Have you seen any strange things?', then 'Vigilant?', then say 'I will hunt robots.'. You'll then get the mission icon. (Note that Dodga might be camped, because he will vanish for 2 hours after someone gets the icon or completes the quest.) NOTE: I have not checked out the keywords after the 13.6 patch, so these might have been changed.

5: Get down from the mountain, and take the northish road till you get back to the gates of Old Athens. This time, take the northeastern road, until you zone into another part of Agaean.

6: Go directly north from the zoneboarder until you reach the Danger Dunes. I've found this place to be the best for this mission. If you find someplace else, please let me know. And people, please share.

7: Once there, look for white dots. They are most likely scooplets or shovlets, which are the things to be mean to. Watch out for anything else. If you find a lone -let, violently take it apart (they are not aggro).

8: From time to time, you will find a NODROP item. There are 8 different ones. Get one of each. You will have to kill a LOT of scoop/shovlets though.

9: Combine all the items in the right order. They do not require any skills, as far as I know. Check the list at the end.

10: Go back to the rising sun and see if Dodga is there. If he is, open a trade with him, and trade the item you made to him. You should get a Dogma's card I.

11: Wait till Dodga comes back again, and give him the card (Try to just click it on him first, then trade...). Note that if 2 persons does this at the same time, only one person will get the reward. You get a normal token, a bravery token and a clan shoulderpad of the su****ious for this.


Things to think about:

- Although scooplets are fairly calm, they will group up on you if you attack one of them. Try to find some that are alone.

- If you find a mob called Desert Eremith, then RUN!!! Look out for Vultures and Anun mobs too.

- Take a screwdriver along. You get a lot of junk to convert.

- The items you need have NODROP and UNIQUE. You can't pick up those you already have, but you can pick up those you've already assembled.

- The last few items takes a LONG time to find. Don't despair.

- You can get these items from certain kinds of robotic mobs. Scooplets and their kin are the best shot, but it also occurs on the A-series, and perhaps some other mobs. Zone does not matter, as long as it is outdoors.


Credits:

Thanks to those who has posted on the boards about this.


List of items and the order they should be assembled:

1. Su****ious Looking Parts of Biotechnology
2. Scrap of Notum
3. Microscopic Pulsating Nano Crystals
4. Bundle of Nano-Tubes
5. Liquid Based Conductors
6. Large and partly fused lump of strange tetrahedral CPU bundles.
7. Ancient Looking Lamp Relay
8. Highly plastic mother-circuit array

These things combines into something really nasty. Check it out for yourself. Most of the later steps comes with a really descriptive message, and there are sepparate graphics for all the products. They did this part right.


- Areas to look in-
The robot 'village' in Athen Shire seems popular, and Agaean is quite okay. Other than that, anything with the proper mobs is okay.
