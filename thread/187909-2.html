Blackpetal lay back on her bed and relaxed, enjoying a precious moment of solitude and quiet. Suddenly, she became aware of a presence in the room with her. She glanced at the foot of the bed and saw Icarus standing, looking at her with cool, glowing eyes.

"What is it, Icky? I told you I wanted to rest. I get little enough of it these days..."

"Yes, master... Does this countermand your instruction to draw attention to grid traffic regarding your former organization and its members?"

"What? No! No...it doesn't. Give me a synopsis."

Blackpetal sat up in bed and stretched. Her mechanical servant continued to stare impassively.

"It appears that Don Carnage is engaged in negotiation for the return of a Vitae Covente member, whom Devil's Advocate is holding currently. They are asking 191,000,000 credits for the girl's safe release."

Blackpetal's eyes grew wide for a moment, and then she blinked and laughed.

"Someone obviously has a sticky '0' key... Hand me my comm."

Icarus rummaged through the clothes Petal had left strewn about the apartment in her haste to get undressed and into bed. Shortly, he returned and handed the communicator to his master.

"Thank you," Petal said, flipping the comm open. "Don, this is Petal. Hostage-taking now, are we? I'm sure this is all a big misunderstanding, but you really shouldn't play games with tensions as high as they are. Just return the girl... I'll reimburse you for the bandages and ammo, alright?"

Blackpetal flipped the comm closed again and tossed it aside. As it clattered to the ground, she lay back down on the bed and sighed sadly to herself.

Don had even made a concession of allowing the VCs to pay just 50 million for the return of the girl and allow the rest to be payed in installments. Nevver thought this was a foolish idea and that they would never see the rest of their money, but she also realized that keeping the girl longer would only amount to more bills since Usia had a voracious appetite for burritos. Better to cut their losses.

Nevver sighed.

If the VCs didn't want to pay the bill, then perhaps Usia could be of some use - "Usia, use!" - by working off the tab as a bartender at Baboons or, if worst came to worse, those freaks at Omni-Med were always willing to pay decently for "live subjects." It was one thing to hand over Cyborgs to the freaks since they had lost their souls long ago, but even Nevver couldn't stomach the idea of handing over a Clanner to those sadists.

A smile pressed upon his lips after hearing a frantic Don talk to him over the com, stating that he was releasing Usia. He further asked for this daughters safety, as he fumbled the words to her name when making his threats known. He further shared his plans with Vixentrox, someone he could trust. Now all he waited was the final confirmation.

"Stand down with the plan, lets wait to see if Don pulls something on us again. If he does... well I'll feel sorry for him." He spoke into his com, encrypted from everyone else. A pause came to his lips as he decision was being questioned. "YES, STAND DOWN! There's no need right now with the pursuit of his daughter... he's scared as is right now, there's no need to go further. I think Don finally realizes that I mean business. I'm not just some new leader he can toy with."

The anxiety only continued to run through his body, he wasn't sure if it was going to work, and he knew they spoke a bit too soon before any action was done. He hated the idea, but if it meant getting his sister Usia back, then it was worth it. His other brothers and sisters rejoiced at the news, thinking that this was all behind them. Zane knew better, nor was he done with Don Carnage at all. If he was going to start striking fear into Omni's heart, it would start with Don and it wouldn't stop.

This victory though wasn't his, entirely, he knew it. There were a few other people he had to thank, a few other people who pledged their support to him. He knew that they made him a bit more afraid, and possibly shocked, because it shocked him. So many people willing to come to Usia's and his aid. Pressing a few buttons he opened his HUD to the public gridboard to add his own personal message.

Fellow friends, clanners, and family members;
I would like to thank you very much for your support in this issue, it has been a blessing to have so many friends willing to back myself, Usia and our family Vitae Covente in the time of need. For those who thinks that we are done, we are not, for those who have pledged help, and assistance, I know I will need it very soon.
I would also like to thank Don Carnage for his return of Usia to the Clan members, and I'm glad that no further incidents were caused from these acts.
Remember my friends, clan members, and family members, have faith, keep the dream alive.

He closed the com sitting back and walked out onto his garden in his Jobe apartment. Gently taking a leaf from one of the plants he placed it into his hand, making a few small marks onto it, and then placed it into the pot of the plant it came from.
