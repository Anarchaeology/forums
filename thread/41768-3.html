Soleet
Perhaps I didn't word it well enough:

What I meant was that for there to be any stability among the clans, a much stronger leader is needed. By stronger, I don't just mean someone who talks big. For a serious attempt at reunification, the clans need someone with an Iron fist who could forge the clans into the Omni-smashers they very well could be.

Unfortunately, 'forging' will probably involve making sure everyone is on the same side and are directing their anger at the same people, whether they like it or not.

You might argue that your new CoA would never do such a thing--but don't be so certain of what you're capable of... it might surprise you.

However, I'm still leaving out the possibility that the CoA might be just and fair, in which case you can expect to have even less success as Radiman has achieved. It took a lifetime for Radiman to earn the level of respect that he has today, what makes you so certain that you can earn that in a few months?

My belief that Radiman is better as leader of the Clans lies in an old saying that goes something like this: better to go with the devil you know, rather than the devil you don't. I've prospered under the relative stability that Ross and Radiman have provided; Yes, there have been difficulties, but I am confident that they can be worked out. Throwing a leader out because of your impatience seems unwise, and could endanger us all.
