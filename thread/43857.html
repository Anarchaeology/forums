Snyyper Bio
No one is really sure how old Snyyper, or "Snyype" is. She was a toddler when she was found sleeping under an old sign that had been beaten up and discarded in the junkyard where the 34-1 Helper had found her.

The 34-1 read the sign with the dirt and grime hiding its true message and saw simply "Botos, sun" As if opening a container with a labled-lid, the robot thought the child beneath the sign was named Botos, Sun. When the sign was located later, the dirt and grime wiped off, it was seen as "Go to sunny Rome"....an old advertising sign pushing vacation-sites. And so, Sun Botos was given a legal name by the custodian she was assigned to. Not that it was a momentous occasion. The custodian was neutral to the war, in fact, used most of her money granted to support the many orphaned children assigned to her for "medicene" in the form of vodka.

Now, as to how Sun got the nick-name of "Snyype" or "Snyyper"....a little street-rat, living by her wits, learned to team with the other "rats" while cruising the markets for food. All it took was one rubber band and a small rock with her sniper-aim to send a merchant chasing her while her team-mates grabbed the food from the distracted merchant's cart.

Cultivating a true-aim and a fast running ability were Snyype's specialty; first from stealing food to stealing information, to finally being employed by the clans. Today she works in the Vox Populi and can be found between missions at the Cup in West Athens, or lounging in any other Java-House she finds near.
