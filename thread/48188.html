Captainspunk is dead! Long live the Punk!
I realize I have not been a resident for all that long but somehow, unknowingly, I have been a walking, talking, chatting, buying, selling, fighting advertisement for debasement. While I was cognizant of the multiple meanings engendered in my moniker, I was woefully unaware that they would cause such so many to loath me. Alas, I have angered the dear people of RK1 and those who lord over us their extensive database authority have shown me no mercy.

Captainspunk is dead! Long live the Punk!

From this day forward I shall be called "The Trader Formerly Known As Captain Spunk." That handily abbreviates to Needname2, which is also quite catchy, if I do say so myself. And I do.

Finally, please let me sat that I am very, very sorry to everyone that I have wronged thus far on RK. (No, I'm not talking to you Sweetfixer.) While I was eventually made aware of the multiple meanings engendered in my moniker, I by no means meant to offend any of you. Apologies all around.

--The Trader Formerly Known As Captain Spunk
