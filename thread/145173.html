Ah memories memories...
I remember one day, i was young ( lvl 20-30 i forgot ), and i was Neutral at this time. I was born in Borealis and after getting my lvl 20, i wanted to explore more country.

After spoke with some people, i discovered for the first time that a city called Newland was neutral too. At this moment, i decided to go to this "new" place.

Using Grid, i was surprised to warp outside the city! *glup* as a newbie i was terrified to be outside and i started to run like a fool to the entrance of the city...Well...

After having made the complete circle of the city,( grmblbl ), i found the entrance and took some time to recover.

"Ok, now it s the time to discover this city...

Bleh, all chars are red... bleh.... where i am..."

After getting loss 4-5 times i was able to locate me in the city, i found shop and reclaim booth, mish booth etc...

" Oh a Whompa ! " i said... to myself, i wouldn t appear to be stupid... i was surprised...

well checking where these can bring me, and i saw Newland desert...

" Oh i guess it s for mission", let s try one to see this part of RB world i thought.

after got my mish at booth, i used the whompa for Newland desert.

"OH!! it s amazing!!!
It s a desert!"
remberering the name on the whompa and i started to blush, so stupid i was...

"Ok, my mish is at N-E-E from my position.

It shouldn t be hard to go to."

I started to run to the mish, following this yellow line on my compass.

After moved to a strange canyon, i felt bad, like a strange odor which make me puke the couscous of the last day...

"What is that F****!!!"

I heard some strange scream, and i saw who was running to me from the canyon.

"/bow
Hello " i did

...

no answer and the guy ran near me without looking at me...

"bleh, no smart this guy"

I was looking him on the sand dune, when something growled behind me...

I turned back...

...

Bleh
There were at least 30 Rhynos front of me!!!!

The last thing i remember is the reclaim booth....
