REVISED Ideas! I swear, it's (almost) my last...
Ok, here's yet another revised conglamoration of my ideas. I'll try and put this neat if possible.

Leet Form
-------------
1) Make the form give evades (+50?), a leet should be hard to hit.
2) Place the Pronouncement of Greatness(Other) nano in a much more accessable place in our list (where it would be with a normal nano line progression).
3) Create a Team version of the nano, also in an appropriate spot.

Reet Form
-------------
1) Make Flurry of Feathers give flight.
2) Remove the Calia's Form: Parrot versions of this spell.

Wolf Form
-------------
1) Give the same + to run speed as it gives now.
2) Have the form give initiatives as well. Perhaps +80, 160, and 240?
3) Have the form increase DMS slightly, maybe 25 to 80 based on QL

Cat Form
------------
1) Increase the damage bonus on the higher QL (a progression of possibly 34, 70, and 110 or so)
2) Increase the critical hit probability to 2, 4, and 6 percent (or thereabout) based on QL
3) Remove the AC bonus
4) Remove the negative runspeed
5) Increase the AMS mod to 25, 50, and 80 based on QL

Pit Lizard Form
-------------------
1) Leave the AC and HP boosts as is
2) Increase DMS mod from -50 to positive 80
3) Increase AMS to positive 80
4) Increase the damage mod to +120 or so
5) Provide a critical damage % bonus of 6
6) Give it the ability to take off and land after X seconds of clicking a button, much like a yalmaha does

AC buff
----------
1) Continute this line to higher QLs
2) Give a maximum bonus of around 600AC (not high, but useable)

Run Buffs
------------
1) Continute this line to higher QLs as well
2) Give bonuses to the new line of perhaps +200 and at most +300 run speed (try and balance it so we don't rival a fixer, but come REASONABLY close with wolf form and runbuff)

Damage Shields
---------------------
1) Make the absorption aspect of the shield affect all types of damage, and refresh the absorption aspect of it every 30-60 seconds.
2) Alternatively (only if 1 is not agreeable!), change the shields to a reflection shield, and get rid of the absorption part entirely, so we can cut damage with them.

Heals
-------
1) Limit the range of the heals a bit, so we don't get a ton of 600pt maximum level heals in a row. Perhaps narrow them to 800-1k heals for top of the line, instead of 600-1200, and try and do this for all heals.

This is really all I want to see. In order of priority...
1) Morphs
2) The rest

Morphs are, in my opinion, the defining spell line(s) of the class. Every class has them. For NT's it's the AOE nukes usually. For Engineers its the bots, for MPs, it's masteries and pets. For us, I think it should be morphs... it's by far and wide, the most unique line we have. I'd love to see all these changes. But if I had to choose from all of them, I'd hands down pick morphs. I want to actually use my morphs and see the difference!

Honestly, I can deal with the "total changes to be made" coming in later. I just want to see morphs fixed ASAP. If they can fix those within a patch or two (the sooner the better), I'll be quite happy, and I think I can easily overlook the other nanos for the time being. Morphs could really make us unique, and good.
