Need ability of create 0-25% mission zones!!!
What really irritates me with the whole apparent attitude of FC to make every zone devoid of any fun except hunting mobs, is that it is now 10x harder to run a "safe" Arena event for my org. I understand that zones with quests in them may need to be a bit safer but give us an alternative, dont just take away all PvP and replace it with quests.

I was told by a GM a while ago that to avoid being ganked in the arena go to a Mayhem zone for PvP. That worked great! We would hold Arena combat for our org in some of the really cool locations in CAV (ie, the Lava Pits, Tree Houses etc) but now, the whole damn zone is 75%!! All lvls of members could easily and safely get there and not worry about mobs.

So, what to do for PvP now. It seems that holding events in the Arena only serves to attract gankers and title farmers to the arena topick off anyone they can. We can enter a completely empty arena and fight for a bit, then the gankers show up. That is simply not fun.

I agree that if you are in the arena you should be ready to fight and not complain when you die, but I also think there should be other options for "Private" PvP events, such as ability to create 0% or 25% mission areas ( I was told by an ARK once that this was possible then told no by another) , more mayhem zones, or something!

PvP in the arena now is a joke, and it is impossible to hold private or even semi-private events without having someone show up to disrupt everything simply because they can! Even if you manage to kill someone who is interfering, chances are they will retuurn with friends and turn a fun event into total chaos.

The ability to set up private combat zones wouldnt hurt anyone and benefit a great many people. There are so many players now that would like to PvP but dont want to be ganked by a lvl 200 who just wanders into the Arena for a quick kill. People not wanting to PvP wouldnt even know there was a mission zone around, and people who wanted to PvP could do so without fear of ganking, or any interferance at all. It would be the perfect slution for org PvP events, or "Underground" fight clubs

Obviously Player created missions were originally intended, so why not implement that with the option to set Suppression lvl and mob number. (ie a toggle for ARENA mission)

Would it be that hard to implement? Would anyone but the gankers be adversely affected?
