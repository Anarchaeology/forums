Unbeatable Opponoents are no fun...
So a friend brought up a good point the other day. She noticed that she had never seen a green named NPC die from a players hand. Sure there were one or two times that a green killed a green. But never from a player. And I thought it over And I've never seen it happen either. Whether it be Omni-Poll, Masked Commando's, or the Son's of Ashes they just don't seem to want to die. The minute they are close they run away and then *poof* disapear. Now please understand I'm not saying these bad fellows should be easy to kill, but currently I don't even see any reson in fighting If I know I'm going to lose.

We (Unity of the Rose) held a party in Omni Ent. Probably not the best idea but we are rebels right... and rebels do crazy things some times. Omni raided and we had to leave. Why.. because if we would have fought we would have all died and neither of the two green OP officers would have been killed. This forces us to not have parties in Omni anymore. Whereas if we had been able to take out those two guards, even if later they had broguht in the heavies and nuked us... we would have had a much better time and would have more Raves in Omni just to piss them off and create more conflict.

I understand that the events team is trying to build up anger and tension amoung the factions. But I just can't be terribly excited about a story line that seems there is no way of affecting. In a pen and paper RPG oftentimes the players will totally F$%& a the GM's story by killing of a main charachter or doing somethign they haden't planned. But you know what.. sometimes those are the best games, because you know that some you did mattered, you changed something, and your not just playing a scripted role.

Before I go on let me make it clear that I very much appreciate the time and effort that the Events Team put into this game for us. Because have been GM in pen and paper RPG's, and I know how difficult it can be to manage 6-8 players, I can't imagine how hard to manage 50-100.

So... consider this feedback and suggestions for future events.

1. Allow a green NPC to die occasionally. I mean.. they have insurance tech also right?... why dont' they fight to the death then instead of running away?

2. Instead of having them go poof right in the middle of a group of players when they disapear have them get in a Yalm and fly away far then go poof when their out of sight. Let some players try to chase them, make it more exciting you know.

3. When and if we can kill these stout foes, have them drop something nifty, not uber neccesarily, just unique. So that those high end players who I hear are bored have something to look forward to in the game.

4. ... The rest I leave to you my fellow players, because lord knows I dont' have all the answers.
