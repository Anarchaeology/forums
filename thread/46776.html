Backyard Suggestion
Well, i remember starting off in AO, and always being misguided (first char was a opifex enforcer who used rifles, stupid agents in FP made me think that was the right choice!) So here are a few suggestions that would really beef up the backyards, its kinda like DAoC, but much better i think.

A. First off, we need a Basic Shop in every backyard, that sells ql 1-10 items, its a must.

B. Static Missions to get a feel for talking with NPCs

C. Make them the same size, you can hunt until around lvl 10 in clan backyards, but only about 7-8 in omni. Increase the size of omni, or decrease the size of clans.

D. More aggresive monsters in backyards, butnot too close to the easy parts.

E. Heres the DAoC part, we have Profession Professionals =]. One building in the backyard, would have 12 NPCs (or ARKs) dressed in armor, and weapons apropriate to what their class should dress like, (i.e. An Atrox, weilding a melee weapon, with str/stam armor) that you can talk to, and they will teach you, what skills are useful for your class, answer questions you have about your class, and help you out in in whatever field you need help in. I know if i had an enforcer to talk to when i was lvl 1, i could have made the right choices =].

I really think that improving the backyards, would encourage more people to play AO, not by much, but i think its worth it, considering there isnt too much programming in this idea that couldnt be done easily.

Thanks guys, please drop feedback.
