"Rahzelk The NanoTech"
It was 21 years ago....All my familly was living in a little house at Athen-Shires. People who knew my father, MrBrumble always said that he was a good man, and the family had never got any problem in the region.

Unfortunately, one night, an A4000 bot, another creation of OmniTek, went crazy and killed the guards of the little town and fire on citizen and on houses. All was in fire in the little Athen Shire, and the A4000 left without being punished. No one know how I survived, but I was close to death when I was found.Mummy and dad died in the fire.

I was burned all over my body, and my face was terribly affected too. Strangely, in a small zone around me, everything was frosted. Specialists recognize that only a psychic power could have saved me and froze the fire surrounding me.

Blinded, with my face deformed, I had to put a mask that he'll never remove. Proofs was establish that I'd got psychic predispositions and I was placed in an Orphelina in Tir, where I was following NanoTechnology class.

Behind my black mask, I could develop my sense skills. Particularly, I develop a great feeling for girls, becomes able to see them "mentaly" !! Regrettably, I was so ugly that most of the time, these girls run away when see me...

When I left the orphelina, I had no precize goal, apart learn more and more from RubiKa, travel, and of course, find a wife to share my life...

I'm always joking and trying to seduce girls. You generally can find me near Newland, my favorite place to meet gals and sell stuffs on the blackmarket. I am an happy quack, roaming in streets of Clan's controlled cities and ready to help my friends when they need me.

Actually, my main work is to make nanocrystals for the Vengeance of the Clan guild, and I hope that i will become the best nanoMaker ever on RubiKa ! It is my real goal ! So if you have a nanodisc to be done, ring me !

I also founded the Fellowship of the LostSouls with my friend Corpsanct to help lonely folks. We gather all lost souls in a peacefull vision of the RubiKa planet, and we try to enjoy the life by becoming usefull to our fellows and do something else than killing, killing, killing....

In some words, you can remember me as the friend of everyone on this planet ! Actually, I am not really a friend for all the A4000 robot series ! sorry for them, anyway, they are made for kill, so no regrets
