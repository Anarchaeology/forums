Tiny things FC could do...
Here just a tiny list of things that FC could do which would be nice but shouldn't be too hard to do. (list in no perticular order)

1. Make the text showing how many treatment kits, etc in your tool bar bigger. Getting hard to read how many i have left at a decent resolution.

2. Give small bonuses for complete sets (from another thred)

3. Allow the text showing how many bullets you have in a stack change after reloading in your tool bar. (If you've placed your bullets on the toolbar to see how many you have left, the picture won't update one you reload)

4. Fix those Omni-tek armors that go on your back and cover up your armor like a suit (make it look like you have omni-pol armor on) so that it doesn't drop after you zone, log or go into a store.

5. Allow Miir shirts, pants, etc to be worn over top of regular armor. I want to be able to customize my look so that I can look good for role playing but still keep all my AC from my armor. Please don't make me wear mismatched armor just because i can't afford it. Ever hear of police wearing bullet proof armor under their uniforms?

6. Allow all the heads (for male or female respectivly) to be avaliable for all breeds. There are plenty of cool faces, but none on the breeds you want.

7. Allow nick names of 3 characters ot less. Allowing some symbols would be nice, even if only simple ones like -, _, +, [, ]

8. Get rid of randomly generated first and last names. ( taken from another thred) If they have to be kept, then let us choose them from a list.

9. Make some items that allow you to change your physical characteristics (height and build). Even if not instant it would be nice. Would be nice to be able to change your physicial proportions especially since you can change yourself into a car, or jump into a grid.

Players could even make themself fat during the holidays or after a big party, then eventually get back to normal after the holiday.

10. It should be harder, but not impossible for an omni-tek person to get to a clan city and vice versa. this could be done by adding additional comp lit. requirements for gridding to a city held by your rivals

11. Allow players to keep trying nicknames after they've got one accepted. Eg) If i get Ryu1313, i should be able to keep chooseing until i hit the down arrow.

12. Not likely to happen but, increase the size of the tool bar. Even if only by 1 or 2 in each row.

13. If a gun has Full auto let all the bullets have a chance of hitting. I've seen guns with 300 ammo (i doubt that specific one has full auto though). IF you firing that many shots why can only a max of 6 hit the target even if your firing dozens and dozens of bullets at the target.

This would probably have to have some working though, has the potential of making some guns really powerful.

14. Make some way to buy X amount of items at once. This would be used in buying ammo. I don't want to be double clicking 100 times just to buy 1000 flamethrower ammo.

15. PLEASE lower the price of arrows even just a tiny bit. I want to start playing a bow MA or adventurer but i can't afford 440 credits for 10! shots

16. Try and make things tradeable straight from your backpack. My doc use to be able to take bloodplasma from his backpack and sell it straight to the shop. Now i have to move it to my inventory then sell it.

17. Make tools like screwdrivers, bio-communicators, lockpicks, etc NOT dissapear from the toolbar if you use them. I find it annoying personally yo have to open my inventory and search for my lock pick every time i come up to a locked chest

18. Allow us to used names that have been abandoned for more then the three months. (Taken from the tread about all those lvl 1 people squatting on names, i've forgotten its title)

19. Make every profession a god. LOL just kidding. couldn't resist

Anyway your either not reading this part or stopped caring around number 6 or 7, so im going to stop now. I have plenty more, but i'd rather be playing then typing so bye. I also appoligies for my english.
