What ARK is
The Advisors of Rubi-Ka is the volunteer program for Anarchy Online that strives to provide 24/7 assistance for all AO inhabitants. The group works closely with Funcom Genetic Manipulators (GMs) to answer petitions, greet new players, conduct weddings and Events, troubleshoot, test and report bugs, moderate the public forums and provide input to better the customer support for players.

The Advisors (ARK) utilize IRC, in game channels, private forums and a variety of tools to assist the community. The team is strongly supported by Funcom and has received recognition for its outstanding organization. Some of the benefits for ARK include an ARK Radio station, longevity and activity rewards, gatherings in game and out, occasional beta testing of new products and a close knit family that has become home to hundreds of fans.

The organization began in early beta of AO and has members who have been a part of the world since the early days.


What ARK is not
ARK is not there to better themselves or their friends or to interfere without cause. ARK do not receive any benefits for their play accounts. ARK cannot provide buffs, money or spoilers or help their own characters/guilds/clans etc from their ARK Characters.

The group does not have set schedules as they are volunteers, but members must meet minimum activity requirements to remain in ARK.

Players with a history of warnings, accounts not in good standing, those under 18 or who are unable to agree to the Non Disclosure Agreement are not eligible to join.


Joining ARK
If you would like to join this group and serve the citizens of Rubi-Ka, please go to : http://ark.funcom.com/Public/InsertARKApp , see if you meet the minimum requirements and apply. Also keep an eye out at the ICC HQ for in game recruiters.

Applications are normally processed within 2 weeks but delays are sometimes inevitable due to high volume. Recruitment has been opened up as the number one player request is to provide more ARK for the community. We seek to add approximately 100 more volunteers to the group over the next two months.
