FYI, I just completed the 1st part of the Alvin quest and am starting on the 2nd.

EDIT: This is on *TEST*. You guys may not be able to start 2nd quest if you're on RK1 or RK2.

For part #1, you combine all the parts as people have mentioned, make sure you have the mission from Alvin, and trade the combined result to him. You get the reward(s), card #1, and the 1st set of pads.

Trade card #1 back to him when he respawns, you get the bugs and the radio. You start out with trash king, target him (find person mission at that point), and when you get a mission complete you plant the bug on him (right click it on him) and then right click the radio on him to pick up your next target.

For me, it was the lacky (easy) and then Electro. Bah. All I know about Electro is that she (?) is supposed to spawn in wailing wastes somewhere.

I've been told that you can't do it while target is in combat, so good luck trying to tag your bot when they're camped.
