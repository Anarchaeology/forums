My final Suggestion...
My Account runs out next week, and thru-out my AO life (in charcter) ive never once voiced my opinions or feelings *ingame* to any of the devs/gms/or arks, because that was my ingame character and it wasnt the place or time, thats when i was having fun.


With my account running out, i can say that i definitly wont be back. Ive seen this game from its shabby beginnings, through the times of decent GM held events, through the times of clubs like Rompa and Reets opening and the growth of RP by the players despite bad conditions.


My suggestion, is that if FC, if you want to see a birthday next year for AO, listen to the players.

This game has gotten itself a reputation for being ignorant towards its playerbase.

On other forums of other MMOG's ive been to, people actually gather and say "wow, you played AO too? im another that knows your pain"


Take someone's bright idea from forums like this (and there are LOTS of implementable ideas from this board alone), and introduce it and PROVE that you listened to at least one player.


Another point. AO, due to it only being on 2 servers, could still offer sometihng the competition doesnt. One continious storyline.

GM Events are much needed, not just a bit of tat that you've written and place in the login handler of an event that never took place ingame.

And another thing, you've claimed to be non-biased, yet ive been a leader and seen that arks/gms aution off an event sometimes to be sponsored by guilds, the person with the most cash, wins.

How is this non-biased gameplay?

Create events in a place where n00bs go, a newbie hunting ground (like the early lush field camps) dont make GM Event characters interact with just the leaders and the lvl 100+ players, let them interact with new lvl 10-20 people, some of which might be new to the game.


And dont just create GM Events (if you ever will bring them back properly) for lagged up servers where 500 people congregate after they've heard an event is gonna happen.
Spring events on people in remote places, so that they can feel important for a few days.



My final part is on the game itself.
I never did and never will like the gameplay as long as the only way to progress is to fight.

Tradeskills, why implement things like wen wens, when half of the weapon ranged and melee weapon creator kits dont work?
instead of putting stuff in and saying "look, another small thing added" (failing to tell them it doesnt work), you should finish what you've put in to begin with.



If you just try on only HALF of these issues, the playerbase will show you their gratitude, ....by renewing their subscription.

At present, the Apartment issue shows FC's contempt down to a T. Since Beta 4, people have been asking for you to fix them, to add more than 10 items, to add furniture and make them PROPER apartments, instead of places that no one goes to or uses. 50-100 identical "Apartment Petition" threads have been seen over the year. You only once mentioned you were working on it, and that was 7 months ago, which obviously was a lie. This is the sort of customer relationship im proving how bad you are at. If you'd simply make a post STATING you have no intention of fixing apartments, then it would save EVERYone a LOT of time fretting and wondering.


10 of my friends havent let their accounts renew, and are leaving AO. Im leaving now as ive said, my account expires next week. I havent been ingame for a week now.
(Drtiny gets the famous award for "last person who spoke to Lev", treasure it next to your "Nyadach bravery of an idiot" award Tiny...) lol


So, in short. Listen to the players and not your wallets.
If you'd took the time to implement apartments (like u promised 3 months into the game) and not by adding MICROPHONES THAT DONT WORK/HAVE NO FUNCTION, then people might be a bit more happy.




I hope this sounded like constructive suggestions based on my experience, i tried my hardest not to run down into a rant, but i think i nearly scraped the barrel
