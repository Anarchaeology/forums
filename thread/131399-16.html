Someone asked what can be done to maintain a healthy culture within the raidbot.

I have to clarify the word culture or people wont understand what im talking about.

Imagine some ants living together in an anthill. These ants might like to eat, sleep, make babies and so on and so forth. Some ants enjoy carrying sticks and stuff too. Together they kindof enjoy doing what they do and their culture ends up beeing pretty good at building anthills.

If your culture is aimed at obtaining loot rather than haveing a good time (you can have a good time in AO without getting loot too) you will create a culture that does nothing to satisfy the long-term NEED for having an enjoyable experience from your time spent playing. Loot in itself yields temporary satisfaction, usually after getting a new piece of loot randomperson Bob will be happy about this new loot for an average of 9 days until he needs new loot to satisfy the hunger for loot. You can create a guild that keeps this hunger for loot at bay using certain well known guild-creation tricks. However I myself always prefered creating a guild that deals with getting loot as beeing just one of the things we do to have a good time together.

Statement: As long as a group of players highest motive is to obtain more loot you will create an "unhealthy" culture within that group of players.

Next topic was someone mentioned that you can award points for killing Clan Control Towers. Thats an interesting idea, hehe. The problem here is that if you would be successful your bot WILL USURP and UNDERMINE the leadership of the already established guilds whose memebrs participate in the bots activities. Why should guildmembers Fred listen more carefully to his guild leader when the actual authority over who gets loot, who lives or dies or who wins or loses really lies within the power of the bot admins?

Is the bot and the loot it can help you get more important than the health of your own guild? The major guilds should beware of allowing their members participate in large-scale bot activities. Omni will benefit more from having a number of healthy guilds than having one humungous bot and a large number of obsolete guilds.
