The Death of the MA(Cosmik Gaute read on)
Hey Cosmik,


Your a great guy and all, you do an excellent job. The problem here is FC(leads) doesn't care about the MA's. Notice the hundreds of MA posts that used to frequent when talking about minimum damage, and the useless special attacks. No neither do I, they are all gone, almost all MA's have lost hope.

What we have here is the great intention of balancing the ma vs the NT. Both almost great classes, both lacking in the same fields. AC's for instance, an ma who stays to his path I.E. ma armor and doesnt uber twink his character with STR STA buffs etc and don, the almight level 80% over equip is stuck with a nearly unbuffable AGI/Sense. The same goes for the NT with their INT/PSY. So by trying to balance these 2 classes against eachother totally, it completely nerfs them verse all other classes. They are the opposites of eachother, but the gimps of all.

MA's supposedly not good in the nanotechnology field, are useless without their buffs..yet have second rate buffs to everyother class. The ONLY good buff for ma's is the crit ones. They are the ONLY buff that sets the ma's truly apart. That's very shameful, especially since a MA without his nano's is fodder to grey cons. I won't even get into the fact that all nano skills are blue and higher. When does the MA profession get anything that sets them apart besides one nano? Why is it almost every profession now has a 'cool' nano except for the MA. Flight, warps, teleporting, gridding, morphs. Yet MA's are stuck with no special nano to get once they reach high level. The best attack they can get in the game(Shen) can be recieved by anyone else as well, and it is a 16 minute charge time.

Let's see our specials can be used by anyone. We have the second worst heals in the game, for a supposed healer. Our dimach which is the equivelent to a burst/fullauto, only goes every 30 minutes. Our damage is capped at minimum unless we are fighting low con greens and have maxxed implants. Forget about high green to yellow. Than you give us weapons that are useless for 120 levels, because if you choose a weapon at a lower level and follow it you loose the ability to raise almost all your nano skills. So without your nanoskills your pretty much dead.

After 3 months of hearing nothing from Gaute or any Higher Up's at Funcom. We have all come to the conclusion that you feel MA's are perfect the way they are. they are 'balanced' now. Yes they are against the NT but not against PvM or anything else for that matter. It's obvious no-one on the DEV team plays a MA. All the MA's of old are pretty much gone to other professions now. That trend will only continue till it is completely weeded out, in my opinion. Why play a profession that is only designed to frustrate you after level 75+.

To me it looks like FunCom has been so busy fixing either professions they play, or trying to create a balance between 2 different professions to realize that there is a whole lot more to it than who can kill who. I've stuck with the MA since day 3 hoping gaute would wake up and smell the coffee. I've watched MA's like kicky, NastyNate, dawnll, among lots of others come and go. All saying the same things, and FunCom never listening. Yet when another profession begs for this nano or this skill, or that ability(warps) etc. funCom bows before the might of the crowd. Yet when it came to the MA's and the Doctors of this game FunCom laughs evily, ever so chaotic like, and walks past them without offering them a meal. For another 3 months will these 2 professions have to deal with this? Agents are complaining now but According to what has been stated about conceal going to be working in pvp etc, agents should be very happy shortly. If the fix is like it is on the map readout that is. visual etc, etc.

In the end Funcoms attention or lack there of WILL result in the death of 2 professions completely. The MA and the Doc, hopefully gaute wakes up and realizes what he's doing before it's to late. Or is it already to late.....
