Kodiak vs Yalm
As a L63 Enf currently saving up for a Yalm I find the Kodiak invaluable. It really speeds up time to mission. Even if I could find a fixer for a buff it would still involve waiting around, and anyway I prefer to be self sufficient.

I have made about 1.6 million in the last few days of playing, some of it was very hard earned. Enf aren't great at sneaking, so I am simultaneously working for my 250 token board (I have 230 currently).

My method is to get identify object missions from a term at Avalon, at about 50% - 60% (0% XP and 100% evil). Any higher and the missions take forever. The missions I pick are The Longest Road, and if you get them at approx 3500,1500 (from memory) they will be in or near Bliss, which is one whompa jump away. The Wailing Wastes is another good spot, but involves a bit more travel.

There are at least two mission spots in Bliss that are only 15-20 mobs, which makes for quick missions. When you find one, keep a note of the co-ords and look for them when next getting missions.

Each mission should net me around 25-30k credits for the reward, and at least the same again in cash from the mobs/chests. I also gain 30-50k XP, although I don't want to level too fast at this stage otherwise I am going to have to upgrade - which I want to save until after I have my shiny new Yalm.

Some may baulk at the prospect of 100 missions to get a Yalm, but from the journeys around RK on foot I am really looking forwards to a bit of siteseeing once I get airbourne.

One last though, it is worth investing in specialised Pill Knowledge implants (head, eye and right hand) and a bio-communicator. It cost me about 60k to get these things, but I have made several hundreds back so far through converting monster parts.
