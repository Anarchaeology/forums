The vehicle sizes were changed because at their fullsize, to put it bluntly, they are just too darn big. At its full size its very easy for a vehicle to block objects that should be easily seen or accessed such as mission terminals, scan terminals, Grid terminals and other objects. Essentially, they tend to clutter in confined or close spaces.

So, General Yalmahas decided to scale down their vehicles, but to also use nano technology to allow customers to fit into their scaled versions (Dr. Who and the TARDIS Technology it was dubbed).

I have been chasing up the resize issue with vehicles (and bracers. Last reports from the Professionals mentioned this), but vehicle size itself has no plans to be changed right now. I shall certainly suggest it though.
