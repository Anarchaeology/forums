PVP Scale
Teaming with a lower level player changes the min-max level of the player teamed with you to the min max level of the highest level player on your team.

level 10 teams with a level 100.

level 10 minimum becomes level 75.

1.) Level 10 will be able to INITIATE PVP with other players level 75 and up.

2.) Level 10 will no longer be able to INITIATE PVP with other players his NORMAL min/max range. No one in his team can do so as well.

3.) He is however still within the level range of other players. If a Level 8 player initiates combat first with the Level 10 player then 'said' Level 8 player becomes fair game for all the team members in Level 10's party regardless of their Level. So the Level 100 can then counter attack the Level 8.

This was changed since launch. Initially a Level 100 teamed with a Level 10 can initiate combat with all players up to Level 10's minimum combat level range.
