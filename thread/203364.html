I've had a friend sort out the refining when I've needed it for blueprints. I'm sure you could pay somebody to do it too if you needed it - just as people get MP friends or pay one to make them their filled Crystals. Where used in a quest Subdued Novictum should drop in the right QL range with reasonable frequency. In general, quest item drop rates are a bit of an issue... but increasing the drop rate on a non-quest item that gets round that isn't really the answer. If it's not dropping enough - then the Novictum drop rate should be changed not really the refiner drop rate.

I'm not sure that there's really anything wrong with building a relatively rare tool into the game that makes a process easier. Over time, the number of people who have the tool ensures that there are usually people around who can be asked to make the item - just as you can find somebody to build implants for you or to build armour for you etc.. Also keep in mind that the refiner is a multi-use item. Once you have it you have it - and it's tradeable too so you can pass it on. Over time, there will be refiners everywhere in the game.

New tools like refiners take some time to be available in large numbers on a dimension. But once they are there - they stay there. So right now, it might be really tough to get a novictum subdued by somebody else for the quest - but in 6 months time you'd be able to ask just about anybody and they'd be able to do it for you.

The difference between being absolutely essential and very, very useful is precisely that. It should be possible to complete quests without the item - but for the service connected to the item to be markettable, it should not be available to every single person too easily. Those two elements should be balanced. Right now it looks like the ability to find the high QL subdued novictum is a bit roo rare. I'd leave the tool as it is.
