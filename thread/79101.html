An open letter to GSP
From the desk of Major McCovery,
Leader of the Desert Winds

GSP

The planet of Rubi-Ka has a young ecology only recently populated by funcom, despite troubles the increase in population has been going apace for almost 2 years. Now YOU of the GSP have opened up the entire planet for bagels and doom with NO THOUGHT for the consequences that excessive bad jokeswill have on the DELICATE balance of life here. A GOLDRUSH for a quick joke has blinded you to the facts, Rubi-Ka is not ready for EXCESSIVE public humour. I ask you in the spirit of HUMANITY slow or even stop the terrible joking before it is TOO LATE.

The Desert Winds have established monitoring stations to PROTECT areas of normal humour and to OBSERVE the process of population increases. If our data tells us Rubi-Ka is spinning out of control we WILL take steps to protect the DELICATE balance here.

We are brothers and sisters of the land UNITED
ONE
INDIVISIBLE
FREE

Rubi-ka for the Rubi-kans

Major McCovery
Leader of the Desert Winds
