1. What makes the NT unique from the other players?

(a.)

As stated by many, the buff which replenish your nano pool is very good and is liked by many, especially by Enforcers and Doc's. I think this buff is good for all levels/situations.

(b.)

The area damage nanos. No other profession comes even near the effectiviness of area damage nanos when used well. For normal hunting with mixed party it is mostly used at relatively low level which is made worse by the fact that there are not too many viable outdoor hunting areas where you could hunt 100+ lvl and still use area nanos or atleast survive the consequences.

Combination of 5 NT and 1 Crat (example) might be quite good though or 6 NT with nullity but ofcourse those kind of parties are quite rare.

The area nanos are also good for titles and taking out many njoobs at once. I said it months ago that I fear the day the 4-5 NT's team up go to some corner of 2HO and start area nuking... nothing will survive that long enough to kill them or simply same party just jumps out of Yalmahas over your exp party and the moment they hit ground they all cast area and blam.. lots of dead bodies.

(c.)

High constant minimum damage. It is a good sight when you hit for that Real Mean Enforcer type char for crappy damage even with insane overequip and crit buffs and there is NT that still hits high 1000+ every few seconds.

In my earlier, somewhat naive vision (NT was my first char) I thought that at the end, there would be super mobs (even exp) that would require NT's since the mob AC would go so high that NT's would be almost only ones doing damage. Later I realized that there is no such mob (atleast not exp ones) since every mob you do for exp dies in less than 20 seconds, usually faster.

(d.)

Versatility. Using every trick of the book, they fare quite well also as a solo profession. This applies to most "caster" types though, atleast to those with root/calm.

2. How they have outdamaged the other players in your group?

Usually they dont except for special cases (Real Mean Enforcer types or when they use area damage nanos).

At low lvl's when area damage nanos are option in "normal" group they outdamage everything for overall damage. They dont get the big and noticeable 2400 damage float but some also notice the 15 x 400's...

At higher levels our 150 NT also fares well (after all his nanos come fast and do good min damage every time) and I dont think he is anyway dimishing our damage potential, especially since with that Ithaca and same crit buff running he is hitting bad ass damage with that too.

I personally would prefer if NT's wouldnt be forced to use weapons to be good but then it would need that ability to be taken away, hence idea or "Orb" or something that takes both hands and raise nano skills/damage would be the solution but as long as he is wielding the same Ithaca I am and using the same crit buff as I am and also have DD nanos... well...

Also I would reserve judgement to after the overequip rules are in effect about how bad or good they are.

3. What strategy do they use in PvP, and how successful was it?

I have died every time to NT that has attacked me. Then again I am not very good PvP'er and I dont use root breaking exploits. Normal tactic has been root + nuke, nuke, nuke, nuke.. dead.

4. If they were able to operate independantly of needing buff for casting from any other class?

Yes they were. Moachas etc are good addition but not necessary. I do ok without and our lvl 150 NT dont really need them either. Sure he takes it if its available but its not like "wait for me as I go to look for moacha".

5. Who actually values an NT in their team and why?

I do for their abilities. Root has saved my ass many times as have the area damage nanos. I also like the nano replenish they offer me. I have to agree though that only _good_ NT's will make the grade, ie who also know when its time to move to give rest of the group time to save your ass. Dont just stand there dumb and die.

I am not sure though if I would take NT (with current crit/overequip balance) over few other professions when doing my "dream team" for high lvl exp.. One combination would maybe 1 enforcer, 4 soldiers and trader... Full pet teams also do well.

For low lvl (under 100) hell yes. I take 5 NT's (preferably twinked solitus ones with good AC/Evades) and 1 Doc or Crat and Area nuke everything
