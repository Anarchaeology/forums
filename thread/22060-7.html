Wow, and to think some of you call me a n00b!?
Expansion? Yes!!

Name the last MMORPG you played that didn't have an expansion. Hell, name the last **good** game you played that didn't have an expansion or a sequel (which is usually just a higher priced expansion). Some of you are obviously new to this whole gaming thing.

Expansions *gasp!* expand on the current game! (who'da thought such a thing?!) They add areas, perks, and yes, even fix bugs that are too complicated (aka too large) for a simple patch. This isn't an "EQ" attitude, it's a MMORPG attitude. It may even be an industry attitude! Minimal work, maximum reward, that's an expansion for ya. It's what keeps the game worth playing years down the road. Here's a free clue, UO has expansions. You think they got the idea from EQ? Umm..yeah, sure.

They are going to make an expansion. They aren't asking for your permission. They are stating their intentions. If you're game experience is so miserable then why the hell are you here? Good god! Go play something you have fun with!

I've never seen a company that stopped patches because they were working on an expansion (staying in the MMORPG area here). They will continue to fix, balance, and nerf just to piss you off even more. The day the patches stop you can quit threatening to quit because the days of the server are already numbered.

Someone mentioned Midgard? Nice page, too bad it's last update was 2001/10/16. Hmmm...apparently not putting too much effort into the project if they can't even update the website in over 6 months.

*P.S. someone mentioned the new graphics vs old graphics. The new item graphics will either be downloaded to everyone through a patch (which I hate since I'm stuck on dial up) or they will show a default graphic for those who don't have the expansion (which I hate since I like to see the new, cooler looking stuff). It's not going to cause a catastrophic meltdown of the server. Most likely they'll have an option for people to avoid the extra load on their computer so a person can stick with the default looks from the release game even if they are playing the expansion.
