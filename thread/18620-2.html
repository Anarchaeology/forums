DAoC Dealt with this well
The original point made about DAoC solving the unique item problem cannot be understated. Their system of one unique item per PC per game period, did wonders for solving the problem of item farming so very prevalent in EQ. Furthermore if a group was grouped and killed an NPC to get a unique item the whole group recieved copies of the item, which further solved the problem of spawn camping! And another thing, any game designer that uses any of Verant's (EQ) tactics to deal with player exploitations and game problems is making a GRAVE mistake. Historically EQ's attempts to implement game balance have only added futher frustration to an already anoying game. (The tactic I am referring to is spreading out the spawn time further and further) but there are many others, i could write a book about all the things I hate about EQ and Im not alone in this one.

Making items no-drop (as silly as it is) is not a terribly bad method of preventing ebay item selling, however I believe DAoCs method is alot better and alot less anoying.

The solution:
Disclaimer: I cannot claim this idea as being original.

Camping sux and we all know it.

The solution is so simple that it seems to have been overlooked.

Remove all of the Unique, Special, Nodrop loot from the game and make all of it Trade-skill only. This would give a much needed boost to tradeskills and turn the dungeons into less of a camp-fest...

However, special care would have to be taken to make sure that the 'intructions' to these items are NOT rare loot or experimentation-only to prevent people from abusing the system similar to the camping problem of today.

Under this system, one could find the materials (common through excedingly rare) and have an item build for him/her.

PS. The economy is fooked, add all nanos/instruction discs to the *solo* mission loot tables. Stop screwing the solo player!
