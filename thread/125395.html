Level Req. on HoT's
I have heard many complaints on this problem, me included. Having gone to many tower battles, I get tired of running into people with huge HoT's.

Most recently my lvl 48 Enforcer (semi-twink) went to a tower battle. At his level he does almost 600 damage a hit PvM (About 300 dmg/hit PvP). I ran into an agent 2 levels lower than me, who had GSF and such a high level hot that I could use Furry of Blows, where I hit about once a second with Legion Scythe. His HoT healed him to full HP every second or two, it was impossible to kill him.

I find this extremely cheap, and I think the best way to fix this would be to put level requirements on HoT's. I have told this to many people and they agree with me 90% of the time. If you have a HoT that you can cast at lvl 50 as a fixer, the target should be level 35 at least (debatable). You should not be able to get a HoT like Nano Cauterization that heals 200hp every couple seconds at lvl 30.....

While I agree that high level buffs on low level players make low level PvP just really stupid, please for the love of god no more level limits on my buffs. It's bad enough when I am in a team of very mixed levels (but within grouping range) and I need to cast my damn NCU buff three times to hit everyone. Think I'd stick to solo'ing if I had to do the same with HoT's (least NCU is a team buff, it's tedious, but I cope).

I think something that would work better, but require alot more coding (thus is likely to never happen):

- First tighten the PvP range to a smaller range. It's rather large right now, and really not sure why FC feels it fair that a level 200 player can attack a level 150 (just as an example).

- Instead of using your characters actual level, we're going to use a newly invented PvP level.

- Your PvP level is dynamic, it will change according to the gear and buffs your using but uses your normal level as a factor.

- Your gear's QL and the QL of the buffs your using are averaged with your current level, this becomes your PvP level.

- PvP level determines who you may or may not attack

So let's say your level 20, your using QL 50 armor and implants. You receive QL 50 buffs. Your PvP level would be set to 42, and that would determine who you could attack and who could attack you.

Doesn't stop twinking, or twink duels (which some people enjoy). It will stop low level players from destroying low level bases witth almost no real effort, and almost no risk. It also alows non-twinks to enjoy pvp without being ruthlessly slaughtered by twinks.

I'm not against twinking, but I am for fair fights (least as fair as they can be made, nothing is perfect).
