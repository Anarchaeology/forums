Soldiers get it pretty bad on rubi-ka at the moment because there arent that many things exclusive to us. Most other proffesions are instantly recognisable but soldiers are sometimes hard to tell (the only thing that gives them away is the nova flow/div9 and prehaps the armour which any other class can use anyway) So I would like to see a new set of body armour (inc coat) for soldiers only.
