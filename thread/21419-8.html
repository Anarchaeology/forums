PvP sucks in this game because there is no purpose behind it. There is absolutely no reason to kill another character.
You make some good points here but I think you contradict yourself some.
quote:

PvP sucks in this game because combatants can kill non-combatants with impunity.
If your complaint is that playerkilling has no lasting consequences to a player's reputation, well, that's sort of true - but it's an unfair assertion because nothing like that is supported in the game. All information with any moral or roleplaying attached is done in chat or out of the game. Titles have no real moral significance.

Having said that, political and mayhem zones are called political and mayhem zones for a reason. I don't think it is reasonable to demand someone assume you are a noncombatant while traveling in such areas.
quote:
PvP sucks in this game because it encourages 1 vs 1 'duels' rather than battles between factions. PvP sucks in this game because the 1 vs 1 PvP 'duels' have led to nerf after nerf after nerf as particular professions complain about the power of other particular professions rather than looking at their 'role' in a true battle.
Damn straight.
quote:
...there is no lasting effect of death beyond some ganker getting a title.
Does not have to be the case. What's to stop you from gaining some experience, gathering some nice loot you don't need, sending out a dare, and running off into the forest?

Certainly you're not going to suffer much for losing loot you don't need, but at least someone else gains.
quote:
...it all comes down to the random number generator deciding who wins.
I'm not qualified to crunch this game's numbers so I'll leave this to someone else, but it really strikes me as a mischaracterization way outside the otherwise sound logic of your post.
quote:
PvP sucks in this game because there is no way to opt out.
Rubi-Ka is divided up in a way that is extremely weighted toward creating total safety and immunity for players who dislike PvP. A rudimentary understanding of the world's geography provides you with total and lifelong safety. There are no mandatory missions, there are no mandatory hunting grounds.

Were the reverse the case - an equivalent policy but favoring the PvP crowd rather than the anti-PvP crowd - I think you and others would claim that this was very unfair.

Why should people who dislike PvP be exempt from it any more often than people who enjoy it can subject those people to it? Massacres, town raids, bandits along the trade route, and that sort of stuff all belong in games like this one. PvP in which all parties are looking for trouble is a duel or clan match, and has NO place in a roleplaying game. (For proof of this look no further than the fact that it has no effect on this one.)

That is why I say you contradict yourself. You want PvP to have purpose and meaningful consequences, but you also want to limit it to involve only people who are out actively looking for a fight. Well let's say Ross did send troops into an area. Why should we care? What will happen if the Clans retain or lose control over it? When your clean, safe, organized CONTEST is over, either it will have been without purpose, or the losing side will feel harm to its mobility, shopping convenience, or some other element people feel they have a god given right to.

"I'm paying $13 a month for this game, why can't I opt out of dying at the ferry ports?"

I'm not saying make everyone an unwilling participant; what I am saying is give everyone a compelling reason to make themselves vulnerable - at their option - and let's have some equality of content for people who like hunting other players and people who like worrying about being hunted.

What if we herded you into little arenas to avoid playerkilling and you had to wait until level 60 to get missions in 75% zones? I'd like to see people who prefer life under suppression required to spend as much time in mayhem zones as people who'd prefer life without it presently need to spend under 75%.
quote:
Then some jerks decided to kill each other over and over and over and over up in MMD last July and all of a sudden the 'PvP Scene' was born.
Jerks why exactly?

Because they fired on the enemy in a political zone? What in the hell were we supposed to mistake the place for? I sure failed to mistake it for anything else, as did nearly everyone else in the game as I recall. The place was infamous, feared, and avoided.

Now it is an arena shaped sort of like Newland City. Thanks for that. :P
quote:
The whole planet should be 75% except when a military exercise is going on. Those who want to go to war would then sign up and head out for battle.
Given all your other premises with which I totally disagree, I think this is a great idea.
quote:
...should...should...fair, equal...should...integrity...should NOT...should...
No offense, but one thing the game does NOT need is even more of the sanctimonious moral outrage that seems to be the sole guiding principle behind the design of both the professions and the political landscape.

constructive criticism
First of all, do you people honestly think funcom will take your opinion into account if you ramble on like mad raving animals with no sense of understanding? I mean come on guys, Funcom is trying to IMPROVE agents, but wants the high end mp's to retain their identity.

I see three things standing in the way of what the average agent wants the be.

1) concealment just doesn't work as it should.... please take a look at it, and I honestly don't think a team conceal buff will work to fix this problem. When a mob is half dead before you get the aimshot off something is wrong, 1200 conceal.

2) Aimshot damage output. In all reality, aimshot requires the most IP to use effectively. Conceal for me is maxed, buffed, and implanted to 1200 without a cloaking device or leet form. It takes a while to get aimshot off on green mobs sometimes....... not to mention reds (sometimes it seems to be easier on higher level mobs than lower ones) Aimshot just doesn't do enough damage for the skill requirements to use it effectively. Aimshot is pretty much agent only considering the outlandish concealment required to get aimshots off: a good way to make agents viable in groups and be good damage dealers would be to improve aimshot damage output. (please make aimshot do 60% max health in pvp after 14.2). We're snipers, let us snipe.

3) Remove false profession from the game. Although I know I will probably get flamed for this, fp will be rather useless after mp pets and ma crit buffs are gone. Agents will have to find some other way to be viable in groups other than giving ma crit buffs or healing with the mp pet. I don't see how any good can come from the FP line of nanos...... all it is going to accomplish is make every other profession mad and take away from the agent's core abilities. The only reason I use fp now is to replace a ma in a group (nobody ever wants an agent), or use my mp heal pet. Take away false profession, and supplement agents with needed nanos and abilities (aimshot/conceal) to fill in the void fp leaves. For instance, agents could get a range decreaser debuff to cast in pvp. (which will just let agents use their range. I understand the range was limited to 40m because of technical issues, at least let us use our range). This would be a great addition to groups also, we could use the range decreaser debuff on ranged mobs reducing them to a melee type, then we could root them..... which would be somewhat helpful to a group.

Agents could and rightfully should be the alter-ego of the rogues of everquest. False profession just doesn't strike me as an ability a stealthy profession should have. Thank you for listening Funcom, some of us out here are still hopeful of improvement. (btw the crit buffs are a good idea, but aimshot improvement, or at least a better consistency to aimshot would help agents become more useful in groups). Please do not think the crit buffs will solve the aimshot problem, it just won't...... aimshot is still not solid enough to be considered a good special, fling alone does about as much as aimshot but without concealment requirements.

p.s. Cosmik, cz, dai-gailean anybody please take this post to mind, I think I speak for most of the agent population when I write this.
