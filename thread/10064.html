Name Assault-class Tank Armor

Description This tank armor has been specially modified by Omni-Pol engineers to provide even more protection than is normally available from tank armor. In addition, the tank armor is able to briefly project a defensive barrier around the wearer that can absorb up to 2,000 points of damage. It takes the armor 40 minutes to recharge once this ability has been used. Special nano sensors on the underside of the armor have been fitted to somewhat lower the penalty for using nano programs whilst the armor is being worn, but it is still difficult and requires additional nano energy.

Name Combat Medic's Light Tank Armor

Description This tank armor has been specially modified for field use by Omni-Med technicians. Much of the bulk material in the tank armor has been removed, lowering the weight of the armor but also reducing the protection provided. However, the removal of so much material was necessary to remove the normal penalty tank armor imposes when it comes to using nano programs. This version not only has no penalty, but has special circuitry that enhances the wearers Biological Metamorphose and Material Metamorphose nano skills, in addition to Treatment and First Aid skills.
