I will refrain from making any potentially derogatory comments (for those have already been posted in the threads ) I will simply give the stats for the item.

Type: Armor
Slot: Head
Requirements: Intelligence below 150, psychic below 124
Modifier: Proj/Melee/Energy AC: 150
Fire/Cold AC: 120
Chem/Rad/Poison AC: 100
Psychology: 20
Runspeed: 25
Psycho Mod: 20
Sense Imp: -20
Intelligence: -50
Psychic: - 35
Description: This mouthpiece receives and interprets all sound waves entering it through your mouth, scrambles them, and plays them back as your voice. Thus, you are rendered incomprehensible to all but the most primitive and the most learned peoples of society. Oddly enough, though, those in between seem to be drawn to you.

Whenever the user speaks, every 3rd and 4th letters will be reversed, and every 3rd word has the last letter misplaced. Thus, the sentence "I have problems speaking properly," becomes "I hvae porblme ssepaknig porpelry."

When put on, there should be the floating text "Plz hle pme" should be displayed above your head (floating blue text). The item can be attained from Hompa's corpse (autospawn, like xp finger) or from a new mob in the new Steps of Madness mob, "Inane Jibberer."
