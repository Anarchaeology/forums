I still say the best route would be to attempt to take advantage of the tradeskill system and AO's QL system.

As it stands right now, if I want Decranium armor I have to go to the Smuggler's Den to get it. I, personally, do not want to go to the Smuggler's Den with the way things are currently implemented. But that is the only way to get the armor in any QL level. So Funcom is forcing a choice on me: Go to the Den or Forget the armor. This would be fine if only I had that choice. But it's being forced on the entire playerbase. And regardless of how many people like myself that choose not to go to the Den, enough people will choose to go to the Den. Instant camping situation when there are more people in the Smuggler's Den than the area can support at one time.

Now what would be fine for me is if by hunting different mob types, in different areas, I had enough pieces to make a lower QL piece of Decranium armor than I'd normally find in the Smuggler's Den. But also had collected the pieces to make something similiar (but lesser in QL) to Neleb's Robe, Shades of Lubrication, ect...

This would give incentives to everyone. People that absolutely, positively had to have the absolute best QL of an item would have a place to go and get it. People that want to hunt would have a means to get a version their willing to settle for by varying their hunting locations and collecting the parts. You create a demand for tradeskill capable characters and spread the population out among the world.
