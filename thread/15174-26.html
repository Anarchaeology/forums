The 25% reduction in efficiency on weapons and armour will happen for each step of 20% that you over-equip.

E.g. having a weapon that require 23% higher skill than your current skills will reduce its damage by 25%, and having one that require 43% higher skills will reduce it with 50%.

To which I say:

The math Cz described says that any player who overequips a weapon that is 80% higher than his normal stats will find that the weapon becomes useless until he gets rebuffed.

This is a good thing, because only a complete fool would overequip a weapon or create a 'bot by that large a percentage. Now the same rule applies to everyone ... and that's the important thing about a game.

An analogous situation occurs whenever an NT character boosts his/her MatCreat skill by too much. Sure, the NT can get more powerful DD nano programs to kill tougher MOBs and NPCs more quickly, but ... the nanomage breed has no HPs so he/she dies as soon as he/she gets hit, and all breeds run out of nano pool if the wee beastie is just a tad too tough.

There IS a balance between HPs, nano pool, and MatCreat for NT characters ... the problem for NTs is finding it.

... Oh, and it will not affect belts, belt components and implants.

it wasnt mentioned that mobs were going to see any changes to make them easier, since we will no longer be able to hit as hard and they are too difficult as they are (or annoying with the questionable AI)

In Gaute's original article about over-equipping, Gaute stated that the HPs, AC, and resists for MOBs and NPCs would be changed at the same time the over-equipping fix went in ... or shortly thereafter.

In other words, there will be some screw-ups and imbalances between players and NPCs beginning with patch 14.2; however, Funcom should fix them as soon as we inform them about the problem ... whatever it may be.

Why, you ask? Because it's in Funcom's best interest to do so. Even Verant/Sony is realizing that AO is becoming a tougher competitor for their EQ game ... heck, Verant has gone so far as to put in "special" (read Ancient) DD spells for their wizard class just to keep up with the AO nano-technician profession.
