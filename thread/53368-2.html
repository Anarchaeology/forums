Well, I have posted something about the Tractor-Beam-0-Matic elsewhere, and my conclusion:

It sucks! Why?

Because I'm a damn explorer. A good percentage of the reason I play this game is to explore!

I am not hunting these damn things, and if I was, I wouldn't be flying around in my Yalmaha trying to size up "the perfect shot." Warping has, in essence, prevented me from getting half decent shots of anything new or interesting since 14.5. This is unacceptable, but since there's no way the animal AI is going to differentiate between a threat and a non-threat, I'm sure this will fall on deaf ears.

I shouldn't need any damn strategy when all I'm trying to do is take a picture of the animal in question. It should simply be: line up a good shot, take the picture. Now, if I stop to line up the shot, I get warped.

I understand that it adds the need for a strategy to fighting the bosses, but my point is: I'M NOT FIGHTING THEM. This is typical of the blanket solutions that get applied, and I for one hate it.

Just my 2 cents. I'm sure it isn't going away, and I'm not one for FC bashing, since I think they've done great with a lot of other things. But, simply, I want to explore, and thus, for me, this is a great, big, fat nerf.
