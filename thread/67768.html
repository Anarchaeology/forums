Maxtor's rules for KSing:
1. you have 5 seconds to stop attacking after the mob/npc runs to the other person (that is, they shot first, not outdamaged after 30 seconds)
2. A line is drawn equidistant (or; a line is drawn between the 2 players/groups and another line is drawn perpindicular to it at its midpoint. They are the same thing) from the two players or groups if hunting. Everything on one side of the line can be killed by one team, everything on the other side can be killed by the other team. Adds do not count as long as the mob first attacked is on the correct side of the line (as adds could have been hit by the other group and bring the mob/npc from the other side of the line with them).
3: If it is a rare and spawns often (Neleb-like);first come, first serve
4: if it is rare and spawns rarely: try to negotiate a deal or set a system. If it is like the lab director; the person who can use the item gets a boost in priority because they can use it.

There, I might update this sometime.
