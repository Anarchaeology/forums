Soleet
actually, both boosted and symbio grafts just have "hacked" in front of them when you remove prof restrictions. the difference is that boosted grafts have a base recharge of 30 min and symbio have base recharge of an hour. so boosted are much better depending on how often you want to use the graft. also, the higher the ql, the shorter the recharge. so a ql200 symbio graft will have something like a 40-45min recharge compared to a ql1 symbio graft which would have almost the full hour recharge. a ql200 boosted graft would have a REALLY short recharge.

the skills that are locked don't seem to matter except for t&s. i can't think of any other items that lock nano skills off the top of my head.

also remember that a hacked graft will sometimes just not work. that's part of the price of it being hacked. you'll see yourself wave your hands in the air and nothing happens. it seems to happen quite randomly. my own hacked grafts will malfunction something like once every 3-4 times. your own experiences will vary.
