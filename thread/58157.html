A Fixer at work
Tapping the terminal with his finger, Enerbias was pleased to see the flicker of static replace itself with the message boards of a certain security firm. He sat back smugly, placing his hands behind his head as he read the scrolling messages. Suddenly he leant forward, hands dropping to the keyboard as a box flickered on the screen, alerting him to the presence of his name...

"What have we here?"

The light from the terminal flickered across his face as he leant closer, casting shadow across the frown that creased his forehead. He sighed and shook his head as his shoulders visibly slumped. A voice from the shadows behind him broke his gaze and caused him to turn. "What is it honey?"
He span slowly in his chair to face the voice...

"Oh...nothing I can't handle Princess, she's just trying to draw attention to herself, but pretty soon I'm sure she'll have all the attention she can handle."

He winked at the darkness and smiled before spinning to face the terminal again...

"Well, two can play at her game..."

He chuckled as he attached his comlink to the terminal and downloaded a gridfeed as the voice behind him spoke again. "What are you doing?"

"She wants people to view the thread on the Neutral boards...I'm just giving her a hand."

Pulling the comlink free, he held it close to his face and removed the back before placing it delicately onto the table. He bent over, rummaging around a bag that lay at his feet, and pulled free a long metallic rod. Probing the innards of the comlink carefully with the rod, there was a sudden shower of sparks that settled quickly.

"There...that should do it."

Picking up the comlink again, he smiled, turned a dial, and transmitted the gridfeed to any who would care to listen. It simply read:
http://pub64.ezboard.com/fneutralsof...picID=85.topic
"Come to bed honey"

Enerbias grinned. He could deny his wife no longer, for she meant more to him than the squabbles of a company that had taken exception to his words. The updates to the information he was displaying via a hacked bank terminal would have to wait.

Doubts
It was the next day, and Enerbias leant back from the terminal somewhat uncomfortable, for a new message had appeared in the Neut forum from a member of the company he had doubts over. A man who went by the name of AlteredState, whom he had met on several occasions. Despite the obvious tension of such encounters, Enerbias could not deny he had respect for the fellow, for he seemed to be the only one who had spoken to him in a reasonable manner and understood the reasoning of his actions. He sighed heavily, wishing that his wife was around to wash away his woes with her voice.

He did not like the dilemma this put him in. Of all those that worked within the company, AlteredState was the only one to whom he felt any debt of civilness. As he read the message again, he found himself wishing he had seen it earlier, for he grew weary of watching his back, and would welcome an opportunity to cease in his investigations, yet the problem remained.

He rose from his chair and leaned over the terminal, swapping some of the leads that fed into the back. The screen flickered momentarily as code danced across the screen, bathing him in shades of green that scrolled across his chest as he sat back down. He pulled the keyboard closer and started responding to the various password requests that addressed him, until eventually, he had worked his way back into the mainframe of a bank terminal that displayed certain information planted there by himself not a week previously. His hand hovered for a moment as he stared at the screen. Should he delete it? It would seem that AlteredState was giving him an opportunity to drop his case and return to a hassle free life, but he could not shake the doubts that swamped his head, it was, after all, not Altered that posed the problem, but his boss, who had already proved that she could not be trusted.

He cast his hand asside, unable to make up his mind, and rose from his chair as he switched off the terminal. It occured to him that perhaps he should inform the man first of the banks contents. But would he be believed?

Too many questions, and not enough answers. With a sigh he strode to the door, eager for some fresh air.

Old Bio (No longer in IR)
*OOC - For those that have started looking into me, this tape of an old conversation may have come to light...just thought it would be nice for some to understand my make-up *

"I never really knew my mother, she passed away when I was very young. There had been some mix up with reclaim that meant her DNA was forever lost according to my Father...he never really elaborated on it...all I really know is that he missed her dearly, spending many years scouring the DNA records in the memory buffers without success. He was a bit of a computer whiz my Father, which I suppose hit him all the harder what with being unable to find any trace of my Mother. He was much in demand from people.... so many strange faces I recall, and all of them jumpy...*laughs*...I didn't know it back then, but he was in contact with a group of Fixers who specialised in criminal activities. It was the only way he could make enough money to support us both, particularly as Omni had refused to pay out on my Mothers pension, as there was no record of her DNA. With no record of her in reclaim, they claimed that she was simply missing and not dead. It hit my father very hard, and left me feeling very bitter toward the corporation. He raised me as best he could, illegally downloading education packs from the Grid, and teaching me to respect others...
He was very particular about that...*smiles*
It's as if he was going out of his way to ensure I had manners. "Enerbias..." he would say..."It's important to have a female perspective on life if you ever want to get anywhere...women are privy to many more secrets than men, and there is nothing a woman appreciates more than good manners and a gentle hand."
*Chuckles lightly*
I was fascinated by his work, and often watched him, much to the annoyance of his customers. They had a very low tolerance of me at first, with my manners and polite nature being the subject of much ridicule among them. My Father scolded them for it, and eventually, they realised I could be a future asset to them, and so they began to teach me things...behind my Fathers' back. *Looks down, as if recalling a bad memory*
I...I started to do things....to bring in extra money, so that my Father might get his life back...I...started tampering with records...using techniques taught to me by other Fixers...but my actions were traced, and the Omni corporation sent some heavies round when they had traced my hacking. When they stormed the apartment, they overlooked the boy and arrested my Father...*Falls silent for a moment and swallows*.
My Father feared I would be taken to the mines, and knew I would not survive such work, so arranged for his contacts to whisk me away to the Grid. From there I emerged only at night to steal food and a little money, but it soon became obvious that this was no way to live. Several years passed, until eventually, I was able to hack into the mainframe from within the Grid. Deleting my records I proceeded to acquire an apartment illegally under the name of a deceased citizen, who had failed to reach reclaim in a manner presumably similar to that of my Mother. I re-activated his records with my own DNA structure, and set out for the real world once more. My nickname, "Enerbias", is in fact what remains of my real name.
Upon emerging from the Grid, I discovered my Father had died in custody a year previously, beaten for a confession, and denied reclaim for reasons unknown...
I came out a very bitter young man, hateful of Omni, yet despising the terrorist actions of the Clans who I saw as encouraging the very policies within Omni that had made our lives so difficult in the first place. I also blamed myself for my Fathers death, and vowed never to involve myself with the darker side of crime...I owed it to my Father to make something of my life...
It was several years before I became comfortable with making friends again, and was fortunate enough to make Destractor the first of them. His jovial and helpful nature re-kindled my faith in people, and I soon found myself yearning for the companionship I had missed so much while confined to the Grid, so I made inquiries into several guilds. I particularly liked the fact that the Independent Rubikans fought for the rights of Neutral citizens and guards, so attended a meeting to see what they had to offer. After just one meeting it was an easy decision to make *Smiles*, for they had high social values that appealed to me greatly. Soon after Destractor joined too, and IR quickly became the family I had longed for. Funnily enough, it was around that time that I met Sharonne. We bonded very well, never realising that love would blossom. *Smiles fondly while feeling a ring upon his finger*
I quickly made a name for myself with my eagerness to encourage Neutrality among the new arrivals, as I was eager to prevent the escalating hostilities on Rubi Ka. I spent many nights scouring the backyards for those in need, taking pity on those I saw struggling in much the same way I had when I had emerged from the Grid. Indeed, for a while it seemed I was cursed by my generosity *Chuckles*, I even ended up making regular donations to a young lady who had fallen on unscrupulous work in an effort to support her family off world. Her name? *Raises an eyebrow* I do not think I should tell you friend, she is...how should I put this?...a reliable contact for me at the moment. *Winks* She has picked herself up well now and repays my original kindness with information. *Brushes dust from his knee*
Alas, from the many acquaintances I made from so many factions, it soon became evident that there was much corruption within the world, some of which seemed to point to the guild itself. I grew fearful for the many friends I had made, and decided to investigate. I chose to leave IR so as to aid in my investigation of the possible corruption within, but my inquiries led to an interrogation by Omni personnel, alienating me from them even further. *Frowns* As is often the case with rumours, my fears for IR turned out to be just that, rumours, but I felt unworthy of re-joining them, despising myself for ever doubting the organisation that had welcomed me with open arms, and so I turned to drink. *Glances sideways, recalling another bad memory*
I was in a sorry state, until Tagorn sat me down one night and sorted me out. *Smiles* I was very lucky to have him as a friend. Shortly after I plucked up the courage to face Silenna, who welcomed me back to what I now regard as home. I shall try not to make the same mistake again, indeed, this is one of the reasons I no longer drink.
Shortly after my renewed membership, I was noticed by an old friend of my fathers who invited me back into the Fixer network. It seemed to make perfect sense at the time. I re-established my Fixer connections in the hope that their global network would be interested in championing the underdog. With such resources at their disposal, they could influence what went on in the world and put an end to all the crazy things that were happening. I know now that it was foolish to think of them as having the same principals as myself, for it soon became evident that corruption was just as rife if not more so within their own organisation. There is a mutual yet fragile trust among us. My polite nature, reputation, and high morals earning respect, yet at the same time filling them with fear. For now at least, I have broken contact with them, not wishing to be associated with the criminals that helped mould me into what I am. *Sighs*
Ironically, one Fixer grew on me over a period of time. Our growing fondness was very subtle at first, which helped make the bonding between us all the stronger, ultimately leading to our marriage. I speak of course of Sharonne, a woman with great strength of character, although she would probably tell you otherwise. *Chuckles* I guess I sensed that there was something about her that needed my help and patience, but I digress *Waves hand in shooing motion* She will tell you herself when she is ready. She is a source of inspiration to me, and to be honest, I'm not sure I could get by without her *Smiles fondly*, this is why I chose to adopt her surname in our ceremony, through fear of my own illegal name voiding the marriage. I am very much in love with her. *Blushes slightly*. It hasn't been without its ups and downs, but the fact is, we survived them and came out all the stronger, undoubtedly helped by the many friends we have made within IR, such as Brigman, Yarko, and DrTiny to name but a few.
*Comlink bleeps*
I'm sorry, you must excuse me, my contact is coming through..."
*Bows and leaves*

More hacking
Enerbias checked his wrist scanner, and was happy that the coast was clear. Gingerly, he stepped out from behind the bank terminal and looked around nervously, after all, scanners had been know to suffer from the occasional glitch. Content that no one was around, he logged on to test the relay circuits. The bank hummed into life and displayed his posessions as he would expect. He logged off and on again....and again....and again. A frown cut deep into his forehead as it continued displaying the same objects. Would it work he wondered? It was, after all, a very complicated piece of kit he had placed inside. As with all the other banks he had visited that night, it was very discreet, designed to pick up the signal randomly, so that one could never be sure which bank terminal would display the information. He had designed it this way, as now that the IR news had coverage that a bank terminal had indeed been hacked, he wanted to make sure that it would be reasonably difficult to trace which terminal would next display the information, thus making it virtually impossible to trace where the signal was coming from. Now that he had fitted most of the banks with the tiny device, it would randomly display around the globe, ensuring maximum coverage while keeping the chance of the transmission being ceased to a minimum.

The terminal display flickered with static. He chuckled lightly to himself as it announced that the screen contents were from an unknown source...

"Bingo!"

http://www.quillmaster.pwp.blueyonde.../Enerbias.html

He snapped his head quickly from side to side before returning his gaze to the screen. He logged off and chuckled, secretly amused that even he did not know where it would transmit to next. With a sigh he turned. The heat would undoubtedly mount now, and it would make sense if he laid low for a while. Such an action would no doubt displease his wife, but he grew tiresome of the war-mongering activities on the planet and could remain silent no longer. Checking his belt, he activated his run buff program, and fled into the night.

Turning Legit
The rain pattered softly on the window outside as Enerbias waited patiently for the clerk to return. This was a bold move he was making, yet somehow, it made him feel far more relaxed and comfortable with himself. A female voice behind him spoke softly.

"Thank you for this opportunity Mr McKesson. Funds have proved a little hard to come by since my training is no longer required. I think I shall enjoy working for you."

Enerbias tilted his head and smiled at the well dressed woman who stood beside him.

"Glad to hear it Multima, and please, call me Enerbias, it is a name I am far more familiar with."

He turned to face the counter again as he heard footsteps approach. A young woman appeared holding some paperwork. He smiled.

"here you are Mr McKesson. Everything appears to be in order, if you would just sign here, oh, and here, then the company will be registered and you can go about your business."

The woman returned the smile to him as she slid the papers across the counter. He couldn't help but smile wider in response as he delved into his pocket for a pen. He half pulled out a thin metalic cylinder before rapidly returning it and blushing slightly. Multima sensed his embarrassment and strode forward, volunteering a pen of her own. He took it, the blush becoming more evident as Multima spoke.

"What good would I be as your P.A. if I couldn't even hand you a pen?"

She winked at him, causing a stifled chuckle to escape his lips while the clerk simply looked bemused, unaware that Enerbias had nearly pulled free one of the tools of his trade.

"Indeed Multima. Where would I be without you?"

The young woman giggled and raised her hand to her mouth as Enerbias signed the papers. Sliding them back across the counter to the clerk, he turned to Multima and returned the wink. She smiled in response, casting a glance at the clerk as she inspected the signatures. the woman nodded approvingly at the pen strokes before raising her head to address Enerbias.

"And the name of this Detective Agency?"

Enerbias faced the clerk again and smiled, pleased that his work could now rise in status.

"Foresight"

"Very well. You are now permitted to trade and recruit under that name. I wish you every success Mr McKesson."

"Thankyou very much. You must excuse me now, I have business to attend."

"Of course."

The clerk smiled as she tapped the papers lightly on the counter to tidy their appearance. Enerbias turned to Multima, raising his elbow as an invitation to her to loop her arm through his, forever the gentleman.

"I think a business lunch is in order Multima"

Multima beamed happily and accepted his offer. Tucking her arm through his, she followed him back outside, pleased to be working for such a man.

Dark Thoughts
Marsalis glared out of the window at the gathering gloom as night approached, the soft glow of his desk terminal screen causing his own reflection to be an eerie silhouette .

"Damn the man!! I gave him a way out and he didn't take it!"

"As you knew he wouldn't...but you still had to offer it.", his reflection replied.

"Fantastic, now I'm talking to myself."

With a sigh he returned to his desk and sat at the terminal, reviewing, yet again, the reports on Enerbias' recent activities. Activities that everyone on Rubi Ka could read about if they wished. He leaned back from the desk, rubbing his chin with his thumb. So many options, none of which were particulary appealing, what to do? Reaching for his comlink he opened a comline. A voice muttered in his ear.

"This is Altered. Is it ready?.....Good....it took longer than you projected."

The voice muttered with a higher pitch and volume. Marsalis felt a smile tug at his mouth.

"Of course I prefer it being 'done right', and not 'done quick'. Have you tested it?....Excellent, send me the report........no, not yet."

He closed the line with mixed feelings. A smashed terminal lying in the corner of his room caught his eye. A slither of shame slipped through him as he flexed his hand in memory of the pain. Healing left no scars to remind you, it was always good to remember the result of anger and precipitous action. The smashed terminal had been a victim of his rage after the first reports on Enerbias had come through.

A soft sound from behind caused him to turn. Rain whispered gently against the window. Marsalis deactivated the terminal and rose, returning to the window. A storm was approaching.

"More than one."

Marsalis suspected that, with mining deregulation, a storm of violence would descend upon Rubi Ka, the likes of which hadn't been seen since the last civil war. Good for the company, but he feared for the neutral community. He was loyal to the company to the end...but still he worried.

Without the light from the terminal screen, night had turned the window into a dark, black hole. A perfect mirror for the dark thoughts that flowed through his mind.

"It's always darkest just before the dawn.", he muttered softly.

His reflection was no longer there to answer.
