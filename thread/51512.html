Mob AI, Solo & Team missions, etc..
There needs to be changes to the NPCs in missions and how they are spread throughout the mission....

First, NPC mobs should have HP pools that look more like a PC's HP pool. To compensate they should be smart in the tactics they use. For instance, how many MP mobs (the ones with them pretty pink shields) have had a healpet healing them and a demon attacking you? Zero right? Same with NT mobs. I've never seen more than 1 nuke (and only 1/10th of the time do I actually see a nuke) come from an NPC NT. What gives???? And why have I never seen a Crat mob with a ninja droid beside it? Soldier mobs never seem to use their 'alpha strike'..never gotten ASed by an Agent mob, never once seen an Engie mob with a big slayerdroid attacking, etc...

And then they way mobs are dispursed in missions. The main room always has one mob carbon copied 2 or 3 times. How many of us have formed a group comprised of nothing but one profession? Very very rarely, if at all. The distribution of mobs in a mission needs to change. In solo missions you should never have to deal with more than 2 mobs at a time...period. In team missions you should never have the luxury of dealing with any less than 3 mobs at a time comprised of different professions where each mob in the team uses their unique abilities to the fullest, just like we would.

And then the layouts of the missions themselves. Missions in general should all be the size of your typical BS mission. The layouts? Solo missions should have smaller rooms where you could fit maybe 1 or 2 NPCs per room. Team missions should be all big rooms with each room holding a team of mobs.

Maybe then missions wouldn't be an exercise of patience and tolerance but more one of tactics....
