Bronto saved from a watery grave!
((every so slightly tongue in cheek, its boring guarding towers. and NO THAT IS NOT A HINT hehe))

Whilst on routine patrol around Newland lake, Eldridge McCovery was startled to hear the plaintiff wailing of a babybronto coming from the direction of the lake.

"Was a mournful sound i heard, at first i thought it was the legendary Newssie (the mythical beast said to live in the lake ed.) but when i looked I saw a poor bronto baby floating on a peice of debris some planet hater had dropped in the lake."

Major McCovery was soon joined by Tazze and Gesuss and they tried to push the bronto to shore but it was to no avail. Alas thw bronto was out of range of a graple gun and could not be towed from sure. Fikksit tried to use his yalm to pull the bronto to shore. Air-sea rescue was eventually called in and the bronto was airlifted to safety using a special harness attached to a Yalmaha. A local vet checked the bronot and proclaimed him in good shape for his watery adventure and the bronto was released back into the wilds.
