Mantis Threat Examined
independent newsfeed originating from the Neutral Territories
Borealis 16th August

The Mantis is a well-documented breed that inhabits Rubi-Ka. For some months a large hive has been known to stretch from the Southern Foul Hills to below Borealis. This hive has been well studied by many different groups and the behavior of the colony members relatively well understood and documented. However, in the past year Rubi-Ka has undergone various climatic changes and the effects of such changes on the various species inhabiting the planet are unknown. For example a warming of the climate could cause a migration of various species northwards. One indication of that a species may be about to migrate is a change in the composition of egg yolk, an increase in yolk quality leads to a smaller number of stronger progeny more suited to long travel.

Concerned over recent reports regarding the hive a small group of independent citizens from groups as diverse as Desert Winds, Divine Retribution and The Nanomage Liberation Front mounted an exploratory operation into the hive. Their objective being to recover several mantis eggs for further examination. The, squads objective was nothing other than the throne room of the mantis queen herself! Recently a smuggler group operating out of Borealis has been busted by the authorities, examination of their den revealed they had inadvertently broken into the mantis hive.

The group of explorers were assaulted every step of the way into the hive by ferocious mantis intent on defending their hive. The bugs were no match for the modern day weaponry and nanoprograms that the squad were equipped with. After several hours of hard fighting they eventually won through to the throne room and egg chamber of the mantis hive. Here they found the massive Mantis Den Breeders who tend to the eggs laid by Her Majesty the Mantis Queen. In order to safely remove some eggs the Breeders and the Queen had to be subdued, which was no easy task. After an intense subterranean gunfight the Mantis Queen and her attendants were made harmless using the latest in insectoid-anesthetics. Knowing they had to work quickly before the guardians awoke several eggs were recovered from the egg chamber. These were distributed between the participating groups for further analysis.
