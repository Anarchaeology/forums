Dual Wield Question
If I am duel wielding a weapon that has "specials" such as fling and burst...will I be able to hot key the special on each gun for my toolbar? Or will they just shoot straight without any specials that can be used. Or will they both "fling" together when I hit my hotkey?

Example: 2 S.A. Home Defenders shotgun only skill. This one is easy...no specials...hit Q and go.

Example: 2 Pistols with Pistol requirement and 1 or 2 specials. Will I hit Q and fire away? And if so...how will I get 1 or both guns to launch their "special attacks"?

If you can't use "specials" while duel wielding, wouldn't it make more sense to use a weapon with only 1 skill requirement as opposed to 2 or 3...or more?

My observation has been that the special follows the weapon in the normal order the weapon fires in.

Example: Fling in left hand and burst in right hand.

If I hit the hot keys for burst and then fling, and the left hand is coming up to fire, then fling goes off first, with burst going off when the right hand goes off.

If it were the right hand coming up after the hot keys were pressed, then burst would go off and fling would wait until the left hand fired.

There does not seem to be any precedence setting taking place. The specials are applied, as appropriate, to whatever pistol is next in the firing order.

Yes, you will be able to place hot keys for specials on your toolbar. But, you can only activate a special once if you're using two of the same pistols. For instance, if your pistols had Fling as the special and you're dual-wielding them, you only get to activate the Fling once and then have to wait as you normally do using just one pistol. It's better to have the second pistol with a different special like Burst, unless you're lacking in IP's.
