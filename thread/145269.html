Notum Wars Vehicles Costs vs Benefit
Hi,

In Notum Wars beta, I tested the vehicles more than towers. I really enjoyed them, particularly the Mantas.

I have active characters on both Rimor and Atlantean, though most of my time is spent on Rimor, and I do not think I have ever seen a player in a Manta.

My main character is a level 104 clan Doctor, so I do have a little play time in.

I think that you folks at Funcom should take a hard look at the Notum Wars vehicles, and the Mantas in particular, and see if there isn't a way to make them more accessible.

In theory, you can get a QL60 Sportline Manta, but I haven't seen a level 40-60 player willing to spend 16+ million credits for a ground vehicle, especially when a faster, flying yalmaha can be had for a third of the price.

I would love to have a Manta Neo, and arrive at my clan's towers and use the heavy gun to help defend out notum mine.

My suggestions:
Cut the prices to 1/3 of what they are now.
Make the Mantas viable combat vehicles by increasing armor, and lowering penalties.
Lower the skill requirements a small amount so that more of the inhabitants of Rubi-Ka can use the truly fantastic vehicles.

These are just my opinions and suggestions. But I will tell you this: I was truly excited about the Notum Wars, for the vehicles. And as yet, they remain out of my reach. I know only one former clan mate who has a Yalmaha Adaga, and she's a level 165 fixer.

I guess that since you offer a QL60 Manta that level 60 players should have access to them, and currently they don't as the benefits are dramatically outweighed by the costs.

Finally, I want to thank the folks at Funcom. I love this game, and spend as much time as I can here. The ARKs are great also.
