Looking for a Role Playing org?
Tired of running missions? Looking for a Role Playing org?

Then check out Project Revenant. Project Revenant was started by players who wanted to introduce and run their own events and storylines in the abscence of any FC driven story. PR consists of clan, omni and neutral orgs and is always happy to accept new members willing to RP of all levels. All you need is a willingness to have fun and Role Play!

Project Revenant events have been often featured on many of the AO news sites and new events are happening all the time! Wanna kill a few clanners for the glory of Omni-Tek? Wanna track down that Omni spy, or raid an Omni-Pol camp? How about a hired gun, selling your services to the highest bidder? Stop by and check out Project Revenant today!

The current orgs in PR are Division 3 (Omni), Vox Populi (Clan), Shattered Dreams(Neutral) and Rogues Gallery (Neutral). Information on the orgs of PR can be found on our main website (Project Revenant) or by contacting members of these orgs in game.

If you have any questions about joining Project Revenant stop by the website and check it out! (Project Revenant)
