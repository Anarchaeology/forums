Kudos to The Devs for NW
Over the last few days I have partaken of heavy fight at Lucifers Dawns Base, DSA base in PW. And watched with fun the destruction of a redemption Base in dav......who in folly tried to retaliate at a MOK base which reopened their base to more destruction just as it was about to go 100%.

There are glitches, there are bugs niggly to big, Crowd control is still a problem. Zone hugging.

But thru all those glitches etc. I have had a great time....with some massive battles toe to toe with the enemy.

The best so far was facing off about 20 clan and 20 omni in LDs base yesterday as they slowly pushed us back and back against the LDs towers who were actually at 5%. and the clock ran out for clan. There was no zone hugging, lots of running to and fro. IT WAS A BATTLE.

I have to say kudos to the DEVs. ITS GODAMN FUN!
