Hmm...

I know that other classes can boost their life up to 10k. I suppose I was comparing the enforcer class with my own class, which has no chance of getting anywhere near 10k life. Even with essence + doc life buff, I don't know if 10k is possible. It's the combination of mongo, damage shield, insane nanoresist, and reflect which makes the huge life pool on an enforcer mean a lot more than it does on another profession.

Anyways, I really don't think that an enforcer needs love right now. I've spoken with several enforcers who will freely admit that they are uber, and only have problems with some doctors and grid armor fixers. I know several enforcers who have fun manhandling traders. Fix the other classes first that really needs love.

No profession should be able to easily kill every other profession. That's not balanced. I like the idea that every profession has a weakness.

Yet, at the same time, no profession should be able to completely eliminate another profession. That's not balanced either. For example, when I duel with my 72MP, I either waste my opponent or else I get wasted. It's rarely much of a battle. That makes things really lame because there is no strategy involved and chance doesn't really even factor in that much either.
