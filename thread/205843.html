Two Bombs in Rome Bay Area, Questions Remain Unanswered
Two Bombs went off today outside of Rome Green in the Rome Harbor, causing cautostrophic damages to an Omni Outpost situated near the docks of the trade meccha. The explosions sent Leutenant Bret Michelson to the reclaim, where he was questioned after ressurection shock had worn off.

"There was only one," he was reported saying, "I only saw one person dsahing along. I dont think it was a fixer, because the bombs were built right there. He looked Omni, we didn't bother to question him."

Lt. Michelson has been placed in one days confinement for lack of duty, and his hearing will be held next week.

No suspects can be held to the case.

Three Gaurds are dead, they're names are not to be disclosed. A shipement of BBI-50 "Sticky Love Rain's" have been lost, and seemingly a shipment of GGX21 Chemical Compound Kits have been seized. Omni Pol was not availible for comment.
