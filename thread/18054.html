LTK Radio: Live At Reets (schedule)
Okay, I've got a lot of people asking me for schedules for LTK Radio Live At Reets, so here it is:

I'll be doing a show at Reet's Retreat on RK1 every Monday and Wednesday evening at 9pm EST / 2am GMT.

The party will run for about 3 hours on those nights (til 12am EST).

Omnis, Clanners, and Noots are all welcome. These parties are celebrations of peace and the love of techno and metal, no matter who you work for.

The Saturday night shows (The Reethead Megabash) will not be held every Saturday, so those times will be announced here in the Player Events forum as they are scheduled.
