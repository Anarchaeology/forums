Can't pick up items while fighting
I'm an agent, I do a lot of agent stuff like sneaking and sniping. Consider the following scenario, which has happened to me on several occations:

So, you need a piece a equipment or whatnot and take a mission some levels above your own. Since you're an agent, you can't engage any of the enemies (and survive) so you have to rely on your stealth.

After half an hour of careful sneaking, moving around high perception reds, sneaking behind the backs of others, you reach the goal of your mission: some item your employer wants returned. But, alas, it's guarded by some red mob which promptly attacks you.

Now, of course, you try to do the only logical thing: snatch the item before their very eyes and get the heck out of there while chanting "All your stuff is belong to me".

But you can't, and that sucks!

Please allow me to pick up items while engaged in combat.

I'm not an agent - does it increase concealment, or was it just an whopsie? )

it's not really "interface" issue... at best it's a "policy/decision" issue.
A game designer decided that "players can't pick up items or open chests while in attack mode".
As you've shown, the interface does a very good job of enforcing this

Think of it this way: It's not the aggression bar that prevents you from picking item up; it's the fact that you're in attack mode.
So even if they did what you wanted, if bar was lowered when you clicked on item - you'd still be unabel to pick it up.

Now, if you wanted it to automatically stop attacking when you click on item... ugh, now _that_ is bad interface design . Can you imagine the complaints people would make when they die because they accidentally clicked on something and stopped attacking? :-)

At any rate, sympathies on your premature death in that mission .
It's a frustrating thing to happen for sure . It happens to me every time I forgot to lower my aggression bar, then I spend a bit of a time cursing at myself, and I feel better
