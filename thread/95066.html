OOC: The problem with this board
There are not nearly enough "bad guys."

Going from most of the debates I see here between Omnis, Clanner and Neutrals, it always boils down to what are really one-sided arguments. Generally, it goes something like this:

Faction Person A: "[insert faction] is evil! They do [insert evil deed]!"
Faction Person B: "No, not all of us are evil! Some of us are good and defend others against [evil deed]!"
Faction Person A: "Well, [faction] is still evil even if not all of them are! I pledge to support those from [faction] who are not evil because I am good!"

All we have is people talking about how they help these other people or they would never do anything to hurt anyone else or pointing fingers at the other side saying they are the evil ones yet there are never any evil people here to reply to them.

Where are the evil people?

First off, I dont think there can be any "Hard Core Neutrals"...Kind of like asking for an Agnostic Jihad, isnt it?

Im a roleplayer. I saved my allowance for 5 weeks to buy my first set of dice, and a book of D&D character sheets...that was 1980. I have played D&D (Advanced,2ED,3ED) Shadow Run, Warhammer, Carwars, Top Secret you name it.

I had every intention of playing a hard core omni (and sort of do still) However when people cant see you across a table and read your face for your real intent, it gets too easy to get your feelings hurt, and get mad. Only on the internet do we have this trouble in RPG's.

I think this community of peolpe is the largest group of RP'ers ive encountered on any one game platform, and you are all incredibly creative and dedicated.

Bad guys are hard to play, because...well no one likes a bad guy. they are by nature reviled and shunned. Zealots are often even disliked by their own kind...Kind of a lonley way to play a MMorpg.

I dont think its an issue that the hard core poeple are not so much role players, but Who wants to invite Tavix the Terrible to Reets for a party? Who wants to team with them? What would a group of people enjoying tea in an outdoor cafe in Newland think, if the guy at the table next to them was the Butcher of Borealis? Its just not a very social stand to take, and most RP people want to interact with other RP people.

I find myself wishing that I had not chosen such a hard liner stance. Heck, Fixerben wont even talk to me, and hes a real nice guy (He will say its all lies though)...

People want to participate socialy in this community, so they chose a moderate aproach so as to be liked by the broadest range of people, In Character.
