How to Test Your Sig without posting
Method 1:

  1. Modify your sig:
  2. Open two browser windows displaying the Anarchy Online Forums.
  3. In the first browser, Click the UserCP button.
  4. Click [Edit Profile]
  5. Modify your Signature and [Submit Modifications]


    Preview it:
  6. In the second window go to any post, Click the New Thread or Reply button.
  7. Type anything in the message field
  8. Hit the [Preview Post] button

Repeat steps 4 and 7 until you have the desired effect.
Now you have the sig you want, without all that messy spam!
...unless of course you..ya know..want your post count to go up...
