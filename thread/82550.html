I agree that using IP on tradeskills should result in some benefit. I even agree that the PPPE scarcity is ludicrous. I love tradeskills and mastered almost all of them in EQ before coming here.

There needs to be a balance between making them ludicrously difficult (so that only a few absolute maniacs benefit) and making them so easy that everyone either has a character that can make the items, or knows someone who can. I just would hate to see things become easy enough that tradeskills become useless, like many of them did in EQ.

My major concern with this thread is that if PPPE's are made available in Shops, I fear you will see a huge influx of tradeskillers and a huge influx of carbonum and bronto armor pieces into the economy. The price will basically drop to a fraction above cost, non-tradeskillers will start re-selling the suits they grow out of for less than cost, and the whole thing will get horribly fubar'd.

That's the kind of thing I've seen happen in other games, and I'd hate to see it here.

My preference would be to see FunCom make getting a no-drop PPPE as a quest reward. Make the quest take long enough (no matter what your level) that people won't want to bother doing it for their twink. But make it short enough (with no annoying static camps), that a dedicated tradeskiller could get it done without feeling like it was hopeless. This kind of thing is possible, but it takes a little thought and planning from FunCom. Wait, maybe it's not possible.
