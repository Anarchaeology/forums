UoR/RUR deal on "Green" robots
from a RUR press release -

RUR was awarded a multi million credit contract by clan Unity of the Rose to supply maintenance robots to notum mining sites. The robots (from the new Logitronix S600 range) will be used to operate revolutionary, new anti-pollution equipment designed by the Tir School of Engineering specifically for this project.

Clan Unity of the Rose has for a long time been active in the area of environmental protection. Though the environmental impact of mining operations is still a matter of heated discussion, studies have shown that during the mining process proto-notinium particles are released in the atmosphere and that in large quantities these particles can be toxic to humans.

Accordingly the Tir School of Engineering Mining Department have developed a Quantum Reaction Field Generator device to trap the free proto-notinium particles which are then collected into safely disposable canisters. RUR is providing robots to operate with equipment which requires constant supervision. Theses robots have be modified specifically to shield their cpu from the effects of the Quantum Reaction Field.

This project is currently being piloted at one of the Unity of the Rose notum mines at an undisclosed location. If successful it will be extended to all Unity of the Rose mining facilities.

Mister Ricks:

We did not award this contract blind to the many incidents of RUR robots running amok. However, normal robots are unable to perform the complex task of monitoring QRFGs - and indeed, the standard RUR robots have had to undergo modifications to shield them from the field generator's adverse effects on sensitive electronics. Rest assured that these robots have been given no combat capability whatsoever and it is well within the power of Unity of the Rose to dispatch a wayward machine.

As for the effects of the proto-notum particles on humans, initial indications seem to point to the fact that personal nanobot fields deflect or neutralize low levels of this pollutant for humans with active and functioning Nanobot Control Units. However - people with non-functional NCUs, without NCUs, and with very small Nano Pool, are at high risk of genetic damage and disease from long-term exposure (on the order of several months to several years) to even the trace amounts of proto-notum that are blown into the towns and cities of Rubi-Ka by the prevailing winds.

Notum Mining using drilling towers on the surface is a relatively new technique, which we have found releases these particles diretly into the atmosphere - unlike underground mining which tends to trap the proto-notum in the mine shafts, where it was doubtlessly one of the many sources of sickness and death for notum miners in Rubi-Ka's past.

The effects of proto-notum on wildlife have been apparent almost from the beginning of mining deregulation: how many animals have you seen alive in tower areas recently? For that matter, how many monsters? It's highly likely that these particles are responsible for the total absence of any wildlife whatsoever inside the perimeter of a tower base.

The QRFG technology acts like a really big air filter for proto-notum. Its field attracts proto-notum particles, which are then trapped in a containment unit. Over time, these containers will fill up and need replacement and disposal, which will be one of the tasks performed by the RUR bots. I am particularly concerned that containment unit replacement by human beings could prove fatal, given the necessarily high concentration of proto-notum in the containment unit.

Note that this is only one of several projects that we are funding and supporting research on towards the goal of developing environmentally safe and economically feasible mining technologies for general distribution. Personally, I view it as an interim step until towers can be developed that do not release proto-notum into the air as a side effect at all.

Tapping the side panel close button for the Control Tower, Corvain turned and looked out across the lava fields. He scanned across the different turrets and guardians looking for anything out of the ordinary, then sighed as he initiated the Quantum Wings program in his ncu.

Flying low, he made one last circle around the facility and headed north. Maybe he would find what was tugging at his mind at the other base. The feeling was starting to get very annoying. It had started when he woke from a dream where he was hovering in the grid next to the exit to the controllers. The dream had seemed normal until slowly descended into the platform. The area he was transported to was oddly foreign, yet familiar as if it were a place he should know well, but couldnt remember. The notum in the air had smelled funny, almost like it was laced with a carcenic substance. From that point everything became a blur of motion, the only thing he could make out distinctly was humanoid shapes slowly moving around and moaning as if they were in some strange ancient earth zombie movie.

Gliding down next to the other Control Tower, he issued the command for his ncu to terminate the Quantum Wings program. Reaching out for the control panel, an arc of electricity reached out and threw him to the ground, momentarily stunning and blinding him. As he began to regain focus, panic clutched him as he saw his dream come to life around him. Blurring motion of moaning creatures, intermixed with flashes of a battle sweeping across the base. He commanded his ncu to initiate SaAngreal and the fear swept over him more as nothing happened. The urge to run set in, and then the world spun counterclockwise.

Vision returning to normal, he found himself standing in front of the Control Tower, hand outstretched against the main panel. For several long seconds he stood, wondering if he had just had one of the waking dreams he had studied about so much when he was young.

Deciding that it was not the time or place to meditate over things that he had long since lost faith in, he initiated the Control Towers diagnostic protocols and began an inspection of the base. A flicker to the south caught his attention, a moment later his Cacodemon stood at his side asking for guidance.

Guard me Soul Fragment, Corvain commanded as he cautiously advanced south.
I will protect you with my life Master the creature answered immediately.

Reaching the Southern perimeter, he cursed silently as he released it was probably just some wayward Yalmaha flying out to the Dome. Nothing to see here Soul Frag, lets finish up the diagnostics and head home. The demon only nodded silently.

Back at the Control Tower, Corvain glanced at the diagnostics report. It was all standard as far as he could tell, Perhaps I should take a hard copy to Lyricia he thought to himself. Keying the commands for copy to NCU, he closed the panel and looked out across the base again.

Opening a transmission to the UoR global relay channel on his comm device, Corvain here, nothing anomalous to report from the towers. I checked everything, Quantum Reaction Fields are stable and Proto-Notum levels are well below acceptable range. I guess it was just me and my dumb superstitions working overtime. I still think we should raise security level a little, but thats up to you guys.

Okay Corv, thanks for checking anyway. Ill discuss it with the Knights and see what they think. He heard as he began the long trip back to Athen.

Jynne sighed as she stepped out of the grid and materialized by the Waylander control tower. It was a slight sound, not the deep sigh of sadness or the expaserated huff of anger.

Rather, the sigh was regretfully resigned: it was the sigh of someone who'd been right about something but wished she hadn't. She'd known that the Neo Luddites would do this since their meeting. And she'd been equally sure that the ragtag fanatics would be butchered by the well-equipped, battle-experienced Roses. As they had been.

Ideologues were always so predictable.

The notum drilling towers hummed and chimed their harmonics, the defensive guardians emitting their auras of beneficial nanobots and the dangerously armored turrets swivelling their cannons to track unidentified movement. The machines either didn't know, or else didn't care, that the control tower was emitting a 100% suppression field and that the area was, for now, safe.

Jynne moved over to near where the Neo-Luddites had their poor semblence of a success, now discernable only by an oily black scorch mark on the rocks where the robot had been destroyed. It looked to her so much like a miniature of the burn left behind when a mining tower went up in flames during an assault, an event she had seen too often, and indeed caused more than a few times.

She wondered at the effect this "war" had on the people who fought it, like herself. Touching the sooty burn she sighed again, and this time was sad. The rain and wind and growth of plants would cover over the scars on the land, like this one. But she was increasingly concerned about the psychic scars being left on the soldiers who fought it, both in her organization, and in others.

The doctor stood up and brushed her fingers off, looking around at the remaining RUR bots performing their tasks. Checking the timer on the control tower she made a mental note to check back when the gas levels dropped. The NLM would probably be back soon.

Ideologues were always so predictable.
