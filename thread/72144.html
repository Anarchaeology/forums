Hmmm...
Ya know, if you call someone a "buddy", it's sort of assumed that you have some type of working relationship with this person, at the very least. That means that you guys have helped each other out in the past, and there's been a long-lasting back-and-forth exchange between the both of you. In my opinion, perhaps you should weigh your relationship with your friend agaisnt this one little incident. I realize that 45 million credits is a large amount of credits, and who wouldn't want to get a piece of that action, but, I think that you should also realize that this buddy of yours is probably worth more to you than just a one-shot sum of credits. Plus, how long did you actually give your friend? 2 days? One of my best friends in real life plays this game with me. I've levelled past him to the point where I can no longer team with him. This is due primarily to us being able to play for different amounts of time, obviously, me having more time to dedicate to games. However, we are constantly helping each other out. I give him gear, nanos, NCUs...whatever I can. In turn, he hooks me up with nanos, other gear, and whatever other interesting stuff he finds. Sometimes, though, I tell him I'm going to get him something, and unfortunately, I don't end up getting it for 3 days, when I told him I'd get it "tomorrow". My friend doesn't go crazy over it...he just waits. We both realize that our relationship is worth more than just 1 incident. Why go crazy over 3 days when we've passed stuff back and forth for a month already?

This isn't a flame, I'm just simply sharing with you that maybe you judged your friend too harshly and too quickly. I also beleive that public forum is NOT a good place to flame your friends...ex-friends or current friends.
