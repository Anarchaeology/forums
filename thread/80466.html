Twilight by the Broken Shores"
Twilight by the Broken Shores"
by Randa "Poetica" Whites

Twilight
Calm Water
Evening breeze
It curess my cheek
The ocean smell of salt
Like tears
Grains of sand
It runns trough my fingers
Like time
Like tide
Twilight
Calm water
And the evening breeze
I wipe my tears
Gaze upon the ocean
Endless ocean meet the skies
Calm water
Yet still
The smell of tears...
