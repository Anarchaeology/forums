Tir Grid has been laggy for several reasons:

1) Proximity to Save (Both old and new)

2) Proximity to mission terms (Again, both old and new)

3) Proximity to a shop (Old. There was George's armor and the Arena Sup shop)

4) Proximity to bank (Old and new again, w00tar BY5)

Many other reasons. Proximity to mobs to drain for Wrangles. Etc etc. Now, unfortunately, we're zoning into a bigger playfield with mobs and guards everywhere, usually things dying. People zoning in and out of Tir. It's really quite a mess.
