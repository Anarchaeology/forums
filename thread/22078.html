Nerf 'em all!
I've been playing AO on and off since it was released last year, and have found it to be an enjoyable game. What has puzzled me is the wailing and gnashing of teeth every time there is a change.

People, this is a game. It's supposed to be fun. If you find yourself worked up to the point that you're angry about it, take a step back and ask yourself why you are playing?

On the subject of nerfing: I seriously think AO (and other MMORPG's) need to think about either wiping "old" characters after a time, or simply having characters die of old age. It would encourage trying new (and sometimes risky) adventures, and keep old players mixing with newbies. And (they'll take away my republican party membership for sure now) I think there should be a 100% inheritance tax. Make everything worthwhile nodrop.

Think about it. When was the last time you went and tried a new profession? Or played a character on the "other side"?

Gaming is supposed to be fun. It's supposed to be new, and different, and a little unpredictable.

There are plenty of things in real life to get stressed out about. AO is supposed to be an escape from those, not a reinforcement of them.
