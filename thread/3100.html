Time to clear up some misconceptions
Amongst the various Omni-Tek factions there seems to be a few misunderstandings. I hope to lift the fog that has clouded so many minds. First off Amnesty dose not equal ceasefire. Secondly Ross is not Omni-Tek. Once you understand these two things you may better understand why the guilds of The Black Alliance feel compelled to work together for a better Omni-Tek.

Many have raised this point but some still associate the current offer of amnesty for the criminals on Rubi-Ka with a cease-fire with the Council of Truth. There is no such ceasefire in effect. Omni-Tek employees are encouraged to "try out that new gun on a clanner today" or "Kill a clanner today. Be happy tomorrow" whenever they are Omni-Tek cities and outposts.

It is every employees duty to kill every clanner in sight as the opportunity arises. We are at war! Anything less should be taken as collusion with the Council of Truth. Yet many employees discuss treaties with factions of the Council of Truth. Many more think there is a ceasefire in effect and they should avoid killing any clansmen. Believe me. The Council of Truth is not under such delusions. They are bent on the destruction of Omni-Tek on Rubi-Ka. To believe anything less is naive.

The second misconception is the most widespread and potentially the most dangerous. It is common in humanoid history for people to latch on to a figure and associate this person as hero ... or savior. Many times this has lead organizations or nations into tragedy. To blindly follow an individual is dangerous.

Ross is not Omni-Tek. Omni-Tek is not Ross. If what Ross is doing is bad for Omni-Tek then will you follow him blindly into Omni-Tek's destruction? Omni-Tek is bigger than Rubi-Ka. They own many other planets. Ross is only running this one. He is truly doing a bad job of it. Lets take a look at his highlights.

1. Uprising
2. Lose of Athens
3. Lose of most of the northern territories
4. Unleashing Shock Troopers on Omni-Tek employees ( Yes they actually did this. You can thank Derva for this one.)

I don't know about you but I want whats best for Omni-Tek. Is Ross whats best for Omni-Tek on Rubi-Ka? He's not done that great of a job so far. I hope that you all will not blindly follow Ross. We pay for his mistakes everyday. Remember... Ross is not Omni-Tek. Omni-Tek is not Ross.

Recently The Black Alliance has been demonized by some. These people while meaning good are just very misguided. We guilds of The Black Alliance want what is best for Omni-Tek on Rubi-Ka. No matter what the cost. Victory over The Council of Truth is the only option.

Whoa, whoa, WHOA there!

Let's clarify your 'clarifications', because you've simply stepped into untruth and poor logic here.

1) "Ross is not Omni-Tek."
  • Your goal: Since Philip Ross issued the amnesty, and has been vocal in his support for the amnesty, the cessation of violence, and peace, you think that you need to undermine his authority so that you can indiscriminantly attack clanners.
  • The truth: Indeed, Philip Ross is not the head of Omni-Tek Prime, which is headquartered off-planet. Where he falls exactly in the chain of command is unknown.
    HOWEVER: Philip Ross IS the CEO of Omni-Tek Rubi-Ka. Additionally, it's publicly known that Omni-Tek's major success has been Rubi-Ka and the very profitable notum mining here. It could be argued, then, that Philip Ross is likely a very HIGH ranking official within Omni-Prime.
  • Flaws in your reasoning:
    1. All Omni-Tek employees on Rubi-Ka ARE employees of Omni-Tek Rubi-Ka, not Omni-Prime. So, despite Philip Ross's position in the overall corporation, you answer to him, unless you hear from further up the chain, which we have not.
    2. You assume that Philip Ross isn't acting under orders from Omni-Prime. I believe that assumption to be incorrect, at least for the current issue here. I'll elaborate more below.


2) "The Amnesty does not equal Cease Fire
  • Your goal: You now support the amnesty (which has been VERY much supported by Omni-Admin, especially in the Tribunal with Truwind and Aberic), but you attempt to redefine its spirit so that it fits your goal, which is (again) the indiscriminate killing of clanners.
  • The truth: You are right. The Amnesty is NOT a ceasefire. As you mention, the amnesty is just a 'cleaning of the slate'--a forgiveness for all past crimes of clanners who wish to come back into the employ of Omni-Tek.

    The Amnesty is, in fact, only an extension of the ICC Contract, which requires Omni-Tek to allow third parties to peacefully settle on Rubi-Ka. The Amnesty is Chairman Ross's way of making a very public peace offering, while requiring each clan member to decide whether or not to abide by it. It is arguable that once the period of the amnesty is over, Omni-Tek is fully within its contractual rights to pursue those who would not accept peace by any means necessary.
  • Flaws in your reasoning:
    1. You believe that Omni-Tek is at war. (Untrue, and confirmed by a ranking Omni-Tek General last night at the Tribunal, General Telion). Omni-Tek has never declared any type of war against the clans. However, before the amnesty and Chairman Ross's recent negotiations of peace with the Council of Truth, Omni-Tek did encourage the use of force in defending Omni-Tek's interests. I can understand your confusion, but we have received several clarifications on this, including these comments by Philip Ross in the Omni-Herald:
      quote:
      "Nothing can halt the progress of peace. The amnesty has the full support of the Omni-Tek Corporation of Rubi-Ka, as well as the support of the Omni-Tek Board of Directors on Omni Prime."
      "Omni-Teks goal  my goal  is to commence peace negotiations with the Council of Truth without further delay. Any individual claiming to be affiliated with our corporation, working against a unified corporate goal, does so in direct violation of official company policies. Furthermore, we condemn any and all terrorist activities conducted by said individuals. Omni-Tek will pursue peace at all costs. Nothing will prevent that."
    2. You believe that you can fire on clanners if you can get around the implication made by some that the amnesty forces peace (based on the fact that Omni-Tek guards no longer fire upon clanners.) The incorrect assumption here is that you were allowed to instigate indiscriminant violence (aka 'kill on sight') against clanners before the amnesty. That wasn't the case. Let me refer you to a clause in the ICC Contract:
      quote:
      Omni-Tek® will not persecute inhabitants of Rubi-Ka.
      Some might squabble with the definition of 'persecute', but I'm pretty sure that attacking those who do not attack you first, nor have shown a history of attacking you, would be considered persecution.
    3. You believe that the PA messages "Use your gun on a clanner today" justify your interpretation of the amnesty and your aggression against clanners. It is unfortunate that those announcements still play, but I think even you can understand the simple fact of the timeline. The amnesty, and the current negotiations of peace, are the more recent of the events. And the most recent instructions (when policy conflicts) should be considered current company policy.
    4. You imply that with the amnesty or the ICC Contract, you cannot use force against clanners. Again, simply untrue. You should not initiate force, but you are certainly allowed and expected to use it against those who use force to jeopardize the lives of OT employees, or OT interests on Rubi-Ka. You can't shoot anyone with clan affiliation you see. However, you can protect yourself and Omni-Tek interests when they are assaulted.
    5. Finally, you believe it to be "every employee's duty to kill every clanner in sight as the opportunity arises"; that Omni-Tek's best interests are served by full-scale military mobilization against the clans.

      While that certainly might bring a quick 'peace', and seems to be the most simple solution to our problems, it is not allowed. The Non-Persecution clause of the ICC Contract prevents Omni-Tek from doing this. That's why the clans are where they are today. If Omni-Prime could have removed the threat hundreds of years ago by simple use of force, they would have.

      No, to pursue this solution is to jeopardize Omni-Tek's right to even BE on Rubi-Ka. To violate the contract is to allow the ICC to invalidate it, and they would most certainly like to do that, and to make Rubi-Ka their own.

Omni-Tek Announces Unconditional Amnesty
Omni-1, October 25. 29475

The Omni-Tek Corporation of Rubi-Ka (OT-RK) announced today an unconditional amnesty for all registered and unregistered members and supporters  both political and military  of the illicit rebel clans of Rubi-Ka. At the same time, OT-RK reinforces their commitment to the existing ceasefire which has been increasingly strained in the past few months.

"The amnesty will hopefully open up a new channel of communications between Omni-Tek and the clans, particularly the Council of Truth," said OT-RK CEO Philip Ross in a statement to the press this morning. "Our objective is to work towards a new era of peace and understanding between our respective people, and to bolster the provisional ceasefire thats been in effect since the signing of the Tir Accord. The amnesty will allow those who have committed crimes in the name of the clans to come out and hand in their weapons of aggression, without having to fear reprisals or their freedom  and perhaps to even join the Omni-Tek Corporation and start a whole new life."

The amnesty will supplement the already existent ceasefire between OT-RK and the rebel clans.

"The ceasefire has been suffering lately," said Ross, "partly because of the recent influx of new colonists  both Omni-Tek employees and clan supporters  and partly because of a lack of commitment on both sides. The amount of random violence in the past few months has been staggering, and this is something we need to find an immediate solution to. The ceasefire and the amnesty are the first steps on the long road towards reconciliation. I also want to extend a formal invitation to the Council to meet and discuss the terms of a lasting peace. We would like to enter into peace negotiations as soon as possible, and it is our hope that the amnesty shows our genuine commitment to this cause."

Effective immediately, the amnesty guarantees that an unconditional pardon is granted to every individual who has committed criminal acts against OT-RK in support of the rebel cause. Under corporate laws, the very act of being a registered member of the clans  or a political and/or military supporter of the clans  is a crime punishable by imprisonment and/or deportation.

With the amnesty in effect, clan members are also free to apply for a position within the Omni-Tek Corporation of Rubi-Ka.

"Our wish is for this to be the beginning of a new era of cooperation between ourselves and the free citizens of Rubi-Ka," said Philip Ross. "Our sincerest hope is that the amnesty and ceasefire will prove without the shadow of a doubt that we are committed to peace. The ball is now in the hands of the rebel clans, and, most importantly, the Council of Truth."

The Omni-Tek Corporation of Rubi-Ka is a subsidiary of the Omni-Tek Corporation. Omni-Tek is headquartered on Omni Prime, a planet in the Omni system. Omni-Tek was founded on Earth in 12274, and has grown to become the largest and most profitable hyper-corporation in history.
