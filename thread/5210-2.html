Well Yazule, I agree with the following:

- Agents should be able to do high damage (indenpendently of being deadly or not- that shouldn't even matter) from a long distance. It's obvious that Funcom imagined the Agent profession this way, otherwise we wouldn't have Aimed Shot and Rifle as green skills.
The same way I think that Soldiers, Enforcers and MAs should be able to do more damage at close quarters than an agent does.

I don't wish agents or any other profession alone to own in PVP. I wish all professions could own in PvP, each profession in a different way.

What should NOT be happening is what is happening now. Because of an inicial unbalance in favour of the Agent profession, that caused a lot of controversy in PvP, every agent (even the ones that never PvPed) have a really hard time to survive in PvM.
I think you can imagine the consequences that has in doing missions (and therefore in getting money) for example...

- I also agree that agents are in general, nerfed. As I've said before our only special attack (A.S.) is almost useless. Conceal doesn't work right in PvM and it's useless in PvP. Our tradeskills (tutoring, chemistry, etc) aren't implemented yet, ect, ect.

I dont want agents to rule the game, I just want a little more balance, thats all. I think that's understandable.
