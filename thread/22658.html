How about some SOUND?
Something has been bothering me in Rubi-ka lately, and I couldn't put my finger on it until today. Nobody has a VOICE. I haven't heard a single audible voice since I started playing. You would think maybe an NPC would merrily shout, "Hey! Welcome to my shop, look around a while!" when browsing their wares, or that you could taunt monsters with preset keys, saying simple things like "Bring it on!" or, "Your momma was a Bronto!" or something.... It would add so much to the game. Imagine the low rumbling voice of an Atrox, and the high squeaky voice of the little opifex

But as is, it is deathly quiet in Rubi-ka. It's lonely really....not another sentient voice in existence.....just complete...and utter....silence.................................. .................
*tick* *tock*.....

Funcom, bring Rubi-ka alive....add some *****in' voices and taunts!!
