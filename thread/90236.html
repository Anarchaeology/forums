In addition to the buffs mentioned above...

Fixer NCU buff for more buffs. You should be able to have like 350+ NCU total with this buff if you have high NCU, less by about 40-50ish if you use Computer Deck Range increaser for longrange nanos.

Coruscating screen (highest enforcer dmg shield, +240 energy AC, 60 dmg shield, 500ish HP) Combined with RRFE, gives a nasty 109 back every time you get hit, and a good boost to HP.

Dr Hack 'n Quack (or similar HOT), 365-pt Heal Over Time

Gridspace Freedom (or similar runbuff) +720 to runspeed and 70 or higher to evades I think? Great for tower fights - use a Computer Deck Range increaser to get 40-meter nukes, nuke the heck out of them at long range while rooting, and be able to run FAST if needed.

Personally I'd go X-3 instead of ithaca.

All those are good suggestions for a foundation. Situational awareness tho is key. Knowing who is around you and what the profession is able to do is a must. I would suggest making alts and experimenting with them so you know what each class is good/bad at so you know how to respond. There is no set armor/implant/buff scheme that will allow you to win in pvp unless it is in arena. Even then those schemes only work against certain classes. Dieing alot helps. Re: advice in PvP
quote:
Originally posted by 23
I'm a 75 NT and working on Ithaca.
Ithaca is imo a good PvP gun for several reasons. 1. You can maintain or get very close to 1/1 on full def. 2. AS cycles alot faster and is now argueably the best special ingame (been noticing AS nerf threads springing up). 3. An additional Fling every now and then can't hurt.

quote:
my armor is nano type.


I'd recommend going with Biomech over Nanoarmor 9 times out of 10. Considering that youre maxing stamina for the MC, its by far a much better armor even when not considering the extra treatment and first aid.

quote:
I'd like to know what the "skills" are that people talk so much about. What should I buy, what abilities are best to focus on, anything would be nice to know.
Evades are important, if not the most important skill to have in PvP (especially since your going ithaca). Blinds are effective to disorient your opponent but wont be the end all be all until you can cast legions of eyeblighter (AoE blind). 1v1 duels are really hard to win as an NT. IMO I enjoy PvPing most when in tower battles or Camelot raids. When in range of an individual target and behind a few large tanks, I'll AS, Fling, Nuke, Root, Nuke, Root till they are dead or have 'runnoft.' Or try to /assist a large mass of HP...er enforcer. In tower battles, if the gas is at 25%, or while in Camelot, letting lose with some AOE's in crowded areas is always entertaining. I've only started PvPing regularly a month or so ago so I'm still learning the ins and outs.

Good luck with it all!
