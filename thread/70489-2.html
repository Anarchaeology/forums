My thoughts ...
I love the idea of more content for roleplayers. Hope it happens.

First of all, its off-subject, but Thank You Blix!!! I have been asking for this for .. well, almost a year now. It would be great if the AO architecture had some more peer-to-peer aspects so that references could be copied through the server between clients.

With regard to environments for Roleplay, I agree with Jynne. What we need is a 'mission' generator for creating roleplay spaces where you can specify what type of space you want, and have a space is dynamically created with no mobs or chests. And have the area bound to the next mission entrance you enter -- much the way your apartment is created by the first apartment door you enter. It acts just like a mission in the sense that it expires and has a key that can be duplicated. And, it should ABSOLUTELY have a setting for suppression gas level. It would be great to be able to roleplay in an environment that allows for some combat, whether its 25% or 0%. Have some way to be able to regenerate the same area for ongoing roleplaying scenarios.

As for the types of environments..
- Better arenas with fighting areas in the middle and seating for spectators.
- Spectacular ice caves.
- Indoor swiming areas like hotsprings or resort locations with swiming pools.
- Doctors office.
- Scientific laboratories.
- Modest residential houses.
- Romantic getaways.
- Abandoned mines.
- A theater with a stage or podium with seating.
- A speak-easy.
- An underground rave with area for DJ.

And back to the item issue.. it would also be exteremely helpful to roleplayers if:
- Items dropped on the ground lasted longer.
- Items dropped on the ground could be picked up by other players. If you remember, this use to be the case but they removed it. Why not make this a player option?
