Funcom: please let us know the status of the crash to desktop on Loading bug
Ok, I have to vent a little bit here

I think AO has the potential to be a great game and I've shown it to a bunch of my friends. But I have spent most of the evening just trying to log on, because I crashed earlier in a bad spot near mission terminals, and now I get the "Crash to desktop after loading screen" problem over and over with my main character. My secondary character has been stuck in this mode for almost 2 weeks now, and I've given up on ever getting him back (even after trying all the standard remedies posted to this board).

This is simply unacceptable, Funcom. Please let us know that you are aware of and working on this problem. Just read these boards - there are hundreds of people with this problem. It is not just an isolated case or two.

I've worked in the software industry for nearly 20 years, on some very complex products, and I can state with 100% assurance that we would simply never release any product with this sort of problem. It just wouldn't happen.

This problem is doubly frustrating to me not only because I spent all evening trying to log onto the @#% game, but because I could almost certainly *fix* the problem if I was on the funcom programming staff. Although I obviously haven't seen their code, unless the problem is designed in there *real* deep, I am almost 100% sure that I could have found and fixed the problem by now. It's not like it's hard to duplicate or anything. Hire me for a few weeks, fly me out to Norway, and I'll fix the damn bug for you.

Please just let us know that this is being worked on? I've read every update so far, and haven't once seen mention of this problem. Just knowing that somebody is working on it would go a long ways towards good PR here.
