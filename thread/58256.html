adv pets? (as seen in new char creation screen)
in the profession choosing screen, it looks as if the reet-form is more like an adventurer's pet rather than what the adventurer can turn into. rather than misleading your new customers, and fuel more interests from old players, lets make some alternate usage pets for Adventurers.

say for example...

Reet Companion: cannot attack. can be used to scout and pin-point mobs in a further 'radius' than for any individual in the mini-map. makes mini-map more useful to adventurer.

Leet Companion: can be used to relay back information to the controller strange tidbits of information. such as the currently extremely under-used SEARCH function. the leet companion will run around(with the /pet hunt command) and look for boxes with hidden traps. (since leets already have high perception). they can also be 'nerfed/balanced' os when a mob checks agro with this leet, the mob will not attack the leet but the leet will run away(as in a self-casted fear from this mob). thus showing the controller whether a mob will agro him/her.

Wolf/Sabertooth cat Companion: i have no idea. currently my brain can only make suggestions that they are used for fighting similar to other currently available pets. perhaps an additional function used to 'scare away' certain 'families' of mobs. (not necessarily pertaining to level, but specifically to species.) maybe it is a lacking function, but it is fun for RP, and it does have it's limited uses. alternatively, for fighting, it can be quite a formidable companion for fighting, BUT(and a BIG BUT), perhaps we can make it unstable, and will sometimes break off from a fight to chase certain families of mobs, leaving the half-fought enemy to chase the controller of this companion in vengeance.

Pit Lizard Companion: no idea what to do with this one. perhaps in light of the current 'upgrades' available in the booster pack, Pit Lizards can be ridden as a low-flying (= non-flying) combat vehicle, adding additional attack/runspeed/evades to the controller. with a certain amount of absorb? which, unlike layer protection, it will absorb perhaps on a 25-50% chance on a direct hit. and when the absorb runs out, the Pit Lizard will dissolve, leaving the adventurer falling ot the ground, and taking a sever hit of falling damage(which fall damage cannot be absorbed with layer pro or reflect nanos/bracers anyways).
