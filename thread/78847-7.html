he's right to some extent. if you want to point out problems, there are constructive ways to do it. look at the sheer numbers of people who say "if you don't fix X, I'm cancelling my account" ... fine. let them.

as someone who has been a programmer/designer (though never on this scale) ... i have always been willing to work to implement fun and useful features to a game... even more so when the players understood enough about what they were talking about to make calm rational suggestions... and point out "balanced" ways to include them in the existing system without disrupting the existing game.

and i tended to become defensive when people were insanely negative... i knew i was doing it... but that didn't matter... it bothered me terribly... sometimes simply because i felt guilty that people weren't having fun with what i was creating... but you cannot ever please everyone. FC's designers/programers are people. most likely, they're people very much like us... very few programmers/designers for games come from outside the world of gamers... if you present calm, rational and constructive suggestions... they do listen. If you are negative... you cannot expect them to like you or want to deal with you.

i have see evidence of that in the game in the year I've played. like many NTs, I really wish they'd turn us into the creature that we are described to be... and it frustrates me that we have been told it will be done... and it hasn't. but, i appreciate the reminder that we shouldn't become bitter and full of hate over the matter. if we don't enjoy what we're doing, we should play another character... or move on to a new game. simple enough.

and we should never ever ever resort to saying to someone that because they are not an NT they don't know what we have to deal with. it is not us vs. them... and we should never be the ones to make it that way. especially because we (and everyone else) rely on other professions to survive and progress... if we make them hate us... how long do you think we'll survive?

thats probably one of the main reasons I personally agree with the fixers that we should nerf the anti-GA nukes. I don't want to have fixers hate me... i like fixers and i like how NTs and fixers can work together.

This is why an NT patch has been delayed so long. Most other professions are not aware of the IP cost or the machinations involved in an NT equipping the weapons they have. All they see is that NTs are using the same weapons (X-3, generally) as everyone else and they're getting to do nuke damage on top of huge AS hits and crits.

This is true at the top end of the game... where an NT can dump massive amounts of IP into boosting those weapon skills and move implants away from nano skills and attributes that were critical lower in the game. And because it can be done... there is the general assumption that the NT is able to do the same weapon damage as every other profession plus their nuke damage. Ultimately, this just isn't true for the vast majority of NTs.

We're asking for a patch because our profession is designed to sacrifice normal offensive and defensive ability... and die quickly... in exchange for high direct damage output. NTs wear weapons because nukes by themselves suck for damage output, I very very commonly watch nearly everyone in my team crit (and sometimes just do normal weapon damage) for more damage than my nukes and my nukes are many seconds apart... an NT with an ql200 X-3 probably does more with damage in just the AS portion of combat with a single mob than with nukes.

One of the most important features of the patch that we are anticipating is a device that the NT wears that allows access to better nukes but prevents the NT from using any weapons. By cutting out the ability of twinked NTs and top end NTs from working around our problems, the balance between our type of damage and that of the weapon and melee damage dealers should remain stable.

As to the comments about nullity... at best, with NS2... you have an NT who is stuck in one place invulnerable to you for 19 secs. During that time, they have 13 secs (its 14/8 for ns1) with which to nuke you... simple strategy says you use those first 6 seconds after the nt puts up nullity (its plainly visible) to break any root the nt may have put you in and to run away. Once nullity drops... return to thrashing the NT... they can't put it up again for about a minute. If you don't manage to kill them in that time... repeat the actions of the first time.
