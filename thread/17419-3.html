Notes
Go into a shop.
Buy a lockpick for every bag that you normally carry.
Turn them into nodrop lockpicks. (holo cam per lockpick, one id extractor, its not hard to figure out)
Put one in each bag.
When you get loot worth a damn, get off your butt and put it in a bag. Swap out crap loot and put in things you can't stand to lose if you get full. (buy more bags next time you hit town, or bank some stuff)

Seems to keep the whole bag off your lootable items. It does this to keep people from putting nodrops (e.g. yalmahas) from being exploited as pvp loot, otherwise people would go to secluded 0% zones and arrange deals this way. So far this has worked for me...otherwise it would empty the backpack and give them 19 items, or put the lockpick in your inventory and give them a pack with 19 items. Because this could cause you to get more than 30 items in your inv (if you had more than 30 nodrops in bags) it doesn't happen...you never get an 'overflow' box after a pvp loss.

Also, if you have done any of the following since your last save:
-Used bank
-Traded
-Massively rearranged your inventory (heard of bugs...so if you just changed all your ncus and armor, do this so you don't lose the old ones)
-Gotten xp
SAVE before tromping into pvp zones.

If you can, grid out of missions if you find nice stuff.

BTW, if it was something really valuable, it should've been in the bank, or being worn. (if you got something he was wearing, it was due to a bug or you exploiting, and you should damn well give them back and hope you don't get reported, but I haven't heard of this to happening to anyone in the last 5-6 patches or so) Now you have a third option...a 'pvp safe' backpack.

I doubt funcom will EVER change this, because it would cause far too many nodrop bugs and exploits.
