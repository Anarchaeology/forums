Jitters...
The climate controller hummed soothingly, gently breathing cool air into the small room where they slept. Lying on her side, she could barely make out his features. She knew them by heart, thoughdark eyes closed, tiny lines fanning out from their corners, from too many days in the sun, quick to laugh, yet able to turn cold with anger or disdain, but never at her. A shock of dark hair that she suspected would fall over his eyes, were it not kept neatly cropped in a severe military style. A strong jawline, sensual lips, slightly parted as he slept, oblivious to the inventory she was taking. A good face overall, handsome, strong a face she adored.

Giving an almost inaudible sigh, she started to roll on her back, but was caught up short. He was lying sprawled across her long hair, effectively pinning her. She moved back to lie on her side, and continued her silent appraisal. He had a few silver hairs at the temples, she knew, although she couldnt see them in the darkness. She didnt actually know his age, she suddenly realized, although she knew there was a considerable gap in the years between them. But that was immaterial. This was a man in the prime of his life, strong, vital, a leader of men. Her lover, soon to be her husband.

The wedding was in a few days time. Something su****iously like a wave of panic swept over her, a nameless fear. She took a deep breath, let it out slowly. Wedding jitters. Pure and simple. She reached her hand up and touched his cheek, gently. He stirred, but didnt awaken. Oh, she loved him so much, it was like a palpable pain inside her. She traced her finger across his jawline. And he loved her. That was the wonder of it. He could have had any woman in Omni-Tek, perhaps all of Rubi-Ka, but he wanted her. He loved her. How frightening, she thought to herself, to be put on a pedestal, worshipped. Knowing that at any moment, the door to the gilded cage could snap shut, and there would be no escape.

Just then, he mumbled something, and rolled over, moving off of her hair. She eased away, careful not to awaken him. Slipping out of bed, she snatched up her robe in one hand, slippers in the other. She made it as far as the door, before hesitating, turning to look at his sleeping form. He looked so young, so defenseless, lying there. She set her slippers down, draped the robe over a nearby chair, and climbed back into the bed. He reached for her in his sleep, and she snuggled close, his arms wrapping around her, making her feel safe, loved. And she lay there, staring up at the ceiling, tears slowly trickling down her cheeks, thinking, My God, what have I done?

He shuffled through his clothes silently. His fiance continued to sleep soundly on the modest mattress, and he had no intention of disturbing her rest...

He looked back at her and smiled softly. She was so many things to him... Confidant, best friend, lover... And soon, she'd be his wife, and he her husband.

His smile grew brighter and he turned back to his clothes. As he fished out a black outfit, his mind as it usually did, pondered her.

She was so amazing to him, they were in many ways very alike... Yet always just different enough to enlighten each other. Sometimes he felt he could almost read her mind... Almost. She still always made sense. And she listened to him... That really amazed him in itself. He was known for speaking, but it was the rare person that actually heard. At least, when they knew it was him. He smirked to himself.

He had no doubts in his mind about the wedding, no jitters... Or if he did, he didn't listen to them. He'd faced death so many times, and endured it on more than one occasion, that irrational fears had little power over him. No, every time he thought about their marraige, he felt nothing but warmth.

He knew he was strange in this regard. Most other men dreaded committment, the end of their adventures... He almost laughed. It seemed so clear to him. Those adventures were illusions, none of them really ended happily. No, it was just profess to love one person, lose them, profess to love another, lose them ad infinite... Until love became nothing if not a pipe dream, and all that remained was cheap sex.

Marraige, as he saw it, was not the end of any great adventure, but just the beginning of another. Any idiot could give into temptations, the challenge and value came from laughing at them. Living in the moment, at all times, to him was among the stupidest things a person could do if they wanted any lasting happiness. In the moment, a person doesn't necessarily think of the cost of their actions. Leaving them to pay terrible prices, that they must work to convince themselves are worth it.

No, he looked at a larger picture. Aphrodite herself could appear from ancient myth, and try to seduce him. She'd leave with wounded pride. Because even now his fiance held treasures no other woman could possess, or possess as much of.

Knowledge of him, who he was, and love for it.

He looked back over at her. She was not really poetic, or obsess over details. He made her engagement ring himself. The ring she was going to give him, she'd clearly found in the course of a mission. His vow was a page of prose. Hers was a sentence.

He didn't want to tell her these things, of course. Even being as blunt as he usually was, he feared to tell her that she had any flaws, for fear she'd become as angry as those he generally upset with what he saw as simple truths. He was afraid to lose her.

He didn't love her for being as obsessive as him, or for being as good a writer. He loved her for what and who she was. Sane, reasonable, his equal in thought and action, and so much more.

As he pulled on his leather shirt, he made a decision... He'd tell her his opinions of the ring and the vow. As softly as he can... If she becomes angry, he'll take it. But he knew he was wrong to lie to her. She was not perfect, niether was he, but between them there was trust and understanding, something more valuable than praise.

He pulled on his boots, then silently walked over to her and bent down to kiss her cheek softly. She smiled softly in her sleep, and rolled over onto her side. He smiled down at her. His eyes glistened slightly... Though it didn't make sense to him, sometimes he felt he loved her so much he could cry... Closing his eyes and bringing himself back under control, he brushed some of her hair away from her eyes, and then rose to leave and report to work.
