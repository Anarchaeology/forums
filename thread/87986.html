If you want a guild type with unlimited "second tier" people, you have to use department or feudalism. The others are limited to a max of 10.

Department has 2 more ranks (pres/gen/SC/UC/UL/UM/app) feudalism has 1 less. (lord/knight/vassal/peasant)
