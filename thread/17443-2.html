The High Level Game
Oh, yeah. The other thing about many skills--and parry may be one of them--is that if a skill only starts to work at high level, that's no good.

I mean, not everyone is level 150 plus. And most people spend their whole gaming life below 150. So, when people say, "Just wait until you are level 150 (or 100, or whatever). Then it will be worth it."

That isn't good. That deprives so many people of useful working skills.

Well anyway, that sucks. I don't think engendering elitism is a good thing. A game should be rewarding at any level. And investing in a skill should be rewarding at any level, too.

For instance, deflection shields. Cson correctly pointed out that deflection shields aren't very useful at lower levels. It is true. With good armor, you are already close to minimum damage on most creatures you should be fighting.

So, again, I wasn't creating this thread to talk about deflection shields, but there also you have a skill (parry requirement) that offers very limited value until very high level.

evades and parry
In my opinion, the whole way Parry and evades work needs to be overhauled. It looked ok on paper, but it's clearly not working in practical gameplay.

As a 99 MA, with over 500 in parry, I never parry on anything but maybe the occasional green, and greys. Same for evades.

Now, obviously, you shouldn't be able to parry and evade well against a red mob. Red means significantly more powerful then you. That's ok. It's reasonable. But because of the continusly growing gap between a characters power, and that of a mob at the same level (we get stuck with title caps, and limited ip, whereas mobs get full maxed skill (ALL skills) every level) ) something needs to be changed.

Even fighting yellows now, I'm seeing parry pop up about 7 to 12 times a fight.

A few idea's I'd like to see put in are:

Brawl and Dimach should NOT parryable.

MA's get a set addition to our ATT rating every level (like, maybe +5 to attack rating with every level, regardless of our actual MA skill. This would help to offset our title cap problems, and allow us to not be parried/evaded so much)

Armor that gives a bonus to our MA skill (like the biomech stuff for docs)

A 5 or 10 minute Dimach reload time.

Do something with the sharp objects skill! If we could equip thowing knives that ONLY fire when the mob is outside our fist range, it would make us more viable in PvP. (Ie. if the mob is outside your fist range, you throw a knife. If it's inside first range, you do normal fist attacks, and can use MA specials, brawl and dimach).

Give us a continuing line of Brawl buffs

Change our Controlled line nano's to either make them equal to the pet damage buffs, or change them so that instead of a tiny dmg increase, they give us a short term self only huge crit buff (or massive att rating increase)


Any or all of these idea's being put into the game would be great. But... I'm not holding my breath.

Saille, I hope this brings a grin to your face. After reading your post I went and talked to the designers about parry. We decided after some testing that parry (and related to it, riposte) needs a boost. So, the chance of you being successful in the role to parry will be increased. As for evades in general there is a common train of thought that they don't work all that well. One factor contributing to this is that you, as a player, do not get any feedback on when you successfully evade an attack. For example, if you successfully evade a projectile attack you are not told - if you resist a nano program however you do get told. So, we're working on giving you feedback via the chat interface for when you do successfully evade an attack.
