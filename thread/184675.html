NOTE: Subdued Novictum is lootable from various mobs - though spirit mobs seem to drop it most often.

You can also get novictum in its base forms:

The Novictum comes in varying QLs with differnt names:

Disturbed Sequence of Novictum: QL 1 - 89
Provoked Bond of Novictum: QL90 - 129
Enraged Pith of Novictum: QL130 - 159
Vicious Novictum Continuity: QL 160 - 189
Furious Novictum Nexus: QL190 - 219
Cataclysmic Birth of Novictum: QL220 - 300

To make these into Subdued Novictum you need an Ancient Novictum Refiner (Hard to get ahold of, can be bought from Yuttos Tools or sometimes it drops from The Lady, she spawns at the little spirit outdoors temple in the Nascence Valley that leads from the Jobe Research bridge to the crack in the wall.
Thanks Xtremtech for that nice Copy & Paste Material ). No skill is required to "subdue" the Novictum.

Mobs also drop "Unpolluted Crystals". These are at present fixed and can be filled at Rubi-Ka Control Towers and will now work in the process at the moment.

HUNTING AREAS FOR NOVICTUM: (with ql's most likely to drop)

ql 60-95 Near the incarnator NE of Cold Rock
ql 80-140 (the odd 160+ every now and then): Northern Scheol, by Ergo. Kill named Spirits/Soul Dredges for guaranteed drops, regular Spirits and Spirit Hunters may also drop. Good drop rate if you aim for the Scheol pocket bosses, I spent 15 minutes today finding 6 x ql 125+ Novictum for a guildie.
ql 130-180 or so:
Adonis Spirit Bosses/Soul Dredge bosses
ql 160-170ish: Penumbra Spirit Palace
ql 180+: Inferno Spirit Room, currently the best spot
ql 255+: (Quite rare) - drops from Dragon Bosses in Inferno, or you can find some pricy drops on the market forums.

SPAWNING PROCESS:


1. MP Boon-nanoprogram summons Unpolluted Crystal
2. Fill crystal on control tower (RK CT is now accepted) - Yielding Crystal Filled by the Source (The MP will most likely have to do this, since it's a temporary item :P ) And just so know, you do not "initiate attack" on the CT while filling your crystals

3. Combine Complete Blueprint Pattern of 'The Elysian Soul Dredge with same or higher ql crystal - Requires 6 x ql of pattern in Nano Programming (NP) *(Was earlier 7,5 x) yielding Notum Crystal with Etched 'The Elysian Soul Dredge

4. Combine Notum Crystal with Etched 'The Elysian Soul Dredge with same or higher ql Subdued Flow of Novictum requiring 4,5 x (NOT 4 x) ql of pattern in Quantum FT (QFT), yielding Novictalized Notum Crystal with 'The Elysian Soul Dredge

5. Use Novictalized Notum Crystal on incarnator to spawn boss. You need to target the incarnator and then single right click on the Novictalized Crystal in your inventory. Remember to keep the Incarnator targeted while spawning, boss won't spawn if you i.e. target yourself while spawning is underway. NOTE: You'll lose your hp when spawning boss, be careful. NOTE: Do *NOT* spawn another pocket boss directly after killing one, check if you have a DoT running first
