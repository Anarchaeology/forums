Blade Corp - A development
Most of you will not know me. Many of you will not care to. What I report here is offered without comment, and is fact.

I am Ailish, leader of the First Light Clan. As a loyal clan leader I do my level best to assist my fellow clan's whenever possible, that we might grow stronger together.

Neptunous, former Blade Corp employee and now leader of Scorpio within the clans, requested help over the clan leaders channel, and I offered assistance.

He told me that he was concerned that many Blade Corp employees considered him a thief, and he wished to make it clear that he was not. To this end he passed to me 4 bags of equipment, and I asked that I pass them to Blade Corp. I was pointed in the direction of Docwild, arranged a meeting with him, and delivered the aforementioned 4 bags to him. He will corroborate my story, should that be necessary.

Further to this, Neptunous also asked me to post a message to this satellite forum, explaining what I had done. This I do now.

I do not know whether this will be enough for you to make your peace with him, neither is it really any of my business, but I wish you all well in the future, and hope that any difficulties that you encounter may be resolved in a way conducive to the betterment of Rubi-ka, the clans, and Blade Corp.

Without prejudice, and in friendship.

Isobel "Ailish" Wright.
President of First Light.
EndGame Board member.
