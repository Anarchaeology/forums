Tales from the dungeon
As many of you know, I have been in the dungeon since Saturday hoping to find an opportunity when there is not an 100+ level there camping the director. A couple of the 100+ levels were very nice, today I met one who wasn't.

Logged in to find myself in the normally safe hallway surrounded by bodyguards who started firing. Turns out that this level 100 MP had drug them into the hallway. She was sitting in there and wouldn't even turn her healing pet on me so I would have a chance. Now I was mad enough that she decided that she would spend her day stealing loot that was meant for characters lower level than her and I was not surprised in the least that it was for her alt. But then, just to aggravate the situation, she said it was for her level 9 alt! LEVEL 9! She's in there keeping the people that the dungeon was meant for from the loot because she someday might play a crat up to the level where he will be able to use the drop!

And Funcom doesn't even have the professionalism to respond to my complaints and questions. Man, I am pissed!
