Offering my services!
I want to offer my services to the clan's.

I am only a lvl 5 Agent, but I am very dedicated so I expect to be gaining ranks pretty quick.

Since my father was executed by the Omni Police for the false accusation of stealing equipment from their labs. Because he had openly stated his oppinion on some of the Omni ways,
I've been fighting for the supressed of Rubi-Ka.
Hoping that one day there can be peace on our world and poeple can live happy lives again without having the constant fear of being arrested by one of the secret Omni factions.

At first I started going to the Omni cities emptying my gunclips at everything that even resembled Omni. Now I see that's not the way because for every kill you make, two off them return to hunt you. I'm using my mind now making only well thought hits.

I became aware that you can't make it on your own, you need allies to have greater impact and get to larger targets.

So I'm offering my services to clan's which feel the same way I do.
