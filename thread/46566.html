Q&A from Pro Forum
This is the recently completed Q&A from the pro forum. It's largely useless and I disagree categorically with all the responses but you can judge it for yourself. The questions are mine, the answers are Cosmik's.

quote:
Originally posted by Cosmik


1) Your claim is that engie bots a) tank b) deal with adds c) do good damage over time. None of these claims ring true with anyone who has played an engie in the current team environment for any length of time (and not just due to poor pathing). Where do you get such misconceptions?

The Engineer bot can certainly tank. It has the most health out of any pet and can deal good damage - put it up against a MOB (of adequate level. I'm not talking 50 levels above you) and it'll tank well. If you or your team is attacked by more than one MOB, an Engineer pet can pull a MOB off and continue to deal damage to that MOB and keep the MOB on itself, and won't need any immediate attention. It can therefore deal with adds. Because of the Engineer bots tanking capabilities, it won't die as quickly as other pets and can deal good damage over time. I've seen these things in solo and team play - and I know Engineers are played by a number of our designers too. So, that is what the last answers were based on. I'd welcome any situations you have experienced in which these claims do not ring true - I know you have alot of experience here, so you know what you're talking about. I'm just asking for some specific situations you may have run into, or even tests you have seen done.

2) Do you think that the main nano lines of engineers (reflect, AC, beacon warp, high-level-Trans) are comparable to either MPs (nukes, nanoskill buffs, stuns, cost reduction, animas) or Crats (nukes, calms, baton, crit speech)?

2b) Harder to answer - Do you think nukes are better than all the engie nanos combined minus Transference?

Comparable in what way is something I must ask, because each line is different - each have their benefits. Comparable in a team sense? Solo? We must also remember that each profession has it's place and abilities, so shouldn't other abilities and professions be included here? And if possible, please elaborate a bit on 2b) for the designers. Nukes from any profession? Any nuke? And why nukes? I'm just trying to get at the underlying question so the designers can answer it in full and to give you a quality answer.


3) Is it the designer's intention for engies to have the worst damaging pet between a charmed melee/soldier pet, a slayer, and a instilled/animaed demon?

A charmed pet cannot really be brought into this equation to get a good comparison, as MOBs are on a different scale than created pets. The Bureaucrat pet would work better here. That said, each pet has it's own place, and an MP pet with those buffs on it will most probably outdamage an Engineer bot. But what does the Engineer pet have on it in this situation? The Engineer also has a variety of buffs to give to his/her bot. Again, this is something I'd like to test on the test server.

4) The 200 game is the same as the 180 which is the same as the 160 which is the same as the 140 and for some the same as the 120 game. Partially due to repition, partially due to lack of difficulty. Obviously, what was difficult at 120 or 130 is trivial by 160 let alone 200. Is there any plans to change this before Shadowlands?

Yes. Top levels will find increasingly harder things to fight and different things to do (lets give land control as an obvious example). Also, you'll be glad to know, that risk versus reward is an issue that the designers are particularly looking at (Vets versus Aces is the typical example).

5) Engies are near the bottom for damage output and probably at the very bottom for strength of nano lines and team desirability for such nano lines. Is this by design or is this the by-product of having made many new weapons and augmentations that have brought weapon damage to near double the original game's release?

There's no denying that augmentations made to other professions and the game in general have affected every profession in some way. Engineers are by design not a damage class, but a support class. I understand the nano line is one particular area that you want to be looked at, so I will certainly follow up on it. But, you guys are part of my ammo. How does your line lack? NCU too costly? Buffs not effective enough? Not enough different types of buffs? Give me that ammo for the designers.

6) Tarasque is on an 18 hour spawn. Is it the desire of FC to make all Tarasque drops impossible for anyone but the hardcore members of "uber" guilds to get, as it is now, and additionally compound the effect by making the items very twinkable? This question applies to the desirable unique spawns as well.

I'll start with the twinking - how twinkable are you referring to? For instance, the Edge of Tarasque currently can only be weilded by a small amount of players (and Enforcers too). It is not very twinkable at all. Alot of these items are also nodrop, meaning you can't give them to another player to twink with. If you can give me some names of twinkable items, we will certainly look into them. Onto the spawns itself, MOBs such as Tarasque are meant to be brought down by a large number of groups. In many cases, guilds will group together of course. But that's not to say that loot from Tarasque is for uber guilds only. If you get a good number of guildless players together, they definately able to claim looting rights to a spawn. This also works with groups of smaller and medium guilds as well. Unique loot should be hard to get, and there should be benefits from being in an organisation, but nothing is limited to just for 'uber' guilds only.

7) AO's skill req system is being eroded by a new level req system that looks su****iously unlike AO. There have been loud complaints which have been met by an explanation that made the problems seem far deeper than people believed. Will AOers get any further explanation on this issue?

The new nano requirements have been explained quite alot on the boards, and no, the information won't stop there. What unanswered questions are you looking at?

8) Between 125 and 190 there is only 60 points of direct skill pumping available. This is about 12% of the total skill pumping in the game yet constitutes about 60% of the experience. Are these levels meant to be this monotonous?

We aren't too sure what you mean here. 60 points in what respect? Only raising skills by 60 points? We just need a bit more information before it can be answered.

9) Nanomage stink to high heaven and are getting worse. Any plans for fixes?

Yes. Homo Nano are going to get some more benefits. I can't say juuuuuust yet what this entails, but I'll get on someone's nerves to see if I can give you a sneak peek as soon as possible.

10) Why do most of the new dungeons like Camelot and Steps of Madness have so little to offer? Camelot is filled with space yet there are only 2 mobs worth killing. Steps has very little worth getting as well.

Well, it comes down in terms of offering what? Steps of Madness is currently an introductory dungeon and offers a variety of MOBs as well as loot. What do you suggest is lacking? Camelot, also has alot to offer (look how many people are often in there). It offers great loot, a PvP experience and, yes, although a lack of dofferent MOBs, its aim is on the atmosphere and a means to the final room. So, what do you suggust is lacking in that dungeon? Different MOBs, or more? I know the worldbuilders love feedback.
