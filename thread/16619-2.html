To be fair to funcom (and specifically Cz and Cosmic) dealing with boards like this is a very difficult matter. Often any participation in debate (particularly in contentious debate) is likely to force further participation and you quickly find yourself engaged in argument, or being seen as taking sides on an issue.

Similarly, taking a position on 'Funcom's plans for NTs' for example, without having clearly thought through and quite detailed proposals would probably end up putting Funcom in an even worse light than now.

It's a very difficult thing to balance, but I would have thought that the key role that CZ and Cosmic could play would be one of distilling the rants, raves, suggestions and proposals into a form that could usefully be used by the design team. If they do that, whether or not they post here, then their job is done.

Still, as I mentioned above, when a community run by Funcom themselves expresses such strong feelings of alienation and the feeling of being ignored and put on one side.... then it might be wise for Funcom to at least communicate with that community in some small way, just to let them know that the community has a purpose for Funcom.

Just my two credits worth.
