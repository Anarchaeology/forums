My final words, and about the booster patch...er pack
Most of you don't know me, and I don't know you. Most of you won't care about htis message and will decide to flame or troll or hold out your ph4t l3wt b4gs (although I think Crispy is gone for good...), but I don't care. My time in this game is about to run out as I have cancelled my acount, and I wished to post at least one last time before my privelage is taken away.

I may not have played this game as long as others (I started at release...so beta players still beat me), but I think I have played long enough to gather an adequate sample of the Rubi-Ka world. I have also not played for the entire time since my intial purchase, but have watched the progression of the game throughout.

Timeline of my account:
- played for first free month +1 more - cancelled;
- played for the free month Funcom gave me at Christmas +1 more - cancelled;
- played for the month after the full IPR was released +1 more - cancelled for good.

I have also spent some time in the world of EQ, so can draw upon that experience as well when I make my opinions. In the real world , I am a Medical Software R&D Engineer, so I believe I have a good handle on the professional aspects of this game. That being said and my background laid out, I would like to move on to my actual opinions.

When I first bought this game, it was a decision between this and the expansion pack for Diablo 2. It was a hard decision, and despite having friends that played Diablo 2 and numerous characters, I opted for Anarchy. The Sci-fi realm of RPGs is not explored enough in the game industry I feel. Many games of this environment tend to focus on FPS, action, adventure, or some combination of these elements with a dash of RPG. But to have a MMORPG in the Scif-fi world is truly a fine idea. This, and the pretty pictures on the AO box made me pull the trigger and grab the game.

After installing (which was very painless...sorry for all the people who were not so lucky), I entered the world and was stunned. Imagery is a big part for me in any game, and to this day I have to say Anarchy is one of the prettiest games I have ever played. My first character was an Neutral NT named Dyton.

In the beginning, I met a character named Viro, and aspired to be like him. His character image was aluring, being a solitus male Adventurer, long black hair, a black trenchcoat, and carrying a katana. He was vary slick, and I wanted to be like that too. So I started using swords as a weapon (horrible choice...) ...time passed and I grew frustrated with this character (lack of IP being the main culprit) and retired him at lvl 32.

Soon, I introduced a Real-life friend to the game, and we made new characters to play together. We ended up going through this process about 3 times before we settled with characters we liked, and started climbing the level ladder. We managed to progress to about lvl 70, and things just went to hell. It seems that there is a very distinct point in playing the game when it becomes corrupted and changes. And I don't mean a change in just that character, but a change in playing the game entirely. There is a point where you stop 'playing a character' and start 'playing a game'. Now I'm not a role-player by any means. I don't speak in Old English at the local Renaissance festival, nor do I play D&D. I am at heart a game player, not a role player. But in Anarchy I felt good building a character that could be different from most others. There is a point when that option is no longer viable, and you must conform. This ruined the game for me.

I quit after that and went on to other things. I came back for Christmas to see what had changed. Some things were better, but many bugs persisted, and my afore mentioned dislike was still there. After experimenting with new character alternatives for my free month +another, I left again.

I returned when I heard of the full IPR, and thought that I could reclaim some characters. During this time, I realized that even after a reclamation, my characters were still inadequate. So I then decided to start a new character - one of Funcom's love children - an MA. In about a week, I hit lvl 104...a far cry from the challenges of my earlier experiences. And I know some of you uber 1337 are gonna say "1 week!! j00 sux0rz. It took me 1 DAY" blah blah blah...So anyway, I hit lvl 104, then took my time to work up to 113 and stopped cold. I realized that there was nothing left in the game. ql 200 warez was just about 2 lvls away for my character, and I have seen every corner of the Rubi-ka world. After that, there is nothing except mission after mission on the long road to 200 with the ocassional stop at your local static mission to camp for hours for an item...yeah right.

I started some alts and had the most fun playing with any characters from about lvl 15 to lvl 65. I was on the brink of quitting the game when something pushed me over the edge. I was playing with an alt trader in a mission, and ran out of ammo for my shotgun. The gun didn't unequip and fall into my inventory, but rather stayed in my weapon slot. However, when I attacked, I hit with my fists and there was no visual representation of my weapon. My team progressed through the mission with me only calming and team skill wrangling the whole time. I found a chest with some shells and decide to reload my gun. Well, the reload didn't work. So I tried to unequip the gun manually. The little yellow unequip bar appeared in the top left of the screen, but my gun didn't come off. I tried this a few more times with no success. So I tried equipping a lower ql gun to force the main one off. That didn't work either. By this time my team had made it to the boss and we decided to fight it without my gun. We beat it pretty easy and collected the loot. Then a fixer in the team warped us out to the grid. When I zoned to Omni-Trade, my gun was no where to be found. It wasn't on me, nor in my inventory. Before we left the mission area, I had 2 slots in my inventory open so it's not like it couldn't drop into the inventory from lack of space. I petitioned and after a heated conversation with an ARK and her talking to her supervisor, she informed me that it was a known problem and their reimbursement didn't cover it...

Bye bye Funcom. It's not even like the gun meant much to me. The thing that bothered me was the totally inadequate way in which it was handled. And further more, ARKs are totally useless in this game. Their only really use is to help n00bs that don't know how to play. Every encounter I have had with an ARK, and I mean EVERY, has been the ARK basically saying "...It is a known problem, and there is nothing I can do about it" That is the response to about 20 petitions total in my AO lifetime. These varied from dying from ghost dmg, to the gun thing I explained, to not being able to multi-weild some pistols I met every req for, and other things. The ARKs have no power and no use...but that is another story, and one I may go into at another time.

Now, about this booster pack...The only thing the 'booster' part of the description means is that it is a money booster for Funcom. Paypatch is totally accurate. This idea needs to be squashed now, and hard. Funcom has stated that this is a new concept that they are trying to initiate. That means it is our duty to smash it before it catches on with other companies and we end up paying for all additions to a game along with monthly fees.

I was never a fan of expansions in the first place. The idea of paying for an addition to a game I already payed for, and this addition couldn't be played on it's own without the base edition bothers me. And now this booster idea takes that one step further. As Funcom has stated, they weren't able to get the full expansion out in time, so they are releasing this add-on as a tidbit until the expansion is done. Translation - they get to charge you twice now (Booster + Shadowlands) for something that they were going to charge you once before (Shadowlands alone). And there are still bugs existent from launch that haven't been fixed...

Let me tell you, if this happened in my line of work, people would die. I'm being totally honest, people would die. I would be in jail, and the company I work for would be out of business. Luckily there is the FDA and other regulations to keep me in line...

All in all, this post was basically a /rant - read it how you want, reply how you want. The message is don't let Funcom rake you over the coals and take your money. Stop this booster idea now before it get's out of hand. If you are a fan of the booster idea, please re-evaluate it before you go balls-to-the-wall.

Finally, I would like to say that this game is not all bad, and I have had fun at points. And I would like to see if there are any other people out there that have one of these...Cause I do, and I don't know why.
