Nasty Bugs since 14.6
Please check on the following things as there is something very strange going on:
- Distracting Sphere pets (for the MP) really screw up combat. For some reason they cause they are attacking to save up all of their attacks and fire them off at once.
e.g.
...combat starts, combat and distract pet attack
...distract pet effect goes off on the mob
...mob stands there looking stupid for a while while we beat on it.
...suddenly get spammed with 5-15 attacks (and sometimes die) instantly (in less than 1/4 second)

-Melee and ranged attacks now black hole just like direct damage (i.e. you attack...and nothing is displayed in chat). This is not my imagination as I have watched it closely. Fairly often an attack/recharge cycle will go by and I will get no You missed X, X Parried your attack, or You hit X for Y damage message at all. Sometimes this will go on for 3-4 combat rounds. Sometimes the whole fight. I still get all the messages about being damaged by the mob and I still see my pets hurting the mob.

-Health bar does not update properly.
I can be in a fight and appear not to have been hurt at all (client not getting damage notification), but use a heal stim on myself and heal more than 1/4 of my health (which the client was never told I lost). I have died with no message previously due to this and really hope you can track it down.

All 3 of the above are new since 14.6.0
