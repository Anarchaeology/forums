Here is how it could work. A guild has to specify which hours in the day it will be vulnerable to attack. They can choose anywhere from no hours to the entire day. The catch would be that the more hours they are vulnerable, the greater the benefits the guild could get. Anything from lower Guild house rental fee, to guild discounts on equipment can be given.
