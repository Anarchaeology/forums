ShieldAC vs. ReflectAC?
I have several "reflect" bracers; some of them have ShieldAC while others are of the ReflectAC variety. I thought I knew the difference between them but now I'm not so sure - can someone enlighten me?

Reflect bracer
modify self maximum melee reflect
This is the % of damage that gets reflected away from you
modify self reflect melee damage
This is the max amount of damage that gets reflected back on the attacker for each attack done on you.

Shield bracer
modify self shield melee damage
This is the amount of damage that gets inflicted back on the attacker for each attack done on you.

Also, note that Reflect(damagetype)AC = the percentage of that damagetype that doesn't hit you, simply.

With a ReflectProjectileAC of 10, a 100-point attack does 90 pts of damage to you, as 10% is reflected away from you.
