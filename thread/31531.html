You must keep the goal of an elite server in mind. The goal of these servers is to redo the game the way they always wanted to but were too afraid to for fear of scaring off the newbs.

The changes made to the elite server would be focused on:
a. Maintaining a healthy economy.
b. Removing the ability to powerlevel.
c. Making high levels something truely difficult to accomplish.
d. Putting more emphasis on PvP and roleplay.
e. Adding more risk.

The Elite server is also a chance for them to go nerf crazy and tweak things in ways they never dared on a normal server. If things go horribly wrong you can always wipe the server and try again with new rules (Siege perilous was wiped a couple times when the old rules didnt work out)!
