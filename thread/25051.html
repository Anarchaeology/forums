HP DOES MATTER
Everyone who says HP doesn't matter in PvP, even today it matters to anyone with enough.

These numbers are taken from antiguardians damage calc, using 1200 attack rating (yes, this is a worst-case scenario).

Krutt Q184 Max Crit: 5248 / 2624 PvP (2624 is 40% of 6560)
Vektor Q180 Max Crit: 4792 / 2396 PvP (2396 is 40% of 5990)
Ithaca Q199 Max Crit: 3712 / 1856 PvP (1856 is 40% of 4640)

This means that worst-case scenario -- a trader with 1200 attack rating carrying a Krutt special with UVC will never hit the 40% cap on anyone with more than 6560 HP.

So we are left with Full Auto and Aimed Shot as the only 2 ways to hit 40% on an enforcer (or anyone) with more than 6560 HP.

If I'm wrong here, please correct me. But I'm sticking to my assumption that HP = GOOD™.


Editorial:

When UVC is gone, mark my words, noone is going to be able to kill anyone. HP will matter much, much more. Zoning is going to be a whole new problem -- now, you just need to kill someone before they zone. Since it's going to be lots harder to kill people, EVERYONE will run to the zone and will actually make it Of course, if the 15% scopes make enough of a difference, noone will be able to kill anyone except those with scopes. But that remains to be seen
