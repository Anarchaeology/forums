Nano resist is definitely not broken at lower levels, where skill levels are more reasonable. I would imagine the reason it gets more broken the higher you go is because mobs have pumped up skills, and unlike evades nano resist is all or nothing. At low levels, my nano resist might be maxed at 150, and the nano skills of the things I'm fighting might be only 200, but at higher levels I would imagine it would be impossible to have a skill that was that close to a mob's skill in anything.

At lower levels (10-20ish), I resist stuff a lot (probably at least 40% of the time with my maxed nano skills chars). By medium low level (40-50ish) I find that the resist rate has dropped to 10% or so.
