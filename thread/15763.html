Overequipping and what ppl don't seem to get about it
Hello there,

i'm Elizabet 'Needlz' Dartt, your average Level 200 Doc. Just for the records, i've been 'playing' this piece of Software since Beta4, i don't exactly like FC and i REALLY don't like what they've made of such a promising game. I usually don't bother reading and/or posting on the boards cos its usually 99% Bulldrek/Whining going on, but when i read about the preliminary 14.2 patch notes and certain ppls responses about em, i felt like voicing my opinion and here it is.

The new OE rule set is probably the first right step FC is doing in quite some time now, Overequipping is what kills the game in the long run (even more then the obvious lack of high level content, but that can be added, eventually).

First up, OE frags pvp balance over and over again (Hey i love getting ganked with 3 shots by ppl 50 levels below me).
Its unfair to new players, players who don't have high level friends or ppl who don't have money to burn for ebay stuff.
OE unbalances the game and makes the Game harder for everyone in the end(If FC sees that monsters are easily overcome, they just make em harder, so everyone who's not OE'd is hosed).
OE keeps us from getting new 'toys' from FC (like they said, they felt reluctant to add new beneficial gear as long as everyone overequipped like mad).

these are just a few reasons i can think of right now, but everyone should be concerned about at least one point.

The basic idea of limiting OE is good, but i don't think it FC's approach (20% skill lock) will yield the best results, so i've come up with my own idea.

I'm a solitus, my Abilities (Sta/Str/Agi etc.) hard cap on level 155 at 480 Points (472 for Str, prolly someone at FC was drunk), being a Doc i have a preference for Biomech armor (QL200 req 501 Sta 551 Psy) using a combination of Implants and Buffs i can squeeze into QL200 Armor at 155 and even lower. Using FC's new OE rules (20% withhin Skill range) i could still wear it at that level (480 * 1,20 = 576), and even below it, without penalty (its even easier for Sta/Str Armor as there are way better buffs around for that).

Now i DON'T think that FC intended QL200 Armor to be used by Lev 150 or lower Players, thats why i suggest to limit overequipping NOT ONLY to SKILL but also to Player LEVEL, and reduce the 'no penalty' margin to 15%, 16 to 30% 'useable with penalty' (Preferrably dynamic reduction according to the degree over overequipment, 16-30%. Or static 25% reduction if the FC program boys can't get the dynamic solution done) and a total 'unuseable' lock to 30+% (If the piece of equipment you want to equip is 30+% out of your level and/or Skill range, you won't be able to use it). This should be for EVERY piece of equipment, Armor (no more level 50's with QL200 Armor), Weapons (no more Level 15's with QL100 Guns), Nano Formulas (no more Friggin Lev 100 MPs/Engineers with QL200 pets), Implants (Thats a special topic, i'd say make the check there with the ability req and Level only, and not with Treatment) and even Tradeskill items.

Example:
Solitus is the basic template, so they should be able to use any armor type without penalty with fully developed Abilities and without implants (480 Base * 1,15 margin = 552, enough for any QL200 Armor piece). But as long as you are not Level 174, you still can't wear it without penalty (174*1,15 = 200,1). Other races just have an easier time 'buffing' into their type of armor (or make em basically unuseable for that race, i really don't think FC made Nano Armor with Atroxes in mind).

A level 100 goes on a 60% difficulty mission (which should be a real challenge for him, considering he doesn't posess any UBER gear), gets lucky and finds a level 113 Gun, his stats are just a lil too low to equip it, so he pays a Trader (Yea you guys are always the buff *****es =P) to buff him that extra little bit. He's withhin the 15% in level and Skill, so he's able to use his new gun without penalty.

Equipped with his new gun, Mr. Level 100 goes on a new mission, lets say 70% now (a real challenge). He finds a QL120 Armor piece, its Agi/Str Armor, being an Opifex he meets the requs on skill right away, he's able to equip it (below 31% OE) but the Level lock penalizes him for 20% (25% for lazy programmers), so his new armor offers less protection then his current QL110 armor, so he just saves it for later.

Now we take one of our typical overequipper (lets call em 'Freen'). Its an 137 Atrox Enfocer wielding a QL200 Beam, using a customized set of implants, Self buffs and challenger ability he's able to reach the Skill required for the weapon. FC's overequipping approach would let him wield it, but it would be only about as good as a QL160 beam once the buffs drop. I'm a meanie =D so i say thats too much still, cos he couldn't get a QL200 weap on his own anyway.

This is good for you because:
Its unlikely you can aquire Gear you can't use on your own. (Like i read that guys post about, 'I'm a Level 50 Soldier, i FIND a QL100 Nova' let me tell you YOU DON'T find QL100 gear in Missions a Lev 50 can survive other then by running and even that is tricky.)
You can be sure that your PvP opponent isn't 'Twinked' and thus unkillable.
Monsters might appear harder at first, but FC will make em easier because everyone dishes out about the same damage overall for their level (Yes, i still have some faith in FC =P)
It makes the game more enjoyable for everyone.
It gives you a reason to level up (Yep FC, no one likes Lev 162 to 189, and theres little reason to do em).
With OE under control, FC will be less reluctant to 'give' us new Gear to play with.
Gives lower level equipment a 'reason to exist'.
With OE'ers out of the way, there is no excuse for FC left, to readjust the Skill reqs on Nano Formulas according to level. (Every Level 100 should be able to use Every QL100 and below Nano of his class, with maxed skills but WITHOUT Implants! I won't hide the fact that it REALLY pisses me off that i can't use about 15 Doc Nanos at 200, even tho i got very good plants).

Now don't think of this as a nerf (All ppl who don't have the resources to OE won't think of it that way anyway), and don't think of me as the big bad Lev 200 that has everything (i mostly play a 82 Enforcer now whose highest piece of equipment is a QL115 Native Alloy Staff she bought with her own funds. And yes i know i wouldn't be able to use it with my rules in effect, but i'm cool with that).
Think of it as a problem that concerns us all (as players) and FC (as they can't keep it under control). Something has to be done about OE, its THE biggest problem of this game.
I spend some of my precious time on this article, so at least give it some consideration.
