I need more quickbars
A rather Agent-specific request, although other professions might find this useful, too.

At the moment, the quickbars are the only useful place where to store nano formulas, the favourites window is too tiny and rather forgetful. For most professions this might be enough, but for agents who use visual profession nanos a lot, there is simply not enough space to fit all.
Currently, all my quickbars are completely filled, and every time I learn a new nano I have to throw an old one out, and if someone requests an odd buff from me, I have to dive into the depths of the nano program window to find one icon among hundreds.

I'll put a few suggestions up for discussion, at least one of them should be possible to implement

1. More quickbars
The most simple solution. Simply add 10 or 20 more quickbars, and make hotkeys to access them. For example, LeftShift+1 and RightShift+1 could bring up different sets of quickbars, or you could have Alt+Shift+1 and Ctrl+Shift+1. Since the quickbars are stored locally anyway, this would even be a client-only change, not affecting the server in any way.

2. A better Favourites window
Make a larger Favourites window for nanoformulas, that undocks from the right-hand menu bar or is completely separate from it, and that allows the users to freely arrange their nano formulas. Probably the best solution, since it frees the quickbars for the task they were originally intended for, providing quick access to nanos used in combat

3. One set of quickbars per visual profession
An Agent-only fix. Keep the 10 quickbars as they are, but give agents one complete set for each profession, automatically switching when a fp/assume/mimic nano is casted or runs out. If I'm in a visual profession, I don't need access to the nanos of the other professions, this would actually allow agents to arrange their nanos just like the other professions do.


What do you think?
