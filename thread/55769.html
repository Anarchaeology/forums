PvP is Fun
No matter how it is sliced and what area you are fighting it is fun.
I love gridding into Omni and jumping into the Arena. Suddenly I have everyone's attention while they target me. If I pop someone I KNOW I am going to have that many specials ripping me apart.

If I attack someone there I hear them whine to me. Those same people gank me while I just stand there. I am Clan of course. I expect it.

Fighting a doc yesterday and HE is getting outside heals... go figure. I love it. Getting ganked by a smurfy Fixer myself... gotta love it... sucked for him when his doc stopped healing him though.

In Tir the Omnis show up super buffed in gank mode. 1v1 has disappeared from the Arena on most occasions. You should expect to get hit if you are a killable target. I was fighting a nice adventurer last night when some MA that I just beat 3x jumps in... then another Omni attacks.. then another.. classic. The class the adventurer showed by stopping was honorable.

I love the hypocrisy. You ganked me! I gank you. What is a gank? People standing in the Arena are there to fight. I cannot walk up to you and ask for a fight or I have 7 other guys jumping me. You cry. You send me nasty tells. Then you come to Tir arena and gank the 100 lvl player. Bad. Tsk Tsk.

You cry about GA. nerf nerf! I didn't program the game. I don't control it. I play the game. Stop hassling me about it. I had a MA last night complaining to ME about crit buffs. Funny.

Anyway all of the hysteria and contradictions truly make PvP fun. To really make PvP more fun they should move the doors away from the Arena so that more people will die instead of zone.

And keep the titles. Widen the 100 point spread. Not getting PvP xp by killing someone too high is just silly.

I love all of you. You entertain me. And that is what a game is for.

2ho? simple.

Grid in. Run north. Zone into the nearest bunker...refreshes grace...look around, take notice of how many omnis and how many clanners there are. Use warchat as a source of communication and coordination with other players.

There are several 'hotspots' in 2ho:
1. 'bunkers' is commonly referred to as the boxed in corner that a lot of clanners prepare from to charge the 'hill.' The bunkers are a main source of retreat for clanners, and any assaulting omnis. It stretches from North to Northeast of the outpost.
2. The 'hill' is across from the bunkers and has a large omni-tek logo on it.
3. The 'coffee shop/grid area' is no doubt, the first place you see when you enter 2ho
4. The 'helipad' is south from the grid terminal. a lot of people use this area as a rendezvous, but if you're caught, youll have to be careful since the only zone nearby is a bunker which is 25% inside.
5. Garden. The NW most area, not many people fight here.
6. Whompa. Omni's main source of retreat...wars go to crap when clanners camp the 10-3 whompa..very lame imho. It becomes omnis just zoning in to debuff people then zone back out. But atleast it pushes us back a bit.
7. Gate. The Gate is located to the left of the whompa, where youll be aggro'ed by 4-6 'omni pol XS'...They hit for 600 each and have a high attack rating. A lot of the time omnis camp out here stupidly expecting clanners to 'come to them' and get raped by the guards...The other night there was a 'phony war' between hill campers and guard huggers...Keep in mind that these gate guards were only improved/expanded AFTER 14.5. Omnis won't do this on the most common occasion, but when they do, the battle becomes very stale.

KEY PEOPLE:
Mikal Watts
Ron Mcbane
level 100ish mobs

I have no idea what these guys are for, but they have 1k damage shields. Don't AoE snare or use cluster bullets on anyone nearby them, you'll be aggroed and the reflect will kill you. A common thing a lot of clanners do against omni NTs is stand by ol' ron and let a AoE nuke hit them...NT is in hot pursuit. They are omni npc's btw, so check your tab button on who you're targetting before you fire a shot.

The level 100ish mobs were increased in quantity in 14.5...low levels literally have no viable way of pvp'ing there now if they are clanners. about 15% of the pvp area is safe to walk in for them without the fear of being jumped by an npc.

All in all, being that this is an omni outpost, without surprise the omni's have the upper hand. They have 2 more safe zones than clanners do, no guards to worry about (unless an omni enforcer mongos), and will never accidentally tab an npc that has a 1k damage shield.

At times it will be empty...at times it will be too populated of one side, and at times it will be fun. 2ho, i feel, will never really 'suck' because things seem to change every day.
