I think some of these ideas are wonderful!

I doubt my soldier would be considered "uber" by the higher ups in any sense of the word, but I've been getting my feet wet in the past few months and have scratched a few notches into my novaflow. As fun as it often can be to bolt across the countryside and blast away meanies (in the wake of some noticably more colorful professions) I can't help but wonder what awaits me in the post 100 life of a Ruki-Ka soldier.

What really would appeal is the simple knowledge that one managed to nurture something unique and not available to those other *colorful* classes; something that made countless rounds of ammo worth firing off in the first place under the proud title of Soldier. I'd hate to think that the only *unique* quality of the soldier class is that they have the softest voice in the forums.

At the risk of restating the obvious, we are warriors. We wield guns, and are supposed to wield then like no other. Any implementation that demonstrates this specialization has already won my vote. Top on my list are nano lines that improve our prowess using guns (not the dmg output of the gun itself, but rather our ability to USE it) such as faster recharge on burst, full auto and flingshot, or even on snipe. Second on my list are soldier specific items. Somewhere in these forums (sorry this is my first post I don't know who to quote from a specific post) Sredni mentioned an ammo feed belt that can be fitted into one of our utility slots and would modify the reload rate of our guns. That's thinking! That's what a combat-hardened soldier would have.

Also, I agree that AC buffs, damage buffs, health buffs and the like should be left to the nano-trained classes. We're not mages, (although many soldiers are medics) we're mobile firepower.

All in all, soldiers need something that can make them a saught after asset in combat. Why? Not just to make it easy to find teams, but because that is what soldiers are about. I have no problem with being considered a "gun toting grunt" as Zeroshift (I believe) put it. There is definately logic in a team designating a class such as ours as tools for combat situations. That is, after all, what soldiers are. Which is why one suggestion in particular was so ingenius: "Throw Yourself On The Grenade". I have only one thing that I can add as a suggestion to that already great idea. Instead of a running nano it would make sense as a last minute, do-or-die recourse. It should available only to be cast at the descretion of the soldier, when the team looks doomed, hope is lost, and as a heat of the moment decision. In my mind that would seem a little more true to the concept of this nano. As an added bonus to saving the lives of your teammates, it has the advantage of ****ing up the role of the soldier by putting in their hands a feeling of heroic deed when they sacrifice themselves for the survival of the team (they could have just as easily fled shamelessly, albiet still supporting a pulse, whilst under the protection of TMS while their teammates dropped like flies all around them and went to nerfed-class heaven).

Okay. That's it. A post that was supposed to be a vote of support wasn't supposed to be this long.

Just wanted to pass along my thoughts.
