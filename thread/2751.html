I like this idea. Funcom already has a number of worldbuilders busily creating new dungeons, items, critters, and clothing. Why not augment this with volunteer artwork from customers?

As it stands, Funcom has art-director types who have to OK work from their artists. Why not increase the submission load on them? If they have the time, they can switch from sifting through FC-employee made stuff to customer-submitted stuff.

I think it would be great to be able to go to a clothing retailer and discover that a new pair of boots were available, designed by another player. As a bounty for good work, give the character that designed the clothing a small cut of the profits from articles of clothing sold: I design a trenchcoat, FC puts it into play, somebody buys it for 8k, I get 80credits for it. It should be chump-changes, for sure, but it would add up for very popular/good designs.
