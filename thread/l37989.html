PvP and the ONE major issue that ruins it all.
Here is my experience in meetmedere yesterday. I decided it was time for the Clanners to stand up and fight back for once, so I organized a decent sized raid to free meetmedere from thoose Omni scum! We had 3 or 4 groups (I cant remeber) and it took alot of time to get everyone together and crap. So we get to meetmedere and we succeded in taking it back. We did have a few with us who thought it was funny to reclaim camp... We got that to end pretty quickly (Unlike Omnis who never stop, I was camped there for 2 hours once). Now here is where the problem comes in, I just learned how Omni is always in control of meetmedere. Within an hour about 4 70ish guys come in.... they would have a 30ish and a 50ish guy come in and snipe us, then the 70s would totally annihlate us since they were in the 50ish guys team. They never even had to leave the 75% area. Now I think PvP can be fun, but I don't think that have 4 70's gangup on 30-50 guys is much fair, it basically took the fun right out of the raid. I think the level limit should STILL apply in team, there is no way a lvl 70 should beable to group with a lvl 10 then the lvl 10 can start a fight and the lvl 70 comes in a just annihilates the lvl 10.... its pointless and takes alot of the fun out of PvP. Now I have NO problem with say a 60 coming up and attacking me when Im lvl 50.... atleast I stand SOMEWHAT of a chance. But when a guy is 30 levels higher its pointless and dumb. So funcom please keep the level limit in on teams, it will not only make PvP more fun for everyone (Except 70s who get their jollies out of killing lvl 30s) but also more fair.

I admit, I have done this a few times and it's pretty damn amusing.

What you do to get around the level limit is, say you are level 20, and see another level 20 but don't want to risk dying and there's a level 70 of your same faction near by.

Have the level 70 invite you BEFORE you enter battle. Now find that level 20 you wanted to kill and smack them, then click the "yes" button in your team window and voila! You are now teamed with the level 70, and the 70 has free reign to attack anyone who attacked you.
