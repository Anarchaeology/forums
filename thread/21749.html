Nano Armor
Nano Armor – make them useful to NTs. It is good that many new items are appearing in the game, but improve on the old ones like nano armor, which is soon to become obsolete.

The dev team should add bonuses to this armor so they are more favorable to the nano classes.
Some ideas:
- Bonus to nano points
- Faster nano rejuvenation
- Increased nano resistance
- Quicker execution
- Bonus damage to offensive nanos

I know the dev team can be creative.
