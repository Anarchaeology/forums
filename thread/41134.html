Any more PF changes/Dungeons ?
If im bored of missions and dont want to do anymore what can i do?

5 dungeons - camped by red con 120+ players, no matter the level of dungeon. I went into Steps of Madness whilst mobs were green to me, the whole place was full of people killing what are grey cons to them, some would even kill the ones we were.

Outdoor hunting - Andromeda.

Thats... um... it. Levels 1 - 200, which were meant to take 4 years to do, sees 5 completly camped dungeons shared by the entire population of RK1, or 1 zone.

Other outdoor hunting is random clusters of iceskating mobs.

Wheres all the life in the outdoor zones ? Why arent there any NPCs explaining what the places are (all those towns, military bases, mines, weird structures, parks etc) why are the mobs randomly placed in a certain area and just iceskate about rather than placed in a specific way so we can strategise ways to pull a camp ?

I would like to see the towns populated, and the 'camps' given more life, and set up in a structured manner, guardmobs on the entrance, mobs patrolling round the camp itself, a boss in the middle with his own guards. Monsters can also be placed more logically outdoors, woods full of wolves, vultures clustered around a high cliff, grassy plains populated by Golfes with the occasional predator cat, criminal type npc's in isolated refuges, anun tearers in certain places etc..

Now, if we are in a wolf area and we have a mission in a cave, why isnt the mission a cave type full of wolves? If you do a mission in a cyborg base, why isnt it the base style mission populated by cyborgs ? Little touches like that would make the world all so much more believable.




I *really* hope Shadowlands just isnt more dead and deserted places with no soul, story and real identity to do missions in.

Im so sick of missions, this is my 3rd resubscription now, and like all the previous ones its fun for a few months and then you realise you cant face another mission.... and theres nothing else to do. The whole thing is beginning to feel like Nethack, random mobs in random missions, with random mobs scattered across the outdoor world.
