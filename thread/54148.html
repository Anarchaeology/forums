A New Home...
The twin suns of Rubi-Ka cast their piercing light down on the surface of the world, bringing life to many things, and driving others back into the shadows where they were more at home. A Heavy Carrier moved slowly through the skies above the river Stret, traveling south above the calm waters.

"Now Boss, You know I've only been here with you flying, but are you sure this is the right way ?", said the large Atrox piloting the carrier, a large hand rubbing the bright pink mohawk that stood atop his head. Next to him sat Garret "Valaik" Clasen, who's feet were resting lazily on the console as he slouched down in his seat trying to rest after the mornings excitement.

"Yeah Flat, We're almost there. Just keep an eye out for an L-Shaped rock in the Stret off to your right, when you see it cut back on the throttle and I'll take over.", said Valaik with a grin as he leaned back again to get comfortable, checking his wrist comp to confirm their position.

The carrier cruised along, slowed considerably by the amount of cargo in the hold, recently evacuated from a warehouse in the north. Five minutes passed and the pilot, Malcom "Flatbed" Giscombe, spotted the L-Shaped rock off to the right at the same time Valaik's wrist comp beeped twice. Pulling back on the throttle he put the large transport ship into a hover above the waters 300 meters out from the rock he had spotted. Valaik sat up slowly and took the controls that were duplicated on his side of the compartment, pushing the throttle forward as he dropped the nose of the carrier towards the water, heading toward the L-Shaped rock near the shore.

"I got it from here, just sit back and enjoy the ride.", spoke Valaik with a grin as he lined up the carrier to fly over the low spot on the rock ahead.

The carrier moved forward slowly moving closer to the rock ahead, Flabed noticed that the angle they were comming in at caused the L-Shaped rock and the one behind it to form a V, and the carrier was moving towards the exact center of that V. As the first rock was passed Valaik slid the throttle up a little more and kept the course steady, the carrier heading toward a wall of solid rock that jutted up 150 Meters from the water below. The ship moved towards the rock with no sign of slowing or changing course and Flatbed glanced over at Valaik with fear in his eyes.

"Boss, you know I hate the Reclaim Terminals Right?", snapped Flatbed as he glanced from Valaik to the wall of rock ahead and then back again.

"Yep, Don't we all?", said Valaik with a laugh, still holding the course for the wall which was rapidly approaching.

"Boss....Wall...There's a wall there..Boss..BOSS!!....WALL!!!!", Screamed Flatbed as he covered his eyes, the carrier a meter from the wall, and then....nothing.

Flatbed uncovered his eyes as he heard Valaik laughing next to him and looked out the canopy to see a Tunnel dug from rock, lighted by glow-lamps along all four sides. Flatbed punched up the rear camera on the ship on the terminal in front of him and saw a Door grinding shut and beyond it the haze of a holographic image. Grinning, Flatbed shook his head, knowing he had done exactly what Valaik had expected him to do. Valaik flew the Carrier down the short tunnel to the hanger that took up the lower level of the Site Two warehouse, Parking it beside two similar Carriers and a line of Yalms in various states of disrepair. Blast doors on the far side of the hanger slowly slide open and a group of 10 workers hurried out across the hanger to the Carrier. Smiling happily Valaik hit the control that would open the cargo bay, letting the workers begin to unload the cargo and move it into the upper levels with the rest of the merchandise. Dropping a hand reasuringly on Flatbeds shoulder, Valaik made his way out of the Carrier, shaking the hands of the three Atrox that had helped get the stuff loaded, making sure he got their names so that he could remember them in case he needed their services again.

Confident that everything was in good hands now, Valaik made his way across the hanger towards the tunnel from which the workers had emerged. Passing the blast doors he caught the sent of plasma torches and in the distance saw the workers fusing the Plasteel support beams along the walls that would add needed stability to the tunnels that had been cut in the rock here. Stopping to talk with each worker Valaik made his way to the far end to the lift that would take him to the upper levels of site two.

"Geez, I really need to think of a good name for this place now...Site two just won't cut it.", Said Valaik with a chuckle as he tapped the code that would bring the lift back down.

The lift arrived, the doors splitting with a hiss of air, sliding smoothly back into the walls. Nodding with satisfaction Valaik stepped onto the lift and hit the button that would take him up to the command level, Remembering the last time he was here and the doors to the lift sliding open about 3 inches and then stopping, leaving him stuck on the hanger level until the engineers could get the lift fixed. The lift rose swiftly up its tube to the command level, starting and stopping with nearly no sense of movement. The doors slid open, and halfway along its track the left door caught and stopped. The nearby workers glanced towards the lift and laughed at nearly the same moment as Valaik did, Site two was far from complete but it was all they had left now. Stepping through the lift doors Valaik waved to one of the Engineers nearby.

"Make sure you get that lift working for me, Its alot easier than using the ladders that I had put in last time it messed up.", he spoke with a smile on his face, watching the engineer go immediately to work on finding out the problem with the lift door.

Slowly Valaik walked down the tunnel, glancing into the half complete Recreation Room and Comm Center as he moved along, stopping to chat with the Engineering teams as they were busy installing electronics on this level. At the end of the hall was the command room, The one place that was finished here. Valaik made his way to the door and placed his hand on the scanner next to it, letting it verify his identity as he slid his shades off for the retinal scan. Satisfied that he was who he appeared the computer released the locks on the door and let it slide open, spilling bright light into the hallway causing Valaik to shield his eyes with his arm.

"Damnit, I find the Engineer that left the lights up this high and I'm gonna put my boot in his ass.", growled Valaik as he slipped his shades on to protect his sensative eyes from the light.

Moving into the room he quickly located the lighting controls and cut the ambient light level down to half of what it was at. Sighing contently he took off his shades and moved to the chair that occupied the center of the room, dropping into it and smiling as the seat matched the contours of his body. Sitting up slowly he spun the chair around to face the far wall, its surface covered with Display Screens and readouts for the various base facilities and his interests around Rubi-Ka. Typing in a few commands he brought up what little information was still available to him on the Northern Storehouse after the destruction of its command center. One camera was still active and the main door had been breached. Scratching his chin for a moment Valaik keyed in the command to display the cameras signal on the main screen, static filled the screen for a moment as a picture struggled to form, the EM Grenade having caused slight damage to this cameras circuitry. Finally the picture resolved, the camera was the one stationed outside the command room, it had to drop from a hidden container in the ceiling so he did not use it often, but there was no need to be secretive now. The image displayed exactly what he thought it would, Two Dust Brigade Elites making their way down the coridor with the Officer behind them, the door to the Command room their was blown free in the explosion and lay across the hallway. One of the Elite troopers spotted the camera and signaled its location to his commander, who nodded to the soldier, and then the picture disappeared in a white flash as the Trooper fired on the camera.

Sighing Valaik leaned back into the chair, letting it again mold to his body and tried to relax. Moments latter the comm in the room beeped that he had an incomming message. Reluctantly he pressed the button that would acknowledge the signal.

"Boss, I found something down here that wasn't on the inventory list. Seems to be a case of Whiskey. Its not on the manifest so I was wondering what's up.", spoke the comm at Valaik's elbow in a bad electronic reproduction of Flatbeds gravely voice.

"Oh yeah, forgot about that stuff, It arrived the other day from offworld. Its Wolfie's stash, he had me hold it at the warehouse for safe keeping. Bring it up to the command center when you get a chance, would ya?", Spoke Valaik into the comm as he made plans to have the Engineers work on the comm lines in here. Slowly an Evil grin spread across Valaik's features as he thought of what he would tell Wolfie when he next saw him.

"Now what should we call this place?", Said Valaik as he looked slowly around the room.

"For now I guess we'll just call it Home."
