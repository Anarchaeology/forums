Gernades and Traps for Fixers
Why is it that Fixers can disable traps with bombs, yet we can't use them offensively. No profession is really the proficient in gernades, not even soldiers. Fixers are always looking for an alternative to the SMG, why not up color of our gernade skill and let us lob some gernades and drop some claymores?

Wouldn't it just be cool to trap a hallway in a mission then lure an enemy into it? Or maybe toss a few gernades into a room to lighten up the opposition?

As well as being the rogue/thief, Fixers have potential to be good demolitions experts. I mean, our disarm trap skills IS green.

Forget all the gank and grief complaints, there are ways to code around this. This is WAR!

Anyways, we always here in the story line about terrorist bomb attacks, but yet we never really see it. It would just be cool for an Omni to sneak into Tiir and cause a lil' mayhem with trapped doors. Funcom says its an inconvience to players? Yeah right. Get hit by a bomb and not die? Just sit down, pop a medpack and be on your merry way.

There could be some real potential here.
