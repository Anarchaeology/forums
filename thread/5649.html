How to solve 90% of the PvM imbalances
(This has probably been said before, but it's time this idea got FC's attention)

Over the past six months, one thing that has become clear is that the AC system in AO leaves a lot to be desired. For every 10 points in an AC, it takes 1pt of damage away from an attack. If for example there were two players, an Enforcer using a hammer and an MA using their fists attacking a mob with 1000AC, the following damage would occur:

(the following are just estimations of the damage dealt - although the modifiers for AC stand)

MA Dmg - Apprx 200 per hit vs 0 AC
Enf Dmg - Apprx 1000 per hit vs 0 AC

Vs a mob with 1000 Melee AC the AC deducts 1000/10 = 100dmg per hit

So the players in fact do the folowing damage:

MA - 100 per hit
Enf - 900 per hit

The MA will hit 4 or 5 times in the time it takes for the Enf to commit one swing, so over the same time period the MA will do 500 dmg while the Enf does 900, a 400pt difference.

What I propose is that the AC sytem is completely ovehauled from the ground up. Instead of reducing damage by a set number of points, it reduces damage by a percentage. The AC of the defender could be compared to the Attack rating of the attacker to determine the percentage by which damage is reduced. I'm not exactly sure of attack ratings and mob AC at higher levels so if someone could suggest a formula to determine the % I'd be extremely grateful.

This would completely balance the fast weapons that don't hit for much damage against the slow heavy-hitters like the sledgehammer.

If a method such as the above was implemented, and providing ACs on high lvl mobs were reduced, a lot of class imbalances would simply dissapear. MAs would no longer complain about being 'Minimum Damage Artists', Pistol users would be completely un-gimped, Agents and Soldiers would no longer complain about low dmg at high levels etc.

Please, tell me what you think, and give some suggestions as to the formula to determine.

Well, armor is a tricky thing due to differing physics behind weapons.

So as long as all armors and weapons interact the exact same way to each other (AC versus dmg amount), then fast weapons will always be useless ultimately.
