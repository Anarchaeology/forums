A solution to NPCs doing one shot kills, and also the dependancy on healers in PVM
I had an idea for reducing the total dependency on healers for teaming, based loosely on the EQ thing where your character falls over unconscious before dying.

What if, when "killed", instead of actually heading for the reclaim you were knocked down for a statutory time, say 3 mins, and ALL nanoprograms, both hostile and friendly were removed. Also you get rez effects exactly as if you had died. All monster agro is removed against you as if you have just zoned out and in again, so the monster that killed you would switch targets.

If you get hit again in this state you die, but if the 3mins runs out you can get up and recover without XP loss. (or maybe actually die at the end of the 3mins)

If someone uses a treatment lab on you, you can get up immediately. (First aid and heal nanos dont work at this point)

Mobs can still aggro on you as normal if they see you first, so teammates should try to shield you as best they can.

This would have no effect on soloing as the next hit the mob landed would kill you anyway.

The point to this is that teams could fight on, and that friendly casualties become less important as long as the team wins the fight and can recover their lost teammates. If the team wins, the fallen can recover, if the team looses the fallen will die.

This would also generally solve the problem with NPC nukers dishing out insane one shot kills, they would probably only have time to do this once before they were killed themselves and the person who got nuked would be alot less upset if they didnt loose all that XP.

So for example a team with no healers would be far more viable, with healers still adding a deal to the effectiveness of a team anyway.

Comments please.
