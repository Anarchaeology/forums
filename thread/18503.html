Mission Spawns Asheron's Call style
Quests are a staple element of AC, and nicely done as well! In AC the chars are on a timer, you can only complete said quest once per said period. Also the spawn timers are not so bad, as they currently are in Anarchy.
Is there any reason Alvin can only serve a max of 12 people per day? And WHY must he disapeer at all??? why not make him persistant? Simply because in doing so the quest becomes too easy and handling it in this fashion is designed to throw a wrench into the plans of those who seek to exploit? There should be better ways of dealing with exploitation. This game should not always be shifting and reacting to explotation adn PvP imbalance.

From reading the IRC logs of the whole Blacksnow thing, Funcom employees DO play AC and are aware of how their timers function.

When static dungeons were first being discussed one question Funcom was asking was should there be a maximum occupancy on the dungeons. If more people were to access the area than was allowed then another dungeon would spawn. While not a perfect solution it certainly is unique.
