Exploring
I'm a newbie to AO, so this may be an old subject, but I'd like to see more insentive for exploring put into the game. Hunting gets boring fast because it seems a little pointless and I see no reason to take a mission that I have to travel to another city for, if I can easily find one in the town I'm in.

What I'd like to see is special items or creatures to search for, maybe something along the lines of special power rings or weapons, but they'd have to be RARE and USEFUL.

Otherwise I don't see the point of the empty space on RK.

They could possibly give clues through NPCs and news reports(which might get more people to role-play, making the game and story more interesting.)

For example, there could be a news report about an experimental OT plane that's crashed in the desert some where in Clan territory. Omni characters could ask Omni NPCs where it crashed and go in to Clan territory to search for it and bring it back to Omni. While the Clans could be searching for it as well.
The reward could be something very special (that any level could use). This would create a universal mission that could be completed by anyone at any level and would draw people into the story.

If not this idea, I'd like to see the programmers add something to give people something to do outside the cities, other than hunting.

Does anyone else have any ideas for this?
