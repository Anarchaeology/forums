Inconsistencies in weapon ranges and casting ranges.
Please feel free to verify. Weapon users using weapons with a high natural range and using high ql scopes to extend their range further are shooting past 40m.

PvP'ing in MMD the other day and noticed this. Engaged a trader (I know, I'm a stupid pillock for even trying), sitting on the wall. Run into zone and edge up, tried to cast on him (Coronet to debuff, for all the good it does you), eventually get within range with 3 seconds left on grace timer and let him have it. I follow the Coronet with a root, the trader hadn't moved the entire time and was now rooted in place. Before anyone asks yes I did have my best range extender buff running which put me way past the 40m cap on the formulas I used.

I backed up taking me out of 25% to avoid gankers, a sad pastime that many engage in, and promptly get hit with 2 Ithaca specials and a regular shot. I was like, "WTF???!!!!" because I was way out of range to cast or attack back myself so I ran for MMD zone border.

Thinking it might have been a client/server lag issue misreporting the position of the omni so it just appeared he was out of range and was actually closer to me than I thought, I approach the same trader who hadn't moved incidentally. I edge up again and this time root him first and then peg it out of range and into 75%. Sure enough the same thing happens again, Aimed Shot, Flingshot, regular shot, all of them critical hits. Again I haul my sorry ass off to the zone border.

What gives? I have questioned others about this and they're reported the same thing. Is it just a select few with a peculiar weapon/scope combo that can do this or is it across the board. If anyone else can arrange a test I'd be grateful.
