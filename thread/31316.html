just a theorical discussion on the difficulty of implenmenting face change
In addition, I am still investigating the difficulty for funcom to implentment the Face/Model change.

Fact: There exists a nano program call: Face Graft, agent only and high level. By casting this nano, agent can select a different face each time the nano is cast. The result is not random, but cycles through the available faces, just like how character creation works.

Theory: I am still unsure on the difficulty of making a general use, or one time only face graft being able to be casted by a Surgical Machine, which takes money and cycles through the face screen each time after use. The idea is to mimic the Face Graft nano so it could be modified as a Stationary Machine.

I have talked to two different GMs, Brillie and Ashe... both stated that face cannot be changed currently. Brillie declined to teleport me to the character creation zone to "re-select" my face due to technical difficulties (teleportation not possible between char-crea and outside zones).

So based on above observation, character creation screen works differently than the Face Graft nano... if so, Funcom might have to modify Face Graft, or make it a 20 charge Pill that can allow us to change a different face at each charge... and run out after all the faces are cycled through.
