Dai-Galean,

This is a partial response to the previous thread on Basher... where someone mentioned the game crashing with 25 people.

While I think that number by be exaggerated a bit, even I have noticed that the client has been unresponsive with not that many people in visual range.

I was looking at the network packets one day, and noticed that there was a lot of name lookup (code 21) packets being thrown around frequently by the chat server. This is partially due to my friends list being big, but also because I was just logging in. My thought - and I can totally be mistaken on this issue - is that the lag/unresponsiveness some people have been complaining about is the client attempting to process the substantial amounts of tcp based name lookups/responses that the chat server is throwing back, especially in event areas where you have the standard friends list being refreshed plus the names of the people as they come into range.

I'm not sure if the rate at which these uids are reissued by the chat server are staggered, but it could explain why even some people with lots of memory/good graphics cards/cable+ modems are suffering this problem. Connection speed doesn't help the routers process all these uid/name packets that much faster. Note that I am not saying graphics processing doesn't play a role, but this may be somewhere else to look at and could explain why it's been somewhat hard to pin-down and reproduce onsite.


In any case, I know this is a technical issue, but somewhat related to holding successful massive events so I've posted it here.

Can I suggest that you pass this along to the programming team and get someone external on an ISP with a really crapper router to test it? :P

Here's hoping we can get rid of that evil gremlin.
