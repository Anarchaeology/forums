At the moment, we work closely together with any official Omni-Pol officers, such as Commander Scarney, General Telion, Captain Vaskez, ex-Omni-Pol member Throll and some other Omni-Pol people. Also Derva Meloche is not unknown to us (or myself in this matter). Whenever we are needed, we are there. We dont see us as a replacement for the official department, rather an addition to help with their work as best as we can - game mechanic wise. The added benefit of having the name Omni-Pol is, that we are contacted by FC officials often to help them with events, which we gladly do. Also.. I think that the guild is now even a political instance, with the membership in OTSEC and at least me being well known to OT Leaders as a loyal OT citizen and leader of the Omni-Pol guild. My profession also helps that.. seeing that I am a Bureaucrat. If possible, I had no objection to be the official department.. but for this to happen, there would have to be a lot better tools for guildmanagement and roleplaying interaction in the game.
