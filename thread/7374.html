Capturing Territory - Ideas - And why it is needed now.....
To me the amnesty seems to have been a planned "hold" to the story until the "ship" was righted.

Funcom has turned the corner on the basics of the game (although lag in crowded places does persist).

What the game needs - not in six months - not in a year - is a determined effort by Funcom to establish captured territory.

Let me start with the last part of the title of this post.....

Why is this needed now?

The war needs it. The players need it. Players need to feel engaged by the story. They need to believe that their efforts, even if seemingly small, are making an impact on the war. This implementation is also needed now - to beat DaOC to the punch - and to put this idea in action BEFORE the launch of Star Wars Galaxies.

Simply put - capturing territory is the greatest concept in online gaming to ever come along. We need it NOW. Devote all resources other than those necessary to fix bugs to this issue.

Ok - Now to the ideas and why the implementation should not be too difficult.

General Ideas.

1. Start Small. Establish a couple "frontier" zones or "frontier" areas where this code is implemented. Watch and learn. Test, test, test before full server implementation.

2. Establish a city that can be fought over --- Make the small area a city. The city will likely change hands numerous times - but PVP will have a purpose.

3. Implementation. It is quite simple really. Establish objectives within the city. I provide the following three-tiered objective example to illustrate the implementation. Outer walls. Inner Sanctum. City power grid and defense.

The following is how I conceive this working.

THIRD OBJECTIVE.

The most difficult objective is located in the middle of city. (City Power Grid and Defense). This objective, which is the third to be reached, spawns either OMNI or CLAN guards depending on control. This objective consists of a simple switch. The switch can only be reversed by a person of opposite faction. (So, if the switch is to Omni, then only a clan member can "flip" the switch to Clan). When the switch is "flipped" the server times for 30 minutes (or whatever you want). When that time is reached indicating control, guards are triggered first at the switch itself (wave one - 30minutes), then the inner sanctum (wave two - 45minutes) and lastly, at the outer walls (wave three - 60minutes).

Guards are therefore spawned from closest to the most important objective, towards the outer objectives. Thus, reinforcements are provided where they are most necessary first. If the switch is captured and timed for 60minutes, guards will spawn in the entirety of the city rendering the city under the control of one faction or the other.

ONLY the THIRD objective spawns guards.

FIRST AND SECOND OBJECTIVES

So then, if only the Third Objective spawns guards, what is the importance of the outer walls and the inner sanctum walls? Easy. Guards on the outer walls and inner sanctum are respawned if those objectives have not been captured by the opposing faction (I will explain all of this in a simple example). If either of those FIRST or SECOND objectives are captured, the guard spawn is halted for that area - leading to the effect of a real "fall of the city" battle. If the switch is turned back on at either the FIRST or SECOND objectives, then the guards beging to respawn.

Example 1.

The THIRD OBJECTIVE is turned on by Omni. Omni hold the THIRD objective for 60minutes without attack to either the FIRST or SECOND objective. All the guards spawn in the city at the FIRST, SECOND and THIRD Objectives.

Clan assaults city. Clan cannot capture first objective because of the guards and players. Clan cannot flip switch at first objective, therefore, guards continue to "respawn" to offer reinforcement support. Clan assault is broken. City remains Omni.

Example 2.

Same first paragraph.

Clan assaults city. Clan captures first objective by overruning players and guards. Switch turns off OMNI guards at first wall. Clan begins assault on second objective. If OMNI recaptures the first objective, guards will respawn BECAUSE Omni controls OBJECTIVE THREE.

Example 3.

Same first paragraph.

Clan overruns objective one. Omni cannot recapture. Clan overruns objective two. Omni cannot recapture. Clan kills guards and captures objective three. If the timer starts, Clan guards will begin to spawn at OBJECTIVE THREE then TWO then ONE if the third objective is held.

However..... pretty simple - If Clan captures switch three and begins the timer - BUT loses objectives ONE AND TWO in the process to OMNI - the guards wont spawn at objectives one and two, because the switches must be on. Guards will spawn at Objective three providing reinforcements, BUT - Clan must recapture objectives two or one to respawn guards there.

Walla - that's it..... now lets see it get implemented tomorrow

Problem.
Don't get me wrong. I am all for the aspect of the game where we will be allowed to capture territories.


Two problems:

1.) After awhile of capturing territories, people will figure out what makes the game revert an area to clan / omni allegiance. Like say, hold an area for 60 minutes. Or what have you. The problem is, once you capture a city, you can always send troops in there to log out in the area that makes the game revert that area from clan to omni or vice versa. Troops in the form of alts. Once you have alts, there you can just leave the city alone. Because if it is captured you can simply log in your alts that were in that city, and capture it again, again, again and again.


2.) No Point to capturing areas.
What do you do when you capture an Area? Say Omni manages captures every single outpost that was once clan. What then? We win the game? Obviously Funcom wouldn't say Omni wins or Clan wins if Clan does the same with omni territories. Do we see any shipment of yalmahas if we capture say a space port ? Do we get more nanos if we capture a mining facility ? Probably not. And if not, then there's no real motivation for capturing other areas other than to have better hunting grounds. And if that is the case then that means the game now revolves around exp gaining and not about politics or any sort of chaos theory concerning government cognititional development. It's hack and slash then. Pure and simple.

The only other way Funcom would get around this is implement capturing of areas in the very late aspects of the game. This is the End Game time. Which is probably a few years from now and not this year as Funcom promised last year. Then, the only way players would be able to effectively "win" is to capture omni or clan facilities and ultimately capture athens and tir, or Omni-1 and Omni-HQ.

But then that would mean, that Funcom is bankrupt and trying to draw in a flux of players to supplement their cash because that kind of implementing is drastic, only done in the death throes of a company using their last ace up their sleeve.

The mission key idea seems to be a good one since the code is already implemented.

The code as I understand it spawns dungeons based on missions. The problem of course is that the mission generator never spawns keys for the same mission. Obviously, keys can be duplicated.

Now - the implementation of the current system seems tricky BUT I can conceve of this idea.

There is a mission generator just outside the main "switch" room (or it could be at the second switch). The mission generator is only accessible to the side that does not currently possess the third objective switch (the one in the center of the town).

This mission generator is different than others because it allows multiple players to take the same mission. So, when reaching objective number two (and taking it) - say, 20people grab keys to the main room to throw the switch at objective three.

If the main switch is thrown, all the keys disappear since the mission is determined complete BECAUSE - at that precise moment, the mission generator for the keys has now generated the next sequenced number mission (for the opposite faction).

So - for the key generation here is how it works.

First, mission key generator is set to opposite of the internal switch. If omni holds main switch, mission key generator is set to clan. Clan can grab these keys. The mission generator number for the key is "2" say (since 1 was Omni's capture of the main objective). So key 2 is needed to enter main switch room. When the clanners get inside and throw the switch, all number 2 keys disappear. That dungeon / playfield (like others in the game) ceases to exist. So, key 2 no longer opens the door BECAUSE....

Objective 2 mission generator has been set to key 3 now - Omni. Now only omni players can grab the key to objective 3 and open playfield 3..... etc.

AH BUT WHAT ABOUT CAMPING IN THE ROOM?

Well, two thoughts. First, the switch once thrown disappears like the radar displays in missions. This is not a problem since the new playfield is spawned at teh exact same spot. So those that log in the playfield are spit out when they log back in. The new playfield is for the opposite side and needs the new key (#3).

Walla - I think we solved it.
