Shades coords...
The nice shades spawn is located 800x2800 Greater Tir County. I leveled in that area for quite a while, because once the shades XP gets bad you can form vulture hunting teams (great XP up to about 65-70 if I remember correctly). Beware of Sandworms (800+ per hit) and try not to get aggroed by vultures. My advice is to at least have 1 MP and 1 engi. Once you stop gaining XP, send a /tell Harbodus and ill hook you up with a great spot. Good hunting,
