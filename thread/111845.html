Boycott Omni products
As a clanner, i am offended to see how many clanners use Omni products.

How do you clanners, who want freedom, rationally chose to use omni products? Dont you understand the connection between your credits and their power.

I know that Omni makes the best toothpaste, but there is a certain percentage of the profite from the toothpaste that goes to buy the bullets that they shoot at you.

If you refuse to do business with Omni, it cuts the power of Omni at the base. Omni is a corporation, with out money, it has no blood.

Think of it this way, if you chose to use products created locally, in Tir or Borealis, or athens, the money flows back into the communities you are trying to keep free.

Keep your credits at home. Boycott Omni.
