Blood Sport(Arena Battle#1)
As I Hit the Transport button it sends me into a Zone, a zone of Havoc, a zone of blood, a zone of battle, Called the arena. As i step in, i smell the scent of battle in the air, I can feel the tenshion in the air raising.
As I walk by, i quickly go stealth, Using my Agent skills to dodge any attacks comming my way, While still in the grace of the Supprestion Zone, I sneack to the East wall, searching for someone my level to duel.
Taking careful step to remain stealth i find a Enforcer, At my level range, I call him up on his cummicator Asking for a duel, He then agrees, I take my postion for away, As he is getting ready. I carefully get my shot ready,
Aiming for the spot that will cause the most Damage, He tell me He is ready and i open fire sending a bullet flying across the arena To the west where he has buffed, I hear a "ooof" come from him and suddenly root running to the North wall,
to line up my next shot, as he struggles to break free. My next shot would not be so effective, very low dmg, and he breaks my root, Charging towards me at full speed, I jump mid air and quickly run to the south wall. He follows, and hits me with
a massive amount of brawl dmg, before i get my root out and take my shot again, I begin to shoot faster now, shooting at a solid rate of once per 2 seconds, with mixed dmg shots, he Mongo slams me and breaks the root. I root him again but by then he right up next to me.
Hitting me with his massive Beam. I run to the west wall again with him hitting me all the way before i get a root out and run down the wall. " Ive had enough of This" i think to myself. I start running the Nano "Total concentration" sharpening my eye and shooting and greater dmg now.
I get him down to 3/4 of his HP. I notice iam out of Nano Points and as the program terminates the root is broken... He comes at me again, but i have no where left to run, and no Nano point left to root. He comes up right into my face and hits me with is beam, again, and again, stunning me from even shooting.
Then luck seems was on my side, near death I aimed up my last shot, if i was gonna die, i wanted him to be hurting. I line up my shot and shoot him right in the stomach for a massive amount of damage. As his body hits the floor, I breathe heavily and drop to the ground thanking what ever forces allowed me to win....
