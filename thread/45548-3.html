It's hardly 'simple'. Damage and heals are geared around fighting a mob with much different stats then a player. To just say the rules should be the same in pvp is moronic to say the least.

I have very limited experience, but it seems to me that pvp can be broken into two basic categories. Mass battles and dueling. The current system seems quite fair for mass battles. Who do you think will be able to live more then 3 seconds at 2ho with 100% damage? *ponder* I look at dueling and I can almost agree that perhaps heals are 'overpowered'. Though I think it's a lot more to do with the reflect issue then it is to do with 50% damage. My question is...why in the world should we balance things specifically for 1 on 1 duels? That isn't pvp. Pvp is fighting the mass fights. Or ganking someone in DAV or anything outside of the arena. Duels should have no weight on pvp balance. Because there is no way in hell that every prof can be perfectly balanced 1 on 1 against each other. It has never worked that way and never will. However, you can balance it so that every prof can contribute meaningfuly to a mass battle. ie: Somewhere other then tir/ent arena. When I'm at 2ho, I am quite content knowing that I'm never going to get a kill. I curse and ns every clanner I see to help my SIDE get the kill. Stop being so egotistical and think of something other then youself. Now from reading the pvp forum, it does seem like many seem to think pvp is all about who they duel at the arena. Am I the only one who thinks that's kinda stupid? *shrug* Perhaps if Funcom just made all arena fights unweighted, then more people would be motivated to participate at 2ho etc. The arena, and what it brings, seems to be at the root of the idiocy surrounding pvp.

Bah. Anyone who's for 100% damage has quite obviously not thought through what they are asking for. Just my opinion.
