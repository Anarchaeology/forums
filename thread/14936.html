game mech.: dmg bonus (rings, fist of...) just add to dmg - not increased by AMS
** disclaimer: all this is what i gathered from various posts, "studies" by some players, common sense, experiements and equation solving . Nothing official from FC, and all this could be very wrong . Also, this may not be exactly the same for professions other than MA.


A bit of explanation first:

When you read the damage level of a weapon (incl fists) you will see something like: 35-230 (300).

35 is the min dammage, 230 the max, 300 the crit addition.

But the actual dmg delt is increased by your attack rating (attack figure visible on stat screen).

The formula is not known specifically, and may have been changed. But the latest known was:

actual dmg = bas dmg * (1+ attk rtg/132).

There has been a lot of debate about this formula. Two things strike me: first, it does not account for the fact that many ppl seem to indicate that the dmg they receive reduces with higher DMS. Second thing, it not sure whether/how it applies to the MA as fist are a special kind of weapons. For the time being, let's just keep in mind the principle: actual dmg is base dmg multiplied by "something".

Now the question is: WHERE DO YOU ADD DMG BONUS? (ie "ring of power - melee", or the "fist of ..." nano line).

The nice answer would be "to base dmg". Because the multiplier (if any?) would be applied to them. This would even make sense, and explain why those bonuses are so low at high level. This is what was believed by many knowledged ppl, that had read the very good Vampi's guide. And it may even be that this is or was supposed to be the case.

HALAS! the answer is the BAD one: the bonus is only added to your actual dmg score. ie a ring +10 will give you +10 in actual dmg, no more. same for fists of... (an other explanation could be that for MA there is no multiplier, hence no change whether the bonus is added before or after)

needless to say, thats not much

More accurate formula not counting AC is:
actual min dmg = (1+ams/400)*(min dmg on the weapon)
actual max dmg = (1+ams/400)*(max dmg on the weapon)
actual min crit dmg = (1+ams/400)*(min dmg on the weapon+crit dmg on the weapon)
actual max crit dmg = (1+ams/400)*(max on the weapon+crit dmg on the weapon)

So for instance a 43-90(701) weapon with 800 ams will hit for 129-270 and it will crit for 2232-2913. Now if we take AC in account those values get decreased by 1 for every 10 AC your target has and will not go below min dmg (129 in this case).

And yea, the +dmg stuff adds their bonus at the very end, making it almost useless (after AC has been checked)
