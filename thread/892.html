Funcom the Deranged
The problem is, the dungeon doesn't always provide the same lvl of difficulty. For instance, last night, when my group finally made it to the last room, there wer like 20+ mobs waiting for us, and they just gobbled us up. Whereas, a week ago, there were only 5 or six mobs in the last room, not including neleb. The dungeon is inconsistent, and frustrating.

My last group had a lvl 65+ doc, a lvl 68+ martial artist, a lvl 96 enf, 60 meta, and a couple of others in their 60s. The question, ultimately, is why is this dungeon being passed off as a lvl 35-45 dungeon, if a team of 60+ characters can't even pull it off. Surely Funcom didn't mean for the dungeon to be unfinishable by the range of lvls that the dungeon was i ntended for...

furthermore, shouldn't the lvl of the mobs in the dungeon reflect the difficulty or vice versa? What's the sense of having mobs that are lvl 100+ difficulty if they give lvl 30- XP? This is a major frustration.

Basically, what we've ended up with is a mission that is nearly impossible, that gives neglegible xp, worthless loot (unless you're lucky enough to beat Neleb, and yes, for all intents and purposes it is mostly luck when there aren't more than 6 mobs in the last room), and wastes our time...

The maximum size for teams is hard set to 6. So, for those in the team who want the "Nano-Circuit Cloak" Neleb has to be killed up to 6 times. Take this into account along with the fact that it is often to defeat him with one team, and the problem becomes compounded by the fact that the other assisting teams members may also want a cloak. So, in essence Funcom has us like donkey's with carrots in front of our faces. Constantly dangling what we want in plain view, as though it is attainable, but keeping just far enough away so that we cannot reach it. I don't know about the other peeps, but I don't like being made to feel like I'm a donkey.

Funcom needs to re-evaluate their position on the Neleb dungeon. Heck, they need to recognize that many players are disatisfied with the dungeon, and do something about it. No, I don't want them to get rid of the dungeon all together. Less of what little content there is would be a BAD THING. I just want them to make a few tweaks to the dungeon. Here are some suggestions:

1. No more than 6 Mobs in the last room.
2. Either give the MOBS HP, DMG, and AC that is in line with their lvl, or give them a lvl in line with their HP, DMG, and AC. Prefferably the first option. (There is no way that a lvl 60+ team should die in a lvl 35-45 dungeon)
3. Figure out why multiple MOBs are spawning near existing fights, and fix it. (They are hard enough without the adds)
4. Would it be possible to give a "Static" mission reward? Instead of having to kill Neleb 6 times, a group could kill him once, and everyone would get the same reward, a "Nano-Circuit Cloak". (It is ludicrous to expend so much time and effort in one place, only to have to repeat it 6 times. It just isn't fun this way)
5. Fix it so that no more than 2 mobs can attack the group at the same time. (let's get real for a second. How many of us, who can actually use a lvl 50 nano cloak, can find the muscle necessary to take down 6 mobs at once. This is obviously supposed to be a team mission, but it has become a multiple team mission, even to handle a few of these "extra hard" mobs. In my experience there are typically more than 6 MOBS in the last room. So, even if you root all of the MOBS other than Neleb, and constantly re-root, that's 6+ roots, which would take more than one rooter to maintain, so you're left with a maximum of 4 team members going at Neleb, and that's only if none of the roots fail. Additionally, this method is so, flemsy it is comical, and I'm sure this is not what Funcom had intended)

Anyway, that's my 15 cents. What do you guys think? And for the ones who were able to get Neleb, don't just say that it can be done, tell us unfortunates how you did it...

I'm going to send these suggestions to Funcom, cause they don't seem to be listening to our complaints here. I put on a petition for this, and the rep that answered did nothing at all. What a waste...
