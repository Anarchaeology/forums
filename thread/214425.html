Phear Leet
Blackpetal picked up the letter and read it for the twentieth time, then sighed and, reluctantly, put it down...for the twentieth time.

I would like to help him... she thought to herself.

Finally, she looked over to Icarus, sitting motionless in a corner.

"Icarus. Exit standby mode. Please take a letter."

A slight motion of Icarus head indicated that he was active. Petal began her dictation.

"Mr. Domtar:

"You are, unfortunately, correct. Schuemann & Associates is in transition at this time, and we are not accepting new clients."

Petal paused for a moment, then shook her head and sighed.

"However...I will do some research on this matter for you, and I will contact you again in the next few days. I make no guarantee that I can take this case."

She paused again and thought, reviewing the letter in her mind.

"Please accept my condolences on the passing of your brother. I will be in touch shortly.

"Sincerely, Racheal Schuemann."

Icarus stood up. Petal nodded to him.

"Have the note delivered promptly, Icarus. I have other matters that demand my attention now."

Icarus bowed slightly.

"Of course, Master."

Mr. Domtar:

No apology is needed. Please rest assured that I would not have offered any level of assistance I did not feel I could provide. I simply wanted you to understand that I may not be able to take your case. No case is a small matter to the parties affected by it, and my reluctance to involve myself reflects my perception of its importance to you. If I do not feel I can provide you with the quality and dedication of service your case requires, I would be horribly remiss in accepting the case.

That said, I will, as I indicated in my last correspondence, continue to research this matter, and I will contact you in a few days. In the meantime, I recommend that you be patient.
