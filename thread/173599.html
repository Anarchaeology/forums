Yes we have roleplayers in AO! We might be a minority, but Beleive we are less of a minority here than in most other games.

Clan-side, you might want to take a look at any of the End Game Alliance orgs including Unity of the Rose, Whispers Edge, Rubi-Ka Freedom Fighters, First Light and Valor Eternal. These are all established guilds with some of the finest roleplayers in the game. The alliance format gives them the strength to take part in the more "gamesy" aspects of AO like tower battles and raids.
I've can also recommend the Assembly, a newish guild run by veteran roleplayers.

There is also a sticky thread on this forum with a fairly good list of Roleplaying guilds on RK1 (and another for RK2) by faction.

Finally, there is also a brand new occ chat bot for roleplayers. Its there to help rolepalyer get to know each other and advertise/plan events. Next time you log in try "/tell roleplaying !join"

Clan RP Orgs
On Atlantean, I suggest fine orgs such as Valor Eternal and Unity of the Rose.

On Rimor, the best clan faction roleplay org is the Pilgrims (hands down).

I'm sure the German Server has a few, but as I've not been there, I couldn't give you a reccomendation.

Roleplaying sans guild is also an option, but it's a much more difficult one. Should you decide this best suits your character, I'd contact members of the suggested RP orgs to let them know that you're an unaffiliated RPer who is interested in interacting but not joining.

Good luck and enjoy your time with us!
