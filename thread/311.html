A promise in danger of being broken: Mission Blitzing
quote:
Must not be in a fight vs. Find person
Because "Find NPC" type missions are hard to solve when the NPC attcks you and you can't be in a fight when you solve them, we removed the necessity to not be in a fight. You can now solve the mission even when you are in a fight.
Adding the 'Must not be in a fight' requirement to any mission is a mistake. Introducing this requirement removes the ability of players to run through missions in order to gain cash. If this change is left in place, Funcom will be reneging on a promise to its players.


quote:
12.8 Patch Notes
Sneaking and running through missions(Mission Blitzing) will still be useful strategy to grab the items and money youd need and earn the mission reward  just not the EXP.
Funcom does not need any more black eyes. Its current reputation cannot withstand any further hits.
