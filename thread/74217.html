From the neutral perspective
Yes, I dislike the man as much as the next person. Yes, maybe killing him will make you feel better, but since there is nothing that can be done on a permanant basis (at the moment, anyway), we'll just have to see how the situation goes.
I think it would be interesting for Funcom to just open it up and let the players decide how it would go (imagine the all-out battle that would be), but remember. Part of what makes the game as good as it is is the constant state of chaos and disorder. Keeping things new and ever-changing make the world dynamic. You just never know what else might change between when you log off at night, and when you come back on the next time.
So, yes I think he is a hateful man. I would like to see him removed from power in a peaceful manner, and it made sure that he can't do this kind of thing again, but the method behind the madness (the dynamics of the world) are one of the reasons I keep coming back.
