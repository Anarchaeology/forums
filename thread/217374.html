Chronicle 1
The solitus stared at the terminal for awhile, running a hand through his long blonde hair. His eyes traveled over the letters, the words, the news, absorbing the nonsense and facts, and sifting through them like a gold miner through his ore. He tabbed a key on his datapad, scrolling the holo-screen down as he continued to read. A finger idly lept to his face, correcting the glasses that balanced precariously on the tip of his nose. News...trash....obituary...ne--..Obituary? On Rubi-Ka?

Dead...old age...bah..

What a waste. Something worth reading and it was a dissapointment. The kid shook his head and continued to read trhough, untill the gridfeed cut itself short. He sighed, closing the terminal and rubbing his eyes. Tapping a button on his datapad, the time displayed in the air in front of him, hovering and twisting a moment before coming to a nice halt. It was early. The suns wouldnt be out yet, but that was fine.

Work never waited for the light anyways.




He watched the dawn break with a half empty expectancy. He never much cared for the outdoors, nor much cared for the light. Hackers will be Hermits, someone had told him once. Placing a ciggarette in his mouth, biting the end, and letting loose a deep breath, he began the ardous task he was told to undertake. Lifting the Bi-Specs to his eyes, he began to watch ID Number 992123F begin her daily routine, making her coffee and assorting her briefcase as she readied for work. But something was...different...this time. There was no...music, as she usually listened to, and there certainly was no...caution, as she usually took, fear, of whatever she detected. No fear...this couldnt mean..

She was looking at him. Straight at him. Waving.

The boy swore, clenching his teeth and sitting up quickly. His hands shakily put everything into it's place in the satchel.
No time...No time...she knows...****...She's gotta have someone com--
He never had a chance to finish his thoughts. The cold diamondized blades had slit the jugular viens in his neck, and had sent him spiraling downwards towards Rubi-Kan earth from the Plaza Platform. The woman gazed at the falling sight of her kill, smiling faintly. She looked towards the window of her contract, lifting an arm straight up. She turned on her heel and strode off, replacing the diamondized dagger into the sheath, hidden in the bun in her hair.
