Name Rubi-ka's Moons Contest!
Have you ever wondered what our planet's moons are named??? Many of us have. Well its about time Rubi-Ka
named its moons....

The Council of Clan Leaders has decided to sponsor a contest to "NAME RUBI-KA'S MOONS"

All you have to do to be a part of this event is post your idea of suitable names for our moons in this thread (There are three of them. A red one,a blue one, and a large grey one). The names must be appropriate to say publicly and the moons cant be named after yourself(ie we dont want Patk9, Patk10, and Patk11 ^_^). However names of *other* people that have done great things for Rubi-Ka are acceptable.

On Saturday 28 September the judging panel will select the best names and submit them to the ICC's Galactic Cartography Board where they will (hopefully) be made the official names of the moons.

As an added incentive, the winning entry will receive a great set of prizes including a Division9 Plasma projector(QL200),a Ring of Physics(+17 to Chemical, Radiation, and Poison Damage), and 1 million credits. And the list of prizes will grow as more items are donated.

This contest is open to ALL of Rubi-Ka's citizens regardless of affiliation. So hurry up and submit your ideas. Remember to post them in this thread.


Also: The judging panel is in need of judges. The Council of Clan Leaders is asking that two representatives from Omni-Tek as well as two Neutral representatives volunteer to be on the judging panel for this event. Send a tell to me(Lyricia) or volunteer on this thread. Thanks in advance ^_^
