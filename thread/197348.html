Discoveries of Wonder, Inventions of Marvel!
Sat Nov 22, 20:00 GMT - Reets Retreat - RK1

Rubi-Ka Universal Robots, RUR, would like to invite scientists, engineers, medical researchers, mathematicians and inventors of all factions to take part in the first Isaac Asimov Scientific Symposium at some date later this month. If you have made any interesting discoveries, formulated a new intriguing hypothesis or just stumbled accidentally on the killer new invention and discuss this with your peers in a friendly environment with a lot of alcohol, please give get in touch.

Thank you in advance,

Dabblez

/ooc

This is obviously a roleplaying event. The idea of a scientific symposium was first raised by Kronocide ages and ages ago and recently revived by our very own Sizzleen. What we are looking for is a few speakers willing to give a little in-game presentation about something vaguely scientific and vaguely AO related. The science doesnt have to make sense as long as its entertaining. You can work alone or in groups, use any prop, mix the in-game presentation with web-based visuals (asking the public to swap between AO and the browser at appropriate points); anything goes.

If you think you might like to get involved in this, reply to this thread or give Dabblez a tell.

This event will be based on Atlantean.
