#1 = EQ clone

#2 = this idea has been around as long as bags in the bank have. Here is why it won't go in the game: every labeled item represented correctly is a new database object and has to be so that it appears correctly upon login and instantiation. Yesterday alone I made 3 sets of carbonum armor at 3 different QL's. Under a labeling system, I just added 21 items to the database. Now consider how many folks out there who make implants, armor, weapons and every other tradeskilled goodie. The database would melt down. FunCom does not have unlimited resources and database servers cost money.

#3 = Morrowind clone of Mark and Recall. Currently, Cartographer will allow anyone to teleport to their most friendly city. Fixers have their grid, engineers have beacon warp, NTs have WT&S, etc. Oh yeah, anyone with enough credits can have a Yalm. This in addition to the Grid and the Whompah network. Somehow, travel doesn't seem too hard.

#4 = The problem in your PvP system is that it works in UO because UO didn't already have sides. AO has sides and the map upgrade of Side will tell you on your map if someone is PvP-able. Also, the suppression system rocks because it let's people like me, who could care one whit for PvP, know that they are in a bad place where bad things can happen. I played UO for a while and it was a grief festival, nothing more. Grief repeatedly until you can build home, build home, go grief some more. Bleh. For pure PvP with little RPG...play Planetside. You will be much happier.

#5 = Why have a bounty system in a game that already rewards me for everything I kill? I make money of dead clanner mobs every single day. If I actually killed a Clan player someday, talking smack would be reward enough (and smack would be talked since engineers are not the best PvP'ers out there). Having a /helpbot info that listed who had the highest PvP rankings and which side they are on would be cool, but hardcore AO PvPers already know who most of those folks are. Again, since rewarding and fulfilling PvP (grief-fest) is your aim, play Planetside.

#6 = The day FunCom adds corpse runs, lost gear, poofed corpses, lost levels, lost skills/abilities or any other crap-tacular aspect of EQ death, is the day I cancel my account and launch into a smear campaign that FunCom is a SOE wannabe. But you have the classic PvP gank maniac need to not only kill a player, but to make their death as painful to them as possible while making it rewarding for you. One aspect of corpse fetching you may have overlooked: think of a Tarasque raid with corpses littered about, or a tower war where corpses are persistent. Yeah, that wouldn't any lag at all, as if.

#7 = As already stated by others, the credit counter is just that, a counter. Your credit card has a $5,000 limit, does that mean you are carrying 5,000 one dollar bills with you? Do you max your credit card and stick the cash in a bank for safekeeping? The credit system works fine, but again, it seems as if you want PvP to be rewarding and the credit system is no good for that. Once more...PLANETSIDE!!

#8 = 80% of the freaking map is already land control and the upcoming ToRK will add to it. Wait and see.

#9 = SWG has all sorts of emotes, cool building interiors, NPCs, entertainers and all manner of neato eye candy. Guess what? The game is boring. Eye candy is great, for about 5 minutes, then what? If AO had girlie dancers in Omni Ent, or thundering herds of whatever rolling randomly across Greater Tir, it would be neat...the first time you saw it. Sorry, at the end of the day, I prefer game play, character design and PvM content instead of boring eye candy. But again, I play pen & papaer, so I can happily go without visuals at all if need be.

#10 = goes back to database models. Every face has to be itemized. Adding that adds space and at the end of the day, there are more AO models than any game out there right now, including SWG. We also have way more clothing items as it is.

#11 = ToRK. To me, this will be AO's downward spiral because like UO and SWG, it will become (and someone already said this) suburbia, with the joyous bonus of increased lag. Yippee. My question is: where will these buildings go? Most free space is already overrun with towers or gameworld entities. Where will the buildings go? When I played UO, it already looked like the Sims and only got worse. SWG is the same way now, houses as far as the eye can see, with nobody and nothing in them.

Methinks you are PvP focused, with roleplaying as a secondary concern. I highly recommend Planetside for that and UO for your UO fix.
