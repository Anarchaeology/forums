Funcom: Quantize treatment and nano kits
I know the devs are (at long last) looking at making the treatment kits and nano chargers stackable. Woohoo.

[I would have supposed this would have been a much higher priority to fix if the devs played the game more or even at all, considering how trivial the fix must be and how annoying the problem is at high level. But that is neither here nor there....]

But of course above QL 125 you wind up finding every possible damn QL there is, and of course these won't stack. Size 50 stacks are useless and painful to deal with.

Please do at least one of two things (preferably both):

* Quantize the QLs of the kits so they only exist at 20 QL intervals or so, so that it is more likely you will find kits that stack.

* Force disparate QLs downward for stacking, so that if you shift-click one kit of QL x on another kit of QL y, you get one stack of QL MIN(x,y).
