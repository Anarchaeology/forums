Are NOT used in Crystal or Ring creation.

In fact you could say that Notum are same as Coal which is used as energy source supply for Nanobots

Possible use of Notum items will be a creation of Nano recharge units. AKA Trade skill based on Pharma and Chem skills. But right now which are not working yet.
So right now it is ONLY use are to be sold to NPC for some cash thats all.

While we are on this i would like to add somemething more.
Very often when you are doing ID/Pick item missions you might ends whit weird things like Pinky Jar/Cutter/Triple or some kind of nano enchanted IO coolant/main frame optimizers those items right now have no use to and they are worth 0cr when you try to sell them to NPC. So only ppl who want to RP whit them might get some use of them. And no they are not rare and you dont se them on sell channels because nobody bothers to pick those things up.

Now to rings/bracers. Sure there is some nice rings/BRACERS out there (+damage, +XP, +IP, Shield, Reflect, Luck) which can be bo sold to players (some of them are non drop though).
But rest of rings and bracers right now have only one use they can be remelted/remaked by engineer to make more expensive rings which you sell to npc.
Same goes for Gemstones. Let one engineer to work whit it and he will return you one more valueable gem.
