There is nothing wrong with Multi-Ranged. The problem people see is that Multi-Ranged is incredibly high on some weapons such as a S.A. Home Defender or a Desert Reet. This makes it literally impossible to dual weild that weapon. Most 1h weapons damage has been changed or reduced because of the fact that it can be dual weilded, yet the multi-reqs are so high that your offhand is usually much to inneffective to be worthwhile.

Your damage output is less than double. Check out this thread:

http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=17426

Plus, if your multi-ranged skill is low, it'll hinder your ability to use the weapons effectively.

Quoting FC's discription:

Multi Ranged
This skill is used to define how well you attack with more than one ranged weapon at a time (as in one in each hand). Wielding more than one weapon will decrease the attack skill for each weapon, but training this skill will compensate that penalty to a certain degree. It depends 60% on agility, and 40% on intelligence.
