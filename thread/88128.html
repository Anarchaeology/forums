Blue Midnight Moon
by Sun "Poetica" Catt

Its the blue hour
Just past midnight
The ghost hour
The air is fresh
Clean and crisp
Cool with a breeze
Im sitting
In my open windows post
Looking outside
Gazing at the blue moon
On the infinite
Midnight sky
Stars shattered
Like lights from housings
From distant dwellings
Im tired and my body aching
Yet I cant sleep
My heart is restless
It keeps me from sleeping
Alone in my cold bed
So I sit here
In my open windows post
Looking outside
Gazing at the blue moon
Where the air is fresh
Clean and crisp
Cool with a breeze
Like a hand touching
My cheeks
Like a hand stroking
My hair
Wishing
Wishing for a calm heart
Wishing upon a blue moon
