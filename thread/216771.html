During a dull day in a Clan listening post somewhere in the Wailing Waste region.
"Sir."

"Yes Private?"

"We have picked up a transmission from the Omni Notum mine in The Longest Road region."

"Where is it going?"

"As far as i can tell it´s being recived by Omni-HQ."

"See if you can breach the encryption."

"Already on it Sir. Here we go, putting it on speaker."


Omni Mining:"*Static*es Sir, we are still pretty much in a state of chaos here. The damage to the medical database mainframe was exstensive, but the dump to the lower secure memory units was complete so we suffered no data loss at all."

Omni HQ:"How about the surveilance cameras? Do we have any kind of recordings of what happened?"

Omni Mining:"That is the odd part Sir. From the moment the opposing force training team entered the compound there is no recordings at all untill 20 minutes later, and then all you see is Dust Brigade troopers cutting down our security forces and setting demolition charges on the mainframe, then evacuating by grid transfer. All logs up to that point in time has been corrupted beyond recovery."

Omni HQ:"I´m getting a report from the opposing force team leader that they never came near the mining compound. They had a change in orders to investigate a crashed ship in Avalon."

Omni Mining:"Then who were the people that came here in the first place?"

Omni HQ:"Ahem."

Omni Mining:"Oh. Sorry Sir. I forgot myself."

Omni HQ:"Your guess is as good as mine."

Omni Mining:"Sir, we have been able to get some information from the T.I.M prototype memorybanks. During the surveilance blackout there seems to be some kind of commotion up in the research area, then unknowns moving around for a few minutes followed by a power spike that must be a grid transfer activation. A few minutes later... Hmm. That´s odd. Some kind of variation on the Engineer Beacon Warp program i think."

Omni HQ:"Get to the point."

Omni Mining:"Sorry Sir. Anyway, after this warp signature there is a lot of weapons fire moving out of the research area and towards the mainframe room. Aproximatly two minutes after the fight had left the research room there is some more movement in the research room, and then another grid transfer activation. After that our security system came back up and caught the Dust Brigade troopers on tape."

Omni HQ:"Omni HQ will launch a full investigation into this. Send all the data you have so far."

Omni Mining:"Transm*Static*"

"Sir, they switched encryption. It will be a while before i can crack this one."

"Very well Private. Get to work, and send a copy to Commander Silverstones office."

"Right away Sir."
