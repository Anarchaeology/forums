Just to expand on what others have said, remember that a llts/ellts does not (at least not currently) negatively affect your recharge speed. Recharge is only affected by the amount of ip you have invested into the appropriate init (or of course it's not affected at all for nanos)---buffs and boofs will not change it's speed. If you don't believe me, go have a doc hit you with a big init debuff--your upspeed will drop quite noticeably, but your recharge remains static.
