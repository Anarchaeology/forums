What do you LOVE about being a keeper?
What I read the most on these boards, is people complaining about why they dislike their class (not as much in this forum, but in other forums). And when I was picking my class when I first started, I think it would have been nice to read some good things instead of the mostly negative posts on the boards. Basically, instead of making another post that says "My prof can't do this....", lets make one that says "I love my keeper because he can do this..."
I only have a few things, but hopefully everyone will ad some things on.

1) I love inspecting a monster and seeing the words "It will be almost impossible to kill this alone", and then never dipping below half heath the entire battle because we have awesome heal auras and perks.

2) I love always being wanted in a group. It seems that a keeper is always a great addition to any group, whether it is for the possibility of an extra tank, the added damage we bring, or just to have the nice team healing auras which everyone appreciates.

So if you think of something you like, post it and let everyone know!
