Fixers USED to be strong classes back in Beta.

You see back in the day the roots worked, Maussers had 45m range and our HoT's were CHEAP as hell to cast and run buffs had no dmg penalties.

So what a fixer would do was root the mob, run out to max range of Mausser, 45m better than most sniper rifles in AO.

Shoot it to death, if root breaks, and back in beta it almost never did, it just timed out. Of course the Fixer has his best HoT running 24/7. So when the root breaks the mob can barely scrach a fixer before another root while running away at insane speeds. Fixer retreats heals up all the way with hit HoT and continues to blast the mob.

Due to this the first char to reach to 200 in beta was a Fixer as far as I know. Might have been an Enforcer, lots of high lvl Enforcers...

Obviously in Beta 4 they nerfed the roots to absolute uselessness, then in release nerfed the mausser, HoT's and run buff.
