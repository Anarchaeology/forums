You may also want to consider that despite you not ever having one because there was absolutely no advantage for doing such, there ARE level 200 Neutrals. When those level 200 Neutrals get 201, they are stuck Neutral forever.

If you REALLY think it's fair for people to be perma-nerfed because of some silly, ill-concieved notion that Neutrals must always suck.. well... I'm not sure what to say. But it sure doesn't make a lot of sense.

I just gotta say.. if a 220 Neutral who toughs it out in Pandemonium after putting up with 220 levels of bulls**t downfalls for the last two+ years and has a shot at being slightly parrellel with everyone else...so be it. Anything else would just be crap.

Perhaps one shouldn't throw stones unless one thinks it would be just as fair if FC decided to arbitrarily lay big nerfs on Omni compared to Clan....oh wait, Omni already complain about that! Go figure.

/me goes back to his UBER QL 75 towers, and his UBER 0.9% side bonus, and his UBER bowtie neck item and tries to remind himself that Neutrals are simply UBER because of items that Neutrals will get far after the sided orgs do, and ignoring the fact that Neutrals still have no real advantage whatsoever aside from a short-term SK benfit which is easily negated by all the other pitfalls

I also note that the title of the thread was, "Theres now no real reason to have a side"... Interesting. Yet, Clan and Omni are always whined about by both sides to be equal. So what are the sides? Neutral and Sided--with Sided as identical.

There never WAS a reason to have a "side" if you want to pretend (as you say) that Neutral is not a side. After all, Clan and Omni have been practically identical for quite some time now. Nobody viewed Neutral as a "balance" or "reason" for having a side. Neutral wasn't the benchmark...sided has ALWAYS been the benchmark--and being Neutral just meant you were a gimp. The game is designed and balanced for sided players, not Neutrals. Thus, since the homogenous grouping (Omni and Clan) are the high point and benchmark, there has NEVER really been a "reason to have a side."

So, the complaint itself is rather contrived.. What's the difference? Why do you care if Neutrals are mostly parrelel? How does that impact anything? It's not like Neutrals are by any stretch BETTER than sided players. Side bonus, land control, sided mobs, faction woes, etc., etc.. All is not rosey on the Neutral front, believe you me.

The only real issue here is a complaint based on the incorrect notion that Neutrals are "not a side." Obviously, that is not the case. And, to be perfectly blunt, it's about time that Neutrals aren't treated like second-class citizens when it comes to high-end gameplay.
