I whipped up a macro for my engineer characters after I /afk'd for five minutes, and came back to find my chat window full of vicinity chat from someone who became quite irate about my "ignoring" them.
code:
/macro afk /pet rename "Sorry - Soyuz is AFK"
For my non-pet characters, I usually find a secluded niche on top of a building or something when I want to go afk for a while. I really wish we had a feature like the one you describe as standard.
