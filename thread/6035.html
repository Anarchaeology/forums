Thoughts on outdoors Concealment.
This is a two-and-a-half part idea; note that I don't seriously expect this to ever see the light of (virtual) day, but it's neat anyway. ;)

1) Terrain affects concealment/perception.
1a) Appropriate armor/clothes boosts concealment in appropriate terrain.

2) Concealment as no-TAB.

Okay, breaking it down:

1) If you're in a dense forest, a dark swamp, hilly area, tall grass, or whatever is deemed appropriate, there is a flat, I don't know, 20 points (or such; just an example) added to your Concealment; or subtract that from Perception. Or both. Any of those would work.

Likewise, if you're on open plains or desert, or on top of a high rock, you're easier to spot and it's harder for you to hide.

You could either average it out and apply a flat number for a playfield, or split it up into sub-areas (when you're in the woods in Omni Forest you get +20 Concealment, step out and it immediately drops down).

Night would give a plus to Concealment/minus to Perception, and vice versa for day.

1a) This ties in with the first part, though it could be used seperately. The basic idea is that if you're wearing a suit of Omni-Pol Forest armor, and are lurking about a forested region, you get extra points to Concealment. You could also apply penalties to armor for a region if it really blatantly stood out.

Likewise, if you went around a designated town area in civilian clothes - and not wielding an obvious weapon - you'd gain Concealment points.

2) This is really how it ties in. Funcom has stated (pretty sure.. correct me if I'm wrong?) that there are no plans now or ever to make people invisible. This has rendered Concealment useless in PvP conflict.

In other threads, there's been talk of making neutral-aligned players no-tab; that is, by pressing the TAB button to cycle through available, enemy targets, neutrals would simply not show up.

So why not apply this to Concealment? Concealment functions as a no-tab function, countered by Perception. If you see someone sneaking up, you can still click on them to target and attack, but TAB simply passes over them.


Thoughts? Comments? Flames can be directed to the nearest wall of your choice. ;)
