Some explaination of Tara raffles as far as I know of them:

Tarasque raid - raid on the dragon by the name of Tarasque in the dungeons underneath Castle Camelot. (Which is a silly place.) The dungeon is a 25% zone, so it's PvP galore, when lag allows.

Raffle = lottery. You can enter the raffle, and you get a random chance of being selected for a piece of loot, provided you enter the draw on that piece of loot. E.g. 50 people enter the draw for a Piece of Dragon Flesh, and you enter as well. You have a 1 in 51 chance of winning the draw. Not a lot, but it's better than nothing.

Example pieces of loot from Tarasque: Gaily Painted Hood, Signet Ring of the Green Knight, Aura Magnifier, Edge of Tarasque, Heavily Padded Overcoat, Smelly Butcher Gloves, assorted pieces of dragon-bits which can be made into very nice armor.

And yes, I won a raffle once. The first time I participated, in fact.
