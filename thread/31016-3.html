Since I'm aparently one of the few readers of this forum who isn't a fanboy I feel I need to shoot that gun apart for you..

- AR/RE based attack rating.. Soldiers have been asking for 100% AR based weapons for a long time. If you have to make it AR/RE then at least make the components add up to more than 100%, say 70% AR/40% RE.

- Only burst special.. Of all the 4 specials on guns, burst is the one that does the lowest damage. How about stacking burst, fling and FA on there? That would be more like it.

- Range and clip size is good. At least you got those right.

- Energy damage.. *ENERGY DAMAGE*! Can you make soldiers any more predictable? Unless you noticed, everyone is wearing energy bracers in PvP simply because soldiers *always* need to use the damn energy damage weapons. How about making two versions of theese guns. Both should be the exact same stats, and do cold and fire damage respectively. That way we would have some sort of surprise in our weaponry.

- Equip delay.. 2 seconds might as well have been 2 days. It's too slow to do effective weapon switches.

- Damage.. You need to buff it up even more to have this thing work. Crits will be too low for instance.

Allow me to post some links to what we want:


Twice the crit damage of the Div 9 gun, single skill based attack rating, and useful special:
http://auno.org/aodb.php?t=ag&cmd=view&id=161130

With decent inits you get this kind of damage at more or less equal speed if you go multi skill. Also notice the damage type:
http://auno.org/aodb.php?t=ag&cmd=view&id=160172

Or you can go for this one and have two specials (including bow special):
http://auno.org/aodb.php?t=ag&cmd=view&id=160167

For a directly comparable weapon in the SMG family, check out this one. Higher crit damage, bigger clip, single skill attack rating, and *multi wieldable*..
http://auno.org/aodb.php?t=ag&cmd=view&id=160151


The good old nova flow will out damage this new gun any day of the week, simply because it has fling, faster equip time and higher crits.

What we really want:

- 100% AR. Bring the req up to 1000 for QL200 if you have to.
- All 3 soldier specials, that is fling, burst and FA if you don't know that already.
- 0.5 sec equip delay
- Speed of 2/2, 2.5/1.5 or 1.5/2.5 (<- in that order)
- Damage equal to the X-3 rifle
- At least two available versions, with equal stats and different damage type.
- 25m or higher range
- 40 or higher clip size
- If you have to make it AR/RE then this should give some bonus for blowing ip in two skills, not a penalty.


Or better yet.. Make it in the same way you did the sol riders for adventurers. Make 3-4 versions with different specials on them, fling, fling/burst, fling/burst/FA, and also have two different damage type versions in each category.

This is the kind of weapon we really need to get up there with the other professions combat wise. If we can't get this then we might all just go for the X-3, KIWD crossbows or suppressors. We will of course be outgunned because the professions we will be competing against will have better buffs for rifle/smg, and also have green skills while we have aqua, but it's still going to make us do more damage than we can ever dream about doing with all theese junk weapons that runs on the AR skill.

If all you want to do is add yet another useless weapon to the game under some sort of assumtion that you are helping the soldiers, then please - save yourself the trouble.
