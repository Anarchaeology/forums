Classes the balance issue. 02/22/02
My experiences have let me see a couple things about balance. First lets start off with PvP.

Fixer, from what I have seen they really dont have much to compare in weapons/armor/hit points.

Agents, in the same class as a fixer but better buffs.

These two classes above fall in the catagory of weak weapons especially when compared to a Nano caster just using a vekt or another crit gun. Sad really because I think funcoms vision was for them jsut to use a pistol. I have seen some using pistols.. I am not sure which one it is but around level 30 I was getting nailed by a pistol for 300 some damage each time(projectile). These two classes IMHO need a major overhall.

Engineer, yikes poor guy is in trouble if someone is on the roof. But the shotgun can save em.

MA, great healing chances not the greatest melee damage. Also cannot twink fist .

NT, They were the best with the stun for a little bit but boom that fun was taking out fast. NT's are better in gorups if you ask me very close to being in the weakest class rank.

Trader, ransack skills root and shot gun em.. what else can I say. Only downfall is not the greats hps.

Soldiers, Nice hit points usually can get a hold of great armor and some nice weapons. Close to best but Nova Flow has been nerfed so much I am not sure anymore.

Doctor, seen a level 90 doc kill a soldier that was about 130 cause of heals. Heals are rarely interrupted from what I have seen. Strong class but could use better weapon then again were they supposed to be made to be good at 1on1 PvP?

MP, wow pet doing great damage... in trouble put med pet on yourself... still in trouble put mez type pet on enemy hold a shot gun type weapon that crits like crazy.. ownt

Enforcers, Great hitpoints(atrox especially)... killer damage out put... great AC.. only flaw is the melee factor, but they totally make up for em in damage when they do hit someone.

In PvM Enforcers will win also. The only fights I have seen Enforcers lose in were fights where they ran out of nano to get out of a root .


Most probably wont agree with what I say but remember this is what I have seen. Tell me what you have seen and what you think. Thats what this board is for, please add to the comments under classes I would love for you to add anything if you experienced anything in PvP.
