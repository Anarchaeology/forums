The tape closet
Nevver, Kittensoup and Trybes left the VIP lounge at Baboons and rounded the corner to the back of the DJ booth where the various electronic equpment that makes it the premier night club of Rubi is kept. Nevver opened the closet door where the video recorders were kept, pulled the tapes out of the four machines and put them into her pack.

"Better get the others too, Nevv," suggested Trybes.

He was right so Nevver grabbed the other eight tapes from the shelf and stuffed them into the pack as well. "That's all of them. Let's go."

The trio walked casually across the dance floor and exited the club. Once outside, they held a hushed conversation and decided on a place to hide the tapes.
