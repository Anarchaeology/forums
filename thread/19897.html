Armor suit sets with special ability!
well...

I find that most fun in game now is for me to complete armor sets through mission hunts... for my twinks :/

It would be nice to design special armor suits what give a very special ability once they are fully equipped - maybe with time-based restriction like the symbio grafts have.

The abilies could range from 20 minutes high stealth (for all classes), charm a MOB, fly like Quantum Wings, critical hit buff, etc etc

Maybe even some special armor suits like:

° mermaid - gives 800 to swimming for 30 mins
° professor suit graft - gives 500 teaching
° burrower - allows you to go 6 feet underground and travel like that
° X-men - discharges a series of area effect radiation attacks arround you
° blaze - turns you into a searing light source like little sun, mezzing and stunning all MOBS in area

etc etc sky/fantasy is the limit...

----
The benefits are threefold:

a) you give high levels things to do: collect pieces of armor
b) individual characters get more unique abilities, e.g. distinguish themselves by being uniquer - a true goal of many.
c) you allow broken/unused feture skills to flourish (swimming, teaching, etc)
