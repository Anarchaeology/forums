The explosion in Omni HQ
Tonight, March 27, I had just returned from Rome and was involved in a conversation in Ent-east when I noticed malfunctioning slayerdroids taking out Omni citizenry. Before I could react I too was dropped pretty quickly and found myself on the outskirts of Rome. Teach me to insure more frequently...

Upon return to Ent-east, I decided to head over to Trade, and lo and behold citizens there were being attacked by the slayerdroids. Pretty soon we gathered our senses and fought back the mechanical beasts...and what followed was astounding...a rather large explosion in the area of Omni HQ...or so came the message to my comm unit...

Anyone care to elaborate on anything else that may have happened?

*****
OOC: On a side note, I've noticed many players complaining about the lack of story elements, or events, within AO. As a former two-year vet of Ultima Online, and the lack of "events" within that gaming world, I truly felt like a participant in tonight's events. UO began scenarios a while ago, but mainly they were in the form of fiction on their official website with very little in the way of interaction among the players in the game, save ridiculously camped new spawn areas within certain city limits. While their fiction was okay--nothing like the depth I have seen on the AO website--as a player I never enjoyed having to read about the latest event, and then log-in and find the new spawn area, without anything tangible happening in the game world around me. Tonight's "event" in AO was incredible; first the attack by the droids, and then the explosion...nothing like this ever happened in UO. So to all of you with a complaint--which may be valid in your eyes--consider yourselves lucky to be a part of a gaming world where things actually "happen."

Peace.

The 'world' exists on Funcom's server and in the minds of the players who inhabit it. We just pay Funcom our rent for being able to walk outside of our Omni, Clan or Neutral apartments; not for them to live our lives.

It's not Funcom that makes AO alive. It's the people. It's the individuals such as Kirrana who have taken the initiative. It's every single individual who has posted in the In-Character section of this board. It's the people behind HelpBot, ShopBot, AuctionBot and all those other user-created tools to help other players live out their lives here and give it a little more depth at the same time.

The key to making AO flourish actually comes from Quake. That is, the game itself isn't all that great. Look at that game, you have to wonder why it has gathered such a following. The single player missions that ship with it are rather dull. The standard multiplayer deathmatch gets old fast.

What's so special about it? Well, the people behind the mods. The people who've taken the time to extend the game. To think outside of the box, if you will. The people like those I mentioned for AO. They're the ones who have made the game much more than it was. They're the ones who hold the future in their hands. You can very well say that the quake modders were doing "ID's" job.

So, why the long diatribe? Well, it just gets to me when people off-handedly say "good work" and then back-slap with a "why are you bothering?" without comprehending that that is what had made the game they were playing special. These people pour heart and soul into something for the community and then someone from that same audience shows nonchalance towards their work. Maybe it's an issue of pride. Maybe it's the need to feel that your work is somehow important. That somehow, it has made an impact in someone's life.

Whatever it is, all of the "why are you doing it?" comments will ever do is keep these people from ever doing such things ever again. And when they're gone, and the world feels emptier, have solace in the fact that you at least had the chance to say "Funcom should be doing it." Maybe by then, when all the fans have left, you might be right. Funcom will be doing it all. All that is left.
