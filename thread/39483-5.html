The main problem with Soldiers right now is that there is nothing for us to work for after we get to about 160 or so. I can use whatever gun I want, whatever armor, cast all the nanos (not selfbuffed of course, but I can cast them), but there is nothing to build up to. An MP can keep leveling and get on a 200 Nova or CHS or Flashpoint and still have his pets as well. By around 190 any other class can use the same weapons and do the same damage as us, as well as use their own abilities. How can I beat a Doc that can do the same damage as me, plus easily heal all the damage I do to him?

A list of my ideas:

-Remove the self-imposed Nano Shutdown from the TMS and replace it with a recharge time, like on the Agent crit nanos, or the Adventurer frenzy nanos

-Speaking of which, maybe soldiers can get a short term +damage nano of some kind, call it "Reckless Assault" or some crap, make it lower AC's by 1000, they do nothing anyways.

-Give us something to work for, for the love of Pete. About the only thing I can get out of leveling my Soldier now is to say "Look, I'm this level! Woohoo!" I got nothing new to do from level 160-200, and no real reason to take the time to do it.

-Make a new +% Attack Rating buff, make it self only, make it a level 175 requirement, but just make it! I'm tired of having a lower attack rating than any Agent, Fixer, Adventurer, MA, etc. I feel like a chump when I'm supposed to be the tank and an Adventurer 20 levels lower than I am keeps pulling agg off of me.

-New weapons, Soldier only. Some cool looking stuff, too. And make it BIG. I love the CHS, but it looks like a damn Super Soaker. I want to look like I'm going to kill someone, not get them damp.

-And DEFINITELY fix the TMS nano so that I don't have to go hunting for my shield in the NCU display in a frenzied rush whenever I get into some serious business.

-Since we have no reliable healing available to us of any kind, it would be nice to have a getaway nano for when the new and improved mission lag pulls 2 or 3 adds because you're actually 3 rooms away from where the client says you are. Something that does a 3 second stun to all enemies in an area would be perfect. I would really love to be able to solo again. Or maybe a nano that cuts our First Aid recharge by 50% for 3 minutes, then locks the First Aid skill for 5 or something, so we can actually live through 2 mobs at once occasionally.

These are all the ideas I have time to write down, as I am super tired right now. To conclude, give Soldiers something to work towards, or just give me a Grid Armor MK 3 so I can play my Fixer again. : P
