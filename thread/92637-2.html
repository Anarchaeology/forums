There is no planet side... there is no walking around. In that sense it is somewhat limited. The Eve designers saw how bad E&B screwed it up and decided to put their efforts toward actual gameplay and things that mattered.

The fact that you are absent from walking aroudn does not mean you can't role play it .... it requires a bit more imagination, but there is plenty of RP oppertunity (like the rp on the boards from the perspective of someones office or home ... apartments arn't much ;p)... look in the library at player made stories, some very good ones there. Look at the archive of Peek of the Weeks ... Anything happening outside of the ship will be imagination. You can dock in stations, but shows nothing but your ship there. You do have a chracter portrait, but its only for when people are talking to you or for your own use.

The reaosn for this is their planned 5000 star systems ... its a bit massive to allow walking around. Tho I imagine some time in the future when there is a game like that, that melds with AO. Something of that magnitude would Beat all out... but its not possible at this time ...

Its more of what do you prefer? the sci-fi side? or the fantasy side?


Its been compared to E&B a lot ... but the fact that both have starships and both set in space is the only similarity. Eve is more a blade runner/ aliens feel as far as ship design. Give it a long look over it takes a long time to absorb it all.

Serious points of EVE that should be made is it's 100% pvp ... Now before ya say thats it ... there is a method of security ratings ...the farther away you are from core empire systems, the less security there is. While there is talk of a potential of griefer problems, that is mostly curtailed with a harsh death penalty, and the fact that it takes real time to learn skills, and continues to count down while offline. There is so much more that I also suggest reading all FAQ's ... both official, and ones stickied on the forums.(general and new player).
