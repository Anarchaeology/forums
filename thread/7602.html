Less Cave Missions, more City missions!
Ok, one thing I've noticed about this game .. is it seems EVERY single mission is in a cave of some sort.

It's almost as if I'm playing a game in a "Cave of the future" constantly.

I really really enjoyed the City/building style missions, and honestly that's what I kind of expected to see in AO.


Why can't 100 plus Omni AND Clan have missions located in the other sides citys?

It would really increase the enjoyment of this game to have about 80% city style missions, and about 20% cave style.

Instead of about 95% cave style, and about 5% city style.
