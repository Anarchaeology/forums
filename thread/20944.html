Actually, I do see a fair amount of yalms in the cities, I'd say about 1/3 of people are in yalms. Another third is not wearing their yalm because they want to fight, trade, roleplay, etc. The last third is the yalmless minority.

And I wouldn't call the reqs insane. A low-ql yalm (meaning 30s) bought from a store (or got in a mission, but that's not under your control) will have a Vehicle Air requirement around 90. This is a teeny tiny requirement; you can bring your base value to 90 using only some of your ip points you receive from ONE level. Anyway, ever heard of implants and buffs?

If anything, yalms should be MORE rare, expensive, and difficult to equip. Not just anyone can hop into a Cessna and fly from New York to Washington; not just anyone should be able to hop into a yalm and fly from Rome to Omni-1.

Nanotechno, I want to ask you a question. What level is your the character that you're playing currently? By the way, I've been playing my lvl 52 MA for just over a month, and I'm on the verge of getting a yalm. I could have afforded several Kodiaks and many Mids in the time that I've played this char, and I would have been able to spare the ip if I chose to buy them.

Cars aren't used in AO like in the real world because AO isn't the real world. You don't have thousands of players trying to get to work in Omni-1, with traffic jams backing up to the Arena from the Omni-HQ bridge. This is because at one time, the majority of players in a city are standing still, and just a handful are attempting to get across the city. At a level where you're doing city missions, it's unlikely that you'll have a yalm anyway. But if you go out into the country, you'll see everyone flying to their missions. But, there just aren't that many players for traffic jams to form up.
