This all would make AO the best game ever, maybe its not only a dream :)
(no native English)

After the 7 Month i played i feel more and more bored. I cant find anything to do anymore beside the old missions. I have some people in my guild that are trying to level from L170 to L200 and then want to Quit because they see L200 and the final target of AO, i really cant understand why they invest all the time when they know they gonna quit. I am L150 and i cant see the point of doing the same Missions for another 50 Levels which will be noticable slower than all Levels before.

What is needed now, and not in 5 month is:

More Content like Missions for class specific items like armor weapons special fun things.

A real planned outdoor world with interesting places, the minotaur villages arent a bad start, but the clueless Minotaurs that are just placed over the map are no fun. I would like to see some challenge in example pulling the Minotaur clanleader out of his house with a little chance that not every single Minotaur Links up and we got 40 grey mobs in addition to kill.

Sky Cities that are only reachable with Flying Vehicles or by MP´s at least opened for L80+. There you can buy special items have nice meetings with your guild and friends and look down to Rubi Ka while talking. In addition there would only NO DROP items be sold in the shops so that players below L80 have something to look forward. Make the next Sky city L120+ with interesting no drop items. i see the chat in the guild like that "WOW in 3 Levels i can visit the "Skycity" and buy me a XXXX!!"

More Vehicles like group transporters that are able to hold 6 persons. The Mission holder would be the driver aswell

Make the Yalmaha textureable, introduce special shops for those vehicle textures, make a random generator for the textures so that not 100 yalms look the same. Make the price real high like 500k per color change.

Battleships buyable by strong Guilds that have alot cash maybe like 150million for 1 battleship. The battle ship is slow but is a good thing for big wars or take overs of omni or clan bases, slow but deadly. that would improve the conflict alot and more people would build up big guilds.

Siege and Ballistic weapons buyable by Guild.

Underwater areas visitable in Swimsuits (like battlesuits) big underwater caves and alot good interesting loot. At least Enable diving in AO the Swim skill is useless and put some water mobs that are fun int the game.

Make every single Cave or mission entrance like a Mission randomed and depend at the level of the visitor or the medium level of the group, so its possible to explore a bit. Make the random genereator so that each cave has its own specials.
So the Mission terminal horror comes to an end and you can travel around the world and just enter any Cave you want, and you can be sure you wont find greys in them. Put a Boss mob with some loot like 20 levels above the mission intself, so it stays interesting.

Enable the Search function and put some hidden items into the missions and also in the outdoor world, choose old big trees or big Stones or whatever as reasonable search objects. the chance must be low to find something but there should be a chance for the one that searches for a long time to find something.

Armor and weapons need alot more new textures. Almost everyone looks the same when u played a while.

fix the known bugs


thats it. if that all would be included in AO this game would be the game of my dreams. But at the moment i am barely playing longe than 1 hour, and that time i use for talking with friends and guild. No more missions or xp for me because its really boring atm.
