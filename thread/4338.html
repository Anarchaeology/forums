3rd Rubi-Ka Bureaucrat Convention!
Good news, due to popular demand I'm going to organize a 3rd Rubi-Ka Bureaucrat Convention! Currently on the convention agenda is the current views of fellow crats on our partial "fixes". With the next one 13.4 with a crucial change to way charmed pets are handled, things are looking up. Satisified? Are we still the nerfed "lost profession" without a clear purpose or what to do with us? We will see.

Event: 3rd Rubi-Ka Bureaucrat Convention

Place: Rompa Bar in Omni-Entertainment

Date: December 21st, 2001.

Time: 21:00 GMT (1pm Pacific Standard Time)
