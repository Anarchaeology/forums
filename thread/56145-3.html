Depends on the weapon... Usually, weapons call "special" nanos, which are non-castable. However, in that MTI G-90A3 Thotep you spammed, it casts a "normal" nano--which, unfortunately, has a Trader casting req on it. Probably won't work for anyone other than a trader. (Or an Agent FP Trader) Also, those health funnel nano calls--unlike MOST CastStunNano calls--being "normal" nanos, also have normal resists on them. (Most stuns called from weapons have 0% resist on them.)

On the other hand, the Neutrino Flash has 0% NanoResist on it, and no "real" reqs on it--so it should work just fine.

-Jayde
