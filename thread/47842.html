Suggestion: Make Tarasque room with more possible entrances
Funcom, please add more fun to Tarasque room by adding more possible routes to it and by removing the single narrow pathway that leads to it currently. It would be fun to see battles occuring more then one front and where defender does not have such a huge advantage. This way more strategies could be used then just one front full on assault...which is a huge lag fest.

By adding perhaps 2 more entrance to both sides you would also reduce the amount of lag there is currently. Also make all doorways at least three times in size and wihtout doors.
