Template for Bug Reporting
Ok several people wanted a template to use when reporting bugs to me at Testserver@anarchy-online.com. So...I will give you just that =).

----------------------------
----------------------------



Version: (ie 14.5.0.4)



Summary: (brief overview of the problem)



Description: (The problem in detail)



Step by Step: (How 'I' can easily reproduce the problem)



Sidenotes: (here explain your screenshots if you have any in as much details as you see fit...remember before taking SS's to hit Shift+F9 first So i can see all the information on your screen. Mission bugs it is VITAL that you include a SS with that information.)


-Also note sometimes I don't reply...that doesn't mean anything except I have all the info I need. I may contact some of you to get more info though so don't be surprised to see a mail from me in your inbox.
