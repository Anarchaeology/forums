After Shadowlands Suggestion: More RK playfields
I'm hoping shadowlands is going to add a lot of exploring again, there just isn't any wilderness nor much to be discovered on Rubi-Ka anymore.

Which is going to make things complicated when you try to add in a lot more towns with the Towns of Rubi-Ka booster. We're just running out of real-estate on Rubi-Ka between the land control areas, and boss camps. Opening up the towns will probably make it even more crowded.

So my idea is just to open a few more playfields between SL and RK. There are many that never got completed. Just one of each to be oriented towards clan, omni, and neutrals in turn. My suggestions would be:

Endless Plains/Stria Rubi Desert: The home of the Eco Warriors clan could be a redeemed/clan paradise for those wanting to build a utopia away from the front lines of the war. Many of those tired of the war could come here and develop towns.

Kapar: This neutral city is a center of commerce, a free port where neutral, clan, and omni goods can be exchanged. Entreprenuing neutrals would come here to set up markets away from the ravaged war front.

Bay of Rome: Maybe almost the suburbs of Rome? A nice protected seafront dwelling place for retirees and the corporate elite. Rome is supposed to be the best city and Bay of Rome is where they could live when not relaxing at Lush Hills resort. The people here can be proud of being the closest settlement to Jobe.
