Very few professions can solo the Trash King unless being very high level. A L160 MA was beaten into a pulp when trying to do so.

Tactics for taking down TK:

You need: At least 1 pet-profession, 1 calmer, the rest damage-dealers (healer optional).

1. When TK spawns, send in pet (the tougher the better). Everyone wait until the pet attacks TK and actually lands the first blow, thereby getting aggro.

2. Everyone else, let rip. Calmer should calm the Trash King's Lackey who is sure to lurk around, concentrate firepower upon TK. Pet-owner heals pet as needed.

With this tactic, TK concentrates its attacks on the pet, so everyone else isn't harmed unless they are inside melee range.

Good hunting.
