Something uniqe for NTs: Refresh Buffs nano
Since NTs are supposed to be masters of nanotechnology, why not add something like this to make them actually masters?

Imagine a nano that when run resets the timers of all your buffs to full duration. This could be done in 3 versions, a trivial one that resets one buff on the NT himself (self-only), one that resets all the buffs on the NT (also self-only) and finally a team version that resets all buffs on every member of the team.

Imagine the possibilities. A team with an NT could get buffs from outside the team and then run through one or more missions without spending time buffing and also getting the benefits of buffs from outside the team.

Would this make the NT profession too Uber? No, I dont think so, since it only extends the time of Uberness (outside buffs), not adding more Uberness on top of existing buffs. This could actually be one of those "do we have an NT in the team?" that currently doesnt exist.

Well, Im pretty sure this will never happen, but hey, I can dream

Ew, good point. Nullity would be refreshable.

Options: long-ass cast time. If it takes 1 minute to run, you can't refresh ultra-short buffs (Nullity, agent crit buffs, etc.), and it makes refreshing TMS, Chall, or wrangles pretty useless, as you'll be spending 1/3 or more of your time running the refresher.

Now, have the buff-refresher of type N *only* refresh buffs which have NCU requirements less than N, and it still takes 1 minute to cast. I.E. "Enfraam's Nano-Renewer Type 14" would refresh all buffs that take less than 14 NCU. The top of the line would be "Izzgimmer's Moebius Strip" and could refresh all buffs in-game.

Bingo, you can refresh those Marks before your teammates' run out, keep that Behemoth running through those ungodly long missions that sometimes spawn, keep dem Mochies going long after the MP is gone... and it shouldn't game-break, it should just reduce down-time.
