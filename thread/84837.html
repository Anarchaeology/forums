=== 12/26
Hiya. I have developed a sequence of small roleplaying scenarios and need NPCs to move the story forward. Some of these roles are one-shots, but most involve multiple appearances. There is always the chance thay any role will be expanded. I write these roles as NPCs because I don't have anyone in mind to play them. On the other hand, my preference would be to have the roles filled by PCs who can weave the events into their own personal storylines.

If you are interested in any of these roles, please send Szentasha or Syntasha a tell, or send me an email at szentasha@darksphere.com. There is also a potential opportunity for cooperative story writing -- read below. Thanks.

1. Concerned sister who cares for brother with mental disorder. (clan) 2+ scenes.
2. Brother with mental disorder. Must be able to leet speak. (clan, hopefully same race as sister) 2+ scenes.
3. Quirky old man. Lives alone in wilderness. (any side) 1 scene.
4. Unsympathetic scientist/physician. (clan, doc prefered). 1 scene.
5. Old wisened sabertooth mystic. (any side, preferably can self-cast grinning hunter). Minor recurring role.
6. Mad scientist bent on vengeance and destruction. (omni prefered, engineer/nt prefered, reasonable level prefered). Major recurring role.

The mad scientist role is a major focus of the storyline. I need someone who is really into assuming the role and will bring their own creativity to bear on it. Also, this character will become involved with an Omni org with their own goals and motivations. I am begining talks with one Omni individual about this, but its not a done deal yet. What I am looking for is a storywriter for an Omni org who wants to write stories in parralel with my story where the scientist is the point at which the stories overlap. It will involve an interaction of our guild members. And it doesn't require the Omni org to be 'evil' .. just corporate. This person should have the time and motivation to do this for the benefit of their own organization and hopefully have some experience running/writing RP events.

No one RPs to get rich, but my org enjoys being able to offer a small amount of money to individuals for participating in our events as an NPC. Our base tip is 200k per character per scene.

Thanks for taking the time to read this.
