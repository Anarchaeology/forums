Well
Well now that the MA patch is in the assertion that MA crit buffs self only will keep MA's out of groups is Moot.

1. Not enough Doc healers at the high levels
2. MA's can solo most orange mobs now so damage is fixed

As to roots. Those people that say pick up a bow, well, go look at a bow's range sometime you will notice that clip size and range are proportional. Meaning that the lower the clip size the longer the range and bows are slow as hell and don't crit well.

Now roots should not be 100% effective against anyone which against MA's they are. That's like saying that if I as a MA hit a NT that I should always crit for max damage. Roots should aslo not be a instacast with low nano usage. Make them either slow to cast or in efficient to chain cast.

Currently in pvp half the classes have the ability to chain root and it still takes up to 3 shots to break a root. I can rarely ever get close enough to a rooter to do damage before I'm rooted in place. Another alternative would be to make the root range 10m so you would have t be close in order to cast it thereby adding some risk to a riskless strategy.

As to the Rooter Whiners, who are you trying to keep at bay with root? Half the classes can root as well, the rest (not counting MA's) can hit you with range damage at nearly the same range as yourself or break roots. I would think roots weren't that big of a neccesity in PVP. If you root a soldier he is still going to alpha strike you and probably kill you. The same goes for the other classes carrying Nova's or Shotguns. So it would seem to me that root is really only good for 4 things:

1. Letting you run away
2. Slowing down but not stopping enforcers
3. Rooting pets
4. Killing MA's with impunity

In it's current state MA's will never have enough NR to counter an NT(example). It's a dark blue skll to us. If you don't want a fair fight then just have the balls to admit it and be done with the discussion. Very simple really.
