Major clans and clan structures
We're currently working on gathering the affiliation from all the free clan leaders - step by step (don't contact us, we will contact you ), and hence though it was about time we gave you a short run-down of the different options you have. Any free clan can also choose to entirely independent, of course. (Belonging to a player run alliance counts as Independent, at least for now.)

Note that the list differs a bit from the one previously found on the old AO site. More detailed descriptions will come in the Story section (The Present) of the main site.

The Council of Truth
The Council of Truth is a cross-clan political union led by a Council of Delegates, under the leadership of Henry Radiman. Headquartered in Tir. Its the only clan organisation recognised by the Omni-Tek Corporation.

The Sentinels
The Sentinels is a large, radical, anti-Corporate militant clan, led by the charismatic Simon Silverstone. They believe in a military solution to the conflict, and the total annihilation of Omni-Tek and its employees.

New Dawn
New Dawn, led by Ruth Montezuma, is the largest and most liberal of the Council of Truth affiliated clans. They argue for a diplomatic solution to the conflict.

The Knights
The Knights is a Council of Truth affiliated clan created around the romantic ideals of King Arthurs Knights of the Round Table. Led by Lord Galahad, the Knights are headquartered in Avalon.

Terra Firma
Terra Firma is a political clan working to establish a publicly elected, democratic Rubi-Kan government. Affiliated with the Council of Truth, and led by Hayden Okoli.

Vanguard
Vanguard is a capitalistic clan with business interests across Rubi-Ka, working for a mutually beneficial  and monetarily satisfactory  solution to the conflict. Affiliated with the Council of Truth, and led by Alan Jacobi.

Gaia
Gaia is an environmentally oriented clan, led by Sister Elsabet. Their goal is to construct a new nation, a Garden of Eden, on the opposite side of the planet. Affiliated with the Council of Truth.

The Unionists
Radical political activists, led by Aideen Landau and affiliated with the Council of Truth, who want to exile all Corporate-affiliated citizens, and place Rubi-Ka under a clan-led regime.

Eco Warriors
Eco Warriors is an extremist militant clan with conservationist and environmentalist dogma, which states that humans have no rightful place on Rubi-Ka.

The Coalition of Rubi-Ka
The Coalition of Rubi-Ka is a direct rival to the Council of Truth, and counts many small clans amongst their numbers. Led by Dr. Shaun T. Broussard.

Solidarity
Solidarity supports a peaceful solution to the conflict, albeit without the Council of Truths involvement. Led by Argot Mullen.

The Consolidated Clans
The Consolidated Clans is a militant political assembly of smaller clans established to compete with the Council of Truth.

The Dust Brigade
The Dust Brigade is a terrorist organisation. Note that while you can choose to affiliate with them, this will be only as sympathizers.
