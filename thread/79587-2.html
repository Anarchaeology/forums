My suggestion forto new prof.
Jester

The Jester is the master of awkward situations - both creating and withstanding them. The Jester can evade almost all forms of attacks, often succeeding in turning them back on the attacker. Common household items become deadly weapons in the hands of a Jester, often taking the unwary opponent by complete suprise. When faced with a single or large numbers of potential foes, he has the ability to reduce oppent's atticking initiatives by making them laugh so hard they lose concentration and musculary control. This skill works even better at close range, when he can tickle the enemy to the point of causing physical injury, madness or even death in extreme cases. The Jester can also summon a pet monkey to help him entertain the 'audience'

Key Abilities:
Pros: Quick, hard to hit, can handle multi-opponent situations better than most professions, can wear various artificial body parts (notably a large, red nose) to help him do his job. Can weild almost anything as a weapon, inluding buckets of water, foodstuffs and big, latex gloves. Some of these can also have the effect of stunning the opponent for a short period.

Cons: The Jester is not the greatest damage dealer, nor can he wear heavy and cumbersome armor types. The Jester is too clumsy to use normal ranged weapons with any success. Bureaucrats are somewhat immune to the Jester's pranks and jokes.
