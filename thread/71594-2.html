I am not a title hugger. I have been Apprentice a few times and have had it taken from me almost as quickly. hehe mostly by Traders. Most other professions won't fight a GA IV Fixer. So I am relegated to ganking Omni in Omni Ent with the hopes that a Rookie will be around and a doc won't... and that it will only be 1 v 2 instead of the usual 1 v 5.

I have beaten Traders even with my GA OE'd to 25%. Two nights ago I had a self buffed duel (except for the rrfe thrown onto me cuz I HoT'd someone) with an Omni Trader and it was close but I beat him. He was so surprised that he kept yelling at me in /tells that I wasn't self buffed and that he would kick my ass if I was.

First off when you gank someone (he ganked me) there is no "agreement" to a self buffed duel and secondly I WAS self buffed for the most part. I had my weak health and normal Fixer buffs and expertises running. The Trader assumes he will beat everyone which is his downfall. Some people just are not equipped well enough to PvP.

Traders SHOULD own GA Fixers but not all of them do. I have fought some Trader friends to 50% without drains and they have come close to beating me. These are the uber Traders... all i have to do is think and what if I was -415 skills.... These are the guys you don't go picking on.

There are titled people out there that are meat. Easy kills. And there is 2ho. Mass PvP (which I don't do much of) where individual skills and tactics can be countered by more organized group of players to target you and take you down in 5 seconds.
