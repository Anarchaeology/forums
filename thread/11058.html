Idea: Friend Or Foe list.
I was read a thread about bad teaming experiences on aovault when I came onto this idea.

We all had bad experience teaming before. If not we've dealt with *******s in some way. People throwing up fake bids for auctions, people who being or harass you for credits, buffs, wrangles. At the same time, we've also had great experiences with other players. Player who go out of their way to help you and doesn't ask for anything in return, higher lvl players that take down a red that's kicking the a** out of you out in the middle of no where. Or that nice guy who gave you a ql10 armor when you were just a newbie.

It'd be nice to be able to create a list of Friends and Foes and (FoF list) be able to trade/exchange them. The chat menu lets you create a "Friends" list, but it doesn't allow you to keep track of "enemies" or "annoyances". I use the "Friends" list to remember people whom I know I can really trust. As for big time jerks, I keep their name on a notepad by my desk.

If you play AO in a Window. It is trivial to switch to notepad and write down the names of people you like/dislike and keep track of them. But it'd be great if we can swap/exchange this list with other players as well. I mean, I want to be able to ask a trusted guild memeber for his list, and download a list of people he considered to be *******s or people he considered to be cool. Or maybe download a list of griefers from a trusted web site.

Assuming I get my FoF list from a trusted source. OK, I mean, assuming I'm smart enough to get my FoF list from someone I can trust. I'll know in advance what kind of people I should stay away from and what kind of people I can trust. I'll know what kind of reputation each player has. I think a reputation system like this, will encourage players to act for the good of the community, instead of acting for selfish needs. If you are an *******, players everywhere will eventually know who you are and stay away from you. Hell, some might even attack you. But if you are nice and friendly, you will be treated nicely in return.

What I really hope for is to be in a guild and learn immediately who our enemy/friends are. It can really encourage people to act more socially responsible. It can really bring a sense of "being a part of a community" to Rubi-Ka.

I am thinking of a FoF, or Friend-Or-Foe list. I think a system like this would be easy to implement.

Like the "friends" list in chat, you can create a list of players in-game. But next to each player is a score, from 4 to -4, that rates how "good" or "bad" they are. Where 4 is someone you can trust and can be good friends with, and -4 is someone that'll be the equal of OJ Simpson, or, to put it another way, someone you'd be disgusted with.

4 - Really Trusted Friend
3 - Played with, had fun
2 - Told by friend to be really trust-worthy
1 - Told by friend to be trust-worthy
0 - Neutral/Unknown
-1 - Rumored to be an jackass
-2 - Rumored to be major jackass
-3 - Had bad experience with
-4 - Want to muder this SOB

Adjust ratings - The value next to each player can be changed at anytime. I can mark someone as a -4 (because he used to be an ass), and change it to 1 if he eventually starts to improve. I can download a FoF from a guild mate, and change a rating 2 player to rating -3, because I played with the person before and he was an ass to me.

Trade lists - My FoF list should be tradable with others. I mean, I should be able to download a friend's list and compare the people who we consider to be friends/enemies. And when I do get a FoF list from another player, I should be able to confirm conflicts or outright reject all conflicts.

Exchanging lists - If another player marks someone as 4 (really trustworthy), the person should, by default, only show up as 2 on your list. I mean, if you really trust your friend, you can go ahead and change that 2 to a 4. But typically we want people to just take another player's word at face value. A 4 rating should only be given to a player that you have spend a lot of time with and, in your opionion, deserves the highest trust rating.

Encourage community spirit - I'd like to believe that if a person knows he will be branded when he commits a "bad" act, or if a person knows that his actions will affect his reputation, he is more likely to act towards the good of the community, instead of his selfish needs. And I think a system like this will really help improve many people's games.

Regarding having seen this idea before, I am not surprised. I don't think it's the most innovative, I'm sure something like it have been brought up before. I just wonder what came of it (reject/considering...)

peekaboo, I think you misunderstood me. I am not saying you HAVE TO rate and share your views with everone. I am asking for an in-game tool that helps one do that if he chooses.

I already keep a list (in notepad) of people I've had good and bad experiences with. I think a lot of us keep track of who's a friend and who to stay away from (using notepad or smoething, we all use the friends list in chat). You say you will warn your friend if you see them dealing with bad people. This is only a in-game mechanism to help you do that.

Of course, the quality of a list is only as good as the people you share them with. But rational players like you or I wouldn't just take another player's list at face value. We would only "trust" the list from a good, reliable source.

An analogy would be rumors, you probably wouldn't believe a rumor you heard just on the radio. But if you heard the same rumor from a good, reliable, intelligent friend, it'd be easier to believe.

Again, this is only an in-game tool to help us keep notes. I don't think keeping notes in-game will adversely affect the game world. But I could be just not seeing the defects of my own suggestion. What do you think?
