GridStream Productions New Schedule
Press release as of Sunday, 23 March, 29477

NEW GSP SCHEDULE

GridStream Productions announces today that we have re-arranged our weekly schedule somewhat, you will notice that some of the regular shows have changed days and/or times, so don't get too surprised when it's someone other than the 'usual' on the air.

The following schedule is permanent until further notice, and is effective immediately. Thanks for listening to GSP!

Mondays
DJ Wulfven: Lunar Eclipse - 8pm EST / 1am GMT

Tuesdays
DJ Otori: The Abyss - 8pm EST / 1am GMT

Wednesdays
Lance Orbin - 4pm EST / 9pm GMT
DJ Tarryk: LTK Deviant - 8pm EST / 1am GMT

Thursdays
DJ Tastyvixen: Ecstasy Radio - 9pm EST / 2am GMT

Fridays
DJ Xtrophic: Temporal Vortex - 4:30pm EST / 9:30pm GMT
DJ Tristalyn: The Zone - 8pm EST / 1am GMT

Saturdays
Reserved for scheduled events

Sundays
Tarryk & Metalynx: Talk Deviant - 6pm EST / 11pm GMT
DJ Wulfven: Sunday Night Solace - 8pm EST / 1am GMT
