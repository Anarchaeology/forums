Biggest visual flaw
One of the things that bugs me most in this graphically beautiful game, is that whenever you see a group of guys over the level of 100, you can always bet on one thing - they will all be dressed the same. No innovation whatsoever, always a Hanshi Primus coat (ugly as hell and you wish its AC would be better in RL scenario than a Tank Armor).

Why is that -
FC nerfed the Tank armor, for really no obvious reason other than to put us all into this UGLY UGLY primus coat. If you get the same AC on a Primus but a Primus isnt nerfed as the Tank Armor is, the choice is obvious.

So I call out to the FC dude who made the sorry ass mistake by nerfing the Tank - LET US DRESS AS WE WANT! ENOUGH WITH THE PRIMUS BULL****!

Bring back the Tank guys, Soldiers looked awesome then, not like my grandma after shopping.
