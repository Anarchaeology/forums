Theoretically, in a large organization such as Omni or the clans, you would expect inter-agency rivalries to come up. Different departments would try to one up each other for status and funding. Sometimes, the leaders of different groups would be openly hostile to each other, with it being common knowledge that each would love to see the other fail.

However, only in extreme situations would you expect to see a murder occur because of this, and certainly not open armed conflict. Also, any murder would be targetted at key individuals hoping to cripple another groups ability to function. With cell scanner technology, the idea of permanently removing an individual by killing them is obsolete. This leaves us with only being able to use political means to elimate or marginalize an opponent.

We do see these sorts of conflicts happening in game, on RK2 the guilds Quizotic (clan) and Total Chaos (omni) have been, at one time or another, under fire from their fellow guilds. Perhaps not in a "I'm trying to kill you" sense, but certainly in a political sense.
