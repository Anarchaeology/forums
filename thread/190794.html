The Calm Before The Storm
I was sitting in the eerie electronic light of my appartment.

My head bowed down slightly, my hands holding each other...
The black couch I was staring down at underneath me, giving me no comfort.

My white Omni-Pol armor giving off a soft glow as I sat there...
Pondering.

"Audio. Search database for the 21st century."
I spoke softly, tiredly... so weary.
I had always been a dreamer... a thinker... someone with a fading hope. When it came to some things... recently, a born pessimist.

Had my friends seen me now though...
They'd see something they had grown used too,
me bursting out in a tirade of how fruitless some pursuits were.

But I'd always settle down after a bit,
Dismissing the matter.

Not so this time.

"21st century loaded, Mr. Storm."
It almost sounded like the sensual woman's voice was pleased.

"Tell me... Erinne... would you betray me, like everyone else has?"

A long pause followed.

"No, Mr. Storm.", the voice sounded soothing... caring.

In the darkness of my loneliness,
I smiled.
Faintly.

"Erinne...", I spoke gently... that same faint smile on my lips.

"Yes, Simon?"
If you could give a massage with a voice... Erinne was rubbing my shoulders right now.

"...Sometimes I think you're just as much a woman, as any other I've met."

Another pause... shorter this time, Erinne seeming amused.

"Why's that, Simon?", the tone changed slightly... teasing me lightly, "...I'd hardly call me a woman though... for one thing,
I seem to be much better at keeping you with me.
While they come and go."

I fall silent, closing my eyes... a painful remainder.

"...Simon...?", Erinne knew she'd said something wrong.
She couldn't understand what though... from her limited perspective, she was right of course.

I sigh, tormented by memories... some very fresh, and very real.

"I merely meant one thing, Erinne..."

She stays polietly silent, waiting for me to continue.

"...you lie just as bad as the rest of them."
My eyes fell on my plasmaprojector lying next to my couch, along with my helmet.

The dark visor seemed almost alert... wary... dangerous.
No other emotions except that of vigilance,
with a hint of death.

Smirking to myself, I picked up the helmet in heavy hands.

"...I didn't lie, Simon..."
The voice seemed pained, hurt.

"...That's what they all say, Erinne... that's what they all say."
As I held the helmet in my hands... the sensors in my armor informing my skin exactly how it should feel... if my own hands were placed ontop it's cold alloy.

It felt right.

I opened the visor, the helmet a hollow cranium, and a protection from the outside world - together with the rest of my armor.

I deftly put it on and picked up my Division 9 plasmaprojector.

Slamming my visor shut... Not much could harm me now.
Not physically... nor emotionally.

I was weary... tired into my very bones...

The visor informing me exactly what was around me, the threats nearby...

And a soft voice speaking in my interior comlink.

"...Simon... please...", Erinne whispered... only to me, "...I didn't lie... you must believe me..."

I close my eyes... my walls falling like domino-bricks.

Slowly... I retrace my steps back from an abyss of depression that for just a flicker of a moment threatens to overwhelm me, though for me... it doesn't feel like "just a moment".

Twisting, changing.
My thoughts that were directed at the past...
Altered, bent.
They seem to swirl...

I find myself standing in a deep sand-blasted valley.
I take a ragged breath, the stench of sulfur and melting copper heavy in the air.

Friends and foes alike lying in heaps of blood,
shattered by explosions or direct fire...
Their features twisted...

I stand there numb.
Gazing through horrified eyes...
Watching.

A woman wearing combat fatigues made of light kevlar...
Special Forces.
Black armor.
Both her arms are missing at the elbows, I'm not sure what hit her... nor do I want to know.
But the stumps hang useless at her sides, blood gashing forth from her torn flesh.
Eyes staring unseeingly... the back of her head missing...
It looks like someone sliced half of it off with a monofilament dagger.

A clean cut... I can see her brain... and other organs throbbing faintly...
Her body held upright by a hover tank's cannon that flew up high in the air
after the tank itself blew up in a fiery explosion...
The entire cannon turret breaking off in that explosion....
Obviously the cannon itself pretty much flew level into her, staking her to the cliff behind her....

I'm not sure how long I stood there, dazed... at the unfamiliar... yet old memories flooding my senses.

"Erinne... pick something fitting from the audio library."

I leaned back heavily in the couch,
as a soft, slightly desperate tune began playing...

Record showed it as "Bring Me To Life"... some obscure group.
I guess she did pick something fitting after all.

"...How can you see into my eyes... like open doors...
Leading you down into my core...
where Ive become so numb without a soul...
my spirit sleeping somewhere cold...
until you find it there and lead it back home...

The melody calling out to bring me to life...
For someone to wake me up inside...
A haunted voice.

"...Bid my blood to run...
before I come undone...
Save me...!
save me from the nothing Ive become...

now that I know what Im without
you can't just leave me
breathe into me and make me real
bring me to life

...don't leave me to die here..."


...The music faded, leaving me once again sitting in the dark, although this time...
Not alone.

My visor tracking four sentinent beings, approximately 1,8 meters in height,
outside my door.

"...yes, Erinne... you do see some things more clearly then most.", I speak silently... tired, not wanting to break the magic the words leave behind.

She gave a soft "mhmm..." as an only reply.
It was enough.... a light kiss the way it came out.

"...let's deal with our visitors then...", slowly getting to my feet, my combat armor now fully activated, the mechanized attack suit beginning to augment my movement and strength already. The only hint of the power build-up was the visor turning a complete black.

"Erinne."
My words now precise, commanding.

"Yes, Lieutenant?"

"Activate full defensive countermeasures and any and all offensive and augmenting nano-programs."

The air was already shimmering around me in every imaginable colour I could think of at the time. The so tiny nanobots that were spread throughout the entire atmosphere obeying Erinne's commands to alter both myself, my armor.... even the air around me.

"Nanobot-link established. Suggested programs running at 100%. Do you wish to activate the fail-safe at this time?"

Frowning slightly behind my helmet,
I shake my head.

"No, let's keep that for later. Whenever I fall to wound status 2, activate it automatically."
