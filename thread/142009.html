Murder... maybe another Perma-kill?
Rizz walks around the Rompa bar on his usual routing, checking that his employees are working well. He looks stressed, the job has not let him have many opportunities to relax, and he is thinking about getting some aspirin for that headache...
He suddenly hears an ear-piercing scream, sounding an awful lot like one of his employees, and yells back, hoping to get to her in case there's trouble. After she yelled back, he ran to the ladies' bathroom at the bar, only to encounter one of the foulest odors he had smelled in his life, and a strange puddle on the floor... it looked like blood... but it had other things in it, the waitress was trembling as she looked at the puddle. Upon closer examination (and a few toilet-fulls of vomit) Rizzior managed to figure out it was actually a melted body... someone's body. He took DNA samples as people from the bar rushed to the scene, stepping on the puddle, making comments and whispering questions about the puddle... He shook the little DNA sample and analyzed it. He managed to identify that it was actually two different people melted in one puddle... and the first name popped up instantly... "Stindy" an omni employee...the second one, since it was not on omni databases, took a little longer... "Shadowgrider".. a clanner... there were hints of acid mixed in with the sample...

He glances at the puddle in disgust and calls Division 9...

He knows that if corpses do not evaporate it only means one thing... those people aren't coming back to life any time soon... but why did someone melt them?....
