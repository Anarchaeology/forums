I walk on in and get jumped by a level 8 doctor who I was only hitting for 5 ma damage, and well he was hitting me in the 80's I was dead fairly quick.. Then I go in again this time an enforcer about level 19 I think. I rooted em down and got pretty far away since they can hit at a good distance with a melee attack. I start shotting and boom got a crit I guess of around 95. Then started shooting regulars... 5 damage.... 5 damage.... then he walked over and hit me once with regular attack for about 160. So no go against those two. I went out and leveled up for a couple days then went to a open PVP zone. I am level 30 now and still no confidence but went in anyways. That all sounds good, but means nothing with the all mighty tab button, show names clicked on, and show other effects off. I liked this game in beta, PvP some there though in later halfs of beta2 and begining of 3 with flammers .. :-P. I wish I could play with others and PvP without worrying about every other person has 50 million creds on his level 20 guy. I really do not know what to tell anyone. All I can tell you is this story.

I conclude tha Agent class does not have any weapons that compare to most others.
