Yet another (rehashed) idea for apartments.
How about tapping into the mission builder system? Pick a subset of apartment-friendly rooms and use them. Heck, let players roll and reroll apartments until they have something they can live with. Put a limit to the number of rooms, or the square-footage of rooms.

If you did this, you could even let a character re-roll for a new apartment every title-level, with a higher limit. Similar to my title-level item-limit suggestion from much earlier.

One special building with a locked whompa/door, that uses the existing apartment key to gain entry and activate. Take the apartment key, and provide private access to a new room with two doors, one console, and a special bank/reclaim. The exit, a door leading to a randomly rolled apartment playfield, the button to re-roll the apartment, and a bank/reclaim holding all the contents of the previous apartment. Leaving this special playfield locks this as your new apartment playfield, and entering any apartment door provides you with a new key to it.

Make sure its use is limited to those who haven't used it since their last title upgrade.

Perhaps just one such playfield hidden away in the ICC area so everyone is welcome to access it. Otherwise, use a few doors that are currently inactive in the main cities.

Might be fun, and even useful, if the sign over the door said "Housing Authority".
