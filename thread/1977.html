This is the team I would recommend for combatting a single L120 mob, with a team of L70's.

First stage is the pulling of the mob upto half mob HP.
Second Stage is from half mob HP to mob death.

1. Soldier - Purely as the frontliner. First Stage RF Tank and Puller.
2. MA - Prime melee damage dealer. Second Stage.
3. MP - Sanoo/Wrath. Sits on MA.
4. NT - First Stage : Fast non-aggro nanos. Second Stage : Major Nukes.

The other 2 you can be flexible with, but I'd advise the following:

5. MP - Sanoo/Wrath. Sits on Soldier, or is on NT so they are open to use larger nukes during First Stage.
6. MA - Backup Healer and damage dealer.

or

5. Enforcer - Backup damage dealer and good buffer.
6. Doc - Healing. Shouldn't enter combat at all.

Other line-ups that I've seen that work well are:

1. MA - Tank
2-6. NT - Area Effect Nanos * 5

Made for cleaning up groups of mobs VERY fast.

The problem I have is that NT's, Sanoo, etc will take flak from time to time, and that aggro enhancers are at times very unreliable. I've grouped with too many people that are afraid to leave the room because they feel that they won't get XP from the mob, whereas I would sooner see them cast one last big nano/buff/heal/nuke, then leave and heal.

You'll find that the MA(s) or Soldier will be able to pull the mob off the NT fairly easily via damage, unless they are underachieving in the damage stakes.

As far as I'm concerned tho, Meta-Physisists are without doubt the most useful of all people to have on a team.

The last thing I would like to mention tho Chirurgie (fellow AlphAStrike guild member) is that it's one thing people dying in the cyborg dungeon and having to come all the way back, but making 7 other guild members wait 45 minutes for you to get into the grid and come to Tir is quite another issue. If you want a group to be prompt and fast acting, then practice what you preach fine sir.

Initial timekeeping is more important than having to wait for a member of the team that died for a good cause. Usually they die to keep the doc alive.
