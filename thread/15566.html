Top game issues and suggestions
1) The random crashes/lockups/VC runtime errors/etc. I've no doubt FC are trying
to fix these, but being a developer, I am all too aware of how difficult this
sort of thing can be.

2) Efficiency of criticals. Some weapons ahve very high critical values relative
to their normal damage, others have very low. I would remove the critical
damage all together and simply have a critical do normal damage +75% of maximum
damage. This puts all weapons on a similar footing and leads to the next issue...

3) Weapon balance - fix weapon balance so that a much greater variety of weapons
become viable choices for players. This would require fast weapons to have
greater minimum damage (as they do lower maximum than slower weapons, they hit
AC limits far more often, increasing minimum damage compensates to some exent).
Other weapons could have interesting skill bonuses (roughly +1 per 5QL) such
as attack skills, evades, nanos etc.

4) Class balance. Points 2 and 3 above would help a lot in this direction, but other
issues need to be addressed. This really needs a lot of experimentation, I'd
suggest finding some experienced players and asking them to set up on test, then
give them 3 chars of their preferred class (lvl 50, lvl 100 and lvl 150) along
with suitable levels of cash, then asking them to go out and solo dfferent MOBs
and to PvP each other. Collect hard evidence, identify issues from that evidence,
work out solutions, then put these solutions back onto test and repeat.

5) Static content. Ways to supply this type of content but without camping (or
significant camping a least) need to be found. For the static dungeons, keys
are one possible solution (I'd suggest each part getting a key will get a new
instance of the dungeon, but once there are, say, 5 instances, new keys give
access to one of the existing instances picked at random - allows for some
interaction to occur, Camelot Castle should probably have less allowed
instances).
For the static missions (more please), the NPCs shouldn't vanish,
instead, players should be prevented from getting a mission they already have
or have already done. Another way would be to have the NPCs spawn in one of
several locations at random, but much more often (every 15min - 30min).

6) High level content. Doesn't affect me yet but... New high level zones are needed
with tougher monsters and hunting areas, new mission areas etc. Special high level
shops could sell new items designed for high level play (at nice money-sink type
prices) that player's will actually want. Many should be 'charged' type items
such as super heal packs, One-shot portable scanners - would need to be labeled
NODROP and UNIQUE to allow in field saving, etc.

Any other issues/suggesions - constructive and thought out posts only please.
