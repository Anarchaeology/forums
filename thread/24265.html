Black market items: How I learned to love the bomb
Are you ready to use that Disarm Traps skill? Someone set us up the bomb:

Name: A.Y.B. Bomb
Description: Created by the secret terrorist organization, known only by it's leader K.A.T.Z., for the intent purpose of pushing it's control of outposts against opposing factions. It sends out a virus that reprograms nanobots in the suppression field to transmit and then terminate.
Type: Container
QL:200
Value:100000000
Flags: NO DROP, BLACKMARKET, ILLEGAL
Requirements:weapon smithing 800
Effects: modify area supression 0%

Name: Fallout Bomb
Description: Using nanobots and Time/Space to pull a small portion of a star through a warpgate, this detonates and realeases solar radiation into the area, irradiating all of its victims within a large area.
Type: Container
QL:200
Value:10000000
Flags: NO DROP, BLACKMARKET, ILLEGAL
Requirements: weapon smithing 800
modify area health -100 - -100 10 hits, 5s delay

Name: Temporary Mind Control
Description: This insidious device enrages the battle-hardened to the point of mindless fury, causing those that guard the city to go berserk and attack all of it's citizens. This effect lasts only for a short period, but the damage is done within this period.
Type: Container
QL:200
Value:100000000
Flags: NO DROP, BLACKMARKET, ILLEGAL
Requirements: Weapons smithing 800, Psychology 750
modify area NPC faction: Clan (Omni)
Chat shout: You have no chance to survive, make your time!

Just a few ideas from your friendly neighborhood mad-bomber.

Other items would include:
Illegal weapons (KOS when equipped near same-faction NPCs)
Illegal armour (Same KOS)
Illegal Nanos (when used, KOS against same faction)

The reason why they are NO DROP, is they can only be transferred by either Fixers, NPC Underworld contacts, or assembled as a tradeskill. To create, you will need some other tradeskill items of the same, 10% below, or above in order to assemble them using parts purchased from Fixers or Underworld. To activate, use a screwdriver, which will change the NO DROP status, create either a bot or a treasure chest item on the ground. Next step is to either get your guns ready, or run like a flying-mouse out of a fictional place Dante described.

As always, constructive input (from players) greatly appreciated.

[Addendum]Imagine what this would do to PvP and the Storyline?[/Addendum]
