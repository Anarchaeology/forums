a samurai's tale....
pt.1

Staring deeply into the fire, Theo watched the inky mixture roll to a slow boil. Soon it will be ready, he thought. Rising, he turned his gaze away, staring deep into the moonless night.

"The night is a time for listening."

Thats what Sensei had told him early in his training, long before he'd left the Clans. Perhaps if he'd have paid more attention to that early statement, it all would have turned out differently... Sighing, he recalled that night now, as always, with a brutal clarity.


__________



Theo came fully awake in the space of a heartbeat. It was not what he heard but rather had felt. Something had reached deep into his mind and given the command to awaken.

He was up and across the pitch-black room in an instant. Took up his dark blade Suigetsu, and, naked, went out into the upstairs hall. Instinct took him past the Master's room. The sliding door was ajar; he did not need to look to know that he was not there.

Crept down the stairs on the insides of his soles. A breeze would have made more noise. He held his blade sideways with both hands, elbows slightly bent. He advanced as he had been taught, left side forward. His fists were wrapped tight across the haft of the blade, ready to be used as a shield should the need arise.

"Without sangaku you are nothing", Sensei had said.

"Discipline, Concentration, Wisdom. These three constitute Sengaku. Without these three elements you can attain nothing. You may learn to slash, to maim, to kill. But you will be nothing. Your spirit shall wither, your body shall weaken, and surely there will come a time when you will be cut down. This will occur not by the sword of a more skilled opponent, but by the force of his enlightened spirit. Without the wisdom of truthfulness, survival is impossible. This is a tenet of the Way."

Discipline, Concentration, Wisdom

These were what theo summoned as he assumed the wheel. The strong defensive blading stance that would allow him to rotate his blade in any direction he chose.

At the bottom of the stairs, he could see that the doors leading to the training hall were open.. there were tiny sounds, then a grunt... Sensei was in there!

Part of him screamed to rush in. Discipline, Concentration, Wisdom. He could almost hear Sensei's raspy voice intoning those words to him now through lips that rarely seemed to move. "To prevail in battle, you must relinquish life and death. They must cease to be of concern to you, only then.. you shall be a samurai."

Theo advanced step by step, through the partly open door. It was far darker in here than any other part of the house.

Listening, tiny noises became a recognizable pattern. The grunts and strains of unarmed combat. Hearing this, he nearly sheathed his blade, figuring to use his body.

He advanced a single step, then something stopped him. He could feel the aura of the intruder. He knew -KNEW- that whoever was inside also wielded a blade.

Moving swiftly with absolute silence into the training hall. Nevertheless, he was overheard.

A shriek in his ear as a candle before him was sheared in two. Master! his mind shouted. Where are you? Are you safe? Or.. He then felt the alarming closeness of the gleaming dark blade, and lunged forward and out with his own, regretting it immediately as the other blade struck the top of his own, forcing the tip deep into the carpeted floor.

Theo cursed himself. His anxiety over the danger to Sensei had caused him to lose concentration.

"An inau****ious attack will fail and, having failed, will both alert your opponent and bolster his determination."

In the instant it took to free his blade, he felt the presence of the others sword.. a predators shadow amid a forest of shadows. And in that same instant, without looking, he knew where it was, and now that it was in motion, what its target was.

Tucked his head down into the pit of his stomach as he rolled into a ball. The hardest part was letting go of Suigetsu, but he had felt the enemy blade's intentions: to sever the head from his body.

Rolling hard into the shadowy figure, he felt its weight on top of him, pushing him into the ground, curling over him. Instantly he felt a hand searching to cover his nose and mouth as the others body fell down upon him, folding around him. The pressure upon the small of his back raised an alarm. The enemy would most likely maneuver him into a position where a low-force kick could snap a vertebra or rupture his spleen.

Using his elbows to gain momentum, Theo struggled to roll past this vulnerable spot.

But now his shoulders were mashed against the carpet, the shadow pressing down on him. With no way to protect his face, he smelled the chemical even before the cloth made contact. Holding his breath as the fumes burned at his nostrils.

He wanted desperately to use his hands, but the pressure upon him was so great that if he were to move his elbows at all, he would be open to a lethal kick. Rolled up into a ball as he was, his legs were of no use to him.

His training allowed him to hold his breath for extended periods, but even he had limits. He could see nothing but distorted shadows, smell nothing but hate and blood, hear nothing but the rush of blood through his veins. In the state his senses were in, the only clarity was the singing in his brain. The silent scream that presaged the fall into unconsciousness, the swift slide to defeat.

Struggling, Theo found himself thinking of the moment he had struck out with his blade, of how that had been his fatal mistake. Replaying the instant over and over, he wondered what would have happened if he had heeded Senseis warning.

"To meet the enemy with your mind settled in that spot where your fists grip your sword."

Then the weight was off of him suddenly. Wobbling to all fours, struggling to stand, he glanced upwawrd with great effort to see a broken figure against the wall.. "grandfather... ?" he worded mouthlessly as the last of his strength turned to water and his insides churned.. he heard a soft chuckling an instant before the boot crashed down, the chuckling was that of sensei..

Then, only falling..
