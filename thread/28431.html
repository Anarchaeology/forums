Jedi skills are broken into spheres of powers. Some effect physical attributes, others mental. Others simply have their own trees. Not all are confirmed, but these are some jedi abilities.

Forge Lightsabre- Make a lightsabre. Using one you created gives you bonus attributes.

Forge Double- Edged Lightsabre- Make a lightsabre which protrudes 2 laser-blades from both sides.

Force Influence- With a wave of a hand, enemies do what you want.

Force Speed- Enhanced Speed.

Blaster Deflection- Deflect Laser Blaster.

Force Telekinesis- The ability to "push" objects/creatures manipulating the force.

Force Meditation- Strengthens a Jedi's resolve while weakening an enemies' conviction.

Quick Draw- Jedi can quickly draw lightsabres and put them away
