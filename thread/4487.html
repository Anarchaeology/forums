****e up guilds - objectives
Ok, how will I describe my idea well as it now its not possible for missions to conquer areas small town. I would like to give guilds the possible to capture/hold small town and outposts and guilds from other faction to take it back. This would of course mean that many more zones would get turned in to 25% zones. One of the problems is that guilds miss an objective. An objective could be to attack and capture a small area/or outpost or hold a small area/outpost in the greater good of your faction. I know that some guilds are mostly online in a certain time span according to what continent theyre from. This could be helped by giving guilds a number of guards (as the one seen in cities) calculated on the number of members or/and the lvl of these members. The guild leader/generals could then assign what coordinates these guards should guard (of course limiting them to defense only). This would greatly increase the objectives for a guild (add more RP to the game) and add to reasons why to join a guild. This would also add to reasons why a guild should find allies within a faction to efficiently secure areas.

You could ****e it even more up with vending machines changing sides when an area where occupied with a clan/omni affiliated guild occupied the area. Or the guild could itself define what factions could use them. That would especially good if many remote outposts included some vending machines with valuable equipment.

PvP: More PvP, yes there would be more risk leaving cities. But as long as you stay to areas that are secured by guilds there wont be much risk. The only zones where PvP would not be allowed would be neutral citiesSince theyre neutral. (Would also give them(neutrals) a chance to RP)
I know many casual gamers are not happy about PvP, but again its a RP game with a conflict: clan >< omni.

Storyline: I dont think it would ruin the storyline. Not with the Amnesty ending at one time. FC would probably have to be more flexible in their storyline, since the gamer would have much more influence on the conflict, but if theyre removing the animated series that would probably not be a problem.

I know these ideas nay seem far out, but its just elements I think would make the game so much more funAnd turn away the leveling focus the game seems to have atm.
