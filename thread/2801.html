The Trials and Tribulations of Kaiyne
quote:
Sometimes you need the barrel of a gun to change your mind, sometimes you need it to splatter a leet. It's all the same thing.
Kaiyne started his life out on the hard road, always trying to get everything the easy way, and failing at every turn. He tried to turn to theivery to make his money, but on Planet Earth, there was no chance. The Police were brutal, out in force and taking no prisoners. He was constantly shot at, swung at, and generally beat down to a pulp. Soon he heard about a small organization of elite fighters that were currently in battle on Mars with Earth forces. Sick and tired of the way he was living, he ditched his parents on Earth. He figured that they cared nothing about him anyway. He infiltrated a small Cruiser on it's way to Mars and slept in the holds of the ship. Soon after it's landing he found his way through the ship. He stole an EnviroSuit and made his way onto the Mars surface. He had a tough time getting across to the other side, they were shooting at him like he was the only target at target practice, but he was somewhat agile, and continually flipped and landed on his feet, until he was in hearing range.

"Hey stop shooting at me you idiots! I want to join you." Kaiyne shouted.

"Yeah right. Get 'em boys. Fry him like a goose on Thanksgiving." one of the Mercenaries replied.

He ducked into one of the sand dunes. His head slowly rised to spot the M.E.R.C. barracks. An explosion erupted behind him, and with his quick thinking he threw himself up in the air as if he was hit, and flew torwards the ground in a lump. After he recovered from his overly-dramatical feigned death, he crawled through the sand dunes unnoticed, and left for dead. He made his way to the M.E.R.C. barracks, through the sun-roof, and jumped down. His suit slapped hard on the metal floor as he jumped into a M.E.R.C. suit. He hid the Earth EnviroSuit in a drainage ditch that lead to the Mars core. He then picked up an AR-49 Energy Pulse Rifle and proceeded to the front lines..


M.E.R.C. won that day, but Kaiyne drew a little too much attention to himself with his sniping skills. At the lineup at the barracks they noticed he was the only one without a cot to sleep on. The general approached him with a sullen look on his face.

"What's your name son? You're not with this company." the general asked.

"Well.. I'm an Earth Colony defector. The one you guys were shooting at when I was running across the field. My name is Emile "Kaiyne" Dellamora." he muttered.

"Well I'll be hot and dandy, I should kill you right now. I don't know what you are, you could be a spy." the general barked.

"Give me a chance, I can prove myself. I seriously do not want to stay on Earth Colony." Kaiyne pleaded.

"Fine.. I'll give you one shot. Get some rest now." The general hit a few buttons on his ComWrist and a cot fell out the ceiling into an empty spot. "I've got a job for you in the morning. That goes for the rest of you too. Get some rest."

That night Kaiyne rested peacefully, he had a monumental task on his hands for tomorrow.
