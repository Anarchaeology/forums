We aren't capitalists here...
Rubi-Ka's economy isn't a capitalist one. In a capitalist economy, a person or small group provides the money (capital) to form a business. This money pays for production facilities, raw materials, and worker wages. The workers take the raw materials and use the production facilities to create a product, which is then sold at a price-per-unit calculated to be above what it cost to produce. The difference between the production cost and the sale price (the profit) is kept by the capitalist. The capitalist system effectively does not exist on Rubi-Ka, where the vast majority of items are found (ie, have zero production cost) rather than built.

What Rubi-Ka does have is a free market economy, where there is no governing body enforcing rules on buying or selling. The only rules of Rubi-Ka's economy are the basic rules of the marketplace: supply, demand, psychology, and inflation.

As I said, there's a free market, and of course people are motivated by profit. But there is no private ownership of the production and distribution of loots.

Edit - To clarify what I mean, let's take two examples - carbonum armor and the X-3 rifle.

The X-3 is a boss loot, and a good one. There's strong demand for it from several professions. But if you don't want to buy one from someone else, say if the price is too high, you can just run team missions until you find one. And eventually you will find one. Team missions that anyone can do are the means of production for X-3s.

Carbonum armor is produced through a trade skill process that requires tools, skills, and raw materials. If you want to make carbonum armor, you either need to get the tools and skills, or you need to pay someone who already has them to do it. So in this case the means of production (MRR - shape hard armor) is owned by players, not everyone can go out and do it.

Heavy Capitalism?
quote:
I would like to hear peoples view on the heavy capitalism which plagues our beutiful world of Rubi-Ka.
I agree with your points on greed, and how people put money in front of the lives of their friends. Yes, a life is not worth much, even though the insurance technology is only 99 % safe, but still, death brings pain.

Though I cannot agree with you that Rubi-Ka is a capitalist regime (if it is that you are trying to pass on). At least not as capitalism was known as back on Earth 30k years ago. Look around you. You have the Bronto Burger, and you have Yummie (the lesser known producer of Malle-Mix). But that's all. There is nothing being produced, all weapons and equipment have been imported by OT or Sol. Omni-Tek is not making money on a market on Rubi-Ka, but on a product they retrieve. If the Notum supply ran dry, OT would most likely leave the planet, even though there would be a market for other OT products here.

Therefore, RK is not alone a capitalist machinery, but rather a small gear in a big one. But the 1% of the population fighting versus capitalism, not in alliance with it, are not fighting in vain. Hurt OT, and you will hurt their power in the rest of the Galaxy. Though we would then have to fight the other powers, like Sol too.

The whole situation very much reminds about how situation used to be back on Earth. How the big, Western companies (Omni-Tek) came to the East (Rubi-Ka), built their machinery (terraforming) necessary for industry (Notum refining) and enslaved the population. Where in the rest of the world (Galaxy), work was being paid and workers having a certain degree of welfare, there in the East (Rubi-Ka), wages were low, accidents (cave-ins, creature and borg attacks) frequent, and all Trade Union (Clan) attempts were punished with death. They were doing nothing good to the countries except adding their means of industrial technology and corruption (mutants and monsters). All this just to deliver cheap luxuries to a spoiled Western population. Notice the similarities.

I joined the Clans to fight the capitalism, but soon learned that they were just as bad. There is no ideology visible on Rubi-Ka, just a dictature and a republic (which only differ from each other in the ways that authorities are being elected) clashing together in the efforts for Profit.
