Some random thoughts.

For most players (or at least most posters here), it seems the mechanics of combat are fast, high damage. They get the best armor they can, and may take evades to a level where they don't get critted too often, but the defensive abilities are always seen as secondary to the offensive abilities. And given the orientation of many professions (mostly offensive, defense is IP costly) and the nature of PvM combat as you get closer and closer to 200 (where battles don't last more than 5-8 hits per group member), it is hard to fault them. Removing the init penalties from the scopes significantly alters this. The weapons that were good prior to the change (fast, low damage, high crit) are no longer viable because the slow weapons are now just as fast and do more damage on regular and critical hits. The ability to reduce the aggro bar, while nice, is secondary to the ability to dish out damage. And in order to dish out damage under the new rules, a new weapon has to be used - understandably upsetting those players who were optimized for the old rules.

My class (MA) has green defensive skills. I have them all maxed. I use a lower level VE (2%, -77 inits) so I can keep the aggro bar at a reasonable level (55-60%). A significant number of attacks made against me do not hit because of this. But I only play in the PvM world, so even for me it might be better to go the usual route of high crit, full aggro bar (under the old rules). Under the new rules (no init penalties), if I could obtain an LLTS it would clearly be better than any VE I could get my hands on. I would be worried about the effects this would have on PvP - being able to crit as much as the next fellow (LLTS vs. VE) but him not being able to hit me due to my high evades and lowered aggro bar. If you do go down this route, please make certain that the "new LLTS" is not making things worse in PvP. You've seen the reaction to the current LLTS nerf announcement, imagine having to nerf the nerf.

Question: what is the motivation behind the possible ranged init bonus (compared to the removal of melee/physical init penalties)? The current LLTS treats all three of those equally; the VE does as well (and has a small nano init penalty for most QLs). Is the proposed bonus to ranged init due to: 1.the name of the device being used (scope), 2.a perceived superiority of melee damage over ranged damage, or 3.another reason altogether?
