See you in SW:G
This is me quitting. no diatribe against funcom, no moaning, whining about class balance, no accusations of exploits, no unfair treatment by GMs.

Im bored. Im level 98, and i have done everything in this game. i havent logged on in weeks, the last mission i did was about a month ago. I leveled slowly, hoping to keep the interest there, but it just delayed the inevitable, a year after buying this game, im leaving it.

Reasons? well, apart from the boredom, loosing my best ingame friend to "RL" and the degradation of the community that we had into what everyone else is, jealous and unfriendly.

Thanks go to Cz, cosmik, dai, all the devs and mods for doing a hard job, the fixers on rk2 you guys are the best, fixers everywhere, my ingame friends , funcom for trying to push the boundaries (even if you did screw up ) and my guild, Pandoras Wrath, the best people you could ever have the good luck to meet.

Ill avoid saying individual goodbyes as much as possible, but i will be back later to say them to each and everyone of my friends personally.

Good luck out there, and ill see you all in SWG.
