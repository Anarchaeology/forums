I think the whole point of removing the caps would be so that we could make unbalanced charcaters.
Sure, you'd have that super-nuke. But, you'd have no humidity extractor, no nanopool, no armor, and no health. And, you'd be using crappy nanokits since you didn't raise your T/S. And think of all the time wasted sitting around waiting for health since you never raised treatment.
At least it would allow people to make characters that were different from each other. You could make "Specialists"....
But, those specialists would be unable to do things that the balanced characters could. Sure, you can cast "Super DD-NANO #6", but I can cast "Super DD Nano#2" and "Nullity Sphere", and I can afford to cast them a few times, as opposed to your nuke thats eats your entire measly nanopool.
We would all end up close to the same in the end, but the first 100 levels or so would be a lot more interesting. Any class could be a badass in some way, but those badasses would also have weaknesses.
As for pet classes, They have to raise a lot of skills to get there, so I don't see that as much of a problem. The only major change required here would be making mobs have a decent chance of attacking the caster instead of the pet (i.e. no pets gaining XP for the caster while 100m away)

I'm all for the idea. Sounds like fun!

I think the caps are a necessary evil, especially in a skill based system with harsh repurcussions for those who lack the foersight to plan a balanced character.

Lets face it, this game was designed for mass consumption. The average player doesn't take the time to research what skills are required to make an effective character. Hell, it took me 2-3 beta characters and 50+ levels in final to get a solid grasp on the skill system and the relational aspects of attributes to skills....and I a veteran to RPGs and MMORPGs.


Sure, a comletely specialised character might be fun to experiment with on an environment like the test server.
But if players were allowed ultimate freedom with IPs, you would see a lot a characters that are totally ineffective. To be hoenst, I would not want to group with someone who is a liability to my group.

On the flipside, the min/max powergamers would come up with the ultimate pokemon character in no time.....and people would model their characters after this uber template and there would be even less variety than we currently see in game (ie: Nanomage NT in Nano armor wielding a pillow, Atrox Soldier in Elite with a Novaflow, Opifex Fixer in Obtru with a Mausser)
