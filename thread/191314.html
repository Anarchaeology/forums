Return of a Criminal
It was a sunny afternoon when Serter recieved a message from a man long forgotten. "Lehx".... He was jailed by Serter and Daetona a few months back for the bombing of Omni-Entertainment Platform during a rally by the infamous "Kithrak". They engaged in conversation for a brief period, explaining what they both had been up to the whole time "Lehx" was in jail.

"You've missed alot Lehx... I punished myself for not being able to kill you the first time we met. But it will all change soon. You are why 'Daetona' is dead, and I will get my revenge."

Lehx responded, completely ignoring "Serter's" last comment.

"I hear your Family had made itself known. Well its time I got my revenge. Consider all Kenleys dead. And as my motto is, 'Women and children first'. Watch 'Joanofark' closely Serter. You will die, Serter, but not before your whole family."

*static*
_________________________________________________

*Later that night*

"I am a good friend of Serter's and I need you to meet me in ICC alone. A family member of yours is in grave danger.", Lehx said to "Joanofark" via comm.

"Joanofark" immediatly told "Serter" of this, and "Serter" and the recently returned "Aoick" proceeded to ICC to confront "Lehx". Earlier that day "Aoick" and "Serter" were nearly killed by "Lehx" outside of Reets. This was the perfect time for revenge.
Upon arriving at ICC, "Lehx" was standing in the entrance way to Tir. "Lehx" flashed a purple bullet, that of Soul-Blind. "Serter" immediatly teleported to the safety of Omni-1, but left Aoick behind. A few shots could be heard over "Serter's" comm, then silence. "Serter" rushed back to ICC only to find it as empty as space itself. "Serter" then recieved a message from a member of the "~ Society of Salvation ~" informing him that "Aoick" was at Rompas. When he arrived, Aoick was on the ground lying in his blood. "Joanofark" and "Serter" put him on the couch and let him know what had happend.

Aoick interuppted abrubtly, "Kill me. There's no saving me here. Just end it now."

"Serter" loaded an arrow onto his bow and was hesistant to release. After promising he will get Aoick back, he let go and the arrow pierced "Aoick's" head cleanly and quicky. Within a few moments he was dead....

*beep* "Serter" grabbed his comm and answered in an angry voice.

"What?"

"Sir, Eternalforce has just been found dead. He was killed with Soul-Blind and people have reported sightings of Triple D."
