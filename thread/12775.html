Important Adv consideration needed
Adv needs: (btw, im a lvl 140+ adv that duel weilds rider execs and has played since release but stopped playing my adv way back due to their lacking, im also one of the first rookie adv pvps that was in the game eheh)

1 ) Better 1 hand slash weapons( True 1 hand slash weapons not requiring heavy weapons=dark blue). Pistols and 2 hand slash need a boost as well.

2) Increase the power of duel weilding weapons that do fast but lower damage. As it stands if you use a q200 merchant executioner you hit fast but for a ton of 5, 15, 30 damage hits vs anyone with decent armor ( so then u take more damage from reflect/damage shields then u inflict on your opponent). ***Adventurer X hits for 20 points of melee damage***
***Enemy Y's damage /reflect shields hits you for 80 points of damage***

3) Also, I have mult weild maxed, speed maxed and yet I dont hit that much more often then someone with just 1 slower weapon, namely a hardcore AR or rifle which does great damage. As it stands if u duel weild you basically get one weapon u can wrangler on ( which still is sub parred vs other weapon platforms) and one weapon (offhand) that is far lower Q due to your offhand pops off if you die and are saved in another zone( zoning checks the requirements on your weapon and with rez effects your offhand de-equips). So basically you have pistol/slash users with already subpar weapons being further gimped. Change it so the game doesnt check your weapon skills when u die; therefore, you could wrangler on both weapons ( all other non-duel weilds would raise holy hell if they had to re-equip their weapon after dying).

4) Increase the absorb factor on damage shields, they absorb such a small that it is nearly useless. They absorb factor is only a 1 time issue anyway ( first time u take that specific type of damage and absorb it one time its gone) that upping the absorb factor wouldnt be soooo unbalanced.

5) Ok..... Change Sabertooth form to +100 run speed and not -100 run ..... I mean seriously, how many slow cats has the world ever known? heheh. Cheetahs are not known for their snail like speed and leopards for their gimped speed and agility. Vice versa, I think you should make sabertooth the top pvp form ( I mean come on, who will seriously pvp in dragon form as everyone knuckle head on the opposing force will shoot the big lizard first eheh . Add the run speed, maybe up the crit chance, add some agi ( just makes sense), and work around other ideas u got running in your heads. I also think morphed forms should be more resistant to root type nanos = Big phat cat would be harder to root then lil ol' 2 legged Johnny Kill Em soldier whom is running terminator style. Also, add a morph special atk = Maul which does X- Y amount of substantial damage( like a brawl ) and would also have a very high chance of disrupting nano casting.

6) Skill colors for Advs completely got worked over bad on release. I know beta is only beta but at least in beta it made sense that advs bow skill color was green and not light blue. I mean, the profession title is "Hunter" for one of the lower level titles and yet training the simplest weapon is hard? ehhe Then, bump the power of bows and put a bow buff in the game. The adv should be the jack of all trades but with the color of his skills his the jack of naught .

7) Need an update to free movement. Its the only true run buff we get and we get it early on. Put like a q130 ish run nano that ups run like 200 or such.

8) Possible put a nano type in the game that would summon wildlife to come to your add. = the Q120 version would summon a Black death cat or a wood wolf. Have this lock a nano skill so that you could only do it 1 time in X amount of mins or put some other sort of negative factor on this to balance it.

9) Melee special atks are substantially less powerful then range specials. The only good special atk for melees is dimach which is dark blue for most professions, Advs for one so that training it takes a take of ips. The ip investment would be worth it but the skill is only availible every 30mins and has a good chance of not working at all ( Cant tell you the number of times I hit dimach and nothing happens). Now that specials are capped at 40% u should increase the dimach timer recycle ( this would help MAs too)

10) Originally the partial deflection shields stated they would reflect partial amount of damage back on to the enemy and part of it off completely ( paraphrased), but all it did was add to your melee/prot AC for a short short time. I think if the parry shields actually did the originally reflect factor that would help with the times your rooted ( you can only use them every 5mins or so). Also, both the shields and the fast atk based inititive items have a very high chance of bugging the user = Hit the shield and when it runs its cycle your ac is lower then when you started. Same with the fast atk which raises your melee init but when it runs its timer out your speed gets gimped till you zone.

11) Work on the "Please wait till previous action is completed" deal when your fighting in pvp and your target runs out of range. Cant tell you how many times I fought someone in pvp to have that person run away (but not zone) so that I cant atk anyone else till I zone or relog ( which isnt good in pvp battles)

12) IP reset, the game is in need of a one time only ip reset to offset the crucial game changes that have morphed it and therefore gimped many of your trueist fans ( those crazy ppl like me that bought release version . Advs are one of those profs that got reamed bad ip wise and I think it would be good business sense nonetheless to appease your true gamer base. Making hte ip reset optionally and requirement taking off all your armor/implaints etc is the way to go and I dont see any problems unless somehow its too taxing to carry it out. If so plz say so as whether you do an ip reset will weight heavy on my staying when the next biggest game comes out soon.

(Extra) Just an extra thought but for kicks if you could put another morph in game that would rock= Like Hammer breeder form, Vulture form, Bilebreeder form, whatever. Just a thought
