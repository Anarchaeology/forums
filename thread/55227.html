Promotion
Stepping outside of The Cup into the dry sandy streets of West Athen, Orion Lupoh Hunter stood still thinking of the recent conversation inside the bar. Firstly, the praise he received from his superiors on his devotion to the guild and how his business had thrived over the past few months. His business was running smoothly, consistent orders had come in for fresh supplies to the surrounding ICC planets and from that of the customers he had solicited on this world. The point of the conversation, the soul reason he had been called though was still fresh in his mind. The words will you accept? rang between his ears over and over again. Orion Lupoh Hunter received his long waited promotion inside the Mockers to that of a Boss.

He grinned as he watched the many warmongering clansmen dash by him charging to the grid, probably running off to some ungodly battle. Thinking of how such things will only lead them to be under the oppression of another person, and another after that. Lupoh raised his hand and brushed off the sand from his shoulder feeling his chemical mausser rub against his chest beneath his trench coat. Suddenly hearing a chirp from his comm link Lupoh turned up his wrist to see a recent message left for him.

[99291shadow]: Mr. Hunter, we are checking in on you. We are not pleased. Intern Ops has been interfering with our recent transactions again. This is your final notice.
[99291shadow]: Deal with the agent.
