Rather than nerf...14.3
Can you stop nerfing please? And start adding MORE to the game to achieve balance INSTEAD of removing, altering, restricting, etc.?

Our interest in the game is in the finding of new items and playing for better pets, as well as taking different mission styles (sneaking viable maybe? not having to kill stuff for exp viable).

If you don't have the imagination to make MORE for the game, and finish implementing the tradeskills as a viable means for leveling/developing a character, than admit it and hire people who do.

14.2 demonstrates that you've settled on a very static form of gameplay for AO. One where you have to kill mobs to level (Gaute admitted that tradeskills and sneaking are too easily exploited in his view), that you'll only be allowed to equip so much (OE, so don't bother trying to do more damage than you SHOULD be allowed), that you'll only be forced to team to find items above your level (mad aggro, invincible mobs with unreal hitpoints and skills). In short, a simple 30 level game that plays more like quake (on highest difficulty setting) every patch.

FC: please finish what you are doing here with 14.2. Then take a big step back and look at what you are PUSHING so hard to complete. It's a short-sighted version of the game. AO has so much more promise than SWG, EQ, WOW, Neocron.

Please don't kill the game. Please don't kill professions. Please add stuff to the game to achieve balance. Please don't remove stuff or nerf stuff because it's easier. You've been doing that last part for a while now. It's the easy quick fix that loses you a few subscribers each time, and doesn't gain anything in the long run.

For people who have gone through developing characters for FAs, and then shotguns with crit-scopes, this has been a repeatedly punitive process.

ST00PID FUNCOM...BE MORE FUNNY...be more creative.
