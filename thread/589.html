I'm level 91 and I use my portable surgery clinic regularly for switching out my implants. Perhaps this isn't the thing that people who want to be UBER prefer to do, but it adds considerable versatility to a character. Its also much more affordable to buy the implants that are at a level that you could implant yourself with your own treatment skill than it is to buy the much higher level ones that you can't.

Some of the ways that hot-swapping of implants can help your character:

You can drive a faster car when you have to go to one of those really far off missions.

You can do nano programming for people out in the field and not be hassled with having to schedule appointments for people to make implants and not have to make your character completely useless offensively when doing so.

You can use those one or two grid exits that require 400+ comp lit without having to blow another 100k IPs.

You can shift from a mode more suited to healing to one more suited to doing damage in the event that your group already has a good healer, or vice versa.

You can buff yourself and your group with better buffs using nano skill implants then revert to combat speed and skill increasing implants to do the real fighting.

You can put in implants to allow you to cast your teleport spells which you only need to use occasionally even if the nano skills are different from the ones you need to fight.
