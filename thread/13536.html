A Worthless Planet
My name is Imbersin. I was placed on this planet by doctors who played god. I am a geneticly formed being, a Homo Atrox.

I lived my first twenty years in the mines a happy atrox. I was smaller than most for some reason, slim and short. But my strength was no lower than anyone elses. By the age of twelve I found my comrades almost useless for discussion. They had no grasp on the world outside our little shaft.

Then, when I turned thirteen, he came. He was skinny, sly, and badly treated by the guards. He was what I would later know to be a Homo Opifex. This one was a criminal.

I never learned his name. But he tryed talking to many of the atrox, none could understand a third of the words he said. But I did, he was intelegent. Eventualy he discovered my strange talent and in turn his only friend. He told me of the war, he told me of the Omni Corporation and the Rebel clans. "They are both weak" He said over and over again. It would be a long time until I discovered what he meant. He told me how and why my kind were created. He told me how his kind were too.

We were a work-force, we were created to surve the Solitus. "The ones who created people like us were cruel, evil, sick, disturbed, briliant people." I later shared his views. He tought me not to make bias opinions, that for every wrong that someone could do, there were just as many right that they could do. Vice Versa as well. He told me that he was known as a side-switcher. Making money off of backstabbing each side.

"Every war has them." He would say. He told me how he was born into this mess, how his parents were over-righteous bimbos. He told me of the battles they fought against omni. How they gave their lives for a cause that didn't even make since.

But what he told me, that realy changed things, was how little this planet mattered. He told me just how many planets were colonised. That in all reality, the universe was considered at peace. He told me that there were weapons that could destroy an entire planet. He told me that omni-tech had access to weapons that could wipe every clan and neutral outpost on the planet out.

He told me of so many things. The government was what interested me the most. No not the clan government, not the omni government, just 'the government'. They had weapons that could supernova a star, armour that could take the blast of a nuke and go unstained. That my friend, he said, that is power.

I learned that omni would not destroy clans with bombs, because they were scared of the media, thus omni was weak. The clans could destroy omni if they would stop holding certain groups (The Dust Brigade) back. Thus they were weak.

When I turned fifteen, he told me of the plan he was making to break out of the mine. But the guards knew that the hate for them was growing. So one night they killed fourty atrox. Lined them up and shot them in the back, like cowards. The atrox never knew what was coming. I was more angry than I had ever been. I was ready to jump at the guards, but my friend held me back. "The only way to get ahead in this world is to control yourself." These were the words that decided my fate. The only way for someone like me to get ahead, was to control myself, to control my anger, to be a meta-physicist.

I started training, and the guards continued pushing. My anger grew. My powers grew. Everytime a guard would hit one of the miners with a whip, my power would surge. My friend one night, when I turned twenty told me that his plan was complete. He told me we would do it tonight. He told me that if anything bad happened to just keep running. I nodded, we would escape that night, dead or alive.

We ran out the door and he grapped a guard by the shoulder pulling him against the wall. The other guard standing watch started shooting, cutting straight threw the first guard's armour, and killing him. My friend then took the gun off the dead guard and shot down the other one. "RUN!" He shouted at me. I did run, and many guards poured out of the mine only to meet a blaze of bullets from my friend. "DIE! OMNI SCUM! DIE!" He screamed, and for once in all of the filth's history, it listened. One after another died to my friend's gun. They could not hit him due to his speed. But he hit them, one after another they dropped.

But then, out of the second floor room of the mine, where only the overseer went came a bullet. BAM! Hitting my friend in the shoulder, with no armour, one bullet was dealy. His shoulder burst into an open gushing wound. His arm barely attached to his body any more, he dropped the gun. "Hurry, before he recovers", one of the idiot guards shouted.

I turned around, and saw the seen, I quickly pieced everything together and stared. The guards quickly pushed their feet againt him, pinning him to the ground. Then all ten of them fired, bursting all over his armourless body. My anger soared!

My brain began to hurt as I contained as much anger as a I could. I screamed! But with how little a fraction of my anger I contained, still an Anger Manifestation appeared. "I am ready to serve you master" It said. "KILL!" I screamed, and it obeyed. Flying quickly down to the guards below, it struck one, denting the guard's armour. "AHHH!" He screamed as the armour cut into his skin. But he died quickly. The manifestation rammed into another guard, knocking his head so hard that it snapped.

The guards were unsuspecting, and thus killed easily. When all the guards were gone I asked my pet to report "Anger Manifestation: Life 89% Location: 0,0 Time Remaining: 9 minutes...
Target: Everything

I nodded, it was a good pet. "Follow" I said, and it did so. It's power rested in my anger, and my anger was now infinite. They had killed my one light in the world, my mentor, my friend, and my brother. Not just omni would pay for this, they would all pay, all who fought against opifex, all who fought against atrox, and all who bring children to this worthless world.
