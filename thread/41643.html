Car size, unequip size bug...
Hello there Funcom!

Now, I know you've heard this before, but really, the current size of an equipped Kodaik or Yalmaha is ridiculous! I remember the old days when they were normal sized, like a real car. And now? I don't enjoy driving a shoe-sized toycar at all. If the size reduction was a 'solution' to the people crowding the mission booth and therefore preventing other players from clicking on it, then I have a better proposal.
Simply size the vehicles back to original, and make them 'float' 5-6 feet above ground ( fixed height ). This way we get back the original cool looking cars instead of the current toy ones and we can't block other people from accessing mission terminals. Just junk the 20% size reduction from the database for vehicles and set a minimum height for the Kodaiks and Yalmahas. It sounds simple, and I have faith in your abilities to implement it.
