Can we have this loot added?
I was reading the threads, and stumbled across and idea that has serious merit.

http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...&threadid=3635

creepycool posted that he'd like to find a winning lottery ticket worth 5 million credits (when sold to an NPC vendor).

I like this idea. 5 million is a bit much (except for those rare ones). How about making a lottery ticket loot item. On average, you would get something 2x the cash reward on a full-bad full-money mission of that quality and distance.

These tickets shouldn't be that common, but that is still one of the coolest loot ideas I've heard in a long time.

creppycool was being a bit bitter when he posted that, but regardless, it's a good idea (I hate finding crap in chests)
