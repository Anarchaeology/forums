Nanomage breed is only the best breed for a particular profession if and only if that class has the ability to convert Nano Pool into Hitpoints in some way. Unfortunately, this does not include the two stereo-typical classes you would think a Nanomage Breed would want to excel in: NT and MP.

This Nano Pool to Hit Point conversion of healing and certain HOT nanos is why we have the skewed dynamics of Nanomage actually being better than Atrox for classes like Enforcer...and Atrox being better than Nanomage for classes like NT and MP who can't heal themselves via a direct Nano Pool to Hit Point conversion (the MP heal pet heals the Atrox MP no better or worse than it heals the Nanomage MP, and the Atrox has a ~1600 Hit Point advantage right off the bat).

This will be the case when 14.6 hits live. The Breed balance will favor Nanomage if you're a healing class of some kind. The Breed balance will favor Atrox if you can not.

Ultimately when you boil it down, the breed balance of patch 14.6 is a balance between Healers and Non-Healers. If you argue to keep the status quo, then Funcom needs to re-write their official strategy guide so in their little Breed/Profession table, Nanomage reads "Poor" in all the Non-Healing professions; regardless if its a caster class or not.

This is a very very far cry from striking a balance between Casters/Non-casters and Weapon Specialists/Non-Weapon-users which arguably Breed is supposed to do; where Atrox is supposed to sit on one extreme, Nanomages on the other, and Solitus and Opifex in between.

Yeah, you are right, Pumpkin, there are Profession restrictions on weapons, but there also level reqs on Nano programs.

The way I see it, the reason we have these level reqs and class restrictions is *because* there is no appreciable difference between the Breeds. Class restrictions and level reqs are going into the game BECAUSE of marginal differences between the Classes and Breeds.

This means that Mr. Nanomage Doc can use all the same weapons as Mr. Solitus Doc and STILL have better nano skills and nano pool.

That's what marginal differences get you. This is *why* Funcom is now introducing SELF-ONLY buffs into the game, because they DON'T want the game to be about marginal differences; where Mr. Nanomage NT can use similar weapons to Mr. Atrox Soldier.

If Mr./Ms. Nanomage Doc had to take an appreciable weapon skill *penalty* in exchange for better nano skills, only then would there be a substantial *non-roleplaying* reason to play a Solitus or Opifex Doc. Breed becomes a way to specialize yourself into different playstyles. You increase your weapon offensive ability by being non-Nanomage.

Look at this way. Enhanced/Penalized Dribble Down gives you a reason to be Solitus.

14.5 or earlier, Solitus is king. Why? Because of marginal differences. Solitus pretty much had the benefits of being both Atrox and Nanomage because neither of the latter breeds had more than a marginal difference in hit points or Nanopool.

14.6 Solitus is gimp. Nanomage Doc is better, uses all the same weapons with better nano skills combined with better nano pool.

14.6 Solitus Soldier is sub-standard. Atrox Soldier has 650-700 more hit points under a TMS, which equals ~3000 more hit points in a long fight. I know Atrox Soldiers who can cast all the same nanos that a Solitus can.

14.6 Solitus MP is sub-standard to Atrox. Same reason.

14.6 Solitus NT is sub-standard to Atrox. Same reason.

14.6 Solitus ADV is sub-standard to Nano. Better healing abilities and nano skills, again with no appreciable sacrifice in weapon skills.

14.6 Solitus Enf is sub-standard to Nano. Better self-healing abilities and nano skills, again with no appreciable trade-off in weapon skills.

Under 14.6, Solitus is just some breed thats sub-standard to a Healer/Non-Healer specialist Breed. With enhanced/penalized dribble down, Breeds would have to pay for their advantages with penalties.

Solitus would actually sit in a comfortable and moderate balance of nano skills and offensive weapon abilities.

Pumpkin, you insist on seeing cookie cutters, but I honestly IMHO see MORE cookie cutters under 14.5 and 14.6 than I would if Breeds had substantial differences.

PS: And stop arguing against a strong nanomage MP or NT combo because its "cookie cutter." Nanomages are *supposed* to be good at those two classes, should be *at least* as good as any other breed in it.

So with that being said, let's go over the problems that may arise, and the counters to those arguments:

Problem: Nanos will now rock at professions like Soldier and Enforcer because they will get more nanoskills, and thus get their nanos sooner.

Counter: Nanos under 14.6 already get their nanos sooner and also have more nanopool. If dribble down were adjusted, nanos would have decreased weapon skills to counter the fact that they have increased nano skills. Since both Agility and Strength are dark blue to nanomages, and those are the bases for most weapons, nanomages would have a large decrease in weaponskill.

Problem: Nanos will suck because they can't use good weapons.
Counter: They would be able to use their nanos much sooner, hopefully balancing out the deficiency in weaponskills. Nanomages would be able to cast almost all their nanos without buffs and without special implant sets at high levels.

Problem: Atrox will suck at nanos, this will make them the worst choice of any breed.
Counter: First off, they would get an even bigger HP bonus because body dev is based on Stamina. Secondly, they would gain a huge boost to Stamina and Strength related Weapons. Full Auto, Burst, Heavy Weapons, and melee weapons would all get a very large bonus.

Problem: Solitus would be pointless because they have no bonuses.
Counter: Solitus also would have no weaknesses, making them precisely what they were meant to be: average.

Problem: the best weapons are all profession specific which means that unless you are an enforcer you don't benefit from better melee skills, and unless you are a soldier you don't benefit from better ranged weapon skills.
Counter: SOME of the best weapons are profession specific, but certainly not all. As already mentioned, there are quite a few weapons that are quite effective but not profession specific. Are you complaining that almost all nanos in the game are profession specific? Do you complain that an crat can cast DD nanos but can't cast the best DDs? Do you complain that a Trader can mezz but can't cast the best mezz nanos? Since almost every profession can raise their weaponskills high enough to use the best highest QL weapons, it actually makes sense to make some weapons that use similar skills but are specific to the professions that are meant ot be specialized in them. Under the dribble down system, an Atrox MP could use a QL 200 Support Beam with great effectiveness but could not use a Shining Black Staff of the Watchful Eye.

Problems I have not seen satisfactorily addressed:
Problem: Opifex would dominate since their only dark blue is Stamina. They would get a minor deduction in HP, but that is nothing compared to the gigantic boost in all Agility based weaponskills, as well as all evades and inits. One suggestion is to make another stat dark blue. Possibly Strength or Psychic.

Problem: The low caps for Int, Psych, and Sense would make Atrox too weak in nanoskills, too slow with Inits, and too poor in evades. A solution to this might be to raise the caps of those 3 skills by 40 or so and to make Sense blue instead of dark blue.
