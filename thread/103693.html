The Blue Moon Part 1
The Blue Moon

(Part 1)

On a clear night you can witness the splendor of Rubi-kas three moons. If your lucky you will see them all in their majesty hovering in the sky together unobstructed by the frequent storms. For some people the moons are just there, barely noticed. For others, the moons provide light to hunt by or take a nice evening stroll. Still others have romantic liaisons under the pale light of the three moons. For me, the moons are all that and yet, something more. It is something hard to explain. One moon in particular, the blue one, affects me more than the others. This blue moon without a name has been my friend, my enemy, but most often my solace. This is my story.

My name is Cathern Vixentrox Flowers. My friends usually just call me Vix or Vixie. I am the leader of the clan, Whispers Edge. I suppose I should explain why I am sharing this with you dear reader. Many people, friends, companions, and even a few enemies have often asked about my past. Typically I would direct these folks to a piece my stepsister wrote for, her story is not so different than my own. Still, it was told from her viewpoint, not mine. She also left out many things. Things that happened before her and I were thrust together. Things that happened afterward that didnt concern her. I decided to try my hand at this, autobiography, business that my sister started.

Any good autobiography has to begin with details from the earliest childhood. I was born some 31 years ago as of the time of this writing. I imagine I came into the world like most Opifex babies do. My parents were, well I am not certain of much regarding them. I can barely remember my mothers face. My first memories are of her face, and a blue disc in the sky above her. Of my father, I have no memories. Suffice to say that from an early age I was raised by people not my parents in one of the infamous Omni prison mines. You see, unlike many of the happy Omni children, I grew up with a life of despair, brutality, misery, hunger, and cold. Much later in life I learned that my parents had been executed, for what I know not.

Anyway another woman raised me. I must have been about four or five when my mother was taken form me. Even Omni isnt so heartless as to execute a child for the crimes of her parents so I was assigned to one of the other female prisoners by the name of Marta. I must have been a difficult little girl because I remember getting beatings a plenty from this surrogate mother. I dont remember all what I did to earn the beatings but it seems like I got an awful lot of them. Still, I learned many valuable lessons from Marta.

While Omni and rich Neutral children were living it up, Marta was teaching me how to steal food out of the guards mess hall. While they were learning about art and music, I was learning how sneak quietly and win sympathy form certain guards. When they were going to school, I was learning how to scrub floors and toilets in a dirty barracks. Marta managed to teach some basic education thing like reading, writing and math. Being just a child, not much was expected out of me at first. As I grew older, things changed. Marta was relocated and I was finally starting to work longer hours. I had a tent of my own now that Marta was gone

When I was about seven years old, I was assigned trash detail. I was given explicit instructions that the refuse was Omni property and that taking so much as a crust of bread would be considered stealing from the corporation. It was my job to empty the trash from the mine foremans house and from the barracks. This was the first time I got to see inside the foremans house and most of it was off limits to prisoners. I must have been gawking at all the finery, art, statues, ect. How do I know? I received a stern blow to the back of the head and a warning to stop gawking from the guard who was showing me where the bins were located. Never had I imagined that such pretty things existed.

As we were making our way around the house we entered the bedroom of the foremans daughter. The guard pointed to the trash bin in her bedroom and then pointed to a girl who must have been about ten. He said, This is Steffanie, the foremans daughter. Give her the respect as if she were the foreman himself. Steffanie immediately started laughing and pointing at my rags that I called clothes. Then she said to the guard, Get it out of here; it smells bad. With that she spat on me and started laughing again. I was so ashamed. I knew that somehow she was better than I. I started crying and the guard escorted me out.

From that moment on Steffanie would seek me out no matter where I was in the mining complex. She would constantly taunt me, throw things at me, and tease me. I began to fear her less and hate her more. It was during this stint as a trash hauler that I got my first real toy. Steffanie was spoiled, and she would often throw perfectly good clothes and toys away. She would usually drop them in the trash when I was there to empty it. She would always remind me that the trash was Omni property and for me not to even THINK of stealing from the company that put food in my mouth. Food in my mouth! I thought about that for days. Why was I always hungry and needing to steal food out of the barracks? I thought to myself if I steal food why not steal one of her cast off dolls. So I did.

The doll was a beat up one. I remember it well though because it was MY doll nowor so I thought. This doll was my most precious treasure. Many weeks after I claimed the doll as my own, Steffanie must have seen me playing with it and reported me. A guard burst into my tent and yanked the doll away from me. He proceeded to beat me and admonish me for stealing Omni property. He then said to teach me a lesson he would make me burn my new friend and treasure into ash. I was in tears. My only friendmy only treasureto be burned by my own hand! He found some flammable liquid, doused it on my doll and gave me a lighter. Do it! he shouted. With tears streaming down my face I caught the sight of Steffanielaughing at me and sticking out her tongue. I whispered good-bye to my only friend and lit her on fire. I had made up my mind that I would get even one day with that horrible girl. That night, the blue moon shown brightly and I thought of my mother.

Time passed slowly after loosing my one and only real toy. I was consumed by hatred for Steffanie. In my mind I thought of hundreds of tortures and pains I wanted to inflict on her. I plotted her death hundreds of times over. Keep in mind I was only seven or eight years old at this time. Such thoughts at an early age cannot be considered healthy. Still, there was little I could really do to get back at her. Sometime during this period I got a new female to share my tent. She was a young woman by the name of Katelin. She didnt stay with me long and I barely remember much about her. The reason why I mention her is because she showed me something new and exciting! She had a long piece of string that she would wrap around her fingers and make intricate designs. She told me it was called Cats Cradle and it was best played with two people but you could do it with just one. She showed me the basics and helped me scrounge up a long sturdy string of my own. As I said, Katelin didnt stay long with me. Apparently she had some rich relatives who paid for her release. Once again I was alone in my tent, but now I had a wonderful new thing to occupy my free time with. A piece of string may not sound like much to you, but to me, it was everything.

Time continued its relentless march. I became on expert on survival. I knew which guards could be trusted to turn a blind eye to me stealing food out of the trash. I learned which were the meanest and most sadistic. I learned the complex layout in perfect detail. I knew the guards schedules and surmised how to use a rifle by watching the guards practice. I learned how to sneak and stay hidden. I had the mine tunnels mapped out in my head. Finally, I became an expert with my sturdy piece of string.

As the years passed, my plotting against Steffanie never ceased. I had thought up plan after plan to harm her, make her look stupid, yes, even to end her life. I never got the courage to actually follow through with any of my plans. Then one day, it was too late. Steffanie moved away to some boarding school in Omni-1 or so I heard. I had lost my chance at revenge. All those hours I spent plotting were for naught. I hated myself for not being brave enough to at least attempt one of my schemes.

The next major event in my life came when I was around 12 or so. Like most females, this age is a time of change. Hormones, growth spurts, and interests in boys affected me like any other girl that age. I got my first kiss from a boy who was slightly older than me by the name of Johnias. He came to the mines during the summer of the previous year. Like so many others, his entire family was incarcerated because of some silly offense. Anyway, we became pals. I suppose I would be considered a tomboy since I never had a chance to be anything but. Johnias and I got along well enough. I really liked him. One night he snuck into my tent. We played Cats Cradle for a while and chatted. Thats when it happened. We just looked into each others eyes, and then kissed. The feeling was electric. Once again the blue moon was shining brightly. That was to be our first and last kiss. The next day he and his family were missing from the complex. I found out they had been released. Im guessing that the kiss was his way of saying good-bye to me.

I was very upset at loosing Johnias but the biggest turning point in my young life was about to happen. I was sitting in my tent one evening, peering through a small rip in the flap. I was watching the blue moon, wondering what it would be like to walk on its surface. I heard a commotion. The guards came up to my tent, opened the flap, and dumped three people inside my home. It appeared to be a husband, wife and their daughter. One of the guards snarled at me, Here is your new family, enjoy them and teach them the rules! My first thought was that they had taken dead bodies and dumped them in my tent to frighten me. As it turns out they had been heavily sedated, not killed.

By the next morning they began to awaken from their drug induced sleep. They were very confused and seemed at a loss. I introduced myself to them and they to me. I found out Mr. And Mrs. Clancy and their daughter Shawnta had only just recently arrived to Rubi-ka. I explained to them where they were and began running down the list of rules. The Mr. Clancy became outraged. I pleaded with him to keep quiet lest the guards come investigate. He ignored my pleas and burst out of the tent shouting and cussing up a storm. After a few moments of shouting between him and the guards, a few shots rang out. Mrs. Clancy screamed and attempted to leave the tent as well. I grabbed on to her legs and held her down. Luckily, she hadnt fully recovered from the drugs and was still weak. I never saw Mr. Clancy again and assume he was perma-killed.

Through out the day I explained to them about life here in the mine complex. I explained that I had been here in the camp for as long as I could remember. That evening there was a frightful storm brewing. It seemed worse than the usual storms. Perhaps it is just my mind coloring the past. Suffice to say that evening one of the cruelest guards came to the tent and ordered Mrs. Clancy out. She refused to go till he said to Mrs. Clancy, You or your daughterchoose. Reluctantly she left. I explained to Shawnta that the mine foreman often requested attractive females to come work at his house in the evenings. I suspected what went on in there but I didnt tell Shawnta that, just that sometimes the women came back in tears or sometimes not at all.

Mrs. Clancy did return the next morning with gifts of actual food that had been given to her by the foreman. Mrs. Clancy continued to make the occasional evening visits to the foremans house. The food and rarely, a new piece of clothing was a godsend for me. I began to think of her as my mother and Shawnta as my sister. It was apparent that the feeling was mutual. It was strange for me to call this Solitus female mother at first. Time passed however and any discomfort ended for she truly acted like she was my birth mother. She loved Shawnta and I equally in my eyes.

Days became weeks, and weeks turned into months since the arrival of my adoptive mother and stepsister. We all had plenty of work to do. My mother typically operated some mining machine and while Viks and I often worked around the foremans house. ViksI forgot to mention that she chose Viksentrix as her nickname here on Rubi-kasomething just about every citizen has. People new to Rubi-ka sometimes find it odd that no one uses our real names except in the most private and personal settings for the most part. Perhaps this is something unique to Rubi-ka. I never bothered to study the customs on other planets. Anyway, I digress.

Viks and I being youngsters still only had a fraction of the workload of the adults. I taught her all there was to know about the camp. She in turn taught me about what life was like outside the camp. Having known nothing but the regimented and decrepit lifestyle here in the mines, I thought her tales were fabrications or at the very least gross exaggerations. I just couldnt comprehend having or doing the things she told me of. I must have been wide-eyed as she told me those stories. Sometimes I think she just did it so she could feel superior to me. Her stories did lead me to believe that I deserved more. Why is that I had to suffer under the heel of Omni-tek corporation? What had I done to deserve this treatment when it was obvious that most people didnt grow up in a tent with little sanitation or possessions? I wanted the things my sister had told me about and the things that Steffanie had. Someday I would have nice things and be respected and treated like a normal person. Someday.
