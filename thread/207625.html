Darn that rain..
The rain poured heavily down throughout the Omni-Tek area.. RK TV News report warned people in the Rome Green and Red area for a possible flood but that didn't affect this mans plan at all.. he was no where near Rome but in the damp Omni-Entertainment sewers. The sewers is where Rubi-Ka's so called "scum" hide out from the world, police, people and many other things. Even those people were in there huts away from the rain but one was out.. someone in a black cloak that blew with the harse wind and a large red sword shined with a red albrecht tank covering him.

He ran into a large sewer pipe but instead of taking shelter he mumbled words and all of a sudden a door opened up to reveal and pipe large enough for a small Atrox to slide down. The man jumped down the pipe and into a large lobby like area.. the man threw off the black cloak but his face was covered with a black helmet which he did not remove but his sword was hanging from his back by a few straps tied to his armor. He had a small frame behind the Tank Armour, one of an Opifexs most likely. The man rushed to a door and inputed a code and opened a semi-messy bedroom, there were some posters hung up and a messy bed. The man searched around the room probably looking for something.. he almost seemed like he was in panic. After an hour searching around he found a small disc with some Old Earth Label tape on it there was some words written on the tape.. "Bret "Serter" Kenley"

The man looked about and walked over to large Grid Vision Box used for Old Earth discs like those. The man inserted the disc and the screen crackled and a semi-fuzzy vision of a Solitus man appeared with a strange expression.. he lifted his head and spoke...
