Million Clan March (on RK2)
All clan members are encouraged to join in on a march around our homeland to protest Phillip Ross's recent dissolve of the Tir Accord. The walk is designed to show that we are strong in numbers, unwavering in our unity, and we WILL be heard. It will begin on Friday October 25th at 7pm Est (00 GMT). We will organize in the area of West Athens and march through West Athens to Old Athens and whompah to Tir where we will gather around the COT building to hear a few words from some select clan leaders. Afterward there will be a party at Reets with refreshments and dancing. Any clan orgs wishing to participate or clan leaders who would like to speak at the assembly please feel free to contact Myiria, Bettybam, or Katsue of Vox Populi for more information.
