Neutral Guards for a Neutral City
Reporter Athol

The controversy over faction oriented guards in Newland City continues as many neutral citizens of Rubi-Ka unite to discuss the Newland Guard Initiative (NGI) and the impact it will have on the future of Rubi-Ka.

In a meeting last night outside of the club Neuters R Us, neutral citizens gathered together to discuss this very issue. Many elite citizens of the neutral community were in attendance at this meeting. Pyromanche chaired this gathering and was seeking support for the Newland Guard Initiative (NGI). She explained to everyone there that they would have a chance to voice their opinion either pro or con. She stated the purpose behind NGI, and then opened the floor to discussion.

Jereziah explained that Ms Warr and the mercenaries were brought to town in the past to protect Newland from Clan sentinels. Nyadach mentioned that the thugs where there to protect neutral citizens and property, and that the mercenaries were not doing their job properly. He also added,  that if a neutral was being shot the thugs did nothing to protect him.

Nyadach stated quite plainly that the thugs were Omni aligned. Most players voiced their support for the NGI, but some wanted certain concessions, and many wanted to know where to lay the blame if their future plans failed. Dreamskape suggested that everyone who voted for NGL should take the blame, but that they could personally blame him if they needed to.

President Aazamon of the Nanomage Liberation Front (NLF) had a quieting effect on the meeting as he spoke. He stated that the neutral citizens were stronger now than when the mercenaries were brought in, and that they had ways to make the new guards stronger and more beneficial to the neutral community. He also added that he didnt care if citizens were neutral, backed by Clan or Omni, but Newland City was a city that should be run and guarded by neutrals. When he finished speaking, he received a tremendous round of applause as he quietly returned to his seat.

Pyromanche closed the meeting with a positive note by thanking everyone for attending. It appears that all present want the NGI to succeed. Auntbeast, a well-known citizen, said ominously only time will tell.

Thursdays Newland Guard Meeting
Greetings all, long time no rant ok, maybe a few days but it has been a long time since I last took out my notepad and publicly reported on the news But Im doing this as, wellwell I was there so why not hehe. And hi there Athol, hope I ain't steping on ya toes, but thought I might add a article of the same type, but a slightly different angle

A meeting to bring together Neutral leaders so they can discuss the NGI (Newland Guard Initiative) was brought to meeting by Pyromanche, the elected voice to the NGI for the Neutral Community. Many leaders couldnt attend but had sent a representative such as myself to the event, but everyone understood how important this will be to all Neutrals, that steps were made to restore a more neutral feel to the Neutral population, and to also provide friendly relations once again with the Clans.

A passing Clanner was unfortunately gunned down, right in front of the meeting to much the sadness of most of the delegates, but for the delegate from Elite Operations, those who paid for the Thugs in Newland who cheered the Clanners demise, to much the scorn of the others present. With majority of the Community having already experienced issues with the Thugs, of the lack of protection they provide, and how on some occasions they actually attack local citizens, and made it clear they had to go.

Beamboom of the company Circle G who backed the Initiative due to the incompetence and inability for the Thugs to use any form of intelligence when they are supposedly protecting Newland, offered the services of Circle G to be hired as the replacement guards, or assist in the training of the replacements. R.U.R. have also offered security droids to protect Newlands, but unfortunately werent at the meetings. And it might be an interesting bidding situation for Newland Council to look at with these two companies so strongly interested in the security of Newland.

The biggest concerns of the meeting came from Auntbeast of Divine Retribution who felt that neutrals didnt need outside funding sources to finance the Initiative, and accepting any, including donations was a sign of weakness. While others felt that accepting the assistance from all factions was a sign of strength, and to show camaraderie with people from all back grounds being able to assist each other for a better future. But this was just a minor point as currently funding is still to be calculated by the Newland Council, so no one can yet state how expensive this Initiative will be, let alone work out how to pay for it.

With everyone having their own unique views, yet everyone being shown that everyone believes that the Neutral Community needs to be protected and made secure. It was a great mornings work to show that the Community can come together. The NGI will no doubt be meeting again soon. But with the Community firmly behind removal of the Thugs the issue now firmly stands with the Newland Council. And with this many people wishing to see change, they have to listen and act.
