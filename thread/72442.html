Yalmahas need headlights
I don't know about you guys, but sometimes I find it impossible at night to find the door to missions if I'm in my Yalm. Its especailly a pain if the door is on the second or third floor. This happens in Home a lot, but its even worse in cities out in the middle of the jungle. Mission entrances tucked into a nitch in the side of a hill are another problem.

My suggestion then, is that Yalmahas need headlights that you can turn on, so you can actually see where you're going and what's there. For simplicity, you could have a headlight item that fits into the slot used by the floating lantern. When its equipped, the light is on, when its not, if off. Simple.
