Dark Empire


A new organization formed by a group of friends tired of the immature and mistreatment of members and hamper the fun of the game. We are building up forces to protect the honors of Omnitek and drive the clan forces back.

Do you follow our values to join the forces of Dark Empire?

Act mature will still being able to enjoy the game?
A team member following rules and instructions?
Able to help out when you can meaning to leave that exp team when needed?
Treat others with respect?

If you do not hold these values important then there are plenty of orgs out there that may suit you better.
