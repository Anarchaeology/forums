To get to Reet's:

1. Get to Newland City (reachable via Grid)

2. Take whompa to Borealis

3. Step out, walk around to the left and go in the next whompa (Stret West)

4. Step out, head through arch on left and head up the street to the town centre

5. Reet's is the biggest building at the centre of East Last Ditch - head up the ramp and take the first door on right

6. The "Glass Floor" Bar is reached by running forwards until you reach a 'T' junction, turn left and follow your nose (or ears)

7.

8. Buy Greyman a beer for being so good with maps and stuff like that
