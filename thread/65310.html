NOTUM WARS! More like Class Balancing WAR!
HEy all!

Just reading the topic I think you know where i'm going here. I just want to know what funcom is planning todo with this booster pack? Anyone else feeling that class balancing should at least be looked at before moving on? I play a Crat. (suprised?) and i'm sure classes like mine (ie. engineers, mp's, Adv ect..) have to be wondering what the hell will our purpose be in the booster? Sheep? Something funny to shoot and watch fall down in one shot? I feel if I buy this pack I'll be killed faster then kenny in a South Park episode.

Let's face it I know there is no chance when agents can aim shot for 10k , Traders can debuff me with a nano program that will make me disabled, or MA's can just slaughter me without a doubt. Does this pack require me to change my class? Please dont use the fact that each ("special") or should I say HYbrid class can contribute with buffs. That does not count for playability. Mainly with pet classes I say what is the point when pets have no resistances at all.. root it, run, and shoot the guy that controls the pet, nuff said. Ok enough ranting hehe but I really wanna know what you guys think. This booster shows the demise of some classes and there is no word of any balancing. At least let our Damage nano's CRIT. I mean let some of these hybrid classes actually do Damage. Hope this doesnt come off as a flame I just want some comfort in knowing if anyone else feels this way or if there is another option of just making a new character that does damage.
