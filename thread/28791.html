There's no story anymore right now.

The ingame story started with some Pumpkinheads I never got the background on and I've never seen since.

We were treated to a small essay written as a dialogue between a father and son Cyborg duo who are working on some sort of super Cyborg and we got to see the hideout where they do their dirty work.

When last we visited the Story: Phillip Ross and Henry Radiman were negotiating, we were exposed to infiltrators high in Omni-Tek's ranks involved in some sort of cloning plot, Eleena Oak, a Council of Truth member, was wandering around up in Mort and was being tailed by another CoT member sent by Radiman.

Finally, we were given glimpses of an apparently powerful stranger who has been given orders by an even more powerful stranger (who we now know is a member of the Dust Brigade owing to his headgear) to do some dirty work.

The Dust Brigade blew up an apartment complex in Omni-1 and Ross and Radiman had to postpone their 'chess match'.

Then everything came to a screeching halt.

Players make things up a bit. A lot is inconsistent with the Official story, but some of it is actually pretty good. However, most of it is just a bit of fun the players are having to fill the void left by the lack of any plot movements. The majority involves Clanners launching terrorist attacks on Omni positions. Some makes a bit more sense. But, very little moves the plot.

There are FunCom run events here and there. But, swim contests and cocktail parties don't advance the story aside from the Dust Brigade reinforcing the fact that they are some sort of bad men. They are increasing in quality as of late as the Ark staff becomes more experienced and the less dedicated and talented drop out (my guess).

It's still next to impossible to get a statement out of a Story Character. Practically none of the movers and shakers in the storyline has been seen or heard in months.

A few E3 interviews indicate that a lot of the backstory will be fed into NPC's so that players can get more history about the planet. This is a much welcomed consideration.

The only thing we've heard nothing on is when the story will start moving again.
