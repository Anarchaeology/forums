Well, let me start by saying I'm a comic fan. I've been an active collector for the past 25 years of my life (yes I'm a geek and proud of it).

If the story follows any continuity of what is already known in the DC universe, Batman, will of course win, hands down.

This has been stated and determined by the creators many times. Reasons being:

Superman was born and raised with his powers. He grew up and learned his powers to the best of his ability throughout his life. At the same time, he is a slave to his powers, and is nothing without them.

Batman is the ULTIMATE in human perfection. Since his parents death, he has trained all his life to be the best and smartest. He IS the greatest detective, and one of the worlds best combatants. Everything he does also comes from a superior / honed intellect.

Also, as established a long time ago in the DC universe. Batman does have kryptonite to defeat Superman should the need ever arise. This was taken from a ring that Luthor had and Superman gave it to Batman for fear that one day...he "could" go bad.

The DC writers have shown time and time again that Batman could / can beat superman (most common Dark Knight Returns and DKR2). This as I said, made mainly possible by Batman outthinking superman.

The geek has spoken
