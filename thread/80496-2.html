As someone who came to AO from EQ I think there is one major difference between the games that might help account for players attitutes toward each other: EQ was engineered to make players dependent on each other. If you want to go somewhere, you can spend 30 or 40 minutes walking or taking a boat, or you can find a wizard or druid to insta-port you. There is literally no way for most classes to heal themselves above 50%. Soloing without twinking is almost impossible after about level 15. Come to think of it, those are all a big part of the reason I no longer play EQ, but you can see how this state of mutual dependence might incline people to play nicer with each other.

Having said all that, there are plenty of jerks in EQ too. I'm not convinced AO really has a worse community. I have been surprised to occassionally see something like someone asking in OOC where to find Commander Brock (a basic question most clanners should be able to answer) and not getting any replies, but I've seen worse things in EQ. I tend to agree with the theory that there is a certain percentage of the player base in both games which are somewhat socially-challenged.
