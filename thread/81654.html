Soldier revisited (quickfix)
Suggestion that wont make it:

--------
- Lower versions of OMHH (50-100 and 100-200).

- Another line of guns that is less crit reliant.
(something around the stats of cold lasers atleast, that aint dualwieldable, reqs around Div9, burst&fling)
that actually looks like a soldier weapon
(not something from legoland, not some wimpy pistol)

- Make TMS +50 reflectAC on top of regular rrfe.

- Nanoshutdown from TMS = TMS time +40 sec
--------

these are 3 simple suggestions, and would get rid of some problems there are:

- TMS can be cancelled by engies
- Low ql TMS cant be cast on top of rrfe
- Soldiers gotta kill lvl 250 mobs, or are stuck with a crit-reliant weapon(nova) which sucks in pvp.
- Lower level soldiers who got short TMS, can last longer with a low heal, and save TMS for when they really need it.

No I'm not saying soldiers are gimps, a lvl 200 soldier with TMS MK X, Aug. Hellspinner can't be referred to as a gimp..
However, most lvl 50 soldiers can.

.
I know atleast the +50 reflectAC has been up for discussion, but please discuss what this would do to the current soldier profession (overpowering, nerfing, etc)
With the reduction in nanoshutdown on lower end TMS, I think the reduced reflect would not mean much.
