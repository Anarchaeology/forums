What happened to Soloing?
The new camps are fun and all (if you like being killed in 5 shots by a purple sumo-wrestler with an alien fetus for a head and then never being able to leave without dying) but what happened to soloing? Where are the days of 'nah, I don't think I'll team right now, I'm going to go solo for XP'.

The fact is, not everyone wants to team all the time. I like people, but after dealing with at least 20 new people every single day that I want to get XP, I get more than a little burnt out. It's not just me either, sometimes I know start freaking out just because of the stress of having to cooperate with so many other people, just to be able to get anywhere with your character. Last time I checked, that kind of stress comes from work, not fun. I hear there's this other game called EQ that emphasizes this kind of 'work' to get ahead too.

I'm not saying soloing should be a breeze or anything, but how about some quests where we don't need an entire guild to complete them. How about a quest without any camping involved, just go up to one guy, talk to him, go to some other guy, say something, go talk to the first guy, and etc. until you talk to this one guy who gives you some item that makes it all worthwhile. Uhm, while we're off in the fantasy world of where this game -should- be, make this item something like the Batclava Helmet, something that's actually useful, something that can be upgraded to meet the level of the wearer, and something that's a bit more fashionable than a ski-mask. And most importantly, we need to get away from 'to get anything, something else must die'. I don't want to have to kill a mob, I don't want to have to raise some strange tradeskill that I would never normally use and has no relation to the item to a ridiculously high level, I just want my item and I'll pay a few million cred for it if I have to. They need to let some money out of high level game somehow anyway.

And for godsakes, FC, play a quest game before you make anymore quests. Final Fantasy, King's Quest, Space Quest, Leisure Suit Larry if you really have to, but at least learn how to make one before firing off on some insane new tangent that involves killing the Ub3R-D3Th-M0N$T3r-0f-ANn1H1LaT10n-W1Th-NuCL3Ar-PLa$Ma-BR3aTh-0f-D00m which only spawns once every week and takes 50-60 people level 190+ to kill. That's just my opinion though.
