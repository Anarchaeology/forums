Society of Salvation
The Society of Salvation should be added to your list. Previously known as the Masonic Templars, we reformed, loosing the action adepts in the process. Essentially the bar hangers, talkers and dancers are left, recruiting anew. We are around 30 active members.
Dont join us for tower attacks, duels and war in general, handle your own training by yourself and come have a beer with us in the rompa, there will always be a couch for mature people.

/ooc
I wish to differenciate different kinds of roleplay here, as I can find in the list orgs that can be called RP indeed, but are so very different from one to the other.
We have the action rp orgs, the social rp orgs, and a wide range between the two.

Action rp orgs will tend to organize pvp events, rp scenarii, special missions. Individuals are not necessarily deeply involved in the RP process. Yet they help develop an org history and can be close to the storyline.
Social rp orgs will be more individually based, enhancing conversations and parties to help develop char personalities. Far from the storyline, far from the war, they will be noticed in parties, social events and everywhere where the beer flows well.

I would classify the Society of Salvation as 90% social, 10% action.
