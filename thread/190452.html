Dust Brigade Strikes In Rome And Borealis
DUST BRIGADE STRIKES IN ROME AND BOREALIS
Investigative Reporter Legman

A peaceful afternoon was shattered by the explosions and fighting breaking out across Rubi-Ka. First in Rome and then shortly after in Boralis the infamous Dust Brigade carried out a series of attacks and one confirmed assassination.

Entering Rome via the Whompa in Rome Green, the Brigade lead by Xelona lead approximately six mutant bombs- genetically altered mutants that carried bombs inside them- to the most populated areas. Once in place, the mutants proceeded to self destruct inflicting harm to innocent bystanders in the area. Chanting Ashes to Ashes and Dust to Dust the mutants proceeded to explode in balls of fire, killing nearby citizens and damaging surrounding buildings. The squad leaders opened fire into the crowds spreading mayhem and destruction as they made their escape.

A few minutes later the Dust Brigade was spotted entering Borialis once more using the Whompas. The Brigade detonated several more mutants, however they did not open fire. Instead they sought out a fixer by the name of Itel. Xelona confronted Itel in front of scores of witnesses and said simply She wants you to stop before one of the mutant bombs grabbed Itel and exploded, killing him instantly. In the wake of the blast, members of the Brigade made good their escape.

When asked what happened, Rockjock stated, I heard murmurings about people wishing the Dust brigade would kill off some people. I didn't think it would happen.

Falikos, president of Shattered Dreams, said Any of you see any more of those people, give them a message for me. Tell them that they're mysterious 'She' they work for isn't going to get much happier, because we aren't doing anything worth killing people over.

Confusion and anger were all that remained in the aftermath of the attack.

No one knows how the Brigade managed to pull off these attacks without being stopped by the guards in Rome or Borealis, or how they managed to attack and kill anyone in a city while the suppression gas was functioning.

The assassination of Itel and the reasons behind it remain a mystery to even his closet friends. One thing is certain, the Dust Brigade has an agenda and only they know what it is.
