New to Omni-Tek, looking for department
Hello. My name is Akaran, a Martial Artist formerly serving under the Council of Truth.

Note the 'formerly', for I am no longer with them.

And when I realized I didn't know why, I asked myself "Then why fight them?"

The end result? I gathered up my possessions, left for Newland, and signed up with Omni-Tek.

I realize you may not trust me, and I do not fault you for that. I ask only that you allow me to prove myself...prove that I am no longer a threat to you.
