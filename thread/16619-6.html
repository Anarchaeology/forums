Some very good responses too. But one thing I've noticed is a deffinite tendancy (which has honestly always existed) to lean towards only 2 areas, damage and NCU usage.

Both are very valid issues.

In a big part though my concern is professional distinction. Why not explore some angles that follow different veins but still would be emminently useful.

One idea that was posed awhile ago was the ability to either surgically remove hostile nanos or completely clear a target NCU. I think both ideas are stellar and deserve some looking at. The clearing ability at least would be a massive boon to the profession.

How about adapting existant abilities that muck with HP and make them work with nano? A version of the trader health funnel that attacked nanopool instead could help in those long, drawn-out fights.

Or expanding what we already have: maybe a Warp T&S line that allowed us to set a location we wanted to go to instead of the faction-based 2 destinations? Maybe speeding them up a bit too, so that Warp lines are 1 min cast and Fold are 30 sec. It's a non-combat thing, but overall I do enjoy the flexibility the teleports give.

Or for even less direct ideas, a nano line that dramatically increases a target's chance to fumble would be a massive help.

Just a few one-shot ideas, but I'm trying to look at ways to improve the profession without making us a one-trick pony. What we really need to do is formulate solid input to FC based upon a general consensus of what we think this profession should be.

Ey, I LOVE the idea of clearing someone's NCU. To make it not overpowered tho, you could give it its own nano line. MKI, low level (40ish?) randomly clears one buff from X's NCU. MKII would clear two buffs, etc. This could make us viable in PvP.
