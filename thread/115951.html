Family Time
The night time air over Omni Entertainment was quiet, it was a saturday and yet everything was vastly still, and a cold air whisped over the omni-pol armor the grid guards wore causing a couple of them to shiver. They could be heard talking to one another through the bulky helmets. "I gotta get a new job..I stare at this grid terminal so long I'm starting to see it in my sleep for crying out loud" one of the guards sputtered, gripping the barrel of his rifle because it emitted a warm glow. "Stay silent private, talk whatever crap you want AFTER your shift is up." The stern commander interjected as he stomped his foot on the pavement to drive his comment home. Just then their scanners beeped loudly, they had an incoming unregistered citizen coming back into normal space time. The guards hussled in a flurry, training their guns appropriately and loading the chambers. "Keep your eyes peeled men, another filthy clanner trying to get into our city!" just as the commander bellowed his words, a shiny yalmaha fluctuated into reality, and as fast as it did, zoomed upwards into the Omni night sky. The guards tried in vain to stop the tresspasser, but the yalmaha raced out of firing distance very quickly. The guards all halted their fire and looked at each other disappointedly, then back to their commander. The commander rubbed the top of his helmet and whispered "umm..this doesn't go on the record ok?"


Lexus laughed to herself as she piloted her yalmaha through the clouds, too slow as always boys" After popping a few painkillers for her sometimes unbearable headaches, she opened a secure channel and uttered the words "i'm coming in momma, is your apartment secure?" just then, a rather rotund older woman appeared on the comm channel "Delana? you here already? damn girl..I almost fuhgot about ah littah dinnah, ah took an extra shift guarding the mines tonight, I'll be home soon sugah, just use muh old password to get in the house" Lexus smiled brightly at the screen and nodded "I'll go wait there then momma, see you soon" she clicked off her comm and continued her slow fight into the high rise area of Omni Entertainment, it was near the park, green and well kept, with this odd brat always shouting at people who passed by. lexus sat down near the entrance to the highrise and exited her yalmaha, she aimed her remote at it and it quickly imploded upon itself and became small enough for her to put in her purse. As she fiddled with her belongings, a man approached and stood just behind her. He was so close that Lexus could feel his sake soaked breath on the back of her neck. He stared for a moment at her long slender and well toned legs, forged from excercise and battle within the harsh deserts of the clan controlled deserts.


"mmnnn mnnn, you have it all where it counts....you need a job baby? they paying good over at the rompa bar and I have connections.." Lexus turned and raised a brow at the druken waste of humanity that stood before her. "clan girls make a killing here baby, they ain't as uptight as the omnis..and clan ho...I mean ladies..always need the money" Lexus chuckled and reached into her purse and pulled out a revolving cold laser and trained it at the man. "whoa whoa whoa, babycakes, I mean I didn't...that is umm..hey now wa..wait a minute" the man stuttered as he slowly backed away. Lexus slowly aimed for the mans kneecap "Well, you got one thing right, I CAN make a killing here. Lexus looked around to make sure no guards were passing by, and when she was satisfied that she was alone on the street, she smiled and released a round of heat draining energy from the man's leg. frostbite almost immediately set in and the man fell to the ground in agony, gripping his kneecap and shrieking wildly. lexus stared coldly at her writhing victim "Maybe when you go out pimping now, you'll have the walk right"

Lexus entered the apartment highrise and was greeted immediately by the obnoxious guide spewing insults and taunts. Lexus just laughed and continued her walk, she never really figured out why guides are there anyway, the all look the same, and possibly all have inferior programming..at least she thought they were robots anyway. She located the appropriate apartment entrance and entered the passcode before going in. The door swooshed open and immediately Lexus was greeted with the smell of warm food heating in the kitchen. The aroma of fresh buttermilk biscuits and red eye gravy, all the good foods that her mother regularly imported from earth. She closed her eyes and let her sense of smell float about the dwelling, it was better than anything she had on the clan side, where her diet mostly consisted of nutritional suppliment packets for her long campaignes on the battlefield. After regaining her senses, she continued to stroll about the apartment, she gazed at the pictures on the wall, the family that she once cared for so much. One photo caught her eye in particular, it was of her sister rouge. A spitting image of Lexus, only her hair was long and fair, and she had a tattoo on her face, a mark she carried to symbolize her martial arts school. She was the complete opposite of Lexus in every way, Lexus stood for fighting and hostility, but Rouge was caring and nurturing. lexus always seemed gruff and had a toughened exterior, but Rouge was gentle, and soft and admired beauty a great deal.

They were close, no doubt of that, they even had joined the same organization at one time, Whisper's edge, but after the fall of the CoA, Rouge joined the red tigers before vanishing completely, she had not heard a word from her since.As she traced a finger over her sister's last taken photo, the door swooshed open. Lexus quickly put the photo down and took on a look of idle innocence. Her mother stood in the doorway, husky but strong looking woman, her hair combed up into a huge afro and she wore a clean and pressed Omni uniform, in most circles she was known as Soulmomma, but at home she was simply Cathleen. "Delana!" she shouted as she ran to embrace her daughter tightly. "How ya been gal? still giving the corporation hell?" Lexus hugged back firmly, she knew in the back of her mind, with the reputation she had, she'd never feel another hug again. "Hi momma, good to see you too, sorry I haven't been around as much...busy, you know how that goes.." Cathleen laughed and slapped Lexus on the back "ahah yeah I know how that goes child..I watch the grid streams too, seems you always into it with somebody or another...that Fixerben character, haha in his wedding announcement?" Lexus laughed, that feed had been banned for some time, but thinking back on it did make her chuckle a bit. "You taught me always momma that people don't like hearing the truth. It hurts too much and it pinches nerves...i guess that's why the sentinels don't get the respect they deserve, they represent a truth no one wants to hear"

Cathleen scrunched her nose and twisted her lip "Come on child, ya ain't gonna turn this lil get together into a political debate is ya?" Lexus took a seat on the soft sofa and reclined, it was a long needed break, but she felt it all the more important to spin her tale. "You're the only one who understands me momma, I'm tired of trying to justify my actions for the clans, they don't want peace, they don't want anything for themselves. they don't feel comfortable unless they are debating against someone, or fighting needlessly, they feel an unnecessary struggle defines them as people" Cathleen sat next to her daughter and put her arm around her shoulder, it was obvious that Lexus was very tired, and it appeared that she hadn't been sleeping well either. Cathleen stared at the pictures on the wall, she silently reflected on her family as well before speaking. "Omni don't care much for the clans anymore, it seems they cause their own share of problem ta themselves honey, I know ya tired, and I watched for all those years as you fought for them, you and ya sistah, but they don't appreciate it none sugah, you know they don't. The real heroes are never honored while they living"

"Let's not talk about this though delana, there'll be plenty of time fer that latah..how's your personal life hun? that nice boy you used to see, umm Flareon?" Lexus scowled, and crossed her arms defensively "He was a jack off momma, only cared about hiself, think he went back to Omni-Tek anyway, I'm better off without him, but I told you a few months ago I broke up with him..or rather, he did with me" Cathleen stroked her chin "Oh yeah yah, you told me about that, you were seeing this pale gangly looking girl named Dixon..." Lexus quickly corrected her mother "Vixentrox momma..and we aren't seeing each other anymore either, she cared more about her guild more than me, and she wasn't very supportive of my goals either..." Cathleen grinned and looked down at her daughter "Well honey, when ya eat fish, you can't start crying that things are actually GETTING fishy" Lexus bucked up and sat erect on the chair "What's THAT supposed to mean?" Cathleen burst into laughter as she got up and walked into the kitchen area "Nuthing hun, nuthing, I'll get dinner on the table, ya look like a stick"

Lexus sat quietly at the table, she knew that as soon as she started talking, her mother would ask her a million questions she was just uncomfortable about answering. She instead focused on the rich bounty of food that was being placed before her. delectable buttermilk biscuits, hominy corn, red eye gravy, fried catfish..she thought for a moment..and could have sworn that catfish didn't even exist on earth anymore. "where'd you get catfish momma? it looks delicious" Cathleen scratched her chin and smirked "well ah got my own little connections,,,catfish don't exist on earth no more, at least not these days, when humans started to move off earth and settle in other places, this family took the few they had left on they farm and raised them on other worlds, have been doing it ever since, it's highly illegal nowadays, so I keep they names a secret. question is, how'd YOU know it was catfish?" Lexus looked a bit off and to the side "i've had it before momma, an old friend of mine had some for dinner, she's um..well she was lost a long time ago, doing time now offworld in a penal colony for treason. I'd rather not talk about it if it's ok" Lexus added grimly as she bit into the warm biscuit. She ate voraciously while her mother just stared in awe. "Don't the clans feed ya gal? damn girl..slow down"


Lexus halted her feast and wiped her mouth with a napkin. "Umm sorry ma..I don't really eat much, just some rations and what not, and I always eat alone, you know I can't really cook." Cathleen leaned over the table a bit and in a whisper almost asked "Don't you got no friends honey? or at least someone who can cook a decent meal for ya?" Lexus rolled her eyes in thought, pondering the question before answering, her mouth still stuffed with food "I never really thought about it ma...not really I guess, my job don't leave room for many friends. Rather I guess, no one would bother being friends with a person like me, but their loss I guess." Cathleen crossed her fingers, chapel style and placed them near her mouth "don't sound like to me the clans care for ya at all, why do you stay hun?" Lexus sat silently, a question that was so simple in nature had stumped her, she had never been asked before. "I dunno ma, I thought I could help bring order, but it seems no one wants order, they want to serve their own greedy interests. I fight for them and they spit on me...everyday..all the time, and what I stand for" Lexus looked in her bag and admired her shiny clans merits, she had the highest available, the double suns. "I bled for these...I thought that made me a clanner more so than anything..I went through the struggles, but I am hated, and despised and called a thug, a tyrant, by a bunch of ungrateful people....they don't know me, how can they judge what I am momma?" Lexus tossed her clan merit board against the wall with a loud thud and it shattered almost instantly into hundreds of pieces. Cathleen stared in shock and didn't speak a word, instead she disappeared into her bed room before emerging moments later.

Cathleen placed an Omni-Tek application form in front of the shivering form of Lexus, who was so upset she appeared almost catatonic. "don't seem like they care fer you much at all Delana, why don't you come home sugah?"

Lexus stared blankly at the form and palmed her face with her hands..it was a choice she had to face, but could she make the right one?
