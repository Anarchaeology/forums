I've been throwing this around in random threads about zone crashing, as it worked miracles for me.

If you have a SBLive based sound card, try turning off all sound (not down, off). This fixed my zoning problems, as well as the crash a few minutes after loggin in problems, as well as most of my crash sometimes when loading a bunch of new graphics problems.

After playing without sound most of the time since release, I recently purchased a SB Extigy external soundcard. I have since been able to turn the sound back on and have no major problems with zoning or crashing. I also had no problems on a different system that was using an internal Crystal-based sound chipset, but that might have been a fluke.

I have posted this to a few other people, and it has worked for them as well. I don't know if it is a good generic fix, but it certainly worked for me and a few others.

Second solution I have seen (might even be the same issue really) is that if you can reconfigure your system so that nothing is sharing an IRQ with your videocard and sound card (difficult on an acpi system sometimes) then that fixes a myriad of lockups and crashes.

Hope this helps someone.

For people w/ XP that are still having a lot of the bluescreen issues, setting FSAA to completely disabled fixed my bluescreening. As for the people that are getting random video lag issues, check and make sure your video card is on a unique IRQ. Win2000 and WinXP are both designed to try and route several devices through as little as 1 IRQ. FSAA=Full Scene Anti-Aliasing, which means it smooths out the jaggies you see on edges of polygons, something the PS2 has trouble with. On newer cards, like a geforce 2 or 3, you can manually change it so that it is 1x, 2x, or even 4x AA, but you take a performance hit when you go up higher. The default setting is automatic, meaning you let the game itself decide what to use.

Just want to mention that I have run AO on:
- GeForce 256
- GeForce 3 Ti200
- GeForce 3 Ti4400
and all of them with all sorts of different drivers, both official and "leaked" beta's. And I never had any trouble at all. I'm not saying that there's no problems with GF4, but it might be connected to other hardware too. Always remember to use the latest official drivers for ALL the hardware: motherboard chipset, soundcard, NIC, HD controller, etc.

I had a problem some years ago where I couldn't use my printer! because of a driver conflict between my graphics card and my sound card. Just because the graphics looks weird, don't assume it must be the detonator driver that's the fault. Finding the right drivers for ALL the hardware can be a severe pain in most peoples behinds.

Oh yeah, be carefull not to play too much around with driver settings. There are loads of settings in there that sound really good, but alas they are not. Not all settings work like You expect them to, and also lots of games are made to work best with the drivers default setting. I don't know how sensitive AO is, but I have seen too many games run like #¤%& because people play around with settings they are not supposed to.
