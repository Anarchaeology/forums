There's valid concerns from the Engineer community regarding the nerf being dropped on them in 14.2. The changes are just so simplistic and childish that they are right to complain.

However, I would not bring class comparisons into the argument. Understandably, when you feel you are being beaten into a corner you start grasping at anything you can to survive. Dragging down the MP is not the right course of action.

FunCom owes it's players a little of their time to think out how to PROPERLY address the weapon and pet availability range and punishments for those who stray too far beyond what the game designers want them to be using. I'm behind ALL professions
I don't want a single profession to take any hit in any area right now.

I'm in the group of players who enjoy every player in their group having fun. If my Engineer friends get their enjoyment of the game lessened, then that makes me sad.

I'm also in the group of players that feel a fairly wide (but reasonable) difference in the power potential within a particular profession for any given level is a positive point for the game. I approve of their being strong Soldiers and weak Soldiers at lvl 100 for example.

I'm also in the group of players who feel that there is too much potential for a disproportionate variance between the uber-elite and clueless. I agree something should be done to make skill more important than items. I however think FunCom is so screwed up in how they plan on doing it that it's almost comical.

I'm also in the group of players who feel that each profession should be equally powerful in the sum of their capabilities and contribution to a group. I however, don't feel that this should spill over into one-on-one PvP. I feel that some professions should not have a chance in hell of killing most other professions. However, I feel that these professions should have this weakness offset by a tremendous capability to help their teammates be killing machines.

I've been called a jackass and genius. I don't really care. It's all about opinion. I have one and so does eveybody else. As long as I know I'm being true to my beliefs and I'm honest with myself that they are not driven by envy or selfishness then I can log out with a settled conscience.

4-point skills quickly catch up to 5-point skills during title caps. No big deal.

The whole game is going to revolve around the lvl 150+ game by this Summer. At that point there is little to no concern about spare IP's. Proper character design with respect to Profession/Breed/Skillset will be the determining factor behind effectiveness.

1-120 is a bump in the road to the point your 'real' character's innate playstyle emerges. One best envision that now rather than focus on what is afforded earlier.

AFAIK, every profession is like a butterfly or dragonfly that starts as a nymph, larva, chrysallis, before completely turning in to something else. Some professions are more susceptible to this than others.

If you wonder why higher level character often play less homage to the posts/requests of the lower level classes, this is why. The game is completely different. You don't need robots to tank. The Slayer II is a drag on a party and a general reduction in damage potentia. Over-equipping is something you used to do a long time ago. You level once or twice a week if you are a frequent but not addicted player. You know everybody in your team and team with the same people frequently even if you're guildless.

4-point skills just cost more. Don't take the path of least resistance just because you may be tight on IP's at the 50, 100, or even 150 title caps. In the end, as crazy as it may seem, you may be gimping yourself by going with all green skills. Just take a look at all the higher level Agents with Ranees who still have plenty of IP to go around.

Hell, if they ever fix the Agent, mine's going for a Snakemaster.
