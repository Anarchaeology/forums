Some suggestions for the game...
The range cap was implemented because people would be able to shoot at plants, run away heal, shoot, over and over and...
Why not make plants give out DoT's that would fix that problem...

But to my main suggestion...
Why not make MOB's along the way be able to speak to you, if u didn't just attack them... for example one NPC around Hole in the Wall is talking of a story when he walk to Tir and find a old lady who has her nose in a tree stub (a fairytale yes I know) but she then ask him to help her, and he kill her, and consider that good enough, what if we had similar events around, that if u walked up to them, and said "Hi" while targeting them they would ask u for help on something, and if u helped them in a certain matter u'd get for example a unice item or a useless item, or just a token, or just a BIG "Thank you !"... ?
That would make those of us not too triggerhappy a bit happier, and would make the Roleplay portion of the game more existent...
