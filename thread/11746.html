"The Pit"
In the world we live in today consensual duelling has become impossible.

It used to be that a sportsman-like Clanner and a sportsman-like Omni could consentually engage in combat in a place like the Tir arena. These days have left us.

I dont long for war. But I miss the days of honest and decent combat between balanced foes. Ganking someone unawares has little value for me. So I suggest.....

"THE PIT"

A neutral zone like a large arena with seating. With a 'duelling terminal' at each end. 2 large display screens showing the protagonists and ther statistics (limited). And a referee (NPC) annoucing the fight. Two toons enter - one toon leaves.

This would be a place where both sides could gather for honest combat and provide true entertainment to the masses. A meta-game perhaps but one that is sorely needed.

I think this would provide great excitement in the game. Please let me know what you think about "THE PIT".
