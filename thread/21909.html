RK2 Killing Squad forming
We're looking for a few people to help us start-up our org on Rubi-Ka 2.

We are all currently lvl 50+, but are welcome all levels, all breeds, and all professions to join.

This is not a roleplaying or themed guild. We are simply a group of good friends looking to make more friends and have a good time playing AO. One thing - we are Omni affiliated.

So, come join us - if you're looking for a steady team, new friends, or you just want to laugh at some of the stupid things our founding members tend to do.
If you've ever teamed up with Astriell, Yuowin0, or Misura on RK2 you know how nuts we can be.

If you are interested please send a tell to Misura on RK2. I am on most weekday evenings from around 7pm (US EST) til whenever I can't keep my eyes open any longer. Weekends I am on more often, but a little tougher to find. Keep a lookout for me and my pet bot, Mr.Coffee in Omni Trade!
