Ideas about terrain (brainstorming)
Okay, just a few things i thought would be cool. No guarantees of workability:

Terrain

Ever noticed how in every single MMORPG the terrain breaks down into high bits and low bits and water... and that's it?

The difference between desert and plains and concrete is the texture and the sound your feet make.

Here's my idea for a difference:

First up, I'd love to see scrub / low vegetation that makes a difference. Whether it is just the existing grass stuff or even something akin to a water layer to indicate the 'surface' of low-lying scrub (people 'wading' through the undergrowth) I think it'd add a lot to the world.

At the moment I know the game engine is 'aware' of the kind of ground you're standing on. After all, it has to know what footstep sound to make. So how about this:

Give different kinds of terrain both a concealment and movement speed modifier. You can run at full speed on a road, but you can hide like a hidey-thing if you take cover in a bush or in the long-grass.

Give a penalty or even some 'environment' damage (melee would make sense) to people trying to run through heavy terrain. Ever tried running through thick undergrowth? It ain't easy, and there are spikey things!

I'd make the run penalties associated with environment directly linked to the 'adventuring' skill. This would give the skill meaning, and probably make for some very happy adventurers.

I've been running around in the great outdoors a lot recently, and I really really feel this would be nifty. When going up into mountainous regions having to work your way through the snowline scrub would be neat-o. Hiding in the grass near a bridge and ambushing people of the other side would be neat-o.

Heck, you could go as far as to create 'disturbances' in areas of certain terrain when someone is sneaking through - birds flying up or similar.

NB: I think the 'surface texture' for low foliage like this would work well without adding unduly to the poly-count. For people who sit down in it, just give them some kind of really low-visibility branches-and-crap filler. Or make it low enough that you can't sit down in it and see 'under' the surface

Desert is particulary unpleasant because it's both easy-movement and easy-concealment. But then, desert fighting has always been miserable and horrid.

And while I'm on the terrain brainstorm, I'd love to see different armor types that affect your camoflage. Omni-Pol Forest or Desert should, for instance, give a bonus in the appropriate terrain. To make things fair there should be some generic (not as good?) armor that does the same for everyone. I always envisioned Omni-Tek urban troops or police running around in their white uniforms, but Omni-Tek agents or adventurers running around in their yellowish or greenish metaplast and knowing how to hide.

After all, we learned the downside of wearing bright colors into battle in the early 1900s.
