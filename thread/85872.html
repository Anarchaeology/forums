Inventory Control and XP Bar
Inventory Control
* Make Bags resizable...PLEASE!!!!!!!!!
* Game Play- AUTOLOOT TO BAGS PLEASE
Thats all we want.. I could care less about naming a bag.. that is covered in the variety of bags we get now.. Also with various stuff I carry I am left with 9 to 10 open slots for loot to pop into. I am constantly stopping to put stuff into bags. In the meantime tickers on nanos are running and mobs are aggroing.
We would put all of our empty bags up top and all stuff would flow into those. Coding has to be possible because with Unique items they get extracted out of bags when pulling a bag from bank.

* However. I would create some bags that have different colors or symbols that dont have stats increasers.. ie those are costly for newer players with no big brother alts. and would help them to organize their loot...
