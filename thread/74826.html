This whole release date mess is rather funny.

First the Non-Americans are screaming because they think they can't get NW until December, and now Americans are screaming because FC decides to delay NW implementation for one day, but that particular day won't help.

And its true. Both factions should be upset. The whole idea of Notums wars being available in stores (not for download) is for AO promotion. FC would have a much better profit margin by selling the game by downloads. But then the boxes don't show up in stores and the clerks aren't talking about the game. This is about marketing and advertising, and trying to make the retailers happy.

So, why not please both worlds? Get the darn boxes into the stores before the biggest shopping day in the world (American--the day after Thanksgiving). And, set the NW 'implementation date' to a time when many people will have the game already. I don't think two days after an announcement appears on the AO site is enough time. Especially since it may be true that people who build first will have an advantage.

Why did it just suddenly occur to FC that they could sell their darn 'keys' well before allowing people to play the game? I'm not the most experienced gamer, so I assumed that I could buy the game in a store on the release date. Wrong! It is also funny that the FC website doesn't appear to have an announcement of the release. Today is the first official announcement I have seen, and it was to announce a delay, not to announce release. Sorry, am I blind? Did I miss the first announcement? FC's site still says 'Coming Soon.' That is totally hilarious!

So, why didn't FC put their shiny boxes of buggy code that will be patched anyway on the store shelves a week ago? At the same time, they announce that an implementation date will be planned ASAP, as soon as they are sure everyone has a chance to buy the game. Wow, that would have been a good PR move, and would have allowed them to delay implementation for 'humanitarian' reasons. What is so important about making the release date and the implementation date coincide so closely?

This is terrible strategic planning. People will get mad, people will complain. And it really could have been forseen. Sure, people will ALWAYS complain, but the complaints can be minimized with some forethought and a little common sense. Too bad this doesn't seem to be the case with the NW release.

I hope the game is fun. I'm going to buy it, and I am going to play it. I'll try not to whine too much.
