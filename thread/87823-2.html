Take a step back please people
I'm sorry but I've been through all of this before. I didn't enjoy it then -- and I am not enjoying it now.

Lets take a look at what Clannet is supposed to be. It is supposed to be a system by which all clan members can work together to ensure fair loot for all those that participate at killing tarasque. Thus, it is a community based system, not individuals.

However, it is not currently run in a way that reflects the community. The best evidence of this is that individuals that have problems with the current system have little to no way to express their problems in a way that gets them heard. Ie. The majority of people going to Clannet raids don't have someone they can talk to that they think will fight for them to change the system if they have a good idea.

There is also the issue that the majority of people attending Clannet must accept the administrators and it is highly possible that they do not know them at all, much less have built up any sort of trust for them.

So the problem with all of this -- and the problem that Nemesis is acting out on -- and the problem that ODM acted out on -- is that Clannet claims to be a community based system when in fact, it is not.

So now that we have the problem clarified -- what about the symptoms?

The symptoms are that groups of powerful individuls will attempt to influence the system in the only way they know how -- taking loot or running damage teams.

These individuls will face massive critisizm from the community and probably banning, but NOTHING will actually come of it in Clannet.

A decent number of these people will then get so frustrated that they leave the community -- either by canceling their accounts or going omni.

Thus, we lose some of the most determined and active individuals we have -- all over a system that wasn't doing what it was supposed to do and which they couldn't fix.

So I ask ALL of you -- is this really what you want?

Options
The options I see for Clannet are as follows in order to prevent this happening again (which it will unless something changes):

1. Make it a community based system -- difficult to implement and insanely difficult to do well, but probably worth it.

2. Make it invite only. This would truely reflect what Clannet is atm, since only a few people in reality make desicions on what is happening.

3. Keep Clannet as it is and accept that some groups will form damage teams. This may be somewhat stable for Clannet, but it is very difficult on the community and no one seems to know what ninjaing and KSing and all those nasty phrases realls mean.

What is happening now is a default #3. A terrible choice imo.
