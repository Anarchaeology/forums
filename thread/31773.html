Do not put level restrictions on nano formulas.

If you can meet the skill requirements you can run the program. This is the fundamental paradigm of AO.

The removal of the ability to "step" implants, and the changes to overequipping rules already in place are enough of a curtail on twinking. Adding a hard level cap to the mix removes a large portion of what makes this game both unique and fun - the ability to customize your character & plan your IP usage and implants to be able to run nanos a little earlier than the average template for your profession. A large proportion of the playerbase derive satisfaction from this.

If in your (funcom's) estimation a nanoformula in unbalancing to be used before a certain level, then you need to rework the nanoformula - increase the skill requirements or reduce the effect, not introduce some bogus hard cap on when it can be used.

If you're intent on introducing a paradigm shift of this magnitude, then you may as well add elves and dwarves, change the game's name to EverQuest: Camping the Future and be done with it because you've just destroyed what made AO AO.
