Funny you mention this...

I found out the other day that setting your bar to max defense will not only make your nanos cast slower, but it will make the recharge time VERY fast. It's almost as if the casting and recharge times were switched on full defensive.

This leads me to believe two things:

1. As a caster you aren't losing any time on full defensive. The time you lose casting pays off in the recharge. Probably makes this best for a NT to volley off nukes, but not so good for a doc that has to get that heal out right now.

2. As you gain higher levels you can probably set full defensive and not only insta cast things, but also have very low recharge times!
