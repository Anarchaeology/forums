OOC: Meta Whining
ooc

Hi!

I'm just going to whine a little about the number of negative posts I see here these days. I'm a noob here, so there's a big risk I'll make myself unpopular, but I'll take that.

Some background on my play style, to put things in context: First of all, I love the game. I like the fantasy about it, not the level thread mill. What RP:ing I do is usually fan fiction-oriented, plus some posting here. I don't really depend on the official story line, and I've seldom read the news on the official site (that recently disappeared). But I have a lot of fun, and it's not while camping.

I think there are an infinite number of ways to solve the problems that FC is generating for the RP community.

Official News Sources

There are no "official" new sources in the real world. There are privately owned papers and TV stations, news agencies, press conferences by governments and organizations, etc. If news about the events on Rubi-Ka were to move to private web sites, AO Vault, and the like, then wouldn't that be just as good, and even more realistic than having one "official" news source? If you want news, start a paper! (I'd love to contribute.)

Official Story Line

Okay, I admit I'm a little in the dark here, and would love some more info about this. But I suspect that this is about world-, or at least, area-altering events. Although such changes are cool and definitely increase game immersion, very few people in the world are directly involved in that kind of events. Organization-altering events are still pretty major, and events that change things for a number of individuals can be just as exciting to them, no? I guess I just don't see how a lack of major political changes in the world is an obstacle to role playing.

As far as I've seen, this is a great role playing community with excellent role players, and I think that those who are frustrated with the game at present are so more because they've seen everything in it once too many times, and have grown somewhat tired of it--and perhaps used to how things have been in the past. I just don't think that there's anything in the game that prevents good and fun role playing.

But I see threads about all the current problems getting three pages of posts in a few days while posts with stories, songs, and other RP ideas, disappear from page one with one or two responses.

I guess AO is a little like the real world, it is what it is, and it's up to us to make the best of it. (How plucky of me, but you get my drift. )

"If news about the events on Rubi-Ka were to move to private web sites, AO Vault, and the like, then wouldn't that be just as good, and even more realistic than having one "official" news source? If you want news, start a paper! (I'd love to contribute.) "

I guess the problem is that you'd invariably get into the situation where Org A says they destroyed Org B's facilities on one paper, while Org B says the opposite on another paper. While that happens to some extent in real life, it's minimized by the paper's concern over respectability and more importantly, lawsuits.

Fansite news don't seem to have this problem and post everything under the sun. So try as they might to sound authoritative with their news, they don't pull it off unless they have full-time professional editors and journalists that track down every lead and verify it - and also talk amongst other sites to make sure no information is contradictory (since most of it is fan-fic anyway).

That said, it'd be nice if someone would setup an Associated Press type of system (using one of the standard XML newsfeed formats) where other fansites could download a somewhat agreed-upon news/history of the world. Then anyone who wants to submit news can dump it there, have a contributing pool of fan-editors to okay the entries, and have that reflect on all fansites.

It distributes the work amongst all involved. And no, that "someone" isn't me (I've got too many projects). But it's an idea and one I hope someone out there can/want to tackle.

Krono,

I think you are dead right. I personally never felt I needed FC consent, assistance or recognition to roleplay in AO. However were I to play devil's advocate on this, I can try to sum up the reasons people are so upset.

Replacing the official news and fan sites is an issue because:

1. News items that FC picked up somehow became "canonical". There are a lot of people who search for the magical instance moment when your private roleplaying becomes part of a shared reality. It's not just about fame, its about validation your character and your creation.
2. The official news site has, we assume, a much larger readership than any fan site.

Losing completely the official storyline is an issue because:

1. Historically, we were promised a game with an interactive storyline. Many people do not like to be deceived.
2. Some players who have been involved in the odd official story and event developed a taste for it.
3. Again it comes down to the dream of having one shared reality. We can all of course do our own roleplaying in our own groups as if each one ran their own server, but for many this falls short of what a mmorpg should be.

Like I said these are not my views in the least, but I hope they are a fair representation how a number of AO roleplayer's feel.

I also agree the whining can get excessive, but atleast we are not flaming each other. I always find that much more distressing.
