Mjolnir now recruiting
Hello all, I'm posting here to announce the opening of recruiting for Mjolnir (which is the name of Thor's Hammer in Norse mythology) on RK-1. We are a clan affiliated guild which is based on one simple concept- members have control, not the council.

We are open to all levels of players and styles who are clan. Our only rule is to treat your fellow guildmates and players with respect at all times. Any rules in the future will be decided strictly by a vote of the entire population of the guild. All voices have equal weight from level 1-200, from advisor to member.

So you want to PvP? Great. So you don't want to PvP? Great. The flexability allows for the members to decide the direction of the guild and there will be room for raid parties, role players, power levelers, whatever. You won't be walking into a situation where someone will tell you that you have to do this or that.

If you'd be interested in a truely democratic organization and just want to have more enjoyment playing AO, then you should take a look at what we have to offer. You can learn more about Mjolnir by visiting our guild site and forum which can be found at www.4monkeysoup.com/ao (please visit the About section of the site). If you like what you see, simply post a message about yourself in the forum under the Applicants section in the public area. You can also send a tell in game to any of the advisors: Demagouge, Sepal, or Xeraseus.
