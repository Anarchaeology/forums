Welp...
Actually, it's correct...It's just misleading!

It says:

chest X 4
head X 3
pants X 2. 5
boots X 1. 5
gloves/sleeves X 1

full body multiplier (full suits of same type and quality OR battle suits) X 14

4+3+2.5+1.5+1+1+1 = 14...true

It just would have made more sense if they said "Gloves", "R-Sleeve", and "L-Sleeve".

There is no additional modifier for a full set of armor...
