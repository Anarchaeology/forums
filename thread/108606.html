Download TALK DEVIANT Episodes!
Press Release from Tarryk Kozar of GridStream Productions

Talk Deviant Episodes are now available for download!

If you head to our main site at www.gridstream.com and check out the Media Section, you'll see that the past 2 episodes of Talk Deviant are available to download.

I will be occasionally offering special temporary "sneak" downloads of various episodes throughout the way-back past (we've been regularly recording for nearly 10 months as of this press release), so keep an eye on that section.

The episodes are mp3 files and are roughly between 11 and 15 megabytes each in size, and are of low (16kbps mp3) quality. Hey...it's talk radio, gimme a break, you don't need it in STEREO, do you?

If the download is going VERY slow for you, please understand that bandwidth is limited, and there are probably many others downloading the file if it is very slow. If this is the case, please cancel and wait a little while for a faster download.
