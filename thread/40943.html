Press Conference on RK2
Press Conference to be held in West Athen on Sunday.

As you all have now been made aware, Henry Radiman, the Leader of the Council of Truth, has made his return from relative obscurity. He has issued an official statement regarding his whereabouts and his condition, and where he stands as far as the Council is concerned.

There have been many varying responses to this statement. Some feel positive, and some are against it. Some are even indifferent to Radiman.

One recently spawned entity, The West Athen Militia, has not yet declared allegiance to the Council. In the midst of Radiman's return, and possibly the Council's reconvening, the Militia's President, Myles "Tahrus" Barchus will be holding a Press Conference on Sunday, July 28th at 6pm EST at The Cup in West Athen. During this Conference, President Barchus will make his views regarding Radiman's return, and the Militia's stance very clear.
