Why level reqs when...
We all know those beloved level reqs by now.. but it seems to me that FC has overlooked some of the more obvious alternatives to having the requirements based on *level*.

Example springs to mind; the NCU buffs. Does it make sense that these reqs are based on an arbitrary level? Or would it make more sense if the requirement was comp lit? Say if your CL is below 200, you're not getting that nifty +85 ncu, no matter what your level. This is what FC wanted, right? Not a game based on levels but instead based on skills.

Other skills that might be used instead of levels is Body dev (hp buffs), intelligence (skill buffs), etc.. Now, body dev is notoriously hard to buff, implants is the only way to go right now that I know of, and you only swap those out of mission (normally), so buffing in mission really isnt a problem. And who would mind if a 7k hp buff (adventurer buff) required ~800 body dev instead of lvl195?
