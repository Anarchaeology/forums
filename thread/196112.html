All Hallows Eve History Lesson
Neleb the Deranged's Uncle, who he referred to as his Uncle Pumpkinhead due to some deformity, abused him as a child, took his teddy bear from him on halloween and other such vile atrocities. Neleb ran away from his uncle and joined Omni-tek, his considerable abilities in metaphysical research gained him high favor and large expense accounts with which he developed many useful tools.

According to rumor, his NT assistant was jealous and tampered with an experiment which caused the downfall of Neleb's sanity. The side affect was that Neleb became even more attuned to the metaphysical and his manifestations became more abundant and uncontrollable. OT exiled him from Omni-med and discontinued his insurance in an effort to stop the rampaging manifestations. Confused, afraid and lost, he stumbled into the cave we have come to know as The Steps of Madness. Here he was able to regain some of his sanity and control over his manifestations. He set up the great eyeball as a gate into the cave, and instructed it to only allow him into the cave.

In this cave he quietly went about his business until the next halloween, his nightmares of mistreatment by his uncle led to a bout of uncontrollable manifestations appearing across Rubi-ka. The pioneers of Rubi-ka set out to rid the world of these evil monstrosities, and found a teddy bear (the childhood memory) on the corpses of the pumpkins. After consulting with the MP's and NT's who knew of Neleb's exile and location, they were able to trick the eye into letting them into the cave, by presenting the childhood memory to it.

Deep down into the cave they crawled, facing all manner of Neleb's deranged psyche made flesh. Everything from his fears to his jealousys wandered the halls. Finally they found Neleb, of course Neleb in the height of his most insane part of the year was unable to understand the people trying to come help him and attacked them. Neleb was slain.

..or so it seemed.

Neleb's early years at OT
Neleb's early work at Omni-Med was in the research and development of the ressurection technology. OT had already successfully cloned empty shells, they'd also seen the miraculous discovery made by Enfraam when one of the test subjects died right as the clone was created. They were having problems understanding how the soul transfer could be accomplished reliably enough to make a profit from the discovery.

Neleb discovered the link to the cell scan technology with the metaphysical nature of notum. He developed the "soul beacon" as he called it that programmed a few thousand nanobots to constantly monitor the life status of a scanned individual. These nanobots would instantaneously travel to the scanner where the subject's DNA data was stored at the precise moment of death. The bots would then trigger the cloning mechanism, and the newly formed body would be ready to accept the soul of the deceased before thier old body even collapsed to the ground.

As I said before, this discovery gained Neleb a certain level of prestige within the ranks of Omnitek and he was afforded certain luxuries and expense accounts to further his research. As OmniTek began marketing the insurance scan across Rubi-ka, he was heralded as the savior of humankind. However, shortly thereafter, OT began selling the insurance technology in its other colonies and work centers. The technology did not work. Neleb was forced to work longer and longer hours to find the reason and to fix it, OT had already lost uncountable amounts of potential revenue.

When Neleb discovered the answer it was with regret that he informed his superiors that the technology would never work outside of Rubi-ka. The trace elements of Notum that is so plentiful across Rubi-ka were the missing ingredient on those other worlds. Without it, the "soul beacon" nanobots could not travel to the DNA scanner fast enough to generate the new host for the departing soul.

OT prime was outraged, they ordered Neleb's funding be pulled and a permanent demotion. Boss Ross felt sorry for Neleb and left him in his research position but did have to reduce the funding. Subsequently, the rumor of his Nanotechnician assistant sabotaging the experiment which caused his decline into insanity may be less true and more the fault of reduced funding.

However, back to the history lesson at hand. While Neleb may have been a madman hiding deep down in his cave outside Omni-1, he was still a brilliant metaphysicist. His instincts for survival forced his memory of his research with the Resurrection technology to begin construction of a method to extend his own life. Through his extended abilities with the Metaphysical, he devised a method to program nanobots to reconstruct him at his altar at the base of the Steps of Madness. Until someone is able to break that connection, I am afraid we will be visited by his Uncle Pumpkinhead every year.
