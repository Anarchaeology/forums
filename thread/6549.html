The village
The screenshot your looking at is a VERY cool place called the "Enigma Forest" and in particular your looking at the Enigma village. This is in the "Artery Valley" area between Central Artery and Deep Artery. An extremely dangerous journey on foot but a cool trip in a Yal. The enigma people are almost like "stick" people, they are non aggressive by nature but supposedly very dangerous when provoked. There is some good high level hunting and incredible [and weird] sights to see in the area but keep in mind there is 0% gas, total mayhem.

To get to it, grid into 2HO and head due east or slightly north or due east, fly above tree top level and theres a number of cool things to see.
