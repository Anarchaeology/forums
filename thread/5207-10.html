More stuff
Adventurer definitely needs more stuff that suits their proffesion. I have some of my own ideas of what they should be.

New Nano Programs:

1. Modification to the polymorph nanos - When you use your polymorph nanos, the monster that you turn into should be one with nature. When I say that I mean, you shouldn't be aggroed by monsters of the same breed. In fact, they should help you. If you turn into a leet in an area of other leets and get attacked by a hammer breeder, then the rest should help to defend youif youre still in that area. Think of them as your army scratching and clawing for you at your disposal. But they can't follow you or anything. Just help defend you when you're in their area. And don't think of it as a faster way to get xp. They aren't in your team hence either less or no xp if they help kill it for you. Think of it more as an easier way to explore.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Single/Team Tranquil Beasts - When using this nano, all outdoor monsters will not aggro you for a duration of time. They will attack though if attacked first.

(NOTES) This should make exploring alot more easier. Want to do that mission in Clon**** but it's surronded by reds? Then this can make the trip easier if you can't afford the luxuries of a yalmaha. But of course this should only work outdoors or else missions and dungeons could become real easy. This would also be useful in teams or as a buff for someone else. The nano line could extend on durations. Starting from 5, 10, 20 minutes and so on. Adventuring should definitely be a check on this and at least 1 nano skill (NOT 2!) like bio meta.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. Single/Team Treaties - This nano will allow you to walk freely in oppisite side territories for a duration of time. The oppisite side will not aggro you and will talk to you as if you were on their side. This nano though will not allow you to access opposite side terminals or affects pvp.

(NOTES) I dunno about this one, it sounds like something for a bureacrat. In any case it helps you explore more areas without fear of guards "gankin yo ass". But you still have to worry about players sitting in political zones. Again this line can be extended upon durations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

New Dungeons

Make new dungeons where you need an adventurer to go in. It doesn't have to be adventurer only just that you can only get in with an adventurer. They could guide you through the dungeon past "Indiana Jones" type puzzles and traps. Now there's the question of how a puzzle could work in AO. Here are a couple ways for it to work:

1. Make it NPC oriented: Make the dungeon a huge maze with twists and turns here and there. Make it so that when you turn on your little map it just says "no map available". To get through you encounter a bunch of NPCs that ask random riddles. The NPCs will only talk to the adventurer so the adventurer has to get it right. If you mess up 3 times, the NPC will kill you and your team. The NPC will have to be uber for it to seem approprate. If you do trigger the correct anwser the NPC will give you directions to find the next one. If you decide to give up, you can go outside and redo it but with different riddles. Eventually at the end of it all is a big surprise.

(NOTES) Theres also the question of other teams following you through the dungeon. To avoid that, make the NPC give you a key to the next area (make it so you zone to the area). It's no-drop but only the adventurer needs to have to let everyone through. This could work opposite apartments how everyone without the key goes first then finally the key holder goes. The key dissapears when the key holder goes through.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Zoning - You find an occasional button here and there that zones one member of the team to an enclosed area with only 1 door. Only one member of a team can go. Beacon warp should of course not work in these dungeons. When they have completed the task they can go through the door which will zone them right outside the dungeon. The adventurer might have to come back out to retrieve them. When they reach this area, they will either find a treasure chest, NPC, or monster.

Treasure chest - The best thing to find. The chest will contain a key to the next area and maybe a rare item. Easiest of the 3.

NPC - A 1 minute timer will appear on screen and when the timer is up an NPC will spawn. Like number 1 you will have to anwser a riddle. When completed he will give you the key and maybe a rare item as well.

Monster - A 1 minute timer will appear for this one too so as to make the player nervous as to whether or not they get an NPC or monster. The monster will spawn and will aggro the player until it is dead. The loot will be the key and of course possibly a rare item.

This kind of dungeon should have no monsters in it except maybe for a boss kind. There should be fewer rooms for this. Finally you get to what you were seeking.

(NOTES) You could do the team thing like number 1 but how are you going to avoid more than 1 person pressing the button? Well the button could dissapear after being pressed or it could be non-selectable for a period of time (10, 20 minutes tops). The way you find one of those 3 rooms as above is it could be totally random. Or it could be dependant on one of your skills. The higher 1 of your skills is the better the chance of not getting a room with a monster. It could change for each room with a button.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

So I think these ideas fit the adventurerer nicely. I would add more but I'm tired of typing.
