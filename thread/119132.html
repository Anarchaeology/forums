Making Money
I think there should be some interesting ways to make money and lose money,

Like:

A casino- this has been suggested by numerous people but it is such a cool idea i had to mention it again. betting on card games, leet races, slots, etc.

Arena betting- Players can volunteer to fight various MOB battles in the arena and people can bet on the outcome (no xp of course). example: Level 100 enforcer Vs 2000 leets, players can set the rules like (No buffs, no nano using, no mezzing, etc). This could prolly be exploited but it would be fun even without betting.


How about mission chests containing credits?
