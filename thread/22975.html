patches
No. Major patches (the even numbers, 13.8, 14.0, 14.2 etc.) are about once per 2 months at the moment. Other patches (the odd numbers 13.9, 14.1 and even lesser revisions such as 13.91) usually occur within the first week or two after a major patch, hence the apparent speed. These typically just fix bugs and exploits however.

The next patch (14.2) should hit testlive any day, expect it to be there for a couple of weeks minimum (14.0 was on testlive for about a month).
