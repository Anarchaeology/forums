Neutral Grid Emergency Exit
The neutral grid emergency exit has been changed from newland desert, near Stolt Jensenbergs OP, to the NLC grid terminal.
Why? The emergency exit was near the new wompa system and could be used as a short cut to Borealis (thru Hope-Last Ditch). With the new drop site you're forcing people to go via Newland City, one of the laggiest places in the neutral territories.

If the drop site in the desert was too dangerous for lowbies, then why not just move it closer to the OP?

The point FakoNamo is that there are no other exits that will take you safely to Borealis without having to go thru NLC or run from 4 holes/Athen.

I'm sure a complete newbie would feel safer landing in NLC than in the desert, but is s/he entitled to feel safe after making such a big misstake in the dangerous world of Rubi-Ka?
Just kidding ofcourse. Newbies should feel safe (within reason), but I'd rather have them feel safe at Stolts than in NLC. And I think they do. You can see the wompa from the drop site and Stolts isn't even 25% anymore.
If the newbies are unfamiliar with the wompa system, then maybe we should have them start in Borry BYs rater than NLC BYs?

Furthermore I think that exiting to the exact same spot as if you'd used the Newland exit takes away from the feeling of an emergency exit.

One of my first "big adventures" on Rubi-Ka was when I boldly went where I'd never gone before and dropped out thru the emergency exit. I ended up in the south(?) parts of the desert, without a map. Scorpioids and sand everywhere. It was a little scary but definatly an adventure.
I've been thinking a bit on this and I've come to the conclusion that part of the reason I dislike this change is that it reroutes my non-fixer chars, but an equal part is that it takes the game away from the exploring of the world and sends the player off on the narrowminded path of NLC->BS missions.

Forcing my own ideas of what this game is or should be is not what I intended with this thread. Maybe this change is for the better, but I don't think so (yet).
