/list
Displaying names and alignment for a zone is helpful. Displaying the location is ridiculous.

1. Knowing that their is another clan/OT/Neutral in your zone is a valuable tool for finding help.

2. You do not need their location in /list. You can just ask them. This also gives the other person control over whether they tell you where they are or not.

3. The fact that your name and alignment show up on /list is not an advantage for PvP hunters. Hunters will assume there are opponents in the zone and hunt for you regardless.

4. Showing your position in a PvP zone is clearly bad. Hiding becomes nearly useless. "Concealment" becomes far less valuable.

As far as 4: I am amazed anyone would consider putting location in the /list command for a PvP zone. I'm going to assume that Funcom isn't even considering this and some people have made a bad assumption.

Knowing the relative size and location of opposing forces is the job of SCOUTS and SPIES. Scouts and spies are critical components of warfare. Some of our professions have many skills dedicated to scouting and spying. If you give player locations in /list, it devalues those professions tremendously.

I don't think Funcom is planning on doing this, :-).
