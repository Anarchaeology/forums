3 simple ideas to improve a few things..
Ok these are just a few ideas I think Funcom could add pretty easily and would make sense and help with some enjoyment of game.


1) Make it where when you are in your apartment you get a +25 buff to all trade skill stats.

The age old "Back to the Lab" theory. This would actually make apartments a bit useful. People would go back to them to do tradeskills.


2) Hurry and fix the costs on tradeskill related items.

It is still way to expensive for people to make nano crystals and even rifles. I mean cmon...its enough the people have to have the proper stats and take the time to find the parts....you have to gouge them on the price of the tools as well?

3) Add bank terminal inside tradeskills area in stores.

Just makes sense.


Just three quick ideas I wanted to throw up for Funcom and anyone else to comment on.

Thanks.
