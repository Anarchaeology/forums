Neutrals MATTER
OOC

I spent a lomg time thinking about which 'Faction' to choose.
RP for my character leaned me towards Clan... but... a bit of research and forum hoppping made me realise that there is little 'RP' consistency at Faction levels. There are Omni departments who seem hellbent on ignoring the storyline. There are Clan orgs who seem to pay little attention to the war....

Don't get me wrong... that's good. But when you look at what's 'really' happening on Rubi Ka, consistency of behaviour and teamwork, shared ideals, joint working etc etc happen at org level. AO politics isn't faction based, its org based.

I maybe started life as a fence sitter but within a few days of playing I came to see that loyalty to my org is the most important thing to me... and that inter-org relationships mimic real life situations for a hardened Rp'er more than interfaction storyline wars... that never seem to go much further than Funcom storylines. this may just be lack of ecperience... maybe I just haven't seen the co-ordinated work of the Omni or Clan hordes working together to pitch battles or regain territory by actual players. If so please educate me.

Instead I see individuals working at org and interorg levels doing some great RP work... Fixerben is just one example ...there are many more... and these guys can be found amongst our Neutrals in the Clans and in Omni.

Auntbeast is right Neutral is a side. Our indignation at feeling 'shot from bith sides is not only understandable its fun!

Think of the game without Neutrals. I for one think Rubi Ka would be a poorer place. We may never dominate ... but the co-operation of many orgs (the vast majority of which have a no first strike policy on Clan AND Omni) has been really refreshing.

IC

I too am a proud member of Wolf Brigade. My org is my home and my family. What our leaders say... I do. Up till now they have chosen wisely on a no first strike policy, but our relations with like minded orgs mean that when we are attacked (most days) like minded Neutrals are more and more likely to come to our aid... and together the Neutral Orgs can field a force that can run off most attacks.

Until democracy - true democracy - comes to Rubi Ka, I put my faith, first, last and always in my org.

I am by nature a pacifist. I am not hopelessly idealistic I do manage to concentrate my rage rather well, as any Mugger will tell you. I believe in choice. Some days I am neutral because I'm glad there is a 3rd option. Some days, just to piss off a clanner. Some days, just to piss of an omni. Some days, to annoy my spouse. Most days, because while every side has it's good/bad, I sleep better knowing I am neutral.

Why didn't I choose Omni or Clan?

Well the honest answer....

Looking at the various websites and forums before hand

Omni - Corporate World.... I've lived in Corporate World in Real Life for too many years to be attracted to hierarchy in RP... also (being really honest) Funcom's own description likens Omni to the Empire in star wars... I kept seeing Benmor in ome of these stupid little caps the Empire guys on the Deathstar had to wear!

No Mission Honey!

With Clan... I may have been misled but everywhere I looked as I said on my earlier post I saw no dedication to a common cause, I also saw a lot of Power Gamers and Uber qualities in a lot of Clan posts.

Lastly - I've always been a bit of a sucker for the underdog... Now that could have made me choose Clan but reallly? Clan, if anything, have the upper hand... they just don't seem to want to co-ordinate and win.

I liked the spirit of the Neuts I read about... They honestly seemed more like the Storylines version of Clanners than the Clan itself.

Now I'm onworld I simply like the Neuts (enmasse) more than the other guys... though I have good friends who are Clan and Omni.

Lastly... I really meant what I said about orgs... The Wolves have taken me in and given me real and substantial help... and I am in awe of Neutral orgs like the Healing Hands...

So I'm happy to fight and die (and reclaim!) for my right to remain Neutral.

Hope that explains this inddividuals choice
