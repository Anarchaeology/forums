quote:

Dai-Galean wrote this in the "Top Ten Soldiers Questions and Answers", reporting Gaute's statement about the role of the Soldier :
quote:
The role of the Soldier:

The primary role of the Soldier is that of the tank; being able to take and deal damage. For this purpose the soldier has cheap body development yielding a high hit-point count, and nano programs that enable him to sustain more damage than anyone else (partial invulnerability shields). This gives him an obvious role in teams as the focal point of enemy attacks. Who hasn't wished for a soldier shield when clicking the boss-room icon in a team mission?

The Soldier is also the long-range weaponry expert, with the cheapest skills in distance combat weaponry compared to any other profession. This enables the use of high output assault rifles and special attacks with solid damage (burst, full auto). The combination of these enables the Soldier to be a steady source of damage and also to solo adequately. They are not the ones who are supposed to deal the highest amount of damage over short periods of time, nor the highest overall damage, but they should be among the best. They are, though, among the hardest to kill.

In PvP, the Soldier doesnt have a niche as the best at something specific - but there are few professions with an equally predictable ability to survive massive amounts of damage inflicted on them at critical times. Since they are also using long-range weapons, they are not as vulnerable to roots and run speed reduction nanos as i.e. the Enforcer.
To sum it up :
1. AO Manual states the main and only function of a soldier is to have a "huge potential for dishing damage at long range". This does mean, all things even, that a lvl 200 Soldier equipped with the best equipment possible should concretize this potential.
2. Dai-Galean adds another function to the soldier profession, as important as his supposed huge damage dealing capacity, which is to be the best at surviving massive amounts of damage (i.e "to sustain more damage than anyone else").
3. Cz is not aware of #2.

Conclusion : contradicting or uncomplete statements (AO Manual/Dai-Galean/Commnunity Representative) makes it difficult to precisely define the soldier's role. Funcom designers themselves were not able, so far, to answer this question with any precise and argumented enough post or statement.

As a consequence, me viewing soldiers as huge ranged damage dealers should-bes or you viewing them as the best tanking machines is neither a right nor wrong statement. Without any concrete and precise answers from the developers, we can only suppose, so that we can't work towards any reasonable and definitive conclusion (i.e if it's not clear for Funcom, it is not clear for us, no matter what).

P.S : your statement "A Soldier is the only profession that can deal the MOST damage in the smallest amount of time." is wrong, btw, according to Dai-Galean's answer : "They are not the ones who are supposed to deal the highest amount of damage over short periods of time, [...]."
