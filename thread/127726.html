Miss Omni-Tek 29476 *Event* Updated
Miss Omni-Tek 29476

Miss Omni-Tek of 29476 will be kicking off in the week.

It will give all the lovely ladies of Omni-Tek the chance to claim stardom as the most beutiful woman in Omni-Tek!

Prizes are as follows:

1st place: Flowers, Choclates, 30 million credits
2nd place: Flowers, Choclates, 20 million credits
3rd place: Flowers, Choclates, 10 million credits

To apply you must be Female and Omni Alligned (No Atroxes in thongs allowed).
You must leave your name here along with your time zone and the song that you would like to perform to.

E.g.

Name: Nancy
Time Zone: GMT standard
Song: Shiny Disco Balls

Entrants will be required to strut their stuff down the catwalk to their chosen song, you can walk or dance your way down or do other things ( You are not required to sing or give a "I would make the world better speech") This contest is judged on style outfit and beauty.

Please state clearly and choose the song carefully that you are going to perform to.


Miss Omni-Tek 29467 will only kick off if we have a minimum of six contestants.

All Entries will be accepted until further Notice, Event time and date will be posted 1 week before the event.




Prize donation was given by General Gencop of Omni-Pol

Many thanks Gencop

Best of Luck!
