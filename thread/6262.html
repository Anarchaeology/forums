on the subject of pet pathing
i had an idea about this one, looking at it from an AI path finding problem perspective, watch out, this is the science bit:

instead of doing a heuristic search using proximity of pet to master, and treating each doorway (or co-ordinate) as a state node in the search space, thereby getting the following algorithem stuck into a local minima when a wall intervenes between the current state and goal state. i.e. the pet refuses to move away from the master to a less favourable node because this does not present a more favourable situation, even when it would allow the pet to move around a wall, and then into the room with its master in it.
why not create "tracks" within each room that a pet cannot leave, guiding it through doors, peventing it getting into the walls, and keeping it close to its master at all times. ie the corner room should have a track that runs from door to door, possitioned as to allow the pet to hit anything in that room, and if its master moves to the next room, the more favourable condition (in terms of the state space) is for the pet to follow its master into the room, following the track, preventing it from getting stuck.

erm... for you guys wh oread the above thingy and went... erm??? wtf? it means that your pets would only have a limited amount of movement freedom in missions, but would move through the mission and never get stuck anywhere, always being close to you.

First comment if you get a misison with the catwalk after the automation/android series immediately delete said mission.

Secondly. When i was still using the gladiator bot i LOVED those other cave missions. Seeing as i had done a couple hundred or there abouts of those missions i found enough tricks that my pets rarely got stuck.

first hint. If you come to a pillar room don't try and go through the pillars. In every case but one you could get to the other side of the pillars through a pathway through the rest of the mission. the only exception i found was once when both rooms on the other side were those real small rooms which only have a loot chest or nothing. in other words you should never have to get your bot to the other side.

next tip. If you come to a room where you have those 2 small ledges on each end and the water in the middle tell your pet to wait in the room before. drag the mob in from the water and kill him in the room your pet is already in. then move to the doorway of the room with the water. tell you pet to wait again right near the doorway. cross the water to the other rooms and find a mob. Pull the mob to the doorway of the room opposite of where your pet is waiting. Then tell your pet which is still waiting to attacik. 99% of the time you pet will warp immediately across the water room.

If however your pet has already gotten in the water don't worry you can still save, him. Go back to the room you were previously in before the water room with your pet following you. When your pet runs up against the ledge and won't follow you any farther tell your pet to wait again. Now drag a mob to the doorway opposite the side the pet is on and tell your pet to attack. once again 99% of the time your pet will warp across the water.

Before you do this though i recommend checking the rooms ahead because if there is only a couple rooms which lead to a dead end it is probably better off just dragging the mobs to your pet in the room before the water trap because you are going to also have to warp your pet back across later if it is a dead end and you might have to find a mob thats a fair ways away.

and for those low engies under lvl 70ish right now just wait til you guys start using the guardbots or higher. Its amazing that although they use the same model as gladiators they won't fit through any doors anymore. go figure. at around lvl 70 your problems will be compounded exponentially
