Caol - Until Rubi-Ka implodes.
Greetings friends and foes.

It's a sad day on Rubi-Ka. Many of my friends are leaving, just recently two great PvP'ers from OT left, seemingly forever. In the midst of all this I have come to a conclusion about Caol's future on Rubi-Ka. It is as follows:

I will never cancle my account. I will never 'quit' AO. I will never ditch Neo Core or my remaining friends.

Star Wars Galaxies is on the horizon and it promises to be something great. It might suck just as much as AO, it might even be worse, but it is still something 'new'. That counts for alot these days when AO in reality is the same all over again.

I am confident that I will see most of you, my friends and foes, in SwG. I think it will be great. But nontheless, my AO days will not seize until FunCom decides to pull the plug. Will it last 4 years? I'll be around to find out.

AO ain't giving me the 'atmoshphere' anymore, and it's been a long time not doing so. However, friends ingame is what keeps me from going bye-bye.

The GREAT peeps in Neo Core, my friends in The Council, my friends in Apocalypse, my foes in Storm... the list goes on, and to one person above all, Silkyblade, whom I've known since lvl30 on my first char created 9-10 months ago. I thank you all for trying to turn this game around. I still have hope that FC will haul itself in. It will be though, but I am optimistic.

Anyways, as long as there is Rubi-Ka, there will be Caol.
Comming to a mission entrance near you!

Happy Hunting!
