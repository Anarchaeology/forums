Azazzel attacks Scarney
I just read the news report and I REALLY think that whoever filed that as news should be shot for falsifying information.
While TBA DID attack Scarney in Omni-Entertainment and were controlled by Omni-Pol, Scarney held a lengthy talk with Azazzel which was nowhere in the news post.

"The first step was to secure Omni-HQ from any danger and secure every entrance to the area. At the same time an Omni-Pol unit was gathered to neutralise the criminals and bring order back in the town. The Omni-Pol units rushed from different directions and took control of the West side of Omni-Entertainment without further incidents. Many citizens were identified and controlled but there was no trace of the dangerous criminal with the name of Azazzel."

Instead many citizens after witnessing TBA members strike at Scarney wanted to help to protect the Commander, due to the supression gas in the area though no non OmniPol Personal were able to assist. Again the same thing was repeated in Omni-HQ after Azazzel showed up to talk with Scarney about their 'rights', 'rewards' and 'medals'. As if they deserved more reward then simply being allowed to work for Omni-Tek.

However, none of this is repeated in the news story posted by Staley.

Scarney and Azazzel proceeded to argue over TBA's criminal actions, many citizens voiced that TBA had forfitted their rights as Omni-Tek citizens and should be discharged from the Company. However their 'conversation' was interupted shortly after Azazzel agreed to back down and withdraw his troops from Omni-HQ. The interuption was due to an attack by Snarf and another clan member who gridded in and ran a swift series of powerful nanoprograms which wiped out a good many people in the area, amoung them Azazzel and most of his TBA guards. These clan members were quickly brought down by Omni-Tek's Peacekeepers, the Commander Scarney was bruised but otherwise unharmed after this assualt.

More accurate reporting should be done by the Harold or other news agencies before things are allowed to be printed. Not only did that article by Staley make Omni-Tek appear to be incompetant and unable to control its own citizens, but it innacurately reflected the Opinions and Actions that served to protect Omni-Tek.
