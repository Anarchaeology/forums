A New Beginning <clan>
Hi all,

We have recently and formed a new guild called Blue Dawn. As it stands right now, we only have 4 members within the guild. (a lot eh? )

Jemia, 193 Trader
Neimad, 200 Adventurer
Warboc, 200 Adventurer
Drunksteve, 182 Agent

Short term goal is to gather a base membership group of 10-15 members, lvl 146+

Long term goals are to establish bases, regularly visit unique spawns, grow membership well beyond 50, and to generally help one another out and enjoy the game.

A laid-back attitude and sense of humor is a must!

If you are interested in being a part of a new guild and influencing its growth, send me a message in game.

Note: We strive to accomplish things in game. For instance, on day one of Blue Dawn, we claimed a 150 base. Day two, we claimed a 200 base, with support of fellow clanners. Of course we lost those the next day. After all 4 people can't defend. Thats where new members, like you, come in.
