It's complicated. I think its pretty clear everyone subscribed to the view Omni aligned mercs in NLC should be replaced with true neutral (and possibly robotic!!!) guards. There is no such agreement as to what the new guards should be instructed to do.

Without going into the too much detail, some neutrals are in favour the current ban on Clanners in NLC, other neutrals believed this ban needs to be lifted. Both sides feel very passionately about this and there does not seem to be much room for compromise. Under these conditions I doubt the Newland City Council will affect any changes, for whatever they chose to do would end up upsetting a substantial portion of their residents.

Some progress could be made if the two issues 'Guards should be neutral' and 'NLC should return to be open to the Clans' were divorced. For instance, if everyone agrees that the guards should be neutral, then the campaign could focus on that and leave the issue of lifting the ban on Clanners in NLC for another day. But I am sure many would argue that the two issues cannot be divorced. And so a state of stalemate persists and as such RUR has put development of our ST49 GuardMaster robot on hold, at least temporarily.

Like I said, it's complicated.
