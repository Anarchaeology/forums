Don't really know if they're found in basic stores, _but_ :

the upside of the Aleph compared to the Mausser is higher minimum damage. As that won't be an issue 'til you level well past 50; the mausser is still the best choice if you could buy them both in a basic store. At higher levels, none of the SMG's are much good anyway (too low damage, compared to speed), so I started using the Aleph at ql100, just so I could pack a higher minimum damage when joining up with people well above my level (guild trips).

The Michal Patriot looks nice for it's quality level at first glance, until you realize that it's got much higher requirements for use than the other SMG's that ql .. basically a "downmarked" gun. Might be nice for the eventual ql cap at 200.There are also quite a few that swears to suppressors at high level, because of the Full Auto (another minimum damage alleviation)

The only "sweeeeet" SMG ingame that I know of, is the Gamma Ejector, which can only be found in the static mission in Longest Road, and only come in one quality level - which means : they'll be very nice for a time, but when you have to abandon it, you won't find any (to my knowledge) good dual wieldable options, and will have spent a fortune of IP's for dual wield. This will be an unused skill until some other gun like this is introduced into the game world.
