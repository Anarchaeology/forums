Player (ARK?) run duel events!
With the inevitable booster pack resulting in land control, I have a great idea for weekly (daily?) events that people can participate in for money and experience, maybe even items. They will include team kin of the hills and capture the flag type games. This is what I have in mind:

Tower Lords (King-of-the-Hill style game)

In Tower Lords, 2-8 teams of 6 people each battle through a monster (and ARK?) infested land to reach a tower. All teams have a special color (it's a 0 cost NCU buff that eminates your team's color) to decipher who is who. Inside the tower, they must hold it for up to 10 minutes. If someone dies, they respawn (with NO rez sickness, exp loss, or body to reclaim) back at the start and must again battle through monsters to reach the center tower. If they make it to the tower and save at their team's insurance terminal and die, they will respawn outside of the tower, and only need to make it back to the top to hold the tower. The tower will be guarded by monsters (and ARKs?) that respawn every time a holder is killed. In order to "hold" the tower, you must click on a special terminal. Your team's color will apear and cover the tower in a glowing shell to show who is currently holding the tower. Only one person from a team can hold the tower at any given time. The holder's name will be able to be seen and read through walls and from the beginning starting point. The holder's name will be the color of the team. For this reason you cannot give colored names to your bots, it would too easy to confuse attackers. In order to revert the tower back to a neutral status, you must kill the holder. When the holder is killed, then you can click the terminal and hold the tower for your team. For this reason, remember to ALWAYS let the strongest teammate hold the tower. Making your 1200HP NT hold it would be a bad idea when he has 3 2400HP MAs on his ass, so this would require strategy. Depending on the amount of teamns playing, the time you must hold the tower can range from 2 minutes to 10 minutes. 2 well balanced teams going at it together would be 10 minutes since it'll be much easier to keep the holder alive. However, 8 teams would be 2, since the holder of the tower could be r@ped by 14 or so people and die in 3 seconds Flying vehicles and the MP's flying nano are not allowed in these games and thus could disqualify your team if you use them. Only run buffs and land/water vehicles are allowed. These teams should be thought out well before these things begin, so that you have exactly who you need: A doc, a tank, a holder, and mezzer/rooter should be the bare minimum (the tank can act as holder, of course). You must sign your team up before this starts. Price to enter depends on lvl range of your team. Rewards include money and boss loot or experience.

Can't think of name (Capture-the-Flag style game)

The CTF is a tournament style game. Up to 16 teams of 6 people each will battle one-on-one for at least 10 minutes each game to see who can capture the opposing team's item the most. All teams will be given a color that is constantly eminating frm them to decipher who is who. The item is basically like "Red's Flag" or whatever. If you pick up the opposing side's flag, your character's scail is increase by 500% to show who the carrier is. The carrier then must return to his or her base and hand the flag to a terminal or NPC. The flag will be NODROP. If a flag holder dies, the flag is automatically returned to the flag owner's base. Unlike Tower Lords, the use of any sort of vehicle or the use of the MP flying nano is restricted and could call for disqualification. In Tower Lords you could use land or water, you cannot here. If you beat your oppoising side, your team is reembursed for the fee they spent to join the event. Depending on the size of the tournament, people can get moneym, experience, or even boss loot after defeating another team. If you are the winner of the tournament, your team is givin experience and money depending on their levels plus choice of boss loot items near their QL. You must sign your team up before the deadline (15 minutes? a day?) to enter these. Price to enter depends on lvl range of team.

Not sure if these would work, but lemme know what ya think!
