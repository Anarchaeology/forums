Gambling, Casino's, and lets all go to the Races!
Ok, a fair bit of this is off other RPG's/MMORPG's.
I always thought the addition of a casino, or some way of gambling would be good. Not sure if many of you've played it but Fallout used to have Casinos, they were pretty basic just slot machines but you could sit around and recover your hp while wasting cash in these. You some times won, sometimes didn't but it was a nice change of scenery. Lineage: Blood Pledge (MMORPG) has slime races...these where very cool. In that instance they had race tracks at various locations on the planet where you would go along and see a couple of NPCs, one u placed bets with, and another who gave you the form of the competitors. Thier implimentation was nice as the favourate (had as expected poor odds of making money) didn't always win, it all came down to which slime had been raced too much, which might be the natural fastest, and basic luck and a few other things. It used to work great...and what was really sweet was as always, overall the winner usually was the people taking the bets, not those placing it hehe. But it was social, and fun!

So I was thinking, wouldn't it be kinda cool to say have gambling events on Rubi-ka? Maybe Leet races kinda like the slime races in implementation. Or maybe boxing matches? Have two Ninjadroids beat the living daylights out of each other hehe.

Just an idea for some more "gold sinks".
