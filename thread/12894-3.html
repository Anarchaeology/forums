Just as a side note you mentioned that MK X is too powerful. Thats not true at all. Rather a soldier is the only class with enough defensive power to survive the over-powered damage of pvp while rooted - long enough to zone.

Damage is the problem, not defensiveness.
Normal fight: Bang bang bang (and maybe one more bang) dead.
Vs soldier fight: Bang, bang, bang, bang, bang, bang, bang, bang, (roots finally broken) zone.

Crit nerf should help to fix this problem. Instead of MK X reducing 900 damage a hit (from a 1.2k crit), it will usually be reducing 150 damage a hit (from 200 normal damage). In essence you have your nerf of TMS right there. Over 80 seconds the difference between adding 20k hp (as Snarf used to say) and adding 5k is a big one. Everyone fights and survives longer, everyone's happy.

My point is that the effectiveness of TMS is directly proportional to the amount of damage being done per second against it. Less damage against the soldier = less effectiveness for TMS.
