Soldiers love, finally - BUT.......
are you kidding with us FunCom????

Look at the requirements of this Nanos:
NanoCrystal (Soldier Clip Junkie)
NanoCrystal (Swiss Cheese)
NanoCrystal (Metal Barrage)

Soldiers will never be able to cast the 1st nano. SI/PM is base capped at ~660, QL200 Implants will add either 207/42 SI/PM (PM/SI) or 147/102 SI/PM (PM/SI), because SI and PM are in conflict. So the maximum is 867/702 PM/SI (SI/PM) or 807/762 PM/SI (SI/PM) at level 200. Even with 140 Mochams and 20 Expertise its absolutely impossible to get more than 922 (+/- a few pts.) in the lower skill. And you will always need a Trader or a MP to cast the other 2 nanos.

Im lvl 182 now and wouldnt be able to selfcast this nano:
NanoCrystal (Metal Strike)
My nanoskilles are capped at 621 and with ql200 Implants and expertises I could get 753 in the lower skill.

Ok, I dont tell you about the 3 pts. damage improvement in this nano, which is an average damage improvement of 0.0033% for my level with average damage of 900 (Hellspinner), which means 553-1353 instead 550-1350 damage on mobs. Even the add of 10 pts, as in the Soldier Clip Junkie, is an improvement of only 0.011%. The Ranged Init add of 15 pts. makes me able to shoot 0.025 seconds faster, because buffs only take effect on the attack, but not the recharge of the Inits... and even if this will be fixed in 14.6, I would shoot 0.075 seconds faster - WOOT.

Now, lets take a loot at this nano:
NanoCrystal (Art of peace)
The effects are:
+70 Assault Rifle which most likely wont stack with AR Mastery, so you only get 10 pts. more.....
+5 in every damage... I told you above what that means....
+10 in Burst
838/838 PM/SI requirements and a lvl 155 for this 'huge' improvement makes me speechless...really

And here are the other nanos:
NanoCrystal (A sergeants knowledge)
NanoCrystal (Alleysweeper)

NanoCrystal (Power Volley)
NanoCrystal (The Power of Three)

These nanos might be usefull, especially the shotgun line and some FA buffs. I wont judge about them until I see them ingame.

Ok now lets see what highlvl soldiers got - these soldiers are the one with the most problems, not the medium or lower soldiers...
  • We get useless +100 FA, ridiculous damage modifier and RI modifier, with horrible requirements (which we can never meet) and the cost of 50 NCUs
  • We get usefull +92 FA, ridiculous damage modifier and RI modifier, with horrible requirements and the cost of 43 NCUs
  • We get a usefull +70 Shotgun and ridiculous +2 Fling, with horrible requirements and the cost of 45 NCUs
  • We get a useless (if it really wont stack with AR Mastery) +10 (+70 - 60 AR Mastery) Assault Rifle, ridiculous damage modifier and Burst modifier, with horrible requirements and the cost of 24 NCUs more (AR Mastery 16)
  • some really usefull lower-medium level buffs for Shotgun, Burst, FA, AR, which wont make us really better in combat, but we can equip a little bit higher guns earlier in our career.


Currently I need 350 NCU of my 448 for my own buffs. 1 more FA buff and Im not able to carry outsidebuffs from other professions, because I have to keep 45 NCUs free for my TMS MK X.

So whats the improvement for higher levels here? Its a bit higher FA skill for the cost of a filled up NCU Memory, which makes us unable to carry outside buffs. Am I excited? yes, but not for the good...
The only thing I would try is the shotgun line and if it makes me able to use shotgun-style of combat, but we will see...

I dont whine here, but I state my disappointment.
