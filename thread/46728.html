What I think is the point is that Urbanmech thinks that if FC were to come out with two seperate (at least) ways of marketing this box, it would come out to be less expensive for him, and that's what he wants, as anyone does, the most for the least expenditure.

The fact that marketing this in two (at least) seperate ways would lead to even higher production, packaging and marketing costs doesn't enter in to this logic at all.

I posted this earlier in a thread that I believe was subsequently deleted, but here is the bottom line:

1 - the company manufacturing a product (any product) determines how they will sell that product - in what quantities and so on;

2 - consumers decide if the product is worth it to them at the price dictated by the company.

That's really all there is to it. I'm pretty sure that when each of us starts marketing our games, we'll have infinite ways to obtain it available, but for now, we will just have to settle for what is offered and base our decisions on that.
