Lorddoctor

Yes, Doctors are a critical part of the team. The reason they get shut out of a lot of loot is because they are also a time intensive character during missions. Instread of wandering to get chests and things docs are dropping down to recharge nano's etc.

If loot is alpha you can sometimes get your loot from a mob but when the team runs on I find myself often forgetting that and doing my duty to the team.

When boss loot is distributed the best armor etc is often given to other profs than the doc. They use excuses like "You don't do damage and don't draw aggro, I need this armor" or "You don't solo so your team mates can get mobs off you" etc. Docs use as much nano as any prof out there, are expected to keep up with all of the latest and greatest heals etc. It is an expensive prof and yet docs can barely solo for credits (some can't solo at all). Most of my chars soloed a lot and so got the armor and loot off the mobs to fund themselves. This is not available for docs unless you play a special type (I played an MA Atrox and could solo 50-60% missions until level 100 or so).

So docs are a poor prof and are often overlooked in loot distribution. I have always been a fan of team tipping docs but most say they are just doing their job. I see it as a way of compensating them for forgoing the solo experience in prof choice.

So make sure when loot is distributed from the boss that you ask for your fair share or more. Explain to the others just what the reality of doc hood is.
