FlameThrower Improvements
Recently I decided to try a new gun with my level 81 soldier, something instead of the good ol' Nova Flow. I purchased a QL 99 Flamethrower.

While the flamethrower is not bad, it is certainly not as good as the nova flow. They fire at the same rate, but the Flamethower has no special attacks and uses more expensive ammo. Monsters do not seem to take more damage whether one shoots engery attacks or fire attaks at them. My flamethower does 7-195 damage, while my Nova Flow does 1-176 damage. However, due to burst and fling-- the Nova is the clear winner.

To improve on the flamethower, I suggest a different style of attack rather than a basic increase in special attacks or higher damage output. Some flamers come with special attacks, but not mine. Anyway, the idea is simple: since flamers are spitting out flames for damage, their attack should have a small area effect. For instance, the main target monster would take 100% of possible damage from an attack, while all monsters within one yard would receive two thirds of the damage. Say I hit one Mutant for three hundred fire damage, two right next to him would receive 200 damage each.

I feel the area damage should only effect monsters and not PvsP. I do not feel that a small area damage would unbalance anything, this is just a way to improve on a gun that no one uses, because all the advantage is put into a standard Nova Flow. Instead of making a variety of Novas with the same stats and attacks, I say the actual style of certain gun's attack should change. In the case of flamers: area damage.
