COA and the COT
Both the COT and the COA have their good and bad sides respectively. The COT has the experience, and they are the ones currently in the most control. The COA, has little experience and little control, but their call to arms is alluring to most of the clans in this time of struggle. This is because of the current vacuum of power and leadership. Radiman has been out of sight for to long and has done too little of late, his ability to lead us has started to fail because he has left, and shown himself very little. He has not provided anything to the clans of late except unanswered questions. The people are sick of it and the COA is stepping in and starting to provide that leadership, they are answering the questions of the people and giving a course of action. Their ranks swell by the hour, but they are hard pressed to truly unite the clans. Many of the people are still hanging on to the hope that Radiman will step in and make everything better. His leadership of the COT is detrimental to the clans. He is dividing us through his inaction, and he has done through his actions of late except to make things worse. I hereby officially call for the official resignation of Henry Radiman from the COT and the leadership of the Clans. It is time for new blood within the leadership who can hopefully unify us, and give us direction, whetther is be the COA the COT or a merging of the two, one thing is clear, the time for a change in leadership has come. Radiman's time is over.

I can understand the ooc reason for creating the CoA. I can appreciate growing frustration among players as the story has been on hold for so long. I can also appreciate players wanting to take part and shape the storyline as this was meant to be a feature of AO. I don't necessarily share these views, but I can certainly sympathise and even support it just out of solidarity to fellow roleplayers.

The in-character justification for the CoA though seems a little flimsy. The reasons given for the CoA's formation suggest some sort of national emergency. We are told that "time has come for action" and because the inactivity of the CoT, the plight of the clans is getting worse with each passing day. To be honest, I just don't see that. Granted everyone's in game experience and roleplaying stance is different, but in so far as there is an objective, shared reality in AO, things have been going pretty well for the clans. It kind of ironic, but it is precisely because the story is on hold that we have all enjoyed (as characters if not as players) a prolonged period of peace and stability. Sure there are the odd border skirmishes and raids, but lets be honest, OT is not doing anything the clan are not doing as well and in the scheme of things these skirmishes don't amount to much. As Bionitrous put it, this could well be a Golden Age for the clans. I would not have thought that clamouring for a change in leadership would ellicit a lot of support from the masses at this specific time (again from a purely in-character perspective).

On a more personal note, I do find it a little sad players choose to vent their ooc frustration on Radiman who all in all is a very likeable NPC. I feel like saying, "Hey, its not Radiman's fault FC don't have the resources or inclination to give him a more active role." But I reckon how people feel about NPCs is really none of my business.
