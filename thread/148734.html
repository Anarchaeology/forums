Apology for actions of former MD
An Applicant known as 'Perfectangel' has been released due to unappropiate behavior toward officers and females in our organization.

We suspected him a few days ago and began an investigation (with the possibility of a Court Martial or immediate release), which ended early than expected, as he was foolish enough to act up in our clan channel while a few officers were listening today.

We do our best to root out anyone unfit to be known as a Mercury Dragon, so that meeting a member of our clan is a pleasant and memorable experience.
