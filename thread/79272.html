Actually, most orgs probably wouldn't bother building bases higher than QL 40 or lower than QL 200, if this were the case. Anything but the lowest towers and the highest ones are too easily killed on a whim to justify bothering to have them if they are going to be vulnerable when you aren't around to defend.

The tower bonuses are nice, but what's the point of having them if you never benefit from them and if you aren't around to fight for them? Build a base, log off, come back and your towers were destroyed without your ever having fought there.

Result? You blew a few hundred thousand credits per person in your org, blew a few hours making a nice base, then never benefitted from the bonuses that are supposed to make the towers attractive to the general PvM population, and you never got to fight around them, which is what was supposed to make the towers attractive to PvPers. So in other words, almost nobody will do it.

And since almost nobody will do it, you'll have even fewer people to fight. At least as things stand, if you attack a base, you're reasonably sure that some people will show up to defend it.

But building a nice base takes time. Ignore the credits, they are pretty much trivial. Why put time into building something that you won't benefit from in any way? Most people would go back to leveling in missions instead of fighting around towers.
