Camping out mission terminals is boring!
Why are we forced to camp the mission terminals to get anything decent as a mission reward?

It's sad that in the past month I have gotten all of maybe three missions from the mission terminals that yielded some item that was actually relavent to my proffesion, and my level.

It seems odd that funcom would want us to spend more than a couple of minutes doing what is quite possibly the most boring thing in the game, getting missions.

So, what gives? Why have they made good mission rewards so elusive? Why is it that as a Level 74 Meta most of the rewards that come up are for other proffesions, or below the level of equipment and nano crystals that I'm already using? Shouldn't the rewards be based on the person who is querrying the mission terminal? Who wants to spend even 10 to 15 minutes looking at a mission terminal, only to come up with missions that give junk loot?

It's not like the dev team doesn't know what items the different proffesions want... i.e. nano crystals, armor, etc.

So, here it is. At level 74 I can't solo a lvl 90 mission. I'm lucky to get through a lvl 81-85 because combat is unfairly biased in favor of the MOBs. Furthermore, at my level most of the equipment I use is atleast lvl 90 or better. So, I'm stuck getting missions at levels that are lower than the equipment I use, so even when the rewards are in line with my proffesion, the equipment is often too low a level for me to use. Why bother? I mean, really, what's the point?

It seems an extraordinary waste of time to spend a half an hour camping a solo mission term with its obscure and apparently ineffectual sliders, trying to get a mission that is probably going to be too difficult for on level 74 meta. As a meta I don't want missions that yield crat junk, or any other proffesions junk. Furthermore, I don't want to have to rely on the good will of others to sell me meta junk that they get from missions at a reasonable price, seeing as it barely ever shows up as a mission reward for me anyway.

A good example are anima nanos. Since their introduction into the mission loot system I have only found one of these nanos, and the one that I found had requirements that I could have met 20 levels before. If I try to buy the same nanos on the streets I might as well kiss a cool million good bye! So, it's not even an option!

Ultimately, the current method of loot distribution is far to limiting. I don't see the harm in actually giving people what they need as mission rewards. Funcom's only deffence in the matter would have to be to deny that players don't equip items which are above their level. We all know that this is absurd, as funcom is constantly trying to find ways to limit our ability to "over-equip". So, they are well aware that a level 74 meta has no use for a level 90 pet. More importantly, the syatem knows exactly what we have in our inventory all the time. Why offer something that we already have as a reward. Sure this would be fine if it was the exception but not the rule.

It should be common to find rewards that are actually rewarding. I haven't played much in the past couple of days because of this. It's just plain annoying, and no fun at all to have to camp out at a mission terminal and keep dropping dimes until something decent pops out, if it ever does. As for me, I haven't seen anything worthwhile in a few days...



Please consider what I'm saying Funcom...
