13.6 Patch and the Delay
This message went out from Dai-Galean to community sites just now, and we wanted to post it here as well.

quote:
We know you are all wondering why patch 13.6 has been delayed for so long.

Well, here is the latest update: I was informed today there would be a 5% chance the patch will be approved this week and here are the reasons why:

The new static dungeon, Camelot, is the main reason the patch has been delayed. This is the first time we have made a dungeon with two different levels of suppression gas, and this combination has caused a few unforeseen problems. In addition, the dungeon is rather complex on the world builder side, so tweaking it has taken longer than we anticipated. Its also the home of the toughest boss-mob yet to visit Rubi-Ka, and we have had to spend a little time making him fearsome enough for everyone to be adequately intimidatedJ

In addition we have gotten more feedback from the players on Test Live then we have ever done before. This has been taken into consideration, and we have had to implement features and fixes accordingly.

So you all wonder: "ok, Dai what does this mean, after that fancy explanation". Well basically no patch this week but we are working hard to fix the issues that were brought up by the testers on Test Live - and by all good fortune, patch will be next week.
