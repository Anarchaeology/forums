Looking for a clan. Details inside.
I recently took up Funcoms offer of a free month after cancelling my account 4 months ago (I played both beta 4 and some time after release). Before leaving I was clan leader of a small group called Rubi-Ka's Hope, but unfortunatly the whole clan left and is non-existant today.

So I find myself in want of joining a new clan. First a small list of things that I look for in a clan:

- Mature players that focus on fun more than power.

- Many european based members (I live in Denmark)

- Focus on personality rather than level (I hate those exclusive "level 80+ only uberguilds")

- Encourages roleplaying. I dont mean enforce it, but simply encourage it.

- Preferably, you are on the clan side. But I am willing to switch sides if the there is an Omni guild that suits the other points.

These are not ultimate demands, but simply to give you an idea of what I am looking for.

What can I offer?

I am very active in supporting my clan and helping those that are less experienced than myself. I can support myself and wont ask for or expect handouts from higher level clanmates.

To celebrate my return to AO, I rolled up an enforcer and have leveled up to 14 so far. This is not a lot and it will be a long time before I hit level 100+, so thats why I need a clan that focuses on fun and friendship more than power.

I am 27 years old and an experienced MMORPG gamer (played all of AC, EQ, UO and DAoC). Mature, honest and a casual roleplayer who enjoys a good story as much as a good night of clan fun.

Send me a tell if you still haven't joined a clan. I lead the Free Spirits of Rubi-Ka, and it sounds like you'd fit in quite well.
We're a pretty new clan with only around 20 members currently. Of course this lets everyone, even the newest recruit, get a lot of influence in shaping the clan.

To be brutally honest, we don't care at all about levels. I once had to kick someone from the clan because he thought he should be a leader, just because he was higher lvl than me
Currently we're all below lvl 50, simply because we want to have the clan established and having plenty of people around at our own level, before we start recruiting anyone around lvl 200 (Who probably wouldn't join anyway)

I live in Denmark too, and we've got plenty of other Europeans (surprisingly many from scandinavia, actually)

We don't put a lot of demands on our members, instead leaving it up to them to decide how much they want to participate. For the same reason, we're very relaxed about recruiting. Pretty much anyone can join if they want to, as long as we think they're nice people

Our clan structure is pretty simple too. When we get to know a new recruit (usually after a week or so), we promote him to advisor status. He is then just as much leader as me, and is allowed to recruit or kick members, and anything else leaders are supposed to do. Yep, this doesn't sound very organised, but the funny thing is, so far it's worked.

Everyone who's been promoted to advisor has taken a lot of interest in running the clan, and it's worked at least as efficiently as other clans where there is a big fixed hierachy of leaders, second-in-commands, and other more or less powerful positions within the clan.

We don't have a website yet, though we're working on it, but send me a tell (To Parakeet) if you want to know more, or just want togive us a try.
