screaming fits
Okay, I sincerly hope that in the next patch or 2 you give us the option to turn OFF auto attack. As a trader, I can tell you it can be increadably hard to calm multiple mobs when I am constantly being FORCED to attack cause a mob attacked me. And of course, hitting q to stop the attack is useless since you will attack again when you get hit again.
Also, please fix it so that your own team mates will not show up as an attackable target. If they are in your team, they are not something you will want to attack (well, okay, mayber there is the occasional idiot you want to blow away).
And last, but not least, here is my screaming fit. WTF! were you thinking when you introduced Drill Inspectors!! Was someone on the team smoking something and said, 'Hey lets make a lvl 150ish mob that hits as hard as a lvl 170 Tough mob and fast as a borg. Then we can make it a lvl 130ish mob and give it an attack skill high enough that it can constantly crit'. I can normally sneak/calm a mission up to lvl 160ish. Now a 130ish mission should not give me much trouble. I can understand dying 1-2 times, but I have died nearly a dozen times and even got a decent team around my lvl (103) and we got slaughtered by the Drill Inspectors.
Now, I know Funcom claims that they test out everything before they release it from the test server and put it on the live servers, but honestly, no one believes that line and unbalanced mobs like this proves it.

P.S. I hope you have a bunch of new people ready to sign up when you release the OE patches cause if it gimps people as bad as expected, you will lose alot of customers. I know you want to make it fair for new players, but like any other game, the longer you play it, the more resources you accumilate, after all, a person who plays 5+ hours a day will have better resources and characters then someone who plays 2 hrs a day, and trying to stomp out OE just cause some PVP people are unhappy is just crazy. My first serious character is a bit gimped but by my 3rd serious character, I have learned how to find stuff, what sort of missions to take, how often to upgrade armor and weapons and the best ones to use. If you want to really help newbies, then how about dumping most of the useless weapons out there and giving a better help guide and pointing them to websites that can give detailed steps on leveling a character until they can find thier own playstyle. Well, said more then I expected on this P.S. part, but meant it all.
