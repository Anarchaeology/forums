I believe the booster will be a fiasco and will cause a lot of players to feel cheated when they realise that they will not be able to keep their tower for any longer that it will take the gas to cycle. Towers will be camped, guaranteed. Small to middle sized factions will not stand a chance of keeping a tower up. The booster will sell, but it will also piss a lot of players off...

The booster pack is also probably one of the reasons that a lot of zones got switched to 75% since no one in his/her right mind would build a tower in a 25% zone. I guess that the booster is also a very big reason why they want to nerf the LLTS.
Expansions and boosters should not affect the existing world in this way, they should be designed to work within the current game rules.

With FC's track record of not being able to balance hardly anything in the game, why should they suddenly succeed with something as big as this booster pack?
Then there is the question of where and how many towers you will be able to place in each zone. I read an interesting comment in an article somewhere that 'in some playfields more than one tower can be placed...' Beats me if they mean one tower per faction or just one tower in total for each playfield, I'll guess we'll find out.

It will also be interesting to see how many of these booster packs the community will be able to raise anything resembling enthusiasm for. The response for the first one hasn't been overwhelming to say the least and one has to wonder how many game clients of AO one person will need during a lifetime.

The idea of the booster is too convoluted. Towers that makes the suppression gas cycle, how pressed for ideas can you be? Why focus the pack on something that the majority of players never have done and according to many, is way too unbalanced? Why focus on PvP without fixing it first in the main game?

The new Yalmaha designs in the game seems like a tacked on feature, it's there because so many players have asked for it. These new vehicles could just as easily have been made available in a regular patch, but now they want to sell boosters...

Sorry for the rant but I think that the booster idea in itself is a cheap way of making fast cash without considering the long term effects it will have on the game, think 12.6 and you will have an idea.
It seems that AO is built around a very rigid set of rules, that is very hard to balance anything without causing numerous new little bugs and glitches and I'm afraid that this will cause havoc and be the subject of several emergency patches.
