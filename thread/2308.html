Are the factions being played properly by players and funcom?
Personally, I think the current faction system is broken/unbalanced in functional game terms as well as in RP terms. I appologize for the length of the following post, but beg your indulgence.

When initially creating my first character for AO, I read up on the differences between OT and the Clans and decided to go clan for a couple different reasons. Personally, I like to be able to go my own way, associate with those people I desire, and have my policy decided by a leader who isn't at the top of a massive bureaucracy and has been voluntarily selected by all of that groups members. As I understood it, people who choose Omni-Tek are allowed to keep their individuality, and may even privately disagree with the decisions the company makes, but public disagreement and blatant disregard for company policy and insubordination would not be tolerated as in any military force or major corporation including Omni-Tek. Employees would have to tow the company line and act in accordance their employers it if they wanted to keep their jobs within the company. They would also be allowed some organization on a guild like level, but always with their focus being that of Omni-Tek at large. In return for these restrictions, people in Omni-Tek would be afforded better and cheaper equipment and the support of the organization at large. All very fine and good, but not the type of character I had in mind.

The clans on the other hand, are allowed to either strike out and be completely independent or organize themselves to whatever degree they so desire, with the only enforcing body being themselves, their corresponding organization or the clans all uniting against a rogue group or individual. They would not have access to the same equipment as Omni-Tek because they are not a member of the Corp and don't have the finances to have such supply, but they would also be able to have specific group advantages for getting organized into guilds and clans.

However, in game, the situation is VERY different. To dispense with the obvious first, Omni-Tek has their advantage, but the clans don't. This is blatantly unfair, I don't care how hard it is to code clan only guild advantages, you should have held off on the omni only equip until those advantages were ready in the interest of fairness. However, the problems in the current faction system runs deeper than game mechanics and affects the role playing element/setting of the game as well.

Apparently, its okay for Omni-Tek employee's to not only disregard direct orders from their employer, publicly lambaste him and their fellow employees, but they suffer NO punishment or censure for any of it. I have the Black Alliance in mind here. While not a specific group, they are a collection of guilds that have publicly announced a directly contrary opinion to that of Philip Ross, yet they still, in game, retain all the rights and advantages of the entire support of Omni-Tek. Funcom, how in the world can you allow to continue? If they secretly harbored these feelings, and managed to gank the occasional Clanner during the Amnesty, that would be one thing, and perfectly in the spirit of RP. As it stands now, however, these groups seem to be simply people using their in game affiliation with Omni-Tek to get the equipment advantage while completely disregarding their obligations to the company that are supposed to come hand in hand. Aren't these type of alliances also prohibited within the company? Isn't anything that goes against the interests of Omni-tek supposed to be dealt with?

An example of Omni guilds doing their own thing yet still being in harmony with the policy of Ross would be Div9, who entered into the Athen Accords as a step toward peace with the Clans. This isn't in contradiction to their employment with Omni-Tek, in fact it is in keeping with the general policy of the amnesty ordered by Philip Ross.

Given this current state of affairs, I have to ask myself, why in the world should I be clan and have none of the in game perks currently available, when I could go Omni, still be able to do and say whatever I wanted and also have access to better/cheaper equipment?

These rogue factions of Omni-tek should be punished or kicked from omni-tek, even if they say they are doing it "in the name of Omni-Tek." Those groups affiliated with the Black Alliance would be better suited as a Clan guild anyway, since the clans aren't all supposed to be in harmony with each other, but are supposed to have elements within the faction that want to fight with all the other clans, and not align themselves with the CoT.

Maybe you should create a 4th faction affiliation of independants who wouldn't receive either the (future)clan or (current)omni benefits, but would be able to attack whoever they wanted in political zones as well as mayhem zones. This faction would still be distinct from Neutrals, because neutrals are trying to avoid conflict whereas the independants don't care. Anyway, maybe this wouldn't do, but its not my job to think of solutions, but you have a big problem here consisting of a very real imbalance between Clans and Omni-tek and a story related inconsistency regarding what working for Omni-Tek would really require. Omni-Tek employees shouldn't be required to be mindless stooges of Ross and Company, but they should suffer harsh repercussions if they engage in actions and rhetoric that are in direct conflict with the policy and spirit of the company. Any good military or company doesn't permit the level of disension and insubordination within the ranks that Omni-Tek is currently allowing in game. At a bare minimum, don't allow them to enjoy all the benefits without paying any of the costs, otherwise faction affiliation is meaningless.

If I am completely misinterpreting the purpose of factional alignment in game, however, please Cosmik or anyone at Funcom, enlighten me as to how its supposed to work in game and Role Playing terms. I'm willing to listen.

Leet
I haven't been arguing that Omni-Tek can't Roleplay the way they like, but there should be consequences for their actions. Also, they are not "criticising" him, they are blatantly ignoring him and questioning his intelligence. Additionally, omni-tek is more than just a corporation in the contemporary sense...it is a military-industrial structure, otherwise it wouldn't be running around with tanks, armored soldiers, etc... Even leaving out the military aspect that is there, if you work for a company, the boss tells you to do something and you do the opposite while screaming that he's an idiot, there will be consequences. Currently, there are none which means the distinction between the Omni's and Clanners means little more than who you can target during PvP because one of the fundamental philosophical differences is pointless. What are the clans fighting for if not to have a free government, not a corporate dicatorship (dictatorship taken in the sense like running a whole planet as a company, with the rules of a corp in place of the civil freedoms enjoyed by democratic governments)? All IC rhetoric aside, this is a central RP issue between the two factions.

As to Omni being evil. While it seems that Funcom has toned down Omni-Tek's menace quite a bit, I remember reading interview after interview with the design staff stating that while the outcome of the story would be open, it would quite easily boil down to the idea that Omni-tek=bad guys and Clan=good guys. This may be a root of many people's impression that Omni is evil in the extreme.

I don't like this simple of a distinciton, but this was Funcom's original intent. However, it doesn't seem that they've decided to push the evil so much as a corporate dictatorship. So we can't hold them to this traditional evil/good distinction as easily.

The comparison of the CoT to Boss Ross, and the idea that omni's have as much latitude as clanners is just wrong, or at least doesn't fit with the setting Funcom has provided. The clans are supposed to be all over the map, whereas Omni's should act like a company: Some internal disagreement, but a common policy and goal uniting their actions with punishments for those that step outside their bounds. The gray area you talk about is within these bounds, not outside it.

When it comes to dissension among the clans, that is to be expected and perfectly within the bounds of the story...the council of truth isn't equivalent to Boss Ross, they only stand for those clans that align themselves with the CoT whereas Ross is quite literally the Boss of all Omni's. If an omni-tek employee doesn't like it, they should leave or try to change it internally, which is not what they are doing.

I don't agree with these radical groups in game, but they aren't beholden to the CoT in the way that any omni-guild is obligated to listen to Ross.

Now, to Cpt.Fallout, I have no problem with some stat pluses going to all guilds, but there have to be superior clan ones, and I'd actually prefer them having to be earned rather than bought, but thats another issue.

And yes, I do contend that any discount amounts to a decent advantage because that REALLY begins to add up when you take into account every save, and the exhorbitant price that Funcom has set things at. This adds up and I can tell you how. I have two chars who recently bought a full set of implants, one with about 75 pt higher comp lit then the other. When I bought a set of implants from the store the one with the better comp lit saved over 20k on the implants alone, and over 60k on the clusters. 80k easily, at a lvl where 80k is a hell of a lot of money, 4 or 5 missions worth of work at the lvl I'm talking about. This is just one instance of a discount making a huge difference, so yes, every little bit adds up. Its less time soloing missions just to keep some money in your bank account and affording decent equipment. Considering the fact that soloing is one of the most boring aspects of the game, I consider this a significant advantage. And even if the omni only armor isn't the best in the game, its available earlier (at least as mission rewards for a clanner ) than much of the decent other styles of armor. And why do you think Funcom had to tweak how easy it was to change factions? It was because so many clanners were switching just for the armor. Would they do this if there was no value in doing it?

Is all of this the worst travesty in the world, no, but its still unfair considering the clans have nothing at all so far and Omni's have been running around with ingame advantages for months.
