Competitions December 16 for all Clan and neutrals
Greetings citizens of Rubi-ka!

As a proud member of the clan Revolution i have the pleasure to invite you to some great events.

The events will start on December 16 @ 22:00 GMT on RUBI-KA-1 and are open to clanners and neutrals.

1. For lvl`s 1-50:
The event will start from Avalon mission terminals @ 22:00 GMT and will have something to do with looting,details on the spot.
Reward for 1st place is 250.000 credits;2nd and 3rd place will also receive rewards.

2. For lvl`s 51-100:
The event will start from Avalon mission terminals @ 22:15 GMT and will involve some running,details on the spot.
Reward for 1st place is 1.000.000 credits;2nd and 3rd place also get rewards.

3. For lvl`s 101-200 The event will start at TIR Arena @ 22:30 GMT.The event will consist in a good old free for all arena-rumble.The players will enter the arena in groups of 10-20 depending on how many will be there.The reward for 1st place is 2.000.000 credits;2nd and 3rd also get rewards.

The rewards will be given when all events are finished-around 23:30 GTM in the Happy Rebel, Tir grid, where we all get together for a beer afterwards.

If u want to be a part of this events send a /tell to cyberdeath including ur lvl.

Good luck to u all and see u online.
