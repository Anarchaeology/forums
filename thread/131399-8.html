I rarely if ever demean myself from answering flame fest threads however I will make an exception here and sum things up from my perspective as leader of Tranquility.

Approximately 1½ month ago the first omni loot bots starting running full time at Smug/Fred and the result was devastating to the "normal" raiding OT guilds. From the second week of that bot Smug/Fred was effectively chain killed and regular raid guilds like Legion, MR and Tranq with far less raw manpower was effectively blocked out for the next month.

No matter how loot bot owners percieve themselves they are now sitting upon the largest single group of active high level OT players, but without any or atleast a very slight social bond between them.

The core issues from our perspective is this.

1) Loot bots "poach" guild people killing guild raid activity.
2) Due to the high number of people and point 1. they can effectively outcamp regular guilds.
3) Players frequenting loot bots are substituting guild chat with that of the loot bot. I often see people joining those channels just as they log on and conduct themselves as they would in normal guild chat.

Why is this a problem at all is a common responce by the owners and players of these loot bots. Well their entrance to mercs was no better and no worse of any other normal guild going there for the first time. To tell the truth these issues have been there every single time a new group be it guild or loot bot have entered ace. However as a loot bot regular people have no clue about who to talk to as there is no leadership and little or no loyalty between the people there. So where do we voice our concerns? On these forums of course.

But that is just a minor issue compared to the real problem loot bots represent. And that is that they are taking over the guild responsibilities of providing raids, social interaction and fellowship to the guild members. This again wouldnt at all be as big a problem without the tiny aspect called NW's. NW's introduced guild bonuses and side bonuses, but are still focused around guilds and as such requires a well functioning guild to be able to enter this content area. SL furthers this concern even more as bases will become even more important in the future.

What we in Tranquility really fail to see is why players that effectively substituted guild chat for loot bot chat dont reorganise themselves into one guild. Most of the regular people on these raid bots fail to realize the extend at which they have substituted guild loyalty with bot loyalty.

Why would we in Tranquility have these concerns and prefer to kill loot bots entirely you might ask. The answer is simple. This would make OT guilds stronger and in turn help the entire side instead of bringing it down as it is now.
