Unique Items
Okay... I do kinda understand the need for the owning only one type of unique thing. But is it really necessary. What if someone was collecting Items to give to their guild or whatnot. If this was initiated for bracers there are other ways of dealing with that problem.

I am bringing this up because I lost a ql 200 Senior Interops Coat because i was already wearing a ql 132 Senior Interops Coat. The damn cloak just deleted itself when I went to pick it up off boss loot. Know I was albiet upset but what upset me more was that there was no warning to let us know this code was included in patch 14.5. The offical notes say nothing on Uniques... only a post by Cz say they will let us know what would be going on with this and it was never said... Just it was added and we are supposed to find out...

I don't feel its a very fair thing to be put into the game. I mean honestly what can be done with Unique items that is so bad you can't have 2 of them. What happends if you find a better Item and you want it but you are wearing something already... how can you pick up the new one... do you delete it.. I think not.. do you give it to someone.. what if you are not with people you really trust? This is a problem I see coming on.. so I just thought I would bring it up.

If I am wrong on something or if someone can let me know about the unique thing please let me know..
