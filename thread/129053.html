Demons in the dark
Gestava sat up in his bed with a tired sigh.
Lately he'd wake up even more exhausted than before he went to sleep.
Gestava wiped a bead of sweat off his forhead.

He'd had that dream again.

He couldnt remember the last time he'd ever dreamt about *anything*. Yet these last few weeks he had dreamed almost every night.
Or perhaps had nightmares would be a better word...

In his dreams there would be angelic looking creatures fighting demonic fiends on a battle field that at the same time seemed very close, yet far far away.

The battle was titanic with bits of the ground itself trembling and disintegrating as the combatents fought. It reminded him of old holo movies he had seen as a child.

But the dreams were far more real than any movie. They seemed to be more than just dreams...memories...or perhaps the future?

Gestava smirked at that thought. He wasnt a man that believed in those kinds of things.

Yet...he couldn't shake the feeling he had been having ever since the dreams started.

The feeling that something terrible was coming...or something fantastic...or both?
