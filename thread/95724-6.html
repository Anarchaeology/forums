Wednesday Night

"...can we meet somewhere?"

It was Braedon. He was concerned over her safety, she having been unseen for about a day now. Raquel confirmed their meeting and reassured him that she'd only been laying a bit low. Threats were threats, while there had been none made to her directly, she knew she was in danger.

Hurriedly, she selected the Athens grid exit and soon her body was reconstructed amid the streets of West Athens. She looked up and saw his form on the roof just across from The Cup, his sabretooth form yawning lazily in the evening sun. She gave him a small wave and he slipped down from the rooftop as his body morphed back into that of a solitus. Raquel smirked, commenting on his highly armoured state.

"Considering things of late... you may see me like this more often. Shall we?" With a nod she accompanied him into the bar, The Cup.

Raquel immediately felt eyes on her as the door swooshed shut behind them. A snort of disgust came from a nearby table made up of two con****uous looking men. The agent sauntered in further, near the bar and was greeted by a friend of a friend, Izobel. They exchanged greetings, even a hug, and Raquel was soon presented with a nice cold beer. She thanked Braedon and followed him to a table tucked into the corner, not far from to the two strange men.

They spoke in low voices very briefly before a sound erupted next to them. One of the men, the taller one, was clearing his throat. Raquel didn't acknowledge him, instead concentrating on her beer. Something else caught her attention too, and she made sure no one saw. Lightly she had pressed a small button on her wrist-comm, and casually studied the results while the two men obviously irritated her company, Braedon.

"Actually, we do. You see, we are a bit lost...," the taller one replied, "...for words, that is." The other man laughed, emphasizing the first's tone.
"I don't have my thesaurus handy, but I'll help if I can. Always willing to help a couple of... Clanners." Braedon retorted. Sezmra smirked very lightly, enjoying the wit of the company she kept tonight.

"Perhaps you should instead keep a copy of the recent grid feed news with you," was the reply.

"Indeed. A great deal actually." Raquel felt eyes dart to her, first the tall man's then his friend's. She turned suddenly to meet their gaze, as if meeting some unspoken challenge. The commotion ended, and Raquel relaxed once again in her seat to continue the reason she had come there: to find out what Braedon had learned...
