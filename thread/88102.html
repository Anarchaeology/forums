Enforcer etiquette - All breeds
Etiquette. What can we set as "normal" etiquette for an enforcer in todays modern Rubika with everything from "kthxbye" to "I'm delighted to recieve your buff. Have a splendid day, kind sir".

Here is a pointer on how you can ract certain situations as an enforcer.

Atrox enforcer:

This is the arnold amongst us. use that in a good way
If anything goes wrong, if you do something wrong or doesn't understand something... even if your'e insecure of anything, the wise things to say are:
"<- Trox", "Me trox" or "mmm.. waffles. I like waffles.. do you?".

Atrox enfs are usually just big teddybears. Loved and all and is allowed to do more mistakes than any other class as they are just.. cute oversized teddies..

Solitus Enforcer:

If you screw up, you screw up. Somehow, the intellect of a solitus is supposed to be higher than with an atrox. if you try "<- enf" you'll be whacked even more. try something like:
"Aaahh.. been spending time with to much trox' lately" or
"God damn trox only items. I'm rerolling"
don't ever use "kthxbye", "fukstfu" or anything in that line.
a solitus have to do more emotes than a trox. stay away from /disco.. it looks ghey.

Opifex enforcer:

You are most likely to be under su****ion of being an FP'ed Agent. Other than that, act like a Solitus. /flamenco as often as you can.
if you have to talk, say something like "The moon is in it's 3rd retrospectacular phase" or "I am not a crook".
Generally, Opifex enfs are cuddly ones too

Nanomage Enforcer:

Allways act mad. Cackle like a Professor just finnishing his doomsday device as often as you can.
should you screw up, sentences like these will see you safe through:
"*Cackle cakcle* i stabbed myself" or "that was a bus, and the driver just got a divorce. I will not be angry".


Generally, we don't need to speek up so much as enfs.. the little we say, people tend to listen to anyway.

Enfs don't brag too much, they don't whine too much.
