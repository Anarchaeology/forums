quote:
Green atrributes.
What about em? With the ability to OE things so easily (even with a 20% window), there is rarely a need to have hugely-high stats (i'm talking 500+, where green skills and breed caps come into play). Having an extra couple of points on a weapon you can already wield within the OE limit is usually IP wasted. Because of OE and the fairly limtied effect that attrs have on a skill ( 0.25 contribution at most on 100% determined skills), "average" for the mostpart is the best strategy: Solitus.

There are a few RARE exceptions to this, such as NT's who want to wield flashpoints (which is totally against Funcom's intended design). Because NTs have a cap on full auto and AR at 400, green str/sta ARE advantageous to help boost FA and AR to levels that allow equiping of high level (Q195 or so) flashpoints. I've done the math and it works, whereas a Solitus can only wield a Q190 or so due to the FA limitations. But this is a very extreme boundary case. Since core skills at 150 easily title cap at very high levels, even dark blue attributes mean little after buffs.

> Why foolish and dangerous?

Because these items are far easier for funcom to nerf than core attributes. Items could be removed, weakened, spawns made impossible, camped to the point that they're impossible to get, restricted, given insane requirements, requirements changed, level changed, etc etc etc.

The problem is that ALL breeds should be balanced without special items. THEN special items should be balanced against special items of other breeds (in other words, Solitus should get special items too). It should be a two step process, and unfortunately Funcom has tried to balance the non-solitus breeds by giving them special items. This, IMHO, is a lazy, half-assed short-term solution.
