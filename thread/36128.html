Player Economy Inflation / Money Sinks Needed!
Now before I begin, this is not an insult to Funcom for not having created money sinks, but more of a request for them due to the mass over inflation of credits within the AO player's economy.

As it stands there's no true money sink other than the auction world. While that worked at first its gotten out of hand. It starts out as some "rare" item. That someone who has managed to either purchase credits or earn an endless amount to pay an ungodly price for. He does so because it's of no consequence to him. He has more than enough credits to splurge, and it quickly kills off any competition and its better than waiting around for hours or days to hear an auction end. The only problem is, once this happens, it suddenly becomes a standard. Or a high level makes a secondary and has oodles of cash in his pockets. When on the market to purchase items for himself, he can spend double. He can pay an nonstandard price because it doesn¡¦t hurt him, but it hurts the lower level community and it inflates the economy once more, setting another nonstandard price and watching it become the norm.

Before you make real life comparisons about high price buy outs, please stop. I don¡¦t want to hear your apples vs. oranges arguments. This is NOT a real life environment so the actions of people are extreme. Why? Because there _IS_ no consequence and there _IS_ no true value to credits other than to get what you want. If this was real money, none of you would be spending this outrageous, and if you would, the person selling would know better than to except the average man to pay that price and merely cater to the rich. While I do think auctions are a part of a healthy thriving economy, the one we exist in on Rubi-Ka1 is a complete joke with over inflation beyond comprehension. And with suckass people who like to squeeze as much money as possible. The honest trader hardly exists these days, and for those that are still out there, I commend you for not contributing to this downward spiral.

Wen-wen's are a perfect example of this. At first due to their rarity they were going for MILLIONS of credits, now they're worth very little, but people are still trying to squeeze those millions out to sell them. One bad step sets a horrible precedence to the rest of the market.

I don't suggest we set prices on items or that funcom polices the auction situations. I don't believe its possible to expect players to be reasonable and to think reasonable when making purchases because it may set a horrible standard for the rest, instead I think Funcom should develop ways to spend money that doesn't circulate its way back into the player economy, thus creating money sinks. This wouldn't put an end to people with more money paying more, but it would help to lower those prices and make those types of purchases less frequent.

Suggestion 1: Guildhalls. To make it easier to create these, how about only making Guildhalls only in major cities. IE. Omni Ent/Omni Trade/Rome/Athens/Tir/Newland and the like. Create a central area with a key system, much like Apartments. Allow guilds to purchase extra rooms if they so desire. Allow guilds to purchase stores within their guildhalls or vendors, bars. The options are endless. It would definitely use up a lot of credits to create guildhalls.

Suggestion 2: Customizable Vehicles: Allow players to upgrade their vehicles, faster, different colors, and possibly different body styles. Giving players a way to spend money that enhances their vision of fun game playing is a the way to go. A big part of AO is the style aspect, this would be huge.

Suggestion 3: Tax the extremely rich. I'm just kidding

There are other methods of creating money sinks, but I think the trick behind making them, is creating money sinks PLAYERS want and DO want to pay for and wouldn't MIND paying for. Creating money sinks where players feel nerfed or forced into is a bad idea. While they may work for the inflation situation, they totally kill the spirit of happy players.

These are just a few of my ideas, and yes, an open call out to players who like to inflate the economy. We should speak out more against nonstandard and outrageous prices. We can only hold ourselves responsible for situations that are circumstance to player actions.

I am formally creating a list of players who I know to be honest traders. Individuals who are merely seeking a fair price and who sell at reasonable prices that don't offend the average player. Maybe even forming a community that sets standard prices to certain items and sticks with them. I for one am not big on auctions, if you are, stick with it, if not send me a tell and maybe we can help each other. There will be times when all of us will pay that outrageous price for that direly wanted item, but this situation shouldn't be so common.

Money sinks are for combating the rampant inflation of the economy (take a look in the exchange forum). The problem is not for lower levels, their moneymaking ability seems balanced as is.

The current high prices in the vendors for armor/weapons might in fact be counterproductive with regards to alleviateing the pressure on the economy. The same holds true regarding the restriction on the max quality produce for sale, only less so as this encourages teamplay and missioning due to their inavailability in shops. The message there: No one can afford to actually buy their own armor; better blitz it or buy it at the player markeds. Thus selfreliance is taught to such a level that it hurts the economy.

We need more money to be taken OUT of the game, and after the mandatory yalm purchase, there is almost not a single thing that does this. Taxation might be a passive way of going about it, implementing cool new trinkets for higher levels would be a better way. Having moneysinks in the form of something that enrichens the game thus serves a dual purpose.
