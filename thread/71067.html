GENERAL HEALTH WARNING

Area: Northern Rubi-Ka
For Solitus, Opifex, Nanomage and Atrox

The Medical Branch of the Clan Mercury Dragons, part of the S.T.O.R.M. Department for Support Services, issues a warning for the benefit of the citizens of Rubi-Ka.

From the Desk of the Chief Surgeon, Captain Madhava

Citizens of Rubi-Ka, the Clan Mercury Dragons would like to warn the general populace of a risk to the health of all free Clan and Neutral peoples as well as travelling Omni-Tek employees peacefully moving in the northern hemisphere.

Our scientists and doctors have been taking daily samples of a variety of flora and fauna about Greater Tir County, the Aegean, Athens Shires, Mort, Newland, and a variety of random territories to gather information on mapping the mutation rates in the background species on the planet. To this end, data gathered forms a continuing record that allow for further studies in monitoring species extinction, growth and changes that might be further impacted by industrialization, mining, and environmental changes.

Comparing the data with those gathered by Omni and Neutral botanists, xenozoologists, entymologists, geneticists and environmental scientists, a consistant pattern of mutation cycles is discernable and can be understood in the greater context of planetary and seasonal progression.

It is well known that Rubi-ka's environment is well suited for high levels of mutation in the general population of the flora and fauna. The increased radiation from the two suns, the electrical storms in the atmosphere, the lack of boundaries for stable species and the amount of notum in the atmosphere and soils create a "perfect genetic soup" allowing for wide variation. However, even in an environment of high mutation, there is a stability of expected change that allows the ecosystem to function without collapse.

Samples taken since the advent of the Omni Weather System changes just prior to the ICC landfall, have shown additional strains on the ecosystem. This has undermined the stability and changed the mutation rates normally expected.

The mutation rates, normally expected for this time of year from the last several hundred years should be in the range of 10 to 15 %. Tests done on fungal blooms, the eggs of Anuns and vultures, aquatic life where it has survived, a variety of trees, leets, rats, brontos, mutant populations have shown an increase in mutability upwards of 30 to 45%.

This mutation spike represents potentially dangerous variations in formally safe species. For those citizens that experiment with natural substances for cooking, for adventurers and others living off the land, ingesting natural flora and fauna and those practitioners that treat ills with herbs and native plant life we recommend REFRAINING FROM ALL INGESTING OF NATIVE PLANT AND ANIMAL LIFE UNLESS FIRST IRRADIATING AND TESTING IS DONE.

It is expected that eventually the mutation spike will settle back to normal levels or the environment will find a way to relieve the stress with a collapse of some fundamental ecosystems. We know that when excess energy is placed into a system a way must be found to redirect the energy. We will keep monitoring the environment and report when the spike has receeded. Please be more aware when travelling into the wilderness. Formerly docile plants and animals may become more agressive, behavior patterns may have shifted as well as population.

Please be aware of what you put in your mouth!

COMMUNIQUE FROM DESERT WINDS HQ
LOCATION CLASSIFIED

Citizens of Rubi-ka

A few months back the Desert Winds began monitoring the environmental and eco-stability of Rubi-Ka. We sent a team of investigators to Stret West Bank to monitor the changes (if any) in that region that were caused by the then recent changes to the Weather Control System (WCS). The WCS is the device that controls the weather on Rubi-Ka and is fundamental to the survival of life on this planet we all call home.

Our team found that the majority of species in that region were within normal parameters expected for such an area. However, we observed that certain predatory species such as Wasteprowlers, Bloodcreepers and most alarmingly Roller Rats were present in numbers far beyond what would be expected. Such a 'top heavy' system is inherently unstable.

The roller rats had established a breeding colony on the banks of the Stret and were guarding it in numbers. We found a great deal of extremly fertile Matriarchs and Queens in this colony. We culled what we could but expect the numbers to soon return to their levels in the coming months. If this was brought about by the changes in the WCS we can not say for sure without further tests. Otherwise the ecosystem in Stret West Bank is in itself unstable and possibly on the verge of collapse. The entire balance of life is dependent on each and every being that makes up the ecosystem, if one link breaks it puts more strain on the rest. We should all be concerned and encourage further work to be performed in this area.

Secondly, the WCS mentioned above. When this system was altered prior to the arrival of the ICC 'peacekeepers' we began an extensive weather monitoring program. We travelled in every region in the Terraformed zoned, talked to other travellers and guilds.

We noticed several common themes amongst all the observations and tales. The weather in the north of the terraformed zone is 'worse' than in the south, higher rain fall, more electrical storms and also more for. All indicitive of a higher humidity in this region probably brought about by a reduction in rainfall in the south. If this was design or a 'bug' we do not know and have no data to support either theory. Shouts of Omni-Tek using the WCS as a weapon are probably the ravings of the paranoid.

Members of the Desert Winds will continue to monitor the environment in all terraformed areas of Rubi-Ka and will gladly work with any Clan, Omni-Tek or unaffiliated organisation to continue this project.

Stay safe Citizens

Rubi-Ka for all!!

Major McCovery, President of the Desert Winds

SEVERE HEALTH WARNING

The Medical Branch of the Clan Mercury Dragons, part of the S.T.O.R.M. Department for Support Services, issues a warning for the benefit of the citizens of Rubi-Ka.

From the Desk of the Chief Surgeon, Captain Madhava

Citizens of Rubi-Ka, the Clan Mercury Dragons would like to warn the general populace of a risk to the health of all free Clan and Neutral peoples as well as travelling Omni-Tek employees peacefully moving in the northern hemisphere.

NOTUM RESISTANT FLEA FEVER

What is Notum Resistant Flea Fever?

NRFF is a newly discovered acute infectious disease transmitted by the bite of an insect, most likely a flea bit possibly a blodbag, spider, or tick. Note that several species might be carriers for these insects especially vultures and anuns. The disease has recently been discovered in the northern hemisphere of Rubi-Ka and appears in blood tests and an indirect immunofluorescence assay test as a variation of Rickettsia rickettsii. The disease has spread quickly in an observed population.

Who gets NRFF?

So far only observed adults of Opifex, Solitus and Nanomage can be affected as well as animals. At this stage Atrox appear to have a natural resistance but also serve as natural carriers or vectors for this disease. The large muscle mass and insensitivity to pain and light pressure often prevents Atrox from being aware they have been bitten by small insects. Atrox DNA structure makes them resistant towards notum based diseases and probably helps this anomalous disease.

How is NRFF spread?

NRFF is spread by the bite of an infected insect or by contamination of the skin with infected insect blood or feces. Person-to-person transmission occurs when blood and fluids of an infected person mix with an unaffected person.

What are the symptoms of NRFF?

NRFF is characterized by a sudden onset of moderate to high fever (which can last for two or three weeks), severe headaches, fatigue, deep muscle pain, chills and rash. Hallucinations, signs similar to notum poisoning, cough and more extreme symptoms have been observed. Sometimes a rash begins on the legs or arms, may include the soles of the feet or palms of the hands and may spread rapidly to the trunk or the rest of the body. Not every case of Notum Resistant Flea Fever will have the rash. The symptoms reoccur no matter with what nanite medical program be it Fixer, Doctor, Martial Artist or Adventurer.

How soon do symptoms appear?

Symptoms usually appear between three and 14 days after the bite of an infected flea. Any person experiencing illness with a fever following a flea bite should consult his or her physician and advise the physician of the exposure to vermin bites.

How is Notum Resistant Flea Fever diagnosed?

Diagnosis of NRFF is based largely on the patient's signs and symptom's of illness. Blood tests are essential in confirming a diagnosis, but symptomatic treatment should begin promptly if symptoms and exposure history support this diagnosis. Quarantine by use of closed helmet with filters and gloves as well as isolation is recommended. The Mercury Dragons are committed to the safety of all Clans and will willing share the diagnostic tests with others.

What is the treatment for NRFF?

There is currently no "cure" although we are working on that. Any information on experience with such symptoms should be directed to Dr. Dianechti Cifuentes of the Mercury Dragons. Symptoms may be contained with specialized stims and pills. Overall mortality is projected between 3 percent and 5 percent but ranges from 13 percent to 25 percent in untreated individuals. The absence or delayed appearance of the typical rash contributes to a late diagnosis and thereby increases the projected incidence of fatality. This is not something that can be treated with nanite programs. The strain is resistant and produces fast enough that cellular replacement only slightly hinders growth. Once an insurance pattern is made with an infected individual all subsequent reclaims will continue to replicate the infection.

How can NRFF be prevented?

Persons spending time outdoors should take precautions against all vermin born diseases:

The Mercury Dragons can offer a newly devised experimental repellent serum containing XV-11 and enigmamethrin to treat flesh and clothes (especially pants, socks and shoes) while in locations where biting insects may be common. Follow label directions; do not misuse or overuse repellents. Always supervise children in the use of repellents. We will give this out free to those who ask, we do not have the production centers of Omni but we are working on a store now.

Remove any biting insect promptly. Do not use bare hands. You may want to put the insect in a jar of rubbing alcohol labeled with the date and location of the bite in case you seek medical attention and your physician wishes to have the insect identified.

Be sure to wash the bite area and your hands thoroughly with soap and water, and apply an antiseptic to the bite site.

Please report such incidents to the Mercury Dragon Medical Staff or Combat Medic as we are in the process of tracking down the hazardous areas and working toward an effective treatment plan.
