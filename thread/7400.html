A fix for 80% of the game
I played this game since beta and I can tell you how to fix most of the complaining/moaning/whining:
change how the game cons.
Right now the game cons on numerical level. I may be level 20 but that Rhinoman Beater is level 50 so he is red to me. Fair enough. Ok, so I look at another player running by in Meetmedeere and he is green to me so I attack him. He kills me fairly fast and so I send messages to him, "Exploiter! Over-equipper!", blah blah blah. I am now mad because this guy was green to me but he killed me. I relied on the information that Funcom provided and acted on it. His NUMERICAL level was well below mine so he conned green.
Ok, the problem with this is the fact that human players equip imlants and use buffs that are many levels ahead of their NUMERICAL level. (Sorry to keep emphasizing that word but i am trying to prove a point about the flaw in the con system). So how do we fix this? How do we keep people from yelling at over-equipping and using buffs? One way is to change how people and mobs con. Instead of looking at the numerical level, the game should look at a more important stat, like hit points or attack rating. It is not cheating to obtain information about level, nano points, and health points becuase they have information tools in the game that they finally fixed in the last patch. Since this information is "public domain", why not include it in the con system. One bar but with 4 different areas for each stat:
__________________________
(______|______|______|______)
health nano attack level

Some people will complain and say this is revealing too much information or making the game too easy, but Funcom has already provided this information to anyone who wants it, they just need to use the information tools. There are probably better methods swimming around in someone's brain but the bottom line is if i have the above con system in place, and i see someone close to the same attack rating, then i wont complain if he beats me. I wont call him a cheater or an over-equipper because the game will finally be giving me a con color based on something useful and not the numerical level. In a system with implants and buffs, conning on the numerical system is a bad idea and it is why so many people complain about not being able to solo an orange mob. Flames/suggestions are welcome.
