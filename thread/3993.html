Neutrality
Why have the neutrals to be so disadvantaged ?

The opinion of Funcom is clearly: Neutres are there for a few moment, they will change their minds and join Clans or Omni.

The problem is that "a lot of" neutrals think in another way. We want to stay neutral. We want to participate to the story, but in another way.

We have ideas, I'm sure we can improve the game, at least for the story part. We may be the commercial ones, the ones that really inhabits the world (take for example our real world, in a conflict, is everybody implicated in the struggle ? NO NO NO, the majority of the people is not).

I do not say that neutrals have to be the majority, but I say: "let's do something to improve the status of neutrals".

As letting them access to all shops, neutral, clans or omni, all missions... It's a little idea, that comes to me like that, but we have to find something to offset our disadvantages (no tokens, not all nanos, not able to attack the first in PvP, ...)
