The pvp level brackets are borked up and should be based around LC area levels, not character levels.

If you are "in range" to fight the towers in the LC area, you should be able to fight and heal everyone else who is "in range" for the LC area.

If you are not "in range" of the area (ie, of all possible towers on that area), then you can neither heal nor fight anyone else there.

So if the LC area is level 100-120, people from level 80 to level 150 can fight there. Level 150s can attack or heal level 80s in that specific LC area, and level 80s could attack or heal level 150s. Anyone level 79 or lower, or 151 and higher, can neither fight nor heal.

If the LC area is level 150-200, then 120+ people could fight there, 119 and lower can't.

If the area is level 30-40, then the level range would be 24-50. Etc.

In general - if you aren't the right level to attack any tower that's built there, you can't fight and have no reason to be a part of the battle.

If you are the right level for attacking a tower, then you shouldn't have situations where level restrictions prevent you from being effective against other people who can attack the towers.
