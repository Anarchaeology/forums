In-game newspapers
The idea is easy - main resource with game news we have is login messages on game start.

Suggestion is - player made newspapers.
Neutral, Omni and Clans.

Add terminals in main citys where you could see latest newspaper for a little money.

Articles could be submited by players in internet form or send to GMs ^,so for every accepted article player will recive some money and may be later journalist identification.

This could be good addition to game.

May be it could be made that organisations would be able to make their own newspaper, but all we need for start is ONE in-game newspaper that would be often refreshed by players-

Articles from Weather in ROM to latest news from War front.

Thats all. Need just a hour of scripting, but is better for gameplay as new weapon or armor.
