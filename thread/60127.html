Clean up the maps!
Ok, here's the map rant.

Map options. Be able to disable some of those map upgrades.

Doors. Why do the mission doors show up when you can't go through? What good is seeing a door do when every building has one? How about just shop doors and static doors, like the ones in Omni Trade you can enter without a mission key? Don't show us stuff that we can't do anything with. If we have a mission for that door, it already has a marker on it.

Buildings. Get the shapes right. You know which I'm talking about. The ones that show as two or more on the map, but are really only one.

Walls. How about putting a line on the map for things you can't walk through, like walls, fences, cliff faces. Related to the buildings, a lot of buildings are joined by walls or butt up against the invisible city wall.

It's like these maps were made off of an aerial photo, with some booby saying "I think that's a building, mark it as solid. This area is all the same color, must be flat. What do you mean, there's a cliff there?"

On a final rant note, in SWG you will be able to set waypoints. Wake up, FC! When you lose customers it's not so much the quality of what they are going to but the quality of what they are leaving.
