Pawn Shops Are The Answer !!!
Greetings all. The problem with the tedious problem of selling all of our items player to player without wasting hours of our $12.99 a month time sounds like a simple question to answer. Funcom needs to add pawn shops (hey, they could even be clan/omni guild owned, where the guild has to put up a ton of money to open it, then gets a bit of profit off of every sale in hope of recouping money, just like a real pawn shop). When someone wants to sell something, they go in and "list" it at the pawn shop, listing the price they want for it. The normal price for the item if bought in a store would also be listed beside the item. The pawnshop screen would show how much markup they will ask for (by item), with a total for the item so you can adjust the price you want to ask by setting the price of your item lower than in a normal store. This will also be a great way for people to put up things for sale that are found in missions only, without having to camp on the shopping channel in hopes that someone is looking for it - of course this could be said for every item we want to sell, too. These pawn shops would be put in the seedier area, like in wastes, or area like East Athen City that are trashed - lower income areas where deals can be found. All of us that now have to waste time camping the dang shopping channel would be able to just dump off our treasures and hit the mission terminal again and again. To recover your cash, they could either make it automatic via a tell ("you just sold your QLxx Chilled Plasteel Armor Helmet for 8,300 credits") with the money being automatically added to your account, or the requirement to go to ANY pawn shop and check at the guy at the counter to get any money you have coming (just like if you cosigned something someplace for real). Just think, no more hauling around 6 small backpacks and filling your bank with even more - we could just do like we would in real life if we found a bunch of really expensive stuff to sell EVERY day - we would get someone to sell it for us and go on with our normal lives. For me, the fun of this game is the missions and grouping, not shopping. I would go to the pawn shops all the time for stuff - just think, you could find some amazing deals where 1 guy is selling that QL 90 32 NCU chip fr 10,000 credits less than someone else, in the SAME pawn shop! And then I could get on with killing things, getting more IP to spend, and buying those killer implants I have been waiting for. Can't do any of that without phat cash, and can't play the game if you are always trying to sell things, just so you can play the game. Peace.
