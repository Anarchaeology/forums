Food being more than an rp element
At the current, food in Anarchy Online serves 3 uses:

- Makes funny text appear over your head when consumed.

- Can be preferred as a better dmg alternative for nukes for hi-lvl NTs

- Lets character hold a bugged polygon cluster

What we ask for is for food being something more. It could be critical to survival; not eating frequently would make your HP drop after a while. It could heal you or it could give you better stats for a short period of time.

The Bronto Burger boycott is forgotten by most people at the current moment. If food was being implemented as an important factor, such events could actually have a meaning and lead to something more.

As for food being survival critical; that's even possible with the current technology: Make any char above lvl 20 or so lose 3-4 % of hitpoints by default. To avoid this, a nanoprogram must be activated. To activate, you have to consume a meal or a drink. Just like pills and blister packs (where did they go?)
