char & org info on community site
I have looked at this feature and it seems pretty cool so far.
I would also like to see a few things added:

I would like to be able to search in the following ways:

-by character (in-game name) *already enabled
-by gender
-by side (omni/neutral/clan)
-by level (so you can find other people to team with)
-by profession (for those seeking a Doc, or an Enf, etc)

For organizations, I'd like to see:

-cost/dues needed (may influence someones decision to join)
-contact name (in case you want to join or need more info)
-list of what benefits the organization offers
(like those stat boosts that the organizations can buy for all of their members to take advantage of)

For individual characters, I'd also like to see :

-armor/clothing currently worn
-type of weapons(or item) character is currently using (if applicable)
These two suggestions should be available for ANYONE to view,
since, if you were online, someone would visibly see which armor/clothing a character is wearing and whether they have a sword, a pillow, or a beer can in their hand. This could be as simple as displaying the little icon/images of the item(s) similar to that which we see in the stores. I do NOT think that you should get anything other than a graphical image of the item, since you would not have info to this information if you were only viewing the character in the game and pressing [T] for thier info.

I'd like to have secure access (via password or something) to my own characters personal items:

-weapons I am currently using (QL/requirements, etc)
-armor I am currently wearing (QL/requirements, etc)
-implants I am currently wearing (QL/requirements, etc)
-items in my inventory (QL/requirements, etc)
-nanoprograms which are uploaded (QL/NCU cost, etc)
-maps and map reader upgrades which are uploaded
-PVP kills/title (if applicable)
-IP points available to spend (if any)
-What my current stats are (how much STR,CompLit,etc)
-bank account inventory* (what items-QL,requirements,etc- are in my bank account)
*Hey, if I can see my statement book showing what I have in my RL bank account, and I can get a statement showing what is in my Safety Deposit Box , why can't I do it 30,000 years in the future?

Above should be only accessable by the "owner" of the player,
but should be accessible even when NOT playing AO. Disable Modification of above info if not LOGGED IN AND PLAYING THE CHARACTER, Enable Viewing only, so we can plan ahead how we are going to:
-use our possessions
-write scripts for items we want to sell in the shopping channels
-plan future IP use (mentioned by another (?) in an earlier post)
-research online/in forums (which most of us don't use while actually playing)

I would also like to have a short text-only* area where we can write whatever we want. That way, players can:
-leave it blank, if they don't want to disclose any info
-post stats, PVP status, character history, items for sale
-post declarations of superiority, quotes, etc.

I think, if you leave this area a decent size (for char histories and whatever), you can cover most of the players requests, and you won't discourage players who don't want to use this feature, as it will be optional.

*Although I personally enjoy posting my personal banner graphics onto things like this, to avoid all the potential problems which may arise from this, you may want to only allow text
(a-z, 0-9, and regular characters).

My guess would be that the players would rather Funcom spend it's efforts fixing real issues (whatever they may be at the time), than creating a new feature like this and then having to waste time fixing any problems related to it.

I think if you created a simple text imput box - at first, then come back and add more as time/effort, allows, many would approve of this sort of "slow/constant" way of adding new options.

Even text boxes have their own problems
...never enough space for some people
...some people will want Markup language allowed
...graphics & hyperlinks (to inappropriate sites)
...inappropriate language to deal with
The above would require Funcom to delegate someone to either monitor the text being imputed, or to deal with the issues that arise. Delegated personnel = more cost (both $ & time) which could be better devoted to fixing the other "issues" in the game...[not even going to go here..]

These are probably some of the reasons that Funcom hasn't already impletmented text imput as a feature here.

Well, it's something to think about, anyway.
