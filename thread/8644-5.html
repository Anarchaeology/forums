Dodge :
Dodge range attack skill is used to avoid getting hit by bullet. It is an extremly important skill to be proficient in. Notice, it does not reduce the amount of damage done if the attack hits you. It is dependent 50% on Agility, 30% Sense and 20% on Intelligence.

Evade :
The evade close combat attack skill improves your chance of not getting hit when attacked by melee or martial arts. It is dependent 50% on Agility, 30% on Sense and 20% on Intelligence.

Duck :
The duck skill is the one used to determine if your are hit by thrown weapons. But also your chance to avoid high explosives. It is dependent 50% on Agility, 30% on Sense and 20% on Intelligence.
