Stop the guards killing!
Since the beginning of AO, its been a well known fact that the most dangerous job there is, is that of guard duty. Our trusty guards not only stand ready to protect the young ones from the terrors of eleets and ravenous reets, but also triggerhappy players that merely are bored, or testing their gun.

Now to the roleplaying community out there, it is more than just annoying to try and chat with guards being slaughtered left and right, it makes realistic roleplaying (reacting to murderers) very hard.

Anarchy Online is a roleplaying game and even though you dont have to roleplay to enjoy the game of course, I think at least the world AO should react as a «normal» place would. There has to be some sort of reaction when a player shoots his own guards! I have heard people suggest that a player killing one of his own guards, should automatically change affiliation (Clan/Omni).

My guild consists of both roleplayers and pure gamers, and we usually hang around the bank in West Athens, just next to the guards. We have considered other places but Tir is too laggy, Wine, VW and Bliss dont have easy access to a grid terminal, Mort is hard for our low levels to reach, Camelot is the only alternative ... but why should we move? Cant something be done so that the killings stop?

I suggest that either the guards are given the power to summon much tougher guards if killed or under attack, a realistic way a city guard would function. This makes opposite faction fly-bys still possible, but if you try to attack them (or kill) more and much tougher guards will spawn.

I see this as no problem for the pure gamers, as they will have to deal with a stronger guard. They will just test their guns on leets instead. Also, they dont kill guards today because they are evil or want to be labeled as murderers, they do it because its easy. making it harder, will remove the problem, because it IS a problem for many roleplayers and I have heard of several people actually considering to quit because of it.

As I only hang around in West Athens, I dont know if the problem is similar elsewhere but my guild is very upset and would like to see some actions being done to prevent this from happening.

I have no intention of lowering the gaming value of the pure gamer, only to up the overall enjoyment of a realistic community and living world. If someone find themselves personally attacked by this post, I am sorry and it is not intentionally.
