Unity of the Rose Second Year Anniversary Party - December 6th
The Unity of the Rose will be two years old this Saturday. To celebrate we are throwing a big party in Borealis. The party will be held in a mission in Borealis city at 9pm Eastern (02:00 GMT). The exact location will not be known until that day. Roses will be distributing keys to the mission during the day for those who want to get keys early.

The party will feature:

- Gridstream Productions DJs Veldron and Laurie!
- Cash and item prizes througout the night!
- A dance contest!
- Dance and musical performances!
- Free booze!
- First public demonstration of the UoR poker bot.

Please come and enjoy the party! All factions welcome!
