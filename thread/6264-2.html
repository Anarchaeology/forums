Mission successful (or not)
Hello,
I completed the mission (kinda) and I cannnot say that I'm particularly happy with the result. As it turns out, there are quite a lot of 0people who have been clued onto the existance of the quest by this thread...


I first went looking for the named NPC Dodga, after scouring the Rising Sun, I then asked around and discovered exactly where he spawns. Lo and behold, he was camped.
So, I set my goals on finding the 8 pieces first so that when I managed to get my crack at the NPC, I could get the mission icon and complete the mission in one fell swoop. After a couple of hours in Athen Shire, I managed to find the entire set. So I headed back to Dodga.
When I got back to the Rising Sun, I found that the camper had left and was replaced by a new camper. I sat down behind the fellow and started up a conversation, afterall the NPC couldn't possibly have a long respawn period considering that the static missions are somewhat important....
Almost an hour later, Dodga spawns and the person at the head of the line gets his crack at the fellow. At this time there are three people in line. He inserts the relevant phrases and gets his mission icon... then Dodga vanishes. Not too bad, considering that I'm under the impression that Dodga has a one hour respawn. The fellow leaves...
TWO hours later Dodga respawns... It's 3am and I don't have the patience to sit here for 4 more hours (there is one other person in line). I hand Dodga the completed item and get the card in return, completely skipping the process of getting the mission icon and losing out on any form of reward for completing the mission.

My review of this static mission is; Don't bother doing it. The GM's do not intend to change the respawn time of this NPC, and the fact that he is usually camped, make this a mission only for those who have too much tiime on thier hands.
