The mission settings have a small effect on what kind of mission surrounding you will get. The closer to order to go, the more likely you will get a house mission instead of a cave mission. However there are no guarantees. Where the mission is can be more of a deciding factor than what the settings are.

There is a set of mid-level mission are shacks in Stret Weast that consitently generate clan house envorinments. The "Holes in the wall" area is as likely to generate Omni House missions as cave missions, even though the entrances are mostly caves. There is a crashed transport in Agean that will 100% be an under-city tunnels environment. (Some call it sewer, but the water is quite clean.)
