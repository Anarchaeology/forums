Letter of recommendation
This idea might not be new but I couldn't find any subjects about it so here it goes:

How about making a Letter of recommendation?

This letter should work kinda like the old organization cards where people could put their signature.
You could then use this letter if you played in a group and you thought your teammembers were doing a great job. Then you could sign their Letter of recommendation if if they were impressed with you they could sign yours..

an example:

Obscura shouts: lvl 35 fixer lfg .. Letter of recommendation (Obscura)

Then you could click the letter and see who has signed.. perhaps you'll see some people that you respect and that gives you an idea of the player you're about to recruit.

The reason I thought about this is that until now the game hasn't been fully balanced and there is always some professions that are better then others.. This will give the weak professions a better chance to be chosen for a team over the "uber" professions..

Please state your opinions on this subject
