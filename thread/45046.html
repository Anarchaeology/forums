I think this is a GREAT GREAT GREAT idea.. and have been mentioning it from time to time as well! I think all armor, NCU, Weapons.. Everything should go to QL250.... it would really help make the game MUCH MUCH harder... and then everything wouldn't be over when you equip those QL200 items... because of breed caps and the difficulty that comes with getting QL220+ items that are currently in game... I'd have to say it would make things really challenging again. So suddenly there is QL250 Elite armor that reqs 1250 AC rather than the current one with 1000 AC.... but the reqs are incredibly high in str/stam that they now req 687 stam and 626 strength (all 25% higher than the QL200 because it is 25% higher in QL)

This would be awesome to let the players be able to find ways to get into the armor... obviously Atrox would have the best chance of getting in this gear as my solitus caps at 476/str and 480 stam... however.. if I implanted stam or wore fredrickson sleeves and other boosts or implants I may be able to equip it without it being overequipped.... Everyone would have different levels of max armors and different levels of their max weapon etc. etc... all based on what they were able to do with implants and items that modify...

There truly would be no two unique characters if the weapons and armor went this high in QL... I can't imagine the pride gained from equipping an item you thought might be out of your reach.

Plus it would help change the design where players spend levels 120+ doing QL190 missions... that's 80 levels of the same mission level... players would be apt to attempt the more challenging levels for QL200+ boss loots... and QL200+ rewards.. right now the rewards are useless over QL200!!!

It would help add much needed variety to the game, when you are faced with the decision, "is it better to have a QL200 CHS or a QL220 Div9 or a QL240 nova.... " All would be based on strategy and setting up your player for the route you want to go

Do you want to have an extra 1500 AC or be able to equip a QL220+ weapon... It could be so interesting...

Wow this idea really got my brain firing.. and it doesn't seem like it wouldn't be incredily impossible to implement...

The title of the article may help get some flames.. because I think Atrox need a little loving for their unfairness in ability breed caps... but the idea is SOLID!
