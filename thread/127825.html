Black-Flag Open Recruitment period now in progress
Hello Omnitek employess,

I come to you to announce the formation of a new orginization for OT employees located on Atlantean.
Black-Flag was created today, by the former members of Devils Advocate, Catuela and Templus. And by Poire formerly of the group "Drink Mt. Dew".
This new guild will be dedicated to spending time doing the finer things in life. We will spend time at the arena, as well as in missions and at unique mob areas. We hope to promote a family like atmosphere within the guild and are looking for like minded people that want to help be part of something new.

So ask yourself this

Are you an Omni Employee on Atlantean?
Are you over the level of 130?
Do you want to help build a guild that is set up for success?

If you answered yes to any of these questions please send a tell to a member or post here and a member of the guild will be happy to answer any questions you may have.

Thank you for your time.
