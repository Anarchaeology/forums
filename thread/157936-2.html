Follow up report
Maroula has informed me that several people were in the Weapons Dealer that Pyromanche and the others were sitting on. They apparently went in there knowingly and shot off a couple of rounds of display weapons into the ceiling, sending the nanomages to reclaim at that time.

One of these was an Omni fixer, possible the same one that attacked Yarko a few months back. He used his ability to grid out to go un-noticed. He and his accomplice had knocked the shopkeeper unconcious, and did some damage to the store itself, including the holes in the roof.

So, although I still don't know who it is, we are getting closer to some type of answers. We have aquired the video tape from the store's security cam, and it is being analyzed by Dyan and Maroula, they hope to at least ID the accomplice since the fixer in GA will hard to determine who he is. If he is reading this and would like to possibly turn himself into Pyromanche or the NLF for questionsing, that would be most welcome.

As for the signal.. Maroula is applying for all new permits and after the manages to cut thru the red-tape.. I expect NMR to be back on the air.

The Report
This as of yet un-named Omni MP states that he is not doing this as a direct order from the Corporation, nor from any organization at all. He would only meet with Pyromanche in a indoor area with all technology turned off (computers, phones, comm links) and the lights needed to be turned down as low as possible.
He told his story:
He considers the technology harmful and as he moves in the shadows, under the cover of night, he uses the technology he fears most to destroy anything that transmits thru the airwaves. He has no gripe with NMR, as a matter of fact, he listens to the live shows regularly, but.. be feels that the "Corporation" is using airwaves to spy on the citizens of Rubika. Worse, he thinks that they are trying to use for purposes of mind control.
Now, he doesn't know about the 2 in the Weapons dealer that night, and he can not speak to why they felt the need to shoot thru the roof and kill innocent people, but of course, perhaps, they were from a rival radio station or from the "Corporation" itself.. he was not sure. He was however sure, that there was more to this than met the eye.

He asked to be sent directly into the grid so that no one could see him leave.
