Neutralizers: Medic's Combat Light Tank armor(Doc only, nodrop). A very rare drop, out of approx 200 kills i have only gotten 2 Tanks.

Lab Assistants: Enhanced NCU chip with Recompiling Core, nice tradeble NCU, with the added effekt og -1% Nano cast. Uncommon drop.

Blaster Beetle, T.I.M. and Lab Director. Well normally all who go there knows what they drop. Then theres the ring which i havn't gotten yet, primarily due to low Agility.

what and where ...
the TLR mission is a static mission on The Longest Road zone. This zone is basically west from West Athen (refer to a map to get a better idea). Once in the zone follow the road to the big pothole (about 3/4 the way down the road) and turn south and follow the small road.

This will put you in an Omni Mine where there are Omni Guards and NPCs in the area so if you are neut or clanner, take care. Once inside the compound, stay to the right and go into the second building marked 'Foremen'. Once inside, you are in the mission.

Make sure you have a full and decent team, it starts off easy for a group of 55-60, though gets tough at the end.

Good luck and have fun. And once again, thankyou everyone for all the good information on the drops.

- oZ

PS. To add to this list, there is a unique ring that drops that adds 166 or so to max life, that drops in the sewers, but is a very very rare drop from what I've been told. Unfortunately never got the mobbie that drops it, but I've seen the ring and it is cool.
