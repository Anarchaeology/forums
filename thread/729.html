Scopes were recently nerfed, but MA crit buffs remain.

Actually, I think the higher level MA crit buffs are probably the most imbalancing things in PvP right now. LMA and UVC are hard to cast, but Traders can wrangle MA friends to get them much sooner than normal. One way to fix this would be to make these MA buffs self only.

On the other hand, these crits buffs are a blessing for high end PvM. As a matter of fact, with VS or better, I crit enough that I am guaranteed aggro by the middle of every fight (so maybe a mixed blessing, heh).

If I had to pick a quick fix, I'd say lower the crit rates in PvP fights. The defensive skills do work, but not enough to overcome the advantage of higher level MA crit buffs.

already getting nerfed
Hey, we're already getting back-door unannounced nerfs. Low Light Sensor crit decrease and up to triple the attack time taken now due to initiative changes. I haven't really seen anyone else post about it in this forum so I'll repost what I mentioned on the trader profession board.

I have a pretty slow firing rate due to my low light scope as I'm sure several of the other traders do as well. I time my nanos between shots as I'm sure most others do. It seems that now the nanos interrupt your ranged initiative meter once upon casting the nano at which point your shot meter restarts..

Fine whatever, the two meters don't work seperately now for all intents and purposes. I can see why they would want to do this. The real problem for me is that it also restarts the meter when a spell is cast on you now. So when casting a ransack the cast interrupts and then if you don't get the shot off before the ransack goes into effect the buff interrupts the init again.

Same thing with the health drains.. On cast and on heal it resets your shot meter effectively making you take twice as long to shoot and depending on the level of your gun probably making it not worth it to ever cast anything due to a much higher damage output by the gun effectively saving you more health in the long run. Our two part nanos make us the most badly hurt by this change from what I can tell. They can actually reset your attack up to three times if you are refreshing a deprive/ransack.

I confirmed this last night with a GM as a "balance issue." Traders get to deal with the initiative reset twice to three times as much as any other class every time by design. We must have been too powerful. Beware the awe inspiring trade skill character of doom.

Personally I feel like several other posters that we have extreme problems soloing or grouping due to our team heals causing great ammounts of aggro, our drains doing little damage for the large ammount of nano/time taken, and having slow firing rate with little chance to crit in pvm. We do well in pvp on the low end and can solo easily until 50 but that's all. There's not a ton of traders and I'd say most are specialist twink characters made for pvp.
