Well, if you're going off pure stats, he's got a point. Advents have a really versitile range of available green/auqa/light blue slots to pour IP into. Compare to, say, an Agent. IIRC, Agents only have 7 green/auqa skills (forgive me if I'm off by 1--just going off of memory...also not counting Tutoring), 4 or 5 of which are WORTHLESS to them. (Sneak Attack, Psychology, Sharp Object, Chemestry...Concealment is until 14.2, at least) Really, they only useful stats are: Aimed Shot (nerfed), B&E, Matt. Crea (of course, only SIX of the entire Anget nano line uses this), Matt. Metam. (Buffs and DoT's only), Mech. Engin., Perception, Rifle. Considering that more of their nanos (esp. FP's) use Bio Met. and Psy Met., their green/auqa on nanos is nearly worthless as well.

Compare this to an advent, that can basically cheaply use any melee weapon, has a cheap ranged alternitive (although many don't like handguns), can dual wield easily, cheap nano abilities are actually applicible to most nanos, great healing via cheap treatment and first aid, etc... No denying that advents have a good set-up in terms of IP.

The flip-side to this is that advents need to use a wider range of skills than most classes to perform well. Agents only need to work about Rifle and Aimed Shot... Advents need to worry about Pistol/Fling/Burst(sometimes)/Multi Ranged for Pistols, and 1HE/Brawl/Heavey Weapons/Dimach/Multi Melee for most 1HE weapons (Riders, for instance.) Also, advents have to up all their nano abilities save T&S to be useful with polymorphs, heals, damage shields, and AC buffs--all of which are important to the overall value of an advent.

So, all in all, I think it either balances out, or becomes harder for advents during "IP crunch" periods. (The last 1/4 segment in any title.) It definitely not a huge advantage to advents...because if we didn't have that many greens/auqas, we'd be sunk.
