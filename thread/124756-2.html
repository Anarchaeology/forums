This is more than simply threatening to tear down mines. The very thing we all depend on to survive here, the artificial environment, is controlled by a non Rubi-Kan source, namely Omni-Tek at this time. The clans control over 50% of the mining/drilling fields in the terraformed region of Rubi-Ka and fight to take more. But how does each mine you take sever Rubi-Ka from the clutches of the corporations?
