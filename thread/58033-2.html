Ah, recent history
Being forewarned and hence ready and waiting, from a hidden position (i.e. standing 'hidden' right next to it) I watched Valaik, Thyme, and Lupoh (who was still DA at that point) appear at the Omni-HQ grid arrival point, then cluelessly run past me off into the HQ proper, refrigerator sized Weird Looking Bomb stuffed under Valaik's arm.

It seemed the grid then jumped up and attacked poor Lupoh, who was suddenly caught in a mad circle-running episode. Now reduced to two, Valaik and Thyme moved on to their objective behind one of the admin buildings.

I fondly recall appearing in front of them as Valaik planted the bomb. There was some surprise on seeing me, but to his credit, Valaik did not panic, and we struggled, he holding me off as Thyme set the detonator.

On finally freeing a hand and pistol-whipping poor Valaik, Thyme ran away, leaving him alone to face the mercy of the authorities, only to be caught herself at one of the Omni-HQ bridge gates. There is no honour amongst thieves it seems.

Luckily, for Omni-Tek, a few more pistol-whips later and Valaik was convinced for the need to produce the code and disarm the bomb.

Ah. The memories. Still, it was only mid-July. Therefore, they are quite fresh.
