Attention Clanners: Your help is needed!
Omni Mining For Generosity has identified a faulty production of T-547 valves that were shipped to Towers R Us shops in the greater northern area of Rubi Ka. These valves have been shown to make a high pitched whistling sound in laboratory tests that is unacceptable and, thus, must be replaced with T-547B valves.

You, as a Clanner, are probably saying to yourself right now, "So? I can go right down to my local Towers R Us shop, buy T-547B valves and replace the ones on my towers." This is a very dangerous task and one that should be handled by only properly trained Omni-Tek personnel as the whistling sound can, under certain wind conditions, make a person whose ear is within six centimeters of the valve go blind for eight seconds, causing distress.

Here is what we need you Clanners to do. Go to each and every Clan tower, disable the defense shields so that our teams can go in and replace the valves, then return to Tir where you will be safely out of range should wind conditions become unfavorable (representatives from Omni-Pol will be on hand to serve refreshments of punch and cookies). During the time of repairs, you may receive false alarms that your towers are under attack. I assure you that there are no attacks and this is simply a result of testing the alarm systems on the towers - a necessary step in completing the valve replacement procedure. For your own safety, please do not go to your towers if an alarm sounds; this is a crucial and very touchy step in the replacement and any disruptions can cause the towers to explode. We hope, for the safety of our personnel, that no towers will explode while testing the alarms, but for the good of all, it is a chance we are willing to take.

Thank you for your cooperation during this vital task.

Omni-Tek cares.
