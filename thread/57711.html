Grid and Whompah nerf.....
Travel today is so easy and safe today that its boring....

So what we need is some unpredictability in travel...

One way to do that to whompahs and grid is that they would randomly stop working or... send you to the wrong location, aka nothing truely dangerous. Just something to ****e up travel abit.

Random... well lets say there is an electrical storm in the zone you go to then the following could happen

whompah: they stop working altogether. "Sorry the destination Whom-pah isn't functioning right now due to an electrical storm at the destination."
Grid: It could either stop working or (the more fun verison) drop you at a random location around the normal exit point.


This could be used in a RP/event senario also... aka FC shuts the whompah down during a clan attack on 2HO... and so on.

Now this shouldn't happen all the time, that would be just annoying!
