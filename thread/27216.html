That is a nice idea. I thought of another one in addition, not sure if its been suggested before, but I'll post it anyway.

We have back worn armor to match nano, omni-pol, primus/secundus/decus, and bio-mech, but, that leaves all the other armor types with nothing. Why hasn't there been anything added to match the cyber armor types, MA armor, tech armor, etc?

I picked up a back-worn suit the other day in a mission. Basically it looked like I was wearing a full omni executive suit. Why can't you simply add Cyber armor suits, MA suits, etc into the game? It wouldn't give as much variety as everyone would like, but it would most definitely cut back on all the dress-wearing people playing the game.

Or you could get even decide to get creative and design back worn armor for each armor type in the game. I haven't thought all the types out myself, but I'm sure someone would be able to think up something. MA armor could be a black robe with the dragon symbol on the back, maybe have a white sash along the waist. Oh, and ya know, MA armor could really use a headworn piece, as well as some gloves. Same for Kevlar armor.

Just a couple ideas. Feel free to add/take away from what I suggested.

As for Shadowlands, I heard that new armor types would be introduced. Now, I think that's great, but I'm really hoping you guys at FC touch up the existing armor before that expansion comes out.
