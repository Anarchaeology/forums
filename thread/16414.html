I got no problem with grid monsters
As a Neutral, I get used to being chased by MechDogs and whatnot at lower levels when running from the Grid terminal to the 20K Whom'pa in O-Trade. Getting to the point where you can safely walk from the Grid terminal to the 20K Whom'pa with the respect of the Mechdogs and continuing approval the guards is sort of a 'graduation'.

I think it's entirely too easy for low levels to use the Grid with impunity. If at least they had to be scared when doing the Newland -> Athen traverse in the Grid until at least level 40, it would add a bit to the game. Not a lot, but a bit.

Then, after all these viruses turn grey to us, they could at least present a view of the Grid as a functioning entity. Simply add ambience.
