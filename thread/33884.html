About the Protest
Hi Guys/gals..

Not that I disagree with you I actually do agree but I don't know if disrupting the game like that is really the way to go. This change in fact angers me greatly due to the fact that they used our *FIX* nano's to implement this experimenting on us and in effect fixing and nerfing us on one move gotta give em credit for that they didn't even wait till the next patch. This is just not acceptable. Its true we need to find some way to vent our concerns and frustrations of the Adventuer/Fixer communitys.

I know alot of people that are not of our two professions are finding it hard to be sympathetic to this but down the road everyone will be affected by this. Its pretty much a sure bet they are so firm about this issue because the entire expansion is most likely based around these ideas and they would have to revamp it so if its not handled now it never will be, a few months down the road when the next profession is hit with it will be to late.

The question is if we did a protest that did not disrupt others game play how many could we get to participate in it?

Would something like having 50 dragons in every zone do it or would that in itself crash same zones just pissing people off?

Or lining 200 or 300 dragons around the lake outside newland shouting No level restrictions get some attention and perhaps get alot of people sympathetic to our cause to join in?

A mass letter writing campain is definately called for as well as all fan sites and such being asked to support us and post a well thought out and written letter by someone in our community.

Or would it take everyone just canceling their accounts in protest perhaps not permanantly but temporarally in protest, this would be noticed by the money people and probably garner the largest amount of notice of all the ways.

While its true that protests like the one proposed do render change it has much historical precedence to it, this is however just a game and passionate as we may be me included we really don't want to anger our friends and fellow gamers who would otherwise support us for the most part.

I agree with you Serena.

Severely inconveniencing people who are potentially in the same boat as ourselves is not, in my opinion, a justified answer to our problems. Sure, we may try and speculate that these victims of ours will see us ruining thier game-play and "see the light" and get pissed at Funcom...but most likely, and if I were them, they are just going to be annoyed with the dragon-morphed players that they see on their screen.

Anarchy Online can be hard enough to enjoy at times without other players using your anger to make a point.

There has to be a peaceful solution to this, maybe starting a "March of the Leets" (more adventurers can join in this way) in which we and any other players who wish to join us shout our concerns and show our disapproval. We could use convincing slogans (eg I remember reading a post about "what if there were level requirements on wrangles, etc.") about the over-use of level requirements, and/or copy(there are several out and about on the general boards at the moment)/construct a post about that same issue and post it on the other profession boards.

I just don't agree with using the currently-speculated form of protest (ie disrupting the game-play of others) at all, or at least until we have tried every single viable alternative.

I, for one, refuse to participate in ruining the fun of others. Some of you may be thinking "but when every profession has level-restricted nanos, they would have wished for the inconvenience", but there is surely more than one way to go about being heard, being influential, and being effective. I suggest that we try those other ways before stooping so low as to what seems to be a situation in which we are apparantly "taking a hostage" (What else would you call harassing other players so that the authorities will fulfill our demands?) and soiling our honor, our reputations, and our decency (and my conscience and others besides) in the process.
