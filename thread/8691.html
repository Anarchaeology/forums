Ive been a soldier since launch and just moved onto an engie. Ive seen the changes Soldiers had and what they were supposed to do.

Since launch Soldiers saw two changes to them and nothing else, where every other class has had huge changes, perhaps maybe not including ENF.

We had our MK shield changed drastically. We could take on Slayerdroids at lvl40 cos we would reflect 75% of all dmg back to the mob, meant slayerdroids only did 200 max dmg...Hell i even saw Soldiers killing Juggernauts no sweat.

They then changed it so mobs would hit us for min dmg and we would reflect a certain amount of dmg back....like 48 for MK5 or something (cant remember exactly)

FC said it was temporary and they made the change pointless when Mobs of our alignment stopped giving EXP so people would stop doing it. However they haven't changed it back.

The second change was lowering the overall dmg from burst and flingshot and lowering the chance of us getting a crit thru burst and flingshot.

Now seriously in the 6 months of AO could this really be all they've done to soldiers?

Well they added the nano reduction thing to Tank armor and not to Coats.......

At lvl120 with a QL200 Nova and 4k ac and with MK5 shield i must admit im no pushover, but when i see NT's dealing 5k dmg and Agents shooting normally for 8k, even traders with vector shotguns hitting for 4k damage quicker than i can shoot for 200, flingshot for 1200 and burst for 1800....WITH crit buff....i wonder what the point is.

And because Agents can use MK shields 4 times higher at the same lvl, which negates anything unique to our profession theres no point to the soldier profession.

We are just tag along in team missions, we can take some flak and do some damage but master at nothing.

Its a shame ive been a soldier for a very long time and know the profession inside and out, straining it to get the most from him...

Soldiers are a joke of AO.
