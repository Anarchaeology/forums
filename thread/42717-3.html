Albrect is very nice indeed to every class (well almost, if you can gather enough str/sta to use it). To counter the 'all wearing tank armor now' -effect the solution should in no way be a Tank armor nerf but to make, again, more alternatives suited to other professions.

Look Nelebs coat for example. It has all the right componets to be desirable to all caster classes but because it being always dropped with a static QL its life span is really short. So at the moment Albrect has the following perks:

modify self chemical ac 250
modify self cold ac 750
modify self energy ac 1100
modify self fire ac 875
modify self max nano 400
modify self melee ac 1100
modify self nano cost modifier 0
modify self nano init 50
modify self poison ac 250
modify self projectile ac 1100
modify self psychology 20
modify self radiation ac 250
modify self run speed -50

Impressive.. Really impressive. The nano cost modifier means that for quite nice AC (and a very impressive appearance i might add) you get no penalties to casting and it even gives your nano pool a nice boost. Why not to use it? I'd suppose people, especially every caster class, would give up using tank armors if they had a similiar counterpart as the tank but balanced more towards the nano skill abilities Now lets see if we could recreate those Albrect perks to some new item:

modify self chemical ac 750
modify self cold ac 250
modify self energy ac 800
modify self fire ac 275
modify self max nano 500
modify self melee ac 800
modify self nano cost modifier -10%
modify self nano init 75
modify self poison ac 250
modify self projectile ac 800
modify self radiation ac 250
modify self evade clsc -15
modify self dodge ranged -15
modify self dodge ranged -14
... etc ...

and modifiers to perhaps selected nano skills and for example to intelligence. Perhaps this item could be a cloak in appearance, one not being over the armor but hanging from your shoulders or something. Bonuses to nano skills, lowered nano casting cost and nano pool but poorer ACs than with Tank armors would most certainly create desire to wear it before the Tank armor naturally because it'll allow you to cast your nanos more efficiently and cast them before than without the 'cloak'. One'd think an item like this would be really hard to balance out but something like this would help to remake the tank armor only desirable by tanking classes, or should i say, the non-casting classes.
