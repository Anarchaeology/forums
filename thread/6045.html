I respectfully disagree
Are debuffs a pain? You bet! Unfortunately, I don't think having them expire at the end of combat is a preferred solution.

If the NF is designed to last for X minutes or X seconds, then it should. An RPG has to walk a fine line between getting caught up in the minutia and abandoning all feel of 'immersiveness'. Simply taking the expedient route around every pain in the ass feature deeply cuts into the truly immersive experience.

I hate having to waste time waiting for a debuff to wear off. So what does that mean. It means that I try very hard every level to find IP for Nano Resist. It also means that I approach spell casters with extreme caution. If debuffs just magically dispersed after combat, my approach to combat and IP would be different.

In all fairness, i won't say that this approach is superior to having the debuffs dissolve after combat is completed. And, as I have already stated, it is a pain in the posterior for me too. However, I think that the 'feel' it adds to the gaming experience is good.

I'm just glad we don't have to 'eat'! :-)
Just my 2 creds.
