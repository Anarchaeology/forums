Dead, NTs should naturally own melee classes with roots. However, roots and NR are so strange in function(or lack thereof) that I see mobs and PCs that keep running after the root, and I see people getting chain rooted like crazy.

In a sane, balanced world we would have something similar, or at least close to this:

NT beats Enforcer, MA, Melee Adventurer with range. MP beats NT by debuffs. Fixers, Traders, Crats beat MPs by rooting their pets and laughing at them. So on and so forth, with maybe one class having an OK chance at winning all fights.

If this happened, you'd see MORE group PvP, since you no longer "own" all the other classes. In a massive battle, it really doesn't matter if you TMS MKX or Nullity, or Grid Armor, or whatever buff you have. You're seen as a threat, and you get stomped on.
