My thoughts...
Just to put a historical spin on this, there once was a time when you wear a full set of armor underneath a swimsuit. Although this was unrealistic, it didn't give any sort of advantage. My problem with this as someone who likes to have the greatest flexibility in clothes wear, is that this "social armor" could no longer be paired with headgear like sunglasses or different style shoes.

My suggestion is that they differentiate between back armor and social wear so that social clothing is not tied to battlesuits. Also, they should add a tag for swimsuits and lingerie so that they appear underneath other clothing/armor, and not above. Suppose you are wearing a full set of armor, a cloak worn in the back slot should cover that armor. On the other hand, if you are wearing lingerie, then I would expect the armor to cover the lingerie. But it would be revealed partially if you removed your chest armor. Doesn't that make sense?

I understand that most people don't care about this issue, but I would really like to see it addressed. Thus, my tagline.
