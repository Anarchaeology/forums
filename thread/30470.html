Bring back Old-School LLTS!
Okay, I know some people are gonna support me, and most are going to flame me (probably because they spent 50m on buying one)

Anyway, now that crit buffs are nerfed (-MAs), I don't see what the harm of bringing back the old school LLTS. I believe this is a much better way to balence the game (i.e. those with old LLTS and those without) than nerfing them. (it seems a little unfair, doesn't it?)

Also, the only time there was really a true problem with crits is when you have UVC on. Now that's nerfed.

Here's the math now:

Old LLTS:

13 - 15 + 7% (MA crit buff or Crat Speech) + 1 from an extra item, perhaps a globe of clarity or wolf form, etc. That is 21-23% crit chance.

New LLTS:
6 + 7 + 1 = 14%

23%, pretty much maximum crit chance. It's already like that for most high level players anyway - so why make it so the newer ones cannot have it? The initive reducer on them is not as bad as many people think; I have a 14% scope and have positive 94 initives in a dark blue skill with no buffs.

Anyway, I have a 14% scope and still support bringing them back.
