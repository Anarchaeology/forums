To-hit formula - Dodge , Duck Explosion , Evade , Nano Resist
I have read a few threads on the bulletin board that people (how many i do not know) feel that the above skills mentioned - Dodge , Duck Explosion , Evade , and Nano Resist - do not work at all and Nano Resist "only work 10% of the time" . I personally have not experienced those skills not working , just that they either hit or miss during a time period in combat - A couple of shots hit , followed by a couple of shots missed , and couple of shots hit again , and a couple of shots missed , and repeat - or i hit all the time . The only exception is when i use MA on monsters which i will miss all the time , afterall i only have 24 MA and i am level 64 against monsters of similar levels or higher . However , my brawl almost always hit , and i also have about 24 brawl only .

While i do not know the actual formula for the to-hit chance and why it is working this way (bugged or bad formula) i suggest a new to-hit formula -

Hit ratio = [Weapon Initiative + Weapon Skill*] ÷ 2

Miss ratio = Evade skill (Dodge , Duck Explosion , Evade or Nano Resist)

% To-Hit chance = [Lower ratio ÷ Higher ratio] x 100

Weapon Skill* = As defined in the Attack category in the weapon description , for example a weapon using Pistol and Grenade weapon skills may have "Pistol 67% , Grenade 33%" . If it is MA and special attacks it should purely use that skill's number , such as the Brawl special attack purely using the skill points you have in Brawl for Weapon Skill .

If Soldier A has 110 Weapon Initative , using a weapon that requires Shotgun(67%) and Assault Rifle (33%) , have 136 Shotgun and 143 Assault Rifle , and attacking a Leet A with 98 Dodge -

Hit ratio = [110 + (136 x 67% + 143 x 33%)] ÷ 2
= [110 + (91.12 + 47.19)] ÷ 2
= [110 + 138] ÷ 2
= 248 ÷ 2
= 124

Miss ratio = 98

% To-Hit chance = [98 ÷ 124] x 100
= 0.79 x 100
= 79

This will make use of the weapon initiatives more so that they are actually of use . Unfortunately , i have noted three big problems with this formula so far -
1 - The % to-Hit chance is dramatically lower at low levels since the Hit ratio and Miss ratio numbers are smaller .
2 - This cause a major problem to people using Low Light Scope .
3 - Since weapon initiative is useless as felt by some people (not so how many) , this can dramatically affect those that have not put any into weapon initiatives at all .

A way to fix this is to modify Hit ratio formula to the following -

Hit ratio = [(Weapon Initiative x 25%) + (Weapon Skill x 75%)] ÷ 2

However , i will leave it to people who reply to this thread to decide on that . Thanks for any positive replies you make . And if you think all that i have typed in this thread is complete junk , say it , i have gotten use to it . I do know i have spent at least 40 minutes with typing this thread , however , with at least half the time used to think how the formula should be . I am only trying to improve the game that we play .

Addition - Offensive Nanoformulas such as those employed by a Nanotechnician should use the nanoskills required by the nanoformula as Weapon Skills , as defined by the nanoformula description .
