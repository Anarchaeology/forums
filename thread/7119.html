Could AO be more futuristic?
I don't know if this has been covered before but I thought I'd bring it up anyway.

Although I've only been playing AO for a short while, every now and then when I'm playing I get the feeling that the game should be more futuristic. For a world that's thousands of years in the future, with the exception of nano-technology, there isn't really a whole lot of evidence that Rubi-Ka is that far in the future. Yes, there are advanced guns and monsters, implants, planes and so on, but it still seems kind of ordinary, and I still have the nagging feeling that the game is missing a real futuristic essence.

This is not a whine or a nag, even though I've had some horrendous problems with the game, I still like it and I believe in the potential of the game. Admittedly a lot of ideas are still in development but there still seems to be that "otherworldly" aspect missing. Something that really makes me feel that I am in a far, far future.

Having said this, unfortunately I can't really come up with any concrete ideas, I just wanted to bring it out and see what people think. Perhaps stimulate some brain-storming. Alternate dimensions are an option (which may already be on the drawing board). What about having a skill that develops psychic/telekinetic like powers (but not through nanos), even to the point of having more outrageous architecture........... I don't really know what that "thing" is that's needed, I just feel that it's not quite there yet.

Just some thoughts.........<putting flame suit on>

For far-future sci-fi, I suggest you look up an author named Cordwainer Smith. Pretty unknown, but he did a great job at extrapolating a future society (and societies) over an extended period of time (up to 16,000 AD or so, I think). Excellent stuff.

I feel the same way as you do; unfortunately, I'd take it to the extreme and say that, by the year 30,000 (or whatever year we're short of in AO), human society would be -nothing- like it is today; and thus nothing like it is on Rubi-Ka. But that makes it sort of difficult to play.

Nanotechnology basically allows for anything and everything. We've got genetic engineering down pat. We've colonised thousands, perhaps hundreds of thousands of worlds. And yet.. we still run around, shooting each other with guns and hacking with swords? It makes very little sense. The problem is that, no, it's -not- otherworldly, and it's not far-future, and there's probably nothing that can be done about it.

The other problem is that the backstory - the history of humanity up to this point (as far as the game is concerned) - can only be bought for $20 or whatever in the form of a so-so book (judging by the chapters on the website). We don't know what brought humanity to this point. Personally, it's a game, and if FC doesn't have the decency to sum up the apocalypse or whatever the heck it is that happened to provide for their "immersive story", they're not getting another $20 out of me. =p They've tossed us part one of a rather skimpy timeline that only handles a little bit of the settling of Rubi-Ka, which, I feel, is rather less important than what carried us to this point.

So instead, I look at AO as a MUD with guns and some magic stuff, and leave it at that. If I want something compelling and believable, I pick up a book. This is just filler.
