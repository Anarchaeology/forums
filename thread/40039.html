Mid Level PVP
I know i'm not alone in this, but as a 90ish level peep, i'd really like to be able to pvp somewhere other than the arena's without getting ganked by bored 180+ folks in Kill-me-dere or 2ho.

Why not have a 50% gas where there are level restrictions on pvp, such as 75-124, 125-200 or something. These could be new dungeon like areas so as not to screw up current places where you can have wars with lots of people at most any level.

I want to kill Omni's that i have a chance to kill, instead of dying from 900pt crits at every shot. It will be a long time before i can participate and have a chance to survive, and its a downer.

Heck, this game needs more interesting pvp areas if you ask me.
