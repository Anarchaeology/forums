welp!
1) The best archer, skill-wise, is the Martial Artist.
2) Arrows, for one reason or another, are the most expensive ammunition (20x the price of bullets).
3) The "bow special attack" does not work. You simply "wave" at your enemy as it kills you.
4) The damage of bows in not that great.
5) The maximum ammo of bows is dismally low.
6) Bows are fairly slow.
7) For a decent bow, you will need to add to horribly "blue" skills (ones that are tough to raise) such as aimed shot.

Nice to RP an archer, but might not be worth it...

Either way, have fun!
