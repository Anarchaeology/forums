Bureaucrat Pets! Simple tweak?
Originally posted in the Bureaucrat forums, repeated here for reasons of obvious utility:

There are a lot of common gripes about pets -- their pathfinding capabilities not the least of them. But what really irritates me is the fact that my 'crat's pets call him "Master."

That's not very professional, now is it?

Given the nature of the Bureaucrat and the fact that their pets are warped in from an office environment, wouldn't it make more sense for 'crats to be referred to by their pets as "Boss" or something similar?

I realize that the code itself may not be robust enough, and the international (multi-lingual) nature of AO might not facilitate this change, but who here agrees that this is at least a marginally decent idea that should be simple (painless!) to execute?
