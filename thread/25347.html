Phear Leet
Agree
I have to go along with this suggestion. I remember when I was a total newbie seeing NPC mobs wearing at most one piece of armor (usually a vest) usually with no helmet, and with social armor sleeves and pants and thinking, "This'll be easy." And I could never figure out why it wasn't.

Then I find out, many months later, from more experienced players, that it has been all but proven that NPC armor icons have no relationship to mob armor. NPC armor class is the same across all 8 ACs, and related only to level and type. The icons are just decoration.

Similarly, someone from Funcom, I think it was Cosmik, admitted that NPC weapons are there only to provide range and fire rate. Mob damage is also related only to level and type. I always wondered where they were getting pistols that had such insanely high minimum damage. Now I know. They're not really "using" "pistols." They just do impact AC damage at pistol range. The pistol icon is just there for decoration.

Well, as decoration, they fail. NPCs look totally unrealistic. They act unrealistic, too. At a bare minimum:

  • NPCs should be wearing plausible armor for their breed.
  • By NPC level 10, they should be in matching armor sets with helmets.
  • If they're going to be consistent at all levels and unrelated to the armor type (and it shouldn't be), then NPCs should have something like 100% melee/impact/energy AC, 75% chemical/fire/cold, 60% radiation/poison, in order to make PC weapon damage something closer to realistic.
  • At the same levels where PCs typically change weapons, so should the NPC mobs, even if all it affects is the icon. I don't want to see level 240 enforcers with baseball bats. Who does this?
  • By level 10, OT NPCs should pretty consistently be wearing the Omni-Tek Suit and Omni-Tek Armor over their armor.


These are not ridiculous things to be asking. I've seen that NPCs do change their armor appearance from level to level. It never gets plausible, but it does go up in appearance, including yes, lots of Clan NPCs wearing Omni-Pol Desert helmets. So they do have some kind of algorithm in the software already that skins them differently based on level, and in the case of the helmets that suddenly appear around level 30-40, even re-mesh them. I'm just asking for that skin to be more plausible looking. I'm not asking them to recalculate NPC ACs based on those icons; I'm just asking for a more realistic formula. I'm not asking them to randomly select weapons and use those weapons to generated damage and rate of fire, I'm just asking for the weapon icons to change three or four times per NPC type over the total level range.

These are all capabilities that I know that the software already has. I'm just asking the Community Team to kick the art departments' and the developers' butts until they stop making NPCs look silly.
