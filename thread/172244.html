Akaran idly sipped some pseudo-coffee as he looked over some of the recent intelligence summaries from Clan territories. It wasn't something he was assigned to, but he had had the security clearances for months now. It had become something of a relaxing ritual for him, even if he didn't know why he was allowed to look at the summaries.

"Same old same old for notum mining...bah, AF and Reform will cover things for now. I guess we'll have to deal with it if they get too close to the southern lands."

Shuffling the papers briefly, Akaran glanced at a form marked with the three blue stripes of a Class 2 event. Class 1 and 2 occurences were less about the Clans, and more about the fate of Rubi-Ka itself. The Tir Accords being voided, the Shadowkands being opened -- these were Class 1. But a Class 2 in the intelligence reports?

The text was no more illuminating. Someone calling himself "Vision", who claimed to be the last surviving heir of "Alyssa". The name seemed familiar somehow. The report indicated there was some sort of connection to the legendary David Marlin believed, but...

Akaran shook his head, putting it out of mind as he lifted his Omni-Pol marked coffee mug back to his mouth. This "Vision" seemed to be an apoclyptic preacher of some fashion, perhaps connected to the recent development of a stable portal to the Shadowlands.

Part of him wanted to write this "Vision" off as just another unimportant nutball, but Akaran's gut told him that such a wacko wouldn't be worth a Class 2. And in Omni-Pol, you either learned to trust your gut -- or you died.

Can any ordinary day be considered such in extraordinary times? Nathan Ammicus Age mused to himself while watching the throng of investigators, engineers and regular employees surrounding the central monument in Omni-1 Trade.

The day had certainly begun ordinary enough. A morning at the office, followed by general field training, ending with a quiet moment discussing Rubi-Kan politics with Zack Eternalforce Craddock over a cup of coffee in a darkened corner Rompa Bar. Then the call came.

Eternalforce reaches into his armour and retrieves a small silver datapad, which is flashing wildly

"Seems like something's going down in Omni-1 Trade." Eternalforce muttered aloud, eyes fixed on the screen in his hand. "Want to head out?"

"Definitely." Nathan replied, standing from his seat and placing the half-full cup on the adjacent table. "Lead the way."

The pair had departed Rompa with some haste, arriving in Omni-1 Trade a mere minute after Eternalforce had been warned. What they had arrived to was nothing short of pandemonium; seven or eight official investigators questioned any and all who approached them, an Omni-Tek engineer worked frantically at an odd scanning device, and the empath...

Gartsyl smiles softly

"I'm showing only normal.. well.. relativly normal citizenry..." The mysterious woman scanned the crowd intensely, her eyes seemingly burrowing through layers of armour and cloth and into the very souls of those around her.

"Lieutenant Cygnee, what happened?" Nathan, turning to the nearest Omni-Pol officer, asked.

"Ammicus, we are looking for ones who have info on the visions ones have had" The officer replied.

Visions? Nathan didn't ask. It had been a topic of conversation with Mr. Craddock earlier in the eve; Nathan had read the reports by Omni-Pol Director Meister Capo regarding his encounter with one such apparition, but was it truly this widespread? Pervasive enough to warrant such a legion of official personnel scanning everything and anything in the capitol of Omni-Tek's reign?

"Engineer Gerromer, a moment of your time, please."

"Yes Citizen Ammicus?" Then engineer, shaken somewhat from the readings on his various devices, turned to face Nathan with a scanner held aloft.

Gerromer takes some readings from a small device

Ammicus glances at the scanner

"What...is this concerning exactly?" Nathan asked, taking a small step back from the engineer.

"We are investigating reports of some kind of "Visions" that have apparently been appearing in the area recently" The engineer responded, his gaze unbreaking from the his screen.

"It is not only this area, good sir. Rumours abound throughout Omni-1 as well." Nathan replied.

"Are these "visions", as you put it, a threat to the populace?"

"That is something we are as yet unsure of, we dont believe so, but we need to find out more about them." The engineer sighed ever so quietly, an overt sign of inner frustration.

"This is most coincidental to the recent work of JAME..." Ammicus glanced off in thought, twisting those words around in his head. Jobe...this mysterious Portal...the Shadowlands...riddle upon riddle upon riddle.

"I'm afraid i couldn't possibly comment on that Ammicus" The engineer's response shook Nathan from his musings.

"I understand completely, Engineer Gerromer. But I must say it is wonderful to see the authorities looking over us." Nathan replied, smiling. He turned to walk back to Eternalforce, who was being questioned by one of the investigators.

"Have you yourself experienced any "Visions" Ammicus?" The engineer asked, and Nathan froze in his tracks.

"Me? No. Aside from the burn in my retina from staring at my datapad for far too long."

Gerromer smiles

"I have suffered at the hands of Retina burn myself."

Nathan turned ever so slightly, and, smiling at the engineer, replied "I think if anyone can truly appreciate that it would indeed be an engineer."

"Well that was interesting." Eternalforce had returned, and now stood at Nathan's side. Both watched as the crowd slowly disbursed, and the offical entourage moved through the Whompha into the Entertainment district.

Ammicus nods solemnly

"Indeed, Mr. Craddock." Nathan exhaled a confused whaft of breath. "And quite disturbing as well..."

"Here I was thinking those visions were something I ate...The Commander and Investigator seemed very interested in what I saw...or heard."

"And what was it that you heard?" Nathan asked, turning to Eternalforce.

"A lot of gibberish. Stuff about entering a portal, and realizing our destiny, and evolving...Really spacey stuff."

"Wonderful. Now I see what you mean about not understanding this meta-crap."

Eternalforce chuckles

"Well, I think I've had enough excitement for one evening. I'll bid you a good night, Mr. Age."

"Indeed. It has been a pleasure meeting you, Mr. Craddock. Though I do hope our future meetings are more...sane." Nathan smiled, as he and Eternalforce parted ways.

Coincidental indeed...
