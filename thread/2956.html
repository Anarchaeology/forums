I am sickened. I heard Brad McQuaid left Verant, is he working for FunCom now? Cosmik, I love(d) this game, please use what little influence you may have to try bring some semblance of balance back in favor of the players. You know if it happens, there will be a major re-surgence in us players disillusioned with what we're doing now. You know this, bro, give us a sign.
