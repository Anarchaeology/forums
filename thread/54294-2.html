Do a latter system like I suggested. If you are good at PvP, you would likely to end up with high rating in it. If you suck, well you probably would not even participate in a ladder.

The whole point is that you can challenge anyone within your level range, if refused they will lose automaticaly points.

Make a system that works outside the game itself and you would know who are good PvPers and who simply like to farm titles. I had this same problem when playing Ages of Conquerors, albeit Zone.com had a ladder system, it was flawed.
