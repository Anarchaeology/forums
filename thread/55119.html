Maps and Containers - new enhancement ideas
Would be awesome to get some new map enhancements (in addition to arrow, monsters, people, etc etc)

1. Mark Spot; would be really cool to be able to mark your map, with a different color "X" or something, as well as being able to attach a note to the mark... "here's that nasty boss that always eats me on the way to missions" and of course "here's the Elevator button"

2. Retain Mission Maps; why oh why couldn't we retain mission maps while the mission is still incomplete???? Seems like a perfectly feasible gameplay thing to me; I can't think of any reason why deleting them is the only way it could work design wise.

3. Backpacks; need adventuring skill? Hrm. Why??? Just need strength (stamina?) to carry the dern thing I presume. Doesn't take a rocket scientist to just *put on* a backpack Mebbe just for the regular ol' large backpacks? (I haven't really looked at all the others yet, dunno their special "adventuring" type capabilities if any).
