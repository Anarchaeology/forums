easier mission making needed
I think its way to complicated to make a mission and try to get the one you want. So here is a list of changes I would like to see.

1.) You choose what enemies you want to see in the mission. If you want to see all greens that are in your level range then you should be able to choose. The way it is right now you just move the marker in the green area, but sometimes you end up getting all grays, or greens and oranges. It turns into a guessing game, and that is annoying.

2.) I want to see missions actually give you something that you need as rewards. If you are a agent for example then the only weapons you get are sniper rifles. And you get weapons and armor based on your skills. I'm sick of seeing enforcer nano's or soldier weapons as rewards.

3.) Missions should always give their exact location. I dont understand why this was never put into it before, but its very annoying to have to guess where the zone is that you are suppose to be going to. Even using the rubi-ka map doesnt really help, because it just list the city's, not the zones.

thats all I can think of for now, please add more idea's if you have them.

Soleet
1. Choose the types of enemies that are in the mission? I don't think the enemy would change who they have guarding the location just to make it easier for you. There is a difficulty slider there so you can pick a mission within your range.

2. Always give items that you need? I am against this idea. It will make people start to play solo, ending up in a single player game that you pay a monthly subscription for. It is intentional for two reasons. Reason one, it adds challenge to the game. If everything was handed to you on a silver platter, this would get boring real fast. Second, it is meant to promote interactions between players. You want agent stuff but keep getting enforcer and soldier stuff? Well, maybe the soldiers and enforcers are getting agent stuff! Try looking around for the stuff sometimes. It even helps teamplay. At my current level, I am now in Warbot range. I keep trying to pull missions for a Warbot, which is find only, but one rarely comes up. However my guild leader was able to pull Warbot missions galore to help out me and a fellow engineer.

3. Always give the exact location? Well there goes the fun in looking for it (not that there was much fun anyways). I think it helps people by making them explore and appreciate the work done in this game.
