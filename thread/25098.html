Class specific only Missions
Since not all proffessions are created equal thought i would start a thread about class specific missions that could be implemented to make it so non-combat heavy classes can earn their livings in AL and also to breed some more variety into the game.

These are just a few i came up with feel free to add your own.


Fixer class:

B&E mission:
Object of the mission is to go and retreive/steal, an object of information for a client. These missions would be the type of mission where almost if not all the doors chests would be trapped with bombs of varying destructive power, very little mobs inside but enough to keep us on our toes.

Perhaps make it so mobs will get spawned with unsuccessfull disarm, make it so they are responding to the commotion made by the bang of the bomb going off. Mob levels would be a little lower than normal mission to enable fixers to survive trough and earn what would be considered normal xp.

Class to obtain mission: proffession Fixer
Obtainable from: Shady Blackmarket contact NPC


Agent class:

Infiltration mission:
Another variant of solo missions with agents in mind would be the infiltrate and retreive object . The agent will have need to sneak trough the mission area to obtain the object. The mission will be populated by NPC mobs and if the agent sneaks away successfully and he brings back the item back to his employer he receives a very hefty exp bonus for his troubles.

This mission type would be of a size considered enough both in time investment and difficulty to obtain the item to justify giving enough exp as a kill all mobs type of solo mission we enjoy today.

Class to obtain mission: proffession Agent
Obtainable from: Human ressources department Omni- XXX/Clan.../Neutral... (xxx beeing the division the agent has chosen to affiliate himself with )


Adventurer class:

Reconnaissance/ search and retreive mission:
This type of mission would be conducted in outdoor areas where the adventurers abilites shine best. With above average map navigation and outdoorsy type nano's the advanturer would be most at ease with this type of mission.

This mission could be of a nature as to ask to either travel to a remote area ( area determined by difficulty of mission) and identify an object or NPC or to travel to an area and confirm some specific locations for the Travel agencies mapmaker division of
Rubi-ka.

These types of mission could require the adventurer to utilize their morphs to observe and "live" with certain animal type mobs and check up on their migration paterns or other easily implementable features unique for the advents.

Class to obtain mission: proffession Adventurer
Obtainable from: Rubi-ka tourist industry.



Tradeskill oriented class:

Retreive a prototype weapon mission:
This type of mission would need a little more planning to implement to make it possible and availlable to all tradeskill classes, could also be a team mission. This type of mission would be of a type as to offer this scenario:

Item X has been stolen from our labs in destination Y. According to our sources the "ItemX" has been dismantled and is stored in different areas of the mission. Please go there and gather all the essential parts, re-combine the weapon/item and return it to us ... blah blah

Class to obtain mission: All classes (tradeskill check?)
Obtainable from: different NPC sales merchant


I think you get the idea of where i'm going with this =). These should be fairly easy to implement.
They would bring different ways for different classes to get exp. It would make each classes have
a little something that's unique to them and them alone, or something unique they can bring to a team.
Every class specific mission types would be made so everyclass that chooses to do them have the option
to be able to gather exp in much the same abundance as heavy combat proffessions can do at present.

It would also infuse Rubi-ka with a more rich and diverse mission system.

Please, post your ideas and comments, for new ideas on class specific missions please keep in mind that it should be fairly easy to implement so as funcom would not have to have to put a lot of their ressources to implement and give us better chances of seeing something like this in the near future =) Post ideas for your own proffession or comment on the ideas posted above
