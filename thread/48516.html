Minor Engineer Fixes
Engineers is a support class, and have bad weaponskills. The Engineer is supposed to support the team with auras, gadgets, a tanking robot but most of them is useless or isn't working as it should. Here's a list of minor things Cosmik may take a look at and report it to the development team.

1. The auras will break a sneak mode since it refreshed every 20 second, that's very annoying for agents and others using Aimed Shot. Please fix this, what's the point with our auras when this happens?

2. Engineers have an aura that works like a blind (Disruptive Photon) - but it only refreshes every 20 seconds. That would be nice if it also pulsed right away after a cast - but that isn't the case.

What I want to see fixed with this nanoline is that it pulses right away after a cast (so the enemies get affected when you cast it). Today you have to take 20 seconds of beating before it works (or stand really close for 20 seconds) so it very useless in a team. Remember, this nano isn't as good as charm and the blindness nano for NTs since it breaks whenever it get hit. So if anyone in the team is using reflect bracers etc will the nano break and the enemy starts hitting you again.

Another thing is that it also breaks when the enemy is casting a nano at you (not sure, but that's what the mob did at me), it shouldn't break, it should only break when you are hitting or hurting (eg. casting a nano at it) the enemy.

3. Engineers isn't that good making high quality stuff, and can never do this alone without the traders tradeskill buffs. To make this nanoline more usefull both for traders and engineers is to increase the duration from 30 minutes to 2 or 3 hours. This will also make things easier for the traders, since they don't need to drain all the time.

4. Just a question, will the Kamikazee arrive when the petpathing is working as it should?


I only want engineers to be more usefull in teams, and the blindness aura could be useful if it worked at the same time as you cast it, instead of waiting 20 seconds. I would like any comments from you Cosmik, especially about point 2 and 3.
