Rested Sentiment

It echoed down the cityway passage for mere seconds before swallowed within the storm pushing forward across the Entertainment zone of Omni-1.

No-one else would have heard it, no implant could even heighten ones senses to detect the slightest whisper of the uttered word "... peace?" The end of a sentence spoken out to judgement.

It had been two years of becoming one with what had been taught...

... no.

What had been fed throughout his internal training, his development. Arriving amongst a crowd that numbered in their thousands, dripped through efficiently into Rubi-Ka and the company. Things are different now then what they were, is that not always the way of existence? Pushing on onto pages that are not quite completely written if not at all. Of course most would not remember their creation or recreation. Older members of Rubi-Ka would not recollect the machines that changed their bodies, the devices that constantly forced energy through unconstructed bodies... the beginning. Currently, people arrive from beyond this process, recreating their lives after processed through the orbiting station.

Rain followed wind. The notum storm now finding new pastures beyond the Entertainment zone and the creator of his own finality, for now, still seemed unsure as to what do to. At a total loss of how to allow himself to discover the answer to the incoming wave upon wave of questions he could hear screaming to himself, pleading for haste to pull through this situation he found himself kneeling before.

What must he do?

Take to what is to be done. Fatal choices were to be forced into reality now. Again. As always.

The Daemon Squad Specialist kept his stance taunt, aggressive toward any motive in objection to an incoming future to become part of countless histories. Feeling it uprise within, Gabriel had heard it before it was repeated a second time. Finality now flavoured the words.

"Please... " The objective yet again searching for a means to an end, to escape what was to become. "Please, there must be another way... Wait! Take a step toward showing that there can be peace?"

Gabriel could simply stare ahead at the kneeling figure, keeping himself to the shadows away from clear sight. Reclaim residual effects were not always effective on removing all previous uninsured experiences from individuals."I will... offer you your peace. Consider yourself saved."

Standing slowly, the objective nodded slowly. Heavy eyebrows knotting as eyes searched the darkness, another question sliding across the inside of his mind to his mouth to be spoken. Second thoughts slashed across what he suddenly considered a risky move. He instead began to move away from what had threatened him. Away from what now saved him from erasing what he stored within himself, now he could continue his mission.

Once again, across the district, the sound of energy being released into the air errupted. The storm swept again as if it had returned for something forgotten. The rain increased and soon soaked into the body of a man who lay dead across a cityway passage. Pinned to his clothing near to the fatal entry wound around the lapel of a smouldering shirt collar, a dark winged figure.

Thicker than Water - Stolen Thoughts
Do you feel it?

Savour it, breathe it in through tightened jaws, gritted teeth. Suck up the air, filter the sensations.

Can you feel it?

Yes... yes you can.

Keep at it now. Why stop? Do not stop. Never stop. Motion is what keeps you safe. Keep moving. Keep going.

Feeling it searing comfort into your veins now?

Runs thicker than water. Precious that it is.
No more now. Replicated over and over... over and over.

Oh you can take more.

Bring more, take more. Plead away to escape the numbness that threatens to take this away.

Kill it now.

Kill it...

Keep taking. Give no more. Give no more, for tears shall pour from ducts bled raw... mark it down to shadowed sound.

Feel the fear. Feel the bitter savouring pain.

The hooded shape heaves shoulders violently, breathing hard with mind intent on controlling itself from losing all control, all automotive biomechanics. Forehead rested slowly against the jagged rock wall, eyes intensely focused upon things that do not exist. Things that he ignores. Tensing his neck to tilt his head to a slight angle he focuses on a single point beyond the surface before him, beyond a point any mind would fail to create and he spins that paradox into his brain until the voices become angry, frustrated that each other voice has it's opinions out there over theirs... and so he hears them scream over screaming.

Before moving back one step from what he faces he lowers his shredded fists away from the wall, congealing, sticky matter clinging to both skin and stone until both arms rest alongside his body which suddenly calms in radical movement. Gabriel holds a final stare at the nonexistent with a tightened brow... then walks away into the city, pulling on his armoured gloves. Two pools of his own blood on either side of his boot and what covered and drips from the wall now left to starve. The flow contained for now.

He has much work to do.

So much unfinished business to start, to find, to seek him out... to use him.

Within... he can hear the echo's of his own howls above the many as he fades away into the city crowds of Omni-1.

"...use me! USE ME!"

Caught in the middle of somewhere...
Before he chanced himself away to the darkened areas of the city, usually his means to pass through unnoticed, Dericad must have somehow happened across his location and Gabriel would have to make certain that if a next time was due, it would be safe guarded.

The dull click gave away the weight of what was held in the desk workers hands. Satisfied that the revolver was potentially a prank, competence had arisen long before. Using his agile body and heightened senses, Dericad would have found the metal barrell end firmly placed between his lips.

... Of course this wasn't to occur.

"Enjoying your walk out from the grid?" Motioning a finger to press behind his ear a strand of hair. Slow breath pushed through Gabriel's nostrils, head tilting slightly with his eyes cast over the rim of the rounded, dark sunglasses. "Or are you trailing the spook?"
Blinking once through a moments confusion, Dericad's own special brewed caffiene, induced brief twitch that rippled across his left cheek. Revolver lowered ever so slightly although still pointing to the back of Gabriel's neck and Gabriel seemed to be making no move at all against this mock hostile action. Dericad shrugged.
"Not at all Gabe, not at all. On the trailing reference. Just showing interest and curiosity as to what you get up to. That... is all."
Taking a few focused steps forward, Gabriel smiled to himself. "And you do this by such a dangerous method of interest."
"You're a dangerous man Gabe, I've seen your file. April has placed me in charge of keeping the files in order and..."
"... I wouldn't continue."
Dericad looked bewildered at the sudden interruption which quickly faded with a quick nod. "Yes, you're right. Too much information."
"Rather, information that isn't important for me to know."
Dust picked up between the two men with a sudden, strong gust. Gabriel had kept his figure poised for any hostile actions that the desk jockey could show, as the wind swept Gabriel's long coat out across his left side, Dericad could see that Gabriel's leg muscles were tensed for sudden movement. Picking up one corner of his mouth into a half smile, Dericad nodded and lowered the revolver back into a holster, strapped neatly within the suit he wore.
"You should not believe everything you... should not read." The voice trailed away with the dying wind, Dericad wiped desert sand from his eye and Gabriel was gone. No hope finding him now with the agents honed skills on concealing his whereabouts or direction. Casting a glance over his immediate area and to a raised platform of construction piping he concluded that Gabriel was no longer in the area. Perhaps. Picking up his breifcase and giving it a quick brush with the sleeve of his suit, Dericad fancied himself another coffee.
"Heck, didn't want to get my new suit dirty anyway..."
