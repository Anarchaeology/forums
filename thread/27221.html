Marriage system??? => ****e up your AO lifestyle
Observing all (well... most of) the discussions about the improvement of apartments, more variety of furnitures, renting houses, and having tamed leets as pets, led me to believe that what players want is unique lifestyles (depending on the alignment and/or the profession, I suppose). I also came to think that a marriage system (and a divorce system if people really want it. :P) would really ****e up those lifestyles.

Just imagine. You are a clan soldier. After a rough day of struggle against OT oppressors for freedom, you come back to your home, greeted first by an adorable leet who's ecstatic to see you, and then you give a gentle kiss to your spouse who volunteers to take your flamethrower and put it aside. You go to the refrigerator and grab a cold sludge cola (is that right? sludge cola? I still consider my self a noob. :P) , you sit yourself down on that comfy sofa of yours. You chat with your spouse about your heroic efforts of how you nearly wiped out a platoon of OT hired killers but you let the last surviving one go as a warning to the OT oppressors. (Bravo, clan soldier. ) After a nice long chat with your significant other, you turn on the stereo system and set the channel to "easy listening" (or if you much prefer hardcore stuff, you would set the channel to something like Rubi-Ka Heavy Rap Metal. (Just something I made up. :P) ) And you notice that your pet leet is sound asleep on your lap. You gently lift it up and put it in its house and give a soft kiss good night on its head. You go take a shower in the bathroom and go to bed, and just before you fall asleep with your dearly beloved spouse in your arms, you think of getting a playmate for your pet leet, in the hopes of having "leet pups."

OK. I'll admit. That was a cheeeeeeezeball.
But you get the idea.
Life after a long day's hard struggle for your cause. I believe that's what everyone wants to see happening soon.

Forever watching the stars,
Stargazer

p.s. Yes, yes. I know that marriage system is sooo AC thing.
