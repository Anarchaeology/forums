Serious organization issue needs to be resolved.
I joined an organization and was promoted to rank 1 (second rank down from the leader, who is rank 0).

For some reason, the leader left the organization and deleted his character. Myself and the other rank 1 member now are in essence the head of the organization, yet we have no real power. We have an organization treasury that cannot be accessed, we cannot modify our goals, objectives, etc. In fact all we CAN do is recruit people, promote them to one rank beneath us, and kick people.

I cannot believe we are the only organization something like this has happened to.

I also cannot understand why the programmers didn't forsee this as a problem and have safeguards to make it not so.

All that needs to happen is when the leader leaves, the power goes to the next rank (or everyone is promoted one rank). In the case of multiple rank 1 holders (such as mine), give them the option to vote in a new leader.

But basically, as it stands now, our organization is screwed until Funcom changes something, or we leave. And since only the leader can disband an organization, were we to all leave you'd have yet another empty organization taking up space on your website with no members in it and no way to get members in it or delete it.

I realize we could just leave and start another group, but I don't see why WE must pay for Funcom's mistake. This issue needs to be addressed. Preferably in the next patch, if possible.

-Rielle
