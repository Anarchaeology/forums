Simple
Simple is often the best, however I have a short list of ideas for added features to help solve repetetive tasks:

- Ability to macro mouse movements according to coordinates on screen and bind these macros to keys. (Would only work in windowed mode)

- Load and save mouse movement macros

- Ability to include keystrokes in macros. This would enable users to make complex routines that executes macros put in the "quick bar" and other things.

- Pixel color detection. Im still evaluation wheter or not I want the program to support this. This was used by exploiters to do things extremely fast and get xp. The misuses for this is obvious and I DO NOT want people to exploit! At the moment Im more inclined NOT to include this.


The uses for such a program is quite big and would be nice to have. The limits would in some cases only be the imagination of the user. However, such a program could evolve into something of a "bot" that plays. Further integration with other applications like WinPCap could for example enable someone to write a buff-station bot that buffed people on request.

However, this is only ideas. At the moment Click v1.0 is final and I have no immediate work going on to add the functionallity listed above. And before I did start to develop such tools, I would need to have a formal chat with the GM's and also the person responsible for exploits in Funcom. As I said, misuse is possible. And I need to make sure what limitations on third-party software Funcom has decided on.

I will NOT be the one to provide people with the tools for exploiting!
