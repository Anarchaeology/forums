First Week Jitters
Hi Everyone,

Just dropping a note to address the "First Week Jitters" we have been hearing about. Ok, to put it plainly...What everyone calls lag.

We are aware of the issues related to lag in certain areas of Rubi-Ka. We are constantly working on these issues and with every server-side patch we are hammering out another dent. Soon you will see how smooth the metal of Anarchy Online can be. NO BONDO!

One thing we would like to ask of each of you, ok a couple...

First, please make sure you have all the latest drivers for your video cards and sound cards, preferably certified and not beta, as well as the latest DirectX drivers. There are many different links for the video cards, but to get to the latest DirectX drivers, please click the following link.

Microsoft DirectX


Ok.... Second, please watch the forums for possible fixes from players. Sometimes the best information for specific system fixes comes not from the creator of the games, but the players. This is escpecially true in the MMORPG industry. There are so many configurations, it is possible we may have missed something. Even with our extensive testing. We are proud to say we have some of the most resourceful players in the industry.

Thanks for staying fans, and thank you for being patient.
