~*Vixen Entertainment - CD Release 28th Sep*~
Vixen Entertainment is proud to announce the launch of it's first, original CD!

The CD, entitled "Vixen Entertainment - Got it Goin On",
Is available now for you to own by visiting http://www.geocities.com/becka11563/Music.html

The CD contains 15 original songs by Bladelaw, Aktrez and some of our Vixen staff - Including all of the hit songs you have heard on Gridstream Radio - "Faithful", "Got it Goin On" and "Energy Lover". Plus many, many more!

All proceeds from this CD will go towards the Vixen Radio Live365 server. We will be soon bringing you LIVE music and DJ's direct from Omni-Ent. As well as a 24/7 play list that will be populated by YOU!

To launch our new CD, Vixen entertainment will be hosting a CD release party on Saturday, September 28th. The party will start at 6pm EST and will proceed with the following DJ's in order, each broadcasting a full HOUR! Each hour we will premiere one song from the Vixen Entertainment - Got it Goin On soundtrack!

6:00 pm EST - DJ Tenchi
7:00pm EST - DJ Roxxor
8:00pm EST - DJ Solidforce
9:00pm EST - DJ Carnage
10:00pm EST - DJ Aktrez

Yup thats right! Five hours, jam packed with 5 diffrent DJ's! This will be a night to remember and just for our loyal patrons and friends of Vixen Entertainment!
