NPC NT's casting Izgimmer's Last Word
Funcom has said that when a nano is not meant to be used yet it has requirement set at 1000 each. This held true for the fixer grid nanos until they were finally implemented.

However the NT Izgimmer's Last Word nano also requires 1000 skill but the npcs being uber buffed in stats gain enough bonus skill points to cast the nano!!

This leads to the fact that NPC NT's can nuke players with a nano that is essentially a death spell to 99% of players with no chance to avoid it (due to nano resist not working)

And for the uninformed Izgimmer's Last Word deals 4,118-8,907 points of radiation damage. It has a 20 second cast time (supposedly but uber buffed npc's cast it in about 7) and because pc's cannot do controlled interrupts on npcs (how about making brawl have a high chance to interrupt nanos cast by npcs) it is instant death to most even with minimum damage

It is possible to interrupt the casting, I have seen it happen - but it's extremely unlikely. The only reliable way to stop the casting of LastWord by an NPC is to Cast Nano-Shutdown on them and hope it succeeds.

In fact, this would make going up against LastWord casting mobs relatively possible, if they didn't cast through walls and such before you even know they are there.

Is this a bug? I think not. Everything in a game is designed to slow you down - and rightly so. If you obtain Q200 gear at lvl 3 you will get bored and quit the game. Here the designers have overstepped their bounds, and thrown in fickle 'time-wasting' tactics to cause players to lose XP. There is no other reason for LastWord. However, as long as they are using this method, they really should throw in something else for good measure - perhaps they could have the save terminal zap and kill you one out of every 100 times just for fun?

Honestly, it is quite alright to create an extremely dangerous opponent such as this. As long as the XP gained justifies the risk. In the case of Vet Exterminators, the XP is laughable compared to the risk.

Of course, in the case of Aces and Real Means it isn't a matter of risk - nothing in the game justifies the time spent killing them. But that is a whole other issue.
