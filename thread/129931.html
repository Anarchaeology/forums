Where Do the Clans Stand?
We are often told not to judge the Clans as a whole by the actions of a few Clans. And many Clans have tried very hard to distance themselves from the brutality of the Sentinels, going as far as engaging the Sentinels in combat.

And so we come to the dramatic events of yesterday, 'The May Day Massacres'. Storm and allies, having gained somehow intelligence about a high level Omni Tek meeting, broke into the conference hall and, in act of terror reminiscent of the Dust Brigade, gunned down a large number of Omni-Tek faction leaders. And not just in once, but twice for when the venue was moved from Upper Stret to 4 Holes, the Clans terrorists were once again there.

I can easily imagine if the reverse had happened, by now the pages of the media would be saturated with the usual Clan apologists pointing out Omni Tek's oppression and the need to keep fighting for freedom. As in this instance OT was clearly the victim of naked aggression, outside from a few self-congratulatory remarks from Storm members which you would expect, the Clan media is remarkable quiet about the affair.

So I am left wondering, how do those moderate Clans feel about this latest act of terror? Is there any point in dialog, any hope left for a negotiated settlement, or are Storm and the Sentinels the true face of the Clans?
