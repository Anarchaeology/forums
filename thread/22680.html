Okay, let me start out by saying I have nothing against soldiers being the heavy damage dealers/secondary tanks in this game.

There, now that that is out of the way...

1. Grenades

While it is a good idea to have grenades that do DD and stun (ala Frag and concussion grenade) I don't think that having them as items equipped into the hud is the right way to go. They are a weapon, simply put. Now maybe if you had assault rifles that had underbarrel grenade launchers, that took grenade ammo, this would work, using it as a special. But individual grenades? The only way I can see these working would be if they were sold in stacks from the stores. Buy a stack, ql is nonexistent on them, damage scales in accordance with your grenade skill. Put the stack in a hand, there ya go. Maybe a five second attack time (cook off) and a .5 recharge. Granted, this would probably also require the reduction of equip time on them and other weapons, so you could switch back and forth quickly, but I think it would work. Having a grenade in your HUD that recharges indefinately is the wrong way to go about it though.

2. Mirror Shield

I know nothing about this, so I won't embarrass myself by making comments on it.

3. Assault Rifles

Let me see if I'm reading this correctly. You want professions that don't have a green assault rifle skill to have to work harder to use the assault rifles than they currently do? Essentially, a penalty for using an assault rifle and not being a soldier? Because if that is what you are asking, then I think you should rethink it. It's quite selfish to think that. Assault rifles are not profession specific. Very few weapons in the game, aside from the unique/special weapons have any profession or breed specifics at all. That is the way this game was designed, so that if you wanted to be a nanomage martial artist who wields assault rifles, you could do it. Granted it would be difficult, but not impossible. It's not fair to try and impose an additional penalty on people who try and be diverse. That's like saying anyone that isn't an atrox, who tries to use str/sta armor should be penalized even further than he is by having to spend extra IP on abilities. See my point?

If that is not how you meant that statement to mean, then please disregard the previous paragraph

4. First aid should cycle faster for everyone, not just soldiers. That should be an across the board change.

5. I don't understand this, could you please elaborate?

Catch ya planetside.
