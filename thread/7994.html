Not many are aware of the complicated situation and emtions around and stirring in an Atrox. Too many not even daring to speak the words 'I'm Atrox.. and I am gay'. Many feel that there is too much prejudice around atrox homosexuality and gay Atrox love. The less others know, the better.

But you know what you are. The mask squeezes even thighter. And your secret grows. And so does your lonelyess. Can you escape?

Let me tell you that there IS room for You. And that there are others who know what you are going through on a daily basis.
Perhaps you didnt know, but there is a MOVEMENT going on. The Gay Atrox Pride Parade of Athens (GAPPA for short) is comming togheter, Uniting as we speak.

Please - if you want to talk, and share your emtions and problems, dont hesitate!

Send email to gayatrox@hotmail.com today! Mark it: "Gay Atrox".

Thank you, and be proud!
