Semi-Random Quest Missions
I have been thinking about the current state of missions and how the basic premise is always kill everything until you get to the objective.

I have also been thinking about the current state of camping for unique items and how it pretty much sucks for those will little amounts of time (and no huge guild backing to help compete).

Wouldn't it be nice for Funcom to sort of combine the two into a quest mission generator for the solo player and teams as well.

If they spent some time to design several static quests for level gaps (for example 10 - 30, 50 - 80, etc) that would be available through a terminal like the current missions. It would basically give you a mission based on several factors when you use it:

-Level Range
-If you are in a team or not.
-And perhaps loosely even Profession (like an Agent could get a mission that had extreme mobs but would have to sneak all the way, and perhaps kill a weaker opponent at the end.)

These missions could have pre-defined layouts so as to better fit the mission, and generally look and feel better. Items rewarded would be unique/no drop so to prevent farming, and would be similar (or even completely new) to some items currently available.

The location of the mission could also vary like current missions, but the internal structure and objective would be the same for the same exact mission. Meaning two different people could get the same quest around level 25, but they could be in different mission entrances.

This is basically a move to prevent camping, improve solo player and small team ability to acquire unique quest items, add content to the masses, and give developers an ability to tailor the mission more to the player/profession.

There could even be more restrictions to help make the mission even more specific to a number of players, create keys that cannot be duplicated (so the mission is defined for the strength of one player only), or keys that can only be duplicated 2-6 times to restrict team sizes.

I doubt we'll see anything like this for a number of reasons, but I can only hope to give Funcom ideas if in the case...they have not thought about it themselves.
