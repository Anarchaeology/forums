Level 200, new stuff, IPR, and IP...
Okay...I'm not really encouraged to keep leveling 200 right now. That is probably what FunCom wants because they seem to think that discouraging leveling and slowing down the processes will somehow cause people to play the game longer...lol?

Anyways...the big problem I see is that at level 200, with all the changes that FunCom keeps introducing, I will end up screwing myself. I will eventually run out of IPR at level 200 switching to use all the new goodies or to avoid the effects of the newest and biggest nerf. Because I will be level 200 I won't have be able to level to gain anymore XP. I also wont be able to gain any more IPR points.

Here's what I'm proposing:

-- At level 200, you should be able to get 1 IPR point for every time you fill up your XP bar again. Instead of going 'Ding level up' you would go 'Ding new IPR'.

This would make it so that the new changes would not be an issue at level 200 and would also make level 200 characters the master of the game. Talk about a reason to be level 200!!! People say there is not really any big reason to be level 200 right now...this would for sure change that.

Okay...time to see what the rest of the people think about it: /flame on
