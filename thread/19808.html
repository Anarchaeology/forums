Item database Search..
Ok, does everyone hate this as much as myself? You need a NCU (nano crystal, weapon. fill in the blank) upgrade, you go to the local store, nope none here, hit the Whompa, nope, not here either, rinse and repeat. Couple of hours later you have seen most of the known world and still no NCU upgrade. So as my mama always told me dont complain unless you propose a solution. Well here it goes.

Why is there not a database lookup for items in game? The database could be accessed via the web site and updated every time the shops reset. The database could tell you what shop, city, outpost etc. the item can be found. A search function could be used for a particular item or item type. Perhaps some subsets could be built in like only clan/omni cities included or pick a particular city and a list of all the items available in the that place would be produced.

Criticism appreciated, Flames ignored (Well most of the time).
