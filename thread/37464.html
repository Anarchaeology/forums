Let's Clear Something Up.
Last night I teamed with an old friend for the first time in something like 60 levels. In my team also were members of my guild which is, and always has been a Role Playing guild. That is, Omni is at war with The Clans. Act like it. And for the love of Phillip H Ross quit shooting your own guards. I digress...

Anyway, so the old friend wants to get a clanmember into the team with us at which time my guild leader and president explains to her that teaming with a clanner is tantamount to proclaimning yourself a traitor and that no Good Omni would ever stoop so low as to aid the deserters that call themselves "The Clans". My dear old friend messages me that she will indeed finish this mission with us, however she could not team with us again because as a half (leaving the nationality out) she was offended by something that she felt was racism. Of course I was quite surprised at this and felt that she was just being oversensitive, but all things being equal has always been a very pleasant person and an excellent teammate who is always welcome to team with me. However, I must question her reasoning here.

Perhaps my skin is too thick or my brain too saturated in malted hops and bhong resin but wouldn't racism be if all clanners were purple and all Omnis were green? THEN perhaps it would be racism? Or maybe If we all just went around and hated Atrox? That would be racist I think. I don't know since I am neither discriminated against nor racist and judge people on their individual merits. This is not a war to eradicate SkincolorX, brought to you by your friends the Tyrants of SkincolorY. This is a war of Loyal Hardworking Line Toe-ers against the Traitor Terrorist Dogs. This war is occasionally observed by the Commitment Impaired Freedom Loving Individualists.

I know FC never saw this question coming. Honestly I never would have thought that anyone would interpret the conflict in this game as racist since the only thing racially offensive is an atrox in bikinis (JOKE!) that is an offense to humanity in general (Joke again!).

Since I am not really qualified to have an opinion on the "-isms" how do you feel? Was my friend being oversensitive? Or was she voicing a valid concern that to RP that there is a war goin on is to be prejudiced? (Prejudiced against something called "The Clan for Phillips sakes?)


And yes for those of you that wonder I KNOW that Omni is technicly the Evil Entity, why would I settle for the lesser evil?
