Breathe deeply, count to ten...

It boils down not to SWG in itself, but to SWG and the players of the AO community.

SWG is going to put a lot of stress on all aspects of the AO community. I don't think it's overreacting for those of us who care about, and are invested in, the AO community to feel threatened by SWG. Many bad things will probably happen to AO's community, at least in the short term, upon SWG's release. Friends will disappear, some guilds will migrate, other guilds will split, and other guilds will just collapse or implode.

It won't happen in one big apocalypse, of course, and new AO players will come - and old AO players will come back - but let's be blunt: SWG is a direct threat to our way of life on Rubi-Ka.

Whether or not SWG will hold on to the people who leave is an entirely different story, and will probably be determined by the nature of the SWG community. If it actually turns out to be Star Wars: Geeks (dewd! I wanna be the Jedi!), or Star Wars: Griefers (transfer players from evercamp), I think a lot of the roleplay/social community from AO will return.

If SWG has a generally positive community environment, then I doubt there will be much reason for the socializer to return to AO, unless and until Funcom implements features that can compete toe-to-toe on the grounds of non-combat advancement and engine support for social interactions. On the other hand, the most serious roleplayers may well return anyway, since the Star Wars universe is, well, rather played out while AO is newer, and richer, in material.

The last note I'll add is that there is a 'wild card' AO has yet to play: Shadowlands. The booster, like its medical namesake, will give AO a needed shot in the arm. But in the end it is Shadowlands that will determine if AO will be competative with SWG.
