Guide to spawning Otacustes AND pulling Ian Warr and gang at Ace Camp...
[Just added getting a letter/Ota spawning tips and cleaned up the pulling guide.]

Like the rest, I'm a self-proclaimed n00b at mercs, with about 70 raids under my belt.

In the past few weeks I've started pulling for the group in addition to giving letters and spawning Otacusts.

STEP ONE: Getting a letter

In order to get the mercs to appear, you need to spawn Otacustes and then give her a sealed letter.

Everyone who comes to the camp should get a letter before they head out -- we've had long pauses where no one had a letter to give and someone had to head out and get one.

Anyhow, it's EASY and PAINLESS to get a letter. You can read all about it at AO Stratics -- they can tell you much better than I:

http://ao.stratics.com/content/guide...ian_warr.shtml

STEP TWO: Spawning Otacustes (a.k.a. Ota)

Now that you've got a letter, join warrnet (clan) or mercbot? (omni) and head to the ace/primus camp in SW East Foul Plains.

I've been involved in untold hours of spawning Otacustes. Here's the method we've used, and been pretty successful at getting her to spawn.

First, open this graphic and follow along...

http://www.mclainhome.com/screens/spawningotacustes.jpg

Put your group right outside the entrance. (See the group in the bottom center of the image? Good.)

Have your puller get all of the mobs in the center and right side of the camp during the 14 or so minutes between PH spawns. These mobs are shown with red boxes.

You'll need to be killing (that is, pull the remaining mobs from their spots) and have them near, inside, or just outside the entrance within a few seconds of the PH (place holder) spawn time.

In this way, no mobs are within range of Ota and she can feel safe to come out. The place holder and/or Otacustes herself can be found in the spot with the red star.

NOTE: There's an additional Primus found above/behind where Ota/PH's spawn. It's shown as a yellow box in the graphic. I've found that you DO NOT need to bother with this Primus (that is, pull or kill it) to get Ota to spawn nearby using this method.

This is our theory. God knows it's prolly way off the mark in terms of the REAL/QUICKER way to spawn Ota, but it's all we've got and it works.

STEP THREE: Pulling the Mercs

Before you start a Merc raid, look on your map in Pleasant Meadows (just West of East Foul Plains) for a place to save. It's called the Treepine Hut on my map, and it's roughly NW of the ace/primus camp. It's also a large white dot just N of the word Meadows on Dovve's. Head there and SAVE before you come to the raid. That way, you can get back quicker when you die.

Next, you need good nano range increaser installed in your ncu or given through nano range buffs to pull the mercs from a fair distance.

Head up to the entrance of the camp with a group of high level toons (10ish with a doc or 2) to clear out the trash (that is, the mobs that clutter your path to the rear arena where the mercs spawn down inside.) OR, you may just want to mezz all the trash mobs in the very center of the camp leading up to the arena.

Have a second or third toon volunteer to be backup puller in case you die. And you will die, many times, till you make this work.

1. Standing at the entrance, buff up your ac's/protection nanos to the hilt.

2. Remove damage reflect nanos. If you attempt to pull a merc with a reflect on, you'll get more than one -- and that's instadeath. [This has been deemed an urban legend -- and I've now pulled mercs with and without reflects. I've died/lived about the same number of times, so I am fairly sure that reflects play no role after all.]

3. Get GSF from a fixer so you can run down to the group faster and live.

4. Have a doctor and approx. 4-5 other high-lvl peeps waiting at the entrance of the camp who can help you keep the pulled merc attacking clanners and moving the mob down to the group. (Read: If you die near the arena or running down, these toons will pick up the agg by shooting at the merc and then complete moving the mob down to the group.)

5. You pull Nelly, Patricia, Ris, Ian and Peter by using an aggro nano or small debuff nano -- NOT with a weapon shot. I've had good luck with BioMet Inexperience and Diminish Strength.

Go ahead and try a shot -- if there's more than one merc in the arena, they'll all insta aggro you and you'll be hugging reclaim. Sooo, there you go.

6. Move your aggro bar all the way to the left.

7. Once all the trash is clear, run straight to the back of the camp and stand near the arena marked as Mercs on the graphic. (Note the red box just in front of the arena. That's a good spot to stand. Or some stand to the left of the ramp on the awning sticking out of the building there.)

8. Go to third person view and move the view until you can see the mercs inside the arena. Click on the merc you want to pull.

Note: You don't want to walk around behind the arena at any time because there are nasty mobs behind the arena that will snag you and kill you. (Read: Notum Profundus BAD.)

9. Once you're ready, turn to face the entrance, move to the very edge of your nano range -- then cast your nano through the arena wall. You may have to click the nano and move several times until you're in range and can cast it.

Hopefully, you'll only get the one you want to attack you.

[Here's a tip from another experienced puller from below:]
...dont wait for the red you have been attacked by so and so msg, as if you do, likelly you will be aggrod by all of them... Hit debuff and at the same time start running towards the camp exit, you will pull your target only.

IF you get more than one, stay where you are and DIE. DO NOT run out of the camp with more than one/or the wrong merc. You will cause many toons to die and may get omni mad/resulting in the loss of clan towers, etc.

Trust me, these mercs are key to both sides, and you don't wanna mess things up. Death is OK -- you can come back heh.

10. As soon as you cast your nano, start running to the entrance of the camp, and in bot chat say "Ris inc" or something similar. You should get your target to aggro you and move to you.

At this point when you're inside the entrance tunnel, you may want to get off a shot or two to keep him/her on you.

11. Run down to the bridge, stop once or twice to be sure he/she is still behind you.

12. Run to the tank in the group down by the trees and stop your attack. At this point, the tank should attack and will take the aggro from you.

13. Max your range and wait for the word to attack.

Your placement near (not on top of) the arena is critical and can only be learned by practice. Also, what works today won't tomorrow -- best thing is to keep trying and you'll get more than your share of good pulls over time.

Please feel free to add your .02 and expand. Note: Made changes based on some of the replies -- thanks for the info!

About 75% of the time I can pull the Mercenary all the way down and across the bridge by myself without dying. There are instances when a Mercenary will triple-hit you (lag probably), or at times warp you (sometimes freezing you, love that bug) or stun/runspeed debuff you.
