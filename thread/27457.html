Mission Wednesdays on GridStream
Mission Wednesdays on GridStream Productions!

What Location?: Doesn't matter. You can be anywhere on Rubi-Ka 1 or 2 or off-world to tune in!

What Time?: 8pm EST (1am GMT) until 11pm EST (4am GMT) every Wednesday night!

What is it?: Mission Wednesdays with Tarryk and Metalynx on GridStream Productions is a streaming talk-and-music show that is all live! Metalynx and I will address concerns, questions, topics, and current events on Rubi-Ka during the 3-hour show. I (DJ Tarryk) will be on Rubi-Ka 1 accepting tells from anyone wishing to address a topic or request a song over the course of the show. It's called Mission Wednesdays because it is a slightly off-beat style for GridStream Production because it is not an actual party. You are encouraged to take a mission, go socialize, or just hang out in your favorite spot on Rubi-Ka while listening in.

We'll be keeping you up to date on current events on RK1, bantering about our own overly-opinionated and often humorous outlook on life in the world of Rubi-Ka, and playing great mixes of metal, techno, grindcore and industrial music between talk segments to keep that thirst for hardcore quenched!

How do I Tune In?: Very simple, really. Just point your web browser to WWW.GRIDSTREAM.COM (or just click right here, hehe) and click the big "Play" button on the page! The live365 server will use Winamp or Realplayer if you desire either one, but it can also stream directly with it's own software, so you'll be listening in no time at all!

There are different live broadcasts with different GridStream DJ's every night of the week, usually around 8:30-11pm EST (1:30-4am GMT), so tune in on any evening!

You can email me at tarryk@gridstream.com, or go to www.gridstream.com and email any of the other regular DJ's if you want to give feedback, request information, or schedule an event with any one of us!

Thanks for supporting GridStream Productions, Rubi-Ka's #1 source of live entertainment!
