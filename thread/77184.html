A guide to Role Playing - translations
Over the last month, I have tried to go 100% RP. The problem I have found is that AO is such a good place to talk about things that dont fit RP. I have been working on a few handy phrases, but keep me in character. I would like to know if other people have other phrases that keep you in character but reveal ooc information.

For example:

Where do you live? - I live in Rome, but my ancesters were from canada

Do you have an alt? - Sometimes I have vivid dreams about being a ...

Do where do you study/work? - I work in the bronto burger in Tir, but on the weekends I strap on the armour do do some missions, It helps pay the rent.

Are you on the chat client? - well, I had to hack into the network because my normal com badge is getting repaired.

Who are your alts? - my sister is a MP...

Where are you now (to if you are in the chat client) - I am on a special assignment in the unterraformed areas, as such I have hacked into text chat to stay in touch.

Can you do a transfer? - I would be glad to hold a present to your brother or sister.

whats your ip in mech eng? - Sorry, didnt really study that in school, in my last test I only got a 52.

Do you RP? - You mean something about our bodies being connected to energy and life sucking machines in a huge global network. The matrix is just a bad movie man, this is the reality.


Please add more...
