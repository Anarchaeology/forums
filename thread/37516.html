Title's vs the real intent of this forum <= please read
People, seriously, this is getting out of hand.

Farming is a personal choice made by the player!

Lets look at the arguments from both sides.

1. Who gives a flying rats ass, as another member said "'tis only to impress lvl 10 newbies...." bla bla bla. If they want to farm, then let them go at it! All serious Anarchy Players know the who's who of PvP, who get their titles and how they got their titles. Stop whining for our sakes...please!!!!

2. People at level 200 have not much left to do, this is true. We have all worked really hard to obtain our titles and it makes us angry to see someone out there farming for titles. It also discourages us when someone stands there and allows there titles to be lost to another person who has a supreme advantage over the victim(angel....whatever) So this must end!!!

Ok, I am a newbie at this game, but i have been playing video games since Pong. And one thing that I know is this...

You cant always expect things to be fair. There is NO perfect game out there(except starcraft, and, oh, pong). So everyone is gonna have problems with how the game is set up. How players take advantage of gliches in the game. How players cheat, how players work together to make your life a living hell. And how the game offers players the ability to obtain things without much work that others bust thier ass for.

We all know this i am sure.

So can we use this forum for PvP tactics, strategy, conflict(as in between characters, or guild/clan members), help to the needy, and general discussion not related to the actions of another character/characters on how they obtain their title.

We all live in Rubi Ka! We know what goes on, well most of us anyway.

I come here for help about strategy and no one has time to answer my question because they are to active in the Farming thing or whatever.

Sorry if i piss ed anyone off.

But I think there is a general chat forum on the other side of this website you can discuss your non actuall pvp strategic related whimpers with yourselfs.

Otherwise, cool people like Garv, Cloudeh, and so on, please stay and continue with your wonderful support of us newbies!
