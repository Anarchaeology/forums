New Items spell death for Namomages
I just finished perusing the new items database for 14.4, and there is an easy conclusion -- Nanomages as a breed are now completely undermined... (if they werent already lol)

For those of you who havent gotten a chance to look yet, there is a wide variety of new armors that add to both max nano and nano skills. Some of them might be hard to obtain, others are clearly trade skill. Some nice exciting stuff. But if you think about it a little...

Because health is more important than pool, playing a nanomage was already rather dicey. There is almost no diminishing returns to adding health -- more is always better. Adding more nano, however, while nice early, becomes a lot less important when your total is already high. The reason is simple -- nano costs dont scale all that much, while dmg does. It is always better to be able to take more dmg, preiod. However, what little advantage NM's had with there nano totals is now eroded by the new equipment.

Aside from pool, NM's had only 2 small advantages: higher int, and thus minorly better nanoskills, and two very nice pieces of equipment :
The shades of lucabration, and the very expensive vest of Dr. Jones. This allowed nanomages to cast their top nanos somewhat earlier than the other breeds -- and because of title caps, this can be a very strong advantage (although it almost completely erodes when you hit lvl 200, even pre-14.4), depending on class and other details.

But now, all breeds have access to multiple items that improve nano skills. These items do not have that high an opportunity cost either -- Putting on the new NT armor is not the same as equipping a pillow, or a pepper pistol. It has solid, if not spectacular acs. Thus, whatever small advantage NMs has vanished almost entirely.

Conclusion: No one should ever be a nanomage. That was pretty clear before, and completely certain now.
