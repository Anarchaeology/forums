What I remember
is the following.

- Player level range it was 'designed' for: 30-70, though 30 might be a bit low.
- Team oriented -> bring a full team if you and your companions are lvl 50 or below.
- Monster level: Well I don't have exact numbers, but I was there at around level 50 and the guards at the entrance where green, but they have a 'lot' of HP. Deeper in the facility the MOBs/NPCs became yellow.
- Nearest clan city is Athens (ignoring Bliss, because there is no external access), nearest neutral city is Last Ditch.
- The main route is - tataa - Longest Road (who would have guessed). Just enter the zone (clan outpost nearby!) and follow the road. The factory is left hand side from the road in small valley system.
- If you are not Omni or don't have a Yalmaha you might have a hard time to get in there right now. The entrance is guarded by a pack of slayer droids. If you are able to handle these, then the dungeon might not be a challenge for you.
- The director has long respawn time (4 hours, if I remember correctly - I am an occasional player, so four hours is long for me) and it could happen that he is camped by some higher level players.

Personal impression: I enjoyed our trip to the factory very much, although our full team was devasted - bad pull. A team with a good leader or some experience could be advantageous.

So long,

Additional note on TLR dungeon
Note for pet users: Pets have an easy time of it getting 'stuck' in the stairways (even the wide ones) and sloping corridors, so take care not to leave pets behind. Keep an eye on that map screen. It can take several tries and commands of /pet follow to 'unstick' a pet.

Overall, I'll heartily recommend The Longest Road dungeon, even if the road leading to it is... well, long.

Beautifully made, worth going there just to take in the scenery alone.

Would love to claim credit, but the TLR dungeon was made by another world designer, Morten Byom (creator of TLR, Borealis, Rome, Omni HQ and a host of other great places).
