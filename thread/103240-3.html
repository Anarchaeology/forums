You do not question your employer? You scare me, you are the worst kind of corporate fundamentalist.

What if your employer orders you to kill innocent people? Will you then not allow you personal feelings intrude?

I see everything that's being said in this thread and one thing is clear. In 25 years, when the lease expires, Omni will leave.

What will be left then are all nanomages and whatever people willing to stay behind.

So it's up to us that intends to stay to make this planet as hospitable as possible. The first step in this is to secure Rubi-Ka's supply of notum, so that all the nanomages don't die. I would hate for that to happen and I would feel very bad about myself if I didn't do anything about it. Sadly brute force seems the only way to make people listen to you these days, as we in DW has been screaming our lungs out for some time.

And by removing the mining operations the notum supply is secured along with a stability in the wildlife where once the mining facilities stood. If the wildlife becomes stable the whole ecology will benefit and grow.

The sad part is that the clans seem the bigger foe in this, and perhaps the biggest of them all is ICC. When Omni-Tek leaves, thousands of corporations will land on Rubi-Ka to seize the old mining spots, and the clanners with their already rampant mining will race them to said mining spots.

At least Omni-Tek has some sort of organisation behind the mining. Everyone else just mines like crazy. OT at least has an interest in seeing that it don't run out of notum, while everyone else just mines for the money, and can't even see any consequenses. I assume of course, but to become the most successful corporation every you have to have some sort of strategists working for you, and they should clearly see that running out of the thing that is making you money is a bad thing.
