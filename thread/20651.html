Timeline (1949 to 28702)
(OOC)

This is for those who are interested in the history of Earth from the year 1949 to 28702. I have posted the timeline on my guild's website.

Please note:

There are material on the timeline that IS against the principles of role playing, that is, some events which characters on Rubi-Ka couldnt possibly know of, is reported, how you treat it ingame and in discussions is up to you, if you are a staunch role player and believes that knowing anything beyond what you character ingame could possibly know is downright cheating (or any other way you wish to put it), then be warned.

There are SPOILERS, but none of which is about the future of Rubi-Ka, only the history of Earth leading up to the landing on Rubi-Ka on the year 28702, so if you want to keep guessing about stuff such as what Omni Tek is, again be warned.

This timeline is a draft, so its rough around the edges, if you have any suggestions please post replies here or on the forum's thread.

The timeline is based on the story Prophet Without Honour, which can be found online here:

http://www.anarchy-online.com/content/story/past/novel/

My intention when I published the timeline is to give players a deeper insight into the events that led to the "discovery" of Rubi-Ka, perhaps then people would enjoy the game from a different perspective, beyond the direct conflict between Omni and Clan.

So without further chit chat here is the location of the timeline, it can be found under Resources, Forums, Stories:

http://www.geocities.com/derrasalli/GNN/index.html

I also plan to write a report on the various factions involved in the conflict on Earth during its tumultous history in the future.

So, I hope you enjoy it!
