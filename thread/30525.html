Best 2h0 pvp'n i've ever seen
Total chaos. i had a blast. I couldnt even guess how many omni or clan people were there. Conservatively i'd say 15 clan and a significantly larger number of omni.

The lvl distribution was hilarious though. I've never seen so many greys in the zone - and these guys werent just screwing up targeting either, i was shooting some agent for 300 point crits and still couldnt kill the bugger. I assumed he was fp trader and using premium delayed to make up for his purposely small number of hp. Later i tab him in omni ent and he's lvl 45 had a doc healing him....

Now thats the sort of reckless enthusiasm and enjoyment of pvp that is so sorely missing from our normal, incestuous, bitter fights. People were dying left and right and being continually replaced - it didnt stop.

Then the clanners managed to kill/scare off most of the omni. Using the most deadly pvp tactic available, the omni reinforcements showed up en masse with their mysterious UberLag dmg which pretty much froze up everything in the zone.

So first, what was going on in 2h0 before i showed up, cause there were a whole lotta people there. Anyone else have a great time in the madness?

Second, is there any way 2h0 could be managed so that it could better adapt to the variance of people that can be in the zone at any one time (I know nothing bout what's required to run a game like this). Often times 2 on 1 turns into 4 on 2... 7 on 4... 10 on 6... 18 on 15.... and then i die in lag trying to get back to the bunker after pulling my stuff outta reclaim. With all the other problems pvp has now at least if we could interact in the same timeframe it'd be a start.
