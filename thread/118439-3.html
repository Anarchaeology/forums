Don't add unsheathing to attack time
It would be very nice to have both melee and ranged weapons put away when they're not in use, but it would be a big mistake to effectively increase the attack time on a weapon just because it has to be unsheathed or unholstered first. Likewise, yet more manual actions to be performed would be a very bad idea.

Let this be implemented in an automatic, behind the scenes manner. If the weapon has not been used for (say) 30 seconds then sheath or holster it automatically. And if an attack is started, then unsheath or unholster it automatically, without increasing the attack time.

In other words, a purely graphic change in this area would be very nice indeed, but if it were to impact on gameplay then the "improvement" would actually turn into a liability.
