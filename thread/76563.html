Low level tower PvP with high level healers...simple solution
This is a pretty major problem and excludes "honest" low level guilds from competing with "alt" guilds. No average level 60 player stands a chance against a level 60 player backed by a level 200 doc, whether they are attacking or defending.

The simple solution is to put in a PvP level range check for friendly nanos just like for hostile nanos, but only when in a PvP zone. If that level 200 doc tries to heal someone in a PvP zone or in a PvP fight, they must fall within their targets PvP range, or they're SOL.

Very easy and it will let everyone enjoy the Notum Wars equally, regardless of level.

I think he's taking the opinion of a guild consisiting of low lvl characters, say lvl 50 characters. The limit of their resources is low, yet a guild with many 200 characters could easily make lvl 50 twinks and take out the other guilds tower. As one guild would not be able to generate the lvl 200 healers but the other would, the larger higher lvl guild would win.

With the way it is now, the wars will always favor the guilds that have the most people with the highest level healers. This tends to favor larger guilds with higher leveled members, and leaves the lower level guilds with no land to control.

One could argue that this is fine and the high lvl people need/deserve/pay for/blah blah the enjoyment of towers in PvP, but from the looks of it NW was not designed explicitly for high lvl people, otherwise what is the purpose of ql 50 and 10 towers? To give high lvlers twinks something to blow up?

Smaller guilds should be able to get just as much enjoyment out of making towers as collossally huge guilds. The same is true with high lvl guilds and low lvl guilds.

Also there was a distinction in the suggestion between PvP and Non-PvP zones. It seems like the intent is to prevent a 200 character from giving an significant advantage to a lvl 50 in a PvP zone, via heals etc. However, he made no issue with doing so in a non-PvP environment. In otherwords, a 200 doc giving a lvl 50 HP buffs outside the PvP, but be unable to heal them inside said area.

To this last bit I would make the following changed. Have it only affect those higher then the limit helping those lower then it. IE Allow a character who falls below the PvP limit to aid those who are above it. This would help those that are two low to actually fight in the conflict still feel useful by helping in it...even if in a minor way.
