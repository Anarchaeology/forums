OOC: A Goodbye and a Thanks
Well folks, real life has been harsh and (insert flames, please) have been playing EQ (yeah, i know, its a lame game but its all new and exciting to me)

Ive been around in one form or another since beta, and ever since my friend Lindsay introduced me to the game ive never looked back.

I came back for a little while and had a great time, and who knows, if Shadow lands is good I just may come back

I love the game beyond words, its just not exciting for me anymore- ive done it all it seems.

If I could be that newbie soldier again, losing all my exp at level 6 in the pitch black newbie yard (little nostalgia there =p) only to grow up and run my ass off from Pumpkin heads and mobs with like DaftNanoHu (thats way back ;])
Id sure as hell do it again, its been a trip.

AO is a great game, and if I had the chance id start from day one all over again in a heart beat. Enjoy yourselves and remember that a little RP can add alot of depth and enjoyment to the game.

I was pretty well known in the past, but as I stopped playing people forgot about me, but for those that still remember the prez and technon dont forget to look for me in other games (most likely SWG, look for Prez or Presidential =p)

The thing that brought me back all those times is, without question, the awesome community this game has. Some of you happen to be the coolest, funniest people I have ever met.

I owe a special thanks to the role-playing community for keeping me in the game for over 4 months, ive sure got some awesome memories from that.

Well, this is goodbye for now everyone, and if you ever feel the need to contact me hit me up at stlsoldier2003@yahoo.com

peace,
Phil D.

Edit: Streamliner, Zephyrus, Technon, Prezidential, Chubblies (ahh the secret it out =P), Constable, Lostlegend, Californium and definently a few others I forgot to mention would be my list of freaks =p
