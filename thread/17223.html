The ultimate solution to PVP
I don't know if anyone suggested the following idea before but it popped in my head a few days back and I thought to share it with you guys:

A lot of players have dropped the game since the dreadful 12.8. While the patch itself solved many problems, it also nerfed the one big thing that 90% of the hardcore AO players like - PVP.

Since 12.8, PVP has become a cumbersome and ridiculous hide'n'seek game, where players keep zoning back and forth 30 times, hoping that in the 31st time they would finally kill someone. I'm not putting myself out of the general, I do that too, along with 100% of the PVPers out there.

Traders cast Ransack, then keep zoning and use AS/fling.
Soldiers cast MK, then in the last second before they die, they zone back.
Agents root and run for the 75% zones.
NT cast MK, root the offender and run for cover too.

What I'm saying is that there is ALWAYS a way to run for cover in 25% zones due to the low volume and effectiveness of firepower 12.8 gave us.

My idea is simple - why not make a Rubi-Ka 3, where the rules of PVP would be the same rules as the pre 12.8 rules with a minimal grace period of lets say 5 seconds.

I know many, many people that would transfer to this RK3 leaving behind the lame way PVP is handled now. I'm aware to the fact that the current PVP rules were forced because of the high volume of whiners that sent msgs to ARKs in the kind of "This and that just ASed me for 6003 dmg and killed me in one shot" but since those lamers will stay on RK1 we would have no problem.

PVPers will be real PVPers again, not hide'n'seek players. Agents will play with rifles like they meant to play in the first place, not with shotguns. Soldiers won't have the possibility to shoot and run for cover.

That was the real game, with Adrenalin and the whole deal.
