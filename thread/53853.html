E-Corp is accepting Limited Applications
Greetings Omni Employee

We at E-Corp are pleased to announce that since our launch, business has been booming, and we need some more hands to get things done proffessionally. Currently we have 10 positions available.

What is E-Corp you might ask? well, much of our work is classified in nature, but here is our mission statement:

"E-Corp is a independent affiliate of Omni-Tek Incorporated that specializes in Data Gathering and Warehousing in support of, but not limited to such Omni departments as Omni-Pol, Omni-Reform and Omni-InternOps. E-Corp will entertain contracts from all Omni organizations to gather information not currently in its databank.

E-Corp provides these services beneath "true market value" to all recognized members of the Corporation. To see if you qualify please submit a project request form (in triplicate) at http://members.shaw.ca/nomatter/ec . All contracts will be negotiated with a member of the board of directors and allocated to the corporation staff in priority sequence.

E-Corp is committed to using all of its available resources in intelligence gathering and diplomatic services against the enemies of Omni-Tek - both within and without. "

Still not sure? Well we offer a pay package and benefits right from applicant status, as well as bonuses when you advance to Member, and executive. Our work is challenging, but rewarding. Our training program has fantastic reviews and our employee turn around rate is low.

OOC: E-Corp is a Role play Guild. We are in character all the time unless (( )) or OOC are used to show that you are not. This includes Guild chat as well. That means it is not to be used to seek team members for missions, chat about weapons and amour, discuss the latest South park episode, etcunless it is part of your storyline of course. Other out of character chatter can been done in private /tells, or you can always make a new chat group to share with your friends. If a role play situation presents itself, and a call for players is sent out all available are expected to attend. Should you be occupied for whatever reason, it is expected that you acknowledge this call and give a ETA to your availability.

Please visit our site for the complete rules and regulations

http://members.shaw.ca/nomatter/ec/

IC: We look forward to hearing from you. Only those applicants concidered will be contacted ((which doesn't mean we don't want to RP with you, just that we had far to many apps)). If you have any questions or concerns please contact Jesska Rhees, Director. The deadline for applications is Sept 29.

Submit your application ((and backstory)) at the link below

http://aoecorp.topcities.com/app.html

Thank you have a nice day
