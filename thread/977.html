Opening - Presidential Political Advisor
There is an opening as Presidential Political Advisor (PPA) in the Knights of Rubi-Ka.

Rank: General

The PPA answers directly to the President.

Situation:
There is a difficult political situation on Rubi-Ka at the moment. This concerns the members of Knights of Rubi-Ka, in the daily life. This calls for tough decisions for the President.

There are often meetings between the Clan Leaders in the Clan Leaders Council (CLC), and there are also meetings with representatives from the Council of Truth, as shown in the Referendum.

The PPA must be genuine interested in politics and must be able to attend the meetings, witch the President orders. The PPA has to give a summary of the meetings to the President after each meeting where the President is not present. The PPA has to speak out and make the Order heard at the meetings after guiding lines given by the President. The PPA has to organize search for information which is important, this to make the right decisions. Therefore is cooperation with other advisors essential, especially Agents.

Other:
The meetings usually starts on Saturdays:
10 PM EST
9 PM CST
8 PM MST
7 PM PST
3 AM GMT
Have this in mind.

Other questions can be forwarded directly to the President at: president@knights.no
