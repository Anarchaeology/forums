Could MA/Enforcers get a way to deal with damage shields?
Last night in what was gonna be my last mission of the night, I enter the area and proceed to take out the two even level mobs in the first room, yes there were only two... more on that in a sec.

I took both of these guys out with a bit of a struggle, after all they were both the same level as me, and I had to dimach one, and got lucky with a few brawl crits, so I was able to keep myself healed up enough to stay ahead of the damage I was taking which was considerable (1900ac at lvl 54 and I still get pounded like I was wearing paper). I beat both of them and healed up and restored my nano pool, and got ready to head into take on the rest of the mission.

I enter the main room and from the other side of the very large room, 2 mobs come running out of one of the side rooms, both wearing damage shields. Oh crap, but at least they are low green, so if I can take out two even con agent mobs, I should be able to take out 2 low green adventurers, or so I thought. The two of them where hitting me fast and hard, their damage shields were tearing me up, so I zoned out to try and heal up and then head back in... When I do I get attacked by some mob outside who chased me back into the mission (fortunately I was back at full health and nano when I was attacked). I head back in and both adv mobs are still there waiting for me, and they proceed to kick the living crap out of me... CHANGING AREA...

So here is the dillema, I can barely take out mobs who have damage shields, since I hit them for minimum damage, and their damage shield adds so much extra damage that I cant heal myself up fast enough to stay on top of the damage. I posted this before, but the numbers are very signifigant:

Mob A with DS: damage taken = 3000+
Mob B without DS: damage taken = 4-500

Keep in mind I only have about 1250hp, you do the math.

All things are equal on both mobs, except the damage shield. Im not anti damage shield, nor do I want to see them nerfed, but I think the close combat specialists should have some way of dealing with damage shields. We need to be able to dispell them or something. I try doing missions with no human npc's but I end up with all grey bots on 50%, or scooplets who dont give any xp.

Im not into the run around and kill stuff method of getting xp, I dont care for outdoor hunting, EQ is to blame for that, but I really like the mission system, where I can go in solo, and clear out the entire place and accomplish the task. Its one of the best things in AO imo. Please please fix the problems in them, and please give us some way to deal with damage shields. I dont like just sitting there watching myself die, I like to have at least a fighting chance.

Or it could just be me... I can addapt, but its just that these mobs with DSs are sooo much tougher than any other mob (cept debuffing trader mobs but thats another whine hehe).
