1. If someone asks for cash in advance, I will not use them from now on.

2. If someone expects a certain amount of cash after the job is done, then I will give then 1% of the cost of the nano and the cost of 2 nano kits.

3. If someone casts the buff on me whilst I'm stood around, and doesn't expect any payment, then I will tip them. Last person that did that I gave 25k and a QL76 Battle Suit to.

4. From now on, I'm refusing to give doctors nano kits or cash for nano kits for doing their job. I have roughly the same expenditure in terms of nano-kits for RF shields, and I'm the one stopping the mob from killing them.

5. Someone ever tried to "hold me for ransom" over clusters or implants then I'd firstly petition them, then I'd tell every opposing side guild I know to keep their name flagged for me, and I'd give them 1 million credits reward for the kill.

6. People think that all because I want to get 500k for a certain item doesn't mean I am will to sell for less. Make an offer... barter... do something to make selling fun.

7. If someone is looking lost, or I find out that they are a completely new player, with no in-game friends, then I will stop, chat, help them, buy them items, give them cash, and try to make their introduction to the game better.

8. If you come to me begging for cash, then I will give you nothing. If you come to me saying that you are new to the game, and that you need help, then I will help. I've spent 8 hours one night, just teaching someone all about the game, items, grouping, mobs, weapons, you name it. I even explain what all the different skill actually affect. Most fulfilling thing I've ever done in AO.

9. If a higher level player (higher than L77 atm) attempts to pull a fast one on me or others on the channels, then I expose that fact. I've given away over 20 pairs of keelars just to show people that they ripping others off, 15 of which I gave away in what can only be described as a "trade-war".

10. Be reasonable, and be honest. There are people on the channels that are reasonable and honest also, but some are not. It's easy to flush out a pretend counterbid, by asking publically who is bidding against me, and sending them a tell asking what they bid. I've had countless occasions whereby the item I've bid for has been flushed out after the very first bid.

You don't want to know how many FA's, Flowers, OPE, Tank, Nano's I've given away, but every last person that I gave stuff to, stays in touch and has returned the favour.

Hope this "Top Ten" helps.

Firstly, for some unknown reason, people think that just because you are L77 they think you are loaded with cash. Err... no. If you're playing to consolidate your char, then you'll find that Soldiers rarely have cash. Those that do, are getting their kit from their guild or from higher-level friends.

More often that not, the people that ask for money up front are asking insane amounts of cash. I've had doctors ask for 100k or more in advance for an IC, and agents with FG are just as bad.

The way I see it is that I don't want to fuel an economy that is based on charging everyone for everything. I'd like to see the day when people will do "good deeds", and I reward those people for doing that. They are not capitalists, they are valued members of the community the way I see it.

I do reward doctors from time to time, but I will not reward those that "expect" it. That is what I really should have said. The other thing I do at the moment, is keep a bag of monster parts in a bag. I don't use them, but they're most from QL60+ so docs tend to like them, and they don't cost me anything.

The point that I want to reach, and I'm part way there, is that I don't want to have to pay for buffs. OK, you may say that casting that buff is expensive, but all because I won't pay for the buff, doesn't mean that they won't get something back. Destria gave me alot of help early and still does to this day, and any opportunity I can help Destria in getting something useful to her, I will drop what I'm doing. The other night I had a Enfraams Superior nano, and was asked by an NT about buying it. I said I was looking for about 500k for it. He said that he'd only got 30k. After a nice chat with the pleasant guy, I ended up giving him the nano and 30k. Last night I also gave away a QL115 Senpai Cloak to a friend that was higher level than me that was using a QL53 one, as he'd helped me in the past. Now that's ALOT of cash tied up there alone.

The crux of what I'm really trying to say is that I don't deal with people that expect, or are just there for "business". Those people would just as sooner take the same amount of cash from a L7 player, as they would a L77 player, and that makes me livid, knowing that younger players are being dragged kicking and screaming into a "commercial" world, rather than a fun world.

To answer Cloudeh, if they asked in advance, then I wouldn't use them. If they cast it on me without any fuss at Ents BB, then I'd give them alot.
