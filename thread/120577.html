Wednesday Night Fights!
When you think of fighting, I want you to think of Wednesday Night! Why Wednesday Night? Why not?


(edited 4/30/03 to update the Honorable/Dishonorable Lists)

My goal is to put aside some time when those who want to PvP can find challenging opponents. Wednesday Night is as good a time as any. It is in a bit of a lull in the week, and I don't want to interfere with Weekend Events. This may take several weeks to get off the ground, but I hope interested parties will help get this idea rolling.

Here are the rules to participate in Wednesday Night Fights!

1) Be willing to submit to Typothetae as the final arbiter on issues relating to everything posted in this thread.

2) Post a challenge below with the following qualifications:
a) All Challenges must be for a Wednesday with a starting time between the hours of 7PM and 11PM Eastern Daylight Time. Include the Date you want to fight, the time does not need to be stated,
b) All challenges must be Team-Based (mimimum of 3 players). You must accurately post the level ranges of those participating. For example: Team of 6, level 97-124. At the time of the event, you must accurately disclose the level AND NAMES of all team member you are including. Professions need not be disclosed. Its okay if your initial post is slightly inaccurate.

3) You may include extra rules and provisions in your challenge if you want. However, I will not be the judge of your rules or provisions. I will not put anyone on the Dishonored or Banned list because they broke your rules.

4) The FC/AO Code of Conduct is strictly enforced! No exploits! Any documented exploit will land the accused on the Banned List. If you want someone put on my Banned List for an exploit you must petition the exploit in the usual way. I will not judge exploits. If you have a log that shows an Ark or GM agrees that a person committed an exploit, I will ban them. If you don't report the exploit to FC, and you don't log FC's response, don't report it to me.

5) It is possible that I might improperly place someone on the Dishonored List, due to the fact that I am not perfect. Tough-toenails! I'm not going to take a bunch of time trying to be perfectly fair. Don't play if you are worried.

6) You will land on the Banned List if you
a) post a challenge outside the stated time-frame,
b) post something irrelevant,
c) you lie about your team make-up, or
d) it seems you are involved in more than one case of ganking. Ganking, yuck! Explanation: if it seems that you are involved in a challenge where 'extra' participants appear out of nowhere to help you, I will consider this one case of potential ganking. If it happens again, you will be banned. I may be wrong, but, oh well.

7) You will land on the Dishonored List if
a) you are involved in any case of suspected ganking,
b) you don't show up for your confirmed challenge. Both teams should confirm their acceptance by posting below. (If the fight doesn't occur because the team is slightly changed from the posting, you probably won't get on the Dishonored list. Just be honest and forthright, ok!) You can get off the Dishonored List by completing a challenge Honorably. A player can only visit the Dishonored List once: a second infraction gets them banned, even if they made it back to the Honorable List. Two strikes and you are out!

8) You will be entered into the 'Honorable' list by completing a challenge (show up, and follow the rules). Anyone on a team participating in a Challenge may be put on the Honorable List, if they so desire, and if the competing Team agrees that the Challenge was well met. Team leaders should submit their team lists by posting below. Hey, report on how the action went, too! Anyone who is on the Dishonorable List must complete one Challenge to get off the Dishonorable List, and another Challenge to get on the Honorable List.

9) Its pretty simple, there are very few rules. All normal game mechanics are allowed. Running away? Hey, its possible. Zoning? Why did you make the place of battle so close to a zone boundary? Outside buffing? Uh, this is fine, except you cannot have someone casting nanos on you (like healing) who is not in the team while you actually fight. (If this occurs, ask the person buffing you to stop! If you don't ask the person to stop and cancel the nano, I will consider you Dishonorable.) If you get a buff on the way back from reclaim, what is the harm? I only judge by a few very simple rules. I don't want to be a judge, by the way, I just want to help people find fair PvP challenges.

It is suggested that you post a general challenge, then contact interested team leaders by personal tell to go over the details (exact time and place).

EXAMPLE: Here is my CONTINUING WEEKLY CHALLENGE.
I tried to keep it simple, but it is hard. I expect most challenges won't be this complicated, but they probably won't offer rewards either.

Challenge:
Every Wednesday Night, for the next four weeks, a small Clanner team (3-6 players) will be delivering payroll to various points in the Political Zones. They usually carry 2-6M in credits. If you kill them all within 90 minutes, you can get their loot. I you don't kill them all, you can earn 50% of the Loot by preventing them from completing the delivery within 90 minutes.
Extra Rules: There will be two checkpoints that the payroll team must visit. If a payroll team member dies before reaching the first checkpoint, the payroll team cannot finish the delivery at the final checkpoint until that team members visits the first checkpoint. If all payroll team members die at least once before all team members visit both checkpoints, the attacking team wins the loot. All participants may rejoin the fight after reclaim, if they so wish.

Please post below to be considered for this challenge (Team size, Level Range, and the Date). I will do my best to match challengers with an appropriate payroll team at least once every week. Lower level teams will get less loot than higher level teams.
I will be responsible for paying out the loot, not the Payroll Team. I promise not to stiff any Honorable Challengers.
I probably can't muster a team in every level range, so post your own challenge below, too!

No Exploits Rule is just common sense
Thanks for the reply jhh.

It was probably unecessary to include the 'rule' against exploits. The main reason I included it, was the part about don't report it to me unless you report it to FC. I don't want to be the judge of what is an exploit and what is not an exploit. That is not my role in this. I don't want to listen to anyone trying to convince me that a particular action was an exploit. I can't decide that. I don't want to listen to a bunch of arguments. I just want to help people find fighting partners and help people have fun. Arguing isn't fun.

Sure, text files can be forged. But, if a player has actually committed an exploit, will their char still be ingame? Maybe not, and that would be a dead give-away that the text log is true!

I will be very surprised if this issue actually arises, where I ban anyone for an exploit.
