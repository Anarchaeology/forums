Balance, tradeskils, combat and events
I think I might be getting out on some thin Ice here, but I'l give it a shot.

I'm a long time player of AO and feel a bit left in the dust compared to the promised land when I see what is going on in the upgrades to the game.

AO is turning out to be very much a combat game with x number of races and professions that do the same.

Here is a few thoughts on the way forward:

On tradeskills ake tradeskills viable to use. Doc's can make Stims and Emerg tretment packs. But they cost way to much to make to make them a viable trade skill. I have been playing a doc for 3-4 months and sold 1! kit... Let the kit's be 3 times mor exp. that in shop equivalent kits to make and let the market adapt to that. And on special nano's. Get some sort of information up on WHAT nano's are special an incourrage that they get built. Why is all game information so da.. secret? Get the trade running!

On balance and combat. Distinguish the professions more. And make guidelines on how FC sees the professions in use. Maby even some short stories on teams hunting this and that that NEED a fixer to be possible. Everybody should not be able to solo over 25% missions. (Doc's, crats) Some should only be able to sneak missions. Enf/Soldier should be able to solo 50% missions. ANy higher -> get a team mate. Only engi and Fixer should access the grid (I don't play any of those) Let Doc's heal at least 3 times better than next class.

On events and static dungeons. Limit them to certain classes, min lvl player and max lvl player cap. The static dungeons are cool, but it's sad to get in to one that is totally trashed by a high lvl team out for special loot. There was an event with ICC here the other day, a lot of burocratic nonesens, so allow only crats. Have a attack on an outpost aand allow only Enf and Soldiers. You get the picture.

One last issue separate patches ie. bug fixes and content adition. Make content additions on specific dates.

Thx for your attention \

my Main disagreement is on your idea of soloing. I think EVERYONE should have a chance at soloing 50% if they are equiped with weapons and equipment of a ql = their level. The more you over equip, the higher missions you should beable to do. you SHOULD be able to Solo 100% missions. after all they are in the solo mission terminal, so why are they there if your not supposed to bee able to solo them? or at the very least some prefessions (soldiers, enforcers) should be able to solo 100% mission, with other profs and a sort of sliding scale of how far they can Solo the normal type "fighting" missions. I would also bring in a few new types of missions. Nothing radically different to what is there now, many different ways of gaining tokens. for Agents, have a Mission where you must fulfill the object, but not get detected (sneak in, "borrow" an item, sneak out). Obviously for this you have to unnerf conceal. For fixers, make it so the mission has a time limit once you are inside, so they have to run it (obviously make this something that is actually possible to run in the time given). For Crats, maybe something where they have to calm all the mobs in a mission. These changes arent much, but would certainly help out the classes who arent able to fight their way through a mission.
