Uhm..
Here's my typical day;
I wake up at 16:00. I'm unemployed without friends
16.05 I'm in a robe drinking cold coffee, closing down WinAMP that's been on during the night, next I press the shortcut for AO, get some more coffee, light a cig, log in.
I go through my most interesting characters (i.e. those I feel like playing right then and there) and look through viable things to do, find a team, get XP, do missions, get XP, money, equipment. Get some more coffee, pondering if a mission or camping is my fav today.. back to AO. Suddenly I multitask to start up winamp since it's too quiet, hit § 3 times to get rid of the only bug I encounter while Mtasking (map not working) - suddenly the suns up! Ieee! The horror, and I quickly hop into bed to avoid the light

I don't eat much these days, when I do eat, it's something those Solitus'es shove under my nose when they think I'm looking too pale/scrawny or that I realize that I can't use the mouse as I'm shaking too much, mostly it's pizza driven home by something that looks like a cross between a nano/opifex in clothes an atrox could use.
