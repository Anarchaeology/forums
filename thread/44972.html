1. You will need a graphic artist to do new artwork.
2. You will need a sound engier to record new sounds.
3. You will need someone to code this stuff into the old engine.
4. You will need someone to do netcoding.

A patch in simple a change of code, a change of loot tables, a change of skills, all code, maybe some netcode, and many once in a rare while new items/buildings etc...

A booster pack is just a bit over the hill to be provide for free.

AO is a wonderful game, and is only getting better look at what a year and half has brought us!

When shadow lands come out, yes it won't come out until next year. Its FC maybe is trying its best to fix everything, trying their best to make our eyes glow with joy. Or maybe really bring more money in with a well finished product!
