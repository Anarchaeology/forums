The "Doctors Should Be Better Healers than Traders" Fallacy
I can feel the flames already but here goes.

There is nothing wrong with a Trader healing better than a Doctor. Hell I have seen Agents tank better than Enforcers. People who argue for strict roles based on what your view of what a profession should be might want to look at all the Docs with Shotguns before you question the Trader's healing abilities. You also might want to find another game that isn't skills based.


The issue is one of game play. Not every trader is by default a better healer than a doctor. Only very well equipped traders with lots of very expensive nanos (mulitple divest plunders + the DHP) can heal better than docs - and certainly not as much overall if you compare available nano reserves. Docs team heals use BM and MM - light green skills for a doc - these are core skills that every Doc maximizes. A trader's DHP uses BM and TS - light blue and dark green skill - BM is not a core skill for a trader except that it provides the DHP. I have a lvl 112 trader that can cast Adv DHP without Divest/Plundering. In order for me to be able to do this I had to max BM every level and other skills of mine have suffered because of it. Only a Trader who has maxed his BM, wears good implants, has purchased or found all the divest/plunders and DHP's necessary and had stacked up multiple divest/plunders and keeps them going (meaning no more than 3 mins inbetween fights) can out heal a Doc who only has to add to his basic skills and buy the single team heal nano to cast his heals. What is being nerfed here is intelligent game play.

Fix the Doc nanos to tighten the healing range and speed the recharge, fix the Team Health Status window and loosen the range for the DHP - but don't make DHP useless.

But should it be POSSIBLE for a Trader to heal better than a Doctor? Doctors focus on healing. It's all they can do. If (hypothetically) a Trader became better at healing by focusing on healing, then there's something very wrong. I'm not going to debate whether this can actually happen or not, but your post seems to suggest that, as long as it took some effort, it was 'acceptable' for Traders to heal better than Docs.

Arguments about IP cost quickly become weaker. As you get past 100 (and particularly past 150), IP becomes a LOT less tight. You can pretty much max all nanoskills, regardless of profession.

Regarding implants... as far as I can see, Time and Space is the primary nanoskill for a Trader. So you would probably plonk that one in the Shining slot. Bright/Faded clash with Matter Creastion, used for your Armour lines. All Traders I've spoken to never use those. The NCU cost is too high, and when you reach higher levels you just do NOT have the NCU for them due to other buffs. So the chances are you'd implant for T&S instead of MC (unless you chose... say... 'Shotgun' for bright, which would give you 63 more ATK at best). For Bio Meta, it clashes at the Bright slot with Matter Meta. This is only used for your 'Damage buff' lines, the best of which (Guard Convoy) adds +21 damage for 46 NCU. I'd include the +23 buff (Relentless Slayer), but as it debuffs initiatives it's utterly useless . Damage buffs, as they stand, are too ineffective and use too much NCU to be useful at high levels. So you might as well stick Bio Meta there.

Other people may play differently, but from my conversations with Noer I can be sure that MC and MM are both not worth implanting for high-level Traders. Whether you put T&S in the head, or PM/SI isn't known by me for sure though. But the way I see it, the only thing stopping a Trader implanting healing skills 100% is the loss of 105 ATK from the Bright/Faded Shotgun skills.


Note that I'm not touching the Doctors vs Traders argument, I'm arguing that a Trader can focus on healing at very little tradeoff. Agree? Disagree?

The problem is, the only area where Doctors become comparable (and better than) Traders in terms of healing is Target heals. And remember that Complete heal is only self-castable at around 150+. Unfortunately, at that time, the way teams work in missions changes sharply. Because nobody does missions 200+ for XP (because, due to the mob difficulty/XP ratio it becomes less effective) people move faster through missions and get attacked by multiple mobs. Often it isn't just one person under attack. Factor in the current bugged HP bars (which don't show the damage to buffed HP) and you can see why Team heals begin to become the most-used heal.

Thus, due to a combination of an iffy XP difficulty curve, unreliability of single heals, and such, Traders become able to take the place of Doctors at healing.
