Heal please?
Ok, I SORTA understand the "no nano activity" as a reet. I now cannot heal myself by ANY method, and I cannot heal anyone else. Why are beneficial programs prevented? Now I have to be ready to come out of reet in case a team member gets attacked on the way somewhere, since I cannot run ANY programs while morphed as a reet. And then I usually die because the monsters can catch me then, and the rest get away. So I lay down my life for my friends...hmm methinks that should be a choice, not a requirement. I used to do it anyway when it was necessary...

And another thing, as a neutral, I am annoyed that I cannot heal those I choose in political areas. "Opposing side in the conflict?" Um, hello? I picked neutral for a reason, I am not opposing anyone (and I am not into PvP). Now If I join a particular team, I can understand that if it is not a neutral team we have a problem, but on my own, I don't think so. I tried to help someone in trouble in a political zone, and had to just stand there, and watch her die, because she ended up running off, and I could not get the monster's attention, and it would not let me heal her...sigh.

BTW....
Since Neutrals cannot really target by <TAB> anyway, *pats her trusty mouse* you might even make things interesting by giving us (or everyone?) the chance to accidentally heal the "wrong" players (not npc's monsters) *grins*
