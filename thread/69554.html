Locking In Clothing/Worn Items..
I don't know if this has been suggested already or not but I'm suggesting it from the frustrations I've personally encountered in the game. FC please give us an option to Lock In Clothing/Worn Items that we are currently using. For example, this prevents those accidental clicks on a Gray hood in your bag/inventory that would have previously replaced a 200 helm that required you to get buffs and/or swap out implants to equip. Yes it can also be a hassle but you can always turn it on and off when you have to swap your armor for a med-suit or a social armor. You can "lock in" all 3 types of wearable items (Weapons/Armor/Implants).

Good idea or bad idea? Would appreciate some response to this idea. Thanks in advance...
