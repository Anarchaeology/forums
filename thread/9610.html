STORMS should be interactive!
I would like to see the storms more interactive. Especially the really nasty red sandstorms. Its wierd to see people out side in the desert just standing around in the middle of a deadly storm working out their stats or whatever.

What I would like to see be done to storms.

- Depending on the strength of the storm, lightning should hurt people... if not kill(in the most deadly of storms), if they are standing in or near the area(obviously fairly close) that the lightning strikes.

- Wind in the storms should slow run speed depending on the strength. If the storm is incredibly powerful people should practically be pushed backwards unless they have good run speed, and strength and stamina. Yalmahas should be effected by the wind. Ground vehicles should be more resistent to wind and lightning(encouraging people to buy and use vehicles when going places).

- In sandstorms it should get progesively harder to see, I know that it is already visible... but you shouldnt be able to see more than a couple feet in front of you. It may also be difficult to breath and people will have a very minor but slow damage dot from breathing in sand. Ground vehicles would not be effected by the dot, but visiblity would still be short.

In this case people will think twice before running through a desert, people will choose to stay home or in the city during nasty storms... people will retreat when they see a storm coming. The storms are nice graphics, but it would be 100x times better to make them dangerous... make the player actually fear the Rubi-ka storms. A lot can be done with this, including putting in a line of equipment that helps when traveling etc...

It would give Anarchy a more realitic and exciting new viewpoint. People run from the storms, not just say "Oh its just graphics, big deal!".

I agree fully with everyone, even Gil (!).

Dark nights - FUNDERFUL. Make night vision worth it.

Storm effects - even more funderful, but I don't think on the order of outright killing. Hurting a bit (much moreso to a low level char, obviously), yea, perhaps a 10 second stun period.

One other prereq for this, however, would be MUCH longer weather cycles... eg, a bad storm would hit the Newland area for 2 days...... REAL TIME, which gives a larger effect. Now, you say, the RK clock ticks like 2 billion times faster than ours... I think that is silly, since they still date things via our days, and the player sees things pass in real time, as well. Just standardize the time, and make long term weather patterns. It would make for a much more realistic, interactive, and fun world, and Gil's concept of weather reports would be all the more valid; predicting a storm front along the Stret for the next 2 days, followed by sunshine. People would have to adapt, just like IRL, to weather conditions. It would make everything more fun and random.

This would even make night more viable, centering activities around when you can see better... a night attack (or during a storm) on a base becomes much more of an issue and possibility.

Just my 2 credits
Many months ago when I was a real newbie I remember being caught in a sandstorm in Athen West.

I ran for the nearest building thinking that all that flying sand was going to hurt me. It didn't take me long to realize that the "storm" was just a graphical effect and wasn't going to affect me at all (well a LITTLE reduced visibility but that's all).

Frankly I was a bit disappointed. What's the point of having a violently appearing storm if the storm doesn't affect you?

In short I agree with what the others say. Make the storms REALLY mean something. If you are near a lightning strike and get hit by flying debris you should take some damage. If you actually get hit by the lightning you should get a lot of damage. And when the wind is really high, it should slow you down some.

In a sandstorm, people should have to wear helmets that entirely cover their heads or they take damage from getting sand in their eyes and lungs.

And I'm also for special spawns coming out at night and then disappearing when the suns rise. If you'll forgive me for mentioning a rival game, EQ has a zone that low to middle level players are actually afraid to run through at night because that's when the undead spawn and they are perilous. I don't mean that AO should have the undead but you get the idea.

As for making the nights darker---hmmm I already have some difficulty with brightness in AO (and yes my monitor is on highest brightness and I have night vision goggles). It seems to me that until AO introduces a gamma setting in the game that perhaps they should leave brightness alone since everyone's system seems to handle the brightness factor differently.
