I still remember when you got one token per mission. I left before the mutltiple token thing happend and I was so excited when I got 5 tokens for completing a mission that I didnt tell anyone I just figured I was bugged up again.

I remember fondly the virulent bulls in Longest Road. That was fun getting killed by 3 grey bulls when you were 110th. sigh...

I remember thinking the crat pet was so uber with his SMG only to see him run around like he was on crack when he met up with a mob.

I remember when you could summon like multiple pets by zoning back and fort across lines. That was pretty cool my friend used to go to Temple with like 8 pets and clean house.

I remember the day they nerfed the Temple and it was cleared out in like 30 minutes and you had to wait like another 30 to get it built up again.

I remember the day Spyo and Mobiggley said lets go do some BS missions and I said ok It wont be to bad to do a couple of them.

I remember when we all used to sit out at medusas before engineers could warp you back when you died and complained about how you couldnt pull just one only to come back and see that you'ld be lucky to pull one.

I remember when the Shades where the place to be for 50 to 70's and how we would sit out there all night bsing. Remember the joke we all thought was funny. "I'm going back to save." OK bring the save term back when your done. It was funny after camping shades for 8 hours straight.

I remember all those people that have left and I miss them alot.

I remember all the people I've met that arent old school and I think they are as cool as any of my old school friends.
