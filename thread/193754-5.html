Supa Leet
"Not taking part in the conflict" from a story perspective is a different thing from not being able to play like a normal player.

As most people know, pre-towers.. 95% of all PvP was done simply for the fun of PvPing. Really, it was the only "high-level content" in the game.

Barring Neutrals from that is stupid.

There are plenty of logical reasons why someone would want to attack in a PvP, regardless of side or the "conflict."

Furthermore, the presuppositions regarding Neutrals really need to die. "The Notum Wars" technically has nothing to do with the true Clan vs. Omni war, thus the supposition that Neutrals shouldn't be allowed to have towers because they are "neutral in the conflict" is flawed. Towers have nothing to do with "the conflict", and "The Notum Wars" are clearly defined in the story as being a seperate entity.

Likewise, Shadowlands and the Shadowlands conflict is a whole different ballgame as well. Different story, different circumstances.

Neutrals are aligned with Ergo in Shadowlands. That is a very notable role, and thus Neutrals have a very important and substantial place in the story of Shadowlands. Limiting Neutral options in Shadowlands would be counter-intuitive--given that Ergo is at least as influencial as Redeemed/Unredeemed.

Even more to the point, don't forget that Jobe is Neutral as well. Trying to pan off that Jobe--the epicenter of high technology and advancement on Rubi-Ka--is Neutral, yet Neutral players still must like with being inferior is silly. Story-wise, the only reason ANYONE is in Shadowlands is because of Neutrals.

Neutral is an extremely valid and supported "side"/"faction" in the Shadowlands story, and thus should have the same level of gameplay viability as well. It's really that simple.

-Jayde
