So long, and thanks for all the fish, adios, buh bye, thank you and goodnight..
Adventurers again

Yes, I can hear the question. "Why not throw Adventurers into 14.0 then?" The honest answer is that we simply were not prepared to focus on Adventurers when the over-equipping was postponed, and we chose to focus on other changes already in the pipeline. This means we will have features like team missions and extended in-game help aimed for 14.0, together with a lot of other fixes and changes. If we have to postpone the over-equipping patch even more, something I doubt will happen, we will again look into doing the Adventurers before it.

Apology

With that finally explained, I would like to extend an apology on behalf of the Community team in this matter. We should have stayed more updated, and kept you, the players, in the loop on these schedule changes as they happened, not as they became obvious without any word from us. We need to be more aggressive towards getting information, and also share more of it with you. I hope you can trust that we can do that, and that we will have better news than postponed profession focuses next time around.
