Clan Role Players-Lets do some role playing
Hello fellow Clanners. My name is Dargaria. Me and my friend Freaked would like to invite all true role players to "the happy rebel" Club in Tir city, for some good times and great stories. I'm not very good at time zones so I'll have to give you mine. We meet areound 11pm Central time (the best I could do as a canadian) and would like to do it every night. You don't have to come evry night of course, be we'd love if you did. We want you to come in character and share your stories, and we'll share ours. In a case like last night, you may even be able to add twists to ours, by just role playing, and we might be able to do the same for you.

Lets be honest, after a hard day doing missions and hunting, we all need to unwind, so why not at the happy rebel with friends and soon to be friends for some good honest role playing. Everyone of every level is invited I only have a few rules

1. come in character. /tell for anything out of character
2. don't give anyone a hard time if thier story isn't as good as yours. everyone will get a chance
3. role play, role play, role play. make it as exciting as possible, with real emotion and excitement
4. have fun
5. Do not insult, harrass or be cruel. we are all here as friends

I hope to see some of you wednesday night, and every other night (although I might not make it to all of them, doesn't mean you have to stop)

Take care folks, and try not to get killed out there. look forward to meeting you


Darg


ps by the way, if anyone has any information for me about my sisters please let me know. I am struggling to find them.


(all genders and races welcome)
