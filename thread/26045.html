Arenas are specifically for sanctioned combat and challenge. This carries with it protocol and a responsibility. If you are going to fight in the arena, it should be consentual and you should notify someone before attacking them.

Now, if you attack someone who's just standing there, I wouldn't call that "ganking". I'd say you "jumped" them. Ganking is when you attack someone who is already fighting someone else, or who has just finished.

However, this is just semantics. In either case it lacks class and honor.

In MMD, or 2HO, or any other non-arena combat zone, it is expected that war-like combat is going on and as such, it's acceptable to kill the enemy at all costs. Everyone should understand that going in, or don't go there.

This is, of course, only my humble opinion on the matter. However, I have a very specific set of ideals that unfortunately seems to be dying out in the world today.
