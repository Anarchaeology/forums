Please fix the opifex model
There are a number of model glitches with the opifex that have been around since the days of yore...about time to see them fixed?

Back items like backpacks and tank armor don't sit correctly and swing back and forth when you move.

Keelar sunglasses hover somewhere out on the end of your nose.

I know of at least one opifex head which sits too far to the right of the model...very odd looking.

The shoulder pads from the Alvin/Dodga quest don't sit properly and end up sitting half through one shoulder.



Granted, this is just one group of many model issues in the game, but it's particularly annoying because we have to run around as these glitched characters all the time.

Other bugged models are the adventurer polymorphs being unable to handle fast attack speeds, Battle Suits not displaying weapons or attacking properly, and nano casting animations not ending when the recharge bar goes away.
