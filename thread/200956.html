From my own experiences, soloing wasn't easy at low levels, probably because I was too broke to buy decent equipment.. but it wasn't impossible. The key is max evades (dodge/evade clsc), def implants, and max physical init which allows you to move the agg/def bar to full def. I also chose to raise nano init to 500 which allows me to (almost) insta-cast the best heal while staying full def.

I'd say shortly after level 100, with def implants, you'll be very impressed with your soloing ability. At level 150+, you'll be hoping you don't get nerfed. I was soloing Jack "leg-chopper" around level 170ish (had PNH), and took 3 bars off the 250 borg boss in PW before I got bored.
