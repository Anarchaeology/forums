The idea I think
...is that you COULD group with a doctor but you didn't HAVE to group with one to get some healing. However I have to agree with you on several things. First, any profession can manage a substantial amount of self or even team healing, making the doctors a liability rather than an asset. And second, in roleplay terms, Doctor's incomes should be huge. I have never once heard of a poor doctor in real life that wasn't A) just out of college or B) volunteering in some third world country. Omni Tek Docs at a minimum should receive payment automatically for their healing the same way our accounts are credited when we kill some undesirable. And since a Clan doctor had to be educated somewhere, perhaps on scholarships from Sol Banking, it's easy enough to introduce this for the clan as well.

A cash payment of 10% of HP healed, or a minimum of 1 credit (ie if you heal a teammate for 20 points, 2 credits are automatically placed in your account) seems a realistic way for the organization to reward the doctor. This encourages the doctor to actually team up and use his or her skills (I wouldn't recommend letting it work in non-team or self-healing) rather than hiding as far away from the action as possible, weeping over every nano, treatment and first aid kit as credits go down the drain. And moreover a doctor's job is to heal others - at present, every doctor on Rubi-Ka is a volunteer. If balance is a concern then reduce the credits they receive for killing enemies - that's the job of the combat classes anyway, and if missions that didn't require killing for tokens could ever be implemented, it would actually be fair.

The reasoning is this: In reality, doctors are wealthy. why? Because they are paid for their very desirable skills. Now on Rubi-Ka, doctors aren't paid unless a team decides to pay their doctor, something I've never experienced and wouldn't expect to - because a doctor ends up being a liability to keep alive, and is generally about as much trouble as a benefit.
