I must say I am rather disappointed in the performance of Warr. Not only does he/she not fufill the contract fully, but also brings troops that have no concept of ranged combat for a better definsive posture.

The original intention in these actions were defensive in nature. Since then, tempers have been flaring and more has been added, but in its base form, it was the defence of newland. Other independant organizations may care less what happens to newland and borealis, but we do care.

EO is indeed the ones responsible for this action. Read and accept. The other independant organizations had little if anything to do with this. We can be blamed for the death of many clan in the future and recent path, fine. Sometimes in a warzone, it takes something drastic to let the world see your plight. I think this achieved the attention ... tho it didn't go as well as one might have hoped.

We fear no reprisal. We expect it, but this was something that needed doing. If this will help bring down the sentinals in an indirect way by putting more focus on them, then it is worth it.

I personally stay in Borealis, as many independants do. Borealis provides anything you might want in newland, including low level missions. At last report the new mercs in newland don't hang around the whompas unless they are taken there. So it is still possible for new clan people to travel to newland, then use the whompa to borealis, or to newland desert if thats what you need. If you are wary of going through newland and are lvl 30 or so, you should have the computer literacy skill available to grid into borealis. From there you will find a whompa to stret west bank, in stret west you can find a connection whopa to hope in mort, and from there another whompa circling to newland desert. The option of route is yours. Borealis mission terms also give missions to the city of Home (at least I know for a fact the ones by the girdterminal do).

If borealis is not to your liking, then maybe traveling to Stret West bank's town of Last Ditch would be your cup of tea? Reets retreat there is rather nice and plenty of buildings there you can sit on top of.

With plenty of ways to avoid newland, they only reason I can see clan people still going there would be to either be looking for trouble or mis-informed. Its unfortunate for the mis-informed, but they will learn.

What I ask is for you all to not let selfish pride dictate your actions and cause unnecessary death. You have been warned... much longer warning then what neutrals had when the sentinals invaded Tir. For you to continue to go there, based on principle is not going to do anything but make you look rather foolish. In long past history, one of the reasons to commit suicide was because of honor, or how highly one valued their leaders. This is niether of those ... and worse cause ya keep comming back... the death is meaningless... that is one of the problems with rubi-ka. The insurance terminals ensure that almost any death can be nothing more then temperary ...

Which brings about another point. If no death, but perma-death means anything ... why does it matter? Because EO will lose their notum mining facilities? No one else cares about that. Don't pretend you might or did in the past.
