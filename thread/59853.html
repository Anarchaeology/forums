Making Yur Own Guns (silly extreme idea)
As tradeskills stand at the moment its pretty pointless to bother making a gun which you could get much more easily in a shop or something. Instead i propose that gun making is completly re-done, let me explain.

An engineer heads into his trade dept and gets his weapons constuction kit. Then he gets his basic gun metal pieces, after combining the two he has a base weapon (smg Base/AR Base etc..) Each gun base has its different advantages, e.g a smg has less base speed. Now he plods over to the gun design department, here there are lots of items similar to nano clusters which add different things to the guns. All guns start with a time of 10-10 (unless the base of the gun is SMG or pistol for example in which case a small amount is taken away) and in order to shorten that time the enginer must install an item into the gun (the higer the ql the more speed it takes away). Also there are proffesion specific gun parts, using these will make the gun proffesion specific but it will also add a bonus based on that proffesion (soldiers more dmg, docs more treatment and so on) these will also be affected by ql.

So an engineer could make a ql 200 Customized SMG which has every special skill there is and does insane dmg with insane speed, But the reqs would also be insane so noone in the game could wear this gun even at level 200. So the gun maker has to take this into account, prehaps make the gun a little slower and remove the FA skill, or maybe replace the ql 200 cluster of Radiation DMG with a ql 150 (making the gun do less avg dmg). I think you get the basic idea, i know ive left a lot to imagination but its much more fun that way.

Oh and the same applies to melee weapons too but with some little changes.
