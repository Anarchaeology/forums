Circle G - Under new managment
Nearly two weeks ago, Deanne "Dreammate" Tedder officially announced she would be officially stepping ouf of the leadership cirlce of Cirlce G's Rubi-Ka division, and that if new leadership was not implimented from within the current circle, Cirlcle G's Rubi-Ka division would be disbanded.

After the death of Circle G's founder Greatfather, there was much turmoil in the Tedder family. It seems that in his Will, Greatfather left control of the family business in the form of his 52% stock shares of the family owned company to Ayanna "Littledream" Tedder, instead of Deanne "Dreammate" Tedder, as analysts widely expected.

The only Tedder sister happy with the arrangment is Destiny "Minitronix" Tedder, who simply stated: "Im a soldier, not a leader. I never wanted to follow my father's footseps and lead Circle G. I'm happy just being the best soldier I can be, and letting those skills add to Circle G's bottom line."

With the Tedder family in disarray, something unheard of in Circle G's histroy has occured: a person not of the faimly was given control of Circle G's holdings on Rubi-Ka.

When asked for comment, Circle G's new president, a mysterious woman known only a Glarawyn said that "This is a new era for Circle G. We're in unknown territory now, but that's good for headlines, right?"
