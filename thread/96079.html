The Clans come running two by two...
The Clans come running two by two.
Hurrah! Hurrah!
And Omni marches for Virtue!
Hurrah! Hurrah!
For each there is no other way,
Indeed, the Gods say this we play
And all they want to do is...
Kill!
Kill! Kill! Kill!

At rifle's 'crack' its them you own!
Hurrah! Hurrah!
The notum swirls at lifes last groan!
Hurrah! Hurrah!
They fall in piles of steaming flesh,
The more you see your blades will thresh
And all you need is more to...
Kill!
Kill, kill, kill.

The battle slows 'cause most are dead.
Hurrah. Hurrah.
Some few have fled far overhead.
Hurrah. Hurrah?
You look around to find a mark,
The God of War your patriarch.
But now there's no one left to...
Kill,
Kill, kill kill.

They're counting all the gains they've made.
Hurrah! Hurrah!
Chests swelling on each accolade!
Hurrah! Hurrah!
When battle's done, they're blood has slowed,
And rest a single thought has sowed
Looking toward the next to...
Kill!
Kill! Kill! Kill!

The skies could stay forever gray.
Hurrah? Hurrah?
The crickets die, birds fly away.
Hurrah? Hurrah?
And all that they would ever know,
Is how to war and hatred sow
'Cause all they think to do is...
Kill,
Kill, kill, kill.

I sit in quiet reverie.
Hurrah, hurrah.
I'm wondering what I'm to be.
Hurrah, hurrah...
It doesn't seem this pacifist,
In such a world can coexist
With those that only want to...
Kill,
Kill, kill, kill.

I've heard the drumming in my ears.
hurrah, hurrah.
And up your arms you'll throw in cheers.
hurrah, hurrah.
To find that I have seen the Light,
It's Peace that is no more my plight.
I see no path except to...
Kill,
Kill, kill, Kill!

How proud to stand where blood is spilled!
Hurrah! Hurrah!
With comrades all whose souls are chilled!
Hurrah! Hurrah!
When in the end life's blown away,
Our dust has settled, here to stay
So makes no difference who we...
Kill!
Kill! Kill! Kill!

Go to your play my little ones.
Hurrah, Hurrah.
In younger years you'll pick sweet plums.
Hurrah, Hurrah!
But soon you'll find you're only numb,
Each time you die is tedium
'Cause all there is to do is...
Kill!
Kill, kill, kill...
