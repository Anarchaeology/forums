I take it all back
I've just run a new character through the arrival hall to see what all the fuss was about and I have to say, I'm very impressed. I'm impressed with the architecture, decor and layout (it's always good to see a new room for the first time), I'm impressed with the NPC's and the new chat system but most of all I was impressed by the balanced manner with which the sides were portrayed.

It seems that the three "official" representatives standing behind their desks at the far end of the hall are there to spout the established party line. However, for each one of these there's another, informal "representative" in the hall itself (Young Woman, Middle-Aged Man and Old Geezer). These guys give a sort of counterpoint to the standard spiel, so while the official clan rep will tell you all about freedom and brotherhood, Young Woman (the informal clan rep) will tell you that "The ends justify the means". The official neutral rep gives you the old "Can't decide/just want to be left alone" line while Middle-Aged Man opines that Omni and the Clans are the problem, please don't make the situation worse by joining them.

If you're put off by the official Omni line, definitely go and have a word with the Old Geezer.

The only stereotyped part of the whole thing was the "I can't decide which side I should be" test. However, IMO that's really only going to sway people who have talked to the rest of the NPC's and still can't make up their mind. Overall I thought that the whole thing was very well done, and perhaps indicates that FC's view of Rubi-Ka is a little less black-and-white than IGN's review made it seem. I certainly hope so.

Cheers,
