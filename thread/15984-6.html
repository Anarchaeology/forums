By the way, it is my belief that range for nanos and weapons is determined using the same engine as the pathing for mobs.
We see similar issues for mobs and attacks on them. Often textures in the environment affect the action in unexpected ways: a small sign near the OP in 20k interupts a nano targeted at someone just to the other side of it, pets run all the way around the map to attack a mob that is right behind a wall, etc...

It makes sense for FC to use the same method for dealing with these two features of the game. After all, having two seperate engine running pathing and gridding/mapping of the game would make it a beast on the server. The problem is that distance will not be standardized throughout the realm. It seems that area would most efficiently be determined by assigning a value to each texture in the playing field. So a wall, a sign, a patch or grass all have value X. The sum of those values is the "distance" between them. However, there is no guarantee that a huge rock outcropping or a body of water have values corresponding with their (graphically) perceived size. So one can conceivably be far away from a target yet have very few individual graphical objects between them. Therefore they will be able to reach the target that appears to be too far away to reach. Conversely, it is possibly, in graphically "busy" areas, to encounter so many individual "objects" that their sum produces a number far larger than the perceived distance. And so you can't shoot something that seems to be right in front of you.


Clearly this is all postulation. It is at least an attempt to make sense of that which seems senseless. I agree that this needs to be looked into, but I doubt much can be done about it. The problem might be rooted in the very heart of the structure of the game.

In my mind, if we wanted to make PvP interesting and fair we would be asking FC to fix the problems with the present system of the grace period upon entering 25% areas. It is ridiculous that successful PvP entails timing your entrance into the zone so that you go in after the enemy. Taking advantage of ones temporary immunity to attacks is just as lame as using range bugs to kill people from far away. If your are enjoying immunity from attacks in a PvP zone you should also be unable to attack those who have been sitting in the zone the whole time. This alone would even the score with the trader debuffs, stopping PvP traders from always getting the first attack. It would also make people think twice about running into 25% zones so confidently.
