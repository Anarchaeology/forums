OOC: Life expectancy?
I was looking through the official timeline the other day in search of conflicts with Asura's storyline when it hit me, heck, i dont even have a clue as to how long a solitus live! Or any other breed for that matter hehe.

I mean considering the fact that Homo Sapien over the millenia has been so heavily gene modified that it has been deemed justified to give us a new name (Homo Solitus), i would be greatly surprised if longevity havent been tampered with, since that was one of the main reasons humankind started reaseaching geneticts in the first place.
On top of that from what we can tell, during the ice age a human male died from natural causes in his thirties, during the middle age in his fifties and as of A.D. 2003 in the western hemisphere - in his seventies.
And the trend does not seem to dissipate.
So genetic modification or no, the Homo Solitus should live longer than we do.
But by how much?
Has FunCom ever made any comment as to the longevity of the breeds?
Or does anyone have a theory on this?
