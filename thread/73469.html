OTSEC Operation Destroys Sentinels-Council of Ares Arms Stockpile
OTSEC Press Release
12:00 November 24, 29476 RST

ROME (OTSEC/PR)  The joint forces of the Omni-Tek Security Council (OTSEC) took the field last night against the Sentinels and Council of Ares (CoA), destroying a significant stockpile of offensive military-grade weaponry at the Sentinels bunker complex in Mort.

Operation: Prelude to Justice, a deep infiltration intelligence operation into the CoA armed forces initiated some two weeks prior to the announcement of Security Council resolution OTSEC/R-001 (concerning the CoA surrender of military-grade weapons), has yielded strong and incontrovertible evidence that the CoA has been supplying the Sentinels with offensive military-grade weaponry. Furthermore, following the issue of resolution OTSEC/R-001 the Sentinels have provided the CoA with safe house facilities to conceal military-grade assets from OTSEC investigative teams, and hence protection from potential forceful removal by the Security Councils forces.

In the light of the Sentinels aggressive occupation of Tir on Friday, the Security Council elected to accelerate Operation: Prelude to Justice, and to act immediately against the evidence currently in hand.

OTSEC Field Marshal Glenn "Barzillai" Craddock lead teams from Division 9 RSGE, Omni-Pol, GPCore, and SPARTA in joint cooperation, forming an elite special operations strike force to achieve an objective pursuant with Security Council resolution OTSEC/R-001, the disarmament - by force if necessary - of offensive military-grade weapons from CoA forces, and now it seems her allies.

The joint special forces coalition swooped on the Mort bunker complex in a classic pincer style operation, and after fierce resistance from Sentinels personnel, finally secured the facility. Following the intelligence provided to the Security Council by its covert infiltration operatives, the special forces personnel quickly located, documented, and then destroyed the significant stockpile of offensive military-grade weaponry.

Its mission accomplished, the coalition special forces ensured the area was secure, before withdrawing to prevent any further unnecessary conflict or loss of life. However, the Security Councils forces were counter-assaulted as they extracted by individuals from CoA guilty party Clan Anarchist Syndicate, though after a brief skirmish at Meetmedere the disorganised rebels were quickly put to the sword, and the Security Councils special operations forces moved into The Grid.

This action would have been entirely avoidable if the Sentinels and Council of Ares had not flagrantly disregarded the Security Council resolution OTSEC/R-001, a resolution to designed to bring a halt to the conflict, acts of aggression, and to bring peace and stability to the area.

I am often found quoting the corporate line in defence of instances such as this; "Terrorism will not be tolerated. Talking, diplomacy, and caution did not get us the needed results," - CEO Philip Ross, October 15, 29476 RST. It is as true now as it is each and every time I quote it.

The time for talk is now over. All further acts in defiance of Security Council resolution OTSEC/R-001 will be met with similar actions and consequences. I urge the Council of Ares to disarm their military-grade assets immediately, or face more of the same.

I salute the loyal company employees; the men, women, and atrox of Security Council forces Division 9 RSGE, Omni-Pol, GPCore, and SPARTA who bravely and selflessly took part in this operation, and the corporations department heads who lead their people in the field; Glenn "Barzillai" Craddock, Monty "Meister" Capo, and Katelin "Ukblizzard" McChristion. It was an honour to serve alongside you all - the corporation salutes you!


Omni-Tek protects.

Strength through unity.
