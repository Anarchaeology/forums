If you're Clan, take the Whompha from Tir to ICC; if you're OT, take it from Trade to ICC.

Once there, take the Whompha to Newland, then to Borrealis, then to Stret West Bank.

When you enter Stret West, look to your left. There's a doorway in the stone wall. Head through the doorway into the laneway, and turn left.

Keep running straight, and lo and behold Reets is right in front of you. The entrance is at ground level, on the left side of the building.

Once you're inside, follow the hallway straight and then up to your right (and make sure to check out the first room on your left - it's one of the coziest places on RK).

At the top of the ramp, turn left and keep going straight until you can't go straight anymore. Turn left again, and follow the one-way path into the GSP Glassfloor Lounge.

And, of course, THE DOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMMMM.
