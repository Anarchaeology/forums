Why i turned...
It's been hard for me over the last two months, my life has changed... for the better or for the worse? i am unsure.

On Earth i was a peaceful man, i had a keen interest in technology and the discoveries of notum. After a few years and making some very minor discoveres in the area of nanos i was headhunted by Omni-Tek to join them as a reserch partner in the field of new nano-tech.

The years past and i worked away under the crushing workload of the Omni-Tek managment and i carried on my life as normal. One day a man contacted me, i am unsure of how he knew me but all i knew of him was he called himself "Friends of the Clans" which made no sense to me at the time. He asked me to look at a few files on the grid network, sounded simple enough and my level of access could easily read these files. I thought nothing of it and passed on these files which seemed to be useless to the man.

Two months past and i never heard from him again. One day i had my manager step out of his office and call me in, in his office was two Omni-Pol officers and a Omni-Com representive...this never can be good. I was charged with Corporate Espionage and Traitor to the Corporation, i fully denied these charges until they uttered the name "Matalok" and quickly followed with "you may know him as 'Friends of the Clan'". My heart sunk, my life has been ruined by the stupidity of myself.

I they deemed that the death penalty would apply to me, but they decided that "near-death" was a better alternitive. The next day i was on a prison fleet to a strange planet called Rubi-Ka, rumors within the hold spread that you cannot die on Rubi-Ka. We landed and i was dragged into a security complex where Omni-Tek changed my genetic makeup. I looked in the mirror only to be shocked at whats happened to me, others around me looked the same. Accordingly i was changed to what they called a "Nanomage". This breed of man can only survive on Rubi-Ka so we had no chance of escape from this barren planet.

I worked in a prison colony doing menial tasks and helping the construction in the wastelands. I was asleep one nite and woke to a large crashing sound, i turned to see 3 men...or what seemed to be 2 men and a beast standing in a hole thats been blown through the side of my cell. A man stepped forward, cladded in large metal armour. "Hello, my name is Matalok. I am here to save you, Komarosu". My eyes opened wider when i heard the name, could it be? Why has he done this?

We fled out of the compound, Matalok and his team put down any Omni-Pol enforcers that tried to stop us. He took me to a place called Tir where he introduced me to some powerful local clan leaders. He filled me in on the last year of the conflict and how my help has assisted them with there struggle. At that time i decided to join these rebels, i will fight along side them against the Omni-Tek oppression.

And thats how i arrived her, i now live as a trader. I've learned that Nanomages have great skill of handling nanotechnology and using my existing knowledge i've been able to learn so much more.

And so my struggle continues...
