Hello Flash,

The South Rubi-Ka Society is an Omni-Tek roleplaying guild. All our members are roleplayers, we are not much good at anything else.

Our aim is to provide a sustained roleplaying environment for our members. We do this by regularly running "pen and paper" style roleplaying adventures for our members and by meeting up to socialise in-character.

In game the South Rubi-Ka Society is in essence a gentlemen's (and ladies!) club with a butlyer and all. His foramt means we can accomodate characters with widly different views and backgrounds. It also mean you will not be taking order from other players. We are all equals.

We are a small guild, but we've been around since last November. We have a fairly equal spread of US/Euro players of varying levels.
