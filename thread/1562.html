I think we all owe it to ourselves to prevent a few vocal "leaders" from directing our history. Clanner, do you want to fight OT for your freedom? Then stand up and demand that your voice be heard.

Already, we hear that the Council of Truth has decided to accept this OT offer of "amnesty," in effect criminalizing the great accomplishments of our revolution by falsely acknowledging those great deeds as criminal acts and further falsely acknowledging Omni-Tek as an authority suited to grant pardon.

That this is a tremendous set-back to clan causes is patently obvious. I hold these misguided clan leaders responsible for this tragedy because they fostered the idea of peace with Omni-Tek before freedom was secured. In very real ways, they have betrayed the toil and suffering of those who have won us the precious toe-hold on liberty that we now have.

Stand up, Clanner. Demand that the Council of Truth listen to your strong, clear voice.
