The Glacier
I wrote this when Whisper's Edge was newly formed after breaking away from Clan Anarchist Syndicate. It's not very good but, seems a bunch of people are sharing so here goes:


Once we were many and close,

Indvidual crystals of ice bound together in a glacier on the northern seas,

The heat of summer broke us down into two icebergs,

One small the other large,

We drifted apart like the southern ice on the northern seas is want to do,

What was once proud and strong melted,

Reformed on the southern ocean us few crystals of ice,

We persevered through the trip,

The lessons learned on our trans-ocean journey will make us strong again,

A mighty glacier on the southern ocean,

One that will never melt in the summer sun again.
