The way I understand it, it was intended that you would be able to tailor your organization with advantages/disadvantages, for a price. For example, an Engineer guild could buy a "Advantage: Mechanical Engineering", and then all members of the organization would recieve a boost in that skill.

When Org Tax was introduced, I was sure that these things would be next in line, but sadly they haven't developed this idea. I think this would be a great money sink. Org tax would go towards keeping up these advantages, which would constantly suck money along the lines of a subscription fee. If your org doesn't make its dues one month, then you lose the advantage.

Hopefully FC will complete the implementation of this feature, or if they're not going to, then please take the items out, they're just a constant reminder of missed expectations
