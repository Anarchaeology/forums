14.0 Pet pathing issues tested and reporting
Various problems born or enhanced since patch 14.0 :

1°) Pet is running a lot away from me. Typically when entering the first room of a mission, he will dash in the main room and be back after a while. That is if he finds the door.

2°) Pet has problem finding doors. The pathing of the pet is obviously aimed at me in a straight line, while trying to compensate as much as possible for various furniture and walls. If I am in a room and the pet in another, he will often be blindly trying to pass through the wall, while being at 50 cm of the door. Unless I go back in his room and slowly pass through the door, he won't find it.

3°) Pet has no LoS behind a table. Classic one in rooms with a table inthe center. The pet will run madly, trying to see the target on the opposite side of the table. Quite often the pet will try to exit room and find another entrance...

4°) Pet stuck in shelves, tables and corridor. Three typical scenarios where the pet will get stuck :
- the small room with a table in the center, a couch in an angle and two shelves. Pet will get stuck in shelves or behind couch. Some mission entrance with shelves that look like scanning devices right and left from door, will get pet stuck too.
- the main conference room where there is this loooong table will get pet stuck in the middle of the table.
- the corridors that have a round section will often see pet get stuck in the part that is round near floor.

5°) Pet can't run and attack. When a mob is running after someone, the pet will have a lot of difficulties to target it. He will run back and forth, taking his time still running back and forth when the mob will eventually stop.

Better pet pathing :

The command /pet hunt seems to be more effective. When you are in a room door closed and a mob is next room, the pet won't hesitate 1 sec and just dash at mob's throat. This is true if the mob is the closest one of course.
This very effective attitude is quite astonishing given the various dumb behavior in many other situations.

Disclaimer :

I took time before posting this to double check everything. There is some other concerns that are not reproductible or plainly involve exploits that I reported and that I won't publicize.
This also has been tested with a Bureaucrat pet that is fairly small and don't get stuck easily. This has been tested and reproduced with all other pets (except the first Engie pet type).
