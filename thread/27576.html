You can use a metal extractor on the golden nugget and get a gold ingot. If the nugget is q190+ it can be used in making Virral Eggs and that makes it valuable to some.

Other items I've found can be used for this are:
Bracer of Rubi-Ka
Marriage Ring
Golden Sphere

..if you find any of these items in the qlvl range 190-220, I suggest you save them.

Soul Fragments are also valuable these days, as they are needed to make these eggs too.
