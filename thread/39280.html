RK2 Neut Guild looking for applicants
The Neut Guild the Rogues Gallery is now looking for applicants. We a small band of solid players (non-twinking) that is looking for a good time, good people and to make Rubi-Ka 2 fun.

If possible, at the same time we wish to improve conditions for those that are neutrally aligned. Demonstating to FunCom that neuts can have an active role in the Clan/Omni story without being a 'third side' or being left out of the conflict.

If roleplay, events and a good time is what you are looking for (as well as making a statement to FunCom) give Damon "Larsonny" Moscardelli a /tell in game today.
