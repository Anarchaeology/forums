Unique fix
I realize that not allowing persons to store uniques in bags in banks keeps persons from exploiting. However, this means that those persons storing many different types of unique items can no longer store them in bags, thus taking up more space in the banks. I'm not sure if Funcom is working on a fix for these people, but would offer a suggestion.

I currently have 8 different types of unique items in the bank. I would save 7 spots if I could put them all in 1 bag. But since I can't, I lose 7 spaces. So, why not make the banks bigger to compensate? Would this not also be easier from a coding aspect?
