Tarryk leans back to take a long pull from his nanodeath cigarette. The bluish cloud of smoke that he exhales seems to hang in the air for a few moments, distorting his view of the display terminal at his desk.

A newsfeed comes up that sparks his attention. An eyebrow goes up.

Tarryk sends a mental signal to his comlink without moving a muscle. He practically forgets about his cigarette as his eyes dance across the screen, skimming over the newsfeed.

The person on the other end of the comlink eventually picks up. It's a groggy male voice, having obviously been risen from a rather enjoyable slumber.

"Wuddaya want..." it doesn't sound like a question.

Tarryk snaps back to reality at the sound of the voice over the comm, and takes another pull from the nanodeath before replying. The cloud of smoke seems to deepen his words, muffling the excitement behind them. "Check out news feed number two-three-three. Get me something more, this is good."

"Two-thirty-three? Read it already. Another omni traitor org gone nuts."

"No, there's more to it. Read the follow-ups and the bylines. This one's got attention from more than the standard trollers. And for goddess' sake, go get a glass of water, you sound like *&#$."

Tarryk leans forward, reading the last few paragraphs of the feed, and blows a ring of smoke directly at the terminal where it distorts a bit as it passes through the holographically-projected text. He moves his thumb over the Save button on his termpad, and mashes down with intent.

I do not know whether to be amused or saddened by the current plight that Omni-Pol and Name Witheld currently find themselves in in regards to Omni-Tek Rubi-Ka's (OTRK) political situation upon this planet. A young member of Name Witheld by the name of 'Poppop' came to the establishment (bypassing the guards via our infamous unguarded grid term) and was whinging upon the fact that other employees of OTRK were refusing to assist him with assigned missions or to provide beneficial nanoprograms (colloqially called 'buffs') for him. While I could almost sympathize with his plight brought on by his Organizational superiors, when I heard his barrage of propaganda he brought to bear against his own fellow employees I had to cringe.

Every fellow clanner of mine stood and stared at him dumbfounded for a moment as if we could believe that this OTRK employee was preaching in The Cup against the might of Omni-Pol. As much as I loathe that particularl section of OTRK, when asked for anything to back up his claims, he could not. Shovel fed propaganda, which is obviously his core belief system, shows exactly how ANY OTRK organization (including this Name Witheld org) that attempts to feed such type of training (regardless of how Pro-Clan it can be) is a part of OTRK that needs to be torn down.

Needless to say, the esteemed Mr. Poppop was sent on his way out of The Cup, apparently upset at his lack of nodding heads and 'me too!' support from the Clan members in The Cup. As much as I feel recruiting disaffected OTRK employees is a good way to strengthen the clans, this particular fellow would not be the type of free minded thinker that I feel is the best person to add to our numbers.

In the end, if I were P. Ross (which I'm not, of course), I'd be firing a great many employees in both OP and NW, particularly the ones that led to this situation rising to the level it did, specifically their leadership. Instead, I continue my work organizing my own clan, and realize that at least here, other Clanners will be looking out for me, not just their bottom line.
