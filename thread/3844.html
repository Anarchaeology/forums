Stitchings' Pillow Arguments
Hokay, being the first and last dual pillow wielder on RK2 I feel I should present my feelings on this matter.

I've been dual wielding pillows ever since they put shining ambidexterity clusters into stores. Ever since I equipped my right hand pillow I have NEVER removed it.

Argument 1 - FOR allowing the use of dual pillow wielding:
It was legal when I did it. I should be allowed to continue using this pillow until the day I decide to drop it. Funcom has set this precedent before when they set omni-only restrictions on certain armor pieces - clanners were allowed to continue using it, but once they removed it they could no longer re-equip it.

Funcom has made clear, through various nerfs to the pillow, that they feel it is overpowered and unbalances the game. Now they have come up with this policy that dual pillow wielding is bad.

Argument 2 - AGAINST allowing the use of dual pillow wielding:
Other items that Funcom has felt were unbalanced have been removed, for example burst from Sunbursts and knight armor. Therefore, they have the right to determine in this case what to do with pillows.

Caveat: Knight armor, which was not an exploit, was personally removed by Funcom. My right hand pillow, ALSO not an exploit, must also be removed in the same way if they wish to maintain the precedent. I will not comply with any verbal order to remove the pillow, whether it be from my guild leader or an ARK or the Guardians on RK2.

I hope that I will be allowed to continue to use my pillows, as they are now part of my character and familiar to many who walk RK2. If I am not... then come get them yourself, Funcom. You won't get a willing acceptance from me. Don't bother sending an ARK to me, I've already given you my response. Either remove the pillow yourself or leave it be.
