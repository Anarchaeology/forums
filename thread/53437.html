Allow us to heal guards
There are a variety of reasons many players would like to protect guards from being attacked and killed. Currently, it's possible to use health stims on guards, but I'd like to see this extended to allow healing nanos to be cast on them as well.

Players would stop arbitrarily killing guards if there were groups of players standing around healing them. This would allow the players to solve the problem of guard killing on their own.
