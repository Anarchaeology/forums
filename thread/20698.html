A thank you to funcom.
I know this only partly belongs in this forum but it gets much more traphic then the Test forum.. and still pertains to the 'next patch'. I also thought it had more of a chance for the mod's and such to see this thank you and pass it on to the devs (if you would ).

Anyway I just wanted to say thank you. I just found out that on test in v14.07 West Athen, Borealis and Rome were added as newbie backyard home cities. Now to some this may seem trivial but I've been pushing, long and hard to open these backyards up again. I see back to the day when people actually explored the world, looked for new hunting grounds and area's of interest. Back then when you started in West Athens (as my first character did in Beta4) you end up hunting Athen Shire.. taking a chance of a Virulent Minibull run in just so you could run around the beach and hunt Malle.

Now adays it seams people all just hang around Tir and hunt Tir County/GTC and this is because everyone starts in Tir when creating new characters. The ONLY time I see people in other cities such as west athen or even athen are when they are fairly high level and have been in the game for some time.

Can you believe that there are people who have NEVER left Tir/TC/GTC until there 40th or 50th level?

So this is my formal THANK YOU to funcom.. for listening to me and all the other oldies who've been with this game since it's beginning (or soon after). I am VERY eager to see newbies in Athen.. that can experience the wonderfull and terrifying things I experienced when I was young (well my avatar).

Again.. I'm sooo glad to see this and so happy that someone at funcom actually listened to the few of us who said that having everyone start in Tir/Newland/Omni-E backyard 1 was a bad idea and would discurage exploration and team hunting.

Those that reply to this.. although not necessary.. please add your fond memories of the early days when exploration and outdoor hunting was the highlight of the game .
