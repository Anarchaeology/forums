Yalmaha changes
The "STORMS should be interactive!" thread got me to thinking about our good old friend the Yalmaha. So here are my thoughts.

Yalmahas are too powerful. They make all other vehicles obsolete and they allow risk free passage to even the toughest zones in the game. I suggest adding some small flight characteristics to make flying more challenging, provide some advantage to other vehicles and to give a reason to continue to raise the air vehicle skill.

Id also like to see more airborne hazards introduced into the game to make ground travel a viable option for at least some of the time.


Proposed changes
  • Speed  add two commands, accelerate and decelerate that are mapped to the keyboard. The rate at which a player can change speed should be based on your vehicle skill. Getting to top speed or decelerating to a full stop from top speed should take a while
  • Turn rate  planes cant turn on a dime. Add a rate of turn that is based on air vehicle skill. The turn rate should also be significant.
  • Stalling  A plane must have a minimum speed to be airborne which is also affected by vehicle skill
  • Hit points  Plane can take damage that will affect speed, turn rate, stalling speed plus add random changes in direction and height while flying. The more damage a plane takes the more pronounced these affects would be. A plane will not work at all when hit points reach 0, but will probably wont be flyable before that.
  • Crashing  A drastic change in speed will signify a crash. Hitting a tree, ground Crashing will do significant damage to the player and plane. The player would also be stuned for a period of time, perhaps with an effect similar to the rez effect. The amount of damage and stun is relative to the speed that the plane was going and the pilots skill at the time of the crash.

    A player should have to look for a suitable takeoff and landing spot. Doing so in a forest should be impossible unless they have a very high skill. Crashing should be bad. Crashing near an angry mob should be near fatal.
  • Can be repaired by using trade skills
  • Is affected by weather. Storms will cause damage to a plane that is flying in them.
  • Allow weapons/bombs to be mounted on planes. Perhaps only non or low damaging attacks could be allowed such as snares, roots or small DoTs.

First off no one is saying get rid of Yalms. However I am saying that it shouldnt be the best vehicle to use always. Sometime a ground vehicle or water vehicle should be better like during storms.

Part of the mission difficulty is based on distance. The further away a mission is the better the reward. Yalms make that meaningless. I agree that there should be close quick missions that you could get for those times that you dont have long to play. However, if you do have a while, you should also be able to get a mission in a far away and hard to get to area that has a much bigger reward. With Yalms allowing you to fly over everything without risk, those kinds of missions will never happen

You also have to ask yourself, where would you rather the risk come from in a mission. In a dungeon or out in the wilderness. If you get a mission that is hard to get to, the dungeon can be made smaller. I dont know about you, but the number one thing I hate about dungeons at high levels is that they go on forever and are not very interesting. Enter room, fight mob, get loot rinse.repeat.

Fighting in close cramped quarters doesnt work well for some profession, like NTs. I think they would welcome having smaller dungeons if they could fight most of the mobs outside. On the other hand, an Atrox Enforcer might like a large dungeon that is closer. I think both types of missions should be obtainable.

Anyways I am getting off track into how I would start to fix missions. The point is with Yalms the way they are now there is no sense of distance. I didnt really like EQ that much but one thing that Ill give it was the feeling of travel. When I moved into a new zone away from a city, I felt like I was really out there. In AO I can get to the furthest part of the most dangerous zone in no time and I could have done that 30 levels ago. In fact any level that can get into a plane can go anywhere without risk.

We have heard players complain that there is nothing to look forward to. In EQ there were zones that you had to work for to even be able to see. This gave characters a sense of accomplishment. I ask you, Who has that feeling in AO?

Planes should be a fast way to cross large distances and get close to your destination. Flying one should come with some risk that is lessened with higher Air Vehicle skill. Ground transportation should allow you to go exactly where you want to go in all kinds of weather/situations (Anti air guns anyone) and is better suited for short distances.

If planes are kept exactly the way they are, then tell to me what purpose do Ground or Water vehicles have?

What I am saying is that a player can choose to select missions that are farther away but the actual dungeon is smaller. All you are doing is trading time in the dungeon for travel time. If you prefer the short distance but larger dungeon you should be able to select that too. Not all classes are built to go toe-to-toe with mobs so this allows players to choose a mission that is more suitable to their character.

Mission travel times should not be an issue. If travel in AO takes longer, then the missions should be made closer.

I only want four things.
  1. High level zones should be too dangerous for low levels to enter. When you are finally able to see it, it should feel like a major accomplishment
  2. Traveling to an out of the way spot should mean something. If need be, Funcom should implement smaller settlements out in the boonies so that players dont have to come back to the major cities as often. But when you are away from civilization, you should feel kind of detached.
  3. Ground and Water vehicles should have some advantages over planes beyond price. The best way I see to do this is that planes will get you quickly from one general area to another, but once you are there, you will have to travel on foot to finish the trip. Ground vehicles wont let you cross great distances as fast but you will be able to drive the entire time.
  4. Adding new types of missions that are based off of traveling. If you want to talk boring, how about missions where you: enter room, fight mob, get loot, enter room, fight mob, get loot, enter You get the point. You cant have travel based mission if traveling is trivial.


I repeat, I dont want to increase the time it takes to do missions. I think that they take too long now and need to be shortened.
