Angels of Harmony
A new Clan Guild has arrived on Atlantean. Based on the same ideals and concepts as the Angels of Light was, Boglwe and I are creating a new guild. I am leading this organization, and Boglwe is my recruiter, event planner, and closest advisor.

Angels of Harmony is creating a family for our members. A group that offers fun, friendship, and support for them when they need it.

Our first goal is to have fun. ((After all, we're playing a game, why are we doing this if we aren't having fun?)) Second is to help our members, and the rest of Rubi-Ka, to understand that having fun on this world doesn't have to be limited to levels and power and uber loot.

Expect to see us sponsor some events in the near future for everyone to enjoy.

In the meantime, feel free to speak with either Boglwe or I about what our guild is about. Or, you can check out our gridsite (Angels of Harmony).
