There is a full novel, it is included in PDF form in the Special Edition box, and it can be ordered in hardcover from the FC online store.

They are essentially posting that novel to the website over time, as resources allow for it.

The novel really is the 'prologue' to the game - the ingame storyline and the animated series basically start at or shortly after the end of the novel. This kicked off on October 31st of last year with the amnesty announcement and the start of personal negotiations between Radiman and Ross and the appearance of a horde of Pumpkinheads who dropped teddy bears named "a Childhood Memory" that served as keys into the brand-new Steps of Madness static dungeon.
