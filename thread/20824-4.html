level 100+
Ok, people have been askin' about level 100+ hunting areas.

If you're clan, and got a good team together with a couple healers or a doc, you can fly north of meetmedere, zone to mort, and you will see the crater. There is a borg factory that you will see on your left, which has really good spawn rate, and borgs are scattered all around this crater. If you fly right along the crater when you see the factory, you will find an NPC near a tent, and there is a save terminal near this location. When you find the outpost/save, there is another crather to the northeast, typically called 'rock crater' that has a cave in the middle of it you can zone into. This is a heavy spawn rate, and not very easy to break, but once you do, borgs spawn every 15-20 seconds if you can keep up with the rate.
PS- Outside the Clan Outpost, it is PVP territory...so keep your radar on, and watch for the omni raids that can happen here. Reccommend saving every 200k xp or so from lvl 100-120, after that is probably ok to take some chances.

Happy Hunting.

Oki Tahrus, here are the coords for the Places I know of,..

and I don't know where the scorpian pit is, and can't find any around tir,but there might be one out there, maybe you were thinking of the Flea pit, which is pretty well known.

These are all in Greater Tir county, which you reach by leaving the Westgate of Tir and going North. to run right into the temple OP you just head to the Outpost just outside of the westgate thats NW of TIR, then head North, you will Zone, then run along edge of bot mountain, and OP is right there.

20+ Bot Mountain, Rocky Outcrops 1700X2170

20+ Omni outpost, East Broken Ground region 3270X1880 East of temple OP, a good ways, inside a rock formation that can be seen far off.

20+ Scorpion Pit, Greater Tir Country....Flea pit in just Tir county maybe?

30+ Temple, Temple of the 3 winds region 1800X2700

40+ Shades, Plains of Salt 740X2850 West along wall from the Temple OP

40+ Borg Camp Outside, Borg encampment 3229x2347 East from Temple OP

50++ Borg Camp Inside, Same area but inside the encampment


There Is an Outpost in the East by the Borgs and Whale bone woods[ good 20-30 hunting with snakes and reets, just have to watch the scorps, its Heavy aggro land] that outpost is at 2600X2700

IN the West but still GTC is another OP which is south and a little East of the shades. it's at 1000X2000

Outside borgs can be pulled safely with a good 40s team, even less than full, but inside you will need a full well rounded team of 50s, or just a full team of 60s.


There are also A-4000 soldier robots roaming on the Back side of the Wall that is Far north and where the Shades are, you can start at shades and travel East on bleak side of wall killing soldiers till you end up at the Gap by the Borg camp and outpost.
