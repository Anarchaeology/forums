Interview Subjects Needed
Dear Rubi-Ka citizens,

Death in our world is, unfortunately, a daily occurrence in our lives. We risk our lives for whatever cause we believe in, and sometimes pay a high price for this. We trust in the equipment that Omni-Tek has provided for us in the form of Insurance Terminals so that our deaths will not be permanent. Those of us in the medical field are pleased that such technology exists as it tremendously reduces the death rate on such a hostile planet as this. But on the other hand, some of us have also noticed some strange symptoms that seem to arise from the re-forming of the body after it has been through a recompiler a number of times. This is where I need your, the people of Rubi-ka, assistance. I am beginning a study on what has been named Repetitive Death Syndrome and am in need of subjects to interview. Please read the following questions and if you can answer yes to any of them I would appreciate it if you would contact me to schedule an interview.

1. Do you experience any kind of rush or high from death?
2. Do you ever get excessively emotional when you end up dieing, yet are happy to run the risk again?
3. Do you find yourself putting more traditional and supportive relationships on hold in order to put yourself in life-threatening situations?
4. Do you have any nervous habits, i.e. excessive cleanliness, nervous ticks, weight problems, since youve begun to experience frequent deaths?
5. Do you have problems going for any length of time without the need for the risk of death?
6. Do you find yourself thinking for days about your latest death experience(s)?

I will keep all interviews confidential, so if you are having any of these problems please feel free to contact me. You can contact me on-planet via com, or by my e-mail at: linaa@doctor.com.

Thank you for you assistance,

Dr. Linaa Flowers
The Healing Hands
