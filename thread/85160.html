Actually that article is about Everquest.

And the insecure, needing, unfufilled instinct in all people, to find something outside themselves to complete themselves.

Ive been playing Anarchy Online since beta and 11.0

I quit in 12.6 with a lot of my friends due to some major technical issues with the gameplay.

I returned in 13.5 and have been here ever since.

Yes, I have taken some things too seriously, as alot of you know, Ive been in more guilds then changes of armor.

But, the point is, I enjoy AO still. It snot a chore to me, I like chatting with my ingame friends, and doin whatever with them.

Im not stuck on a levelling treadmill of doom,

I rarely do BS missions, and when I do its' with friends.

Mostly these days you'll find me at primus camp, either teamed or healing the teams there.

But, I still explore, and thats amazing. Ive seen every inchof land, and yet I still explore. Why do you ask? Cos I take friends with me who havent seen these places, and thats fun for us both.
