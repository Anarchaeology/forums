I love the trade system, but couldn't it be a BIT more realistic?
Okay, I've played, (and still play) two MMORPG's. EQ and AO. EQ's "trade container" system has to be the least realistic concept imaginable. In this, AO's tool applied or ingredients combined system is far preferable.

However. In the real world, quality has two factors, (well, three, but I'm discounting vaguarities of Fate, as I'd rather not ROLL for QL of the completed item.) These two factors are the quality of the components, and the skill of the tradesmith. In AO, if you pick up a QL 150 item, you can only combine it with other items within a percentage of that QL, and you HAVE to have a certain QL to assemble it.

I'd like to see the following two changes.

1. Allow the assembly of ANY two QL's, but test. If they wouldn't normally assemble by todays system, reduce the QL of the greater component until they will.

2. Allow someone of deficient skill to assemble items of any QL, but automatically limit the output to the maximum QL that skill could produce by the current system.

Basically, if a player has JUST enough skill to complete a QL 31 item, (random number, intentionally not a multiple of 5 for emphasis,) has a QL 40 part, and a QL 140 part, in one of those recipes where the QL of the former has to be >= the QL of the latter, allow the player to produce a QL 31 item from the materials.

Allow a player with the skills to produce QL 46 items, with the same components, to produce a QL 40 item.
