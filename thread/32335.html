Headline News
Good Afternoon, Here is the update on current events In Neutral Territory.

Borealis Currently run by the Rubi-Ka Asset Management is doing well as the financial market is on the rise, which is bringing, new and old face's back to the new secured streets of Borealis, where trade and commerce is booming.

Newland Cities financial market is also on the rise, since the new control of the Free Souls, has fallen upon the neutral territory, with their soldiers patrolling the streets people are starting to come out of the woodwork to trade and have fun once again.
It is good to see the city starting to get back on its feet and security tight.

Already the cities engineer's have started to produce robots which are currently repairing water pipes and fixing communication satellites, which is starting to breathe new life into newland.

With financial wealth on the rise in neutral cities hopefully the cities will be recognised as a commercial point to where prosperity and welath is common.
