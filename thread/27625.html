Mission Change Idea
First off let me say I think you should not get a reward if you sneak a mission where you are supposed to "kill everyone inside".

Just let us open chests and pick locks while in sneak mode, WITHOUT losing our sneak mode.

If other classes can Calm an entire mission, get ALL the loot, then let Agents and other classes "sneak" a mission, and open these chests too.

This includes our inability to even pick open a locked door. Once we try to pick it open, the whole place knows we are there, and it's a sprint to the exit or the reclaim.

The calming classes can also "calm" a much higher mission then any agent can hope to sneak.

I am not saying we should change the calming classes!!! Just let us open chests and pick locks w/o blowing our cover.
