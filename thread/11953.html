"""Is a PvP zone identical to a 0% supression gas (This is chaos!) zone?"""

PvP zones are 0% (mayhem) and 25% (politcal)

"""If not, can neutrals attack in a 0% supression gas (This is chaos!) zone?"""

They can attack first in 0% and not 25%

"""Does the PvP trophy do anything for your character or is it just an ego thing? If so what? How much? How can ya tell?"""

Ego thing.

"""Does the PvP title do anything for your character or is it just an ego thing? If so what? How much? How can ya tell?"""

Ego thing.

"""Whats up really with the "Team Level = #" thing? Do we really get more xp as some folks say? How much? How can ya tell?"""

As team level goes up, your character chance to hit increases and so does your chance for a critical hit. It has nothing to do with exp.

"""If you are neutral, how come you want to PvP?"""

Some may, some may not. The option should at least be there.

"""Where are all these hostile neutrals anyway?"""

People who choose to PvP never seem to join the neutrals with good reason.
