The longest journey
Taxtor stood over the control panel of the ship, pounding it ferousciously. "why isnt it working?" The control panel made a few sounds, and began functioning normally. "finally..." Taxtor looked out the window at Rubi-Ka, and tried to remember why he had come in the first place.

***********3 months ago***********************

The alarm clock woke Taxtor suddenly. "Damn!" he cursed as he bumped his head on the bunk above him. He quickly turned off the alarm clock and got out of bed. He got his equipment and went down to the mess hall of the facility. When one of his friends, Traton saw the messy-haired, sleepy looking Solitus walking towards his table woth a tray of supplements, he asked "what the hell happened to you?" "that damn alarm clock again." "Tell me about it. They've been putting the things all over the barracks." "I know... Wish they hadnt." After he ate, he walked towards the gym, hoping to get a workout before the next raid. After passing the R&D department, he was stopped by a Luitenant. "Sir, the want to see you in the Tech Lab. Something about a private message that was routed through there." "Ill head over there now." He walked on the normal route to the gym, and turned into one of the doors marked "TECH LAB". When he came in, he sat down at a computer, logged in, and opened his private messages. There was one encrypted message, which he ran through a decryption program that only the ICC peacekeepers had access to. The message showed the following:


TO: CORPORAL NED "TAXTOR" WASHKO
FROM: COUNCIL OF TRUTH-HENRY RADIMAN
SUBJECT: RECRUITING


Hello Corporal. My name is Henry Radiman, I am the leader of the Council of Truth on the planet of Rubi-Ka. I have heard of your special abilities, and your...past traumatic experiences with Omni-Tek. I would like to recruit you personally in the fight against the Omni-Tek corporation for the freedom of our beloved planet. We regret that you would have to resign from your position of ICC peacekeeper, and you would have to start anew as a private in the Clans, but you would truly be a valuable asset to our cause. Please reply with your answer. I eagerly wait for your reply.

END MESSAGE

Taxtor couldnt believe what he had just read. The Council of Truth chose him, out of the millions of ICC peacekeepers, to come to Rubi-Ka and fight Omni-Tek. The day after, he told Traton about the message he had recieved. "I'd go," he immeadeatly replied. "Everyone knows Omni-Tek are all scumbags, the ICC only lets them keep Rubi-Ka because they're not doing anything against ICC laws." "True..." "And after yo-" he had almost gone to far. "After the incident, why wouldnt you want to get back at them?" "It wasnt an incident!!!!!" He shouted has he pounded his fists on the table. "They did it on purpose, and all the rest let them do it. They killed her, and all the people on that ship, when they were delivering some food to Tir!!!!" "Listen, I know how you felt, she was my friend too, but you have to get over it, Tax! Its been three years. I know you hate Omni-Tek more than ever, and so do I, but this little grudge cant go on forever!" "You're right." "Thank you, you see-" "It will end when Omni-Tek has been completly annihlated." Taxtor then sped to the Tech Lab, eager to send his reply to Radiman.

TO: COUNCIL OF TRUTH-HENRY RADIMAN
FROM: CORPORAL NED "TAXTOR" WASHKO
SUBJECT: RE: RECRUITING

Mr. Radiman, I would be honored to serve your cause. Unfortunately, scince im all the way on the Mars Colony, it may take several months to arrive. I will be departing later today on a transport, so I wont be in contact until I arrive on the planet. I hope to rid Omni-Tek from the, or rather, our planet, for the freedom of the people of Rubi-Ka.

END MESSAGE


******************3 months later**********************

Taxtor flew the ship down to the surface, carefully managing to pinpoint Tir on his tracking system. He landed on a docking pad, and walked to the Council of Truth building, hoping to see Henry Radiman in person. He approached a guard near the entrance, and asked to see Radiman. "Sir, dont you know? The Council was disbanded weeks ago." Taxtors heart stopped. Disbanded? He thought to himself. How? When? or better yet, Why? He walked towards the grid access point, and made the jump into the Grid.
