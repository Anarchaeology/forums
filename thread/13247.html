We could call our organisation Freedom Federation ( Omni Side ) and
Freedom Alliance ( Clan Side )
( and not "Peace Federation/Peace Alliance" )
because we are not against PvP in general.
Just there could not be 100% fair "PvP sport" in persistent universe,
and even if it would be possible,
players should have right to enjoy the game without to be involved in PvP,
since PvP is just one of many aspects of AO and in our opinion should be optional.

2 ways to join FF/FA : as Member and as Fellow;
(1) join the FF/FA in game + in yahoo ( members )
or (2) just yahoo group + give a tell to any advisor of FF/FA,
that you ask for fellowship and tell your yahoo username.
Fellowship or membership: it would help a lot,
if you mention your nick in forums, but it's not needed.
Characters lower then lvl 10 will not be accepted,
exept they belong to trusted FF/FA members
( there is no need to go in PvP areas for low level players anyway )

Every fellow should not go in PvP areas as long he/she did not checked the
Fellows-List longer then 7 days ( and preferably printed this list )
This way the safety is granted to fellows at least 7 days after thier signup.
Every member need to check the fellowship list ( not older then 7 days )
before attacking everyone.
If you see the player is on the list -
you may not attack him unless he accept the fight.
If you attacked a member/fellow by mistake, and found it during the battle -
explain it, try to leave the weapon range, ask for excuse.
( violation if this rule will lead to rejection,
exept the member/fellow have a good excuse, like computer crash )

Sure even after less then 7 days a fellow will be not attacked in most cases,
since many members/fellows will check the fellows list.

Fellows/Members should not insult each other
( if they do ---> reason to be rejected )
Critics on someone, who dont accept the fight may count as insult
( something like "too scary to fight?" )

Why join ?

- To show your protest about pushing players even more into PvP ( these who dont want to )
- As a way not to fight these, who respect other ppl.'s right not to engage the PvP

Membership or Felowship in FF/FA does not means the player never PvP.
Even between FF/FA members fights are still possible,
but nobody ( who respect other ppl.'s rights ) should be forced to PvP.
Every time a Member/Fellow want to PvP other member(s) he have to ask player/players
who he want to fight.
If he is sure other(s) want, he dont need to ask
( and stop, if he see, he was wrong, when the attacked player(s) say so,
so better to ask anyway )

Non Members/Fellows are "free" for PvP.
That dont mean we encourage ganking peaceful ppl., but gankers deserve it to be ganked,
it would be diffucult or even unpossible to prove does the player respect
other's freedom or not, that's what FF/FA founded for

Be aware, we may be targeted by gankers before any other players in game,
just because of our attitude.
On the other side, if we have fight, why not to fight at least on the right side ?
Lets hope FF/FA becomes strong, so we can deal with any gankers !
