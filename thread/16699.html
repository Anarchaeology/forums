Whoopee. 9% more experience per kill still won't change the fact that high-level characters need 48 octillion experience to level. Again, I am merely playing DA here (fitting for a 'crat): Isn't that more a drop in the bucket compared to the (good) points about DDs, charms, and pets?

quote:
A nanomage crat can selfcast Rule of One at early 190s (keeping max MC/TS implants) while a solitus needs late 190s.
According to Antiguardians, Rule of One has a 6 second cast, 3 second recharge time. That's 2x the cast time of most of the other nukes, which have around 3 seconds cast/recharge time, and roughly 50% (3 seconds) longer overall. Let's say I use Erratic Laser (probably not even the best DD you can use), 3/3 (roughly), and Mr. Nanomage uses Rule of One:

I cast for ~800 (for the sake of argument) damage each time I cast. He casts for ~2200. That seems like a lot of difference, but again, I'm using a weaker DD than you probably would. But let's see what happens over time:

3 sec: I hit (800), begin recharge. He's still casting.
6 sec: He hits (2200), begins recharge. I begin casting again.
9 sec: I hit (1600). He begins casting again.
12 sec: I finish recharging and cast again, he's still casting.
15 sec: He hits (4400), I hit (2400), we both start recharging.
18 sec: We both finish recharging and cast again.
21 sec: I hit (3200), he's still casting.
24 sec: He hits (6600), I finish recharging and cast again.
27 sec: I hit (4000), he finishes recharging.
30 sec: I finish recharging, he's still casting.

Note that while my damage over time is still about half that (and again, with a weaker nuke than I suspect ANY crat at that level will be using), it's not at all bad. AND, this doesn't factor in shooting/punching/etc., which can be done during recharging, but can't be done during the attack-time window. So the damage is probably far more homogenized, when you factor in weapon inits, nano-casting init, and aggression.

quote:
A nanomage can possibly access 1 step higher charm than a solitus after lvl 130+.
Probably, and I suspect this is the biggest advantage the nano breed will have. I don't know how pychic checks at that level work out though, so the charm may or may not be useful after all. Anyone sure?

quote:
Unlike NTs, nanomage has very huge advantage over solitus as crat. A high level nanomage crat is argubly the highest damage dealer (3 attack pets + gun + nuke) in game and still have very high survivability with good crowd control skill.
I'm not sure I'd say it's a 'huge advantage,' but it is there. As for survivability... well... consider that solitus means relatively evenly-distributed stats, meaning a solitus 'crat can use higher-level OP Elite or Flowers Tech, mixed in with some nano, to get more viable ACs, they can more easily raise agility and strength to wear a Decus coat, and in terms of MA, punch better.

The differences may be more than we suspect. One hit from a mob may not sound like much HP, but you'll wish you could have taken that one extra hit if that's what kills you...
