How about an in-game webbrowser?
I don't know about the rest of you, but oftentimes if I alt-tab out, I cannot alt-tab back in without freezeing my computer on a (usually) black screen. However, I often need to alt-tab out to get information or whatnot for either myself or someone else. For example, the other day, I told one of the people I play with that she looked like a character on a cartoon show (in the outfit she was wearing), and she hadn't seen that cartoon show, so I alt-tabbed out, found it's website, and got a URL of a pic that showed the char I was talking about. Unfortunatly, I froze, and had to reboot. I got back in and gave her the URL, but still. Annoying.

So what I wanna know is if we could do something like specify an exe file that is the webbrowser of choice. Then we could pull up the browser and do whatever.

It would also help those people who are waiting for debuffs to wear off. I imagine alot of people wait for things like exhaust muscles to run out open a browser and check mail, or post on these boards, or whatever.


Long story short, please let us run webbrowsers in-game; it would be uber.
