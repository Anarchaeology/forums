When fighting humanoid mobs:

MA, Enforcer, Meta
Defense check is Evade Close Combat

Soldier, Agent, Crat, NT, Engineer, Advent, Fixer, Doctor
Defense check is Dodge Ranged

Trader
Defense check is Duck Explosion



Unless you PvP, you can afford to let your Duck skill fall behind.
Professions that 'tank' should always keep their evades high.



The use for evades (duck/dodge/evade) is as follows:

The mobs attack rating is checked against your defense rating in their specific attack type.

The higher your defense, the less likely you will get hit and/or crit.


These checks run independant of Armor Class.

Raise all 3 evades. If you absolutely must let one fall behind, it can be duck, but watch out for Trader mobs.
