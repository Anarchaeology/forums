Yalm vs. Kodiac
When finally I got my Yalm and before I did an IP reset and could still equip my Kodiac, I did a speed comparison that may be of interest.

Yalm: Ql31, just over 5M, Vehicle Air 85
(Run speed spec. appears to be meaningless.)

Kodiac: Ql57, $74547, Vehicle Ground 244 (needed 27000 IP, blue skill).
+286 run speed.

I had run speed with Kodiac+Buffs+Implants of 760. (I'm MA, run speed skill green.)

I started at the Military Academy and headed north in my Yalm for 2 minutes. I stopped and switched into my Kodiac. It took 3 minutes 45 seconds to get back to where I started. The Yalm was 188% faster.

I can see where proffesions with expensive run speed skill and/or Vehicle Ground would have problems. With my Kodiac, I was running missions from Avalon to Mort and Perpetual Wastelands, easily out running the Mobs. Travel time was not to excessive and I would get two or three missions at time out of Avalon with good cash reward. I made the missions easy enough that chance of dying was minimal and I could rip through them fast and make cash at a good rate. Still, at the end, I was getting seriously weak (above min damage) from not having upgraded armor for several levels. I started saving for the Yalm at about level 74 and finally bought it at level 82.

So I bump for lower skill requirement on the Kodiac, as an intermediate vehicle. I see very few people in Kodiacs. It ought to be more common. Seriously, this is the 30th century and people are running all over the place? Give us break.

But leave the Yalm alone. It is something to work for and be proud of owning. It opens up a whole new p.o.v. on the landscape. There should be more items like Yalms for the higher levels. Milestones to work hard for and be proud of accomplishing. (And also to sink credits ought of the economy.)

(Of course, I'm biased, I already got my Yalm. Bwahahaha.)
