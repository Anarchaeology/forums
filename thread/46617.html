Super-Pol: Dat's No Ordinary Pol!
Marco: Hey Pete! What's up man I got you one of those Air Cakes you like!

StinkiPete: Aww man! I hate those things! I hate Air cake, you can't even taste it. What's the point of a cake if you cannot taste it?

Marco: Well I thought...

StinkiPete: Happy Cake! I told you I like Happy Cakes!

Marco: So... you don't want it?

StinkiPete: No, it's yours...

Marco: Great! *gets an evil grin*

*Yalm speeds by at a very fast pace*

StinkiPete: Wait a second!?

Marco: Yo Stink! We gonna get that guy or what?

StinkiPete: What did you call me?

Marco: I called ya Stink. Now are we gonna get that guy or what man he's speeding!

StinkiPete: Ok... First off, I told you never to call me Stink... EVER!

Marco: He's getting away...

StinkiPete: What? Wait! I wasn't finished! Second I KNOW you knew I wanted a happy cake, you bought that stupid Air Cake just cause you knew I hate them and you knew you'd have more to eat. You KNEW I wouldn't eat it.

Marco: Woah! Looks like someone's grumpy. Someone needs their Happy Cake!

StinkiPete: See! You did know! I knew it! It's go time... A$$!

Marco: Woah! Your freakin' out over a happy cake!? I'm getting transfered to Omni-Pol... this outfit is whack!

*another yalm speeds by*

StinkiPete: You moron... you can't join Omni-Pol. If you could join Omni-Pol you wouldn't be here you be there, and besides remember that thing?

Marco: What thing?

StinkiPete: Well... your... a leet.

Marco: What!? No way man!

StinkiPete: Hey stop dening it. That adventurer you tried to "put the moves on".

Marco: Yeah she was cute!

StinkiPete: Yeah well she also turned you into a leet. I told you not to worry about you insurance, but no you saved and the system got hacked and look what happened.

*a third yalm speeds by*

Marco: Yeah but I am still with her!

StinkiPete: Ugh! I need a new partner. Your her pet now! That's not a relationship! Your fed from a freakin' bowl!

Marco: So? Doesn't everyone eat from bowls?

StinkiPete: Yeah and plates, but when I eat dinner I do not get a seperate bowl of water right along side my bowl of leet food.

Marco: Aww... man... I never thought of it like that. No wonder why she takes me for a walk on a leash. All this time I thought it was just something kinky!

*fixer runs by faster then the three previous yalms*

StinkiPete: Oh yeah! We got one Marco!

Marco: Pete... How we gonna catch anyone? We're on foot.

StinkiPete: No, I am on foot... your on paw. *chuckles*

Marco: Shut it Pete!

*they both continue standing along side the road, as they watch the sign blink people's speed as they drive and run by*

Marco: Pete?

StinkiPete: Yes Marco?

Marco: How are we ever going to catch anyone without a car, or even a radar gun?

StinkiPete: I told you Marco. Omni Tek cut our funding, completely. We can't afford a Yalm, and they gave our radar guns to that new nanoball league opening up.

And thus ends another typical day on Super-Pol patrol!
