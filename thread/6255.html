Titles like "Freshman," "Rookie,," etc. etc. are earned by sheer kills. It doesn't really matter whose side the victm was on. So it shouldn't matter if you switch sides.

As for the token board, Omni & Clans don't honor the actions of a trator. When you sign the application, your record is wiped clean by terms of the contract. Of cource that means you lose your token count. Omni isn't going to honor merits you earned from the Clans in actions against Omni, and vice versa. But it could be a lot worse.
