At the Foresight Office
Enerbias looked at the maintenance man with a raised eyebrow, frustrated at the amount of time it was taking, and couldnt help wondering if it was wise to have someone on the outside installing their systems. The man had come highly recommended however, and he trusted Multimas judgement in such issues.
As if detecting his discomfort, there was a polite cough behind him. He turned in the chair and saw Multima standing before him with a pot of tea poised above his cup. She smiled.

You looked as if you were in need of refreshment Mr McKesson.

He smiled back as best he could, and not for the first time wondered how she read him so well.

Aye.

Multima frowned as she poured the hot beverage into his cup, concerned at the distant expression on the face of her boss. Raising the teapot to her chest, she tilted her head and looked at him quizzically.

My, that is a long face you have there. I would have thought youd be more joyful with our first case officially solved?

He pondered, opened his mouth as if to speak and was rudely interrupted by the engineer who crawled back out from under his desk.

Thats it Mr McKesson, shes up n running now.

He swung round in his chair to face the screen and bashed at the keyboard. It flickered to life, but, much to the annoyance of the Engineer, voiced no gratitude. Sensing the tension in the air, Multima escorted the young man from the room, returning a short while later.

I paid the manits good is it not that visitors to the office now have access to our updated records?

Enerbias leant back in the chair, causing the leather to creak softly. He closed his eyes and pinched the top of his nose as if in deep thought, then slowly span the chair round to face her. Drawing his hand away from his face, he sighed heavily and opened his eyes.

Aye, it is good.

and the new contact weve established too. One cant deny that it looks as if the company will be going places. All your hard work has paid off.

Enerbias sat silently for a moment before speaking.

Any news on my wife?

Multima blushed slightly, afraid of his reaction to her response.

No.although I have heard she is attending a meeting.

He rose from the chair, stern faced, and it occurred to Multima that he looked somewhat different to how he had been when they had first met long ago under similar circumstances. Back then he was very flustered, upset even, but now he had an air of purpose to him. He was stronger somehow, far more composed, but this gave him a frightening edge.

Fetch me my coat, I have business to attend.

The meeting
Enerbias approached the doorway to The Cup with trepidation. His day had not exactly gone according to plan, and he found his mind wandering onto things other than what he had come here for. He paused at the doorway, wondering if he could handle the atmosphere inside, yet knew deep down that this was an ideal place to meet. The social gathering within would provide the perfect cover for his meeting, particularly as he himself was scheduled for several readings, but the lump in his throat caused him to wonder if he would be up for the task. In truth, he had almost cancelled his attendance, but Multima had convinced him it would do him good to be in the company of a jovial crowd. He had his doubts.

He opened the door and stepped inside.

A drink was his first priority, to soothe the swelling in his throat, and although provided with Coffee by one of the hosts, he quickly found the taste far from satisfying, and topped it up with something stronger.

Scratchnsniff made him feel welcome, for which he was grateful. He relaxed a little and took up a seat. Before long several others arrived whom he recognised, including the chirpy Katpaws, who, even in his current frame of mind, managed to put a smile upon his face. They exchanged pleasantries, and before long Scratchnsniff stepped up to the stage and announced that the nights entertainment was to begin.

He felt a mixture of feelings wash over him as the various poets stepped up to recite their prose. At times he found the words soothing, wishing his wife was there to experience the event with him, but sometimes his mind would slip, and the world around him grew fuzzy. He almost seemed surprised when his name was called, but quickly came to his senses and stepped up to the stage.

A combination of the drink and applause boosted his spirits somewhat, and with the arrival of Boglwe, who seemed truly moved by one of his renditions, he soon managed to focus on why he was really there, and spotted a face within the crowd. A young Fixer sat some way off , acknowledging his presence with a simple nod.

Glad that the crowds focus fell upon the next reader, Enerbias tilted his head in the direction of the doorway, and headed outside to establish contact, briefly pausing as he bumped into two old friends entering the premises. He greeted the woman with great enthusiasm, having not seen her for so long.

Cyani! You missed my latest offering!

She giggled, as was her way, and apologised, stating that she had been held up by overflow in the wompah buffers. He smiled and made his excuses, disapointed to have to leave her, yet eager to deal with the business at hand. He faltered on her parting words.

Give my love to Sharonne!

Seeing his contact waiting patiently, he managed to force one foot in front of the other, grateful that business would allow him not to ponder too long on what Cyani had said.

The discussion was long, and soon had Enerbias in the right frame of mind. The contact had two things going against him, both of which warranted caution, but he seemed genuine enough. They discussed terms, and it soon became clear that an association would be of benefit to the both of them. The conditions were soon settled, and an exchange took place, at which point, a shot rang out.

The man tumbled into Enerbias lap, blood seeping through his hands as he struggled against the dead weight. Horrified, he pushed the body aside and looked up. There was no one present. Leaping to his feet he considered the grid, then thought better of it, choosing instead to run back into the bar where the crowd would afford him some protection should he be next. It was doubtful that the killer would strike in public. He could only hope that his contact was fully insured, otherwise, he was in a whole world of trouble.

His comlink bleeped. Striding behind one of the pillars for privacy, he answered the call, and was relieved to hear his contact come through.

What the hell! If this was a setup!.

I assure you it was not, I was about to make the same statement, were you followed?

The voice became somewhat calmer, pain evident in the tone.

I dont think so, but its difficult to say in such a crowded place.

Did you get everything?

Yes, although it would appear someone out there didnt want me to. Weve learned a valuable lesson Mr McKesson. We shall have to be more cautious in future.

Enerbias agreed, and promised to look into the matter. It was not good for business to have his contacts become targets, particularly when such contacts were newly established. With a troubled brow, he tucked the comlink back into his pocket, and proceeded to weave his way back into the grid. He needed to get back to the office quickly and deposit the newly acquired funds, and start looking in to who else may have known his whereabouts that night. One name sprung to mind instantly, but he cast it aside just as quickly. It was not a thought he wished or even intended to entertain. The implications would be too devastating.

Not nearly as devastating as the sight that greeted him when he opened the office doors however...the place had been ransacked.

Unpleasant Discovery
Several days had passed, and for the most part, they had done a good job of tidying the mess that had been left behind. It appeared that nothing had been taken. It was more a case of damage done. Electrical components had lain all over the floor, crunching underfoot when Enerbias originally entered his office several days previously. Luckily insurance had covered most of the damage, and the money from their first case was more than adequate to cover the expense of a new security system.

It was late evening, and Enerbias was disturbed by the latest job that had come his way. He had explained to the patron that he was currently understaffed and could make no promises, yet felt there was an uncomfortable obligation that needed to be met, for the fellow had done him a favour in the past, making him aware of the prices on his head. This was a little tricky however. His regular contacts were not in the best position to obtain the required information. It required his personal attention, and alas, the subject matter was a territory more familiar to his wife. He bit his lip at the thought and poured himself another drink before turning to the screen, and began scouring the networks for any information that might give him a clue to the location of the missing person currently laying in the file on his desk. He logged onto the most obvious place first, and started searching for names listed in his file in the hope it would provide leads to the information he needed. A long list of text flickered upward across the screen, and suddenly, a name caught his eye. It wasn't a name he was actively looking for, and from his perspective, it shouldn't have been there. He stabbed at the keyboard with a finger to stop the text scrolling then moved back up the screen a line at a time, and there it was, bold as brass. He felt his chest tighten and a knot form in his stomach. His eyes flicked across to a date displayed on the screen, and he turned to study the date shown on the calender on his desk.

"The day before!"

It came out as a whisper. His gaze returned to the screen, glazing over as his jaw began to clench. He studied it, as if willing it to vanish before him, but it remained etched there within the pixels, mocking him.

Reality hit him hard. He felt betrayed. He had always known there was a fascination, but this? It was simply too much to comprehend. There was simply no logical explanation other than a shift in loyalties, and suddenly it all seemed to make perfect sense why he had been shunned and so humiliated in their company. She knew she was betraying his trust in her, and was dealing with it the only way she knew how.

He picked up the terminal and started to scream, yanking leads from the wall and sending sparks flying into the air as he lifted the terminal above his head. He smashed it to the ground forcefully, sending shards of splintered plastic spinning through the air as the ripped powerlines cackled.

He stopped and stood mesmerised by what he had done, his chest thumping rapidly as the adrenalin coursed through his veins. He felt as if his whole body had been pumped with Notum, and he was about to explode.

Acquisition
It didn't look good, and Multima knew it. More than a week had gone by, and she was beginning to wonder if she would ever see her boss again. It didn't seem right that the business had hardly had time to take off before everything had gone so horribly wrong. First the break in, and now this. She held the cup of tea before her as if in prayer, staring into the vapour in search of an answer, but none came. With the rise in insurance premiums and the cost of new security equipment, the company was pretty dry with regard funds. What she needed was a miracle.

A light flashed on the tabletop where her comlink lay, immediately snapping her out of her trance. She rapidly placed the cup down with a clunk and picked up the comlink, her voice sharp and distinct. "Yes"

A female voice came through at the other end. It didn't sound as controlled as Multima, and yet, oddly enough, it was the best agent Foresight had in the field. Multima raised the comlink to her ear and listened intently, reaching for her cup and leaning back in the chair as she did so. It sounded like this might end up a long conversation. She did her best to calm the voice on the other end down.

"Hey hey hey! Slow down! He's okay you say?"

A long pause, and a frown cutting into Multimas' forehead. She lowered the cup to the table, no longer in the mood for It, and continued to listen. The faint babble of the agent could be heard but was not discernible within the room, particularly now the hiss of the office door announced a new arrival. Multima looked up, offered a half smile and beckoned with her hand for the visitor to be seated. He was a handsome man, well groomed, and certainly full of charm of that there could be no denial. He brushed his free hand over his knees as if wiping dust from them as he sat, then pulled a large case he held onto his lap. He smiled at Multima and began drumming his fingers on the surface of the case, ceasing abruptly when he realised it seemed to be the source of irritation to Multima by the expression on her face. She looked at him, a little bemused by the case on his lap, but continued to deal with the call at hand.

"I see...hmmm...and you don't think..."

The query trailed off, and Multima pulled the comlink away from her ear in front of herself to study it. She sighed as her visitor spoke.

"Cut off?"

"Yes...now what on earth have you got there Karadain?"

She didn't seem concerned about losing her communication. In this business it was a common occurrence. One never knew when circumstance would make it difficult for a field agent to talk, and right now Karadain had her intrigued.

"You did say they wanted three million didn't you?"

Multima looked stunned. She stared at the case almost willing him to open it, but feared her hope would be ruined if he did. Her voice trembled slightly as she spoke.

"You...you have it?"

"Aye I have it, but it has to be as we agreed. This is purely business. I need to see some return at the end of the day, and I believe getting him back will guarantee that return."

He opened the case slowly, revealing what looked like a rather large sum of money in used notes, and smiled in his irritatingly charming way at Multima. She avoided his gaze, staring instead at the money, and battling with her conscience over what she was about to do, but what choice did she have? With Sharonne having been missing too these past few days, it was impossible to seek advice, and without the safe return of Enerbias, it was doubtful Foresight would survive much longer.

"Fifty one percent you say? I doubt Ener would be very happy with that."

"Happier than he would be if dead surely?"

Multima looked up and caught Karadains gaze directly. She held it there, challenging his smugness with her stare. She did not like the fellow since employing him, but it seemed as if this was her only option. As a director of the board, she could pretty much do what she wanted with the company shares, and Karadain was offering the ransom money needed for the return of her boss in exchange for control of the company.

"Tell me Karadain. What exactly drives you?"

Again Karadain smiled, reeking of confidence as he closed the case. "As I told you before Multima, I am a business man, and have fallen on...hard times shall we say. This money was by no means easy to accumulate. I look upon it as an investment to get my funds back. We both know Enerbias is rather skilled at what he does. With me on board, there is opportunity to acquire more contacts, and with Enerbias back, I can see great potential for both this company and my pocket."

"But like I said, I do not imagine him being pleased with the arrangement. He has put a lot of work into this company. What if he chooses not to work for you?"

Karadain sighed, but the smile remained. "Like I said before, he knows this business far better than I. I will be his boss only on paper. In truth, I have no intention of telling him how to run his business. That would be rather silly of me don't you think? Why put my own profit potential at risk?"

Multima glanced at the case again and bit her lip. The call she had taken earlier had confirmed that the kidnappers were willing to trade, and that the health of Enerbias was at risk if they stalled too much longer. Reluctantly, she agreed to the deal.

Karadain tried to grab hold of the electrode in his arm again, but the Droid grabbed him by the wrist as it continued to speak in its monotone voice, devoid of any feeling, enhancing the fear within the Bureaucrat.

"Deleting safety protocols..."

"What the!...Let me go! You're hurting me!"

The Droid simply squeezed harder and drew blood. It continued to speak in the same emotionless tone, but the wording was clearly alien to it.

"Hello Karadain, guess what? I hacked into your Droid"

Karadain looked terrified as it continued to drone on.

"Haha...you look quite shocked Karadain...here, allow me to give you another shock..."

He screamed as more energy surged into him via the coupling relay embedded in his arm. Smoke began to rise from his hair as a buzzing sound continued to draw a crowd, although much to Karadains' horror, no one appeared willing to offer assistance. He fell to his knees and whimpered, pleading for the Droid to stop, but it clearly hadn't finished with him yet.

"I took the liberty of programming this Droid to teach you a lesson Karadain. How well did you program it to brawl I wonder?"

The Droid punched the prone Bureaucrat, sending him sprawling to the floor. It followed with a kick to his ribs, causing him to yelp with pain.

"What's the matter Karadain? Don't you like the sampling of the beating I went through while you set about acquiring my company?"

"Please! Is that what you want? I'll give you your company back, just stop this Droid from..."

Another punch broke his sentence.

"Don't worry Karadain, it will all be over soon...I've programmed your Droid to detonate in your presence..."

Karadain tugged at the wired electrode from his arm and scrambled to his feet, panic evident in his face as the automation continued to mock him with the Fixers' words.

"Haha...you can run if you like, I'm sure the Droid can keep up with you."

The Bureaucrat decided to do exactly that, although his knees screamed with the pain of the electrocution as he stumbled forward. His pleas for help seemed to fall on deaf ears, and his face soon filled with despair as he noticed someone laughing at him from the crowd who had gathered. The Droid tripped him, and began to rain blows upon him once more as he screamed for help.

"Please somebody help me! My Droid is out of control!"

"This Droid is perfectly IN control...it does nothing I haven't programmed it to do Karadain."

It punched him in the face, breaking his nose and sending a spray of blood down his jacket. Again he screamed for help, but again his plea went unheeded. It seemed the crowd were mocking him, amused by his predicament and grateful for the entertainment.

"HELP ME SOMEONE!!!"

"Come back here. I haven't finished with you yet."

"HELP! My droid is trying to kill me!"

"Ignore him everyone, he is just a common crook getting what he deserves..."

A male Solitus Soldier by the name of Slicse tried to trip the Droid while an Opifex male Fixer called Arieste laughed at the scene, no doubt amused at the handiwork of a fellow Fixer evident in the behaviour of the renegrade Droid as it hopped over Slicses' foot and kicked Karadain. The two onlookers quickly decided that help was futile, and watched amused as the Droid pulled Karadain to his feet and punched him in the gut. It was becoming to much for the Bureaucrat to bear, and he pleaded miserably once more.

"Please! Have mercy on me Enerbias! I won't bother you anymore!"

Arieste turned to his companion and spoke, his statement causing Karadain much distress as it became evident that no one cared about his predicament.

"Hey Slicse, do you have the key to the meeting place? I erased mine again."

Slicse simply nodded as the Droid kicked Karadain in the head. He was bleeding heavily, and had difficulty standing. The Droid continued to speak in its' emotionless tone.

"Now for the best bit Karadain...this Droid is about to detonate..."

"Please somebody help me! My droid is out of control!"

"This droid is perfectly IN control...it does nothing I haven't programmed it to do. Detonation will occur in 5 seconds..."

It threw another punch to the Bureaucrats face as the crowd began to back away, clearly having no intention of being caught within a potential blast.

"HELP ME SOMEONE!!!"

"5...4...3...2...1..."

"NOOOOOOOOOO!!"

"Detonate."

There was a small explosion, but the proximaty showered Karadain in shards of flaming metal. He fell to the floor lifeless.

So ends chapter 1 of the Foresight story.
