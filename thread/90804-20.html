outside of the loot that drops from hecklers, I don't think anyone knows of any other way of making quick fast cash in SL. There are probably some things that you might loot that might fetch a nice price on the market, but you should check the markets to see what's hot and what's not.

As for Clicksaver, please be aware that CLICKSAVER does not cost you anything. Clicksaver does not take your credits away. CS is a third-party tool. Go do a search on the forums for Clicksaver to understand what it is. The mission booth set up by Funcom ingame is what is taking your money. That money for 1 roll will equal your current level. At level 28, one roll will equal 28 credits. When you are level 200, guess how much 1 roll of the mission booth will cost? yup, 200 credits PER roll.

Now, if you had done some research on CS, you will see that CS allows you to scan through several rolls with ONE click of the mouse. You could do manually what CS does, but it would take hundreds or even thousands or hundreds of thousands of clicks. That is why Clicksaver is aptly named CLICKsaver. It doesn't and cannot save you credits.

You can change the number of rolls CS does in search of the items on your CS list. From 5 to 5000, or whatever. I don't know what the highest number it allows for.

Sorry if you knew all this already, just felt the need to clear up any misconceptions of CS that might still (and continue to be) out there. Funcom does nothing with, or to, or for, CS.

There is another, often overlooked way of slowly but surely adding to your bank account. And it can be done from almost backyard level. Keep collecting monster parts. Wen you have a god stash, invest a little IP in Pharmaceuticals, or get some buffs (General pharmaceutical buff adds 20 to your skill and if you find a nice trader who buffs you with Apprentice or Journeyman Pharmaceuticals, you'll get a lot more) and make plasma out of the mosnter parts.

You need 250% more skill then the monster parts QL, but that's not hard to achieve for the QL of the parts u can collect, at least up to level 70-80.

At level 50-70 I collect monster parts for a week, then turn them into plasma. That makes me between 1-2 million a week Not a bad business practice and a good investment of some IP and a lot of buff begging. Of course if you're in an org, you will find the buffs easily or have a friendly doc do the crafting for you.
At level 67, my MA can process monster parts up to QL 80 without even needing external buffing and i only spent 2k IP on pharma.
