Additional Pet commands that could help.
Having a secondary character that is an engineer, I have noticed a few commands I really wish I had. Thought I would list some of them here for comments

/north
/east
/west
/south

--Pet moves a few steps in that direction or until he loses site of his owner.

--add "move" in front for consistant movement in that direction. ex. /move north would send the bot north until he gets about 100 meters from the owner where he would /wait

/protect

--bot follows and guards target. If he "gets stuck" he will warp behind the target.

/come or /warp

--no brainer. bot clips obstacles and appears behind the owner.

Anyone have any others that should be added?

PS - I would also like to add my vote to have all commands given the range of 1000 meters. WTF don't we have radios or something?
