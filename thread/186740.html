You have to cast the nano Fury. If I recall correctly, it's available from the Keeper of IPS in Jobe Market or Harbor. If not, it's available as a mob drop from factioned mobs like the majority of the keeper nanos. It's a 4 hour nano and you'll have access to the special attack for the entire duration of the nano.


Due to a bug, the Righteous/Unhallowed Fury attack does not show up in the new GUI. You'll have to switch to the old GUI, open up the actions panel (control-2), and find the Fury icon. It'll look like a katana with no grey background. Click on it, then drag it to your hotbar. Once you've done this you can switch back to the new GUI.

To access the old GUI, hit F10, then click Misc, and uncheck 'use new control panel'. You'll have to relog. To re-enable to new GUI, do the same, only put a checkmark next to 'use new control panel'. Again, you'll have to relog in order to do this.
