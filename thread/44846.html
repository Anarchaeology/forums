Ancarim Iron Legion invites you to Rubi-Ka Run II. Win and claim your price.
Ancarim Iron Legion invites you all to Rubi-Ka Run II !
The run will be held Saturday 17th of August and starts 12.00 GMT outside Tir - West gate.
The first who completes the route can choose between two prizes.

You can win:

A complete QL 200 basic implant set, and you will also get all the help you need to combine them with clusters and all buffs that you need to get them installed.

Or 5.2 million Rubi-Ka credits ( Enough to buy a QL 40ish Yalmaha )

Rules in this race:

At each post you will get riddle. Solve this and you will get the answer where the next post is. You will also get a keyword at each post. Find the commmon denominator for each keyword. You can claim your prize if you are the first person into goal and give us the right common denominator.

No vehicles, no grid, and no other warping / beaconing buffs are allowed.

Wompas are allowed.

Run buffs are allowed.

Best wishes,
The Ancarim Iron Legion,
Event Coordinators,
Cybcer and Lillemjau.
