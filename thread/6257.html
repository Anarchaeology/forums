IP reset and overequipping
well..i might be quite new in AO compared to all those lv100plus characters hanging around...but...still...i got a few things to say(considering i m not so new to rpg s)

for the IP reset ..please....it s just needed....many players need that to save their characters from digital oblivion......i got a lv38 trader with really messed up skills.....it s all my fault maybe.....maybe not.......AO deserves the "massive" part in MMORPG...so...sometimes it s just too big for a new player and you can easily make mistakes.......it s very logical to limit the IP reset to once for every character...but it should be available...

i talked to an ARK about it and he told me about overequipping etc that might occur after the reset....sure a lv200 guy can equip items he won't be able to after the reset...and then get that reset and keep em all equipped....

but the solution s easy....and it should be already in the game....it s strange that it s not in the game....

take this ex:
i cant equip a pair of gloves cause i need str of 50....my str is only 38 ....so icast a str boost,buff my str for 12 and equip the gloves...ok so far....then i cancel the buff...or it times out....the gloves should be automatically unequipped or i need to get penalties thta will force me to unequip....

many players wouldnt like that...but.....even if such openings allow us to get stronger...it s just POWERPLAY and this is not a mmoPpg.....

this is only a simple example...(and someone has to do something about all those people running around in cities with weapons in hand....guards should warn them or weaps should be auto unequipped when you enter a city for god s sake..........i d like to see a DAOC style "hanging from your belt" type solution but...ahhh....AO needs to have totally rewritten ranged combat rules anyway....-so the guys with guns s not the highest priority prob-...can someone please tell me how a shotgun can fire single shot and then a time based burst!?!?!?!? or how you can make an aimed shot with your sniper rifle that is also time based but comes out fast? AO is a much better game in overall compared to all those powerplay only games"eq,daoc..etc" but it certainly needs new combat rules and lots of fixes....)

sorry if someone already posted all of this things before me 100 times....i didnt have the time to read the whole forum.....

hope we see lots of changes in a good way soon........
