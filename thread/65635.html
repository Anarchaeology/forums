Angels of Light
We are building something new here on Rubi-Ka. Perhaps new to all online games. We have two primary goals in mind...

1) To create an "online family" where players can feel welcomed, supported and loved. Our members should feel that the Angels are happy when they arrive online and sad when they camp off. The leaders have committed ourselves to the idea that there is nothing more important to us than our members. We will always make ourselves available to help however is needed. Even if that help is nothing more than sitting with a member and listening.

2) We want players to enjoy all that this world has to offer. Missions, hunts, quests, etc. are part of the game and are best enjoyed with friends and people you like to spend time with. But there are also other gifts and talents to be enjoyed here as well. We are striving to help creative writers, poets, playwrights, and actors bring their gifts into the world of Anarchy Online. We believe that this balance will help our members better enjoy the world we spend time in and will enrich the lives of those we meet.

You can find out more about us at our website: Angels of Light

Or send a tell to Dooley or Boglwe when you're online. We'd be glad to speak with you about what we are building here and how you could benefit and help us.
