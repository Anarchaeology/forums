Windguard's Journal
These are the de-classified entries of my father's personal log.

=================
August 26 29461

Omni-Tek is on the move; their propaganda states they're going to "reclaim all occupied territories by force" because the clans are "disrupting commercial interests". Lisa insists on joining a peaceful protest tomorrow in Omni-1, but I think it will be useless, the Omnis once again want to destroy the clans. She wants me to join her, but I have guard duty in West Athen again, and with all that is happening I doubt Lt. Bolieu will allow it. I heard General Keppler say "I see another war as inevitable" while I was passing the High Command office in Athen. I've made a few calls and made sure my son is not alone; I fear something may happen soon.

August 27 29461

Lisa is dead. My wife is gone. OT forces fired on the people protesting, not one person was armed, and most were uninsured. My Lisa was among them. How will I raise my son alone? How can I tell him his mother is no more? God give me strength, I'm going to meet my son in an hour. I've been given orders, by nightfall everyone is on full duty status; I must leave my son and join the fight for the survival of our people. I don't know if either one of us will make it through; it has already cost me the love of my life.

Assaults took place today at a few OT military and mine facilities close to clan borders in retaliation to the murder of hundreds of innocents, I wish I had been there to pay them back for what they've done to me and my son.

August 31 29461

I'm a lucky man to be alive. Yesterday we lost thousands of men to an Omni attack; we've had heavy casualties but some idiots up in the higher ranks boasts that we won the great "Battle of Stret". We barely made it out, the General didn't look too happy, I overheard him talking to one of our Captains, he had told his superiors it was not a good idea to move so many troops in the open without cover, high command ignored his warning in their eagerness for a quick "victory" to show our people. Some victory, over half of my Platoon is still laying on the ground. They would be at a reclaim if it weren't because we don't have the finances to fund so many insurances, but they made sure every officer was insured of course. Grunts can be replaced any day. I'm going to ask my Lieutenant for a few hours of leave so I can see my son.

Saw my son again, he's got a new tattoo with a snake around his arm and he's got a rifle now. He told me everyone was armed now for self-defense, and I could not argue with that. October 9 29461

Omni factions tried to force their way into Athen today, but we got word from a clan sympathizer hours ahead. General Keppler got all Platoons on the move and ordered us to hide in the rubble of Warthorn Valley east of Athen to surprise the enemy. The Plan worked quite well, the Omnis sent a scout ahead who didn't seem so sharp as he just took a look around without searching and went back. I thought maybe he had spotted one of us and didn't have to search, but when I saw the Omnis on the move on the road I knew the scout has been overconfident. We waited for the General's signal to attack, and we waited so long I thought the enemy was about to walk into our town, they were really close to the gate. The signal went off and we started to opened fire from our strong position, with the ruins and junk as cover. Half of the Omnis fell in seconds, the other half fled in panic, the battle lasted less than 10 minutes. We got some decent loot from some of the corpses; one of them even had some expensive wine bottle on him (can't figure how a soldier can carry a bottle instead of another round of ammo). Tonight we'll celebrate our little victory. I hope they give me the first shift on patrol so I can join the officers later tonight.
