Diary bit...
The following is an excerpt(sp?) for the diary of Garishna, an advisor to the Airhawk Alliance leader Kissysuzuki.
October 14th.
After a failed attempt to preform a team mission with Moonsword, a veteran of the organization, ending with his death at the claws and mandibles of several spider like creatures, I decided to embark on a solo mission of mine I had hoped I could complete.

The mission would take me into the Perpetual Wastes, an area I had never had the misfortune of visiting to this date. A jouney that would fill my mind and heart with fear.

I took the whompa to the neutral small town known as hope and aquired insurance there, as any intelligent person would. I found this place to be full of persons scared and hardened by life on Rubi-ka, that alone should have been warning enough.
After I left the gate and the protection of the ICC Peacekeepers, I entered the vast nothing, or so I had thought only dotted but the occasional cacti oftem found in any other area of our planet.

I soon saw an Anun Tearer, I dont' know it's name for sure, but I'm sure the creature was tough. Much tougher than the ones I had encountered outside of Athen beyond the Wartorn Valley. Using the best of my sneekyness, I made a path around it and immediatly began scanning the are around me for other members of that nasty species. Fortunatly none were to be seen and I soon left the other behind.

I continued my travels east toward the area of Tranquility, not knowing what to expect next. The next thing to catch my eye was a creature I had only seen as of yet in service to some of the more powerful persons known as metaphysics, or atleast it looked similar. I cared not to venture any closer to it and immediatly set about finding a path around it's vicinity, only to be cut short by what looked like a whale from old earth laying on the sand, I guess it was some sort of worm, lying in the sun, not wanting to become a snack for it's gaping maw I set about carefully treading a path between the demon and the beast, hoping not to draw the attention of either. Thankfully I made it.

My remaining travel through this area was uneventful, and I soon entered the Mantis Hunting ground. I saw only one of the carapaced bugs skittering its way across the sands, so the name seemed less menacing than it should have. I took it to mean they were the ones being hunted and felt safer somehow.

I made it all the way to eye sight of the bastion known as tranquility I asume, my compas was pointing me in the direction of the settlement, though why anyone would live in this forsaken area I didn't know.

When I got closer that is when I knew entering this area would be a challange. The area was crawling with bugs. I knew from my previous experience that this wouldnt not be pleasent. I began to circle just outside of their senses and found a pass from the northwestly direction that looked clear, indead I had found the safe zone, an area not covered with the chittering mass of insects. I passed clearly to the wall, hoping to jump from the sand dune near by onto the top, but, couldnt, possibly due to lack of leg strength and my lack of nimbleness in full palsteel armor.
I followed the wall for about 5 feet and then heard a sound. It was a mantis and it had found me and I was on the run, away from the area instantly. I back my self up aganst a steep dune, hoping to atleast cover my back and try to fight off the beast, but it was too late, by the time I had turned around several of them were upon me... I was finished. I soon found myself outside of the Hope gate and set about writing this entry to while away the time waiting for my belongings to be returned to me. I hope that somehow it maybe of use to those who have not yet ventured into this dangerous area and serve as a warning.

Personal note, Stay Clear of the Prepetual Waste Land at all cost!!!
