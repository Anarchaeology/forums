I think this is a good thing. I don`t quite understand why everyone is complaining all the time? I have played AO for some time now, I love the game even with it`s flaws and fault. For the game it self, is an extremely playable and enjoyable game. And when Funcom is going to release lots of lots of new cool stuff, all people do is complaining. I will gladly pay for this expansion-pack, to show my support and passion for this game. If we don`t pay, we won`t get anymore AO now will we? Now extra income will also increase the amount of stuff we see on Rubi-ka. More income will make the developers realize that people love AO, and they will make more stuff for AO. It`s how the world of gaming works. You have a winning concept, and the last a developer wants, is to loose it`s affection. But to affort to continue, they got to sell, because making games is NOT free. But by showing our support by buying this exp.pack, they will see that they can affort to continue, and with increased income, they could maybe fix some of their servers...(?)

Besides I seriously doubts FC will charge the Expansion pack very much. It IS an expansion pack, built on the original game, and expansionpacks is always cheap.
Well, I can`t wait for this expansion-pack to be released....Profession specialization sounds pretty NEAT to me...
And that new town....and the new weapons...and the Shadow Realm...oh man...I can`t wait.
