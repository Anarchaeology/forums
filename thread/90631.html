Needs some limitations though, otherwise the Engineer would be able to stand in the entrance room while the bot cleared the mission. Bot died? No problem, as long as the Engie is out of the mob's aggro range. Just re-cast and try again.

What this would be useful for is for freeing stuck pets (and oh God, do we need something to free stuck pets) and for pulling. The limitations are simple: camera view, first person view and targeting are all still centred on the character, not the pet. Range limitations come off the macros while the pet is being remotely controlled, to allow you to use /pet attack if you're not standing right next to the bot, "Q" and all other hotkeys still cause the character to start combat, not the pet. In fact the only keys which control the bot are the movement keys.
