Omni-Teks child (part1)
Tamiyana.... Omniteks child (part1)



She awoke to a world of pain and hunger... not the hunger that can be satified with mere food. A deeper hunger that gnawed at the soul.



The room was bare, a few curious looking machines flashed quietly in a corner. A lone operating table the only other thing the room contained.

"Where am I?... who am I?..." she thought, as she scanned the room for an answer. A small, dis-coloured wall tile gave away the presense of a camera, or at least a window of some kind. She was being watched in any case, somehow she knew... sensed it.

Her memory was blank, void, unable to even answer the question of her own name. She looked down at her wrists and removed the electonic sensors that were attached. She was naked save for an untied medical gown.

She could hear voices in her head? no...not in her head, these were far off, through the wall.She stood and walked over to the tile and pressed her ear to it. The voices were louder now, clearer.. she listened carefully...

"I TOLD you it would work didnt I!!" said a mans voice... a soft voice but somehow not a voice you would trust.

"okay okay... but we still dont know if it will be viable. Alot can happen in the early stages. You are too quick to congratulate yourself Demitri!. Remember no4! it seemed fine until the memories seeped through.. We MUST be cautious this time, We have come too far now to let a silly mistake ruin the programme." a womans voice this time. Cold, harsh and almost sounding angry.

"yes, yes of course Dr Kenderson. I am aware of the risks we now face... but she" he paused for a second "sorry I mean it.. looks so perfect this time! LOOK at it! magnificent!" answered the man. " This time i am sure we have it right... look it knows we are here.. listening even now!"

"dont be silly Demitri!" the woman snapped "there is no possible way it can hear us in here.. the containment room is soundproof as you well know." the woman sounded unsure as she said the words.. "you know as well as i do that the process cannot enhance senses! stop over-reacting. It is merely examining its surroundings as an animal would."


Surely they werent talking abour her? an animal?... She moved slowly away from the wall and carefully moved over to the flashing machine, she made a point of pretending to listen to other parts of the wall. If they thought she should be unable to her them then this may help to convince them that indeed she was only "reacting like an animal" as they put it... No reason to allow them to know she could do something "impossible".



Suddenly a hatch slid open at the far end of the room and a bowl was thrust through.. the hand that pushed it though quickly retracted and the hatch slammed shut. The bowl appeared to contain a sickening mixture of raw meat and offal.. covered in blood. Surely this wasnt meant to be her food?... She moved over to the bowl and caught a smell of the blood, she covered her nose and backed away. "disgusting.. " she thought to herself and moved back over to the table and sat down, her face turned from the disgusting mess on the floor.

"dont worry... it will grow hungry soon enough... then we shall see the fruits of our labour" said the womans voice from behind the wall "have patience, soon we shall know"




She hugged her knees up to her chest and sat gently rocking on the table... "where am I?... Who am I?... "
