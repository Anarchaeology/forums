New Ideas : Vehicles that can be made
Seeing other threads of ideas for vehicles, I have here tried to suggest something that is within the reach of easy programming, so character movements dont have to be made or something like that.

In the game there are already several other vehicles types that are not in use and with the current way of the yalm, there is little incitement for them to ever have a use.

Thus, I would like to introduce

- Space
- Armor protection
- Yalm prohibited places (all have read about the latest accident I presume, see AO-site)
- Fuel use as in Notum
- Speed

There are several unused car and plane models already in the game. They are displayed at harrys, in trade, in ent and in missions. The come as 2-4-6-8 and even 16 seat models I believe and include sports models, carrier models and space landing modules

Furthermore
Planes should not be allowed in town
AC can be used to land people in difficult areas, like invading outposts
Fuel use is just natural and would make notum usefull for something. Notum could be sold in shops as normal items, maybe in various block sizes like 50 notum block, 200 notum block, 1000 notum block etc. It could also be that Fuelcells are recharged at some of the outposts that exists (like using a save terminal). That would be one time use, charging some money and lasting a while.

It would also be cool if they were in need of maintenance once in a while, not often, but sometimes. This could be done by an engineer or a fixer maybe

I would personally also like to see vehicles and planes to be transferable and sellable!

This would give some meaning to vehicles, be easy to do (as everything exists already).
