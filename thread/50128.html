The new and improved - OMNI ARMY!!!!!!!
This day was so strange..I don't even know where to begin...

All day i was pvp'ing in 2ho. I can't stress anymore that this was the weirdest AO playing time i've ever had since i've been in the game.

We held omni down for a very, very long time. Whompa camped, gridwatched, wished we had surface-to-air stinger's for those damn yalms marking spots in the sky, and all of a sudden while we're buffing on hill/bunker 30 omnis in green text in battlesuits come marching in shouting insane babble about omni-tek's greatness. These ARK-controlled units made me think the ARKs were playing AO in 2ho like someone would play Age of Empires, having all the power at your fingertips. I saw several ARK-controlled units that had the same name such as Tennek, who had ql 200 enforcer boss hp, full omni elite(definately modded armor, my chemical mauser wasn't doing crap to him and everyone knows chemical on Omni Elite armor is like poking a stick through horse dung), insane evades, NO reflect (and he was a soldier?), seemed to always say 'Drats!' when he got to 25% HP (As though it was scripted) and he was orange to me at 155. The next moment i zoned out of a bunker, i saw tons of people with colored bars ranging from grey to red, and names from white to green surrounding us, well **** what do you know? The omnis needed the ARKs as their escort. It died down, the ARKs eventually left, and then they came yet AGAIN for a short period of time, a well-assembled group of clanners many from Synergy Factor, Apoc, and even one from CAS retook 2ho. I heard from an MA friend on warchat that 'Jacksr and Firedragon' were in a bunker talking to ARKs. I decided to check it out.

Right when i zone in -

Jacksr: Are you sending the 300 GM and the ARKs?

Very funny, he went on about the 'ARK' situation while i was silent and he literally shot himself in the foot over and over again. I have a screenshot thats going to be sent in, and this guy will be so banned the only thing that will get him out is bail from his 'ARK connections.' He also tried a petty attempt to say Fryli was using Rapid Palsy from behind walls, yet she rarely uses it in pvp, according to her.

Seeing the oppurtunity, firedragon was there and me and Fryli on several occasions saw him run inbetween crevices of bunkers, target in third person and stack debuffs on people...No one could fire back at him unless they went around, so in effect Firedragon was exploiting and casting these debuffs through walls that guns can't shoot through. When i got the aggro, it pointed me toward him, and when i tried hitting burst - 'No line of sight..' I decided to tell the GMs about it, and firedragon with his poor english speaking skills tried to defend himself saying he was using 'concealment' and that i was a 'pvp noob whining.' I took it as a compliment since i wasn't the one exploiting to get kills.

Boy these two are in hot water. Fryli got some screenshots as well. If i were them i'd cancel my account before they get an unpleasant /tell. So many other omnis had no idea what the hell was going on. A lot even told me it was completely unfair they have ARKs there. But of course, this game is all about 'connections' huh?

PvP means Player vs Player. Not Player vs. Players And Some ARKs. I've seen omni CREAM bunches of clanners on the hill by themselves, but for some reason they need ARKs to help them do a thing now...Someone must really not like the reclaim.

This is only the icing on the cake. After Jacksr grids out of the bunker, 30 minutes later i went over to 10-3 seeing tons and tons of omni PLAYERS getting ready for an offensive. I had a feeling we would lose to this anyway..But guess who was the first man off the whompa? 'Lieutenant Tennek.' An ARK controlled player being used as the cannon fodder that took 20+ clanners attacks to kill, and by the time that one guy died omni players were in, and ARK-controlled units came in 3 by 3 until there was too many, we all had to zone, but i went linkdead having a ping of 140k. When i logged back in, it took literally 30 seconds to actually 'load up' the full screen and log my character in to start grace period, i figured fighting was going on. No, i saw over 25 omnis sitting around the bunkers, and shouting at people to 'stop attacking the GM!' if i came anywhere near the GM i wouldve gotten killed, grace or not, since grace is so bugged with lag (i think a lot of you know what i mean if you've been to camelot.), so i decided to ask 2 omnis i was on good terms with. They had no idea what the hell was going on, 1 of them agreed with me that it was stupid and GMs and ARKs should not be interfering with a pvp battle. And how about clan ARKs? we got none. Outnumbered 20 to 1, we lost because of the overflow of lag and ARK-controlled fighters who had similar and some same names being used as the meatshield for omni players.

I remember so long ago reading a post about Storm being 'connected' with GMs and ARKs. I really doubt that's true, but whether it is or not, this just makes it look like a pig wallow. I will be sending my screenshots to funcom via email and i will see to it, no matter what guild they are in, that these two folks are banned. I'm not alone in this, either.

Lastly, some omnis were complaining about clanners using the '/quit' bug which i later found out explained why people had 0 hp and were taking shots, and would log back in grace period without a kill point loss. I saw this on omni's part as well, i assumed it was lag but until i put some more thought into it and asked some other omnis/clanners, it is an exploit to stop you from losing points. I'll be keeping my eyes open for this one especially from now on.

A fair fight, is this too much to ask?

If funcom intended all this to happen and clan not be able to fight off ALL the ARKs at once, i suppose we know that our decisions on Rubi-Ka do NOT effect anything and that the victor of the 'story' is already set. Strict roleplaying ruining PvP, hard at work. A lot of people left warchat after that crap happened to go xp or log off AO for the day.

BTW grats to Jonknoxville level 68 trader who got over 10 kills today =). And to all those who helped us retake 2ho at incredible odds, i look forward to fighting along your sides again in 2ho in the future.
