Last post about tanking.

It is very important for pet class to understand both sides of tanking. The Enforcer forum will provide a lot more of informations of course.

But specially at Ace camps or other crowded / big social spawn zones, tanking with pets is not 100% smart. Getting a puller to grab a single mob and bring it to a safe fighting spot is the first step. Using aggro enhancers or dedicated nanos for an Enforcer is really nice, specially when he has the support of a Crat to take mezz / root the extra mobs pulled.

Then tanking with bot is of course very interesting for reasons that are obvious to everyone. Still you must be able to get back the aggro as an Enf as soon as it is required. Typically when you NT goes 'oups' by launching an early nuke that pisses the mob off. Then if you've been sitting behind your pet you won't get back aggro, even with a Mongo (granted that your are away from any other spawns). So being able to hit the mob during the fight at a moderate speed while the pet out damage you and keep the aggro, and being able when needed to regain aggro and tank for 2-3 hits is a fine art.

This is something widely overlooked and people usually just rely on the pet or the Enf in a given situation. But I am sure you all know this.

5th BureauCon summary and suggestions to FunCom
Thankyou to all the fellow bureaucrats that attended the 5th Bureaucrat Convention at the Reet's Retreat. We have a lively disscussion and many useful, practical improvements to our class was suggested. Here is a really short summary of the points we went over.

Psychic Checks
We all aggreed something needs to be done to help us when our old charm has worn out its usefulness and the new one is still too far off. The psychic checks are far too unforgiving.

Give us stronger psyhic debuffs. - this has some support because its by far the easiest to impliment

Have a percentage of the psychology skill auctually increase the max psychic limit. - For example, you had 100 in psychology and get 5% of that increase (or some other percentage) +5 in the psychic check.

Have our psychic checks be dynamically adjust in a team. - Larget teams would greatly increase our psychic check limits to let us charm relevant mobs, and smaller teams would mean we stay as-is. This can provide the flexibility for us to be far more useful to teams.

Speech Nanos
Most of us aggreed that sounds better in theory than it is at the moment.

Motivational speeches do not confer notable bonuses. - We have 3 lines of motivational speeches, one that increases off/def, crit increase and, nano resist. Of those 3 only the crit increase is even remotely useful and the bonuses are rather paltry. In otherwords, noone will notice or miss the speech in a team.

Demotivational speeches are a health hazard. - The demotivational speeches also have 3 lines. One that drains nano(but does not give it back.), one that decreases nano resist and, one that decreases crit rate. I can't comment on the drain nano because I have yet to see a mob run out of nano. The crit decrease could be of minor use in pvp but because its aggressive you break your 15 second grace period. For mobs, it breaks mez and aggros everything....not exactly useful if you want to stay alive.

Xp loss reduction nanos and briefcases

XP loss reduction nanos to improve team desirability greatly. - Most if not all of us have been eagerly waiting for that since it was first announced about 4-5+ months ago. It would really give teams a reason to have us crats around. Right out we don't have a genuinely unique ability to call our own. Some rather innoative ideas were introduced at the convention.

XP loss reduction nano as a speech! Yes, that would make us very much sought after no? At the least people would auctually want it now.

Briefcases??? Well, some of us were curious what happened to it, but noone is holding their breath for it. There are far more important issues with our professions that needs addressing. See also, psychic checks, envisioned xp loss reduction nanoline and the now awol paperwork tradeskill.

Paperwork tradeskill
What happened to it? Its no longer on envisioned or in-progress sections! - I mean is the new paperwork tradeskill just for us to get a bureaucrat suit and tie with? Thats it? We are the only nano intensive profession lacking any way to earn credits to support ourselves. There are weapon smithing, jewelery, nano crystal creation, pharma tech and nanoprogramming but bureaucrats aren't proficent in any of them. The paperwork tradeskill is supposed to help address this shortcoming. We need a response, anything to know if its not feesible or still being worked on.

Comments and suggestions for the 5th BureauCon goes here
