The Completion of the 200th Trial
Note: I'm certainly no RPer...but hitting level 200 and writing stories about my character has just driven me to make a little RP post about this..hehe. I also mentioned just how much Gothy seems to hate teaming..when in fact the people I was teamed with were wonderful, and I thank them for my big 200.


She never was much for group work. It was only accepted with neccesity; something that simply had to be done. It was fast, it was easy, and furthermore, the rewards were far greater.
Regardless, she was a loner. She enjoyed her solice and thrived on her solo work of assasinating each person on her Seek and Destroy list, carved and created by none other than Phillip Ross.

No one knew of course. Assasins never reveal their employer. And she was the best at what she did. She was a hacker, a smuggler, a mercenary, a body guard, a killer, a seductress, a shadow. Few knew she existed as anymore then a runabout omni-tek employee; fewer that found out ever lived to think about it. Pistolero by day, hired killer by night, or whatever the job called for. Her talents were many.

Much to her dislike, it comes to the point where as a Pistolero, she must train and gain more skill to hone her technique, aim, style, and effectiveness. She would be the best, if it killed her. Which meant sucking it up and joining forcers with others to take grunt work jobs and clear huge buildings of whatever infestation chose to make itself comfortable there. She hated every moment. But she pushed herself.

Three hours. Final floor. The mother hellhound had dropped, the reward granted. But there was one left. One that had hidden itself. The biggest, foul-smelling, rabid hellhound of them all. Smart, strong, and just plain ugly. She knew she could leave it. She knew that if she chose, her and her teammates could simply let it be. But no, she could not let it live. It was enormous, a superb challenge. She would not retreat.

Calling to her teammates to her, she drew her pistols and took aim. It would take quite a few pounds of lead to bring this dog down, and she was well prepared to fill him neck full of it. Her partners still too far to help, she fired the first shot. Instant agro.

It was a battle like none other, like no one had ever seen before. Fast as lightening she was being lunged at, and faster she dodged the blows. Several times it's yellow crusted teeth scraped her expensive black armor, and several times it did knock her down, causing her to take some damage - but she had the upper hand, as not one single shot missed it's mark. Her teammates arrived, all in awe at the spectacle before them. Moving with such speed and agility, literally making the dog's head spin, she came up behind the dog, pistol cocked and ready to fire.

"Target is terminated." The trigger was pulled, and the dog dropped to the hard wood.

Her teammates gasped as they saw her skills increase, as they saw her rise above to the elite - to the truly great. She had indeed endured the trials, and was granted her place among the best -
She had reached the 200th level.



Just wanted to thank that team for getting me 200 once again, and I hoped my little story was entertaining, although mostly untrue.
