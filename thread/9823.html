quote:

The first "neutrals" appear when Omni-Tek employees - as well as clan members - abandon their affiliations and settle in non-political zones. Fearing more ICC interventions, OT authorities turn a blind eye to these neutrals.
This was taken from the timeline on the AO website, page 11 for those that want to verify its there.

Now, since funcom has put it in writing that omni employees and clanners became neutral, I think its about time we let players have the choice to become neutral as well.

I would like to recommend that rather than the standard 24 hours online time that people are required to wait to switch between omni and clan and back, the time be extended to 1000 hours online time for joining the neutral side, this should completely negate people trying to take advantage of passing through enemy lines as neutrals.

I know for a fact that there were quite a few neutrals that went faction because funcom outright made it too hard on neutrals, now that funcom have given us superior stores, these measures aren't needed, and these people are deeply regretting their decision. Do not make these people prisoners of your faction, please, the neutrals would like to welcome our people back, and I'm sure you don't want people trapped in your faction only giving half hearted effort to your cause.
