However, I choose to play a solitus (I just don't like atrox. I've never like the huge beef-boy look. they're labeled as neuter, but they nearly all have beards. This...is very confusing. but I digress). since I'm not atrox, beams are out of the question.

A trader friend got me a few of the new 2he true katana's, and I love them. They look cool, the attack animation is kick ass, and the damage is on par with the beams. But since the only buffs we have for 2he are the very short lived challenger series, I'll never be able to compete with 1hb and 2hb users.

It makes no sense that the challenger line buffs 2he, but the headcracker line doesn't. It seems obvious the origonal design of the enforcer was the make them the best in melee weapons. Then the released the true katana's for the solitus who can't use beams. But they didn't put in an equal buff ability for 2he. It's.... puzzleing.

So let's keep this tread alive. It's an important point, and one that shouldn't require much effort or time to fix. It could be easily be thrown in with any class fix patch (dear gods of Ka, please let them fix the advents and fixers! Make them worthwhile, but don't go massively overboard with it like they did with the MP's.)
