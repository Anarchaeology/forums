PvP not for everyone
I've tried a number of games that incorporate PvP. Ultima Online back when it first launched, and before PvP existed as a term...Dark Age of Camelot with its "realm versus realm" system...and now Anarchy Online.

I've more or less reached this personal conclusion:

If I make a significant investment in my character, I have no interest in PvP.

If its a "character development" game, PvP is too frustrating for me. Even DAoC, where there is really no negative impact to being killed in RvR, I still felt unpleasant killing and being killed when I know how much time and effort goes into making a character.

The reality is this: there is *ALWAYS* someone who will have invested more time than I possibly can in creating and perfecting a killing machine character or group. Therefore, I will always "lose" at PvP at some point, most likely more often than I "win". And even if I "win", I know I do so at the cost of making someone else lose...their time, their sense of pride in their character, and so on.

For me, that isn't very fun. It isn't about skill, or intelligence: PvP almost always boils down to how much time your opponent has invested in playing the game, in levelling, in getting "uber" gear. And its always about denigrating the effort the loser has put into their character- its never enough.

Perhaps a lot of other people who play AO feel the same, and thus there is limited PvP activity. Expanding the scope or range of PvP zones will likely not encourage people who don't like the negative aspects of PvP to participate in it more. More likely, it will encourage such players to move on to another game.

I hated Ultima Online: its "wide open" PvP environment in the early days basically made me feel like I was surrounded by the worst possible excuses for human beings. That game was a woefully depressing comment on the worst aspects of human nature, and on the ultimate flaws with PvP in a game with significant character investment.

DAoC reduced the risk in PvP and made it "optional" with the RvR system...but gave me no choice once I reached 50th level but to participate in RvR. Basically, it was that, or do nothing- there was no other real content in the game.

Its too early for me to tell with AO: I've only been playing a while. But if I reach, say, 30th or 50th level, and find that everything I want to do means getting ganked by a bunch of guys with callouses on their backsides and no life outside of the game, well, I can find better things to do with my time.

Increasing the scope and range of PvP activities won't make the game more desireable for me if it means I am "required" to participate. Hopefully some way can be found for the people who enjoy PvP to have what they want without taking away what the other people who play the game want.
