The Cyborg threat is known to both Omni and Clan although it has thankfully not been an issue recently. Late last year there was quite a bit of conflict over the matter and finger pointing about who exactly was responsible for the cyborg incursion.


After some rather nasty surprise attacks on Clan and Omni territory by the cyborgs, the Clan Leaders decided to organize a wide-scale retaliatory strike against all known cyborg outposts, including their primary location in the Perpetual Wastes.

While most of the attacks were successful, the attack on the Perpetual Wastes headquarters didn't go as well as planned as resistance was extreme. However, the Clans did manage to spearhead into the center of the compound where they had spotted a hostage. The hostage turned out to be an Omni Engineer, who, against the wishes of many of us, was handed over to Omni-Tek almost immediately. Shortly after that, the Engineer "exposed" the cyborgs to be a threat created by the Clans.

My personal feeling was that an Omni Engineer being held "hostage" in the open of the cyborg capital is just a bit suspect. This, along with some questionable things said by the Engineer lead me to believe at the time that some aspects of Omni-Tek were actually in league with the cyborgs. However, no investigation was ever conducted, and the entire ordeal faded away.
