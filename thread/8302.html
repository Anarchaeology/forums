Same Thing Happened In Tir County
From Dr. April "Supergnat" Sotto:

I saw the same thing in Tir County, approximately 21:00 RST on 1/26/29476.

I had already read Cloudeh's account, so I wasn't terrified. I was annoyed. And admittedly, a little curious. And I had an advantage over the previous observers: I saw it from the air.

To elaborate on my earlier report, I was enroute from the west gate of Tir to the Crater Farms area, on a freelance law enforcement gig -- tracing stolen property. I was cruising at normal (QL36) airspeed in my Yalmaha, at an approximate altitude of 30 meters, on a bearing of roughly SSW.

As I approached the crater, I saw a bright yellow glow on the far side of it. I flew over the crater, and as I cleared the far wall, I could see more clearly. I got one camera shot, which I'll post if someone recommends a place to upload it. Here's what I saw:

Two bright yellow vertical disks, with feathered edges suggesting energy discharge, were sunk not quite halfway into the terrain, and moving towards my position rapidly. I am no good at estimated height of ground objects from the air, but I would say that they were at least 7 meters tall and at least 12 meters across. They were moving face-on towards Crater Farms at a pretty good clip.

As they passed underneath me, I turned and began to follow. They swept across the crater, passing through the walls on either side. (I saw at least one clan member hunting down there, perhaps he or she can describe it from ground perspective.)

They were not radiant, or if so not very, because they were not illuminating their surroundings. They were ignoring ground contours, and passed through the crater walls without affecting them or being affected.

As the phenomenon continued, I used one of the wide-area comm channels (Clan OOC) to call out a warning, as it was clearly heading back along my previous heading -- towards Tir City. The phenomenon was moving slightly faster than I was, and due to turn-around time had a head start on me, so I never again quite caught up with it.

It seemed to me that they stopped far short of the Tir city walls. In Western Tir Approach, apparently just about even with those rocky pillars where the road first turns, both disks rapidly evaporated.

I was annoyed, because I'm a historian. Historians aren't supposed to observe history. We're supposed to record history. And when we do observe and record history, it's supposed to be plausible history.

What do I think I saw? I think I don't know. No, I'm pretty sure I don't know. I saw nothing to contradict TAG's theory that it's yet another malfunction in the weather-control system. I saw nothing to prove it, either. As with Cloudeh's sighting, this was under mostly-clear blue skies.

I leave it to the big-domed science types to tell me what it was. All I can tell you for sure is that if it is the same phenomenon (as it appears from our respective photographs), then it has been seen in two very different locations in a fairly short span of time.

Author Bio: Dr. April Sotto has her doctorate in Economic History from the prestigious University of ****a, and was the author and narrator of the documentary gridshow about martial arts culture, "The Killer Application." She is on Rubi-Ka to research her next gridshow, "Pirates of the Notum Coast."
