Idea about apartments.
I am not sure if this idea has been said before, but I was thinking about a way to improve houses.

For here on in, I will say "my system," pertaining to my idea.

Okay, fist and foremost: You begin with the normal old apartment, maybe have them come with furniture as you see places in missions (the nice looking kind). With the furniture that comes with your orginal (and free apartment), make it moveable like the furniture is now. Of course, allow us to have couches and chairs and tables (etc.). Also, make the amount of furniture unlimited.

-----------
Now, for the addition in my system.
-----------
Have a "Real Estate Terminal"; much like that of the mission terminal. Now these terminals can be placed through out the city like a bank, or in a shop; which ever.
////////////////
Okay, if you have the terminals scattered around the bank (or would like to keep the real estate all in one terminal) then you it works this way:

You right click on the terminal to bring up the real estate menu (again, maybe like the mission terminals, but with more than 5 options) and your options (house style options) are now available to purchase.

Now, you have about 3-4 house types (size, shape, and room quantity differences) available. The bigger and more rooms there are, the more expensive. With the bigger houses comes more furniture already in the house.
-or-
Another way of doing this type of apartment-style-selection is, instead of having ONLY 3-4 static house types, make a slider system, like the mission selection. Have sliders for Size, Room Quantity, etc (I would need more ideas for the sliders, if not these two).
///////////////
If you wish to have the "Real Estate Terminals" all in one special real estate shop. There would be about 3-4 terminals for each style. Of course, the slider style probably could not work with this idea, but you get the idea.

-----------
Now, with either method, when you select an icon, either after using the slider method and ****-Left clicking the house option, or shift-left clicking one of the 3-4 premade house types (again, depending on the real estate selction method) it will show you the locale of the apartment (OT being in OT cities and areas, Neutral in either (or maybe neutral area), and clan being in, well, clan area).

With the slider method, you can just reroll your house types (like the mission rolling mind you) for a locale that is more suitable.

With the real estate store or premade apartment method, just hit the new house (or reroll, what ever you wish to call it) button. Then the the 3-4 apartment types will showup again with different locales.
--------------

Along with this system, I say the way the furniture is handled must be changed. Make furniture can have in your apartment unlimited, make more types of furniture available, and have the furniture "sit-and-standable" if not already. Kind of like the furniture you see in bars and missions. Also, make the furniture moveable.
------
------

Now this system is of course just an idea, and it can as well as should be, modified. Modified for various changes needed due to limitations and sacrifices. If anyone has any ideas or modifications that would better suit this idea, then please, go on ahead. Let's be constructive about it as well

See you all In RK1.
