Elusive Darkness is recruiting (omni, RK1)
Elusive Darkness is a young, european lead guild looking to increase its active member base inlight of notum wars.

As we are european everything revolves around the GMT timezone. Non-europeans are expected to adapt or not apply, although I will be drawing up plans for a US division internally.

We are currently looking for people lvl 80 or higher who are experianced AO players, anyone lvl 150+ would be welcomed especially that we as a guild may take part in more strategic raids.

I am currently drawing up plans to make us an effective, fun and active guild to be with with the aim of being known as one of the 'big guilds' along side guilds such as TBH, First Order, DSA, etc.

If you are looking for a fresh start, and prepared to put up with a young guild then get in touch with myself or Hydral.

I look forward to meeting and working with the successfull applicants.

Please note: we are not a 'n00b' guild, nor are we a roleplay guild (yet). We are not an alt dumping ground therefore we are only taking on 2 alts per main char.

If we recruit you, you will be expeted to take a full and active role in the development of the guild, putting forward ideas, concerns and questions at guild meetings.

Also note that "I'm in a mission" is not going to get you out of guild meetings, you will be expected to attend all meetings.

Inktopia,
Director Elusive Darkness
