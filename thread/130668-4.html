2cents
read thru each person's comments on this and wanted to drop my own opinion here.

I would consider myself a very active pvp'er (esjaytdr, calio, nekkon) and have friends on both omni and clan side that I have known since beta. I also have been an active participant in Notum Wars since it's arrival, so I like to think I speak with a little experience.

It was not until a recent conversation with Madde and another Tranq member that I really understood how this balance shifted. Initially, I was convinced it was because of loot whoring and omni's focus on things outside pvp that was screwing things up. I have also done the "numbers" research that so many use as an excuse, but I can tell you that for each major battle I have logged in my alts at camp spots throuhout the game and sure enough you find plenty of omni there..so that's out the window.
What my conversations with various Omni buddies has showed me is that it's more than just the numbers, the politics, and the lack of unity on the omni side.
The core of the issue really is the personality of the two sides of the conflict. Of course this is just my opinion, but I like to think that it's grounded in a bit of fact. I am very worried about the balance in the game right now, and the lack of overall content. PVP and wars are two elements that keep the game dynamic. I therefore am very curious about why things have gotten to where there are.
I post "going to shoot a tower in EFP bla bla bla" in one of the war chats and I guarantee word of mouth spreads like mad and people flock to the area looking for some fun and a little battle.
I see a "tower attacked" message and I watch people from all over the place and all guilds start spamming each other about how to get there fastest etc.
I do not see the same reactions in the same situations on the omni side.

I've seen a lot of people, that I thought I knew better, post some bitter threads in here about exploiting and other various grudges that have taken some of the fun out of the game. I think it's safe to say there has been exploitation on all sides, and it's time to get over the past. There will ALWAYS be idiots out there trying to take advantage of the game, there will always be jerks trying to ruin it for others. FC does what it can to prevent things like this from happening.

Every time I see Omni at Tara...flooding the zone so that nobody can get in, and 90%(if not more) getting the loot, I think to myself how much I wish I can see that same thing in tower wars.
As much as I'd like to beat down Noer and some other Tranq members in real life for having some magic ability to single out my towers in the base when they attack, I still must give them respect for making an effort. I wish I could see that same spirit across Omni as a whole.
(edit*-I also wanted to throw in the fact that I haven't seen any Omni guild band so well together in defending their base than Ragnarok..seen some really big attacks repelled by a small force using good defense strat)

Complaining about past exploits, ragging on clan guilds for political issues, and making up excuses doesn't change the % balance. Notum Wars has pushed this game to be much more pvp oriented, and at the root of that are the bases. If something isn't done to even things back out, it will continue to be nothing more than a "camp for loot" game without any real "hook" to suck us all in.
I am very glad Wolfe started this thread, and I hope it does inject some of that passion into the Omni side that we saw when NW first came out. No question some of the finest moments in the game came when the scale kept tipping back and forth.

Btw..just a side comment to Kink's post on page 1...I would say that not taking down towers on a regular basis doesn't necessarily constitute an inactive clan. Apoc is usually the first to respond to ANY clan base attack, always willing to help the major crews out in any takeover, and has been the scale tipper in some of the most significant battles thus far.

I don't think it has anything to do with TC to be honest.... it has a whole lot more to do with politics, loot and RP than anything else. As for the powerbalance, I think Wolfe is pushing a few little un-truths in claiming that TC were eliminated by themselves because of various reasons (i.e. people leaving, FC not supporting things properly, etc).

Sitting there and slating TC as causing the problems that exist today isn't fair and right, especially given the amount of time and effort that TC (and Apoc) members have put into moving this game forward in terms of forcing issues with FC behind the scenes.

AO isn't AO anymore... it's NW, and the problem with NW is that it shifted the game from a guild-based MMORPG to a guild-based political simulator. You have the back-benches (the smaller/weaker guilds) who can make or break situations, you have the ministers (the larger/stronger guilds) who help enforce policy, and you have the party leaders (a select few people from the larger guilds) who decide policy.

The reason that this fails is because the party leaders (OT in particular) basically hate each other, and will only work with each other if they can see that they are each going to gain from a situation.

The day that OT people realise that NW isn't about take, take, take will be the day when they get themselves organised. You only have to look back at the time when Legion, Renaissance, MoK, BoB, etc, etc, etc all went on a rampage through CAV, Mort and a few other zones for about week (3 days of that was 24 hour base takedowns). During that time, people didn't care who got the base, they didn't care that other guilds didn't come along, and they didn't care who they worked with.

This gives the first real clue as to why the power balance shifted... after that week of attacks, OT got burnt out and many people admitted that fact.

I think alot of OT people would like to fight and would fairly easily even the balance between OT and clan, but to be quite honest, they can't be bothered to put in the hours, only to be shouted at afterwards or moaned at because someone somewhere didn't agree.

It's a shame really... AO was really cool, there are one hell of alot of really cool people that play AO, and if NW had have been thought out properly in the first place, then it would still be really cool.
