Give the Crats some attention.
Planned changes to the game are only going to make things worse for us.


Where the hell are our Buffs?

Where the hell are our Tradeskills?

Where the hell are our Pistols?

I am sick and tired of groveling at the feet of MPs, Engineers or Traders just so I can get the same level of ability as everyone else on Rubi-Ka.

Without our own line of viable tradeskills making money is very hard for us Crats, and like engineers we are constantly having to buy new nano-formulas just so that we can stay in the game.

Charms are becoming a joke, with the constant threat of you pet turning on you due to the lack of a Pet-Timer. And the HUGE gap between charm levels mean that for the most part crats arent able to charm anyway due to the Psy Caps on the charm lines.

Pistols?
Unless your heavily twinked, Pistols are nothing more than a gnat bite to our enemies.
Use DD nanos I hear you say.
How many Crats have enough IP to spare after maxing all basic abilities and thier required skills to plough any significant amount of IP into Nano Init?

Give us a usefull tradeskill that other classes are going to require from us.

Give us new Charms to midline between the existing charms.

Give us a Pet Timer, or significantly increase the duration of our charms so that our pets remain in our service for the duration of at least one battle.

Give us Pistol as a Green Skill (yeah you heard me), and Fling and Burst as Light Blue. A crat wielding a QL200 pistol is almost as dangerous as a Fixer with QL50 shotgun for pete's sake.

Give us Masteries, I mean what use is a Psychology buff? Know any items that have a psychology requirement? We need Matter Creation, and Time and Space.

Come on FC give us some attention.
