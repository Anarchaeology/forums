Originally posted by Thyrra
how much woodchuck would your woodchuck chucker chuck if your woodchuck chucker could chuck woodchuck if you were elected?
Why, that's simple. To power a conventional Woodchuck Chucker requires two people: a "plucker" to pluck woodchucks from the Woodchuck Chucker's woodchuck truck and chuck the woodchucks to the Woodchuck Chucker's "chucker" who plucks the woodchuck the Woodchuck Chucker's "plucker" chucked from the air, tuck the woodchuck into the Woodchuck Chucker's mechanical woodchuck chucker, yells "duck" to the Woodchuck Chucker's "plucker," who ducks so the Woodchuck Chucker's "chucker" can fire the mechanical woodchuck chucker and thus chuck the woodchuck without hitting the Woodchuck Chucker's "plucker."

Since it takes the Woodchuck Chucker's "plucker" two seconds to pluck the woodchuck and two more seconds to chuck the plucked woodchuck to the Woodchuck Chucker's "chucker" and it takes the "chucker" two seconds to pluck the woodchuck the Woodchuck Chucker's "plucker" chucked from the air, five seconds to tuck the woodchuck into the Woodchuck Chucker's mechanical woodchuck chucker, one second to yell "duck" to the Woodchuck Chucker's "plucker," one second for the Woodchuck Chucker's "plucker" to duck, two seconds to chuck the woodchuck with the Woodchuck Chucker's mechanical woodchuck chucker, and five seconds (with a bit of luck) to reset the Woodchuck Chucker's mechanical woodchuck chucker and start the process over.

Thus, this whole procedure requires twenty seconds per woodchuck fired, thus giving the Woodchuck Chucker a rate of three woodchucks a minute and 180 woodchucks an hour.
