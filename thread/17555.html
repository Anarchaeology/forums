Thank you Funcom! Funcom DOES have the ability to run an event!
Dear Funcom,

I would like to thank you for the successfull "Protect a caravan" event that was organised last night between omni-clan participants.

I know that in a game like AO it is difficult to control the movement of people but last night you managed to keep the numbers of people involved at reasonable levels and once we set off the pace was kept reasonably high to avoid picking up ininvited guests etc.

More of this sort of small event, done at frequent intervals with differing levels of players would certainly be welcomed by me.

The nice thing was that I even got paid "in-character" for participating.

Funcom and Events
Yes, unless you have the right contacts who can inform you when an event is taking place, the chances of partaking in an event aren't very big.

I partook in a few events - a rescue mission to TLR in December, and the 'peacekeeping' mission to Sabulum in January. Both by pure chance, stumbling over someone RPing in the first case, and them catching my interest, or receiving a /tell from a friend, in the latter case.

I understand that if the events were declared openly on major channels, the lag would be so bad the event couldn't possibly be held. Thus, it's not really such a bad thing to limit the # of people attending. I remember the attack on Four Holes and the chemical factory - two minutes delay on my character's actions once the action started. And 40 seconds after my character ran out of hitpoints, he finally lay down and died properly. Ah well.

Lag of that caliber is something I want to avoid, as most people would.

Events are happening, however, to those at the right place and at the right times. So keep your ears perked, people.
