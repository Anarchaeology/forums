Pathing is certainly a major problem with all pet professions, not so much in solo play, but mainly with fast moving teams.

When soloing you have more time to move your pet(s) about, and engies certainly can solo missions quiet high compaired to them, if they get mochams and a wrangle (at least at lvl130). Missions are slow as my slayer takes an age to kill a mob, but the xp is good. If you are careful, pathing can be worked around (some mobs you won't be able to get your bot too, but most can)

Problems soloing is if you do can a pathing glitch like a rubber band fight, where the mob and bot run about like fools, you can easily get aggro, and aggro = death.

A lot of professions see the fact that engies (and crats/metas) can solo as a reason to think we are not gimped. But solo has, and always will mean slow xp compared to teams. (not to mention a little lonely)

(All that assumes the engie can get mochams and maybe a wangle)


The major problem with pathing comes with teams, where speed is needed. A team of experienced players move from mob to mob very fast. Mobs also die a lot faster than solo. This means the bot has to be fast enough to reach the mob before it dies and has to keep up with it's owner when moving from room to room.

This is a major problem, you move to the next room, but your pet is stuck in a wall behind you. The number of times I have lost my team because I have been trying to get my bot through a room is large.

This is the reason that many engies, crats and metas don't use pets in teams, and this means that they are getting rid of their major damage doer, therefore gimping themselves.

When was the last time you saw a high level crat using 3 pets in a team mission? Why? pathing!

It is mostly engies who seem to complain about this due to the lack of anything else they bring to a team, (crats can calm, metas bring mochams), but this is a problem for all 3 profs.
