Shifting Sands...
"I'm not sure where we've 'landed', exactly, though it appears that this place has abandoned life, all life, long ago. The horizon is far, taunting me to go further, yet reminding me I can't. For as far as I can see, even with the aid of my ocular implants, the barren desert spans, turning with the winds and stinging sands. Albiet this is my first full day one this planet, I doubt I shall have many more."

Sliding back his data-pad into a thigh-strapped pocket, the stranded fixer took step out from under the shade of his overturned ship, broken with the promise of staying so. The twin suns of this death-bowl shown down with a fury apon his worn cyber armor, heating it to a nearly unbearbable degree. The sands, golden and scorched, sifted away with every step, flittering aimlessly down the steepening side of the dune he now reigned; scouting the mimicking lands that seemed to stretch off without end.

"Kriffing frell..." The fixer, Loakan, sighed, hope slowly drifting away from his usually optimistic view. "Low on water and field rashions, and I crash-land us in the middle of a bloody desert... Heaven forbid I land on a tropical planet."

Plopping heavily down apon the heated sands, the armor-adorned man slipped open his helmet's view-plate, retrieved from his backpack a cantine of water, and guzzled it down with thirst, pondering in the back of his mind on what to make of his new 'home'...

Their mission, he recalled, licking up a few stray droplets of cooled water that had escaped his mouth, had been a simple one: Safely navigate the transport craft, that now lay ruined, to spacial anomoly Omega T-401, and let the scientists do their work. Childs play....

Arching over his knees to aid with standing, Loakan, with notible effort, made it up, and proceeded towards the cooled safety that the ship's shadow created. The suns, both near each other, as if they two orbited some invisible body between them, ruled over the mid-day sky, chasing off any stray cloud that may have let loose some life-giving rain otherwise.

"Wonder if--" his words and thoughts were stiffled with suprise as an oddly cast shadow sprung up over the dune before him, though its source covered by the protruding wing of the starship he now rested against. "The devil is that?"

With one swift motion, his military training acting now, the fixer and un-holstered his mausser, stood, and readied an ammo clip. With as much guile as he could muster, Loakan snuck his way towards the end of the ship, and cocked his head just enough over the end to give sight of what he thought impossible; a living creature in a lifeless desert. Or was it lifeless?

Silently, the man hastily took notes on the creature: its size, biological make-up, and all other information he could gather.

Perhaps he would not die here after all...
