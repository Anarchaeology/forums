Apartment Relevance
Maybe I am a hopeless roleplayer, and maybe I am just a noob, but I would like to see apartments have more in-game relevance than just a roleplaying gimmick. Some improvements to apartments I would envision in the future:

1. The ability to pay for a larger or different apartment, or obtain an organizational room/building.
2. More variety in furniture options - I am still trying to find a bed of any sort.
3. Apartments being more tied to game mechanics, like an instant heal up point, or a permanent spawn point, or something like that to make them relavent to more than just hopeless roleplayers like myself.

This is not a ***** thread, just things I remember from an old MUD I played that had the Rubi-Ka feel. Does anybody else have any suggested apartment improvements?
