14.2 pet oe changes tested
All,

First, a caveat: this was how it worked in 14.20, things might change before it hits live!

Just wanted to say that I tested out the new pet oe changes last night and they seem to work exactly as intended. I was in a fight with two mobs, one was hitting my pet and one was hitting me. I debuffed myself so that I was no longer where I could control him and he continued to beat on the one that was fighting him until it died and then refused to fight the one beating on me. This was with a crat, btw, so I don't know how a healing pet works.

I tested this stuff for about an hour with an ARK. All the commands that are supposed to still work even after I'm debuffed do still work. Once the debuff wears off I regain control without a problem. While I can no longer control, the pet answers my command attempts with "Are you my Master?"

A couple of important things to remember:
1) Pets do not zone with you when they are in /pet behind. So if you run from a mission the pet will stay in the first room.
2) If you no longer can control your pet and you try and give it a command then it automatically goes into /pet behind even if it is still being beat on! So it will be very important that you know when your buffs have worn off or when you have been debuffed and not try and switch the pet's targetting because it will just then not be attacking anything!

Some good news: /pet report now tells you the Nano % in addition to the Health % (that will be interesting with healing and mez pets). It also tells you the total and occuppied NCU that your pet has. Crat pet NCU increases with pet levels.

And one rollerrat I charmed as a test had a -2 billion Nano %. Sounds like rollerrats need some lovin'! :]
