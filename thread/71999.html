I was ready to quit the game (I didn't come here, whine and threaten) but then decided instead to see if I could improve the game for myself by improving how I played it. It worked for me. I got into a good role-playing guild, worked hard on molding my character to fit his role in that guild and to make him more interesting for me to play. Dropped characters that I was finding stale. Started some new ones to explore other professions.

The role playing as well as the fun of having a new character that has the advantage of funds and more knowledge on being prepared for leveling have returned much of the fun to the game for me. I have posted this in other threads but maybe it bears repeating. You can create your own content. You can add to the game, explore, change the dynamics for yourself. You may not level as fast, you may not be the most Uber player on, but you can make the difference. If you decide not to and would rather wait and be spoonfed then I don't care how long you play you will never be happy.

I am sorry if you don't think I could have figured some things of this nature out in less than a year. I also reject your opinion that by playing 6-8 more months I will be back here a whining sniveling complainer. It won't happen. I appreciate the threads that point out flaws and ask for or suggest improvements. I do not honor those that have knee jerk reactions to changes.

I hope this clarifies my position. If not I will come back in 6-8 months and write the same thing and maybe then you will give it some consideration. Of course by that time your time frame may change so you can still ignore me.
