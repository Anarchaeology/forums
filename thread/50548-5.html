As far as the Handy Steel-Ribbed Gloves go, you can also look at getting Cyborg Death Squad Armor Gloves. They may be a little harder to get your hands on but they are a little easier to get on and also add to B&E. They seem to be more popular since they add to AC's a little better. They do however lack a couple minor bonuses to skills.

MM is nice to implant if you want good HoTs running, but it is important to remember that most of your Long HoTs are level restricted. I have Shining MM implanted and the only benefit is that it gives me better short HoTs than most at my level.

When it comes to your head implant though, I would recommend going SI over MM.

Illicit Laser Backup
Agree with everyone in this thread. Its a personal choice how you like to spend yout ip on different skills, and which weapons suit you best.

I have play a "typical fixer", typical skill raise in abilities, burst, msg and nano line. As always, fixers are 10-25 or sometime 30-35 ql behind with their nanos. (Nanos start going better after L100+)

I started out with Mausser, got a Gamma Ejector at L41, go over to MSC at L78. MSC, yes a great gun. I continue using MSC until i got tired of it at L114. I need to try something new. Many fixers said to me, go for the Manex. Some told me to go for a SOL, and lucky me, i got a SOL, and borrow a Manex August.

SOL, nice gun, but in between SOL or MSC, i will choice MSC.
Manex, Oh, yes, great gun, great burst, however slow one.

But, something is missing, I need something "new", something with style, something looking good...

A "mentor" fixer that has help me alot, use a Illicit Laser Backup, and a few other did advice me to go for ILB.

So, I did 3 missions and got 3 fine ql.145 ILBs. But the problem is... they require pistol skill and if I want to dual with them, I do need multi range skill. So, a quick run to SL and borgs to gain xp for leveling up. After 4 levels, I can at last dual with ILBs. Yes, 160k ip spend on 2 skills. If you ask me if it is worth 160k ip? I will say, yes, every single ip is worth to be able dual the ILBs.

Illicit Laser Backup can be found as mission reward. No boss camp, no trade skills are needed, just a simple mission role with help of Click Save, and you will own a ILB. (Tips. team up and role for a mission, ILB will easie popup as reward)

Its a very good lucking gun, no clumpsy parts, long, white and elegant looking. People has stop just to ask what kind of weapon it is.

Ok, ILB is nice looking, easy to get a hand on, but how is it in performent?

Burst time is 15 secs. Yes, 15 secs. (10 secs if you are using ILB Intra Edition)
Overal normal damage is good, maybe not as MSC, but very good.
Higher crit bonus whan Manex, a little lower whan MSC.
Attack/rech. time is slightly lower whan Manex, but also slightly higher whan MSC.

Overal its a very good gun. Worth a try.
