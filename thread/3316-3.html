About biomech.
Biomech is still broken in two ways:

There is no backslot armor in it: Chest and Cloack pieces both use the chest slot.

The other thing is, that the First aid / Treatment bonuses are added wrong the instant you zone. If you unequip your biomech armor after zoning, your first aid and treatment skills end up being red. The way around this is to remove the pieces before you zone and put them back on after you have zoned.

Really annoying. Thats why i dont use biomech at all until they fix it.
