The more I think about though, the more it bothers me. Can you really compare IP costs like this based only base costs and percentages?

Say, for Fixer Bob, he has 189 run speed at the base cost of 5. For next point Bob would need 160 IP to get to 190. That's a total of 12710 IPs spent. Now, for Fixer Frank, he has 190 run speed also at the base cost of 5. For next point Frank would need 161 IP to get to 191. That's a total of 12870 IPs spent.

189 is 0.9947368 of 190, but 12710 is only 0.9875680 of 12870. These are real, verifiable numbers.

The way percentages are in our lists. We are saying that skill with a base of 10 IP cost is twice as costly as a skill with a base of 5 IP cost. If that were true, then 100 in a skill of base 10 should cost the same IP as 200 in a skill of base 5. This is simple not true.

Let's take Rifle and Vehicle Water. For a Fixer, Rifle has 10 base cost, Veh. Water has 5. Now, I am going to raise rifle by 50 points, and Veh. Water by 100 points. Record the IP, the hit cancel :P

Rifle 38 -> 88, 2950 IPs
Veh. Water 37 -> 137, 5450 IPs

So you can see, even though the ratio of base costs is 2:1, and the ratio of skills increased is 1:2. The resulted IP costs aren't equal. That is why you can't compare IP costs with a percentage and/or base costs only

This thread has turned into an interesting discussion in IP costs.

If you know the current cost of a skill (shife-left-click on the skill name) and the base cost of a skill(create a level 1 char of the profession you are interested in, and shift-left-click on skill), you can figure out the total IP spent in a skill. This is an approximate value because of the rounding up that Funcom does.

The formula for total IP spent in a skill is:
code:
TotalIP = (5 * CurrentCost^2)/(2 * BaseCost) - (CurrentCost / 2) - (2 * BaseCost)


For most of the skills that Fixer has a big advantage on (Section 3), they aren't raised VERY high. For example, the difference of IP costs between 138 First Aid for a Fixer and 138 First Aid for an Enforcer is not very big, around 4K of IPs only.

This is why I said in my original post that you can't just go and say skills in section 3 will compensate for skills in sections 1 and 2. The reason is that the skills in section 3 contribute a fairly small amount of IP to the total IPs used on your char because they aren't raised every level/frequently so they remain fairly low in skill.

But at this moment, though, I am not completely sure about IP costs for Fixers anymore, since the only sure way to compute IP costs is to compare a Fixer and an Enforcer around the same level, with similar emphasis in the skills they used. That won't be easy to do. One thing I can tell you though, at my level, I've spend nearly 200k worth of IPs into nano skills. :P
