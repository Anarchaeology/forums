Suggestions for Arena upgrade
Have you ever challenged to a duel, and that other SOB came and ganked u and ur duelant straight to reclaim? A friendly fight, agreed to half HP, u got ganked and shot to reclaim?

Well i have suggestions to work the Arena duels more as they should, and not just a mayhem chaos.

First make a option for duel as the "team" window. Where up to 6 players can duel. The leader that invites sets a Option window with rules, and everyone that joins the Duel group, must accept the terms.

In example:

Duel Options:
Private: [check]
Selfbuffs only: [check]
First to half HP wins: [check]
Time: [check] [input mm:ss]
Reward: [check] [amount of money] [reward item bank]

Ready/Accept [button]

This ofcourse have room for alot of ideas to options.

All that are figting in private duel groups should be no attackable for others. No more friggin gankers this way.

If not in duel group, everything can work as before.

I once suggested something similar. It wasn't nearly as involved as your good suggestion. I simply suggested having a machine that dispenses keys. You share a key with those you duel with like in a mission. You can't shoot anyone with a different key, and they can't shoot you. Anyone entering the arena without a key is fair game to any other person without a key, but is safe from those with a key.
