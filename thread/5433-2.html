Returning from the grave...
Well, from about mid december until now, my personal life turned to hell, Christmass travel was required, and otherwise I had little intrest in playing my fixer. Yy is popular, has a lot of people willing to help him out, but psychologically, I was at a wall... I could see my mistakes, I knew where I had failed him, and what many people didn't realize, he was the first character which I ever rolled on Rubi-ka. I was *very* lucky to have made some of the choices I made early on. So, there sitting in slot 0 of my character list is Yyerinfahr, level 67 fixer - possibly retired. So with all the time I had in and out of home, I retired a very depressed fixer - at probably a very bad time.

With less time to devote over the holdiay, I picked up an engie... I figured - heck, I'll need something to someday make me a set of grid armor, and there are very few tradeskill characters whom I trust enough to do so, and those that exist rarely log.

With 50 hours of play, I achieved 40th level, a level 67 bot (as the level 50 MP said holy sh*t! that's way higher than me! when e cast the masteries to summon him), a car, and pretty hefty tradeskills. And no, I didn't move over a ton of stuff from Yy. I made him on the spur of the moment. If you want an easy class to play, play an engie... but that is not my point.

Yesterday, to mark the beginning of the new year, I started a new fixer, Nulleffect - level 8 clan fixer who *will* have 250 tokens before he turns level 60, because I will never deal with the mission hell Yy put me through. That is the plan anyway.

At level 6, I found as a mission reward a QL6 Michael Patriot. I took the mission, and ran with it. After firing it 3 times, I put up my QL9 mausser - for a indeterminate period of time. I tell you now, the sound is at least different, and it feels like you are holding a SMG when you fire it (much faster fire/recharge). When I turned level 8, I made contact with my old friend fixers, he inherited a QL16, which I can tell you now, will knock the socks off any QL16 mausser. With Nulleffect, I fear that I have no choice but to keep him superpimped, because I know the woes of Yyerin.

I'm not looking forward to the /terminate command. I've always thought that was kind of cheap, but - I fear I have little choice in that matter. So, as soon as I have my first runbuff, I see Nulleffect as beginning that route in order to preserve the mission size.

Null effect is a MC/Runbuff fixer from the start. I have chosen to concentrate on the skills necessary for these things, and my Evades at almost every other cost. With Nulleffect, I hope that grid armor will be possible, and I will be able to choose when to level quickly to use it. Until then, I will focus on 250 tokens, and obtaining grid armor.

I will play my engineer occasionally, with an effort to get him able to manufacture grid armor, and make new weapons for fixers. Unfortunately, the SMG kits still do not work. It is a fun class, but - let me say it now, engies are slow and I have less intrest in really playing them. It is a support class, for my lower level fixer.

So, here is the rundown:

Yyerinfahr, 67 opiflex clan fixer, retired.
Chizzle, 40 solitus clan engineer with David his 67 bot
Nulleffect, 8 opiflex clan fixer - 17 tokens and counting.
