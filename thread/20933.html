Sell Terminals
""There have been suggestions on an in-game "sell terminal" of some sort, where players can sell their items and others would be able to buy them from there. Give us your feedback on how you would like us to implement such a feature. ""


Perhaps something like a trade window you can open up with a terminal where you can type in the number of credts you would like for the item, and then it would be offered in a "buy" terminal/shop by other players? have a small increse in the price that the termianl itself taxes. like a 1k service charge or a percent based inversely on your computer litercy whichever is higher. this would keep higher level people selling an item to a terminal so they can have an alt buy it as its offered everywhere at the price they put it in as and theres a good chance if they put a sweet goodie in someone will buy it before they can log out and back in. players will make money as items are sold, and say if a day or 12 (even 4?)hours pass and no one buys it its put in their bank so the market doesnt get to cluttered with someone logging on and then just trying to sell 1000 things for silly prices will get nowhere.

just a few notes on what good things would happen from such as well as bad things



Pros -
A service charge will help lower the oodles of credits high level players have, if they try to sell something for 6 milion credits and it costs 6.6 milion thats .6 milion credits that gets taken out of player hands

No more oodles of spam on shopbot, it takes to long to get a shopbot item as it takes days to find whos selling it, and there are so many auctoins from people not knowing how to delete them that its a mess.

you wont have to look at 300 messages on the shopping channel after you zone and before you can click off listening ot the shopping channel

lower level players can see whats out there


Cons -

People could just drop all their trash in the terminal or whatever is being used for ptp (player to player) tradeing as they wont actualy come face to face with anyone specific.

This still facilitates the high level players to get all the money as they will be the ones doing most of the selling

it changes rubi ka's economy drasticly, curently its hard and takes days to get something, it would let people get items faster and trade from almost anywhere.

Some ways to help keep out the cons-

Limited numbers of items to be allowed by 1 person into the ptp sell terminal.

limited time that items are offered means it wont clutter up the system as much

Add a charge to selling the item so flaunitng and joke items that dont get sold is expensive (also a good way to keep sell prices down, an advantage to a lowever level player and a bane to a high level one)

How to limit the cons.

1. Limit the number of items a character can put in the sell shop.

2. put a percentage surcharge of the selling price on each sale.

3. After a time period - 2 days, 1 week, 2 weeks??? - the item is removed from the sell shop by one of two (or maybe 3) methods the seller chooses when the item is listed:
A: The item is sold at the going price at a shop
B: The item is sold back to the seller at the Sell Price + Surcharge, and if that cash is not available in the sellers bank account, Option A is used.
C: (maybe) Allow bids to be recorded on the item, remembering the highest. If the hightest bid is => the minimum bid + surcharge then it is sold to that bidder.
If no bid is high enough, Option B is used with Minimum Bid as the Sell Price.

I guess to make things work correctly in the bidding system, a player would have to have the cash deducted from his bank account at the time they made the high bid, even if the bid wasn't high enough to buy the item in order to keep from being able to feel out the minimum bid by slowly increasing the bid offered...

AO-Bay
I like the idea of bidding for items. Limit one item per player-character in auction at a time. A fee is charged to put an item up for auction that is a percentage of the minimum bid. No time-wasters will be posted. To bid, you spend the money, but if you are outbid you get the money back. The auction has some time limit. At the end of the limit, the high bidder gets the item in his bank and the seller gets the credits. If no one bids, the item is sold for the going market rate and the seller gets those credits. Of course, a message would be sent to the bidder when he either wins or is outbid. The seller would get a message at the time-limit with the number of credits he received. The auction booths could either be unique, so that each maintains its own inventory, or you could have all booths within a given zone share a common inventory. It would be nice to have a global auction domain, but I'm guessing that would pose implementation problems.

The difficult part would be organizing all that trash so that a bidder could find what they are looking for.
