A user-friendly suggestion
This is for any FC people who take notes, I got a suggestion to make life a little easier via the UI.

Currently when we hover over items using our mouse, a little tip pops up with the name of the item. Can this little tip also include the "Ql Level", as I find myself shift clicking on a whole slew of items only to see the ql. This would make sorting items and finding what you need potentially faster and hopefully will not be too hard to implement.

Also, in reference to the looting dilemma and what not. Announcing what each person looted is a great idea (should be able to turn it on/off), but I was thinking you can also have a translucent text box above the slain individual, which simply lists all the items. I thought at first to do it graphically, but that doesnt seem terribly helpful. For most experienced player seeing a list of all the items, would make the game progress faster and avoid ninja looting in places like Smuggler's Den, team Boss and etc.

These are only suggestions, people feel free to criticize.

Osy
