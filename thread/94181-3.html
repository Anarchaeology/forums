Mr. 'Alamexis' Age,

you know I consider you one of those people within Omni-Tek with whom peace is possible. Unfortunately, I have not yet met a member of Omni-Pol that would fit into this category. Perhaps my view on this topic is biased. Perhaps it is biased because since I have been back after years of absence, all I keep hearing about Omni-Pol's actions is of unprovoked and premeditated violence. As a man of education in the field of law you should also know about the philosophy of law and justice. Justice is a moral concept, and even more so a very personal thing. What one finds just might be unjust in the view of another person. Law should, however, thrive to do justice to all.

As General Templus puts it, Omni-Pol is here to protect lawful Omni-Tek employees, which implies that everyone else is considered the dreg of this planet, and unfortunately that is how Omni-Pol acted in the past 18 months since I am back here. I still remember my first encounter of a member of Omni-Pol. He was at Newland City shooting guards. When I inquired what he was doing there, he said he was "testing guns". I know that he was not the only person I have seen committing such an act of barbarism, but it shaped my view about Omni-Pol a lot.

Furthermore, Omni-Pol always refuses to publicize anything about their operations. I acknowledge that there might be ongoing operations that might be compromised if information is disclosed beforehand, but the secrecy used by Omni-Pol is su****ious. It leads me to think that these people have to hide from the public as they do things that are not allowed to be public, and thats exactly what a police force in a non-authoritarian state should never do. But of course Omni-Tek is no state, its a corporation. And of course, all the laws you have you make up yourself. And that is exactly why we are fighting you. Cause your rule has become nothing more than any totalitarian dictatorship on some backwater planets: Rule by fear and surveillance.

But perhaps I am wrong and what Omni-Pol needs is only a working Public Relations Department. And then perhaps they need some basic lessons of human and civil rights. Cause a police force is usually expect to defend those rights, not treat them as obstacles and generally ignore them. In conclusion, I do not consider Omni-Pol as a law enforcement agency. It is an armed force that deliberately resorts to violence to protect the interests of the corporation by using methods that I would only expect of thugs and criminals. People they claim to protect us from. No, not us, only the lawful citizens.

Resorting to ICC if the corporations channels wont listen? I do not trust ICC at all, and thats even less than I feel for Omni-Tek. At least there are a few people within Omni-Tek that I do trust. I cannot say that I have friends with ICC. All they have done for this planet was to create an even bigger mess than it was before.

So, to conclude this, I cannot and will not comply with your wish. My words were well-thought and well-placed. Omni-Pol is not doing anything for the clans, but only against them. Thus they are not regarded a police force by us, but an enemy just as most of Omni-Tek - and meanwhile most so-called neutrals. And if Omni-Pol does not understand words, we will have to resort to the only means that we have left to clean our cities. If I see Omni-Pol running around in any clan-held city, I will not hesitate to alert the guards and - if necessary - pull them on the person, as I am not able to resolve the problem myself.

I act civilized upon every civilized person, but I fight fire with fire. Dont blame me for outlawing Omni-Pol in clan land, its their own actions and words that outlawed them.

Captain Corrine "Daimoness" Wright
