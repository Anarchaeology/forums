coldwarkid
Supa Leet
Buying up land in large tracts has enormous potential problems, since land area is a finite resource in the game and money is present in abundance thanks at least in part to the credit exploit.

However, you could do a player-owned town. Start with three small outposts, one Clan, one Omni, one Neutral, with nothing but the basic amenities at the centre. Around the outskirts are vacant lots, which can be bought for varying prices depending on what you want to build there. Go for set layouts, for the sake of simplicity. When most of the lots have been bought in a given area, create some new vacant lots around those. Let the towns grow organically and sprawl a little.

Some options and variations:
  • Allow the owner to redevelop the lot when money allows.
  • Limit the type of development depending on the part of town
  • Add the usual wish-lists for player-owned shops, guildhouses etc.
  • Move the character's apartment to the new area, and restrict characters from directly owning more than one.


Even this idea has obvious problems with dormant characters etc. but at least the problem is restricted to a tightly-controlled area, rather than being spread all over Rubi-ka.

Cheers,

~R~
