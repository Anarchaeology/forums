Will 14.2 leave Enfs out in the cold?
This may be a dead horse... but I didn't hang around to read the thousands of posts when 14.2 was announced. So, I'll ask. Enforcers Challenger nanos are used for over-equipping. Little else. No one uses them in a fight because it would mean they can't mongo and can't taunt (not only can't taunt with nanos, but can't taunt with 'da taunter!'). If we're expected to be within %25 of the skill for our weapon we'll have to cast challenger before every single fight. This simply isn't viable. We cannot recharge our nano between fights if we use challenger; even when we can it takes forever because no one can afford T&S. Will something be done to prevent Enforcers from becoming the next Adventurer? A novelty class of players who weren't willing to recreate their character.

Its a troubling thought, but I've no reason to be upset yet. I've not heard word on the subject.
