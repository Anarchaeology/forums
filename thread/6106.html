EMP, LOL!
Ok, EMP weapons work by frying electronics. This isn't a OFF switch, this is burnt cinder electronics. Granted, all hostile nano programs have terminated, but so have the good ones. Oh, and your NCUs and Belt are fried and useless. If you were in your Yalmaha you just dropped from the sky, and your car is now useless. Your kodiak will never function again. Pray you had no implants in, because your limbs are about to become a whole lot heavier if you did. If you are an MA or Atrox wielding a support beam or other blunt low-tech instrument, the good news is you can keep fighting. If however you use any gun with a built in target analyser, photon emittter, radiation damper or other electronic component, you have just made it useless... And your digital wristwatch? Well, that's dead too. Heck, you might have even scrambled your digital signature enough that the reclaim terminal brings you back as a whole mess of zeros. Remember kids EMP is far more costly than it is helpful.
