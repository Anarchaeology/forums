End camping now
I love AO but it has 2 major problems as I see it, high level hunting and camping.

The high level hunting part is hopefully beeing solved with dyna-camps, so on to the camping part.

I believe keeping camping away from the game is more important than keeping a few items rare. why? With the situation as it is now, people believe that they have a chance of getting a rare item, but might find that they have no chance of outdamaging the other ppl/teams camping the mob in question. Thus they know they would've been able to get the item if it wasn't for other ppl of higher level camping the mob. This, ofcourse, is likely to create a good deal of frustration among players. As time goes by (and ppl level up) it will be more and more likely that you'll find a ppl/teams of lvl 200 there, to camp the mob. In the end you'll have no chance of getting the looting rights on a mob unless you are in a team of 6 good lvl 200s. And that can't be what you initially intended, right?

*Static dungeons: make them accasible in separate instances. Is getting rust-pitted ring and the mantis egg without competition to easy? I dont think so, but if so, make them harder to get, e.g. make tri-plumbo harder to kill. camping is not the way to go.

I believe camelot should not be treated as the other dungeons, since it is a PvP dungeon. Imo PvP oupost with an advantage for the controlling faction is a much better way to satisfy the PvP players, but until then, keep camelot PvP, lower the respawn time on mobs, with items in it dropping all the time and beeing nodrop (that way those few guilds currently camping camelot might loose intrest in it and allow other guilds to have fun in there aswell).

btw, except the camping part, I love the static dungeons, add more of them after you've solved the camping issue, please

*Unique mobs: I've read several suggestions how to end camping of those mobs: 1. reduce their respawn time to a few secs and make their respawn location random 2. lower the respawn rate to 30 mins. (18hrs is a long time...). 3. Make them harder and lower the respawn time. 4. remove them and make their loot drop from random mobs. 5. Make them accesible in separate instances.

Suggestions No. 4 might be a bad sollution, see threads about grid armor and nullity sphere... I believe suggestion No 1 is the way to go, I didn't like it at first, but it has several advantages: 1. It gives an edge to large guilds (beeing able to spend more time/ppl to find them), still it gives the smaller guilds a chance of finding them. 2. It gives an edge to power gamers (beeing able to spend more time to find them), still it gives the casual player a chance of finding them. 3. keeps the items rare. 4. No camping. camping is not the way to go.

As a side note: I'm just a casual camper. I have however camped tri-plumbo for 3 days once (I missed 2 respawns). I got to kill him 2 times (it was rare to see tri-plumbo without someone lvl 150+ beeing around). One guy there got looting rights on him for the 13th time (still no ring, might have been bad luck). As far as I can tell, he didn't drop the ring during those 3 days. If the chance of getting the ring is as low as I think it is, I suggest you change the drop rate percentage aswell, as stated earlier in this thread, you should get something useful if you manage to kill a boss mob. If he's to easy, make him harder to kill, but make him drop something useful. Those 3 days were the most boring days in my time playing AO. And before camping something again, I will not only be level 200, but I will also be thinking atleast twice about it...
