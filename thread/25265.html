"With the decision to make an expansion pack came a few shifts in priority. Even though we will of course continue to patch new content into Anarchy Online on a regular basis, it has become a little more difficult to predict when certain features will be made available"

We dont care about an hypothetic expansion pack , we care about AO content , gameplay and world improvement that we are waiting for such a long time, guild house, guild bank , territory capture, more "in world" interactivity, anything that could give us an opportunity to change the boring and repetitiv hunt/miss cycle.
