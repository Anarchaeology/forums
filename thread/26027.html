GUI Update - Mark unusable items
Something really simple for the shopaholic...

Mark items in all windows with a red background if they are unasable to you at that point (thnk Diablo 1 / 2).

Its a pretty simple check, but allows you to see instantly what treatment pack to buy by just looking for the last one that isnt red.

Also stops you mistakenly buying a nano for the wrong class (something I keep doing anyway).

I realise this wont work for all tradeskill items (i.e. items that you want to combine), but you dont tend to buy these nearly so often as the basic tools for the job.

Just my 2 creds...
