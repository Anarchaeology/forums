Part of the confusion here is due to the inherent ambiguity of Notum Wars. NW was designed as the PvP "land control" expansion. Those involved in playing out the war, which to many remains the central theme of AO, had asked since the start for some way give meaning to the cross faction PvP battles beyond just bragging rights.

Symbolically tower ownership translates into the in game's manifestation of the war on Rubi-Ka between Clan and OT. People check their sides' XP bonus, and from that get a feel how well they are doing.

The trouble is the story based justification for the NW does not map neatly to the Omni/Clan political war. What we are told about NW is as follows:

1. The ICC deregulates mining so that a fix set of land allotments can be opened to mining by any party. The mine's rights of ownership is given on a "who can hold then can keep it" basis.

2. There is no indication that land slots deep in Omni territory should be given to Omni organisations or vice versa. Nor does the ownership of towers in a zone have any effect on the colour of the guards, cities, shops or indeed anything in that zone. That is to say ownership of a mine is outside the political sphere.

3. The mining is described as being opened up to individual organisations pretty much as a means for said organisations to supplement their income. It is not come under the direct control of Omni-Tek Rubi-Ka or (the now defunct) Council of Truth. Again that is to say it operates outside the wider political scheme.

4. There is no suggestion that the day the clans control all the deregulated mines they will have won the war and Omni Tek will be sent packing. Quite the contrary I believe the official line is that clan owned mines still are legally bound to sell their notum to OT for processing and export.

In this context, a neutral having a go at Omni or Clan tower doesn't sound much like an act of war. Heck, its not illegas as far as the ICC is concerned!

As always FC is sending out mixed messages to its players. Is the NW central to the Clan/Omni conflict? Its is a mad Rubi-Ka style gold rush, filled with claim jumpers and gangland squabbles? As with all things in AO people see what they want to see, which is okay just as long as we can all respect each other's view points.
