A Fixer Poem by Fixerben, who we are:
Who we are:

Dead bodies lay at my feet
I feel my racing heart beat
Chemical Mausser cold in my hand
I hit the Burst and Omni bodies start to land

Counting the corpses
100...200...300...4
I'm taking them down to even the score

I grid myself in and take the pain to em'
Then disappear into a cloud of Red Notum
All you will see is flashes of blue
Puzzle you, leave you wondering who...?

Who are these with such great power?
These who come from technologies darkest hour

Rising from the swarms in purest of form
We are those with greatest control
With the power of our minds
The world around us we mold
Be careful clan brothers
And Omni be warned
We bring with us a blue lightning storm.

-Fixer Ben
