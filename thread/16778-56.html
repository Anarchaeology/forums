Virus Alert!
The Exchange virus/trojan poses as a MTA/MDA server. It is currently distributed on CD only. It costs between $1500 - $5000, typically, although some versions cost much more.
Once installed & deployed as a mail solution, the exchange virus will periodically crash, causing you to lose all your data. When you perform the "maintenance routines" specified by the creator, it can randomly crash as well. The virus is designed to prevent any back-ups of your data from being successful.
Once crashed, the very clumsy restore "tools", used in the proscribed manner in fact corrupt the data further, and lead to hiring a consultant to reinstall the virus, usually with even worse settings than before.
The virus uses its documentation to replicate, in that it fools users by saying that at least two servers must be running it to allow it to be stable... Soon, a typical office will have five or six machines infected by hapless administrators, hoping that the next replication will make it stable.
An infected server also randomly slows down to a crawl, and generates bizarre and nonsensical error messages, which appear and disappear for no good reason.
It requires knowledge of "hidden settings" to actually close the software to open relay; if you follow the documentation it will look like it is closed, but if you inspect closer, you will find it is not. The hidden features do the trick, but it may still automatically report itself to ORBS.ORG as a open-relay, and get your mail blocked.
Finally, the virus plays shadow games with the users, providing spotty authentication, bogus errors, and denial of service to non-Windows-based machines. The virus also occasionally runs DoS attacks against its parent network.
Virus Name : Exchange
Creator : Microsoft
Fix : Install Sendmail/Unix solution or Lotus Notes, or use any other available form of communication. It is better to write your own packets by hand then to get infected by this virus.
