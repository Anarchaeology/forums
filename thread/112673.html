OOC: Something for us.
Hello Everyone.

I would like to address this with you because I think it would be a good Idea whilst also keeping with the story too.

Some of the cities on Rubi-Ka have gardens, that people can go and relax in and just have some peace. There are very influential players on Rubi-Ka who have done alot for the community, such as GSP and Dovve who have been given some recognition.

What I would like to propose is that we have statues created of the most influential people on Rubi-Ka, A tribute to them for all the hard work that they do for us.

It wouldnt be hard to do, and I it will leave a personal mark on the planet of truly great people, not statues of people who mean nothing, like the ones dotted around now.

I would like to nominate a few:

Xtremetech - for his outstanding work for the MP community.

Charles - For his outstanding work in the RP community as well as a crat professional.

Meister- For his long time commitment to Omni-Pol and AO Stratics. And for being head of the Omni-Tek police force.

Seppubot - For his master tradskills and his fair prices, that give people a chance to have decent items and armour made.

Thos are my own personal suggestions, I would like to hear how the rest of the community would feel about this. It wouldnt be hard to do, you just take a skin of them and paint it grey.

Well lets see.

-Tik

Entirely OOC
I actually do like this idea. But I think it should be reserved for those that have made a substantial contribution to Rubi-Ka as a whole. No attack on Meister intended, but just because he's the head of a long standing guild doesn't mean he deserves a statue. Hey, he plays the part he has chosen as good or better than anyone I know could possibly do it, but there are a great many others that I can say the same of and Rubi-Ka doesn't need a Great Wall of Statues.

The people I would like to see honored:

Tarryk - He added a new dimension to Online RPG's that I have seen adopted to many worlds. He may not have been the first but he is the most recognizable.

Dovve - The creater of the map that I would say the vast majority of players have chosen. If my memory is correct Dovve is no longer among the players so a statue would be a fitting honor for the hard work that so many of us use on a daily basis.

Creater of Nano Nanny - Unfortunately I do know know this persons name but again they created a tool that so many of us use.

Cosmik - Many of our favorite goofball. He may be gone but will never be forgotten.

The Professionals - They work hard for all of us, even if most of us don't see it. They put in extra time to make sure Rubi-Ka grows and becomes better for us all and that deserves a measure of respect and maybe a statue in their honor.

We see billboards and signs around Rubi-Ka now that show that Funcom sees who makes a contribution. There are many that haven't been honored with these ingame but may one day.

I truely think this is a good idea and would love to see statues of those that have made our time on Rubi-Ka that much richer.
