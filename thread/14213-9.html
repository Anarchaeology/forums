They NEED to fix overequiping for several reasons:

1. It makes the game totally impossible to balance. Example (just relatively speaking, not exact numbers) If an overequipped level 150 can hit for 900-1500 and a normal equipped player can hit for 150-300 then the game is a joke for the overequipped or impossible for the normal equipped. Either or, but you can't balance for both.
2. It makes PvP impossible to balance because you can't balance classes who vary from normal to 10x normal in terms of ability.

My suggested fix for overequipping is:
1. Max QL of an item you can equip is your level * 1.15 or 15%. So a level 100 could use a 115. and a 10 could use a QL of 12. That's a big boost itself, but level 110 players shouldn't be running around with QL 200 items.
2. Make it such that BUFFS OR DEBUFFS do not effect what you can equip, including installing implants. Only items your currently wearing, and your base statistics should effect what you can equip or use. Buffs should just make what your using more effective (IE weapon/nano buffs make you hit more consistently and have a higher chance of critical).
3. Rebalance the monsters at the same time the above is done.

The number one reason people are *****ing about specific classes being unbalanced is because of overequipping. Classes right now aren't balanced by their abilities, but by how much can X class overequip.

Let them fix overequiping so they can finally actually balance the game.

The reason you need to overequip at the high levels is because they had to tune the monsters for those overequippers. Now those who don't overequip have a hard time BECAUSE overequipping is possible.

Personally I don't think any class balance should be even looked at until they've eliminated massive overequipping. Because it's impossible to balance right now with overequipping unless you do one of the following:
1. Balance for no overequipping. Then those who don't can play a fun and challenging game. Those who do overequip then get to one shot kill in PvP those who don't, and kill anything and everything in the game with contemptuous ease.
2. Balance for overequipping. Now people MUST overequip or they won't be able to play the game at all. Now you've got to consider huge variances in Player power in PvP due to amount of overequipping you can do at different levels. IE you have to consider a level 20 soldier with a QL80 gun relative to a nano-technician who can cast QL25 nano's max. How do you expect to balance that?

Remove overequipping and now you just have to balance classes based on their skills at their level and available weapons for that QL.
