The crit is Back! (for fists)
Worked in the Arena on test last night with Mattie (agent professional) some to try out the PvP crit rate. Now so far I have only tested for pure fists - fist + weapon testing will come later. Here are the results listed in crits/# of attacks:

--------------------

31% Crit Rate + 1250 AR vs. 974 Evade skill (+60 addalldef as well)

Mattie on Full Aggressive:

13/50
15/50
11/50
18/50
14/50

Avg: 14.2/50 or 28.4% crit rate

Mattie on Full Defensive:

11/50
13/50
12/50
15/50
11/50

Avg: 12.4/50 or 24.8% crit rate

Me with 1025 AR (lowered from 1250 - everything else the same) tested once each:

Mattie Full Agg - 14/50 (28%)
Mattie Full Def - 13/50 (26%)

---------------

Now those percentages are based on total number of attacks, and also include times I missed Mattie, which were probably anywhere from 3-5 times per test. Based on these results I was wondering if perhaps our crit rate was static (not based on AR vs. Defensive skill checks) so I tried it later that night against Vanguard a GA4 fixer with 1k+ evade skill. Sadly the results were much worse - 1 crit out of 50 attacks both times, landing only about 5 attacks total each time. It certainly seems that the Off skill vs. Def skill preliminary check is still with us after this, and thus any testing I do in the future will count # of crits per # of landed attacks to get a better reading. If you subtract 5 attacks total however as an arbitrary "evaded" amount from the above tests with Mattie, you get almost exactly 31% for the full aggressive test, which was the crit % mod amount I was testing with.

So from these early figures I would conclude that yes crits are restored to our fists in PvP, and yes we still have tons of trouble against GA
