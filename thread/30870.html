I've been trying to integrate sneaking into my PvP and I have yet to figure out a way to make it fun (fun, not just funny). Everytime I attack someone who doesn't know I'm around, I feel like I'm ganking. Strangely, this is even true when I am really bored, and I decide to randomly attack folks who are either obviously teamed or 40 levels higher than me.

I have definitely noticed that with the introduction of sneaking, people have been teaming or group ganking a lot more often. I know it's insurance for when you get sneak ganked, but that is taking some of the fun out of PvP. It's hard enough to get a balanced match when it's just 1v1. It's even harder to get a balanced match when there are more than 2 people involved.

Oh well. I guess I'll keep trying to find something fun.
