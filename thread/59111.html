Something I'd like thats simple
Lately I've been in groups where at least someone has a /script that can read from the logs, and gives a nice damage readout for the group members. Its nice to see Enforcers soldiers MAs and them guys at the top of those, but as an NT - my damage is always 1/10 of theirs. No I'm not whining about nukes being weak, but the scripts cannot read nanobot damage and attribute that damage to me because in the log, it just reads "(mob) is hit for 2249 fire damage by nanobots" or something.

My suggestion would be to have it display WHO is dealing this seemingly random nanobot damage. This would be a good way to tell exactly how NT's fit in PvM as far as overall damage goes (as well as crats' nukes, MP nukes, Doc and whoever else DoTs also).

Would just be a nice gesture for nanobot users since you give credit to those using weapons, why not give us credit for nanobot damage??
