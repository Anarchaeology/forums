Well you can build weapons, but only those this instructions. Few of these are worth building and you will be very lucky to find anyone willing to use them. (some of the instructions are wrong as well)

You can build robot brains, to improve sense

You can build aggro trimmers

You can make rings (for mucho creds)

You can complete receptical weapons.

You can put together implants.

there are a fiarly large number of weapons that can be built from scratch,
but #1.) it's worthless to build one, when you can buy it from a shop for cheaper,
#2.) you'll have a very hard time finding customers willing to buy one from you, when they can buy one from the shops,
#3) you'll be hard pressed in finding a weapon that won't "BUG" on you halfway thru completion

the above is for weapon construction kits only

implant creation is pretty cool

i love making the jewelrys (witch sell for a nice penny to shops) or tailor made rings for weddings/engagements/gifts/etc..

alot of the new weapon receptacles are VERY nice weapons, and there is a HUGE market for the Sols/Alsaqri/Soft Peppers/and the ocassional Summer SMP Shells

as to the weapons that are upgradable with the MRRs, there isn't a weapon so far that's worth upgrading
