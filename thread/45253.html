The Barbie Analogy
I know some of you are upset about FC releasing stuff before they fix the bugs, but we don't know if they're gonna be fixing those or not before they release the booster, so I'll go ahead and say that group of people is woefully uninformed. But for the militant few that refuse to give in, I give to you a wonderful example of what FC is trying to do here. Something you've all heard of.

Behold, the Barbie Doll.

You go out to the store and buy a basic Barbie. Barbie is fun, but you can only do so much with her. You wish she had bendable elbows and knees, but she doesn't, so you make due for X number of months until finally you get tired of it. Well, then Mattel goes out and makes a new set of clothes/accessories for Barbie. Did they fix what you wanted? Can she bend her elbows and knees? No. But look, you can still do more stuff with her. So you buy the new clothes. You play with those for a while, still wishing in the back of your mind that those elbows and knees could bend. Months pass, and you get tired of Barbie again. Then, behold, the Barbie Playhouse is released. You've got a car, a bathroom, a patio, your own freaking private telephone to God. Now you're having so much fun you completely forget that the elbows and knees don't bend.

And with any luck, they'll make a Barbie where they do bend, and then we'll all be one happy family.

The end.
