Problem with zoning and leet farms is that the different play areas are designed to have certain numbers of mobs in them. There've been times in the past when mobs stopped spawning in certain areas because there were too many in nearby/associated areas.

To allow a player to bring large numbers of leets into any zone he wants would probably end up stopping spawns in those areas because there'd already be enough mobs there. This could be used for exploiting in high spawn areas - bring in tons of leets, kill the resident mobs, then just kill one or two leets at a time until a manageable number of mobs spawns. Also useful for griefing - want to annoy the campers? Bring in a leet army so the smugglers stop spawning.

The leet farm concept could possibly be done in 100% areas, but needs to be limited in number so that the server doesn't have to deal with 1000 leets crowded into one area.
