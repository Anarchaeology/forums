Yalms need to be nerfed
I think that Yalms need a serious nerf. It destroys all sense of distance, which the mission system was based around. It also negates the run speed advantage of classes with run buffs. On top of that, it means that ground vehicles really serve no purpose.

Yalms should provide a quick way to travel from one general area to another general area. Ground transportation should be more effective to travel short distances. I envision that players would use the Yalm to get close to their destination but would have to exit and travel on the ground to get to the exact spot.


Proposed Yalm nerfs:
  • Bad weather should have the ability to ground all flying vehicles
  • Anti-aircraft guns and flying mobs should be added to premium hunting spots. A nice twist to this could be that the guns could temporarily be taken out by a ground assault or controlled by a side.
  • The ability to hover should be remove from the Yalm. The Yalm should gradually increase its speed (dependant on Vehicle skill) and only after a minimum speed was met take flight. The Yalm should also gradually reduce its speed when de-equipped. This would further promote the Yalm as a general transportation mechanism and only useful for long distances.
  • The turning rate of the Yalm should be slow and dependent on vehicle skill. The Yalm should be thought of as a general straight-line mode of travel. If there were a need to search an area, either the player should have a very high air vehicle skill or do the search on the ground.

Well

1) It provides greater choice. You can still get closer missions or hunt near home, but with the change, Funcom could provide real incentives for the hard to get to missions.

2) They could make farther away mission smaller. Part of the mission would just be getting there. In fact that is how I thought is was going to be when I first started playing the game.

3) Yalms would still be in the game and have a purpose. Like I said they would be best for getting from one general location to another. Id see it like this:
  • You find a nice open area to take off from
  • You get lined up in the right direction and equip the Yalm
  • You a slowly gain speed. Your acceleration is dependant on you vehicle skill. If you hit something you take damage, come to a complete stop and are stunned for a few seconds.
  • Once you reach a certain minimum speed you take off
  • You fly the Yalm more like a normal plane, that is that is doesnt turn on a dime or hover
  • You approach you destination and look for a safe place to land. Youve heard reports that a mutant base has reactivated their anti aircraft defenses so you decide to play it safe and land in a safer area that was cleared by an Omni raid only the day before.
  • You now make your way by ground for the rest of your trip. This can involve:
    1. Youve bought a ground vehicle so you equip it and continue
    2. You do it the old fashion way and go on foot, avoiding or fighting mobs along the way
    3. You go on foot using you Conceal skill to avoid mobs
  • You make it to your mission and are rewarded for taking a farther out mission by having a smaller dungeon or getting more experience and loot.


4) Funcom could provide maps of Rubi-Ka showing the status of the ever changing no fly areas. These area could be affected directly by player actions.

5) Weather has real meaning beside ambiance. You might want to check you in game weather chanel before heading out

6)Vehicle skill has more meaning. If you got your skill high enough you should be able to get a vehicle that behaves like the Yalms currently do now. The current problem is that once you get a Yalm you never need to upgrade.

7)Tough zones will maintain there mystique. If you like EQ or not, one thing you have to admit that they did right was to have zones that you could not expect to see unless you were high level. It provided a reason to continue with the level treadmill. In AO once you get a Yalm, you can go anywhere. Does anybody really feel rewarded when they can hunt in a new zone? When you can even survive long enough in EFP to see the sights, it should be a major accomplishment.

8) Raising concealment could mean more than sneaking missions. It should affect your aggro radius. That would mean that an Agent could act as a scout and avoid most of the anti aircraft guns. Since players would spend more time on the ground, being able to avoid detection by mobs would have greater value.

We all know how boring AO can get. Simply put it would give players greater choices and more variety.
