Part solution for weapon speed problems
here we go.

2 handed hammers are the dest weapons. why?

i: you will take minimal damage from damage shields as you dont hit often
ii: high acs will only block "x" off your attack once, where it blocks it multiple times off a fast weapon.

to fix item (i) damage shields could send a % back against the attacker rather than a set damage.

e.g. a damage shield sending 15 per hit back at an attacker at the moment:

a dual-acroblade wielding MA will inflict an horrific ammount of damage back at himself during this fight, really horrific. a 2 handed hammer wielding guy, no problem.

if this shield relected 10% instead of a set 15 points, the field would be evened. whatever it takes to kill the guy with the shield, the attacker with any weapon will take roughly the same damage.

Discuss. :-)
