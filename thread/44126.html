enforcers...hmm.... get a doc friend, convince him/her to turn monsterparts into bloodplasma for you, sell the blood plasma. (I did that, with the help of a doc which has grown way past me teaming limits for a long time ago. You know who you are!)

Or you could continue full cash-missions. Try to notice whish locations give you more money (further away tends to give better reward) Remember to bring a backpack and gether all loot and sell it (to players or vendors. You can normally get more money from a player than a vendor.



Or you could begin blitzing missions. Set the mission-slider at max difficulty. Don't worry. The plan is not to kill anything in there. All you need to do, is pick up that item or repair that radar/food-dispencer/whatnot.
How?
Buff up all hp-buffs you got. Forget the weaponbuffs, damagebuffs and that. Defence is what you want.
I don't remeber when I got which nano, but you should be able to cast your first mongo. Learn to love it!

Do not cast it in front of guards or NPC's. that would mean certain death. But as you enter the mission area, hide in a corner, cast it, and run as fast as you can through the mission, trying to spot the item (repair-missions are easier to blitz, as they have a huge thing. Pickup missions can be difficult, since the items are so small, and difficult to spot right away.

If you are low on HP, run out and heal. When you get in again, try to remember where you have been.



One last thing. It is not necesary to upgrade you equipment every lvl. At lower lvls, you can easl\ily survive with armour found in missions.

Instead of running misisons for money to buy stuff, run missions to get the stuff (and get some cash while doing it) If you don't use clicksaver, you should start now. Clicksaver Site

If anything of what I said here don't make sense, let me know, and I'll try to refrase/explain closer/correct whatever I said wrong.

Good luck!
