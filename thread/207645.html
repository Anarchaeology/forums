What have those crazy scientists done now?
A slender form sat in the darkness. She was perched on the edge of the table, ready to flee at any opportunity. Since they had turned off the bright laboratory lights she was at least sitting still. A faint beeping came from the machine that only minutes before had been spurting horrible noises.

They obviously considered her dangerous. Over the past few days while she was in captivity, only an atrox guard now and then came into her room... and she had already managed to kill him three times with only her bare hands. A calm voice spoke to her from the adjacent observation chamber.

"Mysten?"

A small, cold voice answered without feeling, emotion, or even a distinct tone:

"Yes." The word was hissed, but the shadow crouched on the table didn't move even an inch.

"Oh, good. You can speak. We were afraid the program hadn't uploaded correctly since you attacked the technician."

"Let me out." The grey figure disappeared from her perch on the table and moved swiftly to the door.

"We can't do that, Mysten. We have a few more tests and questions. We are trying to find out how possible survival in the Shadowlands will be when we allow citizens to enter."

"Let me go."

"Mysten, the Shadowlands... where we placed you a few months ago. You've been living there all your life now... we need to know about this place... wh--"

"Home."

"Yes, your home. Tell us how you've managed to survive there on your own."

The shadow in the dark room paused, stopped breathing. The interviewer watched closely through the glass, but the outline of the woman blurred and disappeared. The beeping of the machine slowed, now only making a sound every few seconds.

"What's going on? Did we lose her? Turn on the light in there! Where is she?!"

The interviewer pressed his face against the window, only seeing darkness. Finally, white lights flooded the room. The man jumped back from the window, falling over a chair and hitting his head against the back wall.

There, just on the other side of the window... a face was staring back at him. The light made her smooth, grey skin look like a perfect corpse. Large black eyes pierced through the interviewer, chilling him to the core. Her beautiful black lips curled up in a deceivingly innocent and twisted smile. Her expression was that of a child gleefully pulling the wings off a butterfly and watching it panic.

"Hunt or be hunted. Attack or be attacked. Kill or be killed. Life comes only from death."

Morning in a metal box
"Good morning, Mysten."

The white lights flickered on, revealing the small Opifex woman crouched on the bed staring straight forward. Her eyes were fixed on some nonexistant point in the room and her slender form was visibly tense. She looked frozen, the white clothing she had been dressed in was torn to shreds and scattered across the floor. She was a perfect, unblinking silvery statue.

The researcher sat down to write a few more notes in his observation log. He avoided looking into the black eyes filled with nothing. It seemed likely to drown in the infinite depths of those orbs if one stared too long.

He continued to write, listening for any sound of reply from her. He had spent much of his time watching her, watching the surveillance videos of her at night when she was alone, taking notes and trying to find out how or why she had become so unpredictable and animalistic. Her genetic makeup was not that different from any Opifex, after all. Only a few small changes to her DNA... but DNA was safely altered every day, with every new body born in the labs.

"Mysten, I know you can hear me. You don't really need to respond, but we are taking you before the board this morning. We'll need to get some clothes on you so please try and cooperate. I know you can be well-behaved when you want to be..."

Two atroxes entered the room tentatively, expecting to be attacked. Mysten still sat motionless, staring at the spot no one could see, the only sign of life was the gentle rising and falling of her breasts as she inhaled and exhaled slowly.

As the guards approached her with the white robe, a low growl came from her throat. No movement... the guards looked to each other, and seemed to mutually agree to chance a few more steps to her. One moved behind the Opifex, the atrox holding the robe stayed to her side. The growling became louder and the guards stopped, looking nervously from each other to her, then back.

"Just get her clothed! Hurry up or we'll be late for the meeting. There are two of you, for the love of..."

The guard behind Mysten clutched the tranquilizer needle and nodded to his comerade reassuringly. The atrox to the side took one more step, and froze. Mysten's eyes widened and her head turned quickly to face the intruder.

In the span of a few seconds, the syringe crashed to the floor, splattering the tranquilizer liquid. The white robe flew to hit the window where the researcher stood just behind the glass. The pretty Opifex had jabbed a shard of glass into the jugular of one guard and was ripping at the throat of the other.

The scientist stared in a mix of fascination and horror as both guards drowned in their own blood and the girl returned to where she had been sitting. She ignored the face in the window and proceeded to lick every drop, smear, and splatter of red substance from her pale silvery skin.
