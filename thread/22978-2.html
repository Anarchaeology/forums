An ironic death
I was playing my doc and she was only about level 16. I was in a team and we were outside of Newland City down by that big rock where all the MOBs walk in a line.

Someone spots "one" parspalum just past that line. I knew better than attacking those things (already had learned that lesson by dying once). But as the Doc you got to do your job and hope for the best. Well all we could see was "one" but, obviously due to a glicth in the graphics there was a whole bunch in the big rock we couldn't see. My team was getting tore up bad so I cast a big team heal and my chat window went red with "a parspalum does xx poison damage to you" (or whatever it says), nice color for damage too, red.

Anyway I am going down fast with every green plant MOB in the desert hitting me. So I decide to be brave and turn and run as fast as I can. I run towards this big boulder thinking that if I run over it maybe I can break LOS. Instead I find another graphics glitch and fall under the boulder taking the last of my hit points in fall damage and go into the death animation. Rolling around under this huge boulder with some fleas and a slug looking thing, in the dark.

Sort of an ironic place for my dead body to be. It really creeped me out.

My funniest death ever was a few weeks ago in a mission in COH. We had a good team going, and we were pretty far inside a fairly large mission. Mobs were a breeze.

One of the agents switched from primus coat to that black OT coat and was sneaking around. The enforcer next to me notices this and suddenly he's wearing a yellowishy Miir coat and a yellow Nadir helmet. Looks awesomely funny on an atrox. Promptly I too switch into my 'disco gear', put on my white coat with black dots and a biomech helmet (biomech helmets look awesome on atrox . The other agent switches to a bikini, and our doc switches to some weird pink polkadot outfit. We all think we look pretty great when suddenly 3 Ace Ruffians comes running at us from the next room and starts crushing our doc. At that point we find ourselves fighting in there with close to zero AC, so we all promptly die one by one. I think I lost something like 9 million xp.

The sight of us all suddenly standing around in reclaim, all wearing the most hilarious disco outfits was so funny that I think I laughed for 15 whole minutes. Everyone agreed that it was so funny it was well worth the xp loss.
