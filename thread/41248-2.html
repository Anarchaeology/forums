I re-rolled my melee Adv to use pistols sometime ago when my melee Adv was involved in a fatal IPR Bug/Reset and was not rolled back by tech support.

(I will not rehash the story, it was a lesson in misery versus customer support and I quit AO for sometime afterwards. It would seem they quietly 'fixed' this non-existant IPR Graphical Glitch with this last patch.)

...but I digress....I LOVE my Solitus Adventuress much more than my wimpy Opti anyhow.

I re-rolled a 'Pistol Packing' red haired Solitus to replace my milky-skinned Optifex.

I knew BBI's were junk months ago, I know they are still junk and I sadly shake my head when I see any Adventurer use them, especially post level 90. From day one, I was hell-bent on equipping Kaen-Archibalds, which came up nicely on tests ran by the weapons generator at auno.org. I must say, I have never once regretted the decision to use them over BBI's.

At level 133, I pack a QL 169 (soon to be a Ql 188 Kaen) in my right and a Ql 142 in my oft hand. I do precisely 6k'ish of damage every 10 seconds, and that dose'nt count crits. I generally crit for 1200-1500 at *least* once per fight. These numbers are on Ql 175+ MOBs in team missions.

Just to make sure I could STILL do it, I soloed a 60% solo mission two days ago with these guns. Now, don't get me wrong, I am well aware of the fact Enforcers or Soldiers or MAs do more damage, but thats not what an Adventurer is about afterall.

Adventurers...adventure. We pack pistols or swords, we can carry our own weight and take care of ourselves, but we excel most at supporting a team.

I would also like to add, I now have shottie just about maxed and plan on testing out the new Vultures. I am all for trying something new instead of walking around looking like everyone else and using the same armor/weapon scheme.

BBI's are old, they are not working and it's a lesson in pain to continue to use them then complain about them knowing this.

Kaen's are readily available as mission rewards, I always planned ahead, rolling missions for guns I would not need for 5-10 levels, but blitzing them anyhow for the future. As with all weapons, always equip guns that match your skill levels (with buffs and/or slight wrangles) to use, you will always benefit greatly from this sort of planning and still remain non-OE.

I hope this helps some floundering new Adventurers out there, I know I learned the very hardest way possible and lost alot of time. There is absolutely nothing wrong with the Kaen-Archibalds, I see no one using them and I chuckle. They are fast, they do nice, SOLID, even damage, they have % and range Init boosts, they crit nicely and often and more importantly, they WORK off of ranged init, unlike BBI's. and are available through missions.

I am available in game for any help or advice, I have changed a few people's minds with my 'Crazy Ideas', so if anyone ever needs an answer to a pistol Adventurer related question, give me a tell in game.
