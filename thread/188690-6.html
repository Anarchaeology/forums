My personal opinion on SL quests: I think all quests should be optional, but if you do them, you get a worthwhile reward. Instead of requiring quest completion to advance in SL, I think we should be able to access any area (with the possible exception of Inferno) just by running there. SLs is causing player frustration for two main reasons: risk verses reward and pointless tasks verses meaningful challenge. The general rule for RPGs is that the more you risk, the greater your reward. If you take on an orange mob, you are rewarded by more xp and better loot then if you took on a green mob. Unfortunately, in SL, many players are finding the risk to be way too high for the reward they get. Also, many of the quests in SL feel like pointless tasks that, instead of offering a meaningful challenge, are something to finish as quickly as possible so players can get to the stuff they want to do. They are like the chores you make children do before you let them play, only with SLs, the chores take many hours and many require several friends to help them.

And combine both of these elements, and you have many people who aren't happy. People came to AO as an alternative to EverCamp but it feels like there has been a great increase in camping in AO. I enjoy the fast pace of AO where I can solo or team, get to the action quickly, and quickly fill hp and nanos between fights (I HATE the idea of sitting down and recovering health or nanos over time--this is NOT fun). Now, I spend all my time in RK and will only go to SLs to visit my apartment because I hear from all my high level friends about the impossibility of soloing in SL (except at lower levels), I can't get anywhere within a reasonable amount of time, and the forced downtime from things like coils.


But, just to show that not everything is negative, here is a list of the things I like about SLs (this list isn't complete, but you get the idea).
  • beautiful worlds
  • great looking new mobs
  • floating city = great idea
  • very nice new character faces
  • new GUI is a good improvement (still has issues and isn't finished, but I have high hopes)
  • nice new professions
  • despite its flaws, SL is still less broken then SWG


Remember, people may sound negative, but that's just because they are passionate about AO and they want what's best for it.

Making fish non-exploitable
The answer to the problem of teams shooting fish from the beach, and the fish can't fight back is quite simply to let the fish fight back with projectile weapons.

You only need to look at real life to know that fish can indeed be armed with projectile weapons. There is a certain type of fish that will shoot a stream of water at bugs on leaves overhanging water. The water knocks the bug into the water, and the fish eats it.

Therefore, the lakes and rivers need to be populated with poison-spitting fish that can shoot a stream of poison at people on land. Make it a BIG fish, and that stream will realistically travel a long ways, at least as far as any rifle or other projectile weapon.


Next, there is a real-life type of fish known as the mudcrawler which apparantly is able to work its way over land to cross into adjoining ponds and rivers. (It probably can't cross totally dry land, but it can handle marshes and beach areas.)

These fish are orignally from Asia, and so they are causing a great deal of trouble in the Midwest USA because of their pond-hopping abilities, and because there are no predators for them in North America.

Therefore, there is additional precedent for a type of fish that can occupy the shallow waters of Rubi-ka, and when fired on with ranged weapons, it can have the capacity to swim out of the water and squirm up onto the beach to attack people with razor-sharp fins and teeth.


So, the fish can return, and as you can see, they can be quite deadly, even for the land-roamers who blindly think they are safe standing on the beach with their trusty pistol....

Spitters and mudcrawlers should occupy the shallow waters that can be targeted from the beach. Big sharks with only teeth for melee should normally be restricted to deeper waters that cannot be fired on from the shore. The piranhas can probably be dropped from the game, since the Sharks can do the same duty as them in the deep waters.


This way people who fall into deep water from rhe Shadowlands (it can and should happen..) can be killed off quickly by the Great Notum Sharks, while people near the shore can get a run for their credits from the Leet Mudcrawlers and the Deranged Spitters..
