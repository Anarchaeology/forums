Fix The Slow Spawn Rates
FIX THE SPAWN RATES FOR CLAN!!

It's bad enough you have crap hunting for any faction level 100+, but as a Clanner I have to do missions starting at level 35. I hate doing missions, give me places to hunt like the Omni players have.

If the Omni advantage is Omni only armor/weapons and more credits for selling items and cheaper shop purchases, then make the Clan advantage better hunting areas. Currently the Omni have the hunting area advantage. The Clan have no advantage!!

Clan only items...go fly a kite!! I want the game to be fun to play, right now it is not fun

FunCom screwed it all up in patch 12.8. Before that patch Clan and Omni hunting was pretty equal, the hunting in The Temple area in GTC (Greater Tir County) could handle three teams at once, after 12.8 one team spends it's time trying running around to find a mob to kill.

The retarded thing here, is that FC put in all these OPs in out in GTC with 12.8, and then killed the spawn rates making these new OPs and their save terminals worthless. So FunCom's right hand does not seem to know what the left hand is doing, no suprise there.

The only OP that sees any action is the one near The Rocky Outrop, called Bot Mountain. This OP was supposed to serve both Bot Mountain and the Temple, but the Temple is a total joke since 12.8 and Bot Mountain is for characters up to level 30.

Lush Fields has more spawn areas then all Athen, Tir and Avalon put together.

So, it's been 7 months now and FC seems completely ignorant of the terrible hunting that Clan players have to put up with.

How am I supposed to train new members how to coordinate teams in PvP, when I don't have have any hunt/spawn areas to train them in???

Besides, hunting in the great outdoors is fun, missions are boring. And I see a real slow down in peeps on line, the game offers less and less fun, and more and more camping. Camping for missions, camping for mobs, camping for this that and the other thing.

If you dont believe me, then monitor the shouts in save areas, then check out the Clan and then the Omni "seeking team" channels. The Clan channel is all for missions, cause it's all we have. The Omni channels are alive with numerous hunting areas, in every city and surrounding area, and there are requests and missions too.

Patch 14.2, yeah oh boy, can't wait to see where I'm going to camp next for the new uber items. I want to have fun, not camp for crap.

I will leave this game for SWG if it does not address the no fun factor issues, and increase the spawn rates for the Clan.
