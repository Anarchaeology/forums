Message to the displeased
This may result in a load of flame, so be it.

<Disclaimer - this isnt directed at anyone in particular....its just my comments regarding the general reaction to the OE patch</disclaimer>

I'm been quiet on the whole OE patch, but my stand is basically. this is a good thing.

The people who dont want it are the people who this is designed to restrict (anyone see a pattern here?) therefore its no surprise they are objecting. Contructive debate is a good thing, but all I see left and right is 'we've been screwed again' 'I'll leave' etc etc. This is not constructive or helpful, and heres my reply to you all.

If you dont like the OE fix, do me a favour ask yourself the question "Am I acheving something in game, that is far beyond my level?" If your being truthful the answer will be yes. Funcom are actually doing you a favour. Dont believe me? Well if you keep on OE'ing, sooner or later (actually sooner) you'll hit Lvl200, be able to kill anything on the planet and be back at these boards *****ing that theres nothing to do, the games boring and Funcom has screwed you (again). By slowing you down they make you take time to progress through the game (like the rest of us), and along the way new content will be added to the game that you will be able to enjoy at the appropriate level (as opposed to being Lvl200 going to Jobe when its added and just killing everything 'for a laugh' or 'because your bored' and ruining the game for the people that want to go to Jobe to play the game)

If you think weight of opinion will change some things in this game, then try this: Start a post asking how many people would like to play this game for free (everyone like a freebie) and then ask Funcom to make the game free because "Thats what everyone wants". Aint gonna happen! Once you realise that just because you appear to have a lot of support for something, it doesnt follow you will get your own way, then life may be a little easier. The other thing most opponents of the OE patch dont appear to realise is that just because only a few have posted their support for the OE fix in the forum (those brave fellows), doesnt mean they are the only ones who support it! Since the change is going to happen, those that approve need do nothing but sit back and wait.

The fix is to restore the game balance so that the 'normal' players here can actually talk to the NPC's that you guys camp out and kill repeatedly for vast XP (do you think we're having fun while you do this! Do you even care? Thought not) and dont hit me with the "Well they should fix that" reply. Yes they should. But I bet when/if they do your the first in line *****ing about it being 'unfair' and will 'nerf my XP cashcow' etc. Perhaps you need to look at it another way. Rather than, what combination of implants/buffs etc will give me the max stats possible ever and then some! You need to now think. What combination of implants/buffs will give me the best stats possible for my level. The challenge is still there just a lower end result.

With regards to the "Its part of the game" line. Yes it may have been part of the game (thats probably open to debate), but hey do you like your character dying? I dont, but its in the game right? The game is constantly evolving and as such things will change (like more 25% zones, no more amnesty etc.). Also people make mistakes, and no amount of testing can simulate a live environment, and predict what other humans will do (which is part of the fun). Your not asked to like the changes, people more often dont like change in any form and you'll never please everyone, be greatful your invited to give your thoughts on the proposed changes (yes feedback has resulted in modifications being made so it is worth it) but you dont give any incentive to open the process up to debate if your rude to people.

I have a question that I dont expect a well reasoned and rational answer too (but it would be nice).

What problems do you people see with the effects of the OE patch that DO NOT revolve around being able to equip/use stuff well over your level? Are you saying you'll be whooped by stuff much lower than you? cant solo missions at 50%? what exactly?

People are more motivated to vocalise about something they dislike than something they agree with (how many protest rallies have you ever seen that have been in support of something? "Rah Rah Rah, We like burgers!" for no other reason than they do!) so in future before you write "Nobody wants blah" stop to think how many people have actually said that vs how many people actually play the game (remember Funcom know the answer to the last bit)!

Best thing you guys can do is accept it, its coming there nothing you can do about it. Get over it or leave, dont threaten, dont flame, vote with your wallet and leave.......still here? Good now behave. Thank you
