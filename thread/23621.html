Tank armor variations? Hover tank etc...
Hmm I noticed you're modifying Tank armor in the next patch, but I can't help but wondering if it wouldn't be cool to add in some variation in the their abilities in addition to, or perhaps in place of their protective capacity.

How about a high-end scouting tank-armor with a built-in hovercraft or built-in runspeed modifiers?

How about a short-lived emergency defensive screen?

How about a suit with built-in radar? (maybe adds some of those map upgrades).

Camoflague suit? Tank + cloaking device?

I'm sure you guys could come up with a lot more variations than just heavy, light, etc. I realize that with everything you give, you have to take something away. I'm not suggesting that you make tank armor all-powerful, but right now it's just kind of a big armored backpack. I wonder where the technology is.
