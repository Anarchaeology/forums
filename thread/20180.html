Doctor Heals Fall Short
I was stunned earlier to find out that that Doctors actually have worse team heals than the Trader Profession. Having played my Doc as a pure team healer up to lvl135, I am now feeling somewhat redundant except as +80 Treatment buffer on tap.

(putting aside Alpha and Omega which is almost useless during combat)

Take these top end team heals as comparisons:

Doctor Nano
Conglomerate Health Plan
Heals 485-970
Attack Delay 3.29 secs
Recharge Delay 4.88 secs
Biological Metamorphose 856
Material Metamorphose 772

Trader Nano
Premium Delayed Health Payment
Heals 920-1144
Attack Delay 2.8 secs
Recharge Delay 3.8 secs
Biological Metamorphose 862
Material Metamorphose 777

As you can see, these take roughly the same skill base to cast however the Trader team heal:
a) Heals your team MUCH more than your best team heal does
b) Is faster to cast (negligible with a good nano ini skill)
c) Recharges over a second faster!

For 135 levels I have been under the impression that Doctors were the best healer profession....seems I have been deluded.

I am still stunned and wonder what Funcom where thinking!
Is there any hope in the future of Docs ever getting better heal nanos?

Please enlighten me if you have any information or add your comments if you are as equally stunned.
