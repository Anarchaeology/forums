Hi,

The South Rubi-Ka Society is a guild dedicated to roleplaying. We run table-top style rolepaying adventures for our members, in-character events as well as ooc events like the clandestine "Leet fights" (under the banner of the WLFF - World Leet Fighting Federation). We are active in as much as we do (from our perspective) interesting things. We are not all that active in that we don't do these things every day. Each event is a lot of work and AO is after all a hobby not a job.

Note, our player base is mostly European. That too might colour your impression of how active the guild is.


Anyway if you want more info you can check out our site
http://rubika.no-ip.org/srs

Alternatively check out this thread for a non-comprehensive list of
roleplaying orientated guilds.
