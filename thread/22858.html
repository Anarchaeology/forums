assist key
You can sort of do it, just kinda backwards

create an assist key: /macro assist /assist %t

1) target the person you want to assist (from the team list if they are out pulling)
2) when you want to attack, press the assist key
3) press Q (attack)

I know many people already use this but I posted it in case anyone wasn't aware of it.
