Backpack changes
Backpacks.

We asked for 5 or 6 colors of small backpacks, that's all. Just so we could sort our inventory.

I go on Test, and what do we got? 15 or more new "types" of LARGE backpacks, each of which has a req of "Adventuring 200", and most of which offer some trivial +5 or +10 bonus to something. The prices range from ~15K to ~65K.

(/me bangs head on table)

These things are ridiculously expensive.

The reason they are expensive is because they give bonuses, however paltry.

The bonuses are worthless, because nobody is going to waste the IP to boost an utterly useless skill like Adventuring clear up to 200.

So my question is still WHY???

Yes, they will work to organize your bank account, if you want to spend a half million on them.

Why didn't we get what we ^&%$#@&*%!!-well asked for, reasonably inexpensive small backpacks in a half-dozen or so colors?

The colored small backpacks would have been completely and absolutely neutral in their effect on the game. Gods only know what bugs and exploits Funcom have introduced with this crap.
