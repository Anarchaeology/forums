Strange happenings in NW Mines?
Today (June 8th), many people were out hunting in the Northwest Mines area- a common hunting spot in Lush Hills. Today, however, hunters experienced numerous 'red' sandstorms and many claim that the amount of Fatesealers, Chaos Minions, and Lifebleeders had increased. These particular creatures are extremely dangerous, and I have personally witnessed a Minion wiping out an entire team of 6 in a matter of seconds. One of the highlights was when at least two, if not three Minions wandered up to the cave, where almost 20 unlucky people were waiting. Some escaped into the cave, others tried to battle the Minions and died. Frightened hunters were forced to hide in the cave until a more experienced person came over and killed them (The Minions).

Throughout the rest of today, many Lifebleeders, Chaos Minions and even Fatesealers (An uncommon sight in the NW Mines area) have been roaming the fields near the 'Mines, wiping out many a player. What could be the cause of this?
