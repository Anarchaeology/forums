Bring our boy back home! (Black Reet Down part II)
A vigil will be held on

Sunday 30th at 20:00 GMT in OMni HQ (RK1)

in honor of the missing Omni pilot Christian Furuseth who is currently being held in a Clan prison in Athen.

First a little background.

On October 30th lieutenant Christian Furuseth's plane was shot down by clan forces over Stret West Bank in an incident the media has dubbed .Black Reet Down . An Omni-Tek rescue party was mounted, but by they time the got to the crash site, clan forces had already captured lieutenant Furuseth and taken back to Athen.

On November 16th, after two weeks in of silence, with the clans neither confirming nor denying holding Furuseth and no information whether he were even alive, Omni-Tek forces mounted a daring raid on Athen in order to free him. They did not succeed.

A month since the original Black Reet Down incident I would like to invite all Omni citizens to spare a few minutes, come to Omni HQ where together will honor the bravery of this pilot and show our solidarity to his grieving mother and young wife.

Please bring a lava lamp with you, it will be used at the key moment of this vigil. And if you wish to say a few words at the event, you are most welcome.

Many thanks,

Savoy
