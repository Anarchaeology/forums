October 12th
On October 12th 29209 the valient defenders of Omni-2 were slain by the clan uprising. We are fast approaching the anniversary of that fateful day. As I look back on the recent events, I see the Clan members attempting to restore Omni-2 to its former glory, and Omni-Tek laying claims to Jobe, the Shadowlands, and more...

Yet what are we, as Omni-Tek employees doing to remember those brave souls who fought and died for us? Yes, this is perhaps "ancient" history to some, but for some of us, it is important to remember our past.

In 10 days, we as Omni-Tek employees have to look at what the Clans have done with our once beautiful city, and wonder: What have they done to it? The city is still rubble. The clans have failed in their attempts to clean it up. What does this say about the clans? What does this say about Omni-Tek that we have remained so quiet about this for so long?

Perhaps I am a sentimentalist, or just stuck in the past. Either or, I am holding a personal vigil in remembrance of the fallen. 10 days from now, I will be remembering the loss. Will you?
