Possible Alternative
I have two suggestions.

1. The Clan population shouldn't have to belong to a guild in order to receive any type of advantage. The clan's entire belief structure is based on their desire for independence, so forcing them to belong to an organization of any type is tantamount to Omni membership anyway.

2. The common theme for this thread seems to be attaching any sort of advantage to skill development. My suggestion would be to tie the advantage to missioning. The current system allows for bonuses through token accumulation. Perhaps this could be expanded by either making these bonusus juicier or allow more frequent bonuses for the clansman as he gains these tokens. Alternately, perhaps that as a clansman reaches his/her next title level he/she would receive greater rewards in this manner. This would eliminate any bias towards a particular profession and at the same time encourage the individual to level faster & play more often.

Just a thought.

Are we getting somewhere ? I think so.
If I summarize a bit the different views that are expressed here it's plain and obvious that CLAN needs now distinctive advantages. But these adavantages cannot not be superior to OT ones.

OT monetary advantage and Elite do exist. They are limited but should not be discarded. They have an impact in the game. This impact is not what will make a claner loose in PvP vs. an omni, but still, ask someone if he's ready to have 5% more money and check his answer. The few people above lvl 150 that don't need money anymore are not really in the position to say if monetary advantage is good or not. Unless they remember what being lvl 80 was, when you needed to buy from players Q150 equipment and when 50k did make a difference.

My point is : finalize OT advantages and create CLAN adavantages.

I will try to develop these two things now...

Some ideas where expressed an it seems that tradeskills CLAN advantages could be very interesting from both a RPG / economical point of view. And if we want to be consistent it's quite obvious that the OT monetary advantage must prevail. As a matter of fact the Star Wars analogy is very transposable to AO.

But OT monetary advanatge cannot stand alone. Specialised armors for other profession should be added too : Elite Medical Corp. Tunics - Elite Priesthood Robes - Elite Scout Armors - Elite CEO Board Suits - etc ...

Hence the STR/STA armors advantage will be extended to other OT divisions, inforcing by the way a dressing policy : omni will tend to look all the same as it should be in such a mega-company.

On CLAN side, getting new armors by unique tradeskills based on scavenging monster parts, will add by opposition a lot of diversity. The CLAN tradeskills armors should not be organized in full suits, but as patchwork parts of assembled stuff collected and re-ingenered from mutants / cyborgs / exoskeletons etc ... They could also die their armors or coats to be a bit more distinctive.

I am talking about armors because we already have the example of the OT Pol, but this can and should be applied to weapons too.

This view of OT / CLAN advantages could be balanced by FC and be good to players :

- OT get a bit more money and can buy / mission unique suits of divison specific armors and weapons (stop the OT stuff poping in CLAN missions please).

- CLAN can make many different armor parts and items by investing IPs in unique tradeskills.

I all this seems fair and well, still some decisions have to be taken so that players cannot get the best of both worlds. I think that we don't want to see someone switching side each week to creat a new rhinoman helmet, die it green and then switch to OT to get his new OT Elite Medic Corp sleeves, and switch back again to CLAN for some specific missions.

Having a more rigid way of preventing this is unavoidable. This can be easily done by :

- Allowing players to switch side ONCE per character. No more.

- Preventing the use of OT armor by claners or CLAN armor by omnis, by making the guard shoot players wearing illegal stuff. Or just forbidding the use of side specific items by the other side.

Story events as the truce we are all living now, can of course change these rules. But it's just like an IP-wipe : it's not something that will appear every month, it's a unique and very tremendous event in the life of Rubi-Ka citizens.

Now we are talking business.

Bottom line : when someone will create a character make him think twice at the side he will want to follow, because he will have unique advantages and because he will take a long-term allegiance.
