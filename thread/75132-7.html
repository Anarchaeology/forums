QL33 BBI Faithful
MR 156
dmg 6-95(49) - average dmg=50.5 (not including crits)
atk 2.00
rech 2.00
(gives an average base damage per second (dps) of 12.63)
(if you hit the max every time then max dps would be 23.75)

-------------------------

QL33 Kaen-Archibald Kolt
MR 101
dmg 1-62(62) - average dmg=31.5 (not including crits)
atk 1.50
rech 1.10
(avg dps=12.12)
(max dps=23.85)

The Faithful does more dmg per shot and slightly higher dps average. the K-A Kolt shoots much faster, has a higer max dps, has a higher crit, and has a much lower MR requirement. I find the highest QL K-A Kolt I can squeeze into my right hand, and the MR is so low that I fit the same thing in my left hand.

The only real drawback to the Kolt is if you don't like a fast weapon because of damage shields. The K-A Kolt also uses flingshot, is adv only, can be had from solo missions (I blitz a pair every few levels).

The adv is not supposed to do as much dmg as an enforcer, or be able to survive as well as an enforcer, or do as much damage as an ma. The adv is less focused than all the other professions on purpose. The adv is the only healing profession that has a dmg shield; the only healing profession that has ac buff (ma heals suck so bad, they don't count). In fact, adv has ac, ds, shield (self only cyclic line) and heal, so adv is a very defensive profession and so has weaker offense. Seems pretty balanced to me. If you want to be a butt kicking dude, be a soldier, don't expect your adv to be one.

The real solution, though, is to get rid of the synch period of attacks, make the main and offhand attack cycles independent, and lower the MR reqs to a somewhat sane (though still high if you insist) level. FC seems to be obsessed with the QL thing. Just modify the effectiveness as a whole, and QL stops being an issue. In any RPG (except for AO, I guess), the offhand weapon is effected by 1) hitting less frequently and 2) hitting for less damage.
