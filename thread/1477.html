Trimmers are one of the few things in this game that isn't bugged.

And yes, the effects aren't really obvious, but they are there, I've tried out tons of different ones. Try out a divert energy to hitpoints if you want to know the actual increase in numbers.

As for the positive agg trimmer, the max QL of those is 100. They switch the bot to the far right of the slider. Thus, 1 QL = 1% AGG.

The minimum QL for the buildable trimmers is 30. This is just hinted in the manual, not with an actual number though. So, NEVER buy a casing under QL30. And they make a difference.

You can only use trimmer of each kind at the same time. Using a new one either don't work until the timer (some has a 5 minute timer) runs out, or it will overwrite the old setting (changing the AGG/DEF to another value).


Higher QL make a greater difference.
