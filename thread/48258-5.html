Ok, again YOU ARE NOT THE ONLY PERSON PLAYING THIS GAME! Yes, it's nice that you're computer can handle the mess and I'm sure we would all like top of the line machines. Not everyone can afford that, and it is very self-centered of you to assume that people can just upgrade their computers on a whim when they may have real life concerns that take money. Say food, car payments, ISP payments, phone and utilities, credit card debt, insurance, rent or mortgage, just to name a few.

As for mission lag, let me explain this to you all. One, in a mission, there are 6, and only 6, players usually. In an outdoor field, there can be around three to four times as many. The server has to constantly update your computer for all these players. Players tend to run, which causes more updating as they come in and out of you sight range faster. Secondly, and mission has walls, something that is very easy for the computer to compute for and render. An out door playfield has no walls, but each individual tree and bush (I know we've all been hung up on bushes we thought we could walk over) has to be kept track of. There position, relative to you, changes as you move. Sometimes there is ground gradient (i.e. hills and mountains), some of which is unclimable. The computer has to keep information on ALL of this too and update and render it, as well as determine how you interact with it. It is not at all the same like missions tend to be. Also, your visual range is farther in outdoor fields, which cause the computer to have to render and update even more than it would in a mission, which sight is normally blocked by walls, so rooms only need to be rendered one at a time normally (ever wonder why some people say they experience lag when a door opens?). Third, mobs are organized well in missions, one or two per room. They computer rarely has to render more than 4, the others it just keeps track of. In an outdoor field there can be at least a dozens mobs being rendered, the guards included. It has to keep track of each mob, even those just outside your character's sight (every tag a mob, have it disappear out of sight and follow it, then run that way and find it again?). Lastly, when combat breaks out, a whole NEW set of factors comes into play. Not only does the machine have to render combat with weapons fire and nano effects, but ALL machines need to be updated for battle effects (i.e. health/nano pool).

So yes, outdoor lag can occur on machines that don't experience lag in missions.
