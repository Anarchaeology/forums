I'm in no mood to post anything serious, so here's something from out in left field that made me laugh after I was done...

The End of Conflict, the Age of the Shiny
A bunch of high profile omni's contacted a group of high profile clanners in a desparate attempt to keep omni's nano-heroin business running. The clanners, unfortunately, had no nano-heroin supply left and offered notum-weed instead. Having never used notum-weed, the omni's were intrigued and requested a sample.

So there they were...the most powerful of all omnis & clanners, sitting in the Tir arena armed with 3 foot notumbongs and 10 yalms full of the highest quality notum-weed. As the sampling turned into a full fledged Cheech & Chong session, the two sides decided that war was stupid and that we should all use notum-weed as a means of controlling the respective societies.

Soon thereafter, both omni & clan announced to their citizens that the war over the land was over and that omni & clan would join forces to prevent notum-weed from going extinct....

This came as a total shock to the citizens, who were infact the ones who had stolen and consumed what was left of the nano-heroin supply. The side effects of nano-heroin were well-known throughout the high profile society of Rubi-Ka: abnormal changes to certain parts of the brain resulting in short tempers, extreme desires for violence, and a shrinking of the reproductive organs (Atrox citizens showed no signs of that particular symptom however). What was unknown to the leaders was the fact that the dosages of nano-heroin that had been given to citizens was actually 1/3rd of what was really being consumed.

However, the lack of knowledge was short-lived and it was only a matter of time before notum-weed was forced into the neural pathways of every citizen on Rubi-Ka, stemming the ferocious appetites for violence. Nowadays, if you see clanners in omni territory or vice versa, do not be afraid. There is no hostility..there is no conflict. We are all shiny happy people and we are supposed to be holding hands, so they say.
