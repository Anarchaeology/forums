Australian and Asian Players wanted
Division 3: Omni Reclamation is seeking players from Australia and Asia to become part of a guild dedicated to running events on RK2 in line with the official storyline. Currently we have a playerbase in the US and Europe that have been involved in staging events. We would like to build up our Australian and Asian playerbase to enable us to do the same, at a time convenient for these players.

Also, if you are a Clan player dont despair! Project Revenant also has a Clan guild known as Vox Populi (Voice of the People) dedicated to the same goal of creating player run events.

If you are interested in joining Division 3 or Vox Populi please visit the website ( www.projectrevenant.com ) check out the charters and regulations, and leave a message on the recruitment forums. For Division 3 you can /tell Major Paz "Ripsnorta" Timas (Director of Asian Affairs) in game, or for Vox Populi /tell Buford "Zydrik" Delenick.

Make a difference in RK2. Join Project Revenant and help shape the course of history on Rubi Ka.
