Pistols generally suck.
With current game mechanics....fast, low damage weapons are useless past level 50.


Armor class subtracts an amount of damage per hit (not sure of the equation).

An example....

We have a pistol user and a Shotgun user.
- Pistols have twice the firing rate of the shotgun but do half the damage. Pistol will do max 150 damage every 2.5 secs and Shotgun does max 300 every 5.

Hypothetical weapon stats:
Pistol: 1-150 1.25/1.25
Shotgun : 1-300 2.5/2.5

Lets assume that each weapon will do averange damage every shot...75 and 150.

If a mobs armor class absorbs 50 damage with each hit we get the following damage rating numbers.

Pistol - 600 Damage per minute.
Shotgun - 1200 Damage per minute.


Because of the low damage rating on pistols, they frequently hit for minimum damage on high AC mobs.
