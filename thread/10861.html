Neutral Guild Conference
Greetings,

We are putting together a conference of all neutral guild leaders.
The forum will be that each guild send a rep (preferable guild leader) and will have 10mins to talk about his/her guilds missions, goals and objectives. All neutral guilds are invited (new or old). Conference will get logged and placed on message boards and news.

Date : Saturday Feb 16th 2002
Time: 4:00pm central standard time
Loc: Reet Retreat (1st floor at fire place room)

This is not a debate, this is a forum to promote awareness of different points of views and goals. After meeting guild leaders may if meeting goes well, talk about another conference to debate, promote the neutral cause and ratify treaties. Hope to see you there.

Sincerely Sumokan Pres of Elite Operations.
