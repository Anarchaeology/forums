6 QL200 NCUs = 384 NCU.... which leaves us with 9 NCU free.

Then add in that the MP combat nanos have amongst the shortest ranges of any nano in the game. NSD = 14 metres. Even the nukes are only 20 metres whereas the majority of NT nukes are in the 25 to 35 metre range and the Crat nuke has 25 m range... and you'll understand that a PvP MP would like to have the Range Increaser.

Then as mentioned above, the MP has very high nanocost on his nanos, so we'd really like to use the CPU upgrade.

And then with the very slow attack debuffs (NSD 9,3s/Dominates 6s), the MP would prefer to use the Recompiler of course...

And you begin to see that the effective MP has very little NCU space for outside buffs at all - if any. This is actually another part of the issue for MPs and is being addressed in wishlist threads talking about ways of reducing the NCU footprint of the MP nanoskills debuffs somewhat.

Sacrifices should be made of course to gain advantages, but the MPs certainly feel that something should be done with regard to improving their surviveability in PvP and that we have one of the strongest cases in terms of need right now.
