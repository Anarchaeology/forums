It's ridiculous that a character can get buffs to wear armor or a weapon, but then can't take them off because they won't be able to reequip it with the lower non-buffed abilities. As a result, fewer players wear non-combat clothing and this makes the game less visually pleasing.

Not allowing buffing of lower lvl characters isn't the answer, because that is one of the perks of higher lvl players: being able to use their nanos to benefit the less powerful. And it shows initiative in the lower lvl characters that they seek the power of the mighty, and separates the lazy players from those seeking to be stronger than an average character of their lvl.

A solution would be to implement code that would allow a player to reequip a weapon or piece of armor even if they no longer have the attributes to wear it. Once they get buffed and wear an item it could be "tagged" so that player will always be able to equip the item.
