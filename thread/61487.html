Lazy, sloppy, or otherwise?
In my profession, when the designers release a patch without providing release notes, we question them if they are lazy or sloppy. I do not expect that funcom developers are guilty of either of these things. I do not think they would make something without direction or be able to express what they have done. Lacking release notes the testers are set adrift without knowing what to test. Lacking release notes, the rest of the downstream groups are unaware of what has gone on. While that information may be understood within the design community, its an assumtion that never survives too much probing. The designers are doing a good job here, and we like the stuff they are putting out. I am sure that they are neither lazy or sloppy, and that failing to provide good release notes to their has just been a failure in the communication process. I would hate to think that the design community is spending more time saying its someone elses responsibility to do this work, and just make sure it is done. These release notes do form a part of the whole package that some of your customers really enjoy.

So, could some responsible, competant person in FC make sure that we get these notes?
