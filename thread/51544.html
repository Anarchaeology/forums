Bows and init...
Bows use melee init, as far as I've gathered. Why?
It is a ranged weapon, right?
Then it should have ranged init, right?

Wrong. Performing a bow attack requires some movements that are not unlike melee movements (move a whole lot of arms to put the arrow onto the bow, draw the string back, and let go. Lots of physical movement.

As for "normal" ranged init, it all comes down to aiming, and then pulling a trigger.

Difference.

Bow should have melee init.

Am I right?
Or am I wrong?
