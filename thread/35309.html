Got my first mission in Tir, something about killing Emil Grosland for being a spy. I'm not sure what to do next. Do I just hunt around Tir looking for this guy?
No, here are the steps that you should take:
1.) Ctrl-4 to bring up the mission tab.
2.) Right-click on the mission icon and hold-drag your mouse over the second icon. This will upload mission coords. to your map reader.
3.) Ctrl-6 to open your mini map.
4.) Stand still and slowly turn around. Watch for a thing yellow line that is on your compass. When the yellow line reached the center of your compass, that is, it is between the two red dots on your compass, stop rotating.
5.) Move your mouse to your mini map and drag the map to reveal the direction you are facing. Note where a white X appears on the mini map and the shortest routes that lead you to there from your current location.
6.) Get there and enter whatever door the yellow line is pointing to.
7.) Start killing NPCs left, right, and center.
8.) Find your mission target and killed it/him/her.
9.) Collect your reward and go around and pop any and all un-opened treasure chests.
10.) Leave the mission area.

Steps below are for any misguided newbies who confused freedom fighters with the clanner scums.

11.) Goto a basic supermaket and buy yourself a low level Omni-Tek application form with the money you have earned from the mission.
12.) Use the whom-pa to reach Andromeda.
13.) Right click on the Omni-Tek application form to switch your alignment to the Mega Corporation.
14.) Use the whom-pa in ICC HQ in Andromeda to reach Omni-1.
15.) Enjoy the benefits of the Mega Corporation!

Remember, Omni-Tek is your friend .
