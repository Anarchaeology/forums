*Party* The GSP GrifterBash-Fri Feb 21
Just when you thought it was safe to listen to that same old rock...

THE GSP FRIDAY NIGHT GRIFTERBASH

Tonight, Friday, the 21st of February, we will be rocking out to the funkiest metal, rock, and techno this side of the digital universe.

Tarryk of GridStream Productions will be located above the GSP Lounge at the glass dance floor at Reet's Retreat on the Atlantean server starting at 8pm EST / 1am GMT tonight!

I will be making attempt after attempt to destroy the ears and faces of all bagel-eating yxxil-phearing listeners out there for the duration of the night with various genres of music including metal, techno, industrial, grindcore, and rockabilly.

"Rockabilly", you ask? Try Gothabilly. I'm talking about the hardest-edge band ever to take the music genres of rock, metal, gothic, and funk, mix them together, add some radioactive material, and throw it in your face. They are called Eight Ball Grifter, and there will be a full tribute to them on tonight's show (goddess willing that I get the CD's in time to prep, that is).

The Grifter is born of my home town in Lansing, Michigan, and I know from first-hand experience on many occasions: These guys rock both live and in the studio. You will be shown the way of the Grifter.

Tune in tonight, whether or not you show up to Reets! If you've never tuned in, head to http://www.gridstream.com and rtfm, y0!
