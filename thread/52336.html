Supa Leet
Ganking is jumping someone already engaged in a one on one duel with another opponent, or has just finished that duel and is moving out to heal themselves. Period.

Ganking carries a negative conotation in that it implies the person being attacked is being done so without consent.

Attacking someone just standing in the arena or anywhere else is not ganking. It may not be polite, but its not ganking. If you stand around in an arena, you are consenting simply by that action.

Attacking someone in a warzone one on one, or a 100 on 1, or even if they're dumb enough to be in a yalmaha is not ganking. The moment you enter a political zone, you are in fact consenting to be attacked.

Jynne
Supa Leet
The non-duellist that I am, I consider the definition of ganking to be a matter of motive. To me, ganking is the intentional killing of people who are unable to reasonably defend themselves from you, either in the arena or in the field, with no motive beyond getting easy and safe title points or an ego boost.

It doesn't matter how or why the person isn't able to defend themselves - you could attack them five-on-one, they could be linkdead, they could be hurt from a previous fight, they could be in another fight at the time, they could be under zone loading lag still when their grace period expires, etc. What matters under my definition is that you're killing someone who doesn't have a chance, just because you can.
