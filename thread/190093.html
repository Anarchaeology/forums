Trial and Error
Eventually in ones life, they encounter a split in their path. The two diverge so oppositely that once gone down, you can never come back. It is at this moment, this instant, that one must make a choice that forever will alter their future and destiny ~ From the Handbook of the Atanis

Calm yourself Atani you are fine now. The voice came from inside the room. Lem turned, but she could not see. My eyes they are closed but I cant open them? Why? Slowly she tried once again to open her eyes, all around her seemed as a bright blur, the eyes were not adjusting. I said be calm, you are fine. That voice again! So clear! But how, where, what spoke its voice? The eyes began to re-adjust finally, the room coming into slight focus. I am here, Atani, do not worry.

Lems voice spoke but the sound did not come from her lips. Nothing was heard at all in fact in the room. The words she spoke came from inside her mind, her thoughts responding to this unknown voice. Who are you? Where am I?

The confusion over-whelmed her. The eyes came more and more into focus, why did they hurt so much? As I told you Atani. Or you might not remember, I must admit I never had to do what I did to any other so it is possible your mind could not comprehend it.

Remember remember what?

Here, let me show you the truth.

The room began to swim in circles out of focus once again. In its place, images, one after the other. She saw pictures of her reporting, her arrests, her now entrapment into the depths of the sewers then a swirling ball of light. The light came into her, she absorbed it. She became it and it became her. Words began to flood her mind. Broken promises, lies, truths, vows, then the conversation with this figure. It said its name was Lume. He said he was on a mission to hunt a renegade spirit. He said him and his people have been since the beginning of the foundation of the human-race, that which they call the Atani, been watching and care-taking for. They gave up their human bodies to become free-formed spirits, with the one negative being that could not appear in physical form unless freely given room in a body. In return, he promised her knowledge, and help in her endeavours. She agreed. Then a sudden flash of light blinded her, when her eyes re-focused again it was upon this room she now dwelled: a mattress in the corner, a wooden sofa-like bench one floor up. Where am I?

You are at your new home, an apartment in a facility called Jobe. Jobe that name is familiar but from where? Yes, a long time ago, some scientists leaving to form a city in the sky. I had done a report on that linking it to numerous disappearances, strange occurrences Yes, a wonderful job Atani. However, your report was not true. The SOL Corporation has not yet invaded Rubi-Ka, and nor is anyone in league to conspire against Omni-Tek that *I* know of at least.

Then her thoughts stopped suddenly as she reached her hand up to her face. My hands! My body what happened?! The shock hit her. The skin was no longer gray, more of a bit white with some strands running down her skin. Her hair, was no longer smooth on the top with two edges sticking out from her ears, but now spiky leaning to her right.

Well Atani, that is not easy to explain. Your race was foreign to me, since it was made from bio-genetical research. I had to revert back your DNA sequencing to that which is familiar to me, thus the altered genes in your opifex body now turned you into a solitus.

Impossible! That is just impossible!

To you it is yes. But how can you doubt the truth that lay in front of your eyes? That is the body of a solitus teenager, not an opifex one.

Dont call me that!

Atani? It is our name for your race, that is all.

No I meant a teenager! I am fifteen! And I have a name, its Jacelyn. Or Lem, as I am nicked.

Fifteen? To my kind, that is still a baby A alright then. Jase, will that do?

Yes that will do I guess. Lem smiled.

We must do something about that temper young one. I read your mind while you were on the floor recovering. The time? It has been two weeks since your transformation. Two weeks?! You have been through much but you have no one. That needs to change.

Look Lume, Ive lived many years all by myself and I have had never a problem with it.

Shut up Jase, whenever something happened before Loria made sure you never got in trouble, remember? Loria an old name. A care-taker when her parents died five years ago. He taught her to fight when needed, though nothing in great detail. Her job with the secret ops of the Immediate Solution (IS) was mostly to forage information, to get into places an adult would not be able to without risk of getting caught. Yes, she realized, many times she did get caught but due to her under-aged status and Lorias puppet-mastery over the Juvenile Courts of Omni-Tek was never in any trouble. But now, yes, Lume is right. Now, I dont have Lorias influence. Now, I just have myself Yes, this means one of either three things Jase. One would be a Juvenile Detention Center. Two would be to be sent to an orphanage. Three would be to be sent to a boarding school. I also believe youve already been in trouble with the Pols have you not?

Yes a bit the realization of the truth struck like a hammer. Un-protected, anyone could claim guardianship of her and then send her off with a snap of their fingers.

What you need is those with a little influence and those who care. I can help you Jase, but not in the way you need. I cannot stand up for you in a court, or protect you.

Lem thought for a moment before responding. She didnt like where this was heading, but she knew it was heading only one way. What do you recommend?

You know what I recommend Jase. You need to goto those who know you and make them your friends. Even more so, allow them to be involved in your life somehow. But you need someone who can defend you if worse comes to worse.

I dont need no guardians Lume. I do fine by myself

You can lie to me but not to yourself. I would suggest you start with three people. First, seek out those who helped you before, Markarian and Dinnen. Helped before? Oh yes thats right she found herself re-constructing the memories lost to her from before. A traumatic experience a Dark Angel wait You need to learn to trust me! I did not restore that memory for a reason!

But the memory flooded into her mind. Using Lumes power she saw pain, horrible pain. Needles, long strands of iron, piercing her flesh. This Dark Angel He is going to pay

You see! This is the reason you need someone! Look, I will help you with this Angel, but first you must promise me to do what I advise. Got it?

Alright already. Ill trust your thirty-thousand year old wisdom

Dont get sarcastic please with me Jase. And its longer than that, but any how seek out Markarian, Dinnen, and Matusz. They can help.

Out of these three, Lem wasnt looking forward to see either one of them. Markarian had helped her when she was poisoned by one named Clyburn to only be re-paid by getting into a fight with her friend and fellow employee, Dinnen, shortly after. Lastly, Matusz, the father of her now-little sister a problem she herself had a hand in causing. Though, to fight against Lume did not seem the wisest thing. Better to go along for now he can be useful Very well, I shall then. First Markarian, then Dinnen, and then Matusz. Will this make you happy Lume?

Very happy young Jase. I shall be residing within the corners of her mind, waiting, till then or till you need me. Just speak loudly and I shall hear

Great a date with the three people whom she has had the most trouble with this would be an interesting week
