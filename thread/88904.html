Greetings from Rompa Bar (and other stuff that's on my mind)
As the new Director of Vixen Entertainment, I wish to take this opportunity to say hello and welcome to all the new faces Ive seen in the bar recently. Ive enjoyed serving you and hope you all make a point to stop by frequently. (The old ones too! )

I also want to express my sorrow for Aktrez being so burdened as to feel the need to take leave of our world and pass her director-ship to me. Thankfully though, we will continue to work closely together in other endeavors. (I really don't want to entertain reasons of her personal decisions, please.) If she pursues an interest in SWG, I hope to offer help in any way needed. In her absence however, I proudly accept her leadership duties and hope to live up to everyones high expectations and to serve Mr. Throll with the quality standards he has come to expect and rely on from Vixen Entertainment. I also intend to strive to be a respected leader to our wonderful staff members.

And now for some clarity  In Aktrezs farewell post she expressed hopes that I will be strong enough to fight the wars without her. Aktrez had many stressors on our planet, some of which were specific individuals determined to give her a hard time. I attribute this to personality conflicts. With her decision to back away and focus on other ventures, ie Sims, I do not feel as I am left to fight a war. Most of you all know me and know I have been a part of Rompa Bar for a very long time (10 months to be exact.) In that time every single one of you has been nothing short of kind and supportive to me. I have never had an unpleasant confrontation with anyone. My desire now, as has been the primary principle VE has tried to live by, is to continue to serve our community and provide the comfortable atmosphere you have all grown to expect and appreciate. Our goal is to ensure everyone enjoys peace and camaraderie each time they enter the doors of Rompa Bar. I truly feel that the only war that exists is the one our forces bravely battle against the rebels (no offense to my Clan friends ) If anyone is interested in fighting, I urge you to lend your services where they are gravely needed  with the men and women of all breeds who fight for our existence everyday. (I am pleased to add that my fiancé, Neboja, has done just that, and now battles the scum of our society as an Omni Pol officer to ensure we all live in safety. I am very proud of him.)

Next, for a couple of weeks now, many people have come to me and voiced concerns and disgust over several new groups who have chosen to peddle their wares in the bar. I assure you every step is being taken to preserve the integrity of the environment we all enjoy at Rompas. It is not my policy to be confrontational to any group with honorable intentions, nor do I harass another group for simply being there, but please know that any complaint or concern will be taken seriously and looked into by me and reported to Throll, as per my obligation to him. And to reinterate what I've said at least a million times this week alone- Throll has the final say on who is welcome to work in the bar, not me nor any other VE member. If any VE member is seen harassing members of another org, please let me know. (* for the record, I am not against two consenting adults cybering wherever they choose and where discretion is observed. However, I do not support guilds who's purpose is to cyber for credits and publicly offer their services in the bar. Theres a big difference in this statement and the ones I see everyone up in arms about.)

Because the complaints Ive been receiving have been so numerous, I offer this suggestion to anyone who finds offense with any group, including VE  to combat undesirable activities, 1) Share your concerns with that groups leader (as the actions may be limited to the individual in question and not the actual policy of the guild itself), and 2) Refuse to support their services. I will always be available for anyone having issues concerning the bar and with VE, good or bad, and will always be open to suggestions on how to better serve you and our beloved community.

Lastly, I want to make sure to send a special thank you for all the kind words and support I've received from our friends in Devils Advocate, Kaviera Family Mafia, Gridstream, and Omni Pol. Thanks guys!


From myself and the entire VE staff  Blessings be plentiful to you all.

P.S. I promise future posts will not be this long
