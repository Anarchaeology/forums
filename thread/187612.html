Case File: 001-01 Incident Report Location Rome Blue.
Date: 051003

Time: 23:23 RST

Location: Rome Blue Traders Bar Superior shop

Incident: On or about 23:15, Shots where Energy weapons blasts where heard in the location of the Traders bar Superior Shop Rome blue. the on Duty Omni-Tek Bartender was found murdered 10 Minutes Later. Request: A Formal Request of Investigation and Witness questioning is being submitted through proper channels.
