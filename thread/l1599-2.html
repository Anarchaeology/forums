It's morning, so what the heck
I'll take another stab at it and reason with the intelligent PVP community (Mor, you can decide for yourself if you are one of them). Think about the time involved in coding a whole zone. Then think about how huge AO zones are supposed to be. Then think about having to track someone down through the zone (which we don't even know is possible). Fact is, a truly FFA zone wouldn't get many people.

A lot of the PVP community is saying "Oh yeah, I would go there and play!" But really, would you? If some lvl 200 was camped out in there killing every person that walked across the zone line, would you really try and hang out there with your lvl 35 Soldier? Of course not... after all, you'd just keep dying. So you'd what, call in your level 200 friends? Ok, say you have some... they manage to kill the guy. What do they do now? Well, I guess THEY start killing everyone who zones in.

Here's what I wouldn't mind seeing (Mor, you MIGHT even like this idea). A Mayhem zone where level limits only applied to your alignment. I already have a problem with Omni going out and shooting other Omni. Its just weak. So why not make it a mayhem zone where you CAN'T kill you own alignment, but CAN kill everyone of opposing alignments, reagardless of level?

Now, there's still some reasons against it. From an RP standpoint, it makes little to no sense. But what he heck, we're apparently ignoring RP motivations with this thread. From a coding standpoint it might be difficult to accomplish, but I bet not. And one side could end up "owning" the zone for months on end, but that's a risk that would have to be taken.

And you know what? I bet the place would still be pretty much empty. True lovers of PVP aren't as common as some seem to think. They just like to post a lot. Most people are like me... we don't mind the PVP retrictions in the Mayhem zones because we understand that it is a personal risk when entering them.

The truly amusing thing about this argument is that it has raged for over a year, and people think they are still making new points that Funcom hasn't heard. It's gone all the way from "I hate PVP, why do we even have it" to this extreme. In fact, about the only other argument to come close to it's length was the permadeath argument (and please, let's not ressurect that nightmare). Nothing ever gets decided, and no one seems to ever be converted. We remain in our 3 different camps (no PVP, restricted PVP, and FFA PVP) and continue to argue it. Guess we just need something to do.

Prof-Xeen
No restrictions SOUNDS great in theory...
But the fact is that the in the MMORPG community, there are quite a few players who don't have the maturity to handle it. It has nothing to do with age, and everything to do with personality. You present no restrictions as a true RP experience, and it certainly COULD be. I guess I'm just a little too cynical to see that happening, based on previous MMORPG experience. Fact is, you're going to have players who see that as an opportunity to kill everything that moves, regardless of affiliation, history, or storyline. At least with restrictions you cut down on the damage they can do.

Expecting the player communities to police themselves is a theory that has been floated before. Unfortunately, aside from bounty hunter quests, there is no way to effectively do it in this setting. You seem to think everyone will respect the story, when in fact we all know there will be hundreds (if not thousands) of people who won't care two licks about it. Its a sad, but true fact. I don't see FFA mayhem zones solving anything but making a small (albeit vocal) minority happy.
