Heh. This post reminded me of something that caused me to laugh.

I remember that back when I actually started looking at the boards (I played for several months without peeking into these forums even once; and yes, we were asking for a fix back then, too, only with more fervor), we advents all thought that, unfortunately, we wouldn't be fixed till March, and we'd have to wait about three months. And we thought that that kind of sucked, but figured in the meantime we would help Funcom along on the idea train.

And now it's April.
[sarcasm] *Giggle* *Snort* [/sarcasm]
...

You know, I don't think that I really like the turn that my already strange sense of humour is taking...

Oh, and Psyphen, I'm not sure that will be our patch (I'm not sure though, I forgot the number of the patch that was supposed to be dedicated to our profession...). We aren't next, Agents are; I guess they'll need it after 14.2, and I suppose that I've gotten over the fact that we aren't next after all, but the thing that bothers me is that the major patches are so far apart. It's a little disconcerting.

So I guess I'll set my sights another three months ahead and focus on June or July now.

Edit: And I really wouldn't expect them to give us any information about fixes at least until the over-equipping patch has been out long enough for them to judge its effects. The effects of that patch will gauge how badly we need to be fixed, and I guess that I'd prefer Funcom to keep their lying mouths still then to tell us something to get us all excited and then come by a week or two later saying "oops, nevermind." They've already done that once already on our forum, and it wasn't recieved too well.
