PvP or PK, you make the call!
This may or may not count as PKing.

In UO (pre Trammel) our guild would perform economic warfare with competing vendors. The victims were people who were customers of our competitors. Some of these people were interested in a PvP encounter, some were not.

If going to Vendor A had an increased risk of death to Vendor B, people tend to go to Vendor B. It worked like a mobster protection racket.

The part I enjoyed most about this arrangement was dealing with the countermeasures our economic rivals put into place. I felt like I was affecting my environment. I was making a difference in the game.

Is having a purpose for attacking people the same as PKing an unsuspecting miner?
