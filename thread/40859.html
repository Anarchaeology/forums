I agree that PVM should not be screwed around with because of PVP.

The problem with reducing damage and hitpoints, though, is armor class. If damage was halved every weapon would perform, err, like pistols. That's a lot of nerfin' and most people wouldn't like it. Me included

Personally I think the best solution is for the damage cap in PVP to be modified. Keep specials and crits capped as-is, change nanoformulas to maybe a 70% cap or so - that'll keep 1-shot/1-nuke kills or alpha strike kills from being common or easy. Uncap regular hits entirely. That should do it, really, without having to nerf anything - damage will catch up to healing and reflects. Fights won't be over in an eyeblink, but it's better than where we're at.
