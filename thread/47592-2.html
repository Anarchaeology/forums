but you would think that a soldier...an individual who is trained in the arts of warfare and has taken the job so seriously that he has actually become a soldier as a profession would be able to defeat a trader, who by definition is a merchant.

right now, i think there are only a few professions that are functioning as intended....i think fixers are fixed (finally), i think traders are fixed (regardless of the health nerf), i think Metas are functioning properly...and doctors....and maybe NTs (but don't ask an NT).

but i think there are a lot of professions that still aren't working as intended. that's what i mean by balance.

i understand that all professions cannot be equal. the very best doctor should probably lose to the very best soldier. but that isn't the case in AO. you'll get a doctor with a ql200 nova and ql200 armor versus a soldier with a ql200 nova and ql armor, and i think the doctor would win simply because of the heals.
