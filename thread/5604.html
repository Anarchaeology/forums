Steps of Madness needs improving...
.. and future static 'dungeons' should have this consideration.


This is what often happens:


Group successfully fights to the end and defeats Neleb, after they get their robes if they can't warp out they run back to the entrance, almost always dragging every mob in the place with them. Any other groups or ppl have to run for their lives too or be drowned under a sea of misshaped monsters.

After everyone has escaped/died, about 20 mobs just sit in the entrance room and camp, quickly killing most ppl who try to enter, and they stay for ages too


Simple solution is to have a small corridor beyond the boss' room which leads to a small empty room which has an exit.


<edit> to deter ppl dragging hordes of mobs into the boss room and then running out through the exit you could have Neleb drop a unique key which unlocks the door sealing off the corridor to the exit.
