Just to clarify things...

There are 2 ways that you can get skill bonuses off of your controlled land.

First there are the controllers. Your guild can only place one of these on any plot of land. They determine who owns what and must be destoyed in order for anyone else to take over. The guild is allowed 5 of these towers (ie 5 regions) at different ql's. FOr example, a guild could own a ql10, 75, 100, 150, and 200 tower.

The bonuses that you get from this type of tower are the org advantages. The total ql of all the towers you own (565 in the above example) allows you that many ql in advantages. These can be bought in the tower shop and installed by the leader of the org. THe other restriction is that you can have 2 advanatages per tower that you own. So, you could have 9 ql50 advantages and 1 ql115 advantage in the above example.

These bonuses are generally small, maybe 20pts to a pair of skills for a ql200 when the individual is level 200.

The other way to get skill bonuses is off the personal towers that you build to protect the controllers. The number of towers you can build is determined by your level: 1 at 15, 2 at 75, 3 at 150, and 4 at 200. The ql of the tower that you can build is determined by skills like comp lit, psychology, and nano programing as well as title level.

The bonuses that you get vary by tower. A ql200 fire turret, for example, gives +30 to MA, brawl, dimach, and riposte. Other towers buff ac's, pairs of nanoskills (PM/SI for ex.), life, nano, etc.

Both the org advantages and the personal tower advantages work everywhere, not just in the vicinity of the towers. Some personal towers also have negative effects on the enemy in the vicinity or positve vicinity effects for the defenders.

Hope that helps
