Nano Regen rate
Not too far back, we did a lot of talking about nano regen clusters. We wooted a lot and told everybody they'd get +110 nano every 10 sec with QL200 implants in.

Turns out that's not 100% true. Got in trouble with an enf in my guild because he redid his implants, but was only getting nano about every 30 sec. It wasn't anywhere near as nice as I'd told him it would be.

At this point, it appears that the rate of nano regen is tied to Psychic. In the most straightforward test we could come up with, the enforcer raised his Psychic by 40 points and his nano regen rate improved by about 4 sec.

In asking other people what their Psychic was and how fast their nano ticked, we think Psychic may not be the only factor. But it clearly is at least one factor.

So, bottom line is that your mileage may vary with these implants and Atroxes get the short end of this particular stick because of their lower breed max.

The most thorough way to test if it's between Psychic or Nanopool would be:

1. Time your current rate of regen ticks. Note down your Nanopool stat value.
2. IPR Nanopool and time it again.

If Time(1) and Time(2) are equal, or reasonably so, then we know that Nanopool has no effect on nano regen rate. The test can be stopped here. Otherwise, continue.

3. IPR Psychic; Max Nanopool, time it again. Note down your Nanopool stat value without any IP in Psychic.
4. Max Psychic again and put your stuff back on before you forget and run off to defend some towers as a gimp

At this point we'd know that Nanopool affects regen tick rate; the question we're trying to determine is how much effect Psychic has on that. By comparing the difference in tick times from Time(1) and Time(3) and coming up with a percentage, and by comparing Nanopool with Psychic to Nanopool without Psychic as a percentage.

If the percentages are about even, then we know that Psychic itself doesn't matter, but the trickle-down to nanopool does.
