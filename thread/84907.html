Faith
Faith
by Randa "Poetica" Whites

I have faith in the rising of the sun
I have faith in the dawning of a new day
I have faith in the sunrays opening the blossoms
I have faith in the dewdrops in the grass
I have faith in the birds singing
I have faith in the sun that moves across the sky
I have faith in the sun that sets in the evening
I have faith in the moon rising every night
I have faith in the stars shattered across the sky
I have faith in the dark velvet night
I have faith in seeing you again
I have faith in feeling your arms around me
Another day has past
I close my eyes and sleep
And I keep going, because
I have faith we will be together again
