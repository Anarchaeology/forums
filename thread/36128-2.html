I like the idea of more expensive ammo... Or maybe even special-use ammo... Like hollow-point bullets for people wearing "soft" armor like Bronto, and AP bullets for people wearing hard armor like Carbonium. Delayed explosion ammo that slugs the enemy, then 10 seconds later blows up if they have not been healed or first-aided yet, dealing massive amounts of damage.

For the melee types, "tipping" your weapon... For example, an enforcer takes his longmoon to an engineer and gets a carbon treated coating on their edge, then they buy a stack of 100 "acid wipes" which are turned on and off, and every swing adds extra poison damage. Or add freezing caps that you stick onto the edge of your weapon that work like those cold packs you can get in the 20th century, just much faster and more efficient... Upon impact the blister pack explodes mixing the two chemicals inside, causing massive cold damage to the enemy.

Glue-based shotgun shells could be added which explode a cloud of nanobots which create an intense sticky foam on the enemy, causing them to be rooted.

etc etc... You get the idea... But instead of making these replace normal ammo, make them cost 10k a shot, so they are affordable, but still an expensive, situational-based item. To make it more affordable and accessible, make multiple levels... ie... Glue Shell MKI gives an extra 10% damage and a 5 second root for 500 creds. All the way up to Glue Shell MKX which triples damage, gives a 30 second root, and costs 50k a shot.
