Smuggling run quest (outdoor mission)
  • Player must drive a cargo vehicle through dangerous territory
  • The general location of the vehicle is given. The exact location must be searched for. It will appear as a circle the map for player(s) doing the quest. Perception and map skill determine how close the player has to be to the vehicle before the indication on the map is given.
  • The vehicle can only be equipped and not carried in inventory. If it is double clicked it will automatically equip. If it is taken off, it will fall to the ground.
  • To avoid the merchandise falling into the wrong hands (or the police) there is a self destruct mechanism that will activate if the vehicle is unequipped for more than 20 minutes at a time after it is first used. There is also an over all time limit that must be met.
  • The driver of the vehicle must meet its required vehicle ground skill. Higher level quests require a higher skill. The vehicle will move at the unbuffed run speed of the driver but can be equipped and unequiped instantly. If the player does not meet the vehicle required ground skill, they will still be able to drive it but it will move at a slower speed using the proposed new overquipping rules.
  • Once completed, special credits are given that can be used only on certain underworld NPCs that will give a list of items to shop from
  • PvP version of quest
    1. Opposing teams have to take the vehicle to separate destinations.
    2. Quests only available at certain times during the day.
    3. In order to get the PvP quest, players must first complete a special mini mission. All experience points and loot gained during the mission will not go to the player, but instead would be put into a pool that would go to the winner(s) of the PvP quest.
    4. At the designated time, a tell is given to all those that registered stating the general location where to find the cargo and where it should be transported to.
    5. A player can only be registered for one PvP quest at a time
    6. The reward given out will be proportional to the number of players participating
    7. At most 6 players per side will be given the quest. If too many people register, more quests will be made in different locations, but they will all run concurrently. If there are multiple quests, the one that the player is assigned to is always random and determined at the time the quest starts. Opposing players should be matched up by both level and PvP title.
    8. For the entire duration of the quest there is no gas protection at all from players given the opposing quest. This can allow all or part of the quest to be done in a non PvP zone to prevent outside interference.
    9. If the quest is a draw all players will get the loot that they offered up in the pool. A percentage of the special credits offered for the quest will be given to the player/team that got vehicle closest to the destination when the quest ends. The percentage is determined by the closeness of the vehicle to the destination.
