RUR & the Red Tape Inspectors
"Gentlemen, welcome to RUR laboratories!" announces Dabblez.

The delegation from Omni-Admin's Red Tape look at each other with a mixture of confusion and embarrassment. Red Tape had been invited to inspect RUR's production facilities in order to ascertain whether the sale of RUR robots in clan territory should be allowed to continue, or whether there were potential military applications to this new technology. What they found was not at all what they had expected.

"Okay, so maybe it is just the spare room of my apartment, but don't let that cloud your judgement. What you are about to see is set to revolutionise the domestic bot market forever." She turns to one of the RUR technicians, "Noholds, would you prepare a bare QPT brain for imprinting?"

The atrox nods "Sure thing Dabz."

Crattyjack, one of RUR's salesman steps up. "Consider this, what is the number one problem with robots in an ordinary household in this day and age?"

Pretty much at the same time the three Red Tape delegates offer their views.

"Chairs, definitely chairs."
"Doors and walls."
"Furniture in general."

"No!" replies Crattyjack, "The problem with the modern domestic bot is that the robot will do what ever it owner tells it to do, and not what the owner WANTS it to do."

The delegates stare back at the salesman blankly.

"Let me put it this way. Ever heard one of those 'Three Wishes" stories? I guy finds a magic lamp or leprechaun or a yuttos dozer and is given three wishes. But the wishes always fireback because the guy fails to make a clear precise wish, right? So, like, he wishes to live forever, but omits to mention that he wants to stay young forever, so he just keeps getting older and older, his body all weak and wrinkly till the point comes when he can't even move or talk anymore, and yet he keeps living, century upon century..."

Dabz glares at Cratty.

"Um, well anyway, the point is if the djinn had a Quantum Positronic Thingy installed, it'd wouldn't just take the literal meaning of the wish, it would interpret the wish, read between the lines and get the full meaning of the wish right."

"QPT was developed to improve communications between robots and untrained humans. This is why we are targeting the household robot market." says Dixie, the marketing director. "It's the untrained, average Joe that has problems giving clear instructions to his robot. The trained military engineer faces no such problem."

"Besides, " adds Limona, RUR's robot psychologist, "QPT robots are very sensitive. The stress of battle field conditions would not suit them at all."

"Be that as it may, " interject one of the Red Tape delegates, "if the technology can be copied, it can be modified and maybe even turned into a weapon."

"Which is why we invited you here." add Dabblez. "What you are about to see is the imprinting process of a QPT brain. Are we ready Noholds?"

"We are indeed"

"Let's start cooking then."

Noholds lowers his visor. As he pulls down a lever, the room goes dark and two lasers held by mechanical arms move close to the bare positronic matrix. The lasers start moving slowly in what looks a bit like an elaborate dance firing beams at the brain at irregular intervals.

"The positronic neural pathways in a our bots are not made, they are developed, much like an ancient photograph." explains Dabz, her own visor down. "The basics of the techniques are common knowledge. You can find this in any positronic journal. The bare positronic matrix is exposed to a series of pre-programmed stimuli. As each layer is imprinted, a new layer is formed, and the next and the next."

Dixie, Limona and Crattyjack make there excuses and quickly flee while Dabz proceeds to explain the whole process in painful detail for the next twenty minutes.

"So, as I am sure you'll agree," she concludes, "simply cracking open a QPT brain really going to teach any engineer, clan or otherwise, anything the would not already know. The secret is in the stimuli we provide to the matrix. That is what we have patented and what keeps us in business."

"Um.. ..very... interesting.. yes interesting." replies one of the stunned delegates, trying had to suppress a yawn. "Of course we will have to review these findings."

Dabz nods. "I understand."

"Thank you for you demonstration. We will be in touch."

Once the Red Tape inspectors are gone Dabz calls out to Noholds. "Don't forget to destroy that QPT unit."

"Already on it Dabz."

Triluminary, who up to that point had been quite asks "Destroy it, why? Aren't postronic matrices rather expensive?"

"Tri, it's not like I doubt the Red Tape inspectors' integrity or anything. The demo was real enough, but I'll be damned if I show anyone outside RUR the exact imprinting sequence. The whole future of our little company depends on keeping this process secret. "

Debriefing and Report
Kithrak was sitting at his at his Argentum office in 4-Holes when the doorbell chimed. He stoped his work and pressed a button to call up a video feed of who pressed the doorbell. Seeing that it was his two trusted employees with the Red Tape insignia on their shoulder patches his hand hovered over the green button and glanced momentarily to the glass covered red button next to it.

The red button was linked to a pair of autoturrets outside his door. Ever since the ICC deregulated the Notum mining, security at 4-Holes has deteriated steadily. When he first had it installed he accidentally pressed the red button and sent one of his secretaries to the reclaim in short order. Since that incident he had a small piece of glass over it to prevent any more accidents.

The doorbell to his office chimed again snapping him out of it and reminding him that there were still two people waiting outside. He pressed the green button and the door soundlessly slid open admiting in a nanomage Meta-Physicist and a solitus Trader. The two snapped a salute and the Meta-Physicist called Hans "Deuscloud" Zanter handed over a small folder. Kithrak took it and began to flip through the pages as Deuscloud commented on their visit.

"That is the report from our visit to the RUR offices. We have to say their homebased manufacturing was not very impressive...."

The female trader called Minerva "Akaras" Hallas cut in "The size of the operations do not matter, we've seen enough of the homemade atomics."

Kithrak held up a hand to for quiet as he continues to scan through the report. "You say here in your report that the robot's brain cannot be copied directly?"

Both of them nodded and Deuscloud replied "They have told us that there is nothing that isn't public knowledge on the public gridfeeds about the positronic matrix. Through some special process they manage to cause the droid to understand and process the semantics of a command rather than the syntax."

Kithrak arcs a brow and asked Akaras. "Do you believe them?"

Akaras shook her head lightly. "Something dosen't quite add up with public knowledge yet at the same time claims to revolutionalize the domestic bot market."

Deuscloud put a hand to his chin thinking. "Yes, I believe they mention that their stimuli or something like that used in the makeing is what makes it special."

"Either way, a droid that understands semantics will be far more useful than existing droids. The droid will be able to follow a general order, versatile and something that Omni-Tek's battle droids lack." Kithrak commented dryly and closed the folder with their report.

Kithrak got up from his desk and motioned to his droid. "Go get me a glass of water."

The Omni-Admin Bodyguard droid buzzed and started to head towards the door but missed it by about three feet. It tried in vain to attempt to walk "through" the wall and manages to knock over a large picture of Phillip Ross and sending a plant crashing to the floor.

Kithrak sighs and rubs his temples. "Our droids currently can't even get the syntax right. What your report says only comfirms my worries that their droid with the ability to understand semantics will be infinately more versatile and reliable."

As Kithrak is talking the droid continues to try to walk through the solid wall. It managed to run itself into a corner of the office knocking a filing cabinet over and a pedestal with an expensive vase from his homeworld.

"Stop running in place!" Kithrak shouted at the droid.

The droid replied "Error, input not recognized. Please wait and try again."

Kithrak grumbles to the two "Can someone shut that thing down before it destroys my office?"

Deuscloud fired off a direct damage nanoprogram and Akaras fired several rounds into the droid from her shotgun. The droid screeched as electronics and metal parts melted and the thing turned into a smoldering mess. The smoke triggered the smoke detectors causeing the fire alarm to go off in the Red Tape building, the klaxon sounding throughout the building. A computerized voice calmly asked everyone to proceed calmly to the nearest exit.

"This is coming out of both your paychecks!" Kithrak looked at the smokeing remains of his droid.

Both simultaniously said "But we just did what you told us to boss!"

Kithrak smacked his forehead as the sprinklers went off in his office caused by the papers around the smouldering droid catching fire. "That isn't what I meant. You two used the wrong semantic interpretation for my request."

"So, you think you can give me a hand here?" asks Silvio Provinciali, senior vice-president of Aegean Consumer Products. His companion, a middle ranking but shockingly well connected Omni Admin bureaucrat, notes with distaste, and not for the first time, that Provinciali seem to have no compulsions about talking with his mouth full. "Just bury the case for a few months to help for a friend, huh?".

Provinciali draws a few glares from the other patrons of Omni HQ's exclusive St. James's club's dinning hall. "Its not that simple Silvio. Red Tape is taking care of this case now. It is out of my hands."

"Surely, even Red Tape understand the important role ACP plays and wouldn't be adverse to bending a little rule here and there to help us, if you present the case right?"

The question at hand was all about protecting market share. The company known as Aegean Consumer Products, or ACP, was in actuality just a sub-brand used by Omni-Manufacturing. It was used to trade in Rubi-Kan markets where the Omni-Tek brand name wasn't exactly considered a selling point. This of course was no big secret. For years students at the Universities of Tir and Athen had been making a show of boycotting ACP goods. But at the end of the day everybody needs a place to get their cheap blender or holo-vid recorder and so ACP prospered, securing for itself a sizeable chunk of the all important, notum rich, clan consumer goods market.

Among the various goods ACP sold were household bots, nice high ticket items with healthy profit margins. ACP viewed the upstarts at RUR's intention to sell bots in the clan markets with deep apprehension. Realistically, it wasn't likely that RUR would make any sizable effect on ACP's profits any time soon, but Silvio knew he had be seen as handling the situation.Things could get very competitive at the top and he's seen vp's get sacked for a lot less.

"Silvio, even assuming the case could be, as you say, buried for a month, what difference would that make?"

"Maybe nothing, maybe a lot. You take a look at these." says the vp, sliding his datapad across the dinner table and knocking down a dessert fork in the process.
"See those figures? There RUR's financials. Their cashflow situation is bad, and I mean bad. It wouldn't take much for them to just go under." Silvio snaps his fingers to emphasis the point.

"If it's just time you need, maybe something can be arranged. Red Tape are very professional, very thorough, but that too can be a fault at times. One could slow things down a little just by muddying the waters a bit. To coin a phrase, we could serve Red Tape with a few red herrings."
