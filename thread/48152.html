Nah, FunCom refuses to release the actual formulas for fear that players might then have a better understanding of how to build/use their chars. Sounds like a rant, I know, but the official reason is they don't want to encourage players trying to find a single best way to do things.

The Agg/Def slider effects your Inits (thus the attack/casting speed change) and your evades. I've heard that the attack difference is 2 sec from far left to far right. Haven't heard any guesses on how much evade you get from the move, though.

The people I've heard of that tested items with Agg/Def modifiers say they work. Never tested it myself, though.
