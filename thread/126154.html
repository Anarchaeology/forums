Echoes and Ashes
Rejkavik stood in a darkened alley, nearby to the Rompa bar. He occasionally glanced out into the street to ensure he wasn't being watched. There were only a few people in the streets tonight; the Rompa bar had been dead for quite some time. Most of the partygoers had either retired for the night or headed for Baboons. The soldier gave a snort and pulled a flask out of his coat, unscrewing the lid as he continued to survey the mostly desolate street before him. He was just about to raise the flask to his lips when he saw a figure approaching.

The figure continued walking down the street, and it appeared he or she would pass the alley. The figure ducked into the alley at the last minute, however. The figure was that of a female Opifex, though she was wearing a hood. The only thing Rejkavik could see within the blackness of the hood was a visor, which reflected the pale Rubi-Kan moon.

"It's about time you got here." Rejkavik scowled.
"I told you, I might be late." She replied.
"Nevermind that. Have you acquired the merchandise?"
"Indeed I have. All 500 million credits worth. Quite a hefty
purchase."
"My superiors demand a hefty purchase."
"I see."

The two just stood there for a moment, and the woman could make out the sounds of a drunken brawl from somewhere down the street.

Rejkavik spoke again.

"Did you bring a working prototype, as was requested of you?"
"Of course."
"Let's see it."

The Opifex had been wearing a small backpack; she took it off
and laid it down on the ground. Kneeling down and un-buckling the pack,
she pulled out a sleek black rifle. As she did so, Rejkavik noticed an array of wiring and what appeared to be circuits ran up her forearm. He thought of questioning her, but decided that it was of no importance. What -was- important, was the rifle. A low-light targetting scope was mounted on it, and the weapon gleamed like it was fresh off the assembly line.

"Very nice." Rejkavik smiled.
These things are highly illegal here. I'm sure you know that,
however."
"Of course I know that." Rejkavik snapped. "Why would I order
weapons from smugglers when I could just as easily buy them from on-planet stock for a lot less money?"

The woman shrugged.

"They -are- perma-kill weapons...?" Rejkavik asked.
"Yes, as you specified. Gauss weapon, fires bullets at
supersonic speed. The bullets release an EMP field upon contact with any surface, designed to disrupt and scatter the molecules of the 'spirit' whilst it's on the way to the reclaim terminal. But I'm sure you know all the details on how that works."
"I've done my homework. But do they work?"
"I assure you that they do."
"That's not good enough."
"What, do you want me to shoot somebody with the damned thing for you?"
"Perhaps that would be an option, but there's not enough time.
I'll just have to take your word on it for now. And for your sake, I
hope these weapons work properly when push comes to shove. Now, if you'll excuse me a moment. I must have a word with my superiors."

Rejkavik stepped away from the woman, and produced a small comm unit from his coat. He punched in a few buttons, then waited a moment until the static cleared. A gruff voice sounded out of the machine.

"Lightning?" the voice asked.
"Thunder." Rejkavik replied.
"Has the transaction gone accordingly?"
"Yes, High Commander. All is according to plan. The weapons
have arrived, and they appear to be in working order."
"You have not tested them?"
"Ah... no. But I will have the agent test them, if that is your
wish."
There was a pause from the other end, and then the gruff voice
sounded again.
"Nevermind that. It is His belief that the weapons are genuine.
I will send a team to pick them up immediately."
"Yes, High Commander."
"And remember: our Master does not wish unnecessary harm to
come to any of the Omni employees. The Corporation still has the
potential to become a force capable of destroying the Heretics.
Certain... individuals simply need to be removed from its inner sanctum before they can realize their potential."
"Yes, Commander. We will avoid pointless casualties."
"Very good. Remember, I am His Messenger..."
"...and we are His hands."
"Confession, Warrior."
"And True Belief."
With that the gruff voice faded, and the comm. link again lapsed into static. Rejkavik turned and walked back to the young agent,
who was packing the rifle back into her bag.

"My superiors are pleased. A team is being sent to collect the
cargo."
"Very well then." she replied. "My payment?"
"Your payment will be given to you when we've secured the
weapons."

The Opifex woman scowled a moment, then waved her hand.

"Whatever you say. I don't have any pressing business for the
rest of the night."
"I will send you a private message when the cargo has been
collected. Nice doing business with you...?"
"Valkyrie."
"Still using 'Valkyrie', eh? What -is- your real name?"
"That's not important. Who are you going to use the perma-kill
weapons on?"

Rejkavik snickered.

"I guess we still have secrets to be kept from one another,
then."
"Apparently so. I'll take my leave now. I expect to receive a
message from you soon."
"You will get your money, don't worry about that. But... I want
the prototype."

The Opifex woman only stood still for a moment, then pulled off the backpack and held it out to the soldier.

"Probably doing me a favor by taking it anyway, I wouldn't want
to get caught with this."

Rejkavik took the bag.

"Good night, Miss Valkyrie." he said. The Opi woman nodded,
then left the alley and was gone. Rejkavik switched his comm. unit to a different channel, and punched the 'Send' button. A female voice sounded this time.

"Thunder?" it asked.
"Lightning. Have you received orders to move?"
"Yes. We're on our way now."
"Good, good. I hope our trust in you is not misplaced, Agent
Tsunami?"
"It's not. We'll get the weapons intact."
"Excellent."
"What are we going to do with these weapons, if I might ask?"

Rejkavik didn't reply.

"This channel is secure." the female voice said.
"I know it is."

Rejkavik was silent a moment longer, then he spoke.

"The Master desires the head of Phillip Ross. And we are going to deliver it to him on a silver platter, and guarantee our place in Paradise."
