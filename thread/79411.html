The level range for attacking towers needs fixing
I'm level 75. I show up on a piece of land that's 50-75. There is one QL75 tower and 8 QL50 towers.

I can destroy 1 tower but that's it. The QL50 towers are out of my range. Maybe it's just me, but if you're going to let me attack one tower in the area, I'd like to be able to clear the joint out. I brought friends and only 1 friend was able to attack the level 50s.

I realize the ramifications of this are huge. Because this would affect the PvP ranges, but here's a thought.... make the PvP ranges similar to the land ranges.

IE. a 50-75 PvP range. 50s cannot attack 49s, and 76s cannot attack 75s.

Just kind of gross when you can siege half a base and that's it. Also, the high level doctor thing has got to end. It totally corrupts low level base sieges.

Bleh,
