What this is about
For every engineer that started this thread (i.e. me) this isn't simply about making trade skills worth it. This is about class definition! Engineers are defined in a certain way within the game itself, by the game's own content (see the transcript above). I am hoping Funcom will fulfill that.

There are now two more posts appearing one showing that other robot assistants are equal to ours (not to mention the MP pet) and that we are not the 'master' of robot pets. The second posts demonstrates clearly the futility of trade skills in general and my own comments center around the fact that Gadgets (central to the engineer) for the most part do not exist. Those that do (deflection shields) have reqs on them that make them untainable by an engineer.

The exception are the trimmers and the lowlight targeting scope. While the trimmers do help dramatically, I fail to see them as being all inclusive of the Gadgets an engineer should have at their disposal (per the game description of engineers). Deflection shields should use trade skill requirements. There should be recipes for making nano equivalent gadgets! As I said above the Sympathetic nanos would have been excellent gadgets! Jetpacks would be a fantastic gadget!

On the whole, I don't want more nanos. I want more GADGETS that have to be built but are as effective as nanos.

Granted the cheap and easy way out is to just make a new nano for engineers. After all there's far less effort in designing nano reqs than designing reqs for the assembly process of an item. That doesn't make it the better way! We're engineers not NTs or MPs! At this moment though, we little more than a gimped MP (and I'm not just talking about pets) even if our pets were superior (which they should be) we're still just a shadow of the MP because there is nothing that sets us apart.

GADGETS would set us apart. Let us BUILD the things that define our class, that have effects similiar to nanos. Heck if you're worried about balancing reqs do it this way.

Building the item takes 'average' trade skill levels that most engineers would have at the QL of the item. Activating the item requires nano skills and is where you balance it.

What's the difference one may ask?

Night and day, it's in how the character is played and how the character feels. With gadgets, even though you need nano skills to activate them, you still had to use your engineering skills to combine the parts and make the item itself. Since they have additional requirements (ala trade skills) they could be a touch better than an equivalent nano. Also with the amount of imagination I'm seeing appear in the game (sympathetics, hacked maussers, etc) you open the door to creating some very interesting GADGETS.


GADGETS GAUTE! GADGETS! WHOOO LET'S HEAR IT FOR THE GADGETS!

Perhaps this is where FC is balking at implementing gadgets. How do you control them?

Well firstly I don't believe flying is an MP specialty (after all anyone can get a Yalm it just takes time). However, here's how to deal with the jetpack in particular.

1) Different QLs of jetpacks that have different speeds, not as good as a Yalm but near to quantum wings (or less if qw is as good as a Yalm). Low QL jetpacks are very slow, high QL jetpacks are decent speed. All require tradeskills to build and nanoskills to activate, heck it could even cost some nano points to activate.

2) Charges, putting on a jetpack costs one charge. The jetpack has say 5 charges before you have to rebuild it. When the character actually logs out (fades from the game, not just LD) the jetpack becomes inactive until removed and put back on (similar to how Yalms do that if you die with one on and don't have the reqs to use it).

3) Cost, keep the costs fairly low for building, after all a Yalm or QW is a one-time investment. Jetpacks would have to be rebuilt, while not overly frequently at least every once in a while.

All the potential engineer gadgets could work this way: charges, costs, trade and nano skill requirements, nano points to activate. The only thing is these are gadgets and should not require NCU there is still an effect that could be registered in the active programs list but its a 'zero' cost.

They've already shown with trimmers that you can set how long it takes a gadget to fire off. Anywhere from a second for the offense trimmer to nearly a minute with the lowlight targeting scope. I'm sure everyone can think of examples of object time for activation.

Gadgets are actually pretty disadvantageous to a nano. They would cost more in the long run (due to being rebuilt), they would be harder to use (requiring trade skills and nano skills, make the gadgets no drop so each engineer has to build their own). BUT Gadgets are the 'feel' of the engineer. They could have the advantage of 'No NCU cost' and still be regulated by activation time and locking certain skills on use.

Even with the disadvantages, I would (and I speak for every engineer that started this thread, i.e. me) rather play such a character. Engineers would be more interesting and have a completely unique feel to any other class.
