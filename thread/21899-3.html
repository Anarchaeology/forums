AO is all about killing. That's the only way to play this game. High end game, it's all about killing in missions.

Problem is, other than our SMG/burst and evades, there's NOTHING that fixers get that contributes to battle in the form of cheap ip skills. And in case you didn't notice, SMG's completely and utterly blow compared to other weapon types. And they're slow, to add insult to injury.

Run speed? Doesn't help us kill things. B&E? Nope. Perception...? Vehicles (most of which are worth nothing)...

We have SIX nano skills, all fully dark blue, that we would HAVE to raise in order to be on par with other classes. If other classes have to raise 2 dark blue evades, 1 dark blue B&E (which, incidentally, doesn't need to be raised near as high as your level cap in order for you to be capable of opening doors/chests in a mission), and dark blue run speed (although I can't imagine why anyone would even consider this necessary in the "age of yalms", where everyone and their level 20 brother has one... so in fact, I won't include that)...

Point is, Fixers are left with six completely dark blue nanoskills, all of which they need. I have a 280 in SI, MM, and PM, and I need 324 or something like that to get the first team evac. 20k ip, an entire level, won't even get ONE of those skills up that high for me.

I recently dumped an *entire* level into comp lit just so I could get more NCU to get more buffs from other people to make up for the fact that I simply can't afford to invest ip in pretty much anything I need... The fact that I dumped enough ip to get the personal evac by level 60 has almost crippled me as it is...

Agents can kill things. Agents have options. Agents don't have ALL DARK BLUE nano skills. I'm not saying you're not currently gimped or broken or that 14.2 is anything other than a PITA, but don't waste your time trying to justify fixers having "useful" green skills.

The only way to level in this game, and the only way to play this game, is by killing things. As soon as there's a way for me to run something to death, fly it to death, drive it to death, yacht it to death, perceive it to death, break and enter it to death, disarm it to death, well...

Either Funcom needs to fix something with Fixer nano skills, or they need to make our green skills not so relatively worthless compared to other skills in the game.
