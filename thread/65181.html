Comunity update - Nov 5th
A new week, and I think I'll start it with an update.

Cz activity
Some of my time right now goes into revamping some of the work in Community, and getting new routines and reports for it. Please understand that this will pull me a little bit away from the bulletin board, e-mails and such this week.

And I might mention too that yes, I do read every single e-mail I get. I do not, however, reply to them all. Some are simply feedback, some are just whining, and sometimes I simply get too much to be able to answer them all (and just print them and read them on the tram home).

Bulletin board
My time on the bulletin board varies a lot from day to day. I try cover as much as I can, and drop by in different profession forums now and then.

The bulletin board is primarily for player to player communication, and even though we try to cover as much of it as possible, please don't expect designer or community feedback on all questions. And you don't need to pose questions directly to us to get our attention. (In fact, I am less inclined towards answering a thread that has my nick in the topic.)

The Notum Wars
Beta is running well, and we're looking at adding another batch of testers today. Maybe you'll be one of the lucky ones. (If you applied, that is.)

Name wipe
The launcher now has more info on the name / character wipe. The day is Wednesday November 27th. Remember to log in your level 1 chars.

Also make sure to move any chars you have in character creation out of there, as we're killing those off when we introduce the new character creation system.

Professions and Professionals
The discussion with the current Professionals is turning out to be a very interesting one. I am waiting for one more round of feedback before I'm sure of the future of the program.

---

Ok, probably something I forgot. I'll keep an eye on the thread, and a few other threads I already follow, and get my other stuff done.
