I've always been of the view that the official story line was just a backdrop. Roleplayers shouldn't necessarily wait for an official event to react to, but write their adventures, invent thier own enemies and conflicts.

Their really is a whole range of stories that can be set in modern day Rubi-Ka that don't have to affect or be validated by the official story line. Sort of like in the way Casablanca or Gone With the Wind are stories set during a war but not about the war.

As for NW, my feeling is that PvP and roleplaying don't mix that well. Roleplaying is about feeding the imgination and doing things with the appropriate style. By style I don't necesssarily mean "ultra-cool, dark glasses and attitude", playing a clumsy character is just as valid though not as popular choice.

PvP is about getting results. If people discover some cheesey, unrealistic tactic that works best in PvP, that is the tactic people will adopt.

Of course I am aware that many people in AO equate RP with PvP. What can I say, it is a very confusing world.

I agree with what everyone has said so far - this is the first MMORPG I've ever played, and chose it specifically for its story. I'm always amazed at how in-depth some of the stories are generated by the members of DA, OTSEC, and even the CoA.

But at the same time, how the world around us came about and especially where it's heading, as well as the politics of it all which define where clanners and OT people can and cannot travel, for instance, is defined solely by the storyline.

Even with Notum Wars, say Unity of the Rose goes on a bloody rampage and claims every piece of real estate on RK: what then? The storyline would dictate that OT would never let that happen, yet it is very possible that a single group could hold this territory in perpetuity. So what would Funcom do in the event that happened? Suddenly reactivate the story and send in an army of level 300 uber-soldiers to usurp such group from the land? Or let such a situation exist in complete contrast to the story?

Similarly, what's the point of the Clans if the storyline is abandoned? They're oppressed as it is, so if there is no story, and things stick the way they are, then they will always be oppressed. Any changes to that situation will be made in a seemingly arbitrary manner.

The key RPing element that an active official story provides, aside from context, is uniformity. An active storyline defines the overall reasoning that motivates OTSEC to duty, that justifies the CoA's militancy, and which above all else supplies the bonding element that integrates all affiliations, factions and organizations into one common world.

Otherwise, the world of RK fractures as individual groups pursue their own storylines; what storylines are created inter-organizationally are often transitory and are rarely contiguous with such groups' interactions with others.

Along the same lines, without an overall "guiding hand", newer players may find it difficult to relate to an organization when such player knows nothing about what may or may not be going on. The community ceases to be the overall macrocosm of RPers and increasingly tends towards individual groups.

[works getting a bit busy, so I have to cut this here. I know I was overly broad with the topic; I serious doubt people like Nevver, Sezmra, Tarryk and Savoy will ever completely abandon the RP experience. The only point I'm trying to make is that the abandonment of the storyline will cause a very vicious downward spiral that will either reduce the RP base to < 100 or eliminate it altogether. You also have to account for the fact that, by diverting resources previously devoted to the storyline to game development, FC might actually (--dare I say it--) make the mechanics of the game more interesting and draw more players away from the RP world...]

The beauty is that we can play along with this eccentricities when they fit in our "world view" or politely ignore them when they don't.

Basically fragmentation is what allows us not to step on each other toes quite so much. Or if you prefer, divided we stand, united we squabble. :-)
