She lay scarely covered save for heavy markings on her skin traced with an almost inklike solution.

She made a mental note to herself to examine all of JimmyT's known accomplices when she got back home..perhaps one of them could point her to her target.

Each ripple of water was like tiny fingers rubbing against her skin, sonic showers with their notum scubbing filters were more hygenic yes, but there was something to be said for water, it seemed more...personal, she thought. Lexus felt herself drifting into a sleep, it had been some time since she had a good rest, even her slumber from the previous night was interrupted by comms from her mother. Thursday morning at Braedon's apartment...

Braedon angrily threw the newspaper aside and grabbed the bottle of Scottish whiskey. Coffee alone was tasting more and more bland each morning, it seemed. Pouring the whiskey into what was left of his morning coffee, Braedon pondered the latest news...

Nelida implemented in Alamexis' death. What rubbish! I swear, the media will print any damn thing that sells...

Exiting his bedroom, lit dimly by a lava lamp in the corner, Braedon stepped out into the bright main area of his apartment. After squinting a moment to adjust to the unwelcome light, he noticed his Hanshi Primus armor laid out haphazardly in the corner...having been shed late last night after his meeting with Sezmra. With a sigh of resignation, the Clan leader began putting the armor on. While he had no fear for his own safety, he had promised someone that he would exercise caution. Braedon kept his promises. And, last night's activities only strengthened the possibility that he was a target. He reviewed the previous night's happenings...looking for any subtle clues he might have missed...

Wednesday Evening at The Cup...

The previous day had generally been quiet...until the evening, which more than made up for it. Seeking to find out more information on some of the names tossed his way by Vixentrox, Sezmra and his other contacts, Braedon morphed into sabretooth form and took to the rooftops. Finding a good vantage point directly across from The Cup, he crouched low, waited and watched...pausing quite frequently to see if he could establish a comm link to Sezmra.

It began to rain, and Braedon wondered why he was putting himself through this misery. Shivering a bit, he growled softly and continued his watch. After a long period of quiet, his comm suddenly came to life with a bizarre message. It was a threat, similar to the ones many of his friends had received throughout the day. Like theirs, Braedon too was warned about his support of the Rubi-Ka Mining Cooperative. Unlike the others, his threat seemed to focus more on his "disgusting association with the Omni spy, Sezmra."

Braedon felt his claws unsheathing in anger. He had just about had enough of threats, and particularly those that had Sezmra's name tagged to them. Realizing he was practically snarling and would soon be drawing attention to himself, he did his best to calm down and continue his surveillance, though his tail continued to twitch in agitation.

Before long, his decision to remain concealed were rewarded. Two men met near the entrance to The Cup, one of which called out to "JimmyT" to come under the nearby awning for cover from the elements. The name instantly brought Braedon to attention, his sabretooth ears perking to hear the conversation taking place below him. The other man was soon identified as "Radein."

The two figured dressed in black spoke about Alamexis in an unfavorable light, suggesting he got what he deserved. The conversation soon turned to Clanners who were "traitors to the cause," referring to the RMC meeting and those present.

The shorter man, JimmyT, then inquired as to the name of "that one fool." After listening to the two of them butcher his name while trying to get it right, it took every ounce of willpower for the Adventurer to refrain from screaming out "BRAEDON, you idiots!" But, showing remarkable poise, Braedon remained hidden above, hoping to hear something useful out of the two...even after Radein referred to whatever the name was as..."almost feminine sounding."

Unfortunately, nothing terribly useful was said in the conversation, but it was clear these two were involved in the recent uprising of radicals, and likely somehow involved in the death of Alamexis. Although, how these two incompetents could have played any role in the deed itself was a mystery to Braedon.

"I'm here," her familiar voice said over his comm, sending a pleasant shiver through his body. A mountain of worry crumbled from his shoulders with those two simple words and the sound of her voice.

"Sezmra, I've been worried sick," he responded. "Can we meet?"

The two agreed to meet at The Cup. Braedon wished to keep in the area in case the two gentlemen returned. Sezmra soon appeared via the grid and they entered the bar together, Braedon back in his normal form and well-armored.

Braedon was surprised to see the two gentlemen seated within...he must have missed their entry while speaking with Sezmra. He cursed himself for not staying vigilent, but he had wanted to get a closer look at the two. Glaring at the pair a moment, the Adventurer made his way to the bar to buy himself a much-needed drink (and one for the lady too, of course).

After Sezmra exchanged pleasantries with a friend, the two of them found a seat in the corner and began comparing notes. It wasn't long until Radein and JimmyT made their way over for a delightful exchange of barbs. The only thing that would have made the exchange more enjoyable is if the suppression gas would have dropped for a moment so the two could have been skewered on the tips of his SOL Executioners.

The revolting excuse for Clanners eventually made their way out of The Cup, Braedon suggesting with a smirk that they might want to remember his name from now on.

The unlikely couple, Valor Eternal leader and SPARTA agent, remained huddled in the corner for a time, exchanging theories on the murder of Alamexis. The night came to an end with a subtle exchange of affection, work calling to Sezmra and sleep to Braedon.

Thursday Afternoon...

Drawing his SOL's, Braedon stepped into the dungeon entrance. Quickly, three Omni atrox enforcers came at him in a rush, weapons raised. The Adventurer's blades danced as he faced off against them, releasing his pent up rage. His SOL's sizzled against flesh...blood stained his armor and colored the walls...and, they all fell. Standing before the three corpses, breathing heavily and bleeding profusely, Braedon sent out a silent call to those who dared to try to dictate who he will or won't associate with...

Come for me if you wish...I'm ready...

Am I safe? Emiliy wondered as she looked towards the exit. She would have to eat; her stomach informing her of this through its incessant gurgling. She was also fully intent on having real coffee in the morning, and was dying to visit the old Council of Truth building in Tir.

The info terminal hummed as Jynne's fingers sped over the virtual keyboard, entering query after query. She could have "linked in" directly through her cyber implants, but she always felt more comfortable using a manual interface, even if it was slower.

She was searching a library terminal in Tir, in a side room of the old Council of Truth building. The Sentinels hadn't seen fit to make it into their headquaters, and it had stood, mostly abandoned, since Radiman and the rest of the Council disappeared. Dust was thick on most of the surfaces... few people were still able to enter the building; of them, fewer cared to bother. None were maids.

Jynne didn't really consider it her responsibility to defend Vixentrox's ward, that Emiliy girl, but she did feel a certain professional duty to look into this Amacia, and possibly stop her from practicing medicine in the future. Be this mystery doctor either incompetent or malicious, Amacia could be a serious danger to the public if she were giving out prescriptions for powerful drugs.

The fact that she was Omni-turned-Clan weighed on Jynne's mind as well. If Amacia had committed a malpractice offense so serious as to merit her being expelled from Omni-Med or brought up on charges, she was either evil enough or unskilled enough to be a deadly threat to the innocent. And that was ignoring the possibility that she was an Omni agent engaged in a covert anti-Clan operation.

So, Jynne had pulled some strings - friendships are more valuable than all the notum on Rubi-Ka - and gotten herself into the Council Hall. The notum crystal still hung suspended in the vaulty central chamber, as striking and beautiful as the first time she'd seen it. She'd went there first, to look at the room with its serene pool of water and the crystal there under the spire.

It was an inspiration, recalling to her the old days when she would listen to the Council of Clan Leaders debate beneath the shining crystal, and betimes, take part herself. When she'd still had hope for peace, hope that the Clans could forge a common, unifying bond out of more than an opposition to Omni-Tek. More than just a memory of better times, it was a symbol of public gatherings and a truly democratic process, where all were given their time to address the assembled Clanners.

Then with a sigh, Jynne had checked the ammunition in her chemical streamer and turned away, down the hall, and gone into a side room that was used as a library. She was armed and armored for heavy combat - there was no such thing as being too careful anymore, and she was aware of threats being issued to many of her friends and colleagues. What better - or more ironic - place for extremists or fanatics to lay an ambush for her, than in the old, gloomy, forgotten Council Hall?

After locating the terminals she searched across the grid, speeding tendrils of electronic queries across the archives of medical trade journals for articles containing the name Amacia. Omni-Med's policy was to hush things up, but serious cases tended to leak, especially through Thru-Space Network and Sol Banking sponsored publications. If Amacia were guilty of a severe medical infraction, it would almost certainly show up here.

A Hawk and a Dove...
Jennifer sat at the table she usually occupied in The Cup. She didn't claim it as hers, even though lately she was the only one who routinely sat there. She blinked twice, on purpose, and with her left hand pulled her auburn hair behind her ear. She lifted her right leg up and sat her foot on the seat beside her, tilting her head to lean her cheek on her knee. Her right hand was splayed out on the table in front of her, papers and various other documents haphazardly arranged underneath it, while her left hand curled around a cup of coffee. She blinked yet again, forcing her mind to concentrate on the information laid before her. She shook her head, and lifted it off of her knee glancing to the wall on her right side where her katana's leaned, always within arm's reach.

Jennifer unsnapped the comm link from around her left wrist and laid in on the table in front of her, the various colors and lights blinking and winking at her soothingly. She stared at this small device, one of the first pieces of equipment that she had procured when she was replicated onto the surface of Rubi-Ka. This one small device...what a lifesaver and soul damner it was, all at the same time. She stared at it a moment longer, as she lifted the coffee cup to her lips and sipped it sparingly. Lots of paperwork, and a large amount of reading caused her hesitancy to drink cup after cup of the stimulating brew. She glanced down at the first piece of paper under her right hand, and immediately frowned. The confusing mass of numbers, percentages and names immediately assailed her mind. With another set of blinks, and another head shake she tried reading what this form was trying to say and looked for the signature line. After a minute of searching, she banged her right hand on the table top loud enough for a couple of patrons to glance her way before she punched a single button on her comm.

The channel opened smoothly, and the clear picture of Delisa Age ,the guild Bureaucrat, appeared without a glitch. "Yes, Ms President? How may I be of assistance?" the nanomage female asked in her perfectly timed voice.

"Lisa, what the hell am I signing here?" Jennifer asked, annoyed.

"Just standard forms, President Lizor. Acquisition forms, applications for land control, appropriations for credit due to recent losses and member gains...." Delisa replied in her usual crisp fashion.

"Stop. Stop," Jennifer interrupted Delisa's barrage of information with a wave of her hand. "Why do I need acquisition forms? And what is this Application for Legitimacy?" She did her best to not sound too irritated. She had paid good money to hire Delisa, and having witnessed her incredible organization skills did not want to have to search for another Bureaucrat.

"The Application for Legitimacy is required by Omni-Corporation policies, and procedures. Without completing an Application for Legitimacy, no guild or organization can be recognized, or considered for control should Omni Corporation Rubi-Ka cease functioning, or be taken over, either by shareholder support or outside influences," Delisa spoke slowly and deliberately, as was her custom. Her facial features only changed enough to phrase each word, as if emotions were impossible to those muscles.

Jennifer blinked at Delisa, and cleared her throat, "Considered for control? You mean by filling out this form we would be able to rule Rubi-Ka?"

"Yes Ms. President. The Application for Legitimacy allows Vitae Covente to enter into whatever governmental form is chosen, should Omni Corporation cease operations."

"Wow. Well, are all these other forms absolutely necessary too?"

"Yes President Lizor. I am happy to report that your own personal efficiency has increased to 25.8% under my care. That is an incredible 8.4% increase in the last two weeks alone," the nanomage almost seemed to smile as she reported this information.

"Okay, okay," Jennifer raised her hands up in defeat. "I won't argue with you anymore about this but my standing order of only ESSENTIAL paperwork requiring my signature be filed stands."

"Of course, Ms President. Is there anything else that you require of me?"

"Yes. Have you gotten your hands on all of the information I asked you to get?"

"All except one, Ma'am. I am expecting it's arrival within the next 3.2 hours. I have already forwarded all pertinent information to your digipad for your perusal." Delisa replied succintly.

"Great. I'll read it just as soon as my hand falls off from all these signatures you constantly require of me." Jennifer smiled at Delisa, hoping that her constant friendliness would break through the shell that Delisa had built up around herself.

"Yes Ma'am. I will continue to pry into all underground activities, as per your orders. Standard compensation to all informants?" the nanomage asked curtly.

"Yes, Lisa. Double the fee paid to anything regarding Malcolm Age though. I am not liking how this is starting to develop, or the speed by which the effects are being felt." Jennifer sighed at her comm, the words spilling softly past her lips.

"Yes, Ms President. Are there any other instructions you have for me?" Delisa asked.

"No, Lisa. That will be all for now." Jennifer reached up and turned off the comm link, and proceeded to wade through the stack of papers lying in front of her. She scrawled signature after signature on the forms, until their meaning became a blur to her.

The patrons of The Cup shuffled in, and rotated through but Jennifer barely noticed them. She grabbed up the pile of paperwork, tapping the stack in her hands on one edge to arrange them more neatly and laid them back down. She lifted her arms over her head and yawned loudly. Her loud stretching must have startled some of the Clanners there, because they turned their heads in her direction and regarded her for a moment before returning to their drinks and conversations. She flashed a smile to those admirers, and finished off the coffee in front of her with a final gulp.

She sat back down at the table, and pulled out her digipad. This little device she had only recently acquired, in part to Delisa's urging and her own desire to feel important. Cracking the two pieces apart, she arranged it on the table in front of her and began to read through all of the official reports, news feeds, The Omni Herald articles, The Voice of Freedom editorials, and other tidbits that Delisa had gathered for her. Two messages in particular grabbed her attention and she spent long moments reading, and re-reading their content:
quote:
"Cease your traitorous activities at once. Supporting the traitor MacGreggor or supporting a democracy is seditious. The Sentinels are our leaders. If you continue this illegal behaviour you will be paid a visit much like the one MacGreggor was paid. You have been warned"
quote:
"Are you a Hawk or a Dove?"
Two separate messages, sent directly to her but with no identification attached to reply to the sender. Jennifer frowned, and shook her head silently to herself. Reports of problems with a Rubi-Ka Mining Corporation meeting, increased Sentinel activity, and the recent murder of Alamexis passed her screen, one by one.

Jennifer leaned back from the table for a moment, as an image of what was being claimed as Malcolm's last moments caught on film lay before her, on her digipad. She slowly started to piece together all of the different events that she had been involved in over the course of the last week. The mob of reporters storming a young girl here in The Cup, watching her fall as her knee struck a table and she knocked her head on the floor, Jennifer's rage and subsequent dispatching of nearly half the reporters. She giggled to herself, and quickly covered her mouth with her hand least more patrons glance over and think her psychotic.

That was an interesting day. She had been engaged with her guild members regarding new policies she wanted to institute in Vitae Covente, when the mob rushed that young girl. She had watched with a certain amount of disdain, having been used to such sights many times before but when they caused the girl to fall and be physically harmed is when it was too much for Jennifer. She had snatched up her katana's, rushed into the crowd of reporters, activating her Challenger program and proceeded to swing. Those who could not scramble out of the way of the whirling blades fast enough, found out that reclaim really does suck sometimes. The rest rushed for the door to The Cup. Jennifer had relaxed once the crowd had been banished, and turned to help the girl up but she had gone missing.

The next incident to float through her mind, included a report about an assassination attempt for a planned RMC meeting. It had just been a rumor, at first, but some slight investigation later turned up a real attempt. Jennifer, along with Kirk "Fatswifty", and Timika found the two hulking Atrox Enforcers, with their Agent escort just outside of Tir. One fierce battle later, and Jennifer was pushing the body of one of the Atrox into the gaping maw of an Offal Broodling in Mort. The huge chest the Atrox had been carrying was summarily deposited in the toxic lava pits not too far away.

Jennifer closed her eyes, and softly sighed. Murders, assassinations, intrigue, espionage... all of it stunk of too much politics. Other guilds came right behind these thoughts: Valor Eternal, Whispers Edge, The Mockers, Devil's Advocate, Arion, Kaviera Family Mafia, Omni Pol... she had friends and people she respected on both sides. She opened her eyes and finished reading through the reports. Accusations, sympathizers, traitors. All of those words meant very little to her, but she would be damned if anyone was going to harm her friends, if she could stop it. She sighed again. It was becoming increasingly obvious that despite all of her attempts at only being focused upon her goal, that sooner or later she would have to seek out the political arena. This thought did absolutely nothing to comfort her.

Jennifer pushed her auburn hair back behind her ear again, and slowly clicked her digipad closed. Her mind was troubled, in turmoil and confusion dominated her thoughts. She had just recently seen Braedon in armor. BRAEDON!! IN ARMOR!! That very thought itself had been enough to clear away her equilibrium, but her mind kept returning to that one message...
quote:
Are you a Hawk or a Dove?
She reached out, and pushed a button on her comm as the link opened with a crackle. She really needed to get the security settings on this device updated, but she smiled as the link was established. "Hi hunny. I miss you. I'm... I need some help...."
