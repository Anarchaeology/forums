Leetas
Well as someone who has been playing a soldier from day one I have to say that I mostly agree with the criticisms of what Funcom has done with the class.

I should stress first that I have no comments about Soldiers vis-a-vis other classes. I think there are a lot of general balancing issues that need to be addressed but I will confine my comments to the one issue I do know about and that is playing a Soldier PVM.

While some people have said that the damage output of Soldiers is fine the way it is I can only flatly disagree. The whole idea of a the Soldier class (or so I thought when I started playing) was that they are the class who dish out damage. There are simple reasons for this expectation that I won't bother to go into as they have been addressed by others.

As everyone who has played a Soldier knows being able to dish out damage is critical for the class for the simple reason that they have absolutely nothing else to fall back on if this does not work. This is all they are about. They have no healing nanos, the cost of first-aid for the class in terms of IP is such that the first-aid stims in game are all but useless (healing 5-10% of your max health every 30 secs hardly makes a difference). Once you have entered combat as a Solider ALL you have is the hope that you can dish out more damage then the other guy. You can't run (hell most soldier are not exactly speedy especially with the tank armor reductions to run speed), you can't heal yourself, the shield nanos are no huge help... the list goes on, but I will assume you get my meaning by now.

This was not a problem before the various nerfs described in other posts, but now I find myself constantly on the verge of death (and dying), all but incapable of soloing missions, and completely useless in terms of fulfilling the sort of role in a team that I used to be able to. As for soloing, something which I personally enjoy, forget it, it is out of the question as a viable and fun alternative to the group hunt.

The fact is that I don't want to have an "uber" character. I am frankly more of an RPer and a social player, but that said, I don't want to feel like I am fighting against the game mechanics. In no other MMORPG have I ever even really paid attention to game mechanics so the very fact that I notice these things makes me feel that changes are needed.

Solutions? Well I think there are a couple that have been mentioned that are worthy of repeating:

1) Reduce the HPs of the mobs. I don't WANT to spend a thousand years killing one creature (if I am not killed first of course)

2) Healing Nanos (I repeat for emphasis: HEALING NANOS!!!!). Should a Solider (or any other class for that matter) be able to heal as well as a Doctor? Obviously not, but give us something that means I can solo through a mission or not be constantly relying on the Doc (or MA or Adv for that matter) to heal me every 3 seconds (He has 4 other people to take care of afterall)

Idea that were mentioned that are not central to my concerns but are good ones:

-More Assault Rifles: like all good Soldiers these days I have my trusty Novaflow. It's been as good to me as the game will let it, but I would LOVE some viable options

-Uniforms: not sure who mentioned this but neat

-Reduce the nano penalty from tank armour: "hmmm let me see, my nanos suck to begin with and now I am drained when I cast one...great... this is going to be a bad day"

So that's my 2 cents worth. The basic fact is that Funcom has a lot of balancing to do for all classes and in many different situations. I doubt it is easy since they have to worry about each class operating in PvP, PvM, soloing, in groups etc. but it is, I think, the single most important issue in the game. While I agree that they should be concerned about balancing the classes against each other, I think the priority should be to balance each class against the GAME first (I know PvP fans would not agree). This is simply a fundamental that is central to the game being fun (which is the point, afterall). I don't want to hit level 200 in a week. I want to have fun!
