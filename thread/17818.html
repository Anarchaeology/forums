Special items aside (They're too rare in my opinion to be called a 'breed advantage'), the differences in Attributes between Solitus and Nanomage only comes into being as they start to cap. Before then, the only difference is the different starting values of attributes at the begining (15 Int for Nanomage, 6 for Solitus I believe), as they can only be raised 3 per level regardless of breed.

Solitus will cap at 158 in all (slightly earlier for Str), and Nanomage will be spread out a little more. Nanomages can get Int/Psy to 512 base. Solitus 480. That's a difference of 32 Int, equating to approximately 6-8 (depending on the skill's secondary stat) across all Nanoskills at level 200. That's based entirely on breed differences alone, special items excepted.

f you exclude the Shades and the Vest, there isn't a great deal of difference between Solitus and Nanomage. Certainly not as much as there should be really. I'd also find it hard to call an item as rare and expensive as the Vest (especially the +20 variant) a defining 'breed advantage'.

In the end, I'd just go with what you want. As long as it's not Atrox . An Int/Psy cap of 400 will REALLY hurt your Bio Meta. And if you're still unhappy about choosing Solitus... remember one thing. We can leave this planet if we want. The poor Nanomage is stuck here
