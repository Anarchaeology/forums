Turin,

I seem to owe you an apology. Having just reviewed many of your other posts, I find myself in error about your behavior and attitude in general. However, on this one specific subject we will not be able to agree.

You see, here is how I see the problem.

Everybody talks about a certain class/profession in this or any other MMORPG being "gimped". What then happens is that people either leave those professions or never enter them in the first place. The players begin to congregate to the "leet" professions, to the point that these professions soon out-number the other, even balanced, professions horribly.

Eventually what you end up with is everybody playing the same few classes, in the same way, with the same equipment, and you end up with EQ, AC or DAoC. Further, as the "gimped" professions become less populated, they begin to get less focus from the game designers/developers, who tend to focus their efforts towards what will effect the most people. So the "ubers" become for uber, making the "gimps" even more gimped.

You want to help the fixer cause, group with them, encourage people to play them, and actively lend your voice to supporting their cries for help. Don't ignore and shun them, after all, someday you may be on that "gimped list".
