Be aware that the static dungeons (not missions/quests, which is what dogda/alvin run), are designed for GROUPS of the stated levels. Not A group, several groups. Maybe even MANY groups.

Creatures in Steps of Madness, for instance, can wipe out my 78th level MA, while being very very grey, because they have, in general, FAR more hit points than 'normal' mobs of their level.

Don't plan to run over to a static dungeon and solo it, unless you're level 150+, and even then, well... good luck. =)

Leetas
There are numerous low level ones for Omnis...
...in Omni Sewers and Omni Forest. I'd say soloable at level 5-10; the one by Omni Barracks is full of mobs no higher than level 3. However these dungeons are simply permanent, larger versions of the standard missions, and do not contain any bosses or super loot that I'm aware of. I was too high level for them to be worth messing with when I found them, so if there IS some uberstupidbad boss in there, well, it's player beware.

In the sewers, just go into the 'pipes' which thankfully for the inhabitants do not carry any of the toxic sludge that's in the sewer itself. I think there are 3 or 4 of these static playfields. In Omni Forest it's easier to find the static playfields with the machines upgrade to your map - the doors show up when you get close. Head south along the area of the shantytowns near the Omni - 1 wall, and a few more seem to be scattered around the southern part of the map. There may be more, I didn't go looking - just found them when I installed my map upgrade and followed my curiousity to see where they went.
