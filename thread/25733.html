Thank you Funcom (Seriously)
I know we all B*t*h and whine about what we want and what we don't have, but I just wanted to start a thread on a big THANK YOU for what has been fixed, and how far AO has come.

AO has come a LOOOOOONNNNNNGGGGGG way since it went live. I am enjoying the game more than I did in the beginning. Compared to EQ, DAoC, UO and Asheron's Call (Only games I've played). AO is still much more fun to play and more versatile. It is a huge world, and not the same thing day after day. Yes there are missions you can do over and over ... Now there are TEAM missions which are Uber and the boss always kicks our butts ... And there is always Hunting.

Compared to the other games I mentioned. NEVER loosing my lvl is such a WONDERFUL thing. Also, no corpse runs to get my items back. Woot! Not having to wait on a rezz, or hope that someone comes along to help out and doesn't charge me.

Anyway, in comparision there are SOOOOO many pluses to AO. Yes I agree that there are many things that can improve AO, BUT I am VERY thankful for what you have all done so far.

I just feel you guys need a good pat on the back for all the great things you have accomplished with the game enhancements since it started.

THANK YOU FUNCOM!!!
