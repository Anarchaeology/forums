The Yorae
I am Oshovah
The farseer
The voice of RubiKa

To all of you out there that do not know what to believe in, believe in yourself, and do not listen to others of what they say or think about you.
To all of you out there who know what to believe in, believe in that and you shall overcome most, do not listen to what they think about you or others that think the same.
When you realize this, you have come a long way on the road of enlightenment.
We all have the gift of the Shovah in us, but not many awaken this gift. One must be taught in the way´s, for only Shovahs can see what must be done.
They are the equalizers, the Judicators and the Judge´s of the planes.

And I have a confession to make, even though I am Shovah I do not know all.
When I came to this plane I knew nothing about the customs of man nor did I know about the feelings and emotions of them.
Little did I know what will happen to me.
At first I thought I was corrupted by someone, but I was not.
Secondly I thought I had lost my connection with RubiKa, but I had not.
Thirdly I asked my friends what could be wrong, and they knew.
They said I had been influenced, that I had learned. And I knew not what I have learned or what had influenced me.
I had fallen in love.

She is with me always.
She is Shovah.
Her name is Jehna.

The blessings of RubiKa be with her always, and Jehna I am always there for you.

For I am Oshovah
