CASINOS: REAL Casinos...
You want social interaction and RP in a game I have an idea...
Add Casinos in one or two towns... Real ones not just fake fronts.. Where you can win or lose credits as you want. You and a friend after a mission can decide hey lets go hang out and play some BlackJack with the cash we just made etc...

Of course you will see some naked broke addicted chars runnin round who lose everything but I think it would add a nice diversion and a good social touch with 6 players all at a table or another game chatting and playing...
