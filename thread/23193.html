Up the stakes!
First off AO rocks! I think though it's time for some major changes.

Just let me know if you agree with these ideas or not.

1- 1st off, I know it's beeing thought about, but the apartments need to be able to hold more than 10 items. As well as created group halls or something for groups of players, or even just a small one for 2 people. Keep the role playing alive.
2- Fixers really need to be able to do more damage in the computer field and theiving field. Even the book declares them as hackers and theives. Why not let them use their power? Sure they can run fast and jump into the grid. But what about hacking terminals? getting better prices on items or even sometimes getting them for free. As well as mabye letting them choose multiple missions out of a group. How annoying is it to see two missions you really life but you have to choose only one. Just think of a thief in any other game. Add some nice hacking abilitys and we got ourselves a great Fixer. I'm sure all you fixers will agree with me there.
Plus mabye make the hackers able to do more options in the grid...mabye hack other players for xp, items, cred only while in the grid...or even damage people in the grid with viruses.
3- PVP, it's fun, it's small...but it's not avaible everywhere. What happens when a rogue omni guy jumps into tir, the guards yell at him to leave. If he/she they don't the guards attack. But can the other players? NO! Why not change it so that in clan and omni citys, players can attack their opposition. I know this opens up the doors to some big problems with people who didn't want to be involved in pvp. But here's the thing. This planet by the stories at least, is constant under threat from one side or the other. But hardly anyone ever sees this. If you lower the suppression field so you can attack the opposition at almost anytime anywhere. it would allow this game's story to take off....imagine the possibilities of taking control of Tir, newland, or even Athens! for your side. Huge wars could take place all over the planet. Plus it would create better teamwork within the sides, not just between your own friends.
