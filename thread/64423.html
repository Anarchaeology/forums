the ammounts being bid will not go down apriciably at all, you will just see more money passed around more often. The selling spree will end in a few days, and then prices will be about the same.

The only way to reduce inflation is to increase the value of one cred. The only way to do that is to reduce the # of credits in the system.

for example, if you want 1 cred to buy twice as much as it does now they you have to halve the number of creds in the game.

not simple economics, but the way economics work.
