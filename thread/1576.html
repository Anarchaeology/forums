PNG Tactical Revolver is a solid pistol that only requires pistol to use.

The closest "sword" I have seen to a single skill weapon is the MTI sword, which uses 1h edged, and brawl. Just about every melee weapon in existance has some sort of special attack attached to them, which means no cheap IP weapons here.

All rifles have either aimed shot, or fast attack attached to the requirements, so these are two skill weapons.

There is a Flamethrower that uses pure assault rifle skill. It's actually a really good fire weapon, if you don't mind spending the cash for flamethrower ammuntion.

There isn't a SMG in existance that doesn't have at least burst tacked on as a special attack. It's in the definition somewhere.

Closest thing I have found to a "pure" ranged energy weapon is the Magical Stave of Inferno. This toy uses ranged energy and aimed shot skill.
