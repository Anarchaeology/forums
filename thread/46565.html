Fix #1. Blackholing nukes. This affects all profs, but the NTs get hit a little harder than most by it being that most of our offense come from nukes.

Fix #2. Calm bugs. If you calm a mob before it knows you are there, the calm acts like a root.

Fix #3. Lack of nukes between MC 871 & MC 1000. There are no DD nanos that require MC between those numbers. Localized Dimension Inversion reqs 871 and is the last usable nano. Izzy's Last Rectal Spasm isn't worth the cast time. So far, the best use I've seen for Izzy's Last Rectal Spasm is when Pacifica used it on a Leet to ding to 200.

Fix #4. Useless buffs. Yes, I know. We are not a buff class. We are a combat class. The line in my sig was taken directly out of FunCom's overview of an NT. And let me tell you...none of the self-buffs we have do what that one line says it does. Enfraam's is a useless line of nanos. It eats up way too much NCU for the AC/HP boost. HE is useful, but only if you have spare NCU space after getting the buffs you need. Crunch? Go find an MP. They got better (Izzy's compiler is better but there's no way you'll see an NT self cast it until 190+...and then maybe not even that). Dark Movement? MAs got better evade buffs. And how many buffs do we have that actually enhance our ability? Umm..0. Not a single buff for MC, NanoC Init, Nano Resist, Nano Pool, Int or Psy aside from the crap general ones.

There's more, but those are the main issues that I see with the NT.
