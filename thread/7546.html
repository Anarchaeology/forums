Mausser = decent PvM
Suppressor = decent PvP

Suppressor is good only when it crits in PvP, otherwise it does terrible damage. However, since Full Auto and Burst can get you to -80% health instantly...it works pretty well.

Haven't seen the other weapons in action but:

Void Spinner = junk min damage weapon.
Fusion Sprayer = junk min damage weapon.
Michael Patriot = junk min damage weapon.

Can't find the Gamma Ejector on HQ.

Both the Void Spinner and Fusion Sprayer require you to be level 71 before you can use them. If you use them at that level they might be ok...for 1 or 2 levels. After that you will hit for min damage in PvP and PvM.
