RK2 Bureaucrat Summit Meeting
I'm proud to announce the first Bureaucrat Summit meeting on Ruba-Ka2. The meeting will be held on June 15, 2002 at 21:00EST in the Green room of Reet's Retreat in Stret West Bank.

This will be a very informal meeting that will last as long as people are interested. It's a basic "meet and greet" event for Neutran, Clan, and Omni Bureaucrats to get to know one another and discuss various political views and interests in a protected setting. This IS a ROLE PLAYING event!!

Couple of rules:

Weapons and Driod check at the door! No one will be allowed entry into the meeting room with weapons or a droid in tow. We are not here to fight, we are here to open the channels of communication.

Each Bureaucrat is allowed to be escorted by one personal bodyguard. Each bodyguard must remain outside the meeting room, and they will act as the security force for the event. Please bring a mature bodyguard that is interested in role playing this role as well, and is familiar with his/her duties at this event.

That's it. If anyone has any questions, or is interested in contributing ideas, please post them up here, or give me a /tell in game.

Thanks all, and I hope to see you there!
