FC worry a lot about exploits with /pet warp. To some extent I can see where they're coming from - warping the pet out of combat when it's in trouble, for instance.

What about a /pet control command? Very straightforward, just set it up so that it switches your movement keys from controlling your avatar to controlling your pet. To prevent exploits, first person view should still show you what your avatar can see, not what the pet can see, and the camera in 3rd should still be centred on the pet user. Targeting should still depend on the character, not the pet etc. No seeing through the pet's eyes, in other words.

As far as I can see, it's a non-exploitable workaround. You can't use /pet control to simply drive the pet through a mission while the character stands safely in the entrance room, because you can't see where it's going, you can't target and you can't attack with it. If the pet's rooted, it's rooted. If the pet's slowed, it's slowed. All this would do is allow you to manually free your pet if he's stuck.
