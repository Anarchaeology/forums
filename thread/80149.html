Is it possible to beat a trader in PvP?
I had the opportunity to fight a trader 30! lvls lower than me at 2ho many times.

Reason it was many times was because I was normally getting owned until someone else either jumped in on him and he zoned or I was getting healed. Each time he came out we resumed the fight but there were always ppl interfearing. Anyhow, it was my first real taste of a prolonged battle trying different tactics each time.

Round 1

First fight I wasnt prepared. He debuffed me. My pet would no longer attack and I could not cast. I didnt have lower lvl spells ready so i was in panic mode of course. So i just shot with weapon which was doing much less damage due to debuff. If he was yellow to me i would have been almost instakilled however, i was able to take some hits because of lvl range. However, if he did not run because of others i probably be dead.

Round 2

I got some lower lvl spells rdy on bar. I released pet and got a new pet 30 lvls less than myself (didnt know how much debuff devalued me so i guessed at his lvl but i probably could have used a higher pet) He was in a buiding, so I put my pet on wait near the building and I moved out of range hoping to get him when he gets out with pet. He zoned out and tabbed on me i guess , did /follow to find me. I could not attack him because of 15 second timer. He hits me with plunder first because he has timer i dont. My pet in wait mode is too far to hear my commands DOH! So i low lvl root him and run towards pet and send pet on him. He roots me before i get out of his range. Hits me with some specials/crits and the like and i'm again almost dead. This time someone healed me so he panicked and ran.


Round 3 final round

So i'm learning right? I keep my pet beside me so it will be on command. I stay good range out form zone door. (hindsight i should have gone and charmed a mutant lvl 70ish not far but didnt) This time I have a trader 10 lvls below me next to me who is also waiting for him. He comes out and instead of plunder he area roots us. Doh my pet is useless by my side. I root him, I snare him, I start firing my pistol. He plunders me in that time however, the trader on my side plundered him as well. That did him in, a little of his own medicine. He coulndt run away this time and the trader on my side killed him off.


SO, basically i would have been dead 3 times probably to a trader 30 lvls less than me and I'm not sure what I could have done tactically different. THis bothered me a lot last night and I logged off thinking why bother to advance this character if we cant PvP since that is where this game is focused and I do like to pvp now and then.

Comments? strategies?
