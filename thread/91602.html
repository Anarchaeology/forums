The Sleekmaster Classic is actually an SMG, not a pistol. The Sleekblaster range uses pistol, but is primarily based on Ranged Energy, so none of that family of weapons can really be considered pistols as such.

Sleeks of all types are average weapons for their QL, but have extremely low reqs to use. That means they're great for the low-to-mid levels, since you can easily equip a sleek way above your level. At the higher levels though you'll run out of sleeks

Going with the Sleekmaster Classic means that you can also use the Light Suppressor, a boss loot gun which is very nice indeed. Performance for its reqs is about the same as for the sleek, but it achieves that performance at much lower levels. Like the sleeks it can be dual wielded.

When you hit level 41, a trip to the Foreman's Mine on The Longest Road may net you a Gamma Ejector, a pure SMG/Burst weapon that can also be dualled. If you're into pistols instead of SMG's this is also where the Customised Desert Reet drops. The CDR drops only from the Lab Director, which the Gamma is a random drop from the Bodyguards in the facility.
