Skill multiplier is the factor by which the Nanopool skill or Body Dev skill is multiplied. So the formula for working it out would be:

Begin Value + (Skill Multiplier * Skill) + (Pts/Level * Level)

I'm guessing that the actual level multipliers are purely breed specific and not dependent on profession - but I haven't actually confirmed this.

Two Cluster combinations are out of place to me.

Shiny Normal, Bright Jobe, No Faded has higher reqs than
Shiny Normal, Bright Jobe, Faded Normal. Moral of the story, never leave Faded cluster empty.

No Shiny, Bright Jobe, Faded Jobe has higher reqs than
[i}Shiny Normal, Bright Jobe, Faded Jobe[/i]. Moral... never leave Shiny empty either.


Basic Rules (Mathematically)

Normal Shiny - 1.5
Normal Bright - 1
Normal Faded - 0
Jobe Shiny - 2.5
Jobe Bright - 1.5
Jobe Faded - .5

Take all clusters to be in an implant, add the values for the clusters. If the value is 3.5 or higher then its a High Req Imp. If not then its a Low Req Imp. The numbers above don't account for the way Bright Jobe seems to affect the empty Shiny and Faded spots of an implant. I think its probably a DB Error. To make it not happen just use Shining and Faded clusters whenever you are dealing with a Bright Jobe.

QL1-200 Normal Implants
QL1-200 Jobe Implants

QL201-300 High Req Jobe Implants.
