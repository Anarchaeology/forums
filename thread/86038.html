Tired from a long day of uber leet hunting? Just looking for a place to kick back and poke fun of a few random thong wearing Atrox? Not quite tipsy enough to take that almost sexy nanomage home for a roll in the hay? Have no fear, Clan Anarchist Syndicate has purchased the Happy Rebel in Tir.

Our own Mahaveera cordially invites you all to a new day in Clan entertainment. Our grand opening will be on Saturday January the 4th at 8p.m. EST. All attending clanners will get their first drink ON THE HOUSE!!! We are attempting to line up Gridstream Productions to help us kick this off with a bang. And ladies there will be a million credit give away for the hottest female in attendance, so dress to thrill. All courtesty of CAS.
