In teams of people you don't know all that well, I can see that looting system work well.

For an all-guild team, at least for my guild, we do the following:

* Team loot set to ALL.
* Person with the most complete set of MK2 loots.
* Looter loots the MK2 piece if he doesn't have it yet, and if he does, give it to the person who has the 2nd most complete set of loot.

This is what we've done for over 6 months now and it works wonderfully. Nobody complains that they're losing raffles, and everybody knows that eventually they will be at the top of the loot ladder, since the teams change every time we go in there. It's all about helping each other. And it only makes sense to complete as many MK2 sets as possible because the more people who have a complete set, the fewer people in the team who will need loot, so the people who do need the armor have that much more of a chance to get theirs.

Long-winded, but I think you understand.
