I can't give any useful solo advice, as I um, haven't been soloing for quite awhile. I can share what I've been doing since 90ish though... I realize this is certainly tangental to the thread, but I had it all typed up, and it seemed silly to plop it in a new thread when you were asking for post 100 thoughts

At 103, I'm in Scheol. There are numerous things to fight at this range, and most give really good experience. For an easy intro to Scheol, the ~100ish Beit just north of the portal into Scheol are a good way to start. NE of the start, near the first Unred temple are several 100+ Rafters. Farther north of that, near a lake, there is a large Spider camp, including several named (who will rarely drop tats).

Yet farther north of that, on the eastern edge of the mountain range (north of the Incarnator there), you'll find four giant packs of Chimeras at level 120ish, including four bosses. These have a very wide aggro range, making pulling them quite easy, though you need to be careful. They also drop a lot faster than the Spiders, given their significantly lower hp.

There's some other neat stuff near the Chimeras, but I don't want to spoil it

Farther north into Scheol underground, there are several more static camps of Spiders, Shadows, Hoathlans, and Imps, all of which are suitable as you gain levels into the 100s. With a competent group, you can also begin to figh the Unred/red for weapon drops (first Sacrosanct/Inamorta stuff shows up here). DKNs drop from the named mobs in northern Scheol, though I haven't seen any drop from the named mobs in south (yet?). Quite a few patterns though, which while relatively useless for Shades, tends to make friends happy with the Symbiant rewards.

A few other options are the Hecklers in Elysium, though you'll need a competent group to do this. Insane experience. You can also hit the Catacombs beneath the temples in Elysium, though again, you'll need a good group for this.

I'm also a dungeon proponent. Though the dungeon loot in SL still largely sucks, you can occasionally find some useful stuff. Dungeons stocked with the right type of mobs are also useful for stocking up on spirits or other loot. Hell, I even found a GA disc in a chest in one of them (since the loot tables in dungeons seem to mirror mission loot atm, right down to RK treatment kits etc :/). Hopefully they'll revisit this sometime. Find several around your level, grab a few friends (or a single keeper, quite a good team), and go pummel some dungeons near your level. Generally slower xp than a tight group working full speed, but a good change of pace, with the possibility of decent loot. Plus you can store gems for your Arul Sabas, though these are hardly rare.

I moved into Scheol in the 90s, but in retrospect, I should have gone there sooner. You'll need a competent team to make this worthwhile.

One final comment. If you are interested in leveling, you really should group. That's about the end of the story. I don't even mean spending the time to get a full group and go hunting (the larger a pickup group is, the faster it tends to disintegrate when things go bad, and most teams are still sitting on the OA hill doing team missions - zzzz). Even a single keeper, doctor, adventurer, crat, MA, (shade!), or several other classes can greatly expand what mobs you can kill, how fast you can kill them, and how safe they will be to kill. I was hunting in the Nascense Unred temple killing the 50ish mobs from high 20s to late 30s with ONE other person, and leveling far faster than I would have outside. Took me a few minutes to find a person willing to do this on any given day, assuming my friends weren't around.

It's also (to me at least) far more fun than soloing. While I enjoy solo exploration of the world, you're not going to make a whole lot of friends. SL is already badly fragmented, and I see too many people running around alone, or in very small groups, when together, they could be taking on much more challenging encounters, leveling faster, getting better loot, and making friends for later down the road. If you're more interested in spending time in SL than RK, start grouping. I'm surprised that I still don't see groups forming in the gardens on any regular basis.
