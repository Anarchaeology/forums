Player/GM created missions
I was fixing a picture above my toilet, when i fell and hit my head on the toilet bowl and got an image like a 'Y'. Little did i know what this would lead to.......*cue Back to the future theme*

Ok my idea is for the creation of missions.

As we know theres an option to make missions. However it doesn't work right.

My idea is to give players a tool which lets them create their own missions, thru a click and drag system.

ie, Player hits the create mission icon and a VGUI screen opens with a grid. Each square is a room and some rooms take up more than one square, as we see when we do missions...

Along with the grid is a menu with themes and another with the rooms for them themes.

Players simply select the theme and then click on the room and drag into a location on the grid.

Starting with entrance/exit and making the next one.

Players then choose the type of mission. If kill person or radar, they select the room they go in. If object related they provide the items from their back packs. They can also place chests around and fill them with items from their backpacks. They can also set a level bar (1-200). They also set how much creds the person gets and how much exp, from their own exp bar and bank account. Player next selects the zone the mission will be in, depends on alignment and level of the mission..whether its solo or team, solo means key cant be duped, team will allow for cool events. The last thing to choose is the time the mission lasts.

At the bottom of the screen is credit bar with 2 slots.

1st: Tells you how much the mission will cost to create, the higher the level of the mission, the more it will cost. Also can charge a few creds for each room and chests.

NOTE: Maybe they can also place a striclty limited amount of mobs from a stricted database so they dont spoil the number of mobs surprise.

2nd: This credit slot is how much is taken away from the mission cost from items placed in the mission and their QL. Vendor price of course. Also the exp and money reward also reduces cost.

The player hits accept, the mission is created and an icon put in there mission slot along with a special key and any other items needed for the mission. Items found in the mission are taken from the invntory during mission creation. Player wont accept if rooms are not aligned or mission is missing any required fields.

Server side, the mission is created and depending on the level of the mission, monsters are added to reflect it.

To stop level 100+ people making missions to hog exp, the mission creatures do not offer exp or cash or items, sort of like special droids. Also the exp from completion of the mission is from the player who created it.

Looking at the description of the mission shows a description added by the player with,

/mission description ""

Also a general stats of the missions like the level of monsters. Maybe special key could have level, mission type as its name.

ie, Level 35 Assasination mission key.

The description will have,

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description: Player created, ie, Whilst patrolling Omni-forest I was jumped by a group of cloaked figures who beat me up and
stole my Vector ND Shotgun. I want you to go in, kill everyone and find the Shotgun. In return you may keep the Shotgun, this mission is a matter of principle and because my doctors advise i must rest to let my wounds heal, im offering it to you....

Level: the lvl of the mission, ie, Level 35

Location: Omni-forest.

Co-ords: 345, 876 <randomly created>

Mission value: How much the mission cost the player.

Additional tools: <like spiked food sack>

Solo or team: Solo mission. <Unless its an event, player should buy solo only so the creator doesn't sell same mission to many people>

Reward: QL50 Vector ND Shotgun

Cash: 3000 cr

EXP: 500 exp.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If the player buying the mission likes it and wants it, the player who created offers a price. After agreeing on it, they transfer creds and mission key, plus items needed ie spiked food sack.

---
The reason for this mission creation idea is for huge player interaction. As players progress they get themselves SO much junk and just sell it to a vendor, eventhough a younger player might have use of it. This will also be good for player and GM events.

I can envision GM Missions, with speical stuff being made easier and more frequently, along with their GM tools.

Most of all this will be fun!

In my opinion FC should at least look over what ive typed. Im not expecting it next patch if they want to do it or next year hehe, but it would be very cool to do it and offer so much in terms of,

Player interaction: High players meet low players.

Events: Either guild or GM, maybe only Guild leaders and GM'S can make the team missions with items and cash from guild bank or the special place GM's keep their stuff

Items: Placing usless junk in the mission will reduce its cost for the creator and get rid of it, but give a lower player more items to see and experience and make a few creds or use.

By restricting one created mission per character it will not run up missions keys no one is doing, also a message to the creator upon completetion will let them make another.

Perhaps let them make 3 missions so they aren't stuck waiting for the 1st one to be done

ok thats it hehe

Here comes my own crazy idea for player created missions:
-----------
1. If player A needs something very valuable, like nano crystal that is not available at shop, player A creates new find and retrieve item mission to "Player Created Mission" terminal.

Either this terminal would have list for all items in game (this list could be limited to show player level only items) so that player don't need reference to item OR player could buy reference to item from another player or from shop (biggest problem in current player created missions is that you need to have reference to item what you don't have). (There could also be "Get reference" missions...)

Player A would also define:
- Items quality range
- How many items he or she needs (this could be useful for example monster parts)
- How much time there is available for mission
- Possible mission reward items
- How much credits player is ready to give as reward
- How much experience player is ready to give as reward (player can't give more experience than he or she have available at current level)
- Player could limit mission for clan or omni only players
- Player could limit mission for guild members only

2. Player A accepts mission creation. Following would happen:
- Mission reward items are transferred to mission terminal
- Mission reward credits are transferred to mission terminal
- Mission reward experience is transferred to mission terminal
- Player would pay some credits for using mission terminal services. This payment might be depended from item's quality range, type (how common this item is) and mission duration. When player is creating mission he/she would see how much it would cost to create mission.
- Mission is made to available for other players

BUT this wouldn't create dungeon with item (It would be just too easy to get items with this way). Player B, who is accepting this mission, should find item from normal dungeon, OR should already have a mission item, OR should buy mission item from another player or from shop.

3. Player B is browsing "Player Created Mission" terminal. Player B could browse missions by:
- Mission type (there might also be other kind mission),
- What items are needed and item QL level range
- How much there is time left in mission
- When mission is created (newest missions)
- Credit/experience reward
- Etc.

4. When player B accepts mission he/she will get normal mission icon. It is also possible that more than one player accept mission.

5. Completing/cancelling mission:

A. Player B completes mission. He drops mission item to terminal, as normal retrieve item mission and he will get his/her reward. Player A is notified by message and can get item from terminal or it is transferred directly to inventory/bank.

B. Someone else completes same mission before player B completes his/her. Player B doesn't get anything. Actually player B might lose some time and credits, because he/she tried to completed mission or bought items from other player. Player A is notified by message and can get item from terminal or it is transferred directly to inventory/bank.

C. Player A cancels mission. Player A will get back all items and credits and HALF of experience what he/she offered as a reward for other players, but he/she will never get back terminal service credits. Player B and other players, who accepted mission, might lose some time and credits, because they tried to complete mission.

D. Player B cancels mission. Doesn't effect for other players.

C. No-one completes mission. Player A will get back all items, credits and HALF of experience what he/she offered as a reward for other players, but he/she will never get back terminal service credits.
-----------
Risks for player A (if mission is not completed):
- Loses HALF of rewarded experience always (if offered experience as a reward).
- Loses his/her payment for terminal's services (this might be very high payment for high quality and rare items)

Risks for player B(if missions is not completed, someone else completes mission before or player A cancels mission)
- Loses time and credits what he/she might have already invest to the mission

Rewards for player A:
- Mission item
- Doesnt need to do dungeon mission and mission camping

Rewards for player B:
- Reward item(s)
- Reward credits
- Reward experience
- He/she could get some items, credits and experience WITHOUT fighting!

I guess that it would take some time to implent this kind system... but what I can lose?
