New post from reformed Trader Hater
Well, it seems that for now, Traders are finally not as much an issue as they were before.

Notum Wars has brought about a new age of big PvP battles and has dropped one on one fights to about nothing. This, coupled with the fact that FC finally gave us a way to kill drains with the virus scanner instead of letting traders drain everyone for long periods of time, has made them much less of a threat.

Not to say they're not ridiculously overpowered one on one, or that its still a bit unfair to have "One class to own them all", but it seems we don't have to look to one on one fights anymore just to find some PvP action.

Traders, you may just dodge the nerf stick afterall. As long as the focus remains big battles that is.
