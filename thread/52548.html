I like the new outdoor content. I have only gone up against one disgustingly hard to kill boss (two teams of 150+ whittled her away over about half an hour), but the several other outdoor camps I've been to have been very fun. The number of mobs and the spawn times on them were fast enough to support a full team at my level, and the bosses - while tougher than mission bosses - were doable with even an under-strength team.

The best part about fighting outside is that a new set of tactics and methods is useful, and it's a good variation from missioning. Rooting, snaring, and calming are great for it. The mobs could use a bump in their exp yield, to make up for the mission completion bonus, but by and large I'm very happy to go out there and fight them.
