Stiners have amazing bursts and nice fling shots. People don't use them much in PVP but they are a great PVM weapon. Their ROF is too slow for PVP, and as you mentioned they only have 2 specials.

Soldiers really don't do weapon swaps/Alphas anymore. Most just over equip one gun, since weapon switching is bugged & nurfed and you can't switch with MK on because of the recharge. Sooo, if you are a soldier and you want to alpha, don't bother, I know you think you can drop someone in a fling/burst/FA combo, maybe you can some of the time, but most of the time you won't get the 3 crits in a row to do it, the exception being if you are level 150 and your target is low 100s or below.

Better just to max out your ranged init, throw on a ql200 gama beamer, add a tach and a low light, and you are good to go.

I see more doc's, nts and metas using flashpoints now, they are nice since you can fire off specials while casting without interrupting the cast, so you can start a nice nuke, debuff or dot, and burst, fling, FA while its casting.
