An Open List for 14.X + patches
Just thought I'd start a list anyone here can continue on with.

Just in simple form, with maybe *1-2* sentance's at most to explain, create a list of what YOU would like to see in forthcoming patches and what YOU think needs changing/introducing/upgrading etc etc

Then at least they have a nice little compliation to look at.

K, i'll start. Itd be nice if everyone kept the same format for continuity too


THE LIST

--- Apartments
Ive whinged on about this for nearly a *year* now and im getting tired. Finish them.....PLEASE (couches, beds etc. More than 10 items)


--- Tradeskills
I was shocked to find with my last character that i got him lots of ip into weapon smithing and found half the recipes dont work, only release ones that DO, or make a list of which ones do and which ones dont so we all know what NOT to waste our time on.


--- Content control
FC keeps seeming to add BITS of stuff and never finish them, like that clothes making vending machine, what the hell is that for? Only put stuff in when its finished


--- Officials running places
Like Baboons or Rompa, designated ARKs who in their spare time, roleplay the owners of those places and organize stuff for it. Making the world a bit more real. If not, find players to do it. There'd be no shortage of offers. Give these *responsible* people (if they are arks too) powers to kick rude peeps out of the place.


--- Storyline, or lack of
Since i came back a few days ago, all ive heard from people is how the storyline has died. Bring it back, and also, be a bit more involving.


--- Involving
What i mean is, have GMs or ARKs playing small scenes out in places, unannounced, so the chancer who just happens to be walking around Omni-sewers manages to witness part of the storyline and go tell his mates and be uber for a few days. If he takes a screenie he's amazing. Or just have a character walking about the place, who attracts attention coz of his rank/look level or who he is etc


--- New content
You dont need a lot. Just maybe a patch were you make a new set of clothes, or a new set of APARTMENT stuff.....


--- Fighting oppotunities
Some peeps in this game want to roleplay, others want to fight.
Get an ark or GM to organize the Arena Combat League or something like it to make a weekly fixture, where the fighters are introduced, and there's a crowd to cheer people on.


And Finally
--- Roleplaying oppotunities
Some peeps in this game want to roleplay, others want to fight.
Help organizations/guilds more to make roleplaying situations.
Make an ark or GM who deals and helps organizations/guilds to set time and create little places, with maybe help in setting up special incidents with the help of an ark or something, they pick and choose what little set pieces are good enough to be "official" storyline and they let the guild do it with their oversight.



.......list on everyone
(hope you like my ideas, keep em rolling on)
