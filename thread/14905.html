1. Right now you can see father with it. Lets you use a few information tools and range increasers. Will probably be useful depending on what changes to concealment come about.

2. At lower levels you won't notice much but they can reduce the number of crits you get hit by later on in the game. You'll want to raise them depending on how you play. If you've build your character to do lots of damage you might want to put some into evades since you'll be taking lots of agros in groups.

3. Ranging int speeds up your firing/reload rate. +600 ranged int will make you fire and recharge 1 second faster. There is a 1 second fire/recharge cap. Also if you have your agression bar at half instead of full agr its +1 second, if its full def its +2 sec.

4. Nope. The agent only rifle is pretty much junk. I you ever need anything just get a friend or someone else to buy it for you.

5. I think your talking about the new static dungeon. Its a place mainly for multiple teams of lvl 125+ people. not a place you should be at lvl 62

6. tlvl 2 = lvl 15,
then lvl 50, lvl 100, lvl 150, lvl 190 after that
