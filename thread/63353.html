To my knowledge, the item was only available to those people who had stayed with Funcom since the beginning and had not cancelled their accounts. They were rewarding their supportive customers for sticking with them through the very rocky launch.

I believe the command was /claim, but that has been removed as a command. You can try /petition'ing, but I'm sure the GM will tell you that you are no longer eligible for the backpack because you left the game.

So, in other words, the item was not rewarded to people who started at launch, but to those who started at launch and continued to play for the next 6 months.

Two reasons why you can't get a pioneer's backpack:

1) You registered too late. The pack was only available to those that registered their accounts within the first two weeks or so after launch. The game launched on June 27, so if you got it in August, I'm afraid you're too late.

2) The command isn't available anymore. It was available for a while, then disabled. Then, I think they brought it back for a few weeks, after which they disabled it again. To be complete: the command was "/command claim"

If you want one, you're gonna have to buy it. They appear on the shopping channel from time to time.

I just keep mine in bank nowadays. Never use it. Yet, somehow, I can't bring myself to part with it...
