ACTOR'S NEEDED to enhance rk2
here's more detail on this idea about theatre's
ever get sick of logging on, do mission kill monster, make money?
want that roleplay element? blame funcom for not giving you more to do?
why not put on a play?
instead of taking a mission, audition!!!
it could best be handled by an org made for the perpous, or perhaps ark.
we could have it in a secured area, and by tickets to get a mission key, the more tickets sold, the better pay each actor gets
i think we could start with a short play, work our way up!!
wondering when you'll wear that god awful dress you spent a fortune on? donate it to the costume bank , maybe even sell it? there are tons of social outfits to use as costumes, and could we get some masks?
we could make it a good way to make money without fighting, a great social event, even work it into the storylines, Lincoln was shot at a play, perhaps ross would be vulnerable to attack...) the possiblity of this is amazing, and best of all, while it could be supported by funcom through adding functions, we as players can do this NOW!
is anyone else interested in starting this? if we can get this going, funcom would be far more willing to code us goodies to enhance it!!

ok, how many reader's are willing to put on a production? i've decided to start recruiting people for a trial play on rubi-ka 2.
i have some specific ideas for a small group of preformers and an interactive audience in a murder mystery style production, set in reet retreat. is anyone interested in roles? have specific ideas of their own? i'm looking to open in a few weeks time

as part of the audience, i'd like to have a gm if possible, or a gaurdian i want to have offical funcom support on this, as the goal here is to make rubi-ka a more immersive place
