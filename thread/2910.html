A notice to all clan faction leaders.
Both sides have been ordered to Amnesty.

First Order will be observing this Amnesty, however.


If our organization finds any.. and I mean ANY armed aggressive clan members within the Omni-controlled base 2h0. They will be executed.

Although we are ordered to Amnesty, that is not an order to put down our weapons in the face of aggressors. A direct attack on an omni controlled base, is an attack on Omni itself. This we will NOT tolerate.

All First Order members have been ordered to return fire in ANY case of ANY party opening fire on our members. As such be warned that there will be severe penalties for ANY clan faction found violating the amnesty and attacking omni property in cold blood.

These cowards must be eliminated. To think there are people out there that would open fire on unarmed innocents sickens me and the organization I represent.

As such we will be routinely patroling the area to ensure the safety of our unarmed citizens.

*Our members will not open fire first, rather will be on hand to deal with aggressive partys that may*.

An order to Amnesty is not an order to take a bullet.


First Order is a full supporter of Mr. Philip Ross, and a full supporter of the Amnesty. We would like to see the partys responsible for trying to destroy this peace accord removed from Rubika with extreme force.



You have been warned.
