Originally posted by Benjacrat
So, In summary, Omni-Tek is neither losing or gaining money from the Clans mining Notum. They still get their fair share of the profit.
Mmm... not strictly true. It costs OT money to stockpile and ship the material the Clans sell to them. If it's forced to resell that stock at the same price it paid for it, OT isn't able to take a profit on its resale to defray those costs. As a result, OT loses on the deal. The Clans, by contrast, don't have to pay anything like the same warehousing, shipping and distribution costs, so they gain quite nicely from it.
