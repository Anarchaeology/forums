The decline in Team Level will be a result of your team dissolving. Of note though, it must be your whole team that dissolves for the Team Level to drop. If 2 or 3 or even 4 of your team members leave your Team Level will stay the same. Even if your team leader leaves your Team Level will remain the same. Your whole team must dissolve for the Team Level to drop.
