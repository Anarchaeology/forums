Chat message: Mob hit other player
Ok here's my suggestion. Make a separate message for "Mob hit other player" instead of the current only way to see if a person gets hit "Other hit other". With the HP bars currently buggy and even if there's a fix that makes them work without a single miss this "Mob hit other" would be of great use.

Currently when teaming with people you see:
A hit M for 2pts
B hit M for 3pts
C hit M for 3pts
You hit M for 3pts < displayed in white
M hit A for 10pts
B hit M for 4pts
A hit M for 3pts
M hit A for 12pts
C hit M for 2pts
You hit M for 3pts < displayed in white
and so on...(add reflect damage and all other stuff... it gets messy)

Better would be to just see:
You hit M for 3pts
M hit A for 10pts
M hit A for 12pts
You hit M for 3pts

In this way you can tell even if that A persons HP bar is stuck or bugged in some way that he's getting hurt and needs some lovin from the doc or any healer(I'm a MA and would love this).

Dunno of this is hard to implement but it would sure be useful.
