ATTENTION : Clan/Omni Leadership
~ghosted gridfeed broadcast simultaneously on both clan and omni news channels...origin of broadcast unknown~

We, the members of The Forsaken (A collective of independant pariahs and outcasts) wish to request a meeting with both Omni and Clan leadership to discuss a Non-Aggression Pact. Independant (Neutral) lands have been unprovokedly attacked, harassed and annihilated constantly by both Clan and Omni-Tek since the first Independant towers were erected. As a gesture of our good intentions, The Forsaken will remove all offensive and aggressive towers from our land and only erect passive support towers. No turrets will be erected on our land in Holes in the Wall. This is not an invitation to attack, but a gesture of good-will. We would also ask that all Independant Organizations of the Independant Midland Federation do the same as a show of faith in the peace process.

We realize certain factions and organizations in Tir and Newland generate effectively violent situations in both cities, but I believe these are simply actions and reactions that can be quelled with talk instead of violence. We have attempted to work with what little leadership we have in Borealis and are still working to get them to aggree to have this meeting in Borealis which is the last true bastion of free-land where all factions are welcome. Please respond truthfully and give this situation much thought. The majority of us no longer accept the title of Neutral, but embrace the Independant Midland Federation of Independant Organizations. We seek true freedom and balance for all who live on Rubi-Ka and have been attempting to contact those who reside in Jobe. We believe things lurk on the horizon that dwarf this minor war for land and believe that now is the time to heal this horrid wound that tears apart all Rubi-Kan coherancy. War is not the answer to balance and peace, please speak with us.

~the gridfeed fades to static as the emblem of The Forsaken fades off the screen~

Hmmm ..

We clanners fight the tyranny of omnitek, a corporation that wants us to work in mines that slowly kills the planet we live on. Omnitek says that peace is only negotiable on the terms that we clanners accept Omnitek as ruler and go back to being employees.

I think only an extreme minority of clanners would even consider peace on these terms.

However, I suppose you want to talk peace over neutral lands. Unlike OT, the clans are not governed by one single clan, alliance, document or pact, and unless all clans are with you, there is no guarantee whatsoever peace will hold. Actually, I think most clanners would take the opportunity to push you further further south.

Also, most clanners percieve the neutrals as sided in this conflict. They are our neighbours, but still, as a whole, they accept Omnitek as rulers. As said, we clans have no one clan or one law to govern us. Neutrals don't have to accept any clan rule to be clan. They just have to stand up against the OT opressor.

As they don't, they're not looked at too favourably by most clans, and since recently, they have been percieved even more sided .. I'm not sure how much support you'll get here.

Sides...
As far as I can tell, there is only one side that should be taken in this "war" and that is the side of the people. We can not percieve a true dawn in the future when the only thing that exists is dusk in the wake of war, oppression (Tir/Newland), hatred and bigotry... Perhaps we are in the twilight and the dawn is about to break, perhaps we are destined for a destructive end. The nature of mankind is to destroy one another. We are not mankind, we are Rubi-Kans... We should make the choice to embrace a different nature and look towards mutual habitation. Borealis has remained open to both Omni and Clan and I would like to see it stay that way. Likewise I would like to see Omni and Clan open up one city for all factions. It would be a step towards mutual living, but will take some work and some time to hash out, and this can only happen if people are willing to entertain the possibility of co-existance. If you continue to hold a negative outlook on the possibilities then you will never see that peace is possible. If the clans have no true leadership, then stand up and become that leader. Become the voice that says we should stand as one and live in harmony instead of warring over pads of land that are not ours to begin with. They belong to the planet and the natural inhabitants of Rubi-Ka. I fear the cult of the Three Winds is but a glimmer of what is to come... We should stand together or we will fall apart.

I have given my thoughts...
Whether or not you choose to hear them is up to you...
