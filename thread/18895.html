Funcom plz respond: idea about user feedback
this is an idea i had for user feedback which relates to a current thread on the vnboards about some player drama and how FC gets opinions/info from players about their classes and game changes. a response from the big FC would be neato.

http://vnboards.ign.com/message.asp?...start=27316846

would be cool if FC had class/proffession surveys/questionaires you could fill out pertaining to each proffessions current state and related to patches, that way they could get more direct and precice info from the players (their levels, their experiences with certain skills, weapons, equipment, outlooks, opinions) in a much more organized manor.

i understand this would be alot of feed back, but thats because its a more convenient method for the players...

i dont know about some of you guys, but its pretty random when i post on the boards, i may see a topic that i have an opinion on and pass it by, i may see a topic that i have a strong opinion on that even relates to my class or important game changes, AND pass it by.

just wishful thinking i guess, but survey forms posted on their site directed to specific classes and or changes to the game would be a blessing. atleast that way you know your input is being read.
