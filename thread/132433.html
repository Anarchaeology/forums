The RK-Expo!
On May 13th the RK-Expo started where people show off their items to a private group and people get to see new unreleased items that will be in stores soon , the cost for a single ticket is 21 million credits and you could buy a ticket only by being invited. The highest members of the Clan, Omni-Tek, and Neutral were invited.

Henry Radiman, Phillip Ross, and Loren Warr were up there though the line to talk to them in their private sitting room would have been so long and huge they made exclusive tickets which cost 5 times the cost of regular tickets. The Jobe Scientists were there showing off a Virtual 3D Jobe demo which consisited of a 2 week Virtual Reality setup and all the people were triangles since it was in another part of The Grid.

Well, I better get back since I get to meet Phillip Ross in an hour!

This has been an exclusive story from the long running 5 year RK-Expo
