Agree, Cyblade
The professions were pretty well balanced a bit after release. Only thing was most people were pretty low level. They likewise refused to see the big picture and took the concept of one character versus another character as meaning PvP. Meetemedere basically ruined the game as far as what it could have been.

I too have teamed with high level (meaning 130+) NT's who know what the hell they are doing and it is a pleasure having them on the team. Their HE's help out TREMENDOUSLY the Doc, the MA, hell every member of the team. The one's who calm properly are priceless. The one's who know which Nuke to use at which point in a fight and exactly when to start the big badass finisher earn everybody's respect.

Oh, and I've played with crappy high level NT's. They die alot.

The Agents just don't have much to offer to a team unless they are false professioned for some buff. This usually means a healpet for the tank or MA for a crit' buff. Beyond that all they offer is mediocre damage or a rare crit Aimed Shot that likely gets them killed if they draw agro.

The Higher Level Fixers are some of the better pullers in the game. With their crazy evades, silly speed, and Grid Armor (I know, I know ...), combined with HoT's and an Essence from an Enforcer, they can easily pull a MOB from the corner of a mission to the main room virtually unscathed. Being able to root that Real Mean Enforcer in the corner of the main room is a bonus. And the six second Evac saved me over two million exp the other day. Still, they are missing something. The class is just not right. I proposed that they get a 'doppelganger' or 'shadow' to fight alongside them and confuse the MOB or PvP opponent and be given the ability to 'hack' those reclaim terminals in the middle of nowhere to really help with surface hunting.

Adventurers don't seem to have much to 'offer' to a team. And still they don't detract from a team. They do decent to good damage. They take care of themselves and others healthwise and for the most part cannot be complained about. If you know what you are doing and really like the idea of the morphs and the jack-of-all-trades idea, you can make this profession work. That said, I think the Adv's deserve a little love in terms of fixing their broken Morphs and giving them something really class defining such as the ability to 'track' PvP enemies within a zone or having ADV-only maps that will appear on the display of anybody currently teamed with them. For example, an ADV-only map of Mort would require 250 Adventuring and would display on all his team mate's map displays and be very detailed.
