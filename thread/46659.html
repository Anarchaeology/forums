I've done a little blitzing myself. I'd guess the two best blitzing profs would be Fixer and Adventurer- we both have green Run Speed, and we both have runbuffs (They just specialize in them, that's all. I think I can do +160 total.)

Now, a few tips before you get there:

If you're not in a hurry, you could always try to have someone pop you with Gridspace Freedom.

I've heard a full set of Omni-Pol armor, around ql75 can sell for, oh, 1.5 mil? (This is just what I've heard, correct me if I'm wrong.) Adventurers would probably pay for a set of SOL Fire Execs...Popular mission-only items are usually good.

I've heard the item reward missions -usually- have the item hidden in one of the corners.

When you get in:

Set the Agg/Def bar ALL the way to Def.

Buff yourself with whatever you have, and ALL runbuffs.

Review mission objective.

Now run like hell. Don't stop unless you think you see the item. If you're fast enough, sometimes you can run past some of the mobs and they won't aggro. You may have to sit down really fast before you complete the mission- I don't think you can be in a fight.

Once you've got the item, run back to the entrance.

Agg/Def bar to full DEF, and TURN OFF THE AUTO-ATTACK!!!
If you attack back a mob that has taregeted you, you can't take the item, and with the trail of MOBs you'll have after your hide at this moment, the second you'll loose hitting 'Q' can cost your your lfie.

Fixers are also the best prof for blitzing, not only because of the Runbuffs, but also because of the HoTs... usually during a blitz you'll be considered 'under attack' all the time, and won't be able to sit to heal yourself... so it's a very good point if your HoTs can do the job for you. (Note that Advys have their Heal nanos, as long as they still have soemthing in the nanopool of course. I personnally keep my nanopool for Recklessing out )

Settings for find-item missions are mainly full BAD and full MONEY, but I also put the slider toward HEAD ON, as it gives me more "white caves" missions (very small caves, with sometimes no more than 2 MOBs inside; easy blitz, easy money). Not full PHYSIC, because those Enforcers hit really hard, but beware of not putting the settings to too many nano-enabled MOBs: rooters and Traders that debuff you are a real pain... and keep in mind that if Rooted, 1 chances out 2 that it means death for you, depending on the situation...

Ok....blitzing:

1. Max Defense, Auto attack OFF, Cast all the defensive nanos you can (evades, HOTs, Shields, ACs, etc).

2. Look to the corners of the dungeon (left and right) for the item/machine.

Professions:

Trader, NT, Crat: Use calms and work your way to the "corners" of the dungeon to complete the mission. (item pickup or poison sack missions are the most popular).

Solider: TMS and run for it... you got 45-75 seconds to find the item or machine before you die (soldiers don't heal and can't calm).

Fixers: Run buff + HOTs, use mass snares and MEEP at the end

Other classes like DOCs can pull this stuff off as well. Just don't have it as easy (calms, MEEP, and TMS enable easier blitzing). However, at higher levels with just a 30% solider reflect on, just about anyone with 600 run can pull off a blitz.
