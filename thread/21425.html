14.2 - Playfield Changes
Playfield Changes:

*The zoning between Clon**** and Andromeda is now more accurate. The trade-off is that you won't be able to zone where you previously could. You can now only zone to Clon**** from the southwestern part of Andromeda, which was the original intention.

*Warning: A number of NPCs have been made unkillable (such as Stanley Adams, etc). They are not meant to be a source of XP.

*Updated path finding in Biomere dungeon

*Tir, 20K and Newland city: Moved and added mission booths and saving booths to spread out the population more.

*Tir: Added a bridge, moved a house and removed some walls to ease transportation through the city.

*The Whompa net has been reworked to ease transportation throughout Rubi-Ka. This will be done in three steps. The first part of the new Whompa system has been implemented in this patch. The next two steps will be released in coming patches.

Handle with care...
Has it been established that what we really want from this game is ease? I would like to see a poll taken.

Difficulty in travelling around cities because of goofy layout or lag is an obvious accident and not legitimately part of the game. Adding bridges and that sort of thing is great, although I hope you are careful about spreading out the population too far.

But with the whompas - they are part of the game. Their layout is a source of problems and solving those problems is enjoyable. The Grid and ferry systems factor in here as well.

I've had a lot of fun trying to get to places in enemy territory such as ICC HQ and post-amnesty 20K; and to remote locations like Home, Wine, and Sentinels HQ at low levels. Certainly these are challenges people can set for themselves, but how many will learn to enjoy them if there's a convenient alternative?

It's also important to note that setting a challenge for yourself despite a convenient alternative requires you to go out of character.

The goal of changes to the mass transit systems should not be ease but added challenge. What we have now is nice (although the whompa to Last Ditch ruined the remote feeling of that city) and gives inexperienced travellers opportunity to see much of their home territories while requiring planning and persistence to see the rest of the world.

I would like to see any additions or changes to these systems create new, interesting, frightening, and difficult situations. Grid out-points distant from Grid terminals, terminals with higher entrance cost than exit such as you once had in Meetme Dere, unsuppressed exits which can be garrisoned, distinct one-way loops of ferries or whompas such as one sees in Asheron's Call.

You've created a staggeringly cool world here and it frustrates me to see you blunt the elements which are enjoyable to us explorer & infiltrator types, in service to the constant cry for convenience and safety that emanates from some very loud and intolerant segments of the Rubi-Ka population.

It is good that you listen to feedback from people who are unhappy with your game, but please consider the implied approval of those who are not moved to complain.
