Hello Dookman,

The South Rubi-Ka Society is a gentleman's club founded on the principles of good fellowship and civic duty. We are a non-political, civilian organisation. The Society's activities consist primarily of sponsoring a number of Omni registered charities and organising a range of cultural, educational and fundraising events. Recently we have purchased a detective agency which looks after the Society's security as well as working on private casework.

/ooc The SRS is a roleplaying guild. All our members are roleplayers, we are not much good at anything else. We are a small group but we've been going since November last year.
We run regular table top style adventures and assorted roleplaying events. Despite the overall air of respectability surrounding the Society, more often than not these adventures conspire to drag our characters into more dangerous and unsavoury territories, exploring such topics as drugs, corruption and land speculation, murder, pornography and of course clandestine leet fighting.

For more information, visit our website http://rubika.no-ip.org/srs/ , drop us a line at gsoylent@hotmail.com or just send Savoy a tell.
