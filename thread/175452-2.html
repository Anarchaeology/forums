12 December 02 AOVault

A word from Opposing Force-AO President, Rauri

I received an e-mail from Opposing Force President, Marylynn "Rauri" Kozar this morning. She makes her organizations stance crystal clear! She calls the attack on Tir "senseless brutality".

These are troubled times we live in. I find I must constantly make one stand after another. I am a Clanner through and through. I am nothing, if not loyal. I will support, defend, and fight to my last breath for the freedom of all Rubi-Kan citizens. However, the bottom line is: I am Clan. No ifs, ands, or buts about it. I may not always agree with my fellow Clan factions. We may not go about things the same way at all times. But when it comes down to it, I will defend my fellow Clansman at all cost.

Opposing Force has always supported the Neutral community, we believe they have the same rights to freedom as any other person living on Rubi-ka. The brutal attack on Commander Steel has me so torn. Yes, Fisk was brutal, but
we fought and urged for Tir to be a safe haven for Neutrals.

Fisk may still feel the way he did, but the attacks on the Neutral community have come to an end. So, I was
horrified to find out that President Sumokan of Elite Operations, had lead a brutal attack called "Operation Gank Steel" and went as far as ordering Steel's head cut off. This was not just an attack on The Sentinels, but an attack on fellow Clansman and on our great city of Tir. This affront to the clans came shortly after Opposing Force and several other guilds put their lives on the line to aid the Independent Rubikans.

Members of Opposing Force and Apocalypse, to name a few, were brutally killed in cold blood after defeating the Omni and running them out of the area, saving the IR's base in the process. While the IR's were busy thanking the Clansmen who had come to their aid, Sumokan repeatedly killed the very people who were there to try to help the Neutral community, in cold blood.

When President Sumokan was contacted, because it was hoped that it was a mistake, he and his men replied with more threats to those who had just risked heir lives on the battlefield.

After Sumokan's senseless brutality and the attack on Tir, Opposing Force's advisor, Kunn, was so angered that he lead a raid on the Elite Operations base and destroyed it in retaliation.

The next day I met with the President of EO and one of my members who had been attacked by him the day before and an understanding was reached. As long as EO does not attack another Clanner we will leave them in peace and
never attack them again.

I must say this now, not as an idle threat, but as a promise. If any of my people or any of my fellow clansmen are attacked, Opposing Force will respond in kind and no mercy will be shown. We will not live in fear of anyone, Clan, Omni, Neutral or otherwise."

Marylynn Rauri Kozar
President Opposing Force AO
