The only really major bug in 14.6.04 (new on test today) is

The Return Of The Inventory Gnomes

That's right, inventory is shuffling (sometimes) on zoning again, which drops all hotbarred inventory items, etc.

Haven't checked yet with .04, but previous versions also have a 'Crash Game if you Alt-Tab out' bug, so you may or may not be able to alt-tab out of fullscreen mode and not crash after a minute or two.

Folks should love the new Subway, for low levels, though.

Wave goodbye to 'hit' animations, though, or sound effects.

Now, you just swing. No hit 'blood', etc. A bit less realistic, but, I guess it fixed some initiative problems.
