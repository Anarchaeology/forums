Adventurers top problems
Hi,

on behalf of the Adventurers I have tried to accumulate the problems from the old forums.

Dual wield pistols & Adventurers

Adventurers has an affinity towards dual wielding pistols so many goes down this road. The problem with this is not necessarily only within the Adventurer profession, it also relates to pistols.

We think pistols are underpowered at high levels. Character of level 80-100 and above suffer a lot from the low damage output. There are a lot of testimonies and examples about this in the archive. Fairly viable at lower levels, sucks at character level 80-100 and above. We dump a lot on IP for combat when we choose dual pistols, what's the reward?

- MOB AC eats up all damage. The increased speed of the pistols don't make up for this in higher levels. For those who chosed pistols it's their only source of damage. We have no pets, no damage nanos to add to out damage output. Pistols just don't scale well at higher levels. Balance against MOBs are terrible.

- Dual wield requirements are much too high. A part of the balance problem. When you took away the Freedom Arms you also increased the dual wield requirements of all pistols _a lot_ which mean that everyone has to use a much lower off-hand pistol. Either change the pistols or give us a multi ranged buff of 150 @ lvl 80 and 250 @ lvl 120. Make them self only if necessary.

- Reflect shields on NPCs hurt dual wielders with fast output much more than anyone else. At higher level NPCs damageshields are relatively much more powerful than at lower levels compared to the players HP.

- Off-hand pistol drop to inventory when zoning/loggin in. Goes for all dual wielders. We basically can't use the overequipping that those with one weapon can (skill wrangler etc etc). We can't go look for a trader every time we zone... Also, we all need two free slots in inventory when zoning, one for Yalmaha and one for off-hand pistol. Easy to make mistakes.

- Reloads during battles seem to take much too long time. This strikes particulary hard against dual pistol wielders due to low ammo clips on most pistols. Manual reloads in between fight don't always work. I can go into battle with two loaded pistols and still I get a reloading msg after the first shot fired.

- Pistols in general today seem to be balanced for other profession that have other damage sources then pistols, we only have pistols.

Polymorphs

- Too few. We have five polymorphs. Leet, (reet-flurryfeathers-parrot), (cub-wolf-cerberus), (Grinning hunter-Sabretooth), and _very late_ Pit lizard.

- We feel that morphs are pretty much for showing off. We think the recent upgrade on the fighting morhps should have been done a lot more generously.

- None of the morphs can be used in missions. Leet (sneaking) doesn't work anymore. The rest don't fit through doors or are outdoor-only.

- Wolf form comes with night vision which stack with low-light targetting scopes. Remove night vision on the morphs. Night vision goggles cost 500cr for those who wants it.

- Flurry feather and parrot etc serves little or no purpose. 99% of the players has a Yalmaha at this level.

- Cat morphs & pit lizard comes with a pretty severe speed penalty. It isn't like we become tanks because grinning hunter gives +60AC. +60 AC at level ~80 is nothing. Grinning hunter for example only gives +1% crit, +60AC, +34dmg. +34 dmg is decent, not more, +1%crit and 60AC is close to nothing.

Nanos

- Third best heals behind Doctors and MA's. We are far behind the MA's. Our heals are slooow and they heal much less.

- Team heals are too far apart in levels.

- Low range on heals. 15m on some and 20m on some.

- Worthless skill buffs. Treatment, first aid & perception-aimed shot is what we have a few buffs for. Not only are these pretty useless buffs they also require us to raise Psy mod. Another nano skill just for a few useless buffs. We want skill buffs that support at least our most common weapon lines: Multi ranged, mulit melee, pistols & 2he.


Fellow Adventurers! Please continue post your thoughts in this thread, I feel like I missed a lot of them...

I did not re-roll because I like this character and spent many hours developing it. I'm fairly satisfied with the change over - plenty of interesting shotguns to choose from.
