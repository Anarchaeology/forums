Leet
Apartments
Apartments... Furniture... Even since before AO came out I'd been incredibly interested in the ability to have an apartment... Coming from EQ (ohh the horrible memories), I was glad to see we weren't all going to be homeless bums roaming the landscape. Anyway... Here are a few ideas:

News Terminal: You know that weird machine thing on your wall in your apartment? They could make that into a News Terminal, which would act as a weekly newspaper. Every Sunday (IRL time, for the sake of simplicity and to make it easier on those writing the articles) they could update the e-newspaper, with coverage on the war, interviews with Ross, and even restuarant or club reviews if they had the time. I think this would give even the non-RP'ers reasons to visit their apartment, so they could check if anything new has happened since they staged an attack on a rebel outpost and if they should be careful now (Headline: Rebel Outpost captures huge plasma shipment)

Static TV: This would be a TV, but instead of showing actual programs (incredibly difficult and really not worth the effort), it would have a few pictures that it cycles through and a static sound. They could even make it so you could have a choice between 4 general channels for different pictures (action, comedy, romance, horror - ala The Sims)

Working Stereos: Make it so we can use those stereos we decided to save up for the minute we saw them back at level 2.
You could just have the music from the clubs playing, and if the player uses the stereo, it plays the next song.

Furniture as Containers: This is a popular idea, and I think it should be implemented. Certain furniture could have an extra feature "something like 'container' in the description" which lets people know what they can and can't put their precious food into.

At this point that's really all I would like to see (except for chairs and tables, which are in progress), and I think it will be a great day when people actually want to hang out in their apartments rather than go out to club... Simply because their apartment looks so much cooler.
