It's amazing how many people try to answer your question when they don't actually understand what you're asking :P

The question, for those of you who are confused, is whether or not you can preserve shortcuts on your shortcut bar after you die. If you've ever tried putting a link up there to anything in your inventory (a gun or firstaid, etc) you would find that when you die, it vanishes from your bar as well.

Would be nice if it would stay, but I think the fact that the link breaks when the item leaves your inventory implies that there is no way to make the link stay. As long as the items go away, the link will go away too.
