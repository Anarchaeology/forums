The Nanomage...
I have seen alot said here about my worthy brothers, the Atrox. Omni-Med did their job well, creating a breed of human more fiercely loyal to their own ideals than most any human before had been. Omni-Tek then took this wonder and warped it, using supression gas and propoganda to pervert their ideals to most benefit OT. Those who have broken away and seen the light of freedom are the most stalwart advocates of freedom I have met. And I feel your suffering Sllaagor, you have been robbed in a most di****able way.

Omni-med did their job well in creating the Opiflex, with a lighter tissue structure and enhanced metabolism to be able to run the mines for improptu surveys as well as scout the sometimes inhospitable surface of our world. But their light structure makes them far less resilient than they should be, and the metabolism seems to age many before their time. And did OT care? Not at all, an acceptable loss on the spreadsheets.

Omni-med did their job well creating the Nanomage, with an attunement to nanotech and to notum itself that is unparalleled. The nanomage is different though, our patent cannot be leased to other worlds as we cannot exist on other worlds. We are mearly guinea pigs to see what can be done with the ultimate merging of nanotech and the human form...

You may take this as whining, but this is not so. I'm proud to be a part of a breed of human that could never have existed before or without this world. As with our hardy brothers the Artrox and our quick brothers, The Opiflex, we have no heritage of our own. We were born from labs and meant no more to them than the numbers the first were branded with. But we can have a heritage, but it must be here, on our Rubi-Ka.

I refuse to allow this heritage to be further tainted by OT. My descendants will not be under such a Tyranny as corporate rule. My descendants will live in a peaceful world where freedom is treasured. My descendants will _not_ be numbers.

And that is what was missed, by the originator of this thread. That the clans are all united in one respect: That our lives are less important than a heritage of freedom for the future. This surpasses all political boundries and any dictate from the CoT. If either the Council or OT threaten this future, they will have all the clans baring down upon them.

May the heavens have mercy on whomever tries to disturb our future heritage.

A word from the Opifex corner.
Thank you, Seishin, for the kind statements regarding me and my kin. I once also worked for Omni-Tek. Not in the mines, but in the wastelands. My missions were compulsory, not voluntary, and I would always be ill-rationed for them. Sent out on scouting missions for new notum sources and other minerals without the proper rations for my metabolism, nor liquid to last me for long. I had to learn how to treat Bloodcreeper flesh so it would be edible, to cut the cactus so it would bear liquid without killing it and losing that water supply if I need it again. Even to use Scorpoid parts to enhance my own clothing as protection from the sandstorms. What I wouldn't give to never have to have gone through this.

When I was found near Bliss, I was emaciated and haggardly to such a degree, the gaurds thought I was some new type of mutant OT had let loose. I have never been made to perform "volunteer" work unless I was willing to of my own accord regardless of my motivations. My lot in life has greatly improved since aligning with the clans. Fantastic furnishings, a Yalmaha, a pleasure yacht and 2 cars are at my disposal. Had I remained with OT, all my fringe benefits would consist of an inexpensive funeral. My life span is also expected to run for a whole 23% longer than it would've had I not lost my mind temporarily and wandered away from my mission area and OT. Stress really can be a detrimental thing to your health.

Now even though my treatment at the hands of Omni-Tek was "less than satisfactory", I do not bear a grudge against it's employees who have done me no personal wrong. That's not to say I don't hold my cards close to my chest when dealing with OT personnel for whatever reason I might. After all, freedom on Rubi-Ka should be unconditional for all who want it. And if you don't want freedom, that's your choice. Ironic, that that would still be considered a form of freedom in and of itself, choosing not to choose. I choose never to return into the OT fold. Who'd water my plants and feed my Leets?
