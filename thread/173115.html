RELOADED : adult recruitment drive
Hello
If you're a clanner you might have seen us around lately, with teh big nasty CAPS name

First of all, let me introduce ourselves :
RELOADED started a few weeks ago, as all the active ones of "The Fellowship", and members from "R - D"...we started over to get rid of the...uhm...people that dont play anymore that fills up lists that made us look big when we in reallity was a small group of friends rather than a guild.
And that is still what we are...a group of friends
If you check our size on the net you'll see quite a few members, but half of those are alts, and without beeing sure on the exact number of players, I estimate 30ish...15ish high levels, and 15ish a bit lower (on the way up )

We have some "rules", and they sound something like this :
1 : everything inside guild is free
2 : we help eachother out if someone needs something
3 : no mooching

in addition to this we far prefer "adult" players, and almost all members are in the range of 20-40 years old (a few under, and a few over)
This is to avoid...-how to put it without beeing flamed-...stupidity that grows off when you reach "adulthood" :P
But it comes back to the "we are more a group of friends than a guild part"...we are adult friends that have fun together...and not everything that amuses a 14 year old amuse a 30 year old and vice versa...

Other things worth mentioning is that the remainders of Scorpion Noirs are joining us these days, making us even stronger, and we are planning to start raids at the end of the week along with another guild...wich will be revealed after our guildmeeting on wednesday...
we dont have any towers - yet - this is beeing discussed as we go, but so far we have agreed that we need a few more players to defend it properly (wich this post is a part of acheiving )

So to sum it up short :
If your a adult player looking for other adults to share your time with, having fun while playing, even if it's for the social part, send me or any other member you might see around a tell, and we'll be more than happy to have you.
level is of no importance (as everyone playing, sooner or later will ding from time to time )
