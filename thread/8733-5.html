I can't believe I'm doing this ...
OK, I can't believe that I'm posting in a heated debate with the newest post nearly two weeks old, but I stumbled upon it by chance, and it got me a little riled up, so bear with me, OK?

Thanks.

First off, I'm gay. (Gay=Homo, in this case. Actually, sometimes I can be pretty lame, too, so who knows?! )

<sarcasm>I hope this gives me the credibility to make a comment on this topic. You need to be gay to have a worthwhile opinion on it. </sarcasm>

Unbelievable!

But I should qualify my sarcasm: I think that gay people sometimes , yes sometimes , have unique opinions because of the unique situation they are in. There are times when I think to myself, "This is a topic gay people understand better than straight people." But then I usually run into a gay person with a wholly different (and completely valid!!) take on the issue. Then I talk to a straight friend who shares my opinion, and I'm right back where I started. So let's not discredit someone's thoughts because of their sexual orientation, disclosed or not.

Here's my issue:

We can say that in this case, "gaaaaay" is used to mean "lame," and probably, we can all agree. I don't think anyone intended any harm or insult from this ad campaign. In fact, I got a big chuckle out of it. But don't you dare quote me out of context, because that doesn't mean that I think it's acceptable usage of the term.

Here's why. When we say "gay" to mean "lame," we are strongly implying that the two terms are equivalent. If "gay"="lame"="useless"="something laughable", what are the consequences? (I'll ignore, for this purpose, the word origins of "can't walk"="lame"="useless"!)

Here are the consequences. Young people grow up hearing "gay" used to describe anything people don't like. The logical conclusion is that people don't like anything gay! So, if you're a young gay man or woman, what are you going to think about yourself?

Consciously or subconsciously, you learn that "you"="gay"="lame"="useless"="laughable". And it takes a very long time to unlearn that.

Some people NEVER get to unlearn this.

Do you know the biggest reason that teenagers commit suicide? The leading cause of adolescent suicide in the United States is the fear that you MIGHT be gay.

Did you read that? MIGHT BE GAY! Not, "because they're gay." They just aren't sure. Maybe a teenage boy has a dream about another guy, and he starts to wonder why.

Can you imagine the shame of that? Each day when he goes to school, he hears someone make a gay joke. And each time, he has to struggle not to blush, because if he blushed, someone might think he's gay. But it's hard not to blush, because he just had a dream about a guy, and he's scared about what that might mean.

So he goes home. And maybe he hears his brother make a gay joke about someone. Again, he has the same trouble, trying not to blush.

Dinner. Dad makes a joke. Maybe Mom doesn't laugh, but she doesn't say anything, does she? And his brother laughs, while he tries even harder not to blush, because what would his family think if they knew he had a dream about a guy last night? Would they make the same jokes about him? Would he be a joke to them?

Finally, he goes into his room, where he can stop hiding from his feelings. Maybe he goes online to blow off some steam. Maybe he's an AO player. Maybe he spends time in these forums.

Two questions:

How does he feel when he sees that ad?

How would he feel if he came to these forums and did a search on the word "gay," and found 61 threads, 60 of which use the word to talk about nerfs, players, and Funcom staffers they don't like, and this thread?

Among 12000 threads, only 1 talks about gay people who might play Anarchy Online.

I know how I'd feel. I'd feel lonely.

And I guess that's my point: why isolate people that way, when you claim not to care about their sexual orientation?


/rant


In closing, I'd just like to say that I'm a little surprised how heated I just got writing this post. I came out nearly ten years ago, and just thinking about what it's like for young gay people still gets to me the same way.


I play Zephyn on RK-2. You know what? He's gay, too.
