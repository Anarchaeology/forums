Oki, most of the Numbers you see like---> 1730 X 2640 <-- are location coordinates on the maps. if you put your mouse over a spot on the mini map, the coords are there on mouse over. If you press F9 the Coords are displayed in the chat window. For bugs and info you might give to ARK and support, a Shift+F9 is good for a little more info.
