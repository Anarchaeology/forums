Outdoor missions
Ok, like everyone else at my level I'm tired of spending time underground because there's nothing else productive I can do for my character aboveground. I spend a lot of time helping others, RPing, wandering, etc... but there's no viable rewards that compare to indoor missions with a team. Everyone knows this.

How about outdoor-based mission playfields? I don't mean missions set in the wilderness where everyone can go to, but outdoor areas specifically spawned for missions in a similar manner to the indoor areas spawned for missions. Like an arch that you can pass through somewhere that leads to a new temporary playfield.

The possibilities for this are endless... a swamp somewhere, ruins of something or other, a town or outpost (possibly one that's monster infested), a secluded valley, etc.

I'm not sure of the coding differences between indoor and outdoor areas, so I have no idea whether this is an easy or an impossible addition to the game. I do know that it would be really fun to have a mission in an outdoor area somewhere instead of yet another dark indoor area. Being outdoor, it could have its own set of challenges and can reward various character abilities that aren't particularly optomized for indoor areas.

I'd like to see the fun of outdoor hunting combined with the rewards of missions.
