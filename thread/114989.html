Woot! 14.9 is Crat Luv Patch
Wow, Funcom actually listened. Look at these new changes:

1) Psychic checks to be removed from all Charms and Fears (some mobs will still have immunity tho).

3) Nelly's Dress can now be converted into a Tuxedo.
