I'll probably be adding other odds-and-ends in the next couple weeks. Getting my new search system set up sucked away most of my development time recently.

One of the more interesting additions recently is JB and my revelation regarding what I call the "ModifyResistance" function...

These are often used in MonsterEquippers...such as:
http://v020u46eff.maximumasp.com/AOD...sp?AOID=165339

As you can see, Mr. Warr has a 100% resistance to 7 different nano lines.

This is interesting because it shows what MOBs (mostly bosses) are specially resistant to...anything not listed is castable on them. (Although they still may have absurd Nano Resist!)

Tara has a few more invulnerabilities than Ian...in that I don't believe she can get hit with Nano Shutdown and something else...

This also applies to bosses, since boss mod rings have these as well. Certain bosses seem invulnerable to MA fear nanos, while others are not...

At any rate, something fun to play around with. =)
