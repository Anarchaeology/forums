A Message to Fellow Veterans
Hello fellow veterans,

It's been a long while since I have posted on these forums, though I do play, though not as much as I once did, back in the old days.

AO is a year old now, and we've been around since the beginning, we've seen it all, the bad, the worse, and the somewhat better. It seems things are still evolving, though ever so slowly. Radiman is back in town, if only in spirit. I can see the boards are a bit more lively since his return, and hopefully with that will come the story we have all hoped for for quite some time.

There was a vision in the beginning...though it is fading fast, I can still remember it. In this vision we were all one personality in a great big world of unlimited possibilities. I can remember logging in and feeling totally overwhelmed, feeling like the decisions I made that day in-game could have effect on the Clans, or Omni-Tek, the missions I took helped my faction. I remember seeking out ways to affect the wonderful world I was a part of.

Do any of you still remember logging on and feeling like I did? Can you remember back when everything was so unpredictable, and the possiblilities for our character's journeys were unlimited?

Where do you think we are headed? Will things grow to be better or will our twinkling flame of hope and possibility be smoldered by business, marketing, and the competition?

I not sure if any of you know or care about this, but for a long time I have been fighting to bring some of the storyline to Rubi-Ka 2, and myself and my guild have bent over backwards to try and provide some roleplaying and events for our fellow Rubi-Ka 2 citizens. Sadly, it seems nothing has made much impact from where I stand, though the fight is worthy, and my friends are wonderful, I cannot keep from feeling our light is being smoldered. I do not want to believe that this could be happening, and I will stick around until the very end, trying my very best to do what I can, I still fear the worst.

My fellow veterans, I beg of you, help me continue to believe. Post your opinions & experiences of where you have been, and where you think the game is headed. I WANT to breathe new life into this game, this world...if only I knew how...

Hi Sepentura,

I am sory to hear things have taken a turn for the worse for you and your group. I recall when we met you had a really good thing going.

I think the key the South Rubi-Ka Society's success, if success is the right term, has been the fact that we are not a story-based guild and that we look at small local stories rather than large, Earth-shattering ones.

There is a real problem in mmorpg in that, try as you may, you cannot really affect the world, and many would argue that given all player pay the same subscription fee, that is exactly how it should be.

Find a niche which is your own, which you can control and one which you are not waiting on FC or other guilds to progess your stories. Rubi-Ka is a big world, there must be a lot goign on besides the war.

To make a perhaps frivolous example. Remember Ghostbusters? There was even an rpg on it which I enjoyed running very much.
Who is to say you could not transfer the Ghostbuster concept to Rubi-Ka, perhaps givning it a notum/metaphysicist twist?

One thing we found that works well is we have one fixed session per week (Tuesday) for a table top roleplaying session for which one of us will prepare an adventure to run ( or continue on an existing campaign). This does mean that things can get very quiet during the rest of the week, but many of us have been playing for a year nowand there is more to life than AO. But even when it gets quite, our members know that come Tuesday, the old magic will return.

Anyway I hope this helps. At the end of the day it is hard and you most also accept that at some point we will all leave AO. But a year on, I am still loving every minute.

Jynne
Supa Leet
Yes Bionitrous more or less hit the nail on the head. It's not about big things, it's about little details. I don't want to replace the COT. I don't want to have hordes of mutants popping up once a week. Those kinds of events are too often GM uberkill-fests or lag-fests where you can't do anything even if you can stay connected.

What I want is to be able to use a hacker tool on a sub-atomic memory storage unit or a mainframe-human interface, and access a data file with story background info in it. A chunk of some scientist's notes, an explorer's journal, something. I want to use a lockpick on a nanobots container and get a random nano buff, debuff, explosion, heal, or nothing. I want to go to a 'library terminal' and be able to do research on names, places, creatures, and items in the game. I want to be able to talk to an NPC wandering the desert in Broken Shores and find things out from them, like how a Quake Lizard gets up on its hind legs and does the macarena to attract its mate. At the very absolute least, I'd like a few more descriptions of mission messages and extra mission types, and lots of unique from-scratch tradeskill-only items.

I want the world to be an immersive and colorful backdrop for my character to come to life in... not primarily through big one-time events, but through rich, varied, and useful information that I can seek out and explore to find.

A mutant attack would be great... if it were more than just a mutant attack.
