Reposting an article
Sorry for Spam but here is an article I wrote about this issue on the professionals forum and also reproduced it on the adv and fixer forums. A last note before the article. When people discuss uber-twinking, remember that equiping a gun is a permanent advantage. Casting a nano only works once then after an hour the advantage is gone. There is no way to get considerable nanoskill benefits at low levels with gear. Only with buffs that require a load of ncu (which is why the one exception is the fixer buff). The article:

Example #1:
Cyclic Artic Gale... Level req 75

Reqs 394 Matmet/Matcrea

A Solitus level 75 with maxed abilities for mm/mc has a 341 base with no implanting or buffing. That's about as trivial as it gets.

A level 65 nano has about 294 before items/buffs/plants. I'm pretty sure at level 65 its trivial and 60 would be possible.

Example #2:
Cyclic Retaliatory Venom Spit ... Level req 175

Reqs 741 Matmet/Matcrea

A Solitus 175 with maxed abilities for mm/mc has a base of 624/627 in mat crea/matmet respectively. You would gain 105 in both of those two skills from implants, and around 5-6 more from int before any gear. Expertises and we are in the 760 range for the skills. A 162 Soli has only about of a couple of points difference in skills. This is all with no effort at attatining any modifier-boosting items or switching to nanobreed.

This isn't a unimportant issues folks. Please do not let it die. Here are the obvious ramifications:

1. Atroxs will eventually be better than other races and nanomages will become unplayable. Once the level caps are in play for the majority of nanos, nanoskill advantages are 100% evaporated.

2. Mochams/Wrangle are eventually useless.

3. Since the primary skills reach their threshold easily, players won't max those skills and will instead put all their points into secondary skills. I would ipr out if I were an adv for 14.4 out of primary skills and dump them in blue skills since more points in primary skills doesn't help.

4. Modifier items are eventually useless.

This change will eventually become a breed-killing nerf for 1-3 breeds and nerfs to MP and Traders. Additonally its a nerf for any profession who gets "fixed". You will always be worse than profs whose nanos aren't confined by level reqs.

The adventurers and fixers should be wailing at this issue since it will effect them first. It would be wise to continue at this issue until there is a justification from an official source or an admisison that its done because the IP nanoskill system is a failure.
