I had an idea for dungeons how to solve this.

I heard EQ has a thing where if you are high level, and you get loot rights cause you outdamaged, you are unable to loot, and it just prints that fact. Thats a start, but I think that just gives more griefing opportunities than anything.

How about make areas that you cannot enter if you are too high level. It could be like gas zones now, where it gives you a grace time, except if you are too level it warps you back out (or kills you), and make it considered 100% gas to that player so they cant attack anything in it. Remember a given zone can have different areas of gas, so we're not talking about locking high levels out of entire zones, just certain areas.

That way, theoretically, dungeons are actually hard to get through, its not just a game of who has more higher level friends, but who can make their current characters powerful enough.

You might say, what if you are really high level and really want the item? For example I saw a level 180 solder in stairs of madness getting a nelebs because he was just a few points off from getting some higher nano (TMS i think). Hmm, how to make that work? Maybe spawn a new dungeon per level range? Havent thought of a way to make that address that issue. The goal being make it a challenge, not a camp to complete a quest, while also keeping the items more rare. I would rather die 100 times trying to take out some really high level bot, and try to gather up a force of people in my level to take it out... then just wait for 6 hours waiting for the spawn. I think making it a tough challenge could keep the reward as rare as camping if kept challenging enough.

With TK, it could be some complicated solution where if you are over a certain level, the zone becomes 100%, so they are unable to damage the quest mob. Like, conditional gas supression.
Then you are only competeing with people who are also of the right level for the reward, and the most high levels could do is buff and heal you.

I think this might be a more relevent solution when there are tons of quests, for all level ranges, so for example if you are level 100+ you wouldnt want to do the TK quest because theres pads of uberness from another quest you should be doing. That way the level 60 people (who TK quest is for) can compete among themselves only for it. And really TK is VERY hard for that level, so they could make the spawn time fast (10 minutes?), and if you can kill him with a team (or 2 teams!) of level 60-90, then you deserve all the decks you can farm.

Of course, I start to get inspired to help brainstorm ideas with some of you smart people, but then I realize, oh yeah, Funcom really doesnt listen to us, so why bother?

Make dungeons 25% and let the players kill each other over it. This is why Camelot might be a really beautful dungeon, but I'll never know because I dont like pvp.

Make it so if you kill someone, and they havent saved in awhile, you get their xp, and get to loot items they got since last save. This only encourages ganking/griefing, not fair pvp. If you wanted to encourage people to camp the grids/whompas or common mission entrances, is there a better way than to reward the person who saved recently versus the person who just completed a mission and got a lot of xp/mission reward?

Level requirements on new nanos because they gave up on trying to tweak the other requirements in a balanced way.
