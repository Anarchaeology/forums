Another IS Employee Found Dead? - An OI Report
The Omni Inquisitor
February 24 29477 RST

Nita Insegeli, a female in her later thirties was found permanently dead last week in her apartment. Cause of death was found to be a disease that stopped her heart. Omni-Pol media Liaison Burton Shogren told the Inquisitor shortly after.

Nita, most widely known for her strong voice speaking out against some of Omni-Corp's policies within Rome Green, has raised some eyebrows within the OP-IS.

"Ms. Insegeli was not in any way harmed or attacked by someone within the corporation." Deputy-Director May Zhen said yesterday. "The cause of death was natural and her services to the corporation shall be missed. She has voiced some problems within our Company in the past few months and it is a shame she could not work out these differences with us, and instead deciding to try and become a martyr for her cause. The funeral services will be held tomorrow mid-afternoon."

What Deputy Director Zhen refuses to comment on is why another worker earlier this month was also found to have committed suicide. The first death, a male in his late twenties by the name of Frel Detra, was considered by many of his associates to have a passion for life.

"I knew Frel. He would come in everyday, and work till everyone had left. Then, come back and do it again the next day. He cared for the status of Rubi-Ka, and if he had a problem with the Corporation, he'd sooner join a Clan than to kill himself." said Employee Jack Rilian of the OP-IS.

Sooner join a clan? Many thought that was exactly what had happened. Making a choice to leave Omni-Corp, he was murdered before he could make that choice. Deputy Director Zhen had promised to look into this case, and as of yet no other information has been told. Though she denies these two events are unrelated, some think otherwise.

"If you'd ask me... I'd say that Deputy Director Zhen is trying to launch a coup against the corporation, by putting all her people in place and eliminating those who are not." one Employee who wished to go un-named said.

A Corporate Coup indeed? Zhen, what do you have in store for others in Omni-Corp? Being the head of Internal Services, who is watching how you handle affairs within the Company?

Director of Omni-Pol on Rubi-Ka, Ivan Sergeyitch, and Deputy Director Zhen declined to have a private interview with the OI.
