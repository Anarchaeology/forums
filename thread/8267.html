Want to make MA's happy?
With the upcoming nerfs.. err.. I mean "fixes" (aka "lovin") coming to the MA's in the next patches. Here's some ideas for FC to think about to make MA's happy again.

The 2 biggest nerfs (oops.. did I slip again?).. I mean fixes will be the reduction of the damage BwT inflicts (and I think I heard Shen is getting the shaft too, not sure though) and also making it so MA's can ONLY equip MA weapons. So basically no more hammer MA's... not that I'll really miss them since they looked ridiculous, but I understand why they used them. I'm hoping along with this change, the patch will add A LOT of new MA weapons because the majority of the current ones suck and suffer the same minimum damage problem that our fists do because they are all fast, low damage weapons. (Not to mention that fact that there are NO 2HB or 2HE MA weps currently in the game) And also the fact that we will be the only profession forced to used certain weapons. Oh.. and that bows still don't work right.. oh ya, and that our MA special AoS doesn't work at all over QL100.. oh ya.. and Flower of Life hurts us instead of heals us.. did I mention these have been bugged since beta 4? I hope those are part of the "fixes" and "lovin" that FC has in store for us. Anyways.. I digress...

My first suggestion is actually 2 about the same items: MA special attacks. First, these are MA special attacks. If you are going to make weapons that only MA's can use.. why aren't MA special attacks MA only. I mean.. they are "MA SPECIAL attacks" are they not? Why can any other profession use them also? Second, since now you are making all these MA specific weapons.. why not allow us to use our MA special attacks while the weapons are equipped? If you want to add a requirement like you must have 300 in MA skill before you can use both a weapon and MA special attack fine... but let us use them both. You're nerfing all our special attacks anyway.. at least make them somewhat useful to us again. Or better yet.. still allow us to use ANY weapon we want.. if we use a non-MA weapon then we can't use our specials.. but if we use a MA weapon, then we can use specials at the same time.

Second suggestion is about dimache. Give MA's a bonus to the recycle time. Let us use it every 10 minutes.. or 5 minutes. I mean.. we really don't have any kind of powerhouse attack. Sure, we occasionally crit a Brawl. But nothing like the damage I see Doc's doing with Vektors. I was proud of myself when I crit a brawl for 1100 but then a Doc lower than my level (I'm level 70, btw) comes up and crits 4 times in a row for 1300 damage. I see level 80 agents hitting 4000 damage aimed shots and hear of higher level ones hitting 10000 damage aimed shots. My Dimache is fully maxed and implanted and I dimache for around 1400.. when it hits... which it seems to only hit maybe 6 or 7 times out of 10. Then we have to wait 30 minutes. Agents only have to wait until the next fight to try and pull another Aimed Shot.. a soldiers Full Auto and Burst recycle after a few minutes (I have no numbers on how hard they hit for, but I know its higher then my dimache). MA's are supposed to be specialists at that kinda thing anyway.. it even makes sense that we would be better with Dimache and not find it so draining (as the description says). Oh.. you say some MA weapons will have Fast Attack? nice.. but see, in order to use an MA weapon we already have to raise 4 skills.. you want to add another dark blue skill to that so we can Fast Attack? As far as I can tell.. Fast Attack is basically just an extra attack.. I've never seen anyone Fast Attack for 5k damage. What did that Doc have to raise to use his Vektor? One skill? Hmm.. I see...

Yes, I'm a bitter old MA.. hehe...
