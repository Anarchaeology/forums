Funcom Please Mark Team Mission Portals
This is a request to Funcom to consider marking the up and down portals (maybe green and red) in Team Missions so that you can find them easily again. The reason is because of the many many times me or a team member has died and has to get back to the group deep in the mission, or you need to leave and come back for some reason, or you have completed the mission by killing the boss and you just need to leave the mission (cannot grid) and you spend a lot of time running around everywhere on each level trying to find the portals. It is bad enough when you know it is in the SouthEast for example, and you have to go north, then west and find how to get back to SouthEast because access is not straightforward, but marking the portals at least would help you to know where to find them.

They could show as marked after the first time the team activates them by using the up portal. Then both the up portal and the down portal can then show on that level, and repeating for each level.

Please think about this suggestion Funcom.
