Missions
Ok we all know that missions are THE way to get the XP, the cash and the loot.

Hunting is now done by only a few, but not because they think its better than missions, but because they enjoy the outdoors more, they are bored of the same old mobs and indoor zones, and it's always cramped and repedetive.

Sure they are releasing the all new mob camps and all. But people will still do missions, no matter what.

So here's a possible solution.

Instead of luring people away from missions, make the missions enjoyable.
I'm talking instead of having indoor mission rooms with the old '3 mobs in the main, 1 mob every few rooms' layout, why not make 'outdoor missions'. Not literally outdoors, but instead, you zone into the mission and instead of killing mobs in caves and complexes, you have to locate the mission objective amidst a large herd of <insert big scary monster name here>, all wandering around a river between two large cliff faces, or a dense forest packed with extremely tall trees...
Sure it wouldn't REALLY be outside, or a very large zone, but it's an improvement.

And of course, don't just change the location, add many wierd and wonderfull monsters to kill that fit the landscape.
One of the funner things I did was to hunt out at mort near the dungeons, sure the xp isnt as good as inside, but its great seeing some bloke riding a huge dinosaur-like creature and a horned demon come running at you with bared fangs etc....
A lot of effort must have been put in the think up and create those beasts, and all we do is shoot up other humans?

I typed this all up knowing very well that actually adding this type of stuff to the game would be impossible/extremely hard/time consuming, but hey, just something to read in between the:
"I'm quitting the game, so I'll just try and convince everyone else to hate the game too" threads, as well as the
"nerf this, nerf that" threads
the "I lost 300 xp and the GM did nothing, cry bitc h moan complain" threads
the "this guy asked me for a buff, kill his family!" threads

You get me.

Cheers

I'd like to see some profession specific missions.

Adventurer: You'd get an outdoors one, as you said. Maybe the reward would involve finding and staying near ('observing') a creature for a set period of time.

Agent: This mission would involve sneaking. A lot. Extra bonuses for not getting 'detected', but it would be a lot harder to stay secret. Probably would involve sabotage, or assasination. Or both.

Bureaucrat: Some sort of secratary thing... Gah, I can't think properly right now. Maybe the reward could be finding, picking up, and 'using' order forms to gain a reward item..

Doctor: Doctors would need to infiltrate a warzone-style mission area. Lots of corpses would be spread around, as well as enemy mobs. Token reward would rely on not amount of kills, but amount of 'corpses' the Doctor used spare Treat kits on.

Engineer: Something that would revolve around robots and mechanics...perhaps the mission would take place in a junkyard. Maybe bots could even get a little damage boost for taking an Engi-specific mission. (Oh, come on- Engis have it tough, give them a break) The reward might be given for assembling robot parts that could be found in the mission area.

Enforcer: Something that involves beating the living %^#$^$ out of everything in sight. (Some sort of extra bonus for killing all the mobs in the mission?)

Fixer: Oooh, now this is an interesting one. I've got a special idea for this one..
Fixers would go to a specialized terminal. This terminal would give them some sort of item. Now, the higher level the mission was, the more 'illegal' the item is. This means that if you hang around in a guard's LOS for too long, they'll shoot you regardless of faction. (In reeeaaally high level missions, guards would shoot you on sight.)
Anyway, when they get the item, the fixer would have to travel to a playfield and find the named NPC. Upon giving the item to mentioned NPC, Fixer recieves award. (Don't you just love my grammar?!) Of course, the further you have to travel, the larger the reward.

Martial Artist: Let's face it...MA = Ninja. Period. For the MAs, we'll have some sort of cool night assasination mission. You can either bash your way through or sneak past the mobs.

Meta-Physicist: I'm really not sure, but I imagine MPs would have to use their pets to complete the mission.

Nano-Technician: Might involve collecting 'super' nano-program parts from mobs, then assembling them for a one-time-use nanoprogram that would allow the NT to blow up the mission area- and any remaining mobs. Long cast time.

Soldier: Involves blowing stuff up. Soldiers would act as a demolitions man, possibly activating some sort of explosive device at the end of the mission, then given a timer to get out.

Trader: I don't know, but I imagine it would be something similar to the Fixer missions.

Questions, comments, and suggestions are welcome.
