Then I stopped being asked, and someone from Omni -Tek invited me, and I accepted, as an observer. As for the benefit I might be providing other than a glorified secretary, I have to ask what the issue really is here? I have never asked anyone for anything, so I am not sure who said I was wanting to go along with Clans on "their" raids. I have asked for little else besides safe passage through, and other than a few pot shots at the dragon, (which promptly got her attention and a few 2300 point hits back, ouch, "hi reclaim"), I have not been inside that dungeon since.

Am I providing some secret tactical advice to either side about the other? No. I will never betray one side to the other, ever. Am I there for loot? No. Well ok, I did win a piece of dragon marrow on an OT raid once, which I promptly gave back, you got me there... *smirks* And I have even stopped entering any raffles now, (yes I was invited to the raffles). Am I helping a team with PvP? Er no, since I am not even on a team, and the only time I was, I left the team so the rest could PvP, and I got killed very quickly, but that was a long time ago. Am I providing some vital service to either side? Certainly nothing anyone else could not do. But yes, I do see your point. I have carried no weapons in, and still got tabbed by both sides. But I must say I now have the image of myself being perceived as a seagull following a shrimp boat, waiting for leftovers thrown overboard. What would we gain by going? We would have little purpose, little skill, and little success.

I never wanted to cause this type of friction, and I do not want to go where I am not wanted. Apologies to Azz and anyone else that might be upset at neutrals presence, as tourists or otherwise. Thank you also to all of you for allowing us to visit.
