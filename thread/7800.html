Entertaintment (official tip to FC/Cosmik)
I once did a small standup-comedian act in Clon****, outside grid, telling omni jokes.. Im clan myself... Fun, yet it points at something which is missing in game: Entertainment.
What i really would like to see, is:

Entertaintment bars, where a comedian perfoms. This could easily be done by programming a NPC to tell random jokes, maybe one in the minute, where he shouts them. (aye, i know regular players can do this, but typing sometimes takes time, and its not easy to attract someone to a bar to tell them jokes.. If every bar already HAD this then... )

Fashion shows: WHEN will there be annual fashion shows, where Miir presents his latest collection, which will be available in shops soon? With a catwalk etc?

News broadcasting: "Latest breaking news! Several omnis was spotted holding the bridge in Holes in the wall under siege" etc. Let some GM or every guildleader be able to post such news on a shared channel, or maybe on the new channels where CoT and OT post theirs.

Im sure that if FC would take their time, they could easily implement some major features which would do alot to both gameplay and entertainment..
