My experience has been that I ultimately end up with one do-able mission out of every five missions I accept. When you consider the time it takes to find an infuse nano mission then compound that by the time it takes to travel to five different locations, it yields a fairly small amount of credits per day in the grand scheme of things. Even if I were able to successfully complete two missions per day and find buyers for both items recovered in relatively short order and then if I could find sellers of QL 170ish implants without a lot of hassle, it would still take 19 RL days to acquire all the implants I need.

Though I didn’t do a lot of experimentation, it actually seemed a little more efficient to me to spend the extra time looking for QL 170ish missions where both the pick-up item and reward were implants. While my success rate for the two I did was one success per 10 missions attempted, at least I didn’t have to spend time finding buyers for my items and sellers of the right QL implants.

The same "it sounds good in principle but the reality is different" rule applies to selling treatment buffs. The theory is that all one needs to do is sell 4 or 5 +80 treatment buffs for $200k per day and you’re set. But it just doesn’t work out that way.

Hmmm, it may seem like I’m dismissing your suggestion but I don’t mean it that way. I do appreciate your positive approach and constructive guidance. If money were the only issue with doctors I would probably find the motivation to blitz missions, stand on the street corner selling buffs, and advertise for monster parts. As it stands now though, I think the profession has been overlooked for way too long and desperately needs attention.

There has not been one noteworthy addition to the doctor line of nano or skills since release. Well, there was one, we got a grid teleport, which takes 2 ½ minutes to execute. What is the point of it taking so long, by the way? I'm very glad to have it but why so long to execute? Many of our nanos are still broken or useless. There has not been a single positive adjustment but many negative adjustments like Funcom’s decision to twink mobs with mega-hitpoints and having them aggro on docs. While the game itself is dramatically improved, the doctor profession has grown weaker in every aspect. This is a problem much greater than blitzing mission can resolve.

Rosey, I'm not saying whatever it is you think I'm saying.

Ohsorry, I think there are 2 issues you're talking about.

One is the difficulty making money, and the 2nd is the difficulty being able to buy specific items... especially blank implants.

As to making money, I still believe you could do it better than you have been doing (I could be wrong).

What I would try to do in your situation is to make a list of all the small cave missions in the zones I got missions in, and then as I was rolling up missions, don't even look at the ones which are in other spots. Scroll the window down just far enough to see if it's in one of the spots you can do, and give up on it if it's not.

So then I'd look at the mission if it was in a good spot, and see what the reward was. Infuses aren't the only items which sell. Trader divests and plunders and wrangles will sell, chest armor and back armor from the popular item types will sell for decent money, MP pets will sell, popular weapons will sell, etc.

I'd get a fixer run buff on myself before I did the missions if at all possible.

So roll up 3 missions, fly out and do them, put the rewards in a backpack. Then, don't stand there for hours on end in the city trying to sell those 3 items. Just hit the shopping channel with everything in your backpack, once, every time you're in the city. Each time, maybe only 1 will sell, but over time you sell everything, as long as you don't do missions for crap no one wants.

As to the 2nd issue, being able to actually buy a full set of blank implants... that is a tougher one. You could afford it, probably, but where are you going to find the damn things? You'll probably have to spam the shopping channel with "want to buy q175 blank implants" every time you're in a city for weeks to find them all.

This is one area where professions who are able to blitz/sneak (they're much the same these days, blitzing and sneaking) high level missions have a big advantage, as they can obtain their own items solo while others cannot.

I think here you either have to spam the hell out of shopping channels, message boards, etc, forever, planning way in advance... or more likely, find friends or people in a guild who can do missions that level for you.

When I was a lower level (than I am now)agent, I used to have a few higher level players who would pay me to do missions for them. They could roll up full hard missions of a level I could not roll up myself, but I could actually sneak the missions successfully while they could not. This is another possibility for you.

I'm curious about how much money you could actually make per hour blitzing 60% missions, if you only took them in small cave areas and were as efficient as possible about the whole system.
