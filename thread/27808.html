Guild clothes
This might have been suggested before:

How about Guild clothes, both for social and combat occations?

As it is now, most people run around in either Primus coats (and sometimes Decus or Tertius) or Nano coats. This is boring because most people looks the same.

There could be a generic template of a coat that the guild can pick up, adding its own logo onto the chest or the back, and dye it in whatever colours the guild want.

By adding Guild clothes, all guilds would be a little bit more unique, and Rubi-Ka would probably be a little bit more colourful than it is now...
