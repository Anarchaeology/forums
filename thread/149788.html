MoK Changes - (Press Release)
I am stepping down as president of Mercenaries of Kai(TaG) and promoting Medset to this position.

Our organization has progressed to a point where my attention is needed outside of this world. I'll be moving off planet for some time to look after our endeavors elsewhere.

Medset has my confidence and my backing in any decisions he makes. He's a well-seasoned leader and an honorable warrior.

(OOC)

I'd like to thank everyone who helped me accomplish things in AO as a leader. Unfortunately, remembering all of those people would be nearly impossible but I'll try to recognize those who stand foremost in my mind.

Cosmik (You da man, wherever you are!), Cz, Saurun, Psiraven, Fiix (the real fiix), Scorpion, Ashed, Piikey, Princ3, (ALL of you old TC'ers), Medset, Combateer, Mightymouse, Claire, Messman(Who made me look good all the time... HA!), All of the original professionals team, all the ark's who put up with me screaming at them even though I knew their hands were tied and everyone else I forgot to mention.

AO was fun... The game was mostly great. What made it worthwhile however, were the people who honored me by trusting me to lead them. All of my MoK'ers, my friends... Thanks very much for an awesome 2 years!

I'll be around to check up on things, flights are getting cheaper to and from Rubi-Ka due to the Omni stock dropping like a rock so I hope it will be often.

I wish you all the best of luck in Shadowlands!

Best,
