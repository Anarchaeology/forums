Top Ten Nukes
I have played a bit with my Formula Search Page and thought I'd post the top ten nukes for NTs. If you want the background for this page and values, you can read the post New Nano Init page under General Chat on the NTL board. The top ten nukes list is based upon average damage per second, given that:

1. Each 200 nanoinit drop attack time by 1 second
2. Attack time can't drop beneath 0.1 seconds
3. Damage type has nothing to say

I am not sure how much the aggro bar affects casting time, but if you know just reply here or on the NTL board. Do the same if you have comments on the values and/or calculations, or some other things

So, if you have 1200 in nanoinit and 1000 in MC, these nukes are your best choice for casting the best average damage per second:

1. Volcanic Eruption (AE) - 625 dmg/sec
2. Tremor (AE) - 602 dmg/sec
3. Blaze of Hephaestos (AE) - 550 dmg/sec
4. Ferocious Impactor Missile - 508 dmg/sec
5. Glacial Finality - 499 dmg/sec
6. Frigid Landscape (AE) - 496 dmg/sec
7. Stargasp - 471 dmg/sec
8. Gravitational Anomaly - 459 dmg/sec
9. Izgimmer's Enveloping Flame - 458 dmg/sec
10. Localized Dimensional Inversion - 452 dmg/sec

To use all these nukes, you will not need more than 871 in MC (just to cast).

The best regular nuke (not AE) is Ferocious Impactor Missile - 508 dmg/sec (MC req. 747), which means 5080 dmg per 10 seconds. How is this compared to good weapons?
I guess we have a problem about lag and stuff which can cause us to not be able to actually cast the stupid things as fast as we really could (we can't hit 'Q' and then relax until the fight is over).
