150+

Everyone loves the +9% xp, makes leveling a little bit faster.

If no MA, everyone loves the +7% crit speech.

If people got MOP, they surely love the +AR/+DMS speech

Calms aren't really used a lot at theese levels, but I guess they can come in handy once in a while.

People really hate it when you charm a mob, they want to kill it for xp, and charming just disrupts the flow of xp. Don't bother doing that. The extra damage from that charmed enf or whatever means you save 1 second per kill, and people really don't care.

The bot is pretty much useless, and no one wants to wait for you while you drag it around. Forget the bot and stay in range so people are affected by the speech instead. Make some green self castable bot just before breaking in the entrance room and boss room, then let it tag along until it gets stuck and terminate it.

With a gun and nukes, a crat will do more or less the same damage as everyone else, so there is no real drawback to bringing one.

So to sum it all up, implant for baton and speeches, not bots. Get a gun. Don't charm mobs. Don't waste time trying to make the pet follow you.

Hmm, I wonder what type of crat Marcell has been teaming with?

There are good crats and bad crats like in any profession. But, I think unlike other profs, there are certain ways a good crat always stands out beyond the color of his bot, armor or weps.

Rules of the Good Crat:

Rule #1
Never tell people to cancel reflects/shields or take off bracers. In fact I usually insist the Soldier or Engie buff everyone else with reflects. Why? See Rule #2

Rule #2
Always run ahead of the team and mez all potential adds. Thus there is never a problem with reflects since you make yourself the target. That includes going up a lift alone. If your team is hitting a mob, put the bot on it and then run around to mez. This means you need to work on AC, health and evades. You're not an MP or Engie who needs to spend all their IP on the best pet possible.

P.S. As soon as you are done mezzing run back to team to refresh their speech aura.

Rule #3
Chain mez mobs when they are beating up the Doc or Agent. Mez is 100% effective in wiping the hatelist.

Basically, you have to be very proactive, lead a team, take tons of damage (but not tank). You get to shape the battlefield for your team. Unlike NT's or Traders, you have a bot that can contribute to killing mob while you run around and mez.

Playing a crat like an MP or Engie is bad.

At 150+ I agree with all the above posts. Everyone is expected to deal damage. But only a crat can add 9% XP, and buff Attack Rating by 100 and Evades by 310 or add 7% crit for less than 5 NCU.

Crats are good in xp settings at all levels really. Up until 130-140ish or so we are wanted for crowd control, after that our xp buff as lvling is such a chore. The only real requirement for a good team at high levels is 6 competent people that manage to not fall asleep in a mission.

In pvp we fall into the can't heal/no defensive nanos/dk blue evades + body dev/no debuffs category. We are annoying with roots and some nukes but pretty easy to kill

In the high level camps when fighting those new uber loot mobs we suffer a bit too. Charms don't work on much anything past level 220 or so due to psychic checks.. and only then on standard non boss/minion mobs. Our other crowd control is reduced alot too. Usually something of calm/root/snare works. However these mobs have such insane nano resist that it's a real pain for us. We also face the problem of the calms/roots/snare being broken so quickly due to the reflect shields needed to stay alive. But the main disadvantage is that these camps seem to be set up around the tank + doc combos with little regards to others.
