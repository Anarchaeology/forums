This happens to me too in BS/Homes, I'm using a

Win98SE
P3-500mhz

800x600x16 res

What happens with me:

AO either FREEZES, or Disconnects either right before, or right after loading up the interface. It will stall right before it starts to draw the world. It's a flow problem, AO servers interpret your computer as unable to process the information it's sending you in a reasonable amount of time.

I have tried the following things to fix the problem, but it had virtually no impact

- Manipulating Direct3d settings
- Manipulating AGP port settings in Bios
- Manipulating Virtual Memory
- Manipulating Resolution
- Manipulating TSR's

I have tried the following with MINOR success

- Loading up an alt character and reducing all visual characteristics (view distance, texture quality, etc.) Then relogging the main character, getting to the grid or a 'safer spot,' raising all that video stuff back to normal, and relogging. This does NOT work all the time.
- Running AO in windowed mode, if that's possible with your configuration. When you are in windowed mode, you don't have to reset your system in cash of a freeze, and can Alt-Ctrl-Del and cancel the task. This allows you to keep trying to login faster, and for some reason i have a better chance of logging in with windowed mode.
