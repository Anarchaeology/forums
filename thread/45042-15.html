Just so's you know, on Testlive, the bracer changes are thus:

Reflect bracers are now UNIQUE, NODROP, *AND* Fit only in a specific wrist.

Melee, Projectile, and Energy Reflect go in the right wrist.

Physics and Nano (and probably Elemental) go in the left wrist.

A QL 200 Bracer gives Max Points Reflected 21, Reflect AC 7%

So you not only can't get more than 7%, you can't have both melee and projectile reflect from bracers.

Oh, and while they don't note it in the patch notes, it appears the shop inventory bugs have been fixed. Armor and Attack shops have proper rotating stock now, and the cluster machines in the trade section have different selections again in the multiple copies of the same machine.
