Professionals Feedback 28-MAR-03
quote:

Enforcers
We definately agree that there could - and should - be more diversity at high levels, both in weapon choices and other aspects of character building.

However, it is a fact that introducing high level items takes more time than low level items. Low level items can normally be thrown in anywhere, while high level ones shouldn't be gained as easily as going to the shop and spend some credits, or using ClickSaver. If we did that, everything new would be explored fully within 3 hours of the patch going live. Special drops, quests, trade skills, etc, is what's used.

This means that more diversity in weapons - and other items - will come over time, often in small bursts as we get a new set of mobs ready, or a new dungeon, or a new set of quests (most likely with new NPCs), or - of course - an expansion pack.


I don't necessarily think this is a complete answer, but it is an answer, and explains some of the reasoning behind the designer's actions. All we can do is hope (and remember that we did get quite a few new weapons recently )
