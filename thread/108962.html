Omni-Mining Looking for New Employees
Greetings Omni-Tek Citizens

Looking for a new and better job in Omni-Tek? A job that is honored and respected among Omni? Are you looking for respectful co-workers that know how to get the job done? A job that makes you feel like you are helping Omni-Tek? Well your search is over.

The department of Omni-Tek, Omni-Mining, is always looking for new employees. Since 28718, Omni-Mining has been fueling Omni-Teks economy to make it stronger. We take pride in our products and our responsibilities. Being a member of Omni-Mining demands respect and honor from your fellow Omni-Tek citizens. For each employee assists in the continued supply of notum which fuels our advanced nanotechonolgy.

What can you do in Omni-Mining? Well there are many tasks one can do. Omni-Mining branches off into departments which perform the various tasks for Omni-Mining. These departments are Shipment and Communication, Engineering and Production, and Human Relations. Also our newly reformed Private Notum Mining department who takes care of the private notum towers, defense and offense.

As you can see there is a place for everyone. Not an engineer/production type? No worries, we are always seeking new employees who wish to protect our mining interests. Or not a soldier type or producer, you can help in shipment, to make sure everything gets everywhere. These are just examples of what one can do in Omni-Mining.

If interested in joining, contact our Head of Human Relations, Ambyrle.

OOC:

We are somewhat newly made RP org that role play the department Omni-Mining of Omni-Tek. We look to have a good time with our members while keeping interest strong with RP events/story. We are looking for respectful members, who are good natured and helpful to not just our members but others as well. We have 4 simple rules that members must follow:

1)Omni-Mining requires that all employees treat each other with the respect and courtesy that they would like to receive from their co-workers. Any counterproductive behavior will not be accepted. Repeated transgressions will result in psychological re-evaluation and/or reconditioning.

2) All members of Omni-Mining are required to attend any scheduled meetings or organizational events. If one is unable to attend said meeting they must contact a board member prior to the event date. Any failure to follow this procedure will result in penalization by the board. Continued attendance failures will result in dismissal.

3) Members of Omni-Mining are not to attack any bases belonging to another organization without permission from the board.

4) Role Playing is highly encouraged for all members, but not mandatory. A certain level of pertinent decorum is requested during planned guild events.

There is no minimum level to join but you must be level 30 or above in order to reach member rank. Of course applicants who are already above 30 wont become a member rank right away. One week of playing is needed before becoming member rank. This time is used to get to know each other and see if you are the right kind of member for us.

You can go to our website, http://omnimining.topcities.com/ , to find some more info about us. But I must admit the site needs some serious updating, which we are currently working on.
We are a RP org, thus some RP experience/participation is required.

Please contact Ambyrle in-game if interested. If you cant reach her at all for some odd reason, contact me.
