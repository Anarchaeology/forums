Shaking ground
Has anyone just been sitting down around the Omni area, be it in Lush Fields, Harry's, Omni Entertainment, or Clon****, and the ground shakes as if there was a smalll tremor and a short rumble is heard??

This happens quite frequently to me so I asked a few people what they thought it was.

Some didnt have a clue what I was talking about, perhaps it was the way I explained it. Others said to me it was lightning striking the ground, now this is correct when the weather is sour, but not on a clear sunny afternoon. Then I heard someone say that it was the cannons fighting the clans. So I investigated.

Now what I found was very impressive and by total accident.

Far west of Clon**** there is some kind of massive MASSIVE outpost. It has lots of troops and Slayers. Now in the centre of this Outpost was these huge structures looking like some kind of cannons. Then when these things fired, the whole place shook and rocked so bad for about 20 seconds!! Now I think we feel these things all over the Omni Area, and being near one I can see how!

This is purely speculation on my behalf, and no-one as of yet has confirmed my su****ions!

Does anyone know what I am talking about?
