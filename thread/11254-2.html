Problems with the idea
I love the idea too. But there are reasons why it can't ever be done...


You say you want to be able to have guilds be allowed to "build" their own guild houses. This can't be done for the siplle fact that any changes made to the gameworld can't happen without a serverside and clientside patch. I doubt Funcom is willing to release a new patch everytime a guild wants a guildhouse/upgrade to previous guildhouse.

However, they CAN take existing buildings and turn them into guildhousing. I believe in fact that there are tons of buildings all around the main cities which were originally intended for this purpose.

I love the idea of guild housing, I wish there was a way to be allowed to build new buildings, but there simply isn't. The next best thing is to be allowed to make guildhousing in buildings that already exist, kind of like the way apartmentws currently work, only without the stupid bugs associated with them (and with added stuff like guild shops, guild bank, etc.)

I think the current technology of the game is going to prevent any very cool guild housing. For one thing, you can't see outside of a building while in it. You can't walk up to the roof, etc. Everything is a mini-zone.

Now, you could have guild house mini-zones that work like apartments do today and maybe have a public area (for selling stuff in booths etc, ) and then a private door that leads to the rest of the guild area.

It would be better than what we have now (nothing) but I don't think it will ever be as cool as UO or AC in regards to being able to see people milling about behind the windows, on the roof, etc. The game engine would have to be redesigned to manage this, and I just don't see it happening.
