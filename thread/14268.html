NTs are not overpowered. They were very powerful and good in PvP at lower levels, and that's why they got nerfed. As professions level up, they're putting on Low Light Scopes and getting crit buffs. Crits drastically increase damage output. Unfortunately, NTs do not benefit from this.

Do you know why NTs got nerfed? For the same reason that Agents got nerfed. They would stand around the Grid in 2H0 and killed everyone that came through there. There were more petitions about NTs and Agents grid camping that all other issues combined.

So Funcom "fixed" it so NTs and Agents weren't as effective in PvP anymore. Unfortunately, this had PvM consequences as well.

How do I beat an NT in PvP? I gank em from behind. Then I get spammed about how he/she wasn't prepared for PvP. What am I suppose to do? Ask politely if an NT wants to root me before I start attacking?

NTs, however, are not underpowered. There are more NTs above lvl 150 then any other profession except soldiers.
