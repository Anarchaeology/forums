There aren't many who would say PvP in AO is perfect. Bugs, 'sploits, and profession imbalances (FunCom's love child and so on) just a cursory list of problems involved. But that's not what I'm here to rant about. No, I'm here with the good news. The PvP gospel if you will.

The following are 3 types of PvP you might see on your way to work on Rubi Ka. I'm sure there are more (namely the arenas, but that's a whole other rant), but I don't care about them.


Grid Camping (grief ganking, title torquing, lagger’s lament):

By any other name is no less retarded. How do you sleep at night? You spend all your time working for a title over your head showing your superior fighting skills, but at the end of the day all you've done was exploit the engine to give you an advantage (the author apologizes for such heinous use of the word exploit). AO needs to be rid of this. Don't get me wrong, I'm all for having areas where one needs to travel with caution due to the risk of PvP. But does that point really need to be the point where you are defenseless, motionless, and essentially helpless? The term "fish in a barrel" fits ever so well.
Diagnosis: Grid camping is a freaking disease. It nullifies the efforts of those who try the PvP thing in an honest fashion. It needs to be disposed of.


Kills in passing:
Simple enough, Faction A sees Faction B shopping at kiosk in MMD. Faction A unleashes an alpha strike, dimach, fast attack, full auto, brawl, and a last word for style points. Not a lot of role-play, but at least no one was cheating. One party is pleased he bested the other in fair combat. The other is pissed that he was caught looking at lingerie in the middle of MMD.
Diagnosis: Nothing wrong with it. It may have been mean spirited. The less fortunate may go home and cry. But overall, no harm done.

Team PvP:
Whats this?! This, is all too uncommon. And as long as it stays somewhat uncommon (that is, no 15 teams at any given time tromping through mayhem zones) it will remain a truly enjoyable time. I know some guilds do it already, but I'd love to see a few larger guilds doing it. Guild leader A (from the aforementioned faction A) contacts Guild leader B (from... yeah you get it) and arrange a little impromptu meeting in the middle of say... artery valley. Each takes the first 5 guild members to volunteer for a super secret mission. The fact of the matter is you can take a team of 5 and have a good time in artery valley without another team to kill. Teams meet, natural selection does it's thing. One walks home the victor. Other gets a much quicker trip home, with a handful of stories to share with the guild. Pick a new location, rinse, repeat. Congratulations you're a certified role-player. Didn't think you had it in you now did ya?
Diagnosis: like you couldn't guess. I'm a HUGE team PvP advocate. With classes like trader just BEGGING to play a team role in PvP I can't think of a good reason not to! Tired of a slow moving story? Make your own!
