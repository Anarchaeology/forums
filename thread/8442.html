Many shout, balance! Others shout, nerf!

I want to enlightn others oh PvP caps, and why healing should not be capped.

First off. We all know Specials do 40% Maximum of targets dmg, as well as normal hits doing a maximum of 50%. They propose we are the 'uber' kings of PvP with our 100% heal compared to their 50% dmg raito. Not likely, for most.

Capping healing would make PvM red impossible. If not near impossible. As you know, any damage delt to another player is caped. Accept healing. Even damage to your self.
