Trying to Stay Positive
To Funcom (players may read if so inclined):

I started playing AO during the 2nd week of the U.S. AO launch. I made a NT, but retired him after the brilliant 12.6 patch. I have played several professions since then, and here is my 2 credits on the current situation.

AO is good, but it has the POTENTIAL to be great. One of the only reasons I hang on AO is because of its uniqueness to other MMORPGs. However, with Star Wars Galaxies in the near future I am growing concerned. I don't want to play another D&D / Hack and Slash so SWG may be the way to go if AO doesn't shape up. I am not threatening at all, just concerned really. I have put A LOT of time into this game, and will stay if the mighty BUGCOM corporation will pull their heads out and see some day light for a bit.

Here is my suggestion: Stop being dumb. Pretty simple huh? I don't give a damn about where the storyline or game was initially headed. The problem is the destination got so messed up that it is not really possible to recover now. STOP trying to get things to where you once saw it, adapt to where it is heading. You created a world (that was terribly bugged when it was first created). Since then your world's inhabitants have adapted and begun to enjoy their environment (finally). LISTEN to your player community and move forward from there. If I see another surge of people leave due to unbelievable incompetence in your development department (it is quite sad really) I will have no choice but to follow.

Bugcom, here is the point, again: Give up on your initial plan, because you messed it up to begin with. Go with the ideas of your player base and adjust to the world we live in (not the world as you think you see it). Stop stepping on the toes of professions to 'fix' something, adjust accordingly.

I don't know a single person that can complete (token and all) a mission of 55% or higher beyond 50th level. This is pathetic and it tells me that you have not done proper testing or planning. Make the profession specific missions; Make the game fun for both solo players and team players.

I can see a Rubi Ka world covered with nothing more than Agents and Enforcers in a few months. Flame away fellow players, but answer this: Can you see a more powerful team than 4 Agents and 2 Enforcers working well together? [2 Agents as MPs with healing pets; 1 Agent as MA casting Sub G and acting as the puller; 1 Agent rooting bad pulls; and the 2 enforcers playing tank.] I see more and more agents everyday, and there were already too many enforcers...

Rubi Ka is getting better as far as stability, but the lack of player (customer) input is getting worse. I may have to go play "Wannabe Luke Skywalker" soon if we don't do something fast. Let me emphasize, so there is no misunderstanding. I want to stay on AO, I will do whatever I can to help PLEASE listen to us so I dont have to go somewhere else.
