Barter Armor - how to make it
Since the board is filled with spoilers about making the CAS Symbiotic armor I'll provide you with the info on making the Barter Armor.

All the armor is done by modifying existing nano armor. This armor is ql75+ and can be found as mission rewards. Cloak and Cloak hood can't be modified.

For each part of the nano armor you do the following:

You need 2 pieces of notum. That can either be a notum chip or a notum fragment (notum nuggets wont work). These are drops from mobs, and unfortunately pretty rare. And to make it even harder these must be within 10 qualitylevels. So to make a ql200 set of barter armor you need 14 pieces of ql190-200 notum. Good luck getting that.

Take Inactive OT Metamorphing Liquid Nanobots (remember this has to be within 10% ql of the notum pieces and combine it with Nano Programming Interface (find in the normal tool shop) and you get: Activated OT Metamorphing Liquid Nanobots.

Take the Activated OT Metamorphing Liquid Nanobots and combine it with a piece of notum and you get Stabilized OT Metamorphing Liquid Nanobots. And combine it with a second piece of notum and you get Super-Stabilized OT Metamorphing Liquid Nanobots.

This is the liquid you need to modify your armor with. The nano armor as to be within 10% ql of the liquid. Combine nano armor piece with liquid and you get a piece of Barter armor.

Same way you make CAS Symbiotic armor, just use Organic armor instead of nano armor.

There you have it - good luck with camping the items. In my opinion this is totally overkill with 2 pieces of notum for each piece. Armor isn't good enough to make up for how hard it is to get the resources.
