Clan and Omni
Be rest assured, i for one do not wish war between us and not just for the reason i mentioned earlier.

One saying always springs to mind.... 'Better the Devil you know'..... (apparently it was also a song, many thousands of years ago) and i think this applies for Clan, Omni and Neutral.

Clan wishes freedom and independence. OT wishes for higher profits and order amongst the chaos. Neutral... well i dont know.... peace obviously i assume.

I have no personal gripe towards OT.... i make a fine and steady 'business' from them. Some of them are shall we say..... 'friends', particularly during the Amnesty. I find it truly disconcerting however when, on my travels, i observe OT AND Clan hurling petty abuse at each other. This does nothing but arouse further anger and hostility where it is not needed. If some wish to express that anger, let them meet in an Arena.

I had to laugh yesterday, but i want to share this amusing moment. I was in VW heartlands with my Warbot, armed to the teeth with bot in Guard mode. Suddenly a very young OT soldier, solitus i believe, appears from nowhere...... he approaches tentatively.... i scanned him and he was lvl 27. His first words????

"Hi, i'm Omni-Tek, but don't worry i won't hurt you." I could not help but silently giggle underneath my helmet. We chatted a moment and then i asked, "What are you doing so far out here"?
His answer? "I'm lost, can you please help me back to a Neutral City"?

Needless to say we teamed and i escorted him on foot back to NLC grid point. I watched him Grid and a fellow Clansman made a comment about teaming with an OT member. I ginored him and carried on about my business, thinking about how pleased my older brother would be. My brother being an OT Engineer!

So, he learned a small lesson.... not all Clanners are bad, just as i know not all OT are bad either.

We co-exist, we are to some point, in reality, also co-dependent.
