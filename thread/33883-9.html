More Ideas
I played AO back in beta, and for a short while after, and just recently returned. I have been playing Asheron's Call for a while, and EQ before that, which I also played back before AO beta. Anyway I got some ideas which I think would be nice to add.

1) The ability to Inscribe an item. Such as a wedding ring, or a leet doll. And while at it, let others be able to select items weilded, such as your gun, floaty things over shoulders, etc, so they could actually look at the stats or read inscriptions.

2) Personal computer pads (books) which you can write in and keep notes. Connect it to your map so you can mark customized locations. This should be possible if 1 is possible above.

3) Public Map Terminals where you can pay a small fee, like a mission terminal, to find local shops, whompas and exits from the city. Finding the right shop as a newbie is really hard.

4) More items in shops. And distinctive shops. There are numerous NPC venders who sell the same stuff as a machine shop, but with less selection. Make specialty shops (like Amandas Boutique in Omni-Ent) actually sell specialty items. I went all the way out there last night, and they sell the exact same thing as several other shops. Only Miir sells different clothing it seems. Make some competing fashion lines. And some clothes that even newbies can afford.

5) Furniture and Apartments. Said many times above, and I agree. Make an entire furniture shop with multiple machines selling different things, such as art, furniture, plants, lamps, etc. And make some affordable. Like a cheap, white trash looking sofa for newbies, with better quality for more money.

Instead of having multiple keys for apartments, have a right click-hold option on your passkey to disable it, so you automatically go into other peoples apartments with them, even if it's the same door as your own apartment.

And definately increase the number of items in a apartment. In Asheron's Call (AC) you can have 3 hooks in a apartment plus a storage chest. A cottage has 50 hooks with 25 usable, plus a storage chest. A villa has 75 hooks with 50 usable, plus a dungeon thru a portal, and 2 storage chests. A mansion has like 100 hooks with 75 usable, and 5 storage chests in it's own dungeon, and anyone in the guild can access a mansion.

6) Also better documentation for housing. I looked and looked to find stuff, but it was very hard to find. Better documentation on travel would be nice as well, and how to use the F9 key to find your playarea and location, so you can give someone else directions to find you easier.

7) More dance emotes. Every kind you can think up. And static emotes. A emote which you use, and then it holds that emote or position until you cancel with the stand/sit key, or another static emote. Let non-static emotes be usable in a static emote, so you can interupt it at times, then return to that emote when finished. AC has these also. And a laying down or playdead emote. I liked the option someone said, of sitting, then using the back move button to lay down, with side moves to roll over.

8) A blank machine in shops where you can sell stuff, which is then displayed in the inventory so others can buy from it. Someone elses junk might be your treasure. All you have to do is be able to look for it, and the current shops don't seem to display anything sold.

9) Sorting in shops would be nice also. Sort by QL, Alpha, Name, Cost, Type, etc. And with machines, have them scan the users QL to show them stuff they can actually use. No need then to have Basic, Superior, and Advanced Shops. Everyone would use the same ones. Have it display items within 10 levels +/- of your character, with a option to expand the range listing, or call up specific QL items, for when you are buying ahead, or perhaps just finally got enough money to fill in nanos you don't have yet which are below your current level.

10) Multi-passenger vehicles. If you buy a dune buggy or Hummer type vehicle, you want to be able to carry friends. And it could open up a new portion of the economy. Someone could be a bus driver, taking people on trips to other cities, or playfields for small fees. Perhaps an Agent could hire someone with a fast stealthy vehicle to take them into enemy territory to drop them off. Or use inventory space to carry either passengers, or cargo, for smuggling operations, or just legit trade. And make cheap transportation. Like hoverboards for the lower levels, bikes, motorcycles, jetskies, jetpacks, rollerblades, etc. Things which can boost movement a bit, though not as much as a Yalm. In a game where cybertechnology is so cheap that anyone can get it, it doesn't make sense that transportation is so expensive.

11) And definately more items in shops. Variety is the ****e of life.

12) The ability to go to the tops of towers and buildings. People could have rave parties on a skyscraper, or in a Sky Club.

13) Put Out of Order signs on non-working doors, such as the Apartments buildings by the front gates of Omni-Ent.

14) Make 1st person mode camera moveable. When in 1st Person Mode, I can't look up or down using the num pad. Makes it hard to look at signs.

15) More info about random NPC's in cities. Make them talk and stuff. Like a woman in the redlight district might say a come-on line to you, or a shady guy might whisper about drugs. Right now, I see a few just wander around, but they don't do anything as far as I know.

16) Make the line of 9 buttons in the upper right corner blink when something is added. Such as a mission update in a team makes the mission panel blink orange. A team request does the same to the team panel, etc. It's small, out of the way, and can be ignored, but would help newbies alot.

17) More Nano-Cluster machines. I went into the trades room in a shop, and there are 3 levels with machines. But most of the machines are duplicates. Make them all different. Have a different machine for each type of nanocluster. Head, Wrist, Hand, Feet, Legs, etc. That way you don't accidently spend 1500 creds on a bright nanocluster and then it won't work, and you can only sell it back for 53 creds. (Happened to me last night.) And that seems Very EQish to me. When buying something for that much money, you shouldn't get totally ripped off by selling it back within just a few minutes. In real life, you would get a full refund, perhaps minus a restocking fee. Prices in this game seem very disorganized.

18) More profession usable items on solo missions. I have requested solo missions, where all 5 items were unusable or just not worth using to my Soldier. Stuff like Nano-Clusters for other professions, and vague item names in the mission text. In Identify Item Missions, since you get the item you ID, as well as a reward, the ID item should have a reference so you can check what it is as well as the prize items.

I think that's enough ideas for right now. I'll come back when I think of some more.
