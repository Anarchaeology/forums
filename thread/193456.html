Why is a Neutral neutral?
I've attended a couple of Neutral leader meetings in my time, and it appears to be difficult for us (neutrals) to reach a common ground because of the fact that we're neutrals for different reasons. Inevitably, the conversations tend to end up in discussions that are phrased something like "I'm a neutral because... and so this is what I think we should do about this." Due to the fact that it can be difficult for some to adequately explain why they're neutral at these meetings, and because it's not always beneficial to do so at that time, I'm taking the time now to bring this into open discussion. Forgive me if this has been done before, as I'm new to these types of discussions.

This discussion is intended FOR anyone who has asked the question "Why be neutral?", but is intended to be discussed BY those who are neutral. The explanation that some Clan and Omni express (being fence-sitters) has been expressed often, and this is not the place to express that again.

So... I will begin:
Ten months ago, I began my journey in the now bustling city of Borealis. I decided that my path would lead me the way of the martial artist, a skill that I love and continue to work on to this day.

I was given the choice to join the Omni-Tek corporation, the opposing Clans, or to remain neutral. I carefully read the histories of both Omni-Tek and the Clans (keeping in mind that history is often written by the biased victors of any conflict, and can therefore be intentionally or unintentionally written as a bit of propaganda). I read of the histories of the Omni-Tek Corp., the great achievements they made in technologies since their arrival on Rubi-ka, and the later poor treatment of employees and rumored harsh experiments they conducted. I didn't agree with the more recent Omni-Tek history related to that, and so I considered the Clans. The Clan originally started as a band of highly disgruntled employees of Omni-Tek, who stood up to the OT Corporation. That soon became more of a threat to OT as the Clan grew into the Clans, and several groups posed a ... let's say... "military" threat to the OT Corp by destroying supplies and eventually killing OT employees. I didn't (still don't) agree with these types of attacks, despite the harsh treatment OT had given their employees which created the Clan in the first place.

So I remained neutral. I am not neutral because I can't make a decision between OT and Clan. I'm neutral because I don't agree with either side, and therefore live my life as I see fit. To paraphrase a comment Auntbeast once said (which I agree with whole-heartedly)... neutrals aren't "sitting on the fence"... WE ARE THE FENCE!

We are the dividing line between the OT Corp and the Clans. However, in my ten months here as a member of society on Rubi-ka, I've been killed numerous times by both Omni and Clan... sometimes for no better reason than the fact that I was there and I didn't wear the same OT or Clan symbol that they do. If not for OT's reclaim technology, my career as a martial artist would have been cut short before it even began.

Due to the fact that I (and my neutral friends) have been killed by both Omni and Clan, as well as had our lands taken from us by both, I have a sizeable dislike for both OT and Clan people. So as not to interfere with the current talks of improving relations, with regard to Newland and the NGI, I will currently keep my opinions to myself in that regard until I see where that path takes us.

Let me say this though... neutrals have long been the underdog in this battle between OT and Clans, taking flak from both sides. Looking back at history, the Clan were once the underdogs of an OT society... and look at them now... they now rival the OT presence on Rubi-ka and fight them (with guns or diplomacy) on a daily basis. The neutral people will not continue to be walked on by both sides indefinitely. One who understands the way of most martial artists knows that a martial artist will train to fight so they don't have to fight... to know how to get out of a bad situation without conflict... however, if that fails they have the skills necessary to deal with the situation.

With that thought, I open this discussion to my fellow neutrals to express why they began neutral and why they remain neutral. Perhaps this open discussion will help to unify neutral society, as well as enlighten those OT and Clan minds that believe us to be "fence-sitters".

Not to trrivialise this subject matter, but from a roleplaying perspective the most likely reason anyone is neutral is because the mother and father were neutral, they grew up in a neutral town, went to neutral schools and maybe even married a neutral in later life.

In real life some people do change or acquire a new nationality either through marriage or by migrating to a new country for economic or political reasons. But that is just a minority. By and large most of us stick to the nationality we were born with. And even when our leaders send us to wars we do not agree with, we still normally stay loyal to our country, still support our athletes in the Olympics and still prefer our own beers over all others.

To a roleplayer, the implicit assumption that neutrality is just a stepping stone to either Clan or Omni-Tek is very, very artificial. You do not need a reason to stay neutral, nor do you need to dislike or disapprove of the Omni and Clan factions. Most people just tend to stay true to their roots, its just the normal course of things.

Of course if you start talking game mechanics, that is a whole different ball game.

The analogy to where you were born, or your nationality, seems a bit off the mark to me. I see neutrality more as a philosophy, and specifically more as a political position. If it were possible to take my clan characters and make them neutral now, I probably would do so.

Shadowlands gave me a reason to play neutral from the start of my char... and I will have to admit that it is a unique side and one that is a bit difficult to understand easily. Perhaps many people choose clan or omni because it's easier, as the benefits of neutrality (other than travel and teaming options) are minimal. This results in each person choosing neutral for personal reasons.

It is enlightening, to say the least, to be neutral and watch both clan and omni behave in similar ways, particulary with regards to towers. Neutral, to them, means pawnship (or pwnership :P)... and it's exciting to find out that neutrals don't want to be pawns or be pwned. Neutrals want to be neutrals.

Neutrals seem more complex, more independent, than either omni or clan. And more interesting for in-game reasons: the most able to define themselves and their own history.
