Wedding on Azure Lake
Weddings on Rubi-ka
Investigative Reporter Legman

It was a clear, sunny, calm afternoon on the Shore of Azure Lake. Rockjock and Justhealin stood knee deep in the waters and recited their wedding vows as Nimion of the First Circle of Mystery hovered above the warm waters. Friends and guild mates lining the shore gazed on as the joyful couple dedicated their undying love and pledged to a lifetime of commitment.

Let us return now the beginning of this fairytale. Justhealin was in her usual location providing aid to the less fortunate adventure seekers at the reclaim terminal in Borealis. Rockjock, a fledgling enforcer, had just appeared at the terminal after being overcome by wildlife in the wilderness. The two, sensing a kindred spirit in one another, were nigh inseparable after their first meeting. From taking their leisure relaxing in Boralis, or bashing through missions, they were rarely parted.

Ten months have passed since Justhealin and Rockjock first met. As they stood in the waters of Azure lake and recited their vows they reflected on all the moments they have spent together- good and bad, happy and sad. It was then that they realized that they have the rest of their lives, bound in love and respect, to bring each other joy and happiness.

Weddings are a blessing to Rubi-ka, provided by the Rubi-ka Marriage Registries and authorized by ICC. These momentous occasions require a great deal of planning and preparation. This is where the RKMR comes in. They assist with planning of the event down to the last detail as well as performing the ceremony. It is through the dedication of this group of people that weddings transform from mundane to magic, memorable to unforgettable, average to magnificent.
