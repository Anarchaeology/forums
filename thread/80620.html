OT Elite Recruiting
If you fancy joining Ot Elite, then you must go through a simple Interview. However you must meet all the requirements.

1) Do you live in an area that is the same time zone as Uk's (GMT)

2) Do you intend to buy Notum Wars - we would prefer you to have Notum Wars to contribute to the Towers, however it is not necessary.

3) You must be nice We don't want any 'silly' people in the org. You must not be nasty to n00bs as we do not tolerate such behaviour.

If you can comply with the rules above, or most, then please give either one of these people a /tell in game. thanks for your time :

i) Tretz
ii) Witeboog

Thanks again, have a nice day - kill some clanners!
