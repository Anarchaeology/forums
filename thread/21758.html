What really happened with the multi ranged requirements on pistols?
As many of us know the pistols multi range requirement were increased quite drasticly sometime during August last year. The change took place the same time as the good old Freedom Arms 3927 were taken out of the game.

As I recall back then a lot of professions including soldiers were running around with a pair of Freedom Arms. I can agree that something had to be done, it wouldn't have been very funny to have every toon on RK going dual pistols. What I'm after though is if someone has seen a good explanation of why all pistols was 'nerfed'? We all know that pistols aren't very competitive at higher levels today, sure there are some fairly ok, but the majority are collecting dust in the shops.

This is my interpretation of what happended:

- Everyone is using dual pistols!!! (=Freedom Arms back then)
- Action!(without any analysis) -> Remove Freedom Arms + Increase multi ranged requirement on all pistols insanely
- Forgot that some Adv are 'relying' on dual pistols -> No action (Funcom unaware)

Well, time goes by and everyone starts to adopt to that pistols are lousy and everyone think it is 'ok' to have multi ranged as a limiting factor on the 'offhand' pistol. Well, I don't think it is ok. I want to wield two equal level pistols, just like most everyone going 2he/2hb can do.

I don't know why I wrote all this... I think I'm just amazed about how a relatively small change can affect so many players and a whole weapon line. I'm amazed at the time it takes to fix this. I'm amazed at how someone thought that pistols were so overpowered that they had to give them insane multi range requirements. I'm amazed at how unknowing Funcom seem to be about this change they did back then and what the results are. Do they hate pistols that bad that they don't want to see them?

What does the future hold? How many more 'small' changes will screw things up and how many months will it take to correct them?

Check out the multi ranged requirement on the good old Freedom Arms 3927, it is 542 on the ql200 version. I don't find this wrong, I find it completly adequate. What I do find wrong is that a single pistol, Freedom Arms 3927, 'brought upon us' a change that broke the whole concept of dual wield pistols. For what reason??? When I look on the stats today on the high ql Freedom arms, they don't look uber. Sure, high crit but nothing compared to shotguns. Tune them down _slightly_ (if you really think you must) and reintroduce them, the 4200 is tuned down far too much and has insane multi range requirements. Give us a reason to dump IP into our dark blue ranged energy skill and reward us with a nice set of pistols. If we ever get a huge multi ranged buff, don't see it as something we were given, see it as something that was taken away from us and eventually was returned.

-> Bring back the real Freedom Arms.
-> Make me able to wield two pistols of equal QL.
in other words: -> Give me, what was taken away from me.
