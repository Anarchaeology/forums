Pro Re Nata
Ripped the shirt from my back
Bang her head on the closet door
Blood on her forehead
Place her hand to my face
Her hand covered in feces.

I strike back, or understand?
Do we kill that which offends?
Do we hope to heal instead?
Is a shirt so important?
Take this pill to calm down.

The battle with herself,
does one side need to win?

My lady, go back to bed, it is time to sleep.
Is there wisdom in sanity?
On the steps of madness,
Are you climbing or falling down?

With all my ability to heal the body,
Yet still the mind can be injured beyond repair.
She takes my torn white shirt, and waves it in the air.
A symbol of my own surrender.
