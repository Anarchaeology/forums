--==((The Cyberite Spirituality))==--
"The Grid-Born"

Crusaders of Data are we, as swift as rumors
Through cable flow we; as the data highway
Like Water in Nature, Be life maintained.
The Truth; our soul be, for we are the Children of the Grid!

No Lies can Hide, On either Side.
Enlightment shall come
When lies succumb
The Truth; our weapon be, for we are the Children of the Grid!

The Grid: our feathered wings; identifying and valuable
The Trance: our fortress; mesmerizing yet steadfast
The Tech: our friend; complex yet easy
The Truth; our frame-work be, for we are the Children of the Grid!

Armed the weapon of fanatic Will
Skilled are we indeed to Kill.
Maintain Justice and Truth we must,
The Truth; our oath be, for we are the Children of the Grid!

Techno we favor, the Beatz of Trance
Synchronized with the Grid our souls would be.
Groove and joy offers it thee
The Truth: our desire be, for we are the Children of the Grid!

Oh Nerf, Oh Woe, No longer are we
For Funcoms amend, we have enjoyed.
Unite we shallt, under comprehension.
The Truth; our blessing be, for we are the Children of the Grid!

Stop the fued, Stop the violence!
Many blood has spillt for petty Titles!
Why must Omni suffer near by Stret?
Why are the Clanners in such a Fret?

Unite we shallt, under comprehension
The Truth; our teachings be, for we are the Children of the Grid!

- Kaydra. Fixer RK 1, a Grid-Born.
