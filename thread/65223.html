Science Conference on Rubi-Ka -- poll for interest
I'm thinking Rubi Ka's first (to my knowledge) interdisciplinary science conference, to happen perhaps in January?

Are you interested to attend?

Would you like to make a presentation? I will send out an official call for papers if first impression indicates that there is interest.

I can more or less promise two presentations by myself. One theoretical/philosophical, about science in general on Rubi Ka, and, since I seem to be delving into the mission system now, perhaps something about that.

All disciplines are welcome.

((I figure this to be about 50% RP and 50% real game info, technical or otherwise. That is, it's ok to hold a non-technical presentation. Anthropology anyone? Or to make stuff up completely, if you get any good ideas.))

EDIT:
Maybe start on RK1 and then do it on RK2 as well if we get good feedback. Most people have or can make an alt on the other server, and scripts can be used with any char.

As for factions, I have signalled from day one that science knows no borders and that scientific progress can't be stumped by political conflicts. I.e., all factions are welcome, and the general attitude should be of cooperation between scientists, and not competition between factions. There is competition in science too, but on different premises than in politics.

Thanks for responding so quickly.

About me:
I am an inhabitant of Tir on Rubi-Ka 2 since 6 months back. I'm a long-time student of science theory, or the philosophy of science, and I follow the progression of scientific knowlegde, as well as of the scientific community, here on Rubi-Ka with great interest. I'm mainly a theoretician, but have taken up the study of Rubi-Ka's mission system, as theoreticians need practical experience too.

My very first notes can be seen here:
http://utterly.org/mission_types.xls

The science conference initiative involves Tenfour, of Athen RK1, to an equal extent, and coincidentally, he shares my background completely. Which is probably why we work so well together.

This is the first time I post on this board, I have been more active on the AO Vault board:
http://vnboards.ign.com/board.asp?brd=5216

If this poll provokes some positive responses, I will send out a call for papers. I will make sure that we have several months for preparations. Special invitations will be sent to individuals who have already distinguished themselves in the spiring science community of Rubi-Ka.

After that we will send out invitations on a larger scale, with a tentative program.

Even though I have noticed with some interest that neutrals on Rubi-Ka often seem to comprise yet another faction, rather than being a neutral party in the conflict, I'm leaning towards having the conference on neutral ground. Measures will be taken to ensure the physical, if not emotional, safety of the participants and guests. Verbal assault, alas, is part of academia, regardless of faction or planet.

Please, if there is already an organized scientific movement on Rubi-Ka, I would be greatful to hear of it. Don't want to step on any toes. Also, I will be collecting names of distinguished scientists as the preparations proceed. There is already a list but additions will most probably be necessary. Please send suggestions directly to me: kronocide@utterly.org.

Thank you!
