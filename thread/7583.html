AO is the BEST! Here is a few ideas to make it even better
I just want to first say, " FUNCOM" what a amazing job you have done turning this game around. I played this game when it first came out, and quit. However I regret that I did not give you a chance now since I could have been a level 70 by now

In anycase I have a few idea's that would even enhance "my" gaming experience and hopefully other agree.

1) I think that guild names should be displayed over the heads of guildy's. I always liked this feature in other games because it not only advertises the guild but also helps me to know who is in my guild when we travel in big groups. If people didnt want to see that maybe you could have a turn off feature.

2) When inviting members and or promoting/demoting their is no text to let you know if it was succesful. For example if I do a /org invite "billybob" I should see text that says "You invited "Billybob" to your guild.

3) I also think that maybe adding the feature to allow you at level x to change your first and lastname to whatever you like would be a nice feature and a good goal to work towards in the game.

Just a few idea's. Thanks for taking the time to read my post.
