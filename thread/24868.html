Shouldn't be able to root a vehicle
During my roughly 6 months in AO, I've heard numerous times about players trying to get through a PvP area in a Yalm or Kodiak, and getting rooted. Hasn't ever happened to me, but many friends have gotten stung this way. One Clan friend was actually at high altitude in a Yalm and got rooted by an OT who flew above him, de-planed and rooted him while she was falling, then flew back up and rooted herself in mid-air to kill him. (As much as I deplore this action, I have to admire her creative manipulation of the game mechanics.)

I think if you're crossing a PvP area, there should be a risk that you will be attacked and killed. That's just part of the game.

However, I think it's nonsensical from both a game mechanics and a simulation realism point of view that you should be able to root or snare someone when they're in a vehicle. If you cross a PvP area in a vehicle, you should still be subject to attack, but you shouldn't be prevented from running away.

From a game mechanics standpoint, when you're in a vehicle you can't cast nanos or attack--you are unable to fight. The tradeoff for not being able to fight is movement. If you're in a vehicle, the ONLY response you have to attack is attempting to run. Because it takes so long to get out of a vehicle, if you get rooted it's very unlikely you'll be able to get out before you're dead and there's absolutely nothing you can do about it. The purpose of the vehicle is nerfed by a mechanics loophole.

The PvP mechanics should not allow slaughter of players who completely unable to act. IMHO, rooting a vehicle is more unbalanced and damaging than many of the other "exploits" that have been patched. This should be fixed too.

And also from a game realism standpoint, I can appreciate a snare or root working on an organic body, but it seems really strange to me that it would not at least have a reduced effect if you try to apply it against a more massive mechanical object like a car or airplane.
