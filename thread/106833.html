Omni-Pol wants you!
If you are an Omni-Tek employee of level 50 and above, if you have been looking for an established roleplaying guild, if you look forward to represent Omni-Pol as lawabiding and lawenforcing officer - then it is time to apply for membership with us.

Enemies to our organization have constantly been trying to undermine us, hurt us and humiliate us - to no avail. Omni-Pol always stands strong and will continue to do so in the future. As one of the oldest remaining guilds from the days before Rubi-Ka was opened up for colonists, Omni-Pol stands stronger than ever with a membership count hovering around 100 and levels from 50 to 200.

Would you like to be in the field, doing patrols and shooting the enemy? Then the Assault Corps is your place.

Would you like to be a healing hand, a helper with technical things, a trader to increase your riches? Then the Medical Research, Trading & Engineering department is your place.

Do you enjoy talking to others, training others and do you have an ear for everyone and everything? Then the Publicity, Recruitment & Training department is your place.

In addition to these 3 main departments, Omni-Pol can offer a 4th department, which is called Black Ops and which is our intelligence department. You can be part of this elite part of Omni-Pol aswell in the future, gathering intelligence about the enemy, infiltrating opposing organizations, undermining criminal networks and many other things.

As an Omni-Pol-affiliated organization, this guild often helps out in roleplaying events, such as hunts for cyborgs, gathering of intel for other Omni-Tek departments, escort services for important Omni-Tek officials and many other things. Just recently we took part in 2 roleplaying events, helping "2nd Lt. Jacia Nimrod", a rather well known "greeny" as we call the green named roleplaying ARKs.

If you want to become involved with Omni-Pol, you must remember the following:

- we do not accept employees who had a past as clan member
- we do not accept employees who were once member of guilds who are our enemies (Devil's Advocate, Name Withheld, Kaviera Family Mafia - to name only a few)
- you must be fluent in English writing (and you must understand this language) so you can communicate with others on a decent level
- you must be level 50 or higher to be of any use for the police
- you must be at least partly a roleplayer - Omni-Pol is first and foremost a roleplaying guild, so non-roleplayers will most likely not enjoy their time with us

If you fulfil all the prerequisites above, then feel free to come to our website (http://www.omni-pol.org) and apply for membership with us: the premier law enforcement department on Rubi-Ka today. Every loyal Omni-Tek employee should have served in the police to make our cities safer and spread Omni-Tek's ideology.

To get to know us, apply for membership as stated in the application form which you will find on our website and meet us ingame. We are numerous and always around, so it should not be hard to find an Omni-Pol officer on duty.

Omni-Pol Line up

Omni-Pol protects!
