Deep Winter Faction Seeking New Operatives
Omni-InterOps faction, codenamed Deep Winter (DWF), is now actively seeking Omni Operatives.

DWF, an agent oriented organization, has openings for all professions above 50th Level.

DWF is an OmniTek InterOps Intelligence agency that monitors the Rubi-Ka situation and disseminates the information to the appropriate parties.

The objective of DWF is to serve as an independent source of analysis on topics of concern and ensure that the intelligence consumer receives the best intelligence and support possible.

Please submit your application to Redvulture on Atlantean/RK1 (via /tell).

(DWF Supports both both serious and casual players with raids and RP Events.)

History:
In 29437, three years after taking office, Omni-Tek, Rubi-Ka CEO Phillip Ross issued the Rubi-Ka Expanded Security Initiative (RESI). RESI charged the Chief of Omni-Interops ('Janus') with coordinating the planet's intelligence activities and correlating, evaluating and disseminating intelligence which affects Omni-Tek security on Rubi-Ka. Janus, in turn, established several sub-agencies (factions) designed to effeicently collect information and quell terrorist activites.

The Deep Winter Faction (DWF) is one such faction.

For addition information, visit the Deep Winter Faction communications site.
