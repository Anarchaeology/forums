The first class based self-only, lvl limited nanos were good, the fixer ones. The requirements were low enough that we could easily fit into them not long after we got to there level limits. For example my fixer is lvl80 and he can use Major Supressor and his nano-skills aren't even close to maxed.

I can only assume, as my main is a Fixer, that the requirements were actually brought down from what you originally had them at to compensate for the fixers dark blue nano skills, the most costly skill to raise in the game. Well you need to do remember that soldiers and enforcers nanoskills aren't the best either, especially with those poor atrox people.

The new soldier nanos (and enforcer nanos) NEED to have there requirements dropped by a full 20%. If that's to much, at least calculate how much a Solitus lvl200 Soldier/Enforcer has in nanoskills, when maxed, with no implants. And at the very least, reduce the requirements to that number.
