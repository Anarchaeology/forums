That is a sign of good luck, you have managed to impossible -- Getting a dev to respond to your post!


I would say an enforcer, 2 docs, MP, and a crat.

You would have to moniter the enforcer and 2 docs pretty closely... using your agreesion enhancer on the enforcer and constantly healing with the 2 docs.

If you play it right 2 docs will keep just about anything alive.

The MP is thrown in only for their nano skill buffs, which will allw your docs to cast very high level heal nanos.
You won't need to give much attaintion to the crat or MP. Just click a button to make the MP pet attack, and set your healpet to automatically heal the enforcer.

The 'crat is thrown in for their experience buffs (+9% exp), AND for their crowd control ability.
Use the crat when you pull more than one mob. It will happen quite often so its important that you be able to calm them while you focus on one at a time.

For your enforcer you will need to use mongo slam a lot, and get yourself a top quality agression enhancer with "Essense of Pure jealousy" put in it.
