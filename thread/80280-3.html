A note on "hate".

In Anarchy Online, most preconcieved notions of groups of people come from past experiences we have had personally, seen or heard of in the forums, or have related to by friends. These accounts may or may not be completely accurate, yet every occourance has either a negative or positive influence on how we speak of, interact with, or deal with the said group (or individuals within it) in the future. For Example -

When I was a very little baby crat, around level 45 or so, I was in the Steps of Madness trying with my group to kill neleb for his coat. We were all still learning about the game, and doing well however while we were there, a lvl 160ish Storm Martial Artist came with his friend. They camped him, killed him, then deleted the loot, just to waste time and harass omni.

Looking back on that, although it may seem insignificant now at the time it was stressful and upsetting. It made me feel helpless, and frustrated with the not only the individual but everything related to
them. I unconsciously catergorized him as a representative of his organization - created a bais "filter" through which all future information about the storm guild would come through and be tainted by.

I realized a while ago that there are good and bad individuals, as posted here earlier, in all groups of people and when we judge people and groups based on our prejudices and stereotypes and treat them differently, that it is infact discrimination.

A stereotype is a generalization about a person or group of persons. Our society often subconciously creates and utilizes stereotypes when they are unable or unwilling to obtain all of the information they would need to make fair judgments about people or situations. In the absence of the "total picture," stereotypes in many cases allow us to "fill in the blanks." , but these stereotypes often lead to unfair discrimination and persecution when the stereotype is unfavorable.

Regardless of faction, personally I feel that people who judge and flame without first hand knowledge of a stituation should, recognize that they are not acting fairly if they treat people differently because of preconcevied notions and prejudices.

So I'm sorry, Tolbat, that you've been injustly singled out for harrassment because of your guild tag. I feel however that this sort of behavior will continue until the time that people as a society take the time to make their judgements based on facts, instead of perpetuated stereotypes.

/idealism off

This by no means condones or justifies cheating/exploiting.
