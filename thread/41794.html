Marriage and Roleplaying
Ok this should soud strange from me of all people, but everyone gets a wild hair up theri ass sometimes...

I've been around the world and and seen things many peopel have'nt. One thing however ive noticed, is a trend of people getting married lately. Same sex opposite sex, it does not matter. Now I have nothing agasnt this in general, but I notice, after a great deal of hoopla of the event (the actual ceremony) which is usually set up weeks in advance, with ones guildmates and freinds in admittance. Again nothing wrong with this. However, I noticed one key common thing with this.

A week alter the Happily married couple are no longer Happily together. Most of the time they dont last even a week.

So my question is (puts on his flamesuit) Are you all doing this for attention?. I can personally count at least 6 weddings ive been to that the couple broke up withing 1-2 weeks. this is after over a month of being "engaged". If this is a trend, why bother? Unless you want to make it into a event that is a excuse to invite all yer freinds. I'm sure ther are many "happiliy" married couples out there who have been together for a long time (so please spare me a long line of posts defending yourselves), but what I am talking about are peopl who do this, and shortyl break up. Im not a moralistic preacher, standing on my Pulpit telling people what to do, but in most places, marriage is taken a bit seriously, not as a excuse for a Event to get attention.

No I really dont have any Idea why I did this post. Honestly I dont know. I guess the trend of these events I ahve to go to repeatedly gets annoying sometimes, mainly becasue the couple is usually broken up a short time later.

Bah I dont know. Flame me for some reason...perhpas thats why I wrote this Post.
