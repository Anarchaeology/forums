Well, if the articles and stuff are true, there's a limit to how many towers any one player/guild can place. The QL of the towers is limited by the PF. Presumably, the bigger guilds will go for the higher-level PFs, to get maximum benefit out of their towers. There's also a lot of real estate on Rubi-Ka.

My guesses:-

1) Lower-level playfields, lower-level players. It'll be interesting to see how this pans out. Given the more realistic PvP level-limiting at lower levels, this should be chaotic, but it could well be a fun free-for-all. Maybe.

2) Highest-level playfields. I see the bigger, higher-level guilds monopolizing the highest-level playfields. More organized war, in fact, they're going to need to be extremely organized to grab and hold their bit of turf.

3) Mid- to high-level playfields. While the really big guilds might monopolize and fight over the highest-level playfields, I'm unsure as to how much of the high-level stuff they'll be able to control without spreading themselves too thin. For more mid-sized guilds, with a range of players in them, there should be some good opportunities to fight over the mid-level playfields. Probably a few high-level ones too.

It should be interesting to see how this is all implemented.
