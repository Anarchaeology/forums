Operation: No Grid For You!
Hi, i'm not an adventurer, i'm a fixer. But we share a common problem. Level reqs on new nanos. its just plain stupid!

I'm very angry about this. And i have a plan to protest this. We need your help!

Ok, i was going to call it Operation: GRID NAZI (or no grid for you!)

but i thought some people might get mad about the nazi part.

ok so this is the official NGFY thread

The Problem:
The level requirements on the new adv and fixer nanos are stupid and rediculous.

1- Wasnt OE enough? Why do somthing like this?

2- This is a game based on how much skill you have, not what level you are.

3- Character customization, a skill based system makes it so that every character is different to a degree. example: a fixer your level might be able to cast higher nanos than you because he spent more IP in nano skills. Of course, you probobly have a
nicer gun and better B&E skill. You might have invested more into trade skills rather than nanos. and on and on.

4- with level requirements, the skill system is useless. They might as well make everything based totally on class and level. so that every fixer that is level 45 is using the same armor, weapons and nanos. How fun would that be! woohoo! /sarcasm off

5- I'm very happy about the fixer love, until i found out about this. I'm still pretty happy with the fixer love even if this goes through, and i will continue to play. With this patch they are nerfing us at the same time as fixing us. Alot of our love seems to be bittersweet like this.

The Solution:
The solution is obvious, we have informed Nettie and Argulace about the problem, they will talk to FC, and hopefull get this issue taken care of in one way or another.

This is what I HOPE happens.

HOWEVER Based on FC's past performance, this is unlikely.

Some people will threaten to quit if this goes though. I am not one of these people. I will stay reguardless. I need another way to apply pressure to FC so that they notice how mad we are. Unfortunatly the only way i can think if is by staging a planet-wide protest - and yes - disrupting other peoples gameplay. its ugly and nasty and mean. but its all we can do.

The Plan:
Ok with all of that out of the way, here is the plan as i see it.

This is the plan i will inact if and when the patch goes live with the level requirements.

1- All interrested fixers, adventurers and other sympathizers will meet at a location that has low lag, i have chosen the grid terminal in galaway county. We will start to meet there as soon as the patch goes live.

2- The operation will go forward from there, all clanners and omnis are welcome to come. The omni players will focus mainly on omni grid terminals, where the clanners will focus on clan territories.

3- I will dispatch everyone into teams of 1 fixer and 1 adventurer. if we get alot of people, we'll include someone of another class with them.

4- Each team will be assigned a grid terminal to guard on the planet. The adventurer and fixer in the group will poly into dragons and sit on the grid terminal.

5- if we are short of adventurers who can cast this nano, we will need some high level adventurers to make rounds to each grid location with a fixer to get him into and out of the grid - he will need to refresh the dragon poly on the participants.

6- we will sit on the grid terminals of the whole planet until FC listens to us! WE WILL NOT BE IGNORED!

The first official NGFY meeting will be held on Friday, June 28th at 6:00pm EST (11:00pm GMT) At the Grid terminal in galaway county.

The meeting will be short and sweet, to gather ideas and discuss our options and plan of action. If you want to oppose our idea, you are welcome to come, flamers and spammers will be put on ignore. This will be a very mature and organized meeting. If you come to yell at us, we will /ignore you.
