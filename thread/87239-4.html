Here's a post I made in another thread (http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=88106):

quote:
I'm not an expert on Enforcers, but I do have some experience with initiatives (as all classes do, my main is a Fixer).

What if Funcom changed the QB/EQB to 7.25/5.25 and remove the 1200+ speed nerf? That would make it impossible for 1800+ initiative guys (highest I've seen is a tad over 2000) to get the QB 1/1. At the same time the guys who don't completely base their characters around initiative (or while FoB is NOT activated) wouldn't suddenly have their damage cut all to hell by an arbitrary 1/1s nerf... and non-Enforcers wouldn't complain about the arbitrary speed cap any longer.

In this scenario, init. beyond 1200 wouldn't help weapon speed as much as it would from 1-1200, yet those who tweak initiative would still be satisfied with the linear decrease in time (but still keeping the EQB away from the 1/1 that Funcom dreads so much) and those with a sane amount of initiative could still wield the QB/EQB with the results for which they're accustomed. Even at 2400 Init, attack+recharge would be 4s, 1800 Init would be 5s, 1200 Init. would be 6s, 900 Init. would be 7.5s.

If Funcom wanted the guys at or below 1200 Init. to have similar damage to what they do now, just subtract 1.3 seconds off the attack. Subtracting 1.3 seconds off attack from 6.25 would put total attack speed exactly the same as 4/7.2 @ 1200 init, both of which would have a total attack/recharge speed of 4.7s. The basic idea would still work. Actually, now that I've thought about it for a minute, it'd be much better to have a new EQB at 5.95/5.25s (to keep in line with the old EQB @ 1200 Init.), otherwise a Fixer with a Manex would outdamage an ENF with the EQB (assuming the Fixer can get 1200 Init. somehow, maybe with Offensive Steamroller?).

My idea for this attack/recharge change was from the old Fixer discussions of why the Manex is so superior to the MCS after a certain point (when rechage is no longer a factor with the Manex @ 750+ RI).
