Roleplayers Association [RPA]
If you or your organisation are interested in being affiliated with The Roleplayers Association let me know. The Roleplayers Association is a Tag [RPA] which acknowledges you and your org as a Roleplaying organisation, and a member of the Roleplayers Association. This is just an idea that might differentiate the roleplaying orgs from just the leveling orgs. The purpose is to plan and organize roleplay storylines and set the stages that will accomplish that end. Feedback is welcomed. All orgs are considered if you roleplay regardless of faction, or your reputation as being on one side or the other, or any orgs that roleplay enemies of each other. If your org is a roleplaying org, then you are qualified. The Tag does not have any Bias or Partial relationships. If you are a roleplayer...you are a roleplayer regardless.
