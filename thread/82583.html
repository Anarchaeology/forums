Hi,

I currently have no idea where it comes from since i noticed this message while draining NPCs and even Players (Bug!?). Everytime you get this message it takes your nano. As a current work-arround most players just keep hitting their Hotkey till it works. This costs alot of nano everytime and when using 5000 Nano to calm a Level200 Mob i think something is definitly wrong with this whatever-it-is.

That's the message you get when a mob/player has a innate resistance to the hostile nano you're trying to cast. For example Grid Armor gives a 10% innate resistance to roots, so if you try to root a GA fixer you may get that message. It also seems more and more of mobs are getting this type of resistance lately
