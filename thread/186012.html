Intelligent Life Forms of Rubi-Ka (vol. 2)
Hello everyone. This is Zargin again conducting another research on a vocally cabale life-form of rubi-ka. This time have studied a Reet of Paradise from the outskirts of Borealis city. It did not go as well as I had hoped, but here is the logging information from that conversation:

Start logging of "Vicinity" at 09/28/03 13:56:15

13:56: Zargin: Hello this is Zargin and I am here with a Reet of Paradise
13:56: Zargin: Tell me reet, how does it feel to be a creature of Rubi-Ka?
13:56: Reet of Paradise: NEED MONEY PLZ
13:57: Zargin: Oh? So your saying the harsh society has left you penniless and on the streets?
13:57: Reet of Paradise: SPARE STUFF PLZ
13:57: Zargin: Ah, and the facist omni-tek has left you with nothing left
13:57: Reet of Paradise: NEED MONEY PLZ
13:57: Zargin: I think we have been over that part already
13:57: Reet of Paradise: CAN I HAVE UR SWORD?
13:57: Zargin: I'm sorry.. but I dont have a sword on me, perhaps talking to an adventurer or enforcer would be of more help
13:57: Reet of Paradise: NEED MONEY PLZ
13:57: Zargin: Anyway, how do you think the economy has been in the past few years? Apparently not too well considering your money needs..
13:57: Reet of Paradise: CAN I HAVE UR SWORD?
13:58: Zargin: Well this has been very interesting, but I must say farewell now
13:58: Reet of Paradise: GIVE ME MY STUFF BACK
13:58: Zargin: I dont have your "stuff"
13:58: Zargin: Perhaps you have mistaken me with another
13:58: Reet of Paradise: CAN I HAVE UR SWORD?
13:59: Zargin: Well, check out the next time I interview some other intellectual creature..
13:59: Reet of Paradise: WHY DID U LOOT ME?!
13:59: Zargin: I wonder why I even try..
13:59: Reet of Paradise: GIVE ME MY STUFF BACK

I will move on to, perhaps, some creatures with more stimulating conversationalist abilities. I hope you will all join me as I continue my research around Rubi-Ka.
