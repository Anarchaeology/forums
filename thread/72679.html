New agent nano idea
I was trying to think of something that would give us a bit of a hand in the pvp stakes wihtout making us uber or unbalancing to other classes in any way....

thnk of this...if we are fighting a Melee based opponent, what is the tactic? Chain root and hope for the best....
This actually work pretty ok if done right and we have a good runbuff. ( prolly better now with the changes to root )

Why not give us this opportunity to use this tactic against ranged fighters?

Nano : Jam weapon
Effect : Reduces the range of the targets weapon by 20m
Effect self: Reduces the casters evades by 200
Dur : 20s
Cast: 5s
Recharge: 2s

Nano: Jam Psyche
Effect: Reduces the range of the targets casting by 40%
Effect self : Reduces the casters evades by 200
Dur: 20s
Cast: 5s
Recharge: 2s

It would tie in nicely with the type of nanos we have now for the crit. Short legth, devastating effect which also Gimps us quite badly for the duration of the nano, so its still a risk to use if they manage to break root and get some shots in on you.

To be more agent like it could even be a New hacker tool that invades thier ncu to produce the effects..

Whaddaya think?
