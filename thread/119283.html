Omni Supreme Court of Justice
Good evening Citizens. I have the pleasure of being transferred from old Earth and I am hereby ordered to oversee The Omni Supreme Court of Justice for Court proceedings. Within my short time here so far..I have seen a few disturbing accusations pertaining to certain parties of Treason and Sabotage. Please direct all inquiries to me and The Court for any disputes of this nature. Witnesses are welcomed as well as evidence. Evidence may be submitted via Screenshot or Log to killgoor@attbi.com and will be presented in a Court of Law which will be held in the main room at Baboons for a time of agreement between two parties involved. Also we are seeking a seat of Judges and a Procecuting attorney. If anyone great Citizen feels they are worthy of being on The Seat of the Courts, please send me an email or message me off or online. Thank you Citizens. Omni-Tek is your Friend!
