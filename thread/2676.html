For those in doubt, within OmniTek.
There is some commotion within OmniTek, and I will hereby explain a couple of things, that might have been forgotten by employees in general.

We are all employees of OmniTek, nothing else. An employee is not to make decision he thinks should be best for the company, that is for the companys board and chairman to do.
An employee is not to question actions taken by the company.

As every company, OmniTek hires everyone of its staff by the individuals expertise. I my self has been hired to spread the word of OmniTek, since my expertise is within public relations, laws and to some extent theology. That is what I am hired for, and that is what I am contracted to do.

Those people we never see, those dwelling in HQ, wandering the dark halls, and living in the offices in HQ are hired to think for OmniTek. That is what they do, and all they do.

And, you: Soldiers, mercs, agents and field agents of all kinds (including field bureaucrats and so on) are hired, contracted to fight for the company, since this is what you do best, this is within your expertise.

Now, if employees should go ahead and do what ever they feel like, within the company, it would be rather obvious that the company would fall apart. If the thinkers in the offices in HQ all of a sudden would pick up guns, and try to defend Omni-1, OmniTek would fail miserably, there is no question of that. And the same is to happen if those hired to fight for the company, would all of a sudden try to think for the company. OmniTek would fail miserably.

The words beyond ones duties, is not to be interpreted as beyond ones expertise. And thinking for the company, is not within your expertise. You are not qualified enough to do that, and nor am I. There are employees better suited then us, for that. So, if all you, hired to fight and die for our great caretaker, now, please would fall back in line again, and remember what OmniTek is all about.
Unity! Loyalty! And above all, trust! Trust Omnitek and its wisdom!

Thank you for listening.

PS: If for some reason, you feel you have been employed in a field that currently is outside your expertise, there are but two ways to solve this. I personally recommend the second one.

1 Resign

2 It just so happens that I personally made available extra room in Reform, where you can talk to our excellent staff regarding your problems. I am sure you will be able to work something out with them.
