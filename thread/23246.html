OmniTek Red Cell recruitment
Red Cell is now recruiting all professions! This is the recruitment bulletin posted on Red Cell's site, http://www.shatteredcrystal.net, and a brief explanation of Red Cell.

OmniTek Red Cell was established as part of Philip Ross' Operation: Shattered Crystal, a series of military campaigns to rid Rubi-Ka of the brutal, terrorist conduct of the Clans, and to protect the innocents of Rubi-Ka from being victimized. It is a newly-established group of counter-terrorism specialists, soldiers, intelligence personnel, and a variety of other professions, all dedicated to ensuring the safety and stability of Rubi-Ka.

quote:
From recruitment bulletin 1:

Red Cell is currently accepting applications for all levels of recruitment. There are no profession restrictions. Applicants must meet the following guidelines:

  • Must be at least level 10 at the time of Academy graduation (any level may apply).
  • Must be located on the Rubi-ka 1 dimension.
  • Must be aligned with OmniTek, or be willing and able to align oneself with OmniTek at the time of acceptance.


If you would like to apply, please fill out the application by clicking here. If you have any questions, contact recruitment@shatteredcrystal.net.
