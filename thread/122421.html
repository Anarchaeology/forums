Newly Updated 'HUGE' Map with Land Control Areas and DynaCamps
Greetings!

A newly updated 'Huge' In-Game Map for AO patch version 14.8 is now available for download!

After numerous requests to include both Land Control Areas and DynaCamps on the 'Huge' map, both are now included (the inclusion of Unique MOBs will be added in an upcoming map version). Absolutely NO Leet DynaCamps have been included in the map! All Leet DynaCamps have been deliberately omitted.

The latest updated version of the 'Huge' map, now includes Land Control Areas and DynaCamps. Also included in the download are supplemental Playfield Help Files with complete information on Land Control Areas and DynaCamps.

The latest version of the 'Regular' Map now includes Land Control Area markings.

_________________________________


The 'Huge' map consists of two maps, a regular sized map with general overview information, and a larger sized map (four times the size of the regular map) which provides incredible detail of playfield terrain and structures. These maps also provide the following information:

- Playfield borders with zone lines
- Playfield names
- Land Control Areas *NEW!*
- Whom-Pah System routes
- Grid Access Terminal locations
- Public Grid Access requirements
- Fixer Grid Exits
- Playfield Locations in the Fixer Grid
- Ferry Routes
- Guarded Locations (outposts, guard posts, etc.)
- Startup Backyard Locations
- Carbonrich Rock fields and qualities
- Labeled Points of Interest
- Dungeons
- DynaCamps *NEW!*
- Amenities (bank terminals, insurance terminals)
- Supermarkets
- Vendor/shop general locations
- Special Vendor locations

Included in the download are supplemental Playfield Help Files with complete information on Land Control Areas and DynaCamps.

A legend can be found at the bottom of the large map, where there are also listings of the fixer grid by floor, public grid requirements, Whom-Pah connections, and a mini-map of the Whom-Pah System routes. On the smaller map, listing of the fixer grid by floor is located on the bottom; listings of the public grid requirements, and Whom-Pah connections are located on the right side; and a mini-map of the Whom-Pah System routes is located in the upper left-hand corner.

These maps function just as the original maps do, allowing for zooming in and out, centering the map to your current location, and uploading your mission location. Please note: Due to the inconsistent placement of playfields within the actual game itself, the reticle may be off by a few pixels in certain playfields. I've come up with the most consistent balance, however, and this should will be a very minor inconvenience.

INSTALLATION

After downloading and unzipping the file, place the HugeMap or RegularMap directory (depending on which in-game map files you downloaded) in the following location:

Funcom\Anarchy Online\cd_image\textures\PlanetMap

While in-game, open up your world map by clicking the 'Map" icon on the bottom of your in-game interface, or pressing "P" on your keyboard, and click on the '?' icon on the bottom of the map window. Select the Travel To Rubi-Ka - Huge Map or Travel To Rubi-Ka - Regular Map link (depending on which in-game map files you downloaded and installed) to view the map.



SUPPLEMENTAL PLAYFIELD HELP FILES

The 'HUGE' map has a number associated with every LCA in each playfield. To find more information on an LCA, look at the corresponding number for the appropriate playfield in the LCA Help Files. The Playfield Help Files also give complete information on DynaCamps.

To access the supplemental Playfield Help Files, open the Playfield Help Files directory and drag the .txt files located in that directory into the following directory:

Funcom\Anarchy Online\cd_image\text\help

To access the Playfield Help Files while in-game, type the following:

/showfile info-playfield.txt

For easier access, you can click on the macro link within the Playfield, LCA, and DynaCamp index helpfiles to create a macro, or you can create a macro by typing the following and place the macro icon on your hotkey bar:

For the Playfield Index helpfile: /macro PF /showfile info-playfield.txt
For the Land Control Area Index helpfile: /macro PF /showfile info-lca.txt
For the DynaCamp Index helpfile: /macro PF /showfile info-dynacamp.txt
