Edvieira
Supa Leet
The AO servers are fine.

A system will only work as fast as its slowest component (bottleneck). For example, having a 2GHz central processor with only a 2 MHz system bus to get data into the processor won't get you very far. Thus, the "lag" experienced by some players is the fast system (in this case your computer and even the AO servers) waiting for the information to pass through any of the bottlenecks.

Point is, new servers won't improve the ping times much more. There is nothing we can do for now but wait until the routers and the end-user (fellow player) components are upgraded.
