quote:
Originally posted by Dhurdahl
If I must choose, then I go for the static missions!!
Different strokes for different folks. I am not a big fan of quests. It is tough for me to role-play "It is essential that you take this vial to Base X and give it to Player Y so that they can cure the population of disease Z, people are counting on you!" when there are four other players around with exactly the same orders. I've camped enough spawns and seen 6 people all get the same "unique" item to have trouble pretending that I'm doing something important and unique for the clans.

With missions, you are doing something unique. You are the ONLY person who the clans are sending to point X to solve problem Y. That's much easier to role-play. The problem is that it is doing nothing to affect the storyline. So: story missions. Imagine that you are sent to kill someone that REALLY matters? Imagine that you are sent to rescue someone that your side needs (say you rescue an important nano scientist and therefore your side gets to see more nano armor in shops?) Imagine that you are sent to retrieve a vial from some neutral mad scientist which will do something that will affect the game (cure virulent minibulls, for instance).

To me, that beats camping for one-time drops that everyone gets any day!
