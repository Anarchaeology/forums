Strange Places and Things of Interest
I have started this thread for people to post on any strange places, people, locations, buildings etc. Anything they have found in game.

They may be quest related, they maybe just fun.

1. In Pleasant meadows not far from 20k is a medium sized cave. This cave has a static dungeon in it that I havent started to explore. What is unusual is that unlike all other static dungeons (that arent named i.e Steps) this one doesnt have Level 1 creatures. It seems the mobs here are set for L70 players.
Will check it out tomorrow and post exact location.

2. In Eastern Fouls there are a few zones with strange names like N.A.B.C.D.W. In one of these zones is a notable outpost on a hill surrounded by a moat. This is a neutral outpost. There a few regular mobs with names like "Mean Bully". One mob standing in the corner is named "Very Mean Bully". Some sort of boss mob or quest mob in a remote location?

3. Zone from Pleasant Meadows into Milky Way and stop at the first Omni outpost you find. You will think you are imagining something as you seem to be hearing "police radio" traffic. You are. Police radio noise is broadcast here. Only place in the game I have heard this.

4. In Pleasant Meadow there is a Clanner/Knight fortress guarded by aggressive guards. The building is easily enter by Yalmaha from the air. Mainly inside youll find a selection of very large empty rooms. At the very top of the structure is a small room witha few clanner terminals - deep in Omni Territory.

Add you own No.5,6,7 etc
Add comments to any of the above if you have further information.

Actually, there are quite a few of these and at many levels. In #1, I believe you are refering to the one that is full of snakes. Part of the fun is in the finding them, so I won't add to much.
There are two right next to each other in mort, a short distance from the grid terminal (you'll probably need to fly there since most players at this level can't grid into sentinels). One is good for 70-90ish and the other for 90-100.
There's a low level one the rocks at the center of the lake at the bronto oasis in Newland Desert. This is where I got my first NCUs.
Just goes to show that when you explore a new area, you have to try every door and cave.

Here is an oddity of interest. Start from the northeast corner of newland, where the rectangular newland meets the L-shapped newland desert. There is a big chimney shaped rock at the corner. Run southwest at the north-south zone line about 100-200 meters south of the corner. If you hit the zone at just the right position and angle, you will run through instead of zoning. Now, run to newland city. The city isn't there. the grid terminal is there, the pavement is there, the mountains are there, but none of the buildings, players are guardsa are present. There is also no water in the lake. I have been able to do this 3 times, but it is finicky and I haven't tried in about 4 patches. I am sure that what has happened is that since the zoning didn't take place, you are still on the server that runs newland desert, even though physically in the area that should be newland. Topology and geology seem to be the only things populated at this level. It is both interesting and a relief that the grid terminal is there and active so you don't have to self terminate to find your way out.
