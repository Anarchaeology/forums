Funny, I'm new to AO and I disagree with almost everybody on the first page. Granted, hunting might have had a glory age that has long since past, but the situation isn't that bad. I've seen MA friends level 6 times in one outting to the SW mines (lvl 30~), and I've had some rather nice experiences hunting throught the full range of Omni territory.

I'm lvl 65 now, and have only been around AO for a few months, but I personally enjoy hunting far more then missions.

A few spots that come to mind are:

  • Virus Builders in Pleasent meadows, great for xp and cash since the items they drop are QL 90s.
  • 800 x 800 in Pleasent Meadows. Nice XP.
  • Claw Camp (400 x 800) in Pleasent Meadows. Potential GA drops.
  • Strike Mamba and Cythorn (sp?) hunting in the Clon**** swamp. Another potential GA drop zone.
  • Shade Camp in pleasent meadows.
  • Leet King camp in andromeda (for level 30s, king drops QL 46 gear). Many other camps are positioned very near here.
  • SW mines. Great for monster parts, which can net an easy 100k for a low level character if they snag up a small backpack full. I go there still at lvl 65 to powerkill herds at a time for quick cash (I don't care what anybody says, if you know somebody willing to do the plasma fast, it's quicker and less of a pain then blitzing at my level).
  • NW mines. Another potential GA drop zone, but not a good one. Decent XP, fun hunting, not a lot of density though.


Hunts allow more freedom in movement, more risk (which = more intensity and more fun to some), better pet pathing, a chance to really make roots and snares worth using and a handy excuse to use speed buff nanos .

I find most people are simply lazy and never put forth the time or effort to explore the uncharted territories that Rubi-Ka has to offer. Don't take that the wrong way, this includes me as well, time is a vary valuable commodity in the real world once one is out of high school, but take some time and go see everything you've omitted from your world view.

To the original poster, I know many people that say the city they grew up in is all they have every experienced because everything they've ever needed was attainable around the block, and I know others that have taken the time to travel the globe. Those in the first group have a tendency to blame the world for there state of affairs, but it all boils down to personal perspectives. The world is the only objective constant between the two visions.

Personally, I've never seen an enigma, so tomorrow I plan to follow my own advice and fixer grid to the enigma village to see the sights. Will any personal reward come of it? Maybe not a material one, but something ephemeral will definitely be gained.

Lastly, I want to make clear that my list above is in no way complete. Like I said, I'm lazy as well.. they are just the hunting grounds that have fallen into my lap, but many others probably exist, you just need to take the time to explore.
