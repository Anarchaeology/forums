Jumpy CLowns
Your worst enemy in team missions are the eager and fearless player that open doors in combat and run into a 4 door room whith a mob in the middle not even thinking about the 3 other doors. That the typical death scenario.

If the team always stay 1 room or half a room behind the puller can even pick singles from rooms with 2 mobs. Its easy to pull melee dealing NPCs because the run to you. The good old trick with NPCs using ranged is for the puller to open the door.. fire one shot and then hide for 1 sec on the left or right side of the door. IF the team now stand in the other side of the room the will be positioned where the NPC will run. Now you can kill it in a controlled enviroment.

IF you got a room with 4 doors.. pulling the back to one of the side rooms are the only solution.

As you level increase. Team missions will be easier and easier. So what startegy you use is based on the teams average level and damage output. Later on you can even split the team in two. 3 kill the left side and 3 kill the right side. Thats efficient XP when mobs drop in seconds. Using 50% of the time running is a waste of time.

Actually ... we should change the name from Puller, to "Picker". The one "Picking" dont have to be the the tank. Your task is to pick em out and drag them to the team. As mentioned.. the line of sight trick is excellent. By the time the NPC run towards your team. the puller/picker have probably lost the aggro.

Pulling as Enforcer and MA works great too. Just taka advantage of the range you got. you dont have to lick the laseraims shoes to get attention.

I've been pulling/picking ... alot. And there always seems to be one jumpy clown that just HAVE to stay in front of me getting aggro from 1-4 mobs. They dont know that they are team killers for some reason.

I made some macros eg . :

/say "--[ Pulling to this room ]--"
/say "--[ Clear ]--"
/say "--[ 2 of them, trying to pick one ]--"

This turned out to be very efficient. Ifcourse some people dont pay attetion. But if you got 2-3 people in the team that are used to team with you, it usually works quite well. Just tell the new guys to stick with your friends, and they usually get the point after 1 mission.

There much more to say about pulling... How to act when you got a mezzer in the team. As you level up.. you will soon see that the picker/Puller role is no longer there. You just stick with simple rules... like.. always go left or right. No matter with pathetic the path end up to be.. you are sure to get a 100% completed mission.

A good thing to do at lower levels is to leave the team in the first room til all the mobs in in the rooms that have doors the the main room is killed. Standard death scenario is when entering the main room after the the 3-4 first mobs are killed. NPCs start poping out of doors and people dont realize how many adds they got before someone die.

If everyone follow the rules. your team can do high QL missions for XP and blitz the boss. If you roll team missions with XP reward you get a fat bonus in the end.
