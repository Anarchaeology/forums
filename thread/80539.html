Hmmm
Under normal conditions I would agree with the people that say that IPR points should not be given out by FC for any little reason. If you screwed up your character, thats your problem...

... HOWEVER..

No one.. and I mean *NO ONE* (but maybe FC people) could have known what the requirements for certain Tower/Gaurdian/Turret types were going to be.. there is NO WAY people could have planned for that. For this reason and this reason alone I think FC should again give a 1 time full IPR or several individual IPR points.

For those of you that sit around on your throne of "Stop crying about IPR points.. you should not have used them up" you need to take a step back and look at what FunCom has done.

With the introduction of Notum Wars, Anarchy Online has changed a ton. And I dont mean just simple land control, Im talking about everything that Land Control effects. A major change in game mechanics such as land control has a ripple effect on the game itself.

For example... with the introduction of land control some people might now favor weapons with a longer range due to the 'open land' nature of warfare now. Prior to this, open land warfare was never really an issue, as pvp areas were somewhat contained because no one really fought anywhere besides 2ho and MMD. Sure.. zones existed in the game that were full 25% but not many took advantage of those areas.. most conflicts occured in MMD and 2ho... which were in the grand scheme of things, contained areas. This is just 1 example of a ripple type effect introduced by land control.

Someone pointed out earlier that some gaurdians take Psychology to build - which is true. Now I would like to meet 1 person in AO that raised Psychology for ANYTHING other than to put on Decranum armor (or to use an AggEnhancer - which most people use cuz theyve increased Psych for Decranum armor anyways). Decranum MK2 armor requires Psychology of 401.. so if you raised it to 401 to get the armor on you still need more Psychology to build one of the new gaurdians. I forget how much now, but I needed 600 or 600+ to build a QL200+ Gaurdian. I have been very careful about how I used IPR points, so this wasnt a problem for me, but it could be a huge problem for other people.

Again.. I normally agree with the people that want to keep FC from giving out new IPR points.. but TOO MUCH has changed with Notum Wars.
