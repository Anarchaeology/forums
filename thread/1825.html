Someone correct me if I'm wrong, but:

Fling Shot is just an extra attack, like any other. The value is that it's instant and additional, but it does the same damage as any normal shot from your weapon. Therefore if you're a soldier with a 1-100 energy gun, that's the range you'll do (plus modifiers, blah blah blah, of course).

Also, the attack bar simply shows you your attack speed. It's a function of the weapon (a 1.35 attack is almost twice as fast as a 2.50 attack, etc.) and the attack type (it takes several seconds to empty a clip with Full Auto).
