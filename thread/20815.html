IP are Improvement Points, they raise skills, once you raise a skill, you cannot lower it after you hit Accept.
IPR will give you points that make it possible to reset one skill (one point per skill), you gain more of these IPR points after new titlelevels (1-14 for I, 15-49 for II, 50-99 for III, 100-149 for IV, 150-189 for V, 190-200 for VI)
