another factor
Don't forget, the skill of the person who assembles your implants also effects the resulting quality (and therefor reqs) for those implants.

Generally, nanny is best used to get an approximate idea of the reqs and bonuses implants will give. There will usually be a small degree of shift when you actually get them made.
