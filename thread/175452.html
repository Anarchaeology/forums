FREEDOM! My proposal for a democratic clan election.
Weapons.

Today I walked the streets of Old Athen and Tir and saw them everywhere. I remember asking myself why, if we are truly free, why do we feel the need to openly brandish instruments of war?

And then I looked down on my Manex Catastrophe December Submachine gun and it hit me. We are not, nor have we ever been in our lifetimes, truly free.

We live in a day and age in which weapons permeate every aspect of our fragile society. Whether at a social gathering, a wedding or merely running down the streets of tir, theyre everywhere to be found. We clutch our guns, clubs, swords and bows next to our bodies, terrified that at any moment our lives may be taken from us. Again.

A great man from long ago named Albert Einstein once said I know not with what weapons World War III will be fought, but World War IV will be fought with sticks and stones. Over 17,000 years have passed since those famous words were uttered, yet they are a testament to us all. 17,000 years have passed, world and galactic wars have passed and here we are, still we fighting with sticks and stone. Be it no matter if the sticks are charged with nanobots and the stones are rocks of Notum. Still we fight.

We kill and kill again and we kill until we ourselves are killed, yet what has it accomplished? What has all of this killing rewarded us with? Peace of mind? Freedom? One might say that the only true freedom IS peace of mind. Until we as a people have attained that, we are all enslaved in one manner or another.

Friends and fellow clan citizens, if I may, Id like to share with you a piece of my mind about peace of mind. While it may seem a jovial play of words, its a very troubling topic weighs heavily on my soul and plays on the fringes of every clanners conscious and subconscious. Today we will speak much of words and what they mean to us all and then I wish to make a proposition for every man, woman and Atrox on the face of Rubi Ka that bears the Clan or Neutral faction status.

We live in a world broken and cracked by hatred and greed, scarred and charred by conflict and dissident and baked with the seething resentment of a thousand thousand dissatisfied citizens. Clan citizens, clutching their tools of destruction, the stink of fear hanging from them, hungry or terrified for their next battle. I am one of those citizens.

And who wins these battles that we fought? Death is not permanent here, so when you give your life for a cause nobody truly believes in anymore, watch as your homes are burned and your spirit is broken, who claims victory?

The Federated Cell Structure technology is owned by Omni-Tek, as are the reclaim terminals that were all so familiar with. Every time you scan, have you ever once given pause to consider where your money goes?

Omni-Tek.

Whether you win or lose a battle, it is Omni-Tek that claims victory that day. Many lives were lost and many credits were spent insuring those lives. Your hard work and death only serves to further the Dues Ex Machina that is Omni-Tek.

Omni. Denoting all, every, everywhere; as in omnipotent, all-powerful; omnipresent. Were that true, there would be no need to fight this war as we would have no hope of victory in the long or short term. The very definition of Omni is blatantly arrogant.

Clan. A word used so often in so many contexts with no real definition of what it means. Its been said in the past that reality is based from our own perceptions, our own opinions. So I implore you all: What is your perception of the word? What is your opinion of the definition of clan? Does clan mean Storm, Synergy Factor, Redemption or Apocalypse?

Were I to rely on the definition provided by ancient texts from a planet I will never see, the definition of clan would appear something like:
1. A traditional social unit in the Scottish Highlands, consisting of a number of families claiming a common ancestor and following the same hereditary chieftain.
2. A division of a tribe tracing descent from a common ancestor.
3. A large group of relatives, friends, or associates.
Were a long way from Scotland, however were all cut from the same cloth of simple miners that were brave enough to rise up against the injustice of Omni-Tek and fight for what they believe in! Those that died, nanomage, Atrox, Opifex and Solitus, those are our ancestors. Our Chieftans.

Take arms against a sea of troubles and by opposing, end them? To die, to sleep. No more.

There were no insurance scanners in those days and still they fought. Fought for you and I to have a home to call our own, fought for independence against Omni-Tek, fought for freedom. Fought and won. How many bodies lay in piles in the streets of what is now known as Old Athen so that you and I could squabble over petty pieces of armor or trinkets like scavenger birds? Is this what we have become? Is this what our ancestors fought for? To win a battle with Omni-Tek only to lose the war against each other?

You want courage? I show you a man nearing permanent death that still possesses the moral fiber to stand for what he believes in and stand strong when the tide turns against his favor, even at the risk of his own life. That my friends, is courage! And us? We run for fear of losing our silly titles. But the foundation is strong. We can and will be rebuilt with the pride, the unity and the honor that our forefathers have laid down so many years ago.

My fellow clanners, today Henry Radiman crawled from whatever hole he was hiding in for so long to walk the streets of Old Athen, perhaps looking to reclaim his haughty throne as leader of the clans. One would not know for sure, as he was too busy to spare a moment for his so-called subjects to explain his actions or his whereabouts for the past year. Is this who you want as your leader?

That word, leader. What does it truly mean to you? Were I to rely the same ancient texts, the definition of leader would be

1. One that leads or guides.
2. One who is in charge or in command of others.
3.
a. One who heads a political party or organization.
b. One who has influence or power, especially of a political nature.
The word leader is one that transcends any worded definition. It cannot be explained, it can only be experienced. For Radiman to walk amongst us with his lifeforce still clinging to him is the biggest affront to us all. To betray the faction he previously represented and then to walk proudly before us without so much as an explanation for his betrayal, let alone an apology, is an insult that I cannot and will not abide!

Does he truly think that his presence here will re-establish his rule?

What has Radiman done for the clans that is so pervasive as to make him once more the de facto leader of the clans? While we remained on the forefront of the battlefield, Radiman played chess with Ross and explored the possibility of peace, a lofty goal inspired from the mind of Philip Ross. Not Radiman. While Radiman held secret meetings in his expansive chambers, we all trudged through the hundred year old war rubble left behind in Old Athen. While Radiman addressed the public from grand podiums in the exclusive Council of Truth building, we swam through the polluted rivers of Tir. While Radiman destroyed our chance of lasting peace, we huddled in dank apartments, terrified of the Omni-Pol Shock Troopers that slaughtered us in the streets of Stret West.

Where is Radiman while we fight and die for our right to mine Notum? Where is Radiman indeed. Should he decide to reclaim his figurehead title as leader of the clans, he will be met for opposition.

TURNING and turning in the widening gyre
The falcon cannot hear the falconer;
Things fall apart; the centre cannot hold;
Mere anarchy is loosed upon the world,
The blood-dimmed tide is loosed, and everywhere
The ceremony of innocence is drowned. -W.B. Yeats

And what of the Sentinels you say? Well what of them? They will leav of their own accord or will be brought down by the force of Storm and whoever shall stand by our side as brothers in arms. Their original purpose to free the clans from the ICC and Omni-Tek was served brilliantly, however instead of sharing the freedom with us all, their greed won over their altruism and they chose to keep it all for themselves, killing those neutrals they saw unfit to walk amongst us and treating us all as their personal servants. By removing our choice, they have enslaved us no differently than Omni-Tek once did. While we all appreciate their efforts in keeping Tir safe from invasion, Simon Silverstone has proven himself unfit to lead. So drunken with the wine of his own power and dictatorial rule, he has done nothing to further the benefit of the clans. The leadership of the Sentinels is drawing to a close. And when the last of the Sentinels have fled or fallen, we will lay down our arms against each other, never to raise them again, put aside our difference and embrace each other as clans, as family, and usher in a new age of prosperity for us all.

And who shall lead us in this new age?

We will decide. We. The clan.

I propose to you all today that a democratic election be established to choose our new leader and council instead of having on imposed on us all. The time to act is now, before the changing of the guard, before more lives are lost at the hand of Silverstones lackeys or the blundering cowardice of the Council of Truth.

And what of the neutrals? Each and every day they are senselessly slaughtered by Silverstones paranoid dogs for setting foot in a land they were once welcomed graciously in and manipulated in allying with Philip Ross and the ICC, made de facto Omni-Tek employees by an obsolete Tir accord and the word of the silver tongued Ross himself.

Should they not welcome Radiman back, as they would regain access to clan lands? In a word, no. Like the clan, Neutrals have fought for their independence and right to lead a life free from rule; to be their own men, women and Atroxes without the dogma of some political figurehead and yet both sides have used them as an end to their means. While Radiman accepted these neutrals as family, his foolishness and absence have led to their continued misery at the hands of the Sentinels. They too would benefit from a new clan leader and council, one that will welcome them back into Tir to live the lives that they choose fit for themselves, celebrate their presence and the diversity they bring and not abandon them to die repeated deaths at the first sign of trouble.

I humbly nominate myself as the first of many candidates and would also like to nominate Sheffy, leader of the clan Synergy Factor, as well as Azzazzimon of Apocalypse.

Before I go into any further details, I am awaiting word from the ICC, Silverstone and a representative of the elusive Genetic Manipulators, to discuss the manner in which nominations and the election itself will be held, but in the meantime, hold fast my brothers, sisters and Atroxes.

The light of freedom is near, let it guide your way until the glorious day that we are once again masters of our own destiny.

Blessings,

Thedeacon.
