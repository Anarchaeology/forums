ah...ok...

basically under level 75 there is a (roughly) 1.3xlevel of the lowest level fighting range cap...so if you're level 30, you should only be able to attack (and be attacked by) people up to level 40...and the same goes in reverse...but if the person is lower level than you, you'd go off his level when doing the math, so you'd only be able to fight people down to around level 24...

now the above is to be taken with extreme emphasis on the word ROUGHLY...I don't know the exact calulation, and I don't think FC has decided to let anyone else know either...some of the math hungry posters here might have had the patience to sit down and figure out the exact numbers...but I"m not one of them


now if you're over 75, you are basically carte blanche for anyone over level 75 all the way up to level 200...and can still be attacked by people lower than 75, if you fall inside their attack range...

Those may be the rules, but ATM there are a number of exploiters that are using a bug that seems to be related to rooting.

If say a level 150 was teamed with a level 10 NT, that level 10 NT could root a level 6 player, and then the 150 player could attack the level 6 player. Normally when a level 10 is teamed with a level 150 they should not be able to initiate combat, nor should the level 6 because of the level caps. The level 6 would get a message saying the target was teamed with someone too high, and the level 10 NT would get a message, you are teamed with someone too high. But ATM roots seem to skirt this rule, so when the root is cast it initiates auto attack on your side and this lets the level 150 attack you.

If you see packs of mixed level players moving around a pvp zone, just don't enter it.

I don't want to mention any names but I've seen people like GRANALITH, CAOL and NOER in teams doing this from time to time in 2ho and MMD.
