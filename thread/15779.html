As you can see, I have canceled my account and it will be closed very soon and I find it very doubtful that I ever return to this or any other FC game.

I have really enjoyed some of the people I've met and I hope to meet you guys in some other game too.

I will still pop to #ao-basher from time to time and I think I'll stay more or less in our guild channel #antiguardians atleast for now but my ingame will be limited to one time visit for today and I will soon fade away from the forums too.

Many people are asking me as why I leave...

Well... I could write a hundred page essay about the problems this game has and even suggest how to fix quite a few of them but it is not something I am willing to spend my free time without payment. If you want to hire me, thats different matter and we can perhaps negotiate a suitable payment for my work

Anyways, as it is, I dont think FC is capable of handling this game and I suggest that they find and hire some really experienced hardcore players, roleplayers and yes, even exploiters to help them in their task if they want to fix this game since it is obvious that FC itself has lost the touch of their creation long time ago.

As for me, well I basically grew tired of waiting another 9 months for them to fix it to be even half of what they promised.

Oh well, I am done with this game and no, you cant have my stuff. I gave it all to guild.
