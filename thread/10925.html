Borderline guard duty
Day.5

I grid into Mort early in the evening for my shift. Imediatly upon arrival I start setting up my team.

Minutes later my team is full and I have the personell needed for a footpatrol of the area.

Always before taking personell I am unfamiliar with out in the wilds I like to test their abilities and if time permits educate them some.

We go to a OMNI hideout I know about and start cleaning it out.
My men seems competent enough, so I declare we head out for our patrol.

As of late our patrolling have been mutch hampered by OMNI personell that lays in hiding, waiting for us.

Anyway we head out and regroup at Elmers before venturing deep into cyborg infested territory. Two Mayors fall before us, then it happens: OMNI attack.

One man took a bullet in the head and was transfered to ressurection. I managed to root the perpetrater before I too fall victim and is awakend in the ressurection boot.

My whole team gets slaughered and mutch needed experience is lost due to the effect of ressurection and the fact that I am too young to have mastered redusing experience loss upon being killed in combat.

Still in rez shock the call comes: 'Masked commandos' attacking OP. I immediatly stand up, swoon. Then finally I am at my feet.

I run down to where two guards are failing in defending our base.

This will not do I think to meself, so I engage the commandos in combat, while calling out to my fellow clanners for help.

Help is forthcoming too, mutch to my surprise we are also getting assistanse from Omni personell in the vincinty. Our atrox guards are a bit daft so I am not sure if my ordering them to sease fireing upon them is heard.

We fight long and hard not really making any headway with theese commandos. All of a sudden they disapear and I feel that our OP was held with the help of Omni personell.

Now this incidend got me thinking, I suspect the commandos to be from the dust brigade. Furtermoore might it be that clan and omni cooperation is not to their liking.

Do they have investments that dictate Clanners and Omni should not unite. Must investigate further.
