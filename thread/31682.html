Seems like an awful waste of space...
Am I the only one that thinks AO could benefit from some serious creature poplulation in the zones? I run from mission to mission and barely run across anything worth fighting en route.

I know there are a few static spawn points and things like that, but in general, the only things I have to really watch out for out in the wild are trees, rocks, and other things that might snag my character and make me have to take him off of auto-run.
