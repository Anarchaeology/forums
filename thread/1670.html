The Notum Society
The Notum Society is finally open to expanding its member base...

Affiliation: Clan

What we are:

In character description:
The Notum Society is a small group of like-minded individuals who seek the truth behind the secrets of notum and Rubi-Ka. We believe that there is more going on than Omni-Tek is willing or able to tell. We also seek a Rubi-Ka where non-Omni citizens can exist peacefully. We do NOT seek the complete destruction of Omni et al.

Out of character description:
A small group of people (which plans to stay fairly small) that likes to Roleplay, but also appreciates being able to step back and see the game AS a game from time to time. We're not big on organization/military practices. PvP is something that SOME of us do, but not all of us. If you like to go on raids, etc, we may participate sometimes, but will probably never lead one. We ARE fairly involved in the storyline and have participated in multiple events. We also do things for each other (run missions for items/help buff/etc)

Give a tell to Canary in game or send email to canary@notumsociety.info if you're interested in joining or just want to talk.
