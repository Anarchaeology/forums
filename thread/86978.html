Nano QL has nothing to do with the level at which you should be casting it. The level you should be casting something has much more to do with your implants and your IP allocation to your nano skills.

For example, if the nanos you can't cast require MC or PM and you aren't raising and implanting them, then it is perfectly sensible that you can't cast them yet. This is fairly common for doctors, since at low levels we focus on raising and implanting BM, MM, and TS to be the best team-oriented heal characters we can.

Atrox generally will not be very far behind other doctors in terms of what they can cast until they reach their breed caps for intelligence and psychic. They will always have somewhat smaller nanopools, but not notably lower nano skills until title 4/5. In fact, most breed differences, regardless of what breed you are, don't appear until title 5 when your base abilities start to cap and the atrox and nanomage health/nano bonuses and penalities have had time to add up.

Glad you're enjoying your doc, spyD6. We have at least 2 level 200 atrox docs on RK2 now. So it's certainly possible. To add a bit to what Jynne said...

Make sure you max out your Intelligence every level. You'll get some trickle down into nano skills by doing that.

Also, as you get higher level, some of your nano lines will always lag. The reason for this is that nano skills use conflicting implant locations. If you decide to implant for BM, MM and T&S like most docs do, you won't have the bonuses from implants to raise MC, PM and SI. So nanos that require those skills will lag.
