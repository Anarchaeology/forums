In my opinion, what it comes down to is natural selection. People see storm, tranq, MR etc in azure/af and they want some, but how? So they hang out with these guilds at raids maybe even join them.. but only so many can get loot this way.

Eventually small groups form, i was part of one of these small groups that formed some months ago before AORH or Acom. Steffani of tranq was basically in charge and kept a mental note of people who deserved loot. Primarily we did smug and fred, almost only these mobs and steff would decide who got loot. And as they say in the animatrix "For a time, it was good"

Then more and more people who weren't part of large orgs began to show up. At this point. smug was not always camped. It was a matter of CAN you kill him not WHEN you kill him. We died alot and sometimes just plain didnt succeed.

then came the next evolution... a Bot! it was a botname everyone recognized and we used it to kill smug and fred! and it was good. more people more success on smug. About this point it started to become what we see today. Acom became highly efficient at killing smug, we never failed, we always knew the time and we kill him and fred with swift precision... sometimes 3-4 times in a day.

Now a point system is in place, and the "idea" behind it is that we help each other.. i help you get a DMM you help me get a Aug Fury! ok makes sense.. but then it grew.. and grew.. and it is what it is today.


thats the history of one of the bots. if your curious.


basically what it comes down to, putting it as bluntly as i can..

People want a chance at this uber loot without having to join a larg org or kiss anyones arse to get it.

bots give alot of people regardless of guild a chance at some loot that was generally reserved for a few large orgs before hand.

We can raid mobs without worrying if the person who decides loot doesnt like us and will favor someone else.. we can see who is getting loot and its fiar. We can do mobs without people screaming in caps and treating us in a condescending manner. It Was nice.

I say was because i havent raided recently with acom for a few reasons.. but its getting bigger and bigger. its not gonna go away because people enjoy the chance to get some loot and frankly we enjoy ourselves.
