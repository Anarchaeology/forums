I miss skill practice
Just want to get people's opinion on this...

Do you like the way AO is where you assign your IPs to your skills and you are instantly skilled,

OR

Do you like having to actually go out and practice your skills to get better?

That's one thing I liked about EverCrack and a number of other games (online or not). You were not only going out to gain experience points, you were also gaining experience points for every skill separately. In AO there's really only one thing to *practice* - gaining EXP. Every skill is just given to you after that. I understand that you definitely want limits on skill gain. You don't want everyone getting good at everything.

What I would like to see is that you use your IP's only to guide your player's skill progression. If you put 10 points into martial arts, you don't imediately get 10 points better, you have to practice for some time to gain that skill. But you are capped at the level of points you have spent.

You can freely remove skill points from any skill and put them into another skill but you will have to practice this new skill to gain the benefits. The skill that gets points removed will either come down over time or just immediately drop, whatever is fair.

With something like this, we have no need for IP reset patches and we have a whole slew of new things for players to do. We might also see that there are less characters being generated because instead of restarting your character from scratch to concentrate on different skills, you can just drop a current skill and pick up a new one. But of course you have to practice for a long time to get good at it.
