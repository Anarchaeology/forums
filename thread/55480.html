Armor / weapons query
I don't understand why most of the weapons and armor in the game are useless. Instead of different items having disadvantages and advantages, we have Uber lines which have slightly higher reqs.

For example, Albrecht tank armor, before this was introduced most people wore cloaks as tank armor limited some of your skills quite heavily. However, they fixed that but also added this new "albrecht " line of tank armor. I was in newland the other day and nearly every single player regardless of proffession was wearing an albrecht tank. The main problem with this is that before, tanks came in white, red and brown varietys, also light medium and heavy. The only Albrecht I have seen is heavy and red, so now, everyones got big red tank armor on because it adds to nano pool, something that all proffessions benefit from.
How boring is it to see every player with the same clothes ? Its bad enough that most proffs only have one option when it comes to armor, and you can quite easily tell a fixer / doctor / enforcer etc by the ONE set of armor they wear.

If your going to create an item that ql for ql beats the crap out of similar items then everyone is gonna use it and all the characters within a proff become the same, game becomes boring.

Items that lower certain skills as well as offering advantages offer players choices in character development, and bring a uniqueness to each character. Why do 90% of the items offer no disadvantage ? Why are there loads of items in the game that no one uses because 1 or 2 items rule over all items of that type ?

To me this is nearly as stupid as the camping debate. Am I calling for nerfing ? I don't know, maybe and I'd be prepared to accept a lot of nerfs if it meant we all had more options on how we looked and the items we wear. I guess a lot of people wouldn't though, as a most the people that play this game now only care about having better equipment / more money than the next player, and not the good of the game.

I've been playing for nearlyu a year now, I've got as far as 155 and I don't care that a lot of people have got to 200 in a lot less time than me. I do care though that I am pretty much the same as most other enforcers and if I try to be different I would effectively gimp myself. I guess I cannot speak for everyone but I'm sure we'd want Options over Uber Items.
