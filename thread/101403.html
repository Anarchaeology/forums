Ranged Init is easier to buff than Phys Init (cough TIM cough), as is Pistol vs. Bow.

The Schuy looks like it does a bit more damage over time, by a smallish margin, and assuming 100% agg/def... 600 init won't make a Schuy fire at max speed at even 50% by a good margin. But then, it will also get parried a lot (no word on when FC will fix this). Neither will make you a damage dealer, but Schuy will do a bit more damage, while being harder to get equipped at lower levels (Docs TL4 cap Bow at 450 or so, which makes it impossible to use a ql200 Schuyler, I think, and difficult to use even a 180 (with full implants, +132 wrangle, Dragon sleeves, and the band you can do it in TL4), until into TL5). I'd also point out that it is more realistic to find a good pistol with specials than a bow, as bows with specials are generally poor (tiny clip size = not good with healing a lot).

I went with Schuy for style, but I am also considering IPRing to pistols, should the opportunity present itself. I don't have any personal experience with the Elect., though, so hopefully someone else can fill in that blank.

Any weapon is restricted by your knowledge of it and how to use it. You also have to be carfeful with the settings on Auno and Jayde's databases. Jayde (for some reason) defaults target AC to 0, and Auno goes at 3000, which is pretty low. The attack rating, speed, crit chance (Jayde 3%, Auno 15%) all need to be set correctly to get a realistic damage output... and also note that Auno goes by a 10 second timer, Jayde by 30 second, which can affect the important of specials (like a burst recycling at 15 seconds versus 20).

Also, <warning: Understatement coming> AO is buggy. Healing can jam a reload proc faster than anything else.

Aimed shot is popular because it does good damage and tends to recycle fairly quick.

Concealment is not really needed to do an AS. In PvP, sneaking is completely irrelevant. You can fire at the same person while standing toe to toe every time the special attack becomes available. In PvM, you do have to try to surprise the mob. Concealment can come into play here, but it also works fine if you AS the mob as soon as you see it - the mob might not have noticed you yet. After you AS the mob, you will not be able to do so again as long as you stay in line of sight of the mob. If you have teammates distracting the mob, you can go hide somewhere and then come out and attack again.
