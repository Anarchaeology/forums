The amnesty and how to keep it
I've not spent a lot of time here so forgive me if this idea has been mooted before.

Since we are having problems with both clan and Omni-Tek members breaking the amnesty why don't the guilds do something about it? What I imagine are clan and Omni "police" made up of members of various guilds who patrol their respective 25% areas and take down anyone from the opposing side who is seen breaking the amnesty.
This provides an RP opportunity for the guards who can walk around saying "Evening All" to passers-by. People who want to PvP now have the added fun of trying to find somewhere where the guards aren't around, which kinda makes sense from an RP viewpoint.

I like the idea as it gives guild members something "useful" to do on Rubi-Ka (aside from missions/levelling) and it gives an opportunity to effectively change "happy gas" levels in a small area.
This is open to abuse of course and you may think that it is the same as teams hitting any "enemy" they see but I imagine people taking on the role of police and only attacking those they see breaking the law.

What do you think?
