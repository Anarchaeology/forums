Currently, Agents & Traders need to find the highest-possible ql rifles & shotguns, in order to keep up with an equal-level MA, for instance (who has no concern with the endless weapon-scavenger-hunt).

But my arguement has *nothing* to do with profession balance. Profession balance can theoretically be attained (in PvP, PvE, whatever) under either set of equipping rules (i.e., if we're reducing the effectiveness of Agents' rifles, then we can either give them some other advantage, or else nerf the MA's, in turn).

The joy of equipping comes from the extra element of control that the player has, in the character developement process. Playing a weapon user is comparable to driving an auto with a manual transmission; whereas MA-playing is like driving automatic. Some people enjoy that extra element of control; others find it bothersome.

Character building (which includes weapon equipping) is one of the most enjoyable aspects of the game, for many. Funcom, if you really, really, really feel that Agents & Traders are horribly out of line, in terms of balance, then fine. Nerf whomever you think needs it.

But please not like this. Not this way. Don't reduce the tactical gamer's ability to fine-tune their character. Don't remove any of the colors that we like to paint with from our palette. The "casual gamer" who wants to blaze through levels with less worry over planning gear & weapons can play an MA. But what will we play?

Definately not AO. Not me. Not if these changes are implemented.

All of these people who have cried about equipping are having an issue with balance, not player-developement-freedom. So just go and balance the game for everyone who whines. Never mind the fact that THERE ARE CHARACTERS WITH PVP TITLES FROM EVERY SINGLE PROFESSION OUT THERE! They couldn't *possibly* be balanced, because someone who doesn't understand the game mechanics seems to be crying, again!

So come again with the nerf stick & the facial tissue. If this is how you're going to react to the tears of whiners who just don't get it, then you're going to be "balancing" the game every month for the next four years.

Yours until the changes are put in place,
