Clearing the Controversy
Its about time I make a statement since these days there are is a large massing of jealous individuals who seek to defame myself for my efforts and contribution to the game. Some of this will be IC, some OOC.. trust me you'll know the difference.. it is me after all.

Alright first up: I am forever Omni-Tek, I work for Omni-Tek, and will deffend Omni-Tek until my last breath. Any one who is traitorous to Omni-Tek is in my eyes an enemy and a threat to the growth and productivity of the company. Hence my recent mission into TBA where I set to weaken the traitorous faction for its crimes against the corporation, which I should point out, had gone unpunished. I did this in hopes that Omni-Pol would soon after act and arrest the guilty parties since their force was not as great as it once was, this did not happen, but TBA was weakened none the less, despite the claims by TBA officials. After I had completed my mission into TBA I was due to resign, but TBA did beat me to the punch when they discovered my true intentions. Now, with a faction like TBA, who here can condem me for trying to rid Omni-Tek of such a group that kills their own brethren? If you do, then I strongly suggest you look at the facts closely.

After my departure from TBA I set into the suspected traitorous player run guild Omni-Pol (OTSEC) to investigate matters concerning OTSEC officials meeting with clan leaders and disclosing confidential information in exchange for credits. I did indeed find this and was due to make a report on it, Omni-Pol then removed me for inquiries to another suspect OT faction. Thats fine, no love loss there.

Now since I began these missions to uncover people who are with Omni-Tek, but also working against it, I have come under alot of fire, mainly due to false statements being made against me, some IC, majority OOC. TBA has issued many false statements such as "I work for the clans and am set out to destroy Omni-Tek from within" That couldnt be furhter from the truth. I indeed am set in my ways, and that is to make certain Omni-Tek remains powerful and contains loyal employees, when I discovered what I did about TBA and took action, it was expected that they would retaliate with such hateful attacks, given their very poor reputation. And so the anti-Tisantous campaign began, and all who have envied the accomplishments and contributions I have made to the RKT and the story (or lack there of) jumped on the band wagon. Let me tell you all this, I enjoy Role Playing, I do it well, I do not enjoy however, childish tactics taken by some to destory the game play because they have a burning rage of jealous hatred.

Points I will clear up:
I am Omni-Tek forever and do not work for the clans, nor I will ever work for them.
I always will seek out the traitors to Omni-Tek and see to it they are dealt with, I will never and have never set out upon those who remain loyal.
I am not a man set on destroying the Omni-Tek family, despite the bitter statements from those who have displayed their immaturity.
I Role Play In Game 98% of the time, but when some begin OOC attacks, I can and will step OOC to deffend myself.
I will continue to contribute my ideas and influence the story despite the jealous players who will protest and release false statements about me or my character.
I am not an evil, mean person, I can be when you step into the OOC attack ring, and those who have, are witness to this.

I expect to get slammed even after this post. Most likely TBA members, OTSEC, or OP members, or the followers to the anti-Tisantous campaign. And it will not surprise me since I have received hate tells in game on a daily basis from all of these parties, ones that include slander to me OOC such as calling me a "******" "Idiot" "someone who will never amount to anything, and is nothing" "A waste of time and breath" "And I need to get my gay ass beat in a gay bashing in RL"And to those people I say, grow up and stop being so childish, so I found you out In game, so go along with it and RP, dont make things up to destroy me or my reputation by attacking my ethics, morals, or intentions, and especially dont attack me OOC as you have so commonly. I hope this clears up alot of the controversy surrounding me as of late, Im not here to bicker, or argue, but after these past few weeks I have had my fill of being slammed and I thought it best I clear the air once and for all. I am a decent man, one you would like if you get to know, and I implore all of you to not be swayed by the misguided statements of people who IC work for Omni-Tek and stab it in the back, and OOC attack anyone in RL insults. I will monitor the replies here in case I need to step up to the plate again, and Im sure I will. Be well, and as always:
Omni-Tek Forever!
