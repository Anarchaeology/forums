Biggest news
I think the biggest part of all this that didn't get too much high profile discussion is that they finally got a body of one of the Dusters.

Both Omni-Pol and the Council security forces have been wondering how the DB has been able to bypass the happy gas.

With a complete 'specimen' in hand I'm sure we're going to be treated to a bit of information.

Other information should now be at hand as well. For example:

His boots and the creases of his uniform should surely contain mineralogical clues at to his recent whereabouts.

His lungs and blood chemistry should contain information regarding the atmosphere he spends the majority of his time in.

His hair and fingernails should contain clues as to the composition of his diet.

A lot of this information should be key to piecing together the puzzle of where they hang out and how they sustain themselves outside the borders of Rubi'Kaan society.
