Try this
Can not take credit for this actual explanation but this has always seemed about right to me.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

If an attack cycle is going, only that is going.

If no attack cycle is going, then both weapons recharge at the same time.

So:

Attack(rhand) + Attack(lhand) + Recharge(both)

If dual wielding two 2.5s 2.5s atk/rch weapons, the cycle goes like:

Atk(rhand, 2.5s) -> Atk(lhand, 2.5s) -> Rch(both, 2.5s -> start over

If dual wielding a 1.5s 2.5s and 2s 2s weapons:

Atk(rhand, 1.5s) -> Atk(lhand, 2s) -> Rch(both, 2s) -> Atk(lhand, 2s) -> Rch(both, 0.5s) -> Atk(rhand, 1.5s) -> Rch(both, 1.5s) -> Atk(lhand, 2s) -> Rch(both, 1s) -> Atk(rhand, 1.5s) -> Rch(both, 1s) -> Atk(lhand, 2s) -> Rch(both, 1.5s) -> Atk(rhand, 1.5s) -> Rch(both, 0.5s) -> Atk(lhand, 2s) -> Rch(both, 2s) -> FINALLY start over

Heh, anyways.

What this boils down to: to get most bang for your buck, find as fast as possible atk timed and the "penalty" of weapon timer on the recharge side for dual wielded weapons. A x:x weapon dualed is only 1.33 times as powerful, whereas a x:2x weapon dualed is 1.5 times as powerful, all the way to 0:x (too bad 0 attack time is not possible heh) dualed is 2 times as powerful.

In an ideal setting, you can get 50+% damage output dual-wielding. But, when you factor in everything that occurs during combat, it fluctuates.

Off the top of my head, things that can make your timing off are casting nanos, using a special, reloading, and moving around. Unlike using a two-handed weapon, dual-wielding requires more player thought and attention. You can't press Q and stare at the screen while activating a macro once in a while.

To get the most damage output in the shortest time possible, wait until you've fired both pistols before activating a nano. Don't move around if you can avoid it. Use pistols with decent sized clips to offset the fact that your special attack will make the number of rounds remaining uneven, making one pistol require reloading earlier. And always reload after a battle.

These are just my thoughts on how best to dual-wield pistols. Others may have different ideas and tactics.
