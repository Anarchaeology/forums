Nerf's versus bugs
I've been playing this game for a while now, and for each patch the stability has increased, but at the same time nerf after nerf has been forced upon us. At this point i'm reading the upcoming 14.2 nerf's, and still they arent fixing all the problems. There are still to come highlevel areas/shops, and shadowbane isnt being released this summer (ive been told the new release date is next year or so!?!). Doing teammissions from level 110->200, isnt much fun, and what is the point of getting all that cash with no shops/items to spend them on?
FC is very good in nerfing/removing things that works too good, but not replacing them (quickly enough) with new items/skills. The fun of the game has steadily been removed for my part, where its now becoming increasingly boring to play the game. What is the point of playing my trader, when they are gonna do some bigtime nerfs? There isnt much point in playing petclasses at the moment (does pet pathing ring a bell?), and with the future OE patch, there will be NO reason playing the game as engi/crat/Mp/(and even agents). FC needs really to know what makes a game fun, dont worry too much that people will level too fast. When they implement changes that affects the dynamics of the game; they should be aware that a lot of people have spent a lot of time developing a char, and suddenly that char is practicaly worthless. And now they're gonna make it easier for new chars???? I've never had any problems with the startup conditions.. Are FC desperately trying to get a lot of new players to start the game? But what about the veterans? Is FC going to put that much consideration into them as well? Well well, this was just my little rant.... And yes im actively searching for an replacement game for AO, they've removed the fun of the game too much. Byebye AO

OK, so if your character cannot hang with anyone else without a SUPER wrangle or OEing like crazy, then how good is the character anyway. I know I am asking to be flamed here, but the people who have put REAL time into this game are the ones who have only equipped stuff with self buffs or minor wrangles and who can live on Rubi-Ka under their own steam.

If your character needs other to survive or to be Uber, what is that saying? It says nothing about your character, but that other characters have really cool buffs.

I use other people to help assemble imps for me and to cast immediate buffs for combat (VS, essences) and for implants (doc buffs, IC, and Esenses). I will always cast masteries for free on people if they need it, I dont care what they do with it, but now, I think it just makes more sense. If I need a new wrangle from someone everytime i need to change the way my char looks (armor) or what weapon i am using it seems like i would spend more time looking to get wrangled than actually playing. A SUPER low level engineer who has put all IP into Comp Lit get masteries then mochams to cast a DARK red pet to me (50 levels his senior), and he can walk around all day with a killer bot walking through missions like water seems wrong to me.

Oh and if you think SWG will be bug free and solve all the problems you have with this current game, you are kidding yourselves. The same issues plague all games of this sort. Try another one, and read their boards. Camping, no-high level content, and as with everything a boredom level will eventually catch up with you. Just because it is new will not make it best. AO proved that. These games hit their stride after about a year and Rubi-Ka is on the rise my friends. OH AND TO THE GUY WHOS ROOMATE IS "Beta Testing"? In closing, take your time with these type of games. If you want to be the best FAST, play a single player game where you can cheat. Ever notice that after you cheat in game once and get to the point where it is no challenge, you lose interest? It happened to me with single player Starcraft. Soon as I cheated once, it became boring to continue and I only played multi-player after that. Think that might be the case here? OF COURSE i am not saying OEing is cheating BTW. And remember you can still OE 20%. A major bump up still my friends... And if you were doin more than 20% anyway, it wasnt your character that was good, it was other peoples buffs.

Step out of the flame bag for 1 second and seriously take a look at what I am saying about this. It makes sense. It evens the playing field between someone without a high level ALT or powerful guild and the guy who just starts out. Like I did.

My 2 cents.

Be kind. Just my thoughts. As valid and as right or wrong as anyone elses.....
