I quit a long time ago. A few weeks after release actually. I'm glad I did too. Spent that time playing the competition. It gave me something I didn't have but now realize is a must for playing MMORPGs. I didn't have lowered (realistic) expectations.

In the time since I left and my recent return AO has made a huge!! improvement. Anyone who thinks it's been FUBAR all along needs to go check out some of that greener grass the competition is growing. After trying a few different brands and finding out they taste a little bland and aren't as smooth as you thought, you might have a new appreciation for AO and Funcom.

Since I've come back I've been dreading getting up in levels because I didn't want to deal with the result of OE. Now that it's being fixed the game will be more balanced, more friendly. You won't have to min/max, have a "friend", join an uber guild, or level up a different character just so you can "equip" your character to play the high level game. You also won't have the d00ds hanging out in the arena with thier lvl 1-10 OE chars getting easy kills off of n00bs... who really are newbies getting their first impressions of the game. I can't see how leveling the playingfield can be a bad thing.

The only people I see having a real problem with this OE change are what were so effectionately known in other games as "gankers". I think they will quit. In my experience it'll be a better world without them.

There will be growing pains as balance is brought about, it's unavoidable. It's not something you won't see in any other MMORPG. They are all flawed with balance issues, bad customer relations, and bugs. AO is making a noticeable improvement. "Cosmetic" is the word that comes to my mind to describe most other MMORPGs "improvements". It's the amount of change that has happened that will keep me around AO in the future.
