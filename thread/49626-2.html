If the loot is in a 25% zone, its nodrop, and everyone involved hasn't already agreed on who gets it then you have to roll for it whether its dangerous or not. That is simple fairness. If its not nodrop then you should pick it up and move to a suitably safe place to roll for it.

Of course we all know that there is little room for simple fairness when greed is involved. This game is not programmed for simple fairness since other players have no way to tell what you loot from a corpse to observe whether simple fairness is taking place or not.
