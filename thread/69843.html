The effect of the nanoprogram is permanent, but the pet summoned by the effect isn't. There's a subtle difference there

Anyway, only Engineer pets last forever (as long as the engineer is around and the pet isn't killed). The trick is to keep a close eye on "/pet report" be ready when the duration is about to expire.
