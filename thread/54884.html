A few things that you might want to repair...
Here is my list:

1. Retool and Compress NCU do not refresh the nano if it is running, we have to dump all of our buffs just to recast it.

2. Update team members with mission coords does nothing at all.

3. Trader mind control does not end when the mob dies; what are we controlling for 8 minutes while frozen in place?

4. Lag is still present in many places making the game unplayable depending on the area you are in.

5. Items still vanish and reappear in inventory randomnly.

Suggestions:

With this high-tech world, we should be able to:

1. Map out places we dont know by going there. I can do this now with a GPS device from Best Buy.

2. Helmets that give us a HUD; things that show us auto levels, life, aggressions, etc. The tools that now do this should be able to be put in your helmet.

3. Actual machine gun fire; The weapons in the game now are not even as good as what I can buy on the street from the guy on the corner.
