GREAT idea about how to make Anarchy Online the best it can be.
I think I have a great idea about how everybody can be involved in the making of AO... Let the players decide what is going to be new in AO!

This is how it would work.
The programmers would make a list of suggestions made by the AO community and themselves, and give an estimate of how much work it would take to complete the project. Lets say that one suggestion is to make templates where people can write information, and pass the words to other players. And another suggestion is to make an insurance form for certain NODROP items, so that if they're lost to a buy, they can be retrieved.

Tradeable Template - 4 days of work
Insurance forms - 8 days of work
Capturable territory part 1 - 12 days

Then, the people or Rubi-Ka can vote to which order they would like things to be handled, instead of just voting for 1.

Insurance forms 1st place
Capturable territory part 1 2nd place
Tradeable template 3rd place

There would be a page showing what the programmers are working on and when they start certain projects.

Current projects:

Insurance forms - ETA (date)
Capturable territory part 1 - ETA (date)
Tradeable template - Estimated Time of Start (date)

Their could be forums attached to each project so that people can give their opinions.

And each month or so, the patch would come out and we would get what we want . Sounds good to me.

BTW, Their would have to be some dedication to fixing bugs of course.
