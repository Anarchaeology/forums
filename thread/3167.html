Could Be Fun. Could Be TOO Much Fun.
So let's temporarily go with the assumption that it's something like this: you pay money to build it, activate it like a pet, select a target, "/pet attack," and it goes and does One Big Noisy Attack and dies.

My first thought was, "Gee, if they make it look exactly like an Engineer Automaton, that could give some people a nasty surprise." You know, "Oh, look how harmless you are, all you have is an Engineer Automaton." Bwa ha ha.

My second thought was, "Well, now we know what we're supposed to do when our Slayerdroid won't fit through the door."

Unfortunately, that led to my third thought:

Crowd of mobs at the edge of perception range. Engineer targets one, backs way off, says "/pet attack" -- and the Kamikaze Pet runs over and blows it up.

Can the other mobs tell who to aggro on? I mean, right now, it seems to take mobs a while to figure out that that harmless looking guy over there is where the brain is stored, and distance and barriers help fool them, but they do eventually figure it out. With a one-attack wonder, will they have time to figure it out?

If it's affordable, this makes an engineer an interesting sniper in suppression gas levels 25% and below, too. Hide in the tall grass or weeds with your 3rd-party camera set to overhead view, way overhead. Hostile enters vision range, blow it up.

I guess that's not too overpowering though. To fire a second shot, you've got to activate another shell, which you can only do standing up. And if you don't want it standing over your hiding place, you'll have to lead it away to somewhere and have it "/pet wait."

So yeah, could be interesting. But given perception limits and range limits on everybody but puppeteers attacks, could be way, way too interesting.
