Part 2

“There are no weapons on this thing?” Andrea kicked the rear wheels of the ‘truck’, a towering mobile research station standing over eight meters high and resting on ten massive tires that were each more then a meter wide.
“It wasn’t designed to fight your pitiful war,” the frail nano-mage shifted his weight onto his ornate cane “it’s sole purpose is to be a field lab for scientific missions, little girl” Christian spat out the last two words.
Johan could see the anger building up in the tiny opifex. He quickly moved to get between the two. “Both of you, stop it!” He turned to Andrea, pushing her away from the coming conflict. “If all goes right we shouldn’t need any weapons. Now calm down.”
“Calm down?! After the way he just spoke to me? The hell with that, I’m going to rip off his friggin’ head off that gimpy little body of his!” Andrea pointed over Johan’s shoulder, drawing a crude grin from the mage.
“And then what?” Snapped Johan, grabbing hold of her hand. “Kill him, feeling the gratification that you can do that to another living being? After you do that I’ll make damn sure your ass gets thrown right back into Omni-reform. If you really want to go back there I’ll save you the trouble of having to kill him and send you back right now. Is that what you want?”
Andrea looked sheepishly away from his gaze. “No, Commander.”
“Then I suggest that you straighten up and let me deal with him.”
“yes, Sir”
Johan released her from his grip. “Go make sure all the communications equipment is working and get ready to move in five.”
Andrea turned to the truck’s access ladders, kicking at random rocks and pebbles that happened to be in her way. “Damn arrogant nanos... we’ll see...”
Johan watched her ascend to the roof and listened to her mutter simple threats and hexes as she did so. This mission was already off to a bad start, he hated the fact that he wasn’t able to pick his own crew. The chances that he would get stuck with a team that was always bickering with one and other was a situation that he never liked to be in. It seemed he had found his way into one now.
“Be careful of that equipment, girl. It cost more then you are worth.” Shouted Christian to the opifex on the roof.
“Enough out of you!” snarled Johan. “I’m not liking the fact that I’m only here to baby sit you. The least you could do is show a little hospitality to your protectors.”
“My protectors?” chuckled Christian. He took the bulk of his weight off of his cane and held a hand towards Johan. A small ball of flame manifested itself in his palm. “These are my protectors, mister Washington. Tiny nano-bots fueled by the notum element of this scared planet. I put more trust in these bots that I can’t see rather then the flesh and blood of my own kin.”
“And the fact that where we’re going there is no source of nano-bots doesn’t worry you?”
“But there is nano-bots where we are going. The scans of that dust cloud confirm that.”
“Just remember that we have to get through the outlands to get there.”
“I’m not worried mister Washington. As long as you keep your rouges in check, everything will be just fine.”
Christian walked away from Johan, slowly making his way up the metal ramp that lead into the truck. Melvin came out, bringing his hand up to shield his eyes from the harsh suns; Christian passed him, only recognizing his existence with a snort of disgust. The hefty atrox made his way down to Johan.
“What’s with him?”
“Who in God’s name knows. He sees us only as an unwelcomed insurance given to him by omni to ease their worries.”
“We could always tell Omni where to stick it and walk away from the job.”
“If only we could. For you and the rest of the team this mission is nothing more then a test to see if you’re Omni material.”
“Oh” replied Melvin, scratching his bald head. “What about you then boss? Why not decline this mission if you don’t like it?”
Johan chuckled “My last mission before retirement, Melvin. If I don’t take this one I lose my pension check.”


Johan and Melvin entered the truck, Melvin had to bend down so that he wouldn’t hit his head on the low ceiling. The temperature inside the truck was more then ten degrees higher then that outside; with the airlocks opened the environmental system had powered down to conserve energy. Alexander busied himself by putting away supplies into cabinets and onto various shelves. He snapped to a ridged salute when he saw Johan. “Commander, almost done securing supplies, Sir!”
“At ease lieutenant, this isn’t the academy. No need be that formal around me.”
“Sorry, Sir” Alex let out the breath he had been holding in and let his shoulders slump. “Guess I’m still learning.”
Melvin made his way over to him and patted Alex on the back with a heavy hand. “Don’t worry kid, stick with me and you’ll learn all ya needs to know.”
“Like how to end up in prison?” joked Alex, poking the atrox in the ribs with an elbow.
“Getting in is the easy part, it’s surviving your stay there that is the hard part.”
Andrea stepped into the truck, sweet heavy on her brow. She closed the airlocks behind her automatically lower the inside temperature down to comfortable levels. “All set, Commander. Even boosted the signal on the transmitter, we should be able to get closer to the target area now.”
Johan nodded “Good, get prepared to move out then.”
“Um, Sir... we’re missing one aren’t we the brief I got told me that there would be six of us.” Questioned Alex.
“Correct, we’re meeting our sixth on route at Omni-2.”
“Athens!?” Melvin’s head reared up slamming into the ceiling with little notice from the atrox. “That’s clanner territory.”
“I’m aware of that. As long as we convince the clanners that we are ICC representatives we will be fine.”
