Please Remove Crit Buffs from MA NPCs
Funcom, please remove crit buffs from MA NPCs. They are not only casting wayyy above their level (I was testing on a gray NPC that should never have critted my new level 99 MA that critted a lot- indicting that a mob, under at least level 70, had been able to cast LMA.)

In fact, in a team today,we were trying to take on some light orange (it was more for the loot than the XP for that mission) hardened techwreckers.. We had a team ranging from 80-110.. It should be easy, right? Wrong. The main room had 3 orange mobs. All MAs. They critted nearly every time they hit, most of us had maxed evades. This is very imbalencing as far as mobs are concerned, who have skills far above their level allowing them to have super-high crit buffs at low levels. Either remove them, or make it so only very high level MA NPCs can cast these nanos.
