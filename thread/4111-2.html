Re: Re: ok...
This might be true if the chance was always based upon the number of tries. However, it's probably based upon something more like flipping a coin. No matter how many times you flip the coin, it's always a 50% chance to get heads or tails. Your chances never go up because you've flipped it 1 or 1000 times..

If the terminals are using a random number generator, your chances will always be 5%, no matter how many times you try the terminal..

This is unlike trying to draw an Ace from a deck of cards.. everytime you pull out a card (assuming you don't put it back), you increase your chances of pulling the Ace, because you're eliminating the non-Ace cards..
