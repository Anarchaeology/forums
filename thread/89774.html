what do you guys have against 1hb users?
I've been using 1hb since I started playing this game with my enforcer over a year ago. I'm closing in on level 200 and now and am fed up with the 1hb situation. While the premium alloy staff is a decent weapon, there has never been a good option for the offhand. The Premium Pipe is a joke and the lower ql alloy staffs are not that much better. Furthermore, all of these weapons are *crit* dependent which makes them horrible for p v p.

At the very minimum, the multi-melee requirement could be lowered so it would be possible to wield two premium alloy staffs. While us 1hb guys would "still" suck in pv p, at least we could do some real damage in p v m.


As for the bloodbat, might I ask why the blood bat is the hardest weapon to obtain in the game??? I mean the qb is incredibly simple to get. Every enforcer seemingly has one these days. So get a qb u say? Perhaps I don't want to use the same weapon as seemingly every other enforcer on rk-1 or whatever it's called now. Perhaps I, and I know it's a concept, like to actually have my own identity than be just another atrox with a qb.

So how putting a good 1hb weapon with obtainable multi-melee requirements on a hard mob to kill, but not an almost impossible mob to kill who even when he has been killed has yet to drop the stupid bat!

-sludgeee
