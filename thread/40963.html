I have experienced lots of times that funcom uses floating point numbers (i.e. 149.78) for just about everything, skills most importantly. They round number off to display whole numbers (i.e. 150) to us. This is noticable occasionally when someone in your guild or group says "dammit the grid says I need 150 complit and I *have* 150!!" Well what they really have is 149.78 and the game rounds up to show 150 but then uses the 'real' number of 149.78 for checking to see if we can grid, equip that new weapon, wear that armor, etc. The solution is usually putting a single point into the skill that's 'not working' or buffing it, etc. This is a common problem with any program that uses floating point numbers and is definitely not something that just funcom wrestles with.

The formula that calculates how many IP you have probably has several such numbers in it and, therefore, generates something that's hard to dupe outside the game.

I think.
