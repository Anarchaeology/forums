Request for a Leet Reservation.
Daily, Omni-Tek sends hunters out into the hills of Borealis and other equally leety places with nets and cages. They capture as many leets as they can, then grid back to the training grounds. They release these leets, where they will be promptly killed by newbie hunters.

This is UNFAIR, it is CRIMINAL, and it is INSANE. Omni-Tek should know better than to restrict the rights of leets.

I am assuming you all know of the park in Omni-Entertainment. Hardly anyone ever goes there, making it a beautiful place with un-trampled plants to sit and watch the sunset. Occasionally I go and have a stroll there.

Because of its non-population, it would be perfect to convert into a Leet Reservation. Of course, people would still be allowed to come and go as they pleased, but there would be whole packs of leets breeding and living there.

Here are my requested additions:

1. Construct gates at each entrance so the Leets may stay inside, yet people can come and go.
2. Increase the suppression gas to 100% inside the park, so nobody can kill the leets and they are allowed to breed.
3. Leet chow dispensers that output chow 3 times a day.
4. Various toys for the leets
5. Several people ((NPC, maybe)) to stroll around and make sure the leets are safe.
6. Artificial underground Leet nests, made as realistic and comfortable as possible, with small fireplaces, bedding pads, and pockets to store leet chow when it's cold outside and they don't feel like coming out for food.

When the leets reach full growth, they can be returned back into the wild, far away from where the hunters can get them. While leets breed fast, the hunters (both Omni-Tek and Newbie) have already cut the leet population down by HALF! This reservation should do just the job to build their numbers back again.

Sincerely,
Ms.Maul
Supporter of all things Leet
