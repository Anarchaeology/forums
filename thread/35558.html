To me, "ganking" is only possible within the confines of the arena.
Where Omni goes and attacks another Omni (or clan/clan) unprovoked.

The arena in my mind is meant to be an area where you hone your PvP skills against various profs of different lvl/skill. This is where you politely ask for a duel, and stay out of other people's duels. However, since we are at war... a Omni in the clanner arena (or vica versa) has the right to attack any and all clan members that they can. That is what this game is all about. Cival war between each side. So, while in the arena you must be ever vigilant of attack by a member of the opposite side. You should not have to worry about those with whom you side.

In any other place, be it 2h0, 4h0, MMD, Camelot, or any mayhem zone ganking is not possible (as per my definition). Because these places are designated battle zones. Where anything goes at any time.

Now, some say it is uncouth to attack another same side member, but it is 0%, and anything is possible.
