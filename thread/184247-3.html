Chapter 1~Unfinished Business(Part 1)
Are you sure these coordinates are correct?, Timebombz mumbled into his comm, Id think that these Kenleys could afford better burial grounds for their dead Chairman. A voice mumbles something onto the comm and Timebombz just closes it, ignoring the person. Damn Ill be glad when I get this over with, he said to himself, sighing gruffly, as he slipped the hood of his dark cloak over his head, the darkness covering his face.



He approached a small gate with a booth, looking at a man leaning against the post, yawning from rugged, long hours. Time saw the man and scoffed quietly, not wanting to disturb him, and slowly crept to the nearby fence. He rose his hand only slightly, and the tips of his fingers started to glow a very dark Red, as well as the tattoo on his forehead. He just barely tapped the fence and it melts onto his hand, the hot smelly, notum-coated titanium oddly not burning his hand off. Just with a light push, the fence crackles open, allowing a gap for him to slip into. Sticking to the shadows, he starts moving toward the guard at the booth, sliding his pistols into the large pockets in his cloak. Creeping up onto the security officer, he swiftly grabbed the guards neck from behind and applied just short of enough pressure to break his neck.



Hello there, Time said, chuckling in the shivering guards ear, a loud strike on lightening hitting the ground, making the guard jump. Time twisted the guard around, still holding him above the ground, but now face to face with the man. You help me and Ill help ya back? That sound good?, Time said, snickering. The guard stammered, Y-y-es sir! Whatever you say! Just dont hurt me please! I-I have a wife and kid a-a-and we need the money! This is the only job that-. Shut up! I dont give a leets ass!, he growled out coldly, squeezing the guards neck making him squeal in pain, nearly crushing his Adam's apple. All I want to know is the coordinates for the grounds of Malcom 'Shadowgrider' Warn, he stops his sentence, rubbing his forehead in frustration, Kenley, excuse meMalcom Kenleyhe was just buried here earlier today. The guard appeared to be thinking, but quickly spoke, He is in 184n, 162e of the A4Z Quadrant, I dont know the exact grave number! Im sorry!. Time looked at the guard with much anger, as if not satisfied, but just started loosening his grip as he calmed down.



Suddenly, Time dropped the guard and jabbed the bottom of the his chin semi-gently with his pointer and middle fingers, some mysterious force holding the guard from falling. Please! What did I do?!, the guard yelped out, begging for his life. Who said you did anything?, Time smirked, his eyes glowing orange as he presses his finger tips into the guards chin. About five seconds, later, the guard let out a animalic scream, as purple and orange notum lava started spilling out of the guards mouth as he gargled lava, moaning,, the lava muffling his voice. He turned pale as his eyes started to tear up, only with drops of notum instead of watery tears. The guard reached for his comm, about 5 feet away from him inside the booth, not even coming close to grabbing it. The guards eyes balls rolled into the back of his head as he vomited pure notum all over Times arm. Rest in peace, Time spoke quietly as he dropped the body onto the ground. Time knelt down and pressed his hand onto the chest of the dead guard, mumbling a quiet prayer of some sort, and got out his comm. Clear, He spoke gruffly into it. He pressed a button on his wrist, opening up some small computer of some sort, and he imputed the coordinates into it. Moments later, three other hooded people walking out to the gate the greet Time..........

Unfinished Business(part 2)
Time bowed slightly to the one in the middle as the mysterious person slipped off the hood, revealing a beautiful, black haired woman, with a tattoo over her forehead, similar to Time's. Time stared into the eyes of the cyborg woman, staring at the nano-bots in her pupils open and close. Yes.., Time spoke out calmly, turning around, starting to walk into the dark, cemetery. Clean this mess up before Omni-Pol gets here, he said to the woman. Unfinished Business(Part 3)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~2 hours later~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~



*THUMP*The large casket hit the ground after the two cyborg grunts who were carrying it, backed off into the Shadows, leaving only Time and the cyborg woman looking down at the casket. Two hundred and fourth century and you would have thought we would have more advanced burial grounds than this, Time laughed, looking over at the other cyborg next to him. Open it!, Time grunted, glaring at her as she gave a stern look back at him, then looking down at the casket and sliding the cover off with ease. They both looked down at the body of the man laying in there, white, spiky hair, amazingly a not pale face or stiff body to the touch.



Both of his hands were crossed across his chest, his right hand holding his legendary dagger. Time gloomed down at the dagger as if he had some bad memory of it, and tried to pull it out of the mans hands, an odd grip holding the dagger. With one sudden jerk, the dagger left his hand and Time held it in his hands. Time looked at the dagger and pulled it close to him, licking it gently, the taste of blood overwhelming, almost making Time vomit. At that very moment, Time started hearing screams and cries from men, women and children. Looking around the cemetery, only seeing the cyborg next to him looking at him with a puzzled face, seeing no one around him, as the screams started getting louder.



Time dropped the dagger back onto Shadows body and the screams stopped immediately. Oddhe wasnt holding this during the burial service.., Time said to himself uneasily. Time looked back at the woman next to him as if nothing happened and looked down at Shadows other hand, opening it with caution, revealing a small, locket-like thing in his hand. Time picked it up out of the hand and pressed a button on the side, the locket opening up and expanding, showing various pictures((If you have ever seen Minority Report, its sort of like that)). Time looked at the pictures, seeing a picture of a beautiful blond woman with pigtails, smiling. Hmm.this must be the widow, Time scoffed, looking at the other pictures, all of either the woman with his son, lil Shadow, or of Shadow with Notorious Serter next to him. Grunting quietly, he closed the locket and tossed it over his shoulder, having no value to him.



Can we stop screwing around and get this over with, the woman next to him finally spoke, glaring at Time. Whats the rush missy? Need to go to an engineer to get a tune up?, Time snickered at his own joke, the cyborg glaring at him furiously. NoOmni-Pol will be here shortly.., she spoke, desperately trying to control her temper. Yeah, yeahwhatever!!, Time said, blowing her off. Where is it? Time thought to himself, examining the body closely. He then stuck one of his hands inside of Shadows pocket and pulled out a small card that read Kenley Criminal Syndicate on the face of it, and read Chairman Malcom Shadowgrider Kenley on the back. That was all he could make up, as the rest was in the unknown native language of the Kenleys. He examined the card closely, looking deeply at the letters that he could not read and the words Chairman and Shadowgrider. This must be it, Time said to the woman, as she nodded, smiling for the first time..

A day without Malcom
It was a late October afternoon. Bret "Serter" Kenley went to end the day at Rompas after long and hard work at the Kenley Mansion that day. Upon his arrival, he seen a face he hadn't seen in a few months...."Rizzior". It had been awhile since he was last heard from. Nobody knew where he had gone off to, but now he has returned for reasons unknown. "Serter" knowing of his disappearence, approached "Rizzior" cautiously. He took one look in his eyes, and seen that he was a changed person, but how....he did not know. He continued walking into the back, never exchanging a word with "Rizzior". Another guest was waiting for him there. Guy "Stefano" Kenley.

"So has everyone done their rounds for the day?"

"Seems so. Everyone has covered their area and made reports of the activity. Nothing new so far. Maybe soon we'll get something significant."

"Oh well. No leads as usual. Hopefully something will pull up."

There was a long pause, followed by the breaking of a glass towards the front. Stefano and Serter approached, weapon and fists ready. All they saw was "Rizzior's" chair spinning, and his glass on the ground, but "Rizzior" was nowhere in sight.

"Freaky..." Serter said. "The man disappears for 5 months, comes to Rompas, looks at me, breaks a glass, and leaves....how rude."

"Anyways....." Stefano jumped in, "Its been hard getting along without Malcom here, and i'm sure Joan is taking it harder than us."

"Yea, I feel sorry for her. Barely a week into their marriage and Malcom comes up dead. I've been meaning to stop by his gravesite sometime, but haven't had the chance. But i'm free now, want to come with me?"

"Sure. We've got nothing else to do, and we haven't been there since his funeral. It'd be nice to see how things have turned out there."

"Then lets get going."

Serter and Stefano quickly head off to the cemetery where Malcom "Shadowgrider" Kenley was buried....little did they know what was waiting for them there....
