Warping should be removed entirely from the so called "crowd control" system.

A better fix would be to impede (by slowing down greatly) or prevent (not allow) movement toward high-density areas (defined by player density). If the system is "smart" enough to warp someone to a "low" density area, it is obviously smart enough to identify the high density areas as well, which could be used to prevent/slow areas from becoming overcrowded in the first place.

The current implementation is a classic example of a KLUGE.
