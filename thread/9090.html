Please Fix Nanocasting Mobs!!!!!!
I think there is a bad problem with nanocasting mobs and after testing it I am certain the timing is off. The mobs are basically able to insta-cast any nano. What seems to be happening is that Say Veteran Bothacker wants to cast XXXX on you to debuff you. Veteran Bothacker begins casting XXXX. Immediately you see You are Hit for 1397 Energy Damage in the white nano cast lettering... Now Veteran Bothacker continues to finish casting while you are shooting him. Then when he finishes the casting you see the graphic (i.e. vet BH leans forward with his arms pointing towards you as a colorful graphic gets blasted in your direction). After the graphic you see in your Chat Window "Veteran Bothacker forces your NCU to run XXXX"

Honestly this is hardly noticable early on in the game as the Nukes aren't quite so violent... but when you are talking about mobs that Nuke for 1400+ you are talking about half of my hit points... so for an instacast on these often means a very quick death for me. A vet mob needs only to shoot me 5-6 times and start casting this nano for me to die. Of course the higher NT's can insta cast Izgammer's last word.

This definitely needs to be looked into!!!! Mobs are already overpowered enough... they don't need to be casting there nano's up front. If a nano takes 12ish seconds to cast, I want to see that nano not hit me for at least 9 seconds or so (depending on his Nano Init). Those extra 9 seconds could easily be the difference between life and death. Every Nano Casting mob did this to me last night. And it was very easy to see on the Veteran Traders. They would cast Ransacks and Deprives that lowered my Attack skill by 400 and last for 3 minutes on my NCU. I was able to see my Attack skill lowered and see the graphic effect on them, before the Nano was ever finished casting and before I'd get the chat message that my NCU was forced to run Nano XXXX.
