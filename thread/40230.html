The user interface is somewhat diffrent

Short summary of what I found out:

To change a value withing <min> - <cap> (for your question 1 - 200), just point on the value, hold down the left mouse button, and drag the mouse left and right. That shoudl change the value.

You've probably found the max abilites menu item.

To simulate a buff, you just do the same as above, but hold down the right mousebutton. Text changes to red, and there is nice status text at the bottom stating how much of a buff you've placed.

I also liked the implant section. Combined with nanonanny, you could spend more time with that tool, that you do ingame
