PvP Mechbots!
well dunno if the idea has already been shared/published here, but methinks lotsa players (not only japanese!) would greet an functional expansion that allows for MECHBOTS, i.e. war machines populated/driven by players.

There are already 3D models one could use to represent 1-pilot, 2-pilot and 3-pilot mechbnot vehicles: maybe bigger battlesuit, war robots etc.

One would 'dress' a mechbot by putting its icon in coat slot, and one would share a 'seat' in multi-pilot mechbot maybe by copying mechbot's icon with key duplicator, i.e. allowing a friend to 'jump in'.

Mechbots could have 2-5 different engineer crafted weapons and attenuators, from close combat to guns, to armor shields and nano-pool and program enhancers. Mechbot would have an inherent higher armor rating and could be used only outside missions, not bringing any exp to pilots - so just for PvP battles.

My idea was, in 2-pilot mechbot, one pilot would have a seat that allows driving and close combat, while the other has long range weaponry. In three seat mechbot, third member has fileds, shields and nanos (maybe one would liek to seat a NT or MP as 3rd pilot, therefore).

Sure some programming, but lotsa fun potential.

I think people would program for free under NDA to get it going, I know I would
