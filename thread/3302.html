Roleplaying, AO, ppl's moaning and the Trial
Well there is a lot to consider here. I think everyone needs to take a step back and begin to have some thoughts as to why everything is analysed OOC all the time.

To start with lets begin with the Trial. The Trial was unruly yesI have no doubts about that. Being present was slightly different from reading the logsbut still the Trial was out of control. But lets think about this IC for once

OT arranged the trial on very short notice. So there was little time for preparation. The Judges did not keep a strict control over the Court room. But why? Well perhaps OT didnt really care too much about the issue. This is only confirmed by the farce of a punishment issued. The whole trial was simply arranged to ease the publics attitude. Clans and OT alike were maddened by what happened against Ross wishes. And so Omni-Admin had to be seen to be doing something. Its all about appearancenothing more.

Now secondly. Why has no one yet questioned just why Radiman and Ross are so chummy all of a sudden. I mean just check Philip Ross statement. Lately everything that occurs like this it seems Omni-Tek forces and CoT forces are always mentioned in the same sentence as working together! Why? Hundreds of years of warand suddenly this? Dont make much sense to me

Perhaps the CoT and Radiman have sold out on the Clans? Or perhaps CoT and Radiman have gained some sort of hold over OT and/or Philip Ross?

This isnt all as clear cut as you lot seem to think. You keep analysing this OOC. But this is a RP gameso why not start thinking In Character for once?!

I have lost count of the number of RP games I have been involved in that have been spoilt because people constantly ***** OOC about the story or the GM or other characters. And they do this before the story has even got of its first page! This is what is happening hereand with all this OOC whining you lot are missing out on the most fun part of the story.

YesThe Dark Carnival were out of line in the trial. And yes there were a lot of OOC commentsbut from and in character perspective all this can be viewed as a farce. Clearly the Judges couldnt care what DC did or had to say. They had no interest in issuing a punishment to DC other than a slap on the wrist. And I believe they did this only because the public (us players) demanded it.

Think more about this. Over a week laterand the Clans are still moaning OT are still complainPhilip Ross is even taking a stance. But as far as I can see Radiman couldnt care much less about the whole thing. In my eyes he is up to somethingthere is a conspiracy going on hereand it is one that goes to the highest levels, involving either Radiman or Rossor maybe even both.

When this game begunI saw OT as the Evil Emipreand the Clans as the Good Rebels. Ross headed his evil empire and Radiman led his Rebel Clans. But now it appears it isnt as clear cut as that. We have good and bad characters (players) on both sides i.e. Division 9 = Good, The Dark Carnival = Bad. Red Freedom = Bad, Fist of Chronos = Good. (I think. But all this depends on your In Character beliefs).

More importantly though. I think Philip Ross in indeed good, but someone somewhere has some sort of hold over him. And he is struggling with something we know nothing about. I think to win this struggle he needs a united front with OT and the Clans. Radiman sees that Ross has these problems and thinks this gives him an advantage somehowI dont trust Radiman anymorehe is up to something.

And all these thoughts come from what we have seen in game and on website and movies.

Soinstead of constantly whining OOC start thinking in terms of Rubi-Ka, and what is going on. OT is Omni-Tek NOT FunCom. Omni-Tek do things for reasons that are occurring on Rubi-KaNOT because of some players playing a game. Get in characterlook at what is going on. For crying out loud this is a role-playing Game so ROLEPLAY dont whinge!!

We are all in the middle of something greatbut with all the whining most of you are missing it. Role-play, act in-characterbut most importantly think
