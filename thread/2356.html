Different game play rules for different players
In pvp and pvm there is different playing rules for weapon users and nanousers. Weaponusers are only checked for their ability to pick up and hold a weapon, not for actually using it unlike nano users who are checked every time they use a nano. Result: weaponusers can use far to high weapons and any debuff has no effect on their combat abilities while nanousers must go for the nanos they have skill levels for at the moment and must use much lower nanos if hit by any debuffs. And yes debuffs stack so being hit by two means a major drop in usable nanos. Imho weaponusers should also be checked for skill level every time they tried to pull the trigger, swing the sword, bang that sledge or whatever. That would be fair.

Another point, why do dmg nanos do much less than minimum dmg on orange-red mobs, but never higher than maximum dmg on grey mobs ? A weaponuser get bonus for high skill lvl so that she/he/it do more than described dmg (up to 2,5 times i think) but a high skill lvl nanocaster get no such 'bonus' to dmg. Is this fair ? And don't say high end nanos do much more dmg than any weapon, have you ever seen the dmg from a agent's aimed shoot, or full auto/burst dmg from high end players ? 5-15k dmg they do, more than the 4-9k dmg coming from the highest NF - and while a weapon user get the usual ordinary dmg from 'ordinary' shoots while waiting for the special shoots becoming available a nanocaster is locked up during recycling time. Iz' last word has a recycle of 30 secs. And it can fumble, be resisted or give less than min dmg, but never higher.
