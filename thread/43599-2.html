Shion,

People in this thread really have gone the extra mile to be helpful to you. I think your frustration with the game mechanics is coloring your view of how helpful the answers are.

When someone in AO tells you, at level 40, to have armor 10 levels above you, and imps 15 levels above you, that is a perfectly valid answer. That's how AO works.

To be honest, I am new enough to AO that I understand exactly where you're coming from. AO is a game of extremes, it's really not balanced for combat at all. Logic tells you that you should wear armor of your QL and fight yellow mobs. But that's not how it works - you wear armor you can't put on alone, and fight multiple reds.

It's not a fighting game, because the fighting really is trivial. It's a tinkerer's game, where you test out your avatar by fighting.

TBH, if you don't like to tweak, and upgrade, and polish, and refine, you will not like AO. That's perfectly acceptable not to like it, it's a niche game, that appeals strongly to a certain type of player.

In EQ, it takes multiple teams of lvl 60 people to beat a single lvl 60 human NPC. EQ toons get weaker, relatively, as they level. Does that make any more sense? No.
