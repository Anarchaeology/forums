The thing is...every time FC says something, and it takes time to actually deliver what they promised..people scream for more information. If they say "we're still working on it" people scream more and call them liars. If they don't say anything you get the usual "you don't like us anymore" posts.

You know Advs are up for a fix after MAs, so are agents. 13.8 is part of the MA fix (they aren't done yet) so you should see some adv changes in 14.0 or 14.2. They can work on several professions at once (13.8 is part Fixer/MA/everyone) so even if they say 14.0 is an agent patch it doesn't mean it's 100% agent changes.

One way to get your answers:

First: Search this board. Most of the questions asked have been answered before, a quick search could be all you need.

1. Write a post listing your questions/suggestions etc.. Avoid whining or flaming as it's completely uninteresting to read and extremely stupid if you really want a reply. Calling for nerfs is also a bad idea, you never know what you might get.

2. Send them an email with a copy/paste of your post. Don't use threats or foul language as this will get your mail moved to the big archive called "Deleted items".

3. Join #anarchyonline and ask Cz or Cosmik if they could read your post. Again, threats, cursing etc. will not work. If it's a short yes/no question just ask them directly, no need for a post.

These are just suggestions...I'm not saying you'll get the answers you need but it's worth a shot. If you've done all of the above then you can complain a bit
