Turn OFF Video Shadowing in the BIOS!!
There is a note in the nVidia FAQ on their web site that suggests you turn OFF Video Shadowing in the PC BIOS.

I did this, not because of crashes, but to improve my framerate. It worked. I gained 10fps by making that simple change to my PC configuration.

I'm running my GForce2 on the nVidia Detonator XP (23.11) drivers now. Funcom have approved them for use with AO (check the AO graphics FAQ).

BTW, if you press Ctrl+Alt+f while playing AO, your current fps will be displayed in the upper left corner of the screen.

Hope this helps ...
