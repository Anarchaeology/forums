I'd say keep it unless you really need the credits.

I have a dirty secret, I wore GA I until I was level 136. The only reason I stopped wearing GA I was because I found GA IV. I figured out that I was actually taking less damage against mobs in teams by sticking with GA I then wearing ql 190ish flowers or omni-pol. ((edit: mind you, this is when nobody bothered to do team missions over 190 since fighting RM/aces wasn't considered worth the effort.))

Of course, GA I isn't optimal. Its not solo armor, and it will get you killed fighting RM/aces. You REALLY need to max your evades in GA I.

If you really play casually and infrequently (like you say you do) you will probably get alot of milage out of GA1. Just be aware of the fact that it's not optimal by any means, and wearing it isns't a ticket to solo things way over your head and you'll be fine. Keep in mind it won't last you 'forever' like the higher marks of Grid armor can.

If you plan to level quickly, like soloing things way over your level or demand having the very 'best', you might be better off selling it to a twink.
