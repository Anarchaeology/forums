First, there are no stupid questions. just stupid answers.

Second, there are two known ways of getting a yalm'

1: finding one in a chest inside a mission
2: paying around 5 mill in a "special" shop (you know, those with just four shop-machines in them)


Yalms found in missionchests are completely random (someone might tell you different. They might even be right about it, but I believe it is random)

As a referance, I found my yalm in a mission around lvl 50-60 (think it was about that time. not TOO sure though)
Some has found them earlier, others later.

If you're omni, there's a thing called yalmbot. /tell yalmbot help for more info. Have not tried it, but it supposedly work something like this:

One guy allready having a yalm finds one, let yalmbot know, and the next lucky guy on yalmbot's list get a message. The two players then haggle a price to allow the yalmless one loot the chest.

Getting cash with missions can be done, and you'll get some lvls along with that, and a chance of finding a yalm while at it. just remeber to check ALL chests in your mission. I was almost on my way out, when i decide to "clean up" som more. Three chests after that, I was speechless, and unable to breathe for at least a minute. It DOES change the world for you.
