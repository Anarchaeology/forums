Dreams of Expansion
The Expansion Disk, set date - Christmas 2002

FC's Official word (taken from the community board)

"Official word
Just to make absolutely sure: yes, the article is genuine, not a hoax. Some people still ask me about that . Also, we were told there would be an official statement on Monday regarding the EP. So please don't e-mail us to death with questions during the weekend. We'll get you the info.
Playing and paying
Just clarifying this, since we do get questions about it: You will still be able to play AO without the EP. And you will not have to pay two monthly fees (or a double monthly fee) to play with the EP.
Patches and content
We will not stop working on improving AO due to making the EP. We're working on content, fixes and other stuff for the existing version, and will continue to do so.
That's what I had for now. We'll be back with more later. - CZ"


OKay, i just got off from talking to my guild about this, some think its good others think its bad.

I'll just tell you this in chronological order.
Half a year ago, just around after patch 12.6 was THE biggest exodus of players in this games short lifespan. The patch screwed up things so bad that people noticed how the cities emptied by the day.
It was then that people like me said we needed an expansion disk, simply to fix the game since there is so much data needed.

Also, the player exodus put FC under big financial strain, if u dont believe me, i dont need financial figures, it was Christmas time 2001 when FC had to let go of 30 of their staff and halt production entirely on Midgard to concentrate on AO. I said they probably would do an expansion pack because they needed to fleese us paying customers (who already pay the highest amount for any mmorpg out there) for more money.

Since then, the game has got better, and with a lot of hard work and dedication, they got the game running very well, and added lots of stuff to make it a lot more playable and increase its longevity...
Its an enjoyable expereince now....

But then you try tradeskills, and you cry. You wasted HOW much money and xp to find only 15% at best of weapon tradeskills work?
You run around and find places like Jobe arent there, and Kapar, and others.
You notce that your APARTMENT, is pretty much a waste of time, with only 10 items allowed in it, and none are decent items, just flowers, pictures, and fridges....


FC lied to me when i bought the game. My apartment didnt work.
FC kept on making patches that introduced tradeskills that dont work, they keep adding items that have not much relavence (like the coffee tradeskill items for bearaucrats and the "clothing and armour making" machines....)
Why should developers spend time on righting the Wompah system, when i cant even make tharg wings in my weapon smithing??
Why should they be spending their expensive time adding vending machines that dont have any point (ie the clothing and armour one)?
When they could be making the 60-80% of broken tradeskills *work*, when they could be working on bugs their new patch has just introduced (i get stuck while running in Last Ditch, i never used to)

FC has lied to me and taken my money before, they made me play a beta game for months while taking my money. And IF, ...and only IF.....this expansion pack added Jobe, Kapar, all other playfields that are missiing, ALL tradeskills *working* properly, fully functional apartments with all the apartment blocks working, with key systems and couches and no item limit, IF they fix half the stuff that is still broken (like primers, and all the other bits and bobs u find) IF they fix the pvp system, IF they can sort something out about twinking (or try) and IF they can add all the other stuff they promised (like all the rest ofthe guild commands, like banks, houses and a million and one other things)

*THEN*

and *ONLY* then, will i pay my money for it and consider staying, coz frankly..... FC has given up with the storyline, Kirrana, a great RP player who gave such an interest and devoted so much of her time to this game and the RP community is leaving which has annoyed me!
No major events ever happen, no GMs actively continue the storyline and there's never anything to involve the games populace in the game other than a levelling race.....


Also, on a continuation of my estimation of FC's ability to go thru with its promises, i ask every person reading this to note down the part of the official post that states

"You will still be able to play AO without the EP. And you will not have to pay two monthly fees (or a double monthly fee) to play with the EP. "

If you believe that 1-2 patches after the expansion that you dont need the disk then i'd be highly shocked.
You will need the disk at some point, soon after, i guarantee it.

There's two things to this, that goes back to the 12.6 patch, they want your money, they need your money.
Notice the timescale of this patch, christmas, by then, i'll be playing SWG (star wars galaxies), and i dont care who says what, a good deal (maybe 1/4 to 1/3) of the game population of AO will drop out. subscriptions cancelled. Now, there'll be a few who'll post "great more bandwidth for me!" but that will also mean FC will need money, the reason this pack is coming out when it is.
You will have to buy it, coz with 1/4 to a 1/3rd of the games population gone, FC will be back to a more narrow time hitting the break even margin in their finances, and because of this, they'll *make* you have to buy it by putting something in it, to try and guarantee at least 80% of the players left, all buy this one disk...

This is my chance to show my disatisfaction with how ive been treated over the last year,....with my money, or lack of.

Just dont believe all the hype they'll give u about this expansion disk. You believed them when they released the game, and if you were here for the first 5 months, you'll know what i mean!!!
