Website: www.kimone.net

History:
Our name, Devils of Kimone, is from a bad ass Japanese animation called Ninja Scroll. In this animation, there is a group of evil ninja's called the Eight Devils of Kimon.

DoK was created one night while our original founder, Saciro, got drunk with a few roommates. While in this drunken stupor, their spelling was a bit off, and the "e" was added to the name. Thus, the Devils of Kimone were born. The names of the founding members were based on the names of the Eight Devils of Kimon (and of course each was misspelled).

The guild started in Ultima Online on the Napa Valley shard. DoK dominated the Britain East roads for the first two months of UO's existence (when you didn't lose stats if you died as a "Dread Lord", aka "Murderer" in modern UO terms).

The day UO took a turn for the worse (aka no more Dread Lords) was the day the original Devils of Kimone broke up. They were die hard PKs. So for most of them "Stat Loss" meant, goodbye UO. Sacrio was DoK's last remaining member.

Since his buddies were gone, Saciro decided to call it quits in UO, aswell. For his final act in UO, he decided to go "Stat Loss Red", killing anyone he saw with his random partner Garland Green at the Britain Crossroads. They came across a small war guild by the name of CoW (Circle of War or Cow's of War) originally lead by SpaceCow, Da Smurf and MinDsEyE. MinDsEyE and Da Smurf instantly jumped Garland Green - killing him quickly. Saciro sat there for a second then said, "Damn, nice kill." MinDsEyE, Da Smurf and Saciro became allies that day. (Ironically, Saciro had a bow which would have most likely killed both Da Smurf and MinDsEyE in one shot.)

After the Crossroads incident, the small group (Saciro, MinDsEyE, SpaceCow and Da Smurf) fought at each others sides for 2 weeks. Saciro told MinDs, Smurf and Cow stories of his small guild of real life friends, the Devils of Kimone. After some discussion, they agreed it was time for a change.

A change that will forever live in infamy. Legends were created! Hero's were slaughtered! Cities burned! Most importantly, Devil's of Kimone was reborn! Massive battles took place that changed the face of a once dormant shard.

When AC came out, DoK soon followed. In AC, Dark Kimone became the Monarch - making DoK a well-respected name on the Darktide server. DoK fought on all sides of the fence (above all else emphasizing fair play  well, fair play can be twisted). During our reign on Darktide, several members of core served as GM - among them Dark Kimone, Kpkimone, and Talbot (aka Grath/Xezious).

DoK then moved into the world of DAoC. The current leaders are Talbot (aka Grath/Xezious) (GM) and Catyana (co-GM).


DoK Today:
In the face of modern gaming, DoK has adapted to meet the needs of its player base. We are expanding into new games (mostly mmorpgs..but we do support some first person shooters). We are a guild of about 100 players who love pking and pvp. "One" game cannot support the needs/interests of all of our players. Our members love DoK and we love our members. Therefore, our guild is adapting.

We are moving from a one game guild...to a multi-game guild. Many experienced mmorpg players (with high level characters in several games) want to be able to play more that one game with the same friends and guild members he has come to trust and rely on. Our guild is shifting its focus to support our players who feel this way...while at the same time maintaining solid support for the one-game players and website support for each game.

While we have a great website with good information, we are completely redesigning the website and adding alot of content and interactive features. We are also adding a pk flavor to the website. We are planning to support pk activism (standing up for the rights of pkers in a gaming world full of carebears and companies that need to add well-developed support for pking/pvp into their programing).

Devils of Kimone continues to this day as a medium for one of the strongest binds of friendships in (and out) of online gaming.

DoK in AO:
We have several guild members with high level characters coming back to AO after a long absence and quite a few members coming to AO for the first time. Our experienced AOers starting fresh characters and recruiting new players to establish solid groups that work well together and develop good pk characters. We can be found with our newbie characters in the Arena testing our skills just about every night.

Membership Information:
DoK has a very simple ranking structure that will be translated into AO. We have 3 basic levels: Core (leadership council of long-term dedicated members who have vowed never to join another guild), Members, and Maggots (trial phase).

Members have come and gone throughout DoK's history but those that stay and continue the legacy are known as "Core". These are people who have been with DoK through all the hard knocks as well as the good times. They have dedicated themselves to the guild. The only way to become core is for the current core to see this in you as a player and as a person. Core must unanimously vote you into this status. Core is who votes on all matters concerning the guild and will seek your input as well.

Everyone else is either a maggot or a normal member. A maggot is a new member on trial basis, this is our time to show our worth and your time to show yours. As a maggot you will be hazed and will take a little abuse. This is to see if you break under adversity or overcome.

Everything is based on your participation. The more you participate the more you better yourself as well as the guild. Your future is in your hands not ours. We already work as a team and it is up to you to become a part of that team.

If you would like to become a member of DoK, message the GM - Package (aka Kulata/Kekko/Ravingkimone) in game...or post on the DoK AO Public boards at this link: http://www.kimone.net/phpBB/viewforum.php?forum=29&16

Following in the DoK tradition... have fun and dominate!
