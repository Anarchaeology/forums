Sometimes there are only small differences, but I could see them. always changing. Making changes. Perfecting, but not. Making something out of something else. Is it perfect? Research is never harmless. I wanted to make them fly and swim. But it all came to nothing. Nothing is always the smallest denominator.

Tell me about yourself

Indeed Oh, me? I am...
Indeed I am. Almost completely . The remains of Elian

Oh, I am not quite the person I could be. Or was. Indeed I am not. I have died. Many times. Yes, indeed. Every time I an less me. How to know, you say? I can feel it. The soul is going away. Birds with wings. You think you can DIE and not SUFFER THE CONSEQUENCES?

The information they gather is important, indeed. Very important, indeed. Very important, They need it to control the people. they store you, you know. When you resurrect. When you come back from the dead. Does tat make me a zombie? Do they have bottles. Very strange. Very strange indeed.

They keep sending people to take me back, but I wont go! They're so righteous, Indeed they are. Omni-Tek. Self-made gods with only the power to destroy. children maiming insects. what information do they gather?

You are like a quilt to them. They can look at it an tell it's you. I know. They keep murdering me, but I always come back. I am not myself anymore. Indeed I am not. We all have one. a pattern. Like a quilt. It's all you are and all you will be, rolled into one. Like a time machine stuck in time. Indeed it is! How appropriate. Its one of the three.
