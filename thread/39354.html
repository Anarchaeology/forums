/Ignore Is A Role Players Best Friend
I have discovered as of late that there are players coming to socializing areas (such at Reets or Rompas) specifically to annoy the other patrons of the club. By annoy I mean several things. Spammy Vicinity talk that isn't role playing. Standing in or on players who are sitting. Spammy /shouts that isn't role playing. Running around someone sitting over and over again. I had someone start making rude comments because I was sitting with another female, calling me a **** and bashing on lesbians. Heck, I have even had someone come sit on the couch beside me, and start posing undressing me while I was sitting having a conversation with Xtrophic. Now /that/ was major annoying.

At first I just tolerated these annoying players, chalked it up to ten year olds playing their older siblings game, but one can only take it for so long. That is when I finally started using my /ignore command. No, it doesn't make the player's character disappear (though I really wish it would), but it does stop the spammy little annoyances while you are trying to role play or hold a civial conversation.

So I would strongly recommend the /IGNORE command to all role players, remember you have it and don't be afraid to use it when you are randomally assaulted by ten year olds.
