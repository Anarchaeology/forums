Fixercon I
Fixercon I

Saturday, July 13, Start time: 19GMT
Time zone translation

Chatbot: A chatbot will be set up for the event, to be used for announcements. Fixers should send a tell to Fixercon to be added.
Gathering spot: Borealis. Look for Taboo near the insurance/bank that's on the way to the Wrecked Shop. [Taboo, being one of those slimy agents, has decided to pay his penance by standing out there and grouping people for warps for the whole event.]
The con itself will take place inside a cleared mission in Borealis. Engineers will be on hand inside the building to warp only fixers inside, and also to warp PvP participants back from the arena (or reclaim).
Level limit: You must be 25 to attend. Exceptions made by contacting Trellame or Nettie in advance, and will mainly be granted to those joining us from RK2.

Events:
1. Contests!
There will be contests. There will be prizes. One will be a Playa of the Year contest, where you can prove just how pimped out you really are (Hosted by the always huggable Lilnymph). Also, the winner of the AO-Fixer.com Grid Armor contest will be announced.
There will also be many random contests with phat l00t prizes!

2. Tradeskills
We'll let a couple of those geeky engineers slip in and set up an area for people to have tradeskills done, free of charge.
3. PvP tourney (Off-site)
Prize currently 50 million credits to the winning paired team.
Click here for details. Arrangements will be made for easy spectator access to the finals. Hosted by Hydeo.

4. Swap meet
Trade yer junk.

5. Grill the experts
Expert fixers on hand to offer advice on everything from role playing to weapons.

6. PARTY!
With Rubi-Ka's most popular, live DJ, Tarryk!
This event will be on RK1, but DEFINATELY open to RK2 fixers who want to come. Contact Trellame or Nettie if you'd like a little help getting set up on RK1.

Schedule:
18 GMT - Hashbang begins work on his masterpieces. Staff arrives for planning.
18:30 GMT - Begin warping
19 GMT - Start, Tarryk goes on
19:30 GMT - End PvP registration
20 GMT - PvP start
20-22 GMT - Experts available
22:15 GMT - Playa of the Year voting
22:45 GMT - PvP tourney award, AO-Fixer Grid Armor contest winner
23 GMT - End

19-21 GMT - Tradeskills/swap meet open (swap meet will be held in a separate room to avoid extreme spamage)
19-22 GMT - Random contests
19-23 GMT - DJ Tarryk spins tunes [yes, he takes requests]

Tradeskills available: Implant assembly, implant stripping, weapon assembly, nano creation. NOTE: Please bring the disposable components for the tradeskills you would like performed. Tools will be on hand. We don't want to overwhelm the warpers with people running in and out looking for Space Symbol Libraries.

Contacts for info: Trellame, Nettie, Poizun (Raline), Hydeo [PvP], or check the Fixer forum.
