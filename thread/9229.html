Diffentiating the High End Game, from the Low End Game
The thing that will keep a player going from level 1 to level 200 is there has to be something in the end game that justifies the time commitment it takes to get there. Right now, the high end game in AO is virtually identical to the game play at level 15 or level 50 or level 125. At level 50, a Martial Artist for instance will have the same armor he/she is going to use til lvl 200, and all they have to look forward to is increasing the QL of it. Sure its nice to have the extra AC, but what really seperates the lvl 180 player from the lvl 80 player besides higher QL items and higher skills?

There needs to be something in AO that makes the effort to reach the end game worthwhile, and there definitely HAS to be something thats gonna keep the level 200 players playing.

The carrot on the end of the stick is what AO lacks. Where is the epic battles, and the epic weapons and armor that are a reward of reaching the upper echelon? Where are the "God" mobs? Where are the fights so tough that everyone will level like crazy to be able to beat them, becase they have loot so incredible they make the work it takes to get there worth it? Where are the fights which require a guild sized force to do? Sure you have added some tough "Boss" type mobs, but their loot is hardly worth the effort it takes to kill them. I have yet to see an item in AO that makes me say "Whoa, I have to get that."

When EQ introduced the Epic Weapons for classes other than Paladins, I knew my ranger was going to do whatever it took to get it. I was one of the first 5 rangers on my server to finish the quest, and the swords were so worth the effort it took to get them that I had no interest in other items for such a long time, and then I had to do tougher fights to improve them. A damage dealing class with one sword that provides 40% haste and the other has a slow proc that slows the mob 50% with a 500 pt DoT. Not to mention it had incredible stat boosts and amazing damage. Those items are worth the effort. What items in AO could compare to that?

There needs to be something that drastically sets the high end game apart from the low end game. You have to make the high end game worth the effort it takes to get there. There has to be items so good that people will level like crazy to get them.

You cant make the game so tedious and hard, just to keep people from leveling too fast, just to keep them playing, you have to give them a reason to keep playing once you hit the top. Right now, people quit playing rather than keep on leveling til the end. Im sure you dont want that. Im sure you would rather have them continue playing for years to come. You gotta make it worth while to stick around. Conituous leveling to increase the same items you've been using for 125 levels is rather boring. Continuous leveling to be strong enough to get that uber set of armor or weapon makes it all worth while when you finally get it.

I know a lot of people dont like the high end game aspects of EQ, and yes I think EQ's high end game is what seperates it from the other games and what keeps them going strong. But I know I dont feel the need to reach the high level game, cause there is nothing really compelling about the high level game. EQ has some great high level content and not just raiding content. I've seen unguilded people finish their epic quests, so you dont have to be a part of an elite force to do a lot of the high end game.

Right now AO is basically a big, single player game up to level 100, and then most players have to group just to keep going. But they still play it like a single player game.

One thing the high end game should provide is the epitome of gameplay. There should be challenges so tough that it requires massive teamwork to finish. In EQ for instance, it takes a lot of skill and coordination to fight a mob that requires a cleric healing rotation and MA rotation based on discipline timing and transferring agro from MA to MA. (here MA means Main Assist)

Im rambling now, but this is, in my opinion, what AO really lacks. No I dont just mean raiding mobs, cause if thats what you think then I didnt explain myself well enough, what I mean is something, anything, to make the high end game worth getting there. For me it would be epic battles and epic rewards.

What are some other ideas for differentiating the high end game and making it worth getting there?
