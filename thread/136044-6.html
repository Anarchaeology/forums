The above is a VERY far cry from stating nothing is broken OR that they will do nothing about broken things. Indeed, that response clearly indicates only that they do not see a need to make MPs a viable (read: powerful) solo class.

It also reads like a reply from someone who is deliberately not providing details (I analyze writing for a living, etc.). I'd say you aren't likely to get the level of detail you want from this person. I'd also say, generally speaking, if you email like you post here, you probably don't get detail at all.

But, to give you another viewpoint:

1) The mezz pet doesn't work as I had hoped it would (i.e., mesmerization/long-term non-aggro status), but this may just as well be my expectation being incorrect as anything. After all, it isn't actually referred to as anything more than a 'distraction' or 'clouder' or 'mindreaver', etc.

It has occurred to me that the distraction pet can take a bit of a beating on its own... which tends to support the notion that is it *suppose* to be taking that beating, which in turn supports the notion that my initial concept (coming from other games where any manner of crowd control = mesmerization) is simply incorrect.

Given this, I choose to explore using the pet as a distraction rather than as crowd control (i.e., healing pet on the distraction) when I'm solo and guess what? Works like a charm.

I'm not even going to call it a 'mezz pet' because it so obviously isn't one, and I now believe it was never intended TO be one, not in the sense one typically thinks of it (see above).

2) The 'phantom damage' bug I have seen and reported. This time, because it seemed to me the damage happening was occuring despite the mob being stunned beside me. Obviously, a monster wouldn't get seven free retaliatory acts upon waking had they slept for seven seconds, so this is obviously a bug.

3) Yup, it sure is hard to find a decent weapon. I get the distinct impression that MPs aren't intended to wield much more than their creation weapons. This tends to jive with the lore concept of their being priests, and is in line with priest classes from other games for the most part. So I put that slider on max defense, make sure I stay behind my melee (be they pets or other players) and watch my nukes (i.e., knowing the line for overnuking or timing to fall behind known aggro-creating classes, etc.).

As much as I'd like to think a classic priest class could stand alone, they really never have (outside the terribly overbalanced shadowbane healer/channeler, and they eventually nerfed that back into line).

4) Yup, pathing can be a royal pain at times. So I have to take the time to make sure I spam behind when I know there's potential for trouble. I figure doing that is going to gain immediate results, crying for a complete re-visiting of path coding for three of the fourteen classes (when two aren't complaining) likely will not.

5) Yup, we are hard pressed for NCU..... WHEN WE ARE CASTING AHEAD OF LEVEL. When we're not, there's no issue at all. I cast ahead of level as a matter of course. But I'm smart enough to realize the gift of being able to do so and I'll cope with juggling NCU to do it or I'll stop. Choice is completely mine. Trust me when I tell you that anywhere else, that would have been hit not with the nerf bat, but with the nerf FOREST.

6) Yup, our nanos cost buckets to cast. But I really can't complain about this when I can get fully buffed, cast all three pets, and buff them all and still have a sliver of nano showing. Downtime is part of any game when you're a 'casting' class. Comes with the territory. Only place I've ever seen it any different was DAoC and honestly, the stims and packs bring us real close to that level of recovering nano.

In the end, from reading all of this discussion (and re-discussion and then, discussion of the discussion), it just sounds like FunCom's idea of what we are and some people idea of what we are or should be are different. The way I see it, my options are four:

1) Play the way they want.
2) Play the way I want.
3) Play something else.
4) Try to convince them that #2 is #1.

Seems to me like a few folks have been pushing for #4. Problem is -- you don't convince with a 2x4. I got here and all I see are a bunch of plank-slingers. The closest to neutral I see are the wishlists. But honestly, the rest of the forums tend to detract from that in the worst way.

One new players opinion. Take it for what it is worth, don't take it at all, your choice. Regardless, play the game, don't let the game play you.
