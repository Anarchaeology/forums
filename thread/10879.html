AO is not suited for PvP, at the moment.
Not a whine, but rather a request to the devs.

I'm going to Belial to have some fun with outdoor hunting, and get ganked at 2Ho grid exit while still in grace period... as usual.

I make it alive, and my friends and I regroup, buff through the roof (Enforcer 139, MA 140 and myself trader 122) and go for the fight.

Good opportunity to have fun, since we are all old UO players (since 1997), and aren't afraid to fight.

We rush to the grid terminal, and by the time the textures are loaded (1 second, maybe 2 ?), our enforcer, leading the party, is nearly dead.
He's got 7.000 HPs by the way

No time to see who's who, we are there for the fight, so I chain cast team heals (average 1000 since I could buff on an NPC just before rushing) and save his butt.

We kill the big hammer guy we are targetting, must be 165 or higher.

From begin to end of fight, our enforcer is at 10% health, but I manage to save him (he has top armor and evades for his level).

Of course, I'm quickly dispatched (I have close to 5.000 HPs being Solitus, properly implanted and Gargantula essence).
I get hit for 1680 a hop 2 times from a soldier (with 600 evades), nuked, rooted and whatnot, heal like mad, and don't even have time to run of nano before I die.

Then the enforcer dies, and my wife, the MA, dies too, she was mostly healing, since MA's suck like nobody else at damage dealing.

Duration of the fight: 15 seconds.

Did we go back ?

No.

It all boils down to whom has the first strike and who's got the biggest party.

It's too fast, there's no room for tactics or anything, I cannot find again the thrill of PvP we had in UO, and I miss it.

My point:

1° Get rid of grid camping once and for all: all grid terminals and whompas must be in 100% zones.

2° Make evades reduce damage, not avoid it. Evades are broken anyways, my wife is MA, maxed evades, 200 implants and +120 buff and doesn't evade anything her level.

3° Fix the nano resist bug: you don't gain nano resist from attributes, that's a bug. It's already a pain we have to raise nano resist and duck for PvP only, which is unfair to those who want to PvP.

4° Makes different gas areas in the same zone: borders 100% (100 meters), center 25%

5° Disable zoning when engaged in a fight. If you enter a PvP zone, be prepared for it. If you don't get hit for 1 minute, you can zone again.

6° Remove guards where players would like to PvP.

PvP could be ok for most clases in this game, but it's not correctly implemented ATM, IMHO.

Peace.

You guys are arguing about apples and oranges.

Dot damage is caped in that no one tick will do more than 40% of a players total hp damage, and each tick is reduced by 50%. Since say a 450 dot is reduced to 225/tick, if you had say less than 562 total hp when full the dot would be capped.

What garzu is talking about is that the total damage of the dot isn't capped. If say that 450 dot had 10 ticks so that it did a total of 2,250 when it terminated and was placed on a player with 3000 total HP Garzu is suggesting that each tick of the dot past 1200 should do no more damage, because the total damage of the dot over time has hit 40% of the players total HPs.

I hope that's clear to all of you. His statement is true the total damage of a dot over time is not capped at 40%, each tick of the dot is capped at 40% which of course it will never do, and is reduced by 50%.

This is why my 50 MA is so totally uber. BWT dot on top of high brawls and constant high average damage. It beats crits hands down in that level range, higher this isn't true, but of course I'll never level that character.
