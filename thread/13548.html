Unique Professions.
I think the best improvement Funcom could make would be to give every profession a unique role to play. This means - making sure every profession has an ability unique to them. There's been too much nerfing in the form of spreading unique abilities around to other character classes.

I would also like to see unique clothing/armor for every character class. They could have the same stats just look visually different. I think you have to be careful about changing the stats.

I would love to hear someone say, "Well we need to get to Mort quick. Let's find a Fixer."

Now this is not to say that solo missions couldn't be done, but just that a team mission would be much easier if we had so-and-so profession.
