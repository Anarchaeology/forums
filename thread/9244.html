My 2cents
Ok, Well, let me say this, I have laptop 1gighz with 256 megs ram and a 16 meg video card integrated in it, I use a 56k modem and dial in. Even on RK1 the only time I hit lag is when I grid into trade or whompa from ent to trade and only if I have my view distances maxed out. That problem is the "video lag" which is the time it takes for the system to draw the pretty little pictures. It had nothing to do with the connection to the net. The netcode isn't as bad as you might think, its more the display code, and as far as saying that AO doesnt release the memory is incorrect. With both windows 2000 and windows 98 and even xp for that matter, the program releases the memory but it doesnt instantly release the memory. I would say that it takes at least two minutes from the time the visual program closes to the time the clientr.exe actually releases all the used memory. Also if you want to get in right away and are on XP, or 2000 you can go into your task manager, and under the processes tab, sort the colums by memory used, you will see that clientr.exe is using most of your memory. Either wait the few minutes for it to release, or end task on the process. The system appears to hang while the memory is being forced to release, but the system responds within 30 seconds
