To Rpers New and Old: A Renewed Call to Tolerance
((In almost every RP community I have been in, from table top to virtual, there are small segments of folk that choose to behave in loose terms as 'rp elite' and 'drama queens'. The two are not mutually exclusive.

The purpose of my post is not to construct a list of people who would fit into those categories :P, rather to promote a renewed call to tolerance.

If you have a preferred style of RP I would suggest promoting it in actual practice rather than theory, then proceeding to kick everyone in the virtual shins that doesn't fall into your paradigm of genius.

Many have said there is no one way to RP and I heartily agree. My first choice of action is always to hopefully promote some manner of goodwill and tolerance. If that is met with hostility and misconstrued as weakness, I have no problem delivering a few kicks myself.

As I say though, I would much rather see many diverse styles blending and complimenting rather than tearing down others to build up someone's superior arm chair theory.

In closing, create AND tolerate...))

Quite simply my point is ,demonstrate your style, in stories and in game. It need not be defined by listing why others aren't as intelligent as you for making different creative choices. I have my preferences, and tastes, I don't feel the need to list them though and then tell all of you why you "can't rp" because you don't agree.

Now what I meant by drama queen is a person who over reacts to virtually everything in a childish and defensive manner. I have seen message boards where twenty some posts consisted of someone who blew up because another corrected their spelling.Its being petty and devisive.

Now I am not saying that being rude and inconsiderate in game should be tolerated. I think beyond that is the fact and reality that some folks will never mesh. In those cases it is best to walk away from that situation, and possibly leave that person be. In extreme cases I would notify an ARK etc. I have had all sort of lewd comments leveled at me in the past when I was playing a Teir'dal rogue in EQ. I chose to stay in RP mode, and deal directly with the offender. Is that correct? No. Another would have been perfectly right to summon a game guide and petition etc.

The bottomline is though, by RPing in a MASSIVE rp game we are much more likely to run into problems than say when we Rp in NWN with friends. It certainly has its draw backs, and also its advantages.

In closing I still assert that the more accommodating, mature and tolerant we are, the more benefit to be had for the RP community as a whole.}}
