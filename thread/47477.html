Needed Organization commands EDITED.
EDITED TO ADD SOME VERY GOOD POINTS...
added -MOTD, Applicant rights, AFK messages. and Whiteboog's suggestion for org wages...

I am General Stallk, Head of Personnel for the Rubi-ka 2 Detachment Angels of Retribution. As Head of Personnel, I have to keep track of all the members that enter and leave the guild. This can get very difficult at times because of not knowing if your members are online, when they have last logged on to track their activity, seeing if there is a rogue/disgruntled member "kicking" members out just for sh**s and giggles, etc. I have played other online games and programmed for a MUD (text game). These games had the ability to do certain commands that greatly enhanced the capability of organizational leaders and I think that these small tools would help greatly in AO.
Some of the commands I suggest:

1. Online Member Listing for organizations (/org online)
A listing of the organization's members that are currently online. This would stop people from yelling on the org channel, "Yo, yell if your online.", /tell'ing them to see if they are on, or overloading your buddylist. A pop-up window, like the item identification window only larger, could display the info. Displaying in the chat window would not be good if there is a large org, with alot of players online.

2. Organization Log (/org log)
A server bond log that keeps track of certain events that happens to the org. The log could include these items:
A. Member Recruitment (Name of Recruitee, Name of Recruiter, date/time of recruitment)
B. Member Exit (Name of member that left organization, date/time)
C. Member Removal (Name of member "kicked", Name of who removed him/her, date/time)
D. Org bank withdraw/deposit (Name of person doing the transaction, how much, date/time)
E. Member Promotion/demotion (Name of person affected, old and new rank, name of promoter, date/time)

3. Member Information (/org member <membername>)
A way to look at a players information without them being online or in your presence. The one main characteristic is a field that tells when the person last was logged on. This would help find out the activity of members and find those who have haven't logged on over long periods or have just abandoned their character. The info would include:
A. Name
B. Level
C. Rank
D. Profession
E. Gender
F. Breed
G. Last time logged on.

4. (/org MOTD)
"Message Of The Day"... A very useful tool, and currently the only way to do MOTDs is to write a /tell script to every member, which takes a long time due to the size allowance on scripts of 4k bytes and having to break them down to smaller files. This could be the easiest to implement since the /tell buffer thats already in place.

5. Applicant Rights.
In using the the /org vote option, applicants should not be allowed to vote on guild topics. Thats why they are applicants. They are not technically in the guild. They are applicants so they can test out the guild to see if it is the right one for them, and for guild members to see if they should allow the person in the guild.

They should be able to use the guild channel but it should also be that they dont have to pay the tax until they are at least a rank of Unit Member (or rank number 5 for other org types) since they cant vote on guild issuses.

6. Member Rights
Have a way to set certain rights to certain members other than giving them rank. With setting member rights to certain commands would help give greater control in what everyone can and cannot do. Such as withdrawing money from the bank given to certain trusted members so that they can do it when the President is not online, or giving a certain unit member the right to recruit(maybe because he/she is the secondary character of member, but you dont want to promote that character just for that ability).

7. (/afk <away message>)
There is another very small but highly useful to implent. Add a 80 character buffer (or even smaller) to /afk command so you can add an away message. This away message could be read if you /tell the afk person. So if you /tell someone that is afk and they put a message on it, one example reply could be "Joe is AFK, 'I have to go make dinner. Be back at 7". Tiny in programming, Big in usefulness.

8. Whiteboog's Org Wage Suggestion.
quote:
"Also an /org wage command wouldnt go amiss, obviously it could be set up for different ranks (so a guild which doesnt have a lot of money can only pay wages of 1k to applicants and all applicants can be level 20- or something. But a command like this would be nice, especially for the members who do an awful lot FOR the guild but dont get much back (eg a level 180 guy in a level 40-50 guild)"

These items that are most likely on all organization leaders minds. They are not hard to implement, they don't add incredible amount of bandwith on the servers. PLEASE COMMENT. And check out our website at http://otguild.tripod.com. Thank you.
