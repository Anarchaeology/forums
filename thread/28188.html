The Answers.
1) Buffs for every melee skill (Every skill - 1he, 2he, 1hb, 2hb, 2he, Melee energy, Piercing, Multi Weild, HEAVY WEAPONS, Parry, Perhaps Fast and Sneak Attack (probably not really a massive issue.) Similar if not idential to the soldier's mastery lines.


Blackmane:
Yes, enforcers will almost certainly be getting buffs for the melee weapon skills, at least the main skills - 1/2-hand edged and blunt. These are not currently on the schedule, however.

2) A couple more versions of mongo to fill in the two larger gaps in this line a bit would be nice.

Blackmane:
No more mongos right now. Mongo was a famous fighter, but not that famous. There is meant to be a long step between each of the mongos.


3) Weapon diversity. Instead of having one good weapon in each class (1he, 2he, 1hb, 2hb and so on) make a couple good in different ways, but make them somewhat comparable.

Jim Salabim
- Ok. Maybe I'll make something like the Sol Executioner line you're refering
to.


4) Make melee init work. Seriously look into this. Yes, it has an effect, but with even the fastest melee weapons (Which are the same speed as a MA punch at 1.2/1.2) You CANNOT move the slider to def AT ALL without losing tons of speed. Even with 1300+ Melee init. A MA with 700 init with the same speed base weapon can fight on 30% agg/def. Fix this.

Blackmane:
4. I did the same tests that I did to verify the problem with the MA's fist attack not using physical prowess initiative, and can not see any problem with melee initiative. My tests showed that I was able to move the aggdef bar down into the negative with a high initative (tested to 1000 initative) and a fast weapon. If I am at min aggdef (all the way to the left) and have 1000 in skill, my attack time is not as low as it can possibly be when using a 1.2/1.2 weapon - in fact the recharge will have hit the minimum cap, but the attack speed will not. From my tests, initiatives look to be working fine (the problem with the MA's punch was not initiatives working, but rather that the punch 'item' was using the wrong initiative).



5) Maybe an Enforcer only armor? That looks nice and adds to weapon skills? Or that gives us a taunt affect to our attacks or something? (Ie gloves that we can right click for an instant taunt..)


6. Enforcer specific items. Ones that are possible to get and are useful at the level at which they are available. Like the SOL Fire Executioner line for adventurers perhaps. And not just unique ql 200 items, but a line of items, weapons, armor, whatever that are unique to us. (and making things atrox only doesn't count.)

Jim Salabim
- We're constantly adding content to the game. You will see more profession
unique, breed unique and side unique items in the future. Items with a limited
Quality Level range are normally only found in static type dungeons. I've
taken notice of your ideas.


7. Tank armor being comparable to coats by advantages/disadvantages at the same ql. From what it looks like, even after 14.2, they will still have almost the exact same armor levels at the same ql, but will also have speed and other skills decreased. Just fixing the nano cost still leaves it falling behind.


Jim salabim
- We do want to see more people using Tank Armors, especially tank professions
of course. We will improve them until we see them being used more.


8. Rage. I like it and use it often, but if it would break other debuffs it would be great. And if other debuffs didn't break root, all the better. It stinks that it will break a 2 minute root, but when I get a 20 minute slow cast on me I am stuck with it. (And having it work even when an absorption shield is up would be magical)

Blackmane:
8. No. Rage is not going to break other debuffs. Rage is the enforcer's anti-root line of nanos, not anti-debuff in general. It is meant to allow you to get back into melee range if you are rooted/slowed. Rage works fine when an absorb shield is up - now that it is fixed, that is. Before it only looked like it was working because the DoT associated with Rage sometimes broke the roots/snares, which was masking the problem that the way that should have been removing these debuffs was actually broken. That has been fixed, so Rage no longer relies on the DoT breaking the root/snare, which is how it is meant to be working.

9. 2hb weapons that do a minor AOE hit. If I'm swinging a support beam, when surrounded by 12 mobs, I would guess that I could hit more than one.

Blackmane
9. I don't think so. AOE is a double-edged sword. There is no way that we would be
putting damage on the AOE that matches the damage inflicted by the normal attack, and using an AOE weapon would mean that it would always be breaking mezz and roots. This would make you very unpopular very quickly. We are certainly not going to make the support beam even more powerful



10) Challenger line of nanos - how about having this lock some skill for 2 minutes instead so you can't recast it, and be a bit less effective to compenstae - would actually get used at later levels then...


Blackmane
10. Challenger line is not going to be changed. It is really a special-case nano that you can use to increase your damage output when you don't need to worry about executing other nanos - when you aren't being the primary tank, for example. If you are the main tank, or are in a situation where you need to run other nanos such as a Mongo, then you won't want to use a Challenger.
