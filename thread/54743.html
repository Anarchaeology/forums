Request: A trading system that DOES work
Request: A trading system that DOES work

Please give us a trading system that is not as spammy and clumsy as how the trading goes now in the trading chatchannel.

People just spam their items over and over again, hoping someone who 1) needs that item 2) has the money 3) is just paying attention at that very moment (too many uncertainty variables).

We need a system that allows you to offer your items over a longer time (like how you can browse through the offered items that were offered before on a webbased forum).

The best suggestion I have read, is to make it possible for a player to rent a shopping terminal and put his items to sell in the terminal, so that those items are up for a longer time then the 10 secs of attention they get now in the shopping channel.

If you want people to have fun doing trader en engineer professions, you should also remove the burden of actually selling the things made!

Marketplace
You know what'd be nice? A market terminal where you could pay a commision to list an item at a set price. The items would then be sorted into categories (much like the stores now) by type and quality level. Then if you're looking for a MCS QL 70, you go to ranged weapons, shift down to QL 70 area, and quickly browse. The items would all be timed (a few days perhaps) after which they are returned to your bank/inventory.

Alternatively you could have an auction where things are bid on, much like E-bay.

Unfortunately both would be difficult for Funcom to manage, and I don't see a user-friendly system coming anytime soon. I have yet to see a MMORPG make shopping reasonably simple.
