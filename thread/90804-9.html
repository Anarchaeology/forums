Earlier in this thread someone mentioned how as a real newbie, missions were always too far away or dangerous to get to without a yalm. Well I had the same problem, but here;s the tip I got:

If you want to get a mission for a lot of money and in a nice safe location, the trick is to roll it at a mission terminal a long way away. Take the Whompa to the furthest place you can go - the remotest outpost and roll your mission - you will be very pleased at some of the locations you get.

Another post mentioned passing down gift packs of old equipment - well you can also make these up to sell to twinks who want to save time - make a pack with matched set of armor, wpn, belt, ncu, implants etc, and you will save someone else a lot of time - and they will pay for that saved time.

for another suggestion I'd like to make:

When you've outlevelled the rifles and the cushions,

http://aomainframe.info/showitem.asp?AOID=123950 ql44 adds computer literacy and can be easily duel wielded for a total of +40 to comp lit. Now why is that important for a level 200 person? TOWERS. Let me give you an example. To place a ql246 tower on a land control area you need something like 1200+ comp lit. Folks want the HIGHEST ql tower they can possibly place, so another +40 is an incredible amount.

It's best to get them at the lowest possible ql, as they are much easier to equip without spending much (or any) IP. The QL44 Galahad Inc. T70 Beyer should be sold for 1-2 million per pair, considering how useful they are.

To find out what QL missions you are able to pull, type /tell helpbot level (and then insert your level).

http://aomainframe.info/showitem.asp?AOID=124156 ql38

Adds psychology. Psychology is use for building certain jobeclusters such as defensive mastery (most of the time the req are so high that a crat or enforcer is better off building them), but is also used for placing towers. 500k-1 mil each.
