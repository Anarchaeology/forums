It was fun yes
First assault to the Storm EFP mining site was lag fest and we were clearly outnumbered when we attacked which ofcourse is always a bad sign.

I thought I had it ok when I ran toward base then suddenly "chunk chunk chunk" and graphics barely moved. There really wasnt room for any "strategy" there and I just tabbed someone and took a shot and prayed for the best.

I noticed during the lag that the one I was shooting was also shot by some others and he went down as I was hit by multiple NT nanos (prolly AoE)'s but Baffo got to CH me three times before I got again hit by few NT nanos (2.2k damage) and brawl and died. Was quite fun nonethless as I saw several clan casualties also.

After that we were thinking about second wave but we really didnt seem to have numbers for it at tha time and so I did some zone hugging with Yield and some others where we managed to kill few clanners by bait, lure and bite tactics, me being hidden and rdy to assist Yield

Most funny kill for me came in the form of some greenish Engineer who was low enough lvl to be attacked by Mantis. Man that was funny. I could barely sneak toward him as I was laughing and watching the Mantis beat the crap out of him and then I took the few shots and finished him off.

We then heard that Storm was about to attack Renaissance base in EFP and moved there to defend.

I gotta say this straight. As defender I didnt lag even 1/2 as bad as I did as attacker so defender _clearly_ has advantage here in the lag department. Actually I didnt lag almost all and could keep shooting targets that were called out and assisting where ever I could.

When Storm was beaten off (I think they had about same odds as we did ie there were less attackers than defenders) and clearly they were at disadvantage, it was time for counter attack.

At this time one more Omni guild joined in force and we managed to overrun Storm defences. This time we had the numbers on our side as attacker should and I was enjoying it to the fullest.

I also found out my true calling as Agent in mass PvP which is to keep hiding, finish of the weak, the running and the occassional person who gets lost of the herd. Nothing more satisfying than seeing someone in 20% health running past you toward border and take Aimed Shot at them and watch them fall like a sack or spring from the surprise and root somebody for others to finish off.

Anyways, thanks for all the Omni's and Clan's too I suppose, who made this a fun battle for me.

Zarch and da chars...
