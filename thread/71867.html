A players (and therefore also an NPC's) Parry skill is a passive skill ... so everytime someone swings at you, the following is what roughly happens :

-Skill check to see if you Evade the hit (Enemy Attack vs. Evade)
-Skill check to see if you Parry the hit (Enemy Attack vs. Parry)
-Skill check to see if it becomes a Critical hit. (Enemy Attack vs. Random number generator + Evade ... I think)
-Damage absorbtion calculated from your buffs (layers etc.)
-Damage absorbtion calculated from your Armor (-1 Dam per 100 Armor I think)
-You get hit.

This is done each time, with some random modifiers at each stage except the Absorbtion stages. Which means that on each and every hit, you have a chance to parry your opponent.

It's never Parry vs. Parry and Parry does not get locked.
The higher Attack you have, the less you get Evaded / Parried.

Also, personally I don't think Dimach should be parried at all .. that seems silly.
