For some reason, I actually believe it's you. So I'll join you as a first time poster (long time lurker). If it's the "story" dungeons that you're talking about, then there are 3 that I know of.

First, in order of appearance, is the "Steps of Madness". This one is located in Omni Forest (exit Omni-Entertainment District). I don't want to ruin your exploration fun, so the coords I will leave a mystery. I forgot what the lvl rating that Funcom claimed was (35-55?), but a full team @lvls 50-65 should be able to get you pretty far. *notices that you asked for EXACT* 800x2800.

The second was one in Greater Tir County, but I haven't been and therefore am unsure of the details.

The third is located west of West Athen, in a zone called The Longest Road, and is, I believe, officially nameless (commonly referred to the 'TLR Dungeon'). Never did jot down the coords for that one, but follow the road through 2 zones west, hang a left (second left along this stretch of road), and head south into the Omni Mining OP. It's the Foreman's office. The level reqs are around the same as the first, perhaps slightly higher.

Hope that helps.

The Longest Road dungeon is actually "Named" something along the lines of OT Bio Facility. What kills me is that OT teams are happier camping there than clans are. Everything inside is of alignment "monster." I was between 56 and 57 when I got help getting into the inner reaches of the dungeion. The entryway guards were either solid green or very gray, but as I got in, the bodyguards outside the director's office were quite red. It seems to be a 40-70 dungeon.

The Steps of Madness has an official "level" of 30-50. Everything up to Neleb for me is solid green or solid grey. However the things here hit a lot harder and take a lot more punishment than their level should allow for. Some of the "green" critters here sends this Fixer running like a shoolgirl, since I can barely put a dent in them before I run out of hitpoints. Don't even think of attempting this dungeon without the combined talents of a meta-pysicist, an engineer, and a trader. I have only seen the entire dungeon thanks to the full teams of level 80+ groups who decide they want one of Neleb's cloaks. I have also seen some level 100+ metas solo most of the dungeion.

I have yet to visit the Cyborg barracks, but I hear that the minimum level is 70.
