Steps of Madness has no level restrictions in place. Adding level caps to this dungeon would not be fair, because it contains a nodrop item that is (arguably) useful to players of any level: nelebs robe. If FC wanted to add level caps to SoM, they'd need to remove the NODROP flag, or provide high-level characters another means (another location?) to get said robe.

You can chalk this one up to poor quest implementation on FC's behalf. Every static dungeon has been camped since people wanted their rings, not just SoM.

Also, high-levels were not taking any 'easy way out' to get pieces for Ergo's ring quest. The parts were made available in static dungeons, so that's where people are going to go. No matter which one we choose, we're going to be faced with killing easily-overpowered grey mobs. This is because an appropriate-level static dungeon for level 200 players does not exist (the only exception would be Inner Sanctum... but there were no reports of ring-quest pieces dropping at this location).

As far as kill-stealing (attacking mobs that another player has engaged first) goes, I feel that this is rude behavior... in most situations. The majority of AO's playerbase would probably agree. However it is not illegal as per Funcom; this is how players compete for mobs that are universally deemed free-for-all.
