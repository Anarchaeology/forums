- You try a MP : they suck because they are nothing without pets, have so few HPs and their summoned staff do ****ty damages.

- You try a MA : they suck because their Dimach cannot be used three times in the same fight and their heal is so weak.

- You try a Doc : you quit it very soon when people tell you that they own PvP past level 150 and that up to that level you just have to sit behind your team and can't solo a Leet.
