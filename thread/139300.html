Resurrection buff
Just a thought here.

Lets say a doc could get a new nano, which lasted for like 10 secs, when casted on a target would resurrect the target to a couple of points of HP IF it died there. It would have a cooldown so that it couldnt be misused(Somehow like the Agents Concentration line), lets say 10 minutes. It would work as an emergency buff.

It shouldnt shut down the nanos, because you would be useless as a doc.

Best example would be that someone gets aggro, and you see you cannot rescue them, its too late, so you cast the nano and they get a program in their ncu which resurrects them right after they dies(Or they dont die, they just go down to 0 and up to 10-20). A Dead body dont get aggro, so it should, hopefully save that poor sod.

What do you think?
