Blade Corp official recruitment (RP, RK1 & 2, Clan, Omni and Neutral)
Greetings!

I represent Blade Corp  leading mercenary corporation in the Galaxy. Blade Corp in itself is a mega-corporation known throughout the Galaxy as a leader in its field. It had opened its branch on Rubi-Ka in order to bring its great employment opportunities and services to the citizens of this planet.

Services we provide are very diverse. Unfortunately many believe that mercenaries are strictly military people. That is not exactly true. While we have such departments as Armed Forces, Special Forces, Enforcement (more focused on protection) and a few others, we also have peaceful departments, such as Medical Services (called Red Blade), Research and Development (R&D), Manufacturing, Sales, Exploration, Public Relations and many other. We also provide such services as protection, escort, delivery (of goods), information, support (ooc: assistance in hunting and missions) and many others. As such, we seek experts in all fields to provide citizens of Rubi-Ka with best services in diverse areas. Your current skill level is not a major factor, we have professional trainers that can help you achieve all that you can be. What we are looking for is people with good character and attitude. Because we have so many different positions and thus fields of expertise, you can be doing whatever you like doing most and not only get support in doing that, but also get paid for it. We have internal jobs, those that work for Blade Corp directly, such as producing goods to be later distributed to other Blade Corp employees or sold on the open market, as well as outside contractors. Based on the position you obtain you get paid either on per-contract basis or have a solid salary, either of which will provide you with everything needed for your prospering life on Rubi-Ka.

Because we wanted to bring these great opportunities to all citizens, we are not limiting to one specific side. As such, for many it might simply be a second job. For example, you might be doing whatever you are required to do for Omni-Tek and than spend your spare time in Blade Corp labs.

If you would like to learn more about Blade Corp., please visit our HQ located at www.bladecorp.com and check out all the amazing career opportunities, as well as application form to apply if interested.

Blade Corp. does not discriminate based on breed, profession, age or affiliation.

OOC: Blade Corp is a mostly RP guild, with mostly mature-minded people. It is based on both servers and available to all sides (Clan, Omni and Neutral) through our branch system, which in game are all represented by different guilds. More information about our structure and positions you can find on our website, mentioned above. We accept people of all levels as helping you level is one thing that we do. Of course we also try to supply our members with best possible equipment and services (such as implant building) and enough credits to fulfill all your needs. There are some management and other high positions available, so if you wish to take of those please specify so in your application. Role-playing is not a must, but is proffered, however all guild events, interaction, etc. is mostly carried out through role-playing. We are involved in the Story and world politics, but participation on those is optional. Blade Corp has a flexible management system, where you can be sure that your voice will be heard. It is also not very strict on policies, unless you are in certain positions.

Thanks all and have a good day!

PS: Please note that listing on the AO website does not represent our actual numbers as many members are not part of the in-game guilds. For example most Special Forces operatives are not included in the in-game guilds because of the nature of their job that requires them to hide their affiliation.
