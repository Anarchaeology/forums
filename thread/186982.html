The following is a message from the Devils Advocates community outreach center.




Tired of being aligned with the goody two-shoe Redeemed? Sick of watching your new allied faction heal their enemies as they endlessly get cut down? Or are you just bored with dancing around like a blessed unicorn? Want to be cool and almighty like the Unredeemed?

Well now you can!

For a limited time only Devils Advocates is proud to announce that they will be opening their doors for any clanner that is tired of being one of the so called enlightened saviors. Devils Advocates is known (and loved) through out all Rubi-Ka and is rated as one of the most sociable organizations. But fear not, just because were high society socialites doesnt mean we dont enjoy the thrill of the hunt; in fact frivolous killings is something most of us enjoy the most.

To sweeten this already wondrous offer we are also giving away a free bar of soap* as a gift to any clanner just for signing up. No more stinky pits for you my friends, at Devils Advocates we care about you and your hygiene.

Not sure your faction points will allow you to switch sides of the conflict? No worries! We already have members standing by with sharp pointy sticks and big ass artillery ready to massacre as many happy shiny Redeemed as deemed necessary!

So dont delay, contact any of our members today and get set to enjoy your new life with our family!


This message brought to you by the good guys, Devils Advocates.
