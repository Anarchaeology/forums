The Makers Shades Recruiting!!!
The Makers Shades are recruiting!

New guild forming up, I seek fellow Shades of the Makers, to learn more of the Worlds we have before us and to fulfil our duties as given to us ( read the story on this thread to learn more http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...hreadid=190863 ).

Together we shall prepare ourselves initially through observation, against the many threats to the Makers, to walk in their Shade between the Shadow and the Light.

May Hazak-Jar go with you all, till our sighting of each others visage.


[OOC]

Role-playing, creative story line creation and involvement are the paramount purpose of this guild. To work together in ideas for events, story-writing and adventures. To help other guilds in their own events/adventures if deemed as relevant by those who are interested. To encourage being in character (when dealing with the non-guilded).

Basic requirements:-

1. Must be Neutral and of the Shade Profession

2. Must like to be in character, to have role-played or at least wanting to.

I highly encourage those who already are in guilds and role-play to join in should they make a Shade character, as they will not be required to spend many hours of game time, only for the occasional brain storming sessions and of course when the events/adventures are run.

Regardless, the guild will need some to help with coming up with ideas ( I suppose you could call them advisors ). Should any guilds out there think that our Guild could be used in any way, contact me and well see what we can rustle up

All thats left to say is If you are interested, send I, myself and me a tell and Ill get back to you as soon as.


Sightsee ( The Makers Shades ).

[/OOC]
