Couldn't take it anymore. I thought I could hold out until something else came along, But considering how only three or four of the TWELVE classes are functional for PvP or PvM...Considering how the system for PvP is decidedly lacking...Considering how much I hate the leveling treadmill AND that's the direction the game is going in...Considering how we're almost never listened to, only when it's to cave into a bunch of people who have only played for a few weeks and whine a bit too much...Considering the whole silliness of the Superslag scenario...Considering what passes for "Story" and "Theme"...Forgetaboutit. A waste of time and money.
