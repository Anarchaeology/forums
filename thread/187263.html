New raidbot opening to omni
Mercsbot, a bot up until now restricted to clan, is now opening to omni players as well. We will be raiding numerous uniques, such as mercs, smug/fred, eel, tarasque, ljotur and others.

The bot works with a point system, where a point is assigned every hour of the raid and every time a unique dies. Loot is auctioned off, and the one who bids the most points wins the loot. (the last part pretty much goes without saying, but I thought I should mention it)

As of now the bot does not have a website, but it's under construction.

If you are lvl 150+ and interested in joining /tell Taracan for more information.
