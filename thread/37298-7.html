Usually some of these changes are so dramatic for certain professions that they should be offered a patch-FULL-IPR each time it happens (read trader, engi, etc). Its very conserning that FC doesnt respect the time that players have put into the game, and FC may blaim that the things they changes were bugs, but when theyve been around since the release i accept them as bugs. Then they have been the way the game worked, the way that the dynamics of this game worked. It looks like FC is in economic trouble with the loss of a lot of customers ( why wont they publish official subscripton numbers???) and that the game lost lots of customers in the beginning of the game (if i dont remember wrong i think 100.000 subscriptions was the goal for anarchy online) due to a huge amount of bugs (lets accept it, the game was released way too early, and FC lost a loot of customers due to the decision to do this). Well i still want to say that the development team have done a good work, and its a totally different game than it was at release time (lots of nice content too). I wont comment on the bugfixes, since they shouldnt have been there at all, and there still are some serious points to resolve. The difference with the different breeds are way too small: is there any point in playing anything else than Solitus at the moment??? Atrox isnt that increadible more healthy/Sturdy breed, nanomage isnt that very nano'ish and opifex is not at all a sneaky kind of breed

When i first started this game i totally fell in love with the IP system, and still thinks its a feat of genious, but why do the want to change it now suddenly. AO is sold as a skill based game, and most certainly not a level based game. The developers and moderators (Cosmik/Cz) has said that due to shadowlands they will not change this new change in the game dynamics whatever happens. Most of the players hates the idea of having level requirements forced upon them, and i think it would be wise of FC to give a much better explanation to this decision. Dont just tell us that we will understand when we see shadowlands, we want to know now, we like to be informed (this is the information age, right?) There is no point in keeping your customers out in the dark, if you're going to implement this change in the game dynamics, you should give us a better explanation. And due to the reason that we are entitled too it! I pay for this game, i spend time playing it, but i dont like being kept in the dark.

It looks like FC has the tendency to punish their customers then to reward them. When you LD, due to lag/crash etc, you can still die and loose a lot of xp. The current reimbursement policy is totally wrong, if ive spent several hours obtaining an item/cash/xp, and loose them to a bug, i most certainly want them back. If it happens due to my own mistake (attack the wrong mob, press the reset button, etc) i can and will understand it. Well this is sort of a rant, but still some of my thoughts ive gotten after playing this game for a while.
