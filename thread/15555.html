The Desert Paradise of Bliss
So eventually I surrendered and starting hunting with the rest of the Clan people lvl 25-40 out by Temple and bot mountain, which more of less means running around for five hours waiting for slow spawns and trying not to kill steal. Temple is not a great hunting area. Too many people, too few critters, maybe good enough to support one full team but definitely not the thirty or so people that always lurk there.

Meanwhile, The Desert Paradise of Bliss, lies virtually empty, except for the rare occassion that someone takes a mission out there. There are literally hundreds of scorpions and giant spiders out there, enough to support an army of lvl 30-40 hunters. Sure they add like crazy, but that means more xp for those brave enough to take the chance. It isn't really that far out along the longest road, not much more of a walk than tir to temple. So why can I never find a team there? Its also really hard to raise a team in a city and head out, cause people who want to hunt head out to popular hunting spots....

Fleapit - Rhino Cockpit - Temple. There is more to clan hunting than these three areas.

Kramer: I know perfectly well the location of Bliss, but the problem is not related to the 'physical' distance from a nearby town, but rather to the need for people to interact before and after missions, hunting or whatever (marketplace, buffing, teaming etc.) Bliss doesn't have any backyards, so people will familiarize themselves with Tir, Athen (Old) and Newland before they even know what TLR is. And a town without a puplic transport system will unlikely become major social arena, however beutiful and well-assorted shops it may have. Bliss is on the very outskirts of the current map of Rubi-Ka, meaning travel time from wherever your team or guild usually hangs out. For those who don't mind running the few extra miles, I would thoroughly recommend it for both hunting and missioning. Things might change alot when the new zones in the West are opened, that's my opinion (and hope).

Cro: I totally agree with you about not having the public access that would make it more of a social spot, I was only offering my theory as to why it's so quiet, in respons RedRazors question why it's so hard to find a team there.
