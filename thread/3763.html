SWB
Well, try a little south of Last Ditch (new whompa in Borealis), or far west of Borealis (same place). There should be enough to keep you busy once you get in deep enough. Plus there is a save terminal in Last Ditch (neut) and a save terminal at the bronto burger in 4holes. Yeah, its 25%, but if are smart enough to pay attention, there shouldn't be too many omnis in your level range around, and the Neuts can't fire.

There are a good numbers of singleton Scorpoids and rolerrats around. Be on the lookout as things will agro you to your dismay. The best part is, its not particularly packed by other characters, so you've got a bit more leeway in target selection
