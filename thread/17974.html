Hunting Lost Forever??
In the beginning of AO there was a balance struck between time spent hunting and time spent in missions/upgrading your character. You could go to a hunt spot and get into/build a group with a certain degree of ease and go hunt a large variety of wild mobs. Missions were a means of making a living. This is now very far from the case.

I have played since day 1 and have watched the game unfold to what it is today. Unfortunatly what it is today isnt what i was sold on when i first went out and blew the $60 on the game (and yes, i do realize that the game must change for the sake of progress). Some major changes have happened in which the balance between missions and hunting has been disturbed. Hunting is no longer viable, e****ally at higher lvls.

Hunting was always my favourite part of the ao experience, but now the only way to get xp in groups is to go to bs for missions. No one wants to go to 25% or 0% supression gas zones to hunt as their too far away (mutiple zone crossings required, which means an increased probability of crashing (crashing = disbanded group)), aswell at too dangerous (who wants to go somewhere to gain xp when it can all be taken away in an instant (along with any cool loot you may have found during the hunt) by someone twice your lvl?). Missions are safe and easy to get to (e****ally in bs).

So then what needs to be done?? Well ive been thinking about this alot and Ive come up with a few ideas;
1. Make mission xp much less (if lvl'n in missions isnt viable then people will once again go back to hunting).
2. Remove 25%/0% zones (if there is no risk of loosing all your gained xp to someone twice your lvl im sure that people would rather hunt in the wild (except for the ease of getting to the hunt spot (refere to #3))).
3. Make grid exits in the "anarchy zones" and even if the zone is still 25%/0% then make the areas around the grid 75% (would make it possible for people to mass near hunt spots in the wild).
4. Make wild mob loot/xp much more apealing (same idea as #1, just opposite effect).

Any other ideas on how to make hunting a part of the game once again???

Please dont flame as im sure alot of you dont agree with these (rember that their just ideas).
