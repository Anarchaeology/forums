14.2 - Chat Changes
Chat Changes:

*Added a few commands to make chatting easier:

-"/o" to send a message to your organisation.

-"/g" will send a message to your team.

-"/v" will send a vicinity message. ("/(space)" will also do this)

Could I also recommend that "/s" be removed? It tires me to be in an area where people are too lazy to type "/say" (granted the new "/ space" command may ease this) and the whole zone is spammed with people selling items. In real life it takes extra effort to fill your lungs with air and shout - why not also make people use extra effort to type "/shout"?

The only viable reason someone may want to shout is to fill a team or LFG. (People really should learn that there is a damn shopping channel!) Perhaps a new command "/lfg" could be added. This could generate a shout in the form of:

<your_level> <your_profession> LFG

This could effectively make teaming easier since a standard format would always be used and people are more able to determine possible new additions to their team.

Taking this further, maybe another command, "/lfm" could be introduced. This could generate a shout in the form:

Team <lowest_level_member> - <highest_level_member> LF<available_free_places>M

I know myself I have to cycle through any team I am in to find each level of my team mates in order to find the levels of future prospective team members and this can be time consuming if several are out of range in a shop or another zone and I have to actively get the people in question to answer me in team chat.

Since teams aren't always filled with all the people who have been invited, this "/lfm" command could also have an additional property - that of a number to specify the amount of additional members the team is searching for. Perhaps "/lfm 2" would still generate the correct amount of free places where, for example, one person is temporarily LD or the team have one person on the way to join them.
