First Order on Rk1 - Taking applications!
First Order is looking for a few good players to join the ranks of an already established guild!!!


-- We have held events before, and will hold more! .. Examples of this would include our annual "Underwear pillowfight to the death!"

-- As of this posting, we have a guild population of around 300 members listed on the AO homepage.

-- Our members are some of the better informed guild members around, events and happenings are given to the guild as they happen.

-- We run a Department style org, which means plenty of room for promotions

-- Everything in the guild is done by general concencus or vote, as leader I make very few decisions without involving the guild.

-- We have regular meetings, plus a guild member dedicated to banking old gear and such for use by guild members.

-- We aim to fill the guild with Mature gamers that are basicly out to just have a good time.
