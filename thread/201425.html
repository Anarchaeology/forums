Pinky got the runs in Scheol...
"What do you mean, Ergo went coocoo?" Pink was puzzled, this didn't make any sense whatsoever.
"Simple, that little floating thing decided you don't need to do all sorts of things anymore and now if you high enough in experience, you can use his portals to a certain point." Unknowhn stood there, the smug little leprechaunlike trox agent wanna-be. Pink looked down on him even though he himself was no taller than the average agent that was running around with Ruse of Taren 3 on him and thought to himself why it was that even the lowest of intelligence every now and then managed to get into knowledge before he did himself.
This time it was easy, he had hybernated for a while. "Oh well, time to go see mister Ergo then I think to have him let me into the snowlands isn't it?" Pink smiled when saying this since the puny trox before him wasn't allowed anywhere near Scheol yet, so this would be HIS little trip.

Time to get going, off to the lands beyond Jobe and into the great unknown once more. It had been a while since he last was there and of course he ran inot the wrong Whompa, straight into Newland City...
Upon entering he got hit in the helmet by a piece of paper, making him grunt all sorts of unbelievably rude words and as he grabbed it off his helmet he noticed it saying something about getting rid of the hired ones in NLC and replacing them with neutrals and so on.
"They should do that yeah and then make them shoot those Omni buggers that lurk here too for all I care" he muttered to himself. His helmet had a bit of a disturbing peeping sound and that was easily recognizable as the sound of being attacked. A quick look at who it was turned him paler than his Opi butt already was. Loren Warr. Oh hell.

Time for some fast thinking made him cast his short term heal over time on himself and also throwing knives expertise. "That last didn't make any sense, what the hell happened to my program listing?" He thought as he ran off hoping to catch the whompa to Borealis in time before getting his insides smeared all over the pavement.

"Ouch ouch ouch!" Too many little parts of the little man hurt, so it was time to do some serious personal healing. After finding CH in his program listing it was a mere case of regaining his nano and going forth on the rest of his trail.

"Okay, I need to make sure I get my brain checked. There is a Grid entrance here, why didn't I use it?" His thought struck him as rather on the late side, but oh well... He was there, ready to go into some serious conversation with the infamous Ergo! After entering Jobe Plaza and trying to broker a deal with the guy at the gate which didn't work, he managed to get into th etopmost part of Elysium and from there in Scheol. On a cliff. Where the heck is that Ergo supposed to be over here?...
