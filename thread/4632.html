Dust to Dust, Beer and Booze
quote:
Now,what the hell is 'dust to dust'?
Apparently, a nihilistic slogan that one of the deadlier Clan factions used, about five years ago, during a month when they were able to hack (and screw up) one or both sides' insurance terminals ... which they then followed by some of the worst slaughters in the whole war.

That month is still called Black October (now that's original) by the colonists on both sides who lived through it, and revulsion at the death toll from Black October had a lot to do with "Boss" Ross's declaration of a cease fire.

This was covered over the last couple days in the in-game media, on http://community.anarchy-online.com. See the articles "Black October" (VoF), "Intercepted Transmissions Spark Rumours, Fears" (RKHS), and "Dust to Dust?" and "Threre's Dust in the Air" (TAG).
