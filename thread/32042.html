Not So Easy
There are a number of ways to find places to hunt. The first is to explore yourself and the second is to ask. Since a lot of people are running multiple characters they probably have their favorite places. Finally (and I am starting to do this) keep a list of coordinates because that is the easiest way to direct others to these places.

Keep in mind what makes a hunting spot "good". Mobs and monsters that are of a fairly consistent level (what level doesn't matter since that just relates to which level hunts there) and a close save terminal.

For levels 85-120 or so Sentinels is a good hunting spot. To grid there requires CL of 460 or you can grid to MMD and go North.

The directions to the Rhinoman cockpit is okay as long as you know where the oasis is. The oasis is out the North Gate in Newland City, go to the Wiiky Valley, head east to the outpost which is where you can save, then continue east to zone, go East NE after zone and you will hit the oasis very quickly.

Now see, if I had coords that would have been much shorter, but I don't.

As for mid-high to high level (90-150) hunting grounds I have found some really good ones in Perpetual Wastes but there are no save terminals. Also there are locations east of Stret East bank etc. that have lots of mobs but no close saves. It is the requirement for save terms that makes some of these excellent grounds unfit for common hunting.

For General Information: I applaud Emiko who has adventured around as a leet. It isn't as dangerous as running but there are sure some risks. Me I didn't really start exploring until I got my Quantum Wings. Aside from a good Yalm QW is the way to go. Of course you have to be a MP to use them
