List of Topics slated to be disscussed at the 9th BureauCon, first portion. Estimated total time alotted to the disscussions, 1 hour. May be extended to 1hr 30 minutes if required.

-------------------------------------
Crowd control(First 30 minutes)
-------------------------------------
As you may know, one of the bureaucrat's main assets to a team is their ability to mez and later on, area mez. This allows a team to survive encounters that would otherwise have left them at the reclaim terminal. However, past level 150 this crowd control aspect becomes much less important and in some cases can even be completely ignored. This is because teams can kill the mobs fast enough as to make mezzing impratical.

The new dynacamps added a many more uncharmable, unmezzable and due to the psychic checks, also unfearable mobs. This has been detrimental to the bureaucrat's usefulness in a team. Symptoms of this can be clearly seen with bureaucrats that have used weapons for the sole purpose of damage.
-------------------------------------
Bureaucrats in PvP(Remainder of the half hour.)
-------------------------------------
In the upcoming booster pack The Notum Wars, pvp is slated to be one of the major attractions. The bureaucrat profession is handicapped in pvp because half of our nanolines simply stop working. These lines include mez, fear and charms.(If the opponent(s) do not have any pet users.)

Of the things that do work, like our debuff speeches become a liability when the other players "auto retaliate". Even when they don't, some say the debuffs are neglectable and are fairly useless.

Despite this, there are some bureaucrats that pvp and a few of them claim to do well. Is our profession "good" at pvp? By being good I mean that are we able to stay competative in a solo or group pvp environment(seperately).

The bureaucrat can stay somewhat competative in pvp by the use of high damage dealing weapons. Assult rifles(CHS), Shotguns and, Rifles(X-3) are the most common weapons of choice. But is this really "natural"? Or is it merely a workaround? Is chooseing these weapons, a dark blue skill for the bureaucrat really a solution in pvp? It gives results, but are we useing them because they are a good or is it because we HAVE to choose them?

Most other professions have some unique ability(s) in pvp. The Agent can sneak, aimshot,limited crit buffs. The Doctor can self heal, hp buff, dot and init debuff. The Enforcer can mongo, essence, rage, absorption shields. And so on for the other professions.

What does the bureaucrat have to offer in the way of unique ability during pvp? Root/Snares are not unique to our profession. Our nukes are less effective than an NT's. Charms only work in special cases if there is a pet user nearby and can be considered non-functional otherwise. What do we have?
