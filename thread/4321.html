Did Funcom nerf small cave missions?
There are 2 main types of cave missions. First, the large horrible cave missions which everyone hates, with brown walls and 100 tiny rooms, some so small you can't see what's on the floor, and pools of water and acid. They've been specially designed to make pet pathing impossible.

Secondly, the smaller cave missions with less rooms, with grey or green walls, larger rooms, no pools of water.

I used to write down the coordinates of the smaller cave missons, hoping that I could get them again and not the large ones which I hate.

But lately when I've gone back to the same mission locations, they've all been large cave missions.

Is this just because they're higher level missions, or because I've set the sliders differently or something?

Or did Funcom nerf small cave missions, thereby forcing everyone to do even more of the mission types which everyone hates?

What should normally be similar for the same location, and generally is, is the general type of mission textures.

If you get a cave there, you'll probably always get caves there. Maybe large, maybe small, maybe the dreaded corrosive catwalk one, but not 'tech type rooms'.

If you get 'building interior' type missions there, you probably won't get cave style missions from that location. But you might get most any type of interior.

A given location will have only certain styles of interiors available, and NPC's/Monsters available lists for that location. So certain areas give you certain types of creatures, intended to match more or less with the zone they're in.

Usually if the entrance outside is a building, you get building interiors, while 'pile of rocks caves' entraces tend to give you caves. Not always, but normally.

i have confirmed that one particular location never gives me a mission larger than 4 initial doorways or 6 initial doorways.

now as i have leveled, the ensuing complexity, ie increased number of adjoining rooms has definitely increased. but as I am primarily a soloer, I like these small missions, and am glad to have been told of this location. I now can attempt to get 2-3 missions from the same location and be able to limit my online time as I would prefer, as opposed to getting 3 LARGE missions w/ seemingly infinite adjoining rooms (LOL) and feeling like I am missing out by logging out w/ 2 unfinished missions.

Do this.

Previous to 13.0, when we were being sent to CLon**** and Galway for missions, I started a spreadsheet and found some locations for sure fire small layouts.

No different now, you just have to re-discover where the small ones are.

Open an Excel spreadsheet and label columns for Zone, Location (coords), mission type (size), mission style (grey rock, brown rock, pharmatech, etc - though this is not necessary), where you saved, where you got the missions, reward value in creds, reward type, level, etc. the amount of info you can provide to yourself is infinite...i even delineated what type of mobs, and how hard they were, but never found a constant that was associated.

Then you can look back over time, and see which ones consistently gave you 4 or 6 initial doorways (usually grey rock), and then adjust where you get the missions and where you save, to optimize how much $$ you will get for them (usually distance based - I save in Aegean and get missions in Avalon and get Aegean missions, then i terminate, or go shopping and head back to the general area, as those are where the loc's are that I have found to be consistently small.
