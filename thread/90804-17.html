Quartz rifles occasionally appear in the basic shops in Trade. Usually I've got 3 or 4 bags full of them, I'll give all my friends a tell to see if they want one saving (including clanners), and pass a bag on to any guilds that have been helping the cause. I think Div9 had 3 bags of them given to them at one point. Same with QL50 Gripo's, they appear from time to time, so if you're looking for some time to fill, then do a quick loop of the basic shops in Trade. Oddly enough I've noticed they spawn at relatively the same time.

The one other thing that new people should know about, is the value of information and doing favours. I spent a long time gathering info, asking silly questions, doing runs for those aggro-enhancer things from SoM, helping someone get a Neleb's, camping for hours for a mate to get a TIM hud, etc.

Then came a time when I cancelled my account, and left the game for a couple of months. Came back and found I'd got nothing on all my chars, except a token board and a carrier on one of my other old chars. I was gutted.

But, all those favours got returned, and within a day, I'd got all QL200 implants (complete), full armour, weapons (including fury upgrade), Yal, and still had 45mil credits to play with. Even had clanners meet me in Newland to pass me items.

If I'd spent all my AO time (must be getting on for 200days playing time) levelling, then I'd probably have 4 or 5 chars at L200. As it is, my highest is L177, and second highest is L101. Why?

Because the value of doing favours, helping folks out, listening to people with problems, cheering them up, celebrating the victories, makes AO what it is... multiplayer. It also means that when I'm in a bad mood (as Cz will testify), having people to help pull me back up is invaluable. And the number of times I've dragged folks like Piikey, Destria, Noer, Zerosignal, etc half way across RK for some silly reason... yet they still talk to me even now, is unreal. But, I'd do it for them.

Oh, and one last thing... ask for money and you might get 10,000 credits, or you might get nothing. Then again, the last person I helped in a backyard prior to me leaving once, ended up with every possible QL200 soldier gun, full sets of armour, etc... prolly about 100mil (at least) worth of stuff. Just because when he asked for help and I barked back something like "go away", he asked if I was ok. Now that's classy.

Information and friends are of far more value than you could ever imagine in AO.

I don't see how you guys can get "handed" money. Oh sure it's easy to mule a few mils onto your char if you have a higher ql. But what about us starting mains that are stuck with doing things the hard way.

Early one I was faced with 2 major decisions. Either I go and level up in teams and lose money or I can do missions and gain money.

Here's what I get for each.

If I went on a team I'd have gotten several levels in a number of days. However there is always the big problem of all that ip and no money to spend it with.

If I go with terminal missions I'm stuck with SLOW xp gain and large money increases if I do far away missions. This is becoming increasingly harder to do because of that bug that won't let you into the mission.

My decision was to go with the terminal missions. Why? Well first of all it's actually the most bestest way to level unless you do team terminal missions but then you always have to share the loot and usually get stuck with the lower end of the stick. Another upside is you can upgrade yourself accordingly as you level.

I'm level 34 and I have 200k+ credits. Only once did I ever ask for money and that was from my clan. The reason? Well there was this nice Yalm that had a air req. of only 175. It's cost was about 5.5 mil. What I did was I asked one of my friends for a loan that I would pay off asap. He shrugged it but I convinced him to agree on me paying him at least half of the amount off.

Transportation is probably the best thing you can waver off in the rules of not begging. It helps a soloer so much by increasing the time to get to far away missions.
