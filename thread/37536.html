New nano lines
Yes here I am suggesting 2 new nano lines for an already uber prof. But I am not uber enough. I'm realizing that MAs are roughly equivalent to enfs. We each pretty much have 1 weapons class choice (Blunt and MA) and we each have the absolute best buffs for it. We also have good inits and evades, but are susceptible to roots. However, while enf's big temp buffs are geared toward HP (at least mongo is), MAs are geared toward truly disturbing amounts of damage. Behold my ideas, neither of which will ever get beyond this board, but I'll give it a shot

The first one: Aura line
This little line will be similar to rage... For a short time, the caster is consumed in an aura of <damage type>, and there will be one for each type dammit! Aura of lightning, inner fire, icy stare, corrosive skin, shocking heat (rad dmg), and painful death (that one's poison and the names a little weak). These will do thus: Convert the caster's damage type to said type and increase it marginally (more than fists but less than the big damage buffs). Duh. Create a damage shield, and raise the AC of the caster in said damage type. Also like rage though, it will have a DoT on it. You're surrounded in fire! Of course you're gonna get hurt! They won't last very long, maybe 40 secs max, and then you can't use 'em for 2 or 5 or whatever mintues. And we need graphic effects people! For this and for rage! THE ENFORCER IGNITES!!! He does not ignite! We should have lightning running around our body/fire/all that good stuff.

Second line: Fleeting Tingling/Thought/Feeling/Emotion/Recollection
This line will be named in that order. These will give a boost to all inits as well as evades and runspeed, in a 125/250/375/500/650 fashion. I'm going to recommend level reqs on them, being 0/30/60/90/120/150. They will lock nanos for two minutes.

Them's my ideas. Like 'em.
