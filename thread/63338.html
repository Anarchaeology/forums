Team/guild Beacons
I wanted to make a suggestion fpr future patches of ao, that I have gone over with many other players that are having some difficulties with ping time and all around confusion.

A team/guild beacon for missions and the like.. Mostly for non-static missions, a team leader would give out beacons or the team would Automatically Assume beacons in their inventory that would help the leader/organizer the ability to not have to guess where the other team mates are at in the generalized local area.

These beacons could be purchased at basic tool terminals by Players with the intention of forming teams.. I was thinking the LEADER would be the only one holding the item in inventory so that the others would have more room for more useful and important items.

Just a thought.
