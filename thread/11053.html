Arresting of Omni-tek Employees

The same thing with the apprehension of the a valuable source of information, who was assignated by two Omni-Tek 'employees'. Because of the gas fields reprisal was impossible against these scum and they likely escaped unharmed, however their names are recorded and proof has been placed in Omni-Pol hands.

Have the Omni-Pol post the names of these indivduals in an accessable location and label them criminals. Let us punish them for their crimes even if ICC regulations allow for their existance in the regenerative memory banks.

For Omni-Tek.
