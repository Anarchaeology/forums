Clan Leaders Thoughts of Mar 9th CCL Meeting
March 11, 2002

Dear FunCom:

First off, I want to say I have been on Rubi-Ka since Open Beta and absolutely enjoy spending my day planetside. In the beginning, yes, there were issues but you held stead fast and worked through these issues at a pace well worth applauding.

Its been 2 years and I have been an active Clan Leader during these times. I know that does not count for anything but I sit and observe the banter that goes on in Clan Leader chat and in fact when I am on, I log each day and post on the Anarchy Online Clan Leader message boards that was created for the Clan Leaders to have a place to go and post / interact off line for their guilds. The URL is : <edited for public viewing> if you ever wish to stop by. This way the Leaders can go back and read the days account if they could not get planetside.

We hold weekly Clan Leader meetings on Saturdays, 9pm GMT (various locations) to discuss how we can better this world with our efforts. At our last meeting (March 9th)  I threw a curve in the normal agenda items that we usually have.

I asked the Leaders present what they would like to see implemented into the world. I know that you are not going to jump up and do a patch to accommodate these wants and desires but I thought youd be interested in hearing what they have to say. I know you have the AO Official message boards for folks to post their wish list but many do not frequent there so I offered to write this email and send off  in hopes to get a positive response back on things we as Clan Leaders would like to see.

7. Guild Vendor Terminals  ~UO~
To have a Guild Store. You have the General, Basic, Armor, Weapons  etc but to have one that says Guild Store. When you enter  you would see terminals as normal but over head would be the Guild name. Any member of that guild can deposit items to be sold to other players and can set the price of said item. How a guild acquires a selling terminal  when first implemented  the terminals would be empty  with a sign For Rent on it or something. The Clan Leader has to buy the terminal. You determine the price, then by right clicking the terminal we, the Clan Leader, buys it and then you charge us a monthly fee to maintain the terminal. Once the terminal is bought, the For Rent sign changes over the Guild Name and open for business. You can limit the terminals to be sold to any given guild- say 3 per guild. This to be modified later.
