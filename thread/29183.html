A monitors Brightness setting is used to set the black level of a monitor. So you need to use that to make black look black.

Contrast is what is used to set the white level. That is what makes white look white. So you need to adjust that to make things brighter.

Only adjust monitor settings after your monitor has been on for more than 30 minutes or so to give it a chance to warm up.
