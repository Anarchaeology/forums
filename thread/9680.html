Clubbing! The fun way!!
I am sure Funcom has alot to do already... however when I first read over the previews for Anarchy Online there was ALOT that was mentioned that doesnt actually exists...

Anyways here is what I would like to see be done with social interaction.

- The NPC dancers in the club is good!! There should be more though so it looks busy! It would be great if they walked around too, and walked over to the bar to buy a drink, or to go sit somewhere.

- A drink seller in "The Cup" would be nice .

- I would like to see the possibility to actually "sit" in the chair. This could be done VERY easily if you made a seperate button(a button like sit) that shows a person sitting in a chair... now when you hit the button you dont actually interact with the chair... it just moves your character into bent knees with your bottom horizontal and your arms resting on your knees etc...

Maybe it would be possible to code in that a person can only use the "sit chair/object" button if there is an object next to them that is x long and y high.

Last but not least the pivot point would be the body. Everything you could do sitting down crosslegged would be possible with this as well( I wouldnt think you would have to change the social animations)... however old ones would open up like tapping you foot since your feet are not crossed. (This would be so simple to implement and so worthwhile)

- Bouncers at the clubs!! There should be Bouncers at the bars and clubs!! You should have to pay the bouncers for a key or a pass that you would use to right click on the door to get in.

- Interactive bad boys in the clubs/bars that can give you special quests... like breaking into someones home(npc) to retrieve for them something of value. The wont always be in the club/bar though, sort of a random arrival.

- This one is killing me, alchohal should graphically effects us!! Ok I know that the new breeds cant be intoxicated... but everyone wants it bad! You could make new drinks... that are a bit more expensive that will visually effect you... Ideas for this would be great as I dont have many.

- With every futuristic story comes drugs, star wars has them, star trek has them... Something called Rubi-ka dust, or other varieties like notum smack, mindreaver flesh, nanobot shooters. There would be drug sniffing dogs or detecting machines around the city that could detect if you had these unless your conceal or other skills are not high enough. They then proceed to arrest or kill you and remove the drugs from your person. Drugs would be buyable in the upcomming blackmarkets... or in dark alleys where drug sellers reside... and in clubs in the dark areas... sellers would be random spawns in areas. These drugs could affect you in many ways, visualy, physically, and your senses.

Visually... change in colors in your area, halucinations(imagine sitting in the green room, shooting up or taking some drug... looking back up and across the room is an ugly Tac mutant standing across from you that only you can see)

Physically... Your stats would drop dramatically, change at random, and maybe even kill you if you do too much.

Senses... Random noises that only you hear... etc

- Last thing on my mind would be an ingame mp3 player or music players... like the one Black and White did. Or the ability to punch in a site location to open a music stream. This could turn into people making their own music streams.. or AO could have their own.
