Biozopreph
As he reloaded his clip again he noticed that his hands were shaking and covered with blood. Bio, as his mates called him, had to get out of there, but there was only one way out. Run!
Quickly he packed up his gear and ran to the nearest outpost. The damned mutants were on his tail, for he had nearly killed a dozen of their kin by himself. This agent was not to be trifled with. He was now on top of the nearest hill when he noticed a strange object in the sky.... Bump!


He had woken up in a strange house, not like any he had ever seen. He quickly picked up that he was not in the safety of Omni Tek. He reached for his customized Tsak rifle but it was not there! His legs and been mangled and could not move, but what was worse were the strange noises coming from the next room. He still remembered a few of his nano programs and decided the best course of action. He was now a Meta Physicist with his trusted pet Sanoo.
"What are you doing, you are too weak!"
The strange bald headed lady in white uniform was informing Bio that his condition was critical. He was angry and then puzzled for she was not an ordinary doctor, but one of the Clan!
