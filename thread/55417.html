Don't underestimate the MP my friend
If played right, an MP is a whirlwind of damage. You got so many things happenning at once, you're not sure who to hit or how to do it. it's confusing to say the least. Not to mention the combination of energy damage (nukes), melee damage (pet) and (put away the x3) something like a chem mausser or sol and you have three damage types to deal with.

Yeah, definitely go for the MP's throat. I'm wondering if adv calms work on the heal pet, as the heal pet is not in combat...if you you have an advantage there if you're fast enough.

Only adv that really stands a chance is one that uses a ranged attack with some good specials. A strong alpha or disabling the heal pet is the only thing that kills an mp....except for...drumroll...

900+ nano resist! you'll resist everything we have and you can simply move in for the kill. And don't believe what they say about not being self buffed. All of my fights ive been self buffed

MPs are gimpy, but we have so many tricks. I've found the best way to fight and adv is to just slam nuke after nuke. The pet is doing good damage too, but the nukes are what does it. I dfon't even bother with nanoshutdown anymore. just dominate biomet for a fast -120, stack it with mind quake for another -100 and lay on the nukes. If my hp starts going down I stun and move to the other side of the arena, let the pet heal me, hit the first aid stim and go back to it.

But the MA....good god the MA. I couldn't understand why, as an MP, it was so easy to kill and enforcer but not an MA. What magic did those pajama wearing freaks have that nobody else did? So I rolled up another MA and decided to twink him all to hell. He's at lvl 128 now and the damage he dishes is ridiculous. WAY too powerful. I'm not asking for a nerf, but please bring other classes to the level of the MA sometime soon. In smuggler's den, I was teamed with lvl 180+ folks and held quite a bit of aggro. No, most of the aggro. 8% crit scope, 19% lma and a 910 attack rating, yet I was critting 75% of the time, same with missions.
Okay, im getting off subject.....

Adv vs. enforcer is a hella cool fight. I used to pit my old adv against my enforcer buddy last year and it was like a 50% win, 50% loss (although we were also complete noobs back then too). But yeah, he had it right when he said to grab a gun that checks against duck explosions. the swords just dont seem to have that little alpha oomph that's needed in pvp and melee adventurers usually drop like rocks. But get a good ranged weap with some specials and combine it with your excellent damage shields and healing powers and you have a force to be reckoned with!

Get a Hardcore CPU Upgrade

Trader: Get rrfe, essence, hot...then maybe, if they aren't buffed to hell too...or you get lucky and counter one of their debuffs with your amazing 600-700 NR :\ (Yes on full def too)

Metaphysicist: If you have max NR you can fight on full def to try to resist NSD...get a root graft for the pets if you like.

Soldier: Beat on em till they TMS, then stop attacking, sit on full def and heal while they take shots from your dmg shield, when TMS drops if they haven't zoned they should be fairly easy to kill.

Fixer Non-GA: They don't do all that much dmg but you probably won't kill them, unless they fumble RGJ a few times

Fixer w/GA: Sit on full def and let your dmg shield + specials do the work, and if you don't have good specials you'll have a bit of a hard time here

Crat: Same as MP

Engi: Same as MP

Agent: Most I've fought haven't done much dmg past their 100% crit, so if you survive that you shouldn't be too bad off.

Adventurer: Just hope you do more dmg

MA: They do a lot of dmg so root graft and/or run away a little to heal if you need.

Doctor: Hope they don't have HE/Jobe, put on a Recompiler to (kinda) counteract the init debuff and hope your nanopool outlasts theirs.

NT: Get some lower QL heals ready because of Crown of Frost If they have NS, set full def while its up.

Enforcer: Heal like mad Again hope your nanopool outlasts theirs (not as hard as doc obviously )
