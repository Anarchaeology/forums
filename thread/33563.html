Party At Baboons!!! CANCELLED
PARTY CANCELLED

An announcement from Devils Advocate's president Thyme:

Devils Advocate is sad to announce The OT celebration party planned for Sat at Baboons is now canceled. No further dates for this party will be planned. Agent "Garrisen" has sent an offline message to me stating, "I have left Rubi-ka and am taking a much needed rest and I enjoyed speaking to everyone even the scum that hated me."

We are also sad to announce that all of GridStream Productions dates at Baboons are called off as well. Much to our dismay, there is a boycott on appearing in OT. Contact Wyldthing in game on how you can find out more about GridStreams stance on this matter and help them out.

I would like to thank Shadowrunrr for the awesome show the other night. Was a blast. A special huggle goes out to The Mockers who came and provided Showrunrr and the party security! There were smurfs everywhere! I cannot thank The Mockers enough for there support they give us when in need. Have you hugged your local Mocker today?!

Devils Advocate wishes to extend Garrisen a heartfelt good-bye. You will be missed. It is always sad to see someone leave our world. Hopefully one day you will return. Anarchy Online will not be the same without you.

We hope that all these horrendous lies and plotting and the never ending rumor mill comes to a close soon. Devils Advocate has always, will always be about great parties and having fun.

Often Immated, never duplicated. That's us!



**************************************************


Omni Entertainment, Rubi Ka (June 25, 29476) - Devils Advocate is pleased to announce that Agent "Garrisen" has contracted the guild to host a party for him at Baboons Nightclub this upcoming Saturday evening, June 29 at 8 PM EST. DJ Tarryk of GridStream Productions will be spinning his famous mix on LTK Deviant Radio and Nano-Lust will be on hand as professional wait staff.

"I hope for it to be a great celebration for all of Omni. With Diamondcut... now I feel that we have dealt a blow to the criminal ring inside of Omni," explained Agent "Garrisen". So, I feel a party has been earned by all of Omni and I hope that everyone comes out and has a great time."

Agent "Garrisen" led the arresting team that captured Dave "Diamondcut" Duster and "Insomnias" Shades at Reets Retreat.

"I do not see it as my party," continued the agent, "I see it as a party for all of Omni. Sometimes we forget that there is more to life than war."

Said Devils Advocate's president Thyme, "Mr. Gary and I don't always see eye to eye, but it is our job here in Devils Advocate to make sure anyone that wants an outstanding party gets it. Hey - even tuff guys get to boogie! We are tickled pink about a new drink that is being served exclusivly at Baboons! And, we are very excited that Nano Lust will be on hand offering up these cocktails that can't be found at any other nightclub on Rubi Ka! This party is going to be a blast and I hope to see everyone there with their booties wiggling!!"

GridStream DJs will also be performing at Baboons on the following dates:

Tuesday, June 26th: DJ Shadowrunrr
Monday, July 1st: DJ Tarryk
Friday, July 12th: DJ Shadowrunrr
Thursday, July 18th: DJ Boco

Tarryk's shows all start at 8pm (EST)
Shadowrunrr's shows all start at 10pm (EST)
Boco's shows all start at 8pm (EST)

Baboons Nightclub is located in Omni Entertainment and is easily accessible from the Grid:
  • From the Grid drop off in Omni Entertainment, go directly north to the center of the city where the Arena is.
  • Go around the Arena to the north side of it where the Mongol Meat stand is.
  • Take the road near the Mongol Meat stand that goes NE. There's a sign over it that says "Weird'N Wonderful."
  • Follow the road to the end and you'll run right into Baboons.
