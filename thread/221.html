Some things I'd like to see in future patches:
1. Ability to adjust the Aggression/Defense slider value via macro.

2. Ability to filter out all non-group/non-personal attacks, nanos, etc. As in, if I'm in a group, I only care about the attack effects that my group and me produce. I'd like to filter out the rest.

3. Ability to adjust via a slider the transparency of the chat window. 0% to 100% (opaque to clear).

4. I'd like the option to see my parry and other successful defense messages in the chat window.

5. Ability to alter the chat text colors.

6. Things to fit into the shoulder slot.

7. An in-depth explanation of all the graphical options such as "Environmental Effects" and "Other Effects". What exactly do those encompass?

Thanks.
