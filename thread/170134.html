Why Don't More Enforcers Use...
Okay, now my "main" isn't an Enforcer but I have quite a good guildie Enforcer that I "mimic" with my setup, and I'm a One Trox Wrecking Crew because of it. I've teamed with quite a few Enforcers in my time and I ask these questions :

Why Don't More Enforcers...

A. Use Improved Tango Dirks?

B. Use Challenger Constantly In Missions?

A combination of A+B+C is an enforcer that up to level 185 (EQB) will outdamage ANY 2HE, 2HB, 1HB and 1HE at least 15-30 levels above them. How do I know this? Because I've teamed with all those kinds of Enforcers in "Severe Twink Mode" and I have seen the results for myself. I'm not talking about "Uber Twinking" either, you can have your basic implant setup and still be the king of the hill with Improved Tango Dirks.

Now here are the reasons I can come up with "not" to use the two items above...

------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Why Don't More Enforcers... Use Improved Tango Dirks?

A-1. They Simply don't know about them. Well, let's get the word out then, because if you want to be the best Enforcer you can be WITHOUT an EQB, this is the way to do it. ITD's outdamage all other Enforcer weapons up until level 185 (Yes even the "beloved" Queen Blade), so why not be the best you can be?

A-2. They just don't "want" to use those silly butter knives. My answer to that is you can call them butterknives all you want, but once you step in the ring, they speak for themselves. If you just don't want to use them because you think your swords look cool or you like that silly old beam that is so two years ago, that's cool, I can deal with that but don't try to come up with wacky excuses why a ITD Enforcer 20 levels below you is outdamaging you by a ton.

A-3. They don't want to waste IP on Multiple-Melee Weapons. It's a green skill, get over it. With ITDs you don't have to poor craploads of IP into Melee Initiative to do decent damage so the IP is an offset really.

A-4. They think they can't afford them. Look, I have a level 200 Trader and I have had all of 5 or 6 non-guildie Enforcers ask me if I can check the nearest Trader Shops for Tangos, and if I could improve them for them. I help EVERY SINGLE ENFORCER or any other profession that asks me to check the Trader Shop if I am in town. It takes all of two seconds and I know most of the Traders out there don't mind. Tangos are DIRT CHEAP compared to most weapons because you can buy them in the store up until QL149 and most high level Traders have at least 1k Comp Lit. Since you can buy them in stores that means prices aren't jacked up like other in-game weapons like X-3's and Manex. As long as you tip the Trader decently (roughly 10k per QL) for improving your tangos they will be more then happy to go back in the future for you. Tired of asking Traders to check the shop for you? Create a twink to do it for you. Regardless of how you do it, Traders are everywhere and I'm sure everyone knows one or two.

A-5. They want to use them, but don't want to use a IP Reset. Unless you've been crazy and switched weapon types everytime a new one came around you should have plenty of IP Reset Points. If you've used up 8 IP Resets by level 100, just reroll because you've severly gimped your character... and I think we all know it takes very little effort to get to level 100 now. From the time you hit the Title Level 5 Cap (level 162) to level 185, you'll have plenty of IP to spare to raise for the Enhanced Queen Blade so it doesn't even require a reset really.

A-6. They like all of the fancy 2HE weapons added recently to ToTW and IS. I'll say the same thing here that I did in A-2, If you just don't want to use them because you think your swords look cool or you like that silly old beam that is so two years ago, that's cool, I can deal with that but don't try to come up with wacky excuses why a ITD Enforcer 20 levels below you is outdamaging you by a ton. A 2HE will be outdamaged by a decent Piercing Enforcer all day long.

A-Conclusion : Well those are some of the "main points" I considered when I pondered why the hell more enforcers don't user Improved Dango Dirks as their weapons of choice. The funny thing is 95% of the Enforcers I've teamed with have all said they know that Tangos do better damage, but they choose another weapon. There is no doubt in the Enforcer community that it is the Enforcer's job in a team to be the Meatshield AND the Main Damage dealer... So shouldn't Enforcers use the best weapons then can?

I understand and respect the fact some people just "want" to use certain weapons, and it's different strokes for different folks... I'm just trying to get the word out about Tangos here, and for some reason I think that it's more about Enforcers not knowing then anything else. The Enforcer community should embrace the best damage in game and ITDs give that to you, but I run around all day long in Omni-Tek territory and rarely see ITD Enforcers, and it boggles my mind.

Notes: If you can afford it, and equip it a Slayerdroid Crystal Claw/Improved Tango Dirk combo is supreme until you get the beloved Enhanced Queen Blade. If you can't afford it, who cares dual ITDs are still better then anything else pre-185.
------------------------------------------------------------------------


------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. Why Don't More Enforcers... Use Challenger Constantly In Missions?

B-1. They don't have enouch NCU. There are so many ways around this one. Team with a Fixer or cancel "less important" buffs to make room. The Challenger line adds so much more then your basic outside buffs like Dirty Fighter and shields. It's basically a constant Wrangle WITH damage adds. In my opinion this is more important then using your best Essence and Reflect, it makes you the best enforcer you can be. As an Enforcer your job is to take the beating and deal the most damage, Challenger is your best buff for doing just that. If you don't have enough NCU for your best Challenger use the one below it then, any is better then none!

B-2. They don't have enough Nanopool. This is a problem for some Atrox Enforcers at lower levels, but yet another reason that couldn't hold up in court. Ask some of your support members in the team to use Simple Nano Stims on you istead of their lousy First Aid Stims that they are always slapping you with anyway. Tell them that 250 Nano is a LOT more important then 250 HP considering you have five to six times more HP then Nano Pool. Have your teammates refill your nano for you after fights if you can't handle it on your own. Get HE before going to missions if no NT is in the team if you can't handle the recharging on your own. It doesn't take a crapload of IP into Nano Pool and Time & Space to be successful at this. At level 120 I am using QL70 Nano Rechargers and Nano Stims and I find enough nano to keep Challenger, Mongo, AND use Rage as much as possible. With the NanoPool adding armors out there today you will have to spend just about no-IP in it. Carbonum Armor(s) and Mantis are the best for their NanoPool adds until you can get into the NODROPs like Azure.

B-3. They want to keep Mongo, Stare, Scare or Rage "Ready" incase things get ugly. Well my response to this one is, if your Doc or other frail teammates grab aggro from adds Mongo RARELY pulls those adds off of your teammates, meanwhile if you are dealing the best damage in the team the adds recognize that and start attacking you instead. The nanolock time on Challenger is only 15 seconds, which is far from an eternity and I usually do it after every 2-3 fights instead of during fights so when I start a new fight the nano-lock is almost gone anyways. In a 120 levels I have yet to be in a team where members died simply because I couldn't mongo or use a stare. If you use Challenger at the right time (before you go into a fight) then before things get ugly your nano-lock will be gone.

B-4. They are just too damned lazy. If so, then why the hell did you ever become an Enforcer? You have to be the first one in battle every time, you lead the team so if you are a lazy player then in my opinion this profession just isn't for you. The only two jobs in a team that people notice when they go afk for a second or two is Tank and Healer. If you just want to be an Enforcer and not a tank that leads, then you better let the rest of Rubi-Ka know first, because they are all going to expect you to be the tank.

B-Conclusion : Whatever your excuse (yes excuse) is for not running Challenger constantly in missions you need to find a way around it. Funcom has given Enforcers no reason NOT to use Challenger. It's not a huge NCU hog, it doesn't cost ungodly ammounts of NanoPool and it adds a tremendous ammount of power to the Enforcer. There is really no excuse other then pure laziness for not using it when you get down to it... So put a little thought into your gameplay and you'll see easily that you can pull off running Challenger constantly. The faster the mobs go down, the more XP you'll get in the end.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The point of this topic isn't to say that Enforcers that don't "Use the best weapon" or "the best skills" suck, or that I'm better then them... I basically just want to see if my assumptions about the reasons most enforcers don't do the things listed above are true. Now my Enforcer isn't my main but I wish he was, and one day he will be it's just a matter of levels at this point. Enforcers are the strongest profession in game, and I just want to know why most don't use the best strengths they have to be better is all.

Hopefully this post doesn't come off as "I'm all-knowing" because my Enforcer is far from super-experienced, but I have mimiced other very successful high level Enforcers in my Guild and am hopefully following in their big ugly Trox footsteps all the way to greatness.
