The most important roleplaying feature!!!
The ability to write notes and pass them around is incredibly important, and would expand the roleplaying possibilities of this game overnight.

There is very little roleplaying content in this game, and the roleplayers in AO have been doing their best with what they have been given, but when it comes right down to it, Funcom is much more interested in providing a well balanced combat-oriented game. I am not criticizing them for this, but roleplaying is the one feature of this game that could transform it into an exceptionally fun game.

With this feature, individual players could implement their own roleplaying without having to wait for Funcom to add new content. The creativity of the roleplaying community would be released!!!

Please don't overlook this Funcom. Implement this feature at any cost .. it is critical to the roleplaying community!

- Szentisha
