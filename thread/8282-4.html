My last word...
My last word on this subject.....woot if you like

After reading about every single post in this thread, I have come to the following conclusions....

More people want an IP reset than do not.
It has good and bad points.
It really has to happen like it or not.

What I would suggest, to stop the nerfing of new chars that would have no benefit to an IP reset (at this time) is this....

Every existing and new character gets a ticket, and ONLY 1 ticket. That ticket can be handed in at any time in that characters career for a full (excluding maps) IP reset.

So a char who does not need or wish an ip reset now, can save the ticket for if they ever do.

Thinking about things, the little booths in the stores, the Shop of Fortunes, could be for this reason, any level 40+ char can go buy a ticket (only 1) and use it to get a full IP reset.

This method, I think I would be happy with, as it would put everyone on an even footing. Also, if FC nerf any more skills, they can just issue more tickets.

It would be left up to the player 'when' to do his/her reset, if they are wise, they would leave it until their char is either definitly gimped, or they really just fancy a change

Anyhow, that's just my view

Rastus-SD
Leet
Consider, and this is a big consideration, how differently you would spend your IP if you got them in one lump sum as opposed to slowly, over time...

At lower levels it is sometimes necessary/helpful to take certain skills that may be rendered useless to you later in the game when your other skills/nano's become a far more powerful alternative to the given skills. Your 'drip feed' of IP at each levels forces you to make decisions about where to spend them.

Given the same amount of IP to spend in one lump sum, your spending patterns will be significantly different, allowing you to ignore skills your character -required- to make the progress he did because they are no longer of use to you now.

That's a basic flaw that will give high level characters who reset a -big- advantage over characters who choose not to reset or, indeed, future characters who progress as per the norm.

Example - At level 10 you get an item that requires certain skills, so you boost those skills. At level 100 that item is completely worthless to you and you rarely ever use the skill in question. You IP reset, still at level 100, and spend the points you 'wasted' in your initial progress up the levels on a completely different skill, leaving the skill you had/used at level 10 at 0. That's a fundamental problem with an IP reset that -will- unbalance the game.

Also, IMHO, anyone who quits/abandons a character because it is 'gimped' or 'unplayable' is failing to understand the concept of 'the game'. Evolution. Lose the nazi idealism of perfection and LEARN.

And finally...

If skills have been 'nerfed' then what is needed is some work to correct the over compensation that originally nerfed the skill, NOT an IP reset. An IP reset to fix game imbalances would be like melting the polar icecaps to prevent ships from hitting icebergs.

Use some common sense, people. *boggles*
