Meeting: Newland Guards Initiative @ 20:00 GMT, Oct 11th
Event: NGI - Public Meeting
Time: 20:00 GMT(13:00 PDT)
Date: Saturday, Oct 11
Location: Reet's Retreat - Fireplace room

The event is open to everyone. Side is not an issue in this matter that affects the prosperity of all on Rubi-Ka.

This will be a public meeting for all people and groups interested in supporting the Newland Guards Initiative(NGI). Details of this can be found in a previous thread on the grid forums. We are raising awareness on the issue and have over 100 million credits pledged from generous supporters towards the cause.

General outline of the meeting will be as follows. I will be moderateing the meeting.
  • Opening Ceremonies
  • Explaination of NGI
  • Disscussion
  • Creating a list of peole pledgeing credits.(No money will be collected at this time until the Newland City Council acknowledges.)
  • Closing Ceremonies


There is no dress code and everyone is encouraged to come as they are.

Regards,
Administrator Charles 'Kithrak' Houston
Red Tape, Omni-Admin

P.S. - As always, I can be contacted in the days prior to the meeting if there is an issue that needs disscussing or need clarification.

[OOC Notes] This event will be on Atlantean - RK1[/OOC Notes]

The meeting was pushed forward and Xnemth selected the representatives. And yes they are representatives. I spoke with Pyromanche just today and she said she's organizing a neutral leaders meeting for ALL neutral organizations to be represented at where she will primarily listen and take notes to present to the council. I think Jereziah fears that Pyromanche was givin some kind of extraordinary power over the neutrals. In fact she was simply given the job to meet with us and relay our information to the Newland City Council regarding the Neutral Guard Initiative. The previous situation was to have kithrak do it alone but that was rejected by many people there as being viewed as possibly biased. Therefore Xnemth chose a member from each faction and one neutral member to make up the 3 person committee. The basis of the Neutral guards initiative is still the same. The terms EO agreed to are still the same. Elite Operations stated that if we trained neutral aligned guards that where strong enough to defend newland city, they would be in favor of this. Not that it's Elite Operation's decision, thier role in the mercs was only to assist the Newland City council hire mercenaries.

Jereziah's objection to Pyromanche being on the council smacks more of a personal dispute between the two of them dating far back in time rather than any real issue at stake in these proceedings. *EDIT: Misquoted Jereziah here, all apologies*. In fact Elite Operations was fine and dandy with all of the proceedings until the point when Xnemth selected Pyromanche to help in the committee. Sumokan did petition for a non guilded neutral to represent all of the neutrals but this could not be accomplished because non attended.

If there is any problems with anyone of the representatives on the committee there are two others you may go to. I'm sure either Kzak or Kithrak will gladly listen to anyone's concerns no matter what side they are. Simply ask them to represent your wishes should pyromanche be unsuitable to you.

However I see no reason that Pyromanche won't make a great unbiased neutral, neutral representative. She is not as anti mercs as Independant Rubikians are, and not as pro mercs as Elite Operations. She is however in suppor of the idea of making sure neutrals are protected and stay independant. This is why I think she makes an excellent representative for the NGI committee.
