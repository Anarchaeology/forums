Actually, it's not the ithaca per se, it is any weapon that requires a SINGLE SKILL TO USE still having great damage or crits. Whis is this bad?

1. Your base AMS is higher because it is 100% based on a single skill. You max that skill you are in good shape. Weapons that are 66%/33% should have higher damage because you ahve to max two skills to have a similar AMS.

2. Raising a single skill for weapons that do more damage than a similar QL weapon that uses 4 skills is grossly unbalancing. Weapons that require Pistol, Aimed Shot, Burst, Flingshot and SMG should do more damage that just a Pistol. This is in ADDITION to the damage you receive from AS, Burst, and FS. I am sacrificing AMS and leaving my self open to being dequipped (in 14.2) in order to wield this gun. The benefit of AS/Burst/FS do NOT make up for this. Increase all-around damage of such weapons and nerf Burst/FS a bit if you have to; that would be better than the current system where a SINGLE SKILL weapon rules the roost.

In short, a Soldier should be able to outgun a trader. If the trader can debuff him fast enough, and get good hp drains going, then who knows. But weapon vs. weapon, the trader should be a spot on the floor.
