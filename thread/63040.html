Dual logins same account
I think the fatal flaw in the arguement about twinkage and dual accounts is Guilds and muleing.

If you have not received a tell saying "can you spair 2 minutes to hold something, i will give you XXXX creds" then you are in the extreme minority. Obviously other people out there are doing it.

Guilds are a structure that promotes this behavior. It would be quite interesting to get a history on some of the items in some guilds because they have been passed around more than the village bike.

Because banning dual logging is akin to handing out speeding tickets at a racetrack, why not just allow it, and recoup the revenue.

If people want to ruin gameplay then let them, its their own experience anyway, and these people would be unlikely to stay longer if the rule was deeply enforced. One possible way to enforce things, that has not been done, is that items cannot be traded back a previous owner. Obviously FC has not taken this route, so this is not their greatest concern.

But back to answering the question raised by the initiator, I feel that a dual logging with revenue recoup feature would have little substantive value than the way it is now. Those who would use the feature have already gotten multiple accounts.

I do feel this should have been something more thought out and implimented before release though.
