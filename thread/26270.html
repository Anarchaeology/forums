The real issues (from one player's perspective)
Hello citizens of Rubi-Ka,

This post reflects my views only, but it is hoped that everyone who experiences what I have in the past few months will post their own views following this one.

For the past while, I have read certain articles from Funcom talking about how AO was awarded "Best MMORPG" and another mentioning the upcoming expansion pack. These posts are all well and good, however Funcom seems to be avoiding the big issue. Why should I be paying money for such an unstable game? I give Funcom a wide berth because running a MMORPG is no small task, but my patience is wearing very thin.

I did manage to solve the continuous lock-ups I was experiencing, without any help from tech-support I might add, but I still fail to see why I was only experiencing this problem in AO and AO alone. All of my other games run perfectly on my system. This does, however, leave a large number of issues unresolved. Some of these are as follows:

Very high lag inside buildings compared to outdoors, failure to zone when I reach a zone boundary, failure to morph back into my human-form when I no longer want to use my Yalm and the large delays I experience when I want to use special attacks or attack other targets in general. The first problem has been going on for a long time now, while the other three have popped up within the last few weeks. There are other issues as well, but I do not intend for this post to be a "whining post". Do not misunderstand the point of this post; I simply want justification for me spending my hard-earned money each month and I want Funcom to tell me why they do not let their customers know that they are aware of the problems and what they are doing to fix them.

AO is my first MMORPG and I still do like the game, but I can promise that I am strongly considering closing my account and switching to another game. I do not want to do this as I have invested a lot of time and have made a lot of friends through AO, but as afore mentioned, my patience is wearing very thin.

Please, fellow players, post follow-ups to this message and voice your opinions and concerns with AO.

Thank you for reading and happy hunting,
