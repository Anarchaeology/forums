player-controlled structures in grid...
Has anyone ever read the book, "Snow Crash"? GREAT book if you have not had the opportunity, yet.

I was thinking about it the other day, and wouldn't it be COOL to be able to do some of the stuff that was done in that book (from a virtual perspective of course)?

For example, fixers (or whomever) could be given a new line of abilities to construct objects within an enlarged (or entirely separate) "grid". Player-owned housing, shops, vending machines, guild houses... Because it is in the grid, people with uber comp lit skills might even be able to hack into certain things and steal, spy, etc... It'd be a quasi virtual world in the grid, which would be totally different than every other game's real-world player owned housing, etc. More possibilities in cyberspace...

Why do something like this? Players seem to be clamoring for a way to leave a persistent mark in the game environment, which personalizes it and makes it "theirs", so to speak.
