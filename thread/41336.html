Community answers - part 5
A little information for you all.

Patch information
We get asked for info about the next patch every day now. We have an official patch info thread which will be updated as soon as we have new information about this.

Trade skill requirements
The requirements to make the highest quality level items in the game are so high that players will need to specialize to make them, even swapping implants several times if they want to make them all by themselves. This is just as we want it. The highest QL items should take the best skills and tweaks to make.

One of the nano crystals is reported as impossible to make. A bug report has been filed on this one.

Looting messages
In the works, and has been for a little while. I just didn't find the bug report last time I searched (so I filed a new one) as it had a 'weird' topic line.

No ETA though.

Healing vs Damage in PvP
No plans to change this it this time. Changing healing in PvP is a lot of work (way more than changing damage), and the damage cap will not be lifted, as we want PvP fights to last more than a couple of seconds (yes, I know about "Alpha strikes" ).

Nano resistance
No plans to change the base chance of resisting nano formulas.

Apartments
No ETA on any apartment upgrades, like new apartments and such. We're looking into whether it would be easy to increase the item limit though.

---

That's what I have for now.
