I fully agree with ya Stromm. PvP needs help and so does the game.

The whole "supression gas" aspect of AO causes problems in zones where you have 75% & 25% right next to each other. I say rework the gas layout to adhere to the following guidelines:

In no zone shall there be a mixture of gases.

The only 100% zones should be: NLC, Tir/Athen, Omni 1-Ent / Trade, and the various nubi pits. All other zones should be 25% or 0%. 75% should be for missions only.

The level restrictions of PvP do not make sense either, particularly when it comes to once you hit level 75. The 'logical' level spreads previous to 75 should run up until 150. Only after you hit 150, should you be open to anyone above you...not at level 75. Either that, or make it completely open (any level can fight any level) with severe negative effects (loss of XP) for killing someone more than 20 levels lower than yourself.
