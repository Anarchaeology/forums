Petition to UnNerf PVE Stun
There were obviously issues with Stun in PVP. Some seem to feel it's because nano resist doesn't seem to be effective. Other's think it would be over-powered in any case.

However, 13.7 overnerfs stun for both PVP *and* PVE, and makes it so that stun is rarely useful in PVE (I can't speak for all classes here -- feel free to chime in with your opinions about your class' stun capabilities in PVE).

You (FC) need to review this decision. If balancing for PVP is too difficult / time consuming right now, then simply disable it for PVP (chech out *your* polls -- 3.72% voted for PVP). Balance it properly for PVE so it is useful for 96.28% of your player base.

You worked hard to fix stun. It obviously met the quality bar of a bug that needed to be fixed. It would certainly give your players (and your project team) satisfaction if it was properly fixed, instead of being left in the useless state it is now.


Regards,
