Funcom: Remember the Martial Artists
From character creation and the manual, here is the 'definition' of a Martial Artist:

When it comes to dishing out raw combat damage a Martial Artist outshines all other professions. Fighting unarmed, the Martial Artist's main strength lies in special attacks, knowing how to cripple opponents by attacking their weak spots. The Martial Artist is also a very proficient healer, truly surpassed by only the Doctor and the Adventurer.

The Martial Artist is a spiritual warrior, mastering the silent bow and unarmed combat. Some Martial Artists may even decide to follow their path to illumination with melee weapons as an aid. His focus is absolute, making him a deadly foe, especially when he puts his arsenal of nano programs to proper use. However, the same focus makes the Martial Artist pay dearly in all other fields.

MA's can be summarized as this:

pvm: supposedly third best healer and best dot profession.

The gap between adventurers (second best healer) and ma healing abilities is enormous. Once you step outside of BS team missions the difference becomes very clear. I'll do the nr crunching on it some day but I'm fairly convinced that when it comes to selfhealing ma's actually rank about on par w an mp.

Best dot profession: nowhere near it atm. after parry fix I'd expect us to do relatively better again though.

Enforcers will still outdamage us and they aren't supposed to. Pet classes may outdamage us as well but I can kindof live with that. I have a very hard time accepting enforcers outdamaging us as they already have a ton more hp; much better nano's aso.

The big problem as a dot profession for ma's is that fc seems to have developped a tendency the last months to introduce distinctly better weapons w every patch, which an sich isn't a bad thing, but it seems that the 'ma weapon' (fists) and the place of ma's as a damage dealing profession gets forgotten when they do that.

Tanking abilities against the uber mobs: non existant. the ma role as a tank ends once you did your last BS team mission.

pvp: same issues mostly.

* short range, don't have the hp or evades or heals to go into 'enemy lines' w even a slight chance of survival. I hate the fact that towers can't be critted as well. Basically reduces hays damage to a tower to the damage a level 50 char of any other profession would do against them.

* no reliable defense against roots

To me it seems that the ma role as a (secondary) tank is over in FC's mind. Gaute's interview on gamespy pointed in that direction as well: enforcers will be the only tanks left in shadowlands. I'm not happy about that, especially as somehow FC seems to have gotten the idea that enforcers need to do tons of damage to do that as well. I'd rather have their taunts upgraded a lot.

Our heals need improvement but shouldn't be the main focus as then we would basically be a melee adventurer wo morphs.

A damage boost is needed badly imo. If we were to judge from the profession descriptions MA's should do distinctly more damage than enforcers.

There's a problem here though as I don't really think it is realistical that they would upgrade our fists that much that we would start outdamaging eqb enforcers by a wide margin. Mostly because the eqb already is overpowered when it comes out to dealing damage.

A weapon that can just punch right through tms and kill is too much, I doubt FC would give us a way to do even more damage than that. I'll leave it to fc to sort out that mess Things like fists draining nanopool of the opponent in pvp, or adding some specials like sneak/fast attack to fists would be a nice way to circumvent that for pvp at least I suppose.

Giving us a very high evade buff would help as well, but personally I like offensive play much better than defensive play and to me it always seemed as if the ma was mostly an aggressive/offensive class.

Besides that the ar's of the various professions vary so wildly that it would be difficult to balance. Ie it should be good enough as to be usefull against agents/enforcers/... w uber ar's while at the same time not be overpowering vs the profs with much lower ar's like the pet profs. Think that could be tricky to pull off.

Or wait, just up our damage and heals a bit and give rage to ma's. Enforcers can have WUS instead I'd be very happy w that
