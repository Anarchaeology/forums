Trader - in need of some fixing??
Well, I have been playing my Trader character since day 1 and I am damned happy that I finally is reaching lvl 100...
But I still think that the profession needs some fixing...or is it just me?

I'm quite happy about the weapons situation - Shotguns rules!
Wouldn't trade my good ol' trusty Vektor for a fancy pancy Nova Flow! I could get annoyed at the dark blue Ranged Init., Dual Wield, FlingShot and Burst...but something has to be costly, that I have realized or else it would just be too easy...

But besides that...it doesnt look too good...

I got a bunch of Trade Skills that can be used for...yeah, I really dont know...There isnt one thing that promotes investments in these skills besides Comp Lit and Nano Programming...
I can create a few unique items, the rest can be bought cheap in stores or in trade channels...its first after QL125 it could get interesting, but heck, there are so many high levels that gathers high level items, so it doenst even matter any more...

It would be really nice if we could create armor...Its always in demand and there is a need for all types of QLs...

And get rid of the static Trade Construction system...
It is quite boring to sit and create a battered gluegun, when U can get it thrown in your face in any store on Rubi-Ka...
Make the system dynamic...let the different Trade Items have certain abilities (like now) and let us combine these items freely - then there should be a chance for creating something new and an even bigger chance for the whole thing to explode between your hands...Yeah, I know its impossible, but if U ever is going to make AO2 - remember this!!

To conclude on Trade skills - pretty much a waste of IPs...even if U went for the QL140+ items, it would only dilute the market and hence lower the price for the items....

And now to my personal grudge in AO - the Nano lines....

Actually it isnt that much of grudge...more a nagging...
As I see it U (=Funcom) have really messed up something I found to be really vital in the game - the character unique nano lines...

Why in the name of the arid deserts of Rubi-Ka are you throwing new nanolines around like that?? Engineer get a team/single teleporport line, which in my twisted brain oughta have been a Fixer line (Since he is the Master of the Grid - read your own description) and only Ross knows whats gonna happen in the future...And why did Doctors, Traders and some more profession get Grid access nanos (Another Fixer only - and no, I dont have a Fixer character...)?
And I laughed a certain body part off, when I saw that NanoTechs had received a dodge/evade buff...Now that seems really logical that those stiffbacked bookworms suddenly can evade like an eel greased in butter...dodge/evade buffs oughta be MA only...
Theres too much overlap between the different characters now:
Pretty much everyone have a root, AC, health, skill buff/debuff of some sort and I find that kinda lame...wheres the originality? Its like Funcom only managed to get 10-12 ideas for nanos and hence they decided to plaster them on to all characters and just do some small cosmetic changes and renames and bingo - the birth of hundreds of nanos...I'm rather harsh right now - its intentional - I want a reaction on this...

Oh, I do have something to b*tch about in the Trader nano lines:
1) The Mind Control line
No one in a sane state would accept to be paralyzed for 14 to 20 minutes while doing some sort of Jedi trick on a mob...not on a single mission and definetly not on a hunt/team mission!!
Besides being bugged (Cannot recast it - have to zone, mobs attacked by controlled pet aggros me and not the pet, team kills pet for fun/accident you're still locked until the time runs out...), the prize it self (Locked in position, without any abilities (fighting or nano) and only able to move after being hit by a mob (Usually means you're dead on my levels...) is way too high!
And everyone else with a pet/charm (Not including Engineer - Engineer with 3 bots is Death!) line will now or at a later state be able to have 3 pets going...and are still free to move and attack with them...The bureucrat might have a sweet tongue but I cant get into my head how he can sweet talk 2 Gargantulas into aiding him - do gargantulas speak a language we all can share??

2) Wheres my Shotgun Mastery buff? Give it to me!

Think I will stop now - I could start on my speech about Unique Character/profession Skills/Nanos and other stuff but that would require too much writing in one post...so look out for part 2 of this rant in the coming days...

Oh, and just to point it out - I do like AO - or else I wouldn't be playing it...

Inquina
Trader lvl97 Dimension 1
