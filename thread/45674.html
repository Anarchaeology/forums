An idea to promote team PvP
- Make an indoor playfield like, say, Camelot.

- Make the whole playfield 25%.

- Put a badass mob in there with something like the difficulty of Tarasque. Basically, something that takes a while to kill.

- Make the dungeon ONE playfield only with a limit of say, 60 players.

- When the body of the boss mob is looted, ALL members of the winning faction (clan, omni or neutral) currently within the playfield gets a temporary skill bonus of, say, +100 AMS with a 10 hour duration, much like the shadow rifts in solo missions.

This would give EVERYONE a reward for fighting, and wouldn't cause the tension that arises when a lot of people fight over a small amount of loot.

Just an idea
