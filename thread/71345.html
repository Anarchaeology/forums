Here are some ideas I thought of:

Biologist:

The biologist is a scientist who is specially suited to understand life. Nano lines include healing, body modification (such as ability boosts and runbuffs), biomet buffs, and the ability to make permanent pets out of non-human animals (i.e., there is no time limit on charms). Their tradeskill specializations include pharma tech and chemistry.


Cyber Warrior:

The cyber warrior is someone who uses implants, computers, and other technology to be a different kind of soldier. While they are suited to manipulate nanotech, in the end the nanotech that they use is geared to improve their combat. Nano lines include those that buff AC, evades, concealment, and perception. I'll try to think of more for this.


Veterinarian:

The vet is someone who is able to control and manipulate animals. Thus they can permanently charm non-human creatures, heal them, buff them, and do other things. They can also buff any human player that has been polymorphed into an animal, such as the adventurer in sabretooth form.


Synnoiont:

This term was coined by the author Poul Anderson (that's the only place I've seen it, at least). A synnoiont is someone who is specially suited to communicate with machines and computers, to the extent that much of their time is spent in a virtual cyberspace, where the rules of perception and information flow are different. This ability to communicate well with the machine world is what gives the synnoiont his advantage. Typically a synnoiont has high intelligence. Nano lines include the ability to get into the normal grid and fixer grid and intelligence buffs.


Android:

This is not actually a human at all, but a robot. Actually, I wish they would add this (or some other sort of robot, or maybe a cyborg) as a new breed. Maybe they will some day.


One thing that makes visualizing new professions hard is that the 12 professions already cover a wide spectrum of combat and play styles. One addition I can think of is that the new professions have special characteristics and/or abilities, such as the ability to wield both a gun and melee weapon at the same time, or the ability to cast two nanoprograms at the same time.



Just as a fun exercise, I'll probably modify/update these speculations as time goes on, just to see how close I am to the eventual professions! Unfortunately, my account expires on New Years Day...for good.
