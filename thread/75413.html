Toolbelt
With the introduction of the root/snare reducing items and the new health/nano items I suddenly find that my inventory is even more crowded than it was before. Rather than requesting an increase to the size of my inventory (although that'd work too), I offer the suggestion of a tool belt. Make it 10 slots like the toolbar, only it actually holds the items, not just a reference. I don't believe the ctrl+0-9 hot keys are used for anything yet, so use those for the new tool belt. Let us put stims, rechargers, lockpicks and anything else 'toolish' in it and free up some space in our inventories for, well... inventory!
