Bows arn't expensive
I use a ql 200 urban crossbow. I'd like to point out that while 400 credits for 20 ammo ammo may seem like a lot it actually is absolutely nothing. Anyone at or over level 75 has an easy source of income from blitzing missions and selling suits of armor that there is no need to worry about it anymore. Bows are meant for the 'elegant' rich hunter and as such should not be easily affordable by lazy newcomers otherwise they will become tacky.

Leave the arrow price as they are so those of us who want to spend the time and money for these quality pieces of equipment can keep our style and not become just another conformer in the equipment market.

On a side note the same holds true for granade launchers, except there are NO good granade launchers to make up for the money cost. They need to have a massive damage increase, and if they are going to continue to be one-two shots only then they should be an AoE shot.

Bows should get an overhaul though, there are only two or three bows that are actually usable since Bow Spec is a total waste of IP. Not to mention the hybrid bows have burst, fling, fullauto, bow spec and about three weapon reqs on top of that. What went through the developer's mind there? Anyways...LEAVE THE ARROW PRICES BE.

-Pyrothos, 77th level Hunter.
