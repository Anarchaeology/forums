FYI
I have investigated and observed the Rhinomen extensively.

Rhinomen

They are a mutant breed first bred in Omni-Tek laboratories then outcast into Varmint Woods when the experiment was abandoned. When rhinomen communities started to flourish all over Varmint Woods, Omni-Tek was appalled and hired poachers to trim their population.

The Clan traders in Varmint Woods, in support of the Eco Warriors, started supplying weapons to the rhinomen so they could defend themselves. The poachers, hired by Omni, will tell you a different story, of course - according to them the usually docile rhinomen have become a hazard as they have been supplied weapons by rebel bands eager to spread their violent ways.

Notwithstanding the propaganda surrounding the poachers and the traders in VW - the anthropological fact remains that the rhinomen are a gregarious breed with a highly developed and structured social hierachy. They have continued to flourish and indeed beyond VW into other zones.
