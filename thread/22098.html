Maybe you aren't supposed to be able to dual wield them. :-p

The way off hand weapons almost ALWAYS work in RPGs is that the offhand is much less powerful and less likely to hit than the main weapon... the way AO delas with this is by making you use a lower ql offhand.

So, say you can use a ql200 gun. The multiwield reqs are too high for you to use 2 of them, so you have to use a lower ql one in the offhand (say, ql150). You don't do 2x damage (using 2 of the same kind of gun would pretty much double your damage output - or double the bonus to skills on some guns), but you still do around 1.5x as much for raising the multi skill.

It balances out.

It's not true. You *will* and always will do Double damage when you dual weild. You don't lose any round.

These are the myths that cause people to think this.

1) Your first shot goes off immediatly when you press Q. A slower main hand weapons therefor goes first, which is weirld.

2) You cannot fire twice at the same time. This is true but your offhand waits in limbo for the main to fire, and your offhand will immediatly fire afterward. From the first shot on, your attack bars are offset (if you have the same speed weapon) so that your main hand fires, and your offhand fires while the main recharges. The minimum time between shots is 2 seconds, which is ample time for another weapon to fire.

3) "Phantom Shots" make people think they lose damage rounds. They don't, although I wish they would fix Phantom Shots.

I've tested these damages extensively. When I dual weild a same qlvl gun (for example my Customized Reet) I do roughly 2x damage (I only get specials on main hand).
