I mostly agree, with the following exception: Just because we're the only political group (face it: by opting out you made a political decision) whose major population centers have faulty suppression gas generators does not mean that we should have tougher guards.

I agree with your other two suggestions.

Neut Guards should assist Neut humanoid mobs. Similarly Omni Guards should assist Omni humanoid mobs, and Clan Guards should assist Clan humanoid mobs.

People should be able to buff and heal mobs of their own political affiliation. Why shouldn't an Omni doctor be able to heal a "fresh OT laseraim" out in the field? This could potentiallly be abused and cause tensions in popular hunting grounds with humanoid mobs, but we are living in tense times. Besides, if I see some guy blasting away at the guards in Newland, then ducking out into the general store when things get hairy, I'd like that guard to have full health when he comes back out.

I would like to add two more suggestions:

If players cannot buff or heal mobs, let guards buff and heal themselves and each other, much as mobs in the missions do. If the guards are Soldiers, let them run damage shields on themselves.

Make guards remember who attacked them. The popular pastime of ducking into a shop and healing up would become much less useful if the guard were to immediately aggro when the offender comes back out. This, especially if guards were buffable/healable would allow repeat offenders in this guard-killing business become persona non grata in the towns they have been preying upon. There is no valid RP reason not to do this, unless it is a side-effect of the suppression gas. In the interest of "fairness" let the guard forget everybody who attacked it in the event that the guard is actually slain.

But mostly I'd just like to see the guards actually protect their townsfolk.
