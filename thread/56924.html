I share all of your frustrations.

Thing is, you should go in there expecting to have to outdamage another team. Go in there with some higher level damage dealers and you'll have a better chance at getting the loot. Also, learn the spawn. When I go in there, I literally have Notepad open with the spawn order. Helps a lot.

And I wouldn't consider Smuggler's Den a place where people KS. If anything, Funcom created that mess of a dungeon by themselves, and with the rarity of the drops, they can only be advocating groups fighting to outdamage each other.

It is nice, however, when you meet another team in there who agrees to split the dungeon. It means 1/2 as many mobs to loot, but saves the hard feelings, the drama and the stress from having to outdamage another team every time.

Been in there countless times, was probably very close to Peisinoe's number of hours spent in the dungeon before I got my full set (not just two pieces, ouch). The key is really to go with people who already have a full set, or close to it, so you get most of the loot.

I don't think shes grumpy at all. Shes very correct. If everything was easy to get in the game, the game would soon become stale and boring.
