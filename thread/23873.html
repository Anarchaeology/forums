I have a Dream!
I have this dream for Rubi Ka, this wonderful invention that will set us ahead by thousands of years. It is called... the light bulb. Anyone else notice just how dark Omni Entertainment is, or how hard it is to see in Tir at night? LIGHT BULBS.

My other dream, though less exciting, is a /beep command that would permit people on your Friends list to /beep you and wake you up.

Now we all have done it at least once. Been enjoying a nice mission to the to the middle of no where, when all of a sudden the sleep monster jumps out of no where and eats us. Snoring away at our computer desks, our character stands like a dork in one place for nearly four hours before we awaken to find the shape of the keyboard imbedded in our foreheads, and our character standing on the resurrection pad. Come on, admit it... you've done it too. The /beep command would not be for just any joe-shmoo to use against you, but would be reserved only for those people whom you have added to your friends list. The people you know and communicate with. The people who won't tell that you feel asleep again and needed to be awoke by ten /beeps upside the head.

Come on Funcom, /beep is your friend! I know you can impliment this one!

~Phoenx / Wyldthing
