Perhaps FunCom was being devious and the other fork the storyline could have taken would have been Sentinels out of Tir and a blanket statement of brotherly unity with the Neutrals as a whole assuming the Neutrals didn't cough up the dough. If that were the case, then this is a mistake.

I am laying odds that if the money weren't raised, the option would have been that February would be just like January except all the Twinks and item whores would be in Temple of the Three Winds and the Inner Sanctum rather than farming NCU rings in the Subway. I've been so hooked on the planet as a living evolving backdrop for us as players that I simply can't see nothing happening being preferable to something happening. If it means you all hate my character in-game, then I'm willing to accept that. If it accentuates the passion that the pacifists and holders of whatever ideologies within the Neutral community have, then good for them.

I've been victim OOC for actions me and mine have tried to take while staying IC. It's unnerving. OK, but would anyone betray their in-game character for fear of reprisal by the little 13-yr-olds who lie to their parents that they are sick so they can stay home all day and play the game? It wouldn't feel right to me.

That's why I've just about had it. Everyone's getting personal about this issue. I thought it would be fun.

If Silverstone came and blew down the walls of Newland next patch, I would think that'd be fun. Wouldn't make me go Omni, mind you (some of you still can't grasp that concept). But, the game I pay thirteen bucks a month would have some added value.

Being forced to live in caves in the Outzone huddled amongst my brothers- and sisters-in-arms? Fun.

This game won't last forever. Or at least its relevence won't. I don't remember doing anything in UO that went down in history. That's because it wasn't possible. 18 or so servers and no story. When the next patch hits, there will be something that I can point out to a newbie in Borealis Backyard 1 and say 'I was there'.
