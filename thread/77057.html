Notum Wars Revealed
Learning as we go ... edited for clarity and to reflect the corrections posted. Thanks for the input!

Guide to become an UBER tower owning guild
Ok first off there are 4 parts to the org-controlled towers.

Land
The first and foremost is your plot of land. If you visit this page here You will see a map of Rimor with a bunch of little triangles on it. Each triangle is a place where you can build a tower. (Although i have found that in some zones there are more spots then little triangles so it is not completely accurate) The outlined ones are land that has no tower and the colored ones are land that has a tower built on it all ready. (See the legend on which colors are which clan omni neut) The map is updated pretty much in real time so you can get an over all feel for whats going on in the world of towers. In addition each plot of land has a lvl range you have to actually visit the plot of land to see what lvl range the plot is. It shows up in the top left hand corner of your map when you enter one of these zones.

Towers
Once you have picked out a place to call home you must claim it by placing a tower on it. The towers can only be bought and placed by booster owners and you can find them in the tower shop. The towers come in lvl 10, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200 and 250. Each org can place a maximum of 5 towers BUT you can only place one for each lvl. Meaning you cannot place 5 lvl 100 towers. You must place 75, 100 ,150 ect....

To place the tower you must hold a ranking of Squad Commander or above. It takes a boatload of comp lit and some Psychology to place a tower. (600 comp lit and 450 psy for the ql 150 towers) The ql of the tower must be within the lvl range of your plot of land.

Another side note: The main towers don't count twords the Turrets and Gardians limit.

Turrets and Gardians (Defense towers)
Weee so now we got the plot of land and our new shinny Tower. Hmmm but it needs something .... ahh yes .... Defenses! There are two types of defence towers you can place Turrets and Guardians.

Turrets
These towers are like your big guns they shoot at your enemy. In addition to to this also provide a secondary permanent bonus to the placer. (by permanet i mean you recieve the bonus no matter where you are as long as the tower is standing) These don't however have an area affect, which i think is where allot of people are getting confused as to "why isnt my tower working?"

For example:
The Fire Sprouting Turret dosent give any area affect but it does add a nice MA, Brawl, Dimach, Riposte bonus to the placer. I know a few MA's that wouldn't mind a little bonus like that

Gardians
Gardians are towers that give area bonuses to your org or add a negative effect to any enemy AND a permanent bunus to the placer. The area bonuses work as long as you are within the towers range.

If you look at Guardian Conductor of Focus you can see where it says Personal Modifier those are the skill bonuses that are given to the placer and OnFriendlyInVicinity is the area affect that is given.

There are also special towers that do some fun stuff such as drop people out of their yalms, bump them out of sneek mode, and disabling the enemies from using normal recharge equipment.

Defence towers are placed on and around your tower to help you in battle, they have a circular range to them that defines their area of affectiveness. You can over lap them to a point so that you can receive two or three bonuses in one spot. Funcom calls these personal towers since they are placed by individual members of your org and are essentially that persons tower. They are different however from the service towers so i call them defense towers so as not to confuse the two. Members will want to set up ones with bonus are usefull to them. It seems as though there is no limit defense towers that can be placed in a plot of land as long as there is room to place them. These towers can be removed by selecting it and typing /tower terminate. Altho, the owner of the tower is not the only person that can remove the tower any member atleast one rank above the owner can remove the tower as well.


To place a Defense tower in on your orgs land you must meet the following requirements.
1- you must have the skill required to place the turret (mostly a Bucket-o-Comp lit and a dash of Psy and NP for a few of them)
2- Each person is limited on how many towers they can place according to your lvl.
>lvl 15 you cannot place turrets
lvl 16-75 you can place 1 turret
lvl 75-150 you can place 2 turrets
lvl 150+ you can place 3 turrets
3- again you also need to own the booster to place the towers

If you build it they can come
Once you have your tower placed on you plot of land everyone in your org gets the nifty feature of being able to grid right too it. There is a grid point marked controller its on the bottom floor of the grid. Stroll over it and it will pop up a box with a list of all the towers you own. Select the tower you wish to visit and viola your whisked away to your tower. Once you get there you'll have to huff it back ..... UNLESSS.... you happen to have one of these nifty devices Personal Grid Converter. They come in different QL's you will need one that is = to or greater then the QL of your tower. They come in packs of 5. If you have the booster you can buy them in the tower shop, if you dont have the booster you can get them as mission rewards or have someone with the booster buy you some.

My tower is built... now what? You wait.
Once you place it takes 18 hours for the tower to become attackable. When it first becomes attackable it turns to a 25% zone and can only be attacked by the other side. This last for 5 hours. Then after that it turns to 5% at which time it can be attacked by anyone for 1 hour. If you can hold your tower for that last hour then it turns back to 100% for another 18 hours so you can take a break to relax and go back to your regularly scheduled lvling. BUT and this is a big but .... if during the time that the tower is in its down stage one if the members in the guild attacks another tower then your tower automatically becomes attackable again for a short period of time, this seems to be about an hour.


Universal Advantages
Each tower allows us to use two org advantages. They boost your skill continuously no matter where you are. You get two advantages for each tower you own and the total ql of the advantages cannot exceed the total Ql of the towers. The advantages need to be set up by the prez. See /org help for more info on this.

To see a listing of all the advantages go here http://v020u46eff.maximumasp.com/AODB/index.asp

And search:
Universal Advantage


Few side notes:
You do not need to have the booster to use the grid features, defend the towers, or get the area bonuses from the towers. I am not sure about the org advantages.

You may also want to think about the time of day you place your tower to start because the will end up being the time that it goes live every day. Personally I don't really like the fact that once you place your towers it will become attackable everyday like clockwork at the same time, it seems to me that that could get boring after awhile. But thats just my oppinion

About the tradeskillable defense towers.
I made one the other day and here it how you do it. First you will need an item called Cloud of Polychromic Nanobots if you are planing to make a Guardian tower or Cloud of Infuriated Nanobots if you want to make a Turret. The seem to only drop from the mobs at the tower camps the clan one is in WW in the far NE corner of the zone and the omni one is in Avalon south of Camelot. I dont know the cords of the Camelot one but it is a big snow globe looking thing, you can miss it. Once you have your Cloud of Nanobots the rest of the stuff can be bought from the tower shops. Simply buy the how to book and follow the instructions. This process relies heavy on EE, Comp lit and some NP. One quick note is that part of the process requires a "Conductor Program" That only come in certain QL's and the Ql of this program defines the Ql of the Tower. Their are also defense towers that you cannot buy the Conductor Program for and you cannot buy premade. One of these is the Anti-Aircraft Pulse Turret a lovely tower that keeps people from scouting out your tower in a yalm. I'm still not sure how to make this one but i would love to know. So if anyone can find more information on this and would care to share it would be much appreciated.


About the personal towers
I tried one of the premade towers they are not that expensive but they require a bit of comp lit and ME to use. They must be outside to use, you can place then anywhere but once you place them thats where they stay.

To use your tower you use commands similar to the /pet comands.

To attack:
Select your target and type /Tower attack

To defend:
type /Tower defend

To remove your tower:
Select your tower and type /Tower Remove


To make some of these you will need a Multi-Purpose Tuner that i believe you get from the tower bases. I havent tried making any of these yet or done the quest if anyone has more infromation on this please feel free to share.

And there you have it the low down on all the towery goodness
