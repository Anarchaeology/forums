I'm a 67 soldier... a friend of mine, also a 67 soldier, ran into a 69 MA out in 2HO while Storm was rampaging around with a bunch of 150+ people. We headed out there to try and take out the MA, and we run into him... we both let out bursts and flingshots from our nova, healthbar untouched, and I cast MK shield, still hitting me for 50+ damage. His first hit was a FIVE HUNDRED damage brawl, right before I got up my shield. His health bar didn't move the entire fight, and we tried to run for the whompa because obviously something wasn't right. I died, my friend made it out. I don't know if people that are high level have any fun, but from what I've experienced as a casual player, player vs. player combat in this game is terrible. AO may have Dark Age of Camelot beat in a few ways, but I can say this for sure: DAOC beats AO's PVP hands down at any level. People whine on the DAOC boards about how poorly it is balanced, but in comparison to AO the balance is superb. I know that AO is more PvE then PvP, but seriously, if the only time you ever get any action is at 150+ when you one-shot people with an alpha-strike, no thanks. Just my rather angry thoughts.
