Tokens Merit boards and Dynamic Charecter Build A rough draft
I would make the highest merit board 100,000 and then make tokens a trade Item ones that could be bought in the store or out on the street. Tokens would be very expensive in the store like 30-50k depending on the store maybe 80k in busy towns. This would help a dying and somewhat boring marketplace for players. Also if you have clans selling to omni you could end up with some intresting results for RolePlaying The slimy sales guy with no morals the buyer who has a secret etc. One other option with merit boards is to make specific breed and profession rewards. i.e a soldier at the 30000 merit board recieves a Generals star. Only a soldier at the 30000 merit board can have this non drop item and the star would give him some special ability or be a weapon. another example an Mp at every 20000 merit board recieves a follower "similar to a pet but more like a mule. the follower would carry items for mp and also have social commands like preaching for mp. Etc etc. Increaseing the merit board levels and rewards creates excitement and diversity in the game. Also makeing tokens available for sale at high costs creates further RPG playing. This also settles the arguement of the unfair advantage now that some players have who were able to buy and sell tokens in the past and achieved the 1000 token merit board quickly.
