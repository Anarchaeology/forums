Well, it would of course depend on your alignment (OT or Clan).

But as OT, there are tons of spots to see.. The tower on the plateau in Ent gives a great view, but requires a Yalmaha to get on top of it.. Basically, with a Yalm you can see the WHOLE world without any qualms... But it's the cost of course.
See as much of Rubi Ka as you can, it's a huge world.. Travel The Longest Road, see the outpost with a toxic moat in Southern Foul Hills, Go to Newland and see the deserts (and don't talk too much to Greasy Joints, he gets mad eventually )
Find a hill and just watch the sunset/sunrise.. It's a great sight
