Once you hit higher level play 100+ one thing you like to have in your group is a Fixer. Nobody can get your asses out of a bad situation faster than a fixer.

My typical hunting group is usually this :

Enf (Tank)
Doc (Healer)
MA (Mid-High Damage with Backup Helaing)
Fixer (Mid Damage and Emergency Evac for when things go horribly wrong)

After that it gives you 2 slots for other classes depending on what you need and what your hunting.

This weekend while hunting with two fixers in our guild (We have 2 x 100+ Fixers) we required evac on 5 occassions when the hunting turned nasty either to bad pulls, super mobs [killer super meds that actually fight like level 200 mobs for a change] or when same lamer decided to train Sand Mantis Guardians through the area in an effort to kill as many people as possible.

In all occassions people were happy to be ported, it probably saved each of us in total of 3-4 million xp, that a group total saving of 18-24 million xp for a weekend.

If you go to the higher level hunting gounds you can see people saying things like "Team LF Doc & Fixer" or "Team with Doc with 1 slot open, prefer Fixer"

Fixers could be improved but your by no means unwanted or unplayable.

I think the problem is that some players under value classes like Traders & Fixers. Its groups like that I avoid playing with. Smart players and groups know the value of a good fixer or trader.
