A Rude Awakening...
The Twins suns of Rubi-Ka shown down on the desolate landscape of the northern deserts, a great sandstorm raging among the dunes masked the terrain. A Yalmaha dove swiftly into the storm as fast as its Ion engines would propel it , the pilot fighting to keep his course straight as the storm rocked the vehicle. The proximity warning beacon had gone off nearly 45 minutes ago at Garret "Valaik" Clasen's hidden warehouse in the depths of the zone nicknamed, Perpetual Wasteland. Someone was snooping around the facility.

With a low growl Valaik forced the Yalm back on course after the crosswinds threw him off course yet again, but he could see the canyon ahead of him and dove into it the moment it was beneath him. The canyon walls stopped the worst of the winds and allowed the Yalm to fly along smoothly toward the point where he would exit the canyon and make its way north to the Warehouse. As his navigation computer signaled that the exit point was approaching fast, Valaik pulled back on the control stick bringing the Yalm out of the Canyon and back into the storm. Again the storm rocked the vehicle, tossing it about as the engines fought to keep it moving above the ground, slowly the Yalm settled to the ground just north of the Canyon.

Valaik slipped on his Nano Armor helmet to help filter the sands from the air and openned the hatch of his Yalm, prefering to walk the few kilometers to the warehouse instead of flying in where anyone could hear him. He made his way to the base quickly over the uneven and shifting sands with the use of his nanobot enhanced speed, the sand pitted, rusty surface of the base barely visible through the storm. Slowly Valaik approached the base stopping on the ridge opposite the base to look for any signs of visitors, with a curse he rolled back out of sight. Standing on the sands in front of the base were three troopers, from their unique armor and equipment they could only belong to one group, The Dust Brigade.

"You've got to be kidding me, what the hell are Dusters doing at my warehouse!?", Valaik mumbled as the winds roared around him.

Crawling slowly he peered over the edge once again, watching the three closely, slowly removing a pair of binoculars to get a better look at the intruders. All three were wearing seemingly standard DB armor, but each seemed to have rank insignia that looked like more than just standard troops, one even looked like an officer.

"I hope they haven't breached security or found the back door yet.", Valaik muttered as he slide back from the hill, once again getting to his feet to jog around the adjacent dunes.

Slowly he circled the base to where the hidden entrance to his storehouse was hidden beneath the sands, the indicator in his helm pointing him directly to the location. Triggering the unlock code Valaik stepped back as the sands seemed to rush up at him, the hydraulic door pushing aside all that had collected over the entrance since he had last used it. With a smile Valaik sealed the door behind him and made his way inside, sprinting down the long hallway to the command room, as he neared the door he saw that it was intact, no sign of forced entry. Taking a deep breath he slid his SMG into his hand from the strap that held it secured to his back, his other hand moving to the scanner that would unlock the door. The scanner quickly acknowledged his handprint and unlocked the door, the door gliding smoothly along its path. As the door clicked to a stop Valaik rushed through the door, his SMG held with both hands as he scanned the room, releasing a deep sigh of relief as he saw that no one had been inside.

"They haven't got in yet, but that won't last, now I gotta get all this stuff out of here to site two. Too bad its not finished yet." he growled as he moved to the comm terminal, removing his helm and placing it on the console as he routed a communication back to Omni-1. The display flared to life filled by the image of a large, one-eyed atrox with a pink mohawk, a smile spreading on his rough features.

"Val! What can I do ya for ?", spoke the atrox with a light laugh.

"No time for fun Flat, we got a problem. I got Dusters crawling over the storehouse in PW and we need to get this stuff out of here ASAP.", barked Valaik.

"Sure thing Bossman, I'll have the carrier fired up and moving in 15 tops. I'll grab a few folks to help get the stuff aboard too. Where you planning on putting that stuff?", spoke Flatbed as he began typing a few things into a terminal on his end and waving to someone offscreen.

"Only one place we can, we're gonna have to use Site two before its ready. Land the carrier at the back door, I'll let you in. Make sure you come in the long way, don't want the neighbors knowing we're moving. See you when you get here.", Flatbed nodded as the screen went blank. Valaik hastily began packing up any loose merchandise that he saw, making sure everything was ready to go when the carrier arrived, moving things closer to the rear entrance so everything could get loaded quickly.

His comm chirped twice, signaling Flatbed had landed the Carrier behind the rear entrance, sprinting down the corridor he slid to a stop and hit the controls that would open the door. Flatbed and three other Atrox rushed in quickly, the storm still in full force outside, nodding curtly as they began to ferry the goods out to the waiting ship. Valaik watched them work for a moment then moved back along the corridor to the command room, the room was crowded wall to wall with electronics that had cost millions to assemble, growling again he moved to a panel on the far wall. Unlocking the panel he swung it open and removed two devices from it, an EM and Plasma grenade, one in each hand.

The center of the room was occupied by a large map of the Terraformed regions, slowly he placed both grenades atop it, arming them both and keying them to a certain comm frequency. He adjusted the detonator on the Plasma Grenade to ignite three seconds after the signal arrived, the EM grenade would detonate as soon as it recieved the signal, frying all the data contained in the room and then its companion would destroy the machines themselves. Flatbed poked his head in the door saying the carrier was loaded and ready to go, turning quickly to head down the corridor to the ship. Valaik shook his head as he though of all the money going to waste, removing the central memory core from the system he picked up his helm and stepped out into the corridor, sealing the door quickly he began to jog out to the waiting carrier.

He sealed his helmet on tightly before rushing out into the storm and running the 50 meters to the ship, climbing inside the cramped interior now that it was fully loaded with cargo, he sealed the door and moved up to sit next to Flatbed at the controls.

"Lets get out of here Flat.", With a grin Flatbed fired up the engines, a roar that rose above the storm outside as it began its slow climb into the sky heading Southwest.

Glancing at the base one last time, Valaik transmitted the comm signal to the two grenades, detonating them and annihilated the command center of his former warehouse. A deep laugh made its way to the cockpit from the cargo hold, glancing back Valaik saw two of the Atrox there putting on "I love Jugz" T-Shirts which brought a bright smile to his face.

"Take us home."

Back at rompas
Ralph "Wolfmann" Galland was as usual sitting around in Rompas enjoying a bottle of Wisky, when Valaik wandered in with a grim expression on his face.

Wolfmann noticing the looks on Valaiks face and quickly jumped up from the chair.

"The rumor was true?, Dustbrigade have moved in on your warehouse? Did you get the stuff out of there? What about my Wi..Wis...Wiksy...Wisky?

Wolfmann stuttered as the thought of his precious Wisky being drunk by Dustbrigade troopers hit him.

Valaik gazed slowly upon the bearded soldier, who obviously had'nt stopped drinking since the last party at Babboons. A small smile slowly apeared on his face.

"Wisky? You had wisky at the warehouse?..I think we forgot those crates Wolfie, you should have mentioned them when you sent me that message about the warehouse."

Wolfmann blinked his hazy blue eyes toward Valaik as he slowly sropped his body down toward the chair again. His hand holding onto the Wisky bottle like a drowning sailor holding a lifevest. He muttered a few curses on a language long forgotten by most, and started to pound on his old battered com link.

"Forbaska! Hvorfor skulle jeg kjøpe en gammel Com Link av en Atrox!!!"
(Translation: Damn, why did I buy a old Com Link from an Atrox)

Wolfmann stared down on his bottle, the last one he had. His packs were all empty, as he had planned a trip to the warehouse today to refill them. He raised his eyes up to face Valaik again.

"Not a single crate Boss?....Oh Boss, you know I live for that stuff? What am I to do without my pure scottish Single Malt? Drink the watered out stuff they sell here?"

There were horror in Wolfmanns voice, and Valaik walked over to Wolfmanns side of the table, putting his hand on his shoulder.

"Oh darn Wolfie, I was joking. Your Wisky is now safely stored in our other location."

Valaik was laughing now, the horror on Wolfmanns face had shown him that the joke was just as cruel as Valaik wanted it to be. He patted Wolfmann on the shoulder again, and turned around to leave.

"Oh, and by the way old drunk, maybe I will tell you where our new warehouse is located some day. Time will show."

Wolfmann stared on Valaiks back with narrow eyes. His hands slowly moving down to his rifle. Thinking to himself: "One day boss.....One day I will joke with your most precious items too.."

Wolfmann smiled to himself, as he moved his fists away from the rifle and back to the bottle. Looking around he saw a blonde girl looking lost.

"hey come here lass, and Wolfie will tell you what this planet is all about."
