New backpacks!
I was just cleaning out my bank and organizing all the junk I have collected, and I ended up buying several of those large backpacks with the logos on them. I like it very much when I can tell what's in the pack without opening it. I did have quite a few things that didn't really belong in any of those packs, so I'd like to suggest some additional backpacks that could be added in. They could also buff skills when you wear them, like some of the packs already in use.

Pack Name - Skills Buffed
  • Monster Parts - pharma tech (I know I would buy a ton of these)
  • Maps - map navigation
  • NCU belts & chips - comp lit
  • Jewelry (rings, bracers, gems, etc.) - mech engineering
  • Furniture - no ideas for skills for this one



Bump if you like this idea, or have suggestions for more packs that can be added.

My suggestions:

- More bp's for Nano crystals, either for each prof or for each type of nano (Combat, Medical, PSI etc...).

- Cluster backpack (to complement the implant bp).

- Augmentation backpack (for the completed augmentations (implants with clusters)).

- At least one more weapon backpack... the current is great, and with the pistol(?) logo on it it's perfect for ranged weapons... now a version with a melee weap logo on it would be excellent.

- Tools & devices. Somewhere to keep that screwdriver, ore extractor, lock pick, bomb defuser, cloaking dev etc. when not in use.

No suggestions for skill adds on these, as I don't see that as a prerequisite... I need storage options more than the puny skill buffs, so the general, cheapo packs with logos will suffice

My dream, of course, is tag-able packs, where you can enter your own 5-7 letter name to show on mouse-over... to bad it won't happen...
