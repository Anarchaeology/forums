Whisper's Edge & End Game
Greetings,

My name is Cathern "Vixentrox" Flowers, leader of Whisper's Edge and Chairperson for EndGame Alliance. I'd like to extend an invtation to all people that are interested in making some history and taking part in life on Rubi-ka beyond endless missions to consider Whisper's Edge or any of the EndGame alliance Clans as a possible candidtates for your new familiy. EndGame consists of the following Clans:

Unity of the Rose - A great Clan with that's been around well over a year. Dedicated to ridding Rubi-ka of mutants. Led by Szentasha

Valor Eternal - Is there a party at Reets? Valor Eternal is there! Some really nice folks. Valor Eternal is led by Braedon

First Light - Nice Clan that has been around for a long time. Fairly active in events around Rubi-ka. Currently led by Ailish

The Mockers - If youre a lover of the color blue and Grid Armor, this is the place for you! Most well known Fixer guild on the planet! Led by Knightswulf

As for my own guild, here is WE information:

Faction: Clan

Government: Department

Description:
Roleplay guild that is involved in storyline aspect of the game. WE will use diplomacy, politics, subversion, and military actions to achieve our goals. Visit us at www.whispers-edge.com

Objective:
To force a peace on Clan terms. Cripple OTRK primarily by converting Omni employees to Clan.

History:
Whisper's Edge was founded to intelligently fight against Omni-tek with both weapons and words; aid Neutrals in maintaining their independence; and helping all non-criminal Clan citizens survive and thrive on Rubi-ka.

OOC: Guild Info
For those that don't know us, Unity of the Rose is a very friendly social/roleplay guild. We encourage roleplay, but don't enforce it. We try to create an environment for people to roleplay and have occasional roleplay events. We also have social events like parties and barbeques, and combat-oriented events like hunting, dungeon crawls, and uniques.

We have many members who are low level and still learning the game, and we also have many active title 5 members for high level play. We don't care what level you are -- we hope to find people who are kind, respectful, and enjoy helping others.

We interview every applicant extensively before allowing entry into the guild to make sure there is a good match. If you think we might be the right guild for you, click on the link below and check out our home page. If you like what you see you can apply online and someone will contact you for an interview.
