The Bird Watcher's Guide to Rubi-Ka
Ah, as a n00b I remember fondly massacring (sp?) reets in the holo-training field. As I grew in strength and skill I was able to venture beyond the bounds of the backyard and delve into the world that is Rubi-Ka. Now I have never liked bird watching. It seems akin to watching grass grow or paint dry and to be honest the reets were never very entertaining except for the occasional "I'LL GET MY MAIN!".

Correction. It seemed akin to painting grass and watching it grow and dry.

In any case, on my quest to find a hermit to translate the book I pieced together in the Temple of the Three Winds, I took a wrong turn and stumbled into a vast and arid desert where I was immediately set upon by a pair of impatient, ravenous, and ruthless vultures. Being a poor man I couldn't afford a vehicle or even a decent run buff and was unable to escape. In two strokes I was facing a reclaim station much humbled and with a renewed respect for bird watching. To be more accurate, a thought was running around my mind along the lines of, "Holy @#$%!!! I just got my a$$ handed to me!!!".

Therefore I have taken it upon myself to collect information regarding the birds of Rubi-Ka in order to aid the discriminating (or warn the inexperienced) bird watcher in the pursuit of his or her's exciting hobby. All submissions are appreciated including those regarding reets as I have rarely seen them in the wild.
