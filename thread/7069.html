This is incorrect. Nano techs are quite potent defensively with both a root spell line and a psychosis (sleep, calm, distract, whatever you want to call it) spell line. If a monster switches to me or an add comes in the middle of battle, the first thing I do is cast my psychosis spell on it. It has saved my life and the lives of others in my group hundreds of times. Nano techs do have a harder time raising defensive skills such as dodge and evade up, but they only need to be raised to perhaps half of max to give enough survival power to take the few blows you'll need to take before your tank is able to regain the aggro unless you stupidly keep nuking after the monster switches to you.
