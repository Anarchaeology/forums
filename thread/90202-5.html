You can't do that in these games. That's what a lot of people don't understand about MMORPG's. There are consequences to actions that may last for 3 more years. It's not like 'ok, let's have a good battle wednesday, may the best team win' and then Thursday it's all over. People lose bases, factions lose experience, one side gets stronger and the other side starts to suck. In two months Omni may or may not be down to only 4% XP bonus. This isn't Team Fortress Classic. People are paying money for this. 13 bucks a month. Your side loses all their towers and the game is less fun to play.

Team Fortress Classic is fantastic. Assemble your team, fight another team, hope for a good match, and win or lose the game's over and you can start again. It's not like anything really mattered. Now things matter. How much fun you have in this game is in great part now determined by what the other guys in your faction are doing. Even if you never PvP, the difference in how much fun your character has can very much be determined by whether or not you are Clan or Omni. In TFC, you can be Red team, Blue team, Purple team, it don't matter and you stand as much a chance of winning everytime you fire it up.

FunCom made HUGE mistakes with Notum Wars. Not the least is providing such incredible advantages to tower owners and the faction with the most towers. That crap is worse than PvP titles ever were. Sad so to see that FunCom has effectively introduced the mechanic whereing the ganker is rewarded by giving them more tools to be an even better ganker to bring the old-school arena image into current focus.

Rather than Notum Wars properly implementing a fun analogue of Team Fortress Classic where people PvP for the pride of their organizations and some minor perks, Notum Wars is bringing out the worst elements in not only ourselves but bringing the worst types of personalities to the forefront. I can't say I've been proud to watch anything that has unfolded in this game since a week or so after Notum Wars was released. And this includes my own organization at points. Hell we were almost ripped apart by the horse crap those towers are causing.

Give me a server without all the underhandedness, poor sportsmanship, griefing, and towers and I'll gladly leave Rubi'Ka to rot a rotten death as it's taken over by whatever infected Rubi'Ka-2. Roller has a problem with his supposed faction mates dealing with the enemy. Well, I applaud him for being forthright and not being squeemish and saying so. Unfortunately, it's FunCom's fault for putting out Notum Wars with such a ruleset as to reward thug mentality and poor sportsmanship. You wanted war, you got it. We're seeing that war is only fun for the victors and nobody likes to pay money to not have fun.

War is hell. War games aren't supposed to be.
