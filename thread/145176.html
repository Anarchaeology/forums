Armor Upgrades.
With The Organic armors we now have CAS with the help of the Metamorphing Nanos.

My Idea is to allow the upgrade of stock Armors with new Recipes to use with the Metamorphing nanos..

Example:

1 Activated metamorphing Nano bots
1 Notum Fragment/nugget
1 Bloodplasma
1 Faded Nano Cluster Chem AC..
(All near same QL)

Result
1 Bio-Chemical Metamorphic Nanobots

Used on Armor.

Adds
4-400 to Chem AC (Depends On QL)
1-100 to all other ACs (Depends on QL)
+ to health (Depends on QL and Armor Part)
+ to Nano Pool (Depends on QL and Armor Part)
+ NCUs (Depends on QL and Armor Part)
Also changes the Appearance Slightly
(Examples Nano Armor is Reddish instead of Blueish tones, Omni-Elite get Red Highlights in places. Matches Better with the Albretch Armor.)

Downsides
All armor so treated in Gene locked to the wearer..(Becomes NoDrop)

well thats my Idea...summ it up to too many hours driving Truck at work and lots of time to think while avoiding the people who can't drive.....
