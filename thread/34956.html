Soleet
Dynamic Spawns have their good and bad sides.

I will give 2 examples.

1) Trash King has no dynamic spawn time. He spawns every 12 hours (example) with +/- 5 minutes delay. This will cause high level people who have the power to visit the place every 11 hour 50 minutes and kill him the moment he spawns thus rendering those people who have waited for him to spawn from getting the item they require.

2) Trash King is a dynamic spawn. This means he can spawn when killed within 6-12 hours. Which will cause mass camping him, people staying there for days hence getting burned out. This isnt right either.

Now the reason Funcom has made this spawn a dynamic one is because they think it is too easy to finish. It indeed is not too hard to finish with the right friends but a dynamic spawn will always cause *****ing and moaning and unfortunately in this one case the people will be right.

The solution for this would be to either make the Trash King spawn like every hour or make him triggerable or if FC thinks the reward vs. risk on this fight is too unbalanced to raise the dmg output and difficulty of the TK but keep the respawn time low.

Either way the spawn needs to be fixed or another solution must be found because this will keep causing problems between everyone that is able to hunt for TK in a group and result in grief playing or hard feelings.

Afterall we all are playing AO to enjoy it not to keep working
