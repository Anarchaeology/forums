Update
Had a meeting with the designers, and have gotten more information about future changes, both for Shadowlands and patches before the expansion.

Basically, as Blackmane said in his answer, it's a matter of increasing the skill budget, which is an important part of expanding the game. New nano formulas will have higher skill requirements, even before the expansion, and the level requirements are there so that we don't have to implement the full new skill requirements right away. (Imagine the leet charms having nano reqs of 900+.)

The next batch of nano formulas will most likely have the full skill requirements. Hence, the previous batch (Adventurer and Fixer nanos) was an in-between solution, and the option of change (if any) would be increasing the skill requirements, not lowering the level requirements.

We will try "leak" out information about the next batch of nano formulas as soon as we get our hands on it.

Another aspect of the level requirements is the fact that they add something to work for when you have maxed out the skills for your title. Level requirements go in steps of 25 until 125, then in steps of 10. This means when you have maxed out your green skills at level 121, you still have nano formulas at level 125, 135 and 145 to look forward to before reaching the next title at level 150.

Feedback appreciated.
