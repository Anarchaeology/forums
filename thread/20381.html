An Omni in City of Home
Hi!
I'm Omni, but i don't care about conflict ( flame me for it if you want ),
i like cooperation much more.
I was really bored with 20k missions, sometimed i feel,
i can not see this place anymore,
too many ppl., 20k is so crowded +
really not the most beautiful place on Rubi-Ka.
So i went yesterday to City of Home,
really cool place, nice musics, much more space,
enough for everyone - only one thing -
almost everyone is Clan.
I though, OK, i give it a try, shouting "MP 92 lfg",
after the second try someone invite me,
sure Clanners , i ask " i'm Omni, is it ok ? " - "it's ok" - i'm happy

But the problem - TAB / CTRL-TAB;
there was no way in the hell to TAB-target mobs,
i only could use <F...> /assist, but since i used of it, it was OK
( and hey - i could even target my pet as i would be alone *grin* )

But what scares me -
someone i team said
"... i can not tab mobs, i only target Quantar and his pets..."
the answer was:
"thats because an Omni picked the mission"
the answer of the first person -
"that sux..."

I'm even not sure if it only happend if it's my mission,
or even thier ?
( with Clan team members having trouble targeting mobs )


The thing is - if was fun, it was a nice team, we was successful.
And it was even something original,
not just brainless "tabbing" & "CTRL-tabbing",
a welcome break of routine.
Not sure how for others, may be they was just polite...

I'm not sure what to do now.
I would come in CoH time to time again,
but if i'm just a trouble for the team - i would stay away ... *snif*

Another question is - if not a Clan "domain" -
is there a real alternative for Omni's around 90-100 besides 20k ?

Oh, by the way: i would accept any Clanner in my team,
i remember, once we was teaming at gates in 20k,
it was one of my first team hunting at all,
there was a lonely Clanner, who asked if he could join us,
i told him, he should ask our leader,
and since he dont joined i sent him a tell asking why -
he said, he received no answer, probably because he is Clanner :/

In my team it would not happen
( i would rather left the team now heh )

/Quantar MP 93
RK1, Omni ( just cuz i love this view at the sea from Omni-E heh )
