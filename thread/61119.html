www.clan-anarchist-syndicate.net

When I tried to explain to them what was going on, I was nothing short of jeered from the establishment for my concern, even by members of our own Omni Security agencies. Should we seriously entrust our safety with such persons? how long will it be before these forces are bold enough to attack the cities where you live? where your children sleep? If interested let me know and I'll be sure to set an exact date. Show that you still care about the future of Omni-Tek!
