Does anyone care about PVP imbalances?
Does anyone care about the insane imbalances in PVP? I mean its really absurd on more than one level how totally stupid the issues with the traders drains are. Not to mention the Docs complete heal. I'm not trying to be a hypocrite I recently started a doc as my alt mostly because I felt that she'ld be good at PVP and PVM I was right. But when you look at docs and the complete heal Nano you see something that makes dueling them (unless your a Trader) a moot venture. Same with traders whats the point in dueling them? The only way you can take them down is in groups. NO ONE GROUP PVPS!!! Come on is it just me whos a little upset at this. I mean this has from what I have heard been going on since they patched the game for over equiping. Seems like the only people that care about balance in the game are the players themselves or else Funcom would have fixed this a long time ago. It would only take one little thing on both sides. Give traders one drain thats usable on players. Let them have 2 drain lines but make only one usable on players. Or shorten the time limit on the drains, make them high cost low time limit drains. I'm talking 10 seconds. let them stack I think that would make traders a little more evened out. Make doctors have a time limit on Complete heals much like the Agents have on their crit buffs. That would make doctors much more balanced at higher levels.

These seem simple solutions to make both classes more balanced but still viable classes in PVP. I mean other people have to be as pissed off as I am that Funcom shoved this on the back shelf till they got ready to deal with it. Something that really screws up a whole section of the game. I mean fix the NT's and Soldiers but also fix Doctors and Traders cause they're all broke.

Give the players what they want for a change. It seems that funcoms biggest problem is they refuse to listen to their customers as a result more and more leave the game out of frustration for the most part. This is not a very solid business practice as I see it.

Enough of my whining.
