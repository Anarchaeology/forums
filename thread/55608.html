Rare Items
There are several items in the game that are rare. Grid Armor and Nullity are a couple. I understand and agree that they need to be rare items. However, I do have a problem with being forced to rely on the honesty of other players to get these items. Nearly all players are aware how valuable many of these items are. The problem is, how am I to know weather the item has been looted.

For example, Grid Armor is a Fixer Item. Yet, when the boss is looted by say, a Doctor or a Soldier. That person cannot use the item, yet if he decides not to tell it was looted then he is free to sell it. If there is a fixer in the team, he has earned that item and should be able to get it. It is already accepted that an Item that 2 or more players want can be "rolled" for by number guessing or something else.

Find a way to make these items less subject to the whims of another player. I feel this is a just and fair suggestion. Anyone else got some feedback??
