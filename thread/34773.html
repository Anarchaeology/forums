Maybe I am a fanboy, not really sure how much one has to like things before one is considered such, but I suspect it's something like "if I like it and you don't, then I'm a fanboy"

I like the world of Rubi-Ka. I like the athmosphere in the game. I like the places one can go to. I like to talk to many of the people I play with regularly. Yes there are bugs. Yes there are issues. But to me, the good stuff overshadows the bad. To many of those who sees the game only in numbers I'm sure it's completely opposite. And these are the ones who will leave AO for other MMORPG's as soon as they hit the shelves, and that I am looking forward to. I don't mean to offend anyone by saying that, so if anyone is offended I can only apologize.

As for the storyline, I wish something will happen soon. I hate the fact that nothing has happened for ages. It pisses me off sometimes when I think about it. But I still like the world, and it's possible to make ones own story along with a guild or other players in general. Someone told me yesterday "This isn't a roleplaying game because it has no story" - I disagree. Playing a roleplaying game doesn't mean having things thrown at you that you have to handle - that sounds more like an adventure game or an FPS. In a roleplaying game you play a role. If you're bored and nothing is happening then you can do something to make things happen. To make i interesting, others may need to participate, but in AO that shouldn't be a problem since there are quite a lot of other roleplayers around.
