Too buff, or not to buff, and the Rubi-Ka Economy
OK, I've seen it about a few places on the Forum, and Im gonna throw open a little debate....

Charge for buffing or not ?

Vote here


People of all professions get random tells asking for buffs of one sort or another and some professions get it worse than others... ( P.S. no whining please, I know some of you get it bad ! )

Agent's - FG, TTS
Enforcer's - Essences
Doc's - LOL, well where do I start, SFA please :-)
MA's - Crit. Buffs, MA buffs
Fixer's - HOT's, Run Speed
MP's - Mochams... please can I have mochams
Adventurers - Perception
Soldiers - RRFE, OS
NT's - HE
Trader's - Wrangles
Bureaucrat's - Baton

and finally the lowly Engineer, who rarely gets asked for a buff, but gets loads of spam tells asking for help with trade skillz. The list goes on and on. We've all seen it, been there done that...

So who charges and who doesn't ? I'd be curious to find out. Maybe we could get a poll running for this. Charge for Buffs / Not charge for buffs, or generally not bothered.

As a high level Adventurer I constantly get tells asking for perception buffs and damage shields, but this cannot compare to other professions who have far more useful buffs. I personally get great pleasure out of helping a 'toon' get into that LLTS or rock in perception at PvP. I know what it's like when Im building a nOObie toon. We all need buffs to go that extra mile.

I, generally, do not charge for buffing, someone wants Parrot they got it, unless they are Omni off course :-P but that's a whole different subject, a bribe to make me look the other way maybe.

Typically all fellow clan members I buff free, unless of course I meet a particularly demanding so and so who demands a buff.

Im curious, because as I started my nOOb Engineer I was wondering weather I should tip for Mochams and Wrangles. I generally did, unless I'd forgotten to transfer some creds. from my Alt to my Engineer. ( Thats another point, I wonder if Funcom can make a character transfer system ? save us loggin' in and out all the time ? That's another topic right there ).

But back to buffing, do we or don't we charge?

My rule is if I see someone struggling to buff me, like a low-ish level MP who has to buff to buff-me mochams then I'll tip, whereas with a LVL 170 MP I generally offer my gratitude and any buffs they want in return.

As a nOObie who builds his first Mocham casting toon, tipping for mochams could prove a vital financial income, but most of us these days tend to have higher-level ALT's feeding us cash and items as and when we require them.

How do you feel the Rubi-Ka economy is evolving ? Prices on Rubi-1 for ub3r items are now spiralling, like a bad case of inflation... NS MKII, GA, Dragon Armour, VT Eggs, just to name a few, should there be some governoning body to control this inflation ?

I'd like to see a poll started on this, but it's fair to say that economy reflects society... its is driven by a population of customers all seeking those rarer items, the rarer the better for the person selling, but is it time Funcom stepped in ? or do we continue to pester Guild mates to spend 3 days at Claw-Camp to get us that GA disc ?

This is not a whinge or a complaint, Im just curious as to how other people feel. Now the game has been around for a couple of years, items are becoming more and more common. I remember when the were few lvl 200 toon's on Rubi-Ka and high level buffs were a figment of many a person's imagination, and were seen as a gift from the God's when you received that Leviathan, or OS buff. Now 60% of the population can cast those buffs.

Same applies for ub3r items, X-3's, Albrechts, all have seen there prices drop as more and more filter into the economy.

So come-on lets here it...

Concerning the buffing:

When I played my MP at first I never charged for buffs. After a while I noticed that 95% of the buff beggars never bothered to tip and maybe 50% even bothered to say thanks.

After a while I started adding people who repeated asked me for buffs and never tipped or said thanks to my mental "never buff" list. After this list became too large to maintain on paper (it grew to immense proportions when I started adding people who spammed me with vulgarities to that list as well) I decided to start charging a flat rate for buffs.

My reasoning was this: anyone who can't afford the price can usually easily find another MP who will do the buffs for free. Anyone who isn't willing to spend 3 extra minutes looking for another MP can afford to pay for their buffs. My goal was never to generate profit... just to reduce my level of stress and aggravation. After I built up a reputation of being a greedy bastard my buff requests dropped to almost nothing.

P.S. I never charged guildmates, teammates, friends or newbies

Now that I am trying a new trader (got sick of pet pathing) I always charge for buffs as well. Anyone willing to overequip their weapon by 50% should also be willing to pay for my trouble.

ANYWAY if you are going to ask for some buff you should always offer a tip. You might think everyone is rich but some people don't have alts and could really use the cash. Not offering a tip is insulting to the person giving the buff. Its up to them to choose to refuse a tip (and as you can see from this thread many people do).

P.P.S offering a tip of 10k for a 132 wrangle is always a bad idea
