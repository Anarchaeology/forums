Though it was really nice to be helping out with the destruction of the Cyborgs and the finding of the tokenboards, what really surprised me was the condition of the Cyborg Camp in the Milky Way.

One of the reasonings behind the attack was that the Cyborgs are bent on the destruction of biological matter to the advantage of the machine. The test result on the earth, air and water samples that the Desert Winds took are still out for analysis but from visual observation I can say that the area looked pretty clean.

Not a sign of the usual waste the borgs dump everwhere, and plant and animal life thrives!

This puzzles the members of DW and we will continue to monitor this particular camp an its development.
