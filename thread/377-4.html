Well, here I am checking out the game during the free month. And here I am still seeing the reasons why I left This has been a fairly good thread to read. A fair amount of productive input from thoughtful players is a lot different to the "old days"!

Dual wielded pistols SHOUDN'T be useless. They should have the same damage capability as a melee weapons, but they should have a different set of skill requirements with similar IP costs. I have never seen any good reason why melee should be better. Saying that a pistol is for the NT, MP, etc, etc is fine, but don't forget we are talking dual pistols, with associated specialty attacks that we can spend points on.

For this game, there needs to be a baseline for damage output and that baseline has been ignored. A player with QL weapons and armour that are even to their level has 50/50 chance of killing a mob/player that is equal to their level. To increase the chances of killing, you add in your buffs and select your implants to equip yourself beyond your level. It's really that simple.

Choice of weapon type should be irrelevant. The amount of IP you spend and the associated implants/buffs/etc should determine the QL of the weapon you can equip. If I use QL 100 pistols, I expect the same base damage output as a player with wiith QL 100 swords. After that, I will grant either type of weapon "specialty" damage and speeds based on other skills. This currently does not happen in Anarchy Online.

So now, the Adventurer class still screams for help. When I played this game in beta, then from release up to lvl 65, I saw two paths. The defensively hardy melee Adventurer, and the heal/buff ranged Adventurer. The former having the ability to tank to survive, the latter having the ability to buff/heal to survive.

A lot of complaints about the Adventurer are superflous. The biggest issue are pistols and healing. After these problems, the morphs need to be useful, especially for the melee guys. (I haven't menetioned "broken things, just fix them. I'm trying to focus on improvements here).

The makeup of the skill set for the Adventurer allows us to choose Pistols or Melee. Then we can choose associated casting and init skills, special attacks, etc, etc, etc. Any of the chosen paths made should result in equal enjoyment.

ATM a pistol Adventurer is a joke and cannot be enjoyed. Solve that problem and you'll get my monthly subscription back.
