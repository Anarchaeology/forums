This has probably been suggested before, but..
.. I think this solution will satisify the vast majority of the AO player community, even most PvP'ers (the non-griefing ones):

First, lets face it, there will ALWAYS be grief players in a game where mechanics/exploits permit it. By 'grief players' I mean players who get their thrills by ruining the other peoples gaming experience. I do not think greifers themselves realise that is actually what they are doing, and here lies the problem - you cannot make them stop voluntarily by being polite or nice or by making a logical argument.

Second, the community should have room for PvP as well as pure RP'ing fans who don't necessarily want to participate in the 'front line' of action. How to accomodate this, while preventing the griefer minority from ruining the game for the rest of us?

Make PvP a VOLUNTARY aspect of the game by enabling a 'flag' that a player can activate/de-activate ONLY when the user account logs on. So, if someone only wants to roleplay or do missions all over Rubi-Ka for their entire AO session, by all means let them, without the frustration of getting ganked by zone-campers and other griefers.


Just my two cents worth, comments welcome.
