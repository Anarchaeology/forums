about lvl 25-30 is good for hunting there.


You can get there by heading out the west gate of tir. Then you'll go up to the NE corner of the city. From there head NE, and you'll come across a dirt road heading up to a little outpost. From there head north through the little forest with no leafs up to the zone.
When you zone, keep going north through the forest, when you come out, you'll see bot mountain to your left, and an OP in front of you. Save there and try to find a team, or explore it yourself.
