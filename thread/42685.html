Community answers - part 6
New list of information for you guys...

Two hot issues
Just thought I'd let you know that we have heavy focus on both the team health bars and the pet pathing right now. Hopefully we'll have improvements there for 14.6, though I can't promise anything yet.

Dyna camps
More camps to come, to get something for all levels. A level limit on them is not very likely though, and shouldn't be needed with the number of camps coming.

PvP strength
Not all professions are intended to be equally strong in PvP. Some are good at PvP (which technically is more or less a solo game right now), others are better in teams or with e.g. sneaking or trade skills. (No, that does not mean Agents and Traders are supposed to be worst at PvP and teaming. It's an example.)

I'll try bring the issue of profession balance up with the Professionals. Just never expect all professions to be balanced in all areas. Some will excel in some areas, some in others. Some might be ok in all, but master of none. We simply don't have 12 different areas where professions can excel.

Nano execution animation
The animation is supposed to stop when the recharge time is over, allowing you to use a new nano immediately. It's registered as a bug, and I've pushed to get some priority on the fix.

Bows
There are some changes slated for the existing bows coming in a patch soon. I'd like to see details on those, and have your testing comments on them before I start pushing the issue of clip sizes on them. I mentioned it though, so the designer knows about it.

And a Bow Special Attack buff is in the plans, but not scheduled or anything yet. Note: I wouldn't rely on it being a general (non-profession) buff.

Variation in boss MOB loot
Variation in boss MOB loot will improve all the time. More items will be added, thus adding variation, and at the same time making those items found now more rare.

Debuffs and root / snare
Debuffs are not supposed to break root and snare formulas while you are in combat. We're working on a fix for this bug.

BBI Faithfuls in shops?
Yup, they're there. They just rotate with some other weapons, so you might want to check the shops more often.

In-game chat channels
Profession specific channels is not very likely. If they come sometime in the future they will most likely still be separated by side and area, and do we really need more chat channels now?

The newbie channels might very well get available until level 10. Adding a task on that.

---

Guess that was it for now.
