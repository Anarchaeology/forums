Class enhancement suggestion - Bow, Bow Mastery and question for FC
I'll start with the question for FC:
Heh, is there something wrong with bows you're not telling us?
I mean, I haven't seen a mob/NPC use a bow. Not once. Interesting huh?

Okay, now on to suggestions for weapon variety and adventurer proffession enhancement:

Would you consider high-tech bows and crossbows using high-tech energy quarrels/bolts? So in that case, how about ...

* Pure bow or pure crossbow that requires bow/crossbow skill requirement only. No specials but has high crits (similar to the pure shotgun line). Have variety in damage type to compensate for lack of specials - eg. energy, chemical, poison.

* Bows or crossbows with bow/crossbow and fling skill requirement.

* Bows or crossbows with bow/crossbow and fling and aimed shot requirements. These are in the shops and in mission loot but come on, at least make them decent weapons so that people will want to use them.

* No more expensive arrow crap. Instead, have the shops sell high-tech energy quarrels/bolts that cost the same as energy ammo and come in same number of charges.

* Range of bow/crossbow say between 16-26m. Remember, Adventurers have some (not great but decent enough) tanking ability because of their AC and damage shields as well as heals. The long-range domain should remain with Agents and NTs.

* Dump the 3-ammo-clip bow. It is, quite frankly, silly and does not fit into a futuristic concept.

* Bow and crossbow masteries (+60) for adventurers. At the same time, lower the QL and reqs for Eagle Eye so that a perception and aimed-shot buff makes sense for an adventurer to have. Think along the lines of what the agents have in terms of their Sniper's Bliss and Enhanced Sense buffs.

Well, those are just some wild ideas that just came out of the blue. Hopefully, these will inspire more and better ideas from others. As for myself, I would like to see more weapon variety - it gets old and boring to see everyone running around with Vektors or Novaflows or Lovemakers or Hammers.

Good hunting, fellow Rubi-Kans.
