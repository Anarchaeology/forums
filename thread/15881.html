Temple grounds of Tir
Yesterday rumors came to me as I sat enjoying my lunch at the Bronto Burger in Tir, that foul creatures was desecrating the sacred Temple of Tir. When I asked around no one seemed to know what these creatures were, but several people I talked with mentioned giant spiders. I did not like what I heared, and decided to check it out for myself. I have been to Bot Mountain, that lies close by, to hunt the failed Omin Tec experiments, so I knew that the were packed with nasty little things. Pvt Divilian joined me in Tir as we headed out for the outpost to stock up, and update our incuranse one last time, just in case. First we decided to check out Bot Mountain, but quickly realised that mine and several other clan members had lowered the threath from this area, so we decided to head out to the Temple. When we arrived, the area was quiet, eerie so. No one was aroun, not even a cute little leet. But in the center of the Temple the grave was opened. No sooner had we discovered this, a powerfull nanomage attacked us. Divillian and myself fought for our lives, but Divillian's powerfull nanoformulas and my constant healing tipped the scale in our way. But it was a close call. As I healed Divillian for the last time, I realised that I was all out of nano, and hadn't quite recoverd from my last stim pack. Luckily for us, Divlillian finnished him off with one last blast. After the combat we wondered if the rumors were true, as this seemed to be a crazy lone wanderer, and no creatures were around. After patroling the grounds for a while, we decided that the rumors were false. No sooner that we had decided so, we again were attacked, this time by a Martial Artist that used a powerfull mystical shield. This shield should decide our fate that day, as several of Divillians blast were deflected. But we fought on, even when we saw that we where fighting a loosing battle. I spite of my many heals, in the end, I could no longer keep her from me, and the last thing I see before I wake up back at the outpost, is her shield 2 inches from my head. Divillian kept on fighting, but I was soon joined by him at the outpost. Two wandering mad people could not be a coincident, so we recruited two more people. My friend Azarro, and a Martial Artist named Vilhelm that we met at the outpost. Once again we returned to the Temple, and the increased power of the team saw our revenge over the lady that had slain us, not to many hours earlier. But madmen were not the only creature desecrating the Temple. Several enormous spiders crawled around in the Temple, and with their powerfull poisenous bite, they were quite a challenge. We managed to kill several of the beast as we surprised them, but they apparently are smarter than we thought. Suddenly out of nowhere we were attacked off guard, and before anyone really knew what happened, Azarro where in deep trouble. When I arrived at the scene, he was already killed, and the giant monster had turned his attention to Vilhelm. This time there were no stopping the angry monster, and in turn, Vilhelm, Divillian and finally myself sucumed to the beast. But have it said, no MD ever stepped down or turned away from such powerfull opponents. After the battle, Divillian left, as he had other more pressing commitments. I returned several times, all with the same result. The beast of Tir Temple, beware. I will return with more powerfull formulas, more powerfull friends, cause you will not have the better of me, or my friends. A MD never gives up, he just withdraws to fight another day. And that is what I will do, because the rumors that led me to the Temple are true, and I for one will not stand by and watch the monsters have their way at our sacred place!

Pvt Nahadra out.

PS: Thanks to Pvt Divillian and to Azarro for their brave fighting.
