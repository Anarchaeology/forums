Ntrox,

Nope you're wrong.

At lower levels I saw a definite improvement on the attack pet pathing when using Quickness. A pet's runspeed is set at casting to be the runspeed of the owner at time of casting. However, I did testing ages ago - when I had a low runspeed - with using Quickness. I tested outdoors and noticed a definite speed improvement from the pets. When you first start running the pets take a few moments before responding and thus are usually a fair way behind you. With the runbuff it was noticeable that they were not as far behind.

We saw above that one of the known issues that the dev involved confirmed was that pets were trying to go straight to their owner and not following nodes properly. The speed buff to a pet could make it possible for the pets to be considerably closer to you and as a result less likely to find that the direct path to you (which we know they try to take) is through a wall.

Also - it's a regularly suggested improvement for pet pathing that the pet should just stick to the owners path better and stay right next to him. If the pets runspeed was considerably faster than the owner's runspeed, then the end effect would be that the pet would be much closer to the owner. Since the pet seems to path directly to the owner - the very close pet would essentially follow the same path as the owner.

Pets will only warp when they get to a certain range away from you. The pets can be within that range and still behind you - thus causing the 'as the crow flies' pathing to make them get stuck. Attack pets in particular only seem to warp properly when their attack/defence mode AI is switched off - which is when you're flying. That's why when you have wings on they do the jumping thing and stick with you pretty much.

A base runspeed buff could be an improvement. Not a complete solution by any means... but nevertheless an improvement.

I'd probably suggest a base change to the demon runspeed itself by auto-equipping an item that buffed its runspeed by an amount relative to its QL. Then the pet would get the owner's runspeed at casting and the item buff on top. I don't think having yet another range of pet buffs would necessarily be desirable.

To put it better:

0 metres to 10 metres: Follow Range
10 metres to 20 metres: Warp Range
20 metres + : Stop and wait range.

The pet checks where you are every couple of seconds say and decides whether to follow, warp or stop.

If you run fast enough, then you'll go from the follow range through the warp range before the pet gets a check in - so you skip the warp range entirely. If the pet is running faster than you then you'd be less likely to run through a range between checks.

Lag and latency would add extra seconds between the pet checks which would exascerbate the problem... which is another issue we see too.
