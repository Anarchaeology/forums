When omnis come through whompas to tir, the guards attack with their noisy rifles, that might be what you heard...also me and a few friends once in a while like to equip noob weapons (Ql 1-10 OT 12s, magical staff of inferno, 1-10 heavy suppressors, banjos, MPS, etc) and 'carry the aggro' from the arena to fair trade...its funny to have a bunch of people trading and getting missions then the eruption of gunshots. It's pretty funny to watch

Anyway the level 80 omni couldn't attack you, below lvl 75 there is a 10 level radius requirement to fight another player (or around that) and you were 65 levels below him. After level 75 though, you can attack or be attacked by players from up to 200. Things get a little tougher after 75

Unfourtunately there really is no specific gains from pvp. The enjoyment i get out of it mostly, is that im fighting another player that uses nonscripted words (mobs have aggro messages) an ability to think and reason (well, most ppl anyway) and a customized character not comparable to a profession mob you'd see in a mission.

Loot rules apply. If the person you killed loses 10% or more exp from when you killed him, you may loot any non-insured items. The way you can prevent from being looted should you die in pvp combat is by using the insurance terminal always before you pvp, insuring any new items you recieved, and keeping exp safe so you don't lose it.

There's also a very unfourtunate loot exploit i heard about in pvp. But I won't mention it. Already contacted support about it anyway.
