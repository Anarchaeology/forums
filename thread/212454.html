Unexpected Questions
Not much really occurred to the agent as she wandered into Rompa's that evening, she had heard Katelin was in town, and she just had to track her down to say hello. Things seem to always change, but not completely. As she traipsed up those steps to the bar, she noticed the crowd in the back, shadows huddling at a booth. Raquel squinted behind her shades, eager to get a crisper view. Familiar faces began to fade into reality as she stepped closer down that aisle, booth after booth decorating each side. Memories of days spent in the bar washed over the back of her mind, smells, sounds all picking at her psyche... teasing.

She saw Don, Kate... even that Clyburn fellow. They were talking away, surrounded by other vaguely familiar faces, and some unknown to the agent. It didn't matter who those people were. Katelin was facing away from the opifex, probably unaware of who was floundering nearby. Raquel reached down and poked the young lady in the side as she sat there, on the floor. She jumped and looked up in surprise to see Raquel standing there. The agent smiled and said hello to the group.. they teased her as they always did, and while many clanners would have found it to be an invitation to a fight or struggle or debate on freedom, Raquel merely shrugged it off with her own banter.

One young girl in particular, Chrysallys, seemed curious of what Raquel spoke.. she knew turning her face from Omni-Tek was not for everyone, and she merely suggested that the girl do what she was told for now. She sensed the youth there, rash decisions with a child's life in hand was not something Raquel would ever put in danger, even if it meant stealing another Employee away from the Corporation.

The agent, morose about her past, gave a small wave and farewell to the group. Turning, she relished the sensation of familiarity once more as she made her way down the aisle again, to head out to meet her friends...

"Um.. Sezzi... Sez.. err", Raquel heard that same young voice chime up behind her... she turned to find that same young girl trying to get her attention.
"Hmm?"
"You said you were from the clans, but why you so nice to us here?", the girl asked.
"I used to be an Employee. I didn't badger clanners then either, why would I start badgering Employees?"
"Used to? Why you leave, just because of a lesser job?", Chrysallys asked, she had remembered one of the reasons Raquel gave Don for leaving to the North. Grr. Raquel smoothed back her hair.
"A few things were.. personal. My department was shut down, it was very painful.", she fed the child a truth.
"Still..., doesn't make sense why you kill just for that?"
"Kill?", the agent's curiosity was tickled slightly at the almost harsh but innocent girl.
"Well, um, aren't you a clanner?"
"I don't really hunt people down..., we aren't all murderers.", she gave the girl a smile, Raquel was rusty, in fact, she could almost admit to herself that she was lightly nerved.
"But that's not what mom says..."
"Oh, well. I'm sure she does it for your own good, but.. I've learned to judge a person by their actions, not their affiliation.", her skin grew tighter around her face and neck, nervousness used to be so foreign to the opifex, "Don't worry.. I know, I'm probably confusing."
The girl scratched her head, probably lost in processing this new information, she merely looked up and introduced herself, finally. "I'm Chelle... nice to meet you."
Nodding once, the agent returned the gesture, "Raquel. Nice to meet you too, Chelle."

"So you going back now... away from Omni-1 because why?"
"To meet some friends of mine, it's almost my bedtime too.", the mere thought of sleep ripped a small yawn out of Raquel's chest.
"Mom is working over-night, I don't have to worry too much long as I get to school in the morn.", the girl giggled at Raquel. Only a few seconds later was Chelle right back into her curiosity, "I'm curious... doesn't it seem wrong to side with an affiliation just because you're angry at another?"
Grinning, Raquel merely replied, "In a way, yes, but Omni-Tek will not allow neutral citizens to be "ousted" Employees; so my choices were to stay and be miserable or try to find something to make me happy."
"So why not come back then? I'm sure all will be forgiven, and I'm sure you can work in a department away from what makes you miserable?", Chelle's wonder was refreshing to Raquel, even now.
"No, I don't think the misery will go away.", Raquel tilted her head and looked at the girl, "Chelle... if you spent many years, for something you thought was right..."
"Yeah?"
"And found it was a lie, and false... you'd feel bad, right?", the girl seemed to wrench her brain into a fit of pondering, rolling the question back and forth before Raquel interrupted her response with an almost passionate rant, "I didn't care about clanners dying, neutrals being oppressed, revenge to Omni, not something I cared about. I just got tired of living a lie."
"What lie?"
Raquel slowly exhaled and shook her head, "It's too painful..."
"But if it's too painful, it's probably something that isn't too well-known, so.. it's possible.. you might not hate working with Omni-Tek, but just being around someone..", Chelle kept trying to find that niche, that "bruise" of Raquel's.
"Uhm, miss jaquel.. can I ask ya something?"
"What's that?"
"Well.. say I were to hit you. Would you not like me?"
"No, I wouldn't necessarily not like you. I'd be curious as to why you hit me though..", Raquel grinned. She explained it wouldn't really matter what the reason was, either.
"Hm.. say you told me a very, very secretive secret..."
"Yes..?", the opifex was intrigued by the upcoming analogy.
"Something that.. if I told someone... would destroy someone you loved.."
"Alright."
"But I told someone, and not just anyone.. everyone! Could you trust me again after that?"
"Most likely not, no.", the agent replied, still wondering where this was going as the girl smiled victoriously.
"Okay... say.. um.. I have a sister..., would you not trust her as well?"
"Your actions would have no bearing on her.", the agent finally understood.. it did nothing for her somber mood, either.
"Yeah! That's the point! Maybe your hate or whateveryoucallit.. is not really directed at the group but one individual...? I mean... you're here! In Rompas.."

Raquel sighed, explaining she wanted to just say hello to Katelin.."Chelle, I do not blame anyone in particular for what has happened. Not even Mr. Ross.", she reached for the girl's shoulder and squeezed, the girl's gaze following her actions.
"But the death of my parents is not something that is comfortable to be reminded of.. my aunt.. because of me, and my decisions.", she let go of the girl's petite shoulder, "It's easier to ignore up North, for I don't have a constant, daily reminder."
"But then.. um.. aren't you just running away?"
"Yes.. I am. I admit it.", she smiled awkwardly at the girl, "Your concern is charming, it makes me smile.", the girl blushed a bit, "Your mother has a good daughter."
"Is it really easier to spend your whole life running, or is it easier to spend some time in pain to never have to run again?"
"Ohhhh it's much easier to run.. that's why I'm doing it.", she winked playfully, trying her damnedest to lighten the mood, but Chelle only frowned, "I'm sorry."
"You should be... but not about this... about the fact that you won't be anything but a coward..", something actually made Raquel saddened at that moment, perhaps it was the truth, or perhaps it was something else, "I gotta get home.. before mom does.."
"Good bye."
"Bye Jaquel... hope your legs never get tired..", the girl sighed and hurried to the foyer of Rompas, to leave.
Raquel smirked to herself.. surprised at how youth treated her.

Moments later she, too, was leaving Rompa's. Her friends were playing games at their usual hang out, and she was trying to get there too, to participate. She could use some relaxation... some humour. Gazing out of her yalm and down to the buildings of Omni-1, she felt thankful. Not for her time spent in those walls, but for the friends..and family she had waiting for her in Old Athen.
It didn't matter what she said to anyone, what her heart possessed was more truth than any of it. Whether or not she could ever share those truths completely with just anyone, she did not know...

Chelle quickly left the bar, numerous of things going through her head. Curiosity swirmed all around her mind, wondering the 'ifs' or 'whys' of the world. The things she was being taught at the school, were simple when it came to the clans. They were basically bad, working to thwart the good of Omni-Tek's mission on the planet. Therefore, they were to be avoided. However, what happens when the truth doesn't seem like the truth?

Mom wasn't much help... though did provide her with some important information. First off, she found out this Raquel was a former worker with Ms Markarian. Secondly, not all clanners were indeed bad. According to ICC Law, they are in violation and therefore Omni-Tek is justified in hunting and killing them. Most of them at least... but this one seemed different. The way she handled herself, the lack of any spite or hate in her emotions or speech. Gotta be different...

Well, there's one person that would know. It was hard to bring it up though. First came her interview for training with Vector Blue, Markarian's division. This had gone well, but the fact she can't go out of the 'Safe' cities without permission and without accompaniment irked her constantly... but... it was a start. Through training came the forms to be filled... not to mention the class-work that seemed to pile up on itself. Finally, she was able to get a moment alone with Markarian about it over the link when no one else was listening.

Demenzia
Isis In Darkness
Retaining a tired sigh, Jen slowly let herself slump on her chair, feeling the weight of a whole day of work on her shoulders now - a whole day of training-overwatch and paperwork, rather, filling the documents she needed for Chelle's training, evaluating the young girl's current skills and level of education, beginning to gather certain data in order to update the training software, so that they'd reflect a more accurate environment for her. She had promised this to Hayley, and not only out of friendship: because it was the right thing to do. Chelle was too young for insurance, and sending her on real field missions would very likely mean her death... even though she was more talented than it had seemed at first.

Opening her laptop to check her latest Grid-mail, Jen absently pressed a key on her comm, turning the <record> function into <play> mode, absently listening to what she had, following her habit, recorded during the day, various conversations around her, the usual noise of the town, calls at the office... And she froze, when Chelle's voice popped in, reminding her suddenly of the conversation they had had in the morning, before taking the girl to training. Her words, her questions. The tone of her voice, when she had mentioned Raquel. The tone of Jen's voice, more angry and bitter than she remembered. For a long moment, the young woman stopped, resting her elbows on the edge of her desk and her chin on her crossed fingers. Raquel. Was there still a need to hope, as Chelle seemed to believe? Hayley was keeping on telling her to move on, to leave the past behind, and Jen knew she was right. And still... part of her had hoped, yes. Part of her was still hoping, childishly, that one day things would be like before again.

She slowly shook her head. It was impossible. Too much time had passed... too much resentment, too. When had things begun to change that much? When Raquel had left? When they had parted, after the return of Damon Clyburn? Or even before this... when Raquel had been meeting with Clanners more and more often, in Reets, in these parties Jen couldn't even stand hearing about anymore now...

"...Tha question is... would I want things to change? Can I accept things to change? Am I ready... to start over again?..." the Fixer softly whispered for herself, in the deep silence - and again, she shook her head. Chelle was too young to understand... or not experienced enough, rather. She had tried. She had cried over the loss. She had run after Raquel - called her, almost begged her to tell her why she had left and joined the Clans. She had trusted her once, too, when she had been so lost, without anyone to turn to, before the first forms for what would become Vector Blue had ever be filled. When she couldn't sleep, when Malcom's absence was driving her crazy. Raquel hadn't turned her in to her new friends... but this had been in the beginning only. When she was still going to Reets in the hopes of seeing the Agent, even if it meant ending up surrounded by dancing, half-naked Clanners, having to drunk herself with sake to forget this little detail and going on being with Raquel all the same. When she would enter the club for a coffee, in the hopes of meeting her 'by chance'.

Thinking back to this was filling Jen with shame now. How could she only have done that!... Chelle was wrong. She had tried. But Raquel didn't seem to be so willing to go on with what she had once taken for friendship. The good old times when they were calling themselves 'sis' were over now. Jen had realized that no matter what, it was always her who called... always her who walked in. Except for the one time Raquel had entered Rompa's, barely two weeks after her defection, the Fixer had been the one making the steps, calling her to meet and chat, or have a coffee, or simply taking news. Until Demoneye's return, until the darkness had come back... until they had worked together against him, in what now appeared to be the last time. It hadn't been friendship or old memories that had reunited them in the end, but blood and shadows... darkness and deceit.... leaving one of them blind, and the other half-dying from the cursed wounds. Darkness and lies. Oh yes, Jen had been angered when Demonantous had then taunted her with the facts, smirking about how Raquel had been 'punished' too. Angered, woried, sad, trying to call her, never finding her comm open, leaving a message in absence that had never been answered to. And then the realization, over the following weeks, had been clear enough. Raquel hadn't called either. Raquel had preferred bring her 'problem' back to the Clans... back to people who never had been in contact with the nightmare, back to people who hadn't anything to see with this sad story. And Raquel hadn't called her once, either.

What fer... why calling this stupid, idiot Jenae, huh... The harsh memories caused the corners of her mouth to twist in bitter folds. I've walked to tha darkness fer her... I let Damon Fadri bit me.. infect me.. .to understand her pain, to be closer to tha source, to become more able to help!... and I wouldn't have done that if not fer Raquel... and what fer... I'm dying, I'll probably kill my friends in tha process, while SHE comes to Rompa's paying a visit... Why should I bother!

Jen had given up, plain and simply, only looking from far away, from time to time, hearing about how the Agent went on going to Reets, to the parties, being seen with her friends, taking care of their Alliance.. .whatever was keping her busy, now that she seemed to be physically better too. Simply watching, worrying, thinking to the old times, swallowing the bitterness. It had been plainly stupid, a wish condemned to never be fulfilled. How could they be friends any longer, when the gap had already been digged without them knowing it? How come she had still dare try? Now, she had Vector Blue to lead. She wouldn't associate with Raquel anymore. She had her job, her duties to fill, the daily monitoring, her role within Project Infinite Circle... the trust placed in her by tha Administration who had given her the rights to open her own department. She had friends again, even though she had needed months to learn how to trust people again. She had Lemmy, her joyful little daughter whom she was more and more proud of. She had Hayley, who had tasted the nightmare, and they were lending themselves shoudlers mutually, supporting their pain since these awful days.

The young woman sadly smiled at this thought. The two Fixers hadn't know each other for as long as Jen had known Raquel, but Hayley had opened her heart to her way more than Raquel had ever done. Maybe they hadn't as much fun together this times, true - but they could share way more. The love and worry for their daughters - their experience of young mothers, so to say. Their work. Their concerns. Their projects. Their problems, too. Letting out a long, tired sigh, Jen closed her eyes for a moment. Friendship wasn't only about being happy together. It was also being able to voice the troubles, to seek comfort in the other. Something she hadn't really seen Raquel ever do, even when her problems had begun to arise... her family, her worries, her doubts - doubts that she had very likely brought to her new Clanner friends , slowly, if unconsciously, beginning to already plan her defection, maybe? - doubts that she had refused to tell Jen, when she asked her.

"...No. Ain't worth tha trouble..." the Fixer whispered again, reporting her attention on her laptop, trying to wave the sadness and resentment away. "I've enuff to bother 'bout without adding this to tha strain. She doesn't care 'nymore. Fine fer meh. I dun't care either."

And, though knowing that her words weren't as true as she would have wanted them to be, Jen tried to resume her work.
