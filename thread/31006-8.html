Re: Re: 14.4 Where is crat changes?
quote:
Originally posted in patch notes
The sun will no longer set in the training grounds. Permanent daylight will make things a little easier for new players.
I haven't gone through this thread, so I'm not sure if anyone's commented on this yet. But, in my opinion, this is a mistake. When I was 'new' to AO, the night and day cycles were something I got a taste of in the backyards/training grounds, and one of the things that made me think "this game is cool". There's no denying that AO has some magnificent sunrises and sunsets, which I got to see plenty of while back there. The area looks continually different throughout the day as the amount of light available changes. It, in some respects, kept me from getting bored.

Honestly, how much easier do new players need things to be? I managed to get through new player playing easily enough, and that was back when the servers were crashing every 10 minutes with 30 second pauses during battles. I would find it hard to believe that you're ever going to lose players because it got darker in the training grounds at night. What you are losing, though, is a fairly cool element that helps immerse players at a stage where youre just starting out and wow-factor is all you see.

Having said that, and having started a character in all three backyards types.. if there is any problem, its that the Omni-Tek training grounds are too darn dark. I remember having a hard time training there (even when I was familiar with the game) because I had an extremely difficult time seeing there at night. All you could make out was the blue energy field around the shop area. The Clan and Neutral backyards were fine, though. I recall the Clan training yard having nice torches helping keep the place suitably lit. Maybe Omni-Tek could invest in some of those.
