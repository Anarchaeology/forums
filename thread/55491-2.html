Now to add to the ideas.

I was thinking the other day about NW towers. I'm guessing that whoever owns the towers, guildies and friends will be hanging out there to guard their property. Housing for guilds and housing for individuals should be there on the property and I have the idea:

In NW we will be mining for Notum. Machines working endlessly to mine. After a area is mined there is usually an empty space left afterwards. This mined out area could then be utilized by the owners to have a living area/meeting place.

The front door would be on the towers and a person would zone into a underground mine looking area. The longer the mine is in operation the bigger the mine gets. If the towers are destroyed the mine area is also destroyed and anything in it is destroyed as well. This gives an added bonus towards fighting incentive and this would follow with the losses of war. Keep your valuables/rares in the bank.

Allow the owners to customize the area of the mine( ie. texture changes to give the look of an office area, med lab, tech lab etc.). Allow the owner to declare the entrance room open to the general public and give us our own vending machines for those entrance rooms so we can sell stuff. When players are used to going to the same private vendor and return one day to find it destroyed, the effects of war are felt again.

This could be yet another money sink. Of course the ideas from here on out are endless. I refer you to any and all housing/apt idea links.
