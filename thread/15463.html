Team Wrangles and 14.2 - Game Balance Solution
I see room for improvement in Team Wrangling to balance out the OE changes that would make everyone happy in accordance with OE changes and game balance while promoting teaming.

A tiny bit of background info. For Traders to function at an effective level we need to be able to use all our nano lines - Heal, Mez, Nano Lends, Skills xfer, etc. Traders will not be able to contribute to a group as significantly as others if we run around with all negative skills. Going with that - Deprives/Divests/Ransacks/Plunders need to be a dependable nano for the Trader to xfer skills with. Traders are the middle man for trading skills. Take from mob, give to group. We Trade.

Ok here goes.

Team Wranlges after the OE patch will only allow slighly higher QL nanos to be used and increase the ATTACK rating of the group slightly.

Why? Problem will be this after 14.2. While teamed, Team Wrangling is not emminently effective because of the seldom occourance of having both of these conditions met at this same time grouped:

1) Team Wrangle is up 100% of the time without heavy penalty to the Trader.

>Because of mobs' resist to Ransack/Plunder and the extremely short duration of the nano this is near impossible to maintain 100% of the time on teammates. If you take the chance and OE your gun because of that Wrangle, you need to count on it or it wears off and you have the OE penalty.


2) Chances that team mates ( all peers near equal level) having better equipment on-hand and within Traders Wrangle bonus.

Obviously people used to plan it out all calculating implants, other buffs including IC, Mochams, etc and get a Wrangle finally enabling them to equip whatever gun/pet while in front of BB to a decided advantage. Now, there will be no planning because the team will spontaneously form and people will need to have the Wrangle on 100% of the time to use that Wrangled piece 100% effectively with fighting gear on.

After we all get used to not being able to OE, who is going to carry around an effective group enhancing set of 21%+ overequipped gear {including nanos above your nano skills (including pets) and weapons} just for when teaming with a Trader? Only to achieve a slight advantage? Would it even make sense to do all that switching out for that slight advantage?

In the long run, not many will be able to benefit by in-team Wrangles as people will use to it and carry around under 20% gear mostly. How often has anyone been Team Wrangled by a Trader in your team out hunting (not talking about a 132 Wrangle when your level 4 for 250k) and used it to your advantage. How big was that advantage?

Given the disparity in QL of items the Team Wrangle may put someone in a new weapon or nano they have in inventory but that can be unpredictable. When the Team Wrangle wears off in
three minutes you would be better off with your usual gun not even counting the fact you lost time/damage changing out weapons. This is amplified for pet users who would get 0% percent usage when the Wrangle wears off prematurely after current target is killed by the pet.

I constantly struggle with that fact, as a support Trader who doesn't really fight for damage, to either land a Deprive or pray to land a Ransack so I can use a decent QL Team Heal, Mez, whatever.

I propose a better solution than what we will be left with. It would be much better if Team Wrangles in addition to increasing skill IP would directly increase damage with pets, nanos and weapons on items currently equipped on a lesser scale than the negative OE modifiers. Say like +%5, %7, %10 increased effectiveness for every 2%, 4%, 6% your skill is over the required skills for the item. This would be just like OE modifiers in reverse. Give damage bonuses in percentage increments.

Face it, after this patch we are gonna need extra damage. Weapons already take into account your attack skill for that particular weapon to calculate damage, but lets take that one step further. This would be a seperate calculation all together and would be a bigger difference.

Also, one of the key notes here is applying this to Nano Formulas and pets. Having a Team Wrangle up running in NCU should increase the effectiveness of your current pet and NFs.

This might be hard to code for the nano and pet bonuses but the devs could play around with fumbling rates, "Nano aborted" rates, adding nano crits (do we already have that?), pet HP, pet crit, pet attack rating.

This might be too much and fall under some other spells lines' responsibility but I think along with the OE changes it is now a given to bump up the durations of skill drains and Team Wrangles otherwise the system would not near be near as effective. After leaving a grouped I don't want to be paying for that Team Wrangle forever but I would say, maybe up the duration to a total of 7-10 minutes. That's not asking for much.

The current system of keeping both drains up to be able to perform any other functions (Team Heal, etc.) is completely necessary yet takes much to much upkeep. I would like a little more effectiveness.

Keeping up both skills drains every 3 minutes is just pure monkey work on the keyboard.



Thanks for reading,
