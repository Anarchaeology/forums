(RK2)Omni-Tek Secret Service Fight Night Oct 19th, 1800 GMT @Omni-Ent Arena
OTSS Fight Night. October 19th, 1800 GMT@ Omni-Ent Arena.

This event will be ON RK2

Yes, after a long, long breakwe bring you the Fight Night, sponsored by the Omni-Tek Secret Service. Ever had a grudge? Ever been ticked off and wanting to kill someone? Just wanted to kill someone for the heck of it? Do you even enjoy the art of ganking?

Then this fight night is for you!

This event will consist of the following

1v1
2v2
Battle Royale

To maximize fairness, albeit is still not entirely fair, the 1v1 Battles will have profession groups as follows:

MP/Crat/Engi

Soldier/Enforcer/Adventurer

Agent/Fixer/Trader

Doc/MA/NT

So now your thinkinghey how are levels going to be organized?

Well, thats a game day call because we dont know how many will show up! If it gets bad enough, we will have saymaybe a set amount of seconds handicap for the lower level. Lets just see how it goes!

2v2 Battles will be the same dealhowever 2v2 has no profession groups, just pick your teammate and get ready to rumble!

Note: You may participate in all THREE events if you wish, just let us know your intentions beforehand.

Battle Royale: All participants level 75 or above are eligible to participate in this event. There will be no reward for this battle royale, but hey, there is reward enough in killing, isnt there?

PRIZES:

Various prizes will be awarded to the best fighters/groups. These prizes will range from various weapons/armor, to nanos, as well as credits. Prizes will be given out at the registration site. The quality of the rewards will depend on the turnout!

REGISTRATION:

Game Day registration will take place at the Rompa Bar in Omni-Entertainment, located at 702, 683. Or, go directly NE from arena, you will see the signs! Registration fee is 100 credits per level.(IE, if your level 100, you pay 10000 credits, etc) This will be used to partially defray the cost of rewards.

Who can register?

Any Omni-Tek, Neutral OR Clan Member can register for this event. Clan Members do so at their own risk.

ORG Registration:

Before game day or on game day, if an org wants to participate, someone from that org can give us some goods for rewards, and everyone from that org pays no registration fee!

PREREGISTRATION:

You can register before this event by contacting one of the registration people via tell. They will get in touch with you ASAP and you can pay your money beforehand. If you advance in levels before the event, your not paying extra!

Registering a friend:

You can pay for your buddy to participate, but we have to confirm their profession/level beforehand!

NOTE: All registration monies and rewards will be NON-Refundable.

RULES FOR THE FIGHT

All fights will last for FIVE minutes(With the exception of battle royale, which will last until there is only one person left alive)

Fights will commence with the word BEGIN, and end with the words CEASE FIRE. If the match does not result in the death of one side, then the health of the individuals will be used. A percentage number will be used to determine the winner. In a team, each individual is 50% of the total health of the team! Please make sure to insure beforehand. We are not responsible for lost experience, PvP Titles, or items.

Lag Death:

We are not responsible for lag deaths. If, after BEGIN has been shouted, your link terminates, and you are killed, you have lost the match. If you lose link with Rubi-Ka before the match starts, another match will commence in its place and give you time to return!

If your not there when called

If you are not around, or distracted, whatnot when your name is called, another match will go in its place. If your not there a second time(a couple other fights will go first) you forfeit your match.

Buffing:

You may acquire any buffs you wish from friends, etc, before you step into Arena. Once you step into arena, all outside buffs WILL CEASE. You may buff yourself or your teammate once youve stepped into arena, and that will be it.

RULES Enforcement:

We take our rules very strictly. We will give the benefit of the doubt for those with slow machines, but only up to five seconds. If you attack beyond that, you FORFEIT. We intend to employ a few title five/six or high title four individuals to provide security in the arena during the event. In the event that we have issues with compliance, security will KILL the offending individual. You will pop up at reclaim, so no big deal!

Main event organizer: Milford Jawsman Mustache, President, Omni-Tek Secret Service.(The guy you give your money items to if Shondoctor isnt around)

Treasurer/Registration Coordinator: Meda Shondoctor Olveda, Unit Commander, Omni-Tek Secret Service.(The person you give your money, and items too. Also the person who will be organizing the fights overall)



Contact either of us with any questions you might have about this event.


Respectfully Sent,

Milford "Jawsman" Mustache
Chief Engineer
President, Omni-Tek Secret Service
