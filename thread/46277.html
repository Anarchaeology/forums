I do not use weapon. I do not think you should, exept if you want to PvP.

I'm newbie crat, but I got from my doc/engie/meta experience that fighting raise agro, slow down your casting and... mess everything.
With my first doc, I tried to use weapons, and I discovered that it was fine... while fight was soft. But deadly when it was tought. Rather than increasing my power, weapons was reducing it by a lot.
That's why I stoped to use weapons, and learned how to life without.
It looks the same aply to every casters.

I have read many stuffes from doc, crat, NT... complaining about the lack of team "common sens". My reply to such had always been the same: they have to do their jobs despite the team fight.
Doc and crat are overpower. Both could dream to dictate a team it's tactics. I do not want to do that, I prefer to adapt me to the team I'm in. It gives a best chalenge.

I imagine some crats complains because they do not get fun at protecting a team. They should pick another prof, imho.
It's like those big atrox enf or ma complaining about AO being just "mass killing", they make no sens...
If the only raison you pick crat prof is to be "Uber", I seriously doubdt you'll like it.

By now that I learned how to life without weapons, using them with casters looks loosy a max to me.
When I want to throw some nice brawl, I relog with my ma. To use a cool mauser, I would create a fixer... and so on.
With all my chars I do my job, not someone else one.

Okay, sometime, I read stuffes from brainless guy who try to convince with args like: "weapon=more damage" and "more damage=more power".
That's just bull****. It's capacity to deal damages count for nothing when talking about a crat or a doc power.
Crat's power is its capacity to block adds. Rest are cherries over the cake. You can fight if you like, but do not imagine it count positively in the mind of your teamate that died while you did it.

Just to weigh in on the pro-weapon side:

I use a weapon because it allows me to focus on the wider situation. I sic my bot and pet, hit 'Q', then start rotating the camera in third-person to check for adds.

I find when I am chain-nuking, I am more likely to focus on a what we're fighting, and less likely to notice things that we're not fighting YET .

Also, I like to save my nano for the times when it goes: pulling...add...add, countered twice...oh shoot, add...add, run?

Like a lot of things, it's probably as much a matter of playstyle than anything else.
