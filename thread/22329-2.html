1. make pvp death downtime zero: this has the effect of speeding up pvp. right now one of the most annoying things about pvp is cooling your heals waiting to rez if you die.

2. make damage 100% again: for everyone complaining about 1 hit kills and fights going to fast see number 1. if downtime is zero and you can pop out of a reclame run back in and drop the guy who just fragged you its no problem.

3. give pvp objectives: add outposts to capture items to take from one base to another base kind of like capture the flag. giving pvp a solid point adds depth and is only helpfull. cause when you really look at it, currently pvp is pointless.

4. remove titles: even though id never thought of its i am in favor of it. titles lead to the behavior that we see today with zone hugging and ganking.

5. add a wounded status that happens when hp hit zero in pvp, the player falls to the ground keeping all buffs on but needs a doctor to come pick them up. this lets docs act as doctors in combat not the ultra self healing shotgun commandos they are now.

6. provide a neutraly located arena so that sides can duel without the cheesyness of doing it in a political area.

Alot of these changes rely on other changes to work. I agree 100% damage would suck if downtime for pvp was not cut but i wouldnt mind dying fast so much if i could get back into the fight right away. Fast paced gameplay promotes more fun especially if objectives are added.
