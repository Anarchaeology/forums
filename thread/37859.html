Dust Brigade
In relation to what story has happened. I've noticed people seem to ignore, when talking about the story, the Dust Brigade. When most people consider the story they only think about the Clans against Omni-Tek. While this is important to the story, the Dust Brigade and the people behind the Dust Brigade are obviously major to the storyline as a whole. When they mention evil vs. good I wonder that the true evil isn't what everyone thinks it is(Omni-Tek).
A side note: to say that your omni and you have to be evil and your clans and you have to be good (which Ive had heard said on these forums by people not Funcom) is rediculous. In real life it doesn't work that way, and in the game it doesn't work that way. I also mean it was never meant to work that way.

P.S. Please don't mention the fact that there is no active story. I'm talking about past events and what I've gathered of Funcom's future story plans.
