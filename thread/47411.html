I want original org features before new org features
I'm one of those who has no intention of buying the booster and for one simple reason: Organizations aren't what they were originally promised, so why should I go buy something from someone who already hasn't delivered on what was promised?

Bugs in the bank/tax system, governingform's that are meaningless (and senseless with OT Monarchy's and Clan Departments), org contract that doesn't work, Clan org advantage which has never been delivered, plus the whole system for managing the orgs seems rather "inellegant".

If you can deliver on the original promises and design then I'd be more optimistic about any "extras" like land-control, org property (towers, guild-houses), clothes, etc, whatever that you babble about now.

I know people in-game are bothered by the lack of delivery and "boosting" what is already bugged and incomplete. Does this bother anyone else on the forum? (I know the forums are a very different community) and does anyone at Funcom care and is anything being done to get us some proper patching before the booster is released?

Responses from anyone and especially funcom personnel would be much appreciated.
