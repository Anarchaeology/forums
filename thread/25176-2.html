Hey everyone, again.

Ready for a classic Fadinaway novel?

Ok, the basic answer to the first question is the obvious answer I would get in the Role-Playing and Story sections. Yes, you want events. Believe me, I want them too.

There are many remarks about how to deal with the event spoilers, but probably the best possible answer was Ignore them. This would be up to the players as well as the events team. The main issue is to make sure everyone is having fun, and spoilers are ruining it for you, as much as us teleporting them out is ruining it for them. So, we can ignore them and hope they go away like a nice roach, or give them a couple warnings and then teleport them to a nice safe shop somewhere and hope they shop away their troubles, or we could RP them to death. ;p Again, some of that is up to the players.

Getting Players Involved.
Barring any mechanic changes, as this cannot be a guarantee, we can say the best way would be announcing or having news in game before or during events. This can limit which kind of events can happen. There are some events that should be announced, mainly community events (Fight Clubs, Leet Fights, Dance Parties, etc), but some that really shouldnt. Clues are always a good idea, but most of the events are built up and that leaves clues as to what may be happening. For instance, the Cyborg events were a series of events leading to a series of results. (Cyborg attacks, Cyborg buildups, Cyborgs looking for body parts, etc) You really have to be on the look out and have people in guilds communicate what is happening. The clues are there, but like any clue they have to be found or communicated.

Player events and GM assistance
I have noted suggestions of how GMs can assist in player events. Most of these involve the GMs role-playing a part or, in some cases, spawning monsters.

The first step in addressing this is to talk about what GMs can do vs. Players and how they can both benefit events.

GM Event Powers
1. GMs can teleport people.
2. GMs can spawn items and monsters.
3. GMs can create and populate dungeons.
4. GMs can use special effects (buffing effects, etc).
5. GMs can use all public chat channels.
6. GMs can create or become various characters.

Player Event Powers
1. Players can network with 1000s of other players
2. Players can lead guilds
3. Players can communicate with guilds and guild leaders.
4. Players can use public chat channels / guild channels.
5. Players can create various characters
6. Players can role-play.
7. Players know what they want to accomplish in their own events.

GM Abilities seem to be best suited for the more world dynamic events creation, dungeon creation, monster attacks, etc.

Player Abilities seem to be best suited for the role-playing, networking, atmosphere, guild conflict type events.

Does this mean GMs shouldnt assist in player events? No, but it does mean GM abilities should be used in a way that best exploits the abilities. It also means the player opportunities are endless in a conflict game. You cant blow up buildings, but you can continuously build and create conflict.

If a GM were to come into the game as an NPC and play the informant, that would be great. But, wouldnt it be even better to convince another player to be your informant? Or, for the sake of guild, build a character and make him your role-playing informant? This is a role that could be just as easily played by a player as it could be by a GM, and it would actually add to the atmosphere for one more player.

A GM could easily come in to create the nemesis guild, but wouldnt it be better to actually have a nemesis guild? It is much easier for a guild leader to contact another guild leader and set up this role-played conflict than it is to rely on GMs to create emotionless NPCs to act as a nemesis. Imagine the possibilities if only the guild leaders knew it was just a scenario created by them. And, imagine how much fun it would create for even more players.

Now, both of these examples are easily created by GMs, but you are taking abilities and scenarios that can easily be done by players and asking the GMs to do them, therefore removing the GMs ability to create events that cant be done by players. (Creating and populating Dungeons, Monster Attacks, Stories etc.) I am not saying we can come in and make a dungeon for a specific guild, but I am saying that making dungeons or other dynamic abilities could be a more effective use of the resource when the other events you mentioned are much more effective when player driven.

Xenomorph  Hi.

There was also mention of getting news up on the site related to events and Rubi-Ka 2. Any player is able to submit news, but it may not all get on the site. You can submit news at the following link: http://www.anarchy-online.com/conten...le/rubikanews/
Please feel free to send something.
I am also going to get some more information on the Rubi-Ka Times and Voice of Freedom channels for Rubi-Ka 2. Unless there is a really good explanation, they should be there.


So, to end this long post; we will not stop events, we just need to find a way to improve the delivery. The events are happening, a lot of it comes down to communication. How we can better communicate events to you, and how you can better communicate events to each other? My first step is getting information on the channels, and making sure they are used by the Events Team once they are in place.
