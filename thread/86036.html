Higher AS modifies the amount by which the regular AS damage is multiplied.

For instance (and all PvM), right now I'm a level 74 agent using a ql 98 X-3, and my base AS damage is ~600, which I rarely hit for. I've hit AS as high as 6.5k, and usually hit for ~1.2k.

I think that crit % modifies this further. When I cast sureshot, hit sneak and crack off an AS, the higher multiplier occurs MUCH more often (it was under sureshot that I hit the 6.5k AS, and that was at level 69).

It also seems that evades do play a part in how likely it is that you will get a multiplier.
