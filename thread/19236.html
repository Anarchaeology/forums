Ganking in Arena...
Okay, now I'm probably gonna get some flames (Well it's your fault for going into arena in the first place! It's mayhem zone, etc) for this, but the ganking in the arena is extremely annoying. The arena is a place for duels, not to gank. I do not care about ganking in other mayhem or political zones, as that is perfectly fine with me, but not the Arena.

I play on Rubi-Ka-2 and it is getting pretty bad there, and I bet it is probably even worse on RK1. Ganking is especially bad at the Tir Arena because it is much easier to run from it than at the Omni Ent arena.

This situation could easily be fixed by simply having a popup screen of who is trying to attacking you, and yes to accept the duel, and no to decline it. Then a 15 second timer would count down to the beginning of the duel, instead of a 15 second timer when entering the zone.
