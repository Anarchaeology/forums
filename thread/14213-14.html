quote:
Hello all,

I've been logging the situations in which the disconnects seem to occur, and it boils down to a combination of issues working to disconnect the game from the internet.

I believe the problem boils down to the video card losing the refresh messages from the server. The video card drivers + the loss of packets from delayed internet traffic seems to cause the video card to try and refresh when there is no information available to draw, thus causing the video card to give an error message to the OS which shuts down the offending originator of the invalid draw message, which in this case happens to be the TCP/IP stack. The problem occurs much less frequently on DSL or Cable based systems, since the packet loss is much less than on modem systems.

There are beta Nvidia drivers for the GeForce 4 MX440 which seem to alliviate the internet dropping, but cause other video card instabilities and therefore are not suitable for all system configurations.

Funcom needs to figure out a way to cache the draw messages, and error trap invalid draw messages so that the video card doesn't attempt to draw according to a lost packet of information. They can't assume that the video card drivers can error trap invalid draw messages itself, since most cards assume that a draw message includes a pointer to what needs to be drawn.

Anyways, this is what seems to be the problem to me, though I may not be able to articulate it elegantly. I hope someone can figure out a solution, because it is quite frustrating when the game is otherwise very fun.
