Official DA reply concerning Baboons
~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*

In a surprising developement in the entertainment community yesterday, veteran party organizer LevRivers disclosed a conversation with Dave "Diamondcut" Duster in which Lev was handed over ownship of "Baboons" nightclub. Mr. Duster has been missing for the past five months, and this surprise conversation has shocked many of his friends and business partners who were not contacted.

Baboons has been under the management of Devil's Advocate for the past six months. Devils Advocate has been working tirelessly to fix the strange vein of supression gas in the club which slows movement and prevents speech. At their last party, Engineer Znore, a suppression gas specialist observed the strange phenomenon, and was in agreement that what he labled as "lag", sucked.

While rumor suggests that actual ownership of the nightclub is in dispute due to supposed forged documents, Devils Advocate is pleased to turn over the management of the nightclub to LevRivers and wishes him success in all of his future plans. Director Thyme was contacted for her reaction and replied "Hell, we snuck it out from under him when we got it. Turn about is fair play. Devils Advocate has moved on from hosting parties to... well, "other" activities. But you can bet your arse that we sure will show up to welcome Lev and pick pocket his guests."

~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*
