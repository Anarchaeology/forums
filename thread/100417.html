/reply They knew this would come. They accepted the risk. So should you.
/tell Loren I do accept the risk, but this is all happening too damn fast! No one was prepared. A day has not passed without massive outbreaks of violence. The atmosphere between us and the clans is more strained than ever. And your presence just makes it worse.
/reply Do I sense a whisper of doubt? You do still believe in the cause, dont you? That is why we are here.
/tell Loren What cause would that be Loren? I am beginning to wonder...
/reply Now, now, lets be civil.
/tell Loren Seriously. What am I to think? I worked my ass off to get the council to hire you and your posse, and what happens. Shiny outfits. Big guns. Even bigger words. And mayhem! Our people are being wiped off the face of the planet as we speak.
/reply Not at all, not at all. Your people are just making choices as I predicted they would.
/tell Loren Oh, come on! We are being blamed by our own citizens of being Omni-Teks slaves. And it is because of your affiliations with them, however slim they are.
/reply I would not have taken this job if I did not have my doubts. You know that.
/tell Loren Whatever. Cause or no cause, were being labelled Omni-Tek scum by the clans. They jump on this excuse to take our bases down.
/reply Not all neutral organisations are being accused of siding with Omni-Tek. Some of them, actually parttook in the battles against Elite Operations and the annihalation of their base.
/tell Loren So what! So What! Dont play blind Loren, these organisations are now labelled clan sympathisers and that is no better. What about the true neutrals, the population that the organisations are supposed to protect and stand up for? Who will take care of them?
/reply Now who is being blind? You know full well why neutral guards were not instructed to kick the clans out of Newland City.
/tell Loren We should never have done anything in the first place.
/reply You do not see the whole picture. You went to the man beforehand, he will take care of this. Dont wear yourself out worrying. I am here, you helped me get here. Now deal with it. Im turning my comm-link off now. I have things to do.

ICC Peacekeeper says: What is going on?

ICC Peacekeeper: What did I miss?
