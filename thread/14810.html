Input please...reactivating account
My current version is 12.5. I have a L40+ Adventurer, an L40+ Doctor, and a L30+ Agent.

I started the Agent as my last character. I last logged into the game in September or October of 2001, I believe. The reasons I left were: lack of stability (still crashing out of the game 50% of the time when zoning into crowded cities), lag in crowded areas (basicaly a 1 fps slideshow on a top of the line Alienware system. But the main reason I quit playing and cancelled two accounts was the way Funcom made my main characters (Doc and Adventurer) useless to play when they removed Freedom Arms pistols from the game.

So I started an Agent, which bored me to tears. I finally cancelled my accounts in disgust.

I've read about all the great improvements in the game, and since DAoC has become stale as hell, have decided to oil up my rusty AO gameplay skills and try to get back into it.

Most of my issues that caused me to quit the game are so passe and old that I have no way on the forums to tell if they have been addressed. What I am begging for is the opinions of those who have been playing since release, if any of you are still around.

Are my Adventurer and Doctor (both Clan, btw) worth playing now? All I could do with my adventurer was use Concealment and reet form to try and do Hide and Run missions for money and items (basically looking for a Yalmaha for my Doctor). The Doc was simply put away and not played again when it became impossible to equip him with FA's his level or higher. Both are pistol users, and the only pistols in the game really sucked butt.

Please give me your opinions, they would be much appreciated!
