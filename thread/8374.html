I know that this doesn't appeal to a lot of players, but when I get bored of missions or hunting, I just get on guild chat and see if any of the low level guys need me to make implants or go flying around for a while. I'm a clanner, so there always something new to see in Omni territory. I also enjoy making deals- love to comb the trade channels and haggle with some guy for a useful piece of equipment for myself or a guildmate.

I think that one of the most enjoyable things to do away from the normal grind is to organize a guild event. I have a good idea right now for an open event to be hosted by my guild, but I'm not letting it out yet until I have the specifics hammered out. I have a feeling that it will be a big success though.

There is a guide to player created events somewhere on this site that is very helpful. I don't know exactly where I found it, but it's on the offical site somewhere.

I think that the bottom line is that players often leave the game becuase they aren't into the social aspects of the game. If you're not into that stuff OR aren't interested in PvP or getting to lvl200 then you can't really expect for a game to hold your interest for more than a few months. I love when new content comes out, but it can never keep up with the players want for it.

I suggest that if you're into the social aspects of the game, join a guild (if you're not in one), create your own events amongst your guild or make it multi-guild. If you can get in contact with a guild that is on the opposite side of you, it can get really interesting, with players competeing for a common goal or opposing goals

(i.e.- player A has been captured by guild B and is being interrogated. Recon forces have determined that player A is being held somewhere in 2ho [this could be a sub-mission for members interested in investigation work, with clues given by the event coordinator] the enemy stronghold is some where near 800x1200. Go there and rescue player A . Do it at all costs)

Stuff like this is great fun, but you need to have someone who is interested in thinking up stuff and organizing.

Just a suggestion
