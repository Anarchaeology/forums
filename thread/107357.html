Tikon, your logic is flawed...

Rubika is the only known planet where notum is found. But let's, for the sake of argument, assume that notum is indeed a reusable resource...notum isn't 'consumed' when it's used but released back into the environment or recycled.

If, for simplicities sake, we say Rubika has 1 million 'units' of notum within it's biosphere. And if eventually all 1 million units are packed up and shipped one unit to each of 1 million other worlds then the concentration of notum in the air of ANY of those one million other worlds may be too low to sustain nanomage life.

It MAY be that a small and compact notum breathing apparatus may be able to be built BUT it will eventually come to pass that the notum stripped from Rubika is 'bound up' in other applications across a million inhabited worlds in this galaxy. Rubika's notum, spread across the galaxy, is going to be a rather valuable resource and I rather imagine manufacturers of notum products will do anything they can to recycle the notum and not let it drift off into the atmosphere. Who's to say that that kind of recycling can't be applied to nanomage use also but all recycling has inefficiencies and leaks.

Is it right to force the entire nanomage breed into competing for notum for their very survival with the manufacturers of notum-powered self-cleaning toilets? Is it any more right than if you forced each and every living organism to compete for the right to breath oxygen? Or drink water?

And your comment about if you're Clan then that's just too bad is uncaring of sentient life and rather shortsighted at best. If you look forward in time to when nanomages are no longer found ony on Rubika but live across the galaxy using your postulated breathing apparatus then you're likely to be living in a galaxy where there are quite a few non-OT employee nanomages. Do you actually condem ALL non-OT employees galaxy-wide? Don't you imagine that's carrying corporate loyalty a BIT too far?
