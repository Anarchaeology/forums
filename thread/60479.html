FFS-Implement Pathing Workarounds
We all know pathing will never be fixed.

Considering for all pet profs, this results in a loss of damage over time, it is a serious issue.

Now, think.

If weapons/fists were suddenly not firing 30% of the time...or hell, 70% of the time, do you think Funcom would just say...we're working on it?

This problem has been around so long (forever) that all pet profs have learned to not rely on their pets. It has been around so long (forever) that it has become quite the fad to say 'it is broken, just deal'.

Well, **** that. Some soldier sure as hell wouldn't say 'I will just chill' if their weapons didn't work most of the time. But...after a year? Maybe they would.

Entropy and apathy have settled over this issue. Current pathing has gone from being a serious bug to being the norm for game play.

Well, **** that too.

I challenge you, Funcom, to provide a work around that does not require spamming /pet behind. I challenge you to do this before this thread is 50 pages long.

I will bump this thread every damn day as well.
