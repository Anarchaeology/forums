an idea
Why not give soldiers the ability to specialize in some kind of weapon or combat skills to make them particularly good with those weapons/skills, but maybe weaker with everything else(thus locking them into one kind of weapon or weapon skill).

The specialization should be linked to a bracer item that can only be obtained from certain NPCs(maybe 3 npcs, one in Omni-1 Trade, one in uh Tir, one in Newland? OT/Clan/Neutral). It's no-drop, unique, and can not be removed without being destroyed. Such bracers will only be available once per title change, giving you up to 5 chances to pick your specialization. You could spend all your choices on the same bracer type to upgrade an old bracer, or you could destroy an old bracer(and all its upgrades, if any) to pick a new bracer type.

There would be three spec options:

#1: Skill spec

The Soldier selects 1(and only one) ranged attack skill(yes, this includes heavy weapons, ranged energy, bow, basically anything that can affect the attack rating of a ranged weapon). That skill is multiplied by 1.25 for the purpose of determining attack rating when using a weapon dependant upon this skill. Example: troosoldjah69 gets to title level 2 and picks Assault Rifle. With his 200 in AR skill(I'm making this up), his AR skill is considered to be 250 when using his nova flow. Drawback: all other ranged attack skills are multiplied by .9 for the purpose of calculating attack rating with a weapon. So his RE skill of 200 would be reduced to 180 for the purpose of calculating his attack rating with his nova flow. Note that this specialization would NOT affect any ranged attack skills for the purprose of actually equipping any weapons.

Upgrade options: picking this bracer type multiple times at subsequent title changes could either:

a). increase the multiplier on the chosen skill by .03 and all other ranged attack skills by .01 per upgrade
b). add a second chosen skill, though a second chosen skill would only have a positive multiplier of 1.15, and all other ranged combat skills would have a multiplier of .80(primary chosen skill would still be 1.25). Option b). could only be picked once.

option a). could be applied to either the primary or secondary chosen skill if option b). is picked early, but it would only apply to one chosen skill or the other per title change.

Note that these multipliers would apply to skill bonuses from equipment, implants, and buffs(i.e. the multiplier is rated against the current effective skill, not the base skill).

#2: Weapon spec

The Soldier selects a single "type" of weapon("type" being defined as a gun that has multiple qls, but the same basic name, functions, damage ratios, and skill requirements. Cheap Heavy Suppressor and Custom Heavy Suppressor are the same type, but Heavy Suppressor and Light Supressor are not). The Soldier is now dedicated to the use of this weapon and this weapon only. While using this type of weapon, the Soldier multiplies all his ranged combat skills by 1.30 for all purposes, including attack rating and determining whether or not the weapon can be equipped. Note that, unlike in option #1, this multiplier will logically apply to skills like fling shot, burst, full auto, etc, though this will likely only have a bearing on whether or not the weapon can be equipped(aimed shot may be an exception here. Not sure what high "special attack" skill really does other than facilitate equipping of weapons). Weapons of the chosen type can never be OE in this Soldier's hands under any circumstance. Drawback: whenever using another type of weapon, the Soldier multiplies all ranged combat skills by .80 for the purpose of determining attack rating and whether or not the weapon can be equipped. The weapon will be OE if the modified skills drop even 1 point below the weapon's standard requirements. Note that skills such as fling shot, burst, etc will again only be modified for the purpose of determining whether or not the gun can be equipped, and will not determine attack rating or whether or not the gun is OEd.

Upgrade options: Only one option would exist for this specialization type. Picking this type of bracer multiple times(in the same weapon) would increase the multiplier for the chosen weapon type by .03 per upgrade without changing the multipler on other weapon types. The Soldier can not specialize in two weapon types at the same time.

Note that, as with spec option #1, all multipliers from option #2 would apply to effective skills, not base skills.

#3 Alpha Striker

The Soldier elects to become an "alpha striker" when choosing this type of bracer. This bracer grants a flat bonus of +100 to the following skills: Fling Shot, Burst, Full Auto, and Aimed Shot. Whenever executing an attack using one of the above skills, the Soldier's current attack rating is multiplied by 1.50. Drawbacks: Wearing this bracer applies a ranged init modifier of -300.

Upgrade options: Like spec option #2, there is only one upgrade path for the alpha striker Soldier. Picking this type of bracer multiple times will give the alpha striker Soldier +50 to Fling Shot, Burst, Full Auto, and Aimed shot per upgrade. The attack rating multipler for attacks using the above skills would be increased by .05 per upgrade. In addition:

First upgrade: Equip times on all weapons are reduced by 1s for the alpha striker Soldier, reducing them to a minimum of .5s.

Second upgrade: The alpha striker Soldier may now swap weapons while reloading an equipped weapon. Whenever equipping a new weapon, the weapon will be fully loaded with available ammunition as it is being equipped.

Third upgrade: The alpha striker Soldier may now equip/remove weapons regardless of whether or not the alpha striker Soldier is running or standing still or is in or out of combat.

Fouirth and final upgrade: The alpha striker may now inflict as much as 15000 damage in a single strike, surpassing the usual 10000 damage cap(there . . . is a 10k damage cap, isn't there?).

As with spec options #1 and #2, the multiplier in #3 applies to the effective attack rating of the Soldier, not the base.

Summary: Every time you move up a title, you get a specialization choice. At TL2, you can spend a choice on any of the above specializations(a skill, a weapon type, or alpha strikes). Each time you move up in title, you gain one more choice, which may either be spent upgrading your current bracer(symbolic of your specialization, unique/nodrop/can't be removed unless destroyed) or choosing an entirely new bracer, though you can not choose an entirely new bracer without destroying your old one. You won't get your previous choices back if you destroy an old bracer. By TL6, you should be able to upgrade a single bracer 4 times(upgrades at 3, 4, 5, and 6). You can "save up" choices accumulated from multiple title advancements(i.e. you don't lose your choices by refusing to make them until later in your career). All the bonuses and penalties would be bound to your bracer, so they'd be treated like bonuses/penalties from any other piece of armor/equipment.

Whew! That was a mouthful. Feel free to critique.
