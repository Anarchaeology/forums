What if...
DISCLAIMER: I do not want any flames. I am not saying I think this should be implemented, I just want to know what all of you think. Again, I AM NOT SAYING I THINK THIS SHOULD BE PUT IN.

Example: You cash in a level 2 alt.

xp needed to level to 2: 1000

So, you would get 1000 xp to transfer to another character.

What do you think? Would this be good, or bad? Do you want it in the game? Discuss.
