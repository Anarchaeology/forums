Tradeskills
Theres a couple things that could be done to make tradeskills worthwhile.

1) Give real XP for tradeskilling. 3-5 times what is currently given. Who gives a beerfart weather you get your levels via killing mobs or assembling implants? Theres room for both in this gameworld. It would STILL be more lucrative XP-wise to kill mobs, but at least the option to do something else then the mission grind or hunting.

2) Make player-assembled items better then stuff you can buy in the store or loot. This would be a HUGE enticement to get folks to do trades (which a heckuvalot of folks want to do anyway). Someting as small as a few more points of minimum damage would be a real bonus to using a player-built weapon, for example. Additinaly, the better you were at a particular tradeskill, the better the finshed product could be as compared to an identical store-bought item of equal QL.

3) Maker's marks. This has been mentioned elswhere, but it would be soooo dang cool if items we made bore our names. Maybe not implants, but armor/weapons and the like. For example, "ill-treated blackened blaster rifle, by Redtaype". I like the sound of that!

4) More access to the tools you really need to assemble stuff. PPE's are a glaring example. I have NEVER seen one drop as boss loot. Perhaps Im just unlucky. The point is, there are items that you =need= to secure to do certian types of tradeskilling, and these items are vanishingly rare. Make em worth a king's ransom, but put em into shops. Take it one step farther and perhaps only put them in class-shops, like fixer or trader terms.

(note: I can easily see how point 3 could cause database problems. So we're not likely so see it. But it would be undenyabaly cool!)
