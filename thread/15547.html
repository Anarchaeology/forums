They have more or less removed the concept of weight from the game. It used to be so that we had both an item limit and a weight limit, but having both was kind of redundant so it was removed.

For some time, even one mission worth of supplied (100 treatment kits, 500 rounds of ammo, some nano chargers and stims) was enough to put you over your weight limit. You could just forget about picking up loot.
