Quests
I was really looking forward to this patch. New quests!
Finally something other to do than mindless BS-missions over and over again. (I really respect those that reach title lvl 5+6 for their endurance cause I'm already bored to death)

Nice change to have a quest you can do in your own pace - get a nice item and some xp (nothing uber).

Profession quests - that was interessting I thought when I first heard about them. Something unique for every profession.

Then to my horror I found out that every profession quest you have to camp an uber mob. Yeay camping Thats enough to make me run away

Only other quest that seems to be there is the Commander Brock quest. A quest all can do. Oh but I forgot - only clan can wear the item...so I guess no quest for me :/ here either or any neutrals for that matter.

Quests in my oppinion are something you can do solo. Of course it doesn't mean that there shouldn't be quests for teams. But I was just wondering what happened to the quests for lowbees and the ones who like to solo? Rewards don't have to be uber but something usefull or something for your appartment.

We need more quests - for all lvs and all professions and factions. It makes the game more fun and especially more varied. They could be used to draw people to pvp zones or make them go explore Rubika.

Of course they should be available for all not just campers. I would rather spend two hours running around Rubika in order to talk to/aquire something/repair something/kill something in different places to complete a quest than sit on my butt camping - getting bored and wonder why I ever left AC in the first place.
