Am I here yet?
My take on the boss room is it shouldn't be a boss room at all. It should be a boss level. Place the mobs in random spots on the level, link them so if you aggro one you aggro all, and put in some code to make them wander all over the level.

What would happen is by the time you got to the teleporter into that level, the mobs could be anywhere. You could enter into an empty room. Or a couple mobs could be waiting for you. Whenever you do encounter a mob, it would trigger the rest to come running. This would provide uncertainty in completing the mission (Is anyone waiting for us? How many people could be waiting for us? ect.). It'd require tweaking to get right but it could easily be implemented over time. Especially since I seriously doubt these are the final form team missions.
