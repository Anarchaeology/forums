How about some persistance to the "War"?
I am new to AO...admittedly....

But I have an idea. I just left WWII Online because it is crap...but one idea there might make sense in AO and everyone's craving for RP: A persistent war.

Let Omni players take ground/fortifications/installations from clan members...and let Clanners do the same. Once ground is taken, it stays taken until the loosers take it back from the winners. That would get people's blood boiling.

In WWIIOnline the Allies started getting their butts kicked a while back by the Axis. Lots of towns fell (many people attributed this to changes made by a patch...but that is not important)...Once the allies realised we were getting thrashed we totally rallied, fought with passion, and took back lost ground. That was RP!! Made us MAD!!!!!

What do you folks think?
