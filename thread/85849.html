Blitzing, great money @ lvl 112
Hello all, I just wanted to let you in on a terrific way I've been making $$. I know this is not a brand new idea, not gonna change the world, but for all the new docs on the way up, its an excellent source of income.

Blitzing, of course. I've been blitzing for a couple days, mainly ql200 NCU, and at 1,000,000 creds each its well worth the effort The NCU's are highly desired by high level players and sell very quickly. Every set I've sold has moved in under 20 minutes. And with a bit of practice, I'm now able to blitz 1 NCU in about 15 minutes, that's 4 an hour, or 16,000,000 for 4 hours playtime. Not bad money. I've already picked up some great cash, and used it to buy some Über unique items for my Doc.

Here's a step by step;

1: STAY AT LVL 112!! You can't do it at any other level.

2: the most important single thing is a very high run speed. Now I would not recommend raising your run speed past 350 or so, just find a high level fixer (140+) and ask for their best run buff. This should easily put you to 800-1200 run speed in mission. While youre asking the Fixer for a run buff, make sure you get the next thing on the list, a Hot...

3: The second most important thing to have as much HP (health) as possible. A good fixer Hot (heal over time) is a wonderful way to maximize your available health, also you want to get a good essence from an enforcer (140+ also). The ess will add 800-900hp to your character, and a good hot will heal you for 200-400 every few seconds. As long as you keep moving while in mission, this is enough to for you to stay in the mission and look forever for the item\person you need to find. JUST KEEP MOVING!

3b: these buff's along with your own Hot, team hp buff, and individual hp buff (which your running, right??? will take up around 175-210 ncu. You'll want to make sure you've got a decent amount; otherwise you'll find yourself unable to get all the health possible...

4: you'll need good evades; I would recommend a minimum of 350.

5: good armor, either a cyber armor or go like i do and use Carbonum. I know its not the best armor for my doc, but I'm an addict.

Those are the things you will need for your character. Here are a few more you'll want to have to maximize your blitzing experience.

Clicksaver is a godsend. Without it, I seriously wouldn't consider blitzing anything. Just enter the item you want to find in the section, set the thing for about 500 retries and let'er rip! However, be careful... most missions you'll get will be ones where you have to kill someone. You simply can not do that. I don't care how Über you are at pvp\pvm you can do it. The reason is that you'll have 3-7 mobs chasing you at all times and you won't live more than a few seconds once you stop moving. You need to click the item\person and split! Otherwise your toast.

When you are using Clicksaver, plan on spending 5-15 minutes in front of the mission term waiting to get a mission with a ql200 NCU as a reward. Their pretty rare... (hint: thats why they are worth so much money.. Use the time to chat with your guild... or do your taxes, feed the cat... whatever, you don't have to watch it the whole time.

Yalm's are the only way to fly... ehehee... sorry, bad pun, I know. But its true. Most of the missions you'll pull will be in some god forsaken part of Rubi-ka, you know, the place that takes 2 hours to run to, and every step of the way your dodging high level mobs looking to kill you. Besides, your buffs won't last forever... if you walk there, your screwed... no buff = quick death. Remember, the Fixer hot only lasts about 20 minutes... You need to get that and boogey to your mission.


Finally, this is something that will generate you a lot of money. The reason these instructions are so detailed is that its hard. You will need to practice a bit to get good, and you'll need to plan on dieing your first couple of times. I think the first evening I played; I pulled in 2 NCUs for about 5 hours work. Decent money, but not what i wanted to make. SO, I did it again, and the next time i played it got 3 NCUs in 3 hours... much better...

Now I'm to the point where I can take a single 20 minute Fixer Hot and use it to blitz two missions... 2m for 20 minutes work... You can't beat that with a steel beam...

Happy Blitzing!!

Why did you say this only works only level 112? or 111 for QL 199? I'm not following.
The basic concept of blitzing works at any level. However, since most people get focused on QL200 items, those levels where you can roll missions that are exactly QL200 tend to get the most focus. 112 is such a level, as is 154, and I think 167. Helpbot has that info: /tell helpbot mission 200

Some people point out that QL199 loot is as effective as QL200 but often has slightly lower requirements, so is actually better. Thus, blitzing QL199 missions when you are level 111 is also a good thing.

If you're blitzing to sell to a shop, any level will do. But if you want the better money that comes from selling to players, then find the items they want at the QLs they want.
