Newborn Noob Suggestions
From the many many comments and questions i have received and had myself these quick and easy changes will make people's first moments in the game enjoyable enough that alot more will stay and not give up in frustration.

These suggestions refer to the Holo Room only,

1) Darkness: either, a) make it always light; or b) give players enough money to buy a Floating Torch c) equip them with a torch

2) Camera: make the default camera postion "Fixed 3rd person", and pull it back a few yards.

3) Equipment: Have people's starting equipment "pre-equiped"

4) IP points: either a) pre'assign the starting IP's based on the profession's base abilities or b) have an EASY option to overide the settings.

These suggestions seem like common sense to me. You have no idea of how many people ***** about AO having a lousy interface and quit the game. Little do they know that its fine and just needs to be properly set up. This is too much to ask NEW players to do in their first few moments.

Cheers :0)
