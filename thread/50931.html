The Important Fixes....
this is my list of the most needed fixes...

1. remove the function that causes you to directly face the mob hitting you. this takes away from movement tactics in combat, and suspends player control of their character.

2. set a 8-10 level range limit for pvp, it's not perfect, but in my experience, it generally works. increase the role of the nano resist skill in pvp, remove the damage nerf, give everyone a "suppresion gas mask" as default newbie gear, wearing it will enable pvp everywhere, but only if you want it. (and yes, you will have to use a gear slot to wear it.)

3. make good on the promise for outdoor content, static missions, story events, high end zones and static "epic" missions. random missions should become the second or third choice for gameplay.

4. revamp the whole vehicle system...the biggest problem i have is with the actual visual scale.

changing this would be the best thing funcom could do, and also the most complicated. vehicles could be coded to only be usable in certain areas, that doesnt sound too difficult. allow players to stand on the roof, shouldnt be any harder than showing players mounted on horses. have "parking lots" outside of cities, and docking bays inside. limit the capacity and time limit for the space, and of course charge for it (price based on level). make an animation of some sort of "miniaturization ray", so the vehicle can then be stored in inventory. (this is a big one for me)

5. make original apartment keys duplicable, and the duplicate keys "unique" and un-tradable. armor and weapons can be stored in the apartment, but only in a "trunk" that has a set limit of spaces. otherwise, increase or remove the item limit. allow people to "move" if they want to carry all their stuff, load by load.




to me these are the main ones, and #3 covers alot of ground. im not one to make veiled threats, but the release of SWG gets closer every day....
