First let me say that my pvp experience is limited (but rather "twinked" nevertheless), but playing a Meta here's some observations:

Due to low hp (nanobreed), it will take at most 4 hits - specials, high crits, nukes have no trouble hitting for the 40% limit - to bring me down. Every time I find myself ganked, there seem to be a 50/50 chance that the heal pet gets off 1 heal (which in Metas cases heals for a lot less than any doc, usually lower than 40% of the health) before I die.

Healer pets heal every 7 seconds - none of my battles have lasted longer than that.

It might be just my perception of things, but compared to pvp Metas feels quite weak, so correct me if I'm wrong. In all battles (mostly Soldiers, Enforcers and NT's), they always go directly for the Meta, ignoring the pets alltogether, which brings me down quite fast.

And lastly, Metas "peak" around 130, at wich point all the other classes will grow stronger compared, while Metas have extremely limited options to raise their offense power (strengthen their pets/attack rating/hit points/offensive programs etc).
