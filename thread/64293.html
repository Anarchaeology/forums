Nano Clusters, choose which of the 3 locations the bright/faded/shining install into
I think it would be good if we could choose the implant into which we are allowed to install our nano-clusters.

Just to the point where we can choose which implant of the 3 available that the bright/faded/shining can go into...

IE.

Instead of

Bright STR Cluster - Left Arm
Shining STR Cluster - Right Arm
Faded STR Cluster - Chest

Could we have :

Bright STR Cluster - Left Arm / Right Arm or Chest
Shining STR Cluster - Left Arm / Right Arm or Chest
Faded STR Cluster - Left Arm / Right Arm or Chest


Ive found it a bit of a limitation to the effect of what, armour and weapons i can use simply because, ie. my Shining 1H Edge and STR Nanocluster both want to go into my Right Arm...

Since i dont want to use less then the best armour/weapon i can use.... i have to either choose another weapon or another suit of armour..


I like. that Nanoclusters are location specific to some amount of sense... ie. Intelligence = head implant..

but i think the varying qualities of cluster should be swapable into either 3 of their available locations.


..
just a thought..

anyone agree?
