2 things i would love to see...
So there I was reading through one of the new game magazines, and what do I come accross, but a add for a new MMORPG. well I read it, but it doesn't really appeal to me cause it's fantasy, and I am sick to death of fantasy...sci fi is why I play AO, and is why I stay....so far...

Anyway, in the add for this game (and believe me I know all about these games not living up to their adds ) there were 2 things there that I thought to myself "if these were in AO it would be fantastic (not saying it will fix the problems with AO, but they'd be nice additions)

1. "tools such as grief log, kill on-site lists, bounty hunters, no anonymity and player locator greatly enhance player accoubtability" - I would love to see some way to make players more accountable for thier actions, and how they behave in the game.

2. "Complete personal housing system allows players to build thier own cottages, houses, villas and citadels" - Maybe this is not a big thing for a lot of people, but think of it...build your own guild house.....run a tavern...you built......


well.....I can dream anyway......
