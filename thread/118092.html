Mission quality
It seems like a simple thing, but why can't the mission quality level be input my a number rather than the sliding scale. The problem I have is I prefer to do the vast majority of my mission solo. Currently I am level 101. When I select a mission that is approximately in the middle of the slider I get a mission of level 101 quality. While this mission is challenging and occasionally I like a challenge I don't want that all the time. However, if I go just one pixel to the left of center my mission quality level is 90. This results with many greys in the mission and is not any fun at all. If I could input a quality level I could get a mission which is more in keeping with what I am looking for. This change would have no effect adverse effect on any players as those who want a real challenge (and thus the better reward that goes with it) can still dial up the quality level they want. Those who want a slightly easier way to go (and thus the smaller reward that goes with it) can do the same.

Thanks for hearing me out.
