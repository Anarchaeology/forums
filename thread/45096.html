chests (and their 'phat lewt')
How about just deleting chests from team missions? Make all those chests w nice über items solo mission only.

Advantages :
* no more ninja's with chests (as there are none)
* no more conflicts between pple that want xp as fast as possible and pple that want phat lewt from chests.

I agree the loot whores take the fun out of the game more then anything else. However, if you get a good group free from them and everyone shares the loot equally everything works well.
