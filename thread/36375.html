The Smugglers, both upstairs and downstairs drop both MK I and MK II. MK I is really for levels 91-146 (and even that is debatable, as you can get on QL200 armor around 133ish and wear that until 146, or just not wear MK I at all).

MK II armor is by far better than any QL200 armor sets in the game. If you compare a full set of MK II with a full set of Ql200 Flowers, you're only getting about 500 ACs more with Flowers. The trade off is that you're getting 980 HP and 49 NCU from a full set of MK II.

Consider that the average MOB will hit you 2-10 times before they die. With MK II you're getting hit for 50 more points of damage than you would wearing Flowers. So that is only 100-500 points of extra damage during a fight. Your new 980 points of health can easily cover the extra damage you may take.

I would recommend people start camping for their set of MK II around level 110. That gives you 36 levels to complete your set, and at 110, with a decent group, killing the MOBs won't be too hard. You can go in there around level 80-90 with a really good group or even higher level players in your team.

Just my thoughts on the subject.
