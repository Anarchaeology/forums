These tower types are:

Guard Turret Towers - These towers fire shots at opponents. You can see their range on the map.

Guardian Conductors - These towers give beneficial skill bonuses to your friends, or negative skill bonuses to your enemies. They have a quite large effect range, and you can see this on the map.

Special Towers - These towers do special things to your opponents. There are basically three examples in the game right now: Dropping enemies out of flying vehicles (Yalmahas), kicking enemies out of sneak mode, and disabling the enemies from using normal recharge equipment.
