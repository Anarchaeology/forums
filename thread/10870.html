The problem, as anyone who has ever played any other MMORPG in addition to AO has to admit, is that there are as many definitions of what roleplaying is as there are roleplayers. Personally I get hacked off when someone rping a doctor or MP makes horrific spelling errors...hey, aren't you supposed to be SMART? If you're a dummy, play a class that a dummy would be but then that's really rl intruding on RP, and if I were a good roleplayer, I would translate the spelling errors, etc, into what the CHARACTER would have said, rather than what the player says...

More to the point, you would find that on one hand, very few players would confess to never actually roleplaying. "I am not really a 350lb enforcer, therefore simply by being here as one, I am roleplaying, d00d" is sort of one point of view. On the other hand, even though I play my character more for RP than for loot or XP, there are times that I really AM just running a mission to get the cool tunic or pistol or whatever.

Honestly on RK2 there seem to be more rpers and fewer l33t d00d idiots, only because those types need a bigger crowd to show off to, I think. Usually characters speak to mine in character.
