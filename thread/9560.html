I suppose Atrox wouldn't be a bad choice.
I am under the opinion that Solitus is the optimal choice for for a Crat.


I like to nuke fast. Sense is the main attribute for Nano init. Sense is dark blue for Atrox.

You'll want to keep your nano skills maxed out. Int is the main attribute for nano skills. Int is dark blue for atrox.

Atrox have a small nanopool.
At level 65, I have 1300+ nano. I nuke so fast that I go through very quickly. When I sit and recharge nano, I lose 2-3 nukes.


It really depends on your playstyle.

Atrox have a lot of HP and can take a beating.
On the other hand, crats have a lot of skills to avoid getting hit and to avoid getting aggro.




I heard that Craaats are pretty nerfed in High lvls

Well, the mez and charms become pretty useless in grouping situations at high level. It sorta makes sense that a lvl 100 player shouldn't be able to charm a level 150+ mob.

I think it comes down to people expecting that they should be able to kill mobs 50 levels higher than them in a group. It's a strange situation. People grow so accustomed to min-maxing for XP that they think they should be entitled to it.
There's a big difference between a group of level 40-45 taking on a level 60 mutant and a group of level 90s taking on a level 150 Cyborg.


Another issue is title caps.
Charms are a main weapon in a crats arsenal.
When your level 4 title caps is hit for your main skills (level 82-83), you will end up using the same nanos for 15-20 levels. A charm you use at level 80 will be of no use at level 99.

Solitus All The Way
Solitus is probably the best rell rounded (average) race for this class, while the rest have cons and pro's- the nanomage breed has a disadvantage in Body Development, Atrox have an advantage in Body Development and the related stats (Strength and Stamina) which means more Hitpoints. But the Atrox lacks profeciency in nano skill and nano pool. This breed has advantages in Agility mainly but also Psychic and Sense and is best for Agents or Martial Artists. BUT the Solitus is the standard breed for human characters in Anarchy Online. This breed is not specialized in anything, but is an all-around talent in the use of Nanotechnology aswell as body development. This means that the solitus has an easy time raising nano pool or most of the Nanoskills, and is also not too disadvantaged in the raising of Body Development. This will give you a balanced character with Healthpoints similar to your Nanopoints.
