The Dojo Presents. . .Dojo Dance Party/Competition!
The Dojo Presents. . .

Dojo Dance Party! RK2 - Featuring a special couples dance competition!

Who? - All Omni citizens, and neutrals are welcome as well!

When? - Sunday, August 11'th - 7pm GMT/3pm EDT

Where? - Reet Retreat in the glass floor room

Why? - In celebration of The Dojo passing the 300 member mark

What?

In the history of our org, we have thrown a dance party for achieving the previous two century marks in org population. The prior two events were internal only, but we wish things to be different this time!

Everyone Omni or Neutral on RK2 is invited to stop by and dance their cares away as we celebrate our latest milestone.

There will be the general sort of drinking, dancing and socializing that you would expect at any party. The only restrictions we will put on attendance is that you must come dressed for the occasion, and please, no weapons on the dance floor!

The special "event" of the party will be a couples dancing contest! Grab your favorite dancing partner (sex and breed do not matter!) and compete for the title of Best Dancing Couple on RK2. You will be judged on both appearance (matching outfits are a plus!) and dancing skill, which of course will require a bit of syncronization.

Those who wish to enter the competition can send me (Chronita) a /tell stating your desire to do so and the names of the couple. Those who prepare ahead of time will most likely have an edge, but we will accept participants shortly before the competition begins as well. There will be prizes for 1'st, 2'nd and 3'rd place, with awards currently set at 3 million, 2 million, and 1 million credits per couple (this may very well increase as the event date nears!).

We hope everyone that can will take time out of their busy missioning schedules and join us for what should be a great time!
