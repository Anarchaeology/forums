Titlecap = leveltreadmill = boredom?? Say NO to titlecap!
I was want to share my view on the titlecap system and how it affects the higher level gameplay of AO.

I really dont see the point of using the levels as a definition of titlelevel and hence force every one to be a certain level before they can use equipement such as tokenboards and bracers.
A much better solution would be to use the character skills to define your title level.

Say you a play a NT then your titleskill should be focused around your main Nano abilites, when all skills are above 100 you are title level 2, all above 250 you are 3 and so on. If you have the IP can you can raise the skill regardless og what you level is.

Whats the fun in just having to powerlevel because you no longer can raise you skills? With good implants you have to level from 122 to 150 before you can raise you skills again!! Thats 28 levels with NOTHING to look forward to! I can't se any benefits to titlecap what so ever.

Can you?

If you can please explain it to me. If you dont see the point either, bump this thread and lets make 14.4 the titlecap remove patch.
