First of all, many thanks to everyone who showed up. The turn up was absolutely amazing. I am glad to say the mission was 100% successful. Battle Groups Virgo, Gemini and Taurus all accomplished their primary missions pretty much simultaneously and with minimum casualties. The cyborgs never even new what hit them.

Once the areas were secure, technicians from RUR and Uncle Bazzit's Foundry released designer nanobot programmed to seek out and essentially eat up the stolen token board prototypes. I am confident that the danger of token board enhanced cyborgs has been averted, at least for now.

Having defeated the cyborgs, Cog's lead Battle Group Taurus to strike on a nearby Dust Brigade camp; soon to be reinforced by Swiftmind's Battle Group Gemini. Seeing this multi-faction force smashing into the Dust Brigade, who for so long have terrorised all civilised peoples on Rubi-Ka, was awe inspiring. But our moment of our greatest triumph was also marked by our greatest tragedy. Just as the dust brigade general died, members of the Nanomange Liberation Front (NLF) turned on members of Omni Mining. Blackswords was killed though Swiftmind managed to escaped unharmed.

The destruction of stolen token boards pretty much marks the end of RUR's involvement in the Neutral token board project. We have simply run out of time and money to develop this. All hope now lies with Emmet Bazzit and the Profiteer's Helper. I sincerely hope the Neutral community will not have to wait much longer for this.

Thank you all once again. You were simply magnificent.

Dabblez

Director of RUR

Recclaimed...
As awareness returned to John "Blackswords" Rm his first words were

"Ouch...." he muttered aloud "Bombs under tank armor really Sting...."

Taking In his Surroundings John looks up at the swirling vortex that leads back to Rubi-Ka...

"Great....back in Shadowlands....the bent broken twisted ruin that it is.."

John wandered over to the terminal to reclaim his Items...while he did so he checked the time on his ncu..

"Wha?!? I've been reclaiming over 12 hours!!!! What the hell?!?"

"Computer show Reclaim Info of past 24 hours Please."

The computer shows only 2 reclaims one 12 hours ago In Hope just after the Bombing and one less then five minutes ago.

"What the?? where was I for the last 12 hours?"

As he pondered this a Huge Demonic looking form of a unredeemed approached him, and spoke.

"We shall be watching you....Closely..." said the hollow and deep voice.

with that the Unredeemed wandered away..

"Okay I'm now confused, Puzzled and now a bit scared...."

John Ponders this and says aloud.

"Confused I can handle, Puzzled I enjoy, but this scared bit really pisses me off."

"So now are they watching me because I did something Bad or that I did something good. Either or means Trouble to me."

The reclaim terminal Beeps and disgorges his equipment. John collects it all and settles it about his person and then sets out to find if there are any answers to this latest Problem.
