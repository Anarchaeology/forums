OMG FC can you make a better set up for storylines?
At this point your story line events go along these lines. You have a patch then in a few days (that day or up to 2 days later) you have a story line event that you were able to have bc you added a new mob model. For a week or so you have events with what ever models you added. Then in less then a week the storyline of that model(s) died down. First was the pumkin heads, they came on the day of the patch and lastest for a short while then nothing happened. You patch again then the cyborgs, they attack some cities and they are fought off. In about a week no real cyborg talk/news happens. Then the colons.... then the dustman. And now these damn small mutie thing that just showed up (here is a tip if you name something small ppl will know there is something bigger then it (logical you would leave it alone if it didnt attack bc you dont know what could happen but ppl are stupid and full of their own powers so the mess with what ever is out of the norm)). I'm sure in some grand story before summer they will all connect (at least I hope so or you need some better writes, more so then I think now).

Now that I've told you what you are doing I'll give you ways to improve what you are doing.

Have more storylines for one. At this point all that happens is a major storyline, everything else seems to be player driven. What I think would be an improvement to this is to have a few storylines. Have a storyline start, (I'll use the old events for explaining), you take the cyborgs and have them attack cities on one of the sides of conflict. Have this last more then a week and dont make it come out of no where. Have it start with a cyborg doing some recon on an outpost then have a small number of cyborgs attack the outpost (enough to take it over not to swarm it) then have them controll the outpost and use it for their advantage. Then have them attack more and more outpost and more rapidly. (this outpost would be in the same general area. (not have them attack outpost in rome area and lush. Make them attack form a general direction. Then have them start attacking citys. Have this happen for a few weeks. (BTW when they attack dont have them appear out of no where have them travel the land scape, this way a exploring player will see this army of cyborgs and inform others through channels.)

If this goes on for a few weeks the other side is going to feel pretty bored, its not fun reading about others doing events so have a different storyline event happen to the other side.

When the cyborgs are finally fought off you could have a week or so (a random time not, cyborgs stop attackin on friday, next friday have events start again (to perdictable)) then after a while you can have the cyborgs attack again, they wouldnt attack as powerful as they did before, take over a outpost or so then get destoryed and then that would be the end of them, move on to other storyline ideas.

When ever you have a patch that adds storyline events wait a while before having them come into the game, roll a dice or pick a number out of a hat to decide when to have them appear. I really dont care but not rite way. It would be nice to have them come a day before the next patch or two days before, less predictable, even if it is 2 weeks since the last patch, like I said less predictable.

This of course will take more man hours to do but I think it will make the story lines event more of what ppl thought of when they read that AO will have an interactive storyline spanning 4 years not some crappy, things attack kill them till they are dead... omg somethin else is attacking kill it.. its dead, etc

Also have the storyline have more effect on the world. Rite now you are added new items. Heres a bright idea dont just add items into the game bc we want them.

Fat Atrox: "Hey look whats this new item in this shop, oh it must be magic that it came from no where."

Make all items you add to the game be bc the storyline adds them. Those shields you added that use parry could have been added with the storyline. When these cyborgs factory was invaided new tech was found, it was these new shields that helped the cyborgs resist damage, omni scientist have found ways of taking the tech to be used by races on omni. Then make it so only omni can use them and clans be like give me give me give me, (like with insurence terms).

When i read about the story line be active in AO thats what I thought it would be like, was I just flat wrong or did you change your minds at FC.

FC guy#1: "So we have these new items in this patch, how will we add them with a/the current storyline?"

FC guy#2: "Oh hell to it lets just throw it in so we don't have to take the time to think of a way to add them in with a story line."

I know I've said alot but I think these are really the things you need to make the story line better. Sure some story line events will be better then others but your can improve the over all qul and importance of the story line in AO ALOT with these things.

P.S. If you add this stuff I'm sure alot more ppl will luv you and your game will be more appealing to RPers (more appealing to RPers = more players, more players = more $$; like the classic saying, it takes money to make money) btw if you want some ideas with make story line things I'm creative
