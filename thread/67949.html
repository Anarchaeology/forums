This is from the RPG Vault interview:

"Jonric: Please explain what land control actually is and how it will work. How expensive will controllers be and will there be continuing costs for maintenance or any other reason?

Gaute Godager: The controlling of land will be done in the following way. You join an organization. You find an uncontrolled area - or you kick out the people there and make it uncontrolled. Then you buy or construct a controller item. This is used to build a Controller that in effect turns the area over to you. This controller will be relatively inexpensive on the lowest Quality Levels. (They exist from QL 10 and up). There they cost around 8,000 credits per version. The higher QL versions are another story. They cost a lot of money, but then we know that higher-level players can have several hundreds of million available. This is meant to be costly!

When you have placed your controller, you can add towers. You can also find more details about the controllers here: http://www.anarchy-online.com/conten...ticles/02.html

There will be no upkeep costs on towers or controllers. It is important to note that they can be destroyed by others, though. So it is not a "no-brainer" investment."

That, at least, partially describes the planned cost.
