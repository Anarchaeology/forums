Keaorata
Phear Leet
Fossegrims can be found in Belial Forest aswell. I found a huuuge spawn of them there once. At least 15-20 over a small area.


Their distant cousins Nokkens are much easier to find tho if ya wanna go find one in the wild and are Yalm-less.

From Athen head SE through Aegean til ya reach the Stret River. Continuing SE- Eish you'll find a kinda rope-bridge heading over the Stret. From there keep heading SE til ya zone into Upper Stret East.

After heading past the Rhinoman Village here you'll cross over the Stret again (still going E-SEish) via a sandy beach/walkway thingy.

Now this is the part where ya gotta concentrate if yer still fairly inexperienced. Pretty much everything here will kill you in seconds if your spotted and/or not very fast on yer feet.

Keep heading SE over a clearing towards some hills. If your heading the right way there will be some marshy-looking woods to your left. You'll enter an area called "Barchlows Jump" as you cross the hills. And to the South of you will be a small swampy area heading into the Stret River.
Now make sure ya carry on dodging the big nasty stuff around you and try to circle the swampland. Ya dont wanna go into it just yet unless ya see the Nokken from on yer hill (The trees are very tight together and the Nokken might see you before you see it if yer not careful in there - and then ya wouldnt get a good look before yer sent to Reclaim)
Have a bit of a wander and ya should be able to see the two Nokkens that live in the area. The first time ya meet em is an awesome experience.. Well it was for me anyway

Alternatively send me a /tell ingame and ill take ya to see them
