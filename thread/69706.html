RK1 Roleplaying Convention
It seems everyone has a different view of how to roleplay and what is "Good" and "Bad" roleplaying.

I think its time that the Roleplayers of RK1 came together and talked about what bothers them about each style.

Nothing can change without help.

Right now this is a basic Idea that I will be piecing together and hope to pull off on Sunday, December 1st at Reets Retreat Fireplace room.

Moderated Discussions about RP "Do's" and "Don'ts". Kidnapping a member of Omni-Pol then sitting in Rompa come to mind on the don'ts. But that may not be the same for everyone.''

With the upcomming release of many other MMO's AO will be hard pressed to hold its members unless we use its one advantage to keep them together.

Roleplaying. Rubi-Ka is a new world, full of things that no one else is doing, a world that no one knows and that still holds surprises for us all. With the Shadowlands Expansion due out the beginning of Next year that will further enrich our world.

But for AO to last until Shadowlands is released we need to pick up the pace, so to speak. We need to make people that RP get more involved and get along better, at least OOC.

Communications is a big step and this could be a good start. The discussions would be held OOC. One person would bring up something new and others would respond to it. No attacks. Only comment if you have useful criticism or ideas.

This would give alot of the newer RP groups a chance to meet those that they might not contact for one reason or another.

If anyone would like to help me with this please post her or ingame. I want this to be a turning point for RP on Rubi-Ka. Where everyone that wants to be involved can be.

While I think a conference is needed, I'm concerned that a meeting would deteriorate into pointless arguements (a recent thread comes to mind) with both sides refussing to listening to or consider the point of the other. In my opinion, the best way to manage this is to have a neutral party coordinate the meeting. I don't know if this has ever been done, but is it possible to have an ARK preside over the meeting?

Regardless of how the meeting is organized, I also think it's very important that we all put aside our characters, alliances and biases, and meet as players. If we come to this meeting as irreverant criminals who hold the r/p'd policing authorities in complete contempt, and vice-versa, then nothing will be achieved.

Before the meeting is held, I also think that organization leaders should submit an outline of topics they would like to bring up. Key points should then be posted on the boards to give us all a chance to think about these issues before we begin.

I would like to see this conference happen. I have no desire to leave AO, and would like to see the r/ping in this game become less volitile. The creativity in story creation has not come from Funcom, but through the minds of the players who spend there recreational time, exploring a rich world, with a completely open future!
