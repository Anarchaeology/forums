Some Ideas(Kinda long)
Content. More places to go. More stuff for the high levels to do.

More zones

More Non PVP zones. And more PVP zones for the pvpers.
DONOT change any of the exsisting zones from monser only to PVP.

And let neutral people have the ability to set a flag to non pvp(Can't PVP or be PVPed)Because neutral people should be just that(neutral).

I have seen many posts from people that are upset that their favorite zone was changed to a PVP zone. Or that the new dungeon is PVP. Now some of you will say, but this is a PVP game. Well yes and no, if it was a PVP game you would be able to do it everywhere but in the newbie zones. And me and quite a few others would not be playing. With my idea everyone that wants to PVP can and people let myself who don't want to will still be able to see all of the game. As it is now I always take missions in non PVP areas. I know I won't be able too forever.

Spawns. Make it so when a boss spawns he spawns in a different place everytime. And when he spaws so does a pile of treasure(Random loot depending on level of mob)and 1 to 5 henchmen. Make it so the boss all the henchmen and the loot all spawns at the same time to reduce solo people camping a uber mob. Make spawns for everthing much quicker. Trust me this will reduce camping and make it a lot more scary.

Add wandering mobs/uber mobs. Mobs that are not room based and can walk anywhere in that zone.

Lower the hitpoints of the MOBs in the dungeons. I mean really by the time a small group can kill this stuff they are gray.

Treasure. First off if a MOB is gray it should not drop loot. This will stop high level Chars from farming items.
Make treasure on Boss chars always random loot, and rare items just that.
So if you kill a boss he should drop a item within his level range. You can use a range of random uber items. Not the same item all the time, that is just boring. I remember in EQ when a "Named" spawned we all knew it would drop 1 of 2 items before we even killed it. Boy that was gay.

Missons. Make them bigger. Multifloored. Make them huge.
in fact when you take the mission have a slider to adjust the size of the mission. Some people don't have much time, put the slider all the way down. Some people have a full group and hours to burn, turn it all the way up. Make rooms traped.. Not just the doors. Make a floor that is really a invisible pit that drops down into a room full of MOBS. Again make some Mobs wander the whole mission. So you don't feel quite as safe as you used too.
Try to add some atmosphere to them. Look at games like AVP, system shock2. They scare the f^%& out of you. Make the player set on the edge of their chair. More room types. looks like right now there are only like 12 to 15 different room types per dungeon type. Add like 50 to 100 different room types to make it not the same old all the time. I can help you with this if you wanna hire me hehe. I would love to work for you guys.
Add more than just chests. Barrels and safes to crack and loot. Desk drawers that can be looted.

AI and pet pathing

The computer AI really needs to be improved. Most of the time if I run away I can lose a MOB in a mission hehe. The AI and pathing in general needs a overhaul.

Pet pathing is terrible, make a command to summon your pet to where you are. It can be very frustrating trying to get my robot through a mission heh. Or just make it so the pets can pass through the objets in the room. Not the walls just the objects. In a mission with little or no objects in the rooms my pet rarely gets stuck.

Add a reload key and make it remappable.

Don't let people go back and forth through RK1 and RK2.
If you do it let them make it a one time thing and they transfer the char off of one server and onto the other.

For the most part you guys are kicking ass. I havent played in a while(Left for DAOC) Just been back 2 weeks, looks like you guys are getting your **** together. Now if you can keep up with what the players want you should be good.

I probably have more to say but I need to leave work soon

Cheers!
