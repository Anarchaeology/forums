Change the way Aggr/def works for casting!
As it is right now you take a casting AND attacking penalty for lowering the Aggr/Def slider.

This makes no sense! Casters should be focusing on avoiding physical damage and concentrating on nanos. But you are forcing them to play full Aggr in order to avoid a speed penalty. This means that they will automatically start counter attacks which break roots, take more hits, take more critical hits, and spend time attacking with weapons which logically would make you "think" they weren't spending as much time on concentrating on those nano programs!!

This isn't a big problem for classes that get a green nano initiative but for classes like the MA who have to rely so heavily on their very slow casting heals it makes their green evade skills totally useless.
MA pay a big enough penalty just trying to keep up with the blue nano skills required to cast the heals!! But your forcing them to also give up ALL of their evades, as well as one of the supposed advantages of being a MA... High attack speed while still playing high defense.

It makes more logical sense that the opposite of melee should end up on the opposite end of the bar if you really think about it anyway.
