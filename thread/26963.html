Player shops
Theres this guy i found in newland desert the other day, sells a load of crap right in the middle of the desert by some little house. And theres another guy i found near reet retreat who sells a lot of stuff too. After seeing these guys, I thought it'd be great if players could do the same. You could buy a device in a shop, price tagger or something, pick it up, then right click on items you want to sell and a dialogue box would come up saying "What price do you want to sell this item for?", with a box for entering the price. These items would then become locked (so you couldnt use them or sell them, to prevent exploits or something), until you used a price tag remover on them or they were sold to someone. People would buy things off you by right clicking on you and then seeing all of the items you have tagged and their prices, just like a trade window with a vendor NPC. As for the traditional trade window with players that you get with right clicking on someone, maybe there could be an action in the action window to switch to 'vendor mode', and when this is inactivated you can trade normally. People could find regular places to set up a shop, like some of the places in Tir with tents, at popular hunting grounds, or at static dungeons maybe. Good idea? Bad idea?

Trading
