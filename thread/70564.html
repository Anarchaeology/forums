Depending on what you consider as the 'basic' level of RP, I suppose... Maybe there's been a drop these few last weeks in the 'official' RP - I was away from any internet connexion at home for a little more than 2 weeks, and haven't had time to play every day since I'm back (1 week ago), so I may be highly mistaken - yet something like 3-4 weeks ago there were kind a certain number of 'greenies' stil wandering. New OP recruit hanging in Rompa, OP officers coming to arrest a stim-dealer... maybe that's what you were referring to.

Now as said, this last week's been pretty calm in this regard, but on the player-run RP side, there still *is* movement. People just have to gather their wits to organize it. I jump online this very morning, poof, 10 minutes later I'm already in the process of organizing something with a few other people. Same thing happened 2 days ago. The CoA/anti-CoA battle(s). And some other things i'm not aware of, I'm sure (I hope we Omnis aren't the only ones RPing! LOL) I can't thank enough all the people who take part in this, may it be in helping organize things (Trellame, Lillemjau, Valaik - I *love* you folks, you know why) or creating the opportunities.

Sometimes, the scene is kinda dead, I admit (3 pm GMT - ROFL!), some other times there are things going on, maybe it's all a matter of perspective. But if people get involved in this (and not simply waiting for RP to fall in their mouths, all ready and all), lots of good things can still get out of it.

Or maybe I'm phasing between the manic (hapy RP lotsa fun fun fun) and depressive (bored, it's dead, booored) states without knowing it... :/
