Battlesuits and Armor analysis (Bloody 'uge)
Base protection on Battlesuits is 5000 AC.
Weak is 1250 AC against that category.
All Battlesuits subtract 100 from all evades and run speed.

Name: Assault-Issue Omni-Pol Elite Battle Suit
Omni-only
Weak versus Chem, 50% nano cost increase.

Name: Augmented Urban Battle Suit
Weak versus Cold, Fire, Rad, Poison, +60% Nano.

Name: Enhanced Battle Suit
Weak versus all but the 3 main, +75% nano.

Name: Flawless Plasteel Battle Suit
Weak versus Cold, Chem, Rad and Poison, +75% nano

Name: High-Quality Desert Battle Suit
Weak versus Rad and Poison, 88% protection from Fire and cold,
63% protection from Chem. 80% nano.

Name: Assault-Issue Omni-Pol Battle Suit
Omni-Only
Weak versus Fire and Cold, 88% protection from Chem and Rad,
62% protection from Poison. 50% Nano.

Looking over the battlesuits, there is only really one suit that is decent: The assault-issue Omni-Pol suit. 5K main ACs + 4375 Chem and Rad means that it should make decent adventuring armor.

Fire and Cold damage are very rare in the wild, though you will get spanked by properly equipped PvP combatants. (There doesn't seem to be a battlesuit that is even vaguely decent on the Clan side).

The Nano-cost mod of 50 still hurts and the evade penalties are non-trivial. However, it still might be worth it, if the bonus ACs are sufficient to make up for the downsides. Are they?

The following armor comparison does two things: Is a handy high-end armor guide and shows that Battlesuits *suck*.

Right, so now that I've proven conclusively that Battlesuits are
totally inferior, how to bring them up to par with other armor?

We could raise the ACs, but frankly, the only ones that would matter
are the weak ACs (since once you force the mobs to minimum, there
is no point). I doubt Funcom wants to create perfect armor, so
that's out.

The suits are hermetically sealed, right? Maybe a nano-resist buff.

Or, since they are basically powered armor, have them buff
weapon skills. Heavy weapons would be a perfect fit!

There's gotta be some way to make these actually viable, as the
concept is good, but the execution is "teh suck".
