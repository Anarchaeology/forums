Airhawk Alliance has recruitment drive
Airhawk Alliance (AH) is looking for a few good men and women

Airhawk's mission is to strike fear deep into the heart of all Omni scum..we are heart breakers and widow makers.

Airhawk is divided into many different assault and supporting assault teams.

Currently has 200+ members and an aggressive recruiting campaign. We currently have MEMBERS ranging from LEVEL 1 - 172

Airhawk is for all types of AO players we are a STRICKLY optional guild. Meaning if you would like more responsibility and a more active role in the guild you are encouraged to pursue such endeavors. If you would like to simply join to get the benefits of promotion bonuses, free items, and the other guild benefits you are more than welcome.

There is NO buying/selling allowed to fellow members, everything is handed around we believe of taking care of our own.

we do practice a strick hierarchy and promotion system should you choose to be the involved player.

We welcome all levels and encourage our members to help the new players...lets face it this game is a "b" word for the first few levels not having a clue...so if you can't put up with the lower levels don't bother.

Look forward to reading your application.. Good luck to all Clan Guilds.
