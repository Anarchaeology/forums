Special Items... Special missions
I keep thinking of ways to come up with uncampable missions and rare treasures. Here's another idea.

Keeping treasure and mission locations rare is trickey but if you can keep everyone in the world from finding the front door you can have a very rare item without the insane camping that goes on.

Give a special mission(s) to any character at sometime in there life. This can be done in a few ways without others being involved.

1. The home office sends you a message thru chat that they have a mission for you to run. The mission icon appears under the mission tab and away you go.

2. The home office contacts you thru chat to meet someone who will give you the information needed.

3. The home office intercepts your resurrection site and diverts you to another destination with someones there to brief you while you recover from rez shock.

These are a few examples of how it could be done. I may add more later.

Don't get me wrong. I don't wanna see the static dungeon disappear. I wanna see more variety in game. I see right now that the static dungeons and camping are outweighing all other possible options for quests and rare loot. I think this needs to change to balance this issue.

I know there is new stuff in the future but I fear that all of it will be camporama.

Feedback?
