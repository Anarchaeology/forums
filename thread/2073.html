Overequiping?? What BS!
Here's an analogy: "If some omnipotent, creator-type dude meant for us to fly, he'd have given us wings, RIGHT??" Welp, Funcom decided to give us skill buffs, so just what the hell were we SUPPOSED to do with them?! I mean, what?? Ooooh!! Lookit the pretty nano fireworks!!! It that all?

If they don't want us to "over" equip, they should not have given them to us in the first place! Now, look at all the [percieved] mess it has brought...

Let's try to move forward with this advantage, and use it for what it was obviously intended. Don't think of it as cheating just because you don't have friends that can buff you. This is a social game, too, is it not?

Don't think of it as cheating just because someone has better equipment than you. As long as (s)he isn't duping, they have legal right to use the equipment as they see fit.

An interesting note I may have misheard is that as you gain skill, weapons become better (damage output-wise) due to your skill. I think this is reflected by getting more criticals against much lower level mobs than yourself.

Why not tweak the code a bit to reflect the absense of skill? If you can NOT meet the req's, sure you can pull the trigger... but can you actually hit something? Probably not! However when your buffs are ACTIVE (ie. when you meet the requirements) you actually have a chance at hitting your target or using the equipment successfully.

This is the most realistic to me, and seems to be less difficult to administrate than instigating a witch-hunt and nerfing the whole nano and zoning routines to drop unusable equipment back into your inventory, or whatever...

What would you have to do? Simply modify the already existing equation to take into account the player's skill compared to the equipment requirements, and allow us to use all equipment regardless. And to use that ql 200 first aid kit, a skill 'roll' would be needed

Here's an example: creating implants requires a minimum skill to even attempt the action. Instead of making this a static requirement, why not just greatly reduce your chance of success when your skill is low? RPers should like this. If you meet the skill, you get a 100% succcess rate. 90% of the Nano Prog skill needed would give you...gee, let me think... a 20% success rate or whatever...

If it would require too many server resources, than at least apply it to combat, where the roll is already being done. Other equipment, like the stims, kinda make sense to have a minimum skill.

As for armor, which is in constant use... That is another problem altogether, and I will not address it, yet. I do not know...

The most upsetting option for ALL players: limit the player to use only equipment that is of equal or lesser quality level to his own experience level. But even this would need work to ensure the quality level system corresponded with the character level system. It would also pretty much obliterate any need to increase IP in much of anything...

Finally, we could simply ignore this and let everyone overequip to a point that everyone balances out anyway... Balance IS what ya'll wanted in the first place, is it not?
