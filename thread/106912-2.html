I was 18 my first time.
I was 18 my first time.

It was one of those golden summer days in Omni Forest. We had spread out a blanket on top of a hill that had an unobstructed view of the horizon. The violet sunset was almost as beautiful as the soft look in her eyes. We had been planing this picnic for weeks. Both of us had time off and wanted nothing more than to get lost in eachother.

I had spent my evenings shopping for the perfect meal. Sliced fresh fruit, cold cuts, warm bread from the oven to the basket, cheese, and...damn...I left the wine in my appartment. She laughed at me...but it was an infectious laugh that soon had me smiling again. I told her that I would be back, we kissed, and I dashed over to RELAX! to pick up a bottle of wine.

Meanwhile, a world away there was a war on. War parties on both sides of the conflict were carrying out guerilla raids on whatever targets preseneted themselves, and to the small team of clanners wandering the forest that day, a beautiful aparently unarmed girl was too nice to pass up.

I was running back with bottle in hand, when I heard the shots. One thing I had always insisted on was that neither of us ever travel unarmed, and her BBI's she had stowed in the basket packed an unexpected punch...but not enough to save her. She had killed 1 of them, and hurt annother, but one was unscathed. By the time I droped into conceal, and lined up a shot on the third, she was coughing on pink frothy blood.

BANG. I dropped the unhurt clanner in a singly lucky Aimed Shot.

CLICK. My weapon wsa empty, and I couldnt wait for it to recharge...I droped it and ran the few steps to the injured clanner, pummeling him with the wine bottle. The bottle and his skull both broke, covering me in red wine and blood.

I knelt beside my girlfriend, and wiped the blood soaked hair from her face...The soft look was gone from her eyes, replaced by the lifeless gaze of dead eyes.

I cried. I cried amid the shambles of a picnic, 3 dead clanners, and the dead girl in my arms.

I cried the same tears for her that I had for my parents. But these tears ran down my wine and clanner blood soiled face, landing on my lips...I was 18...and found that the taste of clanner blood was to my liking.
