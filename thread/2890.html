Tendencies
Hi RoboCop - eh ... ka (bad joke as opener).

Some of my observations (just impressions and tendencies):

I am aware of the same two types(styles) of missions areas:
a) entrance room - big room (several types from 'square-ish' to stretched)
b) entrance room - small room (4 doors)

Occurrence:
Just yesterday, we had, by chance, two missions in the same location (two different people). The first mission area was of type a) and the second mission had type b)! It seems, that the mission type (might) influences the type/style of the mission area. As I am using mostly the same type of missions (collect item) I never had an eye on that so far.
For myself I have found type b) mission areas in a variety of playfields (Clon****, 4 Holes, Stret West Bank(?), Aegan, Wailing Wastes).

Characteristics:
Type a) mission areas 'generally' tend to be larger (I am aware of expceptions) and more packed, but this seems to be mainly influenced by the mission type. The 'bad/money-get item' missions (are they fixed again?) are usually much shorter (higher percentage of type b) areas) than the 'hidden/mystical/stealth/money-get item' missions. Especially the latter setting produces large mission areas (type a), better suited for teams).

-> Helions: I know this type of room. So far, I had a few of them in type b) mission areas, but not as second room, rather deeper in the area. Although there are a lot of 'floors', non of the balconies ever showed a door. This specific room type never had more then one door (for me) but more than one encounter .
