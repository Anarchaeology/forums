Cyclic Retaliatory Venom Spit
QL 159
matter creation >= 741
matter meta >= 741
level >= 175

Now...at level 159, a Solitus Advent can have a base of 609 MC/MM. That leaves 132 to make up somewhere... With QL 150 implants (being conservative here, it's quite possible for a level 159 char to have QL 200's) one can get 158 in one, and 78 in the other (due to overlap.) That leaves 54 to make up in one... -20 for Expertise and we're short 34. (This is assuming we're not using QL 200 implants--it would be AOK with those.) 34 is damn simple to make up...we're talking a level 159 char here that most likely has access to many of the MP buffs that raise more than +34.

Basically, Funcom is saying: YOU MUST SELF-CAST ALL THE NEW ADVENT NANOS. Even then, because of race caps...there isn't much difference between a level 159 MC/MM and a level 175 MC/MM. If you don't have QL 200 implants, you'l probably have to get an outside buff anyway.

If they are dead-set on the idea (which, to be honest, if they're gonna pursue will totally nullify the whole skill system), someone at Funcom seriously needs to rethink their level caps.
