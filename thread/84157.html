People don't want to PvP in RPG's for the most part, period. It's because you have built a character. 90% of the players feel attached to their characters and don't want to expose them to the perceived humiliation of being killed by another person.

DAoC addressed this to a degree by making PvP somewhat anonymous. Sure you may get killed by someone from another realm, but the game tries to make it feel like yer getting killed by just another MOB.

I'm not trying to put down or prop PvP. Just explaining why it's been a steadfast 90% of the RPG community who don't enjoy it. I thoroughly enjoy it as long as it is done in the spirit of roleplaying. Unfortunately, that's rarely the case in most RPG's. Just look at the nonsense on the PvP forums. A lot of people don't want to get pulled into that muck.

Unreal Tournament, Quake, Team Fortress Classic all have a loyal following because you make up a name, enter the fray, die or kill and the game is over to start all over again. Pick another name or another profession if you want to and do it all over again. No attachments to your character if you choose not to.

To turn the topic of the thread around, I gotta say that 100% gas sucks ass big time because it's basically ruined outdoor hunting. You can't find a decent place to hunt due to all the damn 100% gas everywhere. Look at the Undead City in TLR for a prime example.
