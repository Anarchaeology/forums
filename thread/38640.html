Thanks for the bump Missmaul. I thought I would give you all a bit more information on the event.

In ooc terms the Leet Fight is an event that simulates what a Rubi-Kan version of clandestine dog or **** fights might be like. The fights are conducted by charmed leets dragged and pitted against each other in combat in a makeshift ring. So in that respect are quite real,leets do die. We've run this event once before so we know it works.

Part of the fun of the event is the show surrounding the fights, from the WLFF annoucer calling the fights to the leet dawg vendors. We do encourage people to place bets on the fights, however we do not handle the betting ourselves, that migth be percieved as a conflict of interest.

If any leet supporters feel impelled to demonstrate against the fight, thats okay too, just use common sense and show respect (ooc) for the people who wish to enjoy the show.

The roleplaying background to this event works on the assumption that leet fights were banned by Omni-Tek some 12 years ago. The sleazy World Leet Fighting Federation (WLFF) however still runs a network of undergound leet fights. This background is of course entirely unofficial. You are not required subscribe to this background story to enjoy the event, but again we would hope will show common sense and not openly challenge this backgound during the event itself.

So if you have a free hour Sunday after next, or need an excuse to dodge that dull guild meeting, why not come to the WLFF Leet Fight?
