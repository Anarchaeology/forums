>> What I don't like in EQ <<
These are totally personnal views, but... I like to share.

It does not require any comments, I guess I write this mainly for me and a bit also for those that only know AO...

What I didn't like in EQ
What I've found in AO

Having to group for 6 hours with 30 ppl to be able to take a random shot at a very rare item, loosing XP in the process.
The good security I have other my XPs, granted that I can died just before leveling and without any save (happened twice) or that missions XPs can be flushed on the way home by a nasty encounter. These are necessary thrills.

For 90% of the profession being unable to solo and having to find an open spot in a group, if you are lucky enough to be a Tank, an Healer or a Mezzer.
Up to a relatively high level, 90% of the professions can solo. Finding groups is relatively easy and missions are always here.

Too much teenagers uber guilds.
Not as many teenagers (read immature no offense intended here) but no uber guilds too. An uber guild is not just a pack of 150+ lvl guys. In AO it would be an organization that can hold MeetmeDere for a whole week 24/24 or that can kill all guards and control an opposite big city like Athen / Rome. Fun ? Sure. But too much predominance means also less fun for the average population.

Meditating or requiring external assistance to heal no matter what.
All professions can recharge nanos and HPs.

Begging for Spirit of the Wolf (extra run speed to outrun mobs), not being able to teleport (except to the bind point) or having to camp the infamous Journeyman's Boots (built in SoW, but requiring to camp for many hours a spawn where 50% of the server is waiting in line).
Being able to just buy cars or planes. Being able to put IPs in running AND feeling the difference. Being able to grid or use whompahs

At same level all the professions have the same abilities / spells. Only difference being what they wear and wield.
You have the choice in IPs repartition and can focus a character on combat / evasion / nanos / etc.

Being a passive customer
Getting your voice heard, if not listened.

Updates should come ...

Hmmm
I have to agree with you on some of those points but there is also the contrasting look as well...

Sure there is no rare item camping blah blah... however... there isnt much variety currently, you wield a (insert weapon here) levels 1-200. Whereas in EQ it was kinda nifty to go from a scimitar, to a screaming mace, to a Paw of Opalla, to a Nature Walkers Scimitar that had little leaves floating off of it and Proc'd a huge DOT by simply targeting and right clicking... IP kinda restricts you until level what, 120?

AO is much easier to play as a casual gamer... I like that. Good point.

Uberguilds sucked, I didnt want to be in one, but at the same time, there became a point that if I wanted to go and explore new places I needed their protection... Uberguilds dont have time for RP events... catch 22...

Meditation and down time gave you time to chat... I find that when I am in groups in AO there is never any time to chat, as people group for xp and chatting detracts from xp... I end up socializing less in a group than when I am soloing a mission at my own pace.... I find that no down time is actually a curse and a blessing...

My main was a druid so I never worried about SoW... however, what is the benefit of being able to travel in AO? Its not defining, its not something rare, people end up traveling no farther than one whompah or one grid zone away to hunt. How many fixers get offered credits for a grid jump? It makes the fixer class less special imo. Then again... thank god I never have to take another %$^$ing boat!

Yes every character in EQ pretty much played like another, there was no personalizing, however in AO, if you want your character to be viable, you cant really deviate from certain templates until level 120ish. The problem I've found with AO is that tradeskills detract from combat power, and personally I dont think that is right. Swimming, Adventuring, Cars, Planes, Water, and all trade skills should have a seperate IP pool from combat skills... it kinda sucks that if I want to be able to make a weapon in AO, I also wont have enough skill to use it? In EQ learning a trade skill enhanced your playability and effectiveness, without hurting your combat validity.

In EQ I once deleted a bag with several thousand plat worth of items including a 10,000 plat Diamond Wedding Band I had bought for my RP wife. A GM quickly responded, reviewed their logs, and returned all but one of the items, which he did not find a record of, it wasnt of much importance. AO treats their pixels as if it was 1000 shares of microsoft stock or some rare #$%#ing jewels! I honestly got better customer service from Verant even if they were very militant at times. This business is all about customer service, not pixel pushing.

All in all... I wish I could have the best of both worlds. "In the end, there can be only one!" Unfortunately I cannot bear having to sit in a damn cave for another 20 hours of my life killing the same frogs over and over to gain a level, nor will I section off 12 hours of my life to do a "raid" for the 8th time, from beginning to end, and not get a single item. 12 hours of my life is not worth being bossed around by a raid leader for a damn pixel. AO is more of that instant satisfaction gaming that I need. FunCom has 14 days left to hook me. Work it baby!
