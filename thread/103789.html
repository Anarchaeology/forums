E-Corp Back in Business ((IC and OOC))
Omni-Herald Feb 26 29477

Omni-Tek Affiliate E-Corp announced that they have recently opened thier doors after Several months of remaining in the Shadows of Omni-Tek. In a Press release today, Director Jesska Rhees states that contrary to popular belief, the company was not closed, and has been working silently to avoid compromising the nature of thier business.

"E-Corp has always been commited to new sciences" Ms Rhees says in the statement "and data storage, and although we have played a smaller role in the last few months, we have reached a breakthrough in our intellegence and science departments. Business is booming, and now we need to make up for the demand"

The nature of E-Corps business has always remained classified to the general public, and this move surprised many. Not only because the company has gone public in its search of employees, but due to teh controversy surrounding its Director and a pending murder investigation in the death of Jena Honer.

Jena Honer was killed last October by a virus type, designed specifically to scatter a beings lifeforce, that was injected in her system, by a unidentified projectile weapon. Ms Rhees was wanted in questioning regarding this as there appeared to be evidence that she might have been involved.

"Our corporation offers a steady salary, benefits and regular Vacation interests" She said in the statement. "The nature of our work will remain classified and all employees must sign a Non disclosure agreement to begin employment. This is a exciting time for us, and for Omni-Tek, who we continue to do research for"

The statement specified that they were looking for Fixers, Agents, Scientists, Engineers, Bureaucrats, Traders, and Nano Technicians but they were a equal opportunity employer and all professions would be concidered.

If you would like to apply you can contact "Jesska" Rhees, Refugio "Zerics" Mischler, and Sarah "Hostbody" Tarlmak.

We of the press do not discourage citizens to apply, but remind all of the current implications against Ms Rhees, and that the true nature of her business is not public. We do encourage caution when applying for a position with E-Corp.
