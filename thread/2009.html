Changes to grey mobs...?
What would be nice is to make grey mobs less of a problem, after all they take one hell of a beating to kill and you only get 1 exp.

It'd be nicer if when attacked they have a certain chance of running away, say 50% and for every percentage of their health goes down the chance of them runnign away increases.

This does one of two things it stops annoying long fights with worthless Grey's and it introduces a certain morality whether you chase after the Grey and finish it off.
Infact Introducing a certain chance that any mob will run away according to the damage dished out would be interesting so when I manage to take a green down half his hp with a burst flingshot and normal shot, he would panic and try and escape.
