Belle Rouge started hanging around Baboons and left Rompas to Lunar Entertainment and Xero to pick over as, even though Baboons didn't have a lot of customers, there was a man named Dave "Diamondcut" Duster who ran the place and was a very interesting character. He wanted a guild to run the service staff there and said that we were just what he was looking for so, even though we were small, he gave us an opportunity to prove ourselves. He ended up liking us and kept us on. After some time, a young Aktrez wandered in one night, proclaimed she was going to be a star and Satyrnine and I decided she was probably right so we snatched her up into Belle Rouge where she quickly rose in the ranks.

In the mean time, Solar Entertainment (Lunar by then) and Xero were squabbling over Rompas, but that didn't concern Belle Rouge as we were doing our own thing at Baboons. Xero bounced back and forth between Lunar and various other guilds and at one point must have decided that learning if a bunch of waitresses had come up with an evil plot to wear a particular color of high heels with their uniforms was important as we found out that he was placing spies in our guild. We patched things up between Lunar and Belle Rouge and continued doing our own thing but eventually we saw that a merger of the two guild would be a good thing. Meanwhile, Xero had left Lunar for the umpteenth time to either start yet another guild or join some other (he was hopping guilds frequently at that time) while we were in talks with Solarasis and Lunar about a merger. During the talks, Xero popped back into the picture and decided that he should run the merged guild (!!!) and tried to have several meetings outside of the full circle that was handling the project to make that happen. Eventually, Solarasis agreed and Phoenix Entertainment was the result of the two merged guilds. We worked Rompas most of the time and just worked events for Diamondcut at Baboons when he requested us.

After a few months, Ditzie and I weren't happy in Phoenix anymore as we had grown tired of the idea of working a bar and became more interested in other things ((leveling, exploring, other kinds of RP)) and left Phoenix to join the new Devil's Advocate with Thyme. Due to other things going on ((long story short)), Phoenix ended up folding and Aktrez formed Vixen Entertainment and continued working Rompas while Devil's Advocate moved into Baboons after Diamondcut told Thyme she could "run" the club.
