quote:
Originally posted by Vydas
For a reason that I don't believe was made clear, it seems that the rule for this, in FC's eyes, is that the DoT duration = time until you can cast this nf again.
The reason is as follows. If the DoT lasts for longer than the time until you can cast the NF again, the new casting "refreshes" the DoT. Therefore, you got the full benefit of the NF (the healing), but circumvented some of the downside (ie you did not take all of the damage you were intended to take for one casting of the NF). It is apparently not possible to make the DoTs stack, so in order to ensure that you take the full damage, the NF has to be locked until the DoT has completely run it's course.
