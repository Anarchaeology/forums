refresh timer huh?..
i dont see the logic in why buffs need to have refresh rates?...i think its just unnecessary downtime...why not make them refresh instantly...but i mean only things like the ac buffs....etc.....

since well...once u put on a 30 minute buff..and it refreshes after 10 seconds..why would u want to rebuff after 10 seconds?...when its going to last for 30 minutes?...its kinda..well...pointless...and it can also get tiring for someone trying to cast it on others too....call it just super duper imatience..if you call it ...the only effect i can think of for this refresh rate, is the effect on wasted time..even if its just couple of seconds...and i dont see how it would effect game strategy by delaying the time for you to cast it on someone else after you casted it on yourself....
i mean i know for some poeple, couple of seconds of waiting for a long duration buff to refresh so he/she can cast it on someone else doesn't really bother them...but some poeple just want to do these tedious things quick as possible and actually go on with the Fun..you know what i mean?....since i dont really think the "refresh" times of these long duration buffs have any factor in strategy of the game...etc..and if it did...i'm sure it wouldn't effect it in anyway by that much....

and i dont mean this should apply to healing nanos...or nanos that are for battles..including roots/nukes/heals...etc....

i even think even the casting time of the "long duration buffs" is just pointless also..but i dont really care about this part...but i think its not pointless when you just want to add that snazzy effect of you casting it...heh....i guess its just a way to add that cool little content to the game...etc..

i also think this could save time/speed up in preparing for battle...despite the fact of one variable...the nano kits..which has a refresh rate of 12 seconds or something..and once ur nano pool goes dry from buffing everyone else..u gotta wait for the nano kit to refresh...but i dont have a problem with that...since it seems the refresh rates on the nanno kits/treatment kits...can have a factor in strategy...
