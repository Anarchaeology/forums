AO Combat Option! Plz Read.
I feel what would add a TON to AO, is the availability to use combat as a first person shooter, as in, when fighting in first person, be able to AIM the gun yourself, and shoot it with a mouse click, or whatever key you want. The monsters could stay on the same system they are on, it would work nicely with the addition of a fps style combat interface, and would be a TON of fun to be able to play it that way, and also the old system if you want as well, but there would be advantages/disadvantages to each. If you use the old system, you'd have less of a chance to miss, but you would have a chance to auto miss or their dodge skills would be more effective vs this style, but if you controlled the shots yourself, you couldn't auto miss, and if you say hit their head you could get critical hit, lesser hit for hitting arms and legs, the damage done would still vary but their dodge and evade type skills would not be as effective, there would still be a chance but not as much of one. PLEASE post what you think as I'd like to gain alot of support for this idea, it would make alot of things a TON more fun.
