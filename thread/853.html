NTs do have alot of problems with new PvP rules, but the range cap was due. Basically what happened is that some NTs were using there extreme range to obtain a ridiculous advantage in PvP in the wide open 0% zones.

What you would do is fly around the zones looking for hunting parties or hot mission spots. When you found a target, you could fly back out to around 100m and land. You would still hold the target that far out, but you opponent could neither see nor target you back (view range max's around 80m). So basically you could root and nuke without any fear of retaliation.

Before 13.0, people were being warned this was an exploit and that a range cap coming. If you want to blame someone for this, blame the handful of people who were abusing this feature. You just can't give any single class that much ganking power relative to everyone else. It's not unlike Agent's AS before they nerfed that.

That being said, perhaps 40m is a bit stingy. Actually, it doesn't feel like casters really even get 40m... seems more like 30m to me.
