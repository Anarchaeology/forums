You're both kinda right... One of the things changes recently, I think. =)

Burst = Recharge Delay * 20 + [Burst Delay] - Burst Skill / 25

[Burst Delay] is the value in the DB attributes for that weapon divided by 100.

Recharge Delay is the base recharge posted for the weapon. Init and AggDef have no bearing on this value.

-Jayde
