Farewell to a Friend
I dont think she ever said goodbye...to all of you anyway..

You mightve known her..her name was Hylle..and Schu..and Dannii..and littledove..and a few others

I have known her longer then anyone in this place..since before playing at the NW cave in Lush Fields. She helped me through many bad times, kept me from quitting early on. She was my friend, truly a friend, and I know this because she was there for me, although I had nothing to offer her. She was the first I made in this place, and remains one of my closest.

The greatest game of all, Real Life, has stolen her from all of us...but shes made the right choice...I dont want this post to be sad, Ive had my time of mourning...if you counted Hylle as friend..Post here..wish her luck in life, with her son, and keep her in your prayers...

"....Because I've lost another good one..."

Boci...Friend to Hylle..always
