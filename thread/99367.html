quote:
The neutrals.

Lately I have received several e-mails from neutrals who feel that their side is being left out, forgotten. I will start by admitting that I never wanted the neutrals to be a strong faction with the desire to "win" on their own. Be that as it may, it's part of the history now. I always felt that for the conflict to work, it would have to be two "clear" sides, not many shades of grey.

Anyway, the vision of the neutral side has changed with the passage of time and with the influence of strong groups of players. I'm ready to follow some of your wishes and make the neutral side a bigger force.

As a result of this, we could, for instance, give the neutral side their own leadership chat-channel. This might be a small thing, but it would make the social structure within the neutral faction more clear.

The role of the neutrals in the story has been the subject of much debate within Funcom. Some feel that the neutrals are essential to the story, and that they will play a part in deciding which side wins. In many ways the neutrals are the victims of the conflict. If or when they rise and ally themselves with one of the opposing sides, maybe the balance will change, and the outcome of the conflict decided?

There are actually quite a few advantages of being neutral today. As a neutral, you may go to all cities without being afraid of getting killed. You may shop in all trade-skill shops, and thus have cheap and ready access to all implants and other trade-skill items. You may listen in on both the other sides' out of character channels, making it easier to find teams, knowledge, and goods to trade.

Of course, there are many disadvantages, too. As a neutral, you do not have access to most of the clan and Omni-Tek shops, you will be hunted by sided monsters, you may not attack first in political PvP areas, and so on. Life as a neutral isn't a dance on roses.

We're seeing a growing collective consciousness among the neutrals. They are a growing force, becoming more and more a united faction rather than a collection of individuals. You can see neutral organisations like the The Independent Rubikans wanting to take part in story events, and a growing demand for a unique role in the conflict.

For a long time, the neutrals couldn't make their own organisations. Now they can. We are planning to change the Whompa network, and, as a result of that, we'd like to offer the neutrals their own unique network. I can't promise that their Whompa stations will be visually unique (the neutrals have to purchase technology from either the clans or Omni-Tek), nor that their network will be very extensive, but you will see a unique system anyway. The neutrals will also most likely endorse a meeting place for all the Whompa networks to merge, most likely in the ICC headquarters in Andromeda: Feedback from neutrals on this particular issue would be much appreciated.

Actually, feedback on all neutral issues is encouraged, and hopefully some of you want to contribute with ideas and suggestions about how the neutrals can play a greater role in the conflict and participate in shaping the future of Rubi-Ka. If neutral players want to role-play as true neutrals, there must be an emphasis on negotiation and diplomacy, and a willingness to service both the clans and Omni-Tek. If the neutrals want to become a force to be reckoned with in the conflict, a third party with the intention to "win" - just like the clans and Omni-Tek - it's going to be a lot more difficult.

What do you think?

Gaute Godager
AO Game Director
What do you think? We sure as shinola gave him feedback. Let's see how it plays out.
