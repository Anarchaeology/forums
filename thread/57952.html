Variable damage Cluster Bullets
I was asking some questions on the fixer forum about cluster bullets and skills involved when the idea popped up to have cluster bullets that you can change the damage type on. Example:

Poisoned Cluster Bullets - Cluster Bullets + Plant "thorn" (since plants seem to do poison damge) = Poisoned Cluster bullets.

Radiation C. Bullets - C.Bullets + Lifebleeder organ = Radioactive Custer Bullets.

You get the idea...by combining objects found around rubi-ka and using trade skills (mech. engineering?) you can change the damage type of the bullets. Disease, radiation, melee, projectile, fire, cold...all damage types would be preferred. They're already chemical damage...leave "regular" cluster bullets as they are.

There is always a balance...perhaps for some of the more powerful damage types, like radiation or disease, the resulting damage the fixer takes from recoil could be increased. If a disease area burst does 500 damage to my target, I'd happily take a 50hp penalty. Perhaps an extended amount of recharge time for the more dangerous damage types as well? slightly higher burst reqs...

I could easily see some of us dedicating an entire shortcut bar to a full range of multiple damage type cluster bullets. I believe a similar process is already in place for arrows...I'd love to see it in cluster bullets too.

Just a thought.
