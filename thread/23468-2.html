Router or hub?
Is it a router or a hub? If it's a hub, it offers no protection and yes, you should get yourself a firewall. If it's one of those LinkSys (I think that's what you said) routers that you can configure to send certain types of traffic to certain machines (i.e. port 80 traffic goes to this machine, port 22 goes to that machine), you can get away without a firewall. Just make sure you don't allow any incoming traffic and you'll be fine.
