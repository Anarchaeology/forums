Remove crits from the game.
Critical hits are inherently unbalanced; it's impossible to balance the capability to hit for 3k points of damage in a swipe, and the various attempts to are destroying other aspects of the game.

Lower high-end monster HP's across the board, remove all crit hits, and replace all crit buffs with simple +300 or so (max) damage buffs. Remove the 50% reduction in PvP damage.
