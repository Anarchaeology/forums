Kithrak Asks Clanners For Help Recovering Nanomage Suit
Korev Jurik

ANDROMEDA (IRRK) - In a shocking turn of events, Charles "Kithrak" Houston has appealed to the clans for assistance in recovering the stolen nanomage environmental suit.

At a briefing Wednesday in ICC headquarters, the former leader of Red Tape of Omni-Admin announced the identities of those responsible for the abduction of the suit. Clan members were asked to aid in recovering the data and prototype suit.

"With their efforts to reform the Council of Truth," said Kithrak, "it is time to see if the new Council is truly ready to rejoin Rubi-Ka politics."

The environmental suit, developed by Red Tape and Magi-Tek scientists, was recently announced at a science symposium. It was created with the intent to allow nanomages to survive outside the notum-rich atmosphere of Rubi-Ka and the Shadowlands. The briefing reported that a band of mercenaries, believed to be based out of the Milky Way district, was responsible for the theft of the data and prototype suit in response to the proposed Newland Guard Initiative.

Many of the clanners were less than enthusiastic about Kithrak's request, voicing concerns about personal safety. "All he can offer us for security is a one-time pass to make sure Omni-Pol doesn't arrest us," said Tussa, leader of the clan Assembly. "Doesn't really sound like a good deal to me."

Several Omni-Tek employees are no more amiable to Kithrak's plan. "He has overstepped his bounds as an Omni-Tek executive," said Eternalforce of Omni-InternOps. "This is Omni business...the clanners have no business being involved."

Not everyone present was opposed to Kithrak's plan, however. "I've got no problem including the clans if they decide to pay us more than Omni-Tek," said Avrik of the Obsoleet mercenary organization. "Either way, this is going to get out to all of Rubi-Ka."

Kithrak has been assigned to the Midnight Reveries detachment pending resolution of an Omni-Pol investigation into the theft.
