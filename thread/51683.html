Mission XP- reward the brave
The current state of mission rewards encourages players to do easy, boring missions.

I can do a 50% mission with no real risk to my life (or rather the accrued xp) with each fight rather predictable and sterotyped. For me, an easy mob is about 10,000 xp. The reward for these missions is something like 50,000 xp.

I found that I prefer harder missions where the outcome of an individual fight is unknown. Each battle puts my life in jeopardy.

Just today, I spent 30 minutes trying to crack the front room containing 2 reds, 1 dark orange, and 1 yellow. So 30 minutes, 0 xp earned. So even though the mobs will yield higher xp per kill, this is a very ineffiecient way to earn xp. In addition, I am burning up treatment, first aid, a nano-rechargers like crazy. So not very financially efficient either.

In the end, I complete the mission earning 110,000 xp (110,000-50,000=60,000. 60,000 is 6 easy kills, so increased xp really means nothing).

Bottom line, I can earn xp and money much more efficiently in easy missions. True, I can get higher level loot in the harder missions, but I have rarely ever obtained an item from I chest I needed.

Conclusion:

I would like to see substantially increased rewards for difficult missions, to get me out of the rut of doing tons of easy missions so that I can get enough xp to level (I am currently leveling 3 times per 2 weeks). It would take forever, just selecting hard ones.

bump if you agree.

Bump

Simple way to get people out of missions is to increase the xp they can get outside. People never did missions back in the day, because pet pathing was terrible, human MOB AC was bugged making them almost impossible, and a few other reasons I can't recall. However, once all those bugs went away, everyone went indoors, because missions were just the best xp:time ratio.

Increase the xp reward for fighting outdoors and people will go outdoors. Increase the xp reward for fighting non-human MOBs. They are too hard as it is, and typically give less xp than their human counter-parts. Increase the xp reward for killing more than one target at a time. It's much harder to kill 3 MOBs at once than it is to kill 3 one at a time.

Bottom line, hunting is harder. Things that are harder provide more actual experience, and should translate into game xp.

It's simple....very simple actually...

How it should be:
Indoor mission.
Should have low risk factor (exit right behind you if main room gets out of hand). Chance of death should be minimal.
Should have token reward and cash reward at end. No XP kickers.
Should have good chance of scoring diet phat lewt.
XP gain should be decent. Not great, just decent.

Outdoor hunting.
Should have high risk factor (no exits except zone lines). Chance of death should be possible.
Should allow rare loot to drop off mobs, but not using the same loot tables as missions (the stock price of the Kin Of Tarasque disc doesn't need to get any lower as supply has flooded the market)
Should have very good XP gains compared to indoor missions. I back this with the simply philosophy of risk vs reward, which seems to be absent from the equation.

How it actually is:
Indoor mission.
Risk factor exists only when the game blackholes or lags. Chance of death in a fight with a mob when there are no blackholes is almost nil (at +150, fighting RMs & Aces isn't worth the time and Vets fall down like dominoes).
XP gain is best in game with the huge XP kicker at the end + token reward.
Diet phat lewt in every mission.

Outdoor hunting.
Risk factor is abnormally high. Death at one of the new camps can happen in an instant as I've heard from multiple level 200s that have been to the camps of boss mobs critting in excess of 8k. One crit would drop ANYONE unbuffed. The same crit could drop about 90%+ of Rubi-Ka's population fully buffed.
Haven't heard of or seen any phat lewts coming from the new camps. It just may take time for that though.
XP gain over time is nowhere near what it should be in light of how tough they made the new mobs.

...It's one thing to have to deal with a very powerful mob who presents a challenge. It's another thing to have to face certain death when mobs are able to drop you in one hit....
