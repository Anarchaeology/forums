*poof* im gone
I realized the other day a major flaw with AO NotumWars.
There is no incentive for one to fight on the loosing side (Clan on rk1, OT on rk2,... and neutral everywhere).
This is a wild guess, but I would say that in the area of lv150+ on Rimor, Clan outnumbers OT 3v2, perhaps more.
Therefore it doesnt matter how well organized or how well thought out an OT attack is, clanners can win just about every time via raw numbers. And dont give me that croud control thing, we all know it doesnt work.
Have you ever played tribes/counter strike/MOHAA/ any other on line fps? Then you know that its no fun when one team outnumbers the other 2v1. You will see people shouting "Teams!" and some players will switch to the side that is missing people. Some servers have auto-balance mods that do this automaticly. But AO has nothing of the sort.

Untill this get fixed, it is no more fun than playing a game of MOHAA where the teams are always locked at 20 vs 10.
So, myself [bigstupid] and bettiepaige are going to go away for a while. Out accounts will be active till mid march. and we will probably be droping in from time to time. But unless there is some sort of change with AO, I dont see us sticking around.

My only regret is all the good people we are leaving behind.

Good thinking there, Whites. Might work, might not. But, at least someone's looking at ways to introduce the semblance of normal real-world difficulties that larger masses have to overcome.

Having people pecking at your towers more often (perhaps 24 hours a day) for the largest orgs is not too far off from what you would expect to see naturally. Might not work. But at least it's a stab at things.

Look at the US and look at what percentage of their resources are put out just to keep the borders safe and maintain a prosperous economy and viable armed forces. Look at a company like Microsoft and see how hard they have to work just to keep all the competitors at bay.

That's not the case in AO. You get bigger, you get stronger, you are stronger so you get things easier, you then get stronger, this means you get bigger and get things easier. It never ends. There's no checks or balances to keep the game interesting other than the infrequent internal squabbling in a guild. If this were a session-based game like most FPS's, then I'd say 'fine' and accept that one side or one guild had 'won' and we could start over and explore the mechanics all over again. Just like Tribes or something. The problem is that this game is supposed to be a continuous struggle. Basically, as the original poster stated: it's over.

What if Towers were ALL destroyed every two weeks or once a month by a freak of Rubi'Ka's geology or the the bizzare notum flow? Would that be such a bad thing?
