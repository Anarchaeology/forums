A properly set up trader (or agents, or crats) will kill a soldier in 1 on 1 PvP every time (in my experience) unless they really screw up. The only exception is when the soldier actually has a better set of scopes on than the trader in question, but because high level traders can equip LLTS easier than anyone else, this is not likely.

Here's usually what happens:

Scenario 1:

Trader debuffs/roots the crap out of soldier and moves out of range, healing any damage that the soldier has managed to inflict up to this point.

Soldier must now waste time trying to break the root with a general debuff.

Soldier moves towards the trader again, only to get rooted again, all the while taking massive Ithaca crits; a soldier without some serious HP buffs is unable to withstand many of these.

Trader manages to heal away some if not all of the soldier's alpha strike/crit damage. Trader crits soldier to death.

But what about MK shield you ask?

Scenario 2:

Trader meets soldier. Soldier activates his best MK shield immediately, assuming the trader has not caught the soldier by surprise/lagging and already landed a debuff. Trader roots soldier. Soldier, now in nano lock from using MK shield is forced to sit helplessly. Trader moves out of range again to heal, re-rooting if necessary. The trader stands and waits for the soldier's shield to drop (or sometimes not), at which point the soldier will still be unable to break a root for at least 40 seconds. The Ithaca has a base range of 24m, beating the Nova Flow's 20m and tying the Flashpoint. Assuming that both trader and soldier are using Q200 LLTS, with no Yatamuchy, and that the soldier has already spent his alpha strike, the Ithaca using trader now has a 1.8m greater range than the Nova Flow using soldier. Trader can sit safely outside the soldier's range and crit/aimed shot away until the soldier goes down. In most cases like this it wouldn't even be necessary for the trader to wait for the shield to go down, as you can still crit for over 300 dmg while MK is running.

Essentially a soldier's only chance to defeat a properly set up/played trader (or any rooting class really) is to gun them to death with a single alpha strike. This is definitely possible, but its sort of a crap shoot. When you factor in heals, roots, and debuffs (that deny the use of the best mk shields, flashpoints, novas), it becomes extremely unlikely. Agents that use Ranees (28m base range) and scopes have an even easier time rooting and outranging a nano-locked soldier.

I am not trying to say that soldiers have a more difficult time against traders than other professions; but since I play a soldier, I thought I might be able to give the "soldier's perspective" on fighting traders, as I've seen traders compared to soldiers several times in this thread.

At just level 128 and "Rookie", I'm by no means the ultimate authority, so if something I have said is wrong and/or misleading, feel free to address it.

couple of things:

nano shutdown - Angel knows this hurts...thats why she hits Noer and I with (as well as our docs) as soon as we engage clanners....it basically shuts us down entirely and makes it damn hard to stay alive

init debuffs - when we start shooting at 2.5/2.5 or worse in PvP, plus our heals/roots debuffs all stop being insta cast and start taking 3-5 secs each to cast (with an extra 3-5 secs recharge), that tends to poop on our party as well

Besides that....a soldier's well placed alpha can make us run like lil girls too....if you area soldier tho, you should listen to Mantra and Zero tho....they are rarely caught un awares by traders and debuffers. They go into PvP expecting to be debuffed and therefore have backup Debuffed HOT-Key bars rdy, as well as far lower lvl nova/FP's in case they are debuffed. The reson most people get so destroyed is because they were not prepared and they get flustered after the first debuff comes in.
