How good is AO.
OK! I have bashed AO for a long time.

I have been mad, hated the Game Driector for some of his moronic ideas.

I have looked at other games out there just to switch.

But, I have to admit, AO is the best game out there.

Its better the NeoCron, AC2, E&B, EQ, and UO.

The game is so eazy to play. That is, it is very intuative to play. The HUD is amazingly human friendly.

Yes! Even though I dont like the Game Director, I have to say the game machanics are the best. The graphics is the best. The concept is the best.

Now if the Game director would stop nerfing everything to hell and back, maybe, Just maybe things would get back to normal.

You balance the the game by pulling professions up, not bringing them down.

Even though I agree, this is quite off-topic...

I have tried a lot of MMORPGs before, but none have had the pull for me like AO has. Some of it is the graphics, because I am constantly amazed at some of the wonderous sites I see there. Some of it is the storyline which, even though I think still needs some work, still can be very compelling and reminds me of some of the finer sci-fi novels out there. And finally, it's the people. People tend to make the most compelling content, and the people who play on AO are the best, more friendly and still more serious about the game than most EQ players I know (but in a good way).
