To fight or not to fight...
Today I was involed in two battles in Belial Forest and it got me to thinking. With the deregulation of mining allowing for the free mining of Notum is there a need for confilct. While it is known that a side effect of the Control Towers is to reduce the levels of the Supression gas does that mean we have to fight?
