Bwahaha MA world? We're still working on nanomage world. Maybe after we've done all the species, we can do the profs. I'm with millenko though. All profs should have special dungeons for them, with entrances in Omni-Ent, Tir, and Newland (Same dungeon, just 3 of them), Give it say 20 rooms, and to go to the next room you must be a certain level. In each room have prof-oriented items of the room's rough level. Of course the last room should be only level 190+ or something, have a super-tough boss mob that respawns.. eh... every 6 hours, and have him carry some super nano or item for MAs, and only that mob carries it. Of course, the powers of the mob should be based according to the professions, since only members of that profession will be in there. For example, the MA would have a pretty standard super-tough mob, since we do good damage and can heal. For a more specialized profession, like the agent perhaps, there should be a mob that does melee damage but can be rooted, has lots of hp, but does not hit very hard. For a fixer, have a mob in a HUGE room that is melee and runs pretty fast, thus the fixers can kite around w/ their runspeed buffs while other fixers blast it. The hardest might be the doctor mob, which I suggest should be something that saps your nano and does AE damage, but has low hp. Just some ideas
