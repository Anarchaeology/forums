MP Creation Weapons-1hb/2hb
MP creation weapons can be broken in to two categories. 1hb. 2hb.

2hb at best gives you 6% nano cost and 1021 nano.

1hb at best gives you 14% nano cost recution and 1003 nano.

If you want the best mods, then you use 1hb creations.

So what purpose does having a seperate line have? 2hb has high min damage, high max damage, and an AR based 80% off of TS. However, these weapons have a 3 second attack and 12 second recharge time, so obviously they are not meant to deal damage. So they're for mods.

So...once again, what is the purpose of the 2hb creation line. Do you realize that stat wise it is worthless?

I know that you, Funcom, have stated that the creations are perfect and do what they're intended to do. But can you honestly look at these numbers and tell me that 2hb has a purpose?
