something i have noticed
One thing that I have noticed is how the general quality of teamtactics decreases the higher the lvl players are.

At lower levels you tend see fantastic teamcombo's and tactics, everyone doing his part in order to guarantee a victory in a fight.
Teamwork is necessary at those levels and i have seen some truly amazing teams with my rerolls. On the other hand, many a team of 170+ just storm in, dont even wait for all to be fully buffed, dont care if someone LD's or is behind, each teammember "grabs" his own mob, mezzing adds is not even considered, ninja looting and people just heading in all 4 directions at once; generally very crappy teamplaying, if one can still call that teamplaying. Recently i was in a team, about to do a ql 190+ or something, when me and a trader asked for no reflect shields to be used as to ensure smooth mezzes. One member said "doesnt matter if you use or not use a shield, cause im running MY shield anyway so mezzes will break regardless of what you do".... Now, regardless of the matter if mezzing is still useful or not is debatable, this just to highlight how little people can care for their own teams well working. My conclusion is: many lvl 170 players play like total n00bs and have no coinsideration at all for their own team, whereas one would assume the experience gained to get at that level would have taught someone how to play in a correct way..
One thing IS fun tho....when you get in a team like this, and then someone dies, and everyone goes : "Huh?!?!? You died??? how is THAT possible???!!??" .....

I rest my case
