Vox Populi Calls for Trial of Colonel Belerafon
Greetings. My name is Hierodule, and I am Public Relations spokesman and Interim President of a Clan organization called Vox Populi, on Rubi-Ka Dimension 2. On Sunday, June 9, a military tribunal will be held regarding the charges against Colonel Garry "Belerafon" Ye, of Division 3: Omni Reclamation. This event has already been added to the player events calendar at the official Anarchy Online site, at 11:00 U.S. Pacific time, 14:00 U.S. Eastern time, and 19:00 U.K. (British Summer Time). What appears below is the Vox Populi's official response to this trial.

=========

Subject: Vox Populi Calls for Trial of Colonel Belerafon
From: Vox Populi guild headquarters, Public Relations Director Hierodule
To: All concerned citizens of Rubi-Ka (Dimension 2)
Press Release: June 3, 29476

Fellow citizens, you may already be aware of the upcoming scheduled military tribunal of Colonel Garry "Belerafon" Ye, a member of the band of criminals known as Division 3: Omni Reclamation. However, all you have received so far is a whitewashing of the story, sanitized and prepared for your gullible consumption by Omni-Tek employees. We of Vox Populi wish to set the record straight on the events leading up to this scheduled travesty, and enlist public support in bringing a ruthless criminal to justice.

On the morning of May 24, a squad of Vox Populi members, led by Commander Sonny "Ramid" Bichrest, attempted a search and rescue mission to assist the crew and passengers of an Omni-Tek transport which had crashed in the eastern region of Stret West Bank. In the pursuit of this selfless humanitarian mission, the brothers and sisters of Vox Populi were attacked by a cadre of thugs from Division 3, led by Colonel Belerafon. Not only was this was a blatant violation of the Tir Accords, which state that no citizen shall hinder another, regardless of faction, in the pursuit of a rescue operation, but it also showed the callousness which is typical of Omni-Tek in general, and Division 3 in particular, as they endangered the lives of their own people in pursuit of a petty grudge against Vox Populi.

Mistakenly believing that Division 3 operatives would understand and adhere to the Tir Accords, several members of the Vox Populi rescue party laid down their arms and attempted to explain the situation. Regrettably, they were immediately taken into custody at gunpoint by the Divison 3 gangsters, leaving Commander Ramid (still free at that point) only one option: He bargained his own freedom for theirs, placing his faith in the few remaining uncorrupted Omni-Tek leaders to uphold the Tir Accords and set him free. He was never given the chance, as he was shot in the back by a cowardly (and as yet undisclosed) Division 3 operative.

This may in fact seem to be the end of the issue, as we know death is not permanent. However, Commander Ramid's resurrection was most unusual, in that it took him several days to recover his full strength. Unnamed (but reliable) sources within Omni-Med have informed Vox Populi officers that in fact Ramid was an unknowing participant in Omni-Tek's latest attempt at creating a weapon which disrupts the life-pattern to the point that resurrection is not possible. Fortunately, this was yet another Omni-Tek failure, but apparently not by a very wide margin.

We charge Colonel Belerafon, and/or his subordinates, as appropriate, with the following crimes:

1. Interference with a search and rescue operation
2. Violation of an offered and accepted battlefield truce
3. Wrongful execution of a prisoner without due process
4. Use of an illegal resurrection disruptor, a.k.a. "perma-kill device"

Regrettably, we expect that the evidence has been destroyed or hidden, making the most critical charge impossible to prove. However, the facts of the case plainly speak to the first three charges. As these events took place in Stret West Bank, clearly they fall within the jurisdiction of the Merchant Council of Borealis, a neutral court. Vox Populi has requested that Colonel Belerafon be turned over to the Merchant Council for trial.

In response, President Chang "Nortsrum" Brockmeyer of Division 3 has "generously" offered instead to convene a military tribunal, charging Belerafon only with trivialities regarding his ineffectiveness in carrying out the ambush and attempted murder of Ramid and his team. Nortsrum has made it clear that he will shamelessly control every aspect of this tribunal, including the charges brought, the conduct of the trial, and no doubt, the final verdict to be rendered.

The tribunal is scheduled for Sunday, June 9, at 2 PM U.S. Eastern time (11 AM U.S. Pacific, 7 PM U.K.), at Reet's Retreat in Stret West Bank, Rubi-Ka Dimension 2. We ask that concerned citizens of all denominations be present to protest this travesty, and demand a fair trial of Colonel Belerafon. We also ask that members of the Council of Merchants be present to conduct such a trial in response to the anticipated public demand.
