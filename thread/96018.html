For PvM they're probably about equal - at lower levels the X-3 will be better because of its faster speed, and it's a little easier on your IP. At higher levels (TL5 and 6) the manex will pull ahead as your ranged init passes the 600 mark unbuffed/unimplanted.

TIM scope is pretty much standard for both guns. The Manex, due to speed, is a bad gun if you have a low attack rating because if you miss you lose a lot of damage/time. But it makes up for that somewhat at bosses, compared to the X-3, since you can't really AS bosses, whereas burst works fine.

For PvP the X-3 is far superior at all levels. 35m vs 14m range. No more need for discussion. Plus, at TL5 and higher, you can switch your X-3 for a caterwaul for AS recycle if you've been rifle. Before TL5 you won't have the IP to pursue an effective AS-recycle PvP strategy, however, so the X-3 is preferred to that point, for its higher regular damage and crit.
