Suggestion: Two new petcommands: (No bull****)
I suggest two new commands for pets.

1: /pet walkmysteps
This would make it go excactly where I went. Unless there is a bug in the map all my movement is legal and so would my pets movment be. It would stay in the steps I was 2-3 seconds ago and therfore its movement would also be legal. With this the coders would not have to consern about pet walking path and we could make it come thrue the missions without having to go back and lurk the pet around obsticals thats not there. Et could be equal to /pet behind in any other actions.

2: /pet attacksticky
This command would make the pet stick to one target no mather how much agro it may get from others. It is rather irritating to send a pet in, it fights tre persons and when it die you see that all tre is halfway down. If it stuck to one at the time it would have killed one maby two of them before it got killed. After all it is we who order the pet? And in normal circumstances we would have the chance to fight sticky. Think of runing in with an enforcer and from time to time the game changed your target for you. It would have been a SCREAM in protests. If this is a problem in PvP then just ignore it in PvP or make it run /pet attack instead.
