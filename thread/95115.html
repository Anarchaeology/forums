My Proposed Democracy.
I grow very tired of repeating myself 5 times a day. Somtimes I feel like I am talking to a crowd of brick walls. I am going to explain my proposal one time, and one time only. From the time after this post I will not explain myself again, I will simply refer to this post if anyone questions my motives.

I am what you could call a Grass Roots Clanner. If you look back to what our Forefathers wanted. You will see that it is not to kill all Omni, or to erradicate their presence. The simple statement of their goal was "to bring a democratic government to Rubi-Ka. A government by the people, for the people."

No where in that sentence does it say that all Omni should die. Nor does it say that everyone on this Planet has to join us. It only says "To bring a democratic government to Rubi-ka."

I am here to do just that, and in the most peaceful way possible. I will not force anyone to live under me as Simon Silverstone, and Philip Ross do. If you enjoy their lifestyle, then by all means, stay where you are. I am only here to offer a more liberal alternative. I would not force anyone into subserviancy, or force them into a second class position. In a Democracy people are equal, and everyone has the same rights.

I respect everyones rights that are given to them. Freedom of speech, Freedom to bear arms, Freedom of Life, Liberty, and the Pursuit of Happiness. No matter where your happiness may be I encourage you to find it. (So long as it isnt self-destructive, or destructive to others.) Be it with me, or roaming free out in the Deep Artery Valley.

The Northern territories to be run by the people that inhabit them. Not to be told what to do by any one man.

The RMC that is currently forming is not this Democracy I propose. The RMC is simply an interfactional organization of nonagression pacts. Basically its a gathering of guilds from different factions that agree not to attack each other, and will live in peace with one another. Enjoying the added prosperity that comes with this peace.

Guilds in the RMC will not have to worry about their neighbor attacking them, because if the 2 guilds are in the RMC they would not attack each other. And by eliminating a nearby enemy you have time to do other things. Things such as increasing the productivity of your mine. Or decrasing, maybe even eliminating the amount of pollution it secretes.

For more information on the RMC please click the link in my signature.

As for the Democracy I proposed, it is as of this moment simply a dream. If you have any questions lets please keep them mature, and not begin asking questions I have already answered in this post.
