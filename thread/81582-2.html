I play Agent and in my play style the top 3 most feared professions in NW large scale battles for me are:

1. NT's

Area nukes, blinds, roots and occassional nasty single nuke too.

Area nukes are most annoying of all those although the damage alone usually is not enough to kill me, it is enough to make me retreat and regroup.

Blinds lower my ATT rating so much that my chances for good hits and crits is next to nothing, so when I am blinded, I am out of game till I am back at normal. Visual dont affect me that much, I can still use map...

2. FIXERS

Their snares make me slow. For my play style, speed is essential. Also root makes me target for longer than I like and they can dish some very respectable damage while I keep missing (GA IV Fixers). Also they are VERY hard to root.

Basically I try to avoid confortation with Fixer.

3. AGENTS

What you say? Well, again this is my play style. What I cant see, can kill me when I am weak. Believe it or not, for another Agent, the opposing Agent is dangerous. Popping out of nowhere to root me (again exposing me to danger longer than I want) or AS'ing and then running 100% crit... lets just say it HURTS.

///

Ok. Where are all the big bad Traders and Enforcers etc you may ask?

In current form of NW mass battles I dont really "see" Enforcers from closeup. Every Enforcer I have standed right next to, have been either super lagged or just ignoring pesky Agent since I have never been hit by Enforcer in NW tower battles

Yes, being in the receiving end of Punishing Rod or EQB _WILL_ HURT but as said, in my play style its a rare encounter.

As for traders. Shrug, they are just another Soldier in my view in NW tower battles. Sure they can debuff you (seems I resist 1/4 in full agro) but basically it just takes me away from battle for a moment.

I am sure Enforcers, Traders, Soldiers and whatever are far greater risks than any the three I mentioned here for _other_ professions or in other situations and play styles.

For me and my style, the list is above...
