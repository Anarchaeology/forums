AO's mission system leaves a lot to be desired, it is totally random, has no tie-ins to story or affecting anything at all.

But NeoCron is worse. Only type of missions currently working there is delivery missions

EQ is somewhat functional quest wise. Didnt do a lot of them, and those I did was delivery missions...

I'm digressing a lot, but please bear with me...

Overall, AO's missions are not too bad, when compared to other games No really ! but they still could use a LOT of improvement.

Especially tie in in with the currently missing storyline. I'm thinking sabotage missions that actually affect installations etc.

Too bad most current installations are only decorative.

I mean, there are factories that dont really produce anything, and shops just automagically spawn items...

I'd love to see some working economy, with mines and plantations supplying raw materials to factories, which in turn supply shops, and prices fluctuating with supply and demand.

Not to mention giving vehicles like the Carrier a use for those inclined to haul cargo between, say, factories and shops making a buck on actual buying and selling produce.

But I'm dreaming again. Sorry.

No, I'm not sorry, and I'll dream some more

Tradeskills. Again comparing to other games...

EQ was a bit fiddly, with myriad kits, recipes and extremely hard to find components looted of ultra rare and uber camped mobs.

NeoCron... Now this is where NeoCron really shines. If I were to continue playing NC I'd consider forgetting missions, hunting, PK'ers and ninjalooters and just stay in the city doing tradeskills.

You can build just about anything by blueprinting it finding ( or constructing ) the materials and then building it. Provided you have the required skills for the tech level of the item.

And, get this, you can build everything cheaper than then shops. That gives tradeskills a purpose.

And parts do not affect the quality of the result. The blueprint and you skill does.

AO has something to learn here...tradeskills in AO are a pain, and are not profitable to do, which is sort of self defeating. ( Ok, there are some things that actually work, like building nanos and modifying guns etc )
