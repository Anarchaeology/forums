Division 9 R.S.G.E [Omni RK1] Listed as R.S.G.E. (Division9)
Division 9 R.S.G.E. is a organisation dedicated to carring out the will of Omni-Tek of RK1 and is very storyline driven.

Division 9 is the embodyment of a cross profession paramilitary organisation containing many different departments with core objectives such as :
* Intelligence
* Covert Operations
* Medical Services
* Trade and Construction
* Special Forces & Security

Division 9 also works closely with other factions to bring about story change and roleplay oppertunity for those that wish it. We participate in regular events such as Omni-Teks leaders Meetings, Raids against hostile threats, The signing of the Galway Pact and The Athens Accords, The March for Unity & Peace, The Defense of Athens.

If your interested in finding out more about our organisation please view our website at http://division9.rsge.net
