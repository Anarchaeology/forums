I think the difficulty usually seems to balance out in the long run. I usually do my missions with the same settings, unless I'm either slumming for easy cash (doing 0% difficulty), or want something totally suicidal. And with my "standard" settings, I've found the following:

(1) Three reds in the front room, and the rest of the mission green or yellow.

(2) All green NPC's on the south side of the dungeon, all red A-2000's and A-4000's on the north side. (Couldn't finish that one -- 'bots are much tougher than humans.)

(3) A nice even distribution of medium to dark orange throughout.

(4) Solid reds all the way through, but they were NPC's that were easily persuaded to fight one-on-one.

(5) Whole darn thing deserted! Just treasure free to be scooped up.

Sometimes a mission seems easier than it should be, sometimes harder. It's within a range, just like the size of the mission area can vary from micro to mega, without varying the settings. But it should always average out, over the course of several missions, to be about what you'd expect.

actually, your mission location is a product of a couple of factors:

1) What level/ql the mission/reward is

2) Which faction mission terminal you're using.

Moving the Money/Exp slider just determines how much money/exp you'll make for finishing the mission. Best bet is to set it to full Money since Exp is piddly.

At around lev 1-30 you should be getting missions in Rome, Omni-1, Galway, Lush, Omni Forest and Southern Artery Valley (lol, mission terminal should not be giving these out to lowbies). After that, you'll be getting stuff like 4 holes, Stret East/West, Borealis, even later Clon****, Pleasant Meadows, Andromeda, Milky Way, much later, Broken Shores, Artery Valley, Foul Plains
