Laboratory Proposal 23-7C
Supervising Technician: Dr. Vernon Houston
Timestamp: 22 Feburary 29476 15:42:41
Project Title 'Opifex Cortical Adjustments for Accelerated Pattern Recognition'

Proposal
While the Opifex breed have proven useful in the field as espionage and intrusions staff, the researcher believes it is possible to, through minor modification of the cortical patterns, use the same neural structure blueprint to create a heightened ability to pattern-match at the expense of distance perception, while maintaining the same levels of hand-eye coordination and a slight loss in reflexive capabilities.

The ojective is to create a sub-breed of the Homo Opifex that excels in research, nanotechnology and learning. While the Homo Nano can and will exceed the capabilities of this project in all ways, this research team proposes that these developments will create a breed that can function in a Notum-deprived environment.

Total Budget: 110 man/days, 30 Foetal Development Accelerator/days

Detailed project breakdown attachments: GANTT, RAc, Cost/Benefit Analysis, Risk Analysis

Laboratory Report 23-7C-32
Supervising Technician: Dr. Vernon Houston
Timestamp: 19 March 29476 19:05:55
Project Title 'Opifex Cortical Adjustments for Accelerated Pattern Recognition'

Stage 1 development is going ahead of schedule. Test subject 23-7C-512-2 has yielded positive matrix results. Furthur use of multiple development paths has been suspended pending results of this trial. All other foetuses have been terminated due to neural development faults.

Note that all -1 series have failed to take from the moment of introduction of any of the 7C forumale. The multiplexing capabilities inherent in the double-X chromosomal makeup seem a requirement for the necessary neural development. Any -1 series foetus has suffered immediate and irreparable neural damage.

The test subject is being advance-educated in pattern recognition tasks and exercises during growth. The injection of nanoformula 512 has altered the development of the visual cortex as anticipated and the brain forms pattern matches and recognises structure with surprising rapidity.

Dr. Hallidey is working with Psychosocial to generate a memory-set for 512-2. Parental candidates have been determined to assist in proper psychosocial integration should the subject prove viable.

He was working at an engineering fabrication plant on Eden at the time, and that was the day he decided to show me around. That was certainly one of the better days of my life - probably the only day my dad treated me like an adult. Funny how these are the things you remember, isn't it?

Disjointed. I find it very difficult to remain focused on anything for any length of time. I was reading that this isn't normal for Opifex?

Makes my colleaues rather ... uneasy.

My Diary, by Shanelle age 9 and 16years

Dear Diary,
I accepted the promotion to command and control today. They've posted me as a diplomatic liason to a mining consortium near 2H0. Daddy says tomorrow he will take me to the zoo again. I'm confused. I'm only 9, and my posting doesn't happen until I'm 25.
Love, Shanelle. Age 9.

In 72 seconds it is possible for a lot of people to die. A coordinated strike by terrorist agents against an Omni-Tek mining facility adds fuel to the flames. ICC support of Omni-Tek initiatives against the clans in the light of such atrocities is understandable.

The site security commander was one of the first to die in the initial wave. Nerve gas in one of the back tunnels killed an entire mining team and secured an entry point to the mines for the attackers. They know the exact weaknesses in the mines, and the exact timing to launch an assault.

As site foreman I've been given the task of managing a fighting retreat. I can't help but think the situation was engineered this way - a good company girl is put in a position of military command to put her where OT want her.
I'm still thinking this as I watch the disposable pistol melt into a puddle of nanogoo. This is the first time I've been ordered to kill someone.
I turn away from the still twitching corpse of the site security commander. I'm sure his insurance is up to date, which made killing him easier.
The camera teams pull away as the gunship approaches. I stop to wonder just how much effort it will take them to airbrush the OT logos from the 'rebel' uniforms before the press release goes out.

My Diary, by Shanelle age 5 weeks

My memories tells me I am 27 years old, that I have a father and a mother. My mother died when I was young and my father died two days ago by the company's request. I carried out the operation myself, and I did not feel the slightest twang of remorse. Not when I deleted the insurance records, and not when I pulled the trigger.

I know that my meories of my life are false. I know I was grown in a laboratory, and that I am a genetic experiment - like so many others here. However, unlike the others, my changes are psychological - the memory implanting process does not fool my altered brain. Their inconsistencies are just too easy to see through.

Its a strange mix. Trusting and paranoid at the same time. I don't think like other people do. I'm not smarter, I'm not stupider. I just think in a different way. Non-linear adaptive thought processes. Perception and Pattern Matching. At the expense of my eyesight. An Opifex who needs to wear contacts for reading and driving, and for whom firing a gun accurately is more luck than anything else. There is some form of twisted irony in there somewhere, I'm sure.

I still keep the mementoes of my false past. It is a nice fiction, and gives me somewhere safe in my mind to run to should I need it. Even a false comfort is better than facing the world alone. It is strange. I know who my father is, but I have never known my mother. I wonder how many others can honestly say that?
I am a native of Rubi-Ka. I have been nowhere else, and I know nothing else. I often wonder how many others here are actually from off-world, and how many just think they are because of the memories they have been given? At least I know the truth about myself. It is a cold, hard truth, but a truth nonetheless.

This is going to be my last diary entry. In many ways it is my first. While I remember writing a diary when I was nine, the diary that I hold in my hands was not written by me. It is part of the illusion, nothing more.

They gave me a name I remember. But I think I will take another name. Anita. It reminds me of how little one person can control their fate, and how much the company really cares about one individual.

At least I am not going to psych.eval. any more.

They have issued me with an apartment and an opportunity to acclimatise to working on Rubi-Ka. I've got instructions to join a subdivision of Omni-Tek who operate in a variety of areas tomorrow. I guess they are still testing me - still trying to see how they can use me.

I am and remain an asset. A tool. A weapon.
