Please Read! Looking to make money in a different way!
We at the High Courts Of Justice, are always loooking into new ways in which to advance the players enjoyment within Rubi-Ka.


So we have come up with a way for you to earn money in a more different manner!

This will be on Rubi-Ka 1.

The High Courts Of Justice will be issuing bounties on criminals.

e.g Player monsoon attacks guards outside of Newland for no reason, and player Monsoon happens to be Omni. Now under the Tir Accord they are not allowed to do that. Therefore the High Courts would issue a bounty on that persons head for their capture.

The bounty size will depend on the elusiveness of the player trying to be caught.

We will assign contracts to players who wish to take on the role of bounty hunter.

You will get paid when the criminal is succesfully brought before the court.

You ask well how am I supposed to do that!

Well we are paying you to get this person to court, that is your job.

So all you bounty hunter wannabes keep a look out for upcoming contracts.

Thanks for listening
