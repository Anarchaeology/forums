Fixer NCU buffs - NON FIXERS READ
In order to better educated the non-fixer public regarding the workings of these buffs and to keep at least myself, if not the entire fixer population from going insane, I would like to present the following information.

1. They are team buffs only. In order to get one, you must be teamed with the fixer. Asking for an NCU buff while you're already in a team without the fixer, or asking the fixer for an NCU buff when that fixer is already in a full team of his own will not allow you to get an NCU buff.

2. I cannot stress this one enough. NCU BUFFS DO NOT REFRESH. You must wait until the buff runs out or physically cancel the buff to get another. Now, when the buff runs out, you will NOT lose the programs you have running that occupy the extra NCU you got from the buff. In fact, you can even refresh those buffs. You cannot, however, get additional buffs once the NCU runs out and in order to get a new one, you must cancel enough buffs to get back down to the point where you have 1 NCU available for a new buff.


I post this because I know that I personally have NEVER been able to just cast this buff on any of my teams just once after I do it the first time. Someone inevitably does not cancel the NCU buff before asking me to redo it, or never pays attention when I announce I'm redoing it.

As the man said....."help me help you". Don't drive the nice smurfs crazy.
