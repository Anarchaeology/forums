Omni-AF proposes "Omni-Wide Tower Alliance"
Greetings!

As you might have guessed, Omni-AF has returned from Beta testing and is nearing readiness for tower-ownage.

However, rather than one faction battling against the next and helping the clanners disarray ourselves, Omni-AF would like to propose that Omni-Tek departments join them against a common goal.

Total ownsership of all disputed land.

Any and all loyal Omni factions are welcomed to join up. Land ownership will be divided among factions but more importantly, all adjoining Omni departments involved will gain the protection and strength of all others. With every new department that joins, Omni's hold on the Notum Towers will grow even tighter, as will our grip on the disorganized anarchistic clanners nads.

All interested departments are urged to contact myself here or board-members "Alamalmithal" and "Zeuxs" ingame.

Fight Anarchy and reap the spoils of victory!

-President Theodore Shakkles Ganes
