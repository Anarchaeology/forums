I think that clanners/omni tek do not roleplay together that much (I mean together in the sense of fighting one another but roleplaying while doing it, etc) because of two things:

For one, probably any RPer who has tried to roleplay during PvP has got a response from the person he/she was fighting somewhere along the lines of 'OMG! Ur ghey man, y r u talking bout' clanns vs omni and ****e lololol' and never bothers again, thinking most people who participate actively in PvP are just power gaming title farmers (which a lot of us are, truth be told).

Secondly, a lot of people take this game too seriously. I was in a group out at the cyborgs in Mort Crater one time and a bunch of Clanners showed up and started yelling things about 'Omni scum', etc. etc. At first there was actually some productive roleplaying going on (I suppose you could call it that) and nobody was getting offended... but eventually it just deteriorated into an all out flame war and in the end a lot of /petitions were filed and ARKs were sent to sort the matter out (a few of the people on the factions of either side took the whole 'Clanner/Omni Scum!' shouts a little too seriously, and decided to avenge their egoes by kill stealing from the other faction... and anybody that has been around when killstealing occurs knows that it usually gets out of control and very nasty very fast).

That's my two cents on the matter... I sort of feel like I'm butting in here since I don't roleplay myself in game (it was my plan to be a hardcore roleplayer when I first started playing AO back in October of 2002, but I ended up joining an org that doesn't roleplay, so I guess that's just how the cards played out)... but I know from other mmo's that there is generally a lot of animosity between the 'RPers' and the 'Powergamers'. And judging from past experience I think that anybody trying to roleplay during a PvP fight would probably just get flamed and called a lot of names.

It's my opinion that more roleplay would make this game a lot more interesting, but, unfortunately, a lot of non-RPers are quick to slam anyone they see trying to roleplay as a 'loser'. And on the flipside of that, there's probably a lot of roleplayers who are quick to slam non-RPers as powergaming nitwits.
