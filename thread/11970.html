Concerns about the future of AO
I recently started playing since you started your free seven day trial, and I think the game is a fantastic concept with huge potential. I am an avid gamer, and I played for 21 out of the first 26 hours I had the game. It was several days before I even started role playing, where the some of the best fun is. Then I started reading some of the forums. It sounds like a lot of people are VERY discontented, and from what I have read about your patches, there seems to be some serious internal problems on your development team in regards to the direction of game play. I also have to make something very clear, and I am sure that you are painfully aware of it already. There are other futuristic games hitting the market very soon, and if a majority of your problems are not solved BEFORE then, you will loose almost everyone to them. And I do not mean addressed in forums, I mean SOLVED. I read a note from one of your programmers about how you develop patches for the game, and it seemed to me that he took his random polls of players in the game over the posts in the forum. In every business I have ever seen, the squeakiest wheel always gets the most oil. I can honestly say that I have never seen the squeakiest wheel get ignored, which is what is happening on your forums. The idea that I do not think you are considering, is that the people who take the time to post to the forums, are the people who are most involved in the games and notice the changes or lack there of the most. I can do math very well, and the formula of $10 twelve times a year per person is an easy one. I loved the game when I first played it, but why should I sink $120 per year into a game that is going to be more frustrating than golf, and less satisfying. The answer is I wont, and I will go on to another game on principal alone, even if it were guaranteed to have the exact same problems. I would do it just because it would be something new, and all my friends from Anarchy Online would be there with me, along with the friends that did not bother trying the free seven day trial because of the problems they heard about. I am not jaded against this game yet, and I hope to see enough changes quickly to keep me from that path. It is already too late for some of your long time players, but I hope you decide to make a big push very soon.

Sincerely eager to see changes,

Lukahn
