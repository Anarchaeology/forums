Eleet
One of my members, Hibernaculum, has been investigating a clan token board known as Double Sun and we would like to share our results with you.

Token board
We had a clans person volunteer who had recently been awarded the Double Sun, this is the most prestigous clan award at this time.

Experiments
We tried two experiments
1 - transfer and use of the board to a second person
2 - an attempt to elucidate the function of the board itself ie is it a reciever of some kind.

results
The functional board could not be transfered from its official owner to a second person. We note that new boards can be bought very cheaply in stores.

In an attempt to determine how the board functions we asked the clans person to dismantle the board. Interestingly, they did this with no hesitation. The board contains complex nanotechnology which we are still analysing. However, our subject purchased a new token board and after 'activatation' it had the same properties and augmentations of the original.

conclusions and some discussion
The current merit boards identify their owner in some way, perhaps by a DNA scan?
the board itself may act as a reciver and grant its benefits on the recipient due to some stored data elsewhere and not in the board per se. suggesting at least one database is present which contains the data for each award recipient and directs some sort of signal to that person. We would think that the merit board must know who it is currently owned by and sends a signal back to the central database, which in turn activates the board to grant the appropriate augmentations. Further work is required to elucidate the nature of this signalling process, if it exists.

the full article can be found in
Hibernaculum et al J. Nano. Res. 12573 pp1658-1663

((basically you an delete your board, buy a new one and still have your original benefits. the board is not updated but the numver of tokens is stored in your character info))

I have another update. After some additional testing it appears we have figured out how the switching of sides works; and why the benefits bestowed by the symbiotic bots are lost when switching sides. Like most people I thought that the token board itself was the key to the beneficial effects of having a full or partially full token board. And when changing sides the token board was destroyed and the benefits lost. Being neutral I did not have any knowledge otherwise.

Earlier, however, we discovered that the token board is much like a computer chip that is continously refreshing beneficial nanos and is much like a battery that is charged from symbiotic bots released into the token board from bravery tokens. The tokens are crytalized nano bots that are released when added to the token board and are released into the system of the host. The tokens are electronically faction encoded and cannot be added to the opposing factions board. This was tested by a subject that had a token from each faction and only the token matching the boards faction was able to charge the token board. We did not notice any harmful affects from trying to add the opposing factions token to the board. Having said this, neutrals should be able to use the discs from both factions once the board is made. Its just a simple matter of having the board be able to decode the discs from both factions.

When it came to changing sides the application form and not the token board proved to be the vital key. The application board uses a DNA signature. What most people failed to notice is that when the application form gets the DNA sample it also injects a liquid substance into the applicant. This substance is not harmful to the host but it is leathal to the symbiotic bots released into the host from the original token board. It has an additional side affect of allowing someone with the proper map reading skills to determine the faction of the applicant. This suggests that a slightly different form of symbiotic bot is used to erradicate existing faction token bots as well as those that are used by the map reader to determine faction. Having never been charged with the symbiotic bots used by the factions application form, neutrals appear as grey on the map reader. Omni Tek and Clan appear as red or blue denoting the type of application form bot and and color.

We will have to do more research into Uncle Bazzit's Profiteers Helper. It is most likely a precharged nano transferance system of limited capabilities and not scaleable like the current token boards even if it is of similar strength of a fully charged token board.

I will keep everyone posted as we make new discoveries.
