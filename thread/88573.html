Shadows of Conspiracy
As the shuttle broke free of Rubi-Ka's atmosphere, Randa was still puzzled about her mission. Intern-Ops had messaged her, asking if she would go and investigate a mysterous unidentified shuttle that had been discovered orbiting above the planet. Hoping that the craft might possibly be of value to uncovering those behind Tis's caputre, She gratefully agreed to go in the company of 2 engineers from Intern-Ops.

As they glided through space, Randa had wondered why they had chosen her to go, considering that there were dozens more qualified than she was that could have taken her place. As they approached the orbiting craft, she noticed that it looked a bit like the transport craft that the ICC had used when they made planet-fall some time ago.

The docking with the ship went smoothly, and when she had finished hacking the outer hatch of the other ship. The two engineers slowly went in to begin thier survey. While Randa walked around the main cabin, she began to notice some pieces of technology that she recognised. ICC, Sol, Omni, even TrueSpace technology was strewn about the deck, all in what appeared to be well working condition.

Randa wandered toward a large console that contained what appeared to be a very sophisticated radar system, she began to open the fileing cabnets that surrounded it. They were filled with incredibly detailed notes and pictures of the surface of the planet, mostly of Omni-1 and Rome.

As she opened to last file cabnet, she suddenly heard the ships speakers chirp up with a grave message.

Security System Compromised, Self-Destruct Engaged.

Randa stood up, and stared at the screen in front of her. She had 15 seconds to make it out and away from the ship alive.

She streamed down the ladder, down to the airlock chamber that was at the back of the ship.

10 seconds remaining.......

Seeing the security door shut, Randa desperitly began to hack it open as fast as she never thought possible.

5.....

The door shot upward, and the way was clear to the chamber.

4.....

3.....

She reached the airlock, slammed the door close and waited for the other lock that lead into her ship to open.....

2.....

1.....

On Rubi-Ka, the explosion looked like a faint star, hovering just over the horizon of the clear night. It flashed briefly for a second, then slowly fainted from all view......
