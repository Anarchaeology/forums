The Shepherds

Objective: A group of clanners who dedicated to finding the Lost Souls of the Rubi-ka and helping them to achieve lasting peace for selves.

Description: A guild of people who help each other and other clanners. The Shepherds believe that the tyranny of Omni-tek corporation is responsive for the problems of Rubi-ka. The shepherds know that in the end, lasting peace will only comes from a political agreement. Shepherds will support the clans in the struggle, however they hope for peace.

Rules and principles.

1 Shepherds help each other
2 Shepherds never charge or accept donations for buffs from a clanner (they can refuse to buff a non-shepherd).
3 Shepherds never attack clanners unless both sides agree to fight.
4 Attacking Neutrals including npcs is not in the spirit of the Shepherds.
5 Shepherds are expected to help newbies.
6 Any non-immediate decisions will be voted on at meeting of general membership.
7 Applicant can vote at meeting but a majority of members and higher is sufficient to carry a vote
8 A shepherd member or higher can only be voted out by a majority of the active members.
9 An applicant can be kicked by an advisor or higher.
10 Prejudice of any kind is not permitted.
11 Roleplaying is encouraged
12 PvP is not required

Organization

A democracy.

-A president
-Advisors (3-6 is preferred)
-Veterans (any person who has been in The Shepherds for 3month and been made a member. Or any member who demonstrates that deserving the rank and who has been a member at least a month)
-Members (An applicant will be promoted an Advisor. Veterans and members are e encouraged to remind Advisors that an applicant is worth of full membership when that person has show that he is capable of membership. After a month a applicant should be a member or kicked out)

Alts of members and higher are automatically made members. But cannot achieve higher rank. Only one Alt per player can access the stashes for items. Item which are for sale in shops or below ql75 can be given to Alts. Members are expected give any item not for sale in shops to the stashes.

Send tell to Asal, Armana or Makir
