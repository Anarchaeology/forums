Allow players to label their inventory and bank packs
The most annoying thing about packs that everyone uses in their inventory and banks is that they aren't labeled. There are times that it seems like I have 100+ packs in my bank and some in my inventory to stuff junk I find. What I wouldn't give to be able to label each pack I have in addition to the little icon (implants, clothes, nanos, etc., etc.) to whatever short text label I choose. Even the limit of 8 characters to label a pack would be helpful, at least allow enough text so we players can differentiate between all the similar looking packs when we have dozens of them.

Yes, I know that. But what about items that don't correspond to vehicles, implants, etc. I'm not talking about being able to hover the mouse over and see the pack's label, I'm talking about having a short text label right on top of the pack that you can just glance quickly at and know exactly what is in them without having to open it to remind yourself.

For example, I have one pack loaded with Advanced Nano Rechargers and one loaded with HQ Health Treatment Laboratorys, both of which are too high ql for me to use at the moment. Yes, I know there is a Large Backpack - Medical that one could store them in, but how would I tell what pack has what in it without dragging them both out of the bank and opening them up just to check if I forgot which one had what in it when all I wanted to do was put one set of Advanced Nano Rechargers in one pack for the future? With one short text label on the pack, without having to move the mouse over the pack to check the pack's label which is always the same mind you, I could tell instantly which pack was what.
