You can't get stuff above ql 125 in shops. (with exception of clusters?)
if you want items above that ql, you must either:

1: buy it from another player
2: do a ql 125+ mission for that item, and use it yourself, or sell it to another player.

If you simply want information about a certain item, go to www.antiguardians.com They have a nice database of the equipment available.
(note: not all equipment in the database is available to players. Not all equipment are in the game at all)
