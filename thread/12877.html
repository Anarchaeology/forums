As far as the bots being lost due to crashes: while the ideas sound good, but I think the games development side of this is probably alot more difficult than it seems. The timer for recon after ld would have to be a different timer than the recon after death and it does not appear that the bot object knows anything more than MASTER_IN_ZONE=FALSE therefore start TERMINATE_TIMER. So I doubt this is gonna happen anytime soon.

In reference to the carry bots in the Yalmaha thing: I have never lost a pet during yalmaha transit. The only way I can see this happening is if you have the pet on guard and you are flying low enough to get agg on yourself which would cause the bot to attach (although there would be no battle as long as you remained morphed in the yalm. Whenever I travel in my Yalm with an active pet, I put the pet on follow. The pet will never agg that way and the pet is never the recipient of gaurd attacks.

Pet loosing buffs/trims on zone: Maybe MP pets are different, but my pets don't loose buffs on zone. Proof of this comes from damage shields. If I buff the bot with a damage shield, it begins to glow. Not only will this glowing persist if I take him in and out of the missions several times, but the damage chat that the shield causes will also still show up. However, I have always like the reshell idea as a way to save pets when we log. If I log with mochams, when I come back on, mochams is still there. Actually, storing the bot should be an automatic part of the /camp process, but reshell would do, unless of course it is still a skill requiring shell like the one when we first build it, in which case, whats the use. Yeah, nevermind, scratch that...storing the bot should be an automatic part of the /camp process...this still doesnot solve the crash senario.

Pet disappears when leaving missions: same solution. When leaving missions, always put the pet on follow, particularily if the mission is complete and you know longer have a key. Sometimes, when the pet is on guard or if the pet is still in battle with an MOB (even if you have finished the mission and recieve the reward) the pet will not follow you out the door and is lost cause you can't get back in. As a matter of fact, even with the pet on follow, if it is being attacked, it will occasionally turn around and return blows. To be on the safe side make sure the pet is in the entry room to a mission with you AND on follow before you go though the door.
