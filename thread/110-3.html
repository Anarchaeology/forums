a rehash, with some nutritious additives (long)
I've been playing other games for the past few months due to some real disappointment with how AO turned out, but I decided to give it a try again recently (not due to the free month thing - I paid for 6 months from signup unfortunately) and was immediately struck with how some things have changed for everyone else but it's about the same for me.

I'm inserting a lot of my views on the issues into this, having spent quite a bit of time in these sort of games, considering these sort of situations.. so this post is as much for fixers who might read this as for Funcom.

Class type issues:

First, I found missions extremely difficult.. for some reason mobs that are the same race and class as PCs have 2-3(sometimes 4)x as many HP, but do less damage. Some kind of balance issue I never understood. It is bad enough that NPCs seem to have max ALL skills. If PCs are to be represented as the heros/action type/villain/whatever people in this game, with good equipment (as I know we all strive for the best) we should be able to take out a yellow mob fairly easily. Not really fixer specific.

Our weapons. SMGs have some sort of definition problem in AO at the moment. A submachinegun is generally a short range, not very accurate, mid damage, but -extremely- rapid firing weapon with moderate clip capacity. I do see most SMGs as being not very damaging to armored targets, as some have noted (because of the Proj damage type.) SMGs at current, even the fastest, fire no faster than a semiauto. Oddly enough, other weapons, such as sniper rifles, pistols, and shotguns quite unbelievably fire faster than most submachineguns.

A black market channel.. This would be helpful, and I don't think it would damage other aspects of the game. Perhaps some sort of bot that sold random rarish items could be added to the channel.

I have no love for the idea of a shop within the grid, though I might like to see enhanced fixer usage of existing shop facilities, such as discount hacking, etc.

Fixer cell scanner. In some ways, I've always thought, death is both better and worse in AO. If you know you're going to die somewhere you can scan first, and lose very little. But there are times where you die unexpectedly, and can lose 95% of a level (has happened to me two or three times.) I always thought a more elegant solution to this would be to lose all xp since last scan, or say 15% of level, whichever's lower. Perhaps the Fixer with a new class ability could be used to facilitate this?

Fixer only grid exits, this has been visited and revisited on the forums. I'm not really for or against this, but what I would like to see is one post's suggestion about being able to -enter- the grid via shopping terminals and so forth. IE Fixer only grid Entrances, not Exits. I suggest the use of all those computer terminals in missions, in the hallway in your apartment.. Make a special exit so you can go directly to your apartment. If containers in apartments would be made to hold items this could fit very nicely into what I envisioned as a gun runner/fence profession.

Also the ability for a fixer to use enemy shops would be useful, but may open the door to some exploitation.

NCU Deck slot items we can use. There seem to be great deck slot items for docs/NTs/etc. But nothing for us. Seems odd, given the fixer's technological affinity. An antivirus device that increases nano resistance or actively counters running hostile nanoprograms comes to mind.

Missions that use our skills. Concealment needs to work again. Or we need to be better at it. All (self) buffed and cloaked, I still need to stay 5+ meters from a yellow mob to avoid detection, nevermind passing through the same room. Also mentioned was the enthralling idea of having some sort of search and destroy (virus removal) mission... a hidden mob in a large grid-dungeon with a time limit would make use of both our exceptional run buffs and our high perception. This would not work if the dungeon was populated with like level mobs though, see our poor fighting ability for details.

On to skills:

The reqs on our nanos are vicious. Most class nanos need 2 skills, 1 maybe 10-20 points higher than the other. Ours seem to have the habit of requiring 3 skills, with the high one being 80+ higher..

Factor into that equation that all of our nano skills are dark blue. Yes I know every single post lists this as a problem, but truly I could do without fixers being good at any tradeskills at all to get my nanos just a shade lighter.

Assuming IP cost on nano skills is ever changed, will we get the IP back? Or a skill raise to use the points we've already invested more effectively? I nightmarishly consider that my points might be gone forever, even if a fix does come..

As a specialist in B&E and Bomb Disarm, I would like more of a reason for groups to take me along on those hard missions. More types of traps could be an interesting addition, or more difficult locks.

Full Auto is a skill that is both overpriced and underused by our profession. Around level 50 I had desperately pumped points into FA in a vain attempt to find a gun other than the mausser to use. The Automatica2 came close to being a nice gun at higher QL, but the IP expenditure of FA along with the brutality of nano investments made it impossible to keep FA up. Also, the FA requirements on almost all SMGs are outlandishly high (as high as the SMG skillreq.)

Finally, Nanos:

It's been widely lauded that the farm has been given away to the eng, doc, and NT crowd with the teleport, etc. nanos.. but nobody said we were the exclusive lords of the grid or anything of that sort. This should be viewed as an option of having a fixer in the group, not a primary reason to have a fixer in the group.

I'm of the opinion that we should get an upgrade to our Minor Suppressor buff at some point, which I still use religiously along with the Expertise nanos for what little edge it may give me.

Also, I see no reason why Traders get all the complit buffs, when we are supposed to be hacker-types.

Mark/Recall. If we are truly masters of travel, we should be able to mark locales and teleport ourselves (mid level) or our entire group (high level) back to said location. This might be a pain to engineer on the backend, however.

Our summon nanos need to do something useful. To borrow a quote from a friend,
"I know there's been many a time when I've been out hunting in groups when I ran out of ammo and wished there was a fixer there that could slowly summon me more ammo at random instead of flying across the zone and back in my airplane in 3 minutes to buy more."
If we are to have ammo summoning, we should be able to a) pick which type and b) summon it in significant amounts. Also, these skills can be engineered via description (for RP purposes) to use MattMet skill instead of MattCrea by describing them as assembling the necessary components from nearby materials. Cheesy, but functional. As it stands I am positive I -will not- invest in MattCrea to get these abilities.

On a side note, new nano additions should be made available on vendors or in some other fashion to players who are high enough level that they have pretty much no chance of getting them in missions.


It was mentioned that we should be considered for pet summoning ability.. I think there is quite enough of this ingame for other classes, and don't believe it fits our role very well.

Lastly was mentioned a Death hologram (feign death) sort of ability.. Considering our difficulty soloing and our emphasis on spying skills, this seems like it could be beneficial to us.


That's all I really have, and admittedly only a few of those ideas I consider mine. Fixers could surely benefit by getting probably around half or more of the points I've listed, but as in all things, moderation. Having us overpowered would just be grounds for a future nerf.

P.S. Please make any additional SMGs make some sound other than that godforsaken 'zap, zap' that the newbie gun and mausser have made for my entire career.

Let the Fixers be Fixers
Up to this point in time, I think we all agree that some of 'what defines a Fixer' is strongly flavored by the various subjective emotions one developes in the process of playing a broken class for some time...

examples:

"Hey... i cant deal damage like i would like to - i think that Fixers should be able to do that, since we are not really Nano-Freaks either (and never will be)..."

"I should be able to Hack this-and-that... afterall im a Hacker type of person... and im shady and all..."

Up to this point tho; all that defined us as _Fixers_ was our ability to *roleplay* the shady 'dont-come-over-my-turf' thug, with Mission blitzing being our way to "reach items and places far away from our current location" ... If you bought a QL XX Item of some sort in the last few weeks, chances are, that it was originally obtained by a Fixer.

(or a Meta for that matter, but Metas take alot longer to 'get the reward', since they are playing thru the whole mission. advantage: token; disadvantage: takes several hours to get the rewards)

When Prices on RK1 went to hell, I stopped Blitzing missions. Not worth my time anymore - but even if i still was blitzing; i think i could actually gain some handsome pile of cash in the process...

Now tho, with the implementation of B&E being actually useful, i see our strenghts appearing on the _for Fixers intended side_;
You need a B&E Specialist? Get a Fixer. We are the 'ruff boys' - sneaky to an extent ...

"Think your way in, shoot your way out" - i like that idea (as far as its possible to actually 'live' that in-game as it stands today..)

The Fixer is kind of a Thief / Rogue Character - nobody started being a Fixer for "being uber" - well if you did; this is where your career ends.
Fixers are supposed to be the kind of 'specialist' you see in many a CyberPunk(tm) / ShadowRun(tm) environment - you have a bunch of muscle as insertion team - getting the Fixer/Hacker/Decker on site in one piece; and guarding him while he's doing his Mojo - soon as hes done, the Fixer/Hacker/Decker switches his role - he whoops out his SMGs and gets the HELL out of the run, ready to cover his own butt while his 'muscle' extract him. Mission accomplished.

I think that AO resembles that kind of Roleplaying idea slowly but surely...
Being hired for a certain 'run' that a Team of Muscle couldnt accomplish by themselves is what the Fixer class is all about in my eyes - if you disagree, be Muscle - Soldier / Enforcer / Martial Artist -

The Fixer is a Professional - not designed to compete in the same league as Soldiers for Damage; no match for a Nano-Freak in terms of Mojo; and rather using his 'toolbox' to get into the killzone or get an item than building a Bot, or a new weapon...

I dont remember where i saw it, but there was actually a post about 'what is up with locks in missions' and I found myself ROtFL when i read a post by a NON_FIXER who was saying something like "B&E is a DARK BLUE SKILL FOR ME - how am i supposed to open chests????"....

FunCom, Cosmik, Cz :

RAISE/adjust Chest difficulty. Thats a Fixers job.
Comparable to the Adventurers (no morphing indoors makes them the kind of person you would want to have around on your 'outdoor hunting runs')
the Fixer is a Specialist in an Indoors environment - Missions are our Speciality.

A soldier who is able to open a Chest in a QL 200 mission doesn't make sense to me. Even if they brought their skill in B&E up to 230something points.

Yeah i know you hate me for saying that - but I grew weary of begging for Soldier like power, and NT like Nano skill-colors.
I want to be a Fixer.

- Raik out
