Dungeon idea
I had an idea for a new dungeon. Hopefully this will be harder for people to camp over and over. Making the
items/monsters inside actually a rare or unique item(s). The new static dungeons are great but its like there
comercialized. There should be somthing on Rubi-Ka that can remain mysterious and not be comercialized.

Basically and ancient lost city inside of Rubi-Ka. A series of different rooms connected only by teleports,
with a main (largest) room that would be the main portion of the city. The entrance of the dungeon would move
from place to place over the face of Rubi-Ka in some random fashion. The doorway to the dungeon must remain
moving so as to keep the location from being camped thus ruining the "Lost" theme. The doorway could be some
object that a person would have to right click on to enter. In order for this not to be a campground the doorway
could not be in one spot for more than a few minutes. At that, the only way for a group of people to go inside
would be to have a group see the entrance appear or an engineer would have to see it and use the beacon nano
to get his group there. For the most part I only see Solo individuals finding the doorway, sometimes groups.

In this way the mystery could be kept better than a permanent front door with coordinate address. This would
also mean that the dungeon might not be seen by anyone for weeks. This would make the items found inside very
unique.
