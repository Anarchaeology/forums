Enough With The Eat Flag
There comes a time in all of our lives where we have to admit we were wrong. We did a nono and we must rectify the situation.

You all know what I'm talking about. I'm talking about those Rusted urns and their damned EAT flag and this is something I'm actually pretty upset about.

Now I will be the first person to say that the changes to the pet were absolutely frelling awesome. Putting the melee damage back in was the right thing to do. The pet has no evades, no AC and less HP than the cacodemon, which is EXACTLY what balances it. All offense, almost no defense. To me, it is the perfect attack pet and truly one of the best thing that were ever added for any class ever. It's balanced.

THIS IS WHY THE EAT FLAG NEEDS TO BE REMOVED.

You cannot throw a delicious snack to the starving masses and then tell them "this isn't going to be an everyday thing". Uh-uh. I think we've suffered at the bottom of the heap for long enough. We finally get something that I would consider to be an *excellent* addition to our class, yet has the WORST downside I ever have seen EVER, for any profession, any game, ever.

One hand giveth and the other taketh away.

Camping day after day for our pets is absolutely absurd and is the biggest example of a forced timesink that I have ever seen.

1) It's not fun. I would seriously like one of the developers to spend a day with me in their god forsaken three room dungeon to prove this. This is a video game, meant for enjoyment, relaxation and fun. Camping the tiny Inner Sanctum is flat out mind numbing.

2) Teaming is difficult. It's hard to gather a team or two that already has permanent kewys to go up to the third floor. Not to mention the fact that nobody will feel like going to Inner Sanctum for the rest of their lives. They have no reason to return once they get their loot. The XP is great, but there's no tokens and no jobeclusters. Not to mention the chances are that they will all eventually die on the third level regardless, losing all of their gained XP. So even if we can get a team to go to IS, nobody will want to camp the third floor.

3) Droprate is low. That's the biggest slap in the face. The mobs are hard enough to make urn camping prohibitive as it is, but then we get slapped twice by making the droprate absolutely abysmal. Although it still will be far from perfect, change the droprate to 100% on the third floor perceptors and dominus' mobs. Two of them are soloable and a high level MP could still solo farm for his or her urns if nothing else. Still boring, still pointless, but a bit more fruitful.

4) EAT flag has to go. That's simply the only "real" fix. Balance it by making the urns NODROP once more, and remove the EAT flag from the charged urns altogether. After the pet is casted, the charged urn should turn back into a regular urn. It would be balanced because the pet takes such an incredible amount of time to cast. We can't exactly keep spitting these things out.


Bottom line is, we deserve to have something nice like this, much the same as fixers deserved their boost to grid armor.

This is a simple fix as far as I can tell. Make us camp it once, but don't make us camp it every day. This is a cruel and horrid thing to have to go through. It sucks up our gametime and prevents us from doing the things we'd much rather be doing, such as enjoying our limited gametime.

I'm in a giant guild with hundreds of people and I can barely get these things. What chance does a casual player have and do you consider that fair?

This is a plea from the MP community: Tell us why the EAT flag is there! I would not consider this to be an overpowering item. Our other attack pets will not be improved upon, so this is really all we have. Please fix this immediately, as right now this is the biggest issue facing our class. Mezz pet is second....

I spent hours in the Inner Sanctum dungeon tonight, as I do every night and only saw one urn drop. I told my friend Lorahxi to take it, as I still have a few left and I saw she was using a 160 pet.

I'll be posting an event on the player event calendar where we can all go together and camp urns for members of smaller guilds that would otherwise never have an urn of their own. While my supply is low, I will not take any urns during this excursion. I feel bad for all of us that we have to go through this, but I feel worse for MPs in smaller guilds that lack the manpower and resources to camp :/

Hang tight guys, maybe we'll be shown some pity here :/
