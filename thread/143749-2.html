Proposal for Metaphysic Transference
==========================

Here's a proposal for another defensive measures idea for the MP.
  • Emotional Void ql 100
    nano cost: 2000 nano point
    requirement:
    title lvl 4
    T&S:500
    MM:500
    PM:500
    attack: 2 sec.
    recharge: 20 sec.
    duration: 30 sec.
    effect:
    add concelament of 400
    add all evade 400
    add nanoresist 400
    run speed -2000
    and stun for 25 sec.

    Description: With great self-control the MP is able to empty his mind completely of emotions... through the power of meditation the MP is able to phase himself onto a metaphysical plane. Any movement, command or action other than using conceal will break his meditation.

    Explanation: Even when the meditation is broken his state of semi-meditation cause his slowness.

    Emotional Oblivion ql 150

    nano cost: 3500 nano point
    requirement:
    title lvl 5
    T&S:800
    MM:800
    PM:800
    attack: 3 sec.
    recharge: 20 sec.
    duration: 35 sec.
    effect:
    add concealement 700
    add all evade 700
    add nanoresist 700
    run speed -2000
    and stun for 25 sec.

    Description: With great self-control the MP is able to empty his mind completely of emotions... through the power of meditation the MP is able to phase himself onto a metaphysical plane. Any movement or action other than using conceal will break his meditation.

    Explanation: in this version the more powerful MP is able to control his pets during the effect.

    Emotional Black Hole ql 190

    nano cost: 4000 nano point
    requirement:
    title lvl 6
    T&S:1000
    MM:1000
    PM:1000
    attack: 4 sec.
    recharge: 20 sec.
    duration: 45 sec.
    effect:
    add concealment 1000
    add nanoresist 1000
    add all evade 1000
    run speed -2000
    and stun for 20 sec.

    Description: With great self-control the MP is able to empty his mind completely of emotions... through the power of meditation the MP is able to phase himself onto a metaphysical plane. Any movement other than using conceal will break his meditation.

    Explanation: At the highest levels of concentration the MP is able to command the pets and also cast nanos during the effect.







This proposal was designed in the MP Wishlist: Metaphyisic Transparence Thread . For more information about how the design was reached please reference that thread. To propose changes/additions to this design - please post to that thread.
