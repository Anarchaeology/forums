Projected 14.6 Changes
I have gathered complaints of every profession from the various profession boards and projected some 14.6 patch notes assuming FC listened to the complaints. Enjoy.

Adventurers
Added several new adventurer-only weapons. These weapons allow adventurers to do more damage than any other profession.

Soldiers
Added several new soldier-only weapons. These weapons allow soldiers to do more damage than any other profession.

Enforcers
Added several new enforcer-only weapons. These weapons allow enforcers to do more damage than any other profession.

Fixers
Added several new fixer-only weapons. These weapons allow fixers to do more damage than any other profession.

Agents
Added several new agent-only weapons. These weapons allow agents to do more damage than any other profession.

Martial Artists
No changes.

Nano Technicians
Improved nukes. Nano Technicians should now do more damage than any other profession.

Traders
What traders?

Doctors
No changes.

Bureaucrats
Removed psychic restriction on charms. Now bureaucrats should be able to charm level 300 mobs and henceforth do more damage than any other profession.

Meta-Physicist
We thought about fixing pet pathing, but we decided that any meta-phsyicist who put up with it this long can wait another few months. Sorry.

Engineers
Pets now attack their owners if the engineers fail to control their pets and let them break calm. Thanks for the idea, Paxton.

Pet Pathing
The following items happened within the past 3 days;
- Pet turning in circles after being told to attack mob. Mob standing stil immediately next to myself and dancing slayer. (Solo mission.. Cost me 1.7 mil XP)
- When being told to attack mob in room, pet exits room and enters neighboring room.
- When told to attack mod in same room, pet leaves room, circles in next room (distrubing other mobs) and returns for attack.
- When told to attack mob chasing a team mate, bot gets stuck on table until team mate dies when bot finally breaks free of table and shows up to save the day..hehe
- When told to attack mob in same room, bot leaves room, turns to come back and is stuck against the outer wall of said room.

ALL of these happened in the last 3 days. Do any of them qualify as a pet pathing problem?

There also seems to be a new pathing problem that has similar characteristics to pet pathing...
If a pet is stuck against something in follow mode, one can "Pull" him through it by walking away from him. This includes walls and other objects.
Recently in a solo mission, a mob on the otherside of a wall aggroed me and shot directly though a wall to get me in a manner quite similar to the way pets will shoot through walls to keepup. I first saw this directly after the last big patch when 3 probes came through the walls from neighboring rooms on one of my lower chars. Most recently it happened 2 days ago. I have to wonder if mobs arent getting some of the pet pathing attributes.

Lastly is invisible pets and teammates, Pets mysteriously turning into monsters and pets vanishing without a trace or reason all together.

Invisible pet...
The pet (or team mate) is not visible to some or all members of the team yet responds. Doors do not open as the pet passes thourhg them bot mobs are attacked and take damage. This can happen just about anywhere.. no zoning is required, and although zoning does seem to fix it sometimes, other times it just fixes itself.

Pets into monsters....
This was a cute one. The pet was visible to all.. followed me as normal, but when commands where issued, 'You have no pet" was the reply. Others could see the pet but their monster map upgrade showed it as a red dot. The pet was never released. Apon zoning all was back to normal. This happened when entering the boss room.. pet never followed.
I Abondoned the pet and exited the mission where I found him again, normal and quite obedient.

Pet vanishes alltogether...
Unlike the invisible pet, this time the pet is actually gone. Issueing commands results in "You have no pet". During my last occurance, I was in the middle of a boss fight.. pet in good health and poof.. gone. I created a new pet, finished the job and apon returning to BS grid from being gridded out of the misison, I had my new pet in tow and heard my old pet die in the water next to the Grid entrance.

My personal all time fav pathing fun.. Your in the start room.. just killed a mob and back to the entrance wall as much as is possible without exiting. Issue a pet Behind command.. pet leaves start room.. traverses main room to adjacent mob room.. if your lucky he turns and comes back into he start room.. if not lucky, he's attacked and you hope he dies out there.

I have reported these things and been told they're being worked on.. they're saying they where fixed.. color me confused.
