The G.I. Joe form of RolePlaying (ths got way off subject)
There really is no "problem" with RP. I have posted here before saying there was a lack of, or it was almost non existant. I was wrong and I will admit that. Instead of putting my foot in my mouth and ranting about stuff i want in the game I am more mature now, and more civil.

I would like to suggest some ideas now that may further enhanse one's roleplaying experience. (excuse my spelling) I know there is PvP that goes on, but the role-playing behind this PvP is simple. Players get mixed up in OOC problems, or something goes wrong inside Reets, or Rompas or names any place, shortly after a duel is issued. While there is nothing wrong with this, with a little more creativity, these few parties could actually instead walk away from this innocdent and actually setup "a hit" where they would go and actually jump the other player.

Stop! I know what you all are thinking. "To do that we'd have to stalk them until they went into a PvP zone or trick them somehow." This is not true. If properly role-played, both parties can get together and discuss a story that would unfold. SO instead of hearing, "I kill j00 nubie!" "No, I RoxXOr j00!", inside Rompas we could instead hear something like an insult followed by "Oh, we shall see my friend, we shall see..." When in reality side A has already spoken with side B and setup a fight date/time which will take place later on.

After all this happens a story can be written about it, these people can draw their own guilds into it. Inner guild wars can break out. People can talk about something besides the lack of story line.

This is a huge world. I am sure everyone in the US doesn't talk about what is going on in the rest of the world, or even people from one state talking about what is always going on in the other states. Same goes for the rest of the world talking about what goes on in the US. (This was just used as an example since I am from the US.) So why should we all focus on the main story line of Phillip Ross, and Henry Radiman?

Right away people are going to read this and say, "Ah what a bunch of crap!" (That is if people care at all) One thing in life I am learning is that if you want something done you gotta do it yourself. Also nothing comes right away, everything comes in time and that if you TRUELY want something to hapen... you can't give up and you must keep working at it. Basically I discovered that if you really do build it, people really come!

Now, I want to add a remark about Gridstream Productions in here. My reasons for this are simple.
#1. I like popularity too, so I hope they mention me during their shows.
#2. (The a$$ kissing to get on the show) They are a good example of staying consistant with something and making it work.
Well dispite the good things there are some bad things about Gridstream as well. However, I personally do not feel the need to bring these to attention because they are minor issues, and we all know that the world is a better place with Gridstream then without. Besides I do not want to be dark or bitter... well maybe just a little bitter. (Sorry had to say it)

People like PvP, people like the story, but why do we always have to follow funcom? Not to dis Funcom, but seriously, if the Funcom pulled out of this game, what would be left? DAoC? *shudders* We do not take advantage of the resources given to us. Long ago I had thought of using helpbot to setup a craps-like game I had run in UO using their dice available in UO to roll. I was told not to do this by ARK, but when I saw Kasino start it... I was a little upset. Now I see they not only run craps, but many other games as well. Thus taking it way beyond my simple idea that I NEVER did, or even tried.

Again I learned a few things from this. I learned that if you don't try anything your holding yourself back from what could be greatness. Also I learned that If you do try something, maybe someone else will come by and blow your ass out of the water with newer better and a hell of a lot more awesome ideas. And if they do, so be it, it's better for the game overall that way.

In reality this is a game. It's not a game like Monopoly, it's not a game like Doom, or Half-Life. There no end to this game. Things you do effect your character for the rest of the duration of the game, which again is almost never ending. Anyone that has ever played a non-hack & slash pen and paper game knows that sometimes, you gotta be the bad guy. If you step away OOC though, it ends there. Get together OOC, write storys for your guild, setup joint events with other guilds... I great place for these events that will eventually break out into wars would be at parties. Now I do not mean shouting at each other so the whole party starts spamming the place, that's bad, I just mean Pre-script an argument, act in out, and begin some stories from there. Some of this is done now, but I would love to see more of it being done... oh well sorry for the long boring post. I hope you all read this. Feel free to rant on it, or flame it, or compliment on it :P I'm always open to constructive opinions.
