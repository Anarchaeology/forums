Secret changes in Sunday downtime?
Am I on crack or has the mob aggro range along with the spawn rate in certain areas been dramatically increased?

Had a mission in TLR last night and noticed there were a TON of mobs in the forest before the ruins where the trash king is. Thought hmm thats interensting never seen that before. Had another mission out at the tooth in agean what was this? 3 vultures? Too low level for me to hunt now but 3? Seen 2 before but never 3. A low level MP was having a good time there though at any rate. Just a couple of things that seemed odd for spawn rate things. I was not the only one, one of my guild members said that he had observed the same thing in other areas before I even thought to mention my observations.

Aggro seems to be much more intense and possible at much longer ranged. Pre sunday I had yet to be aggroed by a mob that was out of view range. One exception were the Bioswarm protectors, if you have been there you know what I am talking about. But last night I was in broken shores and a offle worm went aggro from what seemed to be an incredible distance. I have very high perception and when I got the aggro it several seconds before I could target, and even longer before he was in attack range. This was not the only instance I noticed. Specifically this pertains to outdoors, as we all know how out of no where the entire contents of a mission can be up your arse in a matter of seconds from every direction. Just funny these two things that seemed to have changed a bit. I play enough to now when things are a bit out of norm and this seems to qualify.

Wondering if any others have noticed oddities after Sunday's so called "Hardware Upgrade"?
