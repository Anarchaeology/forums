If you're clan....
For those levels, the best places would be the flea pit, the crater, and the oasis.

Flea pit, level 12-15 group should go ok, fleas will be dark red

Crater, similar to flea pit

Oasis, can go there as low as lvl 10, but need to have a full team that know what they are doing

To get to the Flea pit and crater, leave the west gate or tir and follow the road. There will be a dirt road that heads off to the south, it leads to the crater. Continue along the main road until you reach the oupost, the flea pit is directly south of the OP.

As for the oasis, head out of the north gate in Newland. Go east until you zone. After you zone head north east, you'll soon see it, it's not too far.

Good luck, and give me a /tell if your looking for a doc.

another place i want to suggest is bot hill. take the western tir gate and head north until you zone. go ahead walking north and you will arrive at bot hill, lot's of bots around lvl 25+ there, but take care, they are aggro. there's also a little outpost with a shop and a scanner nearby. if you choose to hunt there, be aware of a bot called "living metal". he looks like all the others there but he's a quest mob and above lvl 110 i think. he isn't aggro by himself, but if you attack other bots nearby, he will become aggro
