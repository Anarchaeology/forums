Not having people to gank is a direct result of ganking itself. I have always wanted to pvp, did for a bit before 75 and occasionally since then. I think the reason I haven't given it up is because my first pvp fight was a very close loss and there were no gankers around. Since then I've won some, lost more, but there are those times when you do duel someone and its a clean fight and even if I lose, I'm not bitter about it.

Fact is that people come in the arena the first time and get 3 shot killed or ganked or something to that effect. Maybe they try again, but they always just end up pissed off. If someone is out ganking people for titles eventually they will run out of target cause a lot of the time the ganker is turning a potential pvper against it. How many times have we seen people come on this forum and go off about being ganked or rooted in a yalm or whatever. Sure, that's a valid "tactic", but that still turns people against the pvp aspect of this game. Of course, 2ho and mmd and those areas like them are a free for all, but a guy can't even go in the arena and have a fight with his friend. Do you really expect people to just keep coming to the arenas to get shot in the back or what have you?

Yesterday, I was in the arena and had a duel with an adv. We go in, we fight it out and I win with about 30% of my hp left. Then Gravyomlet uncloaks and attacks. Of course, I lost, and he was omni. Technically there is nothing wrong with that, but if I was new to the whole pvp thing, that alone would have probably turned me against it. I sent him a tell asking if he'd like to have a duel and no reply, asked again, same thing. People can go on about this isn't about being honorable and all that, but if you want to fight people and get new people into pvp, you can't be so sorry about things.

In my opinion, titles are the cause of a lot of it. I'm sure when FC implemented the title system, they wanted a way for those good at fighting to have a badge of sorts. The way it is now, for the most part, its who can die the least and run around shooting people in the back while they're fighting someone else.

I roled an enforcer to mess around with a bit when I get tired of having to get millions of xp to level my trader. When I talked to the true noobs, a lot of them say the same thing " I would like to pvp, but I'm not all twinked up" or " I went to the arena or heard about how people just get ganked all the time".

Let me be clear that I'm not making an argument against ganking. It's a part of the game, but it is also a big part of why more people don't pvp.
