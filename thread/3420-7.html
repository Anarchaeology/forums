Doc's Perspective
You must take into account the date the origional post was made and the level of the doctor. I have played a doctor as my primary char since Day 1 of release, and I am level 174 at this point. When i was a newbie i was more strapped for cash then anybody i knew. I could NOT afford to buy nano kits, much less a new nano formula. I would actually LOSE money by grouping in expenses. Many days i would do nothing but hunt and i would loot anything in sight, but still by the end of the day i would be more poor then when i set out. Not only would i have less credits, my formulas would be all outdated after just 3 levels making me an inferior doctor to my skills. Luckily i had made friends in my teams and they would buy nano kits for me since they are terribly expensive for a doc. No other profession uses as much nano kits as a doctor. The doctor by far has the highest amount of expenses to keep running, including nano formulas compared to any profession. Many times i was asked to join a team but had to decline because i was low on nano kits and i had to go run missions to get more. Sometimes the group would pitch in so i could afford to team up with them and lose the money to expenses.

Now its a different story. Once a friend gave me a yalmaha i could actually make enough money to laugh at my expenses of formulas and nano kits. I would no longer be forced to spending a day to recover my credits to prepare for the coming days grouping. And of course once you hit level 125 or so to finally self cast the +80 treatment your money troubles are over. I am now a very very rich doctor with over 250 million credits, but it was not long ago that i had to loot every monster part for its 8 cred and ask for other people in the group to give me a pack of nano kits.

I dont know when the change occured, but the frequency of mobs dropping loot and the quality of the loot has increased dramatically since the early days of AO. Its now a common sight to see a gem or bar of gold drop, when before it would be a joyous occasion worth noting. But now the credits are out there and they should not be capped in any way. Its much easier for newbies now to make the credits they need to survive as it should be, but now there is to many credits out there in the high game.


Sorak - Level 174 Clan Doctor

Same in every MMORPG
Economy issues occur with every MMORPG, and will continue with every one in the future.

In every one we have played so far, we have been dirt poor in the beginning, barely able to buy proper armor/weapons/spells for our level. But eventually you progress to the point where the money curve slopes abruptly upward and instead of worrying about how you will afford item x to survive, you chuckle at your horde of whatever currency there is or do stupid things with it, give it away, twink, etc.

I just wish games would smooth the money curve out. I doubt any game makers put much thought into the game's long term economy because many we've seen are messed beyond belief. DAoC has done somewhat well with requiring expensive door repairs and seige equipment, but there is still A LOT of money in the high level DAoC game and precious little in the beginning.

However being that I have yet to see a perfect real life economy, I'm not holding my breath for a perfect one in a game. :-) After all there are the haves and have-nots in real life, and it's for a variety of reasons: luck, hard work, inheritence, greed, etc. It's all about perspective - you can be poor and happy with it, or rich and miserable too. :-)

Another doc chiming in
I have a lvl 73 Doc who is my main character. I find it very, very difficult to make money.
Mayombe is maximized for team play as a healing machine.
She's a nanomage, using a Q58 Vek as her weapon (costs too much IP to max out shotgun). At 73 most of her implants are around Q50 because implants are damn expensive and she can't afford the Q90s she's capable of wearing. Likewise, she could wear Q90 int/psych armors but wears mostly Q60-70 because it's what she's been able to acquire. She keeps her healing and health buff nanos as up to date as possible. Her poisons and debuffs are not up to date, due primarily to the high expense. Missions at 50% are possible, but usually take 2 hrs and the cost of nanochargers pretty much cancels the profits. Maybe this has changed now, as I hear that kits are appearing more frequently in chests. Still, 2 hrs for a single mission reward is not high earnings. I've experimented with lower level missions, but with mostly greys there's no xp and the loot is low value. The one hugh unexpected bonus I got from this experiment was finding a Q35 Yalm in a chest inside a 25% diff mission. But I think I was incredibly lucky to have that happen...I've talked to many who have played much, much more hours than I and have never found a Yalm in a mission, regardless of difficulty.
I do go thru a crazy amount of nano keeping teams alive. Many don't realize it, but docs may refill their nano pools 2 or 3 times in a long fight. At my level, try adding up the cost of those Q90 rechargers...you quickly see the doc is the most expensive class to maintain. Most expense *AND* among the owest earning potential is what creates the difficult situation.
I've been told I should charge teams for my services. I think this is totally inappropriate. Should they tip the tank or the puller for doing their jobs? Of course not! Then why should they be asked to pay for healing?
I never ask for tips. Rarely, they have been offered and I'm always very grateful for this help. Most teams, not only are no tips offered but members rush to loot the chests while I'm still healing everyone up and I wind up shorted on loot. Sucks when that happens...I try to take it in stride, and just remember not to team with those people next time they're asking for a doc.
So anyway, that's my experience. Money is much harder to come by and I go through more of it during normal play than any other char.
There are a couple things FC could do, separately or in combination, which would help immensely:
1) Make Biocommutor doc-only. This would make our service for converting parts essential and give us better ability to earn tips.
2) Make Omni-Med suits doc-only. This would create a market for our Treatment buffs, again giving us something marketable.
3) Lower the cost of stim and treatment kit components dramatically, and/or have them drop in loot or mission rewards. I think there would be a terrific market for these things if the cost of making them was not absolutely ridiculous compared to the usefulness of the stim/kit you get from the parts. Occasionally I hear that they do drop as loot but in 73 levels I have *never* seen a pharmatech tradeskill item drop.
4) Reduce nano cost of heals and/or give us something like Humidity Extractor to reduce what we have to spend on nano rechargers.
I don't want to nerf others or make docs uber. I think on the whole the docs are fairly well balanced. I just want a little assistance so I can have the cashflow to keep up with the nanos and gear for my level.

I just wanted to point out that if you put in maintenance costs, like having to refuel your vehicle all the time or weapons wearing out and having to be replaced constantly, you are only hurting the lower level people who never have money anyway. The higher level people can still easily afford these things.

As someone else pointed out, the problem is that higher level people are generating money by doing missions and such, but then they have nowhere to spend it because the shops don't sell anything that's of interest to them at their level. That's where the underlying problem is. If you want to take money out of game at these levels, you need the shops to be selling up to and above QL 200 weapons, armor, nano recharge kits, etc., so that money gets taken out of the economy instead of just being passed around from player to player. I don't think keeping these higher-end items out of the stores presents anything more than a minor inconvenience to experienced players, and it creates a surplus of money in the game's economy.
