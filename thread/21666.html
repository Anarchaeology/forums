True.
Lemme give a quick rundown on damage output here:

From top damage dealers to the lowest. (NOTE: I will discuss the pet classes later on. The are hard to fit in correctly here, especially since I have limited experience in playing them. These are also my personal opinions.)

1: NT. Ranged class, very low hitpoints. The NT should reign supreme in the damage department. Defences; Calms and the rare nullity. If a melee class comes in melee range, NT is (or should be) toast.

2: MA. Melee class, medium hitpoints. Defences: Heals and good evades.

3: Agents. Ranged class, low hitpoints. Defences: Roots and the 14.2 aggro-dehancer.

Why this order? Well, NT's are the weakest defensive class in the game. MA's are the melee class with meager HP and bad heals, Agents are also lacking in the defensive department. So, whats the best defence? Offence. These 3 classes need to take down an opponent fast. Melee classes should do more damage then ranged classes, since melee users are far more vunurable to roots, snares and range weapons. Which 2 classes are getting self-only crit buffs? Right on. Something needs to be done about the NT's though, the are rarely pulling aggro from teams, no matter how unwisely (aggressive) they play their profession


This bring us to the tank classes:

4: Enforcer. Melee class, superior HP. Defences; HP, healing (mongo), root-breaking, good AC.

5: Soldier. Ranged class, good HP. Defences; HP, good ac and MK reflect shields.

Now remember, just because these classes are the best at taking damage does not mean that they should be the best at dealing it. I'm sure many enforcers agree that it's a chore to keep aggro at certain times, this is why we have aggro enhancers, taunt nanos, da taunter etc.


Anyways, the adventurer is hard to put in correctly in this scheme, an adventurer could go weak defense/strong offence and vice versa, thereby moving around in this rating.

6: Adventurer. Melee/Ranged as chosen, medium HP. Defences; Healing, decent AC, decent reflect shield, decent evades, decent runspeed. Medium damage dealers.


Here things get a little difficult. This is my opinion on how professions should be in the damage department, please don't take offense.

7. Fixers. Ranged class, medium HP. Defences; Superiour evades, roots, snares, rather bad HoT, and amazing runspeed.

8. Trader. Ranged class, medium HP. Defences; Healing, mezzes, roots, deprives.

9. Doc. Melee/ranged as chosen, good HP. Defences; Superiour healing, init debuffs.

Should fixers really outdamage Traders? Yup. Look at their defenses. The Trader is the strongest profession in the game, from a defensive point of view. Fixers are excellent at running away from danger and I've enjoyed many an astounding Fixer tanking with their superiour evades. But they have far weaker healing/rooting capability then the Trader and lack the mezzing capability. But Fixers, Traders and Docs are/should be of utmost importance to have in a team due to their team benifits (healing/mezzing/wrangling etc).

Now to the pet professions, they are a bummer to fit in here. Let me rank their order between themselves.

1. Engineer: Ranged/Melee as chosen, decent HP.
Offence: Nothing apart from pet and weapon.
Defence: superiour AC, damage decrease.

2. Crat: Ranged/Melee as chosen, low HP.
Offence: DD nanos and pet.
Defence: Roots, mezzes.

3. Meta: Ranged/melee as chosen, low HP.
Offence: Nano buffs, DD nanos, attack pet, mezz pet.
Defence: Nano and Damage debuffs. Heal pet.

Now, since the engineer doesnt have any other offensive/defensive capability then pet/weapon and AC they/their bot should do the most damage hands down. Crat and MP's should be a close second since their offensive/defensive capabilites are so much stronger then the Engineers.

How do we fit the pet professions in the general ranking then? Engie at 4th place perhaps, just after Agents and before Enforcers. Crats and MP's somewhere in the 7-9 range, they should be warmly welcomed into a team for their team benifits, not per damage dealing capability. I'm not sure, feel free to argue with me on this.


Anyways, these are my opinions based on a risk/reward analysis and I hope you find it interesting. Also, think about the changes made in 14.2, how they fit in this ranking adventure of mine.
