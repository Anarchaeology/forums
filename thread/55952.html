NTs would be ok with some smallish changes
Well, I can only speak for low-level NTs here (<50), but so far from playing an NT:

It seems to *mostly* be fine. I can solo and be useful in a team. I don't PvP. But some things I would like changes on:

1) Enfraam's takes up a ridicuous amount of NCU compared to the effect.

2) A major line of nanos, the range increasers, are totally useless. After you boost the 25m nanos to have 40m range (the game limit), there is no sense running better ones. FC should either rethink the whole range thing, or replace the higher-lvl range increasers with something. Maybe something like (3) below.

It's ok that some nanos are less effective than others, but no nano should be 100% useless in the game. If nothing else, just remove them.

3) The Dark Movement nano should be a line of nanos, not just one.

4) HE output could maybe be boosted a bit, but it's quite useful already.

5) I'm not sure about the usefulness of the blinds. They only last for 10+ seconds, and their effect seems to be minimal. Maybe if they were *very* cheap nano-wise, or had a longer duration? In addition, the blinds draw aggro very easily - maybe this should be fixed, it further reduces their usefulness. Besides, how do *blinded* mobs instantly know who to attack?

6) A low-level version of the Layered protection line would be nice.

7) The bosses being immune (not resistant, but totally immune) to calms, roots, etc makes them almost impossible for NTs. They already have a high NR, why do they need total immunity? It ruins things for lots of other professions, too, crats especially.

So, with some fairly minor changes I would be a happy camper. I'm sure high-lvl NTs have more complaints, can't speak for that. A lot of problems probably begin after lvl 100 or so.
