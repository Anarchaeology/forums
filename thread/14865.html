Truth
The fact of the matter is...NT's are almost impossible to fix.

I was a Wizard in EQ, so I know the history of the issues that WILL come up.

In both games, you have a profession/class that has low HP's, and is 100% dependant on his nano pool/mana pool. Once out of nano, an NT is useless. Soldiers and such will argue thatit's the same with ammo, but that's not true. They can buy 100000 ammo if they want, and other than reloading, they won't run out. Not that it's an issue, because with the low HP's of the NT, they are dead anyway.

MP's/Magicians are similar to NT's. However, they are NOT as dependant on nano pool. They rely on their pets for their primary damage.

Funcom didn't really think the 50% damage cap thing through very well in my opinion. Taking our range (Which, yes, was way too overpowering) and reducing our damage while requiring the same amount of nano was a BAD idea.

Ignorance from other professions also comes into play here. I see a LOT of posts on how NTs are already so uber that any improvement would make them into Gods. This is SO untrue.

I see MA's complaining A LOT about root. Deal with it. It's fair. If my NT roots you and nukes you to death, of course I'll be ok with that. However in the MA's opinion, they should be able to break root, and melee us. Sure, fine for the MA, we'd die almost instantly. So it's the same argument on BOTH sides. MA get's rooted, MA dies. MA not rooted, NT dies.

Yes we get Calm, humidity, Layered Protection, etc. But realise that other than Layered protection, none of those is going to help us in PVP. And Layered only gives us an extra second of life really.

Overequipping comes into play here too. Soldiers, Enforcers, etc can weild QL 200 weapons and maybe armor. Str/Sta is aparently easy to increase. There are no INt/Psy boosts in the game that let us do this with armor. Odin's is selfonly...I think...and by the time an MP is able to cast it, they should already be close to QL 200 armor anyway.

In addition to this, where a soldier can get buffs and equip a gun way above his level, use it whenever, and do well with it. An NT can't do the same with his nanos. Sure you can get Mochams and upload a NICE high level nuke, but once your buff is gone, so is the ability to use the nano.

No I do not have a solution for NT balance. I don't know that it can be done honestly. MP's can take us out FAST. So can pretty much anyone with a gun, or anyone able to keep us in melee range.

NT's DO need work, and LOT'S of it. Sad as it is, I'm thinking the IP reset may help us a lot. I'd use it to equip a gun of some sort so I could still do damage with an unmake on me.
