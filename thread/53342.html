Yes you know me, Merc. *wink*

This is Stexer, the level 1 neutral trader this thread is named for.

First of all I'd like to point out that I do not exploit and never will. I'm clean as a whistle. There's no need to take action with a /petition for it's already been done. I spent a while talking to an exploits GM to make sure that they know I'm clean. It's all in the math, kiddies.

About me ruining the game....? Yes I ask people to fight me, and if they do, they do. I'll hit people without their direct consent for various good reasons, and even if you look at it from an RP point of view, it's okay. I can't recall just jumping another neut. If I have and I forgot, please respond to correct me ^_^;;. I don't target 'nubiz' at all - in fact just today I turned down a fight from somebody who wanted me to tell them how to get to the arena because it was their first character. There's no fun in that!

People have killed me. Yes it's possible =P One that I remember well that was in my level range was a level 5 agent named Iswet. His X-3 went right through my armor. He's a wonderful twink. We both stopped at a very low health, so he didn't actually kill me. I got killed by Ourburst today, a level 9 fixer. He had a great strategy too. Perschett is also a challenge, we haven't fough fully yet but he's scary indeed being a Rookie level 9 enforcer.

I've had some fun kills though! I got a level 17 soldier once, and an 18 martial artist. Exciting to fight people that much higher!

I enjoy PvP, and I've been doing it since sometime in the 12.x era. Boy those days when MMD was full.. so fun.. Anyway. I don't look to ruin people's fun, it's just that many of you are seeming very ignorant of what "NCU" is.

My NCU (Nano Containment Unit) holds nano programs that increase some stats. I have enough NCU to get certain nanos into my NCU, so I do. It's actually a wonderful tool for PvP. I respect people that enjoy fights where people rely solely on their own nanos because that's a perfectly fine and interesting wayh to fight. However I do not respect players who, in their seeming ignorance, show their dislike of 'outside-buffed' players in vulgar and abusive terminology.

It's our choice. And it's their choice to fight using their own nanos if they wish, but judging from the /shouts I see and /tells I get while in the arena, many of these players mistakingly point out that somehow 'self-buffed' fights are the standard. This cannot be true, for the game gives very little suggestion of this. Self-only nanos are for the caster only, so nanos that do not have this restriction must be meant for other people. I'm getting a tad tired of the players that are ignorant to this fact and I've been happy to reveal the truth

If you'd like to contact me, you know my name! I usually hang out at the Omni-Entertainment Arena or the Tir Arena. Good luck out there, PvPers!

Stexer
