R1 Roleplayer's Conference
This is a follow up to the thread "RPG Session" http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...?threadid=8297

The planned Rubi-Ka 1 Roleplayer's Conference will be held Sunday 10th of February at Reets Retreat at 20:00 GMT.
It will be open to roleplayers of all alignments. Guilds participating to this event will we asked to give a short, OOC presentation about themselves. If your guild intends to come but has not already signed up for this in the "RPG Session" thread, please reply to this thread or drop me a line at gsoylent@hotmail.com so that I can draw up a batting order for the guild presentations.

After the main event, we hope to hold a series of optional seminars and workshops and of course the full range of Reets entertainment facilities will be available. If you have a special roleplaying interest or project you wish to discuss in more depth than a short presentation would allow, let me know and I will try to reserve one of the Reets side rooms for you. For instance the South Rubi-Ka Society will be talking about the Rollerball League.

For non-guild roleplayers out there, this event might be a good chance to find a home. Note also that while the presentations and workshops are geared towards the guilds, we are open to submissions from private individuals or other forms of associations as well.

If you have any other questions, feel free to contact me.

Regards,
