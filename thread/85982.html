This Game is just a Glorifed Sim City
Okay, a few weeks ago I picked up my copy of NW just like eveyrone else and started playing. Oh this is gonna be really cool I thought. Well, I thought wrong. Everything about Notum Wars is false on what they would have you belive.

Towers

The Intent:

A Way for you to have high level content involving Personal/Guildwide Advantages and Mass PvP Combat with the ability to Attack/Defend your base in a time of war

The Reality:

More like Simcity. You build it and Hope something doesnt come around, because when it does its a disaster that you can not defend against. The Tornado that sweeps your base will render you unable to move, sit, attack, or cast a nano. This Disaster will cause your computers network card to have a heart failure, and cause your video card to play at 1FPS. Nothing like this has ever been seen before. But O yes my friend, there is more. When you finally manage to chop away from the battle, it will kick you back into the fray, only you can never get out...its a neverending labryinth of doom. If and when you do get out all you will see when you sit down is an Army of invisible attackers shooting you from 10 miles away until you die. There is absolutely nothing to do at this point but watch the screen become stained with the blood of your character as you pray for an end to this nightmare. Finally, Changing Area: Please Wait...You can return to leveling and enjoying the game. But no This disaster causes all guild leaders to order you back into the fight. No Not that Anything but that.....but you have to go for you phear them kicking you from the gulid for lack of participation. Dont wanna lose your benefits and all. This is a neverending Hell! Dont get involved ....because once you go in....you can never EVER come out.


Just my opinion anyway
