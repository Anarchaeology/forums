hehe... the one with lots of rocks... it's called Ramussen Moss. Difficult to find the entrance if u don't have a yalm. Took me quite a while when I was "young"... and got killed by the roaming snakes there.

The one by the edge of the zone have X coordinate of 300+, the one at Ramussen Moss is about 600+.

Both are usually small. Though these days u can get the subway type.. which can be very big.

As for where to get mission to these spots. As u level up, u need to go further away.. ie. initially u can get them from trade, then later newland, then later.. maybe mort of something.

IMO, the optimum level for token farming is 126+. That is when u'll get 6 tokens per mission. That is about the time when u are trying hard to get comp lit, etc. to squeeze in QL200 NCUs.... ie. when a 1k board will be most useful.

At level 126+, get clon**** missions from newlands.

U can also try those with Y axis at 1k or less. Usually small. Take 3 a time.. u'll learn to recognize the small ones. Just delete the big ones (no point wasting time if u're after tokens and not xp).

Or.. instead of clon****.. just go team missions at Broken Shores. Broken Shores' missions are not so big. With a good team, u get to level up fast and get lots of tokens at the same time!

I farmed tokens ... and got 1k. But.... as I level up... now I have 2k tokens! I wasn't going for more.. it just happened that u'll get lots of tokens as a result of doing mission after mission in the process of leveling up.
