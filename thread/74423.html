Part of the problem with soloing is that you have to be a generalist. If you're going to solo you obviously can't rely upon your team mates for buffs, healing, calms, or added attack power. You're totally on your own, and this affects how you develop your char because you're forced to spend IP more broadly. Also, as a soloist you're going to be lacking in at least one or two areas, so its going to limit the difficulty of missions you can do.

My point here, is that, yes, I agree. There has to be something special that you can get soloing that you can't get in a team, because you're paying a significant price restricting yourself to soloing.

What would be cool is that you'd get special nanos from soloing that would offset some of the deficiencies your char has. Say as a MA, you'd get a stronger AC buff, or as a doc, a short duration calm.

This sort of thing would make soloing a viable alternative. You'd still be far from the pinacle you could achieve as a specialist who relies upon team members to offset deficinecies. But your char would at least be respectable.
