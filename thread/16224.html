Bliss, now a safer place
This weekend, I missed out on the big Operation. Unfortunalty, more pressing matters arouse, so my attention was needed elsewhere. This is NOT something that I hope will happen again, as upholding and supporting MD is something that I belive in. That aside, I participated in an important cleansing of the Bliss suroundings this weekend. For a long time now, Bliss has been under a constant threath of a large horde of aggresive spiders and scorpions. For that very reason, the beatifull city of Bliss is mostly a deserted place that few people knows about, and ever fewer visit. For too long this has been alowed to go on, so my good friend Azarro and me decided to do something about it. We were joined by another good friend of mine whose name I can not disclose here, due to his current situation. We quickly cleared the close vicinity of the city, but as we pushed on westwards, we came agains massive hoards of scorpions. At one time I counted as many as 17 foul creatures on my HUD at the same time. But we would not let that stop us, and great comunication, and patient work allowed us to lure one or two, sometimes three creatures, so that we could take them out. This tacitic worked very well in the dry desert, and as the sun rose on the sky, and our clips of ammo diminished, the scorpions became fewer and fewer. Unfortunatly we were unable to find the scorpions lair, so the threath is not over. Lt Gen Melayie observed our team for a long period of time, and gave the whole teams performance two thumbs up. Proud of our performance and the Lt Gen. kind words, we gathered at the city entrance to rest. Hopefully, this will lead more people to Bliss. I myself will return soon, to go look for the scorpions nest.

Pvt Nahadra out.
