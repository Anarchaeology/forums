An idea for those that want neutrality, and an illegal market.
hey all, I know I know.. "why are you here creepy? you said you quit"

Well I still havent run out of time on my account but anyhow listen..


I had an idea!

A lot of people have been saying they wish they could become neutral, and though I support the idea.. Heres the thing, you STILL wont be able to switch to neutral, completely.


1) Neutral advantage: Illegal Market, since neuters work on both sides of the fence, and sometimes in a totally different back yard all together.. Neuters get access to a black market wherein items such as concealment devices, bot heals, stigma rifles, ect are sold.. Basically the "un shopped, pre-125' items.(to make it risky, put the black market in a pvp zone)

2) New nanos: Agents, and Fixers would get a nano which allowed them to be "neutral" for a period of time (maybe 30 minutes) this would be a hostile nano and would bring with it the neutral voulnerabilities of pvp. This would be a "neat" and RP nano for both the fixer and agent, and would definately give the fixer class something it sorely needs.. RP-Ability.

Now, I know an argument could be that the stigma, and the cloaking devices are good ways to make money off other players and that someone would make a neut just to sell the stuff at profit to other players.... Well , so? In NYC you cant buy handguns without an FFA (Federal Firearms Liscense) , yet people drive to Virginia, Georgia and other states and freely buy the guns at cost then sell them for street value in NYC.. So it would be realistic


This doesn't ease the people that want to leave the "conflict", but it gives a pretty good reason why they "cant".. Atleast in my mind.


Thats my idea, save your flames for your bongs fellas
