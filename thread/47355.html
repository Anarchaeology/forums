Bionitrous
Supa Leet
Actually, they are hopefully fixing one of their biggest mistakes. The Padded Overcoat and Dragon Armor are _ok_ but should never have been extremely powerful.

They should look as cool as hell and identify those 'oh so leet' people who manage to slay the all powerful dragon. But, they should not be so powerful as to create a class seperation from those who find what those 60 people do every 18 hours to be highly exclusionary or distasteful.

Got your Dragon Armor? Yeah, you look cool. Seriously. But, let's not imbalance the game for the sake of a playfield that can handle only 60 people and is run by cliques that exclude 99.9 percent of the playerbase.

Things that should be in the T loot:

- Ticket to get your Yalm painted red or black.
- Plastic surgery gift certificate to get a head model not available in the startup room
- Warp device to playfield (or Club) not accessible to most
- Yellow tank armor
- Burst of Speed stims with 2000 Runspeed boost
- Bracers if Growing Flesh 115% Title Level 4/5

Things that should NOT be n the T loot:

- Items that give you anymore than 2 or 3 points in nanoskill
- Armor that is _easily_ identified as the best in the game
- Items whose effects can not be gotten anywhere else in the game by any other playstyle

By all means, I feel that the loot from the PvP dungeon ought to be unique. But, the fact that that cesspool can only handle 60 players at a time tied in to the fact that at MOST 10% of the playerbase is interested in direct PvP competition for loot basically makes it clear that what you get there should be 'interesting', 'unique', whatever. Just not overpowering (like it used to be).

If they took ALL the loot off the dragon would people still go there? Of course they would. Would they still brag over who got the kill this morning. Of course they would. Would the lag drop. Yep. Would all the pissing and moaning about ninja looters and 'rules' disappear? Guaranteed.

I just hope this isn't a mistake and that FunCom finally realizes that there's another 99.8% of the playerbase who needs to be catered to in addition to the four dozen people who get to play cowboys and indians squabbling over a stupid lizard that thousands upon thousands of people will never even see before they eventually hit the 'cancel' button.
