Although i understood what you explained (thank you for that), using a %HE will just increase the gap between NM and Atrox. Yet using a fixed-HE will make the gap stay the same.

%HE for NM will give let's say 10 more nukes for NM and 6 extra nukes for Atrox. Damage gap is increased by 4 nukes.

Fixed-HE gives the same to everyone. Damage gap stays the same.

Now using math tricks you can say that the ratio gap/total damage will decrease. And it's true.


Just a comparison. If a company decide to raise salaries of all their employes :
- first method : + 10% to everyone
- second method : + 100$ to everyone

The guys who earns more than 1000$ will prefer the first method because it will benefits them most. The guys who earns less than 1000$ will prefer the second.


First method increases the difference between salaries. Making the rich ones richer and throwing a bone at others. Second method is a better improvement for the lowest salaries and not much _noticeable_ for highest salaries. Noticeable is important imo : when you're rich 100$ isn't big money (but it still afford you some shoppings).

Now it's a matter of opinion in order to decide which method is the best. Yet 'Best' is an absolute concept. It can't be applied for relative situations. So coming back to nanopool :

The more nanopool you have the better you will benefit the %HE. The less nanopool you have the better you will enjoy the fixed-HE. Being a solitus, i'm the guy with 1000$ and both ways will give approximatively the same amount of nanopool.

This leads me to a conclusion, if you want to increase the power of Nanomage vs Atrox/Opifex then you choose the %HE. If you want to let it the same (little nuance here) you go the second method.

Nuance : i write the same. However i know it's a small boost for Atrox/Opifex vs Nanomages. But Nanomages still get something good. So i value it as 'same'.

If you think NT Atrox is stronger than NT Nanomage in overall then you will want the %HE (Opifex being completly ignored here). If not, the fixed-HE.

And here is the point we disagree i guess. I don't see Atrox are too much powerfull vs Nanomage. You do, don't you ?
