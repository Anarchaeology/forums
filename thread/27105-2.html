Apprentice Cosmik1 has 1670 points
Novice Cosmik2 has 1750 points

1750 - 1670 = 80 = Rating Difference
16 = Base Change
0.04 = Change Modifier

16 + (0.04 x 80) = 19.2

If Apprentice Cosmik1 wins, he get's 19.2 points
If Novice Cosmik2 wins, he get's 19.2 points

---------------------------------------------------

Novice Cosmik2 has 1750 points
Novice Cosmik3 has 1755 points

1755 - 1750 = 5 = Rating Difference
16 = Base Change
0.04 = Change Modifier

16 + (0.04 x 5) = 16.2

If Novice Cosmik2 wins, he get's 16.2 points
If Novice Cosmik3 wins, he get's 16.2 points

---------------------------------------------------

Alot more static system than I hoped for. I wonder about a few things tho. The Change_Modifier only makes sense if the one with the less points, actually win. Thus he get's more points than fighting one of his own point-range.

BUT, and this is a big but(t). This also applies even if the winner is the one with the most points. He actually get MORE points from killing someone with less skill (in this case Apprentice) than killing a theoretycally more skilled Novice opponent.

The Change_Modifier works in 2 out of 3 effects. (#1 and #3)

1) Winner has less points.
2) Winner has most points.
3) Risk of getting killed by one with less points.

In #1, the winner gets more points from the kill, because his victim had more points, thus beeing more skilled. This also creates #3. The looser loses more points from the kill, due to risk of dying to a less skilled opponenet.

In #2 however, the more skilled player gets an advantage, point-wise, from killing an "easy" opponent.

Why was it created this way?
