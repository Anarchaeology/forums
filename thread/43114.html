Hunting Grounds for post LVL 150
Hi there,

I've recently passed LVL 150 - Hooray! no more Skill Cap.

However....

I was just thinking to myself the other day wouldn't it be nice if there was some more content in the game for high-level characters rather than mission, after mission, after mission, and occasionally helping your buddies out to collect MKII and do those damn Alvin / Dodga and other static quests.

Im an Adventurer, and as such I like to occasionally take a break from all the stress and rushing of missioning and go and do a bit of hunting... but you see... there's the thing !! Where ?

The only thing really left for me to do now is the mighty Tarasque, but at level 150, unless I take some of my lvl 200 buddies along, I'll get panned in PvP and to be honest its bloody boring camping that damn thing.

I've managed to find a few places to hunt. Mort... quite a few green Mantis and Demons, wow! greens... Then there are red Ace's at the Ace Camp in SFH... put that is PvP and as such I dont feel ub3r enough to go and fight there yet... then there are the Cyborgs in Mort... but damn they are still too hard... and... er.. that's about it...

Someone out there is bound to correct me... but couldn't we have a few more hunting grounds aimed at the higher levels... and NOT in PvP zones...

It seems there is a lack of content for higher-level characters. Mostly I enjoy my time now socialising with a regular group of peeps at my level, and we occasionally team mission, then go and help some buddies out collecting MKII, but that's about it ?

Could we please have something more to do ?

I'd be interested in hearing other people's views on this, so if you peeps wanna post, please do and give some idea of what you'd like to be added.

I mean obviously these monsters would have to be situated in far away places so as to avoid low levellers accidentally stumbling into them, that shouldn't be a problem as by this time most of us have Yalm's anyway and / or amazing run speeds, and I wouldn't mind the travelling because it would mean I'd get to see new parts of Rubi-Ka.

I recently enjoyed a trip to Endless Wastes with a few buddies hunting Mantis and Demons there in the hope of finding some Soul Fragments. HEY! and thats the other thing !!! Please could you increase the QL190 + Soul Fragment drop :-)
