Three-four ideas to improve missions.
I've given these suggestions before, shortly before I quit playing AO for awhile due to boredom.

Okay. the short one first. Limit reward items to ones that the character could THEORETICALLY use. No more false profession nanos offered to my engineer please.

Now the long one(s).

Despite the scripting of the missions, there are really only, at this time, 5 missions. There is the assassinate mission, the recover item mission, the repair mission, the spot item mission, and the spot person mission.

How about two-three more mission types.
The give item to person mission. Take this object and trade it to the person you're otherwise just supposed to find or eliminate. It could be an ultimatum, instructions to a sleeper agent, death threat, the next move in a chess game, etc.
The get item from person mission. Visit this person, and he'll give you an item you have to take somewhere else, (in a single layer mission, the mission booth.)
The kill person for item mission. A recover object mission, but with the object a guaranteed loot item on a named mob.

Multi-level missions. there are 5 now, with the other suggestion, that'd be 8 missions. By making the missions multiple stage, you could have 25 off of just the original 5, and 64 with the suggested 3 additionals. Such as, (original mission types), the spot item mission/repair item mission. A saboteur stole the challenge-response module from our radar base in Tir County. We tracked his wherabouts to a secret staging facility in Greater Tir County. We need you to go to Greater Tir county and recover this item before OT technitions can decode it and return it to the radar display system in Tir County.

Three level, using some of the suggested mission types.

Spot object mission/kill for loot mission/deliver item mission.

We have had some problems with a certain orginazition, who is going around giving us a bad name. Not that we normally condone such agressive solutions under normal circumstances, but this time we have no choice. John Doe, a prominant member of this group, feels that his leutanant and wife is safe from all types of weapons. Deep cover agents within the ranks of our adversary, however, have reported to us the existance of a prototype weapon which we believe could be used to kill Mrs. Jane Doe. Your job will be to locate this item, (spot item mission), and use it to kill Jane Doe, recovering her wedding band as proof, (kill for loot mission), the weapon, from what we've been led to believe, is so simple that even a child could use it, but it has only a single charge and we doubt very much it could kill a leet, if that leet weren't protected in the complex way that Mrs. Doe is, (special subsectability to an otherwise useless weapon.) Once you have Mrs. Does' wedding band, you are to deliver it to John Doe, so that he knows once and for all that, yes we can still reach him, and no, we don't care for the type of reputation he has been giving us, (deliver item mission).

With three missions strung together, and 8 basic mission types, you could have 8^3 possible DIFFERENT missions. Very few people would find themselves repeating a mission any time soon.

As far as missions that have the person doing the same basic mission two or three times in a row, you could either use that condition to signal the more familiar single stage mission, or you could have a person completing a circuit. (Fix these three radar stations, we need the names on this list eliminated, they can be found at..., we have recently found the locations of 3 of the paintings, and we need you to spot them for us so we can get them off the market.) If the former, you might make the number of elements a random roll 1-x, where x is level divided by some constant, newbies would still have a single step, more advanced characters could find themselves running 8 sub-missions, but all within a tighter radius than they would be traveling otherwise, and with fewer mobs per mission zone.

One more idea for missions. Some more choices for mission areas. The military base, the corporate/government campus, and the crater camp. Each one outside. Each one more open. Each one composed MOSTLY of "rooms" that are little more than a knoll, or a tree surrounded by grass. Some of the rooms, however, can be buildings, with mobs inside. By making them areas that are routinely delimited by a high fence, wall, or crater edge, you can make accessing them the same as for the existing mission areas.
