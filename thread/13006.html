Guild housing? (Walkthrough)
Guild housing was promised from day 1 and Im surprised that there hasnt been done anything about it, this far into the game. To implement GH should be fairly easy and thats why Im a bit surprised that we now enter 13.8 and still no GH. Clearly this is an issue that has been neglected and not regarded as important by FC.

I think this game is all about guilds and interaction between players. Guild housing should be a truly great tool for us players, to look for teams/stuff/Rping/whatever. Thus further enhance what this game is all about, interaction with other likeminded. Enough rambling, we all know why and what in this case.

Please FC consider this: (Walkthrough, Howto make Guild housing)

Take an apartment and make it bigger, with a lil furniture. Then give all members of the guild an extra key. Make a new entrance somewhere central for each side. Voila, guild housing.

Guild banking, may be a bit more time consuming, but is equally important. Simply place a bank inside the guild housing and set permissions. All guild members can put stuff in and view the contents of the bank, only a certain ranks can withdraw stuff.

Important note: Lets concentrate on the stuff what we can do, rather than staring ourselves blind on the stuff we cannot do (yet). Thus, implement guild housing even if you dont have a suitable solution for the guild bank.

I believe you guys have underestimated the importance of guild housing and the impact it will have on the people of Rubi-ka. I realize that there are a lot of things that needs to be fixed, certain professions etc. This is one of the things that should be prioritised, even if it means a naked room without textures. It seems to me that it shouldnt take to long for you guys to whip this up.

Ill send you guys a case of hollow chocolate bunnies if you fix this :P
/bribe
