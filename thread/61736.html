Write up of the Rally, Nov 2nd
This Saturday, the 2nd of November, saw the open declaration of presence by a new faction that seems set to leave their mark on the face of Rubi-ka's history.
The faction is of course the Desert Winds, a moderately large collection of neutrals who showed their colours this day by the Independence rally held in the ICC HQ this weekend.

The rally took place in the evening of the 2nd, and invitation was open to all whom would come, clan, Omni and neutral alike. Though it is safe to say that the first two got a wakeup call already with the first speaker, one Ryan "Bedside" Silvestrini, as he greeted the neutrals welcome, and told Omni-Tek and he clans in attendance, that they were free to listen to the role they would not play in this planet's future.
This of course caused some mutterings in the gathered crowd, however those did not come to much as the stage was quickly taken by the president of the Desert Winds himself, Eldridge "Naxilite" McCovery.

The speech by the President of this guild was a much more moderate, but possibly also more effective than the previous speaker, now that the interest of the crowd had been caught. It started with pointing out the unjust behaviour of Omni-Tek as well as that of the clans and the ICC, towards the planet and those who call it their home. It would be wrong to say that at this point, there was no jeering from the clans and Omni personnel in attendance. This however soon died down as the speech went on, elaborating on Rubi-Ka's place as a home for millions and not just a "corporate plaything" The speech ended on a supplication to the masses to support the Desert Winds, and even more, their cause for a free Rubi-Ka. Free from "money hungry corporations and egotistical clan leaders".
As The President stepped down to allow the next speaker to take the place of attention, there were already heard cries of "Free Rubi-Ka" amongst the masses gathered.

Katelin "Elizera" Keppler, and advisor of the group, was next on the stage, and though she did in her speech claim to be a poor orator, her speech was one that reached deep into more than one person's soul in the assembly and gripped their emotions tight. This speech, while illuminating problems with the ICC, Omni-Tek, and a somewhat cryptical remark about Omni-Tek's oft failing weather control system, was also one of emotion and inspiration. Supplicating the people to fight for their home, not for someone else, not even for the Desert Winds, but for their own cause, for their freedom, for their home, for their future.
As she stepped down, again shouts of freedom for the planet was heard, this time in even more voices, some of them even from Omni-Tek personnel and clanners.

The Speeches drew to their end as Naxilite again took the speakers podium with a final rousing supplication to spread the word of a free Rubi-Ka to all others as well as letting it be known that all who share that view are welcome within the arms of the Desert Winds.

This ended the official part of the rally as the members of the Desert winds then proceeded in slow and stately procession through the crowd and through the Newland Whom-pah, supported by shouts calling for the Freedom of Rubi-Ka, drowning out the detractors in the crowd.

In reporting this event would it not be fair either to not mention the long discussions later held in the ICC headquarters, long after the Rally of the Desert Winds was over, This clearly shows that the emotions and thoughts of people were set in movement by these speakers and the cause they represent.
Will this day later go down in history as they day when Rubi-Ka started it's rise into it's own, independent freedom? Only time will tell, but now all know that there are those out there who will fight for that freedom with all their strength and soul.

By Serraglio of the Desert Winds
