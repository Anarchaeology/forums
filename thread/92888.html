The Future
From the desk of Major McCovery
Leader of the Desert Winds

Date : Jan 21st

Citizens

A new year and a new begining. A traditional chance to start afresh. How many of you will though? how many of you will stop and consider your actions? how many of you will consider the future of Rubi-Ka?

In the last year the ICC at the bequest of certain member corporations has opened up Rubi-Ka to public mining. Now the plague of towers has spread across almost the entire surface of the planet. There has been no consideration for the future and no consideration for the delicate balance the planet needs to sustain life (an artifical state of affairs as it is).

The mines poison our home

I will be poisoned no more

Who said I was a hippy?!
The ICC, or the Idiots Collected Corporation...we knew they weren't the brightest of bunch when they sat there on the door to Newlands and took the same butt kicking that our own poor guards used too. And these are supposidly trained peace keepers...roflmao, yeah so well trained they like getting there backsides sent to reclaim every 30secs. Anyhow, towers...or the seed to the destruction of Rubi-ka as it seems would be more accurate description.

I had to say, at first it almost seemed a kinda good idea, opening up mining, nice kick in the privates to Omni-tek, and nicely spread a bit of wealth around the population of RK through the mining. It was also good to see the mercenary groups and general war mongers have something to cage them up and fight over. It even seemed to start out as a good thing for the local community what with people all coming together to assist one another...all seemed kinda good with the world (for once)...and then things went a little bit outta shape, kinda in the way a cookie factory would if under control of a group of leets.

Groups getting bitter with one another as fights break out. These groups no longer talking to other groups as they are of the same side as someone who attacked them. Its destroying the community feeling which we all had. Yes Clan, Omni, and Neut never really got along...but it was usually pretty much a bearable peace which allowed for negotiation...not the shoot first, negotiate later problems we have at the moment...or the I ain't ever going to talk to them problem

I have to admit though, I am extremly intregued by the work of Professor Maitland and those in Omni-tek who have been tinkering with all this notum at the moment in regards to the trans-dimensional experiments...but that still comes at great risk to us all, and all life it would seem.

Either way, only way to get things back to some kinda norm, and some kinda hope is peace. War benefits no one. Peace, peaceful trade of goods and services, and the working together or everyone is what is needed to get the planet in some kind of shape again. The only problem is will people actually stand up against the war mongers and work for peace?
