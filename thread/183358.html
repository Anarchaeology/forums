Calling for a Council of Truth
Fellow Clan Members,

We have heard the words of Mr. Radiman for the call of a rebuilding of the Council of Truth, we have heard the words of Sir. Beargar of the Knights of Camelot to unite. But what have we done? Nothing much as of yet. All we have done at this moment is talked about the possibilities, the potential, the want for a new council, and have also argued with other Omni-Tek Employees who would further hinder our abilities to progress as free persons of Rubi-ka. This is why I feel that instead of continuing this arguing, we should put into motion what we have been talking about.
Within the next few weeks, I am going to begin to start talking to other Clan Leaders, and also other Clan members, and citizens about what a Council of Truth should really be, how it would be implemented, and how we clan members would be about to voice our opinion through one unified voice.
I will put more information and details as the days pass of what this would mean.

My fellow clanners, remember to keep the dream alive.

Lets take this from a realistic standpoint for a moment here. It is impratical and impossible for Omni-Tek, a diplomatic and legal entity to make aggreements with each clan on an individual basis.

Without a governing body the 'clans' cannot become a viable diplomatic entity. This is extremely dangerous form a public safety standpoint because there is no consistency that can be expected from one clan to the next nor are there any laws in place from the lack of a binding legal enforcement entity.

No, might does not make right. On Rubi-Ka of all places where people cannot realistically die short of young/old age or freak accidents the fighting and posturing of some of these aggressive and millitant clans do nothing to contribute to the solution.

Realize this, Omni-Tek still has another 50 or so years left on its lease. This is coming from the ICC, an intergalatic legal entity. It is impossible for the clans to think they can speed this along. Hope this sheds some new viewpoints on the matter.
