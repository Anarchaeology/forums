Deus ex Machina recruiting, Omni
The newly formed organization, Deus ex Machine, of which I share presidency, is now welcomely recruiting all Omni level 35 and higher.

The goals of Deus ex Machina are....
1. To provide a nurturing and helpful environment for all members
2. To sustain an enjoyable, active, and well-humored organization
3. To regularly host raids and role play events, with or without ARK involvment
4. To gain minor tower-based resources


Deus ex Machina roles as an organization based heavily around machinery and around technology. We are not cookie lovers. Members are encouraged to role themselves in such a manner, but are also encouraged to create their own personal roles to be cooperated with inside of the organization.

In all, we are an org of people who are ready and happy to explore the world, share knowledge, and have a good time IC or OOC.

If you'd like to know more, visit our webpage, albeit under construction, and feel free to apply at:

www.deusexmachina.cjb.net

Just fill out the form, and we will reply ASAP.

Thanks, cyabye.
