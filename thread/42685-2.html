Before I say this, I should point out that I've never been in PVP. The one time I wondered into a PVP area by mistake, I /terminate d to avoid being killed by someone of the other side. (This was meetmedere about 6 months ago.)

Okay, personally I agree with the three tier system proposed on the first page.

However. I think that two engineers, of equal level, should be able to jump a first tier individual and at least one of them walk away while the first-tier person runs to reclaim.

I think that seeing a lone soldier shouldn't be cause for alarm, unless you're by yourself as well. The same goes for a martial artist and an enforcer.

I think that two first-tier players should be fodder for just one first-tier teamed up with one third-tier to fill in the holes in capability.

Here's an idea. Half damage in PVP ONLY where the struck individual is not currently targeting anyone or has the damage-dealer targeted. Leave damage at full potency if they're ignoring you to deal with a more important threat. (You might also half damage if they have a MOB targeted instead of another player.)

Make attacks on persons that aren't targeting you have a chance, equal to crit chance, of unselecting the current target. Interrupt their concentration, so they at least OCCASSIONALLY swipe at the gnat on their backside.

Give the first-tier people, or maybe a healer class or trader, the ability to "tie in" to the health, or rather hits, of a teammate, so if the teammate gets hit, the first-tier has a chance of taking it, (with the added advantage of any reflects.) This shouldn't represent so much an actual tying of health, but rather the combat specialists ability to "step in" and receive the blow intended for another. You might give pets this same ability. Sort of a reverse evade. You might even tie the "nano", or ability, or innate ability, to these skills. These should probably only apply to PVP, as code already exists to make MOBS target the most painful thorn.

Make sure there are weapons out there, and reasonably available, that are so high up in skill needed that only a combat specialist is likely to have increased the skills to this degree. If this isn't possible, due to buffs available to other classes, then make a gun that requires even higher skills to use effectively, but gives combat specialists a stacking buff to tip the scales.
