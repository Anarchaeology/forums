class strengths and pvp
with the crit nerf coming in 14.2 i'd like to talk about what makes certain classes stronger in certain areas in pvp.
i personally think it was a good area to make the current crit line of ma's self only and add a lesser crit others line. fights were too short and shotguns too powerful.
the point of this thread is too identify what certain classes are strong at or should be strong at compared to others.

i believe MA's should be the best at critting consistently and hitting fast. they will be the best consistent critters in 14.2 due to the crit nerf.

enforcers have the most health points and hit hardest up close. they have essences and the mongo line(self only).

soldiers have the best protection with mk shield (self only) and thier other reflect sheild line. they should also have the best ranged damage output overtime.

agents should be the best alpha strikers imo and then have alot weeker damage output and left vulnerable after the alpha. the new 6sec 100% crit goes along with that idea but its abit too damn strong imo.

fixers should be the fastest class with their run buffs and green run speed skill and be the best escape artists. we are the best at escaping due to our instant grids and the new nano resist increases to grid armour and that in 14.4+. but anyone with 1 of their buffs can run as fast or faster (adv , enf can run alot faster with rage and 1 of our buffs).

im really sick of people being able to run as fast or run faster than myself. i believe enfs should be able to catch us or keep up over a short period of time but not constantly. im also sick of people like karill a rookie omni adv who constantly bugs me for a duel knowing he/she has grid space freemovement on him and with max nano resist my roots/snares brake instantly.

i propose they do what they are planning to do in 14.2 with crit buffs to our current run buff line. make the current 1 self only and add a team run buff line with lesser speed gain.

feel free to comment on my views and ideas.
