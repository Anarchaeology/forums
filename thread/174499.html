Some Neutrals and Clanners find common ground
It's late at night in Newland City. It's quiet as usual, abandoned by most neutrals who dislike the oppressive nature of the cities current "Protectors". There are a few of us though, doing business or whatever, and usually we get into and out of Newland city as soon as we're done. But tonight is different, there is a gathering at the whom-pah. Clan members from several organizations looking ready for a fight. There are many neutral citizens, but those arn't there target. They are looking for Thugs. As they start to work their way to Lorren Warr a call goes out over neutral communications frequency "Clanners attacking Newland city!". But when several high ranking neutrals arive we see that no, Clanners arn't attacking Newland City, they are attacking the opressive thugs that have been a force of division for us for so long. Many neutrals, whome had no relationship with these Clanners, join them in thier attempt to free the city of the Thugs.

Lorren Warr and many Thugs went to reclaim that night, and a message was sent. Their not welcome in our city. I would like to thank the Clanners who initiated this attack and know if you do it again you will recieve similar help. And I offer my services should you need assistance with the Sentinals in Tir.
