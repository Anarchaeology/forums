SWAT teams
I kind of find it irritating when people just stand around and blast away at local shopkeepers, etc.

It isn't so much that I don't think they should be allowed to do it as I find it a tad unrealistic that there is no consequence for doing it. I mean, I think it would be super cool if someone attacked a clan shopkeeper and suddenly ten super high level clan SWAT team members grid jumped into the area and started blazing away!

Or even something as simple as temporary suppression gas levels rising. (Though I recognize that changing the suppression gas levels on the fly might be much harder to code than it seems.)
