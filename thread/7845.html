I have played Gemstone, EQ, AC, and now AO. Gemstone was fun but the price structure with those premium accounts pissed me off. EQ was a horrendous time sink and I did play necro, AC was just crappy although if I were hardcore that alleagence system is ok. AO still has its problems - u still cant affect the storyline, but u can have items be named after u. U can play for just 2 hours, I play engie here so I can just log in, do a mission in about 2 hours and log out, or spend a day at the borgs and gain 2-5 levels. The variety of items and detail in AO far surpasses anyother games on the market so far. DAoC has far better interface and less bugs but leveling at high levels is slow and very time consuming. Daoc still does not have relics implemented .... and idea of weapons/armor wearing out is just not for me.
AO also has that nifty skill system, that not only allows for customizing but will let u correct your mistakes during cap levels.
Bottom line, if u are lookin for good class balance and are not looking to find a job that requires another 5-7 hours commitment per day go with AO.
If you are into no-brainer, null learning curve "keep making that sword, till you drop" routine go for DaoC. There!
