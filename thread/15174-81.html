I hope someone hears me through the noise
I can appreciate the fact that Sumbug is at least trying for a less severe system. But just "keeping the numbercruchers happy" isn't gonna cut it. They crunch numbers for a reason. They over equip for a reason. Putting all that work in for such a meager increase is so counterproductive, so no one would waste the time.

I like to OE my alts to a certain extent. My main (MA btw)is only in the 50s, so my resources are not all that great. Therefore I can't afford to pay 100ks of creds for wrangles to equip my little brothers and sisters. But I do what I can. Use an alt to mission for a particular weapon of implants, get a buff from a friend, etc. Take my Fixer for instance. She is level 10 now. She has a set of lowbie implants (20s I think) for boosting up SMG. She has a 30 Mausser. She does *decent* damage, as in barely enough to feel above average. With a QL 10 she was having serious trouble doing even missions. Now a 10 wearing a QL 30 weapon is nothing compared to most twinkies. But even under Scumbug's reduced penalty guidelines, her damage will suffer tremendously. My guesstimate (late... too late for heavy math) is that she would be knocked down to ql 15 or so in comparative weapon strength, maybe less. The difference between a ql 10 and 15 weapon is virtually nil and not even worth the time spent on equipping it. If anyone thinks to try to pick apart my math and tell me I am an idiot or whatnot, don't bother as you are missing the main point anyway.

The reasons folks OE is varied. Some do it to show off, some for PvP, others just to amaze themselves. But they all expect some kind of tangible benefit from it. Both your rules and the FC rules but a limit on what is incredibly fun for most players that have been around for awhile. Everyone is a true newbie ONCE. We all had our first character. We all walked in to the shop and wondered how the hell we were gonna afford ammo, much less something to shoot it with. But with time comes experience and money. Everyone has the opportunity, given a bit of time to do everything in their power to make a better character, even MAs and NTs. The only ones who don't are the true newbies, which all of us used to be too. The folks that have the means DON'T OE are just artificially limiting themselves. Why ruin my fun? I paid my dues...I'm not a newbie anymore. Just because you don't want to OE, does that mean that I shouldn't? Does that mean that I shouldn't reap the rewards of my time and effort into my characters?

Now someone will step in and tell me that you can still OE. BS, this is strident anti-twinking in full effect. The end effect is cookie cutter syndrome DAoC style. Contrary to popular belief you CAN twink and OE in DAoC. But the weapon maxes out at oh, let's see...about 20% more effective than a level appropriate weapon. And I will tell you from my own experience as a high level Hibernian Elf...It is *ungodly* boring. Take not of the fact that I so not play DAoC anymore. Not being able to use the resources you have aquired ingame to the NOTICEABLE advantage of your other characters is a travesty.

Everyone twinks. It makes new charcters interesting. Newbie levels suck as a rule, this helps it be a bit less tedious. EVERYONE with the resources or inclination twinks. It's human nature. To take that away is unjust in my opinion. Cookie Cutter Syndrome absolutely kills any game that is based upon character development such as AO. What will it matter how I spend my ip if I will end up not being noticeably different from Joe "Noober" Blow? This has been brought on by the havenots who's petty jealousy has run out of control. The whole thing really makes me sick. I can't even really overequip my MA to a large degree and I am still dead against all of this. This is all just my opinion and experience, but I pay monthly for this game too, so my opinion is just as valid as anyone else's. I am not the only one that feels this way.

My final point is regarding PVP balance. If you are either a newbie or a person who doesn't OE for whatever personal conviction, PvP is probably not the place for you anyway. PvP is full of people who do nothing but sit and daydream about the best most efficient ways to gank you. If you are getting killed fast now, even if these changes go through, you can bet that they will all be tweaked to the maximum of the new rules. Then, even if you are too, they will probably still gank you. PvP is not for the squeamish or the jealous. Just wanted to clarify that.

Thats the whole point. Twinking. Oh yeah its great. That is why so many games try to diminish it or eliminate it.

Twinking takes so much out of the game it's unbelievable. Then twinkers clamor about how if you make them actually experience the game without comparative God status that it ruins the fun of the game.

Well think about it. Instead of a struggle to battle and overcome the difficulties IN-game, via developing characters and strategies within the realm of the game, twinkers make it about beating the game (which is impossible in a MMORPG) and they end up skipping most of the content in the game. They rush to end-game (lvl 200) and then they will whine about lack of content now that they are uber~~ mind you the content they ask for is the same type of content they SKIPPED earlier in the game.

AO attempted to create a system that helped to diminish the prevalence of twinking by establishing a skill-based system in which you have to CHOOSE your own weaknesses. Then they left open a huge loophole --OE-- which basically let people create characters (so long as they had money--usually coming from their first or second ill-built character) without having to CHOOSE a weakness for themselves.

They made a mistake. Period. They ended up with a game that after a month of its release began to REQUIRE EVERYONE to twink. That is the entire problem.

People often degradingly refer to "casual gamers" with terms like lazy or stupid. And if they want a level playing field they are deemed "carebears". Great. Add a label and its easy to insult the other person, whippee.

Most are not lazy, and they are not stupid. They have other responsibilities in their life that requires an often significantly greater amount of their time and attention than "power gamers" require. What one person calls lazy can often realistically be translated to mean "They have children and/or a job and bills and other RL hassles that keep them from doing what they wish they had more time for...but Don't"

Now add in the normal rationale that "If they will put in the time and thought that I did, then they can catch up to me and be even". That may very well be true, but once again what defines a casual gamer is that they don't have the time to do what you did. Now here comes the Rub that everyone seems to overlook.

A casual gamer does not expect to have a lvl 150 character that will stomp your level 150 char into the dust, or even necessarily compete with your level 150 character as they may never get there--due to time constraints again sheesh. What they do desire is the possibility that their level 5 character will not get demolished EVERY time by someone elses super-twink level 5 character.

Add to this FC's first response to OE in which they systematically made all the Mobs tougher to keep up with the second or third generation characters that all had the super-twink thing going and you just took away all that cool content that typical twinkers SKIP in the early part of the game.

Basically when you twink and it changes economy and game mechanics, you really do hurt the game and the people that play it. You are not ruining casual gamer's lvl 150 plus experience, you are ruining thier experience from day ONE.

Even if you don't like or appreciate the specifics of the changes coming up in this patch, you have to recognize the thought and consideration that was put into this. FC knows that a relatively small, and sickeningly disloyal group, are poised and ready to retaliate with cancellations and flames and other methods of showing disgust, yet FC is still going through with it. They are trying to salvage this game from becoming some cult-like Twinkers wet dream, in which only a handful of people gather in the murky light of their monitors to cackle about how they remember when the "carebears" tried to ruin their fun.

These changes are so necessary it isn't even funny, and the more FC can do, the better.

Next time you start thinking that twinking is the best durn thing in the world, find a good book you always wanted to read and then skip to the ending first. Then see how enjoyable the book is to read from the beginning. Better yet, find someone else that is just settling in to read the same novel and blurt out the ending to them too. I am sure they will thank your for relieving them of the necessity to enjoy all the CONTENT from the beginning for themselves. Oh, don't forget to write the author of the book and complain that after you skipped to the end that you ran out of content to keep your interest and threaten to quit reading the book.
