Reminder for Agent Sector B-Day Party!
Just wanted to remind all you hard working agents....this Sunday at 3pm EST is the agentsector.com 1 year B-day party at Reets Retreat (the glass dance floor area). This is a RK1 party.

This party is the party to go to if your an agent. Lots of other long time agents will be in attendance. Lots of dancing, exchange of info, meeting of new friends and I am sure trading and great deals to fellow agents on items.

How to get to Reets...
No matter where you are...just get to ICC so it's easy to start.
Sorry I can't give navigational directions as I am not in game and don't know what the directions are.(n, e, s, w)

1) Start at ICC(You can get there from Omni-1 Trade/Newland/Tir)
*If you are in Newland go to step 3.
2) Whompa to Newland
3) Whompa to Borealis
4) Whompa to Stret West(Last Ditch)
5) As you come out the whompa go forward about 20ft then turn left
6) Once you turn left, turn left again.
7) Go straight up towards the big building(you will go up a small incline)
8) As you reach the marketplace type area go to the left and head toward the end
9) You will see a door on the right just past the three NPC vendors.
10) Enter that door and you will be inside Reet's(I am not sure where to go from there... =\

I know the Fireplace room is just to the left as you enter. Go straight past it and follow the pathway, it will take you up and follow it to the left. From there you are on your own, hope this helps a lil... =)
