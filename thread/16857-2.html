quote:
Recharge Delay

This is the time the weapon uses between the shots, to do its "own" thing - to revolve the drum, refill the energy cells, cool down the propulsion chamber etc. etc. The most important aspect of Recharge Delay is that this is the period in which OTHER attacks can take place (Including use of items and Nanos). Although this delay stems from the item, it is still influenced by your initiative and Agg/Def stats.

Item Delay

This is the time you yourself use on the weapon between the shots - the time you use to recover from the recoil, **** the gun, get in position for the next thrust etc. etc. This time gets lower the better your initiative with the weapon is (Melee Initiative, Ranged Initiative or Physical Prowess Initiative for Martial Arts,).

The time between each shot is mainly decided by the sum of the two above factors, but also the Agg/Def - slider (and Reload time of the weapon).
