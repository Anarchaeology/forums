(ooc: fan story!) The Fallen Prophet
The Prologue

Never chase a lie. Let it alone, and it will run itself to death.
--Lyman Beecher

Rubi-Ka. December 25, 29476  Unicorn Tower, Omni-1  10:14am
Joshua looked out of the window; yet another Christmas had past. But it was only one of countless others. But he didnt care about it anymore. It was a silly holiday, at least to him. He still remembered the last one that meant something to him. It had snowed that day, and it was wonderful. Now there was nothing to celebrate, no one to give presents to, and no snow. Not that he cared about that either, actually. He didnt care about anything, and had not done so for many years. The nano bots in his body made sure of that as they kept his brainwaves and emotions under control. Nifty little devils. It was the basic taint of his people. People? That word was so wrong. His kind were not people. They were monsters, liars, and killers. Cold blooded killers. Yet, most people over the galaxy had never heard about what his kind had done to them, all those years ago. Nor did the people seem to care about it, or the fact that the biggest hyper-corporations were run by their nemeses. Omni-Tek, Sol Banking Perhaps even the ICC was controlled by one or more Omega. After their defeat the most powerful of them had established a new kind of control center, and were now battling each other. Roman, creator of Omni-Tek and the Omega. Their leader and father. The traitor Murderer. Too, there was Anna. Another Omega who survived the war. Joshua saw her as the possible leader of Sol Banking. It had to be her. And then there was David, the one who destroyed the Omega during the long war. Half Omega, half solitus. Who would ever have guessed that a half breed would lead the puny solitus to victory? No one did! And that had been their downfall.

Joshua smiled. Even though he was one of the first Omega, he had never been one of them, at least not to Anna. And perhaps she had been right. He had foolishly supported the Omega, created countless weapons and nano systems for them, and how had they thanked him? By killing his wife and daughter; shattering his heart and soul. And finally betraying him. But that is what they were good at. Betraying each other. Anna had left several Omega behind to be captured and executed by the solitus. Roman had done the same with the main board of the old company. Once they were useless, they had been removed. Joshua sat down at the computer again. He had been working on his journal for a long time, yet surprisingly he had not written much at all. He didnt know why, but he had a hard time concentrating on it. It could be the pure source of notum that was messing with the nano bots in his body. Not that he cared. He wanted to write his life storyand it was a long onebut he didnt have much time left.

They were coming for him, he was sure of it.

Joshua turned the monitor on and looked at the text. Most of it was mindless words. He had already written about how the Omega had awakened and enslaved the solitus, about the virus, and the Destruction that had led them here. What could he write now? He didnt have a list of the Omega who survived. Their total numbers? Only a guess. Besides himself, Anna, Roman and Davidwho had survived? Joshua shook his head. It didnt matter, their time would come, he was sure of that. Even for an Omega, death cannot be avoided forever.

He knew, because he had personally killed several of them, because of their betrayal. Joshua leaned back and looked at the mirror that he had put above the computer. His hair was grey, his skin pale. He had replaced an eye with an implant, just like most people on the planet did. His face was scarred and looked old. Joshua had the ability to control how old he wanted to look. He could, because he had created the bots necessary for the task. It had allowed him to survive, with different names and ages, yet he could not control the pale skin, which was the mark of an Omega. Some children who had seen him the other day had called him a living dead. It seemed to fit. He was dead, inside. Every glow of life, soul and feeling. He came to wonder how it all had started. Joshua smiled. Of course! It was a girl. What else? He could still see her face at times. She had been something special. Her eyes; her smile. Joshua began writing again, now he knew what to write in his journal. If anyone were to understand what had happened, they had to know the whole story. How it began The only memory he had ever wanted And lost

He had to go back, to Earth To her
