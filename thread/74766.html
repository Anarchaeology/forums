PvP crit suggestion ...
Since it's becaoming apparent that PvP isn't getting longer as FC had intended I have a suggestion re: crits in PvP.

1) For anyone using a melee weapon they are under the PvM rule (crit chance NOT nerfed). That is ANY profession using a fist or melee-style weapon (using physical and/or melee init's);

2) As soon as any profession switches to ANY ranged weapon (bow/shotgun etc) their crit chance goes to the current 50% rule.

My rationale for these suggestions is that melee professions should be feared at close ranged ... the stop-what-your-doing-and-run-to-zone type fear (with the exception of the Queen Blade because that weapon is just plain stupid to have it in-game as it is unchanged).
Because root nanos are such a major tradeoff I think it would be quite balanced. Nano Resist is such a fickle skill it can't be banked on.

As I have seen it for the most part melee classes (MAs being my experience) use bows etc. as a way of still doing damage when rooted. Their not really meant as a permenant weapon because with reloads and recharges their melee weapons have a far superior damage output. As I see it so should it be in PvP.

Ex. A:So an enforcer has the PvM rule applied to his/her/it's weapon(s). They get rooted and either wait/pray or switch to a nova/bow. If they switch to ranged their crit chance drops to the current 50%.

Ex. B: An MA gets rooted and switches to a bow (or starts a fight with one on) they drop from the PvM crit rule to the current 50% because they have equiped a ranged weapon. Nevermind that the bow uses Physical Init ... because it's ranged the crit chance drops (Most [not all] use bows for the alpha anyway).

Ex. C: A Melee Engi with Transferance gets rooted will hopefully be able to break the root and/or the one on their pet and continue.

This wouldn't effect Agents as they still have their 100% crit nano which would remain unchanged. But Traders (rooter), Crats (rooter/pet), MP (not sure about MPs sorry), Advens, Soldiers etc would have their crit chance running @ 50% because of any ranged weapons equiped.

If an MP or Trader is using Fists or another melee weapon then their crit chance is on the PvM rule until such time as they equip a ranged weapon.

Ammendments? Flames?
