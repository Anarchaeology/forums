Hi Viz,

You are correct in assuming we would want the largest player base to see the more storyline impacting events. This would allow more people to know what happened and be able to spread the word. But, please do not assume that means you will never be able to see a major event at a reasonable hour. As I mentioned earlier, conflict does not have a schedule. Conflict just happens.

One thing we do understand, being players ourselves, is that it is very frustrating to hear a large event happened but only be able to hear a partial story from friends or guildmates. We are working on ways to change this and make the correct or maybe even politically/faction tainted event information available to everyone that is willing to look for it.
