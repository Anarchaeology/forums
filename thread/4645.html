Omni 1 is the city that is made up by the zones of Omni Ent, Omni Trade, and Omni HQ.

Omni 2 is the city that Omni -Tek vacated when it became unprofitable to run. It is now called Athen, and is made up by the zones Old Athen, Athen West, and War torn valley (sort of).

Rome is made up by the zones Rome Blue, Rome Red, and Rome Green.

Other cities are Borealis (neutral), Tir (Clan), Newland (neutral), and Camelot (Clan). These cities are all single zone areas.

Hope this helps.
