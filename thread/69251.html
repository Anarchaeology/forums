Mission Areas
I dont know about the rest of you, but the current mission zone are boring. I would like to see a mission that insead of being a cave, complex or a small flat closed area, be a section of a city.

You could enter a mission, and have a small city areas to explore and fight in, complete with streets, buildings, alleys, maybe sewers etc. This would be much the same as now, but with an open sky, stairs, non combat mobs etc.

Or for the wilderness, its a Valley, or a forest, or a volcanic crater. Sections could be blocked off by rocks, ferns, trees etc.

You could have different styles of mission to reflect the area. A mission in Home would look like a section of Home, a mission in Omni Ent, would actually look like OMni Ent.

Imagine entering a mission and being on a street in Omni Ent slums. There could be several buildings to enter, a sewer to explore, NPCs to talk with and open sky above you. Elevators or stairs could take you to different lvls of buildings. You could have differnt areas of a mission to explore, while looking for you objective. The item you are looking for could be in a Weapon Shop, guarded by shop security (the entire mission wouldnt be the weapon shop, but rather the section of the city with the shop in it), it could be held by a gang of thugs hiding in the sewers (again, the mission wouldnt be all sewers, but a section of city that has access to the sewers).

The missions would remain the same, but with better looking scenery and some more interaction. Also, add in some elements of danger, like acid pools that actually hurt, lava pools, traps in the floor, security systems etc.

There would still be the same number of mobs, still have areas like rooms (or rooms if you are in a city) to prevent alot of aggros like in hunting.

I know its probably impossible, but it would sure be nice to see the sky in a mission, to actually do more than run around in the same rooms doing the same thing
