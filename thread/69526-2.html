Phear Leet
Well, Funcom basically is a tiny bit disturbed by Clicksaver, mostly because of server load from constant rapid mission generation (which caused them to patch to limit mission generation speed), but so far isn't actively hostile to it.

It is, however, a violation of the EULA (indirectly, slightly), as the EULA requires you to obey the 'Rules of Conduct' posted on the Funcom website, which includes:

9. You will not attempt to interfere with, hack into, or decipher any transmissions to or from the servers running Anarchy. You are however allowed to use status pages provided by Funcom.

In short, any program that 'hooks' into AO to read the packet stream is a violation. No matter what it does.

Right now, Funcom doesn't seem to 'mind' Clicksaver.

Should they get a new lawyer, or a new CEO, or some other employee change happens in the chain of command, this could change overnight.

Unlikely, but possible. Still, at least Funcom is far better about this sort of thing than Verant.
