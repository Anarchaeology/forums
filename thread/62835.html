Agent PvP idea.
Remember back in the old days when we were in missions we could conceal and if spotted we could just conceal again? Yeah that was probably a bit overpowered; however, now we have a penalty where we cant conceal while under attack. The second penalty is the 10(?) second rule. If we kept this second penalty would it still be overpowering to be able to conceal in a PvP fight even though we are under attack?

We still would incur the problems as far as becoming unconcealed by doing virtually anything. We also would have the 10 second rule which would eliminate shoot, hide, shoot, hide tactics. Further, we would still be susceptible to perception checks.

I see this as a good escaping tactic, which has many more chances of being overcome(perc checks, area nukes, etc.) in comparasin to a fixer evac nano. Thoughts?

-arsiine

P.S. oh and for those who give the arguement of, "its not realistic to just all the sudden disapear." I say, how realistic is it for two people to be shooting at eachother with shotguns at a range of 1 meter for over 2 mins?
