OFFICIAL CLAN ANARCHIST SYNDICATE PRESS RELEASE


CAS Position On Dust Brigade

By Shawnta Clancy - Sunday 19 Jun 29476

CAS Leadership Reaffirms Position on Dust Brigade

Clan members, Omni-Tek employees, and Neutrals,

Dust Brigade (DB). Everyone has heard of them by now. A mystery organization, whose objectives are unknown. Speculation abounds on their motives, leadership, funding, and everything else that can be imagined. Miir probably speculates on the current state of fashion in DB. Anyone who claims to have full details on their operations and organization is either a master liar or is working for them. Despite the claims of many, CAS leadership is just as much in the dark about DB as anyone else.


Many seem to believe for various reasons that CAS works with DB on operations. There are even some who believe that Commander Redruum actually is the head of DB. Even though Commander Redruum has denied these allegations till he is blue in the face, some just can't rise above petty su****ions and in fighting. CAS leadership has decided therefore to issue a statement regarding their official stance on DB. The statement is comprised of three points:

Official CAS position on the Dust Brigade guild
1. CAS has never undertaken joint operations with DB and does not have any intentions of doing so in the future.
2. CAS does not condone unprovoked attacks on neutrals, other Clan guilds, innocent civilians or their property by the DB.
3. While CAS does not cooperate with the DB, CAS does not condemn any guild or organization for attacking valid enemy military targets.

CAS leadership and members are very tired of the same old groundless accusations and insults hurled at them because it was once mentioned that CAS had no problem with DB attacking Omni. This mind you had little to do with the terrorist bombing in Omni-1. CAS issued a press release at the time CONDEMNING the act. It was never completely proven that DB was without a doubt, behind the attack anyway. People just assumed that it was true. CAS leadership has decided to try and clear the air and restate once again that CAS and DB have not ever worked together in the past and have no plans to do so in the future. Any questions regarding this statement may be addressed to Laticia "Nelida" Shone, CAS advisor and Ambassador to the Neutrals. Long live the Clans!
