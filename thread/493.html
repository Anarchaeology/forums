Ideas for better bag management
Easier bag management. Who wouldn't want that?
Here are my ideas.

1. Customizable name.
All us to tag a bag with a customizable name. If we check the bad description, it shows both the customizable name and the real name of the actual bag.

2. Bag description also lists contents.
Being able to have the bag contents listed in the item description would be wonderous. Here's a sample of what I'm thinking of:

-----
*GUN BAG
Small Backpack

Contents:
L33T Pistol (QL200)
MK499 Pistol (QL54)
-----

This improvement alone would make for:
1. Better item management.
2. Safer trade.
3. More enjoyable gameplay.

Thoughts?
