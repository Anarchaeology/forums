More Symbio-grafts for fixers
I think the game needs more symbio-grafts. For those newbies who don't know what they are, they're those little devices u stick on your wrist and allows you to run the nano program that it specifies and they are one of the very few things we can hack. Since we fixers have so much trouble with our nanos, why not just hack them then? With more symbio-grafts we could have access to other proffesion's nanos (yea, just like agents except more in a hackery way).

But then of course you could put restrictions on the differnt types of nanos we could use. Same rules as agents, in which you can't use all the MP's pets or blah blah blah.

some points though:
  • No general buff symbio-grafts
  • Lesser cost on nano reqs. than the original nanos
  • Tradeable (well I don't know bout that)
  • Must only use B&E to hack. No other skills.
  • Easier access to low ql nano symbio-grafts vs. the high ones
  • Symbio-grafts in stores
  • Shorter time to be able to cast


Our nanos aren't that great, we can barely use them, it's already in game, Funcom doesn't have a clue as to what to do with us so just give us access to more nanos.
