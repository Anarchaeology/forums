Announcing new Atlantean clanner org: Section 9
The people of section 4 and section 9 have decided that now is the time to come out of the quiet and fulfill the mission of the Council of Truth and the Council of Aries.

In now what is distant history, the COT were in control of the clans. They attempted to create better lives for all people for those who believe in human rights. Humans of all species have inalienable rights to life, and self determination. Omni Corp has stood in clear violation of these rights by using humans as assets, Rubi Ka as an exclusive domain. Corporate philosophy sees these people and land as nothing more than a profit making machine. Most of the galaxy has long ago given up this idea of humans as assets, or as the more unfamiliar term is applied, Slavery. The people of Rubi Ka has been used as slaves, unable to choose their place of work, the conditions of their work, w either they can leave the company, and indeed be ordered to their death, or deaths. Standing against the slavery of Omni and the clear violation of human rights, the COT strove to make a legitimate government.

One of the mandates of the COT was to create a representational government, rather than allowing the spread of feudalism in the clans. Too many clans have become havens of thugs, acting as private armies without a clear mandate from the people. This has unfortunately pr oven that they are no better than Omni in its view of citizens.

Great hope abounded when the COT was finally realized. The average citizen on the street felt that they would now be free. Many came forward to assist in this goal. But this created the problem that the COT had was too many volunteers. It was decided that forming all parts of a government would appear as terrorism, and that the people were not in place to form government services. So as people showed up at the COT building to volunteer, they were organized into sections and started to form ministries that would grow to take its place in the government. Some of these sections were intended to deal with diplomacy, immigration, revenue, social services, military services, even as mundane as road building and street cleaning. No official names were given to these organizations as their existence was hidden as to prevent undue attention. The councilors felt that if these sections were publicized Omni would use it as a leverage to declare the COT as terrorists. So people often stayed in other organizations as they continued to work for the ideals of the COT.

Two groups of volunteers stayed on course. Section 4 was devised as a research branch, seeking new forms of equipment, medical equipment and technologies that improve the life of clanners. One notable success of this group was contributing the the development of Clan Anarchist Syndicate armor, as some of the members were affiliated with that organization during its creation. This section desired to create and provide for not just civilian but also military life, to support and train members of the army in waiting, which at that time was called section 9.

Section 9 was designed to be an elite, well provisioned team of military and police personal able to do any of the wishes of the COT. The COT realized that a military would be necessary to protection of settlements from human and non human invaders. Many of the members of this section stayed in other orgs during the dark time including Airhalk Alliance, Confederation Helvetica, Shadow wolf delegation, The Dust brigade and others. There were even some neutrals who had not yet joined the clans, but had seen the value of this arrangement. Section 9 would be trained and equipped by Section 4, but also self reliant on the helpfulness and tutelage of members inside the org.

It was the idea of the founders to create a system whereby volunteering would not only give you opportunities for service, but also expectations for support. It was decided that volunteers would not have to ask, or to play the social game with the leaders of the org to get the equipment that was necessary to do their jobs. At certain levels, agreements of service between higher ranking and lower ranking personnel to analyze combat techniques, equipment layout and personal development.

Unfortunately, the COT was disbanded in mysterious circumstances. Many of the sections melted back into other orgs and into their daily lives. The flame had died, and the heart of the spirit of freedom once again darkened.

So, fast forward to the present. Jobe has opened. All eyes across the galaxy are straining to see the mysterious shadowleet and to discover the Shadowland mysteries. The people of section 4 and section 9 who have remained quiet have decided that now is the time to reveal themselves, to start up the process that was first described by the COT. As the public and press are distracted, we now reveal ourselves.

The time has come to form our military and other civil services that stands for all clanners, not for specific warlords. The time may have come to show the products of the years of quiet research. Section 9 is now calling previous and new volunteers to service. We are looking to expand our ranks to thwart the present trend to factionalism in the clans. We offer not only opportunities for socializing, but planned raids, planned support for each member, and raids to get you the equipment you need to excel. But more importantly we have plans for your development, help from higher level characters, and an expectation that you will help create other fighters in your org. We have expectations of both service and reward. Please contact one of the generals if you interested in enhancing your life on RK, want help others, and feel that now is the time to stand up for our freedom...

CONTACT US TODAY, FIGHT FOR YOUR FREEDOM

Charter of rights and responsibilities
Our org is developing a charter of rights and responsibilities.

This flies in the face of most orgs, where lower players have to beg from higher players, hope someone notices or play the favoritism game to get the equipment they need.

The process we are developing will ensure that each character who behaves according to guild standards will get their equipment.

For example, we intend to take every character who has served at least ten levels in the org, reached a certain level, and participated in a guild teaming event, these characters will get taken to the SOM and have neleb killed for them at least 3 times in order to get their neleb's cloak.

We are other items will include, cash, carbon armour, yalms, and help with the Dodga quests.

We are still in the early phaze of developing this charter of rights and responsibilities.

But, if you are interested in joining the org, we are presently recruiting.

contact one of the recruiters in our org for an interview if you would like to join.
