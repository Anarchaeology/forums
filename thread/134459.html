A new arrival
In an attempt to get into character a little I have started a little background on my character Aloque. A recent addition to the RK-1 population. A slightly melancholic man, Aloque is seeing little brightness in the future.

Rubi-Ka, an exciting place to be. Rubi-Ka, where death isnt fatal. Rubi-Ka the basis for the most advanced nano-tech in the universe. All that and more. The truth, however, is somewhat more grim then this. But then again isnt it always?

Rubi-Ka is home to millions of opportunistic bastards, most of them would kill their mothers for a few ounces of Notum, fortunately, most of them even knew their mothers. Because death isnt fatal on this world, people have taking to killing en mass. Killing for entertainment, for profit, for about everything one can think of. Rubi-Ka, hell on the mortal plane. Yet Rubi-Ka is the only place I can live.

My name is Aloque, or at least it is the name that I took. Rubi-Ka is my home, against my wishes I might add. But I am of the Homo Nano breed, so there is little choice for me. The choice I did have was where to go. Where to settle. And even tough I was bred by Omni-Tek, their concrete, glass and steel towers have no attraction for me. And rather to join the neutrals and become a rebel without a clue, I settled for the Clans and became a rebel without a cause.

My body still aches from reconstruction, I ended up in a garden of sorts, without any money or means to support myself, I am forced to take up vermin control to make a few credits. And even tough an announcement was made that the Leets where not dangerous, and should be left alone, the bounty on their tags is good money. Ill work here, for now.

OOC : The story continues a bit further.

It has been some time now. My research is starting to pay of a bit. It seems that sub infusion theory is sound. A new form of greasing the conduits used by the user to direct nanos might be found. It is unfortunately that some people were so reluctant to cooperate.

How did I come to be here? That was somewhat strange, Ill admit. My mind was firmly set on not going with Omni, their control over my research would be overbearing to say the least. So I worked on my theory of sub infusion in minimal accommodations in Tir. I was short of funding very soon. Hunting vermin wasnt exactly bringing in large amounts of money. And even the processing of raw materials found on my forages through the city and countryside were barely able to keep my supplies up to date. Then it started happening, a few choice deposits into my account, a few thousand at first, then more. Thank something I didnt grow su****ious. I continued with experiments, trying to prove that sub infusion technology could work, that it would work.

Something blew, whether it was me not taking safety measures to seriously or the equipment being faulty, I do not know. I do remember feeling my arms evaporate and then the world went black. When I opened my eyes again I was in a strange building, my nano cloud was hardly active at all, cut off I felt. Then they started to explain it to me.

I wear a suit now, no longer the rough shirts and breeches of a clan rogue. I have a comfortable, clean and efficient laboratory at my disposal. I see the truth now. The rebels care for nothing but themselves. They would never allow my research to continue.

My research is progressing as planned. Fields tests will commence in the near future. I am where I belong.

Omni-Tek is my friend.
