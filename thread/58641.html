Notum Wars Teaser analysis
Yes, it looks interesting and now we're getting a bit more information it could shape up to be pretty funky.

I've been running the teaser, looking at the details it contains and I've got some queries.

First up, orbital facility. Is this just eye candy - possible intro sequence for NW, the new character creation location (airport longue, as someone coined it?) or an actual feature?

Skip the character creation bit, past the girl with the fast forward hair style...

...and into the arrivals lounge. Note, on the right, what appears to be a full size whompa map and some bizarre kind of brown statue with wings/arms???

As we skim thru the lounge area, we head towards what I assume is the actual alignment departure area, past the exit sign above.

Fast forward thru a day/night cycle in Tir (?) and follow a Yalm flightpath over possibly Omni Ent and a clan/neutral city. Bleeds into a brief shot of Omni Trade.

And this is where it gets interesting...

In this shot, there is a craft descending the verticle, towards the whompas in trade. The next shot shows the flat discs carried by the statues in Trade which flank the exit to Harry's, etc. Perched on these discs are a number of craft.

From the angle, it looks like they're facing away from us. Twin, possible quad engined gull wing vehicles, with what appears to be some kind of container slung below them, grasped in arms beneath these craft.

So far, this is what intrigues me the most. What are these vehicles???

At first guess, it's more eye candy, but I hope not. They look like cargo haulers, but so far we have nothing worth hauling in bulk...
unless you consider the idea that they are the components of the notum towers.

Either way, they look interesting and I look forward to the arrival of these craft over the skies of Rubi Ka.

Incidentally, there is another craft which appears to be flying over the towers in this shot, travelling right to left, diagonally across the screen. From the shape of it, it's one of the floating blobs found hanging over Harry's and Omni Trade.

Next we get a shot of a Notum tower in operation, then finally treated to a glimpse of the new Yalms/ground vehicles. Personally, I'm not that keen on them yet - perhaps it'll grow on me.

Overall, I'm left with more questions than answers, but hopefully as time goes on, we'll get more information on the content.

Looking forward to this much more than I was now... a possible convert.
