Profession Specialization Is Disappearing
Im noticing a disturbing trend in Anarchy Online lately. Originally, the game was all about teaming and getting buffs from various professions that werent available to you. Martial Artists would cast critical hit buffs while enforcers would cast hit point boosters, etc., etc. Each of these professions specialized in a category i.e. healer, tank, nuker, etc. The team was better as a unit this way. Funcom is ruining this game by making more and more nano-programs self-only. For example, take the new line of Adventurer nanos that buff multi-ranged and multi-melee. These are self-only buffs. What about the soldier who, God forbids, decides to go with dual-wielding shotguns or MG/SMGs rather than the standard NovaFlow? You would think that the soldier would be pretty good at dual-wielding ranged weapons since that is his specialization. Soldiers should have their own multi-range buffs. What about enforcers and dual-wielding swords or bats or whatever?! They should also have their own multi-melee buff. They should be able to cast it on others as well. It only makes sense this way.

Next, lets discuss some of the new line of nanos coming out for Adventurers and Martial Artists in the 14.4 patch. The MA is getting a crowd control or mez nano. WHY?! Although I play a Martial Artist and think this would be cool for me in solo missions, it goes against the specialization theory discussed above. And dont get me started on Adventurers!! They are going to be a one-man wrecking crew after 14.4 is released. They are getting WAY TOO MUCH LOVIN! Adv get special weapons and armor, every line of nano practically available to all professions in the game to include mez, heal, nukes, etc. Not to mention morphing, damage buffs, and hit point buffs. Should we expect the next patch to have a new line of Enforcer nukes? If this is the case, then just merge all professions into one and get it over with.

So what I want to know is; wheres the profession specialization?! There is less and less reason to team and even less reason to play.

All of our new buffs (for the most part) have some -major- problems with them.. I'll even go into details about said problems!

Pistol/1hE/multi-wield buffs: Self only, which is ironic since they are only found in team missions. Multi-wield should be self only, IMHO, but the others should not. The big problem with these, however, is that they have LEVEL REQUIREMENTS TO CAST. Not just PM and SI, but a LEVEL requirement. Stupid idea on Funcom's part, trying to nerf a nano before it exists, and another way to do what you're fearing.. get rid of helpful buffs from other team members (forget about getting a mochams to cast those nanos).

(Morph Nanos) Team Wolf Auras: I've looked at these... they're not super. However, they are -team- enhancing, which you should be all over. They are neat, and I like the idea. Cannot (obviously) be stacked with other morph nanos.

(Morph Nanos) Solo-Cat nanos: These are damn nice for the solo adventurer. Short duration damage buff that increases aggro-def and attack/damage skills.. slightly. Cooldown time of 2-3 minutes... much harsher than the MA destruction line. Self-only insta-heals used every 30 secs. Nice... but again, LEVEL REQUIREMENTS!

(Morph Nanos) Pit Lizard: Oh no.... not nukes?! Not a 7k HP buff!? You do realize that there are 7 people in the whole game who can cast these nanos, right? 7 Adventurers in the level 190+ range across ALL servers... not a whole lot to work with here.

Secondary Damage Shields: GOOD. Advents were meant to be damage shield kings... self-only is again BAD.

Leet Charms: Uhm.................... uh.............. they're worthless? They're cool, yes, but the leet doesn't zone with you, making the use of the nano limited to the backyard zones. Dumb. :\ Should have been Summon Leet instead.

Night Vision removed from Pit Lizard and Wolf.. YAY!

Fly mode disabled from Pit Lizard: This was a slap in the face. Granted, very few advents are ever able to cast this morph... but this is basically Funcom saying "We're lazy, so instead of fixing bugs, we're going to remove the functionality. Have a nice day!" Anyone remeber Encumbrance? Yea...

New Deflection Shields/Flurry of Blows: Mixed bag. We really don't know -anything- about these yet. And, I am really upset by their introduction, as they are doing nothing but pushing people farther from being pistol-adventurers.

Crowd Control: most worthless line I've ever seen. We can now mez monsters... but ONLY if they are not attacking! When's the last time a crat said "hold on guys, I'm gonna mez some stuff so it doesn't attack us?" No, mezzes are used to control adds.

Along those lines, Adventurers were promised crowd control a LONG time ago, and we get this... which is rather disappointing.

At any rate, you see my point. Adventurers don't get a TON of cool stuff... they get some interesting things, but not amazing. And every single one of their nanos (except the lowest level ones) are team mission only.. which is stupidity. Also, as I mentioned on a couple, EVERY new advent nano has a level requirement! EVERY ONE! This is a terrible idea, and again, it really hurts what you complain about... the lack of any good team help. I feel for Fixers, too, because their new nanos also have level requirements... as do some of the highest level Agent nanos in 14.2.

Frankly, I agree with you, sort of. Profession Specialization is making a -strong- comeback, but the unity is what's disappearing. MPs not being able to give an advent or fixer Mochams, Adventurers not being able to give a doc the pistol buffs (or another Advent!)... I agree. But PLEASE stop *****ing about Adventurers. I'm so sick of people whining about a class that's finally getting fixed. Unless you play on the test server and can show me that Advents are now uber, I can't and won't believe it.

Adventurers were supposed to be every class rolled into one, to a small degree... but don't say our "mez, heal, nukes, etc" are tearing other classes apart. The fact that the mezzes are 99% useless, the heals are still well below a doc's (which I'm not complaining about.. they should be), and the nukes will be cast by almost no one, there really is nowhere to go here. It's exactly this kind of attitude that gets classes nerfed into oblivion that don't need it, much like MAs did back in beta. Need we mention the Freedom Arms nerf? Or the Concealment nerf? Yea......
