That's not true, a twinked ma is better than a twinked soldier from level 1-60 hands down if you do it right. My level 52 MA twink has lost to 1 soldier in her range, and I've killed 50 or 60, once I killed 1 level 63 and one level 61 in a single fight.

After level 60 the concensus is MAs suck at PVP.

MA twinks suggestion:

Get good implants, Brawl, MA, Dimach and nano skills (they are light blue and you need a lot of them).

Don't use a weapon

Equip the highest sub ql100 BOP as soon as you can, then the lowest ql BwT after that. I see BwTs starting around ql60.

Max your evades, keep focused on your nano skills, heal esp.

Keep your run speed up, and buff your ma and brawl skill all you can.
