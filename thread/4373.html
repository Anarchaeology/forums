Overequipping and viable weapons
With the gameplay changes in the past few months, over 90% of the weapons in game are not viable at mid to high levels.

The was AC absorption is handled makes quick, low damage weapons are completely worthless. Certain mob types are unkillable unless you use a slow, high damage weapon.

My level 66 Enforcer has to use a QL125-130 sword to take down even level enforcer/soldier/advent mobs.


A player who uses pistols has to massively overequip to be effective. I have a pistol Soldier who was using a QL49 Minieletroniums at level 25 (self equipped) but could do nothing higher than minimum damage against even level or higher humanoid enf/sol/advent mobs. Against advents, their damage shields were doing more damage than the pistols that were double QL.
After switching to QL58 Sentinels ETE Strike Guns, my pistol soldier could handle resonable level missions.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Take a look around at players over level 50.
Almost all of them are using the same weapons.

This is not a choice, it's a requirement.

Over level 80:
An Enforcer must use a Beam, Hammer or Rider Execs to be effective.
A Fixer must use a Mausser that is 30-50QLs higher to be effective.
A Soldier must use a Novaflow that is 30-50 QLs higher to be effective.
Any Advent that uses anything but Rider Execs is going to have a hard time soloing anything.


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I would like to see Armor Class absorb a percentage of damage (not a set amount).

I would like to see a better balancing of mob professions (crat/fixer mobs are too easy, soldier/enf/advent mobs are incredibly difficult).






One thing I used to like about AO is that there was such a variety in character appearance. Now it seems that every player over level 100 looks like the same cookie cutter (decus/nano coat and nova/mausser/hammer/beam/pillow) design.

Skill based damage...
I read the post suggesting that your skill could determine the damage done by a weapon - to 'fix' overequiping.

That's fine with me...but shouldn't it work BOTH ways???

I mean, if you OVEREQUIP a weapon and it does LOWER than the stated damage because your skill is below the requirement, then the opposite should take effect.

By UNDEREQUIPING, I mean using a weapon with a lower requirement than your skill, the damage output from that weapon should be HIGHER than the stated damage(because you REALLY know how to use it).

This would fix A LOT of issues. For example, by underquiping a pistol you would guarantee doing a much higher MINIMAL damage than stated in the weapon description. If your skill is high enough, maybe you ALWAYS to MAXIMUM DAMAGE of the weapon.

Heck, if I had a choice between OVEREQUIPING a weapon that does (1-200) damage vs UNDEREQUIPING a weapon that can guarantee the maximum damage all the time of (95), I would probably go with UNDERQUIPING.

This could be a 'fix' or 'balancing' pistol wielders have been asking for.

Yes, I have FULL of ideas...come on FC, maybe you can hire me as a beta tester.
