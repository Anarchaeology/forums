Trybes walked down the Omni-Ent streets in the brisk morning air, the cleaning crews and a few early commuters shuffling off to work. For Trybes work was the farthest thing on his mind. All he wanted to do was sit his comfortable booth in Rompa's and sip at some coffee.
A quick shadow moved out of the corner of his eye, he stopped breifly turning to look in the movements direction.

Nothing.

He rubbed his tired eyes. "Damn Roller-Rats, stay in the sewers and Tir where you belong." Trybes started to climb the ramp to Rompa's when a small opifex fixer with a Vector Blue badge suddemly appeared in front of him.

"Hello there Mr. Clyburn. Lovely morning no?.. what's that behind you?" the fixer pointed over his sholder to which in turn he twisted his head to look.

As he turned he felt a rather nasty pick on his neck where she had struck him with a surgical needle. "What the?!"

"Just getting a sample, sir.. good day" In anflurry of nano bots she vanished into the grid, leaving Trybes stunned and bleeding slightly from the small wound in his neck.

"Why the hell would they want my blood sample?"
