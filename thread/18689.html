On Team Play - Part One
I couldn't find a comedy forums anywhere.. but we can all go ahead and laugh together=D

Healer - This person heals the team, and tries to make sure that nobody dies during the fight. The most effective healer is the DOCTOR, but the Adventurer and Martial Artist are not far behind, especially if they have specialised in this area. The DOCTOR also has Team Heal programs, which can heal everybody in the team. Traders can heal the team by sacrificing their own health. Fixers and Doctors also have Heal over Time (HoTs) programs, which regenerate your health as time passes. A Meta-Physicist Healing-Pet is a good additional healer. If there are multiple healers in the team, it is often a good idea that they decide between them which team members they have the main responsibility for.
