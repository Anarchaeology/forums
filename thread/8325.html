Items are far too affective!
Now, many of you are coming here to say "hey I cant do enough damage with {gun name} already!" OR "hey! I already have to overequip armor to survive!" For those of you saying this just read on and think on my post for a couple seconds, then post your opinions and/or flames as usual =)

Alright, now, I'm going to lean my attention back towards EQ, yes the highly addictive downtime filled time waster that it was... but it did have some very slick and great things about it!

The PvP was fairly un-balanced between some of the classes, and the game had a slower pace to it in many situations. BUT, the items, while important, weren't everything in that game. A player with increadibly high powered gear in that game... way over his lvl, could still get significantly beat up by another character... especially if the other was higher lvl than him. How did it work this way?... well, the game was more based on skills, even if you had great items, extra hitpoints, good buffs... when it all comes down to it, you weren't able to use that item to its "full potential" if you were of a low lvl for it, and your total Armor would not be what it actually said on the piece of armor, and you would not be able to hit for huge amounts just because your weapon said it could.

Now don't get me wrong, having incredible gear was a good thing, and did give you a decent amount of help... it wasn't everything like it is in AO... EQ had a much better system for how your character uses and wears armor and weapons...

Quite frankly, if they made the bandages heal your character past 1/2 life in EQ, and if they had something that worked in a similar way to boost your mana... I would drop AO quicker than if someone put a pile of dog crap in my hand when my eyes were closed.... but as it is EQ has too much down time for my preference. I just wish AO would rework their overequiping system to help make it not quite so one-sided when dealing with heavy twinks.

(don't get me wrong, I am not one of the lasy bastards that doesn't even try to overequip or anything... I am doing what everyone does... but it is hard when you have no cash because you don't have a 100+ character to hand down any goods... and EQ made it so that if you weren't a twink, you could still put up a fairly decent fight (although you were usually going to have a much harder time if you werent twinked))

Ok, I'm done.
