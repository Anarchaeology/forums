Omni and Clan goals compatible
It occurs to me that fighting on Rubi-Ka is pointless. The clans say they want freedom, Omni-Tek wants notum. Those two things are totally compatible.

Why cant the clans form a seperate govenment or several clan states, mine all the notum they want, but sell it to Omni-Tek for export? Currently the cost of war and disruption of notum trade for OT must be more than any increased costs they would have to pay the clan government for the notum they mine.

Why not form a new treaty similar to the Tir Accords simply stating that OT would guarantee a cease fire and non-intervention into clan territory and governemnt in exchange for exclusive notum export rights at reasonable rates based on the current market and a guarantee from both sides that any hostilities by radical factions would be dealt with accordingly? Hostilities by radical terrorists of either faction could be dealt with by a coalition of clan and omni forces.

Currently unless I am mistaken, OT controls all import and export to Rubi-Ka with the exception of smugglers. I have read in several sources that OT has a starship blockade of the planet preventing rival corporations gaining access to the planet. So whats is being done with all the notum the clanners are mining? Is it stockpiled, just sitting there gathering dust when it could be sold for a tremendous profit to Omni-Tek?

Wouldnt a treaty ensuring an end to hostilities be beneficial for both factions? For the clans it would mean not having to fight for every freedom they desire. For Omni-Tek it would mean an increase in profits since they would no longer have to fund a war, even if they had to pay the clans for notum.

There are obviosly those that fight simply to fight, and those that are to extreme. Shouldnt those groups be subdued in the intrest of peace on Rubi-Ka?

The reason that Omni-Tek can not allow the Clans' occupation to continue is because Rubi-Ka is too precious a resource and the Clans are a failure as a society. Rubi-Ka has become the center of the universe, with the discovery of Notum and its proven, and more importantly, untold potential uses in the development of humanity. The cost to humanity's progression is too high to risk such things to the selfish goals of a minority splinter population.

Omni-Tek did attempt, in fashion, what you propose. Omni-Tek signed treaties and attempted peace with the Clans through the one force they, the Clans, could gather in agreement. The Clans failed in that respect. The Council of Truth could not control thier own members, and fell apart due to the Clan's inherent instability. The Clans are lead by individuality, self expression, self worth, and self gain, and because of this selfish purpose, they can not control themselves. Omni-Tek employees repeatedly were the victims of Clan Terrorists and there was no "guarantee from both sides that any hostilities by radical factions would be dealt with accordingly" proven. The Clans failed to follow through. They cannot police themselves and we as a universal corporate entity, and leader in human society, can not risk the attempt again. The cost to humanity's continued development is too great.

The Clans fail because they can not see past themselves. While many see Omni-Tek as cold and uncaring, we are simply seeing the "big picture." We are not after our own agendas. We understand how important our place is in the universe and we serve the greater purpose. That of progressing the company and humanity with the development of this most prescious resource, Rubi-Ka. We gave humanity immortality. We have made the impossible, commonplace. We can take humanity to wonders beyond imagination, if the Clans would simply understand how important it is that we work together for a common goal, under the leadership of Omni-Tek.
