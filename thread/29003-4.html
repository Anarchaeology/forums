Okay... Old thread possibly, but new infos....

MP, Crat, and Engy mobs now come equipped with a new toy: PETS. To make soloing easier they will only spawn that pet if there is one attacker.

Its confusing in team missions. Sometimes the pet will spawn a pet, other times it won't. There is a trick to it... if there is ONE person in the room then it won't spawn the pet. In other words if your calmer runs ahead to calm a room with multiple pet class mobs, then following the calmer into that room results in twice as many mobs to deal with and usually in one dead calmer.

If your calmer goes up into the boss room alone to calm the mobs do NOT go up and try to save the calmer from themselves. I've been killed because I'd go up to calm the adds and had the tank or doc follow behind me thinking I needed them to save me from myself. Since I die they think they obviously made the right decision.

Calmer, it is your job to tell the team to stay down and don't follow if you are going up there for that purpose. Don't assume the team will stay put if you tell them "brb" and then use the boss button. They won't. It is also your job to make sure to max out your Body Dev and have Max Health clusters, and get HP buffs if you are going to be suicidal like that. Absorb sheilds are also a good bonus, get the Enf or the NT to cast one on you.

I rarely get killed for going up into the boss room to calm the adds. The few times I have been killed it was due to:
1) 2 Real Mean Enf mobs, an Enforcer boss and alot of lag, or
2) 2 Engy mobs and a Pet class mob and having that second person enter the boss room after I have aggro.

Learn it though: 1 Character in room results in NO pets. 2 Pet mob classes, and 2 characters in room at all the way up to mid-levels could very well result in a dead calmer.

I need to verify if having 1 character in the room with the mob and everyone else attacking the mob from a different room will result in no pets. If it does, Great! If it doesn't Oh well, as long as the puller is good, pets are just a minor annoyance before the boss room.

My squishy nanomage NT has been solo calming QL180-199 Boss rooms since 120 without Nullity and living. It is possible, it just takes a bit of care, practice, and a slight bit of patience from the rest of the team.

Completely agree with you Borrace.

An adventurer even WITHOUT a fixer's HoT or with help from an MA/2nd adventurer can play as a "Doc".

I'll mention a few things I learned and tricks I picked up while bringing up my lil adventurer.

Use single target heals as much as possible. Team heals work just fine at sub level 50, but as you get higher they drop drastically in their effectiveness to almost 50% effective as a single target heal. Even so, team heals still have their place if you know when to use them. If you haven't quite determined whos being attacked, team heal and use the recharge time to figure it out. If you get an add, team heal, it will give your team time to do whats necessary to adjust to the situation and save whoevers been attacked by it. If the mob you're fighting has a particularly high damage shield, use a team heal once for about every 3 single target heals to your meatshield just to keep everyone at full health in case the mob should switch targets.

An /assist macro is extremely helpful if you're an adventurer filling in as the healer for a team. Not only will /assist help you in attacking the same mob as the meatshield, it can be used on the mob to see who IT is attacking so you can instantly know if the mob switches aggro to someone else. Since you'll generally be using single target heals, its very important to know WHO needs to be healed NOW. Especially if its one of your teammates who will get splattered in one or two hits.

Buy the nano "Ego Pummel" from the general nano shop. Since you're an adventurer, you'll most likely be #2 on the mobs hatelist from chainhealing. If for whatever reason your meatshield is in dire straits, use Ego Pummel on the mob and you'll more than likely draw aggro on to yourself. This will buy some time for him to sit and use a treatment kit or for you to throw an extra heal on him before he regains aggro. If you are 75+ and can use Grinning Hunter with Minor Lick Wounds this works great. You can heal yourself with it and throw that extra single target heal on your meatshield at the same time(just be sure to MLW first as it has no recharge and normal heal does).

Don't forget your first aid skill. Adventurers get decently light first aid as a skill, as well as some first aid buffs that will stack with first aid expertise. Use your first aids to help extend your nano pool just a little bit more. If you know the fight is going to be a long one, use it the second it will top off your nanopool which is usually after the first or second heal. This way it will be ready again hopefully right before you completely run out and will need to use a nano charger. You can also use first aid healing stims on your meatshield. Just be careful when your meatshield is an enforcer, sometimes it is better to use a nano first aid so they can Mongo which will give them a short duration hot. In most cases Mongo will heal much more than your first aid heal would have.

And like Borrace said, if theres an NT in the team, always keep HE and and any nano cost reducers running. MP's also get a nano cost reducer so be sure to ask them. Also you may want to carry around the next healing nano you will be able to use. If you have an MP in the team you can get nanoskill buffs and possibly upload the crystal and use a better than usual healing nano.

Probably the most crucial thing is just to know your limits. In general "your limit" is going to be something just slightly easier than you would take on with an actual Doc in the team, around 10-20% easier depending on your team's makeup and levels.

Excellent guilde. I just want to add a few tips of my own, having recently rolled a doc and brought her up to 59. (disclaimer: I don't presume that these tactics will work for all levels, just that they work to lvl 59).

Some games have a "pealer," a secondary tank whose job it is to peal off the primary mob and go after secondary mobs that aggro other targets (like docs). If your team isn't able to calm the adds reliably, designate someone to peal off the main target and kill or draw aggro off the squishy people in your team. This is especially important with docs, because if they are being attacked, they can't use their nano recharge kits, thus meaning when their nano pool runs out, they can no longer heal. This is especially important for fighting bosses, because many of those long fights are only winable because the doc is able to keep his/her nano pool up. And if no one else in your team takes the add off the doc, do it yourself, even if you are also squishy, because it's better to have aggro on an agent, then a doc who is out of nanos and can't heal. BTW, docs, it is your job to make sure the team knows when you have add aggro and can't recharge.

If the team is figting a particularily tough mob, like a boss, and the healer isn't able to keep up with the rate of damage (like fighting Aztur in Temple of the Three Winds), then a secondary tank is needed to bounce aggro back and forth. A second player taking aggro will buy the team time and allow the tank to recharge some health, so be aware.

If you are on stairs, be wary of being higher up then the doc, since there is a line-of-site bug that often keeps docs from healing you even if you are close by (note that team heals should still work).

Don't run off on your own without telling anyone. My doc hates seeing someone's life bar suddenly start dropping, trying to heal (even with longer range team heals) without success, and wondering where the hell you are. I'll try to come help you, but if I have to go through mobs to get to you or the rest of the team is fighting, you're on your own because the docs first responsibility is keeping the team alive, not the individual.

If you are the puller, have the team window open and don't pull another mob until the team has recharged its health and nanos (and make especially sure the tank is at full health and the doc is at full nano).

Just because you have a doc on your team, don't get lazy between fights--use your health kits to recharge your HP. Docs burn through an insane amount of nano kits, and it's common courtesy to help out (besides, it keeps the doc from running out so quickly, thereby extending fighting time between store visits).

Pullers, if you have accidently pulled something that will wipe your team out, do the honerable thing and don't run back to the team.

Team Heals....
Somthing that hasn't been mentioned here is to use team heals only when absolutely neccessary.

MAs, Advs and Docs all heave team heals. Its a great nano when more than one peep is taking damage, or line of sight becomes a issue, but please don't be lazy and spam team heal when single will do.

The reason is simple. Any nano casting proff will get a message saying "please wait nano's are recharging" when they try and cast. It lasts for a few seconds after you've cast team heal. That means, if you spam team heal, nano casters can't cast att all if your recharge time is quick enough.

Another reason to use team heal sparingly, is that its an aggro magnet. Not bad when you're an MA and want to draw aggro off the Doc, but very bad when you're the Doc and get aggro'ed from mobs in adjoining rooms.

I hardly ever use team heals with my Doc or MA. Only when its the only option for keeping peeps alive. When I play my MA and we get adds... I hit team heal a few times to draw aggro from the doc hoping like heck that the Enf can draw it from me afterwards.

Like Zanthyna wrote..its better for me to have aggro and have the doc able to heal, then to have doc run out of nano and cant recharge cause he has aggro. Cause we all know once the doc dies, one or two more peeps in team will prol dies as well.

Just my 2 cents....
