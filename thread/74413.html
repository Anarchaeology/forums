Actually, I wasn't talking about the storyline - I was talking about the player-submitted news items that appear in the Voice of Freedom (Clan) and the Rubi-Ka Times (OT) (and on that subject, why is it that neuts don't have their own news source on the main site?).

But I do agree that the storyline at present is pretty useless and non-existent. Think about it - if this were an actual society, as soon as the Sentinels walked into Tir and slaughtered the ICC people, two things would have happened:

1. The ICC would have immediately issued a release condemning the actions of the Sentinels, and would undoubtedly have strengthened their forces elsewhere in case Tir wasn't the only target. All forces planet-wide would be put on a hightened state of alert, and they would certainly appear more nervous than they do now.

2. Ross would have stood up, banging his angry fists on his desk, and similarly condemned this action.

Instead, we get silence. The ICC peacekeepers in Andromeda still pace about, oblivious to what's happening more than 10 feet directly in front of them. There are the same number of guards in the neutral territories, and they are similarly lifeless.

We have all of these new shops and billboards, but no one has told us why. Do you not think that people might think it odd to suddenly wake up one morning, see all of this stuff, and not know how or why it was put there? More importantly, given that NW goes live in less than 48 hours, you would think the media would have been alerted by now as to why, come Thursday, the planet is suddenly going to be thrust into a chaotic civil war over mining properties.
