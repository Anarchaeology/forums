Guild tax is set by typing "/org tax <amount>". Only the leader can set the amount.

You cannot enter percentages, which is a shortcoming. That means that the same amount will be taken from your low-level members as the high ones. We didn't find out a definitive system for when the amount is deducted, but it seemed like it was taken out an hour after a member logged in. Presumably inactives or alts wouldn't have problems with withdrawals taken out while offline if this is the case.

All members can type these commands:


'/org bank' will show the amout of credits in the guild bank.

'/org bank add [amount]' allows you do donate credits into it.



The leader can also use:

'/org bank remove [amount]' to get credits for whatever the guild needs.



I think these are the commands. Otherwise, try '/org help' for the exact wordage.

Note that the guild bank ín no way resembles your own bank. You can not put items in here, just credits.
