Demono, your sig definately says it all

At any rate, FC does need to do something. It's not that mission hunting is bad.... but it IS extremely repetetive. Hopefully, the 14.5 patch, with it's dynacamps and tons of actual quests, will change all that. Now, granted, the changes will probably need some tweaking after the initial patching.... that is to be expected. But hopefully, when it's all said and done, the outdoors will be good again. Heck, if the camps alone are done right, that could change everything... any EQ player knows that camping can be a great source of EXP, IF it's done right.

I've tried to hunt outdoors a few times myself.... finding things to hunt really isnt the problem for me. Problems I notice are:

1. Much too long of a run to get to the good spots.
2. Hard to find a group for tougher areas.
3. There arent really any "guards" or safe areas to run to if you get in trouble most of the time.... if you find yourself in a losing battle, well, you are gonna lose.
4. Rarely will a higher level player be around to help you if you are in trouble. That is one of the things EQ does well... if you are in trouble, there is a fair chance that a higher level char will be nearby, and if they are nice they will help you out. But that doesnt happen in AO except in missions, making outdoor hunting even more risky.
5. Even rooting something doesnt help, much of the time. Either the monster will have a ranged attack or nanos, or it will melee you regardless of distance.
6. Fixers (and maybe transformed Agents) are really the only ones that have any reliable method of escaping QUICKLY. In EQ, almost all of the classes that can cast spells got access to the "Gate" spell, and also "Shadowstep". Gate was slow to cast, but if you were able to pull it off in an emergency situation, it would save you and warp you back to werever you were binded. "Shadowstep" would bounce you to a random spot on the field, getting you away from the monster, and it was instant to cast. You could then run to the zone boundary, or gate out of there. No luck here. Only in missions is it really possible for most classes to escape.
7. Quests, in EQ, are another reason to go through many different areas. In AO, there arent really any quests, but hopefully the 14.5 patch will solve that.

FC needs to look at ALL of these problems, and... hopefully... do something about them.
