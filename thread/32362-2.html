Just a thought
Lately after server maintanances and patches, a small group of people and I have strategically camped one spot. The one in question doesn't drop the item very often.

There's 2 things that could be done, as the way I see it.

a) Let the mobs drop their loot every time, every single time they are taken and reduce their spawntime. This would mean that items will be in excess and fewer will camp these sites unless they really need the item.

b) Remove all items from npcs, except for dungeon specific mobs and make npcs a part of the story world.

The way it is today is that we kill for instance nugget we don't know why. We don't even know where the shades come from or why we should kill him to get the loot. Or Van Horn, why is he carrying "The Expensive Kevlar Vest of Professor Jones" ? Did he eat Professor Jones ? Maybe he ate Indy's dog and drank a bottle of tequila and the vest which belonged to indy got eaten by the dog. Which the bad bad Van Horn ate for supper, and managed to eat the vest as well...

Or why not make it a static mission to kill van horn and rather return an item he has stolen to professor jones and let him give the vest. Enough rambling, just my 2 cents.
