Lasagna with Viray sauce
Lasagna with Viray sauce

Ingredients:

1 box lasagna noodles
1 ½ lbs of Ground Viray
16 oz ricotta cheese
8 oz Mozzarella cheese - grated
1/4 cup parmesan cheese
1 can or jar marinara sauce
1 onion - diced
4 cloves garlic - chopped up very small
4 sliced fresh mushrooms
2 eggs
1 tablespoon parsley
dash pepper
dash basil

Directions:

In a large pot, set water to boil.

Meanwhile, brown Viray, onion, mushrooms and garlic. Add marinara and set aside.

Mix cheeses and eggs in a separate bowl with parsley and ****es. Save back about ¼ of the Mozzarella for the top.

When water in pot is boiling add the lasagna noodles. You typically do not need to wait until the noodles are edible because they will be cooked in the oven as well. When noodles are ready (8 minutes or so) begin layering the various mixtures in a 9X13 pan. Start with the Viray mixture; then add the cheese, then the noodles. Keep layering until you run out of ingredients. Add the reserved Mozzarella last.

Cover dish with foil and pop into oven at 375 degrees for an hour or so. If you want to harden off the top, pull off the foil for the last 15 minutes.

Evalissa Stirfry
Evalissa Stirfry

Ingredients

1 teaspoon vegetable oil
2 Evalissa, cut into strips
1 red pepper
1 orange bell peppers
1 bunch broccoli
1 sweet onions
1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
1 tablespoon basil
1 teaspoon paprika
1 tablespoon chili powder
1 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes (optional)
1 dash salt
1 dash pepper
1 cup long grain brown rice

Directions

1. In a frying pan, add the vegetable oil and put the heat on low.
2. Slice Evalissa into strips.
3. Add to pan.
4. Chop up all of your peppers, broccoli and onion and add to pan.
5. Take all of your ****es and add to the mix.
6. Boil rice.
7. Simmer the vegetables and Evalissa on low heat for 45 minutes or until rice is cooked, stirring occassionally, making sure it's not burning.
8. Pour the vegetables and Evalissa over rice and enjoy!

Lauri and Shiitake Mushrooms
Lauri and Shiitake Mushrooms

Ingredients:

800-900 g Lauri thigh (without bone)
8-10 pieces fresh Shiitake-Mushrooms (black mushrooms)
1 pack Shimeji-Mushrooms
1-2 Negi ( Japanese leeks)
1-1.5 tablespoons grated ginger juice
3 tablespoons soy-sauce
3 tablespoons Sake
1 tablespoon sesame oil
2 tablespoons Potato starch
Salt
Pepper.

Directions:

Trim any excess skin from the Lauri carefully, and chop into small pieces. This work takes a little time, but after that the rest of cooking becomes easy. Slice the Shiitake Mushrooms. For a more savory taste you amy add Shimeji Mushrooms, Cut the Negi diagonally into slices. Mix all ingredients and seasonings. Add the ginger juice which has skin into the Lauri. Add sesame oil, Sake and soy-sauce which is less than pre list. It is better to adjust seasoning carefully later, that's why I say that. Add salt and pepper and then mix well using hands. Add potato starch and mix well. Place on a big plate to steam. While cutting ingredients, prepare and heat a steamer. When you put the plate in the steamer, it's better to put the plate in center of a big napkin and grasp two sides of the napkin, and then place into the steamer. Stir once, because the center part will take longer to cook. Taste the cooked part and adjust salt and soy-sauce as you like.

For the more "rural" chefs
Here's one for the "Rural" section of your cookbook, Bliqz:

Pickled Clanner Feet

Ingredients:
4 clanner feet
water to cover
2 tablespoons salt
2 cups white wine vinegar
1 tablespoon whole cloves
2 teaspoons whole black pepper corns
4 star anise pods
2 tablespoons pickling sauce



Directions:
1 Rinse feet well, pat dry with paper towelling and place in a large saucepan. Add enough water to cover and stir in 1 tablespoon of salt. Bring to a boil over high heat; reduce heat to low and simmer for about 4 to 5 hours or until the meat falls off of the bones.
2 Remove meat from bones and place in a shallow dish or bowl. Set aside. Drain saucepan, reserving 2 cups of cooking liquid. In the same saucepan combine the reserved cooking liquid, remaining salt, vinegar, cloves, peppercorns, star anise and pickling ****es. Bring to a boil and boil for 35 minutes.
3 Strain liquid and reserve. Pour reserved liquid over the top of the meat. Cover and refrigerate. Chill for at least 10 hours before serving.
Makes 8 appetizer servin

Votary Parmesan
Votary Parmesan

Ingredients

3 whole Votary breasts, split boned and skinned
1/4 lb. butter
salt to taste
pepper to taste
garlic powder to taste
1/2 to 1 cup seasoned bread crumbs
1/2 cup or more Parmesan cheese (depends on your taste)

Directions
Melt butter in casserole in which Votary is to be baked. Combine bread crumbs and cheese on waxed paper or plate. Season Votary with salt, pepper and garlic powder. Rub seasoning lightly on both sides of Votary. Dip breast in butter and coat with crumb mixture on both sides. Replace in the buttered casserole. Bake 25 to 35 minutes at 350°.

Vegetable Braedon Soup
Vegetable Braedon Soup

Directions:

BROTH:
2 quarts water
3 pounds Braedon short ribs with bones
1 large onion, quartered
2 medium carrots, quartered
2 celery ribs, quartered
8 whole all****e
2 bay leaves
1 Tablespoon salt
1/2teaspoon pepper

SOUP:
1 quart tomato juice
3 celery ribs, sliced
2 medium potatoes, peeled and cubed
2 medium carrots, sliced
1 medium onion, diced
2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
1/2teaspoon hot pepper sauce
1/2teaspoon-dried oregano
1/2teaspoon-dried basil
1/4teaspoon chili powder
1 cup uncooked noodles

Directions
In a Dutch oven or soup kettle, bring broth ingredients to a boil. Reduce heat; cover and simmer for 2 hours or until meat is tender. Remove ribs; allow to cool. Skim fat and strain
broth; discard vegetables and seasonings. Remove meat from bones and cut into bite-size pieces; return to broth. Add the first 10 soup ingredients; bring to a boil. Reduce heat; cover
and simmer for 1 hour or until vegetables are tender. Stir in noodles. Return to a boil; cook, uncovered, for 15 minutes or until noodles are tender.

Yield: 12-14 servings (3-3/4quarts)
