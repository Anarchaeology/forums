Overequipping nerf planned for 14.2
Hi there!

You all know that funcom has planned to "nerf"overequipping for patch 14.2, which is in my opinion a real mistake...why? let me try to explain:
-Overequipping is part of the game:Missions and hunting can be really boring on a long term and its funny to try to find tips (right implant, right cluster,right prof for the right buff..) to become as powerful as possible.I equipped a new beam 2 days ago and it was really a challenge to meet the requirements to wear it I succeded at last and this part of a game was a real break with doin missions and hunting.
-Anyone can overequip if they really want tof course, it costs a lot of money and a lot of time, but when i started my character from the beginning i had no particular friend in the game and was broke as all newbies are.Now i've been playin for 5 months, i have met lot of ppl(by doin missions and chat), and have made quite a good amount of cash by selling stuff, doing missions again and again.Now im a lvl 108 enforcer and i'm quite proud of it, cos i made lots of efforts to reach this level, equipping best stuff i could find.

And now? Funcom has decided to nerf:that means ruining all my efforts and all those from players who are really involved in AO.
Mmorpg are long term games and those who don't want to spend a lot of time on it should try mine sweeper for windows......

When i started playing 5 months ago, Ao was more reliable than it is now: i keep on crashing and lagging especially in broken shores where its quite unplayable due to lag problems; so please Funcom fix those bugs first rather than trying to disgust players from AO. A lot of compettitors will soon be available(Neocron, star wars galaxies) and i think if you want the Ao community to stay as large as possible, you should more take us (customers) in consideration.

I'm not tryin to criticize the game, cos i love Ao, but i experienced lots of problems(lost millions of xps due to lag....) i am soooooo patient and can forgive lots of bugs but please don't go to far and let players equip as they want (without exploits of course) just using all game possibilities(implants and buffs) which is a good apect of AO.
