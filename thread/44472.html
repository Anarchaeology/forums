Some calculations..
Let's say that the servers go down for a total of 12 hours each month. Let's say each month has 30 days.

30 days * 24 hours = 720 hours.

12 hours is 1/60th of the total time in the month (720/12=60)

You pay $12.95 per month as the standard subscription fee.

To be fully reimbursed for the server-downtime, you'd get back one sixtieth of the fee, which rounds up to 22 cents.
