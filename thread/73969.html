I think he's trying to say that FC initially gave a street date to GAME for NW synchronised with the release of AC2.

Sounds like good marketing to me not paranoid conspiracy?

The computer games market overheats around christmas where games will make 4-5 times what they would make any other time of the year. All publishers attempt to exploit the christmas window meaning competition for shelf space in major retail outlets becomes incredibly intense over christmas.
In the UK there is so little shelf space over christmas that publishers actually have to pay for it and typically a lot of games won't end up on the shelves and will only be available by order.

In the States the retailers prefer concrete release dates with big lead times. Some of the bigger stores insist on getting stock weeks in advance. Getting games publishers to play on the store's terms has lead to the slow realisation in the game industry that old school standards need reform. The dichotomy between marketing and development traditionally left the marketing boys out on limb come release date. Hopelessly flapping about like beached fish spouting vacoous excuses for why their game wasn't ready without actually knowing why. As the industry has matured the larger companies have started adopting working practices that on the whole are leading to the development process becoming far more predictable and deadlines are being achieved. Even so getting games out on time is still a bit of a black art.

So for there to be confusion over release dates and lack of supplies is probably no great surprise.
