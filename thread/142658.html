IPR for sale
A shop/booth where players can buy IPR points. This would serve to flatten out the economy of the game a little more, as people will always be wanting to experiment with different things for their character. If it costs 1, 5, or 10 million (whatever) to buy an IPR point, it would still be in range where it could be used by most of the player base, not some ridiculous amount that only a select few players could purchase. This would also be good for lvl 200 players who want to try out different things and have no IPR left, or not enough to get themselves back to where they were if they don't like the new setup. All the while sucking those millions of credits out of the economy little by litte.

First off, it is amazing how worked up people get over something that wouldn't affect them if they choose not to take part.

If the idea of instant skill resets is distasteful, then I know many level 200s would jump through a zillion hoops in order to reset and learn new skills.

I'm sure many would be willing to go through a gradual recovery of IP.

Say you want to reset assault rifle because the UVC days are long long long gone and you're starting to look silly with your nova flow. Choose to reset it, and based off either time or xp gains, the IP slowly is returned to your IP pool as your assault rifle skill lowers back to 0.

This isn't instant gratification. You won't have the IP to just dump in to...oh...SMG or Pistol right off the bat. You gain the IP back slowly. But also, your skill in Assault Rifle is lowering and your already vapid performance with a nova flow will get worse and worse.

But it's a sacrifice that you will make because you want those IP back. And some won't make it because they are plain lazy, and in a goal oriented game, there's no reason why they should be rewarded.

Just an idea since I feel the biggest stumbling block for allowing level 200 characters to continue to stay up to date is that Funcom is against instant gratification. Plus, let's face it. There's a bit of malicious joy in seeing other people's misery.
