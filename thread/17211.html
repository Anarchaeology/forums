Scopes
Its in Enviroment and it is a check box for Show Other Effects.

Uncheck it, this will eliminate the night vision effect with scopes. It will also stop you from seeing other minor effects like Zone boarders and Heals but they are no big deal. You don't have to leave it off all the time, only when you want to equip scope. But do it before you equip or you will have to zone to have it work.
