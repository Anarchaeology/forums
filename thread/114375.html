Nanomages, Notum depletion, evidence or lack thereof
In many heated discussions here in the planetary forums ("The Nanomage People", and all the BORPAC threads) those questioning the validity of NLF & Desert Wind's claims argue that there is insufficient evidence to back their claims, and even that official OT documentation suggests an abundance of notum.

One cannot immediately discount the value of, nor dismiss the truthfulness of, "unofficial" ((non FC sanctioned)) reports. Indeed, anecdotal evidence simply expresses the existence of knowledge outside of the mainstream domain. Who can claim to know everything? ((FC ofcourse, but damn those storyboards that none of us can influence!)) Its is the responsibility and priviledge of those in traditionally unheard/oppressed groups to present the facts of their lives. To doubt their experience without careful and thorough examination is to fundamentally disrespect their lives.

However, I'm happy that BORPAC is now an established part of Rubi-Ka culture: few adamantly question its existence; disruptions to various organizations mining operations have not generated outcry. So even if we stop having conversations about this, people throughout the terrafromed regions of Rubi-Ka knows the signature mark of the NLF, fallen private notum mining towers, in wake of shouts for freedom for the nanomage.
