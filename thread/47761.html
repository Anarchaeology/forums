Plot and Gaming Suggestion
I have cut and pasted most of this comming discussion from our guild chat board. I work many odd hours and was very tired upon writing this. So please excuse any gramatical errors or misspelled words.

Long as you get the jist of it is what maters.
This message was as followed from me to guild mates after a discussion of plot topics and being able to do more true RPG in game.

I believe it would be in FC's best interest in promoting and gaining new players and keeping old ones entertained by adding an affiliation system and a war option against both rival guilds that are omni ,even war function to war against supposed clanner and neutral guilds. The PvP zones are and were a good start but as we all know they are basically just gank zones for higher lvl omni and clanners to gain title agains weeker lower lvl characters and have very little to do with plot in game.

Now I am not praising EAO gamming incorp with the Ultima Online game. I played it for 3 years and they ended up ruining the plot the game and the whole experience with the caving into everyones little request who whined and whined it took to long to become uber. So eventually everyone was uber and there was no RPG.... then it was just like Mortal Combat gank online .

However UO had a good idea but was never really expanded upon.


Thorn has mentioned a part of this already used idea in UO.

Geting FC to utilize buildings as guild halls instead of singular appartments which if ur like me havent used once since u been gaming and even if u do u really cant store anything like a cash of supplies or wpns there as it is limited on space to begin with(due to lag and server space issues) from a programmers point I understand this.

How UO delt with this. was to dedicate overlaping servers to use for Housing and Guild halls and the additonal servers aloted the neccessary space for storage of items and equiptment which then could be accessed by those on the Halls friend list.


Basically FC could add inner city houseing for guilds and cut back on the apt which really only u can access so whats the fun in that.
cant invite friends in to show it off and even if u could u rarely go ther any way.


So in a nut shell what I propose and you may elaborate to FC with your own suggestions.

Is that:

A} Impliment a guild hall structure innner city
that we all in guild can access and store items and build a cache of wpons and supplies up for us all to utilize.
B} Impliment a guild stone function in the Guild Masters(president in our case) that would allow after a vote for the GM to propose war against specific omni, neutral, and clanner guilds that would also allow us a better flushed out story.
c}Impliment a faction support system where we could declare our alliences to specific in game story plot critical guilds and would also allow us a reward and title based system like in PvP where if we did a raid on Omni and managed to take hold of the city or outpost we could hold it for a certain ammount of time before it was taken back by rival guild or omni itself. also reward us for such efforts by aloting credits in mass to guild fund for accomplishing such a task and giveing reward items and rareitys to individuals who participate and win in a senerio like that.
just like in missions and dungeons where u beat the boss.

If FC wants more info on such subjects or Any of you would like to know what im talking about go to www.uo.com (which is UO main) and no dont play that game and leave this one. it has good ideas but lousy development team who ruiend them.

but as a basis to expand our senerio and add flavor and a purpose to us true RPGers who love a good plot as well as a good hack and slash these ideas would impliment the necessary tools to do this with.
