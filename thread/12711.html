Announcing Battle Royale MoK II
Well we had a rough start for Battle Royale MoK I, but the event went through. The winner has been selected and will be announced on the MoK website www.totalaggression.com shortly.

There will be a new entrance procedure for going into the arena next week to take into account chat lag and chat spam from trade and OOC. Some participants had trouble entering the arena on time or didn't know when to enter due to these issues.

The new procedure will be posted this week. But for now, know that the Battle Royale program continues. We have our first winner. See you there and die well.

So once again:

Battle Royale MoK II
Sunday, March 3
8:00 pm GST/ 3:00 pm EST/ 12:00 pm PST
Omni-1 Arena
