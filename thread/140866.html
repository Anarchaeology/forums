Ditzie inserted the key-card into the scanner and waited a second.

"Jeez! I wonder if it even works anymore," she mumbled. "I haven't even been inside since before the wedding."

The scanner emitted a soft beep and the door slid open revealing a dark hallway. As she stepped inside for the first time in almost a year, the lights flickered faintly then bust into a shower of sparks.

"Cheap!" she muttered, disgusted with the state of her former residence.

She passed through the darkened corridor and came to the inner doorway. As the door slid open, the odor of decaying plants hit her nostrils, and brought on instant nausia.

"Oh well, guess I should have invested in that Watering-bot."

She stepped back away from the odor. There was no chance of salvaging anything in the residence. But it was no big loss, just a stereo, some vases and a couple of paintings.

As she stepped out into the busy streets of Omni-Trade she grinned. Only six weeks until her new lease begins in a luxury apartment in Jobe. Would that give her enough shopping time? Time to get busy!!
