A time to be counted
A time to be counted.


Hi I am Kirrana,

On the world of Rubi-Ki I am not much. I have served Omni-Tek as instructed to do for many years. Not always without question but always remaining loyal to the cause. I work in guild, small, hardly noticed by the powers that be. In all a small drop in a vast ocean that is Omni-Tek.

Normally I would not speak out but events over the last few months have trouble me. I watch the news headlines dominated by the guilds in Omni-Tek that have shown it the least respect. The guilds that have openly denounce Mr Ross as the leader of Omni-Tek on Rubi-Ki, who constantly disobey his orders, and break the very rules of Omni-Tek. The papers are full of how the The Black Allegiance grows in power each day or to report on the latest traitorous act committed by the Dark Carnival. To an outsider this would seem this is the face of Omni-Tek on Rubi-Ki.

However this is far from the truth for every Dark Carnival there is a hundred drop in the oceans such as me. It is time to be noticed to show the True Guilds of Rubi-Ki support Mr Ross 100 percent that we support the Omni-Tek way of life on Rubi-Ki. It is time to stand up to the misguided guilds to show them the errors of there ways.

This is NOT about the amnesty or the right for any Omni-Tek person to protect Omni-Tek from the rebel clans.

This is NOT about signing pacts or agreements for protection or land.

This is to protect the Omni-Tek way of life to give support to Mr Ross and to Omni-Tek itself.

So brothers and sisters I see you nodding, so I call upon from the smallest guilds in Omni-Tek, the Omni-Pol who protect our very streets, the mining companies. It is time your voices where added, it is time we spoke. It is time the man and women on the streets of Omni 1 or Rome had a role to play. This is not a pact, no agreement no complex treaty to benefit a few guilds. All I am asking is for you to make the same pledge to Omni-Tek your guild forefathers did many years ago to show solidarity in this time of crisis on Rubi-Ki.

So I ask you to join me to start what will become hundreds of guilds across Rubi-Ki to pledge once more your allegiance to Omni-Tek to give Mr Ross your fullest support.

Now is the time to be counted.
