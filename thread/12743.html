Communication (once again)
In a recent interview with Savant of the much touted Themis group (which FC said they hired to improve customer relations) the following were some of the questions and answers as posted on The Punchingbag website (http://www.thepunchingbag.net/interv...av02192002.php)

-----------------

E-Interview with Daniel "Savant" Manachi (The Themis Group)
02.18.2002

Is your arrangement with FunCom over now? If not, is there a target date? Contract up for renewal at a specific point?

It is just about over. We have two people completing some final work on AO this week. The remainder of Themis has already moved on to new projects such as Jumpgate.

If over, was it ended as was originally agreed to (i.e., for the contract length) or was the relationship terminated unexepectedly?

The contract ended under terms originally agreed to - we achieved our objectives and Funcom learned what they needed from us. Some people thought we got terminated when I stopped posting on the forums, but we had just moved behind the scenes to focus on more important organizational and procedural issues ... that first month of visibility was just "fire fighting." Important work, but not what we were really there to do.

What have you accomplished, exactly, for AO? What specifically can you point to and say "we did this?"

Our actual recommendations are confidential. But we're currently writing a case study of AO that will detail exactly what we did, which we'll be happy to share if and when its cleared by Funcom. I can say that we impacted a variety of areas including community, support, volunteers, production, and marketing.

What haven't you accomplished for AO that you originally set out to do, if anything?

As is always the case in consulting, not all of our recommendations were accepted. But in terms of results, we accomplished everything we set out to do.

-----------------

So, we see that according to Themis, they achieved their goals with the recommendations made to Funcom. The stated goals by Funcom and the actual achieved goals seem to be a little different, at least from my point of view. To me, improving communications with customers means actually communicating with customers. Apparently, to Funcom it means something completely different.

Early on, there was a lot of conversation between Funcom and the players. Posts were made every day (even on weekends) about known problems, what was being done to address them and in many cases, some kind of estimated time of completion (even if that estimate was in terms of months).

As time has gone by though, we see less and less posting here by official personnel. Instead of many posts every day we're down to post roughly once a week from Cosmik and CZ, the self proclaimed primary contact points here. By once a week, I'm not saying one post per week, but one day per week are posts generally made by these two. In most cases, these posts are null events giving little or no answer to any issues and the best answer we can get is "I'll look into it and get back to you" without any follow up.

Here's the funny part (in a twisted sort of way). Every time Cosmik or Cz responds to the lack of communication issue we're told one of the following:

1. "I've been really busy with other tasks and haven't had time to post."

2. "We're going to try really hard to get more information to you on a regular basis."

3. Some combination of the above.

However, after each such statement, the posts from them are fewer and farther between.

I managed to corner Cz on irc recently about this issue and was basically told that they don't have time to post here because they spend so much time in irc or sending exclusives to fansites. That's interesting since often, when I see Cz in irc, as soon as the questions to him get start getting beyond topics like when the next party at Reet Retreat is, he soon finds he has other things to do and has to log off.

That was the case when I asked him why all the information that they supply to fansites can't be reposted here on the official boards. I didn't get an answer, he just suddenly had to go take care of other business.

So here's the conclusions that I see from all this.

1. Funcom, either on their own, or at the urging of Themis have concluded that less is more when it comes to communication. And the less time they spend talking to us, the more time they have for "other things" (whatever those other things are).

2. Assuming #1 is not true, then either the official front people aren't putting in enough hours or have become so overwhelmed that they just can't keep up and have decided to spend hours on "other things" so they can look busy and still keep their sanity.

In either case, there's something seriously wrong here. If you folks can't keep up, either drop the "other things" and concentrate on keeping us informed, or get people who can. I've seen other service related businesses that begin to communicate less and less with their customers. In almost all cases, unless a serious turnaround in policy and procedure is made, profits drop, people get layed off and soon the doors close because the customer go somewhere else where they actually feel like their hard earned dollars mean something.

All we're asking is for you to talk to us. Tell us what's going on. And when we find problems, let us know that you hear us and are working to fix them. Is that really so hard?
