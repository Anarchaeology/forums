Utility/app to pull down character stats?
This was originally posted in Tech forum, it was suggested that this would be a better place for it... thanks Kupotek.

There are some very cool utils and apps out for AO, Clicksaver, nano nanny, AORC etc. Just wondering if anyone knows about, or would care to develop , a util that could log on to your AO account and pull down the stats of a character?

I'm often screwing around with nano nanny or checking stats on www.aodb.info and want/need to know what my char stats are up to. Just being able to get unbuffed/un-implanted stat info and the max for your current level would be extreemly useful to me and I would assume handy for alot of players.

It would be great to be able to pull down the stats and view them offline, then I could work on implant setups, armour configs etc when I'm on a machine that cannot run AO or say on a train with a laptop.

I have no idea if this info is available from temp client files or only available from the AO servers or if this is even possible but it sure would be nice.

Comments and suggestions appreciated.

Ultima Online did something like this. One could go to my.uo.com and log on to view their character's statics and other skills. Was very helpful as UO had a "power hour" system - soon as you logged on you were in "power hour" and your skills (UO is a skill raise system instead of a level raise system) raised better in this time period than any other time. So you would log on to my.uo.com and then figure out what you were going to work for "PH", then log on and play.

Anyway, I'd like to see something like this - howeve I'd like to see what the skills are before and after modification (implants).
