Mea Culpa II
Desert Winds and Nanomage Liberation Front
Joint Co ordinated strikes by BORPAC members

Somewhere in Borealis

We need to show them mining must stop, and we must show them BORPAC mean business.

Exactly Major, I suggest we perform a Mea Culpa operation, The bald nanomage male replied cooly.

The solitus have too long grown complacent, our cause will wake them up again.

Very well Dr, show the defilers of the planet we can strike them when we want and where we want. A coordinated attack with our forces striking at bases in the north of the terraformed zone. Too many people are calling us clanners, this will distance us from them.

Exactly Major, exactly. Now to business.

A few days later, Borealis

After lowering the shield, detonate the device to damage the facility you have been assigned too. This will not destroy the mine but will damage it and show the world BORPAC can act whenever and wherever we want. And should any of you desire to hang around to show the miners the meaning of reclaim, well I wont stop you.

The group laughed slightly at this and headed off to their targets.
