Near-death slowdown, time to rethink it?
Going near-death can be exciting (also brain frying if close to lvl and no save in between). Dying ain't exciting.

In the beginning, being near dead and running away gave BONUSES to not getting killed. Now being near dead and having to run means certain death due to slow-down.

I liked the old way much better. Why don't you remove the near-death slowdown again? It's not like dying penalties ever made a game better when they happened, only through their existance bringing an edge to dying. The dying process itself has never been a fun part of a game, the excitement of avoiding it has though.

Bumps for support, no need for adding the bonuses (I believe they were +evade bonuses) back, but get rid of the slowdown again.
