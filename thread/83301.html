The speed difference from moving the agg/def slider is very noticeable.

But when you move it towards def, you don't take less damage when you're hit, rather, the chance of someone landing a critical hit on you is reduced, and your chance of evading (ie, having someone miss you entirely because of your evades) and of resisting nanos (with nano resist) goes up.

If you have low evades and low nano resist, moving your slider to defense won't benefit you very much.
