I could be wrong about that last part, tho--it may have just been a heal over time nano. I noticed all of this just today (19th) after the most recent server-side patch that fixed the mission distance and locate person / object missions. Mobs just after that patch and up until this morning were perfectly balanced--for my doc.

I think the coders tried to stop the mid level mission zoners by increasing the regen for mobs. However, I believe they inadvertently pushed the difficulty up for lower level characters too much, and here we are.
