R.S.G.E. [Division 9] General Recruitment Open Once More!
For those of you wishing to join the ranks of Div 9, our general recruitment is now open again. There are currently 13 openings in our Cadet Academy, so get those applications in! We do our best to process applications within a couple of days of receipt, however it could take as long as a week..so please be patient.

Division 9 is one of the premier guilds on RK1, and we are Omni-Tek tried and true.

One thing I also want to stress to all applicants...once you receive the questionaire back from us, please get that back to us ASAP..your answers to those questions is what determines your admittance to Div 9...

And also to reiterate, we are still extremely interested in recruiting any Doctors in the European time zones...for those of you that fall into that category, you need not worry about the state of the Academy being open or closed...just /tell Gen. Tredge, Col. Pasqual or myself in-game for the contact info.

**Special Note** Div 9 may not be for everyone. I highly recommend you read through our site extensively before submitting an application...many questions can be answered there and on our message board...If you are not heavily into RP (or at least marginally), we may not be the best guild choice for you.
