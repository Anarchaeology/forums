Absolutely right and to the point.

At lvl 80 and with no uber equipment, I can solo 2-3 greens or a light red very reliably (equiped with a Q124 self-buffed Support Beam).

I'm not so good in PvP by lack of practice, but don't try to take me down fast 'cause your nose will bleed.



Now uniformity is BAD and many of us are asking to have some enhancement in existing weapons : they are dozens of them that are fairly common but not remotely competitive with a Support Beam or an Hammer.



We do feel (and I take the risk of speaking for the whole Enf community here) that the way AC is handled makes us suffer when we start reaching higher level.

As we are not really used to complaining I'll make a proposition here that should not be very difficult to get working :

Increase AC efficiency for Enforcers so that 100 AC absorbs 10 pts of damage BUT if mob min dmge is reached, then the dmge is further reduced by 1 pt for 100 AC.

So for instance a mob hits for 200-800 pts, Enf has 3000 AC, dmge will be 170-500 pts.

Example : an hit is 257 pts unmodified : 570 AC will absorb the dmge above the min dmge, and 2430 AC are left to diminish this min dmge of 24 pts : net damage = 176 pts instead of 200 pts.

The min damage is reduced slightly, but it's like a damage shield : small numbers added make a difference.


That would be a nice tweak that will make us slightly better tanks at higher level, at least competitive with some bots and without adding any healing nano to our profession.
