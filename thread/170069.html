Old Athen project surveyors' meeting
Well if there are truly enough of us interested in restoring and "clanalizing" Old Athen then I would hope we could have a meeting for it. Post here for a quick show of support if you'd like to participate in something like this. People to spread the word around Rubi-Ka are certainly needed as well if this is to take off.

In particular I'd like all Engineers, Bureaucrats, and Meta-Physicists to come out for this. You're not such critical war leaders that Omni-Tek fill find it too timely to strikes blows with your absence and we need all the architects, administrators, and artisans we can get for this. All other professions are welcome, I just don't want people to be too drawn away from guarding Clan territories.

How about having a meeting on Saturday the 30th of August? Time TBA. We'll make a simple show of support and brainstorm ideas and suggestions for the renovation and how it could be done as simply as possible.

All Clanners and even contributing neutrals are welcome. For those Clanners who have misguided disputes with neutrals, please put away your grievances for this time and show the neutrals a vision of a peaceful Clan utopia they can live in.

If this idea meets with approval I'll add it to the events Calendar. I would hope some leaders would volunteer to organize it (Atlantean, Rimor, and DNW seperately). So please let me know!

Some history background on Old Athens:

Please read the history behind The Battle of Omni-2.

In honor of this I would like to in fact propose a commisioning of statues in Old Athen. Besides tearing down the Omni-Tek statues which should be done anyways, how about we commission a statue on the southern portion of Old Athen hill after Dominic Athens?

Perhaps we should also honor Romana Athens and Dolap Red in a similar way. There are many statues around Old Athens which could be replaced with appropriate Clan memorials.
