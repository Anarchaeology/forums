that just means that you can't have things like the Pioneer Backpack (which changes your size by 4%, if I remember correctly), Ruse of Taren (agent nano that shrinks you), or Bracers of Growing/Shrinking (which increases/decreases your size) on.

If you don't have anything on that specifies that it changes your size, you are considered 'normal' size. Regardless of short, med, tall and skinny, med, fat
