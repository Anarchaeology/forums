Options, yes. Options are key. Who cares if an IP reset is going to "let people undo mistakes they should have to deal with"? Games are fun, or at least they're supposed to be. Just because I goofed up doesn't mean I should *need* to reroll. With this, as with anything, you will get the casual players who spent 50 points in Mech. Engineering who will shrug and just keep leveling, and the die-hards who will reclaim 20 points in Electrical Engineering (ME!!!) just because they aren't satisfied with NVG and *MUST HAVE THOSE 500 IP ARRRRRRRGGG!*.

Ahem. My point is, some people will want to use the reset extensively, some won't. I say, let everyone have the option. You made a big mistake? You wanna be hardcore, suck it up, take it like a man? Fine, don't use the IP reset, ever. Then you can get to 200 and brag about your 50 unspent permanent IPR points and watch everyone gawk at you, thinking you are either a planning master, or tough as nails and never a quitter. There, now we're all happy.
