An OOC note to those who just don't get it...
This entire post is OOC and in relation to the Intercessor events on RK2.

[rant]

This is for the closed minded idiots who couldn't roleplay their way out of a paper bag and continue to insist that what we are doing is not RP and that my organization should have been clanners.

If you're not one of those people - whether you RP as friends or enemies of the Order of the Lamp, your efforts are appreciated, and please disregard the rest of this.

This is only directed at the (numerous) people who insist we are not "roleplaying correctly".

Yes, we are Omni-Tek employees. No, we do not kiss Philip Ross's ass. Is that so difficult to comprehend? Not everyone who works for a company worships their boss and always does what he would approve of.

Why are we Omni-Tek employees? We like the pay, we like the access to goods that yes, we can sell to outside parties. Does that make us "poor roleplayers" ?

Quite the contrary - think of it like this.. we're the guys who work 9 to 5 and come back at midnight to rob the warehouse. We like credits, and damn straight we like them more than we like licking Ross's boots.

Don't like it? Maybe you should convince Funcom to have Omni-Tek court-martial us. I got a lawyer ready.

If you hate us in character, good. If you like us in character, even better. But if you're going to go OOC and attack how we choose to roleplay our characters, save your breath. I don't mind tacking you onto the end of my rather lengthy ignore list at all.

[/rant]
