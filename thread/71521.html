Check the ql on the reward. ql of reward=ql of mission. Nano program crystals is exception to that rule though.

For pulling missions, there is two things that can make it easier for you.

1: Helpbot.
code:
/tell helpbot lvl 58
Will give you a reply with what ql missions you can pull. You can also write /tell helpbot help for help on helpbot's features. Just remember one thing. Helpbot is NOT made by FunCom. Helpbot is made and run by a player.

2: Clicksaver. Download clicksaver here, and use it to ease your mission selection.

Well, good luck!
