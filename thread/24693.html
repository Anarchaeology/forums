Make references editable
If this is already in the game, then please enlighten me Otherwise I feel this would be a very useful implementation. I often have the need to create a reference of someone. Perhaps a person to be target for my next snipe, someone I teamed with and would like to team with again, etc.

Right now I can add them to the top of my friendslist to see if they are online and/or create a reference of them. But I have no way of knowing _why_ I want to remember them... My suggestion is to make a textarea in the references so I can add my small notes. Just to help me remember who to kill and who to team with
