Need Information - ((OOC))
((OOC))

My lady friend and I came over from DAoC from the Nimue server. Both of us are very roleplay oriented and enjoy being "someone else" when we're in game.

Diane is much better at it than I am, as I shoot from the hip and she actually sits down and develops deep backstories to her characters.

The question, as it stands. Is which server actually has a better roleplay focus between RK-1 and RK-2?

I see alot of potential with the enviroment in Anarchy Online to do all kinds of interesting things. But since we started out on RK-2 we've only really met one person that roleplays. Other than that person its been alot like EQ and AC....people wandering around talking uber lingo and yakking about Eminem and stuff.

So I thought I'd pose the question here to see if I could get some leads on which server hosts a more roleplay oriented playerbase. Because we'd love to meet up and interact with other roleplayers in this game. But since there is no "Roleplay" server...its sort of a case of getting to know people.

Thanks. I hope for responses.

I have switched fom RK 1 to Rk 2 in the last few months and found a very alive and welcoming community of Rp'ers on Rimor. I think it depends where you are looking. yes it is less populated, but that is also a good thing imo as I have had a lot less greif since I went there.

I think it also depends on what side you play on that server. Clan outnumber Omni there and most of the RP seems to be clan/neutrals with the odd Omni org here and there. I am sure there is a lot of room for some new omni orgs too...if that is what your into.

It will be easier to find a group of RP'ers right away on RK 1, mainly because there are more people, but I find a much more satisfying community on RK 2. Some keep to themselves and others have ongoing relationships with each other, but everyone is pretty open to new people who want to RP

It's kind of a touchy subject for me when talking RK 1 cause I try desperatly not to come accross as bitter, but the RP community there is a lot less welcoming, from my experience, than it is on Rimor. I don't know if it is because there is now a set group who are comfortable with one another or if maybe they have delt with more greif from people who don't RP there...but I can't deny that the same bs I went through on RK 1 doesn't seem to be there on RK 2. I'm sure it is, in some way, but to a much lesser extent I think

That said, I have nothing against any RP'er on RK 1 who welcomes people into the community, and in that kind of situation, I'm sure it is a rewarding experience. I say try both servers out and then decide where you fit in
