On clearing a mission to get tokens. IT SUCKS!!
I hate having to kill all the mobs in a mission to guarantee yourself to get a token. It makes no role playing sense and is just plain annoying, especially if you find the objective but have to leave it just to clear the mission first.

What should happen is that the token is hidden in a chest. If the mission rewards multiple tokens, then split them up into multiple chests. Players have to exit Conceal to open a chest so they still couldnt sneak an entire mission. If you also combine this with traps on random chests that will alert nearby mobs, players will still have to fight through the mission to get tokens.

Well it would seem like an easy change, so it wouldnt take many resources to do. Second it would add a lot to the game.
  • While it wouldnt be possible to sneak an entire mission, you could sneak parts of it. When you found a chest, then it would be a good idea to kill the nearby mobs. If you came across a mob by itself then you could leave it alone. The point is you have a choice and more choices = good
  • Rewards disarming traps and perception. Currently there is little reason to use these skills and few players invest in them. If a player wanted to sneak a mission, it would make sense to find and remove all alarms. You know, let sneaky profession be sneaky.
  • Able to do part of a mission if you dont have much time to play. At higher levels the missions get huge, requiring a large investment in time. It would be nice if you could do half a mission and get about half the tokens.


Those are some of the billion important things that this game needs.
