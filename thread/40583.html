Improve the Quality of CS
Check out this screenshot.

This is the way one gets treated when he wants to report a bug. It was late at night, I was supposed to go offline ages ago, but didn't because I wanted to report a bug. Makes it easier for Funcom who can locate it more easily, doesn't directly benefit me in any way. And since they are that engaged and stuff I thought I report it to them.

Well, I did, this guy, Pandamonicom, he bombed me with his script, didn't even bother to make it print out my name, instead of that I get addressed by my character ID. No wonder I reacted pissy in the next sentence.

Then he typed in some quick sentence, and bombed me with his other script that of course immediately blocks the communication channel so you couldn't even reply with something like "Hey!" or anything like that.

I try not to rant on here and remain calm and serious, but don't forget that with what online fees I pay your wage gets afforded. You could at least try to make the entire CS request sound engaged on your side. And don't tell me that the petition queue was that long and you need to move on smoothly.

When I petitioned there were 17 petitions in front of mine in the queue. And it took approximately 3 hours for them to answer it. Three hours is 180 minutes, divided by 17 makes how much time per petition? 10.6 minutes, on the average. How come apparently everybody gets them, except me? This entire chat you can see in the screenshot lasted for about 20 seconds. Alternatively you can see it the other way round. 20 seconds multiplied by 17 makes 340 seconds, that's about 6 minutes. What are they doing the rest of the 3 hours?

I used to say that the GMs are the competent ones and the ARKs the polite ones. But as it seems this changed too now. Now GMs are the competent ones and ARKs... hmm... Okay, I guess I am drifting off serious complaint into ranting now.

But seriously, Funcom, if you want to keep your engaged to fix and improve and care for your customers as good as possible image you should seriously change something about the way some of your CS Representatives actually do represent you when a customer contacts them.

Khalai.
