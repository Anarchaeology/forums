I saw it all... Randiman was a sitting duck
The rumours were flying... Randiman was coming.

"When?..."

"No WAY !..."

"I dare him to show his face..."

"Today?..."

Thamar snapped her gum and adjusted her glasses... Not impressed by much of anything after her years in the trenches, patching together broken bodies. She thanked her friend for the monster parts and headed for the supermarket...

" 30,000+ years of astounding medical improvements and Doctors still get paid in livestock. Well, at least I don't have to EAT this!" she sighs. She dropped the newly processed plasma in the store's collection tray and pocketed her credits.

The din of a HUGE crowd coming down the street made her pause and turn around.

"He-ello? What's THIS?" A familiar little blonde soldier ran up to her and raised her Kataras...

"Heya Thamar! Are you gonna come over to HQ with the rest of us? I hear Randiman is very, VERY handsome in person !"

Thamar rolled her eyes at the sound of her 'friend's" giggle. Never, NEVER would she be caught dead making that dreadful noise. Unfortunately, her husband found her little looks and noises appealing and had been spending ALOT of time with this person Thamar used to be able to call her friend.

Maybe it was just to get her to shut up or maybe she needed to admit a small fascination to see a man who cried peace while his followers turned her patients into goo... in any case - she went.

There was a number of people running all over HQ. Not your usual bunch of bureaucrats either but a huge number of Atroxs in Omni-Pol and Omni-Pol Elite armor. Her NCU made note of some of the ranks some of these Goliaths while she pretended to check out the scenery.

Her nerves were totally raw and battlefield ready and her instincts were to be trusted after all they had helped her to survive. Outwardly she smiled and nodded to those who happened to greet her, but inwardly she prepared for ... something.

Suddenly, Randiman was there and not at the usual grid drop either... How odd. Her soldier 'friend' gushed and waved but hadn't even shouldered her weapon. Couldn't she FEEL it? She glanced around. Sure, there were some bodyguards, but they were all too close to really see what was going on. Randiman greeted the crowd and headed down the stairs. Then he did something VERY ODD... he turned LEFT! He didn't head strait down the wide and well lit path to Omni-HQ and Boss Ross's office but rather down some dim and narrow alley.

Suddenly a voice rang out a threat... "Hey RANDIMAN! The Black Alliance means to assassinate you AND your escorts! Take Cover!"

Thamar's NCU made note of the precise moment that Randiman started taking hits. She started to send her NanoBots on their way but before she could finish the program... Randiman lay dead at her feet. Shouts of panic flew through the air and people scattered... Thamar noticed someone named Joseph "Aberic" Capo calmly walking away, down the dimly lit alley with a very satisfied look on his face. While her NCU made note of his rank and profession she also took a still of the crime scene - her training coming to bear on an impossible situation.

Sure Ranidman had insurance, who didn't, but what signal would this send to the clanners who were taking FULL advantage of the amnesty and parading all over Omni owned land. She could barely hunt for the little bit of bounty she had time for because some clanner would appear on her scope making her auto targeting system go berserk because she didn't happen to be in a low gas area... so often she dreamed of hacking that gas system and shutting it off... permanently.

Very shortly, the officials made their appearances, decrying the outrage, assuring everyone that Randiman was safe... In fact, they claimed the man she'd seen slaughtered in the street was a decoy - that they'd discovered the plot and Randiman and Ross were actually meeting in an 'UNDISCLOSED location...' Could be true, but it sounded like damage control to her jaded ears.

So what would this do to the talks, the amnesty, and her home... Sounded like time to overstock on NanoRechargers...

Thamar shouldered her shotgun and fished in her pockets for a fresh piece of gum...
