Mission problems
So missions are about soloing?

Well everytime your servers have a problem I lose my map.
I like to solo when I play this game, but its getting frustrating at higher levels to get 2 hours into a mission that is out in the boonies and be playing with attentivness and skill and have the game kick me off. This more often than not results in death, mostly due to the map being blacked out while I am in the middle of my raid.

I like the concept of farther travel time and larger maps as you gain levels but I think that the only way your map should go black is if you run out the entrance. I have lost so much time from server connectivity problems its sick.....at least find a way to not punish me when I am playing the best I can. I see the trend here where it soon will be very hard and take very long to do missions solo.

I totally accept when I have wasted time trying to take on more than I can handle, but when everything is under control on my end and the game pulls the plug and punishes me as a result. That time is lost and it tastes real bad.
