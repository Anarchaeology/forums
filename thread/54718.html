A couple points
First of all, both sides warp. Its part of the game and agreements by a limited amount of people will never solve the overall problem

2. The ability to warp was given by funcom to Engies. What they didn't foresee was that people would be trying to warp into full playfields. This is funcom's fault...not Clan or Omni faults.

3. Warping needs to be fixed. Engies that attempt to warp another player into a full playfield should just receive a message that the pf is full.

4. The bigger problem is that even without warping, any side can just fill up the pf and thus guarantee winning that spawn. Most of us that enjoy the fighting more then the loot raffles would rather see more of the enemy up there and the last thing we want to see is non-participation of the other side.

My proposed resolution:

Allow only up to 50% of the pf to be filled by any 1 faction. Lets say that camelot holds 100 people. After the 50th clan walks in, the next one would auto zone into another pf. Same goes for Omni.

This way, any faction that wanted to participate in the fun, would have an equal change to get equal numbers into the pf.

If done correctly, this could add a lot to the pvp situation at camelot and hopefully turn it away from the bingo hall raffle crap that is currently going on when one side has overwhelmng numbers.

Ellph
