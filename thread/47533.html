quote:
Originally posted by Sheffy
Does anyone else find it outrageously stupid that you get PvP points for killing someone of your same faction?
The PvP points system doesn't seem to be related to the conflict at all. If you want to get credit for helping your side, you do missions and collect tokens (my apology to the neutrals out there). The PvP title system is intended to be a ranking of skill at PvP, not a faction rewards system. In other words, it's like a ranking system in boxing as opposed to the medals a soldier would wear on his uniform.

It's certainly not perfect at indicating skill, but I think that was the original intent.
