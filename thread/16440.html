Party at Reets Tonight (live DJ)
It's party-time at Reet's Retreat in Stret West Bank on RK1 tonight. I'm having some friends, and I invite anyone of omni, neutral, or clan to join in on the fun.

I'll be the live DJ for the evening, using direct mp3 streaming through winamp. The IP play location for winamp will be announced within Reet's, so you gotta show up to listen in!

We'll be listening to Metal, Techno, Industrial, and my own little sarcastic quips for a powerhouse 3 hours of fun!

The time of the party is officially 9pm EST (Eastern Standard Time, you do the math) tonight, Tuesday March 19th, and will end at midnight.

But hey, show up early if you like! Should things go well with my mission, I'll be there nice and early, kicking things off with some of my private metal-mixes!

Oh yeah, I'll be setting the max listener base to 30 when I kick things off (though that may increase as time goes on), so show up early to garauntee your slot on the stream!
