Reason I pointed out that they need to flesh out the missing months is because it's supposed to be a Story.

If you read a story and one of the protagonists simply goes away in Chapter 4 and comes back in Chapter 21 without so much as an explanation, that's not good storytelling. Storytelling, yes. Good storytelling, no.

Just because Radiman is back, it doesn't make everything all right. As part of a well told story, there should be some ramifications to his absence. He learned something while he was away, he made a new alliance, perhaps it's not Radiman at all (remember the last Video?). Point is, that you don't just drop and restart characters like you do in TV Soaps.

This is supposed to be a well told Story and I don't appreciate deux ex machina. I believe that's the term where a story teller simply creates some mechanism to 'explain' something that just don't work. Kinda 'forcing' things to move along.

We are entitled as listeners to a recount of Radiman's revelations and perhaps some of his exploits (even if they are lies told by a clone).

A protaganist who goes away and instantly reappears with no rhyme or reason for the in-between is NOT good storytelling.

FunCom is our Dungeon Master. Least we can do is expect them to do a good job. I never paid any Dungeon Master thirteen bucks a month to do a crappy job when I was a kid and I wouldn't expect to do so now.
