Well, nobody fully understands the aggressive-defensive bar....and they have stated that is exactly the way they want it- a trade secret.

However, what I can tell you is that is has nothing to do with getting or not getting aggro. The higher you set the aggressive the faster you can attack, with the downside being that your defensive rating suffers (how or by how much nobody really knows).

What can be tested to at least some level of accuracy is the effect of your initiative to the speed of your attack based on the attack speed of the weapon. Everything else is purely circumstantial evidence.

In the end, almost all low level players of all professions play with full aggressive as they need it to get the most out of there weapons (or combat nanos). This is even true with nano-centric professions as the higher the agg bar the faster the attack time is on your nanos...all the way to instacasting. (The nano recharge is NOT affected by the agg/def bar however like it is on weapons)

What I have noticed is that there is something in the formula that gives a attack speed bonus to dual wielded weapons which is only discovered after you have hit the max attack/min threshhold (40/min). It appears to me that you can drop your agg bar about 10% lower dual wielded as you can with one weapon maintaing the 40/min attack speed.
