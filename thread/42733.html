a community weapon pool.
I have a great idea for soldiers everywhere (on RK1 and 2).... namely to get a WeApOn PoOl going on.

(all the atrox solja go huh?)

...lemme explain. Everyone's ALWAYS moaning about having problems finding a new gun, or having to pay insane prices and stuff. Well, if EVERYONE comes together and starts helping the fellow solja, we could have a chain of donations/loans going on.....

to give you an example, i have now in my possesion

a ql 14 div9
a ql 21 nova MK 1
a ql 29 heavy supressor
a ql 39 heavy supressor ( which i will use next level)

So i would let another soldier use one of my old guns, lend it to him, until he doesn't need it anymore, and he gives it back. So say someone else has a ql 50-60ish heavy supressor they not using.... i would borrow after i'm done with the 39 and give back after.

This way, we could have the whole community helping each other out, passing equipment, commenting... etc. It would help form an even bigger SOLDIER brotherhood between us. And at the same time, it's a great "help the newbies" move.

What do you say?
