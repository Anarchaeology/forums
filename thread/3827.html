Fed up
I really really get fed up with all of the bickering, whining and other BS. You want a nerf? Fine an agent KNOWS about nerfs so here is it the final nerf ... the uber nerf so to speak

First off remove the agents ability to switch professions. None of you will leave our profession alone till this happens so there ya go. The amount of concealment you have should determine how well you sneak and stay concealed...a large amount of conceal should mean something. Failed lockpicking and trap disarmament attempts should alert any mobs in the area of your presence...a chest blowing up should cause any mobs in the nearby rooms to go to the room and investigate the loud explosion. There should be an option to put a silencer on your rifle to allow you to attack a mob without alerting any of the other nearby mobs of your presence. Agents should be retooled into the sneaky snipers or pullers that they should be...reliant on their own nanos and skills instead of borrowing them from others...maybe then people will stop constantly begging to nerf us into oblivion.

Make all higher level buffs unstackable with lower level buffs of the same line. This removes the stackability of proficiency and expertise general buffs as well as deprive skills and ransack skills. Next remove all race determined ability buffs from the game entirely (strength, agility, sense, intelligence, psychic, and stamina). Remove all race determined ability nanoclusters as well. This would remove everyone's ability to overequip implants or armor through the use of lower quality implants to abilities and temporary buffs. The ql of armor and implants would be determined primarily by race choice when you develop your character in the beginning and how high you pump up those skill levels. Not by who you know or what you can cast. If you can get an implant equiped with just your race abilities alone then you should be allowed to install that implant. Doctors would then be able to use surgery clinics to increase the treatment level of anyone needing a little extra...as was intended in the first place. Overequiping implants would no longer be possible...the quality level of the implant would depend on your base abilities and how high a doctor could "buff" your treatment.

Incorporate negative modifiers to all overequiped equipment...ie weapons that you don't have the skill for. These negative modifiers would lower relevant speed abilities depending on how much you are overequiped. For weapons it would lower ranged or meele initiative and reduce chance to crit to zero...in essence an overequiped weapon would fire at minimum damage as long as you didn't meet the requirements. A weapon you barely have the skill for would fire normally with the normal chance to crit. If you have more than enough skill to equip the weapon then you should have an increased chance to crit with that weapon and do the maximum damage. This would show your familiarity with your weapon. A weapon you don't have the skill to use shouldn't be as effective or more effective than a weapon you can equip normally.

The effectiveness of the special attack would also work similarly. First check if the player has met the requirements for the weapon. If the requirements are met then calculate the chance to crit with that weapon based on special attack skill - required special attack skill on weapon/required special attack skill required for the weapon...or something similar you get the idea(I'm not a mathematician). The idea is to ensure that a person who has more than enough skill to equip a weapon should be able to use it more effectively than someone who is underskilled in the weapons use or can just barely use it. You should only have to change weapons when you want to get a weapon that has a higher max damage hit range or a higher special attack max.

Nanoprograms work a little diferently in the fact that they either work, fumble, or get countered. However once a nanoprogram is uploaded there is nothing they physically carry or weild...the program appears from the nanobots within their body. All nanoprograms have statistics on them determining their attack rating with that spell. Problem is currently there isn't anyway of determining your attack rating with any one particular nanoprogram. Here is an idea click on the spell once to upload the spell into ready to use mode(wouldn't use nano and would occur almost instantaneously...but would only be possible when you aren't actively casting something else). Once the program is in ready to use mode its "Nano rating" would appear in the statistics window. Your Nano rating would be used to determine how easily a spell can be countered by your enemy target based on their nano resist skill. Nanoprograms you barely have the skill for would have a higher chance of being countered, but lower level nanoprograms would have a much lower chance of being countered depending on how much your nano skills are over the requirements for that nanoprogram. Nano Resist of course would only effect hostile programs casted by others on you. Your fumble rate would be determined by your nano rating for that nano. High Nano rating = lower chance to fumble, Low Nano rating = higher chance to fumble, and if ya don't have the skill for the nanoprogram 100% chance to fumble. This is fair because nanoprograms don't have a minimum-maximum attack or a crit...they either hit at full or they don't hit at all (fumble or get countered).

Skill levels of mobs should be within the average expected skills that a normal player would have. This includes health, nano and perception by the way. Most nanos and weapons should effect players and mobs equally. A root or a snare should work the same against a player as it does against a mob. Charm spells should only work against nonhuman mobs...that way tactics don't change in PvP or PvM. Current AI allows mobs to teleport attacking distance once they are attacked regardless of your position or difficulty of getting there so a root should never ever break even against mobs. Snares currently have no purpose in PvM because the mob just teleports or walks through walls into attacking distance when attacked. Snares are only useful in PvP and even then only against some professions.
