I couldn't careless for myself, 'Zeph' is really a nickname anyhow.

I would SUSPECT the reason Funcom made this decision was to add realism... FOR THE ROLEPLAYERS.

Here, let me explain:

- Kid logs on and enters the name "Kewldewd".
- The game assigns "Bob" and "Johnson" as first and last names respectively.
- RPer sees the kid "Sigh... 'Kewldude'..." and IDs him.
- "Oh... Bob 'Kewldewd' Johnson... OK, it's his nickname..." It isn't so bad.
- If "Kewldewd" get a high enough level and can afford it, he can change his name to "Haxor Kewldewd Ownzjoo" if he wants, but at least we won't have a bunch of level four "dewdz"

I like that. The problem is with the people who have already spent days/weeks/months developing their characters. They should be able to get their proper names.

Now, instead of bitching, time for a solution.

1. Keep the randomly generated names (Haxor Kewldewd Ownzjoo is going to have to cost you).
2. Allow players to request for a new name ingame. The GM would check the URLs provided by the player and ensure that they had a built up persona and that it fits into the Anarchy RPing scheme (a story about "kewldewd haxor" won't pass). Then the GM could change the player's names.

This would only take a little time and effort. The satistified customer is worth it I would think.

But that is just my opinion.
