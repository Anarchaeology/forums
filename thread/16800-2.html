My thoughts
Hmmm, well here's my thoughts.

First off, I love the idea of gadgets. I really enjoy roleplaying and an engi is all about gadgets. So it just makes sense that we should have some more. I've seen a lot of comments about engi shortcomings, but to my mind the most severe is in the pvp area. So here's a few ideas from me.

Give us some more gadgets that function like the bot NCU upgrades. Things that inherently weaken the bot's structure (lower Max HP) but add a useful capability in return. Preferrably we'd make these ourselves, but I'd be happy enough if we could just buy them outright (saves FC some coding, therefore more likely it'll happen)

1) Gun Arm. Several versions of these like a Blaster arm, a Flamethrower, etc. they change the bot from doing MA melee damage to various types of ranged damage. If the QL of the new arm is lower then the bot, then use the arm's QL to determine damage output, otherwise use the bot's original QL to determine damage. These would be very handy for pvp as they let the bot still be useful. Sure, the other players can still go straight after the engi, but the bot is at least fighting back now instead of standing there like an expensive statue. (of course it'd be some VERY nice fluff if the bot graphic changed to reflect it's new arm type) Only one could be applied to any given bot.

2) Weapon Arm. Like the gun arm, but it changes the type of melee damage dealt. An energy blade to replace the bot's hand, for instance. Same general rules as for gun arms, with same wish for fluff.

3) Anti-Virus. Raises the nano resist of the bot by 4 or 5 times the QL of the gadget (assuming bots have 0 resist normally). This enhancement installs some hostile nano-hunters in the bot's system that help negate the effects of enemy NFs.

4) AI Governor. This litte doo-hickey will increase the bot's psych score by constantly running AI-checking and re-building routines, making it more difficult for the robot to disobey it's creator.

Both one and two could alternately be solved by letting us give an actual weapon to our bot that we could recover afterwards similar to the chance of recovering the bot shells, but better (weapons cost a LOT more then bots). Or add another MRR for "Bot Modification" that can be used to make a weapon from player-only to bot-only (it changes the interface on the weapon or the grip or whatever to fit a bot), and you could then give it to your bot to weild, but it'd be useless otherwise.

And for the idea of giving us Trader skill buffs, I don't quite like it like that. Leave the traders alone for all I'm concerned, I don't want more buffing NFs. Instead... let's do something more fitting. How about Tool-Bot? He's another pet we could have out, but he doesn't add diddly squat to a fight and he's fragile as a wet paper bag. In fact, leave it on permenant "/pet behind". It's sole job in life is to be our gopher for tools. Any engineer in real life will tell you how much easier the job is if you have a good assistant. And that's just what Tool-Bot is. He's a walking toolbox basically. As long as he's with you, you recieve a bonus to your Electrical and Mechanical Engineering skills, based on the QL of the Tool-Bot. If Fc doesn't have time to implement it as something built, I'll live with another NF line. Hey, at least it's neat new bot then and not some re-named buff from another class

And how about some shielding gadgets? Like a true Force Barrier. Base it's use off of the Quantum fields skill and let it draw it's power from your nano pool. Either in a fixed X amount of nano for Y amount of seconds, or let it drain X amount of nano per second you leave it running. Make 1 line of these gadgets for each type of AC, and one more line that covers them all but drains horrendous amounts of nano compared to the others. Or heck, rather then use large amounts of nano to activate it or keep it in use, instead let any damage taken by the shield be subtracted from your nano pool as the shield rebuilds itself after each hit. And you could make the shields created NODROP since they would need to be created to harmonize with an individual's unique nano signature in order to draw their power. If need be, it could even be a two-way barrier. Nothing in, nothing out. The more powerful barriers may even make you immobile. Therefore making them useful for forcing your opponents to deal with your bot for a few precious seconds while you try to get off some healing nanos/stims/whatever to preserve your extremely fragile hide.

And how about being able to make a work-suit exoskeleton? Similar to Tool-Bot but better bonuses and adds to some other skills like Quantum Fields. But it's requirements would be a high skill in the very skills it boosts. After all, to use the best tools you need to already know what you're doing. This would also tend to keep them out of non-engi hands without the need to make them NODROP as very few others are likely to have really high engineering skills like us. This could be a dropped armor where each piece added to particual skill (like the doc suits that up treatment and first aid) or it could be a built suit that we have to make. Or rather then a segmented peicemeal suit, make it a full body suit kinda like the Battle Armor full body suits, maybe even using the same or very similar graphics. Naturally it would have little to no AC, so you're not going to wear it out of the shop most likely

Now, for a couple of odd things in my mind. Why do our pet heals use BIOLOGICAL Metamorph when there's nothing remotely biological about them? Shouldn't it be MATTER Metamorph since they're, you know, metal? I'm guessing they just did it to make us use a dark blue skill, but there's got to be a way that makes sense from a role-playing perspective rather then arbitrarily pick a skill that we use for absolutely nothing else past general buffs. (at least to my knowledge) I don't mind using a dark blue skill, it just bugs me immensily as an RPer, kinda like that splinter in your mind thing Just like what the heck does your ability to parry an attack have to do with your ability to press a few buttons to activate a shield device?

For exp on tradeskills, I think it's fine. Heck, I'm happy to even be GETting exp from building things. However, I do agree that it would make sense for the cost in parts to be at least 20% less then the shop sale-price for an item. Otherwise the shops are loosing some serious money

But all in all, WE WANT MORE GADGETS!!
