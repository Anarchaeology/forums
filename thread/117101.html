Directly out of the character creation process:

Regarding how all players came to Rubi-Ka:
quote:
Welcome to the ICC943-622 Medical Facilities on Rubi'Ka. The disorientation you are currently experiencing is caused by the lack of coding in your genetic material. Your genetic make-up has been injected into unshaped bio-material that will eventually form your new body. From this point onward, you will be in control of the development of your genetic potential.
Regarding how you got your breed:
quote:
This is the Breed Selection Clinic. Here, you may charge your genetic make-up with one of the four stable-breed DNA sequences. A resonance frequency is emitted through the lab to keep the DNA harmonics from settling which enables you to change your breed as many times as you want. Once you leave the lab your breed DNA will become irreversible. Remember, the choice you make now will follow you for a lifetime.

Regarding the Opifex:

quote:
Opifex. This breed offers both male and female form. Its genetic template has been skewed towards an agile breed focused on being reactive and precise. The Homo-opifex was originally developed by Omni-Tek as tunnel runners in the notum mines, but soon became useful in other areas as well.
Regarding the NanoMage:
quote:
Nano. This breed offers both male and female form. To provide perfect communication with clouds of airborne nanobots, the Homo-nano was developed with superior neural pathways. These pathways run through the breed's body and optimize communication with advanced nano-technology. The over-developed neural pathways put a strain on the development of stamina and muscle tissue.

Artificially created species are restricted to their creation planet for a reason. The possible mutations caused by them settling down on other planets and mating with other species are very unpredictable.
Also, their different genetic setup might make them vulnerable to diseases otherwise harmless to native inhabitants or worse, might mean they could transfer diseases to the planets they visit or settle on.

Also, we should remember that these are breeds specifically designed to live on Rubi-ka. The nanomage is the extreme example, simply unable to survive outside notum saturated environments, but the opifex and atrox breed are also custom made for the parameters of the Rubi-ka world.
Quite likely it would be very uncomfortable for them to spend any lenght of time on other planets.

In this regard, the solitus race is much more versatile and adaptable.
