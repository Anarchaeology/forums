I personally think that there should be no points or titles in PvP. Currently a very small percentage of people on RK2 PvP at all.

One of the reasons is that when people get up to a certain pvp title they are afraid to pvp for fear of losing their uber title. Another reason is people who level and team on a regular basis are not twinked for their level and only upgrade every 10-15 levels at low levels and every 20+ levels at mid level range. These people don't go to pvp zones because the pvp zones are filled with people who leveled to a certain level and get twinked out at that level for the sole purpose of PvPing. (example: The level 2 MP with full set of Carbonium, The level 15 Agent with QL 70 Implants and QL 96 X-3, level 50 trader with QL 150 Ithaca and QL 125+ Implants .... and on and on and on).

I believe that if you took away all the titles and points in PvP that more people would pvp because there would be no gain to stay at a specific level for pvping, and because there is no title system there woudl be nothing to gain and nothing to lose and more people would PvP for the fun of it and not for uber titles that don't mean anything anyway.

There should be no bonus to how many wins you have in PvP, if you want to PvP you should do it because it's a joyful part of the game. I know that in Mass PvP any person that has Rookie or Apprentice+ is the first person I go after (usually a Trader). When the pack comes out with "Land Control" this will be a big issue.
