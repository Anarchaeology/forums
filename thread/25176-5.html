Event Suggestion
I'd like to see the ability to disable/diminish the suppression gas generators of a city. This way it becomes more than just a guard attack.

When this happens, the people in the city have to decide whether to make a stand or flee. This would introduce a true pillaging element to the game. As is, there isn't much drama in a few peeps offing guards in a political or mayhem zone. At worst, it is just an annoying ganker festival.

This could be a GM initiated event where "saboteurs have disrupted the supression gas generators" or it would be even more uber if it could be a player initiated event.

Make a static dungeon or mob the target to activate the supression failure. Surround it with guards to make it more or less difficult to achieve--that way, there can still be some safe areas for people to hang in, but there is always the chance that a large group could overwhelm the city...

That could be an event in itself, defend the gas supressor so the city can stay safe.

As long as it's not something that a loser ganker can do by itself or in a small party, I think that it would add to the tension. A lot more people would have skin in the game if their lives were disrupted and you could postulate much more reason and interest in attacks and reprisals.

Make the effect of limited duration and with a slow respawn so people won't risk getting trapped in a bad situation (like MMD), but then you actually would feel like you were going to rescue your affiliates.
