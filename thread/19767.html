F 16 Fighter Plane screen, I want important messages in the middle of monitor .......
F-16 and all the other high-tech fighter planes shows important messages on the window. That way the pilot can focus on looking out and not down into the controllers. Todays pilots dont need to move their eyes at all to know what is going on, I want the same in AO....


For example I am in 2HO, and I get attacked by Ironhead (fictional player). Instead of getting that message down in the chat, I want it to pop up in the middle of my monitor. The letters should be transparent (maybe a slider you can adjust, just as with inventory). I should also be able to choose what size and what font it is. Important messages is what you decide them to be, I would like messages such as when someone casts a nano on me to pop up, but you can choose what messages you think is important to pop up. I would not like dmg to pop up like that, would only be insane spam, but could be optional.

I would love this feature.
