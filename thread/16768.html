Mini-event in the backyard?
I created a new character (engineer) and a guy with a green name over his head was in the training area. His name was Scranton. Don't remember the first and last names, but they were also displayed above his head, also. And he didn't have an ARK tag.

So he's whining and crying about a sneaky leet stealing his sunglasses...wants the 'recruits' to find them for him, says he'll pay 300 creds. He was doing tons of RPing and emotes, and his name was green and stuff, so I figure, "why not, I'll kill a bunch of leets and see if I find any glasses." I didn't say anything to the guy, but I decided to help. I killed about 20 leets, leveled, found tons of nice loot (for a beginner) but no shades...then I saw a named leet. It's name was "Sneaky Leet"......I killed it, and it did have sunglasses. I gave them to Scranton and he says, "about damn time!" and pays me 300 creds. Then he says, "tell ya what, keep em" and gives me the shades back. I put them on and he says that people will know I was hanging with Scranton. It was really fun looking for the shades, and I liken the experience to a mini-event (which is really cool)....so is that what it was? And who the hell is Scranton?

Guild events...
I am a member of the guild, Omni Tek Wolf Pack. Every now and then we grab a couple of high level t00ns and head to the backyards.

We hand out a few credits, QL5-10ish items, med suits, buffs etc. At the same time we'll have a couple of guild members make n00bs to help form teams and show some of the new people team combat, how to set up their quick action bar, etc. The scenario I described isn't an event at all really, but it takes NO prior planning and just ten minutes of prep time (to stop by a shop to grab a couple of bags of low QL gear).

If your guild isn't doing this, maybe you can coordinate something like this. Most 'events' are rare and only involve mid-high level characters. This effort only takes an hour (maybe 2) and really puts a good impression on the new ppl trying to enter our community.

We do NOT push our guild in anway (it has been suggested, but we decided against it). We just go in, start shouting for free gear and have lots of fun.
