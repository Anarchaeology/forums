As I started on another forum...

Dungeon Masters of Rubi-Ka1 or whatever you guys vote in....

Looking for a creative organizer from each roleplaying guild of all three factions to meet and discuss outlining epic events that put cohesion into the events and this war.

We weave the story with the FC one, create a running storyline that effects our own guilds and we share the responsiblilty for pulling off theses events, if each guild puts their energy to ONE even when it's their turn...we have events that ping pong back and forth across enemy lines that support stories and reason beyond random hits on each other and sloppy slurs.

I can nominate myself as secretary, you guild leaders and creative assistants be the story board members.

Think of the possibilities if you work ooc behind the game and shock and surprise your guildies when murder and mayhem strikes, black market loads get carried by neutrals, masked balls get raided.

If you are yawning and say it can't be done...wrong. I got eight guild stogether to do this on Everquest and it was an absolute blast.

Example (what we did there)
1. Festival of Karana....had huge festival, market, nakked drunk dwarf runs, etc. A murder happened, a mysterious orb was bought (held by guild A)
2. ooc hands off to guild 2 as planned . IC hunt for murderer unveils a vampire with plans, orb leads to investigating far off area (newly patched in) to archeologist that can read the runes
3. guild C starts vampire virus...each person they bite only hunts at night, etc, you get the idea..no one can find the predetermined head vampire
4. guild D finds the orb once unlocked reveals a prophecy
5. evil guild hides that the chosen one is born in guild E and hunt for his sword described in prophecy to rule all Norrath
5 guild e has equal good guy and hunt for the Twin Swords of Good and Evil
6. .....you get the idea.

We outline events to happen over a course of time, the panel keeps it secret but to the ruling council of their guild...
