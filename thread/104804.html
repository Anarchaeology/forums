A tribute to the Angels of Light
Four months ago, my best friend (Boglwe) and I sat on the bridge between Old Anthen and West Athens and discussed an idea. The idea for a guild here on Rubi-Ka. The guild would be based on a couple of simple concepts.

First, it would be an "online family" for its members. A place where members could come for fun, friendship, and support. Where they would feel welcomed when they logged on and missed when they camped out.

Second, we wanted to share our idea that there was more to be enjoyed on Rubi-Ka than levels, and power, and uber loot. We wanted to encourage our members to share their creative talents in-game with the other Rubi-Ka citizens. Not to say that we would ignore missions and hunting and quests and the like, we just wanted to balance that with roleplaying and social interactions.

As any of you who have done it can attest, starting a new guild is hard work. There is a lot that goes into getting it off the ground. Tenants to define, websites to complete, organization structure to figure out. Not to mention that you're trying to recruit members through all this too.

But, Boglwe and I were committed to making this succeed. We both made the sacrifices necessary and did so willingly. Many of our friends really didn't understand that, to us, not gaining a level for a month wasn't a big deal. We were having fun creating this guild together.

Our charter members were Boglwe, Stumm, Deadlykitty, Ceffia, Sanadvenie, and I. Boglwe was AFK at the time; so I was the one who had the honor of typing "/org create Angels of Light". We were standing in the lower hallway at Reets, just outside the Fireplace Room at the time.

The first words spoken on the Angels of Light guild chat were by Deadlykitty when she said "Okay, so what's the first order of business?"

I turned the Presidency of the guild over to Boglwe as we had planned. I became the Speaker for the guild. Responsible for getting our message out in-game and through our gridsite. I was always especially pleased when a new recruit would talk about how the site helped them realize that we were the sort of guild they wanted to belong to.

The guild grew steadily. Boglwe and I were very particular about who we allowed join the guild. Some people didn't like the application process. And, I suspect, few really understood all that we were doing in those interviews. But it helped us ensure that we were getting members who understood why we existed and were willing to help maintain that feeling.

Just over 3 months into our life as a guild we hit 100 members on rolls. Boglwe and I were amazed. Seeing the guild become successful was easily worth all that we had given up to get there. There is an amazing feeling to see your dreams coming true before your eyes.

I would never downplay the significance of having Boglwe as my partner in this. As others have pointed out on other threads here, she is a unique person. She has an amazing impact on those around her just by being who she is. I have learned a lot from her in my time on Rubi-Ka. It has been a sincere honor to continue to call her my friend for all that time. Almost every night, when we're going /AFK and getting some rest, one or the other of us will send a tell saying "Thank you". No other words are necessary. We know all that those two simple words entail for us.

I won't go into the events of the last week here. I don't believe in using public forums to discus private issues.

I love every Angel. I sincerely wish each and every one of them the best and all the happiness they deserve. They have been good friends to me and to each other. I hope those relationships remain strong even if the organization is dissolved.

I will be creating a new guild in the next couple days. The ideals will be the same. Some things will change, however. When Boglwe and I started the Angels, we told ourselves that even if the guild never grew beyond six people that we would learn a great deal in the process. Well, we have learned and we'll apply those lessons to the new guild.

I will be the President of the new organization. That will allow Boglwe to focus on the things that she does best. When we have formed the org (and have the gridsite running) I'll post here again to let everyone know what the status is.

For now, I know that several Angels are currently without guild. I'd welcome a chance to talk to you about what I'll be creating with this new organization. For that matter, anyone who's reading this is free to send me a tell and ask about what my plans are.

That's all I have to say for the moment. So, for the Angels who know me...

TTFN Angels !!!
