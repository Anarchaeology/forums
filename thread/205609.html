Bombs off in Borealis!
Three Bombs detonated yesterday at 22:04 Standard Rubi Kan time. Witnesses claimed nothing but beeping was heard, before the explosions went off. No structural damage was caused, however, due to the type of bombs, all organic materials within the structures were immediatly disintigrated.

Three different appartment complexes were targeted, chants of
"Ashes to Ashes, Dust to Dust" heard moments prior.

Twenty Seven are reported dead. Two children were succesfully reclaimed. Fourteen Omni-Tek employees were killed. Nine Neutrals have been killed. Four Clan members are dead. Kacey "Sempi" Jurik has been detained by the ICC for further questioning, as her apartment was a generating point of one of the bombs.

No alignment has been placed on the matter.
