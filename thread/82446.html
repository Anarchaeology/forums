Shadow Rangers Guild Rules
Owing to the sudden loss of our web site I am resorting to posting here for all our new members (sorry for posting here everyone)

Our Rules are as follows:

1) NO BEGGING!!

2) Trade between guild members is free, if you want money for it don't advertise it on guild chat.

3) There is no tax but donations to the bank is appreciated. This goes towards new towers and uniforms.

4) We have a uniform (Black Leather Trench Coat & Black Hood with White Trim, Black Miir Boots, Black Gloves) This is only compulsory to be worn at meetings and other formal functions. It is normally provided free upon joining.

5) We have a tower in Broken Shores (well we have one at time of writing this ) To get to it enter grid, go to centre and go to tower controller exit (save first is a good idea, especially if you have no way of getting back).

6) Upon Reaching lvl 10 you will be promoted to Unit Member. Reaching lvl 50 you will be promoted to Unit Leader. When you reach lvl100 you will become a unit commander. Unless you have a key role i.e. leader or sub-leader of a division you will not likely be promoted any higher.

Our Divisions are as follows; Intel (by invitation only), logistics/aquisitions, finacial, recruitment/ training, elite pvp squads, specialist-quest organisers, and social/meetings organisers.

7) Be excellent to each other

8) No swearing (well we all swear a little at some time or other, but constant use of profanities is frowned upon, and if it continues then demotion or expolsion from guild could be a likely result)

9) We strive to raise the average level of the guild at all times, so we look at the LOWEST LEVELS of INACTIVE players, send them a message and if not responded to withen 1 week, we will remove from the guild. The player can rejoin be simply sending a tell to somebody of joining rank.
