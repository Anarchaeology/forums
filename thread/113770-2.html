OOC:

It all happened on this one hot cloudless Saturday afternoon while I was walking down by the river. I caught a wasp in a mason jar and began squinting at it throught the sunlight and shaking the jar with all my might. I could feel the wasp's angry pulse buzzing through the glass. I knew that if the thing ever got loose I was doomed, that it would dog me forever and sting me with all the wrath of hell. It would calm down a bit , but it was impossible for me not to start shaking the jar and watch it bump off the sides trembeling and furious. I remember punching five holes in the lid with a ball point pen as I walked slowly back towards town. Soon I began dreaming about the wasp. Loud terrifying dreams, where the wasp would grow to the size of a bird and start hissing at me from beside the jar. I awoke holding the jar with a strange unknown smile on my lips. The next day I walked back to the river dead set on freeing the thing but the trip seemed to aggrovate the wasp then all my shaking combined. I realized as I drop the jar off the bridge into the river, and watched it sink slowly into the brown water, that I'd never planned on setting it free at all.
