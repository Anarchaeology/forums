I totally agree. Trying to balance the classes for PvP is impossible and a waste. PvP in an RPG is just about impossible anyway for this reason.

If you have two teams that are composed of essentially the same player classes, it is likely that the battle will be balanced. However, it just isn't going to happen very often. People keep saying that PvP in AO is broken, I submit that it cannot be fixed by the very fact that it is an RPG game. Different levels, different twinks. It isn't possible to balance such a system.

The only way to get purely balanced PvP in a game is to play something like Quake or UT where you have the exact same characters, the exact same weapons and the exact same power ups available. Of course, that would make a boring RPG. Who wants to play a role playing game where everyone has to be an Enforcer, and there is only one level?

If you want to play PvP in AO, then by all means go ahead, but don't whine that it is unbalanced. It cannot be balanced.
