Zoning should always be allowed. Those zones are artificial barriers that had to be put into the game in order to increase performance.

What should be allowed is for the attacker to continue the fight past the zone border. If you hop into a Whom'pa, I should be able to chase right after yer ass. A fight that is initiated should only be ended through the death of one player, that player logging off, or running into his apartment and locking the door.

Planetwide chases should be the norm. Scrambling to make it to the safety of the guards in Tir or Omni-E sounds about right.

Suppression gas should only control one's ability to initiate violent behavior prior to a fight. Once someone has put a bullet in your shoulder, I feel that you probably will be pissed off enough to follow whoever did it whereever they may run.

That's how I think it should be. Simply imagine how PvP would work if there were no 'zones' and apply it to what we have to deal with. It's only realistic that if you get shot at on a street and the bastard ducks into a shop, you will be busting in right after him.
