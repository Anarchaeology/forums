Sept. 11 Memorial
In memory of the Sept. 11 tragic day for not just the american members of this game but the many good people of the world, the Mercury Dragons will hold a "Silent Prayer" gathering in Athen at 6PM EST.

There will be 5 minutes of silence, in a show of respect for the innocent and heroes lost during the 2001 terrorist attack.

Please show your support and join us in our moment of silence.

Yes sad days indeed...
one year has passed since the tragic events of Sept. 11 shook our country, and many of its victims still hurt in the aftermath. The pain we as humans feel over this event is saddening...and the reality of it all can be overwhelming. But do not allow this to effect your better judgement...people in Afganistan, Iraq, and other countries hurt as well. Maybe not the warmongering elite who control their countries like Saadam Hussain or Bin Laden...but the general population is as innocent as those killed in Sept.11.

My sympathy and pain does not just end at this countries borders..I hurt for the victims in other countries as well. The mother whos 2 year old baby lies dead because of bad coordinations given to an American pilot...the oppressed woman of Afganistan.

Today we live in a world of hurt and pain...those of us who live in the first world countries and who are playing this game really don't see much of the real world. Pain is the daily bread of some, and inside of our candy-land type settings we tend to put our own hurt and pain over others.

My deepest sympathies go out to the victims of sept 11 and to those who have been caught up in this vicious crossfire. May god be with you all.
