Exploration stories
Post your exploration stories here, i consider my self to be a heck of an adventurer, i explored almost every part of every zone at lvl 55 in a kodiak, i have seen almost every mob in game, and i have only missed a couple landmarks, (unique mobs im just getting started on) but i want the community to post their adventuring stories. Heres a short little tidbit from one of my adventures.

Back in my First Order days, i had heard rumors that there were eremites and enigmatrees in the longest road, (turns out to be way wrong) and i wanted to go check em out. I started off in clon****, i forogt to pick up the map, so i used my good old basic gui planet map to guide me, i just started heading north through all the zones to get there. About half way, somewhere in stret west, i found a very humble little omni outpost, near a small lake with a waterfall near it, it had some bunkers and stores, a few omni guards, and a large mansion near it. This mansion was gargantuan, it had to of been 6 stories high, with a huge parking-lot type area infront of it, it looked very nice, but in the back, it was a total wasteland, slayers half in and out of the sand, and buckethead scavengers roaming around, then in the distance, i saw one of the stragest things on rubi-ka, there was a small puddle of water, with 6 Supa Leets all standiong in a circle, their movemnets were synchronized and it was quite cool. I then went up to the longest road after hitting many zone barracades, boy was i surprised when i saw my frist ottos and yottos, they were hilarious, i treked down some more, and found my self in a forest of decay, where i was attacked by Bileswarms, i couldnt defend myself but when i hit that reclaim, the shock of excitment overcame the res shock, and i was proud to be what i thought, the first enforcer to trek through the western lands in a kodiak.


Well, theres a story, more to come, please post some of your favorites.

Ok got two...


My first trip to Upper Stret East. I was about um... 20-25 at the time i think. Its been a while ago so memory of level isnt that clear

I took the route down from Old Athen, got chased over the rope bridge by a Vulture. Which was cool cos i was expecting the bridge to break Indiana Jones style
I flapped as fast as i could and managed to outfly it... anyway zoned into Upper Stret East and flapped around dodging Offals, Wolves and Primal Chirops. At one point i had to sneak round the side of a hill to avoid some Offals. To my right was a marshy area that i was gonna head into next to avoid Offals infront of me. Suddenly a HUGE black lump came into view. At the time for me it was an awesome sight cos it was easily the biggest creature i'd ever seen. It was kinda plodding inbetween all the trees...
Being the intrepid explorer i am, i decided to edge forward for a closer look and get a pic for my scrapbook. The closer i got the more in awe i was of its size. I was so in awe staring at it that i didnt notice it had turned round and charged at me ...
*Nokken hits you for 2190 melee damage*
*splat ... wakes up with a headache*

I cant wait til im high enough level to take down one of those things

Second story:
I'd heard rumours of Eremites in Aegean so i got all my kit together and set off to find one. Entered the Danger Dunes area and wandered around a bit. There it was! My map was reading a Desert Eremite dead ahead! But where was it? I couldnt see it... (i didnt know they burrowed at the time)
I cautiously moved forward straining my eyes to see it. I was even looking up in the sky incase it was a flying creature hehe. Suddenly i get the warning of being under attack. Sure enough its the Eremite. And then ... I see the most amazing thing of my Rubi-Ka experience. Even to this day its my best memory.
The speed at which that dot raced towards me on the map was unbelievable... and i still couldnt see it. By now it was on top of me and i still hadnt run cos i wanted a look before i got eaten.
*POP* It flew up out of the ground and towered above me. I had about a second for my jaw to drop and my eyes to glaze over before it sliced me into a thousand peices. I went back a few levels later and got an amazing pic of it as it towers above ya ready to strike. Its my most prized screenie

Hope these didnt bore ya too much. Probably my two favourites of my early exploring days

I remember my first month with AO. Before I got my new computer, I played on an old piece of junk similar to my bureaucrat droid in quality. I think that is the closest any computer has been not being able to run AO.... It barely worked, but it was a marvellous experience nonetheless.

First time I exited Athen Old (that was starting area in August), I was lvl 3. I went out because my adventurer was, believe me, not able to do 1% missions! Anyway, I found West Athen pretty soon, though I approached it with great caution. See, I had seen screenshots and articles about PvP, and one shot looked very like West Athen.

Next thing I did was exiting the opposite Athen Old gate, trying to get to a mission in Aegan, still lvl 3-4. First thing that happened in Aegan was me being slaughtered by an Anun, and I swear, I've never seen Anuns in Aegan after that! I had a funny tendency to run into uber aggro mobs wherever I went.

My first team I had with an MP. We went to the Beach in Athen Shire, and the place was swarming with creatures (this was before patch 12.5 (or whichever killed spawn)). We took out a number of orange Malles, until my friend got killed. I managed to avenge him, just as a Rollerrat decided to let loose its hate on me. I ran, but to this date, I can still not understand how I did what I did. I just know that I thought I ran back to the road where I came from, but it had to be in the opposite direction. After running in panic for some minutes, I was saved by a passing guy who killed the rat.

At that moment, I was in a place which I cannot remember. It was like a forest, with a steep slope alongside. I decided to ask for directions by another guy I saw. But it wasn't a player, it was a mutant! That was a very nasty shock, and even though it didn't aggro, I panicked again, running right into a zone border.

I had barely read the words "Holes in the Wall" before I was attacked by a Virulent Minibull. I ran up the aforementioned slope, but it managed to wipe me out. The reclaim terminal was for the first and only time a relieving sight.

Because of that experience alone, I stayed out of Holes in the Wall for about 4 months, even though it probably is the least dangerous zone on Rubi-Ka! :-)

When the game had just been released most areas were pretty unexplored, so once I got to level 5 I went sightseeing. I saw the Notum Cannon in Klon****, where I also spotted my first Gargantua (which I very carefully sneaked past ) and found an overgrown little town in the south part, very cool.

I went on a safari to see the Bronto Mamas in Broken Shores, that was awesome as we "camped out" overnight around a campfire (can't remember the nano, but it left a small flame on the ground, which looked like a fire at night), that one even featured in an article I wrote for swedish PC Gamer.

Accidentally gridded to Meetmedere and couldn't grid back. I decided to walk back to Omni via 4holes. This took a few days, but I had quite a few experiences, and saw some unusual (for an Omni player) sights. The final run to 4holes was pretty damn exciting, sneaking around vultures and avoiding wild clanners like the plague. One of them actually wished me luck, but the rest all tried to kill me even though I announced my peaceful intentions .

Made a neutral Atrox and walked east from Borealis, trying to reach the Stret, but ended up dying by some huge castle. I did find some interesting stuff, though, like a glowing volcano and a junkyard for old spaceships in the middle of a desert.

Oh yeah, finding the glassed-in model of Galway was kinda cool as well.

Lost character
I once had an MP which I got to about lvl 40 until I went exploring.

The MP was a clanner and as I'd seen plenty of dessert I thought I might go try and see the sea.

I took the whompa from Athen to broken shores and headed south.

I suspect anybody above 80 probably knows what I'm going to describe but...

The terrain was initially marshy and two small streams drained it south. Eventually this upland marsh gave way to rolling hills which turned to dessert.

I ran south across the dessert and eventually encountered some large half buried skeletons and a sort of empty tomb/temple with many small beetles scurrying around.

I don't know how I managed to avoid all encounters but a little way south I came to a very large (about 1 mile across) volcanic crater jutting out of the dessert. The crater was filled with water with a pyramid at the centre. The crater had invisible walls all around so I worked my way around the rim.

To my surprise I eventually came to a town with many lvl 80+ characters soliciting each other for grouping. The town was called "Home". Lulled into a false sense of security by this I made a very big mistake and saved there.

I then tried unsuccesfully to carry on South to reach the sea many times but always died within sight of the town.

Eventually bored I started heading back due north to the whompa and to cut a long story short never managed to get back to civilisation alive. I must have tried about 20 times dying pretty soon after leaving Home each time and respawning in Home. I even went back a couple of days later and tried again. One of the local high lvls took pity on me one time and gave me a massive run buff and followed me half way back - still didn't make it.

Eventually I dumped the character there - where to this day I guess it must be sitting looking out over the lake wondering what's going on in the rest of the world
