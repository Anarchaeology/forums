implants require 2*ql+4 in abilities. So without buffing up or stepping implants you miss 1 point in a relevant ability for ql 30 implants (this is assuming you are a solitus breed). And a simple boost will get you in them.

You can get this a lot further but that is going to cost you (compared to your income at lvl 19). And at the extremer ends you need loads of NCU to be able to hold all the buffs needed and a bunch of tricks to get your treatment up to a level that you can get them in.

I'll use agility & stamina (most likely the abilities you will encounter making your implants) to explain what is possible.

Agility.
Ql 30 agility implants give a total of 24 extra agility(12/7/5). With 63 (maxxed agility at 19) & 12 for general buff you get to 99, this is 5 short of getting in ql 50 implants. This can be solved by looking for feline grace.
requirements are stamina, agility, stamina. (shining leg, bright feet, faded waist)
Note, you can't use the feet implant to replace it self with ql 50 implants so for that one to be replaced our agility effectively needs to be 111 (still well within the limits of feline grace).
Replacing the ql30 feet implant with a ql50 one results in a 3 pts increase in agility. the total increase is 34 points for a set of ql50 nano's (17/10/7)
This makes it possible to get in any ql50 implant that doesn't replace waist, leg or feet and is based on agility by yourself at lvl19. 63+34+12 = 109, 5 more then needed for a ql 50 implant

Stamina
Stamina is slightly easier to buff into (more buffs to do it).
The problem is that the faded cluster goes into the same spot as agility in the waist, I go for the agility cluster here since stamina is easier to buff)
Ql 30 stamina implants give a total of 24 extra stamina. With 63 (maxxed stamina at 19) & 12 for general buff you get to 99, this is 5 short of getting in ql 50. This can be solved by looking for any enforce essence or enlarge/Iron circle from any doc around.
requires str, sta, sta (shining chest, bright leg, faded waist).

The double stamina requirement makes it a bit harder to equip yourself into ql 50 stamina implants.
The strenght requirement can be solved by a 40 pt str nano that martial artists have (1 pt short on lvl 19 by maxxed str before buffing with strength boost [haven't tried it yet so could be that it can't be done]), or iron circle and a 20 enforcer buff. Replacing the ql30 chest with a ql 50 chest gives 5 extra stamina what compensates for the loss of the 5 pts stamina a ql30 waist gives (if you plan to replace it with a ql50 stamina waist). A ql 50 bright leg implant requires 5 pts more stamina (if you stick in the shining & faded ql 50 first). Which is still only 10 more then you can do without an enforcer buff or enlarge from a doc.
This again results your ability to get ql50 implants in at lvl 19 on your own.


The next step is adding buffs to what you now have.
Implants requiring agility can be upgraded to ql 65 at lvl19.
This is using feline grace ontop of all the other stuff mentioned above.
This is not a type found in the shop. It will take you to level 23 to be able to get into the next shop level (ql70) implants without the use of specific items to increase your agility by 10.

Stamina is a tad different.
You can max buff stamina by 47 points over the general boost
You've already have 109, need at least 144 for ql70 implants.
that is 20 from iron circle (doc) leaving 15 points in either 2 pillows (low level add 8 str/sta each) or a 15 point enforcer buff.
If you combine the buffs with pillows so that you get another 10 points (for a total temp of 154 stamina) you can replace your bright & faded stamina implant by ql70 ones, though this is kind of useless since it adds only 5 points and the gap to ql90 implants still is (without a non implant/non weapon item) 7 points with max buffing. That is at lvl 19, so you can technically get ql 90 implants in at lvl 21 (treatment is going to be a problem here though).
