Estimated Weapon Results


Everlasting Bouquet of Flowers
Estimated Damage Range: 6-6 (6)

In 30s with a cycle speed of 2s, (1s Attack, 1s Recharge) you would have attacked 15 times. Total damage in 30s would be around 90 points.

Special attack damage in 30s would be around 3255 points, bringing total damage to around 3345 points.

Your average normal hit would be around 6 points.
Fast Attacks in 30s would be approx. 2 @ 28s, adding around 12 points.
Brawls in 30s would be approx. 3 @ 15s, adding around 1081 points each for 3243 points total.

Results were calculated at 0.97 times normal and 0.03 times critical damage.
ACs reduced your average damage by around 0 points per hit.

Notes:
Results are rounded to the nearest whole attack. 'Partial' attacks are ignored. For the sake of accuracy, reloads (if applicible) are inserted to the time simulation as they appear. AC is calculated at -1 point of damage for every 10 points.

AggDef has been revamped to use -.25s/-.25s at 100% and +1.75s/+1.75s at 0%. Every 1/8th of the bar is now worth .25s, with the 88% mark being neutral. Initiatves are calculated at 1s of attack and 2s of recharge for every 600 points of initiative.

Special attack recycle formulas are in testing and may or may not be accurate yet. Special attack cycles assume all specials are used in an initial alpha strike
