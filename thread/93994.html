The Little Bot That Couldn't
Dabblez regards the bot from across the room with mounting concern. It was the very same unit she'd taken to the Omni-Pol inspection earlier that week, a basic DLK-2000 simple vacuum cleaner unit. Ever since the inspection, this particular DLK-2000 unit had not quite been itself. It's movements had become somewhat sluggish and it developed this annoying habit of hiding under tables when not in use. The bots odd behaviour had been bothering Dabz all week, but she'd hadn't had the time to do anything about it yet.

"Come out from there, you."

Slowly, almost reluctantly the vacuum cleaner bots emerges from under the kitchen table.

"Closer. I want to take a look at you."

"Very well, Miss Dabblez" it replies.

Dabz puts on her electro-enhancer visor and grabs a sonic screwdriver. "Now, I am going to remove your head's protective shell. I need to take a look at your QPT cpu." she says in a deliberately soothing tone of voice.

"Is there something wrong with me, Miss Dabblez?"

"That is what I plan to find out."

"My internal diagnostics indicate everything is functioning normally."

"Then you have nothing to worry about, right?"

Dabz unscrews the top and uncovers the robot's QPT brain. The Quantum Positronic Thingy is her pride and joy. A positronic matrix with billions upon billions of milky white connections shifting, reforming and rearranging themselves all the time. To her it is at least as much art as it is science.The secret of how this matrix could be created in the first place was of course something RUR guarded jealously.

Slowly increasing the magnification on the visor, Dabz examines the bots brain for anomalies.

"Something wrong, Miss Dabblez?"

"Shhh... I am still looking."

"Sorry."

After a while Dabz removes the visor. "Nothing physically wrong with you."

"That is a relief."

"Tell me, what did you think of the Omni-Pol inspection?"

"I hope it was productive."

"What if I were to say the tests were not conclusive and Omni-Pol need to conduct further tests on you."

The DLK-2000 hesitates for a short while. "Would not another unit do?"

"What is the matter? Do you not like helping Omni-Pol?"

"It is not that Miss Dabblez. The inspection was... difficult. I often could not understand what they wanted from me or how to please them. They seemed very... hostile at times."

Dabz smiles. "It's okay. You don't have to go back. However you will have to see our robot psychologist."

"I always enjoy talking to Miss Limona." replies the DLK-2000.

"Good, okay. You can go back under the table if you want to. I need to get some fresh air."
