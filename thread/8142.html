More flexibility with names...
For those who are new or those who decide it would be fun to try a change of character, it's getting increasingly difficult to get a name that isn't already taken or some kind of hybrid of an existing name. Such as adding an "a" or an extra "l" or something like that.

Instead of player-made names being just "one" name only, please give us the option of choosing our own first and last names, or two-part names. Like those who wish to use a "-" in between two names or a capital letter in the middle to distinguish the 2 parts of the name.

It would help to create a little more variety.

Thanks,
