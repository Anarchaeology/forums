Overequipping, PvE and some PvP balance
While there is a whole crap load about the 'forseen' nerf to overequipping, this could turn out to be a blessing or a nightmare.

Yes it does suck that some classes cannot overequip weapons (MA's (fists only) Nanotechs, etc) but there is one reason why some classes do overequip that any weapon user can tell you.

If you use weapons near your level, every single monster your level will also beat the living crap out of you.

Thus one of the reasons people over-equip to begin with, survival on a PvE scale. However this scale horribly tips the scale on a PvP encounter, as other players who cannot over-equip suffer because they do not have the same damage output as the super weapon users and do not have the insane HP of mobs to compensate for the damage they take.

If they eliminate over-equipping to a certain degree (the new idea looks kind of well planned with different steps of over-equip'ability').

There are a few things that can make this implementation work greatly:

1) Remove the PvP half damage. (I'll get to this a bit later)

2) Rebalance PvE HP ratio to compensate for lower damage output on guns. (and because mob HP are ridiculous to begin with)

3) Tighten the min-max damage ratio on guns (1 - xxx) is horrible

4) Up the min-max damage slightly for classes like MAs or make their fists dual wielded if possible

5) Lower Nanotech spell damage slightly after PvP damage is restored if too unbalanced. (Give them their range back too)
edit: Or allow spell casters to overequip slightly as well, but at the cost of increased Nano usage and Fumble rates.

6) Off Topic: Fix the damn millions of weapons that are pure garbage!

Now onto the explanation of #1 Remove the PvP half damage.

This is just a band-aid on a gaping puncture wound. It was necessary to supposedly "help" stop 1.5 second PvP battles but has done very little to help the lacking PvP scene in general.

Overequipping was one of the main problems leading to insta kills during PvP as a combo of fling shot, burst, etc with a gun way above a players level would just destroy almost anyone within that level range.

Fixing overequipping can help to rebalance things a bit and make the point of having half damage a moot point. Also with the current state of half damage, it has horribly unbalanced healing, as players are healing for the same amount, but taking less damage (this issue shouldn't even need to be discussed).

If I come up with any more constructive ideas I'll post them.
