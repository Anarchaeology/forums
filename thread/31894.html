14.4 and Boss drop items
With the upcoming 14.4 patch, we will now have new Adventurer, MA and Agent (from 14.2) nanos confirmed as being boss drop only. While it doesn't say so explicitly in the notes, it's likely the new Fixer nanos will also be boss drop.

This is a lot of different nano lines, which means the likelihood of obtaining the one your character needs is fairly low on any particular mission. This translates into running a large number of missions for the nanos you need. I find this particularly harsh, given that the Adventurer, Agent and Fixer nanos are explicitly class balance items.

So, I'd like to invite an FC representative to provide us with an explicit rationale for why these nanos are boss drop only. If they will, I'd like to see them address the following:

* Why are these nanos not available as mission rewards?
* Why are these nanos not available in stores?

Putting new items on bosses only serves a two-fold purpose for the Funcom dev team.

#1: It allows them to somewhat regulate the flow of items into the game. While I disagree that they need to do this with the things they're adding into the game to balance classes out, with things they're adding as "bonuses", it's completely acceptable, as the only other established way to regulate flow of an item into a game is through EQ-esque camping. I'd much rather have boss-loot only.

#2: It encourages people to socialize and join up with each other. The main reason EQ succeeded as well as it did was not the gaming model itself (obviously, given how many people have such strong misgivings with it), but rather, the human interaction and interdependence that that game REQUIRES. If everyone in AO knew how to play their class as well as people in EQ know how to play theirs (out of necessity there), then grouping would be much more enjoyable, people would meet more other people, orgs would get larger and participate in player run story and events more, and all in all, more of the vision of an MMORPG as a shell in which players fill the storyline and flesh out the world would take place.

I've been having debates with my brother for the past few days about these very points, well, the socializing issue at least, and even though I hated the forced grouping in EQ at the time, I can see why it was done now, looking back. With enough players in a game world, it's a very viable thing to do.

Soloing is boring, and bored people tend to leave games.
