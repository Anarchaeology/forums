FunCom is supposed to be playing those characters like Phillip Ross, Henry Radiman, et. al. That is one of the reasons we were told that we had to pay an extra three dollars a month over the prevailing online game fee when AO was released.

AO was supposed to have a story behind it that was written by the author of one of the most critically acclaimed adventure games of all time. Phillip Ross was to have his part in this epic story that would span four years. We were supposed to bear witness to this man Phillip Ross as he reacted to and shaped the events on the planet we inhabit.

Only FunCom knows how the story progresses and eventually ends. Therefor only FunCom can portray such key characters as Omni-Tek CEO Phillip Ross. The customer base does not know what Ross' next move is going to be. Only FunCom knows.

For all we know, Ross could turn out to be truly an enlightened man and working for the greater good of all Rubi'Ka as well as his employers.

If a customer were to attempt to play Phillip Ross, they might do something that is in direct violation with the story.
