The only way I can see that this would work is if FC personel reviewed each and every custom avatar, and only promised an attempted 168 hour turnaround.

I'd rather give the ability to anyone BUT the thread starter the ability to bump a thread.

If the initiator of a thread bumps a thread, he's just trying to be heard, when everyone's trying to ignore him.

If someone else bumps a thread, it's because he's read it and wanted others to take notice too.

I trust a third persons perspective of an opinion more than a second persons perspective of his own opinion.

However, you'll never really be able to prevent people from bumping their own threads because they can just come back and bump them with a different account.

Now the idea of a limited number of bumps per week might be good.

However, filtering for the word "bump" would have killed this post, as well as the post immediately above this one.

It'd also kill any post that used the word bump without BEING a bump.

Also, some people modify the word "bump", such as "bumpage", "bumpity", "buuummmp", etc. What if people just started avoiding word "bump" to continue to bump as they have been? Maybe the word "ping" could be used instead, or "bop", "spike", "jump", "dito", etc. Would the filter then be expanded to include these?

Essentially, bumping a thread IS expressing an opinion. Basically that the above disserves your attention, and should stay on top a little longer so more people will notice it. I don't think the author should bump his own works, but I actually have no problem with others bumping a thread for the reason stated above.

A bump button that ANYONE could use, but that could only be activated from the last page, and that could only be used 10 times a week per account, but that didn't clutter the thread with line after line of posts that only bump the thread, now that'd be good.
