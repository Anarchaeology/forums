The cost of creating a nano formula!
Okay.. I rerolled my Engineer because I wanted to concentrate on trade skills. I'm up to level 35 now and its been fun.. I've created a few guns using the VERY few recipes that actually work. Will be more fun once they fix them.. but anyway, thats a whole nother thread...

Recently I decided I'd make a nano formula. I got a disc for Quick Fix (QL14) which I wanted to heal my pets and I figured I'd learn the process for making nanos. So I start the process.. simple enough.. go through the first few stages, no problem.. things cost about what I expected. Then I hit the Carbonrich Rock stage.. wow.. I spent 3 days looking for a rock. People were selling them for insane prices on the shopping channels but then luckily one of my guild members had a QL27 one which they gave to me. Okay.. so I buy the Jensen Ore thing and process the rock.. good, now were getting somewhere. The next stage requires you to purchase another item.. no problem.. I go to the shop and I find one.. it only needs to be half of the QL of the rock so I find a QL14 one.. 70k!!!!! 70k for a QL14 item? Something is not right with this. So then I decide to look at the next step.. I find that item and find its 300k!! What is up with this??

I didn't even bother looking at the next steps... but I would imagine when its all said and done this nano formula is probably going to cost me 700k - 1mil credits to finally build it. Give me a break! Its QL14. What about when we get to these QL100 discs.. you really expect people to spend 5mil credits to make a nano? Why is this so expensive? What is the explaination here?

Generally.. aren't "trade" skills supposed to allow you to make a profit? You know.. build something from scratch for someone and then you can sell it for profit. Tell me where the profit is when I can go to a shop and buy a nano for 15k yet it costs me 800k to make it myself. (Yes, I know.. some nanos can't be found in stores.. but still.. I think you get my point).

So.. Funcom what is the official word here? I would like some kind of explaination for why this process is so costly. Anyone? Anyone?
