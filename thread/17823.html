Silver Petal Fashion Show
Unity of the Rose will be hosting a fashion show at The Cup on Saturday, April 6th at 3pm EST (8pm GMT). There will be four prizes given in three categories. The categories are: Most Fashionable, Best Presentation, and Crowd Favorite. The forth award is the "Silver Petal" award, which will be given to an over-all winner that may or may not be a winner in one of the other categories. Best Presentation is an evaluation of how the model presents herself to the crowd while on the runway.

If you would like to participate as a model, email me at szentasha@darksphere.com with a brief description to be read as you are announced during the show. For example: "Szentasha stays cool in traditional Rhinoman battle attire good for hot days in the Newland Desert." Detailed instructions for models will be available on our web site sometime tonight. There will be a limit of 2 models from any one guild. All sexes and breeds are welcome to model.

The announcer for this event will be Tarryk.

Our web site is http://www.darksphere.com/uor.

Hope to see you there!
