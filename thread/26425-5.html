Damage buffs
-Helepolis of the Besieger

As a side note, this is the damage buff you can hope to get before Helepolis

-Persistent Damage Amplifier

AC buffs(top of the line)
-Heavy Assault Absorption Shield
This is actually the second best AC buff, Engineers can buff better, but the soldier best blows the Adventurer one out of the water. I don't have any issues with our AC line.
