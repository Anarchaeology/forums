Monster/player loading distances in different zones
Well, I went out to hunt in Milky Way today, we were at the LBer ruins (lotta lifebleeder variants, but not many LBers there) killing some Chaos Minions and Fearplanters and stuff.. but we were having a HELL of a problem with the view distance in this zone. It was so short for us there that most of the time, by the time a monster loaded, it was already agro against us. This meant we couldn't avoid groups of monsters or anything, and eventually ended up getting killed when we walked blindly into a big patch of agro mobs, all of which attacked us the second we loaded them.

Now, I'm not sure if this is intentional or not. Some zones just seem to have such a nightmarishly short loading distance that it really turns the zone into a hell hole, Milky Way being one of em. I thought maybe they put it into high level zones to make them more of a challenge, but I've also hunted in Mort and Deep Artery Valley, which have level 150+ monsters, and the view distance there is fine. In Mort especially its a healthy 80 meters. In contrast, I've also been to mid/lower level zones where the loading distance is so pathetically short you can't even use /follow mode effectively (such as Clon**** or Stret East). I've tried to /follow people through Clon**** and have them literally unload and reappear right under my feet, ruining my follow.

It seems like in these zones there are even areas where a kind of wall of unloading exists. Anyone on one side of the invisible wall automatically unloads everyone on the other side, even if they are just 2 or 3 meters apart. In fact, I've been killed in Clon**** because some of the plants there would chomp me to death through one of these walls of unloading totally without me knowing about it and without getting a single damage message.

All I'd like to know is.. is this an INTENDED thing that affects all players equally, or some kind of a horrendous bug that makes Milky Way a Nanotechs worst nightmare.
