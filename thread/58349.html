Greetings All,

Just a quick note on "Anarchism" org types...

As it stands right now, any member of an anarchy-based organization has the abilities that a typical org leader has.

That includes renaming, recruiting, and setting org types. There are no promotions, as anarchies are flat org types. Essentially you have to really trust those in the organization with you; as any member could change the org type and assume control at any given moment.

The only thing that you can't do, to my knowledge, is get access to the Org Leader's chat channel; anarchies don't have this as an option, for some reason. Have to check it out with our org on live and see if that's changed, though. :-)

Hope that helps a bit.

Hello Again,

I'm glad to hear that the earlier info shed a bit more light on your questions, Takvir. You do raise some good ones in your follow up, as well.

To answer you though, first -- no, I haven't been able to find any information on anarchism organizations, on the net, either. My experience in such comes from being a member of Leet-Fu. Our org type has been an anarchy since its inception, and has worked well for us. It does, though; as I mentioned, require a great deal of trust in your fellow org mates.

The fact that anarchy-based org's don't have leader chat, and are left open for all members to control, do seem to be design-intended issues. At least, that's my take on it. The only time, at present, where a majority vote is needed in an anarchy, is when a member needs to be removed. I don't recall the exact procedure for this; but I believe that once the issue is put forth, at least two other members of the org have to /org remove the individual for that person to be gone.

Back to your questions, though, you raise a very interesting question about tower control, for anarchies, as well. I'd be curious to hear the answer to this one, myself. Any information that we could be given on this would be greatly appreciated.
