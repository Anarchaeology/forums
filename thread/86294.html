Happy new year to one and all.
Greetings,


As we all celebrate the passing of another year, let us reflect on 29476; a year of much turmoil. 29476 saw the ICC's ruling of "free" notum mining by privateers, the Dust Brigade, the scattering of the Council of Truth, but it also held a year's worth of experiences unique to each and every one of us; good experiences we can look back to fondly, and unfortunate ones we must consider with a smile no matter what.

The Past is part of what we are; it shapes us, teaches us, and 29476 is now a part of the ongoing chain of events that makes up our lives.

Here's to the Past.

And to the Future. May 29477 be a good year for us all.
