Breakout
(This is a short write up of an adventure currently being run by the South Rubi-Ka Society)

Modigliani, the curator of the Modligliani Art Gallery invites the Society to diner at Reets Retreat to discuss a matter describes as sensitive. Skybert, Poisonivy, Savoy and the Societys long-standing friend, Resetgun accept his invitation and set off to meet him.

After rushing through dinner, Modigliani gets to the heart of the matter. He has recently learned that the clan artist Miroslav Borky, famous for Liberty Leading the People Avec Leet (see link http://rubika.no-ip.org/srs/srsart02.htm ) has not been dead for these past 40 years as is generally believed. He is actually in a clan labour camp in the Wailing Wastes. Borky was sent to this labour camp by his clans warlord, Hugo IV Ravenskull, better known as Hugo the Terrible. Hugo has pathological distrust for intellectuals of any kind. When Hugo took over the leadership of the Ravenskulls from his father, he instigated a purge against artists, writers and philosophers. Those in his clan he did not have executed were sent to labour camps as common criminals.

Modigliani remarks how we may have never learned about Borkys fate were it not for the fact his gallery was able to purchase Liberty Leading the People Avec Leet from a private collection. The news that his works was being displayed in Omni-1 must have reached Magda Borky (Miroslavs wife) all the way up into Ravenskull lands. She had never realised her husband was famous, never really paid much attention to his art. Still she figured if people in Omni cared enough to exhibit his painting, maybe they could help her husband regain his freedom. To that end, and at great personal risk, she managed to get this message to Modigliani.

Modigliani barely has time to finish his tale when Skybert exclaims We go north, we crack some Ravenskull skulls and take Borky back.

Ivy adds, Skyberts right. Ive heard about Hugo the Terrible. You cant negotiate with him.

Savoy nods gravely. Okay, okay. Im with you on this one. But lets not go off half-cocked. Give me a week to gather more intelligence on this labour camp and come up with a plan.

And which point Modigliani breaks into tears. I.. I really dont know how to thank you guys. This means so much to me and to Rubi-Ka.

Part II - the conclusion
Skybert, Heksa, Wolf2020 (the new Chief of Security for the Society), Savoy and Resetgun, who once again has volunteered to help the SR,S set out for the Wailing Wastes and to the location where of the clan labour camp. On the way they notice several times they are being followed. When the prison camp is in sight and they can delay no longer, they turn to confront the man tailing them. This turns out to be Angdao. Angdao it transpires had overheard snippets the party's conversation as the were setting out from Reets to resolved to offer his help. The party are unsure. Some of them have heard of Angdao. His reputation his far from pristine. Still he could be more of a danger roaming out there on his own and truth be known, they could use another gun.

Heksa goes a ahead, sneaks into the clan labour camp. Conditions there are barbaric. She notes the locations of the guards and with difficulty makes out Miroslav Borky among the convicts. She turns back to report to the party.

Based on Heksa's intelligence they draw up a relatively simple plan. While half the party creates a diversion to draw the guards away, the rest rush in and rescue Borky. However they do not take into a account the sudden sandstorm that hits the camp just as they move in and which reduces visibility to nil. The party manages to get Borky away, but not without out incident and Wolf and Angdao are both shot down.

The survivors take a visibly shaken and confused Borky to Newland. There Savoy hands him new documents and a new identity as an Omni citizen. His forty year ordeal is finally over.
