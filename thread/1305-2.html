How about this: Funcom, clone 20 LVL 40-75 characters from the current player population. Those levels should do fine, as this seems to be the point where the hardships really begin. Now set yourselves goals such as:

- Attempt to assemble and install implants 35 levels above your current level. This will allow you to try...

- ...To acquire and equip a weapon (nano, skill) that actually does any serious damage vs. the yellow-green mobs in missions. This will most likely be a weapon at quality level greater than 1.6 x your current level.

- If cannot find such a weapon, build one (spend time/money collecting all the parts, e.t.c.)

- Now that you found such a weapon find all the buffs necessary to to put it on.

- Attempt to collect enough money to purchase a full suit of _good_ armor; one that is bad in at most 2 damage classes, and one that's about 20 points above your current quality level. Alternatively attempt to find all this armor in missions (read: run 75% difficulty missions)

- Do all this and level so that you can allocate IP needed to wear all this armor.

- Try to meet interesting people that would like to play with you on a team (read: you don't die all the time, and deal out some non-trivial damage)

- After acquiring all this equipment, go and try PVP and don't die to a rookie/freshman the first 20 times (read, this is not unreal tournament, it's not just about how big of a gun you wield, and how fast can you run/target/fire)

- Do all this for about 400,000 credits*

- Live with constant unannounced changes to the rules of the game (made your co-workers), which can make up to 5% of your current IP allocation useless (read: you just lost 3 levels worth of experience)

- Offer suggestions for improvement to your management
---------------------------------------------
* 400K is an arbitrary number, choose your own. I figured that at approximately 30k earned from mission, and being able to do about 2 missions a day, one could make about 420k. Those of you who play 24x7, or those with alternative chars above level 150** do not count We all know how much a treatment buff can cost. And the cost of buying a matching implants is simply outrageous.
