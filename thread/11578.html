How is it a bug?

The moment Mongo is cast, it raises max HP.
10-11 ticks (30-33 secs) of the HOT and your current HP will equal your max.

Special attacks can do a maximum 40% of your maximum HP.

Not 40% of your current HP.


-----------------

All HP buffs raise your maximum HP....They don't raise and heal your max HP.

If a doctor buffs your max HP during PvP, the maximum (40%) special attack damage that can be done to you will increase according to the increase in max HP.
