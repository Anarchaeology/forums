I'll throw in some ranged weapons, I'm not that an expert on melee.

Shotgun
Info: +4 skill, Shining implantable +- 800 skill @ 200
  • Home defender Good high dmg-high crit gun, slow. Exellent shotgun-choice in PvM, a no-no in PvP because of its speed and lack of specials. Nice for the IP-hungry too.
  • Ithaca Ki-8 Crit-or-die gun, fast gun that has nice mods (adds to evades). In PvM you will need a good 10%+ scope to do some dmg, in PvP you can't do dmg anymore because of the halved crits. It's nice and fast AS makes this an okay gun, going full def and only using AS for example.
  • Krutt Great mods on it, but sadly after critnerf not viable anymore; its both crit-or-die, has no AS and is slow. PvM it would be okay with a nice scope, but here are better guns.
  • Vektor Much like the Home Defender; but with a nice psychic mod to it. Most doctors go for Vektor at the lower levels, then switch to Home Defender.

Rifle
Info: +3 skill, Triple implantable (but conflicts with NR) +- 820 skill @ 200, buffable by a lot.
  • X3 Rifle The best PvM Rifle; great dmg and good crits, failrly slow so you'd need a TIMscope for it. In PvP its an okay weapon, but you might want to switch to:
  • Caterwaul A great PvP rifle; with maxed AS you will do 17ish sec AS' Use a QL250 VE scope to get the most out of AS.

Pistol
Info: Cheap +4 skills, Shining implantable, around 850 skill @ 200.
  • Pain of Patricia If you are able to get your hands on this camp-gun then your set; nice specials, great damage. Sadly you may need to triple implant pistol for this so you may need towers/special items to compensate for the MC/T&S loss.
  • Electronium v3 A nice cheapskill no-specials gun, great if you dont have IP to spare and think you can get your hand on a PoP later on. Also handy if you want to use some nanoskill enhancing pistols

To toss in my two cents... I've used home defenders, MA, MA + parry stick, and (currently) a mausser chemical streamer.

MA + parry stick with brawl would be the absolute best damage-per-second doctor setup in title 5 and 6 if you didn't care about doing damage in PvP at all. With MA + parry stick I consistently outdamaged X-3 docs and ithaca docs, even counting their AS. In fact, if you have IP for parry as well, you can self-equip a ql 200 parry stick and drop it off to use Blessed With Thunder for a "third DoT" when soloing or fighting a boss, then re-equip it.

PvP of course melee is just a no-go, especially for a doctor.

Letah already hit on the good points of the home defender. Interesting to note: If the Nova Flow assault rifle didn't have specials, the HD would outdamage it consistently over 30 seconds time at equal attack rating, inits, and crit chance. This makes it very good for longer fights, such as bosses.

The chemical streamer is a decent balance between PvP and PvM, with a hyper-fast burst recycle making up for its relatively slow attack cycle and lower-than-manex regular damage. Generally speaking the MCS and manex are considered equal by fixers; for doctors I consider the MCS superior because of the faster burst - burst doesn't ever miss, and we're plagued with a low attack rating for SMG. It's also good for PvP - sit on low agg/def and just burst away. The downside is that it lacks long range, and burst - while great in PvM - is highly variable and unreliable in PvP.

I have said repeatedly that I think the best PvP weapon for tower battles is the caterwaul; this is because it's got a long range and a fast AS. Don't underestimate the importance of range on a mass battlefield; with crowd control and people running around and roots and snares, being able to shoot back at anyone who can possibly attack you is very important.
