Sol Banking Corporation
The SBC has been on Rubi-Ka for a while now. They have provided the citizens with their own personal bank, but nothing more. I think it is about time they expand their business. First of all they should let us store credits in the bank. A safe place for our hard earned little ones. Also, they should set up offices in the big cities where they could provide the citizens with some more services, like transfer of items and cash between customers.

The current bank terminals for accessing your own account didnt need to be changed. Only the new offices would provide the extra services.

Just a thought.
