Furniture Would Be Neat
I'd love to have furniture, but there's a problem. Currently, all purchased items have the "advantage" of being rendered but not actually have any substance to them. If you buy a fridge, for example, you can walk right through it.

So if you buy a chair or a couch, you wouldn't be able to sit on it! Unless, of course, it was turned into a stable object with substance. But this would raise the issues of people just dropping them anywhere. Buy 5 couches and you could literally set up an impassible roadblock somewhere.

And then there's the chance that it's not even possible to code in a droppable item with physical substance to it.

So perhaps there should be a way to petition for a specific layout in one's apartment? Maybe ask some sort of terminal or sent a specific type of petition that would result in a sturdy couch, table, and chairs "materializing" in one's apartment? Maybe give players a choice of several different layouts and styles of furniture, but have them preset in their position and appearance.

Something like that would probably cost an arm and a leg worth of credits, but it would be worth having, in my opinion. Gotta have someplace to sit to enjoy the game.
