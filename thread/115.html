Funcom: possible to have the Pioneer Backpack effects active without equipping it?
I posted this in the old forum, so I'll toss it here in the new one:

I think the Pioneer Backpack is neat, and I think the stats adjustments are neat. Honestly, it was more than I was expecting.

I, however, cant wear it anywhere except socially in a town because I have tank armor that goes on my back. Im sure other players have different things they like to place on their back as well, like cloaks, trench-coats, bigger and badder backpacks that they invested serious IP to wear, etc.

Considering a majority of the bonuses on this backpack seem combat and adventuring oriented, one would obviously wish they could use it at those times. For those of us that make use of other back items in combat (and when traveling through hostile areas on our way to combat), thats simply not possible.

I could just settle and try to restrict its use to when Im in town, however I cant even fast-hotkey-swap the item because backpacks don't equip when you hotkey-press them, they open. Which would mean Id have to open my inventory, open my wear tab, find my backpack (because inventory still randomly swaps position), swap my tank armor and backpack out, all the while fighting the lag encountered while entering a social area like a city, only to repeat the process when leaving for an adventure. Needless to say, it wouldnt be long before I completely gave up on doing this.

Im not crazy about the idea of placing it in a shoulder slot (if such a change were taken under consideration) for similar reasons.. because, currently Im wearing shoulder-armor in both of my shoulder slots. I also suspect it would look quite odd wearing tank torso-armor with a backpack attempting to share the same spot graphically at the same time (which is what would probably happen if you could wear tank armor on your back and a backpack on your shoulder). In other words, the backpack geometry would be submerged within the tank armor geometry unless some specific coding and art changes were made, which we know isnt going to happen.

Would it be possible to alter the parameters of the Pioneer Backpack so that the stat adjustments affect your character whether its equipped or not? The act of just having it in your character's inventory (as opposed to your bank vault) would treat the item as equipped and active. That way, those who can wear it on their backs still can, and those who cant still wont lose out on having the effects from this item. Is this something youre willing to consider?

Thanks for the gift.
