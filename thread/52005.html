I actually asked an ARK what the sliders meant awhile back and this is what he told me (he also mentioned that the sliders do not have a guaranteed effect on things except the Easy / Hard & Money / XP sliders):

Easy / Hard - Controls the QL of the mission.
Good / Bad - Affects the objective. (find item or person vs steal item or kill person)
Order / Chaos - Controls the breed of mobs you face (human NPCs vs Monster NPCs)
Open / Hidden - Even he didn't know...thought it had something to do with the number of locked doors / chests.
Phys / Myst - Controls the type of mobs you face. (Melee mobs / Caster mobs)
Head On / Stealth - He didn't know this one either, thought that it also affects the objective in some way.
Money / XP - Affects the reward.
