In the end!
"Whats you connection to the Rome bombing" The Omni inter pol agent screamed as he slaped the opifex female in the interrogation chair hard across the face.
"Bite me bub!" she growled and spat a mouthfull of blood into his face.
The Inter-Pol agent lost control of his enormous temper and started hitting the prisoner repeatedly. Finally the female went limp and the agent eased of.
"Damm, GUARD" the agent shouted, a second later the cell door opened and a Omni Pol guard stuck his head in "Yessir".
"Take her back to the cell. She is of no use to me untill she wakes up" the agent stated while wipeing the blood of his knuccles.

The Guard entered and notioned his partner to grab the prisoners other arm.

The two Omni-Pol guards dragged the slim opifex female down the corridor, her face a pile of bruises and cuts. The non chalantly tossed her inside the holding cell and reactivated the force field. Turning around and walking away, neither of them saw the disfigured smile cracking on the prisoners lips, as she took a look at the passcard she had palmed of the bigger guard during the trip down here. She staggered to her feet, shook her head and stumbled over to the force field control pad. With a deep breath she ran the magstipped card over the sensor. With a low beep the forcefield deactivated, and the Opifex barely able to stand staggered out of the cell.

The Opi female quietly made her way down the hall way, towards the interrogation chambers, dodgeing guards and security as she went by with ease mostly assosiated with agents on missions.

She stopped at the door she was looking for, on it was a simple sign "Cheif Mitchells" she stood herself up to attention and softly knocked on the door.

"What?" Agent Mitchells literaly screamed from the inside and ripped the door open. He was met with a forehead doing 90mph. With a loud crunching sound Agent Mitchells went down for the count.
"No so tough are you, atleast not when Im not restrained anymore" the Opifex spat at the slumped form of the Agent. She kicked him once in the groin for good messure and procceded to search his pockets for the key to the Nano repressor collar they had put on her.
"Dammit" she cursed as she realised the unconsious form on the floor didnt have what she needed. She sat down next Mitchells thinking.

Her thoughts were interrupted by a sudden frantic knocking on the door. "Cheif, Sir there has been an escape" a voice on the otherside of the door said.

"Sir, are you in there?" a puzzled guard asked the closed door faceing him. "Mitchells usually answears right away, screaming at you" the guard though, as he reached out to open the door.
The moment he opened the door a slim form barreled into him knocking him down and back, with lightning speed the opifex female was on her feat and running down the corridor at a speed normally reached in a moderatly powerfull Yalmahas. The guard staggered to his feet, and pressed the alarm button on the wall next to the door to Cheif Mitchells office.

"Buggers wont quit" the running opifex muttered to herself as she ran down the corridor, doors whipping past at hyperspeed. "Funny" she thought to herself "I've done this so often I could be asleep" her trail of thought came to a sudden halt when she realized that she was running out of corridor real fast. The guards were now in hot purcuit, dropping fast, but not as fast as new ones joined the chace.

The opifex ran down the corridor and then suddenly jumped to the side and out of the window, down all four stories and smashed into the earth.

The Opifex was on the ground when Cheif Mitchells came to the location, one of the more intelligent guards had figured that despite the running speed a bullet might be faster. The opifex was on her back, slumped against the outer perimiter fence, a whole mile away from the compound.

"What you hoped to accomplice by this I dont know" Mitchells asked both the prisoner and himself. "We are in the middle of the Newland Desert there is no where to run to".

The prisoner looked up at him, trying to strip his soul of his body by the look alone "I needed some fresh air, you omni's all stink so badly" the opi spat at him.

Shakeing his head Mithcells gave only order he had left "Shoot her and dump the body in the desert"

"Yessir"
