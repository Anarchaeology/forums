Great Vikings of Rubi-Ka Press Release 020313
From the desk of Dr. Cindi Bolieu
Tir, March 13 -- 29476

Citizens of Rubi-Ka! We have suffered through the excuses and promises of both Clan and Omni-Tek bureaucrats for long enough. All over this planet, substandard housing conditions and oppressive zoning codes prohibit us from doing the most basic thing - living. Socializing. Having a place to call our own. It makes our presence on Rubi-Ka feel temporary and fleeting, as bare as the walls of our homes.

In a rare agreement, Clan, Neutral, and Omni-Tek organizations will be holding sympathetic rallies in cities across Rubi-Ka. The problems we face are of interest to solve for every person on Rubi-Ka. OT's obligation is to providing for its employees, and the Clans and Neutrals for providing better and more free ways of life. Either way you look like it, this is of interest to every person on the planet.

Rally with the Great Vikings of Rubi-Ka, Live Free or Die, and The Dark Carnival in a planet-wide protest. Rally in front of Fair Trade in Tir, in front of Neuts R Us in Newland, the center of Athen, and the sight of the tragic bombing in Omni-Ent this Saturday at 9pm GMT. Spread the word quickly, citizens!
