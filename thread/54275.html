Tradeskills - The personal touch
I'm just thinking that it would be really cool if tradeskillers could add a quick custom message to the items they create.

IE:
This armor lovingly crafted by [Insert Makers Name Here]
This armor crafted for [Buyer] by [Maker]
This weapon expertly assembled by [Tradeskiller]

It would be really cool to see where someone's armor ended up.

And if anyone actually read the description of an 3rd generation hunk of armor, then saw the maker (or orginal owner) down the road, they could say:

OMG! You made my armor!
or
OMG! I have your armor!

Might even be something of a status symbol to have Superslag's (or another maker's) name on your item.

Just the random thought as I was getting a new set of Carb armor made.

I think that's a great idea. Anything that adds the personal touch is very cool in my opinion.

This idea puts me in mind of something from way back...

When Asheron's Call was new, and I was a really, really sucky newb, a lot of higher level players helped me out. Being a newb, I had no way to return the favor, until I came up with this idea:

I'd carry around a bunch of parchments on me. Every time I found a cool view of location (pretty often, since I spend almost all my time running around the world like a mad chicken), I'd write a hiaku and take a screenshot. If I met someone who helped me out, gave me loot, or just role-played in a way I thought was cool, i'd give them an origonal haiku with a link to the screenshot on the web.

Sadly, those pages are gone now, but it was fun while it lasted.
