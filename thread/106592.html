I think this is an awesome idea and I'm TOTALLY there to see the play whenever it happens.

However, I hate to be the only one to point this out, but:

Outside locations are bad, evil, and will crash, lag, and destroy your faces to the point that the cast and audience will have approximately zero enjoyment.

PLEASE do not have this play or any other public production in an outside zone. if you have more than 30 or so people there, crowd control will kick in, and if it doesn't, the zone WILL crash. I've seen no exception to this rule, ever. (the few "unlaggy" events pointed out to me did not have that many people, and even those who were there didn't realize there were about 40 more people who couldn't get into the area without crashing).

My recommendation: Neutral indoor area. Reets is probably the most convenient to allow viewers of any level and both factions. The area below the glass floor would also serve well as a stage, offering viewers a choice of just about any seating location (even directly above!)

And warping can be done to bring more people in once the zone starts rotating (and still little to no crashes...wee!)
