Weapon building suggestion
I doubt this is original, but at least it's obvious. Actually
Tboo mentioned the concept to me yesterday, but
I've at least written it down for him:


Every player-built weapon should have slots for
1-3 add-on modules (depending on QL.)


These modules should include:

* modules to buff anything (skills, acs, etc.) for which
specific weapon variants currently exist which do the
buff.

* modules to just buff damage output.

* modules which run the occasional random
nanoprogram like those rare weapons which have
these effects.


The skill to add these modules is presumably
weaponsmithing, and it would work pretty much
the same way that nano-programming works for
implants, with these modules following the same
logic as clusters. Alternatively, each module could
also have its own secondary skill requirement,
eg a nano skill buff module might require
nanoprogramming or the specific nano skill or some
other trade skill.


The motivations for this new feature are several:

* it would be fun, and actually also be a useful in-game
thing for trade skill classes to do. Currently, weapon
building is almost useless because most of the best
weapons can just be found or bought.

* most weapons are currently useless. The weapons with
the best buffs aren't merely slightly inferior to the
weapons with the best damage, they are hugely
grossly inferior, so that no one would ever use one,
in most cases (there are a few exceptions, like Vektor
shotguns, but for example MC-buffing guns are all
awful.)

* it would serve to make individual items and possessions
more special and differentiated without having to add
lots of unique items on mobs with 24 hour spawns.

* it would be easy to balance based on QL and WS skill
requirements.


Submitted for your consideration,
