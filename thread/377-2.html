Hopefully this will cheer you up Borrace (and yes, I've actually been asking and reading about Adventurers. Not all that surprising really, but sorry for not getting these to you sooner)

Multiple ranged buff: Yes indeed, there are plans to include a nano program to buff Multiple Ranged. There's a very good chance it will be an Adventurer profession nano, although not self-only.

Team Free Movement: This will definately be fixed. No estimate at the moment, but it's on it's way.

Polymorphs: There are no current plans to change them at the moment (or to add more that I'm aware of), but if you guys and gals keep giving suggestions, I'll keep bugging the devs. Muaha.

Heals: Adventurer heals have much shorter recharge times than Martial Artist heals, and also go to higher levels. This helps balance out the large heals that Martial Artists obtain. There are no plans on changing any of the healing nanos at this time.

Damage Shields: There are no current plans to change the damage shields, but like above, I'll keep voicing your concerns along as long as you keep passing them to me.

Pistols: New pistols will probably be included later on, though like the other new items coming in patches, they will be available through missions and tradeskills. I know pistols are of a major concern so I'll be doing all I can to make sure the devs know that.

Again guys, sorry it took a little longer than I had liked to get back to you. Please do give me feedback here as I read it all. Give me a yell if you have more questions.
