JOBE COMMUNITY DENIES CONFLICT ALLEGIANCE
JOBE, RUBI-KA (Sept. 7, 29477). The JOBE Scientific community today denied all charges and accusations about choosing any allegiance to Omni-Tek or the clans. In recent days, the newly stabilized portal facility and team have been accused of causing a new rift between Omni-Tek and rival factions on Rubi-Ka.

Jobe lead scientist, Lady Giljabet Skellibjalla stated that Jobe has NOT allowed sole access to OMNI for inspection of the Jobe conduit facilities, nor has it asked clan to intervene on it's behalf against alleged OMNI aggression and pressure.

Lady Skellibjalla stated, The conduit will open according to the previously published schedule and will be open to all, although there will be a minor tax which will be announced shortly. Any attempts to take control will be countered by security installments already in place in Jobe.

The Jobe portal to the Shadowlands is scheduled to be opened on Sept. 9.

For those of you that do not remember Earth, I would like to inform you all of some interesting information about that small planet.

On earth if you do not live in the country known as the United States and you visit that country, you still have to pay sales tax.

Also if you do not live in the country known as the United States, you have no say on who will make up the ruling body of that government. This is the same government that sets the taxes.

In this situation, the US is providing a product or service to you even though you are a non-citizen. No matter who you are (with a few exceptions such as tax exempt organizations) everyone is charged that same tax, be you citizen or not.

So saying that someone cannot charge you a tax if you are not one of his or her citizens is incorrect. If you are using a product or service that a government or organization offers, then they can charge you a tax for using that product or service, even if you have no representation.

Also supporting or opposing a tax that you know nothing about can be foolish. For instance if the tax was a one time fee of one credit, I am sure that most people would not worry at all about it.

If on the other hand, it was a 50% of your current net bank account balance, for each use of the portal, I am sure that many people would oppose it.

Again, before you decide weather or not you think this is a good tax or not, I suggest that you find out more about it.

Also as a final note. If you find that you find the tax unreasonable, then all you need do is not use the portal. If enough people feel the tax is unreasonable, and stop using it, then they will do one of two things. One might be that they will raise the tax, and tax the people using the portal more, so they make up for the lost profit. The second option they would have would be to lower the tax, with the hopes that they would obtain a larger customer base. From what I have heard of Jobe, it is a group ran by a Technocracy. As such they are likely to pick the second option.
