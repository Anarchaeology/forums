There are only two types of instruction disks that are worth bothering with: Grid Armor and Nullity Sphere. Both of these nanos are only available as instruction disks.

Grid Armor is a fixer nano line, that is the only thing they have to do with those.

If you have either Grid Armor or Nullity Sphere in your pack (lowest QL is probably around 60), then it is worth a TON of money. A TON! Otherwise, they aren't worth much of anything.
