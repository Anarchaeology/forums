Why is it...
that FunCom thinks selling Battlesuits is bad? And that is exactly what this is.

They think a player shouldn't be able to get money in this fashion and that's just BS. What this amounts to is that they enforcing the player driven economy and ensuring that missions can't be truly profitable unless you screw over another player.

Everything comes does to this. The Devs are HURT everytime you devise a USEFUL and productive way of doing things that they did not envision.

They didn't foresee any engie stocking up on bot shells so they stopped that when they realized engies could.

They didn't foresee how easy it would be for an agent to sneak through 100% missions with leet form and the lot so they nerfed concealment.

They didn't foresee what kind of trouble all the +100 to various skill nanos would cause, but instead of nerfing those (becuase they LIKE those!) they make it useless to use them anyway by punishing someone who uses the player driven economy to get the buffs.

They didn't foresee that a fixer, who would have a darned hard time casting Grid Armor, would cast is as much as possible and then store it. (You see, don't buy the BS about how it was not released as intended, because it was; the devs are just incredibly short-sighted.) So, they gave GA a wear requirement, which enforces the player driven economy by making the fixer get another buff to put the extras on. Next, when that becomes a problem, they will change it so you can one copy in your back pack, just like bots. You see, they could have made it an armor buff just like Engie/soldier buffs that changed your skin look but
they didn't want to do that because they wanted it to keep you from wearing bracers and the like. (Of course, in making a solid object it also not like any of grid nano the fixer has. After all we don't get little boots of speed when we cast grid runner.)

They didn't foresee how giving the Fixer's supposedly unique grid nanos to other classes would upset the fixer customers. No wait, they DID foresee that. They just didn't care. They just felt we want these nanos in the game and the fixers can't cast them very well, so let's give them to someone who can. (Rather than making sure the fixers would have an easier time of casting them.)

They didn't foresee that they'd ever need customer service policies because, despite the fact that we're paying customers, they're under the asumption that we're happy little fans who'll love everything they make and will kiss their feet for providing yet another ill thought out, bug infested product. Thus, they stand by taking hard earned in game money from people, they stand by not giving in game refunds because for some reason they think that costs them moeny. (What???)

I am not going to cite anymore examples as you all get my point, and I probably don't know all the examples anyway.

But the point is, the developers of this entertainment oriented service business do not have the customer's best interest in mind. They are more interested in keeping their mobs alive and keeping rare things in rare quantities. They do not understand the point of creating and sellling an entertainment oriented service product is to give the paying customers entertainment.

That means that the customers should, above everything, enjoy themselves. Therefore, the developers, who supposedly want to make money off this product, should endeavor to create an atmosphere that is fun for the customer not just for the devs.
