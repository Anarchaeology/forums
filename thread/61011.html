AO's biggest problems
The biggest problem with AO and the reason behind the game not holding more people's attention is simple.

Look at a game that made it HUGE. final fantasy

Why did it make it so huge?

1) Major storyline that had a point.
2) You had to solve puzzles
3) Fight bosses to progress
4) Incredible music and lots of it
5) You always had things to do that went hand in hand with the story.. decisions to be made

Anarchy Online lacks any and all of these elements.

AO has some good music and some good monsters but not enough music and not enough incentive.

The game doesnt grab you emotionally into the story..

and that will be the death of AO.. and i hate to say it, but is true..

Okidokey, I basically agree with your underlining argument, but not necessarily the examples you give. Therefore, I'll offer my analysis of AO and the inherent problems with it.

AO's world is neither interactive nor dynamic, simple as that.

Ask yourself this question - what was the main thing you were doing at level 5, level 50 or level 100 and beyond? Lemme guess.... missions or outdoor hunting.

So, besides getting levels, what else did this accomplish in the world of AO? Answer... Sod all. It's as simple as that.

I've seen the arguments from the RP camp, as well as those who class themselves as explorers of AO, who state that there is more to the game that levelling. Well guys, I'm sorry to say that acting the tourist or agreeing on a personal way of interpreting AO doesn't add to the lack of interactivity nor a dynamic world in which to play. Tough call, but that's the reality of the situation, sorry guys.

At a stretch of the imagination, you could argue that GM events add depth to the game - you only have to see the crowds drawn to an event to understand the peripheral desperation of players who want to do something other than keep on the level treadmill to see the problems inherent in AO.

So face it, it makes not one iota of difference if you're a RP'er, interpreting Rubi Ka in your own personal way to make it seem more interesting, or if you're intent on exploring what sights Rubi Ka has to offer - these are personal choices and do not make the game more or less appealing to the average gamer, or to the world itself. The game inherently lacks any vestiges of a dynamic world which the player can affect.

PVP, such as it is, is pitiful. Static zones, where gankers lurk for players on missions hardly equates to an interesting aside from
the regular treadmill.... if this is the best FC can offer, at least make it equal to something akin to TFC, where teamwork and a variation of classes makes the end result worthwhile!


No number of 'booster' or 'expansion packs' diverts from the basic issue of a lack of customer ('player' is such a derogative term, so I'll omit it's use here) interaction.

So there you have it, bugger all to do except the level treadmill. Simple really, when you think about it. And the irony is, after a year (happy birthday FC/AO, for what it's worth) we still have bugger all to do besides get on the level treadmill or make any bleedin' difference to Rubi Ka or the whole AO world.
