Idea: War and Supression Gas
Here's one of those ideas that is likely to spark a LOT of debate, but lets try it on for size anyway:

Is there any way of the game engine being able to identify with any given MOb what its uptime has been over the last (x) hours?

What I'm thinking is this:

If *all* the guards in a city/populated area are 'owned' for a certain duration (I'm thinking of at least 3-4 hours), would it be possible to have a temporary supression gas failure and drop the gas automatically to 25% so the player citizens can respond to the invasion of their territory?

This would have to be accompanied by LOTS of warning that the gas was going to fail, a clear indication of when it was going to fail and suchlike.

The idea behind it is it would take a concerted effort from a large group in order to meet the requirements to drop the gas - not just a bit of random guard killing. Basically it's to break a 'stalemate' when one faction decides to own a facility belonging to another (Clon**** mass drivers, for example) - allowing groups from the other side to rally and defend their property.

Yes?
No?

Flame? (I'm expecting lots of that)

Since you mentioned that you wanted a debate, I'll go ahead and start off by saying that I like the fundamental idea that you have there, but you can't ignore the neutral issues. For that reason, I'd suggest something slightly different, which is similar to what people have suggested before.

Make all cities attack and defend capable. All citizens should have the potential to defend against enemy/invaders or stage attacks. An opt in option should be put in place for people who wish to participate and an opt out option for those who can't or do not wish to participate. Pre 75 folks should still be subjected to level restriction for attacking and defending.

I'm thinking that the opt in/out system might work by allowing people to choose at logon to opt in or out. A time limit should be put into place that restricts people from changing status as an enlisted guard/soldier, whatever. Perhaps 24 realtime hours is a reasonable amount of time before a change is allowed. That way you don't get people on the front line who are invulnerable who are just waiting for the right time to click their attack enable button or people forming invading parties and all enabling their PVP options at the same time.

Interesting idea but it's both impractical and massively unwanted.

Impractical because people can't help their own guards, partly, and it's pretty obvious that the smartest way to defend the city would be to defend the guards. But mostly this isn't workable because of lag. Tir is laggy enough without anyone attacking the guards, and it goes down on a pretty regular basis without our help. If the gas dropped and people flooded into the area to attack or defend the zone, it would promptly crash.

The reasons it would be unwanted are legion. One is because it basically allows such guilds as are able to mount this sort of effort the ability to force other players to either participate in large-scale PvP or else vacate a major city. Two is that all the major cities are grid and/or whompa nodes, and probably the first thing an attacking force would do would be to start ganking anyone who zoned in. Three is that it really doesn't take a whole lot to kill guards - most people over level 120 can solo one without trouble - so it wouldn't be much of a problem to kick off the gas malfunction unless guards were made stronger. Four, and the kicker, fully 2/3 of the playerbase wants little or nothing to do with PvP, and most of the rest only want to PvP after they're done getting "uber" in PvM. Nobody's going to take kindly to coerced PvP in any way.

Sorry... I am against this because I don't see how it could ever be fun.
