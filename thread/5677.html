Any chance in an AC type 3rd person view?
In AC the 3rd person view is basically behind the back looking forward/slightly down from 1-2 feet higher than your character that was fixed from swaying left/right (Z axis?) but the up/down (X axis?) would follow the terrain so you didnt have to constantly readjust the camera view angle because of things like hills and such. 3rd person view where it follows terrain features in AO is mostly useless aside from pretty cinematic type things since it sways around and doesnt turn to follow your character unless you move forward and the fixed one has the serious annoyance of having to constantly adjust the up/down angle because your now looking at a 6 foot section of a hill. Also one other "nice" thing about it was the first tap of the numpad 4/6 would offset the camera one step to the left/right then if you hit it again or kept holding it the camera would circle normally with the character as the centerpoint (i.e. not offset.)

3rd person fixed is *not* the same thing. 3rd person fixed locks the X (up/down), and Z (spin around) axis so you need to manually adjust the X for when you do things like travel on land that is not flat so you dont find yourself looking at 5 square feet of a hill. The AC 3rd person view locks the Z axis to your character but X is locked to the terrain just behind it so if your on a mountainside and facing the peak you can see up the slope, if you turn around your view adjusts so you are now looking down the slope rather than at the sky.
