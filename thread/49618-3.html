As much as I'd rather see the NT patch come before the engineer patch. I will concede that the engineers need it more. My NT is my primary but I played an Engineer to level 45 (about all I could stomach).

The real problem with the engineer is that they are near useless in PvM at least the higher they go in level the more useless they become. Their entire reason for existence (outside of tradeskills) is their robot. They don't have much of any real potential for dealing damage themselves. But the really sad fact is that their robot is so pathetic compared to the other pet classes and even other players in general in it's effectiveness. All it does is tank and deal damage, neither of them well. It doesn't heal, nor does the engineer have any combat nanos, no nukes, DoTs, debuffs, roots, calms, charms, or heals (pet heals don't really count). All they have is a so-so set of defensive buffs that other team members typically won't want to waste NCU over. These are designed to complement the pet, but the pet doesn't really do it's job. Trimmers have a minor effect. The robot doesn't typically deal very good damage at the engineer's unless it's MASSIVELY overequipped (even then once they hit the max slayerdroid, that's it, they never improve again). Consequently it doesn't tank very well either as it's damage isn't enough to draw aggro, and the trimmers don't work well enough to handle that task. Indoor pet pathing is completely fubar, and it's worse with engineer pets since they don't fly thus getting stuck on tables and chairs among other things. So essentially an engineer has nothing to offer a team on a team mission and isn't of much value either outdoors. If you can't deal damage, tank, heal, buff, debuff, calm, root, or charm, why would anyone want to bring you along on a mission?

At least nano techs are damn good at crowd control with roots and calms. That at least gives us something to fall back on when the other aspects of our profession aren't working properly. That and we don't have to wait around several minutes waiting for a pet to stop humping a table or several hours BEGGING for buffs to be able to summon our pet.

Engineers definitely need help more than Nano-techs. But they can wait? Why? Because, quite simply, the core concept of the engineer is sort of met through their tradeskills. Engineers are very much the masters of trade skills as they, unlike traders can afford to blow IP on trade skills since the other aspects of their profession are so broken that they don't merit the IP spent on them. Nano-techs by comparison do not live up to their defined role as as such need to have their patch first. We are supposed to be ultimate damage dealers and the masters of combat at the cost of everything else. All the other costs are there but we just don't do the damage or have the combat skills to make up for it yet. We've even been promised to have our fix first and have come to expect it. Anything else would be a further injustice to the Nano-tech profession.
