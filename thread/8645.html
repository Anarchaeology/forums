The people who 'do' maps checked into AO at the beginning, started work, then threw up their hands at the buggy game, and quit... so most of the map work never got done.

The maps don't exist in the resource database in 'complete' form, because they have several layers to them.

You can find all the 'satellite maps' at Basher, which are the 'base' maps of each playfield, but to create a complete 'like the radar' map, you have to add layers of data for buildings, roads, etc, and lighting. (notice your radar map changes with the time, shadows move, etc.) No one seems to have worked out a way to do that directly, with a 3rd party program, yet.
