A way to transfer items between your chars
This simply MUST have come up before, but I'm pretty new to this game, so I'm not sure

What i *really* wish is an easy way to transfer stuff from one char to the other. If my enforcer finds a nifty doc nano in a mission or some monster parts he can't convert, I'll have to find someone to trust (not easy in the beginning), ask him to wait while i log out and log in again. Not to mention weapon parts, nanoclusters/implants, armor, clothes and so on...
Why?

Why not just let a part of the bank box (for instance the last 6 slots) be open to all chars on the same account? I wouldn't be that hard to make, and since you can't transfer money that way and you can easily make it no-nodrop-zone, it will make it hard just to have a mule char generating money/tokens, at the same time as it will allow people to actualy use all the weird stuff the missions generate.

Easy. Cheap. No extra support needed. Cannot be exploited. Noone will whine "nerf". Fewer people get scammed. More fun for all.
What are you waiting for? Or am I missing something?

It all boils down to old MUDing tradition.

One of the major rules is that using other chars to advance your 'main' (or alts too) is not allowed. Makes sense RPwise too, since your characters never meet eachother, and aren't friends.



Now, not all people agree with that, and it was mainly to stop people who played a lot of chars at the same time. This isn't really possible in AO, but the mentality is still there: Some people think it's a bad thing, and some thinks it's a good thing.



So unless something radically changes, then you have to find someone you can trust.
