No.

You only need the Notum Wars booster if:
  • You want to be able to Place controllers, turrets, and guardian towers.
  • You want to use the new vehicles and accessories
  • You want the voices
  • You want to use service towers
  • You want to use tower accessories


You can be in an org and receive the org benefits from the towers without buying NW. You can attack any tower within the game mechanics without buying NW. you can participate in the defense of a base without buy NW.
