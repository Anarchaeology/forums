How about a [U]description[/U] of what you're planning to make?
In the tradeskill library shops, there are plenty of books that describe the manufacture of something, but you don't necessarily know what you're building until it's done.

Might I make a suggestion? Add two links to the outer two QL items the instructions describe so a prospective buyer can have a look at what the end result looks like before committing to the manufacture to what, for that individual, is a piece of junk.

I have recently started a tradeskill character, that I'm keeping focused on weaponsmithing. So far, I've made two weapons that make what I already had look wonderful by comparison. This isn't to say these weapons are bad. One merely had a two ammo limit, (but ignored armor), the other was significantly weaker than the 4QL lower one I'm using, AND only holds 5 rounds, (but can be dual-wielded.)

Had I had an easy reference to these two items, I would have passed them by and built something I might actually use.
