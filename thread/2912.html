Re: Really?
quote:
Originally posted by Darkblood

The only 17" ones I have seen that don't are flat panels. But then again, I know I'm a gamer and expressedly look for the resolution capabilities.
The NEC MultiSync 75 max resolution is listed as 1280x1024 @ 66Hz. Similarly for the NEC MultiSync FE700+

As well, the Mitsubishi Diamond Scan 70 does 1280x1024 @ 65Hz and the Diamond Plus 73 1280x1024 @ 60Hz.

Ideally you want to use a refresh of at least 85Hz.

While some 17" monitors are listed as being able to do 1600x1200, for example the Sony CPD-G220S, it is most likely at an unacceptable refresh rate (its not listed). For this reason, the recommened resolution for the CPD-G220S is listed as 1024x768 @ 85Hz by Sony.
