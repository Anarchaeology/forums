Me, Myself & EoT
One of the funniest things I have seen to date happened today at Tir Arena.

It was the worst case of schizophrenia I have ever seen... So bad, even the crowd saw multiples.

Kinkstaah2 & Kinkstaah3 came into the arena wearing trenchcoats & sunglasses. They were also both brandishing Galahad swords.

They immediately began to duel. While dueling, they started yelling things to each other.

2.. I am Kinkstaah
3.. No! Imposter! I am Kinkstaah!
2.. Liar! I am the true Kinkstaah!
3.. Yes, this is the EoT.
2.. I have one too.
3.. I'm sexy
2.. Phear me and my EoT!
3.. All shall fall to my EoT!
2.. See how uber I am with my EoT.

you get the idea..

I, of course, started healing them both to keep the fight going. We walked over to the grid and somehow all survived the terrible lag and the duel continued. Kinkstaah2 was determined to kill Kinkstaah3, but Kinkstaah3 was not going without a fight. They continued their verbal and physical assult on each other for a while and then we moved to the NW terms. They were a big hit at the NW terms and it was great to see a dozen +150s gathered around 2 lvl 3 Kinkstaahs. By this time Kinkstaah2 had an MP heal pet on him so I only had to worry about Kinkstaah3. The verbal assaults started anew and continued for a few minutes. Finally, the Kinkstaahs decided that niether was more uber than the other and began to dance. They put on a fine show and were the highlight of the day for many.

GOOD JOB! WHOEVER YOU ARE!
