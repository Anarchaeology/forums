It is clear that the original vison of AO was one something of a Star Wars knock off with Clan cast as the Good Rebels and Omni as the Evil Empire

However if you look at the story material FC has developed since launch you can see how the emphasis of the story has changed. Lets just list the key events in Rubi-Ka history since Ao was launched and SL.

* Ross offers the amnesty. Clan follow suit. Was it a ruse? Who can say. What remains is FC chose to allow OT to make the first step towards peace and not vice versa.

* Peace talks end after terrosit attack in Omni Ent residential block. Its never been established if the Dust Brigade are clan or not. The point, is FC chose to show OT as the victims here, not the aggressors.

* The CoT falls. Radiman, the "Nelson Mandela" of the Clans is effectively removed from power by his own people. The Sentinels, known to be hardliners and fanatical, take charge of Tir and start killing neutrals. Again, it was FC's choice to portray the Clans in this light. If all they wanted was to get rid of Radiman as a character, they could have arranged for Omni-Pol to murder cold blood and thus create a genunie Clan martyr. Instead they chose to highlight clan disunity and militancy.

The point I am trying to make is the FC had a choice about how it wanted to play out the story. on all these occasions If they were trying to drive the point home that Clan are good and Omni is evil with these events, they messed up big time.

Also, take a look at the full timeline, which was also published by FC some time launch. It's not, as one might have expected, a catalog of OT attrocities. Rather, the timeline presents a complex, muti-faceted situation with no clear cut right or wrong and in which the hostilities are due more to centuries of mistrust than economic or political necessities.

Now out comes SL, and once again we are presented with a simplistic, clear-cut Good vs Evil story. Is it any wonder the players are confused?
