Beggars
There was a very interesting discussion on this exact topic a few nights ago on RK2's OT leader channel. A lot of good points were made, but the general consensus seemed to be:

Higher level players, in general, aren't greedy, but they do dislike beggars..

For instance - if someone walks up to me and says "can i have sum credz plz" I'm probably going to come back with a sarcastic, and likely insulting remark. Too bad.

On the other hand, as any of my guild members or the dozens of newbies I have helped out in the past will tell you, I'm always willing to help a polite new player who is having trouble. I've given away MILLIONS of credits and dozens of items to help people out.. but not even a single credit to someone I did not know who asked me for money. I don't even like it when a friend asks for money unless it's a loan.

Just the same, I've passed out credits to dozens of new players in the Omni Backyards.. anywhere from 10k to 1 million each, depending on how generous I felt and how rich I was at the time.
Last night I met a young agent in the Rompa bar who was dual wielding a Springfield Arms Home Defender and a Light Supressor. He was dressed in mismatched armor, and obviously new to the game. While we stood there talking, I asked my organization to see if they could find an appropriate rifle for the young man. Why did I take the time to help this new player? Because it was obvious that he was trying to make do with what he had found, rather than sitting around in town begging for credits so he could twink himself out.

Lots of people who have come to me for advice on how to play their character have walked away with a new gun, armor, credits, as well as the advice they originally asked for. I also freely dispense information to new players on how to make money on their own - what items sell well to other players, how to sneak a mission (for the agents out there), etc.

Just the same, anyone who asks for credits, in return for nothing, is going to get ignored or insulted.

/shrug
