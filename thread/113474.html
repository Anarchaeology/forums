When Borgs Attack
Recently I have born witness to a few events pertaining to attacks by Borg forces.

My first encounted was with a Sentinel in Tir. Sentinel Abrigan had announced that there was a meeting between Omni-Pol and some Clan Mercenaries in Tir. Omni-Pol doesnt belon in Tir no matter who is controlling it, so I decided to give the thugs a hand.

I located the meeting, and informed the Sentinel of its location. Having done so, we killed the Clan Mercenaries, and captured the Omni-Pol member. Her name was 2nd Leiutenant Jacia. She told us that she had been meeting with the Clan Mercs to talk about Borg attacks on Athen. I had not heard of such attacks, so I thought it to be OP propaganda. A couple weeks went by, and I got an announcement from the Knights of Camelot.

The Knights had lost a patrol out in Avalon. And needed help from Clanners to investigate. A large group of Clanners headed out with the Knights and we found their comrades dead, killed by Borgs.

Only a few days later the Knights summoned the aid of the Clans again. This time to strike at the heart of the Borgs in Avalon. We went to their base. Surrounded and outgunned we retreated back to Avalon a few valiant fighters from Redemption and Arion (me) stayed to hold off the Borgs so others could escape.

My question is, could this be indicative of a threat from the Borgs? Is this something that we as Clanners should worry about?

Thus far I've seen the cyborg base in Tir County where there is evidence of scientist being held against their will (my team was unable to rescue them). I've heard reports of people disappearing in Perpetual Wastes, where there is a significant cyborg presence.
I have seen the ruined facility on the rim of Mort Crater, which bears evidence of having been an Omni-Tek facility. It seems the cyborgs now claim ownership. Under this facility are 2 complexes that are crawling with unaligned personnel who are hostile to any interlopers entering. I've speculated on their presence (as I was unable to question any, they simply opened fire on my team) and must wonder, are they volunteers for cyborg conversion, or supporting the cyborgs in some way? There is also what appears to have been at one time an outpost to the north-northwest of Mort Crater that the cyborgs have seized control of. Yesterday I was accompanying a contingent of Omni-Tek employees into the Perpetual Wastes to investigate, what we thought would be a small enclave of commonplace cyborgs. Upon arrival we set up camp outside of what seemed to be a military installation in surprisingly good repair relative to the rest of the wastes, as this was largly unfamilar territory we brought along ample support in the form of a Scourge-class Slayerdroid as well as a full combat contingent of Omni-Tek personel. Our presence was not welcome, yet all went well as we were able to draw the cyborgs out in small groups. We noted, however, that these particular cyborgs were more resilient in combat as well as more resistant to psychological manipulation than the ones we had previously combated in Mort. After the first waves had subsided the cyborgs seemed to enter a less agressive state in which they appeared to retreat further into the facility. Seeing this development as a prime opportunity to conduct a close examination of the instalation we entered and encountered token resistance. As we skirmished with one particular contingent of cyborgs we noticed that their battle tactics had undergone a major shift, instead of attacking separate targets they focused and attacked cohesively, focusing primarily on our healers and "crowd control" personel despite the fact that the Slayerdroid was the one doing the majority of the damage. It was only though the prudent actions of a fixer that accompanied us that we managed to evacuate without significant loss of life. Upon returning to the installation we once again found that the exterior of the base was eerily quiet while observations from the air revealed little as the cyborgs reacted quite violently to the presence of aircraft and attempted to shoot down any interlopers.

I advise all Omni-Tek personel to avoid that particular region of the Wastes unless accompanied by adequate support as the cyborgs are apparently, and inexplicably, much more resilient and coordinated than those commonly found in Mort. While their ferocity in combat is terrible enough it is pales in comparison to their uncanny organization and ability to attack cohesively.
