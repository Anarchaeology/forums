With the Dev reply there I guess this post doesn't matter anymore, but this is my idea. I think its kinda neat, but then again, I would, I made it up.

Okay, theres Clan/Omni tension on the planet, everyone going at each others throat, towers exploding, loot being lost.. sometimes, you just want a good fight without being wiped out, losing loot and watching towers fall. Sometimes you need to relax. And so I introduce 'Space Wars', the new bloodsport created by ICC. Get your inter-racial tensions out harmlessly.

Theres the lame introduction over. Okay, so this takes place in a specially designed Space Station hovering over Rubi-Ka. You can 'beam up' from a spot in the ICC area, near the whompas. Clan arrives on one side of the station, Omni on the other, both sides have insurance terminals right there. When there are (a set number, you choose) say 20 players up there, which have to be balanced, the two teams are created and the doors open into the main space station. Bulletproof walls, nano resistant windows, this place has it all.

It would be circular in design, flickering lights and dark corridors, imagine Aliens, with the outermost sides having a view to space and Rubi-Ka below, just for a nice touch. In the center of the station would be a table with a single item on it.. could be a Gaily Painted Hood, might be a QL200 X-3, could be anything really. The trick is, these are nano created replicas. They do the same thing, but once you leave the space station they disappear, as the technology in the station is no longer present to keep them together.

The trick is to rush in and try to capture the item on offer, the holder of the item would glow brightly (the nano created replicas give off a lot of light) so you would know who has it. The idea then is to run through to the enemies start point (having a big fight along the way, no doubt) with it where you can distribute it out to whoever needs it most. Of course, all 'loot' is also tradeable between characters, no No Drop items here. The opposing team has to grab it for themselves, probably off the corpse of who had it, and do the same thing. Get it to the enemy base.

The winning side now has the advantage of the new item, everyone is sent back to the starting areas, and a new item is placed down ready to be captured.

I think this would be kinda neat. It would give something to fight for - the nice (if illusionary) item - would be a nice arena to fight in, and keeps everyone close together with no guards or anything of the sort to interfere.
