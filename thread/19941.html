Attacked by Omni Pol
Today on RK1 I fiannly experienced something I´ve waited for forever. An event! When I logged into AO I woke up att BB in Omni-Ent.
To my spurprise there was a lot of people listening to a green named officer, Commander Hmhmhmh Maxias, talking to the crowd about loyalty to Omni-Tek, Clans and all the usual stuff.
So I sat down and listened to all this wich was very interesting indeed. And I even tried to RP a bit.
Anyway, some person named Wulff started talking about deflection from Omni-Tek. All this, of course, ended with a fight.
As a loyal Omni citizen I healed Maxim as much as possible, not that he needed it but he asked for backup so I did my very best to help him.
Now, a few people started taking sides in the fight. This was of course only verbally as none of us was able to PvP.
I didn´t give that much attention as I kept my eye on Maxims health.
All of a sudden he walks back to Omni-HQ ( I think it was), followed by a crowd of people, and walks inside. Leaving the playground, not to be followed as there was no "official door".
Then.....WHAM! I get attacked by....an "male captain" from OMNI who apparently was in the area!!!
So my questions are:
1. Why the ¤##%# do I get attacked by those whom I am loyal to?
2. How come I get attacked when all I ever did was healing their commander?

Is that a way to treat a loyal Omni-Tek member?

And in the language of RL: How incredibly stupid isn´t it that guards aggro a doc who helps their commander. By having normal aggro-rules in an event like this sort of spoils it all for me.
What I should´ve done was to just sit back and let an commander take the damage by himself. He probably would´ve made it alone, but as he asked for backup it was a natural thing to do for me.

3. Did anyone understand who was there understood what it was all about? I sort of missed the beginning.

4. More events, even thoug this one sort of was a huge letdown I still liked it. That´s was not a question but still....
