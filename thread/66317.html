Something to think about....
Yes, I know that as this game progresses more and more, there will be more and more items in the game with a level requirement attached in addition to the skill requirements....some might even have only a level requirement.....anyway...

The way things are now....you kill something green or above you get XP. Muster enough XP to get to the next level and you get some IP.....

Being that the core of this game is skill-based and the addition of level requirements are being put in place to compensate where the current skill budget fails to accomplish whatever it was put there for, ponder this for a second:

What if AO was such that there was no such thing as XP or levels? What if by killing something, picking a lock, or doing something that previously gave you XP gave you IP instead...?

no
it is not completely like ac works because it still has levels and xp you gain when you kill things.

it allowed you to directly spend the xp into your skills however.


basically i think it is safe to assume that with the current tech, there are only 2 options for character advancement.

one is level based and thus focused on killing stuff (pretty much everything on the mmorpg market except uo).

two is to get completely rid of levels and raising skills due to usage either with levels for derived attributes or without (wasteland, uo, daggerfall, jagged alliance).

the problem i see here is that raising skills through use opens the door wide for macroers.

i prefer games with a level added in since it is the easiest and quickest (albeit admitably dirtiest) way to gauge a characters prowess. it is easier to get a handle on the character for designers and fellow gamers alike when using a number to describe your abilities.
