Actually, in your case, I think that you are sort of the victim of poor timing (which you couldn't really know, in advance). The booster has been scheduled for release right around now, and the number of patches we are seeing (and patches to the patches) is higher than is normal.

Usually, the servers are down every Wednesday for some hours for maintenance. Often, patches are scheduled for that same time, so that both maintenance and patches are bundled together.

But, because there is much that has needed to be added into the game to accommodate the booster, we've had more activity than normal in the downtime area.

I suspect that we can look forward to this same pace of activity for a bit after the booster arrives, then, hopefully things will return to their previous schedule.
