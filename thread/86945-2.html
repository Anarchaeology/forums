Sexual harrasment, or harrasment of any kind is just plain unacceptable. Since my 1st char was a guy, i tended to miss out on a lot of the random harrasment that female players get when i started my female MA.

I have run into it a few times though. Sometimes it was blatant and rude from someone I had never met before. In this case, I screenshotted everything, petitioned with "disruption" or "harrrasment" immediately (they can check the logs to verify a screenie) and the arks were very prompt and friendly.

In cases where I knew the person and it went beyond a level of flirting that I was comfortable with, I usually send a tell with something stern stating that I won't accept that from them. (your flirting is going too far, please stop)

If by chance, they are in a guild, if they do not stop after you ask them to, contact their guild leader. If you do not get a reply that you are comfortable with, petition and put them on ignore. If they continue to harrass you with emotes etc. keep petitioning....they will get banned.

In the end though, if you make it clear that you do not accept harrasment in any way, it will stop. There is no need to run or hide when this happens. You have tools to fight back -- use them and people will learn that you are not one to take that kind of crap.
