Wanted my own thread on this one.
I recently posted this simply as a reply, but the more I read the more I wanted to post this as its own thread to express my opinion in a more general location were my fellow players can read, hopefully get a little inspired and respond if they feel so inclined. It is a bit long, I didn't really know I had so much to say until I started saying it but if it just helps one person then it was worth the effort a hundred times over for me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


I'm surely not as high a level as some of you folks, but I don't see the reasoning a good group would not want a Bureaucrat along. The xp buff of course, but aside from that I alone am able to defeat light red mobs with the pet. I can buff a bit of damage, throw gunslinger out as needed, daze mobs when they gang up on us, and even charm one for a bit (easily long enough to make a huge difference in a fight).

I'm doing quality level missions usually about 8 - 10 levels above my level, so when I group to do missions I can easily do missions on max red. Some say our pet isn't the best out here, but I bet you'd play hell trying to convince MY pets of that. I think those darn guys are stud! Using trimmers (QL 25) I can have the pet (which is about 7 levels above me usually) fight two light red mobs with little to no difficulty just by alternating between targets while clicking the 'attack' macro to keep aggro at first, or simply keeping one of them dazed.

Now as great as I think our profession is, I haven't even gotten to level 40 yet!! I can't wait until then, I'll be getting new nanos, a little better equipment a CAR, and exploring further from the city.

I'm assuming that you current Bureaucrats have SOMEthing you like about the class. I mean c'mon gang, you've taken the time to explore what you think is wrong with them, you've used your time and energy to come here and speak your mind, and some of you are even STILL playing a Bureaucrat. I know that in this world it is usually a code of "the squeaky wheel gets the grease" but after reading through ALL of the profession sections of this board and seeing the dozens upon dozens of disgruntled posts about how ALL the classes are broken, nerfed, unbalanced, and generally not worth 2 seconds of time to create I can only come up with one word... depressing.

The bright side is that occassionally an actual gameplay bug, or really bright and uplifting post filters into these boards, however I'd like any of you upset or disgruntled folks to do me ONE huge favor that'd take about 5 minutes or so. Set aside the gripes and complaints with your current profession for just a second, think to yourself what the best profession in the game must be (do not flip through the professions forums yet), when you are absolutely certain that you've figured the best, most un-nerfed, most powerful, most groupable, most damaging, best pvp'er profession in the game I want you to click on over to that profession's forum and read the first page of threads...

When you get there you'll be greeted with complaints of being 'powerless', nerfed, non group oriented, non soloable, and generally disliked. After you've done this I want you to think about a single question... If all of these people on all of these forums and threads feel this way, why aren't they whiling away the hours on a good game of chess, or watching television instead of playing Anarchy online? (Let alone still playing the very class they hate so much)

As a last note, at level 20 I had a bit over 93,000 credits, very nice armor (generally my QL or one level higher), all the nanos I could use at that level except for two pet ones (I chose to skip those even), a very nice belt platform with a few upgrades, and a MID car I found in a mission chest (Not that I use it of course). I can only feel that as I continue to grow and advance, that if this lifestyle should suddenly cease to be so flamboyant that there must be some mistake that I am making, not the class, and not something Funcom or the GMs have done TO me.

You fellow Bureaucrats out there keep your heads up, and always watch the back of your fellow man. And you aspiring Bureaucrats take heed and note that we ARE a force to be reckoned with, and should you choose this profession it is all about YOU, not the profession that will make the difference in your career.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I think I'm kinda getting the idea.
I think I see where you guys are going with the whole 'high level' game. I've played EQ as a Cleric up to level 53, I've also played DAOC as a Cleric (go figger ) up to level 37 and those type of games seemed to allow us as players to get immenently more powerful as we levelled. Although soloing got tougher in each game, the 'absolute' power inherent in the class was always greatly increased by level. By the same token however, there was generally a very gradual and clear curve of mob power in those games as well. If I understand correctly you guys are explaining that this isn't so in AO, that mobs can and will go from managable to outright nightmares in the span of a dozen or so levels as you get higher.

Those are primarily the two games that I've played Tomasin, the high level balance issue is correct. But it isn't the same type of balance issue that I think I'm hearing here. In those games the prime balance issue was "Class X can kite and solo a dozen mobs at the same time, while Class Z can't even solo green mobs."
One of the problematic solutions that Verant and Mythic did that ruined their system for me was nerfing, adding, and boiling down all the classes until everyone was basically e x a c t l y equal, everyone seemed to have the same ability and nuance with only the names of the equipment and spells being changed. I'm hoping that Funcom avoids this with us, I don't mind at all that a Martial Artist can kick my teeth halfway across the zone if I ball my fist up at him. I don't regret the fact that a Doctor can heal others well enough to make me look like I'm sticking bandaids on buzzsaw victims. I personally think the individuality of all of you players is what makes this a fun game for me still, and I wouldn't take a B'zillion point nuke if it meant changing that.
I do also agree that due to the low player base for Bureaucrats there are a lot of folks out there that just don't know what they're missing by having them along. For those of you that were there, it is sort of like Mages in EQ... As players started getting more experienced and familiar with the game they started realizing there was this class out there that could summon party members back to the group if they got lost in dungeons or zones, they could summon up nice utility items if you were far from a city, they could fight and nuke things all by themselves that you sometimes have trouble with in a full group, and dozens of other nifty abilities that suddenly made Mages sought after in a group. Perhaps this is the way of the Bureaucrat, since I'm still pretty low level i cannot really stake that claim though.

All in all I appreciate you guys taking the time to explain how things start to work in the higher level aspect of things. And Jujutsu, no worries about the sarcasm One thing I've learned about these forums is, a little sarcasm and a few anecdotal jabs may come your way from time to time but most of you guys and gals on here are a L O N G way from being outright rude or insulting.
