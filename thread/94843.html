Children of Rubi-Ka
I've been wanting to do a story based around the world and events that happen in Anarchy Online. Here is the start, a letter from the main character, Johan, to his wife. Hope this sparks some interest.



My dearest Sarah,

I would like only to look upon your face and hear that soft voice of yours fall from your lips and sooth my soul. It seems they wish to send me off on one final mission before my retirement. Im afraid Sarah, Im afraid I might not return from this one.
I have been given the task of leading a small group into the outlands to survey an area that seems to have caught the eye of the Omni officials. You would think that a company of their size with all there high-tech scanners, satellites, and probes they wouldnt need to send out human forces. But from what the reports tell me every probe they have sent out to this area has lost contact and the need for human eyes seems to be the only way.
I was given the choice of not taking up this mission but the modest sum of fifty million credits was too much for me to pass up. At sixty five years old I shouldnt be out here running around with the younger explorers risking my life; but Ive been on this world for too long and Ill be damned if I let anyone see something before I get a chance too. I need this one last mission before I retire, my love, i need to get this life out of my blood before I settle down into my chair and watch the sun rise and fall over Rome.
Tomorrow my crew of five and I head out to the Longest Road. From there we head west until we come to the coordinates given to us by Omni and see what there probes cannot. I hope all goes well with this trip, love; but my stomach churns with dire stress. Is this nothing more then a simple surveying job?
Should I not return for any reason know that I have loved you with all my heart and soul. Without you I am cold inside. I will do everything I can to return to you and spend the rest of our lives in comfortable bliss.


Until I see you again,
Johan Washington.
