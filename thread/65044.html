More 0% and 25% zones
The change to decrease the number of PvP areas on RK really disappointed me. I understand that these areas were made 75% so there wold be no ganking of people hunting the new boss mobs. That in itself is a great idea.

However, many zones have multiple Suppression levels in them. Why not simply add in some PvP areas back into zones such as SAV, CAV etc. This would not harm the people hunting boss mobs in any way, but allow people who want more PvP (outside of the arena, and who are sick of fighting in 2h0, MMD etc) to fight in some really cool places.

My org used to hold PvP competitions in areas like the Lava Pits in CAV, or up in the trees of the enigma Village. These areas made a great backdrop for PvP combat with obstacles and danger other than the other players. In addition, fear of ganking was very low because no one ever went to these locations.

In addition to this, my org is part of a conglomerate of orgs (clan, neutral and omni) that organize fair pvp events between the other group members. Now, with the limited PvP areas, we are restricted to fighting in the same places every single time.

I believe that people not wishing to get ganked should be free to explore the world and hunt for boss mobs, but why not add back in pockets of PvP in some interesting areas in which no one hunts to allow those of us that do want more non-gank-arena PvP to be able to have some fun?
