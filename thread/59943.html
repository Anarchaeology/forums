I always thought it was dumb that atrox's could never self-cast offensive steamroller at level 200 and solitus could at level 180. Now i see these soldier nanos and i can't help but laugh. One of them costs around 210 more skill than OS does....lol...And at level 25 for those weapon buffs you can't even get above higher than 170 SI/PM through implants and expertises, even if you are a nanomage soldier (lol again). So atroxes will be getting 1 more point of health per body development but they will not be able to cast their new nanos except for the ones with a level requirement under 155. Not only that, atroxes have to implant their nanoskills to the bare bone already, they will lose the attack rating they need from implants by implanting nanoskills, AND they will not ever be able to use the new buffs...So in effect a level 200 solitus soldier is losing 700-900 hp for the ability to have over 200 more attack rating through buffs and implants.
