As Suricia mentioned, this really has to do with your play style. It also has to do with how high of a level you want to achieve and still be a solo class.

There are several professions that are quite easy to solo to lvl 100.

Pet classes are very good at solo, since they generate their own teams. As long as you take the time to keep your skills up so you can get the best pets possible, there is very little to worry about soloing as a puppet master. All 3 pet classes have their own advantages and disadvantages. The engi pets never time out, so you can get a huge one with the help of an MP and/or trader. As long as it is not over-equipped it will do your bidding. MPs have the benefit of an attack pet, a heal pet, and a mezz pet. Crats can charm other mobs, and have some nice crowd control nanos.

If you are just interested in kickin butt yourself, Enforcers and the new Keepers are very good. They do massive damage and each have their own limited ability to heal and protect themselves.

But in my opinion, the best solo class of all is the MA. They do very comparable damage to the enf/keepers, and come with even better healing abilities. They dont need any weapons, although some MA weapons are very effective. They get huge crit buffs that are only available to the MA prof.

Adventurers can also be very good at soloing, being quite well rounded in most aspects.

Im sure Im forgetting some and people will correct me if I missed something.

I dont have any experience with fixers, traders, or shades so I cant comment on them.

I would say that bottom of the barrel for soloing are docs, because while they can stay alive forever, it takes almost as long to kill something and NTs because they are so squishy and tend to fall down a lot.

Obviously this is all my opinion. Im sure there are others out there.

I'd have to agree and say that either the Martial Artist or Bureaucrat can get by the best soloing. With the MA, you can heal yourself, do great damage, and never have to worry about buying new weapons. With the Crat, you can nuke for decent damage, charm monsters to fight on your side, and also calm monsters that are ripping you up so you can sit and heal.

If you like to be busy during missions, then go for Crat. If you don't want to deal with anything extra, and just want to kill them bad guys, then go for MA.

I solo fairly often (Katpaws had her 1000 token board before she could wear it). In order of my preference... MA, Advent, Fixer, Enforcer. I'll comment on my Keeper as well.

My level 153 MA solo'd a level 183 mission today. Only time she was in the least bit of trouble was against a 187 Atrox Enforcer. She finished that one with 25% of her hp.

But, she does it by healing. As you go up in levels, to be able to solo effectively, you have to do above 50% difficulty missions. Right now it takes Katpaws about 6-7 75% missions to get a level. Bigger mobs hit faster and for more damage. Simply relying on FA stims for heailng won't cut it.

My adventurer (Dooley) who has retired from missions and such was a decent soloer as well. I used to do just under 75% missions with him. Advents (in my opinion) heal better than MAs because of the reduced recharge time on their healing nanos. If I make another advent, though, he'll use pistols. Currently pistols significantly out-damage swords.

At lower levels, a Fixer with both long term and short term HOT(Heal Over Time) nanos can heal faster than the mobs can hit him. But that starts to level out as you go up in levels. However, I know several fixers who solo very well. High evades, good armor, decent HOT and as damaging an SMG as you can equip. Higher level fixers are starting to swtich to the Fixer pistols, but I've heard some people give those mixed reviews.

My Atrox Enforcer (who is no longer with us), was a great soloer up to about level 30. Was doing 100% missions for a lot of his career. His method was simple. He had more hit points than they did. He won battles by attrition. However, I got tired of having to sit on the floor, waiting for my Treatment skill to recharge after every battle.

My Keeper (Solarknight) is only level 35 right now; so I'm not sure I can judge soloing long term with him. I'll say that at his level, with the proper perks and nanos, he's soloing 80% missions. Only time I had to use an FA stim on him last mission was when he took on a level 50ish turret. Had to use two in that fight.

All that said ... If you're hoping to advance quickly, you're going to have to team. At low levels, soloing is a fine option. Properly setup characters can solo very well. Some of my newest ones (admitedly twinked a bit) can level twice in a solo mission. However, over time, that goes away.

As I mentioned, Katpaws has to do 6-7 solos even at 30+ levels above her to gain one level of experience. On the other hand, if she does a full team mission, she typically gains 1-1.5 levels per mission. And even that is starting to decline. The levelling curve definitely has an increasing slope.
