Couple Things
Not hitting the mobs is a line of sight issue. Seems like the Z axis got hosed in the patch, among other things. This is why jump works sometimes. This is why you get the 'Take it Easy' message. Z-axis doesn't explain the attacks 2 rooms apart but again, I mantain it is a line of sight problem. No matter what the cause, it is not something we should be expected to work around.

As for those that are posting that the game works fine, let me explain something that may help you understand. The low level characters do work fine. And almost all of the posts I see with people claiming the game is great having nothing more than a 30ish character max. It isn't until the upper 30's, maybe even 40 and beyond, that the mob difficulty increases. By this I mean you get more casting mobs with more nanos to cast. They also tend to team up more. I ran a couple low level characters to observe mob behavior.

Level 6 and under, no matter how many mobs are in a room, only one at a time attack.

Level 6-15 no more than 2 mobs per room attack.

I did not test 15-25 but I know that at level 30, 3 mobs will attack.

I also know that at levels 50 and higher, you get the whole freaking missions worth charging at you at times as well.

So, in short, before you come here and tell EVERYONE else that the game is OK, qualify your statements. It may work for you at your level but that doesn't mean it works at all levels.

rintrah
I am very sorry about the frustration you have had with the patch. I also agree that the information we put out yesterday should have been out on Saturday, and the more thorough reply in this thread should have been out yesterday. Please understand that we are working very hard to fix the problems the patch introduced on Friday. That is no excuse for not getting out more information earlier in Daily Update and on the message board earlier, but getting out more information faster is something we will make better contingency plans for in Support and Community.

Usually the way I work with the daily update is that it is posted in the evening, Norwegian time. In the morning I write a summary of questions and opinions that is discussed with the team. In the late afternoon or evening a reply is posted.

The reason we patched on Friday was that one of the major crash-bugs was fixed in 12.6. We have compared peak numbers and crash messages for this and last weekend. Based on this statistics, the patch was a big improvement on crash problems for a lot of players.

People had more questions about the test-server. It is important to stress that 12.6 is identical with 12.63. The changes in how monsters behaved in combat had been on the test-server for a week. We are evaluating why we didnt receive much feedback on it. One reason is probably that we need a wider level range on the characters on the test-server. Another could be the focus on other changes, like the item requirement discussion. This is something we will work to improve.

Other questions brought up in the thread:

The bugs that made it impossible to attack monsters has been found and fixed in a patch that will be put out on the test-server today. We expect to release this patch quite soon.

Stats that are wrongly displayed are the reason for a lot of the bugs reported in this thread. It is important that this is a visual bug, that doesnt affect the calculation in combat. The same applies to this as for the not able to attack-bug above.

The monsters are self-healing more than intended. We are considering different ways to fix it, like reducing the available nano pools for the monsters or stop the chain casting.

The exe-patch is on the way, perhaps it already is out before this message reach you. We will work to improve the amount of time between patching and the time it is put out.

Some people commented that they liked faster combat. Our goal is that it should be possible to use special attacks, nano programs and change in strategy during a fight. That means that we probably dont want to make the combat much faster.

The delay on swapping weapons is intentional.

The changes to Nano Techician have been scheduled for the next content patch all the time. We will probably need some small fix-patches first, but the changes for NT will start in the next big patch.

I have read the posting from Jws about Aimed Shot and the patch-message that says that the system caused 20 times more damage than intended. It is more correct to say that the system could result in 20 times damage than intended. If you look at the damage comparison from jws, the range is much wider for the Aimed Shot than the Burst special attack. That, in addition to stats and equipment, often makes it difficult to compare different special attack.

I have also sent other bugs that you have discussed in this thread to the live team.

Parts of this posting will be used in the Daily Update today.
