See what you think about this Idea!
Ok here goes....

I posted this as a reply to a thread a long time ago and I felt that it was time re-visit it and get some feedback....and mabye just mabye someone at FC will read this (I doubt it) but anyhow here it is............

It seems as that as AO has progressed as a game the same issue is at the heart of all the problems. And this would be "CLASS BALANCING". Now before you click the "back" button and sigh and say "Oh my god another My Char is gimped *****er"...read on.

I see many many posts on things like..... Community, new weapons that can't be used, no-one will help me and they are the only ones that can help, I can't find a team, my pet loves to hump doors, why to my Nano's suck......you get the idea.

Now chew on this and and lets get some feedback.....

How bout ANY class can use (gasp) ANY NANO and or WEAPONS and ARMOR!!!

OH MY GOD....Dannix has lost it!

But wait, why not is what I say....why cant a MA use a Damage shield....why can't a Doctor have a Pet.....FC has made sure that class can't fight by itself. Why can't a Trader use MA mastery.....my god its 45000 years in the future and we are still stuck with a Cleric that can't fight and a Warrior that can't heal. WHY WHY WHY!!!!!!

Sure there would be penalities to use the class only nano's but who says that everyone should be stuck in a box and play by FC's rules.

Now the big thing that people would say is "If FC did that then no-one would want my (Insert class and race) in thier team. I say bull.....I for one would like a Doctor that could woop ass and still heal fighting next to my MA....or a Trader that could hit hard and still give people a Wrangle. How bout a ENF that has a pet so they as the main tank can heal up and arent a nano sponge for the healer.

See the problem has become that people are not playing the classes that can't take care of themselves or need extra downtime to play. So in the long run you are seeing less and less of the utility classes and more and more of the "DO ALL" classes. I find this disturbing, AO is supposed to be fun and its becoming yet another Hack and LVL game......

So there it is...my idea, I post this hoping for some constuctive input and mabye the idea that FC will see a future for this, because we all want to have fun and typing "LIST" and seeing 12344 Fixers and 2 nano techs is getting a little old!

Thanx for your time I know this was kinda long
