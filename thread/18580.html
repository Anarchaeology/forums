I was sneaking with my back against the wall, my Engineer pet following closely behind. The enemy was in the next room, yet due to my pet being mind-blowingly stupid, I had to guide it to the door before I told it to attack.

However, right as I'm about 3/4 of the way across the room, the screen shakes violently, then stops.

Where am I now? The floor said "Error" on it, in red and yellow. All around me I'm surrounded by HOM errors. Then I realize I'm trapped in the wall! I haven't had this happen to me since the game first came out!
