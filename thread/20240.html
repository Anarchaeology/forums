Is it really all FC's fault?
Is it really all Funcom's fault that places like Newland, 20k, and Broken Shores are a mess? Or is it our faults? Think about it.

We go to 20k to find a group and then the group leader would go to Newland and get a mission that is located at 20k. But this is bad for both zones because you have tons of people in newland looking for missions and tons of people in 20k looking for a team there because the missions are located there and this is too much for the servers to handle.
Why do we do it? Because we are too lazy to travel.
Sure you can say stuff like:
"The cities are poorly designed", "They need better servers", etc... Which is somewhat true but we still need to stop relying on these places for missions.

So where should we go?
I don't know that's up to you. One place I like to go since i'm omni is Rome. There is no lag there whatsoever but that's because noone ever uses the mission terminals there. I don't see why a team wouldn't just go there and get a team mission to lets say Varmint woods. All you have to do is grid to newland and go south big woop. Or Milky way just go to 20k and head north wow that's sooo far.

Of course that will never happen so what I think FC should do is just let us pick what location we want from the mission terminals and have the really good rewards(like battlesuits) only available in far away missions or something like that.

Ok i'm done flame away.
