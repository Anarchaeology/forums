Supression Gas Increases Over Rubi-Ka
Now most of you may just look at me as a large Atrox merc but, I have a conspiricy theory that I think all the Clans and Omni-Tek employees whould know about.

I feel that through orders from Omni-Prime, Omni-Tek Rubi-Ka has increased the supression gas production levels on the planet. The rational behind this being that an over abundance of this gas will eventually cover the entire planet eliminating the ability for anyone to argue or disagree with anything that Omni-Tek orders. In theory within the year we would all be walking around with big ear to ear grins on our faces chanting all hail Omni-Tek due to a supression gas level of 200%.


OOC : How is that for a storyline. No need for war that way and there is nothing anyone can do to stop it. Well except maybe destroy the supression gas machines.
