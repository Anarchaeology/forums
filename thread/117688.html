A day in the life of BRB
She was bent double over the shell of a Mk3 Omni-Tek Butlerbot. The claw marks on the flanks of the poor thing showing that its first visit to Bronto Ranch Breaks had been somewhat...eventful. Various sounds emerged from the unfortunate bots vicinity...clangs as a positronic spanner was over enthusiastically slammed around, whistle, beeps and what sounded strangely like groans as coming from the almost shattered voice control unit.

Balerienne (Bal to her friends, most of which were electronic) muttered "Damn silly idea...who thought that a butler could ride a bronto!! Bloody country bumpkin, pistol toting bimbo"

"Oh, hi Tex....."

Texasrose was stood behind her, absently polishing the barrel of her newest pistol acquisition, a wry grin on her face.

"Well Bal hun, it was kinda his idea...I 'did' try and dissuade him, but he is here for a break from the norm...and he got it!!"

Balerienne frowned, picking up and peering at what appeared to be a shattered occular enhancement module "Getting his upper torso ripped off is certainly not what one would expect during the day to day running of a Butlerbot, thats true enough"

"Well" Texasrose replied in her odd drawl "I like to see it as 'experiencing the rough and tumble of the country', its good for them!!"

Balerienne muttered something unintelligable under her breath before burying herself up to her lithe waist once more inside the Butlerbot.

Another letter to write...
TR sat in her small office overlooking the ranch. Outside, a couple of new arrivals were getting their first look at a bronto mama. She took a few moment's break to watch before turning back to her work.

She sighed. Another letter to write. "Dear Mr Bumble," she wrote. "I regret to inform you that your MK3 OT Butlerbot has been involved in an unfortunate accident." She put down the pen and thought back to just a week ago when the MK3 - or "Ginger" - had arrived, full of enthusiasm and eager to get at the Brontos for his first spin. They were just...bots.

She looked out the window again. One of the newcomers looked throught the smudged glass at her, his optronic eyes whirling. Funny how like Ginger he looked.

Harvey entered the room, carrying a tray of tea and biscuits.

"Better clean that window, Harvey," she said.
