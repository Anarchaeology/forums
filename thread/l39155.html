When a mob or a player runs a porgram on you read the text closely:

XXXX forces YOUR NCU to run XXXX

Negative programs are like viruses that force your NCU to run their program.

Your nano resist is a measure of the anit-hack security you put on your NCU. If they hack your NCU (failing your resist) then they make your NCU run a porgram IT thinks is normal regardless of the effect. A computer only does what it is told ya know?

If you do get a debuff put on your it WILL remain in effect after the mob dies. Look at Ransack Skills from a trader. This is because they are forcing YOUR NCU to run THEIR program. The program will run it's course regardless of the programer's presence or lack there of.

Look at Basic Insurance Hack. You are hacking into your insurance account and telling them that you have been hurt and need healing even though the policy activates only on death normally. You are hacking the system in essence.

In regards to removing one of your buffs if a debuff goes over your max NCUs would be bad. Whick one would you lose? The oldest buff? The newest buff? The biggest? The smallest? Your Heal Over Time? Or your Grid Jump (SoW)? Or maybe your AC buff? You would not get to chose and any result would be bad. It is enough of a penalty that it takes up NCUs so you can't counter buff to fix the damage and reamins in effect the full time as opposed to player run programs (root - 3 - 8 seconds actual - listed 55 seconds).

I hope this explains some mechanics and why things are the way they are.

1) Pet Pathing- I realize that this has been stated before, but the pet dependent classes are at a strong disadvantage due to the extremely poor pathing.

2) Enemy AI- Pathing is an issue for all these fellows as well, but more importantly there is no depth to the AI whatsoever. Enemy AI should entail nano use (appropriate to their level), tactics which are relevent to their class. Essentially, a fight with an AI enemy in the game is a simple slug and shot fest to see who dies first. There is no 'method' to their madness, they just run up, shoot shoot punch punch.

To elaborate even further...why don't enemy Engineers, Crats and Meta's have pets to use against PC's? How come Soldiers, Fixers and others who have FA, Burst and other special attack modes don't use them (or if they do, why don't they actually work)? Agent and Fixer AI's should use conceal against players, being both undected on radar and visually if a player fails perception. There are numerous examples, but essentially the only thing which makes enemy AI different at this point is their in-game model.

3) Agents. Very serious issues for agents need to be addressed. False Proffessions have been mentioned as relates to 'being stuck' in an FP mode. Additionally, It seems to me that the only reason for using an FP is to have access to nanos. Shouldn't agents recieve some other benefit during the FP duration? For example, if they use Enforcer, shouldn't skills that are native to that class get a boost (i.e., 1H/2H bash/edged, etc.). The only thing that the FP nano provides is a text description of the player as that class, a hit to nano capabilities (several levels below that of the current agent level), and the unavailability of agent specific nanos.

Aimed Shot. This ability seems far to underpowered as to how it relates to increasing the odds of critical shots. Under the assumption that Funcom feels rifles are already balanced (which may be an issue unto itself), having critical shots occur more frequently due to the Aimed Shot skill should not be a question...it just should be. Right now, (and I have been testing this quite extensively) Aimed shot has produces critical shots *extremely* rarely. The agent which I have been testing with has far more frequent critical hits with a sidearm pistol at a much lower skill than with a rifle, both regular and aimed shot.

Concealment. This ability should keep the agent/fixer/whoever visually concealed from both radar and otherwise from all those who fail their perception. I believe that a successful aimed shot should allow the agent to remain concealed but at a temporary hit to their concealment score...whatever Funcom designs as appropriate. This would allow them to be in 'sniper' mode, as the class suggests, but simply more likely to be detected when actually using Aimed shot. Attacking normally should decrease the concealment significantly.

4) Trade Skills- These should be implemented ASAP. The economy of the game is essential to it's long term viability for PC's. Secondly, the availability of trade skill items is ridiculous. Omni-Tek is supposed to have billions of employees and take up an entire solar system for it's headquarters. But trade skill (and other equipment for that matter) items are virtually non-present from the game? There needs to be 'trade-skill' machines offering relevant items at several levels of quality. Doing so will jump start the player made economy and make the engineer and trader classes much more appropriate and viable as classes in the game, as I believe Funcom intends. Items and weapons cannot be enhanced currently which should be possible by those with appropriate trade skills.

5) Missions- Several notes here. Hall of Mirrors. Poor Pathing (again). Lack of depth to mission story. 'Double Clutch' treasure chests (having to reopen twice to find goods inside). Empty or virtually so missions.

Additionally, missions have no 'boss' type enemy located inside. Shouldn't the mission objective be guarded by some tougher enemy NPC or monster? Mission objectives should be defended much more fierce than currently done. This would prevent people from 'rushing' missions just to complete them as quick as possible, since the objective will be guarded by one or more NPC's that must be overcome in some fashion.

Missions require almost solely for combat related skills to be used. Although not necessarily requiring a specific trade skill, missions should permit one to have an 'easier' go should they have skills that apply. For example, computer security systems should be available that can be overcome by computer skill. Nano defended doorways/rooms can be thwarted by nano programming skills. Mechanical engineering could disable 'generators' which turn off/down the lights inside the mission building, making concealment easier. I could go on, but the possibilites are endless and extremely intriguing if Funcom would attempt just a fraction of them.

6) Communications- In game communications could and should be enhanced to permit easier methods of trading items. Others have mentioned 'consignment' machines, which is absolutely brilliant, would be fairly easy to implement, and most certainly would create a very good method of trading goods between PC's.

7) Nanos- First and foremost, they do not 'stick' properly after one has zoned, and require recasting. Although the interface still reports them active, the benefits are not present (with the exception of statistics being raised as a reporting effect only).

Second, the variation of nanos in the game is pathetic. Essentially all classes have equivalent versions of nanos all through their career, albiet increasingly powerful versions of them. If it is intended that new nano programs are to be available due to the trade skill, implement the trade skill! Otherwise, add more variation to the available lines of nanos in the game for all classes! Some examples of nanos that should be present (to some classes in any case) are 'cancel nanos' which can cancel certain hostile nanos or those of certain quality levels (to allow buffs to be cancelled off enemy PC/NPC's), 'information nanos' which permit a caster to gain some specific information about a target (such as Hit points, Skill Levels, etc.). I could go on, but the point is already obvious.

General Nanos. Although the selection of general nanos is quite good, covering most if not all of the skills a player might have, limiting them in power to an increase of 20 points seems frankly sparse. As players reach 100+, it would be nice to have general nanos available that allow buffs more in line with that level of character. If these will be possible to create with nano programming, implement the trade skill!

-Thanks!
