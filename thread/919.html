Recent Omni-Tek efforts to deal with clan terrorist, Portman
This report is regarding recent Omni-Tek activities related to well-known clan terrorist, Portman that have been led by Omni-Tek Assault Corps. All of the following occured on RK1.


11/12 @ 06:00 GMT - Tir, Council of Truth building
Large protest arranged to help capture Portman and bring her to trial. Only Omni-Tek representatives were that from Omni-Tek Assault Corps, but over fourty clan members came to share their hopes, worries and vuglarities.

The discussion of the failed amnesty and ceasefire was important, and clan attendees claimed they knew nothing of Portman's whereabouts. The persistant claims from many rebels that they knew nothing of Portman's location convinced Omni-Tek employees in attendance that Portman is in-fact acting alone and without majority support of the rebels.

This fact helps support the desire for peace, and the need to take Portman into custody. Portman is working alone to destroy Rubi-Ka, and many rebels were in agreement.

Portman failed to show, but we know she learned of this in latter days.


11/17 @ 04:00 GMT - Newland, Neutral city of Meetmedere
After being contacted by anonymous sources, Omni-Tek was alerted of a possible counter-attack on the city led by Portman. For the previous few hours, the city had been under control of a militaristic Omni-Tek group made up of a few fringe Omni-Tek factions.

The only guild that responded to the news quickly enough and spoke out against the terrorist was Omni-Tek Assault Corps. About 8 members came as quickly as possible to apprehend Portman, and fight off her lackeys.

Portman resisted arrest and claimed that she would lead the rebels to glory. She was unwilling fight unassisted, but she offered a duel to Omni-Tek soldier CaptFallout. Not her versus the level 97 soldier, but against an unrelated clan NT: Caid.

The duel began decently, with Caid flinging a powerful root and mocking the flailing soldier. Once the root broke, Caid was quickly destroyed. With his last breath Caid ran away, and and an unnamed clan assailant jumped in and attacked the injured CaptFallout.

Despite ruining the officially sanctioned duel, the clan asassin was promptly execute as was the barely surviving Caid.

Portman quickly left (possibly out of disgust from the dishonorable actions of clan Opposing Force) and MMD was returned to Omni-Tek control with the help of many Omni factions.


Too all those that know of Portman's whereabouts or any of her future actions, please notify your guild leader or any public channel of communication.

Thank you.
Omni-Tek Assault Corps
