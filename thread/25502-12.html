Shortsighted Change
Even with the modifications they have allowed to this planned change, this is a terrible move for the game - not just for traders.

People that are for this I don't think have thought it all the way through.

The amount time a team has to sit around trying to gather the right elements in their team to accomplish any worth doing will go through the roof.

Right now you can add a trader to be a team healer if no docs are available - and frankly there are way too few of them so there rarely are.

Think back to all of teams you have been in that have used a trader to be the main healer - which I guarantee is at least half of them if not more. Now imagine that only docs could heal well.

How many of the groups would have failed miserably or never got off the ground? How much more time would you have had to spend standing around doing nothing while your team leader spammed for a healer?

In groups that had a doc, how many times did a trader as a back-up healer save your doc or your entire team a quick ride to the save point?

The dev's post says traders will still be able to team heal, but they will just have to juggle more. Traders already juggle more than any other class. Typically I am juggling healing, damage dealing, mezzing, calming a mob off a team member that got aggro away from the main tank, and area root. Adding something else to think about to the list just means someone will likely die.

I understand that there is a general bitterness towards traders in the game as the class is perceived to be too strong. I will be the first to admit that we are a stout class.

But do people really want us weakened in the best way that strength can actually benefit groups? Do people really want the pool of capable team healers cut by over half?

Think about it....
