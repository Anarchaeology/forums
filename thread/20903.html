"Hunting" is silly
Am I the only person who thinks hunting is silly? The idea of just running around killing random animals/mutants makes me feel like more of a poacher then a citizen attempting to make a difference in a virtual world. I wouldnt mind so much of they were aggro, or threatened something worth saving but it seems that they keep to themselves and dont bother anyone and I feel like a murderer attacking them. I guess Im alot more interested in having an enjoyable rich experience at each level then powerleveling by going on murderous raids of seemingly peaceful mutant camps. Anyone else feel the same? feel different?

Buy cheap goods at factories and load up a Carrier, then haul it to town selling it at a profit to stores or players ( Yup. I am dreaming )

Get "missions" from city bound employers to deliver or fetch things. No killing, mazes or dungeons involved...just plain ole working for those inclined to do so.

Piracy and theft ( Fixers might like this )
