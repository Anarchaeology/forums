Again, I agree with FC's original thinking when they designed this. Controled land should be easier to defend than attack. 250 towers are harder to own than loot that comes from uber mobs. This just throws out the concept of "risk vs reward," however.

The way it stands now, if you want to get high-end equipment, you have to fight dangerous high-end monsters. There is a high degree of risk involved (even the best guilds wipeout often), and it takes quite a lot of your time to succeed.

But with towers, it's exactly the opposite. If your organization has QL 250 towers, there is an infinitesimal amount of risk to your base, while the bonuses are immense. The QL 250 bases that have been destroyed, were destroyed over the course of several days. On the other hand if you have QL 150 towers, a single team can destroy your base in under 30 minutes. And if you have QL 50 towers, a single twink can destroy your base in 15 minutes.

If the risk vs reward ratio for equipment were inverted as it is for towers, then azure reveries armor would drop off rollerrats in the backyard and you'd be able to blitz PoPs, while Ian Warr would drop a solar powered pistol and a bronto burger.
