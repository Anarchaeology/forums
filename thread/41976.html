Rooting
I think i know what hes refering to.

In the past if you zoned in to a 0% or 25% zone while in a Yalm you always zoned at ground level leaving yourself open to being rooted by anyone in your pvp range once the grace period expires.

If your lagging badly you sometimes are close to 10 seconds on the grace time so it would be easy for people to root you before you could move off.

The other trick i saw was people deyalming above you and casting a root as they fell past you.

Thankfully FC fixed both these last patch so you zone at the hight you were last at on the other zone and you can no longer cast while falling (or jumping).

So i would say just be careful, fly high if you dont want to get attacked and make sure you zone just above peoples attack range.
