Joining the 'farewell' train
If you didn't catch it by my title, here goes:

I'm leaving.

Now that I'm through with that, here comes the required 'why are you leaving' response:

I played this game to get away from it--which doesn't make much sense unless you can read my mind, and that would be very creepy.

My main goal in playing Anarchy Online was (and always has) been not to create an uber character, but rather to create a character who did something. The old 'to be famous' scheme, cliched as it might be was my goal.

Unfortunately, I was a clueless dolt.

Firstly, I figured that I would need a character of some level and be a part of a signifcant organization to accomplish my goal of doing 'something.' Getting into some of the larger organizations wasn't all that hard. Getting a high level character, though, was a bit beyond my aptitude for boredom.

In the time that I spent trying to get a high level character (and re-rolling because I got easily bored of my characters or became frustrated) I began to realize a few things. One of which was that doing 'something' along my definition just wasn't possible.

One idea that I had was a 'march to Rome'--an event which anybody who knows anything about history would freak at. Without giving away exact details, it would involve a large number of people. I can't imagine the lag or even the possibilty of getting people marching into Rome.

So I gave up on my big dreams and decided to settle down in a quiet community, namely Newland. Things were nice... but again, I grew bored and drifted away from the game entirely. I would attribute this mostly to a news article which we (Cosa Nostra) had done to create a little story of our own that never made it to the front pages for an unknown reason. The recent demise of the Neutral Council hasn't made me any more interested in this game either.

Again and again, the same thing happened: I got bored. This game, ignoring all it's other faults (imbalances) is boring. This is why I'm quitting. I love the social stuff. I love the people here, including the *******s. I love the forum. I love the story--I might even buy 'Prophet Without Honor' even though I've canceled my account. But there is no patch, no booster pack, no expansion set that can possibly cure Anarchy Online of its flat gameplay.
