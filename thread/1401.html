Pistols and aimed shots
I am a level 8 Opifex soldier, who is currently dual-wielding two ql5 pistols and using the Damage Multiplier buff. I do around 18-36 points of damage a second. Not bad. But I've heard that later on in the game, pistols become totally worthless. Completely. People tell me that pistols become reduced to their minimum damage and can't do anything more as a result of high mob armor. While it is true, that assault rifles should punch armor better (which is why swat teams are equipped with rifles, not pistols), pistols are still significant weapons. They shouldn't be nerfed like this, or no one will ever use a pistol (and especially not the pistol/smg hybrid the Kang-Tao Spitting Cobra, which I think is a shame, since that weapon has potential to be really cool). I hope that what people tell me is not true, but if it is, I would really like A) for someone here to tell me, and B) for FunCom to find a way to give pistols some more power...perhaps not as much damage per shot as the assault rifle, but if guns could be made to generally bypass armor a bit more, pistols could be a weapon of choice for many people (as they are today, a standard issue weapon for many soldiers).
As for Aimed Shot, while it seems reasonable at first for them to do as much damage as regular shots but have a higher chance of a critical, I feel that the chance for a critical should be more in the 75%s, rather than in the lower ranges as I have seen before. I am not an Agent, but I have played one, and it seems that Aimed Shot has become a slightly harder to pull off version of a Fligh Shot. While an Agent can botch an aimed shot and inflict normal damage, the point is to give them a significant chance to inflict extra damage. I feel that it would be more balanced for Agents to have significantly higher rates of success, to make sniping a feared thing, rather than a mild indicator of someone trying to kill you.

Just a few thoughts, but thank you.

Roix on Rubi-Ka 1.
