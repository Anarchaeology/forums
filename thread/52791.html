Hello.
Who are you.
What do you do?

From that point, this mob seems to take two different conversational paths.

He will either say:

I do important experiments...

or

I was doing some very important experiments before you interrupted me!

From here you can say:

What experiments.

Talk to you about building a crystal and looking for a seed.

Then you say, "How can I get the seed?"

He tells you some stuff.

You say, "yes"

He tells you some stuff.

You gain a new mission.

IMPORTANT

For some reason, he does NOT respond 99.9% of the time to the above. It took me over an hour of saying the exact same things to get to the point where he gave me the mission. He would say 'I dont' understand' or some derivitive at some point along the line.

This may be intentional as he is suffering from radiation poisoning. If it is not, please please look into it. It was frustrating as hell to sit there saying the EXACT same words over and over again for OVER an hour to get this mission.
