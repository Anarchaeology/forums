Ship of Fools
"What do you mean, my luggage isn't aboard this ship?!" I asked her through gritted teeth, trying very hard to keep my voice light and calm. The customer service rep tap-tap-tapped lightly at the keyboard with her manicured fingernails, frowning slightly in pretend concentration. I wasn't fooled. It was 20 minutes to noon, and I'd already seen her glance at her watch. Twice.

"Well, it must have been bumped back to the next flight", she said lightly, completely unconcerned that I was without clothes and most personal effects for the next two months.

"Can you confirm this?" I asked, still trying very hard to be civil. All I could think of was the 150-credit-tip, apparently wasted, on the porter at the spaceport who tagged and checked my luggage.

"No", she said, with the sweetest smile on her face, giving a helpless little I-don't-really-give-a-sh** shrug. We won't have contact with them until they land on Rubi-Ka."

I stifled the urge to reach across the counter, and throttle her with her own perky little bow-tie. "So.... in the meantime, what *exactly* would you suggest I do? Since the only clothing I now have is what I'm wearing" I stared her down. There was no way she was going to get off the hook.

She stared back a moment, squirmed, and finally picked up the telephone. She must have some big lunch plans, to cooperate to this degree, I thought dryly. I looked up at the ceiling, listening to her explain, then a series of hmmms, ahhhhs, I sees....

She hung up the phone, and flashed me that big pageant smile they all seemed to have on this ridiculously expensive luxury spacecruiser. Damned ship of fools, if you asked me. "I'm very pleased to inform you", she said cheerfully, "that I am authorized to offer you a 10% discount on any clothing or toiletries from our gift shop"...

"Don't do me any favors", I muttered under my breath, thinking of my credit balance. I silently thanked Mama, pest of a woman that she is, for insisting I accept a sizeable deposit, 'just-in-case'. The customer service rep raised her over-plucked eyebrows. I spoke a bit louder, pretending to repeat my words, "thank you for your help." She completely missed my sarcasm, and flashed that smile again. "Thank you, for flying Trans-Galactic Airlines". She hadn't even gotten the sentence out before she had the little 'closed' placard set up at her station, and her handbag slung over her shoulder.

** three hours later**

"That will be 32, 748 credits, please", I was cheerfully informed by the sales rep in the gift shop. I was aghast. "What about the 10% off?", I quickly interjected. "That's the total *after* the discount", I was informed haughtily, by what appeared to be the brunette counterpart of the customer service girl who had 'handled' my lost baggage issue...

Over 30,000 credits! For underwear, assorted toiletries, and a horrible floral caftan 3 sizes too big. ARGH! I sighed, and nodded my assent. "Very well, I'll take it".

I headed back to my stateroom. At least this was nice - although I wished, not for the thousandth time, I'd waited one more week and travelled out with the ICC troops. This had been Mama's idea, make a little vacation out of my move to Rubi-Ka. She'd booked this trip for me, and fool that I was, I hadn't bothered to read up on this cruise. As it turned out, it was a mostly-singles cruise - for the senior citizen set. So while it had a beautiful heated pool, sauna, lovely restaurants, etc... - I was surrounded by elderly swingers. Mama's idea of a great time, apparently, but not mine. I couldn't repress a shiver, recalling one particularly ancient specimen who had grabbed my bottom at the lunch buffet. When I'd turned, ready to slap him, he'd smiled an almost toothless-smile, looking for all the world like a wrinkly turtle. I just waggled my finger at him, ah-ah-ah... I managed to smile, not quite nastily, but he got the message and backed off.

Hmm... I balanced my checkbook for the fifth time. If I was careful, I had *just* enough money to get settled in when I reached Rubi-Ka, and and could collect that first paycheck. The ICC had given me a sizeable moving allowance, even though I wasn't their employee, per se. I was a contracted engineer. So while I wouldn't get retirement benefits or medical, the pay would be very nice. And I could purchase insurance at a discount, which was a very nice perk.

I finished putting away my purchases, and sighed. At least I had *my* swimsuit, in my carry-on. I decided to get changed, and go for a swim.

Two hours later, and I was still glued to the window. A smaller craft had detached from the fighter in view, and appeared to be docking with our vessel.

A discreet knock on the door made me jump, and bump my head on the top of the porthole-style window. "Come in", I called, rubbing my forehead ruefully. The door opened, and the porter appeared, wheeling in a cart, from which wonderful smells issued. Ahhh, dinner. Excellent! Oh good, the porter was Robin. All of seventeen years old, with wavy brown hair, and the biggest puppydog crush on me. Time to get a little information. I smiled brightly at him.

"Hi there", I said, still standing beside the window. "About time you folks remembered little ol' me, starving up here". I pretended to narrow my eyes at him. "Or did you have them delay my dinner until your shift started, so you could deliver it?"

He blushed - aha! Too easy. I decided to have a little mercy on the boy. "What's going on out there?", I indicated the view outside my window. He paused in the task of setting the table, to respond. "They're an escort team. Apparently another cruiser in our fleet, the Merriweather, was attacked earlier this week. They stripped everything of value from the vessel, rounded up the passengers and crew, and set them adrift in a shuttle pod. Then they tried to blow up the cruiser. But the charges didn't all detonate, so they're aboard, investigating the piracy now."

He set the plates on the table, uncovering them, as he continued. "The Merriweather was just days out from where we are now, so Trans-Galactic decided to have an armed escort join us, for the remainder of the journey".

My mind was busy, trying to imagine the PR angle Trans-Galactic was going to take. I grinned, imagining that twit in the bowtie having to fend off rumors of war. Something else occurred to me. "I thought this vessel was already armed to the teeth, for that very reason". "Yes", he nodded, "but this ship isn't capable of outmanoeuvering the smaller pirate vessels." I nodded, thinking that made perfect sense.

He straightened up, and turned to leave. "Did you need anything else?" I shook my head, smiling. "Just ring when you're done, and I'll come clear up". I escorted him to the door. He stepped through, then turned, his eyes looking all devoted and adoring. I smiled firmly, and pressed a tip into his hand. "Thank you very much". I shut the door, and sat down to enjoy my dinner.
