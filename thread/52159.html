Supa Leet
Actually, I think it's quite do-able from a programming perspective. After all, it's just another zone. Think of the backyards - those don't exist as a contiguous part of Rubi-Ka, they're discreet zones unto themselves, walled off with a single entrance and exit.

An orbital facility would basically be another, very public, backyard. Whompa access only, and fake stellar "scenery". It's not as if they have to model the weather system of the planet (how would you know it was accurate, anyway?), they just have to make a convincing facsimile and that's not so hard to do.

However, it is another zone, with it's own layout, NPC's, maybe even missions, which would amount to a great deal of hard work. Nothing that's more difficult in principle than adding another terrestrial zone, but still a lot of man hours to implement.
