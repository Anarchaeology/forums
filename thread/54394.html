"The devil of the emerald forrest"
by Randa "Poetica" Whites
The emerald forrest under me
Mighty old oaks
Green moist grass
Rare flowers blossom
Butterflies in the air
Peaceful
I rest my eyes
I rest my mind
I leave my yalm
I sit down on the ground
Breathing
Closing my eyes
Feeling tired
I lay down
Look up at the skies
Heavy eyes
I close them
Then i hear a voice out of the blue
Theres a monster in the forrest
A devil
There is a devil in the emerald forrest
I jump up - eyes wide open
Looking around
Then realizing
The devil is me
