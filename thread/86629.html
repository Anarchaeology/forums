Rimors PvP Tourney
As the title suggests, I'd like to propose a PvP tournament on Rimor (Rk2).

Why would I want to do such a thing, isn't the whole current PvP good enough? Well the only PvP i can recall at the moment is Camelot and Tower Fights.

Camelot is so laggy due to the intricate designs of the dungeon, the mass NPC's in the dungeon, the LOS(line of sight) problems in the dungeon, and the ratio of clanners to omni is always AT LEAST 5 to 1, usually . So in the current state omni hardly shows up to Camelot anymore.

Towers are laggy as well when lots of people are there, (not to my knowledge due the towers cause alot of lag themselves.) and dont forget the whole CC (crowd control) issue at the moment, getting warped while standing still is insane and causes lots of problems.

So why not a controlled PvP tournament....somewhat like how arenas work in FPS's. You get matched up vs a player and others watch. Or your team gets matched up vs another team while bystanders watch. But to get such a thing started....we need support for it and laid down rules.

I need input for:
Level Brackets

Should outside buffs be allowed?

Should we have a website to keep track of peoples kills etc? (would need a webmaster to want to help with this, as i am not webpage friendly)

Time limit? Some fights...(think Doc vs Doc) could go on a long long long time. If we impose a time limit, should we just null the fight if it reaches said time limit. (comp heal just before the fight is over would ensure full health, plus HP bugs it is sometimes hard to discern who has more of a Bar left).

Should we have one day a week where we do a whole event of this or should we have the possibility to do it everyday and people just contact me who want to be up for a chance to fight on any given day (teams included).

Prizes for top killers after a said period of time...like a week or a month. (probably wouldnt incorporate initially, and would ask for donations for such a thing from anyone).

How to deal with griefers who may try to ruin our 'controlled' fun?

Should we have only a few people to watch each fight to reduce lag and if a good match people want to hear about, then they post their viewings at a given place.

How many people should watch over each fight in the pvp area to make sure no outside annoyances occur.

What areas would such fights take place? (2ho, mmd, arenas)

Should it only be omnis vs clans or have same side fights as well?

And I am sure there is much much much more you guys can think of to talk about then leaves thoughts and ideas here and ill make a new thread when i think i might have it sorted out. Obviously if this becomes popular there is no way i can run it by myself as for I am not always on nor am I able to keep up with such amount of tells etc. Please no flame.

However, I see several problems. Griefers and gankers were rampant at the Tir arena when it was open to both sides.
