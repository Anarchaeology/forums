Omni-InternOps Announces Opsnet3/"Watch List"
Greetings citizens of Rubi-Ka.

Omni-InternOps, Division 3, is pleased to announce that its gridsite - Opsnet3 - has been declassified and is now available for public reference.

On top of maintaining up-to-date news from Rubi-Ka (both official and from other grid channels), the department has published it's "Watch List" - a list of current threats to Omni-Tek Corporation as well as other groups being monitored for...various reasons.

Omni-InternOps is also pleased to once again state that it is actively seeking new members. To apply, please visit Opsnet3.
