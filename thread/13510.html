Regarding Anarchy Online:

It is important that FUNCOM shouldn't nerf a profession by lessening a professions primary abilities and characteristics.

I have never played an NT nor do I have any inclination to play an NT (what I am stating here is my unbiased opinion)...

Every profession should have unique abilities and characteristics that are specific to the profession. Making harder for a profession in their own "skillset" while other professions with similar skillsets are not nerfed doesn't make sense.

NT's should have it easiest and shine in terms of DD and Nuke Nanos and NOT HAVE TO WORK HARDER THAN THE OTHER PROFESSIONS THAT GET SIMILAR ABILITIES!

Engineers should have the best pets (bots).
Crats should have the best charms.
Soldiers should be have the best artillery.
MP's should have the best "support" buffs.
Docs should have best heals
MA should have best body attacks and body control.
Enforcers should have the best body development.
Traders should have the easiest trade and marketing skills.
Fixers should have the best "thievely" attributes.
Agents should have the best "espionage" attributes.

If ANY one of these were nerfed...that respective profession would be up in arms...

*TO FUNCOM : Players get attached to their characters and profession types. Severely penalizing them will only create a mass exodus from the game when they have no where else to turn.

I myself will only play an ENGINEER or BUREAUCRAT because that is my mindset. If both of these professions became unplayable to me...I would have no where else to go. No other profession interests me...therefore I would close my account. This is potentially what is happening to the NT's. And from a business standpoint by targetting these HIGH LEVEL aspects of the game you are effectively targeting the customer base who are going to be playing and paying for recurrent months...your recurrent revenue!

Please reconsider allowing each profession their respective abilities and thoughtfully considering each profession before applying "nerfing" fixes.
