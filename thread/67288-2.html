You could go to the steps of madness. I believe it was designed for 35-50. But i could be wrong there. Its also a great dungeon, but much smaller then the subway.

firstly - The Subway is nice. to be honest, its the best thing FCs done to date. It brought me back to the game for a bit.

Next and the reason for the post -

Avoid the Steps of madness. Unless its changed, it is over powered and likely a deadly experiance for most. Before i left/returned, I had done the steps many times. with a lot of alts, It pretty much alwyas turned into a horry blood bath and a free trip to reclaim.

The Mobs were all for the most part "Boss" lvl HP. And thier tend to be 3 to 6 in any one room. not Boss Mobs, with thier cool glow. but normal mobs with Boss type HP.

Some of the mobs are able to stun you, preventing you from attacking/nukeing/running.

The loot is ok, quite a number of no-drop items like bracers and such. the main boss drops a coat that nano users love. (adds 8 to all nano skills) Think about Abmouth the subway main boss. now picture it being created to fight lvl 50ish folks.
Then dobule its HP. scared yet?

But wait thiers more - the room he spawns in, will spawn between 3 to 6 other mobs. (but once you clear it out, the spawn is slow enf that you can normal kill the new mob before another one spawns. letting you camp for the Boss)

Over all, its ok if your ready for it. don't go thinking it will be like the subway. you will likely die, fast and messy. (Even with a lvl 67 NT it was a pain to solo some of the mobs. they just have a LOT of HP. takes awhile to wittle them down.)

A full team, lvl 40ish could be able to handle it. Bring a Doc and a calmer. (Crat, Trader or an NT) But be ready to die once or twice. As i said thier are a few spots where thier are 6ish mobs all willing and happy to argo you if you get near them. little to no chance of killing the boss at the end though.

a team lvl 50ish should do ok, but sadly the mobs will likely begin to turn greenish. though thier still a hard fight. killing the boss might be possable, but not overly likely unless super twinks

a Team of lvl 60ish should have no real issue, mabye one or 2 tough spots. most mobs will be gray, one or 2 green to you. But thier still tough, just lots of HP. Once again might be able to drop boss.

Forway of example, When my old guild desided to get nelebs coat (the coat that the boss drops) for our members who need it, we went with oh 7 people. 5 of them 125+, 2 75-100ish. 2 Crat Bots, 2 Crat charmed pets. For almost 11 total folks bashing on the boss.

It still took a good 3 or 4 mins to kill the boss. With high lvl folks hitting for 2 to 3k per hit and such.

The Boss hit for 'ok' dmg. i wasn't tanking it, but its dmg output seemed ok. Just hugh ammounts of HP.

also, a lot of high lvl folks want that coat as well. The Steps are tough enf alot of folks said forget this, i'll come back later. Or are helping low lvl friends get it. so its possable for you to fight your way through the steps, only to find a large pack of high lvl folks waiting for the boss to spawn. Some may be more then happy to stick around for the next spawn and help you kill the boss. Some wont.

The Steps are fun, but sevral times harder and more risky then the subway.
