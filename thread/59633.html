Faunlore (The Beastiary) Updated!
Hi everyone!

Just wanted to post a note saying that Faunlore's been upgraded to version 2.0. Included in this iteration are:

1.) a big-time facelift
2.) a new classification system
3.) a new menu system
4.) three new animals: Dragons, Leets, and Anuns
5.) updated information sheets on all the old animals
6.) loots for major Guardians attached correctly to Auno.org

Future updates will feature more exact information on HP, NP, and levels for animals in the database, new listings, and more updated loot information. Please note that locations were becoming burdensome, so they have been temporarily removed until a better solution is found.

I appreciate all the help and feedback I've received from the community, especially from Jypsie, Forevernyt, Dovve, Emiko (advertising helps), and Twicer! Thanks, and keep the feedback coming.

Visit Faunlore at: http://beastiary.somnaterrae.org

Great hunting everyone!

Rhiannon "Krystanova" Pourier

On location information
Another note: a couple of friends and I are refining our thoughts on how to show location information. I realize that that information is pretty valuable to most players, so if you are disappointed that it isn't in the new release, rest easy.

The main reason for its removal is, as I think I mentioned earlier, the effect it had on site design and updates. It was simply too bulky to have a smooth, clean design, so I've set about finding a better way. It may take a couple of weeks (though I hope not) before I can release something, but the information will be returning. Thanks again!
