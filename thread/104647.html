How About A Compromise on the Nanomage?
I agree with both positions on the genesis of the homo nano.
Given that they have both male and female genders, it is safe to conclude that they can interbreed and procreate.

However, you will notice that many nanomages also lack various "bits" and pieces that aren't exactly normal. Note sewn lips and wires, pipes, etc. I doubt these additions could come from normal processes of procreation.

So I submit that nano mages can reproduce, with notum infused DNA -but let us say that this infusion doesn't make a perfect feotus or newborn, so extra additions are sometimes needed to allow the newborn to survive into adulthood.
