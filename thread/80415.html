CanBeWornWithSocialArmor List?
A complete list, of CanBeWornWithSocialArmor enabled items, a.k.a. StuffYouCanWearWithGridArmor, can be found here:
http://64.106.154.149/socialarmor.asp

The ones most relevant to us as of now, are:

Sunglasses of Syncopated Heartbeats | No longer drops
Hollow Bone Bracer of Merlin Ambrosius | Tarasque
Corroded Ring | Tri Plumbo
Unbecoming Sleeves of Ljotur | Ljotur
Mirror Mask of Ljotu | Ljotur
Pick-a-Finger | Fixer Shop
Truspace 900 XL - Vision | Primus/Secundus, EFP Camp
Amber/Ruby/Sapphire Arcanum Specs | Zoftig Blimp in Hope (vendor)

Also, any skinchip can be worn with Grid Armor. Well, except for things like the ICC Engineer skin chip, of course.

Personally, I wear two Corroded Rings and some Blimp sunglasses.
