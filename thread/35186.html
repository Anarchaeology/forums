67%/33% Attack rating weapons-soldier penalty
It almost seems that FC is adamant about making soldiers use ranged energy on "viable" assault rifle options. Not only do we have to use ranged energy but the worse part is every "viable" assault rifle weapon uses a 67/33 split for attack. With the new soldier weapon, this theory still holds true. I still believe that even with our new weapons and the added potentail of new nanos, this problem will continue to penalize the soldier class. Either someone at FC doesnt understand the ramifications of spliting our attack rating or the soldier class was never intended to have a high attack rating which would boggle the mind. If you compare the "ranged weapon" classes (rifle/assault rifle/pistol/mg/smg/shotgun) , the only class that has a split attack rating on thier "class" specific weaponry is the soldier. Advent get pistols with 100% pistol, Fixers get MG/Smg with 100% Mg/Smg, Agents get rifles with 100% rifle, and Traders get Shotguns with 100% Shotgun. The "viable" soldier weapons get split 67%/33% on attack which makes it even harder to get a high attack rating which we need to do our jobs as soldiers.

Let me try to show in as simple a way i can as how big an impact this is so we can possibly make whoever deems this as acceptable understand why it is a penalty. A soldier has to implant (6) locations to get the same attack rating benefit that all the 100% attack rating "ranged weapon classes" get by implanting in (3) locations. Its simple math, I get 67% from one set of (3) and 33% from the 2nd set of (3) for a total of 100%. In addition to this we get numerous implant conflicts, the main one being Shining head which to get the full 100% (on a 67/33 AR/RE weap) you have to forgo MC for ranged energy. TMS and reflects are suppose to be the soldiers "hi-lite" , implant RE in head and you just lost 2 Marks in TMS and 2-3 in reflects.

In addition to this, the other 100% attack classes have nanos to augment thier attack as well. All our nanos do is enable us to get back to where the other "ranged weapon classes" are with (3) implants should we forgo (1) of the required (6) implants to get the same attack rating. We dont have a nano line to modify in combat siatuations such as root/mezz/heal (Ive yet to find a group that wants to wait for me to recycle TMS every 2 mins) but the 100% attack rating classes have those as well. The soldier is all about weaponry. In fact the soldier lives/dies or contributes to to group solely based on his weapon and yet has the lowest attack rating of all the "ranged weapon" classes due to this peanalty of 67/33

Maybe because we are soldiers we should have more complex weaponry which in FC's mind is ranged energy. I dont mind that and i dont mind spending the IP for RE to make us unique. But we shouldnt be penalized which we are with the 67/33 split. If you (FC) are adamant about keeping ranged energy as part of the req for a soldier only weapon then how about looking into this suggestion:

Make the new soldier only weapon attack 100% based on assault rifle and 20% based on ranged energy. Yes i realize that is 120% but what it does is give the soldier a bonus (20% of ranged energy) for having to invest in RE to use his weaponry. I cant possibly see this as an imbalance as it corrects a penalty weve been striken with since day 1. 20% bonus to attack rating based on your RE is not going to make anyone a god. If you think 20% is too high, then maybe 15%. If you (FC) want to make our new weapon consistent with the weapons you have have provided for the other ranged weapon classes then change the attacking rating to 100% assault rifle or 100/20 assaut rifle/ranged energy.

I realize soldiers have new nanos coming sometime but that will not fix the 67/33 split on attack rating that keeps the soldier class penalized. If not, please let us know where our added benefit exists to offset this penalty because I have yet to find it.
