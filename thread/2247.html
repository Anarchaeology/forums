Yes, not only is the algebraic notation backwards, but opening pawn to king's bishop four is not exactly normal either, unless you are deliberately provoking the opponent like Fischer did once in a while.... Pawn to queen's bishop four *is* a standard opening, but that's not what was played, though it is what was called, i.e. c4.

You know, I'm not sure I can ever remember in any film a reasonable or plausible chess or go move being played. Since I first noticed that no one ever plays these games properly in films I've started watching for it, and defective game play seems to be universal. In a typical film, people push pieces meaninglessly for a while, and then suddenly someone says "check and mate" -- of course in a real game between good players, checkmate is almost unheard of because someone resigned long before that.
Go is even worse in film, since western writers and directors hardly ever even bother to learn the rules.

Latest film reference: the go game in PI has an idiotic initial position, and the supposedly hyper-intelligent players are making very stupid moves.

How hard can it be to ask someone who knows how to play what to put in your film or animation?
