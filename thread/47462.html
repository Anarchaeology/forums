Booster price and expansion price, you guys gotta think a bit more!
Ok, the booster includes the game and is $20, I understand well enough that you'd want extra cash. but still, if we want the booster pack, why can't we buy it seperate for, say, $10? I'm still debating wether I'll get it or now, $20 is just rediculous when I already own over half of what I'm getting. And, with the booster costing $20, then what will the actual expansion cost? If it's seperate from AO itself, meaning you are not given the original CD, no more than $25, please. 45 bucks alltogether is bad enough when you already own half of what you're getting. I suppose FC has had enough bickering from everybody about the booster pack's pricing so I guess this post won't change anytyhing. But at least consider all of this when you price Shadowlands itself! I at least will have use for at least one extra AO CD because I got a friend who's interested. But I pity those who will end up paying some rediculous price and end up having three Anarchy Online CDs, one(two?) booster(s), and the expansion. I highly recommend rebates for current customers or the ability to purchase the expansion seperate if you end up including the original AO. And will you need to buy the seperate booster or will it be included with Shadow Lands? Again, another thing to make seperate for those already with the originals. Think for a moment, FC, you may end up losing more customers than you gain by doing this. Rebates or options to buy seperately. You have to do one or the other if you hope to keep many of your customers.
