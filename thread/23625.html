Make Wilderness Hunting a more favorable experience
I am lvl 100 and sick of doing mission after mission in 20k for XP..i would LOVE to go hunt in the wild for more variety, I would LOVE to take advantage of the beautifully created zones with tons of cool monsters however missions are so damned convient, no one wants to stop doin em!
