Mission Survival Techniques
There seems to be some confusion STILL about what causes aggro on Doctors and how to circumvent it. I thought I'd post the observations we have found as a regular team hunting together nightly. I did post as soon as the aggro patch was released, but it doesn't seem to have helped as today (a month? later) I just found the following quote on this forum:

"Not sure about the HP BUff aggro issue but I can verify the HOT = next on the list issue........."

lol

The team HP buff has a HoT aspect. You WILL get aggroed unless your team zones and cancels the caster tag. i.e. instead of seeing "DoctorX healed you for 154 points of damage" you should see the message "You were healed for 154 points of damage"

Come on chaps, try reading, absorbing and learning. I posted this stuff already. There is no reason for you to be dying in droves. Yeah you'll get the odd slip up, but ITS EASY WHEN YOU KNOW HOW!..........


Mission Survival tips (from a surviving doc)

Don't cast your team hp buff inside a mission area, (or an outside camp). Zone after it is cast. If you cant zone, DONT USE IT! Use temporary hp buff on your tank before each fight instead. (Yes, I understand this is a work-around, but until they adjust the aggro as promised, it works).

Do not cast team-heal in the main room of a mission. If there are ANY mobs in surrounding rooms THEY WILL ADD. Have your puller fetch them to the entrance room instead.

Main room strategy: Puller slides agg/def to full def, and WALKS into mission room, watching map for opportune moment. Unless he auto-defends or goes dance in their laps they wont even know he was in there. Puller attracts the attention of ONE and runs back to entrance room for his helping of whoop-ass. IMPORTANT: Stay away from the adjoining door!!! Step away so it remains tightly shut. If it is open and you cast a heal while fighting and there are ANY mobs left in the main room they WILL add.

MONGO is your best friend. If your tank runs out of nano, TOP HIM UP! First Aid is YOUR speciality not his.

Don't storm an unsecured room. Let your puller bring each mob to you in the room you just cleared. Remember the main room strategy and if there are ANY mobs in adjoining rooms simply don't fight in that area. Pull the mob back to the room you just secured.

Pet casters who don't have /pet wait or /pet behind macroed are likely to get you killed. When a pet goes AWOL his unusual pathing methods often lead to him pulling 16 rooms to reach the dude who was right in front of your face. (Besides, that goddamn running on the spot makes me crazy. WTF is that all about? /PET WAIT dammit! )

Try WALKING a mission. Helps with pet-pathing and that annoying lag that makes you run madly into an unsecured room (which you just know is gonna be a multi)

Don't even try to fight in a dodgy room. If it's a swimming room, corner room or ice room, or any other crappy pathing room, PULL BACK TO THE LAST SECURED ROOM (and remember that secured doesn't mean empty, it means secured on all sides).

If you run out of nano and you have used your first aid stim and there is STILL a mob on you, just zone, don't stand there shouting "NO NANO GET IT OFF ME!" There are times when we out-hate even the best tanks. Zone out to cancel the aggro tag and zone back in.

Get a fixer in your team. EVAC is your last-resort life-saver.



Hope that helps. Please feel free to add any other confirmed tactics that have kept you alive while all the other docs are whining, retiring their doc characters, rerolling, and quite simply aren't coping with this new aggro.
