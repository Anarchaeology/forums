Welcome Back Zuh (seriously)
Welcome back. I am glad (believe it or not) that you decided to return. I assure you - I feel no ill will towards you, nor do I have any personal grudge against you - seriously. I honestly hope that fixers as a whole can get along - you and I included.

I took a hiatus from the forrums for a significant time, and periodically recieved some information from a few people who kept reading them. When I got back, I read many of your posts, and while several were good, many more were based on a naivity instilled from the very high success rate of low-level fixers. That itsself was not a problem for me. With this innocence came information of several other fixers who told you what to expect at later levels. However, your response to these posts seemed to be an insistance that these players who encountered difficulty were doing something wrong. In addition, you commented on the negativity of the posts that were on the site, which while the criticism was somewhat true, was not grounded on the history of the fixer.

On top of this, there were small bits of misinformation, which you included in your posts (this does not refer to nano skills, this refrers to the freedom to explore weapons choices and so on). At low levels, most fixers will not disagree, our IP expenditure is realtively simple. However, by the mid 40s, the title cap is the only thing which allows you to allocate IP among all the critical fixer skills. Past 50, IP becomes so precious, that early mistakes can indeed cause problems and prematurely 'gimp' your fixer to a nearly unsurvivable state. The unsurvivability makes the fixer seem unplayable, and hence unfun. This winds up making the initial cuts in the fixer population. You'll notice the fixer class starts to thin in the 50s, and stagnate in growth and development. By the time the 60s roll around, a new phenomenon has occurred and the damage curve on the mausser has rendered it completely aggrivating. Luckily, now there is a Gamma Ejector dropped in the TLR mining complex which from all reports, sounds as if it allows the fixer to overcome some of the difficulties associated with this time.

Anyway... back to the point - the result is a steady decline in the fixer population at all levels - especially when it is visible that there are *so many* classes which are steaming by the fixer.

And I will agree with many of vegastar32's points, the fixer does have some nice things with them - these things are what keep me playing the class. (Side note: I find it really hard to pull in dungeons, I keep outrunning the mob ). As far as the whining goes, it depends on what you consider whining...

Personally, anything whcih has constructive critisism, displays a bit of insight, and otherwise analizes specific problems with the class does not qualify as a whine. Solutions to the problems that we percieve, or have found are ineffective here - as history will tell you, they have been ignored (or at least ignored up until this point).

By september last year there were requests for alternate damage types. The Cyborg Barracks included the first changes with servrous's void sprayer (I believe thats its name) whcih does cold damage, but by level 71, the 120+mausser you should be using should be doing you much better. Then the Michael Patriot was included, but while it did fire damage, dropped so rarely that many never got to see it. In addition, it was obvious that at higher levels, this gun would be subjected to the AC constraints, and not until the highest levels would be usefull again. With the extremely high skil cost, it is apparent - especially with the overequipping patch - that the fixer will have to be extremely high level in order to make use of it. This begs the question then of which is better use at that level, alternate weapons, or the pure SMG with alternate damge types? It also seems in this upcoming patch, another suggestion of allowing the gun to be hacked and modified has been included. This suggestion was made in september or october. Until the modified gun is used in the field, it will not be known if the conversion will be a bane to the fixer, ruining their prized gun (which I hope it won't). Now, I'm much less inclined to say FC isn't listening to us in regards to weapons selection, however I'd still agree that they might not be hearing the problem with our damage (low minimums, guns QL30+ higher than other classes are still doing significantly worse damage, etc.).

Now, with that said, since Zuh returned - lets give him a chance, hear what he has to say - and lets try to get along. I hope you (Zuh) realize that the past is the past, and we can get along. But, dont' be surprised if your posts views differ greatly than others, that they are put up to a higher scrutiny than others posts... Good luck, and welcome back
