Attn: Funcom Events Team
To the Funcom Events team:

This is in regards to the Intercessor event on RK2.

Thank you for running events on RK2, especially ones of this depth and which last more than a few hours.

Thank you for allowing myself and my organization to participate in this event in a way that may not have been anticipated and which went completely against what everyone else on the server was doing (at the time).

Thank you for making this event deeper and more meaningful than a simple black & white/evil vs. good ordeal, and for making the Intercessor more than simply an out of control killing machine.
