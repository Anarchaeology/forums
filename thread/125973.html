More 180-220 working hunting grounds
I'm in a slump at 126. There's nothing to do. I'm too low level to be going on raids. I'm too high level to be hunting borgs in a team environment in Mort. There are no dungeons available to cater to my xp and interest needs. I'm simply leveling for the sake of getting into raids, where I'll be waiting to get into a bid on items that I won't be able to use for a while. I guess there's PvP content but there's really a huge gap between hunting at borgs and raiding that needs to be filled with something other than team missions.

What are the problems with current hunting grounds?

1. Location
Mort Borgs is about as good as it gets. From there the hunting is very out of the way and for the most part unreachable. Thus it turns off people who might want to get there because of travel time/danger and the return trip if you hit reclaim.

2. Spawn Rates
Spawn Rates aren't dynamic enough. They should register how many different people shot at something from the spawn point and then adjust accordingly. If there are 24 people at a spawn, the rate should increase.

3. Drops
Obviously professions are going to gravitate towards profession specific gear. Since very few profession specific items at or around 180-220 exist at the range in which I can hunt for it (about level 125-150 IMO) I can't help others get items they desire. I'm not saying ultra-high powered things should drop there. I'd like to see lesser versions of what already drops at mercs and Diamondines and Tara.
