Leetas
Some thoughts
Well, it seems to me that there are a few main things that need tweaked, but I really like the idea of mobs having pets and using more things that players use.

1) Increased risk should yield increased rewards. If someone kills a pet, give them some xp.

2) Make it so that npcs can only summon 1 pet. This will limit their newfound powers, give people more options in a fight (instead of always ignoring the pet and going for the master), and prevent possible trivial xp gain.

3) Making mobs random individually instead of by room would be nice. Sure, there still might be 3 MPs in a room, but it would be very unlikely.

4) Let trimmers work (with good effect) on npc pets. This will give engineers a fighting chance (since they should have the skills for trimmers to begin with).

5) Reduce the overall power of pet casting mobs a bit. They are currently buffed up because of the expectation that everyone will be either over-equipped, a martial artist, or an enforcer. To give Engies, Agents (low levels), and any Nano breeds at least a fighting chance, they should have their power broght back down a little (or a lot if there will be no xp for pets).

6) I guess calms really dont need fixing on them, but please keep in mind how much meaner that makes them for classes that rely on their calms instead of sheer damage output. Yes, calming the master first does work...so at least there is an option.

7) NPC pets should not be way way higher than them (i.e. a green engie should not be summoning an orange/red robot). NPCs have already been made strong enough (especially for soloing, self equipped, and non tank type professions), dont give them another huge advantage unless you are going to go back through them and make them a bit more inline with a pc.

8) Give engies a system override nano that lets them have a chance to steal, disable temporarily, or flat out destroy npcs engineer/beurocrat pets =) or maybe a tradeskill item that they can build to do so. (and no, I don't play an engineer...but I once did and know how rough it is if you arent one of those people that always gets a mochams/infuse before setting out)


Anywise, just my thoughts on what would make this a nice addition instead of driving off new/current players that have a tendancy to solo or play for an hour here and an hour there.

-Religion
