Yet again .. (OOC whine hehe)
(OOC all the way)

Ok, this is a whiney post so stop now if you dont wanna see it. You've been warned.

Ok, long time mmorpg player (never a big mudder, but started with UO at release)

UO europa shard 1997-1999 had the best group of roleplayers you could ever possibly dream of meeting, always in-character be it for mundane things or epic things, embracingly great when new folks showed up, and happy to give reverse peer pressure to OOC folks so RP them to death, rather than drop character and become like them.


Ao has never had as strong an RP base as that, but it would be really hard to duplicate. Even the UO europa scene shrank eventually, so I know it is no easy thing to do. And I point no fingers at all here, please nobody take offense!

Forword complete, I proceed to whine ....


My characters in AO follow my rule of in character in /v /s /g,and in character or out of character in /tell. All of them, no exceptions. I even ask ARKs to use /tell with me if I need help with a game issue so as not to have to break character in public.

This said, I only really hang out with 2 other people in game! sounds strange, I know.. But they are RL friends of mine that I have P&P'd with for going on 8 years. We assume the roles and we have fun.

Every time I have gone to the "famous" bars and clubs, I have been disapointed to no end that RP doesnt seem to matter to the majority. A few times I have had a brief, but refreshing conversation with someone else who was also in character, I cherish those moments greatly. But outside the bars, clubs ect all of them with no exceptions become dewded And it is sad.

"baboons, THE place to role-play on rubika".. I hate that statement passionately. RUBIKA is THE place to roleplay period, its an MMo R P G.

I'm guessing a lot of folks that are RPers are in group, org, or /tell talk and thats why I dont see the RPing right? And all the "lmao" I see is just the "odd few" OOCers too ofcourse.. And I am probably a "n00b" for even stating my stance here too I'm sure.. Well if all that is true then great, consider me a n00b who would do a backflip of joy if he actually found RPers dedicated to being IN character all the time.


To qualify:

Being a guy in real life, but saying you are a girl in real life and talking about OOC things is not RP, its lying.

Emoting does not a roleplayer make.

"lol,lmao,roflmao,plz,thx" ect ect ect Are you so tired that forming words like "thanks" would put you into a deep sleep?

RPing events are nice and all, but how many go to RP how their character whome they RP all the time would act in such a gathering.. And how many go to see GM's turn people into 50 foot tall leets and use light shows so they can go "roflmao k3wl!" ?

I know we all have our styles of play, and I know mine is not for everyone, but it is very depressing when I read this forum and how creative and in character a lot of you can be, then I go in game and see that it's just not so.


Anyhow, thats my whine.
