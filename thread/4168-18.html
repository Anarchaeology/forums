Let's take a scenario from the perspective of a pet user.

A pet profession (*any* pet profession) is walking along with his pet on '/pet guard'. He's not overequipped, the pet is just the best that he can use with his own skill and buffs.

He sees a trader and send the pet to attack. The trader hits the pet user with a ransack. Suddenly the pet user's skill is less than 80% of the skill he needs to command the pet.

According to Gaute, the pet shifts to '/pet behind'. In behind mode, the pet doesn't guard and will not even fight if attacked.

The pet stops attacking. The pet user has no pet and is going to die. He was effectively one-shot killed.

Even if they code it so the pet doesn't stop in the middle of a fight when the master is debuffed, the situation is the same if the trader opens the fight with the debuff. Without a chance to send his pet into combat, the pet user is again dead.

Solution -- have combat pets default into '/pet guard' mode, not '/pet behind' mode. '/pet behind' mode is a fine default for healing pets. But allowing a single buff to remove 99% of a profession's offensive capability is a bad thing.
