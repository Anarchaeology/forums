Red Tape of Omni-Admin Recruitment Flier
Red Tape, a sub-department of Omni-Admin based in Argentum, 4-Holes are expanding our operations. That means that we are seeking and actively recruiting talanted individuals. We have taken part in many historical events that have shaped Rubi-Ka. Our department can offer you a unique atmosphere to work in that isn't repetative becuase of our administrative position great flexibility is needed. We oversee many areas within Omni-Tek such as intelligence, research & development, diplomacy and, public relations just to name a few.

Contrary to the popular belief, we are not a bureaucrat only department. All professions are welcomed. Currently we are accepting those that meet those requirements.

  • An experience level of 25
  • Be an Omni-Tek employee or willing to become one.
  • Able to report in at least twice a week.


Background checks may be run to ensure your eligability. If you are interested please contact either me or a general of the department so a brief interview can be set up.

[OOC]We are a small guild that offer our members a stable environment of friendly people to help one another on Rubi-Ka. Roleplaying is not required but highly encouraged. We have a good roleplay standing in the community.

Spies need not apply, IC or OOC and we are not powerlevelers. No alts that show up to be recruited then never seen again please!
[/OOC]
