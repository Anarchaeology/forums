bugged elevator
I dont know if you would call this funny, but it sure was wierd

i was with a team doing a team mission, and as usual half of em quit on us just before the boss for various reasons. The three of us left didnt harbour much hope of being able to take the boss by ourselves, but it was a fairly low level mission so we give it a shot.

Miraculously, we somehow manage to take down the boss, though it takes some time and a good portion of our first aid reserves. But we do it.

Beaten, bleeding, one of us begins looting the boss and posting the items in chat. Our third member takes the opportunity to go attack the mission objective (it was one of those observe ones, and in this case the objective was one of those goddam plant things that bite for poison dmg)

Well, the thing starts wailing on us, and we, half dead, run for the elevator. I /just/ barely make it, with only a sliver of health left, click on the vator button triumphantly only to see "You take 300 points of fall dmg", followed by a bright white screen and the you lost xp message....which just happened to be only a couple hundred points from the next level.

I guess they hadn't had that elevator serviced in awhile.
