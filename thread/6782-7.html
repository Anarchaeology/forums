This means if you have 120 or better in Martial Arts and wield a non-special MA weapon such as, say, a Hammer, you will be able to punch with it. This is something that was not initially intend to be in the release version. Therefore, as I said in my earlier post, it was not intended.

The system will be changed, but it won't be changed tomorrow. There is no definate date for this change at the moment. So, it's not as bad as my first post may have made out. Sorry if I scared you . The change may not come along for quite a while and when it does, a system equally as good will be replaced, along with weapons that use the IPs you have spent in.
