How to get rich
How to make money. Camp mission terms for high ql battlesuits. Run mission and sell Battlesuit to a *vendor*. A ql200 Omni Pol Elite Battlesuit goes for over a million credits at a vendor. This somewhat depends on your comp lit also. Sell all the loot you pick up to a *vendor*. The loot from one highlevel mission can often be worth 100's of 1000's of credits. How to save money. Camp mission terms for that item you are looking for. Run the mission and get it. I haven't spend a single credit on armor, weapons, or a nano for a very long time. Suddenly you will find that you can bring in a lot of money, but more importantly that you won't be spending it as fast as you get it. Anyway, that is how to get rich in this game, IMHO.
