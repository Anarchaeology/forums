you forgot to divide by 4.

a 100% int based skill gets one "free" point for every 4 pts of int.

edit: the actual numbers
400 int * 80% = 320

320 / 4 = 80 "free" nanoskills

10 stamina * 20% = 2

2 / 4 = 0,5 "free" mat crea which you wont see unless you hit the next full number (game rounds down).

so 400 int and 10 stam net you 80 "free" mat crea.
