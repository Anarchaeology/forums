A New Tide on Rubi-Ka - Sea of Change
Sea of Change is a very active group of roleplayers, who enjoy events, and wish to make Rubi-Ka 2 a more deep and involving place, rather than the forgotten dimension. Our guild has it's own plots and storylines which all of our members take part in, through events and odd happenings. Everyone is able to take part in our story, whether you are low or high level, and the particpants decide what happens, the decisions you make will change the outcome of the whole story. Through these events and plots everyone gets more immersed in the AO universe and has fun doing more in the game, than leveling and missions.
Our aim is to bring our own storylines and events to Rubi-Ka 2, since not as much happens there. In the future our guild storylines and adventures will be taken to the public, but not until the time is right. Our storys will evolve in the future, and come to include both Omni Clan and Neutral people. We must bring the story, because it won't come from above. Rubi-Ka 2 deserves the best. We will bring it.
Sea of Change is a clan organization who seeks roleplayers who wish to roleplay actively. We stay in character as much a possible, and this includes guildchat. If you are a roleplayer and wants the kind of atmosphere that sucks you into the game far beyond the regular hunt or mission, perhaps who may be just what you are looking for.


If you are interested in joining, or are a part of another guild who wishes to become a part of events & stories...
