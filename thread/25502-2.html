So basically, bean, if I'm reading that math correctly, Martial artists can heal more damage, in a three minute period, at level 132, than traders can heal in the same timeframe at level 200, given these proposed changes. At similar levels, Traders will be able to heal approximately half as much as an MA; this may change to about equal amounts of healing if the additional team heal lines enter the game.

And traders take damage, too.

I think part of the problem here may be that funcom is looking at "power" too fuzzily: they're double-balancing. MA heals are weak because they have a long recharge: they're emergency only, low output heals. Trader heals are also supposed to be emergency only, low output -- not because of recharge rates, but because they damage the trader.

Hence, for traders to be "about as powerful healers" as an MA, they would actually have to be able to heal about as much, over time, as an Adventurer -- because they take damage from their heals, while the MA's don't. What funcom seems to be doing is trying to balance the overall healing rate to match that of MA's (though they don't seem to be taking in-combat buff time into account), but they aren't also factoring in the damage traders take, when calculating the proportional amount traders should heal.


If this change goes in, perhaps the amount healed by trader team heals should be significantly increased -- trader team heals would then be the "poor man's alpha & omega," emergency only backup heals that were very effective when absolutely needed, but also very risky to use effectively.

More and more, though, I'm realizing just how necessary it is that the team health displays be fixed ASAP. That alone would return a great deal of trader's healing power to the group, by making the single target heals a viable line; if those were useful, team heals would be a comparatively small percent of the total healing in a given fight.

Beyond that, it is absolutely imperative that the health drain line be fixed before this change is made. If we're going to start taking truly significant damage from our heals, we need a way to quickly and efficiently heal ourselves in combat.
