PvP - thoughts of a newcomer
I've been playing AO since launch but I've avoided PvP because of the whole griefer/uber/overequiiping/exploiting thing - upto last night anyway.

My guild called me to 2H0 and so I went - and all I saw was cheating and bug exploitation on a massive scale.

I attacked a 51 MA (I'm a 44 Enf) and was doing ok - til her health just 'reappeared' - courtesy of an L169 Doctor!!! Cheating IMHO.

I attacked a 46 Fixer and despite this root attempts (broken by Rage) and his ql169 heals - he was going to lose before someone else joined the fray against me - so yes, Fixers do probably suck I'm afraid

The whole 'root whilst in 15 sec timer' and 'root when in a Yal' thing is explotation tho - people come thru grids and whompas in Yals because THEY ARE ON THEIR WAY SOMEWHERE perhaps - anyone who uses the 'root and force auto attack to break the 15 sec timer' cheat should be banned on the spot IMHO - blatant exploitation that one.

Now - there is a way to fix PvP - and it goes as follows - and until it's done, I'll be leaving it as the pit of cheats and exploiters I found it as.

Firstly, PvP should be 1 on 1 or Team on Team - once engaged, no-one should be able to attack, heal, root or otherwise interfere with either party (person or team).

Secondly, a repeat of the 15 sec 'delay' after each fight will prevent a battered person/team from being hammered by another one immediately after a fight - not enough time to heal perhaps - but a quick regroup or retreat should be possible.

Lastly, I really struggle with the '200 can attack a 75' rule - they are WAYWAY mismatched in reality so it makes no sense. 10% either way on level (max min in teams) makes sense to me - no-one could ever explain to me why a 200 can attack a 75... If people really want to do this - put in a 'mutual consent' option to enable fighting at any level...

My my 10p - sorry if I offended PvP players but the ones I met last night were - IMHO - cheats and exploiters to a man/woman...
