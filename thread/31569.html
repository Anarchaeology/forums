Suggestion for helping with looting issues
I'm sure I am not alone when I wonder whether or not someone from my team is looting stuff without mentioning it... Of course, I trust all of my friends but there are always situations where you have doubts. Funcom, why not implement something where anything looted is put into a chat window message? Something along the lines of "XXXX has looted a YYYYYY". This type of alert was implemented in Everquest somewhat far into its lifetime, and with AO not even a year old yet, there is still plenty of time In any case, I think this type of thing would be very useful, especially for helping with any ninja looting problems. Sure, you might lose an item, but you'll know who can be trusted and who cannot. Of course, to reduce spam, you could toggle it off, especially when teamed with guildies or whatnot.

Just a suggestion.
