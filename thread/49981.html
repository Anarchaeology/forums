Reclaiming Castle Camelot for RP
Ok so here's the deal - one of the most ornate and sumptuous locations on Rubi-Ka which is Perfect for roleplay is essentialy closed to us.... Camelot Castle. The reason - the Castle itself and Tarasque dungeon below are all one playfield and only 60 ppl are allowed in that playfield so if we try to have a little party upstairs or anything, the pvp'ers and tara kill teams get really angry saying we spoil their fun, and they can't get people in to fight. Plus apparently if we lots of people in the castle it makes that the 'main' pf and tara only spawns there.

Well this is poor designing on Funcom's behalf and I want to get this changed to two playfields - one 75% one for the castle, and one 25% one for the dungeon - that way we can have our fun in the caslte and the gung-ho's can have their little skirmishes below.

So everybody that agrees and thinks the castle would be cool for RP and events and ceremonies and that kind of thing, go post a message in the Suggestions box or even better fillout a funcom feedback form! Lets reclaim our castle!
