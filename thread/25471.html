UNITING ALL GUILDS interested in JOBE the floating city
Effective immediately, the Disciples of Jobe are spearheading the creation of a new alliance to span Omni, Clan, and Neutral citizens of Rubi-Ka. This new alliance will be named The Jobe Alliance[TJA] and will encompass all who wish to follow the path set forth by the great city.

The TJA is not above advancing your specialties and defending ones self, but we do not take part in the meaningless and pointless conflict set forth by the leaders of Omni-Tek and the Clans.

Our main goals are to advance the art that is the Science of Nano-Technology. We wish to make crystals, program discs, and anything and everything related in nanotech available to all.

I have started this alliance because I have seen Clan and Neutral members who agree with me, but do not want to switch over to Omni-Tek. This was not fair.

Now all factions of Rubi-Ka can work together in striving to accomplish the goals set forth by the leaders of Jobe. We can help each other out instead of fighting. We can share tactics and stories regardless of whether Im omni or you are clan. This is how I envision the perfect Rubi Ka.

So I present you with The Jobe Alliance and ask that if you are interested in meeting with me to discuss allying with the Disciples of Jobe, please send me a tell and I or one of my great Advisors will answer your request.
