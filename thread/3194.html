This issue really illustrates the Funcom communication problems. There were two dominant rumors early: nerf or bug. Then came the rumor that Funcom had said that it was a nerf put in place because people couldn't counterattack in PvP because they had not been attacked. Then came Cosmik's list of patch bugs that didn't include this issue, even though it had been extensively reported on here. That led us to believe that it was in fact a nerf.

Then came an email from CS to someone that was posted here saying that it was a bug. Then came Cosmik's posting that it was in fact a bug. Then came an email from CS to someone that said it wasn't a bug, that it was a nerf and confirming the original rumor that it was in place because of the PvP counterattack issue. Then Cosmik reiterates that it is a bug and not a nerf.

Then came the announcement that this bug was going to be fixed. Then the announcement was amended to say that it would be fixed in PvM but left the same in PvP. This has led many to think that it was in fact originally a very poorly thought out stealth nerf.

But you know what? We don't know. And they aren't going to tell us. All they have to do is tell us the truth in the first place and then we can react to and plan for it. But you know what? I don't believe they know the truth. I think the internal communications problem at Funcom prevent the CS people from having any clue as to what is really happening in Dev.

My two cents. I can't see any other reason for such a hodgepodge of official misinformation.
