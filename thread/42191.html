Food Festival?
I know many of you have seen my tireless character Thadnuriath trying to sell food and drink for 10 credits, mostly in Tir, for the cause of roleplaying and the milieu, though there has not been very many customers...

I think that food and drink is a very overlooked roleplaying aspect in Anarchy Online, though it is a very interesting one. Therefore I wonder whether I should, with help from my guild and friends, arrange a "food festival" in Bliss or Borealis. Would anyone at all be interested in attending to such a festival, or should I rather forget it?

It would of course be even better if FunCom would fix the looks on food, so that the objects get their righteous color, not only that red shade, and make some more 3D models, so that the Thermos don't look like the Tea-Pot when wielded or put on ground.

Oh yes, splendid idea. I would suggest using Last Ditch for setting. The area outside Reets is full of stall that look just perfect for a Food Festival sort of thing.

As with all these sort of events, the key thing is getting the structure right. While people can just mingle and chat to keep themsleves amused , it generally helps if you have a focus to the event, a climax at some set point the event (often the reading out results of a competition). That sort of gives people something to look forward to and signals at what point they can start thinking about leaving. Kind of like the stroke of midnight on New Years Eve.

Not entirely sure what the key moment of a Food Festival would be. Figuring these things out is where the event organiser has to earn his salary :-)
