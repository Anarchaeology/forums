Nice to see,
that people are still talking about something else than XPs, IPs, skills and the 'rest' of the technical stuff - there is hope for this game.

Seriously, this is/was the most fascinating graphical experience I made in my computer gaming life so far (I usually don't play adventures or FPS). The first day I entered AO I was addicted by the looks. Unfortunately you get distracted at some times by 'minor' technical problems.

Back to the topic.

-> Engineress: Lady, your attitude is correct. (/me bows to Engineress)

- Clon**** can be beautiful, just leave the parts that are deformed by the mining of OT (yes, this is some sort of political statement ). Head south and beautiful landscapes with colourful flowers in mansize, rolling hills, open forests and a river opens up. Have an eye on the wild life, especially for the more unexperienced the Panthers can be a quick trip home. Sunsets and Nightfall is strongly recommended.

- For the ones among us, who like it a bit more wild, Andromeda might be the right choice - no Whompa nor Grid connection, be a good walker or fly. You will be almost alone out there, the mountains are higher than in Clon****, the wild life more aggressive. Enjoy a swim in the south lake and/or climb steep cliffs to have a undisturbed panorama view over a rough and almost untouched wilderness.
Don't miss to visit the ICC headquarter when you are out there, the Tempel is nice as well.

- Thunderstorms: Great. From your words, I deduce your are Omni - well, even though the company is great (hrmm, PC here), there is more to see than the southern part of our planet. I was hit by a thunderstorm in Stret West for the first time. They are mostly bright orange out there, orange to red in Wailing Wastes - marvelous Desert Landscape for the 'Livingstones' among us. A highlight are the blue thunderstorms over West Athens - I once got hit by a green one, but can't remember exactly where.

- Another great place to 'fly' by is Avalon, there are magnificant lava sees and craters up there. Wild life is tough, even for the more experienced among us (mid level).

So, besides leveling, when you can afford the time, make a tip around. Neutral adventurer might be the right ones to ask, they usually come around a lot.

So long,

Max(imilian)
