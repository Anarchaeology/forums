I hate the orange crush (Please Fix the Tank Armor!!!)
Please remove the 100% nano cast initiative penalty "fix".

I can understand the "story reasoning" behind it (i.e. it's really heavy, e.t.c.) but then what are the cloaks made out of? Are they magical? If so couldn't the same kind of magic be applied to tank armor?

I won't even get into the whole take-off-your-armor-to-buff, not-enough-nano-to-cast issue...

You have made a cool looking armor available to the players. Why destroy it? I can hear more and more people contemplate switching to the coats for protection because of the nano penalty. Since the coats require str/agility, flowers tech will be the next logical choice for armor...

And soon all you will see str/agility based characters running around in pink helmets and orange or black cloaks.

This game has gorgeous graphics, and I hate the orange coat, but I wear it as an agent, since I can't find an alternative. I'm already maxing out my stamina, and with buffs I could fit into tank. But I won't. Well, at least the orange coat is useful for covering the ugly steel ribbed obtru/nadir stuff I wear underneath. I'd like to pursue the str/stamina line but I can't...

Alternatively, please make the decus/primus whatever come in different colors, or better yet make a kit that can be applied to a coat and which then self destructs. So that I can have a senpai black trench coat, or better yet, a senpai martial arts suit (oh wait, I forgot, those are social only - what the hell are you thinking?)

P.S. Personally I think that the real reason you guys did this was to sneak in a stealth nerf of soldiers and enforcers, for I'm sure that in your eyes, they had an unfair AC advantage. Fine, you fixed it with the introduction of senpai and nano cloaks. Not that I want that to happen, but I think it would be almost better if you would lower the AC of the tank by 20%. Tank armor is used by fighting classes, and those classes have to increase lots of skills just to equip their weapons (in addition to nano skills so they can use the buffs). Why does my soldier have to go through 7 recharges just to buff?

Oh and if you say that I'm overequipping my soldier's tank, well, I never put on more than IC and essence can bring up in stamina (both available in game, and in fact in most of the groups I play with, I just get tired of asking people to buff me with this stuff all the time).

P.P.S. This post is not meant to offend anyone who likes the orange coat look. I just think that some variety would be nice....
