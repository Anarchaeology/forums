Portable insurance terminal
From I have been reading about people *****ing about not going outside of Home or Avalon, or any of the other major cities is due to no nearby insurance terminals and the long distance you have to go once you repop.

So why not? Once you drop it on the ground, it sticks for about a 4 hour duration. It has six charges on it for only the members of that group, think of it as limited memory. Also, it only stores once, and stores your XP only once. If you die, yes, you lose down to that level and can't resave halfway through the hunt. That will encourage people to be more careful about fighting, but there would be alot less unnecissary traveling involved to get to a good spot.

I'll poll on it, but yes, the more I think about it, it would help alot more than hurt. I have a server monitor that tells the percentages of people in each zone, and it is red on all the cities and bone dry anywhere else. Which is really a shame, because it is a beautiful world out there.

I suggested a couple times already that, if you had enough Breaking and Entering, you could 'hack' the Reclaim terminals that are scattered ALL OVER THE PLANET. This would store everybody's DNA at that particular reclaim terminal. Finally, a useful purpose for B&E and that handy Advanced Hacking Device. I'm sure the Fixers would be happy to have this added usefullness. Perhaps as the locations become really far out, the requirements could increase up to the 800-900 B&E skill.

It would not store experience, however, only set the re-spawn location for everyone on your team to that terminal. This takes care of one number one major complaint about hunting in far off places like the Artery Valleys. People simply don't want to get in their Kodaik or Yalm and fly an hour to get back to the team.

Second layer to this is already promised to us by FunCom. The Bureaucrat is to get a line of nano forumulas that can save experience in an emergency. If the Doc or Tank is going down hard and fast, he can slap this nano on them and they'll lose only portion to potentially none of their earned experience. FunCom has had this planned for a while now. We wait patiently.
