It Begins
The room is small, one door and no windows. A solitary light strip flickers in the roof, making shadows dance on the walls. It is small, but it is all she needs. Just another room in another building in the heart of Omni-1. There she sits, on a pile of cushions. Cross legged, arms folded on her lap, a serine smile upon her face, and her eyes shut. However it is these eyes that give her away. While the rest of her body shows only visage of one at peace, a faint glow appears from round the lids of her eyes. Down the inside of these lids, the light from them burning straight down her optic nerves to her brain, flows line after line of pure data. 0's and 1's moving too fast for the human eye to make out, seemingly meaningless, but never the less allowing her access to the sum of all human knowledge on Rubi-Ka. If she can find what she wanted in time.

If. Even as she sits in silence, there is activity round the building. Armoured figures rush into the building, some stopping on doors, others roughly interrogating mere passers by, those whose curiosity may today well and truly killed the cat. However for all this, these men move with a purpose. They are sure they know the location of their prey.

Suddenly the numbers stop, and two words appear, ACCESS GRANTED. Her smile widens as the flow of numbers begins again, though different somehow, no longer are they the image of a hunt, now that of prize at the end. Two lights blink on the belt round her waist, faster and faster as more information flows into in.

The Men are outside the door, poised and ready. The building is secure, there is no escape now. The order is given. The door flung aside and in the men run, expecting to be met with shock, or even violence. But not nothing, no not that. The room is empty, save for a pile of cushions in the centre. One man walks over to them, and removes a glove. They are still warm, but no trace can be found of the one who occupied them. Suddenly something catches his eye. A piece of white silk, just a scrap, adorned with two short words. It Begins.

The pale light of the setting sun shines through the dusty air, and into the cluttered office. Screens showing images from across Rubi-Ka cover the walls, some still and quiet, others full of life and bustle. The light shines upon the desk in the centre, providing a more constant, though fading, illumination for the two men in the room, to counter the flickering from the screens.
"So there was no other trace? This from the man sat behind the desk, at home in his environment, but seemingly troubled by events. His hands played upon a small scrap of white cloth, as he waits for the answer.
"No Sir", the visitor, tall and proud, stands at attention in this strange meeting. Questions flicker across his face, who was this man, and what was his interest in this case?
"You did a complete check of the room?"
"Yes Sir, of Course Sir. The Omni-Pol squad that took over after us also came up negative Sir."
"And what about the grid?"
"Our observers didn't notice anyone entering the grid at any non standard points at the time of the operation Sir".
"Ok, you may leave".

Watching him go, the man leans back in his chair. Thoughts and doubt run through his mind. Who was this vanishing intruder? What had they managed to learn? How had they escaped? But most of all, what did this cryptic message mean. He looks down, to the piece of silk between his hands. It begins. Two words that are causing him so much trouble to unravel.

At the press of a button a section of wall slides back, revealing a window out to the world. He leans back, and lets the world outside flood in, taking in the busy skyline of Omni-1. The soaring towers, the jets fleeting this way and that between them, and the slow lumbering freighters heading for the space port. All this he sees, yet he notices nothing, his mind fixed on the problem at hand. Long he sits, just staring. Final a decision is reached. A wave of a hand brings a silent watcher to life. Stepping out of the dark corner, the robot turns its expressionless eyes upon its master.
Go to Mr Smith Says the man Tell him to bring forward phase two.
A pause, then,
Tell him we must hurry

There she sits once again. Not in the heart of Omni-Prime now, but a dark deserted tunnel, rough rock walls pressing in on all sides. Dust falls gently, faintly visible in the thin shafts of light that somehow manage to make the journey from the surface to this empty place. She sits once again the vision of peace. Arms folded, legs crossed. Her breathing gentle and her eyes closed. But no light is visible from behind these lids now. She is not working then, but waiting.

So it is true then? She is alone no more. A small nod of her head is all it takes; the newcomer knew the answer long before he asked. She opens her eyes and takes in the black clad figure before her. She witnesses thoughts flash across his face. Options considered and discarded. Then a new face appears. Resolution. He knows what course he must take.

You have the package? To this, she opens one of her hands, revealing a small sphere. Small, but seemingly containing a universe of light and colour. In another moment it is again hidden. But it was long enough to show him what he needed to know. Doubt has flown from him.

Then the family is behind you. We will play our part in this. You do not stand alone. For the first time a smile of relief and happiness spreads across her face. She rises, and takes his hand in hers.
Thank you. She says simply.

They both turn and, in different directions, leave this solitary hide-a-way.

Behind them, the dust continues to fall gently, eventually removing any final traces that a meeting every took place.
