Ok people, time to explain some things.

First, I don't how far back many of you go, but i go in computer basically to the beginning. Back when this is all started computer games were basically for computer nerds. The market wasn't as big, there's weren't as many companies, and the companies were smaller. Back then, when a game came out the "nerds" would go nuts over it and pick apart every detail. If the game came up short, not only would people complain, but they would actually commit crimes to express their displeasure. Now, obviously this wasn't the proper way to go about it, but there was no internet to complain on, so they felt harrassing phone calls, slashed tires, and stupid stuff like that was the way to go. I never agreed, but (being a child) i never tried to stop them. I'm wondering how we got from being so critical that crimes were commited to the point that not only do people not care if a product or service lacks, they actually chastise the players that do voice a concern. Not only do you roll over and take the companies crap, but you give flak to the people that don't. Its so confusing to me.

Next, your restaurant example is a terrible one. I'll give you a better example that really fits the spirit of the complaint more. I love Pizza Hut, and i have ever since i was a kid. I love the pan pizza, the stuffed crust, and most of all the garlic bread with cheese. I'd eat at Pizza Hut every day, if it wasn't for one thing. I've been to more Pizza Huts then i can count on my fingers (which is a lot considering i count on my hands using binary) and never, not once, have I encountered a Pizza Hut with good service. I love the food, I hate the service, as it universially sucks. That doesn't mean i don't go, but i now limit myself to maybe once a month, as opposed to every day. Eventually someone is going to make a chain of Pizza joints that makes Pizza I like just as much where the service is good and I'll eat there from now on (Papa John's anyone?). When that happens Pizza Hut will be replaced. Not because i like Papa John's more, but because the service is so much better I'll eat the slighty lesser pizza.

Now, to all the people that say to lighten up and its only a game. To you people I say simply, NOT A CHANCE. Playing computer games has been my hobby for the last 20 years. Its what i like to do. I enjoy it and I take it fairly seriously. Not so seriously that i'd kill myself if my level 50 super ranger got killed and lost the title of grand tree hugger of the forest, but seriously enough that i like the games i play to be quality and have good support. Its like sports fans. I have a good friend that's really into baseball, and he's upset that the baseball season might end bacause of the impending strike. He thinks its sad that Barry Band's season may be cut short and that he'll miss watching the games into the fall. Do I tell him to chill out because its only a game? No, because to him its not just a game, its something he's very intersted in and has been for years. He talks to peope about it, goes to conventions, and surfs the web to learn more about baseball. And that's ok, its his hobby, and games are my hobby. I will take it seriously and you'll just have to get over it.

Now, to all of you that just want us 'trolls' off out of the game I have this to say to you. What do you think Funcom would do if even 10% of their player base just went away? They've already cut staff to the point that nothing has gotten done in months and nobody can communicate anything to us. Do you honestly believe that they're fixed costs are so low that they don't need my money? When SWG comes out how many people are going to leave for that? How about Planetside? Or Neocron? Or a dozen other games? All of this will eat away at Funcom unti only the diehards are left, and that won't be enough people to keep the game going.

I understand that Funcom will not please everyone all of the time. And i get that the things like "Traders are too uber" get tiring and aren't really somerthing that can be dealt with. But my complaint is really a universal one that affects everyone in the gme. Improve your custoemr service and relations. Right now its terrible. IF it remains terrible we will go to a game that listens and responds to what we say. Frankly, its more fun for a lot of people to be in a smaller community, of a new game, and to be actually able to see were your input has been used then it is to be in something like this where the customer service team is just completely worthless. I can't believe i'm seeing people fawn over CZ for saying "we don't know" in different phrasing. Its not new info, its the same old line. And half of this board falls for it everytime. Doesn't it annoy you that your being played as suckers? Not even a little?
