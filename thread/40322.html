That combo is far from as good as some ppl think. Its mostly good for getting a 10k FA in PvM, but even then the X-3 does about same dmg with no swapping with that crit buff.

As was pointed out burst is unaffected by 100% crit. You get a crit fling and FA. You dont have time to switch to anything else until total concentration has worn out, I have tested this.

Weapon switching in itself is a big disadvantage, if you get trader debuffed while wielding a flashpoint and you are in trouble.

Another issue is that agents have all very dark blue fling,burst,FA,AR,RE skills. And we can miss with 100% crit buff during the FA so its not a 100% thing (crits can be misses since 14.2). And with the real crappy atk rating agents can get with AR/RE based weapons that is almost guarranteed to happen. Not to mention this costs like 1.3-1.5mill ip to do. Its not worth it.

During a 100% crit buff you get 3x3500 dmg crit + a 3.5k - 10k AS PvM with X-3 for total of about 14 - 20.5k dmg. Flashpoint can at best give 2.7k fling + 100-4000burst + 10k FA beeing only a bit higher than worst case with X-3 but lower than best case.

Now that might sound nice PvM but in PvP its not, ppl have reflect bracers and buffs, in particular energy bracers because of the flashpoint so the 2.7k fling is suddenly 500dmg fling PvP and the x-3 crits for 700-800. The biggest dmg dealers in practice in PvP is MAs who change dmg type and have permanent 24% crit buff, now realize that and put the focus where it belongs.
