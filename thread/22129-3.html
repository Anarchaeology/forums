I really hate the term 'Support Class'

It really annoys me to all end because it means a support class ends up being a 'sit on yer arse' class. Being a supporting character really in the end means doing nothing but watching the other classes carry the weight of the battle.

Having the total purpose of your character being casting a buff every 40 minutes, or ocasionally doing something, is not acceptable. Classes should all constantly be doing something to help the group. Wether its crowd control, tanking, nuking, healing, or gunning em down, they should all have some relative equal effectiveness in this area.

A fixer does not want to be a support character!

AO has four areas of character effectiveness:

a: Damage production

b: Damage taking (hp, damage absorbs)

c: Damage prevention (crowd control)

d: Damage healing

The sum of abilities a + b + c + d should relatively be equal for all the classes. This is elementary, an enforcer has high a and b, with little c and d.

A metaphysist, while not a great melee or ranged damage dealer, uses his buffs to raise the parties ability overall, and has pets to fill in the rest.

By this method you can see that the Fixer is probly the weakest class of them all, as they have no high measure in anything.
