Autopilot?
What about being able to purchase (most likely very expensive) something similar to a map upgrade, but for vehicles: The Autopilot upgrade.

When you are in your vehicle, you have the option to choose from a list of pred-defined key areas for the zone you are in. The Autopilot takes the shortest route to where you have selected. If you encounter a combat situation, this feature (of course) disengages.

Personally, I don't consider travel to be a gameplay element, so making it *easier* in any way should be allowed without unbalancing gameplay. Is trying to find a place or simply travelling to it manually a necessary part of the game? Perhaps locating a place is supposed to be a challenge. Maybe then the upgrade would only populate the list with areas you have already been to at least once *manually*.
