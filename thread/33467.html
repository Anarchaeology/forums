HE
The top levels of HE (superior, boundless, and Personal Notum Harvester) recharge nano for 131, 191, and 255 nano every 15 secs.

Nukes in the same level range use from 150 (AE nukes) to 300+ nano every 1-3 secs.

So, anyone who is doing continual nanoprogram running (NT, doc, trader, etc..) knows that it is rare for a fight to end that you don't sit down and use a nano recharge from a kit. (Thus, you burn up a 50 charge kit before you complete a single team mission ... and usually two of them ). All the HE does is give you a reason not to use a full charge on those last 300-500 nano that would take you to max and hope that you get a couple boosts from HE as you run to the next mob.

If there were a nano drain that hit for 150-300 nano every several sec. it would be very effective ( as each 'hit' would be one less nuke the NT or heal the Doc could do ). However, the NT can be killed in most cases before even getting close to running out of nano, it seems pointless.
