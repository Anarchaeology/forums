Killstealing - Yes, this can be tricky if a team are dropping a MOB in under 10 seconds, but if team/player A starts attacking a MOB, then some noticable length of time later team/player B does, then that is clearly killstealing. What that noticable length of time is is debatable of course... In 25% areas then a clan player/team and an omni player/team should be permitted to killsteal off each other freely, and in a 0% area then anything goes.

Spawncamping - well, if you are there first and have reason to be there (be it xp or loot) then if someone else comes along and starts to monopolise the spawn then that would be reasonable grounds for harrasment. Again, different sides in a 25% zone should be permitted to compete, and in 0% anyone can compete.

But I agree, it would be pretty much impossible to define absolute rules for this sort of thing.

The ARKs need to have clear guidelines. They need to observe the situation from hiding. If person A is consistantly going after the same MOB as person B when there are other suitable MOBs available, then person A's intent would seem fairly clear. If they are going after the same MOB and its the only suitable one, then that's really just competition.

And they need to make sure that the person they are issuing a warning to is clear as to exactly why. [Assuming the posted logs in this particular case are accurate, then that would not seem to have happened in first instance.]

Whether they play the game or not shouldn't be relevant, observation should provide a reasonable picture of a player's behaviour in many cases. And its the behaviour and not the game mechanics that is of relevance.

And ARKs should be much less strict in enforcing rules in a 25% zone if players/teams are of different sides, and as for 0% - rules? what rules?

Of course, these guidelines and stuff need to be made clear to the players too.
