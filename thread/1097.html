We've recently created an Underground organization to fullfill the needs of Omnitek's Intelligence sector.

We do the dirty work for Omnitek, we're a group of people who understand that sometimes it takes a certain kind of person to fullfill the needs of the Omnitek corporation and sometimes we have to "skirt" the laws to accomplish those needs.

We are looking for anyone who is loyal to Omnitek, be you thugs, Lifters, Monks, Scouts, etc. Your talents and contacts with Rubika's Underground are always in high demand.

We understand that you may have connections in the Clan and Neutral organizations and we want people just like you! Information as well as brute force is power, anyone who has questionable associations and reputations understand this all too well.

Idea
The Organization is not limited to any class at all. The Underground is for more than *just* fixers. Any shady type is welcome to join.

We're Looking at the current Underground as a fourth faction of sorts not limited to alignment or class.

We recognize that Fixers have the edge in the Underground, however we also recognize that there are many other classes that have major connections throughout the world of Rubika 1 & 2.


The Organization is just a launch point for us really, to provide a better means of mass communication between those involved in underworld business and happenings. Since we span the whole of rubika and are not limited to OT/Clan/Neut's, current in game communication is limited at best.

We've started an E-group over at Yahoo due to the lack of decent forums, we decided that a centralized means of communication would be the better way to go.

Hope that answered some of your questions.
