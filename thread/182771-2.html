I can't speak for anyone else, but I don't think that anyone's written a full guide because it takes a long time before someone has the understanding of the profession to be able to do it. And, let's be honest, the kind of people rushing to be the first 200 Keeper aren't the kind to sit down and spend a few nights writing a guide to IP expenditure as a Keeper.

In my personal opinion, hybrid weapons (weapons that use more than one attack skill to determine your attack rating, i.e. 2 handed edge 67%, melee energy 33%) are BAD. They lower your attack rating dramatically because professions generally only have buffs for one weapon skill, and implanting multiple main attack skills is difficult. To see if a weapon is a hybrid, look at the 'attack skills' when you examine a weapon.

For example: My main, Aameul, is a fixer. In a recent patch, Funcom introduced Illicit Lasers. They're cool weapons... easy to multiwield for a profession that's been asking for good multiwield weapons for a while, and they do good damage. One of the drawbacks to them is that they're 67% SMG, 33% Pistol. My selfbuffed attack rating with a 100% SMG is over 1100. My selfbuffed attack rating with my intras is approximately 990. It goes down because I can't get pistol as high as my SMG. Lower attack rating = more misses and less damage. The Lasers overcome that by having a lightning fast burst refresh, so you get an 'automatic' triple hit every 10s.

Most of the hybrids in the AO database, however, are not so well designed as the Illicit Lasers. I'd say probably 75% of the hybrid weapons that survive in the DB from release could be taken out of the loot table and the players would be better off. Newbies wouldn't be tricked into using bad weapons.

However, things like different specials aren't as bad. Special attacks on the melee side are dimach, brawl, fast attack, and sneak attack. Dimach is obviously a good thing for Keepers, since it's easy to raise and has a nice healing effect with it. The same can be said for fast attack. Most of our weapons are designed with the fast attack special and we have pretty good sneak attack buffs. Brawl is slightly less of a nobrainer for keepers. It's a good special from what I've been told, but it's blue and we don't have a buff for it. Sneak attack is blue, but we have a buff o.O. However, IMO, it's not as useful a special since you have to use it before the mob notices you.

Max Nano woes
Many Keepers, especially our Atrox brethren like my Keeper Hethradiah, have problems with casting our buffs due to not having enough max nano. Here is a list of items I compiled in another thread that help get around the max nano problems.

There are two ways to get around the max nano requirements on a nano... add to your max nano so you can cast it normally, or use items that bring down your nano cost, which is figured in before it checks against your nanopool. Both items that reduce your nanocost and nanoprograms that reduce your nano cost (available from NT's and Meta-physicists) will help lower the max nano needed to cast a nanoprogram.

  • CAS armor helmet - adds to max nano
  • Carbonum armor- all pieces add to max nano
  • Angellin's Wristband - A guildie found a low QL one of these in SL for me, but I haven't had to resort to using it yet. Good +max nano per QL, however
  • Exarch Robe - -1% nanocost and offers excellent AC's for the level at which you can acquire one. Available in the Temple of the Three Winds from various Exarchs.
  • Notum Splice - adds 195 to max nano in the left wrist implant slot... I'm currently using this implant rather than making a normal QL150 left wrist implant since nothing important is in the left wrist. Available in the Temple of the Three Winds off of the Defender of the Three.
  • Dark Memories - NCU memories that add to max nano, and a very nice amount of NCU for the comp lit required. Available in the Temple of the Three Winds from Lien the Memorystalker (down past the Deathless Legionairres)
  • CPU Upgrades - goes in a NCU slot and reduces nanocost of programs, which can bring the nanocost under your max nano.
  • Dodga/Alvin pads - part 1 is very easy to get for 75 max nano, part 2 is only slightly more difficult, and fully upgraded dodga pads are one of the top 5 or 6 shoulder items in the game
  • Viral Triumvirate Eggs - +150 max nano, +15 int/psy, in a shoulder slot... one of the OTHER top five shoulder items in the game.
  • Token boards - some serious +max nano and just requires some time spent token farming. The omni equivalents have the same bonuses.


Most things listed at QL200 or QL300 are actually available at a variety of QLs. AODB will let you interpolate to other QLs if you follow the links.

I'll continue adding to this list as I use new and different methods to get rid of max nano problems.
