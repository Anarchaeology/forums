Now they aggro as much as u heal members in team. Im not sure, but would guess 6 times larger chance to get a aggro on doc compared to a even singleheal.

What i know for sure, is that i get aggroed ALOT after patch, and its almost impossible for me to work in a team without a fixer with runbuff to run off aggros, or a partner doc to back up the aggros.

Also do doc get aggroed without any reason at times, and when others clearly should be higher on "hate list" to mob. (it was also at a distance that it was impossible for the cyborg to "spot" me as the first.) And this have happened several times, also at other places.
