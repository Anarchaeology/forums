Fixing the implant system and other current overequipping abuses.....
As has been mentioned previosly what exactly is "over-equipping"?
I definitely feel that when people use implants to raise certain skills/abilities which allows you to equip better implants that raise other skills/abilities which allow you to equip better implants etc etc etc. that is a definite abuse of the system.

I do not feel all equipment use should be based upon your base stats however... Implants are intended to boost ones "normal" abilities and skills to lvls slightly higher than the "normal" lvl... IMO the bonus to ones abilities and skills obtained from a set of implants should be taken into account when he/she goes to equip an item or run a nano-program. After all the character has in essence been augmented beyond his/her previous skill and ability lvls by the implant.... I do not however feel the bonuses for implants should be taken into account when trying to equip other implants... which should not be overly difficult to achieve. It would solve at least one major abuse of the system and still allow one to utilize implants for what, I believe, they were intended to do.... augment ones skills/abilities.

I have never been a big fan of requiring a certain stat, wether it be STR or Psychic or whatever, to determine wether or not a character can utilize a certain implant. (I would much rather see a certain title lvl requirement or some other method I may think up during this message.) I am however a firm believer that Treatment should definitely be the primary requirement.... but do not believe the "patient's" treatment skill should be used.... ( you really think you'd be concious and assisting with your new HEAD implant or LEG implant) I believe the implantation should require a second party (wether that be a Doc, implant terminal or clinic, or someone else who has put a lot of effort into training treatment) The devlopment of the treatment skill should be a little harder to develop for all but the doc (after all they're the doctors I think they should be the only ones that have it green Adventurer should change to aqua). there could possibly be a secondary skill requirement of pharmaceutical tech (you'd have to utilize certain drugs and medication to keep your patient stable and unconcious during the surgery) Last but not least the method in which things were done would need to be redone as well.

I have a few suggestions on how this could be done.... But at such a late point could prove difficult.

There could be treatment clinics in place of the terminals (or the terminals could stay but be modified) these clinics would offer varying services... at appropriate costs.... and therefore perform the implantation. there could easily be a max treatment lvl they can perform their services at and possible a max QL of implant they could insert (perhaps QL50 or 75)... with High cost prestigious clinics offering perhaps a slightly higher lvl of services. (perhaps implanting up to QL 100 or 125). If this was put in place the terminals could be used for fairly basic implantation ( perhaps up to QL 20 or so) for the younger characters.

Any implants beyond the QL the clinics can implant would require the services of a Doctor or other highly trained individual, and possibly specialized equipment that could itself have limits to what QL of implant it could handle. As stated before the treatment requirements for implants would need to be modified slightly to account for the changes... but IMHO I feel it would work nicely, realistically, and would solve numerous abuses of the current system.

With the treatment skill requirements to perform the surgery (not the patient's) the possible use of Pharmaceutical Tech as a secondary requirement, and the possible title lvl (or something else) requirement limiting the QL of implants to be used in a particular character... The current abuses would cease to exist, Docs would be become much more "doctor-like".... (have a use other than just being in a team to heal and your occasional buff) And other professions could still, if they want to, work at become a cyberdoc if they wanted to.... they just wouldn't be as good at it as a Doc of the same lvl (which IMHO they shouldn't be). Well these are just my ideas for Implants I have many more on how to balance/fix other areas that can and are currently abused. But this message is getting quite long. I'll write in my 2 credits on those topics a bit later. stay tuned.

I'd be greatful for any input or ideas...

Thanks all and have a nice day,
